(FADEIN: The Studios of 96.9 FM Talk. We see Donald James Havok wearing a headset and getting ready to come back from commerical break. His is dressed in black dress pants and a grey sweater. CUEUP: Johnny Winter playing the guitar as only he can.) HAVOK: (Over the music) Yeah Johnny...You play it man...(Music ends) That friends is THE best Guitar player in the history of music...Eric Clapton comes in at a solid #2 and there is no shame in that...Now there's no reason to keep on my endless talking, since this show is at it's best when we are engaged in our intercourse. So let's go to the phones, remember the number here is 1-617-922-1969, verison callers pound 969 it's a free call...(OORP this is really the number to 96.9 for many of the shows you can call into, please don't go looking for Havok) And up first is Bruce he is calling from the quiet new england hamlet of car...Welcome to to the show. BRUCE: Hey Don, great show, I'm a long time listener, now I just wanted to ask you about the NFW and this code of conduct stuff, what's the deal with it and how do you think it's gonna shake out?" HAVOK: I think it's going to be a corrupt political system that will basically reward those who need to be helped and punish those who NFW wants to punish...I've read reports about how people will have to figure out how to work out their attacks to make sure that the cost of the attack is less then the amount of points lost by person you mugged...Now let's say it's week 16 and you need to cause basically a 7 point swing between yourself and another man to make the playoffs...So you beat that man to death and as a result he loses his final match, putting you 3 points in the lead...Now this faceless group of "NFW's honor committee" go look at the tape of your mugging, and then they decide who gets to go the playoffs...Was your attack 4 points worth or mugging, was it 2 points worth of mugging? Was it an attack so uninjuring that the sissy you hit upside the head with a chair shoulda got over himself and gone on to victory anyhow? I mean, when you put career effecting decisions in the hands of faceless, nameless men, you end up with Tuck Rules, and since my audience is in Boston, I will stress that that was a rule in the books that was CORRECTLY called by the offical, but where is the NFW "attack" rules...Is a steal chair 5 points or 7? Is an unarmed attack from behind 3 points or 5? The other problem, besides uniform enforcement of the rules is that attacks of any nature should have an effect of equal cost to the attacker and the victim should the attack hurt the victim...Let's say I just can't stand Mike Manson and attempt to cave in his skull with a tire iron, and he goes on and loses his match that night, it shouldn't be hard, I should lose 10 points. I did everything in my power to cost Mike Manson 10 points, so my point total should be reduced by the same amount. But that will NEVER happen because NFW isn't really interested in fair fights, it's interested in keeping the egos of the elite of this company happy, it's going to play favorites and make sure everyone knows where they stand in the grand scheme of things... Let's say on another topic that it's week 6 of the Season and Monty has gotten out to a big lead...And this man has already promised that he's going to spend his points once he gets them, and he cripples me, he has Cojones come out and just put me in a hospital with a concussion, broken arm, he just gets me maimed, and I'm out for a month, does Monty lose 40 points? No, he'll lose maybe 10 if NFW is feeling any real pity for me...But I'm out 40 points, my playoff chances are shot, and there's nothing I can do about it...But NFW wants sneak attacks, NFW wants cheating, and they also want to pretend to be offering an event where the honor of the sport is upheld and it is nauseatingly hypocritial... As for the Handshakes...Well, I'll play along with those who should have their hands shook, but it's gonna get old quick, people in general are gonna get sick of it, and sooner or later the hand shake will go the way of traveling and up-and-downs in the NBA...The day Hornet, Evan Aho, Eli Flair or anyone else from the coddled elite of NFW are kept out of the playoffs due to a non-handshake cost them the points they needed to get in is the day that Hilary Clinton decides not to have a second helping of chicken wings, that fat, socialist, criminal ##### has NEVER seen a dollar she wouldn't take from hardworking Americans, or a piece of food she wouldn't jam in her fat mouth. What's your take on this whole issue, since you have been one of those who's gone after Flair, and I'd say, defended Aho? HAVOK: Now Dale, I wouldn't say I've defended Aho, if I didn't bring out the the more bitter, hateful anger in my soul for Evan Aho, it has less to do with the fact that he's CSWA then the fact that he's not in my division... Eli Flair can't have his cake and eat it to...It's that simple, he can claim he's been around the ballpark and that the CSWA tag doesn't stick to him, but then at the same time he has to tell that crowd of CSWA shills the NFW bussed in from the South to calm up and let him prove himself here. DALE: Now that's not fair, those fans have every right to cheer for who they will... HAVOK: Yes they do, But I happen to thing that the event was a giant celebration of NFW's staff to themselves for managing to twist enough arms, kiss enough ass, and pull enough strings to GET these guys into the company, and part of that was kissing up to the so called elite... Let me put it this way to you...A man who I only refer to as "That Great Man" came into NFW, as a total surprise, he showed up out of left field, the crowd popped, they were stunned, and the fact is, Calvin Carlton, that ass-kissing toadying little suck-up who's only claim to fame is that if he thought stealing the sun's heat would put him over, he wouldn't care if it ended all life on this planet, he'd do it, Calvin Carlton buried this man the moment he got on camera...And now that same Calvin Carlton, that same little heat-leech parasite, when he draws Evan Aho's name out of a drum he has to run to the bathroom to change his pants...I think it speaks poorly of the man that when he draws one of the Dogs Of War, he winces like he stepped on a tack. And when he draws Hornet, he flips out. Basically, through Calvin Carlton, and Doc Martin, who got this job for only god knows what reason...Basically let us know that they've picked Lebron James in the lottery and not some schmuck from the D-League, they are telling the fans what they should expect from the person in question...And well, except for you, Boston's Best and Brightest, NFW's fans have learned to expect that I'm going to yell at Monty a lot and draw heat because I'm a "Conservative" Or as I like to say call it, a real American. The fact that NFW had fifty idiots bused in to cheer when told to proves nothing except that it was a giant dog and pony show to let the public know that we got big name talent, and if they got anything left in the tank or not really doesn't matter, because we also have a set of BS rules to make sure that when those big names get into a jam, we can bail them out...It's a bunch of garbage, it's about as disgusting and unfair as the Supreme Court's ruling that racism is legal in America. It's a sham, and I'm not standing for it. For Eli Flair to complain that he's being called a 'CSWA'er' well no Eli, I don't hate you because you're from the CSWA, I don't hate you because you've had success there, I hate the fact that the company I've joined, one that had a rich history, a legacy that Shane Southern is proud of, a history that some would like to be honored, was buried in one night so that we could have haligraphy of the highest order with you, Aho and the rest of you outsiders who happen to come from that company...That's what I hate Eli Flair...I hate the fact that Craig Miles, Quentin Sullivan, Calvin Carlton, Doc Martins, everyone down to the guy who gets the camera man his water, were throwing crushed rose pedals at your feet and were trampling over each other to try to prove that they worshipped you more then the other guy...To me you're nothing more then Jayson Blair, a man who doesn't deserve fame, doesn't deserve the hype, and sure as hell doesn't deserve my respect just because you signed a piece of paper that said you agreed to come here... I signed that same piece of paper Eli, and I didn't get a big pat on the back, I didn't get to have Craig Miles get so excited over my pick that they just played the opening chord of my theme music, and the mere sound of those notes was SO exciting that three women in the crowd became spontaneously pregnant with my child. You want to say it's unfair you're being labled? Well I say it's unfair that everyone in this company will tell you your crap smells like roses...I think it's unfair that you're already seen as God in this company when you haven't fought match one... I'm about MERIT Eli Flair, not giving someone something because they are brown, or black, or green, or white. And MERIT means you earn something...And what have you done to earn anything in this company? Nothing, that's what Eli, you've done nothing, you miserable over-rated fraud...You Jayson Blair wanna-be...And the day you are exposed as the fraud you are I hope Craig Miles, Sullivan, and the rest of your kiss ass toady bosses in NFW have the honor to do what the editors of that commie piece of trash paper the Times did and resign...You're what's wrong with America Eli Flair, and until my dying breath, I'll do everything to prove that...(FADEOUT) [the thread's next post — 1 Jul 2003] (FADEIN: Eli Flair, somewhere.
