NFW All-Star Week: Randall Knox vs. Blaine Hollywood

13 Jan 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Are we on, Cally?" Shaky camcorder footage. "We're on." A young girl's voice from somewhere off camera assured everyone, even though there was no picture yet. "Push the button, Cally. The red one." There we go. FADEIN on a young man sitting in the back of a black van, the open doors providing an effective windscreen. The young man is dressed in a black pullover hooded sweatshirt with the words "VALERIAN'S GARDEN" emblazoned on the front. He wore a dark blue fitted baseball cap on his head, backwards over his dirty blonde hair. 'Sudden Impulse' Randall Knox looked relaxed and unafraid. Behind him in the van, two boys of about seventeen to nineteen were assembling a pair of skateboards. "Blaine Hollywood is a name I haven't heard before. Like it matters, I'm almost positive that Randall Knox is a name you don't know." He took off his cap, letting his chin-length hair fall down in front of his face. "What's Randy Knox got to do with All-Star week in the NFW? I dunno. I got a call from a good friend who told me she had a match for me to get ready for. She gave me a phone number and said 'Talk to Craig Miles, he'll get you set up.'" "Craig Miles. *******, like I wasn't nervous enough." He ran his hand through his hair and replaced his cap. "Let me tell y'all a bit about myself. Randall Edward Knox. Born and raised in the greatest city in the world - the Bronx. I just passed eighteen, five months shy of a highschool diploma, and have been working on my wrestling in Terry Cooper's gym since I was fifteen." "Want some background on me, Blaine? Call the gym, it's listed. Either I'll answer the phone, Brian McGinnis will answer the phone, or Ivy McGinnis will answer the phone. Or check out the dark match on the fan's Wrestling organization's Countdown to Oblivion 1 (http://www.fwowrestling.com/cgi/events/chapters_main.cgi?countdown_123103&2003&&5) event. Either way." Knox leaned his chin on his arms. "Ball's in your court, Blaine... let me know what'cha got." "This match isn't about a career, kiddo... I'm willing to be we've both got a ton'a years in front of us. This match won't make me or break me... won't make you or break you. I'm lookin' forward to a good, tough, competitive match." "Don't let me down, Blaine." FADE [the thread's next post — 15 Jan 2004] (FADEIN: To a rolling hillside, and a clear, cloudless sky. Suddenly, a object goes flying through the air, spinning, and just as fast as we see it, we hear a loud 'crack!' and the object explodes into a million pieces.) (Beats later, we hear a faint voice scream 'PULL!' and what we now realize to be a clay disk flies into the air, and is popped into confetti after another 'crack!' is heard. The camera pans over to a lush estate, and a patio, next to a huge pool shaped like a capital letter 'H', and a short, hunched over Cuban man in a white tuxedo working the controls of a slingshot device used for skeet shooting. A few yards away, now right in front of the camera is a man holding a sporting rifle - 25, 6 foot 5, and biracial, with a close cropped haircut, crisp white button-down shirt, pantalones with black riding boots, and matinee idol charm with a familiar evil glint in his green eyes. You're looking at none other than infamous wrestler-turned-wrestling announcer Lamont Hollywood's only son, BLAINE HOLLYWOOD. BLAINE calls for another target, and his manservant lets one fly - CRACK! perfect shot. He chuckles like he knows no other option but to be perfect, and no-looks the rifle right at MIGUEL, who snatches it and immediately begins to clean it.) H'WOOD: (Looking up at the sky, nose high, and produces some high-end shades from his pocket, and puts them on) "Hmph. I don't need to explain ANYTHING to ANY ONE. You know who I am. And if you don't - you're an utter waste of air, and a piece of s(BLEEP!) that my waitstaff cleans out of my septic tanks. I am Blaine Hollywood, and I am your superior. While you were learning how to hotwire Nissan Sentras, I was in the richest finishing schools in the country. While you were clogging up your community colleges' computer lab servers with porn downloads and stealing music, I was in OXFORD UNIVERSITY, leading the fencing team to yet ANOTHER national Championship. While you were sleeping in gutters eating pork and beans, I was eating caviar in the company of Kings and Queens. Yes, Blaine Hollywood is like no other man alive. And now, I step foot into New Frontier Wrestling, as yes, a new face to the NFW ring, not a new face to Wrestling, and I will show you WHY, in moments time, I will be the name that will be on the lips of every woman, and the back of my hand will slap the faces of every man who dare opposes me. You want to say that I'm spoiled? a Punk? Hmph. The captain of the Oxford Boxing club and Judo Association is standing in front of you saying otherwise. I can punch you right in your face, or throw you across a room, and never ruffle the seams of my tailor made, monogrammed shirt. I'm an OXONION, and that, you beggars, spells PRESTIGE, WEALTH, and SUCCESS. These things are what I live for. And this skaterat who had the misfortune of being cast into a match with me at AllStar Week - ROOKIE NIGHT... well, sir, I suggest you and your 'buds' don't quit your day jobs, (thinks) wait. I'm sorry - people like you can't HOLD jobs. You're beneath me, 'Randy'. And I'd rather not sully my hands with your blood, so I'll end you quickly, then retire to my stretch Escalade, pearl white." Mr. Knox? It's funny that your name inspires visions of gold, but after a Chance Encounter with Blaine Hollywood, you'll do well to remember what they say about things that glitter - but they never mentioned that those things can also bleed from blunt head trauma just as easily. GOOD DAY." (END.)
