Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Fortune Wrestling opened in February 2003 on a hosting hub, under a company called Headache Empires and a title bar that read “|| Fortune Wrestling || No Cookies, Just Wrestling. ||”. It had a show called Thursday Stampede, an application form, a set of forums and three Chapters linked from its own front door.

The one card it left is for 13 February 2003, live from the Huntington Civic Arena in Huntington, West Virginia: Danny Saint against Soutter in a singles match, and “The Mimic” Judas Brattigan against Robert Hunglestein III against Michael Stryfe in a triple threat — five men, every one of them making his debut. The night was also to end with an interview with Michael Stryfe and the unveiling of the federation’s own President — a woman, according to the news post a week earlier, from a billion-dollar business, known for her looks and for taking companies from nowhere to superstar status, with more to be revealed that week.

She never was unveiled, as far as anything held goes. The champions page still reads Wrestler Name under all three of its championships — World, United States and Cruiserweight — and under the contenders beneath each of them; the tag-team page says “No tag teams at the moment”, and the Pressure Point card is for March ?? 2003, live from ???, its own referee and its own triple threat left just as unnamed. How it ended isn’t in the surviving record.

Storylines

fW’s whole surviving record is one card and a set of unfilled templates. Thursday Stampede carries no result for either of its two matches — a card is a promise, not a recap — and nothing dated later than 13 February 2003 survives to say what, if anything, followed. Five men debuted the same night, and none of them appears again in the held corpus; the President the news post promised to reveal that night is never named anywhere the record survives. No second show, no follow-up post, and no return appearance ties any of it into an arc this crawl can honestly call a storyline rather than a single, unresolved night.
