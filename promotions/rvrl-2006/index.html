Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

River Valley Rumble League's own founding FAQ, first posted 15 May 2006 and reposted 21 June, narrates itself as the revival of an already-closed 1980s territory: founded in 1978 as Missouri Valley Wrestling around southern Missouri and northern Arkansas by “Diamond” Jack Tatum, renamed River Valley Rumble League as it expanded into St. Louis and Little Rock, and absorbing a series of regional rivals — the Razorback Wrestling Association in 1983, Delta Pro Wrestling soon after — on its way to becoming, in its own telling, the only wrestling body of significance across four states.

Its own account of decline follows the same shape as its rise: Big League Wrestling raided its top talent in 1985, including Heavyweight Champion “Dirty” Dick LeBaron and the tag team repackaged elsewhere as “Heavy Metal,” and the promotion spent a decade running down before folding outright in 1996, its name and archive sold off. The 2006 FAQ presents Tatum reacquiring the rights through what it calls legal maneuvering and a few loopholes, and relaunching the league.

The revival's own first show, “Thursday Night Rage,” ran 8 June 2006 from the Little Rock Civic Center, opening with announcer Casey Blain welcoming the crowd into an eight-man tournament to crown both a revived RVRL Heavyweight Championship and a Missouri Valley Heavyweight Championship. Its first documented bout saw Aries Priest, accompanied by manager Darius Walker, defeat “Reverend” Al Horton. The tournament's own two inaugural champions were Rob Cole, for the Heavyweight title, and Jay Himalay, for the Missouri Valley title.

No further document under this name survives past the FAQ's own 21 June repost; how the promotion continued past its own first show isn't in the surviving record.
