RVRL Thursday Night Rage (8 June 2006)

8 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
River Valley Rumble League THURSDAY NIGHT RAGE June 8, 2006 - Little Rock Civic Center, Little Rock, Arkansas [The capacity crowd at the Little Rock Civic Center in Little Rock, Arkansas buzz with anticipation. A few fans near the aisle pop as a gentlemen in a blue blazer, opened Oxford shirt, and slacks works his way towards ringside. He enters the ring and Southern wrestling fans show their appreciation for none other than Casey Blain, the voice of Southern wrestling for several years. With microphone in hand, Blain enters the ring.] BLAIN: Little Rock, Arkansas... [The fans cheer at the mention of their hometown.] BLAIN: Welcome to the Civic Center... [The cheers continue to build.] BLAIN: And welcome back to your River Valley Rumble League! [The fans pop loudly as Blain smiles.] BLAIN: We know that it's been a long time coming, but RVRL is finally back... and this time, we're here to stay! [Another pop.] BLAIN: Tonight, you will be witnessing history in the making. Not only will you see the return of this historic organization, but you will also bear witness to the birth of a new era. Tonight, we will be crowning not just one but two RVRL championships! [The crowd lets loose.] BLAIN: Before the end of the night, someone will be walking out of here as the new RVRL Heavyweight champion! And let me tell you, after a week of talking with these guys, I can tell you this, Little Rock: You are about to get action like no other organization can provide. Tonight, you will see what true wrestling is all about! [Pop.] BLAIN: So without further adieu, let's get down to what RVRL does best: Good ol' fashioned River Valley Rumblin'! Let the tournament begin! [The fans applaud as Blain exits the ring. Moments later, Roy Hardee, RVRL's official ring announcer, enters the ring.] ***************************************************************************************************** RVRL TITLE TOURNAMENT 1st Round Match: ARIEST PRIEST w/Darius Walker vs. 'Reverend' AL HORTON ***************************************************************************************************** HARDEE: This contest is one fall with a thirty-minute time limit in the first round of the RVRL Heavyweight title tournament! ["View that Holy City", an uptempo Southern gospel track by RiZen cranks out from the arena loudspeakers.] HARDEE: Now entering the ring... [The fans let loose with a modest pop in anticipation.] HARDEE: From Dothan, Alabama, weighing in at 250 pounds ... here is REVEREND AL HORTON! [Another pop from the crowd as Reverend Al Horton, a densely built African American garbed in a black preacher's ensemble enters the arena. He pauses for a moment before flailing about as if possessed by the Holy Ghost. He then works his way towards the ring. His music fades out.] HARDEE: And his opponent... [The crowd lets loose with a series of jeers as Darius Walker, the manager of Aries Priest, enters the arena.] HARDEE: Led to the ring by his manager, Darius Walker, from St. Louis, Missouri, weighing in at 315 pounds ... here is ARIES PRIEST! [The massive former mixed-martial arts champions enters the arena to a chorus of boos. He and Walker work their way towards ringside, ignoring or outright slapping away the outstretched hands of the fans. They enter the ring and the bell sounds as Walker heads out of the ring.] [Priest and Horton do a collar and elbow tie-up in the ring. Priest gets the initial advantage, quickly shifting a knee into Horton's stomach. He immediately takes Horton down with a belly-to-belly suplex, planting Horton firmly into the mat. He then picks Horton up and delivers a crushing fallaway slam. He goes for a quick pin but only draws a two count.] [Priest picks up Horton and gives him an armwringer. Horton reverses the armwringer and follows immediately with a short-armed clothesline, which takes the big man down to the canvas. Horton backs into the ropes and then rebounds with a knee drop to Priest's forehead. Horton retaliates with a quick pin, but only draws a one count before Priest powers out.] [Horton picks up Priest and lays into him with a series of punches. Priest retaliates with a swift kick to Horton's stomach, which doubles the preacher over. Priest then rebounds off the ropes and delivers a scissors kick, which drops Horton to the canvas. Horton scrambles to his feet and finds himself immediately wrapped into a half-nelson sleeper.] [Priest holds onto the sleeper for several moments. The referee checks to see if Horton submits, and he nearly does. However, cheers from the crowd gets Horton's adrenaline pumping. He elbows Priest, getting Priest to relinquish the hold; however, he quickly finds himself a victim of a vicious thrust kick to the head. Priest goes for the pin and gets a near fall.] [Priest picks up Horton and whips him hard into the turnbuckle. He then charges after Horton for the avalanche, but he misses as Horton slinks out of the way. As Priest attempts to recover, Horton manages to hit Priest with The Right Hand of God, a loud pimp slap to the face, which draws the first major pop from the crowd. He follows this with a hammerlock pumphandle slam. Horton then mounts the top rope and springs with a flying elbow smash-but meets only the heel of Priest's outstretched boot.] [Incensed, Priest rises as Horton struggles to get to his feet. He picks up Horton, who is essentially out on his feet, and whips into the ropes. He catches Horton and delivers a brutal Spinebuster from Hell. He covers Horton and gets a quick, forceful victory.] [As the referee goes to raise Priest's hand, Walker immediately enters and shoves him away. He then raises Priest's hand as the fans boo loudly.] HARDEE: The winner of the match... ARIES PRIEST! [Priest smirks as he and Walker quickly exit the ring. The ring is then cleared for the next match. ***************************************************************************************************** RVRL TITLE TOURNAMENT 1st Round Match: 'The Motor City's Own' BRIAN McCORMICK vs. COLT DANIELS ***************************************************************************************************** HARDEE: This contest is one fall with a thirty-minute time limit in the first round of the RVRL Heavyweight title tournament! ['Back to the Remedy" by Seether begins playing over the loudspeakers. The fans let loose with a pop.] HARDEE: Now entering the ring... [A tall, muscular figure in silvery shorts with flame decals on both sides enters the arena. The fans let loose in recognition of this perennial crowd favorite.] HARDEE: From Detroit, Michigan, weighing in at 275 pounds... here is 'THE MOTOR CITY'S OWN' BRYAN McCORMICK! [The fans pop again as McCormick, with an intense gaze in his eyes, works his way towards ringsides, slapping hands with the occasional fans. He enters the ring and warms up as his music fades.] HARDEE: And his opponent... [The opening chords of the legend Johnny Cash's version of "Hurt" begins to play over the PA system. The fans begin cheering again.] HARDEE: From Austin, Texas, weighing in at 220 pounds... here is COLT DANIELS! [Colt Daniels emerges from the back in his burnt orange wrestling trunks with the white Texas longhorn displayed proud over the backside. On his head sits a Texas Longhorns baseball cap turned backwards. The fans give the young superstar a warm welcome, Colt returns the appreciation slapping every fan's hand he can on the way to the ring.] [As he finally reaches the ring, he taps the side of the ring with his knuckles and tosses his hat to the side launching himself into a head first dive under the ropes. He hops to his feet raises his arms ready to start this match!] [As the bell sounds, McCormick and Daniels enter into a collar and elbow tie-up. The larger McCormick powers out of it, shaving Daniels to the mat. Daniels quick scrambles to his feet and the two lock up again. Daniels quickly executes a go-behind and tries to take McCormick down with a belly-to-back suplex. However, McCormick propels Daniels back into the turnbuckles, breaking his waistlock.] [McCormick then sizes up Daniels for a series of firm punches to his forehead. He dazes Daniels before Irish whipping Daniels out of the corner. The young Texan manages to reverse the whip, sending McCormick chest first into the turnbuckle. As McCormick stumbles backwards, Daniels finally catches him with a belly-to-back hammerlock suplex. He goes for the pin, but only draws a two count.] [Daniels picks up McCormick and executes a vertical suplex. He goes to deliver an elbow drop but misses completely as McCormick moves out of the way. As Daniels rises from the canvas, McCormick catches him with a back elbow smash. McCormick then shows his power and picks up the youngster and slams back to the canvas with a crushing body slam. He covers Daniels and gets a two-count before Daniels kicks out.] [McCormick picks Daniels off of the mat. However, Daniels executes a quick armscissors takedown and stuns McCormick with a near fall. McCormick rises quickly and goes to clothesline Daniels, who ducks under. Both men run into the ropes, but Daniels connects first with a flying shoulder tackle, which levels McDaniels.] [With McCormick flat on the canvas, Daniels runs into the ropes and executes a stellar springboard moonsault. He goes for the pin and gets a two count before McCormick powers out. Daniels picks up Daniels and then runs back into the ropes. He charges at McCormick, who dumps him over with a high elevation backbody drop that sends the youngster crashing to the mat.] [As Daniels holds on to his lower back, McCormick storms towards him and hoists him into an Argentina backbreaker rack (torture rack). Daniels wails in pain as McCormick bounces. However, the resilient Daniels refuses to submit, so McCormick simply falls back atop Daniels. He covers for a pin and gets a near fall.] [McCormick stands and quickly cinches Daniels between his legs. He lifts the Texan up for a powerbomb, but Daniels, as he rests atop McCormick's shoulders, nails the Detroit native with a stiff right hand between the eyes. McCormick releases Daniels, who grabs McCormick's head as he manages to land on his feet. Daniels executes a spinning DDT and goes for the pin, nearly getting a three count before McCormick places a boot on the bottom rope.] [With McCormick flat on the canvas, Daniels mounts the top rope and springs off with a high elevation body splash. He lands atop McCormick and draws a loud pop from the crowd. He goes for the pin and gets another near fall before McCormick manages to raise a shoulder.] [Daniels picks up McCormick and wraps into an inverted facelock. However, McCormick twists around, hoists Daniels atop his shoulders, and then grabs both of Daniels' legs for a leg capture slam. The jarring slam stuns Daniels. McCormick stumbles about trying to regain his wits.] [Daniels manages to scrape himself from the mat. He charges McCormick, who catches him. With a display of impressive strength, McCormick presses Daniels above his head. He benches him several times before hoisting him for the final time for the big gorilla press slam. He walks from underneath Daniels, who drops hard to the canvas, eliciting a collective gasp from the crowd. McCormick goes for the pin and gets the three count.] [Until the referee notices that Daniels has his feet on the bottom rope.] [McCormick wearily picks up Daniels. He lifts Daniels across his right shoulder and carries him towards the turnbuckles. He turns around, set to execute a running powerslam. However, Daniels slips from his grasp and quickly boots him in the midsection. With McCormick doubled over, Daniels quickly executes a rocker dropper, sending McCormick crashing to the mat.] [Daniels pauses in the corner as McCormick tries to get to his knees. He quickly charges McCormick and nails him with the Colt .45, a shining wizard knee strike. He covers the big man and gets the victory!] HARDEE: The winner of the match... COLT DANIELS! [The crowd pops as the young Texan has his hand raised in victory. McCormick stands up and approaches him. McCormick looks as if he's about to level Daniels, but instead sticks out a hand. Daniels appears apprehensive as he places his hand in McCormick's, but the two finally shake. McCormick gallantly raises Daniels' hand and the two celebrate a hard fought battle.] [The ring is cleared for the next match.] ***************************************************************************************************** RVRL TITLE TOURNAMENT 1st Round Match: JAY HIMALAY vs. THE MASKED KRISHNA ***************************************************************************************************** HARDEE: This contest is one fall with a thirty-minute time limit in the first round of the RVRL Heavyweight title tournament! [The houselights go down. What little light there is illuminates a gold-sequinsed jacket, which sparkles brilliantly in the darkness. Fans begin to jeer loudly.] HARDEE: Now entering the ring ... [The opening hard rock tones of "Lapdance" by N*E*R*D* crank out from the arena loudspeakers.] HARDEE ... from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, weighing in at 229 pounds .... here is JAY HIMALAY! [The houselights rise again and the young Pakistani does a spin kick, which turns him towards the crowd. As the music cranks out, Himalay begins working his way down towards ringside, jawjacking with the crowd.] [Once inside the ring, Himalay immediately mounts the second turnbuckle and keeps shooting lip at the crowd, who lets him have it with a loud chorus of boos.] HARDEE: And his opponent... [A couple jangles of a tambourine ring out.] HARDEE: From Sridhama Mayapur, India, weighing in at 165 pounds... here is THE MASKED KRISHNA! ['My Sweet Lord' by George Harrison starts to play, and from out from behind the curtain, appears the Masked Krishna. He carries a bouquet of carnations in one hand and a prayer mat in the other. He slowly makes his way down to the ring, handing out flowers to the fans as he passes. He climbs the ring steps, wipes his feet, then enters the ring. He goes to the center, unrolls the mat, sits down and assumes the lotus position.] [The bell sounds as Himalay gazes at The Masked Krishna in confusion. Himalay complains to the referee as The Masked Krishna rises from his prayer rug. Much to the crowd's amusement, Krishna offers Himalay a carnation. Himalay frowns, walks away, and quickly turns back around and levels Krishna with a European uppercut. Krishna stumbles back into the turnbuckle, where Himalay nails him with several judo chops to the chest.] [As Himalay walks away from Krishna, cockily jawjacking with the crowd, Krishna hops atop the top rope. As Himalay turns around, Krishna stuns him with a flying dropkick. Seeking to get this conflict over quickly, Krishna goes for the quick pin and nearly surprises Himalay and the crowd with a near three-count. Himalay gets up, complains to the referee, and then charges at Krishna. Krishna evades HImalay's charge with a Matrix-like backbend. Himalay slams into the turnbuckle chest-first. As Himalay stumbles back, Krishna takes him down with a snap mare and wraps him into a chinlock.] [Having held onto the chinlock for a moment, Krishna begins to lose his grip as Himalay rises to his feet. Himalay sends an elbow into Krishna's gut to give him separation. Krishna releases the chinlock as Himalay charges into the ropes. Krishna takes Himalay down with a drop toe hold before scrambling back to his feet. Himalay quickly rises and Krishna charges after him. Himalay greets Krishna with a kick to the groin, stopping him dead in his tracks. Himalay then crouches down low and delivers a Pakistani palm thrust, a lifting palm strike to his opponent's chin. Himalay goes for the pin and gets a solid two-count before Krishna kicks out.] [Firmly in control, Himalay drops Krishna back to the canvas with an inverted backbreaker. He quickly follows the backbreaker with a snap elbow drop. Himalay goes for the pin again, but only draws another two count. Himalay picks up Krishna and sends him into the rope. Himalay executes a standing dropkick-but catches nothing but air as Krishna holds onto the ropes. Krishna quickly wraps Himalay into a spinning toe hold. After several revolutions, Himalay finally kicks Krishna in the chest with his free leg sending Krishna backwards into the turnbuckle. Himalay struggles to get his feet. Krishna charges at him, but Himalay catches his arm and flings him to the mat. Krishna quickly rises to his feet but is greeted with a hard back brain kick, which actually hurts both men.] [For a moment, Krishna lays flattened on the mat as Himalay clutches at his knee. Both men struggle to their feet. Himalay pokes Krishna in the eye before taking him down quickly with a snap DDT. Himalay goes for the pin and nearly gets a three-count, but Krishna somehow finds a way to get a shoulder up. Himalay picks up Krishna, but is surprised when Krishna quickly wraps him up with an inside cradle and picks up a near three-count himself.] [Both men rise. Krishna quickly delivers a kick to Himalay's knee, sending Himalay to the mat. Krishna slowly drags Himalay to the corner before going to the outside, where he wraps Himalay in a ringpost figure four. After the referee admonishes Krishna to break the hold, Krishna mounts the top rope and springs off with a splash. Unfortunately, Himalay raises his knees, striking Krishna in his stomach.] [Seconds later, Himalay weakly limps to the turnbuckle. He climbs and then delivers The Liberty press, a shooting star press. He covers Krishna and gets the three count.] HARDEE: The winner of the match ... JAY HIMALAY! [A weary Himalay finds the energy to keep trash-talking as he has his hand raised in victory.] [The ring is cleared for the next match.] ***************************************************************************************************** RVRL TITLE TOURNAMENT 1st Round Match: JAKE EVANS vs. 'RABID' ROB COLE ***************************************************************************************************** HARDEE: This contest is one fall with a thirty-minute time limit in the first round of the RVRL Heavyweight title tournament! Now entering the ring... [The opening drumbeats of "The Sickness" by Disturbed begin to play as the crowd begins to get restless.] HARDEE: ... from Red Mountain, North Carolina, weighing in at 252 pounds... [Loud guitar strums as the music gets a harder edge to it and we hear the voice again...] HARDEE: Here is JAKE EVANS! [The music is full on and rocking hard as the fans begins booing loudly. Through the curtain steps Jake Evans. Jake is wearing basic black tights and boots. He stands for a second and wrings his hands together in front of him as he takes in the crowd's reaction. Finally, he slowly begins to walk toward the ring with a slight smirk on his face. He slides into the ring.] HARDEE: And his opponent ... [The arena loudspeakers blare with the opening strain of "Angry Chair" by Alice in Chains.] HARDEE: From Hudson Valley, New York, weighing in at 282 pounds... here is 'RABID' ROB COLE! [The curtain parts to reveal the monster known as Rob Cole! The psychopath stalks to the ring, eyes gazing wolfishly at the ring area as he licks his lips hungrily. He finally climbs the steps, and steps between the ring ropes as he gazes out at the audience with a look of absolutely insane glee in his eyes... before being rushed by Evans, who sends a knee smash into Cole's side, doubling him over.] [After doubling Cole over, Evans drives an elbow into the native New Yorker's spine, sending him crashing to the mat. He then works Cole over with a series of stomps and kicks. A stalled vertical suplex by Evans leaves Cole in agony as the veteran Evans continues working him over with a series of punches. Evans picks Cole up with plans to deliver a belly-to-back suplex, but Cole then shoots a hard back elbow smash to Evans' face. Evans holds on still tries to take Cole over, but Cole then delivers yet another hard back elbow, forcing Evans to relinquish the hold.] [As Evans stumbles about trying to recover from the vicious back elbows, Cole charges him, leveling him with a clothesline. Afterwards, he then takes Evans back down with a vertical suplex of his own. Cole attempts a quick cover, but only gets a one count before Evans powers out.] [Cole then Evans up from the mat and drops him down across his knee with a front atomic drop. Evans holds his groin and plummets to the mat. Cole hits the ropes and prepares to deliver a leg drop, but misses completely as Evans quickly rolls out of the ring. Evans then drags Cole to the outside, pummeling him with a series of hard fists. Cole retaliates and begins getting the best of Evans, who then delivers a quick Asian spike to Cole's throat. Cole clutches at his throat as Evans rolls into the ring to break the count and then rolls back out.] [As Cole tries to recover, Evans grabs a steel chair from a disgruntled fan...] WHACK! [Cole delivers a resounding chair shot to Cole's back.] WHACK! [Another one for good measure, and Cole drops to the arena floor.] [An astute veteran, Evans rolls back into the ring and back out again, once again breaking the count. Cole leans upon the ring stairs as Evans charges at him. As Evans approaches, Cole quickly drops down, delivers a drop toe hold, and sends Evans careening into the ring stairs.] [Cole rises and seems incensed, his eyes bulging with rage... and delight? As Evans staggers to his feet, Cole runs up the ring stairs, leaps, and drops Evans with a flying bulldog, which gets the first major pop from the fans. Evans falls to the floor. Cole picks him up and whips him back into the ring.] [Inside the ring, Cole nails Evans with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker, and immediately goes for a cover. He manages to get a near fall, but Evans still has some fight in him. Cole intends to take the fight out of Evans by placing him in a cobra clutch. He wraps Evans in the cobra clutch for a moment, prompting the referee to check in on Evans. Evans's arm drops twice, but on the third fall, he finds some resilience. Cole wraps on the cobra clutch tighter. As Evans's faces grows beet red, he lunges forward and falls to the mat. Evans's fall forces Cole to fall face forward into the top turnbuckle.] [Both men lie prone on the mat for a few moments before Cole rises to his feet. As Evans tries to rise, Cole stumbles towards him, only to receive a fist to the groin for his troubles. Evans scrambles to his feet and then takes Cole down with a brutal Russian leg sweep. He then follows with a Death Valley driver that draws a pop from the crowd. He goes for the pin and gets the three count!] [That is, until the referee takes note that Evans has feet firmly planted on the bottom rope for leverage.] [Evans gets in the referee's face. The referee backs away sheepishly. As Evans argues with the referee, Cole slowly pulls himself up. Sensing that Cole is getting a second wind, Evans cinches him in for a piledriver, but he quickly finds himself dumped to the canvas with a big back body drop. This does not seem to affect Evans much, but the subsequent flying shoulder block packs a wallop! Cole goes for the cover, but only gets a two count for his efforts.] [Cole staggers over to Evans and goes to pick him up. Evans quickly drops down with a chinbuster. Cole clutches his mouth and staggers away. Evans grabs him and prepares to set him up for a brainbuster. However, Cole blocks. Evans tries again and actually hoists Cole into the air. However, Cole manages to slip out behind Evans. Evans turns and immediately meets a swift boot to the midsection. Seconds later, Evans finds himself a victim of a lightning fast tiger driver. Cole immediately goes for the pin and gets the three count!] [The fans let loose with a mixed reaction as Hardee enters the ring and the referee raises Cole's hand.] HARDEE: The winner of the match ... 'RABID' ROB COLE! [Cole glares at his fallen opponent before making a quick exit. The ring is cleared.] [Casey Blain enters the ring to another pop from the crowd.] BLAIN: What a hell of a first round, eh, fans? [The fans begin chanting.] "R-V-R-L!" "R-V-R-L!" BLAIN: The second round will see Colt Daniels... [The crowd shows it appreciation for the youngster.] BLAIN: Taking on Aries Priest. [A round of boos fills the arena.] BLAIN: We'll also see Jay Himalay ... [Another round of boos.] BLAIN: Taking on 'Rabid' Rob Cole! [The fans continue booing.] BLAIN: But before we go on, I want to point some important people in attendance today. Just hours before our opening event, RVRL signed two more superstars to its roster. Please show your support for a long-time veteran of the tag team wars.. RICK RUSSELL! [Russell, dressed casually in a t-shirt and jeans, stands up in the rafters to a warm reception from the crowd.] BLAIN: We also have the son of the legendary Chet Watkins on our roster. This young man has been tearing up the independent circuit and has finally found himself here in RVRL. Give it up for 'DUST DEVIL' WATKINS! [The young Arizona native barely acknowledges the crowd. Instead, he spits a wad of tobacco into a cup and slinks back into his seat.] BLAIN: Now let's get back to the action! [The fans pop again as Blain exits the ring. Roy Hardee enters.] ***************************************************************************************************** RVRL TITLE TOURNAMENT 2nd Round Match: ARIES PRIEST w/Darius Walker vs. COLT DANIELS ***************************************************************************************************** HARDEE: This contest is one fall with a thirty-minute time limit in the second round of the RVRL Heavyweight title tournament! [The fans pop.] HARDEE: Now entering the ring... [The crowd lets loose with a series of jeers as Darius Walker, the manager of Aries Priest, enters the arena.] HARDEE: Led to the ring by his manager, Darius Walker, from St. Louis, Missouri, weighing in at 315 pounds ... here is ARIES PRIEST! [The massive former mixed-martial arts champions enters the arena to a chorus of boos. He and Walker work their way towards ringside, ignoring or outright slapping away the outstretched hands of the fans. They enter the ring to a resounding chorus of jeers.] HARDEE: And his opponent... [The opening cords of the legend Johnny Cash's version of "Hurt" begins to play over the PA system. The fans begin cheering again.] HARDEE: From Austin, Texas, weighing in at 220 pounds... here is COLT DANIELS! [Colt Daniels emerges from the back in his burnt orange wrestling trunks with the white Texas longhorn displayed proud over the backside. On his head sits a Texas Longhorns baseball cap turned backwards. The fans give the young superstar a warm welcome, Colt returns the appreciation slapping every fan's hand he can on the way to the ring.] [As he finally reaches the ring, he taps the side of the ring with his knuckles and tosses his hat to the side launching himself into a head first dive under the ropes. He hops to his feet raises his arms ready to start this match!] [The bell sounds.] [Priest grins as he stalks the younger, smaller Daniels. Daniels cautiously circles Priest before the two tie-up. Daniels quickly drops Priest with a series of armdrag takedowns before locking him into an armlock. Daniels holds onto the armlock until Priest gets to his feet. Priest powers his way out of the armlock and hip tosses Daniels to the mat.] [As Daniels attempts to rise, Priest charges him with a sidekick to the head, dazing Daniels. Priest then picks up Daniels, whips him into the ropes, then charges him with a lariat. Daniels almost does a backflip from the impact, drawing a heel pop from the crowd. Priest drops Daniels backdown with a reverse neckbreaker and goes for the pin, getting a solid two count before Daniels kicks out.] [As Daniels struggles to stand, Priest drives an elbow into the small of his back. Daniels drops to all fours. However, as Priest looms over him, Daniels executes a quick amateur double leg takedown. With Priest down on his back, Daniels quickly ties him up into a Texas cloverleaf submission hold.] [Daniels holds onto the Texas cloverleaf for a few moments. However, Priest does not submit. He begins clawing towards the bottom rope, which he finally grabs. The referee forces Daniels to break the hold. Afterwards, Daniels picks up Priest and takes him down with a German suplex. He goes for the cover but only gets a two count before Priest powers out.] [Daniels picks up Priest and attempts a vertical suplex. However, Priest blocks it and then reverses it. Priest rises, but holds his back in pain as Daniels lies flattened on the mat. Priest storms towards Daniels and stomps on his ankle. As Daniels clutches his ankle in pain, Priest then quickly executes an anklelock submission hold.] [Daniels howls in pain as Priest wrinches the anklelock. Daniels seems on the verge of tapping out but he finally touches the bottom rope. However, with the referee's attention diverted elsewhere, Walker, Priest's manager, knocks his hand from the ropes, drawing boos from the crowd. Priest drags Daniels out to center ring and wrenches on his ankle more.] [Daniels finally manages to twist around. He places a firm boot to Priest's chest. Priest breaks the hold but charges back towards him. Daniels places another boot to Priest's chest. He then lowers his kicks and nails Priest in the knee. Priest falls, clutching his knee. Both men lay flat on the canvas.] [Simultaneously, both men use the ring ropes to pull themselves to their feet. Daniels manages the fortitude to charge Priest with a Thesz press. He begins wailing on Priest with a series of punches. Afterwards, he executes a stunning springboard moonsault and covers Priest for a near fall.] [Still favoring his ankle, Daniels slowly climbs to the top turnbuckle. He prepares to spring off from the top turnbuckle when Walker once again makes his presence felt. He grabs Daniels' ankle, causing him to fall groin first atop the top turnbuckle. The fans boo loudly.] [Priest takes advantage of the situation. He quickly executes a brutal belly-to-back superplex, planting Daniels into the mat. He goes the pin and gets a near fall before Daniels slips out the back door.] [Priest looms atop Daniels and then applies a Tongan death grip. Daniels writhes in pain but is unable to break the hold. After a few moments in the grip, Daniels rises to his feet but finds himself taken back down by a brutal sidewalk slam. Priest goes for a pin and gets another near fall.] [Priest picks Daniels up and plants him on the top turnbuckle. He tries to climb to the top rope to execute a superplex, but Daniels decks him with a solid right hand. Priest manages to land on his feet, but he finds himself quickly the victim of a tornado DDT!] [Both men lay flat on the mat. Daniels claws his way towards the turnbuckles. As Priest slowly pulls himself up, Daniels makes his way towards the top turnbuckle. Priest distracts the referee as Walker moves into position.] [As Daniels climbs the top rope, Walker begins climbing the stairs to knock him off. However, a large clamor from the crowd catches Daniels attention as Rick Russell bounds over the guardrail. He pulls Walker from the stairs and knocks him cold with a solid right hand. Seeing his manager in trouble, Priest grabs a fistful of Russell's hair and pulls him up to the apron. The referee attempts to intervene. Priest shoves him out of the way. Russell cold-cocks Priest, who falls to the mat. Daniels springs off the top turnbuckle, landing atop Priest with a big body splash. He goes for the cover and picks up the win!] HARDEE: The winner of this match and the first finalist for the RVRL Heavyweight title... COLT DANIELS! [Daniels hobbles on his leg before shooting a glance of acknowledgment at Rick Russell. Russell aids the young Texan on his way back towards the locker room. As Priest rises, he and Walker angrily storm away.] [The ring is cleared for the next match.] ***************************************************************************************************** RVRL TITLE TOURNAMENT 2nd Round Match: 'Rabid' Rob Cole vs. JAY HIMALAY ***************************************************************************************************** HARDEE: This contest is one fall with a thirty-minute time limit in the second round of the RVRL Heavyweight title tournament!
