RFHW: Clash Of The Champions

5 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rick Taylor vs. Hayabusa (Barbwire Thumbtack match)for the Extreme title STYLES: Fans, let's go to the ring for one of the most anticipated matches here tonight, as Hayabusa challenges Rick Taylor for the Extreme Championship! RA: Ladies and gentlemen, this is a BARBED WIRE THUMBTACK DEATHMATCH for the RFHW Extreme Championship!! Introducing first, ("Fighting into the Dream" plays) weighing 230 lbs, managed by Victor Quiones, representing the W*ING Alliance, the challenger, HAYABUSA!!!!! His opponent, ("Thorn Within" plays) weighing 256 lbs, managed by Cassandra, the RFHW Extreme Champion, the "DEATH DEALER" RICK TAYLOR!!!!! STYLES: Taylor gets into the ring, and he approaches his opponent, the two men are just staring each other down! Both have immense respect for each other, and thaty've fought over this very title before; Taylor won that match, can he do so again tonight, Mike? TENAY: Well, Joey, both of these men are talented hardcore wrestlers and-- they lock up in the center of the ring! Hayabusa backs Taylor up into the barbed wire! Hayabusa backs up as Taylor tries to get out of it! WAIT! Hayabusa charges Taylor, cross body, BOTH MEN TUMBLE TO THE OUTSIDE AND INTO THOSE THUMBTACKS!! Hayabusa first on his feet, he has Taylor by the hair, brings him up to his feet, and Hayabusa slams his face into the guardrail! Taylor tumbles to the concrete, and Hayabusa picks him up again, and RAKES HIS FACE ACROSS THE BARBED WIRE!! But Taylor counters with an elbow to the gut of Hayabusa, and another, and another, finally he gets his face freed. Taylor pushes Hayabusa into the guardrail, and climbs back into the ring. What's he thinking? STYLES: Taylor revs up and he charges, leaps off the edge of the ring, FLYING SHOULDERTACKLE-- OH MY GOD!!!! He almost snapped Hayabusa's SPINE IN THE PROCCESS!! Hayabusa tumbles face first to the floor, his face buried in those thumbtacks! Taylor stopping for a breather, and he's clutching his knee! He's injured it before, and he may have agrivated it again! But he's making his way over to Hayabusa, and he picks him up, sets him up, OH MY GOD!!! POWERBOMB into the thumbtacks! Taylor hobbles over to the timekeeper's table, and he's got the ringbell! He makes his way over to Hayabusa again, he's holding it high in the air, BRINGS IT CRASHING DOWN ON HIS HEAD!! Respect does not keep these two from pulling out all the stops! Taylor turns around and HEY! He just throws that bell at Victor Quiones! What was he doing to deserve that??? And he almost connected with it! But Taylor grabs Hayabusa by the head and throws him back into the ring! He doesn't follow though as he finds a chair --the one that ring announcer Rudy Rudolph was sitting in, by the way-- and he follows Hayabusa in. TENAY: Hayabusa is slowly making it to his feet, and Taylor has that chair up in the air, in his signature sideways fashion, and he drives it down across the shoulders of Hayabusa!! Hayabusa on his back, and Taylor lays that chair across his face and climbs to the top turnbuckle. He's up there, FLYING ELBOWDROP!!! He could have him beat there, but no, he's going up again, ANOTHER elbowdrop! Now, the cover, 1.......2......KICKOUT!!!! Taylor can't believe it! He picks him up, throws him into the barbed wire-entwined ropes, GORILLA PRESS!! ANd, what's he thinking-- OH NO!!!! He drops him THROAT-FIRST ON THE BARBED WIRE!!!! Hayabusa is out of it! Taylor goes back up to the top, MOONSAULT!!!! Taylor slides to the outside and he's going after something, he's making his way over to the timekeeper's table again, and this time he takes, THE TABLE!!!! He closes the legs, and he slides it under the barbed wire ropes, and he follows. STYLES: Hayabusa has started to come around again, Taylor picks up that chair again and nails him with it! Hayabusa goes back down! Taylor is now setting up that table, and he looks around at the crowd and he's slicing his thumb across his throat! He's going for the super spinebuster! No one has EVER kicked out of that! He picks up Hayabusa and props him up on the top turnbuckle, he goes to the outside and follows up to the top, he's got those arms around Hayabusa's midsection, and he leaps!!! BUT WAIT!!!! Hayabusa twists in MIDAIR!!!! He turned it into a belly-to-belly!!! And it's Taylor that goes through the table!!! Hayabusa rolls over, and Taylor's lying amid those shards of what's left of the table! WHAT A REVERSAL!!! Hayabusa struggles to his feet, and he makes his way over to Taylor and he grabs Taylor's hair, picks him up, GOURDBUSTER!!! Hayabusa goes up to the top, MOONSAULT!!! Cover, 1......2......KICKOUT! Hayabusa is in SHOCK! He lays in a few boots to the head before going back up to the top, FALCON ARROW!!! Cover, 1......2......KICKOUT!!!!! Hayabusa canNOT believ it!!! Neither can I!!!!! Quiones throws in a chair, Hayabusa climbs the ropes once again, chair in hand, ATOMIC ARABIAN FACEBUSTER!!!! Cover, 1........2.......3!!! WE'VE GOT A NEW CHAMPION!!!!! RA: Your winner, and NEW Extreme Champion, HAYABUSA!!!!! ("Fighting into the Dream" plays) TENAY: Joey, Hayabusa can be proud! Taylor beat him once before, but Hayabusa rose above that, and he came back in this rematch to become our NEW Extreme Champion! But Taylor has nothing to be ashamed of! In the time he held that belt, he was a fighting champion, and he, just like Terry Funk, can say that he was the FIRST to wear that belt! There will be many others, but NEVER another FIRST! Hayabusa is celebrating and Taylor hobbles over to him, and he turns him around-- we may have another showdown right here! They stare at each other a moment, and Taylor extends his hand. Hayabusa takes it they shake hands! Taylor congratulating his opponent. Now THAT'S RESPECT! Jerry Flynn vs. Disco Inferno This match began with a literal BANG, as Disco Inferno was heading to ringside. All of a sudden, Bobby Pherris bursted from the locker room area, with his signature guitar in hand. After busting the guitar across the back of Disco's head, Pherris left, amidst boos and chants of "BURGER KING!! BURGER KING!!". Flynn took advantage of the situation, and headed to the outside. After jaw-jacking with several fans along the way, and issuing several "SHADDUP's" into the camera, Flynn removed the Monday Nitro T-shirt he was wearing, and promptly began choking the life out of poor Disco. Flynn still managed to botch things up, however, as he threw Disco into the ring, and began to jaw-jack again with the fans. This proved to be the opportunity if a lifetime for Disco, as he then sent a surprised Jerry Flynn over the top rope with a textbook dropkick. He followed Flynn to the outside, and began sending Flynn's head into the guardrail. With Flynn apparently unconcious, Disco began to dance atop his battered body. Disco then rolled Flynn into the ring, and irish-whipped Flynn into the corner. Flynn reversed the move, and began sending karate kicks into Disco's mid-section. Muay Thai kicks then followed, as Disco then collapsed on the canvas in a heap. After issuing another "SHADDUP!!!" into the camera, Flynn began to really lose compossure. Disco caught Flynn with an eye-gouge, and then grabbed his legs out from under him. Disco then strapped on his patented leg lock, after several minutes of pondering and planning. After placing his leg-lock on Flynn,which is similar to a standing figure-four, Flynn, amidst screams and yells of pain, gave up the match. Winner:Disco Inferno w/ The Disco Leg-Lock Joel Gutman vs. The Sandman This match was incredibly bloody, and quite sadistic. The Sandman showed quite alot of composure in the ring, as he really took it to Gutman. Gutman proved to be one of the fiercest wrestlers alive, as he repeatedly pounded Sandman into the mat with brass knuckles. The match really turned ugly when Rick Taylor headed to ringside and began to distract Gutman. Gutman had the match one at one point, as he hit a HUGE T-bone suplex on the outside of the ring, THROUGH the announcer's table. He rolled The Sandman into the ring and began to head in after him. Before he could climb onto the apron, however, Gutman was attacked by Taylor, who yanked him off the apron and sent him into sailing into the crowd, after lifting him from the apron and up into a gorilla press slam. The ref was attending to a bloody Sandman, and did not see this horrendous act. Gutman eventually arose from the crowd, and made his way back into the ring. However, Sandman had his cane ready and waiting, and began to whack Gutman unmercifully with it. After shattering the cane over his Gutman's head, he then climbed to the top rope. He came down on Gutman with his signature Atomic Legdrop, and then covered Gutman for the 1....2....3!!!! After the match Gutman ran ti the outside in a fit of rage, and began to tear through everything in an attempt to find his assailant. He headed to the office of the president, and after lifting him to his feet, was convinced that this loss would't count against his record. Winner:The Sandman w/ Atomic Legdrop "Kamikaze" Jack Atari vs. Sabu Jack Atari proved to everyone in attendence that he was truly ready for this contest. Sabu brought everything he had to the ring with him, but it just didn't seem to be enough. Jack Atari began to take control of the match, as he began to focus on Sabu's neck. He sent Sabu's head slamming into the mat, with several vicious piledrivers, in an attempt to finish him off. Sabu kicked out of several pin attempts, and then grabbed a chair from the announcer's table. He then whipped Atari into the corner and after setting up the chair next to the groggy Atari, backed into the opposite corner and sailed high into the air with his patented Air Sabu. Atari was then sent reeling over the top rope, and out onto the concrete with a chair to his face. Sabu followed Atari to the outside, and began to pound on Atari's back with closed fists. After whipping him back into the ring, he placed a stunned Atari on the tope rope. Sabu climbed th outside of the turnbuckle and then positioned his legs around the back of Atari's neck. After attempting a turn-around avalanche frankensteiner, Sabu, and the rest of the onlooking crowd, was stunned, as Atari reversed the attempt, and turned it into the Godzilla Towerbomb!!!! He then sprawled across Sabu and got the 1...2...3!!! Mr.Pogo vs. Doug Baxter (TV title match) Sadly this match never got started, as Taylor attacked Baxter from behind. Big Daddy Cool had his knees whacked with a chair, and was sprawled out on the concrete, as Taylor and Baxter went at it. Baxter got the better of Taylor in this brawl, and began to drill him with rights and lefts. All of a sudden, Joel Gutman exploded from the back and began to beat on Taylor as well. Gutman and Baxter set Taylor up for a double powerbomb on the concrete floor, and then HIT, sending Taylor into unconciousness. Baxter then helped an injured Big Daddy Cool to his feet, as Gutman continued to stomp on Taylor's unconcious body. This match was ruled as a count-out, in favor of Mr. Pogo. Baxter wasn't happy with the call, but took joy in the fact that he had thwarted his long time foe yet again!!! Heavy Metal vs. The Lone Gunmen Both teams seemed to really take it up a notch, as neither team really showed signs of quitting. Half-way through the match, The Great Sasuke and Yokihiro Kanemura could be seen taking a ring-side seat to the action. However, they never interfered. Slasher hit Oswald with a jumping DDT, and began to put the boots to him, as Whitman and Thrasher brawled on the outside of the ring. Whitman saw his partner in trouble and sent a kick into Thrasher's groin, as he headed back into the ring and began to pull Slasher off of him. He sent Slasher into the corner, and followed him in with a BIG splash, which shook the ring with it's might. Thrasher, having recovered from the low blow, jumped into the ring and lifted Whitman high into the air with a big over the top belly-to-belly. Oswald arose to his feet and lifted Thrasher into the bodyslam position. However, Slasher dropkicked Thrasher onto a stunned Oswald. The cover was made, but was only as good as a two count. Heavy Metal lifted Oswald onto the top rope and signaled to the crowd. Thrasher began to slap Oswald in the face, as he sat prone on the top rope. Slasher climbed the top rope, and actually stunned the crowd with some flying head scissors onto the mat. Oswald then appeared out, and was covered.....1....2....NO!!!! A groggy Whiman kicked him out, but was knocked over the top rope to the outside, when Thrasher sent a huge lunging kick to the back of his head!!!! The Team of Heavy Metal stood Oswald up, and then hit The Moshpit!!! Thrasher covered Oswald for the 1...2...3!!!! New Tag champs were crowned!! After Heavy Metal left the ring,with their titles in the air, Sasuke and Kanemura lept the ring barrier, and attacked the new champs from behind!!! After whacking them several times with their chairs, Sasuke and Kanemura ran back to the lockerroom area. However, what was remarkable was the fact that they never even touched the Gunmen!!! Ghost vs. Taz (World Title Match) This was a remarkable match!!! Taz started things off with an arm drag take-down. He worked over Ghost's arm, which really didn't seem to work all that well. Ghost kneed Taz in the face, which busted him open, and began to then pound away on his back. Ghost then followed him up with a DDT, which busted Taz wide open!!! Ghost then taunted the crowd, and began to go back to work on Taz's back. At one point, Ghost appeared to have the match one, as he drilled Taz with a big Northern Lights Suplex!!! Taz then was covered, but kicked out at the last second!! Ghost then stood Taz up and had him set up for a powerbomb, with the ropes near his back. All of a sudden, Taz elevated Ghost over the top rope with a HUGE back-drop, sending Ghost crashing through the Japanese commentator's table. Taz followed Ghost to the outside, and tossed Ghost back into the ring. Taz then applied the Tazmission!!!! Ghost was nearly out on his feet!!! However, Ghost gained enough composure and strength to run up the turnbuckle, placing Taz in a pinning predicament. Ghost got the 1...2...3.... But Taz wouldn't let the Tazmission go!!! He held onto the choke hold, until Ghost stopped flailing around on the mat, and slumped into unconciousness!!! He arose to his feet and raised his arms in the air, as he stood over the downed Ghost!! Suddenly, a figure appeared from beneath the ring apron, and climbed into the ring!!! Fans of the WCW were shocked, as they could all see that it was in fact......Sting!!!! In Hardcore Wrestling?????????? But it was in fact Sting, and he approached Taz with a look of hate on his face. The two then stood eye to eye, and continued to stare viciously at one another. Just when each man appeared to be about to throw the first punch, Sting then raised Taz's arms in the air!!!!!!!! Taz and Sting then hugged briefly, showing mutual friendship!!! <The clash then ended amongst scenes of Sting and Taz heading back ot the locker room, and Ghost laying unconcious in the center of the ring. Marilyn Mason could be seen trying to awaken Ghost, however, Ghost seemed to be in a deep sleep.>
