http://www.geocities.com/prezburton/ I'll be getting a domain sometime this week. BWO Frequently Asked Questions v1.0 Q: What's an 'e-fed'? A: An e-fed is a Roleplay based community, except rather than the normal boring Sci-Fi or Fantasy settings we use Wrestling as a base setting. You take up the personality of a fake Wrestler and then you play there part, doing pretty much everything you'd do as a real life Wrestler. Except rather than doing your stuff on TV you do it with words writing up RPs, Interviews,
Flashes, Strategies etc. Once a week we arrange a show (a la Smackdown, Raw,
Nitro etc) where you get a match against another 'e-Wrestler', then the staff rate you on the quality and consistency of your RPs which decides whether you win or lose. Your Wrestlers unique gimmick changes the way your
RP as does the angles (storylines) your currently involved in, eventually you build up to winning a title which is the peak of e-fedding success.
Q: What's a Roleplay/Flash/Strategy? A: An RP is like a short story, you describe the actions and surroundings of a certain character (in this case a Wrestler) in 3rd person or 1st person,
RPs are the main factor in e-Wrestling, the better your RPs the better you'll do. Flashes (not to be confused with flashing) are like RPs except they're based in the show. Like when Stone Cold got run over or when
Christian dressed up in a Chicken suit to lose weight, Flashes aren't as important as RPs but they add alot to a show. Strategies can include numerous things, you send them to the staff before the show and they include things like run ins, special entrances, things you want to say before a match or special tactics you want to use in a match. For example if your a high flying Wrestler (Hardyz, Lita, Kaentai) fighting a Power Wrestler
(Kane, Undertaker, Big Show) you might chose to run around burning up their energy then making a few quick moves, it's all up to you.
Q: How do you write the shows? A: BWO Shows are written by our 'card writers' they give a blow by blow description of each match with BWOs commentators adding their thoughts along the way.
Q: What shows does BWO have? A: We have a weekly show (BWO Damnation) and once every 2 months we have a
Super Card which is like a PPV.
Q: So basically BWO is like WWF or WCW in it's setup? A: Aha, not quite. BWO is a regional federation based in the UK. Whereas WWF or WCW is a multi million pound corporation BWO is just a small company. Our shows are held in far smaller venues (perhaps 2,000 people in each one), no flashy entrances with thousands of pounds worth of pyrotechnics, alot like
ECW when it first formed. Hell most of our shows aren't even televised, except for the odd Super Card which is televised on Channel 4.
Q: ???Did I just hear you talking about a few thousand people watching the shows and TV contracts, I though this was all fake???
A: :-) Yes you did, most of the time we try to stay in character, the more realistic the better.
Q: I've seen other e-feds offering to make me Flash intros and Poser pictures, what can you give me?
A: No Flash intros, Poser pics or other needless rubbish. BWO is what the call an 'Old School' e-fed, for us all that matters is enjoying the sport by
RPing and making shows. It's all a matter of taste whether you prefer the
BWO style of e-Wrestling or the more corporate IWO style.
Q: Why 'British' Wrestling Organisation? A: Because we're based in Britain (using Britain in the traditional sense of the word meaning everyone in the UK England, Northern Ireland, Scotland and
Wales) of course, it doesn't meen we only allow Brits in though everyone is welcome. But for RP purposes all our events are hosted in the UK and our
Titles are more UK orientated.
Q: What Titles do you have? A: Hardcore, British Title, Northern Title, Southern Title and the 4 Nations
Title. As of yet we don't have a Tag Team division due to the lack of Tag
Teams around, perhaps if demand grows we'll create one.
Q: Are you message board based or e-mail based? A: Message board based, it's alot easier for most people to access that way. And that's that, the end of the BWO F.A.Q.
