Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Stone Cold Wrestling opened 22 October 1998 as, in its own founder's words, “a brand new efed based out of San Jose, California,” accepting both real and invented wrestlers under a roleplaying format.

It planned four championships — a World Heavyweight Title, a World Extreme Title reserved for gimmick matches, World Tag Team Titles, and a World Cyberweight Title for wrestlers 230 pounds and under — defended across a weekly Tuesday Terror card, a Saturday Night's Main Event supercard every other week, and a monthly pay-per-view. A handler could control up to five wrestlers.

The same founding post doubled as a staff call, seeking writers for a federation magazine, match writers, and a vice president.

No title was ever decided, and no card or wrestler is attested beyond this founding announcement; a same-day cross-promotion post repeats only the same name and address without adding to the record.
