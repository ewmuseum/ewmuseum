Stone Cold Wrestling (new e-fed looking for talent and staff)

22 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone. I am the owner of Stone Cold Wrestling, a brand new efed based out of San Jose, California. We are currently looking for talent to join the ranks of the SCW. Title's have yet to be decided and we accept both real and made up wrestlers. This fed is a roleplaying fed. So drop on by and feel free to fill out an application and we can get the wrestling underway. There are 4 titles in the SCW. World Heavyweight Title. World Extreme Title (only gimmick matches allowed). World Tag Team Titles. World Cyberweight Title (230 lbs and under). There will one card a week, Tuesday Terror, and every other Saturday, there will be a supercard called "Saturday Night's Main Event". On the last day of every month, there will be a pay per view card. You are allowed to control up to 5 wrestlers. For people interested in becoming part of the staff, we currently are looking for writers for our federation magazine and for matches, and we are also looking for a vice president. Thanks http://members.aol.com/scewcomish/index.html
