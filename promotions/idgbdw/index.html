Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Insane Deranged Gruesome Badass Death Wrestling introduced itself in September 1997 with a wrestler-creation application rather than a founding statement, inviting entrants to build up to five singles wrestlers and two tag teams, each one required to be invented rather than a real name.

Its application asked for a wrestler's favorite weapon, whether he used “chairs, barb wire, knifes,ect,” a finishing maneuver and its setup, a taunt, and a gimmick — the whole of the promotion's own self-presentation is this template, not a founding manifesto or a roster.

One community reply to the post survives, taking a skeptical tone toward the pitch. No card, roster, title, or further document of the fed's own survives past its single application.

Whether anyone answered the application, or the promotion ever ran a card, isn't in the surviving record.
