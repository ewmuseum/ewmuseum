WCWF Site Review

9-14 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 4 —

[detail withheld] Got an e-mail from this guy, saying he liked the way I wrote about GFWA and would like me to do the same thing with his Fed. I was trying to pass the next hour until my doctor's appointment, or I probably wouldn't have. I was a little down already, but as I was reading through the site I suddenly had a feeling I was back in 8th grade and was the "Dungeon Master" again. I don't want to position myself as "the review guy", I just did GFWA because one of their guys asked me to join, and I was sleep crazy, plus had nothing else to do. It was more of a spur of the moment kind of thing. I did check out the Fed and must say it kicks a lot of ass. They've got the most unique site design I've ever seen for an e-Fed ... it looks like the opening screen of a C64 wrestling league simulation game. I think the logo's pretty cool, pure Atari. Normally I don't like it when a Fed is laid out so it looks like it's run by simulation, but if you think about it they may have stumbled onto something here. Somebody should write a .cgi e-Fed simulator, and have it all automated, with the same kind of design as their site. (If this has been done already, somebody let me know). I don't like the "color-coded" format for the cards, but it seems pretty popular. My own preference is screenplay format, as if you're writing an actual script, with extra notation to clearly mark where each match begins and ends so the guys can get the results quick, then read the rest of the card at their own pace. The rules page was also pretty thin, like most Feds' are. The roleplay help page was a nice idea, but didn't give all that much help. The sheer thoroughness of their wrestler utilities was shocking. They've got every one set up to send each message exactly the way they want it to, all standardized. And the simplicity of each page, going far beyond just not using java, they don't even use tables. It's just the simplest, most efficient way every time. No pics on the roster pages and all are done exactly the same way. All the roster pages are even checked for spelling and grammar, scrutinized it seems. I didn't find one mistake on any of the pages I saw. Averaging six or seven RP posts/day, and all the ones I saw were pretty damn long. (I don't like long roleplays myself, think they should be under three pages absolute maximum; but most of the people reading this review probably do like them long.) The belts page here has more actual photos of belts than I've ever seen. They were all different from what I'd already seen. They also had a couple of graphics up, probably original ones. Overall, this looks like a well-managed Fed, but I smell a Comp Sci or Engineering major behind it, maybe an original Apple IIe owner. Everything is just so neat/orderly/efficient, it's insane. Like it may be the Stepford Wives of e-wrestling. My own Feds never looked nearly as good as this, but I also like to think they had some personality. The retro page design is really pretty cool, something I would get a kick out of, but there needs to be something more behind it. You've got to really have a lot of writing talant to pull this kind of thing off, to give the page some character with your words. Your members I'm sure appreciate the efficiency, but you've got to have a little bit of a marketing edge going on your front page -- edgy, hip, fab, etc. I recommend adding a quote of the week like GFWA, make it cool, and I'd also add a couple of features. Maybe a bi-monthly column, a news page that would give some scenery to the Fed and its local area. Realize that--I know you're hip, you know you are, but you've got to reassure people sometimes. ---- Since this is the second Site Review I've posted, I'd like to remind everybody that fwhq and e-zone do Fed Reviews. They go into a lot more detail than I could, and if you're deciding on a Fed to join you really need to talk to the Prez and some of the other members yourself. [name withheld], RWF Press Agent http://www.angelfire.com/mt/RWF
