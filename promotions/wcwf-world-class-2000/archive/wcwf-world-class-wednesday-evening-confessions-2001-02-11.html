WCWF (World Class, 2000) — Card

WCWF presents Wednesday Evening Confessions

11 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[We pull in on EYVAL whipping NUKE into the ropes and catching him coming off with a trashcan to the face. The freak himself lifts the trashcan over his head and buckles the metal can on the back of Nuke. Eyval snatches a singapore cane off the mat and lays down a brutal beating on Nuke before taking to the ropes with a steel chair then leaps into Nuke with a steel chair splash. Finally Eyval mounts the ropes and dives at Nuke, spearing him back into the steel chair on the mat with the... JAWS OF EYVAL! Eyval makes the pin and limps to the back as the Confessions pyros go off and the white lights circulate over the crowd. 'Nothing else matters' by Mettalica plays and we pull into the desk of Big Daddy Cornwallis and Lewis Faharenski. ] Big Daddy: We've got a card and a half here tonight, world title on the line, TVint title on the line, tag action, hardcore action. Lewis: Not to mention the US title about to come back onto circulation thanks to matches tonight, and... [Lewis is interrupted by 'Hair of the Dog' by Nazereth playing and DICK 'THE BRUISER' HART, the company owner, comes down the ramp with his microphone, to the jeers and boos of the crowd.] Dick: Lewis, Cornwallis, shut up a second and listen to the man that puts food in your babies mouths. Now, tonight, we've got another little knight main event, Dane Rennier and his trainer Raymond Chandler. Another pair of little KoA idiots playing at throwing MY belts about, well I ain't putting up with it, I'm making a change, the WCWF main event is a three way dance. [The crowd's boos intesify. ] Lewis: Whatever he's thinking Dick will have to go a way to rock Dane and Raymond from this confrontation that's been building the last few weeks. Dick: Well, in that third corner, the newest member of the Corporation, you know him as Goliath, he is still the giant, but he is JESSE HELMS! [From the curtains looms the seven foot plus form of Jesse Helms, the crowd boo their one time hero, seeing him with Dick and understanding the turn has been made. They stand there a moment, then make their ways to the backstage area. ] Big Daddy: My god, how does Hart do that? He's lured over Duke, Shawn, now Jesse Helms, where will it stop, every one of the WCWF's finest hearts are making the turn to the corporation. Lewis: I don't know Cornwallis, but I think we'll find out. Right now, we need some things settled the right way, right here, in the ring. Micheal K, come out. Micheal K: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following match is a three way dance, the winner of which will qualify to fight for the WCWF US title on its re-establishment. First to the ring, he is a WCWF legend, one of the original charter members of the federation, the high flyer, the man with wings, he is ERIF 'THE ANGEL' GARRETT! ['Leader of men' by Nickelback plays and strobe lights and white pyros light the way to the ring as the crowd goes wild and Erif comes to the ring. He rolls into the squared circle and salutes the crowd while he waits. ] Micheal K: His first opponent, he is another WCWF legend, KoA member, former world champion, one the wrong side of managment one too many times, he is none other than THE JUDGE! ['Guilty Concience' by Eminem plays and Judge comes to the ring wearing a KoA leather jacket. He slides into the ring and nods to Garrett, also a former knight, before stretching on the ropes and waiting.] Micheal K: The final opponent, he is a newcomer to the WCWF, he is a rising star fast and firm in the federation firmament, he is none other than ALEC AZEL! [Alec sprints to the ring and dives in, attacking Judge before the bell and causing the ref to complain and start the match quickly. Erif watches as Alec rails Judge with blows to the back of the neck before coming from behind to drive the newcomer's face into the mat with a big flying face driver.] Big Daddy: Alec here not on firm ground, in with two experienced fighters, and two Knights also, Judge and Erif know each other well, and I expect they way Alec out of this match so that they can fight it out themselves. [Judge whips Alec off the ropes, he comes off into a drop toe hold from Erif that Judge catches him with a huge rising knee that stuns Erif. Judge grabs him and hauls him up into a powerbomb that's spiked with a cross body splash from Erif and the top rope. ] Lewis: Alec is just being double teamed here, the two former KoA members are running him down to decide things between them, and it doesn't look good for the youngster. [Judge whips Alec into Erif who back lifts him over the ropes. Alec clangs into the steel guard rails out side the ring with a clash of his head and back into them. Erif turns to Judge and they nod, locking up, with Judge taking advantage of his weight and power to lift Erif in a fisherman hook suplex. Judge rolls over, hauls up Garrett and catches him with a waistlock suplex that drops him to the mat hard on his head and shoulder. Judge pulls him up, but this time Erif rails him with a series of punches, then chops to the chest and neck, finally backing off into the ropes and catching Judge with a drop kick so finely placed it should be in gallery. ] Big Daddy: Erif remains one of the finest exponents of the drop kick in this or any other federation, and that one took the Judge the mat. Lewis: Hell, that one took the Judge all the way to school. Erif going to the top rope, and a huge senton bomb connecting with the gut of the Judge. [Erif gets up again and this time, facing the crowd he signals for the FALLEN ANGEL. Judge manages to get to his knees though and he hammers Erif with a low blow. ] Big Daddy: LOW BLOW! From the Judge, and his old friend can hardly believe it, I can hardly believe it, that's not Judge style. Judge just shrugging and almost smiling as he lifts Erif from the top rope in a gorilla press. Erif can't stop this now, noone can. [Erif struggles, but he's going nowhere. ] Lewis: HUNG JURY! [Judge covers Erif, the lights start to flicker and 'Brazen (Weep)' by Skunk Ananasie plays and the jumbotron lights up with the phrase 'Control the Flesh' and 'Slavery is Freedom'. ATOM runs to the ring, slides in and breaks up the pin by dragging Judge out the ring by his boot. Judge stares at Atom who grabs his shoulders and... ] Big Daddy: COMPLETE CONTROL! Judge left out on the concrete after that brutal choke headbutt of Atom, Erif still down in the ring. Wait, Alec is up, the man the other more experienced two counted out is up, and he's rolling into the ring. The cover on Erif, 1... 2... 3! The winner, and going to fight for the US champion, he is ALEC AZEL! Lewis: Wow, the newcomer really pulled it out there. Micheal K: The next match features two newcomers to the federation, the winner will earn an International television title against the champion on next weeks Confessions. First to the ring, he is new to federation, he is none other than JT SAVAGE! ['Thunderstruck' plays by AC/DC and JT comes to the ring. He slaps hands and Paradise follows him to the ring. He rolls under the ropes and stands to wait for his opponent.] Micheal K: His opponent, the hardcore hero, the madman, the sicko, he is himself, but only just, he is JASON MYERS! [The jumbotron lights up with the final scenes from 'Texas Chainsaw Massacre.' the arena fills with '666' by Esham as Jason's name fills the screen and he comes to the ring ready to fight Savage. He rolls into the ring and the ref rings for the match to start. Jason locks up with JT and manages to toss him into the ropes, catching him coming off with a knee to the ribs that flips JT onto his back with a brutal smash. Myers lines up and drops a knee across the head of JT before hauling him up and throwing him into a double arm clothesline. ] Big Daddy: These two powerful newcomers really making a mark here in the WCWF, Myers connected to Raziel Lucifer, the man that's made an impact here with the Clash, Meltdown and more. [Savage rolls away from a top rope knee drop by Jason, Myers seems to hit hard and rolls his knee. He grits his teeth and gets to his feet, but Savage fires off the ropes and with a baseball slide chops Myers' legs out from under him. JT grabs Jason's leg and drives it into the mat knee first, then drops an elbow on the joint and starts wrenching at the joint. Jason drags himself screaming to the ropes and gets his hand on the bottom rope. JT breaks away and backs off, Myers gets up and grabs JT, Savage goes for the legs again, this time he locks on an ankle lock. Myers screams, but he is laughing now between the shouts of pain. He seems to relax into the hold, then puts a hand on the ropes and breaks the hold.] Lewis: Myers seems to enjoy the pain in that hold. That sicko wants to be hurt. Big Daddy: But not as much as he wants to hurt. [Myers finally pulls himself to his feet on the ropes and laughs a little. He locks up with JT, this time when Savage goes to Jason's feet Myers just hammers on the back of neck smashing him to the floor. Myers drops an elbow on Savage, then pulls him up and with a heave he pulls JT into a standing spinbuster. ] Lewis: Myers has a capacity for pain that is frankly amazing. I don't know how he can stand after the way that JT has been working on his knee. Big Daddy: But he can stand, and he can do more than that. [Myers hauls Savage up and with a heave flips him into a brutal powerbomb. As JT comes down Jason's leg goes out and he falls with him in an intidy diving powerbomb, but its enough and he gets the three count.] Lewis: Myers going on to face the Duke next Confessions then, and a powerful start for the newcomer certainly. Micheal K: The next match is an ongoing fued between two of the WCWF's most famous faces from old days. One of them a KoA member, the other one of the federations most hated faces. First to the ring, KoA member, Cold Steel member, legend of WCWF tag-teaming, legend of wrestling. He is BOBBY 'ICEHAMMER' MCCANN! ['We're here for a good time' by Trooper plays and the arena fills with pyros and bright lights. Bobby McCann comes to the ring, followed by GREG DAVIES, carrying a crowbar over his shoulder. Bobby rolls into the ring and pulls of his KoA leather jacket. He drops the jacket to Greg and waits in the ring.] Micheal K: His opponent, the dark side, the bad man, he is the other side of the looking glass, he is what's wrong with the youth today. He is FALLEN! ['Falling away from me' by KORN plays and the lights flash and strobe as from beneath the ring comes Fallen, sliding into the ring from behind and hooking Bobby from behind into a roll up. ] Big Daddy: We're out of time, commercials are here. Lewis: Okay, we will have the tapes rolling and we'll have this for you when you come back. [Bobby kicks out and then kicks Fallen in the gut then DDTs him into the mat. The camera fades out to black and the WCWF impact logo lights up the screen. ]

— Part 2 of 2 —

[Commercials commercials commercials. Damn them all the way to hell. I hate them, I hate the moments of my life they steal and I want them back I want them all back. And thanks to this hammer I'm going to get them, ohhh yes. We pull back into the WCWF arena as Fallen perches himself on the top turnbuckle and with a look downward flings himself at the downed Bobby McCann, and it connects. ] Big Daddy: FALLEN ANGEL! Its all over, Fallen making the pin, and that's a three count right there, the winner, he is FALLEN! Lewis: That might not have contained all the excitement we could have expected, but what we did get over the commercial break was something at least as exciting as that. [We cut to a backstage area where JESSE HELMS is standing before a simple backdrop. He is being interviewed by one Dean Doug Daluka. ] DDD: Now, Jesse, we all heard that you've been included in the main event tonight for the world title, so soon after your return, how do you feel about that. Jesse: You know Dean, I feel damn fine. I feel fine that after so long being messed about and downtimed I'm back in the main event that I deserve. I feel fine that just like I beat Raymond Chandler for the International title and I beat Dane Rennier for the International title tonight I'm going to beat Raymond Chandler for the world title and I'm going to beat Dane Rennier for the world title, just like before, history is going to repeat itself. DDD: It seems you have some pretty powerful friends. Jesse: You're damn right, everyone out there should start to learn what side their bread is buttered on. The corporation is the way to go, I've signed up with them and they've helped me. Not only have they gotten me into this match for the world title, but they've given me something else Dean Doug. DDD: Well, what's that? [Jesse pulls out a piece of paper and pulls it open, then reads from it.] Jesse: In order that no cheating, maltreatment or wrong doing should be commited in tonight's main event, all members of the group known as the Knights of Anarchy will be banned from ringside. Any members found at ringside during the event will be suspended. By order of Dick 'The Bruiser' Hart. [He folds the paper and puts it away. ] Jesse: That's what. So Chandler, Dane, bring what you got, 'cause the Gladiator's coming, and he's coming hot. DDD: There you have it, strong words from the returning giant, back to you Big Daddy. [Jesse glares into the camera as it cuts away and back to the announcer's desk.] Big Daddy: I guess that's something that Raymond wasn't expecting. Micheal K: The following match will be a four corner match. The man to score the first pinfall will win a shot at the US title. First to the ring, Ex-member of the corporation and one time World champion, he is the current WCWF cruiserweight champion, he is after all, simply, SHAWN! ['Princes of the Universe' by Queen plays and Shawn comes to the ring. He slides under the ropes and bounces back and forth on them.] Micheal K: His first opponent, he is one of the New Young bloods 2K1, he is the cold one, he is none other than DEREK FROST! ['Wait and Bleed' by Slipknot plays and Derek comes to the ring. He slides in and talks to Shawn a little as they wait.] Micheal K: And their opponents, first of all, he is one of the Knights of Anarchy, one half of the team known as Cold Steel, he is the living legend, GREG 'IRONMAN' DAVIES! ['Ironman' by Mettalica plays and Greg comes to the ring. He slides in and paces back and forth with the ref between him and the other two men. ] Micheal K: Finally, he is one half of the current tag-team title holders, KoA member and all around weird guy, he is JACKSON THERAPY! ['Paper Cuts' by Nirvana plays and Jackson comes down to the ring in his typical twitchy, paranoid look. He slides into the ring and the ref rings the bell. Derek and Jackson lock up as do Greg and Shawn. Shawn starts off with a series of punches and flurries to the head of Greg, driving him down, he thumps Greg to the mat with blows to the back of the head then follows up with a knee springboarded off the bottom rope. Meanwhile, Jackson hip tosses Frost out the ring right away, then follows him with a suicida dive cross body that drops both men into the cold hard concrete.] Lewis: Its chaos here right now, right in front of me Jackson Therapy is driving the head of Derek Frost into the guard rails, and now he's trying to drown him in a forty ounce soda cup. I don't think its going to work though. [Shawn follows up his attack by pulling Greg to his feet then whipping him into the ropes. Shawn comes off the ropes himself and connects a hurricanrana that almost unscrews Greg's head. Shawn runs up the ropes and springboards into a second rope moonsault that connects with authority, and with the body of Greg Davies. As this is happening Jackson has taken it into his head to bounce Derek's head off the ring steps some, in fact its come into his head to trap Derek's head between the two sets of steps and attempt a fist drop off the apron into them. This, obviously, injures both men pretty badly. ] Big Daddy: Owww, you know, when you see Therapy do stuff like that, you have to wonder about the man's mental health. Lewis: And you don't worry about it normally? Big Daddy: Good point, well made. [Greg struggles up from Shawn, and this time when the lightweight comes off the ropes he finds himself on the recieving end of a huge sidewalk slam. Greg rolls him up and locks on the sort of suplex series that has made him famous.] Lewis: Gargoyle suplex, fisherman hook suplex, wheelbarrow suplex, northern lights suplex, german suplex, yellow jacket suplex and there's the huge turning T-bone suplex from Greg. Greg backing off and looking for Shawn to get up, that's the bulldog coming. [Greg lines up for the running bulldog that always sets up the Frostbite, but when he runs out, Shawn falls back to the mat, avoids the attack and follows up by running Greg into the turnbuckle, smashing his head into it and then climbing the ropes around him and landing a reverse frankensteiner that drives the head of Greg into the mat. Shawn climbs the ropes and stands on the turnbuckle, hands held out to show that what's coming next is the... ] Big Daddy: SHAWN'S SENTON! Lewis: It connected, it's good! And there's the pin, 1... 2... 3! Scoring the pinfall and going on to fight for the US title, he is SHAWN! [Jackson climbs into the ring and helps up Greg as Shawn helps the bloodied Derek Frost out the arena. The camera, having a great sense of timing follows them.] Shawn: Good match there. [rost: For you maybe, I think that I got my damn crown busted by that crazy son-of-a.... [From nowhere, SALAOK and QUARTZ, TRUE FEAR jump Shawn and Derek, battering them with steel chair and baseball bat. Shawn takes a crack to the head and Derek falls to the floor under a battering with the steel chair. Shawn is picked up and thrown through a door, he finds himself in a relax room, where THOMAS 'THE ROPER' JAMES and CODY 'THE WRANGLER' BRIGGS are drinking and watching Buffy on TV. Thomas James catches Shawn then throws him through the small wooden table of the room. Cody grabs Salaok and starts to batter him with the bottle he'd been drinking from. Quartz hits James with the chair over the back of the head and batters the big man down. Security pour into the room around the brawling men. ] Big Daddy: Always something going on here, its all action on Confessions... ['Saint of me' by the Rolling Stones plays and Maurice Lucan, Confessions commissioner comes to the ring. His boos are more subdued now, apparently to his great annouyance. He pulls out a mic and gets in the ring.] Lucan: Now, I see Dick 'The head' Hart coming out here and making books and Jesse Helm coming out and making rules on my show, well this it MY show and I have booking power here too. So I'm adding something to the main event, I'm adding a ref, and the ref I'm going to add is... THE JUDGE! [The crowd goes mad at the idea of three knights being in the ring together and what it means for Dick's plans. ] Big Daddy: My god, what a call from Lucan! Lewis: Hart won't be happy about that. Micheal K: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following match is for the WCWF International Television title. It will go to one pinfall or submission and will feature first of all to the ring, the challenger, over seven foot, he is the giant of the WCWF, he is the unmistakable, RAZIEL LUCIFER! ['Antichrist Superstar' by Marylin Manson plays and Raziel step onto the stage in his green flames and walks through them to the ring. He raises and lowers his arms and the flames flare then die away. He stands in the ring and waits.] Micheal K: His opponent, he is the current WCF International champion, he is the golden boy of the federation, two time undfeated world champion, hardcore champion, tag-team champion, the only WCWF's true grandslam champion, he is NATHANIAL 'REDEEM' ELIJAH JOB DUKE! [St. John St. Micheal comes to the ring proclaiming the coming of the lord, the Sisters hold open the curtains and 'In the name of the Father' by Black Grape plays as Nathanial comes to the ring. He stops on the steps to pull of his silver jacket and hand his belt to St. John before stepping in the ring. As soon as the bell rings Raziel grabs for Nathanial, but Duke slips around him and catches the big man in a belly to back fallaway suplex. Duke rolls up and waits for the giant to rise, he dives in and catches Raziel rising with a rolling clothesline that lays the giant out. Nate steps over Raziel and locks on a camel clutch. From sheer power the big man presses out and snap mares Nathanial to the mat. Raziel lifts Nate up in a body slam, then sets about him with a series of punches to the face. Raziel pulls Nathanial up and hits him with a brutal throat thrust that has the ref warning Raziel and Nathanial clutching at his neck, struggling for breath. Raziel grabs Nate and double arm DDTS him into the mat. He bounces off the ropes and comes up to Nate with a well placed knee to the lower back of the Duke.] Big Daddy: I have to say, its been one hell of a night, but here at the Target Centre, the sold out Target Centre, its felt all the bigger, these fans, the night, its madness, but in a good way. [Raziel pulls Nathanial up and drops him with a stomach crusher that drives the wind from him. He follows up with a choke lift that has Nathanial pulling at his throat, unable to get the breath back that's been driven from him. Raziel tosses him to the floor, pulls him up again and slams him to the mat. ] Lewis: Totally, the night has been amazing, like a Pay-per-view with all the action carried on. Big Daddy: Every night's like that at WCWF. [Raziel pulls Nathanial up and whips him to the ropes, catches him coming off in a huge clinching slam. ] Lewis: MORNING STAR! [He pulls up the groggy Nathanial, bends him over and places his hands together. He flips Nathanial up, but in desperation Nathanial kicks free of his hands and slides down Raziel's back. As he goes he reaches back and catches Lucifer's head into a neck breaker stiff enough to send the big man to the mat also. ] Big Daddy: I thought it was all over there. Lewis: Never count out the Duke, that's something that we've learnt over the years. [Raziel is first up, he whips Nathanial into the corner and starts to hammer Nate's head into the metal of the ring post. As the camera pulls in we see that on the pyro package on the ring post there is some sort of blinking light package attached not normally there. ] Big Daddy: Hey, what the hell is that? Lewis: Well, I don't know, but I do know that the UNIBOMBER has been seen in the arena, just, and he's been looking at Nathanial recently, I wonder? [Raziel climbs over Nathanial and stands on the turnbuckle, he pulls Nathanial onto the second turnbuckle and places his hands together. ] Big Daddy: Death drop to hell from the top rope? No, surely not! [The green lights on the package go red and before Raziel can lift Nathanial up a C4 explosion goes off, blasting up along the big man's back and his legs, he falls off the turnbuckle and falls flat on the mat, putting out the flame on his arm that caught for a second. The ref rings the bell. A robed figure runs out the crowd and dodges up the entrance ramp. Nathanial sees him and runs after him as the EMTs come to check out Raziel. ] Lewis: The ref is ruling that explosion to be interferance on the side of Nathanial and he's ruling the winner as RAZIEL LUCIFER by disqualification. [Raziel, cheated of his chance to win the belt, goes mad, raging about the ring, attacking EMTs, the ref, and at one point almost attacking Micheal Kopetski, but thanks to Micheal's fast feet and total cowardice he saves himself. Finally Raziel rages out the arena, much to the happiness of the security guards who don't have to make him leave. ] Micheal K: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, BOYS AND GIRLS, PLAYERS AND PLAYER HATERS, THIS IS THE WCWF CONFESSIONS MAAAAAAAAAAIN EVENT! This match will decide the holder of the WCWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION OF THE WORLD! It will be a three way match and will feature first of all, the challenger, winner of the Clash of the Immortals, he is DANE RENNIER! ['It's my life' by Jon Bon Jovi plays and Dane Rennier comes to the ring through his lights and his pyros.] Micheal K: His first opponent, returning to the WCWF, newest member of the corporation, standing over seven feet tall, he is JESSE 'THE GLADIATOR' HELMS! [Helms comes to the ring and steps over the ropes. He waits out Dane and Chandler. ] Micheal K: Their opponent, the WCWF's Heavyweight champion, he is the leader of the Knights of Anarchy, he is RAYMOND CHANDLER! [Leaves bluster about the entrance as 'One more Kiss' plays and Chandler comes to the ring. ] Micheal K: Finally, the guest referee, Knights member, main stay of all that is good in WCWF, looks like a hung jury with THE JUDGE! ['Guilty Concience' plays and Judge comes to the ring. He climbs between the ropes and rings the bell for the match to start.] Big Daddy: Well, Dane wants to be a knight, both Chandler and Judge are knights, I can't see how Jesse can win this. Lewis: They said that about an international title a while back, that time greed changed the plans, who knows what will happen this time. [Chandler steps forward to lock up with Jesse. He struggles for leverage with the big man. As he does Dane watches, waiting for the follow up on Jesse. And he doesn't see The Judge pull out a cattle prod. Judge tazers Dane who falls to the floor. ] Big Daddy: WHAT THE HELL DID JUDGE JUST DO?! [Judge grabs Raymond from behind and heaves him up. The astonished Chandler is hit with a... ] Lewis: HUNG JURY! JUDGE JUST GAVE CHANDLER THE HUNG JURY! [Judge shakes hands with Jesse Helms. Dick Hart and Nathanial Duke, the last to members of the corporation come down to ringside to join them. Duke and Helms beat on Dane, Helms finally lifting him and powerbombing him back first into the turnbuckle. ] Big Daddy: Judge is... Oh god, JUDGE IS JOINING THE CORPORATION! Judge is turning on the Knights, on Raymond, he's turning on everything he ever stood for! [Judge shakes Dick Hart's hand, then lifts up Raymond in another Hung Jury as Duke and Helms seem to be trying to cripple young Rennier. Before the Jury can hit though, 'Bear Hug' by the Fun Loving Criminals plays and Greg Davies, Bobby McCann and Jackson Therapy rush the ring. Jackson tackles Judge right out from under Chandler and with Chandler slowly getting to his feet the KoA manage to run off the corporation. ] Big Daddy: Oh my god. I cannot believe what I just saw here tonight. [JT checks on Raymond as Davies and McCann bend over Dane. Dane is out cold and looks pretty badly hurt. EMTs come out and roll him onto a back board. ] Lewis: What's happening, is Dane badly hurt? The match is a no contest, but what's going on? Big Daddy: Lewis, I'm sorry, but we don't have time for this. Lewis: Its not? Big Daddy: It is. Lewis: Dammit. Well, to all you people out there, its been one hell of a night. This is Lewis F for Big Daddy C, signing off. [We fade to black on the chaos in the ring and the WCWF logo comes onto the ring. ]
