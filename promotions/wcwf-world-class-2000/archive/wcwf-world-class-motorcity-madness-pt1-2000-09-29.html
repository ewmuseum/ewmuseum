WCWF Presents MotorCity Madness (Part 1 of 2)

Pontiac Michigan Sept 25, 2000 Pontiac Silverdome The screen opens with the Motorcity Madness promo, 'Liberate' by Slipknot plays as images of hardcore madness and blood splatter the screen. Thudding, smashing slams over and over. Hardcore matches to break bones, the unstoppable hardcore reign of the most brutal man in WCWF. The hardcore beatings given out by the new TV champion to anyone he faces, blood, blood and more blood. And the brutal attacks on the WCWF heavyweight champion, the free for alls for the lightweight title, the Elimination 4-way, hardcore, brutality, madness. Corey's voice blares out over the arena. Corey: LIBERATE! MY MADNESS! ONE OF ME, ALL OF YOU! LIBERATE! MY MADNESS! We pull into the announcers table to see a few surprises. First of all, Barry Clintok is not at the announcer's desk, Bob Redding is though, and sitting next to him is a man in a big glittering cowboy hat, glittering sunglasses and a bright pink coat with tassels comeing from the arms and elbows. This of course, must not be real, since for one he hasn't looked like that since what, the eighties? Macho Man: Hooooo yeah. We've got one heck of a card here tonight. Oooo yeah. Bob: Well said Macho man, and can I say welcome to the WCWF for your first job commentating. Macho Man: Thanks there bob, that means a lot to me, oh yeeeah. Wasn't there supposed to be someone else here? Bob: Yeah Barry, but I don't know where he is. We all look around to see Barry Clintok perched on the balcony above the announcer's table. He leaps off with a swandive into the timekeepers table. Barry: HAAARRRRRRR COOOOOOORRE! Ouch. Bob and the Macho man look at Barry and shake their heads. Barry gets up and spits a few teeth onto the announcer's table, then settles into his chair with a grin. Barry: That's what tonight is all about, ooo yeah. Macho Man: Ooooh yeah. Barry: Oooooooohhhhh yeeeeeeah. Macho Man: OOoooooooooooooooooohhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh Yeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeaaaahhhhhhhhhhhh. Bob: Micheal K, please, for the love of god. Micheal K: Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to the most insane place on earth, welcome to MOTORCITY MADNESS! Tonight we start with a tag match staring first of all, representing the Redeemed, Ex-tag-team title holder, they are Eyval and Atom, THE REDEEMED! 'Tattered and Torn' by Slipknot plays and Atom and Eyval come to the ring with the heads hung with noone behind them. They slide into the ring and stand against the ropes in silence. Micheal K: Their opponents, they are Mr. Ultimo and the Cruiserweight champion, Ric Landell, get up, stand up for THE NEW MAFIA! The theme to Halloween plays and Ric leads the two to the ring smiling and shouting up to the crowd. They dive into the ring and start hammering the Redeemed. Atom and Eyval seem confused and hardly fight back. Ric hurricanranas Eyval out the ring, both of them slam into the concrete and reel about in pain, holding their heads in pain. Ultimo whips Atom off the ropes, and finally the Redeemed stage a fight back as Atom floors the lighter man with a shoulder block. Atom lets Ultimo get up and picks him up with a choke lift. The ref counts to four and Atom drops Ultimo on the floor. Again he doesn't follow up, he hardly seems to want to win. Barry: Boy, the big men here don't seem to have their hearts in the match. Bob: No, and its more than the fact that they've already had the tag-belts and that this is the opening match of the card. Maybe its because they're futures are tied up in the main event, something they're not involved in. Ultimo rolls and sweeps Atom's legs out with a spinning lunge. Atom hits hard and Ultimo starts to stomp on his head. Eyval's head is being bounced off the steel of the guard rails by Ric Landell stomping on him. Ultimo pulls Atom up and drops him with a standing drop kick. Barry: The main event with Jake and Redemptor in the challenge that Redemptor levelled almost a month ago to Jake. If Jake could defeat all of the Redeemed Red would face him tonight for his and Jake's belt, and if Jake won Redemptor would disband the Redeemed. Bob: And Jake has beaten all of the Redeemed, from Atom to T so it all comes down to tonight for the Redeemed. Ultimo with a missile drop kick to Atom, and Landell rolling into the ring. Where's Eyval? Barry: Judging by the blood staining the chair that Ric is holding Eyval is a little dizzy. Bob: Steel chair? Ref, DQ! Barry: No Bob, this is Motorcity Madness, every match is Hardcore rules. Macho Man: Oh Yeah? Ric and Ultimo whip Atom off the ropes and together lift the big guy in a huge pancake press. Eyval rolls into the ring just as Ric and Ultimo turn. They grab him and double DDT him into the floor. Ric and Ultimo high five and Ric locks Atom in the... Barry: NATURE LOCK! Atom doesn't tap so Ultimo climbs to the top rope and drops an elbow on Landell. Macho Man: Not like that! Randy Savage pulls off his headset and climbs to the top rope. Ultimo climbs the opposite rope and the two dive off together. They hit Atom together. The ref checks Atom and declares him to be unconcious. The 'Macho man' rolls back out and sits by Bob and Barry. Macho Man: Hoo yeah, if you hear what the Macho Man is saying. Barry: The winners, and the new WCWF number one tag-team contenders, Ric Landell and Mr. Ultimo, THE NEW MAFIA! Bob: Nice start, Atom is half concious and I think Eyval is bleeding. Let's get this one on some, MK? Micheal K: The following match is between the KoA and the Beat Down Crew, first to the ring, representing the KoA, tag legend and KoA mainstay, he is 'THE IRONMAN' GREG DAVIES! 'Ironman' by Black Sabbath plays and Davies comes to the ring. Micheal K: His opponent, from the Beat Down Crew, Ex-US champion, he is 'THE MOBSTAR' DONNIE MONTANA! 'Criminal' by Eminem plays and Donnie comes to the ring with a smile on his face. The crowd boo and shout at him as he rolls into the ring and attacks Greg before the bell. The ref signals to start the match and they start going to it. Greg takes a quick advantage with a snap suplex smashing Donnie into the mat. Greg rolls up and waits for Montana to get up, he spears him into the corner and rams his shoulder into the gut of Montana over and over, finally letting him drop to the floor. Barry: Greg coming out without the rest of the Knights. We know that at least Chandler and Therapy are in the building and I think the Frog is about somewhere. Bob: Greg seems fine on his own to be honest. Greg with a neat fisherman's hook suplex, Montana with a strong kick out. Greg whipping Donnie into the corner and following with a shoulder block, Greg back elbowing Montana in the head, and another. Greg stepping away and Donnie falling to the floor. Greg with a wheelbarrow suplex slamming Donnie into the middle of the ring. Greg with another cover, kick out on two though. Donnie rolling to his feet and pacing Greg out a little, all been Davies' way so far. Montana off the ropes, Greg under that attempted lariat though and snatching Montana off the other ropes with a huge samoan drop. Greg climbs to the top rope and leaps off at Montana with an elbow drop. Montana gets his knees up and drives them into the ribs of Davies. Davies rolls off holding his gut and Montana pauses to take catch his breath. Macho Man: Ah have to do everything here, oh yeah. The Macho Man climbs to the top rope and drops his elbow onto Montana. He gets up and dusts his hands with a smile on his face. Greg rolls over and locks Donnie in THE BLIZZARD! Barry: Ahh no. The Beat Down Crew come to the ring and attack Greg Davies. Dan Bonez whacks The Macho Man with a steel chair. They group around and beat down Greg Davies. A man jumps out from the audience and helps out Greg. Bob: Who the hell is that? Barry: I know that, it's JOHN DAVIES! It's Greg's older brother he's come out the audience to help his brother! The rest of the Knights come to the ring and the Beat Down Crew have to fight on two fronts. The ring turns into a huge brawl. Greg is badly beat up but the Knights have greater numbers. Randy Savage is in the middle of it all when security come to the ring and bundle everyone, him included out the stadium. Bob: Looks like we're an announcer down then. Barry: Looks like Davies' older brother is going to be on the Crew's hit list. Bob: Looks like if John has half the talent of little brother we might see another tag-team the likes of Cold Steel. Barry: That'd be cool. Okay, let's get this moving on this. Micheal, let's go. Micheal K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following is the HARDCORE CRUISERWEIGHT FREE FOR ALL! The last man standing will earn himself number one contender status for the cruiserweight belt. Coming to the ring they are 'POSITIVELY' JAKE NIELSON, JAY 'THE LADIES MAN' ROBSON, PAUL MASTERS, T, THE ANARCHIST ASSASIN, CHAMBER and HANK BRIMSTONE! Half the WCWF's cruiserweight division troop to the ring to the strains of 'Liberate' by Slipknot and roll into the ring. The ref starts the match from outside the ring and they start. Paul Masters instantly grabs Hank Brimstone and slings him out the ring. He follows him with a slingshot body splash over the top rope, sending them both into the guardrails. Jay Robson locks up with T and they swap punches back and forth. T drops him with a drop neck lock to his knee. T sets to booting The Ladies Man in the back and stomping on his head. Jake Nielson catches a nasty diving shoulder block from The Anarchist Assasin who follows up with a leaping double knee drop to the head of Jake. Chamber climbs to the top rope and senton bombs T, The Ladies Man, The Assasin and Jake in one huge dive. Outside the ring Paul Masters is beating down Hank Brimstone with a steel chair. Hank gets out a toe hold and Masters falls to the floor, Brimstone grabs a steel chair of his own and the two face off outside the ring. Barry: T with a moonsault into the Ladies man, oh, and Chamber with a swandive headbutt on T and Jay. The Assasin diving into those three with a twisting elbow from the top rope. Bob: There are bodies flying everywhere in this Cruiserweight free for all. Barry: What do you expect from a cruiserweight free for all. Jake with a big splash into all four of them. It's a mess in there. Bob: Good isn't it. And a mess out the ring, Hank and Paul just beating on each other with those steel chairs in there. Both men are bleeding. Barry: The Ladies man was at the bottom of that pile of flying bodies, and he's not really moving anymore, the ref counting from outside the ring, and he makes the ten before Jay gets close to his feet. The EMTs roll The Ladies man out the ring quickly and efficiently. T is the first of the men in the ring to get to his feet and he catches Chamber in his patented moonsault DDT. As Chamber staggers to his feet Jake holds his hands up and runs across the ring for the... Bob: NIELSON CUTTER!. Jake backs off and points around the top row of the arena slowly. As he does so T grabs him and gives him the.... Barry: BITCHSLAP! The ref starts the count as The Assasin and T brawl in the corner of the ring. Jake just about gets to his hands and knees as the ten count comes, but its too late and Jake has to roll out the ring as he is disqualified. The ref helps the EMTs drag out Chamber. Paul Masters and Hank Brimstone are still swapping chair shots, they're both bleeding pretty bad at this point. Paul gains the upper hand by finally dropping his chair and ducking Hanks swing. He belly to back suplexes Hank into the Spanish announcer's table. Barry: Heh heh, like that Miguel? Bob: Shut up Barry, it'll only be our table in a minute. The Anarchist Assasin whips T across the ring in time to catch a chair shot from Masters out the ring. Masters slingshots into the ring and catches T with a double elbow to the head. At the same time Hank comes around the other edge of the ring and slams The Anarchist Assasin with his own chair. The Assasin drops to the floor, the ref starts to count on T and The Anarchist, Hank and Paul kick them out the ring as the count reaches ten and the EMT's carry them off. Bob: Hank and Paul getting it on in the ring, and they have pretty little left, this won't last long. Barry: In fact, Paul with a moonsault off the ropes, dragging Hank up, THE MASTERPIECE! And Hank isn't getting up from that, the winner, and the last man standing, the new WCWF cruiserweight number one contender, he is PAUL 'THE MASTERPLAN' MASTERS! Bob: Now, the very guys that that match was for, the cruiserweight title. Micheal K: Ladies and Gentlemen, first to the ring, the challenger, Young Blood is running through his veins, he is simply, SHAWN! 'Bulls on parade' plays and Shawn holds his arms up in the Young Blood's sign as he runs to the ring. The crowd go mad as he goes and dives into the ring. Micheal K: His opponent, the WCWF cruiserweight champion, he is one half of the New Mafia, he is RIC LANDELL! The theme to 2001 plays and Ric comes to the ring, he drops his cruiserweight title and drops it on the time keeper's table. He rolls into the ring and Shawn baseball slides Ric right back out the ring. Barry: This'll be a good one, Shawn the high flying risk taker, Ric the submissionist and technical wrestler. Bob: Ric reeling from that taste of Shawn's boot leather. Shawn with a slingshot over the top rope taking Ric right into the concrete. Shawn pulls Ric up and whips him into the steel ring steps. Ric slams into them back first and Shawn charges him, smashing his back into the steps and the ring post. Ric grabs Shawn and drops him face first into the ring steps. Both men reel in pain from the impact with the steps. Ric rolls back into the ring and waits for Shawn. Shawn rolls into the ring and Ric lifts him up into a waistlock suplex. Shawn rolls to his feet only to catch a german suplex from Ric who bridges for the pin, they reach a two count before Shawn kicks out. They both roll up and Shawn runs off the ropes and catches Ric perfectly with a hurricanrana. Shawn locks on a reverse chinlock to Ric. Barry: That back of Ric having just been driven into the ring steps and the ring post, you can tell that's really hurting him, and hurting bad. Bob: Ric can't get to the ropes, but he's crawling with his feet as best he can. Shawn pulling him back and locking it on harder. Ric really is hurting there. Shawn breaking the hold and bouncing off the rope in a face buster. Bob: Typical youngblood mentality, coulda held on for the win right there, but he had to go with the excitment instead. Barry: And a good thing too, and probably the reason he's had the most succesful long term cruiserweight careers in the WCWF. Bob: Mama naa blah blah. Shawn goes to the top rope and leaps off with a knee drop, he misses though and seems to roll his knee. Ric grabs Shawn and whips him off the ropes, drop toe hold, spinning ankle lock, into the... Barry: NATURE LOCK! And Shawn with that damaged knee can't take it, he's tapping, he's tapping! The winner, and STILL the WCWF cruiserweight champion, RIC LANDELL! Bob: I gotta say, as a man who isn't a fan of midget wrestling, I like that. Well, let's move on, Micheal K, have we got a title match going on? Micheal K: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following match is a fourway dance for the WCWF Television Title. This is a No Holds Barred ladder match, the winner is the man who climbs the ladder and takes the TV title down with him. First to the ring representing the Clan, the former TV champion, he'll put your lights out, he is EUGENE WHIFFLEBOTTOM! 'Loser' by Three doors down plays and Eugene comes to the ring with a mixed reaction. Micheal K: His first opponent, six foot seven and two hundred and thirty four pounds, he is DEREK FROST! 'Bulls on Parade' plays and the young blood's pyro's go off, Derek does not come out alone, both him and Dragon come to the ring together. Barry: The bloods showing solidarity here, I guess this match won't be four ways after all. Micheal K: And finally, the WCWF Television champion, he is the man known only as 'THE SEEKER'! The Seeker comes to the ring and snatches the ladder from the walkway. He throws it into the ring and starts beating on each of the other three men right away, a favour that they return. Barry: Seeker not wasting any time, he wants to make this a quick one, the other three all attacking him. Bob: It's always that way when you have the champ in this sort of competition, they all want to beat on you to win. Barry: It's a ladder match, you don't need to beat him to win. Bob(In high pitched mocking tone): You don't need to beat him to win. Mnuh nuh nuh nuuuh. Barry: I wish the Macho man would come back. I'm going to get him. Bob: Please don't, I'll be good. The brawl seems to concentrate on Eugene and Seeker until Frost and Dragon back off and just leave them to it. Seeker waffling the head of Eugene into the ring post, and Dragon and Frost just leaning on the ropes, waiting for them to fight it out. Seeker lifts Eugene in a powerbomb and turns to the other two. Dragon and Frost charge at Seeker and Seeker catches them both with a double clothesline. Seeker gets the ladder and is about to set it up when all three men get up, Seeker runs and dives, clotheslining all three with the steel ladder. Seeker takes some impact from the ladder himself and is slow getting up. Eugene gets to his feet to find himself near Dragon, who he sets to pummeling with kicks and punches. Frost and Seeker lock up and Seeker knocks Frost back with a series of headbutts and a few European uppercuts. Frost is on the ropes when Seeker backs off and springs at him with a diving forearm. Frost goes over the top rope, but Seeker goes with him. Meanwhile Eugene is taking the hard end of some stiff right hands from Dragon, the new comer showing Eugene who's boss, whipping him into the ropes and backlifting him clean out the ring. Dragon up to the top turnbuckle and a diving moonsault into Eugene. Barry: The Youngbloods seem to have this one sewn up. Bob: The Youngbloods? Are they back? Dammit. Barry: Yes they're back. I think. Where have you been? Bob: Minnesota, what of it? Frost grabs Seeker and whips him towards Dragon, Dragon does the same with Eugene to Frost. Seeker and Eugene though avoid colliding by linking arms and reversing momentuum. Eugene goes back to Frost and Seeker to Dragon, both laying out their respective others with power clotheslines. Bob: Do see do and around we go huh? Barry: Sure thing Bob, line dancing it is. Seeker rolls into the ring and starts to set up the ladder. Dragon rolls in after him and tackles Seeker to the floor, Dragon up to the top turnbuckle for a moonsault, but Seeker is up to his feet and dives forward, whacking out Dragon's feet and landing him crotch first on the turnbuckle. Barry: Oooh, that has to hurt. Bob: Never turn your back on The Seeker, and Seeker taking his shot he's going up the ladder. As Seeker goes up the ladder Eugene has slammed Frost into the ringsteps with a brainbuster suplex. Dragon is still mounted on the top rope. Eugene splits the ring steps and has Frost's leg on the bottom step as Dragon regains himself and gets to his feet on the turnbuckle. Seeker just gets in reach of the belt as Eugene lifts the other part of the ring steps ready to smash them into the ankle of Frost, possibly ruining his career. Dragon has to choose, into the ladder and save the belt, or into Eugene to save his friend. Barry: Dragon with the flying cross body block... Into Eugene! Seeker's up there, and he has the belt! The winner and still the.... Barry fades off as the Seeker uses the belt to lay out Dragon, Frost and Eugene. He smacks them all unconcious with the gold of the belt then grabs the mic from Micheal K. Seeker: Now I now you all expected Seeker to lose this one. I mean, the deck was stacked against him. But like I have been saying since my arrival, this ain't my first dance! Seeker lowers the mic to his side and stares at the belt in his hand. Seeker: And since I've been here people have wanted to know who I am. What drives me why do I do what I do? Well there are many reasons. Not only have I been in this very ring before, I have been here since the near beginning of the WCWF. I have watched it rise and fall and rise again. Seeker again pauses and drops the television title to the mat. He then looks at the crowd and removes his sunglasses, then slowly begins to remove his wrap. Barry: What is he doing? Bob: He's removing that surgical wrap. Barry: I can see that, who is it? Bob: Let it be Enigma, let it be Enigma, let it be... Barry: Oh my, OH MY GOD! OH GOD, MY GOD! IT'S THE LOOSE CANNON! Bob: IT'S TONY MURDOCH! He's back. HE'S BACK! HE'S BACK! Barry: Shut up, you're giving me a headache. Murdoch: That's right you ignorant pukes The Legend is back! Hell I never left. I've just been running around making everyone crazy and winning this belt! Now I know you are all saying. He looks like Tony, he sounds like Tony but he doesn't talk like Tony. Well the way I talked was all part of a ridiculous "hick" gimmick thought up when I was signed to the WCWF. I hated it! But I towed the line and won matches and scared the shit out of people despite my idiotic speech. Well that's all over! Big Ton is back and except for this scar. Murdoch points to a long scar from his forehead that crosses over his eye. Murdoch: Yes, this is new. Maybe you remember the Legend's last night in this fed, a few months ago. I was tred of the schtick I was being forced to play and decided if things weren't going to change, well I was done! I was enjoying the show in my hotel room when that sick bastard Redemptor kicked in the door. He tried to kill me. It was a hell of a fight and I don't know, the ol' boy must of just got tired of trying and left me bleeding on the hotel floor. Ya should've killed me Duke! 'Cause if you think you all hated me before, now I've got a reason! Duke wasn't the only one to turn, no there's Danny boy. You remember or team don't you Dan? You remember one of the stupidest moves you have made in your career? You turned on the Loose Cannon waffling me with a baseball bat! Well, I haven't forgotten! And now you are the U.S. champ. Well Bonez, if you're still wearing that belt at the end of the night, I'm next in line! And what about this belt? Murdoch looks down to the mat at the television belt. Murdoch: This belt. You know, the announcers on Wreckage were right. Even though this is the third time I have worn this belt, it is for losers and new guys, Big Ton is neither of those! So booking committee, Lucan, and yes even you Taylor, You can take this worthless belt and shove it up your ass! THAT'S IT! Barry: What does he mean? The TV title is a fine award, and Tony should be proud of... Murdoch throws the mic to the mat and begins unzippig his pants. Bob: Is he? Barry: MY GOD! He's urinating on the teleision title! That is a travesty! Now he's just leaving it in a puddle and exiting the ring! Murdoch's new music begins to play; 'Ironman' by Black Sabbath. He makes his way up the ramp to the back spitting at fans in a hail of trash and boos. Murdoch grins all the way to the backsage area. Bob: How can he do that? Barry: I know, throwing away a belt that many young atheletes give there all to... Bob: No, I mean go with everyone watching like that, I can't do it if the urinal next to me is being used. Barry: I swear Bob, if I weren't being paid to put up with you... Bob: But you are. Have we got another match? Barry: Not until after the commercial break, but don't go away, because we've got some humdingers coming. Bob: And hopefully some news on what we'll do with the TV title, once we've washed it off properly... 'Liberate' plays and we fade out to black and the commericals.
