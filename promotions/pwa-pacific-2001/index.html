Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Pacific Wrestling Alliance published its own FAQ Version 1.0 on 24 April 2001, describing itself as “a new e-fed run entirely through e-mail,” owned, operated, and booked by a single person who hand-wrote every card himself. The FAQ conceded the usual wisdom against that model — “most people think that it takes more than one man to run an e-fed and they are right” — before insisting the PWA would run on one man and a roster of wrestlers regardless.

Its one weekly show, Saturday Night Wrestling, was joined every quarter by a theme show it compared directly to “the Royal Rumble or King of the Ring,” plus supercards and inter-fed cards run as needed. Every show was built only from role-plays its own operator judged good enough to air — “if your role-playing isn't good it won't be on the show” — assembled in a style the FAQ likened to “flashing,” meant to make the PWA's own cards read like the WWF's or another national federation's.

Four championships ran the roster: a Heavyweight Title compared directly to “the WWF's World Title” and reserved for the fed's biggest names; a Lightweight Title under the same rules but capped at 215 pounds; a West Coast Title serving as a secondary belt and stepping stone to either heavyweight division; and a set of Tag Team Titles. Every title was defended at each large event, and the FAQ built a rule for inter-fed stakes directly into its own championship structure: a title could be put on the line between two federations, with the losing federation ceding control of the belt outright, and any new or vacant title filled only by tournament.

That FAQ, posted twice the same day across two served pages, is the Pacific Wrestling Alliance's whole surviving record. Whether Saturday Night Wrestling ever aired a first card, whether any of its four titles were ever awarded, and how or when the promotion ended aren't in the surviving record.
