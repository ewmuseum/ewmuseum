Pacific Wrestling Alliance: Join the PWA

24 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
********************************************* **The Pacific Wrestling Alliance (PWA) V1.0** ********************************************* Company Overview The PWA is a new e-fed run entirely through e-mail. It is owned and operated by one man, [name withheld]. The fed is booked based of quality of role playing and hand written by Tom. Most people think that it takes more than one man to run an e-fed and they are right. However, only one man needs to run the show and wrestlers are what is needed most.
Television and Pay-Per-View The PWA has one weekly show, Saturday Night Wrestling, booked and hand written by [name withheld]. Every quarter (three months) the PWA will feature a theme show, much like the Royal Rumble or King of the Ring. The PWA will also run special supercards or inter-fed cards whenever needed. The shows will contain the best of the feds angles from the role-plays. If your role-playing isn't good it won't be on the show. However, if I think it's good and has promise I will run it. Using this style of role-play, much like flashing, allows our shows to be much more like the WWF or other federations' shows.
Titles PWA Heavyweight Title - The big one. Similar to the WWF's World Title. Only the biggest players in the fed can even try to get their hands on this one. Represents the federation's entire region.
PWA Lightweight Title - Seen as a lesser title by some, this is actually the same title as above but just for the smaller guys.
The weight limit on the belt is 215 pounds. PWA West Coast Title - The PWA's secondary belt. Most commonly used as a stepping stone to the Heavy or Lightweight belts.
Represents the entire west coast of North America. PWA Tag Team Titles - The main title for tag teams. Viewed as a primary belt in most federations and the same goes for the PWA. All titles will be defended at every large event. During inter-fed cards the titles may be on the line between to feds. If you lose our belt the other fed gains control of it if they want to. However, if we in any titles of a new title is created, a tournament will be held to fill the vacancy.
