NGF Monday Night Mayham

8 August 1995

DARRELL>Welcome to NGF Monday Night Mayham report, we had a great card of action tonight...
PHIL>That's right dummy, we did first off we had the big man Big Van Vader facing that sorry excuse for a single wrestler the Flyboy Rocco Rock.
Vader taught that boy a lesson in wrestling did he not, he destroyed him in 4.59 after a Vader Power Bomb...
DARRELL>But you have to give credit to Rock he is a tag team wrestler not a single wrestler, Phil.
PHIL>Well he needs to stay a tag team wrestler cause' he doesn't bring anything to the table as a single wrestler, but the match of the night came next when the crowd yelling fool Sting faced the Polynisa, big man
Yokozuna.
DARRELL>Might I add Yokozuna weighed an amazing 646 lbs... PHIL>Forget about that, let's tell them about the match, Yokzuna went off on him hitting him numerous major moves like the elbow drop, legdrop, bossman type slam...
DARRELL>But Yokozuna messed up when he went for the buttsplash in the corner and Sting moved out of the way...
PHIL>Oh well everyone can't hit everything can they... DARRELL>No they can't but this ended up being the move that finshed the match, Sting hit Yoko with a clothesline off the top rope and then a flying body press, tehn he nailed Yokozuna with a Stinger Splash and slapped on the Scorpion Death Lock and within seconds Yoko gave in and submited, the match lasted 15 mins. 52 sec. Now the next match was the 123
Kid facing Stunning Steve Austin, the kid didn't have a chance against
Austin, for one reason before the match Psycho Sid attacked the Kid while he was coming to ringside...
PHIL>Well the Kid should have been watching his back, when the Kid finaly got in the ring Austin hit him with a piledriver and locked him in the
Thats a Wrap, for a least 2 mins. before the Kid got to the ropes for the hold to be broken...
DARRELL>Then the Kid mounted some offensive throwing Austin in the corner and hitting him with the Karate Kick Fury and then throwing him into the ropes ...
PHIL>And that's when Austin nailed him with th Stun Gun and got the 3 count, I'll give it to the Kid he lasted 5 mins. with Austin but, when him and Sid get in the ring together he won't last 2 mins.
DARRELL>Shut up, the next thing up was the Summer Spectacular Event Center... PHIL>Yeah, let me tell you people out there about the matches: 1.In a submission match for the vacent World title Bret "Hitman" Hart -vs- "Nature Boy" Ric Flair 2.In a texas death match for the vacent IC title Sabu -vs- Vader 3.In a 2 out of 3 falls match for the vacent TV title "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels -vs- Stunning Steve Austin 4.In a 2 out of 3 falls match for the vacent World Tag Team title The Public Enemy -vs- The Gangstas 5.In a texas death match "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel -vs- 911 6.In a no disqulivacation match "Made in USA" Lex Luger -vs- The Sandman 7.In a submission only match Sting -vs- "King of Harts" Owen Hart 8.In a no disqulivacation match The Nasty Boys -vs- Harlem Heat DARRELL>That's a great card of action right there and the event will be held on Friday, August 25,1995.
PHIL>Let's finish this up so I can go home, O.K. DARRELL>The next and final match of the evening was Bob Holly facing Sid, the match lasted around 6 mins. and 46 secs. Bob gave Sid a run for his money...
PHIL>Be quit, yeah Bob surprised Sid a couple of times but in the end Sid power bombed him for the win 123 that's it, goodbye Darrell.
DARRELL>Goodbye Phil, and goodbye readers, oh by the way next week we will see The Britsh Bulldog face Yokozuna, The Public Enemy face The Stud
Stable, The Nasty Boys face The Gangstas, and Bret "Hitman" Hart face one of Flair's best friends Double A, "The Enforcer" Arn Anderson, so see you next week.
