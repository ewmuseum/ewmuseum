One complete two-announcer show report for a weekly "Monday Night Mayham" card plus a "Summer Spectacular" vacant-title tournament event, the strongest single results-shaped holding recovered anywhere in this wave. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NGF's surviving record is a single Monday Night Mayham report, posted 8 August 1995 and called by two bickering announcers, Darrell and Phil, whose report opens mid-run rather than at a founding. Big Van Vader destroyed the Flyboy Rocco Rock with a powerbomb in 4:59; Sting outlasted the 646-pound Yokozuna, weathering an elbow drop, a leg drop, and a bossman slam before finishing him with a top-rope clothesline, a flying body press, a Stinger Splash, and the Scorpion Death Lock; and the 123 Kid, jumped by Psycho Sid before he could even reach the ring, lasted five minutes against Stunning Steve Austin before a Stun Gun ended it.

The same report announced a full vacant-title event, the Summer Spectacular, for Friday, 25 August 1995: Bret “Hitman” Hart against “Nature Boy” Ric Flair in a submission match for the vacant World Title, Sabu against Vader in a Texas death match for the vacant Intercontinental Title, “The Heartbreak Kid” Shawn Michaels against Stunning Steve Austin for the vacant Television Title, and The Public Enemy against The Gangstas for the vacant World Tag Team Title, alongside four further non-title matches.

The card closed with Bob Holly giving Sid a fight before Sid powerbombed him for the pin, and a preview of the following week: The British Bulldog against Yokozuna, The Public Enemy against The Stud Stable, The Nasty Boys against The Gangstas, and Bret Hart against Arn Anderson.

Whether the Summer Spectacular or any later card ran as previewed isn't in the surviving record.
