E.G.W Extreme Global Wrestling - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -=l|[Staff]|l=- Owner/President - [detail withheld] - ("SeanTaSTiC" [name withheld]) Co-Owner/Vice President - [detail withheld] - ([name withheld]) Commish - No Commish Yet, Email Pres/Vice Pres to apply * All questions, comments, and concerns should be sent to the Prez and Vice Prez - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Rules: 1. This is a roleplaying federation. In order to win you must roleplay. The Roleplay line minimum will be 60 lines, and roleplays will be judged on quality not by quantity. 2. There will be one card a week (on some occasions two) and one PPV a month. The card will be held on Tuesdays *Tuesday Night Thrashings*. All matches will be written by the staff. The more you roleplay the better quality results will be given out i.e. if you don't roleplay at all your match will just be summarized instead of in detail. Each card will have a roleplay limit, to prevent people from roleplaying 15+ times for one match. 3. TOSing is strictly prohibited, we're here to have fun, not to TOS others... 4. Use of profanity is allowed but please try to bleep or star it out, and please keep it to a minimum. 5. Spam mailing, and junk mail i.e. other federation info, is not allowed. Those who do this will be kicked out of the fed or put on suspension depending on how bad the offense was. 6. All characters must be ORIGINAL, this is a fake federation... also no mentioning of real feds is allowed. The only acception is if your talking about another original fed, where you might have had competition, or where you would like to brag about what titles you have held and such. 7. All of the staff's decisions are final... remember losing is always part of the game, if you ever have any questions or comments please keep them to the staff and not to any other members of the fed. 8. The last rule is also the most important...
