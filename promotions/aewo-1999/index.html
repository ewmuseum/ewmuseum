Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Absolute E-Wrestling Org's surviving record opens 2 January 1999, when its president, Nolan Madge, announced the promotion “established” with roughly seven dedicated roleplayers, promising two shows a week of “PURE quality, not quantity.”

The same day's Saturday Night CHAOS! broadcast opened with Madge, in the ring, moving the promotion's own Commencement of Carnage supercard up to 6 January and naming its matches: Mangler against Thorne Timberwolf for the World Heavyweight Championship, Rayne against the winner of a Juggelo–ICON bout for the USA Championship, and a three-way among Scorpion, Steele and Scott Manson for the Hardcore Championship.

On the card itself, Thorne Timberwolf stayed undefeated at 3-0 by pinning Serpiente Del Abismo with the Wolfe's Bite, setting up his own title shot; Rayne interfered in 5-o's win over Trailer Park Tommy Trashcan as payback for a match TPTT had cost him days earlier. The main event pitted Scorpion against Steele in a hardcore brawl that carried the two men through an open elevator shaft, a lighting booth's plate-glass window, and the crowd itself before Steele finished Scorpion with a Steele Driver onto a bed of thumbtacks.

How Commencement of Carnage played out, or what became of the promotion afterward, is not in the surviving record; its last surviving document is that same 2 January 1999 broadcast.
