{Scene opens to a bus traveling along an interstate highway on the side is printed NCWA WHERE THE BIG BOY'Z PLAY...... each of the windows is filled with wrestling footage. Eddie: Well, without any further delay, let's here what Craven has to say... That is not Derek Craven...that is merely a man that got caught up in the glitz and glamour of the wrestling industry and what I have done in the past.... The thing that I forgot is that this is not the past...its over and done with...Sure, I have accomplished some outstanding feats here in the NCWA...and am without a doubt proud of those accomplishments....But, the thing that I didn't really put much thought into is that it doesn't really matter one bit...It doesn't matter that I have walked the aisle a hundred times as the Main Attraction of the NCWA...It doesn't matter how many titles I've held, or how many times I have been a champion... What matters? That is exactly what I have been trying to figure out over the past twenty four hours....What matters is NOW...NOT Yesterday, Not Tomorrow...BUT TODAY....and TODAY.... Today is all that matters...What I have been doing for the past several months is living in the past....NO MORE! (Craven begins to raise his voice a bit) There was once a time when People thought of Derek Craven and the New Breed, they thought of the single most dominant force in the wrestling industry...That name was synonimous with Toughness, and flat out taking what we wanted, when we wanted it....It was never about living for the roar of the fans, nor was it about having the most expensive cars, clothes or even the most beautiful women...It was about gaining the respect that had been denied us all our lives...And I am to blame...I let those things slip through the cracks as I became bigger in the wrestling world...I forgot how I got there...and Instead of continuing on with what had taken me to the mountain top, I became someone that I am not... There is this saying..."If it's not broke, Don't Fix it"...and that is exactly what happened...Now, some people may think that the New Breed is just fine and that no changes need to be made...Well, to those people who just don't know...Maybe so...but not to a man that has bled the New Breed Blood...That Blood that ran thicker than water...That blood that boiled in our veins, and that very blood that made us strive each and every time out to be recognized as the roughest and toughest ever to grace the squared circle... It's time for a trasfusion, so to speak....It's time for Derek Craven to re-dedicate himself to what the New Breed is all about...and it begins now... (Craven motions off to the right and and FIVE men, whom are wearing hoods appear from the darkness and line up behind Craven) Craven: These men here....They know what smash mouth is all about....They know how to bring it...not sing it...The days of Telling ya how we are gonna do it , those days are over...The days of showing you how it's done...those days will officially begin tonight... It's time for the New Breed to get back to where it all started, and it is certainly time for EVERYONE in the Nationwide Championship Wrestling Association to sit up and take notice....and TONIGHT, the New Breed takes everyone to the school of Hard Knocks... For the past several months...I have been living in a dream land, and It's time that the NCWA's most demonic and devious individual wakes the "F" up and get's back to his roots... (Craven then begins to walk around the fire and the camera follows) Craven: This here represents the fire that now burns brightly within the hearts of the New Breed....The No Nonsense, take no prisoners, In Your Face... NEW BREED!!! I really and truly feel for those who dare to stand in our paths...FOR HELL IS NOW ON EARTH!!!!!! The Devil walks again...Not with that same cocky swagger....but, with a purpose and a complete intent to Erase any and all that attempt to take what is rightfully ours...and that is the title of being the baddest and single most destructive force in the NCWA... (Craven then makes his way back to the five men standing in front of the blazing fire) These men are those that have chosen to walk with the Devil down the Corridor's of Hell With The Devil...and those that will take the same path as we have previously followed... The Tricky Kid...he is dead and gone...The Devil on the other hand...that is the man that you all love to hate and fear.... A word to the wise...It is now time for many men in the NCWA to make a decision...are you with us...or against us...Those that are with us will reap the rewards of being a part of a FAMILY...Not a "stable" or faction...A FAMILY...and those that chose to stand in our paths on our way BACK to the top of the mountain.... Prepare to take a Walk Down the Corridor's of Hell....We will be your guides...and it ain't gonna be pretty Now, as for a man by the name of SHADES GALLOWAY....Shades...You were right...I was wrong...I was not the man that made you what you are...You are....I cannot take credit for your success...But, I will be responsible for the man you become after tonight....A beaten and batterred individual that will regret the day that he ever decided to turn his back on Derek Craven.... You are the very first man that has ever been able to muster up enough "sack" to do so...and now, You will have to be made an example of for future individuals that may consider on Crossing The New Breed... Your ignorance has spoken louder than your brave words...and now, it is time for you to give the Devil his Due!!!! and that will be your head above my fireplace...In many ways, It will be the hardest thing I have ever had to do...but, sometimes, people are just in the wrong place at the wrong time....and Shades...you are one of those people...Just Pray that your faithful friends of the X5 are near by, or this may, not only be the first time you walk the Corridor's of Hell as a foe of Craven...But...this may very well be your last time you ever, EVER WALK AGAIN.... Pander the fact that you should have been satisfied and content with where you were before you decided to walk on the other side of Craven's fence, and that you may very well be making your last trip to an arena as a competator... After tonight, Dillon....The Devil will own your soul... Beware Shades Galloway and the X5.... HELL IS ROUND THE CORNER!!!!!!!! (With that, Craven motions to the other men to follow him and they dissapear beyond the burning flames of the fire as the scene fades to darkness...)
