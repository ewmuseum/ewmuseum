NCWA fan board: "NCWA?"

21 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Anyone know what happened to this fed? It was hell of a lot of fun and it disappeared. If it is gone, I would like to join another fed similar to it. It was simulated with TNM 6.2 plus and your wrestler improved as he won matches using an experience point system. Every time he won, he would get a certain amount of experience points for the quality of the opponent and if he had a certain amount of experience points he could improve his stats. Anyone know of any feds run like this? I am 18 years old and a quality Role Player. I dont want to join any feds that have participants that dont generate heat or cant spell. In NCWA there was a couple people that sounded like they were nine years old. They would say stuff like "You are a bad person and a heal(bad spelling example) and I will hurt you". Now come on, If I were to roleplay this statement I would say something like "You are a piece of shit and after I have kicked your ass and you are lying destroyed and beaten in the center of the ring Im going keep beating you until YOU proclaim MY greatness" or something to that effect... LMK what you think [detail withheld] ps my wrestlers are Ravishing Rick (a singles wrestler) and a tag team called LEGEND (Savage and Perfect), yes they are rip offs of real wrestlers but what the hell? They are my favorites. :)
