NCWA: All Efeds (Attention Please)

18 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Allow me to introduce myself I am BrickHouse Brown former commentator for the NCWA. I have chosen to go my own direction in pursuit of starting the FIRST EVER *****EFED WEEKLY*****. whats is it you say...well I was sitting around one day Reading my favorite wrestling magazine And said You know wouldnt it be something if there was an on line Newsletter of some sort describing and rating different wresters in different leagues. Well anyways i figured this would be something fun and entertaining to read...you know a new twist to the same old efed biz. So If any Feds out there are interested in this Idea and would like to have your fed and wrestler in it included in this newsletter please email us at [detail withheld] PS If any individuals would like to recive a copy of this newsletter via email let us know as well.. PSS I know someone out there is going to say that most efeds have their own newsletter..But See this would be something for everyone to read not just your fed It would be a good way to get publicity to your fed..
