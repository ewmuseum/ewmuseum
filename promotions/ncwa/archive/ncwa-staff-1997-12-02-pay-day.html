NCWA: E-Wrestling = Pay Day.......

2 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey everyone its ol' Brickhouse here and I just had a brainstorm of an idea. Maybe it'll float maybe it won't. Anyways here it is....You have all seen the posts advertising those monthy tournaments right. Well barely anyone joins them and do you want to know why.....well whats the point you dont get nothing!! But what if there was actual money at stake. I mean ACTUAL money. That might interest you huh?? I know it interests me. Now like I said this may not float so I wont jump the gun and put together a web page and start advertising just yet. But I want everyones thoughts on this.....basically it would have to be simmed to keep it fair but flashing would have to play a major role in it as well. My idea would be that flashing would earn you points which you could divide between different catagories via WLS sim. TNM is good but not always fair in my opinon. I figure it would cost $5.00 to enter the tournament and you would have a chance to bring home $50.00 or more if you win the whole show and second place would pay out as well......... Anyhow this is still in the placebo state and I just want everyones thought, please email me with yours...... -- Brickhouse [detail withheld] NCWA " WHERE THE BAD BOYZ PLAY" http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Arena/1003/ncwa.html "If You Can't Run With Da Big Dog'z....Then Stay On The Freakin' Porch!!!!"
