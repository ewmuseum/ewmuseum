Nationwide Championship Wrestling Association Hotline Report 3/5/01 ----------------------------------------------------------> Scott Michaels: Hello! I'm Scott Michaels; thanks for calling the NCWA hotline report. It is now offical! The NCWA has returned. After being tied up in the courts for nearly 3 years, Brickhouse Brown Jr. has been given full rights to the federation that his father created. As many wrestling fans will remember, the Summer of 1998 was a dark one with the passing of
Brickhouse Brown Sr., then CEO and president of the NCWA. Unfortunatly, the
NCWA would be forced to close it doors shortly after Sr.'s passing due to some discrepenies in the estate. A judical tug of war would then begin between the family of Brickhouse Brown Sr. and NSE Inc., both of whom felt they were the rightful owners of federation.
With the NCWA back in the proper hands. Brickhouse Jr. is wasting no time in bringing the NCWA back to the main stream public. Already he has managed to stir up some controversy surrounding the announcement of an upcoming marriage of former NCWA superstar, Damian Rose a.k.a "Candyman".
Rose now a part of another nationally known federation, the UEW, is said to have agreed to be married on an upcoming NCWA live event which will be broadcast nationwide, March 25th on the FOX Sports network. Check your local listing for time.
When reached for comment, a representive of the UEW claimed that the announcement was a complete farce and that in fact, the UEW had verified the claim to be false with Rose himself. Still, Brickhouse Jr. insists that the wedding will take place as promised despite what has been said.
-------> Chris Thompson, NCWA talent coordinator, has been hard at work over the past two weeks signing new talent to the NCWA roster. Amongst those signing with the NCWA is said to be an handful of young up-n-comers from all around the independent circuits along with a few veteran superstars. The NCWA always seeking new talent and does currently have positions to fill. If you are interested in learning more, contact the NCWA at
[detail withheld] . Thanks again for calling the NCWA hotline report! Make sure to call again next week for more news from around the league. This is Scott Michaels saying SO LONG and Godspeed!
