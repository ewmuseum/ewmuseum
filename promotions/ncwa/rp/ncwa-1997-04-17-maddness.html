NCWA Maddness, 17 April 1997

17 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NCWA Thursday Mid-day Maddness (The camera pans over a compacity crowd in the Jackson Memorial Arena . Sirens are blaring and pyrotechnics are flaring as the NCWA logo appears in flames and camera zooms down to our commentators booth...) NORTH: Hello everyone and Welcome to Jackson Memorial Arena in Columbus Ohio...I am Mike North and with me today the one and only Brickhouse Brown... BRICKHOUSE: Hello everyone. We are in for a great day of wrestling action as we today will see who gets a shot at HEAT's tag title as well as the TV title on the line.. NORTH: A lot of great matches in store but right now lets get to the ring for some action.... MCLEMORE : Ladies and Gentleman this bout will be scheduled for one fall with a 15 min time limit....Introducing first in the ring to my right weighing in at 225lbs from MEXICO....MASKED MARVEL!!!! (Crowd erupts in a tremendous boo...) MCLEMORE: And making his way to the ring at this time...... (Welcome to the Jungle begins to play as the crowd cheers the Ohio native..) Weighing in at 275 lbs from BELLAIRE OHIO....ICE COLD SHAWN VALLORIC!!!!! NORTH: and Shawn Valloric making his debut in his home state of Ohio and what better a place to do so. BRICKHOUSE: Rumor has it that this youngster is full of talent and we'll get to see first hand him in action tonight. (Ref calls for bell...ding...ding) NORTH:And they're underway as both men lock up Valloric with a HEADLOCK TAKEDOWN and he has gained control over the Marvel....Marvel back to his feet and WHIPS Valloric into the ropes...Valloric coming back ducks under the CLOTHESLINE attempt and is coming back the other way and NAILS Marvel with a CLOTHESLINE of his own. Valloric with a STOMP to the head of Marvel and now grabs him and brings him to his feet.... BRICKHOUSE: EUROPEAN UPPERCUT by Valloric has the Marvel stunned....Valloric twist the arm into an ARMLOCK HOLD and drops the ELBOW on the locked up arm...Marvel grabbing a handful of hair now and Valloric releases the hold...Marvel WHIPS him into the ropes...Valloric on his way back..Marvel with a SUNSET FLIP but it fails...Valloric sits down and with the pin.. (Ref counts 1...2...3....ding...ding...ding) MCLEMORE: And here is your winner by pinfall in 7 mins/32secs.....ICE COLD SHAWN VALLORIC!!!!! (The crowd heers on as Valloric struts around playing to the crowd...) NORTH: And Ice Cold Valloric is successful in his debut match against the Masked Marvel and Brickhouse he appears to be full of Talent. BRICKHOUSE: That's right he controlled that match from the opening bell and he never looked back...He definatly looks to be a contender in the NCWA. NORTH: Well our next match up is underway and it appears that Fabio Erotic has no showed but Frank " The Biker" Ewiak has been scheduled in his place...so down to the ring we go... MCLEMORE: This match is scheduled for one fall w/ a 15 min time limit...INow making his way to the ring weighing inb at 225lbs from Great Britain.....VAN HART!!!!! (Crowd boos heavily as Van Hart makes his way down the isle taunting them) MCLEMORE: And being led to the ring at this time by THE GODFATHER...weighs in at 253lbs from Palm Bay Fl....FRANK THE BIKER EWIAK!!!!!!! (Bad to the Bone begins to play as the crowd cheers and jeers the newcomer) NORTH: Frank Ewiak in his debut tonight and we'll get a chance to see what this guys all about... (ref calls for bell...ding...ding) NORTH: And here we goes as Van Hart goes to the attack nailing Ewiak with kick to the mid section....RAKES the eyes now and Pushes him into the corner...Van hart swings and has his punched blocked and Ewiak Nails Van Hart with a hard right...Picks him up and SLAMS him down hard...LEG DROP across the face and picks him up...SUPLEX by Ewiak...STOMPS on Van Harts Head...Van Hart rises slowly and Frank the biker is signaling to the crowd...BIKERBOMB..with the cober (REF 1...2...3...ding...ding...ding) MCLEMORE: Here is your winner by pinfall in 4 mins 20 secs....FRANK THE BIKER EWIAK!!!!! (Bad to the bone plays as GODFATHER raises Ewiaks hand in victory....) NORTH: And Ewiak comes out and wins in quick fashion...I think he's trying to make a point here tonight... BRICKHOUSE: He made a point to Van Hart thats for Damn sure.... NORTH: Folks we have to cut to commercial but we'll be right back..... <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<NCWA FADES TO COMMERCIAL>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> MCLEMORE: This match is scheduled for one fall with a 20 min time limit....NO ONE WILL BE ALLOWED RINGSIDE!!!!! Introducing first.... (Mysyterio begins to play as the crowd rises to their feet in thunderous applause) Weighing 238 lbs ....H2O!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! MCLEMORE: And now making his way to the ring... (Eat the Rich plays as Sweet Ron appears through the smokey entry and procedes to taunt the crowd) weighing 255lbs from Dalls TX...SWEET RON BROWN!!!!!! NORTH: And here is the grudge match we've all been anticipating....No one allowed ringside so hopefully this will be a fair match up between two top quality wrestlers BRICKHOUSE: We'll see how long it last with no one ringside... (Ref calls for bell..ding...ding) NORTH: here we go both men locking up...Sweet Ron with a SINGLE LEG TAKEDOWN....Stomping on H2O's head now and DROPS an ELBOW... SWEET Ron shows off for a sec and now over to pick up H2O...H2O counters with a punch to the stomach and is back on his feet...WHIPS Sweet Ron into the ropes...Ron coming back and is NAILED with a big SPIN KICK... Brown is teetering and H2O nails him with a STANDING LARIAT and that puts him to his back... BRICKHOUSE: H2O with a LEG DROP and following now with a TOEHOLD SUBMISSION MOVE... Brown refuse to submit...sweet Ron sits up and gets H2O with an EYE GOUGE....Ron back to his feet and KICKS H2O in the back....H2O up and Sweet Ron nails him with a well placed DROP KICK.....Sweet Ron picks up H2O and WHIPS Him into the ropes...on his way back H2O Hits Brown with a FLYING BODY PRESS to a pin... (Ref 1...2...thr) NORTYH: Sweet Ron kicks out at 2 and a half.....H2O picks him up and Nails him with a KNEE BACKBREAKER.....H2O takes some time to strut for the fans... BRICKHOUSE: and thats a major mistake cause here Sweet Ron from behind...rolls him up... (ref 1...2...thr...) NORTH: Just does kick out and Sweet Ron Drops an ELBOW on H2O as he trys to get to his feet...H2O grabs a leg and trips Sweet Ron and gets him with a fist to the groin.... (Comotion within the crowd as Crippler appears in the entry way...) BRICKHOUSE: And the crowd to their feet and don't look now Crippler is standing in the isle...H2O has spotted him and goes to the second rope and is signaling for him to bring it on..... NORTH: H2O is distracted and here comes Sweet Ron from behind and NAILS HIM WITH A BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX TO A BRIDGEING PIN..... (REF 1...2...3...ding ...ding....ding) MCLEMORE: and here is your winner by pinfall in 15 min/32secs...SWEET RON BROWN!!!!!! (Crowd boos as Sweet Ron walks back down the Isle with his hands raised high in the air in victory...) NORTH: Oh man H2O suffers his first defeat of his career in the NCWA thanks to the Crippler and now Crippler is no where to be seen...needless to say H2O is not very happy as he is expressing to us right now. BRICKHOUSE: All I can say is that this Saturday they are gonna be bringing down the walls when those two meet.... NORTH: Folks we have to take a break but will return with hour two right after this.... <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<NCWA FADES TO AD FOR SLAMFEST 97>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> [continued] NCWA Thursday Mid-day Maddness hour 2 NORTH: Welcome back for hour #2 of Thursday Mid-Day Maddness. If you are just joining us you have missed a great first hour of action but don't worry cause we have two more great matches for you. Matter of fact lets go to the ring for one right now..... MCLEMORE: Ladies and Gentleman this match is scheduled for one fall with a 20 min time limit.....IT IS FOR THE NCWA TELEVISION TITLE!!!!! (Crowd ignites...) Introducing first weighing in at 229lbs from the Amazon Rain Forests being led to the ring by Bobby Lee....HURRICANE!!!! (Ultimo plays and the crowd rises to their feet with thunderous applause and Hurricane slaps some hands) MCLEMORE:And now making his way to the ring with MISSY...he weighs 235lbs and is from Chicago Illinois...THE NCWA TV CHAMP.....BIGGS CLEVELAND!!!!!!! (Generic rock music plays as The crowd cheers and jeers Biggs as he walks to the ring playing on to the crowd) NORTH: and this should be a great match between two young Talents in the league (Ref calls for bell...ding) NORTH: and here we go..Both men lock up and Biggs with an ARMBAR takedown...Locks in the hold and starts working on the arm of Hurricane....Hurricane starts to get back to his feet now and Biggs gives him a shot to the Solarplex...Biggs now with a HEADLOCK and Hurricane to his feet...Hurricane with a IRISH WHIP that sends Biggs into the ropes....coming off now and gets nailed with a SPIN KICK complements Hurricane. BRICKHOUSE: Hurricane now goes to work Dropping an ELBOW across the face of Biggs...grabs some hair and brings him to his feet...Biggs lands a couple of EUROPEAN UPPERCUTS to the face of Hurricane and now locks up with him...Hurricane WHIPS Biggs into the ropes..coming off and elevates Biggs high in the sky with a huge BACK DROP.....drops the Leg but Biggs rolls out of the way...Cleveland on the attack now STOMPING on the head of Hurricane and Drops the ELBOW across the face....biggs to the top rope now..hurricane to his feet and gets NAILED with a DOUBLE AXE HANDLE!!!! NORTH:Biggs now grabs a hold of Hurricane and WHIPS him cross corner...follows after and misses with The BODY SPLASH!!! Hurricane signaling to the crowd that he's had enough...Turns Biggs around...EYE OF THE HURRICANE..goes for the cover... (Ref 1...2...3...ding...ding...ding) MCLEMORE: and your winner by pinfall and NEW NCWA TELEVISION CHAMPION...HURRICANE!!!!! (Ref hands the belt to Hurricane and the crowd goes nuts with cheer for the New Champ) NORTH: And Hurricane is crowned new NCWA TV CHAMP...beating the seeminly unbeatable BIGGS Cleveland....and what a match it was. BRICKHOUSE: And now the new Champ has to get ready for Saturday Night's PPV cause he has to defend it against Stone Cold Scorpio well rumor has it at least... NORTH: thats right and Whoever he takes on you can bet they'll be prepared..Well lets not break the action lets get back down to the ring and find out who will get the shot at the TAG TITLES Sat. MCLEMORE: This is a Tag Team match up that is scheduled for one fall with no time limit... Introducing first being led to the ring by Bobby "The Brain" Heenan at a combined weight of 470 lbs...WAO!!!!!!! (WAO theme music begins to play as the compacity crowd boos their arrival and throws paper and cups at WAO and Heenan) MCLEMORE: And now making their way to the ring... (The sounds of fiercly blowing winds fill the Arena as the crowd comes to their feet to cheer) Led by Bobby Lee with a combined weight of 540lbs...THE NATURAL DISASTERS!!!!!! NORTH: And here they are the Natural disasters making their Debut along with the WAO and this match up looks to be a good one... BRICKHOUSE: No Doubt about it and what makes it really good is the fact that these two Stables are part of a big on going fued so you can bet your but theres gonna be some head Knockin' tonight ( Ref calls for bell ...ding...ding) NORTH: And here we go ..looks like Perfect and Typhoon will start things off... BRICKHOUSE: I'd say theres a bit of a size difference here!!!!! NORTH:Typhoon starts things off by giving Perfect a boot to the mid section and follows with an CHOP to the throat....Typhoon WHIPS Perfect into the ropes...coming back and Perfect gets NAILED with a Huge CLOTHESLINE....Perfect grabs a leg and trips Typhoon...Perfect to his feet along with Typhoon and they Lock Up....Typhoon with a big EUROPEAN UPPERCUT that sends Perfect back down to the mat...Typhoon tags in Tornado. BRICKHOUSE:Tornado in and DROPS the ELBOW down on Perfect...nails Perfect with a Big KNEE LIFT to the head that send Perfect stumbling back...Perfect tags SAVAGE...No wait the ref doesn't allow it..I dont see why not..Tornado grabs Perfect but Perfect fights back and lands a boot to the stomach...Perfect now gaining momentum and Nails Tornado with a BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX....Perfect attempts the LEG DROP but Tornado rolls out of the way.... NORTH: Tornado able to tag his partner and Perfect better get the tag soon....and he does..HERE COMES SAVAGE with a series of shots to the face of Typhoon and hes got the BIGMAN teetering...DROP KICK by Savage and Typhoon crashes to the Mat....Typhoon struggles to his feet as Savage bounces off the ropes...TYPHOON NEARLY DECAPITATED BY SAVAGE'S LEAPING CLOTHESLINE....Savage tags in Perfect...Perfect locking up with Typhoon and stuns him with a big KNEE LIFT....Perfect with a FRONT TO BACK KICK that put Typhoon down to one knee....DDT by Perfect and goes for the pin.. (REF 1...2...thr...) BRICKHOUSE: Not enough to keep Typhoon down but he needs the tag bad...and Perfect tags in Savage...Nice quick tags shows the experience of these two...Savage over to Typhoon and catches a HARD RIGHT to the nose and TYPHOON makes the tag!!!! Tornado in and locks up with Savage...Pushes Savage into the corner and now WHIPS him CROSS CORNER and Tags out...Typhoon back in grabs a leg and Takes Savage down...LEG DROP across the face...Picks up Savage and whips him into his partner...Perfect down on the floor...Typhoon picks up Savage lifts him up onto the shoulders ..Tags his partner....TORNADO with executes the MOONSAULT and that must be the NATURAL KILLER..the cover... (REF 1...2...3...ding...ding...ding) BRICKHOUSE: And that I believe was the Natural Killer and the NATURAL DISASTERS get the win... (Crowd erupts as Crippler comes running down to the ring.....) NORTH: OH NO!!!! here it goes as Crippler is in the ring and attacks Tornado...Perfect and Savage are all over Typhoon...The Natural Disasters need some help.... BRICKHOUSE: Speak of the devil here comes HURRICANE AND H2O!!!!! (LOAD ROAR FROM THE CROWD IS HEARD) NORTH: HURRICANE & H2O in the ring and are attacking the members of WAO now.. (Another outburst from the crowd..) NORTH: HERE COMES HEAT!!!!! We have an all out brawl in the ring as members of all three stables are in the ring.. BRICKHOUSE: We have WAO , EVIL ERADICATORS and THE BROTHERHOOD in an all out war in the ring and LOOK HERE...... ITS PULSATING PEPE JUMPING IN!!!! NORTH: FOLKS this is pure pandamonium here in the ring and I hate to say it but we are out of time...We'll see you This Saturday at the big Event ..this is Mike North for BRICK...... <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<NCWA FADES OUT>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
