NCWA Friday Night Slam, 5 April 1997

5 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NCWA Nationwide Championship Wrestling Association Here is a rundown of what happened on NCWA Fridaynight Slam....... In matchup #1:(singles comp. one fall , 20 min time) Stone Cold Scorpio(245lbs , Atlanta , Georiga) w/ Suga Baby VS. Midnight Marauder(230lbs , New Mexico) Stone Cold Attacked the Marauder right off the bell and landed a series of mid kicks followed by a vicious clothesline and a painful chicken winged back suplex.....Marauder was in trouble throughout the first half of the match but gained momentum after Scorpio attempted a double axehandle off the top rope which Marauder was able to counter by body slamming him off the top rope...Marauder then went on to perform a series of Acrobatic and high risk manuevers which left Scorpio stunned....It looked to be over when Marauder landed a round house kick on Scorpio after throwing him into the ropes..Scorpio was able to kick out just in time... North: You know Brickhouse this youngster Marauder has really been doing a number on Stone Cold ever since body slamming him off the top rope. Brickhouse: Very impressive youngster indeed but wasnt able to get the pin. North: Marauder picks up Scorpio now and Irish whips him into the ropes...Scorpio ducks under the clothesline and on his way back now...Sunset flip!!! Scorpio has the youngster in a pinning predictament..... (ONE , TWO , THREE...ding ding ding) Brickhouse: Oh my what a change of pace..Marauder clearly had Scorpio staggered but the Veteran came through when he needed too Tony Mclemore: Your winner by pinfall in 13 min. and 30 sec... " STONE COLD SCORPIO" (Back in Black fills the arena as Scorpio plays up the victory to the crowd) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- In matchup #2:(Tag Team comp. One fall , 20 min time) Team USA("Hacksaw"Donavan & Stars-n-Stripes...525lbs) VS. Ghetto Blasters(D-LO & De Vante....515lbs)w/Sister Sherri Before the match in the ring "Hacksaw" Grabbed the mic and had this to say..... Hacksaw: I represent the US of A and there's one thing I cant stand and that is the fact that we have these Gangster wanna be's like ghetto blasters running around acting like there tough when in fact they're decaying the US.... Ghetto Blasters had heard enough and attacked Team USA before the Hacksaw could say another word. When the ref got them seperated and started the match D-lo and Hacksaw were in first and Hacksaw jumped to the advatange first bringing D-Lo down to his knees with pure strength. Hacksaw tags in Stars-n-stripes which landed a huge back breaker on D-LO followed by an earth shaking power slam. When D-Lo was finally able to tag in Devante he came out with the adrenaline pumping and cleared the ring of TEAM USA..where an all out brawl continued on the floor... North: We now have mayhem outside the ring...Devante and Sherri are working over Hacksaw on the far side while the REF's attention is on the near side where Hacksaw and D-LO are going at it Brickhouse: Oh man Hacksaw just introduced DLO's head to the ring post...and now gives him a taste of the stairs and D-Lo is OUT!!!! North: Whats this???Sherri is up on the mat yelling at the ref...now the ref trying to get sher.....OH MY Devante nails Hacksaw with a chair and now climbs back into the ring complaining to the ref for not counting... Brickhouse: Did you see what happened to Stars-n-Stripes he's out cold.... (Ref counts ONE, TWO ,THREE...) North: No I didnt see but this doesnt look good for TEAM USA.... (SEVEN, EIGHT , NINE , TEN.....ding ding ding) Mclemore: Here are your winners by way of count out , in 7 min 32 sec...THE GHETTO BLASTERS North: Well The Ghetto Blasters continue their way to the top of the rankings...to bad that they have had to cheat 3 of the 4 times they've won though Brickhouse: It's not Cheating its called intuitive.. North:You probably don't even know what that means.... ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- Matchup #3 MAIN EVENT (Singles Comp. , one fall , 30 min time ,TV title) **"Biggs" Cleveland(235lbs , Chicago , Il) vs. TAURUS(245lbs , Hawaii) The match started out slowly but gained momentum as TAURUS showed his talent by taking Biggs by suprise with a belly-to-belly suplex followed by a Savat kick which sent Biggs over the top rope. Shortly after Biggs reversed the situation by landing a big drop kick to TAURUS which he followed with a running bulldog and then a front face suplex. Things continued back and forth with each man proving his abilities but in the end...... North: Brickhouse this has been one hell of a match. Brickhouse: That's right North each of these individuals have proven that they are both worthy title contenders. North: Cleveland now picking up the stunned TAURUS and Irish Whip into the ropes ...GUILLENTINE this one is over. (Ref: ONE , TWO , THREE.......Ding, ding, ding) Mclemore:Here is your winner by way of pinfall in 12 min 45 sec and STILL TELEVISION CHAMPION "BIGGS" CLEVELAND....... Brickhouse: When he lands that finisher you know its lights out and Cleveland retains the title again. North: Well folks thanks for tuning into Friday Night Slam make sure and join us for Tuesday Night Livewire our 2 hour spectacular. Once again for Brickhouse Brown I am Mike North and we'll see ya when when we see ya. Goodnight
