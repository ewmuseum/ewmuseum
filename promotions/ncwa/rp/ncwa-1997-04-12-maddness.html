NCWA Maddness, 12 April 1997

12 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NCWA MADDNESS: Special Edition (NCWA logo appears as the scene of a jam packed arena of Diehard NCWA fans cheer the start of MAYHEM) ( Lazers Smoke and Pyrotechnics can be seen) NORTH: Hello fans and welcome to a jam packed Douglas Memorial Arena here in Charleston NC....I am Mike North and my partner tonight..."Divine"Eddie Roderiguez. EDDIE: Thanks Mike..Well tonights the night that we find out who will be going for what title at the upcoming SPRING SLAMFEST 97.... NORTH: I should add that Maddness usaully on Thursday Night but due to the importance of tonights matches it was moved to Friday this week only.... EDDIE: And they seem to be ready in the ring..Lets go to Tony Mclemore..... MCLEMORE: Ladies and Gentleman...WELCOME TO A SPECIAL EDITION OF NCWA MADNESS!!!! (Crowd ignites w/ a huge roar) MCLEMORE: Introducing first..... (Eye of the Tiger plays....as the crowd breaks out in a huge BOO) He's from Newfoundland Canada.....weighing 265lbs...THE CRIPPLER!!!!! MCLEMORE:And now making his way to the ring...weighing 250lbs from Fort Worth , TX....STAR-N-STRIPES!!!!! (Star Spangle Banner plays as the crowd cheers on the American) North: Stars-N-Stripes making only his second singles debut has a big task ahead of him today with the Crippler....winner advances loser goes home... (Ref calls for the bell ding...ding) NORTH: and we're underway...Crippler with a SINGLE LEG TAKEDOWN and adds a STOMP to the head of S-n-S....crippler going to the ropes early...waits on S-n-S...Misses with the DOUBLE AXE HANDLE and catches a fist to the mid-section....S-n-S sets him up with the SUPLEX and down he goes...S-n-S picks up the Crippler and gets shoved into the corner...Crippler on the offensive... unable to force feed S-n-S the turnbuckle and Stars makes some room for himself by shoving the Crippler. EDDIE: S-n-S IRISH WHIP into the ropes and CRIPPLER almost decapitated by that CLOTHESLINE...S-n-S goes for the pin... (REF 1 , 2...) NORTH: Only a two count...Crippler catches a boot to the face...S-n-S going to the Top rope but Crippler is up...POWER SLAM from the top rope!!! Crippler with the cover.. (REF 1 , 2 ,thr....) EDDIE: Kicks out at two and a half...and Crippler picks up the stunned S-n-S...NAILS him with the BRAIN BUSTER!!! Does'nt go the pin instead picks him up and BRAIN BUSTER again!!!! With the cover.. (REF 1 , 2 , 3...ding...ding...ding) MCLEMORE: And here is your winner by pinfall in 13 mins/22 secs...CRIPPLER!!!! (crowd boos loudly as Crippler taunts them) NORTH: Well Crippler advances to round 2 and S-n-S goes home a loser. EDDIE: Well 2 BRAIN BUSTERs and it was icing on the cake... NORTH: They're ready the ring for match #2.... MCLEMORE:This match is scheduled for one fall...Making his way to the ring at this time... (Dirty Deeds plays over the PA.. Crowd cheers and jeers) weighing 215lbs from Great Britian.....VAN HART!!!!! MCLEMORE: and now being escorted by H.S and BODYGUARD...weighing in at 238lbs..H2O!!!! (Mysterio plays as the crowd comes to they're feet cheering on H2O which plays on to them) (Ref calls for bell....ding..ding) NORTH: and no time wasting as this one gets underway.....Both men in the center of the ring..VAN HART with an EYE GOUGE.. (Ref warns Van Hart) NORTH: H2O locking up with Van Hart.. knee lift to the midsection by H2O...follows it up with a powering BODY SLAM...H2O picking up the stunned Van Hart...STANDING LARIAT!!!! EDDIE: Wow what power he posseses...and Now H2O to the top rope and Nails Van Hart with the DOUBLE AXE HANDLE....van Hart down and H2O drops a leg across the face...Van Hart back to his feet now and h2O misses with the LARIAT and Van Hart hits him with a DROP KICK to the back....Van hart LEG DROP..H2O attempte to trip Hart but can't and Van Hart stomps on his head... NORTH: Van hart with some momentum now...Van Hart picks up the stunned Big Man...FRANKENSTEINER by Van Hart...going for the pin.. (REF 1 , 2 ....) NORTH: No H2O kicks out...Van Hart grabs a handful of hair and brings him to his feet...Van hart with a big right hand and now bouncing off the Ropes coming at H2O....h2O ducking under the CLOTHESLINE..Van Hart coming back now and H2O elevates him into the lights with a huge BACKDROP!!!1 EDDIE: I felt that over here...Stomp on the head by H2O and now drops that massive leg across the face....another stomp followed with an ELBOW DROP....H2O picking up van hart...IRISH WHIP into the ropes...Van Hart coming off and nails H2O with a well placed DROP KICK....goes for the cover.. (REF 1 , 2 , thr....) NORTH: just in time with a kickout.....van Hart stomping on H2O and H2O rolls outside of the ring to catch his breath... (BodyGuard is there helping out his friend....) NORTH: h2O back into the ring now...H2O with a kick to the mid-section followed by a right hook and Van Hart hits the mat.... EDDIE:Van Hart struggles to his feet...H2O nails him with the LAZER BEAM...lights out (REF 1 , 2 , 3 ...ding...ding...ding) MCLEMORE: Your winner by way of pinfall ... in 24mins/42secs ...H2O!!!!! (Crowd cheers H2O as Bodyguard and H.S raise his hands in victory) NORTH: There we got our first look at the LASER BEAM and man is it devastating...Folks we have to go to Commercial but we will be right back.... (NCWA AD FOR SPRING SLAMFEST 97 runs...) NORTH: Welcome back fans we are ready for match up #3 lets go to the ring... MCLEMORE:In the ring at this time weighing in at 225lbs from MEXICO..THE MASKED MARVEL!!!!! (Erotica begins to play....Crowd roars with anticipation) MCLEMORE:And now making his way to the ring...from Orlando ,Fl ...weighing 234lbs...escorted by FIFI...FABIO EROTIC!!!!! NORTH: Here will be our first look at the newest member of the NCWA Fabio Erotic. EDDIE: From what I hear he is a great all around wrestler but FiFi is the reason he has won so many matches in other leagues. (REF CALLS FOR BELL..DING) NORTH: Well we are gonna find out here tonight....Masked Marvel strikes first with a SINGLE LEG TAKEDOWN....and procedes to choke Fabio.. (ref warns Marvel) NORTH: Fabio MONKEY FLIPS the Marvel...and there he goes to the outside...Fabio follows him to the floor...Marvel hurries back in and Fabio does the same...Both men lock up..Fabio WHIPS Marvel into the ropes and follows...FABIO GOES FLYING OVER THE TOP as Marvel ducks under the clothesline his attempt...Marvel goes to the floor and nails him with a right cross...Marvel slamming Fabios head into the ring post and throwing him back into the ring. EDDIE: Marvel now slides back in and picks up the stunned Fabio. (FIFI climbs on to the ring apron and distracts the REF) EDDIE: Whats this FIFI distracting the ref...Marvel nails Fabio with the SUPER KICK and goes for the pin but the ref isnt seeing it... Marvel gets up and goes after FIFI but she hops down off the ring...Marvel now arguing with the ref and FABIO from behind....BRIDGING SUPLEX hes got him pinned... (REF 1 , 2 ,3 ...ding...ding...ding) MCLEMORE: Your winner is ..by pin in 8mins/35secs...FABIO (Crowd cheers as Fifi and Fabio blow kisses into the Crowd) NORTH: Fabios first match is a success but as you mentioned Eddie he got a little bit of help from FIFI... EDDIE: I guess the rumors we're true about her.. NORTH: Nevertheless we are about to get the final match in round 1 under way...heres Tony M with the call... MCLEMORE: This match scheduled for one fall..no time limit...Introducing First hailing From The Amazon Rain Forests of South America...he weighs in at at 229lbs...HURRICANE!!!!! (Ultimo plays as the crowd expresses mix emotions toward Hurricane) NORTH: The crowd definatly showing some dislike towards the Big Man..I imagine it has something to do with his recent actions. EDDIE: Well Hurricane came into the league as a major fan favorite but I think they feel he has strayed away from his good guy image.. (Frances national Anthemes blasts troughout the Arena as the fans roar with excitement) MCLEMORE:And now making his way to the ring ..from Paris France....PULSATING PEPE!!!! NORTH: And heres a guy who has made an enormous impact on the fans since he entered the league...but he ha s a big hurdle to leap here tonight.... (BELL SOUNDS...DING) NORTH: Both men lock up...Hurricane bloocks a SUPLEX attempt by Pepe and takes him down with a FIREMAN'S CARRY...Pepe smacks Hurricane with a boot in the face...back on his feet and elevates Hurricane with a SUPLEX..showing his strength holding him there and down...pepe picking him back up and BODYSLAMS him down again...Pepe drops the ELBOW..rolls him into a BOSTON CRAB!!!! EDDIE: Hurricane refusing to give up...Pepe releases the hold and picks up hurricane by the hair and introduces him face first to the turn buckle....Hurricane with a reversal and HIP TOSSES Pepe out of the corner...Pepe quickly to his feet..and nails him with a kick to the mid section and hurricane down to a knee. NORTH: Pepe takes time to strut for the fans and Hurricanerolls him up from behind... (REF 1 , 2 ...) NORTH: Only a two count, but almost a costly mistake by Pepe...hurricane bounces off the ropes...Pepe ducks under the clothesline on his way back..SUNSET FLIP!!! (REF 1 , 2 , thr...) EDDIE: kicks out at 2 and a half...Pepe nails Hurricane with a wicked KNEE and drops the ELBOW on him....Pepe to the top rope but Hurricane up in time and punches him off...Hurricane picking up the stunned Pepe and nails him with the EYE OF THE HURRICANE!!! (Ref 1 , 2 , thr...) NORTH: Unbelievable!!! Pepe kicks out barely in time....Hurricane picks up Pepe..Trys for the EYE OF THE HURRICANE but cant get it this time...pepe smacks Hurricane in the head with a knee lift...Hurricane is stunned...Pepe execute the POWER BOMB!!!! (Ref 1 , 2 ,3 ..ding...ding...ding) MCLEMORE: Here is your winner by pinfall in 36 mins/43secs...PULSATING PEPE!!!! (Frances national Anthemes plays and the crowd produces a Thunderous roar of approval for the French man) Eddie: What a battle that was between two Titans of the NCWA. As good as both men performed its to bad one had to lose.. NORTH: I have a feeling that we will see these two in the ring before long...And thats it for round one...we have to pause for a minute but we will be back for the start of Round two in a few minutes... (NCWA FADES TO COMMERCIALS......) [continued] NCWA Maddness: Part 2 NORTH: Welcome back fans we are getting ready to kickoff round 2 of this special tournament here tonight but before we do lets look at the matches as they have unfolded. EDDIE: Starting things off we have Crippler vs Pulsating Pepe followed by H2O vs. Fabio Erotic and remember the winners of these two will face each other in Spring Slamfest 97 as the two loser will go at it once more tonight and that winner will have the option of going after any of the other 3 belts at Spring Slamfest. NORTH: and they are ready in thr ring..Let's check it out... MCLEMORE:This is scheduled for one fall/no time limit....Introducing first from Newfoundland Canada...weighing 265lbs....THE CRIPPLER!!!!! (Eye of the Tiger plays as Crippler walks the Isle yelling and Taunting the booing crowd) MCLEMORE: And now making his way to the ring..... (Fances Natinal Anthemes plays as the crowd Cheers on Pepe) weighing 310lbs ..from Paris France...PULSATING PEPE!!! NORTH: Man this should be a good one. We have two giant men in the ring with one thing on their mind's..WINNING!!! (Ref calls for bell..ding..ding) NORTH: Crippler attacks first with sharp jab and a kick to the midsection..locking up with Pepe...Pepe whips Crippler into the ropes...but catches a boot in the face after a failed BACK DROP....Crippler now with a LEG DROP but Pepe rolls out of the way....Pepe now choking the Crippler (Ref Warns Pepe) EDDIE:Both men up and Lock together...Crippler raise Pepe up with the SUPLEX and down he goes and now Crippler choking Pepe.. Ref breaks it up...both men to their feet..Crippler off the ropes...Pepe sits on him after a failed SUNSET FLIP. (REF 1 , 2....) North: Only holds for two...Crippler with a knee to the midsection and nails Pepe with the BRAIN BUSTER!!!! (Ref 1 , 2 , thr...) NORTH: Pepe kicks out at two and a half....Pepe now picks up Crippler and BODYSLAMS him hard down to the mat...picking up the Crippler and nails him with a STANDING DROPKICK follows with a stomp to the head....Pepe to the top rope...down on top of Pepe with a big FLYING ELBOW......Crippler up and swinging wildly and falls back down on his face. EDDIE: Pepe stomping on Cripplers head and now back to the top rope...Misses with the DOUBLE AXEHANDLE..Crippler whips Pepe into the ropes...Nails him with a hard CHOP to the throat...Crippler to the top rope now...MISSES with a would be BODY SPLASH....Pepe picks up the stunned Crippler ..IRISH WHIP into the ropes...OH Pepe complete flip from CRIPPLERS CLOTHES LINE...Crippler picking up Pepe..Signals to the crowd..BRAIN BUSTER... (REF 1 , 2 , 3..ding...ding...ding) MCLEMORE:Your winner by pinfall in 19 mins/17 secs....CRIPPLER!!! (Crowd boo's in disgust) NORTH: and what an upset that was Crippler just defeated the 2nd ranked wrestler in the league...and Pepe will have to hope to win the match for 3rd place to keep his hopes for a title alive ....we will be right back (NCWA fades to ad for Slamfest 97) MCLEMORE: This match is one fall/no time limit..introducing first weighing 234lbs..from Orlando Florida....escorted by FIFI...FABIO EROTIC!!!! (Erotica plays as the Crowd exploads with a cheerful roar) MCLEMORE: Now making his way to the ring..... (Mysterio plays and the fans once again come to their feet to cheer) Weighing 238lbs ..being led by H.S and Bodyguard...H2O!!!! North: This should be an explosive match up here tonight..both men contenders and wanting some Gold..So here we go... (Ref calls for bell..ding) NORTH: H2O locks up with Fabio..Fabio with a whips H2O into the ropes...DROP KICK by Fabio..Fabio attempts to lock on a FIGURE FOUR but H2O kicks him and he goes to the outside....fabio grabbing the leg of H2O and draging him out ...both men lock up..after blocking Fabios attempt H2O rams his head into the ring post..Fabio with an EYE GOUGE and Slams the Big man to the hard concrete floor...Stomps on his head and Fabio slides back into the ring....H2O staggers up and slides in as well. EDDIE:Both men now exchanging punches...and now Fabios IRISH WHIP into the ropes is reversed and he goes into thr ropes...coming back now....Leaps at H2O but H2O catches him in mid air...BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX pinning combo... (REF 1 , 2 , 3 ding...ding...ding) MCLEMORE: AND your winner by pinfall....H2O!!!! (Crowd cheers on as H2O plays the victory on to the crowd) (FIFI coming into the ring to comfort Fabio but H2O grabs her by the hair and pulls her back) (Body Guard grabs on contains a mad Fabio..H2O with a mic) H2O: FABIO we can be allies but drop the stupid loser. She is evil you are good and dont even think about attacking me in a fit of love and anger. Body Gaurd will stop you with his love. MRS. CROW BAR. (H2O throws Fifi down to the mat and exits the ring.....) NORTH: Whoa what a turn of events H2O obviously disliking FIFI and showing it here tonight. EDDIE: I don't know the whole story but could there be a little jealousy amongst them??? North: Nevertheless H2O has earned a title shot against the Crippler in the Slamfest 97 and we have one match remaining tonight and that is to determine 3rd place so lets go right back to the ring... MCLEMORE: This is for one fall/no time limit..it is for 3rd place in tonights tourament...Still in the ring at this time w/ FIFI...FABIO EROTIC!!!!! (Crowd cheers as Fabio and FIFI play on to them) MCLEMORE:And now making his way to the ring..... (Fances Natinal Anthemes plays as the crowd Cheers on Pepe) weighing 310lbs ..from Paris France...PULSATING PEPE!!! NORTH: I'd have to say Fabio is to a disadvantage as far as stamina due to that big match with H2O...but were getting under way (Ref calls for bell...ding) EDDIE: Both men lock up..Fabio smacks Pepe with a wicked KNEELIFT...Fabio trying to pick up Pepe but can't so instaed gets him with an EYEGOUGE... (Ref warns Fabio) NORTH: Fabio whips Pepe into the ropes..Pepe nails him with a RUNNING ELBOW SMASH.....Fabio is down...Pepe boot to the head...LEG DROP by Pepe...Picks up the stunned Fabio ...NECKBREAKER and Fabio is in trouble...Once again Pepe picking up fabio...Pepe bouncing off the ropes but FIFI grabs a leg and down he goes...Fabio still staggerring in the center of the ring..I dont think he knows where he is... EDDIE: Pepe is up and goes to the ropes trying to get hold of FIFI...Fabio sees it finally and from behind rolls him up....But Pepe reverses the pin...hes got him in a CRADLE... (REF 1 , 2 , 3 ...ding, ding ,ding) MCLEMORE: The winner of the match by pinfall....PULSATING PEPE!!!!! (Crowd cheers on the hero) NORTH: And Pepe pulls out the win for third despite a little trouble from FIFI and now he will get his choice of either going for the US , TV or NATIONAL Title at Spring Slamfest 97.. North: Folks we are out of time here..but make sure to tune into SATURDAYNIGHT SLAM tommorow night. For Eddie Roderiguez I am Mike North saying GOOD NIGHT!!!
