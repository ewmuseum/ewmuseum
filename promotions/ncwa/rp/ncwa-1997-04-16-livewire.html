NCWA Livewire, 16 April 1997

16 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NCWA TUESDAY NIGHT LIVEWIRE (Hour 1) (NCWA logo appears in flames as Livewire gets under way) (Scence opens to a jammed pack Spectra Arena and the usaul lights,smoke and pyrotechnics can be seen...Camera fade to commentators) NORTH: Hello everyone and welcome to another Tuesday Night Livewire...I am Mike North and with me tonight..."Divine" Eddie Roderiguez. Eddie: Thanks Mike...and hello fans. Tonight is definatly gonna blow you away..we have tons of newcomers to the league and we are gonna see some of them here tonight... NORTH: That's right along with some of the NCWA finest...Well lets get it going and down to the ring we go... MCLEMORE: Ladies and Gentleman this matchup is scheduled for one fall w/ a 15 min time limit...introducing first in the ring to my right...he hails from Great britain and weighs in at 225lbs...VAN HART!!!! (crowd boos loudly) MCLEMORE: and being led to the ring at this time by The Godfather... (African Jungle music plays...as the crowd comes to their feet to see who it is..) weighing 415lbs...from The African Savanah..THE AFRICAN NIGHTMARE!!!! NORTH: WOW!! Take a look at this guy!!! EDDIE: I sure do feel sorry for VAN HART right about now..... (REF CALLS FOR BELL ding...ding) North: and we are underway..Nightmare locks up w/Van Hart...Van Hart sticks a thumb into his eye... (REF warns Van Hart) NORTH: Van Hart WHIPS the stunned African into the ropes...on his way back and CLOTHESLINE by Van Hart but it barely stuns the huge African...Van Hart now introducing The Nightmare to a KNEE LIFT to the head...Nightmare still on his feet and Van hart bounces off the ropes..Hart duck under Nightmares CLOTHESLINE on his way back and Nightmare nails him with a HEADBUTT!!! Eddie: OUCH that had to of hurt....Nightmare drops the elbow but Van Hart moves out of the way..Van Hart picks up the African whips him cross corner but no Nightmare Reverses it and WHIPS Van Hart into the corner and FOLLOWS with a huge SPLASH...VAN HART falls down to the mat..African Nightmare with the cover... (REF 1 , 2 , 3...ding , ding , ding) MCLEMORE: Here is your winner by pinfall in 4mins/23secs...AFRICAN NIGHTMARE!!!!!! (Jungle music plays as the Crowd cheers on the African) NORTH: And in his debut the African Nightmare gets the victory all be it in easy fashion... EDDIE: Van Hart definatly out sized but he gave it his all but unable to beat the Huge African..... NORTH: Eddie we are about to see another newcomer here to the NCWA wonder what he'll be bringing to the ring with him talent wise... EDDIE: Rumor has it that he is a very talented individual and also is part of the highly talented WAO stable.. NORTH: Well lets go to the ring... MCLEMORE: This match up is scheduled for one fall w/ a 15 min time limit...Introducing first (Perfect's Theme begins to play and the crowd shows mixed emotions toward him) weighing 240lbs from Alberta Canada being led by Bobby Heenan and Crippler...MR PERFECT!!!! (MR PERFECT tells the crowd to rise up and cheer him and takes time to pose to the crowd) MCLEMORE: And now making his way to the ring... (High Flyer begins to play...as the crowd cheers on) >From New Mexico...weighs in at 230lbs....MIDNIGHT MARAUDER!!!!!! NORTH: and this should be a good match as we pit Technician vs. Technician here tonight... (BELL ding ...ding) NORTH: There's the bell and Marauder on the attack and nails Perfect w/ a jab...Marauder dodges Perfect's boot and locks up with him......Perfect WHIPS Marauder into the ropes..coming back and IS NEARLY DECAPITATED BY PERFECTS CLOTHESLINE!!!! EDDIE: Big Time ringer there....Perfect piks up the Marauder...Marauder whips Perfect cross ring in the turnbuckles and goes to the top rope....Perfect turns around and Marauder flips onto HIM WITH A MOONSAULT!!!!! NORTH:Marauder dropping the ELBOW onto Perfect and picks him up...Locking up now...Pefect pushing Marauder into the corner to the second rope and is POUNDING AWAY... (Crowd counts 1 , 2 , 3, 4 ,5....Ref breaks it) NORTH: The ref breaks it up....Marauder HIP TOSSED out and Perfect stomps on his head...Picks him up and SLAMS him back down....Marauder struggles to his feet...Perfect bounces off the ropes coming back and is ELEVATED BY MARAUDERS BACK DROP and down he goes and so does Marauder.... (REF counts 1...2...3...4...5...6...) EDDIE: Both men to their feet...Perfect over and CHOKES Marauder (Ref warns not to do it again) EDDIE: Marauder with a EUROPEAN UPPERCUT and another Perfect goes down....Marauder signals to the Crowd and goes to the top rope.....Perfect up and GETS NAILED WITH THE WHIRRLY BIRD..... (Ref Counts 1...2...thr...) North: Kicks out just in time...I cant believe he did kick out....Perfect to the outside now. EDDIE: Perfect grabs a leg and brings out Marauder...Both men outside the ring...Both men lock up...Marauder WHIPS Perfect into the guard rails..and slides back into the ring...Perfect follows..Marauder with a SIDE KICK and a FRONT KICK....Marauder trys to WHIP Perfect into the ropes but Perfect reverses it...Marauder coming back and lands THE FLYING BODY PRESS!!! (Ref 1..2..) NORTH: Perfect kicks out on 2...Heenan now yelling something to Marauder..Marauder goes to the outside after Heenan...Chasing Heenan around the outside of the ring.. EDDIE: Crippler just grabbed a chair and tossed ot into Perfect!!!!!! NORTH: Heenan slides into the ring...Marauder follows and OHHH!!!! PERFECT NAILS HIM WITH THE CHAIR!!!! (Ref calls for the bell ding...ding...ding) NORTH: The bell has sounded but Perfect covers Marauder and insists the ref count and the ref does reluctantly.... MCLEMORE:Your winner by Disqualifacation in 13 mins/10 secs.....MIDNIGHT MARAUDER!!!! (Perfect and Heenan raise each others hand as Marauder lay out cold in the center of the ring...) (The crowd boos Perfect...) NORTH: Well Marauder wins but Perfect raises his hands as of to say that he is the Winner...Folks we have to cut to Commercial we will be right back... (NCWA fades to Slamfest Advertisment) NORTH: Welcome back folks if you are just joining us you have missed a couple of great matches but don't worry we have more in store...Down to the ring... MCLEMORE:This match is scheduled for one fall...Introducing first...weighing 200lbs from Austin TX...THE TERROR!!!! (King Nothing plays as the crowd boos the Terror) NORTH: The Terror in only his 3rd debut has been a QUITE upcomer here in the NCWA...today will have a real challenge though.... MCLEMORE: And Making his way to the ring being led by Bobby Heenan...weighs in at 225lbs ....RAVISHING RICK!!!!! (Crowd Cheers on Rick as he stops and plays to them with a pose down...) NORTH: Ravishing Rick in his 1st debut already seems to be a fan favorite here in the NCWA.. EDDIE: well at least with the ladies he is...It'll be interesting to see how Fabio Erotic a ladies man in his own mind takes to this newcomer.... (Bell sounds ding....ding) NORTH: and here we go as Rick punches the Terror and Terror responds with a combnation of his own...WHIPS Rick into the ropes and NAILS him with a well placed DROP KICK!!! Rick slow to his feet...Terror off the ropes and Rick covers him after a failed SUNSET FLIP!!! (ref 1..) EDDIE: only a 1 count...Ravishing up to his feet.... (Comotion in the Crowd as a big Aaaw is heard...) NORTH: Terror now Grabs a handful of hair...WAIT A MINUTE WHATS THIS???? (A Tall muscular blonde haired man nails Crippler and slides into the ring...) NORTH:Who is this??? EDDIE: Whoever it is he is going after Ravishing Rick and hes all over him...Rick taken Totally by suprise... (Crowd to their feet) (Ref calls for the bell ding...ding...ding) NORTH: AND here comes Perfect into the ring...going after this guy...they are exchanging right hands and the stranger is down and to the outside and heading back to the locker room...Crippler is still down outside the ring and now Perfect is looking at Terror and Terror is saying that he had nothing to do with it...Perfect pointing a finger into Terror's face and Terror pointing back..Nevertheless Terror will win by DQ and now Heenan in the middle of the two and folks we have to go to commercials but we'll be right back for hour two don't go away!!!! NCWA LIVEWIRE FADES TO COMMERCIALS...... [continued] NCWA TUESDAY NIGHT LIVEWIRE HOUR 2 NORTH:Welcome back to Livewire we are getting ready to start hour two but first lets take a look back at hour one.... EDDIE: Hour one was filled with excitement as African Nightmare beats Van Hart , Midnight Marauder defeats Mr. Pefect by DQ and A stranger to the NCWA showed up in Terror match and beat up on Crippler and Ravishing Rick....Terror wins by DQ. NORTH: Well we are ready in the ring for what looks to be a huge match up..... MCLEMORE:This match is scheduled for one fall w/ a 20 min time limit...THERE WILL BE NO DISQUALIFACTIONS....Introducing first.... (Eye of the Tiger plays as the crowd cheers and jeers) >From Newfoundland Canada...weighing 265lbs escorted by Mr. Perfect......CRIPPLER!!!!! (Crippler and Perfect taunt the crowd) MCLEMORE: And now making his way to the ring....weighing in at 229lbs from The Amazon Rain Forests w/ manager H.S.......HURRICANE!!!!!!! (Ultimo plays as the Crowd rises to there feet with thunderous cheering...) NORTH: This is a match that Crippler asked for and tonight he'll get his shot.. (Ref calls for the bell...ding) NORTH: And here they go...Both men lock up and Hurricane pushes Crippler into the corner...Crippler is able to keep from eating the turnbuckle...and he pushes Hurricane back...Locking up again and Hurricane WHIPS Crippler cross corner ...Charging at him...Crippler able to get that big boot of his up and knocks Hurricane silly. EDDIE:Crippler drops the ELBOW down on Hurricane...and picks him up...Hurricane witha boot to the midsection and Picks up and SLAMS CRIPPLER OVER THE TOP ROPE!!!!! NORTH: Crippler slow to his feet and takes a minute to catch his breath...slides back in the ring....Hurricane nails him with a boot to the face...Hurricane signals to the crowd....NAILS HIM WITH THE EYE OF THE HURRICANE!!!! The cover... (Ref 1, 2, 3...ding ding ding) EDDIE : and here comes Perfect into the ring and he's all over Hurricane...series of right hands by Perfect...WHIPS Hurricane into the ropes and CLOTHESLINE....H.s in the ring now and Crippler grabs ahold of him....BRAINBUSTER and now over to Hurricane...BRAINBUSTER's him now...and finally Perfect and Crippler leave the ring...Hurricane gets the win but pays a heavy price for it....man o man!!! North: Officals over to help Hurricane and HS out of the ring...and now it looks like Evil Eradicators will now have to contend not only with HEAT'S Brotherhood but Heenan's WAO... EDDIE: I have a feeling we better prepare for World War III here in the NCWA!!! NORTH: Well we are ready for tonight's main event..... MCLEMORE:This match up is scheduled for one fall with a 30 min time limit...Intoducing... (Frances National Anthemes plays and the crowd to their feet to cheer on the frenchman) weighing 310lbs from PARIS FRANCE......PULSATING PEPE!!!!! MCLEMORE: And his opponent being led to the ring by Lady Divine....hes from Atlanta Georiga and weighs in at 260lbs....STONE COLD SCORPIO!!!! (Back in Black plays on as the crowd ignites with excitement for Scorpio and Lady Divine) NORTH: and this will prove a great match up tonight as we see two of the leagues top guys go at it here tonight.... (ref calls for the bell....ding...ding) NORTH : and we're underway...And Pulsating Pepe right away with a STANDING LARIAT that buts Scorpio on his back...drops the elbow on Scorpio..Scorpio grabs a leg and trips Pepe...Scorpio up and Stomps on Pepe's head and again...to the top rope he goes...waits on Pepe...Scorpio Misses with the DOUBLE AXE HANDLE and Pepe punches him in the stomach. EDDIE: Pepe now with a SUPLEX and Scorpio hits the mat hard....Pepe to the top rope..Lady Divine shakes the ropes but he keeps his balance and leaves Scorpio teetering with a huge FOREARM SMASH...Scorpio blocks the POWER BOMD attempt and counters with a huge BACK DROP...Stomps on Pepe's head...Scorpio hits hard after Pepe rolled out of the way of his LEG DROP... NORTH: Pepe picks up Scorpio...and is rolled up in a SMALL PACKAGE..but Scorpio cant even hold it for a one count....Pepe shoved into the corner and Scorpio pounds away on him... (Crowd counts 1...2...3..4..5....) NORTH:Ref over to break it up...Pepe locked into an ADOMINAL STRETCH ...but he refuses to give up....Scorpio lets go and shoves Pepe down to the mat...Scorpio lands a big LEG DROP across the back of Pepes head....and Pepe to the outside of the ring for a breather...Scorpio waits.... EDDIE : Pepe slides in but Scorpio stomps on his head as he does...Scorpio to the TOP ROPE....Pepe gets nailed by a FLYING CLOTHESLINE off the ropes!!! Scorpio with the cover... (Ref counts 1...2...thr..) NORTH: No kicks out at the last second....Scorpio viciously Chokes Pepe...and the ref breaks it up and now Scorpio arguing with the ref or should I say distracting the ref...Lady Divine over to Pepe and SPRAYS SOMETHING INTO HIS EYES!!!! EDDIE: whatever that was its got Pepe in major pain and Pepe to his feet now swinging wildly into thin air...Scorpio over to him and NAILS HIM WITH THE STINGER!!!! the cover... (Ref 1...2...3...ding...ding...ding) NORTH: And Scorpio with a big win over Pepe and Pepe is still down clinching his eyes..... MCLEMORE: here is your winner by pinfall in 20 mins / 32 secs....STONE COLD SCORPIO (Back in Black plays as the crowds cheers turn to boos and Lady Divine and Scorpio lift their hands in victory....) NORTH: Scorpio signaling to the crowd that he wants a belt around that waist as he walks back to the locker room and that will do it for tonights TUESDAY NIGHT LIVEWIRE...I am Mike North and for Eddie Rodreiguez saying GOODNIGHT!!!!! (NCWA FADES OUT)
