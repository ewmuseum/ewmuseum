NCWA Tuesday Night Livewire (Crowd goes crazy as Smoke and Multi colored Laser lights fill the Spectra Arena as NCWA Tuesday Night Livewire begins...) Goes for the cover.... (Ref counts 1 , 2 , 3 ding...ding...ding) Mclemore: Here is your winner by pinfall in 8 min 23 secs H2O!!!!! (TARAUS' theme music plays and the crowd cheers him on as he slaps hands on his way down) North: And Taraus a talented young man but has caught some back breaks since entering the league we'll se if he can't turn it around tonight. (Ref calls for the bell......) Brickhouse: Here we go as the two lock up....Taruas trys a takedown but Crippler counters with fireman's carry...Crippler stomping on the head and now goes up to the second rope....Down with the elbow but Taraus moves out of the way!!
