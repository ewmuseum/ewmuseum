Real Wrestlers Fed

17 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NEW FED ~~~~~~~ I have decided to form IPW or International Pro Wrestling. The fed will be real wrestlers only and they must all be current. If they are injured, they are still allowed to wrestle. Your stable can include up to 5 wrestlers. If you have 4 or 5, you MUST have one team that will compete in the tag division. CARDS ~~~~~ TV shows will be held on Wednesdays (Wednesday Night Wrestling) and Saturdays (Saturday Night Slam). A third show may be added later on, depending on roster size. There will also be some occasional house shows. PPVs will be held once a month. ROLEPLAYS & MATCHES ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ You MUST roleplay at least once a week. Challenges are the best way to move up in the fed. Interviews are often held on TV as well, so they are also a way to go. Sneak attacks also make for good feuds. However, roleplay will NOT determine the winner of a match. If you are someone who roleplays only once a month and your opponent has been flashing twice a week, don't expect to win. Those who roleplay the most are usually the top contenders. Matches are simmed using TNM 6.2+, where we can have hardcore matches, handicap matches, gimmick matches, etc. CHAMPIONSHIPS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The major title is of course the World title. The North American title is secondary, while the TV belt is third. The TV strap is defended on one of the 2 shows. Should we have a big cruiserweight division, we will have a cruiserweight championship as well. The World tag title leads the way in that division. Once again, depending on roster size, we mayinstitute a North American tag title. BOARD OF DIRECTORS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Don't forget: this is YOUR fed. All handlers are part of the boards of directors. A lot of decisions will be put to a vote and YOU will decide what happens. CURRENT ROSTER ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The Untouchables: - The Undertaker - Kevin Nash - "Stone Cold" Steve Austin - Vader & Mankind The Dark Horsemen: - Chris Benoit - Scott Hall - Diamond Dallas Page - The Giant Send questions, comments or stables to [detail withheld]
