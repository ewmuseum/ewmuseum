VWA Hardcore Saturday Night — 4 October 1997

4 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Part 1 (the post on Google Groups)

["Highway to Hell" by AC\DC plays as the screen shows Times Square in New York, the VWA superstars are brawling and using New York as their ring. All of a sudden flames burst up from the bottom of the screen and it cuts away to the
Norfolk Scope Arena where tens of thousands of screaming VWA fans cheer and hold up signs declaring their support for different VWA superstars. The camera cuts away to the broadcast booth, where Matt Macklin and Jim Silvers are sitting.]
JS: Hello everyone and welcome to a sold-out Hardcore Arena!!! We are live to
TWO solid hours of wrestling action...
MM: Two solid hours because of those morons at the old television studio that pre-empted us!!! JS: But we're are back and ready to rumble, with two titles on the line tonight!!!!! So without further ado, let's goto the ring!!
[Roger Rampage's music starts up.] BB: Ladies and gentleman the following contest is set for one fall, coming down the aisle first... here is Roger Rampage!!
[A small crowd pop.] JS: Here comes Roger Rampage, looking to capture his first title ever in the VWA. MM: The North American title, oh yeah, there's a prestigious title for you... now start talking about the WORLD title!!! [The theme music from Terminator 2 starts.] BB: And his opponent, weighing 339 pounds... here is the PREDATOR!!! JS: Here comes the Predator, looking very focused as he prepares for his match. Keep in mind that the winner of this match has to fight *another* match against Alex Extreme later tonight.
MM: That's robbery, give them a break! They just fought one match! That's the stupid president for you.
JS: Watch what you say Matt... he could be watching you! [Ding...Ding...Ding!] JS: There's the bell and this match is underway, Predator taking an early lead in this match by sweeping Rampage off his feet, Predator bounces off the ropes, comes back, and drops an elbow on the face of Roger Rampage...
MM: One thing I will give Predator, he is a very good fighter... JS: I'm glad he meets your seal of approval. Predator yanks up Rampage by the hair and chucks him into the turnbuckle. Predator charges into the corner...
NO! Roger Rampage gets his foot up! MM: HaHa, what an idiot! JS: Roger pins him, 1... 2... and a kickout by the Predator. Rampage drops a leg on Predators face, and picks up Predator by the hair...
MM: Nail him Rampage! Get him! JS: Roger Rampage bounces off the ropes and charges after him, he goes for a flying shoulderblock... OH! Predator ducks and Roger Rampage flys over the top rope!!! What a move, but it looks like he hit his shoulder on the security railing!
MM: Lucky for Predator... he would of gotten nailed! JS: Predator climbs to the floor and picks up Roger. He pops him in the head, again, and again!! And now Predator whips Roger Rampage into the security railing... REVERSED! Predator falls to the floor, Roger picks up a chair and walks over to Predator, OH NO!!!
[Smash] [Smash] [Smash] MM: Yes! I love it!! JS: You would, Roger has just hit Predator with a chair THREE times!! Predator is bleeding profusely... Roger rolls back into the ring... Predator slowly crawling to his feet, it's amazing he can even move after those hits...
MM: Predators out, come on ref!!! JS: NO! Predator climbs back in the ring... Roger covers, 1... 2... AND PREDATOR STILL KICKS OUT, unbelievable!! MM: Damn it... come on! JS: Still to come in this telecast, Quezzy vs. Dirty Doug Foreyes for the cruiserweight title.. don't go anywhere!
MM: I won't... JS: Wait a second, LOOK AT THIS!! Predator has just rolled up Roger Rampage out of no where!!
1... 2... 3!!! Unbelievable! Out of no where, Predator gets the win!! Roger can still not believe it! MM: What the hell was this?? Come on ref!! JS: We have to take a break, but we'll be back in a moment, don't you go anywhere!!! *****break***** commercials *****break***** [Camera fades back up on the broadcast booth.] JS: We are back live on Hardcore Saturday Night, but right now let's take you to Ken Steeves and your Total War report...
[Camera cuts to the Total War '97 control center.] KS: Hello everyone and welcome to the Total War report. The big match signed is a thirty man royal rumble for the world title, so far five wrestlers have been announced and tune into Tuesday Night Terror, because there is a posibility that more will be announced. Also on the card is the match for the world tag team titles are up for grabs when the Brooklyn Brawlers take on
Storm Inc!!! Total War comes to you live Sunday October 19th and only on pay-per-view... more updates later, for now I am Ken Steeves saying goodnight.
Back to your Jim. [Camera cuts back to the broadcast booth.] JS: Don't miss Total War, because it's gonna be explosive. Right now, let's head down to the ring for our first title matchup here on Saturday Night!!!
["You Shock Me All Night Long" by AC\DC plays.] BB: Ladies and gentleman, the following contest is for the CRUISERWEIGHT TITLE. Introducing first, accompanied by the Slutty Slasher, and hailing from Brooklyn New York. Here is DIRTY DOUG FOREYES!!! [Small crowd pop.] JS: Here comes Dirty Doug Foreyes, ready for his first title match ever. What a shocker it could be if he wins!
MM: Blah, blah, blah! Neither man deserves the title! JS: Oh be quiet! Dirty Doug hops into the ring and prepares to lock up with
Quezzy who is already in the ring.
[Ding...Ding...Ding!] JS: There's the bell, and we are ready. Both wrestlers walk to the center of the ring, and size each other up. Quezzy looking Doug in the face.
MM: Boring!! JS: Dirty Doug Foreyes kicks Quezzy in the midsection!! He grabs the head!! OH! A DDT!!! There is an early cover by Dirty Doug... 1... 2... And a kickout by Quezzy, near fall in the early stages of this match. Doug picks up Quezzy and plants him down onto the mat. Doug climbs the top rope, he's waiting there for an awful long time... Doug leaps off the turnbuckle,
NO! Quezzy gets his knees up... Quezzy covers,
1... 2... 3!!! MM: I don't believe it!! Quezzy won in record time!! JS: Certainly a shocker to all the people here in Norfolk!! BB: Ladies and gentleman, the winner of the match... and NEW cruiserweight champion... QUEZZY!!!!!
JS: What a shocker, we have to take a break but we'll be back in a moment... *****break***** commercial *****break*****

Part 2 (the post on Google Groups)

JS: We are back, live from the VWA Hardcore Arena and ready for some more action!!! MM: So why are you still talking? Let's goto the ring!!!!! BB: Ladies and gentleman, the following tag team match is set for one fall... currently in the ring, the DEPTH OF DARKNESS!!!!! [Small crowd pop.] JS: Well, it appears that the fans here in the Hardcore Arena don't take a liking to the Depth of Darkness...
MM: These fans don't know anything... I tell you want all the fans can do ---
JS: Whoa there Matt!!! We're trying to cater to families!!
BB: And their opponents... ["Welcome to the Jungle" by Guns n Roses plays on the loudspeakers.] BB: Hailing from Nevada... here are the WYLD BOYZ!!!!!!!! <HUGE crowd pop> JS: Certainly a warm welcome for these guys, no doubt about that!!!!! MM: This makes me sick, I think I'm gonna lose my lunch!!! JS: I guess you don't take fan appreiciation well... (Ding...Ding...Ding!!!) JS: Well this match is underway, and it looks like Johnny Raven and Sun will start things out. Sun looking mighty aggressive, more so then the last time we saw him in action. Raven trying to duck under Sun's clothesline, but he got caught... 1... 2... And Ace Diamond makes the save, now that's what you call teamwork. Sun picks up Johnny and sends him into their corner... double-team, Psychotron working on Raven while Sun holds him... but what they don't know is that Diamond is coming in after them... OH! Sun got nailed from behind!!! Raven falls to the mat and tries to make his way over to his corner to make the tag, but is cut off by Sun...
MM: (Yawns) Boring... JS: Oh yeah, I forgot that this match goes by the rules so it would be boring to you. Sun climbs to the top rope now, he leaps... AND he nails a splash onto Raven!!!
1... 2... And Johnny Raven STILL kicks out!!! Sun, now clearly frusterated, picks up
Johnny and sets him up for a DDT... (pause) Whats this? Raven has lifted Sun up and flipped him, over... no Raven falls onto the mat... but it will be enough to make the tag into Diamond!!!!
MM: Come on Sun, get up!!! JS: It's no use Matt!! Diamond is in and working on Sun in the corner with those hard right fists!!! Now Diamond whips Sun across the ring and charges in... NO! Sun gets his foot up and Diamond will get a face-full of boot!!!
MM: Come on, make the tag!!! JS: And he does!!! Psychotron coming in now, and catches a fist right between the eyes from Ace Diamond!!! Ace Diamond swings wildly... but is sweep off his feet by Psychotron!! There is a cover...
1... 2... And NO! Diamond still kicks out, Psychotron now is having an arguement with the referee... Ace comes up from behind, a rollup!!!
1... 2... 3!!! MM: No! No! No! I demand a recount!!! JS: The Wyld Boyz have won the match!!! I don't believe my eyes!!!! The Wyld
Boyz win while Psychotron was distracted!!! We have to take a short break, but we'll be back in a moment with more live action!!!
MM: Unbelieveable!!! *****break***** commercials *****break***** JS: Back live here on Hardcore Saturday Night, we are ready for more action here in the Hardcore Arena, so let's bring out our next matchup!!!
BRETT BUTCHER: Ladies and gentleman!!!!! Already victorious once this evening. He has disposed of all competitors that have come before him. He stands at 6' 7", weighs in at 339 pounds. About to make his way down the aisle, here is ...........PREDATORRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!!! (As the theme music from TERMINATOR 2 begins to play, Predator slowly emerges through the curtain and begins his slow, rhythmic walk to the ring. There are a few boos heard but overall the crowd is relatively silent. He is in his usual attire of black pants, black boots and a black hood on his head covering all but an area around his eyes. His gaze is fixed on the ring and does not vary at all as he approaches the ring. He ignores all fans along the aisle.)
JS: Here comes the Predator looking very focused as he makes his way to the ring, but you also have to remember the brutal match he suffered just about an hour ago...
MM: Now we'll see how big a man he really is!!! JS: Please. Predator is in the ring, and we are ready to bring out Alex Extreme!!! ["Relax" by Frankie Goes to Hollywood plays.] BRETT BUTCHER: Ladies and gentleman, coming down the aisle, hailing from Chicago Illinois and weighing in at 245 pounds... here is ALEX EXTREME!!!!!! JS: Here comes Alex Extreme, certainly he wants the gold around his waist, but it will all come down to who wants it more... and as you can see the Predator is still favoring the legs and shoulder area from his previous match earlier... that may come into play here.
MM: Of course it will!!! (Ding...Ding...Ding!) JS: There is the bell, and we are underway Predator starting things off very slowly by circling Alex Extreme, but it is Mr. Excitment that makes the first move by giving the Predator a fist to the midsection... Predator comes back with a hard, right fist... bounces off the ropes, but goes down by a shoulderblock courtesy of Alex Extreme!!!
MM: This is what I like to see!!! JS: Obviously Alex Extreme knows that Predator is favoring that shoulder and midsection area, so that is going to be his main point of attack... you can bet on that... Alex Extreme sending Predator into the ropes, and nailing him with a fist to the midsection... and a DDT to follow!!!! There is a cover,
1... 2... And a kickout by Predator, almost a new champion!!! Alex Extreme frusterated, but keeping his cool... picking up Predator by the hair and pushing him into the turnbuckle... NO! it's reversed and it is Alex who ends up locked in the corner, receiving many blows to the abdominal area... and now Predator hiptosses him out onto the mat... but you can see right there he hurt his shoulder even more!!!
MM: Oh boo-hoo!!! Predator walks over to Alex Extreme and picks him up... but
The Extreme One is waiting with an eye gouge... Alex gets up and gives the
Predator an atomic drop...another one...and finally a short clothesline and
Predator goes down!!!
JS: Nice commentating Matt... MM: Thank you JS: There is a cover by Alex Extreme, 1... 2... 3 --- No! 2 and 9\10ths!! Alex Extreme picks up the Predator and plants him down with a nicely executed body slam... he climbs the top rope... (pause) and lands on top of Predator with a major impact!!!
MM: Nice move!!! Your looking at the next champ!! JS: You may be right about that because Alex is signaling for the DDT!!! Nailed!!! He now climbs the ropes, (pause) there it is!!! The Extreme, which is a moonsault into a legdrop, what a move!!! There is the cover,
1.. 2... 3!!! MM: We have a new champion!!! Alex Extreme wins it!!! JS: And we are also out of time, we will see you next time!!! What an incredible match that was!!!!
