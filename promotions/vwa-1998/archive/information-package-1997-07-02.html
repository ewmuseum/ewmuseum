VWA Information Package, 2 July 1997

POSISTIONS AVAILABLE: Vice-President: This job is the second most important job (President being the first). The VP's job is to overlook the general players, and evaluate them on their performance. They will also have the task of managing things while I am away. A little light writing will also be required until I hire official writers. Experience Required.
Writers: These people have an important job. They are the ones that will write out all the cards and any other official VWA business. They will not have to write an entire card, rather one or two matches per card.
Sample Needed. Artist: Upon hiring, your job will be to create pictures of all the VWA wrestlers, managers, and announcers... as well as some logos for the webpage (i.e. a picture indicating the next PPV) Sample Needed.
Webpage Creator: This job is pretty self-explanatory. Everyone knows that a webpage is crutial to the e-wrestling world. It can help get the word across about upcomming events, and provide newcomers with a look at what the fed has to offer before they join. Experience Preferred.
Wrestlers: A good e-fed could not function without wrestlers, so this job is what keeps us going. You will be allowed one singles wrestler and one tag team. Form below.
VWA Fan: Not interested in becomming a member, but still want to get all the exciting action? A VWA fan does just that, they receive all the house shows and pay-per-views without having the hassel of handling a wrestler.
E-mail me and tell me "I want to be a VWA fan" HOW MUCH DOES THIS COST: Nothing. The VWA provides it's services solely for the entertainment of it's members. HOW ARE MATCH OUTCOMES DECIDED: Match results and outcomes are based on a wrestling simulator, called "WLS
5.3", and by the wrestlers strategy forms that they fill out when they request a match. All the strategies are, are simple forms that say what your wrestler will be doing to try to win the match, and what the wrestler will do if he wins\loses the match. If your wrestler has any feuds, that will also be taken into consideration.
HOUSE SHOWS: Here in the VWA, we offer two house shows a week. As soon as we get more writers, however, we will be offering more cards. The shows are as followed:
Tuesday Night Terror: Terror is the equivilent of WCW's "Nitro" or WWF's
"Raw". It is a two hour program, and features around six matches. It airs at 8:00 PM EST on Tuesdays.
Hardcore Saturday Night: This program airs at 1:00 AM EST on Saturdays, and is the equivilent of ECW's shows, meaning, all the matches are street fight rules in which anything can be brought to the ring and used. The referees are usually more liniant during this card. It is a one hour program and is guarenteed to feature at least one title defense.
PAY-PER-VIEWS: The VWA will offer one ppv a month, they are as followed: January: New Year's Bash February: In Your Web March: Superclash April: Spring Slam May: In Your Web June: Battle at the Beach July: Heat Wave (followed by the year) August: In Your Web September: In Your Web October: Total War November: Hardcore Hell December: Christmas Clash TITLES: World Heavyweight: The VWA World belt is the most sought after belt in the entire VWA, only the best will possess it. It must be defended at every
PPV and at any house show that the owner wishes.
North American: Equivilent to the WCW's United States Champion, this title must be defended at every PPV and one card a week.
Cruiserweight: This belt must be defended at every house show and PPV. Hardcore Tag Title: The only tag team belt that this federation has to offer, until we bring in another. This belt must be defended at every
PPV, and at one card a week.
ROLLPLAYING: Rollplaying is crutial to the world of e-fed wrestling. It shapes your wrestler, as well as stirs up some feuds and makes things more interesting. We here in the VWA run an open-flash system, meaning that the wrestlers will be able to send flashes to every other wrestler anytime they like. We do endorse this program because it adds excitment to the cards, however, if I start to receive letters saying stop because you are flooding my mailbox this system will be stopped. When submitting a interview or flash, remember the ever important three word phrase:
"Quality, Not Quantity". Cussing in an interview is not banned, but the phrases which can't be aired on television must be "masked" (For example, f***). You are required to roll-play weekly, unless you write me a email saying that you can't roll-play for X amount of days due to a vacation (or something), if you do not send me a letter, you have two strikes and on the second you will be suspended from the VWA for a period of time.
CONTRACTS: When you sign up to create a wrestler, you will be asked to select the length of your contract (1, 2, or 3 years). All this means is, how long will it be until your contract with the VWA will have to be renegotiated.
While under contract, you will still be able to participate in other online wrestling federations, however, you will not be able to use your wrestlers name that you have applied for and are using in the VWA. If we find out that your are using your wrestler in another federation, the result will be an automatic suspension.
If you do want to use one of your wrestlers from another federation, make sure you clear it with me first!
