VWA Hardcore Saturday Night — 13 December 1997

13 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up on a deserted industrial backlot. Barrels engulfed in flames are seen everywhere, as a man weilding a bat walks slowly down the path. "Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson plays, as shots of past
VWA moments appear on the brick walls.
King Suicide stealing the North-American title. Vlad the Impalor standing over a fallen Great White Shark. Storm Inc. beating the Wyld Boyz at Hardcore Hell '97 VWA President, Todd Bloom, handing the title to Nick Danger. The Angels of the Apocalypse taking over Weekend Spotlight. After that final shot, the screen bursts into flames, "peeling awat" to reveal a sold-out, capacity filled Arco Arena in downtown Sacramento,
California. People are holding signs like, "Nick Danger is the Man!",
"Storm Inc. rule", and the ever popular "Rex Rogers 3:16". Camera zooms into the broadcast booth where Rex Rogers and Dan Crewman are sitting.]
DAN CREWMAN: We are live in downtown Sacramento, for Hardcore Saturday Night!!!! We have some great action for you tonight, as well as an update on Christmas Clash... coming up very shortly! REX ROGERS: Plus, the greatest tag team in the world, the Wyld Boyz, might make an appearance tonight, as well!!!!
DAN CREWMAN: And in the main event, we will see Nick Danger... a man who we have not seen much off since he won the world title last month at
Hardcore Hell!!!!
REX ROGER: Big deal! DAN CREWMAN: Right now, let's goto the ring... Frank White and Melcy
Paradise are are in the ring, so let's join this match... allready in progress!
REX ROGERS: Yes let's... it should be over quickly! DAN CREWMAN: Melcy Paradise, has taken Frank White of his feet, and is now stomping on his head... continually. Melcy climbs up to the top rope now, and prepares to frog splash the man that is down in the ring.
REX ROGERS: I don't know, she is spending an awful lot of time up there! I think she's having second thoughts! DAN CREWMAN: You could be right about that, Melcy Paradise leaps off the top rope... but is caught in the stomach when Frank White got up his legs!
REX ROGERS: Ha ha, that oughta teach that b*tch!!! DAN CREWMAN: Rex! Stop it with that coarse language! "Fabulous" Frank
White has gotten up, and shoved Melcy into the corner. And he is now working on her, punching the ribs with sharp, feirce punches!
REX ROGERS: You abuser! You could goto jail! DAN CREWMAN: Not in a million years Rex. Frank White has Paradise in a piledriver, and down she goes!!! That was quite a move! There is a cover,
Referee: 1...2...3! Bell: Ding...Ding....Ding! DAN CREWMAN: And "Fabulous" Frank White has already won the match, in record time! What an athlete!
REX ROGERS: That's nothing! DAN CREWMAN: Well, whatever the case, Frank White will walk away the winner of the match. Right now, we have Ken Steeves standing by ---
[All of a sudden the lights go out and a spotlight stops by the ringside, pointing right at King Suicide, who has the North-American title slung over his shoulder, hops over the security railing and climbs into the ring. Robert Mulligan follows.]
King Suicide: Ladies and gentleman of the VWA!!! I have something I want to address! This past Wednesday, from the Civic Center, we were forced to wrestle the crappy Wyld Boyz, even though I was out sick! What is that all about? We demand respect!
Robert Mulligan: We want to speak to your president, we deserve a shot at the world title... so why don' t you get off of your crack-smoking as* and give us a match for it!!!
[Crowd erupts, when they see Todd Bloom walking down the aisle.] King Suicide: Well, looky what we have here! Here comes the baby now! Robert Mulligan: You'd better watch yourself! We're not that far from dragging you down and beating you to dust!
Todd Bloom: You know, I'm sick and tired of your whining! If you want a match that bad, then why don't you sign a contract with us??
King Suicide: Don't make us sick, we are only here for one reason, and that is to bring you down!
Todd Bloom: Well then, I've made up my mind... and I have a proposition for you. You WILL fight the New York Brawler a week from tonight.
Robert Mulligan: I don't think so, you can't tell us what to do!!! Todd Bloom: Don't sh*t your pants yet, old man! I'm not through. After you get through with that match, each and everyone of your guys will be wrestling at Christmas Clash. King Suicide will be wrestling Predator, in a "Spin the wheel, Make the Deal" type of match. Then, after Predator gets done wiping the mat with your carcass, Those other two boneheads in your camp are going to fight a match!
Robert Mulligan: You don't scare us! Todd Bloom: Oh yeah? Well, if you don't wrestle, you will be fined
$250,000!!! I'll see you at Christmas Clash!
[With that, Todd drops the microphone and walks back down the aisle.] Robert Mulligan: No one does that to us! Get him, boys! [With that, King Suicide,plus the other two men run down the aisle and start to beat up the President.]
Dan Crewman: Oh my god! Look at what they're doing! That's the President!!! Rex Rogers: This just makes me sick!!! Dan Crewman: This is sickening! Come on, get some security out here!! They are now hitting him with chairs! Rex Rogers: They're all a bunch of cowards... they suck!!! Dan Crewman: We have to take a break, but we'll be back in a moment... *****break***** commercials *****break***** Dan Crewman: We are back, live from the Arco Arena. We have gotten things settled, and Todd Bloom is is on his way to the hospital, we will try to have an update on him later on in the program. Right now, let's goto Ken Steeves who is in the locker room with Storm Inc., the current
VWA Tag Team Champions.
Ken Steeves: Allright fans, I'm here to get a few quick words with Storm
Inc, the VWA tag team champions, who shortly have a match here tonight.
Well guys, you suffered your first loss in this fed last weekend at the hand of your opponents tonight, the Brooklyn Brawlers.
Lightning: Yeah whatever. Look, we got the belts still don't we now. It would have been nice of the VWA to let us know that the schedule had changed and we had a match at that card. I mean, we had only just got off the plane when that match took place
Thunder: Yeah, that's right. You brawlin bastards are goin to suffer tonight. We had planned on going undefeated for a while yet and you just wrecked our plans, something we're not very happy about.
Ken Steeves: Well this will be the fourth time you guys have wrestled the
Brawlers, any comment?
Thunder: I know. Then next match we got is against the Wyld Boyz, I mean isn't there any other teams out there that we can kick the c--p out of? I mean, there's only so much c--p in these two teams that we can kick out of them. Surely there's some other team we can pummel around a bit.
Lightning: Yeah, a change of scenery would be nice. Are there any real contenders out there or are we just gonna have to stay champions until we feel like retiring?
Ken Steeves: OK gentlemen, any last comments. Thunder: Oh you bet ya,.........forecast is in......... Thunder & Lightning: STORM TIME BABY!!!! [Camera cuts back to Dan and Rex.] Brett Butcher: Annnnnd nowww!!!! [On the big screen footage of a vicious Storm tearing through a small coastal town is shown. There is rubbish being blown around everywhere and trees are bending at right angles. The Screen suddenly changes to the words Storm Inc as a loud thunder crack echoes throughout the arena.]
Brett Butcher: They are the VWA Tag Team Champions!!! Here are Lightning and Thunder......STORRRRRRRMMMM IIINNNNCCCC!!!!!!!!
[The crowd erupts as "WALK THIS WAY" by RUN DMC begins to play throughout the arena. Lightning and Thunder tear through the curtain, take a few steps, stop, and point at their opponents before jogging down the aisle to ringside, high fiving fans along the aisle. They have the belts around their waists. They stop at the ring, remove their belts, then get up on the apron and simultaneously spring over the ropes.]
Brett Butcher: And their opponents, from Brooklyn, New York... accompanied by the Slutty Slasher. Here are the BROOKLYN BRAWLERS!!!!!! [Carjack and Brooklyn Destroyer appear on the video screen.] Carjack: Storm Inc. Forget you! We allready beat you guys once, and we got nothing else to prove to you!
Brooklyn Destroyer: We aren't gonna fight you tonight, we aren't even going to deal with you any more!!!
Carjack: And that's the REAL forcast!!!!! [Camera fades. Storm Inc. just walk away.] Dan Crewman: Well. The referee is going to award this match to Storm Inc. We have to take a break, but we'll be back with our main event! *****break***** commercials *****break***** Dan Crewman: We are back, and ready for our main event --- Nick Danger vs. Ricky Davis!!!! Brett Butcher: Ladies and gentleman, currently in the ring. With his manager, Bill Price, here is Ricky "The Sex God" Davis!!!!
Rex Rogers: What a loser! This is the only time, I actually want Nick
Danger to win... Brett Butcher: And his opponent. Weighing in at 285lbs. From Hell's
Kitchen, New York, He is the VWA Heavyweight Champion....NICK DANGER!!!!!!!!!!!! [Huge crowd pop.] [The sound of a Harley Davidson revving up brings the crowd to its feet. From behind the curtain Nick Danger and Roxy appear riding down to ringside. The two park the bike and begin high-fiving the fans around the ring area. Nick enters the ring and climbs the turnbuckle carrying a sign given to him by a female fan "Nick Danger is the man" Roxy goes over to Brett Butcher and takes the microphone from him.]
Roxy: Ricky Davis...The self proclaimed Sex God. It seems to me that you've gotten yourself in way over your head. They say God can create miracles, so you better start making them now, cause Nick has your ticket right here and you're on a one way ride TO THE DANGER ZONE!!!!!!!
[Another huge crowd pop.] Dan Crewman: Well, we are underway in this main event, Nick Danger starts thinks out aggressivly, by locking up Rick Davis.
Rex Rogers: This should be an easy win... look at the way he's manhandleing Ricky Davis!!! Dan Crewman: Nick Danger throws The Sex God into the ropes, he comes back... and there it is!!!! The POWERDRIVER!!!!!!
Rex Rogers: What a move!!! Dan Crewman: There is a cover, Referee: 1...2...3!!! Bell: Ding...Ding....Ding!!!! Dan Crewman: Nick Danger wins the match, and we are out of time! We will see you for Wednesday Night WAR!
Rex Rogers: So long everybody! (c)1997 VWA Sports All Rights Reserved
