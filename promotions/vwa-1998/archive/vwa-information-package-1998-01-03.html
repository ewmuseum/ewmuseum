Virtual Wrestling Association (VWA) Information Package. http://www.angelfire.com/ma/VWAhome/index.html A LITTLE BACKGROUND ON ME: I have been involved in e-wrestling for quite some time, wrestling in various organizations like, the UWA, PWF, NICWF, VFWF, and many others. I recently started to run this fed, which started in September of 1997 and still running strong. HOW MUCH DOES THIS COST: Nothing. The VWA provides it's services solely for the entertainment of it's members. HOW ARE MATCH OUTCOMES DECIDED: Match results and outcomes are based wrestler strategy forms that they fill out when they request a match. All the strategies are, are simple forms that say what your wrestler will be doing to try to win the match, and what the wrestler will do if he wins\loses the match. If your wrestler has any feuds, that will also be taken into consideration. HOUSE SHOWS: Here in the VWA, we offer two house shows a week. As soon as we get more writers, however, we will be offering more cards. The shows are as followed: Wednesday Night WAR!: WAR! is the equivilent of WCW's "Nitro" or WWF's "Raw". It is a one hour program, and features around four matches. It airs at 8:00 PM EST on Wednesdays. Hardcore Saturday Night: This program airs at 1:00 AM EST on Saturdays, and is the equivilent of ECW's shows, meaning, all the matches are street fight rules in which anything can be brought to the ring and used. The referees are usually more liniant during this card. It is a one hour program and is guarenteed to feature at least one title defense. Weekend Spotlight: This program features highlights of this past week, a special guest, and flashes from the wrestlers. It airs Sundays. PAY-PER-VIEWS: The VWA will offer one ppv a month, they are as followed: January: Wrestle Storm February: Agony & Ectasy March: Superclash April: Spring Slam May: No Guts, No Glory June: Battle at the Beach July: Heat Wave (followed by the year) August: In Your Web: (followed by a suitable title) September: Rage in the Cage October: Total War November: Hardcore Hell December: Christmas Clash STABLES: Here in the VWA, wrestlers are aloud to have stables --- in fact, I encourage them, because they add excitement and feuds to the whole scheme of things. There are limitations, however. The main point of it, is that you may only have five members in your group unless I give the clearance. The reason for that rule is that if one group has, like, twenty wrestlers in it... where is the fun? So let's keep it small for now... TITLES: World Heavyweight: The VWA World belt is the most sought after belt in the entire VWA, only the best will possess it. It must be defended at every PPV and at any house show that the owner wishes. North American: Equivilent to the WCW's United States Champion, this title must be defended at every PPV and every two weeks. Cruiserweight: This belt must be defended at every house show and PPV. World Tag Title: The only tag team belt that this federation has to offer, until we bring in another. This belt must be defended at every PPV, and at one card a week. ROLLPLAYING: Rollplaying is crutial to the world of e-fed wrestling. It shapes your wrestler, as well as stirs up some feuds and makes things more interesting. We here in the VWA run an closed-flash system, meaning that the wrestlers send flashes to me and they will be mass-mailed to the rest of the federation, usually as part of Weekend Spotlight. When submitting a interview or flash, remember the ever important three word phrase: "Quality, Not Quantity". Cussing in an interview is not banned, but the phrases which can't be aired on television must be "masked" (For example, f***). You are required to roll-play weekly, unless you write me a email saying that you can't roll-play for X amount of days due to a vacation (or something), if you do not send me a letter, you have two strikes and on the second you will be suspended from the VWA for a period of time.
