President: ReX Watson ( [detail withheld] ) Vice President: Marcus "The Stiffest Worker in Pro-Wrestling" Bader -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- 2. Roleplaying Roleplaying, the back-bone of a good e-fed. Well, here in, its no different. We ask that you abide by the following rules. -Profanity can be as often as you want, but don't use "F this, F That, F Your Mother". In other words, don't get gratuitous. -A Minimum of 20 lines is needed for every roleplay. -A roleplay must have a description of what your wrestler is wearing, where he is, and his surroundings. -A Minimum of 1 roleplay a week is required. -For a PPV, a minimum of 2 roleplays is required. -Don't use other guys' wrestlers in your Roleplays. -Send all Roleplays to the above Addresses, as they will put out in Flashes. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- 3. XRW Titles XRW World Heavyweight Title XRW American Title XRW Tag Team Titles Triple X Rated Championship (Hardcore Belt) Occasionally, we will have special tournaments and whatnot. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- 4. Shows XRW's XXX Wednesday Night- The RAW or Nitro of XRW. It consists of some quick matches, some long and hardcore epics. XRW Saturday Night Danger Zone- A Recap and News show of the XRW, as we only like to have one major show a week to keep angles fresh. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- 5. PPV February: "Winter Warfare" (The First PPV of the year.) April- "X" (Our Wrestlemania-type PPV.) June- "Hellfire and Brimstone" (The First PPV of The X Rated Summer.) August- "The Wrestling War Festival" (The two day event, first day is called "Hell's Night" which is a regular "X" style PPV. The second day is called "The X-Rated Tag Team Tournament", where 16 Tag Teams face off in a randomly chosen event, in which the team that makes it to the finals faces each other in a Cage Match to determine Number 1 contender for the Tag Team Belts.) October- "Shocktober" (A Halloween style PPV, in which the winner of The X-Rated Tag Team Tournament faces the Champion.) December- "Death Brawl" (A Supercard that features a 6 Man Barbed Wire Steel Cage Match). -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- 6.
