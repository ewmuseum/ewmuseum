Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

X-Rated Wrestling opened with Handbook Version 1.0 on 24 November 1999, under the tagline “The Name Says It All,” and pitched itself as the newsgroup's hardest-edged option: “the most f'n hardcore, insane, downright sick federation on the net.” Its own president and vice president signed the welcome page together, billed as on-air partners running the promotion side by side.

Four championships were on offer — a World Heavyweight Title, an American Title, Tag Team Titles and a hardcore belt called the Triple X Rated Championship — contested across two shows: XXX Wednesday Night, its flagship, and a Saturday Night Danger Zone recap-and-news broadcast, kept to one major show a week “to keep angles fresh.”

Its own pay-per-view calendar ran the full year: Winter Warfare in February, a Wrestlemania-style “X” in April, Hellfire and Brimstone in June, a two-day Wrestling War Festival in August pairing a regular card with a sixteen-team tag tournament, a Halloween-themed Shocktober in October pitting that tournament's winner against the champion, and a December supercard, Death Brawl, built around a six-man barbed wire steel cage match. Its handbook backed the calendar with an extensive list of gimmick stipulations, from cage and casket matches to a “Spin The Wheel, Make The Deal” format and dueling weapons of every kind, copyrighted in the name of Bluddstorm Entertainment.

No card, roster, or further document survives past this handbook; which of its planned pay-per-views ever ran, and how far X-Rated Wrestling itself got, isn't in the surviving record.
