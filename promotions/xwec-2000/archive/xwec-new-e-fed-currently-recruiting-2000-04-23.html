XWEC - New e-Fed Currently Recruiting

23 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello all. I have just opened the XWEC (eXtreme Wrestling of Eastern Canada) and I am looking for members. The e-fed in itself is very basic, with mostly overlook matches (all the moves, but no announcers play-by-play) inside of full play-by-play cards. The fed itself is an extreme-style fed (meaning : lenient on rules)
and it is based in Quebec and the Maritimes. It is not meant to compare with such organizations as the EWMC, WWO or AMWC. What it is meant to do is provide it's members (and it's fedhead)
with harmless, unpolitical, fun. If you are interested, you can fill out the Application Form (which is at the very end of this message) and send it to me at [detail withheld] . If you wish to know more about the league, you can continue your reading, as I have joined the XWEC FAQ to this message.
Thank you, and join now!!! [name withheld] ---------- XWEC FAQ ------------------------- 0. Table of contents 1- Who is running that fed? 2- What is the XWEC? 3- Shows & Supercards 4- Belts 5- Additional Considerations 6- Application Form ------------------------- 1. Who is running that e-fed? My name is [name withheld], and I've been involved in e-wrestling for about 3 years (mid-1996 to mid-1999). I'm coming back for a much-needed break from the game, and I don't feel like handling.
Instead, I have decided to give another try at handling an e- fed. I've done that twice in the past. The first e-fed I did was well received by it's players, running from September 1996 to
February 1997, it was called International Championship e- Wrestling. Due to time constraints and burnout (the e-fed had become a bit too ambition for my time prospects), I had to close. The itch to run a fed came back in May of 1998, and I started the NEW-A (North East Wrestling - Association), which was a success, if I listen to the players feedback. Eventually, the fed growth more and more ambition and merged with the DWI
(Damage Wrestling Inc) to form the APWA (Atlantic - Pacific Wrestling Association) which was good while it lasted. Due to the fed needing too much time to be ran (the project once again became too ambitious), I closed the e-fed in January 1999.
The main difference between those two other e-feds, is that I will try my best to prevent the XWEC to become impossible to fit in my time schedule. Read section 2 to understand why. One of the things I like the best about e-wrestling is booking card and getting out a finished product, and I'm hoping to be able to do just that in this organization.
------------------------- 2. What is the XWEC? The XWEC (short for eXtreme Wrestling of Eastern Canada) is made to be like a regional independant association which takes a bit of it's stuff off the ECW. This means that the wrestling will be made in front of small crowd, that the events won't have all the glitz and glamour of the big feds, and that the rules will be somewhat lenient (weapons being legal, altrought outside interference is not, as well as destroying the refere). The organization will take a bit where I left off in the past, with the ICeW and the NEW-A, altrought it will be somewhat less based on play-by-play matches. I will still try to get some kind of look that will make the events have some kind of style, which shall make it easier to read, and also more enjoyable to see.
The XWEC, in the scenario, only have two clip shows on regional (Quebec and Maritimes) television. The events will show "match clips" to let the viewers know what is happening. No full matches because the poor video quality would just make it look second or third rate (when compared to the big leagues). In the cards that you will receive, there will be no play-by-play (PBP)
matches (I hate writing PBP). Instead, all the matches will be documented in a medium-lenght, detailed, overlook. This way, you will know all the action that happened in the matches, and I won't have to write PBP. However, the rest of the card, the entrance and the interviews will be done in full PBP (to preserve some sense of continuity in the card itself).
The XWEC will be an equal opportunity organization. I am not really familiar with today's e-wrestling world, and with the politics that are playing there. The XWEC shall be a politics- free organization, altrought booked by me. I will give, at one point or another, the push that every player deserves. If you play regularly, if you do good roleplaying, angles and stuff, then it's sure that, somewhere down the road, you will receive the push that you deserve. The key of the XWEC will be long-term planning. I won't book day by day, more like a month forward
(building to the supercards). ------------------------- 3. Shows & Supercards The XWEC will have to regular, 8-10/12 matches shows (Adrenaline Rush (10-12 bouts) on Mondays, and eXtreme Assault (8 bouts) on Fridays). Once a month, we will also feature a supercard (kinda like ECW's CyberSlam). It will feature the big grudges matches as well as, for most of the supercard, a special theme event
(like a King of the Hill tournament, Survivor Series event, Royal Rumble, and such). I used the concept in the NEW-A and it was well received, with the events proposed being actually enjoyable. The supercard will feature around 15 matches, instead of the regular, and I will involve all the participating players on the card. As I said in section 2, the shows (including supercard) will be overlook-style, instead of full PBP. This will ensure less lateness, and also the capacity to have more matches on the cards (which is why I will be able to provide about 20 matches per week). Supercards will replace the Friday show, and on those instances, Adrenaline Rush will feature less matches (8 bouts instead of 10/12).
------------------------- 4. Belts The top title of the XWEC will be the North-Atlantic Title. It will be a singles title disponible to all weight class. This belt will be the first introduced in the organization.
Afterward, the Quebec Tag Team Titles will be introduced. Eventually, depending of the speed of growth of the roster (and of the needs), the Quebec Heavyweight Title (236+ lbs) and the
Quebec Cruiserweight Title (235- pounds) will be introduced. Other titles will be added (such as a secondary tag team title, when I will see fit). ------------------------- 5. Additional Consideration The handlers will be permitted two wrestlers. If possible, put them in a tag-team. Tag team wrestlers will be able to wrestle singles matches, and win singles title. However, in those opportunities, when a half of the tag team will receive a bigger push, it is very possible that the other half will be somewhat stangnant during that time (unless you provide insteresting angle and RP'ing for both, which shall warrant a push to a disponible spot for both members). The maximum height of the wrestlers will be 7'3''. There was never really a wrestler of more than that, and besides, we don't want a fed populated with monsters. Real wrestlers' music won't be allowed, as well as references in RP's to real organization. We are in a parallel universe where I can only admit the existence of other e-feds, no real feds. Also, most of the cards will be easier to read when consulted in Courrier New, size 8, 9 or 10.
