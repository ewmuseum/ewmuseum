Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Twiggs Extreme Wrestling Federation, named for its own president and founder, dates its founding to the Spring of 1999. By its own account it flourished right away and worked its way into a top-50 e-feds list before its own management found it too hectic to run and shut it down; it reopened, in its own words, “with a bang” on 1 January 2000.

On 26 June 2001, a year and a half later, Twiggs announced the president was back in power and the staff had new ideas “never before seen in the E-Fed World,” promising a return to business once enough members signed on. A second post the same day called it a three-year-old federation running two cards a week and a monthly pay-per-view — a claim the founding account's own 1999 date does not quite square with, and the record leaves the two uncombined.

No roster, card, or rules document survives from any point in its run, and how or when it finally closed is not known.
