HFC — Rules

New League- HFC

3 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello all, I invite you all to join a new E-wrestling league! The name of the league is the Hardcore Fighting COnference(HFC). It is a league for real and fantasy wrestlers alike. You may have a singles wrestler and a tag team for a total of 3 wrestlers. All matches will be decided by either WLS 5.1 or TNM 6.0(which ever I feel like using that day: probably WLS most of the time). What separates this league from most others? Glad you asked<g>! The HFC operates under the following rules for ALL matches: NO DQ, No countout, secondary title matches and ALL tag matches are 2-out-of-3 falls, World Title matches are all TNM-style death matches(3 consecutive falls wins a match). Here are the titles up for grabs: HFC World Title HFC International Title HFC Tag Team Titles (Note: I will add more titles if a bunch of people join) I have my own ranking system for rating wrestlers and determining the who gets title shots. Since I will be using WLS and TNM, and since real wrestlers are being used, I will NOT ask you to just randomly pick numbers for attributes, if you want to make a wrestler(or tag team) just send what the wrestler is like and his mindset. I'll figure out the attributes from what you send. And don't be afraid to get creative on me! I will probably score you higher the more creative the wrestler is! If you want pre-set wrestlers, just tell me which ones. They will be available on a first-come, first-serve basis. If you would like to join, send the profile(s) (or list of real wrestlers) to [detail withheld] . Oh yeah by the way, if anyone even THINKS about making a crack about my beloved Ajax losing form latley, I will make sure your wrestler get jobbed out of the league. ;) Hope to see you, Brien
