HFC: The Sex Junkie

15 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SJ: Welcome to another edition of the Pleasure Palace. We are back with our best season ever. During this season I will be interviewing Farrah Fawcett, Madonna, Sharon Stone and many of the other Hollywood elite. But today lets talk about the HFC. I just had my opening match with Mark Sherrick. This clown is running all over the HFC talking about how he defeated the Sex Junkie. Well BOY, you only got your hand raised due to a little mistake from Tantalizing Tatiana. Heck we all know you couldn't keep your eyes off her during the match. You were so entralled with her looks that it messed up your whole gameplan. Well she got tired of your gawking and decided to give you a little gift to remember her by and you managed to duck out of the way. Well you won't be so lucky the next time. You and I are going to meet again and you can bet your bottom dollar that the Sex Junkie will not be denied the next time. I am the GREATEST wrestler in the HFC and during the Title Tournament you all will kneel before the greatest wrestler of your generation. Tatiana made a mistake but it will not happen again will it Tatiana? TT: No Junkie it will NEVER happen again. Sherrick you are going to get what is coming to you. That is a promise. SJ: This league wants to be the Hardcore league to end Hardcore well step up each and every one of you if you have the guts to get in the ring with the Sex Junkie. <fade to commercial>
