WSEW Handbook

22 April 2000

WSEW >> World Sports Entertainment Wrestling http://wsew.hypermart.net Introduction The funds have come through and we here at World Sports Entertainment Wrestling are now ready and waiting for active or free agent wrestlers to come and join up with WSEW. President of WSEW [name withheld] said "We here at
WSEW are on the look out for top talent whether they are currently with another top company or a free agent looking for an organisation then this is the place to come, we do have our first venue booked for Thursday Fury and details about this will be annouced soon, but first we need some talent!" So there you have it from the very mouth of the president come and sign up for some true wrestling action, be sure to check out ALL the links underneath the Front Office section as these are vital to know before you decide to sign up, that is if you do or not!
Only 18 slots open so be quick, once the roster is full first card and venue will be annouced shortly after!
Rules and Regulations The following rules must be obeyed as closely as possible, a first violation of the rules and you will receive a written warning, any other violations and your contract will be terminated, The President also has the right to terminate a contract at his own discretion, you will NOT be informed if your contract is terminated. You will be removed from the roster without notice.
1. Application Forms The Application form must be filled in as much as possible, application forms with what seems not enough information will be deemed void, the sample roleplay section MUST be filled in and be no less than 20 lines, the president has the right to the change the application form whenever he decides. WSEW has the right to reject an application, also there is a limit of 20 wrestlers in the federation at one time, if we are full we will keep your application on file and you will be put into a waiting list, should an opening arrive in WSEW you will be informed. The Application Form is a web based form and, generates an email to my account if your browser does not support forms you can email the president at [name withheld] and ask for an email version of the form
2. BCC Fed This federation is run on a BCC mailing method, which means all mails regarding WSEW will be sent to me and me only, it is NOT REPLY TO ALL OR
MESSAGE BOARD RUN. All roleplays will be sent to me and me only and i will then dispatch the roleplays in one big batch to the members so you can read the roleplays all in one go and only need to roleplay once every two days at the most. The fed is run BCC for the following reasons: Privacy so that member is not harrased by another member of the federation, you only need to roleplay once a day, your email box will not be filled with roleplays every
10 minutes, you don't have to come to this website every day and wait for message boards to download, you will not get any of those annoying get me off this list messages. email addresses of the members will be witheld, if you wish to contact a member of WSEW it will be done through me
3. Hardcore WSEW began as a family based wrestling organisation, over time we have developed a more adult and mature product. The fed is still basically run with the usual match rules. However if a feud should get out of control we will more than likely sanction a hardcore match to settle the differences.
Hardcore matches can also happen in regular matches and not just to settle a feud. Hardcore and extreme based wrestlers are accepted into WSEW. However we do not base our product around ECW. We are more of a WWF product related
E-Fed. 4. Number Of Wrestlers: There is a limit of 20 active wrestlers at one time in WSEW, therefore you are only allowed to have one wrestler per email address, anyone found using more than one email address will be kicked out of the federation. If you wish to form a stable you must also either start your own and attempt to get other members of WSEW to join, or join a stable that is already active.
4.1. Light Heavyweight Division At the moment WSEW has no plans to create a Light Heavyweight Division, however should a large waiting list for WSEW develop i will offer those people the chance to create a light heavyweight and i will start a new division.
4.2 Womens Division At the moment WSEW has no plans to create a Womens Division, however should a large waiting list for WSEW develop i will offer those people the chance to create a women wrestler and i will start a new division.
5. Changes To Your Wrestler Should you feel you need to change the details that you sent in for your wrestler during the course of the federation please email the president with the new details of your wrestler, you are allowed to change things such as weight and gimmick, appearance etc. Also if you are adding a manager to your wrestler or firing your manager please fill in an application form for the manager details or tell the president through email if your are getting rid of your manager
6. Cards WSEW currently runs one weekly televised wrestling show called Thursday Fury, it contains dark matches as well as LIVE televised matches. WSEW also hosts 6 Pay Per View events every year which span between every two-three months, the reason for this is not rush angles and story lines and have the develop at an acceptable pace and it also makes WSEW more exciting as well as a higher quality.
7. Wrestling Rules The same ring rules apply in WSEW as those that are used in the World Wrestling Federation. one fall, count outs, DQ's for what takes place inside and outside the ring. Matches also have specfic time limits which are as follows: low card match: 15 mins Mid card match: 30 mins Mainevent and all title matches: 60 mins Except TV title matches which have a time limit of 15 mins. Championship belts can only change hands on pinfall or submission, you cannot win the belt via Dq or count out.
Special Matches and Hardcore matches may take place and rules for those matches will be described when the match is announced. WSEW refs can range from strict to lienanent, meaning some refs may allow weapons into the ring while others will call for an immediate DQ.
8. Champions The championship belts are as follows and must be defended in the designated time or the champion will be stripped of the title, Wrestlers are only allowed to have one belt, if you should end up with two belts you will be forced to drop the lesser belt, wrestlers will usually (but not always)
defend the belts on every PPV and on the occasional weekly show, a title may be defended on a house show at the discretion of the current champion.:
WSEW World Heavyweight Champion - Once a Month WSEW Eurocontinental Champion - Once A Month WSEW Hardcore Champion - Once Every Two Weeks WSEW TV Champion - Every Week WSEW TAG Champions - Every Two Weeks 9. Stables Stables may be formed in this federation and there is NO limit has to how many people you have in your stable, however this could change during the course of the federation.
10. Roleplay This is what makes or breaks your WSEW wrestling career, the more and better you roleplay the more chance you will have of success, roleplays should be at least 20 lines long and i will only accept ONE roleplay from you per roleplay show. Roleplays must be sent to [name withheld] and the president will dispatch your roleplay and all the other roleplays received from that day to the members mailbox's through email. You are expected to roleplay at least
ONCE every two weeks to keep your WSEW contract active, if you fail to roleplay your contract will be terminated, if you are going on vacation, doing exams and would rather not roleplay for that period of time you will be granted leave from the WSEW and your contract will not be terminated.
Also we are looking for QUALITY and not quantity, your roleplay could be 100 lines long but if it is repetitive and mind bogging boring you are more than likely going to lose to someone who does 20 line roleplays that get straight to the point. You may only roleplay for yourself and no one else. If you are a tag team or in a stable you can include the other wrestler(s) in your roleplays however they can only make small comments and nothing special or important. You MAY NOT include real wrestlers in your roleplays remember
WSEW is in a reality that WWF, WCW, ECW etc does not exist. 10.1 In-Ring Interviews Instead of doing a roleplay for the week you could try and get more attention for your wrestler by doing an in-ring interview. Working in the same manner as a roleplay but instead of it being sent out on the roleplay show it would be seen on one of WSEW's Premier Wrestling shows. Say what you want and get what you need to get off your chest with the members paying their full attnetion and also with the world watching.
11. Sneak Attacks For the good of the federation and to have as much member input as possible you may use sneak attacks on a card, sneak attacks are basically if you have a grudge against someone and want to get revenge or cost them a match, you can come down the ring, bash them over with a head and cost them a match, beat them up in the locker room, the possiblites are endless, however sneak attacks must be for a reason and i need a paragraph to explain your motives, you may only use one sneak attack per card and it is possible for sneak attacks to go against you if you are using them to much.Sneak Attacks can only be used on weekly TV shows and PPV's not house shows.
12. Upsets (via Sneak Attacks) For the good of the federation, or for the good of angles, there is a distinct possibility that an "upset" could happen to you, for example even if you roleplay better than your opponent you may still lose the match, but
ONLY if someone has used good sneak attacks against you, it happens in real wrestling, someone gets hit over the head with a chair and the other person gets a win. You may be required to do "The Job" but it has been known that these people are usually highly rewarded in the near future. You will only be upset once every three months at the most. If someone has used sneak attacks against you but you absoultly whipped your opponent in roleplaying you are more than likely going to recover and still win the match.
13. Deadlines Deadlines are very important and must be met for your roleplays and sneak attacks to count towards a match. Take into account Thursday Fury is on a
Thursday (obviously) so sending in a sneak attack Thursday morning is not a good idea as the majority of the card will be written and it may be to late to the change the card. Roleplays must also be sent on time to be included in a particular roleplay show and to also count towards your match should you have one, missing the deadline by a few minutes can be disastrous to your chances of victory so make sure you get it all done on time. Also remember the prez does this out the kindness of his heart, so there may be times when he also is unable to post a card on the correct date and meet his own deadlines, should this happen you will be made aware of it through email and possibly be given more time to send roleplays and sneak attacks.
14. Backstage WSEW Backstage is for the use of WSEW Members to spread news and create rumours. The information on the message board can be either true or false.
When using WSEW Backstage please create a fake reporters name, or use the name of any member of WSEW Staff Roster (Except [name withheld]) do not use your wrestlers name unless you feel it is of prime importance that you should use your wrestlers name. Please do not start any Backstage wars or the board will be deleted from WSEW Web Site.
15. Chat Room WSEW Chat Room can be used for members to interact with one another, you may set up your own private WSEW chats. Please do not arrange chats with other members when a WSEW scheduled chat has been arranged. The chat room should only be used for WSEW orientated stuff only. No advertising, cybersex or any other discrimating actions should take place in WSEW Chat Room.
16. No Asking Asking for matches, or asking to win matches will result in you being jobbed in your next match, to get a match against someone specific you will need to challenge them in a roleplay and they must accept your offer.
