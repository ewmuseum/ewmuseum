IEW Re-Opening

Jun 20, 2000, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IEW is reopening. We originally opened on 06/22/1999, and I have a lot of experience writing results and running the fed. I now have a staff to help me keep the roster updated and the like, and the old members are back for another go-round. All we need now is the next generation of IEW superstars to make the fed's return really special. The old members of IEW are quick to agree that IEW is a great fed, and the return of so many of them is a stamp of approval I don't take lightly. Please check out the following documents if you are interested in joining an e-fed. Our first show is
Friday Night Frenzy on 06/23, so get to steppin'!
