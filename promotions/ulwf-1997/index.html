Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A separate promotion's own Hall of Fame announcement, posted 9 August 1997, is the only surviving trace of the Unified League of Federations' own founding: its author, introducing an unrelated project, mentions in passing that an earlier attempt of his, “the ULWF (A Unified League of Federations),” had “fallen through.”

That reading was premature. By 23 November 1997 the same operator posted his own operational update for it as a going interfed alliance and tournament body, inviting other feds to “Register” into a “Super PPV” ahead of a December deadline for a chance at “32-Man Champion of Champions Tournament” action, “Special Interfed Matches,” and matches drawn “FROM YOUR OWN FED,” pitching the exposure directly: “Be known as the Fed that has the Champion of Champions on it's books.”

A third-party federation's own December 1997 merger report independently states that a fed called Lost Horizons had, that month, “dissolved the alliances with the SJPW and ULWF” — corroborating that it was still operating that December, though the report itself hedges the claim as unconfirmed. No card, tournament result, or closing notice of its own survives.
