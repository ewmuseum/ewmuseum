Best finisher

31 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have a couplea finishers for ya...... One is actually my own.....called the Presidential Pardon... I slingshot the person into the turnbuckle....then as they are falling back down...I grab them and turn it in to a neckbreaker..... Another is a tag-team finisher(I dunno if this counts but here goes)... One man picks up the opponent and gives them a brainbuster... as he's about to hit...the other jumps off the top and does the Flash funk double flip legdrop across the back of his head.... The move is called the Snake Strike, by a teg-team called The Snake Strike... Thanx and Have a nice day Prez SanMan President of the BAWF and all around nice guy
