Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The SPWO's own December 1997 recruitment posts dated its run by counting back: “We have been open 6 MONTHS NOW,” one read on the 20th, and “going 7 months now” the next day, placing its own founding around the middle of 1997. It billed itself plainly — “the hottest thing going today” — and asked newcomers to choose one wrestler, “Real or fake,” to join.

Its own roster mixed the two: a wrestler billed as Dean Malenko recruited a stable in one post, while a companion ad the next day named invented wrestlers Weapon X, Sabu, Mr Malik, Ken dAVIDSON, Death, and Insane Wayne among its “top stars.”

No card, roster list, or title of its own survives. A wrestler's own account, given a year later after moving on to a different promotion, recalls having passed through its ranks — the only trace placing the SPWO's own operation as far as 1998.
