The Seorf E-Wrestling Federation, known only from nine surviving Usenet posts between March and October 1997, repeated pleas for a writer and a blank wrestler-application template, with no card, roster or result ever recovered. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Seorf E-Wrestling Federation announced itself in a brief recruitment post on 24 March 1997, naming a president and a vice-president in its own signature block and asking anyone interested in “a fantasy wrestling simulator” to write in for information.

What survives after that founding post is a run of further appeals, not a fed running shows. By June its vice-president was writing under that title to recruit a match writer; by September and again in October its president was doing the same, once attributing the fed's own ongoing trouble directly to “my writting skills” and asking for help “to get the fed running more faster.” The October post also gives the promotion a second name for itself, “Seorf Email Wrestling Federation,” in place of the founding post's “Seorf E-Wrestling Federation.”

A blank wrestler application posted 8 July 1997 is the fullest picture of the roster it hoped to build: fields for name, weight, height, finishing move, hometown, entrance song, motto, heel/face/neutral alignment, and manager or valet.

No card, roster, or result document for the promotion was ever recovered. Its last surviving post is dated 12 October 1997, still asking for a writer.
