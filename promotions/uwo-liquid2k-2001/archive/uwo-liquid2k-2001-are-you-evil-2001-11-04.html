Are You Evil? Join the UWO Today

4 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Below is the kind of RPin that I personally do in this federation I am.. the UWO. A federation where people really strive to make themselves better writers and work the angles so all involved have a good time. This is a game, and no matter what side of the fence you want your chatarcter to be or what skill level you are.. there's always something for everyone. Don't be dissuaded by the rp, just read it =) Its the kinda writing you can expect in the UWO, but like I said.. just a taste and something fun. Critique if you wish.. Be entertained please. =) If the federation interests you.. please head to the webpage at http://liquid2k.com/uwo/ or email me at [detail withheld] ============================================= (It's the Halloween season, when people recreate themselves and everything around them to be a little more frightening, all in the name of good fun. Haunted houses, strange noises around every corner and in the shadows as we walk through the night are just ways to tantalize our sense of fear. Unfortunate as it is, then, that the most frightening things usually happen in broad daylight. We find ourselves outside of a large warehouse, oddly familiar. The camera has been placed directly in front of the sliding double doors and, judging by the shadow across it, it's either the middle of the morning or the middle of the afternoon. The door twitches forward slightly, as if someone leans up against it. There's a scratching sound coming from behind the door, as if a paw or fingernail would sound if it was grating against the plywood backing of it. As the scratching continues, it's obvious that whatever is on the other side is desperately trying to get the door opened to get out. No words can be heard, but a muffled grunting from whatever comes from behind it. The door shakes, but doesn't budge from it's position. It leans forward a little more as whatever props itself up against it.. but it does not open. Another shaking of the door.. one after another, followed by grunting and whining pleas from the entity behind it. Finally. A half inch closer to freedom as the door opens a crack. Settled dust is let fly as it happens, but the entity behind the door does not cease. Whatever reason it needs to get out.. it knows it's not any closer than it was but mere seconds ago, and that it has a long way to go yet. Little by little, though, the door slides open. Inch by half-inch.. until there's enough room that an arm could actually stick through it. But what grips it is far more disturbing that just an ordinary arm. The hand of a large, black male snakes itself out, walking as if it's fingers were legs, and grips the end of the door. The hand itself is splattered with dried blood, a grisly sight to behold as it grips the door.. occasionally tugging to try and open it just a little bit more. As grunts of fear, determination and hope continue to come from behind the door.. another sound can be heard, slowly getting louder and louder. Footsteps. Boots upon concrete, getting closer.. and with each louder footstep they seem to be gaining speed. Seeming to charge, the sounds echoing behind the door and, even though it's muffled, resounding through it. Charging until the door caves outwards slightly and the sound of boot connecting to body is heard. The man grunts with a pain that was expected, but still was not welcome, and yet his hand does not falter from it's grip upon the door. As the sound of boot to body continues, he does not let go of the small ray of hope that he can open the door.. that he can get away from his assailant and get to the outside and to freedom. The sound quickly goes from the boots to fists upon flesh.. The cracking sound of a nose breaking.. the wet sounds made when a fist connects with a bloody pulp of a face. The hand.. remains trying to open the door. Gripped with the furious strength of need. The NEED to escape. Through the punishment, the arm twitches, trying to push the door open. Freedom... Freedom... The punching stops. The scuffling sound of someone standing off, then the sound of footsteps as the booted one begins to walk away. Quieter... Quieter.. Stopping. We hear a piece of what is probably wood hit the floor in a clatter, and all of a sudden the footsteps are charging back.. at a pace that destroys the other. The scuffle of feet as they lift up off of the ground... And the next sound is what can only be made by a very large man, slamming himself and a large piece of wood into a lifeless form against a large door. The door recognizes this, and almost completely buckles under the immense pressure handed to it. The hand... Falls limp. Pieces of wood again clatter to the floor. There's a scuffling of feet, then the limp arm and hand are slowly pulled back into the darkness from which it came. Silence follows for a good twenty seconds after the footsteps and dragging noises fade off.. enough for people to wonder what it was all about and if it was in fact over. That's when a slightly smaller, more lithe, white hand sneaks out of the door and pushes it open with one fluid motion. The camera is left seeing a concrete floor, stained red with the blood of the black man. The lines of blood go down the hall and around a corner, but where the door was is a large pool of it, being soaked up by small bits of wood here and there. Careful to avoid this, is a pair of black boots. Under a pair of black pleather pants. Under a grey wife-beater T-shirt. Under white dreadlocks, pulled back in a tail to avoid getting in one's eyes. The one being Jakob Venom. He smiles cordially to the camera, though it never quite makes it to his eyes. Two steps and he is out of the warehouse, closing the door behind him. The slam echoes throughout the area outside, and in.) Jakob Venom: Examples must be made to keep the general populace in line. Booker T was made an example for not being a part of any crusade against me... (The grin deepens, the look in his eye signifies something very.. very evil.) Jakob Venom: This man signifies those who attempt to join in, defeating me. It is quite simple. Shocktroops mean nothing. Lone rangers mean nothing. People who want to hide from me mean nothing. You are all a set of plebeians who are going to be my personal playtoys until I decide you need to be broken. It is just that simple. I am your terror. I am your fear. I am your dread. I am your anguish. I am your end. (He laughs.) Jakob Venom: Armageddon has arrived. Who is next on the list? No one can know for sure... save for Crossbreed and myself. Don't think that you are doomed just because a little prick of a man schedules matches against myself or my partner. Don't think that if you don't show up to the arenas that you are safe against my actions. You are the weak. I am the strong. I am here to reign over you, to guide you, to PUNISH you. Don't fight it. (The door slides open, and out steps X-Breed. His large, muscled physique coupled by the bloodstains on his black T-shirt and blue jeans, certainly makes him imposing. Jakob pays little mind though, only looking back for a second.) X-Breed: It's done. Call's been made. We should leave before they arrive. Jakob Venom: Let's bone then, eh? X-Breed: Definately. (Jakob reaches in his pocket and pulls out a small case. Opening it, he removes a pair of sunglasses from it and applies them to his face. Looking back to the camera he shakes his head.) Jakob Venom: Do not worry. I don't usually end lives, I only choose to alter them. Your war hero shall receive a purple heart and be shipped back to live in a hospital for a couple months.. but he shant die. I'm not that cruel. (A cold laugh as he turns away from the camera and begins to walk off. The camera pans to follow, watching as both mean head to a rusty, tan van parked about two hundred feet away. About halfway they stop, turn to the camera and motion for it to follow. The camera fades to black as it follows them.) ====================================================== If the federation interests you.. please head to the webpage at http://liquid2k.com/uwo/ or email me at [detail withheld] ========================================= You'll feel it when I stamp it on your forehead So you will never forget That you're a reject, and you're no one, and you're nothing BrKn version 2.0 Excellence through off-topic posting.
