Hi, [name withheld] here. Some of you may remember a thing on here for a league, "CeWF". Well, I am that league's president. We had to change the name, because a league was already using a similiar acronym. WAeW is now this fed. We first announced formation Jan. 8th, and since then we just reached our 25th wrestler! We have 17 solo and only 4 tag. I'd like to bring that 20 solo and 6 tag or so, which is why I'm calling any tag teams intrested in a new fed, whose fed hed has expirience to join. Solos are welcome as well, but we'd really appreciatte tags too. Four tags isn't enough for us in my mind. So, if WAeW intrests you, e-mail me at [detail withheld] for the intro packet and creation sheet. OR, check out the web page (which still is missing a few profiles). The addy for that is : http://members.aol.com/[detail withheld]/index.html WAeW, one of the fastest growing leagues around is calling YOU to come and join. Not sure if WAeW is for you? Ask to be put on the mailing list and/or get a tr out. If you don't like it, feel free to drop out if you so choose. No permanent commitments! WAeW officially begins action Feb. 16th, but roleplays are already coming in and the report show starts Wednesday the 22nd. Spots in the tournaments are filled, but everyone who thinks they can roleplay...come on and join! WAeW Fed Hed/Match & Report Writer, [name withheld] [detail withheld]
