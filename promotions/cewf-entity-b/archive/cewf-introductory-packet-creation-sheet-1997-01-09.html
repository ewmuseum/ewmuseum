CeWF Introductory Packet,Creation Sheet

************************************* CeWF Championship e-Wrestling Federation Introductory Packet by [name withheld] ************************************** SECTION ONE: a.Introduction and background b.local programming c.ppvs d.titles SECTION TWO: a. roleplaying tips & info b.info on creation sheet c.creation sheet d.Staff **************************************** SECTION ONE **************************************** PART A: Introduction and background CeWF is run by [name withheld]. Zach has had some past expirience running a league. Starting in June 1996, he ran ERWI. In January, it merged with another organazation. Origiannlly, Zach was co-president. He stepped down, with plans of doing a project he had been thinking of on the side. That side project would become his main focus:CeWF. Zach runs this league by himself and only himself. While the roleplaying and participation of the members is vital, Zach holds the key to success. A good president and a helper person. For the most part when you don't annoy him, Zach is a fun yet weird person who's easy to talk to. At anytime you have a question, comment or suggestion--Let him know! Zach wants full credit for running a successful league if it works, and like-wise full blame if it falters. In order for CeWF to be a quality league, we need quality roleplays. We currently imploy a one week minimum, one a day maximum. We reccomend for a succesful character to do either a roleplay for the weekly flashes or the reports. For the better roleplays, you may want to do two or three through-out the week as well as one during the report. That is optional, but certianly recommended if you'd like to. Likewise, for those computer freaks who can't stop writing, only ONE roleplay a day will be accepted as a maximum. Should we get a second, it will be deleted no matter how good it is. So don't do more than one a day, and no less than one a week! Read on for more information, and I hope when it comes to the creation sheet you fill it out and join right away. CeWF...we're not the best, we're not the worst. We're just there, and that's all that matters. ============================================= Part B Local Programming CeWF has it's own "channal" on local affiliates across the world, known as CeWF Center. It runs round the lock 24 hours a day with flashes, cards, and reports. Here's a schedule and guideline of the programming. LIVE CARD:CeWF presents one live card a week, CeWF Sunday Night. This show will usually feature three for four "dark matches" as well as 5-7 live matches. Sometimes there may be a little less, but bare with it, sometimes Zach gets a little flogged in real life with time. ARENA CARD:Each Monday night, CeWF presents a weekly arena house show with around ten dark matches. REPORTS:CeWF presents CeWF Rewind every Wednesdays. This features a card report and preview, as well as some flashes and news. FLASHES:In additional to flashes for the report, the more frequent flashes are sent out in batches one after another every other day. Flashes are sent to Zach ONLY, and not to the whole list. =================================================== Part C PPVs CeWF will present pay per view spectaculars roughly every three months to ten weeks. These cards will usually feature title matches, grudge matches, and top contendership matches. The cards usually have around ten to twelve matches. Two PPVs are already scheduled for CeWF's 1997 calender year. They are... MARCH 30TH:Inagural Bash The first ppv, a few weeks after the league is up and running. Tournament finals are held here as well. JULY 4TH:Sunwave Meltdown The hottest feuds at the time come boiling over with FIREWORKS on the 4th of July. Sunwave Meltdown is gonna be HOT. ===================================================== Part D Titles What would a league be without titles to strive for? There are three titles CURRENTLY sanctioned by CeWF. They are as follows. CeWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE:The big one. Universal weight limit. Defended at PPVs only, unless at President's request. CeWF NORTH AMERICAN TITLE:The next best thing. 290 pounds and under is this weight limit. Defended on PPVs and occasionally on weekly shows. CeWF WORLD TEAM TEAG TITLES:The big one for tag teams. Universal weight limit. Defended at PPVs onlu, unless at President's request. ======================================================= ******************************* SECTION TWO ******************************* PART A Roleplaying tips and info As stated earlier, we have a "one a week minimum, one a day maximum" flashing rule. Those who repetedly break this rule will be warned, then suspended if it contines for two weeks. If they_still_don't follow the rule, they are officially off the roster. But I am usually lenient, so if you come up with some excuse...("My dog ate my roleplay") then I'll probably fall for it. :) Here are some tips I give you for roleplaying. -Set the scene. Where is he, what's he wearing. -What are his thoughts on the latest angle or match he's involved in? -put yourself in character...what woudl YOU say? -Make it neat. Use puncuation and correct spelling if possible. Use brackets for scene, and stuff like "laughs" or "John Doe turns around in his chair, a cold gaze in his eyes." Brackets are either [,(,{, or <. I personnaly like the [s, but anyone will do. ==================================================== PART B Info on Creation Sheet Each handler is limited to one solo wrestler and one tag team as of now. That may change later, depending on things. When you're filling it out, make sure this character is one you can roleplay for. IE don't make a russian character than doesn't speak english if you don't know Russian. Also, don't fill out apps for real wrestlers, like Shawn Michaels or Sabu. Original only. Speaking of original, try to stay away from a copy of a real wrestler. No Men from the Darkside or Hitmen or NWO,etc. Be original, and have fun. Think of a cool charatcer you can roleplay for...and make him! ===================================================== PART C Creation Sheet Fill out and send to [detail withheld] as soon as done. /\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\ Handler's Name: E-Mail Address: Wrestler's Name: Height/Weight: Hometown: Ring Music: Ring Attire: Top Moves: Style: Stregnths/Weaknesses: Finisher and Desciption: Manager (if any): Heel/Face/Neutral: Personality/Motivation: Background/History: \/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/ ========================================================= PART D Staff Match Writer, Report writer, booker, owner,president, complete ruler and master of CeWF...
