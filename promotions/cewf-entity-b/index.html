Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Championship e-Wrestling Federation announced itself on 8 January 1997 as a roleplaying-only league planned to open that March, laying out its own shape before it had a roster: one weekly card, one weekly arena show, one weekly report, frequent flashes, World, North American, and World Tag titles, and creation-sheet limits meant, in its own words, “to stop gimmick copies of supersized freaks.”

Its own introductory packet, posted the next day, described a solo operator who had previously co-run an earlier league before splitting off to build this one instead. Programming ran on a strict rhythm: CeWF Sunday Night as the live card, an arena house show every Monday, and a one-roleplay-per-day, one-per-week minimum enforced hard enough that a second same-day submission would be deleted “no matter how good it is.” Its own motto set expectations plainly: “we're not the best, we're not the worst. We're just there, and that's all that matters.”

Nine days after that packet, on 17 January 1997, its own operator announced a rename: another league was already using a similar acronym, so the Championship e-Wrestling Federation became WAeW. By then the roster stood at twenty-five wrestlers, seventeen solo and four tag teams, still recruiting more tag teams under the new name, with official action set to begin that 16 February and its report show the following week.
