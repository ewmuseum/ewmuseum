Sunday Night Slam Masters 11/9/97 Part IV

Nov 11, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[King:] Ladies and Gentlemen, this contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, already in the ring. "The Royal Lawyer" Carl Stevenson. And making his residence from "The Royal Kingdom". Weighing in at 575 pounds. Here is "THE ROYAL CHAMPION"... JONATHAN MONARCH! [Crowd is booing, but then starts to cheer as "Top of the World" by Van Halen plays. A man wearing long blue tights with a vertical white stripe comes down to ringside. He wears white boots with a blue Z on them. A blue vest with "ZODIAC" writing in white against a night sky. A woman follows, wearing a blue dress.] [King:] His opponent. Led to the ring by Diamond. From Knoxville, Tennessee. Weighing in at 296 pounds. Here is... Z O D I A C !!! [DING DING DING] [Adams:] This will be a good one. [Reed:] I hope so, or else I'm just wasting time. [Adams:] Both men locking up, and Monarch using his weight to get an advantage by backing Zodiac in a corner. [Reed:] Monarch is blasting Zodiac with hard rights, and the ref is telling him to back off! [Adams:] Monarch backs off... AND ZODIAC NAILS MONARCH WITH A PUNCH OVER THE REFEREE'S HEAD! [Reed:] Monarch is stunned. Zodiac off the ropes. DROPKICK! Monarch is stunned. [Adams:] Another, and Monarch is teetering! [Reed:] Off the ropes again... and Stevenson trips Zodiac! [Adams:] Zodiac yelling at Stevenson... from behind, SMALL PACKAGE! 1... 2... kickout! [Reed:] Monarch up and rebounds off the ropes... splash... NO! [Adams:] Zodiac back up. Dropping the elbow... MONARCH MOVES! [Reed:] Both men on the canvas now! Stevenson yelling at Monarch, and Diamond is shouting for her man! [Adams:] Monarch is the first one up. Walks over to Zodiac and picks him up and just SLAMS HIM DOWN! [Reed:] Off the ropes for another splash... NO! [Adams:] Zodiac does it again! He grabs a hold of Monarch. Takes him by the face.RUNNING FACESLAM! [Reed:] We have to remember, Zodiac can't do much! Monarch outweighs him by 279 pounds. [Adams:] And he's making the most of it. FLYING LEGDROP! A pin! 1... 2... kickout by Jonathan Monarch, The Royal Champion. [Reed:] Zodiac takes Monarch and throws him into the turnbuckles...STINGER SPLASH! [Adams:] AND MONARCH MOVES OUT OF THE WAY! [Reed:] Zodiac is down and Monarch covers. 1... 2... kickout! [Adams:] Monarch gets up. He walks over to the ropes. HE WALKS ALL OVER ZODIAC! [Reed:] He picks up Zodiac. SIDEWALK SLAM! [Adams:] Another cover. 1... 2... another kickout. [Reed:] Monarch wants to finish this off! [Adams:] He places Zodiac on the top turnbuckle. [Reed:] Up on the second rope... MOVE OF CHAMPIONS! [Adams:] NO! ZODIAC BLOCKED IT! INVERTED ATOMIC DROP FROM THE TOP ROPE! [Reed:] AND THE ROYAL CHAMPION WITH BE FEELING THAT FOR A WHILE! [Adams:] I don't know where Zodiac pulled that out of, but I bet he's glad he did! [Reed:] Zodiac crawls over to Monarch and drapes his arm over him! 1... 2... 3! [Adams:] HE DID IT! [Crowd:] YYYYYEEEEEAAAAHHHH! [Reed:] NO! NO! THE REFEREE IS SIGNALLING A TWO COUNT! [Adams:] And Zodiac is not pleased about that! [Reed:] Zodiac struggles to get to his feet. He walks over to Monarch... CLAWHOLD! HE HAS A CLAWHOLD ON HIM! [Adams:] MONARCH IS IN SEVERE TROUBLE! [Reed:] Wait a second... who's coming down the asile... IT'S THE LBC! THE LONG BEACH CONNECTION IS COMING TO RINGSIDE! [Adams:] What business do they have here? [Reed:] They approach Diamond! THEY'RE FORCING THEMSELVES ON HER! [Adams:] AND ZODIAC IS FUMING! HE RUNS OVER TO DIAMOND AND THE LBC. [Reed:] And Stevenson decided to capitalize on the situation. He just handed a pair of Brass Knucks to Monarch! [Adams:] AND HE NAILS ZODIAC WITH THEM! RIGHT AT THE BASE OF HIS HEAD! [Reed:] And Zodiac is out... and the LBC make their way out. [Adams:] Zodiac placed on the top rope... MOVE OF CHAMPIONS BY MONARCH! [Reed:] COVER! 1... 2... THREE! IT'S ALL OVER! [DING DING DING] [King:] Here is your winner... "THE ROYAL CHAMPION" JOHNATHAN MONARCH! ["Everybody Hurts" by REM plays and the crowd boos.] [Adams:] Impressive win from Johnathan Monarch!!! Zodiac is up, and he's running to the locker room! The LBC are certainly making their share on enemies tonight! [Reed:] And it's only beginning... these Irish folks are very easily drunken... they're _all_ off their rockers. [Adams:] It does seem that way Dallas... but next week, we're gonna be in England! They really know how to get wasted! [Reed:] Indeed. Have we got a camera back there? [Reed:] Why would he be back there? Trying to peek in the locker rooms? [Adams:] He's just doing his job, Dallas. [Reed:] Um, his job is to peek in the locker rooms? Remind me not to ever change back there. [The camers move into the locker room area, and you see Los Raptores, Aquila and Gavilan, closing a pair of lockers. Aquila is wielding Joey Cadwall's Happy Hammer, and Gavilan has Baba Louie's toy lawnmower. They're pounding on Dwayne Casey Malone with them, but they suddenly stop. Aquila looks up at the camera.] Aquila: See, it doesn't even hurt the putita Dwayne. How is this going to please all the foaming GFWA marks? GO hardcore!!! Gavilan: Yeah! WE WANT BLOOD!!! WE WANT BLOOD!!! [The crowd picks up this chant in typical GFWA style, and Los Raptores take off out of the locker room, Dwayne in tow. Joey Cadwall enters from another door, and opens his locker. The picture fades out, but not before...] Cadwall: What the hell is this?! WHERE'S MY HAPPY HAMMER?!? ["Rockit" by Herbie Hancock begins playing over the PA.] King: Ladies and gentlemen, this tag team attraction is set for one fall, with a thirty minute time limit, and the losers of the match will be beaten by the other team's primary weapon! Introducing first, weighing in at a combined weight of 470 pounds...Baba Louie and Destroy Man...THE...EXTREAM... FREAKS!!! [The crowd cheers as Destroy Man emerges from the back...then he goes right back? He comes out again, assisting Baba Louie in dragging a lawnmower to the ring. It isn't the standard toy lawnmower, and Baba looks very upset. Destroy Man is a balding man in his thirties, with black hair, a black beard, and a black moustache to top it all off. He has a pot belly that hangs out over his tights. He wears pant length mauve tights and boots, with a green stripe running down the left leg, as well as green trunks. 'Destroy Man' is written on the back of his trunks in red permanent marker. Mister Meanie trails closely behind DM, carrying a bunch of titles from God-only-knows where. Baba Louie has green and blue hair, with a cyan/magenta/yellow/pink/black/white/green/red/dark blue jumpsuit. He's got a small yellow radio in one hand. Baba looks very confused, as he can't get advice from his toy lawnmower. Baba and Destroy Man climb into the ring, Destroy Man looking quite ready, and Baba pleading to KLEE to guide him through the match.] King: And their opponents... [King:] Introducing first, from South Pennslyvania ... standing five foot eleven and weighing in at two hundred and seventeen pounds. He is reconized as an _OFFICIAL_ dipstick, led to the ring by his girlfriend, Staci, and his cat, Scruffy, here is Joey "the Dreamer" Cadwall! ["Generic Circus Music" by the Happy Island Band plays as the crowd breaks into a chant of "Dipstick! Dipstick!". Staci comes out first, wearing a red skirt and Wylde Dreams tee shirt, holding the orange tabby in her arms. Joey follows behind, dressed accordingly. He's got on his navy boots, sky blue tights with 'Dream' written on the back, and wears a tie dye sky blue and creamy white half shirt. His short blond hair is moused into a sideways part, and his goofy grin is there as always. In his hand is a twelve pound sledge, and he doesn't look happy about having to lug the damn thing around. Joey, Staci, and Scruffy stop in mid-aisle.] [King:] And, his partner ... from Every Girl's Dream - also South Pennslyvania - standing in at six foot even and weighing in at two hundred thirty five pounds ... Scotty B. Wylde! ["Born to be Wild" by Steppenwolf plays, and the crowd basically ignores Scotty's entrance, focused on cheering Joey. Scotty wears his usual red trunks and black boots with 'Born to be Wylde' printed across the rump. His short black hair is slicked back. He meets the trio in the aisle and they begin to walk to the ring.] [King:] Together, they are the RSPWF number one hundred and fifty sixth best team ... ___WYLDE DREAMS___! [Wylde enters the ring, while Cadwall takes his place in a corner.] [Reed:] And Destroy Man isn't wasting any time here, he charges Scotty B. Wylde and nails him with a clothesline! DM whips Wylde into the ropes -- knee to the midsection, and Wylde is down. DM picks Wylde up -- crotch slam, and the freaks are dominating this matchup. [Adams:] Destroy Man lifts up Wylde, he's setting up for a running powerslam...no, Wylde slips off the shoulder, and shoves DM into the turnbuckle! Destroy Man bounces out -- and Wylde hits him with a German suplex! One... Two... Kickout! [Reed:] Baba Louie is still praying to Klee...uh oh, I think he's just received guidance. [Adams:] Baba Louie climbs into the ring, and he looks like he's been possessed! Baba charges into Wylde Dreams' corner and slingshots Joey Cadwall in! Baba picks Cadwall up -- oh my, he just nailed him with a low blow! [Reed:] He'll feel that in the morning...if Baba Louie didn't knock his nads up to far he spits them out, anyway. [Adams:] That's disgusting, Dallas. Baba Louie tosses Cadwall over the top rope, and he stays down, cradling his...uh...injured parts. Meanwhile, Destroy Man has Scotty B. Wylde down in a corner. He tells Baba to get in the opposite corner, picks him up, irish whip -- and Baba charges out with a clothesline that levels Wylde! Uh oh...they're going for the Total Kleemation! [Reed:] Baba stands back...Destroy Man sets up....TOTAL KLEEMATION! DM with the cover! One... Two... Three! [DING, DING, DING] King: Your winners, at a time of 3:52...EXTREAM FREAKS! [DM climbs to the outside and tries to lift up the lawn mower.
