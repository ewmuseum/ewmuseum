Sunday Night Slam Masters 11/9/97 Part V

Nov 11, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Adams:] Extream Freaks with a very dilluded victory, folks. And now... [Tony is interrupted by "All Along the Watchtower" by Jimi Hendrix.] (Jak Martin comes out through the entrance ramp to a very mixed reaction. He's wearing a shirt that says "Halloween isn't in November", a black NY hat, and a pair of jeans. His hair is tied back in a ponytail which sticks out of his hat. He gets in the ring with a mic, to the fans chanting at him.) Jak: First thing, I'd like to do is to apologize for my actions at A Nightmare in Newton. It was wrong of me to do what I did. I shouldn't have gotten involved in that match, and give the victory and the title to Alex Reaver. I should've down something else. I should've taken the belt, for myself, cause as we all know I'm the true champion. Alex Reaver, every night when you go to sleep with that belt, you should get down on your hands and knees and thank me for the fact that you have that belt. Reaver, two weeks from now you have a big match. Remember what happened last time you faced him. Jak: Next up, Shawn Adams. How does it feel to come so close to victory, then have it taken away. It was nice to be on top, wasn't it Shawn. You enjoyed having everything there for you, the world champ. Then, what happened. The past came back, and it bit you right square in the ass. I'm sure all of you saw that little PPV held by MLWO a couple of weeks back. Torture Chamber 2. Well, let me let you in on a little secret. I was in Torture Chamber 1, my very first major card. In a tag team match. Take a wild guess who I teamed up with. Thats right, FORMER World Champion Shawn Adams. I carried his ass in his MLWO run. Till he decided to run scared cause of the biggest bullshit neck injury I ever saw. Now, Shawn Adams is nothing more than a Stone Cold wannabe on a losing streak. And thats the bottom line, cause Jak said so. (shakes his head, and grins) (takes off his hat, and undoes his hair) Jak: So, tell you what I'm gonna do. You two have a big match coming up on, the umm...whats the date?? I don't care. But, since there was such an uproar over my last appearance in your match, I decided to do something. I have signed a piece of paper, which reads the following: (takes out a contract) Jak: (reading): I, Jak Martin, agree to not be in the country of Italy on the night of November 21, 1997, during the matchup involving Alex Reaver and Shawn Adams for the GFWA World Heavyweight title. And should I get involved in the outcome of the matchup, I agree to leave GFWA for a period of 25 years. That is all. (pauses and goes to walk away from the ring, but then turns back) Jak: Wait a second, I aint done yet. There's something else I have on my mind. A couple of nights ago, I happened to catch that thing put out by the EWA called Halloween Hell. Now, correct me if I'm wrong, but isn't Halloween on October 31. When I saw this thing, my watch said Nov 6. So, I went to the Cable guide to see if it was a taped event. It wasn't it was a first time showing. Apparently this great and all mighty federation couldn't get a hugely hyped card to broadcast for a week after it happened. Simply pathetic. Hey... Reed. You can back me up... whataya think of the EWA? [Reed:] [over the arena PA] That... Baptist guy... he tried to *sniff* rape me. [_Big_ pop!] Jak Martin: I can sympathize with you, Dal. And then, how do they start the card off. By giving two guys the tag team titles. Shows you how pathetic the tag division is there. Then, we get 3 matches in the same night with the only 2 guys in the entire company who anyone ever gave a damm about. Only problem is Gaines and Dark D are washed up and over the hill. Dark D was in MLWO a few months back and got beaten by everyone and his little sister. Now he comes out with his Raven wanna be attitude with his 47 buddies. You got Cordell Crane, who needs to have his mouth taped up. Then you got Monkeyshine, what ever the fuck he's supposed to be. Then you got the brains behind the action, the dropout himself, former PCW President Brian Nelson. Oh yeah, and of course you got the Gaineses. The world's most fucked up family. That's one helluva a family tree I swear. Now, you're probably wondering why I'm saying this here, and not in MLWO. Truth is, I wanted to, but Cutter said to me if I said anything anti-EWA he wouldn't air it. He also demanded that I not show up unscheduled at EWA Halloween Hell to bash on EWA. But yet, there's this big war going on between EWA and MLWO. You want the truth about that whole. Its a load of crap. Baptist and Cutter are still buddies. They are just engaging in some sort of mutual dick sucking contest. Hell, Cutter seems to think this place is one big joke. But, yet his buddy Adams is still here. What a fucking hypocrite. Let me wrap up this up simply. All of these feds suck. [name withheld], so does GFWA. You want me to wrestle, get me a match with Adams. Its that simple. ["All Along the Watchtower" by Jimi Hendrix starts up again. Jak Martin exits Croke Park, to a massive pop.] [Adams:] Oh my god! The censors have been turned off, folks! Rick Baptist... Mark Cutter... Shawn Adams... _everybody_, Jak Martin means business! [Reed:] Apocalypse hold those prestigious belts at this time, and the winners will walk away with those titles, the losers with Crusher Tech's Atlantic Shore Tag Team Titles. [Adams:] Let's go up the ring and Jack King! [King:] Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is set for one fall, and is for the GFWA World Tag Team Titles _and_ Atlantic Shore Tag Team Titles! ["Pomp and Circumstance" blares over the PA as Crusher Tech enter Croke Park. The legendary tag team both wear black trunks with "Crusher Tech" in red across the back. They have the Atlantic Shore Tag Team Titles around their waists, and are slapping hands on their way to the ring.] [Adams:] These two guys are focused... they've won tag team gold _everywhere_, and they are looking to win the GFWA World Tag Team Titles right here in Ireland! [King:] Introducing first... the GFWA Atlantic Shore Tag Team Champions!!! [Pop!] [King:] Introducing first... from Boston, Massechusetts, and weighing in at 245 pounds... Mark "Tech" Stephens! His tag team partner, hailing from Detroit, Michigan, and weighing in at 310 pounds... Steve "Crusher" Kingston! They are collectively known as... Crusher... _Tech_!!! [Adams:] These two guys are simply warriors of the ring, and nobody has truly beaten these two guys in a contest. [Reed:] Many teams have come close, and a couple teams _did_ beat them, but they're recognized as the best tag team in wrestling history. [Adams:] Their opponents aren't too shabby themselves, Dallas. [An electric guitar version of "It's the End of the World As We Know It" by REM begins to play over the PA.] [King:] And their opponents are the GFWA _World_ Tag Team Champions! [Heel pop!] [King:] Residing from Miami, Florida... at a total combined weight of 620 pounds... Blitzkrieg... Colossus... _Apocalypse_!!! [Blitzkrieg steps out from behind the curtain in his wrestling attire, he has the tag title around his waist and has a mic in hand. He is all alone] [Blitzkrieg:] Damn... Can you imagine our misfortune... we were driving to the arena and some buttwipe soccer hooligan slammed into the side of my car. Well you see i was driving, and collossus was on the passenger side. While it was only a glancing blow... Colossus' got a bad case of Whiplash and banged up his knee, so the doctors tonight have said he is unable to fight since he is in the 'ospital as you would say it, for observations. [Crowd gasps, but boos at the notion that they may be being mocked] [Blitzkrieg:] But Fear not, because this match will still happen... you see i wanted to come out here and fight these punks alone... but President Coles insisted that i do not do that... so he insisted i find me a partner. So i thought and thought and thought... and then it hit me... who better to help me kick these pukes arses than the one the only Pike Rose. Now this will still be a Tag Team title match... except the players have changed a bit... [Pike Rose steps out from behind carrying the other tag strap, and he shakes hand with Blitzkrieg] [Blitzkrieg:] Lets start this massacre... [Adams:] Ladies and gentlemen, "Devastation" Pike Rose is gonna team with Blitzkrieg! But he's in that main event up next!!! [Reed:] I don't think that it matters to Pike, Tony. He's a warrior, and Crusher Tech are happy about this situation one bit... [Adams:] Folks... the Long Beach Connection are out _again_! They're here, and they're sitting in the front row... they kicked some kid and his dad out of their seats! [Reed:] Damn... I'm starting to like these pukes. First they beat up Colite and Iron Fist, then they cost Zodiac a match... and now they beat up a kid and his dad... [Adams:] ... sickening. [Reed:] Whatever. [DING, DING, DING] [Adams:] Stephens is gonna start things off with Blitzkrieg... he's still not sure what to make of this whole situation. Stephens locks up with Blitz, and gets him into a side headlock... into the takedown. [Reed:] Stephens is a great technical wrestler, hence the name "Tech", but Blitz is a very well-rounded technician himself... Blitz using his weight advantage to stand up, and nails a belly-to-back suplex! [Adams:] Stephens lands _hard_, and Blitzkrieg makes the tag to Pike Rose! [Reed:] In comes Pike, and this big monster pounds a legdrop into Stephens's chest... Pike bringing Stephens to his feet... the Irish whip into the opposite corner... Rose charges in, Stephens moves! Rose hits turnbuckle! Stephens turns him around, dropkick! The big 6'7" monster is staggering! [Adams:] Stephens with a lariat! Rose goes down... and the tag to Crusher Kingston! [Reed:] Oh man! Kingston weighs in at 310 pounds, giving Rose only a 50 pound advantage on him... granted, it's a lot of weight, but he had over 100 on Stephens! [Adams:] Kingston begins to stomp on Rose... picks him up... _scoop slam_! Kingston drops an elbow! Crusher brings Devastation to his feet... and places him in Crusher Tech's corner... the cross-corner whip... Rose hits _hard_... Kingston rushes in... avalanche! Rose goes down on one knee... Kingston with a boot to the side of the head! He almost took off Rose's head there! [Reed:] Kingston drags Rose to the center of the ring... and he stomps on Rose's abdomen! Kingston performing some liposuction at no charge for Pike Rose here... he brings Rose to his feet... inverted atomic drop! Lariat! [Adams:] Rose goes down hard again! Remember that he has to fight another match in just a few short minutes! [Reed:] Kingston tags in Stephens... who prompty escalates to the second rope... [Adams:] ... and he promptly drops a measured elbow! He goes for the pin... 1... 2... No! [Reed:] Stephens brings the large Rose to his feet... but Rose pokes him in the eye! Rose rushes over to tag Blitzkrieg... and he makes the tag! [Adams:] Blitz charges in... Japanese armdrag from Stephens! Into an armbar, and Blitzkrieg is in pain... Blitzkrieg stands up, but Stephens has this armbar locked in too tight... Stephens tags in Kingston... Kingston with a kick to the midsection, and Blitz goes down! [Reed:] Apocalypse... err, Blitz and Rose have been dismantled by the veteran team of Crusher Tech so far... [Adams:] Kingston has Blitzkrieg up... DDT! It's all over! Blitz crawls to the outside! [Reed:] Rose enters the ring! He nails Kingston from behind! Kingston falls out of the ring! Stephens is in! Stephens and Rose are beating each other in the ring... [Adams:] Outside of the ring, Kingston landed hard, and he's out, but Blitz is getting up... wait a minute... here comes Team X and Darkseid Inc.! Pietka, Uraki, X1, and X2 are out! They're all over Blitzkrieg! In the ring, Rose is pummelling Stephens in a corner... the ref doesn't see what's going on! Rose has Stephens out on the mat and... what the hell?! 1... 2... 3!!! [DING, DING, DING] [Reed:] Rose just laid down and put Stephens on top of himself! ["Pomp and Circumstance" plays over the PA.] [Adams:] New World Tag Team Champs! [King:] Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners... and _nnneeewww_ GFWA World Tag Team Champions... Crusher... _Tech_!!! [Reed:] Rose is walking out! Blitzkrieg is getting nailed by Darkseid Inc. and Team X, and Crusher Tech are walking away with the titles... Rose just turned on Apocalypse! [Adams:] Unbelievable!
