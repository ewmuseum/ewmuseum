Terror in the Triangle Part X

Jul 19, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FOR THE VACANT WORLD TITLE! WINNER MUST REACH SHORE! HAZARD vs. RANDALL LEWIS III [Reed:] Well, fans, we've been through a lot tonight, the return of the Boogieman, the debut of Alexander Creed, Mark Cutter geniously placing Shawn Adams in his place during the Northeastern Title Match, the Outcast going psychotic...but none of that matters now. [Adams:] That's right, because now, it's time for our main event! Folks, as you are well aware, the last GFWA World Champion, Greg Knight, was stripped of his title approxiamately a month ago due to injury. An eight man tournament was organized, and run. It came down to two men...the number five seed, Hazard, and the number six seed, Randall Lewis III. [Reed:] Those two men are about to fight each other right here on Terror in the Triangle, live from Elbow Beach, Bermuda. The winner is the first person to reach the shore. That person becomes the _new_ GFWA World Champion. Let's look at the roads that both men went through to get to this point. [Adams:] Well, Randall Lewis III defeated the Royal Champion by disqualification in what can be deemed a shady victory, but nevertheless, a victory. Then, he defeated the mighty "Jumbo" Jim Mongol one on one, advancing to the finals. [Reed:] Hazard started off the tournament by knocking off the rookie known as the Outcast, then went on to upset the number one seed, a man that out-sized Hazard by 350 pounds, King Death=Adder. They led up to this last match, and it's ready to happen. [Adams:] Let's not waste any more time, up to the penninsula ring with Jack King! [King:] Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is the _main event_ on the GFWA supercard, Terror in the Triangle!!! [Crowd:] YEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!! ["Waiting to Die" by Biohazard plays over the audio system on the beach.] [King:] This match is the finals of the GFWA World Title Tournament! The winner is the first man to reach shore, by any means necessary! [Hazard enters the beach area and walks upon the plank. He wears an orange t-shirt with a hazardous waste symbol upon it, black trunks, and black boots. He also has a fingerless black glove on his right hand.] [Crowd:] BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! [King:] Introducing first...seeded number _5_ in the tournament, and hailing from Brooklyn, New York, and weighing in at 213 pounds...here is, HAAAZZZAAARRRDDD!!! [Reed:] He's my pick to win it. [Adams:] I pick Lewis. [Reed:] I'm right. [Adams:] Let's look at the tale of the tape, before the introduction of Randall Lewis III. [The following visual aid appears on the screen, as Tony and Dallas converse over it.] Hazard Randall Lewis III ~-~-~- ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~ Height: 5'11" Height: 6'6" Weight: 213 lbs. Weight: 265 lbs. Finisher: "Hazardous Waste" - Finisher: "The Final Curtain" - split-legged moonsault fisherman's suplex [Reed:] Lewis has an obvious size advantage over Hazard, but that doesn't really matter, because we all saw what Hazard did to King Death=Adder, who was a 7 foot 3 beast. [Adams:] Hazard is just too deadly, he's likely to drown himself, or even Lewis. [Reed:] Have faith in this, one or both of these men is going to be injured severely before the night is out. [The theme from the musical "The Phantom of the Opera" plays on the beach audio system.] [King:] And his opponent is the _sixth_ seed in the tournament! [Crowd:] BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!! [King:] He hails from Oxford, England, and he weighs in at 265 pounds...ladies and gentlemen...here is, RANDALL...LEWIS...THE TTTHHHIIIRRRDDD!!!!!! [Randall enters the beach area, with a completely oblivious look on his face. A jagged shaped white mask covers his face partially. He wears black pants, a long sleeve button-up black shirt, and a large black cape. The cape is tied to his neck with a golden chain with a fireball symbol upon it.] [Adams:] Lewis looks like he's staring right through Hazard. The beach going crowd really hates both of these men, but they want to see a fight, and they're gonna see a good one! [Reed:] Lewis has stopped at the front of the plank. He just isn't moving, and he looks like he's afraid of Hazard...he's removing his cape, and now, he's approaching the ring. [Adams:] What good does a referee do in this situation? [Reed:] None, in fact, somebody should get rid of referee Pee-Wee Malone before he gets hurt. [Adams:] Lewis has finally reached the ring, quite methodically I may add, damn it's hot. [Reed:] Well, it looks like this match is ready to start...ladies and gents..._here we go_! [DING, DING, DING] [Adams:] Lewis continues to just stand in place...Hazard isn't going to go to him, however, since he's very focused as well. [Reed:] Lewis runs at Hazard, and Hazard ducks out and nails a drop toe-hold! Now Hazard jumps over into an armbar! Good technical skills being shown by Hazard, surprisingly. [Adams:] Lewis muscling himself up, and he nails an elbow to the stomach. Lewis runs into the ropes, and comes off with a flying lariat, he almost took off Hazard's head! Nice maneuver! Hazard on his stomach, and Lewis goes into the ropes, here he comes, legdrop to the back of the head! [Reed:] It's called a guillotine legdrop, genious. [Adams:] Sorry, tech boy. [Reed:] Don't make me beat you down like Wednesday...anyway, back to the action, Lewis has a chinlock on Hazard, trying to wear him down...if I was either of these men, I'd throw the other guy in the water and sprint on the plank to shore... [Adams:] That's actually a great strategy for this match, Dallas. [Reed:] I know. Lewis now releases the chinlock, and he's picking up Hazard..._big_ scoop slam! Lewis turns Hazard onto his chest, and he gets on a step-over toe hold, wraps in a facelock...STF! Hazard is screaming, Lewis really has this move locked in. He's really wrenching at the face of Hazard... [Adams:] Lewis finally lets go, but Hazard is hurt...Lewis is leaving the ring! He's walking on that plank to shore!!! He's gonna be the next champ! He's halfway there, wait, Hazard is up! He's running along the plank! Dropkick to the back, and down goes Lewis! What a suicidal move! [Reed:] Now Hazard is walking towards...the shore! He's gonna get out of here, no...he just stopped and went down to his knees. He's...picking at one of the boards that makes that plank! Now he's punching at it! [Adams:] Lewis is coming towards him, and Hazard gets up, not noticing Lewis...Lewis locks in a reverse headlock...double chickenwing up top...two-handed DDT! Right onto the wooden plank! He could've broken Hazard's face right there! [Reed:] Lewis is dragging Hazard towards the ring, and he throws him in! He wants to beat on him some more, and it looks like my pick to win this thing is in some serious trouble...they're back in the ring, and Lewis grabs Hazard's left leg, and sticks him in a half Boston! [Adams:] He's pulling back on the leg, and that's causing excruciating pain to the back on Hazard...the man who has had many near-death experiences in close to one now! [Reed:] Hazard is trying to roll out of this maneuver, as he rocks back and forth...but the semi-masked man keeps a steady hold on that leg...wait, Hazard rolls out of it, Lewis falls to the ground, and Hazard springs up! Yeah, go Hazard! [Adams:] Go Lewis! [Reed:] Tony?! [Adams:] There's too much excitement, maybe I wanna pick favorites too! [Reed:] Hazard is going to the top-rope! Lewis hit the mat hard, and Hazard is flying, sommersault splash!!! He nailed it!!! [Adams:] What a move!!! Hazard picking up Lewis, and he's got him in a facelock...he's running, bulldog! He hit a bulldog! Lewis is hurt! He's going to the outside...he's on the ring apron, and he jumps up onto the ropes, springboard legdrop!!! Hazard is psyched up! [Reed:] Hazard goes for the pin, but he forgot what kind of match this is, there aren't any pinfalls, submissions, DQ's, or countouts...the first person to reach shore wins the match and the World Title! Lewis is getting to his feet, and Hazard is waiting behind him...Hazard goes for a Russian sickle, but Lewis ducks, Lewis catches Hazard, BELLY TO BELLY! [Adams:] Great counter by Randall Lewis! Lewis picking up Hazard by the hair...gutwrench lock, into a gutwrench suplex! He's picking him up off of the mat with both hands, and HE'S HOLDING HIM IN THE AIR WITH BOTH HANDS! TWO-HANDED CHOKESLAM!!! [Reed:] I've NEVER seen that done in my life! NEVER! Hazard is OUT! Lewis is going outside! He's going to try and escape! He's got the match! New World Champion! [Adams:] I don't think so, Dallas...because Hazard is on his feet, and is chasing Lewis down the plank! This time he hit the Russian sickle! Hazard drops an elbow on Lewis right on that wooden plank! Hazard is dragging Lewis back in the ring, just like Lewis did to him! [Reed:] Hazard throws him into the ring...and he's going to the top-rope...it looks like he's waiting for Lewis to get up...Lewis is getting up, and Hazard is flying! HURRACANRANA! HE HIT A HURRACANRANA! [Adams:] Hazard isn't wasting any time, he's picking up Lewis, and he just flung him over the top-rope! He lands on that little five foot foundation surrounding the ring... [Reed:] Hazard is standing at the opposite side of the ring, I have _no_ idea what this maniac has planned for Lewis. Lewis is getting to his feet, he is really groggy...Hazard runs, SOMMERSAULT OVER THE TOP-ROPE AND A CORKSCREW PLANCHA!!! {{{{{{SPLASH}}}}}} BOTH MEN LAND IN THE WATER!!! A SOMMERSAULT CORKSCREW PLANCHA, UNBELIEVABLE! [Adams:] HE CHANGED MOMENTUM IN MID-AIR! Now both men are trading punches in the ocean! We've seen many guys in the ocean today, but right now we've got two of them in there beating the hell out of each other! [Reed:] Hazard just grabbed Lewis's head, and he's dunking him in the water! He just brought him back up, he dunks him again! I used to do that to my little brother! [Adams:] Me too! Unfortunately, Hazard is trying to kill Lewis, we were just playing around. [Reed:] Yeah...that's it. Playing around. Heh. [Adams:] Hazard keeps bringing up Lewis's head and dunking it again! Now what?! Hazard stops, and Lewis is...oh my God...he's floating on the water, Hazard is swimming to the ring... [Reed:] Lewis is dead! NO! Hazard is in the ring, Lewis is on his back on the water....Hazard flies over the top-rope! {{{{{{SPLASH}}}}}} [Adams:] SWAN DIVE OVER THE TOP-ROPE ONTO LEWIS IN THE OCEAN!!! Where did they go? [Reed:] They're both under the surface, and I suppose that they are fighting under the water... [Adams:] This is most unusual. I hope that they are alright... [Reed:] Damnit, somebody get an underwater camera in there, I want to see what's going on! {{{{{{SPLASH}}}}}} [Adams:] They are up at the top of the water, near the plank! They floated under water about 20 feet! Unreal! [Reed:] They're trying to choke each other out, right in the ocean! This is truly insane! Hazard has brought himself onto the plank and he's sitting on it, Lewis trying to get up, but Hazard is choking him, and he can't! [Adams:] Lewis is still in the water, Hazard has an advantage as he is on dry land choking the life out of Lewis! Hazard just pulled Lewis onto the plank by his throat! Both men are getting breathers here...what a match this has been! [Reed:] What a supercard this has been! [Adams:] Absolutely. Both men's chin-length black hair are soaked. These men have put up a helluva fight here, and now Hazard is getting up, and he's going towards Lewis...Lewis with a jab to the face...Hazard with the same. [Reed:] Hazard grabs Lewis's arm and WHIPS HIM TOWARDS THE RING!!! Lewis hits the ring, and falls in!!! Hazard is following him in! [Adams:] This is unusual, they are in the ring. [Reed:] Yup. Hazard going to work on Lewis with a quick chinlock. [Adams:] What the hell? There is a _huge_ man walking along the shore! [The camera focuses on this man, wearing black cut-off jeans, a black sleeveless shirt, and black boots. His face is bowed towards the ground, and his face is hidden by the long hair.] [Reed:] That man looks to be about 6'4" and about 250 pounds...he's a pretty big sized guy! [Adams:] He's probably just here to watch the match, there's a lot of strange people in Bermuda. [Reed:] True...but most of these people don't walk up to the plank of the main event for Terror in the Triangle!!! [Adams:] He's standing at the front of the plank, and he just swept his hair back and it's...OH MY GOD... [Crowd:] YYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH!!! [Reed:] OH MY GOD, CALEB TEMPLE! CALEB TEMPLE IS HERE! HE'S WALKING TO THE RING! [Adams:] I CAN'T BELIEVE IT! HE'S GOING TO THE RING! [Reed:] He just stopped midway on the plank, and he's watching the match! [Adams:] Unbelievable! First Alexander Creed, then the Boogieman, now Caleb Temple! All of these surprises tonight! [Reed:] Back to the action in the ring, Randall Lewis has gained the advantage, I don't know how, but he's got Hazard locked up in a bear hug... [Adams:] Hazard breaks the hold with an earringer! Hazard into the ropes, flying forearm! Down goes Lewis! Hazard gets up now...and he sees Caleb...look at the staredown. They are just eyeing each other and in the meantime, Lewis has left the ring, he's on that five foot foundation surrounding the ring! He's going under the ring now, and he's gone! [Reed:] Hazard is jawing at Temple, but Temple just looks back at him...and in the meantime, Lewis has gotten lost underneath the ring! [Adams:] Here comes Lewis, and he has a canister of...oh my God... [Reed:] GASOLINE!!! He's puring the gasoline all over the water!!! Look at this, he's drenching the water with gasoline! He just emptied the canister, and he's back in the ring, knee to the back, and down goes Hazard...Now he's stomping away at Hazard, really giving it to him. [Adams:] Lewis picks up Hazard...and he has him set for a tombstone piledriver! He hits it! Hazard is out cold! [Reed:] Caleb Temple just continues looking on...this is really freaky. [Adams:] Why did Lewis pour the gasoline all over the water is my question. [Reed:] Now Lewis sees Temple. And he's jawing with him! [Adams:] He's walking towards Temple!!! He's gonna go duke it out with him! [Reed:] Randall realizes that the match is more important and returns to the ring...Hazard on his knees...Lewis approaches Hazard, and Hazard hits him below the belt! He hit him right in Lewis's special place! [Adams:] Good way of putting that, Dallas. [Reed:] Thanks, Tony. [Adams:] He just put Lewis next to the ropes, and he pulled rope from his tights...he's tying Lewis to the ropes!!! He is tying Randall Lewis III to the ropes! [Reed:] Yeah Hazard! Good job! Make those ropes tight! Good! [Adams:] Randall is tied to the turnbuckle, and Hazard is walking, _very_ slowly, to the shore...he's tired. Lewis...has put his hand in his tights. [Reed:] What the hell is he doing, and can we show it on national television?! [Adams:] He just pulled something out of his tights...it's...a lighter. Oh damn. This isn't good. [Reed:] He is burning through the ropes...and he has got himself out. Hazard is getting out of the ring and is on the plank. Oh no. NO, NO, NO! [Adams:] OH MY GOD, DO NOT DO THAT RANDALL!!!! {{{{{{WWWHHHOOOOOOSSSHHH}}}}}} [Reed:] He just lit the water on fire!!! The water is on fire!!! [Adams:] And Hazard has nowhere to go, so he's back in the ring, attacking Lewis! It's a pier six brawl! [Reed:] Folks, Caleb Temple is watching from midway on the plank, the water is on fire, Hazard and Randall Lewis III are beating each other senseless, and the crowd loves it! [Adams:] This is simply unreal, folks. Hazard with a reverse neckbreaker, Lewis goes down! Hazard is going up top! WHAT A SIGHT! [Reed:] Fans, the water is on fire, and Hazard is on the top-rope like a phoenix among the fires...Hazard flies! LOOK AT THE HEIGHT! HEADBUTT TO THE CHEST! [Adams:] I just don't understand how these men continue! Wait...no. God no. [Reed:] Hazard is going to throw Lewis into the ocean! Lewis, who set the ocean on fire, is about to be thrown into the fire by Hazard! [Adams:] Lewis blocks it, and hits a side salto slam on Hazard! Lewis is picking up Hazard, and he puts on an armbar...into an arm twist...short closeline! Nice combination by Lewis! Now what is Lewis gonna do? [Reed:] It looks like he's picking him up and he's gonna press slam him! He's got him over his head, and he just drops him into a gut buster!!! He just snapped the intestines of one Hazard! [Adams:] Lewis picks him up into a side suplex, and he nails it! Hazard is just being manslaughtered out there. [Reed:] Look at Caleb Temple, still onlooking, and the fire continues to blaze around the water! [Adams:] Lewis is picking him up again, and this time he has him face down in a lock, and reverse DDT! Hazard is unconscious. Somebody get some help out there, because Lewis is just trying to hurt him now. Lewis hoists up Hazard again, and this time he sticks him into the corner. [Reed:] Both men are dried out from being in the heat of the fire, despite spending five minutes under water! Knife-edge chop by Lewis! Forearm uppercut! Hazard falls to the mat! [Adams:] Lewis has pretty much used up every move that I can think of...no, I guess not, because he just put Hazard in a chickenwing-crossface and is holding him up in the air... [Reed:] After a minute or so of this chickenwing, he can just throw Hazard down and walk ashore, and there's no way that Hazard would get up. [Adams:] Even if he let go now, Hazard couldn't get to his feet. [Reed:] Nevertheless, he is still applying this chickenwing-crossface, and is not about to let go! This fire could catch onto the ring, realistically. [Adams:] I sure hope that that does not happen. Lewis lets go finally, and Hazard collapses to the mat. Lewis walks towards the ropes, and he steps upon the ring apron, but on the wrong side...he's on one of the three sides facing the water, which is engulfed in flames! [Reed:] Lewis has had his brain beaten in, he's gonna jump into the fire, rather than onto the plank! [Adams:] No, NO! {{{{{{SPLASH}}}}}} [Reed:] WHY THE HELL DID HE DO THAT?!?! HE JUST JUMPED INTO THE FIRY OCEAN!!! HE'S GONNA CATCH FIRE! [Adams:] LEWIS IS SWIMMING TO THE SHORE FROM UNDER THE WATER! HE IS SWIMMING TO SHORE!!! [Reed:] UNBELIEVABLE! WAIT...OH MY GOD, SOMEHOW, SOMEWAY, HAZARD IS UP! [Adams:] He's looking everywhere for Lewis, but can't find him!!! He's rushing the shore via the plank! WAIT! HE IS EVEN WITH LEWIS! HE SEES HIM! [Reed:] HAZARD IS GOING TO JUMP INTO THE FIRE AS WELL! {{{{{{SPLASH}}}}}} [Adams:] And they're fighting in the ocean again, but this time, the ocean is on fire!!! Hazard is pulling Lewis out further from the plank...and he jumps in the air, AND HITS A FRANKENSTEINER IN THE WATER!!! [Reed:] WOW! Now Lewis is swimming towards the plank, and Hazard is swimming after him! This is unbelievable! Lewis is on the plank, and he is waiting for Hazard, Hazard is on, Lewis lifts him onto his back! He goes down with a Samoan drop!!! He hit a Samoan drop right onto the wooden plank! Lewis is bringing Hazard to his feet, Hazard kicks him in the gut! [Adams:] Lewis is doubled over, and what's Hazard doing?! It looks like he's trying to find the board that he punches through early in the match! And it looks like he has found it! He seems to be...sabotaging the whole area of the plank! Hazard is just two or three feet away from Caleb Temple, and Temple is just watching! [Reed:] Hazard has put little wholes throughout the whole area of the plank, and he goes over to get Lewis! He's going to put Lewis through the plank! [Adams:] This is unbelievable! What a match we've had! Hazard now has Lewis locked in a crotchlock, and he's I guess gathering energy...he picks him up, and Jesus Christ! {{{{{{CCCRRRAAASSSHHH}}}}}} {{{{{{SPLASH}}}}}} [Reed:] He just power bombed Randall Lewis III through the plank and into the ocean! Hazard is attempting to cross over to the other end of the plank...and he has, but Lewis reaches up and grabs his foot!!! [Adams:] Lewis pulls Hazard into the ocean! The fire is _still_ going! [Reed:] They are fighting once again in the ocean! Hazard grabs Lewis's head and bashes it into the plank! Lewis with a thumb to the eye! Hazard rams into the plank! [Adams:] Both men are on the plank, and Caleb backs away! Hazard and Lewis are in a fist duel on the plank, both men are completely wasted! Back and forth exchanging blows, really nailing each other here, and Hazard seems to have an advantage! He's punishing Lewis with right hand blows, and he spins around, spinning backfist, and he knocks Lewis into the ocean!!! [Reed:] HAZARD IS GOING TO SHORE, BUT CALEB TEMPLE IS STANDING IN HIS WAY! [Adams:] TEMPLE'S GONNA THROW HAZARD INTO THE OCEAN! [Reed:] Caleb just stepped out of the way!!! Hazard walks to shore!!! [DING, DING, DING] [Adams:] NEW WORLD CHAMPION! HAZARD HAS DONE IT!!! [Reed:] YES, HAHAHAHA!!!
