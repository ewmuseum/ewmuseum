The crowd cannot believe their ears and begin to go absolutely nuts. The curtain is swept aside, and the two figures the GFWA has been longing to see walk down the aisle, smiling. Newtown's adopted son, Alex Reaver, begins his walk back into the rings were he fought and failed once more. He is wearing blue jean shorts, a GFWA Terror in the Triangle old tee shirt, and a flannel around his waist. His red hair is swept back with a brush of his hand, and his green eyes stare out into his domain. Glory is holding his arm, squeezing tight. He walks up to almost right in front of the World champion, then pulls out a microphone from his jean pocket. He gives a deep sigh, a toss of his head, a smile, and then he speaks to Hazard.] REAVER: So, we meet once more, Hazard. [strokes chin] We were both out to make a name for ourselves. A double debut, the most compitive first round match. It was supposed to be my first step in a trail to glory...but I failed. It wasn't anyone's fault, simply fate's twist. Time to think of the pain and suffering, the lonely nights, and storied past ... to take it all in and sweep it away with a single thought. But, then, I got a videotape from a fan, a tape of Terror in the Triangle. I had missed everything, you see, as my match was second of fifteen or so. I never saw what went down ... 'til then. When I saw the legend Caleb Temple strike you down ... when I saw you arrive on shore ... when I saw you hold that title belt ... I knew one thing and one thing only. [grits teeth] I was coming home. What's your theme, "Waiting to Die"? [smirks] Hazard, you _aren't_ ready to end it all ... but on July sixth, I was. No, that isn't something to brag about ... it's something to make a point on. You think you know what I've been through, Hazard? [spits] You don't know the half of it. But guess what, _champ_. The pain, the failure ... it all ends.
