Sunday Night Slam Masters 7-20-97 Part IV

Jul 21, 1997, 12:00:00 AM

[The camera opens to the ring again.] STEVIE THOMPSON: Ladies and gentleman, Matt Hermanator! <"I Don't Believe" by Stabbing Westward starts playing as "The Greatest" Matt Hermanator walks to the ring.> (Crowd is booing) MATT HERMANATOR: (Grabs mic away) Get the hell out of here! I have brought my own interviewer with me. He is one of my friends, we wrestled together in the EWL; "The Masterpiece" Alex Mercer! ALEX MERCER: Allright! Thank you for opening up for me Stevie, I will take over from here. Ladies and gentlemen! You knew him as a three-time champion in the former GFWA. He has since won another three titles in other leagues; he is back where he started and ready to kick butt just like he did before. He is 'Truly the Greatest' Matt Hermanator! (Crowd starts booing.) MATT HERMANATOR: Thank you Alex. First I would like to say that I even though I won three titles and was clearly the greatest wrestler in the former GFWA, I still was not finished. I had yet to win the World Championship Belt in the GFWA. I was cheated out of it a few times, but never quite had the opportunity to get it. But now I have rejoined the league I created to earn that which I had not yet acquired. That being the World Title. ALEX MERCER: You dominated the league when you were with it and shortly after you quit the league, it closed down. Tell me and all these fans about it. MATT HERMANATOR: Well I noticed after I unfairly lost my third belt that the league was beginning the degrade itself; with me not being a champion and all. So I went after the World belt, but like I said, I was cheated out of it. So I said 'enough is enough' and quit. I felt if they were gunna have pieces of trash as champions and not world class athletes like myself, then I didn't need to be a part of it. And sure enough, after I quit, my good friend John Prophet, who was wrestling in the league at that time, told me the the league closed down. ALEX MERCER: What did you do after that? MATT HERMANATOR: Well, we joined other leagues and became champions there. John worked on opening his disco/casino and I enjoyed the good life. I won a lot of fame and fortune when I was in the GFWA, you know. I carried that on to other leagues and dominated those too. ALEX MERCER: You seem to dominate every league, what do you have in store for the new GFWA? MATT HERMANATOR: That's a good question. I have dominated every league I was in. The current total is six titles in my wrestling history. And as I see it, I am only beginning. To answer your other question, I would just have to say that I will be no different in the new GFWA than in the old GFWA with the exception that I am more experienced now. You have to understand the old GFWA was my first experience of wrestling professionally. I was young and inexperienced. I still won three titles. Now that I am more witty and experienced, I know the ropes more, imagine what I will do in the new GFWA! ALEX MERCER: When will we be seeing you in the ring and where are you setting your sights on after that? MATT HERMANATOR: I don't know when I will have my first match, but I hope for it to be soon. As for what I am setting my sights for, I am looking for one thing only and that is to get the one treasure I have yet to have found; the World Title. ALEX MERCER: Any closing comments? MATT HERMANATOR: Yes, Hazard or whoever may be World Champion at that time, you better keep that belt polished for me and, oh yes, I am truly the Greatest! <"I Don't Believe" by Stabbing Westward starts playing as "The Greatest" Matt Hermanator and "The Masterpiece" Alex Mercer exit the ring together.> [Reed:] Folks, Matt Hermanator is back! And it seems that he wants a piece of Hazard! [Adams:] Get in line, Mr. Hermanator! Get in line! [Reed:] Right now, it's time for the World Tag Team Title Match, Dan Kauffman and Craig Anderson are set to defend against the Long Beach Connection! [King:] This match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the GFWA World Tag Team Championships! Introducing first, ["Bow Down" by the Westside Connection plays as the crowd boos] followed to the ring by their manager Big-E, weighting in at a total combined weight of 505 pounds, THE LONG BEACH CONNECTION!! [King:] And their opponents, ["Call the Man" by Celine Dion plays as the crowd cheers] from Hagerstown, MD, weighting at a total combined weight of 481 pounds, they are THE GFWA World Tag Team Champions, DAN "FLASH" KAUFFMAN and "MR. INTENSITY" CRAIG ANDERSON!! [Adams:] Well, we have an incredible match upcoming here as the GFWA World Tag Team Champions, Dan Kauffman and Craig Anderson defend against the equally impressive tandem of Krazy-K and G-Dogg, the Long Beach Connection. Your thoughts Dallas? [Reed:] My opinion? The Long Beach Connection have been on a roll, and we will see new World Tag Team Champions tonight! [Adams:] Well, it looks like Kauffman will start out for his team, and it will be Krazy-K for the Long Beach Connection! Kauffman and Krazy-K lock up! Krazy takes the early advantage as he rakes the eyes of Dan Kauffman! [Reed:] Great technical move by Krazy-K! [Adams:] Great technical move? [Reed:] Sure! Haven't you ever heard of a Greco Roman eye gouge? [Adams:] Can't say that I have. [Reed:] Not a big wrestling fan are you? Everyone I know has heard of a Greco Roman Eye gouge! [Adams:] Can we please pay attention to the match? [Reed:] Sure why not? [Adams:] Krazy-K grabs Kauffman by the hair and whips him to the corner and Kauffman hits the buckle hard! Krazy-K follows Kauffman in and clotheslines him! Kauffman hits the mat! [Reed]: Krazy-K showing championship potential here! [Adams:] Krazy-K picks up Kauffman and whips him to the ropes, Krazy-K ducks his head and gets kicked in the teeth by Dan Kauffman! Krazy-K hits the mat, and Craig Anderson is tagged in by Kauffman! [Reed:] Heads up move by Dan Kauffman! "Mr. Intensity" is in now! Look out! [Adams:] Anderson enters the ring and goes right at Krazy-K! He whips Krazy-K to the ropes and nails him with a big powerslam! He covers Krazy! 1.... 2.... [Adams:] NO! Krazy-K kicks out! [Reed:] You'll have to do alot more than that to defeat Krazy-K! [Adams:] Well, Anderson wants G-Dogg in the ring! He throws Krazy-K to the corner, and G-Dogg tags in! [Reed:] Now that was stupid. He had Krazy-K reeling, and now he allows the fresh man to tag in. [Adams:] Well, Dallas, we all have our opinions. You voice yours more than others. [Reed:] Of course I do. I am Dallas Reed. I do whatever I want to! Who's gonna stop me Adams? [Adams:] Certainly no one in WEW! [Laughs] Anyways, G-Dogg gets in the ring and he and Anderson circle each other. Anderson and G-Dogg finally lock up. Anderson takes the advantage as he knees G-Dogg in the gut and takes him down with a forearm to the back! Anderson picks up G-Dogg and he whips him to the ropes and takes him down with a clothesline! [Reed:] That clothesline took G-Dogg's head off! [Adams:] Well, Anderson is waiting for G-Dogg to get to his feet. [Reed:] Another mistake in my opinion! He should be all over his ass! [Adams:] G-Dogg gets to his feet, and G-Dogg and Anderson lock up again. G-Dogg takes the advantage this time as he punches Anderson in the jaw, and doubles him over with a knee to the stomach. G-Dogg sets up Anderson and DDT's Him! Cover by G-Dogg! 1.... 2.... [Adams:] Anderson kicks out in two! Anderson is dazed though and G-Dogg picks up Anderson and whips him to the ropes. He takes Anderson down with a back body drop! G-Dogg drags Anderson to the corner, and he tags in Krazy-K. Krazy and G-Dogg begin punching away at Anderson! Kauffman enters the ring and gets the referee's attention, and the ref goes to put him out of the ring! [Reed:] Big mistake by Kauffman. Anderson is getting his ass kicked in the corner, and Kauffman just initated it more. [Adams:] Wait! What's happening?! [Reed:] HERE COME THE LETHAL LEADERS! Heatstroke: How the four of you doing? Big fight, huh? Isn't this for these? (grabs the belts and takes a look at them.) Nice belts. I bet the four of you each want this real bad!! Cancer: Well of course you know at the GFWA Pay per View, Summercide, we got our title shot and honestly, we don't care which one of you we face. Just as long as these belts are there and are on the line. Heatstroke: So we just came down here to see if these belts fit around our waists. (Heatstroke and Cancer put the belts around their waists and notice one is too big and the other is too small.) Heatstroke: Kauffman, Anderson, these belts are the wrong size!! Oh man, hey guys, we're going to take these belts and go re-arrange the sizes, ok? Cool!! (Heatstroke and Cancer throw the straps on their shoulders and walk down the aisle. On Cancer's way out, he flips off all four men.) [Adams:] WHAT WAS THAT? [Reed:] Well, The Lethal Leaders took the tag team titles with them, so, I guess that means they are the New Champions! [Adams:] How? They didn't beat Dan Kauffman and Craig Anderson for them. [Reed:] Okay smart guy, who has the GFWA World Tag Team Titles? [Adams:] Well, you just saw The Lethal Leaders take them back to the locker room. [Reed:] And the team with the titles are the champions right? [Adams:] Technically, yes. [Reed:] Then there. The Lethal Leaders are the new champions. [Adams:] Argh..I don't have time to deal with you Dallas. Anderson and Kauffman are still tag team champions. Krazy-K picks up Anderson and slams him down to the mat. Krazy-K bounces off the ropes and drops an elbow down on Anderson. Krazy covers Anderson. 1.... 2.... [Adams:] NO! Anderson was able to kick out at 2. [Reed:] I don't think Dan Kauffman and Craig Anderson are ready to give up the tag team titles just yet. [Adams:] Right you are Dallas. Krazy-K drags Anderson toward the corner again, and he tags in G-Dogg. Krazy-K holds Anderson's arm as G-Dogg climbs up to the top rope and drops an elbow onto the arm of Anderson! Anderson is holding his arm in pain, and G-Dogg takes Anderson over with an armdrag! [Dallas:] G-Dogg slaps on an armbar, and is really grinding on Anderson's arm! [Adams:] Well, look! Anderson is inching closer and closer to the the corner! He can almost tag in Dan Kauffman! [Dallas:] Look! G-Dogg gets up and nails Kauffman and knocks Kauffman down and Kauffman tries to get in the ring and get at G-Dogg, but the ref stops him and G-Dogg drags him further out into the middle of the ring. [Adams:] G-Dogg continues to grind down on Anderson's arm. Anderson gets to his feet as the crowd begins to get behind him! Anderson elbows G-Dogg in the stomach, Once! Twice! Three times! Kauffman drops an elbow on Krazy-K and goes for the win! 1.... 2.... [Adams:] Krazy-K kicks out again! Kauffman whips Krazy-K to the corner and follows him up with a clothesline! He grabs Krazy-K again and whips him to the opposite buckle and follows him up again with another clothesline! He climbs the turnbuckle and begins punching Krazy-K as the crowd counts! 1! 2! 3! 4! 5! 6! 7! 8! 9! [Adams:] Ten Times he punches Krazy-K!
