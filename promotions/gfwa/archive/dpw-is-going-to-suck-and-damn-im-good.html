DPW is going to suck...and DAMN I'M GOOD

Jun 15, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've read a lot of the shit that has been spreading through here, even though I hate wasting my time reading stuff from here every single day, I still do it. But when I come on here and see [name withheld], a man that I _never_ had a problem with, flaming [name withheld], [name withheld], and [name withheld] for being poor e-wrestlers, I raise my eyebrows just a bit. I'm what you'd call a newbie to the "LOOP", as I've only been in it and #e-wrestling since March. I'm not that good in it either. But the point I want to make here is...those that have the better roleplays tend to win the matches in the LOOP. Now then, occasionally, this method doesn't fall into place, as I'm sure it doesn't in the Summit and in every other league. As far as newbie's not being able to win titles...ever hear of the WEW? I just won the World Title there. That theory just got thrown out the window and pissed on. These "Gods of the LOOP" that you speak of, Dark Destroyer, Mr. Robinson, etc., they _all_ deserve there victories and losses. And, on occasion, these "Gods of the LOOP" will get a strange loss. An unexpected loss. We usually call them "jobs". For instance, Bigger Than Hollywood's loss to American Thunder...Dark Destroyer's loss to Mike Stewart...it all goes hand in hand. There is no favoritism in the LOOP, just large ego's. But, ya know what? We deserve them. Ya know why? We earned them. We are _great_ roleplayers, we have quality, not quantity, we don't have grammatical errors (see Alan's post for examples of grammatical errors), and we're just better than most other fed's. It's a sad situation, but it's true. Ameet. -- [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] )-"Athlete, Good Student, Wrestling Addict, and All-Around Good Guy." President - Grapplemaster's Fantasy Wrestling Association (GFWA - http://www.geocities.com/Area51/Corridor/8073/index.html ) Handler of - Punjab (PCW), Ego Astormin (Hammerlock), Dallas "Perfection" Reed (WEW - World Champion), and "Suicidal" Nate McManus (RWA). "We are entering a realm where angels dare not tread." My father before we entered the Bronx, 2/17/97
