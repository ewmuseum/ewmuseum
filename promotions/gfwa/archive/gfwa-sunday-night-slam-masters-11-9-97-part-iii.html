Markes November 9, 1997 Writer: [name withheld] ___________________________________________ [Adams:] We have the battle of two weirdos up next, as Dwayne Casey Malone battles J. Markes. [Reed:] Tell me, Adams, is anyone around here normal anymore? We're surrounded by loons! Wylde Dreams, these two guys, Extream Freaks... I don't know about this place anymore. [Adams:] Well, they are extremely talented individuals. You've gotta give them that, well anyway, Jack King is in the ring, let's get down to him.. [Reed:] Oh great, let's see who Malone plays dress-up as today. [Bell: DING DING DING] [King:] This next contest is scheduled for one fall... ["The Great Diamondback Sturgeon Pt. 1" by Primus starts to play over the PA.] [Reed:] DEAR GOD NO!!! [King:] Introducing first.. from Parts Unknown, Oklahoma, and weighing in at 205 lbs.. "GOLDFISH MASTER" DWAYNE MALONE!!!! [The crowd boos as Malone heads out to the ring, dressed as the Bass Master "Bubba Leon Lockhart", complete with sponsored fishing gear. His manager, Callie Gladstone walks slowly behind him, holding onto Malone's 'fishing pole'] [Just a Little Crazy starts playing over the PA.] [King:] And his opponent, hailing from Albany, New York, and weighing in tonight at 210 lbs.. here is.. J. MARKES!! [Markes rolls into the ring and Malone pounds on him as he enters.] [Adams:] Malone with a Pearl Harbor right off the bat here! [Bell: DING DING DING] [Adams:] Malone whis Markes to the ropes and takes him down with a clothesline! Malone jumps on top of Markes and repeatedly punches him in the head! Malone is biting Markes! What agression shown by the fisherman here in the early going. [Reed:] Fisherman?? Good lord.. He's only playing pretend, Adams. [Adams:] That's what he does best! Malone whips Markes into the ropes.. Markes comes off.. BIG BACK BODY DROP!! Markes right back on his feet however.. and Malone clotheslines Markes over the top! Markes right back on his feet AGAIN.. and Malone slingshots over the top and to the floor! [Reed:] Markes, being the nutcase that he is, is still fighting Malone here! [Adams:] Malone is punching Markes on the outside, but Markes is fighting too! Markes ducks and sends Malone into the ringpost! Markes quickly looks for a weapon.. and sends a chair down across Malone's back! [Reed:] Have a seat, Malone! [Adams:] Markes takes Malone by the hair and throws him back inside. Markes climbs up top.. Malone gets up.. and Markes hits a flying clothesline! Here's a cover by Markes! 1.... 2.. maybe not even two! Malone throws Markes off and simply sits up! Markes is choking away at Malone, but LOOK! Malone is choking Markes at the same time! [Reed:] At least they're not choking each others chickens.. that would be a horrible sight to see. [Adams:] And it would get us taken off the air in no time. Markes and Malone are choking each other over by the ropes.. and they fall outside! Malone landed on top of Markes, however! Malone now back up.. and has that chair.. ARABIAN FACEBUSTER BY MALONE ON THE CHAIR! [Reed:] This isn't a bad fight. [Adams:] Malone has Markes.. WHIP TO THE GUARDRAIL! Malone charges at Markes.. STINGER SPLASH.. NO!! Markes moves and Malone is sent over the guard rail into the first row! [Reed:] I wonder what that nutcase is up to now, he's got that chair. [Adams:] He's setting the chair over by the railing.. [Reed:] He can't be sitting down during a wrestling match, Adams. [Adams:] Who knows what he's doing. Malone is back up.. Markes charges.. a jump onto the chair.. SENTON.. NO!!!! Malone moved out of the way and Markes's leg hit a chair that was in the second row! Markes is hurt! [Reed:] Malone nodding at Gladstone, who gives him that fishing pole.. what's he up to? [WHACK!] [WHACK!] [WHACK!] [Adams:] Malone whips Markes with that fishing pole! Now he's got that cord on the fishing pole and is dragging him back towards the ring, he's choking him! [Reed:] Even with those crappy imitations he does.. he's not a bad wrestler. [Adams:] He never was, but he depends too much on illegal tactics! He throws Markes back into the ring, the fishing line still wrapped around Markes's neck. [Reed:] Markes doesn't know where he is right now, nothing unusual though. I kinda think Markes ENJOYS having that cord wrapped around his neck. [Adams:] His knee though is in bad shape, even more so after that kneebreaker by Malone! Markes's knee hit that chair very hard when he missed that forward flip earlier on in the match, now what? [Reed:] Spinning toe hold! [Admas:] Not just a spinning toe hold, but it's turning into... LOCKHART LUNKER LOCK! Can Markes hold on?? [BELL: DING DING DING] [Adams:] The ref calling for the bell! This one's over! [King:] The winner of the match at 6:46... "GOLDFISH MASTER" DWAYNE MALONE!!!! [Adams:] Malone whipping Markes with that plastic goldfish that was on the hook, and Gladstone finally convinces Malone to let up. Malone and Gladstone leaves as Markes rolls out of the ring. [Reed:] Malone isn't the type of guy that anyone should lose to. But.. he showed me something, he showed me that he's got what it takes to be a big star. [Adams:] If Malone can get more impressive victories like that, then he'll be a huge star here in the GFWA! [Reed:] Interesting theory... too bad he sucks. [Adams:] Folks, we're scheduled to have... {"Under The Bridge" by The Red Hot Chilli Peppers begins to play over the loudspeakers. The sound gets extremely loud and almost shattering.} [Adams:] Hey, That’s Burbank’s music, but he isn’t even scheduled to be here. [Reed:] Well, it’s too late, because here he comes. {Burbank emerges from the back and stomps to the ring as the music blares. His head is wrapped in a black bandana and he is adorned in all black clothing, including black boots and a black leather jacket. The only color apparent is is a streak of gold on the right and left shoulders of the jacket. Behind him walks "Big" Will Axelrad dressed in a white sweatshirt and blue jeans. Danny walks in to the ring as Bill stands outside guarding. Danny then calls for a microphone and stands firmly in the ring.} [Reed:] Okay, what’s the rags to riches to rags senior citizen going to blabber about now? [Adams:] Jeez... I want to hear this, so please be quiet. {Danny leans on the ropes and stares down to the announcers.} Burbank: Would you two, with all due respect, please shut up so I can start? [Reed:] Wha... !?!?! Burbank: I said ‘please’ Dallas. Don’t make me shut you up. [Reed:] Yeah, sure, whatever, talk. Burbank: THANK you. Now let me make my address, which is -long-, but let me say my piece and I’ll be out of your hair, since I of course have none on my head. {Danny walks over to the middle of the ring .} Burbank: Now you folks here in Dublin probably would love to see some fist flying action. You want to see some young men and old farts pound each others skulls in. Am I right? Huh? Am I? [Drunk Irish Fans:] BLOODY HELL!!!!!!!! Burbank: Heh, got to love the Irish. Now, your going to get your (does an Irish accent) "Bloody Hell!", but not till the fans of The GFWA, the wrestlers, the bookers, the president, etc., hears what I have to say. I want you people to learn who I am, what I’m capable of, what the future for me is. Now, if you bothered to watch my taped interview from California from about a week ago, you’ve heard this story, but if not, your in for a rare treat. I started my life in the downtrodden streets of Los Angeles, California. I pushed myself to be the best I could be in everything and anything I did. Eventually L.A. wasn’t enough for me, it made me feel weak and tired. I deserved better, so off to New York City I went to experience life. Experience life I did. Hey it was NYC after all. Then the day I met Mitch Donovan came and I gained a best friend for life. Mitch was already training to become a wrestler and he brought me in to his fold. We became a tag team and we doing pretty well, getting a nice start when I got a call from California to come home for some major work. I really liked what was going on with me and Mitch, how we we’re getting popular, but money came first. So back to Cali, this time Beverly Hills. I got the babes, the money, the life. Then I met Chris North, brilliant professor/author/martial artist who had recently escaped from the looney bin. Somehow I turned him on to wrestling and he started his career calling himself "Imperium", the god he believed himself to be which sent him off to the psycho ward in the first place. Being with Chris and getting back in to wrestling was a thrill, especially since even more girls flocked to me. Boom, things just starting happening. Our first fed closes, so we head to another one. I, of course, gave Mitch a call and said that he would be signed in a minute since the federation wanted me and Chris so much. That was when "The Big Kahunas" began. Mitch Donovan and Danny Burbank, The Strength and The Flight combined to make an awesome tag team. We wrestled all over the world and we won and we lost. Then came that fateful day. Mitch got burned by a fireball. That event allowed to us to create an illusion that Mitch had become totally burned to a crisp and had to have bionic parts. Of course, it was all a fake. It was something created, but it didn’t work. "The Big Kahunas" flourished as a tag team after that, but it wasn’t the same. Then came the cartoon deal, we we’re offered tons of money to retire and we did it. We sold out. Then for months on end we sat on our asses doing nothing. Well, Mitch did something, he got in a car accident and lost both legs, so his chances of ever wrestling again we’re squashed. I got so bored I started managing Chris in the MLWO, but it wasn’t enough. I wanted to wrestle and that’s when I got a call from [name withheld]. He was trying to get me in to GFWA and I was going "Why should I join a regional?" Ameet just told me to watch GFWA and that’s when I saw The Big Kahuna. I saw another wrestler using Mitch and my name and surfer character. Well, that dragged me in to the GFWA faster than you can say... Boom. {Danny looks around the arena and then down at the announcers and chuckles.} Burbank: So, there I was. I was in the GFWA and put in to a match at a event named "Terror in the Triangle" to meet The Big Kahuna and for weeks I planned and I ragged on the poor chump. Then the day came and I debuted in GFWA with cheers behind me. I kicked that fake all over the water, the ring and the plank and I got the win and it was a big thing. I ran him out of the GFWA because he couldn’t think of a new name. There I was, back in the sport, back on top. I’m given another match and what happens? I somehow beat one chump and lose to another. Did this set me back, though? Not at all. Hell, it made me stronger. You see, then I went on and beat others and I then beat Mysterio after being attacked like a mad dog by a ton of people. This big star who no one believed in, I took everything that was thrown at me and spit it back out. Then it was nothing, quiet, I didn’t hear from the GFWA. I attacked the men who attacked me, tried to regain some dignity. Nothing mattered, GFWA decided that I was a throw a way star they could call on when they wanted or needed. Call me they did. Cruiserweight Battle Royal happens and I get a call out of the blue to wrestle in it. I was hardly even given time to train for it. I was lucky enough to be the last man eliminated. Then I go home, sit on my ass and I get a call to wrestle Alex Reaver. The damn current World Champ, they wanted me to wrestle him on his way up the ladder. So I did, and I gave it my all and he came up on top. Hell, I had no reason to keep in shape, the GFWA was only calling me on a match by match basis. Then I got a call to wrestle Pike Rose. This was different, this guy had pissed me off, just by being himself. I got in shape, I bad mouthed him and I planned to kick him a new face. Then we wrestled and I had him on the ropes and that fat tub of lard who will never be royal or a champion again smashed me over the head. That gave Pike the win and the opportunity for a beat down. Four days later at the first GFWA Pay-Per-View I troied to get some revenge on Pike, but it didn’t matter. After I whupped The Peasant Fathead in to a pulp, Pike and his friends attacked me from behind and kicked me and smashed up and down and then proceeded to put my head and hair on fire. The fire burnt every crisp of hair off my head and each follicle died. I have no hair on my head. Absolutely none. That’s not what makes me angry though, I angry about my treatment by the GFWA. My treatment is crap! Crap! {Danny cracks a smile and motions for a camera to close up on his eyes.} Burbank: This is going to sound cliched, but... I’m pissed off and I’m not going to take it anymore. The GFWA doesn’t realize what they’re missing here. Your missing your chances, your wasting me. I’m your superstar, your master of the game, your number one bad boy. You need me. There are so many matches you can have me wrestle, so many situations I can be involved in. I’ll take on the young punks coming in and the old legends. I’ll do it and beat them. I’ll be your marquee star. I’ll bring in the fans, the money and the world. Trust me, you may think the GFWA is big, I can make it bigger. Of course, menial tasks must be done before that happens. First off, Jim Mongol, you wanted me as your partner to take out Pike Rose? You got it! But remember, I take Rose out, he’s my enemy, my kryptonite that must be crushed. A tag team match could be nice. Very nice and I’m sure Pike will hear this and take the match. Yet, that’s not enough... Oh no, sometime down the road on December 21st in Euro Disney, Nuclear Winter will take place. I propose to you "Devastation" a match which the MLWO started. I propose that we wrestle a "Euro Disney Death Match". If Chris North and Steve Spector can go the distance I don’t know why we couldn’t. Are you up for it Pike? Are you up for battling in the Parisian clone of The Greatest Place on Earth? I’m betting you are. So, if you got the guts and the GFWA has the smarts. Let’s do it Pike, me and you, The Twenty First of December, Nuclear Winter. Let’s get it on, Pike. You know I want to kick your ass, please let me... please. Please GFWA, Wake up, it’s in front of you. I’m the future!! {Danny drops the microphone gently, leaps over the top rope and heads to the back with "Big" Will.} [Adams:] Wow, that was amazing! What a challenge, what words, what a speech from Danny Burbank... just amazing. [Reed:] *snoring* [Adams:] Dallas!!! [Reed:] er..huh..? Burbank’s gone? Let's go to him. ["Everybody Hurts" by REM suddenly blasts through the arena as The Royal Champion and The Royal Lawyer come through the curtain from the locker room. The Royal Champion has on his wrestling outfit while The Royal Lawyer has on a light grey suit over a white dress shirt and a grey tie. In his right hand he has his Royal Briefcase. The crowd bursts into boos the second they see them. Both men slowly stroll to ringside as a few fans lean over the guard rail to touch them. Both men go out of their way to avoid all the little kids attempting to touch them. Once they reach the ring The Royal Lawyer climbs the ring steps ahead of The Royal Champion. The Lawyer climbs into the ring and walks over to Thompson in the middle of the ring with the mic while The Royal Champion simply steps over the top rope and walks over sneering at the crowd] Thompson-Welcome gentlemen, well Royal Champion you have a big match tonight with Zodiac and.. [The Royal Champion grabs the mic out of Thompson's hand] The Royal Champion-I didn't come out here to talk about the piece of crap that calls himself Zodiac. I am here to talk about the GFWA! GFWA, the w stands for wrestling, strange, haven't seen much wrestling around here. As a matter of fact, the last time the GFWA World Title was defended, two guys just ran around a school punching each other. I am sorry that is no wrestling. [Gets a very serious look on his face] It isn't even close. The GFWA World Title of wrestling wasn't even defended, [pauses then shouts] IN A WRESTLING RING!!! I don't know what I find more disturbing, the fact that the world title is defended with two guys fighting in a school, or the fact that you fans love it! We got guys fighting in a school, cages matches everyother week, and just tonight we got guys that will be fighting all over the damn arena [crowd cheers] ya you like that crap. Well I am getting sick and tired of it! [Pauses and runs his right hand, the hand without the mic, through his hair] The Royal Champion-When I first came to the GFWA, I held very little respect for it and all the wrestlers in it, now [pauses] I have none. How much talent does it take to fight in a school? How much talent does it takes to beat on some guy around a arena? Why in the hell does the GFWA EVEN BOTHER TO SET UP THE RING ANYMORE? I hate wrestlers that pride themselves on being able to piledrive a guy through a table, big deal. I have reached my limit about putting up with all this hardcore crap when that main event at A Nightmare in Newton took place. So, in light of that I am taking it upon myself to do something about it. [Pauses, looks to The Royal Lawyer as they both smile and then speaks in a loud, clear voice as though he was reading a announcement] Starting today, I, The Royal Champion am embarking on a ANIT-HARDCORE CAMPAIGN! [Crowd boos even though they don't know what it means] [The Royal Champion smiles as he looks around] The Royal Champion-I can see your pea brains don't seem to understand what it means. Not surprising since you all enjoy watching guys get thrown into bared wire. I am going to make it my goal to throw out everyone in the GFWA that stands behind Hardcore wrestling. Everyone from Zodiac to Shawn Adams to Alex Reaver. No one is safe. I am going to destroy everyone with wrestling, when I put everyone on the self it will be from out wrestling them, not beating them over the head with a baseball bat. It is my belief that have the men in the back [points towards the locker room] don't even know how to wrestle. I could put a side headlock on one of them and they wouldn't know what to do. To them wrestling is tables, chairs and bared wire enclosed y a steel cage, makes me sick. I am going to put an end to that crap once and for all. Everyone in the GFWA is guilty. But before I begin this campaign a few changes must be made. [Hands the mic to The Royal Lawyer] The Royal Lawyer-We are getting tired of simply going by the names of The Royal Champion and The Royal Lawyer, it was ok when we started out but now, I guess we have grown out of it and we will now be know as well as are actual names. From this day forward I will be known as "The Royal Lawyer" Carl Stevenson, and my big friend here will be known as "The Royal Champion" Johnathan Monarch. [Hands the mic back to Thompson] Thompson-You mean Carl Stevenson and Johnathan Monarch are your real names? "The Royal Champion" Johnathan Monarch-Well DUH! You didn't really think my legal name was The Royal Champion did you? [Thompson stutters a little before Johnathan grabs the mic from his again] "The Royal Champion" Johnathan Monarch-Last time I ever give you the mic, you never have anything interesting to say. But before I go [crowd cheers a little at that] SHUT UP! Anyway before I go I have one last announcement. I have warned the GFWA, and all it's wrestlers multiple times that if I didn't get a championship match I would take action. Well I have and I would like to show you all, for the first time ["The Royal Lawyer" Carl Stevenson opens his briefcase and pulls out a gold championship belt] THE ROYAL CHAMPIONSHIP BELT! I have gone out and crowned myself The Royal Champion, since I am the only man worthy of holding such a title. The GFWA doesn't want to give me a chance to win one of their crummy titles, no problem I went out and made one of my own. Zodiac, are match isn't for a few moments, you still have a chance to run out of the building, it would be the best career move you could make at this point. [Crowd boos horrendously, but Monarch and Stevenson just shrug it off.] [Reed:] DID THAT SOB JUST SAY "ANTI-HARCORE"? [Adams:] "The Royal Champion" Johnathan Monarch shocking the GFWA world by starting an Anti-Hardcore campaign. [Reed:] ANTI-HARDCORE? You just might as well "Let's kill off wrestling all together!"
