Sunday Night Slam Masters 10-12-97 Part IV

Oct 12, 1997, 12:00:00 AM

[Reed:] Let's get on with it. _____________________ __ _____ / _____/\_ _____/ \ / \/ _ \ / \ ___ | __) \ \/\/ / /_\ \ \ \_\ \| \ \ / | \ \______ /\___ / \__/\ /\____|__ / \/ \/ \/ \/ ___________________________________________ [SECOND MAIN EVENT!] Alex Reaver vs. Bishop October 12th, 1997 Writer: [name withheld] ___________________________________________ [Adams:] Dallas, this match is going to be incredible. Two of the top superstars in the GFWA right now are going to go haywire on each other. [Reed:] Tony, Reaver is a drunken junkie, and Bishop is a Satan worshipping moron. Neither of these guys is worthy of being in _my_ presence, and neither should be wrestling in a GFWA ring. [Adams:] Dallas, these are two excellent wrestlers! You have to acknowledge good wrestlers every now and then! [Reed:] Let me think that over. [Adams:] Okay, well... [Reed:] Okay, no. [Adams:] Let's go to Jack King for the ring introductions! [King:] Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is our _second_ main event on Sunday Night Slam Masters, and it is set for one fall! [PA:] "SnailShell" by They Might Be Giants. [Alex Reaver enters GFWA Arena to one of the biggest pops in GFWA history. He is garmented in navy blue boots and dark blue tights. "ALEX REAVER" is written down the left leg in neon green letters. Reaver is intense as he gets to the ring. He finally enters the ring, and raises his hands in the air, receiving the single loudest pop ever. Following close behind is the lovely Glory.] [Reed:] What? [Adams:] I didn't say anything. [Reed:] What? I can't hear a damn thing. [Adams:] Huh? [King:] Wow. Ladies and gentlemen... hailing from Newton, New Jersey! And weighing in at 255 pounds...accompanied to the ring by Glory... _AAAAAALLLLLLEEEEEEXXXXXX_ _RRRRRRREEEEEEAAAAAAVVVVVVEEEEEERRRRRR!!!!!!_ [The crowd is going insane.] [Reed:] Hello!? [Adams:] Dallas, I don't know if you can hear me, but there are 8,800 fans literally _screaming_! Each and every one of them is going nuts for the hometown hero, Alex Reaver! Unbelievable, I believe we've broken the sound barrier folks! [Reed:] Mayday mayday! I'm going down! Can you hear me Tony?! [Adams:] Dallas, I can hear you fine. Why are you screaming? [Reed:] Wow...I've lost my faculty of hearing after that. That was unreal, what is wrong with these people? You'd think they'd have the common decency to shut up. [PA:] "The Cathedral Remix". [Bishop enters GFWA Arena, wearing black boots, black pants, and a black muscle shirt with a crimson cross on it. The crowd is suddenly livened once again, with an incredible pop, the likes of which can only have been heard ten seconds ago. The lights have completely disappeared.] [Adams:] Again!!! This is unreal! [Reed:] Jesus! [King:] And his opponent! Hailing from the Cathedral of Pain in Chicago, Illinois! Weighing in at 280 pounds!!! Ladies and gentlemen, here is the Avatar of the Extreme...___BBBBBBIIIIIISSSSSSHHHHHHOOOOOOPPPPPP!!!!!!___ [Adams:] TURN ON THE LIGHTS! [Reed:] Seriously, this isn't cool. I can't even hear myself, and now I can't see myself... [Adams:] I'm in the same boat, Dallas... [The lights flicker on, and Bishop is standing in the ring with his arms spread open.] [Reed:] Wow! He's in the ring! This is crazy, Tony! Reaver and Bishop are staring at each other, and nothing more! It's really freaky, and I think I'm gonna high-tail it out of here right now... [Adams:] You're staying, Dallas, to call this match...then you can go. [Reed:] Don't tell me what to do. I'll stay if I want to. It just so happens that I need a paycheck to pay for things. So I'll stay. [Adams:] Whatever. [DING, DING, DING] [Reed:] Drop the subject and call the match crackhead. [Adams:] Okay, well, there isn't much to call, they're just watching each other not do anything. So far, both men are just trying to intimidate each other. [Reed:] They draw in with a collar and elbow tie up...Reaver with a side headlock...and a takedown. Oh boy, we've got a real thriller brewing here! [Adams:] Bishop kips his legs up and gets Reaver into a head scissors... Reaver spins out and jumps into a chinlock! [Reed:] Classic amateur wrestling...classic because it's redundant. Bishop getting up...jawbreaker! Reaver got his chin impounded into Bishop's head! Bishop gets up and he's stomping away at Reaver...kick him harder priestboy! [Adams:] Bishop picking up Reaver...and he throws him out of the ring! He's gonna take this match to the outside _early_...this is where Bishop is most dangerous. [Reed:] Bishop's face is twisted from matches that he's had in the past, and he's gonna try to scar himself some more, I suppose...he's got Reaver, and he whips him into the stairs! ({({({__BOOM__})})}) [Adams:] Oh man! Bishop sent Reaver into the metal stairs back-first! [Reed:] That's gonna leave a mark, Tony. Bishop is bringing Reaver back to his feet once again, and he's got him in a reverse side headlock...DDT on the outside! [Pop!] [Adams:] Bishop is taking control of this match early! Bishop throws Reaver back into the ring, and follows suit...Reaver is groggy, but he's getting to his feet...and Bishop is lingering near the ropes...Bishop flies out of the ropes with a lariat! Reaver's head bounced off of the canvas! [Reed:] Bishop is such a horrible wrestle, I remember kicking his ass from one end of the ring to the other in the IEWF...did I ever tell you about how I won the IEWF Wor... [Adams:] <interrupting> Bishop is climbing the ropes! Reaver is out on the canvas! Bishop could pick up a quick victory right here! [Reed:] What's this going to be? Bishop is gonna soar with the eagles... [Adams:] There he goes... [Big pop!] [Reed:] He missed the headbutt to the chest! Reaver moved out of the way, and Bishop smacked his head on the canvas! [Adams:] Both men are really spent, and we've barely started...Reaver is up on his feet, and he has Bishop up...the Irish whip...flying forearm smash! [Reed:] Man...that was a high quality Irish whip. Decent forearm, but what a whip! [Adams:] Reaver goes for the cover! 1... 2... kickout by Bishop! [Reed:] Reaver is on the offensive now...he has Bishop on his feet, and punches him in the kidney! That's a painful maneuver! Reaver into the ropes...and he passes by Bishop, off the ropes again..._flying bulldog_! [Adams:] Reaver got a lot of momentum going into that move! And now _he's_ going to the top! Reaver is quickly up there, and he jumps... [Reed:] He lands on Bishop with a guillotine legdrop! Legdrop from the top! 1... 2... Bishop kicks out! [Adams:] Bishop kicked out! Reaver is back on top of Bishop with a headlock, and he puts him into the corner...kick to the stomach...boot to the knee... and another boot to the knee. Reaver is going after Bishop's legs here... [Reed:] Reaver wraps Bishop's left leg around the second ring rope...and kicks it again! The ref is counting, but Reaver just avoids it. Reaver whips Bishop into the opposite corner, Bishop reverses it, Reaver hits the corner! [Adams:] Bishop charges in with a flying headbutt! Both men fly to the outside! They're both unconscious on the concrete! What a move! [Reed:] Since neither man appears to be getting up, allow me to take this opportunity to announce that I am the EWA North American Champion, the LWC Silver City Champion, and an undefeated SOB. [Adams:] Look, Reaver's up! [Reed:] Damn. [Adams:] Reaver is going to bring Bishop to his feet...Bishop poked him in the eye! [Pop!] [Reed:] When Bishop goes for a poke to the eye, you know that the angels have fallen. Bishop with a punch to the gut...and he has him in a crotchlock... [Adams:] Where is he going to take Reaver from here? [Reed:] Bishop picks him up... [Adams:] ... [Reed:] ... [_BIG_ pop!] [Adams:] POWER BOMB ONTO THE BACKFIRST ONTO THE GUARDRAIL! What a move! Reaver could be paralyzed from that move! Reaver fell into the stands, and Bishop is going into the peanut gallery after him! The fans have scattered, and these two crowd favorites are fighting in the stands! [Reed:] Bishop brings Reaver up...both men are standing _on_ the chairs! They are literally _walking_ on them! Bishop with a fist to the face... Reaver with a rebuttal punch...and a double axehandle... [Adams:] _DROPKICK_! _DROPKICK_! _DROPKICK_! [Reed:] Both men fly back to the ringside area! Reaver _nailed_ Bishop!!! [Adams:] Reaver is crawling into the ring! Bishop is crawling around on the outside of the ring...Reaver is in! Bishop is on hands and knees on the outside...and he has a chair! Both men are up simultaneously! [Reed:] Reaver is going to the top-rope! He just sailed towards Bishop!!! ({({({__CRASH__})})}) [Adams:] OH MY GOD! DOWNWARD SPIRAL HEADFIRST INTO THE CHAIR! BUT THE CHAIR HIT BISHOP! BOTH MEN HIT THAT ... GOOD GOD! [Reed:] That was a very crazy maneuver by Reaver!!! He corkscrewed straight into the chair, but the chair hit Bishop too! Jesus Christ, the GFWA has turned into a War Zone! [Adams:] What can happen!? It's gonna be a double countout! Unless... [Reed:] Unbelievable. [Adams:] Bishop is..._up_!? How in the world...folks, this is why Bishop is a GFWA competitor. Bishop throws Reaver back into the ring, and Bishop follows him in... [Reed:] Bishop grabs Reaver and brings him to his feet... [Adams:] He whips him into the ropes...Bishop ducks down...Reaver catches him! He locks the arms into the double-chicken wing! He sweeps out Bishop's legs, into the Grinder!!!!!! [_Massive_ pop!] [Reed:] The Grinder is applied! Bishop won't give up! He's...tapping out with his foot! [Adams:] This is a first! He's tapping out with his _foot_! [DING, DING, DING] [_Huge_ crowd pop!] [King:] Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner... _AAAAAALLLLLLEEEEEEXXXXXX_ _RRRRRREEEEEEAAAAAAVVVVVVEEEEEERRRRRR!!!!!!_ [Reed:] Reaver wins it! Bishop just tapped out with his foot! The Grinder gets another victory, and Reaver moves on to the World Title Match in the Schoolhouse Brawl next week! [Suddenly, the lights go out...the crowd shreiks.] [Adams:] What the? [The video wall crackles to life, and a familiar face shows on the board. Alex Reaver merely smiles and waves.] SHAWN ADAMS - Hi honey, how's work? [Reaver frowns, and remains stolid. Adams laughs, as the camera moves backwards. The shot shows that Adams is seated in a wooden school desk, sitting in front of the bell that will determine a victor at the PPV next Sunday. Adams is wearing his wrestling attire. His black jacket has "Superstar" written on the back in white, with the same colored buttons on the front. He also wears black wrestling tights with one single white stripe, and black boots. He has the World Title in his hand.] SHAWN ADAMS - Lookie what I found, Alex. [Adams tosses the World title belt down the bell tower, landing where his first interview of the week occurred. He smiles] SHAWN ADAMS - When I win I'll throw you down there to pick that up for me. [smirk] [Shawn looks around, for no apparent reason] SHAWN ADAMS - As for anything you may have said, or may end up saying about my alliance with Mark Cutter, I suppose I shall need to address a non-numerical time. A time called the PAST. A time when someone else wrestled. You want an explanation as to who that man was? You want to know who he was? Well, he was the man that won the Northeastern Title from Big Ern. He was the man Mark Cutter brought into the federation. The man you face is the one who just happened to be there when the fatal feud continued. [Shawn smiles] SHAWN ADAMS - Who am I? What am I? Does it matter? When the time comes to face off, we live in the PRESENT. We live in the here and now. We live in America's current state. The state of now. We will talk of the future, but when it comes it is the present. Fights only occur now. Pain builds up, but the pain of the past is not the pain of the present, it is a summation. [Shawn smirks] SHAWN ADAMS - Y'follow? Or are ya stoned? [Alex just laughs] SHAWN ADAMS - Come the PPV, we'll be in the most built-up match the GFWA has ever had. And we'll make it all worth it. [Shawn puts his balled up fist under his chin.] SHAWN ADAMS - Just think Alex, in one week we will be wrestling. That's the only time I care about from here on out. [Adams rings the bell] SHAWN ADAMS - One week will have passed when you hear that again. One week will have passed when neither of us are able to stand on our feet as soon as the relieving tones of the bell ring out through the air. I care not about minutes, hours, or seconds...I just anticipate my ringing of the bell for the next time. Believe me, it will come before you are able to ring it in our match. [Shawn smiles, as the arena is again in black. His voice booms out over the arena.] SHAWN ADAMS - Come over to my house and play, Alex. We gotta go to school in a week, and you may not like the teacher...because this teacher is me, and I teach the subject of pain. [A faux lightning bolt cackles in the arena.] SHAWN ADAMS - You WILL fail, just like your suicide was a failure, and your life is still attempting to improve from failure. Don't take me as the last step, it just won't work. [Shawn's laugh is heard.] SHAWN ADAMS - Victim.
