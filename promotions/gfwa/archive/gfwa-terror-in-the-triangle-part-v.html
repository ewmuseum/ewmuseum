S.O.S. MATCH! GIB vs. STEVE PHOENIX [Adams:] All right, next up we have our S.O.S. Match. The ropes are being moved by the GFWA Movers, and that water took to be cold, Dallas. [Reed:] I would agree. In order for a winner to be declared, someone has to be completely immersed in the ocean water! [Adams:] Immersed? Dallas, you probably don’t even know what that means! [Reed:] Shut up, Tony. [King:] Ladies and Gentlemen, this contest is our special S.O.S. Match-up. In order to be the winner, you must throw your opponent into the water completely! And now, the contenders. [“Rock and Roll All Nite” by KISS plays as the crowd splits up. Someone in tights with the name GIB written on the back walks the plank. He has medium length hair, long sideburns, and a mustache that goes into a goatee.] [King:] Introducing first. From New York. Weighing in at 245 pounds. Here is GIIIIIIIIIIIIIIB! [Crowd splits again as “Roller Coaster” by The Red Hot Chili Peppers plays. This man wears black leather jacket that has the image of a fiery red Phoenix on the back and sunglasses. Under it, he wears a Metallica Concert Tour T-Shirt, Tommy Hilfiger Jeans and Black Reeboks. He is followed by a delightly lady, who removes the glasses and jacket, and by The New Dipstick Generation.] [King:] And his opponent. Led to the ring by Snow and The New Dipstick Generation. From New York. Weighing 315 pounds. Here is.....STEEEEEEVVVVEEEEEE PHOEEEEENNNNIIIIIX! [Dwayne Malone walks up to Gib, wearing what looks like a Gib T-Shirt.] [“Ordinary Average Moron” Dwayne Malone:] Hey, Gib! I hear you wanted one of these! Well, if you weren't so dumb, you'd know they sell them at the merchandise stand. Here, take this one. [Malone throws Gib a t-shirt, who promptly puts it on. Phoenix, Lockhart, and Malone begin to break out in hysterical laughter. Gib doesn’t know that the back reads “Hi, I’m a Moron! Kick my Ass, please!” Lockhart takes the mic.] [Lockhart:] Hey, Gibberino, another lesson for you. Always read the back of a shirt before you put it on. [Gib grabs the shirt and tries to pull it off, leaving him open for a dropkick by Steve Phoenix.] [Reed:] OH MY GOD! HA HA! That was perfect! [Adams:] Sick and Twisted is more like it! [Lockhart and Malone still have the mic.] [Malone:] You think Stevie’s trouble pounding this bum? [Lockhart:] Nah, the moron got lucky the first time. Let’s go get some grub! [Reed:] The New Dipstick Generation begins to make their exit and the plank has been retracted. [Adams:] Back in the ring. Phoenix has been pounding it into Gib after that Dipstickian Dropkick. [Reed:] Hey, you don’t knock it! If an opening is there, go for it! [Adams:] Anyway, Phoenix looking around him, and seeing that he has no ropes to bounce off of! [Reed:] Duh, which is why he’s picking up Phoenix and walking towards the edge! [Adams:] Not so fast! Gib with a thumb to the eye of Phoenix and he drops him! Gib goes to the turnbuckles, which are still there, up to the top and......CLOTHESLINE FROM THE TOP ROPE! [Reed:] He grabs Phoenix and throws him towards the water...IRISH WHIP! [Adams:] Gib trying to stop himself! He slips...HE FALLS IN! [Reed:] No, wait! He’s only waist deep. That doesn’t count as a victory! [Adams:] But Phoenix thinks he won! He’s celebrating already! Gib is up! FROM BEHIND! [Reed:] TURN AROUND, STEVE! [Adams:] A CLOTHESLINE THAT NEARLY TOOK PHOENIX’S HEAD OFF! [Suddenly, a shot goes off and a rope is shot across the ring. Someone starts to tightrope walk across the ring. It’s Aurora!] [Adams:] AURORA! WALKING DOWN A TIGHTROPE! WHAT IS HE DOING HERE? [Reed:] Look at this! He’s got the attention of BOTH men! HE MOONSAULTS PHOENIX FROM OUT OF NOWHERE! [Adams:] He rushes Gib! HE’S ATTACKING GIB WITH A FLURRY OF PUNCHES! [Reed:] Here comes the ref to stop Aurora! There are no DQs in this match. [Adams:] Aurora is curtseying for the public and now he’s go..... [***BANG***] [Reed:] WHAT THE HELL? [Adams:] A smoke bomb just went off! Aurora’s gone, and so is the tightrope. [Reed:] This match seems to be a popular one for interferance! [***SPLAT***] [***SPLAT***] [***SPLAT***] [***SPLAT***] [***SPLAT***] [Adams:] DISGUSTING! THE NEW DIPSTICK GENERATION IS IN THE STANDS WITH WHAT LOOKS LIKE THE ENTIRE ROW FULL OF ITEMS FROM THE CONSESSION STANDS! [Reed:] <laughing> They’re throwing it onto Gib! Messy eaters, eh? [Adams:] Shut up! It’s all over the ring! Greasy fries, Special Sauce, Cheese Dip, and bottles of Number 40 Malt Liquor! [Reed:] Hmmm. <in a memo pad> Special sauce. I got to remember that. [Adams:] You are truly sick, Dallas! [Reed:] Still, both men are exchanging blows. Phoenix has Gib in a sidehead lock. A spin around.....BACKSPIN DDT! [Adams:] LOOK AT THIS! PHOENIX IS POURING THE MALT LIQUOR DOWN THE FACE OF GIB! [Reed:] HEY! SAVE ME SOME! [Adams:] DALLAS! [Reed:] Well, I’m thirsty! [***CRASH***] [Adams:] OH NO! PHOENIX HAS JUST BUSTED THE BOTTLE OVER GIB’S HEAD! [Reed:] Wait...what’s in Gib’s hand? [***CRASH***] [Adams:] GIB HAS JUST RETURNED THE FAVOR! [Reed:] OH JESUS CHR.... [Adams:] FOLKS! GIB HAS JUST TAKEN THE BROKEN BOTTLE, SHOVED IT INTO PHOENIX’S CHEST, AND __TWISTED__ THE BOTTLE! [Reed:] OH GOD! PHOENIX HAS DONE THE SAME! BOTH MEN ARE SERIOUSLY BUSTED! [Adams:] And what’s worse! That’s SALT WATER! That is gonna hurt like hell when one of the men hit the water! [Reed:] Well, both men are exchaning blows again! Gib is stunned near the egde of the ring! [Adams:] HERE COMES PHOENIX! [Reed:] PHOENIX JUST SLIPPED ON A MIXTURE OF THAT SPECIAL SAUCE AND CHEESE DIP! [***SPLASH SPLASH***] [Adams:] BOTH MEN HAVE FELL IN THE WATER! THAT HAS GOT TO SMART! [DING DING DING] [Reed:] The officials are in a huddle. They want an instant replay! [Adams:] Let’s go to the tape! [The tape shows both men, bleeding from the chest, in the ring. Gib is stunned and just standing near the edge of the ring. Phoenix starts running towards Gib and slips on a puddle of Special Sauce and Cheese Dip. He goes flying into Gib and both men fall head first and become covered with water.....AT THE SAME TIME!!!!] [King:] Ladies and Gentlemen, the official have decided that BOTH MEN have fell in the water at the SAME TIME! This contest is a draw! [Adams:] This match has been ruled a draw. And both men....wait....NEITHER MAN HAS SURFACED YET! [Reed:] WHAT? [Adams:] The pain from the chest wounds must have been so painful it caused both men to lose consciousness. THEY’RE SINKING! [Reed:] Lifeguards have hit the scene and are diving in. [Several minutes of anxiousness pass as the Lifeguards come out with both men over their shoulders.] [Adams:] Thank god! They are now covering the wounds of Gib and Steve Phoenix and applying CPR. Snow has insisted that she do it for Steve Phoenix. [After a few moments, Gib is revived. A few moments later, Phoenix is too.] [Adams:] I tell you, that was one of the strangest matches EVER! [Reed:] Yeah. I wonder if the NDG still has some of that Special Sauce. [Adams:] Dallas, you sick little....... [Reed:] Just shut up before you say something stupid, Tony!
