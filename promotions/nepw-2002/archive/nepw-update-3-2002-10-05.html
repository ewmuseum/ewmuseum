Welcome One more card in the record books. As I mentioned before, I tried the 5 match card thing, but that didn't work out as well as I thought it would. Sorry about the lateness, but when I find out one of the matches wasn't started days after I send out assignments, it's hard to find an emergency writer, so I had to take upon another match. If the matches I wrote came out shitty, I apologize. Last night I had to finish writing 2 matches before I even started writing the table's match. As far as RPing and on-card participation goes, keep up the good work. I couldn't be prouder of the participation I'm getting from you guys. Also, tomorrow I'm moving back to Queens, NYC, where I was raised. I don't know how often I'll be able to sign online to check on everything since I start work on Monday. I'll be gone mostly for the afternoon time. I'm pushing the PPV date back to November and I'm giving the next RAGE another 2 weeks before stuff needs to be in. That way, I hope that in 2 weeks I have things settled down. With me moving, I will more than likely need 3 match writers for RAGE, so let me know now guy. Who can I count on as far as RAGE going out on time goes. =================================================== 2. News and Rumors - Commissioner Cline has brought in a legend in "Five Stars" Eric Cooper and a man that seems to be a little too weird for anyone to take seriously in Greg Gumbel Mask. Cline says he'll be getting this place in shape soon and they'll be the two guys that will be helping him in doing so. Eric Cooper himself says that he has big plans for NEPW. He's eyeballing the NEPW Heavyweight Title, along with many other superstars. - Speaking of which, the NEPW Heavyweight Tournament will start on the next RAGE. Two first round matches this RAGE, and two first round matches the RAGE after that. Semi-finals and the Final will be held at the PPV. At least, that's what Commissioner Cline has said to make official. - The first NEPW Title holder was crowned this past RAGE, sorta. The Pyromaniac beat Mad Dog Maddox to win the NEPW Hardcore Title, but Robert Cline didn't have the belt to hand to him there. Instead, he's promissed him a ceremony on the next RAGE, where he will be handed the belt when he is feeling better physically so he could enjoy the belt more. Cline going out of his way to make a ceremony for him? Who knows, maybe there is some good in that evil, coldhearted man, way deep inside. - Howie Hardcore suffered a mild concussion, but won't miss anytime. His opponent from the first round of the hardcore tournament can't say the same. A 20 foot drop from the scaffold, being caught by surprised, with only tables to break his fall, literally. He was sent to the hospital to take some X-Rays. We don't know how severe his injury is, but just to be safe and to avoid any type of lawsuit, he won't be wrestling on the next show. - "Five Stars" Eric Cooper made his debut last week with an open contract to anyone on the roster that has the balls to take on him. The man who signed it; Pimpin' Pornstar. He was signed from PAW just a few weeks ago and is already going after a legend. Is the Fiver Star superstar rusty? We'll be finding out soon enough. Upcoming Cards Live on Sunday, October 20 in the First Union Center in Philadelphia, PA. The NEPW Heavyweight Tournament starts this RAGE. Two matches this show and two on the next. The first match in the tournament will have "PLAYBOY" AIDAN MADISON who has just picked up his first win in the NEPW taking on someone who lost his first match due to outside interference, "ANGEL OF VIOLENCE" GREG ALLOCCA! In the second match of the tournament, GREG GUMBEL MASK will be taking on "ACES" BRAD MASTERS. GGM got involved in a match that he had nothing to do with. He cost Greg Allocca the victory, giving it to Durant with a chair shot to the stomach. He attacked Brad's tag team partner and perhaps this week, Brad Masters will get revenge for his tag team partner. "FIVE STARS" ERIC COOPER debuted on RAGE with an open contract to anyone who has the balls to take him on. The man who signed it was recently picked up from the PAW roster. PIMPIN' PORNSTAR said that he's ready to show the world that he's more than just "sex under the spotlight" if you know what he means. He wants to be the one to crash Cooper's NEPW stay party. In the Main Event for the evening, we will have a battle royal to determine the man that will get a shot at the NEPW Hardcore Title at the first ever NEPW PPV in November. Anyone from the roster is welcome to join this Battle Royal. So far, signed for this Battle Royal are FLAME, HABIB NARZAHL, OUTLAW, HOWIE HARDCORE, and MIKE MANSON. Anyone else who wishes to compete can sign up. Just so everyone knows, there will be no ropes. The ropes will be replaced by barbwire and you may bring whatever weapon you choose. RPs are due by Wednesday, October 16 at Midnight Eastern! Strats are due by Tuesday, October 15 at 10PM Eastern! Send all emails to [detail withheld] and [detail withheld] . Don't forget to put [NEPW] in the subject, so I don't miss anything. =================================================== 7.
