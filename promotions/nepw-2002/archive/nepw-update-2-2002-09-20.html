Good-bye =================================================== 1.Welcome Cards out, and hopefully most of you have read it already and didn't just wait for the update to come out so you could see who won. Participation as far as flashing and strating goes was amazing. I will need at least 2 more match writers, so that way I don't use the same writers every week and cause them to burn out. Thank you to Jesse, Blottie, and Jimmy for helping me out this week. I hope the activity keeps up. As long as that happens, this fed will be fun for all and we'll be around for a while. I also need people to do on-cards. I don't need all of you to do them. Since we'll have 4 or 5 matches every show, maybe I could get one or two or three on-cards to put on the show. It makes the card better and doesn't just leave it as match after match after match. People get to read something else in-between. I did two of them this week. The one at the beginning of the show and one after the first match. They're not hard to do. Express your character's feelings towards the match that will take place in just minutes. I got 8 people on the mailing list at yahoogroups. Register people. This will be the last email I send without yahoogroups from now on. Send a blank e-mail to [detail withheld] and subscribe to the group already, you lazy bastards. :-P The last thing I need to let you all know is that I need for angles to come about. If you have no ideas, you can contact me or Jimmy T. Our AIM names and IRC names are listed on the contact lists. You guys could use that list to contact other handlers as well and work something between yourselves. I don't want to hold you guys here any longer. Read on. =================================================== 2. News and Rumors - Robert Cline fired the NEPW head trainer Scorpian on RAGE, saying that he embarrassed the company by losing to a newbie. They signed over from PAW the Pimpin' Pornstar and Flame. Flame was named the new NEPW head trainer. He asked that Scorpian would be rehired because he could use him as a good punching bag in "class." - President [name withheld] says that he has a surprise for everyone. His only concern is when to announce his surprise. What could it be? What could this mean for NEPW? Until the President himself decides to say what it is, we can only guess. - The NEPW Website is on the works. It's hosted by EFU and the URL for it is http://www.efeduniverse.com/nepw . It's still under construction, but it should be completely done by the end of the week. - I need news and/or rumors from you guys. You can send it anytime of the week so I could put it on the update. Upcoming Cards After a first RAGE in Bridgeport, CT that had fans wanting more, NEPW goes to the Providence Civic Center in Providence, RI. It will take place on September 29! This show promises to be twice as good as the inaugural RAGE! In a double debut match, we have "ANGEL OF VIOLENCE" GREG ALLOCA taking on one half of the Aces and Eights. He'll be taking on ELIAS "EIGHTS" DURANT. Greg Alloca looks like a promising young star. Elias Durant looks to mean business. We'll see what these two have to offer. BILLY JOE LANKINS had no chance against a monster as big as Mike Manson. This week his chances are slightly better, as he takes on someone else who faced a huge mismatch in his first match. "PLAYBOY" AIDAN MADISON will try to bounce back from that loss to The Pyromaniac. We'll see which one of these two can pick up their first win this week. OUTLAW took the ex-NEPW head trainer to school last week, winning his match. HOWIE HARDCORE was eliminated from the hardcore tournament, but he feels that he can do better than last week. This match will be a good ol' fashioned tables match. First one to get driven through a table by his opponent loses. The other half of Aces and Eights will take on one half of The Outpatients. MIKE MANSON still has nobody that can match up to his size in this place. He'll take on another smaller opponent, that being "ACES" BRAD MASTERS. Manson humiliated Billy Joe Lankins in his debut match. Brad Masters is much bigger than Lankins was, but not near as big as the 7' 326 pound Manson. We'll see what happens there. In the finals of the NEPW Hardcore Title Tournament, we will see the first ever NEPW title holder get crowned. THE PYROMANIAC might not have the brains, but he's got what it takes to survive in this division, easily taking out "Playboy" Aidan Madison. In what seemed to be the best match last week and the perfect choice for a Main Event, MAD DOG MADDOX. They'll be taking on each other in a scaffold match. Down below will be some tables to break the fall. This scaffold will also have some ropes of sort. Going around the scaffold will be barbwire. This should be another 5 star match if Mad Dog performs like he did in the first RAGE. STRATS are due by Wednesday, September 25 by 10PM Eastern! RPs are due by Wednesday, September 25 by 1AM Eastern! Send all emails to [detail withheld] . Don't forget to put [NEPW] in the subject. I almost deleted an email last week, because that wasn't on the subject. =================================================== 7.
