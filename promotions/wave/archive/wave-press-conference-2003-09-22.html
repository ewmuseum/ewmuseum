[WAVE] Press Conference

Sep 22, 2003, 12:00:00 AM

WAVE Press Conference- September 22nd, 2003 -Osamu Ishimura held a press conference in the Korakuen Hall in the early morning, releasing information about the upcoming tour among other things to the wrestling media. Ishimura-san started off by talking about Junior Crowning, calling it a success and that all the competitors in the tournament worked hard. He named TOORIMA as the junior ace of the company, and called his title tournament victory over Tasogare No Prince amazing. When asked about Raidon Kentaro's involvement in the street fight, Ishimura-san responded by challenging
Kentaro to show up at the Osaka IMP Hall for BATTLE REVOLUTION 2003.
He named Kentaro's actions at Junior Crowning as acts of pure cowardice, and that he would be taught a lesson if he showed up again.
The base roster for BATTLE REVOLUTION 2003 was announced, the names mentioned were TOORIMA, Tasogare No Prince, GLORY Satsujin, Takeo
Tazaki, The Spectre, Kenta Onishi and RAVE Yamadera. Ishimura-san then made an open challenge of sorts, saying, "I would like to use this moment to invite fighters from all over Japan to try their luck in WAVE." (Yeah, send me APP's... biatches!)
Finishing the press conference off, Ishimura-san released information on the next tour, BATTLE REVOLUTION 2003, and the big stand alone show at the 4,000 seat Niigata City Gymnasium.
