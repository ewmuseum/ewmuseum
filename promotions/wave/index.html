A Japanese-style puroresu promotion announcing a five-city autumn tour and an open roster call, framed throughout as a fictional promotion in the Japanese wrestling scene. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WAVE is known through two 2003 posts announcing tour dates for its Japanese-style promotion. The first, a press conference held at Korakuen Hall on 22 September and given by the promotion's own promoter, Osamu Ishimura, recapped the Junior Crowning tournament — won by TOORIMA, named the promotion's own “junior ace,” over Tasogare no Prince — and challenged a wrestler called Raidon Kentaro, accused of “acts of pure cowardice” over a street fight at that same tournament, to show up at the promotion's next tour.

The same press conference named the base roster for that tour, Battle Revolution 2003 — TOORIMA, Tasogare no Prince, GLORY Satsujin, Takeo Tazaki, The Spectre, Kenta Onishi and RAVE Yamadera — and set out five dates across Japan: Osaka's IMP Hall, a Sendai tennis club, Tokyo's Zepp and Korakuen Hall, and a stand-alone show at Niigata City Gymnasium, alongside an open call for outside talent.

A second post, a month later, announced open roster spots in the heavyweight and junior heavyweight divisions and pointed to the tour's latest results, posted externally. No further document survives; whether the promotion filled those spots, or ran past October 2003, is not recorded.
