Eddie (wrestler profile)

Eddie Hall of Famer Titles held:3 time ESW World champion, ESW Tag Team champion. Awards: 2 Time ESW MVP Main Events: 36 (7 PPV) ESW Career: The Man, The myth, the one and only Eddie. ESW/NYCW's first ever World champion, First ever ESW Tag Team champion with Kid Krazy, and still to this day ESW's first ever mega superstar. Eddie was the first guy Steve Sinclair signed to a contract with out ever seeing him in the ring. Steve's good buddy mad Cyril trained Eddie and passed word to Steve and Steve signed him and that was the start of a bright future. The ladies wanted to be with him and the guys wanted to be him. A true winner, driven to be the best and even stood up to Steve when Steve grew jealous of his popularity. If that wasn't enough Eddie also dated Rayne, who everyone stands in her shadow. Without Eddie who knows if the ESW would have grown up to be what it is today. Before Steve paid Eddie off to leave the ESW to kick a ball around for a living, Eddie racked up 29 victories, 3 World title runs and a Tag team title and 2 time voted MVP of the ESW. While Eddie might be "retired" he does pop in now and then to stir things up.

Height: 6'1" Weight: 219 Hometown: Manchester England Theme Music: Run Riot by Def Leppard Alignment: Heel Years wrestling: 2 years Wrestling Style: Mat tech/High Flyer/Martical Artist Wrestling Style: Entertainer, modest technical style with tendancies to be a high flyer, but slightly green and prone to rookie errors.
Age: 20 Nationality: English Finisher: Slash N' Burn Top rope leg drop with a running bulldog as a set up. Secondary Finisher: Supersonic A Superkick The Bio: Eddie was born in October 1982 in Manchester, England. He grew up in Trafford Park living with his mother and sister until the age of 15. His father had left home long before Eddie could remember, ths hadn't really bothered him, what he did have have he didn't know.
His mother was a good woman and worked hard to keep the family home. Eddie didn't excel academically but at 15 yrs of age he was signed as a schoolboy with Oldham Athletic FC. He'd been for trials with all the clubs in the Manchester area, Bury,
Stockport County, Altrincham, and City and United. But it was Oldham who had offered him the best deal in regards to his family, they even found work for his sister who was finishing her exams and was looking to secure so financial security in times ahead. Persuaded by this Eddie signed.
It had been no secret that he was hot property in the local footballing community and rated as the best striker in the region, other northern teams such as Blackburn and Sheffield United had monitered his progress with a watchful eye.
And London outfit West Ham had been very interested. Deep down he wanted to play for Manchester United, but the terms they offered lacked that of Oldhams. And he knew in 2 years at 17 he could sign pro and if he was good enough and United were still interested then
Oldham had a chance to make money on him, so it was only a matter of time. Things went well initially, he settled in scoring goals a plenty for the youths and had a couple of appearances for the reserves from the subs bench. He then earned schoolboy honours for England scoring on debut against Norway at Pride Park, Derby.
After a youth cup-tie away to Chesterfield in which Eddie had scored the wiiner from the penalty spot, the teams bus broke down on the motorway. As they waited for the tow service to arrive a few members of the team decided to use the time to get off the bus and find somewhere to relieve themselves.
The match had been an evening fixture so it was dark and the bus was parked over on the hard shoulder of the motorway. As the boys jumped off the bus, Eddie lost his footing and stumbled onto the road, he was struck by an on coming vehicle and injured quite badly.
Initially the reports were worse than seemed, but when he was taken to hospital and treated he was the victim of a broken wrist, three fractured ribs, a severe concusion and some minor cuts and bruises.
There was also a problem with his ankle, it had been twisted in his initial fall from the bus causing his stumble and it had caused some tendon and ligament damage, he needed some surgery to recover.
The prognosis was a few months on the sidelines. At first Eddie was devasted, but after words with his manager and coach it was thought he would be back in time for the last weeks of the season and the cup final should the team progress, this gave him something to work for and his rehabilitation went well.
He was back before he knew it sitting on the bench for a match against Stockport in the league. the youth team had done so well in the league competition, but had made the cup final and Eddie had 2 weeks to get himself into the reckoning, he knew that if he was fit he'd be in the side, at least on the bench, which was good enough for him, for now.
With fifteen minutes to play and the score 3-1 to Oldham, Eddie was brought on and given instructions to have a light run and not over do it. A few minutes later Stockport pulled a goal back after some sloppy defending, from the restart the ball was punted forward and Eddie gave chase, he felt a twinge in his ankel but chose to ignore it. Moments later he found himself through on goal chasing a ball that was just out of his reach, then he felt his ankle give way and an naseaus feeling take over his body.
It took him 18 months to recover from the ankle injury, and in that time Oldham had found themselves a new golden boy who had been tearing up the school yards of Greater Manchester. Eddie knew of hime, he was a year or so younger and they'd played against each other in school matches. Now he was at Oldham. Meanwhile, Eddies
Sister had found work in Spain working for a travel agency. Oldham was no longer a dream but a nightmare, they had been good to him but were no longer being patient, he had lost pace, gained weight and confidence both infront of goal and going for the ball was waning.
They decided to cut there losses and release him on a free transer, he was then offered a trial with Droylesdon FC , a non league team in the Manchester Counties league, but these were hard men who were part time footballers, he didn't need the shit kicking out of him every week.
Eddie signed on the dole and took to hanging out with his mates, he played for their local side Stretford Victoria, but when word got round who he was and where he'd been, tackles started flying in a lot harder. So he gave football up, aged 17, the year he was meant to turn professional, and sign for Manchester United.
By this time he had become just another out of work lad, liked a beer, liked a bit of blow and enjoyed a good womans company. He started playing bass guitar and with a couple of mates got a band together calling themselves "Too Late." They got a small reputation and played tiny gigs which took away a lot of the pain the day to day boredom brought with it, but it was nothing more than a subsitute.
Eddie soon began to grow fed up of the arguments in the so called band, truth was they were a second rate lot and he knew it. He was now approaching his 19th birthday and decided he needed to get his act together. Firstly get a job of somekind. Then get fit and do something with his life again to pull him out of this myre.
His initial plans were to got to evening classes and do a trade course that might help land him a job, he'd heard of lads in the dressing rooms talk about this, just in case they'd never made it at football. So he did, and signed up for some basic motor mechanics course he had absolutely no interest in, but it maight help with work.
On the way back through town he noticed a small crowd gathereing round this building that used to be a storage centre for the railways.
Eddie thinking something odd was going on and maybe it was the news cameras jumped off the bus and went to see what the fuss was about.
His confusion grew when he noticed the crowd, of various ages and no shortage of good looking women were clutching at pens and slips of paper, some autograph books and shouting at someone. Eddie sussed it, some poor United player had been caught out in town and had been mobbed.
But as he got closer he noticed it wasn't a footballer, rather someone completely different, it was a man, youngish looking, probably late 20's to early 30's, a crop of bleach blonde hair and a smile on him as if to say look at me I'm a megastar.
But he was signing all these autographs and posing for pictures and people loved him, they were calling out to him, calling him
"C." Eddie thought he recognised him a a musician, then there was a Sign on the wall which read "Mad C.'s Gym and Fitness" underneath a banner read Open TODAY!! Eddie continued to watch. He caught a much better glimpse of this person and he tried to figure out where he'd seen him before.
Obviously he was this Mad C. on the building, but who was Mad C. and why was he so popular, especially with the women, the Mad C. looked over at Eddie and gave him a wink. Eddie was incomfortable, what is this guy a queer?
Two girls then approched Mad C. they were blushing, Eddie guessed they were about his age, 18, no older than 20. They were having their photo taken with him and all over the guy like a rash, kissing him and feeling his muscles, Eddie noticed the shirts the girls were wearing, he'd seen them before. His sister Emily had one. Then it clicked, this was that bloke she used to love so much, always talk about, the Wrestler, from Manchester who used to be on telly, the loud mouth.
Eddie walked over and stood infront of him, he explained he had nothing for him to sign but how his sister was ahuge fan, the Mad
C guy disppeared and came back with a signed t-shirt and photo and a few other bits and pieces. He asked where she was today, and Eddie told how she went to work in Spain. He handed the goods and said make sure you send them on, giving him a tenner for postage.
As he walked away, the Mad C guy was going on about turning it up and liking that, people were cheering. Eddie walked back up the road to the post office mailing the mouthy bastards stuff to Emily.
On the way back the crowd had dispersed and so had Mad C. Eddie was looking at the gym, he always enjoyed gym work and this was the only one he now knew of round here. He stood outside peeking in, nobody seemed to be in there, but it looked immanculate. He knocked and walked in, the place was huge inside, it looked like a palace, with boxing rings and work out areas and gym equipment, everything was brand new.
A young woman came over and introduced herself as Heidi, she was dressed in aerobics gear and gave Eddie some leaflets and prices of joining, it was a bit expensive and he was guessing on his dole cheque he'd not be coming here much.
Then that Mad C guy wandered over, he was dressed sharp as f*ck, armani everything, class act this bloke. He was impressed to see
Eddie back, they got talking and before he knew it Eddie had been there three hours. He had initially thought of C. as a bit of a prick, but they had talked a lot and although they had grown up differently, C. had been a raging alcoholic, drug taking football hoolie with a passion for having it large. Infact he was like nobdoy Eddie knew, but he felt they had a lot in common and he began to grow to like this guy not long earlier he thought was a gob shite. Common ground was all it took, just something they shared.
They both reached points where they needed to grow up move on, Eddie was at this point now, he was feeling low and needed some motivation something to help with the day to day grind of life.
C had suffered badly from Manic Depression and gone to a wrestling school somewhere up north, the only one around at that time. Now he wanted to kick one off here, to give something back to the kids who didn't have a chance, who weren't particularly brilliant at school, or who just like fighting.
Eddie never really watched wrestling, he wasn't really interested, he wanted to get fit, but C persisted, he saw something in Eddie that had been in himself a long time ago. It took a few weeks, but eventually he buckled and C. took Eddie under his wing.
Eddie enjoyed it too, there were other wrestlers that came in and helped out with the school too and the popularity of the place grew.
C. flew wrestlers in from Japan, Australia, Mexico and the USA to instruct the kids of Manchester and those who trvelled from the other regions of the country to be there.
One guy who was extremely popular and would pop in every so often was this absolute character whose moniker was Caligula. He was a good friend of C's and would often end a workout session by telling tales of his adventures that some of the most bizarre yet true stories to be told or by booking a couple of strippers and having them put on shows.
By this time Eddie was becoming the star pupil, and though it wasn't going to his head it was felt it was soon going to be time for him to move on to greener pastures.
Mad C. who now lived in Mustique, was working on his soon to be released biography and eventually took weekends only. He saw Eddie was developing fast into a fine wrestler, although he needed more time to grow, after speaking with various tutors it was agreed Eddie would join Mad C. in Mustique to finish his training.
Eddie learned a lot from C. and was ever grateful, the two remain firm friends, on leaving Eddie tried to persuade C. into going to the USA with him to look for work, maybe form a tag team. Mad C.
explained that he must do it alone, Eddie tried hard to convince him to come but it fell on deaf ears,
I'm well and truly retired from the wrestling buisness, it's up to guys like you to keep it alive. These were Mad C's words to Eddie as he boarded the plane, he handed him a list with some names.
"Look for these people, you find them, you'll find work and tell em C. sent yer!"
