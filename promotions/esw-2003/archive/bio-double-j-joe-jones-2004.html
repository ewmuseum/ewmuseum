Double J Joe Jones (wrestler profile, 2005 edition)

4 May 2005

Double J Joe Jones Wrestling Style:Old School, Submission Status:4th year Age: 23 (born 3/14/1980) Nationality:American Finisher:Figure Four Leg Lock Figure Four Leg Lock Secondary Finisher:Hemmorrhoid Joe plants his knee to the most inner part of the inner legs, sometimes hitting his spot a bit too low. He does to stretch the hammies out for the leg lock.
Bio / Background: Who is Joe Jones you ask? Why he is a former BLPW World Heavyweight Champion. He's the only son of XWA legend Green Man. A man who wrestled for over 20 plus years and finally retired when the XWA folded and the BLPW sprang up in it's ashes. Before he was known as "Double J" Joe Jones got his feet wet in the XWA managing his old man. But that's not where his story begins
Joe grew up in Berlin Germany on the wrong side of the wall. Life in the communist block was rough for a young kid who rarely saw his father. Not only was his life strictly governor he was also hiding a secret, a secret that would have him killed if it was leaked. He spent his nights in bed dreaming of the day where he could be free. Well someone must have been listening to him because in his teens he was a free man with the Berlin Wall coming down. Joe was one of the first to cross the wall and leave Berlin and never return home. Life was great a free gay man who could express himself. Yearning to be with his father, a man he saw maybe once a year, Joe worked odd jobs as he made his way from Germany to France.
Once in France Joe need money to get to the states where his father Ted, aka Green Man lived. Joe knew what his father did for a living, traveling the world wrestling here and there, hence is how Joe came to be. A one night stand when Ted was in town. For Ted it was a mistake but one he learned to love. Joe also had the privilege to see his father wrestle a few times in his prime. Joe had a knack at picking up the moves his father used and with Ted being a ambassador of the "old School movement" late in his career when the scene around him went to the hardcore/extreme route, Joe was able to learn the ground roots of wrestling. A solid fountain to which to build on. In France Joe seek worked for a small time promotion.
Many years before Green Man worked for the very same promotion, which in itself is nothing of a surprised. There isn't many places where Green Man hasn't worked. Joe was hired as a member of the ring crew and before and after shows Joe would work on his ring skills. Soon Joe was asked to wrestle which he jumped at. He wasn't great and was really green, no pun intended. Joe pressed on not really winning much in France.
Soon the time would come for Joe to hop on a plane and go home where he surprised his father who was now working for the XWA. The reunion was rocky at first due to Joe's father not believing that his only son was gay. After some time, a bridge was built and the waters were calm once again for Joe in his home life. His professional life on the other hand was in a mess.
Joe who was openly gay wasn't greeted with open arms anywhere's in the United States. If he was to get a fair shake in the exciting world of professional wrestling, Joe would have to work on his skills. This also meant that he left his father who Joe was his manager for. It was hard to leave his father, a man he looked up at his hero and idol but it was something that he must do. Joe climbed back on a plane and headed east, to Japan.
Like everyone else in the wrestling world today, anyone who is anything cut their teeth in Japan and Joe is no exception. With a all ready rock solid back ground behind him which is more than what some have when they leave Japan for the fame in the states, Joe quickly learned the ins and outs of taking risks in the air. He loved his time in Japan and became some what of a local hero there. The Great Western Warrior he was dubbed in Japan. Joe showed no fear in or out of the ring. He wasn't cocky or a bully, he was just confident. Something that isn't learned or practiced.
The XWA closed and the BLPW was up and running. One night Joe got a call for a try out from Gary Barber who heard of Joe Jones and his accomplishments in Japan. It was a long ride home and in August of 2002 "Double J" Joe Jones made his BLPW debut and was good enough to be signed full time. At the time of this printing his BLPW win lose record is being researched but "Double J" Joe Jones hit pay dirt when he won the BLPW World Heavyweight title at Dead on Arrival 11/21/02 and held onto the title till 1/30/03 when he lost the title to Shawn Sanders. A span of 69 days! Joe's favorite number. During his run as the World champion Joe went 6 - 4 with two of those victories coming in tag matches. He won every time he defended his title except for the match against Sanders.
After losing the World title Joe tried in vein to get it back but it wasn't meant to be. Joe went into a slump and while he won some and lost most, Joe tried to keep a bright outlook. That bright out look came in the form of Heath Newton who Joe quickly bonded with. They were on the same wave length and had much fun together, from Got Milk Ads in SI, to the ever so popular Sippy Cock cup to the six foot tall giant penis he used to beat people with, but Joe grew tired of not getting any respect. He changed his name to "Triple J" Juicy Joe Jones but not even that could help matters. In May of 2003 Joe said good bye to the BLPW.
From there Joe's life is a blur at best. He returned to Japan only to strike out there. Bummed he returned to the states and picked up what was left of his other career, Modeling. For the next nine months Joe worked runways and any gig he could get for magazines and such something he did very good at. But Modeling didn't feed that inner desire that Joe had, a desire to get back into the ring and be the best. Joe socked away every penny he could and worked day and night till he had a nice bank roll to fall back on incase his second attempt at wrestling failed and in February of 2004 Joe made his return to the BLPW and beat a unbeaten Sniper to start the second chapter of his career as "Double J" Jesus Jones.
