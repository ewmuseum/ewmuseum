Ritilin Titles held: ESW Television champion. Awards Won : Febuary '07 WOTM Main Events: 9 (1 PPV) Height: 5'10" Weight: 215 Hometown: : Peekskill, NY Theme Music: Devil is a Loser by Lordi Alignment: Tweener Years wrestling: Rookie Wrestling Style:Mat tech Martial Artist Highflyer Age: 20 Nationality:American Finisher:Overdose backflip off the top rope into a legdrop Bio / Background: Born in Peekskill, NY. His mom and dad split up. His dad moved to Miami, FL and Bert went with his mom before he was even a year old to Cornwall, NY. Spending only a few months at a time with his dad Bert grew up in the small town with a friend he met since before he can remember. The friend who is known as Drake in the wrestling world. Bert and Drake grew up always talking about there dream to become wrestlers and making there names known worldwide. Bert and Drake would always wrestle outside when they got the chance. They also spent there time in school and playing video games. Bert was thrown out of five different schools do to his anger and authority problem but this would give him more chance to study and train in all his favorite types of wrestling. He was very fond of high flyers and daredevils so he would always train his mind to not be afraid to do things that could hurt him more then the other person. When they got to the age that they thought they were ready to take the next step to become wrestlers they joined a wrestling school together and since the day they joined they were always thought to be in the top five of the class. They trained there for a couple of years before they thought they were ready. Drake joined a fed soon after training while Bert took a couple of extra weeks to go visit his dad before joining a fed.
