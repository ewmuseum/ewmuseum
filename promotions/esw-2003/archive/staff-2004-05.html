ESW (Empire State, 2003) — Staff

Staff, 2004 to 2005

4 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Staff! And they are: THE PREZ Steve Sinclair (emails to [detail withheld]) Yes were not going to have a "prez" or a "owner" or "Co Owner". Non of the crap here. No here we'll have a Promoter who just happens to own the joint as well. And without his Promoter's license there is no show. So whatever you do, don't do something that could risk everyone's meal ticket in the Promoter's license!
CO GM Raven Sinclair (emails to [detail withheld]) Steve's little sister. Oh did I mention that Steve is a seven foot four inch monster? And Raven is Six foot four herself. She has a mean streak and for the most part gets her way, not that we will see her in the ring or anything.
CO GM Spence Spence used to run the UAWF and now helps in the ESW/UAWF. Web site guy Wilburman aka Will Justice (emails to [detail withheld]) Yes we were able to get the icon, Wilburman. Along with being our faithful commissioner, Wilburman helps out on the web page, banners, T shirt shop and anything else we can think of.
THE VOTERS! Will aka Wilburman Long time efedder and friend. He's help run many feds before the NYCW and continues to help run others like the BLPW and the Triple Crown interfed if you want to call it that by doing top notch web work. Chrissie Not only is she the co owner but she's a voter too. Bethany Friend of ours who we roped into this cruel game of efedding. THE RING ANNOUNCER Marco Polo A retired wrestler turned loser Ring Announcer hoping his star doesn't fade away THE MATCH ANNOUNCERS Howdy Long time friend of the family. He used to be Steve and Raven‘s manager back in the day. Nanook Former IWA commissioner and wrestler, Nanook was picked up on a temporary basis and has stuck around to this day.
Hot Bod Todd Hot Bod was one of those lousy internet reporters who covered the NYCW. And one day, we were short handed and now he’s one of us when we need someone calls out sick
Baba Ganouche This guy is Nanook’s replacement. From Japan he brings old school flair while giving the censors something to do with his sometimes over the top descriptions.
THE INTERVIEWERS Lee Cyril Lee is a veteran roaring interviewer who used to have a cursing problem but has gotten it under control as of late.
Barry Burton The jobless freelance reporter from the BLPW has found a home in the NYCW Nanook He’s bound to keep a job in the company some how In house jobbers/scab hands In other words, these people joined the ESW at some point in time and they either, never showed rp’ed or quit and never told me, therefore we get to use them anyway we want. Feel Free to use them in your roleplays if you so desire.
Pure Playa The Freestyling gang banger. Killer Mike Not very bright, loves to say BRILLIANT as often as he can Hatred He just hates everyone and likes to shoot his mouth off about it. Z Man The Z Man....yeah I can‘t explain it. Pyre I tend to think of a hobbit for some reason. THE REFEREES Rick Evens He makes bad calls, all the time but works for free. And no one likes him but he has a stellar wife let me tell you
Jusin Our head referee and a outstanding Ref. How we can afford him is another story. He catches everything. Nothing gets past this guy and even the fans love him. He works every main event BTW
Scott Herbert He is a bum. Point blank he will let everything slide, steel chairs, outside help, whips chains and dogs. All he cares about is his paycheck, or the lack there of I should say.
JJ Grant JJ is a old timer who still loves to referee. He‘s a little slow these days and some things get by him but for the most part he is dead on
Dani Q He used to be on the ring crew but has worked himself up to be one of our more durable refs
TIME KEEPER Patty Liu THE RING CREW Tory "You pick that up" Fishburne Todd "Big Nose" Carrington Dani "I said no damn it" Q
