Larcen Night.....I don't think one could sum up what Larcen Night means to the ESW. he started out as Blackout's project that went wrong, went out on his own, became the second ever Triple Crown Winner and ran with Zander Thrash in the Happy Happy Fun Team and in the end, came back to Blackout to continue his training. He's been funny, misunderstood and dead serious during his time here with the ESW. He is truly one of the faces that comes to mind when you think about the ESW and hopefully he will be here for a long time to come.
