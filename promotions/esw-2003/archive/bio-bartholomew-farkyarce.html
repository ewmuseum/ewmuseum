Bartholomew Farkyarce Titles held: ESW Survivor Championship. Height: 6'2" Weight: 226 Hometown: Prague, Czech Republic Theme Music: 'Where's Your Head At' by Basement Jaxx Alignment: Heel Years wrestling: 1st Wrestling Style:Powerhouse, heel, shooter Age: 24 Years old. 14th June 1980 Nationality:Czech Finisher:The Bart Fark Pump handle slam, followed by elbow to the throat and pin. Bio / Background: Bartholomew Farkyarce comes to the ESW from Prague and is a distant relative of Zeb E Dee. On a business trip to Europe he was discovered by Zeb fighting peasants at a travelling roadshow. Although not a completely competent athlete and still with very much to learn, Zeb decided to take a chance and immediately contacted New York.
The reaction from the ESW was mixed, but it was agreed that with some hard work Bartholomew could possibly have somethign special. So he was sent to the Nevada Pro Wrestling Alliance on deal that would see him return to the ESW when the time was right.
Working with ESW stars such as Zeb E Dee, Johnny Big Time and Doc Holliday he was soon on his way to a first title. The coveted and legendary NPWA Hardcore belt that both Zeb and JBT have worn. In a night never to be forgotten, Bartholomew was able to nail his opponents penis to the ring bell, whilst being strapped to the ring by cable ties that were firmily gripped around his own scrotum.
It was a stunning upset and many tears were spilled. The time was now right for Bartholomew to travel to New York. At this time Bartholomew is still learning to speak English and although he does have a grasp on the language, his manager Zeb E Dee will be looking after his affairs.
