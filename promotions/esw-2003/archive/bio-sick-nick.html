Sick Nick Now pretty much retired from full time duty, Raven asked Sick Nick to come on as ESW Active Commissioner. Nick jumped at the opportunity to be back in the ESW. Sick Nick has a long and rich history with the ESW in and out of the ring. In the ring he held every title the ESW has to crown himself a Triple Crown winner. Outside of the ring, he tried twice to be Steve’s lackey to run raven from the ESW and to help Steve force the sale of the ESW to the WWE, failing each time. Nick grew tired of Steve’s mind games turned to Raven to dethrone Steve from power. Nick now wants to make amends with the fans of the ESW as he tries to be a “Fair and Honest Commissioner.”
Titles held: ESW World championship 2 X ESW TV championship ESW Survivor Championship Awards: Wrestler of the Month for September 05 Main Events: 14 (2 PPV) Height: 6'1" Weight: 270 Hometown: Kentucky Theme Music: Rough and Ready by Trace Adkins Alignment: Heel/ Tweener Years wrestling: 4th Wrestling Style:Old School, Brawler Age: 26 Nationality:American Finisher:Moon Shine sit down splash, running splash into turnbuckles. Secondary Finisher:6 pack blow Bionic Elbow Bio / Background: Sick Nick the Hick grew up in the woods of Kentucky watching the old NWA on the TV. When he wasn't making moon shine for his daddy, even thou you can buy alcohol these days, his dad didn't want the art of making shine die with him. Proper education wasn't important in the Graves house hold when he was growing up and as a result Sick Nick is slow in some areas. Like most house holds growing up in the south, wrastlin was a stable of the TV and Nick always dreamed of wrastling for a living. Nick lived at home for most of his live, up till 2 years ago when his daddy died and his momma urged Nick to leave home and chase his dreams. Unlike every other Tom, Dick, and Harry out there, Nick didn't go to Japan to get skilled in the fine arts of wrestling. Instead he honed his skills in bar fights, back alley brawls, and as a bouncer at a local music rock club. Nick did find his way to NY in his travels and there he meet up with a Double J Joe Jones, son of wrestling legend Green Man and Joe took Nick under his wing for some time and tried to teach him the basics. The result was Nick was a little rough around the edges but at the ripe old age of 23 Nick was out there looking for work. He got work with a small traveling federation that worked mainly state fairs and stuff like that. Nick got a little better. He also picked up the nickname Sick Nick and Nick the Hick due to his backward ways and odd sense of humor when bodily functions were funny. It wasn't till a year latter that Sick Nick the Hick became his ring name, which was way better than Warrior Nick. Two years latter, Nick is looking for bigger stages and brighter lights to shoot for. He's not the smartest, quickest, or technically sound but in a drinking contest, he's the best.
