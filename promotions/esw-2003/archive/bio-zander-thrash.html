Zander Thrash (wrestler profile)

Zander Thrash Titles held: ESW World Championship.2004 ESW Tag Team Championship Awards: Wrestler of the Month for June 04. Main Events: 7 (2 PPV) Zander Thrash spent over a year with the ESW before he came into his own. He was always popular with the fans but that didn't really land him on the winning side of his matches. He lost more than he won, but it was one victory that changed his career in the ESW forever.
The night be beat Exodus for the ESW World title marked a new era in the ESW. Zander Thrash went from bottom of the pile to pulling off the unthinkable. While he enjoyed his time at the top, and enjoyed some time off, it hasn't stopped him from wanting to be better. He needs to win one title to enter the "Triple Crown Family", Something he wants very badly.
Height: 6'0" Weight: 300 Hometown: Happyville USA Theme Music: I want Candy Alignment: Heel Years wrestling: 2nd Wrestling Style: Martial Arts/Submission Ages: 30...mentally 6 and a half Nationality: Happy Finisher: Mr. Snuggles Favorite Move - Giant Bear Hug Secondary finisher: HA HA Kick to the groin followed by insaine laughter because he kicked ya in your ho ha. Bio Gaint man who thinks he's a 6 year old. His manager/trainer is a teddy bear named Mr. Snuggles who talks to him and gives him instructions. The teddy bear wears high class tuxedos and likes women. Broke into wrestling because the bear told him to.
