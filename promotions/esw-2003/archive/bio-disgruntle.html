Disgruntle (wrestler profile)

"Disgruntle" Mad Matt Madigan Titles held: 2 time ESW Television champion Awards : None Main Events: 11 (1 PPV) Height: 6'2" Weight: 210 Hometown: : East Berlin Theme Music: Slipknot's Surfacing Alignment: heel Years wrestling: rookie Wrestling Style:hardcore Shooter Age: 26/p> Nationality:Anti ESW Finisher:Disgruntled Behavior Rick Rude like neck breaker Bio / Background: "Disgruntle" or otherwise known as Matt Madigan is the son of Ted, better known as Green Man who is the uncle to Steve and Raven Sinclair. Matt born and raised with his brother "Double J" Joe Jones in East Berlin is the black sheep of the family. Unlike his brother is who is liked by the family, Matt was shunned, not talked about and is the family secret till now. Growing up Matt gotten in all kinds of trouble at school, so much that he was banished from school in the 8th grade and just never enrolled in another. From there Matt did time in juvenile jail for theft, muggings and fights. A drunk by the time he was 15 Matt then stepped up his reckless behavior by stealing cars and breaking into homes only to go to the next step of Armed Robbery and kidnapping. He served 5 years to hard labor in jail where he joined a gang. In East Berlin there weren't many gangs and the few that were around were Neo Nazi gangs, and Matt found himself deep in one.
While his father Ted tried to get his son to see light and correct his ways Matt turned a deaf ear to him. When the wall came down Matt found some new freedoms in his life. Unable to leave Berlin before due to his record, Matt was able to go to the states where he found that his brother Double J was making it in the wrestling industry. Matt trying to clean up his act now at the age of 21 tried to talk to his brother to get him a job in the wrestling business, and much like the rest of the family, Joe turned his back on his own brother.
Feeling betrayed Matt started on a downward spiral again. The drinking and the drugs picked up (He sobered up while in jail) and Matt was in and out of trouble with the law in the states. Facing deportation someone finally lent a hand to Matt.
Steve Sinclair, co~owner of the ESW gave Matt a job and for the past year and half Matt has been working in the ESW gym as part of the building maintenance and on his off time Matt trained with Steve's brother KC in the ring learning the trade that has made everyone else in the family a star in the Wrestling world.
After Steve's death and or disappearance in early 06 Matt was let go by the ESW, cut backs in the gym. Matt started to tumble back out of control, angered by what his cousin raven did to him and jaded by the fact that his own father and brother have ignored him to this day. But Matt's luck was about to turn around. Injured and unable to wrestle and disgusted by how Raven has ran the ESW since her brother Steve was "lost" in the fire, Sick Nick hooked up Matt, trained him and dusted off Double J's Disgruntle gimmick he used to break into the BLPW and unleashed Matt on the ESW vowing to run Raven out of the ESW and restore respect to Steve Sinclair's good name.
