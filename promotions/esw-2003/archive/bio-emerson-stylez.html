Emerson Stylez Titles held:none Height: 5'10" Weight: 197 Hometown: Molokai, Hawaii Theme Music: 'Sixth Sense' by Common Alignment: face Years wrestling: rookie Wrestling Style:Hardcore Brawler Highflyer/Luchadore Shooter Age: 22 (1/1/83) Nationality:American Finisher:Crash and Burn Billy Kidman's Shooting Star Press into a Leg Drop. Bio / Background: Daniel Emerson Stylez, born on the first day of 1983. Born and raised in Molokai, Hawaii, Emerson (E. S. to his friends) grew up surfing, and skateboarding. His first across the Pacific was for a family reunion in Philadelphia Pennsylvania is where he met his cousin Phillip Dimani, who was affectionally called Exodus in the ESW. Though they were only children then, they formed a bond stronger than most long time friends. Emerson and Phillip stayed in touch for a long time, until Phillip went into professional wrestling. Emerson chose to train for the Olympic gymnast team, and was on the verge of competing in the Olympics until a confrontation prevented his participation. Before the Olympics began, Emerson and a team mate got into a heated debate, and wound up fighting. Emerson was kicked off the team, and his former team mate couldn't participate in the games because of a broken jaw and four broken ribs.
Emerson wasn't surprised at his ejection from the games, and instead of training for the next games, Emerson decided to take his newfound talent to where it would be appreciated, and began searching for any wrestling federation that would take him. After having no luck in Hawaii, on his 21'st birthday, Emerson left the islands, and came to The Big Apple in search for his cousin, who was rumored to be in with a striving federation called Empire State Wrestling.
