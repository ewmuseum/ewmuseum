Wrestling Technique : Speedy Weapon of Choice : Wooden Paddle Finisher : Dropping the Bomb Sunset flip power bomb Bio The more beautiful you are ... the more enemies you have. Just ask the voluptuous Yvonne Peterson-Darkholme, the woman appropriately known as "Bombshell". Starting off as the inexperienced valet of Tommy Purr, Bombshell quickly grew into her own as a wrestler and entertainer; ditching the ringside gowns for wrestling trunks and knee pads. As vicious as she is beautiful, Bombshell has a reputation for being popular in the men's locker room. During her brief marriage to old high school friend Gerard Angelo, Gerry - a ma with two wandering eyes - grew suspicious of his wife's loyalty ... until she finally snapped. If you're doing the time, may as well do the crime ... and boy, did Bombshell do the crime. Three-fold. In order to get even with her egotistical and misogynistic husband, Bombshell slept with Jack Spade ... Hoshi Yakamoto ... and 6Pac Dudley; effectively ending her marriage to Gerry and settling down with 6Pac. Heartbreak followed months later when 6Pac disappeared and came back, resenting Bombshell for moving on with her life and creating her own success. The provocative and alluring Bombshell fell hard for Spade and even took part in an open relationship with Hoshi ... increasing her popularity with the fans, but not so much with fellow Glamour Girls and Exhibitionists who were jealous of all she had been blessed with.
When Spade became obsessed with Shannon Skanes' then-wife Myst, Bombshell set her sights on Shannon himself, growing so enamored that she even began conversing with a Shannon cut-out. When Bombshell and Spade returned to normalcy, Spade became World Champion of the CWA for a second time. Elated for her boyfriend, Bombshell stood by him as his supportive partner ... only for him to berate her and accuse her of trying to steal his spotlight. Bombshell couldn't understand where the animosity was coming from, until it had been revealed that Jack Spade - like 6Pac before him - had also grown resentful of his lover's soaring career. While Spade had been away on a sabbatical, Bombshell had broken into the Main Event scene, proving she could hold her own against the best in the company and earning the respect of such men as Sal Clemenzo, The Moose, and even Gary Barber.
When Noah Skanes stepped into the picture to come to Bombshell's rescue, that spelled the end for Spade and Bombshell. Now, dating the CWA Owner's only son ... and being the mother of the children to the ESW Owner ... there is no limit to what Bombshell can and will achieve. Right now, Bombshell's sights are clearly set on proving that she can be the World Title contender many have believed that she can be. And if there's one thing history has taught us about Bombshell, it's that …
... Whatever Bombshell wants. Bombshell gets.
