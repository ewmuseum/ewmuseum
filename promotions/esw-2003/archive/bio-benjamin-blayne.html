Benjamin Blayne (wrestler profile)

9 February 2008

Benjamin Blayne Titles held:none Height: 6'6" Weight: 289 Hometown: : Charlotte NC Theme Music: Rammstein Feuer Frei Alignment: Tweener Years wrestling: 6th Wrestling Style:Powerhouse Mat Technician Shooter Age: 22 (May 14th 1984) Nationality:American Finisher:Just Blayne Gorry Special Bomb Bio / Background: Benjamin Blayne, a six year professional wrestler originally out of a small town named Easley in the state of South Carolina began his career when he was only sixteen. Born May 14th, 1984, Casey Alderman was always a fan of wrestling. He had grown up watching it due to his father's love of the art. After his father's death when he was only six he continued to watch it every week, as Casey grew he noticed he enjoyed the spotlight and he jumped in one every chance he got. Casey was an attention hog and loved to make people notice him no matter what. As he grew older his mind clicked to other things like girls, money, cars, fame, but always in the back of his mind he knew he wanted to be a professional wrestler. Taping every show he could he would watch it over and over again, he soon realized the secrets of the trade and started understanding the sport from a 'marks' point of view. He was soon off to high school and he knew what he wanted to do, instead of joining the football team as he did in middle school, he decided that nine years of football had burned him out so...he joined the high school wrestling team. He built his skills and endured hard training, sprints that took you to the verge of puking and and other training that required awesome cardio. He continued as an average wrestler but he knew he could be better so he decided to take some moves from the professional sport and inquire it into his own style of wrestling. He trained this new style for about two months and tried it one night at a match in TL Hannah. A neighboring high school that was known for their awesome ability to win at anything thwy did in the world of sports. With pure luck on his side Casey one his first match as a High school wrestler and from there on out created an almost god-like confidence in his mind that he could beat anyone.
Later that year overhearing the gossip at his local high school from his fellow high school wrestling buddies, he investigated the matter futher to find a small building on the outskirts of town that had been putting on professional and amatuer wrestling shows for years every Friday, Saturday and Tuesday nights. Upon watching the show he talked to the man in charge of the Friday night amatuer show and was soon on his way to training in the backyard of the promoters house out of Cowpens, SC. After a week of basic training he was sent into the ring the following Friday. What was to begin was an era under the name of Genesis, from there he climbed high up the ladder of CWF and became their champion after a matter of months with of course other accomplishments under his belt like: Continental Champion, Tag Team Champions, and Hardcore Champion, thus leading to Grand Slam Champion(Holding all the belts in the promotion) After a two year stay in CWF he headed towards bigger things within, promoting himself to the professional show on Saturday and Tuesday nights which was the EWC. This seemed promising but was taking him nowhere as he only held the Tag Team Titles with a dead end partner, so on after that they lost the Tag Titles and he soon faded as an icon among the fans. For the following months Genesis week in and week out faded into a jobber until one night he got his shot back into the main event. A four way elimination match for the EWC World Heavyweight Championship. The match was brutal, even so that all hell had been broken lose throughout the building and the fans were on their feet for the majority of the match. Genesis had been chosen to go over for this one night of glory. He was to be made champion and he was going to pin the wrestling god of them all and EWC Champion at the time, Wreckage. Just as the finishing spot was going down Genesis had hit the ropes from an irish whip and bounced back looking to duck Wreckage's hellful clothesline but something popped in the knee of Genesis and he went stumbling right into the bicep of Wreckage tearing the muscle and tendons throughout his neck and shoulder. Genesis fell the rest of the way to the mat holding his neck in pain as he realized his night to 'go over' was relished by a torn hamstring along with his other injuries to his neck and shoulder. Wreckage had no other way out so he pinned Genesis fairly with a clothesline. The crowd grew silent as Wreckage motioned for the locker room to clear. Paramedics arrived on the scene and escorted Genesis out on a gurney and neck brace to top it off.
Arriving at the hospital Genesis was wheeled in on a wheelchair and sat in the waiting room and he realized his torn muscles were the least of the staffs worries. What seemed like forever in the waiting room, he met a boy named Phillip Damani. The two became quick friends, Phillip was a basketball player for the school also waiting for medical assistance for a broken foot, and Casey shared his sense of humor. Two months later, Phillip was all healed up, and Casey had talked him into trying out for the wrestling team. Since Casey had noticed Phillip's natural athletic talent, Casey hinted to the coach that he could be the ticket to the State Finals, soon enough Phillip made the team with no problem. Within the following months Casey trained day in and day out after practice with Phillip to show him the art of Greco-Roman Wrestling and some simple holds that Phillip could build on to create his own style just as Casey did. Phillip caught on quick and was soon on the same level as Casey. The next two years, Casey and Phillip reigned supreme in the world of high school wrestling. Neither one had lost a single match for two years. After graduating, Casey had developed a love for the art of wrestling which he had always had, and looked to pass on his dream to Phillip. Casey was the one that told Phillip about a small federation nearby called the EWC, Phillip had no clue that Casey was actually wrestling. For the years they had known each other Casey had kept it a secret due to his jealousy that Phillip would 'steal his thunder' within the promotion. Phillip and Casey quickly became "Genesis, and Exodus"; the hottest tag team in EWC history. After winning there first belts together, the Tag Team Championship, they got bored of tagging with each other, and split up with a mutual aggreement to go single. Both Exodus and Genesis became great single talents, rising up quickly in the ranks of the EWC. Phillip quickly became a fan favorite, using his microphone skills to entice his fans. Casey soon became a phenomenal wrestler to watch, tearing away at his opponents with his powerhouse style and ripping at every limb on their body with his mat presence and technique of old school submission holds.Phillip, with his excellent ability to please the crowd with his charisma, and his ability to defeat his opponents with his superior athletic ability, he won and lost every belt in the EWC except the EWC title while Casey struggled as the underdog with the rungs of the EWC rankings. Phillip soon began a feud with a man called "Wreckage," who was the EWC Champion, and a great talent himself who just two years before had injured Casey in a fatal four way elimination match taking away his right to become champion. Wreckage had held that same belt for two years and just as Casey expected was going to go against the man that he brought in himself, Exodus. Casey had fought with the thought of this since the beginning of Exodus' career and soon came to a conclusion, he was going to show Phillip that he wasn't jealous anymore. Casey knew Phillip had the number one contenders match wrapped and that following week after winning the right, Phillip got his chance to fight for the EWC title. During that heated match, Casey came out in hopes to help his longtime friend gain an advantage and brought a chair to ringside. While the referee was distracted, Casey, who was known as Genesis, swung his chair at Wreckage in a rage of revenge, who was in front of Exodus, with his back toward him. Wreckage ducked and Genesis cracked the chair over the head of his friend, Exodus. Genesis panicked and jumped from ringside and ran back into the locker room. Exodus lost his match, and developed bad blood for who he thought was a friend. After weeks of pleading that it was an accident, sorrow built into anger and soon enough, Genesis shared his bad blood and the two became bitter enemies. After the longest running feud in EWC history, Exodus and Genesis had their last match they would ever be in together. Halfway into there "Caged Fury" match, a match in which the two men were pinned against each other in an enclosed cage (Hell in a cell), Genesis performed a powerbomb from atop the cage, and threw Exodus to the ground below. Exodus landed on the top of his back, fracturing his vertebrate in two places.He was rushed to the hospital, his career seemed to be at an end. Genesis accompanied him into the ER, and met up with him the next week. The two talked, Genesis apologizing for his disgraceful tactics and badly blown spot, and promisedExodus he would help him in any way possible. The next year was hell, Exodus worked vigorously until he could walk again. Exodus then became set on returning to the ring, but he wouldn't do it until he was better than he was before his fall.After accompaniying Phillip through gruesome physical therapy Casey could watch no more and wished Phillip good luck on his journey to recovery.
He quit the EWC, which was the bigger promotion within the building and soon chased his dreams to North Carolina cutting all ties within the state of South Carolina. Casey worked for such promoters in the independent circuit such as Johnny Love(ICW), Rex Rumble(NBCW),Tony Starr(PCW), and Nicholause Cross(MWE) still under the name of Genesis. In 2004, under stressful times finacially he decided to hold back his dream until times were right. Upon moving to Shelby, NC he found one of his old promoters Rex Rumble head of NBCW putting on a local show at the county fair. Interested he journeyed there and found a spot in the NBCW under the name Benjamin Blayne. Only months down the road hoping to make a name for himself within the company he found himself in yet another finacial rut, quitting once again with a reluctant phone call to cancel his appearance in Monroe, NC, Benjamin Blayne lies dormant in Charlotte, NC training everyday he can, hoping to make his dream as a professional wrestler....come true.
