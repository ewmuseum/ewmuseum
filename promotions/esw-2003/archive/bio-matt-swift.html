Matt Swift Height: 6'6" Weight: 295 Hometown: Parts unknown Theme Music: A loud scream Alignment: Heel Years wrestling: rookie Titles held: none Wrestling Style:Mat Tech., Hardcore Brawler, powerhouse Age: 23 Years old. Nationality:American Finisher:Swift Pain F5 Bio / Background: Matt Swift is a pretty mysterious indevidual. He likes taking pain. He started working out at the age of thirteen and now he's all muscle. From what his counseler that was assigned to him at the young age of eight said. He's a pretty unhappy kid. Or so she thought. Obviously she wasn't right. Because alot of people who knew him said he was a nice guy. He was just mis understood. Nobody knew why he liked feeling pin. It was just him. One of his High School friends said he was a great guy. He never needed counseling in his friend's opinion. That counseler wrote down alot of lies on his record. Nobody knows why. His friend once snuck in to her office and looked at Matt's Files. It was all a lie. Matt never hurt anyone unless he was provoked. Which happened alot. Because he was paranoid about people saying stuff about him behind his back. So he hurt them. One of his friends gave him the idea in early 2002 to begin wrestling. Matt liked the idea, and has been training for the past two years in a training facility down in Portland Oregon. Now he is looking to start his wrestling career in ESW!
