The Predator (wrestler profile)

The Predator Wrestling Style:Hardcore/Brawler, Mat Technician Status:5th Age: 25 Nationality:American Finisher: The Hunt Down Fisherman's Buster into a sitdown) Secondary Finisher: Karnage Blowout (Grabs the opponent into a Crucifix powerbomb position, throws him forward and sits down, dropping him down into a Tiger Driver position)
Bio / Background: Born on September 7th, 1978, The Predator (real name disclosed) was born in the suburbs of Newark. He was the healthy baby of John and Heather. He was a good kid while growing up. The Predator was one of the smartest kids around. When he was about 15, his parents split up and got divorced. The Predator moved out and lived with a buddy in the mean time. Shortly after, his parents got back together, but The Predator stayed out on his own. When The Predator graduated from high school, the unthinkable happened, his parents got killed in a car accident. The Predator had a tough year waiting for him. He thought about forgetting about everything, but he came to realize that life was to precious. So when he was 19, going on 20, he got into a wrestling school, as well as learning martial arts and started getting into pro wrestling. He wrestled under various ring names. In the next 5 years, he's had a great career, wrestling in many countires around the world. During 2002 and 200 3, The Predator was approched by a female, by the name of Foxxxy, who was a former porn-star, who had a rough teen age life, as well. The Predator taught Foxxxy martial arts, and trained her to be a wrestler. When February 11th, 2003 came around, he joined Ultimate Combat Wrestling and went under the moniker of "The Predator" and changed his whole wrestling style and gimmik, and has kept it since. He is a mysterious person, both inside and out. He reflects on life every which way. Foxxxy joined UCW as well, and valeted Justin Sane, who feuded with The Predator. In UCW, he captured the Iron Man championship. Near mid-2003, The Predator's girlfriend Daniela was killed in a car accident, along with his one year old son. His life has been in major downfall and was in a heart break, but not his wrestling career. He wrestles to keep his mind off of things in life, his wrestling is his life now. The Predator left UCW in August and joined Crimson Mask Wrestling on the 11th of the mo nth. On September 9th, he got the honor to hold the Crimson Glory championship (a gimmik title match/hardcore title) for 93 days, and is still the longest reigning champion in CMW history. Foxxxy then followed the foot steps of The Predator and joined CMW in October. When November came around, both Foxxxy and The Predator captured the Tag team titles. In the mean time, the Predator has had a new girlfriend since the summer, who he deeply loves, but now, the twisted thing happened, both Foxxxy, his girlfriend Shannon and himself are deeply involoved in a 3-way relationship. They have no desire to split up as each one are deeply in love and don't care about the situation. The Predator is now lurking his way to the foundations of Empire State Wrestling. The Predator is an all-around wrestler, he is known for his brutal hardcore wrestling manuvers, as well as his hard impact innovative moves that he often uses.
