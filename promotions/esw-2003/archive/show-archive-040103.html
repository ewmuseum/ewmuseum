NYCW / ESW Archive 2004: January - March Shows | Date | Location | Description Ultimate Mayhem, One Year Bash | 1-5-04 | TNT Arena | The Year End Awards were handed out and Zander Thrash found his way back to the victory lane after hooking up with Vlad. Devo was up to no good costing Rayne a win over Skorp and Steve Hawkins the World title to Eddie who became a 3 time ESW World champion. Draven Xaiver also picked up the TV title.
Ultimate Mayhem | 1-12-04 | BLPW Arena | This should have been called Ultimate Chaos because nothing went as planned. Rayne was given a Tv title shot when her match was changed from non title to title. Jin Tao and Alex mArtinez battled for the #1 contendership for the TV title, while Skorp faced former World champion Hawkins. Devo got his World title shot from Eddie, or so he thought.
Ultimate Mayhem | 1-19-04 | Raleigh Entertainment and Sports Arena Soul Reaver retrurns and Draven makes his TV title rematch bid. Larcen Night makes his in ring return to the ESW while Steve Hawkins blows off some steam. The main event was a tag team affair putting Alex Martinez and Jin Tao up against New TV champion Rayne and Zander Thrash.
