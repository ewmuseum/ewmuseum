Other Moves: Hip Toss, Hurricanranna, Variation of Kicks, Northern Lights Suplex, Diving Crossbody, Elbow Drop, Foot Choke, Arm Drag, Hair Toss, Jumping Snapmare, Bitchslap, Snap Suplex, Snap Russian Leg Sweep, Flipping Dropkick, Swinging Neckbreaker, Tornado DDT Bio The rough and tumble older sister of Precious Fairchild is an individual who can shoot off at the mouth and back up everything she says in the ring. One of the many women to be labeled as one of "Tommy's Divas," Ebony is not nearly as polished and refined in the ring as a majority of the women who have trained under Tommy Purr, including her timid sister Precious. She resorts to brawling to get the job done, believing in putting her opponents away as simply, as easily, and as quickest as possible. Ebony has been best friends with Rebella DeCesare for years, and their bond strengthened when the two were exiled from Tommy's inner-circle in 2009 when it was discovered that the two had helped Daniel McCoy - then Tommy's nemesis - in his quest for vengeance against his former lover.
If Rebella is around, chances are Ebony is not too far behind. The two rarely go into battle without the other and work as one of the most cohesive female units in the ESW known simply as The Material Girls, seeing as how they have a rich and lavish taste for the finer things in life. Despite their harlot appearances and treacherous demeanors, both Rebella and Ebony are steadfastly loyal and loving to their partners. While Rebella is in a committed relationship with former ESW competitor JC Michaels ... Ebony's bonds to The Purr Clan are stronger because of her actual marriage to fellow ESW Referee Trovon Peterson-Darkholme, the adopted brother of Yvonne Peterson-Darkholme, better known as Bombshell.
Despite taking a few shortcuts in the ring as a competitor, Ebony does not tolerate disrespect in the ring as a referee. Ebony is tough and fair and takes her job very seriously as a referee and if a competitor gets in her face - man or woman - she'll gladly get in their face back.
Careful when dealing with this Femme Fatale, because if you cross the Black Beauty's path ... she'll leave you both black AND blue.
