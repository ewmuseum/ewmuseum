Steve Sinclair (wrestler profile)

24 June 2008

The Monster Steve Sinclair Titles held: N/A Awards: September 07 WOTM Strong> Main Events : 1 (1 PPV) Height: 7‘4” Weight: 400 + Hometown: NYC Theme Music: Sway by Coal Chamber Alignment: Co Owning Heel Years wrestling: 18 years Wrestling Style:Mat Tech Power house Shooter Age: 36 Nationality:American Finisher:the Final Call Pedigree Bio / Background: Steve was born January 8th 1971 along with his twin brother KC. Their parents were more than surprise to say the least. Jack was a professional wrestler following in his father's footsteps and Maggie was a stay at home mom. The birth of the twin boys was a pleasant surprise to Maggie and Jack but the marriage crumbled soon after. Maggie grew tired of Jack's month long trips traveling around fighting in several different towns at a time before coming home for a brief stay and hitting the road once again. While on the road Jack fell in love with a women on the other side of the pond, a young lady named Heather, who would give birth to Steve's step sisters (Raven and Ariana and Michael, which would all venture into the wonder world of professional wrestling.) Steve and KC stayed at home with their mother playing and do what normal little kids did. They hardly saw their father much for the next 6 years. In Steve's young childhood, which most of it is repressed deep inside his soul, there was one bright moment. The day his Mother and Father remarried.
The happiness however was short lived. Jack decided that he needed to stay home more. He only traveled in the states looking for work where ever he could and even then he didn't travel very far. Near the end of 1976, Maggie's brother Ted who was also a wrestler by trade started up one of the first wrestling schools in the Northeast and it was the building block of Sinclair INC. A company which is still around to this day, although it has branched off into more of a commercial land developing firm these day. The school was off and running and everything was going great. Steve and KC have meet their siblings and enjoyed time with their father and uncle, especially when you get horseplay in a ring at the gym. But Steve's time with his father and Mother were short lived.
Tragically, they were lost in a freak car accident on a snowy mountain road in Vermont February 24, 1977. A day in Steve's young life that would change him for ever.
From that point on Steve and KC lived with Ted and in time looked up at him as a father figure. When the boys hit their preteen stage, they went on a growth spurt. Steve was growing so fast Ted couldn't keep pants on the boys. Later Steve would be find out he had a Pituitary Gland tumor which was treated, thus this is why Steve is "larger than life." In his teens Steve started training in the gym day and night learning everything he could about the art of wrestling. Ted (Who wrestled under the name Green Man) would take the boys to shows where they would watch their uncle masterfully display his skills in the ring. Steve and KC would later get their feet wet playing the role of body guards for Green Man when they turned 17. By 18 how ever they were getting tossed around the ring nightly making all kinds of mistakes and paying for them. For Steve he had a hard time with hand and eye coordination and due to his tumor had a hard time moving out of his own shadow. (Steve's tumor would be cured latter in live.) His brother KC on the other hand who was just tall (7 foot) quickly earned a reputation as a powerful skillful big man.
It took a while for Steve to learn and hone his craft and by all accounts, it took longer than normal but he got himself straightened out, Steve would blaze a path of destruction though the wrestling world. IN 1989 Steve singed on with the IDCW and even though he was 27 years old rookie, it didn't stop him from picking up Championship gold very quickly. Steve became a wanted man. His size, his power, and his ability to move around the ring with ease, something he worked awful hard at because he didn't want to be labeled a "walking wall" Steve was picked up the BCFW where he capture his first ever World title and then the RWF came knocking.
The RWF was Chamber's first real place he could call home. Chamber stayed at the RWF till the day it closed it's door. During which he was former World champion and current Tag Team champion when the RWF closed it's door. Chamber wandered around for a while, to the ICW, XWF and returned when the XWF became the XWA before Chamber landed on the door steps of the IWA. When it was all said and done, Chamber became known as a career killer, ending 4 careers (Plus another 5 careers in XWA) picking up Tag Team Gold, World Gold, Heavyweight gold (2 separate titles at the time) became the first ever IWA World Heavyweight champion, a title he held twice. The IWA is also the place where Chamber raised to fame with brutal battles with Wilburman, JT Blackout, Mad Cyril to name a few.
When the IWA went under do to piss poor management in the spring of 2001, Steve took a break. He made a few appearances here and there but for the most part kept to himself and stayed under the radar. Steve made money from the IWA and through some key investments through Sinclair INC. Steve had more than enough money to retire. He started to look into Casinos and opened up a Casino and hotel in Miami Florida. Through the casino Steve was able to bank roll enough cash to start thinking about the future.
Towards the end of 2002 Steve made a brief comeback after sitting out more than a year hiatus. Steve was in and out of the CWF fast than you could say "Wilburman sucks" The desire, the fire than fueled him for 4 years was gone. Steve was known for having a short fuse, in his temper but his longevity in his career as well. It was November of 2002 that Steve along with his sister Raven who by now had to retire due to a career ending injury start to make plans in creating their own federation. In January 2003, the NYCW was born.
While Steve might not have been inside the ring for 20 years like some guys, one fact does remain. In his prime, no one could stop Chamber. Several tried. From Tommy Dreamer, Japhy Ryder, X Cess, Wilburman, Nightstalker, Avenger, Sigma and Khaos all tried to stop the "Master" and when it was all said and done, Chamber still stood head and shoulder above those guys and no one was able to knock him down and take Chamber out of the game.
