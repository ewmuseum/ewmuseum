Kid Krazy Hall of Famer Get all of Kid Krazy's merchandising here! Titles held: 3 time ESW World Champion 4 time ESW TV Champion 3 time ESW Survivor Champion 2 time ESW Tag Team Champion Awards: 6 time Wrestler of the Month, July 04, August 05, January and May 06. August and September 08 WOTM Main Events: 31 (6 PPV) Kid Krazy….Super Kid Krazy…..Kid Freaking Krazy……Double K, no matter what you call him or yelled at him you can’t deny what Kid Krazy has accomplished in the ESW. 12 Championships held in the ESW, a record. Held at least 1 title every year the ESW was open but one, 2007. Triple Crown and Grand Slammer winner. Hall of Famer, lady killer. One of the few stars of the ESW to truly be a Icon and represent what the ESW is really about. Kid Krazy is one of a kind.
Height: 5'11" Weight: 220 Hometown: NYC Theme Music: New Noise by Refuse Alignment: Face Years wrestling: Since 2003. Wrestling Style: High Flyer Age: 20 Nationality: American Finisher: Krazy Sault Moon Sault Secondary Finisher: Krazy Split splash Like a frog splash but he like splites his legs Bio/Background: Well, Kid Krazy has had a tough life in the last 20 years. He went to some rough schools and he had to move from town to town state to state because well he was really bad and punched 6 teachers in the face that's why he had to go to different schools. Like said he's crazy so when he got out of high school he went into wrestling.
But one day he was walking in the locker room and that night he had already wrestled and he didn't know it but a great legend Jimmy
Super Fly Snukum watched the whole match. so you know since Jimmy was a legend and all they let him Back stage and Kid Krazy ran into
Jimmy and Jimmy said Kid I watched you out there tonight and I got to tell you you got an eye for talent and Kid Krazy said thanks.
So about a year later Jimmy Called Kid Krazy and said I've been thinking a lot this year and said I decided if you want to I would like to train you cause I want to pass on some of the stuff I've learned to you and Kid Krazy thought about it and said ok so from that moment on Jimmy thought of Kid Krazy as his son.
