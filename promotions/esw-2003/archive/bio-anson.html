Wrestling Technique : Hardcore, Garbage, deathmatch Weapon of Choice : Chairs, barb wire, light tubes, tacks, glass, fireballs Finisher : The End Swinging snap double underhook DDT Bio Not much is known about Anson’s past; particularly since he won’t share any information about his past. What we do know is that Anson was trained briefly in the states before heading to the Land of the Rising Sun. While in Japan, he sought out and was trained by the Great Muta.
Anson began competing under a plain mask as a jobber and earned his stripes before coming out from under the mask. He debuted four years ago in Tokyo in a merciless beating from his mentor, the Great Muta. Over the next year, Anson showed his ability to absorb pain and continue on with the match and the crowds began to take notice.
Anson left Japan a year ago with the blessing of his mentor, and has learned the secret of Muta’s mist. He has chosen the C.W.A. as his new home. He has been brought in by Greyson Blaze in his quest to eliminate Tommy Purr. At King of Philadelphia on December 14th, 2009, it was revealed that Anson is a former student of Tommy's. It appears Anson has joined forces with Jack Spade and Greyson Blaze, forming an unholy trio intent on ending Tommy's career.
