Bio / Background: Angelo Valour was born in a small town just a few miles out of Boston, Massachusetts called Little Hamlet. Being the oldest of three, Angelo was pushed hard from birth. After the birth of his youngest sibling, Tracy Velour, His mother and father learned they couldn’t stand each other and they split up, his father taking his brother and sister, Travis and Tracy.
For the next ten years, Angelo was treated with little compassion. His mother treated him like nothing more than a dog and had him work whenever possible. Valour learned early in life to hate. After a freak car accident, his mother died and Angelo was shipped to his father in Minneapolis Minnesota. He didn’t stay there long though because his father sent him, like his siblings, to a boarding school in England.
Angelo didn’t last long in the boarding school because of his hating personality and once being sent back to his father’s, was sent straight to the work force. To pass the time as well as keep his mind off of his traumatizing childhood, Angelo took up weight training at the gym and at twelve became a personal trainer.
After eighteen long years, Angelo finally reunited with both his younger brother and his sister. Travis and Angelo quickly became best friends and began training to become professional wrestling. They learned a mix of martial arts and boxing.
After gaining an opportunity in the ESW, Angelo quickly put the ESW Television Championship under his belt and it quickly became the most prized possession in his eyes. This sparked a month and a half feud against former ESW Television Champion, Ritilin. After proving the better man, Angelo also picked up the ESW World Championship. He reigned at the top of ESW for two long months before finally being dethroned by Panik, where he lost his World Championship.
*Updated 4/25/09* Valour’s next obstacle was ESW’s bad boy, Double J Joe Jones, who managed to strip Valour of his prized possession in a Steel Cage match at Merry Mayhem. Luckily, Valour’s slump ended 8 months later when he picked up the ESW Survivor Championship and became a Triple Crown champion.
Due to engagements wrestling in Japan, Valour set out to train and wrestle in Japan. Gaining many pounds and even growing taller, Valour makes his ESW return in top form.
