Travis Valour Titles held: N/A Awards: N/A Main Events : Zero Height: 6‘0” Weight: 210 Hometown: Parts Unknown Theme Music: “Party like a Rockstar” by Shop Boys Alignment: Tweener Years wrestling: 1st Wrestling Style:Highflyer Mat tech Shooter Age: 22 Nationality:unknown Finisher:Ring of Fire Travis puts a cobra clutch on his opponent and then suddenly turns it into a snap reverse cobra clutch suplex. Bio / Background: Travis Valour grew up with his father as his father moved to Minneapolis Minnesota, losing contact with his older brother, Angelo. He and his younger sister, Tracy, quickly became close friends as they were sheltered from any other children. At age seven, Travis was sent to boarding school in England where he only managed to stay there for eight years. After being kicked out for bad behavior at age sixteen, Travis moved back to Minneapolis
After sixteen years, Angelo and Travis were reunited and the brothers decided to train together. With Angelo’s weight training experience and knowledge around the gym, the brothers trained for seven long years before Angelo made his way to the ESW. Seeing the opportunity to become a huge player, Travis Valour took flight to the ESW.
After being overshadowed by his older brother, Travis looks to become the second Valour to become a man of main event in the ESW. Will he ever get out of his brother’s shadow? Only time will tell.
