Frank Merritt (wrestler profile)

Frank Merritt Titles held:none Awards: Main Events: 5 (1 PPV) Height: 6‘4” Weight: 330 Hometown: Kelso Washington. Theme Music: I will be heard - Hatebreed Alignment: Mega Heel Years wrestling: 6th Wrestling Style:Mat Tech Shooter Martial Artist Age: 28 (8/6/78) Nationality:American Finisher:Blunt Trauma Vertabreaker Bio / Background: Frank grew up in a small town in Washington State watching wrestling and boxing. He started boxing when he was twenty and had a lot of promise. He was a heavyweight who was fast and had a great jab. He went 10-0 in his first ten matches before he tested positive one too many times for THC.
He was suspended by the Amateur Boxing Association for a month for his violation and was to complete a drug rehab program and submit to random drug testing. His next offense meant he was to be expelled from the A.B.A. Due to his mouth, he never boxed again. Frustrated and tired of being jobless, he looked into schooling. He couldn’t find anything he liked….so in 1999, he joined a wrestling school ran by a man named Len Denton who was a local wrestling celebrity known as the “Grappler.”
Denton saw the raw talent in this kid and was happy to train. He trained Frank in some of the basic wrestling moves, all the while keeping him in the gym lifting weights. Frank bulked up and trained with Billy Jack Haynes, learning from the master of the Full Nelson, before working the local wrestling circuit. Growing tired of the small time and being cocky as he is, Frank looked elsewhere.
He talked to his sister who lived in Jersey and she told him of this one fed in New York. Some of the wrestler’s are the ones from the C.W.F, a former Portland Oregon based fed……or so he had been told. He packed his bags into his faded Royal Blue 79’ Cadillac and headed to New Jersey. Four days and an oil pump later, he made it to his sister’s home. She put him up in a spare room and after a few days of getting adjusted to the time change, Frank headed to the Amsterdam Mall, to New York Championship Wrestling. He pulled his car into a parking stall near the entrance and walked inside with a backpack slung over his shoulder.
Frank was given an open contract with the company where he worked for the next three months. One night, after a show in Canada, Frank and his then manager and still friend, Mike “Grimmy” Grimm, were arrested for trying to bring three pounds of Marijuana in the country via the spare tire in their rental car. They both served a number of months before being fully pardoned, due to the fact that no fingerprints could be found on the weed or tire matching either Frank or Grimmy.
The two returned for a short stint in the N.Y.C.W. Frank even captured Tag Team gold in the N.eW.A. with his partner Jeffery Hatemonger. Due to backstage politics, Hatemonger got the two stripped of their belts which pissed Frank off. Soon after, Frank jumped ship to join the Flock in the C.W.A. With his new allies, Frank and the rest of the Flock dominated the C.W.A. before moving on to C.M.W. Ego’s backstage, belonging to some of the company’s “Established” talent forced the Flock out of C.M.W. and looking for a new place to roost…and that place is the N.L.C.W.
He has been destroying the competition in the N.L.C.W. ranks for many years now, and is ready to retire, due to the wear-and-tear on his body and the call of Hollywood. He has recently signed a contract with E.S.W. in hopes of obtaining the World Title before eventually retiring from the sport.
