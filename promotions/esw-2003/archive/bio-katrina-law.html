Bio Katrina was born in Dublin Ireland, but her family moved to America while she was still in diapers, so she never knew much of her family in Ireland. After moving around, trying to find a good fit, the Lawrence family finally settled in Easley, South Carolina, finding the people there friendly, and helpful. Years passed, and Katrina found that she enjoyed school more than anything. Her love for books, and learning was astounding, and she rarely found herself bored as a child. Sadly, just after her 13'th birthday, her father passed away. This hit Kat very hard, and she no longer felt the drive to cram her mind with knowledge. Very soon after that, she found herself in her first fight at school, after being teased about her father by some upper classmen. It was three girls against Katrina....they should have brought more girls as Katrina decimated all of them. This is when her junior high school wrestling coach took notice, and invited her to check out the wrestling team.
She was the only girl on the team...in fact...in the league, and she only lost to one man throughout junior high, and high school; a boy named Phillip Dimani. No matter what, he would always take whatever disadvantage she had him at, and use it against her...needless to say she found this frustrating, but even more than that she found it, and him, very interesting. Kat set records for the state in wrestling, and Phillip broke them. Soon enough, Kat was the only person that could hold his or her own on the mat with Phillip, and this is what began their relationship. Katrina was as happy as she was before her father died, and it was only when she was with Phillip, that she reached her potential. So it broke her heart after High school, when Phillip was injured in a wrestling match at a small promotion in their town. She, along with Phillip’s best friend, stayed by his side, and helped him recover fully. Phillip vowed to come back better than ever, and that is when he moved to Japan, and out of Katrina’s life. The devastated Katrina Lawrence found solace in her true love’s words, however, and began training herself to become great in the sport of wrestling. She dominated various small time promotions in, and around her town before realizing that she had been given a talent that not many people every get close to achieving, and set her sights on bigger things. After training, and saving her money for years, she finally set out to New York to test her mettle against true professionals. The first promotion she came across was Empire State Wrestling, and when she reached their headquarters, she was overwhelmed by one of the names on the roster: Phillip Dimani, who now was known as Exodus.
They were reunited, and soon after, married. Phillip went on to become one of ESW’s greatest champions, Triple Crown winner, and eventually Hall of Famer while she stood behind him. After their son, Cole, was born, Phillip retired from the game…now it was Katrina’s turn…and she is determined to finally beat Phillip at his own game.
