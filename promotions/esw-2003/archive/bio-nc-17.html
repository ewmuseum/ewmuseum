NC-17 Titles held: 2007 ESW Tag Team champion. Awards: Main Events : 1 (1 PPV) Height: 6‘2” Weight: 218 Hometown: Unknown Theme Music: Had Enough by Breaking Benjamin Alignment: Neutral Years wrestling: unknown Wrestling Style:Mat Tech High Flyer Age: Unknown Nationality:Unknown Finisher:Disasterpiece While is opponent is laying on his back in the ring, NC-17 goes to the top turnbuckle and hits a twisting swanton bomb
Bio / Background: The crack research staff at BLPW has come up with this brief history on the man that goes by NC-17. He started wrestling over in Japan with the Osaka Pro Wrestling organization. There he feuded with the likes of Ebessan and Gamma, then had a brutal and bloody feud with Azteca. That feud culminated in a loser leaves town match which was won by NC-17 and sent Azteca on his way. Shortly after the feud eneded so did NC-17's contract with OPW and he headed off to Mexico to work for New Wrestling Stars. There he feuded with the likes of Cyborg and the Disciple before engaging in a very heated feud with fan favorite LiveWire. The organization wasn't able to sustain it's funding and went under thus leaving NC-17 a free agent. He now arrives in BLPW with little fanfare a very mysterious reputation.
