Ravenspell, the stars' pub

19 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to... RAVENSPELL!! From time to time you might find yourself wandering into a place called Ravenspell. You might be asking yourself what is Ravenspell?
Ravenspell as the name might imply is a pub owned by Raven Sinclair. Built to look like a castle Ravenspell is hang out for the stars after a show or just to kill time. It's three floors of madness, each floor has a different theme. It's opened seven days a week from 2 PM till 4 am and has specials every night. Monday night being ESW night where they throw a huge party and watch ESW's Ultimate Mayhem.
It's a friendly place to hang out, cut a promo or get drunk. Hang out and have all the fun you want, but beware, any damage to the place it will be your hide that pays the price.
Where is Ravenspell? Ravenspell is located in a old armory in Schenectady a few blocks away from the TNT arena. What's the size of Ravenspell? It's a three story bar and pub. The first floor is a Celtic pub with a sport bar. Aka Hooters with a Celtic theme The second floor is the dance and sometimes Goth hang out joint. And the third floor is the offices, storage, crap you will never see.
A few faces of Ravenspell: Angus the Head bartender. Angelo, Nails (Second floor bartender), and Timmy are also bartenders at Ravenspell. And two bouncers,
KC Sinclair (when in town), Matt, and Rennick.
