ESW (Empire State, 2003) — Roster

Triple Crown and Grand Slam winners

5 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NYCW / ESW Triple Crown Family These are the guys and gals who went on to hold every single singles title the ESW has to offer. They pulled off the Triple feat which we call the Triple Crown. Not everyone will be able to do this, some never will, but to the ones who do pull this off, Kudos to you.
8/9/04 Kid Krazy Krazy was our first ever Triple Crown Winner. Krazy first picked up the Tag Team titles with Eddie (before they became a yearly one night tournament) and went on to be the first ever Survivor champion, a title he would hold for 130 days. He then picked up the TV title and it took him less than a month to win the biggest prize of them all, the ESW World title.
9/13/04 Larcen Night The second ever Triple Crown winner, Larcen was a surprise when he won the ESW World title to cement his place in history. Larcen to date has done the feat faster than anyone. (It's a huge list, I know) From February to August in 2004 Larcen picked up the Survivor, Tag Team, TV and World titles. A span of 6 months which happened when he broke free from Blackout and went out on his own.
1/25/05 Rayne ESW's first huge superstar took the longest to capture the ESW Survivor title to enroll herself into the Triple Crown Family. A former World, 2 time TV champion finally decided it was time to pick up the one title that she was missing from her collection. It was just one more notch in her legacy as a immortal in the ESW.
4/25/05 Exodus Exodus is more well known for being a world class World Champion and for beginning his ESW career 15-0, but after some time off Exodus return to complete the hat trick. He picked up the Survivor title for about 5 seconds then battled Helena Kreuz for the TV title and won it at Broken Rulez.
5/30/05 Soul Reaver Soul Reaver entered the TC hall when he picked up the Survivor title when he beat Sick Nick for the vacated title. It perhaps took Soul Reaver the longest to complete the trick (Over two years) but was well worth the wait when he done it..
2/13/06 Sick Nick It was a long hard road for Sick Nick to gain this honor but he finally did it when he won the World title at Bloody Hell 3.
5/08/06 Darien Sunn It took Sunn just under 6 month and 24, count them 24 matches to capture the 2006 Soul Survivor, ESW Survivor, two time ESW TV and ESW World titles. A feat that can only be matched by the run of Larcen Night. In a short span, Sunn has gone from FNG to the shinning star of the ESW.
8/21/06 Rob "Slipstream" Richards The Day. August 21st 2006, one of the days that you will remember where you where when Slipstream defeated Disgruntle to capture the ESW TV title. The former 3 time World champion, Survivor and Tag Team champion finally won the one title that has eluded him in his promise to become a Triple Crown winner and the 4th Grand Slammer in ESW history.
12/24/06 Widowmaker Widowmaker showed up for the final show of 06 and in doing so he won the ESW Survivor title to add another star to his growing collection of achievements by becoming the latest member to Triple Crown Family..
12/17/08 Christian Pierce On the final show of 2007 The picture of perfection stamped his ticket to the ESW HOF when he finally won the ESW World title to complete his Triple Crown bid. Who says the turtle never won?
7/7/08 Angelo Valour After taking some time off and a period of uncertainty Angelo finally picked up the Survivor title from Rayne 7/7/08 to become a Triple Crown Winner. With still a bright future ahead of him who knows what he can still achieve.
Prozac 2/17/09 Lacking the TV title, Prozac finally won the only thing he hasn‘t won in the ESW, the ESW Television title and in doing so he joins Slipstream in the ELITE class when he defeated Darien Sunn at Bloody Hell. Prozac is a 3 time Survivor champion, 2008 Tag Team, Soul Survivor and MVP winner.
NYCW / ESW Grand Slamers There have been a handful of peeps who have done something that not everyone will have the chance to do. They won and held EVERY title the ESW has to offer. They have held the World, TV, Survivor, and Tag Team titles at one point in their ESW career.
Kid Krazy 8/9/04 Held the tag team titles with Eddie, Held the Survivor title for a record 130 days, then picked up the TV title and moved onto the ESW World title.
Larcen Night 9/13/04 Larcen won the Survivor title, then the tag Team titles of 04 at Bruise Cruise II, moved onto the TV title then completed his journey with the World title. Larcen still remains the fastest to win the Triple Crown and the Grand Slam Feb of 04 to Sept of 04
Rayne 7/18/05 Rayne became the newest Grand Slam winner when she won the ESW Tag Team titles with slipstream at Bruise Cruise III
Rob "Slipstream" Richards 8/21/06 The Day. August 21st 2006, one of the days that you will remember where you where when Slipstream defeated Disgruntle to capture the ESW TV title. The former 3 time World champion, Survivor and Tag Team champion finally won the one title that has eluded him in his promise to become a Triple Crown winner and the 4th Grand Slammer in ESW history.
Prozac 2/17/09 Lacking the TV title, Prozac finally won the only thing he hasn‘t won in the ESW, the ESW Television title and in doing so he joins Slipstream in the ELITE class when he defeated Darien Sunn at Bloody Hell. Prozac is a 3 time Survivor champion, 2008 Tag Team, Soul Survivor and MVP winner. Prozac is the ESW‘s 5th Grand Slammer.
ESW’s ELITE Class The Elite Class is reserved for the few who have achieved something that not everyone can or will do. To be in the Elite Class one must do the following:
A.) Win every singles title along with the Tag Team titles B.) Capture ESW’s Soul Survivor cup. C.) Named ESW’s MVP Rob “Slipstream“ Richards 1/15/07 Slipstream created the Elite Class when he won the Soul Survivor cup of 07 at Soul Survivor. A four time World champion, former MVP and a Grand Slammer, Slipstream went where no one else in the history of the ESW has gone.
Prozac 2/17/09 Lacking the TV title, Prozac finally won the only thing he hasn‘t won in the ESW, the ESW Television title and in doing so he joins Slipstream in the ELITE class when he defeated Darien Sunn at Bloody Hell. Prozac is a 3 time Survivor champion, 2008 Tag Team, Soul Survivor and MVP winner.
