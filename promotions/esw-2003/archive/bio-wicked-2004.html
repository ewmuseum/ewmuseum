Wicked (wrestler profile, 2005 edition)

4 May 2005

Wicked Wrestling Style: Hardcore Brawler, Mat Technician Status:10th year Age: 25 Nationality:American Finisher:The Haunting The opponent is hooked in a suplex position and then raised into a stalling vertical position. The executioner drops the opponents legs onto the nearest ropes resulting in the opponents body to bounce back to the same position only to be dropped back in the same position it all started(technically a 180) in a sit down piledriver. It is a finisher/pin.
Secondary Finisher: Six Feet Under Used whenever possible.....The opponent wherever he lays in on the ground. the executioner has two chairs against his chest on the top rope. He flys off only to turn sideways enough to drop the elbow on top of the chairs on top of the opponents chest.
Bio / Background: David Rhymer(Wicked) started young in the business, greener than ever a promoter approached him only when he was 15. This man by the name of Alvin Richards ran a promotion out of Hendersonville North Carolina that went by SWA(Southern Wrestling Alliance) David was amazed and acknowledged to alvin that he had never been in a ring before. Impressed by only David's persona and looks alvin knew that he could make this kid a star. Alvin had arranged a meeting with David's parents in Louisiana weeks after David and Alvin had met, being a man of his word Alvin sat down with David and his parents over a nice Cajun dinner and talked over the statistics. Agreeing to the terms David's family picked up and left the bayou for good and nessled into a nice little home right outside of Hendersonvill North Carolina. Soon David was training his ass off while along beside him Alvin watched a boy become a monsterous superstar at the training facility located in Chesnee South Carolina. A ye ar later, David was 16 and in Alvin's eyes ready to shine, Alvin sat down once again with David's family and talked over what the deal was. Agreeing once more David was ready to become an Indy Circuit Professional. David built a name for himself by mixing the unique style of Hardcore and Technical and soon threw out his given name and gimmicked his name to Wicked. Alvin was overjoyed and ran Wicked in the first ever Hardcore Tournament, at the end Wicked was over and became the first ever SWA Hardcore Champion setting off a humongous buzz throughout the Indy Circuit. Soon Wicked was a household name, talk about this young boy was rightous and true to the game. This boy was gonna make something out of himself they all said and two years down the road Wicked was crowned SWA World Heavyweight Champion. By now Wicked had won and lost almost every title in the SWA that he could and that same night was also crowned SWA's first Grand Slam Champion. Wicked reigned champion for a yea r and a half....impressive for a 18 year old until his arch nemesis Max Cortez stole it out from under him in a cheap victory due to interference. Wicked and Max ran the longest fued in SWA history until finally three years later it was ended once and for all in a retirement/championship Seven Layers of Hell Match(now Wicked's Trademark match)luckily Wicked went over and became two time SWA World Champion and retired Max Cortez at the same time. Now growing out of his young stage Wicked was 21 and had done almost everything you can do in a promotion, he was the number one Face in the company and had won all the belts he could win. But one thing was missing for him, and a year later he got it. Wicked was named PWI's Indy Wrestler of the Year, still excited Wicked wanted one more thing and finally it happened a couple days after his 23rd birthday, JAPW's Promoter Hiysaki Jumaotu gave Wicked a call and made him an offer, and being the man he was Wicked accepted and moved away f rom home and off to Japan. In Japan Wicked learned certain ways to tweek his unique style that he had created for himself and by the age of 24 Wicked was at his best that he could ever be. He left JAPW after his one year contract ran out and made tours around the US getting booked in awesome TV promotions everytime he stepped foot in any state. Now at his peak in his career Wicked is ready to play with the big boys and dominate every astrospect of Professional Wrestling.
