Wrestling Technique : Powerful and Technical Weapon of Choice : Himself Finisher : Silverback Brain Buster Bio Cory Chevelle was born Cory McCormick in Kansas City, Mo on July 21st, 1972. He grew up in small town USA, competed in every sport he could as a kid, and always had the same dream through out his life; To become the greatest pro-wrestler ever. He became an accomplished amateur wrestler and football player in high school due to his more than remarkable athletic abilities. He even earned the nickname “Hulk” both due to his incredible size and physique. Chevelle of course wrestled through out college and competed nationally. After College, he went straight into the wrestling scene despite what his family encouraging a life in law enforcement or military.
The federation was Xtreme Pro-Championship Wrestling. He was green as could be. Didn’t know a thing about the business and didn’t know who could be called friend and who could be called an enemy. For the longest time he went out night after night getting his ass handed to him. Until he met ‘The Madman’ Alex Wills. Alex took Chevelle under his wing, and molded him into a real wrestler. It wasn’t long after being paired with Alex that Chevelle started tasting success. The two captured the tag titles together, and that caught everyone’s eye and got Chevelle’s foot in the door for a Hardcore title shot, which Chevelle won also. Alex and Chevelle as a tag team were unstoppable, but eventually, Alex became jealous of the attention Chevelle was getting. When Chevelle captured the IC title and held three XPCW titles at once, Alex turned on Chevelle and threw him out to the wolves. (Seriously, it was an eeeeevil stable in XPCW. They were like fuckin mafia there) The tag titles were removed from the now ex-duo and the two were caught in a rush for the XPCW Heavyweight title. Alex would attempt to go to the Wolves of Belfast again, but wouldn’t find any help. The two fought at the Asylum (massive ppv), and Chevelle was crowned the XPCW HW champ after a bloody brawl. Chevelle continued competing in various matches, picking up numerous titles in XPCW and would forever feud with Alex Wills for the Heavyweight gold. His last title reign in XPCW was after retiring Alex Wills, and the reign lasted for a record breaking 8 months before the GM, Michael Sharpton stripped Chevelle of the belt and fired him for personal differences after two years of working together. (True story. Then the place died 4 months later.)
After that, it was AAPW. He had success, but he wasn’t really satisfied with it. Chevelle also had recently married his long time girl friend, and had a child together. He didn’t see AAPW truly going anywhere or getting him anywhere, so he moved to a fed called Absolute Anarchy. AA was filled with insanity, but it’s where Chevelle met long time friends like Tony Daniels and E. E. Faulk. It’s where he feuded with the likes of Faulk and Coc Chaos. Again, Chevelle nabbed titles and experienced great fame. Fame that would take him to that ‘legendary’ status when he signed with W.E.F. Ryan Hart found Chevelle in AA and offered him a boat load of money and the chance to compete with the best in wrestling. In WEF, Chevelle was believed to be the foundation of the fed. A lot of the wrestlers who signed, signed so they could face Chevelle. He feuded with The Cheat, Coc Chaos, X-Factor, Connor Gael, Wildcard and many others in his stint at WEF. But later on there was trouble in paradise, and Chevelle parted ways with the company before returning to serve as a trainer and gm.
Meanwhile, he also signed with TAWF under Wildcard. Wildcard got the Chevelle name under his roof, and Chevelle got new talent to face. TAWF was a good place for Cory, he was happy there, and thought it’d be the last federation he would wrestle at. After years of winning gold and having instant classics with so many, he was considering hanging up the boots. Then came TAWF and WEF’s merger…
Chevelle went into WRW, as GM once again. Chevelle was a positive force for the guys backstage. Again, he served as a mentor for many whether it be his brother Angelus or friend Ty Bahr. But then the ring began calling him once again, and Chevelle couldn’t turn away. Again he was feuding with the likes of Wildcard and others. But his high light feud came with a rookie known as Richie Reid. It would also be his last. WRW was the only fed Chevelle’s been part of that he never held any gold. His last match was with Richie Reid, which Chevelle won, and then he fell off the face of the Earth.
