ESW (Empire State, 2003) — Roster

Championships history, as of mid-2008

18 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Championships History NYCW/ESW Championship! Eddie 3/3/03 - 4/7/03 [35 days] Eddie won the title at the first ever NYCW Kick Ass Inc show on March 3rd 2003. He won the title after going undefeated in the "Road to the Gold" tournament. Eddie lost the title at the second Kick Ass Inc "The Second Coming" show April 7th 2003. Eddie's run as short as it was shrouded in controvercy as he was a "protected" champion. Eddie vows to regain the title he lost.
Rayne 4/7/03 - 5/26/03 [49 days] Rayne is the first female in the northeast to be crowned a World champion to our knowdledge. She won the title from Eddie in a Steel cage match at Kick Ass Inc "The Second Coming" on April 7th 2003.
Eddie (2) 5/26/03 - 6/23/04 [28 days] Eddie won back the NYCW title at Kick Ass Inc The 3rd Strike. In a three way dance between Rayne, now his girlfriend and Jackal.
Horace Pinkerton 6/23/03 - 7/28/03 [35 days] Horace won the title at the June 23rd Ultimate Mayhem show when he covered Rayne in a tag Team match with Devo and Eddie.
Widowmaker 7/28/03 - 8/18/03 [21 days] Widowmaker defeated Horace and Soul Reaver on Mayhem 7/28/03 to capture the NYCW Championship.
JT Blackout 8-18-03 - 10/13-03 [56 days] Blackout won the title from Widowmaker on Mayhem 8-18 in a match that everyone waited a long time for.
Title Vacant Raven stripped JT Blackout of the NYCW World title on 10/13/2003. Steve Hawkins 11/10/03 - 1/5/03 [56 days] Steve Hawkins won the title in a three way at Ultimate Mayhem 11-10-03 after the title was vacated by JT Blackout.
Eddie(3) 1/5/04 - 3/22/04 [77 days] Steve Hawkins long run as the World champion came to a end after the new year at the One Year bash for the ESW on 1/5/04.
Soul Reaver 3/22/04 - 5/31/04 [70 days] Soul Reaver completed his comeback story by beating Hawkins and Eddie at March Madness 3/22/04 ending Eddie's 3 month run as the World champion.
Exodus 5/31/04 - 7/05/04 [36 days] After Soul Reaver pulled a fast one over him the PPV before, Exodus was on a mission to win the World title at Driven Supremacy 5/31/04 and pull his record to 11-0!
Zander Thrash 7/05/04 - 8/9/04 [35 days] Zander capped off a great month by winning the ESW World title at Violent Desires 7/5/04 by beating Exodus. Zander was considered by many a unlikely winner but he proved everyone wrong by winning the gold.
Kid Krazy 8/9/04 - 9/13/04 [35 days] At Run Riot, Krazy did the unthinkable, he became the only star to have won every title in the ESW, beating Rayne in a race of sorts to be the first ever Triple Crown Champion, but his victory did come at a cost.
Larcen Night 9/13/04 - 10/18/04 [30 days] Larcen walked into Suicide Mission on a Mission and won the ESW World title. By becoming the new ESW World Champion, he also became ESW's second ever Triple Crown Champion.
Jack Rice(2) 10/18/04 - 12/6/04 [49 days] Widowmaker returned and made it his mission to regain his World title, which he did at Boiling Point. Becoming the second star in ESW history to hold the World title more than once, Widowmaker looks to hold his title for a long long time.
Griffin 12/20/04 - 4/25/05 [156 days] After Raven stripped Jack Rice of the World title, the search was on to crown a new champion. A week before Merry Mayhem, two rose above the crowd to fight for t he title. Slipstream and Griffin. In the end, Griffin beat Slipstream at Merry Mayhem to capture the ESW World Title.
Slipstream 4/25/05 - 5/30/05 [30 days] With Nanook stripping Griffin after Griffin has been AWOL for two months, Slipstream finally got his shot at the World title. A steel cage ladder match at Broken Rules. Griffin didn't show up thus making Slipstream the ESW World Champion.
Wicked 5/30/05 - 7/1/2005 [31 days] The shocked happened at Mayhem as Wicked beat Slipstream and Bartholomew for the World title.
Rob "Slipstream" Richards (2) 7/1/2005 - 11/22/05 [152 days] Rob won back the World title he lost to Wicked at Driven Supremacy in a wild three way two fall double title shot match..
Jack "Widowmaker" Rice (3) 11/22/2005 - 2/13/06 [83 days] Widowmaker ended Slipstream's title run 4 days short of the record at Boiling Point in a fatel four way match for his 3rd time as the ESW World champion.
Sick Nick2/13/06 - 3/6/06 [21 days] Sick Nick joined the triple Crown family when he finally won the World title at Bloody Hell, with some help.
Slipstream (3)3/6/06 - 5/1/06 [56 days] Sick Nick put the title on the line at Barn Storming against Slipstream to show some respect to Slip, the fans..
"Super" Kid Krazy (2) 5/1/06 - 5/8/06 [7 days] The Super one defeated everyone at Driven Supremacy to pick up the ESW World title for a second time...
Darien Sunn 5/8/06 - 12/24/06 [230 days] One week after Sunn lost to Kid Krazy at Driven Supremacy, Sunn came back to beat Double K for the World title and was welcomed into the Triple Crown family as well....
Slipstream and Hot Stuff Shawn Sanders 12/24/06 - 2/12/07 [50 days] In a bizarre ending in a three way match at Merry Mayhem, both Sanders and Slipstream pinned Sunn to win the World title and become co title holders..
Slipstream (5) 2/12/07 - 4/23/07 [70 days] Slipstream became ESW undisputed World champion when he beat Shawn Sanders in a ladder match at Bloody Hell.
Shawn Sanders (2) 4/16/07 - 4/23/07 [7 days] Steve Sinclair stripped Slipstream of the World title and named Shawn Sanders the new ESW World champion, Then set to defend the title against Rayne at Driven Supremacy……….
Rayne (2) 5 /23/07 - 8/27/07 [96 days] Rayne defeated Shawn Sanders for the World title at Driven Supremacy when Slipstream returned to distract Sanders.
Angelo Valour 8 /27/07 - 10/29/07 [63 days] Angelo Valour picked up his second title here in the ESW when he defeated Rayne for the ESW World title at Mayhem ending her long streak..
Panik 10/29/07 - 12/17/07 [49 days] The BLPW star who joined the ESW picked up the ESW World title when he beat Angelo Valour at Suicide Mission.
Christian Pierce 12/17/07 - 3/17/08 [91 days] Pierce finally pulled it out of the bag and won the World title from Panik at Merry Mayhem.
Prozac 3/17/08 - 5/27/08 [71 days] Prozac cashed in his Soul Survivor prize and picked up the ESW World title at Ultimate massacre when he beat Pierce for the title.
Brain Hunter 5/27/08 Brain Hunter picked where he left off at the last PPV, He won the ESW World title at Kick Ass Inc when he beat Prozac becoming 3rd multi title holder in ESW history.
NYCW/ESW Television Championship! DEVO 4/7/03 - 5/26/03 [49 days] Devo won the TV title at the April 7th Kick Ass Inc "The Second coming" show by winning a 15 man over the top battle royal. This was a huge victory for Devo who was just returning from another shoulder injury...
Soul Reaver 5/26/03 - 7/7/03 [42 days] Soul Reaver finally won the TV title, this is after he stole the title 3 weeks before from Devo at Kick Ass Inc the 3rd Strike. Rayne 7/7/03 - 9/1/03 [56 days] Rayne won the title from Soul Reaver on 7/7/03 on Ultimate Mayhem making her the first NYCW Superstar to have held the NYCW and TV championships.
Samuel Amos 9/1/03 - 12/22/03 [113 days] Samuel who was undefeated in NYCW action upon his entrance quickly won the TV title on 9/1/03 against a match with Rayne on the famed Bruise
Cruise. Title Vacant After a 3 month run as the TV Champion, Amos lost his title when he lost to Steve Hawkins at 12/22/03 Kick Ass Inc and that resulted in Amos losing his job and title.
Draven Xaiver 1/5/04 - 1/12/04 [7 days] Draven Xaiver won the TV title after beating Jin Tao in the final of a one night tourament at the One Year Bash on 1/5/03.
Rayne (2) 1/12/04 - 3/22/04 [70 days] Rayne became the first two time TV Champ after co-owner Raven Sinclair made her non title bout with Draven Xaiver a title match following his attack on the title's 2 #1 contenders, on January 12th 2004's showing of Ultimate Mayhem!
SunFeather/vacant 3/22/04 - 3/23/04 [1 day] Rayne defended her TV title at March Madness 3/22/04 against SunFeather, then she handed the title to SunFeather citing "her boyfriend Eddie is more important". Soon after the match, SunFeather handed the title over to Steve Sinclair. This also ended a 3 month run by Rayne.
Kid Krazy 4/26/04 - 7/05/04 [70 days] With out a champion since March, 4/26/04 at Broken Rules one was crowned when Kid Krazy battled Zander Thrash in a ladder match for the TV title.
SunFeather 7/05/04 - 7/19/04 [14 days] She may have lost the Survivor title in the same match at 7/5/04 Violent Desires, but she upgraded by winning the TV Title pinning Kid Krazy for the title in a 2 pin fall double title match.
Larcen Night 7/19/04 - 9/20/04 [62 days] Larcen with some help from Nanook beat SunFeather on 7/19/04 Mayhem to capture the TV title.
Angel 10/18/04 - 12/20/04 [64 days] 31-16 Angel finally picked up the ESW TV title that was vacont for more than a month at Boiling Point when she beat Chris Toxic inside the Hell in a Cell..
Khaos 12/20/04 - 2-1-05 [42 Days] New comer Khaos got the shot he waited for and took it by the horns at Merry Mayhem but beating Angel, something that doesn't happen very often to win the TV title.
Helena Kreuz 2/1/05 - 4/25/06 [84 days] Helena surprised the champ when he won the TV at Mayhem ending Khaos's run as the TV champion.
Exodus 4/25/05 - 10/31/05 [189 days] Exodus completed his Triple Crown cycle by winning the TV title from Helena Kreuzz at Broken Rules..
Sick Nick 11/22/2005 - 1/16/06 [55 days] After Exodus retired and ended his run as TV champion which he very quietly set a new record for long title run, Sick Nick beat Rayne at Boiling Point to pick up the TV title.
Griffin 1-16-06 - 2-6-06 [21 days] Griffin picked up the TV title at Soul Survivor in a surprise title match set up by Raven in Griffin's return to the ESW
Sick Nick (2) 2-6-06 - 2-20-06 [14 days] Sick Nick gained some revenge against griffin at Mayhem and captured the TV title for a second time.
Darien Sunn 2-20-06 - 3/13/06 [21 days] Sunn won the TV title from Sick Nick at Mayhem, Sunn's Second title in his short stay in the ESW thus far..
Widowmaker 3/13/06 - 4/3/06 [21 days] Widowmaker picked up the ESW TV title from Sunn when he beat Sunn at mayhem..... Blacklotis 4/3/06 - 4/25/06 [22 Days] A long time fixture of the ESW, Blacklotis finally won himself some gold when he beat Widowmaker at Mayhem for the TV title. This victory also gave birth to the Black Diamonds.
Darien Sunn (2) 4/25/06 - 6/19/06 [13 Days] Sunn became a two time TV Champion when he beat Lotis and Widowmaker on Mayhem for the TV Title.
Kid Krazy (2) 6/19/06 - 7/17/06 [18 days] One week after winning the ESW Tag Team titles with Khaos, Kid Krazy beat Khaos at Mayhem for the vacant TV title, Kid Krazy's 9th title in the ESW.
Disgruntle 7/17/06 - 8/22/06 [36 days] Disgruntle picked up his first ever championship when he defeated Kid Krazy at Mayhem. Slipstream 8/22/06 - 9/18/06 [27 days] Slipstream picked up the one title he needed at Mayhem to become a Triple and Grand Slamer in the ESW..
Disgruntle (2) 9/18/06 - 10/30/06 [42 days] Disgruntle recaptured the ESW TV title when he beat Slipstream at WOI to become a two time champion..
Roddy O'Haire 10/30/06 - 12/4/06 [34 days] Roddy picked up his second ESW title in two weeks when he beat Disgruntle at Ultimate Mayhem .
Christian Pierce 12/24/06 - 2/12/07 [50 days] Pierce picked up his first gold in the ESW when he beat Drake for the vacant title at Merry Mayhem..
Ritilin 2/12/07 - 5/21/07 [98 days] Ritilin picked up the ESW TV title at Bloody Hell beating Pierce, making The Meds a team to watch out for.
Angelo Valour 5 /21/07 - 12/17/07 [210 days] Angelo picked up the TV title when he beat long time champion Ritilin for the TV title at Mayhem.
Double J Joe Jones 12/17/07 - 4/28/08 [133 days] Double J picked up his first ESW title ever when he beat Valour for the TV title at Merry Mayhem..
Brain Hunter 4/28/08 Brain Hunter picked up his first ever ESW Title when he beat Double J at Driven Supremacy. NYCW/ESW Survivor Championship! KID KRAZY 11/10/03 - 2/9/04 [130 days] Steve Sinclair appointed Kid Krazy the Survivor champion when the NYCW Tag Team titles were dropped in favor of the Survivor title on 11/10/03 Larcen Night 2/9/04 - 3/22/04 [42 days] Larcen won the title at Bloody Hell, after he was the last man standing in a steel chair steel cage slug fest of death on 2/9/04!
Wicked 3/22/04 - 5/31/04 [70 days] Wicked won the title at March Madness 3/22/04 in a three way match against Larcen Night and Big Chief Tough Eagle.
SunFeather 5/31/04 - 7/5/04 [66 days] SunFeather ended Wicked long run as the Survivor champion by beating him 5/31/04 at Driven Supremacy! She also became the first to hold the
TV and Survivor title in ESW history! BlackJack 7/5/04 - 9/20/04 [77 days] BlackJack took that first step towards climbing the ladder in the ESW by stepping up and winning the Survivor title at Violent Desires in a double title 2 pin fall match. He walked in empty handed but walked out a champion.
Slipstream 9/20 - 11-22 [63 days] BlackJack's long run as the Survivor champion (77 days) came to a end on Ultimate Mayhem 9/20/04 as Slipstream beat BlackJack to become the new Survivor champion.
Bartholomew Yarkfarce11/22/04 - 1/25/05 [64 days] Slipstream was defeated for the first time at the Great American Brawl when undefeated Bartholomew, who is lead and managed by the unlikely winner Zeb E Dee won the Survivor title.
Rayne1/25/05 - 2/28/2005 [32 days] Rayne won the Survivor title at Soul Survivor when she beat Bartholomew to not only win the title, but become ESW's 3rd Triple Crown Member.
Khaos2/28/05 - 3/21/28 [21 days] Khaos beat Rayne on Mayhem for the Survivor championship, with some "help" from Sick Nick. Exodus 3/21/28 - 4/4/05 [14 days] Exodus picked up his second ever title in the ESW when he beat Khaos at Mayhem for the Survivor title.
Wicked(2) 4/4/05 - 4/25/05 [21 days] Wicked won the Survivor title for the second time when he beat Exodus at Mayhem for the Survivor title.
Jada Kaine 4/25/05 - 5/30/05 [20 days] Jada beat two time champion Wicked at Broken Rulez to pick up her first ever title in the ESW.
Soul Reaver 5/30/05 - 7/1/2005 [31 days] Jada was stripped of the title due to injury and Blackout set up a match between Sick Nick and Soul Reaver for the newly vacated Survivor title.....Soul Reaver won and became the newest Triple Crown Champion.
Sick Nick 7/1/2005 - 9/5/05 [66 days] Sick Nick finally won some gold when he beat Jada and Soul Reaver at Driven Supremacy for the title..
Kid Krazy (2) 9/5/2005 - 9/26/05 [21 days] Kid Krazy returned to the ESW and won the Survivor title at Ultimate Mayhem by beating Sick Nick.
Soul Reaver (2) 9/26/2005 - 10/24/20054 [26 days] Soul Reaver became the first 3 time Survivor champion when he beat Kid Krazy at Ultimate Mayhem.
Hardcore Kid 10/24/20054 - 12/26/05 [93 days] the Hardcore Kid picked up his first title in the ESW when he beat Soul Reaver at Mayhem.. Darien Sunn 12/26/05 - 1-30-06 [35 days] Much like The hardcore Kid, Sunn in his second match picked up his first title in his ESW career at Merry Mayhem when he beat the Hardcore Kid..
Kid Krazy (3)1-30-06 - 2/13/06 [14 days] Kid Krazy picked up the Survivor title at Mayhem when he beat Darien Sunn.. Rayne (2) 2/13/06 - 8/7/06 [175 days] Rayne picked up her first title of 06 and first since holding the TV title almost ayear ago when she defeated Kid Krazy at Bloody Hell.
Ayatollah W. Van Gogh 8/7/06 - 10-23-06 [77 days] Ayatollah picked up his first ever title in the ESW by beating Rayne at Mayhem for the Survivor championship.
Roddy O'Haire 10/23/06 - 11/20/06 [28 days] Roddy picked up his first ESW title when he beat Het Dookskind at Suicide Mission after the title was vacated..
Benjamin Blayne 11/20/06 - 12/24/06 [34 days] Blayne picked up his first title in the ESW when he beat Roddy at Mayhem. Widowmaker 12/24/06 - 2/12/07 [50 days] It was a good Christmas for Widowmaker who picked up the Survivor title from Blayne at Merry Mayhem, Widowmaker also became a ember of the Triple Crown family.
Prozac 2/12/07- 3/26/07 [42 days] The Meds started to take over the ESW when Prozac beat Widowmaker for the Survivor title at Bloody Hell.
Dirk Bentley 3/26/07 - 5/7/07 [42 days] Dirk threw a wrench in the Meds plan for total domination when he beat Prozac at Ultimate Mayhem to become the newest Survivor champion.
Christian Pierce 5/7/07 - 5/21/07 [14 days] Pierce finally picked up the Survivor title when he beat Dirk Bentley at Mayhem. Slipstream (2) 5/21/07 - 9/29/07 [131 days] Slipstream beat Pierce for the Survivor title at Mayhem………. Christian Pierce (2) 9/29/07 - 10/29-07 [30 days] Pierce recaptured the ESW Survivor title at WOI 07 when he beat Slipstream for the title. Prozac (2) 10/29/07 - 11/19/07 [21 days] Prozac recaptured his Survivor title when he beat Pierce at Suicide Mission. Jimmy Grandel 11/19/07 12/17/07 [28 days] Jimmy Grandel picked up his first title in the ESW when he defeated Prozac at Boiling Point for the ESW Survivor title.
Prozac (3) 12/17/07 - 6/308 [169 days] Prozac recaptured his Survivor title for a 3rd time when he beat Grandel at Merry Mayhem. Rayne (3) 6/3/08 - 7/7/08 [34 days] Rayne ended Prozac‘s soon to be record run and picked up her 8th ESW title at Ultimate Mayhem.
Angelo Valour 7/7/08 Valour finally brought home the Survivor title when he defeated Rayne at Mayhem to pick up the one title he needed to become a Triple Crown Winner.
NYCW Tag Team Championship! Eddie and Kid Krazy After a small tournamanet, Eddie and Kid Krazy walked away with the tag team gold at Kick Ass Inc Future Four told on June 30th after winning a 4 way match.
Tag Titles were dropped The Tag Team titles were put on the shelve by Steve Sinclair after Kid Krazy and Eddie were no long tag team partners. Zander Thrash and Larcen Night The Tag Team titles were brought back as a yearly award like the "King of the Ring". In 2004 Zander and Larcen won the titles in a one night tournament at the Bruise Cruise 2 beating Wicked and Exodus in the finals.
Slipstream and Rayne The 2005 Tag Team Champions are Rayne and Slipstream who killed at Bruise Cruise 3. Kid Krazy (2) and Khaos Kid Krazy picked up yet another title in the ESW, and Khaos returned to the ESW to team up with Super Kid Krazy to capture some gold at Bruise Cruise 4.
Slipstream (2) and NC-17 7/2/07 In a PPPV that featured the stars from the BLPW, Slipstream became the second to become a two time Tag Team champion when he tagged up with BLPW‘s NC-17 to win the ESW Tag Team titles at Bruise Cruise 07.
ESW's Soul Survivor Griffin2005 Griffin ran the gauntlet in one night beating Hellface, Angel and Helena to win the first ever Soul Survivor Award.
Darien Sunn 2006 Darien Sunn beat Kid Krazy in the final to pick up 2006's Soul Survivor. Slipstream 2007 Slipstream went through everyone to claim the Soul Survivor of 07 beating Ritilin in the finals. Prozac 2008
Prozac won ESW‘s Soul Survivor when he beat Pierce in the finals to pick up the honor.
