ESW Legacy - There is only 2 guys in the history of the ESW to win everything there is to win and Prozac was the 2nd to do so. Elite says it all. Never count out Prozac. When you thought he couldn’t do something he would be the last man standing at the end of the night.

Wrestling Technique : Powerhouse, Hardcore, Shooter Weapon of Choice : anything Finisher : Cheer up Powerslam into a face first power bomb Bio Born in a small town Cornwall, NY to just his mother Rose Drakes, he never knew his dad or why his dad left months before he was born but he didn't care. His mom loved boxing and wrestling and had him watch it with her since he was still to young to know what it was. Since the 1st time that he remembers watching wrestling and boxing he loved it and started looking at all fighting types, he would make it a job to watch every wrestling, boxing, kickboxing, martial arts, any type of fighting there was. Besides for school he spent his time watching TV, playing games, and working out he knew he would do everything there was to become a pro wrestler since wrestling was his favorite out of all the fighting types. In school he would go to all the wrestling training's but he would never join the team he was pretty lazy as a child but he mainly just wanted to train on his own when done with his video games of course. Once he got out of school at eighteen he went to pro wrestling training camp where he was one of three of the top students there and trained there for only two years and one year worth of underground matches before he decided he was ready for the big time.
