ESW (Empire State, 2003) — Card

Show archive: October to December 2003

7 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NYCW / ESW Archive 2003: October - December Shows | Date | Location | Description Ultimate Mayhem | 13th Oct 2003 | TNT Arena Blackout threatens to walk, and Nanook couldn't be happier. Hatemonger squares off against Scythe, Kid Krazy takes on Steve Hawkins in his debut! Rayne faces Merritt, and the Main Event is Eddie and Nanook facing Steve Sinclair and Amos! Plus a major anncement about the NYCW World title!
Ultimate Mayhem | 22nd Oct 2003 | TNT Arena Steve Sinclair takes care of unfinished business with Soul
Reaver and settles the lawsuit out of court, well kind of...
Zander Thrash returns and is back with help as the HHFT team is formed and faces The Boss. Raven Sinclair steps away from the desk and faces Blackout himself. With the NYCW World title up for grabs, Hawkins and Hatemonger battle to be one of the
#1 contenders for it. Amos and Rayne battle for the other spot and the final spot is disputed between Krazy and Eddie! Ultimate Mayhem | 27th Oct 2003 | Amsterdam Mall We take a look back at the past 10 months that made up the NYCW in a small brief review of many things related to the NYCW as the NYCW takes a small break while we prepare for the Triple Crown in New York City. Some of the limit action
show was Zander Thrash vs Doc Holiday. Hatemonger vs Johnny Big Time, and Scythe vs Steve Hawkins. The Triple Crown: Rites Of Passage | Madison Square Garden | The CWA and BLPW and NYCW come together and slug it out to see who is the top dog between the three federations. This is NYCW's first ever pay per view and a must see!
Ultimate Mayhem | 3rd Nov 2003 | Glens Fall Civic Center Post Triple Crown celebration and back to business as Hatemonger sits down with Nanook to discuss why he won't fight
Scythe again. Riptide takes on Zander Thrash. Merritt faces
Hawkins, Amos defends his title against Scythe, and in the
Main Event, Rayne and Krazy with Bulldozer face off in a street fight!
Ultimate Mayhem | 10th Nov 2003 | The Blue Cross Arena Steve gloats about ridding the NYCW of Soul Reaver, Bulldozer makes his in ring debut against Rayne, Hatemonger gets a hold of Merritt's special herb and snaps, as they take on
Zander Thrash and Scythe in tag team action. Also, Merritt steals Mr. Snuggles. The Main Event crowns a new World champion as Eddie faces Hawkins and Amos.
Ultimate Mayhem | 17th Nov 2003 | The TNT Arena The new Survivor title is up for grabs and Krazy and Zander
"survive" the night long challenge for it. Rayne and Frank Merritt square off against each other and Mr. Snuggles comes home! Eddie takes on Hatemonger, and the World title is on the line as Hawkins defends it against Amos.
Kick Ass Inc: 7th Heaven | 24th Nov 2003 | The Pepsi Arena
One of the more lack luster Kick Ass Inc shows, Zeb E Dee,
Zander Thrash and Kid Krazy have their rematch to finally crown the undisputed Survivor champion through 3 different challenges. Scythe finally gets his hands on Hatemonger in what would be their final matches in the NYCW. Steve Hawkins defends the World title against Rayne, and the Main Event is Amos defending his TV title against Eddie.
Ultimate Mayhem | 8th Dec 2003 | The TNT Arena After the NYCW took a well needed break, Steve Sinclair calls out Eddie as both of them look to end their feud that's been 6 months in the making. Jin Tao makes her return and faces Frank Merritt, Kaleb James makes his debut and faces
Kid Krazy, and in the Main Event, the stipulation riddled tag team match between Eddie & Rayne, and Hawkins &
Amos with Steve Sinclair as the REF!! Ultimate Mayhem | 15th Dec 2003 | The TNT Arena Nanook returns to the booth as Killer Mike makes his debut against Zander Thrash and Zeb E Dee. Devo makes his in ring return to the NYCW and takes on newcomer Kaleb James. Amos sees some action against Jin Tao, and after we make fun of
Nanook and Wilburman with old IWA clips, Eddie is crushed as he faces Nanook, and the Main Event features Steve Hawkins defending his title once again against Kid Krazy.
Kick Ass Inc: 8 Is Enough | 29th Dec 2003 | The Pepsi Arena Ultimate Mayhem 29th Dec 2003 Mid Husdon Civic Center The Kid Krazy tribute show. Kid Krazy moves onto bigger and better things and takes on new comer Skorp in his debut as this is Krazy’s last show with the NYCW.
Jin Tao takes on Zander Thrash and the HHFT crew while Devo looks to avenge Draven Xaiver for last week’s accident. Rayne and Kaleb James collide while the Main Event features Steve Hawkins defending the NYCW World title against Eddie. Plus we learn what the ESW is all about from Steve Sinclair himself!
