Jack “Widowmaker” Rice Titles held: Triple Crown Winner 3 time ESW World Champion 2 time ESW Television Champion ESW Survivor Champion. Awards: Wrestler of the Month for November 05 Main Events: 18 (6 PPV) When Jack “Widowmaker” Rice joined the NYCW way back in 2003 he was far from a rookie. He was at that time one of the few established stars that Steve could get to join the NYCW to give the NYCW some credit as a legitimate federation. Jack quickly won the NYCW World title and is 1 of two stars in NYCW/ESW history to hold both the NYCW and ESW World titles. Jack went on to win the ESW World title 3 times, the TV title twice and one run as the Survivor champion crowning himself a Triple Crown Winner. In 2009 Jack Rice was elected to the ESW Hall of Fame. Nowadays Jack comes and goes as he pleases and always stirring up trouble when he is in town.
Height: 6'10" Weight: 335 Hometown: Detroit, Michigan Theme Music: Bodies by Drowning Pool Alignment: Face Years wrestling: Since 2002 Wrestling Style: Powerhouse/Submissionist Age: 30 + Nationality: American Finisher: The Widowmaker He lifts his opponent up into a Power bomb position then power bombs him. He picks him back up by the legs and power bombs him again.
He power bombs his opponent a total of 4 times before picking him up and flipping him over for a Death Valley Driver.
Secondary Finisher: Torture Rack Bio/Background: Jack Rice grew up the second to youngest of 8 kids. Ever since he was a kid his family had always been wrestling fans. At an early age he began running the streets. He was always a fighter no matter what the circumstance was he would as always find a reason to throw down. As he grew his older brothers got him into bodybuilding. But nothing seemed to calm his temper and it eventually cost him. When he was 18 he was convicted of manslaughter for killing a man during a barroom brawl. He spent four years in prison for that crime. During his prison stint he build his body up to a huge 385 pound frame he has today. His only other recreation was watching wrestling on
TV when he could. After his release he found out that three of his brothers had become professional wrestlers. Through them he met the man who would train him to wrestle and control his temper, Jeremy
"The Boss" Beltran. The Boss' martial arts background lent to giving Jack the discipline he needed to gain control over every aspect of his life. He learned not only the fundamentals of wrestling but became a fairly descent technical and submission wrestler, an oddity for someone his size. The Boss contacted a few of his friends in the wrestling business and found NYCW, a perfect place for Jack to pay his dues and learn the ropes of the profession.
Jack wants to one day be at the top of the profession but is ready to begin his true training here under the guidance of one of the industries great wrestling families.
