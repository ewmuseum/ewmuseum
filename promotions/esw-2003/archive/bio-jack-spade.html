Jack's father past away when he was young so Jack and Jake had to start doing most of the manual labor around the farm at a young age. Jack's grandmother moved in with them so she could help with the farm as well. Jack and Jacob were hugh fans of past time dare devil Eval Kneival. Many times they were scolded by their mother and grandmother for often mimicking his stunts. Especially when it came to jumping off the barn, one time Jake missed the hay stack and suffered a broken arm from their crazy antics. From there on Jack was a lone soldier when it came to his crazy ideas. They had to commute to school every day and times were tough when the bus couldn't make it through the snow to get to them so they had to walk. Jack's grandmother showed all her grand children how to play the guitar.
When they were ten, Jack's uncle took Jack and Jake to Calgary with him for a trip, He took him to Stampede wrestling where Jack first acquire his taste for wrestling. Jack was stunned and amazed as he watched his new idol Dynamite kid. When it was time for high school Jack had to commute even farther at attend. Jack was somewhat out of place because he was an out of towner. Though his welcome was warmed up when he struck a love interest with Alicia Paisley. They became instant close and at most times inseparable even though Alicia's parents disapproved of the relationship because they were more well off then Jack's family. When Jack graduated, Jack and Alicia moved to Toronto, Ontario, Canada where Jack attended a wrestling school there, While Alicia went to college. Jack worked hard and in a year Jack was working his own matches in a local Indy fed, that was the same starting grounds for Edge, Rhyno, and Christian.
Jack finally had a big break when Shannon Skanes signed him on a contract to compete in the GWO. Jack pushed on as hard as he could even secure his first major title, The GWO Hardcore Championship. Jack fell to the slick tongue of one Austin Fierro and was persuaded into joining The Dying Breed. Jack not liking his disposition often lashed out against Austin, In Jack's eyes Austin was holding him back. Jack had a great run as Hardcore Champion, defending against Slim Pickens, Gerard Angelo, Thunder, and Rod Gordon. Eventually Austin cost Jack his first ever title against Dizz. Jack left the Dying Breed and took a leave of absence for a short couple months to recover from that hardcore match who sustained him injuries. When Jack returned, Austin and his lackeys tucked tail and ran, before any sort of revenge would come into play. Jack competed aimlessly for a while and had a few close battles with Dizz for the GWO Television title.
Everything came crashing down though when a regular match up against Donna Matrix ended costing Jack a near career threatening injury. Jack had surgery on his knee and it swirled Jack in a depression which led Jack heavily into drinking and taking lots of pain killers. GWO closed its doors while Jack was on the shelf added to the stress in Jack's life that much worse. It caused a hugh riff between Jack and Alicia, Which ended up them breaking up. They had one last argument before Jack seen the most horrific site in all his life. Alicia clearly frustrated from Jack's position ignored the traffic, not knowing that a drunk driver was just around the corner. That horrific scream of Jack's lover before her passing, Haunts him to this very day. Therapy and great support from his family started to turn Jack around and when he noticed that Shannon Skanes had opened another fed in Anarchy Wrestling Jack knew the road was going to be hard and road was going to be long. He knew he had to step foot back in that wrestling ring, Not only for him but for Alicia. Jack started extensive training. Mixing it up with Muay Thai Kickboxing and going back to his roots in wrestling basics.
Jack returned to the ring in Anarchy Wrestling, Claiming the Monarchy Championship within a month of being there. He lost it shortly after to past time rival Gerard Angelo, But that didn't stop Jack though as he pushed on. He lost out to become CWA Alpha Champion as the deck was extremely stacked against him in an Octagon Gauntlet match. Even though Jack lost that match, He acquired something much better and that's a perfect relationship with Hoshi Yakamoto. A month after that Jack won a vacant AW Extreme Championship in a horrific seven man ladder match. Jack held that gold high and he was proud until yet again the doors folded on his face. Jack had to give up the title even though he never lost a match for it and he packed his bags. Jack took many bookings and wrestled in Mexico, Japan, and even Germany for numerous experiences.
When the CWA reopened, Jack was one of the firsts to sign a contract, Along with his still going strong at that point lover Hoshi Yakamoto. Jack had a new set in his mind and that was CWA World Heavyweight Championship. Jack battled month in and month out as he fought through a large grudge with Max Cameron. Jack battled emotionally through to an underestimated opinion by Frank Merritt. He took slander after slander from Tommy Purr until Jack finally rose to the occasion and captured the CWA World Heavyweight Championship at Battle of the Warriors. Jack lead an most impressive and dominant title run in recent history of CWA until finally foiled nearly half a year later by returning challenger Tommy Purr in grueling Iron Man Match. Tired of what Jack feels that the fans and management were casting him aside, Jack turned his back on them all. He let his relationships die and his ego take over as he captured his CWA Championship once more under shady circumstances. Jack’s new attitude has landed him many enemies and appointed him one of the most hated men in wrestling.
Jack’s contract expired while Jack was holding on the ESW Survivor Championship and when the two brands split. Jack signed two Open Contracts with CWA and ESW but the workload played out to be too much as he sustained injury after injury with no time to recover. So Jack stepped back out of the picture for recovery purposes for awhile. Jack enjoying his time off decided to go into “retirement” and to help out with the Toronto based wrestling academy with training. Jack making amends with his baby sister Bishop who is also wrestling decided to take a heavy interest in her progress. Some even say that he oversees her matches’ in house shows from the rafters hidden in dark hooded robe. Will Jack stay in retirement or will the itch to wrestle get to him? Who knows?
