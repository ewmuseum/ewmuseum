Panik Titles held: ESW World Champion Awards: November 07 WOTM Main Events : Zero Height: 6‘2” Weight: 233 Hometown: NYC Theme Music: Whatever happened to Rock and Roll by BRMC Alignment: Face Years wrestling: 16 years Wrestling Style:Highflyer Old School Hardcore Age: 33 8/19/73 Nationality:American Finisher:Panik Alarm Reverse Powerbomb Bio / Background: 20th of June 2002. The debut of Panik in the BLPW. October 2007, Panik reaches ESW. Of course. the story starts a long time before this. Raised in New York and the surrounding area, as a child he was passed between various members of extended family, never settling in one place too long. Whichever aunt or cousin had a spare bed for a few months or a little extra room.
His teenage years were spent changing from job to job, anything to make the next few dollars. One of which happened to be stage crew at a small time wrestling outfit. Setting up and training with the promotions' wrestlers resulted in dark matches, and the rest, as they say, is history.
Panik earned his stripes, taking the long way up from the bottom, moving from company to company, life on the road suiting the man with nowhere to call home. Some periods he cares to remember, however others are best forgotten. Either way he doesn’t often recount tales from his past. Learning his trade well, at each level stepping up his game to match those around him, he finally came to the attention of the XWA. Here he came to prominence, and a following, as well as plenty of enemies. He soon came to relises that alliances were nothing more than a danger, and kept himself from arms length from the rest of the locker room. As a competitor that feeds from the adoration of the fans, close friends turning their back on Panik had an equally dramatic affect.
At 33 years of age he is not the most stable character, however his demons are largely kept behind well locked doors, but now he not only feels he still has something to give, but that he has something left to prove.
