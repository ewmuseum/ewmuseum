Bio Greyson Blaze is the son of Shadow/Wildcard, a man known to many in the mainstay of wrestling for many years now. Having followed or rather chased after his wayward father for many years Greyson caught up with him in the CWA where he ventured to beat his father and retire him from the business for good. Greyson succeed in his attempt, nearly crippling himself in the process as he beat, retired and crippled his father in one brutal grudge match. After many months of rehabilitation Greyson returned to the ring under the management of his cousin RC Pride until he turned on everyone that trusted him in an attempt to better the standings of his generation of wrestlers… though his efforts have all been for naught he continues to wrestle if for no one else then himself as he tries to hold together his budding relationship with the Asian Sensation Hoshi Yakamoto… devoting himself to family before wrestling so as to not become a man like his father before him.
As things have died down from Greyson’s heel turn he has settled the score with Damien Shaw, Tommy Purr and several others at the same time; Greyson has stepped back from being the antagonist for the time being. But the hard fought and won tranquility was short lived as his relationship with Hoshi Yakamoto has come to an end leaving Greyson with a lot of free time. As a result of the hard work Greyson has put in to his in ring performance and matches he had earned a chance to fight for the Triple Crown Championship currently held by Shawn Sanders, by facing of against the former champion and a man Greyson despite appearances he has always respected inside the ring Gary Barber. But things did not go as he had hoped, instead of winning the title from Sanders Greyson fall short of the mark; while keeping the Triple Crown Title in mind he has turned his attention toward Gary Barber in an attempt to earn the man’s respect. But with the current state of mind and thinking of the once great champion will Greyson be able to do the impossible, or will he end up another victim of Gary Barber’s darker side Fantomas?
