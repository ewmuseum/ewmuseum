ESW (Empire State, 2003) — Card

Show archive: April to June 2003

7 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NYCW / ESW Archive 2003: April - June Shows | Date | Location | Description Kick Ass Inc: The 2nd Coming | 7th Apr 2003 | Pepsi Arena This is the show that started Kid Krazy's unbeaten streak at Kick Ass Inc shows as he beat The Doc. Jackal takes on new comer and tag team partner to Johnny Rotten, Terry O Neill.
Nanook steps up to the plate to fight Wilburman, Devo has the pleasure of ridding the NYCW of Cypher in the next contest before the casket match between Johnny Rotten and Soul Reaver. The
Main Event featured Rayne going against World champ Eddie for the title in a can't miss match. The night was capped off with a battle royal for the TV title that featured everyone and Larcen Night.
Ultimate Mayhem | 14th Apr 2003 | Manhattan Collage Dedicated to Michael Sandy jr. Terry O Neill & Johnny Rotten vs Doc Holiday & Ruin Kid Krazy vs Johnny Big Time Zeb E Dee vs Larcen Night Rayne & Devo vs Eddie & Soul Reaver Ultimate Mayhem | 21st Apr 2003 | TNT Arena Time for time Doc Holiday will talk about a beating he received before the show. A beating he blames Kid Krazy for. This is the night it happened. Also, Iceberg makes his NYCW debut as he faces Zeb E Dee. Robert J O'Reilly makes his debut as well and takes on Soul Reaver. Wilburman sets Eddie up with then arch rival Rayne as his tag team partner to take on Rotten and O Neill. Kid Krazy takes on Devo before Rayne defends her title against Triple J, "Juicy" Joe Jones, her own cousin! in the Main Event.
Ultimate Mayhem | 28th Apr 2003 | Buffalo Convention Center
Iceberg takes on Johnny Big Time as Zeb E de fights the law in Robert J O'Reilly. Horace Pinkerton makes his debut in a tag match with Devo as they take on Kid Krazy and Soul
Reaver. A special attraction match sees triple J Joe Jones taking on "the Masked one" who later is Jackal. while in the Main Event we see then friends Eddie and Steve Sinclair taking on Rayne and Raven as the pre lover's quarrel moves to the next level.
Ultimate Mayhem | 5th May 2003 | Mid Hudson Civic Center The "Night of Main Events" begins with Horance Pinkerton getting his feet wet against Zeb E Dee. Johnny Rotten takes Robert J O'Reilly as Kid Krazy and Larcen Night hook it up hardcore style. Devo defends his TV title against
Terry O Neill, a table match pits brother vs brother as Jackal tries to put Soul Reaver through a table. And the match that a 1000 pre teen boys spanked off to, the famous "Prom
Night Match" between Eddie and Rayne. Pink Elephant anyone? Ultimate Mayhem | 12th May 2003 | City Center Centerplex
Kid Krazy stops Ruin as Horace Pinkerton takes on Redeemer in his debut. Doc Holiday returns from his injuries to blame
Kid Krazy. Riptide makes his debut and takes on Terry O'Neill while Devo takes on Johnny Rotten. Jackal faces Eddie for a World title shot bid in a huge match and in the Main Event,
Soul Reaver gets a World title shot from Rayne.
Ultimate Mayhem | 19th May 2003 | Apollo Theater The huge build up for Kick Ass Inc the 3rd Strike. One of the bigger shows. We discover Rayne's secret before Johnny
Rotten kills Ruin. Vlad from the defunct CWA makes an appearance in the NYCW as Devo and Pinkerton takes on Riptide and then tag team partner Scythe. Before Jackal is shipped off to the
BLPW, he must face Zeb E Dee and after that Kid Krazy gets his hands on Rayne. In the Main Event, Eddie takes on Soul Reaver plus the meeting between Eddie and Wilburman, the contract signing.
Kick Ass Inc: The 3rd Strike | 26th May 2003 | Pepsi Arena This was a huge show as Iceberg and Robert J O'Reilly takes on Rotten and O Neill in tag team action. Jin Tao makes her debut in the NYCW and takes on The Silver Dragon who also is making his debut. Vlad takes on Ruin. Kid Krazy over came the odds and beat Horace Pinkerton in a brass knuckle match. Wilburman and Chamber relive old times in a re match of a rematch. Devo defends his TV title against Soul Reaver, and in the main event
we see Jack vs Eddie vs Rayne for the WORLD TITLE in a must see match. The ending will blow you away. Ultimate Mayhem | 2nd June 2003 | TNT Arena Steve is upset after Kick Ass Inc 3rd Strike, rewards Eddie and bashes Rayne. A theme that would last for a while. Kid Krazy takes on The Silver Dragon. Vlad takes on Devo and Rayne takes on Johnny Rotten. Former NYCW World champion Horace Pinkerton faces Soul Reaver. The newly crowned World champion Eddie takes on the one guy who he hates the most, Wilburman and in the end,
It's time to go crazy as Mad Cyril shows up to help his student?
Ultimate Mayhem | 9th June 2003 | TNT Arena A rare look at Eddie's 1974 Rolls begins the show and after that, Johnny Rotten takes on The Silver Dragon. The man, the myth JT Blackout steps in the ring to face Zeb E Dee while
Vlad takes on Horace Pinkerton. Mad Cyril address the NYCW and is soon joined by Eddie who reveals the biggest question known to man: "Who is JL Willis?" Returning to action, Eddie and Kid Krazy face Ruin and Nanook in tag team action and in the Main Event. Devo versus Rayne versus Soul Reaver.
Ultimate Mayhem | 16th June 2003 | TNT Arena We see what happened after Mayhem went off the air from last week. Scythe faces Johnny Rotten while their counter parts Riptide and Terry O'Neill square off against each other. dEUS returns to face Ruin who is on a mission. Kid Krazy faces Widowmaker in his return while Rayne faces Johnny Big Time. In tag team action, Devo and Pinkerton takes on Soul Reaver and Vlad in a quest for tag team gold and the show is wrapped up with Wilburman singing and dancing like a fool.
Ultimate Mayhem | 23rd June 2003 | Armory of Albany We learn that Steve Sinclair will face Soul Reaver next week at Kick Ass Inc as the show starts. Vlad is back in action and take son Kid Krazy. Steve tries to break up Eddie and Rayne before Widowmaker and Soul Reaver hook up. Eddie challenges
Mad Cyril to a retirement match while dEUS and Riptide square off against each other. Nanook and Scythe, the unlikely tag team faces The Silver Dragon and JT Blackout. The Main Event has Eddie and Rayne take son Devo and Pinkerton in a match that has a upset ending.
Kick Ass Inc: Future Four Told | 30th June 2003 | Pepsi Arena
Vlad takes on new comer Shyne. The Tag Team titles are up for grabs as Megan and Stacy, the "Champions" defend their titles against Devo and Pinkerton, Eddie and Kid Krazy, and Scythe and Riptide. Buried Alive Flaming Casket match is next with Soul Reaver taking on Steve Sinclair. A match for the ages followed with JT Blackout taking on Mad Cyril in the
Main Event.
