Trademark Taunts: Finisher and Move set Finisher : Beautiful Oblivion Spinning diving Powerbomb Signature Move: The Mighty Hoof of Pills low Blow kick Bio The short version for the public… Xain owns and operates Stallion Enterprises a flourishing sports merchandise and memorabilia company based in Chicago… he wrestled for a short period of time winning the North American Title of the XWA before returning to a life of seclusion and business… having a rather intense rival with a man by the name of Rob Stallion. He now comes back to the ring after nearly eight years to kill his boredom.
Background #2 : The not so public unknown version… Xain was sold into the life of a mercenary before he could even remember, growing up he was taught and raised to be nothing but a soldier of fortune, a mercenary and nothing else. While other children his age were in school learning math and history, Xain was field striping machine guns for his version of recess, by the time he was old enough to amount to something in the peaceful society, his life as a mercenary was far to engraved into his soul. Living from one war zone to the next until he found his way into the temporary employment of the man known as Shadow for a one time contract to wrestle in his stead. Some time later Xain returned to the ring having found something other then the cold nights of a war zone that felt remotely homelike.
Xain stepped foot into the XWA spending some months there establishing both a wrestling career and an honest job on the side. While feuding with the wrestler known as Rob “the Real Deal” Stallion Xain, had his first taste of gold, as well as taking time out of his hectic schedule to establish and merchandise and memorabilia company known as Stallion Enterprises which logo happened to be a golden colored stallion in full run. A company which he basically stole right out from in under the nose of the man who started it Rob Stallion his only rival inside the ring. After leaving the XWA Xain has remained in business with various promotions as well as expanding to foreign investments and promotion. Having found a bit of free time in his life after eight years he returns to the ring, in order to once again kill some of his free time and boredom.
Aside from his life as the CEO of Stallion Enterprises, Xain maintains a steady flow of mercenary work, contract jobs, as well as private sector contracts from government agencies to fill the long hours inbetween.
