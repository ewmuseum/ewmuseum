Fed history: In the Beginning

22 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In The Beginning... A few years (like 8) ago, I (Steve Sinclair/Chamber) began to get into Efedding shortly after I got onto the Internet. Oddly enough, it was my dad who sent me a link to an efed. Having no clue what an efed was or what it was even about, I followed a small fed ran by a bunch of kids who were out of school for the summer called the IDCW. After a few weeks of watching and learning and discovering what an efed was, I joined the IDCW with a character I called Morgue.
He was a seven foot monster (I was a huge Giant fan when the WCW was around) and he was okay. Two weeks into my "career"
I created Morgue's twin brother Chamber. The skill level of the fed was low and I quickly captured my first title. The IDCW
Hardcore title with Chamber, who was quickly becoming my favorite character. At the same time, as my girlfriend (now Wife) argued over who gets time on the Webtv she too was checking out the efed. Soon she joined the IDCW with a character who's name escapes my mind.
Well soon the IDCW closed. We moved on. I landed a spot in a brand new Efed called the BCFW. Playing just Chamber, (this time with a manager named Captain Howdy, from the movie Strange Lands, who is now [Howdy] in the ESW) I quickly picked a fight with a guy named
Jusin who played a character named X Cess. Long story short, the
BCFW lasted a couple of weeks. Internal fighting tore the fed apart. Jusin who was also new at Efedding decided to open his own fed. Gathering all of the troops from the BCFW Jusin opened up the RWF. The RWF was for the most part my first real fed I was in. My Wife (now playing a character named Cobalt who also is known as Raven Sinclair) and I joined the RWF and for the next 8-9 months we had a blast. Chamber and Cobalt won and lost titles but more importantly we gained friends from that fed, friends that even 7 years later we still get together and chat.
Well, like all feds on the net, the inevitable happened. The RWF fell pray to the dreaded IRL syndrome (In Real Life). We tried to keep the RWF alive and well. Rick took over the fed, then handed the fed over to me to which I ended up putting a slug in it. But from the RWF ashes, two feds grew. One was already alive and well, a fed called the IWA and the other that later grew from the demise of the RWF, the XWF.
It is with these two feds that the first roots of the ESW can be traced to. The IWA was a place that again Chrissie and I went to go play in. At one point we were in both the RWF and IWA. In the IWA we meet a guy that at first I thought was gay but ended up to be a real cool dude. His name was Will and his character was Wilburman (Co owner and Commish of the ESW), and Wilburman and Chamber did not see eye to eye. They had many great battles and at times, only fought each other to which other guys and gals of the fed made fun of.
Some of those guys were Mad Cyril and JT Blackout, both of whom are now a part of the ESW. The IWA did eventually close its doors and I went on to help Will run a second fed, a fed called the WWCW.
The prez of that fed also fell victim to the dreaded IRL syndrome but with help from Will and I the WWCW went on to live for quite a long time, even after Dizz (the Prez) came back to resume control of his fed. That was my first real taste of running a fed. A fed
I ran with help from Will for 3-4 months. I liked the satisfaction of putting out good cards and getting players involved and excited about being in the fed. It wasn't an ego trip from being in control as I didn't really have a character in the fed when I took over to keep it alive, it was the feeling of being able to create storylines, no matter how corny they are, and having people be able to have fun with them and get a few laughs from them. It was something that
I would miss for quite a while.
After the IWA and WWCW, I returned to the XWF, which now has become the XWA. Chrissie who took a break from efedding came back to join me in playing in the fed which is something that by now you see we did a lot of. The XWA went on to grow and be a premier fed for over 2 years, which in the world of efedding is like forever. When the XWA was born, a new wave or style of efedding was growing over the landscape of efedding. It was the dreaded "Hardcore"
theme feds. Efedding as a whole took a slight boom as feds were springing up by the thousands overnight. (Which I think was a direct result of the popularity of the WWF and WCW which were at all times high, and when the ECW hit the main stream as much as they could, efedding and the hardcore/Xtreme style took off as a result.) Efedding seem to have lost that family feeling. It was all about blood shed and carnage more than having fun and meeting new people.
The XWA closed as well. Like all feds, they all eventually close. But where one dies, one, sometimes two,begin. The life cycle continued here. The BLPW opened. I joined the BLPW after taking some time off from efedding (where I learned I would become a daddy and got married.) The BLPW run by Tom who was in the XWA and had just about every other face from the XWA in it which still to this day is alive and well gave me a place to play. The BLPW was fun, don't get me wrong, but with changes in my personal life (more importantly a new job which gives me tons of free time) I had that desire to start my own fed and run a fed that I can call my own. I didn't want to take over some one else's sinking ship.
A few old faces came back into the world of efedding. Namely Will. Will was yearning to get back into efedding and also shown interest in running a fed once again. Chrissie also was itching to get back into efedding as she took almost 2 years off. So in the fall of
2002, Will, Chrissie and I started to brain storm about opening our own fed. After a few weeks, we came up with a idea. That idea was to bring back our old retired characters (Cobalt, Wilburman and Chamber) and have them own a federation. One that was run in the old school way (pre Hardcore ways) and one that would focus its energy on making players better either in roleplaying skills of carrying a feud.
The NYCW was born. (Chrissie and I live in New York, hence why we named it the NYCW) In January we got our first app from a old friend who was returning to efedding, His name was Lee (Mad Cyril)
but now plays the younger quicker and more cockier Eddie. The NYCW has been alive for a year now. January 5th/6th marks our one year anniversary. The first year as been a struggle with getting and keeping a full roster for the most part. It's been fun and a blast but it's time to grow up as a fed. The old "regional" or "indie"
concept for an Efed has just about died out. Not willing to see this fed die, The NYCW has made a transformation and has conformed to what is now considered to be the norm.
The ESW continues the story that the NYCW started but on a bigger picture. We hope the ESW appeals to more players in the long run with the same fun and enjoyment of the NYCW. The NYCW is perhaps the last of an "age gone by" fed and it will be missed. We look forward to the future as the ESW grows and expands in hopes of becoming your favorite fed but at the same time not forgetting where we came from or what we have accomplished. We will still continue to hold our Triple Crown event that brings 3 feds together to have fun and win some bragging rights.
And so, in a nut shell, that's what made the ESW, some of its players and the three faces who help run the ESW.
I hope it wasn't a complete bore, and Thank you.
