ESW (Empire State, 2003) — Card

Show archive: January to March 2003 (earlier layout)

7 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NYCW / ESW Archive 2003: January - March Shows | Date | Location | Description Ultimate Mayhem | 6th Jan 2003 | Amsterdam Mall This is the show that started it all for the ESW/NYCW. Our premier show that started out with a free for all in the opening minutes of the show between JT Blackout, Donnie Karkass, dEUS and a little unknown rookie by the name of Eddie. From there
Doc Holiday battled Scott Zonka, the Z man! A unknown person by the name of Rayne took on Devo in a losing effort while the
Main Event was dEUS vs Donnie Karkass vs Eddie. Ultimate Mayhem | 13th Jan 2003 | TNT Arena The first show in the home of the NYCW for a year. The TNT
Arena is a history book to the NYCW and on this night the first chapter was written as The Preacher showed up and took on Doc
Holiday. Rayne won her first match in the NYCW by beating the
Z Man, Eddie took on Donnie Karkass, then Eddie got to know who and what the Hate Skwad was all about first hand. Then in the Main Event, Devo and dEUS battled it out till JT Blackout came out to light a fire under his ass.
Ultimate Mayhem | 20th Jan 2003 | Polish Community Center The "Road to the Gold" started on this night as the lone title of the NYCW was up for grabs in a tournament that sounded good on paper. The matches of the night were as the following:
JT Blackout vs Donnie Karkass. Jack Rice (aka The Widowmaker) faced dEUS. The preacher faced Rayne and we also get our first glimpse of Frank Merritt and the Main Event featured Eddie taking on Devo.
Ultimate Mayhem | 3rd Feb 2003 | Sports Arena Frank Merritt took on dEUS in the very first match in the famed "Road to the Gold". The Preacher took on Eddie
in first round match with Blackout vs Karkass, Leviathan vs Rayne, Nanook lost to Doc Holiday and our Main Event featured
Blackout and The Preacher slugging it out.
Ultimate Mayhem | 10th Feb 2003 | TNT Arena Nanook, err Mesmar steps into the booth with Howdy as this marked the voices of the NYCW for a long time, a voice that has been hard to replace. Leviathan took on the poster boy of the NYCW at the time Eddie only to get to know Frank Merritt after the match up. dEUS faced Donnie Karkass, Rayne and the
Preacher battled in the second week of the "Road to the
Gold" tournament. Hatred shows up to put out the Z Man's thunder. The Main Event was a no show by Blackout as he was suppose to face Frank Merritt. After Merritt was declared the winner, Jackal invades the NYCW and the aftershocks were felt for a long time afterwards.
Ultimate Mayhem | 29th Feb 2003 | TNT Arena The Preacher Memorial Show dedicated to Allynah... God rest her soul. Hatred vs JT Blackout The Doc vs Donnie Karkass Jackal vs Frank Merritt Rayne vs Eddie Kick Ass Inc!!! | 3rd March 2003 | Pepsi Arena The first of 8 begins here. This was the final leg of the "Road to the Gold" tour which featured Commissioner Wilburman forcing JT Blackout going through the gauntlet which ends with Blackout tasting defeat by the hands of Steve Sinclair.
Nanook showing how tough he isn't when he took on fellow color man Howdy in a match for the ages. Hatred who was near the end of his NYCW career squared off against Jackal, who squared off against Rayne who faced The Dock in a fatal four way. The
Main Event was for the NYCW World title and it pitted Frank
Merritt against Eddie. Ultimate Mayhem | 10th March 2003 | Glens Falls Civic Center
Step back in time and see a glimpse into the roots of the
NYCW/ESW with a classic match from the IWA. From there see Frank
Merritt make fun of The Doc. Kid Krazy begins his career at this show as he took on Rayne with a appearance from Father
Mike. Soul Reaver strolled into the NYCW on this night and took on Frank Merritt and our Main Event features the new NYCW World champion Eddie take on the Dock.
Ultimate Mayhem | 17th March 2003 | TNT Arena The long time feud between Kid Krazy and Doc Holiday began here. Zeb E Dee made his NYCW debut of sorts. Rayne took on
Soul Reaver in a nupset match. Blackout took on both Frank Merritt and The Dock in a handicapped match in what would be The Dock's last stand in the NYCW. The Main Event was Eddie taking on our beloved commissioner Wilburman.
Ultimate Mayhem | 24th March 2003 | The SUNY in Cobleskill Kid Krazy beat Zeb E Dee to begin the show. The match that featured the living dead things, Soul Reaver and Devo was stellar before Cypher showed up to spoil everyone's fun. Speaking of Cypher, who will always have a warm spot in our hearts made his debut and took on Donnie Karkass who was near the end of his NYCW career. Doc "The Doc" Holiday took on new comer Johnny Rotten before our Main Event of Rayne and Frank
Merritt.
Ultimate Mayhem | 31st March 2003 | The Armory of Albany
The Doc Holiday takes on Zebber while Kid Krazy battles Johnny
Rotten with his manager Father Mike of CWA fame. Cypher fights
Soul Reaver and the Main Event features the tag team of Eddie and Frank Merritt as they take on Rayne and Jackal.
