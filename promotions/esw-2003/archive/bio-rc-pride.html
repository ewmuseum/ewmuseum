Bio RC Pride makes her return to the wrestling world after a short hiatus to give birth to her daughter Natalya Ann born on June 3, 2010... Having left the company abruptly and what could only seem to have been on bad terms with management she now steps foot into the squared circle once again to reign supreme against the women she despises the Femme Fatales. Having no love loss for the women of the ESW she has returned with a bold statement of her intents to demoralize the classless women even further by taking back the her title and rightful place atop the ESW as the only Queen of the Ring… as the Tsarina of Wrestling.
