Soul Reaver Hall of Famer Titles held: ESW World Championship, ESW TV Championship ESW Survivor Championship ESW Triple Crown Winner Main Events: 19 (2 PPV) Height: 6'5" Weight: 280 Hometown: Death Valley Theme Music: I Stand Alone Alignment: Heel Years wrestling: 4th Wrestling Style: Brawler/Highflyer Age: 22 Nationality: unknown Finisher: Soul Survivor It's Brock Lesners F-5, but only done with the opponant on the top turnbukle and Soul Reaver stands on the second turnbuckle.
Secondary Finisher: Soul Seeker Mankinds Mandible Claw, but more of a grip around the heart area on the chest. The crippler cross face Bio/Background: Soul Reaver grew up in a small town in the desert. His father used to wrestle in the WWE along time ago when it was known as the WWF.
His father was friends with the Undertaker who also comes from Death
Valley. Undertaker would visit from time to time and train with
Soul Reaver's father. Soul Reaver would watch move after move as they trained. Undertaker took Soul Reaver under his wing as his own son shortly after Soul Reaver's father passed away. Vowing to get revenge against Vincent K. McMahon for the death of his father,
Soul Reaver begged Undertaker to train him and to get him in the
WWE. Undertaker turn Soul Reaver request down. Undertaker wanted no part in his revenge. So Soul Reaver set out on his own traveling from federation to federation until one day he gets his revenge on Vincent Kennedy McMahon.
