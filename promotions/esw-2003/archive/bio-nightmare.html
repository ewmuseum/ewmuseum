Nightmare Height: 5'10" Weight: 780 Hometown: Your darkest Dreams Theme Music: let it all out by Hoobastank Alignment: face Years wrestling: 3rd Titles held: POW World title. Wrestling Style:Submisionist Brawler Highflyer Age: 19 Years old. Nationality:American Finisher:The Darkest Dreams Opponent is in the turnbuckle with his back to Nightmare. Nightmare runs up and dropkicks the back of his head so it slams into the turnbuckle.
Bio / Background: Nightmare is a man who has nothing to live for. He ran away from an abusive father at the age of 18 and started wrestling. He left his mother and a fiancee behind. Comming home years later he discovered that his mother had been beaten to death by his father. Attempting to find his fiancee, he discovered that she had been killed by a drunk driver comming back from watching him wrestle. Now, wrestling is his only sanctuary.
