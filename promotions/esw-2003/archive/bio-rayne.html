ESW Legacy - When the ESW started, we didn't have many things going for us. We didn't have a huge star beyond JT Blackout that anyone would know. We had a hard time staying on TV, we didn't have a champion or a following of fans and sometimes we didn't even had a place to hold a show. But when Rayne arrived, all of that changed. Thanks to Rayne the ESW gained a huge cult like following that help drove us to the top. Her on again and off again relationship with former 3 time World champion Eddie was everything that that Soap stars dreamed off. She is the all time winningest Superstar in the history of the ESW. She has her own clothing line, lingerie line with Victoria Secrets and just posed for Playboy. To this day she is beloved by the fans and remains one of the biggest superstars even though she wrestles on a limited schedule.

Bio Rayne Pezzini was born January 29th 1979 the fourth of six children Her Mother Fiona was a teacher and her father Giovanni a engineer for the LIRR (Long Island Rail Road) Finding life in the suburbs boring Rayne would use her fathers free train pass and travel into the city. Being closer in age to her brothers Rayne grew up a tom boy and would frequently go with them to various sporting events held at MSG. It was at one wrestling event held there that Rayne felt something kindle inside her . She was taken in by the roar of the crowd , the thrill of the competition , the overall skill involved. Inspired by what she had seen the night before Rayne spoke to the wrestling coach at her high school begging to join the team. Not deterred after the coach laughed in her face Rayne was determined to prove him wrong. Already one of the schools best athletes Rayne tried out for the wrestling team and was allowed to join the team . Becoming one of the first girls in the state to do so, she even made it to all state. After graduating high school ,Rayne moved to upstate NY to attend Suny Albany it was there that she heard of a training camp in Vermont run by Ted Madigan who used to wrestle under the name Green man. But before she would have taken that trip to Vermont fate intervened when Rayne and some friends visited the Professional Wrestling Hall of Fame in Schenectady and a chance encounter led her to the Sinclairs.
