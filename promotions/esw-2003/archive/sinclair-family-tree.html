ESW (Empire State, 2003) — Roster

The Sinclair family tree

11 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cead Mile Failte to The Wrestling SINCLAIR'S HOME PAGE Steve and Raven come from a proud family who has made a living inside the ring, starting with their great grandfather. While the earlier days of wrestling wasn't anything like we know it today, the same passion and skill runs through their blood. With more than 100 hundred years of wrestling in their family, Raven and Steve always remember to the past to push into the future.
Steve Sinclair: Born 1/8/1971. Steve wrestled under the name Chamber for five plus years. He married Julie Ann Steele on 8/18/01 and has one child, Steve jr. born 3/13/03
Raven Sinclair: Born: 10/31/1974 Raven who has a different mother than Steve wrestled for years under the name Cobalt. She's been married twice with no kids of her own. Her first marriage was to Neil "Draven" Wolffe (born 10/31/72) on 5/26/1996 (legally separated on 10/13/1997). Raven meet Neil in a small wrestling promotion and the honey moon was short lived.
Raven a couple of years later and after a injury forced her from the ring meet and married Marc Cross who, what else was also a fellow wrestler.
K.C. Sinclair: Born 1/8/1971 Steve's twin brother who works as ESW's trainer. He has spent the last few years going to school to do the best job he could. Before KC was a trainer, he also wrestled under the name Crash and spent most of his career as Steve's tag team partner but did have some success in single's comp. (RWF IC Title) KC did run a small indie fed in Vermont with their uncle Ted that was short lived but did help to produce ESW star Ruin.
Ariana Sinclair Born: 10/31/1974 Like Steve, Raven also has a twin sister and before motherhood called, Ariana dabbled in wrestling too. Under the name "Cerriwen" she rose to stardom in the late 90's only to disappear just as fast. Ariana married Conner McDermott 8/1/1994 (born 3/19/1970) who wasn't in the wrestling business. Currently Ariana is at home raising her two kid Aisling (born 12/1/1994) and Jack (born 7/23/1996)
Michael Sinclair Born: 4/14/1976 Raven's little brother and Steve's step brother and the last child of Jack Sinclair, their father. Michael also followed in the footsteps of his brother and sisters and currently still wrestles under the name "Morgue" in Japan where he is a huge star. He's still single and has no kids.
Mon and Dad Jack "Slaughter" Sinclair. Born: 1/13/44 died 2/24/1977 Jack wrestled under his name actually with the nickname Slaughter given to him by the fans. Jack wrestled all over the world and as result had a wife on both sides of the pond. His first wife Maggie (Born 2/9/1954. died: 2/24/1977) he married twice, 6/1/70 and 6/29/76, the second after cleaning himself up from alcohol abuse. As you can tell the second marriage was short lived as both died in a car accident. Maggie's twin boys Steve and KC would end up growing up with her brother Ted.
Jack's second wife on the other side of the pond is Heather Buchancan (Born: 9/9/1955) She married Jack 1/14/1973 and divorced 4/14/1976. While Heather did go on to remarry (Brandon McCloud on 6/24/1982) she remains close to her two daughters and son.
The uncle and his sons Ted Madigan born: 5/17/1955 To Steve and KC, Ted is dad. Like his brother in law, Ted wrestled for years under the name "Green Man" and when it was all said and done he saw in ring action for 24 years finally retiring in 2003. Jack meet Maggie through Ted when they worked for the same company in the early 70's While Ted has never been married he does have two sons of his own.
Joe "Double J Joe Jones" Madigan born March 14th 1980. Better known today as Double J Joe Jones, Joe was born in Berlin Germany and for him he was on the wrong side of the wall. Little is known about his mother who disowned him when Joe was in his teens and when the wall came down, Joe fled to live his father. Joe followed in his father's footsteps as well and wrestled and now manages and trains.
Mike "Disgruntle" Madigan Born: 12/10/1978 Joe's older brother, Mike was the black sheep of the family always in trouble with the law and spent time in jail untill Steve gave Mike a job in ESw's gym, where he meet Sick Nick and trained as a wrestler to enter the ring as Disgruntle.
Ted and Maggie's parents Orion Madigan born: 8/1/1930 died: 4/27/1996 Shanely Brennan born: 12/14/1932 They married 8/30/1943. Today Ted helps take care of his mother in Vermont. Grandpa Conner Sinclair born 7/21/1914 died 4/4/1996 Conner followed in his father's footsteps and ventured into the wide wonderful world of wrestling. Wrestling under his own name, he wrestled for over 20 years and did teach his son Jack how to grabble in the ring. Considered out of date in the mid 40's Conner called it quits and stayed at him with his wife Rhiannon Shea (born: 4/17/1913 died: 10/23/1989) till he died at home in 96
Great Grandpa William Sinclair born: 2/29/1891 died: 8/5/1966 William is the crazy bastard who started this own mess. He wrestled in a time where wrestling was treated like boxing. It was one match that last for hours and William was said to have a iron skull due to the hits he would take. He wrestled for many a year and did find time to get married too. William married Aileen Murdachan (born: 6/4/1895 died: 8/6/1954) on 6/24/1910 and had a only son, Conner.
