ESW (Empire State, 2003) — Card

Show archive: July to September 2003

7 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NYCW / ESW Archive 2003: July - September Shows | Date | Location | Description Ultimate Mayhem | 7th July 2003 | TNT Arena The dark days for the NYCW started here. We signed with the NeWA, which was a mistake. Shyne takes on Johnny Big Time, Zeb E Dee takes on Scythe,
Shigetoshi Hasegawa makes his debut against Devo, Doc Holiday takes on Captain America in his debut and we get our first look at Jeffery Hatemonger. Horace versus Widowmaker, Soul
Reaver versus Rayne, and the Main event is Mad Cyril versus
Eddie.
Ultimate Mayhem | 14th July 2003 | Civic Center Centralplex
Reported by Hot Bod Todd. Frank Merritt is a free man! Shyne vs Hatemonger Captain America vs Sliver Dragon Devo vs Widowmaker retirement match Eddie vs Horace with guest ref Rayne. Ultimate Mayhem | 21st July 2003 | TNT Arena NANOOK BIRTHDAY SPECIAL!!! Zander Thrash's debut and he takes on dEUS and Shigetoshi Hasegawa. Captain America vs Jeffery Hatemonger Frank Merritt vs Rayne Horace Pinkerton vs Kid Krazy Widowmaker vs Soul Reaver - Steel Cage match ...and the lap dance Nanook never got. Ultimate Mayhem | 28th July 2003 | Glens Falls Civic Center Riptide vs Kid Krazy, Zander Thrash takes on new comer Alex Martinez in his debut, JT Blackout takes on Dr. Fritz Van Bahls in his debut, Rayne takes on Captain America as he gets ready for his NeWA Cruiserweight title shot. NeWA tag Team titles are on the line as Hatemonger and Merritt faces the Demented
Ones. The Main Event is a three way dance for the World title.
Pinkerton vs Soul Reaver vs Widowmaker. Ultimate Mayhem | 4th Aug 2003 | Buffalo Convention Center
Pugnacious makes his debut against Doc Holiday. Zander
Thrash takes on the Dr. Scythe hooks up with Kid Krazy as
Alex Martinez and Frank Merritt battle it out. Blackout takes on Pinkerton, Soul Reaver picks apart Riptide and Johnny Big
Time. Rayne defends the TV title against Jeffery Hatemonger and the Main Event features Widowmaker versus Captain America.
Kick Ass Inc: 5th Element | 11th Aug 2003 | BLPW Arena, Philadelphia
We invade our sister fed for Frank Merritt to take on Zander
Thrash, Alex Martinez takes on Scythe, Nanook tries to get rid of JL Willis, Riptide versus Soul Reaver. Jeffery Hatemonger takes on Pugnacious and Doc Holiday tries to avenge Kid Krazy.
Johnny Big Time gets a TV title shot from Rayne while Pulse defends his NeWA Cruiserweight title against The Boss, formerly known as Captain America.
Ultimate Mayhem | 18th Aug 2003 | Mid Hudson Civic Center
Let the lawsuits begin! Soul Reaver serves paper to Steve
Sinclair in his sexual harassment suit. While we learn more about that, Shigetoshi Hasegawa takes on Kid Krazy, Pugnacious versus Frank Merritt, Mister "I'm suing you"
Soul Reaver takes on Alex Martinez. Samuel Amos makes his debut against Horace Pinkerton, and JT Blackout gets his NYCW World title shot against Widowmaker.
Ultimate Mayhem | 25th Aug 2003 | The Apollo Theater The new champ defends his title. JT Blackout defends it against
Kid Krazy in the main event. Pugnacious takes on Samuel Amos,
Scythe takes on Jack Daniels in his debut while Rayne defends her TV title against Alex Martinez
BRUISE CRUISE! | 1st Sep 2003 | On the Hudson River aboard the SS Bloodbath
Our first annual cruise of the Hudson River. It's a show of returns as Jackal returns to face Kid Krazy. Back from
Cancer, Eddie returns in a huge surprise and rocks the boat with some heavy news. Hatemonger returns from his trip in Canada where he and Merritt lost the NeWA Tag Team titles to face Widowmaker.
Plus we see Rayne's little trip to the BLPW and her reward for it while Big Shot Jack Daniels takes on Samuel Amos, Rayne defends her TV title once again in a rematch against Alex Martinez and the Main Event features JT Blackout defending his title against
Pugnacious. Ultimate Mayhem | 8th Sep 2003 | The Allendale Civic Center Keeping to his word, Blackout shows why he is the hard working champion that he is by defending his title against Riptide in the Semi Main Event. Amos steps up to the plate and takes home the TV title from Rayne in the Main Event, Hatemonger versus
Jack Daniels, Eddie makes his in ring return against Frank Merritt,
"Double K" Kid Krazy takes on a fading Alex Martinez and Widowmaker takes on Pugnacious.
Ultimate Mayhem | 15th Sep 2003 | The TNT Arena We'll learn just how Rayne feels about Eddie and why he disappeared like he did without telling her as she sat down with Nanook for a one on one interview. Coming to the end of their careers, Pugnacious and Jack Daniels square off against each other. Zebber takes on Doc Holiday in a rare appearance,
The Boss returns for a while and takes on Amos, and in a huge surprise, Kid Krazy and Eddie defend their tag team titles against
Merritt and Riptide with a shocking ending.
Ultimate Mayhem | 22nd Sep 2003 | The Blue Cross Arena The Boss and Hatemonger square off a week away from Kick Ass
Inc 6. In a non title match, Amos takes on Merritt, Nanook makes
Blackout run the Gaulet and ends up losing his job, and in the
Main Event, "Down on his luck" Eddie and Zeb E Dee face his former tag team partner and girlfriend, Kid Krazy and
Rayne.
Kick Ass Inc: Sixth Sense | 26th Sep 2003 | The Pepsi Arena Pissed off because of what Steve did the week before, Rayne vents while beating on Nanook. It's father-in-law versus son-in-law as Lee Cyril steps into the ring to defend his daughter's honor against boyfriend/husband/father-to-her-unborn-child Jeffery
Hatemonger. The brothers end their fight as Scythe faces Riptide.
Blackout defends his World title against unlikely opponent Zeb
E Dee! Amos defends his TV title against Merritt, and the Main
Event features Eddie and Kid Krazy in the dreaded Hell in a
Cell! Back to Archive Index
