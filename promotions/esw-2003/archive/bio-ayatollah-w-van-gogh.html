Suburban Ayatollah W. Van-Gogh Titles held:ESW Survivor champion. Awards: Wrestler of the Month for October 06. Height: 6'5" Weight: 246 Hometown: : Sante fe New Mexico Theme Music: Death Knell for Paper Children by Soiled Doves Alignment: Tweener Years wrestling: Vetern Wrestling Style:highflyer Martial Artist Powerhouse Age: 24/p> Nationality:American Finisher:Dancing on the Corpses Ashes Swinging Chokeslam Bio / Background: The Suburban Ayatollah was born >Wesley Gomez to an African-American father and a Native American >mother. At a young age, Wesley became versed in the lessons of loss. >His family had to move around a lot, his father always trying to >find a suitable job to support his family. Because of his heritage >and the fact that Wes never stayed in one place for too long, he had >trouble developing socially and he became an outcast at every new >school that he joined. Because of this, Wesley became developed his >own little world away from other children. His passions were >wrestling and art. He was often picked on, so he began earning money >doing odd jobs until he could pay for martial arts classes. Because >he moved around a lot, he learned fighting from several different >teachers, helping him to develop his own hybrid style. Upon turning >18, Wesley went to Mexico to purue his passion: Luche-Libre >wrestling. In Mexico, Wesley was trained under the famous Villano >family. For matches he wore a mask and went by the name The Great >Allejandro. One day, Wesley attended a travelling carnival were he >met a magician named Cecil Bermuda. After that day he left his >wrestling organization and began working in the underground mob led >by Bermuda, alongside Mr. Shiver.
