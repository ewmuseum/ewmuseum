NAWF- Seeking Wrestlers

10 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The North American Wrestling Federation is searching for wrestlers. Get a chance at the NAWF Heavyweight Title by joining before June 29th. Here is some informations about the NAWF 1- Wrestlers You roleplay your wrestler. Create one and guide him to his goals that you set. In the next weeks, we will have better ways for you to roleplay your wrestler. 2- Cards There are two cards per week, one on monday and one on friday. Also during the month, there is a pay-per-view, where the the President select the top contender for the NAWF Heavyweight Title to face the cahmpion. It is the only match you don't have the liberty to choose. 3- Name My friend made me remark that the name North American Wrestling Federation name might look like it is for only North Americans. It is not, everybody is welcome to participate. 4- Program We use TNM v6.2. We should have in July TNM v6.2 plus. The league can be gound at: http://members.tripod.com/~shadowfighter/index.html If you have any questions, e-mail me at [detail withheld] [name withheld] President of the NAWF
