Be Afraid...

Nov 25, 1996, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We open in the deserted WCWF arena, left empty since 1995, when it was shut down due to lack of time and interest. Majority owner Ted Dibiase is standing in the ring, the master of all he surveys.] Ted: You know, when I bought this two-bit federation a couple of years ago, I never thought it would come to this. I thought it would give me back the respect I deserve. The respect I *earned.* Well, I'm bringing it back, and I don't what the stinking DEWF says, I'll destroy them by building up my *own* federation! [The newly renovated WCWF logo begins descending from the ceiling. We see flunky/bodyguard Virgil straining with the rope while lowering the two-ton sign.] Ted: Slower, Virgil. We're going for dramatic effect here! Virgil: Sorry, boss... [The sign goes slower.] Ted: That's better. I don't care if I *don't* have any decent talent to worth with, or if I'm losing millions just standing here, or if I have become the butt of every joke in wrestling. HOLD IT THERE, VIRGIL! Virgil: Yes, boss.... [Virgil strains to hold the sign while the rope is obviously fraying.] Ted: I'm the Million Dollar Man! If I can't buy the DEWF, I'll take it down one man at a time! I can't be stopped! [The rope breaks, sending the sign crashing to the ground. It hits the ring apron on the way down, springboarding Ted Dibiase across the arena, and slam-dunking him in the basketball hoop set up on the other side of the building.] Ted: This is a minor setback... THE WCWF: BECAUSE WHEN YOU'RE THE WORST, THE ONLY DIRECTION LEFT TO GO IS UP! We're back. Consider yourself warned. -- First name [name withheld]. Don't make me say it again. The only person on RSPW *without* a Steve Austin quote in his .sig! Net.cop homepage: [detail withheld] Ben Reilly and Hal Jordan dead...Sensor Girl's a snake...*sigh*...
