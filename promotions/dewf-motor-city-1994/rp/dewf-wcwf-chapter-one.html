Chapter One.

Mar 7, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Note: Apologies to Robert Rodriguez for the opening...] Prelude. [We open at the Top Turnbuckle bar in downtown Philadelphia. Sandman is slugging back a few cold ones with 911 and discussing whatever things wrestlers discuss when they hang out.] [Suddenly, in walks Stevie Richards, who proceeds to walk nervously over to the bar and order a beer. He tries striking up a conversation with the wrestlers.] Steve: So, you hear about the new WCWF starting this week? 911: [Grunts a yes.] Sandman: What a joke. Steve: Yeah, well you wouldn't believe what happened to me when I went over there to check out the circus myself. I'm standing around, shooting the breeze with some jobbers -- real lowlife bastards, not upstanding gentlemen like yourselves -- when suddenly the door to the place goes flying off its hinges! [911 and Sandman continue drinking, not really paying attention.] Steve: In walks the biggest son of a bitch I've ever seen! He's gotta be 7 feet tall if he's an inch, which arms the size of tree trunks! And it doesn't look like he's on the juice, either. It just looks like it got that way from kicking people's asses. Sandman: [Pats gut.] Yep, this is all muscle from the same exercise program. [Sandman and 911 laugh.] Steve: But here's the kicker...this dude is spooky. I mean, he's dressed completely in black for one thing. I mean from head to foot, every inch of this guy is covered in black. He's even got a black overcoat and a big-ass black brimmed hat to cover his face. And the shadows seemed to follow this guy around as he walked, so you could never quite make out his face. [Now Sandman and 911 really start to look scared.] 911: He got a name? Steve: No. He just said "I'm back" and then left again. Damn near pissed myself, he was so scary! [Sandman and 911 look at each other, real terror building now.] Sandman: Oh, my god. He was at that DEWF card the other night...that was him! Oh, shit... 911: I sure as hell wouldn't want to be in that WCWF outfit right about now. [Steve takes a swig of the beer, spits it out.] Steve: Oh, man, how can you guys drink this? Well, I should be going now. See you later. [He gets up and leaves. We see him exiting the bar, where a mysterious figure waits for him.] Figure: You think they bought it? Steve: Oh, man, they were shitting bricks, thinking that Stranger guy was back! It was classic. Uh, he's not *really* back, is he? [The figure comes into the light, which oddly enough only serves to accentuate the shadows around his face even more.] Stranger: I don't know. You tell me. [We hear him laughing to himself as Richards runs off in terror...] Chapter 1. [The screen is filled with static with a little "WCWF" logo in the bottom corner. We hear yelling faintly in the background.] Voices: Dammit, we're live! Turn the camera on right now! [Suddenly, the picture switches on and we see Toad Pedophile standing there, looking rather embarrassed.] Toad: Ahem. Good evening, and welcome back to the WCWF. We've been away for a while now, but we're back and, we feel, better than ever. We're broadcasting from WCWF Studios in Hackensack, New Jersey. It's quite a bit smaller than you might be used to seeing, more along the lines of an abondoned Safeway grocery store, actually, but we feel the ambiance will increase the intensity of the action. Let's, uh, take a tour around WCWF Studios and see who's hanging around. [We see the camera clumsily panning around as Toad nearly trips over his own microphone cord. We journey from the backroom out to the ringside area, where Hulk Hogan and Brutus Beefcake are just kind of hanging out.] Toad: We're here with Hulk Hogan, who is the first big star to sign on with the WCWF, and his good friend Brutus Beefcake. Hulk, what brings you to the WCWF. Hulk: Money. Toad: Anything else? Hulk: Nope. That's about it, brother. Guaranteed lifetime contract. That's what it's all about. Toad: What about you, Brutus? Brutus: Where Hulk goes, I go. Toad: That kind of friendship is inspiring. Brutus: Besides, I was living in a cardboard box in the alley outside and Mr. Dibiase said I was scaring off the clientele. Toad: Ah. I see. Let's more on, shall we? [The camera moves to the back again, up to a door with Ted Dibiase's name engraved on a gold plate.] Toad: It's the office of the owner of the WCWF himself, folks. Let's see what kind of deals he's hammering out, shall we? [The door squeaks open, and we see Ted interviewing Paul Roma and Jim Powers about jobs.] Ted: So, what have you been up to since the CWF folded? Roma: Well, until recently I was playing a racial stereotype in a pizza commercial. Ted: Oh, the greasy italian? Roma: No...the nagging Jewish housewife. [Ted chuckles at the joke. Roma looks confused.] Roma: What's so funny? Ted: Uh...right. And what have you been doing, Jimmy? Powers: Doing? Are you insinuating that I'm on steroids? Ted: No... Powers: Because I'm not, you know. This is 100% natural. [Looks around quickly for police.] Powers: I can sell you some, if you like. Ted: Excuse me? Powers: Steroids. Got a whole bag of them in the dressing rooms. I'll give 'em to you cheap. [Looks around again, seems worried. Begins whispering.] Powers: [Whispering] I think we're bugged. [Yelling] WHY, NO, MR. DIBIASE, I DON'T DO STEROIDS AND I DON'T KNOW ANYONE WHO DOES! [Winks at Dibiase.] Ted: [To Roma] He always like this? Roma: Only when he's not on cocaine. [Rim shot.] [Ted falls forward and starts crying into his desk.] Toad: Looks like we caught him at a bad time. We'll come back later. Oh! My director is signalling that we actually have a match ready to go! Let's head to ringside for the opening bout in our card! "Ladies and gentlemen, our first match of the evening is scheduled for one fall! "In the ring to my left, from Memphis, Tennessee, please welcome back to the WCWF...the Honky Tonk Man!" [We see HTM wiggling his rather large rump around the ring.] "And his opponent, from Three Mile Island...ADAM BOMB!" [Virgil leads Bomb down to ringside by holding his Power Ranger doll out in front of him until enticing him into the ring.] Even though only possessing the intellect of an 8 year old, Bomb is still at heart a wrestler, and when Honky tries a sneak attack, Bomb reacts quickly, spinning around and firing away with lefts and rights that stagger HTM enough for Bomb to hit a clothesline that nearly rips his head off. He seems confused as to what to do next, so Virgil begins yelling instructions at him. Bomb is now more confused, so he leans out of the ropes to consult with Virgil, and Honky takes the opportunity to knee him in the back, stunning him. He then cinches in the Shake Rattle N Roll neckbreaker and gets the easy three count. Virgil is not pleased. "The winner, the Honky Tonk Man!" The 150-odd fans in attendance clap politely. [We cut back to Toad, reading his monthly issue of "Old Enough" magazine and, um, enjoying himself.] Toad: Oh, yeah, baby, that's it...OH! We're back. DAMNIT, cut to another match. And give me back my magazine... "Ladies and gentlemen, our next contest is scheduled for one fall." "In the ring to my left, please welcome Paul Roma and Jim Powers, the Young Stallions!" The crowd gives the eternal underdogs a smattering of applause. "And their opponents...from Allentown, Pennsylvania, here are Saggs and Knobbs, the Nasty Boys!" The crowd sort of pops for the Nasties, but not really. The Nasties charge into the ring and begin a four-way brawl with the Stallions. Virgil is at ringside applauding the Nasties. However, Roma and Powers quickly the better of the brawlers, with Roma press-slamming Sags out of the ring, and Powers whipping Knobbs off the ropes and delivering a crushing powerslam. As Knobbs gets to his feet, Roma dropkicks him from the front while Powers dropkicks him from behind. Knobbs collapses face-first and rolls out of the ring for advice from de facto manager Virgil, but the pow-wow is quickly broken up as Roma runs off the ropes and leaps over the top rope, hitting a plancha dive and taking out all three. Now the crowd is behind them. Saggs tries rolling into the ring, but Powers dives in with a flying elbow and rolls him over for a two-count. Saggs rolls out again, and Roma whips Powers off the ropes. Powers then uses the momentum to nail a baseball slide on Saggs. Saggs crashes back-first into the railing, and Powers follows him out to continue the punishment. Back in the ring, Knobbs sneaks up from behind Roma and knees him in the back, sending him crashing to the mat. Knobbs begins dropping elbows on the back of Roma as Saggs and Powers are ushered to their corners by the referee. Knobbs picks up Roma and slams him to the mat, then tags in his partner. Saggs struts into the ring, only to have Roma suddenly kip up and superkick him in the face! Roma tags Powers and slingshots him in with a flying clothesline, which sends Saggs spiralling into the air and down on his head. To add insult to injury, Powers calls for a Pit-stop from Roma! Roma exposes his armpit, and Powers drags Saggs over to the corner and rubs his face in Roma's armpit! The crowd is definitely on the side of the Stallions now, and the Nasties are looking terrible. Roma and Powers control the tempo for the next five minutes, keeping Saggs in their corner and making quick tags, until finally Saggs gets a thumb in the eye of Roma. However, going for the showboat method, he decides to call for a retaliatory Pit-stop. He tags Knobbs, who presents the offending armpit. Roma is far from done, however, and nails a quick belly-to-back suplex on Saggs while Knobbs is mugging for the crowd. When Knobbs turns around, with one arm behind his head, Roma snags the arm with one hand and cinches the neck with the other, and Taz-plexes Knobbs into the ring! Going for the kill now, Roma tags in Powers, and both men climb to the same turnbuckle. Powers then climbs onto the shoulders of Roma in an incredible move, and swandives onto Knobbs, nearly hitting the ringlights in the process! He only gets two before a nearly-dead Saggs breaks up the pin, but Roma is still on the top rope, and comes off with a killer clothesline to incapacitate Saggs while Powers covers again, this time for an easy three-count. The crowd is impressed. "Here are your winners, the Stallions!" Virgil pulls his beaten troops out of the ring, to be dealt with later. Back to Toad Pedophile in the locker room. [Toad is standing with Ted Dibiase] Toad: This is not going well for your guys, Ted. Adam Bomb got beaten by the Honky Tonk Man, the Nasty Boys got destroyed by the Stallions, and Crush got assaulted and battered by the Sandman in the first ever WCWF-DEWF match a few weeks ago! Any thoughts? Ted: The matter of the Nasty Boys will be addressed very shortly, and I feel that solution will extend to fixing Crush's problems as well. Toad: Very enigmatic. A new stable member? Ted: You'll see in about three seconds. [Suddenly, the door to the outside is literally ripped off it's hinges.] Toad: Oh, my god! It's... Ted: ...the answer to my little problems. Welcome back, boys. [Two imposing figures stand in the doorway, with spikes and leather clearly visible.] Figure #1: So, who's ass we kickin' today? Ted: I need you guys to have a little chat with the Nasty Boys. Show them the error of their ways. And I believe a friend of yours is here as well. Figure #2: We don't have friends. Ted: Oh, I think you do. He's been getting a little weird lately, gone Amish on us. I think you can persuade him otherwise. Figure #1: You mean Crush? He's gone Amish? [Laughs evilly] Give him five minutes with us. He won't even believe in God anymore. Ted: And what about the Nasty Boys? Figure #2: Oh, I think we can handle them. What do you think, partner? Beating? Lynching? Whipping? Figure #1: Let's just kick their stinkin' teeth in. [A well timed lightning bolt strikes down behind them outside, and we see that indeed, Demolition is here and loaded for bear.] Ted: I knew you'd see it my way. Shut off that camera, show's over. [Static replaces the picture, as the credits roll.] [As the credits end, we hear the cameramen talking.] Camera 1: Let's go have a smoke. Camera 2: Is this thing off? Camera 1: Who cares? It's not our money. [They both laugh at this little joke. Suddenly, the form of Farooq is tossed through the air like a ragdoll from an unknown location. He crashes into them and they all land in the dumpster.] Farooq: QUICK! WE'VE GOT TO GET OUT OF HERE! HE'LL KILL US ALL! [A huge, menacing silhouette comes around the corner, chuckling to himself.] Soultaker: Yoo-hoo, Ron? Where'd you get to? I'm not done ripping your heart out yet! [The cowardly Farooq tosses one of the cameramen at Soultaker. He catches him and laughs.] Soultaker: Don't make me laugh. You will never win. [The cameraman is thrown like a sack of garbage into another dumpster.] Face me like a man, if you are one. [Farooq begins praying to Allah as Soultaker looks into the dumpster and sees him there.] Soultaker: Oh, I don't think he can help you now, little man. [Soultaker climbs into the dumpster and the closes the lid as the storm rages more and more violently. Another lightning bolt drowns out someone's death shriek as we fade out to the laughter of the Soultaker...] To be continued...
