DEWF — Roleplay Archive

Chapter two.

Mar 7, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chapter 2. [The screen is filled with static, and a little "WCWF" logo is in the bottom corner. Suddenly, the picture kicks in and Ted Dibiase is yelling at his yes-men.] Ted: GODDAMMIT! I DON'T NEED THIS! [He stomps around a bit to cool off.] Ted: Two dead cameramen in our first week of operation. Now I've got Virgil running the damn camera and one of the top stars in the world beaten to a bloody pulp in a dumpster outside! Virgil: [From off-camera] I don't think I'd call Farooq *that* big of a star... Ted: That's not the point! This "Soultaker" jerk terrorized the CWF to the point of shutting it down, and I want him stopped! Or signed to a long-term contract. Preferably the latter. [Various yes-men scribble in their notebooks.] Ted: Well, what are you waiting for? GET THE HELL OUT OF HERE! Find this guy and throw huge sums of money at him! [The yes-men scatter.] Ted: AND TURN THAT DAMN CAMERA OFF! [We suddenly cut to our host, Toad Pedophile, inside WCWF Studios.] Toad: Good evening and welcome to another exciting installment of WCWF Spotlight. We've got a big main event this week, as Hulk Hogan defends his long-dormant WCWF World title against last week's big winner, the Honky Tonk Man. But first, I'd like to introduce my special guests this week, the Nasty Boys! [Saggs and Knobbs waddle out to ringside for the interview.] Toad: Well, guys, you weren't exactly welcomed with open arms into Ted Dibiase's fold upon entering the WCWF, and now after last week's dismal outing against the Stallions it appears you've been fired. Saggs: It's a conspiracy against us, Toad! First Disneyland fires us over a few minor incidents, and now this. The system stinks, man. Knobbs: Yeah! We're beginning to think someone's out to get us! Toad: Well, Mr. Dibiase *did* acquire the services of Demolition last week specifically to attack you guys and put you out... Saggs: What are you saying, Toad? Do you think Mr. Dibiase has something against us? Knobbs: Yeah, spit it out. If you think there's something going on here, just say it. Toad: Uh, I think they're here, guys. Saggs: Who? Toad: DEMOLITION! [Toad jumps out of the ring as Ax and Smash enter the ring. Knobbs grabs the microphone.] Knobbs: Hey! This is free TV! Aren't we supposed to be building to a match on pay per view or something? Ax: This is the WCWF, you idiot! We can't even fill a grocery store, let alone a pay per view! We're just gonna take care of you jerks gratis. [And so they did. For the more sensitive viewers in our audience, the author will skip the more painful parts involving the pliers, the tables, and the 1976 Volvo (that's gotta hurt...) and cut to the Nasties making their exit.] [We see EMS technicians carrying the Nasties out on a stretcher. Literally, both guys on the same stretcher because Demolition has knotted them in an anatomically impossible position.] Back to Toad... [We see Toad in the locker room area, interviewing a very battered Farooq and his manager Teddy Long.] Toad: Last week, everyone saw what happened to you at the hands of the Soultaker. How did you survive, and what are your plans in the WCWF now? Teddy: I'll answer that. The supposed rampage of Mr. Charles Wright was nothing more than camera tricks perpetrated by racist factions running the WCWF as we speak. I bet those white pigs are sitting there airbrushing the black out of my face as we speak. Toad: But the camera quite clearly showed him killing two men, live on semi-national TV. Teddy: Lies. All lies. Charles Wright has never hurt a soul in his life, and I *demand* more conclusive proof than has been presented here so far. Toad: He was videotaped killing two innocent men with his bare hands! Teddy: Innocent? What is innocence? I would stake my career and reputation on the fact that those men were hatemongering rednecks, who were simply out to sully the good name of Charles Wright. Toad: You mean the Soultaker. Teddy: That name is an oppressive collar given to him by the Man to slap the blackness from him like a master slapping the bite from his dog. But soon we shall rise up and hit the white man in the nose with the rolled-up newspaper of freedom and equality! Bad dog! Bad dog! Toad: You are a sad, pathetic little man. Teddy: Is that a racial slur, white boy? Toad: Farooq, what are you thoughts on this whole matter? Farooq: Allah is my strength. My leader. My shepherd. All things are destined to pass, and so shall this. Toad: Oh, great, it's philosophy time at the puppet show. And here I thought we were running a wrestling promotion. [Someone off-stage bursts into laughter] Toad: Shut up already over there! Go to a match or something! [We cut to the outside of WCWF Studios, where a huge mother of a man, known by the enigmatic name of The Stranger, and dressed entirely in black with a black overcoat, stands menacingly outside the door. The security guard (in this case, Butch Reed) seems less than anxious to admit him.] Reed: Uh, are you wanting to try out for the WCWF? Stranger: No. I have business. Let me pass. Reed: Oh, my god...you're *HIM*! Stranger: I am many things. Let me pass. Reed: I've...I've heard stories about you! I heard that you come every few years and wipe out all the heels in a federation in some twisted justice thing! Oh, god, don't kill me, man! I'm so sorry... Stranger: As am I. But what I do, I do for the greater good. [The Stranger slams Reed's head into the glass doors, knocking him unconscious but not injuring him.] Stranger: It begins. I sense my opposite number's presence here. I am needed. [Despite being 12 noon, shadows seem to cover his every pore as he steps over Reed and makes his way towards...????] "Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is for the WCWF World Heavyweight championship! It is scheduled for one fall." ["Honky Tonk Man" plays on small ghetto blaster, stolen from a kid who left it outside while going into a 7-11 to get a Slurpee. Dibiase had Virgil beat up the kid and steal the Slurpee for good measure.] "First the challenger...from Memphis, here is the #1 contender to the championship...the Honky Tonk Man!" Honky Tonk wiggles his fat ass into the ring to prepare for his title match. "And his opponent..." ["Real American" replaces the first song as the Hulkster makes his way out to ringside, to the cheers of the 122 people in attendence (96 of them given complimentary tickets...)] "...From Hollywood, California, the last guy to hold the World title before the whole federation folded...'Hollywood' Hulk Hogan!" Hogan enters, with Beefcake following like a lapdog. Hogan charges into the ring and goes right after HTM, hoping for a quick day so he can collect his paycheque and go home. He nails the Honkster with a series of rights, then quickly whips him into the ropes and delivers the big boot! Honky quickly rolls out of the ring, where Beefcake is waiting with the clippers. Honk backs up rapidly, falling ass-backwards over the ring steps. The crowd laughs at his expense. Beefcake pulls out a pair of scissors, and begins snipping away at his hair, which the crowd finds amusing to no end. Hogan is feigning a nap in the ring while Beefcake and Honky do their antics outside. Beefcake tosses Honky back in the ring, and Hogan pops up and kicks him in the ample gut. He then puts a headlock on him, and proceeds to mess up his hair instead of using an actual move. The crowd is loving what is turning into a comedy match. He releases the hold, then pats Honky on his belly. Hogan calls for the microphone. "You know, Honky Tonk Man, you're just wasting my time out here. You're a joke. I know it. You know it. This crowd knows it. So here's the deal -- I'll give you $25,000 right now to lie down and let me pin you, and we can both call it a night. You know you don't have a chance of pinning me while you're still a fat, semi-retired old hasbeen. So what do you say?" Honky looks as though he's about to say something to salvage what remains of his dignity, but then simply decides to lay down and let Hulk have a three count. Hulk, however, doesn't do that. As Honky lays down, Hogan comes off with a legdrop. Then another. And another. And another. Brutus rolls into the ring and proceeds to shave HTM bald. Hogan grabs the guitar from ringside and smashes it over Honky's head, shattering it into a thousand pieces. He grabs the microphone again. "I'd say this is a pretty effective image change, wouldn't you, Brutus?" Hogan rolls him over, finally, and gets the official pin to retain the WCWF World title. Hogan and Beefcake leave the beaten challenger to stew while Toad comes into the ring to interview him. Toad: Well, Honky, it appears that you've sunken about as low as you can sink. HTM: Mama...where's my peanut butter and banana sandwiches... [Shakes off the blow to the head.] HTM: Ungh...what hit me? Toad: A guitar to the head, last I checked. HTM: This ain't no good, Toad. The Honky Tonk Man came to the WCWF to sing for the people and dance for the people, and he's getting laughed at! Well, I don't wanna be a joke anymore! Toad: A bit late, I'd think. HTM: No, it ain't! I know people, you know! I have contacts. The Colonel will find me someone who can make the name Honky Tonk Man respected again. Toad: That's like getting blood from a stone, I think. HTM: I'll be back, Toad! You'll see! [Honky Tonk storms out of the ring and heads back to the dressing room, dragging his ass behind him.] Toad: Well, maybe this next interview will feature someone a little more intelligent. [Checks the card.] Oh, damn. Please welcome my esteemed guests, Vinnie Vegas and the Diamond Studd! [Vegas and Studd meander out to ringside, using mannerisms almost like, but entirely quite the same as, those of Diesel and Razor Ramon.] Vegas: Yo, Toadster. You should be honored to be the in presence of greatness. Studd: Yeah, Toad. We're two of the sport's greats, you know. Toad: Oh, give me a break. You're just the loser twin brothers of two of the sport's greats. You schmoes were washing cars in Albequerque when Ted Dibiase dug you up in desperation. Vegas: Hey, we're starting up a new organization to rule the WCWF! Pretty soon me, the Studd, the Hulkster and our mystery partners will take over the place. We're gonna call ourselves the Former Gimmick Alliance. [They shake hands as Hollywood Hogan comes down to ringside.] Toad: You're all *really* sad, you know that? Just pathetic. You rip off your twin brothers personalities and mannerisms, and now you want to steal their gimmick? Studd: Hey, without us they wouldn't be shit. *We* were around long before "Diesel Power" or "The Bad Guy" became marketing juggernauts, pal! We're gonna hit them where it hurts -- the wallet! [Ka-ching!] [Vegas and the Studd suddenly pull out "FGA" shirts and put them on.] Vegas: Better watch out, Toad, 'cause more of us are coming in the weeks to come. Toad: I'll try to control my enthusiasm. Studd: Just buy the damn shirt. [Thankfully, they leave, as we cut to the alley outside.] [We see Honky Tonk Man angrily slamming the back door shut as he leaves WCWF Studios in a huff.] HTM: Stupid fans, anyways. I won't sing or dance for them anymore. [A familiar form appears.] Soultaker: Good. I hear through the grapevine that you're looking for respect. HTM: Damn straight! I want everyone in the WCWF to know that the Honky Tonk Man isn't a joke anymore! Soultaker: I think that can be arranged. For a price. HTM: ... Soultaker: I'll take that as assent. Now hold still, this won't hurt a bit... To be continued...
