Chapter syxx.

Mar 7, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WCWF Chapter 6: Edgar Winter's naked butt, man! What more do you need? One last word from the FGA before we begin... The following message has been paid for the Former Gimmick Alliance. [We see Hollywood Hogan sitting in a recording studio.] By popular demand -- Hulk Hogan's Greatest Hits! That's right, available for a limited time on this 75 CD collection, Hulk Hogan sings every hit song -- EVER! ["Real American" plays in the background.] Hogan: Feelings...nothing more than feelings... Not just all of his greatest hits, but the greatest hits of every other singer in the history of recorded music, all to the tune of "Real American!" ["Real American" plays in the background.] Hogan: Help! I need somebody. Help! Not just anybody... Each song is digitally mastered and recorded by legendary Beatles producer George Martin, and Hulk plays all the instruments himself! You'll get... ["Real American plays in the background for all.] Hogan: I can't get no satisfaction... Hogan: Come with me, to the sea of love... Hogan: You can't touch this! Hogan: Exit light, enter night... And of course, such sentimental favorites such as... ["Real American" still playing in the background.] Hogan: How am I supposed to live without you... Hogan: Yesterday, all my troubles seemed so far away... Hogan: So now I come to you, with open arms... Hogan: Every breath you take, every move you make... Yes, all this on 75 CDs for 20 easy payments of two hundred and ninety nine dollars and ninety nine cents each! And as an added bonus, if you order within the next century, you'll get Beefcake Anthology 1,2 and 3! [They start to play a clip, then think better of it and stop.] Order now, Sally Struthers is standing by to take your order! Call now! Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Diner's Club, cheque, money order, IOU all accepted* *Offer not valid in continental United States, or in Alaska, Hawaii or Canada. CALL NOW! The preceding was a paid message from the Former Gimmick Alliance. [Static] Dibiase: Camera! God damnit, I'll fire your ass right now if you don't get that... [We cut to Dibiase in the ring with Toad Pedophile.] Dibiase: ...thing working. That's better. Well, we're going to try this again, ladies and gentlemen, now that the FGA has been removed. So this week we're going to stick to wrestling. We've got the MVC meeting the team I went out and signed after freeing up Hulk Hogan's contract, the debut of Brian Lee's new gimmick, and an absolute minimum of stupidity, although that's asking for a lot with this federation. [Demolition suddenly makes their way to the ring.] Dibiase: Ah, good, an actual interview with an actual tag team. What's on your mind, boys? Ax: Bite me, you bloated pig. Smash: We got screwed in the semi-finals by those t-shirt hawking losers and now you're telling me some team off the street gets the title shot tonight? That's bullshit! Dibiase: You're right. And since I'm indebted to you for ridding me of the Nasty Boys earlier this month, I'm giving you two the tag titles right now. [The Demos are actually pretty shocked to hear this.] Ax: Uh, thanks. Smash: Yeah. Forget what we said about you. [Dibiase has the referee award the first WCWF tag team title to Demolition on the spot.] Dibiase: Of course, you'll have to defend against the winner of tonight's tag match at our next house show. Ax: That seems reasonable. Dibiase: Good. Always glad to be of service. I'm in such a good mood now that Hogan and his gang of idiots are gone, I think I'll give everyone in the audience $200 cash. I mean, there's only... [The crowd has suddenly swelled to over 2,500 people.] Dibiase: I won't even ask how many fire hazard laws we're breaking by stuffing 2,500 people into an abandoned grocery store. But if I give you all the money, you have to stay and watch the show! [The crowd suddenly falls back to about 500 people.] Dibiase: Well, as discouraging as that was, it's still more than usual. Okay, get ready for a great show, and tell your friends about us! Opening match: Corporal Kirschner v. Brian Lee. The following match is *not* a paid presentation of the FGA, thank god. Brian Lee, the man who was the Underfaker while with the FGA, has now reverted back to the "Gangster" persona he used while in the former CWF with partner Vinnie Vegas. The referee pats down the Corporal for weapons before the match, and finds an Uzi, two grenades and a Stingray missle, although how he got *that* into his kneepad is anyone's guess. Kirschner seems to have sustained some facial injuries at the hands of the Stranger following their altercation at the first house show, and manager Teddy Long is constantly calling for the referee to allow him to treat them between rest-holds. Of which there are a lot. Kirschner isn't exactly a master technician. This is pretty much a squash for Lee, as he hits the slingshot powerbomb (his new finisher) at the 4:13 mark for the easy pin. Let's get a word with Teddy Long. [Toad leaves the broadcast position to interview the manager.] Toad: Well, Teddy, with the FGA gone, I guess that means you're the resident idiot in the WCWF then. How do you plan on using this to your advantage? Teddy: Don't you get uppity with me, white boy. Even with that stupid goatee, I could still kick your honky ass all over this ring. I've got a pretty formidable stable left, I'll have you know. Toad: Speaking of which, we haven't seen the Soultaker around lately? Have his electroshock therapy treatments been taking a bigger toll on him than you thought? Teddy: He's not crazy. He merely has some issues to work out. 500 years of oppression by the white man will do that to a negro, you know. Toad: Oh, god, you don't really believe that psychopath is 500 years old, do you? Teddy: I believe every word Mr. Wright speaks to be the gospel truth. He is my brother, and my master. Toad: So in other words, he's got you brainwashed, too. Teddy: I am totally in possession of my faculties, Bob. Toad: That's Toad. Teddy: Whatever. Toad: At least tell me you got him a *good* psychiatrist. Teddy: Absolutely. The best one money could buy. [We cut to the offices of "Dr." Vincent B. Baats, resident doctor/lawyer/dry cleaner of the WCWF since 1993.] Baats: So, Charles, you say that the white man oppressed you at any early age? Soultaker: That's right, Dr. Baats. When I was in Harvard, my racial background was viciously cut off because I was forced to learn English, instead of my native Ebonics. I was so heartbroken, I developed the Soultaker persona to cope. Baats: I see. Did you listen to a lot of heavy metal music, too? Soultaker: Lots. Some of it backwards. Baats: Mm-hmm. And Howard Stern? Soultaker: Every day. Sometimes backwards. I'm convinced that years of racial oppression, Howard Stern and heavy metal have turned me into the troubled individual you see before you. Baats: I couldn't agree more. Harvard can be a rough school. Some very unsavory characters hang out there. Soultaker: So you don't think I'm crazy? Baats: No, I think you're the victim. I believe that when you lash out violently with homocidal intent, it's merely a cry for help. You're saying "Help, help, I'm being oppressed." Soultaker: Would you be willing to, say, fill out a medical certificate to that effect? Baats: I'm sure we can work something out. Soultaker: Good. I'll have my people call your people. So I'll see you Tuesday at the country club, then? Baats: Um, actually, I think I have a racquetball game with Bill Gates already. Soultaker: Ah. Well, I'll have to catch you at the funeral then. Baats: The funeral? Soultaker: Yes. [The lights suddenly dim, and Soultaker becomes backlit with a blood red light.] Yours. NYAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAAH! Baats: I'm sorry, where did that dramatic lighting come from? [We never quite get the answer, and I'm afraid we never quite see Vincent B. Baats again.] [Well, okay, we see parts of him flying out the window, and they managed to find a couple of pieces of him and put them in the coffin so his mother wouldn't think he was eaten alive by bears or something, but it sounds more dramatic the first way, and I think that's what really matters in the end.] [Back to ringside.] Toad: Okay, luckily Teddy Long has gone away now. And while we were gone, we managed to convince the high school to let us back! That's right -- WCWF house show #2 is coming very soon now! So far, all we know for sure is that Demolition will defend the tag titles against the winner of tonight's tag match, Shane Douglas will defend the World title in some capacity, and Crush will meet Adam Bomb. More in the weeks to come. And speak of the asshole, here's Shane Douglas. Shane: I'll thank you to speak of the WCWF World champion with a little respect, Toad. I retired Ric Flair, you know. Toad: No, you didn't. That's just another little delusion in your already extremely deluded mind. Shane: Toad, Toad, Toad. [Pinching cheeks.] You're so naive. You know, back when I was WWF World champion... Toad: You were never WWF World champion. You were Intercontinental champion. Shane: Minor detail. Toad: For about half an hour. Shane: I don't care. Toad: And you didn't even win the title. Shane: Shut up or I'll hurt you. My *point* is that I owned that federation, because I am the Franchise! Toad: Oh, please. You could barely fill a bingo hall. You lost to Barry Horowitz to end up here. You're lucky to be wrestling right now. You're better suited to a janitorial job. Shane: And speaking of freaks in coveralls, I'm going to defend my title against Nailz at the next house show, just to show everyone how great I truly am, although that's preaching to converted. Then maybe once I dominate this federation like I dominated WCW, I'll be able to go back to the DEWF where I belong. I've just a major endorsement contract, you know. Toad: Oh, christ, not you too... Shane: That's right, from now on I'll be known as "Frito-Lays" Shane Douglas. Toad: This is not happening. Shane: Get with the times, Toad. Everyone has a sponsor nowadays. Oh, look over there! [Shane turns around to show a blatant "Nike" ad on the back of his robe.] Shane: And did someone drop a quarter? [Shane bends over, and displays a "Hertz Rent-A-Car" ad on the back of his tights.] Toad: This is really, really sad. Someone please run in and chokeslam this idiot. ["Frankenstein" starts playing over the PA system. Edgar Winter runs into the ring and tries a chokeslam, but can't quite get Shane up.] Edgar: Sorry, 911's busy. He asked me to sub. Toad: Don't worry about it. You tried. Edgar: Thanks, man. [Looks at Shane's rear end.] Hey, you sold your butt to Hertz, too? Cool, check this out, man! [Edgar Winter drops his pants, revealing a huge "Hertz" tattoo where the sun doesn't shine.] Toad: Um, Edgar, who would see your naked butt enough to make it worth selling ad space? Edgar: You don't want to know, man. [The author apologizes in advance to Edgar Winter, his friends, his family, and any fans who might be reading this, but the joke was just sitting there, calling out to me, saying "Scott, it's me...the joke about Edgar Winter's butt! Make me, make me! I know I'm cheap, but if Beavis and Butthead can make 20 million dollars on opening night, you can make this joke!" I couldn't resist. I'm weak. I know it. I'm sorry.] [I apologize again for that rambling. It won't happen again. I will try to refrain from making any more cheap or easy jokes involving Edgar Winter, his butt, or any other bodily functions.] [Edgar Winter farts.] [I'M SORRY, ALRIGHT! THE LURE OF TOILET HUMOR IS JUST TOO GREAT! THIS IS WHAT ALL THOSE YEARS OF MARRIED WITH CHILDREN AND THE FOX NETWORK HAVE DONE TO ME. I'M THE *VICTIM* HERE.] [Send cash donations to "[name withheld] Fund, c/o WCWF." Anything under $50 will be cheerfully laughed at.] [But still spent. Every little bit helps.] Toad: Are you about done? Could we finish this today? [Sorry. Go on.] Toad: Thank you. Edgar: Who the hell is he? Toad: The author. We all think he's a bit twisted, but he created all of us, so we kind of have to humor him. Edgar: Bummer. Well, I'm off to have anal sex with a bunch of guys. [See, wasn't that more tasteful?] [Okay, forget I said anything.] Shane: HELLO? I'm still here, and I'm still the champ, you know. Could I finish this interview so I can return all this sponsorship stuff. Toad: Why? Shane: One look at that tattoo has turned me off capitalism forever. [I CAN'T JUST LET IT DIE! IT'S SUCH A GREAT JOKE! I MEAN, COME ON, IT'S EDGAR WINTER'S BUTT FOR GOD'S SAKE! YOU JUST DON'T GET THOSE KIND OF CHEAP HUMOR OPPORTUNITIES EVERY DAY! IT TOOK JOHN CLEESE MONTHS TO THINK OF THAT KIND OF STUFF!] Toad: Let's just move on and forget this whole ugly running joke never happened, shall we? Let's do the tag match and then get out of here before Scott loses all control of his mental faculties... Tag match: Miracle Violence Connection v. The Team Ted Dibiase Signed With The Money Freed Up By Hulk Hogan's Contract. Man, I hope they pick a better name than that. The MVC comes down to the ring first, because otherwise the suspense would just be shot all to hell. Dibiase emerges from the curtains and grabs a microphone. "Okay, I hope everyone appreciates this...ladies and gentlemen, please welcome to the WCWF on a three year guaranteed contract...HARLEM HEAT!!!!" Predictably, they have that ambassador of racial harmony, Teddy Long, with them. But the crowd still pops pretty big for the Heat as they make their way to the ring. "All I ask, and I don't think I'm out of line here considering what happened between Douglas and Crush in that bloodbath, is that you tone down the insane violence. Please. I'm begging you." MVC v. Harlem Heat (winner gets tag title shot): Gordy starts out against Booker T. Lockup, and Gordy shoves Booker to the corner, then kicks away at the midsection. Cross-corner whip, clothesline, Booker T is down. Thinking it worked so well the first time, Gordy picks him up, whips him to the other side again, clothesline, but this time he doesn't go down. Gordy looks a bit surprised, and Booker T chooses the moment to backdrop Gordy over the top rope to the floor, and soon all four men are brawling away. Ted Dibiase can be seen banging his head into a concrete pillar if you look closely enough, but the match is more exciting so we'll stay there. They go non-stop outside the ring for about 3 minutes, only stopping for breath a few times, until all four, including Roddy Piper and Teddy Long, are bloody messes and in need of medical attention. Ted Dibiase's cell phone is ringing, and he doesn't need a psychic to tell him that it's another sponsor dropping him. Finally, Terry Gordy manages to pick himself off the ground, throw one of the Heat into the ring (under all the blood it's hard to tell which) and pin him at 10:45 of a wild brawl to earn the title shot against Demolition, which I'm sure promises to be a scientific classic. Sarcasm intended. [We follow Ted Dibiase to the back as he's crying again.] Ted: Oh, lord, can't anything go right anymore? I was having such a great day, too! Please, I'll be a good person, I promise, just give me something to work with. [There's a knock on the door. Dibiase opens it and his eyes light up with excitement.] Pizza Boy: Did someone here order a medium pepperoni? Ted: Didn't you used to be someone? Pizza Boy: Yeah, you bet I was. But back in the CWF, I made the stupid mistake of crossing the Four Horsemen near the end. Now look at me! Ted: You want to be a wrestler again? Pizza Boy: I've already applied with the DEWF. They don't want me. They say I'm washed up. They say I'm no good anymore. Why do you want me? Ted: I think you're absolutely perfect. [Static] [A lawyer cuts in] Lawyer: On behalf of the World Wrestling Federation, I would like to inform Mr. Dibiase and the rest of the WCWF that the name "Mr. Perfect" and all references to perfection are copyrighted trademarks of Titan Sports, Inc and cannot be used without severe legal ramifications. Therefore, if Mr. Dibiase intends to use Curt Hennig as a professional wrestler in his quote, federation, unquote, he must find a different gimmick that does not refer to perfection of any sort. Thank you, and good day. [Static] [Dibiase returns] Ted: What I meant was, I think you're absolutely...spiffy. [Says the last word with a disgusted look on his face.] Curt: That's why they call me...Mr. Spiffy. [Gets the same look on his face.] Are you sure we can't just use Mr. Perfect? I'll pay for the legal fees if you want. Ted: No, don't even try it. I'm still paying through the nose for use of the Honky Tonk Man. They actually confiscated Wayne Ferris as evidence! Can you believe Titan's lawyers? That's why we haven't seen him since week 2. Curt: Man, what some people won't stoop to for money. [Shane Douglas walks in, naked from the waist down.] Shane: Hey, do either of you guys know where I can this "Jimmy Dean Sausages" tattoo removed from my... [Screen goes static as airtime runs out again.] [Thank god.] To be continued...
