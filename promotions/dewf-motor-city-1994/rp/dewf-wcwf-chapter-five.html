Chapter five.

Mar 7, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chapter 5. "The Big Good-bye" [Static.] Voice: Someone turn on the damn camera! [We see our hosts, Toad Pedophile and Ted Dibiase, standing in the ring with a rabid crowd of almost 100 people, a new attendance record for the TV tapings.] Toad: Good evening, WCWF fans! We're actually making an attempt to be professionals here since our house show broadcast garnered record ratings for the cable access station, and to that end we've had the Former Gimmick Alliance make us these spiffy new WCWF logo jackets! [Dibiase does a supermodel turn to show off the material.] Toad: We've got a great... Dibiase: I'd say it's decent at best, Toad. Toad: I'm trying to be excited here, Ted. Dibiase: Well, let's be realistic. We suck. We know we suck. I think it's safe to say that our champion is a loudmouthed jerk who doesn't want to be here, the air time is dominated by a bunch of capitalistic morons, and the only sponsors we had pulled the plug after our World champion engaged in a bloodbath with his challenger on cable access. We suck, Toad. Toad: Well, maybe the best is yet to come... Dibiase: Oh, please. Be serious. Oh, great, it gets better. The following run-in was paid for by the Former Gimmick Alliance. Toad: Oh, what, they've got sponsors for their *run-ins* now, too? [Hulk Hogan brings Brutus to the ring on a leash, wearing his posh new Dennis Rodman-styled outfit and sporting a yellow fake beard.] Dibiase: Dyed your beard, I see. Hulk: That's right, Zillion Dollar Man. With all the cash the FGA is raking in, brother, I can afford to look good. Not that I don't already. We're the best thing the WCWF has going right now, man. Dibiase: Oh, please. The only way that this day could possibly be salvaged for me would be if an international superstar came out of those curtains over there, ran down to the ring, and beat the crap out of you and the rest of your lackies single-handedly. [The crowd looks expectantly towards the curtain.] [Vinnie Vegas and The Diamond Studd walk out. Not quite what they had in mind.] Dibiase: Well, that trick will work one of these times... Vegas: Ay! Buy da damn shirt! Toad: Don't you know *any* other lines? Vegas: [Embarrassed] Uh, due to threat of legal action from WCW, the WWF and the nWo, that's actually the only line I'm legally allowed to say now. [A horde of lawyers from the firm of Dewey, Screwum and Howe run out to the ring.] Dewey: On behalf of Extreme Championship Wrestling, I'm officially serving you with a summons to court for infringement of copyright! Howe: No we're not! Dewey: Shut up. Vegas: What are you talking about? I've never even been to Philadelphia! Dewey: I know. But business has been slow recently, so we figured that Paul Heyman would get around to suing eventually, and we wanted in on the ground floor when he did. [Diamond Studd clotheslines all three very silly lawyers over the top rope, to the delight of the crowd.] Studd: Aye, carumba, chico. [Everyone looks at him funny.] Studd: Yeah, I know it sounds dumb, but Matt Groening's lawyers are easier to deal with than Vince MacMahon's. Dibiase: IS THERE A POINT TO THIS?!? Hulk: Money, brother. Barrels of it. We're making it and you're not. We just like to rub it in now and then. Dibiase: That's it! You're all fired! [A roar erupts from the crowd.] Hulk: Sorry? Studd: What? Vegas: Ay? Brutus: Woof? Dibiase: You heard me. You four clowns can go to the DEWF and sell your shirts there. I'm sick of you. I'll keep Brian Lee and give him a better gimmick myself. Hulk: You can't fire us! Eric Bischoff didn't fire the nWo! Look how successful that angle is, even if it doesn't make any sense! Dibiase: You're FIRED! F, I, R, E, D! FIRED! Pack up your shirts and dumb commercials and get out! You are all under contract to me, and I am optioning those contracts to the DEWF, where I hope you all become shirt-selling jobbers! Vegas: But we're in the tag team title match! Dibiase: Oh, yeah? I bet I can find a better team, right off the street, before the show ends and put them in there against the MVC. So now I'm going to go find one, and when I get back, I want you losers OUT OF HERE! Hulk: Who's gonna make us? [Dibiase makes a final prayer to the gods of wrestling, hoping it works this time] Dibiase: HIM!!! ["Frankenstein" starts playing, which is weird because they don't even have a PA system...but at any rate, the FGA clears the ring faster than the speed of light, in a Porsche, on the freeway, with no red lights for a million miles.] [Edgar Winter emerges from the dressing room. The crowd groans at the really stupid joke.] Dibiase: Hey, what are you complaining about? It worked, didn't it? [They had to give him that one.] [The FGA emerges slowly from the dressing room, realizing that their adversary is an aging rock star.] Edgar: Uh, this wasn't in my contract... [Vegas and Studd approach him while Hulk mugs for the camera. Brutus wags his tail with excitement, then disappears.] Hulk: Uh, guys, did you see where Brutus got to? Vegas: Hang on, we gotta kick this guy's ass. Hulk: Uh, okay. 911: Hi there. [Chokeslam!] [The crowd goes wild.] [Vegas and the Studd turn in horror, to see 911 standing there with a evil smile on his face.] [Chokeslam!] [Chokeslam!] Dibiase: Ladies and gentlemen, somebody call 911! [The crowd cheers him on.] Dibiase: No, really, I think he broke Hogan's neck. Somebody really should call 911... [The crowd doesn't care as they give 911 a pop bigger than Jesus.] Dibiase: You know, I'm the one who signed him for this show...it's just a one-shot deal. He's not with the WCWF... [The crowd is doing the wave for 911 as he stage dives into his adoring throng of fans.] Dibiase: Whatever... [The FGA scrapes themselves off the floor and heads for the DEWF, and god help them.] [The DEWF, not the FGA...] The preceding ass-kicking was paid for and delivered with style by 911, muthafucka.
