Chapter seven.

Mar 7, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WCWF - Chapter 7 [We open with Ted and new co-host Curt Hennig in the ring.] Dibiase: Okay, we've got the camera working -- finally -- and I'm mildly excited to bring out our "esteemed" WCWF World champion right now, and he claims to have a big announcement. Curt: Maybe we should reinforce the foundations of the WCWF, Ted. These announcments always seems to shake them. Dibiase: Not bad, Curt. Good use of sarcasm. Curt: Does this mean I get that raise after all? Dibiase: No. Curt: Oh. Here's Shane, if anyone cares. [Shane Douglas, wearing a Budweiser jacket, Tampax boots, Hertz Rent-a-Car tights, and Slim Jim kneepads, enters the ring, while smoking a Marlboro cigarette and drinking a Keystone beer.] Shane: I just want to deny the vicious rumors that have me selling out to the corporate sector! Dibiase: You've obviously sold out, Shane. Not that I don't appreciate the added revenue. But you're a sell out, plain and simple. Shane: Okay, you're right. But I still *wanted* to deny the rumors. But since that doesn't seem to be possible, I'm going to go one step further...ever since Hogan and his merry band of idiots were fired, there's been a perfectly good concept left unused here in the WCWF... Dibiase: Oh god no... Shane: THAT'S RIGHT! I'm reforming the Former Gimmick Alliance! [A collection groan comes from the crowd.] Shane: I'm taking back my Dean Douglas gimmick, with a slight alteration. Dibiase: Which would be... Shane: From now on, it's "Jimmy Dean" Douglas! As in the sausage company I had tattooed on my... Dibiase: We're aware of the location, yes. Just bring out the rest of the members and let's get this over with. Shane: Okay, first the man who takes a licking and keeps on ticking, "Prime Timex" Brian Lee. Dibiase: Well, at least it's a better gimmick than the Underfaker one... [Brian Lee comes out, wearing about 50 watches on his arms and with a big Flavor Flav clock around his neck.] Dibiase: ...or maybe not. Shane: And the soon to be tag team champions of the world -- The Miracle Violence Connection! Dibiase: Okay, no gimmick change there. Shane: Oops! Almost forgot -- thanks to the fine folks at Kraft Ltd, it's now the Miracle WHIP Violence Connection! Dibiase: Please tell me there's a lot of money in this for me. Shane: There is. We're sponsoring the next WCWF house show, and it's even going to be on HBO! We're calling it "Soul Doubt." Dibiase: That's so cheesy it just might work! Good work, Shane. Now don't screw it up, please. Shane: Have I ever let you down before, Ted? I think not. Dibiase: Alright, but one wrong move and you're banished to the DEWF along with the rest of your FGA friends. [Ted thinks a minute about that one.] Dibiase: No, wait, that'd be what you want. Okay, one wrong move and banished here with a renewed contract! [Ted thinks a minute about that one, too.] Dibiase: No, wait, that works out pretty good for you, too. Okay...one wrong move and there'll be absolutely no retribution! [Thinks a minute again.] Dibiase: Can't really see anything wrong with that one. Shane: Works for me, Mr. Dibiase. Now if you'll excuse me, I have a marketing contract to finalize. Curt: So what's the main event of the show going to be, tough guy? You against Crush again? You against some 8 year old kid from the audience? You getting your butt kicked by the Sandman again? Shane: You got a problem with me, "Mr. Spiffy?" Curt: No problem, sausage boy. I'm just an announcer now. Besides, I'd need a manager to provide me with the proper motivation to kick your bleached blond ass. Shane: THAT'S A LIE! I'M A NATURAL BLOND! HOLD ME BACK! [The MVC hold him back.] Curt: You're pathetic. I'm about 45 now, and I've been on a Lloyd's of London policy since 1993. What damage could I possibly do to you? [Shane sees the wisdom of this and decides to back off.] Shane: Okay, this isn't really funny anymore, so we're leaving now and we're not coming back until Scott thinks of some better material for us. [They do so.] Dibiase: I wish Hulk and his buddies had done that more often. [Cut to the offices of Frank Dewey, ex of the law firm of Dewey, Screwum, and Howe. The Soultaker sits opposite him.] Dewey: Ever since I quit that silly law firm, I've been looking for a client to take me right to the top of the legal profession. Soultaker: And you think I'm it? Dewey: No, but I think you'll scare the judge so badly he'll have to rule in our favor. Although I must admit that there is a pretty airtight case against you in the Vincent B. Baats murder. Soultaker: I was playing golf at the time. Dewey: Ah. Well, that's another story, then. Who with? Soultaker: OJ Simpson. [Robert Shapiro suddenly cuts into the sketch.] Shapiro: We of the OJ Simpson legal team vehemently object to any and all use of the name, likeness, or reputation of our client without the express written consent of Mr. Simpson. [OJ comes into the scene.] OJ: You want me to cut their throat, Robert? [Shapiro yells "Cut!" and we go back to the offices of Dewey.] Dewey: Well, that certainly was silly. Now, Mr. Wright, as I understand it, you were banned from the WCWF until the investigation and/or murder trial could be completed, is that correct? Soultaker: Right. I feel a grave injustice has been done. I deserve a chance to kick Shane Douglas' ass, just like everyone else! Dewey: Lord knows we all wish we could. Well, I'm pretty sure I can get an injuction against the federation that will allow you to compete until this ugly matter is resolved. You deserve justice, just like anyone else. Soultaker: Yes...justice... [The lights suddenly go out, and a bolt of lightning illuminates the room and indeed the Soultaker.] Dewey: That sort of dramatic moment can't be good for our case, Charles. Soultaker: Sorry. Force of habit. Won't happen again. Dewey: Have you considered seeing a therapist? [Soultaker jumps up and grabs the lawyer by the jacket] Soultaker: I'M NOT CRAZY! Dewey: Uh...of course not. I apologize for even insinuating something like that. Now then, the court will ask for you to be accompanied by someone stable and an upstanding member of the community during your run in the WCWF, to ensure something like this doesn't get out of control. Do you have someone in mind? [The lightning flashes again, and someone walks into the room, carrying a familar looking bag.] Jake Roberts: I think I can be of some assistance... [Dewey shakes his head and takes a slug of Maalox straight from the bottle as we fade out...] [Cut to Missy Hyatt with *your* FGA: Soul Doubt Report.] Missy: Hi, everyone, and I just want to invite everyone to tune into HBO in a few short days for the closest this two-bit federation is ever going to a PPV...FGA Soul Doubt! Let's sneak a peek at the lineup so far... In the main event, Shane "Jimmy Dean" Douglas defends the WCWF World heavyweight title, the most prestigious title in Hackensack, I might add, against the ex-convict turned role model for children everywhere...Nailz! The first ever WCWF World tag team champions, Demolition, will defend against two more members of the *new* Former Gimmick Alliance, in the form of the Miracle-Whip Violence Connection: Steve Williams and Terry Gordy! As always, Rowdy Roddy Piper will be in their corner. No word on any corporate sponsorship for Hot Rod as of yet, although Hot Rod Beef Jerky looks promising as a candidate. Tee-hee! In a token squash for the token fourth member of the FGA, Brian "Prime-Timex" Lee will take on the token jobber team in the WCWF, the Fabulous Hugo Brothers, single-handedly in a handicap match! Newly resurrected clone team The Fabulous Ones, [name withheld] and [name withheld], will take on the Original Midnight Express in an "Identity Crisis" match. Teddy Long's remaining stable members, Farooq and Cpl. Kirschner, will do the job to the Stallions in a match to get them over again as they claw their way back to the top. The Soultaker, with new manager/accomplice Jake Roberts, will meet Crush in what should be just about the only competitive singles match of the night. [Ted Dibiase storms into the picture.] Dibiase: You know, trying to emulate ECW's "toeing the line" style is one thing, but breaking kayfabe with every sentence is pushing things a bit, Missy. Missy: Does this mean I'm fired? Dibiase: Yes. Missy: Does that mean we're not sleeping together anymore? Dibiase: No. I have to draw the line somewhere. Missy: Damn. Oh, well, be there for Soul Doubt, coming soon! [Cut to the rafters of the Hackensack Safeway, where a familiar figure sits and broods.] [Of course, there aren't really any rafters, so he's just kind of sitting there at the top of the bleachers, brooding.] [Of course, building regulations don't allow for rafters, so he's kind of sitting there way in the back of the seats, brooding.] [Of course, this is a grocery store we're talking about here, so the back of the seats aren't much further back than the front row is. But he's still Sting, and he's still brooding.] [Of course, Sting is already under contract to the DEWF, so it's not actually him, but it does look a lot like him.] [So to summarize, we have the nWo Sting, brooding a few rows back from ringside.] [Of course, it's kind of lost all it's dramatic impact now, and besides everyone already knows that Cobra was desperate for work after being fired by WCW, so pretty much everyone in the arena knows it's not really Sting, so the whole exercise has become pretty pointless overall, and is probably just a pathetic excuse for Scott to fill time instead of just introducing the nWo Sting as the newest wrestler in the WCWF, but really that's just his style.] [Whatever. I mean, he's there, deal with it. I don't give a shit.] [Just watch the next match already and forget I said anything.] [I SAID ONTO THE NEXT MATCH ALREADY!] [QUIT READING THIS!] [STOP IT! MOVE ALONG ALREADY!] [GET THE FUC...oh, wait, I'm the narrator.] [Cue the next match.] Next Match: Local laughingstock (and that's saying something with this league) "Fearless" Frank Rizzo makes his way to the ring to get squashed. The only question is who. Lex Luger emerges from the dressing room, having just signed a new contract with Ted Dibiase, since Hogan's contract is freed up. You know the routine, whip, powerslam, Torture Rack. After the match, "Sting" comes into the ring with a baseball bat, and touches Luger with it. Luger smacks him around and tells him never to do that again. They hug after the match, promise never to yell at each other again, and decide to become tag team partners, unless Lex decides to join the FGA. But the fans still don't buy that it's really Sting, even with all the complex WCW-style characterization going on instead the ring. Well, can't blame them for trying. [Cut to one last segment before the end.] [Nailz stands in the dressing room, talking to a mysterious figure.] MF: Nailz, I'm going to be in your corner for your match against Douglas. I know you have it in you to kick his ass and take the title where it belongs. I believe in you. Nailz: Thanks, mysterious figure, that means a lot coming from you. MF: I know. With the two of us working together, this plan should turn out...spiffy. [Suddenly, the not-so-mysterious stranger turns on the light.] Hennig: Oh, man, this is so stupid. Can't we just call me Mr. Perfect again? I can't spend the rest of my career as "Mr. Spiffy!" Do you know how much of an idiot this makes me look like? [Farooq walks in, wearing his centurion gear.] Farooq: Hey, you think *you* got problems? Hennig: Good point. [Roll credits as the misery ends for another week...]
