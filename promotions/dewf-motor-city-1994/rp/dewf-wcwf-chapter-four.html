Chapter four.

Mar 7, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chapter 4. "Ladies and gentlemen, our opening contest is scheduled for one fall, and it is for the WCWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP!" The crowd, which has gotten slighty larger on word-of-mouth, cheers loudly, more relieved at having an actual match to watch than anything else. "Coming down the aisle, from parts unknown, weighing 302 pounds, here is the challenger: CRRRRRRUSH!" "Demolition" blares over the stolen ghetto blaster as Crush, along with Demolition, makes his way to the ring to challenge for the championship. And he looks pissed. "And his opponent, representing the Former Gimmick Alliance, from Hollywood, California, here is the WCWF World heavyweight champion, HULK HOGAN!" Solid round of boos for the leader of the FGA. He just has lapdog Brutus Beefcake with him, for tax reasons. Demolition each grabs a seat, as if to imply that they're not going to be interfering in this match. Brutus takes a seat also, but then he's a pandering twit so that's to be expected. Crush has some choice words for the champ, then they lock up. Hogan shoves Crush to the mat immediately, and flexes his 24 inch pythons in Crush's general direction. Beefcake is very impressed, and claps heartily to show his support. Crush immediately kips up and takes him down with a clothesline, but Hulk rolls out of the ring. Demoltion runs into action, however, and tosses him back into the ring. While he yells at the Demos, Crush runs off the ropes and nails a knee right to his back. Hogan backs up and Crush hits a belly-to-back suplex for a two-count. With Hogan down, Crush comes off and gives Hulk his own legdrop for another two-count. Crush picks him up and whips him off the ropes, going for a big boot, but Hulk ducks underneath and unloads with a series of rights that stagger the challenger. Crush quickly reacts and backdrops Hogan out of the ring to stop the momentum. Ax and Smash run over and beat on Hogan some more, until the referee threatens to DQ Crush. While the referee is busy yelling at Demolition, Brutus rolls into the ring and smashes his chair over Crush's head. Hogan rolls in as Brutus rolls out, and goes on the offense. He lays into the unconscious challenger with lefts and rights, then picks up the 300 pound man and slams him to the mat. Two-count. He drops an elbow, another, another. Two-count. He's too arrogant to hook the leg, allowing Crush to escape. he picks him up and goes for the kill, whipping him off the ropes and nailing the big boot. The crowd is booing Hogan like anything now. Hogan comes off the ropes and hits the Atomic Legdrop for 1....2....KICK OUT! Crush tosses Hogan onto the referee like a baby, then leaps to his feet, alive with energy. The crowd suddenly goes nuts, cheering on the leather-clad monster. Hogan gives him everything he's got...rights, lefts, kicks, nothing has any effect. Brutus, desperate, jumps onto the ring apron to distract the referee, allowing Hogan a chance to cheat. It backfires, as Smash comes into the ring and holds Hogan long enough for Crush give him a bit boot, right to the face. Hogan is staggered, and as Smash rolls out of the ring, Crush comes up from behind the champion and clamps the head vice on him! Hogan is going down, and Beefcake rolls into the ring again to make the save, swinging wildly with a chair. Crush releases the hold, and Beefcake smacks his own gravy train right in the head. Hogan goes down, and takes the referee with him. Crush hits a legdrop and covers, but of course the referee is out. Beefcake takes the opportunity to nail Crush in the head with the chair, before the Demos run in the ring and destroy Beefcake. The Demos chase Beefcake back to the dressing room, as both Hogan and Crush lay unconscious in the ring. Then something really odd happens. Shane Douglas comes down to the ring, carrying a contract. He revives Hogan enough to speak, then puts a pen in his hand and gets him to sign the piece of paper. Hogan, who is out of it, can't even remember his own name and signs it willingly. Douglas then picks up Crush, delivers a belly-to-belly suplex, and rolls Hogan on top of him! He gives the ref some smelling salts, who then rolls over and counts 1.....2.....3! "The winner, and still WCWF World Champion....Hollywood Hulk Hogan!" Hogan claims his belt, on wobbly legs, and is about to leave when Douglas drops the bomb. "Hey, Mr. Champion, I'd just like to point out that you signed a title defense against me for right now!" Hogan looks stunned, not really remembering any of it. Ted Dibiase comes running out of the dressing room, relishing any chance to get rid of Hogan. He takes the contract from Douglas and looks it over. "Hell, it looks good to me. Get your ass back in here, Hulk!" Hogan, not really knowing what to think, rolls back in as the ref rings the bell for the second title match of the night. Douglas blindsides him as he rolls in, kicking away at the helpless champion until he lays still on the mat. He picks up the lifeless champ, delivers a flawless belly-to-belly, and gets an easy three-count on the beaten, now ex-champion. "The winner...AND NEEEEWWWWWW WCWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION....The Franchise, SHANE DOUGLAS!" Dibiase and Douglas hug, as everyone is glad Hogan has been knocked off. Douglas grabs the title belt and gets Francine to strap it around his waist. Then Dibiase steps back and thinks for a minute. "You aren't going to spit on this one and throw it down, are you?" Douglas considers the idea for a minute, then thinks better of it. Let's head back to the dressing room, where we'll hear from Ted Dibiase. [Toad waits for Dibiase.] Toad: Your thoughts on what just happened, Ted? Ted: Hey, the only good Hulk is a dead Hulk, I always say. Congratulations to Shane Douglas, even if he did win the title in an underhanded fashion. Toad: Well said. So what's the word on a possible WCWF pay-per-view special? Ted: Well, we're still working on a TV deal outside of cable access, but we will have a house show this week in the Highland High School gymnasium, where hopefully we can get a match signed between Douglas and Crush. Toad: A wise move. I've heard rumors about a tag title, also. What's going on with that. Ted: Glad you asked, Toad. We're having an 8-team single elimination tournament to crown new WCWF tag team champions. Off the top of my head, the brackets look like this: Demolition v. Hulk Hogan & Brutus Beefcake The Nasty Boys v. Vinnie Vegas & The Diamond Studd The Stallions v. The "Original" Midnight Express Adam Bomb & The Warlord v. A Mystery Team Toad: Did you say a Mystery Team? Ted: Yep. Old friends of mine. I spent a lot of money on free dinners and hookers getting these guys to sign with the WCWF, and I think it'll be worth it to see the roof blow off the gymnasium. Plus, as a result of Teddy Long's little segment last week, Nailz will meet Farooq. Toad: Any other matches signed? Ted: Um, no. We don't exactly have that big of a talent roster yet... Toad: That's to be expected. Thanks for the time, Mr. Dibiase. Let's go now to Bloomington, Minnesota, where Missy Hyatt is at the Mall of America conducting the great WCWF talent search! [We cut to Missy, where a big lineup of ex-stars waits] Missy: Thanks, Toad! We're increasing our ranks by leaps and bounds right now, what with all the washed up ex-wrestlers left in the cold by the establishment of the DEWF. So far, the following people have signed on as of today... Doink the Clown Duke Droese The Berzerker Randy Rose & Dennis Condrey, the Original Midnight Express "Ravishing" Rick Rude Terry Taylor Big Bossman The Trooper Cpl. Kirschner And more are still to come, guys! With our first house show only a few days away, things are really looking up for the WCWF! We're becoming known as the federation that will give *anybody* a second chance. I'm not sure if that's a good thing...oh, well, it sounds like it. Back to you, Toad! [We cut back to Toad, who is indeed standing in the dressing room with Cpl. Kirschner] Toad: Well, Corporal, what have you been up to since you disappeared from wrestling all those years ago? CK: I moved to Montana, Toad. Met some really great guys who had good ideas about the way the military *needs* to be run... Toad: Uh...right. Maybe we should move onto something else... [And now, another exciting episode of "Perspectives", with Teddy Long!] [The theme from "Shaft" plays in the background.] Teddy: Good evening, you rat-soup eating motherfuckers. The name so graciously tossed on my black face by the white racists is Theodore R Long, but you can call me sir. [Suddenly swivels in his chair to face other camera] Teddy: You know, I'd like to talk for a moment about a subject that has come up a little more often than usual around here lately: Anal rape. Now then, Mr. Kevin "Please sir can I have another" Kelly, better known as Nailz, has, I'm sure, taken it up the backdoor quite a few times in his tenure in the state prison... [Suddenly, the back wall of the exceedingly cheap set is ripped down, and Nailz, smashes through to go after Teddy, swinging his nightstick.] [No, not that one.] Nailz: NAILZ SMASH! [Grabs Teddy by the throat and is about to chokeslam him through the desk, when Farooq runs in and smashes a chair into his back.] [Teddy stands and cheers while Farooq brings him to his knees, then onto the floor by battering him with the chair.] Teddy: ALL PRAISE BE TO ALLAH! Farooq: ALL PRAISE BE TO ALLAH! [The all-black police run onto the set and handcuff the ex-convict, then drag him off without even questioning him, after seeing the "Reverend" Teddy Long there. One of them raises his fist in the salute of the Nation of Domination as they leave. Sirens can be heard in the distance as Nailz is driven off god-knows-where.] And in the shadows, the Stranger watches. And remembers. He nods to himself about something only he knows, and then falls back into the shadows again, confident about what he must do to ensure justice is served. Suffice it to say, it won't be pleasant for somebody in that room. To be continued...
