Soul Doubt.

Mar 7, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Author's Note: The following isn't really meant to be funny, so don't expect it to be. Just to warn you.] [We open in the still-being-investigated cloning tanks of the late Vincent B. Baats. Mulder and Scully have been on the job for three weeks now, with no new leads. Yet something is still fishy...] Scully: This makes no sense, Mulder. Baats was a psychiatrist, not a geneticist. There had to be someone else behind the operation, someone who could give him the boost he needed to do this kind of full-scale cloning. Mulder: They just cloned that sheep last week, Scully. No human being could develop this kind of scientfic advance two years in advance of that. It's just not possible. Scully: What are you thinking, Mulder? Mulder: I think we're dealing with something supernatural. [Suddenly, an FBI agent runs into the room.] Agent: Scully! Mulder! We've found a hidden room! There's another tank inside...and I think you'd better take a look at it! [The three run into the newly found alcove.] [A huge, recently used tank sits in the center like a throne. It has nearly four times the equipment hooked up to it that the others do.] Mulder: Amazing. Scully: The genetic code is still programmed into the computer. I can check who this was. [Scully punches in some numbers and waits.] Scully: Oh, my god. Mulder: I've heard of that guy. Scully: Someone had better call the WCWF right now. They've got a major problem on their hands. [A voice comes from the doorway, conveniently backlit so we can't see who it is.] Voice: No, you've got the problem. My plans for revenge were too thorough to let something as trivial as death stop me. So when it looked like I was going to be on my deathbed soon, I hired that idiot Baats to run these cloning chambers for me. But I don't need them anymore or him, or you for that matter, and now, finally, I will have my revenge! Mulder: Did you read a lot of comic books as a kid? Voice: Goodbye, Mr. Mulder. Ms. Scully. And whoever you are. Agent: Agent Morgan. Richard Morgan. Voice: Whatever. [The mystery man pushes a button, sealing up the room from the outside, then runs off into the night as a timer flashes "10...9...8...7...6...5..."] [Which, oddly enough, coincides with the HBO network's countdown to the start of FGA: Soul Doubt...] The following is an HBO exclusive presentation. A "TV-M" rating appears in the upper corner. LIVE! from the WCWF Studios in Hackensack, New Jersey, it's FGA Soul Doubt! [We open in the ring, with WCWF President Ted Dibiase and FGA manager Roddy Piper.] Dibiase: I can't believe we're actually on a major cable network! Even the WWF and WCW only made it as far as TNT and USA! We're on HBO! Piper: That's right, baby! This is cable! We can do anything we want! And just to prove it, I've got a special treat for all the menfolk out there. [A loud explosion can be heard in the background.] Dibiase: Who requisitioned fireworks? We can't afford that. Piper: I didn't order any fireworks. [None of the technical crew know either.] Dibiase: Well, whatever, you were saying, Roddy? Piper: I've convinced a certain former WWF Women's champion to pose, completely naked, for the fans here tonight! [A roar goes up from the male chauvenist pig segment of the crowd, which is pretty much everyone.] Dibiase: If this is some cheap Fabulous Moolah joke, I'll fire you on the spot. Piper: Don't be ridiculous, Ted. I have much more class than that. [Bertha Faye emerges...totally naked.] [Everyone in the audience simultaneously yaks in their popcorn buckets.] Dibiase: Oh, god...I can hear the ratings points falling. Piper: She's all woman, that Bertha. [Nudges Dibiase.] I think she winked at me. Dibiase: No, that was just her fat jiggling. Piper: Oh, I think you're right. Well, at least this'll seperate the men from the boys! Dibiase: Let's go down to our ring announcer... "Weird Al" Yankovic's "I Think I'm a Clone Now" plays over the PA. "This is our opening contest, scheduled for one fall! Coming down the aisle, from the genetic material of others, at a total combined DNA of 43 chromosomes...[name withheld] and [name withheld], the New Fabulous Ones!" [name withheld] and [name withheld] sprint into the ring ala the Rockers and greet the crowd. "And their opponents..." The music stops, as we cut back to the dressing room. The Original Midnight Express are lying flat out on the dressing room floor, knocked unconscious and stripped of their ring attire. They appear to be suffering from tennis racket wounds. "Um..." Suddenly, Jim Cornette bursts out of the dressing room, looking slim and trim and very obnoxious. "Ladies and...whatever you mutants pass for...please welcome to this two-bit federation the only team with any class...Beautiful Bobby and Sweet Stan...the REAL MIDNIGHT EXPRESS!" The crowd pops huge, but no one comes out. Resident WCWF psychiatrist Sigmund Fraud, last seen councilling Virgil two years ago, comes out of the audience to talk to Cornette. They have a one-sided conversation, and eventually Fraud convinces Cornette to sit down at the announcers' table. Evidentally the Fabs win by default. Dibiase: Uh, what happened out there, Jimbo? Cornette: I keep forgetting...I don't manage the Midnights anymore. They fired me about 7 years ago. It's kind of hard to let go, you know. Dibiase: You still manage the Heavenly Bodies, right? Cornette: No, they fired me too. Dr. Freud... Dibiase: That's Fraud. Cornette: ...says I have issues with this that I need to resolve. Meanwhile, the Original Midnights have made their way down to ringside, looking pretty pissed after the blindside. Cornette: Oh, man, guys, I can explain...I'm on medication, and I forgot that I'm not a manager anymore...I thought it was 1988 again, I'm sorry! The Express doesn't look convinced. Condrey grabs him by the lapels and has some choice words about his mother. Piper: Hey, watch what you say about that boy's mother. He may be a racist pig, but leave family out of it. In response, Rose simply shoves Piper to the guardrail, then they continue the verbal assault on Cornette. Big mistake. Before they can even turn around, the MVC are at ringside, and in the Express' face. That's not a good place to be if you're either Condrey or Rose. They decide to beat a hasty retreat into the ring, but Shane Douglas and Brian Lee, the rest of the FGA make their way to the ring and surround the Express, who look to be peeing themselves right about now. Dibiase: You know, on a lighter note, I bet Hulk Hogan just *wished* he had this kind of talent backing him up when he was running rampant over our little federation. Piper: Don't get me started on him... The Express begin apologizing in several different languages, but it's for naught as the beating begins, 4-on-1. Pretty soon, even Jim Cornette has gathered the balls to get into the ring and whale away with the tennis racket to make the attack 4 1/2-on-2. Piper is attempting to look unbiased, but the pom-poms and "Go FGA!" shirt he's wearing seem to give him away. Finally, the Usual Gang of Idiots (tm [name withheld]..hey, wait, that's me!) run into the ring and break up the proceedings before the FGA can inflict anymore damage on the Express. Cornette gets in one final kick before leaving to go sit at the announcer's table again. Cornette: HAH! That showed 'em! Dibiase: I just bet that they're quaking in their boots, Jim. Let's head back for an interview with the challenger in tonight's main event, shall we? [Toad Pedophile waits in the back with Nailz and manager Curt Hennig.] Toad: Well, Nailz, tonight you challenge Shane Douglas for the title. This has got to be the most exciting moment of your life. Nailz: Fuck you, bitch. Toad: Right...and who says prison reform doesn't work? Hennig: What Mr. Kelly was trying to say, Toad, is that he's not afraid of Mr. Douglas and he's rather disgusted by the way he's sold out to the corporate sector, and there are those who would rather not see that happen. Toad: And speaking of selling out, Curt, there's that very interesting clause in your contract that... [Suddenly, the camera goes out and shouts can be heard over the static. They come back on again, and Curt Hennig and Nailz are left alone.] Hennig: Nothing to see here. NOW SHUT OFF THAT FUCKING CAMERA! [Nailz starts smacking around the operator.] Nailz: SHUT IT OFF! HE SAID SHUT IT OFF! [We can see Toad being beaten and dragged off by people who look vaguely familiar, but I can't put my finger on it before the camera is shut off again. Weird.] Dibiase: What the hell was that? What's going on back there? [Dibiase gets up from the announcing position, leaving Piper and Cornette to do the job on the next match.] [The Fabulous Hugo Brothers, Barry and Jeff, make their way to the ring.] "This contest is scheduled for..." But Brian Lee rushes the ring before the match even starts, grabs both Hugos, chokeslams them, picks up Jeff and powerbombs him onto Barry, and covers for the easy pin.] Piper: Well, that was fun. The crowd applauds politely, until the masked man who nailed Tommy Sledge with the lead pipe emerges from the audience and clocks Lee with it, too. Lee is out in the middle of the ring and the masked man goes nuts with it, smashing it over Lee repeatedly until the MVC are forced to charge into the ring and make the save, taking vicious shots with the lead pipe, until everyone is lying on the ground rolling in pain, and the masked man runs into the crowd...and disappears. Literally. He's just there one minute, and fades into the crowd the next. If Ted Dibiase had been there, he would have nailed his identity in about two seconds, but he's not, so that leaves everyone pretty confused. [Meanwhile...] [Backstage, Dibiase comes charging back, looking for Pedophile. Now Hennig and Nailz seem to have vanished for the moment as well. Shane Douglas emerges from his dressing room, looking a bit confused.] Douglas: Ted, some guy just delivered this package right to my dressing room. Dibiase: Is it ticking? Douglas: Well, it's not from my "Sexual Lubricant of the Month Club", I know that for sure. Dibiase: Well, you might as well open it up. I'll take death over bankrupcy any day... [Douglas rips it open.] Douglas: What the hell? [Inside is a one-way ticket to Orlando, Florida, and a Disneyland brochure.] Douglas: This is fucking weird, Ted. I don't think I want to wrestle tonight! Dibiase: I don't think I want this card to continue tonight, either. But I don't think we have much of a choice in the matter. Someone's obviously out to get us, and I'm going to finish this card, dammit! [Shane seems less than sure, however, and goes back to his dressing room to cower before his title defense.] [Back to ringside, where the Stallions and Farooq/Kirschner are already in the midst of their match.] [Dibiase wanders back to the announce position in a bit of a daze.] Dibiase: Did anything weird happen while I was gone? Cornette: Define weird. Dibiase: Well... [Just then, Nailz charges into the ring, nightstick in hand, and totally cleans house on everything, clearing the ring and assaulting everyone within range. Curt Hennig runs in after him to try and calm him down.] Nailz: Where is the cowardly son of a bitch? Bring out Douglas! I'll kick his ass right now! Dibiase: Is it too much to ask that we finish a match tonight? [Hennig calms him down and they leave, and Douglas doesn't answer the challenge, because he's busy pissing himself with fear back in his dressing room.] Let's go back to the dressing rooms for a word with the Soultaker. [Toad Pedophile waits with Jake Roberts and the Soultaker.] Toad: It's a big night for you guys tonight, as you face Crush. Soultaker: He's back! Toad: Excuse me? Soultaker: HE'S BACK! I KNEW HE'D RETURN FOR ME! I KNEW HE HAD NOT FORSAKEN ME! Toad: What's he babbling about? Jake: I'm not sure... [Soultaker runs off in ecstasy, god knows where, leaving Jake standing there holding the bag. Literally.] Toad: Uh...can you still wrestle? "This next contest is scheduled for one fall!" Demolition's music blasts over the PA. "Coming down the aisle, from Kona, Hawaii, weighing 320 pounds...Crush!" Crush storms out of the dressing room, ready for the fight of his life. "And his opponent..." Jake Roberts cautiously makes his way down to ringside, 30 pounds overweight and 10 years past his prime. "Um...I'm afraid Soultaker seems to have disappeared so I guess I'll have to take his place..." Crush seems disappointed, but charges in with a vicious clothesline anyway. Roberts stumbles out of the corner, gets press-slammed, legdropped, and pinned in very, very short order. "Here is your winner...CRUSH!" Dibiase: Any word on the ratings? Cornette: We're neck and neck with the yoga show that the community access channel replaced us with tonight. Dibiase: Define neck and neck. Cornette: They're only beating us by two ratings points! Dibiase: What do they have? Cornette: A 2.0... Dibiase: *Groans* Make it stop... "Due to the injury sustained by the Miracle Whip Violence Connection earlier tonight, Demolition retains the tag team title by forfeit!" [The crowd cheers.] "And now, the main event of the evening! This contest is for the WCWF World heavyweight championship!" Dibiase: Please, god, at least let there be a title match tonight... [Cut back to the dressing room of Shane Douglas.] [Douglas is cowering in the corner, cradling the World title belt for protection. There is a knock at the door.] Douglas: Who is it? [The door explodes inwards with a single kick, shattering into 15 or 20 pieces on the floor.] Douglas: Sorry, I didn't quite catch that... Soultaker: Good evening, I'll just take that title belt right now and save you the trouble of having to go down to ringside and lose it yourself. Douglas: What... Soultaker: Never mind. [We cut to Tommy Sledge running out of a cab and into the arena, having just regained his memory a few minutes ago.] Sledge: [Yelling to someone behind him.] Quick, we have to hurry. SHANE! SHANE! YOU'VE GOT TO GET OUT OF THERE! [He makes it to Shane's dressing room, only to find Shane beaten to within an inch of his life.] Sledge: I'm too late. [He runs down to ringside, with his unknown friend behind him.] [Nailz is celebrating his World title victory, as Shane fails to show, and Soultaker presents him with the belt while Curt Hennig looks on and applauds.] Sledge: Stop! I know what's going on here, and you won't get away with it! Dibiase: What the hell's he doing here? Piper: I dunno, but it sure beats the hell out of Uncensored... Sledge: This is all a plot to destroy the WCWF again! This was planned all along, all of it! They wanted Shane out of the way! Hennig: I don't know who you are, but I'm getting sick of you real quick! Sledge: Oh, yeah, then what's this? [Before Hennig can react, Sledge lunges forward and reaches into Hennig's jacket...pulling out the black mask used by the mysterious attacker!] Hennig: That doesn't prove anything! Sledge: There's more! You're not even Curt Hennig! [The crowd seems a bit shocked at this, to say the least.] Hennig: Then if I'm not, who is? Sledge: HIM! [The *real* Curt Hennig emerges from the dressing room, looking all-in-all a bit confused.] Sledge: I knew he never would have agreed to manage again with his insurance policy. He can't even get close to the ring without voiding it, so it couldn't have been him! Dibiase: OH, NO, NOT HIM! [What Dibiase is reacting to is "Curt Hennig" pulling off his very elaborate disguise, revealing, who else...] Dibiase: OH SHIT...THE DOPPLEGANGER!!!!! Doppie: That's right! My chicken business fell through, so I sold it to someone who was more appreciative of my talents. Dibiase: What about all that spiel you gave about your niece Tina and how you wanted to spend time with your family? Doppie: The little bitch ran away with some greasy biker last year. They stole all my money before they left. What else was left but a heel turn? Dibiase: Oh, well, this can't get possibly get any worse... Sledge: Yes, it can. You see, the clause in the contract that revealed the whole plot to me stated that 50% of the money being paid to the Doppleganger would be provided by [name withheld]. Dibiase: You don't mean... [The lights go out, and when they return...] Dibiase: OH SHIT...THE DRUID!!!! Druid: I prefer the Master, actually. Dibiase: Sorry...OH SHIT...THE MASTER!!!! Master: Thank you. That's right! My plot to destroy this two-bit federation may have failed four years ago, but it won't this time! Now I have your greatest enemy on my side, as well as the Soultaker, as well as Nailz! *I* was behind the clones, and I cloned myself to ensure my survival! Dibiase: This can't possibly get any worse... Piper: So who provides the other 50% of the money? [The lights go out, and when they return...] Dibiase: OH SHIT...[name withheld]!!!! [name withheld]: That's right! I'm going to take back what's rightfully mine...the CWF! Dibiase: It's WCWF now, actually. [name withheld]: SHUT UP! I don't care! You pulled your little stock swindle in 1993 and forced me into retirement, and now I'm back for revenge! I made billions on the stock market while I was gone, and I've waited...and plotted...until the moment when I could put you out of business for good! Piper: You're not too good at making friends, are you Teddy Boy? Dibiase: Leave me alone. [Dibiase is in tears as [name withheld]'s evil team of fiends laughs it up in the ring.] Dibiase: It's over...it's all over... Sledge: Uh, are there any more shocking announcements, because the meter is still running on my cab... [Soultaker smashes the camera, as we cut to static...] To be continued...????
