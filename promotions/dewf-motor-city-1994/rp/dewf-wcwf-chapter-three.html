Chapter three.

Mar 7, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chapter 3. [We open in the law offices of Dewey, Screwum and Howe. Dewey sits at the head of a huge desk, with Screwum and Howe apparently sitting off-camera.] Dewey: Ahem. We are the official legal representatives for Mr. Dibiase... Howe: NO WE'RE NOT! Dewey: Be quiet. Recently, it has come to our attention that accusations of extreme silliness have been levied against the fine federation of the WCWF. Howe: NO THEY HAVEN'T! Dewey: Be quiet. We find these charges to be completely ridiculous and assert that the WCWF is a very serious federation in all ways, as you will certainly be able to tell by the serious nature of the first bit, a serious documentary on the life of Ted Dibiase by the serious producers at the Arts and Entertainment Network. Howe: HE'S LYING! Dewey: Be quiet. So without further adieu, we present "Biography"... Howe: NO WE DON'T! Dewey: Be quiet. Starring Ted Dibiase... [The screen fades out, and fades back in to A&E] You are watching A&E. Time well spent. [The opening sequence for "Biography" is played.] Good evening. I'm Jack Perkins, and this is Biography. Tonight, we go into the world of professional wrestling to present the life and times of one of the most influential men in that great sport: Ted Dibiase. Dibiase, the owner, president and promoter of the newest federation in town, so to speak, the WCWF, has been heavily involved in wrestling his whole life. Dibiase was born in the quiet town of Omaha, Nebraska, as the son of former wrestler "Iron" Mike Dibiase. Mike Dibiase was tragically killed when his car accidently blew up in the parking lot after mysteriously picking up a live mine from the road at one point. Even more mysterious was the note attached saying "Die, father, die, you stupid bastard so I can collect the insurance money! Love, Ted." What the meaning of this cryptic note was may never be known, but what is known is that Dibiase had a multi-million dollar insurance policy, all of which went to his loving wife. Unfortunately, his loving wife was tragically killed after bashing her own head in with a meat mallet, then accidentally severing both her arms and throwing herself off a nearby dock. It was of course immediately ruled suicide, but some people are still baffled by certain details of the death to this day. Can't imagine why. This left young Ted Dibiase with a fortune exceeding $15 million at a tender age. Deciding that professional wrestlers were a cowardly and superstitious lot, he decided to adopt the alter ego of wrestler "Leapin'" Ted Dibiase at age 16 after seeing Lanny Poffo fly into his bedroom window one night. Designing the Tedcave and the Tedmobile, Dibiase made his debut in the fledgling CWF as a co-investor and part-time wrestler. In his debut match, he was savaged by Stan Hansen and was left really pissed off. He decided to use his money for evil instead of good, coming up with many evil schemes to take over the CWF, most of which were foiled by the wrestler known as the Doppleganger... [Suddenly, the camera zooms out and we see Dibiase toss the remote control at the TV, shattering the screen.] Dibiase: DAMMIT! I bought that stupid network specifically so they'd show "Biography" specials about the WCWF, and *this* is the kind of biased journalism they show! I want them biased *towards* me, not against me! And why does that freak Doppleganger even warrant a mention on my show, anyway? [Virgil walks in, eating a chicken sandwich from "Chickens We've Fried", the chain owned by the Doppleganger.] Virgil: Mm-mm, Mr. Dibiase, this here sandwich is so good that I almost forget I'm a racial stereotype sometimes! Dibiase: *Sighs* It's all about product placement and commercialism these days. I'm glad *we're* above that sort of thing. [NOT ANYMOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOORE...] The following message has been paid for by the Former Gimmick Alliance. [Vinnie Vegas and the Diamond Studd come onscreen, wearing "FGA" t-shirts and drinking coffee out of "FGA" mugs.] Vinnie: Yo, Studd-man, how's the FGA stock doing this morning? Studd: [Whips out a copy of Wall Street Weekly.] Oh, man, we're getting killed. Vinnie: Oh, well, then I guess we'd better call the Undertaker! [Brian Lee walks in, wearing an outfit almost like, but not entirely the same as, the Undertaker's. And an FGA baseball hat, of course.] Underfaker: Rest...comfortably. Vinnie: No, man, it's "rest...in peace." Underfaker: Really? Because I'm going for more of an Oscar Wilde interpretation of the part. That whole "doom and gloom" thing just doesn't work for me. I mean, what's my motivation? Who I am, really? Why does the Undertaker undertake? [They all think about that for a minute.] Studd: Whoa, heavy stuff. [Checks watch] Hey, this spot is costing us almost five bucks a minute! Quick, wrap it up so we can still break even. Vinnie: Uh...buy the damn shirt! Only $29.95 each, plus shipping and handling. [Suddenly, Hulk Hogan bursts in.] Hogan: Sorry I'm late...WHATCHA GONNA DO WHEN... [Camera breaks off, with the FGA logo and mailing address on the screen.] The preceding message was paid for by the Former Gimmick Alliance. [The screen suddenly cuts back into Dewey, Screwum and Howe] Dewey: Stop, stop! This is clearly silly and is not helping our case any. We will seek legal measures if the next bit isn't in dead earnest. Howe: WHO'S THIS GUY ERNEST? Dewey: Be quiet. If we don't get entertaining pretty soon, the viewers will switch to PBS...OH, NO, DON'T! [The screen switches to PBS. Alistair Cooke sits in a big comfy chair.] Cooke: Good evening. Welcome to Masterpiece Theatre. Tonight, we present a Shakespearean classic, "Macbeth", as performed by Doink The Clown. [We see the set of the final confrontation between Macbeth and Macduff. Doink walks onto the set, in full clown paraphanalia, with a crown on his head. His big red shoes squeak as he walks.] Doink: Lay on, Macduff! [Squeak, squeak.] [Several of the actors on the set and Macduff himself burst into laughter. The director yells "Cut!" and looks might pissed.] Director: Alright, Doink, this the 34th take of the day, and you know why? Because your damn clown suit is disrupting the proceedings! Doink: I, sir, am a serious ac-tor [stressing the second syllable] who is merely re-interpreting a stale role. Director: YOU'RE A FREAKING CLOWN! Doink: That is slander, sir. My clowning days are behind me. I strive for greatness in the theatre. [He squirts the director with his flower.] Sorry. Reflex action. Director: YOU'RE FIRED! Bring on the next actor. Doink: Harrumph. [Ahmed Johnson walks in to read for the part.] Ahmed: Elkjfjkj odkamod doaokjsdao! Director: Cut! BRILLIANT! Very abstract. Have you considered a career in the theatre... [Closing credits for Masterpiece Theatre.] [We cut to Shane Douglas, who has been standing in the ring, ranting to whoever will listen to him, since the show began.] Douglas: ...and *then* when I was in WCW, the other wrestlers used to plot against me all day, and they'd have these secret meetings where *everyone* was invited except me... [The Director is screaming that he's on.] Douglas: ...and...what? I'm on. [He whips around and looks at the camera] Hello WCWF! As you probably all know by now, I got ripped off, as usual, by the DEWF and now I'm slumming down here in the minors. I'm surprised Ted Turner isn't running that federation, too. In fact, I'll bet that Ted Turner is sitting in this audience right now! [He leaves the ring and grabs a homeless guy who is sitting in front row to get warm.] Douglas: You! That fake beard and missing leg can't fool The Franchise! You're here spying on me! You're Ted Turner! [He tosses the homeless man down again upon seeing a bag lady sitting next to him.] Douglas: And you! YOU'RE VINCE MACMAHON! That fat lady costume isn't fooling anyone, you money-grubbing scam artist! [Slaps around the bag lady.] You think you can hold back the Franchise? Well, the way *I* see it... [Slams the microphone into her head. She juices all over the place as the homeless guy is screaming for an ambulance.] Douglas: Hah! I guess I showed everyone here who the paranoid one is. [Looks around quickly] Damn, I think that AWF guy is here too. [Pulls Francine close to him.] They have spies everywhere, babe. We can't be too careful. [Looks critically at Francine.] Douglas: Hey! Aren't you Paul Heyman? [Francine rolls her eyes as we go to Toad Pedophile in the dressing room.] Toad: We're here in the dressing room of Crush, who is praying to prepare for his big, but totally inexplicable, title match with "Hollywood" Hogan which will be next week, Hogan's movie schedule permitting. [Cut to a quick shot of Hogan arguing with a low-budget porno director about his role in the movie and how he can be worked in as an ex-wrestler.] Toad: Crush is truly a role model for all the young folks watching today. So parents, bring your children close to the TV and see what a good, honest, Amish upbringing can produce. [Crush walks in, dressed in black leather with blood-covered studs and a shirt that says "Kill 'Em All, Let God Sort 'Em Out." He is cleaning an AK-47 and chewing tobacco.] Toad: Uh... Crush: Get the fuck out of my dressing room, you motherfucking little panty waste. Toad: Rather, uh, different attitude you have this week. [Crush grabs Toad by the throat and slams him against the wall.] Crush: I had a little chat with my pals, Demolition, and I'm a whole new guy. They showed me what an asshole I was being, trying to cram that peace and love shit down everyone's throat. I'm going to obliterate everyone in this shithole and then do the same to the DEWF! Toad: Can we negotiate? Crush: You can beg for forgiveness on your knees, worm! Toad: I can work with that.... [Crush drops him.] Toad: Have you thought about seeing a therapist? Crush: I saw one already. He said I needed to channel my agression in more constructive ways and find my happy place. Toad: So what did you do? Crush: This. [Picks up Toad and tosses him out of the dressing room and into a garbage can located outside the door. Adam Bomb, who was sitting near the garbage can playing with his Power Ranger doll, rescues Toad from the garbage and gives Crush a very mean look. Toad passes out as we go to Teddy Long's new show, "Perspectives".] [Teddy sits at a desk with a big iron grating in front of him.] Teddy: Good evening, you white racist pigs. I'm Theodore R. Long, sitting here behind iron bars because that's where The Man would like me to be, instead of out here speaking my mind and showing you ignorant white folks the true way. My first guest this week is yet another in the never-ending line of racist motherfuckas who would prefer to see my black ass tossed out onto the street instead of working at a good job with equal pay. Please welcome the former convict, Kevin Kelly! [Kelly, better known as Nailz, strolls out in a suit and tie, looking very presentable, while Teddy stands and politely applauds.] Teddy: You look about as good as an ugly white bitch can look, Kevin. Kelly: Uh...thanks, I think. Teddy: So, what have you been up to since the non-stop anal rape ended a few years ago? Kelly: I have to object to that, Teddy. That kind of stuff never really happened in the prison I was at... Teddy: Oh, really? Well, it should have, because the black man has been getting done up the ass for CENTURIES in America, and it's high time that some of you white boys learned what it was like! Do you have a good job? Kelly: Yeah. I've been running my own business in... Teddy: And how many African-American employees do you have? I'll bet none, because you look like just another rich, fat, ugly white racist pig! Kelly: I... Teddy: Shut up, you ho! Get off my stage before anymore of your kind oppresses my kind! Kelly: NOW LOOK HERE... Teddy: HELP! HELP! MANIAC WHITE MAN ON DRUGS! HE'S GOING TO KILL ME! POLICE! [About 14 black policemen run into the studio and begin beating Kelly with nightsticks. The cameraman continues videotaping.] Kelly: Can't we all just...OW!...get along? *OOF!* [Suddenly, Kelly grabs the nightstick from one of them and goes nuts, smashing it into all of their heads. He's so angry that when he talks, he sounds like Pat Patterson with a voice distortion box.] Nailz: That's right! I tried to be a good citizen, but now I have to go back to doing what I do best...hurting people. Now where's the Big Bossman? [Everyone in the studio shrugs] Nailz: Oh. Okay. Well, I'll hurt someone bad, that's for sure. Teddy: This has been Theodore R. Long, saying that all you white pigs can go back to watching Chandler and Monica on "Friends" now while the rest of the black folks in this country slave away in the ghettos for half of minimum wage! [Turns to producer.] Teddy: Damn, it's hot in here. Can I get a mineral water? With a twist of lime, please. Nailz: SHOW'S OVER! [Smashes nightstick into the camera, and we cut back to Dewey, Screwum, and Howe one last time.] Dewey: It has come to my attention that our very name is silly. I was not aware of this until my collegues brought this point up. [Howe is giggling away at the joke like a 4-year old] Dewey: Yes, quite. We therefore declare this show a lost cause and will go back to the Vaudeville circuit, where people know the value of *serious* entertainment. Howe: [Makes farting noise with his hands.] Dewey: Oh, be quiet. [The screen cuts to static as we end the insanity for another week.] To be continued...
