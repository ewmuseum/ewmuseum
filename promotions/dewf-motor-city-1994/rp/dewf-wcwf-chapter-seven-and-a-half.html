Chapter seven and a half.

Mar 7, 1997, 12:00:00 AM

WCWF - Chapter 7.5 Tommy Sledge, PWI, in "Dead Men Don't Wear Ring Robes" - or - "The Timekeeper Always Rings Twice" My name is Sledge. Tommy Sledge. The sign on my door says "Private Investigator", but really I'm a bit more specialized than that. I'm a Private Wrestling Investigator, which I should probably explain about. You see, my father, my he rest in peace, was a professional wrestler. So I grew up meeting some pretty weird people. And gradually, I grew to like 'em. I even entertained notions of being a wrestling at one point. Until I actually got into the ring and got my ass kicked...that set me straight pretty quick. But I still know them better than anyone else in the PI field, and I put that to use solving cases for the weirdos who inevitably call me when they hear about my specialty. Of course, business has been slow ever since the WCWF originally folded back in 1995, but when Ted Dibiase re-opened it earlier this year, I just knew my phone would be ringing again. I wasn't disappointed. A few days ago, I got a call from Shane Douglas, the WCWF World champion. Classic paranoid, hates everyone, real asshole. Fun at parties, though. As I answered the phone, I heard his music on the other end, then he started talking. Wrestlers are the only people in the whole of western civilization who use entrance music for their PHONE CALLS. But I'm used to weird shit like that -- it comes with the territory. "Mr Sledge," he began, "I believe you investigated my apparent murder two years ago." "Shane, you know that's not in continuity anymore. You never even went nuts according to the revised history." "Oh, right, I forgot." "It's alright. Now, what can I do for you?" "First of all, I'm facing Nailz on the FGA's first show, Soul Doubt -- on HBO...call your cable company -- and I need dirt on him." "What can you tell me about him?" "He's a psychotic ex-convict who still dresses like he's in prison, speaks with a voice distortion box all the time, and carries a nightstick stolen from the Big Bossman which he uses to bludgeon his opponents into unconsciousness with." "That's a tough one, Shane. Sounds like he's squeaky clean." "Okay, well he's going to managed by a guy named Curt Hennig..." I tuned him out right there as images of Hennig flashed through my mind. That bastard injured my father in the ring so badly that he had to retire. So what if he was 72 years old -- he was in the prime of his career! Look at Ric Flair... "Hennig? That asshole? I'll nail his ass to the wall, Shane, don't you worry about a thing! I'll have enough dirt on him to fill a cemetary plot." "I knew I could count on you, Mr. Sledge." "Now, about payment..." The exit music drowned out the response. Cheap bastards, all of 'em. Figures -- give 'em a merchandising contract and he'll become tighter than a Scotsman. My first stop was WCWF Central, in Hackensack, New Jersey. God, what an ugly town. I've been everywhere, and I can say, without any exageration, that Hackensack is the ugliest city in the entire universe, including Calgary, Alberta. I saw Toad Pedophile sleeping on a couch in between cards. He was mumbling something in his sleep. "No, Mr. Patterson, that's bad touching...I'll sue you..." Suddenly he woke up. "Oh. Hi, what did you hear?" "Nothing incriminating." "Good. So, what can I do for you on behalf of the WCWF?" "Why were you sleeping on the couch?" "It's better than where I was living before." It must have been a cardboard box, because WCWF HQ was a *dump.* It was an abandoned grocery store, a Safeway from the looks of it, and obviously the owner couldn't afford a maid. Food wrappers, jock straps and pizza boxes tossed everywhere...it was like a bachelor pad from hell. "Um, right." I continued. "I'm here to enquire about one Curt Hennig. Is he an employee of the WCWF?" "Oh yes. We just hired him a couple of weeks ago. For legal reasons he had to change his name from Mr. Perfect to Mr. Spiffy, but he's an employee." "Do you think you could discuss his...contractual obligations with me?" Toad gave me a sly look, and stuck out his hand. I sighed and peeled off a twenty to see if that would jog his memory. "Oh, money...I thought you wanted..." He stopped short, gathered himself, then continued as I tried not to barf, "uh, well, it's a standard WCWF contract, in that he waives his right to any money we'd normally pay him in exchange for a percentage of the gate, since we're pretty much on the verge of bankrupcy." Interesting. "Anything...incriminating in there?" Toad had to think about that one. "Um, I'm not sure. CURT!" Hennig joined us. I shot him a dirty look. "Curt, could I see your contract?" He pulled out his contract, which of course all wrestlers carry with them at all times, and Toad and I pored over it for incriminating evidence. "Hey," Hennig pointed out, "that clause on page three is pretty bad." "You know," I reminded him, "we're trying to dig up dirt on you here. Helping us is not really a good idea." He realized his error and left, but he had a point. That clause on page three *was* pretty bad. Now all I had to do was visit some old friends to capitalize on it... The first friend I visited was DEWF Superstar Ric Flair, who had a history with both Hennig and Douglas and might have been able to shed some light on the mystery clause. "Tommy!" He exclaimed as I got to the front door. "Long time no see..." "It's out of continuity, Ric, we've never met." "Oh. Right." He changed his demeanor a bit. "Who the hell are you, private dick, and what are doing snooping around my property?" God, I hate method actors. "I'm Tommy Sledge, PWI. Might I have a moment of your time?" 15 gorgeous women came out of his bedroom, totally naked, a few of them covered in whipped cream. "Does your wife know about this?" I asked. "Who do you think the one in the middle is?" "Right. Well, at any rate, I'm looking for some information about a former associate of yours...Curt Hennig." Suddenly, Flair got visibly upset and his butler had to get him a mineral water quickly. "Don't even talk to me about that traitor! He'd sell out his own grandmother for a title shot or a quick buck." Indeed Hennig had sold out the Horsemen in 1994, leaving their ranks for a shot at the World title. The man had a point. He also had a disgusting growth on his back, but neither made him any more credible. "True. Are you aware he recently signed with the WCWF?" "Yes, I am, and I hope he rots there." "And are you aware of *this* clause in his contract?" I pulled out a photocopy of Hennig's contract and showed it to him. "Never seen it before in my life. Interview over!" He slammed the door in my face. Something was definitely up, and that clause was definitely weird. I decided I needed to get to the bottom of things, and fast. I headed back to WCWF HQ for a chat with Ted Dibiase, the owner of the WCWF. He was at his desk, frantically crunching numbers, trying to keep his league afloat. "Mr. Dibiase, I'm Sledge...Tommy Sledge..." "Yeah, I know, we met when I formed the Million Dollar Corporation and tried to take over the WCWF back in '95..." "No, that's not in continuity anymore." "Oh. Damn, I keep forgetting that. Okay, what can I do for you?" "I've obtained the contract of one Curt Hennig..." "What the hell are you talking about? Curt is on a cash-per-appearance deal. He doesn't have a contract, and in fact he's still making money from his insurance company! He'd be nuts to sign a contract with a federation, unless it was one like the DEWF, which has enough money to toss around on someone like him!" That cleared everything up immediately. I thanked him for his time and went to the nearest pay phone to call back Douglas. I got his machine. "Shane? It's Tommy Sledge. Look, it's vitally important that you don't wrestle Nailz at Soul Doubt! I can't emphasize it enough! There's even more going on than your paranoid mind can think up! Lives could be at stake! Oh, excuse me, a masked man just hit me on the head with a lead pipe." I dropped the phone and fell to the floor. The masked man hung up the phone and called 911 for me. Very considerate. What is with wrestlers and masks anyway? I mean, I can understand the Mexicans with their traditions, but you'd think that Americans would just be themselves. Oh, wait, the masked man took off his mask before I passed out, just because it's more dramatic that way. Wrestlers are like that. Oh. Is that who it is? Well, that makes sense. Why didn't I think of that sooner? Now I just have to...make...the...room...stop...spinning........ ... I'm in the hospital right now with a nasty headache, and the doctor says I have a mild case of amnesia. Everything should come back to me pretty soon. Hey, there's a WCWF show on HBO tonight. You know, there's something pretty important about that show that I was supposed to remember...I just can't quite remember it... Oh, well, I'm sure it wasn't anything life-threatening. TO BE CONTINUED AT FGA: SOUL DOUBT!
