DEWF — Thursday Thunder

DEWF Thursday Thunder for 8/29/1996!!!

29 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sorry this is so short on matches, but I was running late and didn't want to leave you guys without a show this week. :) (Open on Joey Styles in the middle of the ring. He's looking at a piece or paper and stage whispering to someone off-camera "Do I really have to do this?") Styles: Ladies and gentlemen, would you please welcome to the DEWF Arena (rolls his eyes and sighs, pauses) ... The Village People. (Joey throws down his mike in disgust and the crowd starts laughing like all hell and clapping along as "YMCA" begins playing. Out come "Indian Chief" Meanie, "Hot Cop" Steve Richards, "Biker Guy" Disco Inferno, "Sailor" Marty Janetty, "Construction Worker" Leif Cassidy and "Cowboy" Don E. Allen.) As Joey referred to them, "The Village Idiots" got in about a minute of leading the crowd in "YMCA" - and then Sandman hit the ring and started caning anything that moved. He literally caned them until his cane was splinters and then left. Damage Control came out en masse as they helped the stricken VP's out of the ring and to the back. (DEWF opening montage to "Warmachine" by KISS) Styles: HELLO, EVERYONE, AND WELCOME TO THE DREAM EXTREME WRESTLING FEDERATION!!!!! (Massive mutant chants of "DEWF! DEWF! DEWF!") Before we open up with (rolls his eyes) "Mr. Hardcore" Marc Bagwell (BOOS), we've got a very special report from Jim Ross on an event that occured at a DEWF house show at the LuLu Temple. (cut to Jim Ross) Ross: Thank you, Joey, and welcome, everyone, to a very special Ross Report. As you know, over the past month things have really heated up between The Undertaker and 573. Well, they decided they couldn't wait for the French Quarter Death Match to get at each other so they went at it at the LuLu Temple in a *very* physical contest. Chairs, tables, you name it - it got used at the Temple. What was interesting was the fact that the man *I* consider the Clueless Putz of wrestling, Travis Cook, decided to come to the ring with 573. 573, as you know, is *not* happy with Cook, and on several occasions Cook came back to ringside after 573 had had him physically removed. At one point, 573 even *carried* Cook over his shoulder to the back. When 573 tried to return to the ring, Undertaker clotheslined the *taste* out of his mouth in the aisle. The two eventually made it back into the ring. 573 ducked a Undertaker clothesline, ricocheted off the opposite ropes - and ran straight into a *massive* choke slam from the Grim Reaper of the DEWF. It was at this point that Cook came back *yet again*, only *this* time, he had company - Jason The Terrible. While Undertaker stared Cook and Jason down, 573 rolled out of the ring and chased them both to the locker rooms. Unfortunately for 573, when he made his way back to the ring, he didn't notice Cook right behind him. When the big man tried to get back in the ring, Cook *held onto his ankle* and 573 got counted out. 573 Singapore Cane-d Cook all the way back to the locker rooms as Undertaker did his post match rituals in the ring. One question on everyone's mind that night was... where, exactly, was *Paul Bearer* in the midst of all this? Until next time, I'm Jim Ross. (fade back to the arena) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- "Mr. Hardcore" Marc Bagwell vs. Jobber X Bagwell headed to the ring, accompanied by the blaring sounds of "Back In Black" by AC/DC. Bagwell was all over Jobber X like a cheap suit on Gorilla Monsoon. Bagwell's self-professed turn to hardcore wrestling seemed to have been just what the doctor ordered. He tabled, chair shotted and just all out clobbered Jobber X for five full minutes. At which point he whipped the poor sod into the ropes and nailed him with a reverse atomic drop - which led directly into a *nasty* DDT, all in one motion! Bagwell called the move "Hardcoremania". Oh, joy. (match time: 05:11) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Promo: "Next week... The Mutilator is here...") -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Styles: Everyone here remembers the Trail Of Revenge that has been perpetrated on Chris Candido and Sister Sherri. If you'll remember, last week "The Ivory Tower" Justin Cross was taking *on* Candido. (Video clips are shown) As usual, the Heavenly Bodies saw fit to stick their noses into the action, which prompted Cross' ally The Crow to hit the ring. Cross and The Crow were quickly overwhelmed by Candito and the Bodies. That is, they were until *"The Shadow" Michael Monroe* made *his* reappearance known! Naturally, once the sides were evened up, the Bodies and Candito beat a hasty retreat. Well, next week, "The Shadow" Michael Monroe finally gets his mitts on Chris Candito in a one on one matchup. Now here's the interesting part: Candito would *not* sign for the match *unless* Michael Monroe put up the DEWF World Light Heavyweight Title. So them next week it's Chris Candido going up against "The Shadow" Michael Monroe for the DEWF World Light Heavyweight Title. (fade back to Joey) Chris Candido... welcome to hell. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- (cut to a video obviously taken in tha hood. New Jack and Mustafa are walking along a litter strewn sidewalk. We occasionally hear sirens and dogs barking) New Jack: Well, 'Stafa, we got us one more shot at them Road Warriors. We was all set to take 'em to tha hood, ya unde'stand, and some fools decided to stick they noses in our buisness. Well, we got somethin' for they ASS if they decide to try anything again. We ain't happy, and I know damn WELL the Warriors ain't happy. All we want is one clean shot at the Warriors fo' our belts. Cuz the Gangstaz are HEAH, and we AIN'T to be played wit' - SUCKAS!!!! (Cross his arms like an "X" and heads out of shot. Mustafa does The Mustafa Evil Laugh (tm, RevRay) and walks out of shot, himself) Mustafa: We got y'all in check! (Does T.M.E.L. (tm, RevRay) again as he heads out of shot) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF World Tag Team Titles Match* The Gangstaz (New Jack and Mustafa) vs. The Road Warriors (Animal and Hawk w/ Paul Ellering) *Challengers* *Champions* Brutal doesn't even begin to describe this match. These two teams took it to the Nth degree of extreme. Bottles, chairs, ironing boards, billy clubs, tables, ball bats... you name it, it was probably used. Both teams were bloodied practically beyond recognition, but neither team would quit or let themselves get pinned. Not an option. Finally, referee Jim Mollineaux was forced to stop the match, declaring both teams unfit to continue. *Four* ambulances had to be called. (match: 45:26) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF World Heavyweight Title *STEEL CAGE* Match* "The Franchise" Shane Douglas vs. Vader (w/ Sister Sherri) (w/ Paul Ellering) *Challenger* *Champion* No one could get in, no one could get out. That was just the way Tod Gordon wanted it. Douglas was serious this time, all buisness. He got in the ring and here came Vader. Vader practically ran *over* Sister Sherri on the way into the ring. Two of Philadelphia's finest locked the cage door, and stood guard. No interference would be happening *this* time, by damn. It was like Shane Douglas was a man posessed. He by God stood toes to toes and nose to nose with Vader and traded punches, kicks and even bodyslammed the big man three times in succession. After a running clothesline that got Douglas a two and 99/100ths count, even the fans were cheering for him. New found respect for Douglas could be seen in Vader's eyes. But as hard as Douglas fought, it was just simply not to be. Make no mistake about it, both men were a bloody mess and Vader was saved from certain pinfalls on several occasions by sheer virtue of being close to the ropes. He hadn't had an ass kicking like this in a long, long time. The end came when Vader tried a Moonsault - and missed! Douglas went up top for a Superfly Splash - and HE missed! Vader seized the chance and Powerbombed Douglas so hard Douglas *bounced*. Vader was too weak to do anything but lay his right arm across Douglas for the cover. (match time: 27:36) The cage as raised as Sister Sherri stalked to the back in disgust. Vader wobbily made it back to his feet as the chants of "Vadertime!" and "Franchise" rang throughout the arena. When Douglas finally made it back to his feet, Vader raised Douglas' hand in victory as well. Whereupon Douglas promptly collapsed and had to be stretchered out. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Styles: Fans, we --- OH MY GOD!!! There's something going on in Vader's dressing room! Cut back there, quick! (We see Mister Hughes ramming Vader into the lockers. Paul Ellering is a bloody mess on the floor, and Hughes gives him a swift kick and bails as the rest of the LOD comes in. They help Vader to his feet, and then check on Paul) Oh my GOD!!! Mister Hughes, I have a feeling you just stepped in a whole WORLD of sh*t! For the DEWF, I'm Joey Styles! (fade to black) "I abhor the implication that the Royal Navy is a haven for cannibalism. It is well known that we now have the problem relatively under control, and that it is the RAF who now suffer the most casualties in this area." --- Capt. B.J.
