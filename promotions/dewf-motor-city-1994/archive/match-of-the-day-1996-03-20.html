DEWF Match Of The Day for 3/20/96!!!!

20 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(open on JoJo Styles inna ring) Styles: Hello and WELCOME to the MATCH OF THE DAY! (Crowd bepopaloolas) We have got one HELL of match for you today, as "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel will be taking on The Sandman in a 15 foot high steel cage with a roof on top. No one gets in, no one gets out - you either pin your man or make him submit. The loser of this match gets twenty licks with that Singapore Cane! (Mo' Pop, Mo' Pop, Mo' Pop, then crowd starts booing as Eric Bischoff and Hulk Hogan come to ringside) Oh, look, if it isn't Left Nut and Right Nut. What the hell do you two want? Bischoff: You just watch your mouth, peon. You see, we heard what Fairy Horowitz had to say yesterday, and Hulk? Hogan: (looking decidedly less confident than usual) Yeah? Bischoff: I accepted on your behalf for a match here today. Hogan: What the hell gives you the right, you little weasel? Bischoff: Because I *own* you, big boy, THAT'S what! (Hogan grabs Bischoff's collar and Eric B. becomes a touch less mouthy :)) Besides.. heh, heh... sorry.. wanna put me down, now?... you need a confidnce builder and who better than Barry "I'm Not A Wrestler But I Play One On T.V." Horowitz? Hogan: Okay... okay. But I am warning you Bischoff... If I lose this match... Bischoff: Yeah? Hogan: (growls) Your soul may belong to Jesus, but your candy ass will be MINE!!!! (Crowd POPS, and Messieur Horowitz hits the ring, and Hogan, in that order, as Styles and Bischoff bail) ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- *Special Challenge Match* Barry Horowitz vs. Hulk Hogan (w/ Eric Bischoff) Barry was one fired up sonofagun in this match. Hogan looked lost as Horowitz used his horribly underappreciated technical skills to turn Hogan seven ways from Sunday. But, as per usual, Barry had Hogan in a pinning predicament when Hogan went into his "Hulk Up" routine and proceeded to turn Barry into goat cheese. Hogan did the three punch, boot to the head, Leg Drop Cha Cha Cha - and MISSED with the leg drop! Every jobber from here to kingdom come promptly hit ringside to cheer Barry on. Horowitz and Hogan got up at approximately the same time and Hogan went into another Hulk Up routine - which got cut short when Barry kicked him in the .... er... nether regions. Horowitz wrapped up Hogan in a small package, which Hogan reversed, which barry reversed again, which led to about five more reversals. Maybe Bischoff was confused. Maybe he was rattled because of all the jobbers at ringside. Maybe he's just a moron. Whatever the case, SOMEthing had to explain why at one point in the reversal-o-rama, Bischoff, out of the ling of sight of the referee, grabbed a boot and held it down during the three count. Too bad the boot was Hogan's. Ross: HOROWITZ WINS!!! HOROWITZ WINS!!! HOROWITZ WINS!!! HOROWITZ WINS!!! As the jobber brigade carried Barry back to the locker room in triumph, Hogan was demanding to know what the hell had happened. Hogan: WHAT THE ^&*% DID YOU MEAN BY GRABBING MY FOOT, S**T FOR !@#$%^ BRAINS? Bischoff: Aw, pipe down, Hulkster, we'll get 'em next time! It was an accident! Hogan: KIND OF LIKE YOUR *!%@ BIRTH, HUH, JERKOFF? (Crowd senses disaster in the wind and begin chanting "Jerkoff") AND THERE'S NOT GOING TO *BE* A NEXT BLANKETYFILTHFILTHFOULFOUL TIME, BECAUSE YOU'RE !)@(*&#@$ FIRED!!!!! The crowd started going nuts. That was a good thing. Bischoff decided to slap Hogan. NOT a good thing, Eric. Bischoff tried to placate Hogan yet again, but Hulkster decided to show Bischoff first, er, hand why the three punch, boot to the head, leg drop cha cha cha combo was so effective over the years. At this point, even the heel fans were genuflecting to Hogan. The crowd went even further over the edge when Hogan decided to introduce a veritable foreign object-o-rama to various portions of Eric's anatomy, in a generally non-helpful-to-Eric's-body fashion, if you catch my drift. What REALLY made the crowd go totally apesh*t was when Hogan set up Bischoff on a table outside the ring, and went up to the top. Hulk Hogan went from the pariah of professional wrestling to it's new Ghod when he drove Bischoff through the table and almost through the FLOOR with a flying legdrop! He added the piece de resistance by tearing off his shirt as usual and stuffing it and his bandana in Bischoff's mouth. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Styles: (totally speechless) I never.. I never thought I'd... OH boy... OH my God.... Let's get the next match under way... ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- *CAGE MATCH* Diesel vs. Sandman The crowd was totally ballistic and hyped all to hell and gone for this match, after what had just happened. Sandman's CHIROPRACTOR must have been in a similar mood after this match because Sandman couldn't tie his *shoes* against BDC in this match. Diesel was out for paybacks. It took alll of six minutes for Diesel to to hit Sandman with a boot to the midsection out of the corner, JackKnife, 1,2,3. The referee handed Diesel the Singapore Cane, as per the prematch stipulations, but Sandman clipped BDC int he back of the legs, grabbed the cane and went to work on Diesel from there. THAT lasted all of about ten seconds, becuase WHAT time was it? VADER TIME!!! Vader ripped the friggin' door off the hinges and cleaned house on Sandman, beaking the Singapore Cane over his knee as Sandman and Naomi Campbell headed for the high ground. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- Styles: Fans, we will see your tomorrow with the Ross Report! From the single most insane night of professional wrestling I've been involved with in a long time, this is Joey Styles! (fade) "That man does NOT want to know all the things I can do to him with a butter knife!" - Brigadier General Adrienne Kramer *DOCTOR WHO - TIME RIFT*
