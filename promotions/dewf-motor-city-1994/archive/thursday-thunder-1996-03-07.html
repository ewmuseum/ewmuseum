DEWF — Thursday Thunder

DEWF Thursday Thunder for 3/7/1996!!!!! (LONG)

7 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Inside a banquet hall in a castle in the Grand Duchy of Mempho. Seated on one side of a table are Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw, Paul Diamond and Al Perez. On the other side are "The Ivory Tower" Justin Cross, "Shadow" Michael Monroe and Black Sarah of the DAWG Pound of Wrestling. Jeff Jarrett enters and addresses the assmbled audience.) JJ : Gentlemen and lady, I most humbly present to y'all the one true King of wrestling and the legend of Memphis, Jerry "The King" Lawler! (Amists a flourish of trumpets, Jerry Lawler enters the room.) JL : Nice intro, Jeffrey, suitably respectful. JJ : Thanks, Jerry, I made it up on the way in. JL : Not bad, not bad at all. Friends, the reason I have summoned you here is because of a wrestling federation that dares to call itself the Dream EXTREME Wrestling Federation. These amateurs don't know the first thing about Extreme. Jeff Jarrett have fought some of the most violent matches in the history of wrestling against the likes of Eddie Gilbert and Terry Funk to name but two. We've broken more bones and had more bones broken than most of those wrestlers combined. We have sought you out as the ideal combination of toughness and skill to show the DEWF the true meaning of Extreme. Perez, Diamond, are the Razor Boys not every bit as tough and twice as skilled as the Public Enemy or the Nasty Boys? Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw, you have the potential to become one of the toughest wrestlers out of the state of Texas, are you not a match for Stan Hansen or Steve Williams? "Shadow", "Ivory Tower", are you not fearsome enough to destroy the Dark Side? Together, we assembled here have the potential to shed more blood, break more bones and win more titles than any othe collective in the DEWF. What do you say? (Paul Diamond glances at Al Perez) PD : Al and I both agree, we're with you all the way. (Justin Hawk Bradshaw scowls ferociously) JHB : Hansen? Williams? I could tear those fossils apart in my sleep. King, you've got yourself a boy. (Michael Monroe and Justin Cross both look to Black Sarah) BS : Breaking bones and shedding blood, you say ? Sounds like fun. Count the Fear Machine in. JL : Excelent ! (Raising his glass) Gentlemen and lady ... to Evil! All : TO EVIL!!! ("Warmachine" starts as highlights from DEWF shows past are shown. Then, the polished steel logo of the DEWF falls into place. The screen shatters and we see Joey Styles in front of the DEWF logo.) Styles: Hello, friends, and WELCOME to the Dream Extreme Wrestling Federation. You just saw Jerry "The King" Lawler and his Royal Assassins, and Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw wil be debuting tomorrow night on Saturday Night's Maim Event as he faces Buh-Buh Ray Dudley. But right now, let's take you to Jim Ross, who was a report on some shocking events that took place *after* we went off the air yesterday with the Match Of The Day. (Cut to Jimbo in the Control Center) Ross: Hello, fans, and welcome to a very special edition of The Ross Report. Fans, one of the single most shocking events it has ever been my privilege to witness took place after we left the air yesterday with The Match Of The Day. Fans were starting to file out of the arena, and Joey Styles was in the ring preparing to tape some promo spots, when from the back came Bill Alfonso, Cactus Jack, The Giant and The Nasty Boys. Fans, you will not believe what you are about to see. (roll tape - JoJo is in the ring and there are still a few fans about) Styles: Okay, are we ready? Tech: Sure thing. Cue sound, and - roll 'em. Styles: The Dream - (Bill Alfonso enters the ring, along with Cactus Jack, The Giant and The Nasty Boys. Alfonso looks very much NOT like himself. i.e., he's very quiet and very serious) Bill, we're - Alfonso: (looking very tired) Save it, Joey. I've got to do something it's going to be very, very hard for me to do. Styles: (taken aback) What's going on, Bill? Alfonso: As you know, when I first came into the DEWF months ago, I had dreams of being the premier manager in the sport. That hasn't exactly happened, has it? Styles: I don't know, Bill. You've had the heavyweight championship in your stable, twice. Hell, Tommy Dreamer's involved in one of the hottest feuds around right now, with The Harbingers. Alfonso: Exactly. Because of me and how I chose to do things, Taz no longer has that strap. Where has how I've been doing things so far gotten me? Nowhere! Nowhere at all! It's time for Bill Alfonso to get a new attitude, and that's why I have Cactus Jack, The Giant and The Nasty Boys out here with me and not Taz and Tommy. (runs his hand over his eyes) Oh, man - this is still not going to be easy. Giant: (suddenly worried) Bill, what's going on? Alfonso: Like I said, it's time for me to get a new attitude. Besides, you guys done jack sh*t so far. There's no reason to think you're going to do any better, either, so... Saggs: (Grabs Alfonso by his collar) So WHAT, little man? Alfonso: (chokes it out) You're fired! (What fans are left in the arena go nuts at this utterly unexpected turn of events. The Nasties whip Alfonso into the ropes and double clothesline him, Cactus gives Alfonso a DDT on a chair, and The Giant chokeslams him on said chair. Suddenly, from the back comes none other than Paul E. Dangerously as the former memebrs of Team Alfonso leave the ring! Dangerously rips all four of them new a**holes - and then without warning, The Nasties attack him from behind with a chair, which gets used to spike piledrive Paul E on after a DDT by Cactus, and not one, not two, but THREE Chokeslams by The Giant! Alfonso waved away the people looking after him and told them to take care of Paul E, who was not moving - at all. Alfonso left with the paramedics, obviously worried about Paul E, who saved his butt. As they leave, we fade back to the ring area, where a stunned Joey Styles is in the ring. The fans are only speaking quietly, if at all) Styles: Fans, I can tell you - I was there yesterday, in the middle of all that mess and I have never, EVER seen anything like that. (Shudders) I can tell you that Paul E is in serious but stable condition as a result of that beating, and he is going to be just fine in time. (Fans applaud) Wow. Well, on with happier matters entirely, and ironically, one that came about as a result of the actions of Taz and Bill Alfonso. DEWF Commissioner [name withheld] is still recuperating from *his* beating, and has appointed an interim commissioner. Fans, will you please welcome at this time BACK to the Corestates Spectrum - *MISTER* *TOD* *GORDON*!!!!!!!!!! (The crowd goes absofrigginlutely ballistic as Gordon makes his way to ringside, VERY fired up apparently) Tod, I think I speak for everyone in this arena when I say - welcome home! (BePopaloola) Gordon: Joey, you have no idea how good it is to be back in this arena again! (Crowd ... oh, you know) Styles: Tod, you saw what happened on tape. Gordon: That I did, Joey. I never, in my wildest dreams, thought I would find myself agreeing with Bill Alfonso on ANYthing. But he and I both agree although any other fed would ban those maggots for life, the singles best thing would to keep them right here. Styles: Anything you can tell us about what that means? Gordon: (chuckles) All I have to say is - Cactus, Giant, Nasties - welcome to the first day of the rest of hell on earth for you. (Leaves amidst a pop to the stars) Styles: Fans, we will (Crowd starts booing like all hell - BECAUSE HERE COMES ERIC BISCHOFF TO THE RING!!!) What in God's name are YOU of all people doing here, Bischoff? Bischoff: (with his own microphone) I am here to take over this penny ante, bullsh*t, small time, hole in the wall federation. Why? Because I've got BIG money behind me, because I hate these stinking mutants guts, and because I can! (Crowd isn't just booing, it's calling for Bischoff's blood) Your first match today is some jobber to the stars named Brady Boone against a mystery opponent, right? Styles: Yeah, you little - Bischoff: Easy, scumface, before I jam this microphone somewhere you won't like it. C'mon out here, Brady BUNCH! (Starts laughing like hell at his little joke, and bails out of the ring as Boone *hits* the ring) Not so fast, smallfry, because I KNOW who your opponent is - the one, the ONLY, THE IMMORTAL ONE HIMSELF - ****HULK**** ****HOGAN****!!!!!!! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Hulk Hogan (w/ Eric Bischoff) vs. Brady Boone Your basic squash, right? Wrong. Boone used his horribly unappreciated martial arts skills and aerial tactics to thoroughly bamfoozle the ex WCW and WWF champion. Indeed, about four minutes into the action, Boone leveled Hogan with a wicked superkick and nailed a spectacular Moonsault for a one.. two.. KICKOUT!!!!!!! Hogan went into his usual "Hulk Up" routine. End of the match, right? Wrong again. Boone superkicked Hogan right in the mouth, sending teeth flying. It just plain went downhill from there, as Hogan got increasingly desperate, and at one point, threw Boone to the arena floor. He did his usual "Hand to the ear" thing, and then went outside, but couldn't find Boone anywhere! As a frantic Eric Bischoff tried to tell him, all he had to do was look up, because a split second later, Boone connected with a moonsault bodyblock from the top turnbuckle to the arena floor and Hulk Hogan that brought the entire arena to a screaming frenzy! Just when you thought it couldn't get worse for Hogan, it did. He reversed an irish whip to the ropes about five minutes later, and connected with La Boot To La Head, and then connected with The Leg Drop Of Doom. One... two.... KICKOUT! KICKOUT! BOONE KICKED OUT!!!!!!!!!!!!! Hogan was just plain shell shocked at that point and the fans were just delirious as all hell. Hogan stood there, stunned, as Bischoff tried to get in the ring - and was superkicked RIGHT in the mouth by Boone, sending Bischoff flying hard to the arena floor! Hogan tried a running clothesline, but Boone ducked and got Hogan with a Frankensteiner on the rebound! One! Two! THREEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!! (match time: 21:17) Hogan, in a state of almost catatonic shock, did nothing but left and gathered up his manager, heading for the back. Boone was utterly jubilant and celebrated for a good long time before heading back to the locker rooms! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Styles: The noise level in this arena is absolutely deafening! What a match! Brady Boone beats Hulk Hogan! It's almost impossible to try and concentrate on our next match but we have to. Next up we have The Eliminators facing off against Demolition. Before we go to that match, let's hear from Animal and Hawk, The Road Warriors! (The Road Warriors and Paul Ellering are standing in the locker room. The Roadies even have their old black shoulder pads on) [PE] Well, well, well. Demolished goes up against the Eliminators. Well, Eliminators, you jumped into the ring to save the Road Warriors' necks when Demolished jumped them from behind, WITH A CAST!!! [Hawk] Demolished!! Listen and listen good!! Ya picked the wrong team to mess with when ya picked The Road Warriors!! And believe me, you HAVEN'T heard the last of us!! The Eliminators stuck their necks out for The Road Warriors, and The Road Warriors are gonna return the favor!! Demolished, watch your backs boys, The Roadies don't forget things like your attack too easily, and we DON'T let grudges go!! OHHHHHH WHAT A RUSSSSSHHHHH!!!! [Animal] Eliminators, The Road Warriors repay people when they do things like you guys did, and you WILL be repayed, even if it IS at Demolished's expense!! [PE] Now, now boys, we got some plans to make. So get that camera outta here!!! (The Road Warriors step in front of the camera, and we fade out) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The Eliminators vs. Demolition (Crush and Smash) (w/Bobby Heenan) EVERYone, including the so-called rulebreakers, seemed fired up by what happened in the Hogan-Boone match. The 'liminators and the Demos actually seemed to LIKE beating the hell out of each other, and getting it right back again! BOTH teams hit their finishers early on, and neither one worked. This was a long match, both teams gave everything they had and then some. Crush even dispensed with his cast early on and seemed none the worse for it. The end came at approximately twentysix minutes into the match, when The Eliminators once again nailed the Total Elimination on Crush. (match time: 26:05) The BEST part of the match came AFTER it, when Heenan tried to chairshot The Eliminators, who caught him at it and were attacked by both Demos, which brought DEMOLITION AX to the ring, along with the rest of the Heenan family, excepting Bret Hart. The sides were quickly evened by The Road Warriors and Public Enemy joining The Eliminators! Interim Commissioner Tod Gordon quickly grabbed the mic: Gordon: ALL RIGHT! THAT'S ENOUGH! You guys hate each other so damned much you can't keep your noses out of each others matches? FINE! On Saturday Night's Maim Event, there will be a TWELVE MAN Survivor Match with The Road Warriors, PE and The Eliminators facing you Heenan Family idiots! Case closed! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (After order is at long last restored, we go back to JoJo) Styles: What a night, fans! And we've STILL got a rematch for the DEWF Intercontinental Title as the MONSTER, 911, faces Shawn Michaels. Frankly, I wouldn't want to be Shawn Michaels for all the money on God's green earth right now, after what happened to Paul E. Dangerously! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ***DEWF Intercontinental Heavyweight Championship Rematch*** 911 (champion) vs. Shawn Michaels (challenger) Michaels' manager, Naomi Campbell, wisely stayed in the back for this one. Michaels again used his speed to buzz around 911, "floating like a butterfly and stinging like a bee" as he put it. Jesse Ventura once said about Andre The Giant that you could do all the fancy dan maneuvers you wanted. Sooner or later... he'd catch you. Michaels managed to avoid 911 for a solid fifteen minutes before he got caught in a Thesz Press attempt from the top rope. You guessed it! CHOKE slam time! (match time: 15:50) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ***CAGE MATCH*** Sting vs. Shane Douglas This, friends, was just not pretty. Sting seemed mroe than willing to beat the arrogant Douglas to an absolute bloody pulp, and Douglas was giving as good as he got. Both men were bleeding like faucets when, about nine and a half minutes in, Douglas went up top for a high cross body and connected - which Sting promptly rolled over for the win! (match time: 9:44) Sister Sherri, Douglas' manager, was the first one in the ring and kept Sting's attention while Douglas attacked him from behind. Sherri and Douglas handcuffed an exhausted Stinger to the cage as the rest of Sherri's stable (Skip Bodydonna and The Heavenly Bodies), hit the ring and joined Douglas in strapping an almost dead Sting. HERE CAME THE HORSEMEN!!!!!! The Horsemen unhandcuffed Sting and cleaned house on Sherri's Army, who left VERY quickly after that. Douglas kept screaming for Flair, and you can bet The Nature Boy will be more than happy to face Douglas. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Styles: This is one of the wildest nights I have EVER been involved with, and it's going to be even wilder on Saturday Night's Maim Event! As we leave, let's hear from "The Hot Rod" himself, "Rowdy" Roddy Piper. For the DEWF, I'm Joey Styles. [Camera opens on "Rowdy" Roddy Piper standing in the wreckage once known as a wrestling arena, following another night of DEWF action] [RP] Well... looks like 'ol Hot Rod missed out on a little fun right here.. looks like Hot Rod has been missing out on alot of fun lately. Used to be that Roddy Piper was at one time the center of all the carnage, destruction, and chaos.. now it seems I'm only standing on the sidelines. I tellz 'ya.. DEWF can't possibly call itself extreme without Hot Rod in here raising a little hell, time for me to start getting back into things.. time for me to start getting a little.. rrrroowwwwddy. [Piper walks off through the wreckage and out the arena doors] "It's a bird! It's a plane! It's SUPERPUTZ!" - Joey Styles commenting on ECW wrestler "Dancin'" Stevie Richards
