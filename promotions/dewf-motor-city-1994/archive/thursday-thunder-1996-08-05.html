DEWF Thursday Thunder for 8/1/1996!!! (Tape Delayed)

5 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Vader is standing in the locker room, the world title over his shoulder. Diesel is pacing in the background) [V] TONIGHT!!! TONIGHT'S THE NIGHT SHANE DOUGLAS!! Douglas, brutha, you wanted a shot at the World Title!? You got it tonight!! You tried to rip Vader off, but it won't happen!! Tonight Douglas, tonight it's gonna be Vader Time, ON YOUR ASS!!! DOUGLAS, THE THREE TIME DEWF CHAMP IS COMIN' AFTER YOU BRUTHA!!! [D] Well, tonight's the night. You all saw The Two Dudes with Attitudes beat on Cactus Jack and The Giant, well, tonight you see them beat the sh*t out of EACH OTHER!!! Shawn, I kept the Giant from cheating you out of the IC Title, and tonight, tonight you keep YOUR promise, and battle ME to try and keep the title!!! (Vader points to the belt and then flashes the "V for Vader" sign) (camera comes up on Michael Monroe in a hospital bed. He is heavily bandaged, and his face is puffy and swollen, but his eyes are clear and burn intensely.) MM : It's a story as old as the hills. Two brothers who took two different paths. Our brotherhood is not one of mere blood, but a deeper connection, that of the spirit. Crow, neither of us claims to be a saint. We are not pharisees, but rather half sinner and half angel, like all self aware individuals. You chose the path that was best for you, and I took the path that circumstances led me to. We do indeed have our diferences, Crow. But you come to me, my brother, in my time of need, offering your hand and asking nothing in return. Well, I accept your offer, Crow. The Fear Machine never forgets a grudge, but nor do we forget a favour. There is honour among thieves, and my word is my bond. (fade to normal Thursday Thunder opening montage to "Warmachine". We open on Joey Styles in the middle of the DEWF ring and the arena mutants are giving a thunderous "DEWF! DEWF! DEWF! DEWF!" chant.) Styles I'M JOEY STYLES AND THIS IS DEWF THURSDAY THUNDER! (Chant redoubles) At the top you saw the DEWF World Champion Vader along with "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel, and my guests at this time are "The Silent Killer" Al Perez and his manager, Black Sarah! ("Quiet" by Smashing Pumpkins plays over the PA system. Al Perez and Black Sarah walk up to the podium.) Sarah : Joseph, not that I mind of course, but why are they now sending you to interview us instead of that odious Todd Pettingill cretin ? JS : Ummm , he refused, actually. He said he's afraid of you. Sarah : Good, it worked. JS : What do you mean? Sarah : Oh, nothing, nothing. Candito, it begins tonight. You tried to take out the Shadow. You sought to prove yourself a man, and only proved yourself a coward. Well, you pushed over the first dominoe of your downfall. This is the Trail of Revenge. My dear friend Jerry "The King" Lawler gave ne the services of "The Latin Heartthrob" Al Perez to deal with you. This man has faced everyone from the Von Erichs to the Superpowers to the Road Warriors and all points in between. He can out-fight you, out-muscle you, and out-wrestle you. This man knows holds that Dean Malenko hasn't figured out yet. Candito, you should have listened to "New Face In Hell" : always present them with a moving target. Instead, your ego couldn't deal with that, so you had to get our attention. Well, you got it. You are an ant that crawled under a magnifying glass so that people would notice it, and got incinerated by the rays of the sun. You are at the crossroads, Candito, and you are about to get run over from both directions. JS : Al Perez, how do you feel to be a part of Black Sarah's revenge scheme? Al Perez : (stares intently at the camera) Black Sarah : Al isn't talking today, he is too focussed on his upcoming match. But be assured that he is more than ready for Chris Candito. And Gangstaz ... don't think we have forgotten about you! You are like a man falling off a 100 story building, who passes the 50th floor and thinks, "Well, so far so good." But the ground was rushing towards him all the same. And, at the end of that hundred story fall is ... the Fear Machine! (Crowd pops as Perez and Black Sarah walk off. They are no sooner gone than The Bushwhacker's music starts off and here come Butch and Luke, a'whackin' away. They get to the ring and give Joey the dreaded Double Lick O' Doom. They go through their antics for a few moments and then "You Are So Beautiful" by Joe Cocker starts up and out come The Heavenly Bodies) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Bushwhackers (Luke and Butch) vs. The Heavenly Bodies ("Gigalo" Jimmy Del Rey and Doctor Tom Prichard w/ Siter Sherri) The 'Whackers put up their usual good fight, but the Bodies were simply too much. The end came at about the eight and a half minute mark when Prichard hit Luke with a powerbomb and Del Rey hit with a breathtaking Moonsault. (match time: 08:41) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Undertaker (w/ Paul Bearer) vs. "Killer" [name withheld] [name withheld] came down to ringside, taking notes and calling the crowd ghouls (and worse). What the crowd called [name withheld] is something unfit for human consumption. [name withheld] had no sooner gotten into the ring then the lights went out and The Undertaker proceeded to ringside, his dirge eerie as ever. [name withheld] tried to wrestle The Undertaker. Big mistake. Undertaker simply outpowered [name withheld] and did his damndest to turn the "Killer" into goat cheese. One flying clothesline, a choke slam and a Tombstone later, "Killer" [name withheld] was history. (match time: 01:32) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- "The Latin Heartthrob" Al Perez (w/ Black Sarah) vs. Chris Candido (w/ Sister Sherri) Candido came down to ringside with Sister Sherri, but made it only about halfway before Al Perez bolted down the aisle and was on Candido like ugly on Abdullah The Butcher. But to Candido's credit, he proved he could wrestle *and* fight every bit as well as Perez. Candido was patient, and waited until Perez' famous temper got the better of him. Eventually, it did. Perez tried a Stinger splash type move into the corner, but Candido moved. Candido went to work in a surprisingly clean and technical manner, as if determined to prove he could outwrestle Perez. But after Candido nailed a gutwrench suplex and went up top, Justin Cross came down to ringside. Candido went over to the ropes and jawed with Cross. Big mistake. Perez rushed Candido from behind and clotheslined him over the top. As Perez distracted the referee, Cross nailed Candido on the outside with his White Death maneuver (a Scott Steiner-esque pileplex)! Needless to say, Candido was out cold. Cross calmly walked to the back as Candido was counted out and Perez was declared the winner. (match time: 07:04) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF INTERCONTINENTAL TITLE MATCH* "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel (w/ Paul Ellering) *challenger* vs. "The Boy Toy" Shawn Michaels (w/ Naomi Campbell) *champion* The sounds of a diesel horn blared over the PA as "Big Daddy Cool" headed for ringside, accompanied by Paul Ellering. As the bluesy wail of Diesel's theme played, he jabbed his right fist in the air while the crowd chanted "BDC! BDC! BDC! BDC!". The *women* in the audience had their field day when "Sexy Boy" kicked in and down the aisle bopped Michaels, accompanied by Naomi Campbell. Needless to say, Shawn was all attitude. He spent the better part of the match pursuing the rather sensible tactic of stick-and-move against Diesel, while (when he could *catch* Michaels) Diesel battered Michaels with .. er... high octane power moves. Michaels' strategy of stick-and-move gradually wore Diesel down. Naturally, the longer the match went, the better it was for Michaels. Diesel even went for a clothesline at one point, but Michaels ducked under it, bounded off the opposite ropes and leveled "BDC" with a flying forearm. Michaels did "The Kip Up O' Doom" and prepared to finish off Diesel with a little ol' dose of "Sweet Chin Music". BUT - Diesel *side stepped* the move, rushing *past* Michaels! When Michaels turned around, Diesel practically *punted* Michaels with a boot to the midsection, which he quickly followed up with a NASTY Jack Knife! One-Two-THREE!!!! (match time: 21:03) Announcer: "Your winner - and NNNNNNEEWWWWWWWWWWW DEWF Intercontinental Championnnn, "Big Daddy Cool" DDDDIIIEEEEESELLLLLLL!!!!!!!!!!" Michaels was helped out of the ring by Naomi Campbell as Diesel celebrated *in* the ring. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- (cut to Joey in front of the DEWF logo) Styles: Fans, due to circumstances beyond our control, we won't be able to bring you the Sabu-Crow match tonight, but we will have that match for you at the next available date. Next up we have the rematch you've been waiting for and (someone off camera yells for Joey to head to the ring, quick) What's going on? (Heads off and when he gets to the ring, Tod Gordon is waiting for him) Tod, what's up? Gordon: Joey, there's been some kind of attack on Vader in the locker room. I don't know what happened, but I think I've got a pretty good idea. (As if on cue, "Perfect Strangers" by Deep Purple starts up and Shane Douglas heads for the ring, accompanied by Sister Sherri. He's laughing all the way) Well, speak of the devil and his jackass shall appear. (Shane gets in the ring) Douglas: Tod Gordon, why don't you tell these people the truth? That "The Man They Call Fatass" (BOOS) isn't gonna be able to defend the world title tonight? (**BOOS** as Douglas laughs) Gordon: Yeah! Yeah, that's right! Vader will not be able to defend the world title tonight. Douglas: You know, Tod Gordon, I have every right to kick your ass for the way you screwed, blued and tattooed me at Independence Day Armageddon, but I'm not gonna do that. You see, I'm gonna show you what a nice guy I am. Styles: (Sarcastically) Oh, and just how is that, pray tell? Douglas: (Ignoring the deafening chants of "SHUT THE F*CK UP!" and "VADER TIME!") What I'm gonna do is, I'm gonna give Tod Gordon the chance of a lifetime. All you have to do, Tod Gordon, is simply hand me the DEWF World Belt on the silver platter I deserve it on, and everything will be copacetic again. Now, how much fairer can you get than *that*, huh? (**BOOAPALOOZA**) Gordon: (appears to consider it) Well, Shane, I just have one thing to say to your offer (pauses) F*CK YOU! F*CK YOU AND THAT DIMESTORE WHORE MANAGER OF YOURS AND GET YOUR ASS OUT OF THIS RING NOW, YOU F*CKHEAD! (Crowd is going into orbit, popping. Douglas started to leave, then turned around and LEVELS Gordon with a VICIOUS right hand. He nailed Gordon with a belly to belly suplex and then started turning Gordon's face into tomato paste with rabbit punches. Can YOU say "Bad Career Move, Shane"? I *KNEW* you could!) The fans went totally *APESH*T* when "Frankenstein" by The Edgar Winter Group began blaring over the PA as 911 roared down to ringside! Sherri was no coward, but she was also no fool. She bailed and frantically tried to warn Shane who was standing right behind him. Shane stood over the fallen Gordon, and turned around grinning. Well, he grinned for only about as long as it took to turn around and see 911, that is. Can *YOU* guess what happened next? Going UP... GOING down.... ***CHOKESLAM TIME***!!!!!! As the stricken Douglas rolled out of the ring, "Damage Control" came to ringside to tend to Gordon, along with 911. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- (cut to Joey back in front of the DEWF logo) Styles: Fans, I - (is interrupted by a HIGHLY irate Shane Douglas) Douglas: 911! YOU OVERGROWN SACK OF SH*T!!! YOU HAD NO GODD*MNED BUSINESS STICKING YOUR FACE IN "THE FRANCHISE"S BUSINESS, YOU WORTHLESS F*CK!!! I WANT YOUR CANDY ASS IN THE RING, AND SHERRI'S OFF TAKING CARE OF THE CONTRACT RIGHT F*CKIN' NOW! SIGN YOUR PUNK ASS NAME TO THE DOTTED LINE, BIG MAN, AND FEELS WHAT IT MEANS TO F*CK WITH "THE FRANCHISE"! (storms off) Styles: I have a suggestion, Shane - try less caffeine! (shakes his head) Fans, we are out of time! We will be BACK with you SATURDAY night for the RETURN of DEWF Saturday Night's MAIM Event! Until "The Ross Report" tomorrow, I'm Joey Styles! (fade to "Warmachine") "So it is written, so it shall come to pass." -- Raven, ECW World's Heavyweight Champion
