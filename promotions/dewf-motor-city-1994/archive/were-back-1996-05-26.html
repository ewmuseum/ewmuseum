DEWF - We're baaaaaaaaaccccckkkkk!!!!!!!

26 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera is travelling down a New York City street. All of a sudden, Bill Alfonso jumps out of a doorway) Alfonso: We're baaaaaaacccccckkkkkkk!!!!! (Starts a'blowin' that whistle. The screen crashes and we see Joey Styles in front of the DEWF logo) Styles: Hello, wrestling fans, and the DEWF is definitely back! On tonight's card we will have the following matches: The Undertaker (w/ Paul Bearer) opens up against Pitbull #1 (w/ Gen. Skandor Akbar) Shane Douglas (w/ Sister Sherri) vs. Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Naomi Campbell) Jushin "Thunder" Liger (w/ Brandon Baxter) meets "The Canadian Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ "Gentleman" Jim Cornette) - the winner faces DEWF World Light Heavyweight Champion Sabu later in the card Sid (w/Paul E. Dangerously) defends his newly won DEWF Intercontinental Championship against the man he won it from, The Giant (w/ Eric Bischoff) The Road Warriors (Hawk and Animal, w/ Paul Ellering) take on DEWF World Tag Team Champions Public Enemy (Johnny Grunge and Rocko Rock, w/ Paul E. Dangerously, for the titles) DEWF World Light Heavyweight Champion Sabu defends his belt against the winner of the earlier match And in a wholly bizarre situation, NEW DEWF World's Heavyweight Champion, Vader, defends his strap against not one, but TWO men, in a triangle match! For those of you who hadn't heard, Shinya Hashimoto beat Vader in a match to determine who got a shot at then champion "Nature Boy" Ric Flair. But Hashimoto was injured by *The Sandman* after the match. Since Hashimoto was therefore unable to wrestle in the match against Flair, *Vader* got the shot. (roll clips) During the match, as you can see, Tiger Jeet Singh cmae down to ringside to attack Vader. OUT OF THE BLUE, here comes The Sandman and goes to TOWN on the wily Singh! Vader went to work after that, and just beat Flair senseless until finally applying the Vader Bomb and becoming the NEW DEWF World's Heavyweight Champion! Let's these post match words from Vader: (Paul Ellering and the Legion of Doom are in the locker room celebrating Vader's world title win, champagne is spraying everywhere and many other faces are seen in the background celebrating with the LOD. Joey Styles walks in with the mic in his hand) [JS] Ladies and gentlemen, it's one WILD party back here, as Vader and company are celebrating his world title victory over Ric Flair!! I'm trying to get a word with the new champ and the rest of the LOD. Vader, can I talk to you?? (Vader and Paul Ellering walk over to the camera) So, Vader, you finally did it, you're the NEW DEWF World Champ, how does it feel!? [Vader] Joey Styles, IT'S F*CKING AWESOME!!! DEWF, VADER TIME IS JUST BEGINNING!!! VADER WILL RULE FOR A LONG TIME!! IT'S VADER TIME!!! [PE] Well Joey, let me say that it's about time. Now, if only we can get Public Enemy in the ring with Animal and Hawk, and we'll see who the true world tag champs are, and then possibly get the IC title on Diesel. But let me tell you, it is great to finally get the title around Vader's waist, where it belongs. [JS] I have to ask you, what's up with Sandman, do you know?? [PE, looking around] Well Styles, is Sandman here??(doesn't see him) Well, I can't see him around here, but Sandman, if you're listening, listen especially well here. Sandman, while we can't be sure yet, but your intentions seem good, so Sandman, I'mn offering you a spot in the Legion of Doom!! (Styles looks in shock, along with a few others. The Legion of Doom just smiles) [JS] Paul Ellering, I think the celebration is messing up my hearing, did you just offer Sandman a spot with the Legion of Doom!? [PE] Yes Styles, yes I did. Sandman, let me know you're answer. But now, let's get back to partying!! (The LOD turns and begins to celebrate again) Styles: As you can see, Vader was one happy man that night. But tonight, at the DEWF Arena he's got to put it all on the line against BOTH "Nature Boy" Ric Flair and Shinya Hashimoto in a Triangle Match! Fans, we will see you tonight! (fade) "Take my hand.... off to Never Never Land..." - Metallica, "Enter Sandman" [name withheld], ECW-L listowner/poobah - Taking internet email to the EXTREME!!!!
