DEWF Thursday Thunder for 5/30/1996!!!! (LONG)

This goes about 400 lines. I had a LOT to get in this time, but I daresay you won't be disappointed. :) Jody - and SuperBlonde - had better pay attention to this one. :) (We open with a clip from Sunday Slam. JoJo is in the ring..) Styles: Ladies and gentlemen, let me welcome at this time one of the participants in tonight's three way dance for the DEWF World's Heavyweight Championship - he is "The NATURE Boy" Riiiiiiiccccc Flaiiiiirrrrr! ("Also Sprach Zarathustra" begins as "The Nature Boy" makes his way down to ringside. "WHOOO"s abound frm the crowd) (snip) Flair: Mark it down, Vader - TONIGHT that strap comes back around the waste - of "The Nature Boy"! WHOOOO! (Crowd "WHOOOOO"s - and a sweaty, tired Shane Douglas rolls out of the ring and confronts Flair) Well, well, well! It it isn't Shane "I'm not a wrestler, but I play one on TV" Douglas! Douglas: Listen, old man, I've wanted your sorry ass in the ring for God knows how long now. Flair: YOU want ME, huh? Who just lost, punk? YOU, I believe. Douglas: LISTEN you worthless piece of sh*t! You are in The House "The Franchise" Built, not that half assed piece of sh*t in Atlanta you used to work for! (Flair hauls off and cold cocks Douglas. Too Cold Scorpio quickly comes to Douglas' aid and it gets ugly for The Nature Boy quickly. We then go to the usual DEWF opening sequence and end up with Joey Styles in front of the DEWF logo.) Styles: Hello, DEWF fans and WELCOME to DEWF Thursday Thunder! What you just saw took place on DEWF Sunday Slam. "The Franchise" Shane Douglas and his stablemate Too Cold Scorpio viciously attacked "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair, preventing "The Nature Boy" from his scheduled participation in the Three Way Dance against World Champion Vader and Shinya Hashimoto. We understand that "The Nature Boy" is not badly injured, but he will be out of action for another 3 weeks at least. Later on in this hour, "The Franchise" takes on "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz. But right now, let's see The Sandman take on Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The Sandman (w/ Naomi Campbell) vs. Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw (w/ Jerry Lawler) If you were looking for drop toe holds and takedowns, BOY was this the wrong match for you! Sandman and Bradshaw were toes to toes and nose to nose from the opening bell. In fact, less than thirty seconds had passed before the match went outside and Bradshaw was busted WIDE open by a DDT on the floor from Sandman. Bradshaw looked lost against Sandman, to be honest. Sandman made sure Bradshaw didn't have time to think straight. The end came about nine and a half minutes when for the umpteenth time, the action went outside. Sandman was the first back into the ring and when Bradshaw started through the ropes - WHAM - Piledriver! (match time: 10:07) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Styles: Chalk up a win for The Sandman! Now as you may remember, we received a video clip from Ted DiBiase and Virgil some weeks ago. We received a new one this week, but since it's been so long, we'll show you the first one, well, first and then the second one. I wish I knew what to make of this... Million Dollar Dreams - Part One "If it's Tuesday, this must be Needles..." [We open in Duke's House of Waffles in Needles, Arizona, where about 12 people sit huddled around a rather rickety ring. In the background, we can see an obviously hand-drawn banner hanging on the wall, reading "WCWF Championship Wrestling."] [Ted Dibiase and Virgil sit in the front row.] Dibiase: You know, Virgil, I'm beginning to wonder about the wisdom of selling stock to that Billionaire Ted guy. I mean, just about every star we've ever had has defected to the DEWF now. Virgil: But we've still got the Hulkster. Dibiase: Yes, I know. But I think he's finding it hard to follow his own reputation anymore... [As if in response, we see Hogan, with the WCWF World title belt, in one corner of the ring, while in the other we see 24 armed mercenaries, all growling menacingly. The bell rings, and all 24 drop to the mat on cue while Hulk poses after another successful title defense.] Dibiase: This is really embarrassing. Virgil: Even more than my "Ice Ice Virgil" video, right boss? Dibiase: Uh, no. That was pretty stupid, Virgil. [Meanwhile, in the ring, Hogan does a particularly strenuous pose and drops to the mat in pain. He does a tremendous job of selling an incredibly minor injury until the paramedics come out to stretcher him out of the ring.] Dibiase: There must be a better way to run this federation... [Hogan is really into it now, giving out "Get Well Hulk" cards to the 12 fans at ringside, none of whom seem to really care about his state of health.] Dibiase: Let's get out of here. HEY, WHO WANTS TO BUY A WRESTLING FEDERATION? [Several of the rednecks raise their hands. Dibiase picks one and calls out to him.] You, what'll you give me for the federation? Redneck: Fifty dollars and a pig! Dibiase: Sold! But keep the pig. [The redneck appears to be happier than a pig in shit, having acquired a federation for fifty dollars, and a pig to boot.] [Dibiase and Virgil get up to leave.] Dibiase: Let's go. I know insane asylums with better talent than that balding geriatric reject. [Hogan is doing his "epileptic seizure" act.] Virgil: Hey, good joke, boss. Insane asylum. Ha, very funny. [Dibiase isn't smiling.] Dibiase: I never joke. [End part one.] Million Dollar Dreams - Part Two. "...But It Just May Be A Lunatic You're Looking For..." [We open with Ted Dibiase and Virgil being led on a guided tour of Political Asylum by resident quack Vincent B. Baats.] Baats: ...and in this cell, we see former president Richard Nixon, who never quite recovered from his resignation in 1976. Virgil: I thought he died. Baats: [Adjusting his glasses.] You know, now that you mention it, you'd think Nixon would have a bigger nose. And he should be male...oh, well, no matter. So, what brings you here today, Mr. Dibiase? Dibiase: I'm looking for cheap talent to overrun that two-bit WCWF ripoff -- the DEWF. I heard that someone pretty impressive is holed up here. Baats: Ah. Yes, you must be referring to Bryan Clark. A very interesting case indeed. Spent years calling himself Adam Bomb before a rather bizarre encounter with the Undertaker left the urn melted to his face in the form of a facemask and also left him totally out of his gourd. Called himself Doctor Doom for a while, if I'm not mistaken. Dibiase: Good exposition, Doctor. So what happened to him? Baats: Ah. Well, Marvel Comics has been very kind in tolerating the WCWF's use of the Hulk Hogan trademark, but they weren't quite so forgiving when it came to Mr. Clark. Dibiase: You don't mean... Baats: Yes. I'm afraid so. They sued. [Dibiase clutches his heart at the very mention of such a bad word.] Dibiase: How did he do? Baats: Not very well, I'm afraid. Marvel had a team of 55 lawyers, and the litigation was so stressful that Clark was left with the intellect of an 8 year old boy. Dibiase: I knew that part -- I mean what was the settlement? Baats: 2.3 million. Clark lost everything. Dibiase: THOSE HEARTLESS BASTARDS! THEY'LL NEVER GET AWAY WITH THIS! Baats: Well, we're here. Careful, he's still dangerous. [Baats unlocks the double-reinforced steel door. Sitting, chained to the wall with a lollipop in his mouth, is the formerly fearsome Doctor Doom/Adam Bomb.] Bomb: Daddy? Dibiase: It's worse than I thought. Baats: Oh, no, the worst is yet to come. Dibiase: Oh, please, what can *possibly* be worse than an IQ of 40? Bomb: Vote Dole. [Dibiase screams in horror.] Baats: I'm sorry you had to hear that. [Virgil puts an arm around his employer to comfort him.] Dibiase: Can you release him into my custody, Doctor? Baats: No. [wink, wink.] I can't in good conscience allow you to take him. [wink, wink.] My oath as a doctor won't allow it. [wink, wink.] Dibiase: [sighs] How much? Baats: *Cough* Fifty-thousand *cough*. Dibiase: [Rolls his eyes.] Virgil, write the quack a check. Then lead monkey-boy here out to the limo. AND DON'T LET HIM PEE ON THE SEAT! Baats: Don't be insulting. Bryan is very discriminating when it comes to urination. Dibiase: Good. Baats: His defecation habits, however... [shudders.] [Virgil leads his former best friend by the hand out to the car while we fade back to Joey in front of the logo] Styles: Oh God. Next up, we have the Road Warriors as THEY take on the team of Paul Diamond and Al Perez, The Razor Boys. Now this was an interview I tape with the LOD right after Sunday Slam, so we'll also be hearing from World Champion Vader. Let's go to the tape. (Joey Styles is in the ring for an interview) [JS] Ladies and gentlemen, without further adieu, THE LEGION OF DOOOOOOM!!! ("Iron Man" plays and the fans be-pop-a-looza!!! The four members and Paul Ellering walk out to ringside. Vader is carrying the DEWF World Title) Well, here it is, the single most EXTREME group of wrestlers ever gathered by one manager, Vader, Diesel, and The Road Warriors, the Legion of Doom!!!(fans poppity-pop-pop) And here is the man that is single handidly responsible for this group being here, Paul Ellering!! (fans pop again) So Paul, how's it goin?? [PE] Well Joey, it's goin pretty good!! I got the World's Champ in Vader over here, and he has beat the crap outta two legends in the sport to win and retain the title. Then, I got the Road Warriors, the inovators of EXTREME tag team wrestling over here, and then there's Big Daddy Cool himself, Diesel!! [JS] Well, speaking of Vader and the world title, as we all saw, Vader absolutely went to town on Shinya Hashimoto to defend his title. So Vader, what's the scoop on the title. [V] Well Styles, it's simple!! I showed I could back up my words!! I said it was Vader Time in the DEWF, and I PROVED IT!!! WHAT TIME IS IT!?!?(pushes mic towards crowd as a "Vader Time!!" chant starts. He then flashes the "V for Vader" sign) YOU BETTER F**KING BELIEVE IT!!! [JS] Well, the Road Warriors finally got their shots at the tag belts, but nothing. Public Enemy is still the champs. [H] Yea, you're right there Styles. But go ahead and ask Rocko and Johnny, we gave them one HELL of a match!! Who knows, maybe sometime down the road we can do it again boys!! [A] But we've got some unsettled business!! Razor Boys!! Harlem Heat!! We're not done with either of you!! Come on boys!!! Step into the ring!!! [JS] But what of you Big Daddy Cool?? [D] Well Styles, I want one thing right now!! GIANT!!! YOU LISTENIN'!? I WANT THAT IC TITLE!!! So keep the strap and gold shined for me!! 'Cause when I get you in the ring, it'll be mine!! [JS] So Paul, any other news for the LOD?? [PE] Well, I'm always scouting other talent, expecially talent that can take it to the EXTREME!!! So, Sandman, what about my offer?? Let me know!! But now Styles, we're outta here!!! ("Iron Man" plays as the LOD plays to the crowd, flexing, flashing the "V for Vader" sign, and other things) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The Road Warriors (w/ Paul Ellering) vs. The Razor Boys (w/ Jerry Lawler) Shattered patellas - severed arteries - these are the things dreams are made of, to quote Hawk. The Razor Boys are a tough team. No doubt about it. But they had the misfortune of running into a very honked off pair of Road Warriors. They had the misfortune of running into the Road Warriors a week after they lost a title shot. But give the Razor Boys credit, they gave every bit as good as they got. But it was just not meant to be. The Warriors finally put Diamond in the Doomsday Device and it was all she wrote. (match time: 17:21) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The Undertaker and Mystery Partner (w/ Paul Bearer) vs. The Pitbulls (w/ General Skandor Akbar) The Undertaker made his usual spooky entrance, then gestured toward the back, to call out his mystery tag partner. This - guy walked out. 'Bout 6'4", 6'5". Could've been Sandman's baby brother. Even dressed like him. Who WAS this guy? During the ring introductions, "Sex Symbol" Travis Cook meandered down to ringside and began jawjacking with the mystery man. The Pitbulls were introduced first, then The Undertaker. Then the mystery man stepped forward, was introduced as "573" or something like that - and turned right around and BLISTERED the Undertaker right between the eyes with a NASTY Singapore Cane shot and walked out with Cook! The 'Bulls were busy ganging up on the Undertaker and failed to notice "Burn" by The Cure as it began playing - and The Crow roared down to ringside and double clotheslined both Pibulls from the top turnbuckle as The Undertaker did his Zombie Situp (tm). The match officially got underway and OH it wasn't pretty. If Da 'Bulls thought they were going to have it easy with the relatively small Crow, they got taught differently REAL quick. Crow and UT used, confused and abused the 'Bulls for 16 long minutes before, after a double back bodydrop by Crow and Undertaker, Akbar threw his cane into Pitbull #1. Pitbull #1 *glommed* UT with the cane and went for the cover - one - two - KICKOUT. It was all Pitbulls for the next two and a half minutes as they double teamed Undertaker mercilessly. Finally, The Crow had had enough and vaulted into the ring. He went after both 'Bulls with spin kicks and lariats as Undertaker got his wind back. At one point, Pitbull #1 came off the top rope with a chair shot and got dropkicked right in the face by The Crow. The chair smashed right back in Pitbull #1's face, knocking him silly. Pitbull #2 tried to double axehandle Crow, but got savate kicked right in the mush. Pitbull #2 stumbled straight back - and was scooped up into a TOMBSTONE by The Undertaker! (match time: 19:04) (Screen fades to black) (From the darkness comes a spinning metallic circular object. The object spins around in the darkness with several moving and interlocking parts visable. An eery music, somewhat like the 'Mortal Kombat' theme plays in the background. The screen shatters with an explosion as pieces of the circular object fly off the screen.. as the smoke from the explosion clears three words, written in red can be seen coming closer...........) ***BLOOD BOILS OVER*** (fade to Joey in front of the DEWF logo) Styles: Fans, welcome back. We have - (Screen buzzes, fills with static.. Joey fades yet again. Suddenly lightning strikes . It lights up .. a room and we all see a sillohuette standing in the room. It is your worst nightmare, the Black Scorpion!!!) BS; Hi boys and girls, today I am here to each you a lesson about PAIN!!! I am have come to the DEWF for three reasons. Number 1, to eliminate SuperBlonde. He has decided to go against his Karma and now it will decide the fate of him, and that is a fate worse than death. Pain and Suffering will NEVER leave him! Number 2, to become some kind of champion. I will NEVER take a shot against a stable member, but if I have to wrestle against them to get my shot, I will. Number 3, is to make sure the Dangerous Alliance is the STRONGEST, BEST stable in the world. (cackles insanely and we fade via static back to Joey) Styles: Alllllll RIGHTY then. Fans, that was OUR Prozac moment for the week, eh? (shudders) Anyhow, up next we have "The Franchise" Shane Douglas as he takes on "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz. "The Franchise" has so far refused any comment as to what he did to Ric Flair, but we hope to have *some* word from him on Sunday Slam. Let's go to the ring! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz (w/ Bill Alphonso) vs. "The Franchise" Shane Douglas (w/ SIster Sherri and Too Cold Scorpio) Taz came down to ringside, along with Bill Alfonso, who was a'blowin' away on his trusty whistle. Much to Alfonso's surprise, many of the fans at ringside *also* had whistles, and were whistling along. Quite a din, actually. His walk to the ring was the most enjoyable part OF the match for Taz as Shane Douglas quite frankly kicked his ass. Shane got EXTREME on Taz, hitting him with everything BUT the kitchen sink. At one point, Shane clocked Taz with a chair *six* *straight* *times*! But *still* Taz would not stay down. A couple minutes later, after getting hit in the head with a baseball bat by Douglas *twice*, Taz did indeed stay down, courtesy of a wicked belly to belly suplex. (match time: 6:04) Of course, the fun was just beginning. Douglas and Too Cold began double teaming Taz with the apparent objective of putting him out for good. They knocked out all attempts to make them leave. Until "Frankenstein" by Edgar Winter began playing.... Yep.. *911 in da house! PAAAAARTTTYYYY TIIIIIMMMMMEEEE!* Too Cold attacked the big man as he stepped over the top rope with a boot to the midsection. Fat chance. 911 simply heaved Scorpio clear over the top rope and into the front row like a ragdoll. Too bad Shane wasn't paying attention, eh? When Douglas turned around, he walked STRAIGHT into the clutches of 911. Yep! You guessed it! Going UP! GOING down! *CHOKESLAM!!!!!!* Douglas rolled out of the ring into the arms of the terrified Sister Sherri and they beat feet post haste. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Styles: OH MY GOD!!!!!! Fans, we are out of time once again! We'll leave you with this video from The Outlaws! See you on Sunday Slam! For the DEWF, I'm Joey Styles! (Scene shows an old,beaten-up cadillac pull into a parking space in front of a local police station. Smoke pours from the tailpipe as Stan Hansen opens the driver's side door and spits a large wad of brown tobacco juice on the spray-painted handicapped sign he just parked over. The camera follows him into the station where he goes through several doors and makes his way to a jail cell.) Hansen (screaming at the occupants) "WAKE UP YOU BUNCH O' SISSIES!!!" (The startled man on the lower bunk quickly raises his head and bashes into the upper bunk. As fast as he awoke, he's now unconscious again. As the officer opens the cell door, it can be seen that the semi-conscious man is "Captain Redneck" Dick Murdoch.) Hansen (slapping Murdoch) "Wake up Dickie...we've gotta git goin'!!!" Murdoch (hand to his forehead and raising up MUCH slower) " I'm coming Stan...keep your damn boots on." (Wipes his mouth with his "Eat More Possum" t-shirt) (The camera pans to show Terry Funk executing a sleeper on a unwilling victim. After several seconds the man falls to the floor and Funk reaches into his bluejeans pocket and pulls out a box of matches. He lights several of them and begins to burn the man's hair. Hansen catches wind of the smell and turns to stomp out the fire when he realizes he's stomping on another human's head he stomps immediately.) Hansen"Funk...what the hell are you doin boy?! You fellas been couped up long enough and now that the DEWF is back in town...we'll let's just say that the OULTAWS are ready to roll..." Funk (begins to burn his forearm with the matches) "That's right Stan...we're ready to put a hurtin' on the DEWF again...and when all is said and done we've already won!"
