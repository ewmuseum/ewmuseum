DEWF Sunday Slam for 4/28/1996! (Part One)

29 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sorry for the delay, folks. We had a nasty storm last night and power is still iffy, so I thoguht I'd let you know. (Open on Joey Styles in the DEWF Arena in Da Ring) Styles: I'm Joey Styles and THIS is the Dream Extreme Wrestling Federation! (Be-Pop-a-loola) Let's get this party started as Super Blonde takes on "Devious" Don E. Allen! (Poppoppop) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Super Blonde (w/ Kismet) vs. "Devious" Don E. Allen *This* match showed Super Blonde off a lot better than his last one. In what was little more than a workout for him, Super Blonde put on an impressive aerial display, as well as showing off some nice mat work. He put "The Devious One" away with a slingshot bodyblock. (match time: 5:15) As happens a lot of times with the DEWF, the interesting part came *after* the match when a heavily distorted voice came over the PA as Super Blonde was leaving: Voice: (heavily distorted, very deep) Super Blonde! Watch your back everywhere you go, my friend. I shall be watching you - everywhere. (Distorted laughter as the S-Blonde leaves, looking a bit disturbed) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (back to JoJo by the DEWF logo) Joey: A strange voice from on high? No, it wasn't Steve McMichael actually trying to sound intelligent at the announcer's desk. Who was it? Maybe we'll find out at some point. While I head to the ring to interview The Legion Of Doom, let's watch a tape we got from Ted DiBiase! Million Dollar Dreams - Part One "If it's Tuesday, this must be Needles..." [We open in Duke's House of Waffles in Needles, Arizona, where about 12 people sit huddled around a rather rickety ring. In the background, we can see an obviously hand-drawn banner hanging on the wall, reading "WCWF Championship Wrestling."] [Ted Dibiase and Virgil sit in the front row.] Dibiase: You know, Virgil, I'm beginning to wonder about the wisdom of selling stock to that Billionaire Ted guy. I mean, just about every star we've ever had has defected to the DEWF now. Virgil: But we've still got the Hulkster. Dibiase: Yes, I know. But I think he's finding it hard to follow his own reputation anymore... [As if in response, we see Hogan, with the WCWF World title belt, in one corner of the ring, while in the other we see 24 armed mercenaries, all growling menacingly. The bell rings, and all 24 drop to the mat on cue while Hulk poses after another successful title defense.] Dibiase: This is really embarrassing. Virgil: Even more than my "Ice Ice Virgil" video, right boss? Dibiase: Uh, no. That was pretty stupid, Virgil. [Meanwhile, in the ring, Hogan does a particularly strenuous pose and drops to the mat in pain. He does a tremendous job of selling an incredibly minor injury until the paramedics come out to stretcher him out of the ring.] Dibiase: There must be a better way to run this federation... [Hogan is really into it now, giving out "Get Well Hulk" cards to the 12 fans at ringside, none of whom seem to really care about his state of health.] Dibiase: Let's get out of here. HEY, WHO WANTS TO BUY A WRESTLING FEDERATION? [Several of the rednecks raise their hands. Dibiase picks one and calls out to him.] You, what'll you give me for the federation? Redneck: Fifty dollars and a pig! Dibiase: Sold! But keep the pig. [The redneck appears to be happier than a pig in shit, having acquired a federation for fifty dollars, and a pig to boot.] [Dibiase and Virgil get up to leave.] Dibiase: Let's go. I know insane asylums with better talent than that balding geriatric reject. [Hogan is doing his "epileptic seizure" act.] Virgil: Hey, good joke, boss. Insane asylum. Ha, very funny. [Dibiase isn't smiling.] Dibiase: I never joke. [End part one.] (Joey Styles is in the ring) [JS] Ladies and gentlemen, led to the ring by Paul Ellering, THE LEGION OF DOOM!!!! ("Iron Man" comes on and the fans be-pop-alooza as the four walk down to the ring looking like the bad-asses they are followed by Paul) [JS] Well, let's start with the Road Warriors(fans pop!!) Well, at Rumble in the Jungle, you guys got beat at your own game in the Street Fight(fans boo) by Harlem Heat! [Hawk] Yea, ya got a point Styles!? [JS] Well, Rumble in the Jungle also saw your "buddies" win the titles back. So what's your take on the situation?? [Hawk] Well, you're right Styles, we got suckered by Harlem Heat. But Harlem Heat, we're not done with you!! But on the tag titles. Let's do this right!! Me and Animal never got our shots the right way!! We want to get those straps, but first on our list are the Razor Boys!! You boys are talkin bout not gettin in the ring with anyone!? Well BOYS, The Roadies are MEN, and when you get in the ring with us, you'll never live to see what it's like to be MEN!!(fans poppity-pop-pop!!) [Animal] HARLEM HEAT!! Let's do it the right way!! You boys can street fight, we can street fight, so how bout we do this again!? No managers around this time, just The Harlem Heat, and The Road Warriors beatin the SHIT outta each other!! And to the Razor Boys, you made the mistakes of your lives puttin out an open contract!! It's gonna be you BOYS meetin the Roadies, and believe me, IT WON'T BE PRETTY!!! [JS] Wow, I'd hate to be the Razor Boys!! Now onto Big Daddy Cool, Diesel!! Rumble in the Jungle saw you outsmarting and defeating your longtime rival, SANDMAN!! [D] Ya got that right styles!! Sandman was finally beat!! I said it, ANY weapons. Sandman, you brought yours, and I BROUGHT MINE(slaps Vader on the chest)!!!! [JS] And speaking of Vader and Sandman, Vader, you got your revenge on Sandman at Rumble in the Jungle for the injury, and then on Mike Awesome for tossing you from the Rumble just a little while back. But what about what Sandman did AFTER the match with Awesome!? [V] SANDMAN!!! I don't know what you're tryin to pull boy!!! But whatever it is, if you double-cross Vader, it'll be Vader Time, ON YOUR ASS!!!!(fans pop huge) [JS] And now to you Paul Ellering, you're the power behind this unbelie....(fans begin to boo TONS) What do YOU want!? (camera pans to show Vince McMahon walking down. McMahon grabs the mic) [VM] What's going on here!? I thought I was the only one who could interview the Legion of Doom!? What is this Todd Pettingill wanna-be doing interviewing you!? (Ellering grabs the mic) [PE] Well Vinnie, to quote one of your own creations, we DON'T LUUUUUUUV you!!! [JS] OH MY GOD!!! DIESEL JUST NAILED VINCE MCMAHON!! Whips him into the ropes, BIG BOOT!!! JACKNIFE!!! Animal picks him up, lifts him onto his shoulders, OH MY GOD!!! NO!!! DOOMSDAY DEVICE!!! Vader climbs up top, VADERSAULT!!!!! OH MY GOD, VINCE MCMAHON ISN'T MOVING!!! ("Iron Man" plays and it's "Pop 101"!! The four get up onto the corners and begin playing to the crowd before heading back. They just *have* left the ring when Jerry "The King" Lawler and *The Razor Boys* come down to and enter the ring, where Lawler grabs the microphone) Lawler: HEY! HEY! Did I just hear those two poster children for Geritol, The Road Warriors, say they want my Razor Boys? We ain't hard to find, you two jerks! ("Iron Man" begins playing again as the Roadies barrel down to the ring and we got ourselves a match!) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ *Impromptu Tag Match!* The Road Warriors (Hawk and Animal w/ Paul Ellering) vs. The Razor Boys (Paul Diamond and Al Perez w/ Jerry "The King" Lawler) Lawler got the hell out of Dodge as soon as "Iron Man" began playing. He's no coward, but he's no damned fool, either. This match was brutal, brutal, brutal. The Razor Boys gave every bit as good as they got from the Roadies. But in the end, both teams got DQ'd when in quick succession, referee John Finnegan was shoved down by Hawk and then Al Perez. (match time: 9:07) The two teams brawled ALL the way to the back and were last seen fighting all the way down Swanson Street, outside the DEWF Arena. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (Crowd is Mo' Poppin', Mo' Poppin', Mo' Poppin' as we cut back to Joey) Styles: Fans, it is getting EXTREME here tonight! When we come back, we will have Sabu defending the DEWF World Light Heavyweight Championship against Naoki Sano as well as interviews with Interim DEWF Commissioner Tod Gordon and "Too Sexy" Brian Christopher along with his tag team partner, Too Cold Scorpio! (commercial for the May 14th World Premiere on FOX television of the new Doctor Who movie. Okay, so I'm a fan. :))
