DEWF — Saturday Night's MAIM Event

DEWF Saturday Night's MAIM Event!!!

14 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sorry this is so late, folks. I'se been busy. (We open on the packed DEWF arena as "Thunderkiss '65" is playing. "The Mad Dogs Of War", The Pitbulls, are heading to the ring, followed by General Skandor Akbar.) Styles: Hello, everyone, and WELCOME to the return of DEWF's Saturday Night's MAIM Event! I'm Joey Styles, and as you can see, "The Mad Dogs Of War" The Pitbulls are heading to the ring to kick it off tonight against Stevie Ray and Booker T, Harlem Heat. (Cut to an actual shot of Joey in the eagle's nest.) Tonight we will have *eight* outstanding matches for you (Harlem Heat's WCW theme music starts up) so let's get to the ring! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Harlem Heat (Stevie Ray and Booker T) vs. The Pitbulls (#1 and #2) (w/ "Gentleman" Jim Cornette) (w/ General Skandor Akbar) Jim Cornette scrambled for cover as The Pitbulls attacked the Heat in the aisle! Harlem Heat are big and bad, but The Pitbulls are big, bad and EXTREME. They showed it by giving the Heat unquestionably the worst beating they'd ever had. Figuratively speaking, the Heat got burned. Literally speaking, Da 'Bulls beat hell out of Stevie Ray and Booker T for nine long minutes and then finally put Stevie Ray away with a Superbomb for the three count. (match time: 09:46) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Sandman (w/ Naomi Campbell) vs. 573 (w/ "Sex Symbol" Travis Cook) "Bring Tha Noize" brought 573 to ringside, alongside the self-proclaimed "Sexiest MoMutant On Earth" Travis Cook. 573 was yellling at and abusing Cook all the way down the aisle. After they *got* to ringside, 573 ordered Cook to "get his candy ass to the back". When Cook tried to plead with 573, he got a wicked cane shot on the behind for his troubles. Cook beat a hasty retreat. Cook had no sooner made it through the curtains than "Enter Sandman" began playing and out came Sandman, accompanied by Naomi Campbell. Sandman tried to go through with his usual ritual of walking around ringside while "Enter Sandman" played, but 573 launched himself off the ring apron with a flying clothesline to the back of Sandman's head. 573 then began *whaling* on Sandman with vicious shots from the Singapore Cane after he threw Sandman back in. Referee John Finnegan *tried* to stop 573's rampage, but got a Singapore Cane shot between the eyes for his trouble. 573 brought in a steel chair to use - and the lights went out. When they came back up, *The Undertaker* was standing in the aisle! 573 dropped the chair, and began yelling at UT. *Big* mistake. Sandman got up, and hit a HOME run with the chair right in the back of 573's head as Undertaker turned to leave. Sandman threw out the chair, and covered 573 just as Finnegan woke up. One, two, three. (match time: 11:01) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Undertaker (w/ Paul Bearer) vs. "The Man Of 1,000 Holds" Dean Malenko (w/ "Gentleman" Jim Cornette) This match would surely test Malenko's reputation as an iceman, having to stare down The Grim Reaper of professional wrestling. Malenko *was* an iceman, not letting UT's reputation or his spookiness affect him. Malenko, surprisingly, used more of his underappreciated aerial arsenal than the mat work he was famous for. Malenko dominated the early going of the match with it, because UT seemed unprepared for the aerial assault of Malenko. Then, Malenko nailed a *lethal* looking flying dropkick, and went for the cover. One problem - he forgot to hook the leg. UT *benchpressed* Malenko off of him with such force that Malenko flew through the middle ropes to the outside. As Malenko got back in, Undertaker *sat up*. Malenko dove in with overhand forearm shots - but no go. Undertaker got to his feet, flipped his hair back out of his eyes and *stared* right at Malenko. Malenko kicked UT in the midsection and whipped him off the ropes and went for a clothseline. Undertaker ducked, ricocheted off the opposite ropes and *leveled* Malenko with a brutal flying clothesline! Undertaker hauled the dazed Malenko to his feet by his throat. Can *you* guess what's coming here? CHOKESLAM! UT again hauled Malenko to his feet, setting up for the Tombstone. Just *as* UT got Malenko set, here came 573 barreling down the aisle and into the ring - where he promptly caned *Malenko*, costing UT the match by DQ. (match time: 15:52) But the fun still wasn't over. 573 was caning hell out of Undertaker, particularly his knees and back. After all, if Undertaker can't set you for the Tombstone, there goes 95% of his offense. 573 was relentless. When the cane finally burst into splinters, he grabbed a chair and began whaling away with that. The Ref Brigade tried to stop him. They got chaired into the middle of next week. The Jobber Brigade tried and either got chairshotted or A-B-SEEya Spinebustered into a Chiropractor's dream come true. Finally, 573 went outside and brought a table into the ring. He heaved the unmoving Undertaker to the top rope, went up himself - and A-B-SEEyaed the Grim Reaper THROUGH THE TABLE FROM THE TOP! Styles: Oh my GOD!!!!!! 573 finally flipped off Undertaker and left. Damage Control was a long time in being able to get Undertaker out. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Harbingers (Raven and The Crow, w/o Paul Bearer) vs. The New Rockers (Marty Janetty and Leif Cassidy, w/ Dancin' Stevie Richards) The Harbingers came down to the ring, looking grimmer than usual, and with good reason. Paul Bearer was in the back, tending to Undertaker. Then the New Rocker's music pumped over the PA and they danced down to ringside, accompanied by Dancin' Stevie Richards. Styles: That clueless putz is a MANAGER now? Oy vey. Regardless of what most might think of him, Dancin' Stevie was apparently just what the doctor ordered for the Rockers. They absolutely DOMINATED the first six minutes. The Harbingers, frankly, couldn't tie their shoes against the Rockers in the early going. But, as usual, the Rockers were celebrating when they should have been going for the pin. Case in point: Marty Janetty hit The Crow with the infamous Rocker Dropper at about five and a half minutes in and the mysterious one was *out*. Did Janetty go for the pin? Noooooo. He and Cassidy were too busy celebrating how cool they were. Yeesh. BAD move, guys. The Crow kipped up and supekicked Janetty out of the ring, and gave Leif Cassidy a stiff right hand in the mouth for good measure! When Janetty crawled back in the ring, The Crow tagged out to Raven and hit Janetty with a Doctor Bomb, followed up by Raven dropping the elbow on Janetty from the second rope. Raven promptly tagged back out to The Crow and whipped Janetty into the ropes, and flattened him with a spinebuster slam. As The Crow came off the middle of the top rope with a sprinboard moonsault, Raven sarcastically called out "A-B-SEEya!" and gave the finger to the back curtains in an obvious message to 573. The Crow hit Janetty with a swinging neckbreaker and tagged back out to Raven, who lifted up Janetty in an excruciating bow-and-arrow submission maneuver. Leif Cassidy made a baseball slide dropkick to Raven's head to break the hold. As Cassidy left, the Harbingers whipped Janetty into the ropes - but Janetty ducked and on the rebound he hit the Harbingers with a double clothesline and made the hot tag to Cassidy. Cassidy came in, A House On Fire (tm, Gorilla Monsoon) and began hammering away at Raven with stiff forearm shots. But *yet again* Cassidy decided to celebrate instead of pressing the advantage. When he went for an Enzoogory (tm, Dok Hendrix), Raven *ducked* and Cassidy hit the canvas hard. Raven hauled Cassidy back to his feet and leveled him with a short clothesline. For the next *fifteen minutes* the two teams cut the crap and concentrated on pounding each other into goat cheese. Raven was the man in the ring for ninety percent of the time, and the Rockers pounded him into a bloody mess. Richards had finally got the Rockers to quit celebrating and concentrate on winning the damned match, already. :) *Twenty three minutes* into the match, the Rockers hit Raven with a *nasty* double DDT. Cassidy, the legal man, went up top to drop a big elbow on Raven - and MISSED when Raven rolled out of the way!!! Raven made a painfully slow tag to The Crow, who somersaulted into the ring and began dropping the Rockers like bad habits with vicious running clotheslines. Janetty grabbed a chair from ringside and tried to hit The Crow with it when he got back in the ring. Fat chance. The Crow booted Janetty in the midsection, grabbed the chair, and nailed *Janetty* with it. Cassidy made a blind charge and *he* got a chairshot between the eyes for his trouble. The Crow Doctor Bombed Janetty and went up top - and was met by Stevie Richards who BLISTERED The Crow with a chair shot, causing The Crow to fall backwards and into the ring. When referee Jim Mollineaux tried to stop Richards' rampage, he got a Steviekick in the mush for his trouble. Instant DQ, boys and girls. (match time: 24:27) Both teams fought all the way back to the locker rooms. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF Intercontinental Title Match* "Sexy Boy" Shawn Michaels (w/ Naomi Campbell) *Challenger* vs. "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel (w/ Paul Ellering) *Champion* Yet another classic between these two. Michaels dazzled the crowd *and* Diesel with high flying maneuvers and sheer speed and "Big Daddy Cool" nailed Michaels with some, er, high octane power moves. Michaels, for all the speed and aerial maneuvers he was using, pursued the sensible tactic of trying to draw out the match because the longer the match went, the more tired Diesel would become. One problem - Michaels got tired over the long haul too because of Diesel turning him into Silly Putty with power moves at every opportunity. Michaels, though, held the advantage until the fourteen minute mark when he sent Diesel flying over the ropes by ducking a clothesline *against* the ropes. Unfortunately, Michaels missed a plancha attempt and was out cold after hitting the concrete. Diesel wasted no time at all in pitching Michaels back in the ring and *flattening* Michaels with a Jack Knife powerbomb. Say goodnight, Shawn. (match time: 14:33) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- *The Trail Of Revenge* Chris Candido (w/ Sister Sherri) vs. "Hard Rock" Paul Diamond (w/ Black Sarah) Well, *this* was certainly a match for the ages - NOT. Candido rushed Diamond in the corner - but Diamond moved! Diamond quickly jumped on the second turnbuckle and then back off again with a *beautiful* turnbuckle bodypress! The cover! One-Two-THREE!!!!!! (match time: 00:00:06) Six seconds FLAT!!!! Laughing hysterically, Diamond and Black Sarah made their way to the back while Candido ranted and raved about a quick count. It got worse for poor Candido. A *crow* flew down from the rafters and chased he and Sister Sherri back to the locker rooms! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF World Tag Team Title Match* The Road Warriors (Animal and Hawk, w/ Paul Ellering) *Challengers* vs. The Gangstaz (New Jack and Mustafa) *Champions* This one really *was* a match for the ages. The FATHERS of Extreme tag team wrestling going up against the only true heir to their legacy of extreme ever, The Gangstaz. New Jack and Mustafa used everything but the kitchen sink on the Warriors, who were MORE than glad to return the favor. The simple fact of the matter was, as bad ass and extreme as they were, The Gangstaz were simply outclassed against Hawk and Animal. What set up the end was when New Jack flew off the top with the Chairshot From Hell - and Animal moved! New Jack went face first into his own chair. Mustafa was a bloody mess on the outside and completely unable to help. Animal quickly tagged out to Hawk and lifted up New Jack as the crowd went berzerk - DOOMSDAY DEVICE! ONE-TWO-THREE!!!!! (match time: 16:55) Announcer: Your winners, and NNNEEEEWWWWWWWWW DEWF World Tag Team Champions, Hawk and Animal, The ROAAAAAAAAAAAAAD WAAAARRRIOOORRRRRSS!!!! A dejected New Jack and Mustafa were men enough to strap the belts on the Warriors and raise their hands in victory. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Styles: WE HAVE NEW CHAMPIONS! WE HAVE NEW CHAMPIONS! (Crowd starts booing like all hell as Shane Douglas makes his way to ringside, cocky as ever) Shane Douglas, "The Franchise", YOU are going to face 911 tonight in - (Douglas grabs the mic) Douglas: Just a damned second, Joey Styles! Tod Gordon, come out here right now! (Gordon comes out, looking surprisingly sullen) Tod Gordon, why don't you tell these sorry sacks of sh*t (BOOS) what's happened to my match with that COWARD 911. (Laughs like hell) Gordon: Joey, 911 is nowhere to be found! Styles: WHAT??? Gordon: He hasn't shown up yet! As far as we can tell, he's not even in the building! (Shock and BOOOs galore) Douglas: (laughing) Tell him the rest of it, Gordon. (Breaks up again) Gordon: As much as it sickens me to do it, I'm going to have to award this match to Shane Douglas by forfeit if 911 can't answer the bell. Ring it, Rocko! (Flips off Douglas and leaves as the crowd BOOs Douglas to hell and gone) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- *EXTREME Death Match* 911 (?) vs. "The Franchise" Shane Douglas Referee "Pee Wee" Moore began the count. 10... 9... 8... Styles: Oh, man. Don't TELL me... 7... 6... 5... Styles: The jerkoff is going to win by FORFEIT??? 4... 3... 2... Styles: This is disgusting.... 1.... At that precise point..... "Frankenstein" began blaring over the PA as ***911*** hurtled down the aisle and into the ring!!!!!! 911 was all over a shocked Douglas like a cheap suit on Gorilla Monsoon with forearms to the back, chairs, chokes, Boottothehead!s and clotheslines. 911 looked like he was taking the premise of a "Death" match a little too seriously. He looked he didn't want to win so much as KILL Douglas! Douglas became frantic, and hit 911 with several chair shots - but the big man just shrugged them off and kept coming! For seven and a half full minutes, 911 just plain damned *pureed* Douglas. I mean, Douglas was bleeding buckets! At the 7:41 mark, 911 poured Douglas back into the ring and hauled him up high by the throat, showed him to all four corners and then - HERE CAME MISTER HUGHES INTO THE RING WITH A BALL BAT! Hughes TEED off on 911 in the back of the head, and the big man dropped like a ton of rocks. Referee Moore was helpess to do anything since it was no DQ. Hughes rolled Douglas on top of the unconscious 911 - one - two - THREE!!! The ten count was academic - 911 was *out*. (match time: 08:21) Hughes slung the equally unconscious Douglas over his shoulder and smugly strolled to the back while people were trying to climb the rails and strangle his big ass. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Styles: WHAT the F*CK was THAT sh*t all about??? We'll see you on th Ross Report! F*CK this! (throws down the mike in disgust and leaves) (fade to black) Referee John Finnegan began the count "And you must be the boy who makes the tea!" --- Edmund Blackadder (to Baldrick, under a hood), Blackadder 2: "Head"
