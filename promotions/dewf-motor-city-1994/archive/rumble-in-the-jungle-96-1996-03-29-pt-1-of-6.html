DEWF — Rumble In The Jungle

SURPRISE! DEWF Rumble In The Jungle '96!!! Part One (long)

29 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Open on the Madison Square Garden . It is deserted and the sun is just rising. As the sun rises (time lapse photography - ain't it wonderful?), we come to see that a *band* has setup shop on the roof of the Garden. We zoom in to see that it is KISS! Everyone's kind of standing around. After a moment, Gene Simmons turns to the camera, glowering. He picks up his bass and the rest of the band looks into the camera, getting ready to play. Joey Styles walks out onto the rooftop, looking different than we've ever seen him. Torn bluejeans, denim jacket, black t-shirt and a bandana around his head. He swaps high-fives with the band and turns to the camera, himself) Styles: (snarls) Hit the music. (KISS immediately roars into "Warmachine", the DEWF theme song. We pull back to a wide shot of the Garden again and again do the time lapse photography thing, this time watching as the sun rises even further. The streets begin to fill and a line grows and grows and grows outside the Garden. The DEWF logo drops into place, letter by letter, over the exterior shot of the Garden. When the last letter has dropped into place, "Rumble In The Jungle '96" is sprayed across the logo in neon red letters. The screen shatters and we are inside with JoJo looking a bit more like his old self. He is in the middle of the ring and the crowd is doing Pop Of The Gods) Styles: Hello, wrestling fans and WELCOME one and all to DEWF RUMBLE IN THE JUNGLE 1996!!!!!!! (Insane Pop) WE ARE ****LIVE**** from Madison Square Garden in the middle of the concrete jungle - NEW YORK CITY! (See previous pop) At this time, I'd like to introduce my color commentator for tonight - from Glasgow, Scotland - "ROWWWWWWDYYYY" RODDDYYYYYYY PIIIIIPPPEERRRRRRRRRRRR!!!!!!! ("My Bonnie Lassie's Coming Home To Me" starts as the Rowdy One makes his way down to ringside, grinning a mile a minute. He gets in the ring with JoJo and clowns around as things calm down a bit (relatively speaking, of course).) "Rowdy" Roddy Piper, welcome. (The Rowdy One yanks the mic from Styles. Joey shrugs and grins) Piper: This is the DEWF, baby! We don't take dives and we DAMN sure don't do Windows! (Pop 101) Rumble In The Jungle is here, baby, and I gotta tell ya, I've been in pro wrestling longer than Joey Styles has had pubic hairs - well, that's not actually very long, is it? Nice, sweet kid. Love ya, JoJo. Let's do lunch. - and I have NEVER been involved with anything THIS f***ing insane, EVER. Styles: We'll be getting to the matches quickly, but I wanted to tell you about a match we had before we went on the air. In The Battle Of The Satellite Of Love, Joel Robinson took on Mike Nelson! Let's go to some highlights. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ***BATTLE OF THE SATELLITE OF LOVE*** Joel Robinson (w/Crow T. Robot) vs. Mike Nelson (w/ Tom Servo) Special Referee: Gypsy With this match, it really, REALLY helped to be on a bad acid trip to truly enjoy it. Joel displayed some unforseen aerial ability, hitting some half decent planchas and a FrankenHurricanSteinerRanaWhateverThey'reCallingItThisWeek thingy. Special refereee Gypsy was truly annoying, because she kept breaking things up, reminding the boys to "Play Nice". This wasn't nearly as bad a match as anyone expected. Nelson seemed to take on the heel role, choking Joel on several occasions. What about Crow and Servo, you ask? They were playing Bingo with a rather strange group from Missouri. Anyhow, the match was back and forth until about eleven and a half minutes in when Nelson hit Robinson with a *nice* belly to belly off the ropes. Mike set Joel on the top rope and tried a Franksteiner from the top buckle - which Joel converted into a POWER BOMB from the top!!! All together now - "OWIEOWIEOWIEOWIEOWIEOWIE!" (match time: 12:15) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Styles: NICE match! Those guys may have a future in wrestling. Piper: Yah. About like Rush Limbaugh has a future as Frank N Furter in a touring company of Rocky Horror. Styles: ANYway, let's have our official opening contest. From the MMWA, Rajin Cajin takes on "Devious" Don E. Allen! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Rajin Cajin vs. Don E. Allen The Rather Strange Group From Missouri was at it again. As the massive Cajin climbed in the ring with The Devious One, they started chanting "Don E. Allen's gonna die, doo dah, doo dah!" This was just plain ugly. Cajin could hardly keep from laughing as he pounded Allen into Silly Putty. I swear he even tried to dribble Allen like a basketball at one point! About a minute in, Cajin pointed to the RSGFM who called for a powerbomb. A powerbomb they got. Actually, calling it a powerbomb is being euphemistic in the extreme. What it looked like was Cajin tried to powerbomb Allen straight through the ring to floor below. He almost succeeded. The RSGFM started in again, calling for a choke slam. Cajin hauled Allen up so high he damn near threw him over his back and then WHAM down to the mat, and damn near through it, again. Cajin planted a single pinkie on Allen as the ref counted the one-two-three. (match time: 2:23) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Piper: Can YOU say "Spatula Time", boys and girls? I *knew* you could! Styles: All I can tell you is, *I'd* love to be Don E. Allen's chiroprator right now! Piper: (laughs) No kidding! Styles: Next up we have the most unbelievable team in recent years, Taz and his supposed mortal enemy, "The Macho Man" Randy Savage, taking on Knobbs and Saggs, The Nasty Boys. For a long, long time Taz looked like he was still on his Path Of Rage. He and Randy Savage traded the DEWF Title belt back and forth until Taz lost a match in which he was never to receive another shot at Savage, thanks to the mistimed interference of his manager, former referee Bill Alfonso. Then some weeks later, Taz won a Royal Rumble in which the winner received a shot at Savage. Taz came in number one and stayed in the whole Rumble, outlasting 29 other men to come out the victor! It was felt if he could do that, he *deserved* the title shot. Then came the match itself. Taz made a big show of sending a protesting Bill Alfonso back to the dressing room to allay the fears of Randy Savage of Alfonso interefering. The two wrestled in what many are calling one of the matches of the year. Then Fonzie came back down to ringside with a chair and distracted referee Bronco Lubich. Taz abandoned all pretense of a face turn and hung the chair around Savage's neck - and then Tazplexed him for the one-two-three! Then DEWF commissioner [name withheld] quickly stepped in to strip Taz of the belt for "conduct unbecoming a champion", for which Taz brutally attacked the then commissioner, causing him to be put in the hospital. But bizarrely, this seem to bring on a genuine change in both Bill Alfonso and Taz. Weeks later, Alfonso reluctantly let The Giant, Cactus Jack and The Nasty Boys go from his Team Alfonso. The cowards immediately *attacked* Alfonso until of all people PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY came to Alfonso's aid! All this accomplished was getting Paul E. hospitalized in serious condition. Alfonso went on the ambulance to the hospital with Paul E. and has been a changed man ever since. Then, in the incident that brought this match about, Taz was wrestling The Great Muta in Korakuen Hall when The Giant, Cactus and The Nastie attacked HIM this time. Bill Alfonso emerged from the back with HELP for Taz - Randy Savage! (fade from clips back to JoJo and Piper) Styles: That brings us to today. Piper: Ya know, I have seen some guys in my time that were pretty tough customers. Everybody knows what a badass Randy Savage is. Well I'm here to tell ya, this Taz is a throwback to the old days of guys like Dick The Bruiser and Pampero Firpo. Not a lot of fancy dan moves, just gets in the ring, raises hell and takes care of buisness, baby! Well, Nasties? Yer gonna get a one way ride on the "I Got My Ass Kicked" Express, courtesy of Randy Savage and "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz! Styles: Let's go to the ring! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ***SPECIAL GRUDGE MATCH*** "The Macho Man" Randy Savage and "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) vs. The Nasty Boys (w/ Cactus Jack and The Giant) This, friends, was a war. Before that, though, Alfonso had promised a surprise during this match. he went to the back and wheeled down his MOTHER, an old gray thing in a wheelchair (I'm talkin' shawl, grey hair, muffler wrapped around her lower face - the whole nine yards). Taz and Savage immediately used the confusion to lay into The Nastys hard and heavy while Cactus and The Giant kept pointing and yelling at Alfonso's dear, sweet, *INNOCENT* mama. The Nasties gave back as good as they got, and wrestled the match of their lives in all honesty. Unfotunately for them, so did Savage and Taz. I won't even TRY to list all the foreign objects used. Even the Rather Strange Group From Missouri (who shall go nameless) produced a cake pan, which Mister Savage introduced to Mister The Back Of Knobbs' Head, several times. Later on, while the battle wasn't going nearly so well for Taz and Savage, Cactus and The Giant were menacing Alfonso and his mother. From the back came Shinya Hashimoto and Toshiaki Kawada who ran the two off! (Note: Don't cheer for them just yet! :)) As Knobbs was jawing with Hasimoto, Randy Savage came off the top with his patented flying double axe-handle. Savage tagged out to Taz, and threw Jerry Sags over the top when he came in. Taz Tazplexed Knobbs and tagged right back out again to Savage who dropped The Big Elbow (tm) on Knobbs. Say goodnight, Brian. (match time: 33:13) It was at that point that Hashimoto and Kawada got in the ring and raised Taz and Savage's hand in victory. While they celebrated, *Tiger Jeet Singh* ran to ringside and flattened Alfonso with the butt of his sword!!!!!!! Kawada and Hashimoto quickly turned on Taz and Savage and began beating them like eggs. Singh kicked and stomped Alfonso while taunting Alfonso's mama - who *stood up* while Singh had his back turned and had his attention on putting the boots to Fonzie. Alfonso's mama produced a - *cellular phone* from her pocket, and with a home run swing, knocked Tiger into La La Land! Hashimoto and Kawada immeidately hauled Alfonso's mama into the ring - where the wig, shawl and various accoutrements started falling away piece by piece. Gee, just who call in an emergency such as this? Why, ***911*** of course! PARTY TIME! 911 double chokeslammed Hashimoto and Kawada as Alfonso's mama finally stood revealed as none other than ***PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY***!!!!! (Gee, what a surprise, huh? :)) The Usual Gang Of Idiots cleared out the Nasties, Hashimoto, Kawada, and Singh as Taz, Savage, 911 and Paul E. helped Alfonso to the back. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (Crowd is doing the Psycho Apesh*t Pop Of The Gods) Styles: OH MY ***GOD***!!!!! Piper: Ya know what's scary? This was only the second match? What the hell kind of pyschotic sh*t is gonna happen the REST of the card??? Styles: Let's take an intermission and we'll find out! (Coundown clock begins) "That man does NOT want to know all the things I can do to him with a butter knife!" - Brigadier General Adrienne Kramer *DOCTOR WHO - TIME RIFT*
