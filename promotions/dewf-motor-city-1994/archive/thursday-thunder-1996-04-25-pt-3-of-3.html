DEWF — Thursday Thunder

DEWF Thursay Thunder for 4/25/96!!! (Conclusion)

25 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Commercial for Yada, Yada and Yada, Attorneys at law. When it's over we fade back to Joey) Styles: Our final match of the night pits "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair against new DEWF champion 911, in what will surely be 911's toguhest match ever. If Flair is smart, he'll get on his bicycle and stay there! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ***DEWF WORLD'S HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH*** "Nature Boy" Ric Flair vs. 911 (w/ Paul E. Dangerously) (challenger) (champion) Flair must not have heard Joey because he pounced on 911 quickly, chopping away. Flair threw 911 outside almost immediately, making the big man VERY angry. When 911 reentered the ring, Flair gouged his eyes and whipped him into the ropes, planting an elbow squarely in 911's chest. 911 got to his feet, but was quickly wrapped up in an inside cradle by Flair, who actually got a two count out of it. Flair nailed 911 with a swinging neckbreaker when he got up, but only got a two count. 911 bounced right back up. Flair blistered him with a lethal chop. Two count. Flair tried another chop, but got yanked in by 911 into a bearhug. 911 squoze for a good 13 seconds before Flair reached the ropes. Flair went into his begging off routine. 911 ricocheted himself off the opposite ropes - and ran straight into another chop. Nada. Flair fouind himself trapped in another bearhug, but got out quickly by gouging 911's eyes. Flair tried an abdominal stretch, but 911 was simply too big and went flying courtesy of a hiptoss. The match degenerated into an out and out brawl from there and 911 eventually got DQ'd for refusing to release a chokehold on Flair. (match time: 10:58) This extremely unpopular decision got referee Bronco Lubich Jr. chokeslammed not once, not twice, but three times! Flair got one for good measure and Paul E. even spat on him. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Styles: Not a terribly popular decision, and you can be sure 911 will be hit in the pocketbook for his actions after the match. How would *you* like to be the ones assigned to collect the fine from him? For the DEWF, I'm Joey Styles! See you next time! (voiceover the credits by Jim Ross) "Next Thursday on Thursday Thunder, we will hear from 911. We will also see the DEWF debuts of Octagon, and a new team called Absolutely Fabulous as they take on The Eliminators!"
