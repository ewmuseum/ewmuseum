DEWF Sunday Slam: The Road To The Rumble (Part One)

24 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(NOTE: Part Two will in likelihood be out tomorrow morning because - well let's jst say it'll be worth the wait! :)) (Open on the deserted parking lot of the DEWF Arena in South Philadelphia. A few thigns blow across the parking lot as, thanks to time lapse photography, we see the sun rise in very quick fashion and the lot begin to fill with cars, buses, trucks, etc., etc. We then go inside to Joey Styles in the ring as the DEWFMutants are going thoroughly bonkers) Styles: HellllllOOOOOOO, wrestling fans and WELCOME to DREAM Extreme Wrestling! This IS DEWF Sunday Slam: Road To The Rummmmmmmmmmble! (POP) Fans, we have got one whale of a show for you tonight, with four outstanding matches, including a rematch for the DEWF World Tag Team Titles that the Midnight Riders STOLE from South Philly's favorite sons, The Public Enemy (Crowd boos)! I've been informed that interim DEWF Commissioner Tod Gordon has a special stipulation in place for that match, and that the Riders and their cronies Demolition will NOT be happy about it at all! But right now, let's go to Jim Ross, who has a report on one of the single most bizarre incidents in recent professional wrestling history, the apparent rebirth of Hulk Hogan! Ladies and gentlemen, The Ross Report! (Crowd applauds) (clip of various Hogan highlights) Ross: (voice over) Hulk Hogan. Love him or hate him, there's no middle ground. But say what you will, you cannot deny that this man has single handedly turned the wrestling world on it's ear. The wrestling phenomenon known as "Hulkamania" was born in the Madison Square Garden in 1984 when Hulk Hogan pinned then WWF World Heavyweight Champion, The Iron Sheik, to win the WWF title. But over the ensuing years, Hulk began hearing the boos, even though he was supposedly the most popular superstar in the sport. He heard them and he heard them strong. He was accused of being a one-note wrestler, which he himself admits he was. But why fix it if it wasn't broke, he figured? Then, when Hulk returned at Wrestlemania 9 after Bret Hart had been robbed by Yokozuna and fifteen seconds later held the WWF strap again, he thought he'd silenced his critics. Was he ever wrong! Months later, demoralized and angry, he left the WWF for the last time. He surfaced in World Championship Wrestling, and quickly became the dominant force there as well. Still, the voices of his detractors wouldn't cease. He was reviled for taking a size seven shoe in the eye and being injured. But I defy anyone to take a stiletto heel in the eye, or anywhere ELSE for that matter, and not feel it. He tried and tried and tried in the ensuing months to regain his popularity, but to no avail. Then, he disappeared. That is, until recently. Hulk Hogan, along with a vastly different Eric Bischoff, came to the DEWF to try and regain his former glory. In what will probably go down in wrestling history as The Three Counts Heard Round The World, Hulk Hogan was BEATEN by men such as Brady Boone, Jim Powers and Barry Horowitz, men who could charitably be described as having less than stellar win-loss records. Then, frustrated beyond all belief, Hogan went ballistic on the source of his frustration - Eric Bischoff. The Hulkster beat his manager seven ways from Sunday and damn near killed him with a legdrop through a table to the concrete arena floor. What does the future hold for Hulk Hogan? We can only watch - and see. (cut to Jim in The Control Center) Ross: Hello, fans, we are just one week away from DEWF Rumble In The Jungle '96, which will be brought to you LIVE from the concrete jungle of New York City and the legendary Madison Square Garden! Fans, one of the most shocking developments outside of Hulk Hogan was the stunning upset by The Midnight Riders of Public Enemy for the DEWF World Tag Team Championship, with, I might add, a major assist from Demolition. Be that as it may, lets hear comments from the new champs and the former champs, Public Enemy. ("Captain Redneck" Dick Murdoch and BlackJack Mulligan sit on a crusty,beaten-up couch while Dutch Mantell is spawled in a shag-green, 1970's Lazy Boy recliner.) Mantell: (messes with neckbrace) Hehehe...look who's World Tag-Team Champions, that's right two of the dirtiest men in the sport.I told you no-good fer nuthin',wanna be cotton pickers that we'd walk out of Landover with the belts. And By GOD we shocked the world once again! Murdoch: (removes his hand from a bag of "Cheezies" and wipes his mouth with his "Eat More Possum" t-shirt) You're looking at the new DEWF tag-team champions. We're gonna bust more heads and kick more asses with these belts than you could shake a stick at. I've held this tag belts with Adrian Adonis,the Russians and plenty of others...but I'm proud to say that Sunday, I won the belt with the legendary BlackJack Mulligan. Tell 'em Jack.... Mulligan: There's been talk about Demolition having part to do with our victory...lemme say this...Rocco Rock, Smash was just helping you tie your boot. We're not gonna back down from anybody in this federation, the Midnight Riders are gonna be known as the fightingest champions in history. And Dusty Rhodes...your not forgotten boy...Terry and Jimmy Jack are down in Texas as I speak, formulating a plan to put your fat ass out of wrestling for good. Mantell: (looks at the camera and grabs his neckbrace with both hands) Dusty Rhodes, you have caused me a lot of pain boy...and you Will pay through the nose. Just remember...God forgives and the Outlaws don't. (fade to NYC slums) Rocco Rock: Man oh man, Demolition you just set yourself up for a whole lot of ass whupping. Costing us the belts was the worst thing you could have done! Johnny Grunge: We are the Public Enemy. whether you like or not, we are #1. Rocco: Ya see Demolition, we got a litlle help from are friends and they are gonna rock your world. We might be the Public Enemy, but they are true "warriors". Johnny: AHHHH!!!! WHAT A RUSH!!!!! (end clip) Ross: Next is a situation I want to touch on very briefly as Taz will be today taking on his former teammate, The Giant. As you will recall (roll clips), Taz was wrestling The Great Muta in Japan when after the match, The Giant, The Nasty Boys and Cactus Jack ganged up on the weary Taz. Taz's manager, Bill Alfonso, who was chased form ringside early on by Cactus and company. returned during the beating with help - none other than Taz's supposed enemy, "Macho Man" Randy Savage! (back to studio) I'm not entirely sure what will happen today, but you can be very sure that The Giant has stepped into a world of hurt. Before we go back to Joey Styles, let's hear from Jim Cornette, manager of the men who will be receiving today (a) and Intercontinental title shot, Dean Malenko and (b) his long due World LightHeavyweight title shot, Sabu. I ought to mention that this interview was taped at the Landover show. For the DEWF, I'm Jim Ross. (Scene opens as Lee Marshall stands on an Interview platform.) LEE: Ladies and gentleman, may I present, one of the finest stables in the DEWF today. Led by manager extrordinaire, James E. Cornette, former four-time WCW World Tag Team Champions, Stevie Ray and Booker T, Harlem Heat!!! "The Man of 1000 Holds" Dean Malenko!!! "The Canadian Crippler" Chris Benoit!!!! And fans, your favorite flying maniac, SABU!!!!!!! (Most hardcore fans erupt in cheers, and most just boo and throw beer at the group.) JEC: Gimme that microphone!! Ladies and gentleman, you are looking at the best stable that Momma Cornette's money can buy, and before I'm done, I'll be raking in the dough, as my stable rules wrestling as you know it. (Booker T grabs the mic from Cornette.) BT: Lemme tell ya something, suckas! We hear that two old farts won the tag belts from those white homeboy-wannabes. Well, you old suckas, my brutha and I are two straight OG's from the hood, and we'll kick your fat, out of shape @$$es out of the DEWF!!!! SR: That's right, suckas! And when my brutha here drops the Harlem Hangover on ya, those tag belts will be where they belong, with Booker T and Stevie Ray, the Harlem Heat!!!!! BT: HHHHHHHHHHHHHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!! JEC: Next, the Man who knows more moves than Steve Forbes has money, the uncrowned Intercontinental Champion, Dean Malenko. DM: Mr. Cornette, it seems that the wrestlers of the DEWF have been avoiding the talent that we possess. Champions and other wrestlers beware, because I AM the Man of 1000 Holds, and you never know which move you'll be broken in next. JEC: Next, the man who's crippled more wrestlers than Kevorkian's killed people, "The Canadian Crippler" Chris Benoit!!!!! CB: Mr. Cornette, please line me, or if someone has the guts, have Dean and I team together, because today feels like a day to cripple someone, like that person who think he can imitate me, Devon Storm. Storm, I've crippled better athletes than you. Ask Al Snow, what I did to him at "Double Tables in ECW." JEC: And finally, well, since I talk for him, the man who makes table makers smile so much, SABU!!!! Now, that punk Rey Misterio Jr. has avoided Sabu after that, oh CRUSH!!! ON the table!!!! Misterio, get the guts and sign a true extremist's match, a table break match!!!! LEE: Mr. Cornette, you're out of................. JEC: Keep wearing that suit, Lee, and it'll come back into style someday. (Cornette slams the mic in Marshall's chest and walks off with his stable, while walking, Sabu stops and points to the ceiling. The fans erupt in cheers, chanting, "DO THE TABLE!!!," as Sabu walks off.) (back to JoJo in the arena) Styles: Well, Devon Storm heard - (Crowd boos as Storm himself heads to the ring area and grabs the mic from Styles) What in God's name do you want NOW, Storm? Storm: Just keep holding that mic like you hold your pe**er and you'll do just fine, Geekboy. (Crowd BOOS like all hell) Now, I heard Old Man Benoit issuing a challenge to "The Dangerous One". You got it, so-called "Crippler"! In fact, if you're here, why don't we get it on RIGHT NOW!!! (Proceeds to kick Joey in the ass, which sends Styles scurrying out of the ring. Benoit, it appears, WAS in the building, because he FLEW down the aisle and into the ring.) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ *SPECIAL IMPROMPTU CHALLENGE MATCH* "Dangerous" Devon Storm vs. "The Canadian Crippler" Chris Benoit Memo to Chris Benoit - Be careful what you ask for, you just might get it. BOY, did Chris Benoit "get it"! Storm got MEDIEVAL on Benoit, hitting him with a *road sign* not once, not twice, not *three* times, but FOUR DAMN TIMES right in the head. Needless to say, Benoit was on goofy street after that point. Benoit tried to mount a few comebacks after that, but the signshots had taken their toll and Storm put Benoit away with a sleeperhold in just over a mere *three minutes*. (match time: 3:24) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ *GRUDGE MATCH* "The One Man Crime Spree" Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) vs. The Giant Taz surprised The Giant by going toe to toe with him in brawling, and of course Taz far exceeded him in aerial skills and mat work. Fonzie even got the crowd going by hitting The Giant with a *lethal* chairshot to the back. Not long after that, Cactus Jack and The Nasty Boys came down to ringside. Alfonso refused to be run off this time, as he was joined at ringside by Tommy Dreamer and Randy "Macho Man" Savage!!!! The crowd was popping like crazy at this point, but all hell REALLY broke loose when Taz *bodyslammed* The Giant not once but TWICE!!!! As did Benoit, The Giant tried to mount several comebacks, but Taz was right there, every time. "The Human Suplex Machine" went to work on The Giant after that, suplexing the living hell out of him, and finally put the big man away with a T-Bone Tazplex!!! (match time: 13:21) A brawl totally broke out after the match, with one of the highlights being an over the top rope plancha by Taz onto The Nasty Boys! The tide turned against Team Alfonso after that and turned to the favor of Cactus, The Nasties and The Giant. Dreamer, Savage, and Taz were in bad shape and Bill Alfonso was about to be on the receiving end of a Giant Chokeslam - when out came ***911***!!!!!!! PARTY TIME!!!!!!!! 911 clipped The Giant's legs out from under him, clotheslined Cactus Jack over the top rope - you know, it's really too bad The Nasty Boys weren't paying better attention to what was going on. :) The Nasties were too busy harassing Bill Alfonso to notice 911's rampage. When they finally turned around, 911 grabbed them both by the throat. Going UP - GOING down! DOUBLE Chokeslam on The Nasties!!!! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (after the carnage has been brought to order, we go back to JoJo) Styles: OH MY GOD!!!! OH MY GOD!!!! I don't know what the HELL is going on between The Dangerous Alliance and Team Alfonso, but if those two stables are hooking up... oh my GOD..... Cactus, Giant, Nasties - bend your head between your legs and kiss your hairy asses goodBYE! Oh my GOD!!!! (wipes his forehead) Fans, I have got to calm down a little... let's take a short intermission while I go get a drink or something. Oh my GOD!! Woo-hoo-HOOOOOO!!!! (fade to countdown clock) "That man does NOT want to know all the things I can do to him with a butter knife!" - Brigadier General Adrienne Kramer *DOCTOR WHO - TIME RIFT*
