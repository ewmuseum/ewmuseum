DEWF — Thursday Thunder

DEWF Thursday Thunder, 25 April 1996 (Part Two)

25 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(a commercial for Snuggles fabric softener in which the Snuggles Bear gets chokeslammed by 911 and then we are back with Joey) Styles: Fans, we are back! Up next we have The Giant defending his newly won DEWF Intercontinental Title against "Psycho" Sid of The Dangerous Alliance. Let's do it! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ***DEWF INTERCONTINENTAL CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH*** The Giant (champion) vs. "Psycho" Sid (challenger) (w/ Eric Bischoff) (w/ Paul E. Dangerously) Bischoff (in a neckbrace thanks to Hulk Hogan) came down to ringside with The Giant. Bischoff, microphone in hand (WATCH it, Travis!), was alternately badmouthing the crowd and looking for Hogan. The Giant was threatening to Choke Slam Referee G. Neric when the "Psycho" theme began blaring over the PA. The crowd went apesh*t as "Psycho" Sid was led down to the ring by Paul E. Giant stared down at Sid as the two went nose to, well, throat area approximately (how many times can you say Sid was the *smaller* man in the ring?). As Giant spread his arms wide and roared - Sid kneed him in the - er - Giant family jewels. Problem was, Sid overestimated the damage done. He ran off the ropes and STRAIGHT into a BIG belly to belly suplex by The Giant. One - TWO - THR-NO! Giant utterly dominated the next seventeen plus minutes, taking apparent pleasure in just mutilating Sid. Giant got arrogant and held Sid while Bischoff took a swing at Sid with the Microphone - which hit The Giant when Sid ducked!!!!! Sid ripped off the neckbrace and chokeslammed Bischoff as The Giant teetered, then threw Bischoff out over the top rope! Sid rocketed off the far ropes and LEVELED Giant with a NASTY lariat! Sid went for the cover - ONE - TWO - THREE!!!!!!!!! Ring announcer: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, YOUR WINNER AND NEWWWWWWWWWWWWWW DEWF INTERCONTINENTAL HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, "PSYCHO" SIIIIIIIIIIIIDDDDD! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (Crowd is going GONZO) Styles: OH MY GOD!!! Eric Bischoff, I got just one thing to say to you, pal! Ya live by the microphone, ya DIE by the microphone! I don't think The Giant is gonna be any too happy with you when he wakes up! Next up we have the DEWF debut of The Super Blonde (oy vey) as he takes on the returning Arn Anderson, who will indeed be lead to the ring by The Coach! Let's hear from the Blonde! [Camera opens to a television with Game 4 of the Colorado Avalanche / Vancouver Canucks playoff series. Gino Odjick has just scored to make the game 4-2 in favor of the Canucks. The sound of the television is extremely loud as the Vancouver crowd goes nuts. The volume of the television lowers to a whisper as a nearby recliner swivels to face the camera. Seated inside the recliner is a small man, not past 6' tall. He's clad in a red/white/blue trackjacket, blue jeans and sunglasses. Long blonde hair flows down the front of his jacket.] SB: Hey, y'all. Let me introduce myself... I am the SuperBlonde, your newest recruit to this federation. Not recruit as in.. (mimicingly:) "Please... hire me! I'll shine your shoes.." No, I was approached by the prez and offered a fairly lucrative contract to come here. I was scouted out and hounded to be here. Now, I know most of you guys. I'm young, I've seen y'all. Actually, a few of you are the reasons I'm here. Now, you all must be thing, "Who is this little punk and what is he doing here?" I've already introduced myself, and I'm here for some gold. Plain and simple. My first test: "The Enforcer" Arn Anderson. Now, Arnie, I may not know the reason for your departure, but you may have wanted to delay that arrival for a wee bit longer. Honestly, it won't look very good for your return to have a little "L" beside your name when all is done. Ok, you're big. Can't deny that. You're mean. No objections. You're going to intimidate me. I'll have to disagree with you there. "You're going to be a pretty blonde corpse when you leave the ring". Nope. Sorry. I'm small, but as you know... (points to the screen where the Canucks are celebrating the goal).. Don't count out anything from Vancouver... Don't worry, you won't forget who I am after this. Ciao, babe. (Salutes, then turns back to the game.) [Fade out.] ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Super Blonde (w/ Kismet) vs. "The Enforcer" Arn Anderson (w/ Coach) Kismet had every male in the BUILDING drooling, and they'd seen some fine ladies in Da Arena. As the S-Blonde had predicted Arn tried to intimidate him. Fat chance. Anyone who'd seen some of Super Blonde's stuff in the AEWA knows THAT wasn't likely to bloody well happen. Seeing intimidation wasn't going to work, Arn simply used his considerable power advantage to work over the smaller Blonde for twelve plus minutes. Frankly, Super Blonde couldn't tie his shoes against Anderson in this match. Anderson had Super Blonde set for the DDT when - *BONG* For those of you new to the list, the reason *why* Arn had been away for a while was that he had been Tombstoned from the top rope through *three* tables weeks upon weeks before. As Anderson froze, Super Blonde rolled him up for the 1-2-3!!! (match time: 13:04) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (As the Blonde leaves and Anderson and Coach get the f**k out of Dodge, we go back to Joey) Styles: Folks, The Super Blonde got lucky. But I have heard excellent things about him from his AEWA matches. Maybe he'll do better next week, when he faces Devious Don E. Allen. When we come back, we'll have 911 facing the match of his career as he takes on the legendary "Nature Boy" Ric Flair! (commercial for The New Red Green Show)
