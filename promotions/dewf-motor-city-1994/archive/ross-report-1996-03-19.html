DEWF Ross Report for 3/18/1996!!!! (We're BAAAAAAAACKKKKKK!!!!)

19 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fade in on Jim Ross in the DEWF Control Center) Ross: Hello, wrestling fans and welcome to the Ross Report. The biggest news we have tonight is a changing of the guard at the top of the tag team ranks. On Sunday night in Landover, the team of The Midnight Riders, Blackjack Mulligan and "Captain Redneck" Dick Murdoch defeated Public Enemy in what *has* to be the upset of the year so far. I must add that an assist has to be recorded in the record books for Demolition. We still have not been able to get taped footage of that event, but I am given to undersand that Smash of Demolition held "Flyboy" Rocco Rock's foot down while the decisive three count was made, just out of the referee's sight. You can bet the PE are gonna have a lot to say about this. Also, we have an update on the condition of Paul E. Dangerously. As you will remember (footage is shown) Bill Alfonso had just fired Cactus Jack, The Giant and The Nasty Boys from his Team Alfonso stable. Bill admitted he didn't want to do it, it was merely in the best interest of the group as a whole. Cactus, The Giant and The Nasties began attacking Alfonso - but that wasn't the most shocking event of the evening. *Paul E. Dangerously* ran to ringside and tried to *help* Alfonso, and got the single worst beating of his managerial career for his, er, pains. Paul E. may not like Alfonso, but even *he* knows wrong is just plain wrong. I am happy to report that Paul E. suffered nothing more than a nasty concussion, and several compressed vertebrae as a result of the attack. He assures us he *will* be back, and soon. I have no doubt of it. This doesn't end even *there*. (More footage) Last night, at the Korakuen Hall in Japan, Taz took on The Great Muta in one of the matches of this or any other century. We'll never know who might have won that night, because Cactus Jack and The Giant came to ringside and as expected got involved at several key points in the match. The most telling point was the very end when Taz had Muta locked up in his devestating TazMission hold. The Giant climbed into the ring and clobbered Taz with a chair. Cactus took out Muta while The Giant repeatedly chokeslammed Taz. Taz's manager, Bill Alfonso, had been run from ringside by Cactus and The Giant early on, but returned as the beating was going on - with help! He came back with none other than Taz's mortal rival, "The Macho Man" Randy Savage!!!!! Savage cleaned house with some *wicked* chairshots to both The Giant and Cactus while Taz recovered. We hope to get a statement from Taz tomorrow. Also, one of the strangest events in recent memory has to be the apparent bonding of Buh-Buh Ray Dudley and the self-proclaimed "Sexy Boy" Shawn Michaels. (Mo' footage) It was Michaels who saved Buh-Buh from a beating by that lowlife Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw. Let's hear from both camps.
