DEWF Event Center!!! (SPECIAL EDITION)

3 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Open on a harried looking Joey Styles outside the LuLu Temple in Allentown, Pennsylvania. We hear someone from off camera yell "Okay, Joey, you're on!". Joey nods, turns to the camera and begins to speak as Mutants all around Joey are deliriously shouting "DEWF! DEWF! DEWF!") Styles: Hello fans, and welcome to a very special DEWF Event Center. As you can see, we're outside the LuLu Temple in Allentown, Pennsylvania. Some of the events that have just transpired inside this building have led to an event I'm going to tell you about at the end of this report. Let me give you a quick run down of the results and I'll touch on some things that have happened here tonight. In our opening match, The New Rockers were BEATEN by, of all teams, Aldo Montoya and Jim Powers! The Rockers were celebrating after hitting their finisher and Montoya hit Janetty with a Northern Lights suplex and the match was history! What was even *more* interesting was what happened in the aisleway *after* the match! As the dejected Rockers were on their way up the aisle, they were met by none other than "Dancin'" Stevie Richards! What's happening there I *don't* know, but frankly my guesses scare me. Next up, Konnan of the Dangerous Alliance began his comeback trail by getting a DQ win over another comebacker, "Black Death" Ahmed Johnson! Next was a match that was one of two reasons why the surprise you'll hear later is going to happen - the extreme rookie 573, along with his manager "Sex Symbol" Travis Cook beat Buh-Buh Ray Dudley by the skin of his teeth. Buh-Buh Ray took 573 to the extreme and gave the rookie his sternest test since The Undertaker. But once again Travis Cook stuck his damned nose into someone else's business by throwing 573 a *barbed wire baseball bat* which promptly got cracked right on Buh-Buh Ray's head. As Big Dick Dudley attended to his fallen brother, 573 headed up the aisle - and the lights went out. There was a "BONG" - and the lights came back up revealing *The Undertaker* standing right in front of the big man. To his credit, 573 didn't look very fazed at all. Travis Cook, on the other hand, fainted. 573 simply shook his head, picked up his fallen manager and carried him back to the locker room area. Undertaker pressed a sheet of paper into *my* hand and left, himself. Let's go to an interview I taped with Cook just moments after the card ended to see just what was in that paper. (Travis Cook comes out acting as smug and sexy as ever, with two lovely ladies he stole either from Hunter Hearst Helmsley, The 4 Horsemen, or former Presidentiial canidate Gary Hart. The ladies are "Aw, poor babying" Travis over his being scared by that Mean Old Undertaker. Styles rolls his eyes in disgust) TC: Joey, Joey, Joey...still shopping at Buster Brown huh... JS: Hey, I don't want to be here, these people don't want you out here, just let me get to the *expletive deleted* point. TC: Geez! Somebody must have woke up on the wrong side of Divine Brown's bed! But I digress. JS: This from a man who's lose a battle of wits with a pile of pencil shavings. TC: Don't make me get ugly with you... JS: Too late... TC: (registers wounded dignity) Be still oh ye of low patience and lower sperm count! As I was saying, I heard in the locker room that after we beat the crap out of Rain Man Dudley and I was viciously attacked by Mean Mark Callous... JS: You mean the Undertaker and all he did was look at you. TC: (whispers to Joey) Do you mind? I've got these two dates here. Let me tell the story the way I want to! (Joey shakes his head and Cook resumes his normal annoying voice) Anyway, after we put him on the shelf, after after we beat the stuffings out of his special little friend, that reject from a Stone Temple Pilots detox center, The Crow, that Callous had the audacity to mention to somebody that he wanted a casket match with 573! Well, Callous, let me nip this rumor right in the bud. You want a casket match? Ha ha ha, you won't get it, dead man. I know what you've done to people in casket matches before, and I'm not stupid enough to let 573 sign for one. Why would I give the trump card to my enemy? JS: Would you do it for ten thousand dollars? TC: (blinks) Beg pardon? JS: If you hadn't been unconscious, you might have seen Undertaker give me a piece of paper. What it was was a contract for a Casket Match with a proviso that you would be payed ten thousand cool dollars for signing your man, 573, to the match. TC: Gimme that contract!!!!!! (signsignsign - then pauses, gets an crafty look, and chuckles) Underweartaker, get ready to rest in pices! (laughs and leaves and we fade back to Joey) Styles: I don't like the looks of this. Anyway, Michaels Monroe defended the DEWF World's Light Heavyweight Championship against Sabu. Sabu let his temper get the best of him and was disqualified at 5:24. Here's an interview that was taped right after "The Shadow" first beat Sabu for the title. (Todd Pettingill enters the Royal Assassins' locker room. Jerry Lawler, Jeff Jarrett, Justin Bradshaw, Al Perez and Paul Diamond are pouring Champagne all over newly crowned DEWF Light-heavyweight Champion "Shadow" Michael Monroe as "Just One Fix" by Ministry plays loudly in the background. Meanwhile, Black Sarah is , uncharacteristically, smiling broadly.) TP : Black Sarah, you certainly look like the cat that ate the canary. Sarah : "The cat that ate the canary?" Pettingill, from where did Todd Gordon dig you up? TP : Actually, I was hired as a part of a Federally sponsored "make work" project for the hopelessly incompetent. Sarah : (under her breath) Thank you, President Clinton. (normal voice) It was only a matter of time before my managing genius and my men's skills would reap dividends. The World Tag titles was the first step. Now the Light-heavyweight title is ours and the word is out that the Fear Machine and the Royal Assassins are not to be overlooked. Michael Monroe : How many more ? How many more of the "Extreme" superstars are we going to have to break before they realize that the Royal Assassins are the true Extreme stable? First came the Public Enemy. The Fear Machine took your titles, your self-respect and your blood. Next came the Sandman. Justin Hawk Bradshaw left you laying in the middle of a cage. Now they send in Sabu. I showed him who is the true Extreme aerialist and now I am the Light-heavyweight Champion. The DEWF is the Everest of Extreme wrestling, and the Royal Assassins are the kings of the hill. Tell 'em, King. Jerry Lawler : Well, it looks like things are turning to gold in the Royal Assassins. Vader ! Michaels ! Bring it on, boys, we'll take your belts too ! The DEWF better get to work coming up with new titles, we're running out of belts to win. And Dibiase, we have a little unfinished business. Get in the way of the Royal Assassins revenge, and you will find out what the rest of the DEWF is finding out : that the Royal Assassins are the kings of Extreme ! By the way, Pettingill, this is a private party, and you are cordially not invited. (Justin Cross and Justin Bradshaw grab Todd Pettingill and hurl him bodily through the locker room door. The door is not open at the time. Camera Fade back to Joey Styles) Styles: Are the Royal Assassins truly the pinnacle of Extreme? Time alone will tell. Next up, Blackjack Mulligan and Terry Funk, frankly, beat holy hell out of Coach's team of "The Enforcer" Arn Anderson and "Mister" Bob Backlund. This match was a receipt for the attack on Mulligan's son, Barry Windham, during what turned out to be the late Dick Murdoch's last match and Windham's subsequent injury by Backlund during Windham's match with Arn Anderson. On a personal note, I watched Dick Murdoch for many, many years and was as devastated as anyone by his passing. Murdoch was the textbook definition of tough, and he will be missed. The match ended when Blackjack made "Mister" Backlund submit to his devastating "Ace Of Spades" clawhold. Something tells me this will not be the last time these two teams meet. Finally, in the match that is the second reason for our big surprise, Shane Douglas STUNNED the wrestling world when he PINNED DEWF World Champion Vader thanks in no small part to *Chris Candido* DDT'ing Vader *three times* on the concrete arena floor. That leads us to our big surprise. Tomorrow we will be hosting a SURPRISE Pay Per View! That's right, Thursday Thunder will be a Special Edition: Independence Day Armageddon! The lineup is as follows - Rey Misterio, Jr. (w/ Skandor Akbar) vs. Psicosis in an EXTREME Death Match. That means falls will count anywhere in the building. Anywhere! Justin "I'm not Barry Windham but I play him on TV" Bradshaw (w/ Jerry "The King" Lawler) takes ON Barry Windham. I, personally, don't think Barry should be returning quite so quickly from his shoulder injury. In a TRIANGLE match - The Crow (w/ Raven) will take on both Jushin "Thunder" Liger (w/ Skandor Akbar) and Super Blonde. Speaking of Raven, he takes on "Stone Cold" Steve Austin. The Dudes With Attitudes (Shawn Michaels and "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel w/ Paul Ellering) take on Cactus Jack and The Giant. Will Eric Bit... I mean, Bischoff show his face at ringside? We'll see. Let's go to an interview the Dudes With 'Tudes taped earlier tonight. (Joey Styles is in the ring for an interview) [JS] Ladies and gentlemen, I'm here to interview the tag team that has REUNITED for one of the battles of the CENTURY!!!(pop) Together they will face the awesome combination of Cactus Jack and The Giant!! But here THEY are, with Paul Ellering, "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel, and "The Heart Break Kid" Shawn Michaels, collectively known as TWO DUDES WITH ATTITUDES!! (Fans pop like never before as "Sexy Boy" starts up and Paul Ellering emerges followed by HBK and then Diesel. HBK and Diesel have BIG smiles on their faces as they hi-five their way down to the ring. HBK is carrying the IC Title) Well, together again ! You two have united to battle a common enemy and his tag team partner, being The Giant and Cactus Jack!! Wh..(Diesel grabs the mic) [D] Just so we can get this through now Shawn, everyone here knows I should be wearing that belt over my shoulder. And I WOULD be if The Giant didn't interfere. So, after we dispose of the big man and Jack, I think we need to do this again!! Just so we can be sure of who the REAL champion is. What do ya say Shawn?? [JS] What DO you say, Shawn? (Crowd is popping like crazy as Shawn looks around) [HBK] Big Daddy Cool, it'll be my pleasure to get in the ring with you one more time, my friend! (The two shake hands as the crowd pops) Giant, CactusKind, Manjack, whatever you're calling yourself this week (even Diesel laughs at this one) - The BDC and the HBK are like a family. We may beat each OTHER to death, but when some outsider decides to stick his nose into our bidness we do what any family does - WE RIP HIS F***IN' NOSE OFF AND FEED IT TO HIM FOR BREAKFAST! YOU TWO F***IN' LOSERS HAVE A DATE WITH A JACKKNIFE AND SOME SWEET F***IN' CHIN MUSIC!!! (Even Diesel looks slightly taken aback by Shawn's uncharacteristic display of emotion) Yeah, Styles, I've heard the bullsh*t - "Shawn Michaels can't be hardcore. He's too much of a pretty boy!". WELLLLL, you just ask "Playboy" Buddy Rose, Doug Somers or THIS BIG F***ER right F***IN' here just how hardF***INcore Shawn Michaels can get! (Slaps Diesel on the chest and tries to regain his composure. Even Paul Ellering is, like, "JUMP the f**k back" over Michaels) [JS] Well...um...now that we now how Shawn feels about this match, and I would like to thank him for that...um...interesting viewpoint, let's go to Big Daddy Cool, Diesel!! What is YOUR take on the match?? [D] Well first Shawn, man, you just gotta learn to let your feelings out once in a while. If you keep them inside like you are you'll just blow up one day. (He smiles and HBK laughs) But my take on the match JoJo?? Well, everybody KNOWS how f****ing hardcore Big Daddy Cool is!! We all saw that when I kicked Sandman's ass in the Thunderdome Cage a while back (fans pop at the memory, and the mention of Sandman) We all have seen me get hardcore with Vader all over people!! Well, it's time for HBK and and Big Daddy Cool to get hardcore again, on Cactus Jack and The Giant!!(pop) Just like Shawn said, me and him are like a family, we can beat the sh*t outta each other, but nobody, and I mean NOBODY better get in our f***ing ways!!! 'Cause when ya f*** with one of us, ya f*** with both of us, and that is NOT smart!! Cactus Jack, Giant, you two have a date with a Jacknife, and some Sweet Chin Music baby. If you like it, good....if you don't WE DON'T GIVE A SH*T!!! (Fans pop as "Sexy Boy" starts up again and the two play to the crowd) [JS] Well, that's it for this interview!! I think we wore out the censors' fingers with that one!! (fade back to JoJo. He's still shaking his head over the way Shawn was acting) Styles: One word: Wow. back to the matches - Sabu (w/ "Gentleman" Jim Cornette) takes on "Dangerous" Devon Storm in a Table Breaker match! The only way to win this one is to put your opponent through a table! Blackjack Mulligan (w/ Terry Funk) takes on "The Enforcer" Arn Anderson (w/ "Mister" Bob Backlund) in a Texas Bullrope Match! The Undertaker (w/ Paul Bearer) takes on the EXTREME rookie, 573 (w/ Travis Cook) in a CASKET MATCH. I'm still worried about what Cook has in store for this one. In the first of our main events, The Fear Machine (w/ Black Sarah) will defend the DEWF World Tag Team Titles against mystery opponents. They opened their big mouths - will their opponents shut them? And in the second main event, Vader has promised a surprise of his own at ringside when he tries to regain the DEWF World Heavyweight Championship from "The Franchise" Shane Douglas (w/ Sensational Sherri)! Fans, we are WAY out of time! Until tomorrow, this is Joey Styles for the DEWF! (fade out to delirious chants from the mutants of "DEWF! DEWF! DEWF!")
