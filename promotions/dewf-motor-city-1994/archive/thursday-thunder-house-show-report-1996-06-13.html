DEWF Thursday Thunder House Show Report From The MoMutant!!!

13 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Folks - thought I'd try something a little different this time) Wow.... One of the better cards I've seen from the DEWF in a long, long time. I was a little shocked to see Marty Janetty no show for the debut of The New Rockers, but Stevie Richards offering The Blue Meanie!!!! And wonder of wonders - they won! Granted, they *were* just wrestling "Mad Dog" Finch and "Rabid" Rob F, but still... ANYTHING can happen in the *SMACK* Oh... nevermind. This 573 guy is living proof of the old saying "Don't judge a book by it's cover". I mean, he looks like Sandman, is built solid, but wrestles like Chris Benoit! Today, he SQUASHED The Artist Formerly Known As Ubas. What was interesting was *after* the match when The Crow came out of nowhere and blindsided 573 with a *NASTY* Chairshot. "Sex Symbol" Travis Cook beat a hasty retreat, but a match was signed for next Thursday between 573 and The Crow. Super Blonde took on Shane Douglas in what, realistically, should have been little more than a workout for "The Franchise". But then, Douglas and Scorp Dogg made the mistake a few weeks back of royally p*ssing off 911. SB and Douglas went back and forth in what could be considered a match of the year candidate. About three-quarters of the way through, though, 911 was in da house and made his way down to ringside. He broke several Douglas pinfall attempts and in the end, when Super Blonde had Douglas ready for the pin after a swinging DDT, Scorp distracted referee James Beard. Douglas hit SB with a flying bodypress - but 911 entered the ring, CHOKESLAMMED Douglas and put Super Blonde on top! As 911 rolled out of the ring, Beard turned around finally and counted 1-2-3!!!!!! This card also saw the return of The Outlaws, "Captain Redneck" Dick Murdoch and Blackjack Mulligan as they were *supposed* to take on The Pitbulls! But Pitbull #2 no-showed and his replacement - Mister Bob Backlund!!!! (He wasn't the only substitute - usual manager Dutch Mantell was dead dru..er, undergoing religious meditations and Barry Windham served as manager for The Outlaws for this match) Backlund and The Pitbull made for a surprisingly good tag team, truth be told. Backlund even helped do the Pitbulls' usual slingshot shoulderblock from Pitbull #1 bit. In the end, though, Backlund clamped on the Dreaded (tm, Gorilla Monsoon) Crossface Chickenwing and Dick Murdoch was forced to submit. Oh.. did I forget to mention just prior to the submission, Dick Murdoch had been out on the floor and was BLINDSIDED with a shoulderbreaker by Backlund's stablemate, Arn Anderson? Just thought I'd throw that in there. :) A match has been signed for next week between Windham and Anderson, BTW. Next up was "Sexy Boy" Shawn Michaels as he took on The Giant for the DEWF Intercontinental Title. Frankly, Michaels whooped ass on The Giant for most of the match. But later on, he missed a moonsault bodyblock and The Son Andre Never Had went to work with bearhugs, bodyslams and boots to the head. Where Giant made his fatal mistake was relying too much on the outside interference of manager Eric Bischoff. It saved him from several pin attempts by Michaels. But for the one that counted, just as Bischoff was preparing a chairshot for Michaels, *DIESEL* ran down to ringside and yanked the chair right out of Bischoff's hands! Big Daddy Cool threw Bischoff over his shoulder and left the area while The Giant AND the crowd both went hypernuts! When Giant turned around from his tirade at Diesel, he was met with not one, not two, but THREE Sweet Chin Musics from Michaels! Say goodnight, Mister Giant, and HELLO to the NEWWWWWW (God, I've always wanted to say that) DEWF Intercontinental Champion "Sexy Boy" Shawn Michaels! Owen Hart and Hunter Hearst Helmsley took on The Fear Machine for the Tag titles in yet another match of the year candidate. The Machine won when Michael Monroe rolled through a Owen Hart high cross bodyblock and got the pin. After the match, it was announced that next week, Monroe would be taking on Sabu for the Light Heavyweight strap! Then it came time for the Vader - Malenko title rematch. These two had been trading the belt back and forth for *weeks* now and this was the final match between the two. Malenko requested, and got, a Submission only stip added. Be careful what you ask for, you might get it, Dean. Vader *again* showed his under rated scientific skills, and got the submission with a cross arm scissors! Well, until next time, this is the MoMutant - Bite me, it's fun!
