DEWF: The Ross Report for 8/06/1996

6 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Diesel is standing in the same locker room he did earlier. He has the IC Title over his shoulder. But instead of celebrating he has a serious look on his face) [D] That's right, Diesel, Big Daddy Cool, the new DEWF IC Champ. But that's not my main concern right now. Right now this title right here isn't my first concern, my first concern is the mother f*cker who thinks he's some kind of bad ass, Shane Douglas. I'm gonna brging everyone a news flash, Vader wasn't there for his match because Shane, that gutless punk, attacked Vader when I left the room for a minute earlier. Then Shane has the balls to demand he be given the World Title!? I don't think so!! Shane, consider yourself LUCKY 911 kicked your ass!! Because Shane, when you f*ck with Vader like you did, it's not just Vader , it's Vader, the Sandman, The Road Warriors, and Diesel. And ask Cactus Jack and the Giant, when you f*ck with Big Daddy Cool, you f*ck with Shawn Michaels. And then you went and screwed with Tod Gordon, so now you got 911 after you. So Shane, you're gonna need a lot more than Chris Candido and The Heavenly Bodies, 'cause now you got Vader, Sandman, The Road Warriors, me, Shawn Michaels, and 911 all wanting a piece of your ass punk!! So Shane, yea, I'm the IC Champ, but that doesn't matter right now, what does matter is that I'm gonna kick your ass you mother f*cker!! (Fade out as he throws a chair across the room. Fade *in* on Jim Ross in the DEWF Control Room) Ross: Hello, everyone and welcome to the Ross Report. Tonight, we'll be giving you the complete card for the return THIS Saturday night of DEWF Saturday Night's MAIM Event! But first, I want to address what the new Intercontinental Champion, "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel, already talked about at the beginning of the program, the cowardly actions by Shane Douglas against the world champion, Vader, and the interim commissioner of the DEWF, Tod Gordon. Shane, you and I have known each other since our respective days in the Mid South area and Bill Watts' old UWF. What you have done is signed your own death warrant. You have not ONLY the entire Legion Of Doom angry, but also Sandman and ex-DEWF Intercontinental Champion Shawn Michaels. *Then* you have the audacity to lay your hands on the interim commissioner of the DEWF, Tod Gordon, and then be actually *furious* when 911 saves Tod's bacon by chokeslamming your behind practically through the mat! Well, your famous temper got the better of you and you *demanded* a match with the big man, 911. You got one, for this Saturday night: an Extreme Death Match! What that means is, is basically a Texas Death match fought under Falls Count Anywhere rules. Yes, it's falls count anywhere, but it could one, two or *twenty* falls. The match *can not* end until one man cannot answer the ten count. Let's move on. Yet another real prize, Chris Candido, got HIS head handed to him by on The Trail Of Revenge by a compatriot of the very man he and The Heavenly Bodies attacked, "The Shadow" Michael Monroe. Candido got soundly beaten by "The Latin Heartthrob" Al Perez. It could be argued that Al was significantly aided by outside interference by Monroe's tag partner, "The Ivory Tower" Justin Cross. Be that as it may, Candido makes yet another stop on the trail, as he faces on SNME, "Hard Rock" Paul Diamond. With that in mind, let's give you the complete run down of the card for Saturday Night's MAIM Event: "The Mad Dogs Of War", The Pitbulls (w/ Gen. Skandor Akbar) take on Stevie Ray and Booker T, The Harlem Heat (w/ "Gentleman" Jim Cornette) "The Extreme Rookie" 573 (w/ "Sex Symbol" Travis Cook) collides with the man he obviously patterns himself after, The Sandman (w/ Naomi Campbell). Will The Undertaker make his presence known? Only time will tell. Speaking OF "The Man From The Dark Side", The Undertaker (w/ "The Gomez Addams of the DEWF" Paul Bearer) battles "The Man Of 1,000 Holds" Dean Malenko (w/ "Gentleman" Jim Cornette) (cut back to Jim. *Steve Richards* joins him from off camera and puts on a headset) Richards: (taps the microphone) Testing, testing. Is this thing on? Ross: Steve Richards, what in God's name do you think you're doing? Richards: "Jumbo" Jim, if you'll remember, I had me a meeting with Marty Janetty and Leif Cassidy, The New Rockers, right before Independence Day Armageddon, right? (Before Jim can answer) Right! Welll, I'm here to proudly announce that I am now the OFFICIAL manager of The New Rockers! (Beams) Ross: You? A *manager*? Richards: Why not? Raven taught me everything he knows, and God KNOWS I'm smarter than that hairball and his Creature Feature tag team partner, so I should do *great*! Ross: Uh-HUH. Richards: ANYhow, what I want for this SNME is match between MY New Rockers and those cartoon characters, The Harbingers. Later! (leaves) Ross: (shakes his head) Lord. Talk about the blind leading the clueless. Anyway... Shawn Michaels (w/ Naomi Campbell) will try to regain the DEWF Intercontinental Title curently held by "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel (w/ Paul Ellering). As we told you before, Chris Candido (w/ Sister Sherri) faces "Hard Rock" Paul Diamond (w/ Black Sarah) in yet another stop on "The Trail Of Revenge". The Gangstaz will make their first title defense against the awesome Road Warriors (w/ Paul Ellering). Let's hear from the champions, New Jack and Mustafa, The Gangstaz! (fade to tha hood. New Jack and Mustafa are standing around a garbage can that has a fire lit in it) New Jack: Well son, it look like we gonna have to open up a WHOLE can o' whoopass to get past our first title defense. Man, we gonna have to face the guys that used to be the measuring stick of tag team wrestling, The Road Warriors. Well, what ya lookin' at here, son, is tha G's that are the new measuring stick. We gonna give ya ya props, Warriors. Ya a couple of walkin' legends, and ya ain't ta be taken lightly. But son, The Gangstaz are HEAH, and we AIN'T ta be played wit'! You gonna have to bring a ladder and a sack lunch, 'cause it's gonna be a long night fo' yo' asses at SNME. 'Cause when it's all said and done, when the muthaf*ckin' smoke clears, The Gangstaz STILL gonna be on top o' da mountain - SUCKAS! (Crosses his arms like an "X") Mustafa: (Does The Mustafa Evil Laugh (tm)) We gonna bust dat ass! (See Previous Laugh as we fade to "Natural Born Killaz") Ross: Man, talk about a dream match - The Gangstaz versus The Road Warriors! That should be a good old fashioned slobberknocker, to be sure! In the main event, as we told you earlier, the massive 911 takes on that COWARD, "The Franchise" Shane Douglas in an EXTREME Death Match! Let's hear from the manager of 911, Paul E. Dangerously! For the DEWF, I'm Jim Ross. (fade to Paul E. in back of his car, obviously packing after a card) Paul E.: (laughs) Shane, Shane, Shane. Do you have ANY idea what you've gotten yourself into? Do you? I mean, getting the ENTIRE Legion Of Doom *plus* Sandman and Shawn Michaels honked off at you was not a real bright idea to begin with. (quietly, menacingly) But THEN you ALSO got the entire f*ckin' Dangerous Alliance on your ass when you laid you hands on Tod Gordon. Bad mistake, my friend. Because your forced me - you FORCED me - to call those three numbers anybody calls in an emergency. You FORCED me to call for 9 - 1 - 1. (laughs, packs the last of his things, returns to his regular voice, gets in his car and leans out the window) You got a beautiful future, man. (Flips the camera the finger as he screeches off. We close on the DEWF logo as we fade to black) "So it is written, so it shall come to pass." -- Raven, ECW World's Heavyweight Champion
