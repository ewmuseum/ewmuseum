DEWF - The Ross Report for 3/26/1996!!! (SPECIAL EDITION)

26 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The DEWF theme song, "Warmachine" by KISS, blares as we go over the obligatory highlight reel. As soon as the DEWF logo drops into place, "The Ross Report" is "spray painted" across the screen in neon red letters. The screen then shatters and we we go to Jimbo in the control center.) Ross: Hello, wrestling fans and WELCOME to a very special edition of The Ross Report. Today we're going to touch on some of the things that happened on Sunday Slam, plus we're going to recap some of the happenings that are going to lead to some of the great matches at DEWF Rumble In The Jungle, which will be coming to you LIVE from the concrete jungle of New York City in the legendary Madison Square Garden THIS Sunday, ONLY in your e-mail inbox! First off, let's give you the now COMPLETE card for Rumble In The Jungle, including the 30 men who will compete in the Extreme Royal Rumble to determine the new DEWF World's Heavyweight Champion. I must add before we start that a lot of these will be competing TWICE that night, once in their regular matches, and then going into the Rumble to go for the gold. So, let's get to the card. (Cut to a presentation screen of the DEWF logo, upon which is superimposed pictures of the involved wrestlers as they and their matches are mentioned. "Warmachine" plays in the background. Jimbo is doing the voiceover) From the Mid Missouri Wrestling Alliance, The Rajin' Cajin makes his DEWF debut as he faces "Devious" Don E. Allen. "Macho Man" Randy Savage and "The One Man Crime Spree" Taz, who will have Bill Alfonso in their corner, TEAM UP to face The Giant and Cactus Jack. By the way, Bill Alfonso has promised he will have a major surprise during this match. The Crow will settle HIS longstanding score against Hakushi in a Cage Match. In a tag team WAR, the awesome Road Warriors will face Stevie Ray and Booker T, The Harlem Heat. In what promises to be a technical wrestling lover's dream match, "The Excellence Of Execution" Bret "The Hitman" Hart will face "The Man Of A Thousand Holds" Dean Malenko. They call him the "Superputz" and he certainly showed it when he turned on his former mentor. Dancin' Stevie Richards will face Raven in an "I Quit" match. Richards will have Blue Meanie in his corner. Raven will have his "brother of the skies", The Crow, in his. That's assuming The Crow makes it out of that cage match with Hakushi. The EXTREMELY unlikely team of "The Boy Toy" Shawn Michaels and Buh-Buh Ray Dudley will take on Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw and "Dubba J" Jeff Jarrett. "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel will face The Sandman in a Thunderdome Cage Match. 911 will face the man who attacked him a couple of weeks ago, "Black Death" Ahmed Johnson, in a Ladder Match for the DEWF Intercntinental Title! Sabu will face Rey Misterio Jr. in a Falls Count Anywhere rematch for the DEWF World Light Heavyweight Championship. The Undertaker will take on the poster child for Prozac, "Mister" Bob Backlund in a Casket Match. This one stretches all the way back to Missouri Mayhem when Backlund separated the Man From The Dark Side's shoulder with that devastating Cross Face Chicken Wing! In a match announced just Sunday night, The Midnight Riders will be forced to face their cronies, Demolition, with the winner going on to face Public Enemy in a South Philly Street Fight for the World Tag Team Championships! And then, in the main event, the vacant DEWF World's Heavyweight Championship will finally be filled when 30 men face off in an EXTREME Royal Rumble. (fade back to the studio) Ross: Fans, we will have the thirty participants for you tomorrow when we bring you the Match Of The Day which will pit Dory Funk Jr. against his brother Terry. The man of the hour yesterday on several occasions was the Intercontinental Champion, 911. (Clips of all the chokeslams) He came to the rescue of taz first, clippping The Giant and clotheslining Cactus Jack over the top rope during Taz's match against The Giant when Cactus and The Nasties interfered. The Nasties were on the receiving end of a DOUBLE Chokeslam! Then later on, Terry Funk broke up Rocco Rock's pin of Blackjack Mulligan with a branding iron and the Funker was quickly joined by all three members of Demolition in beating on PE when 911 again cleaned house! The Funker was stopped from clocking 911 with the branding iron by his own brother Dory, who grabbed the branding iron and nailed his brother in the head with it!. Poor Terry stumbled straight into a ride on the Chokeslam Express while Dory cleared out the Demos with the branding iron as he revived Public Enemy. (back to Ross) Ross: We then got this clip from the Outlaws, saving poor Joey Styles from interviewing them. From [detail withheld] Mar 26 11:32:54 1996 Date: Tue, 26 Mar 1996 12:23:32 +0000 From: [name withheld] < [detail withheld] > To: "[name withheld]" < [detail withheld] > Subject: Midnight Rumble (Dick Murdoch, BlackJack and Terry Funk look pissed as hell in the DEWF lockeroom. As we join them,chairs and lockers are lying in the floor and a few jobbers are seen locking themselves in the nearest stalls.) Funk (pulling on his hair with one hand and smearing blood on his face with the other) "911...You've stuck your face where it don't belong boy! That's right, you think your Extreme by choke slamming me a few times, you don't know what type of hurting I can put on you boy...I INVENTED THE WORD PAIN. I'm gonna put your sorry ass down like a broke leg horse. And Dory...I've got plans to beat you worse than Momma did!" Murdoch (looks confused) "What the hell just happened in there? We got our titles stripped? Gordon...just who the hell do you think you are? I'm sure Dutch is gonna have a few words for you when he catches wind of this! (grabs his head and gives that "infamous" Captain Redneck "I'm in pain look") Demolition...in the sport of wrasslin' there are no friends. At the Rumble we're beat you like a scrambled egg...and then Public Enemy is gonna feel what it's like to know the true meaning a BITCH." BlackJack (grabs Murdoch and Funk and places them both under his arms) "Boys you both know ole BlackJack ain't getting any younger..but I've still got a few tricks up my sleeves, and at the Rumble...the Midnight Riders are gonna shock the wrestling world!" (suddenly the dressing room door busts open and a slobbering Stan Hansen slides into view with Dutch Mantell[and neckbrace] riding piggyback. Jimmy Jack soons follows as he trips to the floor.) Hansen "Where are they?!!!" Mantell " Gordon....you've got balls son. You just started something that you'll wish never happened. This is War...Extreme style!!! Like I've said before...God forgives and the Oulaws don't!!!!" (again back to Ross) Ross: Fans, this is shaping up to be one good old fashioned Slobberknocker. Join us tomorrow for the Match Of the Day. For the DEWF, I'm Jim Ross. (fade out) "That man does NOT want to know all the things I can do to him with a butter knife!" - Brigadier General Adrienne Kramer *DOCTOR WHO - TIME RIFT*
