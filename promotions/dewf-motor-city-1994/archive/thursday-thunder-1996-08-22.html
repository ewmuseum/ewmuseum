DEWF — Thursday Thunder

DEWF Thursday Thunder for 8/22/1996!!! (On TIME for once! :))

22 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Legion of Doom stands in the locker room. Vader carries the DEWF World Title, Diesel the IC Title, and The Road Warriors the tag titles. The Warriors and Diesel have huge smiles on their faces, Vader just paces back and forth) [D] Well, it's quite a big night for the Legion of Doom!! The group with ALL the gold. The only belt we don't have is the Lightheavy Belt, and that's 'cause we don't have a Lightheavy!! Tonight I, Big Daddy Cool, Diesel, the DE F'n WF IC Champ will make my SECOND title defense. I won the title from my good buddy HBK, and then defended against him, and now, now it's Tommy F'n Dreamer!! Well Dreamer, you're tough, you're one bad ass mother f*cker, I'll give you that, but I'm not Brian Lee buddy, this is the DEWF IC Champ, and it'll STAY that way after tonight!! [H] FINALLY!!! After battling The Public Enemy, Demnolition, and all the others, we meet Da Gangstaz, and we are THE WORLD Champs!! Tonight, Mustafa, New Jack, you get your rematch, but it's useless, because we are the CREATORS of EXTREME tag team wrestling, we LOVE the feel of gold!! OHHHHHH WHAT A RUSSSSSHHHHH!!!! [A] MUSTAFA!! NEW JACK!!! I hope you guys like pain!! The Legion of Doom has ALL the gold now that me and Hawk got these puppies(pats his DEWF Tag Title Belt), and that's NOT gonna end any time soon!! Gangstaz, you guys are big in Philly, you guys are two badasses from the streets, and you are two tough mother f*ckers, but we are THE tough mother f*ckers!! We've proved it once, and we'll prove it again!! [V] SHANE DOUGLAS!!! HEAVENLY BODIES!!! TONIGHT'S THE BEGINNING OF THE END....OF YOUR LIVES!!! You got THE ONLY DEWF THREE TIME World Champ, me, Vader, you got The Sandman, the guy who f*cking broke my arm, and you got 911!!! Tonight's just the beginning, OF HELL FOR YOU!!! IT'S... VADER... TIIIIME!!!! (All four point to the belts, and then Vader flashes the "V for Vader" sign right in front of the camera before we cut to the DEWF opening sequence and then to Joey Styles in the ring surrounded on all sides by delirious DEWF mutants bellowing "DEWF! DEWF! DEWF!") Styles: *THIS* IS THE DREAM EXTREME WRESTLING FEDERATION!!! (Massive POP) At this time, I'd like to welcome to the ring one half of the Fear Machine, "The Ivory Tower" Justin Cross, and his manager, Black Sarah. ("Dead Souls" by Joy Division plays over the Arena PA as Justin Cross and Black Sarah stide purposefully to the ring) JS : Black Sarah, your men are 2-0 against Chris Candito. Are you planning for a clean sweep ? Sarah : We never plan for anything less than victory, Joseph. Candito has found that his actions have put him in our way on the Trail of revenge, and he has nobody but himself to blame for the results. Justin Cross : Candito, Al Perez beat you. Paul Diamond humiliated you. And shortly, you will be utterly destroyed at the hands of "The Ivory Tower". Where did it all go wrong, Candito? You angered the Fear Machine, you angered the Royal Assassins, and now you have the Harbingers breathing down your neck. When you wake up next morning and look at yourself in the mirror, when you see your puffy bruised face, when you look down and see the horrifying damage done to your once healthy body, just say this, Candito : "I brought this upon myself." I hope you've said a tearful goodbye to your loved ones, Candito, because this is where the Trail ends. (Justin Cross and Black Sarah leave the ring, as well as Joey, and "Highway To Hell" by AC/DC starts up, heralding the arrival of Buh-Buh Ray Dudley) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Buh-Buh Ray Dudley vs. "Killer" [name withheld] [name withheld] made his way down to the ring, disdainfully calling the crowd "ghouls" and bellowing something about "that's *one* star off!" or something. Buh-Buh Ray was all over [name withheld] like ugly on Abdullah. As [name withheld] was in the corner, yelling at the crowd, Buh-Buh *squashed* him in the corner with a Buh-Buhlanche. When [name withheld] staggered out of the corner, Buh-Buh *planted* him with his patented Buh-Buh Bomb. (match time: 0:27) Buh-Buh was on his way back down the aisle when Steve Richards came down the aisle as well and slapped the taste out of Buh-Buh Ray's mouth. The crowd did it's damndest to climb the rails and strangle Richards. But as Richards strutted down the aisle "Come Out And Play (Keep 'Em Separated)" started and Raven *freight-trained* over Richards in the aisle with a running chair shot. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Raven vs. Dancin' Stevie Richards This was actually a fairly even match - for all of about thirty seconds. At which point Raven crotched Richards on the top rope with an atomic drop. Raven did his best to turn Richards into tomato puree' over the next two minutes with chair shots, table shots and *gasp* a wrestling move or two. But, as might be expected, it wasn't very long before Richards' managerial charges, The New Rockers, came to ringside. When he saw the Rockers, Richards threw Raven outside and distracted referee Pee Wee Moore. The Rockers promptly nailed Raven with a spike piledriver through a ringside table and then ran like hell to the back. Unaware of what had happened, Moore counted Raven out on the outside. (match time: 04:20) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF Intercontinental Title Match* "The Innovator Of Violence" Tommy Dreamer (w/ Beulah McGillicutty) *challenger* vs. "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel (w/ Paul Ellering) *champion* No doubt about it, Dreamer took Diesel to the extreme. He pounded the bigger man with everything he could find for almost twenty minutes. He tried to extend the match as long as possible to wear the big man down. The end of the match for Dreamer came when (gasp) back in the ring he tried to hit Diesel with an atomic drop - which Diesel countered with a brutal bulldog headlock. Diesel leveled Dreamer with two lariats and then planted him with a Jack Knife powerbomb. (match time: 20:38) Diesel left as Dreamer was getting to his feet. But as soon as Diesel was gone and as soon as Tommy was finally able to stand - OUT OF THE STANDS CAME MARCUS ALEXANDER BAGWELL! Bagwell vaulted the top rope and threw Beulah out of the ring. When Dreamer rushed him with a clothesline, Bagwell ducked, caught Dreamer and reverse atomic dropped him - then promptly nailed him with a DDT, all in one move! The crowd was roaring with boos as Bagwell kicked Dreamer in the ... eh, Dreamer family jewels. Bagwell was bellowing at the crowd as Joey Styles got in the ring. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Styles] Marcus Alexander Bagwell...what in the name of.... [Bagwell] SHUT YOUR FILTHY MOUTH you impotent piece of flab. What you're looking at here is a changed man. All that time sucking up to 2 Fat Scorpio and Snotty Riggs got me nowhere but jobber to the stars....HARDCORE wrestling, EXTREME wrestling, is the way of the future..and there ain't NEVER been a bandwagon that Marcus Bagwell hasn't been ready to jump on. And with the help of my new benefactor, "Mr. Hardcore" Marc Bagwell is ready to make some people bleed. [Styles] "MR. HARDCORE"!??!?!? WHAT IS THIS *$%T? [Bagwell] It's the wave of the future....Tommy Dreamer, you're a HARDCORE HAS-BEEN! I *AM* HARDCORE!!!! And I am the first of many that're finally going to bring some STYLE to the DEWF. I'm gone. (Bagwell walked away, taking time to stomp on Dreamer's ... erm, appendages once again before he leaves. Damage Control got Tommy out of there, just in time for Chris Candido to head to the ring) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- *Trail Of Revenge* Chris Candido (w/ Sister Sherri) vs. "The Ivory Tower" Justin Cross (w/ Black Sarah) Candido headed to the ring, looking (oddly) supremely confident. *Smirking*. "Dead Souls" by Joy Division began playing as soon as Candido was in the ring, and out came "The Ivory Tower". Black Sarah looked to be slightly bemused by the support her men were getting. When she and Cross got to ringside, Cross got in - and then Black Sarah simply held out her left hand. A large, black *crow* flew out and perched on her hand. Candido jumped a foot in the air, spooked. Sherri ran screaming for the back, not to be seen again the rest of the match. When Candido landed, he got leveled with a *big* running clothesline from Cross. It was imperative Candido keep his cool. He didn't. He couldn't even come close to dealing with Cross' power and speed. It didn't help, that crow perched on Black Sarah's hand, I'll tell you. This wasn't so much a match as a massacre. For twelve long minutes, Cross seemed obsessed with putting Candido in the hospital. Finally, Cross hit Candido with a flying forearm and set up Candido on the top rope for the "Falling Tower". Nail it he did and the three count was academic. (match time: 12:51) As Candido held the referee's attention post-match by complaining about a fast count - here came The Heavenly Bodies into the ring! Candido heaved referee John Finnegan over the top rope and joined in the the beating of a weary Cross. The three had just spike-piledrivered Cross when "Burn" by The Cure began playing and *The Crow* hit the ring, trash can lid in hand and began laying all three men out. He got in several hits on each before Candido tackled him from behind. Candido and the Bodies began a three on two on Cross and The Crow - and then "Just One Fix" by Ministry began blaring - AND HERE CAME "THE SHADOW" MICHAEL MONROE!!!!! Monroe quickly cleared the ring, swinging a mean chair, as he helped Cross and The Crow to their feet. All three, plus manager Black Sarah, exited to the thunderous cheers of "DEWF! DEWF! DEWF!". -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Styles: (shakes his head) Chris Candido, you're like a man who finds himself at the bottom of a very deep hole with nowhere to go but up - and then asks for a shovel. (shrugs) Oh, well. Before we go to the rematch between The Road Warriors and Da Gangstaz, let's hear from the ex-champions. (cut to a badly lit street obiusly in Da Hood. New Jack and Mustafa, Da Gangstaz, are walking up the sidewalk toward the camera) New Jack: Well, 'Stafa, let's be hones' - we got our ASS kicked. (Mustafa nods his head, smiling and giggling) We went and unde'estimated the two baddest muthaf*ckas outside o' ourse'ves ta ever set foot in the ring. Well, Road Warriors, we gonna turn up the heat, just a lil' bit. We ain't gone unde'estimate ya no more, ya undastand. We gonna come atcha wit' bricks and sticks, and bottles and hubcaps, and we gonna take y'all to da hood, ya undastand. Now y'all came from Halstead Street in Chi-town, but we done come out of da hood's *nightmares*. (Mustafa does The Mustafa Evil Laugh (tm, RevRay)) 'Cuz Da Gangstaz are HEAH, and we ain't ta be played wit' - SUCKAS!!! (Mustafa does The Mustafa Evil Laugh (tm, RevRay)) again as Da Gangstaz head out of shot and we fade back to the DEWF Arena) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF World Tag Team Title Rematch* The Road Warriors (Animal and Hawk, w/ Paul Ellering) *champions* vs. Da Gangstaz (New Jack and Mustafa) *challengers* For once, the champions came out *first*. Then "Natural Born Killaz" blared over the PA, and Da Gangstaz came out from *either side* of the ring, through the crowd! New Jack heaved The Trash Can O' Doom over the top rope and Mustafa went after Hawk with a billy club. But things *really* got out of hand when out of the back came *all six men* from the grudge six man that was *supposed* to be next! 911, Sandman, Vader, Shane Douglas and The Heavenly Bodies fought all the way down the aisle - and into the ring! It got worse when The Fear Machine (Justin Cross and Michael Monroe), along with The Crow, barrelled down the aisle and attacked the Bodies! Referee Jim Mollineaux quickly declared a no-contest and headed for the hills as bodies and weapons began flying everywhere. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Styles: OH MY GOD!!! This has gotten TOTALLY out of hand! Fans, I think that's gonna wrap it the heck up for this week (shakes his head). For the DEWF, I'm Joey Styles. (fade to black) "Now is a time of great decision. Are we to stay or up and quit? There's no avoiding this conclusion - our town is turning into sh*t." --- From Blazing Saddles
