DEWF: The Ross Report and Match Of The Day for 4/26/96!!!

26 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fade in to the DEWF Studios - Jim Ross is sitting at the control desk) Ross: Hello fans, and welcome to the Ross Report. We have some big news concerning the DEWF World's Heavyweight Title that occured earlier today at the Louisville Gardens in Louisville, Kentucky. Let's set *up* what happened with these two interviews. The first was taken right after Thursday Thunder, and the seconds was taped earlier this week. First up is 9-1-1! 911: That stinkin ref had no right to call for the DQ. What the hell was he thinkin? Flair you are the luckiest man on the face of the earth right now because I was about to make you into a pancake! Paul E.: You damned right , this match was a f***ing outrage! What the f*** was that referee thinking? Damned if I know! 911: I want a rematch against you Flair to prove i am the man and I am the REAL WORLD CHAMPION! (fade to hospital scene where a Flair lookalike is squashed like pancake) Doctor: What happened to this man? Nurse: He was hit with a 911 chokeslam! Doctor: Oh gawd, there is no hope for him, bring him to the morgue. (back to Ross) Ross: Next we have an interview conducted by Dok Hendrix at the palatial home of Tiger Jeet Singh. Pay particular attention to this interview, especially where it concerns Shinya Hashimoto. Because after this interview, you will see the *rematch* between 911 and Ric Flair in which Hashimoto plays a very important role. (fade to Dok and Tiger) Dok: First of all let me thank you for... TJS: No, Dok, stop right there. It's an honor for me to have the cameras of the DEWF in my small home [note: Singh lives in a 30-room palace]. You see, once I'm away from the ring, I become a totally different person. Call it a Dr. Jekyll & Mr. Hyde type of thing, call it whatever you want. The fact of the matter is that I'm first and foremost a business- man, just like the wrestlers I manage. We don't take grudges out of the ring. Anyone that does that is an idiot. Why ? It's simple. Anyone that doesn't settle down once he leaves ringside expends far too much energy channeling his hatred, thus he doesn't have enough time to devise strategies. Dok: Well, let's get to matters at hand. Your man, Shinjiro Ohtani, faces Hakushi in a just signed rematch next Thursday. What are your thoughts ? TJS: Dok, I'd be a fool to reveal my plans on national TV. Believe me, we scouted Hakushi ever since he started with the DEWF. Mr. Ohtani knows more about Ginsei Shinzaki than Shinzaki himself. He has watched Hakushi's every match, and he knows counters to all of his holds. I can't go into specifics now for obvious reasons, but you'll see in due time. Dok: That's not where is ends, though. Just before our cameras started rolling, you said that you have a challenge to make on the part of Shinya Hashimoto. What's up ? TJS: Back at the "Rumble In The Jungle" Shinya Hashimoto turned quite some heads with his performance in the Rumble. You may recall the... let's call it an altercation... we had with Mr. 911. On behalf of Mr. Hashimoto, I challenge 911 to a match anywhere, anytime. 911's actions caused Hashimoto to lose face in his home country of Japan, and as we all know losing face is the worst thing that can happen to you in the Eastern Hemisphere. Believe me, as a native of Pakistan, I know all about the Far East, and I know that 911 will be in the fight of his life against a wrestler determined to regain his reputation. Dok: Speaking about reputation, yours is that you're one of the... umm... how shall I phrase this... *strangest* people ever to set foot in the ring. Are you planning on staying retired ? TJS: I'm 48 years old at this point, but as a former Olympian I'm still in top physical condition. I could return to the ring at any given point and wreak havoc... just ask Atsushi Onita. The thing is, I won't. I wrestled for over 18 years. The ring doesn't offer anything new to me anymore. Managing, on the other hand, is still pretty much uncharted waters . It gives me that kind of impetus that makes a person strive that much more, if you know what I mean. My first and foremost goal at this point is to make either Toshiaki Kawada or Shinya Hashimoto the DEWF World Champion in minimum time. Dok: I can't believe it... for once we got a straight answer from Tiger Jeet Singh (Tiger chuckles). Back to you, Jimbo. Ross: And now let's see the World title matchup between 911 and "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ***DEWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH*** "Nature Boy" Ric Flair vs. 911 (w/ Paul E. Dangerously) (challenger) (champion) The balance of this match was a plain and simple beating of Ric Flair at the hands of 911. 911 had beaten Flair to a bloody pulp for about nine minutes, and it didn't get any better once 911 tossed Flair outside. The big man promptly Chokeslammed Flair on the concrete, and was handed a baseball bat, which he put to good use. Once, twice he hit Flair with the bat. But Flair's legendary resiliency soon surfaced as he reversed a 911 whip into the ringsteps and bloodied the big man! Back in the ring, Flair had just nailed 911 with a *nice* single leg takedown - when Shinya Hashimoto came to ringside. "The Nature Boy" scored not one, but two swinging neckbreakers and went up top. He paused to "WHOOOO" at the crowd, and then jumped - and missed a flying kneedrop! 911 promptly hopped right up and grabbed Flair by the throat - CHOKESLAM!!! The ref counted one - two - and just out of the referee's line of sight, Hashimoto put Flair's foot on the rope. 911 Chokeslammed Flair again - but as the referee went down for the count, Hashimoto attacked Paul E! The ref went over to break it up, but in Paul E's wild flailings, he accidentally (honest to God) threw the phone into the ring - where it was caught by Ric Flair!!!!!!! One home run swing by Flair and a three count later, we had a new champion!!!! (match time: 13:04) (the crowd is going absof***inglutely psychotic at this point) Ring Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner and - NNNNNEEEEEWWWWWWWWWWWW DEWF World's Heavyweight Champion - "THE NATURE BOY" RIIIIIICCCCC FLAIRRR!!!!! But the debacle for 911 was not yet over. Flair wrapped up the big man in a figure four while Hashimoto repeatedly splashed Flair's legs, causing even more damage. By the time Flair and Hashimoto left, 911 had to be stretchered out of the arena. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Ross: Ladies and gentlemen, I have received word that 911 will be out of action for at least six weeks with strained tendons, but he will be able to return. As far as the actions of Ric Flair and Shinya Hashimoto, Interim DEWF Commissioner Tod Gordon has promised both incredibly stiff fines and will make a statement on our Sunday Slam program. On a personal note, I have known "The Nature Boy" for many, many years. Ric, I am begging you, PLEASE don't let this kind of shenanigans continue or you will forever tarnish your outstanding career. Before we leave, we also received an interview from Jerry Lawler and his team of The Razor Boys, Al Perez and Paul Diamond. For the DEWF, I'm Jim Ross saying so long, everybody! (Camera comes up on Lee Marshall. He is standing outside of the locker rooms with Jerry Lawler and the Razor Boys. Al Perez is scowling ferociously, while Paul Diamond is pacing frantically back and forth.) LM : Jerry Lawler, your Razor Boys haven't made much of an impact of any sort in the DEWF so far. Why the low profile? JL : The answer is simple, Lee. The Royal Assassins came to the DEWF with the understanding that this was the home of Extreme wrestling for Extreme wrestlers. Instead we have what looks like a nursing home for cowards and weaklings. Not one team has dared to accept the challenge of the Razor Boys. The booking committee hasn't returned our phone calls. In other words, the Razor Boys have been blacklisted from the DEWF because the DEWF is afraid of the Razor Boys. PD (kicking over a nearby garbage can) : Damn it all, what the hell does a tag team have to do to get some matches in this federation? Do we have to set ourselves on fire? Do we have to go out there and start breaking bones until we finally get some respect? Does every team in the DEWF have to bleed at our feet until we get a title shot? Well, we've had enough. The Razor Boys are signing an open contract. Ayone dares to want to meet us, any time, any circumstances, sign on the dotted line and the Razor Boys will be there. Just make sure your insurance is paid up. And if that doesn't work, well , let's say we'll have to take more Extreme measures. (Jerry Lawler and the Razor Boys storm off) (fade to black)
