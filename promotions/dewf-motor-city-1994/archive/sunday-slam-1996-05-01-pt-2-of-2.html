DEWF Sunday Slam for 4/28/96!!! (Conclusion)

1 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Author's Note: Folks, as some on the list will be willing to verify, we in Missouri have been going through Thuderstorm Hell the past few days. Frankly, for most of that time, it has not been safe to be on the computer. Things have blown over now, so I can finally get this out. Thanks for your patience, not let's get EXTREME!!!!!!!! (commercial for GTE - Profits, what profits? and then we are back with JoJo in Da Ring) Styles: WE ARE BACK! (Popalooza) For those of you who were hiding under a rock this past Friday, 911 lost his DEWF World's Heavyweight Championship to none other than "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair - who had a little help from Shinya Hashimoto. After the match, Flair and that THUG Hashimoto ganged UP on 911 and put the big man out of action via Flair's legendary Figure Four Leglock and Hashimoto Moonsaulting the leglock, heightening the damage done. We got THIS from interim DEWF Commissioner Tod Gordon. (fade to Tod's office) Gordon: Good evening. What took place on Friday's Match Of The Day was absolutely despicable - but then, that's Ric Flair. There have been a flood of calls for the stripping of the title from Ric Flair and his suspension, but I'm not going to do that - no, no, no. (Chuckles) No, I've always been more satisfied if scum like Flair and Hashimoto get their asses kicked in the ring then get away relatively scot free via a suspension. What's going to happen is this - according to the DEWF rankings, the number one contender to the title is Vader. The reason I mention that is Flair might be surprised to find out his partner in slime, Hashimoto, is now demanding a TITLE shot against him. So - Hashimoto will FACE Vader to determine who gets a shot at Prick... excuse me, Ric Flair on this coming Thursday Thunder. Shinya? Have a nice day, a**hole. (laughs and we fade back to Joey, who is joined in the ring by General Skandor Akbar, "Too Sexy" Brian Christopher and Too Cold Scorpio) Styles: Strong words from Tod Gordon for that slimebag Hashimoto and his SUPPOSED crony, the world heavyweight champion, Ric Flair! I'm joined right now by General Skandor Akbar and his men, "Too Sexy" Bri - (Akbar interrupts Joey) Akbar: Please, Joey, give my men their proper introduction! They are "Too Sexy" Brian Christopher and Too Cold Scorpio - The Gangstaz! (Joey starts to object, but is cut off by Christopher's trademark insane cackle) Christopher: Stop right there, Joey Styles! I already know what you're going to say. (Cackle) How can we call ourselves The Gangstaz when there's already another team by that name? (Cackle) Scorpio: 'Cause it goes a little somethin' like this, Joey. We are ten TIMES the team, ten TIMES the wrestlers, and JUST too damned good lookin' - HAVE mercy! (laughs) Christopher: Y'see, Joey Styles... when ya got Too Sexy and Too Cold - Scorpio: It ain't gonna be long 'til The Gangstaz are wearin' the gold! Christopher: (Insance cackle 101) In fact, Joey Styles, I - (Christopher is cut off as "NATURAL BORN KILLAZ" BEGINS PLAYING!!!!!!!!! NEW JACK AND MUSTAFA ARE IN THE HOUSE!!!!!) The crowd was going absolutely apesh*t as The REAL Gangstaz stormed the ring and tore UP Christopher and Scorpio. Even Skandor Akbar got a chairshot right between the eyes from New Jack. Both team brawled all OVER the DEWF Arena before they were finally broke up by the combined forces of the jobber brigade and Philly's Finest. Before he left, though, New Jack got on the house mike and made this challenge: New Jack: You two little punk bitches wanna step to the REAL Gangstaz? If you two suckas got the b*lls, then bring your punk asses on DOWN on Thursday Thunder, and we gonna get BUSY one time! (Mustafa did the Trademark Mustafa Evil Laugh and they left) ------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF World's Light Heavyweight Title Match* Sabu (w/ James E. Cornette) vs. Naoki Sano (Champion) (Challenger) Sabu, frankly, just plain outclassed Sano. Maybe Sabu was fired up by watching what had just happened involving The Gangstaz, who knows. But he took all of a minute and twenty nine seconds to put Sano away with a moonsault. (match time: 1:29) ------------------------------------------------------------------------- *Number One Contenders Match* Vader (w/ Paul Ellering) vs. Shinya Hashimoto (w/ Tiger Jeet Singh) Ali and Foreman. Holyfield and Bowe. Years from now, they'll also be talking about Vader and Hashimoto at the DEWF Arena. This was one for the ages, folks. In just under two minutes, Hashimoto already had Vader busted wide open. But Vader didn't so much as slow down from it, oh no. He and Hashimoto traded shots that would bring down any other two men for good, most likely. Vader finally busted open Hashimoto at about the twelve minute mark by bulldogging him into the ringsteps. From there, Vader took the advantage and worked Hashimoto over for the next three minutes and actually hit the Vader Bomb! We had one - two - and then Tiger Jeet Singh distracted the ref! Paul Ellering tried to attack Singh, but got nailed right in the mush with the butt of Singh's sword. Vader went to see what was happening - and got DDT'ed by Hashimoto right on a chair! 1-2-3! (match time: 15:25) Hashimoto nailed Vader twice with the chair and then held him up while Singh advanced, cutlass in hand! Singh had the cutlass raised high, and looked ready to chop Vader right in two when "Enter Sandman" began playing and HERE CAME THE SANDMAN!!!!!!! Sandman blasted Singh in the back of the head with his infamous SIngapore Cane, dropping Singh like a rock. Sandman ducked a Hashimoto running clothesline and blistered Singh in the back with the cane as he went past. Vader was just getting up as Sandman piledrived Hashimoto on a chair and yelled "Vadersault!" As Singh rolled out, Vader Vadersaulted Hashimoto not once, but twice! Sandman rolled out as well, gave Singh a couple of cane shots for good measure and yelled "VADER TIME!" before leaving. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Back to Joey, standing in front of the DEWF logo) Styles: FINALLY a fed where anything really CAN happen!!! Who'd have thought it? Sandman coming to the rescue of Vader! AGAIN! We'll no doubt here more about this from Vader and the LOD (Author's note: Hint, hint, Pat :)) at Thursday Thunder. Until then, for the DEWF, I'm Joey Styles! (fade to black) "It's a bird! It's a plane! It's SUPERPUTZ!!!!" - Joey Styles commenting on ECW wrestler "Dancin'" Stevie Richards
