The story so far...

Mar 7, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, so you've read the explanation post and now your curiosity is piqued. That's good. Here's the story so far, which will take you up to chapter one of the WCWF... [Note: Everything here was chronicled in DEWF shows leading up to the launch of the WCWF, but since [name withheld] doesn't post here anymore I'll summarize...] When the WCWF originally folded in early 1995, Dibiase had pulled a complex stock deal to end up as the sole owner of the federation, which led to its demise. IE, no one wanted to work for him. Everyone in the federation who was even marginally talented left for the new DEWF, leaving Dibiase with Hulk Hogan as his champion and shows with 15 people sitting around in a barn as Hogan defended his title against local farmers. It was a pathetic situation. Finally, in disgust, Dibiase sold the old WCWF for fifty dollars and a pig to a local farmer and brought Hulk Hogan to the DEWF himself for an nWo-style takeover. Unfortunately, the talent available out there wasn't very impressive. He had Virgil, as always, plus Adam Bomb (who was doing time in an insane asylum), Crush, (who had converted to the Amish faith), the Warlord (who was working as a Hari Krishna) and the Nasty Boys (working in Disneyland as giant Mickey and Minnie Mouse) The "invasion" lasted all of a day, and DEWF commish Tod Gordon decided to give Dibiase a unique punishment -- he would re-establish the WCWF, and gave it to Dibiase to run with his pathetic band of losers. The new federation was immediately on the verge of bankruptcy, and was only saved when Shane Douglas lost a "Loser goes to the WCWF match," which gave Dibiase his first credible wrestler. Since he was still a millionaire, he went out to start signing more talent and hopefully salvage his dignity. Didn't work, of course, but then not much does for Dibiase nowadays...
