DEWF Thursday Thunder for 6/27/1996!!! (take two)

27 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I dunno if this went out or not earlier, so I thought I'd try resending it. (Picture a beautiful mansion, probably somewhere in Southern California. Inside, sitting in a hottub with two bikini-clad Baywatch rejects is DEWF manager "Sex Symbol" Travis Cook, as his charge, 573, stands outside the tub tending to the towels, champagne, and most importantly, his cigerette.) TC: Hello, peasents. The Sex Symbol here, showing you how one should live life. For the last two weeks, people have been asking 573 and myself all sorts of questions, most of them fairly annoying to a man of my class and stature. What with my modeling career, my succesful investments (psst...Kathie Lee Gifford...keep the faith), and my burgeoning managing career, I rarely have time for people below the $100,000 tax bracket. Suffice it to say, (Girl in red bikini hands Cook a glass of Champagne. Vintage 1967, and to 573, vintage Natural Light) Oh, thanks Natasha..listen, why you don't you go wait for me in the rumpus room ? As I was saying, I am succesful in anything I do. Managing is no different. Within two weeks, I have taken the most intimidating, feared, hooligan from the dark side and exposed him for what he is, a over-rated, underskilled, nobody. Underfaker, you may have intimidated everyone else in this territory, but not me, and certainly not this man (pointing to the artist known as 573). You wrestled this man once, and you couldn't get the job done. Now you're on the defensive. Your aura is gone. You got in the ring with a rookie, and only managed a double-countout. (Screaming) C'mon...Where is this former WWF champion? Where is the man who ran Jake Roberts out of the WWF? Where is the monster who damn near killed the Ultimate Warior?! (Quieter, almost sinister) Where is the Undertaker? I have news for you, The Undertaker is dead, may he rest in peace. Long live "Mean" Mark Callous. This is what you have been reduced to. That is what 573 did to you, and will do tenfold should you ever cross our path again. Callous, if you ever challenge us again, mark my words, you will rest in peace! 573 (Taking cig out of mouth while banging a singapore cane at poolside): Bring the noise... ("Warmachine" by KISS blares as we go through the opening montage. At the end, the screen shatters and we are with Joey Styles in front of the DEWF logo) Styles: Hello, wrestling fans and welcome to another hour of the Dream Extreme Wrestling Federation. I'm Joey Styles and what you just saw were words from the self proclaimed "Sexiest MoMutant On Earth" "Sex Symbol" Travis Cook. His man, 573, will be taking on The Undertaker's protege', The Crow, later on in a falls count anywhere match. But right now, we have a match that is a receipt for some events that occured on the last Thursday Thunder as Barry Windham takes on "The Enforcer" Arn Anderson. First though, let's go to some video footage from The Outlaws. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (Fade in on a saloon. Dutch Mantell, Barry Windham, Terry and Jimmy Jack Funk and Blackjack Mulligan are sitting around a table looking very downcast) Mantell: (tips the front of his hat up) I woulda thought the ol' sonofabitch was too tough to die. Terry Funk: Or too damn pickled in Jack to know the difference. (slight chuckle from the group. Funk sighs and rises to leave) I better go check on Dusty. He's takin' this pretty hard. (Mantell nods and Terry leaves with Jimmy Jack) Mulligan: Y'know, me and Dickie Bird were just gettin' ready to open a whole new can of whoopass on the DEWF, and now this. (Buries his head in his hands. His son, Barry, puts his hand on his shoulder - and then puts on Murdoch's own "Eat More Possum" t-shirt and looks straight at the camera) Windham: Arn Anderson - you ruined Dick Murdoch's last match and attacked me besides. For that, I'm gonna put my boot so far up your ass you're gonna have to sneeze so I can lace 'em from now on. (Mulligan stands, smiles and puts his arm around his son's shoulder as they leave. Dutch Mantell turns to the camera, uncharacteristically choked up, but understandably so given the circumstances) Mantell: (wiping a tear from his eye) Arn Anderson - you are about to find out there's truth in that old sayin' - "God forgives, but the Outlaws DAMN sure don't". This one, Double A, is for Captain Redneck. (leaves as "Midnight Rider" plays and we fade into the DEWF arena where Arn is already in the ring with Coach. "Midnight Rider" starts up again, and Barry Windham roars down the aisle and into the ring) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Barry Windham (w/ Dutch Mantell) vs. "Double A" Arn Anderson (w/ Coach) Windham rolled into the ring and was all over Anderson like ugly on George Steele. But "Double A" was up to the task and he and Windham were tradin' some heavy leather early on. Windham was incredibly pumped, but so was Anderson, and the two traded brutal piledrivers, chairshots, and God knows what all in this incredibly intense match. Near the end, Anderson had Windham set for the DDT, but Windham backdropped Anderson and cinched in a sleeperhold - which lasted for all of twelve seconds before Coach entered the ring! Coach wound up with a chair, swung - Windham ducked, and Coach hit *Anderson*!!!! Anderson dropped like a rock, and Windham heaved Coach over the top rope like a shotput. Windham hooked Anderson's leg and got the one-two-three! (match time: 15:20) *After* the match, as Windham was celebrating in the ring, Mister Bob Backlund came down to ringside and began jawing at Windham!! Windham turned to respond, which unfortunately gave Anderson time to recover - and nail Windham from behind with a "Divorce Court" shoulderbreaker DDT! Backlund climbed in the ring as Coach submarined Mantell on the outside with a chair. Backlund hauled Windham to his feet and clamped on the dreaded Cross Face Chickenwing! Backlund held the hold for a good minute as Anderson fended off the jobber brigade. But then Terry Funk and Blackjack Mulligan stormed the ring as Backlund and company cleared out but *fast*. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (fade back to JoJo) Styles: Bob Backlund is like persistant jock itch - every time you think it's gone away, it comes back worse than ever. (Shakes his head) After this, we'll be back with an interview I conducted last week with the Legion Of Doom. But now (rolls his eyes) ... the latest installment of "Million Dollar Dreams". Oi gevault... Million Dollar Dreams Episode Four - "I'm tryin' ta think, but nuttin' happens!" (Ted DiBiase and Virgil are outside Disney World with the braindead Adam Bomb and the lobotomized Warlord) DiBiase: (groans) Do we have to do this? Virgil: Look, boss, you said to come up with something to keep these two happy. This is about it! DiBiase: (sighs) I know, I know. But dammit we should be looking for a tag team! (As if on cue (Gee, what are the odds?), there's a hellacious commotion from inside the gates) What the hell is going ON in there? Virgil: (looking through the gates) Near as I can tell - Mickey and Minnie Mouse are beating the hell out of two kids! DiBiase: (looking interested for the first time in weeks) THIS I have to see. (They go inside and see a VERY bizarre sight - Mickey and Minnie are indeed beating hell out of a couple of kids! Suddenly, Minnie scoops up one kid and *powerslams* him on the concrete as Mickey climbs to the top of a trashcan - and jumps off with a flying elbow!!!) Oh no.. Oh no... it *couldn't* be!!!! (Mickey and Minnie are *handcuffed* by security as an irate park official storms up to them. He gives them the "You'll never work in this town again" routine as he yanks off their costume heads revealing -) DiBiase/Virgil: (together) The Nasty Boys! (Fade in on a gym. Vader is working out with Diesel in the ring, and the Road Warriors are lifting weights. Paul Ellering is pointing things out to three masked men with him. Joey Styles walks into the picture) [JS] Ladies and gentlemen, I'm here at the Legion of Doom's private gym, hoping to be able to talk to the LOD themselves. And it appears that Paul Ellering has something to show us too!! Paul, can I speak with you?? (Ellering turns and, with the three men in tow, walks over to Styles) [PE] Ahhh, Joey, nice to see you again!! At least it's not that rich brat McMahon!! [JS] I must ask, if you don't mind, a question that I'm sure our fans want to know, just who are these three huge masked men!? [PE] Joey, I was wondering how long it would take you to ask. So, Joey Styles, fans of the DEWF, I reveal to you the newest members of the Legion of Doom....(he pulls off the masks) [JS] OH MY GOD!!! IT'S AXL ROTTEN AND THE BRUISE BROTHERS!!! You mean to tell me Paul that these three EXTREME monsters are the newest members of the Legion of Doom!? [PE] Yes they are Joey!! And they want to get an opening match as soon as they can!! But now on to bigger things!!(turns towards the other members) Guys, get over here, Styles is here for an interview!!(they drop everything and jog over. Vader picks up the world title on the way) [JS] Well, let's start with the champ here. Vader you SHOCKED the fans by beating Dean Malenko TWICE in technical matches!! [V] JOEY STYLES!!! YOU'RE LOOKIN' AT THE FIRST EVER TWO TIME DEWF WORLD CHAMP!! DEAN MALENKO!!! I BEAT YOU TWICE IN YOUR OWN GAME!! NOW I'M DONE WITH YOU!!! LINE 'EM UP, AND I'LL KNOCK 'EM DOWN!! IT'S VADER TIME ALL OVER AGAIN!!! [JS] Well, that was.....interesting. Now on to Big Daddy Cool himself, Diesel!! Diesel, we saw what you did at that house show to help Shawn Michaels, the Heartbreak Kid himself, win the DEWF Intercontionental Title. Just why DID you do that?? [D] Well JoJo, ya see, the big Giant was dodgin' me, and he was cheatin' Shawn outta the title, so I thought I'd even up the odds a bit!! I thought I'd make it one-on-one, so I pulled little B*tchoff outta there!! And Shawn didn't waste any time finishin' off the Giant!! So Shawn, how 'bout it now!? You and me, IC Title there bud!! Let's do this!! [JS] And now, the INOVATORS of extreme tag team wrestling, Hawk and Animal, the Road Warriors!! Well guys, you don't seem to have much action lately. What's up with you two?? [H] Weeel, ya see JoJo, we beat on the Razor Boys easy, and we STILL haven't heard from Harlem Heat about a rematch, so we got nothin' to do!! So, how 'bout we go after the tag titles again Animal?? [A] Hey, why not Hawk?? Let's do it!! We want the tag titles!! Let's do this right boys!!! [JS] Well, I guess that's all. Back to the show!! (Fade out as they all turn and head back to their work outs and *we* fade back to Joey in front of the DEWF logo) Styles: After that interview was taped, an *incensed* Giant demanded a match against Diesel *tonight* inside a STEEL CAGE! Interim DEWF commisioner Tod Gordon decided to sweeten the deal a little and added the stipulation that whoever wins this match will receive, later tonight, a DEWF Intercontinental Title match against champion Shawn Michaels. Both men are already in the ring so let's go *to* the ring! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ *STEEL CAGE MATCH* "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel (w/ Paul Ellering) vs. The Giant (w/ Eric Bischoff) Bischoff lasted all of about ten seconds at ringside before World Champion Vader ran down and chased him back to the locker room area. The Giant didn't seem to truly care. He was, to his credit, totally focused on turning "Big Daddy Cool" into "Little Bitty Pool" - of blood. If you were looking for armbars and Greco Roman takedowns, this was the wrong match. If you were looking for boots to the groin, punches, clotheslines and Greco Roman Grinding Your Opponent's Face Across The Cage, this was the *right* match. Late in the match, however, *Cactus Jack* appeared from under the ring and clotheslined Diesel straight into a Giant Chokeslam! The Giant was casually strolling to the door - when Shawn Michaels roared down to ringside and superkicked the door closed RIGHT in the Giant's face! Diesel Jack Knifed Cactus as The Giant staggered backwards! Diesel clotheslined The Giant to the mat for the one - the two - the THREE!!! (match time: 18:41) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Styles: Are we seeing a reunion of the Dudes With Attitudes? Whatever the case, later tonight Diesel will get his shot at Shawn Michaels' DEWF Intercontinental Title. But when we come back, one half of The World Tag Team Champions, Michael Monroe of The Fear Machine along with manager Black Sarah, gets *his* shot at DEWF World Light Heavyweight Champion Sabu! (The scene comes up on a forbidding castle on a hill in the Grand Duchy of Mempho. It is night time. In a spacious room lit only by a desk lamp, Jerry "The King" Lawler stares out of the window. There is a knock at the door.) JL: Come in. (Jeff Jarrett pokes his head in.) JJ: Beggin' your pardon, your highness-ship, but Justin and the Razor Boys would like a word. JL : Send them in. (Justin Hawk Bradshaw and Paul Diamond and Al Perez, sollectively known as the Razor Boys enter the room. Jerry Lawler continues staring out the window.) JHB : Boss, we just wanted to apologize for our performance at the last DEWF card. We got stomped like a narc at a biker rally and we understand if y'all are disappointed in us. PD : Al and I are in agreement on this one, King, we gave our best against the road Warriors and we still got beat. We're both sorry we let you down. JL : Hmmm? Oh ... yes ... it's quite alright. Justin, I'll start petitioning for a rematch for you against the Sandman, only we'll get it in a Cage. Then the advantage will be ours. Al, Paul, for now we'll get you some singles matches. Then you'll come back to the tag scene revitalized and ready to take down the Road Warriors. Now if you'll excuse me, I'd like to be alone now. (Justin Bradshaw and the Razor Boys file out of the room but Jeff Jarrett stays behind. Jerry Lawler goes back to staring out the window.) JJ : King, Black Sarah is thowing a party downstairs to celebrate the Fear Machine winning the Tag titles, champagne and everything. Are you sure you don't want to join ? JL : (silence) JJ : King, what's going on. You've been pretty distant lately . Is it the losses that are getting to you? JL : (sighs) No, Jeffrey, the losses are because I have been too distracted to lead my own men. Justin Cross and Michael Monroe took the World Tag titles because Black Sarah hasn't been neglecting her men like I have. The reason I've been distracted is this. (Jerry Lawler hands Jeff Jarrett a letter that is on his desk. Double J reads it and gives a low whistle.) JJ : My, oh my, oh my. Our own past come back to haunt us. (There is another knock at the door) JL : Come in. (Black Sarah drifts in. She has long black hair and pale white skin. She is currently wearing a floor length black gown with bare shoulders.) JL : Ahh, the divine Sarah. Congratulations, that was quite an upset your men pulled off. I've always had confidence in your abilities but i didn't realize they would bring such drastic results so soon. Sarah : Thank you, Jerry. We miss you at the party. What's going on? (Jeff Jarrett hands her the letter. She reads it.) Sarah : I thought Nixon resigned in 1974. JJ : To Hell with Nixon, it's the rest of it that has us worried. Sarah : I see. (turning to Lawler) an old grudge I take it? JL : You could say that. We have a ... history. When he was put in that asylum, I had thought we had seen the last of him. It seems I was wrong. Sarah, when we agreed to work together, I never mentioned this. I'd understand if you wished to back out. Sarah : Now Jerry, you know me better than that. When was the last time the Fear Machine ever backed out of a fight? We'll make sure that your old friend will wish he had stayed in his padded cell and (smiles evilly) perhaps we'll even make sure Millionaire Ted runs back to Omaha with his tail between his legs. JL : (grins) Now that is an image I find particularly pleasing. (holding out his hand palm downwards) All for one ... Sarah : (placing her hand on his) ... and one for all. Are you with us, Jeffrey ? JJ : Aren't I always? (Jeff Jarrett places his hand on theirs as the scene fades.) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ *DEWF World Light Heavyweight Title Match* "The Shadow" Michael Monroe (w/ Black Sarah) vs. Sabu (w/ Jim Cornette) Challenger Champion Shocker! Sabu finally found met his match in aerial wizardry and sheer, well, extremeness! Monroe, frankly, made Sabu look like a rookie in this match. He outflew, outweaponed, and outtabled Sabu. Monroe even put Sabu through a table on the outside with a rana from the top turnbuckle! Monroe poured Sabu back in the ring and mockingly hit him an Arabian Facebuster slingshot legdrop - and got the one - the two - the THREE!!!!! (match time: 08:21) Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen - your winner - and NNNNEWWWWWWWWWWWW DEWF World Light Heavyweight Champion - "The Shadow" Michael Monnnnrrooeee!!" ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ *FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE* 573 (w/ "Sex Symbol" Travis Cook) vs. The Crow (w/ Raven) "Bring Tha Noize" brought 573 down to the ring, as an exultant Cook led the way. The big man had barely gotten into the ring when the lights went down and a green light and dry ice fog filled the aisle as "Burn" by The Cure began to play. Raven got all the way down to ringside - but still no Crow! Cook leaned over the ropes (from behind 573, of course) and began taunting Raven. Raven said not one word, but merely spread his arms wide and clenched his fists in his trademark gesture. The lights went completely black - and there was a "BONG" - then they came back up again - AND THE CROW WAS STANDING RIGHT BEHIND COOK! The Crow spun Cook around and nailed him with a *vicious* superkick right in the mouth! Cook rolled out of the ring, clutching a bleeding lip, and staggered back to the locker room area, followed by Raven (who assumedly wanted to keep an eye on the devious Cook). 573 and The Crow were just wailing hell out of each other. I mean, they were in the ring for all of about thirty seconds until things spilled outside. They went literally all over the DEWF Arena, and everything *but* the kitchen sink was used as these two seemed to want to *kill* each other. High spots included an "A-B-SEEya!" spinebuster on the bleachers - which amazingly got only a two count, and a Crow savate kick on the second floor which sent 573 flying to the first floor - ten feet straight down! But the end came minutes later when they headed back to the ringside area, trading chairshots all the way. The end went like this: 573 wound up with a chair - which got dropkicked right back in his face by The Crow. 573 staggered back and ricocheted off the security railing into a DDT on a chair from The Crow. During all of this, Cook had made his way back through the crowd and NAILED The Crow in the back of the head with a nine iron! Crow was out like a light. 573 put his feet up on the guardrail as Cook held The Crow's ... er.. feet. Referee John Finnegan reluctantly counted the one - two - three! (match time: 14:10) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Styles: You can better believed that little weasel Cook has NOT heard the last of The Dark Side. Coming up next we have that DEWF Intercontinental Title Match between "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel and champion Shawn Michaels! Let's go to the ring! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ *DEWF INTERCONTINENTAL TITLE MATCH* "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel (w/ Paul Ellering) vs. "Sexy Boy" Shawn Michaels (w/ Naomi Campbell) Challenger Champion This was every bit as good a match as the two ever had in the Whiff. Diesel used his power and height edge to it's advantage while Shawn used his aerial skills and speed to *their* full advantage. Unfortunately, it was all marred twelve minutes in when The Giant stuck HIS big nose into the match. The action went outside, and Diesel was backdropped out of a Jack Knife attempt by Michaels. As both men lay prone on the concrete, The Giant lumbered to ringside and Choke Slammed BOTH men on the floor! Referee James Beard quickly ordered The Giant back to the locker room, and The Giant mockingly complied, laughing all the way. Both men crawled back into the ring. Both rose from the *mat* at the same time, but Michaels got the first move in - Sweet Chin Music! One - two - three! (match time: 13:50) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Styles: Fans, we have just received word that our main event next week will be The Dudes With Attitudes, Diesel and Shawn Michaels, versus Cactus Jack and The Giant!
