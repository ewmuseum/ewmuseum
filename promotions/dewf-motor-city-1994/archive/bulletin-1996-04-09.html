DEWF Bulletin

9 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(fade in on Joey Styles standing in front of the DEWF logo) Styles: Hello, wrestling fans! Rumble In The Jungle 1996 is over and we have three new champions! The Giant took the Intercontinental Championship in that massive three way dance that also featured "Black Death" Ahmed Johnson and 911. Ironically enough, it was the chokeslam that put 911 away and gave The Giant the strap. Public Enemy took BACK the World Tag Team titles in a match that featured some of the most bizarre happenings in recent memory, more about that later. But the highest anticipation of all was for the Extreme Royal Rumble to fill the vacant DEWF World Heavyweight Championship. Bodies were flying everywhere and the man who very nearly stole the show from the eventual champion was Shinya Hashimoto, who went over 1 *hour* in this gargantuan rumble. Still, the man who finally overcame the odds and claimed the vacant strap - was the man who had earlier in the card lost the IC title. 911 IS our new DEWF Champion. Fans, Jim Ross will be with your tomorrow with a very special Ross Report and he will have updates on all the big stories that came out of Rumble In The Jungle '96. For the DEWF, I'm Joey Styles. (fade) "That man does NOT want to know all the things I can do to him with a butter knife!" - Brigadier General Adrienne Kramer *DOCTOR WHO - TIME RIFT*
