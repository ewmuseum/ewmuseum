DEWF — Independence Day Armageddon

DEWF Independence Day Armageddon!!!(tape delayed - part one)

5 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The setting is old Philadelphia. The caption reads "1776". The men in the picture are obviously supposed to be our founding fathers, hard at work on the constitution) Voiceover: Philadelphia - cradle of our nation's independence and city of bortherly love. (Suddenly Sandman bursts into the scene and starts caning everything that moves. After the dust has cleared, he turns to the camera.) Sandman: My, how things have f***in' changed! (takes a drag on his cig, chugs a beer and canes Ben Franklin. "Warmachine" starts and we go through the usual opening montage, except with a flag waving as the background. Across the usual DEWF Thursday Thunder logo is spray painted "Special Edition: Independence Day Armageddon". The screen crashes and we join Joey Styles in the House Of Hardcore, the DEWF Arena.) Styles: (has to yell above the mutant cheers of "DEWF! DEWF! DEWF!") I'm Joey Styles and WELCOME to DEWF Independence Day Armageddon!!!!!! (Crowd starts popping even more insanely as both Rey Misterio Jr. and Psicosis hit the ring. Joey barely manages to get out of the way and we are *under* way!) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- *EXTREME Mexican Death Match* Rey Misterio Jr. (w/ General Skandor Akbar) vs. Psicosis The rockets red glare. The bombs bursting in air. Rey Misterio Jr. putting his foot ALL up in Psicosis' ass. Rey Jr. was a luchador on a mission this night, as poor Psicosis barely got a move in the entire match. Rey Jr. even used referee John Finnegan as a springboard on several occasions to attack Psicosis. Chairs were swung, tables were smashed and it just plain got ugly. Rey Jr. finally put Psicosis away in just under eleven minutes with a hurricarana from the eagle's nest to the stage and right off it to the floor. Ouch. (match time: 10:53) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Justin "Hawk"Bradshaw (w/ Jerry "The King" Lawler) vs. Barry Windham (w/ Dutch Mantell) Bradshaw made his way down to ringside, all business as usual. But the crowd lit up when "Midnight Rider" began playing and down came Barry Windham, his arm in a cast and sling. Windham's manager Dutch Mantell got on the house mic: Mantell: Now listen here, big boy. Ain't nobody in this world got more right to whup your ass than Barry Windham. But that walkin' advertisement for shock therapy Backlund made sure Barry can't wrestle you tonight. (Lawler and Bradshaw begin making "Aw, poor widdle baby" type stuff at Windham as the crowd boos) But what we went and done is we hunted us up one of the toughest sons of bitches that ever walked the face of God's green earth and HE'S gonna whup your six-foot-eight ass! (Dutch turns to leave and he and Barry both get jumped by Lawler and Bradshaw. The crowd goes *insane* as "Man In The Box" by Alice In Chains starts up and "The Innovator Of Violence" Tommy Dreamer roars into the ring, so the match is now...) Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw (w/ Jerry "The King" Lawler) vs. "The Innovator Of Violence" Tommy Dreamer (w/ Beulah McGillicutty) Of the eight minutes and thirty five seconds that this match lasted, about fifteen seconds of it was spent actually in the ring. Bradshaw thought he was tough, but Tommy took his big ass to the EXTREME. Tommy *literally* used everything but the kitchen sink to put a hurt on Bradshaw. Make no mistake, Bradshaw gave as good as he got. What he lacked experience wise in using weapons, he more than made up in kicks, punches and a good old fashioned cowbell upside the head. Both men were bloody messes. At the end, Tommy was running off the ropes to hit Bradshaw with a chair shot and Bradshaw gave it a big boot, which sent it flying right back in Tommy's face, dropping him like a rock. Bradshaw grabbed his branding iron as was about to "brand" Tommy when *Barry Windham* hit the ring and teed OFF on Bradshaw with a *nasty* chair shot to the back of the head, dropping *Bradshaw* like a rock. Referee Jim Mollineaux had no choice but to declare this a no contest. (match time: 08:35) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- *Triangle Match* Jushin "Thunder" Liger (w/ General Skandor Akbar) vs. The Crow (w/ Raven) vs. Super Blonde Fans of high flying wrestling surely got there fix in this match as all three men showed aerial wrestling could get as extreme as anything else, thankyouverymuch. The first man eliminated was Super Blonde, when The Crow put him away with his patented Crowsault at the 8:24 mark. As The Crow leaned against a cornerpost to catch his breath, Liger tried a flying shoulderblock to his back - but The Crow *moved* and Liger hit nothing but post. As Liger stumbled backwards, The Crow clamped on a Fujiwara armbar! Say goodnight, Jushin. (match time: 09:26) NOTE: After the match was over, Super Blonde was found unconscious and bleeding in the locker room area with a note pinned to his chest: "Be watchful, old friend. For everywhere you go, everything you do, I will be there to haunt you. Your sins are about to find their due punishment. --- The Black Scorpion" -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Raven (w/ The Crow) vs. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin Austin was very much a credit to his nickname, as he didn't seem the least bit rattled by the eerie presence of The Crow at ringside. Indeed, much to his credit, he kept the match a *wrestling* match, no matter what Raven tried to do to turn it into a brawl. That, in the end, was Raven's undoing. Austin wore him down steadily and clamped on a devestating abdominal stretch smack in the middle of the ring. Raven had nowhere to go, and had no choice but to give up. (match time: 12:54) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Styles: Fans, when we come back we will have the Two Dudes With Attitudes reuniting to take on Cactus Jack and The Giant! (intermission clock) "Take my hand.... off to Never Never Land..." - Metallica, "Enter Sandman" [name withheld], ECW-L listowner/poobah - Taking internet email to the EXTREME!!!!
