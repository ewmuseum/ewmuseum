DEWF Sunday Slam for 6-02-1996!

2 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Usual opening sequence. Yada, yada, yada, blah, blah, blah.... :) and we cut to Jim Ross in the DEWF Control Center) Ross: Hell, wrestling fans, and welcome to the DEWF Sunday Night Slam. We only have a couple of matches for you tonight. But our big feature for the night is the shocking news from Covington, Kentucky of not one but *two* title changes! But first, let's show you an interview we received from Jerry "The Burger King" Lawler and company. (The scene comes up on a forbidding castle on a hill in the Grand Duchy of Mempho. It is night time. In a spacious room lit only by a desk lamp, Jerry "The King" Lawler stares out of the window. There is a knock at the door.) JL: Come in. (Jeff Jarrett pokes his head in.) JJ: Beggin' your pardon, your highness-ship, but Justin and the Razor Boys would like a word. JL : Send them in. (Justin Hawk Bradshaw and Paul Diamond and Al Perez, collectively known as the Razor Boys enter the room. Jerry Lawler continues staring out the window.) JHB : Boss, we just wanted to apologize for our performance at the last DEWF card. We got stomped like a narc at a biker rally and we understand if y'all are disappointed in us. PD : Al and I are in agreement on this one, King, we gave our best against the road Warriors and we still got beat. We're both sorry we let you down. JL : Hmmm? Oh ... yes ... it's quite alright. Justin, I'll start petitioning for a rematch for you against the Sandman, only we'll get it in a Cage. Then the advantage will be ours. Al, Paul, for now we'll get you some singles matches. Then you'll come back to the tag scene revitalized and ready to take down the Road Warriors. Now if you'll excuse me, I'd like to be alone now. (Justin Bradshaw and the Razor Boys file out of the room but Jeff Jarrett stays behind. Jerry Lawler goes back to staring out the window.) JJ : King, Black Sarah is thowing a party downstairs to celebrate the Fear Machine winning the Tag titles, champagne and everything. Are you sure you don't want to join ? JL : (silence) JJ : King, what's going on. You've been pretty distant lately . Is it the losses that are getting to you? JL : (sighs) No, Jeffrey, the losses are because I have been too distracted to lead my own men. Justin Cross and Michael Monroe took the World Tag titles because Black Sarah hasn't been neglecting her men like I have. The reason I've been distracted is this. (Jerry Lawler hands Jeff Jarrett a letter that is on his desk. Double J reads it and gives a low whistle.) JJ : My, oh my, oh my. Our own past come back to haunt us. (There is another knock at the door) JL : Come in. (Black Sarah drifts in. She has long black hair and pale white skin. She is currently wearing a floor length black gown with bare shoulders.) JL : Ahh, the divine Sarah. Congratulations, that was quite an upset your men pulled off. I've always had confidence in your abilities but i didn't realize they would bring such drastic results so soon. Sarah : Thank you, Jerry. We miss you at the party. What's going on? (Jeff Jarrett hands her the letter. She reads it.) Sarah : I thought Nixon resigned in 1974. JJ : To Hell with Nixon, it's the rest of it that has us worried. Sarah : I see. (turning to Lawler) An old grudge I take it? JL : You could say that. We have a ... history. When he was put in that asylum, I had thought we had seen the last of him. It seems I was wrong. Sarah, when we agreed to work together, I never mentioned this. I'd understand if you wished to back out. Sarah : Now Jerry, you know me better than that. When was the last time the Fear Machine ever backed out of a fight? We'll make sure that your old friend will wish he had stayed in his padded cell and (smiles evilly) perhaps we'll even make sure Millionaire Ted runs back to Omaha with his tail between his legs. JL : (grins) Now that is an image I find particularly pleasing. (holding out his hand palm downwards) All for one ... Sarah : (placing her hand on his) ... and one for all. Are you with us, Jeffrey ? JJ : Aren't I always? (Jeff Jarrett places his hand on theirs as the scene fades.) Ross: (shudders) I'll tell ya, that Black Sarah would have to suntan for three years to be called *pale*. Anyway, speaking of Millionaire Ted, let's see the latest installment of "Million Dollar Dreams". Million Dollar Dreams "Fear And Loathing In Ava, Missouri" (We join Ted DiBiase and Virgil at what could charitably be described as a run down gas station. Ted's limousine is .. welll.. dusty to say the least) Virgil: Well, that seems to have worked. DiBiase: The Power Range doll seems to have done the trick? Virgil: Wellll.. sort of. (We see, inside the limo, the formerly fearsome Adam Bomb with a Pink Power Ranger doll shoved up his left nostril and drooling) DiBiase: Oh GOD.... (a yokel galumphs up to Ted and Virgil) Yokel: Howdy there, Mister DiBiase! Hyuk, hyuk! I'm yer biggest fan! Hyk, hyuk *snort*! DiBiase: (looks at Virgil with a "Please shoot me now" look) Oh... GOD!!! (leans against the limo and begins sobbing) Yokel: Say thar, Mister DiBiase - there's a feller out back who'd make a jim dandy wrestler! (DiBiase looks at Virgil with a "What do we have to lose at this point?" look and they follow the yokel around back. As they walk around back, the Yokel relates the following story) Yassir, there wuz a group o' them thar Hairy Fishnuts .... DiBiase: (eyes glazing slightly, mumbles) Hare Krishnas. Yokel: Thass what ah said! Hyuk, hyuk *snort*! (DiBiase groans) Anyhows, this feller was a big un and they couldn't afford to keep 'im fed he ate s'durn much so they left him here! (DiBiase and Virgil turn the corner and see a large man, in a raggedy robe and sandals, beating his tambourine which already has a hole in it and looking lobotomized. It's...) DiBiase and Virgil: (together) The Warlord! (fade) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz and 911 (w/ Paul E. Dangerously and Bill Alfonso) vs. "The Franchise" Shane Douglas and Too Cold Scorpio (w/ Sister Sherri) After what happened on Thursday Thunder, you would have figured there was no way Shane and Too Cold would even remotely want to step in the ring with a p*ssed off Taz *AND* 911. But say what you will about Shane Douglas, he's never back down from a fight, and tonight would not be the first time. You have to hand it to Douglas and Scorp. They wrestled like a well oiled tag team machine - for about a minute. Then after a back suplex by Shane on Taz, Too Cold went for The Diss That Don't Miss, which connected only with Taz's upraised knees! While Scorpio rithed in pain on the mat, Taz made the hot tag to 911 and the crowd went *nuts*. That signaled the end of any passing resemblance to a wrestling match, as from there it turned into a Pier Six Brawl (tm, Gordon Solie). Unfortunately for 911 and Taz, it was *again* not going to be their night as at about the fifteen minute mark, Shane Douglas chairshotted Paul E., grabbed the cell phone and glommed 911 with it, setting up The Diss That Don't Miss by Scorp and the pin. (match time: 15:20) ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Undertaker (w/ Paul Bearer) vs. 573 (w/ "The Sexiest MoMutant On Earth" Travis Cook) Cook was brave, strong, and determined to stay by his managerial charge, 573. (In case you didn't see Thursday Thunder, 573 was Undertaker's mystery partner against The Pitbulls - and walked out on him) That is, he was right up until the first "BONG"s started - and then "BUHbye"! To make a long story very short, both men fought tooth and nail for about (ironically enough) thirteen minutes and then went outside, where the ref reached a double countout. (match time: 13:17) The new man, 573, impressed everyone with his ability in the air and on the mat for such a big man. Interim DEWF Commissioner Tod Gordon quickly signed a rematch between the two for Thursday Thunder. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- Ross: Fans, we are having technical difficulties bringing up the video footage of the title changes last night, but we will have those matches for you on a very special edition of The Ross Report this coming Tuesday. For the DEWF, I'm Jim Ross.
