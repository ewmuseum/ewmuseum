An explanation...

Mar 7, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Howdy. I'm [name withheld], and depending on your point of view you'd probably either remember me as the net.cop or former WCWF honcho. Well, I'm here as the latter right now. Those who numbered among my fans (you know who you are) seemed to love my somewhat twisted sense of humor for whatever sick reason. There's just no accounting for taste. I gave up the league in 1995 due to disinterest on my part, and that was that. Almost. Now, as you may or may not know, [name withheld] runs a league called the DEWF, which he stopped sending to RSPWF a little while ago because his news gateway stopped working. It was sort of a spinoff of the WCWF which used e-wrestlers in addition to my "real wrestlers only" policy. Being bored a few months ago, I suggested that I run an angle of my own as a subset of that league, and thus my WCWF was re-born. There is, I believe, an e-wrestling federation using the WCWF moniker at the moment, but mine has two important differences: 1) I'm not an e-wrestling federation. 2) I'm much funnier than he is. Besides, I was here first anyway. Just so you know, this is basically a satire, not a federation as such. I have no members, all bad jokes are mine and mine alone. Unless I stole them from someone else. I go by chapters, with little interludes and the occasional house show stuck in between. I'm up to chapter 8 at the moment, and I generally get one every two weeks or so. You don't need to read the DEWF to follow what's going on, it stands as a shining example of cheap laughs on its own. Pay attention, a quiz follows. And now, the WCWF...
