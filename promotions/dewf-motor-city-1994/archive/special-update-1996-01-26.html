DEWF - Special Update!!!!!!

26 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen is black. The polished steel-grey letters of the DeWF logo drop from the top of the screen, one by one. Then, the words "Special Update" drop form across the logo in blood red letters. There is a flash of lightning, a clap of thunder and "Higher Ground" by RHCP begins playing as we fade in to the studio where we see "Diamond" Dallas Studd and - Dok Hendrix!) Studd: GOOD g-g-g-g-GAWD, children! The DeWF is here and the Studd is in effect, baby! Hendrix: (bouncing up and down with excitement) Aren'tcha gonna introduce me? Studd: Ladies, gentlemen, and Newt Gingrich too - Hendrix: CHA-ching! Studd: Lance Romance Russell isn't here today, but graciuously filling in is the one, the ONly - Fabulous Freebird Michael "P.S." Hayes! Hendrix: (whispers out of the side of his mouth) K-a-y-f-a-b-eeeeeee! Studd: Oh, can the crap, Michael. Everyone knows who you are, m'man. Hendrix: True, but Dok Hendrix takes a hell of a lot less time to type than Fabulous Freebird etc.,etc.,etc. . Studd: True. Okay, go ahead. (Author's Note: Thanks, Dallas) Studd: S'okay. ANYhow, we are here to announce the line up for the torunament to determine the VERY first ever DeWF World's Heavyweight Champion. Hendrix: Man alive, these are thirty two names that just absolutely are the cream of the crop of professional wrestling. Studd: Hold it aren't there any e-wrestlers in this lineup? Hendrix: Well, as the author has only gotten two e-wrestlers offering to join so far, he's gonna hold the e-wrestlers out of the initial tourneys until he get more in. Studd: Oh, okay. Hendrix: Anyhow, here's the list - and what a list it is: ("Higher Ground" plays softly in the background as we see pictures of the entrants as their names are read off) (1) "The Macho Man" Randy Savage (17) "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair (2) Bret "The Hitman" Hart (18) The Ultimate Warrior (3) The Undertaker (19) "Sexy Boy" Shawn Michaels (4) Lex Luger (20) Sting (5) Arn Anderson (21) Vader (6) Great Muta (22) "Ringmaster" Steve Austin (7) Cactus Jack (23) "The Madman From The Sudan" (8) Sid Abdullah The Butcher (9) Taz (24) Yokozuna (10) "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel (25) Skip (11) Dan Severn (26) Konnan (12) "The Shooter" Dean Malenko (27) King Mabel (13) Tommy "He's Hardcore Now" Dreamer (28) Hunter Hearst Helmsley (14) 911 (29) Vampiro Canadiense (15) Barry Horowitz (30) "The Shah Of Philadelphia" (16) Disco Inferno Hack Myers (31) Ahmed Johnson (32) The Giant (Fade back into the studio) Studd: LOVE that lineup, but - Barry Horowitz and Disco Inferno????? Obviously the cream of the crop went a little sour! Hendrix: Oh, my. (Cut to close up of Dok) Folks, those are just the entrants. We'll be giving you the *actual* brackets Saturday night. Studd: And this list by NO means represents the total talent roster the DeWF has to draw from. Hendrix: (off camera, chuckles) Not even *close*. (Cut back to wide shot of Dok and Studd) Folks, we'll be back later today to introduce you to the inaugural *commisioner* of the DeWF. Studd: And just who might that be? Hendrix: Now where would the surprise be if I told you? (Studd feigns swooning w/ disappointment) Studd: We'll *also* give you some of the *celebrity* names who'll be involved at Michigan Mayhem '96. Hendrix: (imitating Ted Baxter from the Mary Tyler Moore Show) So for "Diamond" Dallas Studd, this is Dok Hendrix saying Good Morning, and have a nice day. Hee hee hee! (Fade out to "Higher Ground". As we fade, we hear "Diamond" Dallas groan "Have a nice DAY????")
