DEWF — Thursday Thunder

DEWF Thursday Thunder for 9/05/1996!!!

5 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Sean Mooney is standing outside of the locker room. Behind him stands Jerry "The King" Lawler, looking confident, and Justin Hawk Bradshaw, scowling ferociously and wearing the Intercontinental Title Belt. Michael Monroe paces in the background, occasionally being pulled back by his leash by Black Sarah.) SM : Jerry Lawler, your man Justin Bradshaw faces a stiff challenge, facing a very angry Diesel for the belt Bradshaw stole from him. JL : Stole ? Mooney, you are getting too damn moral for your own good. This isn't the Marquis of Queensbury rules, this is professional wrestling. If Diesel can't look out for himself, then perhaps he should look into another line of work because he has no place in the DEWF. Bradshaw : Big Daddy Cold, you're crying your eyes out over losing the IC title? Well, cry me a river, big man, it won't get you the belt back. You were stupid enough to take on Justin Hawk Bradshaw on his home ground of Texas, and look where it got you. You want your belt back, boy? Come and get it ! SM : Black Sarah, The Shadow has to be looking for revenge as well as defending the Light Heavyweight title against Chris Candito. Sarah : Revenge is not the word, Sean. It is a debt of blood that must be paid. Candito, you sought to make your mark on the wrestling world by ambushing The Shadow. Well, when the Shadow falls on you, he will leave a mark on you that will never fade. We're coming for your soul, Candito, you had better be ready to pay the ferryman's toll. Monroe : This is where it all ends, Candito. We have reached the end of the trail. Now the mouse is caught in the cage, and there is only one thing left. Were you really so foolish as to think you could get away with it, Candito ? Try to take me out in the most cowardly manner possible ? Try to end my career just to further your own ? You thought you had paid for it already, didn't you ? Paid for it when Al Perez put your shoulders to the mat. Paid for it when Paul Diamond humiliated you in less than a minute. Paid for it when Justin Cross hit you with the Falling Tower. Paid for it when the Crow nearly pulled your arm out of its socket. No, my dear boy, there is one more price to pay. A price so great that I was willing to put my Light Heavyweight title up against a man who has lost his last four matches just to get to you. The price is more than just honour, more than just pride, more than even blood. The price is your self, Candito. Be ready to give it up. Say goodnight, Skip. This night never ends. (Camera fades to the DEWF opening montage and then to the DEWF Arena where we hear "YMCA" start over the PA as "Indian Chief" Blue Meanie heads to the ring, led by "Dancin'" Stevie Richards) Voice of Joey Styles: Hello, everyone, and WELCOME to the Dream Extreme Wrestling Federation! We're opening with, oh GOD help me, "Indian Chief" Blue Meanie of The Village Idiots going against ("Enter Sandman" interrupts Da Meanie and "Dancin'" Stevie leading the crowd in "YMCA") THIS man, The Sandman! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Sandman (w/ Naomi Campbell) vs. "Indian Chief" Blue Meanie (w/ "Dancin'" Stevie Richards) Welllll THIS match lasted all of about ten seconds. Meanie tried to get Sandman to do the "YMCA" bit. Mister Forehead Of Blue Meanie, Meet Mister Singapore Cane Swung With Much Force. (match time: 00:11) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Styles: To quote the late Graham Chapman, THAT was fun, wasn't it? (Rolls his eyes) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Raven vs. "Mister Hardcore" Marc Bagwell Bagwell made his way to ringside first as "Back In Black" by AC/DC blared over the PA, proimising a surprise for Raven. "Come Out And Play (Keep 'Em Separated)" by Offspring was next, and Raven made it about halfway down the aisle - when "Sweet" Jimmy Lowe from the USeW roared out from the curtains and blasted him with a baseball bat to the head! Bagwell and Lowe hauled Raven into the ring and began putting a major hurt on him. Chairs, ball bats, you name it, they used it. Bagwell and Lowe had just nailed Raven with a spike piledriver onto a chair when Jim Cornette and Harlem Heat made the save! A VERY irate Lowe challenged Harlem Heat to a tag match at the next Thursday Thunder. Needless to say, the Heat accepted. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- "Macho Man" Randy Savage (w/ Paul E. Dangerously) vs. Disco Inferno (w/ "Dancin'" Stevie RIchards) DI boogie and strutted his way down to ringside, along with Richards, totally ignoring the catcalls (and worse) that rained down on him from the stands. DI had barely made it into the ring and started dancin' away when his music was interrupted by a heavy metal version of "Pomp And Circumstance". DI and Richards acted very offended. At least, they did right up until Savage roared into the ring and opened a WHOLE can of whupass on DI. Stevie Richards tried to trash-talk Paul E., but that got him a phone shot to the head. Nighty night, "Dancin' One". It seemed like every time Savage hit a move, Inferno rolled out of the ring and became frantic over whether Savage had *gasp* messed up his hair! It became reasonably that Savage could rip out DI's *lungs* - as long as he didn't mess up his *hair* (God forbid). Eventually, Disco gained the upper hand and began battering Savage with right hands, boots and some damned nasty clotheslines. But, of course, every single damned time he actually hit a good move, he didn't follow up. Oh, no. He had to dance for his "fans". *gag* At length, he nailed Savage with a backbreaker and went up top. After a bit of dancing, he made it abundantly clear *he* was going to nail *Savage* with a flying elbow drop. For God's sake, he even yelled at Savage to get ready for it! Savage did annnnd DI missed by a mile when Savage rolled out of the way. DI made it to his feet *just* in time to get leveled with a running clothesline - and another - and another. DI groggily pulled referee Earl Hebner into the corner to complain, thus allowing a recovered "Dancin'" Steve Richards to enter the ring and blast Savage in the head with a Steviekick! DI let Hebner go and went for the cover, at which point Paul E. angrily yanked Savage's foot onto the ropes. *That* Hebner saw - and immediately called for a DQ!!!!! (match time: 19:54) An irate Taz rushed the ring and T-Bone Tazplexed not only Hebner, but Richards AND Disco Inferno. Savage dropped The Big Elbow on all three as well. Yeesh. Guess it doesn't pay to mess with The Dangerous Alliance, eh, Stevie? -------------------------------------------------------------------------- *GRUDGE WEAPONS FILLED CAGE MATCH* *FOR THE DEWF WORLD'S LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE* Chris Candido (w/ Sister Sherri) vs. "The Shadow" Michael Monroe (w/ Black Sarah) *challenger* *champion* Candido relcutantly got into the cage and was held at bay by referees until "The Shadow" sprinted into the ring to the strains of "Just One Fix" by Ministry. This was ugly. Gruesome. Carnage, was what it was. Monroe battered Candido into a bloody pulp with just about everything you could think of. Bats, trash cans, boards, bottles... you name it. Candido got in a few shots as well, but nothing much. The end came when Monroe locked Candido into his "Shadowplay" maneuver (modified Romero chinlock). It looked every inch like he fully intended to take Candido's head home on a silver platter. He might well have - except for the fact that, from under the ring, came Candido's stablemates, The Heavenly Bodies!!! The referee could do absolutely nothing about it as this was a no DQ match! Monroe fought bravely, but was no match for a two on one from the Bodies. The Bodies nailed Monroe with a spike piledriver onto a trash can, then helped Candido out the door, making a still unconscious Candido *the winner*!!!!! (match time: 15:51) Announcer: Your winner, and NNNNNNEEEEEEWWWWW DEWF World's Light Heavyweight Championnnn, Chris Candiiiiiidddddooooo! The Bodies started up the aisle with the groggy Candido between them, belt firmly around his waste - when they were chased *back into the cage* by The Crow and "The Ivory Tower" Justin Cross!!!! Black Sarah locked the cage door herself as The Crow, Cross and a *livid* Monroe just beat all living hell out of Candido and the Bodies. Candio, Prichard and Del Rey got locked into simultaneous "Shadowplay"s by The Crow and The Fear Machine!!!! After an excruciatingly long time, officials managed to get the cage raised. The Crow, The Fear Machine and Black Sarah left (relatively speaking, anyway) peaceably after Philadelphia's Finest were able to pry Monroe off Candido. You see, "The Shadow" was busily engaged in trying to shove the belt down Candido's throat to the delirious cheers of the crowd. :) (A six-man match was later signed that pitted The Crow and The Fear Machine against Candido and The Heavenly Bodies - with an interesting stipulation. If Candido gets pinned by *any* member of the opposing team, he loses the World Light Heavyweight Title back to Monroe) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF Intercontinental Title Match* "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel (w/ Paul Ellering) *challenger* vs. Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw (w/ Jerry "The King" Lawler) *champion* This match never even really got started. Diesel was on his way down to ringside when he got blinsided with that cowbell by Bradshaw. When the Referee Brigade got involved, Jerry Lawler personally un-involved them via a punch in the mouth each. Ellering himself was on the receiving end of a piledriver from Lawler - which got Lawler's sh*t kicked back to the locker rooms by The Road Warriors! Referee John Finnegan had no choice but to declare this match a no contest, much to the displeasure of the crowd. (Speaking OF The Road Warriors...) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ***DEWF World's Tag Team Title Match*** The Gangstaz (New Jack and Mustafa Saed) *challengers* vs. The Road Warriors (Animal and Hawk) *champions* (Paul Ellering was understandably not at ringside for this match) No sooner had the last mess been cleared up than *this* one got started. It *could* *not* *be* *ended* - not by DQ, not by a time limit draw, not by the referee stopping it for too much blood, not by any way at all except for pinfall or submission. The Gangstaz did what they did best, they threw themselves a Foreign Object-O-Rama. The Warriors did what *they* did best, use sheer power and brute force to pound their opponents into the ground. That the Warriors did. As hard as the Gangstaz fought, the Warriors fought harder. Oh, there was much blood, from both teams. But the Warriors would just not be denied. The end came when Animal reversed a Mustafa Saed Irish Whip into the ropes and *powerslammed* Saed onto a trash can!!! Say goodnight, Mustafa. (match time: 14:58) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ***SOUTH PHILLY STREET FIGHT*** Vader (w/o Paul Ellering) vs. "Da Ruffneck" Mister Hughes (w/ Sister Sherri) This match could end anywhere in the building, the participants could *use* anything they wanted, and in general, chaos ruled. Ain't life grand? :) Hughes and Vader were like two masto.. were like two really HUGE men *would* be, going at it in a match like this. It was Yet Another Match full of pure carnage. Of course, the DEWF Mutants were deliriously happy at the prospect. I don't think I need to describe all the weapons that were used, you can pretty well guess, most likely. I will tell you that for sheer, unadulterated brutality, this match could not even come close to being equalled. The end came when *Shane Douglas* tried to stop Hughes from hitting Vader in the back of the head with a shovel while Vader lay facedown on the second floor! Shane was no friend of Vader's, but this would have nearly *killed* Vader! Needless to say, Hughes didn't take kindly to Douglas' interference and punched him right in the mouth - which got Hughes DDT'd!!!! Douglas brought both the crowd *and* Vader to it's collective feet by helping the big man up. Douglas hauled *Hughes* to his feet and kicked him in the - he kicked him low. Douglas *bellowed* at Vader to power bomb Hughes! Vader did Shane, and the crowd, one better by not only power bombing Hughes, but Power Bombing him *off the second floor* to the stage below. Owie-owie-owie! Douglas shook Vader's hand and left the DEWF Arena for good as the crowd was in ecstasy! (match time: 26:00) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Styles: OH MY GOD!!! OH MY LIVING GOD!!! As you can see, "Da Ruffneck" is being stretchered out of here by Damage Control... wow. Anyway, we will be back next Thursday Thunder with the DEWF debuts of The Mutilator and The Head Hunters! We were supposed to bring you those debuts next week, but due to technical difficulties (Author's Note: I forgot to run the matches. :)) we couldn't. Until then, I'm Joey Styles! (fade to black) "We're fat, 40, and back!" - John "Johnny Rotten" Lydon of The Sex Pistols "I've seen many things in heaven, I've seen many things in hell. But his vultures eye of a cold, pale blue is the eye of The Devil himself..." Arthur Brown, "The Telltale Heart" from The Alan Parson Project album "Tales Of Mystery And Imagination"
