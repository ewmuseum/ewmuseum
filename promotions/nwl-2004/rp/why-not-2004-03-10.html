stuclif — 10 March 2004 FADE IN: PULSAR IS SITTING ON A COUCH IN A MOTEL LONGE ROOM. A PHONE IS SITTING BESIDE HIM, PULSAR IS TAPPING HIS FINGERS NEXT TO IT.
PULSAR: Dont tell me that noone heard the offer that went around backstage... an offer to get onto the card at the third Combat. An offer for more exposure, to get into the peoples houses and into their heads.
(PULSAR SHAKES HIS AHEAD) PULSAR: Well, if you didnt hear the offer when it went around quietly, I'm going to make it public, so if it is not accepted this time round, there will be a few so-called "superstars" backstage looking like cowards. Its not hard, all you have to do is (PICKS UP THE PHONE) pick up one of these, they're easy to use you know, call up Mr. Prez Medina and say you want to fight Pulsar at Combat... theeen, He'll call me and we'll be in business. There is no reason why anyone out there who doesnt already have a match wnat to refuse, afterall thats what we're here for. Also, you'll have the best seat in the house when I...... cue the excitement.
