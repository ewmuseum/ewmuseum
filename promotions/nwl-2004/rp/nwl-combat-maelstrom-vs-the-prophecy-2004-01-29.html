JLebron — 29 January 2004 OORP: Actually, what happens in the match itself is written by the cardwriter Andrew. What you want to do is cut a more detailed promo spot for your character (James Kanyon) where he speaks in front of the camera about his views, feelings or whatever regarding his matchup against his opponent (in this case Maelstrom). The spot itself is meant to showcase your wrestler via having a dialogue with him afterwhich you'd wait for your opponent to respond, then you'd respond and so forth. It's almost like a debate. What you CAN'T do is rp for your opponents character or have him live in your rps without your opponents and/or Medina's permission. (you can use excerpts from your opponents previous mic spots as reference if you like, pretty much like I did with yours earlier) You can look in the RP Archives Folder and read through this match folder to get a better idea by clicking on the link below......
