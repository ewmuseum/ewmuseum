NWL Combat: Dakota Smith vs. The Watcher

3 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AUMedina — 3 April 2004 All RPs for this match goes in this thread. Good luck and have fun. JC — 5 April 2004 Into the belly of the beast... (Fade in to… A desolate South Bronx street, several abandoned cars litter the garbage strewn street as dealers ply their trade… Lookouts in every corner keeping a close watch on approaching cars and people… On the rooftops more watchers, watching those who watch…)
“Walking through the streets of the South Bronx has always given me an uneasy feeling, yet here I am once again in hot pursuit of none other than Dakota Smith… A man who has been on a tear as of late; obsessed with becoming the king of the NWL jungle… Young, strong, talented and most importantly to many out there, a very dangerous man… But what makes him dangerous is not the fact that he is a second generation wrestler, no sir; not even the fact that he has trained under the tutelage of his father Nevada, the NEH and even the UA, and has mastered all their moves, every single hold and has combined them with his own techniques to create a vast arsenal and unique style… Not any of those aforementioned facts are what makes him dangerous… His unpredictability and prowess combined with his ruthlessness are part of that equation; and believe me; he didn’t fall far from the tree… Though he and Nevada are constantly at odds, they are very similar, different yet the same; cut from the same cloth, there is 6 degrees of separation… Only it appears that Dakota has elevated to newer and higher levels than his dad; his actions speak for themselves… In a few moments we will once again hear from Dakota, and like I have said before, he is very unpredictable; some would even say that he is bipolar and temperamental as well”…
(Suddenly and without warning our video feed goes dead and we are left with only audio…) “In case you’re wondering why your screen went black, wonder no more, we have somehow lost our video feed but have been able to maintain audio… So let me cut to the chase and without any further ado… DAKOTA”!!!”)
RS: Dakota, let’s cut to the chase… Your next opponent is none other than the Watcher, the number one contender for the NWL gold, while you went down from third to fourth in the rankings… And here you are, about to take on the man who knocked your uncle Maelstrom from the number one spot… You have your job cut out for you this time; the Watcher is by no means a pushover…
DAKOTA: So now Medina and company has place yet another obstacle in my path… The man who goes by the moniker of the “Watcher”… A man who at present is a heavy odds favorite… A man in a club that very, very few belong to… The “I kicked Maelstrom’s ass”club… (Clapping is heard…) Kudos to you Watcher; you have accomplished what so many have failed to do and that’s to hand a loss to uncle Mael… Very impressive, but so was Karl “the Dragon” Brown, he too was very impressive… A very tough opponent, the heavy odds favorite to win our match; and for part of the match he was… BUT!!! Something happened mid stream Watcher; all it took was that split second it takes to blink and just as fast it was over and his very impressive record was not so impressive any longer… But I am sure that you’re thinking differently, I am sure you see things from a much more different perspective and, HELL, you beat uncle Mael and it all means… NOTHING!!! I hope that you’re listening very closely Watcher, I purposely took away the video… I don’t want you to watch tonight Mr. Watcher I want you to just listen!!! I can almost imagine what is going through that mind of yours and personally Watcher, I really don’t give a damn about your opinions, or this mystic aura you have about you, they don’t even break me into a light sweat… But before I continue Watcher, I want you to understand perfectly that I in any way; shape or form is taking you lightly, I am fully aware of your talents, your skills in that ring, and especially of your gift of gab… But they all mean nothing to me Watcher; all that means anything to me is that you have been thrown in front of my path, so I will have to deal with you… Yes Watcher, I am prepared to take whatever you dish out, as I am sure that you are also; but you will be forced to blink and that’s when I will take you down Watcher; so let the beast inside you escape and go rampant for once, let it Watcher… I respect your prowess within the confines of the squared circle, I respect your knowledge as well as your skills, but Watcher, none of that means anything to me, inside that ring, it’s survival of the fittest and only the strong will emerge and I INTEND on being that one… But you see it differently, I know you do, you feel it deep within your soul, but so do I Watcher… What you feel, I also I feel at times, I too have an encaged beast lurking within Watcher, wanting to escape, I too control and use him to my advantage; I too know suffering and pain… But unlike you I am a snake in the shadows of eagles; all my life I have been forced to walk behind greatness, behind the Nevadas, behind the Maelstroms, behind the NEH/UA…
(A siren can be heard as it appears that Dakota has paused or finished talking, several moments pass before we once again hear silence and it is then that we hear Dakota speak again…)
DAKOTA: That’s all in the past, I am carving my own path to greatness and at the moment, you are the one standing in my way and believe me when I say that I will act with extreme prejudice when we meet… Nothing we say here really matters, it’s all hype to stir the masses and it’s not really that important… But what is important is action; and within a fortnight we both will have that opportunity… It will be then that we will really know who has been paying close attention…
(Again we hear sirens, at first very close and within seconds it trails off… Suddenly our video feed is live again and we see Rico now staring at a departing Dakota… He watches until Dakota fades and blends into the darkness, he then turns and faces the camera…)
RS: What a mess!!! Folks I can now explain why there wasn’t any video; it was the only way Dakota would grant me another interview if you want to call it that… Dakota does in these interviews much like he does in the ring; whatever he damn pleases… He evades my questions and just rambles on about what, I really don’t know, but something is eating at him and he’s out to prove a point… Watcher, do not fall asleep, do not even blink for if you do, Dakota will be all over you like white on rice… I know that you are a top notch contender, just don’t blink Watcher… And most important; be prepared to fight not a human, but a merciless and unforgiving beast… Well time is running out as you can see and I most definitely DO NOT want to be out here more than I need to… So as always, the pleasure has been all YOURS!!! Good night folks, good night Mr. Watcher… Adios…
(Camera slowly begins to fade; slowly we fade to black…) JC — 10 April 2004 Redemption..... (Fade in on Dakota as he sits on top of his car’s hood, a 1998 black Trans Am, Latin music can be heard coming out of its stereo… As we zoom in closer, we see his eyes; narrowed and staring into the night sky… It is a clear night, no clouds just stars, peaceful and tranquil… Dakota also appears to be at peace with himself; tranquil and lost in thought… Several more minutes pass before Rico, interrupts and invades his space… Dakota ignores him, prompting Rico to wait a bit before trying to get his attention…)
DAKOTA: Back off Rico, I heard you the first time… What do you want now!? RS: Just thought I could get some comments from you, you know about your upcoming match with the Watcher….
DAKOTA: Why don’t you go and see if you can find the Watcher and get some comments from him!?
RS: Because I want to get them from you, besides I’m thinking about writing a book about wrestling and the Pandorians will be my first and biggest chapter… From Lord Dread and his group, to JC and his NEH/UA, your father Nevada and Maelstrom, even Brimstone and of course YOU!!!
DAKOTA: So if that’s the case, why don’t you start with them and leave me the f(bleep)k alone!!!
RS: Cut me some slack Dakota, I’m just trying to make a living; just like you and everyone else…
DAKOTA: Yea, sure, okay… Whatever dude… RS: Why are you so hostile!? My articles and shows are global, I can make you famous, a household name!!! Look what I have… (Dakota cuts him off)
DAKOTA: Spare me the BS Rico… I don’t need yours or anyone else’s help!!! However, since you said you needed to make a living, I’ll help YOU instead…
RS: Okay, sounds fair… Your next opponent, the Watcher has yet to be seen or heard from, can it be that this is part of his strategy!?
DAKOTA: (Smirks and stares into the night sky again and then at Rico…) Whatever his strategy is, it isn’t working…. But hey!!! Whatever turns you on Watcher, if by your silence, I am supposed to take that in your eyes I am not even worth mentioning, so be it amigo… You want to take me THAT lightly, go ahead it’s your funeral… It’s not going to change anything Watcher; I still feel the same way I did since day one… I still consider you top dog for now, hell you beat my uncle, something I have yet to accomplish… And with him being on a tear as of late, I was sure hoping that I would be the first to bust that cherry in the NWL… So now, on account of you taking that away from me, you have given me no other option than to beat you down Watcher… Now I know that I am in for one hell of a fight, you’re on top and plan to stay there and I am hell bent on getting to the top…
(Slowly slides off the hood and begins his usual pace, slowly and methodically pacing around his car, tapping it as he paces until he comes full circle…)
DAKOTA: I am not taking anything away from you Watcher, you have it all… For now Watcher, but all that is about to change amigo… You want to play mystery phantom games, play them with someone else… Hey, I can understand that you hate these promos and interviews just as much as I do, and you have proven that you are the best, for the moment that is… So why should you have to even say anything to these stupid peons, why should you the Watcher, have to lower yourself to even say as much as a whimper… You do all your talking inside the ring, which is great, that’s what I like also!!! But if I have to do these stupid promos and interviews, so do YOU!!! You’re no different amigo; regardless of what your contract says and regardless of what Medina has told you, if he says that I have to do them, so do you!!! Bottom line is that you now have to pay for taking me being the first to defeat Maelstrom… and now your total lack of disrespect by making me waste my time having to meet with this piece of crap and FORCED to make the peons happy by making a fool out of myself here, has just pissed me off even more Watcher… Townsend learned the hard way, so did Minion, too little too late did they realize that the silent treatment doesn’t cut any ice with me… It’s not going to give you any edge over me Watcher, neither is the fact that you beat the only man that has been able to beat me going to make any difference… I went easy on him… The rest have been paying the price and so will you…
(Without saying another word, he pushes Rico aside and gets into his car, Rico tries to ask him something and almost gets his head decapitated as Dakota closes his window and drives off…)
RS: THAT MOTHERF(bleep)KER!!! WHO THE HELL DOES HE THINKS HE IS!? I HAVE A GOOD MIND OF PRESSING CHARGES ON HIS ASS!!! SON OF A BIT…
(At this point Rico is cut off by one of the crewmembers who obviously reminded him that they were still live… He composes himself with the help of the crewmember…)
RS: I want to apologize for that outburst ladies and gentlemen, that is not my style… Dakota as of late has been bringing out the worst in me… Here I am playing the devil's advocate and almost get decapitated for my troubles… Folks again, I apologize for my unprofessional behavior… And as always… The pleasure has been all YOURS!!! Good night folks!!!
(Camera fades to black, but not before showing a replay of Rico almost getting decapitated… It then slowly fades to black…)
