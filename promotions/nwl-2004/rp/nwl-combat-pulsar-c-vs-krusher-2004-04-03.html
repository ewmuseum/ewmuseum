NWL Combat: Pulsar(c) vs. Krusher

3 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AUMedina — 3 April 2004 All RPs for this match goes in this thread. Good luck and have fun. stuclif — 5 April 2004 Whats mine. FADE IN: IN THE GYM CHANGEROOM, OBVIOUSLY AFTER A WORKOUT. THE INTERNATIONAL TITLE HANGING OFF A LOCKER DOOR TO HIS RIGHT.
PERSONAL CAMERAMAN (PC): The people are wanting to know if youve recovered enough from your cage match with the Distributor of Pain?
PULSAR: Its a struggle at the moment, but I've still got a few days up my sleeve, and by then I'll be where I was before the last episode Combat... fit, keen, excited, nervous, focused.. except now with a chunk of gold around my waist, the International title.
PC: What are your thoughts about the comments people have been making about you not winning the belt fairly, DoP was distracted by Magellan and Finnegan and may have helped you win the match.
PULSAR: Have you ever been inside a steel cage? (CAMERA SHAKES FOR NO) PULSAR: We were in a steel cage, everything is fair when your surrounded by steel... the rule book is thrown. You sweat, you bleed, you push your body to the limit, and the man who is willing to push the hardest is rewarded. DoP let himself get distracted, he lost his focus and he missed out.
PC: Speaking of missing out, your opponent for your first title defence is Krusher, who had to miss the last episode of Combat.
PULSAR: Yeah, that struck me as being strange, I guess that makes him either a decent guy or real stupid. You have a commitment, and you honor that commitment, that makes you a good guy. I sure as hell hope that it doesnt mean any disrespect towards this belt (TAPS THE BELT HAGING BESIDE HIM). But surely, an important event, such as a title shot, would make for the perfect excuse to forgo a commitment or at least postpone it... I guess it wouldnt of made much difference, that belt was going to be mine no matter who was in that cage. Just like its not going to matter whether or not Krusher turns up to Combat or not, the result is going to the same, I walk into Combat the International Champion and walk out the International Champion.
PC: OK man, that was great, I'll see..... PULSAR: Whoa, I'm not finished, get here. (PULSAR TWO FINGERS TOWARDS HIS EYES, CAMERA ZOOMS IN CLOSER TO PULSARS FACE, WHICH IS DEAD SERIOUS)
PULSAR: Krusher, you want to take what I've got, what I've earnt. All you have to do is turn up and fight, get a 3 count and the belts yours.... simple..... isnt it?? (SLY GRIN) Cue the excitement. Now we're finished
