NWL Combat: Hacker vs. Frost

17 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AUMedina — 17 February 2004 Okay lady and gentleman, all RP for this match goes in here. Have fun and good luck. KGordon — 18 February 2004 A few hours after losing a chance at the NWL World Title, Hacker is pissed. He was so close but fell short of the goal. Now a few days later, we see the set of On Air with Ryan Seacrest, also the host of American Idol. The fans are going nuts as they just came back from break and Ryan is smiling.
Seacrest- My first guests are the first couple of NWL. I tell you one thing, I wouldn't want to mess with these two, not by a long, please welcome Hacker and Data.
Hacker and Data walk out hand in hand. Hacker is dressed in a white shirt and black jeans, Data is in a blue sweater and a black pair of dress pants. They both wave to the crowd and take a seat.
Seacrest- How are you guys doing? Data- Great. Hacker- Couldn't be better. Seacrest- I don't understand to much about the wrestling business but it seems to be going strong.
Hacker- Yeah it's starting to come back and I am just honored to be apart of it. Seacrest- How long have you two been together? Data- We've been going out for about 2 or 3 years. Seacrest- Is the wrestling business hard on relationships? Hacker- It can be. We travel together though so we spend lots of time together. Seacrest- Rumor has it Data that you are singer and that you have a band? Data- That is true, my band is called Amazed. Actually I was going to try out for American Idol last year but didn't want to face Simon.
Seacrest- *laughs* Good decision. *looks at his card* We usually have people email us questions and Gwen from Jersey asks you Hacker about your upcoming match against Frost?
Hacker- Well there isn't much I know about the guy, but I do felt that I was cheated last week. I should have won that match. This week though, it is going to be different. I got the information to destroy this sucker.
Seacrest- *smiles* Now you know why I stay out of the wrestling ring. Data- This is going to be interesting match and Frost is going to be very defrosted after Hacker gets done with him.
Hacker- Oh yeah, this match is going to have everything, blood, guts, and lot of pain. Seacrest- Sounds like a Simon and Paula fight. Data- *laughs* This is going to be one heck of a fight. Seacrest- Ok tune in to watch NWL Combat to watch one heck of a fight as Hacker described it and the very sexy Data. Thanks for both stopping by.
Hacker- Thank you Ryan. Seacrest- We will be back with Joan of Archadia Actor Jason Ritter. Camera fades out as the crowd claps as Hacker and Data continue to talk to Ryan.
