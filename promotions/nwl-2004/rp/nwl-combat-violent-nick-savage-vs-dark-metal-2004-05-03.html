NWL Combat: "Violent" Nick Savage vs. Dark Metal

3 May 2004

AUMedina — 3 May 2004 All roleplays for this match must be posted in this thread. Thank you, good luck, and have fun.
PrimeTime — 3 May 2004 The scene appears in the middle of a road somewhere in downtown Bethany, Missouri. From both ways the streets are empty with no sign of life, but the houses that occupie the lots. The wind continues to blow harder as the sky turns darker with each covoring cloud. The sun is almost set as rain begins to fall over the small town as the camera views in on one small house to see a light come on the first floor window. The nice small white house with blue trimming is very pleasant from the outside. The paint on the house is faded and chipped. The grass is perfectly cut as if done by a professionall mowing down a golf course for the big championship tournament.
Inside of the house it is chaos, just plain chaos. The kitchen has stacked up dishes on the counters, the front room has newspapers scattered everywhere, in the upstairs bed-room there is pot scattered out on the table. Downstairs in the dining room sitting at computer is a man built like a body builder. His arms are the size of cannons and his legs are built like an olympic runners. The man has shoulder length black hair with blonde highlights and a rough black full face beard. He is wearing a black t-shirt with a pot leaf on the back and a pair of blue jeans with holes ripped in the kees. The man is listening to music on his computer while smoking a cigarette. The song is defineitly "Enter Sandman" by Matallica no doubt. The man takes a drag of his cigarette and slowly sits it back in the ashtray as he blows 3 smoke rings out of his mouth. The man begins to talk as if the camera weren't even there.
The Man: Since more than half of you more than likely have no single clue just who I am. The man takes another quick drag of his cigarette. The Man: Just listen up, because I am about to tell you. I am the teacher and shool is now in session.
The man turns fully around in his torn black computer chair to let the camera get a full view of him.
The Man: The name is Nick Savage in most places people call me "The Violator". You may wondor why now, but don't worry the National Wrestling League will soon know why they gave me that nickname. During my high shcool years I was built with furious rage and would of been known as your normal class bully. Blah...Blah..Blah....I got in a fight one night against the World Heavyweight Boxing Champion at the time known as Jaxx. It was in an alley way and the man pissed me off. It had to do with pot, but back then I was mixed up with the wrong people. I was one luck bastard that night, because a scout for The Asylum was there and seen me beat the **** out of Jaxx. Serves the silly bastard right.
Nick takes a drag of his cigarette and looks up towards the camera. You will notices that his eyes are blood shot, either from drinking or the smoke that seems to fill the house.
Nick Savage: Anyways, the scout took me over to see The Asylum immediately after. The Asylum is a 16 foot steel dome cage in the shape of an octagon. I wasn't impressed by seeing this at the time and wasn't interested in taking the job until I saw the $$$ that would be involved. At the time I needed the money so I though what the hell. One month later I fought my first match and got DQed for disoberything the rules. I was pissed and ended up beating my opponent up some more after the fight was over in the locker room. Thats when I met the World Champion of The Asylum. His name was Token Weed, I hate to admit it, but he is the only man I know that gives me the damn chills when I look at him. Thankfully he liked the way I took control of my opponent and offored to train me. He trained me to become a World Class Cage Fighter and soon after he retired I became the Asylum World Champion. I was enducted into the Asylum hall of fame three years later after being the longest reigning champion in Asylum history. Two weeks after being enducted I broke my ankle in the middle of a fight. The fight had to be fought to the end and my title was on the line. The man quickly took control of this and put me into an ankle lock. To me it was not only disrespect to put my broken ankle into an ankle lock, but to steal my finishing move. He had never used it before & he did it on purpose, because he knew that was my move. Thirtyeight minutes later after being locked into an ankle lock with a broken ankle I tapped out. Its the only time I have ever tapped out and only time it will ever happen.
Nick puts out whats left of his cigarette and lights another up with his skull lighter. He takes a drag and blows some more smoke into the room as he continues to talk.
Nick Savage: Afterwards people tryed to help me with my ankle, but I refused. It was the dumbest thing I ever done in my life. Not just the fact that I refused medical attention, but I waited so long to tap-out when I knew I had lost the title anyways. This was dumb, because my ankle was in bad shape. There was no way I would be able to fight again. I went through about a year and a half of physical theropy. I came back in full condition, but decided to wrestle instead of fighting. I couldn't walk back into the asylum with the shame of losing my title that way. Its not how I wanted to go out, but **** happens. I intend to go back later, but here I am. I am a wrestler now, its not how I wanted it to be, but its life. I have gotton over it. Now its time to show NWL why they call me The Violator. I am going to shcool every single one of you until I get some gold wrapped around my waist. Starting with you Dark Metal. Come next week you better bring you books to class and prepare for lesson #1, because when that bell rings you better prepare to be schooled!!!
Nick takes a drag off his cigarette then turns around and plays a different song on his computer. This song happens to be his theme song "Wait & Bleed" by Slipknot. Nick begins to feel the music as he starts to tap his foot with the beat. Nick takes another drag off his cigarette as the scene fades to black.
