NWL Combat: Pulsar vs. Ryan "The Graffiti" Cruz

17 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AUMedina — 17 April 2004 All roleplays for this match will be posted in here. Thank you and have fun. stuclif — 19 April 2004 FADE IN: (PULSAR IS SITTING ON A COUCH IN A MOTEL ROOM AS THE PERSONAL CAMERAMAN (PC) ENTERS. PULSAR IS STARRING TOWARDS THE GROUND, NOT TALKING, HIS LEFT HAND, OBVIOUSLY SHAKING, RUBBING HIS MOUTH AND CHIN. FACE AND EYES RED.)
PC: Hey man. I seen what happened at Combat. Are you alright? (NO ANSWER) PC: Pulsar? Man ar.. are ya good? PULSAR: (FINALLY SPEAKS, SLOW, LOW AND WITH A TREMBLE IN HIS VOICE) Magellan.... Finnegan.... both of you have got your arses in alot of peoples scopes..... Thats twice youve got in my face... thats twice too often.......(MOVES HAND AWAY) If either one of you get in my way again, I will f***ing end you!!
(ANOTHER LONG PAUSE. STARTS TALKING AGAIN WITH A CALMER VOICE.) PULSAR: Distributor of Pain..... I know you werent there to help me, but to settle a score. Despite that, the fact is that I was copping a mass beat down, and you basically saved the day.... You still dont like me and I'm still not your biggest fan, but, if you ever need it...... I got your back.
PC: (SOUNDING RELIEVED TO HEAR PULSAR TALKING) Now your coming up against this young Ryan Cruz. No title on the line though.
PULSAR: Cruz... Medina sees something in you. I dont see it but he does and he runs the joint so I wont question it, but I can tell you what I wouldve done in his position.... Let you walk out that door like the sooky little bi*** that you are. Then I would have put your contract straight through the shredder. You cant win a match so you quit... absolutely pathetic, thats why theres no title shot.
And what the hell is up with the cool new attitude. The Graffiti? Man, countries like America, and back home in Australia, provide oppurtunities that more than half the planet can only dream about. Opportunities for people like us to a make a name for ourselves in a business we love... You would blame a country that offers these opportunities for the fact that you suck. Its no wonder you cant win a match, your head is too far up your own arse.
After the last Combat, I'm afraid I'm going through a little bit of an anger bender, and your just gonna make it worse. Prepare for rage.. prepare for pain.. prepare to.. cue the excitement.
stuclif — 22 April 2004 FADE IN: PULSAR IN THE SAME HOTEL ROOM, ON THE SAME LOUNGE, THE ATMOSPERE ALOT MORE COMFORTABLE, "CALM" BY EPICURE PLAYING IN THE BACKGROUND.
PULSAR: Everyone can breathe easy, I've chilled out a bit, been listening to some groovy music and just realxed... the training hasnt gotten any easier and I'm still focused on a guy by the name of Ryan Cruz, but Ive decided that I'll deal with those other two when the time comes... and it will (SMILES).
It seems that after my last appearance my next opponent, The Graffite (SOUNDS SARCASTIC), has become speachless.. A few of the things that I touched must have hit home and he just didnt know what to say. But... there is one guy out there who is doing a bit of yapping and needs to be shut up, how fortunate, cause I'm the man that would love to silence this d*** head. The American has demanded that the NWL hands over my International title (REACHES OVER TO THE TABLE AND LIFTS THE BELT)... and if he doesnt get the belt he belives that I should be handed to him so he can effortlessly take it from me.... Now... I'm not the smartest man in the world, but I know that if you want something around this place, you have to earn it.. and American.. you have earned nothing. I have no problems in defending this strap anywhere, anytime.. cause I know that to take this belt off me, you have to pry it from my cold dead fingers... not an easy task. So to the American, I say to you, stop your bi***ing, enough with the legal s**t... come... just come, simple.
Now Cruz, the bad news for you is that this intensity, this will to survive, to win, doesnt stop with title defences, it extends to every time I step into a wrestling ring... when I first won this belt and when I come face to face with you at Combat. The news isnt all bad, we're not in the headlining the show, so you wont be embarrased in the main event, lucky you. Cue the excitement.
