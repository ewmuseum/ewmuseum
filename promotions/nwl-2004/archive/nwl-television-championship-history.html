recent NWL Combat Ep. 9 Previous NWL Combat Ep. 8 Next NWL Combat Ep. 10 NWL Recap: 5/03/04 NWL Recap: 4/15/04 History NWL Combat Episode 8 Nassau Coliseum Uniondale, New York Jarod Poe was unbeatable ever since capturing the NWL Television Championship. That is of course, when he went head-to-head with The Distributor of Pain, a man who has become a force in the NWL. These two men were fated to meet and what a display did they put on. But in the end, it was DoP who finally unseated Jarod Poe to become the newly crowned NWL Television Champion.
NWL Combat Episode 3 Hammerstein Ballroom New York, New York The stage was set, as ten men entered NWL's first ever Rumble in the Jungle match. Entering as the first participant, Jarod Poe easily eliminated the next four participants in the match. After The American entered, it proved to be a match of endurance, as in the end, it came down to just three men; The American, Jarod Poe, and Shamrock Finnegan.
However, when it was just down to these men, The American left the ring, deciding the Television Title wasn't worth his time. After being attacked by The Distributor of Pain ealier in the match, Shamrock Finnegan valiantly tried his hardest to eliminate Jarod Poe. But it was Jarod Poe who defeated Shamrock Finnegan, becoming the first ever NWL Television Champion
