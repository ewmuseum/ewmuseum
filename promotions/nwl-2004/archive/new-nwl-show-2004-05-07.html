New NWL Show

7 May 2004

AUMedina — 7 May 2004 Alright everyone, Now I've e-mailed you with an idea on how to work this, but here's what I have as far as concrete plans are concerned:
The second show will NOT be a house show, but would be a television show on syndication, much like Combat is in the storylines. Sometime after the PPV, this will probably go into effect, just to play up the story and such.
I haven't thought of any names for the second show, but if any of you have suggestions, please let me know.
This will be a short form show, however, RPs will count. Now my idea is to restrict the amount of RPs each person writes. I'm thinking of a 2 to 3 RP limit for a match would suffice. I'm gearing more towards 2 RPs since that would keep things real interesting.
RP for the show would commence after RP for Combat is over. I'm thinking a 6-7 RP period would suffice.
Also, this show would be more storyline based if anything, to help build and continue storylines and angles that are built from you the member.
Now I'm also thinking that maybe instead of me writing it entirely, that I would have people who were involved in the match write their own match. Of course I would make all necessary edits to keep the show format in check. But I'm still deciding if this is the way to go. I'm more than likely going to write it myself, but participation in the ways of storylines and segments are a must. Participation with such would also help determine title shots.
This may or may not happen, but I'd like it to happen to ease the wait between cards. So if you have any thoughts, feel free to share.
