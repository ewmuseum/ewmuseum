NWL Combat Episode 1, part 5

January 2004

From Elks Lodge in Queens, New York Gonzago: "Now, let's not delay even more as in our next match, we've got the veteran Hacker taking on a wrestler who's in his second year of professional wrestling, Karl 'The Dragon' Brown."
Kincaid: "This kid Brown is truly amazing. I had a chance to see his stuff over at Empire Pro and before that MCW and I was certainly impressed. However, we can't let Hacker off the hook, as he's a seasoned veteran and with his eye candy Data, he just may have the advantage in this match."
Gonzago: "We will see soon enough, as we head to the ring, where Darius Jones is waiting to kick things off!"
(Cut to: Inside the ring, where ring announcer Darius Jones appears once again with microphone in hand.)
Jones: The following contest is scheduled for one fall......and is the first round of the WLS QUALIFYING TOURNAMENT!!!"
(Cue up: "Head For The Barricade" by Limp Bizkit. Out walks a man dressed in red tights with black lightning streaks on them, with a lovely blonde woman by his side, as the fans pop loudly for the two of them. They head towards the ring, with Data waving to the fans. Hacker gets on the apron and holds the ropes down for Data to enter them. As Data enters the ring, the fans start whistling at her.)
Jones: "Introducing first, hailing from The Internet, he stands 6' 2" tall and weighing in at 246 lbs., accompanied to the ring by the beautiful DATA.....here is the scourge of the Internet......HACKER!!!"
Gonzago: "It is always a pleasure to see Hacker in the wrestling ring, as he's usually the crowd favorite."
Kincaid: "Man, would I love to upload into Data's hard drive!" Jones: "And his opponent...." (Cue up: "Rainmaker" by Iron Maiden. The lights go out and just when the music hits over the PA, white and green lights flicker all around the Elks Lodge, in sync with the music. Out walks a man dressed in white and green tights, with matching boots and training jacket, with his wrists taped up. He seems pumped up as he walks to the ring, slapping hands with the fans on the way. He circles the ring once on the outside, before jumping on the apron and vaulting over the top rope. He lands on the second rope, arms aloft, saluting the fans on each side of the ring. He then finally gets settled into the center of the ring, focused on his opponent.)
Jones: "From Nottingham, England, he is 6' tall and weighs 211 lbs, this is.....KARL 'THE DRAGON' BROWN!!!"
Gonzago: "This is an amazing talent that we at NWL are really lucky to have. He has always put on a display in the ring and tonight will be no different."
Kincaid: "I'm definitely excited, as both men will put on a technical treat for everyone here tonight."
(The referee checks both men and signals for the bell to start the match. Both Hacker and Karl Brown circle each other and shake hands in a show of sportsmanship.)
Gonzago: "Both men show a sign of respect to one another and then begin to lock up in a collar-and-elbow tie-up. Hacker turns it into a twisting armbar, as Brown feels his way around. Brown falls back, flips over, cartwheels, and reverses it into an armbar on Hacker. Hacker now feeling his way around, as he cartwheels, rolls on the ground, and hip tosses Brown over to the mat. Brown gets up and Hacker meets him with another hip toss. Brown back to his feet once more, and Hacker awaits with a picture-perfect dropkick. The fans applaud in appreciate for the exchange of moves."
Kincaid: "It looks like Hacker's starting things off right so far in this match." Gonzago: "Both men shake hands once again and lock horns once more. Hacker now has a standing go-behind armlock on Brown, as he grabs hold of Hacker with his free arm, pushes off the mat and flips over him, grabs hold of Hacker in a headlock, spins around him and sends Hacker into the mat with a drop-toe hold. Hacker gets up and runs at Brown, who split jumps over him. Hacker hits the ropes, and on the return gets hit with a beautiful standing dropkick by Brown. Hacker back up and gets sent back down to the mat with an arm drag, who then turns it into a sitting armbar submission. The fans applaud Brown now, as the referee checks on Hacker. Hacker gets to his feet, reverses the armbar, grabs hold of Brown's head, and turns it into a spinning neckbreaker. Hacker goes for the cover....
.....and Brown kicks out after one. Hacker takes hold of Brown now and tosses him over his head with a belly-to-belly suplex. He goes for the cover again.....
....but can't put away The Dragon. Hacker now goes for a DDT, but The Dragon blocks it and reverses it into a snap suplex. Hacker back up to his feet, to which Brown anticipates him, taking hold of Hacker, and tossing him with a brilliant T-Bone suplex. Brown goes for the cover now....
.....and Hacker is able to kick out. Brown brings Hacker up to his feet, and sets him up with a piledriver.....
....and connects it. He covers Hacker once more.... .....but Hacker kicks out right at the two count. Brown now brings Hacker up to his feet and flings in into the corner. He follows right after him and hits a running clothesline in the corner. Brown now climbs the turnbuckle, and sits on the top. He takes hold of Hacker, setting up a Diamond Dust, to which he flips over.....
......and gets caught by the wily vet Hacker, who stops the follow through on the Diamond Dust, reversing it into a devastating reverse DDT! Great move by Hacker."
Kincaid: "The Dragon seemed to go big on that maneuver in the corner, but Hacker with his knowledge of the ring, was able to capitalize on that costly mistake."
Gonzago: "Hacker now grabs Brown from behind, as he hits a German suplex on him with precision.....
......but Hacker holds on, rolls up to his feet and hits another German suplex on Brown....
.....and continues to hold onto Brown, as he rolls back up to his feet once more, going for another German suplex.....
....but Karl Brown flips out of the third German suplex attempt, and with his moment, grabs hold of Hacker and hits an impressive Russian leg sweep. He covers Hacker....
1..... 2..... ....and Hacker kicks out after the two! Karl Brown now grabs Hacker from behind and locks in a Dragon sleeper! The referee checks on Hacker to see if he wants to end the match, but doesn't signal for the bell. Brown wrenches on the neck and arm of Hacker, as he tries to fight it. Hacker looks to be getting a bit groggy, as Karl Brown continues to lock on that hold."
(Data starts pounding on the ring apron, as she gets the fans to start clapping to the beat in synchronization.)
Gonzago: "Data is getting the fans behind Hacker, as he tries to fight the Dragon sleeper applied by The Dragon. Hacker is feeling the fans cheers, as he grabs hold Brown's head with his free hand and....
....hits a jawbreaker on The Dragon to let go of the Dragon sleeper! Both men are down, as the referee checks on them. Hacker makes it up to his feet, as Brown follows suit. They start exchanging lefts and rights, but Brown gets the advantage. He goes for a spinning clothesline....
.....Hacker ducks the attempt, kicks him in the stomach, grabs hold of The Dragon, lifts him up.....
.....and hits a brutal running powerbomb into the cover..... 1..... 2..... .....and The Dragon kicks out! Hacker almost put away this match, as he was able to get out of that Dragon sleeper and turn it into a powerful running powerbomb. Hacker now signals for the end, as he sets up the Virus. He picks up The Dragon, placing him on his shoulders, going for the Spicolli Driver finisher.....
....but The Dragon flips out of it, hits a drop toe hold on Hacker, dives towards his head for a headlock....
....Hacker slips out, picks up The Dragon and hits a swinging neckbreaker, holding on to it, as he goes for a second.....
....and connects! Hacker continues to hold on, as he goes for a third..... ....but The Dragon reverses it, grabs hold of Hacker, locks in the Dragon sleeper yet again.....
....no, Brown lifts Hacker up and..... .....DRAGON'S BITE! Out of that whole exchange, The Dragon was able to hit his Dragon sleeper DDT finisher, the Dragon's Bite! He quickly grabs hold of Hacker's leg and the cover.....
1..... 2..... 3!!! KARL 'THE DRAGON' BROWN MOVES ON TO THE NEXT ROUND!!!" Jones: "Here is your winner, and advancing to the second round of the WRESTLING LEAGUE SERIES......KARL 'THE DRAGON' BROWN!!!"
(Cue up: "Rainmaker" by Iron Maiden as Karl Brown raises his arms in victory. The referee raises his arm and then goes to the corner and climbs the turnbuckle, saluting the fans in celebration.)
Kincaid: "What a truly awesome match we just saw Emanuel! That kid is something else!" Gonzago: "Both men showed great skill in the ring, but Karl Brown managed to secure the win. We're going to see nothing but greatness coming from him."
