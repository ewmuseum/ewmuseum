NWL Combat Episode 4, part 1

March 2004

From Roseland Ballroom in New York, New York (Cue up: ”Out Of Control” by Hoobastank as just the first guitar rift starts, playing in a loop. The letter N starts to come into view from the left side of the screen, as more letters fade in next to it spelling the word “National”. The word fades out and the letter W comes into view from the top right corner of the screen. More letters fade in next to it spelling the word ‘Wrestling’. The word fades out and the letter L comes into view from the bottom of the screen. More letters fade in next to it spelling the world ‘League’. Then, all three words fly off into the distance for a bit as the lyrics start, somewhat disappearing from view. Suddenly, the words fly right back into view and read from top to bottom,
“National Wrestling League”. They then fly forward and out of view. Then the word “Presents…” flies into view for a few seconds and then right out of view.
Just as the song starts to pick up fast and loud, a loud explosion shows on the screen, with the logo for NWL Combat appearing from the fires.)
(Cut to: Theo hitting The Revelation, a reverse jumping piledriver, on an opponent.) (Cut to: The American walking down the ring with flag in hand, pointing at the ring.) (Cut to: Canada's Hero and Elizabeth boasting in the ring.) (Cut to: The Watcher walking down to the ring very enigmatically.) (Cut to: Jarod Poe hits Fading Light, a double-underhook piledriver on his opponent.) (Cut to: Karl "The Dragon" Brown on the turnbuckle, saluting the fans.) (Cut to: Dakota Smith, as he walks down the ring, focused on his opponent inside with an expressionless gaze.) (Cut to: Lars Magellan sending an opponent through a table with the Beach Bomb.) (Cut to: Maelstrom applying Mortal Sin on his opponent.) (Cut to: Another explosion on the screen. Cut to: Inside the Roseland Ballroom, as the seats at ringside and in the upper areas of the ballroom are filled to the max with NWL wrestling fans. Cut to: Inside the ring, where Emanuel Gonzago is standing in the middle of the ring, as the entire place is in an uproar, chanting "NWL! NWL! NWL!". Gonzago is dressed in a light brown Armani suit and tie, with microphone in hand.)
Gonzago: "Welcome everyone to The Roseland Ballroom in New York, New York!" (The fans start cheering loudly.) Gonzago: "Welcome to another edition of live wrestling action!" (The fans get even louder.) Gonzago: "Welcome to......NWL COMBAT!!!" (The fans start another "NWL!" chant throughout the Roseland Ballroom.) Gonzago: "Hola everyone, mi llamo Emanuel Gonzago and...." (Emanuel Gonzago is cut short by the crazed wrestling fans, as they start chanting "MANNY! MANNY! MANNY!". Emanuel nods and waves to the fans in thanks.) Gonzago: "Thank you. Tonight, in this ring, in the main event, the Gridiron Gauntlet match that was scheduled to happen.....WILL NOT happen!"
(The fans start booing loudly as Gonzago nods his head in disapprovement.) Gonzago: "Canada's Hero recently left the NWL and Krusher is on a tour of Japan, a committment he had before he came to the NWL."
(The fans start booing even louder.) Gonzago: "But fear not mi amigos, instead of the planned Gridiron Gauntlet match, tonight you will see The Distributor of Pain and Pulsar, ONE ON ONE, inside a STEEL CAGE FOR THE
NWL INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP!!!" (The fans start cheering loudly and chant "NWL!" once again.) Gonzago: "Of course that isn't all. We also have two more matches in the Wrestling League Series as
Dakota Smith, who has been on a tear here in the NWL, taking on the best, pound-for-pound, rookie sensation in our sport today, Karl 'The Dragon'
Brown!" (The fans start cheering loudly.) Gonzago: "And in the second of two WLS matches, the huge Pandorian, who most say were upsetted by
The Watcher on this show last time, Maelstrom, is set to keep his focus as he takes on....
(Cue up: "California Love" by Dr. Dre and Tupac. Out walks Lars Magellan as the fans really lay into his with a chorus of boos. He makes his way down to the ring. Magellan enters the ring as Gonzago looks a bit surprised. The fans continue to boo
Magellan, who seems unfazed by their reaction.)
Gonzago: "What a pleasent surprise Lars Magellan. I was just about to mention your....." (Lars Magellan snatches the microphone from Emanuel Gonzago's hand, as the fans boo him even more. He looks around, eyeing the crowd, before he starts to speak.)
Magellan: "Before I get down to buisness, I have a message for you from a friend of mine
Gonzago..." Gonzago: "W-What is it Lars?" (Lars Magellan raises the microphone to his mouth, about to speak, when he smashes Emanuel Gonzago in the face with the microphone, as the fans really get upset.)
Kincaid: "What in the HELL IS GOING ON?! Lars Magellan just punked Manny out! Magellan is now grabbing hold of Emanuel and lifts him up to his feet. He's dusting his suit off? It seems like he was just.......MY GOD! BEACH BOMB! BEACH BOMB ON EMANUEL
GONZAGO! WHAT IN GOD'S NAME DID LARS MAGELLAN DO SUCH A THING TO AN INNOCENT
MAN!?!?!?" (Lars Magellan dusts himself off and grabs the microphone, as he grins from ear to ear.) Magellan: "Gonzago, Gonzago, Gonzago! I'm sorry to interrupt you, but as I was sitting in the back, I just couldnt stand hearing you rattle on so I had to come down and do something about it....and THAT BEATING right there came compliments from a good friend of mine... who you will find out soon enough!"
(The fans really lay into Lars Magellan, chanting "LARS SUCKS!") Magellan: "Now at the last Combat, I TOLD YALL I had a HUGE suprise! I told that Irish IDIOT
Shammy Finnegan that I was gonna set him straight! And dont worry yall... Lars keeps his word! Because tonight I am gonna put the NWL in its place, and very soon, the very foundation of the NWL will be shaken beyond repair! So everybody better prepare! Maelstrom YOU BETTER PREPARE, because tonight, not only am I gonna shock the NWL... IM GONNA SHOCK YOU! And than everybody will realize why I, LARS MAGELLAN, am the BEST DAMNED WRESTLER IN THE WORRRRRRLD!
YEAH!" (Cue up: "California Love" by Dr. Dre and Tupac as Lars Magellan throws down the microphone and raises his hands to the disapproval of the fans in Roseland Ballroom. He looks down at Emanuel Gonzago, still laying unconscious in the ring, as he shakes his head. As Magellan leaves the ring, EMTs rush over to Emanuel and help him on a stretcher.)
