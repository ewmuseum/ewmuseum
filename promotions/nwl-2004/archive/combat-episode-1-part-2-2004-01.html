NWL Combat Episode 1, part 2

January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
recent NWL Combat Ep. 5 Previous NWL Combat Ep. 4 Next NWL Combat Ep. 6 Episode 1 LIVE! From Elks Lodge in Queens, New York (Cue up: "Right Thurr" by Chingy. The lights go out as the music starts playing, to which everyone seems a bit confused. As the lights come back on, out walks D-Dawg, all thugged out as he starts taunting the fans. He gets some heat as D-Dawg makes his way into the ring. D-Dawg climbs the turnbuckle and taunts the fans again, making a couple gestures with his hands. Emanuel Gonzago stands in the ring, not too happy as he watches D-Dawg continue to prance around the ring.)
Gonzago: "And apparently fans, here is one of the new faces to grace our presence....D-Dawg...."
(D-Dawg approaches Gonzago and slings an arm over his shoulder.) D-Dawg: "Yo, what up essa? Dis be D-Dawg in the mutha-fuggin hizouse!" Gonzago: "Yes D-Dawg, we know who you are." (D-Dawg snatches the microphone from Gonzago's hand, as the fans really lay into him with boos. Gonzago exits the ring in disgust, as he starts dissing on the fans at ringside.)
D-Dawg: "I know you bitches came out to see the true...." (Cue up: "Feel The Break" by Dry Kill Logic. The lights go out as the fans pop, apparently shutting D-Dawg up. A green light fills the Elks Lodge and out walks a big individual wearing black slacks and no shirt on. The fans give a mixed reaction, as the Distributor of Pain enters the ring. D-Dawg starts yelling at DoP, to which he takes him down with a running big boot. The bell rings as the fans start popping loudly.)
Kincaid: "And it's on like Donkey Kong!" Gonzago: "It most certainly is Roger, as the Distributor of Pain gives D-Dawg a much needed face full of boot. DoP now picks D-Dawg up to his feet and whips him to the ropes. On the return, DoP almost literally BEHEADS D-Dawg with a ruthless running clothesline!"
Kincaid: "I'm really liking this Distributor of Pain Emanuel. He's just a straight-up, no bullshit kind of guy."
Gonzago: "The Distributor of Pain brings D-Dawg up to his feet once again and hits a powerbomb with pin....
1.... 2..... ....but D-Dawg kicks out! So far DoP has the momentum in his favor, as he almost ended this match post haste. DoP now grabs hold of D-Dawg's arm and pulls him towards him....
.....but D-Dawg ducks underneath! DoP turns around and gets a dropkick to the chin for his troubles. The Distributor of Pain back up again, but down to the mat once more with a lethal dropkick by D-Dawg. One more time DoP gets to his feet, but this time D-Dawg changes it up, as he jumps on him and hits a Hurricanrana with cover.....
.....but DoP kicks out! D-Dawg now gets to a more technical base, as he tries to keep DoP grounded with a standing leg lock."
Kincaid: "By grounding the big man, D-Dawg basically takes away his power and mobility, giving him more of an advantage with his speed."
Gonzago: "Right you are, as the referee is checking on DoP. D-Dawg applies more pressure, keeping hold of it....
....but DoP just kicks off D-Dawg with relative ease! Both men are back to their feet, as D-Dawg runs right at the Distributor of Pain. DoP readies a standing lariat, but has it ducked by D-Dawg, who quickly turns and dropkicks the knee out from under him! Brilliant move by D-Dawg, as he ascends the top turnbuckle and....
.....hits an awesome top rope leg drop. The cover.... 1.... 2..... ......NO! The Distributor of Pain kicks out at the last second, as D-Dawg starts yelling on the referee, telling him he made the three count. Frustrated, D-Dawg puts the boots to the face of DoP. He now grabs hold of both of DoP's legs, setting up for a Boston Crab. He's trying to get the massive Distributor of Pain over.....
....and DoP overpowers D-Dawg out of the Boston Crab! DoP quickly leaps up to his feet and starts pounding the back of D-Dawg with massive axe handles! Irish whip sends D-Dawg across the ring. On the rebound, and DoP hits a massive running clotheslines, almost DECAPITATING D-Dawg!"
Kincaid: "This Distributor of Pain is really an awesome sight!" Gonzago: "DoP has D-Dawg in his grasp now, as he flings him high and over in the air with a head-and-arm suplex! The Distributor of Pain is signaling to the crowd that it is the end. He's....
.....leaving the ring?" Kincaid: "I don't think he's leaving the match Emanuel. I think he's going to get himself a little extra insurance."
Gonzago: "The Distributor of Pain just reached from under the ring and pulled out a wooden table."
Kincaid: "I don't believe he's intending to hold a poker game." Gonzago: "Nevertheless DoP is heading back into the ring and.... (The fans go in a collective "Ooooooo" as D-Dawg baseball slides the table into DoP's head.)
Gonzago: "MI DIOS! The Distributor of Pain is now receiving his share of pain as D-Dawg brilliantly made a move that could have saved him in this match! D-Dawg now goes to the outside and starts pounding on DoP. He throws DoP back into the ring....
....and he's bringing the table inside himself! D-Dawg is looking to use the table on DoP!"
Kincaid: "It looks like that table could prove to be the fatal mistake for DoP in this match."
Gonzago: "D-Dawg picks DoP up and places him on the set table. He start taunting the fans and pointing upward, signaling the end. D-Dawg climbs the top turnbuckle, but he's still fighting with the fans at ringside. D-Dawg turns around and goes for the Diggity Dawg Frogsplash....
....and the Distributor of Pain catches him in mid-air and..... (The fans start chanting "HOLY SHIT!" as DoP turns the mid-air catch of D-Dawg into a huge spinebuster through the wooden table.)
Gonzago: "OH MI DIOS!!! THE DISTRIBUTOR OF PAIN JUST IMPALED D-DAWG WITH HIS DEVIL'S CROSS FINISHER!!! The fans are eating this up as DoP goes for the cover.....
1..... 2..... 3!!! The Distributor of Pain is the first wrestler to advance to the second round!" Jones: "Here is your winner, and advancing to the second round of the WRESTLING LEAGUE SERIES......THE DISTRIBUTOR OF PAIN!!!"
(Cue up: "Feel The Break" by Dry Kill Logic. The referee raises DoP's hand in victory as the fans start cheering him on. Cut to: Emanuel Gonzago and Roger Kincaid sitting at a table in the Crow's Nest.)
Kincaid: "What an intense opening match Emanuel! These new wrestlers are already making the NWL exciting!"
