JLebron — 30 April 2004 Howdy....... I've taken the liberty of creating a basic webpage that will answer any status related questions regarding the creation of your posers. It will be updated on a daily basis. As for ETA-time related questions, there is really no way of gauging this as this isn't a nine to fiver for me (though my wife may tend to disagree with that ), so please, do not ask about an ETA, I will let you know when/if I have a start/completion date in mind.
The requests for posers are growing every day (some of which are from outside the NWL) and while I will gladly agree to do them (NWL Prioritized of course), I need a little patience and understanding exercised here. I'm not doing the posers in any particular order right now as I tend to work on at least two posers at a time. I'm basically utilizing a "designer's discretion" as some poser submissions are less demanding in their designs as opposed to others which may require things I do not yet have or is currently a bit out of the scope of my expertise. However, like I stated earlier, Priority WILL BE given to members of the NWL FIRST.
Below you will find a link to the Poser Update Center. The listing is in alphabetical order by the wrestler's last name and does NOT reflect the order I'm working in.
http://fwrestling.com/host/Powerdots/PoserUpdate.html -Joe
