NWL Combat Episode 1, part 7

January 2004

From Elks Lodge in Queens, New York Gonzago: "Nevertheless wrestling fans, let's continue on with the card, as coming up next, another new face to the wrestling world and NWL as Edy 'The Lion' Feria, the pride of Mexico City, gets set to debut against a man who is coming into tonight with a defeat over Theo from our preview show, The Watcher."
Kincaid: "This Watcher fellow freaks the hell out of me! We don't know where he comes, where he's going, or what his deal is. He really needs to establish an identity in order to make it in the NWL."
Gonzago: "He already has Roger, as The Watcher made an impressive debut last week against Theo, to which he showed a lot of innovative wrestling moves and skill. I just hope The Lion is ready for the enigmatic Watcher. We now take it to Darius Jones, who awaits with the introductions!"
(Cut to: Inside the ring, where ring announcer Darius Jones stands with mic in hand.) Jones: The following contest is scheduled for one fall......and is the first round of the WLS QUALIFYING TOURNAMENT!!!"
(Cue up: "Where Da Party At" by Jagged Edge. A Hispanic looking man walks out from the back, wearing white shorts with blue flames on them as the fans applaud his enthusiasm. He runs down to the ring and slides underneath the ropes. He then heads over to the corner and climbs it, signaling to the fans, getting a cheap pop.)
Jones: "Introducing first, from Mexico City, Mexico, he is 6' 5" and weighs in at 238 lbs., he is.......EDY 'THE LION' FERIA!!!"
Gonzago: "And Edy Feria looks to be primed and poised for his match tonight." Kincaid: "Maybe a bit too eager for his own good as he has to face someone that is very unpredictable...."
Jones: "And his opponent...." (Cue up:”Aerials” by System of a Down. The lights cut out and a few spotlights shine on the entryway where smoke has gathered. A strobe light slows the sight of Watcher, wearing a navy blue bodysuit, gloves, boots, and mask. The mask has grey mesh covering his eyes and mouth, with three black diamonds sewn into the forehead of the mask. As he appears, The Watcher starts walking down towards the ring. The lights cut out as Watcher is walking to the ring.)
Jones:”From Parts Unknown, he is 6' 6” and weighs 244 lbs., he is…..THE WATCHER!!!” (The lights suddenly turn back on and The Watcher appears in the ring to a decent pop. The Watcher turns his attention to Edy Feria, who watches him with a burning focus in his eyes.)
Gonzago: "The Watcher is looking to be in great shape tonight, as both men look eager to get things started."
(The referee calls for the bell, as it signals the start of the match.) Gonzago: "And that sound of the bell signifies the match has started. Both men lock up now, as Edy Feria backs Watcher into the corner. Feria breaks clean as the referee checks out, and a sign of respect seems to be shown. Both men lock up once more now, this time The Watcher getting the advantage. The Watcher backs The Lion into the corner now, as he breaks clean. The referee makes sure it stays clean....and it is. A third time The Watcher and The Lion lock horns, Feria backing The Watcher into the corner once more. He breaks clean....
....and the fans woo in excitement as Feria chops The Watcher in the chest. But The Watcher slaps Feria in the chest back! Feria with another chop to Watcher! Watcher with one to The Lion! Back and forth each men chop one another, neither giving an inch. The Watcher now starts winning the battle of the chops, as he backs The Lion into the ropes. An Irish whip sends Feria across the way, and on the return, gets taken down with a huge powerslam. The Watcher covers....
.....but Feria kicks out! The Watcher picks The Lion up to his feet and whips him to the ropes once more. On the return, The Watcher scoops him up for tilt-a-whirl backbreaker.....
.....but gets it reversed by The Lion, who turns it into a spinning head scissors! The Watcher gets back up to his feet, but is brought back down to the mat with a leaping Hurricanrana by Feria, as he hooks the legs....
.....and The Watcher kicks out after two. Edy picks Watcher up to his feet and scoops him up on his shoulders, looking to go for a DVD....
.....but The Watcher is able to slip out from behind, grab hold of The Lion's head and.... ....hit a hard jumping neckbreaker! The Watcher grabs hold of The Lion, hooks both his arms in Full Nelson position, and picks him up and....
....drops him hard to the mat with a Full Nelson slam. The cover.... 1..... 2..... .....but The Lion kicks out! The Watcher now drops The Lion back to the mat with a DDT and then follows through with several knees to the back of the head. The Watcher brings The Lion up to his feet, locks in a Cobra Clutch submission....
....but Feria slips out from it, grabs hold of The Watcher.... .....and drives his head into the mat hard with a face buster. Feria now runs to the corner, flies up to the top and lands a brilliant corkscrew splash on top of The Watcher. The cover....
1..... 2..... .....NO! The Watcher was able to kick out, as Feria hits the ropes and hits a somersault leg drop just as he was about to get up. Feria brings The Watcher up to his feet and flings him into the ropes. On the return, The Lion goes for a Superkick.....
......The Watcher ducks underneath and hits the other side. He shoots off the ropes; Edy Feria goes for a roundhouse kick....
....The Watcher ducks again, quickly turns to Feria, goes for the Starbreaker..... .....but Feria ducks evade the Superkick finisher, kicks The Watcher in the stomach, sets him up in powerbomb position, as he lifts him up and....
.....he powerbombs The Watcher hard to the mat, but uses his strength to lift him back up, looking for the Double Impulse.....
......but The Watcher flips out of it, ducks underneath a roundhouse kick attempt by The Lion, grabs him from behind......
.....THERE IT IS!!! THE WATCHER HITS THE IMPACT ZONE!!! That awesome inverted suplex into front neckbreaker finisher takes out The Lion, as The Watcher goes for the pin, with the ref making the count....
1..... 2..... 3!!! THE WATCHER MOVES ON TO THE NEXT ROUND!!!" Jones: "Here is your winner, and advancing to the second round of the WRESTLING LEAGUE SERIES......THE WATCHER!!!"
(Cue up: "Aerials" by System of a Down as the referee raises The Watcher's arm in victory.)
Gonzago: "Another win for The Watcher, as Edy Feria gave a valiant attempt, but came up short here tonight. The Watcher is now one step closer from entering the World Title Round Robin tournament, going to the second round."
Kincaid: "As good as The Lion was, he just didn't have what it took to put away The Watcher. No one has yet to figure him out yet and I don't believe anyone will any time soon."
