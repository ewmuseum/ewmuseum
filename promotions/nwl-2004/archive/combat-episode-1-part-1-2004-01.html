From Elks Lodge in Queens, New York (Cue up: ”Out Of Control” by Hoobastank as just the first guitar rift starts, playing in a loop. The letter N starts to come into view from the left side of the screen, as more letters fade in next to it spelling the word “National”. The word fades out and the letter W comes into view from the top right corner of the screen. More letters fade in next to it spelling the word ‘Wrestling’. The word fades out and the letter L comes into view from the bottom of the screen. More letters fade in next to it spelling the world ‘League’. Then, all three words fly off into the distance for a bit as the lyrics start, somewhat disappearing from view. Suddenly, the words fly right back into view and read from top to bottom,
“National Wrestling League”. They then fly forward and out of view. Then the word “Presents…” flies into view for a few seconds and then right out of view.
Just as the song starts to pick up fast and loud, a loud explosion shows on the screen, with the logo for NWL Combat appearing from the fires.)
(Cut to: Theo hitting The Revelation, a reverse jumping piledriver, on an opponent.) (Cut to: Shawn Hart posing for the fans flamboyantly.) (Cut to: Canada's Hero and Elizabeth boasting in the ring.) (Cut to: The Watcher walking down to the ring very enigmatically.) (Cut to: Jarod Poe hits Fading Light, a double-underhook piledriver on his opponent.) (Cut to: Karl "The Dragon" Brown on the turnbuckle, saluting the fans.) (Cut to: Dakota Smith, as he walks down the ring, focused on his opponent inside with an expressionless gaze.) (Cut to: Lars Magellan sending an opponent through a table with the Beach Bomb.) (Cut to: Maelstrom applying Mortal Sin on his opponent.) (Cut to: Another explosion on the screen. Cut to: Inside the Elks Lodge in Queens, New York, where once again the fans jam-pack the place, as they go crazy screaming and yelling. Cut to: Inside the ring, where commentator Emanuel Gonzago stands, dressed in a royal blue Armani suit and tie, holding a microphone in hand. He looks around the arena, as the fans start chanting manically, "NWL! NWL!
NWL!") Gonzago: "Welcome everyone to the Elks Lodge, in Queens, New York!" (The fans cheer loudly.) Gonzago: "WELCOME TO NWL COMBAT!" (The fans start popping loudly once again.) Gonzago: "As always, I am Emanuel Gonzago and tonight, we kick off the official opening of the
National Wrestling League with ten, yes wrestling fans, TEN big matches, as the first round for the Wrestling League Series begins.....TONIGHT!"
(The fans start chanting "NWL! NWL! NWL!" once again.) Gonzago: "Tonight, you will see some familiar faces you have come to know and love, or hate, and you will also see some fresh new talent that are exclusive to the National
Wrestling League! Now, allow me to..."
