"This Is The End"

1-9 August 2004

AUMedina — Aug 1, 2004 at 9:04 PM I have some major bad news. I will no longer be able to keep open and run the National Wrestling League. I just scored a new job as a Jr. Network Administrator and will be working part time at my old job. With 14 hour days, I can't keep you good people held back for long. I'm sorry to report that the NWL is officially closed.
I know some of you will be upset with me and I don't blame you. I failed you all and there is nothing I can do to make it up to you all.
However, I want to point out that all is not lost: There are plenty of great leagues on FW Central. I suggest hitting WFW and NEW, as they both have a real foothold on things.
Once again, I apologize immensely for this. Sometimes you have to do what you have to do. Sincerely, [name withheld] EpyonMarx — Aug 2, 2004 at 2:35 PM Hey, no worries Andrew. You've got a lot going on, and no-one can blame you for what happens in life. It was fun whilst it lasted, and you have my full respect for what you've done.
