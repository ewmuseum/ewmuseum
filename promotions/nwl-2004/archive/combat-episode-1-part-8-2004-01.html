NWL Combat Episode 1, part 8

From Elks Lodge in Queens, New York Gonzago: "Moving on fans, coming up we have another WLS qualifying match as Jack Townsend, a newcomer to the sport, takes on Dakota Smith, who from what I heard in the back before, isn't too happy about his loss to Maelstrom on our last show."
Kincaid: "He almost defeated Maelstrom, which would have put him at the same level as his father Nevada, who actually defeated the Pandorian giant. It doesn't look too good for Townsend here tonight."
Gonzago: "Let's take it to the ring, where Darius Jones stands to kick things off." (Cut to: Inside the ring, where Darius Jones stands by with the microphone.) Jones: The following contest is scheduled for one fall......and is the first round of the WLS QUALIFYING TOURNAMENT!!!"
(Cue up: "Montsegur" by Iron Maiden. Out walks Jack Townsend, as the crowd gives a mixed reaction. He heads to the ring and slides under the ropes. When he stands up, he raises one arm up in victory, even though the match hasn't even started yet.)
Jones: "Introducing first, from New York, New York, he is 6' 4" and 265 lbs., here is......JACK TOWNSEND!!!"
Gonzago: "It seems Jack Townsend is a bit presumptuous as he thinks the match is already over and he's the victor."
Kincaid: "I don't think that's the best idea tonight....." Jones: "And his opponent...." (Cue up:”For Whom The Bells Toll” by Metallica. The lights dim down in the Elks Lodge, as a young man wearing a hooded black sweatshirt and black tights, with golden snakes running up the legs, their heads facing each other at the waist. He has a somewhat quicken pace in his footsteps, as a pissed look on his face is shown. He doesn't budge by the fans boos, as he slides into the ring and starts to beat on Jack Townsend. Darius Jones dives out of the ring as the referee quickly calls for the bell.)
Gonzago: "It seems Dakota Smith needs no introduction, as the reports seem to be true. Dakota just lays into Jack Townsend, followed by a.....
......OH MI DIOS!!! DAKOTA SMITH JUST HIT MEGADETH OUT OF NOWHERE! That deadly powerbomb/powerslam combination almost KILLED Jack Townsend!"
Kincaid: "I don't think he's moving Emanuel...." Gonzago: "You're right Roger, as Dakota doesn't seem to care, picking up Townsend, lifting him up in a crucifix.....
......MI DIOS!!! DEEP IMPACT!!! DEEP IMPACT!!! Dakota Smith just IMPALED Jack Townsend into the mat face first as he flips him over from that crucifix and turned it into a devastating face buster. But Dakota isn't stopping at all, as he grabs hold of Townsend, lifts him up again and.....
.....DEEP IMPACT!!! The referee needs to stop this match, as Dakota Smith is going for Deep Impact again, lifting Jack Townsend up in the air and....
......OH MI DIOS!!! COME ON DAMNIT!!! STOP HIM REF!! STOP HIM!!!" Kincaid: "Poor kid. Just a victim of circumstance here tonight." Gonzago: "Dakota Smith isn't relenting one bit, as he stands up Jack Townsend, who is having a hard time just staying in one place. Dakota Smith goes to other side of the ring, runs at Townsend and....
.....DOUBLE IMPACT!!! THAT SPEAR INTO A SPINNING CHOKESLAM JUST TOOK OUT JACK TOWNSEND!!! Dakota Smith places a foot on the face of Townsend, as the referee makes the count....
1..... 2..... 3!!! DAKOTA SMITH WINS HIS MATCH WITH EASE!!! Jones: "Here is your winner, and advancing to the second round of the WRESTLING LEAGUE SERIES......DAKOTA SMITH!!!"
(Cue up: "For Whom The Bell Tolls" by Metallica as Dakota Smith storms out of the ring, still pissed as hell.)
Gonzago: "It seems Jack Townsend made two mistakes tonight." Kincaid: "Yeah: Don't piss off Dakota Smith.....and DON'T piss off Dakota Smith!" Gonzago: "Dakota Smith goes to the second round without breaking a sweat, as Townsend may need some medical attention."
