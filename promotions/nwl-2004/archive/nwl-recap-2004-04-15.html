NWL Recap, 15 April 2004 (Combat Episode 5)

15 April 2004

recent NWL Combat Ep. 9 Previous NWL Combat Ep. 8 Next NWL Combat Ep. 10 NWL Recap: 5/03/04 NWL Recap: 4/15/04 4/15/04 From: Elks Lodge, NY Good evening, one and all. I'm [name withheld], and I'll be your recapper for NWL COMBAT. It's been a long time coming, but finally we've got ME to recap the shows. Exciting, ain't it?
The show kicks off with Emanuel "Mannny" Gonzago welcoming the fans. Normally, he'd run down the card, but "The Greatest Wrestler In The World" Lars Magellan and his partner in crime Shamrock Finnegan come out to interupt proceedings. Magellan rips on Manny, and proceeds to talk about how we've only seen the "tip of the iceburg," before telling us that the THIRD member of the stable will be in attendance next week!! Intrigue... he then tells us that even though neither is on the card, they've got unfinished business to take care of. Say what you like about Lars, he sure knows how to get under the crowds skin, even if he is 0-2 in the WLS.
We then get a rundown of the card, as well as Rogre Kincaid telling us we had a eulogy sheduled for after the Brown/Maelstrom match for Brown. The delivery was just classic.
RYAN "THE GRAFFITI" CRUZ vs. THE AMERICAN Seems Cruz has dropped his "American Eagle" gimmick, and said he'll leave NWL if he doesn't win. Wow, talk about high stakes. The American starts of with a snapmare, gaining the advantage, only for Cruz to come back with a dropkick, then an arm drag into an armbar. The American makes a small comeback with a couple of right hands, but receives a sunset flip for his troubles. Cruz controls for a while, getting two or three quick pin attempts, before TA collects his thoughts on the outside. Back in the ring, TA scores a two count off a DDT, and starts working on the legs with a toe hold, before Cruz reverses it with a small package for two. A German Suplex sets up a moonsault, but still Cruz can't keep The American down. Shame, too, because TA takes it to him with a firemans carry, before attempting a high risk maneuveur, only to eat a dropkick for his troubles. Cruz then hits a nasty looking spinebuster, then heads to the ropes. Leaping off, flying high, he catches TA...or maybe not, as The American reverses into a powerbomb, holding the tights for the three count. A good opening match, with both guys having some time on the offence, but seemed a little rushed in places. 2.5/5
Backstage, we see Cruz about to leave for the last time. Suddenly, holding the door closed, is President Medina, who gives a motivational speech, and tells Cruz to take a couple of weeks off. With the way the match ended, I wouldn't be suprised to see a feud there. And a good thing about these Medina skits, he keeps them short and doesn't overshadow the wrestlers.
JOHN DOE vs. CAMERON LOVE for a shot at Jarod Poe's TV title Before the match starts, Cameron tells us he's going to make one girl per town he visits happy by spending a night with them. O..K... not the most usual thing you do in a wrestling ring, but it got the crowds attention, and the Hometown Hotties Tour sounds like a good enough gimmick. Both men trade punches as the match gets underway, with Doe gaining the upperhand after an Irish Whip and flying forearm into the corner. A tornado DDT gets a two count, and Love counters a belly-to-belly suplex attempt, hitting a bulldog and two elbow drops for a count of one. Love misses a superkick, getting planted with a reverse DDT, and Doe gets a near fall off a legdrop. The crowd give the obligatory "Woooooooo" for some Doe chops in the corner, before a belly-to-belly and headlock slow down the action for a minute. Love does manage a comeback with a back suplex and DDT for two, but then gets THROWN over the top rope when attempting a bulldog. Doe then hits a most impressive dive over the top rope, before ramming Love into the steps. He tries a chair shot, but Love pulls him in, and Doe's bleeding again. He seems to bleed a lot, doesn't he? Love sends him back into the ring, looking for the 69 Slam, but Doe reverses into a tombstone. He climbs the ropes, hitting a senton bomb. Doe goes for the Amnesia Attack, but Love ducks it, hitting a shoudler block and the 69 Slam for the victory. A nicely paced match, with some good psychology, but I do think that maybe having Doe juice again then no-sell it made things seem a little less...real, in a way. 3/5
After the break, we're told that JC, manager to Nevada Smith, has been seen going into Dakota Smith's dressing room. INTRIGUE!!!!
DISTRIBUTOR OF PAIN w/LT vs. HACKER w/Data DoP rushes Hacker at the start, but misses, eating some knuckle sandwiches, an Irish Whip (anything like an Irish Cream I wonder?) and clothesline in the corner. A verticle suplex gets a two count, as DoP pushes Hacker out of a headlock. Hacker ducks under the BIG BOOT, but doesn't duck the spinebuster. A two count follows, as does a brainbuster and top rope leg drop, but he covers too non-chalantly, so no victory for you DoP. A fujiwara armbar has Hacker in pain, as Kincaid asks why Data would want to marry a "wimp" like Hacker, only for Manny to bend his finger back to show him some pain. Your winner by submission: MANNY!!! Back in the ring, meanwhile, Hacker makes it to the ropes, and counters a powerbomb into a roll-up for a near fall, before hitting two spinning neckbreakers. On the third, DoP hits a DDT, before scoring with the Devil's Cross for the victory. A little shorter than I'd have liked, but both men kept up a good pace, and the moves looked crisp as always. 3/5
Post match, we see why LT's down there, as Magellan and Finnegan come out to attack DoP. Can you say "tag-team match"?
Backstage, Medina confronts Data and Hacker about the wedding next Combat, telling them it's not the place... the wrestling ring, that is, as he says a friend is letting them hold the ceremony in his cathedral. I had been thinking swerve coming, but now it's not in the ring, I have to say congrats to the two of them. Congrats to the two of them.
DAKOTA SMITH vs. THE WATCHER in the WLS ...or not, as Smith wins this in one move after The Watcher was attacked and bloodied backstage. And when I say bloodied, I mean it was coming out through and under his mask and bodysuit!!! Dakota goes 2-0 in the WLS, and now sits in pole position.
Post, erm... well, the point of The Watcher in this segment is made by the two mysterious men who come down and talk about how he should come clean about who he is. Should be interesting to see what happens next week in the WLS for The Watcher, and especially afterwards.
Next, before Smith can even elave the arena, Shawn Hart comes out and launches into a verbal tirade against how "boring" NWL is without the "Phemon". Security get him out of there after a few minutes, but not until after he insults Smith, The Watcher, and "Mild Dust Storm". Interesting to see how this plays out, because you've got Magellan and Finnegan saying that a new member will be joining them soon, and Hart attacking the NWL as well. My monies on Hart teaming with them, but I could be wrong.
KARL "THE DRAGON" BROWN vs. MAELSTROM in the WLS And yes, I was tempted to put Mael"Mild Dust Storm"strom. These two had a GREAT set of promo's going into this match, with Brown playing off his inexperience and Maelstrom giving him plenty of respect. The two shake hands before the match, before a collar and elbow tie-up results in Maelstrom pushing Brown away with ease. A second tie-up has the same results, but a third ends with Brown dropping down to take 'Strom down with a drop-toe hold. Maelstrom gets to his feet, but a beautiful drop-kick sends him back down. They lock up again, Maelstrom slipping behind, but Brown escapes, going for a drop-kick, but ending up swatted away, and on his way back up, Maelstrom gives him a hand with a belly to belly with authority. Brown rushes in, but is met with a sidewalk slam, which results in a one count. A second sidewalk slam attempt by 'Strom fails, with Brown hitting a headscissors take-down, two arm drags, and an armbar. One thing I like about Brown, his moves look crisp, especially for someone with his lack of experience. Maelstrom gets to his feet, overpowering Brown into a stalling suplex in a bizarre spot which you'd have to check out to believe. The resulting cover gets a two count, as Maelstrom proceeds to use Brown for tackle practice in the corner, then kicking practice with a painful looking savate kick. Another two count, and Maelstrom goes for a gorilla press, but Brown counters into a pinning combination. A two count, and he hits a MASSIVE snap suplex, considering he weighs a lot less than Maelstrom. He then runs up the corner, springboarding off with a moonsault for two, which Maelstrom powers out of with ease. A nice drop-kick to the knee and spin wheel kick sets Maelstrom up for a BRUTAL looking bridging STF, which you have got to see. Even made Maelstrom look in trouble, which is no mean achievment. 'Strom makes it to the ropes, but is met with a somersault legdrop, and another two count follows. Brown then signals for a dragonrana, but gets caught in mid-air and jacknifed with authority. Maelstrom gets a three... or not, as Brown kicks out at the very last second, and a piledriver only gets a two. Maelstrom then throws away the pinning attempts, and locks in MORTAL SIN, throwing Brown around like a rag doll. The ref checks Brown, whose arm drops once...twice....BUT NOT THREE TIMES!!! The roar when Brown kept his arm from dropping was deafening, and when he made it to the ropes to force the break, the crowd went almost ballistic. Maelstrom goes for another jackknife, but "The Dragon" escapes, catching him from behind, pulling him into the corner and connecting with a nice Diamond Dust. I've seen him go for that a couple of times, but this is the first time I've seen it connect, and it looked SWEET. But he can't put 'Strom away. He hits a DDT and shooting star, but STILL can't put him away. He looks for the Dragon's Bite, but Maelstrom slips behind and hooks in Mortal Sin again...but Brown slips behind, connecting with the Dragon's Bite, crawling over for the cover, and just as the ref's hand is about to hit the mat for the third time... the bell rings? Yes, it's official, these two fought to a draw when Brown had Maelstrom, in the eyes of this recapper, beaten. Although I can understand the draw; Brown's a rookie, after all, and a win over Maelstrom would have been a little too unbelievable maybe. But what a match. 5/5, if for no other reason than the crowds roars when Brown broke Mortal Sin AND looked to have beaten Maelstrom.
Post match, Magellan and Finnegan come out again, attacking Maelstrom and Brown. They try to whip them together, but what looked like a backdrop by Maelstrom sends Karl dropkicking Magellan, as 'Strom lariets Finnegan. We have the two faces left in the ring, with Magellan basically declaring vengence on the outside. I smell another tag-match. And TAHT would be a sweet match.
Backstage, Maelstrom finds another note, and barges in on Dakota Smith. Some verbal sparring ensues, until they start going at it. Medina lays down the LAW, saying if Maelstrom even turns up to the next Combat, he's out of the WLS. And if Smith attacks anyone outside of his own match, then he's out too. And we get a major announcement next week!!! THIS is yet more, need I say it, INTRIGUE!!!
PULSAR vs. KRUSHER for the NWL International Title Or not, as Krusher was attacked and laid out, so Pulsar wins by count out. Magellan and Finngean come out for a THIRD time, attacking Pulsar, until DoP comes out, as we end the show with a brawl. I'm confused; so that's now THREE teams we could have facing Finnegan and Magellan?
Another strong show from the NWL. Two matches didn't really happen (Smtih/Watcher and Pulsar/Krusher), but the ones that did kept up the standard we've come to expect. Medina put in a few appearances, but only to do his job really. There's a lot of different storylines happening right now, and a lot of seeds were sown tonight, with the possibility of Magellan/Finnegan against DoP/LT, DoP/Pulsar or Maelstrom/Brown. And the Brown/Maelstrom result means we can expect somewhere down the line for a second, or even third, match between these two. Dakota Smith currently sits atop the WLS after two matches, and next week could effectively have the whole thing sown up.
