NWL Combat Episode 1, part 3

January 2004

recent NWL Combat Ep. 5 Previous NWL Combat Ep. 4 Next NWL Combat Ep. 6 Episode 1 LIVE! From Elks Lodge in Queens, New York Gonzago: "I couldn't agree more. Hello wrestling fans. Welcome to the very first episode of NWL Combat! I am as always Emanuel Gonzago and alongside with me for the ride, the wired Roger Kincaid. As you just saw in our opening bout, The Distributor of Pain defeated D-Dawg and will now move on to the second round of the Wrestling League Series. For those of you who don't know what the Wrestling League Series, or WLS for short, is, here's the lowdown: The twenty wrestlers in NWL have been entered in a three month long tournament to determine our first champions. The qualifying tournament will determine who will be placed in what title contention. Since DoP won his match, he will move on to the second round. D-Dawg, while he lost his match, will be entered in the Rumble in the Jungle match to determine the first NWL Television champion. Only ten men can move on to the second round tonight, where they will compete for a chance to make it to the third round, the WLS Round Robin World Heavyweight Championship tournament, to determine our very first NWL World Heavyweight champion. However, in the event the 10 wrestlers who advance here tonight do not make it past the second round, they will be thrown into the Gridiron Gauntlet match to determine our first NWL International Champion. Some exciting times ahead of us for sure, as the action will be non-stop from here on out tonight."
Kincaid: "Yeah Emanuel, but what about tonight's jam-packed card? Just the first round alone makes for an exciting show!"
Gonzago: "You are absolutely correct Roger, as we just had one spectacular match out of the ten you will see here tonight. Tonight, on this program, you will see two Aussies collide, as Pulsar takes on 'The Hardcore Legend' Derek Graham, Hacker facing off against Karl 'The Dragon' Brown, Jarod Poe going one-on-one against Canada's Hero, The Watcher squaring off against Edy 'The Lion' Feria, Dakota Smith taking on Jack Townsend, Maelstrom and The Prophecy hooking it up, Minion versus Krusher, and 'The Phenom' Shawn Hart taking on Theo."
Kincaid: "Very impressive." Gonzago: "Up next however, we have two men, one making his return to the squared circle, the other a student of a wrestling legend, facing off. Shamrock Finnegan, the prodigy of one Patrick McGee, better known to the wrestling world as Pat Gordon, will have his hands full as after being away from the ring for over two years, will face none other than Lars Magellan."
Kincaid: "He's rude, crude, and misunderstood. My boy Lars Magellan has come back to this great sport and even better.....he's right here in the NWL!"
Gonzago: "He is an awesome sight, but let's not forget two factors that play against Magellan. First, he's been away from the ring so long, who knows if he has any ring rust he hasn't fought off yet. Second, Shamrock Finnegan has been under the guidance of Patrick McGee, who has had some awesome battles in his career. I'll never forget this amazing Ambulance Match in the WWL against Billy Matthews. Both men are big and powerful and it'll come down to who wants it more. Without delaying any longer, we take it to the ring, where ring announcer Darius Jones awaits with the introductions."
(Cut to: Inside the ring, where ring announcer Darius Jones stands, all decked out in a nice tuxedo. He stands in the middle of the ring, holding microphone in hand.)
Jones: The following contest is scheduled for one fall......and is the first round of the WLS QUALIFYING TOURNAMENT!!!"
(Cue up: "Four Green Fields" by Fureys. The house lights are replaced by green and orange strobe lights, as out walks a huge man wearing a green, white and orange striped spandex leotard with a green shamrock on the front, waving Ireland's flag. Right behind him is a shorter, older gentleman, who Shamrock Finnegan hands the flag over to. He makes his way to the ring, slapping the fans hands and waving to everyone. He enters the ring, as Pat McGee follows suit.)
Jones: "Introducing first, from Omagh, British Occupied Ireland, he is 6' 4" tall and weighing in at 286 lbs, he is accompanied to the ring by hardcore legend PATRICK MCGEE.....here is......SHAMROCK FINNEGAN!!!"
Kincaid: "This kid looks primed and ready for action tonight." Gonzago: "Shamrock Finnegan looks really excited, wrestling his first professional match. I just hope he's ready for the return to the ring of his opponent...."
Jones: "And his opponent...." (Cue up: "California Love" by Dr. Dre and Tupac. Out walks a huge and cut individual as the fans really lay into him with boos. He walks down to the ring with a cock swagger, throwing narcisstic gestures at them. He enters the ring and climbs the turnbuckle, shouting and raising a fist in the air, getting the crowd more wild.)
Jones: "Hailing from Venice Beach, California, he stands at 6' 11" tall and weighs 242 lbs., please welcome back to the wrestling ring......LARS MAGELLAN!!!"
Kincaid: "The massive beach beau is back.....and only in the NWL!!!" Gonzago: "Right you are Roger, as Lars Magellan looks to be in tip top shape here tonight. I just hope whatever ring rust he has shakes off before he puts himself in a predicament that will have him lose this match.
(The referee checks both men and calls for the bell.) Gonzago: "And we are on our way, as Shamrock Finnegan and Lars Magellan lock horns. Both men are not giving an inch to one another, as they try to outpower the other. Magellan gets the advantage, turning the collar-and-elbow tie-up into a standing headlock. Magellan is now bragging to the fans as he wrenches on Finnegan's head. Shamrock now pushes Magellan off of him and into the ropes. On the return, both men collide.....
.....and Lars Magellan is taunting Shamrock Finnegan. Magellan's telling Shamrock to try his luck to bring him down. Shamrock complies, running towards the ropes and on the return, with a full head of steam....
.....both men don't budge again! Magellan is yelling at Shamrock, and then....." (Lars Magellan slaps Shamrock Finnegan hard in the face, as the crowd gives an "Ooooooo" reaction.)
Gonzago: "MI DIOS! Magellan just slapped the Holy Ghost out of Shamrock, daring him to take him down. Pissed as hell, Finnegan runs towards the ropes and on the rebound, goes right at Magellan, only to be avoided as Magellan boasts. However, Shamrock runs towards the other ropes, rebounds off of them and....
.....HUGE clothesline by Shamrock Finnegan. Lars Magellan gets up now, only to be taken right back down with another huge clothesline. Magellan back to his feet once more, anger setting on his face, as he runs right at Finnegan....
.....and put down by a MASSIVE powerslam by Shamrock Finnegan. He goes for the pin as the referee makes the count.....
1..... .....and Lars kicks out! Great 1-2-3 combination by Shamrock Finnegan, as it seems the rookie's getting the best of the veteran thus far in this matchup."
Kincaid: "Beginner's luck Emanuel. Everyone knows that." Gonzago: "Apparently Shamrock doesn't know that, as he takes Lars up his feet and......OH MI DIOS!!! LOOK AT THE STRENGTH OF SHAMROCK FINNEGAN!!! He has Lars Magellan in a gorilla press, as he.....
....sends him down to the mat hard! Patrick McGee is telling Shamrock to cover him, as he goes for it....
1.... 2..... ....NO! Lars Magellan just kicks out. This match was almost over from that true show of strength by one Shamrock Finnegan! The rookie now takes hold of Magellan and places him in a sleeper hold. Shamrock is trying to wear down Magellan as the referee is checking on him to make sure he wants to call it quits. Magellan PICKS Shamrock off his feet, giving him a piggy-back ride, as he.....
.....falls back and slams Shamrock hard to the mat. Shamrock, lying face first on the mat, gets a brutal wake-up call as Magellan just double stomps on his back! Lars' weight just crushed Shamrock's back, as he seems to be in pain."
Kincaid: "Shamrock's going to need a four-leaf clover to get himself out of his predicament he's in."
Gonzago: "Lars Magellan now brings Shamrock Finnegan up to his feet and scoops him up, hitting a back breaker. But Lars Magellan holds on with his strength and brings Shamrock back down on his knee. A third time Magellan holds him and hits a sidewalk slam on Finnegan. Magellan goes for the cover....
1..... 2..... .....but Shamrock kicks out! Magellan now sets up Shamrock for a Full Nelson slam, as he brings him up.....
.....NO! Shamrock reverses it, grabs hold of Magellan and hits a huge hotshot on the big man from Venice Beach, California. Shamrock now feeding off the fans cheers for him, as he picks up Magellan and....
....Exploder suplex! MI DIOS, Shamrock showed once again his strength, sending the big man over and under onto the mat! He goes for the cover....
1...... 2...... .....and Lars Magellan kicks out! The fans are on the edge of their seats with this one tonight folks!"
Kincaid: "I must say, this Shamrock is certainly impressive. He better hope he can seal the deal here in this match, otherwise he will be in trouble."
(Shamrock Finnegan raises both hands and starts shouting to the fans, "Twenty six plus six equals ONE!")
Gonzago: "It seems like Shamrock's signaling for the end here, as he's calling for his Northern Lights suplex finisher, 26+6=1."
Kincaid: "What in the hell is 26+6=1? I barely made it out of high school, but even I know that math isn't correct."
Gonzago: "Shamrock picks Magellan up to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckle. Magellan bounces off the corner.....
....and evades the 26+6=1, as he turns it into a reverse DDT! He goes for the pin.... ....and Shamrock gets a foot on the bottom rope! Magellan now signaling for the end here, as he whips Shamrock into the corner, and on the return.....
.....he gets caught by Shamrock, as he lifts Magellan up in the air and...... .....Magellan reverses the 26+6=1, spinning with the momentum, as he comes back on his feet.....
.....BEACH BOMB! BEACH BOMB! BEACH BOMB! Lars Magellan hits his deadly powerbomb finish, as he takes the huge Shamrock Finnegan hard into the mat. The cover....
1..... 2..... 3!!! LARS MAGELLAN ADVANCES TO THE SECOND ROUND!!!" Jones: "Here is your winner, and advancing to the second round of the WRESTLING LEAGUE SERIES......LARS MAGELLAN!!!"
(Cue up: "California Love" by Dr. Dre and Tupac. The referee raises Lars Magellan's hand in victory as he plays to the crowd, who shower him in a chorus of boos.)
Kincaid: "I knew my man Lars Magellan would do it! He's is back and off to a terrific start!"
Gonzago: "It was certainly a hard fought match, as Lars Magellan moves on to the second round. But let's not discredit Shamrock Finnegan. In his first professional match, he almost defeated a veteran. This kid has major potential and I wouldn't be surprised if he was the odds on favorite to win the NWL Television title."
