NWL Combat Episode 2, part 2

February 2004

recent NWL Combat Ep. 5 Previous NWL Combat Ep. 4 Next NWL Combat Ep. 6 Episode 2 LIVE! From Elks Lodge in Queens, New York Kincaid: "What's happenin' everyone? Roger Kincaid here and as soon as he gets back up here, I'm here with Emanuel Gonzago. Thanks for tuning in yet again for another episode of NWL Combat. Ten wrestlers are here tonight to prove they have what it takes to move up to the next level: The Wrestling League Series. Two huge men in The Distributor of Pain and Lars Magellan will go at it to see who truly is the big man of the NWL. The enigma known as The Watcher will get set to face off against the street thug turned deadly wrestler in one Krusher. Maelstrom, who literally destroyed a man by his bare hands last time in the First Round, will square off against the man from the Land Down Under, Pulsar. Arguably the best rookie our sport has seen today, as Karl 'The Dragon' Brown will go one on one with a man who upset the great Jarod Poe, your savior and mine, Canada's Hero. Last but not least, a man who is on a TEAR here in the NWL after losing a close battle to his uncle Maelstrom, Dakota Smith will have something to prove against a veteran of our sport, Minion. Five great Second Round matches in the WLS Qualifying Tournament here tonight, live and in living color!"
(Emauel Gonzago walks into view, as he sits down next to Roger Kincaid, putting on his headset.)
Gonzago: "But let's not forget two other great matches here tonight, as Hacker with his lovely lady Data taking on a man making his debut here tonight, Frost. However, up next, in our opening bout, 'The Hardcore Legend', showing that a loss in the First Round will not deter him from winning some championship gold in the NWL, is set to take on another man making his debut here tonight on Combat, John Doe."
Kincaid: "This John Doe fella is a few balls short of a pool game. I bet if he didn't have an opponent to fight here tonight, he'd kick his own ass. If I were Graham, I'd watch himself. Even though he's only 19, the kid's probably so jacked up on lithium, that no garbage wrestling of any kind will affect him."
Gonzago: "However Roger, Derek Graham isn't just some small wrestler. He's bigger than the orderlys that restrain Doe to give him his shots. John Doe's got his work cut out for him, as he has to take on the huge seven footer. Let's see just what he has to bring to the table, as we kick it to Darius Jones, who stands in the ring awaiting with the introductions."
(Cut to: Inside the ring, where ring announcer Darius Jones, dressed in his usual tuxedo, stands with microphone in hand.)
Jones: "The following contest is scheduled for one fall....." (Cue up: "Bombs Over Baghdad" by Outkast. The lights in the Elks Lodge dim down as blue and red lights flicker all over. Out walks a man dressed in psychoatric ward garb, as the fans start cheering him on. He heads towards the ring and slides underneath the ropes. He then walks over to a corner turnbuckle and climbs it. He raises his hands to the crowd as they cheer him on.)
Jones: "Introducing first, from Parts Unknown, he is 6' 1" and weighs in at 210 lbs., here is......JOHN DOE!!!"
Gonzago: "John Doe looks ready for his debut match here in the NWL, as the fans get him all pschyed up."
Kincaid: "I just hope he doesn't start forthing from the mouth and attack the fans here in the Elks Lodge tonight."
Jones: "And his opponent....." (Cue up: "Devils Dance" by Metallica. The lights in the lodge turn to blue lights as out walks the huge Derek Graham to a decent pop from the crowd. He brings along with him a trash can full of weapons and items in one hand, a kendo stick in another. He tosses the trash can into the ring, as John Doe avoids it. He then gets on the apron and enters the ring over the top rope.)
Jones: "From Sydney, Austrailia, standing at a whopping 7' and weighing in at 265 lbs., he is the 'HARCORE LEGEND'.....this is.......DEREK GRAHAM!!!"
Gonzago: "Derek Graham seems to be ready for hardcore combat, as John Doe is trying to safely enter back into the ring."
Kincaid: "It seems Graham is laughing at Doe, taunting him to enter the ring as he holds that kendo stick in his hand like he's such a tough guy."
Gonzago: "As the bell rings, John Doe jumps onto the apron, but here comes Derek Graham as he swings the kendo stick at him.....
....but Doe is able to avoid the kendo stick, as he jumps up on the top rope and...... (The fans start chanting "HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!" as Doe performs a hurricanrana on Graham, sending him over the top rope and straight to the outside ground.)
Gonzago: "OH MI DIOS!!! DID YOU SEE THAT ROGER?! John Doe just took out Derek Graham with a HUGE Hurricanrana , sending the seven footer litearlly FLYING over the top rope and to the outside!"
Kincaid: "John Doe is not joking around. He is pissed now, as he grabs that kendo stick that Graham had and is beating him senseless!"
Gonzago: "The fans are just egging Doe on as he takes a chair from a fan at ringside and SMASHES Graham's head in with it! He lays the chair on the fallen Graham now, as John Doe jumps onto the apron and climbs the turnbuckle. Mi Dios, John Doe is going to do something crazy here, as he sets himself up for.....
.....OH MI DIOS!!! JOHN DOE HITS A SWANTON BOMB ON THE CHAIR ON DEREK GRAHAM!!! John Doe is pumped up now, as he picks Graham up to his feet.....
.....but amazingly Graham picks John Doe up and...... .....MI DIOS! He just launched Doe over the top rope, as he lands wickedly on his back! Graham shakes off the previous attack and enters the ring. Graham walks over to Doe, picks him up with one hand, as he lifts him up for a.....
......NO! Doe is able to kick out of the huge chokeslam attempt by Graham, as he lands on his feet and Irish whips Graham towards the ropes. On the return, Graham ducks underneath as Doe jumps over. Graham hits the ropes again, as Joe jumps up again and.....
.....AMNESIA ATTACK! AMNESIA ATTACK! AMNESIA ATTACK! John Doe just hit his flying elbow to the throat finisher on Derek Graham, landing hard across his wind pipe! As Derek Graham seem to be choking, John Doe quickly rushes over to make the cover.....
1..... 2..... 3!!! INCREDIBLE! JOHN DOE PULLS THE UPSET ON DEREK GRAHAM AND GETS THE WIN HERE TONIGHT!" Jones: "Here is your winner......JOHN DOE!!!" (Cue up: "Bombs Over Baghdad" by Outkast as the referee raises the hand of John Doe. He then heads over to the corner, where he once again climbs the turnbuckle and raises his arms in victory. The crowd cheers him on as they celebrate with Doe.)
Kincaid: "John Doe seemed to make quick work her of Derek Graham, but they'll have a chance to meet up again."
