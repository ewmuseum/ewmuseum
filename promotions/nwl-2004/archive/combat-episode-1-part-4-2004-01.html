NWL Combat Episode 1, part 4

January 2004

From Elks Lodge in Queens, New York Gonzago: "Coming up now folks in this next contest, we have two Australian natives set to do battle as Pulsar faces off against 'The Hardcore Legend' Derek Graham. This should be one that the folks down under talk about for awhile."
Kincaid: "There won't be any walkabouts in this match. And although they both hail from Australia, it seems Pulsar may be the underdog in this match as Derek Graham has a whopping nine inches. This will be one interesting match for sure."
Gonzago: "Let us get this match underway, as ring announcer Darius Jones awaits with the introductions."
(Cut to: Inside the ring, where Darius Jones stands with microphone in hand.) Jones: The following contest is scheduled for one fall......and is the first round of the WLS QUALIFYING TOURNAMENT!!!"
(Cue up: "Whiskey In A Jar" by Metallica. As the lights dim in the Elks Lodge, Pulsar walks out to a mixed reaction, as he heads down towards the ring, focused and ready for his match. He enters the ring and paces around, pumping himself up.)
Jones: "Introducing first, from Sydney, Australia, he is 6' 3" tall and 245 lbs., here is.......PULSAR!!!"
Gonzago: "Pulsar looks very focused on this match tonight, as he should be, because he will have to face off against a fellow country man that is larger than he is."
Jones: "And his opponent....." (Cue up: "Devils Dance" by Metallica. The lights in the lodge turn to blue lights as out walks a huge 7' black Australian. He brings along with him a trash can full of weapons and items in one hand, a kendo stick in another. He tosses the trash can into the ring, just missing Darius Jones. He then gets on the apron and enters the ring over the top rope.)
Jones: "From Sydney, Australia, standing at a whopping 7' and weighing in at 265 lbs., he is the 'HARCORE LEGEND'.....this is.......DEREK GRAHAM!!!"
(Graham swings the kendo stick into the head of Pulsar as he went after him. The fans start popping as the referee signals for the bell.)
Gonzago: "And Derek Graham waits for no time as he starts beating Pulsar with that kendo stick. The fans are really into this, as they are actually encouraging Graham to continue with his hardcore tactics."
Kincaid: "This isn't fair at all! The guy's seven feet tall and needs a fricken kendo stick to beat on his opponent? Ridiculous I tell you!"
Gonzago: "Derek Graham grabs hold of the trash can and empties out the contents within it. Then as he just turns around....
.....Pulsar rams the can into Graham's head with a huge shoulder tackle! Intelligent move by Pulsar, as he now starts yelling at Graham. He then takes a hold of the trash can, looking at it and Graham and....
.....tosses it out of the ring? Pulsar wants no part of Graham's hardcore lifestyle, as he knee drops him on the back of his head. Pulsar places some boots to the back of Graham's head, runs towards the ropes, and again places a strategic knee drop to the back of his head. The cover....
....and Graham kicks out after one! Pulsar now takes hold of Graham and whips him into the corner. Pulsar now on the attack, as he throws a right, followed by a hard chop to the chest!"
(The fans start going "Whooooo!" as Pulsar hits the chop to Graham's chest. They repeat it as Pulsar continues to chop the chest.)
Gonzago: "Pulsar continues to lay into Graham, as now he grabs hold of Graham's arm and goes for the Irish whip across the ring....
....but Graham holds on to the ropes. Pulsar continues to try to whip Graham out of the corner, but he just won't budge. Pulsar finally succeeds, whipping Graham out of the corner, only to find it reversed by Derek Graham.....
.....and Pulsar gets scooped up as Graham has him on his shoulder. He goes for a running head start and DRIVES Pulsar into the mat with running powerslam! Amazing strength by the seven footer Derek Graham! Graham now picks Pulsar to his feet and flings him to the ropes. On the return, Graham hits a HUGE big boot on Pulsar! Graham now is going....
....up the top turnbuckle! MI DIOS! The huge seven foot Australian climbs the top turnbuckle and....
.....AMAZING! Derek Graham connects with a top rope leg drop, connecting perfectly on Pulsar! Have you ever seen anything like that in your life Roger!?"
Kincaid: "Not since Big Bird fell from the top of Bert and Ernie's apartment building after taking a huge dump on the roof."
Gonzago: "You never cease to amaze me Roger. Derek Graham attempts a pin on Pulsar..... .....but Pulsar kicks out after two. Graham now is pumping the fans up as grabs....a Nintendo?!?!"
Kincaid: "Man that was the shit back in the day Emanuel! I know you Mexicans couldn't even afford a board game, but man, when I was growing up, that was the best. Remember Super Mario Bros? Oh wait, you don't, nevermind....."
Gonzago: "I'm not even going to comment on that one, as Derek Graham places the Nintendo on the mat, picks up Pulsar....
.....MI DIOS! Did you see that?! Derek Graham just piledrived Pulsar on that Nintendo!" Kincaid: "Damnit, I could have sold that on eBay for a fifty spot!" Gonzago: "Derek Graham again goes for the cover on Pulsar..... .....and yet AGAIN Pulsar is able to kick out after the two count. It looks like Graham needs to rethink his strategy here in this match if he wants to move on to the second round."
Kincaid: "It looks like Pulsar can take a beating and keep on ticking. Maybe it's a Timex...."
Gonzago: "Derek Graham is now starting to show some frustration on his face, as he picks up Pulsar to his feet and it looks like he's heading towards the top rope once again. This may prove to be a fatal mistake on Graham's part, but he doesn't seem to care. Up to the top rope Graham goes, sizing up Pulsar and....
.....misses Pulsar with a huge flying lariat as Pulsar brilliant evades what looked like to be almost the end of the match for him. Pulsar is now on the attack, as he moves outside onto the apron and hits a somersault leg drop over the ropes and onto the back of Graham's head. The cover....
1..... 2..... .....and Derek Graham kicks out! Pulsar continues on the attack, as he applies an STF on Graham. The referee checks to see if the hold is legit and not a choke. He gives the thumbs up to the bell keeper at ringside as Pulsar wrenches on the head of Graham. Graham is trying to fight it as the fans start cheering to pump him up. Just as Graham feels the fans' cheers and crawls over to the ropes, Pulsar lets go of the hold and places a strategic elbow drop across the back of Graham's head. Pulsar goes for the cover again.....
1..... 2..... .....and Graham kicks out again! Pulsar brings Graham up to his feet, grabs hold of his leg and hits a strategic dragon screw attack! Pulsar, still holding that leg, goes for a figure-four leg lock....
....but Derek Graham kicks Pulsar off of him! Pulsar comes back on the attack, but the big man jumps straight up to his feet with ease and almost DECAPITATES Pulsar with a HUGE clothesline from Hell! Graham now grabs hold of Pulsar's legs and sets up for a.....
....Texas Cloverleaf?! Amazing, as this hardcore wrestler pulls out a technical submission hold on Pulsar! Pulsar didn't see that one coming at all!"
Kincaid: "I thought that big idiot was even capable of a clothesline, but a Texas Cloverleaf? Damn, I've seen it all here tonight."
Gonzago: "Graham is keeping hold of that Texas Cloverleaf on Pulsar, but apparently he applied it too close the ropes as Pulsar was able to grab the ropes with ease. The referee tells Graham to let go of the hold, to which he complies. Graham now takes hold of Pulsar and hits a belly-to-belly suplex, followed by the pin....
1.... 2..... .....and Pulsar kicks out at the last moment! Graham trying to figure out what to do next takes hold of Pulsar and whips him to the corner....
....but gets it reversed as he's sent into the turnbuckle instead. Pulsar now goes to the opposite corner, makes a motion to the crowd, starts running right at Derek Graham, and jumps up for a running turnbuckle splash......
....but is caught by Graham, who grabs him by the neck and lifts him up! He's going for a massive choke slam, as he walks towards the center of the ring and.....
....gets a kick to the chest by Pulsar, who grabs hold of the slumped seven footer..... .....THE BLACK HOLE! Pulsar just hit that twisting fisherman neck breaker on Derek Graham out of nowhere! He goes for the cover as the referee counts.....
1..... 2..... 3!!! Pulsar just defeated Derek Graham and moves on to the second round!" Jones: "Here is your winner, and advancing to the second round of the WRESTLING LEAGUE SERIES......PULSAR!!!"
(Cue up: "Whiskey In A Jar" by Metallica. The referee raises Pulsar's hand as he celebrates his victory in the ring.)
Gonzago: "An amazing match by both individuals, as Pulsar now moves on to the next round." Kincaid: "It just goes to show that bigger isn't always better Emanuel." Gonzago: "Is that what the women say to you Roger?"
