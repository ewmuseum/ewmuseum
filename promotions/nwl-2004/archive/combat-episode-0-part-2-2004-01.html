NWL Combat Episode 0: Special Preview Edition, part 2 (The Watcher v. Theo)

January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

From Elks Lodge in Queens, New York (Cut to: The announcers' table, which is located high up and away from the ring, also known as the NWL Crows' Nest. There, seated with Emanuel Gonzago is a clean-cut, blonde-haired man, wearing what looks to be a Miami Vice suit with a Punisher t-shirt underneath.)
Gonzago: ”Hello once again fans. I'm Emanuel Gonzago and sitting along side with me will be my partner helping me call the action all through the night, Roger Kincaid.”
Kincaid: ”Emanuel, I can't believe business is finally booming. Look at this crowd! They are so red hot that they can't wait another minute to see their favorite NWL wrestlers battle it out and tear each other's limbs off!”
Gonzago: ”Tonight's lineup should be a great one, as we have three incredible matches in store for you. We have two well known superstars who just keep climbing the charts as ‘The Phenom' Shawn Hart takes on Jarod Poe, in which this should be a great wrestling contest. We also have the huge Pandorian, Maelstrom, after being inactive for over 6 months, stepping back into the ring against another Pandorian and coincidentally his nephew, son of Nevada Smith, Dakota Smith. But first up, we have two newcomers to our sport who will be making their presence felt in little to no time as The Watcher, who never really got a chance to make an appearance in the WWL facing off against a fellow enigmatic wrestler in one Theo.”
Kincaid: ”These two men have a very secretive background and the fans know hardly anything about either wrestler. Hopefully their wrestling ability will shock the fans here tonight.”
Gonzago: ”Right you are Roger. But let us not delay any longer. Let's take it to the ring, where ring announcer Darius Jones awaits with the introductions!”
(Cut to: Inside the ring, where a dark-skinned gentleman dressed in a tuxedo stands with a microphone in hand.)
Jones: ”The following contest is scheduled for one fall…” (Cue up: ”The Noose” by A Perfect Circle. As the bell tones of A Perfect Circle's "The Noose" begin to play, the lights dims. Then, green lights go on in the Elks Lodge. Out walks a man with messy, long black hair and wearing a forest-green t-shirt that reads, “Humility is Weakness”. He makes his way to the ring and then gets on the apron. He turns around and smirks at the crowd, which gets an unpleasant response.)
Jones: ”Introducing first, from Seattle, Washington, he stands 6' 1” tall and weighs in at 209 lbs, he is…..THEO!!!”
(Theo enters the ring and chooses a corner, sitting on top of the turnbuckle. He looks over at the entranceway, awaiting his opponent.)
Gonzago: ”Theo seems to a little relaxed in this match as the fans didn't take too kindly to the way he looked at them.”
Kincaid: ”Oh nonsense! Theo is very focused on this match. Hell, he's waiting for The Watcher to make his presence felt here.”
Jones: ”And his opponent….” (Cue up: ”Aerials” by System of a Down. The lights cut out and a few spotlights shine on the entryway where smoke has gathered. A strobe light slows the sight of Watcher, wearing a navy blue bodysuit, gloves, boots, and mask. The mask has grey mesh covering his eyes and mouth, with three black diamonds sewn into the forehead of the mask. As he appears, The Watcher starts walking down towards the ring. The lights cut out as Watcher is walking to the ring.)
Jones: ”From Parts Unknown, he is 6' 6” and weighs 244 lbs., he is…..THE WATCHER!!!” (The lights suddenly turn back on and The Watcher appears in the ring to a decent pop. The Watcher turns his attention to Theo, who climbs down from the turnbuckle, as each prepares for their match.)
Gonzago: ”The Watcher looks to be prepared tonight for his match against Theo. Both men should prove to make one hell of a match here tonight.”
Kincaid: ”Where the hell does this kid Watcher come from? He's a bit too weird for my own liking. Looks like a S&M sailor to me.”
Gonzago: ”I take it we're talking about personal experiences here Roger?” Kincaid: ”Watch it Gonzago…” Gonzago: ”The referee looks to be ready and this match is underway!” (The referee signals for the bell. When the bell rings, both The Watcher and Theo begin to circle one another, feeling each other out.)
Gonzago: ”Both men are looking to size each other up here, as they grapple one another. Theo tries to gain the advantage, but The Watcher, the bigger of the two men, shrugs him off with ease, as the fans look in awe.”
Kincaid: ”If Theo is going to get the advantage here in this match, he needs to use his speed as an advantage. The Watcher is a strong individual, so trying to out power him won't work here.”
Gonzago: ”Right you are Roger, as both men lock horns once again. Theo, who should be listening to your advice Roger, tries to match power with him again, but yet again The Watcher just shrugs him off like he was a rag doll.”
Kincaid: ”I have to admit, the kid's got guts, that's for sure.” Gonzago: ”Theo, who won't back down, goes at The Watcher once again. But as The Watcher is ready for him with a standing clothesline, Theo ducks underneath. Just as The Watcher turns around, Theo is there waiting with a dropkick that staggers the big man. Theo runs towards the ropes, and on the rebound, takes down The Watcher with a Lou Thesz press. Both men get back up to their feet, and Theo hits The Watcher with another dropkick, sending him through the ropes and to the outside.”
(The fans start applauding, as The Watcher stands on the outside, recollecting his thoughts.)
Gonzago: ”It seems Theo is finally using his speed and agility, as The Watcher takes a breather out on the outside. The referee starts the ten count, but The Watcher gets back in the ring. But as he does, Theo runs against the ropes once more. On his return, he goes for a flying body press…..
…..but The Watcher catches him in mid-air and plants him hard with a huge power slam! Incredible reversal by The Watcher on that exchange.”
Kincaid: ”Even though he's big and strong, The Watcher has some brains in that masked head of his.”
Gonzago: ”Right you are again Roger. The Watcher now gets Theo in a sit-down headlock, wearing the man down.”
Kincaid: ”The Watcher knows what he is doing here. By wearing down Theo, he is setting his own pace of the match. Plus, with the Lucha Libre skills of one Theo, The Watcher just grounded his entire attack.”
Gonzago: ”The Watcher is applying more pressure to the head and chin of Theo, as the referee checks on him to make sure he's alright. Theo however, with perseverance, slowly makes his way up to his feet. The Watcher still holds on to that headlock unrelenting. Theo starts to elbow The Watcher in the midsection, but he won't let go of the headlock. Theo again elbows The Watcher's midsection and staggers the big man for a moment. One more time Theo throws the elbow, this time making The Watcher let go of the headlock. Theo quickly then grabs hold of The Watcher and….
….small package as the referee starts the count…. 1… But The Watcher kicks out with easy right at 2. Both men get up, and Theo runs right at The Watcher. The Watcher awaits Theo and scoops him up, looking to go for a sidewalk slam….
…..but Theo reverses it into a spinning head scissors takedown! Theo gets back up to his feet, and just as The Watcher is about to get up to his, he gets taken right back down with a swinging neck breaker. Theo now walks over to The Watcher and stands right next to his fallen body. He stares into the crowd mockingly as he smirks at them and….
…..staredown standing leg drop! Theo now goes for another cover…. 1…. 2…. …..and The Watcher kicks out after that great exchange from Theo.” Kincaid: ”This Theo guy is very talented. My sources tell me that he was wrestling in the MCW before it closed down. I'm glad President Medina was able to get such a find in this kid.”
Gonzago: ”He is one resilient individual, as Theo now continues his attack, stomping the head of The Watcher. Theo picks The Watcher up to his feet, hooking the arm and hits a snap suplex. Theo brings The Watcher back up once more and whips him to the corner. The Watcher hits the corner hard as Theo heads over to him calmly. Theo plants some kicks to the midsection of The Watcher and now starts taunting the fans.”
(The fans start booing Theo as he smirks at them cockily.) Gonzago: ”After he gets the crowd worked up here, Theo heads over to the opposite corner and then charges right at The Watcher who is staggered in the corner. Full steam ahead Theo goes and as he steps off The Watcher and looks to be going for a Hurricanrana, The Watcher catches him just before he throws him over! The Watcher picks him up and just as quickly….
….snap powerbomb on Theo by the enigmatic Watcher! The Watcher slumps Theo over, placing some hard knees to the face. Then, just as quickly, he hits a beautiful jumping neckbreaker on Theo! The Watcher goes for the pin as the referee positions himself for the count….
1…. 2…. …..and Theo kicks out just in the nick of time! Great showing by both individuals as The Watcher has taken back control of this match!”
Kincaid: ”The Watcher looks to be on the attack once more, grabbing Theo from behind. I wonder what power move he'll perform here…”
Gonzago: ”It doesn't look like a power move he's going for Roger, but going back to his strategy of wearing Theo down as he has him locked in a Cobra Clutch. The Watcher holds to the submission move tightly as the referee checks up on Theo. Theo however, with his small frame, is able to slip out of the Cobra Clutch with ease and….
….side suplex as Theo just showed a real test of strength, taking The Watcher down to the mat. Both men now look to be worn out, as the ref checks up on both individuals.”
Kincaid: ”Both these men are taking each other to the limit in this match.” Gonzago: ”Right you are Roger, as now the referee is counting both men. But as the ref gets to five, Theo slowly reaches his feet first. He now walks over to The Watcher and brings him to his feet. He's signaling the end here, looking to go for The Revelation, his lethal reverse piledriver finisher. Theo scoops up The Watcher and it looks like he's about to….
…..NO! The Watcher kicks his legs and uses his weight to reverse it! The Watcher is now setting up for a…..
…...OH MI DIOS! Did you see that?! The Watcher just hit a belly-to-belly suplex variation! Such sheer strength and skill by one Watcher!”
Kincaid: ”I was definitely impressed with that maneuver.” Gonzago: ”The Watcher now awaits calmly for Theo to get up, as he looks to be going for….. …..STARBREAKER….. …..NO! Theo ducks underneath Starbreaker, the Superkick finisher by The Watcher, as he gets behind him, looking for a reverse DDT…..
…..but The Watcher reverses it, grabbing him from behind instead, as he lifts him up in an inverted suplex….
…..IMPACT ZONE! The Watcher just hit his lethal inverted suplex to front neckbreaker finisher on Theo! The Watcher now goes for the cover….
1…. 2….. 3!!! The Watcher has won this match with a brilliant reversal into Impact Zone!” Jones: ”Here is the winner of the match…..THE WATCHER!!!” (Cue up: ”Aerials” by System of a Down. The referee raises The Watcher's hand as the fans cheer him on.)
Kincaid: ”That was one totally awesome match we just witnessed here tonight Emanuel. Non-stop action from both individuals, giving everything they had for the fans in attendance.”
