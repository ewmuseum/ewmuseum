Rules for the Wrestling League Series The qualifying tournament determines who will be placed in what title tournament. The winners of the first round will move on to the second round. The losers of the first round will be placed in a battle royal for the NWL Television Title to be held at a later date.
The winners of the second round will move on to the third round. The losers of the second round will be placed in a gauntlet match for the NWL International Title to be held at a later date.
The third round of the tournament will have the five winners to be placed in a round robin tournament for the NWL World Heavyweight
Title.
The round robin tournament will have all five wrestlers face one another. Points will be awarded by the following: 2 points for a win, 1 point for a tie, 0 points for a loss.
The wrestler with the most points at the end of the round robin tournament will win the NWL World Heavyweight Title.
