NWL Combat Episode 1, part 6

January 2004

From Elks Lodge in Queens, New York Gonzago: "Just when you fans thought it was over, we still have six more matches in store for you tonight. Our next match has a newcomer to the professional ranks, as Canada's Hero gets set to take on a man who we all know, in one Jarod Poe."
Kincaid: "I hope Jarod Poe is able to recover from his lost last time against Shawn Hart, because while he may not know anything about Canada's Hero, he better not overlook him. This guy seems to be legit, as he has the goods to bring home the win here tonight."
Gonzago: "That is true, but Canada's Hero has never faced Jarod Poe before as well, so who knows who has the advantage here tonight. Let's take it to Darius Jones with the introductions."
(Cut to: Inside the ring, where Darius Jones awaits once again with microphone in hand.) Jones: The following contest is scheduled for one fall......and is the first round of the WLS QUALIFYING TOURNAMENT!!!"
(Cue up: "O Canada". Out walks a man wearing a Canadian colors with a beautiful, white-skinned woman around his arm. They walk down to the ring, as the fans boo the couple. He helps the lady into the ring, as he follows her in. He then poses with her, as the fans boo even louder.)
Jones: "Introducing first, from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, he is 6' 5" and weighs 255 lbs., with him tonight is the beautiful ELIZABETH......he is.......CANADA'S HERO!!!"
Kincaid: "He's got the looks, he's got the skills, and he's got the woman. What else can you ask for?"
Gonzago: "Maybe hope that he'll survive against his opponent tonight..." Jones: "And his opponent...." (Cue up: "Shadow Man" by W.A.S.P. Out walks Jarod Poe to a decent sized pop from the crowd. He heads towards the ring, focusing on Canada's Hero. He jumps on the ring apron and then proceeds to enter the ring.)
Jones: "From Hell, Michigan, standing 6' 5" tall and weighing in at 265 lbs., this.....is......JAROD POE!!!"
Gonzago: "Jarod Poe doesn't look too happy tonight, as he is eagerly waiting for this match to start."
Kincaid: "I don't know if this bodes well of Canada's Hero, as when Jarod Poe is pissed, he really pisses on people....royally."
Gonzago: "And just as the bell starts, Jarod Poe starts walloping on Canada's Hero, throwing lefts and rights. He then levels Canada with a devastating clothesline, followed by another, and another. Poe then sends Canada's Hero to the outside with a sharp kick to the head! The referee starts the ten count, as Canada recollects himself. His wife Elizabeth is there to console him, as Poe starts pacing back and forth in the ring."
Kincaid: "Great strategy by Canada's Hero, as he slows down the pace of the match to his advantage."
Gonzago: "The referee is up to seven as Canada's Hero finally enters the ring. But Poe meets him there with a double axe handle blow on his back. He now viciously stomps on Canada's back, as Jarod Poe is exploding on him. Canada picked up to his feet now, as Poe sends him across the ring with an Irish whip. Canada hits the ropes and on the return, Poe goes for a back body drop....
.....but Canada hurdles over him with ease. Once more against the ropes Canada goes and on the return, Jarod Poe jumps up to leap over him.....
.....and is caught by Canada's Hero, as he grabs hold of him and hits an awesome belly-to-belly suplex! Canada springs up to his feet and hits a standing elbow drop on Poe. Up again to his feet and once AGAIN Canada nails Poe with an elbow drop. Canada quickly goes for the cover....
....but Poe is able to kick out with ease. Canada's Hero now snap mares Jarod Poe and applies a kneeling chinlock. The referee checks to make sure Poe's alright, as Canada continues to wrench back. He now stands up, and flips forward and over Poe, still holding onto his head, as he performs a neckbuster on him. Canada now goes for the cover again....
.....and Poe kicks out! Canada's Hero getting a bit ahead of himself, as he starts flaunting to the fans. Canada now picks Poe up to his feet and takes him back down with a scoop slam. He points to the corner as he heads over and climbs the turnbuckle. He turns around, facing away Poe, setting up for a moonsault.....
.....but Poe moves out of the way! As Canada slowly gets up to his knees, Poe quickly walks over and.....
....Blood Rush! That deadly Iron Claw with those steel talon piercings dig into the skull of Canada's Hero, as the fans go wild. Look at Canada's forehead bleed! Those talons are in pretty deep!"
Kincaid: "Damnit! Didn't President Medina ban those damn weapons of mass destruction?!" Gonzago: "Someone call the president! Seriously, those talons didn't do anything more than cosmetic and mental damage, as Canada seems to be freaking out that he has blood coming from his forehead. Poe is just laughing at him, as anger has taken over Canada's Hero. He runs right at Poe, but he waits for Canada, as he scoops him up and....
.....Death Valley Driver! Poe nails a devastating DVD on a loose cannon Canada's Hero and now covers him....
1.... 2.... .....NO! Canada's Hero was able to kick out just in the nick of time! Poe takes hold of Canada now and hits a beautiful tiger driver into the cover....
1.... 2.... .....and Canada's Hero kicks out yet again! Poe brings Canada back up to his feet and hoists him up in a gorilla press! But wait! Canada's Hero wiggles free from the gorilla press and pushes Jarod Poe into the ropes. Canada brings the boot up.....
....but Poe catches it, grabs hold of Canada, spins around and hits a beautiful capture suplex! Poe now takes hold of Canada and whips him into the corner. He runs right after him, as he goes for a.....
....NO! Canada's Hero was able to move out of the way, as Jarod Poe kisses turnbuckle, and as he ricochets off of the corner, Canada grabs him from behind and hits a huge German suplex.....
.....and he holds on as he hits another one.... .....and a third one into the bridge..... 1.... 2.... .....and Poe kicks out! Canada quickly runs to the corner, climbs up..... .....and is able to finally hit the moonsault with exact precision, as he lands directly on top of Poe and covers....
1.... 2.... ......NO! Jarod Poe kicks out JUST in the nick of time! Canada now goes on the offense once more, grabbing hold of Poe's ankle and....
.....he's got it! He's got the Canadian Punisher, that deadly ankle lock, hooked on Poe! Poe's face is showing some discomfort, as the referee is asking him if he wants to call it quits here. But Poe is able to fight it and hop up on his other foot! Canada's Hero is shocked as Poe is yelling at him. Canada now spins Poe by his foot, as he lunges for a punch.....
.....but Jarod Poe catches the punch and with Canada's momentum, tosses him into the ropes. On the return, Canada is lifted up and....
.....is crying in pain after Poe hit that devastating inverted atomic drop, the Breath Stealer, on Canada's Hero. Poe quickly kicks Canada in the midsection, grabs hold of him in a double underhook, and starts to lift him up and....
(Elizabeth gets on the apron and starts taunting Poe with her cleavage.) Kincaid: "That's it baby! Show the goods! Show daddy the goods!" Gonzago: "Elizabeth just saved her man from certain defeat as Poe was about to end it with his Fading Light finisher, a deadly double underhook piledriver. Poe is now walking over to her and.....
.....grabs her by her hair! Jarod Poe's got Elizabeth by her hair as the referee walks over and yells for him to let go! But as Poe is about to comply....
.....waiting in the wings is Canada's Hero, as he kicks Poe in the sternum, and hits a deadly DDT! Canada's Hero rolls up Poe....
.....but the referee still counts as Canada uses the ropes as leverage as he covers..... 1..... 2.... 3!!! Canada's Hero stole the win here tonight, as he used the ropes and Elizabeth to defeat Jarod Poe!"
Jones: "Here is your winner, and advancing to the second round of the WRESTLING LEAGUE SERIES......CANADA'S HERO!!!"
(Cue up: "O' Canada" as the referee raises Canada's Hero hand. Elizabeth enters the ring and celebrates with her man.)
Gonzago: "I can't believe that the referee didn't see Canada's Hero use the ropes to secure the win here tonight! Jarod Poe had this match won and was robbed blind!"
Kincaid: "Everything's legal in the ring Emanuel, just as long as you can get away with it. Besides, if Poe can have his metal talons, why can't Canada's Hero have his woman?"
Gonzago: "Come on Roger, you know damn well Jarod Poe wasn't going to win because of steel surgically implanted in his hand. Does having The Claw help Golem in his matches?"
Kincaid: "That's different: Golem home grows that baby, so it's au natural. And Jarod Poe could of used the steel some more if he knew he was going to lose like that tonight."
Gonzago: "It's a damn shame that Jarod Poe isn't making it to the second round, as Canada's Hero pulls the cheap upset here tonight."
