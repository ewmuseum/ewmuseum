NWL Combat Episode 4, part 2

March 2004

From Roseland Ballroom in New York, New York Kincaid: "Fans, I don't know what to make of this all, but Emanuel Gonzago looks like he won't be joining us for the rest of the night. Hello everyone, I'm Roger Kincaid and welcome to another edition of NWL Combat. Damn.....I'm sorry folks, but I just don't know what to do because Gonzago is usually the play-by-play guy and I don't know if I could fill his shoes.....but I'll try my best. As Manny was stating before he was rudely interrupted, we've got two WLS matches here tonight, as Dakota Smith and Karl Brown go one on one, Maelstrom and that assclown Lars Magellan go at it, and in the main event, The Distributor of Pain and Pulsar face off to become the first ever NWL International Champion. But that's not it folks, as we have the new NWL Television champion, Jarod Poe, who went through nine other men in the Rumble in the Jungle match to become the first ever champion, set to defend his title against ring veteren Hacker. Other matches tonight include the ring debut of The American, set to face off against another man making his debut here in the NWL, The Josh. Plus two rookies going at it, as the red hot John Doe squares off against Ryan 'The American Eagle' Cruz. But in our first match here tonight, two men who are also making their in ring debut, are set to face off as Luster, a man highly known in the WFW promotion, will wrestle a ring veteran making his return to the professional sport, Cameron Love. It's going to be an interesting match and I just only hope I can do it justice. So if you can, Darius Jones, please start the match.....damn......."
(Cut to: Inside the ring, as ring announcer Darius Jones, dressed in a nicely pressed tuxedo, stands with microphone in hand.)
Jones: "The following match is scheduled for one fall......" (Cue up: "Funkytown" by Disco Fever. The lights go dark as little spotlights and a disco ball flash throughout the ballroom. Out walks Luster, struting to the music, as the fans boo him loudly. He enters the ring and poses the fans, which gets an even unwanted response by the fans.)
Jones: "Introducing first, from Atlanta, Georgia, he is 6' 6" tall and weighs in at 250 lbs., here is.......LUSTER!!!"
Kincaid: "It seems the fans aren't impressed by this once porn star turned professional wrestler. He'll have to prepare himself against a man who knows his way around a wrestling ring...."
Jones: "And his opponent...." (Cue up: "Do Ya Think I'm Sexy" by Rod Steward. Out walks Cameron Love, wearing a glittery pink robe and heart-shaped glasses.)
Jones: "From San Diego, California, he stands 6' 5" tall and weighing in at 267 lbs., here is.......CAMERON LOVE!!!"
(The fans start cheering for him, as he has a mic in hand. Love slowly walks to the ring, as he starts speaking.)
Love: "I know, I know. Finally, New York gets to see Cameron Love live and in person!" (The fans give a small pop to Love.) Love: "Hey you, in the ring! You may call yourself Luster, but you certainly don't have half the shine that I do. The TRUE golden boy is coming down to show just how it's done!"
(Cameron Love takes off his robe and glasses, throws down the microphone, and runs to the ring. He slides underneath the bottom rope and starts fighting it out with Luster. The referee then signals for the bell to start the match.)
Kincaid: "And both men go at it, as we get this match underway! Both men are swinging away, landing lefts and rights upon one another. Love's got the advantage now, with Luster against the ropes. Love shoots Luster across the ring, and as he hits the ropes, takes him down with a lunging clothesline. Luster is back up, but Love sends Luster back down with a standing dropkick with authority! Luster is coming back for more, but apparently hasn't learned his lesson as Love awaits him, scoops him up off his feet, and back right down to the mat with a devastating bodyslam! Cameron Love goes up to the top, waves to the fans, launches off and....
.....a beautiful top-rope legdrop! Love now goes for the cover.... 1.... 2.... ......but Luster finds some way to kick out. You'll never accuse Luster of being limp in the ring. Love now brings Luster up to his feet....
....and Cameron Love will not be pleasing any hometown honeys here in New York City, as Luster gave him his just deserts with a low blow. That even hurt me by just watching it! Luster now thinks he can just end it right here, as he sets up for the Funk and Wagner, that huge sitdown powerbomb. Luster sets Love up on his shoulders....
.....I don't think so! Cameron Love flips to his feet! Luster turns around and goes for a clothesline, but Love ducks underneath. But just as Luster turns right back towards Love....
......LOVE TAP! Cameron Love just Love Tapped the hell out of Luster! Luster is getting back up miraculously, but just as he does, Cameron Love scoops Luster up off his feet....
.....THE 69 SLAM! That devastating, jumping spinning tombstone piledriver just laid out Luster! Looks like Luster won't be involved in any money shots, as Cameron Love makes the cover....
1.... 2.... 3!!! CAMERON LOVE WINS HIS FIRST MATCH HERE IN THE NWL!!!" Jones: "Here is your winner........CAMERON LOVE!!!" (Cue up: "Do Ya Think I'm Sexy" by Rod Steward as the referee raises Cameron Love's hand in victory as the fans go crazy throughout the Roseland Ballroom. Love then starts posing and grinding his hips to the fans, as they seem to love it.)
Kincaid: "Cameron Love wins the match in very quick succession, as Luster will have to fluff himself up for his next match."
