NWL Combat Episode 2, part 1

From Elks Lodge in Queens, New York (Cut to: Theo hitting The Revelation, a reverse jumping piledriver, on an opponent.) (Cut to: Shawn Hart posing for the fans flamboyantly.) (Cut to: Canada's Hero and Elizabeth boasting in the ring.) (Cut to: The Watcher walking down to the ring very enigmatically.) (Cut to: Jarod Poe hits Fading Light, a double-underhook piledriver on his opponent.) (Cut to: Karl "The Dragon" Brown on the turnbuckle, saluting the fans.) (Cut to: Dakota Smith, as he walks down the ring, focused on his opponent inside with an expressionless gaze.) (Cut to: Lars Magellan sending an opponent through a table with the Beach Bomb.) (Cut to: Maelstrom applying Mortal Sin on his opponent.) (Cut to: Another explosion on the screen. Cut to: Inside the Elks Lodge in Queens, New York, as the usual fans of NWL go frantic in the jammed-packed venue. Cut to:
Inside the ring, where commentator Emanuel Gonzago stands, dressed in a grey
Armani suit and tie, holding a microphone in hand. He looks around the arena, as the fans start chanting manically as usual, "NWL! NWL! NWL!")
Gonzago: "Welcome everyone to the Elks Lodge, in Queens, New York!" (The fans cheer loudly.) Gonzago: "WELCOME TO NWL COMBAT!" (The fans start popping loudly once again.) Gonzago: "Once again I am as always Emanuel Gonzago...." (The fans start chanting in unison, "MANNY! MANNY! MANNY!") Gonzago: "And tonight fans, we have five great second round matches of the Wrestling League Series
Qualifying Tournament, where only five of the ten men you will see here tonight will go on into the WLS, who will then all do battle to determine our very first NWL World Heavyweight Champion."
(The fans start chanting "NWL! NWL! NWL!" once again.) Gonzago: "We have names like Maelstrom! We have names like The Watcher! Minion! Dakota Smith! And many, MANY more in store for you tonight. We also have two singles competition matches to get to right before the second round matches. But wrestling fans, before we start with our first match, allow me to introduce to you, the owner and president of the National Wrestling League, please welcome Andrew
Medina!" (Cue up: "Out of Control" by Hoobastank. Out walks the esteemed president of the NWL, as he seems to be carrying something wrapped in a velvet cloth. He acknowledges the wrestling fans in attendance, shaking hands of the few along the way. He then climbs up the steel steps and enters the ring. President Medina walks over to
Emanuel Gonzago and shakes his hand.)
Gonzago: "Welcome Mr. Medina. There seems to be something you are carrying there, may I ask what it is?" Medina: "Well Emanuel, I wanted to take this oppotunity to present to everyone here in the Elks Lodge and all our wrestlers in the National Wrestling League what they have been working so hard to win. I worked with some of the best wrestling belt designers in our sport, and following my specific design to capture the essence of what it is to truly be the best, I present to everyone here tonight the National
Wrestling League World Heavyweight Championship Belt!"
(President Andrew Medina takes the cloth off of a beautiful gold World Heavyweight Championship Belt. He raises it up in the air, proudly displaying it to everyone in attendance. He then brings it back down to his side, carrying it in his arm.)
Gonzago: "That is one beautiful piece of gold Mr. Medina. Truly that is something everyone here in the NWL will set their goals in achieving."
Medina: "I want to take this oppotunity Emanuel if I may to say thank you to all the wrestlers who are currently under the NWL. It's been a hard and tough road so far and I thank those who stood by us as we continue to establsh ourselves as one of the best wrestling leagues in our sport today. I know that some of you are looking for big screen TVs, fancy entranceways with spectacular fireworks display, and huge venues that bring in tens of thousands of avid wrestling fans. But I felt that with the National Wrestling League, we need to start from the ground up and I want everyone here tonight to give themselves a round of applause as you have been most terrific in sticking around thus far."
(The fans start clapping in respect to President Medina's words.) Medina: "I will make this promise to each and every one of you here tonight: I will do whatever it takes to bring to you some of the greatest wrestling you have ever seen in your lives. I know most presidents and owners of other promotions wish to thrust themselves in the limelight, taking the focus on what's truly important: our wrestlers. But you will not see that with me. I came here today to show what everyone's devotion to the NWL created. I just hope the wrestler who wins this
World title when all of this is said and done represents what this sport truly is and represents each and every one of you here tonight. Thank you everyone for giving us a chance. God bless you all and enjoy the rest of the show."
(Cue up: "Out of Control" by Hoobastank. President Medina shakes hands with Emanuel Gonzago as they both exit the ring together. Cut to: The Crow's Nest, as Roger
Kincaid, dressed in a No Fear T-shirt and a pair of blue jeans sits at the commentator's table.)
