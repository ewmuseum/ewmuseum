NWL Combat Episode 3, part 1

March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
recent NWL Combat Ep. 5 Previous NWL Combat Ep. 4 Next NWL Combat Ep. 6 Episode 3 LIVE! From Hammerstein Ballroom in New York, New York (Camera fades in to a press conference being held at The Hammerstein Ballroom before the show. President
Andrew Medina has set up a podium and conference room to accommodate the reporters gathered, cameras and notepads at the ready.)
Gonzago (V/O): "Earlier today, President Andrew Medina was holding a press conference concerning the surge of interest in the National Wrestling League and its
Wrestling League Series event. He also introduced one of the men in the WLS,
The Watcher, to the press."
(The NWL owner walks over and stands at the podium.) Medina: "I'd like to now introduce all of you to a man who has jumped out of the pack quickly in the
National Wrestling League, thus far. He is one of the finalists in the NWL
Wrestling League Series and has a match tonight for that very Series, against none other than Maelstrom! Ladies and gentleman, I speak of The Watcher!"
(Watcher comes out onto the stage wearing a pair of slacks and a blue collared shirt. His mask is on, covering his head, and he looks from left to right as the flashbulbs go off.
Watcher then stands at the podium in silence, looking around for a bit as the reporters all quiet down, eventually nobody is saying a word, Watcher included. Finally, Watcher taps the microphone.)
Watcher: "Um....it's good to be here today. Are there any questions..?” (Some of the reporters speak up, causing mini-frenzy, shouting questions out. Watcher points a finger to one.) Reporter: "Watcher, Dale Einstein from American Wrestling Magazine. What do you think your chances are against an opponent the caliber of Maelstrom?"
Watcher: (shrugs) "I think I have as good a chance as anyone does...as anyone ever has. There isn't any reason why I cannot go out to the ring and defeat Maelstrom tonight, and vice versa."
(The reporters start up again, like bees. Watcher points at another one.) Reporter: "Hi, Jane Aldward from Wrestling Illustrated. You've seemingly come out of nowhere onto the wrestling industry. What is your background? Where did you come from?"
Watcher: "I'm not really one to divulge my history to the masses, as it doesn't pertain to the events of tonight. Next question."
(Watcher points.) Reporter: "Raymond Jude of Squared Circle Interactive. Do you think you can win the Wrestling
League Series, and become the first-ever NWL World Champion?"
Watcher: "Just as with my match tonight, I feel I have as good a chance as any of the finalists at winning the World Heavyweight Championship. I will perform to the best of my abilities, and when the time comes, we will find out if it is enough."
(Watcher is about to point to another reporter, but a man leaning against the back wall of the conference room chimes in.)
Reporter:" I have a question..." Watcher: "Alright, go ahead." Reporter: "I don't care who you think you are, Watcher. I want to know why you think you can run and hide here in the NWL, when you should know well that there is NO place you can hide once you've been in the wrong circles."
(Watcher looks at the man; unmoving, unspeaking. He then speaks up after a few tense seconds pass, all reporters turning to look at the man, who has a black trench coat on.)
Watcher: "What? I don't know what you're talking about." Reporter: "Oh, don't take me to be one of these foolish, 'inquisitive' reporters, Watcher. I'm not one of them....I hit much closer to home."
(The man grins, smoothing back his trimmed, black/gray hair before taking out a fedora hat from behind his back and putting it on, his smile growing wider. Watcher leans forward as he looks at the man.)
Man: "Yes, Watcher....I think you get my drift." Watcher: "You bastard...who are you? What do you want with me?" Man: "Stop playing dumb, it only makes you look weak. Much weaker than you apparently are." (Watcher now steps around the podium, walking down the steps of the platform. The man steps in front of the exit to the room, a hand on it.)
Man: "We're watching you....we don't forget....he doesn't forget his own." Medina: "Security! Restrain that man!" Watcher: "He'll wish he did." Man: "Betrayal does not bode well....you're only sealing your fate." (The man pushes on the door's lever and slips out of the room before Security can reach him. Watcher darts forward, running to the back of the conference room and shoving Security aside as he bursts out of the room. All the reporters jump out of their seats to follow. When they get into the hall, they hear a groan from a man and rush around the corner to find Watcher standing over a man in a black trench coat and fedora. The man looks to have been struck down to the ground.)
Watcher: "You...you're not him. You're just a plant." Man: "I'm just a reporter. Who are you talking about?" Watcher: "But where did he go..?" Man: "I'm just a reporter. I was late coming to see this press conference YOU were supposedly speaking at. I didn't expect to get RUN OVER!"
(One female reporter from the bunch breaks through the pack. Watcher is looking around the hallway in a daze, standing there.)
Reporter: "Kelvin? Where have you been? You were supposed to be here a half hour ago!" Kelvin the Reporter: "There was a delay at the subway. I had to take a cab through traffic. And now this...this maniac comes in and HITS me as I was coming around the corner!" (The reporter looks up none too pleased at Watcher, who only snaps out of his daze as she speaks.) Reporter: "What did you do to him?" Watcher: "I thought he was that man....that man! This isn't him, though. He must be a plant." Reporter: "He isn't any plant! He works for Wrestling Grapevine and his name is Kelvin Cooper! I don't know what 'MAN' you're searching for, but this isn't him!"
Watcher: "He's wearing the same clothes!" (The reporter gets up and starts yelling.) Reporter: "There are a million people in this city! Do you expect them ALL to wear different clothes?! I'm sorry if we don't all have our own separate costumes and masks to put on in the morning!!"
(Watcher looks at her.) Watcher: "I'm sorry...but this is a waste of time." (The reporter looks further outraged.) Reporter: "I'm sorry if we aren't all 'worthy' opponents for you to ASSAULT! You're a DISGRACE,
Watcher! And you'd better be sure this man isn't injured, or you can bet your
ASS will be in COURT!"
Watcher: "He fell when I knocked into him. I certainly wouldn't call that assault, miss!" Reporter: "Oh we'll see! WE'LL SEE WATCHER!!" (The lady helps up her cohort and storms off.) Kelvin: "If you can't even tell the difference between a reporter and a wrestler, then you don't stand a chance against Maelstrom."
(Kelvin walks away, dusting himself off. Reporters are stunned at this incident, but not for long.) Reporter #2: "Watcher! Who was that man? What did he mean?" Reporter #3: "Who are you? What is the connection between you two?" Reporter #4: "Who was he referring to? Did you know him?" Reporter #5: "What possessed you to assault Kelvin Cooper?" Reporter #6: "Will this affect you in your match against Maelstrom?" (Watcher is surrounded by the reporters, all barking their questions out to him. He breaks through them all and runs down the hallway, looking around as he does so.)
