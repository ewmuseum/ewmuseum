NWL Combat Episode 0: Special Preview Edition, part 1

January 2004

From Elks Lodge in Queens, New York (Cue up: ”Out Of Control” by Hoobastank as just the first guitar rift starts, playing in a loop. The letter N starts to come into view from the left side of the screen, as more letters fade in next to it spelling the word “National”. The word fades out and the letter W comes into view from the top right corner of the screen. More letters fade in next to it spelling the word ‘Wrestling’. The word fades out and the letter L comes into view from the bottom of the screen. More letters fade in next to it spelling the world ‘League’. Then, all three words fly off into the distance for a bit as the lyrics start, somewhat disappearing from view. Suddenly, the words fly right back into view and read from top to bottom, “National Wrestling League”.
They then fly forward and out of view. Then the word “Presents…” flies into view for a few seconds and then right out of view. Just as the song starts to pick up fast and loud, a loud explosion shows on the screen, with the logo for
NWL Combat appearing from the fires.)
(Cut to: Theo hitting The Revelation, a reverse jumping piledriver, on an opponent.) (Cut to: Shawn Hart posing for the fans flamboyantly.) (Cut to: The Watcher walking down to the ring very enigmatically.) (Cut to: Jarod Poe hits Fading Light, a double-underhook piledriver on his opponent.) (Cut to: Dakota Smith, as he walks down the ring, focused on his opponent inside with an expressionless gaze.)
(Cut to: Maelstrom applying) (Cut to: Another explosion on the screen. Cut to: Inside the Elks Lodge in Queens, New York, as the seats in attendance are filled to the brim. The camera pans around the fans, as some of them brought signs with them tonight.)
(Cut to: The first sign reading “New York loves NWL!”) (Cut to: A second sign reading, “Time to breach the impasse!”) (Cut to: One final sign reading, “Love me Phenom!”) (Cut to: Inside the ring, where none other than Emanuel Gonzago, dressed in a sharp Armani suit and tie with his hair slicked back, stands with microphone in hand. As he holds it with both his hands, he looks around the audience, as fans start chanting, “NWL! NWL! NWL!” Emanuel then begins to speak.)
Gonzago: ”Welcome everyone to the Elks Lodge and welcome to NWL COMBAT!!!” (The fans start cheering like crazy.) Gonzago: ”I am Emanuel Gonzago…” (The fans now start chanting, “GONZAGO! GONZAGO! GONZAGO!” Emanuel nods his head in appreciation, as the fans clap and cheer.) Gonzago: ”Thank you. Hello everyone, I’m Emanuel Gonzago and tonight, you will see a special preview edition of our show. On this show tonight, we have three wonderful matches, showing off the best this sport has to offer. Now, allow me to introduce to you the man who brought life to the National Wrestling League, President Andrew Medina!”
(Cue up: ”Out Of Control” by Hoobastank as President Medina, dressed in a nice business suit, walks out from the back to a huge applause. As Medina walks down to the ring, he shakes the hands of fans along the way. Finally, he makes his way into the ring, walking over to
Emanuel. President Medina shakes his hand, then takes the microphone as Emanuel hands it over to him. The fans once again start chanting, “NWL! NWL! NWL!” from the top of their lungs.)
Medina: ”Thank you everyone. I appreciate all of you for coming out to the NWL’s premiere event. I think this time around you’ll appreciate more of what we are all about compared to the WWL. This will probably be one of the few public appearances from me as I want you all to focus your raw energy on the great superstars we have. Hopefully, if you enjoy tonight’s show, you will come back next time and continue to do so. So please, sit back, relax, and enjoy the show. Thank you.”
(Cue up: “Out Of Control by Hoobastank as President Medina waves his hand to everyone and hands the microphone back over to Emanuel. Both end up leaving the ring and to the back as the ring crew begins preparations for the first match.)
