MY Opinion of the NWL.

13 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ShawnHartXXX — 13 April 2004 Hey kids! Remember me? I'm the guy that quit this circuit in Janurary. As you can see, I still like to pop in for some action here and there, so I thought, "Hey... how's about poppin' in for this NWL business?" I mean, you've got Maelstrom doing his thing, the WATCHER, some new guys I've never really heard of, heck...there's GOTTA be some action here! R-right? Maybe? ...Maybe not.
Fact is, I've been following this fed since it opened and in the beginning, my hopes were high. But after kicking back and watchin' things develop over the last couple months, I've decided this place is in SERIOUS need of HELP! You're all doing your thing and that's jiggy like jelly beans, but you can't deny FACT, and fact is that the NWL has become stagnant.
Heh, what happened to the good ol' days when people had the balls to try something different? Exactly when was it decided that rosters had to become so cookie-cutter in the way they do business? It's a game, people... and games are supposed to be FUN! Maybe you people just need a refresher course. Why don't you go check out MY stuff from the good ol' WFW. You don't have one now, but if you listen to what I'm saying, maybe you'll find that all-elusive CLUE!
