About the NWL The National Wrestling League is a league founded on tradition, commitment, excellence, and enjoyment of a fantasy wrestling league. Everyone is welcomed to join, as long as they provide and exemplify exactly what the National Wrestling League was founded on.
Everyone who joins the National Wrestling League is encouraged in originality and uniqueness, while at the same time enjoying what they are doing, not just for competition, but for their own selves. Those who join or are thinking of joining must know that we only keep members who are active in the league, contribute in their own special way, but above all else, know that this is all in fun and enjoyment of this game.
We believe that the National Wrestling League is defined by its main essence; its members. The members in the National Wrestling League define and represent what it stands for. Without our members, the National Wrestling League would not exist.
2. History Founded and established on Janurary 2004, the history of the National Wrestling League is a story of both success and failure.
The owner of the National Wrestling League, [name withheld], started in this business when he opened the underground wrestling promotion that was a cult hit in the heart of the New York City; New York Championship Wrestling. Started in 1989, wrestlers all over the tri-state area came for the love of the sport and its tradition. Although not without controversy, New York Championship Wrestling was a success. However, in 1995, New York Championship Wrestling closed down to an incident which it could never recover from.
It wasn't until in late 2001 that the owner of the World Wrestling League, [name withheld], put one of the most famous wrestling promotions up for sale after running and operating it for 8 years. Medina saw the oppotunity to start over, as he purchased and took over the World Wrestling League. For about two years, World Wrestling League became stronger than ever, having one of the biggest and deepest rosters in all of wrestling. However, it wasn't until Medina came down with cancer that he gave the reigns over to one of World Wrestling League's greatest champions of all time, Sean Edmunds.
Unfortunately, things weren't working out, and against doctor orders, Medina decided to help Edmunds and create a new way to draw interest back into the World Wrestling League. Along with Vertigo Armstrong, these three men divided the World Wrestling League into regions, seperating its deep roster in three. However, illness caught up with Medina, and after being hospitalized, knew he no longer had the strength to be a part of this sport. It was then that the World Wrestling League gradually faded into obscurity.
Fortune smiled upon Medina however. For over a year, he battled cancer and survived. Slowly recovering his strength, his passion for the sport rekindled. Feeling that fate gave him another chance, Medina spent the few months planning something new for the sport. Rumors ran rampart, from the rebirth of New York Championship Wrestling to bringing back the World Wrestling League. However, Medina felt reliving the past would only cause more problems. It was then, on Janurary 2004, the world saw the birth of the National Wrestling League.
The National Wrestling League is now at its infancy, starting out as New York Championship Wrestling did; small venues and low-key. But with its roster expanding and its popularity rising, the National Wrestling League will soon be going live nationwide, traveling around North American to bring its brand of old-school tradition meets new-school excitement wrestling to all of its fans.
3. Rules These are the rules for the National Wrestling League. If you can not meet these rules, then the National Wrestling League is not for you.
Have fun. This is the most important rule in the National Wrestling League. Yes, this is a league of competition, where people will win and lose. But note this is not a life or death situation. Have fun with your writings and participation with the league and its members.
All matches are judged based on roleplays alone. No angles are used in the ruling of matches. A roleplay is a piece of written work that gets your wrestler across. The most common type of roleplay is a promo. A promo does what is stands for; it promotes your character, giving the reader an idea of what your character is all about.
All roleplays are judged on quality, not quantity. That isn't to say that 5 decently written roleplays will lose to one great roleplay. However, consider that while there is no length or roleplays requirement, note that we look for originality and uniqueness in the writings. Also, it would be best to write for yourself first and foremost, as roleplays that are done as such usually come out as the best.
All non-match roleplays are encouraged. They not only help with the story and development of your character, it also helps in the consideration of who gets title shots, as it shows your participation in the league.
Angles are definitely encouraged. They also help with the story and development of your character, as well as the creation and advancement of storylines. An angle can be anything from spots in a match, the ending of a match, or interviews at an event. Angles also help in the consideration of title shots.
Rankings on the website are based on wins or losses. However, they do not reflect on who gets title shots. Title shots are given based on not just ranking, but also participation with roleplays and angles, as well as storylines during the time.
It is encouraged that if you can not roleplay for a match due to real life situations, please notify the president on this. You will then not be put on the next card. We stress the importantance of your real life and do not wish for the National Wrestling League to be a distraction in any way.
Failure in roleplaying for a match the first time will be given a warning. Further failures will result in not being given matches on future cards, no title shots, and in the end, will result in your termination with the National Wrestling League.
In roleplays, unless you are given permission by the member, under no circumstances can you roleplay another character that isn't yours in your writings. The first time this is seen will result in one and only one warning. Further infractions will result in said roleplays becoming null in void.
All angles turned in are subjected to it being edited for format and/or it not being used at all. The league president will do their best to contact you on each angle. It is also encouraged when involving other members in an angle, that you include them in the development of them. The league president will try to keep every member in the know, as long as it doesn't conflict with certain angles and storylines.
4. Application Applying to the National Wrestling League is simple. Below you will find the link to the application. Fill it all out to the best of your ability. Also note that the application requires a sample roleplay. You must give a sample roleplay in your application in order to be considered to be a part of the National Wrestling League. Failure in doing so will have your application be denied. Once the application is turned in, it will be reviewed. If you are accepted into the National Wrestling League, you will be notified as soon as possible.
