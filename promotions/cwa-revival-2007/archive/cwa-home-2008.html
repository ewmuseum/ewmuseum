Born in 1984, the CWA has been through many restarts. Recently, the CWA has returned after a one year layoff and is now on its way to rebuilding its reputation as one of the best wrestling federations online. Much like a fiery phoenix, the CWA rises from the ashes and is poised to shock the e-fed world once again.
Home to some e-fed legends, at least as much as a legend can be playing this game, the CWA is on its way to once again being the best of the best. So feel free to sign up, get to know everyone here and enjoy yourself. That is really what this game is all about, after all.
We do not have an application set up, so just sign up for the forums and post a bio for your wrestler (s) and get yourself started.
The Championship Wrestling Association prides itself on quality results and the ability to make each other better week in and week out. It has been around in some form or another for twenty years, basically starting out as a combination of audio tapes, written results and matches in my best friend's basement as a teenager.
Where I once dreamt of a vast fedding empire, I now am happy to run a small show with a few devoted fedders who are here to have a good time and we all understand that real life can get in the way of online wrestling.
