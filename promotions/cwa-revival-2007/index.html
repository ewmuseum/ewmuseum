A fantasy federation online from October 2007 under the old name C.W.A., with a weekly Overdrive and a Battle of the Warriors in January 2008, whose own front page says it was born in 1984 and has returned after a one-year layoff, and whose last dated champions are from July 2008. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The CWA of 2007 and 2008 is the same address and the same owner as Anarchy Wrestling, and its own front page says it was born in 1984, has been through many restarts and has returned after a one-year layoff. Its first scheduled Overdrive is 30 October 2007 and its World title list opens with Wren SilverPhoenix a week earlier; it brings back no belt of the earlier CWA or of Anarchy Wrestling.

Four of its cards are in the mirror, Overdrives and a Battle of the Warriors in January 2008, with tag, Aerial and Diva titles beside the World belt; the last dated champions are from 6 July 2008, and the fed never announces an ending.

Affiliations

Anarchy Wrestling (AW, Anarchy, 2005)

Predecessor to CWA (Revival, 2007)
The CWA of 2007 and 2008 comes back on Anarchy Wrestling's tenant after a one-year layoff with new World, Aerial, Diva and tag lists beginning on 23 October 2007; none of Anarchy Wrestling's titles continues.

Championship Wrestling Association (CWA, Association, 2003)

Shared continuity with CWA (Revival, 2007)
The 2008 CWA's front page says it was born in 1984, has been through many restarts and has returned; it carries the name and the owner of the 2003 and 2004 CWA but none of its titles.
