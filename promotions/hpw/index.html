Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

HPW opened 24 June 1997, immediately after its own operator's previous e-fed, APW, wound down. Its sole surviving document is a recruitment ad hosted on an Angelfire page.

That ad allowed up to three original wrestlers per member, ran two cards a week and one pay-per-view a month, and asked every member to roleplay two or three times a week and “give this fed your all.”

The same operator moved on to a third e-fed, VPWF, that October. No card, roster, or results document survives for HPW itself; whether it ever ran a single match isn't in the surviving record.
