AAWA: Wicked Wednesday Wrestling

(Scene fades in on 13,000 screaming fans in the Pensacola-Dome. Some wear AAWA shirts, other wear black and grey shirts with a blurred out ‘Focus’ on the front. These are typically sold at CWO shows) MIKE WILLIAMS: YES FANS…IT IS INDEED TRUE! CWO AND AAWA……TOGETHER IN SOME FASHION AS OF LAST FRIDAY! THE FOCUS MAKING HIS DEBUT HERE IN GRAND STYLE, AND ALREADY THERE ARE RUMORS STARTING ABOUT OF HIM HAVING TO DEFEND HIS CWO BELT AGAINST SOME OF THE AAWA’S FINEST! JM: I PREDICT THAT BELT WILL BE IN OUR LOVIN’ HANDS BY THE END OF SEPTEMBER MIKE! BUT, CHANGIN’ THE SUBJECT. THERE’S PLENTY OF ACTION WE NEED TO COVER HERE TONIGHT! SUCH AS IRON AND STEEL BATTLING THE COLDFRONT, DEATH DEMON TAKING ON YOUR ‘FAN-FLAVOR’ OF THE MINUTE…THE FOCUS…AND RDS GETTING HIS PANSY PUNK BUTT WHIPPED UP THE RINGPOSTS BY THE MAN HIMSELF…JEFF….EASTHOPE! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with no time limit. Currently in the ring, weighing 219 pounds, from Anytown. USA, Johnny Glide! (jobber pop…in other words… a completely silent reaction.) JM: Ahh..the good ol’ mat-mop making his return I see. Who did he have to sleep with to get on this show anyway? Announcer: And his opponent... ('Welcome to the Jungle' by Guns N' Roses plays as the fans boo) weighing 275 pounds, from A place you that you will never know, Chris 'Predator' Moore! MW: Moore quite an accomplished athlete, with several wins over the top talent of the AAWA, including Jimmy Heat… JM: Moore’s good…even machine-like in his perfection, but there’s no comparing him to Heat. (Bell rings) MW: Moore with a stiff right….double axhandle, and a gourdbuster! 1! . . . 2! . . . (on the third count Moore lifts Glide off the mat) JM: Heheh. I like this guy’s personality Mike. He knows how to handle young punks like Glide. MW: Moore going for a lariat…duck by Glide, and a roundhouse right by the youngster! (Moore stares down Glide with a killer’s look before taking him down in a Full-Nelson takedown.) JM: Uh-oh. Those are the words Glide would be uttering if he wasn’t eating the mat. MW: Moore with a release…and stomping away on Glide….Cross-body by Moore! JM: Shades of Carlos Maia….one of those CWO goons. (Chris Moore rides all over the body of Glide on the mat, before quickly applying and releasing the Figure-4, jumping to his feet, and spitting on him.) JM: Ooh-hoo! I love it! MW: Moore fairly confident with his ability to dominate this match…Glide with a DDT! NO! Moore powering out, and backdropping Glide to the outside! (Moore waits patiently in the ring, as Glide makes it in on the 13 count) MW: Moore with a lariat to Glide! And the youngster’s back outside! Moore following through the ropes….and Glide greets him with a flying elbow! (Moore stumbles against the timekeeper’s table.) MW: Glide on the apron! Flying clothesline! NO! (Moore moves out of the way, and clubs the back of the descending Glide, adding extra force to his trip through the table.) JM: Ahahaha! I can tell I’m going to be happy by night’s end! (Moore rolls back to the ring, followed by Glide. Moore grabs Glide by the hair) MW: Moore with a side suplex! Another! A pickup by Moore…..DDT! JM: He’s just taking this schmuck apart, Mike! MW: Moore with Glide! The Prey! JM: Great move! MW: That free-falling piledriver allowing for a cover…. 1! . . . 2! . . . 3! MW: And Moore comes away victorious tonight! JM: Even with the lame level of talent he fought, he managed to keep me entertained! The man just puts up a great match! Case in point! (Footage of Gide getting slammed in mid-air through the ring-side table is played in slow-motion, and repeatedly rewound, played again, and basically dissected by Miller as he praises Moore) Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen! Your winner in 9:13….CHRIS….’PREDATOR….MOOOOOORE! (Welcome to the Jungle by Guns N’ Roses plays to boos) (fade to commercial) Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team matchup is scheduled for one fall with no time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('Iron Man' by Black Sabbath plays as the fans boo) at a combined weight of 668 pounds, Iron and Steel and , Iron and Steel! (Iron and Steel come to the ring, grab the mic..) IRON: SHUT YOUR HOLES…AND WELCOME THE SINGLE GREATEST MAN IN THE SPORT OF WRESTLING TODAY…..’ROWDY’ RYAN… (The fans boo un-assuredly at the entrance of ‘Rowdy’ Ryan.) "Rowdy" Ryan- "CUT THE MUSIC!!!!!" (Pause for crowd reaction) "Well, well, well, it seems as though the AAWA is at it again. Having an illegal man come down to ringside and pull me over the top rope so I can't take my rightful place in the E-Cup tournament. SO, to recap. I beat the World Champion in a non-title match and don't receive a title match, on top of that, I have to stand behind a man who couldn't beat my grandmother on her deathbed. Hi Easthope-less! And now the battle royale. This is why I have hired the two men who watched my back when I started out in Pittsburgh. Iron and Steel. (Points to the announcer) And you will announce them as the AAWA WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS!!!!! (Pause for crowd) "Now for the reason we are out here. I have a list of three punks. Three miserable pieces of mucus who I hold responsible for me not being in the E-Cup tournament. Number one- The Phantom... I brought you in here, pal!!! You think for one second that I am going to let you live after stabbing us in the back? Guess again! And two- (Points to the broadcast stand) YOU! Yes, you Josh Miller! You sit there in you little chair and tell the folks how much of a loser I am... come on down and prove it!!!!! And last and most certainly least, the "Gigolo"... As the song goes, son... you're just a gigolo and life goes on without you... we intend on proving that by ending your career. Heat, Phantom, Miller.... I hold you three responsible for the "Rowdy" one not getting title shots since I have been here. So I don't give a damn when. I don't give a damn where. I want you three in a match against us three in a Survivor Series style match!!!! If you have any balls what so ever, you will accept by the end of this show tonight." (Drops the mic.. Iron, Steel and Ryan start to leave when Ryan turns back and picks the mic up again) "I almost forgot, Death Demon. I'll be in touch soon about that offer of mine." (Iron and Steel enter the ring as Ryan stands in their corner) MW: What offer?! Folks….I’m at a loss here…..Ryan making his goals very clear here tonight, as well as his alignment with Iron and Steel…. JM: Oh yeah….these guys be bad Mike…..but I have a feeling their beef is with Heat who helped eliminate them! I see no reason for them to be mad at me! I call it like it is, and thus far, Ryan has done nothin’ to impress me! MW: They just may get some revenge here tonight as they face off against Heat’s allies…the ColdFront. Announcer: And their opponents... (Hey Man Nice Shotby Filter plays to a mixed reaction.) at a combined weight of 407 pounds, 'Superstar' Steve White, Kurt Gerstung, , ColdFront! MW: Gerstung made one Heck of a showing last Friday, lasting well over 20 minutes in the Battle Royale, and eliminating some of the men that were shoe-ins with you…. JM: Yeah…well….White assisted me with ridding the ring of that filth that is Rip Daddy Scorpio…in under a minute! Ahahaha! Top *that* Mike! (Bell rings) MW: Steel starting off with Gerstung…..Gerstung with an elbowsmash and runs to the ropes! (On the rebound Steel catches Gerstung with a kick to the midsection,and drops an elbow on the doubled over Gerstung) JM: Wow! That’ll play Hell with your back! MW: Steel with a rather clever move, and a tag to Iron! Powerslam by the big man, followed by a clawhold! (Gerstung reaches the ropes, and quickly darts to the second buckle…leaping off with a spinning leg-lariat.) MW: Air-Gerstung with a move that pays off big-time! Forearm smash! Iron comes back with a whip to the corner! (Gerstung hits with amazing force and slumps over in the corner) JM: Oooh..There’s that back again… MW: Iron with Gerstung up top! Superplex! (Gerstung writhes in pain, holding his back, and tags to Steve White) MW: White with a whip….tripping backbreaker! JM: Steve using that weight of Iron’s against himself, and getting some payback for Gerstung’s back in the process! (White clotheslines Iron as he gets to his feet which just fazes him….White comes right back after him with a running lariat which knocks the big man to this back) MW: What a combination by White, and there’s a sleeper! JM: Iron hits the ropes…yadda yadda….Mike, he should be impressing me right now…and he’s not. MW: Iron with a whip…powerslam with authority! JM: I stand corrected. MW: White with a double-chickenwing…Iron just too powerful and a tag to Steel! (Steel charges in, but is taken down by an armdrag into a backslide by White. Steel gets out by the 1 count.) MW: White with a tag to Gerstung! Double dropkick sends Steel over the top! (Gerstung runs the full-length of the ring before launching himself over for a plancha) (Crowd pop) MW: Gerstung just blowing Steel away with a plancha that must have as much, if not more, force than one of Steel’s powerslams! JM: I’ll admit it…these guys have come a long way, but Iron and Steel should never be counted out. (Gerstung gets back in the ring, with Steve White by his side. Steel rolls back in as Gerstung leaps up top. White doubles Steel over with a kick, and Gerstung performs a moonsault into a DDT) MW: WOW! JM: THE ICEBREAKER! (Iron tries to run-in, but White knocks him to the floor, before heading back to his corner by the 4-count) MW: Gerstung up to the second buckle! Elbow to the back! And a flying stomp! Cover! 1! . . . 2! . . . THR! MW: Shoulder barely up! Gerstung with a dropkick to the knee……Stee’s back down! Handspring moonsault and a tag to White! JM: God…it’s gotta be hard to grab something that just flips around like that….I kinda feel for Steel and Iron right about now. MW: Steel with a tag to Iron….hangman by Iron, and White with a quick escape! White with a whip..NO! Iron with the brakes on, and a double axhandle sends White crashing to the mat! (Iron tags back to Steel) MW: WHAT’S THIS?! STEEL UP ON THE SECOND BUCKLE! FLYING DROPKICK AND A MISS! JM: He shoulda stuck with what he knows! Clubberin’! (White stumbles over to Gerstung for the tag. ‘Gigolo’ Jimmy Heat slowly makes his way ringside.) MW: Heat’s coming down! JM: Act a little more surprised, will ya?! The guy’s only here to boost morale in camp-ColdFront. (Heat takes the steel chair from the wreckage of the table and holds it anxiously) Iron with Gerstung to the ropes! Gerstung drops with a leg-extension! (Iron’s head goes through the second-rope, where Jimmy Heat plants a chair across his forehead) MW: HEAT WITH A CHAIR TO THE HEAD OF IRON AND GERSTUNG UP TOP! (Iron turns arond dazed only to catch a spinning leg lariat from Gerstung right across the throat) MW: BEAUTIFUL MOVE BY GERSTUNG AND A COVER! 1! . . . 2! . . . 3! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen….your winners in 15:35….THE ….COOOOLDFROOONT! (‘More Human Than Human plays as the fans cheer Gerstung and boo White) MW: ColdFront wins, albeit tainted due to interference by Heat! JM: Interference my Baltimorian-Butt! The man plays softball on the side of wrestling, and was simply demonstrating to the fans in row 3A his batting stance when he let one rip……. MW: We’ll return after this short break folks! I have yet to see one thing that warrants gold from this guy. (The Focus stomps away on Demon as he rises to his feet, before applying a DDT to the big man) JM: Alright….I’ve seen ONE thing so far. MW: Demon back up! Irish whip and a huge lariat! JM: My God! Demon’s arm covered The Focus’ entire chest! MW: Demon with a cover! 1! . . . 2! Shoulder up! The Focus holding his own in there, and trying to make Demon play the hit-and-run game… JM: Bad move. With Demon hit-and-run quickly turns into hit-hit-lose. Hah. Ain’t it great! (Death Demon gets to his feet slightly before The Focus, and catches the champ in the face with a stunning forearm.) MW: Demon with a brutal forearm! Ear-ringer!
