AAWA: 'W3'

3 September 1997 (part 1 of 2 — part 2 not in this collection's own held corpus; the fed's own last-known post)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Footage of The Green Goblin tearing apart RDS in their No-DQ match, Super and HobGoblin attacking Jeff Easthope from behind, and The All-Star Wrestler DDT'ing Josh Miller plays as the voice of Mike Williams starts up.) Voice: The Green Goblin.....since the beginning of the AAWA, he's proven a thorn in the side to many...a threat in the form of pure mayhem and sadistic tactics.......(Footage of Goblin tethering RDS by the throat to a ringpost plays) Voice: And as of late, he's proven that birds of a feather, flock together.....(Footage of Easthope getting pummeled by Super and Hobgoblin.) Voice: Even those that brush up against the Motley Crew of The Goblin's and The All-Star Wrestler learn their sick and twisted ways the hard way..... (Footage of Miller beating on the All-Star Wrestler in the Battle Royale plays as it fades to All-Star sneaking up behind Miller and DDT'ing him into limbo) Voice: Tonight.....all these men join forces......on Wicked....Wednesday....WRESTLING! (Screen explodes in a montage of AAWA footage and flaming graphics as 'Starface' by White Zombie plays loudly. The explosions give way to a panning view of the Nashville Arena, which holds about 11'000 fans. Pyrotechnics light up the entrance to the aisleway.) MW: WELCOME LADIES AND GENTLEMEN TO W3! THE SINGLE MOST ACTION-PACKED HOUR OF TELEVISION ON WEDNESDAY NIGHT! TONIGHT WE NOT ONLY HAVE THE GOBLINS AND THE ALL-STAR WRESTLER UP AGAINST THE LIKES OF JEFF EASTHOPE, RDS, JIMMY HEAT.... JM: AND ME! MW: AND JOSH MILLER, BUT YOU'LL SEE THE NEWCOMER CHRIS 'PREDATOR' MOORE FACE OFF AGAINST MIGUEL SANCHEZ, BUT ALSO THE INTERNATIONAL SUPERSTAR DRAGON LEE AGAINST THE MONSTER DEATH DEMON ! JM: I LIKE BOTH GUYS MIKE, BUT NONE OF 'EM CAN HOLD A CANDLE TO THE LIKES OF ME AND EASTHOPE WHIPPING UP A LITTLE TRICK-OR-TREATER BUTT TONIGHT! -=Dan Tucker vs. Steel=- Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with no time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('Iron Man' by Black Sabbath plays as the fans boo) weighing 312 pounds, from Pittsburgh. PA, Steel! (Steel comes down the aisle accompanied by 'Rowdy' Ryan and his usual tag-partner, Iron.) MW: The imposing steel coming down the aisle, with perhaps a little backup in case Tucker has any friends making unscheduled appearances. JM: Tucker? Friends? Doubt it. Announcer: And his opponent... ('Bow Down' by Westside Connection plays as fans that recognize his ability cheer.) weighing 216 pounds, from Florence. AL, Dan Tucker! (Tucker comes to the ring wearing a University of Alabama T-shirt, slapping hands the whole way.) MW: And one of the newcomers here in the AAWA, the high-flying Dan Tucker makes his way into the ring. JM: Yah. See how well he flies with Steel's big mitts down his throat. (bell rings) MW: Tucker with a floatover... JM: And that's a lot of territory to cover. MW: And a sleeper on Steel! Quick escape, and a Tucker's sent to the corner! (Steel charges in, but Tucker spins around him, ending up behind him. He then lifts under Steel's arms like a setup for a Razor's Edge, sets his feet on the second buckle, and thrusts forward....making Steel land squarely on the back of his neck.) MW: TUCKER WITH AN AMAZING SHOW OF STRENGTH! AND AN ALABAMA EDGE! COVER! 1! . . . 2! . . . 3!NO! Shoulder up on the part of Steel, and it appears that was pure instinct folks! JM: This ain't fair! MW: Why?! Because Steel can't rely on Iron to cover his back anymore?! (Steel slumps in the corner as Tucker leaps around him, with his feet on the buckles again, and attempts a belly-to-belly suplex.) MW: Tucker with a belly-to bel....no! Steel sends him down to the mat! JM: Batted like the gnat he is.... MW: Steel with an elbow...and a pickup..... (Steel attempts to slam Tucker to the mat to setup the Steel City Plunge, but Tucker slides out of his grasp and hits the ropes.) MW: Tucker with a head full of steam....and a dropkick! Tucker with headscissors applied......and Steel's simply too strong for him at this point in time. JM: At any point. MW: Tucker with a standing dropkick...and a big miss! Steel with a snap-mare takeover, and a pickup! Poowerbomb! JM: What force! MW: Steel up top! Steel City Plunge! (Tucker rolls out of the way as Steel crashes to the mat spread-eagle) JM: What the?! MW: Tucker withstanding a vicious powerbomb by Steel and comes back with a laying-sleeper! Tucker may pull off a *major* upset here! JM: In his dreams! Quit fantasizing about him whenever he seemingly gets the upperhand! It's *damn* creepy! (Ryan and Iron converse in a concerned fashion outside the ring, as Steel finally escapes the sleep with a roll-over.) MW: And Steel using his weight to just *squash* Tucker by rolling over the smaller man. JM: Yes sir. Steel's impressing me more and more each time I see him whip out those moves from his bag of technical tricks. (Steel stomps on the thigh of the grounded Tucker following it up with a dropping headbutt to the knee.) JM: Well! There's one way to stop that little fly from taking off! MW: Steel taking apart the right leg of Tucker....and applying a clawhold to Tucker! This could end it mighty fast! JM: The way I see it, Mike, he's doing us all a favor. He's covering up the mug of Tucker while also preventing an embarrassing win. (Steel lifts Tucker to his feet with the clawhold.) MW: Steel picking tucker up like he's nothing! (Tucker leaps up while still in the hold, grabs Steel's arm with his legs, and flings Steel on top of his head.) MW: And Tucker comes out with a spinning arm-scissor! Wow! JM: I gotta say, that's a neat little flip there, but it's gonna take more than a flashy takedown to win over the big guy! (Tucker kicks Steel in the small of his back as he gets to his feet, causing Steel to stumble to the corner.) MW: Tucker charging into the corner....and a big running dropkick right to the ribcage of Steel! (Steel clenches his ribs and falls flat on his face.) JM: I ain't seein' this! MW: And Tucker flips the big man over! Cover! 1! . . . 2! . . . 3! (big pop) Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen...your winner in 9:32....DAN.......TUUUUCKERRRRR! (Bow Down plays to cheers.) (At the sound of the bell, Ryan and Iron jump in the ring and proceed to beat down Tucker. Steel gets to his feet and rests on the second buckle.) MW: This is uncalled for! (massive boos) JM: Here's where I take over! Iron with Tucker above his head....and he drops him into the Iron Ore Drop! Steel from the second buckle.......Steel City Plunge! You wanna talk about busted ribs Mike!? There's some for ya! Ahahah! (Scene fades to commercial just as 'Rowdy' Ryan's about to leap off the buckle.) (Footage runs of Ryan landing a flying elbow-smash on Tucker before all three men leave the ring with pinky, ring, and middle fingers raised all the way back down the aisle.) MW: Just moments ago folks, this was the scene in the ring. As you can see, Tucker is obviously injured and was in need of assistance back to the locker rooms, and word is that Ryan and Iron and Steel are backstage trying to find Death Demon....for what...we don't know just yet. -= Death Demon vs. Dragon Lee=- Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with no time limit. Coming down the aisle... (Theme from Battlestar Galactica plays as the fans boo) weighing 225 pounds, from Iwate. Japan, accompanied by Mr. Ishihara, Dragon Lee! (Lee gets in the ring, posing in various stances before swaying his long-black hair back and resting in the corner patiently.) JM: Now Ryan's attitude may have helped him as a person, but his skills are *nothing* compared to the Dragon! MW: Lee has indeed been making quite a name for himself here on the house-show circuit. Recently defeating the champ himself, RDS in the return match to the CWO just yesterday. JM: And I ask, why doesn't this guy get the attention he needs.... Announcer: And his opponent... ('Closer' by NIN plays to a mixed reaction) weighing 470 pounds, from The Land Of Darkness, Death Demon! (The huge Demon walks slowly to the ring ignoring the fans that boo. Demon's built along the lines of Jeep Swenson on a larger scale, and draped in black.) MW: The giant of this sport today making his presence known here tonight.... JM: Well, I should hope so. It's not like the guy can actually be stealthy or anything.... (Split screen of a pre-recorded segment from Demon pulls up on the lefthand side of the television.) A reporter is told to go to a large apartment complex, where he was told he'd have the biggest interview of his life. So he enters the large deserted apartment, there are no windows, the place reeks of urine and feces, and there are 1000 different animals running around the place. Death Demon is sitting there, quietly, patiently, but he is very anxious, to do the interview. The Reporter walks up to the large man: Reporter: Death Demon, what do you think of Rich Clark turning you down? Death Demon: As you all know that I was a loner when I entered the sport, but I soon realized that you need someone to watch your back. But I guess Rich didn't think so, and I respect him for that, I also respect him for his wrestling capabilities. I also respect that you are the 2nd largest man in sports today. Reporter: That was a great win over the CWO's "The Focus". Death Demon: Yes it was, but I thought he was the Bad Ass of the CWO, he didn't impress me. If and when any more of the CWOites wanna come and wrestle with the big boys, come on and lets rumble. Reporter: What of "Rowdy" Ryans offer? What is it? What did he say? Death Demon: ................... (Death Demon leaves) MW: Demon letting on nothing about his relationship to the 3-men from the 3-rivers in that speech. JM: Try and make him. He's better off without those dolts. (bell rings as Lee chokes Demon against the ropes while he steps through them into the ring.) Ref: 1!.....2!......3!.......4!....(Lee breaks the hold as the ref warns him) MW: Lee pressing his luck early on with that blatant choke, and the ref making sure he knows about possible repercussions. JM: Yeah. Like a victory and a slap on the hand fine. (Demon gets to his feet and smashes Lee's face with his elbow, but Lee bounces off the ropes with a spinning heel kick which stuns Demon. Both men remain on their feet.) MW: Amazingly enough, these two are trading blows and are still standing! (Demon whips Lee into the corner with great force.) JM: Did you see Lee fly?! There's gonna be buckle-marks on his back! (Mrl Ishihara jumps on the apron getting the ref's attention. As Lee begs for mercy in the corner.) MW: Demon moving in on Lee, and LEE BLOWS THAT MIST IN THE EYES OF THE GIANT! JM: That's the same stuff that put Clark under for a night! ('Rowdy' Ryan and Iron and Steel make an appearance at the entrance of the aisle...watching the match from a distance.) MW: There's Ryan, Steel and Iron! We may not have to wait too long to see what Demon's decision is! JM: Even Demon has enough brains to stay away from jobbronies like Ryan. Lee with a standing spin-kick....and a Hurricanrana! (Hell/high flying fan pop) JM: It may not have been a pretty one, but with 470 pounds of force behind it, there's no doubting it's power! (Demon gets dizzily up to his feet as Lee moves in for another Hurricanrana.) MW: Lee with another Hurrican....NO! POWERBOMB BY DEMON! JM: He puts the 'power' in front of bomb Mike! MW: Pickup by Demon.....overhead stomachbreaker! And a kick to the midsection! Another powerbomb! JM: WOW! I've never seen him fight like this before! (Demon hoists Lee up to his shoulder and places him up on the top buckle) MW: DEMON DEATH DROP! (Ryan and Co. nod their heads in satisfaction as the fans let out a heel pop.) JM: I THINK THE RING NEARLY CAVED IN! (Mr. Ishihara jumps on the apron distracting the ref again.) MW: Cover by Demon.....and no count for the big guy tonight. JM: Awww...too bad. (laughs) MW: Demon releasing the pin, and charging at Ishihara! JM: With intent on abusing a senior citizen like that, the match should be given to Lee! MW: Lee from behind! Back-brain kick! Lee to the ropes....Asai moonsault! Cover! (As Lee covers Demon he begins to rub the leftover mist in his face.) MW: 1! . . . 2! . . . 3! JM: He wins! Ahaha! A true David overcoming Goliath! I love it! Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen....your winner in 8:13......DRAGON....LEEEEEEE! (Theme from Battlestar Galactica plays to boos from the crowd.) MW: Indeed, Lee with a win here, albeit questionable in every sense of the word..... (Ryan and Co. leave quietly to the locker room as the scene fades to commercial.) -= Chris 'Predator' Moore vs. Miguel Sanchez Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup Is scheduled for one fall with no time limit. Coming down the aisle... ('Welcome to the Jungle' by Guns N' Roses plays as the fans boo) weighing 275 pounds, from A place you that you will never know, Chris 'Predator' Moore! MW: Chris Moore has been literally burning up the circuit with his list of wins over many of the top-stars here in the AAWA. Sicne his debut a few weeks back... JM: Yer a nobody until you beat me....the Doctor of Doom...Josh Miller. Announcer: And his opponent... (Six Foot Foam Skull by Shallow. North Dakota plays as the fans cheer) weighing 243 pounds, from Philadelphia. PA, Miguel Sanchez! (Sanchez comes to the ring slapping hands with his right hand, as his left arm holds a folded table beneath it. Once at ringside he unfolds the table, sets it up, and steps in the ring.) (bell rings) MW: Moore demanded this match after the impressive showing Sanchez put up ant the recent Battle Royale 2-weeks ago.... JM: And it just so happens that included dumping Moore out of the competition. MW: Well Moore isn't the only man who wants a piece of Sanchez. The Vampyre is said to be *very* unhappy with him due to the actions we have on tape... (Split-screen footage rolls as Sanchez lays a folded table across Vampyre and Dan Tucker, and Moonaults himself through it, smashing the table to splinters.) (Moore and Sanchez size each other up before Moore belts Sanchez in the midsection and nails him with the 'Prey' piledriver) JM: THERE IT IS! THE PREY! MW: A cover and a fistful of tights! 1! . . 2! . . And the ref sees the tights being pulled. (Moore argues with the ref as Sanchez regains his thoughts outside the ring before rolling back in.) MW: Sanchez from behind with Moore! Big lariat and 'The Predator's over the ropes! (Miguel plays it up to cheers) JM: Look at that gimp. He better watch who he hams it against....that'll get him hurt in more ways than one. MW: Sanchez up on the apron.....flying lariat, and Moore's in the steel! The railing had to move nearly a foot! JM: Oooh.....that's gotta hurt. Sanchez picking up the timekeeper's chair... JM: That poor guy! How many weeks in a row is it now where he loses his seat?! (Sanchez sizes up Moore, who's trying to get up off the floor) MW: And Sanchez swings! (Moore hits the floor like a ton of bricks. As he tries to get up again, Sanchez dives off the apron with the chair to a pop, connecting with Moore right in the forehead.) MW: ABSOLUTELY BRUTAL FLYING CHAIR-SMASH BY SANCHEZ, AND I THINK MOORE MAY HAVE BUSTED OPEN! JM: It's a small price for greatness! (Sanchez and Moore roll back to the ring. Sanchez stomps away on Moore as he gets in.) MW: Sanchez just stomping away on a stunned Moore, and a second-buckle moonsault to the back! JM: C'mon Chris! MW: Sanchez back up.....and a beautiful somersault senton finds it's mark! If Sanchez is this good in a regulation match, imagine him in one of his self-proclaimed gimmick matches! JM: Self proclaimed...those two words are key to Sanchez. He's nothin' without his ego-touting. MW: Sanchez with a flying stomp and a cover! 1! . . . 2! . . . 3! NO! Shoulder up and Moore cradles Sanchez! 1! . . . 2! . . . Reversal by Sanchez! 1! . . . 2! . . . Kickout with authority! Moore withstanding two pinning attempts by Sanchez, and whips Sanchez to the corner.....Big splash! JM: That's high-flyin' for ya Mike! (Moore smashes Sanchez with a few double axhandles before picking him off the mat for a bodyslam.) MW: Moore throwing Sanchez to the mat.....an elbow.....and a half-Boston. (Moore let's out a yell as he wrenches the ankle of Sancez.) JM: Good God. He's bleedin' like a stuck pig! MW: Indeed Moore's suffering from some blood loss here tonight....and Sanchez reaches the ropes to break the hold. (Sanchez limps to the apron of the ring to regain his thoughts.) MW: Moore making his way to Sanchez...and Sanchez lands a slingshot jumpkick! And A slingshot splash just may end it here! 1! . . . 2! . . . Kickout! JM: Takes a lickin' and keeps on tickin'! (Moore rolls to the outside followed by Sanchez.) MW: Sanchez and Moore are just brawling away on the outside....and Sanchez with a whip! Reversal and the ringpost stops his trip! JM: Hah. Maybe if he had a backbone it'd do some damage. (Moore charges in, but Sanchez darts out of the way as Moore's shoulder wraps around the post. Sanchez proceeds to slam Moore's arm around the post before dumping him into the stairs.) MW: These two guys don't seem to care about the match anymore! (Moore fires back at Sanchez with a dropkick to the back followed up by a legdrop to the back of the head.) JM: To Hell with the match! I don't think they know who they are! Moore looked like Jericho for a second there! MW: Sanchez may be out! JM: I like Moore more and more *chuckles at the rhyme* every time I see him! (Ref:) 9.....10......11.......12..... MW: And Moore rolls Sanchez back into the ring for the cover. 1! . . . 2! . . . 3! NO! Shoulder barely up on the 3-count! And Sanchez cradles the neck of Moore! 1! . . . 2! . . . Thr! Kickout and Moore's furious! (Moore charges at the ascending Sanchez with a clothesline, lands it, and a fistdrop for good measure.) MW: Moore with a pickup... (Moore raises one bloody hand in the air.) JM: THE PREY AGAIN! MW: 1! . . . 2! . . . 3! NO! Can anything stop Sanchez?! JM: My God Mike! Last time he landed to 'Prey' drivers on someone in one match, he left the AAWA! (Moore frustratingly picks up the ragged Sanchez in a Military Press and unceremoniously drops him on the mat face-first.) JM: I think he broke his nose! MW: Both men are bleeding rather profusely, and we've gotten word that the cameras are going to have to pull some distance shots if this keeps up! (Sanchez gets to his feet as Moore clings to the ropes) MW: Sanchez back up....and a thrust kick dumps Moor over! (Moore lands onthe table outside the ring.) JM: GET UP! GET UP! MW: And Sanchez from the top to the outside! SPANISH DEATH! (Big pop and tons of camera flashes) MW: Sanchez rolling Moore back in for the cover! 1! . . . 2! . . . 3! NO! Again only a 2 count! JM: (sighs in relief) (Sanchez leaps to the second buckle, trying to steady himself.) MW: Sanchez showing uncharacteristic signs of sloppiness....most likely due to loss of blood....and a second-rope Hurrcanrana! Reversal by Moore into a Powerbomb! NO! Sanchez trying for a re-reversal, and Moore uses the momentum to finish it off in a high-vertical whip powerbomb! JM: WOOHOO! NOW THAT'S RING PRESENCE! MW: Cover! 1! . . . 2! . . THR! Reversal! 1! . . . 2! . . . 3! JM: NOOOO! Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen...your winner in 19:50....MIGUEL.....SANCHEZZZZZZ! (Six Foot Foam Skull plays to cheers as the the battered Sanchez makes his way down the aisle, clutching his 'Master of Mayhem' title.) MW: Sanchez wins a well-earned victory against one of the toughest men in the sport today! JM: What I wouldn't give just to....
