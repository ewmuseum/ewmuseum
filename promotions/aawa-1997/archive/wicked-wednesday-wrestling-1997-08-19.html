AAWA: 'W3'

19 August 1997 (part 2 of 2 — part 1 not in this collection's own held corpus)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-= ‘Excellence’ Jeff Easthope vs. The Green Goblin=- No-DQ/Submission. Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with no time limit and is a submission match… JM: THIS ONE’S GONNA BE WILD KIDS!!!!!! Announcer: Coming down the aisle... ('Perfect Strangers' by Deep Purple plays as most fans boo, while Flair marks cheer) weighing 252 pounds, from NY. NY, 'Excellence' Jeff Easthope! JM: There he is Mike! There is *the* MAN! Announcer: And his opponent... (Paranoid by Black Sabbath plays as the fans boo) weighing 220 pounds, from Parts Unknown, accompanied by Harry Osborne, Green Goblin! (Goblin rushes to the ringas Osborne trails slowly behind, glancing over his shoulder at the curtain) (bell rings) MW: Goblin starting things off with a stinging reverse knife-edge chop! Irish Whip…..and A great legsweep faceslam! (crowd boos) JM: Does he know how much is gonna come outta his paycheck for that last move?! That face is worth millions! MW: Easthope quick to his feet, and another chop by Goblin! Easthope lands a stiff one downstairs! (mixture groans and laughter from crowd) JM: Hello? Vienna Boys Choir? I think we have a prospective soprano for you….. MW: Easthope with yet another lowblow, and a backrake for good measure! JM: Even when he cheats, he does it to perfection! MW: Easthope with stiff right hook, and Goblin answers him with a forearm to the face! (as Easthope stumbles backwards, Goblin shoves him to the ground and attempts a Spinning Toe-Hold.) MW: Goblin with a submission attempt here….and he just can’t seem to keep it on. JM: Of course not. MW: Whip with Goblin to the ropes…..Easthope explodes with lariat across the Goblin’s throat! Pickup! Legsweep faceslam! JM: HAHA! Poetic justice! Just wait until Easthope gets his mits around Scorpio and grinds that punks face in with that very move! (Easthope struts across the apron to heel cheers and several fans ‘WHOOO’ ing before dropping an elbow on Goblin) MW: Eashope up top and anticipating Goblin……flying lariat and a big miss! (Easthope snaps around as soon as his feet hit the mat, and spins the Goblin into an abdominal stretch.) JM: HOW ABOUT THAT?! HOW MANY OTHER MEN YOU KNOW COULD GAIN CONTROL OUT OF A MOVE THEY BOTCHED THEMSELVES, MIKE!? MW: Not too many indeed, and Goblin’s stuck between a rock and a hard place! (The Green Goblin frantically gropes for the ropes to no avail, before eventually snapping his free leg into the groin of Easthope) MW: Goblin with a rather clever escape…. JM: Don’tcha mean ‘cheatin’ escape? (Harry Osborne steps through the ropes and tosses a chair stiffly onto Easthope) MW: Osborne’s even getting in on the action here! Goblin with that chair, and jabs it down into the rib-cage of Easthope! Again! Goblin: (facing directly into the camera) READY FOR THIS FREAK TO BE GOBLINIZED?! (turns and tosses chair edge-wise to the throat of Easthope) NOW THAT! THAT’S GOBLIN POWER! MW: Goblin with an absolutely relentless attack on Easthope with that unforgiving steel chair! (Goblin picks up the chair by Easthope’s throat, and raises it above his head again.) MW: AND GOBLIN WITH THAT CHAIR!!!!! JM: NO! MW: BIG SWING AND A MISS! EASTHOPE AVERTING DISASTER AND TAKING THE GOBLIN DOWN WITH A WHIZZER ARMBAR! JM: YES! MW: Easthope with another abdominal stretch, and my colleague Miller’s doing his impression of the Zodiac! (Easthope has the Goblin isolated in the center of the ring, wrenching away on the lower back of Goblin.) JM: The man’s right! He’s just……Excellent! Hah! (Easthope quickly releases the hold into an Irish Whip to the corner.) MW: Goblin’s rear connected full force with that one, and that *can’t* be good for his already punished lower back! JM: Good……after that chair fiasco, he deserves whatever Easthope feeds him. MW: Easthope charging in….knee-high dropkick nails Goblin squarely in the gut! JM: Hahah! After a hit like that, he’s trying to keep his kidney’s from slidin’ out his nose! (Mike directs a disgusted look at Miller as Goblin folds to the mat) MW: Easthope with Goblin by the mask…..Another abdominal stretch! NO! Goblin with a slick duckbehind….reverse neckbreaker! What impact! JM: NO! MW: Goblin rolling over to Easthope…..GOBLIN GLIDER!!!!!! (Easthope reaches back and jams a thumb in Goblin’s eye) JM: And a beautifully executed Greco-Roman Optical-dehabilitator to the Goblin by Easthope! MW: Greco-Roman……good God…. (Goblin stumbles backwards as Easthope gets to his feet, but Goblin grabs Easthope from behind for an inverted brainbuster as the crowd oohs and ohs) JM: NO! MW: WOW! THAT HIGH-IMPACT MANEUVER BY GOBLIN, PAYING OFF BY LAYING EASTHOPE OUT IN A BIG WAY! JM: What about the gentlemen’s 8-count?! MW: Goblin with a spinning toehold applied! (Goblin laughs his head off as Easthope lays in the ring, just barely missing the ropes with each attempt to escape) (cut to commercial) MW: We’re back laides and gentlemen! As you can see Goblin still has that punishing move on Easthope and isn’t letting up a bit! JM: Don’t worry Mike…..just…..don’t worry…..Easthope won’t go down that easily……. (Easthope miraculously frees a leg and flips Goblin over for the reversal.) MW: EASTHOPE TURNING THE TIDE ON GOBLIN!!!!!! IRISH WHIP!!!!! DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! BOTH MEN ARE DOWN! JM: GET UP JEFF!!!!! (the referee looks on hopelessly as both men roll around dazed) MW: Easthope slowly to his feet…….picking up gobin by the mask….Double underhook piledriver! (crowd pop) JM: *THAT’S* WHY EVERYONE LOVES THE GUY! MW: Easthope with another pickup…..Powerbomb! Scorpio Deathlock applied! (Goblin lays in the hold unconscious as two figures run to ringside. One is easily over 7-feet tall, the other about 5’11. Both are dressed in Goblin outfits) MW: WHO THE HELL…. JM: GOD MADE 3 OF EM?! (the huge Goblin approaches Easthope from behind, and choke-lifts him from the rear) MW: THIS HUGE INDIVIDUAL IS JUST MAN-HANDLING EASHOPE! OH MY GOODNESS! INVERTED RAZOR’S EDGE! JM: NOOOOO! DQ THEM! *NOW*! MW: THERE ARE NO DQ’S HERE TONIGHT, AND THE BIG MAN’S HOLDING EASTHOPE ON HIS FEET……. (the little Goblin leaps to the top of the turnbuckle and performs a devastating top-rope Frankensteiner) MW: ABSOLUTELY BRUTAL! THESE TWO MEN ARE JUST DISMANTLING ONE OF THE TOP MEN IN THIS SPORT!!!!!!! JM: THIS IS BULL****!!!!!! (The curtain parts once again to reveal the ‘All-Star Wrestler’ at the top of the aisle) MW: THERE’S THE ALL-STAR WRESTLER! JM: GO IN AND HELP YOU FRIGGIN’ FLAG-LOVIN FREAK! (The All-star Wrestler looks to the ring, shakes his ehad, and heads back in.) MW: WHAT THE?! JM: WHAT DO YOU EXPECT FROM A FRUITCAKE LIKE HIM?! (The huge goblin rolls Green Goblin on top of Easthope, and begins to apply the Goblin Glider) MW: THE REF HAS NO CHOICE BUT TO GIVE THIS TO THE GOBLIN!!!!! EASTHOPE’S UNCONSCIOUS! JM: BULL****! COMPLETE AND UTTER BULL****! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen…..your winner in 15:46….THE….GREEN…..GOBBBBLINNNNN! (‘Paranoid’ by Black Sabbath plays to extremely cold boos) JM: THIS IS A CROCK OF… MW: Enough! The 8-second delay can only do so much! Ladies and gentlemen….while we try to make sense of the situation here, we’ll bring you these words from our sponsor…. (scene fades to commercial) MW: We’re back folks……we’ve still received no word on the status of Easthope, but we did see him actually get up and stumble to the back of the locker rooms during the break. JM: Name one other man that would get up after a number of cheap shots like that… -=Rip Daddy Scorpio vs. Miguel Sanchez =- Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following matchup is scheduled for one fall with no time limit. Coming down the aisle... (Six Foot Foam Skull by Shallow. North Dakota plays as the fans cheer) weighing 243 pounds, from Philadelphia. PA, Miguel Sanchez! (Sanchez comes down the aisle slapping fans with a folding table beneath one arm, which he sets up outside the ring.) MW: Sanchez has proven, and vocally declared, lately that he has what it takes to carry the title of ‘Master of Mayhem’. Truly a great competitor……. JM: Now if only he’d see the light, and quit conversing with losers like the World Chumpion. Announcer: And his opponent... ('WHOOMP! There it is!' by Tag Team plays to a big pop) weighing 231 pounds, from Denver. CO, Rip Daddy Scorpio! (RDS runs to the ring slapping hands with the fans, before hopping atop a turnbuckle and harvesting cheers) MW: The World Heavyweight Champion makes his presence known in Scotsboro! These people absolutely *love* him! JM: Must be soemthin’ in the spittle…….. (bell rings as Scorpio extends his hand to Sanchez) MW: Scorpio with a display of sportsmanship….and Sanchez accepts…both men sizing each other up….Tiger Bomb by RDS! 1! . . Kickout! That move layed the Goblin out for a 10-Count last Saturday, and Sanchez recovers amazingly fast from it! JM: Wake me when Scorpio gets tabled….. MW: Scorpio with an armdrag takedown into an armbar! Release, and a whip….dropkick by Sanchez and a shooting star press! Big miss! (Scorpio leaps on top of the buckle) MW: Scorpio up top…..flying elbow drop! (pop) JM: What is it with these losers? The man has to copy someone like ‘Rowdy’ Ryan, who I don’t even like, just to try and get a win…..pathetic! MW: Scorpio with a reverse chinlock…rollover into a cradle! 1! . . . .2! . . .3! NO! Shoulder up! JM: Piddly moves like that are no match for someone like Sanchez…..I’ve seen him take down everyone in the CWO, and I *know* for a fact that Mr. ’35 second loss’ Scorpio can’t say the same. MW: Scorpio with another Elbow drop! Pickup…Hangman applied and Sanchez is quick to counter! Northern Lights Suplex! 1! . . . .2! . . . .thr! Kickout! Just a few minutes into the match, and already several close calls! JM: Close only conts in horseshoes and hand grenades Mike! Once these two stop pussy-footin’, then there will be no close…. MW: Spinning wheel kick by Sanchez clips the champ, and Scorpio could be out! JM: (devilishly) Goooood…… MW: Sanchez up top…..Shooting Star Press finds it’s mark! (big pop) MW: Sanchez with a short splash…..up top again! Somersualt Senton! JM: Sanchez really impressing the Heck outta me with those flattening flips of his! Mike, I like! (Sanchez rolls the limp carcass of Scorpio onto his table) MW: What’s this?! Sanchez on the apron…..Asai Moonsault drives the champ through the table and into the concrete! (Crowd oohs and ohs) JM: Eat the floor chump! (Sanchez rolls Scorpio back to the inside) MW: Sanchez with a slingshot legdrop and a cover! 1! . . . 2! . . . 3! SHOULDER UP! Scorpio just eeking out of a pin there, and a whip sends him to the ropes…..flying clothesline! Sanchez’s down! (Scorpio acts out to the crowd to big cheers) JM: Just look at that no-talent punk…….if I ever had the chance….Ooh hoo! I’d ‘Doom Drive’ him till next Tuesday! MW: Sanchez back to his feet and a Northern Lights Suplex…reversal into a bridging back suplex by the champ! 1! Kickout! JM: Kinda easy to slide out from under, what with all that grease runnnin’ offa him…… MW: Sanchez leaps to his feet, Double arm DDT! Pickup….Doctorbomb! (crowd oh’s) JM: THAT’S WHAT IT TAKES TO BACK UP A TITLE REIGN! SCORPIO! TAKE NOTES BOY! MW: Sanchez with another pickup…..Hurricanrana..NO! Powerbomb reversal by the champ! JM: Chump. MW: Scorpio up top! RIP DADDY SUICIDE! (big pop as Scorpio tumbles throught the air, and proceeds to tie up Sanchez’ legs) MW: There’s that standing Figure-4 Scorpio’s so well known for! Scorpio and Vine! JM: Scorpio and vine? Sounds like the avenue all the ‘John’s pick him up at so he can pay his rent th enext day. (Sanchez powers out of the Scorpio and Vine, and greets Scorpio with a charging clothesline) MW: Sanchez up top! Superfly Splash! (Scorpio brings his knees up and catches Sanchez in the gut as the crowd groans) JM: there’s those wuss-tactics from Rip Mammy Scorpio again…..no wonder these country bumpkins love him….. MW: Scorpio with Sanchez! Big Belly to belly release suplex! Scorpio back up top! (Sanchez leaps to his feet and lunges at Scorpio, causing him to fall) MW: Sanchez with a flying shoulder tackle, and following it up with a Figure-4 sleeper! (RDS plays to the crowd while trying to escape, before finally powering out of it) JM: Looks like God has a sense of humor….he made actually made people that think along the lines of Scorpio……. MW: RDS with a whip…reversal, and a big back body drop! Sanchez with moonsault bodyblock! Sanchez back up top! JM: WOW! (Sanchez raises a fist to the air, turns his back to Scorpio, and leaps) MW: Moonsault by Sanchez, and he finds nothing but mat! (crowd pop) MW: Scorpio with a whip! Frankensteiner! 1! . . . . 2! . . . . .3! Scorpio wins! JM: NO! MW: Scorpio continues to add to his winnings here tonight with a *big* win over the ‘Master of Mayhem’ Miguel Sanchez! JM: Just wait till Saturday……when Easthope gets to exact his punishment to Scorpio, and knock some sense into his peanut-shaped head…… MW: As it stands folks, at ‘September Sizzle’, RDS will face the Green Goblin for the World Title, that is if ‘Rowdy’ Ryan doesn’t defeat the Goblin before then, and we have one heck of a 30 man Battle Royale scheduled to be broadcast Friday, August 20th! Until then, this is Mike Williams saying, goodnight for the AAWA! (Scene fades out to a panning view of the Scottsboro arena as ‘Staface’ by White Zombie plays slowly in the background) http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Loge/3706/
