AAWA Special Event: Battle Royale....Part Deux

Oops. http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Loge/3706 ************************************************************** (The Focus stops Synapse from pushing him over with a jab to the eye. Kurt Gerstung lands a flying bodyblock and nearly topples Clark over the ropes as Super chokes The Phantom against the lower rope.) RT: Each of these men giving it their all here tonight! Like I said earlier Mike…I’m especially impressed by Phantom and Miller! One’s a rookie, the other’s supposedly stuffed with ring-rust! (The Focus sets Gerstung up in the corner with an Irish whip and performs the ‘Focal Point’ powerbomb. Miller and Synapse simultaneously wail away on Clark and Sanchez. Gerstung, Bundy, and Phantom fight for dominance in one corner, while the Warlock and Super Goblin trade forearms in yet another corner.) MW: THE FOCUS WITH A DEVASTATING TOP-ROPE POWERBOMB ON GERSTUNG! RT: These two have seen each other fight for quite a while in the CWO. They know each other’s weaknesses, and The Focus just displayed that knowledge with a brutal power attack on the flyer, Gerstung! MW: Miller and Synapse seemed to have gelled here! Synapse with Sanchez! The Cure! Clark with a running clothesline and an elbow! (Synapse hoists Miguel Sanchez to the buckle, sets him up on it, and slaps him off.) MW: AND WITH AN HUMILIATING SLAP, SANCHEZ IS ELIMINATED! RT: Don’t be surprised if these two cross paths soon! Clark just Chokeslammed Josh Miller yet again! ( The Phantom slides underneath the Warlock’s legs and delivers a dropkick to his back….) MW: Phantom nearly taking Warlock off his feet there…..another dropkick! RT: The big man goes down! Warlock’s out! MW: The Phantom has two ways to look at this situation! He’s either making a lot of enemies, or a lot of fans! (Dakota Bundy pulls Miller up from the mat and chokes him against the ropes as Super Goblin is slung to the ropes and lifted over by Synapse) MW: SUPER GOBLIN’ S OUT! RT: OSCORP’S CHANCE AT THE E-CUP TOURNEY IS GONE! (The Focus nails Clark with a double axhandle to the kidneys, and shoves him over the top) MW: CLARK’S OUT! NO! Clark….just barely hanging on to the top and rolling on the apron… RT: Bundy with a whip to Miller, and big avalanche! Another Firearm and Miller’s staggering!!!! (The Phantom and Gerstung double clothesline Synapse as Clark shoulder tackles The Focus against the post. Bundy picks up Miller and drops him into a snap-backbreaker.) MW: Phantom up on the second turnbuckle…….Gerstung with a legsweep to Synapse and The Phantom hits with a Spin-Wheel Kick for good measure! RT: Synapse just got rocked! The big man’s lost in there! (Gerstung runs towards Synapse and attmepts a dropkick) MW: Gerstung with a running dropkick! SYNAPSE CATCHES HIM WITH A BACKDROP! GERSTUNG’S ELIMINATED! RT: Did you see the height he got there?! MW: We’re down to the final 6 men tonight. RT: As I mentioned earlier Mike. Both Miller and Phantom were wild draws here tonight and are holding their own than anyone could’ve guessed. (Clark charges for another shoulder tackle, but The Focus darts out of the way and Clark catches the post with his shoulder.) RT: Clark meets the post and The Focus works away with a series of boots to the midsection! Big kneelift to the ribs! Clark’s down! (The Phantom attempts a cross-body block on Dakota Bundy, but Bundy catches him and ragdoll suplexes him into an unsuspecting Miller, who then stumbles over the ropes.) MW: MILLER AND THE PHANTOM ARE OUT! RT: Bundy passing the proverbial buck with that great move! (Miller and The Phantom fight all the way back to the locker room. Miller slams the Phantom’s head into the railing, but the Phantom comes right back with a DDT which leaves Miller out cold) MW: These two just won’t let up! RT: The final four men in the ring now MW: Synapse and Clark going toe-to-toe here! Synapse with the Synapser….NO! Clark with an amazingly fast duckunder….CHOKESLAM! RT: 370 pounds driven 2 feet through the mat! MW: And here comes The Focus! (The Focus lunges at Clark kicking him in his bad ribs, and starts to work away on his neck with a series of jabs to the throat.) MW: Focus showing that tenacity that’s made him defeat greats like Chris Meyers and Benjamin Blake Bentley in the past….. (Clark retaliates with a boot to the face and a pump-handle slam) RT: And there’s the power that shot Clark to the ‘World Champion’ position in the CWO! (Dakota Bundy slips behind Synapse and kicks him in the back of the knee) MW: Bundy attacking the well-publicized bad-knee of Synapse….bulldog! RT: Clark with a legdrop…NO! Focus rolling out of the way! Figure-4! That may be what it takes to knock Clark off balance and eliminate the big guy! (Synapse whips Bundy to the ropes, and Bundy flies back at him with a lariat. Synapse stumbles backwards tot he ropes and Bundy shoves him over with a clothesline) MW: SYNAPSE IS OVER! (The Focus releases the Figure-4 and stomps on the knees of Clark to tremendous boos) RT: Focus allowing Clark to get to his feet……kick to the midsection and a kneelift! Focus onthe mat beneath a keeled Clark! Leg extension! MW: CLARK’S OVER! THIS ONE’S DONE FOR FOLKS! BOTH DAKOTA BUNDY, AND THE SURPRISE OF THE NIGHT, THE FOCUS WILL REPRESENT THE AAWA IN THE UPCOMING E-CUP INVITATIONAL! STAY TUNED TO AAWA TELEVISION FOR DEVELOPMENTS IN THE TOURNEY, AS WELL AS THE STANDING OF THE FOCUS IN OUR SPORT! RT: Signing off for Mike Williams, this is Robert Taylor inviting you to join us tomorrow on ‘AAWA Wrap’ for standings in the league, rumors galore, as well as the schedule for the upcoming week.
