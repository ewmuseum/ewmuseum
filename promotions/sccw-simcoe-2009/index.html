Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Simcoe County Championship Wrestling ran out of Barrie, Ontario, and its own listing on the fwrestling.com hub calls it one of the original regional old school e-feds, open since November 1999, roleplay-based, weekly and by invitation only. Its 2009 run is a new start under the old name: its heavyweight title stood at To Be Determined in the rankings of 22 July 2009 until the Bunkhouse Brawl decided it anew, and its weekly Monday Night Aggression, taped before a crowd of 150 at a Barrie television studio, was only at number eight on 27 July. None of the 2005 SCCW's titles or champions carries over, so it stands here as the successor of the 2005 SCCW wing and not as a continuation of it.

What survives is a short run of the fed's own pages on the hub: three ranking lists, a preview and the results of the 27 July show, the results of 10 August and the news after it. They follow the Bunkhouse Brawl, which crowned Myke Adams Heavyweight champion, Jay Jerzey's bloody Barbaric title win over Patrick McCarthy, Sydney LaRoux's win over Riflewilly for the All Barrie title, the tag champions War Machine, and a card announced for 17 August. Nothing the federation posted after 12 August 2009 survives in this collection.
