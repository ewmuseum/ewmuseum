Monday Night Aggression Barrie, Ontario, Canada on 07/27/2009 This event currently has a 3.23 rating based on 17 votes cast. Featured Main Event: Main Event: Sydney Laroux v. Riflewilly (All Barrie Championship) Willy lost the non-title last week against Modo WHOA! guarenteeing he gets his shot at BRAWL versus the winner of this week. Sydney has more than cemented her place as a contender to the All Barrie title and now she has her chance.
More Info: Greg Venom v. Patrick McCarthy Patrick McCarthy is coming off a two week losing streak and his feud with Jay Jerzey looks like it has no end in site. Newcomewe Greg Venom has had his own run in with Jerzey just last week. With BRAWL a week away and both men occupied, what will happen here? Venom wants a spot in the BRAWL, and McCarthy wants his hands on Jerzey. Both lost last week. This week is a pivotal week in their SCCW career.
Modo Whoa v. Doctor Ian Modo is coming off a non-title victory over All Barrie Champion Riflewilly. He has a title match at BRAWL. Doctor Ian is relatively new in SCCW, having been here previously for less than a month. Their styles clash. Modo wants the momentum going into BRAWL, Ian wants that elusive first win.
Sharlene Berger v. Kirsta Lewis Two women wiith a lot of history with each other throughout the wrestling world. Berger is already in the BRAWL with Miss Lewis looking to make her mark and enter it. Miss Lewis is a former Heavyweight champion and is widely considered one of the very best female wrestlers in the world today. Sharlene is her perennial thorn, having thwarted her at numurous times in various wrestling companies. This one will be for the ages.
Emanuel Jackson v. Scarlett Emanuel Jackson is making his mark, calling out The Machine, getting a victory over Jace Steele's son Alexander and Patrick McCarthy last week. This week he is facing off against the Internet's Darling, Scarlett Willis. Willis is in the BRAWL already, Jackson wants in. Can he keep his streak against SCCW's longtime roster?
Brooks is on a huge role with a winning streak. Steele has been doing very well, with him trying to fill his father's shoes, the BRAWL will be a good way to get that recognition he craves.
Ladder Match for spot in Brawl: Myke Adams v. Grim Last week the two did not solve anything and if it was not for SCCW owner Jim Diamond coming down, they would have continued to fight. This week it is for Myke Adam's shot at BRAWL. Grim wants to ruin Myke. The "Suicidal Superman" just wants to go into the BRAWL in peace. What will happen when a ladder is introduced?
