News: SCCW's MNA #9 is on the air

12 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCCW's MNA #9 is on the air!!! Another night of Monday Night Aggression is in the books. Coming off of the SCCW’s Annual tradition, Bunkhouse BRAWL”, this week could only be called “REVENGE WEEK”.
In our main event, we saw newly crowned heavyweight champion “The Suicidal Superman” Myke Adams took on the other finalist Samuel Hessingstock in a non-title match. Adams came out with the victory that time, but it was hard worked for. Something tells me these two have not seen the last of each other and next time they meet, it could be for the title and the outcome could be different.
Other matches include: Modo WHOA! & Cain/Willy vs. Sydney LaRoux & War Machine - 6 Person Tag – The tag team with all the gold were victorious when All Barrie champion LaRoux & the All Barrie tag champs War Machine defeated Modo WHOA! (now Galactix), and the team of Paul Cain & Riflewilly.
Scarlett Willis vs. Jen Blackheart vs. Sharlene Berger Scarlett Willis defeated the other two SCCW ladies in a match that could put her in title contention for the All Barrie or the BARBARIC title held by Jay Jerzey at the moment.
Justin Brooks vs. Blade vs. Greg Venom Greg Venom has stepped up his game and is looking for an elusive title shot at any gold here in SCCW. This win over the triple threat could in fact be the boost he needed.
Dr Ian vs. Alexander Steele Dr. Ian took the match in a very, very close fought match. This should put him closer to a title shot at singles gold.
SCCW NEWS: THE TEAM OF PAUL CAIN AND RIFLEWILLY IS NO MORE!!! Paul Cain has let it be known the last two weeks that if Riflewilly keeps failing to garner victories, then he will become a liability. After their loss this week, Cain has eliminated any liability when this monstrous man, who we have been told is Damien Kahn, attacked Riflewilly, injuring him after the match. Word has it that Cain has some really evil things planned in the future for SCCW.
TFWF SUPERSTAR GEORGIE NICKLES IN SCCW FOR A SHOW!!! Georgie Nickles, top wrestler for TFWF, Roughkut 2009 Hall of Fame nominee, current semi-finalist in the Experts Extreme Tournament, perennial pain in Miss Lewis’s ass and now SCCW wrestler…for one night anyway. Georgie Nickles has signed a one off appearance to wrestle this week in SCCW (more on that below). What Miss Lewis will say about this is not yet known but you have to know she is not happy. These two have history going back a while in TFWF and now with Nickles teaming with the rival of “The Hellcat” in Scarlett Willis, that is just another way for Georgie to stick it to Kirsta.
PATRICK McCARTHY HAS STILL NOT SHOWN UP IN THE SCCW STUDIOS!!! McCarthy after his vicious match against Jay Jerzey for the BARBARIC title still has not shown up in the studios or the SCCW office. Rumor has it he might appear this week (August 17th) at MNA, but right now that is an unsubstantiated rumor.
UPCOMING CARD: Darrel l Leon vs. Blade “Vamp” is new to SCCW and its rings with a hell of an opening match. Blade is a former champion in Roughkut Wrestling, still looking to make his mark in Simcoe County. This should be a match to watch.
BARBARIC Championship Jay Jerzey (champion) vs. Rex Masters (challenger) After winning the belt in what can only be called –the- most bloody and brutal in all of SCCW history, Jay Jerzey is going against doctor’s orders and defending against Rex Masters. After an alright showing in the BRAWL match itself, Rex is trying his hand at the BARBARIC division. Jay has been quoted as saying “the belt will not leave his possession until it is pried from his cold, dead fingers.” Rex has been quoted as saying “Hamma ton, innna soo mana da bel…. HAHAHAHA”. Not sure what it means, but I think it means the belt is his.
Scarlett Willis and TFWF's Georgie Nickles vs. J. Brooks/Paul Cain TFWF’s Georgie Nickles has been seen hanging around backstage for the last week. This week she has been contracted for one week, one match. SCCW’s sweetheart and TFWF superstar Vs Paul Cain and Justin Brooks. Why Brooks is teaming with Cain, we have no idea, but Cain has stated in private that Brooks’ world is about to be overturned, as is the rest of the SCCW.
Myke Adams and A. Steele vs. Sydney LaRoux and S. Hessingstock SCCW Heavyweight champion and All Barrie champion facing off on opposite teams with the number one contenders to their belts across the ring as well…. Should have very interesting implications in regards to the winner.
MAIN EVENT (non-title) BROS vs. War Machine This is the first match since War Machine has won the titles and it is against BROS (Dr. Ian & Greg Venom). BROS is known throughout the wrestling circles as a tough as nails team. Surely a win, even non-title Vs the champs will earn them a title shot.
That is all there is this week for SCCW news. Stay tuned to the website and to “A” Channel in Barrie, Ontario for all your SCCW needs.
