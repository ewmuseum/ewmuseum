Damage Federation: Saturday Night Fever

16 July 1997

-Damage Wrestling Federation- -$aturday Night Fever- -------------------Tom E.Gunn vs. Jeff Jacobs-------------------- [Announcers Steve Williams and Terry Branch sit as Tom E. Gunn come in to his intro music!]
Williams: Well folks, here comes a night full of excitement. I am here with my co-host Terry Branch to bring you...."Saturday Night
Fever"!!!
[Jeff Jacobs makes his fancy entrance into the ring and goes corner to corner pumping up the crowd.]
[DING,DING,DING!!!] Terry Branch: Good evening folks, welcome to the Opening night Damage
Wrestling Federation! Both men are preparing to wrestle in the first pin anywhere match of Damage history! That has to....
[Tom E. Gunn, without hesistation, drags Jacobs by his hair onto a stiff knee.] Nasty Steve Williams: Hey! Did you see that, Jacobs just caught one right to the jaw! [As Jeff Jacobs gets up slowly, he catches Tom E. Gunn with a bouble jab to the chest and mouth!]
Terry: Right back at you, Tom E. Gunn! Like my moms always said if you're brave enought to hit... You're brave enought to get hit!1
Williams: Folks, Jacobs looks like he is trying to take control of this match with quick jabs to the chest and kicks to the ribs! Jeff
Jacobs is no joke!
Terry: Gunn is thrown into the ropes by Jacobs and then out the ring he goes! [Gunn is now on the pavement motionless.] Williams: Jacobs is climbing to the top rope... [BAM!!] Williams: ...Jacobs comes down on Gunn with a elbow to ribs like a bad habit! Gunn looks like he is out colder than a freezer!
Terry: Oh no! Looks like Jacobs is preparing for his finishin move... [Jacobs puts Gunn into his finishing move, "Lights Out"!] Terry: That's it folks, Jacobs just finished Gunn off with his finshing move, "Lights Out"!
[Jacobs rolls on to Tom E. Gunn for the pin.] Referee: One, Two, Three!!!! [DING,DING,DING!!!] Williams: That's it for night, if you ask me the that fight was shorter than it takes to cook one minute rice!
Terry: Folks the action does not stop here, stay tuned for Crimson Mask vs. 100 Proof!!!!!!!! Results: Jeff Jacobs by pinfall --------------------100 Proof vs. Crimson Mask----------------------- 100 Proof vs. Crimson Mask was a great fight. Crimson mask came out with fire in his fist, jabbing Proof in his ribs! 100 Proof came back with a DDT, Suflex, and his finishing move, "The Proof Lock" late into the match. At the end of the match it seemed as 100 Proof had this match put away, but suddenly a masked man hit Proof over the head with a steel chair while the ref had his backed turned! The match ended wwith Crimson Mask putting the "Crimson Rush" on 100 Proof.
