Blood Bash '97

19 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<Damage Wrestling Federation>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<07/19/97>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
**************************** ** Blood Bash '97 ** **************************** [Damage wrestlers are in the ring ready to start the main event... Blood
Bash '97!!!] [Carmera pans up to the annoucers booth where "Nasty" Steve Williams and
Terry Branch sit waiting for the Damage rumble.]
Terry Branch: Good evening ladies and gents, I'm Terry Branch with Steve
Williams bringing to you "Blood Bash '97"!!!!!!
Steve Williams: Hello folks, where live at the United Center in Chicago,
Illonis. Blood Bash is a "every man for himselve" rumble where you have to avoid being tossed, kicked, or pushed onto the ringside pavement! The winner of Blood Bash will have a chance either to take the Blood Bash title or take on the World Champ for his belt, look out Tom E. Gunn!
Terry Branch: Every wrestler in Damage is ready to ring each other's necks and there are many grudges! I wonder how allias like KC Boss/
Massive Force and Tom E. Gunn/ Jeff Jacob will get through the Blood
Bash without fighting, I guess we will see!
[The referee gives the nod to the judges to start the rumble!] [DING,DING,DING!!!!!] [The wrestlers stare each other down.} Terry Branch: Here comes 100 Proof with a slap to the face to Sychodic and another one!!!! [Everyone starts brawling in the ring!] Terry Branch: Sychodic comes back with a face claw to 100 Proof's face and then throws Proof into Crimson Mask!
Steve Williams: Massive Force and KC Boss are in the corner teaming up on the World Champion of Damage, Tom E. Gunn!
[Jeff Jacobs sees the the disadvantage and leaves Verderer in the corner!] Terry Branch: Jeff Jacob delivers a crushing flying elbow to the back of
Massive Force's head!
Steve Williams: That had to have hurt!! Terry Branch: Stone Cold trades chops to the head with Armageddon,
Armageddon blocks Stone Cold's chop and lifts him over the top rope sending Stone hard to the pavement!
Steve Williams: Stone Cold's out!! [Armageddon has his back turned from the action and Brad Morris sees his chance!] Steve Williams: Brad Morris charges at Armageddon, Armageddon is now... Terry: Armageddon ducks!!!, sending Brad Morris over the top rope to be the second man put out! So far it has only been Stone Cold and Morris eliminated from the competition.
[Jeff Jacob and Tom E. Gunn are still battling with Massive Force and KC
Boss in the corner!]
Terry: Tom E. Gunn throws KC Boss into the turnbuckle... Gunn follows
Boss to the corner! [Tom E. Gunn gets on top on the ropes!] Williams: Gunn delivers punches to the head of KC Boss. Gunn jumps down and puts KC Boss in the sleeper!
[Verderer and Armageddon are now going at it, trading massive blows with each other!] [General Glove and Spawn duke it out in the middle of the ring!] Terry: General Glove head butts Spawn and whips him around into the
"Glove Sleeper". Ohh!!, I don't think Spawn is going to get free!
Williams: Spawn is struggling to get out. General Glove now is dragging
Spawn over to the ropes...
Terry: Spawn is now eradicated from Blood Bash! [General Glove, with a big grin on his face, waves bye- bye to the furious Spawn!!] Williams: Massive Force is now taking controll of Jeff Jacob. Jacob is looks like he is in a daze!
[Massive Force delivers his favorite move to Jeff Jacob, the "Power
Slam"!] Williams: Massive Force shows no mercy to Jeff Jacob and put him in the boston crab! Terry: Why is Force putting Jeff Jacob in a submition hold, there is no referee in the ring!
Williams: Verderer sends Crimson Mask out of the ring with a arm throw! That is a big blow to the Damage officials considering that he is ranked number one in Damage!!!!
Williams: Sychodic delivers a drop kick to the unexpecting Armageddon, sending him to the canvass!
[Armageddon stays on the ground!] Terry: That is a smart move by Armageddon, you can't get put out if you are on the ground! Williams: Jeff Jacob has been eliminated by Massive Force! Terry: Verderer has been eliminated by 100 Proof!! Williams: Wrestlers are starting to drop like flies! The only wrestlers that are left are General Glove, 100 Proof, Massive Force, KC Boss ahhhhhh, Tom E. Gunn, and Armageddon! I promise you these wrestlers won't get an easy win!
Terry: Sychodic stops pounding Armageddon's knee and picks him off the canvass and thro... Williams: ... Armageddon reverses the throw and sends Sychodic to the ropes! [Sychodic crashes through the middle rope and hits the pavement!] Williams: Sychodic has just been eliminated by a determined Armageddon! [Armagedon limps to the middle of the ring for safety.] Terry: Armageddon has a limp, that may slow him down. Williams: KC Boss and Massive Force has just been eliminated by a double clothesline by Tom E. Gunn! Gunn got his revenge for his eliminated friend!
Terry: General Glove and 100 Proof are giving each other massive kicks to the ribs! [Tom E. Gunn wildly charges at General Glove and 100 Proof!] Williams: Tom E. Gunn pushes 100 Proof and General Glove, General Glove keeps his balance, but 100 Proof is sent over the top rope!
[100 Proof takes Tom E. Gunn with him to be eliminated from the competition!] Terry: Is just General Glove and Armageddon! General Glove goes straight for his weakness and kicks Armageddon's knee!
[Screams to let the pain out!] Williams: General Glove is going for is all. General Glove puts Armageddon in the figure four!! That has to do some damage on his knee! [General Glove gets Armageddon out of the figure four puts him in his finishing move..."The Glove Sleeper".]
Terry: Armageddon is eliminated from Blood Bash, leaving General Glove to have the choice take the title or challenge Tom E. Gunn.
[DING,DING,DING!!!!] Williams: If he challenges Tom E. Gunn, Armageddon will get the Blood
Bash Title! What will he do, we will find out soon!
Terry: Well, that's it for Blood Bash '97. I sure it was all you can handle! Williams: Good night folks, we'll see you Monday night!!!!! [Commercials come on.]
