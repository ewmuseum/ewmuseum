The Rox Network-"Hard Hitting T.V." _________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT TORTURE _________________________________________________________________ WOW!WOW!WOW!-THE SIREN SCREAMS........... !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!FIRWORKS EXPLODE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Scene Opens up to 25,000 Screaming fans in the WWFL Arena in Sacramento,California...Pantera's "The Great Southern Trendkill" begins to play as the Camera begins focusing on the fans..... ) (The camera focus's on Kids with Freedom Fighters shirts saying "We are Gods",A sign says "MPJ fears Trickster",and finally, "I know who Malice Surprise Partner is....") THREE ANNOUNCERS-Tony Mcmahon,666 Kid, and Lawler get ready to speak,as the music begins to die down... Tony:Welcome to OUR First Card of 1998.... Ferry:I got my New Years resolution....Wanna know what it is??? Tony:What? Ferry:To be away from you for Two weeks!!!!! Tony:Oh your real funny. Ferry:I know this...maaaaan. Tony:So buckle your safety belt, and, GET READY TO EXPERIENCE TUESDAY NIGHT TORTURE...................................... Harold "The Powerhouse" Nelson *RETURNS* "The Hitman" John Parks Vs. Ultimate Savage *DEBUT* of The Red Scorpio Vs. "Stunning" Scott Davis Freedom Fighters Vs. Malice/Suprise Partner "Devastating" [name withheld] Vs. Alex Dunn *Double Main Event* Hades Vs. Trickster (The Last two men from the Battle Royal battle it out for the I-C Title again.) Mcdowd Vs. MPJ (For the WWFL Championship) _________________________________________________________________ Tony:Welcome, Back from the quick prevue screen. Tonight we have some BIG matches,so get ready,because it's gonna be "Hell in a Cell".... Ferry:Or to imitate Crash-"Hell in a Cell,dude..." Kid:That was actually pretty good. Tony:Now, lets get to the return of Harold "The Powerhouse" Nelson... DING!!!!!!DING!!!!!!!!DING!!!!!!!!! RING ANNOUNCER:Already in the ring,from Miami,Florida.weighing in at 238 pounds, BOBBY FONZIKE....{Booooooos} RING ANNOUNCER: ANNNNND his opponent,Making his return to the World Wrestling Fantasy League,<Harold Nelsons music starts> coming down the aisle from Atlanta, Georgia,weighing in at 290 pounds..... HAROLD "THE POWERHOUSE" NELSON !!!!!! Tony:The former Intercontinental Champ Nelson, was in a car accident nearly 2 months ago. He appears to be rehabilitated,but he will definitely be rusty in the ring. Ferry:Not to mention the Bed... Kid:Hey we dont' wanna hear about that... Tony:Keep your personal life out of it... Ferry:Sorry. Tony:Nelson locks up With Fonzike, sends Fonzy to the ropes, Nelson with a clothesline,Fonzy back up,Nelson goes running to the ropes, he comes running back,and hits shoulders with Fonzy.Noone moves,Nelson comes back, hit's Fonzike's shoulder and this time,Fonzike goes flying backwards... Ferry:He nearly did a flip. Tony:Nelson is lifting his arms up in Power and the crowd gives a mixed cheer back. Kid:He appears to be fine. Tony:Nelson gets Fonzike up, Standing Clothesline.... Ferry:Yet again. Tony:Nelson sets him up......"HOUSE ROCKER" Powerslam!!!!!!!! 1......... 2.............. 3................... Kid:HAROLD NELSON IS BACK BABY... Tony:Easy win for Nelson.You can hear him yell to the camera "Hades, I'm coming for you...". Kid;That would be a great match. Tony:I think it would be, Nelson,NEVER really actually lost his belt, though he was forced to give it up as a result of a Bad Car Accident. Kid:Our next match is Between, Newcomer John Parks and the Ultimate Non Talker, The Ultimate Savage, making his second run in the WWFL.... Tony:If you remember, The Ultimate Savage was here before the DIWL COMPLETELY took over the WWFL Shutting it down... Kid:And now he's back, as are all of us. Ferry:I'm sure glad MPJ isn't broadcasting here anymore....Glad he's gone. DING!!!!!!DING!!!!!!!!!!DING!!!!!!!!! RING ANNOUNCER:Coming down the aisle,from Long Island,New York.Weighing in at 234 pounds,accompanied by "Killer Kowalski", "THE HITMAN" JOHN PARKS !!!!! RING ANNOUNCER: And... his opponent, coming down the aisle,Brooklyn,New York. Weighing in at 231 pounds,....THE ULTIMATE SAVAGE !!!!!! Kid:Not only is this the battle of two New Yorkers,but a battle of the 230 Pound weight and 6 feet class. Ferry:But nothing REALLY is at stake,except losing the match. Tony:You both are very.......watchful. Both:THANK YOU! Tony:Parks and Savage lock up.Savage headbutt's Parks....Parks appears to be bleeding out of his nose. Kid:Savage comes from behind Parks and tries a quick pin. 1.............. 2.................. 3.......... DING!!!!!!!DING!!!!!!!!DING!!!!!! Ring Announcer: Your winner is the ULTIMATE SAVAGE...{Mixed Reaction} Tony:The Ultimate Savage runs out of the ring Real Quick, as he's scored a pretty big upset here tonight.. Kid:John Parks just kicks the turnbuckle in disgust... Tony:We'll be right back. _________________________________________________________________ "Saturday Night Live"--Every Friday Night at 11:30 pm _________________________________________________________________ New Rage Against The Machine--Check out their New Double Cd, and listen for their new Single "The Ghost of Tom Jones" _________________________________________________________________ Kid:Welcome back.... Tony:Now it's time for the Debut Of the former Mystery Man of Malice. Kid:Known as the Red Scorpio. DING!!!!!!!!DING!!!!!!!!!!DING!!!!!!!!!! ("Won't back down" by Tom Petty begins as Scott Davis begins to walk down the aisle, just as the Chorus begins to hit, Davis gets on the apron and raises his hands to the crowd,wich in turn cheers) RING ANNOUNCER:Now in the ring,from Idabel, Oklahoma, weighing in at 235 pounds, standing 6 feet 2 inches, "STUNNING" SCOTT DAVIS (No music plays, as the announcer starts to announce Red Scorpio's stats.) Tony:Where is the Red Scorpio? Ferry:He could be anywhere....He could be hiding under this table. Kid:Remember he is friends with Malice, so he probably just "forgot" about his match. (SUDDENLEY THE TITON-TRON Turns on) Tony:HEY IT'S THE RED SCORPIO!!!!! I THINK !?!?!?!?!?! (The Red Scorpio appears, almost like Kane on the screen.) Red Scorpio: "Well, well, again, I see that many questions are being asked of me. This time, you all want to know why I am not in the ring, facing Scott Davis. Well, I'll tell you why. Scott Davis is a nobody. He is a nobody, compared to me. And, if you all think that I am going to tip my hand, and allow any chance of an injury, then you're all dumber than I thought you were. I fear not what Scott Davis could do to me. Because, we all know that he can't do anything. He's too busy fighting his brother's shadow, to be a concern of mine. I only have one focus. So, I am not going to open myself up, and allow for someone like Dogg to cheap-shot me. Seeing that is the only chance in the world he has of taking me out, I know he'll go for the easy answer. He always has. And, always will. He can't help it. Desperation makes a man do crazy things. . And, knowing that Dogg is desperate for answers, I know he's crazy enough to try and take me out. If it's an easy exit, Dogg takes it. Why do you think he's hiding behind the Fab 4? Because he knows, and the whole world knows, he can't hold his own. It sort of like the plain chick, from high school, that hangs out with the realy fat chick; to make herself look better. Dogg is a product of smoke, mirrors, and federation presidents that are easily manipulated. And, about the tag-team match that Malice has later tonight? Rest assure, I will be watching. And, soon, very soon, you will all get to witness the greatness that is Red Scorpio. And, maybe, just maybe I'll let you take a peek at who I really am. Devastating [name withheld], you say that I am Scorpion? Nice try, but our judges won't accept that answer. Please phrase your answer in he form of a question. But, no, Scorpion, I am not. But, I know Scorpion; and thanks to me, he wishes he was never born. But, that is another story for another time. See you in the ring very soon, boys and girls. It's gonna' be....DEVASTATING!!" Tony:Wait a minute, the Red Scorpio is Sneeking up from behind Scott Davis. Kid:IT WAS A RECORDING! Tony:Scorpio taps Davis on the back,kicks him in the gut,and Piledrives him. Kid:This man is scary! Tony:Scorpio is setting Davis up for another Piledriver!!! Kid:YES!!!!!!!! Scorpio hit it again. Kid:Scott Davis is cringing to his neck.. Tony:Scorpio is laughing at the crowd...The crowd boo's. Kid:WAIT! Scorpio is setting Davis up again. Tony:Oh no! ANOTHER Piledriver... Kid:This Scorpio guy is scary. Tony:Wait a minute,HERE COMES [name withheld]!!!! Kid:Scott Davis's brother. Tony:Scorpio looks at [name withheld],then gets Scott Davis to his feet and throws him to the outside of the ring.
