Read the rules below.......---- Check us out at - http://members.aol.com/bjdstuff/wwfl.html just to see what were like.... But Please E-Mail me at [detail withheld] THE RULES ARE BELOW, FILL THEM OUT TO JOIN...... thanks bd _________________- WORLD WRESTLING FANTASY LEAGUE RULES EXPLANATION OF WWFL-The WWFL is not one of those highly organized,if you don't join at the beginning of the league your screwed,and rarely get a match because of it.It's a laid back-have fun league wich doesn't favor anyone that joins earlier over later joining members.I know how that feels that's why it's a first come,first serve league.AS IN whenever their is a league-tourney for a belt,whoever E-mails me first with their wrestler(s) will get in first rather than me putting in who I think are the best wrestler's in the tourney and the rest don't even get a chance.I don't think that's fair and it's not the type of league I wanna run.That's why this league won't have to many people in it so everyone will get a chance.DON'T join if your in like 20 wrestling leagues already,because those people tend to pay less attention to the league and get it mixed up with other leagues. And you cannot expect to join and get a match,I'll give you some matches but other than that I'm not gunna manually give you matches you'll have to challenge people and roleplay alot.Well anyway fill out and read the rest of the form and I hope you enjoy the league.REMEMBER You can send this back but you have to put your Wrestlers name where it says "SUBJECT" on the mail form And Erase the Useless Crap I Don't need. 1.JOINING THE WWFL To join the WWFL you must give the President this information: Your Wrestler(s) Name and Tag Team(s) Name(s) (if Tag Team) Wrestlers Height: Wrestlers Weight: Wrestlers Hometown: Wrestlers Age: Wrestlers BirthDate (Month/Day/Year): A Brief Description of wrestler: REAL WRESTLER Your wrestler mostly resembles (Facially,for WWFL Mag): (IF YOU CHOOSE NOONE, I WILL CHOOSE A REAL WRESTLER Who I think your Wrestler looks like,...) Gimmick- Is your Wrestler Face(Good) or Heel(Bad) or Nuetral? Would your Wrestler ever switch Allignment?(Example swichting from Heel to Face) Would you mind if your wrestlers fought each other- (example you make up UTaker and Goldust and they get in a fued) If your Wrestler has a manager what's his/her name : Is your manager a man or woman?: What's manager like? (Looks,Personality) Set-up move(move they use before finishing opponent)- Finisher #1: Finsher #2: (one of those finishers must be a ground manuever,i.e.Not a top rope finisher) As many of your wrestler's favorite wrestling moves as you want (limit 30): Wrestlers Theme Song (If any)- How fast is your wrestler? Yokozuna speed/Undertaker Speed/OLD Ultimate Warrior Speed/OLD Rockers Speed- Does (Do you think) your wrestler want (will) to win the WWFL Championship Belt?- Does your wrestler want to win any belt?- Special Credentials (won championship belt in WWF,Ranked in top 5,5months,ETC)- Background Information- And you wrestlers Stats (read down) 2.WRESTLING MOVES No Wrestler shall make up moves or Finishers wich shall KNOWINGLY 'cause such harm as Broken Bones,Paralysis,or even Death. 3.SPECIAL MATCHES All Special Matches such as Cage,Retirement,or Death Matches,No Rules, must be approved by the WWFL President. 4.WWFL CHAMPIONSHIP BELTS -WWFL World Heavyweight belt -WWFL Intercontinental belt -WWFL United States Belt -WWFL Cage Belt (belt can only be won if wrestling in steel cage) -WWFL Cruiserweight Belt (light weight belt) -WWFL Tag Team Titles Each belt must be defended at least once a month. 5.STATS Divide 55 POINTS into these categories (10 points maximum in a category) -STRENGTH 10=Superior in that catergory -TOUGHNESS 1=Not superior in that catergory -INTELLIGENCE 0=has no realm of that catergory -CHEATING -BRAWLING -TECHNICAL WRESTLER -AERIAL -QUICKNESS -HARM (how much harm your wrestler move have on the opponent) -FAN SUPPORT -TEAM WORK 6.WRESTLERS IT's pretty simple,their is a 6 wrestler limit to each person Unless you want to make a STABLE Tell us how many guys more you want make to be in that stable).No Wrestler's may exceed 8 feet,1,000lbs.No real wrestlers.....Also there are a few ripoffs,but please don't make dumb ones....... 7.WINNING THE CHAMPIONSHIP BELT To win the championship belt you must win by pinning your opponent!You cannot win by Disqualification,Draw,or Countout unless it is a Special Match 8.MEMBERS Members who don't reply back from letters,don't participate,or not hear from at most in 1 month are subject to suspension or ejection out of the WWFL because we want participating members or else it ruins it for everyone.I don't wanna make this sound like Oh I'm leaving Better tell Braden That I'm leaving but please tell me when your going on Vacations or give a new address to where your going if you want your mail delivered there. 9.JUST IN CASE Their are real-life things like injury,fatigue,suspension, and fines in the WWFL.If it happens to happen to your wrestler don't worry it's just part of the WWFL and no offense should be taken.If I suspend your wrestler it just means that particular wrestler you own can't wrestle for the suspension time.All other wrestlers you own will still be put on cards. If I suspend YOUR WRESTLER it doesn't mean YOUR SUSPENDED from the WWFL. Just to make everything clear. 10.CARDS- Cards are every Sunday.Sometimes your wrestler might go up against a jobber for 3 reasons,practice,to get your name out,Variety.These cards are not kid friendly,in other words they MIGHT sometimes say profane words.But I won't put profane words in just to say profane words,it'll be 'cause of the match.Just so your warned and you don't have a cow.All Cards are sent through E-MAIL. 11.CONTRACTS Yes the WWFL is as real as it can get,so that means in the WWFL we have contracts.After you send me your guys I will send a contract and money offer.You can either reject the offer and ask for more weeks/months/or years or just more money (the fake part of the deal).After your contract is up the WWFL might send you another contract offer,if he's getting alot of action,or I might not offer you another contract and that wrestler might not be in the WWFL for a while 'till I decide I want that guy back in the WWFL.The WWFL has the right to release any Wrestler in the WWFL before his contract is up.And also if I release or not offer another a contract to one of your guys and not the other you can create another guy because you only have 1 guy currently wrestling in the WWFL.(Example:I release one guy of yours and not the other,you can create another Wrestler because you only have 1 wrestler in the WWFL,Because the other ones currentley not in the WWFL) 12.LEAVING THE WWFL You can also tell me if you want a wrestler of yours to leave the WWFL for a little while or forever to use that spot to make up another Wrestler,and just to have him leave.You as a screename can also leave the WWFL also.I won't take it personally,but when you get tired of the league just tell me that you want to leave it. THANKS FOR JOINING THE WWFL,Any questions,input or anything,Please E-Mail me at [detail withheld] And it is alright to send this back to one of us.just erase the crap I don't need. Thanks for reading this long-ass thing, [name withheld] WWFL President WWFL Writer WWFL Creator
