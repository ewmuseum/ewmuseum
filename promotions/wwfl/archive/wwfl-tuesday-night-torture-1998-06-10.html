WWFL Tuesday Night Torture, 10 June 1998

Tuesday Night Torture HOUR TWO **FIREWORKS EXPLODE** Tony: Were ready for HOUR NUMBER TWO!!! Stinson: Coming up this Hour we have a match EVERYONE has been waiting to see with, Paul Smith taking on Anthony Huntingon. Tony: Loose Cannon, Will Shelton will defend his WWFL Cage Belt against Madman. Stinson: And in the Main Event we have A member of the Factor Three taking on Dogg for the WWFL Championship...Remember, we dont know wich member it will be to take on Dogg. But for now, we'll just speculate... (All of a sudden you hear the famous glass shatter from Stone Cold Steve Austin's Theme, out walks McDowd with a can of beer and an Arm Cast and a Neck Brace, He climbs in the ring and orders the ring announcer to give him the Mic.) Tony: NO!!! IT CAN'T BE! Ferry: It's Stone Cold Mcdowd!!!! (Mcdowd grabs the mic and begins to speak.) McDowd: Ok everybody shut up because McDowd's in a bad mood! A hell when am I not in a bad mood. Well it seems that Dogg is the world champ! Well since I'm standing here in the ring than I order you to come out here and give me a damn title shot. (Waits and Dogg never shows) McDowd: That's what I thought you're a damn coward. You aren't lookin for an ass whoopin tonight, but that's ok. Because I'll take out any one of you wrestlers in the back there. (WWFL President, Chris Stinson, walks up to the ring) Chris Stinson: Who do you think you are disrupting this program like this? McDowd: Hey look I'm not looking for the corporate angle here like Vince McMahon! All I demand is a match! Chris Stinson: Well It's not going to happen tonight I can guarantee that. Look at you you are unable to compete in your condition! McDowd: I think I should be the judge of that! Chris Stinson: Ah, screw you! McDowd: What the hell did you say? Chris Stinson: Um, Nothing! Ferry Lawler: Hey, McMahon look at Stinson backpedal. McDowd: Well give me one damn good reason why I can't compete? Chris Stinson: Well I'll give you an option, next week if you come back with a doctor's release that says you are capible to compete than the following week you'll get your big return match! McDowd: Well you can bet your ass that I'll have that release and Dogg and the rest of the Fab-4 will know about it!!! Hey what ever happened to Paul Smith? Chris Stinson: Um... contract...WHY AM I TELLING YOU THIS? (Stinson Leaves the ring going back to the announcer's booth as Austin's music plays as McDowd leaves through the crowd) Tony: My god, Mcdowd is looking almost 100 percent!! Stinson: Those 2 months he was away from the sport just went by to quick. Ferry: Scared he's coming back for something Stinson? Stinson: No sir, Ferry. {"Helter Skelter" by the Beatles begins as Anthony Hunnington walks out to a big crowd of Boo's.] Tony: Were ready for a Main Event match in it's own reich. RING ANNOUNCER: Coming down the aisle, from Tuscon,Arizona...standing 6 feet 7 inches...weighing in at 290 pounds... Representing the Rough Riders..HERE IS ANTHONY HUNNINGTON Stinson: I hate this guy! Ferry: Is he gay or straight? I guess Gay because he acts like such a butt pirate. I don't like him either. Tony:Like him or not, Anthony Huntington is a good wrestler. RING ANNOUNCER: Aaaaaand HIS opponent,from Flint, Michigan, weighing in at 265 pounds, standing 6 feet 5 inches, Making his return to the WWFL..HERE IS PAUL SMITH <<CROWD: YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAH>> {"Back in Black" by AC/DC plays as Paul Smith walks out to a BIG CROWD OF CHEERS!!! He has Black Tights and Black Boots on and he shakes alot of fans hands as he walks down and the fans give him a loud cheer.} Tony: The fans sure do love this guy..His return has been WAY too long to happen. Anthony: Shut the [censor] up! You can come out here with your damn fans behind you and shake all these hands, but quite frankly I don't give a damn! You think your something of a legend until "The Arizona Cowboy" puts you back down to size right here on TNT...You came out here for one thing...Me to whoop your ass. Now lay down and don't give me any of your lip. I'm going to win this match whether you like it or not..And that's the way it is BECAUSE HUNTINGTON SAID SO! <<CROWD: BOOOOOOOOOOOO>> Stinson: Huntington sure knows how to make an impression. DING!!!!DING!!!!DING!!!!! Tony: The bell rings and Hunington/Smith meet in the center of the ring..IT'S A GRAPPLE! Huntington with a kick to the gut of Paul Smith..Huntington off the ropes...FACE SLAM onto the mat of the ring. Stinson: Anthony Huntington will use the most brutal moves to win a wrestling way.. The Face Slam is just an one of those many awesome manuevers. Tony: Anthony Huntington now kicks the DOWN Paul Smith in the back of the head...Huntington is going to the top rope. We don't see this often from a 6'7/290 pound guy.. Stinson: Paul Smith to his feet, MISSILE DROP KICK!! from Anthony Huntington. Tony: Smith rolls to the outside in favor of some time. Ferry: Boy Paulie Smith is showing some ring rust..Wouldn't you say? Stinson: I'd say he can still beat your butt any day of the week. Tony: Huntington now going to the outside..The ref has got a count started..SMITH attacks Huntington as soon as he steps on the outside apron. Smith now...DDT! ON THE OUTSIDE!!! Smith raises his hands in the air and gets a loud response from the crowd. Stinson: Paul Smith drags Anthony Huntington to his feet... Irish Whip....ANTHONY HUNTINGTON GOES FLYING INTO THE GUARDERAIL!!! The fans pat a down Anthony Huntington as Paul Smith jumps back into the ring. Tony: The fans help Anthony to his feet.. Huntington jumps into the ring only to be clotheslined by Paul Smith. Stinson: Smith is not showing those same signs of ring rust anymore. In fact, he looks to be at the top of his game. Ferry: You plan on jewing him anytime soon? Stinson: I am Jewish! Ferry: Oh,....sorry. Stinson: It's ok.... I'm not really. Tony: Smith is up onto the second rope....FLYING FORE-ARM to the face of Huntington...We've got a pin. 1..................... 2............................ KICKOUT! Tony: Anthony Huntington with a kickout. Both men to their feet, Huntington strikes first with a HARD punch to the rib of Paul Smith..NOW a punch to the liver..Huntington now takes Paul Smith down with a Spear.. Huntington sets up Paul Smith....FISHERMAN SUPLEX!! Stinson: Huntington now with a pin.... 1...... 2............ KICKOUT!!!! Tony: Smith almost didn't kick out of that one! Stinson: Paul Smith is very slow getting to his feet..Huntington is in the corner egging on the fans.. One fans Coke almost hit Anthony! Tony: PAUL SMITH FROM BEHIND!!! 1............. 2............. KICKOUT!!!! Tony: Huntington up...Looking More PO'ed then EVER!!! Huntington with ANOTHER SPEAR TAKEDOWN!!!! Stinson: Smith gets RIGHT BACK UP! <<CROWD: YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAHHHH>> Tony: We've got another face off! Look at the look in the eye of Paul Smith...EVIL! Stinson: Hunnington with an Ax Handle..He now sets up Paul Smith.. Tony: UH OH! Ferry: HERE COMES THE TUCSON WELCOME! Tony: Paul Smith with a Back Body Drop! [Cheers] Stinson: Huntington surprised back to his feet... Smith with a Kick to the gut.. Tony: ACE CRUSHER!!!!! [Crowd Cheers] [Crowd Count] 1......... 2........... 3...... DING!!!!DING!!!!!DING!!!!!! Tony: Add another one in the 'W' Column for Paul Smith. [The Chorus to "Back in Black" by AC/DC plays.] RING ANNOUNCER: Here is YOUR WINNER.....PAUL SMITH Tony: The legend has made his presence felt tonight on TNT! Ferry: Huntington ain't movin' either! Tony: 'Tony's knocked out! Stinson: Meanwhile Paul Smith makes his way backstage. Stinson: Huntington is awake once again..HE'S IRRATE! Tony: This war is not over. Ferry: I wish Huntington would lie down and die. Stinson: Now we've got Will Shelton up next defending against Madman in a 15 foot high steel cage! Tony: Here he comes now! ["Runnin" by 2Pac & Notorious BIG chimes in over the PA System and the crowd turns hostile as Kory Shinlever makes his way out to fireworks and explosives. Shinlever raises one arm and then makes the walk to the ring. Shinlever once again is dressed in another Armony suit and tie with a white t-shirt on under it. His hands are studded with gold rings. Shinlever has a 24 karrot gold necklace that drapes down from his neck with a gold cross danging at the bottom. Shinlever steps into the ring and calls for a microphone. He gets one and then steps into the middle of the ring.] Tony: Wait a minute, that's not Will Shelton...That's his Partner KORY SHINLEVER! Stinson: What does he want? Kory Shinlever: CUT...THE...DAMN...MUSIC... ["Runnin" fades out.] Kory Shinlever: I'm going to cut to the chase really quick tonight. I've been watching all the WWFL interview shows and I see a lot more talent in WWFL than when I first walked in here 4 months ago in February. However, it's still the same as it was in February when Will and I made our debuts at February Falls. The WWFL is still filled with a bunch of mid-carders and losers that just don't measure up to the talent. The only guys in this federation that are worth my time in mentioning are Will Shelton, Adam Lash, Dan Glamour, Malice and Paul Smith. That's the talent that I actually have respect for. All these others like Owen Masters, Kelner, and Anthony Hunnington aren't up to my standards. Not even the "World Champion", Dogg, is up to my standards. Dogg, I remember you attacking me during a match about a month or so ago. But why haven't I heard from you ever since then? Dogg is just like everyone else, he's a hit and run coward that in my mind, is a pretender to the crown. The two men who should have any claim to that belt are Adam Lash and Malice. But something else crosses my mind, I've been here 4 months and still nobody has been able to pin me in one on one competition. So why haven't I receieved a World Title shot yet? Stinson doesn't want me to have the WWFL World Title just like he didn't want Malice to have the World Title. It's a shame when your President is so damn corrupt. Hey Stinson, you're not Vince McMahon and you'll never get the World Title yourself. So just face it and give your wrestlers their title shots. I make _WAY_ too much money to be sitting on the bench. Put me in coach!! I'm ready to wrestle!! I haven't seen action since WWFL Memorial Mayhem when I chopped the Ultimate Giant down to size. So Stinson, if you can't come up with an angle or a feud to put me in, then just throw some WWFL "superstar" in the ring so I can collect some more W's in the win column. I've beat just about everybody there is to beat in this fedeartion whether I beat them here or in the FWA. I've taken out Paul Smith, who si a hall of famer in the WWFL. I've taken out a future FWA Hall of Famer, Dan Glamour, on two occasions. Come to think of it, in two of my first three FWA matches, I beat two of the WWFL's former World Champions, Mr. Perfect Jr. and Stone Cold McDowd. So exactly who out there is left for Kory Shinlever? I want the shot that I deserve. I want Dogg. All these people out here hate my guts, but I guarentee that every single one of them knows that I could take Dogg and beat him from post to post and win the World Championship Title. It's just that simple. And there isn't a man here today that can say I don't deserve a World Title shot. But by some small miracle there is a doubter, then let them step forward and I'll kick their ass too! Stinson: I'M THE ONLY ONE THAT MATTERS KORY! [Shinlever takes off the Armony jacket and sets it on the ring post. Underneath he exposes a white t-shirt with black lettering that says "LOOSE CANNON 4 LIFE" on the front and "THE LAST DON" on the back. Shinlever walks back to the center of the ring and pulls the mic back.] Kory Shinlever: All I'm asking to do is get my due's here in the WWFL. I've been through all there is to do. This is my last go-around. If I don't get the World Title then I'll drop, shut my mouth, pack, and _LEAVE_. It's that simple. The World Title is the reason why I came here four months ago. So when the day finally comes for The Last Don to get his shot, I'm not letting it go for the life of me. Because you've got my word on it, I'm gone if I lose. The career is coming to an end because there isn't that much left to do in the wrestling world for me. You name it, then I've been there, done that, gone back, and did it again. I have nothing to prove here in the WWFL. I've already done it. I'm a nine time World Champion and I've won them in league's where the legends like Nebgen, Black, Majors, Robski, Styles, and Thunder have all competed. The rookies like Owen Masters, Matty Dee, and Casey O' Casey have everything to prove and everything to lose. They've still got to go through what I've been through to make it in this business. Even guys like Dogg have a lot to go through. Dogg, if you want to make your mark in the wresltling business, then you'll defend the WWFL World Title and not step down from anybody. Already, you stepped down from Mr. Perfect Jr. That's not something smart to do. As champion, you're supposed to accept all challenges. It makes you look yellow. And personally, I think you're yellow. After all, if it wasn't for Stinson, you wouldn't be the World Champion and you know it. I planned on facing Malice, but he got screwed. Now you're his replacement. Now you're in my line of fire. Dogg, you want a challenge? You want to prove your worth as champion? Then step into the ring with The Last Don of this sport. Step into the ring with the ICON. Prove yourself Dogg. Step up to the plate. Take a few swings. See if you can handle the heat. Personally, I don't think you can. I don't think you can handle it. You're the same guy that hit me from behind and ran on Tuesday Night Torture a month ago. I haven't forgotten Dogg. As Dan Glamour says, "it ain't over". Dogg, the "World Champion"...think again. [Shinlever goes back to the corner and picks his jacket up and puts it back on. He buttons two of the buttons and then straightens the suit. He picks the microphone back up and stands with a stern look on his face.] Kory Shinlever: It is simple. Time is winding down and sooner or later, I'm going to get my shot. Dogg, your time as champion is running out and has been draining quickly ever since you "won" the title. You've got a multitude of guys gunning for you, I'm one of them. So get ready Dogg, because the first man that faces you will bash your head in like some poor animal that just got ran over in the street. The Army is coming. The Cannons are still here. Remember... You Can Run... BUT... YOU... CAN'T... HIDE!!!! [Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!] ["Runnin" hits the PA System again. Kory Shinlever drops the microphone and exits the ring through the top and middle ring rope. He walks straight down the aislel, ignoring all the rowdy fans who just continue to harass him. Shinlever stops before he walks out and flips off everyone with both hands and then turns and walks out as "Runnin" fades.] Tony: My god I can't believe the gaul of this man. Stinson: He can't just come in on MY Show demanding a World Title Shot! I'm president of the WWFL.Funk dat' Kory.. EVERYONE thinks they deserve a Title Shot. Your NOTHING NEW! Tony: I think he's better then all the rest. Stinson: You know what...There's 4 other people who want that belt as well. How do you determine that your better? I'd like to hear it.. Ferry: Kory Shinlever will tear you up Chris. Stinson:That man better respect me.. I'll tell you what Kory..You come on this show,Tuesday Night Torture, Next week and give an IN RING speach of how much you respect me and How Great I am...THEN I'll give you a World Title Shot..You got it? Tony: You want him to rant and rave about how great of a guy you are for a Title Shot? Stinson: YEP! Ferry: Shinlever shouldn't have much to say.. Stinson: I want to hear the respect come from his mouth. Tony: This should be interesting.. Will Shinlever comply for a Shot at WWFL World Championship Gold? DING!!!!DING!!!!!!DING!!!!!!! RING ANNOUNCER:The following match is for the WWFL Cage Belt. <<CROWD: CHEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEERS>> [ "Desert Search for Techno Allah" by Mr. Bungle begins ] RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, coming down the aisle, from Glendale,Arizona, weighing in at 225 pounds....standing 6 feet 5 inches... MADMAN Ferry: This guy is crazy! RING ANNOUNCER:AAAAAnd his opponent, from Knoxville,Tennessee,weighing in at 260 pounds,standing 6 feet 4 inches,Representing the Loose Cannons, WILL SHELTON {As "Live and Let Die'" by Guns and Roses plays as Will Shelton walks out to a BIG Crowd of cheers wearing a Loose Cannon's T-Shirt, AND Blue Jeans.
