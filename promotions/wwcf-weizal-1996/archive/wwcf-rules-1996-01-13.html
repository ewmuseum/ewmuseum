Weizal Wrestling Championship Federation Official Rules and Regulations
W.W.C.F. Constitution
The following is a list of rules, regulations and other important things about the Weizal Wrestling Championship Federation.
They are in no order what so ever. But, hopefully they will help us to establish order during our wrestling events.
#Matches include Singles, Tag Team, Singles Tournament, and Tag Team Tournament. #Every person (real people, that is) may only have one wrestler. #Tag Teams may be formed when both wrestlers (their real life counterparts) agree to form the team. #The will eventually (hopefully) be 4 singles titles and 2 tag team titles: Singles W.W.C.F. World Champion W.W.C.F. Intercontinental Champion W.W.C.F. Television Champion and the King of the Ring Champion Tag Team W.W.C.F. World Tag Team Champions W.W.C.F. United States Tag Champions #The titles will all be decided in tournaments. The tournament will be held for the World title, as soon as there are 4 wrestlers enrolled. The tournament will be held for the Intercontinental championship as soon as 9 wrestlers enroll. The tournament will be held for the
Television title when 18 wrestlers enroll. The King of the Ring title will be decided at a pay-per-view event.
#The general rules for a match are: No Count Out, victory by pin fall, submission, or disqualification for use of an illegal object or move, and that's about it. # Of course, we have special attraction matches also. These include: Submission matches, Texas Death matchs, and 2-out-of -3 falls. #The matches outcome will always be decided using the computer program Wrestling League Simulator 5.1 (if there is a newer version, let me know). But, when the match is described, I will usually use some of my own creativity to determine the content of the match, the pre-match hype, and the post-match.
#Challenges are welcome, along with trying to convince another wrestler to become your tag partner. To do this, simply send E-mail to me ( [detail withheld] ) and then I will contact the person involved. Also, I will produces a list of all the members of the
W.W.C.F. and their e-mail addresses, in case your want to confer privately.
