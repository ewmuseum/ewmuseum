"(WWCF) Friday Newsletter"

21 January 1996

W.W.C.F. Friday Night Report Hey, buddies. The first ever W.W.C.F. Pay-Per-View event is tomarrow. I will be posting the results around 10:00 AM eastern time, Sunday morning. It's called WWCF Midnight Raid. It will consist of an 8-man tournament for the King of the Ring title. Plus, there will be a
W.W.C.F. Title match. The new WWCF champion will defend his title against a mystery opponent. Also you will see alot of the new talent that has recently entered the WWCF.
----------------------------------------------------------------------- - House Card Results. January 16, 1996 - From the new WWCF arena in downtown Library, PA. I was a tremendous night of action. On the card we saw the debuts of these newcomers:
Dynamite Dan Golden, 007 Jay Bond, U.S.A. Kid, Masa Yokohama, & Powersurge! The WWCF intercontinental strap was on the line as Tecker defended his gold from Void MacLane. The match was a violent confrontation. In the end, the official disqualified Tecker for excessivly choking Void. Because titles cannot change hands on a disqualification, the Tecker retained his title.
The WWCF World title was also up for grabs. The champion the Soul Swiper defended against former WWCF World title-holder Masked Mayhem. These two bohemoths nearly shattered the ring with all their earthshaking manuvers. The match was even till the end when Masked
Mayhem was able to properly execute his patented Death Attack! Then, Masked Mayhem rolled Soul Swiper up for the belt. After the match, Masked Mayhem said he would defend his title on the first pay-per-view. Little did he know, he had to face the
Executioner two days after he regained his title. The main event of the card was a Texas Death match with The Weizal and Jamie Cutlass. The match ran nearly 1 hour! It started simply, both competitors exchanged clotheslines and chops. Then, Jamie
Cutlass locked The Weizal into his Crossbow submission hold. But, earlier in the bout the ref had been knocked out which allowed the
Hitman to run down to ringside and break the hold. But, has the Hitman tried going back to the locker room he was attacked by Private Plantem.
The Private lifted the Hitman above his head and threw him onto the announcer's table, where his back squashed a monitor! The Weizal, meanwhile had applied his nose hold, which made Jamie Cutlass submit, but he didn't stay down for the ten-count. While Jamie was standing back up, the Weizal had perched upon the top-rope. When Jamie was completly up and facing away from the Weizal, he was nailed with a flying bulldog from the Weizal! Then, the Weizal reapplied the nose hold and then Jamie did stay down for the ten count.
QUICK RESULTS Dynamite Dan Golden pinned 007 Jay Bond U.S.A. Kid earned a surprising victory over Brian Damage Brett Young beat Lone Wolf The Brain beat the Executioner Masa Yokohama pinned Powersurge Void MacLane beat WWCF intercontinental champ Tecker by disqualification. Masked Mayhem regained the WWCF title from The Soul Swiper. The Weizal won a Texas Death match over Jamie Cutlass. January 18, 1996 - Live from the Bullock Coliseum in Gatlinburg, Tennesee. The card was somewhat shorter than the one on the 16th, but the fans were not disappointed.There were two title defenses. In the
Intercontinental title match, Tecker met "Fantasy"Brett Young. Brett Young won the belt when his manager distracted the ref, and he smacked Tecker with a 2 by 4 that just happened to be at ringside. In the WWCF World title match, the boisterous Masked Mayhem was taught a lesson in humility by the Executioner. Mayhem majorly underestimated the skills of the Executioner. Mayhem fell victim to numerous moves from the Executioner. He was beaten when the Executioner used his guillotine manuever. The disappointed Mayhem just hung his head in shame and walked back to the locker room.
