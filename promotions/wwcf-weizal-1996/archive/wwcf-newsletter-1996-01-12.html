W.W.C.F. NEWSLETTER JANUARY 12, 1996 ________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS January 9, 1996 - Providence, Rhode Island - The first ever house card for the W.W.C.F. took place in front of an almost capacity crowd (That damn fat guy took up to many seats). The new titles were all on the line.
In the first match, The Brain met Johnny Banger in a 2-out-of-3 falls match. This was by far the longest match in the history of the
W.W.C.F.! (all of one week,almost). It ran for nearly 80 minutes. The two men were exhausted. The Brain had won the first fall, and Johnny
Banger had won the second fall. Then, during the fight for the third fall, Krisssy Banger pulled her man out of the ring right after he was nailed with the BRAINLOCK! She took Johnny's arm over her shoulder and walked him backed to the locker room. But, here in the W.W.C.F. you cannot lose by countout, so The Brain ran after Johnny, picked him up in a "rebel rack" position and ran back to the ring. Once in the ring,
Johnny received a body slam and was once again locked into the Brainlock! The Brain won by submission. The Surgeon came down to the ring as The Brain was leaving. They gave each other a high-five. Could we see the formation of the "Brain
Surgeons" someday soon? Anyway, The Surgeon faced Brett Young. They fought for nearly 2 minutes before Brett Young cracked The Surgeon's neck with the Dreamer and scored the pinfall.
In the Intercontinental championship, Robbie "Boston" Cooper defended his newly earned gold againest none other than Tecker. This match was one of the best matches most people have ever seen! The bell rang, and immediately Cooper had his arm wrapped around Tecker's head.
He ran across the ring and executed a beautiful flying bulldog. He then squashed Tecker with a knee-drop and a jumping splash. Cooper then picked Tecker up over one shoulder and gave him a power slam into the turnbuckles. Getting his second wind, Tecker nailed Cooper with a spin-kick to the gut as he was attempting a double-ax handle. Tecker lifted Cooper up and propped him on the top turnbuckle. He then gave
Cooper a lightning quich superplex to the arena floor!! Then, out of nowhere, came a huge man out of the audience. It was Jamie Cutlass.
While Tecker's manager Bobby Heenan distracted the official Gob Roberts, Tecker picked up a chair and hit Cooper in the teeth with it. Jamie Cutlass then locked Cooper in his crossbow submission hold! Tecker then dragged Cooper's body into the ring and pinned him for the three count. New W.W.C.F. Intercontinental champion is Tecker.
Finally, in the W.W.C.F. title match-up, the champion Masked Mayhem met the talented Hitman. Masked Mayhem was so furious about Tecker's loss that he smashed his own head into the ringpost when he was entering the ring. He went unconscious and was attended to by manager Josh Brown. The official told Masked Mayhem that he did not have to defend his title if he did not feel able to. But he refused to let the Hitman feel as if he had scared him away, so he prepared for battle.
The announcer was not even finished saying "and his opponent" by the time The Hitman was in the ring. He was so excited to be the number one contender, that he ran up to Mayhem and nailed him with a suplex and a elbow drop before he even took off his ring attire.
Mayhem just wasn't all there. He swung his first into the open air but made no contact. The Hitman delivered a flying dropkick. Then, he picked him up by his hair and nailed him with his Firecracker kick to win the title.
________________________________________________________________ Quick Results The Brain over Johnny Banger in a 2-0ut-of 3 falls encounter Brett Young pinned The Surgeon The Soul Swiper beat The Weizal Brian Damage gained a surprising victory over Shawn Michaels in a Texas Death Match. Jamie Cutlass over Remy in a Submission match. Tecker beat Robbie "Boston" Cooper for the Intercontinental Championship. The Hitman won the W.W.C.F. title from Masked Mayhem. ________________________________________________________________ January 11, 1996 - The Rosemary School for the Blind Gymnasium was jam-packed to the rafters with wrestling fans. There were two title matchs planned for the event. In the Intercontinental title match,
Jamie Cutlass retained his title because the match with Shawn Michaels went to a draw. The referee decided that neither man was able to compete after they had had a 60-minute plus battle.
The W.W.C.F. title match, pitted Champion The Hitman against the dark Soul Swiper. The match started with Soul Swiper delivering fierce chops to the throat of the Hitman. Then he picked the Hitman up for a choke slam. The tides changed when The Hitman gave the Soul Swiper a face-first full nelson slam. The Hitman then climbed the ropes an hit the Swiper with a flipping chest stomp. But, The Soul Swiper just got up as if nothing had happened. This freaked the Hitman out. He gave the
Swiper a strong roundhouse punch but, without blinking, Swiper gave the
Hitman headbutt that knocked him out! The Swiper picked up the Hitman, leaned him againest the ropes and delivered a heart stopping heart punch. The Hitman crumbled to the mat and was pinned by the Soul
Swiper. New W.W.C.F. World Champion is The Soul Swiper.
