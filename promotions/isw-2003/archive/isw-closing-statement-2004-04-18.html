ISW: Turn out the lights.

18 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings from Castillos, I feel more bitter than sad as I write this. As much as I want to keep this fed alive and kicking and as much fun as I've had making new friends and writing these matches.... ....A fed can't stay open if only three of our people on a 10+ man roster will participate. Joseph was coming back with the Cliched Villain, which was going to set up a great angle and group/stable. [name withheld] had joined there were BIG plans for his character. [name withheld] and [name withheld] "Thorin Stonehand" [name withheld] both always had something to add to the card. I hate closing the fed for these guys, along with a few others, that have been unbelieveablely supportive and enthusiastic about ISW. But [name withheld] "Abordar" [name withheld] is headed off on a life changing adventure which I wish him nothing but the best of luck out in BC. Keith has been one of the quintessential guys, along with Joseph,that have kept the cards coming out on time with their match writing. His enthusiasm, along with a few others, have kept this fed going for me, personally. It's not just participation that is calling for me to close, though. I've lost interest in pro-wrestling gradually over the years. There have been times that have pulled be back in here and there, such as Mick Foley's return or Benoit and Guerrero holding the titles. But the more and more I think about it, the less I realize I haven't sat down and WATCHED wrestling. I've stayed up to watch RAW once or twice in the past few months and I'm realizing that I'm just watching it over my shoulder as I surf the net. It's to the point that I don't even consciously watch it, save for a great evening watching WrestleMania this year with some friends. But I digress. It pains me to close Independent Spirit Wrestling. I never thought anyone else outside of me would enjoy my ridiculous characters of Eric "The Eric" Eric and Captain Wonder. It was never my intention to turn this into a parody fed, but I love what it became. Unfortunately, I've regretted bringing Chase Hunter into the fold for my own interests and not finishing the Sherrick feud for him to go on to something else. So as much as I hate to say it, ISW is closed as of this writing. Card #8 won't be finished, which I hate after Joe all ready sent two short matches and Higor sent loads of roleplays and such. I think many of your thought this was pretty inevitable after the period before the previous card. Maybe that's why there was no participation for this one. But it seems that these kind of e-mails get more feedback and reactions than a card announcement. If I had the same kind of feedback from everyone on every card, THEN I could understand keeping ISW open. But it's not. So we're closed. Cheers, [name withheld]
