EWO Sample Match - Sylvester Grey v. Luther Devine

January 23, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The following is a sample of the highlight style matches that will appear on the weekly Eastern Wrestling Organization program Monday Revolution, in the near future.
Take a look. If you like what you see, hop on the roster, there's space left. Highlight style matches make for an easy read, less consumption of your time and cards that will come out regularly. We have two cards a week, one is a summary-style house show and one is in this format.
Check our empty site for the app: http://calypso.spaceports.com/~ewoprez/ Now tell me what you think. =) xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx Sample Match: Sylvester Grey v. "Demon of Death" Luther Devine xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx BEN ARDEN: Hello, everbody, and welcome to this very special EWO exhibition contest. We have a hell of a contrast in styles here, a classic encounter of
David vs. Goliath. The young Sylvester Grey will have the unfortunate task of stepping in the ring with the gargantuan Luther Devine.
MICHAEL KING: I think the odds in this one are in Devine's favor. He's a monster, and it seems that he'll stop at nothing on his way to the EWO World
Title.
BEN ARDEN: It's anybody's guess which waist that title will end up around. We've got a lot of hot young talent waiting in the wings, all are dying to get their hands on that fifteen pounds of leather and gold.
MICHAEL KING: Lets cut the chit chat and head to the ring. [Camera cuts to the center of the ring. Ring announcer Dustin Plant stands in the center, awaiting the competitors.]
BEN ARDEN: As you wish. ["Up Top New York" by A+ hits the PA. Sylvester Grey pushes his way through the curtain to an instant applause. The crowd continues to cheer as he makes his way down to the ring. He has flowing shoulder length brown hair and blue eyes. His pectorals and abdominals are defined. Grey wears a pair of long red tights with black boots and white kneepads. He rolls into the ring and begins to stretch with a focused look on his face.]
MICHAEL KING: Sly looks like he's ready for battle, here. An upset victory could be in the cards for this young man.
BEN ARDEN: Don't count on it. ["One" by Metallica hits the PA. After a few seconds, the crowd begins to boo as the curtains swipe to the sides and a man of mammoth size steps through. He sports a full black bodysuit with "Devine" written on the chest as well as a pair of black leather gloves. He wears a skull mask.]
BEN ARDEN: And there he is! Satan himself! [The crowd explodes into boos as the man lifts his arms high above his head. He stalks down to ringside, and climbs up onto the apron. He stares directly into the eyes of his opponent as he steps over the top rope and into the ring.]
DUSTIN PLANT: Introducing first, in the corner to my left ... weighing in at an even two hundred thirty pounds and standing six feet one inch tall, he hails from Glenns Falls, New York ... I give you ... SYLVESTER GREY!!!
[HUGE CROWD POP!!! Sylvester simply raises his right hand and begins to stretch his legs behind him.]
MICHAEL KING: Exceptional reception for Mr. Grey! They really want to see him pull this one out, Ben!
DUSTIN PLANT: And his opponent, weighing in at three hundred thirty pounds, he stands six feet ten inches tall, from Parts Unknown ... this is ... "THE DEMON
OF DEATH" LUTHER DEVINE!!! [ENORMOUS HEEL HEAT! Devine cracks his neck and stares across the ring. Plant climbs out as quickly as possible.]
BEN ARDEN: The war is on. MICHAEL KING: Alright, here we go, folks. Both men, circling eachother, Grey has a definate speed advantage, but for his size, Devine is certainly not a slow man. You're not going to see a collar and elbow tieup, here.
BEN ARDEN: Not a chance. MICHAEL KING: Both men approach, Devine sees an opening and scores with a quick boot to the abdomen. He grabs his opponent by the hair and delivers a gargantuan headbutt that sends him down to the canvas. Grey quickly back to his verticle base and he's staggered with another boot and fired into the ropes!
BEN ARDEN: Devine's an animal. MICHAEL KING: Devine looks for the powerslam, but no! SPINNING HEADSCISSORS by Grey! What a vertical leap! Devine back up and he's pelted with a dropkick! He staggers back! Another standing dropkick! Devine still on his feet! Grey backs up and hits the ropes -- AND GETS RAILED! DEVINE WITH A
LARIAT OUT OF KNOWHERE! He took the young man's head off!
BEN ARDEN: I was wondering what was going on for second there. MICHAEL KING: Luther Devine "the Demon of Death" grabs him by those locks of brown hair and brings him back to his feet. Devine hastily grabs him and puts him between his legs. Devine lifts ... SPINNING PILEDRIVER! That's career threatening! That could be all, right there! Devine with a non chalant cover!
ONE! TWO! Grey kicks out! He almost had him already, we're only a minute or so into this contest!
XXXXXXXXXXX SKIP 5 MINS XXXXXXXXXXX BEN ARDEN: I told you from the beginning that Devine was going to hammer him. He's been on him since the beginning of this contest, and I don't see a comeback for Grey in the cards. Devine sets him on the top rope, and climbs up to the second.
MICHAEL KING: He grabs him around the head, it looks like he's setting him up for a superplex, here. He lifts! WHAT AN IMPACT! Picture perfect superplex, by the big man. He's got Grey in a vulerable position, and I think he could make a cover anytime, now.
BEN ARDEN: He wants to punish him. Devine is _not_ a nice man. MICHAEL KING: I realize that. Devine, now, climbing outside of the ring. Where's he going? He grabs the chair that Dustin Plant was sitting on and slides it into the ring. Devine, now, climbs back into the ring and Grey is still down! What's he gonna do here?
BEN ARDEN: Like you don't know ... MICHAEL KING: The DEMON DROP!? He's signaling for it! He's going to try to put Grey away with the Demon Drop Brainbuster on a chair! I can't believe he's serious!
BEN ARDEN: Believe it. MICHAEL KING: Devine picks up the prone Sylvester Grey! He hooks him up for the Demon Drop! He lifts ... Grey is wriggles out! Grey scores with a kick to the midsection! DOUBLE ARM DDT BY GREY ON THE CHAIR! Where did he get the energy?! Grey is too winded to make the cover! Both men are down! This could be the turning point in the contest that causes Grey to pull this one out!
BEN ARDEN: I can't believe it! MICHAEL KING: Nor can I! Both men, now, trying to get back to their feet. Sylvester using the ropes to get up, while Devine is on all fours shaking off the blow. Sly is up first, he runs towards Devine and dives for a rollover cradle! ONE! TWO! NO! He almost did it! He almost grabbed the win!
BEN ARDEN: He can't! It won't happen. Luther Devine doesn't lose. It's not in his vocabulary.
MICHAEL KING: Few words are. Both men back to their feet, Grey scores with a forearm to the chest, and now a big chop!
CROWD: WHOO! MICHAEL KING: And another! CROWD: WHOO! MICHAEL KING: And a third! CROWD: WHOO! MICHAEL KING: Grey bounces off the ropes and HE'S CAUGHT -- VICIOUS SPINEBUSTER! DEVINE DAMN NEAR PUT HIM THROUGH THE MAT WITH THAT ONE! THAT COULD BE ALL! COVER! ONE! TWO! THR -- NO! KICKOUT! xxxxxxxxxxx SKIP 3 MINS xxxxxxxxxxx MICHAEL KING: Devine has been in control for virtually this whole contest. He's just brutalizing Sylvester Grey. Grey is down and Devine is mounting the ropes! He's a big man to be going that high in the air.
BEN ARDEN: But for a bigger man, he can fly! MICHAEL KING: Grey is showing no signs of life. Devine up top. He raises his arms high into the air! SWAN DIVE HEADBUTT -- GREY MOVES! GREY MOVES! GREY
MOVES! BEN ARDEN: This could be his last chance! He better capitalize! MICHAEL KING: Grey gets back to his feet, Devine on his way to a standing postion. Grey with a right. Another right! Another right! He hits a boot to the midsection and a swinging neckbreaker on the big man!
BEN ARDEN: Where's Grey going? MICHAEL KING: Grey, now, mounting the turnbuckles and fast! He's up top --
SWANTON BOMB ON DEVINE! Grey scoots backwackward on top of him and hooks a leg for dear life! ONE! TWO! NO! COULDN'T BAG HIM!
BEN ARDEN: But he almost did, and that's a shocker in itself! MICHAEL KING: Grey looking frustrated, he's taken a helacious beating, here, tonight. He pulls his much larger opponent to his feet, and he hits a jawbreaker. Grey drops a knee across the top of the head. Grey, now, stomping the life out of Devine. He pulls him up again.
BEN ARDEN: Grey stopping to catch his breath ... MICHAEL KING: And he picks the big man up for an inverted atomic drop and he scores! Grey backs up and lunges, Devine ducks a clothesline and KICKS HIM
LOW! HE HOOKS HIM FOR A SUPLEX! HE LIFTS! IT'S THE DEMON DROP! HE HIT IT!
GOODNIGHT SYLVESTER GREY! [MASSIVE BOOS!!!] MICHAEL KING: FINGER ON THE CHEST! DO THE MATH! ONE! TWO! THREE! DEVINE TAKES IT! BEN ARDEN: I knew it from the beginning! Devine cannot be beat! ["One" by Metallica hits the PA as the crowd continues to boo. Devine gets up and leans in the near corner, panting.]
MICHAEL KING: Folks, that's all we've got for now! We'll see you some Monday
Night in the very near future ... it's Eastern Wrestling Organization! Where the pain comes to you!
BEN ARDEN: Right. If we find talent. MICHAEL KING: Goodnight, everybody! [name withheld] http://calypso.spaceports.com/~ewoprez/
