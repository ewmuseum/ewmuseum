xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx EASTERN WRESTLING ORGANIZATION Player's Guide v.1.0 xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx by [name withheld] xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx A NEW WAY TO E-WRESTLE It seems that the modern e-wrestler is looking for an organization that takes less time away from real life but provides just as much fun as all the others.
This is the reason for the EWO's conception. Within the last two months, ideas for 'summary' feds and 'highlight' feds have been floating around. This is one of those feds. =)
HOW MATCHES ARE WON EWO will run on a _short_ flash system. Roughly fifty percent of your match will be decided by your weekly _short_ roleplay. I cannot emphasize the word
_SHORT_ enough. This is just a small little league for you to have fun with a little bit of your spare time. I do not want you to send me an essay, just a small flash each week about your matches and talking about your recent angles.
Get to the point, send it in, and be done with it. Approximately twenty five percent of your match will be decided by strats. For each match you have, you are expected to send in a quick little shimmy telling us your basic strategy, a couple of high spots, and a bump or two you'll take. That's all.
The last twenty five percent of your match will be decided by momentum and participation. This is to the staff's discretion. A wrestler who is participating in a major angle, has compiled a decent record and roleplayed for the match has a better chance of winning than a newcomer, but upsets will occur on occassion as well, I'm sure.
EWO MONDAY REVOLUTION Each week on your local public access station will be EWO's Monday Revolution. This will be a highlight based show, at first taking place each week at the
EWO Arena. It will contain roughly four or five matches which will be shown in highlight form. Some strings of maneouvers, high spots, ring psychology and the finale of each match will be shown. It will also be home to two or three in ring or backstage interviews, which will normally be done by one of the top five contenders in the league. Monday Revolution will contain the bigger matches.
EWO HOUSE SHOWS Also each week will be an EWO house show. Coming from a different location each week on Thursdays, seven matches will be delivered to you in summary form.
These house show matches will be roleplayed for and will count towards records. There will rarely be title changes on house shows, but high quality matches will be shown. There will be a couple of interviews on the house shows, but they will be summarized, no transcripts.
TITLES The EWO will start with only three championships. The EWO World Heavyweight
Championship, the EWO Eastern Championship and the EWO Tag Team Championship.
These champions will be decided shortly after the league begins. WEBSITE The EWO website is under construction. WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU All the EWO needs from you is a fairly active character who involves himself in angles and tries to fight his way to the top of EWO.
