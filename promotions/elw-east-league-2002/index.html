Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

East League Wrestling announced its return in April 2002 with fifteen places to fill, a roleplay board, an out-of-character board, a chatroom and a bi-monthly magazine of smark-style articles, and it asked for indy-style locations and atmospheres.

It kept that promise. Its first house show, on 7 May 2002, drew 500 fans through heavy rain to the Thomson CI gymnasium in Scarborough, Ontario, where Android IV was disqualified against the Heavyweight Champion Matthew Golden and the half-brothers of the Saturday Night Asskickers fought each other out of the building and into a nearby park.

Its next card, Wednesday Night Fight, was booked for Jawny Baker's Bar and Grill in East York for 15 May, with its Regional, Tag and Heavyweight titles on the line.

The return notice, that lineup and the house-show results survive; no result of the bar-room card is held.
