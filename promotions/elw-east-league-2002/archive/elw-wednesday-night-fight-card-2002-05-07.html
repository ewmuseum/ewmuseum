ELW Wednesday Night Fight Card Announced

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On Wednesday, May 15th, 2002...East League Wrestling makes its return to Jawny Baker's Bar and Grill in East York, Ontario for ELW Wednesday Fight! This card will feature five huge matchups for ELW's staunch fans. Jimbo Magnum vs. Johnny Thomas Magnum, ELW's newest wrestler, has promised nothing but wrestle his best against Thomas, Johnny, however, ahs promised to beat the living daylights out of Jimbo. can Jimbo wrestle against Toronto's King of Streetfighters? Yamayoshi "Ryu" Polo vs. Kenny One man is a quite martial arts master. The other man is a former member of a boy band. Polo, a former ELW tag team champion with the Japanese Suicides, is in quiet preparation for this match by meditating in his dojo. Kenny is practicing his dance moves for his victory. "ELW Regional Title Challenge" Horny Sam vs. TJ Talgore (c) A former gay porn star takes on a former military major. Sam says he'll beat a hole into Talgore's ass and take the title. TJ says he'll guarantee Sam's departure from the bar -- and ELW -- courtesy of a stretcher. Violence will reign supreme when these two titans clash! "ELW Tag Title Challenge" Calgary Stampede vs. Unstable (c) Tex Roper and Bull Dancer of Calgary Stampede take on Stevie "Power" Kay and 13 of Unstable for the tag team straps. Kay and 13 have been teaming up for little less than a year, while Roper and Dancer have been together for years. Will this factor into the result? And how do the former champions, Saturday Night Asskickers feel about being shunned from the title scene? Check out the match next Wednesday! "ELW Heavyweight Title Challenge" Android IV vs. "Destiny Man" Matthew Golden (c) The man from parts unknown takes on ELW's Destiny Man. Can Android IV and his superiour training succeed against ELW's greatest champion? Golden has never backed down from a challenge. This won't be an exception. A furious fight will follow, for sure. All this is coming, and much, much more. Results will be posted on ELW's website and as well here, at rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy. ELW is still looking for new members...visit East League Wrestling's online website and learn more about us: We're located at http://www.geocities.com/eastleague Thank you; ELW Newsmagazine
