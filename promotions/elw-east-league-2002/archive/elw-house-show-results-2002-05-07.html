ELW House Show Results, 7 May 2002

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ELW held it's first houseshow this past Wednesday (May 7th, 2002) from the Thomson CI gymnasium, in Scarborough, Ontario, Canada. Total attendance was 500 fans, who waited in heavy rain to attend the show. In the first match, 13 (from Unstable) faced off against Jimbo magnum. Jimbo gave it his all, but eventually was hit by the Sweet 13 slam...resulting in a pinfall victory for 13. In the fight between the Saturday Night Asskickers, TJ Talgore and Johnny Thomas went to a no contest, when the two half-brothers fought tooth and nail up and down the halls of the school, outside into the parking lot and into the nearby park. In the third match, Horny Sam challenged Kenny. In what was described as an exciting match, Horny Sam fell victim to the Boy Band Slam and lost the match. During the bout, however, Horny Sam hit Kenny with the Porn Drop but left the ring to flirt with a couple of fans. Kenny recovered, nailed Sam with the Boy Band Slam on the hard wooden floor and again in the middle of the ring. Afterwards, Kenny was quick to serenade a female member of the audience with a cheezy love song. They left the ring arm in arm. Calgary Stampede faced off against Marco Polo in an exciting tag match. Jonathan Marco and Yamayoshi "Ryu" Polo gave the Calgary Stampede (of Tex Roper and Bull Dancer) a run for their money. The ending came as the Stampede hit Polo with the Stampede Lariat and then pinned him. Their celebration was short lived, as from the crowd came TJ Talgore and Johnny Thomas -- the Saturday Night Asskickers -- and they quickly put both Stampede members through tables. Before things could get out of hand, however, Stevie Kay and 13 of Unstable came to the ring to save the Stampede from further damage. Thomas and Talgore left the ring, motioning to their waists, yelling that htey own the titles. In the main event, Android IV faced off against "Destiny Man" Matthew Golden in a non-title match. In a textbook example of cheating 101, Android lost this match by DQ after hitting the Computer suplex off the top rope onto Golden's title belt. During the match, Golden was in control for most of the match until Android hit a low-blow while the referee was tending after some garbage that was thrown into the ring. After the match, Android IV left the ring with his arms held high as paramedics tended to Golden. This was an exciting card, and the fans who attended were happy with the show. The next card, scheduled for May 15th, 2002, was hyped, and was close to being sold out at the time of the announcement.
