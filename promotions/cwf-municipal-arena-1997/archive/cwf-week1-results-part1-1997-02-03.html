CWF Week #1 Results, Part I

3 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera pans out over the thousands of fans cheering wildly in the Herman P. Munson Arena. The CWF theme song plays and the spotlight shines on the broadcast booth. Renaldo and Montel both wave to the crowd and finaly set down. "Welcome CWF fans to another night of Wrestling action! Tonight we have in the main event The CWF World Champion Major Destruction of the Officer Corps. squaring off against The Mysterious Nezbar of the CyberWarriors." "That's not all Renaldo. We also have Emerson Wildside taking on Todd 'The Bodd' Thomas. And The other half of the Cyberwarriors, Zoker, facing The Electric Chair!" "That's right Montel. To start off tonights show we have Captain Carnage of the Officer Corps. going up with Blood of the Gang Lordz!" Blood(w/Crip) vs Captain Carnage (w/The Commander) They lock up. Blood surprises the Captain with a quick backbreaker. Blood then goes for a flying shoulderblock, but Captain Carnage ducks out of the way. Captain picks him up and gives him a Soviet suplex. He then executes a back suplex that leaves blood stunned in the middle of the ring! Captain Carnage executes the Stealth Bomb Drop and goes for the pin. The Ref counts: One, two, in the ropes... Captain nails him with a backbreaker. Captain whips Blood into the ropes and hits Blood with a shoulderblock. Captain throws Blood out of the ring. Captain goes through the ropes and whips Blood into the guardrail. Captain climbs back in the ring and after a 5 count Blood gets in. Captain executes a double underhook backbreaker. Captain hits Blood with an elbow. Captain goes for a spinning backbreaker, but Blood counters it with an elbowsmash. Blood hits Captain with an elbow. Blood executes a single-leg takedown. Blood goes for a powerslam, but Captain counters it with a lariat. Captain nails him with a thrust kick to the head. Captain executes a spin kick. Captain whips Blood into the ropes, but Blood reverses it. Blood throws Captain out of the ring. Blood goes through the ropes. The Commander comes from behind and distracts Blood. Captain executes a back suplex on the floor. Captain executes a belly-to-back suplex on the floor. Blood knocks Captain into the ringsteps. Captain reenters the ring. Blood follows him back in. Blood takes the 9mm and uses it on him. The Ref disqualifies Blood. Blood hits Captain several times before the rest of the Officer Corps runs in and chases him off. The winner is Captain Carnage. Time of match: 0:11:15 "That was some match wasn't it Renaldo?" "Well Montel I prefer to see the matches in the ring. I don't consider this outside the ring brawling real wrestling." "Whatever Renaldo. The Fans like it and so do I. Our next match pits The Unknown Masked Man against Mamba. Mamba vs The Unknown Masked Man Mamba attacks Unknown before the bell. Mamba goes for a backspin DDT, but Unknown counters it with a belly-to-belly suplex. The Ref counts: One, two, shoulder up. Unknown nails him with the Rocker Dropper. Unknown goes for a side suplex, but Mamba blocks it. Apollo Jackson walks to ringside and takes a seat near the ring! Mamba goes for a spinning backbreaker, but Unknown counters it with an elbowsmash. Unknown executes a flying lariat. Unknown then executes a jawbreaker that has Mamba checking for loose teeth! Unknown goes for a kick to the head, but Mamba counters it with a duck-down move. Mamba whips Unknown into the turnbuckle. Mamba charges into the corner. Mamba executes a chop. Mamba takes him down with a rana. The Ref counts: One, two, kickout. Mamba nails him with a sleeperhold. Unknown reaches the ropes after being trapped for 5 seconds. Mamba nails him with a springboard dropkick. Mamba climbs to the top turnbuckle, but Unknown nails him in the stomach. Mamba falls onto the top turnbuckle and flops into the ring. Unknown executes a kick to the midsection. Unknown executes a side suplex. Unknown nails him with a flying elbowdrop and covers him for a pin. The Ref counts: One, two, kickout. Unknown executes the Rocker Dropper. Unknown executes a short clothesline. Unknown runs into the ropes. But Mamba Surprises him with a rana. Mamba executes the Death Strike and goes for the pin. The Ref counts: One, two, three. Outside the ring Apollo stands up and leaves to the lockers. The winner is Mamba. Time of match: 0:18:39 "Now that is the kind of match I like. Lots of action and it was clean. No dirty plays." "You have a pretty boring life don't you Renaldo? The next bout has the High Flying Leaping Poindexter facing off against General Mayhem of the Officer Corps." "Well tonight the Corps is 1 - 0 and I bet The Commander would like to see that they get another win Montel." General Mayhem (w/The Commander) vs Leaping Poindexter They lock up. Poindexter nails him with a standing wristlock. General reaches the ropes after being trapped for 5 seconds. Poindexter goes for an earringer, but General counters it with a backdrop. General nails him with leg scissors. Poindexter reaches the ropes after 18 seconds. General executes a kick to the thigh. General places Poindexter on the turnbuckle. General executes a belly-to-back superplex. General executes a knee to the back. General misses with a shoulderblock. Poindexter hits General with an elbow. Poindexter takes him down with a German suplex. The Ref counts: One, two, thr... kickout. Poindexter takes him down with a kick to the head. Poindexter takes him down with a forearm smash. The Commander throws General Mayhem the Flag Pole and distracts the Ref! General whacks Poindexter with it and goes for the pin. The Ref turns from The Commander and counts: One, two, three. Poindexter argues his case to the Ref but to no avail. The winner is General Mayhem. Time of match: 0:08:44 "That was a very cheap win Montel." "Yes it sure was but notch another victory up for General Mayhem because winning is all that counts." "Whatever Montel. Our next match has the popular Apollo Jackson taking on Commando." Apollo Jackson vs Commando They lock up. Commando executes a Soviet suplex. Commando nails him with a kneeling headbutt. Commando executes a flying lariat. Commando executes the Mercenary Snap and goes for the pin. The Ref counts: One, two, thr... kickout. Commando takes him down with a slingshot. Commando executes a kick to the thigh. Commando is going for the pin. The Ref counts: One, two, shoulder up. Commando takes him down with a double underhook piledriver. Commando goes for a reverse neckbreaker, but Apollo blocks it. Apollo executes a dropkick. Apollo Jackson whips Commando into the turnbuckle. Commando comes back, but is met with a kick to the midsection. Apollo whips Commando into the ropes. Apollo takes him down with a choke slam. Apollo executes a dropkick. Apollo goes for a choke slam, but Commando blocks it. Commando takes him down with a short clothesline. Commando goes for a dropkick to the knee, but Apollo counters it with a side step. Apollo nails him with a flying cross body press and covers him for the pin. The Ref counts: One, two, three. The winner is Apollo Jackson. Time of match: 0:05:02 "A good fast paced match Montel. The fans really liked that one." "The kiddies maybe. I want to see some real action. Where's the Blood?" "Please Montel try to control yourself. Our next bout is the man from the Antarctic Wastelands, Chiller against The High Charged ThunderMaster." Chiller (w/Ms. Steele) vs ThunderMaster Chiller attacks ThunderMaster before the bell. Chiller takes him down with an earringer. Chiller takes him down with a figure-four leglock. ThunderMaster reaches the ropes after being trapped for 5 seconds. Chiller whips ThunderMaster into the ropes. Chiller hits ThunderMaster with an elbow. Chiller takes him down with an armlock leglock submission. ThunderMaster reaches the ropes after being trapped for 6 seconds. Chiller whips ThunderMaster into the turnbuckle. Chiller charges into the corner and nails him with a big Elbowsmash to the head. Chiller takes him down with a swinging punch. Chiller takes him down with a legsweep. Chiller goes for a slap, but ThunderMaster reverses it. ThunderMaster hits Chiller with a shoulderblock. ThunderMaster whips Chiller into the turnbuckle. ThunderMaster nails him with a kick to the head. ThunderMaster takes him down with a kneeling headbutt. ThunderMaster goes for a snap mare, but Chiller blocks it. Chiller takes him down with a legsweep. Chiller nails him with a full nelson. Chiller lets go after 13 seconds. Chiller executes a chop. Chiller hits ThunderMaster with a clothesline that knocks ThunderMaster out of the ring. Chiller jumps onto him with an IceQuake, but ThunderMaster moves out of the way. ThunderMaster throws Chiller back into the ring. ThunderMaster places Chiller on the turnbuckle. ThunderMaster executes a belly-to-back superplex. ThunderMaster executes the Thunderdriver and goes for the pin. Earl Hebner counts: One, two, Ms. Steele puts Chiller's foot on the rope. Ms. Steele throws Chiller the Purse. Chiller hits ThunderMaster with it and goes for the pin. The Ref counts: One, two, three. The winner is Chiller. Time of match: 0:13:18 "Another cheap victory tonight. This time it's for Chiller. This is getting ridiculous Montel." "Yes it is Renaldo. All of these dirty, under handed tactics and not a drop of blood. It's a down right outrage!" "Jeesh Montel give it a rest with this blood thing will you? Did you get into another fight with the old lady?" "Ummm..well...the next fight is Crip of the Gang Lordz facing Another of Ms. Steele's Men, Zed The Bounty Hunter." Zed the Bounty Hunter (w/Ms. Steele) vs Crip They lock up. Crip executes a chokehold. The Ref counts: One, two, three, four. Crip runs into the ropes. Zed hits Crip with an elbow. Zed executes neck-scissors. Zed takes him down with neck-scissors. Zed whips Crip into the ropes. Zed misses with a shoulderblock. Crip goes for a bodyslam, but Zed blocks it. Zed takes him down with a belly-to-back suplex. Zed whips Crip into the ropes. Ms. Steele trips Crip. The Ref warns Ms. Steele. Zed takes him down with an enzuilariato. Zed hits Crip with a backdrop that knocks Crip out of the ring. Zed goes through the ropes. Crip nails him with a chair and then picks him up for a big Powerslam on the Floor! Crip throws Zed into the ring. Crip nails him with a cobra clutch. Zed submits after 15 seconds. The winner is Crip. Time of match: 0:09:04 "Fast and brutal. Crip is one vicious wrestler." "Yep. He sure is Renaldo. My kind of guy." "Hmm....well or next match is The Red Demon vs. Seriel Killer." Serial Killer vs Red Demon They lock up. Killer nails him with a slap. Demon takes him down with a legsweep. Killer takes him down with an elbowsmash. Killer nails him with a piledriver. Killer nails him with a stomp. Killer executes a slingshot splash and covers for the pin. The Ref counts: One, shoulder up. Killer complains about a slow count and while he is distracted Demon misses him with a kick. Killer takes him down with a facerake. Killer executes a piledriver. Killer runs into the ropes. Demon hits Killer with a shoulderblock. Demon takes him down with an avalanche. Demon goes for a backdrop driver, but Killer counters it with a backward kick. Killer whips Demon into the ropes, but Demon reverses it. Demon hits Killer with a shoulderblock. Demon nails him with a flying clothesline. Demon executes the Power Bomb and goes for the pin. The Ref counts: One, two, three. The winner is Red Demon. Time of match: 0:08:13 "That was a good match Montel." "Yes a big victory for the Red Demon. He's sure to move up in the rankings with this win over Seriel Killer." "Our next fight pits One half of the Cyberwarriors, Zoker, against The Electric Chair." The Electric Chair vs Zoker They lock up. Zoker runs into the ropes. Zoker and The Electric Chair hit each other with a double clothesline. Zoker whips The Electric Chair into the ropes. The Electric Chair misses with a clothesline. Zoker misses with an elbow. The Electric Chair misses with an elbow. Zoker misses with a clothesline. The Electric Chair hits Zoker with a clothesline. The Electric Chair whips Zoker into the turnbuckle. The Electric Chair charges into the corner, but Zoker moves out of the way. Zoker executes a chop. Zoker catches him in a power bomb. Earl Hebner counts: One, two, three. The winner is Zoker. Time of match: 0:00:37 "Wow what a fast match! Zoker sure caught Electric chair by surprise with that power bomb." "Zoker is one explosive wrestler! Speaking of explosive our next match features Emerson Wildside, a finalist in the CWF Title tournament going against Todd 'The Bodd' Thomas, who made it to the semis." Todd Thomas vs Emerson Wildside Todd attacks Emerson Wildside before the bell. Todd executes a forearm smash. Todd executes a forearm smash. Todd takes him down with a headlock takedown. Todd nails him with a spinning backbreaker. Todd goes for the ST-S, but Emerson Wildside counters it with a kick-off. Emerson whips Todd into the ropes. Emerson misses with a kick. Emerson executes a swinging neckbreaker. Emerson executes a side suplex. Emerson goes for a spinebuster slam, but Todd counters it with a swinging neckbreaker. Todd runs into the ropes. Todd hits Emerson with a backdrop. Emerson falls out of the ring. Todd jumps out of the ring. Emerson whips Todd into the guardrail. Emerson throws Todd back into the ring. Emerson whips Todd into the ropes. Emerson hits Todd with a backdrop. Emerson nails him with a tiger suplex and goes for the pin. The Ref counts: One, two, three. The winner is Emerson Wildside. Time of match: 0:04:12 "Wow a big win for Emerson and a tough lose for Todd." "Yeah the fans are ready for the next matchup with features The CWF World Champ, Major Destruction of the Officer Corps. going against The Mysterious Nezbar, One half of the CyberWarriors." Major Destruction (w/The Commander) vs Nezbar They lock up. Nezbar nails him with a punch. Nezbar hits Major Destruction with a clothesline. Nezbar is going for the pin. The Ref counts: One, kickout. Nezbar nails him with a forearm smash. Nezbar goes for a belly-to-back suplex, but Major Destruction counters it with an elbowsmash. Major Destruction runs into the ropes. Nezbar misses with a clothesline. Major Destruction hits Nezbar with a shoulderblock. Major Destruction goes for a dropkick, but Nezbar counters it with a side step. Nezbar catches him with a big clothesline. Nezbar then picks up the champ and gives him a HUGE Powerbomb. Nezbar quickly covers him up for the pin. The Ref counts: One, two, three. The winner is Nezbar. Time of match: 0:01:34 "Wow!! A huge victory for Nezbar! Wow This means that Nezbar get's a title shot next week!" "The Champ certainly didn't look very dominating in this match as he did in the tournament." "Nezbar was loaded for bear and found a Squirrel this time Renaldo." "You've got that right. Tune in for the next set of matchups at Star Haven Arena."
