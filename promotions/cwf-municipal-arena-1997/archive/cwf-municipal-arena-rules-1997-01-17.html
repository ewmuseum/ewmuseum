CWF Fantasy Wrestling Rules and Registration

17 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fantasy Wrestling Rules and Registration 1. What is Fantasy Wrestling? Fantasy wrestling is a game where you control your wrestler as he or she faces other fantasy wrestlers in the fight to become the Champion. You decide what kind of wrestler you want to have and you also decide what moves your wrestler does. Based upon this information your wrestler will compete against other wrestlers in wrestling matches. Two times a week your wrestler will wrestle against others for the goal of becoming champion. 2. What do I do to get a Wrestler? First off you send Me([name withheld]) a private message telling me The name of the wrestler, what kind of Wrestler, and a list of his Wrestling moves. Thats it. Email address [detail withheld] 3. What do I name my Wrestler? You can name your wrestler anything you want except for the name of an actual wrestler(unless it is a generic name like The Assasin, etc..) If you don't know whether there is a wrestler with the name you are Submit a list of names that you would like in order of preference and I will go down the list and the first name that isn't an actual wrestler will be used. Also the use of profanity in a name is also not allowed. Or a name that implies anything sexual or demeaning to others( ex. Whacker , Asslover, Jew Slayer, etc...) 4. What types of wrestlers are there? Ground Level Scientific ex. Bob Backlund,Brett Hart The classic type of wrestler that mainly keeps things on the mat and uses wear down/control type holds. High Flyer Scientific ex. Rick Steamboat, Great Muta Uses Scientific wear down/control holds but biggest asset is coming off of the ropes with variety of flying manuevers. Ground Level Unconventional ex. Hulk Hogan, Sid Vicious Uses some scientific holds but prefers to brawl and stay on the mat. High Flyer Unconventional Ex. Sabu, Cactus Jack Uses some Scientific holds but prefers to brawl and goes to the ropes often. Also you need to tell me what sort of cheat factor you desire. High Will cheat often. Ex Cactus Jack, Terry funk Medium Will cheat occasionaly if match is not going in his favor ex. Vader, Shawn Michaels Low Rarely cheats unless in retaliation Ex. Brett Hart, Rick Steamboat 5. What types of wrestling holds/manuevers do I give you? Each wrestler automatically knows how to punch, Kick, chop, and bite. You need to send me a list of 20 wrestling moves. Then list 5 Moves that will be termed your setup moves. Then give me your one finishing move/hold that your wrestler uses to finish off his opponents. The following is a list of holds/moves that you can use to help you create your wrestler. (note if a move isn't listed, it doesn't mean that your wrestler won't be able to do it. This list isn't complete.) Just give me a description of what the hold/move does. 6. What exactly happens when my wrestler wrestles? Each wrestler gets a random amount of Health point(between 85 - 120). Each wrestling move does a certain amount of damage each time it is done( Between 5 and 20) When a wrestlers health has been reduced to a certain level then he can be pinned by the other. The further the health is reduced the more likely the pin will be successful. The exact process that is gone through for each match I won't tell but it is done as realistically as possible. 7. Most wrestlers get better the more they wrestle. Will mine be able to improve and learn new holds? Yes! Every match that a wrestler wins they get 100 xp points. For each match your wrestler losses they get 20 xp points(you can learn from your losses). At every 1000 XP points your wrestler will gain 5 health points and add 5 health points to his finisher or to one of his set up holds. Also your wrestler will be allowed to learn 1 new move. 8. Wrestlers get hurt sometimes. Will mine ever get hurt? That depends. If your wrestler's health is reduced to a certain point then there is the chance that they will become injured and not be able to wrestle for a randomly determined number of matches. 9. Not all matches in wrestling are normal. Neither are these. Right now there are rules set up for cage matches, Battle royals, Ironman, tag team matches, Barbed Wire, Survivor series and several others. As time progresses more specialty matches will be added. But as in most cases there is a higher risk of getting injured in specialty matches. 10. What about titles? Are there titles in this? Yes! The first weeks action will be a tournament and the winner of it will be crowned the CWF champion. From that point on the chamion will defend the title agains the number 1 contender each week until they lose the title. 11. How are the Rankings figured out? The rankings are based upon your wrestlers performance during the weeks matches based upon wins and level of competition. 12. What if I get tired of my wrestler? Well then let me know and you can create a new one. I would prefer if you would stay with your wrestler as long as possible but I understand that sometimes you might come up with something you like better. 13. What if I have a question that isn't answered here? Well...leave me( [detail withheld] ) a message and I'll answer your questions.
