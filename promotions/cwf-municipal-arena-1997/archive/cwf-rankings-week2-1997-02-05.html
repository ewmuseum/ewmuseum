Ironman 20 We would like to take this time to welcome back Maynard and Gaynard Gerkle and Welcome a new wrestler to the CWF Hellstorm. They aren't ranked now but will be ranked next week after they wrestle. Here are the official matchups for Week #2 #1 Major Destruction vs. Crip - Non-title Nezbar vs. Todd Thomas Emerson Wildside vs. Zoker Red Demon vs. Seriel Killer General Mayhem vs. Zed the Bounty Hunter Apollo Jackson vs. Chiller The Electric Chair vs. Captain Carnage Mamba vs. Leaping Poindexter The Unknown Masked Man vs. Blood Thundermaster vs. Commando Ironman vs. Gaynard Gerkle Hellstorm vs. Maynard Gerkle #2 Major Destruction vs. Commando - Non-title Nezbar vs. Ironman Todd Thomas vs. ThunderMaster Crip vs. Maynard Gerkle Emerson Wildside vs. Gaynard Gerkle Red Demon vs. Hellstorm Seriel Killer vs. The Unknown Masked Man General Mayhem vs. Leaping Poindexter Zoker vs. Captain Carnage Apollo Jackson vs. Mamba Chiller vs. The Electric Chair Zed The Bounty Hunter vs. Blood #3 Major Destruction vs. Nezbar Title Match Todd Thomas vs. Zoker Crip vs. Apollo Jackson Emerson Wildside vs. Chiller Red Demon vs. Zed The Bounty Hunter Seriel Killer vs. ThunderMaster General Mayhem vs. Blood The Electric Chair vs. Maynard Gerkle Mamba vs. Commando Captain Carnage vs. Gaynard Gerkle Leaping Poindexter vs. Hellstorm The Unknown Masked Man vs. IronMan There you go folks. The following matches have been requested for next week. Emerson Wildside Challenges Mamba to a Falls count anywhere match. The Gang Lordz Challenge The Iron Fists to a no DQ Tag match. The Officer Corps. Challenge The Iron Fists to a Tag match. Red Demon Challenges General Mayhem to a Cage match. If you've been challenged you have until sunday to let me know if you accept the challenge. Also If you are still looking for a tag partner for the tournament in two weeks you need to let me know who you would like to tag with so I can try to get you two together. Also if you want to do a run in for this week you need to let me know what match and what you plan to do by friday of this week.
