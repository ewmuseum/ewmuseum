CWF World Title Tournament, Results Part 1

28 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cwf World Title Tournament The lights in Municipal Arena slowly dim and the sellout crowd starts cheering as they know that they are about to witness one of the greatest moments in wrestling history. The spotlight shines on the announcers desk. Two men are sitting behind the mikes. One of them wearing a classy black tuxedo while the other wears an eyesore bright yellow and pink plaid suit. The man in the tuxedo speaks first "Welcome Wrestling fans! Are you ready for some of the best wrestling ever?" The crowd roars in approval. "Are you ready to see the most exciting show in your life?" The crowd roars louder this time, you can feel the arena shake! "Well then let's not delay any longer! Welcome to the CWF!!!" The crowd is all standing now screaming and shouting. "Tonight you will witness 32 men in action. All of them fighting for the CWF world title! The endurance and skills of these men shall be pushed to the limits as the man who wins the title will have to face not one man. Not two men. But Five men!" The crowd cheers loudly! The man in the ugly suit speaks into the mike "I'd like to welcome those of you watching this on PPV. My name is Montel Springer and the man you just heard from is Renaldo Rivera. Before we get started with the first match let's show you a little footage of the men that will be in this tournament. "The scene cuts away to a room with several large maps covering the walls in it. Inside are four men dressed in Army BDU's plotting out maneuvers on a table sized layout of the wrestling ring. One of the men wearing 5 stars on his collar speaks up and says: "I'm the Commander and these three men are General Mayhem, Major Destruction, and Captain Carnage. We comprise the foundation of the Officer Corps. With my enormous battle trained intellect and these three men's superb physical capabilities we are assured victory tonight as we capture our objective, The Cwf World title. Now Get out of here these are top secret discusions and you aren't authorized!" The screen cuts away to two men sitting in a locker room with a large woman wearing a housecoat shaking a finger at them vigorously. She turns towards the camera and says: "These here are my twin boys Maynard and Gaynard! Say hello boys" The two identicle looking men both wave their hands and with goofy smiles on their faces and say "Hi everyone!" in unison. "Okay that's enough outta you two!" Screams the fat lady. "show some respect towards your mother! Now where was I . Oh yeah! My two boys are going to the top in this here wrestling federation. I trained them myself. For those of you who dno't recognize me I'm the 13 time World Womens Wrestling Champion, Yolanda Gerkle. My two boys are the smartest two wrestlers in this federation. Why look at them. Maynard is already compiling a program on his laptop that will assure him of a victory." The one without the laptop speaks up "But mom do we hafta wrestle?" "Gaynard! Shut your mouth boy. You speak when you are told to!" The Scene fades with the image of Mother Gerkle grabbing Gaynard's ear and shaking a finger in his face. The scene cuts back to the announcers desk where Renaldo and Montel are looking over the tournament lineup. Renaldo looks up at the camera and says "Welcome back fans. It's truly great to have you here for tonights event." Montel pipes in "Let's get on with some action Renaldo. We dont' want the fans to get antsy." "Okay Montel let's give the audience a run down of tonights event. In the first round of matches there is a 10 minute time limit. In the second round it's 15 minutes. The third and fourth round are 20 minutes and the final match of the evening will go as long as it takes to crown a champion. In all rounds Disqualifications and countouts are in effect." "Gee renaldo it would sure be stupid for someone to get disqualified with the CWF world title on the line." "That's very insightful Montel. Our first matchup of the first round will be Major Destruction of the Officer Corps. going up against Leaping Poindexter." The camera pans out over the packed arena showing the thousands of excited fans and finally settles on the Ring positioned in the center of the arena. A drumroll is played over the PA system and a Military Cadence starts blaring out. An american flag is Displayed on the overhead Jumbotron screen and out of the entrance emerges Major Destruction accompanied by his manager The Commander. The fans cheer but aren't quite sure of what to make of him and his manager. After saluting several fans the Major climbs into the ring. Major Destruction stands a good 6'7 and weighs a healthy 315 lbs. He's wearing camoflauged pants, Black military combat boots, and t-shirt with US Army written across it. Once the fans die down over the PA system the music changes to some odd song being played by a pan flute and charging out of the entrance is leaping Poindexter. Poindexter has blazing red hair and stands 6'1 and weighs 230 lbs. He's wearing dusty green wrestling trunks with splotches of pink and yellow spray painted on them. He gets in the ring and climbs on the turnbuckles and raises his hands and let's out a yell to the crowd. The crowd is rather amused by this odd looking character but soon jump out of their seats in excitment as The Major rushes across the ring and yanks poindexter off the turnbuckles and slams him to the mat. The ref signals for the bell which is rung and the match officially begins. "What we have here folks is a classic case of a much bigger man and ground technician in the Major going against the much smaller but quicker high flying Poindexter." "Thanks for pointing out the obvious Montel" "Hey that's what I'm paid for." In the ring Major has poindexter in a painful looking abdominal stretch. After close to 30 seconds go by major releases the hold and picks up Poindexter for a big bodyslam. The crowd is still undecided if they should cheer for him or not when The Major starts giving Poindexter a series of vicious stomps to the neck and back area. The Major picks up Poindexter and slams him back to the mat with a quick belly to belly suplex. The Major then scoops up Poindexter and performs his finisher, 'Heavy Artillery', on him and covers him for the pin. The ref jumps down to check the shoulders and then counts...One!....Two!...Three!!!!!!! "That was an impressive win for Major Destruction!" "You aren't kidding Renaldo. Poindexter just never recovered from that attack before the bell." 'Coming up in our next matchup we have Nova vs. The Electric Chair." "What! Someone is going to wrestle a Piece of furniture?!!" "I read that right Montel. The man's name is Electric Chair." "Well this next match pits the power of an exploding sun against something used to get rid of undesirables. It should be interesting." A loud explosion booms out over the PA system and a man dressed in a Black and Red bodysuit comes running out of the entrance and jumps into the ring.. Sparks shoot out from the ringposts and the man does a springboard backflip in the ring which the crowd appreciates with a large round of applause and cheers. Nova stand 6'2 and weighs 240 lbs. The next entrant in the tournament makes his way to the ring with little fanfare. The ringside announcers have no information on him. Electric chair charges at Nova before the bell but Nova ducks out of the way and Electric Chair slams into the turnbuckles hard. Nova takes advantage of the moment and plants some savate kicks into the gut of Electric Chair. Nova then picks him up for a devastating piledriver! Nova climbs the ropes but slips and falls to the ring side floor! Electric Chair recovers and jumps off the apron with an axehandle into Nova's back. Electric Chair then grabs Nova by his long blond hair and slams his head into the ring post! Electric Chair then slams Nova's head into the post a second and a third time before shoving him into the ring. Nova has a nasty looking cut opened up and is bleeding all over the place! Electric Chair picks him up for a chokeslam and then climbs the ropes himself. Electric Chair jumps off the ropes doing his finishing manuever The Shocker and covers him up for the pinfall victory! 'Well that certainly got ugly don't you think Montel?" "Ugly? Now that's what I call wrestling. If there isn't any blood then it wasn't a very good match in my opinion." "Well Montel most fans prefer to see a good clean match. But anyway or next match up pits one half of the Cyberwarriors, Zoker against The Awesome Areo.' Zoker makes his way to the ring accompanied by his tag Partner Nezbar. Along the way they get into several arguments with fans at ringside and after a fan spits on him, Zoker gives him a shove that knocks down several other fans. The Awesome Areo comes bounding out of the entrance and performs a series of somersaults and backflips down the walkway. Amazingly the match doesn't start until the bell rings and both men move in cautiously sizing the other up. Zoker moves in on Areo but is thrown across the ring with a lightning fast monkey flip and the crowd cheers in delight! Areo charges across the ring to take advantage of the momentum but Zoker slides under the ring rope to the outside floor. Areo steps back and let's the ref count and on the count of eight Zoker slides back into the ring. From the outside Nezbar jumps up on the apron and points at Areo and yells something at the ref. The ref and Areo both turn towards Nezbar and Zoker moves in a nails Areo with a dropkick. Nezbar jumps back down to the floor while Zoker pummels Areo with a series of kicks and punches. Zoker picks up Areo and gives him a devastating powerslam. Zoker then leaps onto the turnbuckles and jumps off of them with a moonsault onto Areo. Zoker grabs the leg and pins him and the ref counts to three. "Sure are a lot of cheap victories montel." "A cheap win is a win none the less. Besides isn't it prudent to take advantage of the situations to your favor?" "Hmm..Interesting philosophy montel. Our next matchup pits Crip of the Gang Lords agains The Jester."
