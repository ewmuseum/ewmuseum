ACWA Wants Tag Teams

Let me just say that this federation is going to be nothing short of great. First off, this is both a real and fake federation. Originality is what I like. Stay fresh and exciting while RPing. If you are looking for a fun federation with plenty of stiff competition, join the ACWA. But don't just take my word for it. Find out for yourself! C U L8er =)
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (~Ŋ·ļđ°°­ŽŠĶ| Rules |Ķ ŠŽ­°°đļ·Ŋ~) There are a couple of rules we would like you to abide by. Once we get you to follow these rules it will make this fed much more pleasurable.
1. This Roleplay fed is elite, and I would expect around a 50 line minimum. 2. If you don't Roleplay for two cards in a row, you will be suspended for a week. If this happens twice, your fired. I'll give you slack if your on a vacation or something.
3. If you TOS, or punt anyone in the fed for anything, you will be fired -- unless a good reason for it -- if a member is cussing you please report it to me and we'll take care of it. 4. Cussing is allowed but keep it to a minimum. I don't want you to just go out there and cuss out everyone in your RP, and if you do you will be suspended. 5. You may not use another wrestler in your roleplay from this fed without their permission. You can use other people that are not in the ACWA. 6. Here is the rule for wrestlers: 1 Wrestler= 1 Singles Wrestler that can tag 7. No junk mail. I am tough on this since it has ended many federations. If you send junk mail you will be suspended and/or fired. It depends on what it is. You got a problem send it to me and only me.
9. I am not the only one that recruits. If you know any good RPers, send them this way. 10. I do not want to hear excuses about you having no time to RP because you are in other feds. If you are in other federations and do not have time for both please quit that one or this one. I would rather have none of your attention at all than having half of it. If you think you can handle it, like many have, then you will be pretty much okay.
11. Don't join if you are not going to RP. Don't join and then quit as soon as you join. If you are not dedicated to RPing than go somewhere else and don't waste our time.
12. Last but not least, hey guys have fun man. Thats what feds are, if you don't have fun I certainly won't have fun so if you do your best you'll make this fed the funnest on the internet.

They can happen any day of the week.) * Wednesday Warfare * This is the "Raw Is War" of the ACWA. It will include 7-10 matches and usually have a couple of interviews. *Saturday Slaughter * This is the "SMACKDOWN!" of the ACWA. Usually we have 5-7 matches here and maybe one interview. PPV * There will be a Pay-Per-View (PPV) every month. These will be where our big shots face off for some of the greatest action in the Wrestling World
Today. The Heavyweight Title is usually held up in a great gimmick match with some kind of an angle.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (~Ŋ·ļđ°°­ŽŠĶ| Titles |Ķ ŠŽ­°°đļ·Ŋ~) * Heavyweight Title * Well no doubt this is the most Precious/Hard to get title in the ACWA It doesn't get any better than this. It is what all wrestlers dream about. You will have to go through the biggest, baddest, meanest and toughest that the ACWAhas to offer.
* Extreme Title * This is for those Extreme die-hard wrestlers who don't care what happens to their bodies. Anything goes and weapons are encouraged. *IC Title * This is for those superstars that have come a long way. Wrestlers that can't get hold of that Heavyweight Title just yet. If you win this you will be reconized and may even get a shot at the Heavyweight Title if you hold onto it for more then 2 weeks.
* US Title * This is for the new wrestlers in the fed. Defended a lot more than any other ACWA title. Some call it a stepping-stone to bigger and better things as well as a boost to spark your career. The winner of this will have to defend it weekly, and is considered one of the hardest titles to hold onto.
* Tag Team Titles * This is the Heavyweight championship to all you tag team out there. This is the best you can get. These are for any tag team in the division that has earned their shot at the gold. This title will begin to come into play once enough tag teams join.
(You can hold onto any two belts at a given time except the Global Heavyweight championship. If you have it and any other title, the other one will be held up to be decided for at a later time. The most you can hold is two titles. Once you hold two, you will not get another title shot.)
