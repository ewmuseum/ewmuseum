Sunday Slaughter, 3 October 1999

3 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "One year has passed," said the dark man. Hidden in shadows and seated on a throne of stone the man looked out over the dingy room. The thin woman barred her teeth and him, and clutched her hand in a fist. "More than a year S..." The large man stopped Dina Sol's voice with a swat of his hand, backhanding her into the wall. She landed near a feral looking man who wore a chain around his neck that latched him to the cold wall. Fallen watched Dina Sol's head crack into the wall and slide down to the ground, already unconscious. Fallen looked up into the eyes of the man who had attacked her, and his expression was one of dead stillness. The two locked eyes, and for a moment fire appeared in the eyes of John Skyler. It passed as he let his head droop down, Fallen once more. The dark man stood, letting his robes fall about his legs. His face was cloaked in shadows and dark green eyes pierced the dark. "Everything changes now. The time for fun and games are over... I've gotten stuck here, a bump in the road on the path to greater glory. Now... now there are imposters that roam the streets, and my plans are going to succeed. Others might deny it, others might ignore it, but this is the way it must be. There are five that must be dealt with... five that can stop us..." The man in shadows looked down at Fallen and the unconscious woman by his side that was once his wife. "One year has passed. One more must not. Find them... destroy them all." The scene faded, and with a pop and burst of fireworks, Sunday Slaughter began. Primetime (c) vs. Wolf Fang Inside the arena the crowd roared as the first match for the card was announced to be for the fWo Cruiserweight Title. Finally, weeks after making the challenge, Wolf Fang received his long overdue title shot at Primetime. Wolf Fang made his way to the ring first, his first appearance in an fWo ring in some time. Primetime made his way out with the Cruiserweight Title around his waist, and the Boston Strangler in two. The referee signaled for the bell and the match began. The match started out slowly with neither man gaining a clear cut advantage, before Primetime used a low-blow to take over, before following it up with a cradle piledriver. Primetime went for a cover, but only got a two count. Primetime followed things up with a series of short kicks to the back of the head, letting each repetitive blow take it's toll. Primetime went for another cover, and Wolf Fang got a shoulder up before three. Primetime went for an aerial attack next, flying off with a missile dropkick. He then brought Wolf Fang up and hit a fisherman's suplex, and Wolf Fang again narrowly escaped defeat. Primetime threw Wolf Fang over the top rope onto the floor, before diving out after Wolf Fang with a pescado. Wolf Fang moved out of the way and Primetime hit the mat. Wolf Fang whipped Primetime back first into the guardrail, before bringing PT back into the ring. He scooped slammed him, before going up top to follow off with an elbow drop. Wolf Fang took way too long climbing up on the way up, as if he seemed rather uninterested with completing his next move. PT followed him out onto the ring apron, and after several blows back and forth PT positioned Wolf Fang for a Primetime Bomb off the top rope. Soon after a pinfall was made, and Primetime retained the Cruiserweight Title. Primetime left the ring with the championship intact while Slaughter went to a commercial break. Result: Primetime by Pinfall Breakup "Nikki... Nikki... why won't you talk to me?" The Speed Demon ran after Nikki, who walked confidently down the hall. Speed Demon reached out and grabbed the shoulder of Nikki, and the former fWo Womens Champion wheeled around to face him. "Why won't I talk to you? Why?" Nikki reached up and made an L with one hand. "Because you're a LOSER!" The Speed Demon backed up a pace, visibly hurt by her statement. "Wha... but Nikki... I... I... I'm your great love! I'm the father of your future child... I..." Nikki slapped Speed Demon across the face, cutting him off in the middle of his babbling. "Your child? God you are like, such an idiot Speed! I never even had sex with you... you kept finishing... quite a bit before me if you know what I mean. And like I'd let some dorky jobber sleep with me? Please. I was just using you to make Primetime jealous, and you couldn't even do that right you stupid dork! Like I would have sex with a guy that plays video games and trading card games all day... I am SO sure! You and I are so finished!" Nikki stormed off, as the Speed Demon began to sob. Outside in the arena, Nikki and the Speed Demon's names were called, as the first match in the Tag Team tournament was scheduled to begin... Scott Slugger & Ice vs. Nikki & Speed Demon As loud music and other noise was heard, Speed made his way out crying. Speed climbed into the ring and continued to sob and pout, before slumping down in the middle of the ring and crying some more. The infamous music of Scott Slugger played and out came he and Ice. Ice charged down into the ring and began to pummel Speed relentlessly, while Slugger took his time coming down. Seconds later, as seen via the Slaughterscreen, The Hollywood Blondes looked on from a luxury box. Ice continued to beat on Speed with ease, tossing him around the ring like a redheaded stepchild and beating him unmercilessly. Ice then hit his finisher, the 'Icebreaker' from the ropes. He pinned Speed, but at the count of two he pulled him up and laughed at him in his face. He then pointed at Slugger and asked Slugger if he wanted to finish things off. Slugger said "Sure... why not?", and Ice tagged him in. Slugger came in without removing his baseball jersey. He hit the 'Curveball' on Speed and prepared to pin him. He first pinned him with his thumb, but decided that was too easy. He then removed a strand of his own hair and gently let it rest above Speed' chest, but decided that was too easy. He then bit off one of his fingernails and went for a pin again, but decided that was even easier than before. Finally, he removed a button from his jersey and used it to finish off Speed, as Ice continued to laugh his proverbial ass off. James Armstrong giggled like a school girl and continued to give praise to Slugger. Slugger and Ice left without breaking a sweat, as Slaughter went to a commercial break. Result: Scott Slugger & Ice by Pinfall Farewell Backstage some activity was going on in the locker room of Ground Zero. Wolf Fang appeared to be packing his bag, and Jackhammer looked distressed. "Fang... man, where are you going? We've got a match later! Come on... where are you going?" Wolf Fang sat down for a moment, looking down at the half-packed back and shook his head. "Jackhammer... you know nothing would make me happier than walking down that ramp, get into the ring with you, and taking back those tag team titles. But Jackhammer... this is something I've got to do now. I started in the fWo a year ago and look at me... I haven't grown as a wrestler. I need some time... time to train, time to get things in order. I've got to do this man... and I'll be back... I've just got to do this." Wolf Fang shook Jackhammer's hand, and walked out the locker room door. Jackhammer watched him go with a sad expression on his face, and then stared blankly at the wall. After a moment of silence, Jackhammer reached down and laced up his boots. Los Latin vs. Fyre The music of Los Latin began to play, and the Spanish wrestler walked to the ring with a large Spanish flag behind him, waving in the air. Los Latin climbed into the ring and smirked at the crowd, calling them all stupid Americans, and telling them that tonight would be another ass kicking for an American hero, this time one of the biggest one yet. Fyre's music began to play, and Fyre came out onto the ramp with a neck brace. Fyre looked at Los Latin and the bookers incredulously, asking why she was booked for a match when she was on injured reserve. The bookers only threw their bottles of whisky at her, obviously drunk. Groaning and holding her neck, Fyre walked to the ring where Los Latin was waiting. Los Latin dove from the top rope to the outside of the ring, catching Fyre on the outside and driving her into the steel of the ramp. Fyre screamed aloud as Los Latin raised his arms to the crowd in victory, and then rolled Fyre into the ring. With much flair, Los Latin placed one foot on the ample chest of Fyre and "covered" her for the three count. Los Latin told the referee that this was not enough, and told the referee to count to ten. The referee did this, and Los Latin continued to mock the crowd. Grabbing a microphone he said, "Americans... you all the same eh? All los gringos stupidos... all falling before the mighty hand of the Spanish empire. Even now our Taco Time restaurants sweep this nation... poisoning you Americans with our fried tator tots! And after this weak country falls... we will take Canada... and then France!" As this last word was said the lights in the arena went out, and the crowd was pitched into darkness. Sounds of a scuffle were heard, and when the lights returned on a moment later Los Latin was prone in the ring, unconscious... Result: Los Latin by Pinfall The Breaking Point The large man Rain walked down the halls unsteadily. He looked tired, worn, and dark circles were under his eyes. His head darted from side to side, and he looked around him in a state that ventured on paranoia. Geoff Green saw him pass down the hall and trotted after him. "Rain... Rain... what's going on? You look... my god man, what's wrong?" Rain turned slowly and looked at Geoff Green's face. Rain reached out and held Geoff's face, turning it from side to side as if looking for something. Rain nodded slowly as if satisfied. "Go away Geoff... you wouldn't understand what's going on... this is something I do alone." Geoff looked at Rain with some concern as Rain continued down the hall. Rain wandered alone in the halls of the arena, turning seemingly at random as he walked until he was deep inside the bowels of the structure. Rain turned one corner and suddenly was confronted with a strange steel door. From the cracks on all four sides bright light shone out, and the door seemed to bend and pulse slightly as if alive. Rain looked grimly at this, and then nodded to himself. "So... this is how it must be." Rain reached out and turned the handle on the door... and as it slowly cracked open the light spilled out into the hall. Rain's form was obscured by the blinding light, and he walked inside, allowing the door to close slowly behind him... Ground Zero vs. Hollywood Blondes We returned from the commercial break as Jackhammer came to the ring without his partner, Wolf Fang. Jackhammer climbed into the ring as the crowd watched with interest. Out came Chris Universal as we all heard "Do you buy what the $ellout is selling?". Universal was not accompanied by Megan Dawn, nor Lance Star. Universal stood on the stage with a microphone and said that he wasn't going to let Jackhammer put his filthy hands on him ever again. Universal rambled on about playing pattycake with something and shoving it up someone's ass, and with Jackhammer distracted Lance Star slid into the ring from behind and spun Jackhammer around. He hit Jackhammer three times, all of which seemed to go unnoticed by Jackhammer. Star whipped Jackhammer into the ropes and went for the Shooting Star, but Jackhammer blocked it with a shot to the jaw. Jackhammer then whipped Star to the ropes and as Star returned he took him down with a spinebuster. Jackhammer then leapt to his feet and yelled out "ARRRRGH!"; and Universal charged down to the ring. He kicked Jackhammer in the stomach and went for the 'Cha-Ching!', but Jackhammer blocked it and pushed Universal up and over the top rope onto the floor. Jackhammer then yelled some obscenities down at Universal, and turned around to face Star. At the same time, Scott Slugger & Ice made their way out onto the stage and watched on with interest. Jackhammer charged at Star, but Star took him down with a drop-toe-hold of doom. Universal hurried back into the ring and he and Star double-teamed Jackhammer briefly, before hitting him with a double-team Shooting Star and planting him in the middle of the ring. Star then pinned Jackhammer, before they noticed Slugger & Ice up on the stage. Star looked at Slugger and pointed towards the World Title around his waist, telling him that it would soon be his. Sunday Slaughter went off the air as the two teams faced off... Result: Hollywood Blondes by Pinfall
