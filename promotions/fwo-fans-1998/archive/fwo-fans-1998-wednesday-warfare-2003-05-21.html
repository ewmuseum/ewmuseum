Introduction In 1998 Wednesday Warfare began... the fWo's first weekly show and the landmark for the promotion. While monthly events were showcases of talent, it was Wednesday Warfare that began the fWo's journey to the established promotion that it is today. But, as is the nature of the world, all things good and bad must come to an end eventually. Thus, with a series of backdoor decisions and market conditions the fWo has closed the door on Wednesday Warfare forever. The premire program journeys to Monday night, on a new network, and with a new focus behind it. Gone are the devil may care days of the promotion where anything and everything went... and here are the days of big bets and big growth. With corporate sponsorship behind them and a new agenda: dominate the industry, the Fans Wrestling Organization looks to new challenges, and uncertain times. For tonight is a night to say goodbye...
