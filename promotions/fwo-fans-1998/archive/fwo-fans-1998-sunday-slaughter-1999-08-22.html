Sunday Slaughter, 22 August 1999

22 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Sunday Slaughter opened with Chris Finn speaking to fWo wrestler and former Hardcore champion Rain in the backstage area. Rain seemed reluctant to speak with the fWo announcer, and nodded to Joy as the wrestler walked by with his bright yellow sequin jacket and duffel bag on his way to his locker room. Finn pressured Rain to give his opinion on the upcoming tag team hardcore match, and Rain looked at Chris Finn with silent determination. Rain spoke softly and said, "Finn... I didn't ask for this match. I walk alone in the fWo... and if I did choose a partner, it certainly wouldn't be... that man. But I don't walk out on matches... and I won't walk out on the fWo or the fans tonight. As far as I'm concerned it could be Bear, Jack... or Crucifix across the ring from me tomorrow. I am Rain... and I work alone." The lights in the room flickered, and sounds of a thunderstorm could be heard in the distance. Chris Finn gulped hard, and stepped back from the intense and mysterious wrestler. Rain turned to go, nodding at Joy as he walked by in his denim jacket with smiley face on the back on his way to his locker room. Rain stopped for a moment, seemingly confused, and then dismissed whatever he had been thinking with a shake of his head. Rain stalked off toward the ring... ready for Slaughter to begin... Rain & Crucifix vs. Mike Bear & Cactus Jack Slaughter continued on the hottest night of the summer as the fWo Hardcore Champion Mike Bear, along with the unlikely partner of Cactus Jack came to the ring. As they walk to the ring, we take you back to last week on Wednesday Warfare which shows Stone and Death brutalizing Bear with a singapore cane, before Cactus Jack made the save and helped Bear get the win... before both men were chokeslammed through the commentators tables at ringside by Rain & Crucifix. We return to live action as a storm begins, lightning strikes throughout the arena and out come Rain & Crucifix. Crucifix holds his arms up in the air, before lowering them. A huge blast is heard throughout the arena, as he and Rain walk down to the ring, albit with Rain a few feet back from his partner. As Crucifix and Rain enter the ring on seperate ends of the ring, and Rain looked at Rain with unease as Bear and Jack look on from across the ring. As Crucifix raised his arms to the heavens once more, Bear and Jack waste no time in seeking revenge for what happened on Warfare. Bear charges and spears Crucifix through the middle ropes onto the floor. Jack charges at Rain and spears him into the turnbuckles. With Rain now on the ground, Jack begins unleashing a fury of punches to the face and head area of Rain. On the outside, Crucifix remains flat on his back and is out like a light... while Bear felt some of the punishment as well, and is trying to recover. Jack grabs Rain up by the hair and whips him into the turnbuckles. Jack runs in and attempts to follow it up with a knee, but Rain moves out of the way and Jack crashes into the turnbuckles. Jack turns around and Rain kicks him in the stomach, before Rain throws him out onto the floor. He takes him over near the Spanish commentators table and rakes his eyes. He then props Jack up onto the Spanish table, and climbs up onto the table himself. The storm continues to build as Rain locks Jack in for a powerbomb. Instead of powerbombing him through the table below him, he powerbombs him into the French commentators table straight ahead. Jack goes flying through the table with reckless abandon, as Rain laughs at what he has done. Rain then walks over and grabs Bear off of Crucifix. He rolled him into the ring, looking down grimly at Crucifix, and then helping his partner up with some distain. The duo then climb into the ring and double chokeslam him in the ring, before Rain covers him with one boot. With Bear out of it, it's an easy victory for the unthinkable duo of Rain & Crucifix. They're not finished, however... and they take him to the outside. Using their massive hands, they grip around his neck, and double chokeslam him on the cement floor. Deciding they aren't done, they repeat the process ... only this time through the Spanish commentators table. Finally, Wretch and Falcon appear on the scene to help their fallen friend up. Bear appears to be severely injured. Crucifix raised his arm to Rain, who only looked at him and stalked away as Crucifix looked at his back with a mixture of suprise and anger. Slaughter proceeds to a commercial break, as fWo medical personnel arrive on the scene to cart off Foley and Bear. Result: Rain & Crucifix by Pinfall Final Meltdown Drawings Chris Finn entered the ring for the third and final Meltdown drawing. Finn held the microphone, and said, "Ladies and gentlemen... the following are the final three picks for Meltdown, the big fWo Pay Per View for August! The first team announced is the team of PROPHET and COURAGE! The second team to be announced tonight is the team of D-LO BROWN and US MARSHAL! The third... and FINAL team for Meltdown is the team of BRIANA and a mystery partner! The partner will be revealed on the day of Meltdown, and the fWo executive committee assures us that it is a member of the fWo roster, and that one fWo wrestler will win the greatest prize ever offered in the history of our sport... a full sweep of automatic title shots! Thank you, and goodnight." With that, Chris Finn resumed his place at the announcing table, leaving the fans in the arena to speculate on who the mystery partner will be... Black Quicksilver vs. Plague Black Quicksilver makes his way out as his music blares throughout the arena. BQ is accompanied by Stump, and BQ rolls into the ring immediately and begins shaking Stump in the air as the crowd cheers him. The arena goes dark and the number "4" is shown on the Travis-tron as Plague comes out holding a Stevie Roberts action figure. Plague is wearing a shirt that reads "Stevie Roberts Must Die..." on the front, and "NOW!!!" on the back. Plague appears to be talking to the action figure as he methodically walks down to ringside. Plague climbs into the ring and keeps talking to the action figure, while bending it in several ways. The bell rings, and Plague is too concerned with the Stevie figure to pay attention to Quicksilver. Quicksilver takes Stump, and walks him over the head with it, before locking in a sharpshooter. Plague still has the Stevie figure in his hand, but is unconscious already. The referee is unable to get a response from Plague, so he awards the match to Black Quicksilver. BQ releases Plague, before heading up the ramp with Stump back to the lockerroom. The referee finally wakes up Plague, who begins focusing on the Stevie figure again. "STEV..ie must die.", says Plague, before laughing maniacally as Slaughter goes to a word from our sponsors. Result: Black Quicksilver by Pinfall Gap Road After a few ads for Old Navy (Buy a TECHVEST!) the Sega Dreamcast (It's THINKING!) and LEVIS (Hey mom!) a commercial began to air with a solid white room, and a number of fWo wrestlers sitting around it in casual attire like dress slacks and cotton t-shirts. Singing alone, Primetime said "I'd like to be under the sea..." before the Boston Strangler picked up the tune with, "In an octopus' garden in the shade". Briana sang, "He'd let us in, knows where we've been..." and was followed by Darkness who sang, "In his octopus' garden in the shade". Then the next verse began with the Flying Frenchie who sang, "I'd ask my friends to come and see...", followed by Mike Bear who sang, "An octopus' garden with me". At this point, the commercial came to an end with the logo for the GAP, and Slaughter resumed. Ultra Violet & Joy vs. Death & Kid Cool Death's name was announced and after some brief sounds of a scuffle from backstage, Death walked down the entrance ramp, dragging an unconscious Kid Cool behind him. Death tossed the smaller wrester into the ring, and then handcuffed him to the ring rope in his corner. Pointing to the unconscious man, Death made his strategy for Meltdown clear in an instant. Ultra Violet's music played, and the purple haired warrior walked to the ring with Joy, both of them wielding large, stuffed UV and Joy dolls and tossing them out to the fans in attendance. Death fell upon them at once, hammering down with spinning fists from his mighty ARMS. Joy was unable to come to any kind of offense, and Death picked the smaller but happy wrestler up by the neck and chokeslammed him to the mat. Death covered him with one foot, and got the three count easily. Death then unlatched Kid Cool, and began to batter him as well, waking him up from his unconscious state with a hard beating. Ultra Violet and Joy ran into the ring, warding off the big man and saving Kid Cool from any further attacks. Kid Cool at once fell to his knees and began to make "I'm not worthy" motions at Ultra Violet. Unsure of what this meant, Ultra Violet checked his fly as Slaughter went to another commercial. Result: Death and Kid Cool by Pinfall The Vanishing Chris Finn announced to the crowd that as they may already know by reading the reports on fWowrestling.com, fWo wrestler Apocolyptica had gone missing, and that there was still no sign of her. Chris Finn reminded the crowd that he had been missing for nearly two weeks, and that state officials had been added to the case in an attempt to discover her location. He also added that because of her disappearance, Melissa Smalls would be replacing Apocolyptica at Meltdown as Crucifix's partner. The segment ended with a short video piece showing highlights of Apocolyptica's fWo career, and a number that fans could call if they had any information as to her whereabouts. Wretch & VL vs. Black Panther & Mike Bear Wretch and VL walked to the ring separately, with the Falcon accompanying Wretch to the ring, and Stevie Roberts walking VL to the ring. Roberts signaled to the crowd that after this match the pair would regain their fWo World Tag Team Titles back from the thieves that stole it last week. The Black Panther came to the ring, and looked at Wretch and the Falcon with some suspicion before rolling inside and telling Matt Panzer, fWo ring announcer, something. Panzer announced that Mike Bear was on his way to the hospital due to the events that occurred at the last match, and that the Black Panther would be wrestling the match alone. The Panther started the match against Wretch, and did well against the Cruiserweight champion. Unable to pin Wretch due to VL's interference, the Panther engaged VL in combat, and did well against the woman. But in the end the sheer numbers were too much for the Panther, as the Falcon and Stevie assisted their partners inside the ring. Despite the Panther fighting back against several pinfall attempts, Wretch was finally able to land a diving legdrop as VL held the Panther down. Wretch covered him for the pinfall victory, and he and VL shook hands, and Wretch and the Falcon left the ringside area. High on the entrance ramp, Ice and the US Marshal looked closely at the downed Panther in the center of the ring. On the apron, VL and Stevie got ready for their next match, as the music of Trouble began to play... Result: Wretch and VL by Pinfall Trouble (c) vs. Doomsday As the controversial couple waited inside the ring while the fans booed, we take you back to last week on Sunday Slaughter as the returning Trouble came back with a bang, as they defeated Doomsday in what some may call a fluke. We take you back to live action as the newly crowned fWo World Tag Team Champions, Twitch and Thumper make their way out to a mixed reaction from the crowd. They each hold the belts high in the air, as Doomsday awaits in the ring. Trouble finally climbs into the ring and upon entering, are immediately attacked by Doomsday. Solid teamwork by Doomsday gives them the early advantage. VL hits a lowblow on Thumper, while Stevie takes him out with a 'fameasser'. Thumper finally makes the hot tag and comes in and cleans house. VL tries to help Stevie, but the referee refrains her from entering. With his back to the action going on, Twitch holds Steve while Thumper takes him out with the belt. Twitch then covers Stevie, while Thumper goes out onto the ring apron. The Hollywood Blondes hit the ring and pull Thumper off the ring apron, and begin beating him down. Star climbs into the ring, pulls Twitch off Stevie, and hits the 'shooting star' on him, before laying him down and rolling Stevie on top of him. He's about to leave the ring, when he turns around and decides to put Stevie on top of Twitch in a way that one could interpret that they are having sexual intercourse, before exiting. The referee finally turns around to count the pin, and for the second time in 7 days the tag team titles have changed hands. As VL helps Stevie up, she stares at Universal & Star who simply motion to their waists that the tag team titles will be theirs soon. Slaughter faded to black, as a promo for Metldown ran to finish the program... Result: Doomsday by Pinfall
