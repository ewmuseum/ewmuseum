Introduction Jisei to wa sunawachi mayoi tada shinan Death poems are mere delusion... death is death. -Minamoto no Yoshiie / Abe Sadato (1057) Storm Front Kodiak Vic Creed wasn't a happy man. Earlier in the day, he'd gone to have his knee scoped. Of course the doctor wanted to run some more tests and keep Creed around, but the Main Man wouldn't have it. He was the fWo World Champion. If he had to, he'd cut his damn leg off to keep it from slowing him down. Vic couldn't afford to stop. With the entire wrestling world watching him, he couldn't afford to appear weak. The crutches didn't help much with his Juggernaut image, but for now there wasn't much he could do about it. With a sandwich jammed in his pocket, KVC navigated down the hallway. Oh he was definitely going to have a little talk with his assistant about shirking his duties, which included sandwich-fetching. So distracted with thoughts of food and crutches, Vic didn't see the walking land mass approaching him. "Where's your skirt, girlie man?" The words hit Vic like a slap in the face. Few people dared to talk to him like that. "I gave it back to your Mom," he told Nick Brandish. "She tried turning tricks without it and kept scaring the johns away." There was no one else around. Like two destructive forces of Nature, the air itself seemed to crackle with tension. Vic suddenly became very aware of his vulnerable state, staring at a man who was even bigger and more sadistic than he was. He crammed the feeling away. Screw that, he thought to himself. I'm the Juggernaut. "So do we have a problem?" Nick stared at Creed, stealing a glance at his knee. "Problem? You mean, am I still pissed that you got me fired?" Vic turned his body slightly so that Brandish could get a better look, as if to say Stare all you want. You still can't take me. "You tried to screw me over first. You crossed me, I returned the favor." "Heh." Nick took a step towards Creed and slapped him on the shoulder. "I would've done the same thing. I'm back, so I guess it doesn't really matter anymore." "Going to grab a quick bite in my room. Then I've got to find Fox." He took a long look into Nick's face. "You're on the clock? Who's the poor stiff?"
