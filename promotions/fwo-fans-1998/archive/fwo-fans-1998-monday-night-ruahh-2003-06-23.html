Introduction Life is, as they say, a cycle. In 1998 and 1999 the fWo presented two pay-per-view events entitled "Battle Bowl". The concept, which was a tag main event, was short-lived, and with the creation of the Wrestler of the Year tournament the Battle Bowl event became a faded memory. But this Friday, at Unleashed, the fWo will pay homage to the event and mark off the final milestone before the furious path to Meltdown begins in earnest. It is a time for gearing up in the calm before the storm and a time for reflection as old friends come face to face after a long seperation. But life is never static, and as Silver Fox is about to learn, some things change, no matter how much he may not wish them to... Fox had liked Joseph; he was one of the people in the business who had a conscience. They talked about politics, about nearly anything and everything. The two had ideals... and in this business ideas were hard to come by. When you're in a world like professional wrestling... trying to make a better place than all of the filth-ridden companies around them. Everywhere there was politics, backstabbing... small little men trying to create empires. Joseph and he had looked past that, laughed about it, and sworn not to let it happen to them. In the several months since they had seen each other Silver Fox's life had changed dramatically. Kidnapped by Moore, thrust into a stronger leadership position... now with the involvement of ABC and the Disney Corporation they were moving fast, trying to meet and take the ground between themselves and the WWE. They were gaining... and Fox liked the race even if he despised the politics around him. But he felt like he was doing the best he could with what he had to work with... and his problems.
