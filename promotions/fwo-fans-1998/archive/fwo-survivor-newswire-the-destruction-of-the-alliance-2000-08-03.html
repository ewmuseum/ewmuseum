fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE THE DESTRUCTION OF THE ALLIANCE August 3, 2000 August third will go down as the day the alliance crumbled. Voting in the fWo Survivor contest took a radical turn in round six, as the seven remaining voters severed any ties they might have had and voted in a bizarre scattering. Even more strange, voting for round six took on new strange dimensions as wrestlers changed their votes often... sometimes as much as six times. One wrestler before the contest was over had voted for five different people, leading fWo Survivor officials to believe that former alliances were being thrown out the window and that it is now every man for himself. "I tremble as I say this... because once people see the results of the voting nearly everyone is going to be pissed off. Looking at the voting, judging from what people are saying... promises were obviously made, and several people were lied to. If the Survivor members were not paranoid before, they will be now." Voting fragmented drastically in round six, with another first... Survivor had a tie. Arch Angel and the Don both tied with two votes apiece. Many speculated that Nick Allstar would have broken the tie one way or another, but Allstar missed the deadline and thus has an absent vote against himself. Other votes were cast against Ruben Ross and Reno Davis. The thin amount of collective votes pointed to the breaking up of the alliance, with several onlookers speculating that the rift between tied characters suggests the area the fragmentation took place. As it stands Arch Angel and the Don will wrestle in a single elimination match to start Round Seven, with the loser moving out of the contest and the winner moving on to the next round. However, the winner also must realize that his days are numbered now that he has been marked for elimination by others in the group. The immunity challenge in Round Seven will be more important than at any other point in the contest. Votes were cast as follows: The Don: 2 votes Arch Angel: 2 votes Reno Davis: 1 vote Ruben Ross: 1 vote Nick Allstar: 1 absent vote More news as it develops.
