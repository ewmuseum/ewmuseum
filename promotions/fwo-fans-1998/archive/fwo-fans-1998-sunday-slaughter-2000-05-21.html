fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 21 May 2000

21 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Year Three. Workers backstage shuffled about as Slaughter prepared to go live. Several wrestlers were gathered in an area, as a limosuine pulled up. Who was in the limosuine was unknown at this juncture, but curiously as a taxi-cab pulled up behind the limosuine as well. The driver of the limosuine got out and walked to the back of the long white limosuine, before opening the door. Outstepped Chris Finn, who was once again the owner of the fWo as a result of what occured last night at Redemption. Finn stood and looked back at the taxi-cab, before someone stepped out of that vehicle as well: The Boston Strangler. The new fWo World Champion was already dressed in his wrestling clothes, and he walked over to Chris Finn. The two men greeted each other with a handshake, before Finn spoke. "Thank you." The Strangler responded with a half-smile. "No need to thank us, Chris." Finn smiled, before the two men walked into the area of the gathered wrestlers. At once, every person began to clap slowly, before building into an ovation of applause. Finn & Strangler walked side by side into the arena, while we faded into the opening for Sunday Night Slaughter. The Future Is Now. The twenty-five thousand plus fans inside the Skydome in Toronto, Canada anxiously stood on their feet as the first Slaughter in four weeks took to the air. Sirens screeched throughout the arena, while fireworks were released from the stage. Suddenly, "Juke Joint Jezebel" began to play, and the flag of France appeared on the SlaughterScreen. However, a big circle with a slash through it appeared, and "Juke Joint Jezebel" stopped. Instead, "Last Resort" by Papa Roach began to play, and out walked Primetime, Courage & presumably the Frenchie; but upon closer inspection one could guess that it was Lance Star dressed in his Future Frenchie gear. The fans sat speechless as the trio of arrogant young men entered the ring; Primetime with his fWo Internet Title draped over his shoulder, Courage with his fWo International Title wrapped around his waist, and Star with his newly won fWo Cruiserweight Title over his shoulder. Star handed his belt to Courage, who held it while Star took off his beret, the mask, and his Future Frenchie t-shirt, before tossing it all to the ground. Primetime then received a microphone and began to speak. "Pierre... Pierre... Pierre. You know, I've never felt this good in my entire life. Why? The French Foundation is dead. Pierre, you held me back for two years. You knew two years ago when you came to Mitch and I that our careers were a disappointment up to that point; but you knew that given the right roles our careers would flourish. And that they did. So you put together this so-called French Foundation... and even have the audacity to get Mitch and I to become citizens of France! My ancestors aren't French, they're Polish for crying out loud! Mitch and I knew it was bad... but we decided, what the hell. He had been the Dude, a surfer for Christ's sake, and I had been the leader of a group that couldn't get over if our lives depended on it in the Primetime Players. So, surely being French couldn't be worse than that, right? Wrong. Mitch & I sit in that putrid group... and we became a tag team. And we won the belts right off the bat. Surely, the suits started to take notice... and we then drifted into singles competition for awhile. And while we didn't exactly win any singles titles, we were damn successful. Until last Summer, and that horrible angle where we had to pretend we were truck drivers. TRUCK DRIVERS! I'm not a truck driver, I'm Primetime, and I'm the reason each and every single one of the fans is here tonight. Mitch knew it was ridiculous, but I stuck around... went onto win Meltdown, and you were jealous... you were jealous. I end up winning the Cruiserweight Title, then the Women's Title and decide to give it to you out of the kindness of my heart. So, then I go on and defeat Scott Slugger later in the year for the fWo World Title. Did you congratulate me? No! You expected a freaking title shot, and so I gave you one. And who came out on top? ME. And then Invasion! rolls around... I go from facing The Boston Strangler at Twilight, to wrestling Matt Douglas in the opening match in my supposed "hometown"; while you're in the main event against Mr. No-Charisma himself, The Black Panther. The next night, you, I and Courage meet in a three-way dance. Who wins? ME. And then finally you do something good for me... you have the fWo World Title, and you recognize that last December after I lost the title in a match I wasn't even pinned in, that I never got a rematch. So, you give me one at Trapped... and who comes out on top? ME! Do you get where I'm going with this, Pierre? I'm far superior than you, and you saw that... and you decided to attempt to hold me back and you damn nearly did. If it weren't for Courage & Lance here making me aware of this, I'd still be your sidekick. Not anymore. I'm not French, Courage isn't French, Courage is rock and roll, baby! Rock and friggin' roll! And... where are you at now, Pierre? Back home in Toulouse... crying because you lost your mask. Boo-friggin' hoo! When you turn on your television set... this is what you're going to see. The fWo Internet Champion, the fWo International Champion, and the fWo Cruiserweight Champion. Three men who are the youth of the fWo, who are the foundation of the fWo, who are the FUTURE of the fWo. We are the Future, Pierre... and the Future is right now. Right Chris?" Primetime passed the microphone to Courage, who continued where Primetime left off. "You're exactly right, Brian. Pierre, when Brian went to you with the idea of me joining the Foundation you were somewhat relucatant to bring me in. I do not understand that. Why, Pierre, why? I am rock and roll, baby! Courage equals asses in the seats! Courage is bar none wrestling's most charismatic performer, baby! And you... you were reluctant? Well, eventually you wised up and saw that Courage is talent, baby. And you knew that by adding Courage to the ranks of the *spit* French Foundation that you would be holding down another talent just like you did Brian. You knew with Courage on your side that you would be able to keep him out of the spotlight just enough to allow you to have another 5 minute reign as fWo World Champion. Well, you got your two reigns... and you know who you rightfully lost the title to? Primetime baby, Primetime. And sure it was a helluva match because Brian here carried your ass for thirty plus minutes. And then we move onto the month of April.. you and Brian are booked a month in advance, but where is Courage? Courage is nowhere to be seen on the card, and that's when Lance came into play. You see, with the help of Lance, we both were able to secure us a spot on the CyberSlam card. And no, we did not win... but we had to face three other teams in the match. Who were you booked up against? Some purple-haired moron that Courage totally and utterly rocked a few weeks before CyberSlam! And you lost to him? Courage laughs at that baby, because that just proves that you are not rock and roll! You have no talent baby, while we... the Future Foundation... we are all about talent. Last night we walked out of Redemption with three titles, and where were you? Back home in ToLOSE because you're a loser! So, Pierre... I think I just about sum it up when I say this... screw France, screw the French Foundation, and screw you, because The French Foundation is dead... and The Future Foundation is here... because the future is now, baby!" Courage passed the microphone to Lance, who let out a trademark beam before chuckling... "Uh... yeah, I forgot what I was going to say. Dammit... oh yeah! We are the future of professional wrestling! Hoo-ha!" Primetime & Courage chuckle as Lance beams again, before their music hits and they walk to the back, the building still in shock about all the unnice things Primetime & Courage said about their one-time friend, Pierre Delacroix. Ric Chronos & Fallen (c) vs. The Guano Apes Warfare returns as the sounds of "War?" by System of a Down starts up, and the challengers - Sphere and Jackhammer - the Guano Apes step onto the entrance ramp. As fireworks shoot off into the upper parts of the arena, the Apes continue their trek to the ring. Suddenly the arena is filled with the sounds of a clock ticking, and Anthrax's "Time" rocks the Toronto Skydome. Ric Chronos and Fallen, the challengers, step out onto the entrance ramp and revel in the jeers from the crowd, as they walk to the ring with the fWo World Tag Team Titles wrapped around their waists. Chronos & Fallen take the titles off and hand them to the referee who holds them high above the ring for everyone to see, before calling for the bell. Fallen starts things off for his team, while the tandem in the opposite corner of the ring kicks things off with Fallen. Jackhammer meets with the deranged individual in the middle of the ring, and they lock up. Jackhammer, who has the obvious power advantage in this matchup sends Fallen into the ropes. Fallen ducks a short arm clothesline, and bounces back off of the ropes with a flying shoulderblock. Fallen follows it up with an armdrag, turned into an armbar. Jackhammer looks for a way to shimmy out of the move, and he does. Jackhammer uses the go behind to take Fallen over in a release german suplex, a move that the big man doesn't use often, but it worked effectively there. Jackhammer climbs on for a cover, but Chronos steps in, and nails a boot to the head, to break up the count. Chronos flees from the ring, but Jackhammer looks enraged inside. Jackhammer orders Fallen to go over and tag Chronos, because he wants a piece of him. Fallen seemingly obliges, and the "Man of the Hour", Ric Chronos steps into the ring. The two big men go face to face in the ring, before Chronos nails a punch to the jaw of Jackhammer, that sends the big man reeling. Chronos follows it up with a whip to the ropes. Jackhammer comes off, and Chronos plants him with a powerslam. Chronos covers, but Jackhammer kicks out violently, sending Chronos halfway across the ring. Chronos backs off to his corner, and tags in Fallen, as Jackhammer decides to bring Sphere into the matchup. Fallen immediately runs at Sphere, taking him down with a nicely executed spear. Fallen pummels Sphere, before bringing him up, and taking him down with a snap suplex. Fallen climbs the turnbuckles, and sets himself up. Fallen comes off with a guillotine legdrop, but Sphere rolls out of the way. Sphere runs, and takes Fallen down with a clothesline, and then he runs over to where Chronos is situated on the ring apron, and takes him down with a punch. Chronos goes flying, and hits the guardrail. However, Fallen has recooperated from the clothesline, and he gets behind Sphere. Fallen gets him with a rollup, but Sphere gets out at two. Both men reach their feet at the same time, but a double clothesline happens, and both men are down. As they struggle to get to their feet, Chronos and Jackhammer await the tags. Both men reach their corners at the same time, and tag in their respective partners. Chronos and Jackhammer hit the ring, and begin trading fists. Chronos takes the advantage, and whips Jackhammer into the ropes. He nails Jackhammer with a boot to the gut, before setting him up for a DDT. However, Jackhammer nails a few punches to the ribs of Chronos to break the situation. With it broken up, Jackhammer nails a boot to the gut, followed by a powerbomb. He covers, but somehow Chronos is able to kickout. Jackhammer is frustrated, so he tags out to Sphere. Jackhammer brings Chronos to his feet and holds him in place, while standing to his side. Sphere climbs to the top rope and leaps off with a missle-dropkick, but Chronos quickly moves Jackhammer in the way and instead Sphere mistakenly hits his partner instead. Sphere looks at his befallen partner in shock, before he's spun around by Chronos who tags him down with the Chronosphere. Chronos covers him and the referee counts to three, and once again Chronos & Fallen walk away with the fWo World Tag Team Titles intact, as they walked away from Redemption last night victorious after a match with Scott Collins & Stone. Chronos & Fallen celebrate, while Jackhammer and Sphere exit the ring, and look at each other as if some tension is brewing between the team. Meanwhile, Slaughter heads backstage... Result: Chronos & Fallen by pinfall Premature Celebration The Future Foundation talked amongst themselves as they turned a corner, presumably headed in the direction of their lockerroom. Their smiles were turned to frowns as they met face to face with Chris Finn. "You guys don't look too happy to see me." He received no verbal response, instead they remarked with one collective sigh, and a roll of their eyes. Finn shrugged it off. "Courage, you're not getting away with all that cheating you did last night. Tonight, you're going to defend that fWo International Title, and it's only fair that you defend it against the men you were victorious over last night in a four-way dance. That's right, the men you faced last night will be given the chance to have a fair shot at your title, and if anyone interferes on your behalf you'll be stripped immediately." Courage shook his head in disgust, before complaining. Finn ignored him, and looked at Star. "And you... I thought you were a responsible young man with a good head on your shoulders, but obviously your actions last night must lead me to believe otherwise. Tonight, you'll defend that title against Crusader & Matt Douglas in a three-way dance. Good luck... you'll need it." Star shook his head in disgust and began to complain, but Finn ignored him. Primetime kinda chuckled as he looked at his two stablemates, before Finn directed his attention to him. "And YOU... you're not alone. Tonight... you and Scott Hart in a match for the fWo Internet Title. Sure, it's not fair.. but it wasn't fair what you did last night. Good luck to all three of you gentlemen." Finn walked away, whilst the Future Foundation bickered amongst themselves about having to wrestle tonight. Slaughter proceeded to a commercial break. At the crossroads... Slaughter returned from the commercial break. The cameras took to the ring, before focusing on the stage as the arena dimmed, and "Burning" began to play. Out onto the stage walked Scott Collins, looking more focused and determined than ever. Collins entered the ring with pride on his face, before he asked for a microphone. The music slowly faded out, and Collins looked at the crowd, before speaking. "You all know I've been here since day one. I'm one of the cornerstones of this organization. But something's been bothering me lately... and... well, ever since CyberSlam I've been thinking that my match against Slugger was my last chance at the big time. What happened last night at Redemption certainly didn't help any. Maybe I've lost a step, maybe I haven't, the point is... I've decided I'm going to have to prove myself, and I want to beat somebody. Anybody. But it'd have to be the right person, and I'm thinking there's one person right now that I should prove myself against, and that's Mike Bear. Mike, we've exchanged words for a long time now but it's been awhile since we've actually been inside a wrestling ring against one another. You've disrespected me for a long time, and I'm sure I haven't said the most kindest things against you either. So, I'm challenging you to a match this Wednesday at Warfare. I want a good, clean match. No interference from my friends, and none from yours. I don't care if you win that four-way tonight, either. If you happen to be the fWo International Champion, great. I really don't care. I just want a chance to prove to myself and to everyone else that I still belong around here, and I plan on being around here for a long time." Slaughter then went to a preview of Critical Condition, the next big fWo pay-per-view on June 24th, live from the Palace of Auburn Hills in Detroit, Michigan. "The Future" Lance Star (c) vs. Matt Douglas vs. Crusader Slaughter continues with "Higher" beginning to play and the fans cheering the arrival of Matt Douglas, who makes his way to the ring focused on regaining the Cruiserweight Title. Douglas slingshots himself into the ring and awaits his opponents. "Switchback" starts to play and the fans continue to cheer as Crusader makes his entrance from the back and rolls into the ring. The cheers turn to boos as "Guerrilla Radio" starts up and the fWo Cruiserweight Champion, Lance Star appears on the entrance ramp. Star makes his way down to the ring and unstraps the belt from his waist before rolling in and measuring his two opponents. The bell rings and Star, Douglas, and Crusader circle each other, before Star chooses to lock up with Douglas. Star takes Douglas over with an armdrag and as Douglas gets up, nails him with a dropkick. Behind Star, Crusader leaps onto the second rope then leaps off at Star, taking him down with a version of a bulldog. Crusader covers, but Star kicks out at two. As Crusader gets back to his feet, he is met by a huge chop from Matt Douglas, who follows it up by whipping him into the ropes. Douglas leapfrogs over Crusader, and Crusader runs right into a powerslam from Lance Star. Star elects to not go for the cover and instead go for Douglas. Star charges at Douglas with a clothesline, but Douglas ducks and Star hits the ropes. As he comes off, Douglas leaps up, going for a hurricanrana, but Star catches him and drops down into a sitting powerbomb. The count reaches two before Crusader breaks it with a seated dropkick from the side to the head of Star. Crusader himself quickly covers Douglas, but Star pulls him off by the leg at the count of two. Crusader immediately gets to his feet and eats a clothesline from Star. As he gets up, Star bounces off the ropes and takes him down with an inverted swinging neckbreaker. Star makes the cover, but as the count reaches two, Matt Douglas breaks it up with a seated dropkick of his own that knocks Star outside the ring. Douglas brings Crusader to his feet and takes him over with a snap suplex. Outside the ring, Lance Star is getting to his feet, and Douglas takes the opportunity to knock him back down with a beautiful plancha suicida that nails Star dead-on. Douglas then rolls back into the ring, only to be met by Crusader, who hooks him up and takes him down with a brainbuster. Crusader signals that he wants to end it and heads to the top rope, before leaping off with the Gravity 101. He hooks the leg and the ref counts one... two... but Lance Star pulls Crusader out of the ring. Before Crusader can realize what's going on, Star throws him face-first into the ring steps. Star rolls back into the ring and goes to cover Douglas, but Douglas hooks his head and rolls him into a small package. Crusader on the outside sees this and rushes the ring to break the pinfall, but doesn't quite make it in time. Star cannot kick out in time and Douglas scores the three-count, becoming a three-time Cruiserweight Champion. Star gets up and stares in disbelief, and Crusader helps Matt Douglas to his feet and raises his arm high in the air as the winner. The two shake hands, but their mutual respect is interrupted as Star's Foundationmates Courage and Primetime hit the ring and proceed to attack the two. Star joins in on the beating, but from the back Havok charges the ring, weilding a steel chair. Havok clocks Primetime on the back of the head with it, and the three Foundationers bail from the ring, Courage pulling the nearly unconscious Primetime out with him. Havok inside the ring threatens the Foundation with the chair again before helping Douglas and Crusader to their feet as Slaughter continues... Result: Matt Douglas by pinfall Back In Black As Slaughter comes back from a commercial break, over the loudspeakers is heard the opening drums and guitar of AC/DC's "Back In Black", which could only signal the arrival of one man. As the man formerly known as Perfect, Scott Slugger steps out onto the entrance ramp, the fans suddenly erupt with cheers for the former FTfWo member. Slugger takes it all in for a moment on top of the entrance ramp, then a burst of fireworks explodes in front of him. Slugger walks unconcernedly through it, his face etched into his trademarked, copyrighted, and patented arrogant smirk. As he reaches the ring, he hops onto the ring apron and slingshots himself over the top rope and into the ring. The fans continue to cheer as Slugger nonchalantly catches the microphone tossed to him by a ringside attendant. At this, the music begins to die down and Slugger takes a moment to contemplate his words. Finally, he speaks. "Welcome... to the house... the WGA built!" The fans cheer, and a smile crosses Slugger's face. "Ah. It feels so good to use my catchphrases again. But seriously, folks... the WGA is sure that all of his fans out there are wondering exactly why he turned on that bald bastard Deadvis? Well, firstly and most annoyingly, he made me change my name! What the hell was the deal with that? I may be perfect, but hell, the name 'Scott Slugger' is legendary. You say 'Scott Slugger', and you know you're talking about none other than the World's Greatest Athlete. Besides, I missed calling myself 'The WGA' all the time." Slugger smirks at this, and the fans cheer. "Secondly, I joined his pissant little group to get away from the people who were holding me down - namely guys like Chris Universal and Ice. So what happens? I lose to Candyman Collins of all people, on THREE straight pay-per-views! That pretty much speaks for itself. Third, Body Count. He actually had the little Deadies to eliminate me! And then he thinks I'm just gonna forget about it? Fat chance, Dead man. Two more reasons to go. Fourth, at the beginning, I was told we were going to be co-leaders. So who makes all the decisions? That walking talking piece of monkey poo with the physical ability of a crippled slug and half its mental capacity! And fifth... and most importantly... I earned a title shot against Collins at CyberSlam. Did he give it to me? Nope. It's pretty easy to figure out why. Quite frankly... Dead fears Slugger. And that's fine, because lots of people fear Slugger. But what he forgot was a simple mathematical equation. Name change plus being held down plus Body Count plus no leadership plus no title shot equals baseball bat upside the damn head!" The fans cheer wildly at this, and Slugger beams. "And hell... FTfWo kinda sucked anyway. Now, the WGA has one more thing to address. Boston Strangler..." The fans cheer even louder at the mention of their hero. "Congratulations, Paul. You got rid of Dead, with my help. But since Dead never gave me that title shot, and the WGA knows you're a man of honor... I want my shot. You and me, Critical Condition. You're a hell of a wrestler, Strangler... but if you ask the WGA, I'm coming out with the one thing I'm looking for right now... my - unprecented, mind you - FIFTH World Title reign. Strangler... I'll be seeing you." Slugger paused, and the fans looked at each other expectantly. "What? Oh! Oh, that. Yeah. Give me a second here..." Slugger pauses, then raises the microphone to his face once more. "Until next time, it has been your pleasure!" The fans cheer loudly again as "Back In Black" plays once more and Slugger makes his triumphant way to the backstage area, awaiting the answer of the Boston Strangler... Courage (c) vs. Wretch "When Worlds Collide" begins to play and the fans cheer as Havok makes his way to the ring for this International Title match. Havok rolls into the ring and stands in a corner as "Paint It Black" starts playing. The crowd reacts with loud boos as Wretch arrogantly makes his way to the ring. He gets on the second rope and strikes a pose, before the fans pop. Wretch thinks this is for him and continues showing off his repertoire of poses, but in reality the fans have popped because "Dragula" has begun to play and Mike Bear is making his way to the ring. Bear quickly steps into the ring and eyes Wretch warily, before turning his attention to the man who took his title at Redemption, Courage. Courage enters holding the International Title high as "Surfacing" plays, before walking down to the ring. Seeing the three men in the ring who are not too fond of him due to his actions at Redemption, Courage stops at ringside and instead pulls up a chair to watch the wrestlers in the ring fight. Instead, Bear, Havok, and Wretch storm Courage en masse and unceremoniously toss him back into the ring. Havok, Wretch, and Bear simultaneously begin stomping on the body of the downed Courage, who can't defend himself from the actions of three other competitors. This ass-kicking continues, but Wretch sees an opportunity to score a quick sneaky pinfall and rolls up Havok from behind. The ref's count reaches two before Mike Bear breaks it up. Bear immediately heads back to Courage to work him over, and Wretch and Havok continue to duke it out. Bear whips Courage hard into a corner, and charges in with a back elbow that strikes Courage flush on the face. Courage stumbles out, but Bear shoves him right back in and strikes with flesh-stinging chops. Across the ring, Havok whips Wretch into the ropes before catching him with a leaping leg lariat. Havok quickly leaps onto the second turnbuckle then flies off with a vader bomb, landing squarely on the chest of Wretch. Havok makes the cover, but Wretch kicks out at two. Courage meanwhile has gained the advantage over Bear with a questionable low kick and hooks Bear up, before spiking him with an implant DDT. Courage makes the cover, but Havok comes over and drops a leg on Courage to break up the count. Havok brings Courage up to his feet and hits a dropkick, knocking him across the ring. Bear meanwhile makes his way to his feet, as does Wretch. The two tag partners turned enemies contemplate each other for a moment, then Bear charges at Wretch with a lariat. Wretch ducks, and turns to clothesline Bear up and over the top rope and to the outside, before following him out. Inside the ring, Havok whips Courage into the ropes, but puts his head down. Courage comes off and takes him down with a rocker dropper. Courage quickly runs into the ropes and attempts the Courage Required, but Havok lifts his knees and Courage crashes down onto them. Havok makes his way to his feet and looks to finish off Courage, hooking his head and hitting The Headache, also known as an inverted brainbuster. Havok signals that he's headed up top to end it, and prepares to hit the Millenium-Sault. Havok flies off, but Courage rolls out of the way and Havok smashes face-first onto the mat. Outside the ring, Mike Bear has procured the ring steps, but Wretch counters by dropkicking them right into his face. Courage remains down in the ring, and Wretch spots an opportunity, climbing to the top rope from the outside. He leaps off, going for a senton bomb, but Courage moves once again. Courage, having his pick of unconscious opponents to hit with the Courage Required, chooses Havok and runs into the ropes. He leaps onto the second strand and moonsaults off, but never sees Mike Bear, who has recovered and rolled inside. Bear catches Courage, shrugs him onto his shoulders, and takes him down with the Whiplash. Bear makes the cover, and the three-count is academic as Bear becomes a two-time International Champion. Wretch rolls to the outside and head sto the back. On the way back, he is met by Scott Hart, but he simply walks past Hart, who follows eagerly after him to the back. They are passed on the way back by fWo Internet Champion Primetime and fWo Cruiserweight Champion Lance Star, who are rushing the ring. Primetime rolls in first and heads for Havok, not bothering to even unstrap his title belt. Star attempts to strike Bear with the Cruiserweight Title, but Bear ducks the blow and superkicks Star in the face, causing the title to strike him full-on. Star quickly rolls outside. Meanwhile, Havok and Primetime's brawl has seen both men take a spill to the outside, and the brawl continues unabated before a bevy of security, officials, and refs rush the ring to break it up, leaving Bear and Havok on one side and Primetime and Star on the other, Bear holding his International Title high and the Foundation yelling threats in their general direction... Result: Mike Bear by pinfall Return of The Big Blonde Machine. Slaughter returned from a commercial break to the sight of Mitch Wilson, coming off a loss in one of the greatest matches in fWo history, walking angrily to the ring to the tune of "Calling All Stations". Wilson quickly rolled into the ring, looking to be all business. He grabbed a microphone away from ring attendant and got down to business. "Last night... I got screwed. I was thisclose to winning the greatest match in fWo history after I carried Brian to a level his worthless ass couldn't even dream about, when what happens?" Wilson scowled and continues. "Lance Star happens. I had the Internet Title - MY Internet Title - won, and that goofy bastard comes out of nowhere and screws me. What a load of crap! But I'm here to tell you right now that after tonight, that will never happen again. But I'll get to that in a second. Earlier tonight, I heard Scott Slugger out here babbling about the way he betrayed FTfWo. Sluggsy, you don't just turn your back on us and get away with it. Not a chance, hotshot. Sure, you broke FTfWo - in fact, with Dead who knows where and you out of the group, I'm the leader... and my apologies to Hiroshima, but FTfWo is dead right now. No pun intended. But while you FTfWo... you didn't break me. And right here, right now, I have the solution to both my problems. The Lance Star problem, AND the Scott Slugger problem. In fact, Sluggsy, you're probably pretty familiar with him. Just like me, you shoved him out of the spotlight in the past... but together, we're gonna put you out of the spotlight permanently sooner rather than later. Ladies and gentlemen..." The arena lights suddenly darkened to pitch black. "I give you..." Fog began to roll in on the entrance ramp, and the sound of thunder was heard before Motley Crue's "Bastard" started playing. "ICE!" The fans reacted with shock and confusion as the man last seen fighting the FTfWo, none other than the mammoth Big Blonde Machine Ice appeared on the entrance ramp for the first time in nearly three months. Ice stalked to the ring under the blue lights of the arena, and stepped easily over the top rope and took the microphone from Wilson as the lights resumed their normal color. "Three months ago... I was injured by one man." The crowd began to chant "Slugger!" at this, and Ice's face contorted to one of anger. "Yeah, you know his name. The cowardly son of a bitch who did it to me was Scott Slugger - a man Mitch here just happens to have a problem with too. You see, he did something about it. Last night, I was watching Redemption, completely healed and just waiting for the right time to come back, when the phone rings. I think you can figure out who it was. He was looking for some backup... and a chance to hurt Slugger. Hey, how can I refuse an offer like that?" Ice paused to glare icily at the crowd, then continued. "As of this moment, the entire fWo is on notice. Bad Attitude is here, and if you piss us off... consider yourself finished. And in case you don't believe us, just ask Scott Slugger after tonight..." Mitch Wilson took the microphone and finished. "And if that doesn't help, just ask Lance Star after Wednesday. Sluggsy... watch your back." "Bastard" began to play again, and the duo collectively known as Bad Attitude stalked to the back to a chorus of boos from the crowd as Slaughter rolled on... Primetime (c) vs. Scott Hart Slaughter picked up with Scott Hart coming to the ring, and Hart looked a bit tentative in his first match in awhile. Hart rolled into the ring via underneath the bottom rope, and awaited his opponent of the night, the fWo Internet Champion, Primetime. "Blind" by Korn began to play, and sure enough out walked the brash, confident young man. Primetime wasted no time, entering the ring quickly. Hart prepared to lock up with him, but Primetime took him offguard with a knee to the stomach, before immediately going to work on the back area. Primetime hit a gutwrench suplex, before bouncing off the ropes and driving a knee into the throat of Hart. Primetime continued on the offensive, hitting a northern lights suplex without a bridge, before hitting a swinging neckbreaker. Primetime went for the quick cover, but Hart kicked out at two. Primetime whipped Hart into a corner, before charging in with a splash, but Hart moved out of the way. PT hit the corners hard, and as he turned around he was taken down with a belly-to-belly suplex. Primetime kicked out at two, and seemingly out of nowhere Hart decided to go for a Texas Clover Leaf. He locked it in, and Primetime looked ready to give up, before going to the ropes and breaking the hold. Hart brought Primetime into the center of the ring and hit a standing vertical suplex, and then went for a Sharpshooter. Primetime countered out of it however and instead Hart went flying into the referee. Primetime recovered, and down came "Tha Mutha Fuckin' Abusah Of Yo Momma", or TMFAYOM for short. He wore a mask and a shirt that had the acronym TMFAYOM, and a pair of Tommy Hilfiger pants that appeared to be on backwards. TMFAYOM climbed into the ring and as Primetime turned around, he charged for a clothesline. However, PT countered into a drop-toe-hold that smacked him right into Hart as Hart was getting up from the corner. TMFAYOM went toppling backwards, before PT booted him onto the floor. Hart staggered out from the corner and PT kicked him in the stomach, before hitting a reverse neckbreaker. Primetime then gave the referee a hard nudge, and the referee slowly counted the pin. Primetime escaped victorious, but back in the ring Hart seemed frustrated by the TMFAYOM interfering in the match. Primetime headed backstage with his Internet Title intact, while Hart and TMFAYOM discussed the situation back in the ring. Slaughter took to another commercial break... Result: Primetime by pinfall. Demands. Slaughter returned as the fans in the Skydome awaited impatiently. A catchy tune began to play before the once-again owner of the fWo - Chris Finn - walked out onto the stage. The fans applauded greatly, giving him a nice welcome. Finn strolled into the ring and looked at the fans with pride, before nodding and receiving a microphone. He wasted no time and began to speak. "Thank you, ladies and gentlemen. I thank you for continuing to support the fWo, as we enter our third year as an organization. Let me assure you that the ownership of the fWo will remain in our control from this point on. Nevertheless, I'm here to announce the main event of next month's pay-per-view, titled Critical Condition, which will emanate live from the Palace of Auburn Hills in Detroit, Michigan. In the main event... for the fWo World Title... the number one contender - Scott Slugger - will take on the fWo World Champion The Boston Strangler. Without further adieu, I present to you the man who will face Slugger at the pay-per-view, and the man who just twenty-four hours ago put the control of the fWo back in trustworthy hands, The Boston Strangler!" The lights dimmed, before "O' Fortuna" began to play, and out walked the Champion. He made his way down into the ring to a thunderous ovation of cheers from the Skydome crowd, and Finn clapped for him as he stood in the ring. Strangler entered the ring as the lights returned to normal, before Finn continued. "Strangler... I think I speak on behalf of all of us when I say thank you for what you did for this organization last night." Finn held the microphone for the Strangler, who spoke. "Like I told you earlier, Finn... there's no need to thank us. We were just doing our... jobs. And it was as Slugger would say... our pleasure." The crowd cheered largely for this, before Finn interjected. "Well... let's get down to business, shall we? Last night at the end of Redemption... we saw Eron The Relentless come out and steal the fWo World Title. I'm not sure exactly why he did it but in order for us to have the fWo World Title match at Critical Condition we need the fWo World Title. So... Eron.. I know you're in the back, and I know you've got the Championship Belt, so I'm not asking you to bring it out here, I'm telling you to bring it out here and return it to it's rightful owner, The Boston Strangler." The crowd murmured for a bit, while Finn & Strangler stood in the ring and awaited Eron. "Physical" by Nine Inch Nails began to play, and the fans immediately started booing. Eron walked out onto the ramp with a smile that reeked of deviousness and evil. Eron held in his posession the fWo World Title, and he walked down to the ring slowly. He asked for a microphone however, and received one, before stopping just near the ring. "You want this title back, Finn? Hehehe... well, I want something in return. You said there's going to be a main event at Critical Condition. Well, the main event will be meaningless without this belt, correct? So... you give me something, and I give you this belt." Finn interrupted... "What... what... what do you want, Eron?" Eron smiled, before looking at Strangler in the ring. "I want to be part of that main event at Critical Condition. You make that match a three-way dance, and I'll give you back your title. If not... I'll just walk right out of here, and you can have yourself a paper champion." Finn seemed disturbed by this, but he discussed it with the Strangler. After several seconds of discussion, Finn shook his head affirmatively. "Alright... you get your deal. You get what you want, now return the title." Eron slid the title under the bottom rope into the ring, before grinning. "You just made yourself a deal with the devil, Strangler. Come June 24th... you and baseball boy are going to take a breath... OF FRESH DEATH!" Eron taunted the Strangler as he walked up the ramp backwards, not taking his eyes off the champion. But from behind came the baseball boy in question and self proclaimed World's Greatest Athlete, Scott Slugger. Slugger spun Eron around, before kicking him in the stomach, and doing a variation of the Curveball on the ramp. With Eron out of it, Slugger stood over him... grinning as the crowd cheered wildly. However, their celebration was cut short, as out came Ice & Mitch Wilson from behind, and they immediately attacked the number one contender to the World Title. Finn looked at the Strangler, who gave the World Title to Finn. Finn took it over to the ringside table as The Strangler headed up the ramp. He fended off Ice, while Slugger and Wilson went at it. Several referees came out and split it up, before they asked the four participants if they wanted a match. All four men shook their heads, and the referees split up the four men, making sure they were escorted down to the ring away from the other two. Once that task was completed... the other officials headed backstage, taking Eron to the back with them. One official remained, and signalled for the bell, calling for the beginning of this impromptu main event... The Boston Strangler & Scott Slugger vs. Ice & Mitch Wilson The bell rang several times, before Scott Slugger and Mitch Wilson entered the ring. They stared at each other, each of them feeling contempt for one another, despite the fact that just twenty four hours ago all was well between both of them. Both men appeared to toss off the proverbial gloves so to speak, before they charged at each other. Neither man grained ground on the other, and they fought back and forth with lefts and rights. Finally Wilson took Slugger down with a double-leg takedown, but as soon as he did Slugger pulled him down as well before mounting Wilson and unloading with a barrage of lefts and rights. Ice came in, but the referee quickly backed him into his corner. Slugger brought Wilson up to his feet and whipped him into the ropes, and as Wilson walked out Slugger took him down with a backdrop, followed by a dropkick, and then a jumping fistdrop. Slugger covered Wilson, but Wilson kicked out at two. Slugger brought Wilson over to his corner and tagged out to Strangler. Two of the three men who would compete in the main event at Critical Condition whipped Wilson into the ropes, and as he returned they took him down to the mat with a double elbow. Slugger went out to his corner, and Strangler went on the offensive. He brought Wilson up and hit a gutwrench powerbomb, before going for a cover. Ice broke up the pin, but Strangler continued. He whipped Wilson into the ropes and then went for a sleeperhold, but Wilson countered into a quick belly-to-back suplex. Wilson then rolled over and tagged out to Ice, and Ice came in looking to take the World Champion out. He brought Strangler up to his feet and then whipped him into the corner, before charging in shoulder fist. All two hundred and eighty one pounds of Ice slammed into the prone body of the champion, before Ice brought him out from the corner with relative ease, tossing him to the mat with a double-handed throat toss. He then brought him up again and whipped him into the ropes, and as Strangler returned he was driven into the mat with a spinebuster. Ice went for a cover, but in came Slugger, as confident as ever with a kick to the back of Ice. Ice turned around with anger, but told Slugger he would get his in due time. Ice brought Strangler up and draped him over his shoulder, before hitting a running powerslam. He then tagged out to Wilson, who took over. Wilson came in off the top rope with a beautiful frog splash. He followed it with another pinfall attempt, but Slugger broke up the pin once more. Wilson glared at the four-time World Champion, before bringing Strangler up and taking him down with a snapmare, and then backing up and dropkicking Strangler right in the face. Wilson then walked over into the corner and tagged out to the Big Blonde Machine, who stayed out on the ring apron. Wilson then brought Strangler up for a suplex, and Ice climbed up to the top rope. However, Wilson was just positioning the Strangler for the old finishing move of Ice, the Icebreaker. Wilson dangled Strangler body in front of Ice, and Ice grabbed him, and then hit it. Ice crushed Strangler into the canvas. The impact of the move could not be described in words. The sound of their two bodies hitting the mat was inhuman. Ice reached a vertical base and bragged loudly about the move, smirking at the fallen champion, before pointing at the crowd and telling them he just finished him off. He went for a cover, but somehow, someway... nearly impossibly, the Strangler kicked out at two and ?. Frustrated, Ice stood to his feet, before he looked over at Wilson. Wilson instructed him to finish the madman from Boston off, and Ice obliged. Ice slowly brought The Strangler up to his feet, before looking at the crowd and smiling with a mischievious grin. Ice then went for the chokeslam, aka the Icepick... however, Strangler quickly grabbed the arm, and went for the Boston Massacre. He got it locked in, and Ice was about to topple in pain, but in came Wilson with a double axehandle. Both men went to the mat, and a few moments later Ice recovered. He seethed with anger, and gritted his teeth while he looked down at the Strangler. He tagged out to Wilson, and instructed him to finish him off. Wilson obliged. He brought Strangler up to his feet and hooked him for 'The Headliner', but Strangler countered into a waistlock somehow, before hitting a release German suplex. Wilson crashed onto the mat, and the referee began a ten count. At six, Strangler rolled into his corner and tagged out to Slugger, the man who helped him win the fWo World Title just twenty four hours ago. Slugger came in a house of fire, looking to take anyone's head off. Wilson tagged out to Ice, who reluctantly entered the ring. Ice charged at Slugger but Slugger took him down with a flying forearm. Both men rushed back to their feet and Slugger whipped Ice into the ropes. Ice returned and Slugger jumped and took him down with a high knee. As Ice slowly got off his feet Slugger took him down with a rocker dropper. Slugger went for a cover, but Ice kicked out. Wilson and Strangler did battle in a corner, while Slugger and Ice continued in the middle of the ring. Slugger went for the 'Curveball', but Ice attempted to counter into a Northern Lights Suplex. Slugger flipped out of it however, and when Ice turned around he was caught with a superkick. This propelled him into some turnbuckles. Meanwhile, Wilson was able to hit his 'Headliner' on the Strangler. The referee tried to get him out of the ring, but Wilson wouldn't listen. Ice staggered out of the corner and Slugger finally hit the 'Curveball'. The referee saw Slugger go for the pin, and moved into position to count. However, Wilson jumped off with a knee into the back. From behind came the Strangler, who tried to go for the Massacre. Slugger shrugged off the blow to the back, and then went out to the top rope. He prepared to hit his original finisher, the flying lariat off the top rope. Ice struggled up to his feet. As Strangler struggled to put on the Massacre, Wilson saw his chance. He allowed Strangler to grasp him, before driving him backwards into the corner. This shook the ropes, of which Slugger stood on in the other corner. Slugger fell and was crotched on the top rope, and Ice saw this and took advantage of it. He walked over and grabbed Slugger by one hand, and chokeslammed him off the top rope onto the mat. This wasn't an ordinary Icepick. This was an Icepick that sent the World's Greatest Athlete into oblivion. Ice covered him for the pin, and three seconds later he and Wilson had escaped with a victory. They celebrated on the outside, as the referee joined them to raise their arms in victory. Slugger held his throat on the mat, while Strangler looked around, wondering what went wrong, as Slaughter went off the air... Result: Ice & Mitch Wilson by pinfall
