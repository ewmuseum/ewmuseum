Wednesday Warfare, 14 June 2000

14 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Finally, The Gunmen Have Came Back To East... Rutherford? Backstage, a limousine pulled up to the arena. The vehicle was jet black, so black that it hurt the eyes and caused people who watched it arrive squirm and declare, "That limo is black. So black it hurts the eyes." It came to a slow halt, and the engines were killed with a soft hum. For a moment, time stood still as onlookers stared at the limo. It was impossible to tell who was inside with the thickly tinted glass, and after a few seconds passed and nobody exited people began to speculate who was inside. Just as the current popular guess had reached pop-singer and motion picture diva Meatloaf, the driver's door opened and a large man stepped out. Dressed in a full driver's uniform complete with hat and lapel pin, the driver looked down his nose at the onlookers and snorted with disgust. Moving slowly, he walked around to the rear door. He smoothed his jacket and reached for the handle. The door swung open as the group of people looking on held their breath in anticipation. For a limousine this extravagant, this staggeringly wealthy, this amazingly luxurious it had to be someone of exceeding importance. The dominos delivery boy stepped out and hauled his warm bag of pizzas out with a mighty grunt. He smiled at the driver, who sneered back at him in return. "Thanks, pal! I didn't know what we were going to do when the truck broke down... good thing you guys accept the Discover card!" The driver grunted a response as the pizza boy skipped into the arena to deliver the food. The crowd began to disperse, annoyed that the limousine produce such shabby results. As the crowd evaporated, four men stood alone, still looking at the limousine with mild interest. "Do you think there are still any pizzas in there?" Sid shrugged at the Total Eclipse. "Maybe... but why take the chance when there are pizzas for sure walking into the arena as we speak?" Rudo nodded in agreement. "Yeah, come on... let's get inside. It's starting to rain, and I'm wearing suede. Are you coming, Machina?" Machina looked up at the arena with a slightly nervous expression under his mask. "I suppose so..." Under his breath, Machina grumbled, "I knew I should have gone into ballet... mother was always right about such things..." Inside the arena the screams of the crowd could be heard, and now booming over them the explosions of fireworks. Wednesday Warfare had begun. Dropping like flies. As Warfare began inside of the arena, we were taken backstage where we would be subjected to the ongoing antics of Chris Finn & James Armstrong, and the never-ending saga of their obsession with ratings. A desk with a laptop computer on it, and a black leather couch nearby with a snack tray full of such amentities as peanuts, watermelon and bottled water are basically all of the fixtures that occupy the room. Sitting in front of the desk with his chin slouched upon one hand is Finn, and sitting on the couch scratching his head is Armstrong. Both look very displeased, and neither gentleman look to have slept much in the last couple of months. This is not unusual, because when you're in the fWo, the fWo becomes your life. Lack of sleep is not uncommon for anyone in the fWo, from the owner, to the wrestlers, to the guys who help write this crap in the back. Finn looked at his computer in amusement, his screen barraged with instant messages from various people complaining about one thing or another. Finn looks at Armstrong and sighs, Armstrong nods, but at the same time wondering why Finn subjects himself to such crap. "I know what you're thinking. You're thinking... why do you subject yourself to this crap? Well... I don't know. I guess I like to get feedback about the fWo. Good lord, I never thought I'd get this much feedback." Feedback? The camera zooms in on the small laptop monitor to find several instant messages in the open, with several others hidden. "Who would win in a battle, Conan The Barbarian or Wolverine?" "Why doesn't your webmaster help other people that need it?" and "Hey, can you hook me up with some kind of job at the fWo?" Yeah, that was his feedback at the moment alright. Finn sighed again, before looking at Armstrong. "I suppose you saw the ratings for Slaughter." Armstrong nodded. "Ratings dropped AGAIN!" Finn shrugged, before pausing to answer the IM about Conan & Wolverine. What the hell? He didn't even care. He didn't respond. Looking over at Armstrong, he put some thought into his answer, before continuing. "Well, there was that NBA Finals game..." The man addicted to blackjack and hookers for some unknown reason interrupted him. "No! It has to be that "big surprise"... from that other wrestling organization. Say, what was the big surprise anyways?" A disenchanted look came over the expression of Finn. "Oh, they turned their most popular face into a heel." Armstrong sat on the edge of his seat with a puzzled look on his face. "Hey... and that's supposed to change wrestling forever?" Finn frowned. "Well, I guess." "Then we should make Strangler go heel!" Finn sighed, balking at the suggestion of Armstrong. "Yeah, there's an idea." A roll of the eyes from Finn was more than enough for Armstrong to get the hint, and Warfare finally proceeded to the arena for the beginning of what was billed as one of the biggest shows in it's three year history. The Black Panther vs. Ice Warfare returns from commercials to the sounds of Motley Crue's "Bastard". The song echoes throughout the home of the Stanley Cup champion New Jersey Devils, the Continental Airlines Arena. The big blonde machine himself, Ice steps out onto the Warfare ramp, and continues to the ring to face the newly returned Black Panther in a match for the number one contendership to the fWo International Title. Suddenly, "Bastard" stops, and "My Hero" by the Foo Fighters starts up, and the Black Panther steps out onto the entrance ramp. Taking no time to play to the fans, Panther runs to the ring, and immediately begins brawling with Ice. Panther takes control, and whips Ice into the ropes. Ice ducks a clothesline, and then takes BP down with a shoulderblock. BP pops up, and walks right into a chokeslam position. However, before Ice can get him up, BP nails a quick boot below the belt. Ice curls over, and BP lifts the big man up. He takes him over with a big vertical suplex, holding him there for a good amount of time. Cover by the Panther, and Ice is able to get a shoulder up. Panther lifts Ice up and nails a few quick chops, before perching Ice on the turnbuckle. He goes for a superplex, but Ice nails a few quick punches to the ribs. Panther falls to the mat, and Ice comes off with a huge top rope elbowdrop that nails Panther right in the sternum area. With Panther shaken from the big blow, Ice covers, but the man known as Daniel Longshadow kicks out at two. Ice picks the Panther up by the mask, and just throws him to the outside. Ice follows, and picks BP up, and throws him right into the pole that holds the turnbuckles in place. BP's face makes a horrible smacking sound, and Ice simply laughs a devious laugh, probably wishing that the man he did that to was in reality Scott Slugger. Ice rolls Panther back into the ring, and then lifts him up into the position for a jackhammer. However, BP slips out of it, and grabs Ice from behind with a waistlock. He takes Ice over in a german suplex, complete with bridge. The ref begins counting, but Ice squirms out at two. Both men pop up, and Ice looks for a clothesline. Panther ducks, and stays behind. Kick to the gut, and Panther lifts Ice up in a powerbomb position, but somehow the huge Ice reverses it into a facebuster. Ice covers, and Panther kicks out at two once again. Annoyed at Panther's tenacity, Ice decides to bring him up one more time. He grabs Panther by the throat, and takes him down with the chokeslam, the Attitude Adjuster. Ice goes to cover, but suddenly "Back in Black" starts playing. Ice immediately looks up and begins looking around, when suddenly Scott Slugger's voice is heard "Hey, you big blonde goof. Up here, up here. TravisTron, you dumb ass. Now, I understand that you've been talking about how you want to end my career, Ice. How you want to supposedly screw me at Critical Condition, and cost me the world title. Well, guess what, big man? I am the World's Greatest Athlete, and don't think that for one minute that at Critical Condition, I won't run through Eron, I won't run through the Strangler, and especially that I won't run through *you* to win my title. Oh...and by the way, look behind you." Ice turns around, and walks right into a double underhook suplex by the Panther. Panther covers, and the referee is able to record the three count. As "My Hero" plays, the Panther celebrates, but the music is cut off once again by Slugger's voice. "Oh, and one more thing..Panther, that was supposed to happen. Until next time, it has been *your* pleasure." Slugger's face flips off of the screen, and Warfare goes to a commercial break. Result: Panther by pinfall Let's Do Lunch, Part One Warfare heads backstage, somewhere in the rarely seen on camera bowels of the Continental Airlines Arena. In a cafeteria sits The Future Foundation, which are comprised of the fWo Internet Champion Primetime, the fWo International Champion Courage, and the "future of professional wrestling", Lance Star. The trio sits on a bench, not eating anything in particular, but drinking beverages of their choice. Lance Star is drinking Sprite, because he has been led to believe that image is nothing, thirst is everything. As the three men talk amongst themselves, presumably discussing their monumental three-way dance that is set to go down later in the show, two men enter the cafeteria from the right: Scot Hartt & Tha Mutha' Fuckin' Abusah Of Yo' Momma, who from here on out will be referred to as The Abusah, or, TMFAOYM. Hartt & Abusah greet the Foundation, who seem not to be pleased by their presence. Courage brushes them off hard and looks the other way, ignoring them one hundred percent. Primetime laughs at Hartt, telling him he has a stain on his shirt. Hartt' face turns red in embarassment, before they turn to Star, who tells them to run along. Frustrated, Hartt & Abusah exit the cafeteria, feeling used... and betrayed. Xavier (c) vs. Erik Kelly vs. Neo vs. Robert Jensen The sounds of Godsmack welcome us back to Warfare, as "Whatever" plays throughout the Continental Airlines Arena. The mixed reaction for one of the most controversial individuals of fWo begins as Neo appears on the ramp. Looking around with disgust at the New Jersey fans, he walks to ringside. As he walks by a slightly inebriated fan who reaches out for him, Neo pays his respects back to the fan by grabbing his crotch then slapping the fan. Security holds the fan back as Neo continues up the ramp, a smirk upon his face. As he makes his way up the steps, "Bleed" by Soulfly begins, and the crowd's mixed reaction continues for Robert Jensen. Jensen carries a chair with him to the ring...the same chair he used at Slaughter. Seeing this, Neo decides to bail to the other side of the ring, and go under the apron, searching for weaponry. While this occurs, the opening rifts of Limp Bizkit's "Break Stuff" begins, followed by the familiar "POWEEEEEERBOOOOOMMMMB!" warcry of "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly. The crowd gives a loud ovation to the near 7 foot Boston, Massachusetts native as he charges down the ramp with a ball bat in hand. Neo has now found himself a broomstick, and is getting back into the ring. Kelly meets up with Jensen in front of the ring, Jensen with the chair over his head and ready to strike. Erik thrusts the bat into Jensen's stomach, and Jensen drops the chair, as Kelly takes one more thrust into Jensen's chest, knocking the sadistic Jensen over. Kelly climbs onto the apron, where Neo now charges with a vicious swing of the broomstick. Erik ducks this swing for his head, grabs the offbalance Neo by his neck, and hoists him over the top rope and crashing down on top of Robert Jensen. Erik climbs into the ring, puts the bat off into a corner, and gets out of the ring on the other side. The crowd starts to roar as they know what he's going to get: WOOD! As he starts to set a table up outside of the ring, the lights go out in the arena, and a blue flash on the TravisTron indicates the arrival of Xavier. "Bleed" by Godhead plays as Xavier makes his entrance, the fWo Hardcore Title over his shoulder. He walks with purpose to ringside, ignoring the boos from the crowd, his eyes focused on his three opponents. However, he doesn't see that Robert Jensen had moved from being a pillow for Neo, and awaited Xavier, where he uses the broomstick Neo had and smashes Xavier's knees, sending the 7 foot champion down. With Xavier hunched over, Jensen stands and splits the stick in two over Xavier's back. He cheers prematurely as he turns around only to get a swift kick in the nuts by Neo. Neo spits in his face, grabs the nearby chair, and proceeds to thrust it in both Xavier and Jensen's back. On the other side of the ring, Kelly now has the table setup (no glass this time, though), looks over to see what it taking place, gets back into the ring and gets Neo's attention. Egging him on, Erik gets Neo to get back in the ring. Neo, being a little too anxious, tries the same move on Kelly that succeeded with Jensen. Didn't work, his foot caught. Neo tries an enziguri kick, but Erik telegraphs it, and pushes Neo's foot, sending the rest of the wrestler down awkwardly. As Neo gets to his knees, Erik grabs him from behind, and performs a German Suplex. Outside the ring, Xavier is back on his feet, as he sees Jensen is prone from the low blow still. Jensen is picked up by the champion, placed on his shoulders and viciously bounced around in a torture rack. Jensen screams in pain, obviously ungrateful for this handling. Thrown back down to the concrete, Xavier decides to punish Jensen the Hardcore way. He goes under the ring and proceeds to find a stop sign. As he lays out Jensen, Erik Kelly is performing his trademark bearhug spinebuster slam on Neo. As both Kelly and Xavier look at each other, a shouting and finger pointing match begins between the two monsters. Unfortunately both should've paid attention to their fallen victims, as Jensen returns a low blow to someone else, this time Xavier. Meanwhile, Neo drops Kelly into a small package, where a 2 count occurs before Kelly breaks out. Kelly, enraged with this, turns around to put Neo in a Pumphandle Slam. Jensen goes for the nearby chair Neo left as prepares to use it on Xavier, unfortunately waiting too long as Xavier ducks the swing, and viciously grabs Jensen by the throat, picking him up and crashing him down in a move he simply calls "Blue Rising". He drops down, and although the ref is bumbling to get outside to do the pin count, he succeeds in getting the 3 count. All Erik Kelly can do is turn to see the ref count 2 and get to the ring ropes to look down and witness the 3 count. Enraged, he goes down to the opposite side, moves the table back into the ring, sets it up, then goes out again and under the apron to grab....A CINDER BLOCK! He places the block on the table. As Xavier walks backwards up the ramp, Kelly picks Neo up and places him between his legs. The crowd begins to roar, as half the Continental Airlines Arena shouts "POWERBOMB!" for Kelly. All Kelly does is point at Xavier, before lifting Neo up and crashing him down through the table, landing him on the cinder block. Kelly's entrance music plays, and the camera shows Xavier very agitated as he leaves ringside. Kelly chest pumps his acknowledgement to the crowd, and yells at a nearby camera "SAY HELLO TO BLOCK, X-MAN!", as he makes his way up the ramp, with medics and officials going to check on the conditions of Neo and Jensen. A good time to go to commercial... Result: Xavier by pinfall The Most Electrifying Ramp In Sports Entertainment Wednesday Warfare started to go to commercial, but suddenly a commotion could be heard from just behind the backstage entrance. Suddenly the curtains were brushed aside, and out onto the entrance ramp came four men who had not been seen in ages in the fWo. The Lone Gunmen came out onto the ramp, looked around at the capacity crowd, and smiled. Machina withdrew a microphone from his pocket, pointed out into the crowd, and began to shout. "Ladies and gentlemen... boys and girls... and yes, children of all ages... the fWo is proud to bring to you the most charismatic entrance ramp of all time! I'm speaking of this ramp... right here... right under my very feet! Brought to you by the hard working steel manufactures of Richmond, Wisconsin, this solid steel construction has been molded for only the finest in walking, strutting, and yes even occasionally brawling up to the top and then getting pitched over the edge action! The unrecognized world champion of entrance ramps, our ramp has single-handedly taken on and defeated that entrance ramp up north... and the entrance ramp from Atlanta. I think you know what ramps I'm talking about... but despite the shining glory of the ramp before us being omnipresent for all to see, that is not why I have come here today. No, for my duty is to inform each and every single one of you out here, and each and every one of you back there... that starting today, the Lone Gunmen ARE BACK!" Machina raised his arms to the crowd and a cheer broke out from the fans in attendance. This took the Lone Gunmen back somewhat, and they looked at each other with surprise and honest amazement. For this is not what they had expected at all... mixed boredom and exhaustion yes... but this? Actual crowd response? Rudo looked around to see if perhaps one of the girls in the arena had ripped her top off again. One had, but it was only accounting for half of the cheers. As hard as it seemed to believe, the Lone Gunmen... were over. Then Rudo spotted the second girl who had ripped off her top nearer to ringside. She had much larger breasts, and seemed to be the focus of the cheers from the crowd. Rudo turned to Machina and shrugged. Machina looked at the girl with distain. "She's going to catch a cold you know." Sid and Rudo nodded. The Eclipse's stomach growled. Warfare went to commercial. Scott Slugger vs. Mitch Wilson Warfare rolls on as "Calling All Stations" begins to play and Mitch Wilson, surprisingly not accompanied by Ice, makes his way to the ring to loud boos. Wilson rolls in under the bottom rope and stares down the entrance ramp, looking to be all business for this big battle. Genesis (version 3.0) is replaced by AC/DC, as "Back In Black" starts up and the crowd pops. At the top of the entrance ramp stands Scott Slugger, Trademark Smirk upon his face as fireworks explode in front of him. Slugger walks right through them and begins to charge the ring, barreling in and finding himself on the receiving end of a series of stomps from Wilson before he can get up. He shrugs these off and gets up, before unloading a left jab at Wilson's nose. Wilson absorbs the blow and fires back with a roundhouse right, and the two warriors exchange a series of rights and lefts before Slugger finally gains the advantage, sending Wilson flying back-first into the corner before whipping him across. Wilson's back slams against the opposite turnbuckles, and Slugger charges across the ring before leaping up and crashing into Wilson with a leaping body attack. Wilson stumbles out, and Slugger bounces off the ropes before taking him down with a one-arm bulldog. Slugger quickly rolls Wilson over and hooks a leg, but Mr. Main Event escapes at two. Slugger brings Wilson to his feet and attempts a whip into the ropes, but Wilson reverses it into a short lariat. Slugger however ducks the arm of Wilson and kicks him in the gut as he turns around, before planting him with a rocker dropper. Slugger covers, but Wilson again escapes at two. Slugger brings Mitch up again and hooks him, setting him up for a triple powerbomb combo, but Wilson smartly drops to his knees and strikes upwards with a testicular forearm. Slugger doubles over and Wilson hooks his head and legs, before lifting up Slugger and planting his cranium into the mat with a devastating fishermans buster. Wilson takes a moment to shake off the earlier attacks of Slugger, then covers. Slugger kicks out at two, but Wilson isn't fazed. He brings the WGA to his feet and shoves him violently into the corner, before unloading with a monstrous chop that echoes through the arena. Wilson follows this up with another and then rips open the uber-cool leather jacket and Edgar Martinez jersey Slugger has donned in order to get better impact on the chops. Wilson winds up, and unloads on Slugger with another chop that turns his chest beet red. Wilson then grabs the arm of Slugger and whips him hard across the ring into the opposite turnbuckle. Wilson backs up a few steps before charging in, spinning, and delivering a rolling elbowsmash that jacks the jaw of Slugger. Wilson backs away, and Slugger falls face-first out of the corner and Wilson spits on him on the way down. He quickly rolls Slugger over and covers, but Slugger manages to escape at two. Wilson is unhappy with the count and lets the referee know about it, which allows Slugger to sneak up from behind and roll up Wilson with a schoolboy. Wilson kicks out at two, and as both men get up Wilson strikes Slugger with a leaping leg lariat, taking him down. Wilson brings Slugger up and hooks his arms, before spinning around and taking him down with a spectacular reverse double underhook DDT, or 'impaler' as it is known in other federations. Wilson runs a thumb across his throat, before bringing Slugger up and whipping him into the ropes. As Slugger comes off, Wilson slaps on the feared Fujiwara armbar, which has on multiple occasions nearly broken the arm of Primetime. Wilson has neglected to work the arm earlier in a rare miscalculation, allowing Slugger to gather up all his reserves to keep from tapping. Wilson cranks back on the arm, listening for the snap crackle pop of a broken bone, but Slugger still refuses to give. He desperately searches for a way to overturn the hold, but finds none. His last resort is to attempt to reach the ropes. Slugger struggles for the ropes, and Wilson struggles equally to hold him in place. With a last expenditure of energy, Slugger bursts forward and wraps a hand around the ropes, and Wilson is forced to break the hold by the referee. He does so at four, and brings Slugger up again after dragging him to the center of the ring. Wilson attempts to reapply the hold with no ropes nearby, but Slugger anticipates this move and slips free of the grasp of Wilson, going behind him and taking him out with an elevated reverse DDT, the move formerly known as the Perfect Game. Both men are down, and the referee lays down the ten-count. At seven, Wilson begins to rise to his feet, followed by Slugger. By nine both men are up, and Wilson launches a wild, desperation haymaker at Slugger, who, feeling a second wind coming on, ducks it easily. As Wilson spins around from the force of the blow, Slugger catches him and takes him over with a ring-rocking exploder suplex. Feeling it, Slugger yells at Wilson to get up, and Wilson obliges. Slugger whips Wilson into the ropes, but Wilson reverses the whip and sends Slugger into the ropes. As he begins to do so, Eron The Relentless emerges from the crowd and leaps onto the guardrail, carrying a chair. Slugger flies into the ropes, and Eron flies off the guardrail, slamming the chair onto the back and head of Slugger. Slugger stumbles out of the ropes and Wilson, ever the opportunist, hooks his head and plants him with the Headliner. Wilson quickly rolls over and hooks a leg, and the three-count is tolled. Wilson has his arm raised by the ref as "Bad Attitude" plays, and he quickly exits the ring area as Scott Slugger begins to come to his senses. The WGA is justifiably angry, and after making his way to his feet lifts the hapless referee in the air by the collar, 'politely' asking him who did it. The ref doesn't know, but Slugger has a hunch, and he stalks to the back, shouting threats to the ruiner, Eron... Result: Wilson by pinfall Let's Do Lunch, Part Two We proceed from the melee still going down in the ring, to the cafeteria once again. Primetime, Courage & Star continue to speak, and as their conversation shifts from their match to other matters, like the whereabouts of the Flying Frenchie and how scared he is of the Future Foundation, a man enters the picture. The man? Wretch. Looking concerned, Wretch approachs the Foundation and asks Primetime & Courage if he may have a moment with Lance. Primetime & Courage obliges, before telling Lance they'll see him in the ring later for their three-way dance. Lance nods, before Wretch sits down near Lance. Wretch has a drink in his hands, and upon closer inspection it is documented that the beverage is a Lemon Lime Shirley Temple with what appears to be a little green plastic monkey. Wretch takes a drink, before preparing to speak. "Lance... I don't know about you, but this whole... thing... whatever between us has got to stop." Lance asks Wretch to elaborate, and Wretch does. "Well, we keep having Hartt, and now Abusah, to turn on each other. You know, I've known Hartt for awhile, and he... he's demented, to an extent. Nice guy, but you never know what to expect from him." Lance looks at Wretch firmly. "Exactly, Wretch. Nobody expects it. Isn't it cool?" Wretch shakes his head negatively, before proceeding. "Lance... I think we've got to stop this whole charade of games." Lance seems taken aback, and dismayed. "What? I like charades!" Wretch frowns. "That's not what I meant. See... what I mean is, let Hartt & Abusah do their own thing, I'll do my own thing, and you'll do your own thing with the Foundation. It's time we put a stop to this... do you hate me?" Lance shrugs. "Not really. You're weird... but you seem like a cool guy." Wretch beams, before simply saying "Ditto." A pause in the conversation takes place, as Wretch takes another drink, and Lance does the same. "So, are we cool now?" Wretch asks. Lance nods, before the two shake hands. The two sit in silence for several moments, not knowing what to say. Lance is about to leave, and he takes a drink, however not of his Sprite, but of the Lemon Lime Shirley Temple with a green plastic monkey. A disgusted look comes over the face of Lance, before he spits the beverage out. The liquid hits the floor, before Lance wipes the remains off his lips. "Yuck!" Wretch is very offended, and stands up. "How could you?! You just took a drink of my Lemon Lime Shirley Temple with a little green plastic monkey! And then... you spit it out! But did you have the decency to spit it back in the glass? No! You spit it on the floor, and wasted a perfectly good drink!" Lance looks at Wretch puzzeled, before raising his voice. "Wretch, the stuff tastes like crap! We all know Sprite tastes better... you can have your little fancy shmancy drink with the little green plastic monkey, we all know image is nothing... thirst is everything, pal!" Wretch raises his hand, pointing a finger in the general direction of Lance. "Well-ell... if I had some big fancy corporate catch phrase to spew at you, I'd do so... but apparently Lemon Lime Shirley Temples with little green plastic monkeys are too high-brow for the American population... so all I can say is... YOUR MOM!" Lance pauses, before shrugging. "Yeah... well... your mom... times two! Take that! Hoo-ha!" Undaunted, Wretch looks around the room. "You've got me there." Lance looks very pleased, and turns to leave, when a big smirk forms on the face of the native of Long Island, New York. "Your Mom... In... FINITE!!!" Suddenly, Lance crumbles in fear, his lips trembling. His ability to respond taken from him in an instant. Lance simply shakes his fist in Wretch' general direction, before walking away. Wretch smiles brightly. As Lance reaches the end of a hall and prepares to turn the corner, he turns around and expresses himself once last time. "You're an ugly MO' FO, and your MOMMA is TOO!" Lance exits the cafeteria, while Wretch frowns, stomping his feet in disgust. Moments pass, before the camera focuses in on Wretch one last time. "My mom, eh? No, Lance. Your mom... your mom!" Ric Chronos & Fallen vs. The Guano Apes As Wednesday Warfare returned from a commercial break, fans noticed that Lone Gunmen members Sid and the Total Eclipse had arrived at the commentator's desk and were donning headsets for the next match. Sid mentioned that now that the Lone Gunmen were back in active status in the fWo, he and the Eclipse would be setting their sights on a prize that the Eclipse knew all too well... the fWo Tag Team Championship. As he spoke the challengers for the title match tonight came to the ring. The familiar sound of the Guano Apes entrance music pounded through the arena as Jackhammer and Sphere walked to the ring slapping hands with the fans and taking a moment to shake Sid and the Eclipse's hands before they slid into the ring. Cheers came louder as the reigning fWo Tag Team champions made their way to the ring. The charismatic Ric Chronos and introverted Fallen walked to the ring with belts in hand. In less than two weeks the two would meet up with long-term rivals in Damnation, the team formed from the members of the Underground. As the bell rang, announcers speculated on how the outcome of this match could drastically effect the Pay Per View landscape at Critical Condition. Chronos started things off in the ring with a quick armbar into a headlock on the smaller Sphere. Chronos worked in the hold, trying to cut the oxygen off of the member of the Guano Apes, but Sphere was fresh in the match and had little trouble maneuvering both of them toward his corner where a well placed punch from Jackhammer on the outside send Chronos stumbling backward. Sphere made the quick tag to bring in Jackhammer, and then walked back to attend to Chronos. As Sphere held the champion, Jackhammer hit an unusually agile move in a standing heel kick to the face of Chronos. Jackhammer attempted a cover, but Chronos easily kicked out despite being dazed from the flurry of offense from the challengers. Chronos crawled for a tag, but Jackhammer quickly slid him backward and dropped an elbow into the back and methodically began a series of knee and leg drops to the weakened champion. Standing Chronos up Jackhammer attempted a suplex, but Chronos quickly locked the leg and blocked the move. Now it was Ric's turn to bring the big man up, and Jackhammer was suplexed into the ropes, bounced backward, and right into a forward DDT from the champion. Chronos raised a fist to the crowd who responded in kind, and dropped a fist into the head of Jackhammer. But too close to the challenger's corner, Jackhammer made a blind tag as Ric Chronos chopped away at the big man, and Sphere was able to enter the ring and hit a drop kick from behind. As Jackhammer and Sphere double teamed the champion Fallen attempted to enter the ring, but the referee forced him back into his corner, allowing for the challengers to continue to double team the champion. Chronos was dazed now and looked out on his feet, and Sphere signaled that he was going to pick Chronos up and hit his "Never Born", a Falcon Arrow finisher. Sphere lifted Chronos into the air and hit the move, although the weight of Chronos made it look sloppy and poorly timed. A cover was made, but Chronos, aware of where he was in the ring placed the foot on the rope at two to end the count. Frustrated Sphere pounded the mat and argued with the referee as Chronos struggled to get to his feet. Sphere came back to attempt the Never Born again, but Chronos using a last ditch effort grabbed Sphere around the midsection and hit a hard belly to belly suplex that send both men to the mat. Both men lay still as the referee lay on a count. Fallen stomped to get Chronos to stand, and Jackhammer called loudly for his partner to rise. Sphere showed signs of life first, and crawled toward his corner. Chronos was also moving now, but much slower.
