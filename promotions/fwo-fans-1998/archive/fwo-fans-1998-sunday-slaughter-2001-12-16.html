Introduction The lights dim, as Tones On Tail's "Christian Says" flows from the arena speakers, heralding the arrival of Ric Chronos and 3vil. Smartly attired in custom fit tuxedos, Ric in gold, Frenchie in black, and Primetime is a dark crimson, the group marches down to ringside, grabbing a few microphones along the way, and enter the ring as the music dies down. Fans inside the arena notice a large steel structure hanging ominously over the arena, and assume that is why 3vil is coming to the ring. The fans boo and jeer their presence, as 3vil was obviously not a popular group. "I'm not certain why you're all booing." Chronos said, as he took a chance to glare at the fans for a moment, before continuing. "This is a happy night for us - for tonight, we're celebrating our individual wins that will occur at Twilight. Seven days. Seven days until we - 3vil - finally get what we've wanted from day one. At Twilight, Frenchie and Primetime here get the opportunity to put Universal in his place for the last time. The prize? Total control of the fWo. The cost? Five minutes of Frenchie and Primetime's day. You know, this is the deal of a lifetime - it's not often that someone offers you something for next to nothing. And knowing the amount of work that we'll have to do once we have full control, it was the smart idea to start the celebration tonight, while we still had time on our hands." Behind him, Frenchie and Primetime nod their heads in agreement, congratulating each other on what they believe to be a predetermined victory. Ric pulls a small, thick book out of a vest pocket before he continued. "As such, we've decided that a few things are going to change around here, and we wanted to prepare everyone, so that the adjustment to total control by 3vil will be a smooth and seamless one. First off, we�re going to do a little house cleaning, and deadbeats like Ruben Ross, Douglas, Eddy Fate, The Boston Strangler, and others will be removed from the roster outright." "That's right! Bye bye, losers!" Primetime snorts, while he and his tag team partner The Frenchie share a sinister laugh. Ric opens the book, and points to a passage midway through the volume. "Now, something the three of us have noticed, is that there are a lot of oversights in the official books of the fWo's history. Some things have been ignored and forgotten, while others have been outright fictionalized. For example, it states here that I lost the fWo World Heavyweight Title at Countdown 2 last year. Well, since I couldn't be there to defend it, it was really hard for me to lose it, wouldn't you say? So that would go away." With that, Ric tears the page from the book, crumples it, and sends it hurtling into the fans at ringside. Ric offers the book to Frenchie, who eagerly accepts it. "Zis iz true, but t'ere iz so much more zat needs to be addressed. Like zis reference to Mitch Wilson - it says zat he was a multiple champeen. As far as ze Frenchie remembers, zis was the real name of ze man who played ze Dude character, non? So zis page also will be amended." Frenchie rips the page out, repeating Chronos' earlier action. "But t'ere iz so much more zat has been omitted or forgotten. Hey, remember zat time zat the evil purple-headed one, Ultra-Violet, fell into zat meat grinder? Sad, sad t'ing zat was, non?" "It's too bad they put Scotty Cool into a skittles rehab center, but never mention it here, eh?" Primetime offered up, as Ric & Frenchie smile in agreement. "And who iz zis? Matt who? Douglas you say? No, I don't t'ink I know anyone by zat name who has ever competed in ze fWo." "Wasn't that the Wolf Fang guy I beat.....678 times?" said Primetime; the fans responding with boos and even trash was now being thrown into the ring. Chronos swatted down several incoming objects, including a can of cola, which cannot be named for some mysterious reason. "Ah, yes. Zat does ring ze proverbial bell, now zat you mention it." Primetime chuckled. "Hey, Frenchie, how does it feel to be a 57 time World Champion?" asks Primetime, who seems disturbed by a fan in the front row calling him names. "Not'ing quite like being ze undefeated Cruiser, Hardcore, and Buffalo champion, PT. What's it been? 15 mont's now zat I performed zat feat?" Frenchie responds, all the while expressing a beaming smile. "I believe something around 3 years now, actually." Ric turns to the crowd and smirks sarcastically. "Amazing~! The Frenchie truly is one of the all-time greats!" The fans continue to boo and jeer the arrogant trio, who seem to be having way too much fun with this stuff. Primetime turns to his fellow colleagues, and offers up a question. "Primetime Players? What were they? Wasn't that an imposter that I beat the crap out of for trying to ruin my good name?" Frenchie nodded. "Right. We should even bring into light, for ze first time, about zat time when Eron walked backstage and was mistakenly shot 93 times in the head, chest, and ass by a deranged cat. No need to keep covering zat up." The fans in attendance were clearly growing angry, and were turning to the stage for hope that anyone would come out and stop this nonsense. "Or the non-televised match between myself and the Frenchie here for the Woman's title, an Iron Man match that protected the integrity of the belt." Primetime turns and looks at the near rioting crowd, who had broken out in an unflattering chant directed towards 3vil. "3vil sucks! 3vil sucks!" Chronos, Frenchie & Primetime stop their ego stroking session for a moment, but only to tell the fans to shut up. Chronos snickered at the fans, turning slowly in a full circle as he directed his attention towards them. "Shut up! All of you! None of you would be here tonight if it weren't for us... you all paid money to see us... and you may as well sit down, shut up, and enjoy the ride... because tonight is going to be a great night!" This incenses the fans to the point that a few in the front row were having to be held back by members of the security team. Ric turns to his good friends. "Where was I? Oh, yes... wasn't it so sad that Briana was arrested for being 15 and posing in Playboy not too long after that?" Frenchie nods. "Terrible t'ing. However, I t'ink Hellrazor really stepped up and made zat Woman's division his own." Primetime looks down for a moment, before emphatically turning to the crowd. "And it is now officially stated that I have never, ever, worn hot pants, either in the ring or out. I hope that this will put an end to the long standing debate." Ric concurs. "And of course, we'll have to let the record state that the actual original Survivor was Vic Creed, not that piece of inferior scum, Ruben Ross." Primetime and Frenchie nod in agreement with the World Champion. Ric handed the microphone over to Frenchie, who had a few more points to make. "And let the record speak for itself zat Austin Davis was not'ing more zan a very bad wrestler who was inspired by James Armstrong to shave his head, because James Armstrong is lusted after by all of ze females, while Davis is lusted after by filt'y sewer rats and ze like, non?" Ric and Primetime laugh in amusement, as Frenchie apparently is not finished. "And... Cactus Jon... he never held ze fWo World Title; because he was nothing more zan a bad imititation of a bad wrestler who also goes by ze Cactus moniker!" The audience had grown tired of this act, but Frenchie knew this and continued anyway. "And what else can be said about Ice zat hasn't already been said? Ah, zat's right, not'ing has been said about Ice because nobody remembers him!" Primetime reaches for the microphone. "Let's not forget, Frenchie, that David Spencer was never Death. No, in fact, he was never David Spencer. He was just some Brit that nobody cared about, and the only thing he will ever be known for is his sexual escapades with Nikki the Valley Slut!" Primetime passes the microphone over to Ric, who smirks at the fans. "And there's so much that we just don't have the time to cover right now. So, even though we are celebrating tonight, remember that we will be hard at work tomorrow to get things ready for cutting out all the excess baggage that -" "Do YOU buy what The $ellout is $elling?" Chronos snarls while turning to the stage, obviously feeling that he was rudely interrupted. The World Champion since Halloween Hell begins pacing around the ring, as Frenchie & Primetime reacts at the interruption with a pair of snickers (not the candy bar). The fans rejoice with a huge standing ovation, as finally someone had come out to stop this debacle. Chris Universal appears on the stage in what appears to be a very expensive custom made suit. He walks to the ring swiftly; his eyes never leaving the ring. Universal stops just outside the ring, as he looked wary of entering the ring, obviously not about to let 3vil overcome him with a physical assault. Universal waits for his music to fade, before he begins to speak. "You think, that after Twilight, you'll be able to have all that authority?" 3vil stares at Universal, nodding their heads firmly. Universal turns to the crowd, before looking back at the three men in the ring. "You... thought... wrong. Very wrong. You won't have that type of authority. You can't just change history here. You can't just alter the course of history. Ric, you may have not lost the fWo World Title last year, but you were unable to defend it. Frenchie, as much as you despise Ultra-Violet, he did in fact defeat you back at CyberSlam 3. I'm not quite sure if that's a good thing however..." Frenchie looks at Universal suspiciously, his teeth gritting with anger as he knew what Universal was about to say. "...because at the same time, you were forced to reveal yourself... you were forced to take off your mask... and now, to be brutally honest, a lot of us were wishing you'd take that mask and cover your face back up!" The fans respond with rabid cheers, as Universal grins. "And Primetime... it wasn't Ice who slept with the lockerroom whore, it was you! The adventures of Primetime and Nikki The Valley Whore are well documented, Mr. Hot Pants." Universal winks at Primetime, who shakes his fist in anger, while Chronos & Frenchie attempt to restrain him. "But enough of all that. Not only can you not alter the course of history; you won't be able to release the so-called excess baggage from the fWo. Simply because... if you were to terminate the contracts of anyone in the fWo, without a valid reason, well, you'd be wrongfully terminating wrestlers... and you'd be subjected to a lot of lawsuits. And I have a feeling without my wealthy, without my bank... there's no way you'd want to risk something like that. Then again... Ric, you aligned yourself with a man who attempted to end your career... Frenchie, you aligned yourself with someone you had bitter hatred for... and Primetime, well, I already mentioned Nikki, didn't I?" The MGM Grand echoes with cheers inside the arena, as Universal paces back and forth several times on the outside. 3vil begins cussing at Universal outside the ring. 3vil make an attempt to charge at him, but Universal is quickly surrounded by security guards. 3vil think better of it and remain inside the ring. "You know, 3vil... you've worn out your welcome as far as I'm concerned. I don't want to just beat you at Twilight and take back the booking power, no, that's not enough. That's why I'm proposing an extra... stipulation to the match." 3vil collectively raise their eyebrows in interest, and offer Universal to continue. "Not only if you lose that match at Twilight will you lose the booking power, but you will also have to disband as a group. That means... if you lose, the three of you cannot collectively run about as you do now, creating chaos and carnage throughout the fWo!" The fans cheer at this proposal, however, the World and World Tag Team Champions scoff at this notion. Chronos lifts the microphone up; directing this next bit of dialogue towards the fans. "Don't get your hopes up, morons. If you think we're about to accept something like that without the guarantee of something in return, you obviously don't have an ounce of intelligence!" The fans jeer loudly, before Chronos turns his attention to the owner of the fWo. "But I'm sure we'll be willing to discuss it if you offer up something in return. Something of value. Something that you can promise us no matter the outcome of the match at Twilight." Universal obviously already has something in mind. "Well, if you lose, I promise you as the owner of the fWo, I will not fine or suspend any of you after the match solely because of what you three have done with your power." 3vil seem fine with this response, but want more. "It's going to take more than that, Universal." Primetime glares at the former two-time fWo Internet Champion. "And... the three of you are guaranteed fWo World Title shots at a time of your choosing, as long as you provide at least forty-eight hours notice beforehand... and at the time, you have a valid fWo contract." 3vil likes this scenario, and as they discuss the new stipulations, they decide to add another of their own. "Alright... you've got a deal." Primetime says, before handing the microphone to Frenchie. "But we've decided to just go ahead and toss in another stipulation to ze match, you know, just for kicks and giggles. Ze match at Twilight will now be contested under street fight rules; meaning, zat the only way to win is by pinfall or submission, and outside interference as well as ze use of weapons are perfectly legal... and acceptable!" Universal seems upset by this addition, but is willing to take it. "Alright, fine. We seem to have an agreement here." Chronos laughs, staring directly at Universal. "You're a fool, Universal. You made a risk before and it didn't pay off. You're taking a bigger risk now... with that dead weight Star as your partner. He lost to Primetime and Frenchie back to back, and did I mention that tonight he's up against both of them... in a handicap match?" Universal seems displeased by this new development. "Wait a... wait just a second here..." Chronos scoffs. "Wait? No. We've got the booking power... WE just made the match." Frenchie and Primetime smile, taking a moment to stare at their fWo World Tag Team Titles. "Please... you'd be better off with a hundred pound sandbag as your partner. Star is nothing in the fWo, and as Frenchie has said all along, he's a man who based his entire career off of one win! What a joke." Universal is now the one being held back by security, as Chronos is obviously getting under his skin. The fans inside the MGM Grand break out in an "asshole" chant, but Chronos ignores it and talks over fifteen thousand plus screaming fans. "And in addition... I think you're going to like what we've come up with here, Chris. You see that structure hanging above us? Well, earlier this week we made a few phone calls, and somehow... on such short notice, you know, not that we've had this planned or anything... we had a hell in a cell constructed specifically for tonight. Seeing as how we're in a city like Vegas... a city full of people that like to take risks... a city full of people that like to gamble their lives away and end up on the street homeless and without a job... yes, I'm talking about all you pieces of trash right now..." The intense booing grows loudly as Chronos continues. "Tonight, we're going to have us a hell in a cell match!" The fans cheer at this announcement, assuming that 3vil will be involved. The fans obviously hoped to see 3vil get injured, but... "Eight men, or rather, people will compete in the match and the winner... will face me later this evening for the fans Wrestling organization World Championship..." A round of cheers from the crowd as Chronos continues. "The eight participants will be as follows: Ruben Ross. Black Quicksilver. Douglas. The Boston Strangler. Kodiak Vic Creed. The Black Panther. "Oh, and Ruben Ross... should you win the match tonight, not only will you and I collide here tonight for the fWo World Title, but Frenchie and Primetime will not be allowed at ringside at our match next week at Twilight. Assuming you make it there in one piece!" His voice boomed over the speakers. Chronos looks out at the current owner of the fWo and sneers. "Enjoy the last bit of remaining power while you have it. It won't last long. All you do is sign the checks... we, we make the decisions that count around here. Your time as owner... is up." Chronos snarls at Universal, who reacts with a cold stare of his own. Universal turns around to walk up the stage, while 3vil each take to a corner and raise their arms, as Jeremiah Frankton & James Armstrong welcome the fans at home to what should be an incredible edition of Sunday Night Slaughter. This would be a perfect high profile match for Universal to compete in. Win or lose... it would prepare him for next week's battle.
