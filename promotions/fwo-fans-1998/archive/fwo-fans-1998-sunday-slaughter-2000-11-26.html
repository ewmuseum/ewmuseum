fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 26 November 2000

26 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The last Sunday Night Slaughter in November 2000 took to the air with "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson heading in the direction of his lockerroom. Walking towards him was the man who he felt essentially screwed him out of the fWo World Title last Saturday at Fallout... The Flying Frenchie. Wilson & Frenchie came to a standstill in the hall, and onlookers feared for the worst that they may have a situation on their hands. Instead of a physical war, Wilson decided to turn it into a war of words. "Well, if it isn't the man who kept me from winning the fWo World Title last Saturday." Frenchie scoffed. "You lost ze match because of you, not me, Mitch." Wilson smiled. "Yeah, well, if you feel the way you do, and I feel the way I do, then we should settle it... in the ring. Why don't you put up that title shot you're owed at Twilight against me next week? If you defeat me, I'll stop griping about how you screwed me at Fallout... even though we both know it's true." With a sarcastic smirk, Frenchie gladly accepted. "Anyt'ing zat shuts you up, I'm all for. I'll see you in seven days, Mitch. Don't be late." Wilson seemed pleased as Frenchie walked away. "I won't be late... I'll be early. And unfortunately for you, that's too damn bad for your own damn good." Frenchie ignored the comment as he headed elsewhere. Meanwhile, inside the arena, fireworks shot through the air, signalling the beginning of Slaughter. The Abusah (c) vs. Carnage Sunday Slaughter explodes with action to the sounds of Eminem's "Guilty Conscience". Carnage, the crazy Corteia, enters into view. He raises his arms to the jeering Philadelphia crowd, as he tries to regain the Hardcore Title in this four way match. He enters the ring, and Elo's "Fire on High" starts up. Vengence, a man who's wars with Erik Kelly have been unparalleled, surfaces. He walks to ringside with a stolid look on his face, when Gob's "Paint it Black" starts up. What you can best describe for the time being as the fWo's co-Hardcore Champions, the Abusah and Scot Hartt appear on the Slaughter stage. The two men recieve an enormous reaction from the crowd who is solidly pro-Team Ratings. The men charge the ring, and immediately Carnage begins brawling with Hartt, and Vengence with Abusah. Vengence sends Abusah quickly to the outside where he whips him into the stair steps. On the inside, Hartt grabs Carnage down by the afro, and drops a knee across his throat. Switching back to Vengence and Abusah, Vengence maintains the advantage, by dropping him throat first across the guardrail. Hartt solidly has the advantage inside the ring, so he goes to the outside, and pulls out a hockey stick graced with the Philadelphia Flyers logo on it. He goes to nail Carnage, but Carnage gets a boot up to the groin of Hartt. This causes Hartt to drop the stick. Carnage picks it up, and breaks it over Hartt's back the hard way. Hartt falls to the ground in pain, and on the outside Vengence goes to send Abusah into the pole, but Abusah reverses. Vengence hits head first, and walks then right into a chair shot by Abusah. With Vengence startled for the moment, and Carnage headed to the top rope, Abusah does some thinking (for once), and pushes Carnage off of the top rope. Carnage hits the mat, and Abusah decides to go up top. He flies off, but Carnage gets a boot up. He nails Abusah in the head, and both men stay down. Meanwhile, Vengence begins to stir, and he enters the ring, as Scot Hartt gets up. Vengence charges with a clothesline, but Hartt ducks. He stays behind and nails a nice reverse DDT. Hartt covers, but at two Vengence kicks out. Hartt brings him up, and meanwhile, Abusah and Carnage begin brawling. Abusah nails Carnage with a clothesline, that sends him from the ring to the floor. Meanwhile, Hartt who had been looking for a backbody drop on Vengence is set up for the Eclipse. However, Abusah shuns this attempt by pulling Hartt from atop Vengence. He then turns around and nails the ABUSAH SPEER. However, he is too worn down to make the cover. Meanwhile, Carnage sees this situation, and heads to the top. He flies off, nailing the one half of Maximum Carnage, a big elbow drop. He covers, but meanwhile, Scot Hartt has recovered, and he nails Carnage with a trash can lid to the back of the head. Hartt decides to take advantage of this situation by heading to the top with a moonsault. He looks to fly off and nail Vengence, but he moves. Vengence gets up, but is nailed quickly with a trash can to the head by Abusah. This dazes Vengence, so Abusah hits another ABUSAH SPEER. He covers this time, but Carnage pulls him off, and nails the other half of Maximum Carnage, a release german suplex. Thinking that Abusah is done, he brings Hartt up, and nails a release german suplex on him. However, he forgets that Vengence is behind him, and he walks into a big chokeslam from him. Now facing the Slaughter Stage, he brings Carnage up for the Eclipse, but an image of Erik Kelly appears on the Slaughter Screen. Kelly says "Hey, Keene-o.", and this enrages Vengence. Carnage then slides down his back, and nails a release german suplex on him. Carnage then heads to the top and nails the elbow on Abusah. Finally, he makes a cover, and we have a new hardcore champion. Carnage grabs his title and leaves, as Vengence has a look of revenge on his face, and Abusah and Hartt seem to blame each other for the loss. Meanwhile, Slaughter heads elsewhere. Result: Carnage by pinfall Arrival? "Hey, let's clear the area! He's here!" shouted security members, trying to move fans that could not find a way into the First Union Center tonight to catch Slaughter out of the way. A limosuine slowly drove down the arena entranceway backstage, before the limosuine came to a stop. The driver of the limosuine got out of the front of the limo, and began to walk to the back... before we could see who was inside the limosuine, however, Slaughter went elsewhere... Alliance? Kristen Jacobs stood by with the newest addition of the fWo roster, "Kodiak" Vic Creed. Before the cameras started rolling, Creed looked at his chiseled arms, and admired them, while taking a glance or two at Jacobs. Jacobs ignored it, and signalled to the camera crew that she was ready to start the segment. They began to count her down, and finally, we were off and running. "Hello fWo fans, I'm standing by with the newest addition to the fWo, "Kodiak" Vic Creed. First off, Creed... may I call you creed?" Creed smiled. "Sure thing baby. Remember the name; if you're lucky you'll be screaming it later tonight." Jacobs rolled her eyes, before continuing. "Anyways... how do you explain your attacks on fWo Cruiserweight Champion Ruben Ross... and later, the fWo World Champion Primetime?" Creed paused and looked at his arms, before offering up a response. "Jacobs, I got into the fWo by my own skill, and not by some screwed up voting system in an obviously-rigged contest like that no-talent Ross. I punked Ross out twice to prove that the best competitor in Survivor three months ago didn't really win. And that explains why I beat up the poor little Rube. But what about the World Champion? Why would I attack him? Hell, that's easy. Everyone who knows the Main Man, knows that he's a World Champion. Belt or not, I'm always automatically considered World Championship material. So my beating up on poor ol' Primetime was just my way of introducing myself, one World Champion to another." Creed smiled confidently. "That's absurd." spoke Jacobs, who wasn't finished speaking. "You're a newcomer to the fWo, a bonafide rookie, and you think of yourself as World Championship material?" Creed again smiled. "Not only I think that, Jacobs. Everyone thinks that. And to back that up, I'd like to welcome to of my friends here to join us." Creed glanced off camera, and "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson & "The Future" Lance Star joined him. "You see, these two men were the first two to welcome me with open arms to the fWo roster. And why shouldn't they have? Mitch and I are almost like brothers, for crying out loud." Jacobs raised her eye, and asked Creed to elaborate. "Well, in case you didn't know, Mitch' father - Slade - trained me. He admired The Main Man's dedication, his hard work, his never ending quest to be the absolute best he can be. He took me under his wing, so to speak - and became like a father to me. Since Mitch is his son, you could say we're like brothers." Jacobs nodded. "And your relationship with Lance?" Creed smiled. "Lance is a talent who just needed to get his head on straight. Mitch and I have done exactly that." Wilson stepped in. "All we did was point Lance in the right direction. His friendship with Primetime didn't benefit him in any way, shape or form... and in fact, he didn't realize that his friendship with Primetime was actually hurting him. Why? Because Lance had been associated for months with a man who tried to injure others much in the same way Lance was injured around this time last year. And now... Lance has seen that, just as how I saw it... and how Vic saw it. Unfortunately for Primetime... that leaves him with no friends. And seeing as how just about everyone on the fWo roster hates your guts... I wouldn't want to be in your shoes, Brian." Jacobs nodded, and appeared ready to conclude the interview, before reminding Creed his match with Jackhammer was up next. Creed turned to the camera and parted us with the following: "Jackhammer... well, Jackhammer is out of his league. See, he's strong; I'm stronger. He's tough; I'm tougher. But that's where the similarity ends. I'm the Main Man, the Alaskan Juggernaut. Jackhammer is a poor schlub with the mental capacity of a retarded clam. He's going to take an unmerciful beating, and there's only two things he can do about it: Nothing and like it." Creed stared down Jacobs to the point a scared look came across her face, before he shared a laugh with Wilson & Star, before the trio exited... "Kodiak" Vic Creed vs. Jackhammer Slaughter returned to the ring to the sounds of "Step Into The Light", by Dust For Life. This brings out the emerging singles star who has a long storied career with lots of success in the tag team division, Jackhammer. Jackhammer walks out to a mild reaction from the crowd, who were probably just caught offguard by his new theme music. Jackhammer rolled into the ring and raised his arms, garnering some cheers from the crowd. Those cheers soon turned to boos, however, as out came fWo newcomer, "Kodiak" Vic Creed. Creed was greeted with a large amount of boos from the Philly audience, but the Alaskan Juggernaut smirked them off as he walked to the ring. Jackhammer and Creed met in the center of the ring, and the match immediately began with some trash talking. Creed finally switched the match from a verbal war, to a physical war with a shot to the jaw. Jackhammer responded with one of his own. The two men continued to punch each other back and forth, before Creed got the upperhand with a rake to the eyes. Creed then sent Jackhammer into the corner and as Jackhammer came out, Creed took him down with a lariat. Creed brought him up and raked him once again, before lifting the former tag team champion up and slamming him to the mat. Creed then decided to take a high risk. Going upstairs, Creed waited to launch himself, but Jackhammer quickly got to his feet and stumbled to the ropes. Creed now found himself in an uneasy position - crotched on the top ropes. Jackhammer went up tope and took the Alaskan Juggernaut down with a superplex. Both men crashed the mat, which was a little worse for wear after a combined five hundred sixty five pounds hit the mat with authority. Jackhammer went for a cover, but the Main Man kicked out at two and a half. Jackhammer brought Creed up and sent him into the ropes, and when Creed returned he found himself at the mercy of Hammer courtesy of a spinebuster. Jackhammer went for another cover, but Creed kicked out. Both men up to their feet, Hammer sent Creed into the corner, but Creed reversed. As Hammer stumbled out, Creed took him down with a belly-to-belly suplex. Creed then brought Hammer up and took him down with a snapmare takeover, before charging towards the ropes, returning and taking Hammer down with a sitting dropkick. Creed went for a cover, but JH kicked out. Bringing Jackhammer up once again, he unleashed with some chops to the chest, before sending him to the ropes. Jackhammer reversed, and when Creed returned Jackhammer ducked for a backdrop... instead, however, Creedzilla grabbed Jackhammer and hit a suplex into a piledriver, which garnered a two count. Sensing it would take much more than that to finish off the former four-time tag team champion, Creed sent Jackhammer to the ropes, and when Jackhammer returned he was taken down with a jumping shouldertackle. Creed then brought Jackhammer up and hit a reverse Death Valley Driver, also known as the Alaskan Avalanche. Creed decided to take another risk, and went to the top rope. This time he didn't miss, as he hit a frogsplash, also known as the Creedzillinator. Realizing he had to put Jackhammer away if he wanted to win, he brought him up, and hit the Hangman's DDT, the same move he introduced Ruben Ross & Primetime to last weekend. Jackhammer attempted to kickout, but it was all for naught as Creed earned a win in his first match in the fans Wrestling organization. The referee raised the arm of "Kodiak" Vic Creed, who again, was victorious in his first time seeing action in the fWo, as Slaughter cut to commercials... Result: Creed by pinfall Unusually Happy.. Juno walked without a care in the back hallways of the First Union Center at Slaughter. He was happy about something, could have been the phone call he got earlier the week, who knew. He passed Red Dragon, and without noticing bumped him. "Ouchy, you dislocated my solar plex!" Red Dragon exclaimed. "Do you even know what that is?" Juno retorted back. "Yeah" Red Dragon said. "Yeah?!" Juno replied. "YEAH!" Red Dragon came back harsh. "OH YEAH?!" Juno came on strong."Well I kissed a girl!" Red Dragon hit below the belt, he had Juno reeling. "YEAH!? Well...." Juno left the scene for a second, but came back. "YOUR DAD!" Red Dragon rolled his eyes at the comment and left Juno by himself. "Worked for... wait, nobody else used that... " Juno stated, and kept walking in his desired path. He came across a locker ro! om door with Greg Davis written on it. He peered in and saw Greg Davis looking in the mirror. "Davisism is catching on." Davis replied to himself. "You bet Greg, it's catching wind like a para-sailer in Ruben Ross' mother's backdraft." He replied again to himself. "What?" Then again... "Trust me." Juno walked into the room and Greg was a little startled, but kept a natural cool. Juno broke the silence "We need to talk." Juno shut the door and Slaughter continued. "The Future" Lance Star vs. Courage Near ringside, the fans prepared for the next match-up, which pitted two individuals that formed a very successful tag team in the fWo known as the California Kings. 'Bring It' by Soulfly marked the return to the ring of everyone's favorite rock & roller, Courage. Courage was greeted with boos from the crowd, but responded to the fans with a simple smirk. Courage entered the ring and did some light warm-up exercises. Perhaps he should've done more than that as the last time he was in action, he lost in the first round of the Wrestler of the Year Tournament to Scot Hartt of all people. The sounds of Soulfly were replaced by the sounds of Limp Bizkit and 'My Generation', which brought out "The Future" Lance Star. Star was greeted with boos from the crowd as well, but had no reaction to them, instead keeping a serious expression on his face as he walked down the shiny entrance ramp. Star entered the ring via underneath the bottom rope, and he was met with immediate stomps to the head and neck area. The referee saw this and signalled for the bell, and thus, the match was live. Courage brought Star up and sent him to the ropes, and as Star returned he ducked underneath the reverse knife edge chop. Star bounced off the ropes and returned once again; Courage went for a hip toss, but Star blocked it, backflipped out of it, landed on his feet and took Courage down with a clothesline. Lance Star then went to the ring apron, and waited for Courage to reach a vertical base. Courage did, and Star leaped to the top rope, and flew back into the ring with a springboard into a hurrancanrana. This garnered a two, as Courage escaped the pin. Courage and Star got back to their feet. Courage and Star exchanged chops, before Courage sent Star to the ropes, and as Star returned Courage ducked for a backdrop. Instead, Star took him down with an inverted swinging neckbreaker. Star then went to the top rope and flew off with a guilotine legdrop, but Courage moved, and thus, Star missed. Courage took over and sent Star to the turnbuckles, and Star slammed into the buckles sternum first with such a velocity it nearly knocked him backwards to the mat. Instead, Star stumbled around out of the corner, and Courage kicked him in the stomach, and then hit a powerbomb. He brought Lance up again and hit another powerbomb. He went for the third, but Star struggled out of it into a backdrop. Courage hung on for the sunset flip however, which got a two count. Courage went to the top rope and flew off, hitting a Missle Dropkick. Courage then brought Star up and hit an Implant DDT, which was the set up to his finisher, the 'World Rocker'. Star slowly got to his feet, and staggered around, and fortunately for him he narrowly ducked the superkick. Star held onto Courage by the side, and hit a German suplex into a bridge, which garnered a two count. Star decided to go up top once again, and he flew off with a Shooting Star Press, not to be confused with his finisher, the 'Shooting Star'. Again, Star missed, and found himself on the mat. Courage recovered and struggled to his feet to apply an elevated Boston Crab. Moments passed, and as it appeared Star would be ready to give in, Star struggled to reach for the ropes. Just as he was about to reach them, Courage pulled him away. Star refused to give in, however, and finally, Courage released the hold. He brought Star up and sent him to the ropes. Star reversed, and then went for a hip toss, but Courage reversed it into a hip toss of his own. Star charged, and Courage took him down with an armdrag. Courage then applied an armbar, but as the two men got to their feet, Star escaped with a go-behind, and then hit a back suplex. The action stopped as both men were laid out on the mat, exhausted from the furious pace the duo had wrestled at. A ten count began, and as both men reached their feet, Star quickly grabbed Courage and went for the Shooting Star (modified urange suplex). Instead, Courage blocked it, and elbowed Star in the head. Star stumbled away, and this time, Courage went for the 'World Rocker' and nailed it. Both men were out of it though, and Courage attempted to struggle over for the pin. Several moments passed, and finally, he draped an arm over "The Future". At two, Star got a foot on the ropes. Undaunted, Courage dragged Star out to the center of the ring, and singled for his former finisher, the 'Courage Required'. He charged to the ropes, and flew off with the asai moonsault, but Star countered with his knees. Courage was dealt a hard blow to the ribs... and Star got to his feet. He beckoned Courage to get up, and he finally did... he groggily walked over holding his ribs, Star grabbed him, and hit the Shooting Star. At two, Primetime grabbed Star from underneath the bottom ropes, and pulled him to the floor. Star was pissed, but before he could react, he said hello to a steel chair. Primetime then rolled him back into the ring as the referee called for the bell. Primetime brought Courage up, and hit a slingshot Michinoku Driver, aka, the Snapshot. The fWo World Champion then brought Lance up, and dealt one to him as well. Primetime then exited as quickly as he appeared, but not before he mouthed something off to both men, before escaping into the crowd... Result: No Contest Arrived? Slaughter went backstage quickly before the commercial break to find the limosuine still where we last saw it. As the door opened, the camera crew rushed to find out who was inside. Before we could, the limosuine driver instructed the cameras to back away, as the person inside did not wish to have their image shown to the world at this time. The fans inside the arena saw the action on the SlaughterScreen, and booed as they could not find out who was inside the long stretch limo. Slaughter headed elsewhere... Attack? Elsewhere, we saw Vengence leaving a room with a lead pipe in hand. Inside the room, Pamela Dee called for help. Medics rushed to the scene, and the cameras captured "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly laying on the floor tending his arm heavily. He released some pain with a forceful yell, of which could not be understood fully. Dee stood over her man and informed the medical personnel about what had happened, although it was obvious. Vengence had left his mark. Six-Men/Four Corners: Crusader, Red Dragon, Juno Law, "The Ego" Greg Davis, Black Quicksilver, Mike Heftel Slaughter returned to the ring, for a six-men/four corners match for a shot at the fWo Cruiserweight Title next week on Slaughter. The rules were simple. Two men would be in the ring at all times, while the others would remain on the ring apron in their own corner. It would be an elimination style match - if you were pinned or you submitted, you were gone. The lone participant of the match would be the winner. And with that, fWo Ring Announcer Matt Panzer began the introductions. "We're Not Gonna Take It" by Twisted Sister brought out the STUMP Squad in full force - Black Quicksilver (w/ Stump), Mike Heftel & Red Dragon (w/ Claire). With three of the six participants now in the ring, Panzer introduced the other participants. First, to the sounds of "Eye Of The Tiger" by Survivor, came out "The Ego" Greg Davis. Davis looked at the crowd who greeted him earnestly with boos. He ignored them, and then stayed outside the ring, as to not leave himself open from an attack by the ever-so-powerful STUMP Squad. They were then joined next by Juno Law, who walked out onto the stage while the sounds of "Victory" by Puff Daddy blared throughout the First Union Center. Law joined Davis at ringside, before the duo charged the ring. Heftel immediately took to a corner, while Dragon & BQ faced off with Law & Davis. All hell had broken loose while Heftel appeared to have the upperhand on the outside, taking a break. Not so fast. After Law through Dragon over the top rope onto the floor, and Davis had disposed of BQ the same way, Law & Davis brought Heftel into the ring with a double-team vertical hanging suplex. Heftel joined BQ & Dragon on the outside, and now with Law & Davis in the ring quite proud at what they had achieved, "Switchback" by Biohazard began to play, and outwalked Wrestler of the Year finalist Crusader. Crusader charged down the entrance ramp into the ring, and Law & Davis welcomed him with stomps to the head & neck. They then sent him to the ropes and as he returned Law took him down with a drop-toe-hold, right into a DDT, courtesy of "The Ego". Law & Davis got up and seemed proud of their efforts, until they turned around. BQ & Dragon had recovered, and leaped off the top rope from their respective corners, nailing both Law & Davis with missle dropkicks. BQ then sent Dragon to the ropes and when Dragon returned he took him down with a hip toss. BQ then took him down once again with a flying headscissors takedown, before going for a cover. Heftel broke up the pin, and BQ asked him why. Heftel simply shrugged, and the match continued. BQ sent Dragon to the ropes and ducked for a backdrop, but Dragon countered when he returned by grabbing him by his head and sending him backwards to the mat. Dragon then tagged in Heftel, who then tagged in Crusader, because he didn't feel like wrestling at the moment. Crusader went to the top rope, and flew off and took Dragon down with a hurrancanrana. Crusader then sent him to the ropes, and as Dragon returned, Crusader took him down with the Flipside - the cradle michinoku driver. Crusader then signalled to go up top, but before he could, Davis reached in with a blind tag. Davis went to the top rope, and flew off with a frogsplash, but Dragon got his knees up. Crusader seemed flustered about the tag, but went to his corner. Davis sent Dragon into the corner, and BQ made a blind tag. Davis rushed in, but Dragon moved out of the corner, and Davis hit sternum first. BQ leaped to the top rope, and flew off with a flying high crossbody. However, Davis' momentum sent him over BQ, and he ended atop the former Cruiserweight Champion. Davis then hooked him for a pin, and put his legs on the ropes behind him for added leverage, unbeknownst to the official. The first three count recorded, and BQ was eliminated - however, he stayed at ringside. Davis celebrated in the ring, but Crusader came in and rolled him up for the pin. Before Davis could react, he was eliminated. Davis threw a fit on the outside, the referee's instructed him to the back, but he wouldn't listen. Dragon charged the ring and Crusader took him down with a Japanese armdrag. Crusader then grabbed him, brought him towards the corner, and hit a Tornado DDT. Sensing that Dragon had already endured enough punishment, Crusader went to the top rope. Instead, however, Law shook the ropes, crotching him. With Crusader in an uneasy position, Dragon struggled to his feet, and climbed up to the top rope. Dragon hit a Gargoyle suplex from the top, which of course was the set up to his finisher, the Dragonsault. Dragon headed up top to another corner, and flew off with the Reverse Shooting Star Press. For some reason, as Greg Davis jumped up and distracted the referee inside the ring, Law entered and pulled Crusader out of the way... with Dragon smacking the mat hard. Law then grabbed Dragon and hit DDT, before laying Crusader atop him. Heftel tried to tell the referee what had occured, but it was all for naught. Dragon had been eliminated. Davis & Dragon fought to the back, while Law & Crusader went at it. Crusader hit yet another cradle Michinoku Driver, which was good enough to record the three count. With only two men left, Law exited to boos, quite displeased with himself/what had occured. Heftel decided to take a high risk, but Crusader went up top and hit a overhead belly-to-belly suplex. With Heftel laid out in the ring, Crusader went up top once again for the Gravity 101. However, the referee was distracted by Juno Law, who a bunch of referees held back on the ramp. Law was pissed and believed it was a fast count, but could do nothing about it. With the referee's attention elsewhere, BQ entered the ring, and hit a frankensteiner from the top rope. BQ quickly exited, and the referee turned back to the action. However, there, he found Stone who had made her way down to the ringside area through the crowd. Stone distracted the referee, while Plague entered, and laid out Heftel with an Inverted DDT. Plague then laid Cru atop the fallen Heftel, and Stone stopped distracting the referee. The referee turned around and saw the pin, and counted. It was Crusader who would go onto face Ruben Ross for the fWo Cruiserweight Title next week. Crusader stood to his feet in confusion, while Stone stood with Plague at her side, clapping for Crusader, who was absolutely confused... meanwhile, Slaughter rolled on... "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly vs. The Boston Strangler Slaughter returned from the commericals as the fans prepared for the War To Settle The Score between Erik Kelly and the Boston Strangler. A call of "POWERBOMB!" is heard over the PA, which then breaks into Limp Bizkit's "Break Stuff". Erik Kelly comes out favoring his right arm, due to the earlier attack by Vengence. Kelly, with Pamela Dee by his side as always, however, looks determined to wrestle the match. "O Fortuna" starts up, and the Boston Strangler steps out, demonic looking as ever. He enters the ring, and gets in Kelly's face. The bell rings. The match...is on. Immediately a fist fight breaks out between the two, and even quicker the Strangler begins to work on the arm. Strangler, showing some techincality, locks on a Fujiwara armbar, Kelly, however, is too knowing, and he reaches the ropes. However, Strangler doesn't have to break, since this is a no disqualification match. A kick to the gut by Kelly, however, allows Kelly to break free. Kelly nails a few punches to the face, and sends Strangler into the ropes. Strangler comes back, and walks into a big sidewalk slam from Kelly. The Eradicator quickly covers, but at two, Strangler kicks out. Kelly sends Strangler over the top rope and to the outside. He lifts up the protective mats, and looks to set Strangler up for a powerbomb on the cold, hard floor. However, Kelly's arm is too weakened to do so, and he can't get him up. Kelly struggles and tries again, but nothing's doing. Strangler takes advantage, and sweeps Kelly's legs from out under him, and then he slingshots him into the ringpost. Kelly nails it face first, and then hits the floor. Demonically, Strangler begins dropping knees on that arm. He then rolls Kelly into the ring, and nails a beautiful german suplex. Strangler positions Kelly near the ring post, and then goes to the outside. He wraps Kelly's arm around the ring post, and then goes and gets a chair. Strangler prepares to swing, but at the last second, Kelly moves his arm. Strangler's chair shot nails the ring post, and Strangler then is nailed by the richochet by the chair. Kelly sees this, and then heads to the outside. He begins maniaclly driving the chair into the sternum of Strangler, harder and harder each time. After he concludes the beating, he throws the chair to the outside, and then grabs the ring bell. He looks to nail Strangler over the head with it, but a low blow causes Kelly to drop it.
