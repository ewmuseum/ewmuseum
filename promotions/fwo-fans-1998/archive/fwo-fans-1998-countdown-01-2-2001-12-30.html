The weather in the City by the Bay was, to put it mildly, not what fWo officials had been expecting when they scheduled Countdown for California. It was cold outside, and the wind only made it worse. The night sky was covered with clouds. Beyond that, it was raining. Not just raining - pouring. The weather seemed to have set the tone for the evening coming into the show - dark, gloomy, and depressing. The fWo's long-time defender, the Boston Strangler, had sold his soul to join with perhaps the most hated man in fWo history, Austin Davis. The fans were still in disbelief. It was the main topic passing between the lips of those few who had arrived late to the show as they walked in. How could Strangler turn his back on them like that? Nobody could provide a satisfactory answer. The final few stragglers entered the Cow Palace, leaving the downpour behind them. Inside, where it was warm and dry, the final preparations were being made for Countdown 2. Outside, in the gloom and the downpour, lightning struck behind the Cow Palace. A superstitious man might have considered it a bad omen... and it might have been indeed. Countdown To Oblivion 2... was about to begin. Explanation Fireworks exploded throughout the Cow Palace, and the atmopshere was raucus as Countdown To Oblivion 2 took to the air. Fans held signs expressing their love or hatred for their favorite fWo superstars. Signs such as "Ross Is #1", "Neo = Big Meanie" and "Strangler = The Real $ellout" dotted throughout the arena. Fans cheered wildly as Jeremiah Frankton & James Armstrong welcomed everyone at home to Countdown To Oblivion 2. The cheers, however, changed to a mass of boos and jeers as 'Down With The Sickness' by Disturbed started up. Austin Davis appeared on the stage, and in perhaps one of the most strangest sights you'd ever see, the Boston Strangler was at his side. Davis carried with him his half of the fWo World Tag Team Championship that he currently held with Chris Universal, but it was highly unlikely the titles would be defended any time soon from that duo. Davis & Strangler slowly walked down the stage, discussing with each other the reaction they were receiving from the fans. For once, the Boston Strangler was being booed. Unlike late last year and early this year, he knew why he was receiving this particular reaction and more importantly, understood it. A thin smile appeared over his lips as the duo climbed into the ring, Davis stopping midway on the ring apron and bowing his head, before his arms extended outwards... the tag title in one hand, a bag in the other. The fans jeered loudly, but Davis ignored it as he entered the ring and gesturing for a microphone. The ringside attendant threw him one, and Davis waited for the boos and his music to quiet down before he spoke. "I'm sure you have one question... and I'm sure it's a good question. Why?" Davis turned and looked at Strangler, before looking back at the fans. "Well, I would have to respond with... WHY THE HELL NOT?" His voice lashed out at the fans, who were caught off guard at first but they responded with more boos. Davis continued. "First of all, while I was in rehabilitation... and while Paul was in his slump... we fed off each other. And we realized something. That we are each other's only equals inside or out of this ring. Both of us have ruled over this company with an iron first... and both of us have held the fWo World Title numerous times. Inside the ring or out I knew I could always count on Paul bringing out my best... and he could count on the same from me." Davis paused as it finally seemed to the fans that what they saw last night was not a fluke or a figment of their imagination. It was the biter, cold, hard truth. They just couldn't - or didn't want to - believe it. "Second of all... it was us... it was Paul and I who took the fWo to the greatest heights it had ever achieved in early 2000. And it all began at CyberSlam 3. Our match, our main event... cemented the fWo as truly something important. Without us, without that night... it's absolutely certain that the fWo would be at the top of the list of feds that never was... and never would be. Third of all... we both have a mutual hatred for the owner of the fWo... and while I just happen to be his tag team partner..." Davis smiled wickedly "... I think it's safe to say that I'm not very fond of him. As for Paul..." Davis paused... before turning and handing the microphone to the Boston Strangler. The fans jeered loudly as he slowly lifted the microphone to his mouth, preparing to undoubtedly say some things that he had wanted to for a long time. "For three years, we've put your goals ahead of our own. For three years we've been the shield placed in front of the fWo to absorb all of the damage... Now it's time to be the sword. In the beginning, there was a little group call the Dark Riders. We were the first to step forward to challenge them... along with the owner of the company and three very talented wrestlers, all former world champions. Our reward? Beaven turned his favor to a blabbermouth second string baseball player turned wrestler... and pushed him to the moon. We were left to feud with comic book characters and sci-fi horror film patsies. We were the most technically sound wrestler in the promotion...and we were given midcard gag match duties. So... we took a break. We stepped away, and came back when the timing was right for us to do so. We also came back with a plan. That Heavyweight title would be ours... no matter what. It was in our quest for that title that we went head to head with a man who was our true equal in the ring. Austin Davis... you might have heard of him. He offered us a spot with his team... but we chose the fWo's glory over our own. We put everything on the line for the fWo... while some wrestlers were busy deciding what color of neon hotpants to wear...we were defending this company. While some were using terms like 'buyrate' 'popping shares' and 'ratings saver' ...we were using terms like 'honor' 'discipline' and 'respect'... while some were busy appearing on TV shows, or movies, or trying out for a sports team... we were at the shows, fighting, and building our career...and this company Our repayment was to be locked away a few months later. Cast aside... and forgotten. Suddenly, we were worth something again when 3vil began to run rampant. We fought them off to the best of our abilities... but Universal... you didn't make things any easier. You rationed out power as incentives for matches... like a fool. With all of the troubles this promotion has had over the years, you had a chance to stop it from entering another cycle of turmoil... and you missed it. We've stood by and watched incompetence of ownership following the death of the man who started this promotion. Finn was a weakling, Armstrong an imbecile and Universal... you suffer the worst shortcoming of all: Ego. Your ego is too great to let you think that someone could outsmart, outclass, and outmaneuver you. Well now someone has. WE have." Boos followed his last statement, and Davis stood by with a wry smile on his face. Strangler sat expressionless. "And now... we give you all a stern warning. Whatever happens to the fWo in the future is a result of the fWo's callous attitude towards the two of us... the two of us who made the promotion what it is!" His voice boomed loudly as he walked towards the edge of the ring, emphasizing his last remark. The mood of the fans would soon change, as Chris Universal appeared on the stage without warning. The fans exploded with cheers, while Davis & Strangler seemed perturbed at his appearance. "Davis, we may be the tag team champions... but tonight... one of two things are going to happen." Davis' eyes perked up, as he gave Universal his full attention. "Tonight, the Boston Strangler is going to wrestle a match. If he wins the match... well, he will take my spot in the team and you guys will be the tag team champions." The fans weren't fond of this idea, but the two men inside the ring were. "But if he doesn't win the match... well, the titles will be vacated and that's something I don't want. Nonetheless, if the Strangler lost the match... it's not like I would be disappointed." Universal gave a stern look at his enemies in the ring, before continuing on. "But Strangler wins are like a seventy degree day in Buffalo, New York... they just haven't happened lately and quite frankly I'll be surprised if they happen any time soon!" An ovation of cheers rocked the Cow Palace, but Universal wasn't done. "Oh, by the way... your opponent will be the 2001 Wrestler of the Year, Ruben Ross!" Strangler' jaw dropped, but he stood confidently in the ring. Davis scoffed at it, while another ovation of cheers rocked the arena, but again, Universal wasn't done. "Now you two can sit there and take credit for the hard work of many, and that's fine, you're entitled to your opinion.
