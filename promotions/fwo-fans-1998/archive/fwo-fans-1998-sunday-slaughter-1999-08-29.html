Sunday Slaughter, 29 August 1999

29 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Fans poured into the fWo arena for what was anticipated as the biggest Sunday Slaughter ever... with five big title matches and the fallout of Meltdown on center stage. Wrestlers readied themselves for action backstage in their dressing rooms, and tension was high. Already several small fights had broken out backstage, and officials ran to the outside of the Dark Riders dressing room to break up a brawl between Death and Darkness. Threats flew back and forth between wrestlers, and several referees expressed concern for their safety to the commissioner. With tension running at an all-time high, Sunday Slaughter opened... Death (c) vs. Cactus Jon Slaughter opens to find Cactus Jon making his way out to a mixed reaction from the crowd, indicating that some may feel sympathetic towards Jon because of the attack received at Meltdown from Fallen. Jon rolls into the ring underneath the bottom rope and awaits his opponent. The number "4" appears on the Slaughter-big-screen before it explodes into the words "The Dark Riders". The arena goes dark after that, before a lone spotlight shines on the leader himself, Death. Death begins walking down to the ring alone with a look of intensity bleeding from his face. He climbs up onto the ring apron, and soon thereafter into the ring and immediately attacks Jon. He whips him into the turnbuckles and as Jon comes staggering out Death takes him down with a backdrop. Death waits for Jon to get up and then goes for a clothesline. Jon ducks and as Death returns, Jon takes him down with a drop-toe-hold. Both men spring back up to their feet and Jon takes Death down with a dropkick, and after Death rolls out onto the floor, buying himself some time. Meanwhile, the referee checks on Jon... who spots Eron running down the ramp and he attacks Death from behind, before sending him into the ringpost. Jon keeps the referees attention diverted from the action taking place outside of the ring. Darkness comes out of the crowd and picks up a chair near the timekeepers table and brings it over to Eron. Eron takes several shots at Death before giving it back to Darkness, who does the same. The vicious attack continues as the referee finally turns around to see the attack going on the outside. He immediately disqualifies Jon, and Jon doesn't take kindly to referees that disqualify him around here. Jon tosses the referee in the corner and is prepared to do something dastardly when Dina makes her way out, with Fallen restrained by a leather strap around his neck. She points at Jon and releases Fallen, who at once springs into the ring and attacks Jon from behind. He tosses him onto the mat and hovers over him like a predator stalking his prey. He claws at the face of Jon, before biting on his shoulder. He then tries pulling some of Jon' hair out before several fWo security members, referees and other worthless scraps getting paid make their way out. They pull Fallen off him, but he pushes them away and begins attacking Jon some more. Other fWo security members come down to separate the attack on the outside. Fallen continues to attack Jon like a man possessed, not letting anything sidetrack him from his intentions. Dina laughs at the massacre in the ring as Fallen is finally pulled off. Dina finally restrains him and pats his head like an owner patting a dog, or in this case a machine mindlessly hell bent on the destruction of Cactus Jon. Fearing that Fallen may go over the edge and do something to the fans at ringside, we cut to a preview of the next big pay-per-view event, entitled "Fallout". Result: Death by DQ Y2BQ As the ring cleared and things settled down to normal, a "Millennium" clock shows up on the Travistron, and starts counting down. After a minute, everyone realizes that it really is counting down to the actual millennium, Jan 1, 2001. Everyone looks around confused until the image of the clock disappears abruptly, and the lights go out. A few seconds later, a few workers set off bottle rockets, and "JERICHO" flashes on the Travistron. Soon, "CAME" flashes on the Travistron, followed by "TUMBLING", and "DOWN". BQ steps out onto the ramp, without Stump, and with a microphone. Yelling out to the crowd, Black Quicksilver screamed, "Welcome...to Slaughter is....well, uh, pretty good. But it's no Warfare! Now, speaking of Warfare, someone challenged me on last week's edition of the highly rated television show. I'm speaking of Courage, and his good friend Jake. Now Stump can't be here right now, he's too busy doing some bar-hopping, but he sent me here...to save this....well, actually, I'm not saving anything, but, well, to steal, no borrow, no, wait, yup, to steal, a line from some of my friends....Courage, you're a big meanie! And Jake's even a bigger meanie! And his stomach is even a BIGGER meanie! It doesn't matter if I'm breaking Rule #12854694lookIknowabignumberB34...but I don't back down from any challenge! So what if I've lost my last five matches! But I do have an excuse! My match with Stone, where I got beat with a (does finger quotes) "chain", was actually a *prequel* to all my losses before then! Did anyone catch that? If you have proof, I'll give you a free Black Quicksilver action figure, as long as you buy thirty dollars worth of fWo merchandise! No, make that fifteen! As I was saying..." As Black Quicksilver kept walking, he didn't spot where he was walking and tripped over his own feet. He fell down the ramp, and wound up somersaulting forward right into the ring apron. Matt Panzer helps him up, but Black Quicksilver shoved him back. "What are you doing? I have the courage!", and then Black Quicksilver swung at Panzer, but misses, spins around, trips over his feet again, and falls onto the ramp. There was a moment of silence, and Black Quicksilver weakly stammered out, "Uhhh....medic..." fWo Medical personnel come out to take BQ away. BQ gets loaded on the stretcher and starts to speak, "Courage... uhhh....Jake...I'll...gah...see you....this....owie...Friday..." He waved to the crowd as he vanished backstage, and some commercials began to roll... Doomsday (c) vs. Chameleon & Total Eclipse We return from sponsors shilling their products and spot Chameleon and the Total Eclipse in the ring, as Doomsday makes their way out walking down the shiny entrance ramp carrying the fWo World Tag Team Titles. Stevie Roberts looks into the camera and says "Hollywood Blondes... you couldn't have VL, so you got a fake imitation named Megan Dawn. And now, you can't get the titles, so you had fake imitations made. Speaking of fake... hey Megan, those can't possibly be real." Roberts then smiles goofily before saying "YEAH, BABY!" and continuing to walk down to the ring with VL. Roberts slides into the ring and he and Chameleon start things off, as Total Eclipse goes to his corner... slowly. Roberts whips Chameleon to the ropes and as Chameleon returns, Roberts simply gouges his eyes. Roberts then prances around as if he accomplished something spectacular. Roberts tags in VL who comes in and takes Chameleon down with a chopblock. She then pulls Chameleon up and whips him into the corner. As Chameleon crashes in, VL does a cartwheel into an elbow and Chameleon is staggered. VL then takes him down with a Michinoku Driver, but only gets a two count. Suddenly, the Hollywood Blondes make their way out carrying the "fake" fWo World Tag Team Titles. Roberts does not appear to see them, and VL and Chameleon are continuing their battle inside the ring. The Blondes pull Roberts off the apron and stun gun him on the guardrail at ringside. Star walks over to Total Eclipse and begins pulling him off the ring apron, to no avail. Star calls Universal over for help, and Universal obliges. The duo finally get Eclipse off the ring apron, before they attempt to double team slam Eclipse through the table. Eclipse is far too heavy for the Blondes, so the Blondes move to plan "b". Star diverts Eclipse' attention and Universal gets down on all fours behind Eclipse, just in front of the announcers table. Star pushes Eclipse, but that isn't enough. He grabs a chair and whacks him with it, but that isn't enough. Finally, Star tells Universal to get up because plan "b" isn't working. They resort to plan "c", and grab James Armstrong' package of Hostess ding dongs (talk about product placement!) off the table. They then hold it over the announcers table, about a foot above it. Eclipse begins leaning down to get it and Universal kicks Eclipse in the ass, which knocks Eclipse through the table. The Blondes high five each other and the crowd gasps as the table is trapped under all that weight. In the ring, VL takes Chameleon down with a flying head scissors before spotting the Blondes. She immediately runs over and jumps at them with a pescado, but Star catches VL. Trapped in Star clutches, Universal decides to pull VL's head towards his crotch and mimics VL giving him oral sex. Star then tosses VL onto Eclipse, and somehow, inconceivably, VL lands on Eclipse, but then rolls off his body to the right and is now trapped under his arm. With VL unable to move, the Blondes storm the ring and Star catches Chameleon in the uranage suplex, while Universal tosses the referee out of the ring. Universal then lays down on the mat, and places a strand of his long blonde hair on Chameleon. Star then counts the three, and then gets on the microphone and proclaims he and Universal as the "true" tag team champions. Meanwhile, the commissioner walks out onto the ramp with the undaunting task of figuring out how to get Total Eclipse away from ringside... Result: No Contest The attack Backstage chaos continued as fWo medical personnel rushed to get to a dressing room, where one of the fWo superstars had been attacked. Rushing to the scene, it became apparent that it was Mike Bear, lying face down in a pool of blood. The Falcon and Wretch stood by, but had not been present for the attack and were unaware of who had done the deed. Bear was loaded onto a gurney, and an fWo medical official found the Hardcore title and laid it at Bear's feet as he was wheeled away. Something was wrong with the title however... it had a black cross spray painted on the surface of it. On the wall above where Mike Bear was found were the words "It will be mine again"... Nikki the Valley Girl (c) vs. Melissa Smalls Rock music blared over the speakers, and Melissa Smalls walked to the ring looking smug and signaling to her waist. She pointed to a sign that read "Where's Apocolyptica" and shrugged, asking the same question to the cameras. Nikki the Valley Girl's music played, and she came to the ring with the Speed Demon, who paced behind her uncomfortably, looking over his shoulder for signs of the winner of Meltdown, Primetime. Nikki charged Smalls, who sidestepped out of the way easily, sending Nikki into the turnbuckle. Smalls quickly slapped on a swinging neckbreaker, and followed up by a series of three running lariats to keep Nikki on the ground. Smalls climbed to the second turnbuckle for a forearm drop, and attempted a pinfall. Smalls could only get a two count, and she quickly locked on a reverse chinlock to wear down the champion. Nikki went to her knees, a position she appeared to have perfected, turning the hold into a jawbreaker, and laced weak chops across the chest of Smalls. Nikki set up for a piledriver, but Smalls easily countered with a back bodydrop before the move could be executed. Nikki attempted a shoulderblock, but Smalls attacked with high kicks. Nikki grabbed the leg, and Smalls attempts a spinning heel kick which Nikki again dropped to her knees to duck under. Smalls hit a DDT, and the two took it to the mat with quick holds and counter holds applied and escaped. Smalls went for a figure four, but at that moment Crucifix ran out from the backstage area and attacked Smalls in the back with a punch to the head. The ref called for the disqualification victory for Melissa Smalls as Crucifix finished up his attack and walked away, mumbling "That's for Meltdown" as he went. Meanwhile, inside the ring the Speed Demon looked as nervous as ever, but Nikki seemed vibrant... radiant... and had a certain glow about her that she didn't have before... and that women seem to have for nine months... Result: Melissa Smalls by DQ It's Courage The camera takes us backstage, and into the dressing room of Chris West otherwise known as Courage. Courage stands, looking up at Jake, while Jake stands on a dressing table, which seems to have an incredible bend. The cracking and splitting noises coming from the table, due to Jake' obese weight, seem to be the cause for the fear on the face of Jake. "Jake, you heard that masked moron, It's going to be you and the man who puts 'great' in 'greatness', Courage taking on "The government makes me wear a mask, cause I'm so DAMN UGLY" Black Quicksilver and his Barbie doll, STUMP. Now it's bad enough that I have to be seen with you and your fat ass, but to tag with you is even worse. So to not have the image of Courage ruined, by your fat ass running around the ring and the fact that people will realize I'm associated with that ball of fat.. You're going to need to put aside your thoughts about the '2 for 1 Big Mac' meal, and learn some wrestling skills." Jake nods slowly in agreement, though the '2 for 1 Big Mac' meal gets more thought. "Jake, so lets start with my signature move, the top-rope moonsault, otherwise known as 'Courage Required'. I have made this move famous, and every damn person I've performed this move on has walked away saying "I got hit with Courage Required! I'm famous!". Jake, it takes Courage to perform this move, which explains why they label me Mr. Courage, unfortunately even within that structure of blubber, which you call a body, lies not a single inch of Courage nor testosterone, never the less... you Wretch (c) vs. Briana Briana came to the ring slapping hands with the fans, and waving to the crowd. Wretch came to the ring looking concerned for Mike Bear, and made his way to the ring looking dedicated and with the fWo Cruiserweight title draped over his shoulder. The two glared at each other, and locked up in the center of the ring. Wretch took the early edge, but Briana struck with a spinning heel kick that took Wretch to the mat. Briana applied a headlock, but Wretch jerked back and struck her in the head with a backwards head butt. Wretch went to the top rope, and attempted a moonsault, but Briana rolled out of the way and slapped on a cover. Getting only a two count Briana went to the ropes and attempted a drop elbow, but Wretch ducked under and quickly rolled her up for the three count. The two exchanged another glance, but Wretch swung out of the ring in order to get to the hospital where Bear was to check on his teammate. As the show went to a commercial, it was announced that Primetime's first title shot in his "full sweep" that he obtained by winning Meltdown would be against Wretch for the Cruiserweight title, next week on Sunday Slaughter. Result: Wretch by Pinfall A simple contract... Backstage the locker room of the Dark Riders was in chaos, as Death turned over tables and swung chairs into walls. "Interfere in my matches will he? Well give me a contract with Darkness on it... we will finish things and I will ruin him forever... Hotter Than Hell III... Death and Darkness... and only one of us will walk out alive. I'll bring my title Darkness... you bring your soul." Death slammed the door of his locker room after scrawling his name on a contract, sending Matt Loco scrambling backward. He bumped into Darkness as fate would have it, and after being told of Death's challenge, Darkness grinned and laughed an evil laugh, signing his name to the contract without a second thought. Sighing with relief that he had escaped the two encounters more or less unscathed, Matt Loco wandered back to the commissioner's office with the contract in hand... and never saw Eron coming. Eron attacked with a lariat in the hall, dropping Matt Loco into a heap on the floor. Eron snatched up the contract and looked at it. Smiling smugly, he took out a pen and scribbled some new words on the contract, and signed his name to it. Folding the contract up, he stuffed it into his jacket and left the area... Scott Slugger (c) vs. Chris Universal We return from the commercial breaks to a dark arena. Suddenly, the word "Universal" appears on the Slaughter-big-screen, with a dollar sign pyro in place of the letter $. Green and gold fireworks shoot all over the place as "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal walks out carrying his half of the "fake" fWo World Tag Team Titles. Universal holds the title in the air, before licking his lips and walking down to the ring with the title draped over his shoulder. He climbs into the ring and takes off his gold metallic shirt that was supposedly ruined a few weeks ago. Universal awaits the fWo World Champion, Scott Slugger. Slugger walks out to a large negative reaction from the crowd. Slugger rolls into the ring and Universal immediately attacks, stomping at Slugger. He brings him to his feet and whips him into the ropes, before taking him down with a flying forearm. Universal continues the attack, tossing Slugger out onto the floor and following him out there. The momentum swings back and forth throughout the course of the match, but finally goes back to the Sellout. As Slugger jumped off the top rope for a axehandle, at the last second Universal quickly hooked him in place for the "Cha-Ching!" before hitting it. Universal, exasperated, went for the cover and Slugger barely kicked out at two and a half. Universal whipped Slugger into the corner, and followed up diving in at his midsection shoulder first. Slugger moved out of the way, and Universal hit the ringpost. He came out from the corner hunched over, and Slugger quickly hooked him for the 'curveball'. Slugger went for the pin, but Lance Star came running down the ramp and quickly interfered, 'causing a DQ loss for Universal. Star locked Slugger in for the shooting star, and hit it in the center of the ring. He and Universal then stomped at the head of Slugger, before three mysterious masked man made their way in from the crowd and pushed them aside. The masked men begin pummeling Slugger to a pulp, before they spray-painted an "X" on his back. Universal and Star simply shrugged things off and on the way to the back, they reminded everyone that they were the true "real" tag team champions. Back in the ring, the three men dominated Slugger and one even mocked him... taking a batting stance, and pointing at the head of Slugger, before pretending to hit a "home run" after taking a swing and hitting nothing but air. As Slaughter came to a close, the three men left the ring back into the crowd, and Slugger remained in the ring with a look of revenge in his eyes. Result: Scott Slugger by DQ
