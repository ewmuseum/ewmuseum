Wednesday Warfare, 27 October 1999

27 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Backstage at Wednesday Warfare Chris Finn sat on a pile of crates going over the format of the show. All was quiet, although a general sense of unease filled the halls. With only a few days to Mob Rules, the fWo was in chaos and was only now starting to rebuild. The return of David Spencer was certainly on many wrestler's minds, but there were other problems as well. The French Foundation were insisting on armed security to protect them from Scott Slugger and Ice... and the Hollywood Blondes seemed to be playing both sides of the issue. Chris Finn looked down at his format, and turned to James with a sigh. "So what do you say Armstrong? We need a commissioner... the wrestlers are running rampant backstage and aside from the Black Panther's leadership with some of the newer members of the roster... well, a strong presence is needed." Armstrong puffed up his chest. "I smell what you're saying, and I'd be proud to take the job... but... well..." Chris Finn patted James on the back. "Bertha Armstrong won't let you will she?" James Armstrong stifled a tear. "It's unfair I tell you... unfair." Chris Finn looked thoughtful for a moment and then snapped his fingers. "Wait a minute... what about Rowdy Roddy Piper? That's practically all he does now!" James Armstrong shook his head. "Won't work. Scott Slugger and Ice grabbed his kilt and left in naked in downtown Portland as a practical joke. He tried to catch them, but the hip just can't take all that running." Outside the music began, signaling the start of the show. Chris Finn nodded. "Time to go to work... we've still got eight days, so think hard." Arrivals... The Lone Gunmen entered the scene, but something looked different. Machina wore tan Australian hunting gear, and a strange hat. He walked with a swagger, his pale legs showing under his shorts. Rudo was dressed in an outback hunting suit, Billy James wore desert camo gear, and Sid Snow wore a light blue dress. An official walked up to Machina and pointed to the ring. "You're up E..." Machina cut him off with a flat hand. "Right... A'm on 'e trail of some big animals 'ere in the fWo, and A'm gonna get 'em or A'm not 'e 'est Crocodile Hunter in 'e world!" The official pointed to the ring again. "You're scheduled for a match... Ultra Cool 2000... right now actually." Machina ignored him, and paced down a hall, looking for signs of the animals he was hunting. "Now 'ere... A'm gonna find 'e 'retched animal 'an stick my thumb up 'is bum..." The official scratched his head and then spotted Eron, kicking crates out of the way. Stinky followed behind him. The official snapped his fingers. "AH HA!" He ran after Eron, shouting... Ultra Cool 2000 vs. Eron & Stinky Eron came to the ring, flanked on one side by Stinky. Eron looked annoyed, and climbed into the ring and gave the finger to the fans in attendance. Several bottles and pieces of garbage flew through the air, but Eron batted them aside. The music of Kid Cool opened, and Kid Cool came out onto the entrance ramp, pointing with both hands at Ultra Violet, who walked behind him. Ultra Violet and Kid Cool walked to the ring with Violet close behind, clapping and waving as she skipped. Suddenly Violet spotted a D'Beers billboard on the arena wall and stood transfixed, her mouth agape at the giant image of the diamond ring. Inside the ring Eron grappled with Kid Cool, with Eron quickly putting Kid Cool in a headlock. Eron backed into the corner, still holding Kid Cool as he tagged in Stinky. Stinky was a little slow in coming into the ring, and Eron quickly backed away, holding his nose. Kid Cool took big breaths, trying to get air back into his lungs after the headlock. He caught a whiff of Stinky, and tried to run away, but Stinky caught him from behind and executed a quick suplex. Stinky placed Kid Cool's head in his underarm, and Kid Cool's arms quickly went limp. Ultra Violet clapped for Kid Cool, but got distracted by flashing cameras. Stinky attempted to tag in, but Eron turned away repulsed by his smell, Stinky whined to tag in, but Eron suddenly spotted Courage watching from the top of the entrance ramp. Eron quickly sprinted up after him, leaving Stinky in the ring alone. Kid Cool had made it to his corner and tagged in Ultra Violet, and the purple haired avenger hit the ring, a clothespin over his nose. Ultra Violet hit a running lariat, and quickly put Stinky in a piledriver, spiking him to the ground and covering him for the three count. Ultra Violet and Kid Cool waved to the crowd, as Violet continued to look up at the banner, mumbling the words, "A diamond lasts forever..." Result: Ultra Cool 2000 by Pinfall Security ramps up. Backstage John Willis was giving instructions to the large group of security men before him. Big men, strong men, and angry men lined the hall, and John Willis smiled. This was a good thing, for now the halls of the fWo would be secure. No more serious accidents like last week. Willis was going over his final instructions when a larger man still cleared his throat behind the crowd of guards. A gasp went up from the men as they looked up at one of the largest men they had ever seen. The man looked groomed, but a wild look existed in his eye. He crossed his arms, and looked down at John Willis and everyone else in attendance. "Austin the Giant here sir. Show me who to stop." Willis cleared his throat and smiled. This security force could not be stopped... Death vs. Havok Havok's music played, and Havok came out onto the entrance ramp, lights flashing and arms outstretched wide for the crowd. Havok pumped his fist into the air for the fans, and walked to the ring with a confidence that seemed bright. Havok swung into the ring over the top rope, and climbed up onto the top turnbuckle, arms in the air. The lights suddenly went out, and fire exploded from the top of the entrance ramp. A platform hung by cables slowly descended from the ceiling, and standing on it were the Dark Riders. Fallen stood, head down on the ground and hair falling in his eyes. Poison kneeled, clutching Fallen's leg and grunting in a feral manner. Demon looked to the sky, bloody tears slowly streaking his face. Behind them stood David Spencer... Death. The ramp touched the floor and Death stalked to the ring. He swung under and paced toward Havok, looking him in the eye with an angry sneer. Havok smirked and quickly ducked a punch thrown by the big man. Havok came up from behind, hitting a spinning heel kick and nailing Spencer in the back of the head. Spencer went down to one knee, and Havok climbed to the top rope. Havok dove off, but Death caught him by the neck and drove him into the mat with a chokeslam. Death dropped a leg and went for a pin, but suddenly War, Plague, Stone and Death ran to ringside, attacking Fallen, Poison and Demon. Spencer walked to the edge of the ring and began to climb out, going for Collins. Havok struck from behind with a reverse DDT, and before Spencer could kick free got the three count. Spencer rose to his feet in a rage, screaming in rage. Security hit the area as Havok swung out of the ring. Collins as his group of Dark Riders were restrained as Spencer screamed at Collins that revenge would be his... Result: Havok by Pinfall A challenge... Backstage security was involved in another fracas, as the French Foundation struggled to get at Scott Slugger and Ice. The Frenchie pointed to Slugger, yelling, "Coward! Zu hides behind ze title... you are not ze proud champion... bastard!" Slugger spit at the Frenchie, and then smirked. "You want me tonight? One of you little French slobs wants the champ? Fine... I'll take on..." Slugger looked at the Foundation, letting his eyes wander from the Flying Frenchie, to Primetime, to the Boston Strangler. Grinning a sly grin, Scott Slugger shrugged. "I'll take on that other pipsqueak of yours... Wilson. Yeah, I think I'll fight Wilson tonight. Of course, if he's too gutless to show up... then I'm not giving any of you your title shots ever again. What do you think of that?" Security continued to hold back the men as the Frenchie cursed in French and the cameras went back to the ring. Mad Trucker (c) vs. Darkness Darkness waited in the ring as the Mad Trucker started toward the ring. It was announced that the Mad Trucker would be teaming up with the Black Panther, Cactus Jack and US Marshal at Mob Rules. The Mad Trucker raised an arm to the crowd, pumping his fist and shouting, "TOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" The Trucker got a strong reaction from the crowd, but their shouts quickly turned to screams as Rain ran at the Trucker from behind. At his sides were someone who looked something like Austin Davis and another person who looked not quite like Jim Dudley. The three beat down the Mad Trucker, hitting him with chairs and punches to the head. Darkness left the ring and started up the ramp, but the damage had been done. The referee called for the bell, and medics quickly came to the ramp to help the Mad Trucker to the back. Rain raised his fists to the air, and brought them down with a crack of lightening. Rain's laughter filled the air, as the two men at his side joined him. The Mad Trucker held his head, bleeding and obviously in no shape to go to Mob Rules... Result: No Contest Jon on the hunt... Cactus Jon paced in the backstage area, looking for anyone who would admit to being on James Armstrong's team. fWo wrestlers avoided Jon, as he grew increasingly angrier. Finally he spotted a dressing room with a large American flag draped over the door, and rushed to it, kicking open the door. Nobody appeared to be inside, and Jon entered the room confused. Three men quickly snuck into the room behind him, shutting off the lights as they went. Sounds of a scuffle were heard, as well as cursing by Jon. A loud crash was heard next, and shards of glass sprayed out from the room into the hallway. The three men, revealed to be NXS, strode confidently out of the room, Steel holding a large broken mirror in his hands. The three giggled, and Rottdogg said, "Huh huh huh... we kicked the Canadian's ass... huh huh huh..." Black Panther vs. Mike Bear The Black Panther walked to the ring, lights flashing and music blaring. The Panther slid under the ropes, as Mike Bear's music began to play, and the man who might become Tag Team champion walked to the ring. Bear was without Wretch or Fear, and looked determined with his pace. Both men locked up, with Bear quickly working the arm into an armbar. The Panther got to the ropes to break the hold, and struck back with an elbow to the jaw. The Panther ran to the ropes, and came off of them with a running lariat. Bear jumped over the Panther, and struck with a punch to the back. Bear attempted a pinfall, but only got a two count. The Panther powered out and hit a chop to the chest, and then a belly to belly suplex. Not letting go, the Black Panther applied a figure four. Mike Bear struggled, and reached the ropes to break the hold. The crowd jeered as Jake made his way to the ring and climbed clumsily onto the ring apron. The ref yelled at Jake to get off the apron, not seeing Courage running in from the crowd. The Panther hit Mike Bear with a punch and climbed to the top rope, but Courage swung with a metal pipe, striking the Black Panther in the head. Courage laughed, running back up the ramp with Jake and shouting "I'm the real millennium man! Take that Panther!" Mike Bear covered the unconscious Panther, not realizing what had happened. The referee began to count, but then stopped as he looked down at the unconscious Panther. Checking the arm, the referee declared the match a no contest since the Panther was unconscious. Courage continued to laugh from the entrance ramp, as Mike Bear spotted him and began to shout obscenities... Result: No Contest Mystery Partner... Medics loaded the Mad Trucker into an ambulance as Cactus Jack and the US Marshal looked on. The two exchanged glances. "The Trucker isn't going to be in any shape to compete at Mob Rules... what are we going to do?" Cactus Jack looked solemnly at the US Marshal's question, and then at the departing ambulance. "I've got an idea... but I don't know if he will agree..." The two watched the ambulance pull away into the night, the identity of who their forth partner would be a mystery... Wilson arrives... Cameras found Mitch Wilson, entering the arena. Wilson looked annoyed, and was approached by the Flying Frenchie. The Frenchie tossed Wilson's ring gear in Wilson's face, and began to shout. "Get ze ass to ze ring right now! You get ze match with Scott Slugger for ze title... and you had better win!" Mitch Wilson grabbed his ring gear and shoved it back at the Frenchie. "Oh I'll wrestle all right... and when I get the title I'm going to do it for me... not you. Get it?" Wilson paced off to the ring, still in his street clothes. The Frenchie watched him go, and then heard a noise behind him. The Frenchie's already angry demeanor turned even more sour. "You! I'll get you!" The Frenchie raced in the direction of the noise, fists raised... Scott Slugger (c) vs. Mitch Wilson Scott Slugger came to the ring, his World Heavyweight Championship title over one shoulder and a smug expression on his face. He got to the ring and held out his arms, making it known to all of the viewers present that he did not believe that Mitch Wilson would show. Wilson did appear when his name was called however, wearing street clothes and coming out to no fanfare or music. Wilson slid into the ring and at once Scott Slugger began to kick down on him. Slugger picked Wilson up by the hair and attacked with a series of palm blows, bruising the face of the challenger and keeping him dazed. Slugger went to the ropes for a clothesline, but Wilson ducked under and grabbed Slugger from behind, executing a quick and effective suplex. In control now, Wilson measured Scott Slugger, and then delivered a running powerbomb out of the corner. Wilson removed his shirt and tossed it out of the ring, and then went back to work on Slugger, measuring with calculated attacks to the knee. Slugger was able to roll to the outside, but Wilson paced after him. Wilson picked up a chair, but Slugger kicked out at Wilson, connecting with the chair and blooding Wilson's face behind the steel. Slugger rolled Wilson back into the ring, but Wilson again took the advantage with a chinlock, which was turned into a full head lock. Suddenly cameras went backstage, where a brawl had broken out and was in the midst of being restrained by security. Ice and the Hollywood Blondes were attacking the other members of the French Foundation, and the six men spilled out onto the entrance ramp. As the brawl neared the ring the referee's attention was diverted, and a quick thinking Ice slid into the ring and attacked Wilson with a shot to the head from behind. Ice and Slugger beat down Mitch inside the ring as the crowd booed and the timekeeper desperately rang the bell. The brawl now wandered to ringside, and the Flying Frenchie went after Scott Slugger. As the two brawled Mitch Wilson picked up the chair he had earlier and entered the ring, aiming at Scott Slugger's head. The Frenchie hit a blow to the groin at just that moment, and Slugger doubled over... as Wilson swung the chair. Steel connected with the Flying Frenchie's head, and Wilson dropped the chair in surprise. Slugger took the advantage with a punch to the midsection of Wilson, and quickly applied the Curveball on the challenger. The referee, who had been outside of the ring trying to stop the Blondes, Primetime, and the Strangler from further fighting was pushed back into the ring where the count was made. Slugger and Ice left the ring and walked to the back with the Hollywood Blondes, Slugger gloating over his victory. Inside the ring a recovered Frenchie screamed at Wilson... Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall The Invaders Outside the arena Mountain, Angus, Quarymaster and a shaking crate stood arguing with the security officials, demanding that they be let into the arena. Finally Mountain had enough, and began punching security men, tossing them into the air and stomping into the arena. They came to an immediate halt as they found their passage blocked by Jackhammer, holding a heavy steel pipe. To his side was the Black Quicksilver and Briana, and the three of them held their ground as Mountain and Angus leered at the fWo wrestlers. "Ach aye! Don't tell me that 'er fWo wrestlers grew a spine!" Mountain and Angus rushed the three fWo superstars, as Quarymaster flung the shaking crate in Jackhammer's direction. It burst open, and a strange looking green man picked himself up from the shards of boards, eyes wide and screaming. More security hit the scene as yet another brawl was attempted to be contained... Los Latin vs. Mike Heftel "My leg isn't too good after that last show... I just don't think I'm going to be able to wrestle Geoff." Mike Heftel tapped his foot gingerly, where a light cast was applied. Geoff looked at it wish some dismay, and nodded. "Don't worry Mike... nobody would wrestle you in this condition... I mean, what wrestler would be so low as to insist a match with a man with a cast?" Heftel started to respond, but Los Latin cut him off, grabbing his head and hauling him to the ring. Heftel hopped on one foot, giving little yelps of pain as Los Latin drug him to the ring and rolled him inside. Los Latin saluted the crowd, who returned his salute with loud boos. Latin gave them the finger instead and picked up the microphone. "Stupid Americans! Tonight I take out another one of your elite kinds inside this ring, for that is the glory of Mexico, and the glory of Los Latin, el wrestler extraordinaire!" Los Latin rolled into the ring, unsure of how to begin with Heftel, who was sitting on the mat to avoid injuring his leg. Latin kicked at the leg, striking on the wounded area first, and then moving up to the head. He continued to kick, and then broke into a little Spanish dance, every now and then kicking at Heftel in the center of the ring. Finally Los Latin applied an ankle lock submission hold, and the referee called for the submission victory. Los Latin stood and bowed once more, but a wayward flying beer bottle struck him in the head, causing him to fall from the ring in a heap. Result: Los Latin by Pinfall Death (c) vs. Primetime After the commercial Wednesday Warfare returned to the ring for the final match of the night. Back in the ring Primetime stood, ready for his Hardcore championship match. The lights dimmed, and the letter "4" blazed in fire from the top of the ramp. Stepping out from the flames was Death, flanked by Stone and War, with Plague grinning and laughing insanly in front of him. Plague wore the burnt remains of Stevie Robert's "New Wave" jacket, and picked at the cloth, putting the burnt remains in his mouth. Death stalked to the ring, pulling a steel baseball bat from his trenchcoat as he walked. Not bothering to take off the coat, he slid into the ring. Primetime attacked at once, but Death lashed out with the bat catching Primetime in the legs. Primetime went down, and Death continued to beat on the French Foundation member on the mat. Primetime reached into a pocket of his tights and grabbed a small bag, tossing what appeared to be salt in Death's eyes. Death staggered back, and it was Primetime who grabbed the bat and swung at Death's head. Death stumbled back, and a stiff lariat sent Death out of the ring and down to the floor. Primetime went to the other side of the ring, meaning to come off the ropes and leap to the outside. Stone caught his leg however, and Primetime fell on his face, the bat striking him as he fell. Death grabbed a steel chair from outside and tossed it into the ring, screaming. He bashed it over Primetime's head, and continued to swing, beating Primetime over the skull again and again until Primetime's head began to badly bleed. Death tossed the chair onto Primetime's prone body, and went to the second rope. Death jumped off of it, hitting the legdrop on the chair and Primetime beneath. Death covered Primetime, and got the three count. But it was not over there... Result: Death by Pinfall Game hunting The crowd screamed as the other Dark Riders entered the ring, and all four began to stomp and punch at Primetime, who desperately attempted to cover his head. The Flying Frenchie, Boston Strangler, and Mitch Wilson all ran to the ring to save their teammate, but the Dark Riders in the ring had the advantage and the weapons and the French Foundation was fended off. Suddenly the lights crashed into darkness, and Spencer's team of Dark Riders came to the ring. All eight members of the Dark Riders began to wage war inside the ring, batting aside anyone who attempted to interfere in their fight. The battle broke free from the ring, and with a mighty crash War drove Demon through the announcing table, laying waste to Finn and Armstrong along with it. Security hit the scene, but Plague had lit a chair on fire, and waved it into the face of security officials, warding them off. Several fWo wrestlers hit the scene, but could not get close to the warring Dark Riders. At the base of the entrance ramp David Spencer and Scott Collins traded punches as fans screamed in panic. Order had broken down and a riot was starting to ensue when suddenly the lights dimmed for the third and final time of the night. The WarfareScreen was dark for a moment, and then "Come With Me" blared over the arena speakers as a giant circled "L" filled the screen. Machina, Rudo, Billy James and Sid Snow stepped onto the top of the ramp, still dressed in their earlier hunting attire. Machina raised a microphone to his lips and yelled to the men below. "Get out of that 'er ring... stop 'yer fightin' 'ere... and you will do it... RIGHT... NOW!" The two factions of Dark Riders stopped fighting for a moment to look up at the four men at the top of the ramp. Suddenly they broke out in laughter and Collins pointed up at Machina. "And how are you going to stop us little man?" The two teams of Dark Riders grinned evil grins up at the Lone Gunmen, but Machina seemed unfazed by this. The Dark Riders went back to their battle, trading punches and kicks and occasionally lashing out at a fan that got too close. Machina again raised the microphone. "This is your last chance you 'ere varmits... get out of 'ere area and go on home, or suffer the consequences." Again there was laughter from the Dark Riders, and now Collins and Spencer began to advance on Machina. Machina again seemed unfazed, and he spoke to his Lone Gunmen. "Okay then... we do it the 'ard way. Ready?" The Lone Gunmen nodded, and from bags behind them they pulled what appeared to be high powered rifles. This stopped Spencer and Collins, who now looked at the four armed men in front of them with some alarm. Aiming at Spencer, Machina barked, "Trank 'em!" The Lone Gunmen fired, sending tranquilizer darts into the Dark Riders below. More panic broke out along ringside as the Lone Gunmen reloaded and began to fire again. Rudo aimed for Collins, Machina pumped another dart into Spencer, and Billy James fired at a now advancing War. Sid Snow looked at his gun. "Wait a minute... this puts people asleep... makes them unconscious, right?" Machina nodded as he continued to fire, hitting Fallen and Plague below. An idea crossed over Sid's face, and he quickly ran backstage shouting, "Slugger? Where are you Slugger?" In the main ringside arena the fans continued to scream as the Lone Gunmen used their tranquilizer rifles to subdue the Dark Riders... and Warfare went off the air with a final reminder that Mob Rules is just a few short hours away...
