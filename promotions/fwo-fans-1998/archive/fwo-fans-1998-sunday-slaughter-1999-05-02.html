Sunday Slaughter, 2 May 1999

2 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The fWo hailed live from Tacoma, Washington. Shots were shown of fWo stars in the Puget Sound area earlier in the day, including Scott Slugger visiting a hospital in Vancouver, Cactus Jon signing autographs at a local Mountie station in Canada, Briana shopping at Pioneer Square, Chris Kidman learning to toss fish at Pike Place Market, Matthew Zhuk attending a conference on clearing Tacoma of it's scent problems, and Machina and the Lone Gunmen on top of the Space Needle spitting on other tourists. Shots were shown of the beautiful Puget Sound, the beautiful Cascades, the beautiful Snoqalmie Falls, and Tacoma. Shots were shown of the outside of Tacoma Dome, where some homeless people were being clubbed away by Tacoma police, and limos arrived... carrying the Dark Riders. Inside the arena Chris Finn and James Armstrong were on hand for commentary, but were approched by Death, War, Plague, Stone, and Scott Taylor. Death grabbed Chris Finn, and drug him from ringside by the hair, as Scott Taylor took his chair next to a nervous James Armstrong. Scott Taylor told Armstrong and the fWo crowd that things would be different from now on... evilly different. Match Announcement fWo commissioner Travis Beaven made his way to the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd. Beaven looked considerably tired & depressed, which could be attributed to the return to the fWo of the Dark Riders, led by the former Scotty Cool. Beaven climbs into the ring and asks for a microphone, before making his big announcement: "The next big pay-per-view brought to you by the fWo is on May 28, and it is titled "Extreme Measures". That night, not only will you see the fWo World Champion Death meet Ultra-Violet, but I have just signed the following match. This match may be one of the biggest tag team matches of the year for the fWo, and will most assuredly be an explosive match. In this match, you will see 4 champions. 3 former champions, and 1 current champion. Former fWo World Champion Scott Slugger & former fWo World Champion Ice will team up against former fWo World Champion "Baad Ass" Austin Davis and current fWo Internet Champion "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal"... at that moment, we hear "Trust me" several times throughout the arena, before Davis makes his presence felt. Davis comes out to a mixed reaction from the crowd, and he climbs into the ring. He stares at Beaven with a cold look, before grabbing the microphone from him. Davis says that if Beaven wants to live until Extreme Measures, he better put him in a different match. He doesn't care if it's a diaper match, a match in the back of a moving truck, or a match in a live graveyard. Davis says that Beaven could make him the special guest referee for Death/UV at Extreme Measures for all he cares, but he is not teaming up with that overrated piece of crap Universal. Davis demands that Beaven takes him out of the match, before putting the microphone back into his chest and shoving him towards the mat. Beaven falls back, and as Davis gets ready to leave, we hear "Do you buy what the Sellout is selling?" and out walks Universal with Megan Dawn. The crowd immediately reacts poorly to his presence, throwing cups of beverages and other assorted items in his direction. Universal has a microphone in hand, and begins to speak. Universal says that if Beaven teams him with Davis, he will never defend the Internet Title again. Beaven reminds him that he can strip him of the title if he doesn't defend it within 30 days. Universal says that Beaven didn't read the rulebook closely enough, because you can only strip a champion of his title if he doesn't defend the title within 30 days because of an injury. Davis says something to Beaven, so Beaven hands him the microphone. Davis says that he'll "kindly give a career ending injury" to Universal by shoving that belt up Universal' ass and pull it out of Dawn' mouth not a second later. Universal looks shocked, and then Slugger & Ice appear on the TravisTron; saying that they don't blame Davis for not wanting to team with a sellout like Universal, but if Davis was a real man he'd play with what cards he was dealt with. Davis sneers before accepting the match, although begrudgingly. Universal still appeared to be speechless from Davis' comments a few seconds ago, as Slaughter went to a commercial break with the Davis/Universal vs. Slugger/Ice match apparently official. French Foundation vs. Ground Zero Wolf Fang and Jackhammer made their way to the ring amist the chaos that was ensuing in the arena. Scott Taylor called the introductions, and began to glare at James Armstrong as he made his usual remarks. James Armstrong fell silent, leaving the commentary to Scott Taylor alone. Primetime and Mitch Wilson came to the ring carrying the French Flag, with Primetime hitting the ring early and going directly after Wolf Fang. The two traded punches for a short time, until the ref was able to seperate the two men and turn the match into a regular tag team match. As Jackhammer stomped support on the outside, Mitch Wilson and Wolf Fang quickly traded holds. With both men getting pinfall attempts Wolf Fang tagged in Jackhammer. Wilson attempted several holds, and Wilson and Primetime even attempted to double team the big man, but to little use. As Jackhammer tossed Primetime from the ring, the Lone Gunmen exited on the top of the entrance ramp and ran to ringside. Sid and the Shocker quickly laid the boots to Primetime as Billy James held Mitch Wilson down and Rudo attacked with kicks. The French Foundation was counted out as Machina came to ringside to laugh at the French team. From behind came the Flying Frenchie, weilding an offical French club, and he delivered a hard blow to the back of Machina's skull. As the Frenchie cursed at the Lone Gunmen in French, the Lone Gunmen carried Machina out through the crowd, head and arms dragging. Result: Ground Zero by Count Out Briana (c) vs. Chris Kidman Jr. Briana came to the ring slapping hands with the fans, and waving to the crowd. Kidman came to the ring with much the same attitude. Kidman and Briana shook hands, and locked up in the center of the ring. Kidman took the early edge, but Briana struck with a spinning heel kick that took Kidman to the mat. Briana applied a headlock, but Kidman jerked back and struck her in the head with a backwards head butt. Kidman went to the top rope, and attempted a moonsault, but Briana rolled out of the way and slapped on a cover. Getting only a two count Briana went to the ropes and attempted a drop elbow, but Kidman ducked under and rolled her up for the three count. Kidman and Briana parted friendly enough, but as Briana walked up the entrance ramp she was met by Stone, who delivered a hard blow with the fWo Womens title into the face of Briana. Laughing, Stone paced back to the backstage area. Result: Chris Kidman Jr. by Pinfall Black Panther (c) vs. Flying Frenchie The Flying Frenchie came to the ring scowling, and stalked around the ring while waiting for the Black Panther to arrive. As the Black Panther headed to the ring shots were shown backstage, where the commissioner was shown telling Machina that he was not to interfere in the match. Machina promised that he would not interfere, and walked away from the commissioner mumbling under his breath. Inside the ring the Flying Frenchie hit a baseball slide on the Panther who was climbing into the ring. The Panther recovered and grabbed the Frenchie by the leg, pulling him to the outside. The Panther sent the Frenchie into the steel steps, and then rolled him into the ring. The Panther quickly climbed to the top of the ring ropes, and executed a perfect frog splash onto the Frenchie. The Panther was only able to get a two count, and he went to the top rope again. The Frenchie got to his feet before the Panther got to the top, and suplexed the Panther into the ring. The Frenchie then executed several quick suplexes in the center of the ring, and climbed to the top rope for a 450 splash. As he stood on the top rope, Machina ran down the entrance ramp wearing a kimono. Machina grabbed Frenchie's leg, causing him to drop into the turnbuckle. The Panther, not seeing what caused the Frenchie to fall, rolled him up for the pinfall victory. Machina meanwhile told the booing crowd that he was not Machina tonight... but the Jumping Japanesey. The crowd responded by spitting on the masked man, who then began to run as the Frenchie gave chase up the entrance ramp. Result: Black Panther by Pinfall Cactus Jon vs. War Cactus Jon walked out onto the entrance ramp to a huge ovation to the crowd, and Cactus Jon seemed pleased by the reception, after so many months of being hated inside the fWo. Jon saluted the crowd, and was attacked from behind by War, Plague, Stone and Death. The Dark Riders continued to stomp away on Cactus Jon, finally wrapping the leather strap around the neck of Jon and attempted to kick him off the entrance ramp. Before they could do so Scott Slugger and Ice ran out onto the entrance ramp with basebal bats, sending War, Stone and Plague running. Death stood his ground and laughed, signalling Ice and Slugger to "bring it on". fWo security hit the scene and seperated the two, pushing Death away from the fWo wrestlers, to the backstage area. As the Dark Riders were ushered to the backstage area, a young woman approached a laughing and screaming Plague and handed him a single silver rose. As the young woman faded back into the crowd Plague studied the rose, looking quiet and somber for a rare moment... before crushing the flower in his hand and stalking away. Result: No Contest Chris Universal (c) vs. Machina Machina came out onto the entrance ramp, dressed in a tuxedo with small green dollar signs on it. Over the PA system "Money, Money, Money... Money" was played as Machina slowly walked to the ring holding large fistfulls of cash. Machina got into the ring and grabbed the microphone, telling the crowd that they were all a bunch of stupid American assholes who would do anything for money. To prove it, he told the crowd that he would give anyone in the arena $500 to come into the ring and do a bad parody of Tiger Ali Sigh, an unknown wrestler from an inferior promotion. Many members of the crowd jumped at this chance, but suddenly the lights dimmed and a green glow filled the arena. A strange masked man walked down the entrance ramp dressed in a red robe and carrying an Arabian flag. Stepping into the ring, the masked man did a perfect impression of Tiger, and Machina nodded his approval. Machina began to hand over the money, but then remembered that he was a bad guy, and shoved the masked man out of the ring. The masked man waved at someone in the crowd, and as Machina continued to insult the masked man, the gigantic Total Eclipse came out of the crowd and climbed into the ring, grabbing Machina and delivering a belly-to-belly suplex in the center of the ring. The masked man ripped off his mask to reveal the Chameleon, tossed the mask on Machina's prone body, and signalled the Total Eclipse to come out of the ring. Machina jumped on the back of the Eclipse, and rode him to the backstage area, waving to the cheering crowd. Chris Universal came to the ring then, despite Machina being unconcious. Universal put a single pinky on Machina, and "pinned" him for the three count. Universal then laughed at Machina, and spit on him before leaving the ring with his title. The show was about to go off the air, but cameras quickly went to the parking lot of the arena, where the Flying Frenchie had obtained a steam roller, and was using it to flatten the Lone Gunmen limosine, waving the French flag as he did so.... Result: Chris Universal by Pinfall
