Tell me..." He lowered his head. When he raised it, the pain of the last four months, the pain of the last three years, the pain of his entire lifetime was written upon his face. He looked ancient, and very, very sad. "Are you her? You sound like her... but are you her? Are you Stephanie?" Silence. "I just... I just want a chance to apologize. And... a chance to say goodbye. If I could just do that... maybe I could finally have some peace. That's all I want." Silence. It was broken as Donaven Winters stirred again on the ground below him. "Tell me... please. P-please..." His voice broke on the last word. Still silence... and then, the braying laughter of the other voice. Slugger lowered his head again. When he lifted it up, the pain had been pushed underneath the surface. Now his face was a mask of rage. "Fine, then. Go to hell. Maybe I'll see you there!" He raised the knife above his head... And brought it down.
