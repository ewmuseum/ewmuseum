Sunday Slaughter, 13 August 2000

13 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday - The Open Road Saturday evening's are quite hectic in Las Vegas. Fortunately for Chris Finn & James Armstrong, as they tore down the freeway at 100pm in their 1998 Ford Explorer, they hadn't yet reached Vegas. But they were close. The sun was nearly setting in the foreground. As they neared the city of sin, a "Welcome To Las Vegas" sign was seen off to the side. Barely. The sound of a car whizzing by the sign is heard. "Hells yeah!" said Armstrong, while Finn looked a little frightened. "Er... I don't know about this, James. What if something bad happens? What if we get pulled over? How would that look? Huh? 'Owners of the fans Wrestling organization arrested for violating the speed limit four times over. Film at eleven'. And to top it all off, you were drinking earlier!" Armstrong carefully tried to hide the small bottle of Vodka that Finn hadn't noticed until now. "You're drinking NOW? This is ridiculous, James. Pull over - I'm driving." "Finn, you never had a set of balls. How about growing one now? Here... I've got enough swimmers in one of mine. You can borrow the other!" Finn rolled his eyes and prepared to smack Armstrong, when suddenly Armstrong lost control of the vehicle and spilled his vodka. "Oooooooohhhhhhhhhhhh... shiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiittttttttttttttttttttttttttt!" Sunday - The Day After The 1998 Ford Explorer pulled into the parking area of the MGM Grand looking worn and beaten up. It wasn't totalled... but it wasn't in it's pristine condition that it was twenty four hours ago. Nevertheless, the same two men that damn near killed themselves last night got out of it. This time, Finn was the driver, while Armstrong rode shotgun. "You're so paranoid. I've been driving for a long time, Finn. And who cares if all my accidents have came in the last six months? I'm careful... it was somebody else's fault." Finn was still angry, as he had a right to be. "Yeah, it was MY fault, James. It was my fault you nearly killed us. It was my fault you were drinking Vodka while driving. It was my fault..." Finn lifted his head up from staring at the ground to see James already walk into the casino. "Where do you think you're going?" James shrugged. "Maybe go play blackjack... check out some hookers... play blackjack with the hookers... ah, on second thought, screw the blackjack." Finn rolled his eyes, as Armstrong looked back. "Nah... I'm going to stay away from the hookers. Even if it is... somewhat legal here... I'm going to go play blackjack. Think you can handle Slaughter on your own?" Without hesitation, Finn responded. "I always do." Armstrong chuckled. "Right, Finnster, right." Armstrong walked into the casino, while Finn entered the VIP door. As he did, he was checked for his backstage pass. "Okay, you can go, Mr. Finn. Sorry about that. Some backstage passes have leaked out and we don't want the innappropiate people back here." Finn nodded, and thanked the worker for his extra help as he walked backstage. Almost in a whisper, Finn said to himself... "I don't feel good... I don't feel good about tonight at all." "The Legend" Jon Spratt vs. The Black Panther Pyrotechnics explode inside the MGM Grand, and Rage Against The Machine's "Bombtrack" hits the system, which brings out "The Legend" Jon Spratt. A mixed reaction carries him down to the ring; Spratt is obviously oblivious to what is going on around him, and he slides into the ring. As he warms up, "My Hero" hits the arena's speakers, and out walks The Black Panther. He walks confidently to the ring, feeling that Spratt still has some lingering ring-rust. He climbs onto the apron, and then over the top rope, which is an attribute to his massive size. As soon as he rights himself in the ring, the bell hits, and the match is started. Spratt and Panther lock up, with Panther getting an edge. He brings Spratt into the ropes, and whips him across the ring. Panther gets ready for a shoulder block, but Spratt, upon coming off the ropes, ducks it. Panther turns around, and Spratt nails him with a dropkick. Panther gets up quickly, and locks up quickly with Spratt. He drives Spratt to the turnbuckle, and the ref goes to break them up. However, Panther gets the dreaded Moe Stooge Eye-poke onto Spratt, which brings boos from the crowd. Panther follows this up with a rake of the eyes, and then a low blow. As Spratt bows down to deal with the blow to the junk, Panther grabs his head and DDT's him to the mat. A cover garners two, mostly from surprise. Panther gets up and picks up Spratt, before nailing him with another low blow. This time, he uses this to hook both arms of Spratt, and nailing him with a double-underhook suplex. Another cover, another two count. Panther, looking a bit upset at this turn of events, shakes his head as he rises to his feet. He picks up Spratt again, and sends him to the ropes. As Spratt hits the ropes, Panther takes off in a direction that is perpendicular to him. Spratt crosses the ring, ahead of Panther, and both men are gaining steam. Panther quickens his pace, and when Spratt comes across the ring again, Panther leaps and nails him with a side-headlock bulldog. He quickly covers the groggy Spratt, but does not hook the leg. At two and a half, Spratt kicks out. The Panther can't believe it. Didn't hook the leg. How long has he been around? He picks up Spratt and lines him up for what appears to be a spinning heel kick. He starts the manuever, but Spratt ducks, and Panther, not being used to spinning heel kicks, overextends himself into a split, another manuever he is not used to doing. Spratt sees this and immediately runs toward the ropes. He bounces off and hits a low-level dropkick to the head of Panther, which puts him in an even more precarious position. Panther winces in pain, and even lets out a little growl. Spratt helps Panther up, but only to inflict more pain. He whips Panther into the ropes; this is not an easy task for the Panther, who, after suffering numerous strains to muscles he thought only existed in certain situations OUTSIDE the ring, runs like he just got finished from riding a horse for the first time. Spratt uses this extra time to get ready to set up for his next manuever. He leans forward, as if to nail the Panther in the midsection again. Panther, knowing that another shot there might force him to submit, quickly bows down to counter. Spratt, being the wily veteran that he is, figures this was coming. He quickly re-directs himself into what he intended to do in the first place. He grabs the head of TBP, and lives him up into a vertical suplex. From there, he twists TBP into a neckbreaker. The crowd pops at this, and Spratt, still oblivious to the fans' reaction, gets ready to finish the Panther. He picks him up, and hooks him in for the Legend Bomb. He hits the Ganso bomb, or hangman's DDT, and goes for a cover. The ref counts the three, and Jon Spratt continues to live up to the name for which he has given himself. Meanwhile, the Black Panther starts to rise, obviously frustrated by his latest misfortune. As he rises, he looks upward in the direction of the SlaughterScreen. A shot of Ric Chronos shown, and it doesn't please TBP whatsoever. It's not that Ric is being show that's the problem; rather, it's the fact that he's laughing at the Panther that does it. As TBP looks in disgust at this, Slaughter heads to a break. Result: Spratt by pinfall Wretch-A-Mania Is Running Wild Right Into... Slaughter rolls on backstage, as the lovable trio of The Wretchster & company make their entrance into the MGM Grand. "EYE WANNA GO GAMEBLEING!" shouted Abusah, while Wretchster just nodded incessantly. "Well, have you gotten paid lately, brah? Because despite the fact that I'm a wealthy man, dude... I ain't got time tonight, brah. I got things to do... dude." Abusah tried to talk him into it, but Wretch wasn't budging. He turned his attention to try and tell Abusah to shut up about it, and that's when he walked into something that fell - literally - like a brick wall. Erik Kelly. Wretchster nearly fell to the floor, but Kelly helped him up to his feet. "Sorry about that. Anyways - Finn re-booked our tag match for tonight... and let me tell you something - it's going to happen, whether you got a black eye or not, alright?" Wretchster nodded. "Sure, dude... brah... brother, man. Anything you say, dude." Kelly looked at him with a stern expression on his face. "Be serious for once... you've got a big match with Mitch on Thursday, and you can't afford to take this thing lightly and get yourself injured." Wretchster - always ready to come up with a retort - "No way, brother. I say my vitamins. I eat my prayers. Wretch-A-Mania is going to run wild tonight, and it's going to run wild on Thursday, brah. WHATCHA GONNA DO?" Kelly sighed sarcastically, before telling Wretch just to "be ready" later tonight. Taking A Gamble, Part One Slaughter doesn't head backstage, but inside the MGM Grand Garden Hotel & Casino. We see James Armstrong making a confident walk up to a blackjack table, where he begins to do some gambling. First taking it easy, and only playing a hand or two when convenient, he mixes it in with having a drink now and then. "Blackjack on a Sunday night in Vegas. What more could you want?" he asks the dealer. The dealer smiles. "Perhaps a damn fine Dr. Pepper." Armstrong shrugs. "Not big on that... and I guess I should stay away from the Vodka after what happened yesterday." The dealer finally notices who he's talking to. "Oh, right. They did something about that on the news this morning. Yeah, yeah... you're like a top guy in one of those wrestling organizations, huh? James Armstrong, right?" Armstrong nodded. "No offense, Mr. Armstrong... but wrestling doesn't just cut it for me anymore. The sports entertainment crap they air today is downright disgusting. I'm a fan of the olden days, though. Bruno Sammartino... Bobo Brazil... Haystacks Calhoun... Roddy Piper... Ric Flair... Uncle Elmer... the list goes on and on. But today's... what you guys do today isn't worth watching." Armstrong shrugged. "No worries... I'm not a fan of what goes on today sometimes either... hell, get rid of the loocheydores & show more T&A, and I'm a happy camper... at any rate... our organization is fine. We don't like to brag, but god damnit, were good." The dealer looked around in deep thought. "You want in on this hand?" Armstrong nodded. As other people walked away from the table, Armstrong became more comfortable in his surroundings. "Hit me." The dealer did. Armstrong won. This routine continued for awhile, and unbelievably some time later, he had won thirteen straight hands and had amassed a sum of over five thousand dollars. But with money comes notice... and Armstrong didn't know it, but he was being watched... The Watch ... by two men who were sharply dressed for the night. They were at another table doing some gambling of their own. But they couldn't help but notice the amount of luck a bald headed gentleman was having tonight. "Who is that guy?" one of the men said to the other. "I don't know, but he's on a roll." The other man nodded. "Perhaps we should go over there and put a halt to his luck?" His colleague shook his head in a negative manner. "Nah, let's watch for a bit more. He might recognize us." The two men grinned, but the recognizability of the two men wouldn't be obvious to the audience. Perhaps to Armstrong for other reasons... but not to the public... "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly & The Wretchster vs. Bad Attitude Result: Kelly & Wretchster by pinfall Taking A Gamble, Part Two We went inside the lovely casino once again where Armstrong continued to lay it all on the line. At this point, he had amassed a huge sum. Nearly fifty thousand dollars. But Armstrong didn't know when to stop... he couldn't lose. "I'm on a roll!" he said emphatically as other people gathered around to watch. The two men seen earlier had stop their own previous attempts to win money - and now watched Armstrong from several hundred feet away. Practically nobody noticed them... but they had little reason to. Afterall, the goofy bald looking guy was on a freakin' roll, and racking up the amount of chips by the pound at this point. As the crowd continued to grow and grow - and as did James Armstrong pile of chips, as well as his confidence - the two men finally decided to approach James. The cameras headed backstage quickly, as the two men began to walk over. Paybacks Are Hell Backstage, Rain entered the arena with a traveling bag in hand. He ignored fans, backstage workers, and wrestlers alike as he walked throughout the MGM Grand. Last week, he defeated the Boston Strangler in a non-title match, but not without controversy. He attacked the World Champion backstage - and that likely had something to do with the Strangler' condition, as obviously he wasn't at a healthy one hundred percent. Regardless, it's seven days later, and now Slaughter is in another city. The city of luck. Tonight, Rain has another match with the Boston Strangler, and this time should he win, he will get a World Title shot against the champion - no matter whom it is - next Sunday. Rain continued to ignore everything, and walked straight to the back. Even the hilarious antics of Abusah & Scot Hartt insulting him and making lightning noises wasn't enough to throw Rain off his course as he walked by them. But something else was. The Boston Strangler. The World Champion was hidden from view as Rain walked by. The Strangler became visable, but not to Rain... Strangler got his attention by smacking the pavement with a steel chair. Rain heard the noise, and turned around. Whack. Steel chair to the arm. Rain held his arm in pain, before Strangler smacked him with the chair again. Thud. Rain collapsed, as this time the shot went right to his head. The Strangler stared over him and told Rain that paybacks were a bitch; before other wrestlers arrived on the scene to pull him away from injuring Rain any further. Slaughter rolled on. The Meeting Again inside the casino, cameras shifted to the approaching two men. Given that they were the two best dressed men in the house, all eyes shifted to them as well. Armstrong didn't. He had things to do, dammit, like win more money, so he could celebrate with all the hookers he wanted later on this evening. But perhaps he should've noticed them. Either way, he was about to meet them... regardless if he should know who they are, or not. The two men didn't necessarily introduce themselves, as it wasn't a key point to get across at the moment. They wanted to see if they could break Armstrong' streak of luck, and looked wealthy enough to play into the hours of Monday morning. They finally reached the crowd of watchers that had gathered over the last twenty five minutes or so, and as Armstrong took a break in between hands, he ordered himself a Vodka - on the rocks. Moving their way through, and as the crowd murmured as they began to think these two men were very important, they came face to face with the goofy lookin' James Armstrong himself. "Hey, sir." said one of the men, instigating the conversation. James said hello, as he wasn't much for talk at the moment. But the two men pulled up a chair, and asked James if he wanted to have a real challenge. "Enough of playing the house - play me." Armstrong rolled his eyes. "No, do something first. Impress me." The man did, and as time went on, he won a large sum of money himself - though not nearly as large as James' pile. Finally impressed, James replied with two words. "Holy shit." Now impressed, James took him up on his offer... for better, or for worse. Ric Chronos & Fallen vs. Damnation Slaughter heads back to the ring for a four way tag team battle, the winner of which earns a shot at the World Tag Team Championship of the California Kings at the Ryan Moore Memorial Show. "Through The Never" by Metallica begins to play, and the fans react with boos as the first team to enter the match, Chaos makes their way to the ring, accompanied by a constantly-filming Stone. They head to their corner. "Christian Says" then starts up, and the boos turn to cheers as the popular duo of Ric Chronos and Fallen head to the ring, looking focused on getting a shot at regaining the titles they lost nearly two months ago. "From This Day" starts to play, and the cheers get even louder as Damnation and Lamont stomp to the ring, also looking intent on earning their shot at regaining the straps they lost at the fWo's most recent pay-per-view. The roar of the crowd grows even louder as "Connection" starts playing, and the duo of Scoty Hartt and the Abusah head to the ring, Abusah still ecstatic over the SWOORV! he pulled off earlier in the evening. They enter the ring and take their corner, and it is decided that Hellrazor and Fallen will start the match. Hellrazor quickly charges at Fallen, who ducks his clothesline and catches him coming off the ropes with a backdrop. Fallen quickly brings Hellrazor to his feet before snapmaring him into a sitting position. He then runs into the ropes and dropkicks Hellrazor directly in the face. Fallen quickly drops down and makes the cover, but Hellrazor kicks out at two. Fallen brings him up again and takes him over with a snap suplex, before tagging out to Plague. Plague quickly mounts the top rope and flies off with a frog splash, but Hellrazor has had enough time to recover and Plague splats into the mat instead. Hellrazor quickly rolls into his corner and tags in Fear, who blasts Plague with a forearm as he gets up. Fear lifts Plague up into a full nelson, before dropping down into a spine-shattering sitdown full nelson slam. Fear makes the cover, but War quick hits the ring and breaks up the count just before three. War brings Fear up and begins pounding on him, which has the effect of drawing Hellrazor back in as well. Before they know it, the foursome are brawling wildly towards the ropes, before tumbling out. They are quickly back up and at each other, Stone watching the whole thing through her camera lens at a distance. The brawl continues, and Damnation and Chaos are quickly the victims of a double-countout, eliminating both teams. They continue to brawl to the back as Scot Hartt and Ric Chronos hit the ring. They circle and lock up, and Chronos quickly takes Hartt over with a suplex. Chronos brings Hartt to his feet and whips him into the corner, before following him in with a clothesline and a series of chops. Chronos brings Hartt out and tags out to Fallen, who positions Hartt before dropping him with a huge tiger driver. Fallen makes the cover, but makes the mistake of neglecting to hook the leg and it costs him as Hartt narrowly escapes after the two count is tolled. Fallen brings Hartt to his feet and sets him for a snap suplex, but Hartt blocks the first one before rolling back into a small package. Fallen kicks out at two, and nails Hartt with a clothesline as he gets up before tagging out to Chronos. Chronos hits the ring and he and Fallen execute a double whip on Hartt, before catching him with a big double flapjack. Fallen then runs into the ropes and Chronos presses him up, before dropping him on Hartt. Chronos drops on makes the cover, but Abusah manages to break it up at two. Chronos is unconcerned as Abusah makes his way back to his corner, and brings Hartt up once more before planting him with a hard DDT that drives the head of Hartt into the canvas. Chronos covers and hooks one of Hartt's legs, but to the delight of the crowd Hartt somehow manages to get a shoulder up. Chronos thinks it's time to finish the match off, and brings Hartt up before hooking him up for the figure-four DDT. Chronos takes a moment to set Hartt, then looks up too late to avoid a running SPEER from Abusah, knocking him away from Hartt and down to a big pop from the crowd. Abusah quickly exits the ring. After taking a six-count to recover, both men begin to crawl to their corners. At the count of eight, Chronos tags out to Fallen. At the count of nine, Hartt tags out to Abusah. The place explodes as Abusah hits the ring and charges at Fallen with a SPEER. Fallen has the move well-scouted however, which is not hard as it appears to be the only move Abusah knows. Abusah flies into the Fallen/Chronos corner and nails his head on the ringpost before freeing himself from the corner and wobbling backward. As he does so, Fallen grabs his head and plants him with the Lowfall, before making a cover and hooking a leg. A hurting Hartt can't make the save, and three seconds later Ric Chronos and Fallen have earned a shot at the fWo World Tag Team Titles. They stand victorious in the ring to a good ovation from the crowd as Slaughter continues... Result: Chronos & Fallen by pinfall Taking A Gamble, Part Three We go back to the casino for more action. The games of blackjack have been about even, with neither man getting a clear cut edge over the other. Still, Armstrong didn't exactly know who they were, but that was likely the furthest thing from his mind. Besides secks, tonight, the only thing on Armstrong' mind was winning. Money. A lot of it. A ton of it. Thus far he'd won quite a lot, but the ton... was perhaps yet to come. "Hit me." Armstrong said, taking a huge gamble as he had a pair of kings. The gamble paid off, and he had an ace. He won another hand. The man he was playing against was frustrated. Very frustrated. His associate kept telling him that at least they ended James' streak.... and that was enough. They should move on. But he wouldn't have any of it. He wanted to keep going. A few more hands, a few more losses, a win here or there... it was getting late. Finally, he asked James if James wanted to put it all on the line. After careful consideration that lasted the amount of time it took James to take another shot of Vodka, James blurted out with a 'sure, what the hell'. But the man he was playing against - upped the ante. Thing is, James didn't have a whole lot of money other than what was laying in front of him in the chips to back it up. So, a deal was struck. The man would put up a very, very large sum of money... and James would put up his money, and take a gamble. If he lost, he'd promise to run around the casino naked yelling assinine things. Which James had done once back in 1985 after being drunk for forty eight hours straight, but that was besides the point. Still... the man had a lot to gain from the money James currently had in his grasp. And thus, it was on. The hand was dealt. They looked over their hands, and the dealer asked them if they wanted to hit or stay. James stayed. The man hit. Twice. Finally, they revealed their hands. The man had a king, and a pair of fives. James had an ace & a king. Blackjack. James jumped out of his chair, raising his arms in celebration, and thanking God for making things go his way for the first time in a long time. He even promised not to look at porn anymore, or have secks with hookers. We knew that was a lie; but James didn't care. He'd likely be looking at porn in a few hours or having secks with the hookers. Likely both. Maybe even at the same time. Nevertheless, James began to strip anyways, he had to celebrate. He took three more shots of vodka as the associate of the mysterious man tried to calm him down. But before we could see anything else... Slaughter headed back into the arena. The Boston Strangler vs. Rain Result: Strangler by submission Out Of Luck And back to the casino once again we went, where we last saw James Armstrong about to strip in celebration for his huge winnings. But before he could, the man asked James a question. "Do I know you?" James shrugged. "You do now!" "Yeah, yeah I think I know you. You're James Armstrong, from the fWo, and you damn near killed yourself last night while you were here in Vegas. I heard about you on the news this afternoon - you're a maniac. You were reportedly .2 over the legal limit of consumption of alcohol!" James shrugged. "Yeah, so what." The man was not pleased. "So what? So what? You could've killed yourself and the other gentlemen in the vehicle. You don't even care. And I just lost all this money... to you? Wait a minute..I'm going to sue you for everything you're worth..." His associate stepped in. His associate warned him that legal action wasn't an option at this point - and besides, what could he sue James for if James hadn't yet committed a crime? Sure, he was close to it if he took off those boxers... even though, the associate of the man claimed there was something else he could do. The man nodded. "You're also a co-owner so to speak of the fans Wrestling organization, aren't you?" James nodded, while several members of the crowd tried to keep him from taking off the aforementioned boxers. The man finally had James attention, though. "Yeah? Did you have something else you wanted to say?" The man nodded at James remark. "Do you know who I am?" James laughed. "The man I just beat for a large sum of money in a game of blackjack." The man smiled evily. "Besides that. You should know who I am. Your wrestling organization happens to have a show on the network *I* work for." James gulped. He knew something was up, but wasn't quite sure. "Right... so that means you work for..." before he could finish, his sentence was finished for him. "NBC. And it won't look good when our network is put in the spotlight over what you did last night. Not at all. And I'm going to go review the terms of the agreement your company made with my network. I have the stroke to make things happen... your luck just ran out. You may have survived an automobile accident this weekend, and you may have just got lucky and won all this money from you. But I promise you - no, I guarantee you - you haven't heard the last from me, mister. Do you understand? You can expect a call from NBC tonight." The man and his associate got up and walked away. James gulped again, before quickly getting re-dressed, and taking his chips. It wasn't clear if he was going to cash in... because even though that large sum of money was important to him - clearly, something was more important. Scott Slugger vs. Mike Bear vs. Primetime Korn Result: Slugger by pinfall Phone Call Backstage in his office sat Chris Finn, thinking that Sunday Slaughter was over. He was pleased with the main event, and glad that his top performers would be ready for the Memorial Show set to occur on Thursday. As James Armstrong stumbled into his office, barely able to stand because of being really drunk and of course he was still in shock because of the amount of money he won, Finn knew something bad was about to occur. "James, you walk further through that door... something bad is going to happen." "It already did. I just beat someone for a lot of money!" "Er... how is that bad?" "That someone works for NBC and is a top level exec!" A look of horror came over Finn' face. "What?" Suddenly, flashbacks of Finn & Armstrong yelling "Ohhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh shiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiittttt!" just twenty four hours danced in his head. "Don't tell me... they already found out about last night?" James gulped. Finn gulped. And suddenly, the phone rang. Finn told James to answer it. "I'm not going to answer it - they're already pissed at me! You answer it!" Finn asked James what they said. "They know about it? Dammit, I thought we gave the press a good excuse..." James nodded negatively. "Answer it." Finally, Finn did. "Yes, Chris Finn speaking. Oh, I'm doing... okay. Well, the accident wasn't my fault. Yes, I was involved. Yes, I'm aware of the original terms of our agreement. No, James Armstrong is not an alcoholic. No, I'm not an alcoholic. No, nobody in the fWo is an alcoholic... er... well, not really anyways. Uh huh. Uh huh. No, I haven't looked at the terms of the agreement in awhile. Yes, I know it's not good publicity. I was involved, you know. No, I'm not getting smart alecky with you. Sorry. You're what? You can't do that! You can't cancel Warfare because of this? Two week notice? What? You're telling me Warfare is cancelled in two weeks? WHAT?!?!" Slaughter faded to black.
