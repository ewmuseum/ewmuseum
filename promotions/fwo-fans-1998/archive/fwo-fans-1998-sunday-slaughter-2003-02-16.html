Introduction Previously... It's time to put up or shut up. For several weeks the so-called Triple Threat of the fWo has run amuck on the fWo World Heavyweight Championship scene. With Erik Kelly their primary focus, Spyder, Fallen and Neo have staked their claim to wrestling's highest honor and kept Kelly at arm's length for weeks. No longer is this possible however, for tonight on Sunday Slaughter the former fWo World Heavyweight Champion will get his rematch against the masked rudo who defeated him at BodyCount. Spyder and Erik Kelly will vie for the gold once more. Meanwhile, the other two members of the Triple Threat are not idle. Neo and Fallen, both former fWo Tag Team Champions, will take on the undefeated fWo World Tag Team Champions Triality. In what will no doubt be a defining moment for the tag team division, can the Triple Threat expand their power base still farther? What dirty tricks does the heel stable have in store, and what lengths will these men go to in order to control the face of wrestling remains unknown...
