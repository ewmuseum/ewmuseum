Sunday Slaughter, 17 June 2001

17 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Greg Davis looked at the note on his locker room door. "Meet me in the ring. - Tempest" He looked it over. "Crazy chick... what, does she want another chairshot or something?" Davis tore the post-it note off his door and opened it. He blinked, rubbed his eyes. He couldn't believe what he was seeing. The entire room was covered in post-it notes, all emblazoned with the same message as the one that had been on his door. In the aquarium he had set up in the corner, a large tropical fish swam by. Attached to its side was another post-it note. "How the hell did she do that?" Davis shook his head. "Crazy bitch... she wants me to meet her in the ring, she's got me." Davis turned and left the room, slamming the door behind him. Eddy Fate (c) vs. Donaven Winters Sugar' by System of a Down brings out the Insane Wonder...what is he doing here? The unexpected addition at ringside pulls up a chair and takes a seat near the entranceway. He comments to a nearby cameraman that Eddy Fate is about to get 'regulated', whatever that means. 'Disconnect' by the Henry Rollins Band brings out the current Cruiserweight champion, Eddy Fate. Eddy Fate stares Insane Wonder down as he enters the ring and wonders what he's doing here. 'Somewhat Damaged' Nine Inch Nails brings out his opponent, Donaven Winters, in this non-title match-up. The bell sounds and this one is underway as Fate trips up Winters with a drop toehold. Fate waits for Winters to get to his feet and then boots him in the gut and Irish whips him across the ring and then connects with a wheel kick. Winters gets to his knees and begins to crawl towards the corner, Winters gets to his feet but is hit with a running clothesline from Fate sandwiching him in the corner. Winters drops to the ground as Fate backs across the ring and charges towards the seated Winters and delivers a hard knee into the Fallen Angel's face. Winters rolls to the outside to slow the momentum down and begins to complain to the ref. Fate backs across to the ring and looks like he's going for some sort of suicide dive, but as he begins to run towards Winters he is tripped up by Wonder on the outside. Fate gets back to his feet and goes to confront Wonder from the ring but Wonder squirts a pack of ketchup into Fates eyes. Fate stumbles backwards rubbing his eyes and doesn't realize that Winters has returned to the ring and is hit with a belly to back suplex. Winters to his feet and drives his knee into Fate's chest. Fate crawls towards the ropes and uses them to help himself up but is put to the outside with a clothesline over the top ropes by Winters. Winters then decides to complain to the ref again while Wonder is making his way to Fate, Wonder whips out a Spork and begins to drive it into Fate's forehead. Wonder follows it up by throwing Fate into the guardrail and then Winters takes advantage and hits a plancha to the outside taking Fate to the ground. Fate is bleeding heavily as Winters rolls him back into the ring. Winters now on the ring apron and hits a springboard legdrop from the apron to the inside. Pin, but only a two count for the Fallen Angel. Winters bringing Fate to his feet and puts him back down with a cradle neckbreaker. Another pin attempt, but Fate once again kicks out. Winters brings Fate to his feet and Irish whips the Cruiserweight champion across the ring, Fate rebounds off the ropes and ducks a clothesline attempt, rebounds again and puts Winters down with a hurricanrana. Winters back to his feet but has a little trouble standing as Fate dropkicks him the face. Fate brings Winters back to his feet and hits a snap suplex holds onto the suplex and brings Winters back to his feet but this time hits a Brainbuster DDT. Fate now points to the top rope and begins to ascend to the top, he takes off and drops the leg across the throat of Winters. Cover, but only a two count. Fate pulls Winters back to his feet and hits a back breaker, Winters rolls over holding his back but this leaves him vulnerable as Fate applies a camel clutch to Winters. Fate is really wrenching back on Winters' neck and he can't hold on much longer. Insane Wonder now on the ring apron and he begins to scream at the ref that it is a choke hold which it obviously isn't. Winters is tapping out but the ref is trying to get Wonder to drop from the ring apron, Fate releases the hold and charges at Wonder and knocks him off the ring apron but out of nowhere Winters has a school boy on Fate but Fate manages to kickout. Both men get to their feet and begin going shot for shot until Fate ducks a right hand and wraps around Winters with a reverse waist lock, attempts a German suplex but Winters grabs the top rope to stop it and then hits a mule kick to take Fate down and the ref doesn't see it. Winters takes a minute to recuperate and then goes back to work on Fate with hard stomps to the sternum. Winters has Fate on his feet now and hits a belly to back powerbomb on the Cruiserweight champion and follows it up with a pin, but once again Fate kicks out at two. Winters is frustrated and brings Fate to his feet again and hits the Falcon arrow to put Fate down for another pin attempt but it doesn't get the job done. Winters gets to his feet and signals for the reverse Gory special piledriver and lifts Fate to his feet. He's got Fate up but Fate squirms until his feet touch the ground and then he grabs Winters' legs and trips him up and Winters eats the canvas. Fate now applies a Mexican surfboard on Winters in the middle of the ring and Winters once again finds himself on brink of losing this match. But this time there is nothing Insane Wonder can do about it because Fate is demanding that the ref stays where he is until Winters taps out. Winters is struggling and shifts his weight is able to tip the submission over but Fate won't release it but Winters eventually squirms out of it. Fate gets to his feet and out of frustration runs towards the ropes and leaps to the outside and hits a plancha on the Insane Wonder. Fate quickly rolls back into the ring now the Insane Wonder is out and goes for the Twist of Fate but Winters reverses it and pushes Fate into the ropes but Fate kicks Winters in the gut and attempts the Twist of Fate again but this time he hits it and goes for a cover...but Winters kicks out. Fate doesn't have time to argue the count as he runs towards the corner and begins to make his way to the top rope. The ref is checking on Winters now as Fate sets up for the Senton Bomb... but Winters is able to pull the ref onto his chest and the ref feels the Senton Bomb with authority. The ref rolls over in pain while Wonder sees his opportunity and is now climbing the ropes himself as Fate slowly gets to his feet wondering what happened, he turns around right into a jumping DDT from the top rope to plant Fate's head right into the mat. Wonder grabs Winters' arm and drapes it over Fate's chest as the ref slowly regains consciousness and drops his hand once... twice... three times and Winters just scored a pinfall over the Cruiserweight champion but with a little help from a certain Insane Wonder. This isn't over between the Insane Wonder and Eddy Fate, it is just beginning. Result: Donaven Winters by pinfall Pain Steam rose from the vacant parking lot of the Fleet Center. Rain fell from the heavens in a solid sheet, drenching the tarmac. There was a shadow of movement in the steam. Then another. And then Crucifix appeared. His body was partially hidden by the rising fog, which obscured some of his face as well. He seemed not to mind the rain. "Adam Manson. In five days, we meet. This very arena behind me..." Crucifix stopped and gestured to the Fleet Center, looming in shadow behind him like a colossus. "Will be the site where you learn the true meaning of pain. Yes, you call yourself hardcore, but you don't know the meaning of the word. You're nothing more than a poseur, a pretender to the throne. You hide behind your words and your friends, but they will not help you when it's just me and you, alone in the Fleet Center. And then... you will understand pain." Lightning flashed, outlining Crucifix. The steam continued to rise, completely blocking the view of Crucifix. When it had cleared, there was nobody there. Not Funny The brawl had obviously been going on for some time. Both the Boston Strangler and Ric Chronos bore the scars of battle, and Adam Manson was lying semi-conscious against a wall, obviously taken out earlier by the Strangler. Chronos and Strangler traded fists in the loading dock, each looking to inflict the highest measure of vengeance. As Chronos and Strangler battled back and forth, from somewhere a single marble rolled across the floor, barely noticeable. Then a second... then a handful... Suddenly, with a clatter, the floor was covered in marbles. The two men looked down in surprise, but not in time. They both stumbled and collapsed to the floor, each glaring at the other accusingly. Then their gaze was drawn to one side by a light, musical giggle. The camera turned to find Tempest laughing to one side. She grinned happily as the two stood carefully. Her eyes were closed as she laughed but suddenly opened as the immense shadow created by both men falls across her. She blinks in surprise and her eyes widen as it dawns on her that neither man is laughing. "Um... it was joke guys. You know... haha? Um... you aren't laughing." They take another step toward her and neither is at all amused. "Aw c'mon you shoulda seen yourselves. You should see yourselves now in fact!" Both men's faces contort in further fury as the glare down at her in all their terrifying glory. "I mean um... it's just.... well... Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa!" Tempest wheels and bolts down the hall fairly burning a path in her wake. Chronos snarled, and shouted after her. "You stupid bitch! Who the hell do you think you are?!" Tempest stopped running, about 10 yards away from the two, and turned. "If you've got the ba er, the guts, you'll meet me in the ring! Nobody does that to Ric Chronos!" Tempest smirked. "You're on, pal. But if I were you, I would, um... look behind you." She giggled, and Chronos turned, just in time to see the massive right fist of the Boston Strangler descend on his head. Chronos and Strangler resumed going after each other, and Tempest backed away, watching the brawl as she went. Dark Angel vs. Frank Snow "Stigmata" blasts through the arena speakers. Out steps Dark Angel, accompanied by Fyre. DA rolls into the ring, and bounces off the ropes once before waiting for his opponent. The music of The Underground plays as Snow, Robert Jensen and Aimee Horihito appear on the top of the entrance ramp. The dreadlocked monster Snow makes his way to the ring as Jensen and Horihito head to the announcing table. Snow enters the ring and begins to stare down DA, who returns the stare in kind. The match starts off with DA charging at Snow, who counters by powerslamming the smaller man for a quick two-count. Snow drags DA to his feet and tosses him into the corner, before charging in with a big shoulder and following up with repeated shoulderblocks. Snow then picks up DA and sets him on the second rope. Snow climbs up with DA, hooks a headlock, and drops him with a second-rope superplex. Rather than go for the cover, Snow brings the man back to his feet, and throws him into the ropes, catching him coming off with a big boot. With DA down, Snow bounces off the ropes and drops a leg. He covers, but again only gets a two-count. Snow brings DA up to his feet again, and puts him in a powerbomb position. He lifts DA, but DA counters with a series of closed fists to the large skull of Snow, who is momentarily dazed, but counters by tossing DA over his head and into a stun gun. DA remains on his feet, and stumbles back towards Snow, who grabs him for a chokeslam. DA counters this with a well-placed testicular kick, which staggers Snow, who stumbles back towards the ropes. DA hooks Snow from behind with a hard russian legsweep. He covers, but only gets 1 and a half. DA signals that he's going up top to attempt a flying elbowdrop, and climbs to the top rope. DA then dives off and nails the flying elbow. He makes the cover, but Snow kicks out at two. Both men get up, and DA swings at Snow. Snow ducks it, then hooks DA from behind in a full nelson, then turns it into a devastating sitdown full nelson slam, jarring him. As this happens, Jensen and Horihito leave the announcer's position. Horihito leaps onto the apron and distracts the referee. Jensen tosses a pair of knucks into the ring, where Frank Snow picks them up. Jensen then rolls into the ring and takes ahold of DA for Snow. Snow swings, but DA ducks and Snow clocks his partner Jensen instead, knocking him from the ring. Meanwhile, the ref fails to disengage himself from Aimee Horihito, allowing Fyre to climb onto the top rope. She measures Snow, then leaps off with a picture-perfect missile dropkick. Fyre rolls out of the ring, and the ref finally frees himself from Aimee, just in time to turn around and count the pinfall for Dark Angel. The referee raises DA's hand as Slaughter goes to commercial... Result: Dark Angel by pinfall Impressed? Donaven Winters strolled backstage, a sneer etched on his face. Backstage workers diverted their paths to avoid him, and he took some small inner satisfaction from this. Then, somebody was stupid enough not to move from his way. To no great surprise of Winters, it was Black Quicksilver. "Donaven! Hey, buddy! Good to see ya!" Winters looked him over, not amused. BQ continued. "Listen, I know you've won all your matches the last few weeks, but you've still really impressed me. And hey, you beat the Cruiserweight Champion tonight! OK, so you had to have a bunch of interference to do it... but that's beside the point. Tell you what... I want to give you a shot at the Wominternopean Jobberweight Championship! Remember, you have to lose to win it. You game?" Winters looked at Quicksilver and snarled. "I don't give a damn if I impress you. If you want me at Redemption, you got me. But I don't give a damn about your stupid title... I just want to kick your ass." With that, Winters palmed BQ's head and shoved him into the wall. BQ crumpled to a sitting position. Winters walked away without a word. Quicksilver shook his head. "Well, I'll be Damned." Warm-Up "Walk" hit, and the crowd erupted in boos as fWo Hardcore Champion Kodiak Vic Creed made his way to the ring with a microphone. Creed stepped into the ring and smirked. "As all of you idiots out there know, I'm defending my title the title I've held for just short of three months against Hellrazor at Damned. Since that's such a ahem *tough* challenge, I've decided to have a warm-up match tonight. With that in mind, let me introduce my opponent... the King of the Death Matches, the Hardcore Legend, a man more hardcore than Terry Funk and Mick Foley combined... CHARLIE DUDE!" A pudgy man wearing a tie-dye shirt and nylons over his head walked out and rolled into the ring. He got up to his feet, and Creed wound up and threw a huge mock punch as the crowd booed. Charlie flopped to the mat exaggeratedly, and Creed brought him up. He set Dude on the top rope and climbed up after him. Creed hooked Dude for a superplex, but Charlie suddenly came to life, laying in a series of stiff punches to the kidneys. Creed fell, crotching himself on the top rope. Charlie then hooked Creed's head, switched positions with him, and dropped to the mat with a top-rope DDT. The crowd went nuts. Dude then popped up and ripped off the nylons and pulled the stuffing out of his shirt, revealing none other than Hellrazor. The crowd popped as Hellrazor removed the fWo Hardcore Title from the waist of Creed, climbed the turnbuckles, and held it high in the air. Slaughter then went to commercial... Mike Bear vs. Fear vs. Erik Kelly Rammstein's "Engel" brings out the leader of the Underground, Mike Bear. Accompanied by Brittany, he receives a loud roar of cheers from the crowd as he walks to the ring. He rolls in and warms up against the ropes, as "Bullet the Blue Sky" by P.O.D. replaces his music. Fyre makes her way out to the stage first, followed by the seven-foot Fear. After sending an icy glare towards Brittany, Fyre takes her spot on the outside, as Fear steps over the top rope and enters the ring. Kelly enters the ring in the same manner as Fear, and as the bell sounds, he charges at Mike Bear. Bear ducks under a right hand from Kelly and traps him in the corner with vicious stomps and shoulder blocks. His former stable mate and guinea pig, Fear, cuts into the action by turning Bear around and striking him with a right hand. He grabs Bear by the arm, and then shoots him into the opposite corner. Bear counters a charging Fear by sticking his feet up into his face, and then clotheslines him to the mat. A ready Erik Kelly however boots Bear in the mid-section, and slams him down to the mat. Kelly makes the cover, but Bear breaks it up at two. Kelly stands up and towers over Bear, but the Underground leader shows no fear by chopping the Eradicator in the chest. Kelly responds with a head butt, and Bear staggers back. He focuses his attention towards Fear, but is met with a blindsided super kick from Bear. Before Fear can react, Bear dropkicks him out of the ring and covers Kelly. The referee counts to two, but Kelly kicks out. Out of the corner of his eye, Bear sees Fear on the ring apron, and immediately springs off the middle turnbuckle and strikes him with a reverse elbow. Fear falls down on the ring apron, as Kelly delivers a vicious big boot to the back of Bear's head. Fear gets up once again and swings a right at Kelly, but Kelly blocks it and throttles Fear. Fyre attempts to help her leader, but Matt Loco is quick to grab her by the ankle. With great force, Kelly lifts Fear up, brings him in the ring and choke slams him to the mat. The referee however goes down as a result of taking a bump during the execution of the move. Erik Kelly eyes both of his fallen opponents, and selects Bear to Eradicate. Bear tries fighting out of it, but Kelly drops a forearm across his back and sets him between his legs. Fear looks at the unconscious Eradicator, and applies the Blackout. With three drops of the arm, the referee calls for the bell, rewarding the match to Fear. Fyre and Ruben Ross both pile into the ring, followed by the rest of the stable mates from the three respective stables Vengence, Robert Jensen, Frank Snow, Hellrazor, Lamont, and Dark Angel. Boston Strangler vs. Adam Manson "Burning Blood" by Six Feet Under brings Slaughter back from commercial, in the process bringing out Adam Manson. Manson yells something at the referee, and starts...err...strangling the Strangler. Irony at it's best, perhaps, but the referee doesn't care. Manson ignores the zebra until he starts a five count. Right before five, Manson breaks the choke and gets up. He lets Strangler get to his hands and knees before running in and kicking him in the ribs, sending him rolling over. Manson relentlessly stomps on Strangler's ribs, having found a target. Strangler grabs the ropes, once again forcing the referee to intervene. He steps in front of Manson, informing him that when the opponent is in the ropes, you stop whatever you're doing to them. Strangler uses this to his advantage, as it gives him a quick breather. The referee moves, letting Manson free, and Manson walks right into a big boot. Manson gets to his feet again only to find himself being whipped into the ropes again. He comes back and Strangler takes him down with a Samoan Drop. Strangler pins, but Manson kicks out at two and a half. Strangler gets up slowly, and then assists Manson to a vertical base as well. Strangler starts unloading rights on him, but Manson catches on and blocks one. He delivers a staggering shot to Paul Revere's jaw, but Strangler swings again. Manson again blocks it and catches him with one of his own. The pattern repeats itself once more, but as Manson goes for the knockout blow Strangler ducks down and lets Manson go by. Manson turns around, surprised that he missed, and Strangler kicks him in the gut. Strangler hooks him up for a Gutwrench Powerbomb, and after a failed attempt where he couldn't pick him up, he executes it crudely (but effectively) dropping Manson hard on the canvas. Instead of pinning the prone Manson, Strangler instead rolls away, clutching his ribs and grimacing. With both men down, the referee starts a 10-count. Strangler gets up at six, Manson at eight, and the match continues. Manson stumbles backwards into the corner, still feeling the effects from the Gutwrench Powerbomb only minutes ago. Strangler sees an opportunity and charges in, but Manson was just playing possum as he moves and sends Strangler crashing into the turnbuckle chest first. Strangler tries to turn around but Manson shoves him back into the corner with a barrage of elbows and fists, the occassional knee thrown in there. Manson grows tired of simple brawling, however, and reverts back to blatant choking. The referee is on top of things, once again warning him and starting the five count when Manson ignores his warnings. Just before five, Manson breaks and steps back, but puts his boot up into Strangler's throat and starts the five count again. Manson stops just before five once more, and steps back again, as the Strangler's instinctively grabs his throat. Strangler's break doesn't last long as Manson comes back in with more kicks to the ribs, as Strangler eventually falls to the mat, leaning back against the turnbuckle. Manson smirks at this, and starts back on the ribs. Once again, the referee makes him break away. Strangler rolls from the ring clutching his ribs, even moreso than before. Manson follows him out, continuing the assault. Manson grabs his shoulder and turns him around, picking him up in an amazing feat of strength, holding the Strangler in a gorilla press for seconds before dropping him chest-first across the safety railing. Strangler starts coughing, and a little bit of blood trickles from the corner of his mouth. Manson grabs Strangler by the hair and rolls him back into the ring, so as to avoid a count-out. He grabs Strangler by the hair once more and raises him to his feet. He clamps his hand on Strangler's head, dragging his thumb across his throat. However, possibly in a last-ditch effort, Strangler manages to bring his knee up directly into Manson's manhood, breaking the grip. Strangler grabs the arm and goes behind Manson, as the fans erupt. The Boston Massacre. Indeed, Manson finds himself locked in the dreaded submission move with nowhere to go. He reaches for the ropes, but from the middle of the ring he isn't even close. The situation becomes clear to Manson, but he refuses to tap out. Finally, he slumps to the mat, unconscious. The referee raises his arm and it drops, three times. Strangler releases the hold and Manson crumples to the canvas. Strangler immediately grabs his ribs again, and gingerly leaves the ring as "O Fortuna" rocks the building. Result: Boston Strangler by submission Six Days (IV) Sunday Slaughter came back from commercial to a black screen, and the opening strains of AC/DC's "Damned" could be heard. One word appeared. Ric Chronos (c) vs. Tempest The Lo-Fidelity Allstars' "Battle Flag" takes to the speakers, and an energetic Tempest walks onto the stage and poses for the crowd. The crowd adds more excitement to the atmosphere with their cheers. Tempest jogs down the ramp, with her long black hair flowing behind her. She slides into the ring and bounces off the ropes to test the ring out. Her happy mood suddenly turns to a frown when "Christian Says" by the Tones on Tail cuts her music out. With hands on hips, she turns towards the ring entrance to see Ric Chronos make his domineering presence felt. He stalks towards the ring with the gleaming fWo World Title strapped around his waist, walks up the steps, and enters the ring. Tempest is almost entranced with the sight of the title as Chronos straps it off of his waist and throws it to the ref. Seeing her eyes distracted with the gold, Chronos takes a cheap forearm shot to Tempest's face before the referee can even hand the belt off to a ringside attendant. The bell sounds as Chronos pushes Tempest into the corner and stomps away relentlessly at her. She slumps down to a seated position in the corner, but Chronos grabs her by the hair and throws her right across the ring. Tempest recovers against the ropes, and out of the corner of her eye, sees Chronos advancing. She throws a sidekick to Chronos' gut, but the World Champion is unaffected. He soccer kicks her in the gut, the impact causing her to fall to the outside. Chronos follows out and raises his clasped hands in the air, but his double ax handle catches nothing but ground, as Tempest rolls out of the way. An idea springs into her mind, and she immediately begins retreating. Chronos' eyes light up and he chases after her around the corner, but fails to see her contorting her body behind the steel steps. She immediately chop-blocks Chronos' left leg, and then hops onto the ring apron. Chronos gets up and reaches for her feet, but she leaps back into the ring. Frustrated, Chronos slides into the ring, but Tempest grabs him by his hair and repeatedly bashes his head into the mat. She quickly takes off to the ropes, but Chronos rings her neck and lifts her high in the air with a military press. With great force, he throws her high in the air and lets fall flat on the mat. Chronos makes a mock cover, and Tempest escapes by lifting her shoulder up. Chronos stands up and then drops back down with an elbow to Tempest's sternum. She holds her chest in pain, but Chronos shows no mercy by grabbing a fistful of hair and bringing her up. A single chop sends her staggering about the ring. He rushes towards her and boots her to the mid-section. Her small size causes her to double over and fall to her knees. Chronos sets her between his legs, and picks her up in a power bomb, but Tempest's flexibility allows her to jockey around and take Chronos over with a victory roll. One. Two. Chronos kicks out. Seeing herself on a roll, Tempest takes to the ropes and levels Chronos with a spinning heel kick. Chronos staggers back into the ropes, and then staggers forward, just in time for a top rope perched Tempest to fly off and hit a bulldog. The crowd cheers in encouragement for Tempest, as she hooks Chronos in a headlock, and signals for the Rottweiler. As she kicks off the ropes, however, Chronos counters it with a Greco-Roman style suplex. Chronos then picks her up, and sets her in a front face lock. He looks around at the crowd, and then grins before dropping Tempest to the mat with a DDT. He brings Tempest back up and goes behind her, then applies an abdominal stretch. He then uses his freehand to apply a claw to the ribs of Tempest. Tempest's body writhes in pain... and a tap-out follows. The bell sounds in Chronos' favor, but the World Champion keeps the stretch intact. More boos follow as 'Sanity Assassin' Adam Manson rushes to the ring and viciously chokes at Tempest's small neck. The referee tries all that he can to stop Chronos and Manson from inflicting permanent damage. But neither Chronos nor Manson shows any remorse, as Tempest continues to suffer. Greg Davis joins the fray, and begins kicking away at Tempest's mid-section. Every part of her body feels pain. But O' Fortuna brings out the Boston Strangler. Greg Davis bails the ring. Manson immediately stands up as Strangler rushes towards the ring and enters. Strangler ducks a wild clothesline from the big monster, and clotheslines him over the top rope. Chronos releases his hold on Tempest who then rolls out of the ring clutching her ribs and faces Strangler eye-to-eye. Chronos strikes first with a right hand, but Strangler returns with a forearm shot. The two continue trading fisticuffs as Slaughter goes off the air... Result: Ric Chronos by submission
