Introduction The road to Cyberslam has been a difficult one. But now only three shows remain before the celebration that will mark the sixth year in the promotion's history... and likely the last. With attendence dropping and US interest fading, as well as strict restrictions against the fWo within the United States, the road agents and management find themselves struggling against a seemingly impossible foe. The decision made, the fWo returns to New York and Madison Square Garden for CyberSlam VI... and the event of the year. But what the wrestlers are not aware of is that the event will be the promotion's last... although even now at this late date forces conspire in the background. But are their intentions for the good of the fWo or the final blow to the promotion's future... Prove yourself... ten times over.
