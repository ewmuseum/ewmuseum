fWo — Pay-per-view

Unstable 2000, 23 September 2000

23 September 2000

Introduction California. To some, it's their calling card. If they don't live there now, they want to live there in the future. The warm weather & possibility of being famous are just two things that draw people to this location; despite the major reasons not to go there such as the air, the climate, the crime, and many other reasons. Today, the fWo was in the capital of California, where the National Basketball Association's Sacramento Kings played. The Arco Arena. What drew the fans Wrestling organization to Sacramento today wasn't the warm weather, and let's face it, a bunch of them were already famous. What drew the fWo to California today was their next pay-per-view... "Unstable". On the outside, everything did appear Unstable. On the inside, everything was Unstable. The card and participants weren't finalized until a few days prior. The way the show was booked itself was a joke - and James Armstrong was realizing this now. He sat in his office, wondering how the hell he managed to book such a mess. And then he remembered he was afterall... James Armstrong. He let out a sigh, before reaching for a plate of bun cake - his third for the day. Suddenly, the acting owner looked over at three men in suits sitting across from him. Armstrong sighed. He booked a horrendous show, and now he had this to deal with. One of the men spoke up. "If word gets out, it could get ugly. Wrestling promoters doped up, having sex with prostitutes, this doesn't look good, James." Armstrong' only retort was "Happens all the time... dammit, how did they get these photos?" The men - presumably lawyers - didn't know. Another sigh, before Armstrong took a bite of his fresh plate of bun cake. "What do they want?" One of the men pulled out a paper, and handed it to James. "They want the fWo. All of it. If they don't get it, they're going to give the photos to Finn' wife, who most assuredly will want a divorce, that'll lead to Finn losing everything... including the fWo. They don't want to go through all that. It could take months, maybe even years... that's why they've targeted you." "Me?" The three men shook their heads yes in unison. "I never really liked Finn anyways." Armstrong spoke, with a goofy smile on his face, and crumbs of bun cake hanging on his chin. "You know?" The men looked at each other as if Armstrong had lobsters growing out of his ears. "Sir, you really have no choice... the fWo doesn't belong to you. We've come up with a solution to the problem. You can either tell Finn and you can come up with a way to keep it quiet... and give the person behind the photos the fWo..." Armstrong was already shaking his head. "Not going to happen. I'm not going to tell Finn. You know, this person could be lying about the photos. Have you actually seen the photos?" The three men shook their heads no in unison. "Then screw these guys! I'm calling their bluff - and we'll find out what happens when they don't HAVE any photos to begin with! Now get out of my office - I have a pay-per-view to run!" And with that, Unstable was off and running. Strategy, Part One Unstable headed elsewhere in the arena, with the show just about ready for it's first match. Backstage, we saw Stone approaching a locker room... not just any locker room, the locker room of the mysterious Rain. She knocked on the door, and swiftly it crept open. Rain' shadow stood in the distance, before he welcomed her in. The door slowly shut, but before it did we heard Rain ask her what she wanted... "I want to talk strategy." The door closed, and with the camera man not brave enough to stick around to eavesdrop, Unstable continued elsewhere... Partners Carnage & Erik Rave were still new to the fWo, but the Thrill Killers weren't making any friends. Scoffing at some of the older veterans as they walked by, and mocking them when they weren't around, little did they know others would get wind of this and surely do something about it. That said, they were also making fun of some of the other new guys - Ruben Ross walked by, he was mocked unknowingly. Ditto for The Red Dragon, who was seen entering the lockeroom of the Stump Squad. Another new guy though - Juno Law - happened to be their partner for the night. Law appeared to be excited about being on the card, when just a week ago he wasn't sure if he was ever going to get booked in the fWo. Law approached his teammates for the night, and the Thrill Killers acted cordial. The three mens exchanged greetings and talked strategy for the night, and then Juno left to go do some short workouts to prepare for his match. With him out of sight, the Thrill Killers mocked him, and mocked the incident that involved the Abusah spearing him a few weeks back. As they did, Erik Kelly walked by with Pamela Dee swiftly, and caught a glimpse of this out of the corner of his eye... Team Stump: Black Quicksilver, Mike Heftel, Stump & ??? vs. Team Kung Fu: Tom The Bomb, The New Ninja, Karate Fighter Actio Inside the arena, the pay-per-view began. Fireworks exploded throughout the arena, signalling the start of Unstable. "Body Movin'" by the Beastie Boys began to play, and out walked Tom The Kung Fu Bomb (holding the Karate Fighter Action Figure) & The Dark Ninja. Their mystery partner however was not yet present, but it was expected he soon would be. On cue, the lights dimmed, and out walked Jackhammer, complete with a security escort. Sporting a purple colored boxing robe, he walked out onto the stage shadow boxing. He also had a trainer, who made sure to squirt water in his mouth at every opportunity available. Jackhammer entered the ring and continued to shadow box, before The Dark Ninja looked at Tom The Kung Fu Bomb suspciously. Tom simply shrugged, and held up his Karate Fighter Action Figure, prompting some boos from the crowd. Jackhammer meanwhile, was billed as "Lavender Holyfield". "We're Not Gonna Take It" started up, and out walked Black Quicksilver & Mike Heftel, the former of which carried 'Stump'. That trio walked down to the ring, and climbed in, ready to do battle with their opponents of the night. Tom asked BQ where their mystery partner, and BQ shrugged, not knowing where he was. Suddenly, the lights went off, and came on about three seconds later, & The Red Dragon was in the ring. BQ smiled, and the referee signalled for the bell. The unofficial team captains for the match started things off, that being BQ & Tom The Kung Fu Bomb. They locked up, and Tom got the early advantage with a go behind into a hammerlock. BQ reversed out it, then grabbed Tom for a running bulldog. BQ charged, but Tom let go, and BQ went flying through the air. The multiple time Cruiserweight Champion landed on his feet, and then turned towards Tom and charged. Tom took him down with the dreaded drop toe hold of doom, before both men sprung back to their feet. Tom charged this time and BQ took him down with a drop toe hold. Undaunted, they got back up again, and locked up. Tom swung Quicksilver to the ropes, and then ducked for a backdrop. BQ returned and instead flew over the top of him for a sunset flip, which drew a quick two count. They both sprung to their feet, BQ in a fighting stance, Tom in a kung-fu style pose. Tom then bowed, giving BQ an opportunity. BQ grabbed him by the head, went to a corner and hit a tornado DDT. BQ then bowed, mocking Tom, before tagging out to Heftel. Heftel climbed to the top rope and flew off with a frog splash, which failed miserably. Tom moved out of the way, and Heftel landed awkwardly. Clutching his ribs, Tom sprinted over and covered him, but the quick thinking BQ then dropped to the floor, and pulled the referee out. BQ then hopped up to the ring apron, and when the referee confronted him on his actions, BQ blamed it on Stump. Stump denied it with a "..." and the referee shook his head in confusion. The referee climbed back into the ring, and Tom demanded that Heftel tag in BQ. Heftel did, and it was once again, on. BQ charged at Tom took him down with a hip toss. BQ got up and charged again, this time turning the hiptoss into a tiltawhirl headscissors. They both got up, before they prepared to lock up again. Instead, Tom asked for the Karate Fighter Action Figure. Ninja sighed, rolled his eyes, and gave it to him. The referee insisted (for some strange reason) that Tom make a legal tag, so he did. Tom then demanded that BQ tag out to Stump, and they'll see who the better novelty item is. BQ tagged out to Stump, and now they both had their respective partners in the ring. Tom & BQ began talking trash, before Tom tossed the KF at BQ. BQ tossed Stump at Tom. They both looked down at their newfound items, before Tom laughed, and threw Stump into the crowd. BQ was now incensed, and threw the Karate Fighter from the ring. BQ then charged at Tom, and speared him into the mat, and the two started brawling. The referee however, ignored it, and began counting out the legal... participants. He did a traditional ten count (somewhat slow), before deciding to hurry the process up. Counting to ten, they were now officially eliminated from the match. The referee turned his attention to BQ & Tom, who were now out on the floor, going at it. The referee started up the ten count there as well. In the meantime, a familiar face began to walk down to the ring. It was Sphere. His hair was spiked, and stood up on end. Meanwhile, BQ & Tom had been counted out, so all that remained were The Red Dragon & Mike Heftel on one side, and The Dark Ninja & "Lavender Holyfield" on the other. Holyfield entered the ring, and Heftel looked at Dragon, before tagging him, telling him he was it. He then hopped off the ring apron and stood at ringside. Dragon entered with a look of disgust, and hooked up with Holyfield. Dragon took him down quickly with a dragon screw legwhip, and Holyfield was impressed. Sphere joined the commentators at ringside, and informed everyone he was now "Don Static", and was scouting talent for a boxing league he was starting up in Algiers. Holyfield & Dragon continued to do battle, with Holyfield getting an advantage, and backing Dragon into the corner. He peppered him with some punches, before looking at "Don Static", and trying to impress him with a swinging tornado punch. He caught nothing but air, as Dragon stood on the top rope, and flew off with a hurrancanrana, and hooking the legs for the quick pin. Holyfield stood up dazed in the corner, and was embarassed. Meanwhile, Dragon turned and faced the Dark Ninja, who flew off the top rope with a flying dropkick. He hit him, and Ninja & Dragon continued to do battle. Holyfield was now on the floor, and watched the action, catching his breath. Ninja & Dragon fought back and forth, with a brilliant exchange of moves. They went to the floor, and Dragon flew off for an Asai Moonsault, but Ninja moved. Ninja then rolled Dragon into the ring, and waited. Dragon got to his feet groggily, and was met with a jumping thrust kick to the chest, knocking him to the mat, and then covering him for the pin. He got it, as Heftel leaped into the ring a second too late. Ninja then brought Heftel up and hit a 'Diamond Cutter', before going to the top rope, and flying off with a legdrop. Ninja covered him for the pin, but at two, Holyfield entered the ring and grabbed Ninja off him from behind, before hitting a jumping powerbomb. Holyfield then grabbed Heftel and placed him on Ninja, and the referee made the count, which meant that Heftel would go onto the final Survivor match. Heftel stood dazed before BQ joined him in the ring, with Stump in his hands, as he retrieved it from the crowd. BQ & Heftel celebrated, but Heftel didn't know why he was celebrating. BQ informed Heftel that he would be competing against the elite of the fWo, and Heftel grimmaced, not looking forward to it at all... Unstable went to a sneak preview of next month's pay-per-view, "Halloween Hell"... Result: Final "Survivor": Mike Heftel Strategy, Part Two Unstable headed elsewhere in the arena, with the show now officially underway. Backstage, we saw Stone approaching a locker room... this time the lockerroom of the fWo International Champion "The Legend" Jon Spratt. She knocked on the door, and received no answer. She knocked again, before the door opened, and Spratt stood, pulling an elbowpad up his arm. Stone smiled, and Spratt seemed taken aback by her presence. "Hello Jon. We need to talk about tonight..." Spratt welcomed her into the locker room, and once again, the camera man was kept out... for it was a private matter. Unstable went back to the ring for another match. Erik Kelly, Wolf Fang, Robert Jensen & Frank Snow vs. Vengence, Juno Law & The Thrill Killers Eminem hits the speakers, and the team of The Thrill Killers, Juno Law, and the extremely big Vengence make their way down toward ringside. They walk down the ramp very business-like, and climb into the ring to await their opponents. Godsmack hits the speakers, and Frank Snow, along with Robert Jensen, come out onto the ramp. They wait for their partners in this match, and Wolf Fang doesn't make them wait for long. He makes his way out, and the three begin to make their way down the ramp. They stop short of the ring, and await the entrance of one of the biggest men in the sport today. Metallica hits the speakers, and the lights go black for the Eradicator. Erik Kelly makes his way out with this trademark cinderblock, before a sign caught his eye. "Erik, Say Goodbye to Block! Remember NIU!" came to Kelly's eyes from afar, and he looked at his cinderblock, before tossing it for this match. He walks down amid a standing ovation from the crowd, and he joins his teammates at the ring. They climb onto the apron, and Kelly, not waiting for the bell, immediately goes after Vengence. Vengence climbs out of the ring, and The Thrill Killers attempt to take him down. This prompts Jensen and Snow to attack them, and Law and Fang go at it. The ref, trying to break this up, gets pushed down in the melee. Kelly picks up Carnage and tosses him down on the ground. Jensen and Snow work together to hit a suplex on Rave. Fang hits a dropkick on Law, which sends him tumbling outside the ring. The four men stand inside the ring, and the crowd cheers hard for them. Rave climbs into the ring, and he meets up with Wolf Fang, who starts things out for his team. Rave and Fang lock up, with Rave getting the upperhand on an armbar. Fang flips out of it, and quickly hits a legsweep on Rave. Fang looks down to pick up Law, but Rave garners enough energy in the lower part of his body to hit a springboard dropkick on Fang. Rave lands on his hands, and Fang is jolted straight up. Rave wraps his legs around the neck of Fang, and hits a hurricarana. The crowd pops for the latest combination of moves, and Rave goes for a cover. At two, Fang kicks out. Rave moves over, and tags in Juno Law. Rave holds Fang, and Law leaps to the top rope. He then springs off, and nails Fang with a dropkick. However, for some odd reason, Rave lets go of Fang before Law kicks him, and Fang falls backwards toward his corner. He allows his momentum to carry him into his half of the ring, and he manages to tag in Robert Jensen. Jensen climbs in, and Law immediately locks up with him. Jensen powers Law to the mat, and then takes off toward the ropes. He runs over Law, and crosses the ropes again. This time, Law hits him with a clip, and takes him down. Law immediately wraps him into a sleeper, but Jensen gets up, and runs him into a corner. Law senses this, and makes a leap as they head into it. Jensen falls backwards into the corner, and Law, who someone continues to hold onto him, turns it into a top-rope bulldog. Law quickly covers Jensen, and gets a three count, and Jensen is quickly eliminated. Erik Kelly follows Jensen into the ring, and Law, seeing this, decides to tag in Carnage. Carnage looks at Law, and decides to tackle this challenge. Kelly goes to lock up with Carnage, but Carnage ducks it, and lands a right to the head of Kelly. Kelly isn't affected by this, and tosses him aside. Kelly looks at Vengence, and motions for him to come into the ring. Vengence looks at him, and begins to come in. Kelly gets ready, but then is felled by a dropkick by Carnage. Vengence laughs at him for being such an idiot, and Carnage gets the upper hand. Carnage immediately grabs the leg of Kelly, and locks him into a modified figure four. Kelly uses his power to get him to the ropes. Carnage looks at Vengence, and then turns to the ref to complain. Vengence, meanwhile, motions for Law to move Kelly out over the edge of the apron. Law obliges, and he moves the head of Kelly over the edge. Vengence lines him up, and flies off the apron. He extends his legs, and nails a legdrop onto Kelly, going right through him on his way to the floor. Kelly grabs his throat, obviously in pain. Carnage begins to ignore the ref, grabs Kelly, and goes for a cover. At two and a half, Kelly gets a hand up. Carnage picks Kelly up, and tosses him into the ropes. Kelly comes back, and Carnage goes for a dropkick. However, Kelly catches him by his throat, and lifts him in the air. It is then that Law comes in and takes out the legs of Kelly. Kelly lets go of Carnage, who crashes down to the mat face-first. Kelly falls down, and the ref tries to get Law out of the ring. Law moves the ref over by complaining, and Rave gets up onto the top rope. He lines up Kelly, and nails a moonsault, and goes for a cover. Law climbs out of the ring, while Carnage rolls out, and the ref spots Rave going for a cover on Kelly. The ref counts, and he gets two and a half before Kelly kicks out. Rave picks up Kelly, and tosses him into the ropes. Kelly bounces off the ropes to see Rave inexplicably lowering his head for some sort of power move. Kelly counters this move with by placing Rave's head in between his legs and lifting him up. Rave uses his momentum to get out of the move, but Kelly holds onto the head, hitting a neckbreaker that causes both men to go down. The ref begins to count both men out of the match, until Rave begins to get up. Kelly begins to do the same, and, as Rave tags in Law, Kelly tags in Frank Snow. Snow comes in with fists-a-fire, and he clobbers Law with a rope to the head. He picks him up, and whips Law into a corner. Snow comes charging towards him, but Law moves out of the way, and Snow lands headfirst into the corner. He bounces back, and Law whips him into the ropes, before tagging in Rave. Rave climbs to the top rope; meanwhile, Law goes for a clothesline, which Snow ducks. As soon as Snow's head comes back up, Rave takes flight, and nails him with a missile dropkick. He quickly covers, and gets the three count, ending Snow's night. Immediately Wolf Fang climbs in, ready to fight. However, Law comes back in the ring, and, as Rave gets to his feet, Law grabs his head and hits an implant DDT. The crowd, astounded by this, watches curiously as Law runs to a neutral corner. Law climbs to the top, and flies off almost as quickly as he climbed off. He extends his legs, and nails the Just Fierce on Rave. He gets up, points at Wolf Fang, and points down at Rave, telling him to pin him. Fang does, and the ref counts Rave out of the match. Law smiles and contemplates his latest move. However, he doesn't appear to have a long time to do so, as Carnage climbs in the ring, and kicks Law in the gut. He quickly nails a snap German suplex, and looks at Wolf Fang. Fang, a bit admonished as to what is happening, covers the fallen Juno Law. The ref counts Law out of the match, and he can't believe it. Carnage kicks Law out of the ring, and immediately he and Wolf Fang go at it. Carnage tackles Wolf Fang, and the two begin to trade punches. Carnage picks up Wolf Fang, and whips him into the ropes. Carnage lowers his head at the last second and nails Wolf Fang with a beautful back bodydrop, and he quickly goes for a cover. The ref counts, but only gets two. Carnage picks up Wolf Fang, and signals for the Maximum Carnage. He locks Wolf Fang in for a snap German suplex, and nails it. Carnage gets up and turns to the top rope. He climbs to the top rope, and points to the crowd. He takes off, and angles his elbow down towards Wolf Fang. Fang, however, moves out of the way. Carnage lands on the mat at full force, and literally bounces up from the impact. He crumples over, and Wolf Fang makes it over towards Kelly, who gets tagged. Kelly walks over to Carnage, picks him up, and locks him in for the Eradication. "POWERBOMB" is heard throughout the entire arena, and the fans climb to their feet. He picks up Carnage, and hits a major powerbomb. Kelly gets the three count, and Vengence immediately climbs into the ring. As he is coming for Kelly, Wolf Fang tries to catch him off-guard with by charging him and trying to land a spinning heel kick. Vengence moves away from the kick, and catches Wolf Fang around the throat. Vengence quickly picks up Fang, and drives him to the mat. He covers Fang, and the ref counts the three. With Wolf Fang eliminated, it's now Kelly and Vengence, finally. Vengence charges Kelly, and the two lock up. Vengence lays a right hand to the face of Kelly. Kelly retalliates with one of his own. Vengence goes to plant another one, but Kelly ducks it, and spins Vengence around. He picks him up, and lands an atomic drop. As Vengence bounces off and up into the air, Kelly takes off for the ropes. He sizes Vengence up, and nails a bulldog. He goes for a cover, but gets two and a half before Vengence kicks out. Vengence climbs to his feet, and lowblows Kelly. Kelly bends down quickly, and Vengence locks him in for a powerbomb. Vengence picks up the massive Kelly, and runs towards the ropes. Vengence nails a running powerbomb, and goes for a cover. The ref counts. One. Two. Thre...no. Kelly managed to get a foot on the ropes. Vengence, obviously upset by this, glares at the ref, and then advances on him. The ref steps back, and then ducks out of the way of Vengence. Vengence advances on him again. This time, Vengence grabs hold of the ref. As he is about to lift him up in the air, there's a tap on his shoulder. Vengence turns around, and is met with a boot in his gut. He pulls him in for the Eradication, but Vengence blocks it, and backdrops out of it. Kelly however holds on, and pulls Vengence down for the sunset flip. Hooking the legs, the referee counts to three, and Kelly will go onto the final "survivor" type match later on in the evening. Kelly rolls to the floor, before he holds his arms up in the air mightily, as the fans give him yet another standing ovation. Kelly & Vengence stare a hole through each other as the announcers talk about tomorrow's night big World Title Match between Scott Slugger & Wretch. Result: Final "Survivor": Rain The Flying Frenchie, fWo Cruiserweight Champion Crusader & fWo W vs. fWo Women's Champion Primetime, Greg Davis & The California King Unstable heads to the ring for one of its big elimination matches, one that the fans are definitely pumped for. Back in the ring, Kelly turned around and saw Frenchie & Slugger going at it now. Slugger whipped Frenchie to the ropes, but Frenchie reversed it. Slugger didn't run to the ropes however, he ran right into a big boot on the part of Kelly. Wretch was now back up, and he slid into the ring. Frenchie charged at Kelly, leaping at him with a high cross body. Kelly caught him however, and then brought him into the center of the ring. Wretch however took a chance, and dove with a chop block. Kelly crumpled to the mat, and now Frenchie was atop Kelly in a pinning position. One. Two. Three. The Eradicator couldn't muster up enough strength to kick out due to the pain he was in from the chop block, and now it was down to just four men, as Kelly limped to the back, somewhat shaken up. The fWo World Champion - Scott Slugger. The fWo Internet Champion and number one contender to the World Title - Wretch Multiple champions The Flying Frenchie & Primetime. Something had to give. Slugger & Wretch paired off. Frenchie & Primetime did the same. PT & Frenchie brawled to the floor, and then into the crowd. Inside the ring, Slugger & Wretch wrestled in somewhat of a warm-up match for tomorrow night's World Title contest. Slugger sent Wretch into a corner, and Wretch staggered out into a belly to belly suplex. Slugger then brought Wretch up and hit one powerbomb... and then another. He lifted for the third, but Wretch turned it into a backdrop. Slugger tried to turn it into a sunset flip, but the young fan favorite sat down for a pinfal attempt. One. Two. Slugger used his lower body strength to turn it into a pin for him, sunset flip style. One. Two. Wretch sprung up, and rolled atop Slugger for a pin. One. Two. Slugger began to bridge out of it, and then turned it into a backslide. One. Two. Wretch kicked out. Back outside, Primetime slammed Frenchie face first into a nearby wall, as they continued to fight in the crowd. Wretch brought Slugger to his feet and sent him to the ropes, Slugger reversed it. Wretch returned and ducked the Slugger clothesline. Wretch bounced off the ropes, and the duo charged, clotheslining each other simultaneously. Both men were now dazed on the mat. Primetime grabbed Frenchie in a headlock and brought him back near the guardrail, slamming him head first into that, or at least attempting to. Instead, Frenchie blocked, then elbowed out of it. Frenchie then grabbed Primetime and pressed him, dropping him chest first on the guardrail. Frenchie posed for the fans with a trademark beam, before grabbing Primetime and sending him over the guardrail onto the floor. Frenchie however, saw an opportunity to eliminate someone from the match. He climbed to the top rope, looking to fly off on Slugger with the guilotine legdrop. However, someone spoiled this from the crowd. Mitch Wilson. Wilson grabbed Slugger by the leg as Pierre Delacroix flew off, instead of hitting the WGA, he hit the mat with an impending thud. Wilson then slammed Slugger face first on the steel steps, before seeing a groggy Primetime standing dazed over by one of the many announce tables. Not knowing Wilson was coming up from behind, Primetime found himself locked for another Headliner. He couldn't do anything about it. Wilson quickly hit the move, driving one of his arch rivals through the table. He then challenged Primetime to a match for tomorrow night's Slaughter for the Women's Title; before grabbing Primetime and rolling him into the ring. Wilson then grabbed a chair and as Frenchie climbed to his feet, Wilson cracked him over the back with it, before hitting a DDT on the chair. Wilson then grabbed Wretch and placed him ontop of Primetime, and demanding the referee count. He did. One. Two. Three. Primetime was gone. Wilson then grabbed Wretch and draped his arm over Frenchie, before leaving to a chorus of boos from the crowd. One. Two. Three. Wilson had just successfully screwed over his former French Foundation cohorts, something he intended to do earlier in the evening, but didn't accomplish. Mission achieved, he walked to the back, dodging the trash that was thrown at him, as security and medical personnel tended to Frenchie & Primetime. Meanwhile, Slugger saw that it was down to he and his opponent for tomorrow night's Sunday Night Slaughter in Seattle, Washington. Slugger climbed to the top rope, and as Wretch groggily stood to his feet, he saw Slugger flying through the air for the Grand Slam. He couldn't do anything about it, as it was too late. Slugger hit it, and then quickly crawled over for the count. One. Two. Digging down deep, Wretch got a shoulder up. Slugger was shocked, and he brought him to his feet. After sending him to the ropes, Wretch returned and was met with a kick to the stomach, and then Slugger tried the triple powerbomb combo. After the first one, however, Wretch spun out of it into a facebuster, ala Billy Kidman, who is not related to Wretch, for what it's worth. Wretch hooked a leg for a count, which was a closer nearfall at two and a half. Wretch was a half count away from winning the match, and giving him some confidence of beating the certain fWo Hall Of Famer. Wretch brought Slugger up, and hit a Wretchinoku Driver. One. Two. But Slugger kicked out. Wretch decided to take a gamble and go up top, reaching bag into his tricks from his Cruiserweight days. Wretch flew off as Slugger climbed to his feet, hitting a missle dropkick. Square in the jaw. Cover. Kickout. Wretch was worried. He was pulling out all the stops, but had one more left. Reverse DDT. The Depression. He waited as Slugger got to his feet, and from behind, hooked him. Slugger avoided it however, going behind Wretch and then hitting an elevated reverse DDT. The Perfect Game. Slugger looked to the crowd and signalled that it was over, and he crawled over for the cover. A hook of the leg. One. Two. But Wretch kicked out. Slugger was angry. He hooked both legs, but Wretch kicked out again. Flabbergasted, Slugger knew only one 'finisher' of his remained, and he knew it would take that to win this match. The Curveball. Slugger brought Wretch up and took him into the corner. As he hooked him for it, he stared into the sea of fans, wondering if this would be it. But this gave Wretch an opportunity to escape. With Slugger on the second rope, Wretch reached up, put Slugger on his back with his free arm, and then hooked him for a Samoan Drop. He fell back, and hit it. He then wasted no time in going for the cover. One. Two. Three. It happened so quickly. Slugger kicked out, but it was a milisecond too late. Wretch had done it. Wretch had beaten the almighty Scott Slugger. Wretch had momentum heading into the World Title Match. And Slugger was left a broken man, literally. His spirits had fallen considerably. First, Strangler snapping again. And now, this. Wretch stood and celebrated his arms raised. Slugger couldn't kick out of a Samoan Drop. And perhaps for once in his career, Slugger was showing signs of doubt. Would he have what it takes to defeat Wretch tomorrow night... would the World's Greatest Athlete be able to even compete? An injured arm, no doubt a concussion - it was all on the line. One thing was for certain. Slugger would be there. Wretch would be there. The World Title would be on the line. And at the end of the night, we'd either see a bonafide legend with his arm raised and the World Title hung over his shoulder - or we would see an up and coming star on the rise with the fWo World Title around his waist for the first (and certainly not the last) time? The answer to that question would be revealed in just twenty four hours. Result: Final Survivor: Wretch by pinfall
