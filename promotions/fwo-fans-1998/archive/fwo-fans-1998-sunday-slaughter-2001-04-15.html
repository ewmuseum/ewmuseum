Sunday Slaughter, 15 April 2001

15 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The assemblage of talent standing in front of Chris Universal was truly tremendous. Buck Naked. Matt Sterbaiter. Humpapotamus. Gay Timmy. Dick Strong. The Penetrator. The very best the EWF had to offer. Universal looked them over skeptically. Naked spoke up. "So, are you gonna sign us or not?" Universal looked at Naked. Looked down at the contracts on his desk. Looked at Naked. Looked down at the contracts on his desk. "Uh... I'll think about it. Don't call us, we'll call you." Satisfied with this answer, the EWF group left the room. This would prove to be their biggest mistake even bigger than asking for a contract in the first place. As they exited, they were overtaken by a whirlwind of pain. Mitch Wilson appeared out of nowhere, World Title in hand. He quickly laid out each EWF wrestler with a belt shot, dropping them like flies. The look on Wilson's face spoke volumes pure hate and fury. Wilson picked up Naked, then threw him literally head-first through a wall, which shattered. He picked up Sterbaiter and dragged him down the hall, then threw him through a window into a room where he landed in a splatter of glass and blood. Wilson stalked back and grabbed a chair. He placed it around the ankle of Humpapotamus, then leapt onto it. A cracking sound was heard, and Humpapotamus squealed in pain. You get the picture. Gay Timmy? Head busted open on the floor. Dick Strong? Headlinered on a nearby chair. Finally, The Penetrator. Wilson dropped down, then hooked the arm of the Penetrator. He applied the Luminary Lock, and the Penetrator screamed in pain. Wilson simply continued to pull back on the hold, then gestured for the cameraman to drop down and put the camera in his face. "BEAR! I don't know where the hell you are or why the hell you aren't here, but let me just tell you that by not showing up you personally sacrificed these six poor sons of bitches! It's not gonna stop here, Bear, oh no! From this day, you, everybody in the Underground, and everybody who crosses my path is one thing... a TARGET! Look, listen and learn Bear... look, listen and learn Wretch... at CyberSlam, this is whichever one of you is unlucky enough to beat the other!" Wilson then pulled back violently on the Penetrator's arm, and a loud crack echoed through the night, blending in with the Penetrator's howl of pain... Sphere vs. Rottdogg "Back To School" by the Deftones hits and the fans start booing before Sphere even walks from out behind the curtains. A wicked smile on his face, he enters the ring, and awaits his opponent. Rottdogg walks out slowly to, flanked by the rest of NXS. None of them look too happy to be here, but the fans don't seem to notice as they meet Rottdogg with a small pop. With NXS on the outside, Rottdogg enters this Hardcore rules match, confidently, against Sphere. The two start to brawl as soon as Rottdogg gets inside, and Rottdogg uses his slight size advantage to gain early dominance. But, it doesn't last long. Sphere fires back with a couple of quick rights and a headbutt which sends Rottdogg flying backward, nearly off his feet. Angrily, Rottdogg attempts to clothesline Sphere, but misses and goes flying off the ropes. On his way back, he's met with an elbow from Sphere, which nearly takes off his head. Now, laying on the ground, Rottdogg manages to open his eyes just in time to be met, square in the nose, with Sphere's knee. Yelping in pain, Rottdogg manages to get back to his feet, where he's met with a superkick that sends him flying back to the mat. The ref gets a long two, before Rottdogg manages to get his shoulder up. A sadistic smile on his face, Sphere picks up Rottdogg by his hair. Having a complete advantage, Sphere hits Spinal Retraction, which should put an end to the match quickly. The ref's arm hits the canvas another two times, before he notices that Rottdogg's leg is on the ropes. He signals to Sphere this, and Sphere looks up rudely. Upset about not having the pinfall, he approaches the referee, and starts harassing him, insulting him, and viciously bumping the ref with his stomach. The ref keeps stepping back, afraid, until he sees a figure move out of the corner of his eye. Rottdogg is back on his feet. Seeing the small smile on the ref's face, Sphere turns around quickly, just in time to be met by Rottdogg. Rottdogg nails him with a fisherman's suplex and quickly goes for the cover. Even with the ref's slightly quicker than normal count, Rottdogg only manages to keep Sphere down for a two. Picking Sphere up, Rottdogg swings wildly at him, almost taking off his head. Luckily, Sphere has the presence of mind to duck, and Rottdogg misses him by mere inches. With Rottdogg off balance, Sphere's quick kick to the midsection sends him sprawling to the mat. Another cover by Sphere leads only to a one-and-a-half count by the referee. Once again, rising angrily, Sphere targets his frustration toward the referee. Up to this point, he's dominated the match, but has been unable to put Rottdogg away. This time, knowing Rottdogg is behind him, Sphere hits a back elbow on him, and follows it up with another hard kick to the midsection. A hair-splitting kick to Rottdogg's face sends him in the opposite direction. As Rottdogg charges at him, Sphere moves his body out of the way, and smashes his opponent viciously with a headbutt. With Rottdogg laid out on the mat, Sphere decides to go high-risk. After climbing a turnbuckle, he launches himself off the top rope, and splashes the motionless Rottdogg. Realizing there's no chance Rottdogg will be able to mount an offense, Sphere goes to the outside, in search of a chair. With the ref's eyes peeled to NXS, they can do little to stop Sphere, as he picks up a chair from ringside and re-enters the ring. The prone Rottdogg gets hammered with chairshot, after chairshot, after chairshot. Finally, after the fifth shot, Sphere becomes bored with it, and throws the chair away. Deciding against taking Rottdogg to the outside, for fear of NXS interference, Sphere decides to end the match quickly. Not finished with his opponent, Sphere sets him up, yet again, for his trademark move, his finisher Spinal Retraction. Putting the icing on the cake, a cake that's been baked practically since the match started, Rottdogg is nailed with Spinal Retraction. With all the abuse he's taken so far, there's no way Rottdogg's going to kick out of this one. Sphere knows this too, and with a lackluster cover, manages to pin Rottdogg. The crowd dislikes the end result, and boo, while Rottdogg is being helped out of the ring by his NXS teammates. Soon after Rottdogg is helped to the outside, evidence of the disdain NXS has for losing comes in full force. All of them yell at each other, as they walk up the ramp. In the ring, Sphere watches them with a smile on his face. The smile doesn't stay there for long. Jackhammer comes from behind Sphere and starts to pound away on his former friend and teammate. Punch after punch sends Sphere down to the mat. After he gets back to his feet, he backs up against the ropes. A bad move on his part. Jackhammer just runs forward and nails Sphere, sending him flying over the top rope, and tumbling to the floor. Jackhammer raises his arms in victory as Slaughter heads backstage... Result: Sphere by pinfall One Last Chance Chris Universal looked up from Baseball Tonight (which was showing the highlights of yet another Mariners victory) when he heard the knock on the door. "Come in." Eddy Fate stepped into his office. "Hi, Eddy, good to see you. What can I do for you?" "Well, mate, it's real simple. I want one last chance at Greg Davis. I want him here, I want him now. Like I said, simple." Universal frowned. "I'm sorry, Eddy, but I can't give you that. I hope you understand I gotta save that for the pay-per-view. You know what I'm talking about, right?" Fate nodded unhappily. "Yeah, I know what you mean. But you know where I'm coming from too, right? I mean, I'm sure that at some point in your career there's been some bloke who's just pissed you off to the point where you just want to beat the everloving crap out of him, then give him a good stiff kick in the bollocks for good measure? I mean, you know that feeling, right?" Universal's eyes darkened. "Yeah... yeah, I know that feeling. All too well." But the man who had caused those feelings was long gone, and this was not the time for Universal to re-hash old memories. He had a show to run. "But still, I can't give you the match. Sorry, Eddy. You do have a match, though. Against War. In fact..." Universal looked down at the schedule on his desk. "That's next. Good luck, Eddy." "Thanks, mate." Fate shook with Universal, then turned and exited the room, clearly unhappy about the decision but understanding nonetheless. Eddy Fate vs. War "You Can't Deny Fate" echoes through the arena, followed by "Beautiful People". The fans pop as Eddy Fate makes his way to the ring. He slingshots himself over the top rope and awaits his opponent. "Paranoid" begins, and Plague makes his way to the ring, accompanied by War and Stone. War rushes the ring and slides in, then goes right after Fate with a lariat. Fate ducks, and War bounces off the ropes. Fate leaps and nails War with a leaping leg lariat, knocking him to the mat. Fate drops a quick leg and makes a cover, but only gets a one-count. Fate brings War back up to his feet and whips him into the corner. He backs up across the ring and goes for a charging splash, but Stone pulls War to the outside and Fate only hits the turnbuckles. War rolls back into the ring as the ref warns Stone. War nails Fate with a low blow, doubling him over, then puts Fate between his legs. He lifts him up, going for a powerbomb, but Fate fights back with a series of fists to the cranium. War staggers, and Fate uses his leg strength to flip War over with a hurricanrana. Fate covers, and gets two before War kicks out. Fate brings War back up to his feet and goes for a kick, but War catches his leg. Undaunted, Fate leaps up and pastes War with an enzuiguri, knocking him to the mat. War crumples to the mat, his eyes glassy, and Fate covers again. He again gets two before War kicks out, this time with authority. Fate quickly gets back up, and so does War. Fate grabs War and whips him into the ropes, but War reverses it. As Fate comes back off, War catches him and slams him hard to the mat with a powerslam. War takes a moment to shake things off, then covers and hooks a leg. Fate kicks out at two. War brings Fate up to his feet, then kicks him in the gut hard, doubling him over. War begins clubbing away on Fate with massive, slab-like forearms that rain down on Fate's back with thunderous impact. Finally, Fate has had enough and one knocks him to the mat, where he lands face-down. War drops a leg, then rolls Fate over for a cover. He gets two, then Eddy kicks out to cheers from the crowd. War brings Fate back up to his feet, then whips him hard into the corner where Fate hits with ring-rocking impact. War measures Fate, then charges in with a gigantic lariat. War steps back, and Fate stumbles out. War stalks up behind Fate, then hooks him in a full nelson. He then lifts Fate high in the air, slams him to the mat, and sits down. Sit-down full nelson slam. War moves, then covers Fate as he falls back to the mat. The ref drops down to make the count. Fate gets a shoulder up at the last second, to the obvious disdain of everybody in the Rider party. War snarls, then brings Fate up again. He whips Fate into the corner a second time, backs up, and charges in. Fate uses the ropes and lift himself up, then spins and hooks War's arms with his legs, rolling him into a sunset flip position. The ref counts two and is almost at three before War kicks out. Both men quickly get back up and War reasserts his authority with a big back elbow to the face. Fate hits the mat hard, and War quickly brings him back up and places him in between his legs. War lifts Fate up, then spikes him with a piledriver. War smiles and signals that it's about to be over. He lifts Fate up, then stands behind him. War grabs Fate's legs and trips him to the mat, then lifts him up, face-down. The Exploder is coming up. War lifts Fate all the way up. Fate shifts his weight, then rolls down in a modified victory roll. The ref drops down to count. One. Two. Three. Fate pulls victory from the jaws of defeat, and the crowd pops. War is seriously angry, and gets right back up. He tries to clothesline Fate, but Fate ducks it, kicks him in the gut, and hooks his head. He then drops War with the Twist of Fate. Plague hits the ring, and the cycle repeats itself. Charge. Duck. Kick. Hook. Twist of Fate. Chaos rolls from the ring, clearly defeated, and Stone stares into the ring. Fate dares her to come get it too, but she decides discretion is the better part of valor and declines. Fate stands in the ring, victorious, as Slaughter goes backsta But wait. On the SlaughterScreen, an image appeared. Greg Davis, outside the arena, crowbar in hand. He stood by a car Eddy Fate knew all too well. A 1998 Saturn SC-2, dark purple, Ohio license plate "EFATE". Eddy's car. "Hi, Eddy!" said the Cruiserweight Champion. "If you're wondering, I'm here to commence the festivities on the latest chapter of that grand fWo tradition, Destroy Somebody's Car Night! Tonight, the car is yours! Lucky you, Eddy!" With that, Davis began to swing the crowbar and connect with the car. Panels dented. Windows shattered. Fate ran. Fate disappeared backstage at a dead run, and only seconds passed before Fate showed up on the monitor. He charged towards Fate, and was blindsided immediately by One and Two. The beatdown commenced, and Davis joined in with his crowbar in true A.S.S. fashion. The beating showed no signs of stopping as Slaughter finally went backstage... Result: Eddy Fate by pinfall Why? In the STUMP Squad locker room, four figures were visible. Black Quicksilver, Mike Heftel, Claire... and oh yeah, STUMP. Claire (standing) was talking to BQ and Heftel (sitting). "So why exactly do I have to be there? I mean, it's just another match, right?" BQ shook his head kindly. "Not exactly. It's CyberSlam, Claire! The biggest show of the year! Where legends become heroes become... icons... become... cult leaders, or whatever... or something! The point is, it's big!" Heftel nodded. "Big." "It's as big as it gets!" "Big as it gets." "You gotta be there!" "Gotta be there." "And so on!" "So on." "You see what I mean, Claire?" She thought it over a moment, then nodded. "Yeah, I suppose. Alright guys, I'm there. Guest timekeeper, right?" "Right," said BQ. "..." said STUMP (sitting). "Oh crap! Our match! Let's go!" Heftel and Quicksilver both leapt to their feet and ran. They bumped into each other, turned, bumped into each other again, paused to allow BQ to pick up STUMP, then sprinted out the door, leaving Claire behind, shaking her head and smiling. Black Panther/Black Quicksilver vs. Havok/Mike Heftel fWo Sunday Night Slaughter returns from the commercial breaks to the raucous crowd enjoying themselves at the Ice Palace in Tampa, Florida. 'Slave To The Grind' by Skid Row blasts throughout the arena, and out comes Black Quicksilver, with STUMP. BQ slides into the ring and plays to the crowd, before the crowd' reaction turns to boos, as The Black Panther' music starts up. 'Du Hast' by Rammstein rocks the arena, and out walks the Black Panther. The Panther climbs into the ring via over the top rope, before staring a hole in his unwelcomed partner, Black Quicksilver. Panther finds a corner and remains there, not caring about the reaction he received from the crowd. 'Down With The Ship' by Scatterbrain brings out one man, and one man only. Mike Heftel. The Florida crowd pumps their fists in the air as the man of a thousand gimmicks makes his way out to the ring. Heftel climbs in and he and Black Quicksilver nod at one another. 'Awake' by Godsmack brings out Havok, who has the biggest match of his career awaiting him at CyberSlam in two weeks. Havok climbs in and plays to the crowd, but he is blindsided by The Black Panther, who charges and spears him into the mat. BQ & Heftel look at one another and ascertain that perhaps since they're fighting at CyberSlam, they might as well fight now. Heftel charges at BQ, who returns the charge. The two collide into one another and go flying to the mat. Panther brings Havok off the match and throws him over the top rope onto the cement floor, before waiting for Mike Heftel to get up. Heftel does, and Panther grabs him and throws him to the outside as well. The referee, not pleased with the Panther' conduct, begins jawing with him and instructing him back to a corner. Meanwhile, a dazed BQ finds himself getting off the mat, before turning and spotting Havok on the top rope. Havok comes flying off the ropes with a high cross body, and it connects. However, the momentum carries BQ atop Havok for a pinning combination. At two, Heftel pulls the referee out onto the floor. When confronted about his act, Heftel says the ring announcer did it. Not buying the story, the referee gives Heftel a stern warning. The referee returns to the ring and BQ & Havok duke it out in the ring, exchanging a flurry of punches with one another. BQ grabs Havok by the arm and sends him to the ropes. Havok reverses it however, and ducks for a backdrop. Black Quicksilver telegraphs it, hooking Havok for a spinning neckbreaker. Quicksilver goes for a cover, but Havok kicks out at two. BQ walks over and tags in the Panther, who had basically been pleading with BQ to give him the tag. BQ holds Havok up for Panther, but The Panther pushes him aside, and grabs Havok, nailing a snap suplex on the so-called 'Millennium Man'. Panther goes for a cover, but Heftel comes in and interrupts it by pushing the referee gently, rather than going after the Panther directly. Heftel feared a lot of things, but getting his ass kicked by the referee wasn't a concern. Getting his ass kicked by the seven foot Panther, however, was a concern. Heftel returned to his spot on the ring apron, and Panther scoffed at Heftel, before bringing Havok up to inflict more punishment. Panther whips Havok to the ropes, and Havok returns charging right into a big boot from the Panther. Havok crumples to the mat as a result, but the Panther is not finished. He drops a big elbow on his prey, and another. Finally, Panther tags out to BQ, and tells BQ to hit one of his twisty-twirly type maneuvers. BQ nods, and heads up top. Quicksilver flies off with a senton bomb, leveling the prone Havok. BQ covers him, but Havok again kicks out. BQ brings Havok up and sends him to the ropes, before going for a simple dropkick. Havok, however, holds onto the ropes, and BQ finds himself on the mat as a result. Havok reaches over and tags out Heftel, and Panther sighs in frustration. Heftel charges, but finds Panther there to cut him off with a big boot. Panther then stares down at BQ, and tells him to finish it. BQ nods with hesitation, before going up to the top rope once again. Quicksilver flies off with his patented 'Silver Splash', but Heftel somehow moves out of the way. BQ hits the mat. Hard. Heftel, not knowing what to do next, pushes BQ onto his back, before climbing upon him. One. Two. Three. Heftel wins. Heftel wins. Heftel wins. Heftel stands in awe of what just occurred. He had, after all, won a match. The 10th win of his career, to be exact. The referee raised his arm, before the Panther came in, looking pissed. Panther grabbed Heftel and laid him out with a pumphandle slam. Havok charged, but Panther grabbed him by the hair and sent him flying over the top rope in one swift movement, discarding him like a bag of trash in a dumpster twenty feet away from him. Finally, Panther looked down at his partner, and called him a joke, before climbing over the top rope, and exiting the ring the same way he came. His eyes told the story. And his eyes revealed that in two weeks, Havok was a dead man. Chris Titan rammed his head repeatedly against the wall of his locker room, with Clarisse sitting nearby. On this night, he was scheduled to wrestle. His wrists were taped and he was physically ready for his match, but the psychological games that Crusader was playing with him were starting to take their toll. He wasn't afraid to fight Crusader. He knew that he could easily beat him on any given day, but there was so much more to their bitter hatred for one another. "WHY, Clarisse? YOU know him better than I do! Tell me why he's constantly trying to fuck with our heads!!" He was yelling again. She pushed a strand of her hair behind her ear and took a breath. "This is *exactly* what he wants, Chris. Just keep your cool; we'll be all right. Head into CyberSlam and beat Cody. That's it, and we're free from him." She approached him with an embrace from behind. He turned around and kept her a distance away and held her by the arms. She felt his grip was rather firm, but she didn't want to struggle. He eyed her. "That is what you've been telling me for these past couple of weeks, but all he does is continue his little games. Tell me, Clarisse; are you in on all of this with him? Are you two planning some kind of a conspiracy? Is that it?" Before she could answer, he gripped tighter and shook her once. "Did you HEAR ME?" "Stop... yelling... please..." He let go of her and sent her back a few feet. "Clarisse, I gave up so much just to be with you. Sure, I got humiliated. Yeah, people thought I was some kind of an old-fashioned, corny joke. I sacrificed the life of glamour and glitz that came with being a big star. I came to the fans Wrestling organization hoping that I'd find true love, hoping that I'd find you. You are the only person that I care about here." She rubbed her bare upper arms, which were red from his tight grip. "You DARE tell me what you had to sacrifice. You wanted true love? I already had it with Cody, or at least I thought I did. When I remembered you and saw you here, I took it as a sign. I took a chance and left Cody. I gave up my whole world, and you don't see that! And now... you're thinking that I still love Cody? I left him for YOU! What kind of a whore do you think I am?" He stood there, tight-lipped. There was no doubt in his mind that she crossed the line. She took the deepest words he had ever spoken and turned them against him. The back of his right hand caught Clarisse on the side of the face. She absorbed the effect of the shocking slap, and fell down on the couch. Tears began to fall down her cheeks, not so much from the pain, but more so from the emotional effect of her lover's action. He shook his head at what he did and reached for a tissue, before handing it to her. He pushed her hair out of her face. "Your fucking mascara is running again. We're on in five minutes. Get it cleaned up... I'm not going out there with a teary-eyed sap." Not wanting to deal with her anymore, Titan headed for the quarter-opened door, where he saw Crusader's face. Immediately, he pulled it open and grabbed Crusader by the shirt. "What the fuck did you see?" Crusader didn't respond. Enraged by his silence, Titan slammed him against the wall. "Speak." When no answer was heard, Titan sent a fist right to Crusader's face. Crusader shook off the effects of the punch and stood at a five-foot distance from his nemesis. Officials had poured into the hallway, but none of them were ready or willing to get in between Titan and Crusader, for this was something that was to be settled with those two only. "Are you going to hit me? Or are you just going to turn tail and drive off of a fucking highway? I don't mind either way, but the latter option would make my job at CyberSlam much easier!" Crusader clenched his fists... Mr. Lee and his sister Amy appeared, but they showed no signs of stopping him. They only watched, as a group of workers came to watch the confrontation. Titan waved Crusader toward him. Crusader walked away.
