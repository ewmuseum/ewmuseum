fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 23 January 2000

23 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Slaughter opened inside the arena after the opening video. The sound of fireworks screaming throughout the arena leaving a circle of smoke near the stage was heard. The cameras zoomed in for a focus of the crowd and the thousands upon thousands of fans for the first card in fWo history in London, England. Some fans held signs proclaiming their love or hatred for various wrestlers high above their head, while other fans held signs proclaiming they were "with stupid", or their name and "3:16" underneath. As they did, a red spotlight shone on the stage underneath the SlaughterScreen. As "Beautiful People" began to play, several FTfWo banners above the ring area unfurled. The FTfWo flashed onto the screen, while the rest of the arena went dark. Out from the backstage area stepped the entire FTfWo. Dead & Perfect lead the others, and Dead as usual was attired in his patented trenchcoat. Dead smirked at the booing fans as he walked steadily to the ring, while Perfect stared at the audience with smugness printed on his face. The FTfWo entered the ring, and as they did, Dead's theme came to a halt. Dead produced a microphone from his jacket, and as the crowd quieted down, he began to speak. "Silence! Tonight... is a historic night for several reasons. The first of which... your hero... the fWo's knight in shining armor... will have his career and quite possibly his life come to an end. The second... when I defeat the Strangler and rid the fWo of his filthy presence, a special referee will be counting the pin. And that special referee will be... Chris Universal. Strangler, tomorrow when you wake up in a hospital, it will be courtesy of me... with him just there to slap the mat. Take your anger out on me if you wish, but leave Universal alone. He's finally realized that the fWo is nothing... just as you will in the morning. The third reason... tonight's Slaughter will be unlike any Slaughter in the history of the fWo. Friendships will crumble, relationships will end... and by the night's end, everything should be an unruly mess. Perfect and I have taken the liberty of thinking up several matches since all of you seem to be expressing your anger and hatred for us. Hopefully, this will prevent you all from messing around with us in the future. Rather than express your anger towards us, perhaps you should express it to your opponents tonight. Because the majority of you will be facing someone you're familiar with. First of all... Mike Bear, getting our attention was a very bad thing. You want to screw with us? No, screw you and your "underground". Tonight, you will meet Fear & Wretch in a three-way dance. And if you don't... you're fired. Your jobs depend on this match. Compete in it, or lose your jobs. As an added bonus, by winning your match, you will take your name off of our "people to piss off" list. Scott will tell you about the next match, won't you, Scott?" Dead handed the microphone to Perfect, and Perfect looked at the crowd with amusement in his eyes. "You all... really... smell, you know that? Jesus, when was the last time any of you took a shower? Do you guys even have showers in this pathetic country? At any rate... tonight, Collins... you want to piss me off? That's really not a wise thing to do. No, it really isn't. Hmm. I bet you're regretting that about right now, aren't you? Shrug. Oh well. Too bad for you, because tonight you're going to face too people you're quite fondish of. Tonight, you will be meeting Plague... War... and your pimp, Stone in an elimination match. We'll see how you like that. Maybe that'll teach you not to stick your nose where it doesn't belong." Perfect passed the microphone back to Dead, who grinned. "Hey, that's going to be a really excellent match. VL, I saw your little comments about not giving a shit about anybody in the fWo. Well, I think it's pretty safe for me to say no one in this organization cares about you, either. And I don't care that you put your body on the line last night and could have died in that match. Perhaps the fWo would be a better place if you had." Dead paused for effect, and the fans responded with a loud boo. "No, the fWo would still suck... with or without you. At any rate, tonight you will be teaming up with Hellrazor... why? Well, you guys are both a little... I don't know, nuts... and tonight, your two opponents are nuts themselves. We'll see how you fair against the tandem of Ultra-Violet and Kid Cool. Next up, just for our own personal amusement... it wouldn't be fair if we left the new World Champion off of the card for tonight... would it? So... we were thinking maybe... Frenchie should take on two people he's quite familiar with. But there will be no hugging or celebrations for them tonight. You see, whether they like it or not, the French Foundation will be in a three-way dance tonight against themselves. That's right. Frenchie versus Primetime versus Courage. Same rules applies to the other matches... you win the match, you get crossed off our hitlist. You lose... well, you're pretty much screwed. As if that weren't enough for you to handle, well... this Wednesday, Frenchie, will be your first fWo World Title defense. Pretty amazing, considering you've been a two-time champion and have defended the title a meager total of ONE TIME, eh? Perhaps you should stop blaming your losses on Rain and not being eliminated in that three-way dance over a year ago... the fact is, you lost. A loss is a loss is a loss, pal. Unfortunately for you, you won't know until later tonight who your opponent will be... 'cause tonight, the person you will meet will have to EARN their shot... we won't be giving them a shot. Tonight, in... this... very... ring, a twenty-man battle royal will occur, and whoever wins will face you this Wednesday at Warfare. And the twenty lucky participants are: The Black Panther, Havok, Ice, that homeless bastard David Spencer, Living, Heaven, Hell, Eron The Talentless, Hiroshima, Rudo, that freak Sid Snow, Matt Douglas, Mike Heftel, Cactus Jon, Primetime, Courage, Scott Collins, Briana, Oracle & Chris Universal. Good luck, boys... and girls. Oh, one last thing, should the Boston Strangler do the impossible and defeat me here tonight, and let's face it, the chances of that happening aren't very good... should this be our last night of power, well... we're going to take out an insurance policy for ourselves. That's right. At some point in the future, whenever WE decide, we both will receive a shot at the fWo World Title. And at any point in the future, we may put this into affect... whether you like it or not. Have a nice day, and enjoy seeing the fWo crumble here tonight!" As Dead finished that sentence, he threw the microphone out near the announcers. Dead & Perfect raised their arms into the air, and exited the ring along with the other members of the FTfWo as "Humans Being" played throughout the arena. Fans appeared to be quite displeased as the results of friends having to face each other or team with an enemy... it was quite obvious that the night of action was intended for the fWo to go to war with themselves... and they could do nothing about it. Ultra Violet & Kid Cool vs. VL & Hellrazor Slaughter continued after a short commercial break. As it did, "Shock Ones II" by Mobb Deep began to play. VL Tha Murdera limped out onto the stage with her ribs bandaged; and heard a loud ovation of cheers from the crowd as they had remembered Invasion! from the night before. VL took two drops off the scaffold, one of them through two tables in the ring below. The fans appreciated her work ethic with a round of applause, which VL seemed to be uncertain about. She wore a half-smile and her face was almost expressionless, as she limped up the ring steps into the ring. VL slumped into a corner and waited for her partner (and opponent the night before) to come to the ring. "From This Day" by Machinehead began to play, and out walked the hardcore feline lover himself, Hellrazor. The day before he had walked in beltless and catless, today he walks in with the company of Lamont in a petmobile, and the fWo Hardcore Title secure around his waist. He reached the ring and he and VL exchanged glares, before he left Lamont in the good hands of one of the commentators at ringside. VL and Hellrazor engaged in a staredown, before "All Star" by that horrid band Smash Mouth began to play. Kid Cool was excited to be wrestling once again, even though he lost the night before. He had learned a lot in his few days at the Frenchie's dojo, but that didn't help him last night as he lost to Ultra-Violet... via the first ever Ultra Hopskotch Elbow. As Kid Cool charged the ring, he tripped and tumbled down the ramp, hitting his head on the ring as he came to a halt. Kid Cool was rendered unconscious, and "Rage" began to play. Ultra-Violet came out and walked down to the ring pompously, holding hands with Violet. Ultra-Violet looked at Kid Cool with a silly grin on his face, before he asked Violet if why she would ever consider dating a loser who couldn't even make it to the ring. Ultra-Violet proudly pulled Kid Cool up before giving him a giant wedgee. Seeing as how Kid Cool wasn't responsive to this, he gave him several "noogies" as well, before rolling him into the ring. Hellrazor went for the pin, but VL pulled him off and picked up the victory. VL and Hellrazor then engaged in a shoving match, while Ultra-Violet and Violet skipped to the back in search of their doggie, Ultra. A bunch of security officials and referees ran down to ringside and separated the mess, before carrying Kid Cool out on a stretcher. Result: VL & Hellrazor by Pinfall Tag Team Challenges The crowd came to their feet as Havok and the Black Panther walked out onto the entrance ramp and surveyed the scene around themselves. Both men were without titles one night after fWo Invasion, and walked stoically to the ring. The Panther slid under the ropes and looked out at the crowd, as Havok climbed up to the ring apron and swung himself over the top rope. The two gestured to the announcing table for a microphone, and one was handed to the Panther. He held it and said, "Listen fans... I've got something that I think I need to get off my chest right now. Some of you..." The crowd began to boo, and the Panther lowered the microphone and waited for them to stop so he could continue. "Well, it appears most of you might have heard something that I thought was said in confidence. Well, clearly that was said out of emotion and was a mistake. I'm apologizing for my words... and want everyone to realize that it was a misunderstanding and nothing more. To show all of you that we are genuine, Havok and I have come to issue a compliment and a challenge. Now Underground... I realize that you won the fWo World Tag Team Championship last night, and that was a clearly a great accomplishment. So what Havok and I want to do right now is challenge both of you... Bear and Wretch... to a Tag Team title match at the next fWo Pay Per View, fWo Trapped. So if you..." Before the Panther could continue the music of the Underground exploded into the arena, and Wretch, Mike Bear and Fear stepped out onto the entrance ramp. Wretch grinned, and Bear pointed. "You're on boys... both of you, and both of us. Trapped. You're only getting one shot at the champions though, so you better make it count. Because Wretch and I are..." But suddenly the lights in the arena blew out and a blue light blazed onto the second tier of the arena. Machina and Rudo came out from the crowd, and Machina was holding a microphone. "Blah, blah, blah! Blah, blah! Blah blah blah!" Rudo grabbed the microphone away from Machina and patted his head. "He hasn't been the same since being exposed to May Young's breasts... so I'm going to translate. Millennium Men, you may get a match with the Underground, or you may get a match with Machina and me... er... I. Machina and I. Because this Wednesday on Warfare the Lone Gunmen are going to fight... and win... our championship belts back from Mr. Bare and Letch down there. So show up and... um... get beat. Yeah!" The crowd cheered at this, and security arrived to escort everyone away as Slaughter continued. Scott Collins vs. War vs. Plague vs. Stone The arena lights lower, and the ubiquitous "4" appears on the Slaughter Screen, bringing out Scott Collins' Four Riders. Collins and Stone walk side by side, with War and Plague trailing a foot behind and beside either one of them. All four of them appear to be snarling, none too pleased with having to take this match against each other. As they near the ring, Collins motions for War to get on the apron and hold the ring ropes apart. He does, and allows Stone and Plague to enter the ring. He then lets go of the ropes as Collins steps onto the apron, and steps over the top rope. Scotty sneers, then climbs the turnbuckle from the outside, surveying the British crowd. Disgusted by them, he jumps into the ring, then regroups the Riders in the middle. After a few seconds of discussion, Stone and Collins head to seperate corners, leaving Chaos to start off the bout. Plague locks up with War, but is sent to the mat when the big man shoves him off. Plague rolls backwards to his feet, then smiles a sickening smile. He charges War, leaping up at him with a headbutt. War stumbles backwards, then gets whipped into the ropes. Plague grabs two fists full of hair upon his return, then snaps him to the mat. Plague rains down closed fits upon his tag team partner, then rolls off of him, and tags in Stone. Stone steps through the ropes and mounts the second turnbuckle, then launches herself at the downed War, driving her forearm into his throat. While War grasps for air, Stone covers, hooking his leg for two. War explodes out of it, then gets back to his feet. War lifts Stone up for a chokeslam, but a poke to the eye ends that. War drops Stone back to the mat, then backhands her viciously, sending her reeling into Collins' corner. She tags out, and Collins rushes into the ring. He chops at War, driving him back into a neutral corner, then brings him out of the corner with a stalled suplex. Both men pop back up to their feet, War the groggier of the two, and Collins drops him with the Death Penalty. The cover gets three, and War rolls out of the ring, where officials escort him back to the lockerroom. Plague enters the ring once more, and he rushes his leader. Collins uses his momentum to drop him with a back drop driver, then brings Plague to his feet and hits his second Death Penalty of the match. The result is the same as before, and three seconds later Plague is out of the match. Stone enters the ring, and motions for Scott to come to her. He does, and Stone embraces him in the corner. Stone licks Collins' lips, then whispers something into his ear. He nods his head, and drops to the mat like a bag of bricks. Stone covers him for three, winning the match. Miraculously recovered, Collins quickly returns to his feet, and the two Riders leave the ring arm in arm, both of them smirking. Result: Stone by Pinfall Arrivals As Slaughter continued, several arrivals were made. Chris Universal stepped out from a limosuine, and he helped Dawn from the limo. He turned behind to see someone else arriving, and although it was freezing, he waited to see who it was. It was the Strangler, and Universal confronted him immmediately. The two engaged in a shoving match before Wretch & Bear pulled up in another vehicle, and attempted to split it up. Other officials made their way out to let Universal know Dead appointed him the special guest referee for the match, and this incensed Strangler. Strangler and Universal broke through the crowds of people and this time exchanged punches. Other arrivals were made, and as the Underground attempted to separate the chaos, Fear came out to tell them that Dead had scheduled a match where all three would be competing with each other... with their jobs on the line. Universal was escorted to the arena with a bloody lip, while it was feared Strangler may have separated his shoulder. Mike Bear vs. Wretch vs. Fear Three separate times the Underground's music resonated throughout the arena, and three separate times an Underground member made their way to the ring, obviously a bit anxious about their upcoming civil war of a match. The English crowd in attendance didn't know what to make of this match, and neither did the Underground themselves. The bell rang, and the trio of Wretch, Mike Bear, and Fear all stared at each other reluctantly. The referee stepped in between the three and, following orders from Mr. Davis himself, ordered the three stablemates to fight. Mike Bear approached the other two and said a few words to them. Nodding, Fear and Wretch both seemed to agree with whatever his proposition was. Much to the referee's dismay the three separated again, and once more the three stalled. Finally, Fear slowly approached Bear while keeping an eye on Wretch and engaged in a tie-up with him. The two quickly broke from each other, however. Wretch started at Bear, but Bear raised his hands in preparation, and an unsure Wretch backed away. The fans were getting restless by this point, and a steady roar of disapproval was coming from them. Wretch appeared to be getting disgusted with the whole situation, and decided to be the first on offense. The smaller of the tag champions delivered a sharp kick to the gut of Bear, doubling him over, but upon looking to Fear Wretch was sent end over end to the mat with a vicious clothesline. Wretch rolled back to his feet as Bear scooped up Fear's legs out from under him. As Bear turned to his fellow tag champion, though, Wretch connected with a dropkick to his face. Bear stumbled back and over the top rope, tying himself up in the process. While Bear hung there, helpless, Fear brought Wretch to his knees with a boot to the abdomen. Unsure of what to do to his friend now, Fear simply flipped Wretch over and attempted a pin. Wretch, however, easily kicked out before the ref was even in position to make the count. Fear pulled Wretch to his feet and whipped him into the corner, where Wretch hit hard and stayed put. Fear then charged in with a body press, crushing the smaller form of Wretch underneath him. Fear wrapped his arm around Wretch's and hip tossed him into the center of the ring where Wretch bounced hard on his back. Meanwhile, Mike Bear was able to pull his left arm free of the top and second ropes. As he worked on the right one Fear lifted up Wretch and dropped him in a press slam. Fear covered again and got a two count this time. Fear picked up Wretch again, but as he did so Wretch slid down his back and darted for the ropes. Fear turned just in time to be caught on the jaw with a spinning leg lariat. Fear was staggered, but refused to go down. Wretch bounced off the ropes again and, on his way back, slid underneath the legs of Fear. Fear turned right into a spinning heel kick to the jaw by Wretch and immediately went down to one knee. Wretch kicked at the side of Fear a few times before pulling his arm around his leg and rolling him into a la majistral cradle. But, as the ref counted two, Bear, who had just freed himself, interrupted the count with an axe handle to the head of Wretch. All three men regained their feet quickly, but this time there was no reluctance. Bear peppered Fear with shots to the head until he went down, then moved to do the same to his partner, Wretch. As he swung, though, Wretch ducked under and behind Bear, then quickly followed up with a leaping bulldog. Bear hit the mat face first, but as Wretch made the pin he was pulled off by Fear. Wretch got to his feet and began yelling at Fear, but the bigger of the two silenced him with a sidewalk slam. Fear set up Wretch throat first across the bottom cable and ran for the opposite ropes, but on his way there he collided with Bear and both men toppled over to the outside. As soon as getting to their feet both Underground members were on top of each other with lefts and rights. Fear was able to stun Bear by slamming his face into the guardrail, and then took the moment of reprieve to find a chair. As Fear raised it over his head, Wretch came soaring over the top rope with a plancha suicida, taking out both Bear and Fear. Several moments later Bear was the first person to his feet. A less than stable smile highlighted his face, and Bear lifted up Fear's chair. Fear stumbled to his feet, and walked directly into Bear's chairshot. Wretch grabbed Fear and began yelling at him, but as he did both men were bulldozed by an angry Fear. Fear picked up both men and took them towards the ringside steel steps. Fear brought both their heads back and was about to smash them both into the metal when both stuck their hands out to block. Fear tried again, but to no avail. Fear then found himself the unlucky recipient of a double head smash into the steps. Fear collapsed to his knees, and over his prone body the tag champions began to exchange shots. Fear grabbed Wretch by the arm and whipped him into the cades, but, to the crowd's amazement, Wretch launched himself off the guard railing with an Asai moonsault. As Bear and Wretch lay sprawled out on the arena floor, Fear slowly rolled himself into the ring. Wretch was up next, and he climbed up onto the apron. As he did so, though, Bear grabbed Wretch by the leg. Wretch answered him with a quick kick in the face, sending Bear back. Wretch stepped between the ropes, but was once again caught by Bear. Bear relinquished Wretch's leg just long enough to climb onto the apron. Wretch swung for Bear, but Bear ducked and dropped Wretch's throat across the top rope. Wretch was sent flying to his back as Bear landed on the floor. Fear made the quick cover on Wretch and got the three count just as Bear rolled into the ring. Bear raised his fist as if to strike Fear anyways, but slowly lowered it. Then, with an outstretched hand, Bear helped his two teammates to their feet, proving that the Underground was still together. Result: Fear by Pinfall The Frenchie's words Backstage, Matt Loco caught up with the Flying Frenchie, the new fWo World Heavyweight Champion. Loco grabbed the Frenchie's shoulder, and the Frenchie spun around to see who had touched him. Seeing that it was Loco, the Frenchie sneered and brushed his hand away, grabbing the microphone from him as he did so. "Get lost Loco... keep looking for zat photo of you with Jenna Jam'son and leave me alone." Loco quickly got lost as the Frenchie turned to the camera. "Dead, zu are ze biggest pile of uninteres'ing garbage I 'ave ever seen. Zis whole commissioner thing... please. Zu suck man. F'ck ze fWo? Non... f'ck you putain." The crowd cheered and the Frenchie continued. "Now... ze Frenchie must might ze members of ze Foundation? Good... ze Frenchie wants to entertain ze greatest fans in ze world... ze European fans. You know... ze Frenchie is a personal friend of ze Gunners... and ze Frenchie gave zem some tips zat are going to help zem kick ze Red Devil's ass! Man U? Man P-U! Ze Frenchie says zat Man U can kiss the Foundation's ass!" The crowd popped, and the Frenchie nodded with satisfaction. "Zut... it is going to suck to leave." Flying Frenchie vs. Primetime vs. Courage The audience jeers en masse as the French National Anthem is played, signaling the arrival of The French Foundation. Much like the Riders earlier in the night, they don't appear to be too terribly pleased with their match. Courage and Frenchie raise their spirits by taunting the British crowd with their Championship belts, while Primetime procures a microphone. "Dead, you want to throw your weight around, making us fight each other. Fine. But we all know that the Foundation has never backed down from any competition, even amongst ourselves. In fact, we all know that we blow the roof off of the arena when we wrestle each other. Clearly, these Brits aren't worthy to see the spectacle that is sure to happen as soon as that bell gets ringed, but since Courage single-handedly defeated both Wretch and Havok last night for the Cruiser belt, and Frenchie won back his World Title, we're in a good mood. So Pierre, Chris, let's show 'em how it's done." PT tosses the mic into the waiting hands of a ring attendant, and all three men shake hands. The bell rings, and Courage and Primetime waist no time in sending Frenchie to the ropes with a double-team Irish whip, then pasting him with a pair of shoulder blocks. Courage then lifts Primetime into the air for a suplex, before dropping him onto Frenchie in a splash. Primetime sticks on TFF for the cover, only earning two before Courage breaks it up. Primetime gets up, and is quickly hiptossed back to the mat. Frenchie returns to his feet shortly after that, only to be brought back down with a quick suplex from Courage. The Cruiserweight Champion and newest member of the Foundation then heads up top, catching Primetime with a missile dropkick. Frenchie rolls over to Primetime, draping his hand over PT for the cover. Primetime quickly kicks out, and all three get their footing. They respectfully nod towards each other, and then pace the ring, each one waiting for an opening. Quickly, Pierre and Primetime gang up on Courage, first hitting a double-team suplex, then a double-team Cannes Crab. Courage grabs the ropes to break up the hold, then grabs at his back once the hold is actually released. Primetime lifts up Courage as Frenchie heads upstairs. In short order, Primetime flips Courage onto his shoulders, and drops him with the Primetime Bomb as Frenchie comes off the corner with the Guillotine leg drop. Both men go for the cover, and they both break it to lunge at each other. Frenchie gains the advantage in the brief struggle, then sends PT to the ropes. He ducks Primetime's lariat, then catches PT's chin square with a superkick as he comes back the other way. The cover barely gets two before Primetime gets a shoulder up. Frenchie then brings him back to his feet, and attempts to whip him into the ropes again. It's reversed by Primetime, who drops his back for a back drop. The move is telegraphed, and Frenchie stops short, then double-underhooks Primetime, and landing a Tiger Driver. He covers again, and nearly gets the three before Primetime kicks out. Frenchie shakes his head in frustration, then leaves Primetime laying on the mat while he rouses Courage. The two of them formulate a plan, then approach Primetime as a unit. Courage brings Primetime into position for a crucifix powerbomb, then Frenchie grabs his neck, and the two bring Primetime down in a crucifix neckbreaker. Frenchie heads up top while Havok waits by the ropes. Frenchie gives Courage a thumbs-up, and Courage nails the Courage Required while Frenchie connects with the Guillotine legdrop. Courage sticks on Primetime for the cover, but it's broken up by the World Champion. Frenchie and Courage start brawling by the ropes, and the both of them tumble to the outside after some particularly nasty blows. Courage is the first to his feet, and he brings Frenchie over to the guard rail, hooking his arms over the top of it to keep him in place. He heads back to get a running start, then charges at Frenchie, jumping off the ring steps and hitting a wicked leg lariat. Frenchie drops back to the pretty blue mats that surround the ring, only to be brought back to his feet and hit with an Implant DDT. Courage rolls Frenchie back into the ring, then heads up top. He measures Frenchie, then hits a 450 splash. The crowd ooohs at the gall of Courage to try to finish off a man with his own move, and Courage nearly gets the pinfall until Primetime interjects. Primetime and Courage then seem to discuss something, and they come to an agreement. Primetime drags Frenchie to the side of the ring, before bringing him up for a Primetime Bomb. On the way down, Courage comes off the ropes for the Courage Required, and the result is a lifeless Heavyweight Champ, as well as two very tired Foundation members. Courage is first up to his feet, and he targets Primetime, dropping a knee on him. Seemingly stunned, Primetime offers no resistance as Courage sets him up on the top turnbuckle. Courage leaps up, hooking his legs around Primetime's neck for a top-rope hurricanrana, but Primetime counters with a top rope powerbomb. Courage goes splat, and Primetime stumbles over to where the Frenchie remains on the mat. He lifts the Frenchie back to his feet, then connects with a snap suplex, to better position him for his next move. Primetime then climbs the ropes, and comes off with a big splash. He hooks Frenchie's leg, and awaits for the referee's count. One...two...three, and the match is decided. Primetime rolls off of Frenchie and gingerly gets back up. Courage is next to his feet, and he congratulates Primetime on a match well wrestled. The two then help Frenchie back to his feet, but the World Champion's expression reveals a dour mood. He glares at Primetime for a second, upset at having lost, but it passes quickly, and his customary beam once again graces his face. The referee returns the Cruiserweight and Heavyweight Titles to Courage and Frenchie while Primetime raises his arms in victory. Result: Primetime by Pinfall The return Backstage Matt Loco wandered the arena halls, looking for anyone he could interview or perhaps bum lunch off of. He was looking intently down the hallways for signs of movement, and suddenly came to a stop outside a slightly opened locker room door. There was some kind of noise coming from behind the door, and Matt looked at it curiously. He pushed the door open slowly, and somewhere deep inside his head he began to hear alarm bells. Something was not right here. He had a sudden idea that he should walk away from the door and run away as fast as possible. Whatever was behind this door, it wasn't a good idea to go looking behind it. Instead Matt Loco pushed it open. It was dark, and silent. Matt Loco breathed a sign of relief. He reached over to turn on the light, and the second before light flooded the room, the smell hit him. His eyes widened, and then rolled backward as he fell into unconsciousness and collapsed in a heap on the floor. The door was closed, and the lights once again extinguished. Minutes passed, and the door opened once more. Fallen walked out of the room and moved silently down the arena hallway. Dusty footprints marked his passage. Black Panther, Havok, Ice, Spencer, Living, Heaven, Hell, Eron, Hiroshima, Rudo, Sid Snow, Matt Douglas, Mike Heftel, Cactus Jon, Primetime, Courage, Collins, Chris Universal, Briana & Oracle. As Slaughter returned from advertisements and preview for the next fWo pay-per-view on February 26 (Trapped), the last few participants of the 20-man battle royal were making their way out. They finally reached the ring and the match was underway. All eighteen men and the two females began duking it out. Eliminated early in the match was Oracle, via a clothesline from Matt Douglas. Heaven then eliminated Briana, before Megan Dawn pulled Heaven out with Briana' help. Briana and Heaven fought to the back, while back in the ring Mike Heftel eliminated Sid Snow after Sid Snow flirted with Heftel. Heftel's stay in the ring was short-lived as Hiroshima eliminated him, before a group of wrestlers began pounding on David Spencer. They then dumped Spencer out, before Primetime eliminated Eron to a huge ovation. Chris Universal followed by eliminating Living & Hell simultaneously, while Havok & Panther teamed up on Ice in a corner. Suddenly, Scott Collins had a burst of energy and eliminated Rudo, Cactus Jon, Courage and Matt Douglas in a row. Six men were left, and they were the Black Panther, Havok, Collins, Ice, Universal & Primetime. Universal and Panther battled it out, while Ice/Collins and Primetime/Havok did the same. Universal hit the Cha-Ching on Panther before attempting to eliminate him. Havok threw Primetime up and over the top rope, but Primetime landed on the ring apron unbeknownst to Havok who had turned and saw Panther attempting to eliminate Universal. Havok went to help, but as Universal slid back into the ring underneath the bottom rope, Panther was eliminated instead. Panther and Havok exchanged glares as Havok attempted to apologize for the mishap, but as he did Universal and Primetime snuck up from behind and eliminated him. This left just four men, and Ice/Collins continued to battle it out while Primetime & Universal watched. Ice & Collins turned their attention to Primetime & Universal, and charged with a double-team clothesline. Universal ducked but Primetime caught the blow as he was standing behind Universal. Collins then grabbed Primetime and dumped him over the top rope onto the cement floor. Ice & Universal went for a double-team clothesline, before Ice stopped midway through and Collins ended up backdropping Universal over the top rope. Ice charged at Collins trying to get an advantage, but it was Collins who went for a quick hiptoss and Ice ended up on the outside. Collins had no time to celebrate as the entire group of the FTfWo began stomping away on Ice & Collins repeatedly. Heaven & Dawn fought to the back, as did Living & Hell with Ice & Collins. Dead & Perfect helped Universal to his feet and Universal stood warily, before Dead grabbed a microphone and invited the Boston Strangler to come out now and get his ass kicked. Perfect went to the outside while Universal attempted to shake off the cobwebs, while Dead stood in the center of the ring awaiting his opposition for the evening. Slaughter went to it's final commercial break of the night, before returning for one of the biggest matches the organization would ever see. Result: Scott Collins Discovery. Far away from England, a young family was enjoying the waters off the shore of Maine. They had been sailing most of the day, and the next morning when the boy would look into the mirror he would discover that despite the cold weather of January he had developed a deep sun burn. The family often came out on the weekends to enjoy boating, and the privacy they got from the open water. This day was calm, a big change from the night before where storms have ravaged the area. The water was filled with branches and other signs from the storm, floating like tiny islands on the dark Atlantic sea. The boy was fishing around in the water, grabbing the larger branches and pulling them onto the bow for closer inspection. He had been doing this for almost an hour now, and his hand was wrinkled from the water. Now the boy's eyes narrowed, and he reached for something just out of his grasp. He struggled, and then grabbed it. He pulled, and drug the wood onto the boat and looked at it curiously. His father, who had been looking out at the sky now wandered over to where his son sat and patted him on the head, ruffling his hair. "What do you have there, sport?" The boy turned, and pointed down at the object he had pulled from the sea. His father frowned. "That looks like... well... like a rudder. But I can't imagine why one would be floating out here... not unless... well... unless some boat went down out here. But... with the storm last night, well... if a boat went down... whoever was on it would have been a goner." Dead vs. Boston Strangler The arena lights darkened, before "O Fortuna" began to play and out walked the maniacal looking Boston Strangler. The place literally shook as he reached the ramp. In the ring, Dead looked at the Boston Strangler deviously, before he looked out at Perfect. Each man exchanged a look of confidence, despite the overwhelming response for the Strangler from the crowd. The Strangler reached the ring, looking focused on one goal: winning. The Strangler and Dead reached the center of the ring, engaging in a game of psychological warfare with one another. The words could not be heard, but one could sense what they were saying to each other was a ploy at getting an early advantage on the other. Universal signalled for the bell, but neither man cared. They were nose-to-nose, and each man weren't going to budge an inch. The trash talking continued for several moments before the Strangler looked at the crowd. Dead saw his opportunity, and took it. He swung, but Strangler blocked. Dead swung again, and another block, returned by a punch this time. Dead was reeling soon after, and Strangler whipped Dead into the ropes. Dead returned and Strangler pressed him high above the air, before slamming him to the mat. The Strangler looked around as the crowd roared. Strangler shot a glare at Universal, before Dead reached his feet. Strangler chopped him several times, making his chest bright red rather quickly. Strangler whipped Dead into the corner, and as Dead returned Strangler went for a lariat. Dead ducked, spinned Strangler around from behind, and kicked him in his nether regions. Dead then went for a DDT rather quickly, or as fWo historians would note, his old finishing move. Dead went for a cover, but only got two. Dead used his foot to choke Strangler viciously, and Universal didn't attempt to break it up as it was a no disqualification match. Dead released after several moments, before going to his knees and pounding away at the fallen Strangler with lefts and rights. Dead brought Strangler to his feet and chopped him twice, before whipping him to the ropes. Dead went for a sleeper, but Strangler countered by dropping to his posterior, resulting in a jawbreaker. Dead went flying back onto the mat, clutching his jaw. Strangler went for a quick cover, and it would be noted that Universal' count was rather slow, but still reached two. Strangler looked at Universal in dismay, and shook his head. Strangler reached his feet and pushed Universal. Universal smirked in return, before pushing him back. Dead was there to roll Strangler up for a pin, but even with a fast count & a hook of the tights, Strangler would not be denied. His career was on the line, and he sure as hell wasn't going to go down without a fight. Both men reached their feet and charged each other with a double-clothesline. Both men down, and Universal went for a ten count. Even though it was no disqualifcation, he couldn't just let both men lay the whole night. As he reached four, both men began to stir. By seven, Strangler rolled atop of Dead. Dead kicked out at two. Strangler brought Dead to his feet and nailed him with several forearms, before whipping him into the turnbuckles. Strangler charged, but Dead moved and Strangler smacked the turnbuckles. Dead turned him around and kicked him, before hitting the X-Factor, also known as the Dead Death Drop. A cover, and a kickout by Strangler. Dead appeared angry that Strangler had not gone down quickly, and he waited for a chair to be handed to him from Perfect. As he received the chair, he waited for Strangler to get up, before smacking him across the head. Strangler got up to his knees, a few moments later, and told Dead it would take more than that to get rid of him. Another chairshot. Strangler crumpled into a heap, and blood began to pour from his forehead. Dead smelled the blood, and knew the kill was within arm's reach. He bent over and pinned Strangler, who had to be down after those shots. He wasn't. Kickout at two. The match continued, with Dead getting angrier as time went on. He brought Strangler up and hit a death valley driver onto the chair. Another cover. 1... 2... no. Strangler wouldn't give up. Blood spewing from his face, sweat pouring from his body... he would not stay down. Dead asked for two more chairs. He brought Strangler and softened him with a knee to the stomach, before hanging him upside down in the corner. He placed three chairs in front of the Strangler' face. He backed up and then charged, stopping midway after deciding the added chairs would not make the impact any greater. He pulled the two chairs aside, and grabbed the other. He slammed it into Strangler' face not once, not three times, nor even five. After ten consecutive shots, he let Strangler fallout from the corner. He went for a pin, and hooked the leg. Universal counted, and Dead smirked at the crowd. Two seconds later... and Strangler was still in it. Dead grunted loudly, before he set a chair in a sitting position in the center of the ring. He set the other two chairs atop of that one, also in sitting positions. He whipped Strangler into the corner. He followed him in, weaking him some more with several chops. He then whipped Strangler into the center of the ring, but Strangler reversed it. Dead reversed it as well, before hitting a short arm clothesline. He then bent over and picked Strangler up, but Strangler hit a low blow from below. Strangler crawled to his feet and scooped Dead up for a powerbomb, but instead turned it into a running powerbomb. He crashed Dead through at least two of the chairs, while the top one went flying into a corner. Strangler hooked the legs for a pinfall, and it appeared to be over. It wasn't. Kickout at two. The match continued, with Strangler making a comeback. He brought Dead up and hit a gutwrench suplex onto a chair. Another pinfall, and another kickout. Strangler brought Dead up and whipped him into the turnbuckles. Strangler went in to attack, but Dead countered with a waistlock. Strangler countered into a waistlock of his own from behind, before hitting a German Suplex with a reverse. Dead went crashing into one of the chairs, and Strangler stared out at Perfect who paced at ringside. Strangler smirked, and told him it was over. He went for a cover, but Dead kicked out again. Strangler seemed to have run out of options, and began to favor his shoulder that was injured earlier on in the night in the scuffle with Universal. Strangler brought the bald headed man to his feet and went for a punch, but Dead caught him offguard with a poke to the eye. Dead then scooped him up for a tombstone, perhaps going for the sitdown tombstone piledriver, but Strangler shook his legs and instead it was Dead who found himself on the receiving end of a tombstone onto a chair. Strangler crossed his arms over him, sensing it was over. Not so soon. Kickout at two and a half. Strangler sensed victory was his, and slapped Dead into the crossface chickenwing. As Dead writhed in pain, Perfect seemed nervous, and began to plot an attack. Universal, reluctantly, asked Dead if he was ready to give up. Dead screamed out with a "hell no!" that echoed throughout the building, and Strangler added more pressure to the hold. Perfect came into the ring, but as he did, Ice made his way out and they engaged in a brawl while the match went on. Somehow, Perfect ended up getting a chair and hitting Strangler from behind, which broke the hold up. Perfect and Ice brawled to the backstage, area, while Strangler and Dead were left in the ring in pain. Both men down, but not out. Universal began to count to ten. At six, neither man were showing any signs of movement. At eight, Dead rolled his arm atop of Strangler. It seemed as if time suddenly stopped. The crowd grew silent. It had to be over. Kickout at two and three-quarters, crowd erupts in cheers. Every single human being that were watching the action just saw the unbelievable happen before their very eyes. Dead reached his knees, and looked down at the Strangler. He brought him to his feet, and then hit a sitdown tombstone piledriver, also known as The Crucifixion. Instead of going for a cover, he dragged Strangler over near the ropes, and then to his feet. He hooked him in the ropes, and then went to the outside. He reached underneath the ring and pulled out a dark bag that was made out of some form of terrycloth. It appeared to be old & threadbare, and it was rolled underneath the bottom rope into the ring. He began to untie it, before he pulled out a snake. The snake began to hiss, and he lit it slither on the ground. Universal appeared moritifed by the snake, but smiled at what Dead was about to do. Dead let the snake inch closer to the Strangler' body, before he pulled it up and looked at it. He then took the snake by the head and brought it near the Strangler' arm. Strangler looked on in part-horror/part-anger, as Dead was about to let his snake sink it's teeth into his shoulder. Suddenly, Universal grabbed a chair. He then tapped Dead from behind and spun him around. Chairshot. The chairshot rattled Dead for a moment and he tossed the snake aside. Dead charged at Universal and Universal kicked him in the stomach on instinct. He then hit the Cha-Ching, and the place erupted. Universal let Strangler free from the ropes, and Strangler crawled... literally over to the fallen body of Dead. He draped his body ontop of him, and time stood still again. Three seconds later, it was over. Dead lost his power. The career of the Boston Strangler was still very much alive. The place shook from top to bottom, every single fan in the arena was standing to their feet and cheering in unison. The Strangler and Universal exchanged a brief smile, before Universal grabbed the snake and put it back in the bag. He helped Strangler to his feet and then dropped the bag onto Dead. The two men stood over Dead' body. Not only had the Strangler won, but the fWo did as well. As the show came to a close, Strangler and Universal embraced briefly, before Universal raised the madman from Boston's arm in the air. Universal & Strangler basked in the cheers from the crowd, before Perfect walked out onto the stage. He sent the FTfWo down to ringside to help Dead from the ring. Dead resisted their assistance as he reached the floor. He stared back at the ring evily at the Strangler & Universal, before doing a throat slash sign. He pointed at Universal and told him that it was not over. Not by a long shot. Result: Boston Strangler by Pinfall
