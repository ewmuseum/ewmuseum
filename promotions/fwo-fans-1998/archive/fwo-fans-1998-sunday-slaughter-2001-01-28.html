Sunday Slaughter, 28 January 2001

28 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The strides of the large man produced an echo that reverberated through the empty hallway. Clop. Clop. Clop. Clop. Stop. The man gripped the doorknob and turned, admitting himself into the locker room with a creak of the door. In the center of the room, a small, solitary wooden table stood. On the table, there was an envelope. Plain white, no address. The man stepped forward and took the envelope. Opened it. Removed the contents. A plain white piece of paper with typing from what appeared to be an old-fashioned typewriter. The man read the paper. The camera behind his shoulder could only make out one sentence. "Your next victim:" The man continued reading for a moment, then nodded. He reached back into the envelope and pulled out a large wad of $100 bills held together with a money clip. Nodded. The Boston Strangler smiled. His face then tensed, like a cat who hears a noise. He shot an elbow backwards, and it collided with the camera. Static. MUD (c) vs. Vengence The Fugees' "The Beast" hits the speakers, bringing out Vengence with a host of violent objects, including a few board games, a cordless phone, a computer monitor, and a couple desk drawers. Vengence tosses all of the objects into the ring as he waits for the Hardcore Champion. As he is tossing the rest of his ever-so-violent toys to the ground, MUD, along with Toad, runs out to the ring. He is dragging a tub of things along with him, which he leaves at the bottom of the entrance ramp as he climbs the apron and slides into the ring. He immediately attacks Vengence with a... deck of cards? Vengence looks at the pack, and dares MUD to hit him. So MUD takes a swing, and nails Vengence in the head... knocking Vengence out like a light. MUD opens up the pack, and a cement block pops out of it. MUD tosses it aside, and goes for a quick cover. At two, Vengence gains his senses and kicks out. MUD gets up, kicks Vengence, and looks for something else to hit him with. He grabs the Chutes and Ladders board game and swings at Vengence. Vengence ducks it (as he was getting up, of course), and decks MUD with a punch to the head. A kick to the midsection from Vengence leads to a DDT on the Chutes and Ladders game, with MUD dropped when he was kicked. Vengence gets up, and grabs the chess set he brought with him. He empties the contents of the box, and grabs a pawn. Vengence grabs MUD's head, opens his mouth, and shoves the pawn inside of it, yelling "Pawn jumps BITCH!" MUD begins to choke a bit on the piece, before spitting it out. Then, Vengence grabs the queen, which happens to be a very expensive glass piece. Vengence keeps his hand open, and lays into MUD with it, yelling "Checkmate!" He covers MUD, but only gets a two and a half, before MUD kicks out. Vengence leads MUD up, and tosses him through the ropes. Vengence hops down and begins to rummage through MUD's tub. Meanwhile, MUD begins rise, and grabs a chair. He pokes Vengence on the shoulder. Vengence turns around, and MUD says to him. "Could you hold this for me?" Vengence, for some odd reason, obliges. MUD then takes a chair from behind his back and swings it into Vengence's chair, causing Vengence to nail himself in the head and fall into the tub. MUD begins to look around for more objects to use. Meanwhile, Carnage takes off for the ring. MUD swings the tub behind him and gets ready to tackle Carnage. Carnage and MUD begin to brawl, with MUD getting the upper hand. MUD grabs a chair, and nails Carnage with it. However, this seems to only fuel the fire of Carnage, who rips the chair from MUD's hands, and proceeds to rip him into him with it. Meanwhile, Vengence gets back inside the ring and awaits Carnage to toss MUD back inside. However, Carnage has other plans. He has a weapon of his own. He has his knife. He stands over the fallen MUD, ready to cut into him. He strikes down. However, his swipe is stopped short... because Toad has leaped over MUD, ready to save him. Carnage strikes Toad with the knife, stabbing him in the process. Carnage sees this and steps back. Then, he begins to run from ringside, back up the ramp, and into the back. MUD gets up, and sees the bleeding Toad. He picks him up in his arms, and holds him, trying to figure out what to do. Meanwhile, the bell rings, awarding the match to Vengence by countout. Vengence stands in the ring, just as bewildered as everyone else. Meanwhile, Slaughter rolls on... Result: Vengence by countout Challenge Wo cameras went to Chris Universal's office, which has quickly become since Universal's taking control a featured place of booking and matchmaking. It looked as if another match was about to be made in the office of Uni, as Black Quicksilver (holding STUMP) and Sphere glared angrily at one another from opposite sides of the mahogany desk of the fWo owner. Universal, dressed in his typical fine wear, looked back and forth between the two. Quicksilver spoke. "Chris Mister Universal I want " STUMP interrupted. "..." "Yes, I know the Giants suck, but you don't need to be chanting it all the time, so shut up, I'm trying to do something here! I want a match with " "..." Sphere rolled his eyes and spoke. "A match with me?" "Yeah, that's the ticket!" Universal put in, "At In Misery, right?" They nodded. "You got yourselves a match." Black Quicksilver high-fived STUMP. "Sweet!" Sphere scoffed at this display, then spoke. "Come In Misery, your ass is mine. Right now, Crusader's is. I'm outta here." Sphere exited, leaving Quicksilver, Universal, and STUMP alone in the room. Universal's television was tuned to ESPN, which was showing highlights of the day's earlier Super Bowl. Onscreen, a Ravens defender picked off Kerry Collins. Universal and Quicksilver smiled, then exchanged a high-five as Slaughter went back to the ring... Sphere vs. Crusader The crowd remains silent, until "Wicker Man" by Iron Maiden takes to the speakers and introduces Sphere to the ring. Sphere gets a fair amount of boos from the crowd after his actions at last week's Slaughter, but ignores them and rolls himself into the ring. "Switchback" by Biohazard replaces Iron Maiden, as the crowd begins to stir its boos to cheers, seeing Crusader at the entrance with Clarisse. They both head to the ring, with Crusader climbing on the apron and entering and Clarisse taking her spot ringside. Sphere and Crusader eye one another, as the bell rings. The initial grapple begins, and Sphere overtakes Crusader with a headlock. Crusader presses him against the ropes, and manages to shoot him off to the other side. Sphere returns and ducks under a leapfrog by Crusader. Crusader then drops to the mat and shoots his feet up vertically, looking for a reverse monkey flip, but Sphere manages to dive over and roll through. Crusader attempts to get back to his feet, but Sphere flattens him out with a clothesline. Sphere brings the gravity-defying Crusader back up and snapmares him over, before sending a soccer kick right to his back. Crusader arches his back in pain, and Sphere takes advantage of the situation with a pickup into a shoulder-breaker. Sphere drops down and makes the first cover of the match, only to score two. Sphere takes it to Crusader with a few vicious stomps. Crusader manages to drive an elbow into the mid-section of Sphere in retaliation, and gets up to chop him right across the chest. Crusader tears off Sphere's shirt to get a more resounding chop, that sends him straight into the corner. Crusader Irish whips Sphere into the turnbuckles, and Sphere hits sternum-first, causing him to stumble back out of the corner. Crusader quickly heads to the top and hits a missile dropkick on Sphere. Sphere hits the mat near the ropes and manages to roll himself to the outside. Crusader doesn't want the match to stop, however, as he shoots himself over the top with a somersault plancha. Not wanting for the match to end in a count-out, Crusader rolls Sphere back into the ring. Crusader once again whips Sphere into the corner and follows in with a hip-check, the momentum causing Crusader to land on the apron. Sphere stumbles out of the corner, and Crusader takes advantage by springing on the top strand and coming off with a 360 Thesz press. Crusader stays in the mounted position to rain down a few hard right hands, and then heads to the ropes for a springboard moonsault. Sphere however thinks in advance, and gets his knees up.
