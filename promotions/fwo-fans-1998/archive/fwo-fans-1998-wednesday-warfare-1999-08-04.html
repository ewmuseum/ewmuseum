Wednesday Warfare, 4 August 1999

4 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The show opened on the door to the office of the commissioner. The scene was still, and for a moment it appeared as though the camera had been placed in this location by mistake. Suddenly, shouting was heard from behind the door. "They were WHAT?" Silence again. Then, "RELEASED from their contracts? QUIT? What in the name of all that was good and holy were you thinking?" Silence again. Then, "Oh. Well, that makes sense then. Well, you understand then what I have to do. Tonight will be my last match for awhile... I've got to find my people." The door opened, and Machina walked out of the office, looking directly into the camera. "Sid... Billy... Rudo... I'm coming for you!" With that Machina walked away with a florish, whistling a cute little tune... 'Rump Shaker'. Doomsday (c) vs. Ground Zero The three-time fWo World Tag Team Champions Ground Zero come to the ring with one on their mind: becoming fWo World Tag Team Champions for a fourth time. The fWo Cruiserweight Champion Wolf Fang climbs into the ring along with the massive Jackhammer, and the duo await as their opponents are introduced. Stevie Roberts and VL, collectively make their entrance, carrying their fWo World Tag Team Titles. Roberts tells Ground Zero that they are not going to win the fWo World Tag Team Titles for a fourth time because they are virgins and haven't shagged as many times as he has. Wolf Fang and Jackhammer simply ignore him. Jackhammer and Stevie apparently will start things off. Jackhammer and Stevie prepare to lock up, but then Stevie tells him to back off so he can talk to VL for a moment. Stevie & VL converse for a moment, before Stevie goes back to the middle of the ring, just about ready to lock-up once again. However, the crafty Roberts stalls again. He walks over and talks to Wolf Fang for a moment, before going outside the ring and telling Finn that Wolf Fang isn't his type of bag. Finn ignores him and Roberts goes back inside. He and Jackhammer are about to lock up once again, but then Roberts stalls *again* and tells Jackhammer to wait a moment so he can say hi to his mom at home. Roberts walks over to the camera man who was standing on the ring apron in the corner. Roberts waves and begins to say "Hi", when Jackhammer becomes frustrated. Jackhammer walks over to Roberts, and as Roberts turns around, Jackhammer grabs him and takes him down with a belly-to-belly suplex. Roberts hits into the mat hard and then Jackhammer looks down at him evily. The crowd pops as Roberts got what was coming to him. After several more moments, Roberts appears ready to continue the match. As they are about to lock-up, "F-ck Off" begins to play and out walks The Hollywood Blondes, with the striking Megan Dawn wearing a bikini. However, Dawn is covering up her chest area with a white poster board sign that says "$acramento $uck$, Hollywood Rule$". The crowd, who was cheering at first at the sight of Megan Dawn, begins booing after they read the sign. The Blonde$ shrug off the jeers as they have better things to do. Roberts & Jackhammer look at the duo with disgust as they are once again stalling their match. The Blondes tell the two teams that one of them will lose the tag team titles on Friday. The Blondes join Finn & Armstrong at ringside for commentary. Finn asks them why they are here, and Star replies saying that they are scouting their so-called competition. Armstrong asks if he may catch a glimpse of Megan Dawn' breasts, and Universal says "If you wish upon a star, we might let you." Armstrong gets all giddy, and Finn tells him to calm down, because they have a match to call. Roberts and Jackhammer *finally* lock-up, with Jackhammer getting an immediate advantage because of his size and strength. Jackhammer tosses Roberts to the mat with ease. Roberts tags in VL. They lock hands and charge at JH with a double-clothesline. He simply stands there, then takes both of them off their feet with a clothesline. VL slides out onto the floor and Jackhammer grabs Roberts and whips him into the turnbuckles. Roberts comes walking back out and Jackhammer presses him high above his head, before dropping him and catching him on his shoulder, turning it into a powerslam. Jackhammer roars loudly and Roberts slides out onto the floor. He and VL converse and plot what to do next, but Wolf Fang sneaks up on Roberts and dropkicks him into VL. VL falls to the ground, but Roberts only staggers. He turns around and WF nearly takes his head off with a superkick. Roberts falls head first into VL' crotch. WF slides into the ring and he and JH wait for Roberts and VL to get up. They finally do, and Jackhammer presses his partner high into the air. He prepares to drop him onto them on the outside... WF falls through the air, but Roberts and VL quickly move out of the way and Wolf Fang hits with a sickening thud. Jackhammer looks down at his partner, who is in pain from what happened. As this happens, VL sneaks up from behind and nails him with a lowblow shot to the groin. She then turns him around and Irish Whip' him to Roberts, who then nails him with a kick to the stomach, and a swinging neckbreaker to follow it up. A cover, but Jackhammer kicks out rather quickly, not giving up this early. Roberts and VL have the advantage now... Roberts stomps at the head of Jackhammer, three or four times, before bringing him to his feet. He whips Jackhammer into the corner, and then distracts the referee. Wolf Fang finally is back up on the ring apron... as Jackhammer hits the turnbuckles, VL hops to the floor, and grabs Jackhammer legs. She pulls him down to the mat, and then backwards into the ringpost, crotching him. She then walks over and grabs a chair and hits one of his legs several times, before hopping back up on the ring apron and pretending nothing happened. The referee of course believes her, and the match continues. Roberts pulls Jackhammer into the middle of the ring, and follows up with a knee drop to Jackhammer' injured leg. Roberts goes for a cover, but Jackhammer kicks out. Roberts works over Jackhammer's leg some more... before slapping on a figure-four. Problem is, Jackhammer' legs are too damn big. Roberts realizes this and climbs to his feet. He walks over and slaps Wolf Fang in the face, before running away. The referee stops Wolf Fang, and with his back to the ring, VL comes in. She and Stevie lock on a double-team step over toe hold and work over Jackhammer' legs. The referee finally turns around and tells VL to get out of the ring. Roberts complains and says that she's the legal man, which confuses the referee altogether. Wolf Fang finally gets sick of things and charges at Stevie, spearing him into the mat. The referee tries to pull them apart as VL climbs up to the top rope, and flies off with a missle dropkick. Jackhammer moves out of the way however, and VL crashes onto the mat. Jackhammer tries to shake off the injury received to his leg, as Stevie & Wolf Fang fight out onto the floor. The referee tries to split them up as Jackhammer limps for a few moments... but down comes the Chameleon & Total Eclipse! Chameleon nails Jackhammer with a superkick, which propels him into the huge girth of Eclipse. Eclipse prepares to hit a death valley driver, but then turns it into a michinoku driver. Jackhammer is laid out in the ring... and Chameleon drapes VL' arm over him. The referee is still on the outside, but Star informs the referee that a pinfall is occuring in the ring. The referee finally makes his way back in, and counts the pin. Wolf Fang and Roberts continue to fight on the outside... back up on the ramp, Silver Fox rolls out in his wheel chair and prepares to congratulate the Chameleon & TE for what they did. The duo push him down the ramp in his wheel chair, and he goes flying and lands on another portion of the ramp *hard* face first. They then inform the crowd that they are "one step closer to greatness" before exiting... The Hollywood Blondes feel their job is done, and they prepare to leave. However, they attack Roberts and Wolf Fang and lay them out on the arena floor... before climbing into the ring. They look down at VL, and Jackhammer, still dazed on the mat. Star smirks at Jackhammer while Universal laughs at them. Dawn rips up the "$acramento $uck$, Hollywood Rule$" sign and leaves the remains on VL, before standing above Jackhammer and posing. Universal and Star cover her up, which makes the crowd infuriated. As the duo walk up the ramp, they laugh at a fallen Silver Fox. Star lifts up the wheel chair and drops it on Silver Fox, before they walk backstage leaving the area looking like a car accident just happened, with bodies all over the place. Result: Doomsday by Pinfall Meltdown Announcement Chris Finn came to the ring to the offical Meltdown music, and waved from the ring to the crowd. The fans cheered back at him, and sat on the edge of their seats in anticipation. Finn held up a small card, and began to read. "Here are the next three teams we will see at Meltdown, the big August fWo Pay-Per-View! Team number one will include: SCOTT SLUGGER and the FALCON! Team number two will include: DARKNESS and JACKHAMMER! Team number three will include: ULTRA VIOLET and JOY! Remember, we will be announcing three more teams at Sunday Slaughter... I look forward to seeing you all there, and this Friday at Hotter Than Hell!" With that, Chris Finn walked from the ring to the cheers of the crowd... Scott Slugger vs. Tom the Bomb With Tom The Bomb already in the ring after the preview for Meltdown, "Back in Black" begins to play and out comes Scott Slugger, who is accompanied by Ice. Slugger comes down to ringside and he and Ice climb into the ring. Slugger immediately attacks Tom The Bomb and doesn't let up from beginning to end. He throws him over the top rope onto the floor, before distracting the referee. Ice removes a piece of the guardrail and slams it repeatedly into Tom's leg. He then throws Tom' knee first into the ring steps, before sliding him back into the ring. Slugger smirks at the referee before moving him out of his way. He immediately applies a sharpshooter, and Tom taps out after a few moments. Slugger however, doesn't release the hold, and the referee tries to pull him off. Slugger still refuses to let go of the hold... Ice comes in, and nails the referee with a spike DDT, before stomping at the back of Tom' head. The two continue to demolish Tom, before Slugger gets on the microphone and tells Jon that this is just a small example of what he's going to do to Cactus Jon on Friday... and after that, the fWo World Title will be his for a third time. With that, Warfare went to a commercial break. Result: Scott Slugger by Submission D-Lo Brown vs. Ultra Violet The WWF superstar D-Lo Brown makes his way out to a mixed reaction from the crowd, and he carries with him his Intercontinental and European Titles. Ultra-Violet, w/ Violet, came to the ring to a nice ovation of cheers for the most fan friendly star the fWo has on it's roster. Ultra-Violet climbs into the ring, although tripping, as flashes of lights go off in the crowd. He is distracted, and so is Violet, by the lights. Then then look back at the videotron above the stage and see themselves in a highlight video being played. UV looks on in horror, obviously wondering how he could be in two places at the same time. Meanwhile, D-Lo stand behinds him, waiting patiently to begin the match. Minutes seemingly pass by, before D-Lo turns UV around. UV shakes his head back and forth, almost exactly like D-Lo does. UV is just scared of D-Lo, but D-Lo thinks he's mocking him. D-Lo quickly presses him up and down onto the mat with the 'Sky High', before going outside to the top rope, and flying off with the Lo-Down. D-Lo then covers him, and then tells UV that he better recognize just who the hell he is. Armstrong comments that UV doesn't recognize himself, let alone D-Lo. Violet comes into the ring and helps UV up, and UV is still wondering what happened. Violet tells him some meanie that bobs his head a lot beat him up with tennis balls... as Warfare goes to a commercial break. Result: D-Lo Brown by Pinfall Torture Somewhere, unknown, very dark and very quiet sat John Skyler, bound to a wooden chair. His head drooped, and his head was badly bloodied. Matted hair fell around his face, and his eyes were dark and circled. Footsteps neared, and his eyes cracked open to see who the visitor was. "D... Dina?" A soft female hand brushed the hair out of his eyes, and Dina Sol bent forward and kissed him lightly on the forehead. John struggled weakly to free himself, but was unable to move and slumped back into the chair. He looked up into Dina's face, and a tear trickled down his face. "Dina... I was afraid... they told me... I killed you... but... I'm so happy to see you... I love you..." Dina smiled softly, and raised John's head with one finger, looking deeply into his eyes. Speaking slowly, holding his gaze, she said, "You... will... NEVER... have... me." John stared in horror into her eyes, and she began to laugh a cruel laugh. She delivered a hard backhand across John Skyler's face, tipping the chair over and sending John sprawling on the floor. "Now Fallen... now the real pain begins..." Machina vs. Hardcore Heftel Loud porno music began to play, and as fans looked anxiously up the entrance ramp a man came out dressed in a heavy fur trenchcoat and a large purple sequin hat. It was only Machina, and the fans began to boo him as he appeared on the top of the ramp alone. Grinning, he motioned to the backstage area, and out came several women in tight dresses. Closer inspection revealed them to be Geoff Green, Matt Loco, and Dick Venom, looking somewhat concerned about being put in this role. This made the fans boo harder, and Machina smacked Geoff in the ass and told him to shake his money maker. Annoyed with this treatment, the three "hos" stomped backstage once more, leaving Machina alone. Sighing, Machina said, "Aw hell... it's just not the same. I'm going to go look for them right NOW." Suddenly, loud rock music blared over the PA system, and Hardcore Heftel walked out onto the entrance ramp looking cocky and smug. Heftel grabbed a microphone and said, "Now listen up little man! I'm gonna have ta kick your ass because the bookers said so, even though you're a little freak and I'm a super heavyweight who is gonna make your ass famous seven ways to Sunday!" Machina tipped his large hat, and screamed, "Well get in the ring then... and step aboard the Hoooooooooooo Train!" Hardcore Heftel looked confused by this, and scratched his head. "I... don't follow you..." Machina pointed at the hat. "Pimp gimmick. You know how it is." Heftel nodded, "Yeah, I hear ya brother... so this one of those hardcore rules things?" Machina shrugged, "I guess so... haven't had one of those yet on the cards and all we have left is a squash and a legit title match..." Heftel looked under the ring and pulled free a table, along with a tray of weapons that were laying next to it. He placed these in the ring, and then climbed in after them. Machina looked at them incredulously, and scratched his head, taking off his giant hat. "Hey... I'm not getting with *that*... it's dirty, and I've already got little bit of a cold." Heftel sorted through the weapons, and picked up a cheese grater. Machina shook his head. "New mask and outfit. Don't want to ruin them just yet." Heftel held up a chair. Machina looked doubtfully. "A bit overdone don't you think? Too bad we don't have any lightening laying about..." Heftel held up a cookie sheet. Machina nodded, "Yeah... that looks good. Here, let me see that." Heftel handed him the cookie sheet, and Machina smashed it over Heftel's head, knocking him out cold. Machina looked down at Heftel, and grinned. "Damn... I like this kid. Heftel, this one is on me. Tell him that when he wakes up, won't you?" Machina rolled out of the ring, and allowed himself to be counted out. Machina waved out to the fans, telling them, "I'll be back... but not alone! The Lone Gunmen will live again!" Result: Hardcore Heftel by Count Out Death w/ Dark Riders (c) vs. Kid Cool We return to live arena action and the arena is completely dark. Armstrong wonders aloudly why the arena is dark, and then asks for his "mommy". Suddenly, an ominous glow lights up the stage, and out walks the Dark Riders as the number "4" appears on the videotron above the stage. The Riders stand as a unit, with their leader Death in the middle of the group, and Stone at his side. As The Dark Riders march to the ring, the "4" animates into the words "The Dark Riders" and the blueish glow follows The Dark Riders on their way to the ring. The Dark Riders climb into the ring and stand smack dab in the middle of it, and an overhead shot of the Riders is seen. The blue glow suddenly turns into one single "4" in front of the Dark Riders, before splitting off into smaller 4's and branching off into each corner. Stone, Plague & War exit the ring and wait on the outside, before the lights come back on and the massive Death is left alone in the ring. "All-Star" begins to play, and down comes Kid Cool. Kid Cool asks some fans at ringside if they have anything to tie his arm behind his back with. One kid offers a lengthy piece of Twizzler licorice, but it's not quite long enough to tie his arm behind his back. Kid Cool thanks him anyways and walks down the ramp, although reluctantly. Kid Cool scratches his head as he looks at Stone, Plague & War... before looking back up at the stage. He doesn't appear to eager to walk into harm's way, but like previous weeks before... he's going to anyways. He slides under the bottom rope, stopping just after getting up, and then turning around and looking at the top rope. The look on Kid Cool' face says everything. Kid Cool looks at Death, points at the top rope, and then asks for a microphone. He says that if anybody has some scissors, he'd like for them to be kind enough to borrow them so he can cut the top rope to use it to tie his arm behind his back. Death simply charges at him and clotheslines him over the top rope onto the floor. Stone then hops up onto the ring apron and distracts the referee. Plague & War attack Cool on the outside, throwing him into the guardrail, into the ringpost, and doing other dangerous things to him. Meanwhile, Death walks over to the turnbuckles and begins untying it. He takes the top turnbuckle off, and pulls the string out of it. He hands it to War, who then begins tying Kid Cool's arm behind his back. They then roll him into the ring, and Stone hops off the ring apron, having fulfilled her contractual obligations for the match. With Kid Cool already pummeled laying on the mat, Death brings him to his feet. He whips him using his other arm into the turnbuckles. Kid Cool hits hard into the revealed top buckle, smacking into the link of chains. He staggers out backwards, before Death turns him around and hits him with the Death Penalty. He repeats it not once, not twice, but three times. He then covers Kid Cool, and asks him if that's sufficient enough evidence to prove that he IS a "big badass." Kid Cool cannot offer up any response, and then Death tells him that he doesn't deserve the name "Cool" because he's nothing but a punk kid who just got taught a lesson. Ultra-Violet walks out with concern, before climbing into the ring. The Dark Riders let him into the ring without harm, and then mock him. Ultra-Violet helps Kid Cool to the back, along with John Willis. Plague then acts like he's hurt and uses War to help him out of the ring, further mocking UV and Kid Cool. UV looks back towards the ring with a look of revenge in his eyes... Result: Death by Pinfall Black Panther (c) vs. Eron the Relentless Eron The Relentless walks out from behind the curtain, and the ovation of boos grows louder as he walks down the ramp. He bumps into Death, who was making his exit with the other Riders, seemingly on purpose. Death stares at him with a look of violence written across his face and he's about to strike down upon Eron, when the rest of the Riders hold him back. Eron simply smirks at Death as he is held back, and Death tries to escape from the grasp but then laughs, and tells Eron "you're not worth it." Stone tells Eron to "get the hell out of here before *I* beat the hell out of you." Eron tells her to shut up and mind her own business, before walking down the ramp and raising his arms, shouting at the fans. Eron slides into the ring and waits for the fWo Internet Champion to make his entrance. The Black Panther methodically walks out, just barely passing the Dark Riders. The Panther holds his Internet Title high above his head, and the fans cheer him on as he walks down the ramp. As the Panther gets closer down to ringside, Eron bounces off the ropes on the other side of the ring and comes flying over the top rope with a flying plancha, taking Panther onto the floor. He then follows it up by climbing up onto the ring apron, and waiting for Black Panther to get to his feet. As Panther does, Eron goes flying off the ring apron and takes Panther back down onto the floor with a flying crossbody block. He then gets up, continuing to use his agility and speed to his advantage because he is clearly outmatched in the strength and size departments. Eron then pulls out a table from underneath the ring. He leaves it there, not setting it up. As BP gets up, Eron nails him with a side kick, and another one. He then takes Panther and slides him over the guardrail. Eron then goes back to the table and sets it up near the guardrail. He climbs up onto the table, and dives off going for a hurracarana as BP barely got back up to his feet. However, Panther catches him and just drops him through the table, powerbomb style. Eron goes crashing through and Panther takes a moment to recover from the side kicks and the other moves dished out. Panther climbs over the guardrail with ease. He then lifts Eron up onto his shoulder, and prepares to drive him into the ringpost. As he does, Eron falls behind him and BP hits into the post. Eron jumps up onto the ring apron and as BP staggers out backwards, Eron executes an Asai moonsault onto Panther, taking him onto the floor again. The referee finally attempts to restore order as Eron and Panther lay on the floor. The bell hasn't rung and this match hasn't officially started and for the most part, it has been all Eron the Relentless. Relentless gets back to his feet and rolls Panther into the ring. Eron then goes up to the top rope, and waits for BP to get to his feet. He then drops down to the middle rope, before facing the crowd. He jumps to his left and bounces on the top rope. From there, he jumps to his right and bounces on the top rope there. As BP gets to his feet, Eron flies off with a beautifually executed double-springboard moonsault and takes Panther down to the mat. A cover, but the Panther kicks out at two. Eron is pulling out all the stops here. Eron brings Black Panther to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckles. As Panther walks out Eron takes him down with a tiltawhirl flying headscissors. Eron quickly gets to his feet and as Panther does the same, Eron goes to take him out with the superkick, which is the set-up to his finishing move. Panther moves out of the way and as he does, he rolls up Eron from behind with a school boy for the pin. A quick kickout at two, and both men get to their feet quickly. Eron charges at Panther but Panther takes him down with a clothesline. Eron gets back up and Panther takes him down again. Eron gets back up again and charges at Panther, who locks him and takes him down with a belly-to-belly suplex. Cover, but a kickout at two. Panther brings Eron to his feet and whips him to the ropes. Eron returns and Panther goes for a backdrop. Eron however jumps over for a sunset flip. His momentum isn't enough to bring Panther down. The massive Panther reaches down with both hands and grabs him by the throat. He brings him up into the air... choking him out... but Eron uses his feet to knock Panther back into the ropes, before Eron falls to the mat. Eron gets back up and charges at Panther with a spining heel kick that staggers Panther again into the ropes. Eron attempts another superkick and this knocks Panther over the top rope, but he lands on his feet on the outside. Eron gets some momentum and flies over the top rope with a pescado, but Panther catches him. Panther then takes Eron and presses him high over his head and tosses him back into the ring with one motion. Eron lands on the mat and Panther climbs back into the ring over the top rope. As he does, Eron charges at him and Panther takes him down with a big boot. He then brings Eron up, twists his arm, pulls him towards him and takes him down with a clothesline. He follows it up by whipping Eron into the turnbuckles. He charges in with a splash, but Eron steps out of the way. Panther turns around and Eron goes for a superkick again, but Panther moves out of the way, and grabs him from behind. He props him up facing the crowd on the top rope. Panther then climbs up on the second rope and locks a full-nelson on Eron. He then hooks him and takes him flying back onto the mat with a flying full-nelson suplex! Eron tumbles on the mat, before Panther slowly gets to his feet. He stomps at the head of Eron a few times, before whipping him to the ropes. He goes for a clothesline, but Eron somehow ducks. Eron returns for a spinning heel kick and Panther catches him midway through the move, and simply plants him into the mat. A cover, and Eron is unable to kick out as Panther retains the Internet Title in his first defense of his second reign, which began one week ago. Panther holds the Internet Title high above his head as the crowd applauds his efforts, before he drapes it over his shoulder. The cameras began to fade to black as fans were reminded that this Friday is Hotter Than Hell 1... Result: Black Panther by Pinfall
