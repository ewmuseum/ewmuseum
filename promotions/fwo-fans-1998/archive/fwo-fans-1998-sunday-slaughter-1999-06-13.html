Sunday Slaughter, 13 June 1999

13 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The dusty and lonely countryside of Jasper, Wyoming looked quiet and barren of life. From the northern angle. From the south it looked slightly populated with one story houses and large ranches. In the center of this modest dwelling stood a giant arena, built seemingly oblivious to the fact that no serious sporting event would ever host a show in this town. Except of course, the fWo, who happened to be promoting a show that very night. Fans in line were told of some of the title changes that impacted the card, the biggest being that Billy James would face Black Quicksilver instead of Al Snow. Fans accepted this with little problem, taking their "Head" props and severing them at the brow to make "Stump" props. Inside the arena Matt Loco and James Armstrong were on microphone duty, and prepared the fans for a wild night. Primetime vs. Chameleon The lights dimmed and the Chameleon came to the ring to a chorus of boos based on the events of a few nights ago. The Chameleon was alone for this encounter, but Primetime was not as he came to the ring with his tag team partner Mitch Wilson and Nikki. The two wrestled a physical match, with both men taking the advantage at various times in the match. The final outcome came in Primetime's favor, when he reversed a body drop into a suplex and pinned the Chameleon. The real news of the match came after it was completed, as Joy ran to the ring and began to attack Primetime with a bible, claiming that he would come to understand the true horrors of premarital sex. Result: Primetime by Pinfall Sock for sale? Backstage Chris Universal was shown explaining to Mick Foley that he knew he needed some money, and he was willing to make a trade. A healthy sum of money... for Mr. Socko. This bothered Mick Foley, and he clutched the socko to his chest telling Universal "no way". Universal continued to wave big bills in front of the hardcore legend, and Mick Foley seemed to consider... Stone vs. Waru Loud music blared over the arena speaker system as Waru walked to the ring flanked by Aimee Horihito and two large men. He walked halfway down the entrance ramp, and then signaled to the back... and Hiroshima walked out to join them. Waru held in his hand a cinderblock, and yelled for Stone to come to the ring and take a beating. She did come out to the ring, but walked with the rest of Dark Riders behind her. The two wrestled a short time, with Stone taking a hard shot to the groin of Waru. Waru shrugged it off, and battered Stone's head with the block of concrete. In the middle of the match the Silver Fox appeared on the PA system, showing scenes of the "match" on Friday and challenging Death to a match at Fight the Future II. Seeing him on the screen, Death grew enraged and ran back up the entrance ramp after him... along with Stone and the other Dark Riders. Waru won when Stone abandoned the match, and yelled that Scott Slugger was next. His victory was soured however, as rain began to fall from the rafters on Waru and his partners... an ominous omen for the future. Result: Waru by No Contest New fWo member Shots were shown of a contract signing earlier in the day, as Mike Bear officially joined the fWo roster. Bear sat solidly in his chair and looked grimly at the camera, a thin smile crossing his face. Scenes were shown of Bear in the TCW, and recent training in preparation for his fWo entry. US Marshal vs. Judge Steel The US Marshal came to the ring swinging his hat and with a great cheer from the crowd to greet him. He swung into the ring where Judge Steel was already waiting for him, and at once charged the Marshal, knocking him from the ring. Judge Steel continued his attack on the distracted US Marshal with elbow drops and chops to the chest. Judge Steel managed to send the Marshal from the ring, and followed him to the outside, picking up a chair as he went on the attack. This seemed to energize the Marshal, and he grabbed the chair from Judge Steel and scolded him for attempting to cheat. From there the Marshal made short work of Steel, pinning him after a devastating piledriver. Result: US Marshal by Pinfall The war continues... Scenes were shown of the brutal battle between Darkness and the Mad Trucker before cutting to a live interview of the Trucker. He was bandaged and obviously in pain, but looked serious and told the interviewer that even though it was a brutal match between the two at Fight the Future I, nothing was settled. He then challenged Darkness to a match on Warfare this week. In response the lights went out, casting the room into darkness. Sounds of a beating followed, as the camera cut back to the ring. Trouble vs. Ground Zero Trouble came to the ring as a unit, jeering the fans and making fun of them. Twitch began chasing a young woman around the stands as Ground Zero came to the ring, and double teamed Thumper. The two demolished the Trouble member inside the ring while Twitch ran to get back to the ring. Twitch broke a cover attempt, and fought back valiantly. He made a cover attempt, but was suddenly attacked by the Chameleon and Total Eclipse. The two attacked on the outside of the ring, and rolled Thumper back inside where an easy cover was made by Jackhammer. Ground Zero left the ring, on their way to a tag title shot at Fight the Future II. Result: Ground Zero by Pinfall Challenge made Austin Davis came to the ring, brushing aside fWo security and the commissioner. Davis grabbed a microphone and got into the ring, telling the crowd that he wasn't finished kicking rich boy's ass yet, and that he had a big tag match coming up at Fight the Future II with the EX-Internet champion. Davis told the crowd that it may wind up being a handicap match though, as nobody wanted to tag with Universal. Universal came out onto the entrance ramp and looked smug, telling Davis that he had a partner locked up already, and that he would be hearing from him on Warfare... Black Quicksilver (c) vs. Billy James Billy James came to the ring with a swagger, and accompanied by two lovely ladies. James smiled as he got into the ring and waited for Black Quicksilver and his title match. Quicksilver came to the ring, but was met with a diving plancha and then a tope on the outside. James continued his attack on the outside of the ring, dropping legs, fists, and elbows into the back of Quicksilver. Sensing that he had Black Quicksilver weakened enough, James rolled him back into the ring. Black Quicksilver proved to be only faking however, and quickly rolled up Billy James when he went for a cover. Black Quicksilver laughed at James, telling him that he is and always will be a loser. Just then, the Chameleon came out onto the entrance ramp and stared down at Black Quicksilver. The cameras went off the air with this standoff... Result: Black Quicksilver by Pinfall
