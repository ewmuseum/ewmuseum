Introduction The Fans Wrestling Organization enters its sixth year on a high note. Banished from the United States for several months due to restrictive legislation from the government, harsh laws concerning substance abuse have been repealed to punish the individual rather than the organization. Now, with the promotion back in the United States it can focus on rebuilding a image tarnished by months away from home. With the blocking issue of location solved, attention must now be given to rasing ratings and buyrates in an attempt to stop the slide before it becomes too huge to overcome. Already severe damage has been done to the media relationship of the fWo, with more bad news impending. But the road agents that guided the promotion through this difficult period not only have work to do outside the promotion, but from within as well. Trust has been erroded between the roster and the managers because of the lie Silver Fox told in order to bring the promotion home. In addition, the return home has meant that the fWo is more dependant on the group of investors who have self-appointed themselves as the standing Board of Directors. While some view the promotion as a fun adventure, like Texas Billionaire William Humbold, others see the fWo as means to an end in achieving strange goals, as is the case with Mr. Tort. Still other members of the board have yet to make their motivations known at all. The fWo enters their sixth year with a newly crowned champion and the return of several familiar faces from the past. Motivations of these new arrivals have yet to be fully determined, but with a landmark in the Fans Wrestling Organization now in the past, sights can be set on new goals, new ambitions, and new dangers...
