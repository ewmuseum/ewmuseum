Sunday Slaughter, 9 September 2001

9 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Vic Creed sat on the edge of a bench, doing sitting curls with a dumbbell. The dumbbell itself was huge. Though its exact weight was unclear, it was quite clearly well over 200 pounds. Creed...curled...uncurled...curled...uncurled. His face was bright red, though not from exertion - he had been doing this for a while. A snarl was across his lips. "Ross..." "ROSS..." "ROSS!" Creed shouted his enemy's name as he lifted, pumping himself up. The door opened, and Creed looked up. In stepped Dawn Van Dammage. She stopped cold as she entered. She had never seen Vic looking this intense, and that was saying something. "Hey... Vic...I'll come back, alright? I was just coming in to put my diary back." "Sure," he said, caring not in the least. She turned and left, and Creed did one more rep, then set the weight down. "Diary, eh?" He got up and opened the drawer where she had put it. He flipped it open and flipped through a few random pages. His face quickly turned to an even deeper snarl, if such a thing was possible. "Looks like somebody's going to have to be dealt with." The Boston Strangler vs. Douglas Inside the arena, the fans buzzed with anticipation as to what the first match of the night would be. fWo Ring Announcer Matt Panzer counted the fans down, while Jeremiah Frankton & James Armstrong welcomed everyone to tonight's edition of Sunday Night Slaughter. The fans cheered loudly as the pyrotechnics and opening Slaughter music rocked the foundations of the Conseco Fieldhouse, home to the National Basketball Association's Indiana Pacers. Backstage, behind the entrance to the ring and the giant Slaughter video wall, Douglas was busy doing some stretches in preparation for his match with the Boston Strangler. While it would be Douglas' only match of the evening, it was the Boston Strangler' first out of three total. Douglas did some light jumping jacks, while Frankton & Armstrong continued to run down tonight's card at ringside. Suddenly, from the shadows, two men appeared behind Douglas. Kenny Rock, the reigning World Champion of the Asylum; along with A.D. From behind, Kenny Rock nailed Douglas with a sledgehammer in the back of the head. A.D. carried a body bag, and the two men quickly placed Douglas inside, before hauling Douglas to a nearby limosuine and tossing him in the trunk. Rock & A.D quickly got inside the limosuine, and it screeched out of the arena, leaving skidmarks behind. A few security officials gave chase on foot, but it was too late. Rock, A.D and the limosuine were out of sight. And so was Douglas. Inside the arena, Douglas' theme music began to play - "Underdog" by Testeagles. As the fans stood to their feet and cheered wildly, they awaited for his arrival, but he never appeared. Douglas was already gone from the arena due to the backstage attack. The cameras panned the backstage area, but there was no sight of him. The fans in the arena did not know what happened, and as of that time, neither did Frankton & Armstrong. The referee stood in the ring talking things over with Matt Panzer, before Chris Universal walked out from the backstage area down to ringside, drawing a handful of cheers from the crowd. Universal and the referee talked things over, before Universal walked to the back. Slaughter went to a commercial break, amidst a sea of confusion... Result: ? Primetime vs. The Boston Strangler The fans sat in silence in the arena, not knowing what to think of what Kenny Rock had said. Their silence came to an abrupt halt when "Suffocate" by Staind echoed throughout the arena, bringing out one-half of the fWo World Tag Team Champions, Primetime. Primetime stood on the SlaughterStage, soaking in the jeers of the crowd. For unknown reasons, Primetime quickly walked to the right of the stage, and waited for the arrival of the Boston Strangler. 'O Fortuna' echoed throughout the Conseco Fieldhouse, and the fans erupted in cheers. The arena lights dimmed, as Primetime removed the tag team belt from around his waist, before holding the gold in his clutches as he waited for the first appearance of the Strangler. Seconds passed, and still Strangler hadn't arrived. At least, from where Primetime was looking. Down near the crowd by the stage, the Strangler came walking. He walked up the ramp and as Primetime turned he saw him, but before he could do what he intended, Strangler charged and speared him into the Slaughter video wall from behind. The two disappeared behind the curtain as the tag team title flew in the air and landed on the stage. A thud echoed throughout the arena as the cameras panned backstage to find the Strangler getting up off Primetime, before walking up the Slaughter entrance. He appeared on the stage and held his arms in the air as the crowd cheered, while his music finally stopped. The referee joined Strangler up on stage, and the Strangler grabbed the tag team title and waited for Primetime to come out. When he did, Primetime made him say hello to the gold. Primetime clutched his jaw as he laid on the stage, before Strangler grabbed him by the hair, slapped on a headlock, and dragged him down to ringside. As they reached ringside, Primetime pushed him into the ring post, and Strangler hit it forehead first. He staggered backwards and Primetime took him out with a belly to back suplex. Primetime climbed into the ring dazed, where he waited a minute. The referee gave the World Tag Team Title to the timekeeper, before signalling for the bell. The match was on. Primetime climbed onto the ring apron and backed up, before running and leaping off the apron with a diving elbow on Strangler. The referee began a ten count, but the furthest it got was three. Primetime grabbed Strangler by the hair and rolled him into the ring, before climbing in and hooking a leg. One. Two. No. Strangler got a shoulder up, and Primetime continued on the offensive attack. Primetime forearmed Strangler in the face as he brought him up, before snapmaring him back down to the canvas. Primetime stood in front of him and talked trash as he sat on the mat, but Strangler reached up and grabbed him by the trunks and sent him flying behind him to the floor, or so he intended. Instead Primetime caught himself and landed on the ring apron, before climbing up. Strangler stood up, thinking Primetime was back on the outside, but when he turned around Primetime was right there ready to take him out with a superkick to the jaw. Strangler went flying backwards into the turnbuckles, and Primetime followed him in and made his chest a crimson red after four or five reverse knife edge chops. Primetime then pulled him out from the corner with an irish whip, before reversing it into a short arm clothesline. Strangler sturggled to climb back up to a vertical base, and Primetime repeated it. Strangler again wouldn't stay down and this time, Primetime pulled him towards him and lifted him up onto his back for a Samoan Drop. Instead, Strangler fell behind him and hooked him by the arms and legs, before taking him out with a russian legsweep. Both men were out of it on the mat for a few moments, before Strangler draped an arm onto Primetime. Primetime got his foot on the bottom rope, breaking up the two count. Primetime then rolled out onto the floor, bending over and tending to his back. From behind, Strangler baseball slid out onto the floor, and when Primetime turned around, Strangler took him out with a clothesline. Strangler slammed him head first into the ring steps twice, before grabbing him by the head, and slapping him across the face. Ironically, the last time these two men met a month ago on August 12, it was Primetime who started the match with the 'slap heard around the world'. The referee climbed into the ring and began a ten count. Strangler then dragged Primetime over near the announcers tables, and slammed him head first into the table where Frankton & Armstrong was located. He hit him again, before throwing him up onto the table. The fans at ringside cheered while Frankton & Armstrong escaped, looking for safety. Strangler and Primetime were both now on the table, and they exchanged punches, before Strangler grabbed him by the arm and pulled him towards him. Primetime blocked, punched Strangler, Strangler blocked, and then hooked him for a hip toss. However, it was Primetime who blocked that, and hip tossed Strangler through the table next to them. Jose & Pepe, the Spanish commentators, ran for their lives. Strangler went flying through the table, as the referee reached six. Primetime leaped off the table; before rolling into the ring. The referee counted to seven, and then eight. But Primetime rolled back outside the ring, stopping the count. Primetime walked over to Strangler and spit on him several times, before kicking at his head. Primetime rolled back into the ring to break the count again, before walking over and kneeling over Strangler, slapping him across the face, and as he did this, he wore a bright smirk across his face. Primetime rolled back into the ring to break up the count again, and the fans booed. Becoming annoyed with Primetime's antics, the fans showed it by starting up a "Primetime is a pussy" chant. Primetime ignored it, and this time, he kneeled over the Strangler and unleashed several punches to the head to the apparently unconscious Strangler. Primetime stood to his feet as the chant grew louder than before. Primetime responded by telling the fans that their hero was about to be defeated. Strangler slowly climbed to his feet... first to his knees, as he reached up to Primetime. Primetime looked down at Strangler and had no mercy for him, as he slapped him across the face again. Primetime boasted of his accomplishment to the crowd, but while he did this, Strangler caught him off guard with a lowblow. Bent over in pain, Primetime staggered around, before Strangler stood up and rolled Primetime into the ring, breaking the count at eight. Strangler followed in, and stood, before screaming as he held his arms outwards; but pointing them towards the air. Primetime stood to his feet and from behind Strangler grabbed him and hit a German suplex. He held onto him, and took him down with another German suplex. He brought him up once more, and went for a third, but Primetime escaped with a go-behind, and appeared to be going for the Backdrop Driver. The move that ended careers. The Strangler was prepared, and escaped with a go-behind of his own, and hitting the third German suplex w/ a bridge. One. Two. Primetime shouldered out. The two men climbed to their feet and hit him in the face, before sending him to the ropes. Primetime reversed it, and ducked for a backdrop. Strangler returned and took him out with a gutwrench powerbomb. One. Two. No. Both men were pulling out all stops, and now Strangler waited behind Primetime, as he climbed to a vertical base. Strangler grabbed him for the Boston Massacre. He locked it in. It was certain that this match was over, as long as Strangler kept him in the center of the ring. He knew that every moment he held Primetime in his grasp was a moment PT spent in pain, and he knew that Primetime would not be able to take much more. Primetime appeared to be in deep agony, and also seemed to be ready to throw in the towel, so to speak. Primetime however wasn't about ready to give up. He inched his way towards the ropes to escape. Finally, he grabbed the bottom rope, and the Strangler was forced to relinquish the hold. Primetime fell out of the ring to the floor, and Strangler joined him on the outside. The referee began a ten count, as the Strangler sneaked up from behind Primetime and grabbed him him for the Massacre. Primetime however escaped with an elbow shot to the head; and another. He held his arm though, as he was in obvious pain. Strangler shrugged off the shots and this time he locked in the Massacre. Primetime was now in more pain than before. The referee reached six before Primetime escaped with another elbow shot. This time, instead of going for the Massacre, Strangler walked up and hooked him for a headlock, before running towards the stairs... and taking him out with a tornado bulldog. As the referee reached the count of nine, Strangler quickly slid into the ring before the referee made a final count. Ten. The match was over; and it appeared that the Strangler won the match. The referee conferred with Matt Panzer, and it was announced. The Boston Strangler had won the match by countout, and would advance to the third match of the gauntlet against Primetime's tag team partner, the Flying Frenchie. Strangler was happy about the win, as he walked backwards up the stage. Primetime climbed up to his knees and gazed to where the Strangler was. Primetime' expression appeared to be saying "you got lucky this time." Strangler reached the stage and as he turned around, he came face to face with Frenchie, who held a steel chair. He swung and took Strangler out, before standing above him and kicking him. Primetime (as quickly as he could anyways) joined him up there eventually, and the two took turns, before referees & security came out and send them to the back. While the Strangler may have advanced to the final match; it wasn't certain if he would be able to compete against the Frenchie... especially after one grueling match already. Coming Out The camera caught sight of Erik Kelly walking into the Conseco Fieldhouse with his dufflebag slumped over his massive shoulder. Erik approached a door, and walked in. He looked around. The room was uncommonly darker than most locker rooms he was in. Erik figured the maintenance crew hadn't fixed the lighting, and he made no big deal of it. Looking around briefly, he noticed no one was in the room or the bathroom. He put his bag down on a nearby chair, and began to undress to get into his ring gear. When suddenly he heard a noise coming from the bathroom. It was silent again. Erik dismissed.until he heard it again. It sounded like someone was moaning. A man's voice. It was deep and husky. It didn't sound like pain. It sounded like. The sound of paper across flesh. The adjustment of putting pants back on. And the eventual flush of the toilet.
