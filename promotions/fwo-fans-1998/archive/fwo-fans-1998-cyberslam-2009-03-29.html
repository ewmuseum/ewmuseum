Ab Initio Stagehands moved crates, the activity becoming more and more hectic as the clock ticked closer and closer to the time. Beaven smiled, his hands folded in front of him as he watched the activity unfold. This was the culmination of a dream; a dream that had been years in the making. Years of raising money, of convincing agencies to supply permits, of building out endless legal documents and various forms... what seemed like years of attracting talent. Now; it was time. In, he checked his watch with a start, in ten minutes they would go on the air. Cyberslam... the name felt at first strange, then as time went by it became more familiar. Like an old friend, and it fit perfectly. He nodded as the Flying Frenchie walked by, his mask fitted and tight under his beret. Scott Slugger was talking to Austin Davis, the two discussing the event to come with the cautious planning of ring vets. "I'm heading out," said Chris Finn, and he nodded, adjusting the headpiece so he could provide the right cues when the time came. Finn stopped next to James Armstrong and shook his hand, the measured tones of a professional at work. It was going to happen... the first Pay Per View, Cyberslam, and even though it had taken longer than he hoped, the day was finally here. "Nervous?" Beaven turned, and smiled as he nodded. John Smith was pulling on his mask, a green leather one that he would wrestle under tonight as the Chameleon. Most of the wrestlers had seemed interested in the publicity and the paycheck, but Smith had grown closer to Beaven, a friend through the struggle to get back on the air. It had been Smith's advice and Smith's optimisim that had kept him going. Kept him pushing through. "It's been a long time getting here... I'd just like it to be perfect."
