fWo Blacklist (February 2001)

17 February 2001

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Saturday, 02.17.01 "All I want in life is to be happy." -Crusader fWo Blacklist Because of the time spent on the fWo, we take great pride in our roleplays, our artwork, and our site content. We vigorously protect our property, and also look out for other eWrestling promotions on the internet who have their hard work stolen by people who are either too lazy or too ignorant to create their own material. The sites listed below are ones that have crossed the line of theft and stolen images or code from this site or sites that we partner with. We encourage you to look at these sites for material they might have stolen from you, and to contact the ISP and host of these areas. If you are a eWrestling federation owner or a fan of eWrestling and observe or detect a site engaged in theft, send email to this address: [detail withheld] and we will invesigate and add the site to this list if warranted. Please note that nothing gives us less pleasure than adding a site to this list. In many cases it's simply a matter of education as to what material on the web is free domain and what material is not. In all cases we would be happy to work with you to avoid putting on this list, or moving you off of the list if you are already on it. There is more than enough material on the internet for everyone to have a quality web site without resorting to theft, and we will help you get there if possible. Web sites like the fWo thrive on competition, and it's in our best interest and yours for there to be as many great ePromotions available as possible. "CSWC" http://www.geocities.com/cswc_2000/ Toss an eye patch on Austin Davis and what do get? A pirate wrestling federation might be a good guess, but it turns out that the CSWC are the scam artists responsible for the latest batch of eTheft. The CSWC was "kind" enough to paste their logo over ours, but the sheer volume of material stolen makes this one of the most pathetic and disgusting examples yet. "fWo" http://www.fwowrestling.pyar.com/ This site "evolved" from a South Park Wrestling promotion to a complete rip-off of the fWo. In addition to stealing the fWo's art and pages, the owner was also so lazy that they didn't bother to correct the (gray) background colors of the GIF files for their (black) background. Perhaps even stupider was the renaming of the characters. Scott Slugger became... Joey Abs. Lame doesn't begin to cover it. "Empire Wrestling Federation" http://members.aol.com/[detail withheld]/EwF.html The "Empire Wrestling Federation" not only stole artwork and page design from the fWo, but then lied about it to a variety of fWo members who asked him about it. Perhaps the biggest crime this webmaster committed is not the fact that the he stole the artwork and page design, but then succeeded in fouling up the code so the site looks disjointed and, in the words of fWo announcer James Armstrong, "craptacular". If you want to express your happiness at the person responsible, feel free to email him at "[detail withheld]"... but beware, he runs away as soon as he's confronted by proof of his underhanded deeds. "Fans Wrestling Onslaught" http://www.mygo.com/home/entertainment/[name withheld]/fwo.htm The Fans Wrestling Onslaught not only stole artwork, design, and site colors from the fWo, but were even so lazy as to keep the same fed name, changing only the last word in "fWo". Not only did the promotion steal the artwork, but even kept the same names for shows and features on the site. If you want to express your happiness at the person responsible, feel free to email him at "[detail withheld]".
