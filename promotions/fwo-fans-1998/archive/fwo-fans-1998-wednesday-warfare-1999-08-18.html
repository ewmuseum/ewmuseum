Wednesday Warfare, 18 August 1999

18 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Wednesday Warfare opened on a shot of the door of the French Foundation dressing room. Loud arguments could be heard from within, and suddenly the small form of the Flying Trucker burst from the dressing room, running directly into Geoff Green. Geoff staggered back, and then smiled widely. "Oh! Hello Flying Trucker! I've got those coloring books you wanted right here... just a..." But the Flying Trucker grabbed Geoff Green and tossed him into the wall with a loud crash. The Flying Trucker cursed, "Zut! Slow witted American fool! Ze Frenchie is back to himzelf... and ze Surg'en is going to be tortured for zis!" The Frenchie began to stomp away but paused, and walked back toward the dressing room. He went inside, and further arugments were heard. The door thrust open once more and the Flying Frenchie emerged, holding his trustworthy beret. Fitting it atop his head and scowling, the Frenchie marched off in search of the mysterious man known only as the Surgeon. After he had departed, the door to the French Foundation's locker room swung open, and Primetime and Mitch Wilson looked out. Turning to Mitch, Primetime said, "Gosh, whatever has gotten into our pal the Flying Trucker?" Mitch shook his head in confusion, and then brightened. "Hey! We've got that autograph signing at the children's hospital! C'mon, let's get to 'er!" The two walked away as the fireworks sounded, offically opening up show... Briana vs. Pike Briana walked to the ring to start Warfare off with a match, and raised her hands to the crowd who cheered her on. Briana thrust her fists in the air, and got a large pop from the audience. She then motioned to her waist, signaling that she wanted a title shot, which brought another big cheer from the crowd. Pleased with this reaction, Briana paced the center of the ring, waiting for her challenger. An unmemorable Third Eye Blind song began to play as Mike Heftel walked out onto the entrance ramp, dressed in a frat sweater vest and a "PKA" hat on his head. Announced as "Pike", the frat boy wrestler swaggered to the ring, waving his hands at the male members of the audience and pausing from time to time to flirt with some of the more attractive female members of the audience. He leaned over the guard rail and attempted to grope a female fan, but was rebuffed at once. Smirking, Pike claimed that she must be one of those "lesbians" and continued to the ring. Once he got to the ringside area he signaled to the back, and several delivery men dressed in toga apparel brought kegs down on dollys and placed them alongside the ring. Climbing into the ring, Pike looked Briana up and down, letting his eyes crawl all over her skin and pass over her curves. Paying little attention to her face, Pike went for a tie up, but quickly turned the hold into a groping armbar, with carefully placed appendages for maximum contact. Briana grunted in disgust and kicked backward, hitting Pike in the knee and sending him spiraling away. Briana then tossed him into the corner and went for a big splash, but Pike caught her in a bear hug, and loosely maintained the hold while attempting to bury his head in her cleavage. Briana grew more enraged, and began to use closed fists to hammer the skull of Pike. Warned by the ref for this action, Briana backed off, but Pike charged again, this time attempting to grab hold of her posterior. Briana blocked it, and picked up Pike for a quick powerslam in the center of the ring. Not satisfied with this, Briana picked him up again and again, slamming him down to the mat until he was unconscious. Not wanting to make a close cover, Briana placed a foot on his head and asked for the three count. She easily got it, and left the ring the victor. She started up the entrance ramp, but then had an idea and turned back to the ring. Grabbing the kegs, Briana tossed them into the crowd, much to the delight of the fans, who began to chant her name in unison. Result: Briana by Pinfall Remembering themselves The cameras followed the Flying Frenchie as he stomped down the hall. As he turned a corner, he came across another locker room where loud arguments were taking place. From inside the room a rather deep-voiced southern man was arguing with what appeared to be two badly acting French hookers. The Frenchie started to pass the room by, but then thought better of it and walked back to the door, flinging it open with authority. Inside stood the Mad Trucker, along with Cookie and Alice. Cookie pointed to the Flying Frenchie exclaiming, "Zut! He is we'ring your outfit! Kill zim!" The Trucker turned to the Frenchie, and looked questioningly at his great foe. The Frenchie and the Trucker shared a brief glance, and then the Frenchie nodded. "Zis is true... ze Surg'on has tricked us all with 'is trickery. We must help zese poor deluded fools remember who ze are... Come! We must find ze Surg'on and beat zis truth out of zim!" The Trucker nodded, and the two stalked from the room in search of the Surgeon. Behind them, Alice shook her head in disgust. Cookie nodded in response. "Stupid Americans..." Courage arrives. As Warfare returns from the commercial break, fireworks and pyrotechnics burst all over the place. Suddenly, out comes fWo newcomer "Courage", but the fans don't appear to recognize him. Courage walks to the ring with Jake, his rather overweight body-guard, smirking at the fans, before rolling into the ring. Courage demands a microphone and then says "For all you morons out there who haven't had the pleasure of knowing me, I am none other than the greatest professional wrestler to walk the isle... Courage. And right now, all of you are witnessing my debut night here in the fWo. Consider it an honor, and a privelege, to be watching me right now. I'm out here tonight because after spending a couple of nights with a bunch of the other so-called "wrestlers" in the lockerroom... after watching them, and getting to know them, I don't think there's one single solitary "wrestler" here in the fwo that has the talent that I have. I don't think they have the type of wrestling ability that I have. I don't think there's one person in the lockerroom that can defeat me. From the fWo World Champion Scott Slugger, down on the line to the "jobbers" like Ultra-Purple Freak... and so on and so forth. If anyone, thinks they have the talent, they can march their ass down to the ring and make my debut, a special one.. when I completely kick their ass. Because I will make sure you never forget my name... or ever give me a welcoming response like that again.." Jake nods slowly, as the crowd responds negatively. Courage is about to leave, when suddenly, the arena goes completely dark. Some type of structure in the shape of a dollar sign begins lowering in front of the Travis-tron, before we hear, although faintly, "raise the roof and bow before your master" as loud heavy metal music begins playing. On the big screen we see explosions, a tornado ripping through a city, and other various things before the word "UNIVERSAL" appears on the screen. The dollar sign pyro lights up and appears where the "s" in "UNIVERSAL" should be. The pyro explodes with green and gold fireworks all over the place, before the man himself, Chris Universal walks out wearing a gold metallic shirt. Universal has a new hair style, with his hair propped up with thin pieces poking out on the top of his head, while the rest of his hair rests on his shoulders and back. Universal wears some "60s" style sunglasses while licking his lips as a gesture to the crowd. Universal walks down the ramp, while his name still appears on the Travis-tron, and fireworks continue to shoot out of the dollar sign. Universal climbs into the ring where another dollar sign pyro lowers into place. Jericho walks in front of the pyro and rests on one knee while flexing. Fireworks shoot out all over the place, while Courage looks on. The pyrotechnic raises back into the air, before Universal smirks at Courage, and then walks right up to him. Universal' music finally stops, and Universal begins to speak. "Do you have any idea just who the hell I am? OF COURSE YOU DO! Each and every person on this planet knows just exactly who I am. When I walk into a restaraunt, the waitresses gather around in awe at the mere presence of me. They, like all these fans, chant my name. "Chris! Chris! Chris!" When I walk into my financial institution... they bow at my feet, and they kiss my foot in hopes that I will even acknowledge their presence. When I walk into this arena, all these people immediately begin chanting my name, they raise the roof and bow before me because they know that I am Chris Universal. They know that Chris Universal is the biggest, brightest star in the whole damn sky. You, on the otherhand, come onto my show here tonight... and you make a challenge like it's a bodily function. Like that's going to make me impressed. Well, I've got two words for you: it doesn't. It doesn't impress me because everyone sits there and makes idle threats, and nobody has the balls to come through. I'm calling your bluff. I'll accept your challenge, and let me tell you why. #1, I'm Chris Universal, I can do whatever I want in accordance with federal regulation #369481r2d2c3po23 in section C of the federal regulation guidelines which can be accessed via your local library or via the internet. #2, I understand that your real name is... Chris West? Don't you realize that I am the only... and I mean ONLY person on the face of this planet that can uphold the responsibilities, priveleges and the honor that being named Chris entails? Chris Finn can't, and I still think he should change his name to something that rhymes with Chris that starts with a p, just like I suggested to him a year ago. Chris Kidman obviously feels the same way I do, because he changed his name to "wretch"... which fits him, because that's what all these super groovy chicks in the crowd do when they see him. Back to your challenge... I'll accept your challenge because I don't think you have the courage to change your name if I beat you in a match here tonight. In fact, if I win tonight... you may never.... e-e-ever call yourself Chris again, understand me?" Universal smirks at Courage, who pushes Universal down. Courage then nods down at Universal, before telling him to kiss his ass and leaving as his music begins to play. Universal begins throwing a temper tantrum in the ring, jumping up and down wildly, and telling Courage that he "ruined his shirt"... even though no damage appears to have been done. With that, Warfare went to a commercial break with a match apparently set for later tonight. Mike Bear (c) vs. Stone We return from the commercials to find Matt Loco waiting behind the Travis-tron as Chris Universal walks through the curtains to the dressing room. Matt Loco asks Universal if he's ready to wrestle twice in one night. Universal gives him blank stare, when Loco responds by saying; "Don't you remember? You and Lance Star are facing Ground Zero tonight on Warfare... and it looks like you'll be wrestling twice now with your challenge to Courage." Universal looks around as if he's thinking of an answer, but only responds with "I'm Chris Universal, and in accordance with federal regulation #123456789101112 I can do whatever I want!" Loco says "I thought it was federal regulation #369481r2d2c3po23?" and Universal shrugs and says "That's what I said!". With that, we head up to the ring where Stone awaits the fWo Hardcore Champion, Mike Bear. On the videoscreen, the number "4" appears as Stone's music finally dies down. "Psycho Man" begins to play and out from the curtains walks Mike Bear, who has the Hardcore Title draped over his shoulder. Bear walks over near the commentators position and hands them his Hardcore Title, before he turns around and faces the ring. Suddenly, Stone jumps propels herself over and she takes Bear out on the ground with a pescado. Stone lifts Bear up and slams him onto the concrete, as the referee goes out of the ring and attempts to get the action in the ring so the match can be officially started. Stone taunts the crowd with a look of hatred on her face and some harsh words are also directed at the commentators. Noticably absent are the rest of the Dark Riders, who have usually been at her side in recent weeks. Stone continues by grabbing around the mouth of Bear and pulling apart, trying to stretch his mouth open. With his mouth open, Stone musters up a wad of saliva and spits it into the mouth of Bear. A small "She's hardcore!" chant begins in a section adjacent to the area. The commentators comment that they've never seen someone do something that disgusting in their tenure of following wrestling. Stone then rakes the eyes of Bear several times, before shoving him to the ground. She walks over and pulls James Armstrong off his chair and then takes his chair. Stone walks over and slams it into the side of Mike Bear, in the ribs area. She repeats the process, before pulling him up by the head and rolling him into the ring. Stone tosses the chair into the ring and follows him in, and the referee follows as well. The referee signals for the bell and the match is officially underway. Stone does a snapmare takeover on Bear, before kicking him in the back. A cover attempt, but a kickout by Bear. Stone follows it up by pulling Bear up by his head, and whipping him into the turnbuckles. Stone flies in with a cartwheel handspring elbow, hitting right into the chin of Bear. As Bear staggers out, Stone takes him down with a Japanese Armdrag. Stone is relentless in her assault, as she follows it up with a lightning quick legdrop that would make D-Lo Brown proud. She demands the referee to count, but she only gets a 2-count. Stone jumps up to her feet and grabs Bear. She throws him into the turnbuckles again, and again goes for a cartwheel handspring elbow, but Bear moves out of the way and she crashes hard into the corner. As she staggers out, Bear takes her down with a superkick, before covering her. 1, 2; but only 2. Bear brings her up to her feet, and nails her with a lowblow, before hitting a double-arm DDT. Bear doesn't go for another cover, instead, he rolls underneath the bottom rope onto the floor. He then begins looking for something underneath the ring. After several moments, he finds a garbage can. As he pulls it out, he spots Stone flying off the top rope with a bodypress. Instead of catching her, he lets her crash hard into the garbage can. He then waits for her to get up, before slamming it onto her head and knocking her down again. He then looks underneath the ring to find another garbage can, stacked with weapons. He finds a singapore cane and slides it into the ring. He finds a coffee pot, and slides that in too. He finds a package of CD jewel cases and slides that into the ring as well. Finally, he lifts her up by her hair, and rolls her into the ring. From there, he whacks her in the head with the package of cd jewel cases, which busts her open. Then, he takes the coffee pot and breaks it over her head. And finally, he takes the singapore cane, and whips her to the ropes. She returns and ducks the singapore cane shot to the head, and instead it is the referee who gets hit. As Bear looks concerned for the referee, the lights go out. When they come back on, Death is in the ring assisting Stone. They stomp at the head of Bear repeatedly, before whipping him to the ropes and taking him down by clotheslining him with the singapore cane. Death and Stone raise their arms in celebration of what they have accomplished. Stone then whispers into the ear of Death, and then she walks outside and grabs a microphone. She comes back into the ring and her and Death tie Bear up in the ropes. Stone then hands Death the singapore cane, before speaking. "Have you had enough yet, Bear? Are you ready to hand over that Hardcore Title to me?" Stone then puts the microphone up to the mouth of Bear, and he quietly says no. Stone instructs Death to whack him with the cane. She then asks Bear, "Had enough?" and his "no" becomes a bit louder. This goes on three or four more times, and each time Bear' "no" becomes louder. With blood rushing from the head of Bear, Death whacks him again, even harder. Stone asks again, and Bear yells out "NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!" Finally, Stone whispers into the ear of Death, before saying "Alright, Bear... this should be enough to convince you to hand over the Hardcore Title." Death whacks Bear in the testicular area with the singapore cane, and Bear yelps out in pain. "Had enough, Bear?" "hell nooooooooooooooo!" Finally, Stone and Death, obviously frustrated, have another conversation when the lights go out. The lights go back on and Cactus Jack is in the ring. He nails Death with a garbage can shot to the head, before kicking Stone in the lower regions. He then drops the garbage can and gives her a double-arm DDT. He then reaches into HER pants, and pulls out... Mr. Socko? "I knew all the chicks had a thing for Mr. Socko, but Stone too?" as Stone gets up, staggering, Foley puts the sock on his right hand and shoves it down into the throat of Stone. Several seconds later, Stone passes out. Foley then releases Bear from the ropes, and says "Pin that bitch!" before brawling with Death outside of the ring. Bear, staggers out from the ropes, and falls on Stone... and the referee recovers moments later, to count the pin. Bear retains the Hardcore Title, and both he and Stone are too exhausted to get up. Suddenly, sounds of thunder and lightning are heard throughout the arena, and Crucifix and Rain walk down to the ring... together? Rain climbs into the ring and grabs Bear off of Stone. Crucifix walks over near Foley & Death, who were fighting. Crucifix points up into the ring at Stone, almost telling Death to go check on her. Death obliges and Crucifix grabs Foley by the neck. Rain has Bear by the neck near Finn & Armstrong, while Crucifix has Foley by the neck near the Spanish announcers table. Simultaneously, the duo chokeslam Foley & Bear through the commentators tables, before raising their arms in the air. Like an ominous cloud hanging over a city, the the unlikely duo of Rain & Crucifix appear on a path of destruction that will continue at Sunday Slaughter... Result: Mike Bear by pinfall. Fix it, or die. We resume to the search of The Surgeon, with The Flying Trucker (?) leading the charge. As Cookie, Alice, Primetime, Mitch & The Mad Frenchie follow, they turn a corner and spot a room. Frenchie, instructs Primetime to open the door. Primetime looks at the camera and says, "Kids, Primetime says to always knock before entering! You never know what could be going on behind the door, and you certainly want to see something that's not intended for viewing by kids!" Primetime knocks, before they enter. In the room, Surgeon is sitting down, apparently speaking to someone on the phone. The Surgeon looks up at the angry people standing in front of him. He holds up his hand and says "I'll be with you in just one minute." Surgeon then tells the other person on the phone "I'm sorry for the mistake, but looking over your chats, we've mistakenly replaced your heart with a baked potato. You have about thirty seconds to live. Good day." He then hung up the phone, and looked at everyone and said "What can I do you fo..." before he could finish, Frenchie angrily demanded that he fix everything. Surgeon says "Ok, ok, I've had my amusement. I'll fix everything. I'll have to turn the lights off though, because it's a secret process. Also, camera man, could you please wait outside?" With the door closed behind him, the camera man waited outside before hearing a ruckus going on inside. As the loud banging continued, suddenly the Surgeon slipped out from the room. The camera man attempted to get a comment from him, but to no avail. Surgeon stomped off, before Frenchie, Mitch & Primetime came out of the room. Frenchie asked Mitch if he cared about what the kids watched anymore. Primetime said "Kids suck. They're always crying, and wanting toys, and crap. They always burp, and slobber all over your brand new shirt that costed a lot of money." Frenchie looked pleased with his answer, and then he asked Mitch if he liked his road trip. Mitch looked at TFF and responded "Road trip? Road trip? Frenchie... what are you talking about?" Frenchie again looked pleased "Finally, ze French Foundation iz back to normal.", before the trio walked away. The break up of the Hollywood Blondes? Backstage once again, a confrontation appears featuring Lance Star and Chris Universal. Star is yelling at Universal, telling him "We've only been a team for one month, but I'm sick of this crap. It's always Chris Chris Chris! What about Lance? What about Lance Star, huh? I am the Future of professional wrestling and you're always concerned about what YOU want to do. And if it wasn't for my unparallelled wrestling skills carrying the team, along with your BITCH showing off her breasts, we wouldn't be winning all these matches." Universal then reminded him they've only wrestled one match together, and they lost. Star responded by saying "I don't care. We threw out the challenge and nobody accepted because they fear Lance Star, not Chris Universal. The commissioner had to FORCE Ground Zero to wrestle us. They don't want to be embarassed by a newcomer who can outwrestle them on any day of the week, twice on Wednesday. I'm sick of your spotlight hogging ass. You get a nifty new entrance and what do I get? NOTHING! I quit." before he left the arena, with the fWo camera crew trying to get a word from him. Universal appeared to be dumbfounded, and then threw another temper tantrum as we headed to a commercial. The Boston Strangler vs. Speed Demon We return to live arena action as The Speed Demon makes his entrance. Loud music plays as Speed Demon climbs into the ring and awaits his opponent, The Boston Strangler. On cue, O Fortuna begins to play as The Boston Strangler makes his way out, his first appearance on Warfare since his departure from the fWo last fall. Strangler comes out wearing a straight jacket, but quickly removes it and tosses it into the crowd. Strangler walks down to the bottom of the ramp, and watches the Speed Demon pace nervously. Strangler looks up into the ring with an evil smile across his face, and a look of pain in his eye. He dared the Speed Demon to come fight him, and almost foolishly, the Speed Demon applied. Demon got a running start and baseball slid onto the floor, where he and Strangler exchanged punches. Strangler poked the eye of the Demon, before whipping him into the guardrail. As Demon staggered out Strangler spinebustered him onto the floor. Strangler then rolled him into the ring, and slapped the back of his head. He then applied the Boston Massacre, and Speed Demon attempted to tap out the best he could. Strangler refused to release the whole despite having won the match, until Nikki came out onto the entrance ramp and looked on with concern. Strangler saw her, and just applied more pressure to Speed Demon. Nikki motioned as if she was gonna go down to the ring, but Death & Eron made their way out. They charged the ring and Strangler released the hold. Strangler looked at Death & Eron in the ring, Death still suffering from the brawl with Foley from earlier on. Meanwhile, Primetime came out onto the entrance ramp and escorted Nikki backstage, asking her where she's been the last month or so. Strangler walked backwards up the stage, keeping a keen eye on Death & Eron. Strangler reached the entrance ramp and lifted his arms in the air, before Darkness came out and stood at his side. Darkness raised and lowered his arms, and the arena went pitch black before going to a commercial with the fans left wondering what had occured... Result: Boston Strangler by submission. Changing. "I don't understand." Fallen looked up, his eyes dark and bloodshot. Blood caked on his face and on his mouth. "You don't need to." The voice came from the dark, and it was cold... so icy cold. "This... this isn't right. This isn't... God..." There was a faint chuckle in the room, and John Skyler felt his blood run cold. "God? Oh, come now. What does he really care for you anyway? He's a mystery manager. An absentie landlord. All this faith, and what does it really matter? Don't go to church tomorrow. Don't even go next weekend. Don't bother going again, because if there is a God, he will forgive you. Absolution my friend, the escape clause in everything. Because let's face it... I'm just telling you what you already know. Isn't that right, John?" John's eyes bleed as he looked up into the light and saw only darkness... Hollywood Blondes vs. Ground Zero As we return from the commercial breaks (featuring SURGE! and Microsoft), as well as the nifty preview for "Meltdown", we find Ground Zero in the ring awaiting apparently just Chris Universal, as Lance Star left the building earlier on. Ground Zero awaits Universal, as we hear the same music as we did earlier tonight. As explosions and a tornado rips across the Travis-tron, Universal appears on the screen and the dollar sign pyro lowers in place in front of the videoscreen. Green and gold fireworks shoot all over the place once again, as GZ patiently awaits Universal in which presumably will be a handicap match. With their attention diverted up towards the Travis-tron, Universal sneaks in through the crowd behind Ground Zero with a crow bar. Universal climbs into the ring and he nails Wolf Fang with a shot to the head. Jackhammer turns around and Universal smacks him in the face with the crowbar. Universal then tells the referee to ring the bell, before tossing the crowbar out of the ring. He hits the "Cha-Ching!" on Wolf Fang. With Wolf Fang out on his feet ready to be pinned, and Jackhammer sleeping like a baby, Universal has seemingly outsmarted his way out of another tricky situation. On cue, the arena goes dark and "STAR" appears on the Travis-tron. The dollar sign pyro moves over in front of the "s", and fireworks shoot all over the place as "F-ck Off" plays loudly. Lance Star makes his way out to a thunderous ovation of boos by the crowd, and Star climbs into the ring. He pats himself on the back as Universal does the same, before the two "hug" and then Star covers Wolf Fang with his pinky. The referee reluctantly counts with an obvious hint of hesitation, as Star and Universal celebrate in the ring after the pinfall. As the Blondes celebrate and Megan Dawn joins them shortly thereafter, 'Courage' appears on the Travis-tron, with some words for the Blondes. Courage says; "You may have pulled a fast one over Ground Zero, but I'm too smart for that. You gave me your word, I gave you mine... now lets give these fans what they want, and that's me kicking your ass all over the arena!" After saying that, 'Courage' appeared to be headed towards the arena as Warfare quickly went to a commercial break. Result: The Hollywood Blondes by pinfall. "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal vs. 'Courage' Chris West As we return from the commercial break, 'Courage' along with his robust bodyguard Jake made their way out. Courage looked anxious to compete in his first match, and he held his arms up in the air and pointed to himself as the crowd began to boo. Courage got an even bigger reaction when he pointed to the fans, then pointed towards his lips, then towards his buttocks area, telling the fans to "kiss my ass." Courage climbed into the ring and looked at Universal, who was hiding behind Star and mocking Courage. Universal shoved Star into Courage, however, Star baseball slid underneath Courage' legs and out onto the floor. With Courage somewhat distracted, Universal charged and clotheslined Courage. With the match underway, Jake chased Star around ringside in attempt to catch him. Star' quickness and Jake' weight made it easy for Star to keep away from him. Minutes went by before Universal hit the "Cha-Ching!" on Courage, and then attempted a pin. Jake, however, pulled Universal off of him onto the floor. Star ran around and attacked Jake from behind. Universal turned and saw Courage flying at him with a pescado. Universal moved out of the way and Courage smacked the floor. Universal then climbed into the ring and laughed at Courage on the floor, and Star beat down Jake for a moment before sliding into the ring. Jake helped up Courage, as Star & Universal mocked them in the ring. The referee ruled the match a no-contest. With the Blondes continuing to mock Courage & Jake, Ground Zero came in through the crowd from behind and began brawling with Star & Universal. Courage & Jake escaped, but laughed at how easily Ground Zero took care of the Blondes. As Wolf Fang & Jackhammer continued to fight with the Blondes, the locker room emptied with tag teams galore coming down to ringside. Total Chameleon and Eclipse were the first duo out, followed by the Dreamers and The French Foundation. As all five times brawled in the ring, Trouble made their first appearance on the Warfare in quite awhile. They held their newly won tag team titles up in the air, and taunted the teams with them. As they did this, Doomsday came out from behind and attacked them. The commissioner appeared on the scene and had over 40 fWo security guards & officials attempt to break things up in the ring and on the stage, while Warfare went to it's last commercial break before the main event of the evening. Result: No Contest. The Black Panther (c) vs. Black Quicksilver As "We're not gonna take it" blared throughout the arena, Black Quicksilver came out to a nice ovation, while carrying Stump. Quicksilver held Stump in the air and shook it around for a bit, before charging down into the ring. Quicksilver set Stump in the corner and awaited The Black Panther, who came out to a loud ovation from the crowd while carrying the fWo Internet Title. Panther slid into the ring and the match began with a tie-up. Panther got the advantage quickly, and after a series of hiptosses the match went onto the floor. Panther went for a clothesline on the floor, but BQ ducked and dropkicked him when he turned around. BQ used assorted items at ringside, showing a sign of desperation as he clearly wants to become the fWo Internet Champion. The match continued back up into the ring, with the advantage going back to Panther after BQ missed a moonsault in an attempt to finish things off. Panther then put BQ up onto the turnbuckles and went for his patented superplex, and hit it. Panther then rolled BQ up for the win, but BQ got a foot on the ropes. Panther pointed at Stump and blamed him for putting BQ' foot on the ropes, and the referee, blindly, walked over and asked Stump if he did as Panther claimed. Panther then lifted up BQ, whipped him to the ropes and then hit a lowblow when he came back, then finished him off with a Samoan Drop. A cover, and BQ was unable to kickout. The fWo World Champion Scott Slugger & Ice charged the ring and attacked both men, before throwing BQ and Stump out onto the floor. They double-teamed Panther until US Marshal hit the ring. Marshal helped Panther clear the ring and then Panther thanked US Marshal, and Marshal said he was just protecting his investment, because he didn't want Panther to get hurt before Meltdown. Things remained at a standstill as Panther & US Marshal held off Slugger & Ice at bay, as Warfare came to a close... meanwhile, the three familiar men watched on from the rafters, waiting for the perfect opportunity to reveal themselves. Result: Black Panther by pinfall.
