Sunday Slaughter, 19 December 1999

Introduction TONIGHT'S SUNDAY SLAUGHTER DEDICATED TO THE MEMORY OF MR. GEOFFRY BOOTHROYD, BETTER KNOWN AS "Q" IN THE POPULAR JAMES BOND SERIES. REST IN PEACE, MR. BOOTHROYD. "Technically this is the last Slaughter of the year, isn't it?" Wretch tied up his wrestling boots as he looked over at Bear, who shrugged. "Well yeah... I suppose so... wait... isn't there a show after Christmas?" Wretch shook his head. "Nah, that's a recap show... best of and all that crap." Mike Bear snorted to himself, and looked across the wide arena entrance room at some of the new arrivals. At the door, Joy and Rudo were being scolded by Machina. Machina had a large bag of mail slung over his shoulder, and was wearing a large black pair of glasses. Bear shook his head at the sight and turned to Wretch. "You've been here since the beginning... has it always been like this? Wretch shrugged. "More or less. Of course... the matches didn't seem as long or descriptive... and there was also this weird orange and black color scheme. And that guy wasn't here then..." Wretch pointed to Silver Fox, who had just walked into the arena. He held a bag slung over his shoulder, and looked very alone. Eron walked by with the International Title, and laughed at Silver Fox as he went. "Hey, where did your little entourage of chicks go old man? You look pretty pathetic to me... ha!" Silver Fox's shoulder's slumped, and he sighed hard to himself. Just then a hand reached from behind him and massaged his shoulder. Silver Fox jumped, and turned to see Nikki standing behind him. Nikki licked her lips, and continued to massage Silver Fox's back. "I find failure very erotic... or success... doesn't matter." Silver Fox smiled, and on the other side of the room Mike Bear and Wretch exchanged another glance. Wretch shrugged. "Yeah, always been like this... more or less." Inside the arena, fireworks screamed from the rafters, and Slaughter took to the air. Chapter One: Announcement The crowd rose to their feet in anticipation of the night's action, and groaned in disgust when James Armstrong appeared on the top of the entrance ramp. Armstrong waved at the fans, noticing none of the boos and jeers from the crowd, or simply ignoring them. James got to the ring, and after some minimal effort climbed between the ropes. He addressed the crowd with a beaming smile, again ignoring the compliment of reporters at ringside who were snapping photos of James Armstrong for their headlines. James Armstrong was oblivious as to why they were there, and would likely have been more uncomfortable if he knew that they were present because they were covering the story of how the United States Government had filed antitrust lawsuits against the fWo, and were investigating the promotion for fraud on at least eight levels. Armstrong would have also likely been confused as to how the lawyers for the various sexual discrimination lawsuits obtained such key information. But Armstrong was oblivious to this, and only beamed at the reporters for 20/20 and CNN. Taking a microphone, James Armstrong waved to the crowd. "In less than one week the fWo will present Twilight '99... a Pay Per View extravaganza that will be unrivaled in the wrestling world! We end the Millennium with a show filled with great matches... and I'm pleased to announce that the main event for this card has become even more star studded! In a late decision made by myself and myself alone in my infinite wisdom, the current fWo World Heavyweight Champion Ice will meet his partner and former champion Scott Slugger in a big World title match. The winner of this match will meet the winner of last month's Battle Bowl... the Flying Frenchie. It's going to be a huge night... with three champions giving it all they've got inside the ring!" The crowd cheered, with the sole exception of one fan at ringside who wore a Flying Frenchie t-shirt, who booed this development. James Armstrong continued to beam, and shouted, "To celebrate this announcement, we're serving beer and other alcoholic beverages to anyone in the audience... regardless of age! Federal laws be dammed!" The crowd cheered even louder, and the reporters at ringside snapped even more pictures. James Armstrong waved at them, as the first match got set to begin... Ultra Violet vs. US Marshal Classical music filled the arena as an 18th century dutchess walked gingerly to the ring. The illusion of an English noble was nearly perfect, spoiled only by the US Marshal's stubbled face. The dutchess got to the ring and waited while the ropes were held apart for him, clicking his tongue in annoyance as the ring attendants took their time doing so. He stepped into the ring and addressed the crowd. "You will all show the dutchess some respect! I am... I am royalty... and all of you peasants are... are... foul creatures! Why, I can smell you from here you disgusting..." "Rage" blasted over the arena speakers as Ultra Violet appeared on the top of the entrance ramp and ran to the ring with a purple paint can in hand. His half of the tag team title was mysteriously not on his waist, and appeared to be nowhere in sight. Kid Cool and Violet walked behind him, flirting with each other and taking turned rubbing each other's backs. Ultra Violet didn't notice, but as he entered the ring he tripped over the ring ropes and the paint can went flying from his hands, landing perhaps predictably on the US Marshal. Frowning, the dutchess wiped the purple paint from his face and looked down at Ultra Violet with great anger. Ultra Violet stood and looked at the dutchess curiously, scratching his head as he tried to figure out how the dutchess got so purple. The Marshal picked up Ultra Violet and dropped him on his head in a homage to great Japanese worker Kawada, but it seemed to have little effect on Ultra Violet. The tag team champion bounced to his feet and landed a drop kick which sent the Marshal backward into the ropes. Ultra Violet grabbed the waist of the Marshal and attempted a suplex, but the Marshal battered him down with a series of hard punches to the head. Again, the head appeared to be Ultra Violet's strong part, and Ultra Violet shrugged off the blows and grinned stupidly. Outside the ring Violet was dancing for Kid Cool, who smiled and leered appreciatively. He wore the mangled half of one of the tag titles around his waist, and paid little attention to the match inside the ring. Ultra Violet had hit a dangerous drop toe hold, and went for a cover, but the Marshal reversed easily at two. The Marshal rolled Ultra Violet over and got the three count, and walked from the ring the victor. Kid Cool and Violet seemed not to notice their partner's loss, and continued to flirt with each other. Result: US Marshal by Pinfall Chapter Two: A new look Backstage loud noises and sounds of an argument were drifting out of the locker room of the Lone Gunmen. Machina was yelling, and around him stood Rudo, Billy James, Sid Snow and Brandi. "Listen... this just isn't going to work! These glasses are clunky, fragile, and they disrupt matches too easily. I need a way to keep myself disguised from the media and Penny that isn't going to give me a headache!" Rudo frowned. "Well, you shouldn't be getting a headache unless you stupidly have been using real glasses with actual corrective lenses. But even you are smarter than that." Machina looked at his glasses, and tossed them into the garbage behind him. "Er... yeah... of course I am. God, Rudo... what do you think I am... an idiot?" There was a moment of silence in the locker room as the moment played out. Machina cleared his throat, "Anyway... what I need is a solution. A way to disguise myself... now who has ideas?" The room was silent as everyone thought. Suddenly, Brandi perked up. "Of course! I've got the perfect idea... why don't you wear a mask?" Machina looked puzzled. "A mask?" Brandi nodded excitedly. "Yes, a mask! It's perfect... it will cover your face, and nobody will know that it's really you! Here... I've got some mask making materials in my bag... yeah... here's something, a blue towel! I'll just cut two eyeholes into it like so... and you just drape it over your head like this!" Brandi placed the towel over Machina's face, and the blue cloth hung loosely over his head. Machina looked out through the eyeholes at the other Lone Gunmen. "You think this will work?" Billy James laughed and nudged Sid. They whispered together, and then laughed again. Machina placed his hands on his hips and yelled, "What is it?" Billy James cleared his throat and pointed to Machina. "Yeah, that cowl works... but can you just do one thing for us?" Machina glared. "What?" Sid giggled, pointed and said, "Yell 'COBRA' for us, will you?" The locker room burst into laughter and Machina tossed the blue towel down to the ground in disgust. "Dammit, think! Think harder!" His words were largely ignored as the laughter continued... Hellrazor (c) vs. Death "Burning" by Manowar hits the arena and echoes loudly as a confident Death comes down the ramp, a wicked smile across his face, seemingly soaking in the wave of boos that he receives. "Hit 'em Up" by 2Pac breaks out as the Hardcore champion himself comes out. He storms the ring with a shopping cart of weapons in tow. As he reaches ringside, a nimble Death catches him with a top rope plancha that has the match off to a quick and abrupt start, and both men sprawled at ringside without Hellrazor ever having reached the ring. Death is up first and he extracts a metal cooking sheet from the cart that Hellrazor himself brought. As Hellrazor stands, he is met by a cold and hard shot from the sheet that sends him stumbling backwards, and crumples the pan. Death tosses it aside and lunges with quick punches. Hellrazor shoves Death aside and takes a moment to regain his composure. Death grabs a crutch and shatters it over the cranium of Hellrazor. Death comes back at him, but he holds off this attack with a sudden dropkick to the knee of Death. Continuing to work on the knee he rolls Death on to his back and picks up a broom. He raises it above his head, wields it wickedly, before bringing down and splintering it into two over Death's vulnerable back. He follows with a series of stomps and shoves Death into the guardrail. Completely emptying the contents from the shopping cart, Hellrazor grabs it with two hands and drops it onto Death's semi-bruised back. He lifts it once more and follows with two more shots from the cart. Pitching it aside, he lifts Death but is doubled over from a vicious elbow to the gut. Death is up in an instant and after a series of arm holds, cranks on a headlock. Hellrazor shoves him off into a guardrail, and when Death comes back off is met with a kick to the midsection. Death down, grabs Hellrazor about the ankles, and pulls them out from under him sending him down hard onto the concrete. He catapults Hellrazor up and then down onto the steel guardrail. Death comes from behind and executes a Russian leg sweep that take the breath out of Hellrazor. Death crawls over to the spilled contents of the cart and grabs a vacuum cleaner. Rolling over Hellrazor, he places the vacuum on his extremities, and then picks up a metal baseball bat. Brandishing the bat high above he is moments away from bringing it down on the vacuum cleaner and in turn crushing Hellrazor's midsection, when Hellrazor tosses of the vacuum and rolls out of the way as the bat comes down just a foot from where the champion just lay. A ringing sensation shoots through Death's hand and sends him backwards in pain. Hellrazor leaps to his feet, lifts the vacuum and brings it crashing down on Death's battered knee. Death looks to escape crawling towards the entrance ramp, yet Hellrazor catches up to him with a chair, and brings it down firm over Death's decrepit back. He lifts Death, and drags him to the top of the entrance ramp where he plants him with a solid DDT onto the metal. Lifting Death up to his feet once more, he gouges an eye and rams Death's head into a metal post by the entrance. Tossing him backstage, Hellrazor turns and surveys the crowd before venturing backstage. The camera follows the action, yet in the time that Hellrazor had turned, Death had gotten hold of a hammer and as Hellrazor came through the curtain, Death surprised him and smacked the hammer over Hellrazor's head. Death hurls the hammer aside and picking up a fallen Hellrazor, whips him into the wall. After a series of stiff punches, Hellrazor returns the favor and the two begin trading punchers until Hellrazor takes the offensive with a quick headbutt and then pulls off a knee-breaker, dropping Death to the floor. Hellrazor tosses Death into the wall, and then chucks him into the side of a soda vending machine. Hellrazor catches Death with an uppercut and a clothesline, before lifting him and tossing him into a dumpster. From a table nearby, he pulls of a tray of cosmetics yet before he can bring it down is kicked in the groin buy Death, who ripping away the tray, cracks it over Hellrazor's head that send his backwards and onto a table. Death charges yet Hellrazor thinking quickly drop toe holds him onto the table which doesn't give way. Death was opened up slightly and Hellrazor not looking to let up, lifts Death and slinging one of Death's arms over his head turns and using his last energy, slowly lifts Death into a vertical suplex and then brings him crashing down through the table, demolishing it and leaving Death in a mess of shattered wood. Grabbing Death's ankle, Hellrazor lugs Death out of the disarray and into the middle of the backstage area. He legdrops Death and then jumps on top of him with a barrage of stiff punches. Death recovers and catches Hellrazor with a punch to the jaw and tosses Hellrazor to the side. Limping forward he finds a chair, folds it and turns to face Hellrazor, when in one sudden shocking moment, David Spencer and his Dark Riders emerge. Death spins around yet is clocked by Poison who sends the chair back into his face. Hellrazor applies a sleeper hold and allows the Dark Riders to take free shots. Spencer steps forward and molds the chair Death had held over his head with a defiant thud. The five then proceed to stomping away and mauling a now bloodied Death in nothing more than a glorified mugging, and allowing Hellrazor to drop and attempt a pin. The referee, with no choice but to count it out, does so, and at the count of three, the Dark Riders are exiting the area and Hellrazor remains the Hardcore champion. After break, Hellrazor is shown backstage, grinning with his title slung over a shoulder, when he is suddenly jumped by his opponent to be at Twilight, former Hardcore champ herself, VL the Murdera. She drops him with closed fists and then after a brutal kick to the ribs, rips the hardcore title from Hellrazor, and follows with cold shots to Hellrazor's head from the belt. After spitting on Hellrazor, she drops the belt onto him and struts away, leaving Hellrazor to pick up the pieces. "What in the hell are you doing man? What in the hell are you playing at? You're my partner man... what in the hell are you doing?" Scott Slugger put his arms up, shrugging. "Hey Ice man... calm down. It's nothing personal... you're my friend and you know it... so just ease off... back up. It's nothing personal... just business. Just chill out." Ice calmed, but still wore an angry expression on his face. Slugger noticed it, and pointed to the fWo champion. "Just know this... 'pal'. If I don't walk out of Twilight the champion... and I will mind you... if I don't walk out the champion something really drastic is going to happen... so just consider that big man." Ice's expression of anger grew stronger, and he grabbed at Slugger. The two men began shoving and were close to throwing punches as Chris Universal ran into the area and got between them. Universal held back Ice, and both men seemed to calm down. Scott Slugger finished his autograph and tossed the card and pen at Cairn, and then walked away in the opposite direction from Universal and Ice. Ice snorted and brushed off Universal, stomping down the hallway of the arena. Universal looked at Cairn with an expression of disgust, and walked away himself. Cairn seemed nervous, but pleased that he got the autograph and looked at it. His smile faded as he read what Slugger had written.
