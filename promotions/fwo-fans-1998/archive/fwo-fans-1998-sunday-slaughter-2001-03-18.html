Sunday Slaughter, 18 March 2001

18 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The backstage of the arena was relatively quiet. The vast majority of wrestlers and other members of the talent had already arrived and by and large were about their work. Because of this simple fact, security was light at the gated entrance. There were a few fans hanging around outside and hoping to catch a glimpse of their favorite fWo superstar, but the vast majority of them were inside watching the show. The sounds from the arena could not be heard outside, and the guard was happy with this. Wrestling by and large annoyed him. It seemed as though the wrestlers came to town every three to four months, and they usually brought the sort of drunken chaos afterward that only the most extreme heavy metal bands produced. He yawned. For the next hour or so, things would be bliss. Just the night, the noise of the nearby freeway and... A car was coming closer. He saw the headlights draw nearer in the night and narrowed his eyes. A late arrival? Perhaps... but it seemed odd all the same. From his understanding they were broadcasting that wrestling stuff on television... a late arrival would mess up the plans. But the rental car was drawing nearer to his small entrance gate and there was no doubt the driver wanted in. Sighing, he let his hand drift lazily out of the box window. "ID and name." He was reaching for the clipboard which carried the approved visitor list when he was suddenly jerked forward into the glass. Gurgling, he looked with wide eyes out of the window at the driver of the car. An angry looking man with a goatee and sharply shaven blonde hair leered at him. He wore a dirty shirt with a strange blue logo on it. Three letters made up the logo... CWL. "Well god damn, if it ain't the little watch dog son of a bitch of the fWo. Saddle up cowboy, because all hell is gonna break loose... so says me. So says Neo." Rottdogg (c) vs. Kodiak Vic Creed "Walk" begins to play, and the fans boo loudly as Kodiak Vic Creed makes his way to the ring, wielding his trademark chain. He swings it around in the air as he makes his way to the ring, then drapes it over the ringpost before stepping into the ring. Creed stares down the entry ramp, and "Pressure Zone" begins to play. The fans give a decidedly mixed reaction to the fWo Hardcore Champion Rottdogg, whose hair is once again done up in trademark goofy spikes. Rottdogg hands the title off to a ring attendant and rolls into the ring. He charges at Creed with a clothesline as the bell rings, but simply bounces off. Creed charges at him and takes him down with a lariat of his own. Rottdogg gets up, and Creed grabs him and whips him across the ring. Rottdogg slams into a corner with a thud, and Creed charges in after him. Rottdogg lifts a boot and it strikes Creed square, and Creed stumbles off. Rottdogg charges out of the corner, but Creed catches him and takes him over with a spinning spinebuster. Creed makes a cover, but Rottdogg kicks out at two. Creed brings Rottdogg back up again and whips him into the ropes, then takes him down with a powerslam. Creed hooks a leg, and Rottdogg kicks out at two again. Creed brings Rottdogg back up to his feet and hooks him up. He then takes him over with a t-bone suplex, slamming him to the mat. Creed signals that he's going up top, and does so. He reaches the top rope and measures Rottdogg, then leaps off. Frog splash. Creedzillinator. Creed stays on for a cover, but somehow, some way, Rottdogg kicks out at two. Creed shakes his head and brings Rottdogg up again, but Rottdogg lashes out with a low blow that catches Creed. He follows it up with another, making sure the job is done, and then sets up Creed. He steps to the side of Creed, hooks him up, and drops him with a side suplex. Rottdogg covers, but Creed kicks out at one. Rottdogg brings him back up to his feet and whips him into the ropes. As he comes off, Creed goes for a clothesline, but Rottdogg ducks, reaches back, and takes Creed to the mat with a reverse neckbreaker. Rottdogg immediately brings Creed right back up to his feet, goes behind him, and picks him up. In an amazing show of strength, Rottdogg takes Creed over with an incredible inverted powerslam. He drops down to make the cover. Creed kicks out at two. Rottdogg brings him right back up and hooks him. He then lifts Creed up and holds him in the air. After doing this for a good 15 seconds, Rottdogg drops Creed with a delayed vertical suplex. Rottdogg covers again, but Creed kicks out once more at two. Rottdogg, undaunted, brings Creed back up again and sets him up for the Bad Dog. Rottdogg lifts Creed up in screwdriver position, but Creed shifts his weight, drops back to his feet, and lifts Rottdogg up. He then takes him down with the Kodiak Krippler. Creed smiles sadistically and brings Rottdogg up again. He lifts Rottdogg above his head and repeatedly gorilla presses him. He then drops Rottdogg with a picture-perfect Powerdriver. Still smiling, Creed brings Rottdogg up and goes behind him. He shrugs Rottdogg onto his shoulders and sets him up, then drops him with an Alaskan Avalanche. Creed, all business and quite clearly sending a message, brings Rottdogg up again and positions him between his legs. He then lifts Rottdogg up and drops him on his head. Hangman's DDT. Creed neglects to make the cover, apparently still not satisfied. He runs a finger across his throat and lifts Rottdogg up. He positions Rottdogg on his shoulders with a torture rack. He then extends a knee and hurls Rottdogg off his shoulders onto the knee with a mega-backbreaker... also known as the Bodybreaker. Creed makes the cover. One. Two. Three. Creed picks up the non-title victory, and stands up over the unconscious body of Rottdogg and spits on it as the referee raises his hand. Creed retrieves his chain, and apparently just in time as first Vengence, then Carnage hit the ring. They both go right after Creed, who fights back, doing a good job of inflicting punishment on two attackers. As this goes on, Rottdogg begins to revive and rolls to the outside of the ring. He retrieves his Hardcore Title belt and slides into the ring. He runs off the ropes, then leaps and swings the belt at Creed from behind. Creed sense him coming and rolls away, and Rottdogg plasters Vengence instead, knocking him backwards but not off his feet. Vengence immediately begins to brawl with Rottdogg, and Carnage elects to join and make it a three-way brawl rather than go after Creed, who wraps his chain around his first and joins the fray. The four hardcore warriors are still brawling when Slaughter goes backstage... Result: Kodiak Vic Creed by pinfall Knock, Knock Nervously, Clarisse stood in front of a locker room, which had the nameplate 'Chris Titan' on the door. She knocked twice and waited for an answer. Soon enough, the door opened and there appeared one of Chris Titan's lawyers. "I'm sorry, Miss LaFontaine, but currently we don't find it wise for Mister Titan to be in contact with you." He began to shut the door, but Clarisse held her hand up and stopped it with force. "No," she scowled, "him and I *do* need to talk." Titan, who finished taping his wrists, opened the door fully. "It's OK, Mr. Byron, I've got it taken care of. Come in, Clarisse." Clarisse entered the room. "Wine?" offered Titan. Clarisse shook her head and stood in front of Titan. "This is ridiculous," she said. "Chris, you have to drop these charges. I don't want this legal issue to come up at a pay-per-view event." Titan grinned. "You're afraid that Cody won't win, aren't you?" Clarisse balled her fists and walked over to Titan. "Take the settlement, Chris. Put an end to this. Don't be a greedy child." Titan folded his arms across his chest, as if not very amused. Getting angrier, Clarisse got in closer. "If you don't take the settlement... I'm going to have to end up doing something that I'll regret." Still not impressed, Titan smirked. "Is that a threat, Miss renowned she-rapist?" Titan laughed at the new nickname he gave Clarisse, and continued. "I don't care what you have in store for me at Dead or Alive. You screwed me over at In Misery, and one thing about me, is that I *learn* from my mistakes. Cody's won't be man enough to defend your honor next week at Dead or Alive. I've broken him down to nothing more than a spineless wimp. Next Saturday, I'm going to finish the job. Now..." Titan nodded at his lawyers, before looking back at Clarisse. "Get the hell out of here before I do something that *I'll* regret." Before Clarisse could even move, both of Titan's lawyers grabbed an arm and began dragging her to the door. Clarisse showed no struggle, but still kept her eyes fixated on Chris Titan... until the lawyers threw her out of the room and shut the door. Black Panther/Crusader/Black Quicksilver vs. Juno Law/Chris Titan/Sphere Puff Daddy is heard on the speakers, as Juno Law makes his way to the ring, accompanied by Kobi Law. Sphere follows through upon hearing the Deftones blare over, and finally his music is cut out and replaced by Disturbed. Chris Titan is showered with boos, and as he enters the ring with his partners, the crowd begins to boo in unison against the three men in the ring. Rammstein hits the speakers, and the Black Panther comes out from the back holding his tag team title, accompanied by a reluctant Havok, who also holds his tag title. Skid Row takes to the speakers, and Black Quicksilver soon follows out carrying Stump. Finally, Biohazard brings Crusader from the back, unaccompanied by Clarisse. The fans cheer as the three men enter the ring, each one staring down his hated rival. The bell sounds, and the referee orders two men on each side to a corner and orders Kobi and Havok to the outside. This leaves Black Quicksilver and Juno Law in the ring. They start the match off with a basic grapple. Juno takes the headlock advantage over BQ, but BQ manages to shoot Juno off to the ropes. BQ telegraphs a back body drop, and Juno immediately responds with a face jam. Juno makes a cover, but the crafty masked wrestler kicks out. Juno reaches out for a tag, and Sphere hits his hand. Sphere enters the ring and immediately begins stomping away at Quicksilver. BQ manages to get up near the ropes and elbow his rival in the gut. Sphere doubles over, and BQ takes him in a front chancery, hops on the middle rope and hits a tornado DDT. BQ makes a cover, but Chris Titan enters the ring and breaks up the pinning predicament. Crusader stands in the corner, merely eying Titan as he exits back to his side. BQ comes off the ropes and tries for a hurricanrana on Sphere, but Sphere catches him in mid-move and drops him to the mat with a powerbomb. Sphere holds on for the cover, and Crusader nudges Panther to make the save. Panther leaps in and breaks up the count with a hard boot. Sphere scowls at Black Panther, but then tags out to Chris Titan. Titan enters the ring and drops an elbow on the fallen BQ, before bringing him back up and shooting him off to the ropes, but BQ reverses. Upon his return, BQ leapfrogs over Titan and then tumbles forward to tag out to Crusader. Crusader enters the ring and continues to eye Chris Titan, who stands there ready. Titan comes off the ropes and goes for first strike, but Crusader ducks his clothesline and locks him into a full Nelson. Titan struggles, but ends up being taken over with a dragon suplex. As Titan gets to his feet, Crusader charges forward and smacks him in the face with a spinning heel kick. He makes the cover, but Titan powers out at two. Crusader waits on Titan to get to his feet, and then takes to the ropes. Juno however lifts his foot up high, connecting with a roundhouse kick to the back of Crusader's head. Crusader stumbles forward, and Chris Titan greets him with a kick to the mid-section and a double-arm DDT. Titan makes the cover, but out of nowhere, BQ flies in with an elbow drop in his back before three can be tolled. As the referee goes to send BQ back to his corner, Sphere, Titan and both Laws lace in a quadruple team on Crusader. As the ref turns around, Kobi scoots to the outside, while Juno and Sphere head to the apron. Titan begins hammering away viciously at Crusader with right hands, yelling out obscenities about Clarisse. Finally, he tags out to Juno, who enters the ring and picks up where Titan left off with hard right hands. Sphere brings Crusader back up and shoots him off to the ropes, but Crusader holds on as Juno misses the dropkick. As Juno gets up, Crusader flattens up with a clothesline, and then tags out to Black Panther. Juno hesitates at the sight of the big man stepping over the top, but then charges full speed ahead. Panther effortlessly picks Juno up and throws him into the corner, before charging in with a clothesline. Juno front rolls his way to dodge it, and smacks Panther in the hamstring with a snap roundhouse kick. Panther's leg gives out, and Juno capitalizes with a reverse DDT. Juno pulls Panther to the middle of the ring, and then leaps up onto the middle rope. He flies off with an Asai moonsault, but fails to see an already-recovered Black Panther. Panther catches Juno in mid-air, and drops him with a hard shoulder breaker. BP makes the cover, but Titan enters to break it up. Panther turns around and glares at Chris Titan, but this gives Juno the opportunity to scramble between his legs and tag out to Sphere. Sphere springs atop and tries taking Panther down with a clothesline, but Sphere merely ricochets off of him. Panther brings Sphere up and whips him into the ropes, but Sphere reverses. Havok, thinking it's Sphere, trips the man coming, which ends up being his tag team partner, Black Panther. Panther turns around to yell at Havok, but from behind Sphere comes out of nowhere with a schoolboy rollup. Before Havok can do anything, Kobi Law comes around the corner and tackles him to the floor while lacing in hard right hands. Panther kicks out a moment too late as the ref's hand strikes three. The mayhem doesn't stop, however, as all six men pour into the ring and begin brawling. Crusader manages to leap up to the top rope amidst all of the carnage and fly off with a missile dropkick that catches Chris Titan in the mouth. Kobi enters the ring after taking care of Havok on the outside to double-team on Black Panther, while Sphere and BQ tear at each other in the corner exchanging chops. The referee stands in the ring grabbing his hair frantically, as the production team decides that now would be a good time for a commercial break... Result: Juno Law/Chris Titan/Sphere by pinfall Half-Dozen Erik Kelly and Matt Loco turned the corner toward their locker room. Loco was attired in his traditional business suit while Kelly sported his tights, an "Eradication Is Upon You" T-shirt, and a towel around his neck. "Well, so far so good," said Kelly. "I got in my workout, and nobody delivered any of those damn roses. I just have a little business to take care of before my match, and I'll be set." Loco, walking behind Kelly, nodded. "Yeah. I don't know what the deal was with those roses, but it looks like whoever sent them gave up." Kelly reached for the doorknob and turned it. "Or... not," he said, looking into the room. Loco maneuvered around the bulk of Kelly and looked into the locker room. Sitting on a table in the middle of the room, in a vase, were six black roses. Kelly snarled, then stepped forward and swiped at the vase. The vase flew across the room and shattered against a wall, spraying glass fragments everywhere. Water flowed out of the vase's remains, and the roses stared out of the wreckage at Erik Kelly. Havok vs. Erik Kelly "Awake" by Godsmack hits, bringing out one half of the tag team champion Millennium Men, Havok. Havok heads down to the ring and stretches on the ropes before bouncing off them a few times to loosen up for the match. He acknowledges the importance of this match with the intensity in his eyes as he glares down the ramp, anticipating the arrival of his opponent. The fear-inducing cry of "POWERBOMB!" followed by "Break Stuff" heralds the monster, Erik Kelly, accompanied by Matt Loco. Kelly heads straight to the ring like a man possessed, stepping over the top rope and staring down his opponent across the ring. He mouths the lyrics to his entrance theme, "So come and get it," and the bell rings. Havok accepts Kelly's challenge and immediately lights into the big man with right hands. Kelly shrugs off the blows and signals for Havok to keep them coming. Havok realizes that beating on Kelly is useless and lashes out with a kick to the gut, which bends Kelly over. He hooks him and nails a DDT. Havok turns around to survey the damage and Kelly is already to his feet, unfazed. He simply roars and clocks Havok with a big right hand, dropping him to the mat. Havok gets up and is whipped hard into the corner by the monster. Kelly follows in with a vicious clothesline in the corner before following that up with another hard whip into the opposite corner. Havok's impact with the turnbuckle bounces him off and he stumbles out only to be met with a ring-rocking spear by Kelly. Kelly decides to waive the opportunity to cover in favor of continuing his assault as he throttles Havok with a blatant chokehold. He strangles the life out of Havok for five seconds and then pulls him up, sending the smaller man into the ropes with an Irish whip. Kelly catches Havok coming off with a bearhug front slam and makes the cover. Havok manages a kickout at two and Kelly roars with displeasure. He brings Havok up by the hair and whips him into the ropes once more. Kelly bounces off the opposite rope and flies at Havok with a clothesline but Havok manages to grab the ropes, stopping his momentum, and send Kelly out of the ring courtesy of a back body drop. Kelly makes it to his feet on the outside and Havok decides to please the fans, measuring Kelly and flying over the top rope with a slingshot cross body. He hits his target with precision and immediately gets back to his feet. Kelly groggily finds his footing and Havok grabs him by the hair, attempting to smash his head into the steel guardrail. Kelly laughs at the brawling attempt and pulls his head away before grabbing Havok by the head and slamming him face first into the guardrail. Havok reels back and Kelly lifts him off his feet, crotching him on the steel guardrail. Havok grimaces in pain as Kelly walks over and folds a steel chair, tossing it into the ring. Havok falls off the guardrail to the floor beneath with a thud as Kelly heads back over to lift him and roll him back into the ring. "POOOOWERBOMB!" signals an oncoming Eradication as Kelly places Havok in position for his devastating finisher right over the steel chair. Havok sees it coming and manages to wrap his arms around one of Kelly's tree trunk like legs. Kelly is thrown off balance as Havok yanks on his leg and the big man manages to take down the referee with one of his flailing arms. He regains his balance and lays a hard clubbing forearm across Havok's back, causing him to let go, and wraps a massive hand around his throat. Havok counters the chokeslam attempt with a kick which doubles Kelly over and a floatover DDT onto the folded chair. He covers. The referee is still out cold. The crowd counts to three but no official count is made. Havok pulls the referee over and attempts to bring him back into consciousness. He covers once more and the ref counts slowly. Kelly kicks out at two. Havok is visibly frustrated. He composes himself and decides to take the offense, mounting the top turnbuckle. He flies off but Kelly makes it to his feet and catches him coming off. The near seven footer holds his foe out in front of him before taking him up and over with a fierce fallaway slam. Kelly covers but the move only garners a two count. Kelly kicks the dented chair from the ring and brings Havok up. He places Havok up onto his shoulder facing forward and attempts to run into the turnbuckle, sandwiching him, but the agile Havok manages to slide down Kelly's back right before impact. Kelly collides with the turnbuckle and Havok hooks him from behind, looking for the Headache, his inverted brainbuster. He is unable to lift Kelly very far off his feet so he settles for turning around and dropping the monster with a reverse neckbreaker. Havok mounts the top turnbuckle, looking to advance to the tournament finals with one last showstopping move. Kelly, however, refuses to stay down. He makes it to his feet and shakes the top rope, crotching Havok before he can even turn around and get into position for the Millennium-Sault. Havok is crotched for the second time in the match, this time on a turnbuckle, as Kelly once again wraps his mammoth hand around Havok's throat and sends him off the top turnbuckle with a spinning chokeslam. Kelly lets out the "POOOOWERBOMB!" roar once again as Havok lies motionless on the mat. He brings Havok up effortlessly. Eradication - icing on the cake. The monster known as Kelly gets the three count and advances to the Internet Title Tournament finals. He makes his exit with Matt Loco to the sounds of Limp Bizkit, stomping down the aisle to a barrage of boos. Result: Erik Kelly by pinfall High Noon All it needed was a little spaghetti western music, Robert Jensen thought with bitter amusement. Ruin and Damnation had bumped into each other backstage that's all, just simply crossed each other's paths and it was on. Jensen and Frank Snow were staring down Fear and Hellrazor, who stood about 5 paces across from them. Snow spat out a wad of chaw, and it landed on the wall and oozed down. Hellrazor's lips curled up, exposing his teeth in a snarl. Lamont hissed. Fear's hand was poised over the pocket of his jeans, and it suddenly darted forward and pulled out the pocket's contents... a longneck Heineken bottle. Fear looked at it, and shrugged. "No point in wasting this on you two," he said. Fear popped the top and drained it in a single gulp. He then gripped the bottle by the neck and charged at Ruin, swinging the bottle. Frank Snow ducked, and the bottle shattered against the wall. Hellrazor leapt forward and began to engage Robert Jensen in battle, and Fear and Frank did the same. The two brawled down the hall, followed by Lamont, and passed the East Coast Laws, who were leaning against a wall. "Go Frank!" shouted Juno, still a bit sweaty after his match. "Go Hellrazor!" shouted Kobi. The two laughed, and watched the two teams brawl down the hall with interest. Damnation and Ruin showed no signs of stopping as they disappeared around a corner, and the camera panned back to the Laws... Who? "Man," said Juno, "that brawl was weak." Kobi nodded. "Still, you gotta think about who was involved." "True," Juno said, "true." The two exchanged a high-five at their successful diss. As they did so, their attention was suddenly diverted by a shapely Japanese woman, vaguely familiar-looking, slinking down the hall and appearing to go in the direction of Chris Universal's office. She disappeared into the office, and the Laws stared after her. "Dude," said Kobi. "Dude," Juno said. "Whoa," Kobi said. "Whoa," said Juno. They looked at each other. "Wow," they both said. The woman walked out of Universal's office. "Hey! Foxy lady!" yelled Juno. She must not have heard him, as she continued walking out of sight. Juno shrugged, then both the Laws walked toward Universal's office. When they reached it, they peered in. Universal, nattily attired, looked up at them from his desk. "What can I do for you?" he asked. "Who was that?" asked Kobi. Universal smiled. "Oh, just an old face showing up again. Wait and see, boys... I'm sure you'll find out eventually." Greg Davis (c) vs. Eddy Fate "You can't deny Fate" is heard, and "Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson hits the speakers, bringing out the challenger for the fWo Cruiserweight Championship, Eddy Fate. Fate looks, and then turns around, to find the Ego charging him from the crowd. The Ego slides into the ring, and runs towards Fate with his arm extended for a clothesline. Fate ducks it, and catches the Ego's neck as he runs past, planting him with a neckbreaker. The Ego gets up, but Fate doesn't let go, and plants him with another one. The Ego, instead of taking another one, slides out of the ring, to gain a bit of time to collect his thoughts. However, this is interrupted rather quickly by Fate, who takes off at Davis with a spear through the second rope. He nails it, sending Davis sprawling to the floor. Fate gets up a bit slowly, before picking up Davis and tossing him back into the ring. Fate follows Davis into the ring, and he goes for a quick figure-four leglock. Davis, being as alert as he can, quickly kicks out of it. However, Fate uses this kickout as momentum into the ropes. Davis gets up, and immediately hits the deck after feeling Fate's body with a crossbody splash. Fate goes for a cover; however, only two is registered before Davis kicks out. Fate picks up Davis, and tosses him into the ropes. Davis hits the ropes, and bounces off. He ducks a clothesline attempt from Fate. Davis hits the ropes again, and goes airborne with his second attempt at offense in this match, as he tries to answer the crossbody that Fate nailed. Wilson brings Strangler up once more and goes behind him, then locks him in an octopus hold, trying for a submission. Mike Bear and Wretch, though at first reluctant to fight, have quickly pushed aside their reluctance and are now going at it tooth and nail. Bear lashes out with a right hand, and Wretch fires back with a left. Bear fires back with another of his own, one that hits so hard that it literally spins Wretch. Bear then lashes out with a kick to the midsection, doubling Wretch over. He sets Wretch up, then spikes him with a piledriver. Bear makes the cover, but Wretch kicks out at two. Bear brings Wretch up again and lifts him onto his shoulders, setting up for the Whiplash. Wretch however slides off and lands behind Bear. Bear turns and goes for a right, but Wretch blocks it and shakes a finger at Bear. He then hits a series of three rights to the head and whips Bear into the ropes. He then catches Bear with a big boot to the head, knocking him down. Wretch then goes to Bear's head and begins to signal for the Ratings Saver Legdrop. He runs into the ropes, but runs straight into Mitch Wilson, who has released his hold and positioned himself in Wretch's path. Headliner. Mitch makes the cover. One. Two. The Boston Strangler interrupts it. He drags Mitch to his feet, and Mitch swings at him. Strangler catches his arm, spins, and twists into the Boston Massacre. Strangler squeezes it in, but Mitch refuses to give up. Still, he's losing consciousness when Mike Bear spins the Strangler around from behind and lifts him onto his shoulders. Whiplash. Cover. One. Two. Three. Mike Bear retains his title by pinning the Boston Strangler, and the referee retrieves the belt and hands it to Bear, who raises it high above his head. Bear then poses on the turnbuckle, belt in the air, and hops down to the mat again. He sees Mitch Wilson and Wretch, both of whom have more or less regained their faculties and are back on their feet. The three stare at each other. Nobody blinks. Slaughter goes off the air for the final time before Dead Or Alive, with Mike Bear, Wretch, and Mitch Wilson in an eye-to-eye staredown that shows no signs of stopping... Result: Mike Bear by pinfall
