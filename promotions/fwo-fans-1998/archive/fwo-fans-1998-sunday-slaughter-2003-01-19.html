Introduction Previously... Just over a week remains before BodyCount, and the fWo finds itself fighting inner turmoil from members upset with the lack of US publicity they now have, and the ongoing struggle of having to take their show into unknown territories. As fWo managers struggle to take control of the company, the wrestlers continue to pursue their own agendas... and it could not happen at a worse time. BodyCount will crown a number one contender for the World Heavyweight Championship. A spot that will be locked in, sealed, and delivered at CyberSlam. BodyCount will set the tone for the year... but what tone will be set in the most chaotic enviornment the fWo has seen in their six year history? But while wrestlers and management fight over turf, other unknown allies and enemies scheme for their own advantage... The fans respond in kind, letting the first ever United States Champion know they don't care too much for him either. "I'm sick of the way you're treating everyone in the back. I'm sick of the way you're treating everyone in the office. I'm sick of the way you're treating every one of these fans! You want a fight where your opponent doesn't walk away? How about one where they guy shows up sober? Or what about one where the guy you're facing actually knows how to wrestle?" Brand spits the words out, personal life anger seeps in to his feelings for Borst. "Then you've got it. This here is what you call a challenge you British bastard. Pete Borst 'gainst Brand Frontier for that strap... at Bodycount!" The fan's erupt, showing their heartfelt approval. A chant of "Kick his ass" begins to build for a short while before finally petering out. Borst had stopped pacing at this point and had been staring out in to the masses to the side of where Brand Frontier stood. He finally raised the mic back to his mouth, slowly clearing his throat. "Sorry." Borst said after a long silence. "You're really boring and I stopped listening to you for a while there. Did you challenge me to a match or something?" "You know sure as Hell that I just challenged you. Don't try that bulls::censored:: with me!" Brand leaned in as if it would emphasize his disdain for the former fighter turned pro-wrestler. Borst laughed to himself for moment. "Oh no! Don't swear!" Borst continued sniggering for a moment then shrugged. "But OK. See you at Body Count, Batman." "Sure thing... Boy Wonder..." And with that Brand tossed the microphone to the stage with a thud as "Last Man Standing" began playing. As Brand backed through the curtain pointing at Borst the US Champion raised the title high mouthing the words "You ain't beating me fuckhead." as Slaughter came to an end with the fans salivating over the prospect of Brand Frontier colliding with Borst at Bodycount. Result: Borst via Count Out
