He picked him up, and hit a powerbomb into the top turnbuckle, completing The Divine Right. Crusader coughed up blood, walked forward, and then made a Ric Flair face plant. Brand rolled him over and made the cover. The three-count was academic. Brand's music resurfaced over the speakers, and the fans roared in approval as he was announced "winner and STILL fWo World Heavyweight Champion." He was handed his belt and he held it up high for the crowd to take pictures. However, the action in the ring wasn't over as Aimz rushed in and started lacing hard kicks to a hardly conscious Crusader. Brand dropped his belt and went to restrain Aimz. He pulled her over to a corner, telling her that the match was over. She kept struggling to break free. With Brand's back turned to him, Crusader clocked him between the legs and then smashed the World Title in his face. Brand fell tumbling to the outside. Meanwhile, Aimz punched Crusader in the jaw. Crusader took a few more punches, before hooking Aimz up in position for a Rock Bottom. She tried to struggle, but Crusader made the struggle short by leg sweeping her and driving her into the mat with all his force. The move had knocked her out cold. It was apparent that this was his new finishing move. Crusader spat blood into her face and left the ring with one comment: "Cock addicted bitch." His music cued up over the speakers. He made a brisk walk to the back, getting hit with food and drinks on the way. His hair was drenched with sweat and his muscles ached so badly from punishment Brand had dealt to him. Brand struggled to get up on the outside, his knee killing him. He slapped the mat in frustration when he saw Aimz unconscious in the middle of the ring.
