About the fWo (February 2001)

15 February 2001

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Thursday, 02.15.01 "I treat everyone in this company like family... except Dead, but we don't count him." -Chris Finn All about the fWo Greetings, and welcome to the fWo, or as it is known by it's full name, the "Fans Wrestling Organization". The fWo is an eWrestling promotion, meaning an online "federation" built around role-playing and character development with matches determined by the owner of the fWo. Most matches are determined mainly for angle advancement purposes. The fWo was first started in April of 1998, and has enjoyed a large amount of attention and popularity over this span of time. Currently the fWo is hit well over 100,000 times a day, with viewers checking back often to see the latest developments of their favorite wrestling characters. The fWo is designed to simulate "real" wrestling as closely as possible... from typical things like match and workrate details, to smaller details like advertising and merchandise sales. The fWo is designed to give players and fans an enjoyable experience while providing the closest simulation to actual wrestling as possible. The fWo is also solidly built upon humor, and several characters and areas on this site are intended as parodies or elaborate comedy routines. Not everything is humorous however, as players take their characters very seriously and guide their virtual careers with care. New visitors to the fWo are recommended to get to know the characters before diving into reading cards, and this can be done in the Biography area of the site. There you will find a list of active and inactive wrestlers, as well as announcers, managers, and other non-wrestlers. It's also a good place to get an idea of what characters look like and what brought them into the fWo scene. This list can be quite long, and for good reason. The fWo has hosted over a hundred personalities in the time it has spent in activation, and many characters have come and gone over the years. While you can still see the active roster competing on a daily basis, you have to look into the event history to view matches from characters who are no longer with the fWo. After getting to know the personalities involved with the fWo, visitors can read challenges and interviews in the fWo Interview section, and look up old interviews from their favorite characters. The Interview Archive is a good place to go to learn about the history behind a feud, or to learn how a character evolved into the person you see today. As mentioned before, the Event History is your source for reading every card the fWo has ever had in chronological order. The fWo has had well over a hundred events in the time that it has been active, and each of these are available for your reading enjoyment. Currently, the fWo has one show per week, that being "fWo Sunday Night Slaughter". It is considered to be a television event, and is covered in a lengthy recap fashion, complete with backstage interviews and non-match material. The fWo also runs a Pay Per View event every month, and looking at the Event Calendar you can get a feel for when the next Pay Per View is scheduled. Pay Per View events are run live in the fWo arena, and visitors are welcome to drop in and enjoy the atmosphere of a live card play-by-play chat. It's a good place to meet other fWo handlers, and spend a good three hours enjoying wrestling, sometimes more, depending on how many matches are booked on the actual show. The fWo also hosts the Fan Board, which is a place where you can discuss what is going on with the fWo, ask questions to some of the players and fans of the site, or even chat about eWrestling in general. While we do ask that you avoid shilling, the floor is wide open for you to talk about anything you like. Many of the fWo handlers frequent the Fan Board and are always interested in hearing your ideas and thoughts about their character. In many cases, things you say can impact how they perform. If you've looked around the fWo some already you have likely noticed the art. One of the things that makes the fWo unique in the world of eWrestling is the level of graphical design we use to help simulate the realism of the promotion. We are very proud of our artwork, but remember that there is much more to the fWo than just the appearance. The artwork is intended to help the reader visualize the characters and the surroundings, but the overall experience comes from the combination of writing and imagery. Please take a moment to look around the other areas in the fWo web site, and we hope that you enjoy your visit. If you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask them... but please check our detailed fWo FAQ before sending eMail. Many of your questions can be answered there. Thanks, fWoWrestling.com
