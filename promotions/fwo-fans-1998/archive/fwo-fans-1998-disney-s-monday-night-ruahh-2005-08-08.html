Introduction State of the union: The fWo has run like a boat on still waters of late, the championship title of Deacon has been challenged from several sources, and all eyes are on who the world champion will face at Meltdown. The tag team champions, while controversial by the back office, are slowly gaining their stride. In a few weeks an fWo milestone, Meltdown, will return to the air with the glowing promise of a championship title shot at every title the fWo has to offer. Things are as they should be, and the steadily growing popularity of the promotion has made all confident and reassured that the rocky road of the past several years is finally well behind them. Yet, in the shadows of the promotion there is a growing dread. There are people working for their own purposes in the fWo... and their ultimate plan and goals have yet to be fully seen... Major Announcement Last week The Deacon was fed up with all the constant interferences from 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs in his matches, so he decided to rectify the situation by challenging SVJ to a World Title match. The problem was that Ted Tallison didn't sign the match. He wanted that match to be on pay-per-view at Meltdown but Jacobs and Deacon had other plans. Jacobs felt he was the only man in the fWo that deserved that title and he was going to do anything and everything to keep that title on Deacon so he would be the man to take it from him at Meltdown. But now the Meltdown main event seemed to be in peril. Ted Tallison stopped the match from happening last week and told Jacobs, Deacon, and the fans that they would not be seeing Vince Jacobs vs. The Deacon for the Heavyweight Title at Meltdown as advertised. The camera slowly came into focus on an fWo backdrop in the backstage area where an attractive woman in a brown business suit stood holding a piece of paper in her hand. She stood there for a second with a smile on her face before she opened her mouth. "My name Jenny Conway and I am here on behalf of Mr. Ted Tallison. He wanted me to come out here and make a major announcement because he's such a busy man." "Tonight there will be a tag team match featuring the team of Rana Venenosa and the fWo World Heavyweight Champion, Deacon taking on Kodiak Vic Creed and 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs." "Since Mr. Tallison stated that the match at Meltdown between Vince Jacobs and the Deacon will not place he thought of a brilliant idea." The woman cleared her throat before speaking again. "The outcome of tonight's tag team match will decide the fate of the main event for Meltdown. So if the team of Deacon and Rana Venenosa wins the match then Rana Venenosa will be added to the match at Meltdown making it a three way dance for the World Title." "But if the team of SVJ and KVC wins then KVC will be added to the match at Meltdown.
