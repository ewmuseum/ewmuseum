Introduction In a few short days the first OCW show will be viewed for the first time... a fact on the front of the minds of the Disney Corporation, whose reaction to the upstart fed has for the first time fueled their interest in the fWo. Their first move... to return the fWo to the road in an attempt to regain the national interest in the promotion, and their second to hire a wrestling-savy General Manager in the form of the first fWo World Heavyweight Champion... Jim Dudley. But these actions are far from enough for several wrestlers, Craig Miles first amoung them. Now, with a special Unleashed event mere days away, the fWo returns to unfamiliar ground for the first time in nearly a year... Hi. No. "Mr. Wakeman... What a surprise." Chelsea said flatly, folding her arms and stopping just short of the Survivor IV Champion, who had been seated backstage. She took her gear bag from her shoulder, dropping it to her side. This told Rana, the lady wasn't going anywhere soon. Matt's grey eyes looked up at his visitor, "Oh, hi there. I didn't see you." "You know, I don't want to be rude, but you're an idiot." she said, shaking a strand of hair from her eyes. This brought a smirk from Rana Venenosa, who put his hands behind his head, "Oh. I don't know why you made the choices you did, and frankly, I don't want to know." She kept a stone cold face. "You're young, angry, defiant. Chelsea snapped. "You'd think that was obvious." "Ahhh," said Matthew, "that's interesting. Little Thunder The night for the Fan's Wrestling Organization had just begun, but it was apparent that this war was about to get much worse. The evening had a pall cast over it, a shadow so to speak. Many of the heroes of the fWo hadn't arrived yet, and the backstage atmosphere, despite the Disney Corporation's best efforts, was bleak. The gray heavy doors swung open and in walked... four kids. At first glance, they look like any normal group of children, possibly lost given their surroundings and look of awe. Diehard fans of the industry though recognize two of them. Sean and Jarod Roche had arrived, accompanied by their new brother Stephen and their newer still sister, Scerinn. The group certainly looked out of place, especially given the circumstances of the evening. The why and the where though were lost in the air as, following a few seconds behind, the Red Raver and the Mighty Hero strode in. Not exactly looking like they were ready for the events of the evening though. Like the kids, sporting backpacks and a duffel bag, Aimz had her hands full with a pair of small suitcases. Brand himself carried a small gray and metal box himself. A few steps in Jarod rushed back. "Dadda, is he sleepin?" "No J, you won't leave him alone long enough to fall asleep. Let him be, okay? It was a rough ride for the little guy, he's still getting his car legs." "Yeah Jarod, just be glad we don't have to give him another bath to wash off him getting car sick like last time." Aimz chimed in. Jarod peeked into the little gray box. A small yelp came from inside. "Jarod... look!" Sean's excitement was apparent and brought the attention of the entire group. They all turned to where he pointed and lo and behold there stood a small gathering of fWo Superstars. Jarod and Stephen's eyes grew bright when they noticed the throng of larger than life wrestlers, not exactly noticing who the group was. Brand did however, and soon after so did Aimz. Most were members of Death's army and Sean's excitement had caught the attention of none other than 'Nasty' Nick Brandish. He arched an eyebrow in Frontier's direction, straightening a bit in his stance to peer over his fellow members. Brand gulped, and bristled up as the two locked eyes. "Momma, can we go say hi?" Stephen croaked out, it seemed as if he was recovering from a bad cough or something. Aimz looked to Brand a bit nervously. They didn't like bringing the kids to an event any longer, family entertainment this certainly wasn't. The Soldier's of the Fifth Seal had seen to that. If it wasn't for Brandon's ex-wife's plane being held up they wouldn't have even considered bringing them here in the first place. "Not right now kiddo." Kiddo, wow, she was really starting to sound like a mom. Insert shudder here. "We need to get to our locker room and start getting ready. Ana'll be here to get Sean and Jarod in a bit." "But Amy..." Jarod piped in. Sean looked on with a smirk, he knew Jarod's powers of persuasion was unusually strong with the red head. "We wanna see tha' wreslters. Pweez." Knowing where this could lead Brand finally interjected, glancing over to the Nasty One. "Now guys, you'll be able to see most of the show from the skybox okay? You'll hang out up there with Chris' family and get to watch all the stuff in the ring. We can even leave the pup in the locker room. Okay?" Frontier raised the 'father's eyebrow', so Jarod and Sean both knew the chance to disagree or argue had passed. The little passenger in the gray box whimpered and pushed a furry nose through the metal gate hole at the small group of kids, his newfound friends. "Hey T'under. Good boy." Stephen squeaked, Scerinn looking on in silence. "Let's go guys, we've still got to get dressed for the event." Aimz spoke with a bit of impatience also noticing the attention they were getting from more and more Soldiers. With that, the flock with new family member Thunder the Great Dane in tow, sped off in search of the locker room, where they would prepare themselves for the night, the battles, and everything in between. Back Again A van came to a slow stop, and found a place to park outside of the arena. Milo Samus was back again. For the last month, Milo had been attempting to get the attention of the fWo/Disney board of executives. He knew, that he could wrestle with the best the fWo had to offer, in fact... this would be the easiest part. The hard part was getting the attention of the executives long enough to convince them that he was indeed, a talented wrestler. There are a lot of behind the scenes politics in the wrestling world, and Milo's name simply didn't have the credibility behind it to get a spot on the roster the traditional way. He was aware of this, and this is why he started his campaign to make it into the fWo. His first attempt at proving he could wrestle was thwarted by a solid boot courtesy of Death. The second attempt, fell short when Milo was arrested outside the Milkhouse. And the third attempt... well, if you saw Ruahh~! last week, you know that Milo ended up spending 48 hours dangling from an oak tree. "You sure you want to do this?" a friend said, not understanding how Milo could possibly want to sneak into another Ruahh~! show after the series failed attempts. "You're going to end up getting locked up for good man". Milo's spirits were untouched, and the failure he'd been facing only made him want this even more. He was back again, for the fourth week in a row. But why was he so determined to make it in the sport anyways? The answer was simple, he was chasing a childhood dream. "Look Max, you know how bad I want this man." Milo replied, stepping out of the Ford Econoline van. "Every day I spend out of a wrestling ring, I feel like I'm missing something... I feel like a part of me is missing. I don't want to look back someday and think 'Maybe I could have made it'. My dad would roll in his grave if he knew I was giving up... I'm sure Jill would too." "Yeah but Milo, seriously dude, you can't expect to get away with these... these... " Max paused for a second, trying to find the right word. "Stunts. You can't expect to get away with these stunts forever man. Disney is a corporate giant, who can and will crush you like an ant sooner or later." "They won't stop me tho.." "Are you insane?" Max interjected. "You've been terrorizing their shows for almost a month man." These came as hurting words to Milo, and he grew tired of the lecture his friend was giving him. "Who's side are you on anyways? The least you could do is support me bro. I understand the risks okay? I'd sooner rot in a jail cell, then to rot away inside, knowing I could be living my dreams." Milo slammed the van door hard, and turned to start walking away from the van. "Milo wait." Milo stopped in his tracks, and turned, making eye contact with Max. "What?" "I just had an awesome idea. The fWo are on tour now right?" "Yup". Milo nodded in an interested manner, he wasn't positive, but had a pretty good idea where this conversation was headed. "Well, since your plan tonight is more of a hit and run... we'll start a road trip of our own." "You mean..." "Yes dude, We'll follow the fWo around." Max smiled as he tapped the hood of the poorly maintained Ford Econoline van. "Nothing will piss them off more then seeing this rust bucket in every city they're in." Milo let out an excited yell, and the two met each other with a high-five. Disney may have been winning 3-0, but the game was just getting started. Little did she know Jeremy was responsible, but since she was placing blame on someone else, Jeremy wasn't about to jump at the chance to correct her. It was his intentional error to exclude Shirley's sister from the company list of traveling expenses. Tonight was their chance to return to the tag team division, and he needed to rid himself of all distractions. Needless to say, Roberta was a big one. "You know, Shirley, the fWo isn't a travel agency," Jeremy reasoned. "She doesn't have anything to do with the federation, and I can see how they could forget her. "Great," was the weak response. The two continued walking in silence for about a minute, both pairs of their boots audibly clapping on the shiny floor beneath them. "You ready?" Jeremy asked as they neared the curtains. "Yeah, but I'm really excited too. I have butterflies in my stomach. I haven't felt this way since Survivor!" Shirley conceded. Jeremy smiled at her admission, looking back before cementing himself in a patient position in front of the swaying curtains ahead of them. His head quickly shot back and froze as if he had seen a ghost. His heart began to rattle the bowels of his chest cavity as his complexion paled. It was Roberta, rushing towards them. And as one might guess, she wasn't a happy camper. Jeremy quickly rushed over to the A/V guy to tell him the two were ready. "Approaching The Enemy" by John Williams struck up and Jeremy snatched her arm, dragging her through the curtains and into the presence of an erupting crowd.
