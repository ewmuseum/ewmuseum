Wednesday Warfare, 2 February 2000

2 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The simple Washington building sat nestled in a large clump of trees, shielded from the rain by a heavy cover of foliage. The catering van pulled up outside the building and came to a steady halt. A worker stepped out from either side of the van and walked slowly to the back of the vehicle, looking up at the sky for signs of rain. It was overcast, but the sky remained free from rain. They opened the door and began to unload the trays onto small aluminum carts to take into the building. It was a big event today, some rich wrestling promoter had kicked the bucket back east and now it was time for all of the relatives to fight over the scraps. Visitors had already begun to arrive for the reading of the will, and were likely hungry. Begging for money built up a healthy appetite. They continued to upload the contents of the van onto the cart. There certainly were a lot of pies for this will reading... cream pies, cherry pies... pies of several varieties. What a will reading would need with all these pies was beyond the caterers, but the faster they got them into the building the faster they could get out of here and deliver food to that strip club across town. Now THAT was a job. They were rolling their carts into the building when Chris Finn drove his car into the parking lot. Finn got out, and waited while Geoff Green and James Armstrong emerged from the car after him. James looked at the car and gave a long sigh. "How long is this thing going to go Finn?" Chris shrugged. "A couple hours... maybe three." James groaned. "Did you happen to bring any of your magazines by chance?" Chris turned on James. "Dammit James, this is a will reading, not some kind of party." James looked hard at Chris. Finn sighed, and gestured to the trunk of the car. James smiled. "You two go on in, I'll be in there in a minute." More visitors arrived at the building, as hundreds of miles away Wednesday Warfare started to take to the air. Lone Times Backstage Machina paced back and forth and thumped his fist against a wall. Rudo watched him with some amusement. "Dammit Rudo... Ultra Violet getting married... dammit! I know there hasn't been a formal announcement or anything, but this is it! This is final! This wasn't supposed to happen to him... my arch enemy... he's supposed to be miserable! Unhappy! Like someone strapped into a chair and having Sheryl Crow music played at him!" Rudo had heard this rap before, and was growing tired of it. He looked on the other side of the room at Double S, Sid Snow, who was trying to get hold of his life partner David on the phone. Sid was not having much success by the look of things, and was now yelling into the phone. Rudo didn't understand why Sid wanted the overweight beer swilling clown anyway. Wrestling news reporters were by and large complete scumbags... with the exception of that new CWrestling crew. It was always the same with those clowns... trying to get themselves involved in everyone's personal life with their leeching and nagging. If Rudo had a nickel for every time some goofball reporter had begged him to get backstage he'd be a rich man. Rudo turned back to Machina, who had continued to rant with seemingly little signs of stopping. Rudo sighed hard and looked down the hallway. He spotted Scott Collins, and an evil grin crossed his face. It had been a few weeks since the bike incident, and he was ready to move his plan into phase two. Over his shoulder Rudo grunted at Machina. "Listen Ed, if it really bothers you that much why don't you just propose to Penny?" Rudo quickly ran down the hallway after Scott Collins, and missed the look that had crossed Machina's face. Machina was smiling. "Oh course! I'll propose to her first! I mean, revenge is like, one of the best reasons to get married! Thanks, Rudo!" Machina turned and walked in the opposite direction, presumably in search of Penny. Sid meanwhile had slammed down the phone and was breathing hard. His hands were balled into fists, and he grumbled. "Some days... I tell you, some days I swear I'd like to kill that cheating bastard..." Hellrazor (c) vs. Cactus Jon "Bombtrack" begins over the loud system, and heads turn to meet Cactus Jon who steps forth from backstage. Ignoring the hostility that radiates from those in attendance, he jogs lightly to the ring. Once there, he waists no time; having already formulated a plan of attack, he reaches underneath the ring and efficiently pulls out one table before dragging out another. Sliding the first, and then the second in, Cactus Jon looks to take down two birds with one stone; one prove doubters of his ability to compete in the hardcore bracket wrong this very night, and secondly, cast aside recent critics of the hardcore division in general. As he lifts and then begins to set up the first table, "Bombtrack" dies away and is replaced by "From This Day." The crowd rises in excitement as Hellrazor emerges, Lamont, as always, prodding along obediently at his side. Hellrazor acknowledges the crowds support with a knowing grin, showing them all the Hardcore title he pried from VL at Invasion. Then turning his attention to Cactus Jon who has momentarily stopped, he sticks out an accusing finger, before bringing it across his throat with a throat slash gesture. Undoing the title, he tosses it aside and decides to get this encounter underway. He dives underneath the bottom rope where he is met by stomps from Cactus Jon. Having anticipated these, he braces himself, and is able to shrug them off and rise to his feet. Spitting in the eye of Jon, he without warning unleashes with a torrent of punches. His fists embedding themselves one after another into the flesh of Jon, he soon has him off balance. Taking advantage of this, he grabs Hellrazor and soon after buries a knee, deep into his left eye socket. Jon lurches back grabbing blindly, but Hellrazor keeps the pressure on, grabbing him by the hair, and swinging him into and then over the ropes. Cactus Jon meets only the floor with a sickening smack. Hellrazor looks to wrap up the victory, sliding under the ropes and lifting Jon. He grabs an arm and slings him into the ring-steps, Jon's momentum forcing the upper half of them to completely dislodge from the lower and sends he and the ring steps crashing to the floor in an exhausted heap. Hellrazor rolls Jon aside, and lifts the rings steps high above his head with both hands. Austin pointed to the candy wrappers and other assorted food items that were scattered on the floor, the Eclipse's hand, and some hanging from one of the Eclipse's chin. The security guards worked with the large man, kicking at the refuse and looking at the figure of the man with distaste. As Austin began to lift, the Chameleon ran up to the gathering, obviously annoyed. "What in the hell happened here? Who attacked him? And dammit... he's my partner! I'm schedule to wrestle with him in some house shows this weekend!" John Willis placed a restraining hand on the Chameleon's chest. "Now you just hold on a minute... one question at a time. First. We don't know. We found him like this. Second. Again, we aren't sure. But whoever took him out knew what he was doing. Third. You're just going to have to find yourself another partner. Deal with it." The Chameleon looked at James Armstrong with his mouth agape, in disbelief. As Austin drug the Eclipse away, the rest of the fWo security team followed behind in tight formation. The security team passed Hellrazor, who was getting ready to leave the arena. Willis nodded to him. "Hey Hellrazor... don't take off just yet. We've got a problem... Eclipse was supposed to face a mystery opponent tonight... now you're going to have to take his place." Hellrazor started to protest, but then growled and paced back into the arena. The Chameleon watched them go, and kicked a pile of crates before wandering away. The scene did not go unnoticed however. From behind a corner Stump watched with silent speculation, and Black Quicksilver nodded to himself behind Stump. Black Panther vs. Mike Bear "Dragula" begins to play, and out to a loud ovation walks the tattooed tag team champion Mike Bear, who rolls into the ring quickly, looking all business for this big match. Bear gets up quickly, but Panther takes him down repeatedly with a series of lariats. Bear gets up and charges at Panther again, but Panther catches him with a side knee to the stomach, doubling him over, before quickly taking him over with a double underhook suplex. Panther covers, but Bear kicks out at two. Panther brings him up, and hooks him up for a suplex. He charges in at Panther, but Panther lifts a foot which smashes into the face of Bear, nearly flattening his nose. Bear stumbles out, clutching his face, and Panther comes out of the corner, before quickly whipping Bear into the ropes. He lifts him up, and smashes him back-first into the canvas with a thunderous spine buster. Panther covers, but Bear kicks out. Panther gets up, waiting for Bear, and when Bear makes his way to his feet, Panther charges and takes him down with a hard swinging neck breaker. Not satisfied with this move, Panther brings Bear up again before snapping him over with a suplex. Panther holds on, and repeats the move. He holds on once again, bringing Bear up to his feet. Panther brings Bear up again, and hooks him for a uranage slam. However, before he can execute the move Bear instinctively brings his knee up, right into a tender area on the Panther. The ref contemplates for a moment, but declares the low blow unintentional and allows the match to continue. Panther is doubled over, and Bear, not being one to pass up an opportunity to gain the advantage, goes behind Panther and looks to put him away with a backdrop driver. Bear is caught off guard, and Panther whips him hard into the ropes. He catches Bear coming off with a powerslam that causes Bear to bounce off the mat and into the air, nearly breaking his back. Panther quickly brings Bear up again, and puts him in position for a powerbomb. Panther brings Bear up, and spins him in a circle before throwing him down and planting him into the mat. Panther signals that he's going to end it, and brings Bear up and into position for another powerbomb. He backs up against a corner, and lifts Bear onto his shoulders. He then runs across the ring, before driving Bear into the opposite corner with another devastating powerbomb. Panther nearly appears to have snapped as he mercilessly brings Bear to his feet again. He shoves him hard into the corner, and sets him on the top rope. Panther then climbs up himself and, forgoing his usual pause during the superplex, hits the Pounce on Bear, who has been overwhelmed by the sudden explosion of fury from the Panther.
