Wednesday Warfare, 11 August 1999

11 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Chris Finn and James Armstrong sat alone at the announcing table inside the empty arena n Fargo, North Dakota. It was still six hours before the event would begin and the pair of announcers sat in silence. The ring was set up already, and everything was set for Wednesday Warfare in a few hours. Finn and Armstrong sat in silence. Finn looked over at Armstrong. "Nice weather today." Armstrong sat looking forward. "Oh ya, ya." Finn looked back forward again. "I hear there was a sale on Wood Chippers in the downtown market." Armstrong sat looking forward. "Ya?" Time passed, and in six hours the event began. Darkness vs. Eron the Relentless The cameras filmed the opening of Warfare as fireworks and loud rock music filled the arena. The light blazed before suddenly the arena was covered by a blanket of darkness. A candle "flickers" on the screen before the flame disappears, and the word "Darkness" appears on the video screen. A lone spotlight shines on Darkness as he walks down to the ring, while the rest of the arena is dark. Darkness rolls into the ring and waits his opponent of the evening, Darkeron. "The day the whole world went away" begins to play, and out walks Darkeron. Eron walks to the ring methodically, before he jumps into the ring and begins to brawl with Darkness. Darkness gets the upper hand and beats on Eron, before Eron reverses it and is on top of Darkness, pounding Darkness repeatedly. The two men continue to brawl with neither man getting a clear cut advantage. The referee pulls Eron up and Eron just responds by shoving him down. He and Darkness brawl some more, before Darkness gets the upper hand and throws him through the middle ropes out onto the floor. Darkness waits on the ring apron before jumping off with a dropkick from the ring apron into Eron, who goes sprawling backwards on to the floor. Darkness then crouches over Eron and pounds him relentlessly with punches to the head. Darkness then brings Eron to his feet, and whips him back first into the ring. Eron reverses it, however, and it is Darkness who takes the blow. Darkness walks back out and Eron hiptosses him onto the ramp. Eron waits for Darkness to get up, before charging at him and taking him down with a tiltawhirl flying headscissors. Eron brings Darkness up and looks at the crowd, which is a bad move as Darkness quickly thumbs him in the eye. He then takes him down with a gutwrench superplex on the ramp. Suddenly, the arena goes dark. The arena lights back up again moments later and the number "4" is on the video screen, as Darkness and Eron stop brawling to look at the screen. The light arena goes completely black again, before the lights come back on and Darkness is out on the floor being pounded by the Dark Riders. Eron sees what's going on before joining in, only on the side of Darkness. The two fend off The Dark Riders all the way backstage, where nearly 50 fWo employees must separate the six combatants. Matt Loco makes his way backstage where he tries repeatedly to get a word from Darkness and Eron... Result: No Contest Water problems. Backstage Al Snow was shown arguing with his dog Pepper about which of them was going to lead the way to the ring. They seemed to come to some kind of consensus, and Al Snow picked Pepper up and began to walk out to the ring. Water began to drip from the ceiling, and Al Snow looked up in the air in confusion. Suddenly a great wall of water struck down Snow, as Rain emerged from around a corner to see the destruction that he had wrought. Snow crawled to one foot as Rain looked down at the Hardcore champion. Speaking slowly, Rain told Snow, "You tell him... you tell him that what he gets tonight will be nothing compared to what I give him on Friday." Rain walked away as Snow coughed out water and struggled to get to his feet... Briana vs. Speed Demon Briana ran to the ring and waved her arms to the crowd, who cheered her loudly. She awaited the Speed Demon, and when he neared the ring she quickly took the fight to him on the outside. The two traded blows, with the Speed Demon actually gaining the advantage much to the surprise of the crowd. The more focused and intense Speed Demon rolled Briana inside the ring, and after a hard headbutt in the center of the ring the Speed Demon grabbed up Briana and delivered a hard pumphandle slam. The Speed Demon was unable to get the three count however, and frustrated he climbed to the top rope to deliver a flying elbow. He missed his attack as Briana rolled out of the way at the last moment, and quickly rolled him up for the pin. Stomping on the mat, the Speed Demon began to throw a tantrum but was consoled by Nikki of all people... who came to the ring to comfort what appeared to be her new man. Briana shook her head in disgust at this, but was quickly attacked by the Chameleon and slightly more slowly the Total Eclipse who came out from the crowd. Referees pulled the Chameleon and Eclipse off of Briana, but the damage had been done... Result: Speed Demon by Pinfall Meltdown Drawings Chris Finn walke to the ring with a small card, which fans of the fWo came to recognize meant that it was time for another Meltdown announcement. Since matches were not broadcast to the general public on Slaughter, six teams would be announced tonight. Chris Finn took the microphone and said, "Ladies and gentlemen... the first team that we will announce tonight... is the team of ERON THE RELENTLESS... and STONE! The second team we will announce tonight is the team of CRUCIFIX... and APOCOLYPTICA! The third team we will announce tonight is the team of PRIMETIME... and WOLF FANG! The forth team we will announce tonight is the team of CACTUS JON... and THE TOTAL ECLIPSE! The fifth team we will announce tonight is the team of KID COOL... and DEATH! The sixth and final team we will announce tonight is the team of BLACK QUICKSILVER... and LANCE STAR! Please join us on Slaughte when we will announce three more teams for the big Meltdown Pay-Per-View event." With that, Chris Finn left the ring and Warfare went to a well-deserved commercial break. Mike Bear (c) vs. Al Snow Al Snow makes his way out as the crowd chants "head!" after hearing "What does everybody want?" Al Snow comes to the ring wearing a "Job Squad 3:16" shirt, while carrying not "head", but "Pepper", his new friendly little dog. Snow holds his dog in the air, before rolling into the ring. Snow awaits the new fWo Hardcore Champion Mike Bear. Mike Bear comes out with a dumpster onto the entrance ramp. As Bear goes backstage, Snow is handed a little dog carrier, in which he puts "Pepper" in. Bear returns with a small rolling garbage can and brings it down to ringside. In it, he has a golf club, a computer hard drive, a cordless telephone, a ten pack of JVC videotapes among other items. He tosses the garbage can into the ring before rolling in himself. Snow charges at him but Bear takes him down with a leg whip. Both men return to their feet and Bear ducks the Snow charge, before backdropping him onto the garbage can. Snow crashes into it, and then Bear grabs the golf club out of the garbage can. Bear waits for Snow to get up, before he smacks it across the face of Snow. A pinfall attempt, but Snow kicks out. Bear pulls the ten pack of videotapes out and then splits Snow' legs apart, before driving the ten pack into the crotch of Snow. The crowd gasps as Snow clutches in pain. Bear isn't done. He finds the golf club, and drives it further into the crotch of Snow. Snow yelps out in pain, while his small dog begins barking. Bear covers Snow, but again, Snow kicks out... almost foolishly, as this match would be already over for most people. Bear brings Snow to his feet and whips him to the ropes, and as Snow returns Bear nails him with a shot to the midsection, before fisherman suplexing him onto the mat. A cover, but another kickout. Bear brings Snow to his feet, and backs him into the turnbuckles. He hammers Snow' midsection some more, before armdragging him out into the middle of the ring. Both men hurry back to their feet and Bear charges at Snow, and attempts a hurracarana. Snow catches him, however, and then hits a sitting powerbomb on the garbage can. Another cover, and another kick out. Snow takes the computer hard drive and returns the favor from earlier, with a shot to Bear' crotch. Bear yelps in pain, and the look on his face is obviously unsatisfactory. Snow with a cover, but Bear kicks out. Both men foolishly refuse to give up after painful shots to the groin... Snow goes outside the ring and reaches under the ring. He finds a ladder, and slides it into the ring. He then finds a table, and slides that into the ring as well. Snow sets up table in the corner, before taking the ladder, and laying it in the corner against the turnbuckles. Snow brings Bear over and slams him head first into the ladder. He then lays Bear onto the ladder in the corner, before grabbing the top of the ladder, and shoving it backwards into the table as Bear holds onto the ladder... Bear goes crashing through the table with the ladder still laying on him, while Snow goes for a cover. One, two, but Bear is able to get a shoulder up. Snow goes outside the ring and grabs another table. He sets it on the outside of the ring. He finds another table, and places it right by it. Snow goes back inside the ring and props up the ladder. He then takes Bear and and whips him into the ladder. Bear reverses it and it's Snow that goes back first into the ladder. Snow comes out and Bear hits a Death Valley Driver onto the garbage can. He then takes the garbage can and slams it on the head of Snow a few times, before covering Snow. Snow kicks out, and then Bear grabs the dog carrier containing Pepper. He slams it into the head of Snow, which damn near takes Snow' head off. Bear, foolishly doesn't cover Snow... he climbs up onto the ladder and holds the carrier in the air, celebrating his achievement. Snow, gets to his feet, and is back towards the ladder. He trips back over the garbage can, and hits the ladder backfirst... Bear goes flying off the ladder, almost doing a somersault into the air before landing backfirst through the tables at ringside! Snow looks around for the dog carrier, then finds it on the outside. He takes it, and makes sure Pepper is okay. He tries to cover Bear, but Bear is slumped in a position that Snow cannot even make an attempt. Snow decides it'd be best if he got Pepper out of there... he walks slowly up the ramp, and as he gets near the dumpster, he pauses and lets Pepper out of the carrier. Pepper is okay, and appears to be in fair condition with no harm done since nothing landed on him. Snow puts Pepper back into the dog carrier and hands him to an assistant offstage, before yelling out with a scream and running down to ringside. He pulls Bear up and kicks him in the stomach, before hitting his 'Snowplow' on the shards of table on the floor. Snow has a cover now, but Bear kicks out somehow. Snow is disgusted, and he takes Bear by the head up onto the stage. Snow goes to Irish Whip Bear backfirst into the dumpster, but Bear reverses it... Snow hits the metal, and staggers out and Bear kicks him in the midsection, before hitting a Pedigree on the ramp. He covers Snow, and pins him, thus ending the match. Bear's arm is raised, but he is still obviously hurt from what happened. Suddenly, Mick Foley arrives on the scene. He has a chair wrapped in barbed wire. He slams it across the head of Bear, before taking the wire off and wrapping it around the neck of Bear. He then tosses Bear up into the dumpster, before going backstage. He comes back out with a ladder, and he positions it before jumping off and planting an elbow into Bear. Foley then climbs out of the dumpster, and yells "BANG BANG!" before making an exit, with Bear left in the dumpster. Result: Mike Bear by Pinfall South of the border, or at least near it. The scene switched to a darkened room, where a dark silhouette was seen sitting in a chair. A very large sombrero was outlined against his head, and he slowly began to speak. "I was wrestling in Guadalajara, facing some dead beat named the Butcher. So I'm kicking his ass, as I'm wont to do, and he ends up outside the ring. I go to nail him with a no-hands flying press, but the jerk tosses a slab of beef at me in mid-air, trying to throw me off. Didn't work. I knocked him silly with it, and the crowd was awed. That night, I died and was reborn the stuff of legend...I am..." The lights suddenly went on, revealing Mike Heftel sitting in full Mexican garb. Shouting, he said, "I am... Top Con Carne!" He stood, and quickly exited the room on the way to the ring. Tope Con Carne vs. Ultra Violet Spanish music started, and Mike Heftel, or rather, Tope Con Carne walked down the entrance ramp with a large slab of beef over his shoulder. He waved to the crowd, but walked the proud strut of a Mexican warrior. Inside the ring he awaited his opponent, and Savantage blared over the arena speakers. Ultra Violet made his way down the entrance ramp, slapping hands as he went and nearing the ring and Tope Con Carne. Suddenly Tope Con Carne ran to the ropes and sprung over them to execute his dreaded Tope Con Carne, but it was so telegraphed that even Ultra Violet saw it coming, and UV deftly stood to one side and Tope Con Carne crashed into the steel barriers. Ultra Violet rolled him into the ring and covered him, pleased with the cheers and flashes of lights from the crowd. Ultra Violet waved happily, and sprung from the ring and went back up the ramp to the backstage area. fWo referees attended to Heftel, but suddenly a stranger jumped over the steel barriers and slid into the ring. He attacked the refs, tossing them from the ring and then tearing free his jacket. He grabbed Heftel by the hair, and screamed at him, yelling "You think you can make fun on Mexico? You think you can make light of the proudest country on Earth? You, and everyone in this arena will feel the wraith... feel the wraith of Los Latin! Your lives have now forever been changed... make a note of it!" Result: Ultra Violet by Pinfall Pain. Matt Loco sat alone in a small room trembling. In front of him was a small envelope on a table, it's contents a videotape that lay on the table. Loco looked at it long and hard, and slowly picked it up and turned it over in his hands. Almost against his will, he slowly inserted it into the VCR, and turned the television on. With a trembling hand, he turned up the volume. At first there was only static, and then a dimly lit room came into view. The scene was dark and blurry, and at first it was hard to tell what was being filmed. Then John Skyler opened his eyes, and a thin stream of blood trickled down his face from the corner of his eye. His face was sweaty and dirty, and his hair matted against his head. His mouth was gagged, and a burly male hand ripped the cloth free and tossed it to the floor. It landed next to his feet, which were bound to the chair and free of shoes. From the dark, the voice of Dina Sol; "What is your name?" John Skyler looked up wearily and mumbled, "J...john... Dina... why..." The large man in shadows struck with a punch to Skyler's face. Dina again; "That's wrong. Now you learn... hold his foot." The large man in the shadows raised a rusty steel sledgehammer, and brought it crashing down on John's left foot. The sounds of bone and gristle grinding into a fleshy pulp tore through the room followed by a devastating scream from John. He bit into his lip, and shut his eyes through a stream of blood, sweat and tears. The large man raised the sledgehammer again, and Dina said once more, "What is your name?" Still biting into his lip, John mumbled in a near incoherent, "J... j... joh..." Dina smiled a thin, evil smile. "Wrong. Hold his other foot." The sledgehammer swung through the air again, and the sound of the screams filled the room as the thick, wet sound came once more. Dina motioned toward John and barked an order. "Hold out his hand... now... now... what is your name?" John looked up into his bride's face with fear and pain. "Dina... Dina... why... why... why..." Dina motioned to John's hand and the sledgehammer swung down once more, cracking the hand and sending bone splintering through flesh. Blood sprayed up into Dina's face, and she wiped it free with the back of one hand. "Hold out his next hand... now, I ask one more time, what is your name?" John trembled, his eyes twitching, and began mouthing silently. Dina smiled and drew closer. "What was that? What is your name?" John looked up, his eyes dark and blood now streaming from one eye. "I am... I am... Fallen. My name is Fallen. F... f... fallen..." Dina smiled wide and nodded. "That's right Fallen, that's right. Now you're going to do something for us... you're going to do something to this man..." She held up a single photo, and through the red haze of his own blood, Fallen saw the photo of Cactus Jon, and knew only pain... Chris Universal vs. Prophet Loud rock music blared over the arena speakers, and Prophet came out from the crowd and climbed into the ring. He raised his arms and the crowd tentatively cheered him. Chris Universal came out from the backstage area with Lance Star and looked cockily down at Prophet in the ring. He waved Star backstage, telling him that he didn't need his help cleaning the ring of this "goo". Universal ran to the ring, and the two traded blows in the center of the ring, with Universal ducking a lariat and coming back with a dropkick from behind. Universal applied an armbar, weakening the bigger man and yanking back the head and raking the eyes when the referee's attention was diverted. Universal continued to grind in the hold, and Prophet began to weaken, his leg dropping to one knee. Suddenly the crowd began to roar, and missing fWo superstar Oracle ran to the ring, and clocked Universal from behind with a wrench. Seeing this, the referee quickly called for the disqualification as Prophet and Oracle double teamed Chris Universal. The two beat down Universal, and Lance Star ran to the ring to make the save. Oracle and Prophet slipped from the ring, and the two teams stared each other down as Warfare went to commercial... Result: Chris Universal by DQ Challenge made Backstage security officials were on the scene of a massive disturbance involving Death, Darkness and Eron. The officials held back the four wrestlers, who all struggled to get at each other, screaming and kicking as they fought to be free to maim. Eron screamed out, "You want me? You want me you big dead prick? You want to take on the Eron? I'll kick your candy ass, Cool..." Darkness grabbed out at Eron and shoved him backward, and in a commanding voice said, "Shut up you little worm, this is business for men, and the killing will be done by myself and myself only..." Death shoved a ref to one side and signaled for Darkness to bring it on. More officials hit the scene as Death yelled out, "You and me Darkness... you and me... and hell, Eron can come along too... I'll bring a partner, you two jobbers show up... and Hotter Than Hell will just... get... that... much... hotter." Darkness looked over at Eron, and the two men exchanged a hard glance... neither trusting the other but both bound by their hatred of the Dark Riders and their leader, Death... Scott Slugger (c) vs. Mystery Opponent Loud music blared over the speakers as the crowd got ready for the main event, and the second title defense of Scott Slugger in his new reign as fWo World Heavyweight Champion. Scott Slugger marched to the ring and looked nervous as he held the title over his shoulder. Behind him was Ice, but as the pair got to the ring the referee insisted that Ice go back to the locker rooms for the next contest in order to assure a clean match. Scott Slugger argued, but it was clear that the ref would not change his mind, and Ice left the area. Scott Slugger got into the ring, and grabbed a microphone, and addressed the crowd. "Just a few days ago I went to the mat against the fWo Tag Team Champion... now here we are, on Warfare, just a few days later... and I've got to wrestle a mystery man. Well Slugger fans, I found out who this mystery man is... and it's unfair that I should have to take on such a high-caliber athlete. Yes, it's true, this man's mother was arrested for embezzling from a sperm bank... but he's still the toughest wrestler I've ever faced... and now I have to face him again... so I guess there is nothing left to say... than get down here Rottdog!" The crowd looked up in confusion, expecting to see Rottdog on the entry ramp, and wondering if this was really the "high-caliber" man they were going to see. Rottdog's music played, but instead of Rottdog coming to the ring, a much smaller athlete walked out onto the entrance ramp. Mini-Rottdog stomped to the ring, as Scott Slugger pretended to cower inside the ring. Mini-Rottdog charged, but Scott Slugger held him back with one hand as the midget swung the air wildly. Scott Slugger picked Rottdog up into the air, and slammed him to the mat. Wiping his brow, Scott Slugger rolled up the small athlete and hooked the leg, gaining the pinfall. He held his title up in the air as he stood on top of Mini-Rottdog, and beamed at the crowd. Yelling out Scott Slugger shouted, "Suckers! Suckers! When are you ever going to figure out that Scott Slugger will always, forever, and ever be your physical and mental superior? When will you ever learn that nobody beats Slugger... nobody?" Scott Slugger's rant was suddenly cut off as the Black Panther's music played, and the man that will face him on Friday walked out onto the entrance ramp. The Panther stared down Scott Slugger, who stepped off Mini-Rottdog and looked at the Black Panther with a serious expression on his face. The two stared at each other as Warfare went off the air... neither noticing the shadowy figure high up in the arena watching the ring and Scott Slugger with a look of rage on his face, his fists clenching and unclenching with pure, unadulterated hatred... Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall
