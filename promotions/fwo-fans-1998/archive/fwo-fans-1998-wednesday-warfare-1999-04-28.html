fWo — Card

Wednesday Warfare, 28 April 1999

28 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The fireworks exploded as Wesnesday Warfare came onto network television live from Boston, Massachusetts. The crowd was in a somber mood after the heartbreaking events that occured at CyberSlam '99, culminating in the return of the Dark Riders, betrayal of Scotty Cool, and capture of the fWo World Title. Backstage the limosine of the Dark Riders arrived, and Scotty Cool, War, Plague, and Scott Taylor exited. Finding Geoff Green filming their arrival, the group ceased his camera and threw him into a wall, picking up the camera and looking into it, Death informed the viewing audence that tonight will be a tragic night for everyone in the fWo. He then let the camera drop to the ground, where the transmission was abruptly cut off... Fathom vs. Rudo With the disturbing images of the Dark Riders arriving at the arena still in the minds of the viewing audence, the cameras focused on the ring, where bWo member Fathom and Rudo of the Lone Gunmen squared off for a match to determine a Crusierweight Title contender. The two paced each other, and Rudo grabbed the outstretched arm of Fathom and whiped her around, catching her neck in a headlock, and dropping backward, causing her to catch and flip over his body before crashing to the mat head-first. Rudo attempted a pinfall quickly, but Fathom reached the ropes. Still holding the ropes she kicked outward, catching Rudo in the jaw. Fathom then went to the second rope and launched herself into a flying shoulder tackle which took Rudo off his feet. She quickly rolled him up, but as the ref got to two the lights in the arena went dark. Inside the arena screams were heard as the audence paniced, and Fathom and Rudo got to their feet. From the top of the entrance ramp Death appeared with Scott Taylor, and pointed down at the ring. Suddenly the lights returned to normal intensity, and War and Plague had climbed into the ring. Seeing this Rudo quickly slid under War's legs and escaped into the crowd, but Fathom was caught by Plague, who pinned her to the ground. War delivered several leg drops, and with Fathom dazed and helpless Plague began to lick her face and laugh uncontrolably. On crutches, Fallen emerged from the backstage area, but was caught by Death, who picked him up and tossed him easily into the crowd. Death then got into the ring, and grabbed a microphone from Matt Panzer... Result: No contest Death interview Looking out at the crowd, Scotty Cool, now know as Death, raised the World Title above his head, which had a small skull painted on the surface. Behind him stood War and Plague, both with the Tag belts draped over their shoulders. They kicked Fathom out of the ring, as Death began to speak. He said, "Welcome to the Dark Days of the fWo! Well, well, well. Looks like Cyberslam has come and gone, and Death has your belt! It was a great ruse, I must say... no one thought it was their hero, Scotty Cool, under the mask! No one would have suspected it was Scott Taylor here talking to me backstage for all those months. No one would have suspected that it was Scotty Cool planning to take over the fWo. Stevie, when you turned on me, you did me a favor! You got me out of the shadow of UltraCool! You put me on this path, Stevie. And now, you, and the rest of the fWo, are gonna feel the consequences! Let's talk specifics: first thing, Finn, you little weasel, I want Taylor back in the booth. Soon. If you don't get him his job back, your life is going to be a living hell... understand? Second, Ice... you tasted the Equal Opportunity Death Dealing before just about anyone else in this fed, besides maybe the Brawler. Did you like it? Cactus Jon, you Canadian ass, you think you can stand up to the Dark Riders? Not a chance. You're on the list, Jon, I'd watch yourself. Frenchie, Pierre, I want a piece of you just because you have a few more wins... you're going to regret that, you little freak. And your two cronies, well, they've lost to War and Plague, and I'm sure they won't be coming back for more anytime soon. Now let's get into the brass tacks. My former friends... Chris Universal... I have to thank you, you've financed the beginnings of an empire! Now go off and play with your wacko little friend, Cactus Jack. Mad Trucker, well, you're too screwed up to even bother with. Stone, Stone. Poor Stone. You know, you're the first on my list, Stone. How could you be foolish enough to trust me? I'll be seeing you soon. And, the kicker: UV. The last hope for the fWo. Y'know, UV, we go way back. I've been kicking your ass since we were six. What's going to make that change now? Besides, UV, you'll come around. We have a spot in the Riders, just waiting for you. Now, you people, the fans, what the hell is wrong with you? All of your heroes fell Friday night, you idiots. There's no one left to cheer for. There is no hope left... in the fWo." With that said Scotty Cool began to laugh maniacly when suddenly Stone's music began to play. Stone came out onto the entrance ramp flanked by Fyre and Wynd, and carrying metal baseball bats. Stone pointed one a Death, and as she spoke tears ran down her cheeks. Stone said, "Cool... I loved you. I trusted you. I don't know what made you turn your back on all of your friends or what we had, but if I have to burn this bridge then I'll do it. Cool, you hurt one of us... you hurt Fathom. For that, and for everything else, you're going to pay. Tonight." Officals arrived on the scene to haul away Fathom as Death continued to laugh, and said, "Stone, you bring your soldiers to the ring... and I'll bring mine." With that Stone and the bWo left the area with Fathom, as Death and the Dark Riders slowly exited the ring... Black Quicksilver vs. Samuel Smooth With the chaos behind them Samuel Smooth came to the ring for a Cruiserweight title match against Black Quicksilver. He was accompanied to the ring by Stevie Roberts, and the two made their usual sexual gestures to female members of the crowd as they went. Then Black Quicksilvers' theme played loudly throughout the arena as he hit the ramp running like a madman, sliding into the ring and immediately going after Smooth with chops to the chest. Quicksilver placed Stump in the corner and resumed the attack. Quicksilver maintained the offense with a whip to the ropes, and then a nice-looking flying forearm. He followed it up by throwing Smooth into the corner, and hitting a handspring elbow. Smooth slowly staggers out, and BQ grabs a headlock, before catapulting himself off the second turnbuckle with a bulldog. He rolled Smooth over for a pin, but Smooth kicks out. BQ continues the attack as Smooth was groggy, sending Smooth to the ropes again and this time following up with a superkick that sends Smooth out onto the ring apron. Smooth slowly gets back to a vertical base, and as he does, BQ knocks him off the ring apron with a crescent kick. BQ was really a house of fire as he goes on to the outside and sends Smooth back into the ring. From there, he follows it up with a great Samoan Drop. Smooth is flustered and BQ finally attempts a cover, receiving a count of two. With both on their feet again, Black Quicksilver attempted to toss Smooth into the ropes, but Smooth reversed it and Quicksilver was sent to the ropes instead. On the outside Stevie Roberts grabbed Quicksilver's leg, and Quicksilver fell to the mat. As Smooth attempted a cover, the Falcon arrived on the scene and ran to ringside behind an unsuspecting Roberts. From behind the Falcon decked Roberts, and sprayed "REVENGE" on his back. Smooth watched this and grew distracted, turning his attention away from Quicksilver in the ring. As the Falcon left the arena Smooth turned around, and was met by a stump to the head. Quicksilver then rolled Smooth up for the cover. As Quicksilver exited the ring a delivery man brought him a basket of flowers, addressed "For last night" and signed "head". As Quicksilver read this he began asking Stump what he had been up to the night before in it's hotel room, but Stump did not answer. Suddenly, Chris Universal's music blared over the arena PA system... Result: Black Quicksilver by Pinfall Chris Universal Speaks the Truth As the Black Quicksilver exited, we hear "Do you buy what The Sellout is selling?" and out walks "Mr. Sellout" Chris Universal, wearing a black metallic shirt and brown slacks, and the Internet Title draped over his shoulder. The crowd erupts in a ovation of boos, as the cocky champion stands on the ramp grinning from ear to ear. He turns back and looks up at the "TravisTron", and two great big banners roll down on each side, depicting Universal with the Internet Title draped over his shoulder. Universal walks down the ramp cockily, and it appears as if Universal has a new personality since turning his back on his mentor this past Friday night at the biggest pay-per-view of the year; CyberSlam II. Universal confidently walks up the ring steps, walks across the ring apron, climbs up onto the second turnbuckle, and holds the Internet Title up with one hand, before smirking, and climbing back down onto the ring apron, and climbing into the ring. He cockily walks to the center where his custom made dollar sign pyro lowers into place, and instead of posing, he simply stands proudly, as the fireworks shoot off behind him. The crowd continues to boo as Universal asks for a microphone, and receives it. As the boos die down, Universal begins to speak: "As the Sellout stands here in this damn ring, he contemplates and wonders why he didn't turn his back on Mick Foley a long, long time ago. When I asked Mick to come out here on Warfare this last Wednesday, and I apologized to him in front of the billions... and billions of my fans, that piece of Long Island garbage had the nerve to interrupt me. You see, when I said that I was sorry, I wasn't finished. He had the audacity to hug the Sellout, he had the nerve to put his filthy arms around the Sellout's chiseled body, he had the nerve to interrupt the words of damn wisdom that were about to come out of the Sellout's mouth. I wasn't finished. I was going to say; "I'm sorry I didn't do it SOONER". And by that, I meant I was sorry I didn't take my hand and slap it across his face about twenty damn years ago. You see, when you're as good as me, you're as good as it gets and there is nothing... and I mean NOTHING that can stand in your way from achieving greatness. So when I beat that bald-headed Mr. Clean wannabe in the damn ring, I achieved greatness and became the best f'n Internet Champion there ever is, ever has been, and ever will be. And then I took my custom made Internet Title and slapped Mick Foley across his silly looking face, and bid him farewell because we all know that Mick will be too damned embarassed to ever show his face in the fWo again. And now a new era begins in the life of the Internet Champion. I'm wearing a $500 shirt, a pair of slacks that probably costed more than your last month's rent, and a watch that is so expensive, a blind person could look at it and still be able to read what time it is. I'm in the best shape of my life, I'm the best damn two hundred and forty five pounds this side of a negative piss test, and best of all, unlike 90% of you freaks out there I don't need viagra or caffeine to stay UP all night. Like I say, you can't be me, you can only dream about it. Anyhow, I've been told that tonight that baseball playing loddy-doddy freak and that walking, talking, breathing piece of Arizona garbage will be wrestling each other for the right and more importantly the PRIVELEGE to step inside the same ring with the Sellout. And if you want my opinion on it and I *know* you do, I couldn't give forty shares of inflated Microsoft stock WHO I will face on Warfare, because you can all count that I'm going to take their silly ass out with the Cha-Ching, and three seconds later I'll walk out the Internet Champion just like I walked in. It's money in the bank. Trust m... wait, that's not it, that's not it at all. Okay, this is it, this is it... until next time, it has been YOUR pleas... no, that's not it either. Damnit, this is it... if you BUY what the SELLOUT is SELLING!" Universal's music begins to play, and he looks over the sea of fans with a smug look across his face. He holds his Internet Title with one hand high in the air, which only makes the ruckus fans louder, as they continue to boo him. Universal makes his way out of the ring down near the guardrail, and stops to jaw at a fan for a minute, before exiting up the ramp as Warfare heads to a commercial break. Oracle vs. New Ninja Oracle makes his way out with the crystal ball as the theme from "Thirtysomething" plays throughout the arena. Oracle climbs into the ring and sets the crystal ball in the corner, as he awaits the New Ninja. Oracle is coming off a great performance in the battle royal this past Friday at CyberSlam. "Body Movin" begins to play as the New Ninja walks out onto the stage, who in the past has done very well in battle royals, did not fair so well in the one held at CyberSlam. Ninja makes his way into the ring and the two men prepare to lock-up. Oracle with the early advantage with a side headlock, then a quick headlock takedown. Oracle begins to work him over, but Ninja uses his leg strength to put Oracle in a headscissors. Oracle nips up out of it, and Ninja follows suit with a nip up of his own. Both men putting on a great technical showing in the early goings of this match. Both men lock up again, this time in a test of strength. Oracle easily over powers Ninja at first, using his massive upper body mass to push Ninja towards the match. Ninja makes a comeback, using his upper body to push and his lower body to hold his position as he bridges up towards Oracle, then grabbing an arm, and applying an armbar, before sending Oracle to the ropes and hitting a dropkick, which only staggers Oracle. Ninja continues his attack and hits a spinning wheel kick, which further staggers Oracle. Ninja decides he should pull out all the stops, and he goes for the jumping thrust kick to the jaw, but Oracle catches his foot just in time. Ninja however counters with an enziguiri, but Oracle ducks and Ninja falls on the mat face first. Oracle now on the offense, grabs Ninja by his hair and throws him over the top rope out onto the cement floor. Oracle goes on the outside and slams Ninja on the cement floor. He then goes back up on the ring apron, and gets a running start before coming off with a big-time elbow into the stomach area of Ninja. Oracle brings Ninja back into the ring and goes for a pin, but only gets a count of two. He stands up and drops another elbow. Goes for another cover, only two again. He brings Ninja to his feet and nails him with knee to the stomach, and follows up with a reverse neckbreaker. Another cover, and another kickout at two. Oracle is quite frustrated, but continues on the attack. He bounces off the ropes and hits a legdrop, and demands the referee to count... covering Ninja with just his leg. The Ninja quickly kicks out this time however; and it becomes clear that Oracle is losing his patience. He brings the Ninja to his feet and whips him to the ropes, and goes for a tiltawhirl, but Ninja reverses it into a flying headscissors, which takes Oracle off his feet and he slides out underneath the bottom rope onto the cement floor. Ninja takes the time to recover, and as Oracle gets back up to his feet on the outside, Ninja uses the second turnbuckle to hit a springboard body press up and over onto Oracle on the outside. Ninja brings Oracle to his feet and whips him backfirst into the guardrail. Ninja follows it up with a nice hurracarana as Oracle staggers out, and with the momentum Ninja rolls back into the ring and prepares for another high impact move. Plotting carefully, Ninja first executes a baseball slide into Oracle, which rocks him back into the guardrail, he remains on his feet however. Ninja goes outside onto the ring apron and uses the middle rope to propel himself onto Oracle, with a beautifull executed asai moonsault. Oracle is able to use his massive size and strength to catch Ninja, and then he puts him up onto his shoulder. It looks as if he's going to ram him into the ring post, but Ninja escapes down behind him and pushes Oracle into the ringpost instead! As Oracle walks out backwards, Ninja catches him offguard with a reverse russian legsweep on the outside. The referee comes outside and instructs Ninja and Oracle to bring the action back inside, so the Ninja complies. As this is happening, the Black Panther begins to make his way down from the ramp, carrying the International Title. Ninja brings Oracle up and rolls him into the ring. Ninja jumps up onto the ring apron, and then uses his agility to propel himself over the top rope to hit a leg drop on Oracle. The Black Panther makes his way down to ringside, and hops up onto the ring apron. The referee sees this, and his attention is diverted. As he instructs Panther to leave the area as he has no business being out there, Ninja brings Oracle to his feet, and hits a plethora of rights and lefts to the body area of Oracle, before kicking him in the stomach, and setting him up for a suplex. Oracle' weight is just too much, however, and Ninja cannot execute the suplex alone. Suddenly, Mitch Wilson jumps over the guardrail from the ringside area and jumps into the ring. Despite any problems he may have had in the past from the Ninja, he climbs into the ring and helps him, as the two execute a beautiful double-team suplex on Oracle. Ninja looks at Wilson in confusion, as Wilson climbs outside the ring and walks towards the Panther, telling him that he has no business carrying around the International Title when he doesn't even know where Spain is located. Panther jumps down and the two men jaw back and forth, meanwhile in the ring Ninja has Oracle pinned. The referees attention stays on the outside, as Ninja brings Oracle to his feet, and whips him to the ropes. Oracle however grabs the ropes so he does not return to meet the jumping thrust kick by Ninja, and as Ninja comes back down to his feet Oracle charges towards him and hits him with a clothesline. Oracle then follows it up with a piledriver, as Wilson and Panther begin shoving each other on the outside. Oracle hits a powerbomb, before slapping on the Oracle Dream. Another official comes down to ringside and tells the on-duty referee that he will take care of Wilson and Panther, so the referee *finally* returns to see Oracle with the sleeperhold locked on Ninja. The Ninja is fastly feeling the affects, and a few moments later after his arm has raised & dropped three times, Oracle is declared the winner. Oracle goes on the outside where he joins the fray with Wilson, Panther, and now a half dozen fWo officials. Oracle grabs his crystal ball and says "The future says I will kick your ass, Wilson... and then the International Title will be mine!", as the discussion continues and Warfare heads to a commercial break... Result: New Ninja by Submission The Running Russian Arrives The music for the Flying Frenchie began to play, as the crowd began to boo. However, Lone Gunmen leader Machina ran down the entrance ramp to the ring, which garnered louder boos. Machina got to the ring and began to run around it, not stopping. Rudo and the other Lone Gunmen came onto the entrance ramp and addressed the crowd, telling them that they were witnessing the fWo debut of the new superstar from Russia, the Running Russian! Machina continued to do laps around the ring as Rudo announced that Billy James would be wrestling the Running Russian that night. James' t-shirt was ripped slightly, and fans present at the show noted a tiny bit of facial hair on the fWo superstar, who swaggered to the ring. James stood inside the ring as Machina continued to do laps on the outside, watching the masked superstar run. Finally Machina grew tired, and collapsed outside the ring. Billy James then tossed him inside, and "pinned" him for the three count, which Machina counted himself with a free arm. As Billy James jumped up and down over his victory, Machina stood up and shoved him to the ground before addressing the crowd. Machina said, "The problem with this country is simple, and I learned what it was while I was on my noble quest to be president. I'm not running for president any longer, but I just want all of you to know that I'm still a concerned American citiz... er... person-like guy. That's why I come to you with the reason our country is in the cess-pool: the French! It is the French that have led us into this decay of crapfullness and patheticisms. As Rudo is my witness, I will rid this country of French people, one smelly loser at a time!" Machina raised his arms up for a great ovation, but heard only a single "You suck!" from high in the arena in response. The Lone Gunmen exited the area then, as a loud HOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOONK! was heard... Mad Trucker (?) vs. Joy Following the great HOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOONK the Mad Trucker came out onto the entrance ramp, and sneered at the crowd. The Trucker wore a darker flannel shirt, looked slightly fatter, and had stubble. He was joined by Darkness, who walked behind him and grinned evilly. At the ring Darkness informed Chris Finn that he scheduled this very special match himself, and Joy's music began to play. Joy came out onto the entrance ramp with a large men in a Care Bear outfit, and tossed candy out at the crowd. He then warned them to brush away the little sugar monsters with toothbrushes and toothpaste, or he'd make a real mess in their mouths. Joy stopped as he spotted Darkness at ringside, but slowly climbed into the ring regardless. He then pinned a happy face button on the shirt of the Mad Trucker, who responded by spitting in Joy's face, and then punching him there. The Trucker took off the button and stabbed at Joy's head with it, drawing blood and causing Joy to shout "owie" very loudly. The Trucker began to slam Joy up and down on the mat by his head, and then removed the turnbuckle cover. The Trucker picked Joy up high above his head, and slammed him groin-first into the exposed steel. As Joy rolled in agony in the ring, the Trucker placed a boot on his head and got the three count. Joy began to crawl from the ring, but Darkness grabbed the microphone and told him that he wasn't done just yet... Result: Mad Trucker by Severe Beating Darkness vs. Joy The Care Bear attempted to pull Joy to safety, but Darkness entered the ring and informed the timekeeper to ring the bell for this next match. Darkness batted the Care Bear aside, causing the head of the costume to fall off, revealing the Speed Demon underneath. Darkness drug Joy into the center of the ring, and began delivering snap suplex after snap suplex, dropping Joy on his head and releasing the hold on the suplex early for additional damage. Joy got to his feet and stumbled around in a daze, while Darkness measured and told the Trucker to remove the metal stairs from ringside and toss them into the ring. With the steel steps in the ring, Darkness picked up Joy and set him up for a piledriver, ramming his head directly into the steps, causing Joy to flop off at a strange angle. Darkness then tied Joy up in the ropes and began beating on him with the steel steps. Darkness was eventually being joined by the Mad Trucker, who smacked him in the back of the head with a steel chair as Darkness attacked from the front with the steel steps. Finally, Darkness let Joy flop back into the ring and placed a single foot on him for the cover. As Darkness and the Trucker left the ring, Darkness asked the crowd who the most evil man in the fWo was now... Result: Darkness by Even More Severe Beating Austin Davis vs. Scott Slugger "The World's Greatest Athlete" Scott Slugger made his way out to a reaction of boos from the crowd; as his award winning theme "Back in Black" is heard throughout the arena. Slugger appears a little worse for wear after his match this past Friday at CyberSlam with "Death". Slugger however, realizes a lot is on the line in this one, and seems to be a man on a mission as he makes his way down to the ringside area. Slugger rolls into the ring quickly; and taunts the crowd in a confident fashion. As he does, "Trust me" is heard echoing throughout the arena, and "Baad Ass" Austin Davis and two-time former Internet Champion makes his way out to a mixed reaction from the crowd on hand. Davis makes his way down to the ring at a speedwalk pace, and as Slugger eyes Davis, he decides to exit the ring. Slugger charges up at Davis with a running clothesline, and Davis ducks. Davis turns around, nails Slugger with a kick and and goes for a DDT, but Slugger uses his strength to backdrop out of it, and Davis lands hard on the ramp. Slugger then brings Davis up by his head and brings him up the ramp, as the referee follows the action. Slugger grabs Davis by the arm and whips him into the lower portion of the TravisTron, but Davis reverses it and Slugger goes back first into the tall structure. Slugger staggers outwards, and Davis hits a belly-to-belly suplex on him. Davis goes for a cover, but only garners a two count as the referee is on top of the action. Davis brings Slugger to his feet and hits him with a bevy of punches, before grabbing a headlock and bringing him underneath the huge structure above... they continue backstage and down three steps, before Davis throws him backfirst into the wall. Davis continues his assault, taking Slugger further into the backstage area, slamming him head first into the wall. They continue near a row of vehicles that are assembled in a parking lot type area, and Davis grabs Slugger and goes to slam him head first into a car windsheild, but Slugger stops it from happening, and nails an elbow into the side of Davis, which catches him offguard. Davis holds to his side clutching in pain, as Slugger recovers, spots a trash can, and slams it into Davis, knocking Davis back onto the cement. Slugger brings Davis up and rakes his eyes, before whipping him into the car. Davis slams into it backfirst with authority, and a thud is heard as Davis hits. As Slugger pushes the ref out of the way momentarily, Davis leans up against the car trying to regain his composure. Davis is resting up against the hood, as Slugger begins to run at Davis. Davis bends over however and backdrops Slugger onto the hood, and Slugger' boots end up crashing through the windshield. Slugger' back hits into the hood *hard*, as Davis backs up, and drives his elbow into Slugger' throat. Davis then takes the garbage can and drives one side of the opening into Slugger throat, and Slugger begins to cough up some blood. Davis then climbs up onto Slugger and covers him on the car, and gets a count of two and a half as Slugger gets a shoulder up just in time. Davis brings Slugger to his feet and they take a long walk towards a "portal" leading back into the arena, on the way there, they exchange punches, chairs to the head, and other pleasant things. Davis takes a chair, and he goes to swing at Slugger, but Slugger moves out of the way and the referee is nailed to the side of the head. Davis grins as Slugger bends over and checks on the referee, before Davis decides to head back down to the ring. He begins to walk down the stairs; getting a head start on Slugger who has begun to make his way down as well, shortly behind Davis. Davis hops over the guardrail and then turns and looks at the crowd, as Slugger makes his way down and charges up and over the guardrail with a flying clothesline. Slugger goes for a pin on the floor, but realizes there isn't a referee around and he brings Davis up, nailing him with a few punches, before dropping to his knee quickly, and hitting Davis with a lowblow. Davis bends over and writhes in pain, clutching himself. Slugger follows it up with a fisherman's suplex, cradling the leg for another pinfall. Slugger has this match won, but there is no referee around. Finally, Slugger releases his leg and sits on his knees, frustrated, and contemplates his next move. Slugger looks at the ring steps and an idea sudddenly comes to mind. Slugger brings Davis up and walks over to the ring steps, hooks both Davis' arms, and yells "Strike three you piece of trash!"; before walking up the ring steps, and spins Davis back into the floor, nailing the 'Curveball' finisher. He sits up, and marvels at his own accomplishment. He then realizes a referee is still not present, so he brings Davis to his feet and rolls him inside the ring. He hits a somersault legdrop, before covering Davis. At that moment, a referee runs down the ramp and enters, and begins the count. Davis somehow kicks out at two and three/fourths, as Slugger looks stunned. Slugger stands up and pushes the referee, telling him that if he had been there just two-tenths of a second earlier, he would've won the match. Slugger' towers over the referee and they begin jawing back and forth, as Slugger advances to the referee, the referee steps back. The two are near the ropes face-to-face, as Davis regains his strength. Davis sees both men near the ropes, and goes running, taking both of them over the top rope out onto the cement floor with a clothesline. All three men land hard on the cement, all hurt and basically unable to move at the moment. Seconds pass by as Davis is the first to show any movement, and he crawls over to Slugger, placing his arm on him. The referee is unable to move, however, and this incenses Davis. Davis slowly stands up, and begins kicking the referee, before telling fWo security official John Willis to "take this zebra-striped pansy ass back to the dressing room", Davis rolls Slugger into the ring, and then walks over near the announcers table on the other side of the ring. He grabs a chair and slides it into the ring as medical personnel bring out a gurney for the fallen referee. Davis places the chair in place in the middle of the ring and brings Slugger to his feet. Davis kicks him in the stomach and hits the Davis Death Drop (sitting faceslam) on Slugger on the chair, before going for a cover. No referee is around, until ANOTHER referee begins running down the ramp. One, two, but Slugger is somehow able to kick out at the last possible moment. Davis is furious, but continues his attack. Davis slides outside the ring and reaches underneath, and grabs a table. He slides it underneath the bottom rope and climbs in, first setting it up in the corner in a standing position. Davis brings Slugger to his feet and whips him towards it, but Slugger reverses. Davis goes back first into the table, which doesn't do much damage. Davis walks out and Slugger kicks him in the stomach, before hitting a swinging neckbreaker. Slugger then brings the table and sets it up a few feet away from the corner on its legs. Slugger grabs Davis and brings him into the corner. He goes for a swinging DDT, and as he does Davis reverses it into a release Northern Light Suplex! Slugger goes crashing through the table! Suddenly, Ice begins to make his way down the ramp. Davis is slow to get up, as Ice makes his way into the ring. Ice uses his huge hand to grab the referee and throws him over the top rope. Davis goes for the cover, but the only referee who did not have his ass kicked yet, just did by Ice. Davis spots Ice and climbs to his feet, and he walks over to Ice and the two begin exchanging pleasantries. Davis tells Ice to "go to hell and melt you son of a bitch", while Ice responds with some rather unkind words. The two men are face-to-face now, as Slugger removes pieces of the table off of him, and sits up. He sees Davis & Ice and is very slow to get up, Davis & Ice turn and see Slugger get up, who gingerly walks over and begins yelling at both men as well.
