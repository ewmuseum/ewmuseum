Wednesday Warfare, 19 July 2000

19 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Press Conference, Part I As Wednesday Warfare explodes onto the airwaves, we find ourselves in a medium-sized conference room with Chris Finn standing at a podium. The camera slowly zooms in, before reaching a firm shot of the face of the owner of the fans Wrestling organization. The fWo World Title is noticeably present in front of him at the podium, and without hesitation, he begins to speak to the dozens of reporters, camera men and other noted dignitaries preparing for the announcement of the main event set to take place at SummerSlaughter. "Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for joining us here today for the announcement of the main event set to occur at SummerSlaughter on July 29... from the Alamodome in San Antonio, Texas." The camera widened again to see The Boston Strangler sitting to the left of Chris Finn, while Mike Bear sat to the right, both with tables in front of them. Finn continued. "Since May 20, The Boston Strangler has held the most coveted prize in our industry, the fans Wrestling organization World Championship. Since that time, he has defeated several top names in the fWo, such as four-time fWo World Champion Scott Slugger, Eron The Relentless & Mitch Wilson among others. In the meantime, the man the Strangler will compete against at SummerSlaughter won several victories over fWo legend Scott Collins, including one last month where he retired him. From there, he went on to defeat Courage, Machina, Crusader & Wretch to win the Next Level tournament, and thus won himself a shot at the fWo World Title at SummerSlaughter." Finn took a swig from his glass of water, before continuing. "Without question, there is no doubt in my mind that Mike Bear will pose a fine threat to the World Title, and no doubt give the Boston Strangler a run for his money and then some. There is no doubt in my mind that the Boston Strangler is one of the finest champions we've ever had, but there is also no doubt in my mind that Mike Bear is ready, he's determined, he's focused... and his work ethic may be unparalleled in this sport. To be totally honest, I would not be surprised in any manner if Mike Bear came out on top as the fWo World Champion ten days from now." As the reporters continued to take notes, Finn asked them if they had any questions. One reporter in the front row stood up. "Yes, I was just wondering how much confidence you have here today by making this match and automatically assuming that the Boston Strangler will get by Primetime tonight at Warfare... I mean, to be fair, Primetime has never lost that title..." Finn interrupted the reporter. "I'm confident because The Boston Strangler is on a roll... he's defeated Primetime before, and there's no question in my mind he will be able to defeat him again here later tonight. Next question." Another reporter stood up and asked exactly how many tickets had been sold for SummerSlaughter. Finn began to field that question when from behind the curtains behind Finn came Primetime himself wielding a chair. He nailed The Boston Strangler in the back of the head with a chair shot. Mike Bear turned and saw this and sprung to his feet. He charged, but Primetime took him down as well, before grabbing Bear by the hair and throwing him off the side of the stage, which wasn't a big drop, but enough to throw Bear offtrack. Primetime then grabbed The Strangler by the hair and dealt him several closed fists to the head, before hooking him for the Primetime Bomb. He pushed Finn away as Strangler' head rested between his thighs, before Primetime unleashed Strangler through the table in front of him. Shards of glass, cubes of ice, water and wood flew all over the place. Security hit the stage and began hauling Primetime away from the scene, while reporters tried to move away from the chaos as soon as possible. Finn thanked everyone for coming as they left... with an expression of anger and contempt for Primetime on his face. The footage faded to black, and the opening for Warfare began... Black Quicksilver (c) vs. Mike Heftel Wednesday Warfare kicks off with it's usual opening of pomp and circumstance. Fireworks rage inside the arena, before the sounds of "We're Not Gonna Take It" by the one hit wonder Twisted Sister blares throughout the arena. This brings out the STUMP Squad, the fWo Crusierweight Champion the Black Quicksilver, and his opponent for the evening, who is oddly enough his tag team partner, Mike Heftel. The men enter the ring, exchange a handshake, and begin circiling each other. Lock up, and Heftel takes BQ over with an armbar. BQ flips out of it, and turns it into where he takes Heftel down. BQ keeps a hold on the arm, and jumps up, and nails a quick legdrop to the arm. Whip into the ropes by BQ, and Heftel comes back right into a press slam. Heftel pops up, and BQ jumps on looking for a huracanrana, but Heftel reverses it into a tiger bomb. Heftel with a pinning situation, but BQ uses his two feet, and nails Heftel in the sides of his head to break the count. BQ jumps on with a cover but Heftel bridges out. Heftel then turns it into a german suplex, with a bridge. However, BQ is able to fight out of that at two. Both men up now, Heftel attempts a lariat. BQ ducks, and when Heftel turns around, he walks into a facebuster. BQ covers and hooks the leg, but once again, Heftel kicks out. BQ brings Heftel up, and places him on the top rope. BQ looks for a top rope huracanrana, but once again, Heftel hooks it. Heftel simply drops BQ to the mat, with a jacknife Big Lazy Kevin Nash-esque powerbomb, before continuing to the top rope. Heftel then comes off with a guillotine legdrop which connects. Cover by Heftel, and BQ kicks out once again. Heftel brings BQ to his feet, and hooks him for a vertical suplex. However, while in the air, BQ gets out of it, jumps on Heftel's shoulders, and turns it into a victory roll. However, Heftel is able to squirm out of the pin attempt. Both men up, and BQ charges with a clothesline. Heftel ducks, and then takes him over with a fisherman's suplex. Bridge, but once again BQ kicks out. In frustration, Heftel throws BQ to the outside, and then follows. The STUMP Squad members begin all out brawling on the floor, and then BQ stops the brawling. Mysteriously, he begins climbing underneath the ring... for what, nobody knows... and then Heftel follows him after BQ waves him over. The referee counts to ten, and both men are counted out... Warfare proceeds to a commercial break in confusion, as the members of the Stump Squad remain underneath the ring. Result: Double Countout Press Conference, Part II More footage from earlier in the day aired of the SummerSlaughter press conference. Result: Kings by pinfall Press Conference, Part III The footage rolls as the announcers introduce the segment; which is of course to announce who will be facing Mitch Wilson for the fWo Internet Title at SummerSlaughter. Standing at the podium is Jeremiah Frankton, who announced the first three or four matches of the card, before Finn announced the two main events of the card. Since the footage is airing from last to first in order it was recorded, the tables are still intact and everything appears copasetic, obviously this was filmed before the attack by Primetime on the Boston Strangler which aired at the top of the show. Frankton began by saying that earlier he announced the first ever 'Say Hello To Block' match, and now he would announce another wrestling first. As everyone wondered what it might be, Frankton welcomed four men into the conference room. The men who sat to the left of Frankton were Rain & Mitch Wilson, while to to the right of him now sat Primetime & Wretch. Frankton said that Wilson had been a prime example of what they want their fWo Internet Champion to be, by defending it on a regular basis and wrestling consistently. Primetime took offense to that and offered up claims that he defended it more, but Frankton digressed and continued. He said that Wretch was this close to being in the main event of SummerSlaughter, and while he wasn't, he was still going to be rewarded for his efforts in an Internet Title Match at SummerSlaughter in addition to the one he would receive tonight. Frankton shrugged his comments off, but this only incensed Primetime. Simply put, the respective blocks will be at ringside on a table, and whoever wins the match, gets to use it on their opponent first. This is truly a first." Frankton smiled proudly, before Kelly interrupted him. "Yeah, but it won't be a first when everyone witnesses 'eradication' again, as I take that cinderblock and Ice to a thousand pieces with it. I'm not going to melt you, bub. I'm going to put you in a drink, have you for breakfast, and piss you out in the afternoon like you never existed, pal." Ice stood up and looked at Kelly with rage. "You call yourself the master of eradication? I don't buy that. To be fair, we can combine those two terms. You can be the masturbator, because we all know you haven't eradicated anything as of late." Ice grinned evily, before looking over at Kelly with a sly smile. "That's at least what your bitch told me last night in the hotel room." Kelly jumped up with his fists raised, and he reached for his block. The two men nearly came to blows, and sweat began to pour from the face of Frankton. Security intervened before the two men could 'cause any damage, and Frankton slowly sighed before wiping the sweat from his forehead. "Already off to a nice start... haven't even had a question from the media yet." he said to himself as Ice & Kelly walked off opposite ends of the stage to elsewhere in the building where the press conference was being held. The footage stopped airing, before we went backstage to an incident that ironically happened to involve Ice. Crucified (VI) We quickly went backstage to a shot of the mirror with Mitch Wilson looking in it holding his head, still suffering the effects of the Rainmaker he received a few minutes ago. He tossed the fWo Internet Title aside, before walking into his nearby dressing room to see Ice hooked up to a cross. A bottle of ether and a washcloth resided near it. Wilson grabbed a chair and threw it. It hit the mirror into which he was just looking at, and glass shattered everywhere into the sink below it. Mysteriously, the tap water began running. Wilson ran over and began to turn it off, getting angry as the damn handle came off. He threw it too, before Matt Loco walked by. Wilson told Loco to call for help, and Loco got on his cell phone and did. Loco came in after doing so and saw Ice. Moments passed without a word being side between Wilson and Loco, before Wilson shouted "Where the hell is the medical help?!" Loco shrugged, and thought Wilson wanted him to call a plumber for the sink problem that Wilson had inadvertently 'caused. Reaching the boiling point, Wilson grabbed Loco by his shirt collar and slammed him into the wall. "You get on that walkie talkie and call for help. My friend Ice here is unconscious, damnit. I could care less about the freakin' water. They can bill me for all I care." Wilson set Loco down, before Loco made the appropiate call. The self-proclaimed 'Mr. Main Event' walked over and grabbed the bottle of ether, before putting the cap on. He grabbed the wash cloth and tossed it in the sink, trying not to touch any part of the damaged goods. He then began to unhook Ice from the cross, before finally help had arrived. Wilson gave a half-assed thanks to Loco, who walked out kinda grumpy, before remembering he had another call to make to Chris Finn, who wasn't at the arena tonight. "Hey Chris... Loco here... damn, that Wilson is a nutball. What? Yeah, he did more damage to the arena tonight, but the good part is he said we could send the bill to him. Shouldn't we do that in the first place? Anyways..." Strike Three? "... I haven't found them at all. I've called Sid' house, no answer. I called Machina, can't get through, it's always busy. I called Eclipse, but whoever answered said he had gone out to some all you can eat buffet and never came back. I called Rudo... but dammit, I didn't get through their either." Loco paused as he listened to Finn. Looking around some more, he slowly mouthed to some backstage workers if they had seen the Lone Gunmen at all tonight. All of them said no, with the exception of one. "I thought I saw Eclipse at the catering table eating all of Armstrong' bun cake, but it wasn't him. This guy was fatter, I think it was that... Dusty Rhodes guy or something.... I don't know." Loco shrugged. "Ah well, thanks anyways." Loco walked away from them as he listened to Finn some more. "Uh huh. Yep. Uh huh. Right. Uh huh. Yep. Right. Right. Yep. Uh huh. What's that? Yep. Uh huh. Gotcha. I'll have someone send out their Fed Ex' tomorrow." Loco hung up with Finn, before he stopped and thought to himself if this was an angle or not. But as he slowly paced out of the arena, he came to the realization it wasn't. This was real. The Lone Gunmen were for all intents and purposes... fired. The Boston Strangler (c) vs. Primetime Warfare returns to the ring for the final match of the night which was for the fWo World Championship. "Blind" began to pulsate off the Coliseum walls, before out walked the challenger to the throne tonight and one-third of the Future Foundation, Primetime. Primetime was greeted with perhaps the largest ovation of boos in his career, which had spanned nearly two years now, and that was obviously a lot of boos. Perhaps they saw the footage of him attacking The Strangler at the press conference earlier today... but Primetime didn't care. He was all about business tonight, and looked to reclaim the fWo World Title that he rightfully never lost. Climbing into the ring, he ignored the jeers from the audience with confidence written on his face. Primetime stays in a corner waiting for his opponent, the reigning fWo World Champion. "Blind" slowly fades, before the arena darkens, and "O' Fortuna" starts up. The fans rush to their feet and begin cheering, as out on the ramp steps the champion himself. His head bandaged as well as his back, he slowly walks down the ramp with the World Title in his right hand. As he approaches the ring, Primetime wastes no time in walking towards him and starting the match off on the outside. He launches himself over the top rope with a pescado, taking Strangler to the floor. The World Title crashes a few feet away, as Primetime kneels over The Boston Strangler and unloads with punches to the face of the Strangler. Clutching his head in one hand, he unrelentlessly pounds away at the face of the Massachusetts native. Primetime then stands up and walks over and grabs the fWo World Title. He clutches it, and waits for Strangler to get up. He charges and gets ready to nail Strangler in the head, but Strangler drops to one knee and nails Primetime with a lowblow. This stops Primetime in his tracks, and he drops the title to the floor. Turning around to recover from the blow, this gives time for the Strangler to take control. He grabs the belt and slides it into the ring, before walking up behind Primetime and going for the Boston Massacre. He has it locked in, but Primetime charges towards the ring steps. He moves, Strangler releases and instead Strangler hits into the ring steps. Primetime then clutches his lower regions, and shrugs off the lowblow. He grabs Strangler and whips him shoulder first into the other set of the ring steps. Strangler reverses it and instead Primetime crashes into the steel. The top set of steps goes flying off, and as the referee hands the World Title to the timekeeper, he signals for the bell, and finally the match is officially underway. Primetime brings Strangler up and hooks him for a bulldog, but Strangler holds on, lifts Primetime into the air, and drops him crotch first on the guardrail. Primetime surely isn't going to shrug off this like he did the lowblow, and his mouth hangs ajar as he currently resides on the guardrail. Strangler then grabs him and hooks him for a vertical suplex on the floor. The champion grabs Primetime by the head and trunks and rolls him into the ring. Strangler winces in pain as he holds his injured shoulder, before climbing in. He stomps away at the head of Primetime, before bringing him up and into a corner. He begins an all out assault on the challenger, with shots to the body & head. He finally brings Primetime out and whips him to the ropes, but then brings him back into a short-arm. Instead of a clothesline, he punches him in the heart, and the shot is heard throughout the Coliseum with a sickening echo. Strangler then covers him, and Primetime kicks out at two. Strangler brings Primetime up and whips him into the corner. He charges in shoulder first, Primetime moves, and Strangler slams into the post shoulder first, again furthering his injury. Primetime turns and brings Strangler out to the center of the ring and goes for a reverse neckbreaker, but Strangler counters out of it. Whatever Strangler was going for didn't work, as Primetime brought him to the mat with a lightning quick DDT. Primetime then bounced off the ropes and dropped a knee into the forehead, he then went for a cover. The champion kicked out at two, and this time Primetime hooked the leg. Again Strangler kicked out at two. Primetime sent Strangler to the ropes, and Strangler returned. PT went for a Samoan Drop, but Strangler floated over into a DDT of his own. Both men were out in the ring, exhausted from the quick pace they had been working at and the bumps both men had already sustained. The referee began a ten count, but got only to five before Strangler rolled over and laid an arm on Primetime. The referee counted, got to two, but Primetime kicked out. Both men struggled to their feet before they charged at each other with a clothesline. Both men hit it, resulting in the champion & challenger going to the mat once more. The referee began yet another ten count, this time reaching eight before Primetime struggled to his feet. He staggered around and Strangler got up. As Primetime staggered towards the champion, Strangler grabbed both legs and took him to the mat, before turning it over into a Boston Crab. He hooked it, and Primetime winced in pain for several moments. Finally reaching the ropes, Primetime escaped defeat, as he was seconds away from tapping out. Strangler rested for a moment, and as Primetime got up Strangler hooked him for a gutwrench powerbomb. One. Two. Primetime kicked out. Strangler then whipped Primetime into the ropes. He followed in, but Primetime stuck a foot up. Strangler caught it, and Primetime hopped on one foot out from the corner, before hitting an enziguiri. His foot slammed Strangler in the back of the head, and both men fell to the mat. Another ten count resulted because of this, and at seven a familiar face reached the ring. Lance Star. He hopped onto the ring apron and distracted the referee, who was incensed at this. Star and the referee jawed back and forth for several moments. The referee however was intimidated by Star who eventually grabbed him by his shirt collar and brought him several inches off the ground, obviously an easy task since he was much stronger than the man whose job was to just call the match, and not actually wrestle it. Strangler rolled over onto his back, and Primetime did the same, as Star berated the man in stripes verbally before another familiar face reached the ring. Courage. Courage charged in and went for the 'Courage Required'. He obviously intended to hit the Boston Strangler, but it didn't work out this way. Instead, he hit Primetime as The Boston Strangler rolled out of his way. Courage went out onto the floor in a fit of rage, as after he hit it Star stopped distracting the referee. Strangler then rolled over onto Primetime, and the referee position to count. One. Two. Kickout? No. Courage interfered again, as he pulled the referee out onto the floor. He and the referee exchanged verbal pleasantries, before Lance saw his opportunity. He rolled into the ring and brought Strangler up, and hit the 'Shooting Star'. He planted him into the mat with velocity, before going back onto the floor. As soon as he went out onto the floor however, he and Courage were met with an attack from the two men they would face at SummerSlaughter for the fWo World Tag Team Titles. Damnation. Hellrazor and Star squared off, while Fear and the smaller (compared to him, anyways) Courage brawled. The two teams brawled to the back, before Primetime rolled over and covered the champion. The referee counted, this time free from resistence. One. Two. No. The Boston Strangler remained the champion. But for how much longer? Primetime stood up, undaunted, but exhausted. He waited for Strangler to get up, and strategically waited behind him. As The Strangler got up, Primetime grabbed him, he then hooked him for the Backdrop Driver. The Strangler - although dazed - sensed it was coming. He elbowed out of it, which dazed Primetime enough for the Strangler to hook on the Massacre. He had it on for about fifteen seconds, but Primetime quickly countered with a mule kick. This time it was the Strangler who would receive an unpleasant shot to the little Strangler'. Strangler bent over and clutched himself, and Primetime shrugged off the effects of the Massacre. He then came over and grabbed the bandage off the Strangler' head, before throwing it into the crowd. A sea of fans reached for it, as it could've been the only time they ever had any sort of contact with the champion. Primetime then grabbed Strangler and hooked him for another Primetime Bomb. They were too close to the ropes however. Primetime struggled to bring BS away from the ropes, but Strangler held on, then backdropped him over the top rope onto the floor. Primetime crashed, and the fans gasped as it appeared The Boston Strangler was in control once again. Primetime pulled himself up on the outside after a few moments with aide of the American announcers' table. The Strangler saw Primetime getting up, and after briefly looking for his bandage, decided to take a huge risk. Strangler bounced off the ropes on the other side, before charging towards Primetime. Primetime turned and saw the Strangler coming. Suicide plancha. Strangler flew over the top rope, clearing it by a good foot and a half. Primetime had no time to move. Neither did the announcers. Primetime braced himself, and ended up being put through the table. Wood, glass, cables, microphones, glasses of water, bottles of water went flying everywhere. Jeremiah Frankton - who was calling the match - feared for his life for the first time since well... earlier today, and moved away as much as he could. Stevie Roberts did the same. The Boston Strangler and Primetime winced in pain as Primetime clutched his back, and Strangler virtually clutched his entire body. The pain was beginning to take it's toll on the champion, but it was far too early (although nearly two months along to the day) for him to quit. He got up, his body shaking somewhat, and he looked down at the challenger with sympathy and hate on his face at the same time. He reached down and grabbed him by the hair, and as the camera zoomed in on the face of the challenger, it appeared he was nearly rendered unconscious. The Strangler dragged his limp body towards the ring, and then rolled him in. Strangler climbed in himself, before locking on the Massacre. Writhing in pain, Primetime gave up twenty seconds later, and the referee retrieved the belt for the champion. Strangler slumped in the corner with an eerie half-smile on his face as he nursed his injuries... which were piling up by the moment. Primetime was still dazed in the center of the ring... the camera's turned their attention to the stage however, as the man who would face The Boston Strangler for the fWo World Championship on July 29 now stood there. Mike Bear. The camera's remained on Bear for the duration of the show as he stared at the madman from Boston, although as the camera slowly backed off of Mike Bear, the last visual remained was Mike Bear looking into the ring, with the look of the Strangler in the background on the giant Warfare screen. Appropiately, Warfare faded to black. Result: Strangler by submission to retain
