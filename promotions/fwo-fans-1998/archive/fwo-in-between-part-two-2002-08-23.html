fWo: In-between, Part Two (August 2002)

23 August 2002

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization fWo: In-between, Part Two In which we remember two old faces "So what do you think about all of it?" Ryan Moore tossed a glance to the door of the diner, shrugged and sighed. "I don't know... I got some papers from the person handling the inquiry, but I've been out of the loop for months. I'm not even sure why I'm being involved with any of it... I don't have anything to gain from all of this really, I'm only listed as part of the active roster because paperwork hasn't cleared through yet. At least the leg is getting better... but other than that..." Ryan stirred his finger in the empty cup and shifted his weight to one side of the vinyl bench so he could take the pressure off of his leg. Things had improved over the summer, but it still hurt and he was starting to suspect that he would always walk with a limp. One bad injury... at a house show no less, and here he was. He didn't blame the others, a freak accident could happen to anyone, but his scheduled storylines with Knight and Chronos had burnt out and fizzled before they had a chance to go anywhere. He hated leaving things undone, and in the last couple of years it had seemed like that was all he had to look back on. Storylines with Douglas, storylines with most of the locker room... and of course, scheduled storylines with Neo. "I don't buy it, none of it," said Neo, and worked on demolishing what was left of the bread in front of him. Ryan smiled, of all the people he had known in wrestling, Neo was most like his character. He supposed he should find this disturbing, but the thought was oddly comforting. It was nice to see that some things were consistent inside and outside of the ring, even if the consistency boiled down to an often sick and pathetically insecure man. "I mean... come on," Neo said, continuing despite the food in his mouth that was falling down his chin in wet, bready crumbs, "People like Flair and Ross are a bunch of pussies at heart, but do you really see them striking over heath benefits? What would they want with an organized union in wrestling anyway? The whole thing stinks Moore... stinks worse than what the whole wrestling world has come down to. I mean, you look around at these promotions today. Action? When in the hell did wrestling promotions stop having the word wrestling in them anyway? And don't get me started about the Asylum... that whole idea was stolen from me anyway. Who was the first person to try and introduce fighting, underground fighting with a hard attitude to wrestling? Me. I punked out that little Rottdogg bitch something good, and if the fWo would have had the stones to let me just do what I was hired to do then you would see all of those little Asylum punks on the ground licking my... hey, more beer over here! Jesus... jack offs everywhere. Christ on a god damn popsicle stick..." Ryan shrugged. Pure fighting wasn't his game... he had spent years in Orlando training to get where he was, and it seemed a shame to give up the art in favor of the brutality. But it wasn't his to argue with, none of it was, and tonight wouldn't be the night that he pointed out to Neo that his gimmick had been taken from a Brat Pitt film in the 90's. These things, as they say, happen. Life goes on. "I don't know, I'm not in the loop. But you're right, it doesn't seem as simple as that. My thoughts were that with the push to get the new television deal there just wasn't the money available to pay the talent. So Kelly goes and injures himself and somebody mentions something about a union. Okay, so the people holding the purse strings of the promotion get rid of the talent, using the differences and the straw man of a union to not negotiate or give us any warning. They bring up people from the developmental territories, which saves them a lot of money in salaries and allows them to close down the developmental camps at the same time. The country is in a recession Neo... so it all comes down to money." Neo shook his head, grabbing the beer that had been placed in front of him with a dirty, scabbed hand. "See, but that has one big hole in it Moore... most of the fWo roster would have done it for free. If money was such an issue, most of them would have taken the pay cuts. And from what I heard the TV deal was done for a song. That was bringing cash into the promotion, not out. Aw, fuck it anyway..." Neo drank the beer and looked back at Moore with unsteady eyes. "One punch thrown at the network guys and I'm out on my ass without so much as severance. All before I got to bag that foreign bitch on TV. The last memory people have of me is of some happy ass pussy. I left being your old character. Fucking Neo Joy... I can't believe my career ends like that." "There are worse things," said Ryan, and his eyes looked distant, "so just be glad it ended how it did. There are worse ways to leave this business. Look... it's getting late. I've got to go, catch a bus and get this leg into some water. A torn quad isn't exactly the way I was hoping to spend this year, but you deal with the cards you're given. I'll let you know if I hear anything, okay?" Neo waved him off, and watched Ryan hobble down the aisle and out of the door. He sighed, and it was a deep sigh that spoke volumes. He was miserable... and somewhere deep down he understood that his misery was brought about by himself. He didn't know why he did the things that he did... when he was in front of people he just acted. Just reacted. And here he was. If it was all true, if the old roster was standing up to management and forcing them to give them the benefits that they deserved, well, he should be there. They wouldn't have him, but he would want to be there for them. They never liked him in the end, never really cared and why should they? It wasn't like he was exactly opening up the door for their kindness and respect. His attitude was all he had. Why didn't people just realize that... but then again, why should they? Neo tossed crumpled bills on the table and stood up with a wobble and a weariness that reminded him he was getting older. He walked unsteadily toward the door and out into the night air, stopping once he was in the parking lot to lean up against a car that wasn't his and light a cigarette. The smoke filled his lungs and he coughed, the air and smoke combining with an acrid thumping in his chest. God, he was getting too old. Too old for all of this. He dropped the cigarette and it rolled into a puddle, the thin flame going out in a wet rush. Cursing, he reached into his pocket for another. His hand found a piece of paper instead, and he pulled it out and tossed it on the ground as he continued his search. He had lit the second cigarette and had taken several drags before his eyes wandered down to the paper he had discarded. A stock statement, Neo sighed and bent over to pick it up. The market wasn't exactly doing rage business these days, and his modest collection of FWOE shares weren't going to put him on easy street any time soon. Glancing at the numbers with a sigh, his brown wrinkled as he noticed a line about controlling interest and a recent buy back of shares. Strange... but who would invest so heavily now with business so down? The thought brought memory, memories from last winter and someone talking about it, someone talking about what enough money could do... "Hey... got an extra smoke?" Neo turned and looked with bleary eyes at the boy in the flannel shirt and cap standing next to him, slumped in position and with a tired expression on his face. Christ but he was getting old... he didn't hear him coming. Neo shoved the stock summary in his pocket and sniffed. "Piss off boy, I'm busy out... I... oh, screw it. Here, take the pack kid." Neo tossed the half-empty pack of cigarettes at the boy who caught it with one hand and nodded his thanks. Then he took the other hand out of his pocket and brought up the gun. "Now your wallet old man. Hand it over." Neo looked at the gun with dawning realization that slowly ebbed into anger. This, of course, was why he acted the way he did. The people around him were assholes... you don't make friends with them. The whole god damn world was like this, you give and you give and you give... and they take. Every chance they get, they take. "Listen kid, you better just back the fuck up right now before you enter into a whole world of pain. Don't you know who the fuck I am? Don't you know?" Neo took a staggering step toward the boy, and the trigger was pulled. Neo stopped, stumbled, and looked down at himself in surprise. He stumbled, his hands clutching at the charred and smoking hole in his chest. Blood ran over his hands, and he remembered the feel, remembered the touch of his own life. He fell, his face in the water but it did not feel cold. The boy reached down and pulled Neo's wallet from his pants. "I don't give a shit who you are old man... nobody does." Tucking the gun into his belt, he fled into the night as patrons of the dinner slowly came out to investigate the noise. Neo's body was warm all over, and he smiled. They had lied to him after all... he always was told that death felt cold.
