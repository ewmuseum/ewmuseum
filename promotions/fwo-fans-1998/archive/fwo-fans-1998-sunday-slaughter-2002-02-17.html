Introduction As Douglas received a duffle bag off of the private jet, he turned and came face-to-face with the man who owned the jet. Chris Universal. Universal and Douglas stared at each other, but so far their intense confrontation had been very silent. Until Universal finally spoke. "Steve... thank you for being here." Douglas remained emotionless. He looked down at the concrete, but his head shot back up as Universal continued. Universal sighed deeply. "We are taking a very big risk by having you here tonight, and I must warn you. I know you have a lot of emotion, a lot of rage, just bottled up inside you. Please... don't let that deter you. We want to bring back the Hardcore Title next week at Body Count.. and we'd like you to be involved in that somehow. But take heed to this bit of advice: I need you on your best behavior. Otherwise... it's back to the treatment center for you, Steve. You will leave me with no choice." Douglas nodded, before looking back down at the concrete and walking over to a taxicab. Universal sighed again... this time one of relief. He had gotten out what he had to say and Douglas understood, thankfully. But somehow... something told Universal that things weren't always as they appeared. Omega/Alpha The rambunctious cheering of the Miami crowd was interrupted by a low whistle, signaling a veritable firestorm of purple and white pyro. Sunday Night Slaughter was on the air once again, and it was the last one before Body Count 2002 that upcoming Saturday. As the last of the pyro died down, a guitar riff sounded off from the PA system and the crowd erupted with whistles and cheers. They would not last. As the opening of "Defy You" played, Ruben Ross appeared on the rampway, and the once enormous cheers petered out. He was dressed in black warmup pants and an alternate Calgary Flames jersey with his name on the back, the fWo World Title hanging loosely in his right hand touching the ramp. He was wearing a neck brace as a result of what had happened to him last week, and his eyes were dull. There was none of the usual playing to the crowd or mock belt shining--instead, he marched down the ramp and rolled in underneath the bottom rope nearly beating the green explosion of pyro on the stage. He grabbed a mic from Matt Panzer and made a quick motion with his hands to kill the music. "I'm sure you've all heard the rumors that I had something important to say tonight, and rather than drag this thing out...I'm going to get straight to it. Due to the neck trauma and severly bruised vertebrae I suffered at the hands of Austin Davis last week, fWo doctors have recommended I not wrestle for two months. As such, I am left with no other decision..." His eyes shut, and he tried fiercer than he ever had in his life not to cry. "...than to forfeit the fWo World Heavyweight Championship." The crowd gasped audibly before erupting in a chrous of boos. His voice straining him, he continued on. "I...I want Chris Universal out here to claim the title. He'll probably put it up in the Body Count battle royal or something. Don't...don't worry..." A tear rolled down his cheek. "I'll be all right. I-I'll be back for this in due time...I..." He kicked the bottom ring rope repeatedly, clearly mouthing obscenities. "I'll be all right. Chris, come on down." A few seconds passed. Ross looked down at his feet. A drum beat played and the crowd erupted in boos. "Can you feel that?....Ah, shit..." The cachophonous guitar of Disturbed's "Down With The Sickness" could mean only one man: Austin Davis. Paper cups flew at his head and he smiled. Ross didn't look up, and very slowly shook his head. Davis continued around ringside, threatening fans as well as Jeremiah Frankton, before snatching a mic of his own and rolling in the ring next to Ross. "Look what happened to you." He smiled--it looked like he had fangs--and the crowd was torn between booing and hoping Ross would respond with something. "Looks like you've got some sort of neck problem there. A damn shame about your birthday. The man at the store told me that rope was one size fits all." "I told you what I was going to do, then I went out and did it. What a glorious night it was. I got to your pretty girlfriend...and then I got to you." He paused and laughed right at Ross, who still wasn't meeting him. "Look at me!" Ross looked up, as the look in his eyes showed absolute helplessness and despair. "I am the Master Of Puppets. I pulled your strings just like everyone else's. You may have been the easiest mark of my career. What is a woman for, except as a crutch to your own fraility? Why did I make you bleed, cut you open, stab your neck, and choke the life out of you?" "It's because I could. Did you think I forgot December 30th? Not by a long shot. I swore revenge on you, and I got it every way I could. It's almost a shame what happened--after putting your girlfriend through the worst 30 minutes of your life, I was prepared to give you a half hour of hell at Body Count. It would've been glorious, all these bottom feeders of the middle forced to watch their new hero destroyed at my hands in an ironman match. But you were just too damn frail for it even before I attacked you. All talk and no action. Since that plan got subverted, now there's only one thing left to do. The one thing I came back for--give me the title." Ross just looked catatonic, and Davis leaned in face-to-face. "I can make you another Lance Star real quick. And even if I don't get it right now, I'll get it Saturday. I've done it before. Hell, I did it first." A chant of "Fuck you, Davis!" built up and suddenly engulfed the front rows of the American Airlines Arena. "No, not me. Your heroes. Your gods. They all fall at my hands. Either they see things my way or I break their necks. Just like this one. Give me the title, Ruben. Don't make me end your days in the ring for good." The obscene chant got larger, until the foot stomps in the rafters down to the BQ-mask wearers in the front were all seemingly chanting this as one. Beaven. FTfWo. The Strangler. Now Ross. Who was more deserving of this than Davis in the history of the fWo--or professional wrestling, period? "SHUT THE HELL UP, YOU IMBECILES! YOU'RE JUST AS SOFT AS HE IS! SHUT UP!" Davis turned towards the loudest hecklers to his left and screamed at them. Ross pursed his lips, and opened them towards the mic. "THAT'S IT! HE PAYS!" Davis turned to hit Ross with a right hand. Ross opened his mouth and a wide spray of green flew out, catching a surprised Davis right in the face. Groaning and clutching at his face, Davis fell down, a sudden and unexpected victim of green mist. From the depths of the jersey, Ross pulled out a baton, and pushed a button to double it in length. His world was white. He knew nothing but hatred. He could hear the crowd chanting something at him but it was all just one dull noise in comparison to the one thought echoing in his head: END HIM. Davis grabbed the ropes to get up and with a roar, Ross clocked him in the neck with the baton. Davis coughed up some blood and hit the mat once again, and Ross stood on his head and with his hands continued swinging at the neck of Austin Davis. WHAM. WHAM. WHAM. WHAM. WHAM. Davis slipped from his boot, and he immediately rolled towards the floor by the entrance ramp. "YOU WANT 30 MINUTES, YOU GOT THEM!" yelled Ross. The crowd popped. "And I'll tell you another thing you limp-wristed, no-dicked, Strangler-fellating douchebag, you better finish the job this time! The same way I'm going to start doing it tonight... against your boyfriend the Strangler!" He ripped off his neck brace and threw it at Davis, who spat at him in return. "Tonight...you pay." Davis flipped him the bird with a smirk. "Prepare to die, bitch." Ross slammed the mic into the mat, and "Defy You" hit once again. In their heart of hearts, they smiled. Davis knew he had his head. He had him emotionally involved and upset, and that fourth title reign was as good as his. Ross' heart was pounding in his head, and he was filled with a bloodlust that consumed his entire being. All he heard was the same thing over and over: END HIM. END HIM. END HIM. END HIM... A Poem Having already gotten dressed and ready to go for her tag match tonight, Tempest sits alone in her locker room, pondering what lies ahead of her. The strange gifts she has received the last couple weeks were making her think quite a bit. On the one hand, it was certainly nice that someone would care enough about her to do that. However, it also felt a little creepy to know that someone was following her that closely that the person knew she needed new gloves. Whoever it was, they certainly didn't seem in any hurry to reveal their identity. As Tempest grabbed a water from out of her bag, she heard a knock on the door. "Who is it?" she asked. "My name's Carl, and I'm delivering a package here for a...Tempest?" said the voice from the other side of the door. Tempest set the bottle back down and walked over to open the door. She grabbed the box and signed the man's paper, thanking him for the delivery. As the young man walked away, Tempest look curiously at the package before sitting down to open. Inside, she found a very nice looking tin, which she opened to find a plethora of sugar cookies. With a grin on her face, Tempest looked over the tin for a few moments before noticing a slip of paper still inside the box that the package came in. She reached over and unfolded the paper to find a poem written inside. "Dearest Tempest, Your lips speak soft sweetness, Your touch a cool caress; I am lost in your magic, My heart beats within your chest. I think of you each morning And dream of you each night; I think of your arms being around me And cannot express my delight. Never have I fallen, But I am quickly on my way, You hold a heart in your hands, That was never before given away. I have taken the pleasure to hide away, Hoping my love you would be; But Saturday is finally the day, Where my face you shall see." Tempest scans back over the poem several times, taking in the words of the poem one by one. After a few moments, a nervous smile comes across her face, as she sets the poem to the side and stares off into the distance. Violence, Truth & Videotape As the champagne-colored Dodge Stratus pulled into the talent parking lot at the arena, an excited Lance Knight stepped out of the driver's side door and proceeded into the arena. He shrugged his black bag over his shoulder and took in the aura of the arena like it was the first time he had ever been backstage. And in a way, it was. Tonight, everything else was secondary - all the backstage encounters, getting blindsided with a chair, the hell that was jOlt - all that fell to the wayside. Tonight would be Lance's first match in the fWo, and he couldn't have been happier about it. This was his big chance to prove that he could make it in the United States, and he wasn't about to let himself, or Chris Universal, down. He made his way down the hallway to his modest locker room, nodding at the backstage workers and the odd wrestler that he passed. He opened the door to his locker room and deposited his bag inside, failing to notice the small package that lay on the table on his initial inspection. Confident that everything was in order, Knight turned, taking care to shut the door behind him, and he made his way down the hall to where the match assignment sheet was posted. At first glance, he didn't see his name on the list, but upon closer examination, Lance found his name in the second column of the sheet. Tracing a line to the first column with his index finger, Knight was nearly shaken out of his boots. He stepped backwards, shaking his head, and then moved in close to the sheet again, hoping to clarify that he had indeed read the sheet wrong. But no, there it was again, his opponent's name in plain black type. Austin Davis. On one hand, Knight thought, this would be an opportunity to get revenge on Davis, since he was almost certain that it had been the former Dead who had slammed him with the chair on last week's Slaughter. On the other hand, he had recovered from the attack in time to make it to a monitor and see Davis hanging a bloody Ross over the top rope. Could he really be facing that psychopath this week? Well, apparently, he was, but was he ready for it? He, Lance Knight, was still arguably the greenest competitor in the fWo, and Davis was a former World Champion, which, coupled with him being one of the most demented men Knight had ever had the privilege of encountering, made for some doubts in the newcomer's mind. Still more or less undeterred, Lance made his way back down the hall and into his locker room, intent on preparing for this match to his fullest ability. If he was going to have a shot at putting Davis away tonight, he'd have to be on the top of his game and extremely focused - the slightest mistake was all Davis would need, and in his current mindset, there was no guarantee that he wouldn't end Knight's young career without a second thought. Knight moved his bag from inside the doorway, hoisting it up and setting it on the room's only table. As he set the bag down, he finally noticed the small package, which was wrapped in newspaper and had a Post-It note attached to it. Seeing the name at the bottom of the note, Knight smiled, a look of surprise overtaking his face. He read the small and slightly choppy handwritten note quietly to himself. "Lance, Watch yourself; I'd hate if you were next. Good luck, Ruben Ross" Knight smiled again and removed the newspaper, taking care not to rip it, in spite of it not being "real" wrapping paper. It was the thought that counted, and Lance was overjoyed that Ross had taken the time to get him something, anything, in light of how trivial Lance himself considered his actions last week. It was nice to know that not everyone in the fWo was hell-bent on terrorizing the newest additions to the company, as it seemed the triad of KVC, the Boston Strangler, and Austin Davis himself were. As the paper came off, Knight found himself perplexed - it was a VHS videocassette, but there was no label on it, nor any writing on the sleeve that surrounded the tape itself. With a shrug, Knight retreated into the hall, carrying the cassette and the small note with him, and he made his way quickly to an area of the backstage where several monitors and other electronic equipment were set up. Approaching one of the several technicians who were busily readying the area for the evening's activities, Knight asked the portly young man if he could view the tape on one of the monitors. "Sure," the young tech replied, "Just let me finish connecting this VCR to the monitor, and you'll be ready to go." Knight stood, watching intently, as the technician disappeared around the stack of audio/video components in front of him, only to emerge moments later and favor Lance with a thumbs-up. Knight passed the tech the cassette, and the young man slid the tape into the VCR that he had just connected. Much to Knight's surprise, the contents of the tape weren't nearly as pleasant as he had originally expected. Contained on the tape was a loop of the final moments of last week's Slaughter, with the repeated image of Austin Davis hanging Ruben Ross over the top rope. Stunned, Knight stepped backwards, as the sound of the booing crowd faded out and was replaced with two softly whispered words: "Trust me." The mortified technician quickly ejected the tape from the VCR and handed it back to Knight, and as the Pittsburgh native slid the tape back into its jacket, he noticed something inside the sleeve - another yellow Post-It note, not unlike the one he carried in his hand. Taking care not to disturb the note, Knight slid the tape back out of the cover and then carefully extricated the note from its hiding place. With a look of horror, he read the note, which was composed with the exact same hand writing as the original that had adorned the outer wrapping of the cassette. "Lance, Watch yourself; you're next. Trust me, Austin Davis" By comparing the two notes, Knight realized what Austin Davis had done - he had copied the original note in exactly the same position on the second piece of the Post-It pad, choosing to omit several of the letters from the original message in order to send an ominous warning to the budding fWo star. Shaken, Knight dropped both notes to the floor and slowly made his way back to his locker room, the somewhat aimless quality of his walk serving to indicate the daze he was currently in.
