The moment in which they were going to see a free show, some had been flown from all over the world just to come and view what was to happen. The people poured in through the doorways being checked up and down by the security that hounded the outside walls of the arena. Security was everywhere. People wearing even the smallest chain had to remove the object from their body as it was considered a weapon. They were taking no chances and it was the orders of Silver Fox to make sure that everything here was kept in order. Minimal security was on the inside, most of them surrounded the outside of the building and all of the exits just in case Angel Dalton was to show his face. The fans filled the arena by now and no longer did anyone stand outside, aside from the security. The loud roar of people echoed throughout the arena and slowly the lights came to fall into a deep darkness. The sound of jingle bells began to play and the kids grew loud with excitement, the adults would soon join in as an explosion of red and green fireworks let off on the entranceway ramp. Explosions went off all around the arena in specific areas away from the audience and only there for effect to amaze them. Fake snow fell from the ceiling and a final explosion went off as "The Gloaming" by Radiohead began to play. Every single person that was sitting now stood, and they screamed, oh boy did they scream. They screamed for a man who had given them hope, for a man that had flown them hear to help them, to give them the ultimate Christmas present. Little did they know, his intentions to kill them all. Steve Douglas appeared at the entranceway ramp and he held his arms high, they screamed louder. He seemed to almost run down the ramp way smiling gleefully as he did (what would be considered a smile through his hard face). He slapped high fives as he went and the children held their hands out in hopes that he would touch them. He slid in the ring and grabbed a mic that was tossed to him, his booming voice rang the ears of all who listened, "MERRY CHRISTMAS!!!" The audience echo his response back... MERRY CHRISTMAS!! Steve motioned for them to settle down and the audience eased up a bit, "Okay listen up folks, we have a great show for you tonight, filled with non-stop action, and excitement. Steve almost felt like he wanted to hear them forever rather than silence them all. But he knew what he had to do, it was to end tonight. Steve dropped the mic and walked to the back room, they touched his back as he went and he held back his desires to rip every single one of their faces off. He was going to let them all burn. With that, the show began. A Christmas Gift For Dexter. Dexter leaned back in his dim video den to straighten his back. It had been craned in a hunchback position for quite a while, and he felt it would be best to stand up and stretch. His stretching was very amusing in appearance; although his body moved, his eyes never deviated from the TV screen. Amidst a random pile of VHS tapes, the TV flashed imagery of Jeremy Savage in action. Although he hated the man deeply, Dexter couldn't help but emit a sigh of boredom. He looked across the room and saw light peek through the vents at the bottom of the door. He wanted so badly to walk into that light on the other side, but his mind was far too dedicated his goal: To defeat Jeremy Savage and hopefully rekindle what he had lost with Shirley. His ears perked up as a noise outside the room startled him. It was a hollow, clapping sound. The light stopped pouring in from the door vents. Pratt straightened up and turned on a light, squinting every so often to adapt to it. He opened the door, and was surprised with what he saw. There was a waist-high box waiting for him. The Christmas present was wrapped in green, with a shiny red bow on top. Dexter smirked cautiously as he lifted the folded paper that was taped to the top. He opened up the sheet of paper, and noticed the likeness of Douglas in the top corner, accompanied by a menacing text logo. The stationary was torn and slightly stained, and the writing looked like it was scrawled in haste. Pratt frowned as he read the message: Since it's almost Christmas, we thought it would be nice to get you a little something. This probably comes as quite a surprise to you, but we hope that you get some good use out of 'it'. We'd hate for you to be alone for the holidays... Dexter frowned as he looked down at the large box, then back to the stationary paper. His eyes moved to the bottom of the page so he could see who it was from. Merry Christmas, The Savages Pratt received this with a mixed reaction. At first he was angry, but at second, he felt curious. This curiosity led him to tear into the paper and the box. He lifted the lid, and stood in repulsion as he saw what was inside. He quickly regretted ever opening the damn thing. He shook his head as he lifted the object. The fans were slightly puzzled as it emerged, but they began to laugh as the entire object was finally revealed. What was the object in question, exactly? It was a fully inflated blow-up doll. Pratt's face flushed to a light shade of pink as he listened to the howling laughter coming from inside the arena. All eyes were glued on him and his plastic companion, whether they were in the immediate vicinity or they were watching through the TravisTron. His embarassment soon turned to anger as he spiked the doll into the ground. "Oooh Yeah!" the doll shrieked in ecstacy as it collided with the floor. This sent the audience into hysterics; Pratt could feel the ground tremble beneath his feet. He angrily crumpled the note into a ball and tossed it at the camera that was eagerly catching his reaction. Pratt was ill with rage. This gift...this taunt...was completely inappropriate. He turned and shoved the door open, only to slam it behind him once inside. He needed to escape the humiliation, and return to his vigorous study of Jeremy's wrestling style. The 'present' was nothing more than a motivational kick he desperately needed. He quietly vowed victory as he popped another tape in the VCR, and pressed play. In a short amount of time, Nick Brandish had displayed a frightening streak of sadism and savagery. Wrestler and fan alike were all potential victims for the monster. It was all a matter of his mood, his desire at any given moment, as to who would be added to his body count. The Freakish mass of humanity stood in the ring and watched the curtain like a shark waiting for the hapless swimmer to paddle out just a little too far. Evanescence's "Bring Me to Life" brought out what seemed to be the sacrificial lamb being led to the slaughter. Only this lamb was looking to do some slaughtering of his own. Through Pain we find Redemption CrucifiX While Diablo had almost cost Crucifix his life, Nick Brandish had done significant damage in a short span of time. Through Nick's vicious actions, Crucifix had lost the woman he loved. He'd also literally lost a pound of flesh, devoured by the cannibalistic Grim Reaper. The advantage Crucifix had coming into this match was clear. He had nothing else to lose, and everything to gain. Brandish stared at his smaller opponent. Scars, burns, and tattoos covered his gigantic body. He looked every inch the ferocious ogre, looking to grind Cruci's bones into dust. Crucifix stared back at the man twice his size and didn't appear to be intimidated. All the big man could do was bring on the pain, and pain was one thing Crucifix embraced rather than feared. The referee signaled for the bell, and the match was on. Crucifix charged across the ring and jumped into the air, catching the big man off-guard with a spinning roundhouse kick. A stiff kick to the stomach followed up by an axe kick to Brandish's thick neck doubled Brandish over. Crucifix grabbed Nick in a front-facelock and dropped back for a DDT. Nick didn't fall. Crucifix dangled in the air, hanging around Brandish's head like a tick on a bull. The Grim Reaper bellowed as he charged into the turnbuckle. Crucifix could only brace himself as he was sandwiched between an unforgiving turnbuckle and four hundred pounds of rampaging animal. The crowd cringed at the sound of the impact. The turnbuckle groaned, the ringpost shifted slightly, and the entire ring seemed to move. When Nick straightened up, the fans almost expected to see Crucifix fall to the mat in two pieces. Nick stood over the crumpled form of the Angel of Agony. He told the referee that the match was obviously over and that the official should simply raise his hand in victory. The ref waved him off and knelt down beside Crucifix to see if he could continue. His mouth was foaming but he nodded that he was still ready to rumble. The referee informed Brandish that the match wasn't over. Nick shoved him out of the way and pulled Crucifix up by the hair. He chopped Crucifix across the chest and drove the smaller man into the turnbuckle. With hooking forearm blows, Nick Brandish assaulted Crucifix in the corner. Crucifix raised his arms to protect his head, but Brandish simply grabbed his forearms and pulled them down with one hand while punching him with the other. The referee started a five count after warning Brandish about the closed fists. Nick counted along with the ref, stepping away from Crucifix just before he reached five. He moved in again but Crucifix retaliated with a closed fist of his own. Nick punched; Crucifix blocked it and punched back. Punch, block and counter punch. The tide seemed to be turning. Crucifix slowly backed the big man out of the corner and into the center of the ring. Suddenly Nick just stopped backing up. The abrupt stop made Crucifix overcompensate and instead of catching Nick in the head, he punched him in the throat. Nick gagged. Crucifix smiled. "Time to bring on the Pain," Crucifix whispered. "Ref look out!" Crucifix yelled and pointed outside the ring. The ref spun and shielded himself with his arms. Even Brandish turned while holding his throat. Crucifix took advantage of the distraction to kick Nick firmly in the balls. When a man can shatter a cinder block with his foot, a kick to the balls is guaranteed to get someone's attention. No matter how much said testes were atrophied due to substance abuse. Finally Crucifix had managed to bring the big man down to the mat. By the time the referee realized that he wasn't in trouble, Crucifix already had a Fujiwara armbar clamped on Nick Brandish. The referee looked at Crucifix. "My mistake," he said as he leaned back on the hold. Nick didn't cry out. He grunted and rolled from side to side, slowly inching his way towards the ropes. Crucifix didn't wait for the break to come. He let go of the arm, rolled to his feet, and kicked Brandish in the ribs. As Nick dropped his arm instinctively, Crucifix struck him in the head. "That was just a taste," he said as Brandish rolled against the ropes. Blood trickled from his Nick's ear. He frowned. Crucifix smiled again. "Let's get crazy. Let's get nuts." He's the most successful of Santa's little elves." Nick looked up at him. "Shut up and fight!" he barked as he dove at the Hardcore Luchador. Crucifix was taken off-guard by the big man's deceptive speed. Brandish pulled him down and pinned him under his body. He pressed his forearm across Crucifix's neck and hammered away at his body with short, powerful jabs. The referee warned Brandish about the choke, so Nick pulled Crucifix up and bodyslammed him. He dropped a knee into Crucifix's chest for good measure. It took a minute for Crucifix to catch his breath. The referee intercepted him before he could get to Crucifix. Nick stared at him and raised the chair. He was going to swing the steel, and he really didn't care who was in the way when he did it. Crucifix moved quickly while Brandish was distracted. He ran towards the big man and stepped on his bent leg to reach that big skull of his with a modified Shining Wizard. Brandish staggered to the side and fell like a mighty redwood. Unfortunately, the referee was there to break his fall. The referee was motionless as Nick started to get up with the chair in his hand. He closed his eyes, and for once his mind was dark and silent, unknowing and clear. His hair was roughly cut and his coat pulled high around his neck and face. They were looking for him now, he was certain of it. The train sped east toward New York City, and while Neo was not certain exactly why he was going there he knew that there was no other destination for him. It was all coming down to this one night and one moment, he was sure of it. All else was wind. The sleeping compartment was empty save Neo himself. His bag lay on the ground, the paper-thin walls seperating the compartments brushing and rustling against the movement of the train. Taking the train was a slow method of transportation, but the airlines were out of the question. For one, he was a wanted man now. For another, his bag. The metal in the bag rattled like everything else; knives clattered against short steel pipes. His eyes were closed, and the dim memory of the path that had brought him here played softly through his mind. "Neo." The voice came from behind the wall, from the sleeping compartment next to his. Neo did not open his eyes, and while he was surprised at the presence of the man he was not surprised by who it belonged to. "Fallen."
