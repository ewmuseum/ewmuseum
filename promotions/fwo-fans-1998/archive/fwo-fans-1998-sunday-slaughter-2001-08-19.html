Introduction As the clouds rolled through Cleveland, the Gund Arena was primed for Slaughter. The fans were looking forward to a big pre-Bloodbath show from the fWo. And Ruben Ross was looking for revenge. He got out of his rented Altima with torn black jeans and wearing an Indians jersey that had a #20 with share over it in bright red letters. Kristen Jacobs saw him, and quickly ran over to the car. Ross was always good for a soundbite or seven. "Ruben, Ruben, Kristen Jacobs!" "I recognize your voice, you know." Ross stated, pulling a small suitcase out from the trunk. "Last week, Vic Creed pummeled you mercilessly and in response you challenged him to a chain match Saturday at Bloodbath. I think the obvious question to ask is: has losing the Internet Title made you crazy?!" She said it not just as a reporter, but as a friend. Still, Ross' back stiffened against the weight of the question and he exhaled. "Crazy's such a subjective term, Kris." He still didn't look at the camera, his head titled towards the entrance and the night sky. "Some say this business is crazy. Some people say that being my size and going up against a behemoth like Creed is crazy. I'd be lying if I said I didn't miss being Internet champion, but enough to go crazy? ME? Nahhhhhh. Now this " Ross whirled around and looked Kristen dead in the eye. He slowly took off his band-aid, revealing a dark black scar that nearly ran the entirety of his forehead. Kristen looked appalled. The camera focused on the scar and panned out. " Looking For Trouble, I Tempest walked along the hallways of the Gund Arena, peering down each hallway as she passed. She seemed sad and preoccupied and in that state, completely ignored her surroundings and ran right into Ric Chronos. She looked up in surprise and then shook her head. "Ric... hi.. um, sorry. 'Scuse me... " She tried to scoot around him, but he quickly stepped to the side, barring the path, and making escape a much less viable option. What appeared to be a genuine mask of concern etched across his normally stony face. "Tempest... are you OK? You look a little... troubled." She sighed and rolled her eyes ever so slightly, but thought that just maybe he could actually help for a change. "Well, I'm looking for Trouble." A sly grin crossed Ric's face, as he pressed closer to Tempest. "Well, then, you're in the right place." Her eyelids fluttered in irritation. "I meant, my dog Trouble. Insane Wonder kidnapped him and I'm worried sick." Ric paused a moment, obviously taken aback. "Wonder? Kidnapped Trouble? Wow, that's more effort than I'd ever have credited him for. So... uh... do you want some help... looking for Trouble... er... the dog, that is?" Tempest looked at him doubtfully. "You haven't heard any odd noises from behind any closed doors around here have you? Like panting or anything?" "So, you DID get my card!" She smacked her forehead. "Yes, I got that perfectly revolting card, but I'm talking about hearing any doggy noises. Wonder could have stashed him anywhere." "Revolting? "Er... outside? I'll be right there!" Tempest waved and trotted off, snickering to herself as she rounded the corner. Adam Manson vs. Vengence Slaughter heads to the ring for the first time of the night with fireworks, pyro, and a bunch of explosions. "The Beast" signals the entrance of Vengence, equipped with a chain. As the leader of Eradication Inc. climbs into the ring, his music is replaced by the sounds of Six Feet Under. Adam Manson comes out onto the entrance ramp with his trademark razor-wire baseball bat and rushes into the ring. He is viciously whipped by Vengence's chain as he slides in, but stops the attack by striking Vengence's leg with the bat. While Vengence drops to one knee, Manson makes his way to his feet and delivers a hard swing into the back of Vengence's head. Manson goes for the early cover, but Vengence is able to power out at 2. Manson heads to the outside, throws down his bat and begins pulling tables out from under the ring. He slides them inside, and begins setting one up near the ropes. Vengence gets back to his feet bleeding from the back of his head, and is met by a series of punches from Manson. Manson puts Vengence in an arm-wringer and climbs the turnbuckle. He begins to walk the ropes, seeking to hit the Rope-Walk DDT through the table, but the larger Vengence yanks him off and catches his neck with his left hand. Vengence chokeslams Manson through the table and covers, but Manson is able to kick out at two. Not hesitating, Vengence lifts Manson up in a gorilla press and throws Manson to the floor. Following him out, Vengence grabs a chair from the timekeeper. Manson has found his razor-wire bat, and the two face each other in a standoff. Manson attacks first, but Vengence blocks the bat with his chair and kicks Manson in the side. He follows with a chairshot to the back of the head, sending Manson sprawling to the ground. Vengence props the chair up and seats Manson, and picks up the bat. Climbing the apron, Vengence leaps off and smashes Manson's face with the bat, knocking him off the chair. Vengence covers, but somehow Manson is able to kick out. Angered, Vengence tosses Manson into the ring before coming in himself with the chair. When the groggy Manson makes it to his feet, Vengence tosses Manson the chair and charges forward with a clothesline. Manson catches the chair, but ducks the lariat and nails Vengence with a stiff shot to the head. With Vengence stunned, Manson ascends the turnbuckle, and leaps off with a Destiny Hammer, using his knee to drive the chair into the back of his Vengence's head. With the Eradication Inc. leader down, Manson begins setting the other table, placing it near the corner. Picking up a shard of broken table remaining in the ring, the Sanity Assassin produces a match from his boot and lights a makeshift torch. With a sadistic smile, it appears that Manson is looking to repeat his SummerSlaughter performance. In the meantime, Hellrazor has rushed down the entrance ramp with a bottle of Heineken. He slides in the ring, takes a swig, turns Manson around and spits the beer through the flame, fireballing in Manson's face. Throwing the doused torch down, Manson falls to his knees clutching his face and Hellrazor shatters the bottle over his head. As Manson falls to the mat, Hellrazor turns to find a recovered Vengence, wielding the chair over his head. Razor kicks him in the stomach, causing the 6'10" monster to drop the chair onto the mat. Hellrazor drops Vengence with a DDT onto the steel chair, and stomps him a few times for good measure. Having made his point, Hellrazor leaves the ring, kicking Manson once more on his way out. As the referee begins his 10-count, both Manson and Vengence start to move. Vengence is the first man up, and waits in position for the Stargazer. When Manson finally reaches his feet, Vengence lunges forward with the superkick. Manson barely dodges the move, then turns Vengence around and applies a clawhold. Manson lifts the larger man up and spikes him to the mat with Exhale The Vile. The referee makes the three-count and Manson picks up the victory.
