Sunday Slaughter, 2 September 2001

2 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Slaughter opened up inside the Palace of Auburn Hills with fireworks exploding, strobe lights flashing, and the crowd going generally out of their gourds. The opening video package played, but it was cut short by a theme fWo fans knew all too well. "Christian Says" by Tones on Tail. The fans erupted in boos as 3vill paraded out from backstage, dripping with gold and arrogance. They strutted their way to the ring, Primetime taking occasional time out to jaw with and taunt fans. Finally, 3vil made their way into the ring itself. Fireworks exploded behind them, and Ric Chronos gestured for a microphone. One was thrown his way, and Chronos favored the crowd with a smirk. "Welcome... to the first show... of the 3vil regime!" The crowd booed long and loud, but Chronos just shrugged it off. "That's right boys and girls, we've got the book! It's all good for 3vil right now... we've got the belts, we've got the book, that Borst wacko is long gone, our enemies are cowering at our feet... right now, at this moment, we've accomplished what we set out to do we rule the fWo!" More boos. "That's right, I know you all hate us, and that's just fine, because we hate you all right back. Now, to business. As you all know, I am a student of wrestling history, the fWo's specifically. The fWo has a long history of hated people in power... from the Dark Riders to the FtfWo, it's happened many a time. And invariably, one of the first things they'd do was to use their power to run the fWo their way. Our power is the book... so that's right boys and girls, sit back and enjoy, because you're about to experience a grand fWo tradition once more the heels booking!" Still more boos from the crowd, who were incensed by Chronos. "Now, to book the first match... I give you, Primetime!" Chronos handed the mic over to Primetime, who sneered at the crowd. "Listen up, you dumb sons of bitches, and pay some attention, because we're only gonna say this once! First off, I get the honor of booking the first match. As all you morons know, Frenchie and I won the Tag Team Titles at Bloodbath. This marks my fifth reign as tag champ, the most of any active competitor and really, what does the inactive guy matter anyway, considering he's... ugh... Wretch? The man I passed for first place is a man you all know... the Hardcore Champion, Hellrazor. What better way to celebrate passing Hellrazor than watching him get his ass kicked? That's why tonight he'll defend the Hardcore Title against Insane Wonder AND Adam Manson!" The crowd booed, and Primetime smiled, knowing he had just booked Hellrazor against two close friends who would surely work together against him. "Pierre?" Primetime passed the mic along to Frenchie, who beamed. "As I'm sure you all recall, I have held ze book before. A long time ago, I booked ze greatest Warfare in fWo history. Sure, zat putain Slugger was zere, but hey, every rose has it's t'orn, non? Now, ze book has finally returned to my hands, and damn it feels good. But at ze same time, it makes me a bit nostalgic. In zose days, I was fresh off my domination of ze Cruiserweight division and primed to show ze world my true greatness. In a way, I long for zose days again... yet I would never trade what I have now. Zat's why I'm doing ze next best thing... I'm booking a cruiserweight matchup, to remind me of ze beginnings of my glory days. Donaven Winters, zat depressed joker, will face a man who was in ze division when I started and has, not surprisingly, progressed no furt'er... ze Black Quicksilver." Frenchie passed ze er, the mic back to Chronos. "Now it's my turn. Now, before I book anything, I have something else to say. Tempest, come on down." Seconds passed, then "Battleflag" hit. The crowd cheered as Tempest made her way down to the ring, clearly wary. She stepped in and looked at Chronos. "Hey, babe!" said Chronos. Tempest scowled, and Chronos continued. "Now, we all know how you love to be near me... that's why I'm doing this. I've talked it over with Brian and Pierre, and they're OK with it... well, more or less, anyway. Anyway, the point is this... I want you to join 3vil." Tempest's face showed shock, and the crowd booed and erupted in yells for her not to accept it. Chronos urged her on. "C'mon... I'm sure we can think of a name with a four in it!" He handed Tempest the mic. She paused... hesitated... then, "No!" The crowd erupted in cheers, and Chronos angrily snatched the mic back. "Fine! That's the way you want it, then that's the way it's gonna be! You see, I was going to book a rematch of Panther and Kelly from last week, but I've got a better idea... you can team with Black Panther to face Erik Kelly and, hmm... your good friend, Eddy Fate!" The crowd booed again, and Tempest frowned. Before she could protest, Chronos shooed her out of the ring. She left, still frowning, and Chronos gave the mic back to Primetime. "Yeah, that's showin' her, Ric! I tell you, the look on her face... man, that cracked me up. Nothing like a good laugh... in fact, you can never have too many matches held for the sole purpose of laughing at the participants. Like, say... Mike Heftel and Jackhammer against Jon Crisp and, since they're such good friends, La Parka!" Primetime smirked, and handed it off to Frenchie. "Let's see... two matches to go, huh? Well, I guess I better make zis good. There's one man in ze fWo who has truly impressed us since we first appeared... zat man is ze Internet Champion, one Vic Creed. Vic, you have earned ze respect of 3vil... in fact, we respect you so much zat we're giving you a rare privilege tonight. You can defend zat belt against... whoever you want!" The crowd booed, and Frenchie beamed again before passing the mic along to Chronos. "Now, there's a big pay-per-view coming up... Unstable. Teams of four strive to settle the score, elimination-style. As we have the book, we can book the show... and we're booking us against Douglas..." Cheers. "Ruben Ross..." More cheers. "The Boston Strangler..." Huge cheers. "And whatever moron they can find that's stupid enough to team with them! Of course, it can't be a handicap match, and that brings me back to Vic Creed. Vic... congratulations. You're our partner at Unstable." The crowd erupted in boos at this pairing of four of the fWo's most hated individuals. "And, oh yeah, there's still one more match to book tonight. Strangler, Ross, Douglas... you're gonna get just a little taste of the pain that awaits you at Unstable. Tonight, us against you, elimination style!" The crowd cheered at the announcement of this big match, and Chronos snarled, almost as if he was pained by the sound of the cheers. "Spend your next hour and a half wisely, boys... because once we see you in the ring..." Primetime took the mic. "Well, there's just one thing to say." He passed the mic to Frenchie. "Nous gagnerons." Frenchie hurled the mic aside, and the crowd booed again as "Juke Joint Jezebel" serenaded 3vil to the back. Which, ironically enough, is also where we're going... A Revelation Backstage, 'Black Plague' Ruben Ross prepared to enter his dressing room, but when he opened the door, he found something he didn't expect to see. Andrea. Dawn Van Dammage. In his dressing room. Before Ross can speak, Andrea explains what is going on. "Listen..." Ross folds his arms. "I'll listen. Should I grab a seat? This better be good." He gives a stern look at Dawn Van Dammage, the manager of his arch-rival, 'Kodiak' Vic Creed. Ross grabs a chair and sits down, while Andrea nods and stands up. "I and Dawn met awhile ago at a Berkley pub after she wrestled earlier in the evening. We both ordered the same drink and happened to be sitting next to each other. We hit it off, and a friendship was more or less formed." Dawn picked up where Andrea began. "Ruben, I apologize for my earlier actions with the chairs. I didn't want to jeopardize my friendship with Andrea... I'm torn between loyalty to the big lug and my friendship with Andrea. I've decided that it'll be best if I stay out of your business with Vic." Ross nodded, appearing as if he was fine with the situation. Andrea smiled. "We both thought you were going to be angry." Ross grinned. "Well, actually I was hoping that you guys were gonna tell me that you're... well, nevermind." Ross grabbed a bag from his locker, before winking at Andrea, as Slaughter went elsewhere. Jon Crisp/Sphere vs. STUMP Squad "War?" hits, and the crowd cheers as the STUMP Squad, consisting of Mike Heftel and Jackhammer, begin to make their way to the ring. Jackhammer steps over the top rope into the ring, and Heftel simply rolls in. They wait for their opponents. "Anarchy In The UK" plays, and out walks the recently Brit-ified Sphere, who tells the camera that he and Crisp will show those dinnermashers a thing or two over the background of boos. "My Hero" replaces Sphere's music, and the boos continue and intensify as Jon Crisp makes his way to the ring, looking supremely arrogant. He shoots Heftel and Jackhammer a sneer as he steps into the ring, then tells Sphere in no uncertain terms that he will be starting the match. Sphere shrugs at the antics of that bloke, then takes his spot in the corner. Crisp locks up with Heftel as the bell rings, and quickly goes behind him. Crisp lifts him up and takes him down to the mat. Heftel squirms free, and both men get to their feet. They lock up again, and this time Crisp gains the advantage with a knee to the gut. He whips Heftel into the corner and charges in after him, nailing him with a clothesline. Heftel stumbles out, and Crisp takes him down with a, well, crisp snap suplex. Crisp makes a cover, but Heftel gets a shoulder up at two. Crisp brings Heftel up again and goes behind him, then takes him to the mat with a german suplex. He then tags out to Sphere, who hits the ring and nails the downed Heftel with an Asai moonsault. Sphere hooks the leg for the cover, but Heftel kicks out at two. Sphere kicks Heftel in the gut, doubling him over, then runs into the ropes. He goes for a swinging neckbreaker, but Heftel side-steps and Sphere falls on his ass. Heftel heads to his corner and tags in Jackhammer, who steps over the top to meet his former Guano Apes partner. Sphere gets to his feet just in time to eat a faceful of leather, courtesy of Jackhammer's boot. Jackhammer makes the cover, but Crisp breaks it up at two. Jackhammer brings Sphere up again and takes him down with a delayed vertical suplex. He grabs Sphere by the head and brings him up, but Sphere nails him with a low blow. Jackhammer grabs his crotch and falls like a tree, landing fortunately in his corner, where Mike Heftel shrugs and tags in. At the same time, Sphere tags out to Crisp, who hits the ring and runs over Heftel like a freight train with a huge clothesline. Heftel gets up, staggered, and Crisp hooks his head. He then drives Heftel head-first into the mat with an implant DDT. Crisp signals that it's over, and brings Heftel to his feet. He brings Heftel back into a corner, then picks him up wheelbarrow-style. He charges out of the corner, then drives Heftel into the mat with the Icon. Heftel is out, and Crisp makes the cover. Jackhammer, recovered, gets to his feet, but Sphere catches him with a forearm, knocking him out of the ring. The ref tolls the three-count, and Sphere and Crisp procure a relatively easy victory, all things considered. The ref raises their arms as Slaughter heads backstage... Hellrazor (c) vs. Adam Manson vs. Insane Wonder Slaughter returned from the commercial break for a Hardcore Title match. Coming out first to the sounds of 'Sugar' by System Of A Down (new album out this Tuesday!) was the Insane Wonder, a former fWo Cruiserweight Champion and the only wrestler in fWo history to be urinated on inside an fWo ring. Wonder is also now the fWo Jobberweight Champion, a title of which he held proudly. Wonder walked out onto the stage with a shopping cart, before he got inside it and simply waited on the stage. His music quickly faded and was replaced by "Burning Blood" by Six Feet Under; which brought out the "Sanity Assassin" Adam Manson. Manson snarled as he grabbed the shopping cart w/ Wonder inside and pushed it down the stage down to ringside, before climbing into the ring. Manson grabbed a chair from ringside and set it in the middle of the ring; where he took a seat and waited for the fWo Hardcore Champion to make his entrance. 'From This Day' by Machinehead blared throughout the Palace, and out walked the cat loving Hardcore Champion himself, Hellrazor. Hellrazor walked down to ringside and noticed that Wonder was stuck in the shopping cart, and he grabbed it and pushed it into the nearest set of ring steps... before suddenly stopping. Wonder came flying out head first into the ring post, before falling face first on the floor. The fans cheered loudly, before Hellrazor turned and met not only the feet of Manson, but a steel chair courtesy a baseball slide w/ a chair. Manson grabbed Hellrazor and rolled him into the ring, before grabbing the shopping cart. As Hellrazor staggered to his feet, he turned and said hello to a flying shopping cart, thanks to Adam Manson. With Hellrazor out on the mat, Manson grabbed the chair, and headed to the top rope. He climbed up and positioned the chair in between his arm and body, and flew off with an elbow drop right in the face of Hellrazor. Completing the move, he went for a cover. A two count is all he got, as Hellrazor kicked out. Wonder was finally getting into the ring, and the God of Cut-Throat Wrestling instructed him to go to the top rope. Wonder flew off with a top rope somersault legdrop. The two continued to work Hellrazor over with the chair, and Hellrazor took as much punishment as possible. A dazed Hellrazor stood in the middle of the ring, while Manson held the steel chair in front of his face, as Wonder climbed to the top rope and loudly yelled out something that wasn't comprehendable, before flying off head first into the chair, and Hellrazor himself. With both men out on the mat, Manson saw his opportunity to put Hellrazor away. He first set the bent steel chair up, before going for a death valley driver into the chair. Having read Hellrazor his 'Last Rites', Manson went for the pin, but Hellrazor got his foot on the bottom rope. Manson swung Hellrazor to the ropes and went for a vicious chair shot, but Hellrazor blocked it, kicked Manson in the stomach and hit hiim in the head. Knowing that was not enough to put Manson out, he hit him again, but Manson again didn't go down. Hellrazor repeated the process... seven more times, before Manson finally went down after ten solid chair shots. From behind Wonder finally recovered and took Hellrazor out with a clip, and Hellrazor fell to the mat. Wonder then went up to the top rope and hit a 450 splash. A count of two is all he got. Frustrated, Wonder checked on the Sanity Assassin, before going back to work on Hellrazor with some leg drops. Finally, Manson crawled over and held down Hellrazor, while Wonder went to the top rope once again. Hellrazor however broke free and kicked Manson in the face, before reaching back with his free hands, and grabbing the shopping cart as Wonder flew through the air. Hellrazor then stood up and as Manson turned around, Hellrazor took him out with a shopping cart shot to the face. Wonder charged out from the corner, and he was hit with the shopping cart again. Hellrazor then put the shopping cart atop Manson and used it to pin him and as a result, eliminated him from the match. Result: Hellrazor by pinfall Bad News Juno Law looked at his brother. "Pikachu, you evil son of a bitch! You die now!" Juno shook his head and smiled. His brother could be a bit nuts at times, and a bit childish at others, but still, he wouldn't trade their relationship for anything. They had been through a lot together from their reuniting in Tornado Wrestling to their ascendance to the fWo and the World Tag Team Titles, belts that signified their status as the top tag team in professional wrestling a status they'd get back soon, Juno was sure. He had had a lot of friends in his life, but never one greater than his brother Kobi. There was a knock at the door. Juno turned away from his brother and opened the locker room door. "Come in." In stepped Dr. Thomason, the doc who had worked on their injuries suffered at Bloodbath. "Doc! Good to see you. You have the results, I assume?" Kobi looked up. This was something he wanted to hear. Dr. Thomason's face bore a frown. "Well... I have some bad news for you. I did those DNA tests you requested, and, well... I'm afraid you won't like the results." Juno's smile faded. "What do you mean?" "Well... you two aren't actually brothers." Juno's face showed shock, and Kobi hopped to his feet, stunned. "That can't be right! What the hell's going on?" Dr. Thomason held up his hands. "Well, perhaps that was an overstatement. You are brothers... but you're only half-brothers. You see, you have different fathers." Dr. Thomason launched into a long explanation of the situation, but Juno tuned him out. One thought was running through his head. My brother... is not. The man known as 'The Fallen Angel' slowly makes his way out from the back, an oddly blank look on his face as he walks down to the ring. A few fans in the crowd mock Donaven's walk, but Winters doesn't seem to care. As he is stepping through the ropes, the familiar sound of 'Slave To The Grind' is heard as Black Quicksilver comes out and jogs down the ramp, waving at the fans as they cheer for the former Cruiserweight and Jobberweight Champion. BQ quickly makes his way into the ring and starts to climb up one of the turnbuckles. Winters, however, has other plans in mind as he comes up behind BQ and nails him from behind with a forearm to the back. As the ref signals for the bell, Winters whips BQ into the opposite corner and charges in after him. BQ is able to get a foot up to nail Donaven in the face and then drops his staggering opponent with a bulldog from the second turnbuckle. BQ makes a quick cover, but only gets a two count. BQ lifts Winters back up and whips him into the ropes. BQ leap frogs Winters on the way back through, but Donaven stops in his tracks and hits a dropkick to the back of BQ's knee just as he is landing back down from the leap frog. BQ reaches down and clutches the knee in pain as Winters goes to work right away with a series of stomps and kicks to the injured knee. Donaven reaches down and lifts BQ's legs up and then drops an elbow down across the injured knee. BQ rolls over onto his stomach and tries to crawl to the ropes, but Winters grabs the leg and lifts it up in the air before slamming it back down, the knee crashing into the canvas. Normally not known for submission holds, Winters turns BQ over and is able to lock in a sharpshooter. BQ is trying to fight the pain, but the ref is asking him if he is going to submit and BQ seems to be losing the battle. Fortunately for him, he is able to reach out and stretch just far enough to reach the bottom rope as the ref forces Donaven to break the hold. BQ struggles to pull himself up along the ropes, as Donaven looks on with a blank expression. Just as BQ gets to his feet, Winters moves in for more damage. This time Donaven is the one who ducks, followed by a kick to the gut that doubles over BQ, and a vicious Tiger Driver. Winters covers but only gets a two count. The referee raises BQ's hand as Monte stands at the top of the ramp, smiling at what he has just done.
