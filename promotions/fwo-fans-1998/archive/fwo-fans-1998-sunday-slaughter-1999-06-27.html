fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 27 June 1999

27 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction As Slaughter opens up; the president of the fWo Travis makes his way out to a nice, positive reaction from the crowd. Slaughter fireworks and the Slaughter music make the environment a noisy atmosphere. Travis climbs into the ring and asks for a microphone, not wasting anytime in getting down to business. Travis kicks things off and demands that Austin Davis come down to the ring and explain his actions from this past Friday at Numebred Days. Without hesitation, "trust me" bellows throughout the arena, and Davis walks out. Davis has a mixture of boos & cheers from fans, he's not quite as hated as he once was, but hated nonetheless. Travis says; "I thank you for what you did... and that's how you respond?" Davis responds by saying that "It was my way of saying "you're welcome". Travis, you're lucky I didn't kill you". Travis, now wide-eyed and quite alarmed by what Davis has said, asks Davis to elaborate. Davis says; "For over a year, I've been busting my ass here in the fWo. I've carried this promotion on my back to the top of the wrestling world. Others may have been here just as long as I have... and while that may be true, they're not the type of star that I am. I can see why others would be jealous of my stature here in the fWo... but there is only one Austin Davis, and you're looking at him. For the past year, Travis has used me to involve himself more here in the fWo. He's used Austin Davis, and not once, until this past Friday night, did you actually say "thanks". You never thanked me for carrying this promotion on my back for a year, no... you thanked me for saving your ass from Chris Universal & The Dark Riders, when all I was doing was trying to get a piece of Universal again. Well, Travis... "thanks" is not enough. You want to thank me, Travis? You can thank me by giving me control of the fWo. Control of the promotion that I've carried for the last year and a half. Control of the promotion that has profitted off of Austin Davis. *That* is how you can thank me, Travis... " Travis interrupts Davis, and tells him "you're crazy. You think I'd give control of the promotion over to YOU? A guy who, by all accounts, HAS been very successful by ripping off the gimmick of the star of another promotion that will remain nameless? A guy who... has used the fWo as a stepping stone to benefit himself financially? You know what... screw you, Davis... no, Davis... FUCK YOU. I'm sick of the crap. You can take that speech, all that BS you just spewed out, and shove it up your ass because I am not ever... and I mean ever going to give the control of the fWo over to YOU!" Davis simply smirks, before attacking Travis relentlessly. Davis DDT's Travis three times in a row, before "covering him" in a pinfall attempt, and smacking the mat three times. Davis then stands up with his arms in the air, as the crowd continues to boo Davis, and begins throwing stuff at him. Davis responds by saying; "Seeing as how I just defeated Travis 1-2-3 in the middle of the ring, I'm proclaiming myself the new owner of the fWo. The first order of business with me as president will be for some sorry bastards to come clean up the ring and haul this piece of sorry crap out of the ring, because its the least I could do for him as the new owner of the fWo. The second order of business will be for everyone in the fWo to hand over their respective titles to me, because you are now looking at the only man to ever hold the fWo World, Internet, International, Hardcore, Cruiserweight, Tag Titles and Women's Titles all at the same time. If you do not hand over your titles to me by the end of the evening, I will respectively kick each and every single one of your asses until you DO hand over the titles to me. And if that is not enough to influence you, well... your asses WILL be released of your fWo contracts. Do I make myself clear? The third order of business: Megan Dawn, your services are no longer needed. You're fired!" With that, Davis exits the ring holding his arms in the air... jabbering with fans at ringside, before making his exit up the ramp to a loud ovation of jeers. Chaos vs. Scott Hart We return from the commercial break to find Chaos in the ring accompanied by Scott Taylor. The massive War & Plague await Scott Hart, who begins to make his way out as his theme "Hot For Teacher" loudly rocks the arena. Hart comes down to ringside and looks up at War & Plague, and then smartly says he will not enter the ring until one of them is on their respective apron, and Taylor is out of the ring. Taylor reluctantly gives in to Hart' demands and exits the ring. Plague goes over and stands on the ring apron, and now it finally appears Hart is going to enter the ring. Before actually entering though, he walks over near Taylor and attacks him, before throwing him backfirst into the guardrail, and then dropping the ring steps on his leg. Hart begins to escape through the crowd as Death joins the other Dark Riders to chase him away. Result: No Contest Darkness vs. US Marshal The always cheerful US Marshall makes his way out as the crowd welcomes him with an appreciative greeting. Marshal climbs up into the ring and waits for his opponent, the always dangerous and really big meanie Darkness. Darkness comes down the ramp and makes his way in. He attacks Marshal and throws him over the top rope onto the floor, wasting no time in taking it out of the ring. He begins stomping Marshal repeatedly, and as the crowd jeers his actions, he continues his assault by grabbing a piece of the guardrail and dropping it on the leg of Marshal. He then stands the guardrail up, and vertically suplexes Marshal backfirst into it. He brings the action back into the ring and and decides he wants to get it over early, as he is learning from his mistakes made this past Friday at Numbered Days. He brings a limping Marshal up to his feet and puts on the sleeperhold, and it looks as if it will be lights out for Marshal. The referee raises Marshal' hand once, and it falls to the mat. He raises it again, and it falls again. The referee is about to do it for a third time, when Darkness tells him not to. He releases the hold and Marshal falls to the mat hard. Darkness' attention was caught by something in the crowd, and as he walks back over to Marshal... he bends over and Marshal hurry and rolls him up in a small package for the pin. Three seconds later, and Marshal has escaped with a very lucky victory... and this is the second time in 48 hours that Darkness has lost despite having lady luck on his side. Result: US Marshal by Pinfall Panther makes a statement... As the fWo prepared to go to another match, the Black Panther suddenly stormed out onto the entrance ramp and walked to the ring. Unschedule to be out at this time, James Armstrong reluctantly handed him a microphone. The Panther said, "As many of you saw on Friday, Chris Universal defeated me for the Internet Title. He beat me by hitting me with brass knuckles and covering me with his feet on the ropes. In my career in the fWo, this was the worst screwjob I've ever suffered. But that's in the past, and I'm looking into the future. Universal, I challenge you to a rematch, but not just any match. I challenge you to a Screwjob Match! What is a Screwjob Match you ask? Well, it's very simple, the only way you can pin the other man is by cheating. So if you got me with the Cha-Ching and covered me, the referee wouldn't count because that move is legal. Now, if you nailed me with a chair and covered me, that's when the referee would count. What do you say Universal? Name the time and the place, and I'll be there..." With that, the Panther returned to the backstage area... Micky Maliboo vs. Samuel Smooth Samuel Smooth came to the ring looking distressed and annoyed, his green suit rumpled and dirty. Inside the ring Smooth addressed the crowd, telling them all that he was solidly behind Judge Steel this Wednesday because "they didn't need a broad with the men". As if on cue, Micky Maliboo came strutting out onto the entrance ramp, making his fWo debut. Maliboo walked halfway down the ramp, and then signalled to the backstage area. A beautiful woman walked out and joined him at his side as he walked the rest of the way to the ring. Smooth with the early advantage with a side headlock, then a quick headlock takedown. Smooth begins to work him over, but Maliboo uses his leg strength to put Smooth in a headscissors. Smooth nips up out of it, and Maliboo follows suit with a nip up of his own. Both men putting on a great technical showing in the early goings of this match. Both men lock up again, this time in a test of strength. Smooth easily over powers Maliboo at first, using his massive upper body mass to push Maliboo towards the match. Maliboo makes a comeback, using his upper body to push and his lower body to hold his position as he bridges up towards Smooth, then grabbing an arm, and applying an armbar, before sending Smooth to the ropes and hitting a dropkick, which only staggers Smooth. Maliboo continues his attack and hits a spinning wheel kick, which further staggers Smooth. Maliboo decides he should pull out all the stops, and he goes for the jumping thrust kick to the jaw, but Smooth catches his foot just in time. Maliboo however counters with an enziguiri, but Smooth ducks and Maliboo falls on the mat face first. Smooth now on the offense, grabs Maliboo by his hair and throws him over the top rope out onto the cement floor. Smooth goes on the outside and slams Maliboo on the cement floor. He then goes back up on the ring apron, and gets a running start before coming off with a big-time elbow into the stomach area of Maliboo. Smooth brings Maliboo back into the ring and goes for a pin, but only gets a count of two. He stands up and drops another elbow. Goes for another cover, only two again. He brings Maliboo to his feet and nails him with knee to the stomach, and follows up with a reverse neckbreaker. Another cover, and another kickout at two. Smooth is quite frustrated, but continues on the attack. He bounces off the ropes and hits a legdrop, and demands the referee to count... covering Maliboo with just his leg. Maliboo quickly kicks out this time however; and it becomes clear that Smooth is losing his patience. He brings the Maliboo to his feet and whips him to the ropes, and goes for a tiltawhirl, but Maliboo reverses it into a flying headscissors, which takes Smooth off his feet and he slides out underneath the bottom rope onto the cement floor. Maliboo takes the time to recover, and as Smooth gets back up to his feet on the outside, Maliboo uses the second turnbuckle to hit a springboard body press up and over onto Smooth on the outside. Maliboo brings Smooth to his feet and whips him backfirst into the guardrail. Maliboo follows it up with a nice hurracarana as Smooth staggers out, and with the momentum Maliboo rolls back into the ring and prepares for another high impact move. Plotting carefully, Maliboo first executes a baseball slide into Smooth, which rocks him back into the guardrail, he remains on his feet however. Maliboo goes outside onto the ring apron and uses the middle rope to propel himself onto Smooth, with a beautifull executed asai moonsault. Smooth is able to use his massive size and strength to catch Maliboo, and then he puts him up onto his shoulder. It looks as if he's going to ram him into the ring post, but Maliboo escapes down behind him and pushes Smooth into the ringpost instead! As Smooth walks out backwards, Maliboo catches him offguard with a reverse russian legsweep on the outside. The referee comes outside and instructs Maliboo and Smooth to bring the action back inside, so Maliboo complies. Smooth stood on his feet inside the ring, but Maliboo jumped from the ring apron to the ropes, and hit Smooth with a cross-body splash. Acting quickly, Smooth was rolled up and Mailboo scored his first victory in the fWo. As Mailboo celebrated, Samuel Smooth insulted the ref, and then attacked Maliboo from behind, demanding a rematch. Officals arrived to seperate the two, and cameras went backstage... Result: Micky Maliboo by Pinfall Wolf Fang (c) vs. Mike Bear Mike Bear came to the ring to a good reaction, as fans in the crowd who remembered him from the TCW waved banners and signs as he climbed inside the ring. Bear stood on the top turnbuckle and rose his arms for the crowd, who cheered the superstar. Wolf Fang's music played, but he did not appear on the entrance ramp. Instead, the wrestler walked silently through the crowd with the Cruiserweight title over his shoulder. Wolf Fang strode through the crowd quickly, and rolled into the ring and stood nose-to-nose with the challenger. Wolf Fang shoved Bear backward, who responded with a quick standing clothesline and kicked Wolf Fang as he fell to the ground. Wolf Fang rolled out of the ring and paced, trying to kill Bear's momentum. Unwilling to wait for Wolf Fang to get back in the ring, Bear went to the ropes and rebounded for a vicious plancha to the outside. Bear hammered away on Wolf Fang, and then tossed him into the ring. Bear was set to follow, when his name was suddenly called from the arena PA system. Looking around confused, Bear spotted Chris Kidman and the Falcon standing on the top of the entrance ramp. The Falcon held a small bag, which appeared to be moving. Bear started to ignore the two and started to roll inside the ring, when the Falcon, who was holding a microphone, called his name once more. Bear shouted at the Falcon that he wanted him to leave, but the Falcon just laughed, and held up the bag saying, "I have something you want..." With that, the Falcon reached into the bag and pulled from it what appeared to be a miniature collie. Bear's face turned ashen, and he shouted "MUFFIN!", before quickly exiting the ring and running up the ramp after Falcon and Kidman, who quickly made a retreat to the backstage area. The ref applied a count, and declaired Wolf Fang the victor by pinfall. Wolf Fang held up the title for the crowd, who seemed unsure of how to respond. Suddenly they came alive, as Briana, dressed in a long black trenchcoat. Briana held up the microphone, and spke quietly, the crowd straining to hear her words. Slowly, she said, "One week. You... and me. Bring the belt." As the crowd pondered this, Slaughter went to a commercial break... Result: Wolf Fang by Countout Crucifix vs. Rain Cameras went not to the ring, but to the outside after the camera break. In the arena parking lot cameras found Rain, standing alone, and holding a steel baseball bat. A light mist fell, and the hair of Rain fell hard along his face. From a distance, a truck was heard, and as it speed nearer Rain continued to hold his ground. At the last moment, Rain fell to the ground, letting the truck manned by Crucifix speed over him and crashed into the conrete wall of the arena. Not waiting for fWo security to pick through the wreckage, Rain ran to the now demolished truck and leapt on top of it, using the bat to batter the top of the vehicle. Finally managing to get the top of the truck removed, Rain stopped surprised as he discovered that the truck was empty. With a tear of metal, Crucifix emerged from the battered undercarrage of the truck, and grabbed Rain from behind, nailing a DDT on the metal. The rain fell harder, and Crucifix screamed upward, as Rain rose once more and began delivering hard chops to the chest of his attacker. Rain and Crucifix brawled out into the parking lot with neither man gaining a significant advantage. Finally, Rain swung with his bat, knocking Crucifix off his feet and down to the hard asphalt. Crucifix rose his arms to the sky, and screamed once more... as what appeared to be bolts of lightening struck the bat and the ground around Rain. Dropping the smoking baseball bat and shrugging off his charred trench coat, Rain flung himself on Crucifix, driving his head into the concrete. Seeing the carnage have no hopes of ending or getting into the ring, fWo officals swarmed the area and attempted to seperate the two. Crucifix and Rain both attacked the security men on the scene, and then continued their battle. The fWo went to commercials, with Chris Finn announcing that this match would be decided yet... on Warfare, in a sealed steel cage... with no holds barred. Result: No Contest Collection Time With Slaughter just about ready to go off the air, the apparent new owner (self-proclaimed) of the fWo makes his way out to a loud ovation of boos. Davis smirks at the crowd, before making his entrance into the ring. Davis has a microphone in hand, and speaks... saying; "I gave all of the champions until the end of the night to give me their damn titles. Well, it's time to collect. So, to everyone who has a title here tonight... get your damn ass out here, and hand it over". With that, the fWo World Champion Ultra-Violet makes his way out... followed by fWo World Tag Team Champions Wolf Fang & Jackhammer, the fWo Hardcore Champion Scott Slugger, the fWo International Champion Flying Frenchie, the fWo Internet Champion Chris Universal, amongst others. As they all make their way out onto the ramp, Universal calls for their attention. They then huddle on the stage, except Ultra-Violet, who is distracted by a lookalike of himself in the crowd. Universal appears to be forming a plan with the other wrestlers... and that plan comes to fruition as the wrestlers gathered charge down to ringside. Universal has a microphone now, and says "You want our titles? All of them? You've got them!" One by one, they all throw their titles into the ring at Davis... before going into the ring and chasing him out. Somehow, the only title Davis manages to pick up is Universal' Internet Title. As Slaughter comes to an end, Davis is walking backwards up the ramp... holding the Internet Title high above his head, while pointing his middle finger at the wrestlers gathered in the ring, and at the same time, mouthing some foul words in their direction.
