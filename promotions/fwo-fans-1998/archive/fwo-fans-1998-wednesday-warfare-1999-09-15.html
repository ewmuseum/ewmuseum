Wednesday Warfare, 15 September 1999

15 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Cameras opened on Scott Slugger, walking quickly through the backstage area of the arena and holding a small piece of paper in one fist. Geoff Green ran to keep up with him as Scott Slugger stormed by dressing rooms and security guards. Stopping at Ice's dressing room, Slugger yelled inside for Ice to follow him. Ice came with the fWo Internet title slung over his shoulder and a look of anger on his face. The two shoved wrestlers out of their way as they marched, stopping in front of the office of the commissioner. Slugger started to knock, and then simply kicked at the door, battering it open with his boot. Slugger stepped into the office with Ice behind him as the commissioner rose to his feet to confront the arrivals. "What in the hell do you want?", the commissioner barked, getting into Slugger's face and wearing an expression of annoyance. Slugger shoved Commissioner Travis backward and started to cock one fist back, but Ice stopped him before the punch could be thrown. Slugger nodded to Ice, and instead of punching the commissioner thrust the piece of paper into his face. Travis took it and looked at the scrawl of writing, which read: "THIS FRIDAY YOU GET IT ONCE AND FOR ALL". Travis grunted and crumpled the paper into a ball with one hand before tossing it into the garbage. "So? You got a threat. In case you didn't realize it Scott this is the wrestling business. Threats are made. Deal with it like an adult." Slugger turned and looked for a moment as if he was going to leave, but then spun and punched the commissioner hard in the face, sending him sprawling over the desk and onto the floor. Slugger began to scream and advance on the commissioner, this time without Ice stopping him. "The wrestling business? Listen here pops, you've got no god damn idea of what the wrestling business is about! People like us are the ones risking our necks while you sit on your ass and collect the paychecks. You think it's okay that some people keep interfering in my matches? You sanction that? Listen you asshole... you step into the ring sometime and then you tell me how serious this wrestling business is!" Travis rose to his feet, holding his jaw. His eyes blazed at Scott Slugger, and he spoke in a low growl. "You don't think I know it's tough out there? I sign those medical bills Wasner... it case you forgot. I understand this business just as well as you do, and don't you dare accuse me otherwise." Scott Slugger held up his fWo World Heavyweight championship title, and shoved it into the face of the commissioner. "You don't understand shit, pops... but tonight's your chance. You want to let three masked maniacs run around the promotion and take cheap shots at me, then you get your ass in the ring tonight. Against me... and I'll teach you what it means to be a god damn wrestler." At that moment John Willis and other members of team fWo security hit the area and stood between Scott Slugger and the commissioner. Slugger and Ice walked out, with Slugger tossing a final threat to back up his challenge as he went. Willis looked after Commissioner Travis, who held his jaw... but his eyes blazed with anger. "I'll meet you tonight Wasner... you bet your ass I will..." With that, the fireworks outside could be heard, and Warfare officially got underway with the threat of a World title match looming on the horizon... Matthew Zhuk vs. Wretch "Unforgettable" brings Matthew Zhuk down to the ring, as Warfare opened with a tournament match, a clash between two former Cruiserweight champs. Zhuk takes a second to remind all of us that he'd defeated Wretch, then known as Chris Kidman Jr., for the Cruiserweight belt eight months ago, and that tonight would be no challenge for the Great Lookin' one. Static-X's "I'm with Stupid" starts, and Wretch and Mike Bear step out onto the entrance ramp. Bear wishes Wretch good luck with this second-round match-up and heads backstage as Wretch makes his way to the ring. The bell rings, and a tie-up to start things off. Wretch backs Zhuk into the corner, breaking the lock-up at the ref's order. Another tie-up finds Zhuk forcing Wretch into a corner. Again, the ref orders a break. Zhuk breaks the hold, then proceeds to gouge Wretch's eyes and stomp him to the mat. He backs away at the threat of disqualification, and Wretch reclaims his footing. Zhuk mows him down with a lariat, and backs away again, mocking Wretch. Front face-lock by Wretch quickly turns into a somoan bulldog. Wretch heads to the top, and he catches Zhuk with an impressive twisting cross-body, resulting in but a one-count. Both men back to their feet, and Zhuk whips Wretch to the ropes, lifting him high into the air with a back-body drop upon his return. Zhuk grabs the hair and wrenches Wretch back his feet. Still holding the hair, despite the ref's admonishments, he walks Wretch to the ropes and nonchalantly tosses him over the top. He catches Wretch with a baseball slide as the Underground member made his way to his feet, and follows that up with a suicide dive, which drives Wretch into the security barrier. Zhuk now chatting with some fans at ringside while Wretch gets back inside the ring. Zhuk finally decides to follow, and the two engage in another lock-up. Wretch gains the advantage, turning it into a hammer-lock, but Zhuk breaks that with a knee to the gut. Zhuk into the ropes, and another lariat knocks Wretch down. Zhuk goes upstairs, licks his forefinger and holds it out to test for wind direction, and lands a shooting star press on Wretch halfway across the ring. He opts not to go for the cover, runs to the ropes again, nails a rolling senton splash and then a standing moonsault for a two-count. Zhuk "discusses" with the ref the art of counting and covers Wretch again, getting another two-count. Zhuk tries an Asia moonsault but misses his mark, and the referee starts laying down the count. Wretch and Zhuk both make it up at seven, and Wretch does a quick go-behind and applies a rear-waistlock. Standing switch by Zhuk, and another by Wretch, putting him in control once more. A full-nelson applied by Wretch quickly turns into a dragon suplex with a bridge for the two-count. Both men are up, with Wretch catching Zhuk with a fisherman's buster. Back on top, Wretch nails a corkscrew moonsault, which earns another near-fall. Zhuk is brought to his feet once more and whipped to the ropes, but his attempt at a lariat turns into a crucifix for Wretch and another two-count. Zhuk whips Wretch into the ropes, nailing him with a hotshot and covering for a two-count. Zhuk lifts Wretch and goes for the rope burn, but Wretch blocks the attempt and sends an elbow to Zhuk's gut. Wretch with a rocker dropper for two. He goes to the top again, and a 450 splash nails Zhuk flush. Wretch hooks the far leg, and the ref counts to three, sending him into the next round. Result: Wretch by Pinfall Black Quicksilver sets a trap As Matthew Zhuk made his way up the entrance ramp, Black Quicksilver came out to the ring. He was not scheduled to be out at this time, but carried his own microphone and pushed the referee out of the way when the referee began to protest. Black Quicksilver stood for a moment silently in the ring, and then raised Stump up to the crowd and began to shake it, getting cheers and chants from the crowd. Speaking now, Black Quicksilver said, "Courage! Courage, I know you're back there, and I want your butt out on that entrance ramp right now! I'll wait in the ring all night if I have to... the wrestlers can just... work around me or something... I don't know. But I want you out here now!" Courage came, backed up by Jake who stood just behind him. Courage grinned cockily at Black Quicksilver, and shrugged as if to ask what Black Quicksilver wanted. Black Quicksilver pointed at Courage and said, "I keep hearing your rap about being such a great wrestler, being such a great athlete... well Courage, I'm not buying any of it, and none of these fans are either. You may still be in the tournament, but you're never going to win because you would have to wrestle twice in one night for the finals... and everyone here knows you don't have the stamina to do it." Courage snorted with laughter, and asked for his own microphone. "Listen Black Quacksilver or whatever your name is... I can wrestle once a night... twice a night... or in every match of the night and have no problem. You're looking at the finest wrestler the fWo has to offer right here, and I proved that when I beat you in that very ring just a few days ago." Black Quicksilver stepped back, surprised by what he had to say. Black Quicksilver said, "Twice in one night? I don't believe that for a second, Courage." Courage nodded, and pointed back at Black Quicksilver. "Believe it masked boy, I can do it without even breaking a sweat." Black Quicksilver suddenly grinned an evil grin and pulled from his tights a contract, and waved it in the air. "Good! Then you certainly won't have any problem wrestling twice at Hotter Than Hell IV. Once against Ultra Violet, and once against me. No problem... especially against a man you already beat, right? Only a coward would back down from that challenge, right?" Courage now looked concerned, and shook his head in the negative. The crowd began to chant "Coward! Coward! Coward!" and Courage held his hands to his ears, and finally shook his head to say he accepted. Black Quicksilver beamed, but Courage wasn't finished. Shouting he said, "Fine! You want Courage? You got Courage! I'll meet you on Friday... but if... and only if... you can beat somebody tonight first!" The crowd booed at this, but Black Quicksilver looked smug. "Fine Courage... if that's the way you want it, that's fine with me. You bring whoever you want down here, and I'll go through them to get to you!" With that Black Quicksilver raised Stump into the air again and the crowd cheered as Courage and Jake walked backstage. Wednesday Warfare went to a commercial, with Black Quicksilver still shaking Stump at the excited crowd.... Primetime vs. Speed Demon We come back from commercials with Primetime and Speed Demon both in the ring, and the Boston Strangler and Nikki in their respective corners. The bell rings, and Primetime takes it to Speed Demon with a flurry of punches and kicks, backing him into the corner. His whip into the other turnbuckle was reversed, and Speed Demon followed PT into the corner with an avalanche. As Primetime stumbled out of the corner, Speed Demon lifted the smaller competitor over his head in a military press before dropping him hard on the mat. Speed Demon takes the opportunity to stomp away on Primetime's back, attempting to lessen his ability to pull off the Primetime Bomb later in the match. PT returns to his feet, locks up with Speed Demon and nails a snap suplex. Both men back up, with Primetime charging Speed Demon. SD drops to the mat, though, and Nikki pulls down the top rope, sending Primetime to the floor of the arena. Nikki now with kicks and taunts to the fallen Cruiserweight champ. So preoccupied with delivering this punishment to her former lover, she doesn't notice the Strangler coming at her from behind. The Boston Strangler locks the Boston Massacre on the Womens Champion, and the crowd just loves it. Primetime rolls back into the ring, just as Speed Demon whacks the Strangler over the head with a fierce shot from a steel chair, crumpling him and releasing his love. A look of impending doom comes over William Bailey's face as he realizes what he's just done, but he goes back into the ring anyway, to finish off his foe. Primetime has gotten his wind back and catches Speed Demon with a lariat. Speed Demon gets right back up and dropkicks Primetime, following that up with a body slam and a pair of elbow drops to PT's chest. He brings Primetime back to his feet, and places him in a fireman's carry. Speed Demon makes his way to the corner, slowly and deliberately climbs up to the second turnbuckle backwards, and then leaps back down to the mat with a super gut-buster. He makes the cover, and manages a two-count. Speed Demon rolls out of the ring to check on Nikki again, who appears to have recovered well enough. He climbs back onto the apron, but Primetime grabs his head and bangs it against the corner post. Speed Demon drops to the floor as PT mounts the corner and catches Nikki with a missile dropkick. He rolls Speed Demon back into the ring, but misses a slingshot splash as Speed Demon rolled out of the way. Both men back up, and PT strikes Speed Demon with his palm. Speed Demon's nose is now bloody, resulting in a grisly crimson mustache. Primetime attempts a Primetime Bomb, which Speed Demon counters with a backdrop. Speed Demon goes to the top, and hits a frog splash, earning him another two-count. Frustrated, he signals to the crowd that it's time for his Breakdown tiger driver II, but PT catches Speed Demon in the lower groinal region to put an end to any thoughts about that. Back to his feet, standing over the writhing, he goes to the ropes to check on the Strangler, who appears to be stirring. He returns to Speed Demon, and locks in the Cannes Cloverleaf. Speed Demon struggles to make the ropes, and even with Nikki's assistance he just barely makes it before passing out. Primetime to the top, and he lands the guillotine legdrop. Lateral press gets two, but Primetime pulls Speed Demon's shoulders off the mat to stop the count. Again he goes for the Primetime Bomb, and again his back just isn't up to the strain, allowing Speed Demon to take him to the mat with a double leg -takedown. Eager to put an end to the match and advance to the quarterfinals of the tournament, he goes for a power bomb, but this time Primetime uses the momentum of the move to catch Speed Demon in a sunset flip for the pinfall victory. Speed Demon kicks out a second too late, and he and Nikki start attacking Primetime in the middle of the ring. The Strangler comes to as the rest of the Foundation make their way to the ring, chasing Speed Demon and Nikki off with a barrage of French swears...and ominous glares from the Boston Strangler. Result: Primetime by Pinfall Trying to talk some sense into the commissioner... Backstage, head of fWo security John Willis was seen talking to the commissioner. "You're not actually going to do this are you? Come on... you're not a wrestler! Let me go out there for you... I can do it... I've been training to get into the ring. You're giving up over a hundred pounds here!" Travis looked into the face of John Willis with that same angry glare in his eyes. The jaw had stopped bleeding, but a bruise had formed there. Travis grabbed a duffel bag from the floor and stood. "John, I am going to do this. This is a matter of respect... of pride. I can't let wrestlers come barging into my office when they feel like it! Dammit, this is a business, and I'm going to run it like one... and if that means getting into the ring against Scott Slugger, then so help me I'll do it. Scott Slugger may just be a little surprised at what I can do, too. You forget... I've been doing this for a long time now, and I've picked up a few things over the years watching Japanese wrestling tapes that some crazy online people send me. Scott Slugger is going to be sorry he issued any kind of challenge to me... and he's going to be even more sorry that he put his World Heavyweight title on the line. Tonight I'm not Commissioner Travis... I'm Terrible Travis, the wrestler. Now get out of my way so I can get changed!" Travis stormed off with duffel bag in hand as John Willis watched with dismay. Willis looked at cameraman Geoff Green with a sad expression. "You know Geoff, he's going to get his ass handed to him. Go get Chris Finn, try and get him to talk Travis out of this." Geoff left the area, and as the camera pulled away John Willis shook his head. "Japanese wrestling tapes? Oh man..." Fallen vs. Darkness Warfare returned to the ring as Dina Sol escorts Fallen to the ring on a leash, provoking him as they walk down the aisle. Dina Sol releases him, and Fallen crawls into the ring and sprawls out in the center. The arena goes black, heralding the arrival of Darkness. Darkness strides to the ring menacingly, and warns Dina to stay out of the match, 'les things turn ugly. The match starts with Fallen still lying on his stomach, and Darkness starts stomping away on him. Fallen takes it all in stride before latching onto Darkness's leg and gnawing on his ankle. Darkness shakes him off and drives his elbow into Fallen's spine, finally eliciting a yelp from him. Darkness lifts Fallen, and whips him to the corner. He lets loose some hard punches before setting Fallen up on the top turnbuckle. He tries for a superplex, but Fallen blocks, and shoves him back down to the mat. Top-rope head butt from Fallen, and there's some sort of disturbance near the entrance way. The match continues as a bevy of officials block the path of Result: Darkness by Referee's Decision Bringing some Joy to Mike Bear Warfare prepared to go to the forth and final tournament match of the night, but the arena was suddenly filled with loud and scratchy music. Lights strobed around the arena, and suddenly the Warfare Screen burst into life, and was filled with yellow light. Three black dots appeared on the screen, and moved in opposite ends of each other. One dot began to stretch, and formed a "U" shape. The two dots centered on top of it, and the audience groaned as they realized what it was... a giant happy face. Joy ran out onto the entrance ramp, followed closely behind by Glee. Joy pumped his fists up at the crowd and ran to the ring, a giant smile plastered on his pale face. The ring attendant handed Joy a microphone, but he refused it and pulled a bright yellow and silver megaphone out of his tights. Joy yelled at the crowd and waved his arms. "Ladies and gentlemen... let's get ready to LOVE IT! The man with the plan and the smile of slam is back in this house and I'm here to spread the word of joy and love and all that gooey happy silly stuff right here... right now... for all of you! That's right! Tell 'em what you know, Glee!" Joy handed the megaphone over to Glee, who took it gingerly. She held it up to her mouth and said, "Um... hi and stuff. I'm... uh..." This moment stretched out for some time, and Glee finally just dropped the megaphone and giggled. Joy picked it up, and motioned for the crowd to settle down. Many people in the crowd responded by wandering away in search of refreshments. "But it's time to get a little serious... getting busted by the cops when you're gettin' jiggy with it with your woman in the middle of the library. That's a drag, isn't it? Well, anyway enough of that. Mike Bear! I've been watching you from the backstage area and getting a little... a little... a little... um... the opposite of happy. You know, I just don't get you. I mean, first of all you're Canadian. So of course you're a little messed up. I can understand that. But there is that Celine Dion person who is also from Canada, and I can't forgive you for her either. Then you have this little dog, but do you do anything cute with it? No, you just have it walking around the backstage area pissing on things. Dammit, and then there is the name. Mike Bear. I just won't have it... you should use that last name better... like call yourself... Teddy Bear! Yeah, that's it, Teddy Bear! So I'm going to make a little challenge to you. We have a match... but not just any kind of match... a happiness match. Next week, on Warfare. You and me. But if I win... if I'm the man standing happy at the end then you have to change your name to Teddy Bear for no less than one month! Then, and only then will you be on the path to happy. So what do you say Bear? I know you have this tournament thing going on, but gosh flake it, you need a little happy hat placed onto your head, and I'm just the one to do it. So until next time fWo crowd... I'm Joy, and I'm going to make all of you happy!" With that Joy tossed his megaphone into the crowd and headed to the back, much to the relief of the audience... Black Panther vs. Lance Star The Black Panther came to the ring first, slapping the hands of the fans and stopping for a moment to sign some autographs in the front row. Chris Finn and James Armstrong commented that the Black Panther was one of the few true good guys in the promotion, and that he was worthy of holding the fWo World Heavyweight championship... and would hopefully be getting his shot at the World Title soon. Lance Star came onto the entrance ramp and made his way to the ring with Chris Universal. As they neared the ring an argument ensued about Chris Universal's presence at ringside, and a messenger sent word that Chris Finn was needed backstage. Finn looked at James Armstrong suspiciously, wondering if he should leave Armstrong alone to call the match. Armstrong shrugged, and said that he would call for some help if Finn was gone for too long. Finn left as Lance Star climbed into the ring and Chris Universal headed for the backstage area. As it happened, Universal and Finn wound up walking up the ramp at the same time, and Universal who was still angry at being barred from ringside, gave Finn a push that sent him toppling over the side of the ramp and into the crowd. Inside the ring the Black Panther was waving to some fans, and was attacked by Lance Star from behind before the bell. Lance Star put the Panther in a cross-arm submission hold, but the Panther ran to the turnbuckle and flipped up and over, holding onto Star's head as he went. The Panther held onto the head and locked in the neck hold. Lance Star struck back with an elbow which broke the hold and sent the Panther stumbling backward. Star quickly climbed up the turnbuckle and flipped off the second rope in a backward tope which hit it's target. On the mat Lance Star grabbed the leg of the Panther, driving an elbow into the knee and attempting to ground the Panther. Star continued to hold onto the leg and applied a leglock, and then converted it into a figure four. Still bearing down on the leg, Lance Star finally applied the triangle scorpion deathlock, and bore down on it. The Panther held on, and the ref moved in front of him to check for a submission. Star took advantage of this, and held onto the ropes for additional leverage. While this was occurring, the Flying Frenchie appeared on the entrance ramp and made his way to the ring, carrying with him a headset. The Frenchie joined Armstrong at the announcing table, and the two commented on the match as it continued. Star continued to use the rope for leverage, and the referee finally saw it, and made him break the hold. Star argued with the referee about this, while the Panther crawled to the outside to get a breather. Star shoved the referee backward and climbed up to the top rope to attempt a plancha to the outside. Star dove off, but the Panther caught him and flipped him over, slamming him into the steel guard railing back first. The two went back into the ring with the Black Panther giving plenty of time to shake off the effects on his leg. The Panther held Star, who was dazed from his fall, and flipped him up and over in a superplex. The Panther attempted to follow this up with a hip attack, but Lance Star switched the attack into a schoolboy and made a cover. The Panther kicked out at two, but Star kicked the injured knee of the Panther as he stood. Grinning smugly at the crowd, Star picked up the Panther and delivered a powerbomb, but did not let go of the midsection of the Panther. Using his upper torso, Lance Star picked the Panther up again and delivered two more consecutive powerbombs. Star went for another cover but again was only able to get a two count. Lance Star picked the Black Panther up for another powerbomb, but the Panther locked his leg and drove Star down face-first into the mat. Star jumped to his feet, but the Black Panther attacked with a standing lariat and then a hip attack. Panther favored the injured leg as he came around behind Lance Star and hit a quick Dragon suplex, and then a German suplex. The Panther attempted a cover, but Lance Star kicked out at one. The Black Panther attempted a sunset flip, but Lance Star locked the arm and brought the Panther over his back in a backward crucifix. The Panther kicked out at one, with Star unable to keep the leverage on. Both men went to the ropes, with the Panther attempting a flying lariat. Lance Star ducked under and grabbed the torso of the Panther as he passed, slamming him to the mat with a side suplex. Star reached for the neck of the Panther to attempt a piledriver, but the Panther grabbed upward and hit a reverse DDT. The Black Panther went to the ropes and sprung off them, covering Lance Star in a La Magistral. Lance Star struggled, but kicked out a hair too late, and the Panther rolled out of the ring the winner. Star was enraged inside the ring, and grabbed a steel chair from ringside. He ran up the ramp after the limping Black Panther, and drove the steel chair into the already weakened knee. Lance Star continued to attack with the chair, until fWo security ran to the area to separate Star off the Panther. Medical officials looked in at the Panther, who appeared to be badly injured. Warfare went to a commercial as security continued to attempt to establish order... Result: Black Panther by Pinfall Old Navy Tech Vests The commercial opened on a small and brightly colored disco with a number of happy pretty people dancing in a modern hip hop style. They were all dressed in brightly colored vests, and if the viewer looked closely you could read the words "Tech Vest" on the back. At the music center an older woman wearing strange, large glasses sat next to a fluffy dog. The two spun records as the crowd danced, all looking very cheerful in a tech vest. Suddenly, the doors to the club burst open in a small explosion of plaster and dust. Death, Stone, War and Plague walked over the rubble and looked around the room of happy yuppies. Death nodded, and the three attacked the young, attractive dancers with hard punches and kicks. Blood began to flow, and as screams filled the once happy room Death looked down at the floor. He spotted one of the brightly colored vests and picked it up, looking at it closely. There were drops of blood on the vest, and Death wiped at a blotch of the blood with a thumb, leaving a smear. Death looked at the other side of the Tech Vest, and noticed that the blood was not soaking through. Death nodded again, and turned to the camera. Speaking in a clear voice Death said, "Old Navy Tech Vests... they keep the blood out." The commercial began to fade, and what appeared to be a large, fluffy dog flew across the screen behind Death. Once again the Old Navy logo appeared on the screen, complete with some additional text and prices for Old Navy Tech Vests. A scream was heard in the backgroud, and the commercial break ended. Black Quicksilver vs. Jake The crowd came to their feet as Black Quicksilver came to the ring for the second time during the show. Black Quicksilver shook Stump for the crowd, and flipped himself into the ring. Black Quicksilver waited in the center of the ring for Courage's hand-picked opponent to show, but the entrance ramp remained empty. Black Quicksilver began to shout "Come on Courage! Come on Courage!", and the crowd responded to the call and began to chant along. Cameras backstage picked up Courage and Jake standing next to the curtain. Courage was trying to convince Jake to go to the ring, but Jake looked skeptical. Finally, Jake pushed back the curtains and walked to the ring, looking unsure of what he was about to do. Jake was still dressed in street clothes, and was obviously not prepared to wrestle tonight prior to Black Quicksilver's challenge earlier in the night. Courage walked down the ramp behind Jake, clapping his hands and trying to get the crowd involved in the match. Jake entered the ring and Black Quicksilver quickly hit him with a running clothesline, and Jake rolled out of the ring. Jake paced on the outside as the referee put on the ten count, annoyed with Black Quicksilver and the situation. Black Quicksilver looked as though he might fly out of the ring with a plancha, but stopped short and kicked Jake in the face from the ring. Jake was more bothered by this than injured, and he hit the mat in frustration. Jake entered the ring again and charged Black Quicksilver, but the masked man ducked under his attack and attacked from behind, grabbed the neck of Jake and applying a sleeper hold. Jake struggled, and from the outside Courage yelled for him to drop to his knees. Jake did so, turning the sleeper hold into a reverse DDT. Jake made a cover, but only got a one count. Jake punched down Black Quicksilver using his mighty fists, and then attempted a legdrop. Black Quicksilver rolled out of the way, and Jake met only the mat. Black Quicksilver quickly rolled up Jake in a small package, and got the quick pinfall victory. Black Quicksilver raised his arms to the crowd, but was attacked by Courage from behind. Courage punched at the head and back of Black Quicksilver in an attempt to weaken the masked man before their match on Friday. fWo security officials arrived to break up the two, and cameras went backstage to check on Chris Finn's attempts to talk the commissioner out of wrestling tonight... Result: Black Quicksilver by Pinfall Trying to talk some sense into the commissioner, part two... Cameras picked up Chris Finn, who was rubbing his head after the fall he took earlier at the hands of Chris Universal. Finn was walking slowly down the hall in the backstage area in route to Commissioner Travis' office. Chris Finn knocked on the door, but already damaged from Scott Slugger bursting inside earlier the door simply fell into the room with a loud thud. Inside, the commissioner looked up at the new arrival with annoyance. "Well thanks a lot Finn... now I'm going to have to buy this stupid arena a new god damn door. Happy?" Chris Finn stammered and shuffled a foot, unsure of what to say to this. Looking for any kind of opening Chris Finn said, "So... um... weather we are having... nice... right?" Travis looked at Chris with a deadpan expression on his face and said nothing. Chris grew more uncomfortable. "Listen... Travis... the thing is this. A few of us, some of us... people around... we are just a little worried about this, you know? You're not a wrestler, you're a business man. And a good business man, you see? So... you don't have to do this... I'm sure someone can take your place... maybe James Armstrong, you know?" Travis pulled on a wrestling boot and began to lace it up. Not looking at Finn he said, "I'm wrestling Scott Slugger tonight. For the title. Besides, Scott Slugger has it in his contract that James Armstrong is never to get within touching distance in a wrestling environment. Something about James being creepy or something. It doesn't matter. I'm going out there, and I'm going to teach Scott Slugger a lesson about respect... and get the World Title back for this company." Chris Finn shuffled his feet at this, and looked over to the corner of the room where a small monitor had been placed. On it was the Wednesday Warfare ringside feed, and he saw James Armstrong sitting with the Flying Frenchie. The Frenchie was announcing to the crowd that it was "Official Demolish Kid Cool's Car" day, and the crowd cheered and went off in search of weapons. Chris Finn looked back to Travis, who was shaking his head. "Finn, arrange Kid Cool to have a cab to take him home tonight, and write out a check for a new car. Now get out of my office, I've got to finish getting ready and I don't want that damn camera in here." Finn walked out the way he came, and attempted to put the door back in place. Failing to do this, he settled for just propping the door in the jam and hurrying away... Kid Cool vs. Skippy Johnson The crowd looked around, wondering what match would be next in the Warfare lineup. Suddenly bright blue and white strobe lights began to pulse and "IN THE NAVY!" blared out over the arena speakers. A man dressed in full naval uniform, complete with the white hat perched on his head. He sauntered down the ramp, stopping from time to time to glance at some of the cuter male members of the audience before reaching the ring. Inside he began to dance, shooting his arms into the air and thrusting his crotch out at the crowd. Fans who looked closely discovered that this was crazy Mike Heftel, but he was announced as "Skippy Johnson, Naval Officer". "Skippy" saluted the crowd and then blew a kiss at a man in the front row with a tight pink muscle shirt on. The man winked back at "Skippy", which momentarily seemed to surprise Heftel. As this drama unfolded "All Star" began to play and Kid Cool made his way to the ring, slapping the hands of the fans as he went and giving them "thumb's up" signs. Kid Cool rolled into the ring and looked "Skippy Johnson" up and down with an eyebrow raised. Skippy waved a hand at Kid Cool, telling him "You stop, be nice." Kid Cool and Skippy locked up, with Skippy quickly turning the lock up into an armdrag. Skippy bore down on the arm while Kid Cool attempted to escape. Skippy loosened the hold so Kid Cool could move around behind him, and Cool suplexed him up and over to the mat. Cool made a cover, but only could get a two count as Skippy Johnson kicked out. Cool went for a kick, but Skippy caught the leg. Both men stood like this for a moment, until Heftel motioned with his chin that Kid Cool should attempt the other leg. Kid Cool did so, hitting Skippy Johnson with a spinning heel kick. Kid Cool went for another cover, but again was only able to get a two count. Fans watching this match closely began to pick up on the fact that Mike Heftel was having to carry Kid Cool to a decent match, and began to watch with more interest. Both men got to their feet and ran to the ropes for a clothesline. Both men hit the move, resulting in a double clothesline and both men went to the mat. Skippy Johnson was first on his feet, and after checking on Kid Cool he went to the turnbuckle and began to climb, taking his time in getting to the top rope. Kid Cool remained motionless on the mat. Skippy frowned at this, but then dove off the top turnbuckle in a moonsault. To his obvious horror, Skippy landed the move on target when Kid Cool failed to roll out of the way as planned. Unsure of what to do, Skippy picked up the now limp body of Kid Cool and pretended to be in a desperate lock up in the center of the ring. Skippy placed Kid Cool's arms around his neck, and pretended to go limp while still supporting Kid Cool's weight. The ref, filled in on Mike Heftel's plan, checked his arm three times, and rang for the bell. Heftel pretended to fall to the mat unconscious, rolling the body of Kid Cool out of the ring as he fell. Kid Cool landed with a thud, and the Flying Frenchie was quick to point out to the crowd that today was officially "Hit Kid Cool with your garbage day". The fans began to pelt the unconscious Kid Cool with garbage as cameras went to a commercial... Result: Kid Cool by Submission Public Service Announcement A message filled the screen that read simply: "PAID FOR BY THE fWo". This remained on the screen for a moment, and then faded away. It was replaced by a typical suburban scene, with children playing hopscotch in the streets and flying kites in their ample yards. The camera focused in one a trio of children, who were looking down at the ground with some concern. "What is it?", said one child, scratching his head. "I think it's dead...", said another. "Yeah, it's a dead chipmunk... it must have been hit by a car." The three children continued to look at the dead animal, letting their words sink in. Finally, one said, "I dare you to put it in your mouth Jimmy." The child he was speaking to, presumably Jimmy, looked up sharply. "Ew... gross. It's dead!" Now the third child got into the act. "Come on Jimmy... don't be a wuss... what are you, chicken?" Jimmy backed up a step at this new development to the challenge and thought it over. The other two kids slowly began to chant, "In your mouth! In your mouth! In your mouth!" Jimmy bent over and prodded the dead and rather stiff animal with a short stick. Gulping, he began to pick it up and guide it toward his open mouth. Suddenly a loud "TOOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" was heard and the three children turned around quickly. The Mad Trucker stood there, looking down at the children with a bright, beaming smile on his face. The children looked up at him in awe as the Trucker began to speak in a soft yet commanding tone. "Kids, that there is a dead animal I reckon... and dead animals are not supposed to go in your yapper unless these been cooked and properly cleaned. By the look of that deader down there I'd say he was neither. Now Jimmy, you don't have to eat dead things you find on the street to be cool." The Trucker looked up from the children directly into the camera. "Remember kids, Mad Trucker says don't be putting dead animals off the street into your mouths!" The kids cheered, and Jimmy said, "And now I know!" The Mad Trucker rubbed his hair with one large hand. "And knowing is half the match! TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" The kids joined him in a second rousing "TOOOOT" and the scene faded away. It was replaced by a text message of what the Trucker had just said, "Remember kids, Mad Trucker says don't be putting dead animals off the street into your mouths!" and then faded away as Warfare resumed... Nikki (c) vs. Melissa Smalls Melissa Smalls walked to the ring in her rather skimpy attire and waved at the fans as she went, who all gave her a rather lukewarm reaction. She spotted one fan holding a sign that read "WHERE ARE YOU APOCOLYPTICA?" and shrugged, but with a smile on her face. She climbed into the ring and motioned to her waist, signaling that she would be walking out of the arena the fWo Womens Champion. Nikki's music played, and she walked to the ring with Speed Demon close behind. Speed Demon looked slightly nervous about being out in the open, and checked around behind him for signs of Primetime. As they neared the ring the Flying Frenchie threatened to get up and attack Speed Demon, but eventually sat back in his chair and berated Speed Demon in commentary. Nikki locked up with Smalls, but Smalls quickly overpowered the valley girl and flipped her up and over. Smalls held her in the air for a moment or two, and then drove her down to the mat face-first. Smalls continued to punish Nikki inside the ring, much to the dismay of Speed Demon on the outside. Smalls climbed to the top turnbuckle and hit a frog splash on Nikki, not letting the fWo Womens Champion get in any offense at all. Smalls smacked the top of Nikki's head, and then applied a figure four in the center of the ring. Nikki strained, but looked as though she was about to tap out. Not wanting this to happen, Speed Demon jumped into the ring with the announcer's bell and clocked Melissa Smalls over the head. The referee at once called for the bell, but because Speed Demon was holding onto it the bell could not be rung. The referee called for the disqualification and awarded the match to Smalls, who was helped out of the ring. Suddenly the fans came to their feet as Primetime hobbled and hopped to the ring, still angry over the attack earlier. Primetime lashed out at Nikki and the Speed Demon, driving both of them from the ring. Nikki looked up at Primetime defiantly, and patted her belly. She then grabbed a microphone from the announcing table and announced, "Guess what Primetime? I'm like, so pregnant! That's right, pregnant! This man... this wonderful hunk of a man next to me did what you could never do! His little Speeders are actually potent, unlike those dead swimmers you've got! So nah, nah, nah... I'm like, so sure... I can't believe I like ever went out with you anyways... loser!" Hearing this Speed Demon passed out, hitting his head on the announcing table as he fell. Primetime fumed inside the ring, and announced that he wanted his Womens Title match... and as soon as he was done with Eron he was going to kick both of their asses... inside or outside of the ring. Nikki responded to this by sticking out her tongue and making a face. Warfare then went to the final commercial break... Result: Melissa Smalls by DQ The mystery deepens Warfare returned from commercial to find Fallen crawling on hands and knees behind the mysterious large man. Over his shoulder was Dina, still unconscious from the man's earlier blow. Matt Loco walked onto the scene with microphone in hand and attempted to get a word with Fallen. Fallen simply sniveled and slowly hit his head against the concrete of the floor, mumbling unknown nonsense. Matt Loco attempted to coax a word out of him by saying, "John Skyler... Fallen... my close personal friend... can I just get a..." This was all Matt Loco had time to say before the mysterious man grabbed Loco by the cuff and threw him into the wall. Still bathed in shadows, the mysterious man spoke slowly. His voice sounded somehow familiar... but the light was too dim to see who he was. "Fans... children... souls... know this and know it well... I have allowed this bitch a long leash as she has served me... but tonight she has proven that a firmer hand must be taken with her. This woman has given up her rights to free will, and will now suffer the same fate as this one..." He pointed down at Fallen, who cringed as the mysterious man gestured toward him. "Dina was a tool... a valuable tool, but a tool just the same. She now must be honed... sharpened... and mastered to my will. Dina is no more... from this moment forward she will be known as Poison... and you all can see what it means to shorten my leash. Darkness... you have a victory today, but know this... the time will come for retribution and pain. On that day you will stare into my face and be mine forever. Cactus Jon... I was finished with you. I was to allow you to escape away from my grasp. But once again you will feel the fire... for because of this woman's actions you show yourself to still be in the game. Well so be it... Cactus Jon, you will face Fallen once more... but this time you will not survive. Bring out the scaffolding, and bring your soul..." The mysterious man suddenly lunged out at the camera, smashing it and sending the picture into darkness... Scott Slugger (c) vs. Commissioner Travis Fans sat on the edge of their seats awaiting the World Heavyweight champion's arrival in the ring. Warfare's program had just gone over the hour break, but Armstrong assured the fans that they would stick with things until it was all over... no matter how long the program ran over. They sighed with displeasure when the commissioner came down first, dressed in workout pants and a sweat top. Looking rather unimpressive, he rolled into the ring and climbed up onto the turnbuckle, raising both arms to the crowd. Boos followed, and the commissioner looked slightly hurt. He yelled, "But I made all this!" The crowd responded with half-hearted boos, but mainly looked around for the nearest restroom. Discouraged by this, the commissioner walked to the other side of the ring and attempted to get cheers there, but instead got much the same reaction. At the announcer's desk James Armstrong and the Flying Frenchie laughed and pointed, and the commissioner glared angrily at them. Scott Slugger's music played and the fans got to their feet and began to cheer loudly. Despite the fact that Slugger was considered a heel, the fans still determined that he was the better person to get behind in this match. Slugger strolled confidently to the ring, the fWo World Heavyweight Championship belt slung over one shoulder. Scott Slugger waved to the fans, and then gave them the finger, laughing at their surprised expressions at his gesture. Slugger handed the title to James Armstrong, giving the Frenchie a wary look as he did so. Slugger rolled inside the ring and looked at the commissioner, and then began laughing. The referee attempted to get Scott Slugger to settle down, but after looking over at the commissioner couldn't help but laugh himself. Commissioner Travis fumed, and clinched and unclenched his fists in anticipation of the fight. The bell was rung, and Scott Slugger asked for a microphone. With the commissioner pacing the ring and wanting to get started, Scott Slugger said, "This is your last chance Travis... I'll let you walk right now... no strings... just roll out of that ring and get behind your desk and forget you ever came out here. Trust me... it will be a lot easier that way." Travis looked up the ramp for a moment, and then gave Scott Slugger the finger in defiance. Slugger just laughed, and tossed the microphone to the outside. His attention was diverted, and the commissioner saw his chance to strike. Backing into the ropes, Commissioner Travis began to charge Scott Slugger, his forearm held out for a mighty lariat from hell. It struck it's target, and Scott Slugger nearly moved from where he stood. Slugger raised an eyebrow and looked down at the commissioner with an amused glance on his face. Commissioner Travis began to back up... but Slugger grabbed him by the collar and brought him closer. Scott Slugger slapped him in the head a few times, ignoring the punches that the commissioner delivered to his chest. Slugger then scooped the commissioner up and dropped him to the mat in one simple toss. Slugger went for a relaxed a cover, and there was not even an attempt to kick out as the three count was made. Slugger stood and looked down at the commissioner, shaking his head in disgust. He then retrieved his championship belt and began to walk up the entrance ramp... but suddenly the lights went out. Slugger stood still in the darkness, wondering what was about to occur. Fireworks blazed from the top of the ramp and the three masked men who had been tormenting Scott Slugger for many weeks stood facing him. They began to pull off their masks, but then the largest of the three stopped them, and pointed silently down at Slugger. He was handed a microphone by one of his partners, and said only one word: "Friday." Slugger cursed and shouted up at them as the face-off continued, and the cameras slowly went off the air signaling the end of this week's edition of Wednesday Warfare... Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall
