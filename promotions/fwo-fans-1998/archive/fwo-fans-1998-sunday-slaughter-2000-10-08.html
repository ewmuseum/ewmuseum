fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 8 October 2000

8 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The Edmonton Coliseum braced itself for the huge fans Wrestling organization show that was about to take place. Tonight, Ice would face The Flying Frenchie, as the two former World Champions would collide. Primetime & Mitch Wilson would team up for the first time in quite sometime (unwillingly), to take on Mike Bear & Wretch in what could be called a match between the two most successful tag teams in the fWo's history. And later, Wretch would get his rematch for the title he lost last week on Slaughter. The fWo World Title. It would be another match in the endless series of battles between he and Wilson, and for only the second time, would the top prize in the sport be on the line. Sunday Night Slaughter went live inside the arena, as fireworks and screeching music prepared the restless fans for what was looking to be an entertaining night of fWo action. The acting owner of the fWo was not present, as James Armstrong had meetings that required his presence. Instead, calling the shots backstage and steering the ship would be former fWo World Champion and current Color Commentator Stevie Roberts, and at his side would be fWo Columnist and Overall Pain In The Ass Dick Venom. On cue, we spot the two walking down a hall backstage, and neither one looks too pleased. Venom does have a swagger and confidence in his eyes, while Roberts appears to be in a very bad mood. "Dick, why do you have to be such a... dick, baby?" Roberts curiously asked his colleague. Venom smirked. "Because that's what I am." Roberts nodded, and Venom didn't appear to take it personally, or realize it was an insult towards himself. "All I know is, Stevie, I've been here just as long as you have, and the only stroke you've had around here lately is some 'Survivor' contest that you did a very poor job of running, while last week I was buddying up to the acting head honcho. And that's why I've got more pull than you do here tonight, baby." Roberts scoffed at this statement. "Pull? The only pull you'll be doing tonight is removing my foot from your ass, baby, yeah!" Roberts & Venom came face to face in the hallway, before they began laughing, and hugged briefly. They both shouted "Swerve!" loudly as they continued to laugh, while everyone in the area looked at the two if they were buffoons. Roberts & Venom then shook hands, before Venom asked Roberts if he had anything specific he wanted to do here tonight. Roberts thought about it, before smiling. "Yeah, baby, all the women, especially that Clarisse chick baby yeah!" Venom looked shocked at Roberts' wishes, before informing Roberts that she was about to be married to Crusader. "She wouldn't be wanting to marry that twit if she had taken a ride on the magic dragon, baby, yeah!" Venom laughed. "I know what you mean... I'd like to get my hands on Pamela Dee, myself." Roberts agreed. The duo continued to exchange sophmoric sexual overtones before Roberts asked Venom if there was anything he'd like to do regarding the card. "Yeah. Since we have Mitch & Wretch tonight, and so it doesn't get old, and being that I used to write for the Philadelphia indy promotion back east... and you know, we were the innovators over there. That said, we're gonna do some innovating tonight, baby, with a capital I." Roberts raised an eyebrow as they reached their office for the evening. "Yeah, baby?" Venom nodded. "We're going to have a four boxes of doom match!" Roberts laughed. "A what, baby?" Venom got more excited. "We're going to have a four boxes of doom match. That's right. And we're going to put the World Title in one of those boxes... while the other boxes will be full of weapons for Mitch & Wretch to use!" Roberts started to get into the idea. "Yeah, baby, yeah! That match will rule!" Venom joined in. "With a capital R!" The office door shut behind them, as Slaughter went elsewhere... I'm Hardcore. No, I'm Hardcore. No... Cameras cut to backstage, where we see Robert Jensen walking down the hallway, intently reading an "Inside fWo" magazine. Oblivious to his surroundings, he's taken by surprise when Frank Snow abruptly runs in to him, bringing both of them crashing down to the floor. "Hey, watch where the hell you're going!" Frank bellows. "Really? Where was that 'heads up' attitude when took us out of Meltdown by letting Strangler pitch you over the top?" Jensen retorts. "Oh... like 'Mr. Hardcore' here's never cost a win... you were so 'hardcore' that it took a bulldog from Law to put you out for the three count at Unstable... I'm SO impressed." Snow, sneers at Jensen while he picks himself up. "You know what, Snow? Get yourself a partner to make it even, and I'll SHOW you how hardcore I am." Jensen replies."The only reason I'll need a partner is to keep me from breaking your neck, Jensen. But just to make it fair, get yourself someone to tag with too... that way there'll be SOMEONE on the other team I can carry to a watchable match." Snow spits out venomously. "Fine then... just try and remember that your Snowfall is supposed to be a move, not a porn movie snippet." Jensen shoots back, now standing, as he brushes some dirt off his pants. "Why you..." And with that, referees mercifully step between the two, preventing an all-out brawl. Former Foundation vs. Ex-Underground With the fireworks and what not out of the way, Slaughter begins with a huge tag team match, pitting the two teams who very well may be the best tag teams in fWo history against each other. The fans erupt with cheers as "Dragula" begins playing and Mike Bear appears on the entrance ramp. Bear stops and waits for his partner Wretch, who appears to sustained cheers as "Paint It Black" hits. He joins Bear on the entrance ramp, before the two friends turned enemies turned allies walk to the ring together. They both enter the ring and await their opponents. "Snap" begins to play, and the cheers immediately turn to boos as Primetime appears on the entrance ramp. Unlike Bear, he simply continues on to the ring, not waiting for his partner. Said partner appears to even louder boos as "Last Cup Of Sorrow" starts up. The fWo World Champion Mitch Wilson appears on the entryway, World Championship strapped around his belt. Wilson takes a moment to sneer, polishes the belt, then walks to the ring, looking generally angry, as is usual. He unstraps the belt and hands it to the timekeeper, before climbing into the ring. The bell rings, and Primetime and Wretch start for their teams. Primetime charges at Wretch, who ducks his attempted clothesline. As Primetime comes off the ropes, Wretch leaps high, wraps his legs around Primetime's head, and takes him down with a frankensteiner. Wretch quickly covers, but only gets two. He brings Primetime up and chops him hard, before leaping high and dropkicking him, knocking him into the corner. Wretch charges in and blasts Primetime with a leaping leg lariat, causing him to stumble out of the corner, before falling flat on his face. Wretch heads to his corner and tags in Mike Bear, who charges into the ring and attempts an elbowdrop. Primetime rolls away, and quickly kips up. Bear is unfazed, but Primetime is already up as he gets up, and Primetime clocks him one with a hard left, before kicking him in the stomach, double underhooking him, and planting him with a butterfly suplex. Primetime quickly heads to his corner and tags in Mitch, who mounts the second rope and flies off with an elbowdrop to the throat of Bear. Mitch covers, but only gets two. He quickly brings Bear up and shoves him into the corner, before unloading with a series of stinging chops. He then brings Bear out of the corner, hooks him, and plants him with an inverted double underhook faceslam. Mitch makes the cover again, but once more Bear kicks out at two. Mitch heads over to his corner and tags in Primetime, before bringing up Bear. They double whip Bear into the ropes, before dropping him with a double flapjack. Primetime brings Bear up and whips him into the ropes, before dropping him with a powerslam. Primetime makes the cover, but Bear, showing good resilience, kicks out at two once again. Primetime brings him up again and signals that he's planning to end it. He lifts Bear up in a suplex position, before slingshotting him off the ropes. Instead of dropping down into the Snapshot, Bear somehow uses his momentum to slip out of the grasp of Primetime and land behind him. He grabs Primetime, lifts him up, and drops him with a near backdrop driver, with Primetime landing on the back of his neck as opposed to the traditional top of the head. Both men are down, and begin crawling to their corners. Primetime makes the tag first, before flopping down in the corner. Mitch hits the ring, but Bear tags out to Wretch, who comes in with fists firing away at Mitch. Wretch quickly grabs him, picks him up, and spikes him with a Wretchinoku Driver. Wretch makes the cover, but Primetime gets up and breaks it up at two. Wretch gets up and begins brawling with Primetime. Bear shakes it off as best he can and hits the ring as well to help his partner, going after Mitch. Bear brings Wilson up and lifts him onto his shoulders, before dropping him with a Whiplash as Wretch tosses Primetime from the ring. Bear is still groggy from his beating, and simply slumps down next to Mitch. Wretch sees Mitch down and heads over to him, before kicking in an arm and tossing his bandana to the crowd to a huge pop. He crosses his arms, runs the ropes, and drops the Ratings Saver Legdrop to a monstrous ovation. As Wretch pops up to soak in the adulation of the crowd before going for the pin, Bear rolls over onto Mitch. The ref, having lost track of the legal men during the brawl, drops down and makes the count. Three seconds later, the former Underground has defeated the former French Foundation. Wretch turns after hearing the bell ring, and sees the ref raising Bear's arm. He appears somewhat miffed, and begins conversing somewhat heatedly with Bear. Bear explains the situation, and Wretch finally is placated and high-fives Bear. They leave the ring as Mitch begins to get his bearings. From the outside, Primetime rolls into the ring and quickly confronts Mitch, blaming their loss on him. He shoves Mitch back, and Mitch responds with a shove of his own, before the two begin trading blows to the face. fWo security quickly hits the ring, and the fans chant for more as the two warriors struggle to get at one another. Slaughter, meanwhile, rolls on... Result: Bear & Wretch by pinfall Ending The Pain Sunday Night Slaughter returns from the set of commercial breaks to the sounds of "Switchback" by Biohazard. The reigning fWo Cruiserweight Champion - Crusader - is already walking down the ramp, and looks visably upset. He rolls into the ring and asks for a microphone, and quickly receives one. He signals to the guys in the truck to cut his music, and as his music stops, he gets right to the point. "You know, I've stayed calm for too long. Far too long. And tonight, that ends. What Greg Davis has done as of late to my fiancee is downright disgusting. We're both tired of it, and there's no way I'm going to allow him to continue to treat her like he has, and belittle her like he has. Greg, I'm not asking you to come out here - I'm TELLING you to come out here!" Moments passed, before "Eye Of The Tiger" started up, and out walked the egotistical cruiserweight himself, Greg Davis. Davis stopped on the stage, before holding a microphone to his mouth, and then showing his hand to Crusader. The cameras focused in on it, and Crusader saw what was on his hand on the SlaughterScreen. Clarisse' engagement ring. Crusader continued to grow upset, as Davis laughed on the stage. "Hey Cru - remember THIS?" Crusader looked at "The Ego" sternly, before not wasting any time in getting to his point. "Greg, I'm sick of your attitude. You come in with all this hype - and your actions thus far have been repulsive. And it's time that it came to a stop. I'm challenging you to a match at Halloween Hell..." Davis laughed. "What, you want to lose again? So be it. I accept. Nothing pleases me more than kicking your tail around the ring... well, except your fiancee, that is." That comment broke the camels back. Crusader came out onto the floor and slowly walked up the stage, but stopped about halfway. "Not just any match will do, Greg. There's going to be a couple of stipulations." Davis smiled. "Whatever makes you happy, Cru." "The first stipulation, is that this fWo Cruiserweight Championship will be on the line." Davis nodded, as Crusader continued. "The second stipulation is, the match will be a ladder match." Davis again nodded, and Crusader again, continued. "And the ring that is on your finger, will also play a part in the match." Greg laughed. "That's fine. I don't care how you want to do it, the end result will be the same. Me on top... just like last night at the hotel with Clarisse..." Crusader appeared to be very upset, but resisted the temptation to go up to the stage and give Davis a pounding. "How our match will work is like this. That ring, and this title, will be hanging above the ring. Whoever reaches the championship, will be the champion - no questions asked. But if you reach that ring first, Clarisse will be your valet for the rest of this year... *and* next." Greg laughed. "Hey, that's what she wants, so it's about damn time you give her what she wants. But if you get to that ring first, Cru? Then what?" Crusader pointed at Davis. "Then Clarisse is no longer your valet, and you can never be physically near her again." Davis scoffed at this, before realizing that all he really wanted was the Cruiserweight Title. "Okay, okay. You've got a deal... but I don't think Clarisse is going to be happy about this decision. Then again - you NEVER make her happy." Davis smiled at Crusader, who couldn't resist temptation anymore. He stormed to the stage and leapt at Davis, knocking him to the steel ramp. Davis got up quickly, and ran to the back as fast as he could, with Crusader in hot pursuit... Slaughter went to a commercial, with a third match apparently agreed upon for Halloween Hell. The Stump Squad Is Annoying, Neener Neener Neener Slaughter returned, this time in the catering area behind the curtains of the Edmonton Coliseum. Frank Snow walked over to The Red Dragon, who appeared to be drinking an energy drink, and looking for something to snack on. "Come on, Dragon, you're going to be my partner." Dragon backed away, pushing Snow' arm away from him. "Why did you attack the Stump Squad?" Snow rolled his eyes. "Because they are so annoying, okay? They annoy the hell out of me, and worst part of all - they're not hardcore. You? You're hardcore... so come on, you haven't wrestled for awhile anyways - you can be my partner." Dragon resisted, but reluctantly went with Snow to the ring, while elsewhere... High Profile? Boxing promoter "Don Static" knocked on the door of "Lavender Holyfield", who was obviously still Jackhammer despite the lavender colored attire. Lavender hadn't been seen on fWo television since his "debut" at Unstable. Lavender sat on a chair, before welcoming his visitor into his dressing room. Upon seeing Static (who was still Sphere despite his best attempts to impersonate Don King), he groaned. Static however... had a plan. "Come on, Lavender! Come on! I've found you a match for tonight... yes, and it's high profile." Lavender shook his head no. "What? How dare you defy my magicalisism. I tell you, you haven't been in this high profilosity of a match since... well, I don't know when - but... this match could put you into that International House Of Pancakes Division... and come on, let's be realalisticalistic, you could defeat the champion of that division easily!" Finaly, after some prodding, Lavender gave in. "This better be high profile, or I'll bite your ear off." Static laughed at the comment, before realizing Lavender was serious. He shrugged it off and the two headed to the ring.. with Robert Jensen at their side. Frank Snow & The Red Dragon vs. Robert Jensen & Lavender Holyfield The fans at ringside turned their attention to the stage, where Frank Snow & The Red Dragon walked out. The fans didn't know how to react to either of them, but booed Snow because he felt the Stump Squad were annoying, while they didn't. A few moments passed before Jensen came out, but Holyfield wasn't at his side. Instead, we went to a shot on the SlaughterScreen of Holyfield backstage, waiting for his entourage to gather around him to accompany him to the ring. Jensen waited on the stage for Holyfield to come out, but after he saw about fifty people around him with no signs of the entourage' arrival stopping, Jensen charged the ring. Jensen & Snow went at it, while Dragon watched the match from the ring apron, with shots of the entourage' arrival placed in through out of it. Finally, Holyfield got sick of it, and pushed the seventy or so people out of the way, before walking to the ring with Static at his side. His trainer and his entourage tried to catch up from behind. Snow sent Jensen to the ropes and ducked for a backdrop, but Jensen hit a double arm DDT, and then both men were out of it after a grueling four or five minute battle. Holyfield entered the ring and brought Jensen to his feet and hitting a jumping powerbomb. He then rolled Snow atop him, before exiting the ring. The referee counted to three, while Static looked at Holyfield increduously. Holyfield and Static argued as they went backstage, while Slaughter went to a commercial... Result: Snow & Dragon by pinfall Jon Spratt (c) vs. Erik Kelly A roar of Result: Kelly by disqualification Fault Slaughter went backstage once again to a shot of Primetime standing next to fWo Interviewer & Camera Woman Kristen Jacobs. Primetime looked off camera, as if he had something on his mind, while Jacobs introduced herself, and then Primetime. "I'm Kristen Jacobs, and tonight, Primetime, we saw you fall defeat to the hands of Mike Bear & Wretch. Your comments on that?" Primetime scoffed. "Let me tell you something, Kristen. That loss, tonight, wasn't my fault. That loss was Mitch' fault. Just like it was his fault the last time we lost the tag titles when we were champions. June 11, 1999. He and I in the ring as a team against Scott Slugger & Ice, who just happens to be his... ahem, friend. Who was it that got pinned? He was. He felt victim to the World's Greatest Asshole, when just months later it was ME that defeated that same asshole for the fWo World Title. And then tonight... whose fault was it? It was his. He got pinned. Well, tonight he has a big title match against Wretch, and I'll be damned if I'm going to allow him to lose that match. Because on October 28, he and I and that French piece of crap are going to collide in the ring... and if he's not the champion, it's not a title match. Wretch, it's not personal... but tonight, I hope Mitch kicks your ass. I'm willing to help him. And if he can't, then he's going to feel a "snap" come October 28th. And when he finds himself in the hospital - it will again be HIS fault." Primetime pushed Jacobs out of his way, before heading off to his locker room, while Slaughter went to the ring... Crusader & Ruben Ross vs. Juno Law & "The Ego" Greg Davis Result: Ross & Crusader by pinfall The Flying Frenchie vs. Ice Sunday Slaughter returns to the sounds of "Don't Tread On Me" by the Damn Yankees. The blonde machine known as Ice steps out on the ramp. Ice continues on, and KMFDM's "Juke Joint Jezebel" starts up, as the fans rise to their feet. The Frenchie shoots his trademark beam, and then runs to the ring. Ice charges with a clothesline, but Frenchie ducks. TFF turns Ice around and drops him with a quick DDT. Frenchie covers, but at two ice lifts up a shoulder. TFF brings Ice to his feet and sends him into the ropes. Ice ducks a backhand chop, and returns with a shouldertackle. Ice brings Frenchie to his feet, and nails a ring shaking powerslam, before covering. At two, Frenchie kicks out, but Ice lifts him up and remains on the attack. Ice nails a few chops backing Frenchie into the corner. He perches the Frenchie on the top rope, and attempts a superplex, but Frenchie blocks with punches to the ribs. He pushes Ice off the top, and then flies off with a big elbow drop that connects. A quick cover, and again at two Ice can kick out. Frenchie brigns Ice up in position for a samoan drop, but Ice gets out of it. He hooks TFF in a waistlock, but Frenchie nails a few elbows and turns it into a go behind for himself. He lifts Ice up and nails a german suplex with a bridge. One, two, and Ice can kick out. Both men quickly to their feet, and Frenchie goes for a lariat. Ice ducks, and then drops him with a quick belly to back suplex. He drapes an arm across Frenchie, and TFF kicks out at two again. Ice brings TFF to his feet into a death valley driver position, but TFF gets out of it. He runs Ice into the ropes, and then rolls him up. At two, Ice powers out, and Frenchie nails the the ropes but comes back with an elbow to the head followed by a diamond cutter. Frenchie covers, and Ice kicks out. Frenchie nails a little scoop slam, and then heads to the top. He flies off looking for a 450 splash, but Ice moves at the right second. Frenchie smacks the mat, and Ice brings him to his feet. Facebuster for Frenchie, and then Ice quickly brings him up. A release german suplex does the trick, and then Ice heads to the top. He flies off with the headbutt and connects. He makes the cover. One. Two. No. Frenchie kicks out somehow at the last possible moment, and Ice stands up, but immedaitely falls back down, clutching at his leg. TFF is down, and the referee begins to wonder about what has happened to Ice. He begins to motion for medical attention for Ice, but Frenchie awakens, assesses the situation, and climbs on Ice with the cover. The three count is made, and as Frenchie celebrates, EMT's attach a little brace to stabilize Ice's leg, as they take him off to the back on a stretcher. Meanwhile, Sunday Slaughter leaves to commercial with confusion on the status of Ice. Result: Frenchie by pinfall Warning Tonight's Slaughter returned from the commercial break & a preview for Halloween Hell to the dressing room of Mike Bear. Kristen Jacobs stood outside, before the door opened, and Bear looked at Jacobs. Bear walked out, and shook hands with Jacobs, before Jacobs introduced the segment. "Kristen Jacobs once again backstage from Slaughter, and Mike, I was wondering if you had heard the comments of Primetime just moments ago." Bear nodded, and as the microphone was raised near his mouth, began to speak. "Primetime, you're nothing but a little punk. And although it appears there's some tension between I and Wretch because of what happened earlier tonight, I will not allow you to cost Wretch that match later tonight. You can talk about your snaps, you can talk about your crackles, and you can talk about your pops - but we all know Rice Krispies sucks, and Beer and Cheerios combine to make the breakfast of champions. And since I consume that very breakfast each and every morning - I want to be a true champion - and face Wretch and defeat him for the fWo World Title come October 28th... tonight Primetime - the only ass kicked will be yours if you stick your nose in where it doesn't belong in that match... that, I promise you." Slaughter went elsewhere... Severe Injury? Backstage, Mitch Wilson joined his best friend near an ambulance. Ice was clearly in pain, but wished Mitch luck on his big match coming up shortly. Mitch thanked him, and the two knocked their close fists together, before Mitch said that tonight's match was for Ice. Ice laughed, before telling Mitch it sounded like he heard a snap. Mitch looked furious, and as several workers loaded The Big Blonde Machine into the ambulance, Ice & Mitch said goodbye, before Mitch told Ice he'd come to the hospital later. Ice nodded, and as the ambulance sped away, Mitch turned around, and saw a passerby. The Flying Frenchie. Quickly remembering that it was The Flying Frenchie who was wrestling Ice when the injury occured, Mitch snapped, but not like Ice' leg did moments ago. Mitch clutched his fWo World Title and charged at his former Foundation stablemate, nailing him with it. He then stood over Frenchie and stomped him visciously, before referees and security arrived to hold him back. Frenchie got to his feet, and he and Wilson exchanged words, while Frenchie held his face, checking to see if a cut had opened. It had, and blood slowly crept out of his skin. Not caring, Frenchie charged, but the masse of security was too much to overcome. Two dozen or so personalities stood between the two men, before a third man joined them. Primetime. Coming up from behind Mitch, he spun him around, and nailed him with a punch, Wilson responded with one of his own, and then Frenchie joined in after pushing people aside. The three men who were once the best of friends continued to go at it, while more security arrived to separate the three men. Medical personnel also arrived to check out the Frenchie, and after further inspection, insisted he go see a doctor for the wound that Wilson had inflicted upon him. Although reluctant, Frenchie left the arena after the commotion had subsided to seek a doctor's help... Damnation (c) vs. The California Kings vs. Rateings R' Us Result: Damnation by pinfall "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson (c) vs. Wretch Slaughter heads to the ring for its Main Event, a Four Boxes Of Doom match as booked by Dick Venom. "Paint It Black" begins playing and the fans pop huge for the challenger, Wretch. Wretch walks to the ring, soaking in the cheers, and pats one of the crate-like boxes at ringside, testing its strength. Finally, he rolls into the ring and awaits his opponent. "Last Cup Of Sorrow" starts up, and the boos cascade down on fWo World Champion Mitch Wilson, who makes his way to the ring without the belt around his waist due to its being in a box. He quickly steps into the ring, and the ref signals for the bell. Mitch and Wretch lock up, and Mitch gains the advantage with a knee to the gut. Mitch grabs Wretch and whips him into the ropes, but Wretch reverses it. As Mitch comes back off, Wretch catches him and back body drops him over the top rope to the outside where he lands with a splat. Wretch rolls to the outside as well and starts going after one of the boxes. He winds up to punch through the box, but from behind Mitch grabs his arm and, catching him off guard, whips him into the guardrail. Mitch then charges at Wretch and clotheslines him into the crowd, before stepping over the guardrail himself. Mitch and Wretch begin brawling in the crowd, and Wretch manages to gain the advantage. He grabs one of the fans' chairs, causing the fan in it to comically fall on his backside, and whacks Mitch with the chair. Sensing an opportunity, Wretch sprints back to ringside, vaults over the guardrail, and approaches the first box. He winds up and punches it, shattering it. Inside is... A lead pipe. Wretch recoils, sneers, then finally shrugs and grabs it. Mitch Wilson meanwhile has recovered and is making his way over the guardrail. Wretch walks over and blasts him with the pipe. Mitch slumps to the floor, and Wretch nails him a few more times for good measure. He then walks to the other side of the ring to open up another box. He reaches one and tries to punch through it, but he misjudges and his fist glances off it, only cracking it. As he winds up to punch it again, Mitch comes from behind him, wielding the steel steps. Mitch taps him on the shoulder, and Wretch turns, only to eat the steps. Mitch kicks the box halfheartedly, and the box falls open. Inside are... Bun cake and a stack of Finn's old pornos. Mitch smirks, then leaves the box for another. He begins working on the suspiciously long next box, electing to open it manually rather than punching through, and from behind him, a recovered Wretch has the bun cake and is ready to charge. He sprints at Mitch, bun cake at the ready, but Mitch senses him coming and at the last moment spins, hooks his head, and drops him with a Headliner, driving Wretch's face into the bun cake. With a "Wow... this stuff actually is pretty good...", Wretch's head slumps to the floor. Mitch grabs him and tosses him into the ring, before bringing him up and shoving him into the corner. Mitch then unloads with a series of chops. He brings Wretch out and whips him across again, but Wretch reverses and Mitch hits the corner hard. He stumbles out, and Wretch hooks him before planting him with a Wretchinoku Driver. Wretch heads up to the top, and measures Mitch. Mitch however, gets up and falls back into the ropes. Wretch is crotched, and Mitch shakes it off and heads to the top. He hooks Wretch around the midsection, then drops him to the mat with a gutwrench superplex. Mitch heads to the outside and begins working on the box again, but he's still having trouble. A good amount of time passes as he does this, and he never sees a more or less recovered Wretch come up behind him and drop him with a Depression. He rolls Mitch back into the ring, before kicking away at the long box. Finally, it shatters, and inside is... A surfboard. Wretch chuckles, before grabbing the surfboard and rolling into the ring. Mitch is just getting up, and with a mighty yell of "Surf's up, Dude!", Wretch charges at Mitch with the board. Mitch sees him coming and pulls the ref into his path, and the ref takes the brunt of the board. Mitch takes quite a lot of the blow too however, and both men are down. Wretch begins heading to the outside, realizing that the final box contains the World Title. From the crowd though comes Primetime, who spins him around, kicks him, grabs the surfboard, and blasts him with it, knocking him over the top. Realizing that only made Wretchy closer to the fourth box, he prepares to go out and grab him, when suddenly Mike Bear hits the ring and they start going at it. Wretch begins crawling over to the box, coming ever closer. Mitch is groggily getting up, when Primetime finally gets Bear out of his way for a moment, and rolls to the outside. Wretch is reaching for the fourth box, but Primetime kicks him away and grabs the box. He opens it, finding the World Title. He throws it in the ring, but it falls a few feet short of Mitch. Bear meanwhile rolls to the outside and clocks Primetime with the discarded surfboard, then grabs Wretch and rolls him into the ring. Primetime gets up and begins exchanging fists with Bear, who gets the upperhand. He grabs Primetime and disposes of him over the guardrail into the crowd. He turns and sees the struggle in the ring between Wretch & Mitch, both of whom have their hands on ends of the title, and are basically having a tug of war for it. Bear decides he'll help Wretch out and grabs a chair. He comes in and absolutely clobbers Mitch with it. Mitch, however, holds on to his end of the belt... Wrenching it from Wretch's grasp. The referee is coming to at the same time, sees Wilson with the belt, and calls for the bell. Wretch is quite perturbed, and looks at Bear increduously, as Bear more or less cost him the match. The referee raises the arm of a dazed Wilson, and the only one not in a state of shock is the recovering Primetime, who is all smiles walking backwards up the ramp, knowing that he'll receive his shot at Halloween Hell, as Slaughter goes off the air.
