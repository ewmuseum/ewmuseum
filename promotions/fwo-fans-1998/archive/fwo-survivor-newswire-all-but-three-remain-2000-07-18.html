All But Three Remain

fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE ALL BUT THREE REMAIN July 18, 2000 With the dawning of a new day came the next three competitors in the fWo Survivor contest. This batch represented the seventh, eighth, and ninth wrestlers chosen for the event, and of this writing only three remain. Stevie Roberts explained more in a small press conferences outside fWo Headquarters in Redmond, Washington. "Nine wrestlers have now been announced, three remain. Our last three competitors are bound to surprise a few people as well, and we expect to have those names announced before close of business today." To the layman, this meant that by 8PM EST the full roster of the Survivor contest would be officially known. A girl watching the announcement had much to say about what the Survivor contest meant to her. "I think that this is very cool. We are seeing the real new blood of wrestling right here in the Survivor contest. Sure I like several of the wrestlers in the fWo roster, but this is a whole new line of men out to prove they are the best. Other companies might pretend to push the new talent in wrestling, but the fWo proves it. I can't wait to see these people in the ring." Ring action is near. It was also announced today that the first fWo Survivor event will be held in a venue that many fWo fans are quite familiar with: the Key Arena in Seattle, Washington. Stevie Roberts explained the choice of this arena for Survivor, "The Key [Arena] is like home to many of us. What better way to debut Survivor than in the arena that is so near to all of our hearts? Obviously we aren't going to sell out the arena, but ticket sales at a reduced rate have been brisk as many people want to see what the next generation of fWo wrestler is going to look like." Today three of this next generation made their first appearance in an fWo environment. Nick Allstar goes by the name "Mr. MVP". He has a controversial attitude, but is determined to show his ring savvy in the biggest and brightest possible environment. Reno Davis is not a stranger to the wrestling world, and his trademark red jacket was present when his name was announced in the earlier press conference. Davis had several fans in attendance for the press conference, and seemed to enjoy the attention. Finally, Suicide was introduced, and looked slightly nervous but hopeful at his prospects of surviving the contest and moving up to a permanent spot on the roster. All three men looked excited, and anxious, to get to work proving themselves.
