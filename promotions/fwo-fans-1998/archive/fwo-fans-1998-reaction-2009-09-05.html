Krow stared hypnotically at the small Standard Television screen propped upon a lone crate in the darkest regions of the backstage area. Below it was an old VCR which held the tape of last week’s show. And while the show disinterested the Hardcore Champion, there was one portion of the night that remained attractive, almost exciting, to his otherwise stoic attitude. It was the very moment he had taken a lead pipe and sent it careening across the skull of his most hated rival, and his most recent ally. Steven Shadows. Krow didn’t smile and his face remained expressionless. His eyes simply remained fixated on the lone Television light that shined brightly in his face, cutting through the darkness that surrounded him. But through the darkness came a monstrous and tanned hand, that comfortably laid upon Krow’s shoulder. The surprise appearance did not irk the Satanic God, but gained his slight attention. ”What is it, Joshua?” Krow calmly asked without removing his eyes from his point of interest. “You’re disrupting me.” Joshua “Paladin” Listerin sat upon a nearby chair and looked frustratingly at his chosen ally. Momentarily, the legendary CWL competitor stared a hole through the Satanic God (one that was not returned) and soon looked over towards the television screen, wincing slightly at the scene of Graphic Violence having his skull nearly cracked in two by a vicious shot to the head via weaponry. Listerin lowered his gaze and shook his head. ”I suppose you’re proud of what your actions,” Listerin stated stoically. He lifted his gaze towards Krow and received no audible response. “Well, I can’t say that I necessarily am.” Krow did not respond, and thus Paladin continued. ”I understand that you aren’t used to a group dynamic, Krow, so allow me to explain the predicament that you’ve placed us in,” Paladin pulled himself up, his irritation level growing slightly more. “We’ve angered quite a few people in the FWO thus far; Alias, Karina, Ivy, Beaven, Chronos, and Frenchie…just to name a few.” Paladin paused in search of some sort of retort from his deranged ally, but received none. He pugnaciously continued to watch the scene of Graphic Violence’s demise. ”Now, with that many enemies looming over us, just waiting to attack, we need as much help as we can get. Now, I know that Steven Shadows wasn’t necessarily the best choice we could have made for a third man in our small army – but he was strong and he was willing to put everything on the line for our cause. If I had asked him to, he would’ve stabbed a man in the heart on live television – just to send a message. He was infuriated enough at this company to do anything with a little bit of push,” Paladin glanced over at the television screen, and scoffed at the replay of last week’s attack. “But thanks to your actions, Krow, we’ve lost a strong ally. We’ve lost one of the toughest men in FWO’s history; and for what: because you had an insatiable urge to end the man’s career; or because you just couldn’t put your heated rivalry on hold, just for a few more months, to achieve some miraculous? I thought that a man of your caliber would have more self constraint than that.” Krow put the tape on pause. Paladin’s eyes quickly shifted towards Krow, who shot a cold glare towards the Legend. “It wasn’t my self constraint that led to Shadows’ demise, Joshua,” Krow stated matter-of-factly as he turned his gaze back towards the screen and pointed towards Shadows’ visual lifeless body. “It was the lack of his. Steven Shadows was a cancer to our mission, Joshua. It was one of the main reasons this company turned their heads towards the limitless possibilities of promoting him higher. It was also one of the main reasons he could never capture the one championship that took me only a few short months to claim. The FWO World Championship.” Krow played the tape once more, watching it intently. “Our army is stronger now than it ever has been before. We now have two men who are dedicated to a common goal and are willing to put it all on the line to achieve it. There is nothing standing in our path now; no babysitting, no weak link…nothing.” ”Nothing?” Paladin quickly interrupted. “Is that what you honestly think?” ”That’s right.” ”You’re delusional,” Paladin reached into the pocket of his thousand dollar sports jacket and retrieved a piece of paper, presenting it to an uninterested Krow. “I’ll assume you haven’t read the card for tonight, yet.” ”I haven’t.” ”Well, allow me to educate you, my friend. It seems that tonight, the punishment for our wrongdoings has begun. The company has placed you in a Hardcore Championship bout…a Handicap match to precise,” Paladin looked towards the stoic Krow, and grew annoyed at the lack of his interest. “It seems William Beaven has found a loophole and is making it so that the title you’ve held for over four months now can be lost in such a match.” Krow ignored Paladin’s revelation and continued to watch the footage of Shadows’ demise. “Aren’t you concerned in the slightest?” Paladin queried. Krow paused the footage. He sharply leaned in closer to the screen as his eyes narrowed curiously. “What? What is it?” Paladin questioned concernedly. Krow let out a deep sigh and a sly smirk. ”Do you recall that haunting call from last week, Joshua?” Krow asked. ”Yes, why?” Krow chuckled amusingly as he pointed his pale finger at a certain ‘being’ on the screen; one that went unseen last week – but one that seemed to hide itself behind a certain van in the parking lot. Paladin’s eyes narrowed curiously as he, too, leaned in. ”No, I’m not concerned about my opponents tonight, Joshua. But what does concern me…,” Krow chuckled again, pointing playfully at the figure now noticed. “Is this masked man.” "Is that..." Krow chuckled and interrupted Paladin's inquisition.
