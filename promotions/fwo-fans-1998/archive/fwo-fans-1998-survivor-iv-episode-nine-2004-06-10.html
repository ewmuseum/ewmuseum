Survivor IV: Episode Nine, 10 June 2004

10 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The merge is on the horizon... but which team member will be last to go before the all-important merge? Choices, choices...whether he wanted to decide or not The longer the man known as Omar Christensen stayed within these confides of the fans wrestling organization's stucco walls, the more his surroundings bothered him psychologically. For Osyrus especially; he didn't think that these rookies in the fourth installment of the Survivor contest could possibly understand where he was coming from. Not when he entered the ring, cutting promos with such confidence and charisma that was evidently missing on the fWo main roster; not when 'the personification of talent' failed to release a submission hold after five seconds, pushing his adversary past their limits as Osyrus' hand was raised in victory moments later. This was particularly the case when 'the Anti-social hero' was roaming around the backstage halls, looking to bum a smoke and instead Osyrus was bombarded by pleasantries from his fellow contestants. All this friendliness and good cheer made Omar Christensen nauseous as he continued to sit in an unfolded steel chair, inhaling the nicotine from the Newport into his deteriorating lungs. Osyrus knew bullshit when he could smell it, and the members of Team Gold and Silver reeked of the scent. They would try to use him and his awe-inspiring abilities to move ahead, just like the San Diego native would do to them, when the opportunity presented itself. But still, fWo was like the Twilight Zone compared to what Omar experienced. The locker room areas were in immaculate condition, floors and walls so clean you could eat off them. Not like in theAsylum, where Joe Campbell didn't give a fuck if the floors were stained a putrid yellowish tint, blood almost everywhere visible to the naked eye and insects so strange looking that you would assume it was some sort of new species. Omar could also remember back in his Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling days how he and the boys backstage would rib the fWo to no end. Christensen would sit behind the scenes watching a monitor as Sean Studd and Trey Vincent cut the most outrageous promos, they broke kayfabe so much and often, you would imagine a war between the two promotions would escalate at any moment. But it never came amongst all the heated hatred for all involved. In a matter of months, Osyrus would enter the world of a federation he despised so much, that some would think he was a fucking traitor. All the times Omar said time and time again that he would never sellout and join the fWo, but obviously things had changed. Success override a man prides as he tried to prove himself to the masses of smarks in the wrestling industry, which were not familiar with the moniker of Osyrus. As Omar put out his lit cigarette on the spotless ground, he contemplated just how could the man who was once known as one of PIW's future rising stars...could also be known as the current fWo Survivor 4 champion. Hell, the fWo Survivor champion Osyrus had a nice ring to it, which made his current decision a very hard one indeed. What team did Osyrus want to join? To 'the personification of talent' it was like choosing between Aids and Flesh eating bacteria, not wanting in any part of something that had already failed miserably without his involvement. Why did Omar Christensen have to join these factions that had no drive or desire? Un-like Stephen Robert's Team Black who couldn't wait to get into the fWo to make an immediate impact. "Fuck this choosing shit; I don't want to be on either one of those loser teams." Osyrus replied as he kicked his former seat into a near by wall. A few moments later, cooler heads prevailed as 'Asar' thought to himself, while lighting up his third cigarette before he started to chuckle. "...That's fucking ingenious," Osyrus snarled in amusement as he withdrew his cell-phone from his pocket and dialed up Matt Rivers' telephone number. "Hey Daze, I know what team I'm going to," Osyrus waited for Rivers' reply as there was no audio in the locker room. "Let's just say that I need to a bit of research first, and then I'll tell you guys all about it." Happy Happy Happy! Joy Joy Joy! "Jah! I am in ze match! At last!" Doctor Curiosity literally bounced around the room with joy. He didn't know much about his partner or his opponent, but he knew that he would have the chance to show his stuff in the ring again, and surely come out on top. After all, who or what could defeat a man of his genius? Don't say Logic, Christian Elijah, or any of those other guys that have beaten him thus far. Eegor stood in the corner, watching his master dance around like a lunatic. Curiosity didn't see the figure watching through the opened door, but Eegor did. It was Neko Case. Curiosity's partner for the week was standing there, watching every move that the doctor made. "Jah! I vill carry Neko Case to glory! I vill use my moves and my skills... und my skills and my moves! Jah, zis vill be fantastic!" "Doctor" interject Eegor. "I think you should..." "NEIN! I vill not be lectured to be a deformed man!" Curiosity looked his assistant up and down, and spotted no deformity. "It's in a private place, but believe me it's there." The doctor nodded, and unseen to him again, the figure of Neko Case left the doorway and went back to wherever it was that he liked to spend his time. He would take with him an opinion of the doctor's idiocy that would be reinforced heavily when they were to step in the ring. For now though, the doctor couldn't really have cared less. He was in a match! "Come, Eegor. Ve must maketh plans and hoodvink ze entire audience..." Choices, choices...whether he wanted to decide or not (Part 2) If you want information, you have to go directly to the source. And that's exactly what our 'Anti-social hero' intended to receive. Osyrus stood outside the entry way of the Team Gold locker area, posted against the wall with his lit Newport still in his mouth as he ease dropped nonchalantly. On the other side of the door; Osyrus could hear the voices of Rana Venonosa, the man he fought last week and defeated successfully while smirking to himself. Well, he didn't technically win but it was a victory in the book of the man known as 'Asar' or prince, if you weren't familiar with Egyptian words. If anyone thought that the Osyrus gave a shit about anyone else in this contest, they had better do their research again because the San Diego native was willing to do whatever it took so that he was the Survivor champion when the contest was over. Injuring each member of the Gold and Silver teams, until there were only his associates left, then it could be decided by Survivor's best of the best. Standing only a mere foot from the individuals inside, Omar could understand why the Silver Team was defeating the Gold in almost every event; it was because of their stupid bickering. Osyrus stood there for what seemed like hours, yet the Gold team only argued and complained, which surely would result in another voted off team mate...and middle school bitch-fest bored the wrestler/fighter greatly as he yawned. Unlike when theAsylum fighter stole ideas from guys like the supposed "Thinking Man's grappler" Logic, another Osyrus wannabe, Christensen could notice that the Silver Team wanted the victory more...at least more than the Gold. Suddenly the arguing picked up in volume as 'Asar' put his ear back on the door and heard the voice of the big mouth rookie, Brookes. Omar took his ear off the door and just shook his head in the disgust. "What a fucking poser that Brookes guy was," as Osyrus reminisced while thinking to him-self about the promo Brookes gave before 'the personification of talent' arrived on the scene. If this guy was supposed to be a tough, Osyrus had better leave the contest now before he shat himself due to laughter. "This is exactly what is wrong with the fWo right here, guys with no merit think that they can stand toe-to-toe with an individual as good as I. Do these little curtain jerks honestly believe they are in the same league as a former multiple time world champion, a hall of famer and a nasty bastard...that would rip their head off of their fucking shoulders, and shit down their throats just because it would amuse me? ...New flash mother fuckers, you're looking at the toughest Survivor member in history right here." Osyrus snarled. 'Asar' continued to smoke as his spying garnered less importance. Somehow killing everyone off would not get the former ACW champion as far as he would hope or as it should. Omar needed a definite solution to his problem, because Osyrus had to make sure that he was one of the final contestants in this competition...or he would be voted off sooner rather than later. But that would require him to do something that he dreaded, throwing his Newport down the hall, swallowing what was left of his pride. If he wanted to win, then Omar Christensen had to encircle himself with winners, if you can call the ridiculous looking Dr. Curiosity with that fake stupid German accent, not to mention his more ludicrous assistant 'winners'. Regardless of the facts, it was time for Osyrus to make a decision, now traveling down the hallway, abruptly banging on the Team Gold locker room door as he left cackling. This was going to turn out bad, no matter how many different ways Omar scrutinized the situation. Foolish Pride. The scene faded to backstage, where we find the leader of the Gold Team, Rana Venenosa walking down the hallways behind the Disney sound stage that houses fWo's Survivor IV. His step was a little bit less chipper than it usually was, as he'd felt his first taste of the indifference of the wrestling world last week on live TV. He'd stopped walking, and looked up at the door in front of him. In true Survivor IV fashion, there was no name on the door. Rana looked up at the ceiling and said a few words of prayer as he knocked on the door. "If you got tits, take your shirt off... if you ain't got tits, get away from my door." It wasn't an angry greeting. Rather, a greeting that was meant completely honestly. The kind of greeting that would only ceom from the man Rana was looking for. Rana breathed deeply, and knocked again as he spoke, "Brookes, I just need a second, man. I've had some things on my mind all week..." The sound inside the room could only be described as a half hearted laugh, and a little bit of a snort, as though Brookes Davidson couldn't possibly imagine the nerve of Rana after last week's stunt. Rana looked down at his shoes and sighed deeply. He knew he was in for it tonight, but it was one he actually deserved. There was a soft click as Brookes turned the knob, and the door cracked open slightly. Rana pushed through the door, and watched as Davidson walked across the room, away from the door and plopped down onto the couch. He reclined the seat he was in, and crossed his legs as they came to just above his heart in the recliner. His fingers locked behind his head as he shrugged his shoulders as if to say, 'So... whatcha got for me?' "Brookes, I wanted to apologize for last week... apologize for..." "Stealing my match? Ruining my chances at this competition? Not showing me the respect in the ring that I deserve...?" Brookes fired the questions off at a rapid fire, keeping Rana off balance. "All of those things... though I'm not sure I'm responsible for you not winning this comp---" "No, I don't think that's why you're sorry, Matt," came the interruption from Brookes, "In fact, I'm sure that's not why you're sorry." Rana and Brookes Davidson were not friends. Not even close to friends. Rana closed his eyes in aggravation as Brookes used his first name on television. As he opened them, he shot a glare at Brookes. "Then why am I here, Brookes? If I wasn't sorry, why would I have come here?" "Oh, no. You're sorry, alright. You're sorry that you got fucking punked out by some Villem Ender wannabe. You're sorry that your back is going to be sore for the next 4 months because of that roided out freak. You're sorry that you didn't make any real friends in this competition... because the Nerd and the Fanboy coudn't hack it... so you didn't have any help out there. You're sorry that you didn't send me in there because if you HAD, Osyrus wouldn't have had a little ragdoll to toss around like it was his bitch," Brookes smiled as he watched Rana's eyes search the room for something to look at. He loved making these kids uncomfortable. As much as he hated to admit it, most of what Brookes had said was true. Brutally honest, and not said in a very poetic way, but still valid. This was one of those times that Rana was going to have to take his tongue lashing. Once again, he sighed hard. "Maybe he wouldn't have done that... or even tried that if you'd been out there," Venenosa replied with absolutely no conviction in his voice, "And like I said, I'm sorry. It won't happen again." Suddenly, it dawned on Brookes, "This is about the Black Team, isn't it? You're scared of getting sent home all of the sudden, huh? You thought maybe they'd be your boys when they showed up, huh? Now you see what kind of people they are... and they remind you of ME, don't they?" Rana left a little bit of air out of his nose as he bit his lower lip, "They'll be on you, too, Brookes. There's more to this game than foolish pride. I'm swallowing mine here, and I hope that at some point you learn to swallow yours for long enough to do the right thing." Brookes listened with interest now. Watching silently as Rana turned to leave the room. As the door clicked shut, Davidson's recliner was pulled into it's original position. "Heh... never thought I'd agree with something that little masked fuck said..." Bantam Diablo & Osyrus vs. Neko Case & Doctor Curiosity The scene had shifted back to ringside and the fans were anxious for this match-up and fittingly so as it pitted some of the top stars from the industry in an anti-spirited tag team match up if you will. The lights had began to darken and suddenly Doctor Curiosity waltzed on out, "Hermann Loves Pauline" playing as he slowly made his way to the ring. The smarks jeered due to the limited wrestling ability the good doctor had, or was it just the fact that he's a blatant cheat? Meh, who knows, but they jeered alright. Doc entered the ring and started to stretch his old bones out whilst at the same time "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC blasted over the loudspeakers and the rose up and gave a heartedly cheer for supposed Silver team leader in Bantam Diablo. BD made his way to ringside and smirked at the good doctor and then leaped up on the apron and flipped into the ring showcasing his acrobatic skills. The fans cheered and he raised his arms up high in return. The crowd quieted down a little bit and it was then that the sounds of "Ice Cold Man" by Probot serenaded the loudspeakers and the lights began to go into a bit of a flashy state. Then Osyrus was seen as he walked through the curtains to a mixed reaction. Osyrus strutted down to the ring with his head held high in confidence unless it was just a stick stuck up his ass that held his head up so high. One will never know, unless of course you were that man to have stuck a stick up Osyrus' ass, and if you were, let me speak for all of us in saying "We don't want to know." Anyhoo, back to the match and stuff... Osyrus took his place in his corner with parter Bantam Diablo as they stared down the good ol' Doctor. The fans were all riled up and ready to get this one underway when Doc's partner Neko Case stepped out of the curtains with "Same Direction" by Hoobastank blasting over the loudspeakers. Neko ran down to ringside and slid in the ring and started walking up to his tag partner in Doc when he suddenly made a darted u-turn and ran over to the corner of Osyrus and BD and leaped up with a dropkick of sorts that got a kick in on both of the combatants. BD and Osyrus fell off the apron and onto the floor but it wasn't enough impact to keep them down as they quickly got up and "This Life" by Primer 55 blasted over the loudspeakers "Golden Bear" Steven Windsor, the big fuck that he is came running down to ringside sporting his zebra outfit and slid in the ring and called for the bell as this match had officially began. Team Silver's chance to impress the 'new guys' had begun. BD and Osyrus got up quickly and back up onto the apron as Neko grabbed Bantam and slung him over the ropes and into the ring. Bantam quickly got to his feet and then reached back and slapped Osyrus. Osyrus looked at him confused but got in the ring and locked up with Neko. On the outside, the masked luchadore was happier. Fighting Neko Case didn't look like any fun at all. Osyrus quickly got the advantage and threw Neko to the mat, he then jumped down himself and grabbed hold of Neko's arm and started with a wrench leg lock maneuver on his arm. Neko struggled to get free and then gave a few jabs to side of Osyrus' legs that finally were enough to make Osyrus release the hold. Neko jumped up to his feet and Osyrus was soon to follow. Case looked at his opponent, and then to his partner for the evening. It was time to see if this bumbling fool was actually worthy of being in the ring at all. Could he survive against the likes of Osyrus? The tag was made.....as it was on the other end by Bantam Diablo again as Osyrus went back to his corner to prepare to maim the doc. Bantam Diablo ran in the ring like a giddy schoolgirl, and began putting the boots to Curiosity. He was going to win! All by himself. Well...he'd give Osyrus credit for the assist. There was a slight problem with that theory, however. Osyrus wanted back in the match. *SLAP!* *THUD* The slap was Osyrus delivering an open handed slap to Bantam's back to tag himself in. The thud, well...self explanatory, isn't it? Bantam Diablo was sprawled on the mat, as Osyrus entered the ring. Doctor Curiosity decided to let loose with the big guns, and go for his best move. Pleading for Osyrus not to kill him. Osyrus rubbed his hands together, and advanced on the Doctor. Curiosity, however, had plan B. Namely, a vial of powder thrown in Osyrus' face. Unfortunately, Osyrus closed his eyes, stepped through the crowd, and locked a hand around the doctor's throat. Chokeslam....but Osyrus held on. ANOTHER chokeslam...and Osyrus still held on. Thankfully, Osyrus simply threw Curiosity back into the Diablo/Osyrus corner this time. Osyrus tagged out, and ordered his partner to go up top. After several moments of explaining that he was team leader, Bantam saw Osyrus' angry face and listened. Osyrus threw Doctor Curiosity to the ground, and quickly slammed Bantam off the top onto Curiosity. A cover, as it were. ONE! TWO! Somehow, someway, Curiosity actually kicked out. And Bantam Diablo was alone in the ring with Doctor Curiosity. Osyrus sighed. Curiosity crawled. Diablo crawled after him. Curiosity was faster. Neko Case was tagged. Bantam Diablo cried. Bantam Diablo got Dragon Suplexed by Neko Case. Suplex, Neko, Suplex! Diablo was sad. Another Dragon Suplex made him sadder. Case covered, eyes rolling. This match was obviously over. ONE! TWO!! SAVE BY OSYRUS! The Anti-Social Hero decided that losing would be a bad thing, and so saved his partner. Doctor Curiosity rolled outside the ring, as Bantam made a beeline for Osyrus. NOW he wanted Osyrus in the ring. On the floor, Curiosity decided new tactics were in order. Idiot. Doctor Curiosity stepped through the ropes and charged Osyrus. Of all the foolish tactics, this was about the pinnacle. He stopped forward motion the minute Osyrus moves to the side and grabbed him with his thick arms. The grappler wasn't using an impact move though, he was just getting in a clinch; one he used to effortlessly move around the bemused Doctor, before lifting him in to an over the shoulder fireman's carry. What was going through the doctor's mind is up for question, but a mere two seconds later it was the mat, as Osyrus ran across the ring and nailed the Reckless Abandon II. Curiosity was piledriven in to the mat at pace, and whatever brains resided in the skull were shaken up. Osyrus was tempted to go for the pin there and then, but he wanted more of Neko Case. He looked like a challenge, unlike this wretch of a man who tried to rush someone twice his size. SLAP! Osyrus looked round to see that as he had taken a few steps back to admire his handiwork, Bantam Diablo had stretched through the ropes and gotten a blind tag. The luchadore dives in to the action, and ran over to where Curiosity lay. Possible moves were flowing through his brain so fast that it was a good ten seconds before he ran back to the turnbuckles and ascended them like a man born to it. GENERIC CRUISERWEIGHT FINISHER~! ... RAISED KNEES~! Osyrus almost had his head in hands there and then. Was this masked fool going to be this bad the whole time? The answer wasn't slow in appearing as Doctor Curiosity got up and helped Diablo to his feet. BD was about thank the mad scientist when he was met with a kick to the gut and a DDT. Curiosity, feeling he'd done enough work for the day, tagged a distinctly unimpressed Neko Case back in. Case put away thoughts of strangling his own partner, and concentrated on taking apart Bantam Diablo. The masked high flyer was brought to his feet and held at arms length whilst being given a series of snapping kicks to the side. A thrust kick to the ribs that Diablo had just landed on, and Case was in control of the match. A series of hard chops to the chest was followed by an arm drag and then an arm bar. Diablo scrambled for the ropes, but Case was cutting the ring off effectively, and there was no escape for the moment. After yanking hard on the bar, Case released the hold and unleashed a series of knee strikes to the arm and shoulder of his opponent. Methodically disabling BD's arms and weakening them for his Divine Providence submission hold, Case continued with the assault by jumping in to the air and landing with a hard knee drop across the shoulder of his foe. Diablo groaned, the match not going as he wished it to, and then he was whipped hard to the ropes. The generic cruiserweight instinct cut in quickly and he swing his legs between the ropes, coming back in a controlled run, ducking under the clutches of Neko Case and... ...and... ...running off to tag Osyrus! The anti-social hero entered the ring and looked hard at Neko Case. This man would prove an interesting challenge, even though he would surely fall eventually. Whilst Osyrus was walking towards Case and locking up with him, the other anti-social person in the ring, Steven Windsor, was getting bored. Quite why he didn't get to participate in the match was still unclear to him. It was as if the team was trying to lose, putting the two clowns of the team in the ring instead of someone who could have posed a threat to their newest members. Case tried to avoid getting crushed as Osyrus moved in on him and brought his massive arms to bear. The sizeable weight and height difference was going to count against him, but the speed advantage could tilt the balance of power back in his favour. Case chanced a swinging kick to the side, but Osyrus blocked it. The larger man even tried to make a grab for the leg and affect a takedown, but Case darted out of the way again and quickly feinted a thrust kick . Osyrus was taken enough off balance for Case to move in with a strong chop and then a strike to the stomach. It didn't seem to faze Osyrus much, but then an arm grabbing his head and swinging him in to a Tornado DDT seemed to do the job, and somewhat wake the crowd up. In fact, it wasn't just the crowd yelling, but also Doctor Curiosity. It caught Neko Case unawares, and he turned to see his partner for the night yelling instructions to him. Whether it was to go for a specific body part, general encouragement or just a desire to be tagged back in, Case didn't know. He never found out either, as Osyrus recovered quickly enough to take advantage. Grabbing Case by the head and neck, Osyrus proceeded with a fisherman's suplex. With barely a second of hesitation he turned at the moment of impact and had Case up and over again, this time with a T-bone suplex, before The Outlaw Star could even consider a counter. Osyurs went for a pin on the prone body of Case. ONE! ... SHOULDER UP! A long count of one was all that referee Steven Windsor counter. This seemed to anger Osyrus slightly, as the Golden Bear appeared to have taken his time with the pinfall. The split second of hesitation before Osyrus drew Neko Case up was all the agile star needed to slide from the grasp of the largest man in the contest and execute a swift go-behind. One arm swiftly coiled around the neck of Osyrus, and dropped him down hard to the mat with a Reverse DDT. Unable to utilize his full array of suplexes on Osyrus, Case knew he would have to use everything else to pull out the victory for his team. As such, he waited until Osyrus was starting to get back up before running off the ropes, rolling down on the floor and then springing up to catch the head of his opponent and drive it right back down where it came from. Osyrus hit the mat with a colossal smack, and Case rolled away from him easily. Doctor Curiosity's pleadings had escalated a notch now, and he was literally begging to be let back in the match. Case decided to give him one last chance to prove himself, and held out an arm to tag in his partner. The doctor entered the ring, more cautious this time, even though Osyrus looked sore from Case's last couple of moves. The doc smiled and pulled out a set of brass knucks from the recesses of his endlessly useful pockets. He drew back his right fist and prepared to clock Osyrus, when fate intervened. More correctly, Steven Windsor intervened, easily catching the arm of Curiosity and stopping it in flight. The knucks were quickly removed and thrown from the ring. Without Eegor there to act as a distraction, the doctor was going to have to be more subtle about his illegal antics... especially as Osyrus looked full awake. The doctor's eyes grew as wide as saucers, and he stood there motionless as Osyrus grabbed him and threw him to the ropes. SLAP! There was a noise before Curiosity returned, but whatever it was didn't matter to Osyrus, as he lifted the doctor as if for a spinebuster, but then transferred his weight in to a sit out powerbomb. Osyrus admired his efforts, before realizing what the noise was. Bantam Diablo had tagged himself in. Yes, again. For Case and Osyrus it was the final straw. They looked at Curiosity and Diablo in the ring, and decided at almost the exact same time that it was time to end this circus performance. They were here to wrestle, not to take part in some sort of carnival sideshow. It was Osyrus that moved first, or possibly Neko Case. Hard to tell, but they were both angry, and the objects of their anger were about to be introduced to pain. Case reached Curiosity first. The doctor was spun around quickly and kicked squarely in the gut. His arms were both hooked, and he was lifted up in to the Nexus Crisis. It was one high impact move too far for the doc, and he was done. Bantam Diablo was in a similar situation, having just been on the receiving end of the burning hammer variant known fittingly as Oblivion. Above him stood Osyrus, looking every inch the monster. The two members of the Black Team had made their presence felt, and they had shown Team Silver a thing or two about wrestling. Or so they thought. Having taken apart a man each they were off guard enough to let the very unexpected happen. Steven Windsor, the referee for the night had decided to take matters in to his own hands. Sure, the rules stated that a ref isn't supposed to assault people, but they'd just taken out their own partners, so some leeway was surely there. Besides, who was going to disqualify him? Windsor charged Osyrus first, and a might clothesline sent Bantam Diablo's destroyer over the top rope and crashing to the floor below. Neko Case had turned his back and was looking to leave the ring until he heard the huge cheer that accompanied this most unexpected of events. He spun quickly, but Windsor was already in full flight and he smashed in to Case with sufficient force to send him, too, over the top rope and down to the cold, hard floor. The cheers that accompanied this had to be the largest of the night. Whether it was the action itself, or the people that were involved in it, the competitors themselves certainly didn't know. The two that were awake enough to make a choice did so with ease. For Neko Case and Osyrus, this match was over. Catcalls and boos accompanied them as they turned and walked up the ramp and towards the backstage area. If the ref was going to act like this, then they were in no position to hang around in this mockery of a match. Windsor smiled as he looked around, and then realized that he had two severely hampered wrestlers on his hands. In fact, from the look of them they might actually be dead. If he didn't want the Survivor bosses coming down and just eliminating him regardless, he was going to need to get this match going again. A double count out didn't seem like the best way for it to finish. First off was Curiosity. He was dragged to one corner, and then Diablo to the opposite one. Leaning them against the turnbuckles and giving each a quick slap seemed to knock some life back into them. The good doctor was eventually back to his feet, staggering around the ring somewhat comically and looking around wondering where the hell the two mean bastards wandered off to. His eyes eventually struck upon his one remaining opponent and instinct took over again. He was still the legal man, and the job of the legal man is to pin the opposition. So be it. Bantam Diablo was busy wondering if anyone had taken down the license plate number of the truck that had just hit him. To say his brains were scrambled would be more than a minor understatement. Another hand grabbed him, maybe a savior, and pulled him roughly to his feet. The eyes of the masked man flickered upon and he saw none other than Doctor Curiosity facing him. Then the world went upside-down and he hit the canvas, the victim of a suplex. You weren't supposed to suplex the team leader, thought BD. This wasn't right! So as Curiosity moved in for the kill, BD summoned strength from God knows where and rolled to his feet. Before the doctor could react, BD hit a Spinning Heel Kick on the good doctor. The crowd yelled their appreciation as Diablo's fist pumped the air. Curiosity wasn't staying down, so Diablo got momentum from the ropes and ran back to hit a big facecrusher! He covered the sluggish doctor. ONE! TWO! NO! There was somehow still fight left in the body of Doctor Curiosity. For all the abuse he had taken, the good doctor was showing why he had taken Logic to the limits, and why he had entered this tournament with such an impressive pedigree. He was damn hard to put down for the count of three. A mid-rope hop into a spinning headscissors takedown prompted another ovation for Bantam Diablo, and his chance to finish this strange match off for good. Steven Windsor watched in amusement, expecting the worst as BD flew from the top rope, his body forming a perfect frog splash pose as he crashed down with the... GENERIC CRUISERWEIGHT FINISHER~! It hit Curiosity full on, but the impact jarred Diablo clear of his target. His mind seemed to be like so much mush, and his limbs made of lead as he tried to crawl across and make the cover. Just a couple of feet began to stretch out like a desert, an infinity of space before he could find his goal. He began to wonder if he should call a taxi, or at least stop for a refreshing beverage, when his target was within reach. The arm reached out towards Curiosity, when a sound went up throughout the crowd. It started off as a murmur, but gathered momentum, and a life of its own. Dreams of glory flashed through the mind of BD, with the crowd bowing in adoration to their all-conquering hero. They loved him, they really loved him. With his arm a scant inch above Curiosity's chest, Diablo noticed that nobody in the crowd was looking at him any more.
