Sunday Slaughter, 7 January 2001

7 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Over the past few months, the office occupied at each show by the person running the fWo has been a place of infamy and deeds both stupid and pathetic. In short, a room of bun cake and blowjobs. But tonight it was clear that that had changed. The walls of the office were tastefully decorated with art from the likes of Thomas Kincaid, and in the middle of the room sat a large desk of polished mahogany. A computer monitor sat on top of it, and no doubt a top-of-the-line computer was hidden under the desk as opposed to what had become tradition to hide under the desk, a shapely blonde. A television set showing Slaughter sat next to the monitor. Folders and papers were neatly arranged on the corner of the desk. A large, high-backed, leather chair sat behind the large structure. It suddenly swiveled around, revealing none other than fWo owner, honcho, and big cheese Chris Universal, stylishly dressed in an Armani suit, hand-painted Brioni tie, and slacks. Universal smiled, and addressed the crowd. "Welcome to Sunday Night Slaughter, live from Tokyo, Japan!" The assembled crowd could be heard cheering, and no-one doubted that Universal had learned well from his mentor the art of the cheap pop. He adjusted his tie, then continued. "In January, the second annual Body Count will take place. Last year's event was won by some jackass or other, but that's not important. In case anyone has forgotten, the rules of Body Count stipulate that the person who has the most pinfalls at the end of the match will obtain a World Title shot at CyberSlam. Thus, it is an advantage to enter early, as that will mean you have more chances to gain pinfalls. As a result, I have come to the following decision. Tonight, there is an elimination-style Number One Contender match between the Boston Strangler, Mitch Wilson, Primetime, and Wretch. The first person eliminated in this match will enter twentieth in the thirty-man event. The person who is eliminated second will enter tenth. And the person who takes the final elimination will be the first person to enter Body Count!" The crowd's cheers could be heard once again, as they knew that the main event they would witness tonight had even more importance placed on it than before. "With that in mind, please, enjoy tonight's Slaughter... if you buy... what the Sellout... is selling!" The cheers continued, and Slaughter cut to its opening video. After the video finished, loud fireworks exploded inside the legendary Tokyo Dome, and the fans cheered loudly, having not seen an fWo event in over a year. Cameras then headed down to the ring for the first match of the show. Damnation vs. Chaos "Paranoid" by Black Sabbath gave way to the arrival of the Dark Riders, Stone leads the duo of War & Plague to the ring. Their arrival draws massive boos from the crowd inside the Tokyo Dome. Just as they were set in the ring ready for battle, but then "From this Day" by Machinehead signaled the arrival of Hellrazor and Fear, Damnation, the world's favorite Heineken drinking duo and current fWo Tag-team Champions, whose belts will not be on the line in this match. Grapple over the head of Plague, and Hellrazor drops down with a bulldog. Hellrazor flips Plague on his back, and the count starts One... two... no! Hellrazor grabs onto Plague and pulls him over to then Damnation corner, then reaches back and tags the big man Fear in. Fear enters the ring, and instantly he pulls him to his feet, he lifts Plague up for a powerbomb, but Plague suddenly sparks into action with fists of fury to the face of Fear, the big man is now on shaky ground, and Plague follows with a hurricanrana! Plague looks over to War ready to make the tag, but decides against it and flips Fear on his faceside, he walks and drops applying the ankle lock submission hold! But the hold has basically no effect, as Fear grabs onto the bottom rope. Plague maintains the hold until the referee breaks it, Plague gets up and exclaims "BItcHEs". Plague turns and goes to the corner to make the tag, Fear who's now up follows and nails him with a double axe handle but when he hits it, Plague completes the tag with War. War's in and he nails an unsuspecting Fear with a fearsome clothesline. Plague heads up top and leaps off with a moonsault onto Fear! This draws Hellrazor out, as 'Razor pushes past the referee and straight to War. He nails him with rights and lefts, but they seem to little effect War, as he reaches down and grabs 'Razor for a double handed chokeslam. As he lifts him up, Hellrazor pushes himself off with both his feet as he kicks the chest of War. Both men fall to the mat, with 'Razor getting up first out of nowhere Plague launches himself towards Hellrazor, and then 'Razor slumps down causing Plague to slam into the turnbuckles. Hellrazor then gets up and grabs Plague up quickly dismissing him to ringside. But Hellrazor's attention is drawn back to War, who viciously scratches 'Razor's back. Hellrazor writhes in pain, as War follows up with an Inverted DDT! He goes for the pin, but receives a boot to the back of the head by Fear. Fear brings War to his feet, and sends him in for the ropes clothesline swing by Fear but it's ducked by War who goes for a neckbreaker, but it's reversed by Fear who swings it back around... and then he nails a boot to his abdomen doubling War over. Fear goes back against the rope, and nails a swinging neckbreaker! Hellrazor is back to his senses and he goes over to the fallen War, he applies a Single Leg Boston Crab, and War seems to be ready to tap but Stone takes the opportunity to jump to the apron and jaw with the official. The official misses, and for some reason doesn't even feel the pounding of War on the mat. And then he misses Plague who re enters the ring, and plasters Fear, and then Hellrazor with the steel chair. Plague slides it out of the ring, and Stone drops to ringside. Plague gets onto Fear making the cover and hooking the leg along with it, the official checks and counts... One... two... thre... NO! The crowd cheers loudly as Fear gets his shoulder off the mat. War & Plague come out with a huge win, against the tag-champions. Result: Chaos by pinfall Almost Fired Chris Universal clicked his mouse, deftly navigating the Internet. On screen, the browser screen displayed a message that it could not "find server". Universal cursed softly under his breath. "Heh... money can buy you a lot, but it can't buy a damn computer that works." As he clicked the reload button, the door to his office opened. In stepped Juno Law, looking less than pleased. Law approached Universal and slammed his hands down on the desk. "One week, and you already have a match booked for me that's handicap? What's up your ass?" demanded Law. Universal looked up from the obstinate computer and gestured to Law, before speaking in a mildly sarcastic voice. "Please. Come in." Law pressed on, slightly annoyed at Universal's laid-back attitude. "I want answers, Universal. I don't know you very well, but I know of you, and I already don't like you." Universal sighed slightly at the comment, and explained to Juno his take on the situation. "Well, Juno, when you took out Abusah and Scot Hartt last week, they were... less than pleased. Have you ever had Abusah angrily shout at you? You think he's illiterate normally... but anyway. They demanded the match, and the choice between getting yelled at by that goof and booking the match wasn't exactly hard. If you want a partner, fine. Get one. If you can't find anybody to team with, it'll stay a handicap match." Law looked somewhat pleased at this new turn of events, but still studied Universal with an angry glare. Universal returned it in kind. "As far as your dislike of me, big fat hairy deal. You work for me, in case you've forgotten. If you have problems with that, I can easily leave you here. I'm sure you could get a deal with NJPW or NOAH or something." Universal looked with ambivalence at Law. "Your choice." Law snorted in disgust, then angrily shoved a few of Universal's folders off his desk before storming out, slamming the door behind him. Universal calmly reached down and replaced the folders, before turning his attention back to the computer. "Cannot find server, my ass." Crusader vs. 'The Ego' Greg Davis "Switchback" by Biohazard brought out the gravity-defying Crusader, who entered the ring and posed on the turnbuckles for the cheering Japanese crowd. "Eye of the Tiger" by Survivor brought the fans to a fair amount of boos, as 'The Ego' Greg Davis strutted down to the ring with an arrogant on his face. Before wiping his feet off on the ring apron, Greg called the referee over to hold the ropes open and reluctantly, the referee complied. As Crusader stretched his arms out and tested the ropes, Greg flashed his arrogant smile across the Tokyo Dome and warmly accepted the jeers he received. After letting Davis bask in his moment of pre-match glory, Crusader grinned and cuffed his opponent upside the head. Instantly infuriated, Davis jolted his head around and immediately went for a collar-and-elbow tie-up as the bell rang. Greg managed to push knock Crusader down from the grapple and charged towards his adversary, but Crusader thought a step ahead and whipped Greg over with an arm-drag takeover. Greg charged forward again with a clothesline, but this time Crusader ducked it and responded with a dropkick pinpointed to the chest area. Davis was knocked down, but he saved himself some trouble by going to the outside. The referee held Crusader back to allow Greg to gain his composure, and soon Greg rolled back into the ring. Another lock-up ensued, this time with Crusader taking over with a side headlock. Davis pressed Crusader against the ropes and then shot him off to the other side. Greg leapfrogged Crusader and managed to take him down with a drop-toe-hold on the second time back, following through with a waistlock. Crusader tried different ways of reversing the situation, but finally opted to reach back and take Davis over with the snapmare. Crusader ran towards the ropes and hit a seated dropkick right to the chops of Greg Davis. Greg managed to get up from the move, but was put right back down with a spinning heel kick by Crusader, followed by an Asai moonsault. The crowd cheered the move as Crusader went for the cover. Davis managed to get out at two, but Crusader kept the action going by picking up Greg and throwing him into the ropes. Crusader telegraphed a backdrop and Greg took advantage of the situation with a rocker dropper. The crowd reacted with awe, as Davis loaded his foot for a superkick. Crusader ducked Greg's foot but Greg managed to take him down with a hard lariat clothesline. With Crusader down, Greg took a small moment to play to the crowd... and then resumed his in-ring offense. Greg picked up Crusader and dropped him down on his knee with a hard backbreaker. Crusader fell down to the mat, and Greg took the time now to head up to the top rope. Greg flew off with an elbow drop, but Crusader moved away, causing Greg to land straight on the mat. When Greg reached his feet, Crusader kicked him in the gut and set up for a powerbomb. Cameras were brought out as Crusader brought him up, but Greg managed to slide down Crusader back and roll him over for the pin. The referee counted two, but three was just barely missed. As the two reached a vertical base, Greg speared Crusader towards the corner, but Crusader at the last moment got his feet up on both ropes split-legged style and took Greg over with the same type of pinning-attempt. Greg managed to roll out of it, but then broke the chain of technical wrestling as he began viciously stomping away at Crusader, with his face reddening with anger. Greg fiercely whipped Crusader into the corner, but Crusader reversed it and followed in with a flying hip check that found its mark on Greg. Crusader smoothly landed on the ring apron, as Greg groggily made his way to his feet. What followed was a 360 springboard Thesz press by Crusader, which the fans in Japan reacted to with great delight. Crusader held on for the cover, but only got a two-count for his efforts. After hitting a snap suplex, Crusader decided to end the match by going up top and signaling for Gravity 101. Greg Davis got up just in time to trip Crusader to prevent the move from being executed. Greg followed up to the top and attempted a Davis DDT, but Crusader blocked it and pushed Greg off. Crusader then flew off with a hurricanrana that found its mark well on Greg Davis, and he held on for the cover. What Crusader didn't see, however, was Lance Star walking down the ramp with a bottle of Sprite in-hand and the Cruiserweight Title around his waist. The referee distracted by recording the count which now reached two and Lance spat a reasonable amount of Sprite into the eyes of Crusader to cause him to break up the pinning attempt. Crusader was on his knees trying to get the second-rate beverage out of his eyes, but Davis took advantage and schoolboyed him for the pin. Greg celebrated to the back to a chorus of boos after winning his match. After the match, Star entered the ring and began verbally assaulting Crusader. As Lance took a swig of his Sprite, however, Crusader retaliated with a hard right hand, causing the Sprite to spill in Lance's face. Lance tried gaining his vision back, but Crusader gave him no time, as he booted him in the mid-section and delivered the Flipside to a large reaction from the crowd. Standing in the middle of the ring with Lance knocked out, Crusader received a fair ovation, despite losing on this night. But tonight, you can get him back, although I doubt you will." Before Carnage could continue, a second voice cried out from the darkness. "H-h-hellllllp!" "SHUT UP!" "All you have to do... is pin me tonight. That's IT! You pin me, you get Toad back, MUD. But... I DON'T THINK YOU CAN DO IT!" The knife was covered in blood, and some dripped off the point. Carnage stared at the camera, seeming to be looking through it and perhaps into the eyes of MUD himself... then licked the blood off the knife. The cameraman made a noise of disgust, and Carnage cackled, a chilling sound. "HahahahahaHAHAahaHAhaHAHAHA!" Carnage's face withdrew from the camera, and all was dark again... Black Panther (c) vs. Havok Slaughter rolls back from commercials to shots of a packed Tokyo Dome here in Japan. The crowd settles in, and "Du Hast" by Rammstein starts up, and the fWo Internet Champion Black Panther steps out to an arousing chorus of boos, seeing as he's a comrade of the most hated man in the fWo, Primetime. Panther thinks nothing of what the Tokyo fans are saying to him, and instead just continues on to the ring for this grudge match. Then, a little countdown plays, and at zero, Godsmack's "Awake" kicks in, and Havok enters into view. Havok's career has been reawakened, and tonight will be his first match back. The Tokyo fans appreciate what Havok has overcome to return to the fWo rings, and he receives a great response from the crowd. Havok springboards himself into the ring, and enters with a bang by hitting a springboard dropkick to take the Panther, his former Millennium Men partner, down to the ground. Havok whips his trenchcoat off, and then lifts the Panther up, and sends him into the ropes. Panther bounces back, and Havok nails a swift elbow to the jaw which stuns the Panther, but doesn't take him down. Havok hits the ropes, and looks for a flying forearm, but Panther catches him, and nails a powerslam. Panther plays to the crowd a little bit, and then goes back over to Havok, and nails some hard right hands. He sends Havok into the corner, and then charges with a big splash, but Havok moves out of the way. Havok then uses rights and lefts, and kicks to bring Panther to a sitting position right in front of the turnbuckles. Havok then goes over to the other side, and then runs full force at Panther, nailing a sitting dropkick right to the jaw of the Panther. Havok then pulls Panther toward the middle of the ring, and covers, but the Panther kicks out at two. Havok brings the Panther up, and hooks him for a big suplex, but he can't get him over. A second try is denied with a punch to the ribs from the Panther, and then the Panther takes him over with a suplex of his own. Panther brings Havok up again, and locks on a chokehold with both hands, before lifting him into the air, with an impressive slam. Panther covers Havok, but at two, Havok kicks out. Panther brings Havok up again, and looks for an irish whip, but Havok reverses, and when Panther comes back he nails a tilt a whirl backbreaker. Havok covers quickly, but Panther kicks out with ease. Havok brings him up once more for an irish whip, but this time it's Panther who reverses. As Havok comes back, Panther back body drops him over the top rope, and Havok hits with a thud. Panther then goes to the outside after him, nailing lefts and rights and he sends Havok into the guardrail. Panther looks to charge, but Havok gets a boot up that catches Panther in the face. This stuns Panther enough for Havok to drop him onto the floor with a DDT. Havok then quickly rolls into the ring to break the count, and then Havok perches himself on the ring apron, and waits for Panther to get up. Panther finally comes to his feet, and then Havok jumps off with a plancha, but the Panther knew it was coming, and he catches Havok, and then drops him throat first across the guardrail. With Havok knocked down for the mean time, Panther goes under the ring, and pulls out a table. He sets it up on the outside, and then rolls Havok into the ring. He takes Havok to the ring apron, and grabs him by the throat, signaling a chokeslam. The referee admonishes him, but Panther pushes him away, and then delivers a chokeslam to Havok through the table on the outside. Although Panther got a great move on Havok, the referee has no choice but to disqualify him for his actions, thus making Havok's return to the fWo a winning success. As Slaughter cuts to break, we see the Panther in joy over what he has done with Havok, and a scene of Havok lying within the rubble of the table.
