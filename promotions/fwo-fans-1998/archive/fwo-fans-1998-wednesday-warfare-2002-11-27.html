Wednesday Warfare, 27 November 2002

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Previously... Through the aid of current and former fWo members, as well as the crew of the CWL, the machinations of the Board of Directors have been disrupted utterly and the fWo is finally back on track with a stable, if rusty leadership crew. In addition, a new champion was crowned. But this champion is not an active member of the fWo roster, and many wonder exactly what Machina will do now that he has won the fWo World Heavyweight Championship. In addition, how will the roster respond to Graphic Violence and Eron still being around as the two men previously had worked hard to eliminate the fWo once and for all... Lockdown "What are these? What's going on?" Terry Nodar tossed a cable to Hellrazor and nodded to the dressing room door. "Hang out in there... we're doing a lockdown of the ring. Announcement is coming from the arena, and we want to make sure everyone will be able to hear and understand what is being said." Hellrazor frowned. "Wouldn't it just make more sense to have us come to the ring or the ramp then? Why are we being asked to stay in our locker rooms?" Terry shrugged and kept walking, trying to keep a cool exterior under it all. His body was all nerves... why was he here tonight anyway? He had done all of this as a favor, but the holiday tour was about to get started and he was due to look over promotional papers in Dublin on Friday. He looked down at the clipboard he was holding, nodded and tossed it to one of the stage hands who was standing nearby. "Everyone's hooked up, wired and ready to go." Terry sighed deeply and rubbed his forehead. Now didn't that feel familiar... something about promoting in the states in front of the American animals... just nuts, the whole lot of them. "Easy baby... we're getting on that plane tonight." Terry turned and smiled. "Penny. Yeah, I suppose so. It is nice being back isn't it? Seeing all of this?" "Better now that Armstrong isn't around to leer at my ass I suppose," Penny shrugged. "All taken care of?" Terry nodded. "Yeah, and you?" Penny smiled. "Yeah. One speech and we can get on that plane to Ireland and leave all of this behind us. Again." "Yeah, I suppose so. It was nice to see it again... and something tells me we will be back here sooner or later." Terry smiled, and then looked at Penny seriously. "Be careful out there." Penny nodded. "I will." Travis walked over to the couple and shook hands with them both. "Ready?" Terry and Penny nodded, and Travis looked around. "It's funny being back here. Some good people... and a lot of new people. Dudley signed more contracts than I know what to do with. There are going to be people filtering in here over the next several months... sleeper problems we will have to deal with as they come. And then there's him..." Terry frowned. "You have security ready?" Travis nodded. "It's ready. Wrestlers in their rooms?" "They are." Travis sighed. "Then let's do it. And let's hope nobody gets killed." Welcome to the new fWo Fans got to their feet as the Wednesday Warfare music sounded, loud and explosive among the falling fireworks. The scream of air rushing from pyrotechnics filled the arena, and this, along with the urging from ring announcer Matt Panzer brought the fans alive in the arena. It was a large crowd tonight, but there were worried glances and anxious looks from members of the audience that made those around them look uncomfortable. Travis Beaven pushed the curtains at the top of the entrance ramp aside and strode slowly down the metal ramp while the music to open the show blared around him. Fans treated him with apathy; some knew him from the fWo of long ago, others remembered him from the last couple shows and the confrontation with Jim Dudley. But he wasn't a wrestler and he wasn't likely to cause any great disturbances, so he was generally regarded as being nobody worth getting excited about. Fans sat slowly, watching the man who apparently was now once again in charge of the promotion. Travis stopped at ringside and was handed a microphone from a member of the ring crew who was checking that all of the cords were properly taped down. He walked up the steel steps leading into the ring and ducked under the ropes. The crowd noise grew to a dim rumble of annoyance. Too many times the shows had opened with someone talking in the ring rather than wrestling... it was getting old. Annoying. Travis raised the microphone up to his mouth, and noticed out of the corner of his eye that the security force was slowly assembling at the top of the entrance ramp to guard the access point in from the back. He sighed. "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the fWo. No matter if you're watching at home, or here live in the arena, we appreciate your time here tonight." He paused, but this announcement gained little reaction. Very well then... "I also welcome the wrestlers of this organization, the support staff, the managers, the management... everyone who I'm proud to say works hard each and every week to make this show better. To entertain you, the fans, and to bring something special to the ring with them. This business is a business borne out of love, and it is this commitment that has made this promotion, and these people, what they are." He paced slightly, his mouth dry. He was out of touch... years on the island hadn't prepared him for any of this. "I'm going to take you back a little bit..." a groan of anger at this from the crowd and then, "...to how this organization first began, and some of the ideas behind it. Jim Dudley came out here and talked about how things had gone astray, and in a way he had a point. The business had allowed for the kinds of changes that Dudley was able to bring about... changes that aren't good for the wrestlers, the employees of the fWo, or the fans who watch us each and every week. In the old days, wrestling was a little simpler. The fWo was smaller, we were able to run under the radar of a lot of the newsletters, the internet, and the egos of the highly paid wrestlers who have made this business what it is... a business that is ripe with corruption and the destruction of the ideals that wrestling was built upon. "When the fWo was small... everything was easier. But soon the egos of the highly paid wrestlers didn't have to come from the outside, because the fWo had grown to the point where we had the big names and the highly paid wrestlers. We had the egos, and it was this growth that caused the cancer that you saw in the fWo over the last couple of weeks. We built our problems from within. Then on the outside we were being told to go in every different direction from the fans... saying that the fWo was doing great, that the fWo was in a slump, that they wanted to see more stables, that there were too many stables... "Very little made sense. Well... today we try and change that. I built the fWo with the idea to make an organization that would survive the problems that plagued so many others. That I would build an organization that modeled itself after the triumphs of the CSWA, and not the problems the CWL had in the nineties. That our organization would be different... that we would be an organization the fans would be proud of. That we would be the Fans Wrestling Organization. And thus, we were... for a while." The crowd was listening, but growing more and more restless. Travis sighed. When did the audience turn from loving athletic contests to the stage hands from Jackass? "It is with that original mission in mind that I make the following announcements that concern the wrestlers watching this in the back. First, effectively immediately I want it clear that I am not assuming a leadership role in this organization. For years we have seen the promoter try to make himself part of the show. We have seen the greed and ego of the promoters push wrestling to new lows. This won't happen here. I will not be assuming a commissioner role, or a manager role, or any role that can compromise what we were set here to do... and that's entertain the fans. The leaders in this promotion will be the wrestlers, and it is with this responsibility that you will guide your own destiny. To assist you, our hard working team of fWo trainers from the development communities. Silver Fox, Samuel Smooth, Trouble and Twitch... these four men will help you to sign and book your matches, and help schedule them onto the card. If you want to kick someone's ass, you can... get your match signed, and head to the ring." Travis let the words sink in, then continued. "Second, every title in this promotion will be defended every month. You fail to defend your title after thirty days, you're stripped of the belt." This brought applause, and Travis nodded and waited for the noise to settle. "Third, we are hereby retiring the fWo Internet Championship in favor of a new title, the United States Championship, for whom the first title holder will be determined in a battle royal next Sunday. Which brings me to my next point..." The crowd noise rose. Most had read the internet and had heard the rumors. "Fourth, starting December eighth, the fWo is proud to announce the return of Sunday Slaughter to our regular, weekly lineup of shows. It is on this show that the fWo United States Champion will be determined for the first time, and it will continue to showcase some of the strongest matches in the fWo." "Fifth... fifth... I'd like to welcome to the ring, right now, our new fWo World Heavyweight Champion, and fWo Hall of Fame wrestler, Machina." The crowd rose to their feet once again, loudly stamping and cheering as the lights dimmed and Machina's music began. In spite of himself, Travis smiled. Some things were worth returning for. World Champion Machina Machina walked to the ring, the fWo World Heavyweight Championship over one shoulder. Machina passed the security guards, and smiled nervously. Machina knew why they were there, and was worried about the direction this would take. All the same, he walked to the ring, smiled widely, and patted the championship belt slung over the shoulder. Machina reached the ring, slid inside, then shook Travis' hand and waved again to the crowd. The response was huge, and smiling widely Machina walked to each corner of the ring, climbed the turnbuckle, and raised the belt high to the crowd. It was nice to be back in the ring... but it would be nicer in a few minutes in the limo ride to the airport with Terry. Travis handed Machina the microphone, then stepped back and out of the way. Machina smiled, and raised the microphone to a smiling face. "Ladies and gentlemen... boys and girls... children of all ages... welcome to Wednesday Warfare! Welcome to the fWo! Welcome your fWo World Heavyweight Champion!" The response was amazing. Machina smiled again, blushing slightly under the mask and waited for the noise to settle down. There weren't any signs of this happening any time soon though... and chants of Machina echoed through the crowd. "Easy... easy... listen, it was with a lot of pride that I won this title last week. This was a dream come true for Machina... and I only wish he could have been here to see it." The crowd was still cheering, but now confused whispers were dominating the noise. Machina smiled, patted the fWo World Heavyweight Championship, and pulled off her mask. Penny shook her hair out, looked down at the belt, the around at the stunned audience. "Many of you didn't know. There wasn't any reason for you too... but years ago there was an awful accident. I share the blame, but in the end a crazy man... a man you all thought you knew, a man all of us thought we knew... caused a horrifying tragedy. Luckily, everyone survived. Everyone, except the man who wore this mask." Penny walked over to a turnbuckle and placed the mask over the top of one, then returned to the center of the ring. The arena was deathly quiet now, and she looked over at Travis who nodded his reassurance at her to continue. "People thought it was me, because I took the mask. I took the mask and I wore it, and for a time I wrestled in it. But it wasn't for me... I'm not a wrestler and even though I wanted to honor his name, it wasn't something I could honestly do. So I went with Terry Nodar, who many of you knew in the fWo as Rudo. We helped the CWL grow, and my home has been there. Until last week. Until I finally had a chance to honor him by winning this title which means so much to so many of you out there. Which means so much to the wrestlers, the fans, and to Machina. To Ed." Clapping began around the arena, and Penny smiled, sniffed and waved to them. "Last week was a great moment for him, and one that nobody could argue that he deserved. But this week is a new week, and while we will always honor that man, this title does not belong to me. It belongs around the waist of the man who honestly last earned it and held it. It belongs around the waist of Erik Kelly." The crowd cheered, clapped, and nodded in agreement. Chants of "Machina" still chorused through the arena, and Penny waved again. The crowd wasn't so bad... and this was the right thing to do. "Erik Kelly will defend this belt with honor... he will defend this belt against many worthy competitors. People like Ruben Ross. People like the Boston Stranger. People like Brand Frontier. People like..." But then loud music cut her off, and the arena lights dimmed. The familiar blaring of "Just like you Imagined" could mean only one thing... Deeper More security flowed out of the backstage area, flanking the entrance ramp on either side. Fans looked around in confusion, and inside the ring Penny looked surprised. She glanced over at Travis, whose eyes were narrowed and was silently whispering to himself. "Not now... not now. Have some respect..." Eron walked out onto the entrance ramp, red hair dark under the dim lights and a dark leather jacket worn over his upper body. Eron was looking at the ring without blinking, a slight sneer on his face and a look in his eyes that was unmistakable in its intent. "Great contenders? I don't think so. Welcome fWo, to your past, present and future. Take a deep breath... nah, I got one better. Eron's here... and you're all fucked." Inside the ring Travis nodded to Penny, who slid out of the ring and waited by the timekeeper. Eron walked steadily down the entrance ramp, and before he had made it halfway down a commotion exploded from the curtains. The Boston Strangler was fighting through security, rage on his face. Behind him Hellrazor and Fear were pushing, struggling, and trying desperately to reach Eron... to take out their frustrations on a man who they hated and despised even before it was known he was one of the members of the Board that had worked so hard to destroy them. Eron laughed and shook his head as the security team assembled kept the wrestlers at bay. "Idiots... no wonder Jolt kicked your pathetic asses in the ratings years ago. Now... where was I?" Eron glanced back up at Travis, the sneer on his face and his eyes flashing. "Oh right... the deposit." Eron slid into the ring, gave a slight scoff at the mask hung over the ring post and walked steadily toward the owner of the fWo. "So, you're going to let the wrestlers run the show are you? You're going to let these people make the decisions? Why... so one of you can screw me over again? So one of you can run me out of the promotion again? Well surprise pal, while Jim Dudley was busy making himself champion and Cactus Jon was licking his feet I made sure that I was going to land on my feet when it was all over. You think I gave a shit about the Board and their little plan? Please... I look out for one person and one person only. Me. That's why I made sure I had a contract when it was all over. That's why I made sure you were all stuck. With me." Eron looked around the arena at the booing fans and laughed again. He turned back to Travis and lowered his voice so that only they could hear what he said next. "Someone screwed with me all those years ago, and I'm not going to rest until I find out who. And when I do..." Eron's sneer had turned into a snarl of hate, and then he turned away from Travis and addressed the crowd once more. "fWo wrestlers in the back, new and old, be warned. This is my time, my place, and if you stand in my way you will regret it. You're all served notice... here and now. Be prepared, and if you're the one I'm looking for, start running. Not that it will matter." Eron tossed the microphone down to the mat with a thud, and grinning as he left, exited out through the lower backstage entrance with a swarm of security guards surrounding him. With that, Warfare went to commercial, and the fans were left with more questions than answers. Great Dragon & Rush vs. Triality "Reclaim my Place" by Korn blasted over the PA system as Great Dragon exited from behind the curtains, Laura Hanna in tow. The two walked down the rampway, receiving cheers from the crowd. Dragon ignored the cheers, however, instead focusing on the ring, where he would soon participate in a tag team contest with a man he had defeated last week, Rush, against Victor and Carson Covington. Dragon climbed into the ring, and paced around the ring for a few seconds, before "Reclaim my Place" died down, and "Duck and Run" by Three Doors Down replaced it over the PA system. Rush exited from behind the curtains to cheers from the crowd, raising his arms into the air for a second, before he proceeded down the rampway. Rush rolled into the ring, and quickly made eye contact with Dragon. Before anything further could go on, however, "Hatred & Contempt" by Anomy cued up, signaling the arrival of Triality. Carson and Victor Covington, along with Desire, the woman that led to this match being authorized, walked down the rampway to a mixed response from the crowd. Victor and Carson hopped onto the apron, and then entered the ring. The referee quickly checked Dragon and Rush for any foreign objects, before he began checking both members of the Covingtons. With a bit of persistence, the referee succeeded to checking the two of them, and signaled for the bell, as the referee motioned for Victor Covington and Rush to leave the ring, and Dragon and Carson Covington to stay. Dragon circled around the ring, as did Carson, and the two measured each other up for a few seconds, but they reach out for a collar-elbow tie-up. Carson easily got the upperhand, and put Dragon in a headlock. Dragon, however, wouldn't allow that, and elbowed Carson in the Solar Plexus a few times, weakening his grip, and eventually getting out of the hold. Dragon propelled himself into the ropes, and Carson went for a Clothesline, but Dragon ducked underneath. Carson turned around, and Dragon was already on his way back. Carson prepared to grab him this time, but Dragon went instead for Carsons' legs, hitting a missile dropkick to Carsons' left leg, and taking down the big man to one knee. Dragon quickly kipped up, and measured Carson, before he executed a buzzsaw kick to the face of Carson. Dragon covered with a lateral press, but only got a one and a half before Carson powering out, tossing Dragon into the air. Dragon landed on his feet, however, and moved back towards Rush, tagging him in. Rush entered the ring, and watched as Carson got up. Before Carson could react, Rush kicked him in the gut, and hooked his head quickly, executing a DDT onto the canvas. Rush ran towards the turnbuckle, and quickly climbed it up, setting up for a Moonsault. Carson got up, however, and as Rush flipped off, awaited Rushs' arrival. Carson caught Rush in midair, and hooked his midsection, then drove him into the mat with a Tombstone Piledriver. Carson covered, but Rush kicked out at two, and Carson got up, frustrated. He walked over to his tag partner, and tagged him in. Victor smiled, and entered the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd. Victor lifted up Rush, and then hoisted him into the air. Victor displayed his massive strength, and then simply executed a Military Press Slam, going for the pin right after. Once again, Rush kicked out at two, and Victor shook his head in disbelief. Victor lifted Rush up, but Rush responded with a pair of elbows to the side of Victor's ribs, temporarily stunning him. Victor keeled over, and Rush underhooked both of his arms, driving his head into the mat with a Double Arm DDT. Rush ran over and tagged in Dragon, who hopped onto the top of the turnbuckle, and measured up Victor, before he dove off, hitting a corkscrew somersault Senton Bomb onto him. Dragon rolled him over, and covered him in a lateral press as the fans went wild. At two and a half, however, Victor popped up a shoulder, and some of the crowd groaned. Dragon, angered by this, grabbed Victor's legs, and entwined them into a Figure Four Leg Lock. Dragon fell onto the mat, and held onto Victor's right leg. Victor growled as Dragon added more pressure to the leg. Victor tried to withstand the submission hold, but he was slowly losing the battle. Then, Carson entered the ring, and stomp on Dragon, breaking the submission hold, and saving his partner. Rush hopped onto the top rope, and then jumped off, smacking Carson in the face with a Missile Dropkick to a pop from the crowd. Carson fell onto the mat, and rolled out of the ring, as Dragon lifted up Victor, but Victor kicked Dragon in the gut, and put his head between his legs. Victor quickly flipped him into the air, and hit a sit down powerbomb. At two and a quarter, Rush broke up the pin, however, and Victor sneered at Rush. Rush was yelled at by the referee, and exited the ring, back to the apron. Victor then lifted up Dragon, and whipped him into the ropes, then caught him in a power slam, and quickly covered with a lateral press for a two count. Victor began lifting Dragon up again, but Dragon leg sweeped Victor, taking the big man down. Dragon kipped up, and held his head for a second, before he walked over to Rush, and made the tag. Rush hopped over the top rope, and rushed into Victor with another Missile Dropkick, taking him down. It appeared as though the odds were finally going back into Rush and Dragon's favor once more, and Rush lifted Victor up, planning to finish it off. However, outside of the ring, Laura was being attacked from behind by Desire, and Dragon hopped off the apron, and walked over to Laura, pulling Desire off of her. However, as this was going on, Rush was left open. As Rush hooked his arms around Victor's waist, looking for the Exploder Suplex, Victor elbowed Rush in the face twice, forcing him to let go. Victor then lifted Rush up into the air, and hits the Dynamically Ended, a Power Slam Suplex. Victor went for the cover, as Carson slid into the ring, and three seconds later, "Hatred & Contempt" cued back up on the PA system. Dragon turned at the ring, and saw Rush downed. He paused for a second, then grabbed Laura by the arm, and took her up the rampway, backstage, not wanting to see Rush after losing the match. Triality, meanwhile, lifted each other's arms into the air to a mixed reaction, as Rush rolled out of the ring, and stared up at the rampway, sneering at his tag partner who left him to the sharks, so to speak. It wasn't over, yet. Result: Triality via Pinfall (Victor Covington pins Rush) Dead IV: Waxing the Truth "Down With the Sickness" hit the airwaves. Every person in the arena jumped to their feet, exploding in cheers. Austin Davis was still alive. The arena was pitch black; a few lighters were scattered through the arena, but nothing more. They continued to cheer in the dark, not even being able to see their hand in front of their faces. No one cared though, Austin Davis was still alive. The house lights began to come up slowly, revealing a man standing in the middle of the ring. The silhouette resembled Davis, they cheered louder. Pitch black again. Louder. Louder. Louder. They continued to cheer and sing along with Davis' theme as they stood in the dark. Then something went through the air that changed everything. Gunfire. Bang. Bang. Bang. Bang. Four rounds erupted, the cheering stopped. "Down With the Sickness" abruptly stopped. Everyone now stood in silence. "Seizure of Power" slowly came over the loudspeakers and the fans waited. The lights slowly came up. Austin Davis' body stood in the middle of the ring. His head, however, was missing. Fans throughout the arena began to vomit and retch upon each other. A sickening twist of fate had occurred in the ring, and Austin Davis was dead... again. "Nobody wanted to believe me!" An enthusiastic voice came from the entrance ramp. Donaven Winters stood there, different previous weeks. He was clean, his hair was cut, face shaved, clothing new. A microphone was gripped tightly in his right hand, while his left hand remained inside of his trench coat. "I've told all of you, time and time again over the past three weeks that I had murdered another man. Yet, you refused to believe me," Winters laughed while slowly making his way towards the ring. "You see, I told you that if I wasn't arrested that something terrible was bound to happen. And you insolent fools refused to see the truth when it looked you straight in the eyes. You were all afraid that your former champion was really dead." He slid into the ring and stood up, casually putting an arm around the beheaded Davis. "I watch all of you in this arena, throwing up all over each other, and for what? There's no blood in the ring, you will instantly believe anything you see or hear. Except for the fact that I killed Austin Davis. I can continue to prove my point... if you wish..." A coy smile crossed his face as he brought his left hand out of the coat and raised it high into the air. "I hold his head in the palm of my hands..." The head of Austin Davis hung in Winters' hand above his own head. Sickness swept the arena again. "And here all of you are... throwing up over a hunk of wax. Though, looking at this version of Austin Davis is certainly more entertaining than he ever was living. The headless Davis has more charisma than the living one ever did. And for the good of the world, they're both gone." Winters moved the wax head to his right hand and reared back, heaving it up the entrance ramp. Just in time to roll under an unnoticed man's foot. The foot came down, collapsing the wax head and smashing its face inward. A familiar voice spoke. "Just like his real head... hollow." The heads of the fans turned toward the entrance ramp as one, not believing their ears. Inside the ring, Winters gazed out to the area just beyond the curtains, and his mouth became an O of sheer surprise. Standing in an fWo arena for the first time in over half a year was Scott Slugger. Slugger looked much the same as he had in April, when he had lost to Erik Kelly and then announced his retirement. The same high brow, chiseled body, and penetrating eyes - but the cocky smirk that had long been his trademark was missing. Less than a week's worth of stubble frosted his chin and cheeks. He was dressed casually in jeans and a gray pullover sweatshirt. For a moment, all was silent, then the fans erupted in a standing ovation. Inside the ring Winters continued to stare in slack-jawed shock, the wax Davis body forgotten and lying on the mat. Slugger looked around the arena at the fans cheering wildly for him, and his previously expressionless face twitched slightly as memories stirred within. Once, this had been every night and day for him... Slugger shook his head almost imperceptibly, then lifted up an open hand. The cheering continued for a few seconds, then started to die down. When near-silence had returned, Slugger lifted the microphone to his lips. When he spoke, his voice was calm, modulated, but those listening closely could hear an icy chill in every word. "First... Winters... kid, this doesn't concern you. You're in the wrong place at the wrong time. I don't care if you killed Austin Davis. Hell, I don't care if you pulled a Trip and screwed his brains out afterwards. In fact, you might've done the world a favor. I only wish - no, I don't. But you can get back to your sick little games when I'm done. For now, I suggest that you don't do anything stupid, and just sit down and shut up. I don't think I have a lot of time to say what I have to say, because I'm not supposed to be here right now. I'm not here to wrestle or to run some stupid angle. I'm here because this is the only way I can... get my message out, let's say. Something has been going on... something that's nobody's business but mine and two other people's. One of those people I can guarantee is either here tonight or watching somewhere, and it's that person to whom I'm speaking." Slugger paused for a moment, collecting his thoughts and taking a deep breath. The crowd watched, waiting to hear what would come next, whether it was their business or not. Inside the ring, Winters seemed to be recovering from his shock. "Whoever you are... I don't know what I ever did to you to make you play these sick games. I don't know who you think you are. I don't really care either. I know that wherever you are you can hear me, so listen up. For once in your life, be a man. Grow a pair and have the courage to meet me face-to-face. What you've done... it's unforgivable, and if you have even the tiniest spark of humanity left inside your shriveled soul... I know you can't possibly live with yourself. So if you're here... Show yourself. Not right now, but when you've done everything you have to do tonight, you show up in the parking lot. I'll be there. Waiting. And if you're not here, and you're just watching... I will find you. Sooner or later. Count on it. Either way..." Slugger paused again. He looked out at the crowd, and every person who met his eyes felt compelled to look away. There was something in Slugger's eyes that had never been there before, and nobody who saw it liked it. "I heard this on TV once, and I think it applies here... either way, you're not going to jail. You're going to Hell." Winters looked out towards Slugger and laughed as he was being hauled away. "Scott... I can't believe you would assume that someone from this organization would try and terrorize your personal life. I mean... you've been retired for so long. I guess the Mariners didn't need a bat boy, huh Scott? Slugger glared at Winters as he was pulled behind the curtains; leaving Winters laughing voraciously in the ring. "Seizure of Power" began once again as Warfare turned to a commercial. Finished Oktober walked slowly down the hall, his bag slung over his shoulder. He hated being right, and he was right about one thing: professional wrestling was something to stay away from. He had assumed that it was a dirty business filled with people who didn't know how to control themselves. After four weeks in the fWo the lesson had been hammered home. He hadn't been able to control the people around him, or for that matter, himself. Time to leave town. Get to a better place. A safer place. "Hey... where are you going?" Oktober turned and saw Silver Fox coming up the hallway behind him. He sighed, and motioned with his chin to the door. "I'm leaving. This place isn't for me... I was right. Now everything seems to be settling back to normal and you don't need me here anymore. There isn't any point... so I'm going." Silver Fox sighed and extended a hand. "Listen, there's no reason for you to leave. What you saw wasn't the fWo at it's finest... hell, it wasn't any wrestling promotion at it's finest. So stay. Help us build the place you wanted to see." Oktober shook his head. "No, that's your dream. Mine was... well, my dream doesn't involve wrestling. It doesn't involve anywhere here." Silver Fox watched as Oktober turned away from him and continued down the hallway and out of sight. He sighed. He wasn't proud of the way things had gone, but what else could he have done? It was a situation where everything got quickly out of control. Before he knew it everything he had told himself wasn't going to happen was happening. Now, well, now here they all were. Silver Fox turned and spotted Spyder looking around the hallways as if in search of something. "Help you?" Silver Fox offered, and the masked man jumped at his inqury. "Huh? Oh... yeah... just... er... need a dressing room." Silver Fox eyed Spyder suspiciously, then hiked a thumb toward an empty room down the hallway. "Use that one... the guy who had it..." The crowd were on their feet as Frontier beckoned Kinkade back on... like a school bully that'd just taken a shot from one of his unfortunate victims, Kellen wiped the blood, which was now forming around his lip away, and screwed up his face furiously. And proceeded to make yet another fatal error, Kinkade's short fused temper was becoming common knowledge amongst the other fWo wrestlers, and following the blow to the face... he simply balled up his fists and saw red, rushing at Frontier with arms flailing furiously. Brand, much to the crowd's delight... kept his cool, sidestepping his rushing foe, and using his own momentum against him, by tossing him high over the top rope and to the arena floor! Kinkade hit the padding with a wicked thud and stayed there, as Brand raised his hands and took in yet more support from the crowd... a second later, he was back to business, rolling under the ropes to resume his battle with Kinkade. But Kellen Kinkade was ready. He'd waited on the arena floor for his moment to strike... in a burst of adrenaline, he exploded with a spear into Frontier's ribs... it wasn't just an impact spear however, somehow he powered the 6'6" 269lb off of his feet... rushing in one burst of adrenaline and letting out a roar, as he ran him square into the ring post, spine first. The crowd grimaced along with Brand Frontier... but the worst was yet to come, the fWo fans... and the fWo roster for that matter, were not aware of Kellen Kinkade's extensive football past, he'd almost made the bigtime once, and to do that alone... you had something special, you could pack heat. And pack heat, he did. He kept his firm grip around Brand's waist... turning and rushing him again, forcing him like a bullet from the chamber of a gun, he ran across to the alternate ringpost, driving him spine first into the ring steps with shocking impact. The steps jarred apart and fell to the arena floor as Brand lay clutching his back in agony... he was far from weathering the storm however... because Kinkade had not yet finished the assault, in another moment of rage, Kellen tore up the protective padding from the arena floor... exposing the concrete floor and more importantly... broadcast wiring. A second later, he was using it to his own advantage... by wrapping it firmly around the throat of the paragon, locking in a camel clutch esque hold... and sneering bitter remarks into the Survivor II winner's ear. "Now would be a good time to pray to your God, holy man, because no miracle on earth is getting you out of this one." Brand Frontier begged to differ. In a time where faith was tested, Brand believed... slowly but surely, he struggled up to his feet... taking the Immortal champion up with him, Kinkade tightened his grasp with the cable in a flustered manor as he hung from the back of the man desperately trying to get to his feet. Neither man had realized that the ten count had almost been reached... but it didn't matter, because even the official had stopped in awe of what was unfolding before him. Frontier had taken a grip on the middle rope... and to the shock and astound of everyone watching, he'd started to clamber up onto the apron with Kinkade still hanging from his back, choking him with cable... first onto his knees, then finally onto his feet, Brand Frontier had climbed onto the apron with a 275lb man hanging from his back. But suddenly, reality kicked back in... and adrenaline ran out. Regardless of his burst of strength, Frontier was still being deprived of oxygen... finally he succumbed to Kinkade's strangling... ironically however, it couldn't have come at a better time. Brand's legs turned to jelly and he fell backwards, which unfortunately not for him, but Kellen Kinkade... was a less than comfortable fall to the arena floor, usually something that a wrestler could deal with, but given the weight of Brand dropping back onto Kinkade, and the fact that they were falling to an exposed concrete floor, things did not bode well for the Immortal champion. THUD. "Ohhhhhhhhhhh!" The crowd exclaimed in disbelief, as Frontier fell backward onto the Immortal champion, crushing him into the concrete floor with a sickening thud. Time passed by, and the official persisted with his search for a ten count to end the brutal battle, but Brand Frontier would not allow it... tenaciously, he battled back to his feet, rolling under the bottom rope and laying exhausted on the canvas. The ten count reset to zero. Nine roars later, the crowd had cheered along with the official... but in a moment akin to that of a climactic horror movie scene, the evil villain sprung back to life with a second wind... just before the official could reach ten and declare Frontier the winner, Kinkade sprung to his feet and clambered up in a dazed manor onto the apron... just as Frontier staggered to his feet in the ring, the crowd were out of their seats again, as Frontier drew back to strike Kinkade... but Kellen scouted it, ducking between the middle and top rope and driving his shoulder firmly into Brand's ribs. Brand doubled over, and a second later... Kinkade had sunset flipped over him and was pulling ferociously at his legs, trying to yank him into a pin... but Brand had clutched the middle rope, he was going nowhere... as Kinkade threw back his legs to try and tear Brand away... the paragon hooked them and sat down, locking Kinkade firmly into a pin. And that was where the quality of Frontier shone through. He pushed forth... pinning Kinkade's shoulders to the canvas, Brand looked forth to see the ring ropes before them, he could clutch them... he could get the extra leverage and in three seconds, he could be the new Immortal champion, but that wasn't in his nature... that wasn't the Brand Frontier that the people knew and loved, and it never would be. 1! 2! NO! Frontier's refusal to stoop to Kinkade's level had cost him the three... as the Immortal champion squirmed out of the pinfall attempt... countering Brand into a shocking position. Kinkade rolled out of the pin face down... and in doing so was able to clutch Brand's legs on the way forward, a few stern drags later... he'd sat down a firm Boston crab towards the other side of the ring. But the amazing reversals were far from over... the two competitors were not yet done astounding the crowd. After only one cinch, Brand showed another amazing display of raw power, forcing Kinkade out of the crab with a kick of his legs that amazingly sent the Immortal right through a forward flip. Amazing? Yes... finished? No. Kinkade's forward flip only brought out the agile lucha within, as he flipped forth, he caught the top rope with his hands and planted his feet firmly on the middle one... he was in a picture perfect position to hit an Asai moonsault... and as Brand staggered up to his feet... Kinkade did so. Only to be caught a quarter of the way through the flip... landing squarely across the shoulders of the paragon... who spun Kinkade around several times, before dumping him onto his head stiffly at a sickening angle. With the Righteous Thunder. The crowd, quite simply... exploded. Brand Frontier reached forward, hooking Kinkade's leg and locking his gloves hands firmly... pinning him to the canvas. 1! 2! 3! New Immortal Champion. The had counted along with the pinfall, but to Brand's confusion... they hadn't erupted with cheers at his momentous victory, he'd been the first person to ever pin Kellen Kinkade for three, but the crowd had not celebrated his feat. Brand released Kinkade, and checked across the canvas... to see the sickening truth laying motionless on the canvas. After catching a moonsaulting Kinkade on his shoulders... Frontier had spun him several times in order to disorientate him, what he hadn't seen... was Kinkade's boot connecting firmly with the official's head, knocking him flat out on the canvas. No new Immortal champion, not yet. Frontier clasped his head in his hands with dismay, so near... yet so very far. As he approached the official, he didn't see Kinkade slowly crawling up behind him. CRUNCH. The crowd went insane, as Kade thrust a firm wrist straight into Brand's crotch, knocking him to the canvas gasping for air... as Brand lay recovering, Kinkade rolled under the ropes, snatching his Immortal title gold from a technician and taking back to the ring with it in his hands. As Brand staggered slowly to his feet... Kinkade screamed yet another message to the back. "I hope you're watching Crusader! This is more blood on your hands... yet another sacrifice in your name!" Kinkade's second of delay cost him dearly, however. He swung the belt... but Frontier had time to duck, and duck he did... evading the blow and catching Kinkade with a solid punch to the temple, knocking the belt from his hands and sending him sprawling across the middle rope, throat first... Brand stepped forth and leant down, choking Kinkade out as the crowd erupted with cheers... but this time, they weren't for Brand Frontier. They were for the man rushing down the aisle, chair in hand... Cody Williams. Crusader. He hadn't been ducking Kinkade, he hadn't been cowering backstage... he'd just been late arriving, and as soon as he'd seen a tape of Kinkade's earlier remarks... he snatched the nearest chair and got ready to rumble. As he rushed down the aisle... only one thought crossed his mind, he was going to silence Kellen Kinkade, he was going to show him that shit didn't fly far in the fWo. But his thoughts clouded his judgement. As he reached ringside, he drew back the chair with blind rage and unleashed a shot, not seeing what was transpiring in the ring... Kinkade has shot several elbows back into Brand's ribs and pulled him down before rolling out of the way, in a split second... Brand Frontier lay across the ropes exactly where Kellen Kinkade had. CRACK. It echoed through the arena, the sound of steel connecting with bone, Frontier's head instantly started to bleed as he toppled back motionless onto the canvas, Crusader dropped the chair and held his hands on his head... as Kellen Kinkade crawled slowly but surely toward Frontier, draping an arm across his chest just in time for the official to make a count. 1! 2! 3! Boos. Kellen Kinkade had done it again, and the crowd in attendance were furious... they let their feelings be know, hurling trash and abuse at the once again victorious Immortal champion as "This Life" by Primer 55 hit the arena... however, the booing and the music drowned out the sound of something else occurring just beside the aisle. Only a few crowd members could see the battle raging... as Crusader, and a new foe... were obscured by the fans gathering around. Kinkade staggered up to his feet, and was handed the Immortal title, which he raised into the air victoriously... breaking a sinister smile as he did, in luring out Crusader, he'd turned a hero into a temporary, accidental villain. As Brand Frontier slowly staggered up to his feet a defeated man... Kinkade prepared another shot... slowly drawing back the Immortal title. "I'm not finished with you yet holy man, you more than anyone should understand the concept of paying for the sins of others, it's time to make you the martyr." Kinkade finished his sentence, but didn't finish his attack... for as he swung, Brand Frontier once again ducked... forcing Kinkade to spin full circle, and be met by something which not only he, but the crowd had never expected to see. Aimz. Leaping from the top rope with a corkscrew moonsault, and catching Kellen Kinkade by the head... planting him with a DDT. Dead Aim. The crowd exploded with cheers as Kinkade's head thumped into the canvas and he snapped back motionless... Brand smiled, as Aimz got to her feet and dusted herself off... before approaching Brand and embracing him, before the two raised their hands victoriously to the delight of the crowd... ... blissfully unaware of the cruel fate that awaited them. Beside the aisle, Crusader lay motionless... because he'd just fallen victim to a move known and feared throughout the wrestling world. The Figure Four DDT. Cometh the hour... cometh the man. Ric Chronos. Chronos staggered out from beside the aisle... shaking off the few strikes which Crusader had got in, he slid under the ropes behind the celebrating Brand and Aimz, before lifting up Kinkade's Immortal title... to ace Frontier with a shot to the back of the head. As Brand collapsed to the canvas, Aimz turned in shock... only to be met with the same grizzly fate. Chronos struck her down with yet another Immortal title blow... leaving three downed wrestlers in his wake, he raised the title up in the air victoriously. Before feeling a cold grip on his shoulder, a grip that spun him around. Kellen Kinkade. The crowd were on their feet again, but this time... it was to rile up the two men confronting each other in the ring... they would've have loved to see Kinkade and Chronos tear each other apart more than anything else in the world. But they didn't. Instead, Chronos remained cold and emotionless... he tossed the title to Kellen Kinkade's feet, and simply rolled under the ropes and made his way up the aisle. Pointing at him, and then the downed bodies around him. As Ric Chronos left, Kellen Kinkade picked up his title belt and pondered... had Chronos delivered a message of support, or a message of warning? With three foes down... Chronos had suggested that whatever Kellen could do, he could do better. As the edition of Wednesday Warfare drew to a close suffice to say; with prides hurt and emotions invoked... the next Warfare was sure to be just as eventful. Result: Kellen Kinkade, by Pinfall
