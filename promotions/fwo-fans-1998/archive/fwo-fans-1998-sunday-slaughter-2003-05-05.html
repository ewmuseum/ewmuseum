Introduction Back in the United States the fWo learns that their extended abscence and following exile from the country has left their business struggling. With networks pulling out of contracts nearing expiration rather than renewing, the fWo finds itself looking for a new television home. To further complicate issues, the group of investors turned Board of Directors feel the best defense against sagging ratings and falling interest is a good offense... and has instructed road agents to prepare the promotion to compete on a more national level against the biggest wrestling organization in the industry. But as if this distraction wasn't confusing enough, the divide of trust between the wrestling roster and the road agents and management continues to grow ever deeper as tensions increase...
