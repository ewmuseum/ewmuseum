Sunday Slaughter, 4 July 1999

4 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The scene opens outside the office of the commissioner just prior to Sunday Slaughter going on the air. The sound of the crowd can be heard in the distance, the din of conversation can be made out, but the actual words escape the ear. Chris Finn and James Armstrong enter the scene, both looking slightly nervous. Both are dressed in their working clothes, and hold clipboards containing the program of the show. From behind the door, sounds of "Come With Me" can be heard from fWo: The Music. Armstrong looked at Chris, and pointed to the door with a dismissive "you do it" gesture. Finn shook his head quickly, and pointed at James. James looked angry, and jabbed Finn in the chest with his clipboard, pointing again at the door with a fierce nod. Pushing back, Finn whispered, "I'm not doing it... if you're so tough why don't YOU do it?" James looked angrier still, and not as quiet said, "Listen you uncle f**ker, you're the main commentary guy, so YOU do it!" Finn furrowed his gaze, and now shouting said, "Shut your f**king face, uncle f**ker, I'm tired of your..." Suddenly, "Oh, for god's sake, you two get in here..." is heard, and looking down at their feet sheepishly they turned the handle and entered the small room. The commissioner sat behind a small desk, looking over some contracts with amusement. Armstrong craned his neck in an attempt to read the names on the forms, but smiling slyly Travis pulled them out of his eyesight. The music playing ended, and Travis glanced over to the small portable stereo on the shelf and motioned at James. "If you're interested in looking at something Armstrong, how about putting on some new music? Maybe... some punk... I believe that there is something in there by the Misfits. Seems like... an appropriate piece, don't you think?" James Armstrong laughed in agreement even though he had no idea what Travis was talking about, and quickly put the disc into place. Turning to Chris Finn, Travis looked quizzically at the announcer and said, "And... you're in my office... why again?" Finn stammered quickly, and looked down at his clipboard as he gathered his notes. "Oh... well... you see... that is... well, James and I were noticing... well, that we were booked to do commentary again tonight... and that Mr. Venom and Mr. Loco are scheduled for backstage reporting. Well, since we are going to be at the desk... well... James and I... err... just James really...wanted to know... err... because we are the commentators and all... who... ahem... who you would be bringing in to face Mr. Davis. You know, because... James and I... James mostly... thought it would be best, you know... if we were... involved. Don't you... err... think so?" Travis looked solidly at Chris Finn, and then over at James Armstrong. Finally, Travis coughed into his fist and smiled at the two men. "Actually... no. No, I don't think it would be a good idea at all. For you see... Austin Davis can be very convincing, and I think that with this matter, considering who he will be facing... I think it would be best if that information just remained with me. I mean, I'm certainly not pleased with the decision I'm having to make, but if I told both of you... and then both of you blabbed it to Davis... well, that would only make things worse, understand?" Gulping, Finn and Armstrong nodded quickly and slowly backed out of the room. "Hold it," Travis said. "Before you go, I want you to take a look at these shirts..." Travis held up a pair of tye-dyed t-shirts for the two, and motioned for them to look at both carefully. "I'm going to be ordering a... well, a large number of these shortly, and which color do you think works best?" James Armstrong furrowed his nose as he looked at the two and exclaimed, "Well, they both stink... what is that smell?" Travis put the shirts down with a sigh and said, "Oh, it's just the dye on the shirts... they are new after all. Hell, just be glad you can smell anything... not everyone has that luxury you know. Speaking of which, we've got a birthday card to send... er... on second thought, I've got some stuff to send over to our birthday boy anyway, so I'll take care of things. You two had better get down to the ring now, eh? Sounds like the show is getting ready to start..." James nodded, and with that he and Chris left the room on their way to the ringside area. James motioned with his finger in a "crazy" gesture as they walked, and Chris nodded sadly. As the door shut, the commissioner laughed slightly at the two and their obsessive worries, and reached behind his desk for a small set of hand weights. Grunting, Travis began to curl the weights in succession, looking at the calendar with some amount of worry. Mumbling to himself Travis said, "not much time left..." as the cameras left the area to go to ringside. The sounds of Sunday Slaughter's theme began to blare over the arena PA system as the show began proper... Trouble vs. French Foundation The loud music of the Sunday Slaughter opening theme died down as the crowd recovered from the pyrotechnics and stood on their feet, anxiously awaiting the appearance of their favorite stars. As luck would have it, Nikki the Valley Girl was the first fWo "talent" to push back the curtains and step out onto the entrance ramp. Blowing the crowd a kiss, Primetime and Mitch Wilson followed close behind her on their way to the ring. They waved the French flag, and gave the finger to fans in the crowd annoyed over such audacity on the Forth of July. The crowd booed the trio heavily, but suddenly grew silent as Joy, along with his new partner Glee stepped onto the entrance ramp behind the Frenchmen. Joy held a microphone, and pointed to Primetime and Nikki, addressing them by saying, "Listen here Primetime... you're risking your own chance at happiness by fooling around with this harlot that is showing her body off to everyone in that sinful 'Bikini Breakout', and I've tried to warn you... but you just won't listen. Well Primetime, I'll see you this Friday in a mixed tag match with Glee and I, and the two of us will show you just what it means to be pure and happy gosh darn it! But for now... for now I'll leave you with this little song..." With that said, Joy began to croon softly, "Happiness... you make the butterflies sing and dance... you make the birdies prance... I want you to feel it too... my heart it's yellow goo..." With that, the fans began to throw cans of beer and surge at the happy duo, who fled to the backstage area in a panic. This matter concluded, Primetime and Nikki continued to grope inside the ring, while Mitch Wilson prepared himself for the upcoming match. The music of Trouble began to play, and the strange punk pair made their way to the ring, licking some fans and punching others... seemingly at random. Twitch slid into the ring and at once delivered a tackle to the distracted Primetime, sending he and Nikki over the top rope and out to the floor. Thumper grabbed Wilson by the neck, and snapped him down with a tilt a whirl backbreaker. Completely taken by surprise, the French Foundation rallied on the outside, finally sending Primetime into the ring with Thumper while their partners stood on the ring apron awaiting a possible tag. Primetime caught a clothesline and set Thumper up for a quick huricirana, but Thumper caught his legs and picked him up and slammed him face-first into the mat. Outside Nikki cringed as Primetime's nose exploded in a spray of blood. Thumper tagged in Twitch, and set Primetime over his head as Twitch climbed to the top rope. Twitch executed a perfect flying clothesline off the top turnbuckle, and send Primetime crashing backward. Covering him quickly, Twitch was only able to get a two count as Primetime got a shoulder up in desperation. Primetime crawled toward his corner, but Twitch grabbed his legs and pulled him backward, driving an elbow into the back of the French Foundation member to weaken his vertical base. Twitch then went to the top turnbuckle and signaled to the crowd that a big splash was coming... but at the last second Primetime raised a leg and caught Twitch in the jaw with his boot. Twitch reeled backward, looking for a tag as Primetime crawled once again for his corner. Thumper came into the ring, but Primetime made the tag at the last second, and the fresh Wilson ran inside with hard chops to the chest. Wilson cleared the ring, and ran to the ring ropes for a diving plancha to the outside... when the lights suddenly went out. The music of the Dark Riders blared over the P/A system as Death, followed closely by Stone, and then Plague and War ran to the ring. The four got inside the ring and attacked both teams as the ref called for the no contest bell. Death picked up a fleeing Twitch, and raised him above his head and threw him from the ring and into the crowd. Looking to avenge his partner Thumper charged at Death, but Stone caught him from behind and held him fast. Death grabbed the arm of Thumper, and screaming he snapped bone and flesh in one quick movement. Throwing the wrestler from the ring, Death grabbed an announcer's microphone and screamed into it, "Silver Fox... Chameleon... Quicksilver... Eclipse... Fyre... and that bastard..." Result: No Contest The mystery revealed Before Death could continue, Silver Fox stepped out onto the entrance ramp smiling and chiding Death with the microphone. "Now, now big fella... we can't have you giving away the big surprise can we? I mean... all these people have paid good money, they can't exactly have you coming down here and ruining the show can they? I told all of you that the identity of the mystery man would be revealed to you all tonight... and mark my words it will happen... but not like this... no, not like this at all. Where is your sense of style dark warrior? Where is the panache that we all saw when you were attacking people who ate... well, things like this?" With that, Silver Fox held up a single candy bar, and waved it in front of his face. This seemed to further anger Death, who screamed for Silver Fox to get into the ring right now and take his beating like a man. Silver Fox only laughed, and slowly unwrapped the candy bar and began to eat it. This only made Death angrier, and he began to exit the ring and stalk up the entry ramp with the rest of the Dark Riders behind him. Silver Fox stopped eating the candy bar, and seemed to grin a sly smile, whispering the words, "That's right... you big fool... just a little closer..." Death continued up the entry ramp, but now a creaking could be heard and Death had only a second to look down at his feet before a section of the entry ramp swung open, and the Dark Riders fell inside. Working quickly, Silver Fox and the Black Quicksilver shut the doors again, sealing the Dark Riders in a small cage under the ramp. Silver Fox taunted them from above as Death screamed from below... as fWo officials began to haul the cage away to an undisclosed location. Silver Fox told the men to stop, and waved to the backstage area. A large man wearing a mask came out onto the ramp and joined Silver Fox and his crew, laughing at the Dark Riders. Death screamed up at him, and redoubled his efforts to free himself from the cage. Silver Fox handed the masked man the microphone, and the mysterious man addressed the Dark Riders and the crowd, his familiar voice ringing out over the arena. "Look at you, Scotty! Like a caged animal! That cage is where he belongs, but do you know why he's so upset? Do you? Because I was the one to beat him! Me... it was me all along!" With that, the man ripped free his mask revealing none other than the Brawler. He continued to laugh at the Dark Riders, as Death continued to scream and batter the side of the cage with his own body. Suddenly with a great crash the cage wall gave, and the Dark Riders spilled out of the cage. The Brawler stopped laughing quickly, and he and the others quickly fled to the backstage area with the Dark Riders in hot pursuit. As the wreckage starts to clear, the lights go dark, and the music of Darkness begins to play. As the huge and evil Darkness made his way to the ring, Slaughter went to a commercial break... Darkness vs. Ice We return from the commercial break as Ice makes his way to the ring to a modest reaction from the crowd. Darkness is already in the ring, and doesn't look too happy. Ice climbs up into the ring and the match starts off with Darkness charging at him and spearing him into the turnbuckles *hard*. Darkness has already weakened Ice as the referee rings the bell. Darkness follows it up with a snapmare out of the corner. Darkness is concentrating on the neck and back area, which seems to be a keen idea as Ice is obviously hurt in that area. Darkness quickly jumps up to the second rope, and flies off with a fistdrop to the head of Ice. A cover, and a kickout. Darkness follows it up by throwing Ice through the middle ropes and out onto the floor. Darkness goes out onto the outside and nails Ice with a sidewalk slam. He then goes back up onto the ring apron, and flies off with an elbow drop. Darkness brings Ice up to his feet, and goes to throw him into the guardrail... but Ice somehow musters up enough strength to reverse it, and instead Darkness goes flying into the guardrail. The referee finally joins the mayhem on the outside, trying to get Ice back into the ring but Ice wants none of it. The big blonde machine wants a piece of Darkness. He grabs Darkness and throws him shoulder first into the ring steps. Ice is certainly not done yet... he brings Darkness over by the announcers' table, and slams his head repeatedly into the table. He then brings him over and throws him backfirst into another section of the guardrail. Ice grabs Darkness by the head as the referee continues to try to convince Ice that it'd be best to bring Darkness back into the ring. Ice brings Darkness up onto the ring apron and kicks him in the stomach. He then scoops him up onto his back and hits a Death Valley Driver on the ramp! Ice is merciless in his assault on Darkness, and it doesn't appear as if it's going to stop. Ice makes a long walk back down the ramp and over near the announcers table. He grabs a steel chair, and brings it back up near the ramp. Ice walking all the way back down near the announcing crew appears to have given Darkness ample time to recover. Ice spots Darkness climbing to his feet. Ice runs up the ramp and swings at Darkness with the chair. Darkness ducks, spins Ice back around, kicks him in the stomach, and locks Ice in for a sitting powerbomb on the ramp. The action doesn't appear to be stopping as Darkness brings Ice up onto the stage. It looks as if Ice is going to be thrown off the stage down through a concession stand area. Darkness is going to throw Ice off the edge... he prepares with an Irish Whip, but Ice reverses it... Darkness reverses Ice' attempt... and with both men near the edge, they've no choice as both men fall backwards through the concession stand booth! Both men are out cold with packages of M&M's, cotton candy, and other assorted items sprinked all over their bodies. The referee looks as if he's had enough, and doesn't know what to do... before heading back towards the ring. He calls for Matt Panzer to come near the ring, before giving him instructions. Panzer simply nods and informs the crowd that the referee has enough of the antics and is going to issue a ten-count. Should neither Darkness nor Ice make it back to the ring in time, both men will be counted out. The referee begins, and slowly, Darkness and Ice both make a valiant attempt at getting back to the ring. Darkness is crawling, much to weak to stand at this point, and Ice is doing the same. Both men continue to slug it out half-assed on the way back... Darkness climbs up onto the lower part of the ramp as the referee reaches five. Ice holds him by his leg as Ice lays on the concrete near the ramp. Darkness tries to shake off his grasp, and kicks him in the head several times. Darkness makes it to the edge of the ring at the count of eight. Darkness reaches up onto the ring apron and is about to climb into the ring... but Ice is right behind him. Both men continue to slug it out, and the referee reaches ten... thus both men are counted out. Darkness hits Ice a few times, before climbing into the ring. He looks at the referee thinking he's won as the bell rings, but the referee informs him he and Ice have both been counted out. Enraged, a burst of adrenaline comes through the body of Darkness and he spears the referee into the mat. Darkness goes back onto the outside, and he stomps Ice in the head repeatedly. He then throws Ice backfirst into the guardrail. He then climbs back into the ring and grabs the referee. He throws him over the top rope over onto the cement floor. He then looks back and spots Scott Slugger coming down to ringside. Slugger is checking on Ice over near the guardrail. Ice is slumped up against it. An idea comes into the mind of Darkness. He climbs outside the ring and waits. Ice sees Slugger and tells him to turn around. Slugger turns around, and spots Darkness charging towards him. Slugger quickly moves Ice out of the way and is then speared backwards into the guardrail. The guardrail quickly collapses as both men go crashing through. The crowd erupts in a hushed silence after the "pop". Darkness climbs up and begins shouting at fans, tossing them out of his way, and the path of terror continues until we cut to a preview for Summer Slaughter. Result: Double Count Out A response to the challenge The Black Panther makes his way out to the ring to answer Universal's challenge. On his way, he slaps the hands of the fans at ringside. He enters the ring and grabs a microphone. "I'm sure all of you have heard Chris Universal's commentary. He's trying to duck another match with me because he knows his last victory was a fluke. He says that if myself and a tag team partner defeat him and a tag team partner at Fight the Future III, that he will grant me the Screwjob Match at Fight the Future IV. Universal, however, made one mistake in all of this, one big mistake. He's allowed me to not only choose my partner, but his partner as well. First, I concentrated on my partner. This person had to fit two descriptions. He had to be someone I could get along with, and he had to have a history with Universal. When I narrowed down the choices with this criteria, there really only is one choice, and I'm going to introduce you to him right now. A lot of wrestlers have claimed it, but he his the true king of hardcore wrestling. That's right, my partner, Cactus Jack!" Cactus makes his way down to the ring, slapping the hands of fans on the way. He enters the ring and the Panther hands him the microphone. "You know, when the Panther offered me the spot as his tag team partner, I jumped at the opportunity because of two reasons. First of all, I would love another chance to kick Universal's teeth down his throat. Second, the idea of picking Universal's partner intrigued me. So the Panther and I sat down and started to think of a partner for Universal. This person had to fit two descriptions. He had to be someone Universal couldn't get along with, and he had to have a history with Universal. When we narrowed down the choices, there was only one choice. This is someone who I want revenge on for costing me a shot at the Internet Title. You all know who I'm talking about, the man who loves to hate everyone, Austin Davis!" Upon hearing this, Universal makes his way out to the boos of the fans. He stands at the top of the ramp with a shocked look on his face and a microphone in hand. "No, no, no. The $ellout is not going to team up with that bald, overrated jobber. You're going to have to pick me a new partner." The Panther takes the microphone back from Cactus. "Universal, you said I could pick anybody to be your partner, and your partner will be Austin Davis. If you don't like that, you can forfeit the match now... which would mean I get my Screwjob Match this week, rather than next!" Universal appears to be stuck, as he tries to think of some way to respond. Before he can, Austin Davis, sneaks up from behind Universal and attacks him. He downs Universal with kicks and punches to the delight of the Panther and Cactus in the ring. A number of officials run out to get Davis off Universal. When they do, the Panther and Cactus turn around to pose for the fans. While the officials help Universal to the back, Davis runs down to the ring and attacks both men. He grabs the Panther and nails a DDT on him. He grabs Cactus and repeats the action. He then repeatedly kicks both of the unconscious men until the officials get into the ring and get him off both men. Davis attacks a few of the officials and sticks both middle fingers into the air to the boos of the fans before leaving up the ramp. Flying Frenchie (c) vs. US Marshal We return from the break to find The Flying Frenchie coming down to the ring. Frenchie is beaming proudly with his International Title wrapped firmly around his waist. The Surgeon is at ringside in the front row watching all of the events about to unfold with great interest. The familiar theme of the US Marshal begins playing, and out he walks. Marshal climbs up into the ring and awaits the bell to sound, obviously refusing to break the rules and start the match before the bell rings. Frenchie just looks at him befuddled, before muttering something about "stupid hicks". The two lock up in the center of the ring, and US Marshal has a quick upperhand as he overpowers Frenchie in a "mercy contest". His grip continues to overpower the smaller Frenchie, and Frenchie writhes in pain. However, Frenchie catches US Marshal offguard with a knee to the groin, and then Frenchie quickly takes US Marshal down with a northern lights suplex. A cover, but Marshal kicks out, although remaining stunned on the mat. Frenchie shakes his hand, trying to get the pain to go away. Frenchie continues, first bringing Marshal to his feet, catching him offguard with a rake to the eyes, and then Irish Whipping him to the ropes. Frenchie ducks down for a backdrop; but Marshal telegraphs it by hooking him and nailing him with a swinging neckbreaker. Both men are dazed... it's a race to see who gets to their feet first. Marshal gets up, and grabs Frenchie... he slams him to the mat. He follows it up with a quick elbowdrop. A cover, and two only. Marshal on the attack now as he brings him to his feet and whips him to the ropes. Frenchie returns and is kicked in the stomach, and then Marshal signals for the Hangman's Noose. Marshal holds Frenchie high above his head, attempting a hanging vertical suplex. Five to ten seconds pass as Marshal holds him high... but Frenchie escapes and falls down behind Marshal. He waistlocks him and with Marshal in front of him, prepares to run towards the ropes for a roll-up. He proceeds, but Marshal grabs the ropes at the last second, and Frenchie is propelled backwards towards the mat. The crafty Frenchie however rolls up into a standing position, and charges at Marshal, taking him off his feet with a spinning heel kick which sends him over the top rope and out onto the floor. Frenchie now, with momentum on his side, waits as Marshal gets to his feet. Frenchie catapaults himself over with a plancha, but Marshal moves out of the way and Frenchie hits the floor hard. Marshal grabs Frenchie, refusing to cheat by fighting on the outside, and rolls him into the ring. He covers him, but Frenchie kicks out at two and a half. Marshal brings Frenchie up, and whips him into the turnbuckles. He follows it up with a massive running elbow to the side of the head. Frenchie is dazed near the ropes now... Marshal kicks him in the stomach, and goes for a Lone Star Stunner. Frenchie pushes him off and Marshal is propelled into the ropes. He returns, and Frenchie takes him down with a massive drop-toe-hold. Frenchie has the upperhand now, and he hits a quick legdrop on Marshal... before going up to the top rope after signaling for the 450 splash. Frenchie flies off, but Marshal rolls out of the way and Frenchie crashes into the mat. Marshal gets to his feet rather quickly, and Frenchie bounces off the mat back up to his feet... Marshal with a kick to the stomach, and another attempt at the Lone Star Stunner. He hits it! Marshal goes for the cover, and at the count of two, Primetime & Wilson pull the referee out of the ring and attack him. Marshal had the match won, but the French Foundation came to the save of their cohort in crime. Marshal is pissed, and he goes over near the ropes, and begins yelling down towards PT & Mitch. PT and Wilson grab him by the legs, pull him towards the ringpost, and drag him in crotch first... obviously hurting Marshal. They then take turns slamming each leg into the ringpost repeatedly, further damaging Marshal. Frenchie rolls outside the ring holding his neck, and tells Mitch to execute a sharpshooter on Marshal around the post. Mitch obliges, and Marshal begins screaming in pain. However, here comes the Mad Trucker to make the save! The massive man catches PT with a big boot to the head. He chases after Frenchie, but Frenchie is able to escape... Trucker goes after Mitch and Mitch escapes as well. Trucker and Frenchie exchange pleasantries, and Trucker probably had no idea what Frenchie said as he spoke it in French. With that, Slaughter cut to the backstage area where Austin Davis methodically staked out a backstage area. Result: US Marshal by DQ Backstage attack As several wrestlers are spotted back stage in the same area, Austin Davis comes into the area and begins attacking several of them. Mike Heftel is hiptossed through a buffet table. Davis grabs Waru and throws him head-first into a garage door. Davis grabs Samuel Smooth and slams him onto the cement floor repeatedly. Suddenly, he sees two ambulances where Chris Universal, Black Panther & Cactus Jack are all being loaded onto. Each man is on a stretcher, and Davis runs over and attacks them. He throws two medical attendants aside, before grabbing Universal out of the ambulance and drops it onto the floor. He grabs Cactus Jack out, while he's steel on the stretcher, and wheels him into the brick wall. He drops Panther ontop of Universal while Panther is still on the stretcher. Davis then assaults another medical attendant before leaving the area calmly. Micky Maliboo vs. Samuel Smooth The special guest referee Prophet is entering the ring, and he has this aura about him... as if he's acting cool. He winks at fans, and then climbs into the ring and poses as Samuel Smooth is introduced. Smooth makes his way out, still smarting from the attack by Davis a few minutes ago, but not before Micky Maliboo and Vixon sneak up from behind and attack him. The dangerous duo combines for a double bodyslam on the ramp, before Maliboo brings him down to ringside. He fisherman suplexes Smooth on the floor. He rolls him into the ring and his attack continues... first he whips him into the turnbuckles hard sternum first, and as Smooth staggers out he scoops him up and hits a Samoan Drop. Maliboo goes for a two count, and Prophet makes a somewhat slow count of which Maliboo objects to. Maliboo backs up Prophet into the corner, and is verbally berating him. Smooth sneaks up from behind, and nails him with a lowblow. Maliboo staggers out, and Smooth follows it up with a piledriver. Then, he goes for a cover... Prophet goes behind Smooth, places his legs on the middle ropes while Smooth is still pinning Maliboo, then Prophet gives a very quick three count before running out of the ring. Before Prophet goes up the ramp, he blows a kiss to Vixon, before exiting. Maliboo gets up onto his knees and looks very annoyed about what just happened, and Vixon has a "as-if" look on her face. Suddenly, Austin Davis makes his way down to ringside. Davis winks at Vixon, before grabbing Smooth on the floor and taking him out with a short-arm clothesline. He climbs up into the ring and tells Maliboo to "get the fuck out of here". Maliboo reluctantly obliges. Davis grabs a microphone and says "I told you all this past week that my actions would speak louder than words. I've also told you all for a very long time... don't trust Austin Davis, but trust his words or else it'll be the biggest mistake of your life. Tonight, about 9 or 10 people have suffered because Travis refuses to let it be known who the mystery opponent will be. Well, all 9 or 10 of those people will be injured for quite sometime, so we can rule each of those sorry bastards out. Nobody will be safe from now on... you all are marked men, and if it means I have to hurt some innocent people on the side to keep them from being the mystery opponent at Summer Slaughter, so be it. More men will be assaulted. More men will be hurt. And more men will suffer until Travis tells me who the mystery opponent is. That's not a very good way to run a company, is it? The current boss won't take a stand for his wrestlers because he's afraid he'll get his ass kicked. Well, the current boss won't be the boss very long with that type of politics because at Summer Slaughter, I am going to kick whoever's ass happens to be in that ring. It could be Travis, it could be James Armstrong, it could be Chris Finn, it could be Scott Slugger, Chris Universal, it could be my own mother, and the bitch would go down, I guarandamntee ya!" Suddenly, Travis makes his way out with the three medical attendants that Davis assaulted earlier. Also with Travis, is six-armed officers of the law. Travis asks the medical attendants if the person in the ring fits the description of the person that attacked them. The medical attendants all say yes. Davis is in the ring with a "what in the hell?" look residing on his face. Travis asks them if they want him to be arrested for assault and battery. The medical attendants all say yes. Travis tells the armed officers of the law to "arrest that man". The officers all begin marching down to the ring, one of them on a megaphone telling Davis to comply or face further charges. Davis puts his arms behind his head while one of the officers come up into the ring. The officer proceeds with caution towards Davis with a pair of handcuffs. He grabs one of Davis' hands, and begins to attach the handcuffs. The other officers soon climb into the ring and assist in the arrest of Davis. They all begin escorting Davis up the ramp, where Davis just glares at Travis, before mouthing off to him and the medical attendants... before Slaughter quickly cuts to a commercial break. Result: Samuel Smooth by Pinfall Big announcements Still inside the ring, the commissioner asked for the microphone and addressed the crowd. "Listen, fWo fans... I'm sorry you had to see that ugly confrontation. There are times when the fWo seems more like a European Soccer Game than a legitimate sport... but I'm sure if any of those types of people were here today they would have put Davis in his place. But, like I've stated before, all will be settled at Summer Slaughter... for better or for worse. Before we get to our final match this evening, I've got a couple of announcements. First, we would like to apologize to all of you over the scheduled Briana versus Wolf Fang Cruiserweight Title match. We are running short on time, and the match will take place this Friday, at Fight the Future. We hope you will all enjoy that match, which promises to be a strong contest between two very exciting workers. My next announcement also concerns Fight The Future this Friday, and the main event at that show... the World Title Match between Ultra Violet and Cactus Jon. Because of the sensitive nature of the match, and because of the recent troubles involved in title matches, I've decided to appoint a special guest referee for that match. He is a former fWo World Champion, ladies and gentlemen... I present to you the Hardcore Champion, Scott Slugger!" With that, Scott Slugger pushed back the curtain and walked to the ring with a large smile on his face, waving to the crowd, who received Scott Slugger with a mixture of cheers and jeers. Slugger ignored it all, and stepped into the ring with a swagger and grabbed the microphone from the commissioner. "Listen up all you Scott Slugger fans out there! I promise you a clean match... a good match... and not a lot of involvement from the referee! There will be a clear winner, there will be a tough match, and there will be the greatest star in the fWo, Scott Slugger, inside the ring to watch the whole thing!" Slugger tossed the microphone into the crowd, which seemed to annoy the commissioner slightly as he had more to say, but resigned to this development the pair left the ring, as the ring introduction of Tom the Bomb took place... Ultra Violet (c) vs. Tom the Bomb With Tom the Bomb inside the ring, Ultra Violet wandered out onto the entrance ramp looking confused. Ultra Violet started to walk to the ring, but then grew distracted by vendors in the crowd and wandered off into the audience. Annoyed with this development, Tom the Bomb ran into the crowd to retrieve Ultra Violet. Seeing the large Canadian approaching, Ultra Violet screeched "explosives!" and began to run for his life. Tom chased Ultra Violet around the arena, who tossed chairs and fans behind him to slow his path. Tom tossed them out of the way as he continued to try to catch the fWo World Champion, who ran into one of the stadium bathrooms. From inside a screech was heard, and Ultra Violet quickly ran back out again waving his arms and being chased by several angry female wrestling fans. Back in the main arena setting, Tom chased Ultra Violet toward ringside. Ultra Violet finally bounced over the guard rail and toward the ring, but smacked his head on the turnbuckle and fell backward. Tom grabbed the limp form of Ultra Violet and rolled him into the ring. Tom prepared to cover Ultra Violet, but Cactus Jon suddenly ran to the ring and pulled Tom off Ultra Violet. As the ref called for the DQ, Tom turned on his fellow Canadian and shouted at him, wanting to know why he ruined his chances at becoming the fWo World Champion. Cactus Jon only shrugged and said that he had to do what he had to do. Inside the ring, Ultra Violet slowly came to, and looked around confused as to his current location. Outside the ring Cactus Jon and Tom began to exchange louder and angrier words, finally starting to shove each other. The crowd came to their feet as Scott Slugger ran to the ring, wearing a referee's shirt. Slugger got between the two men, trying to separate them from each other. At first the situation seemed resolved, but then Tom swung wildly at Cactus Jon, and struck Scott Slugger instead. Despite what seemed like a light punch, Scott Slugger crumpled at once, holding his jaw. More fWo officials poured out of the backstage area, as Cactus Jon and Tom the Bomb looked down at Scott Slugger in confusion. Slugger climbed to his feet, and slowly wandered back to the locker rooms, mumbling that he had to talk to the commissioner at once about this, "intolerable situation". As the cameras went off the air, angry words were exchanged at ringside and Ultra Violet waved to the fans in attendance... Result: Tom the Bomb by DQ
