Sunday Slaughter, 3 June 2001

3 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Mike Bear wasn't in the best of moods when he pulled into the Charlotte Coliseum parking lot. He had just lost his fWo World Title the night before, to Ric Chronos of all people. Pah. Bear, getting pissed again just thinking about it, cursed under his breath and reached down to turn up the angry, RUAHH~!-filled music already blaring from his stereo. He looked up just in time to see a huge black limo cutting him off. Bear slammed on the brakes of his Dodge Ram, stopping short just before he collided with the limo. "Son of a bitch!" yelled Bear, then noticed the license plate as it drove off. KELLY#1 "Kelly... somehow, I should have known. Asshole!" Bear stepped on the gas again and moved forward, knowing that if he saw Erik Kelly tonight, there would be hell to pay... Mike Heftel vs. Donaven Winters 'Down With The Ship' by Scatterbrain brings out the two STUMP squad members, Mike Heftel and Black Quicksilver. Mike Heftel is fresh of his loss at Redemption against the team of Ruin for the Tag Team Titles in what would probably be classified as a handicapped match. 'Somewhat Damaged' by Nine Inch Nails brings out his opponent who is making an easy debut against fWo's resident jobber, Mike Heftel. He goes by the name, 'The Fallen Angel' Donaven Winters. The bell sounds as the two lock up in the middle of the ring, Winters over powers Heftel and pushes him to the mat and is now stomping the jobber. Heftel gets to his knees but Winters delivers a knee to Heftel's face. Heftel seeks refuge on the outside but Winters is looking to make an impression and doesn't let up by hitting Heftel with a plancha from behind. Winters is making a hot debut and Heftel is trying to crawl away from the hot new rookie. But now Winters throws Heftel into the guard and follows it up with multiple chops to Heftel's chest. Winters has Heftel over his shoulder now and rams Heftel's face into the ring post. Winters rolls back into the ring and show boats to an appreciative crowd while renewing the count, Winters back to the outside and continues to put a clinic on Heftel. Winters has Heftel and uses a Snake eyes to put Heftel throat first across the guardrail. Winters lifts Heftel to his feet and rams him kidney's first into the ring apron a few times and then rolls him back into the ring. Heftel is begging off but Winters shows no remorse and dropkicks Heftel in the skull. Cover, but Heftel is able to kickout. Winters has Heftel's legs and is looking for a figure four but Heftel reverses it by kicking Winters as he turned over Heftel's leg. Heftel gets to his feet but receives a big boot to the face as he gets up. Winters will not quit his brutal assault on Heftel. Winters has Heftel on his feet and hits a cradle neckbreaker, pin, 1-2-kickout by Heftel. Winters now hitting Heftel with vicious lefts and rights to his lower back as Heftel screams for Black Quicksilver to help him but Black Quicksilver watching this match thoughtfully but it seems he's paying more attention to Winters than he is his stable mate, Heftel. Winters has Heftel up again and hits him with a belly-to-back powerbomb with a hook of the leg, but only a two count. Winters brings Heftel to his feet and attempts to Irish whip him into the corner but it is reversed by Heftel. Winters runs into the corner and hops to the top rope, Heftel follows closely from the behind and ends up catching a boot to the face from Winters, Winters hooks Heftel's head and hits a massive Tornado DDT! Winters follows it up with a leg drop to Heftel and then a elbow drop to the sternum. Heftel crawling away but exposes his back and Winters takes advantage with a release German suplex! Heftel crawls to the ropes and helps himself up, Winters hits Heftel with a knife edge chop and then Irish whips him across the ring. Winters telegraph's the back body drop and Heftel kicks him in the face, Heftel backs into the ropes and attempts a clothesline but Winters catches the arm and turns it into a Pumphandle backbreaker. Heftel rolls over and makes another mistake as Winters hops on his back and hooks a Camel Clutch! Heftel is struggling to make it the ropes and is about to tap out but Winters releases the hold and drags Heftel to the center of the ring. Winters now climbing up the turnbuckles and takes off and hits a mean frog splash on Heftel. Heftel is in serious pain and is holding his ribs as Winters raises him to his feet once more, Brainbuster DDT! Winters continues to impress the fans with his skill and doesn't want the match to end as he once again refuses to cover Heftel. Winters backs Heftel into the ropes and Irish whips him across the ring, Heftel ducks the clothesline and rebounds off the ropes again but this time Winters catches him with a sleeper, Heftel quickly falls to the mat and looks to be out of strength already. The ref raises his arm... and it drops. The ref raises his arm again... and it drops. The ref raises it for the third time... but Winters releases the hold. Winters lifts Heftel to his feet and hits the falcon arrow on Heftel sending him crashing to the mat in a heap. Heftel is dead and lost this match long ago but Black Quicksilver could care less as he continues to watch Winters closely. Winters pulls Heftel to his feet by his hair and hooks on the Gory Guerrero Special submission move and Heftel is screaming for a few moments until Winters turns it into the Gory Special 2000. Winters now on his feet and is signaling for 'The Waste'. He brings Heftel to his feet and hits his finisher, which is the Reverse Gory Special Piledriver. Heftel is at least injured or at most dead as Winters gets the easy three count. The Michigan native makes an impressive debut here in the fWo but what is with all the attention he is getting from Black Quicksilver? Result: Donaven Winters by pinfall State Of The fWo "Do You Buy What The Sellout Is Selling?" The crowd popped as the music of the owner of the fWo, Chris Universal, played over the pa system. Universal quickly appeared, dressed in his traditional stylish way and carrying a microphone. Universal climbed up the ringsteps into the ring, and surveyed the crowd. "Welcome to Slaughter, Charlotte!" Universal received his cheap pop with a smile, then continued. "Now, there's a lot of business to take care of, so let's get to getting'. First, Hellrazor. Based on his victory over Crucifix yesterday at Redemption, I am awarding Hellrazor a Hardcore Title shot, at Damned, against Vic Creed!" The crowd cheered at this. "Or, of course, whoever is champion at the time. Second, the World Title situation. Last night, Ric Chronos won the fWo World Title from Mike Bear..." Pause for boos. "... and, in the course of the match, used some questionable tactics. I've thought it over, and I've decided that Mike Bear deserves a rematch tonight!" The crowd cheered loudly at this announcement, but Universal quieted them with a hand signal. "That's not all. Furthermore, there will be a special referee for this match, to ensure everything is fair for both competitors...the Boston Strangler!" The crowd went nuts as Universal announced that the #1 Contender would referee the huge upcoming World Title match. Universal smiled, then continued. "And, last but not necessarily least, the Cruiserweight Title. I've determined the #1 Contender, who will face Eddy Fate tonight. Insane Wonder, Tempest, if you would?" "Sugar" began to play, and the crowd booed as Insane Wonder made his way onto the entrance ramp. "Battle Flag" hit, and the boos turned to cheers as Tempest joined Wonder on the entrance ramp. "Now," continued Universal, "you each have a compelling case for contendership. Wonder, you took Eddy Fate to the limit last night. Tempest, you proved yourself as a true fWo star last night, and proved you could be a great cruiserweight as well. After careful consideration, I have no choice but to award tonight's title shot to " Universal was cut off as "Eye Of The Tiger" began to play, and the crowd booed loudly as Greg Davis appeared on the entrance ramp. He glanced at the other two wrestlers there and scoffed, then addressed Universal. "Uni, Uni, Uni... you seem to be forgetting somebody. Namely, me. You know, the three-time fWo Cruiserweight Champion? The man who set a record for longer fWo Cruiserweight Title reign? The Ego? Hello, McFly, anybody in there? It's obvious to everybody here that I should be the #1 Contender, not Ragin' Cajun or Wonder Woman." Universal looked him over. "Give me one good reason to give you a Cruiserweight Title shot." Ego replied almost automatically. "Because I'm me, that's why." Wonder shook his head as Davis spoke, and Tempest rolled her eyes. Universal took a second to think things over, then spoke. "You know what Greg, you're right. You should face Eddy Fate tonight." Davis smiled, as if vindicated. "And, for that matter, you should also face Insane Wonder." Davis frowned, confused. The crowd began to cheer. "And, you should also face Tempest." The crowd's cheers grew louder as Davis' frown deepened. "In fact, you should all face each other... in a Four Corners Cruiserweight Title match, tonight!" The crowd popped, and Davis threw a fit on the entrance ramp. Wonder began consulting with his ever-present tuna can, perhaps on strategy, and Tempest beamed, happy for the opportunity. "If you buy... what the Sellout... is selling!" finished Universal, hitting the catchphrase, and the crowd continued to cheer as Slaughter headed to a commercial... Jackhammer vs. Sphere vs. Adam Manson "War?" by System Of A Down begins to play and Jackhammer begins to head to the ring, dragging a cart of weapons behind him. As he nears the ring, Jackhammer begins digging in the cart, producing a chair, a 2x4, and a bottle of Mountain Drew Code Red, all of which he throws into the ring before entering himself. "Back To School" begins, and Sphere immediately rushes the ring, sliding in and taking it to Jackhammer with a series of rights. He whips Jackhammer into the ropes, and catches him coming off with a hiptoss, which he reverses. Sphere re-reverses it, and in the process hiptosses Jackhammer up and over the top rope. Jackhammer splats on the floor, and Sphere grabs the chair that Jackhammer had tossed into the ring earlier and slides outside with it. As Jackhammer gets up, Sphere cracks the steel over the top of his head. Sphere continues whaling away on Jackhammer, and "Burning Blood" begins to play, signaling that Adam Manson has finally arrived. Manson slowly makes his way to the ringside area, like a tiger confidently stalking his prey, knowing that once he's there there'll be no stopping him. Jackhammer crumples down to the floor, and Sphere shoves the timekeeper away and grabs his seat. He approaches Jackhammer with a chair in either hand, and as Jackhammer makes his way to his feet again, Sphere swings both chairs, and sandwiches the head of Jackhammer in between them. Jackhammer falls to the floor, but Sphere shows no mercy and brings him up again. He picks up the larger Jackhammer and drapes him over his shoulder, before charging forward and ramming him back-first into the ringpost. Sphere rolls Jackhammer back into the ring, and covers him. Manson finally hits the ring, just in time to break it up at two. Manson brings Sphere up and shoves him back-first into the corner, and picks up the 2x4 lying in the ring. Manson rears back and takes an Ichiro cut. Much like the Japanese outfielder, he nails his target. The 2x4 strikes Sphere so hard it shatters in half. Manson brings Sphere out of the corner and takes him down with a Yakuza kick. Manson then takes the 2x4 fragment and turns the splintered, sharp end of it away from him, before dropping down and driving it straight into the forehead of Sphere, busting him open. Manson grinds the wood against the flesh, before hooking the leg and making a cover. Jackhammer makes the save at two kicks out at two. Jackhammer brings Manson up again and whips him into the ropes, but Manson reverses it. As Jackhammer comes off, he attempts to tilt-a-whirl him into position for an inverted piledriver, but the equally large Jackhammer shifts his weight in mid-move and flips into position for one of his own, and he hits it, driving Manson into the mat. Jackhammer quickly grabs the chair and positions it, then hooks up Manson, looking to hit the HammerFall. Before he can, Sphere taps him on the shoulder. Jackhammer turns, and Sphere sprays a mouthful of caffeine-loaded Code Red goodness right into his eyes, blinding him. Jackhammer stumbles around the ring, and Sphere hooks him up and hits the Spinal Retraction. Before he can cover, Manson comes up behind him and turns him around. He then hooks the claw on Sphere and hits the Exhale The Vile onto the steel chair. Manson makes the cover, and three seconds later, takes the victory. He kicks the broken bodies of Sphere and Jackhammer from the ring, then signals for a microphone. One is thrown to him. "Another match, another victory for the Sanity Assassin. But you know what, I'm sick of easy wins. I want a challenge. So, if anybody's got the balls to meet me in the fWo's third ever Empty Arena match... bring it on." The crowd gasped as Manson invoked the name of one of wrestling's most dangerous matches. Seconds passed, then "Beautiful People" (minus You Can't Deny Fate so as to identify the possessor of the music) hit, and the mammoth Crucifix appeared. "You want it... you got it. But, we can have the Empty Arena at Damned... let's do it in front of all these people, right now." Crucifix then charged the ring, and the two hardcore giants began to brawl. Security quickly flooded the ring to break it up, and Crucifix and Manson simply pushed them all aside and went back at each other again. This continued as Slaughter went backstage... Thus, it stood to reason that, this being the worst possible time, he would bump into Erik Kelly. And sure enough, he did. Bear stumbled back, pushed back by the larger girth of Kelly. Vengence stood behind Kelly, as did Matt Loco. Kelly stopped and stared at him. "Why don't you watch where you're going, jackass?" Bear looked up at Kelly, anger blazing in his eyes. "What... did you just say?" "I said watch where you're going, jackass." "Watch this!" Bear swung at Kelly and nailed him, and Kelly stumbled back. At the first sign of violence, Loco scattered, hiding behind a trash can and watching. Kelly swung back at Bear, and Vengence joined him. Attracted by the sounds of the fight, Ruben Ross, Hellrazor, and the Black Panther poured out of the locker room to join the fray. The two groups fought tooth-and-nail, and were suddenly joined by three new fighters Fear, Fyre, and a man who was familiar to long-time viewers of the fWo Dark Angel, former tag team champion, who had apparently been recruited by Fear to join he and Fyre. The three groups traded fists, with Reckless Abandon, being outnumbered, getting the worst of it. Fear, meanwhile, grabbed Ruben Ross and slammed his head into the wall, which shattered, causing Ross' head to go through the wall. Vengence grabbed the trash can Matt Loco was hiding behind, causing Loco to scatter, and clocked the Black Panther with it. The brawl continued as Slaughter headed out to the ring... "Engel" plays, and the crowd continues to cheer loudly as Mike Bear makes his way to the ring, looking incredibly focused for his chance to regain the World Title. Bear steps into the ring and looks across the ring at the Strangler, then looks at the entrance ramp for his opponent. "Christian Says" begins. The crowd explodes with boos. Ric Chronos makes his way to the ring, fWo World Title strapped around his waist, shrugging off the boos. He climbs up the ring steps and into the ring, where he unstraps the title and grudgingly hands it to the Strangler, who raises it to signify that it's on the line, then hands it off to the timekeeper.
