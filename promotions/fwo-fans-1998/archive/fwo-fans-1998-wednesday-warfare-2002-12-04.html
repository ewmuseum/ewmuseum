Introduction Previously... The ownership of the fWo finally established, owner Travis Beaven made a startling announcement at last week's Warfare. Rather than rejoin the active roster as a commissioner or any kind of public figure, the fWo would not have a public owner at all... hopefully eliminating the corruption and power struggles seen too often in the past. Naming trainers Silver Fox, Samuel Smooth, Twitch and Thumper as road agents and mediators to get matches signed, the former owner vanished from the scene leaving the fWo to rise or fall on its own merit. The fWo World Heavyweight Championship was returned to the man who had never been beaten for the title, Erik Kelly. In addition, an amazing match between fWo newcomer Kellen Kinkaid and Brand Frontier brought Crusader closer and closer into the mix, the animosity building to a fever pitch between the men. But the evening was marred by the return of Eron to the active fWo roster, and the arrival of Graphic Violence as a wrestler that all must now deal with as a regular part of the fWo. Will the attempt for peace be marred by the cancer growing within the fWo? The agenda Silver Fox looked around the dusty room at Samuel Smooth, Twitch, Thumper and Buckley Talbot... aka Kid Cool. He sighed. "None of us expected this. While Travis said that we don't have any direct responsibility, I believe that we do have some responsibility in how we treat the privilege that has been given to us. The fWo is a large organization. Worse, it's a very public organization. What we do is discussed in the dirt sheets, on the internet... hell, every jackass on the internet can create a screen name and say whatever they want. In this new era we are not responsible for pushing wrestlers into feuds or into storylines. From this moment forward the ownership of a wrestler's career is in the hands of the wrestler. If they want to be on the show, they get on the show. If they don't, they won't. There is no evil owner to blame for getting screwed. So where does this leave us?" Samuel Smooth grunted. "It leaves us with all of the work and none of the glory. Damn, this sucks. I'm gonna try to get into one of those other feds, one of those feds where people respect what you do, dig. I wonder if that Action promotion would take..." "You?" Twitch laughed. "Give me a break mate, you're a bloody piece of crap you are. Honestly, you couldn't hang with Homeless Gus... let alone anyone else. Give it up man, you're lucky to even be in wrestling. Ass." Smooth stood up, face growing red. "You take that back... or I'll... or I'll..." Thumper snorted. "You'll what? Lay down some of that blackploitation riff on us? Please sister, sit down and shut up... Fox is right, we do have some responsibility here. Our job isn't to stand in front of the camera, it's to be the grease that keeps this system running. We do our job best when nobody sees us. People get their feuds going, the get a match ready, we help them sign it. New people come in, we give them that first step to get going. You see mate? It's a good job... it's like our old trainer jobs but on a bigger scale." Smooth rolled his eyes, but Silver Fox nodded. "That's right. Speaking of new people..." Silver Fox glanced down at a roster sheet and sighed again, more deeply this time. "By my calculations we've got over sixty people in the fWo right now, and at least thirty contracts outstanding that Dudley signed and we haven't seen drop yet. We've got a massive roster... what are we going to do with all of these people?" "Oh! Oh!" Kid Cool spoke for the first time, holding his arm up in the air and waving madly. Silver Fox nodded to him, a smile on his face. "You don't need to raise you hand Buckley. Now go on." "Well..." Kid Cool stood, puffing his chest out importantly. "As the official webmaster for the fWoWrestling.com property I've been doing some thinking about this very thing. We have Sunday Night Slaughter starting this week. This means the fWo will have two weekly shows. Now, work with me here... what if we split the roster in two? You know, like we could have two separate rosters running with each roster exclusive to each show. Now, to start this off we could have like a draft, with someone picking wrestlers for Warfare and someone picking wrestlers for Slaughter. See? Now, check this... what if we had like a champion for each show? We could double our total champions! Think of it, fans would tune into both shows and see double the action! Then, to keep things exciting wrestlers could occasionally jump from one roster to the other... or be traded! What do you think?" Silence filled the room. Thumper coughed. "Yeah..." Silver Fox rubbed his chin... "Yeah... that idea sucks. It wouldn't draw well, it would hurt the overall brand, and in the end it would only weaken the fWo as a whole. The titles would become meaningless and it would just annoy the fans. Sorry, that idea would just never work on a national level." "Or a local level," chimed in Twitch. "That idea is bloody awful." Smooth was nodding. "Yeah, even I think that idea sucks. Where did you come up with it?" Kid Cool sat down, deflated. "I thought of it on the toilet." "Not surprising." Silver Fox nodded. "Now, we've got a show to run, but let's all think of this problem. Remember, we've got a responsibility to these people out there. Let's make them proud of us." Commentary Chris Finn looked down at himself and smiled. He was back. Back in the fWo, and back behind the desk as a commentator. Back at the helm of Wednesday Warfare, the show that he first started on when the fWo got that television all those years ago. Smiling, he looked down at a collection of photographs taken over the years. There he was, sitting next to James Armstrong with a serious expression on his face. There he was with the short lived beard that had never quite grown in properly. There he was, his forehead sweating badly when the fWo production crew had decided that spotlights were best situated right over the announcers table... "Been a long time, Finn. How you doing?" Chris Finn turned and smiled. "James. How are you?" James Armstrong had lost a lot of weight and looked a shell of his former self. He smiled back, but it was a tight smile, a restrained smile. "One day at a time Finn, one day at a time. I'm lucky you know... they've got me in rehab, paid for through the fWo. Maybe I can kick this thing, maybe not. I've got three years worth of community service, and I suppose I should be lucky... real lucky... that is all. Dudley... he really did a number on me. But I opened the door. One day at a time, one step at a time." Finn nodded. "Take care of yourself Armstrong... I expect to see you back sitting next to me at that desk out there. Hear?" Armstrong nodded, clapped Chris Finn on the shoulder, and closed the door to the hallway with a light snap. Chris Finn looked back at the photographs, then put them into his suitcase and closed it up. It was a new day, a new era. A new partner would be by his side, someone who, at the very least, had to be more respectful of him than Armstrong was. James was a great guy... but Chris was looking forward to less taunting in the future. The door slammed open again. A barking voice broke the silence. "Freakboy, you understand two things now..." Chris Finn turned, eyebrows raised. "Steel? Is that you?" "That's Markus Steel, Finn. Markus. Get the name right, and don't sass me boy, hear?" Markus Steel was wearing a suit, and Chris Finn felt with a feeling of deep dread what was coming next. "Now let's get out there boy, we've got us a show to call! "Anyway, let's get going. The show is starting soon." Paladin vs. Spyder Paladin stepped out through the curtains and eyed the crowd with suspicion. Shrugging, he blinked back the bright lights in his face and made his way down the entrance ramp and climbed into the ring. Paladin slapped hands with the fans as he went, shaking his free arm as he did so. He was badly stiff from ring rust... he hadn't wrestled in weeks and before that it had been months. This was a long way from the glory days of the CWL with Dayrunner and Paladin. He missed his partners, and wished that he could have convinced them to come with him to the fWo. Paladin climbed the steps into the ring, still shaking his leg to clear it from momentary numbness when the lights dimmed and his music slowly drifted off. He turned as he entered the ring to see the entrance of his opponent, smiling grimly as he did so. Strobe lights pulsed, then mild fireworks blazed as Spyder leapt out from the curtains and charged down the entrance ramp. Paladin backed off as he slid into the ring, ran to the ropes, and leaned over them with one fist high in the air. Spyder shook his head with the pulsing music, nodding to the beat and smiling to himself as he worked up the crowd. The bell rang, and Spyder paced around the ring eying his opponent. Paladin winced back the pain of his stiff joints and tracked Spyder around the ring. Spyder was hopping from one foot to the other, and looked mildly anxious. Paladin flexed his muscles, and moved in. The two men locked up, with the larger Paladin easily getting the better of the smaller man. Paladin twisted the lockup into a quick waistlock, managing his opponent in the ring as he eyed the ropes. He moved in, but too slowly, his joints still hurting from the lack of use and Spyder quickly reversed the hold into a cross body takedown onto the mat. Spyder slapped him on the back of the head, then applied a quick headlock in the center of the ring. Paladin shot an arm back and rolled the smaller man up for a quick two count. Paladin was moving now, starting to feel the rhythm of the match. He smiled, reached down, and caught an elbow in the face for his efforts. Paladin grunted and spat blood as Spyder got to his feet with a quick kip up and locked up with a dazed Paladin. This time his speed and momentum allowed Spyder to twist Paladin into a quick headlock, but the bigger man shoved him off. Spyder went for a hiptoss, but Paladin blocked it and went for a hiptoss of his own. Spyder locked the leg and popped an elbow into Paladin's jaw, then grabbed the neck for a quick standing DDT. Both men fell, and Spyder hooked the leg for another two count. Both men got to their feet again, Spyder falling back into the ropes and rebounding off with momentum. Paladin ducked the clothesline and brought a leg up on Spyder's return. Spyder ran full-force into the boot, and fell back onto the mat, dazed and unconscious. Paladin blinked and looked at the referee. Spyder was moaning on the mat, but the impact of the boot didn't seem like anything close to being that hard. Paladin dropped to the mat and hesitantly went for the cover. Spyder jerked an arm up with a rake of the eyes and Paladin stumbled back, dazed and howling in pain. Spyder jumped to his feet once more, went back into the ropes, and hit a sliding dropkick that caught Paladin in the knees. The big man fell, and Spyder went back to the ropes for a huracanrana. The impact was loud on the mat, and Spyder followed it up with an armbar takedown into an ankle lock. Paladin, still bleeding from the mouth and dazed from the quick offensive flurry from Spyder, tapped out. The bell sounded, and Spyder's music played out through the arena. Spyder scaled the turnbuckle and pointed to the ceiling, nodding his head once again to the beat of the music. His eyes were wide, and the smile on his face was unbroken from the mixed reaction from the crowd. He felt alive... he felt energized... and he felt little else. He stood on the ropes, looking at the wrestlers, and then hollered, "If I have to kill him to win this match, so fucking be it!" The watchful eyes of the wrestlers turned into rage, as some attempted to go down the ramp, before being stopped by familiar security. Eron looked over his shoulder, seeing the still downed Fallen. Bouncing a few times on the ropes, Eron springboarded off, and dropped onto Fallen's back, knees first. Fallen held his back in excruciating pain, smashing his fist on the mat to hope the pain goes away. Eron picked Fallen back up by the hair, lifting his limp body up. He held Fallen up, looking at him about to fall. Eron then slowly walked away, a smirk on his face. Turning around, with Fallen still standing, Eron shoved Fallen with his hands, as Fallen hit the mat and slumped out of the ring. The fans booed at Eron's cockiness, as more garbage began to fill the ring. Eron encouraged the crowd, calling them on, telling them to throw more, as he got his wish. The referee took a shoe to the head, as he fell down onto his knees from the projectile Puma. Eron looked at the referee and laughed, before turning around, the sight not as humourous as the last time he seen Fallen. Fallen was back in the ring. Fallen wasn't grudging in pain. Fallen had a steel chair in hand. Eron looked at Fallen, a bit of fear into his eyes, before he shooked his head. "Are you that pathetic? You have to hit me with a fucking steel chair?" Eron exclaimed, pointing at Fallen, who refused to move a muscle or show any emotion. "Ha, this proves just how desperate you are. You know what, go ahead, hit me with the chair. It's a freebie. Because all it tells me is just how damn useless wrestlers are today. No-one can win a match fair, they have to use steel. So go ahead." Eron then turned his back to Fallen, staring straight at the wrestlers up on the ramp, his arms extended straight in a jesus Christ pose. After a few seconds went by, Eron smirked, feeling he won his argument. Turning slowly around, Eron got his answer. Cold, hard steel to the face. Fallen wrapped the chair around Eron's skull with a solid shot to the head, and repeated it two more times, before launching the chair through the ropes. The crowd exploded in cheers, loving every minute of Eron's cockiness getting the better of him. Eron slumped to the mat, as Fallen hammered fists to Eron's face, helping break open his bloody lip even more. The referee gained sense of the match again, realizing the shoe didn't hurt that much. Fallen picked Eron up from his red hair, as now it was unknown whether it was still red due to hair dye or blood. Fallen held Eron in place, and swung him back down to the mat with a hard snap mare Suplex, which took another cheer from the crowd. Fallen slowly covered Eron, having to swim through fWo programs and soda pop cups, as the referee smacked his hand to the debris covered ring. One. Two. Th... kickout. Eron launched his feet up, trying to blink as blood dripped down his face. Fallen brought him back to his feet, before spinning Eron in a reverse DDT position. Not any reverse DDT, the Lowfall. As Fallen began to lift Eron up to drop him, Eron swung his body up, kicking Fallen right in the face with his shin as the two of them fell down to the mat. The two slowly stood back up, as Eron attacked first, dropping Fallen down with a hard clothesline. Eron slowly covered, as the crowd sat still. One... two... kickout! Eron slowly got onto his feet, trying to shake the crushing chairshots out of him. Finally gaining some sense of direction, Eron stumbled over to Fallen, picking him up, then dropping him fast down with a sidewalk slam. The crowd gasped from the thunderous crunch the cups made underneath Fallen's back. Eron cracked his neck back and forth, as the crowd seen a strange rejuvenation in Eron, as nothing had affected him the past ten minutes. Eron picked Fallen up and put him in a scissor lock position, before lifting him up in a crucifix powerbomb. Holding him high, and keeping him there to show the entire crowd, Eron pushed him up high so the thrust of the move would be more devastating. But while Fallen was up in the air, he turned his body around and locked onto Eron's head. As Fallen fell to the mat, Eron's head followed with it, as the crowd erupted off their feet. Fallen had dropped Eron with a high impact Lowfall. Following the momentum, Fallen hooked Eron's leg fast, as the referee pulled himself into position. One! Eron laid still, moving only from the shock of the move. Two! Eron began to move again, slowly rolling his body. Three! Eron kicked his legs out, but it was one split second too late. The referee signalled for the bell to be sounded, as the fans cheered loudly and the wrestlers celebrated. Eron had been shut up. Eron rolled over, smashing his fists to the mat as he obviously still had a bit of energy in him left. Fallen slowly began to move over to his stomach, pulling himself up with his arms. He looked up, seeing the band of wrestlers cheering for his win. Fallen began to curve a smile, but began to see black before he could enjoy it. Result: Fallen, by Pinfall Revelations Eron dropped his foot down on the back of Fallen's head with brute force, crushing his face into the mat. Full of rage, Eron continued to stomp, kick and brutalize Fallen, angry about his loss. The wrestlers started to try to rush the ring, but the security was too much. Eron kept kicking Fallen in the kidneys, viciously attacking him like an animal, with rage filled into his eyes. Wrestlers shoved and pushed, trying to get through the security, but it was impossible to try to push through. Nothing could stop Eron's cowardice and anger, but the words he was waiting to hear. "I did it." The words rang through the building, as Eron stopped his kicks. He looked up at the crowd of wrestlers, as they all stopped struggling with the guards. They all began to split apart, creating a path, as a man began to walk through it. He was wearing street attire, but he usually wrestles in it anyway, but this seemed different. Holding a microphone in hand, the crowd popped at his sight loud. The sight of Mr. Chris Kidman Jr. Also known as Wretch. Eron kicked Fallen a bit, rolling him out of the ring as he felt in a heap to the mats below. Wretch looked straight at Eron, as Eron began to move over for a microphone. A microphone was thrown Eron's way as he caught it, and looked up at Wretch. "Don't waste my time... Wretch." Eron said, having to catch his breath. Wretch look straight at him. "Save your breath. Better yet, don't say a word, because your voice is enough to make me want to vomit, you fucking boil." Wretch sternly commented, the crowd and wrestlers taken back by Wretch's new found anger. Even Eron seemed shocked. "I tried, god damnit I hoped, that I would never have to see you eye to eye again. That I would never have to be in the same company as you. But it seems you slithered your way around that. Yeah. It was me. It was also me who got the writers to set you and me up for Meltdown. It was also me who made sure you never had many matches after Meltdown. Because I can't... fucking stand you! And I don't mean like the crowd out here hating you because you put them to sleep, I mean, you are like a disease. You can't ignore cancer, you can't tell it to leave, and maybe some treatments might keep it away for a while, but it just comes back. And guess what, you did. And I just can't watch the fWo fall apart again. I just can't!" Wretch yelled into the microphone, the emotion dripping through his words. Eron shook his head as fWo personnel helped Fallen to his feet and lifted him away. "I made the fWo fall apart? No no, I..." Wretch cut Eron off. "I said shut up! This company was great until you screwed Frenchie way back at Bodycount! Everything was going great, we were building new stars, we were establishing ourselves as the top wrestling promotion in the world, and then you leak into the spotlight. And it seemed after that, no matter who we pushed, no matter who we brought, nothing could prevent the fWo from becoming what it is today. The fWo looked like Nagasaki or Hiroshima now. It's even hard to find the remains of something great. And now, finally, after that committee was thrusted out, what do we have? You. The one who caused it all in the first place." It seems like taking things the back door didn't work, I guess I'll have to finish you the way I should have way back when I had the chance. In the ring. You and me Eron. Twilight. I don't want any countouts, I don't want any disqualifications, I don't want any time limits. If I have to take you to hell and back to finally silence you once and for all, god dammit I will!" Wretch screamed in the microphone, as the crowd finally cheered loud. Eron looked around, before replying. "Twilight it is."
