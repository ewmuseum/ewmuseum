Introduction Cyberslam and their trials outside of the United States behind them, the fWo enters their sixth year with unmapped promise before them. Their reputation has been damaged over the course of the year thus far, but back on home ground the wrestlers and road agents alike are confident that they can overcome these obsticles. For the first time Silver Fox will meet with the new Board of Directors... a group of investors gathered last year to help support the organization through a rebuilding phase. No longer satisfied with their shadow status, the six investors want to take a more active role in running the promotion. On Warfare new battle lines were drawn and attention focused on Cyberslam has moved toward the months ahead. New alliances and new enemies have formed, as all of the roster struggles to put themselves in the best possible position for the new year. Alliances are formed and nearly just as quickly discarded as the struggle for power becomes ever more dangerous... The meeting begins Silver Fox glanced up at the conference room listing posted on the side of the wall. It was a posh hotel, and by the look of things he was going to get one hell of a dinner here. All the same, it didn't feel right to be voluntarily away from the arena while the boys were putting on a show. He wondered how Beaven, Universal and Frankton had done it... not easily, that's for sure. Or perhaps that was the role they were most comfortable with. Hard to say... but it wasn't for him. All those years of being the guy in the pink suit with the schemes and manipulations... hell, Flair had taken most of his act from him. But in reality... give him a sweatshirt, a towel and put him in the pit watching the monitors... that's where he belonged. Room 42. Board of Director's Meeting, fWo. Fair enough. Silver Fox wondered idly how the management liked the idea of a wrestling organization holding a meeting in a hotel as nice as this. Of course, he supposed, it was quite possible that one of the new board owned the place. Unlike previous managers... this group was loaded. And, aside from the fWo, seemed to have no particular interest in professional wrestling. Times are changing, that's for sure. The group was seen far more as an entertainment property rather than a wrestling organization. Silver Fox supposed there was a lot more money in the former than the later, but it still didn't feel completely... right. He turned a corner and nearly ran into a large man in a cowboy hat. William Humbold, one of the investors. "Boy! You done nearly stampede me over! Come on in here... we're just waitin' on one more... little flaky if you ask me, might not show up at all. Come on in, come on in!" Silver Fox smiled in spite of himself. Humbold wasn't a bad guy at all... likely the most positive member of their new board. Looking around the large conference room as he stepped in, he saw large plush chairs and a wide desk. Men sat around it... well, mostly men. One boy sat at one of the chairs, looking grumpy. His mother sat behind him along the wall, humming mildly to herself while doing some knitting. Around the room there was Mr. Tort, still in a wheelchair and currently being glared at by his young assistant. William Humbold took a seat next to him, his cowboy hat still firmly in place. A man in a black and green suit sat next to him, and hard as he might Silver Fox couldn't quite dismiss the idea that he had met this man before. An empty seat, then the young boy, Jon, still looking bored and sullen. Next to him sat James Miller, the lawyer from what he gathered. Five of them... seven with himself and the boy's mother. "Sit down, sit down... she's not going to show up, so let's get this started." Tort gestured to a chair and Silver Fox sat. She... the sixth member of the board was a woman, and someone he hadn't seen before. He lay his briefcase before him and nodded, ready to get started. "Gentlemen, thank you for the opportunity. I think I speak for..." But he was cut off as the door swung open and a bubbly voice... a very familiar bubbly voice... poured into the room. "Sorry I'm late! Oh it was the most awful taxi ride. The driver didn't know much english and he smelled and he had this funny little hat and... oh, hello sugar!" Nikki leaned over and kissed Silver Fox on the cheek, then grinned and growled and shoved her tounge into his mouth. Silver Fox's eyes went wide and he gasped and pulled backward. "Nikki! God, you can't be in here... I..." Silver Fox stared wildly around the room. "I'm very sorry that..." "You have nothing to apologize for," said Mr. Tort, his brow creasing. "This young lady has a habit of not taking her obligations seriously, despite her status as a member of this board. Now, for the love of God, sit!" Nikki rolled her eyes and dropped down into a chair. "What did I miss?" Silver Fox felt something like rising panic. This was not going to go well.
