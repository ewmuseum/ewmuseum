Wednesday Warfare, 14 July 1999

14 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Chris Finn paced the backstage area, rubbing his hands together and looking nervous. He waited outside the closed door of the office of the commissioner, nervously fidgeting against the wall as security officials, cameramen, referees and wrestlers paraded past. Some waved at Chris Finn, others ignored him, and some mocked him while he wasn't looking. As he waited, the Frenchie walked past and looked at the closed door, exclaiming "Zut! Is he in zere?" Finn nodded, and the Frenchie smiled broadly and clapped him on the back. "Well, zu mention to zim zat I am owed ze match with ze men in ze French Foundation tonight." Finn frowned at him, and shot back, "Hey, I'm not your answering machine, tell him yourself." The Frenchie raised his eyebrows and smiled, holding up a brochure for the French Embassy and waving it back and forth. Sighing Finn said, "I'll tell him. Anything else... sir?" The Frenchie simply smiled and walked away, as Finn sighed and fell back against the wall with a quiet thud. The Speed Demon wandered by, and spotted Finn against the wall looking frustrated. Speed Demon propped up on one elbow next to Chris and asked him, "Hey Finn, what's up? You look stressed... not good for the skin you know..." Finn sighed and waved his arms mildly in the air exclaiming, "Oh... it's this Davis thing. I've never seen the commissioner so quiet and secluded... and he's been dieting... out of the office running... you know, I think he's planning on facing Davis himself. But here I am, the main commentator in the promotion and I don't have any idea of what is going on... I can't work like this, you know? But hey... what about you... how's life treating you?" The Speed Demon shrugged, and said, "Oh, can't complain. I haven't had a match in like... forever, but I've been training and learning some new stuff... oh, and there is this really hot woman I keep running into, but she has a boyfriend or something, and I..." The Speed Demon slowly stopped talking and his mouth hung agape as he looked in the direction of the backstage entrance doors. Finn furrowed his brow and asked, "Hey... what's going on Speed? You look like you've seen a..." But then Finn's words trailed off as he saw what the Speed Demon was looking at. A haggard form stumbled toward them, face dirty and hair stringy. When John Skyler's face rose he was unshaven and dirty, and his eyes were scarred with dark black circles. His eyes, highlighted by the dark shadows, looked distant and red as they gazed around the room looking for something beyond their ability. John Skyler slowly raised his hands, and they were dyed red with blood... blood that splattered his forearms and caked under his fingernails. Moving slowly toward Chris Finn and the Speed Demon, John Skyler's lips began to move and in a trembling voice said, "Chris... I think I've done something really, really bad..." Prophet vs. Matthew Zhuk The Prophet comes to the ring to kick off his "Back-In-Blue World Tour" with the first match of the tour against Matthew Zhuk. Prophet walks out in a "Prophetmania" t-shirt and he rips it off, showing his somewhat impressive physique. He climbs into the ring where Zhuk already awaits, and poses in the ring. He points at some fans and asks them "Whatcha gonna do when the Prophet runs wild on you?" Prophet then turns around and Zhuk goes for a superkick, but Prophet ducks. While ducking, Prophet reaches up and nails Zhuk with a low-blow in the testicular area. Prophet then grabs Zhuk, and body slams him. He then cups his ear as the crowd roars in approval. Prophet hits a big leg drop before covering Zhuk, and quickly finishing the match. Prophet gets to his feet and starts posing, as the fans cheer him on. However, Zhuk gets up and with Prophet' back to him, this time he does hit the superkick and Prophet slumps to the mat. Zhuk continues to pound away on Prophet as fWo security officials make their way to the ring and try to re-store order, while Zhuk continues to open a can on Prophet... Result: Prophet by Pinfall Mike Bear's Rage As Slaughter came back from a commercial, Mike Bear was on his way to the ring, obviously still mad from the match at Fight the Future 3. He circled the ring, going over to Matt Panzer and grabbing a microphone. He then went up the ring steps, got into the ring and once again began yelling towards the locker rooms. "Hey Falcon! Now I know you're ducking me! I had that match won until you came in. You're a coward, Falcon! Now I demand that match this Friday at Fight the Future 4. You promised that match and I deserve it!" Just then, Falcon appeared on the TravisTron with Wretch behind him, hiding. Falcon had a big smile on his face. "Well, you did put yourself in a dangerous situation with Wretch, and you got pinned 1-2-3 right in the center of the ring. The only thing you seem to deserve is my pity." As Falcon continued to rub this tainted loss in Bear's face, Mike grew incensed, hitting the ring ropes and mat in a fit of rage. Falcon, in a partronizing voice, continued, "Hey, settle down. Since you are so pathetic, I'll take pity on you and give you a match" Hearing this, that evil smile of Mike's spread across his face, until Falcon continued, "It'll be a tag match. Me and Wretch will take you on, you got till Friday to find a partner. Hearing this turn of events, Bear started getting flustered and extremely angry at the same time, until finally he just screamed out, "FALCON YOU'RE DEAD!" and sprinted back to the locker room. Falcon faded out of the TravisTron, laughing. VL vs. Scott Hart With Scott Hart already in the ring when Warfare returns from the commercial break, VL Tha Murdera & Stevie Roberts walk out to a chorus of boos from the fans. VL & Stevie have their fWo World Tag Team Titles in their possession, while Scott Hart looks somewhat bored in the ring. Roberts tells the fans that he is the master of shagging, and if they wait awhile, they will be able to see him and VL shag all night long in the ring. Roberts holds the ropes open for VL, who enters the ring looking at Hart with disdain in her eyes. Hart walks over, and appears kind of uneasy about what he's going to say. Hart asks VL if she would like to team with him. VL looks at Stevie, who laughs, and VL laughs as well. VL looks back in the direction of Hart, and bitch slaps him across the face. Hart holds his face with one hand as he looks at VL, who grabs him and throws him into Roberts, who kicks him in the stomach and Samoan drops him onto the mat. Roberts then slides outside the ring as the match begins and VL opens up a can of smackdown on Hart. VL grabs Hart and rakes his eyes across the top rope, before throwing him into the turnbuckles, and kicking him several times. VL then brings him out and hits a piledriver in the center of the ring on Hart. VL appears disinterested in the match, as Hart is proving to be no challenge for her. Meanwhile, the Surgeon comes down to ringside to discuss things with Stevie. Roberts completely blows him off though, and back in the ring VL has dropped Hart on his head with a Michinoku Driver. VL covers Hart and gets the pin, and Roberts goes up into the ring and celebrates with VL... Surgeon continues to try to talk to Stevie, but Stevie continues to blow him off and finally Surgeon gives up hope and leaves. Roberts and VL continue to celebrate as we go backstage to see Chris Universal making a phone call... Result: VL by Pinfall Trouble. Backstage fWo officials tended to John Skyler, who had fallen asleep shaking on a small bench backstage. Geoff Green and Travis stood over him, looking concerned at his condition. "He's lost it Travis," said Geoff Green who spoke in a quiet, confidential tone. Travis asked, "Have you found Dina?" Geoff shook his head silently, and the two shared a long, hard glance before looking back down at Skyler. From above, thunder rolled, and both men looked up at the ceiling. "You know Travis... Dina never showed at Numbered Days... and we didn't hear from..." Suddenly the door on the opposite end of the room slammed open, and torrents of water shot into the room. Everyone ducked as the storm raged, and got to their feet shaken as footsteps were heard from the outside hall. A long shadow formed on the floor, and slunk up the wall as the man drew nearer. Rain entered the room, and looked around at the inhabitants with a hard, dead glare. Speaking slowly Rain said, "Where is he?" Chris Universal (c) vs. Eron the Relentless Eron The Relentless comes out to an ovation of boos from the crowd, who don't appear to take kindly to relentless people around here. Relentless climbs into the ring and shouts at the crowd, telling them to screw off. The fWo Internet Champion "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal begins to make his way out as his music plays in the background. Universal has the Internet Title draped over his shoulder, and he lets the crowd know that it's his. Universal climbs up onto the ring steps and into the ring, before posing as his dollar $ign pyrotechnic lowers into place and he poses as green and gold fireworks shoot out all over. Suddenly, Eron charges at Universal and knocks him back into the pyrotechnic, just as the last fireworks had shot out. Luckily for Universal, he was not harmed by the pyros. The bell rings and the match is off and running as Eron continues the attack. He slams Universal' head into the top turnbuckle repeatedly. He repeats the process, this time into the middle turnbuckle. And finally, into the lower turnbuckle. He brings Universal to his feet and kicks him in the stomach, before whipping him into the ropes. Universal returns and Eron takes him off his feet with a hard lariat. Universal crashes into the mat. Eron kicks him in the ribs several times, before kicking him out underneath the bottom rope and onto the floor. Universal sits on the floor as Eron shouts at the crowd some more, and they continue to boo him back as well. Universal looks up at Eron, before wiping a spot of blood from the side of his mouth. He seems taken aback by the reckless style of Eron, but appears to have a plan of some kind in mind. Universal walks over and grabs a chair... he slides it into the ring and waits on the floor. Eron walks over to grab it, but Universal grabs one of his legs and pulls him down onto the mat. He then pulls Eron outside and the two begin exchanging punches. Universal gets the upper hand however with a thumb to the eye, and then he kicks Eron in the stomach. He throws Eron shoulder first into the ringsteps, before rolling him inside the ring. He bounces off the ropes and drives a knee into the forehead of Eron. He goes for a cover, but Eron quickly kicks out. Universal slaps on a reverse chinlock, which lasts for several moments, before Eron struggles to his feet while Universal keeps the hold intact. Eron drops down quickly, and Universal' jaw goes crashing down onto the skull of Universal, automatically forcing him to relinquish the hold. Eron holds onto his head for a moment, before getting up and grabbing Universal and throwing him over the top rope. He then goes to the outside and ddt's Universal on the floor. He then continues to kick at Universal repeatedly; almost relentlessly. Suddenly, Megan Dawn begins to make her way out. Eron sees her, and goes up to the stage and confronts her. He begins pointing back towards the locker room and tells her to "get the hell out of here". This gives Universal time to recover. Universal grabs a chair from ringside, but first takes part of the ringsteps and slides it into the ring. The referee follows Universal up the ramp... Dawn shoves Eron in the chest, which barely harms him, but it's a good enough push that he goes backfirst into the chair swing of Universal. Dawn and the referee begin arguing as Universal hits Eron again, before dragging Universal by the hair up into the ring. There, he hits the "Cha-Ching!" on Eron in the ring on the ringsteps, before he slides them out onto the floor. Dawn informs the referee of the pinfall, and he runs back down into the ring. He counts it, but somehow Eron kicks out at two. Universal brings him up to his feet and rakes his eyes, before nailing him with a low-blow in the mid-section. He follows it up with a pedigree, before covering him again and this time getting the pinfall. Universal though is still shook up by the attack of Eron, and he slides outside the ring. Universal hugs Dawn, before he holds his Internet Title high above his head. He makes his way up the ramp with Dawn before they reach backstage. The cameras follow him and Universal asks Dawn for a cell phone, and Dawn pulls one out of her suit pocket. Universal dials a few numbers before speaking. The audible isn't clear, but he tells the person on the other end of the phone call that they don't need to show up Friday at the house show, he can handle it... Result: Chris Universal by Pinfall The challenge... Fans got to their feet as the music of Ground Zero played, but looked confused as only Jackhammer came to the ring. Jackhammer asked for a microphone, and stood for a moment, pondering exactly what he was going to say. Finally, Jackhammer blurted out, "You know, I'm disappointed that Black Quicksilver joined up with Silver Fox. And you know what? I'm not the only one! There are all the Black Quicksilver fans out there who were disappointed, and there was someone else Black Quicksilver... someone from your past..." Before Jackhammer could go any farther, the Chameleon and Black Quicksilver walked out onto the entrance ramp and shouted for Jackhammer to get out of the ring. Jackhammer pointed at the Chameleon and shouted, "It's partially your fault too! So I dare you to face me in the ring... at Fight the Future IV!" The Chameleon and Black Quicksilver laughed, and the Chameleon nodded and told Jackhammer that, "I'll be happy to kick your bald ass at Fight the Future, and now you should get out of the ring and stop embarrassing yourself!" Jackhammer swung over the top rope and walked up the entrance ramp to go backstage, looking at Black Quicksilver and the Chameleon as he passed. Jackhammer then stopped and reached into his pocket, pulling out a photo and handing it to Black Quicksilver. A look of shock came over Black Quicksilver's face, and he quickly pocketed the photo, not allowing the Chameleon or the cameras to see what was on the photo... Flying Frenchie vs. Primetime vs. Mitch Wilson Warfare returns from the preview for Summer Slaughter as the familiar music of the Flying Frenchie begins to play, and out walks Ze French Foundation. All three members walk out, again showing their sign of solidarity even though they will be competing here tonight against each other in a three-way dance. Primetime, Wilson & Frenchie climb up into the ring and salute the French flag that is on the TravisTron in a vidcap, before shaking hands and wishing each other luck in this match-up. The trio begin playing "rock, paper, scissors" to decide which two will start off first. Frenchie has reveals a rock, Primetime reveals scissors, and Mitch reveals scissors. Frenchie' rock smashes both of their scissors, so Mitch and Primetime play again. Mitch has scissors again this time, and Primetime reveals paper. Mitch' scissors cuts Primetime' paper, so Frenchie & Mitch will start things off here tonight. As Primetime exits the ring to stand on the ring apron, Nikki the Valley Girl comes down to ringside to lend moral support for her man Primetime. Frenchie and Mitch lock up in the center of the ring, and Mitch gains the upper hand with an armbar. Frenchie however somersaults onto the mat while still in the armbar, goes back up to his feet and takes a knee into the stomach of Mitch, before taking him down with a hip toss. Mitch gets to his feet and Frenchie takes him down with an armdrag takedown. Both men rush to their feet and Mitch charges at Frenchie, who takes him down again with a Japanese armdrag. Both men back up to their feet once more and Mitch charges at Frenchie again, and Frenchie ducks down for a backdrop. However, Mitch telegraphs it and bounces off the ropes, and returns and takes Frenchie down with a bulldog. He goes for a cover, but Primetime comes in and pulls Mitch off to stop the count. Mitch gets to his feet and pushes Primetime, who responds with a beautiful dropkick to the chest that propels Mitch out onto the floor. Frenchie comes up from behind Primetime and grabs him by the hair, before taking him down with a reverse ddt (scorpion death drop). Frenchie goes for a cover, but Nikki jumps up and that distracts the referee. Frenchie brings Primetime to his feet and whips him to the ropes. Primetime returns and Frenchie takes him down with a drop-toe-hold. Frenchie goes for a cover, but there is no referee to count. He walks over and taps on the referees shoulder, telling him he has a pinning situation over in the middle of the ring. However, Mitch is quickly in from behind and he rolls Frenchie up for a pin. Mitch has Frenchie rolled up for a pin, but PT quickly gets to his feet and grabs Mitch off of Frenchie, before fisherman suplexing him into a pin. One, two, but Mitch interrupts things by grabbing Primetime's legs and pulling, thus breaking up the pin. Mitch brings Primetime to his feet and scoop slams him onto the mat. He then goes up onto the top rope and is prepared to dive off. Mitch hits a beautiful senton that hits Primetime in the ribs area. However, it's not Mitch that makes a pinfall attempt first, the always crafty Frenchie picks his spot and covers Primetime. Mitch gets up in time and pulls Frenchie off at the count of two. He brings Frenchie to his feet and the two exchange a series of open hand punches, before Frenchie whips Mitch into the turnbuckles. Frenchie goes running in and hits a beautiful handspring elbow into the side of Mitch' jaw. Mitch staggers out and Primetime is there to take him down with a snapmare. Frenchie brings Mitch up and whips him into the ropes. Frenchie catches him in a bear hug and then propels him into Primetime who takes Mitch down with a diamond cutter of sorts. It appears there's some teamwork going on between Primetime and Frenchie. PT goes for a cover, but Frenchie pulls him off and throws him out through the middle ropes onto the floor. Frenchie goes for a cover on Mitch, but Mitch is able to kick out at the count of two. Nikki the Valley Girl checks on PT out on the floor. Frenchie brings Mitch to his feet and kicks him in the stomach. He then hits a perfectly executed vertical suplex in the middle of the ring. Frenchie goes for another cover, but Mitch kicks out again. Frenchie is obviously frustrated with his inability to pin Mitch, so he slaps on a reverse chinlock on Mitch, trying to wear him down. Primetime recovers however and he slides in underneath the bottom rope from behind the two in the ring. Frenchie has Mitch in the chinlock, and doesn't know Primetime has returned into the ring. Primetime slaps on his own reverse chinlock on Frenchie, and we have a simultaneous double reverse chinlock going on in the ring. The referee asks Mitch and Frenchie if either of them would like to give up. The Frenchie mutters something back in French, presumably swearwords. Frenchie struggles to his feet as does Mitch, but Primetime keeps his reverse chinlock intact and does Frenchie. All three men to their feet until Mitch drops down, thus breaking the hold as Frenchie goes chin first into the skull of Mitch and Primetime does the same, only into the skull of Frenchie. The trio of men are down on the mat, and the referee begins a ten count. He gets to the count of eight before Mitch recovers first. Mitch brings Frenchie to his feet and throws him over the top rope out onto the floor. He then takes Primetime and whips him sternum first into the turnbuckles. PT staggers out backwards and Mitch scoops him up in a version of the human torture rack submission move, before turning it into a neckbreaker as he drops PT onto the mat. A cover, but a kickout by PT at two. Mitch follows it up by bringing Primetime to his feet and whipping him to the ropes. PT ducks the Mitch clothesline, and returns to hit a flying forearm into the chin of Mitch. PT with a cover, but Mitch kicks out. The Frenchie recovers back up into the ring. PT turns around and Frenchie kicks him in the midsection, before taking him down with a Death Valley Driver. He goes for a cover, but Mitch pulls him off. Mitch brings Frenchie to his feet and slams him into the mat. He follows it up with a lightning-quick legdrop on Frenchie, before demanding the referee to count. One, two, but PT is back to his feet and he takes Mitch out with a sitting dropkick. PT covers Mitch, but only gets a count of two. PT pulls Mitch to his feet and hooks him with an armbar. He then knees him in the stomach, and takes him down with a modified version of the rocker dropper. PT with a cover, but Frenchie pulls him off. Frenchie takes his place, but PT pulls him off. Frenchie climbs to a vertical base and he and PT exchange reverse knife edge chops back and forth, until Frenchie gets him into the corner. Frenchie whips him out of the corner into an awaiting Mitch Wilson, who bends over for a backdrop attempt but PT jumps over for a sunset flip attempt. PT has the pinfall, but Frenchie comes up from behind and kicks PT in the back of the head. He brings PT to his feet and scoops him up, but PT lands behind him. PT with a waistlock, before charging him into the ropes. Frenchie holds onto the ropes, and PT goes backwards. PT back to his feet quickly though, but he's taken off his feet with a clothesline by Mitch. Mitch then turns around and is taken down with a French version of the "Sky High". Frenchie with a cover, but Mitch kicks out at two. Frenchie is frustrated again, so he throws Mitch out onto the floor. He follows him out onto the floor and whips Mitch into the ring post. Frenchie slides back into the ring and as PT charges at him, he pulls him by the trunks and PT goes falling out onto the ring apron through the middle ropes. PT lands on the ring apron and grasps onto the middle rope so he doesn't fall onto the cement floor. Frenchie up and he ducks under the middle ropes and nails PT with a shoulder into the midsection. Frenchie follows it up by bringing him back in with a vertical suplex, but then turns it into a michinoku driver, spearing PT back first into the mat. A cover, but only the count of two. Frenchie whips PT into the turnbuckles, and follows it up as PT staggers out with a forward slam. He then drags PT into position and signals for the 450 splash. Frenchie goes to the outside as Mitch slowly recovers on the outside. Frenchie flies off with the 450 splash but Nikki jumps up onto the ring apron to distract the referee again. Frenchie has Primetime covered, and Mitch sees this as a chance to win the match. Mitch slides back into the ring and comes up from behind, while Frenchie is still covering PT for the pinfall. Mitch slaps on the anklelock submission move and Frenchie writhes in pain, but continues to cover PT. The referee, hears Frenchie' screaming in pain, and turns around. He sees Frenchie still covering Primetime and makes the cover. Primetime kicks out a second too late as Frenchie taps the mat, obviously submitting due to the pain. However, the referee awards the match to Frenchie who pinned Primetime before submitting. Mitch is frustrated by this, and rightfully so. It looks as if there may be some tension in the French Foundation, but cooler heads prevail as Nikki tends to Primetime and the three leave the ring together, while Warfare goes to a commercial break. Result: Flying Frenchie by Pinfall Darkness vs. The Infinite Battler Darkness came to the ring, bathed in dark light and looking grim. With the gruesome events of Fight the Future still in the minds of the fans, the arena was silent as Darkness climbed over the ring ropes and glared out at the crowd. As the lights returned to normal, the crowd waited for his unknown opponent to arrive. Suddenly, loud speed metal music began to blare out over the arena speakers, and lights began to flash in rapid succession. As several small fans began to shake in epileptic seizures, a man ran to the ring with ribbons wrapped around his muscles and face paint and slid into the ring. Announced as The Infinite Battler, fans recognized this newcomer as crazy Mike Heftel. The Infinite Battler rushed Darkness, and hit a clothesline that sent the surprised Darkness to the outside. Still jogging in place, the Infinite Battle leapt over the top rope with a flying plancha and landed a direct hit on Darkness on the outside. Quickly rolling him back into the ring, the Infinite Battler whipped Darkness into the ropes and hit a flying shoulderbreaker that sent Darkness down to the mat. The Infinite Battler then attempted a Gorilla Press Slam, but by this time was breathing rather hard, and sweating rather severely. He managed to get Darkness off his feet, but then collapsed in exhaustion and a pool of sweat. Darkness fell on top of the Battler, and covered him for the three count. Looking down at the Battler in disgust, Darkness urged him to get to his feet and take more of a beating, but the winded Battler simply lay on the mat breathing rapidly. Spitting in disgust, Darkness kicked down on the Battler, landing several strikes to the head, ribs, and groin. Picking the Battler up, Darkness speared him into the announcing table, demolishing the structure into thousands of splinters of wood. As the Battler lay helpless, Darkness left the ring for the backstage area, looking for more carnage to inflict... Result: Darkness by Pinfall The attack The commissioner walked down the hallway, heading for his office. Still shaken over the arrival of John Skyler and the condition he appeared to be in, and the sudden appearance of Rain, Travis moved quickly to his office not paying attention to the fact that fWo security had left the area, and the echoing footsteps in the hall signaled that he was all alone. Travis turned the knob to his office, and noticed one moment too late the broken lock and signs of entry. Starting to back out of the office, a rough hand grabbed the back of Travis' suit and tossed him physically into the room and over a desk within. Slamming the door, Austin Davis stalked the commissioner around his office, grinning maniacally. "Is it you Beaven? Is it? Are you the one that is going to get his ass slaughtered? You know... I think it is. And you know what? I don't see why we should wait to make this promotion mine. Davis' Wrestling Organization... I like the sound of that... what do you think you miserable piece of shit? You spineless bastard... the best part of you ran down your mother's leg and I'm not sure you understand me yet..." As Davis moved in for blood, the door suddenly burst open and Briana walked through. "Hey Travis, I know you said you would get back to me about... YOU!" Briana spotted Davis stalking the commissioner, and lunged at him screaming. Officials stormed into the room as Briana and Davis brawled, trying to pull the two wrestlers apart. As Davis was carried from the room, he shouted, "You want me bitch? You want me? Show up Friday... I'll kill you... I'll..." Briana looked over at Travis, who nodded. "Teach him a lesson Briana," the commissioner said, as cameras returned to the ring... Stone (c) vs. Melissa Smalls Melissa Smalls came to the ring with Apocolyptica, and waited inside the ring for her title match. Stone came out onto the entrance ramp, and looked down at Smalls in the ring. Squinting, Stone called to the back, and Plague ran out, carrying a telescope. Stone set it up, and peered through at Smalls in the ring. Snorting, Stone exclaimed, "You want me to face THAT? You've got to be kidding me... that little girl? This division is mostly full of crap... mostly. She's not worth my time... let's get out of here Plague..." Stone turned to leave as the crowd began to boo and Apocolyptica and Smalls began to chase Stone up the entrance ramp. Stone was nearly out of the arena when Smalls caught her, and clubbed the back of her head with a stiff forearm. Apocolyptica grabbed Plague, and sent in spiraling over the edge of the entrance ramp and crashing to the floor below. Plague laughed, seemingly enjoying the pain as Smalls carried Stone back to the ring. Rolling her inside, Smalls climbed up on the ring apron and prepared to launch herself over the top rope. She flew into the ring and landed her body splash, quickly grabbing the leg of Stone and getting a two count. Stone attacked back with multiple head butts, bloodying the nose of Smalls and driving a fist upward into her face. Smalls shook this off, and delivered a standing DDT which took Stone to the mat, and quickly slapped on a boston crab. As the ref checked to see if Stone would tap out, Plague jumped onto the ring apron. The ref walked over to get him to get out of the ring area, leaving Stone an opening to jam a small metal object upward into the eyes of Smalls. Smalls reeled back in pain, and Stone made a quick cover to win the match. Stone and Plague then continued to attack Smalls, but Apocolyptica got into the ring wielding a steel chair, and sent the two Dark Riders packing... Result: Stone by Pinfall Scott Slugger (c) vs. Ultra Violet Fans got ready for the main event, an fWo World Title match between new champion Scott Slugger and former champion Ultra Violet. Ultra Violet's name was announced, but nobody came out onto the entrance ramp. His name was announced again, but still no sign of Ultra Violet was seen. Finally, cameras backstage picked up Ultra Violet staring at a television, replaying the Infinite Battler match from earlier, specifically the entrance of the Battler. Ultra Violet's mouth hung slack, and his eyes blinked rapidly at the flashing lights. Next to him sat Violet, equally stunned. Behind them Malcom kicked at their chairs, trying to urge the two to the ring, but to no avail. At ringside, Scott Slugger's music suddenly began to play, and the champion rushed out onto the entrance ramp, thrusting the fWo World Title up at the crowd. Behind him walked Ice, and the pair strutted their way to the ring, Scott Slugger sneering at the crowd and shining the World Title as he walked. Inside the ring, Slugger grabbed the microphone, and addressed the crowd. He said, "I was sitting in the backstage area, watching this crap show, and listening to a couple stupid, moron fans complaining that the World Title finally has some been given to some dignity. But there are a couple of you... denser fans... don't understand how Scott Slugger could walk out of Fight the Future III with the World Title. Now, I'd expect that idiot Ultra Violet not to understand what happened... hell, that boy's mom makes Kool-Aid in Jonestown! And as for Cactus Jon... well, I think we all know what the Canadian school system is like. So Ice and I have made a little video presentation to help the more dim-witted individuals out there get 'it'. Ice, roll it." The lights in the arena dimmed, as the fWo Big Screen came to life with footage of Scott Slugger from over a week ago. In the footage, Scott Slugger was seen getting the nod from the fWo commissioner to be the referee in the title match between Cactus Jon and Ultra Violet. Shots were then shown of Scott Slugger going down in the ring, and demanding a meeting with the commissioner, to be given additional authority as referee. Slugger was shown getting the nod to have full authority inside the ring, and named Ice as a special outside referee. Slugger was then seen in the ring just before the title match was set to begin, saying that one man in the ring would walk away the undisputed champion. Stopping the footage Scott Slugger smiled at the audience, and said, "So do you all get it yet? If any of you fools had been paying attention, you might have understood that you were getting played... but I guess you people aren't smart enough, are you? Well... I've just got one more thing to say to you fools, and that's..." A loud voice suddenly boomed over the arena speaker system shouting, "STOP!" Cactus Jon walked out onto the ramp, and pointed down at the ring saying, "That's a real nice little plan you hatched Scott Slugger, but I think you've overlooked one little detail... Ice was appointed the OUTSIDE referee... but that's not where he made the count, oh no. Ice made the count inside the ring, and you know, as I look over the contracts you both signed, I see no signs that Ice had the authority to make that count. So what I've done is arranged a little match at Summer Slaughter... a little three-way match. You, Ultra Violet, and me. So you bring your little title and your little plans, and get ready to fight two men in the ring... because this match has elimination rules, and Ultra Violet and I are going to get even with you before laying a finger on each other." Scott Slugger began to stammer and shout at Jon, as the Canadian laughed and Warfare went off the air... Result: No Contest
