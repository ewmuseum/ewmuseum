Many of you, but not all of you, could easily replace them this Friday at their infamous Cyberslam event." Frank held up an envelope. "But not one of you. For the first time in fWo Survivor history, a self-admitted suicide of sorts. Unable to cut it with your team mates and partners, you have vanished and decided you're just not cut out to be one of the elite in this business." He opened it, but did not look at the paper. He didn't need to. "Kenji Lee, your team has spoken. You have been eliminated from the contest, and once more the Gold and Silver teams are aligned and equal. But it will not be so in four days. In four days once more the teams will be imbalanced. By a ruling from our judges, four to three, we award tonight's immunity to the Silver Team. This means the Gold Team must once again return to the elimination chamber and vote out one of their own. If the last two challenges are any indication, you will be into eliminating a valuable member of your team, for a change." Frank turned, his profile to the camera. "That is all."
