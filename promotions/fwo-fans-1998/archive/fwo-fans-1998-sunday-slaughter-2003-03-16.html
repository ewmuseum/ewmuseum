Sunday Slaughter, 16 March 2003

16 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Tel Aviv, Israel. This war-torn nation has lived and died with conflict for decades, and it is here that the fWo finds itself embroiled in controversy. CyberSlam is now less than a month away, and the fWo is no closer to returning home than they were three months ago. A promotion in exile, they have born witness to nations divided by poverty, culture and strife. But nothing can prepare them for what they are about to experience... What You Didn't See on Warfare... PROGRAMMING NOTE: Due to litigation with the Israeli government, fWo was forced to keep a strict timeline on its event and close its program within the alloted time. Because of this matter, fWo programming is now going to present to you some rather shocking and disturbing footage that took place as the show went off the air between Kodiak Vic Creed and fWo World Champion Erik Kelly. --------------------- Erik shook his head. He was in pain. His neck was burning from the chair shot he took. He looked around...dazed and uncertain where Creed was. All he could see was red. Crimson red. His face was covered in blood from a gash he endured on the last chair shot to the head. He wasn't sure how many he had taken. He lost count around seven. Someone else lay on the floor, and was trying to get up. It was Scotty J. Bowman. Erik wasn't sure if he was trying to help or not, but he got laid out as well. Someone else was trying to get into the ring. That had to be "Big" Bobby Bowman. What Erik didn't know was Bobby was distracted by Matt Loco, who whacked him with a chair that didn't hurt Bowman, but pissed him off enough that when he finally saw what happened to Scotty and Kelly, it was too late. Bowman jumped up on the ring apron, only to be speared like a wild boar from a chair shot to the ribs. Bowman lost his balance, and fell off the ring apron, hitting his head against the edge of the mat, cutting himself over his left eyebrow, and knocked unconscious. Creed glowered in his efforts. Matt Loco went and grabbed a microphone, as the crowd booed the duo incessantly. As Matt made his way into the ring, Erik was attempting to get onto his knees. With another vicious chair shot to the ribs, Kelly lay on his back, blinded from blood in his eyes, and wracked with pain throughout all of his senses. "Kelly, you stupid son of a bitch!" Loco yelled in a high pitched squealy voice. Creed walked to stand over Kelly, who was barely aware he was still on the same planet. "You're too stupid to put things together, so we thought we'd take this moment to enlighten you on the truth!" The crowd continued to boo. Creed nonchalantly flipped off a larger man in the front row who was debating whether or not he should throw his shoe at the Alaskan Juggernaut. Loco continued as if it was just him and Kelly there. "Last Sunday was no accident, Kelly! Just like way back in September of 2001, when your bitch of a wife was hit by a car!" Kelly lay too motionless to respond, or even show if he could comprehend anything. "I didn't get the job done, then. I almost got the job done Sunday. But you know what they say, right Vic?" Loco hands the microphone over to Creed. Creed bends down to have his face stand a foot away from Kelly's crimson mask. "The third time is always a charm. And next time, I won't miss that fuckin' cunt!" Creed spat directly in Kelly's face. The crowd lay in stunned silence. For some, it was because of the usage of a certain word. For those who follow more closely, they remember September of 2001, and the incident mentioned. When Kelly and Matt Loco's partnership was beginning to break, it was found Loco had hired a hitman to murder Pamela Dee in a driveby hit and run. What wasn't known....who was 'The Driver'? "Yes, Kelly. Even someone as fuckin' stupid as you can do that fuckin' math. I was 'The Driver', and loved every moment of running that 25-cent slut of a wife you've got." The crowd started to boo and get unorderly. Objects began to make their way towards ringside. It was beginning to look like the makings of a Vince Russo show. Creed started to stand up, laughing. "Come to think of it, Erik. Maybe I'll just rape that cunt to death this time. Her blood would do murder on my new paintjob!" Creed slammed the microphone down into Kelly's forehead. The feedback from the blow freaked out the crowd. Security started to rush the ring to surround Creed. Creed sneered at the security members, as they started to escort him & Matt Loco out of the arena. Kelly and the Bowmans began to receive medical assistance as the cameras did close down for the evening. Leadership "I'm not in charge." Samuel Smooth leaned back in the plastic chair and sighed.
