Sunday Slaughter, 21 November 1999

21 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Machina stood in the arena, playing a "Ms. Pac Man" video game and getting more hostile by the moment. "Ah! Try to get me will you? Well now... see that I've eaten the glowing pill? Well little ghosts... now the hunters have become the hunted!" He didn't see Jake coming in behind him, lumbering along with a pile of suitcases and grunting. Jake was sweating a great deal, and the slippery sweat made the suitcases hard to grip. With a yelp he tripped and the suitcases went flying, striking Machina in the back. Machina was thrust into the video game cursing and spun around on his heel with a snarl. He spotted Jake and advanced on him, but then he heard a song drifting in from a distance. "American Woman" spun through the air, and Machina softened. He extended a hand to the portly assistant, helping him to his feet and patting him on the head. "Run along little scamp... Uncle Machina's not angry at you." Jake lumbered away just as Courage entered the arena, ready for a night of action at Sunday Slaughter. He spotted Jake and an angry expression crossed his face. "YOU! What in the hell are you doing here you fat little bastard?" Courage lunged at Jake but missed him as the fat assistant scurried down the hall and into an empty dressing room. Courage crossed his arms, and glowered. "Machina... call security... Jake's not an fWo employee anymore. Get him outta here." Machina was back playing the video game, and largely ignored Courage's request. Mumbling he said, "Can't do that... he's Primetime's valet.. don't want to piss off the champ, you know?" Courage turned sharply. "He's WHAT?" Billy James vs. Fear Slaughter kicks off in the arena with the lights dimmed, and a large "L" suddenly appears on the SlaughterScreen as "Come With Me" blasts through the arena speakers. Out steps Billy James, who takes a swig from a water bottle before tossing it to the crowd. James rolls into the ring, and bounces off the ropes once before waiting for his opponent. The music of The Underground plays as Fear, Mike Bear and Wretch appear on the top of the entrance ramp. The dreadlocked monster Fear makes his way to the ring as Bear and Wretch head to the announcing table. As Fear enters the ring and begins to stare down James, Bear and Wretch assert that they will have no problems with whoever their opponents are tonight. The match starts off with James charging at Fear, who counters by powerslamming the smaller man for a quick two-count. Fear drags James to his feet and tosses him into the corner, before charging in with a big shoulder and following up with repeated shoulderblocks. Fear then picks up James and sets him on the second rope. Fear climbs up with James, hooks a headlock, and drops him with a second-rope DDT. Rather than go for the cover, Fear brings the man back to his feet, and throws him into the ropes, catching him coming off with a big boot. With James down, Fear bounces off the ropes and drops a leg on James. He covers, but again only gets a two-count. Fear brings James up to his feet again, and puts him in a powerbomb position. He lifts James, but James counters with a series of closed fists to the large skull of Fear, who is momentarily dazed, but counters by tossing James over his head and into a stun gun. James remains on his feet, and stumbles back towards Fear, who grabs him for a chokeslam. James counters this with a well-placed testicular kick, which staggers Fear, who stumbles back towards the ropes. James hooks Fear from behind with a hard russian legsweep. He covers, but only gets 1 and a half. James signals to the crowd that he's going up top to attempt his flying elbowdrop finisher, and climbs to the top rope. James then dives off, but Fear moves and James hits hard into the mat. Fear quickly gets to his feet, and drags James up to his. Fear hooks James from behind in a full nelson, then turns it into a devastating sitdown full nelson slam, jarring James. Fear quickly rolls onto him and hooks a leg, and garners the three-count. Bear and Wretch leave the commentating position to congratulate their partner on his win as Slaughter continues. Result: Fear by Pinfall Chameleon & Total Eclipse vs. Briana & Black Quicksilver The Chameleon makes his way out to the ring, followed several yards behind by the Total Eclipse who can best be described as "waddling" to the ring. Chameleon vaults into the ring, followed by Eclipse who opts to roll in. He becomes distracted by a fan with a hot dog in the front row as "We're Not Gonna Take It" plays and Black Quicksilver and Briana rush the ring, storming Chameleon. They whip him into the ropes and catch him coming off with a double backdrop. BQ then runs off the ropes, and is flapjacked by Briana onto Chameleon. This gets a near fall, and Chameleon quickly rolls to the outside to gather himself. He drags the Eclipse away from the hot dog-bearing fan forcibly, and appears to form a plan with Eclipse. Chameleon then rolls back into the ring as Eclipse slowly climbs onto the ring apron. Chameleon ducks a clothesline from BQ, and grabs him, whipping him into the ropes. BQ reverses, and Chameleon leapfrogs him coming off and catches Total Eclipse with a blind tag. Chameleon ducks another BQ clothesline coming off, and, using the ropes, slides to the outside of the ring. BQ yells at him to come back, and never sees Total Eclipse flattening him from behind with a vader attack. BQ drops to the mat, and Eclipse comes off the ropes with a big elbowdrop. He bounces off the ropes again, and hits a big splash on BQ. He covers, but only gets a two-count when Briana makes the save. Eclipse charges at Briana, but she sidesteps and shoves him on the way into the corner, where he hits the turnbuckles hard. Briana then hits a seated dropkick to the back of his leg, staggering him. She executes this move again, but he still doesn't go down. By this time, BQ is back up, and they perform stereo seated dropkicks on the legs of Total Eclipse, and he falls to the mat with a crash. Finding that he can barely lift himself due to his weight, Eclipse gives Briana and BQ time to climb the turnbuckles. Briana flies off the top with a flying legdrop. A moment later, BQ flies off the opposite turnbuckle with a somersault flying headbutt. The Eclipse is out, and BQ covers. At two, Chameleon pulls the referee from the ring before he can count the three. The ref threatens to DQ Chameleon, but before he can, Black Quicksilver flies over the top with a somersault plancha, taking Chameleon out. He hammers away at him, but after a few punches, rolls back into the ring. The reason why becomes apparent as Briana runs into the ropes, and is backdropped over onto Chameleon. Eclipse comes in to break a count, and Black Quicksilver comes into the ring as well. With all four wrestlers in the ring trading blows, the referee finally has enough and calls for the bell. Security rushes the scene as the Chameleon struggles to get back at Black Quicksilver... Result: Double DQ Eron (c) vs. Fallen International Champion Eron enters the ring first, looking confident as always. Fallen came out next, blank expression in place, not being accompanied to the ring by the rest of the Riders. Eron charges at Fallen, but Fallen ducks and catches him coming back with an inverted atomic drop. Eron doubles over, and Fallen goes behind for a belly-to-back with a leg hook for a two-count. Fallen is up first, and catches Eron with a hard lariat, following up with a fistdrop to the forehead. He brings Eron up and sets him for a piledriver, but Eron reverses, going for a backdrop but holding Fallen 3/4 of the way down and falling forward with a thunderous spinebuster. He hooks both legs, but only gets a two-count. Fallen struggles to his feet, and Eron lifts him up, whipping him into the ropes and catching him with a knee, which staggers him. Eron then hits an inverted russian legsweep, and rolls into a cover. This only gets two. Eron brings Fallen back up, and starts to whip him into the ropes, but pulls him back and drops him with a short sitdown chokeslam. Instead of going for a cover, Eron heads up top and prepares a flying splash. He leaps, but Fallen moves and Eron bangs his head on the mat. Fallen sees an opportunity and brings Eron up, setting him up for the Lowfall reverse DDT. He goes for it, but instead Eron uses superb strength to lift himself up in the air, belly-to-belly with Fallen, and drops down with an ace crusher. He makes the cover on Fallen, but before the count can be made the lights in the arena blew out and the fans were pitched into complete darkness. Smoke began to fill the arena, and from the mist Posion, Demon and Death walked to the ring. They never stopped, and sliding inside they quickly took out the referee and then decended on Eron himself. A weakened referee signalled for the bell to be rung as the beat down continued. With Eron finally rolling to safety and escaping through the crowd, the Dark Riders took their positions in the center of the ring. Picking up a microphone, Death whispered out, "Fourty days... fourty days." Cameras went to the back... Result: Eron by DQ Ultra Cool 2000 vs. Chaos Chaos enters first, with Plague looking as maniacal as ever and War looking intensely focused. Ultra-Cool 2000 now comes to the ring, and simultaneously roll in, climbing onto the turnbuckles to pose for the fans, with Violet staying on the outside cheering them on. UC2K decides that Ultra-Violet will start off the match, and UV circles Plague as the bell rings. Plague charges at him, but UV takes him over with a firemans carry and follows up with a falling headbutt for a quick two-count. Plague climbs to his feet, and UV whips him into the ropes, catching him with a leaping forearm. He bounces off the ropes, and drops down with a senton. UV reaches back to hook the leg, but only gets a two-count. He brings Plague up to his feet, and takes him over with a snap suplex. He holds on, and executes two more snap suplexes on Plague. UV signals to the crowd and heads up top, preparing a flying headbutt. He leaps off, and nails it. UV hooks the leg, but War comes in to break the count at two. This brings in Kid Cool, who charges War with a dropkick as he gets up, barely staggering him. Kid Cool dropkicks him again, kicks him in the shin doubling him over, and runs off the ropes, looking for a rocker dropper. However, as he leaps up, War grabs him in mid-air and plants him with a slam. War then kicks Cool to the outside. Meanwhile, UV has backed Plague into a corner and is pounding away with count-along punches. However, the crowd only reaches "C" before War grabs UV from behind and hits a backdrop suplex from the second rope. War brings UV to his feet as Plague begins to climb backwards to the top rope. War puts UV in a powerbomb position, lifts him up, and as he drops him, Plague flies off with a moonsault onto UV. Ultra-Violet is out, and Plague hooks the leg. Kid Cool attempts to make the save, but literally runs into War, bouncing backwards head over heels after running into the monster as the ref counts three. As Plague's arm is raised, War tells the camera that it's only a matter of time before the tag team titles belong to Chaos. Result: Chaos by Pinfall Rain (c) vs. Machina Rain enters the arena first, carrying the Internet Title over his shoulder and flanked by what appears to be slightly fatter versions of Machina and Rudo. He holds the title high in the air to the jeers of the crowd. Machina enters next with the trademark Lone Gunmen entrance, and vaults into the ring as he reaches it. The bell rings and we're off. Rain attacks quickly, dropping Machina with a shoulder tackle. He covers, but only gets a one. Rain brings Machina up to his feet, and hammers away with rights. He backs Machina up into the corner, and kicks away at him. Rain now grabs Machina and sets him on his shoulders in a powerslam position. Rain charges out of the corner and drops Machina with a running powerslam. He hooks the leg, and nearly gets two before kicking out. Rain brings Machina back to his feet, and whips him into the ropes. He catches Machina coming off with a bearhug, then turns it into a cradle DDT. Rain slowly bounces off the ropes, and leaps high with a legdrop. He implores the ref to count, and the ref registers two before Machina shoves Rain's leg away. Rain is undeterred, and hammers on the back of Machina as he gets up. He shoves Machina into the ropes, but Machina somehow reverses it. However, Rain comes flying off the ropes with a jumping back elbow directly to the neck. Looking to do more damage to the head and neck area, Rain grabs Machina and plants him with a brainbuster. He signals that it's time for the Rainmaker, and gets Machina in position. Rain puts Machina between his legs, but seems to take an especially long time before lifting Machina. Machina, given an opportunity to rest, manages to turn the move into an incredible hurricanrana on the larger man. However, Rain kicks out at two, and is quickly back to his feet. Machina ducks a clothesline and catches Rain with a standing leaping leg lariat, knocking him down. Machina begins to climb up top, but is suddenly grabbed by Rain's chubby Machina. He shrugs this off by hammering him in the face, and continues climbing. He reaches the top and signals for the 450 Splash when from the crowd, out comes Eron, still in wrestling gear, who trips Machina, crotching him. Eron runs to the back as the crowd boos him, loving every minute of it. Rain slowly begins to get to his feet, and climbs up top with Machina. He hooks him for a super brainbuster, and plants Machina with it, jarring his spine. With Machina's neck area already weakened, Rain slaps on a camel clutch and Machina has no choice but to tap out. Rain grabs his belt and the alternate Machina and Rudos as he heads to the back... Result: Rain by Submission Underground vs. Mystery Team Mike Bear and Wretch enter first, tag titles held high, looking confident as always. They hand the belts to the timekeeper as they await their opponents. The arena lights dim, and an explosion rocks the arena. A familiar video and theme play, and we see Chris Jericho standing back-to-ring, arms outstretched, on the entrance ramp. Jericho spins around, mic in hand, and begins walking down the entranceway. "Welcome to SUNDAY NIGHT JERICHO! Obviously all my Jerichoholics out there are wondering what I'm doing here in the Palookaville Wrestling Organization- well, since that thumbless wonder Chyna got lucky against me and kept her belt at the last pay-per-view, I figured I still needed some gold. And what better place to get it than here? And yes, I'm sure my Jerichoholics are also wondering, where's my partner? Well, I am so damn good, I've decided to face Mike Wolf and Wrench singlehandedly and take both those straps myself. Underground, prepare yourselves for the beating of your lives, courtesy of Y..2..J! Oh, and West? Those were my catchphrases and my fat bodyguard gimmick first, BABY!" Jericho is now in the ring, and Bear and Wretch charge him, taking him down with a double-clothesline. Jericho is stunned, and rolls outside. This offers no relief, however, as Wretch flies over the top with a suicide dive onto Jericho. Wretch rolls Jericho back in the ring, where he is pounced upon by Bear. Bear opens up with a series of closed fists to the face, bloodying Y2J. Bear now lifts Jericho to his feet and whips him into the ropes, going for a kneelift. Instead, Jericho rolls to the side, grabs the knee, and rolls up Bear from behind. This only gets a two-count, however. Jericho catches Bear with a dropkick as he gets up, sending him back into his corner. Bear tags in Wretch, who charges in but is caught with a drop-toe-hold. Jericho hits a senton splash on Wretch. He climbs up to the top, and flies off, going for a splash, but Wretch moves and Jericho hits the canvas hard. Wretch brings Jericho to his feet and backs him into the corner. Wretch tries to whip Jericho across, but Jericho reverses and whips Wretch into the Underground corner, not noticing him tagging in Bear. Jericho now grabs Wretch from behind as he stumbles out of the corner and plants him with a bulldog. Jericho runs into the ropes, going for the Lionsault, but instead of hitting Wretch, he is caught by Bear, who plants him with a tombstone piledriver. Bear hooks both legs from the front, and the Underground are victorious. The referee hands them their belts as Bear tells the camera that whether it's Chaos or Ultra-Cool 2000, the belts are staying with the Undergound at BattleBowl. Result: Underground by Pinfall Micky Maliboo vs. Havok vs. Courage The fans are ready and waiting for the main event, but suddenly an unfamiliar theme plays and out to the ring walks Commissioner James Armstrong, carrying the fWo Cruiserweight Title, to a mixed reaction from the crowd. He steps into the ring and grabs the microphone. "As you know, current fWo Cruiserweight Champion Lance Star has been severely injured at the hands of Death. On a serious note, I'd like to say 'Get well soon, Lance'. But, this means that he will have to vacate the title. And even though none of you here in potato-land probably deserve this important a match- except for maybe you there in the front row, miss- yeah, well same to you- and even though the Slugger isn't here tonight to reclaim this belt- I am announcing that the a new Cruiserweight Champion will be crowned here tonight in this very ring! And the participants in this one-fall matchup will be...Micky Maliboo..Havok...and Courage! Thank you, and good night!" With that Armstrong exits the ring, handing off the belt to the timekeeper, as the music of Micky Maliboo plays, and he walks out accompanied by his lovely valet Vixon, whom James Armstrong stares at passing by for much more time than necessary. Havok's music plays next, and he walks out looking confident. Lastly Courage's music plays, and he charges the ring. Havok and Maliboo start off the match on the same side, stomping at Courage as he enters the ring. Havok takes control of Courage, backing him into a corner and kicking away. Maliboo strikes from behind, rolling up Havok, but the pin is broken at two by Courage. Now Maliboo and Courage square off, and Courage kicks Maliboo in the midsection, planting him with a DDT. He comes off the ropes, looking to splash Maliboo, but Havok catches him coming off and Gorilla presses him directly onto Maliboo. Havok covers Maliboo, but only gets two. Havok brings Maliboo up, and Courage decides to roll to the outside to take a breather. Havok backs Maliboo into the corner, and charges in with a back splash. Maliboo falls, and Havok climbs onto the apron. Havok slingshots himself in with an elbowdrop and makes the cover. He gets a two before Maliboo places his foot on the ropes. Havok brings Maliboo to his feet and whips him into the ropes, where Courage grabs his foot, holding him in place. Havok takes advantage by superkicking Maliboo in the back of the head and knocking him over the top rope. Havok takes a moment to play to the crowd, but Courage quickly rolls into the ring and strikes from behind with a reverse DDT. He covers, but only gets a two-count. Courage waits for Havok to get up, and leaps off the ropes with an Asai forearm smash, knocking him down. Courage now climbs up to the top rope, and taunts Havok. He then leaps off with a flying elbow. Courage quickly hooks the leg, but it is broken up as Micky Maliboo, recovered, comes flying off the opposite top rope with a big flying kneedrop. He rolls onto Havok, and covers him, but only gets two. Undaunted, he covers Courage as Havok gets up, but again only gets two. Maliboo gets to his feet and prepares an offensive move on Courage, but Havok strikes from behind, locking on a cobra clutch. Maliboo quickly counters with a low back kick, and hooks Havok up. He then delivers a running powerbomb all the way across the ring. The ref counts and reaches two before the pin is broken up by a seated dropkick to the back of Maliboo's head by Courage. With Havok temporarily out, Courage drags Maliboo to his feet hits a low blow, followed by an Implant DDT. Courage begins to taunt the crowd, knowing that the Cruiserweight Title is nearly his. As Havok starts to recover, Courage bounces off the ropes in one direction, bounces off again, warrior-style, and then leaps off the ropes with the Courage Required asai moonsault on Maliboo. Instead of making the cover, Courage takes a moment to arrogantly smirk at the crowd, then drapes himself across Maliboo, not hooking the leg. He gets one, two, and suddenly Havok flies onto the Courage/Maliboo pile from nowhere, nailing the Millenium-sault onto Courage and also Maliboo by association. With Courage out and Maliboo flattened under the weight of two men, Havok covers Micky Maliboo and hooks the leg, garnering the 3-count and the Cruiserweight Title. Havok takes the belt from the timekeeper and holds it high above his head as the cameras go off the air for Slaughter... Result: Havok by Pinfall, to become the new Cruiserweight Champion
