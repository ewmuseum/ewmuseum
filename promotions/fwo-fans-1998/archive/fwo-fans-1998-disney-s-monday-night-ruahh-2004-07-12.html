Disney's Monday Night Ruahh~!, 12 July 2004

12 July 2004

Introduction Previously... The Second Era Fans cluttered around the doors outside the arena, hoping to catch a glimpse of one of the fWo superstars. Needless to say, the approaching Hummer Limousine caught their attention. The mob moved from the doorway and began travelling down the small sidewalk to the street, curious to see who was inside. Two men sat in the back of the car. One stared through the tinted windows at the clamouring mob with a look of genuine excitement. His eyes darted from face to face, taking in their curiosity. Beyond them was the doorway to a future he could only have dreamed of. But then, the past few days had been a dream come true for Alex Creed. First, he completed a successful wrestling camp with Mitch Wilson. Alex worked for days with some of Mitch's trainers and got a number of helpful tips from Mitch himself. Once the camp was over, Alex was sure he'd be able to go back to Miami Main Event Wrestling and win the Heavyweight Title in under two months. Second, he finally met his father. Alex had always known that Kodiak Vic Creed was his father. His mother Sally kept Vic's press clippings in an old shoe box. But she never contacted Creed, even after Alex was born. She was a strong woman who was fiercely independent. She didn't want Vic's help, but once her son was old enough she left the choice to him as far as meeting his father. Alex was quite shocked when he got the phone call from Vic's publicist. He was even more shocked when he was whisked away to British Columbia to the set of Vic's movie "Juggernaut." After shooting wrapped, it took all of five minutes for the awkwardness to dissolve. Vic had been genuinely happy to see him. They also had a common bond - professional wrestling - and the two became fast friends. The family bonding would come, of course. But they'd already broken down the first wall. Kodiak Vic Creed drained his bottle of water and screwed the cap back on. "So what do you think, Kid? Figure you could get used to this kind of treatment?" Alex shot his father a cocky grin that Vic immediately recognized. "Creed's roll in style, yes?" Vic's chest swelled. "Damn rights." As Alex turned back to the crowd forming outside the car, Kodiak Vic Creed leaned back in his seat. The only child he knew, Sarah, had been taken from him months ago. Now it was like he'd been given a second chance. This was an opportunity he wasn't going to let pass by. With Alex, he could do what his own father didn't do for the Main Man: Give him hope. In a few minutes, the Creeds were going to be signing a contract with the fWo as a tag team. That way, Alex would slowly be integrated into the fWo while getting valuable ring time. Vic's contract would only be short-term, possibly opting for a pay-per-appearance clause. But Alex was the Creed of the future. The Mecca was the next Era, and Vic couldn't be more proud. The driver stepped to the back of the huge car. Vic slapped Alex on the back. "You think you're ready for this?" Alex winked at his father. "I was born for this." "That you were," Vic said as the door opened. "That you were." Breaking the Silence Lights flashing. Sirens blaring. fWo fans who had tuned in the week before knew this meant one thing, and one thing only. As the lights returned completely and "Battle Without Honor or Humanity" began to play, they were proven correct. Because out from the back emerged the man who, one week ago, had defeated Eli Flair in the middle of the ring, 1-2-3, for the fWo Internet Title. Xias The crowd erupted as Xias gave them an intense glare, holding the fWo Internet Title belt high above his head on top of the stage. With a snarl, he placed the belt over his right shoulder and power-walked down the rampway, the camera catching a shot behind him at his new white robe dragging behind him, a red X staring back at the camera. The camera got a few more good shots of his new white-and-red attire- he remained shirtless under the white robe, while his white tights had a solid red X on either leg. So apparently Xias was freshening up his look while he was gone- the question is, what else was he doing? Why had he returned to the fWo? As Xias entered the ring and quickly called for a microphone, it seemed we were about to find out. But the beginning of his speech was delayed, as the fans had begun a loud "X-I-AS!" chant for the conquering hero who they had seen defeat Flair in the center of the ring one week ago. Xias seemed unaffected by the show of gratitude, his face remaining emotionless as he briefly hung his head, waiting for them to finish. When the chant died down, Xias raised his head back upwards, and finally, began to speak. "There's too much talking in the fWo." Xias began. "There's too much melodrama in the fWo. There's too much sitcom-esque bullshit in the fWo. But one thing there isn't too much of in the fWo.....is wrestlers." The fans reacted with confusion. What the hell was Xias talking about? "At the very least, there aren't enough wrestlers who are willing to come here each and every week and compete. Compete without getting on the microphone and whining for four hours because you didn't get your way. Compete without coming up with yet another master plan to destroy the Fans Wrestling Organization. Compete without worrying whether or not you have your girlfriend's approval. This is WRESTLING. And I am officially taking a stand.....for the REAL wrestlers. Because, thanks to all of you, thanks to the reaction you gave me when I came out here and I straight-up wrestled against people like Jon Crisp and Black Quicksilver, I realized what was important to me. It wasn't the melodrama or the elaborate plans. It's competing to be the very best. To be recognized as the best athlete to step into this squared circle. That is my ultimate goal, and it's one I WILL complete. How will I do it? With the belt you see slung over my shoulder." Xias moved his eyes towards the fWo Internet Championship belt slung over his right shoulder, and the crowd cheered. "This belt is the fWo Internet Title. It's been held by some of the best to ever step foot into this ring. Scott Slugger. Ruben Ross. Kodiak Vic Creed. Eron. Ric Chronos. And many, many more. This belt represents everything that is RIGHT about the Fans Wrestling Organization- athletes competing to be the very best, not competing because one of them stole the other one's girlfriend, or competing in a grand quest to destroy the fWo like it's some sort of innovative new plan. I plan to show that I am the very best athlete that has ever held this title. And I will do that.....by defending this title each and every week, against anyone. Anytime. Anywhere. You want a shot at the Internet Title? It's yours. And that starts, toni-" But before Xias could even finish that sentence, he was interrupted by the beginnings of "Unmei [Guitar Version]" by Naoki Sato. The crowd gave a slight pop for the entrance of someone Xias knew very well. That person was Enforcer. Xias watched as Enforcer came down the rampway, a smirk on his face. Their eyes were locked as Enforcer climbed up the ring steps and into the ring. He put his hand out towards Xias, and Xias reluctantly handed him the microphone. "So we wait all these months for you to return, then we wait nearly three weeks after you return to hear some sort of explanation for your actions, and all you have to say is that you want to be the fWo's version of Chris Benoit?" Enforcer asked in mock disbelief. "Well. I couldn't see that one coming. You're really one to talk, Xias. You used to come out here and whine on that microphone just as much as anyone else ever has. You used to swing title belts, and chairs, and anything else you could get your hands on. You took part in melodrama with Eternity.....and oh yeah, with me. And while you may want to wash your hands of it now, make no mistake about it, Mr. Wrestler, you and I have a lot of unfinished business. And since you're out here making grand challenges, I say we finish it, tonight.....and for your title." Xias nodded, then placed his hand out towards Enforcer, who handed him back his microphone. "Fine." With that one word, Xias dropped the microphone to the canvas, and turned and exited while "Battle" played again. Enforcer watched him exit with his title, a hint of disbelief in his eyes. Xias took one last turn towards the crowd as he climbed the rampway and raised the title belt back in the air while Monday Night Ruahh~!! faded away. Eternity (c.) vs. Aimz Last week on Monday Night Ruahh~!!, one superstar earned a shot at the fWo Cruiserweight Title when they pinned the Champion, Eternity, in a tag team match. Unfortunately for that superstar, Jacob Winters, Aimz asked first. "Thoughtless" by Evanescence brought the crowd to their feet for the entrance of the Red Raver. If one was to say that Aimz was on a roll lately, they'd be correct- a controversial win over Nerva at Unleashed and an impressive win over 'Nasty' Nick Brandish on last week's Ruahh has lead Amy Campbell to tonight, and a chance to get some fWo gold back around her waist. Aimz was focused and concentrated as she made her way down the rampway, ignoring the out-stretched hands of the fans. While she was certainly confident in a one-on-one situation, she knew that the fWo Cruiserweight Champion doesn't travel alone. Even on a roll, could Aimz fight off the Avenging Angels on her own and reel in the title? We were about to find that out, because as Aimz entered the ring, the lights dimmed and "Suite [Dragon of Earth]" by Naoki Sato replaced "Thoughtless". The crowd remained on their feet as the blue-and-white spotlights started to flash around the arena, but they were now booing in anticipation of Eternity's arrival. And indeed, out from the back she stepped, head hung low as she entered the light of one of the blue spotlights. She raised her head slowly upwards into the air, before bringing it back down level again and then began an almost eerie walk down the rampway while the music changed over to the sound of blaring trumpets. Behind her was revealed to be X, the masked Xias imposter carrying Eternity's fWo Cruiserweight Title belt over his right shoulder. The two Angels were an imposing sight indeed in the darkness, but inside the ring Aimz remained calm, keeping her mind on the opportunity that laid before her. The lights slowly returned and the music died down as Eternity and X climbed up the steps and entered the ring. X handed the title belt to Eternity, who climbed to the top turnbuckle and raised it over her head to a loud round of boos. Aimz, however, had clearly had enough, and to a loud, surprised pop, rushed to the corner and grabbed Eternity, bringing her down out of the corner with a hard powerbomb! Eternity landed on the back of her head, dropping her title belt, as the crowd absolutely exploded for Aimz. This was clearly a more aggressive Aimz than we had ever seen before. X, of course, wasn't too happy with this early advantage for the Red Raver, and took a swing at her. Aimz ducked it, however, and then caught X with a dropkick as he turned back around that sent him over the top rope and crashing out to the floor! With Eternity's second down, the referee shrugged, picked up the belt, and called for the bell to signal the start of the match. Eternity was still feeling the effects of that powerbomb off the second turnbuckle, and was in no condition to block a snapmare from Aimz, who hit her with the move from behind when Eternity was in a seated position. Quickly, Aimz leaned over and hooked Eternity's leg for the cover. ONE! TWO! THR-NO! And she would have gotten the pinfall, too, but Eternity managed to get her left foot on the bottom rope. Aimz stood up angrily and shoved the referee backwards, before raising three fingers. The referee pointed to Eternity's foot on the bottom rope in response, but Aimz just shook her head as she pulled the Cruiserweight Champion back to her feet. Eternity still seemed out of it as Aimz sent her into the ropes with an Irish whip.....but before Eternity could bounce back towards Aimz, she was pulled to the safety of the outside by X. The fans booed as X helped his leader stand up, having gotten her out of the proverbial fire. Unfortunately for the duo, Aimz decided to just bring the fire to them, as she bounced off the ropes behind her and came leaping over the top rope with a picture-perfect tope, catching both Eternity and X and sending them all crashing to the ground. The crowd loudly cheered and applauded as Aimz stood up, raising her arms in the air, with a cocky smile on her face. Just like at Unleashed, and just like last week on Ruahh, this was her night, and everyone knew it. Not the least of them being Aimz herself. She tossed Eternity back into the ring and used a lateral press for another cover attempt. ONE! TWO! THR-NO! Once again, Eternity's left foot was on the bottom rope- this time, however, it was only because X had recovered in time to put it there. The referee only saw her foot on the rope and stopped his count just before three. Aimz, who had seen the entire thing, was obviously less than thrilled. Again, showing her newfound aggressiveness, the Red Raver stood up and got in the face of the referee, pointing angrily out at X and yelling at him. She didn't notice Eternity getting back to her feet, but she did hear her bouncing off the ropes behind her, and Aimz stepped to the side just in time to avoid a flying clothesline by Eternity. The referee, however, was not so lucky, and was wiped out by the flying clothesline from the Cruiserweight Champion. Eternity turned around just in time to take a boot to the stomach and a DDT, dropping her headfirst to the canvas. She leaned over and hooked Eternity's leg this time for the cover, but of course there was no referee to make the count. Angrily, Aimz got back to her feet, and walked over to the downed referee, leaning over and attempting to revive him. She turned away from the downed referee just in time to duck a clothesline attempt by X, then catch him with a standing side kick to the chin, knocking him down. Aimz pointed down at him then raised her arms in the air to a huge ovation....before she was turned around and nearly had her head taken off by the third and final member of the Avenging Angels, the seven-foot-tall Exel Pure. Yes, the numbers had finally caught up to the Red Raver. Pure picked the dazed Aimz up to her feet, taunting her as he put one massive hand around her throat. Before he could make good on any of his threats, however, Jacob Winters came running down the rampway. He grabbed the fWo Cruiserweight Title belt the referee had kicked to the outside earlier, and slid into the ring with it. Pure noticed him and dropped Aimz just in time to turn right around into a leaping shot with the belt by Winters, wiping out the seven-footer. Winters then turned to see Eternity getting back to her feet, and ran forward, slamming her own title belt right into her masked face, dropping her to the canvas. Aimz climbed back to her feet as well.....just in time to see the referee call for the bell before collapsing again. Aimz looked at Winters, and beginning to piece things together, realized the referee had seen his interference.....but not the interference of the Angels. She had just lost this match, via disqualification. Winters dropped the title belt and raised his hands in defense as Aimz walked towards him. The Red Raver seemed receptive to his words, even thankful that he had come down to the ring to save her from the 3-on-1 assault of the Avenging Angels. At least, until she swiftly kicked him in the gut and then dropped him head-first onto the Cruiserweight Title belt with a DDT. Now, the fans were giving a much different reaction to Aimz as she raised her arms in the air. Instead of all cheers, it was a far more mixed reaction. But it didn't matter. Because on this night, Aimz had shown the entire world that, from now on, no one was allowed to stand in her way. No one. Result: Eternity via DQ; Retains fWo Cruiserweight Title Brainscan "Eli? Kidnapped Faith brings Confusion The man called Deacon had done plenty of disappearance tricks in his career. The Egyptian illusion. The Crucifix (David Cross) Entrance. The Deacon was known for such things, but as Chris Shepherd sat in his car, a cell phone in his hand and a pad of paper on his lap, he knew this wasn't one of those 'illusions' that Chris had taught to the Deacon. Afterall, Chris was a magician (among other skills he'd picked up over the years), not a kidnapper. He dialed the phone & put it to his ear. "Hello, this is Chris Shepherd. No news on Elijah?" Chris asked, seeking out the Deacon. "Nothing.... ok, thanks." He took a deep breath, exhaling loudly as he pulled the phone from his ear. He opened the door to his car and stepped out. The conversation wasn't a surprise. He'd called every day for the last week, but it was another disappointment in a week full of them. He had given up the usual search areas - Damien Xeno, Chad Merritt but Deacon was far from a midget. Chris had no where else to go, and even though the last time he'd seen this man, he was wheeled out of the ring... it was a starting point. If he hadn't done it, then he might know something. Chris walked across the parking lot, his dress shoes clicking lightly on the pavement. The door opened automatically. He strode to the information desk, and the lady seated there smiled, giving those sweet offers to help. He returned the smile, handing her a card. "I do have a question. I'd like to visit a Craig Miles. He was admitted on April 30th of this year." Chris said. The lady, her hair in one of those 60's buns that looks like a protective helmet, began typing into her computer. An odd look came across her face and then she turned to Chris. "I'm sorry, but he was checked out on May 1st." Chris' stomach flipped. The reports of Miles' injury were faked? Or did he simply go home, give up on rehab. "He had a broken back." Chris said. The phrase burst forth as a question inside himself breaking through his mind and out of his mouth. He had seen miracles in his life, certainly, but... Why hadn't he showed up for anymore shows? Where had he been? What happened on April 30th? "Really?" The lady said, surprise registering on her face. "I'll bet someone somewhere was praying for him." Normally, Chris would've confirmed her comment with a response, but as questions raced through his mind, crescendoed by the question of Deacon's kidnapping, his normal reactions were lost and he was left with something entirely different. Confusion "For God is not the author of confusion, but of peace." First Corinthians 14:33 And Chris was finding out... he was reading a completely different novel than the one in which he'd become accustomed. Xias (c.) vs. Enforcer The opening chords of Naoki Sato's "Unmei [Guitar Version]" played for the second time that night, once again bringing out Enforcer. The former hardcore icon raised his arms on top of the stage to a decent pop from the crowd, then made his way down the rampway. He was focused, not only on winning the Internet Title here tonight, but also on settling a long-standing issue with the current fWo Internet Champion. As Enforcer entered the ring, the lights went off as the sounds of "Ironside (Excerpt)" began to play. The crowd rose to their feet as the lights blinked on and off, then as Ironside reached its conclusion, they flashed back on while an explosion of red pyrotechnics rocked the stage. "Battle Without Honor or Humanity" by Tomoyasu Hotei quickly replaced it, and as that song hit its peak, out from the back emerged the new fWo Internet Champion. Xias came out with his belt raised high into the air once again, the fans reacting with a loud pop for the man who had dethroned Eli Flair one week earlier. His face was equally as intense and concentrated as his opponent's, but for far different reasons. As he said earlier, he was no longer a man concerned with personal grudges- he was a man with a simple goal. And that goal is to be the very best. Tonight, it was time for Xias to show that he was serious about succeeding in that goal. Enforcer stretched against the ropes as Xias climbed up the ring steps and entered the ring. He climbed to the top turnbuckle and held his Internet Title belt over his head, drawing another pop from the crowd, before stepping back down. He handed his Internet Title belt over to the referee, who raised it in the air before handing it off to the timekeeper. As he was doing that, Enforcer stepped forward, getting in Xias' face. He began talking trash as the two men were inches from each other, but Xias didn't make any kind of response. He just kept a serious, emotionless face, staring at Enforcer as he continued to bark out inaudible insults. Finally, the referee got between them, pushing them apart, before calling for the bell. Again, Xias and Enforcer each walked forward, and again Enforcer began insulting Xias. Xias still showed no signs of a response, that is, until Enforcer raised his right hand for a punch. Xias blocked the punch and quickly took Enforcer down to the mat with a strong, powerful takedown. With Enforcer down in a guard position, Xias quickly maneuvered to Enforcer's right side and grabbed his arm for an armbar. Enforcer quickly screamed out in pain and with his left arm, reached for the ropes. Xias pulled harder on Enforcer's right arm, and when it seemed like he was going to tap, he finally grabbed the bottom rope with his left hand. The referee yelled for Xias to break the hold, and when he didn't, began his five count. At the count of four, Xias finally released the hold, leaving Enforcer to quickly roll to the outside. When Xias saw him holding his left arm in pain, a smirk was evident on his face. The damage had been done. Enforcer, for his part, felt like his left arm was about to fall off. He moved it slowly, trying vainly to get some feeling back into his arm, when he turned around and right into a baseball slide by Xias, which caught him right in that right arm. Enforcer took the blow and was knocked backwards into the barricade. Xias slid all the way to the outside and, grabbing his right arm, held it up against the barricade. After a glare towards Enforcer, Xias began firing off hard kicks to that arm, causing Enforcer to scream out in pain. Xias grabbed Enforcer and, holding out the arm, whipped him into the steel post, his right arm absorbing most of the impact. Enforcer stumbled backwards, and was grabbed by his head and thrown back into the ring. Xias entered the ring afterwards and dropped his right knee across the middle of Enforcer's arm. He grabbed the arm and hyper-extended it across the knee, causing Enforcer to scream out in pain and reach for the ropes with his other arm. The referee, not wanting to see Xias torture Enforcer for another five-count, quickly got in-between the two men and practically pushed Xias off of him. Xias looked less than pleased as the referee got up, but then rushed past him, pushing him aside as he charged Enforcer. Enforcer, who had managed to stand back up in the corner, saw him coming, and with his left arm he grabbed Xias and used his own momentum against him, throwing him hard into the corner instead! He then opened up on Xias with left hand after left hand, rocking his skull with the hard shots. After many of these blows, he used his left arm again to Irish whip Xias hard into the opposite ropes, sending him sternum-first into the top turnbuckle, then rolled him up with a school boy from behind as he stumbled backwards. ONE! TWO! But Xias was quickly out before the count of three. Both men stood back up at the same time.....and both men noticed two people on the opposite aprons who weren't supposed to be there. It was X and Exel Pure, each member of the Avenging Angels having come out of an opposite section of the crowd. Xias and Enforcer each turned to the Angel behind them, and Xias dropkicked Pure off the apron while Enforcer hit X with a hard left jab to knock him down as well. As both men turned around, however, Xias leaped onto Enforcer's arm with a flying armbar, hooking both of his legs around Enforcer's right arm. Enforcer yelled out in pain as his arm was tortured by Xias' hold, and with nowhere to go in the middle of the ring, he tapped out with his left hand. The crowd cheered as "Battle" began to play again and Xias quickly released the hold, allowing Enforcer to collapse to the mat in pain. Just like that, out of nowhere, Xias had ended the match, and shown just why he was so dangerous inside the squared circle. Perhaps, he was at his most dangerous since he had ever come to the Fans Wrestling Organization. However, as Xias watched, he saw someone emerge from the back and on top of the stage, smirking and clapping. Xias snarled at the sight of Eternity.....and then quickly turned and ducked a clothesline attempt by the massive Exel Pure. He hit Pure in the side of the head with a hard kick, causing him to stumble on his feet, then leaped into the air with a standing enziguri that put Pure down to his knees. Before Xias could continue the punishment, however, he was hit with a low blow from behind by X. The fans booed as X turned the Internet Champion around and put him in position for one of his own finishing moves- the Cradle Piledriver. Before he could do that, however, X was stopped by Enforcer, who turned X around and slammed the fWo Internet Title belt into his face! Eternity's smirk on top of the stage changed to an angry expression as she realized her brother was actually coming to Xias' aide! Enforcer, still holding his right arm in pain, helped Xias back to his feet.....before draping the fWo Internet Title belt over his shoulder. He nodded to him before exiting the ring as "Battle" started playing again. Xias watched him leave before climbing to the top turnbuckle and raising his Internet Title belt high into the air to another loud pop from the fans.....and he pointed at Eternity on top of the stage. Eternity just glared at him from atop that stage, both of these longtime enemies knowing that their paths would only continue to cross in the future. Drinking Buddies Death stalked down the hallway, a menacing shape clad in a heavy black trenchcoat. Backstage workers scattered out of his way as he walked, parting like the Red Sea. Tonight his steps were heavy and his purpose was sure. Woe be to those who got in his way. He turned and the corner and saw what he had been looking for. The water cooler. Then, his face stretched in a grimace. Somebody was already there. That somebody - Black Quicksilver, the fWo World Heavyweight Champion. Death slowly walked up behind Quicksilver, who finished filling his cup and stood up as he saw a shadow extend itself onto the table in front of him. He stood there for a second, then turned around to come face to face with one of the men he would face at Meltdown. Quicksilver looked at Death, who stood in front of him, frowning in a way that would have menaced most men. Quicksilver looked at it and grinned. "I didn't know you drank water." "Move." "Hmm, no." But thanks for the offer." If Death were a cartoon, this would have been the point where his head turned red and starting shooting out jets of steam, but he wasn't and so it was not. Instead he stepped to the side of BQ, took a paper cup, and when he turned around, Quicksilver had moved away from the front of the cooler. "See? All you had to do was be patient." Death was silent and started filling his cup. He then spoke without turning. "You know you won't have that title by the time Meltdown's over. If it's not mine - which it will be - you'll be stabbed in the back for it by somebody. Frontier, most likely." The levity went out of Quicksilver's voice. "You're wrong," he said flatly. "No," Death replied as he turned back around. "I'm really not." Once again the two stars were face to face, staring at each other. "Yes, you really are... you can't beat me, you've proven that already. Brand's a good man... but I can beat him too. If you think you're going to get in my head, you're wasting your time. Quicksilver turned to go, showing his back to Death with no fear. Then he stopped... and turned around. "Here... I'm not that thirsty after all. Have a drink on me." Quicksilver tossed the cup towards Death, splashing the water in his face and wetting his hair. Death stared at Quicksilver, eyes blazing. Quicksilver clenched his fists at his side, ready to defend himself... but there was no move forward. "Yeah. That's what I thought." He left. Death watched him go, furious but controlling himself. The so-called Champion could have his laughs now... in fact, Death wished him as many of them as he could get. Because after Meltdown... He'd never laugh again.
