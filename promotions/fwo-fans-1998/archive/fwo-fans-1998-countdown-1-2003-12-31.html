Neo came awake, his body numb and his mind hot like fire. Blackness filled his vision, and a dull roar could be heard from far, far away. He slumped over, feeling the sharp pain as he moved onto his broken arm. Groaning, he vomited thick blood and bile and opened his eyes on near darkness. He moved his good arm under him, slipping on the sticky blood that coated the floor beneath him. Slowly, ever so slowly, the events of the last three days came back to him. He had traveled to New York, but for why he was no longer certain. To do something, or to stop something... but the reasons, the why, were lost to him. He had found Fallen there, Ryan Moore, and had... Neo moaned, and for a moment his mind drifted to Dina Sol, to David Spencer, and to William Bailey. Now all dead, and he felt a hot tear course down his cheek. He was not sure why he was reacting this way, and his mind clouded as quickly as it had cleared. Whatever was left of himself, his true self, was eaten away like cancer. In a fleeting moment, John Malcolm vanished into darkness and left behind only Neo. It was Neo who was strong enough to slump upward, strong enough to pull his broken and useless arm up onto his lap. Neo was all that could drive him forward now, and more recent memories came back to him. He had met Fallen in the arena, Madison Square Garden, and it was empty save the two of them. The fWo would be running a show there in two days, but for now it was only the two of them. The dull roar came back to his ears, and Neo glanced upward, up from the floor of the boiler room and through concrete to the cold stone and rock that seperated this basement from the arena proper. The roar was coming from there, and with a start Neo realized that it was not from his skull, but from hundreds... thousands... of fans. December 31 was here... God save them. The show was now. Neo remembered rushing Fallen, and then fighting him. Fighting him until both passed out from the pain, then awakening to fight again. They were not fighting a match, or even some bout of honor. This was a fight to finish things... this was a fight to the death. Neo turned slowly, looking for his opponent. Twenty feet away lay Fallen, his back to him, and covered in blood. Neo looked at his broken arm, and grunted at the white bone that had fractured and torn through the flesh. He was bleeding to death now, he felt himself failing and nearing the edge of blackness that would mean passing out once more. He couldn't let himself... he had to finish what he had started. It took nearly ten minutes, but Neo finally got to his feet. He let himself stay still for a moment, his gaze never leaving the still body of Fallen. Perhaps he was dead... Neo remembered tossing him from the top of the stairs onto the cold floor below and the crack, perhaps that crack had been Fallen's skull and it was all over. Slowly, cautiously, he moved forward. He ignored the pain in his arm, the searing burning in his chest. He was certain that his ribs were cracked, broken, and the wet breathing sound more than likely meant fluid was filling his lungs and would soon rob him of his ability to breathe. No matter... he had only one thing to do now. One final thing to finish. Five steps, then ten. Above him the roar grew louder and louder. The crowd were on their feet... it sounded like the show had yet to begin. Fifteen steps... and now Neo was mere feet from Fallen. He could see the rough cuts in his arm and chest, the matted blood and place where the hair had been torn away. He saw Fallen's chest slowly rise and fall... well then, he was not dead after all. But he was still, and Neo knew that in the few remaining moments of his life he could finish it. He was making things right, and the part of him that was now fully Neo grinned despite the pain. He moved closer, but did not see Fallen's hand clench upon the wrench hidden under his body. Nor did Neo see Fallen's jaw set.
