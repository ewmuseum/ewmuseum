Introduction Cyberslam come and gone, the fWo attempts to align themselves to the new world... Gathering Soldiers "So what are your plans?" The voice startled Brand Frontier. He was lost in thought, considering the events of last week by the Dark Riders it was no wonder. On this past Monday Night Ruahh~! he and Ice sought to hold off the malevolent Death and Donaven Winters for one night at least. Instead Ice was martyred in a disgusting display reminiscent of Death's previous incarnations. It was happening all over again, and remembering Ice's cryptic words last week, Frontier now wondered if he was right. How would he weather this storm? Now though he quickly rose to his feet, turning in a guarded position, ready for whomever was attached to the voice. He was startled more however by who was actually standing there. It wasn't another enemy at the gate so to speak, it wasn't another attack from different front. No, it was Paladin. Standing tall in the doorway, taking up the entirety of the space the former CWL Superstar smiled at the paragon. Unclenching his fist Frontier eased his stance, stepping toward Paladin and extending his hand at the same time. "Sir." Brand nodded as the two big men firmly grasped hands. "Joshua, Paladin if you must, but not sir." "Of course." Frontier responded before motioning the hero in to his dressing room. Paladin was quick to get back to the business at hand however. "Now Brand, as I asked before... what are your plans?" Understanding immediately the question and the levels that accompanied it Frontier returned to his seat, propping a leg up and leaning against it as he did. "I fight, same as I always do, it's what I do, who I am. I'd think you'd understand that." "Of course I do son, the problem is, against ones such as the Dark Riders your methods will be ineffectual." Listerin almost had a scolding manner. Brand turned towards the elder hero at that comment. "Being the good guy will always work, but your methods are outdated. This is a fouler world, a dirtier world. To make a finer world you have to work on it... and in some cases force it. You and I both know that evil is killing the world... killing this sport. I tried to start in the CWL, but the heart and soul of the place was gone before we knew it. But it isn't about passing out flowers and slapping hands with the fans... the same fans who cheer evil and bad actions. These people aren't going to learn... and teaching them doesn't work. You've got to do more than that... you have to just take the decision out of their hands." "We have to make that finer world... on top of them if we have to. I would like to do this by your side if you'll let me. I'm not what I used to be, but with my experience and your heart we can gather more. A force to reckon with the Dark Riders, to stop them, finally and for good." The paragon now stood and looked in to the blue eyes of Paladin. "I agree with so much you say Paladin, but I disagree on your terms of execution. We can't simply stoop to their level. In doing so we would do nothing more than propagate their desires. That is what they want, they want for there to be no line between good and evil. They seek to smudge the lines between the two until the gray envelopes us all. They can exist there, we cannot." Frontier's words were stern, and spoke of the years he has crusaded against the likes of the Riders and their ilk. Paladin simply stared back into the eyes of the mighty hero. Long seconds passed as both men stood stoically. "Then we agree to disagree, on that respect. Still you cannot do this alone. Let me walk beside you, I will fight with you and we can still grow a legion in our wake." With that, Paladin extended his hand. Brand Frontier accepted the hand, and the offer, "I will not sway from my beliefs good sir, but we will stand 'gainst Death and the Riders together." Paladin nodded his approval. "That's all I ask son. In time however you will see that my way, unfortunately, is the only way to fight monsters such as these..." With the two mighty heroes standing opposite each other the scene shifted elsewhere many wondering exactly what this union would do to the momentum gathered by the Dark Riders last week as Ice fell to the wayside.
