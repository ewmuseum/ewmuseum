Sunday Slaughter, 5 December 1999

5 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "You see, being in charge of a national promotion at the height of wrestling popularity is no easy task... you've got a lot of ins... a lot of outs... a lot of what-have-yous..." James Armstrong escorted the young Penny Pureheart down the hall of the arena. It was busy, with workers running in all directions to get ready for Sunday Slaughter. There had already been one fight earlier between VL and Crucifix, and both wrestlers had to be escorted off the premises. Penny watched the chaos with a bemused expression on her face that James Armstrong didn't catch. Watching Fury and Nixon argue over who would be in the dark match tonight, Penny posed a question, speaking into her tape recorder as she did so. "With all this activity Mr. Armstrong, don't you find it difficult to keep coming up with ideas that are appropriate for your younger viewers?" James Armstrong shrugged and said, "Not really... I watch a lot of porn, you see? And the plots that come off of those movies are great... they give me all the ideas I need for booking and then some!" Penny blinked hard, and moved the tape recorder closer to Armstrong as she said, "Porn? But doesn't the Government frown upon that sort of thing for children's programming?" James Armstrong laughed. "I don't think so... besides, the Government would be more angry if they knew that I haven't paid our taxes in over eight months." Penny blinked again, and raised the tape recorder still closer to James Armstrong's face. Armstrong looked at it, confused. "Hey... is that a tape recorder?" Penny shook her head. "Er... no... it's a... um... iron." For a moment this seemed to confuse Armstrong, but then he seemed to let the thought drift away. Neither of them noticed Machina looking on from behind a corner. Beneath his mask, he sighed a blissful, lovelorn sigh. James saw none of this, but down the hall he did spot Chris Universal and Scott Slugger entering the arena, and greeted them loudly. "Chris! Scott! Wonderful! You're here!" Slugger and Universal exchanged a glance that consisted of rolling eyes and groans. Universal patted Slugger on the shoulder. "Let me handle this Scott... you go get ready. I'll make sure you have a match tonight... a title match." Fallen vs. Joy Fireworks exploded into the area as Sunday Slaughter came on the air. The crowd came to their feet, cheering and screaming as Chris Finn and Matt Loco came to the announcing desk. The lights dimmed, the crowd lets out a collective groan as a giant smiley face fills the Slaughter Screen. Scant moments later, Joy and Glee emerge from backstage, their faces aglow with friendship and happiness. Joy steps into the ring, a mic in his hand, and waits for Fallen to appear. The lights dim, bringing out Fallen of the Dark Riders, as well as Poison and Demon. Before Fallen can advance to the ring, Joy starts speaking. "Fallen, buddy, pal, you and I, we have a lot of history. And I was thinking that rather than a plain ol' wrestling match, that we could start this show off right. These fans don't want to see some brutal contest! They want to see a Happy Match! Are you with me on this one?" Fallen grimaces, then rushes the ring, sliding in on his belly. He stands up, and motions for the microphone, as Poison and Demon watch on from the top of the entrance ramp. A pleased look appears on Joy's face, and he gives the mic to Fallen, who promptly strikes it over Joy's head. The bell rings, and the match is on. Fallen chops away at Joy, who quickly drops to the mat and rolls out of the ring. Fallen follows out with a suicide dive, taking both men down. Fallen is first to his feet, and begins to stomp away at the back of Joy's head, which Glee seems to genuinely disapprove of. As she voices her unhappiness with this, Fallen shoots her an ice-cold glare, quieting her. Fallen rolls Joy into the ring, but Glee grabs his ankle when he tries to climb into the ring. He goes to backhand her, but Joy prevents that with a forearm to Fallen's skull. Fallen turns his attention back to Joy, who then suplexes Fallen back into the ring. He covers, but barely gets the one count before Fallen kicks out. Both men back to there feet, and Fallen forces Joy into the corner with a succession of knees to the sternum. Fallen lifts the dazed Joy into position on the turnbuckle and brings him back to the mat with a super brainbuster. Joy valiantly tries to stand, and is finally assisted by Fallen, who then quickly drops him with a DDT. Joy's starting to look far more out of it than usual, as Fallen proceeds to piledrive him into the mat. Joy's almost gone when Fallen locks in a Dragon Sleeper, which finishes the job. The referee lifts Joy's arm thrice, and it falls each time, thus giving Fallen the win. He motions for Poison and Demon to join him in the ring, and the three of them tear away at Joy before a bevy of officials rush from the backstage area to disrupt the gang beating. Fallen retrieves the microphones, and lifts it to his mouth. "The dark dawn has fallen... behold the force that will bring about the end of days... night falls on the fWo, and with it your future. Collins will be but the first... and when we are finished you shall all be laid in shallow graves and burned in the fire that leaves but ash. The Dark Riders have come... and the end is here." Fallen drops the microphone to the mat, and the Riders exit to the backstage. Result: Fallen by Submission Chapter One: Payoff "James... I need you to make a match." James Armstrong scratched his head, as Chris Universal smiled at him broadly. "Chris... I'd love to... anything for you... but you see, the card has already been set, and I can't make changes now. The workers like to have stability... not changes to the card while the show is running." Universal smiled even more broadly and reached into his pocket. He produced a handful of bills and waved them in front of Armstrong's face. Armstrong nodded, his eyes taking on a glassy glaze. "A new match... yeah... nobody will mind..." Universal patted James Armstrong on the back. "Good man. Now this is what I need you to do. You see, at BattleBowl Scott Collins escaped with a victory he didn't deserve. I know you are as much a fan of Scott Slugger as I am... so I suggest a rematch. Tonight. For the Hardcore title." Armstrong's glassy glaze faded as a more concerned expression crossed his face. "I... I can't do that Chris. Fans don't want to see a rematch so soon... and besides... Death is... mean..." Universal sighed, and produced another handful of bills. The glassy eyed expression returned to James Armstrong's face, and he mumbled, "Yes... a return match... everybody will like that..." Universal laughed, and left James drooling over his newly found wealth... Havok (c) vs. Waru The lights dimmed as Waru came to the ring, walking out to something by Bruce Springsteen. He rolled into the ring and looked with contempt at the crowd around him. He asked for a microphone, and was handed it with a sigh. He looked at the crowd, and began to speak. "I know what in the hell you're thinking... here's Waru... whoever the hell he is. Well listen clearly fans... the bookers have decided to write a little match between myself and the Cruiserweight champion Havok. Here's a man who has little ring psychology, almost no match working skill, and hasn't proven himself as a great worker worthy to hold such an important title. I looked up in the Observer and I could not find one... not ONE... match above four stars that Havok has been in. Where is the workrate? Coming to watch one of my matches is 'good'. Going to watch one of Havok's matches... well, that's just goofy bad. Almost as bad as his microphone work. This, and many other things, is a sign that the fWo is totally in love with their WORDS, but have no clue about people's ability to *deliver them*. So Havok gets pushed to a title, and I'm wasted on the undercard. Makes sense to me! But today I get a shot to bring some credibility to a match. So to all you bucketheads out there... get ready for a WRESTLING match. I don't know if you're getting the point or not... but Waru will show you watch it means to be a great WORKER." With that, the fWo Cruiserweight Champion Havok came out onto the ramp, flanked by the Black Panther and Wolf Fang. The three walked to the ring to a huge reaction from the crowd, who cheered them loudly. Waru watched them, and fumed at the crowd's reaction to him as Havok slid into the ring. Spinning around on one heel Waru began to yell at the fans, but as his weight shifted he tripped into the ropes and snapped backward, cracking his head on the mat. Out cold, Waru lay on his back as Havok and the referee looked down on him in confusion. Shrugging, Havok put a foot on his chest and got the three count. He raised his arms to the cheering crowd, as Sunday Slaughter went to commercial. Result: Havok by Pinfall Chapter Two: Heaven As Slaughter continued, a familiar looking woman began to stroll out onto the SlaughterStage. Shackles were on her ankles and wrists, although the middle chain was broken so she could move somewhat freely. The woman walked with a slight limp, and a worn out expression appeared on her face. An explosion occurred behind her on the stage as she made her way down the ramp, she walked up the stage into the ring before she knelt down in the middle of the ring, looked up to the heavens, and began to pray. The arena immediately blackened and a spotlight shone on her in the ring, while she continued to pray on her knees. A bell began to toll approximately six times, before the number six appeared on the SlaughterScreen. The number exploded into the word "dead", before the lights came back on and the woman, who now seemed more familiar than before, stood to her feet. Fyre walked over to the edge of the ring and asked for a microphone, before she walked back to the center of the ring, and began to speak. "I am not the person that I once was. I am no longer... Fyre. I am... Heaven. And I am here to forewarn you all of what is about to happen in the fWo. The fWo is going to undergo a series of changes over the next four weeks... and it will be one of the darkest times in the history of this promotion. But the worst will happen on January 5th of the new year. He... will return. He... will take back what is his. And you... will not be able to do anything about it. Life will change. No one will survive. The fWo will become His. The fWo will die when He chooses to. And on that night, when He returns, the second coming will be complete. Prepare for the second coming. It will happen..." Suddenly, "Heaven" knelt to her knees again and looked up to the sky, and a lightning bolt struck the SlaughterStage and a burst of flames engulfed the stage. An overhead view of the stage revealed the fire to spell out "Dead", as darkness blanketed the arena once again. The fire on the stage continued to rage on, before the number six once again appeared on the SlaughterScreen. The number exploded and the fire on the stage disappeared completely. The light returned to the arena and the ring was empty, with no traces of "Heaven" in sight... Eron (c) vs. Wretch Wretch heads to the ring alone as Slaughter returns from commercials, looking much more serious than normal. He waits in the ring for the International Champion, Eron. Eron steps out from the back to a giant wave of boos, which he just eats up. He strides to the ring, arguing with the fans at ringside. Distracted, he doesn't notice Wretch come over the top rope with a twisting plancha. Eron falls hard onto the floor with Wretch on top of him. Wretch springs back to his feet and senton splashes the International Champ. Bringing Eron back to his feet, Wretch whips Eron into the apron, then nails him with a leg lariat. Wretch slides back into the ring, taunting Eron. Eron reclaims his footing and enters the ring without trouble from Wretch, then locks up with the smaller grappler. Eron quickly applies a headlock, then starts smashing away at Wretch's face with closed fists. The ref starts laying down a five count, so Eron stops at four, then starts all over again. The ref reaches four once more, and this time Eron lets go. He blows his bright red hair out of his eyes and heads back over to Wretch. He lifts Wretch into the air and aims a powerbomb, which is countered by Wretch into a facebuster. The subsequent pin attempt gets two before Eron kicks out. Angry, Eron rushes Wretch, who swings onto Eron's back and brings him down into a pinning situation with a crucifix. Eron manages to kick out again at two, but quickly gets caught in La Magiatral from Wretch for another deuce. Eron kicks out, enraged, and rolls into a crouch. As Wretch approaches him, he lets loose a nasty shot to Wretch's "underground", doubling him over in pain. Eron presses him into the air, then drops him throat first onto the steel cable ring ropes. Wretch writhes in pain, and Eron grabs his legs. He looks ready to lock in the De-Rouger, but instead delivers a headbutt to Wretch's groin. Tired of the match, he grabs a handful of tights with one arm, rolls Wretch up, places his other arm firmly into Wretch's throat, and puts his feet on the ropes. The ref, oddly, doesn't notice any of it, merely counting to three. Eron kicks Wretch out of the ring, then starts pumping his fists and yelling in victory. The camera microphones clearly pick him up boasting that he can beat anyone, when suddenly a strange thing happens. Someone steps onto the entrance ramp, dressed in the fashion of an 18th century English Dutchess. Their mannerisms are dead-on, and it's only after the camera closes in on them that it's revealed to be the US Marshal in that garb. In a buttressed dress, he doesn't attempt to enter the ring; rather, he waits for Eron to step out. Eron does so, suspiciously, then makes a crack about US Marshall cross-dressing. US Marshall stumbles backwards, reeling in shock at Eron's comment, then slaps Eron in the face with his gloved hand. The two men start brawling, finally disappearing past the curtain, when the cameras cut back to something else happening backstage... Result: Eron by Pinfall Chapter Three: The hunt for the championship Jake ran down the hallway, his fat wobbling in the wind as he ran. Big sections of gut rolled from side to side, and buckets of sweat poured down the doughy flesh. He looked frightened, and with good reason. Courage ran behind him, a look of rage across his face. Jake turned the corner and half ran, half fell into Primetime's dressing room, letting the door slam shut behind him. Courage began to bang on the door with both fists, screaming as he did so. "You fat little bitch... you tell Primetime that this Wednesday he's dead! Hear me? Dead!" Courage continued to pound on the door, but after the Slugger kicking down a door incident weeks ago the arenas had installed steel doors that held fast under abuse. Courage finally gave up, growling to himself. He turned, and found himself face to face with the smiling, wide eyed Boston Strangler. "You want Primetime? Get in line Courage... we have business with the champion ourselves. You wouldn't... get in the way of that... would you? Courage slowly backed away from the Strangler, not letting his eyes drop from the Stranger's intense gaze. He backed down the hallway, finally vanishing around a corner. With him gone, the Strangler's gaze very slowly turned to the closed door of Primetime's locker room. A thin smile wrote itself over his face. "Oh Primetime... Come out... come out... wherever you are..." Mike Bear vs. War Back from commercial, Mike Bear enters the ring first, looked pumped up for his match with the muscle of the Dark Riders. War comes out next, not being accompanied by any of his stablemates. They circle each other as the bell rings, and War takes the advantage with a quick cheap forearm to the gut. War whips Bear into the ropes, and catches him coming off with a back elbow, knocking Bear against the ropes. War charges at Bear, but Bear lifts him up and back body drops the big man over the top rope. Bear slides to the outside, and whips War into the guardrail, following him in with a clothesline, squashing him against the steel. Now Bear takes War to the steel ring steps, and slams his face into them. Bear now tosses War back into the ring. With War stunned, Bear heads to the top. He waits for War to get up, and delivers a hard flying lariat, knocking War off his feet. Bear covers, but only gets a two count. Bear lifts War to his feet, and tosses him into the turnbuckles. Bear now sets War on the top rope. He climbs up with him, and executes a devastating superplex. War is down, and Bear covers once more. However, this only gets a two count. Undaunted. Bear brings War to his feet, and throws him into the ropes. War instead reverses this into a powerful short-arm clothesline, followed by a quick legdrop. He covers, but this only gets one. War brings Bear to his feet again, and throws him into the ropes, putting his head down for a back body drop. However, Bear catches him, hooks him up, and spikes him with a piledriver. Bear covers, but War kicks out at two and two-thirds. Bear gets up and waits for War to stagger to his feet. War slowly does, and when he reaches his feet, Bear lunges at War and nearly decapitates him with a superkick. Bear then begins to head to the top rope, looking to end the match. He flies off with a big splash, and rolls off of War and to his feet. He brings War up and attempts a Tombstone piledriver, but cannot get him up the first time. He tries again, but is once more unsuccessful. He tries one last time, and finally gets the gigantic War in position, and spikes him with the maneuver. Bear makes the cover, but before the three can be registered, the Dark Riders storm the ring and begin pounding on Bear. With Bear worn down, War lifts him in a Gorilla press, and then tosses him at Death, who nails the Death Penalty in mid-air. Before any more damage can be done, Wretch and Fear storm the ring, chairs in hand, and Fear gets in a good chairshot on Plague before the Dark Riders roll out of the ring, and, still staring at the Underground, head back to the back. Wretch and Fear tend to their fallen friend as Slaughter continues... Result: Mike Bear by DQ Chapter Four: Bad news The Dark Riders walked through the ring entrance into the backstage area, still laughing over their earlier assault. Death pointed down one hall, telling War and Plague to collect their bags so they could get out of here. As they went Death smiled to himself, obviously confidant about what they accomplished. Stone chided him. "Scotty, you've got your wristbands on backward." Death's smiled faded and he looked down at his wrists. "Aw crumbs..." He fiddled with them as an fWo assistant neared, and handed Collins a slip of paper. Death growled at him, causing the assistant to flinch back in fear. He quickly left the area, and Collins looked over at Stone with a smile that showed he was pleased with himself. Stone was looking absently down the hall however, and Death grumbled to himself that she had missed his frightening of the young man. He read the paper, and his faint smile turned to a mask of rage. "WHAT? Tonight?" From behind him came laughter, and Death spun to face Chris Universal and Scott Slugger, who were both trying to contain their laughter at him. Death pointed and shouted, "YOU! You're behind this!" Universal nodded. "That's right candy boy... lace up, and bring that Hardcore title with you. But make sure it's all you bring, because your little goons aren't allowed at ringside. Myself however... well, my presense will be required. Ta, ta, Scotty... see you in the ring." The two walked away to get ready for their match as Death continued to fume with Stone by his side. "He thinks he can get away with this? Well think again baseball boy... you're in for a nasty surprise..." Ultra Cool 2000 (c) vs. NXS NXS enters first, looking as collectively freakish as always. Fireworks explode as Ultra-Cool 2000 enters next, to a big pop from the fans. For some reason, Ultra Violet seems to be missing his title as he walks to the ring. Violet follows behind, with a calculating look on her face. UC2K simultaneously leapfrog into the ring, and the bell rings as Ultra-Violet and RottDog face off first. Ultra Violet takes the advantage, hitting a pair of dropkicks, and knocks RottDog into the corner, where he throws him out with a monkey flip. RottDog charges at Ultra Violet, but Ultra Violet catches him, hooks him, and delivers a release northern lights suplex, stunning the big man. Meanwhile, on the apron, Violet is whispering into the ear of Kid Cool, whose face suddenly blushes. Ultra Violet notices none of this however, and knocks RottDog down as he gets up with a spinning heel kick. Ultra Violet tags the still semi-red-faced Kid Cool, who comes in with a flying leg lariat, knocking RottDog down and into the corner, where he tags Steel. Steel charges, but Kid Cool ducks and catches him coming off with a testicular kick. Ultra Violet, meanwhile, has occupied himself looking blankly at the way the ceiling lights reflect off the shiny metal ramp, and thus notices none of this. Kid Cool rakes the eyes of Steel, and throws him into the corner. He charges in backwards, slamming into Steel, who stumbles out. Kid Cool hooks him up and delivers a hard Russian legsweep, and floats over with a cover. Unbeknownst to Ultra Violet and the ref, Kid Cool puts his feet up on the second rope for added leverage, and scores the pinfall victory for his team. Cool walks over to Ultra Violet and taps him repeatedly on the shoulder, breaking him out of his trance. This done, UC2K walks back to the locker room victorious, with Violet walking noticeably closer to Kid Cool than to Ultra Violet. Result: Ultra Cool 2000 by Pinfall Chapter Five: Threats Backstage a chair flew across the room and crashed into the side of a wall. The thrower, Mike Bear, yelled. "This is BULLSHIT!" James Armstrong recoiled from the noise, and backed against a wall. Bear advanced on him, as Wretch and Fear stood flanking his sides. Bear grabbed him by the collar and lifted him off the floor and continued to shout, "Listen here you worthless bastard... I don't know how you got the job of commissioner... but if Travis were here he wouldn't be dicking us the way you are. We beat the best teams in all of wrestling, one, two and three and then get screwed out of the Tag Titles. We didn't even get pinned! Now two idiots have the titles... or one of them anyway... one of them didn't even bother to bring his belt to the god damn ring! I want to know when we are going to get our shot at the belts... we sure as hell didn't lose them, and we deserve our shot. You don't give it to us... we're going to take it. Got me?" Before James could answer, a loud voice broke in from behind him. "Not so fast, boy." Cactus Jon stood in the doorway, and next to him were Tom and the Ninja. "You had your shot boys... and you lost it. Now step aside. I got screwed at BattleBowl too... if the Boston Strangler hadn't broken into my match I'd be the fWo World Champion right now. Well... you don't screw with Canada. My boys here are going to be the next Tag Team Champions... and you can't stop us." The men glared at each other as the cameras went back to the ring... Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Briana The crowd booed as the Flying Frenchie strode to the ring, the fWo Womens title over his shoulder and a Lamont t-shirt worn over his upper body. He bowed and nodded to the fans as he went, ignoring the cat calls and cursing. The Frenchie climbed up the ring steps and ducked into the ring, and held the fWo Womens Title high above him. Pulling a "Sunday Frenchie" microphone from his tights, he began to address the crowd. "Eh... 'allo... yes, zis is ze Frenchie here once again to try to get rid of zis 'nsuferable title of mine. Here zit is... come to take zit from me, I dares you! Eh... but ze American wom'n are ze worthless group of rats. N'body has taken zis title even when ze challenges are easy as ze pie. Well, bring f'ward ze next challenger... see if zis woman can perform even ze simplest of tasks." Briana walked to the ring, glaring at the Frenchie but getting a strong pop from the crowds. She slid into the ring and walked up to face the Frenchie, reaching for the Womans title over his shoulder. The Frenchie leapt backward and brought a finger up to shake in her face. "Non, non... zis title must be earned cherie... and zis is your challenge. Ze fans, zey demand more... how shall I say... in and out?" The Frenchie held up a box and handed it to Briana. "Go, put zis bathing suit on and return. Zis is ze bathing suit challenge... if ze fans like what they see, you get ze title." Briana hesitated, but then took the box and walked to the back. The Frenchie meanwhile, entertained the fans with a traditional French story. "Il ?tait une fois il y avait un petit gar?on stupide nomm? Eron l'implacable qui a eu un titre qu'il n'a pas gagn?. Il ?tait un petit gosse sniveling qui a ?t? battu par le Frenchie dans un match tr?s facile qu'il a gagn? avec un bras attach? derri?re le sien dos..." Just then Briana came back to the ring, dressed in a robe and climbed back into the ring. The Frenchie waited as Briana seemed to consider if she actually wanted to go through with it, but then seemed to resign herself and slipped off the robe. She was clad in a tiny bikini, the top stretched over her ample breasts and a tight bikini thong barely covering her lower region. The crowd cheered wildly, and the Frenchie nodded appreciatively. However, once the noise of the crowd had died down, the Frenchie started to leave the ring with the title still over his shoulder. Briana put a hand on his arm to stop him, and reached for the Womens championship. The Frenchie pulled it away from her reach and shook his head. "Sorry wom'n... zis title is not for you... zis outfit you wear... zis is not ze traditional topless fashion worn in France... and zis is ze French contest after all. Sorry... zis is grounds for disqualification." The Frenchie left the ring with the belt, as Briana fumed behind him... Result: Briana loses by top wearing Death vs. Scott Slugger Scott Slugger walked to the ring, Chris Universal at his side. Scott Slugger climbed into the ring and motioned to his waist, and then reached for a microphone. "Last week at Battle Bowl I put my record on the line against that loser candy boy Scott Collins. Well, Collins robbed that victory from me... so tonight I'm going to rob something from him... his Hardcore..." Suddenly the SlaughterScreen blazed to life as footage from backstage was shown. Death was on the screen, pacing down the hallways and smiling an evil, demonic smile. "Hello, Slugger. You want this title? You want this title so bad? Well Slugger... you can't always get what you want. You! Come over here!" Death shouted at someone off-camera, and Joy stumbled into the picture, looking frightened. "Y... y... yes Mr... C... c... c...Death?" Death smiled at Joy, and handed him the fWo Hardcore title. "It's your lucky day, boy. You've just become the fWo Hardcore Champion. I'm my gift to you, you silly little bastard. Now beat it." Joy looked at the title with wonder, and grabbing it quickly ran away. Death turned back to the camera and smiled. "Now Slugger... you don't get any title... but I'm going to come kick your ass just for the fun of it." The SlaughterScreen went black as inside the ring Scott Slugger screamed with rage and pure hatred for the leader of the Dark Riders. Death appeared on the ramp alone, and marched down the ring with a sledgehammer hanging from one hand. Slugger bid him to come on, and as Death climbed under the ropes Slugger kicked him in the face, and grabbed the sledgehammer away from him. Slugger swung, striking the face of the Dark Rider and sending a wide spray of blood into the crowd. Slugger swung again, but this time Death caught the sledgehammer and ripped it from his grasp. The two brawled to the corner, and then tumbled over the side and out to the floor. Death picked up a steel chair and swung it down onto the head of Scott Slugger. Slugger fell to his knees, but slowly rose to his feet. Death swung again, denting the chair over Slugger's skull. Slugger lay still for a moment, and then got to one knee again. Death swung down with the chair a third time, and the metal of the chair bent and broke over Slugger's head. Death stood over him, triumphant. He did not notice Slugger swing upward with a fist, connecting hard with Death's groin. Death howled in pain as Slugger struggled to get to his feet with the aid of Chris Universal. Chris Universal grabbed the steps and tossed them at Collins, who caught them and held them. Slugger attacked with a superkick into the steps, which crushed steel against flesh and drove Collins back into the steel crowd barrier. Bleeding from the mouth, Slugger spat and cursed. "Now you pay, Collins..." Suddenly the lights went out, and the crowd was pitched into darkness. From the dark, the voice of Fallen drifted out. "You don't know how right you are, Slugger." Fire exploded from the turnbuckles and Fallen, Poison, Demon and Death appeared on the top of the entrance ramp. They walked to the ring, and both Slugger and Universal backed up from Collins, unsure of what to make of this development. At ringside Demon and Poison bound Collin's hands and feet, and drug him into the ring. The four began to beat down Collins, and Scott Slugger and Chris Universal decided to cut their losses and simply leave the area. Inside the ring Spencer's Dark Riders backed away from the body of Scott Collins, which lay in a pool of blood, his body bent and broken. The cameras began to fade away, as Fallen faced the cameras. "This is only the beginning... it will only... get... worse." Sunday Slaughter came to an end, the screams of the crowd and the darkness all the viewer could see... Result: No Contest
