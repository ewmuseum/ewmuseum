Wednesday Warfare, 26 January 2000

26 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "And have you tried the showers in this country? It's a freaking miracle anyone ever gets clean. I'll tell you one thing... you can take the concept of a bath and shove it right up Sid Snow's ass." Wretch nodded as Mike Bear continued to rant while lacing up his boots. "And another thing... did you try and take the bloody... I mean damn... oh hell, now I'm saying their stupid words. Anyway, I was trying to take the train to get from the hotel to the arena and I'm standing outside the terminal for like thirty minutes before I get tired of waiting and go see what in the hell is going on. So I go up to the counter, and the guy is like, 'oh, sorry mate... but the train is canceled for today, there's a lad fell asleep in the track outside Edinburgh and we've got to wait for the bloke to move along before we can get the rail going again.' And I'm like, god dammit, if this was New York and we saw a guy asleep on the tracks it would be like, 'chalk another one up on the side and push on the gas!' God admit... what kind of screwed up backward country is this?" Wretch patted Bear's back. "Hey man, calm down. Title match tonight, and tomorrow we go back to the US. It's not Canada, but hey... it's a start." Wretch actually didn't mind Europe as much as Mike Bear seemed to. It was different from America, more laid back... and not bad at all. He was going to be somewhat sorry to see it go. He slung the tag title strap over one shoulder and smiled at it. Besides, they had won the belts here. For that reason alone it's not a bad place. Sure, the soccer nuts were a little weird, but every country had their soccer nuts. The fireworks sounded, and Wretch turned toward the sound of the crowd. The roar was gigantic... it was a packed house tonight. Wretch turned to Bear. "Last night. Let's make it a good one." Bear laughed. "Against the Gunmen? Please. The only way this is going to be a good one is if the Gunmen don't show up. Speaking of which... anyone tell Fear yet that Sid wants a match?" Wretch shook his head. "Nope." They looked at each other for a moment and then broke out into laughter. "Better tell him to wear the full suit and not the singled," laughed Bear. Wretch nodded. "No shit." Inside the arena, Wednesday Warfare began. Ice vs. Chris Universal Matt Panzer introduced Ice who came out to a warm reaction from the crowd, as "Crazy Train" echoed throughout the arena. Ice appeared to be business as usual, and this was a big match for him as if he won he would receive a shot at the fWo Internet Title. As Ice reached the ring he raised his arms high in the air, and the fans responded with applause. "Do you buy what The Sellout is selling?" sounded, and out walked The Sellout Chris Universal onto the stage. The fans gave him a mixed reaction as well, but it appeared to be more towards his favor than against. As Universal reaches ringside, he steps into the ring and looks out at the crowd who cheer him in kind. Universal walks to the middle and an exchange of verbal bashing between the two begins. Suddenly, Dead appeared at ringside with a large black bag and got Universal's attention. Universal walked over towards him and bent halfway out to reach for him, but Dead spit some black mist into his face. Universal scampered back towards the middle of the ring where Ice spun him around and took him down with a chokeslam, before bringing him back up two more times and slamming him to the mat again with a harsh impact that would've killed someone much smaller. Ice covered Universal and earned the right to face Eron for the fWo Internet Title on Sunday. Universal reached for his eyes, trying to get the black mist from them as the crowd booed. Dead stood up onto the ring apron and produced a smaller bag. He let a snake's head creep out from the bag and told Ice to leave or suffer the consequences. Ice decided that if he wished to be in good condition for the match this Sunday he might as well leave as it is, and seeing Universal get his ass kicked wouldn't bother him one bit. Ice gave Dead the finger before walking to the back slowly. Dead pulled Universal up and took him down easily with The Crucifixion. He then let the snake slither on Universal' body for awhile before taking the larger bag, which was obviously a body bag, and putting Universal inside of it. Dead zipped it up just a little bit before putting the snake inside the body bag with Universal. He zipped it up fully and then did his Crucifixion pose as he stood over Universal with a look of evil and happiness etched upon his unforgiving face. The crowd booed as the commentators screamed in sheer horror, before several fWoers ran to ringside, such as The Boston Strangler, The Underground and others. Dead left the ring to a massive amount of boos as The Boston Strangler unzipped the bag, pulled the snake from it, and squeezed it around the neck tightly, almost killing it instantly. The Underground helped remove Universal from the bag as EMT's came to the ring to aid the fallen Universal. Dead laughed at the Strangler who looked at him in an angry tone, before Dead "blew" a kiss to Strangler... perhaps the kiss of death. Dead walked to the back as the EMT's helped Universal onto the stretcher, and Universal began to vomit. The damage appeared to have been done, and it seemed to be too late. Result: Ice by Pinfall An American Idiot In London Kid Cool stood outside the window getting rained on. He had locked himself out of the arena and was now wet and cold, two symptoms worse than he was a few minutes ago. It wouldn't be so bad if he wasn't in his wrestling tights, but he had foolishly gotten into them in the hopes that he would have a match. When the booking sheets were posted he was disappointed to find that not only was he not on the card for the night, he also wasn't on the card for the dark matches or even the ring cleanup crew. Not that he liked tearing down the ring, but there were some petty cool chemicals that always gave him neat happy Technicolor dreams. A brit walked up next to him and evaluated the situation. Gesturing with his head at Kid Cool the man asked, "Fag?" Kid Cool gasped, and tried to cover himself. "No. I mean, no! Why does everyone keep asking me that? I can't help the way I look! I'm just young and sensitive, but very masculine and all man I'll have you know!" Kid Cool stomped off. The man who had asked for the cigarette shrugged and wandered away. Kid Cool stomped back and forth in a tight line and grumbled to himself. "I tell you, they wouldn't be doing this if my dad was here." A car drove by and splashed water on him, drenching him from head to toe. Kid Cool started to hop up and down, screaming. An elderly couple gave him little notice as they passed by. "American." Briana (c) vs. Aimee Horihito The women's world champion, Briana, makes her way down the entrance ramp to a polite cheer from the audience. She slides into the ring where her challenger for the night, Aimee Horihoto, already stands awaiting her. Briana undoes and hands off her title to the referee, and upon the bell's ringing, the two begin dancing around the ring looking for an early advantage. Briana makes the first move, lunging forward and snatching Aimee's arm, twisting it into an arm-wrench. She follows this up with three fierce kicks to Aimee's exposed gut. Aimee drops backwards feeling a nauseating pain overcome her as Briana releases the hold, yet the champion is upon Horihoto in a flash once more. She grabs two handfuls of hair and roughly yanks Horihoto to both feet before tossing her, somersaulting into the center of the ring. Briana pounces on top of her before she can regain her defenses and again pitches her head over heels by her hair. She goes for a quick cover, but at the count of two, Aimee delays the inevitable by breaking free from the pin; much to the crowd's dismay. Briana straddles her and begins to smother her with heated alternations of left and right punches. Lifting her to her feet, Briana tosses Aimee into the ropes. She catches her and swiftly plants her to the mat with a DDT, rolling her over and allowing the referee to drop and ring up an accelerated three count, thus ending the match just as quickly as it had begun. Briana rises smiling, receiving her title from the referee and holding it up triumphantly, having retained it once more by toppling Horihoto. Her excitement is short lived though as Apocolyptica hastily makes her way to the ring. As was the case on Warfare one week prior, she holds a steel chair tightly in her grip. Slipping unobtrusively into the ring, she approaches an oblivious Briana from behind. Roughly spinning her, Apocolyptica brings the chair screaming down upon a suddenly frightened Briana. The chair speaks volumes for her, the cruel impact showing her disdain for the woman before her. She is seen disappearing backstage just as quickly as she had emerged, as Briana, unconscious, is helped onto a stretcher by the paramedic staff on hand. Strapping her in, they begin to wheel her from the ring as Warfare takes a break in action and cuts to commercial. Result: Briana by Pinfall A British Idiot In London David Spencer rode the yellow cab toward the airport. He sat alone in the back seat and stared angrily ahead of him. The driver was attempting to ignore him with a large degree of success. David Spencer sniffled. The driver ignored him. David Spencer coughed. The driver ignored him. David Spencer took the back part of his hand and brought it across the back of the driver's head. The taxi was suddenly forced with a decision of going the direction that the road was or turning in the direction of the driver's jerk of the wheel and crashing into the side of the building. The engine was all for following the command of the wheel, but the front end of the car objected strongly to being bent up into a metal accordion. Before they came to blows the driver regained the wheel and continued forward on the road. "Dammit! You nearly got us killed!" Spencer huffed to himself. "You don't want to hear my problems..." The driver yelled, "You're damn right I don't you maniac! Don't ever do that again!" Spencer's eyes narrowed and he grabbed the back of the driver's head. "I said, YOU DON'T WANT TO HEAR MY PROBLEMS. Do you?" The driver gagged as Spencer's fingers dug into his neck. David Spencer nodded to himself. "Fine. You want to know so bad? I'll tell you. Someone burnt down my house. They burnt down Manor Spencer... who would do such a thing?" The driver pulled free from Spencer's grasp and held his now red throat. "Bloody fool... didn't you want the bleeding television shows of the promotion that you're in? The guys who burned down your flat was..." Spencer grabbed his neck again. "Now listen you, are you wanting to listen to my problems or not? You're giving me a lot of mixed signals here." From the back of the cab a thumping came. The driver looked back and hitched a thumb to the trunk. "Are you sure that girl of yours is okay back in there?" Spencer ignored the driver with a wave of his hand. "She's fine, now let me tell you, when I get back to America I'm going to give those bastards in the fWo a piece of my mind let me tell you..." Hellrazor (c) vs. Fallen As Warfare continues, the ever-enigmatic Fallen is announced over the loudspeaker as the night's challenger for the Hardcore title. He emerges and makes his way slowly down towards the ring, with what used to be a chorus of boos now turning into an almost sympathetic applause from the crowd in response to recent events. He arrives at the ring area, collecting a steel chair by the announcer's table and tossing it into the ring before entering. At this point "From This Day" by Machinehead heralds the arrival of the newly re-crowned, three time Hardcore champion of the fWo, Hellrazor. He appears on the entrance ramp, the hardcore strap dangling from his left hand, Lamont the Cat, nestled between his other arm and his chest. He raises the title to a respectful ovation from the crowd. Then smiling, he lowers it and carefully holds up Lamont, the ovation given new life as the crowd's noise escalates to welcome the newest celebrity in the world of professional wrestling. Hellrazor finds his way towards ring-side, and handing off both Lamont and the title, he leaps to the apron. Not about to enter as Fallen readily brandishes a steel chair, Hellrazor turns towards the referee and demands a chair for himself. Forced to oblige, the referee hands Hellrazor a chair. Fallen takes the kamikaze approach, charging Hellrazor as the bell rings. Finding himself in a very precarious position between the ropes, Hellrazor cannot defend himself, and in turn Fallen cracks Hellrazor over the back and back of the head with a barbarous chair shot. Hellrazor falls through the ropes and crashes to the arena floor below, clutching his neck. Fallen climbs through the ropes and drops to Hellrazor's level, lifting the champion before putting him on his back once more with a deafening chair shot that crackles upon making contact with Hellrazor. A slight gash just above Hellrazor's left eye opens just a bit from the force, and slowly begins to release a trickle of blood. Fallen brings Hellrazor to his unsteady feet, and stacking the two chairs before them on top of each other, cradles Hellrazor, and soon has him hanging defenselessly upside down in a Piledriver position. Fallen extends upwards, and then brings Hellrazor hurtling downwards. His heads is swallowed up by the chair, crumpling the hard metal and sending painful shockwaves through his now decrepit body. His body slumps forward. Fallen lifts him and rolls him into the ring. Kicking the chairs aside, he crouches down and pulls a table out from beneath the ring. Balancing it on the top rope and then letting it fall into the ring, he slides under the bottom rope. Fallen props the table up in the center of the ring, and lifts a battered Hellrazor to a standing position. Fallen sends him into the ropes and lifts him on the rebound with a back body drop, sending Hellrazor hurtling downwards and through the table which gives way with a profound splintering of wood and twisting of metal. Hellrazor shows no signs of movement, on his back in a mess of shattered wood; his title reign seemingly in danger of coming to an abrupt end for a third time; this time in shockingly easy fashion. Yet Fallen does not head for a cover. He does not wrap up the match and look for his first taste of fWo gold. Fallen looks down upon a comatose Hellrazor, however does not approach him. The crowd watches, baffled, as Fallen simply exits the ring, walking back up the entrance ramp and leaving a ruined Hellrazor in the ring and a certain win as well. The referee thus begins his ten count, and as Fallen disappears back stage, it becomes apparent that the match will be Hellrazor's. Reaching the count of ten, a stupefied referee awards the match to Hellrazor. He lifts one of Hellrazor's limp arms to declare him the winner. Fallen, showing little trace of emotion, simply walked away from victory and the Hardcore championship belt that came with it. Result: Hellrazor by No Contest Toll Too High Geoff Green ran down the hallway after Fallen, trying to get a word with the superstar. Fallen walked quickly, grabbing a coat as he went. He had to pause as Fallen waited for some maintenance men who were unloading some crates into the room and Geoff caught up with him. "John... John... what gives? You had that match won... what's the deal?" Fallen turned, and Geoff took a step back as he looked into Fallen's eyes. They were dark... and they were devoid of the earlier softness that Geoff had once come to know in the man that he had once called his friend. "John?" Fallen shook his head slowly and placed a hand over Geoff's mouth. "It's Fallen. Now and forever more. Fallen." Geoff took a step backward. "Listen... John... you're into your character and that's great for the show, but you're with me know. Backstage. Just... calm down..." Fallen took a step forward and now looked at Geoff with a quizzical look on his face. "Just a character. Right Geoff. Just a character. Tell me... where were you when I was getting beaten? Where were you? Hellrazor... he wasn't around. That's why I spared him. He bears no responsibility for what happened. But you... you Geoff... you were around the whole time. What did you do?" Fallen was now close to Geoff, and the cameraman could feel his hot breath on his skin. "I... I... I... I'm sorry... I... I didn't know..." Fallen frowned and grabbed Geoff by the neck. "Not good enough... not good enough at all." Fallen tossed Geoff into the wall, and the cameraman's nose broke in a spray of blood as he collapsed on the floor. Fallen looked at his once friend broken and bleeding on the floor with a lack of emotion. "Not good enough. fWo... you're all guilty." Quicksilver tried to follow up with a clothesline, but Douglas held onto the ropes and stuck his boot out. Result: Matt Douglas by Pinfall Maine's Finest The police car rolled smoothly up to the unassuming suburban house. This was a quiet street in Maine, and even though it was January the plants were starting to show some signs of life. There had been snow this winter as there was every winter, but it seemed to be coming to a slow end now. Old man winter had gone to rest for another year leaving the century to finally thaw and begin in earnest. The two police officers looked at the house and then at each other. After some silence, the younger of the two spoke. "I hear he was a wrestling promoter." The driver was an older policeman, and his time as a Maine policeman had been long. He had seen plenty during his forty two years, and this summer he would likely retire and join the old crocks down at the benches by the barrens. Just as well, the country was getting strange on him. He still remembered years ago when the man he had trained Frank Dodd had gone crazy and killed all those poor women and the sight of that smiling man with his blond hair and easy smile always made him shiver a bit. Then there had been the floods in '82 which had just about done his fair little town of Derry in, and that wasn't even mentioning some of the things that floated out of the drains and pipes with the normal wreckage one would expect to find down below. Yes, things had gone all strange in the world and he could do with some rest. The younger policeman looked edgy. This was to be expected, he was new to the job and most young pups jump around and practically piss themselves with excitement at the slightest sign of action. He grinned, and the young man must have mistaken his grin for a questioning glance because he repeated his question. "I hear he was a wrestling promoter." The old man nodded. "Ayup." They got out of the car and headed up the front walk. The young policeman rang the doorbell and the young woman who answered looked young enough to be still in her twenties. Wrestling folk... all alike and always robbing the cradle. "Yes... can I help you?" They took off their hats, and by the expression on her face she knew it was going to be bad news from that gesture alone. "M'am... I'm sorry we had to be the ones to tell you this... but... well... you see..." The young pup was botching it and the older cop put an arm on his shoulder. He backed down, and the older policeman addressed the woman with all of the tact and manner of an elderly Maine gentleman. "Your husband is dead miss. Drowned." Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Scott Collins Warfare heads back to the ring for the evening's Main Event, and the crowd is pumped. "Burning" begins to play, as the large 4R appears on the WarfareTron. Scott Collins, minus his Four Riders crew, makes his way out, looking regain the World Title for a third time. As he rolls into the ring, the lights suddenly dim and go out. Nobody can see anything, and nothing can be heard but a ticking, like a clock or a stopwatch. This continues for a moment, before the lights come back on, and Collins gives an audible "What the hell?" in the ring. Though somewhat confused, he begins to stretch with the aid of the ropes before "Juke-Joint Jezebel" starts playing and out walks the fWo World Champion the Flying Frenchie, minus Primetime and Courage. Frenchie poses arrogantly for the fans, who give a mixed but loud reaction. He vaults into the ring and hands the title to the referee, who holds it high in the air signaling that it will be on the line. Frenchie and Collins step face-to-face, chest-to-chest, and begin engaging in a war of words. Though both the 6'4" competitors stand nose-to-nose, Collins' muscular bulk dwarfs the smaller Frenchie. The exchange of words continues for a minute, before we catch a quick view of Frenchie mouthing one word- "Candyman". Before Frenchie can react, Collins is upon him, and rains down a flurry of punches. Collins backs Frenchie into the ropes, and hammers him with a vicious lariat coming off, sending Frenchie for a 360. Collins quickly covers, but only gets two. He brings Frenchie up, and wraps him up in a head-and-arm lock. He then pulls Frenchie up and over with a suplex, nearly dropping Frenchie on his head. Frenchie staggers to his feet, and Collins blasts him with an Exploder suplex as he gets up. After a moment, Frenchie again drunkenly staggers up, and Collins hits a go-behind into a waistlock, before lifting Frenchie up and slamming him on the back of his neck with a stiff german suplex. Collins holds on, but Frenchie kicks out at two. Collins gets up and waits for Frenchie, before leaping up and nailing him square in the face with both feet in a rare dropkick from the muscle-bound Collins. Frenchie is knocked into the corner, and Collins takes advantage by charging in and driving a knee into the stomach of Frenchie. Collins backs up to try it again, but Frenchie moves and Collins strikes the second turnbuckle full-on. Frenchie quickly dropkicks the big man into the corner, hitting him with such force that Collins' face is launched into the metal post. Collins staggers out, and Frenchie leaps onto his back and rolls forward into a victory roll. The ref counts, but Collins kicks out at the count of two. They both get up quickly, but Frenchie strikes first, kicking Collins in the stomach and dropping him with a rocker dropper. He brings Collins up, and sends him into the ropes, before catching him coming off with a leaping leg lariat. Frenchie brings Collins up again, and begins chopping away at him. Collins is backed up into the ropes, and Frenchie charges at him with a clothesline, taking Collins up and over. Frenchie stands near the ropes, waiting for Collins to get up. As he does, Frenchie springboards onto the top rope, before diving off with a tope suicida that hits its mark. Frenchie drags Collins up and whips him hard into the steel ring steps. Collins goes flying head over heels over them, landing hard on his back. Frenchie, taking his time, walks over to Collins and rolls him back in the ring. Frenchie rolls back in as well, and arrogantly covers Collins. Collins kicks out at one. Frenchie brings Collins up, and whips him into the ropes. However, gathering a sudden burst of energy, Collins reverses it. As Frenchie comes off, he leaps at Collins, going for a hurricanrana. Collins, getting his second wind, begins to reverse it into a powerbomb, but Frenchie cleverly reaches down and pokes Collins in the eye, before taking him over with the rana. He rolls it through into a pin, and nearly gets a three count. Undaunted, Frenchie gets up and begins preparing a superkick. Collins slowly gets up, and when he reaches his feet, Frenchie lets fly with the superkick. However, Collins ducks. As Frenchie recovers his bearings and turns to face Collins, Collins attempts a superkick of his own. However, Frenchie ducks. Collins regains his bearings quickly and both Collins and Frenchie lash out with simultaneous superkicks, each blasting the other full-on on the chin. Both men go down, and the referee begins to count to ten. He reaches six before Collins begins to pull himself up, followed almost immediately by Frenchie. Collins is up first, and as Frenchie reaches his feet, Collins kicks him hard in the stomach. Frenchie doubles over, and Collins hooks him up and spikes him with a stiff brainbuster. Collins quickly rolls him over and applies the Scotty-la-magistral-pinfall-maneuver. The ref counts one...two...and Frenchie kicks out just before Collins becomes a three-time champion. Collins brings Frenchie up, and signals that it's time for the Death Penalty. Collins whips Frenchie into the ropes, and tosses him into the air in the usual setup over the past few weeks for the Death Penalty. However, Frenchie gains control of himself in mid-air, and blasts Collins in the air with a dropkick. Collins stumbles back into the ropes, and bounces off them towards Frenchie, who kicks him in the stomach, doubling Collins over, before lifting Collins up in a variation of the Primetime Bomb. He then repeats the powerbomb, before lifting the larger man into a death valley driver in an incredible display of power. Frenchie quickly leaps up onto the top rope facing the crowd, hops to face Collins, and comes off with a Guillotine Legdrop. Collins cannot roll away, and Frenchie hooks the leg. The ref counts to three, and Frenchie has made his first ever successful defense of the World Title. Frenchie begins to celebrate in the ring, and Courage and Primetime make their way out from the back to join him. As they celebrate in the ring, the lights suddenly go out and the opening guitar strains of "Humans Being" echo throughout the arena. The fans boo as the lights go back on and Perfect, Hell, Heaven, and Living appear in the ring, Perfect wielding a baseball bat, Hell a small blowtorch, and Heaven and Living a pair of containers of gasoline. Perfect hammers the Frenchie in the head with the bat, sending him flying over the top rope to the outside, and the combined forces of the Hell, Heaven, and Living drive Primetime and Courage from the ring as well. Instead of charging back in, they elect to tend to their fallen leader on the outside. Meanwhile, with Collins still down in the ring, Heaven and Living begin to pour the gasoline on the ring ropes, finally covering the entire ring. The Four Riders begin to sprint towards the ring to save their leader, but are deterred when Hell follows Heaven and Living, using the blowtorch to set the ropes of the ring on fire. Perfect stands laughing over Collins, and begins to bring him up. He does so, and reaches into his pockets, pulling out what appears to be a large piece of flash paper and a cigarette lighter. Perfect then whips Collins into the ropes, singeing his back in the process, and ignites the flash paper as Collins bounces back towards him, sending a towering fireball into the face of the leader of the Four Riders. Collins falls back, clutching at his face, and Stone suddenly runs to the back as Perfect grabs a microphone from his other pocket. "Hey, Collins- I told you I'd bring the heat, didn't I? Hey, I know you've always been a big fan of M&Ms, right? And you know their slogan, right? 'Melts in your mouth, not in your hands'? Well, I think you've got a similar slogan yourself. Melts in the middle of the ring!" Perfect begins to laugh at Collins again, but he stops laughing when he sees Stone return, carrying a pair of fire extinguishers. She tosses one to Brian Cage, and they both begin to hose down the ring, extinguishing the flames. Stone, Cage, and John Malcon rush the ring, and before the greater numbers of FTfWo can gain the advantage, spray them in the eyes with the extinguishers before using them as blunt objects to batter Perfect and company. From the outside, Primetime and Courage suddenly roll back in, followed by the Frenchie, looking for revenge on Perfect for the unprovoked attack on their leader. In the process, a few errant blows from Foundation members hit the Riders, and suddenly it becomes a three-way brawl between FTfWo, the Four Riders, and the French Foundation. In the chaos of the brawl, Perfect takes the opportunity to head for higher ground, rolling from the ring and backing away slowly. His momentum is suddenly halted as Ice levels him from behind with a huge side kick to the side of the head. Perfect collapses in a heap, and Ice begins to rain punches down on him. Back in the ring, all nine standing wrestlers are still brawling, with Stone paying particular attention to Frenchie, shouting something about cake. These two brawls continue unabated as Warfare goes off the air... Result: Flying Frenchie by Pinfall
