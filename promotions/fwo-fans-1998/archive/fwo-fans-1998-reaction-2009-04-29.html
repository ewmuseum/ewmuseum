Dangling above it all, a briefcase containing a contract for a shot at the Cruiserweight Title. However, it was not just any title shot. It was a shot where and when the winner chose. It was a very deadly thing indeed. Six warriors would engage in an exciting game of human chess to decide who would walk away with that prized contract. Six fighters entered, but only one would leave. The time for battle had come and the crowd was ready for what shall be known forever more as the Silver Pyramid. "Revoultion Baby" by Queen V ushered in the men best suited for this match. He was the marathon man, Impulse. He briefly paused atop the ramp, making sure to take in all the cheers from the capacity crowd, which had grown since last week. After slapping every hand he could reach on his way to the ring, the young man slid into the ring under the bottom rope and popped up to his feet, looking over the other competitors closely. Knowing this is no time to lose focus; he slipped into the nearest empty corner and started to bounce up and down. Perhaps, trying to work off some prematch jitters. With his head held high, Xander Scott burst through the curtain and looked over the crowd, as "YYZ" by Rush accompanied the growing cheers from the crowd. He bounced around at the top of the stage before taking off towards the ring. He had some extra spring in his step, feeling that he could really steal the show in a match like this. He paused midway up the steel steps to point out an Xander Scott is X-Tra Sexy sign being held by a blonde in the third row. He chuckled at it and slipped into the ring, raising his arms to the cheers of the crowd. He nodded to Impulse, getting a nod in return, and slipped into the nearest corner. Next up, to a swarm of boos from the crowd, was "Fireworks" by Peter Glam. This meant that one Jade Argent was arriving and within seconds, he did just that. Paying no mind to the unhappy people in attendance, the effeminate warrior slithered into the ring and looked to his foes in disgust before turning his attention to the crowd, who were not pleased to see him in the least. He couldn't care less about what they thought, as he leaned against the ropes and ran his fingers through his blonde hair. The musical styling of Peter Glam are soon replaced by those of Fair to Midland and their song, "A Seafarers Knot". With clipboard in hand, the spectacled Mary-Lynn Mayweather made her way down to the ring. She scampered up the steel steps and slipped into the ring. She raised a hand getting a great pop from the crowd, before glaring at Argent, whom she was still rather enraged by. Her attention along with everyone else's was about to be brought to the entrance ramp again though. "Engel" by Rammstein brought Brittany Chambers next to the ring. Unlike last week in her solo FWO debut, there was very little of the posturing to the crowd. She was walking to the ring with a purpose. She was here to win, to reach out and grab the open-ended contract for an FWO Cruiserweight Title shot that hung high above the ring. She made her way down the aisle, slapped a few hands on the way but never taking her eyes off the ring. Brittany climbed up onto the ring apron and shook the structure, then climbed up on the turnbuckle and climbed a few rungs up the ladder to check the sturdiness. Satisfied, she turned towards the crowd and raised her hands, showcasing her balance on the slanted ladders. Brittany swung down off the ladder and slid out of the ring to collect her thoughts before the match began, making sure to keep an eye on her foes. She couldn't watch them for long, as the lights went out without warning. Darkness always makes the crowd come alive and the sounds of "Nobody's Listening" by Linkin Park blared through the arena. The lights burst back on and in the middle of the ring; hanging from the center of the four ladders was Xin Xin Xiong. He safely dropped to the mat and stood dead center in the middle of the ring. He tears off his black "I'm the ninja your parents warned you about" t-shirt and launched it into the crowd before slowly backing to an open area while in his fighting stance, not trusting any of his fellow competitors. With all six fighters in the ring and checked for any foreign objects, like it matters in this no holds barred match, the referee called for the bell. Each competitor looked around the ring at their foes with X3, Xander Scott, and Jade Argent being the first ones to step out into the middle. The remaining three looked on, as the trio carefully locked fingers in a test of strength. Each man tightened their grip as hard as they could, trying to make their two rivals buckle. None of the three really gained an out and out advantage over the others, but their focus on one another led to the three watching on to take advantage and dart for the ladders making up the pyramid. Noticing this, the three men quickly ended their battle and darted to the corners. Each man pulled down a foe and the brawl began. In no time at all, four of the six fathers were slugging it out on the floor. Outside of the ring, Chambers had paired off with Argent and Xander had decided to battle with Mayweather, leaving Xiong and Impulse in the ring. Impulse countered an Irish whip and X3 hit the ropes. Xiong tried for his trademark rolling clothesline, also known as the Rolling Crystal Flash, but was caught right as he popped up with a picture perfect dropkick with the sound of Xan whipping Mayweather into the barricade in the background. Not letting this advantage go to waste, he whipped Xiong into the corner. Impulse took a deep breath and about 3 steps into his charge, Xander Scott appears out of nowhere with a dropkick of his own, catching Impulse in the knee. Xan smacked his foe in the side of the head and bounced off the ropes, as X3 used this opening to slip to the outside. Impulse tried for a hiptoss, but it was counter mid move by Xander with a monkey flip. However, that didn't have much luck either, as Impulse landed on his feet close to the ropes. He went to charge Xan, but he was tripped by Mayweather before he could even take a step, making him fall flat on his face. Xan smirked before hitting the opposite ropes and not hitting the fallen Impulse with a dropkick, but instead, clearing him to catch Mayweather with one. Showing his inexperience, Xan made sure to pose a little bit between the top and middle ropes, getting a small pop from the crowd, as Impulse exited the ring and Mayweather clutched at her face while sitting against the barrier. Unfortunately, for Xander Scott, this left him wide open for Jade Argent to smack him in the back of the head with a running forearm, sending him tumbling to the floor. Argent had no time to rest, as he raced to knock X3 off the apron before turning into a boot to the gut from Brittany Chambers. Her following punch was blocked and she got her head rocked by one from Argent. In a flash, she is whipped into the ropes and sent flying across the ring by a belly to belly suplex from Argent, which didn't exactly make the crowd happy. Feeling things are going his way; Jade Argent picked up his hurting opponent and whips her crashing into the corner. Much like Impulse before him, Argent charged and didn't have much luck. A pair of boots stuck him square in the mouth and he stumbled out to the middle of the ring. After checking his mouth for blood, Argent looked back to Chambers. He didn't find her. What he did find was X3 springboarding off the top rope and his head being driven into the mat with a tornado DDT, exciting the crowd. As soon as he was back on his feet, Xiong was struck in the back of the head by Chambers with a jumping calf kick. Capitalizing on the sneak attack, Chambers spun X3 around and tried for a DDT. The move was countered by X3 with a hammerlock and instantly, he shrugged her away, as his moral code refuses to let him touch a female in hopes of harming them. He charges her and she drops to her belly on the mat. Just as his feet hit the mat, after hopping over Chambers, snagged by Jade Argent and planted with a t-bone suplex, much to the crowd's dismay. Chambers raced over and tried to double team the young ninja with Jade, but soon found herself being attacked by him as well. Argent hauled off and belted both Chambers and X3 with right hands. Seeing an opening, Jade ricocheted off the ropes and looked for a double clothesline, but got dropped along the middle rope by a double drop toe hold. X3 and Chambers looked to one another and simultaneously hit the far ropes. Still in unison, they sent Jade flying back with a double tiger spin kick or a double 619 for the American fans. Xiong pumped his fist, getting hyped up along with the crowd, but was stopped by a kick to the gut from Brittany with both on the apron. She swung wildly, hoping to strike X3 in the jaw with a punch, only to hit air and fall over the top rope. X3 had pulled down the rope, while falling down to avoid the strike. Brittany hurried back to her feet in the ring and looked to try again with her masked opponent, only to have Xander Scott dropkick her in the back. Chambers tumbled between the middle and top rope, catching Xiong in the gut and knocking both to the floor. Mayweather appeared out of nowhere and after a running knee to the bread basket, hooked Xan in a three-quarter nelson. A couple of elbows found their mark and Xan led her back to the ropes and shot her towards the far ones. Impulse quickly slipped into the ring, leapfrogged the incoming Mayweather, and tried to take off her head with a jumping back elbow. His moment of triumph was cut short. Once he was standing, he looked to Xan and was planted with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. The crowd cringed with that one, as Impulse looked to bend in half the wrong way. Exploding back into the picture, Mayweather placed a boot to the gut of Xander and snapmared him over. Keeping her focus on him, she dives over him with a hand full of hair, hitting the rolling neck snap. Just as Mayweather got to one knee, she watched Impulse come crashing down on Xan with a front flip senton. The Marathon Man rolled up to his feet after the attack and had to quickly reverse an Irish whip by Mayweather, sending her into the corner. Impulse charged in and struck his rival with a knee to the jaw. Mayweather was just a step out of the corner when Impulse looked for his superkick. He aimed for her head, but stopped short of connecting. In front of the woman's head was a chair being held in front of her face by Xiong. "What are you doing?" He quizzed the ninja lurking on the apron. "I don't need a weapon or your help." "Okay," X3 shrugged and tossed Impulse the chair. The chair was snagged to stop it from hitting Impulse in the face and ironically, turned into a superkick from Jade Argent that slammed the chair into the young man's face. BOOM! Through the ropes fell Impulse and Jade turned his attention to X3, knocking him to his ass on the apron with a right hand. Brittany was able to gain advantage with a sneak attack and began to brutally redden the chest or Argent with knife edge chops that made the crowd shudder. With that happening in the ring, Xan and Mayweather were slugging it out on the floor. They were soon accompanied by Impulse and none of the three were gaining the advantage. The trio locked horns with Argent and Chambers looming over top of them in the ring. Argent doubled Chambers over with a kick to the gut and positioned her head between his legs, going for what looks to be a powerbomb or piledriver. Xiong exploded off the opposite ropes of the battling duo, stepped up on Chambers' back, and leapt off Argent's shoulder. Xander, Impulse, and Mayweather never saw him coming, as he came crashing down on them with a front flip plancha. Always making sure to make contact with the males only, the collision caused by the plancha sent Impulse crashing into Mayweather. The crowd raced to the security barrier, electrified by what they saw. Argent ignored the distraction long enough to hoist Brittany up on his shoulder for a Vader-style powerbomb. However, she used the momentum to slip out of his grip. Her feet landed on the top rope and instantly, she took to the skies with a corkscrew plancha, knocking down the three of the four fighters on the floor. With the crowd watching the graceful female fly through the air, Impulse rolled to the side, avoiding contact, but four victims were more than enough for Argent to try his hand. After hitting the far ropes, Jade looked to dive through the middle and top rope with a suicide dive and got a boot to the head for it. Impulse was having none of it and caught him just as his head slipped through the ropes. OHHH! The shot turned Argent's lights out quickly, leaving the four on the floor to fight amongst themselves. Seeing this as an opening, Impulse ascended the corner and started to climb one of the silver ladders. The four people on the floor raced back into the ring after him. Xander saw the other three slowing Impulse and took off to the opposite corner, where he made his way up to the silver ladder. Impulse was able to escape his pursuers and make it near the top of the pyramid. Ever the risk taker, Xan ran his side of the ladder like a staircase and speared Impulse off of the ladder. CRASH! Both men came down hard from the ladder and were quickly replaced above the ring by Xiong, Chambers, and Mayweather, as the crowd roared for what they just saw. Stuck between the proverbial rock and a hard place, X3 used all of his martial arts training and amazing balance to defend himself against the pair of women without falling off the ladder. With no signs of hope in this situation, Xiong dove over the top of the ladders, causing the two women to miss their attempted punch at him and strike each other. As X3 held on firmly to the ladder, trying not to fall, Argent dashed up the ladder much like Xan before him and knocked both ladies off with a double clothesline. That didn't exactly please the crowd, as they disliked the idea of Argent possibly winning. Not thinking that Xiong would still be clinging to the other side of the structure, Argent reached up for the title. His focus on the prize was his downfall, as X3 skinned the cat and locked him in a headscissors. Xiong quickly twisted to the side and let go of the ladder, sending Argent down to the mat harshly with a headscissors, boosting the crowd's morale. CRASH! Xander had propped himself in the corner and looked over his foes, all of which were lying on the mat. Seeing the opportunity, he ascended the corner and started to make his way up the ladder. Being the only other person not on the receiving end of the ladder mayhem, Xiong started towards Xan's side of the ring. Unlike Xander, Xiong started to climb the underside of the ladder. Xan was about to reach up for the title, when X3 slipped his hand through the ladder and tugged his foot forward by the ankle. Never seeing it coming, Xan crotched himself on a rung of the ladder. OHHH! That made every guy in the arena cringe in horror and Xander fell back on the ladder with his foot hooked in the ladder, leaving him dangling there. Making sure not to get struck inadvertently in the face by Xanders boot when he fell, X3 was forced to let go and dangle upside down from the ladder. With no place to go, X3 was wide open for a recovered Impulse to bounce off the middle rope and deliver a forearm to his jaw. The man in the Kato mask could do nothing, as Mayweather followed up Impulse's strike by launching herself off the middle rope as well and forcibly removing X3 from the ladder with a neckbreaker. BOOM! Impulse immediately pounced on Mayweather and whipped her towards the corner. Saving herself from the pain of hitting the corner, she ran up the turnbuckle and started to climb the ladder from underneath. The Marathon Man took off after her and was able to grip her waist from the second rope, as she clung to the ladder. Impulse tugged with all of his might, but she refused to let go of the ladder. Seeing this, Chambers hopped up onto the second rope next to Impulse and took him crashing to the mat with a side Russian leg sweep with Impulse pulling Mayweather down with them with a belly to back suplex. The ring was littered once again with bodies and the cheers from the capacity crowd showed that they loved it. Jade Argent ascended the corner and looked to the ladder where Xander was laying. A barrage of right hands rained down on the young Xan and his head was bounced off the ladder repeatedly. Not caring that Xan was in his path up the ladder, Argent started to climb. He was about to pass the young man when Xan picked his ankle and did his best to scissor him with his free leg, clamping on an ankle lock. Argent struggled with all of his might, trying to break the hold without falling off the ladder. Seeing no other option, he wound up and elbowed his attacker between the legs, instantly breaking the hold. After shaking his leg in hopes of eliminating the pain that still lingered, Jade took a couple steps down the ladder and sat Xan up. A couple of forearms to the back of the head led to Argent pulling Xan free of the ladder. Before Xander could even think about doing something with his newfound freedom, Argent had him by the waist. Jade popped his hips and took his foe from the ladder down to the mat with a German suplex, with a slight twist so both men didn't plummet to the floor. CRASH! Neither man landed well off of that and lay lifeless on the mat, but the crowd ate it up despite it being one Jade Argent executing the move. Slowly climbing to his feet, Impulse was shaking off the effects of his fall and found a recovering X3 waiting for him. The ninja struck first with a kick to the gut and clamped on a front chancery like he may be setting up for a DDT. A dazed Chambers rose up behind Impulse and X3 was quick to stop her from interfering. The ninja gave a short quick kick to Impulse's leg, making it shoot backwards into Brittany's gut. As Brittany crumpled to the mat, Impulse countered the hold into a hammerlock. Xiong swung wildly with a back elbow, missing, but he snagged an incoming kick from the Marathon Man. X3 violently forced the captured leg upward, making Impulse do a back flip right into Mayweather who had just gotten to her feet. Impulse looked up after his collision and was struck in the chest with a thunderous kick. OHHH! A second one found its mark as well. OHHH! Xiong growled, as he took a fighting stance and tried to take Impulse's head off with a kick. Luckily, the young man ducked. Impulse hopped up to his feet and fed his leg to his foe, so he could crack him in the head with an enziguiri. X3 was knocked for a loop and he fell face first to the mat. Living up to his moniker, Impulse is a little dazed, but not very winded, as he makes his way to the ladder. Before too long, Xander Scott, Jade Argent, Brittany Chambers, and Mary Lynn Mayweather were back in the ring and each started up a ladder. Impulse had just reached the top, as he noticed foes coming in all directions with Chambers climbing up from behind him. At the top of the pyramid they all met. Impulse turned his attention to Chambers, while Xan and Mayweather focused their attack on the lone Argent. The five warriors battled with their remaining energy. Mayweather and Xander went back and forth socking Argent with right hands, but he just wouldn't fall. While on the other side, Impulse held on for dear life, as Chambers tries to pull him from the ladder. With this happening high above the ring, Xiong had slipped back into the ring with a ladder of his own. Impulse placed both of his feet against Brittany's chest and with all of his might shot her off the ladder all the way out to the floor. CRASH! Mayweather, remembering how Jade had spit upon her, took the first opportunity she was handed and took him from the ladder down to the mat with a huge diamond cutter. KABOOM! Much like the crowd, Xan lost focus for just a second, impressed by what Mayweather did to Argent, and it was all that was needed for X3 to slam a ladder into his rib cage. Xiong set his ladder on the pyramid, creating a fifth way to the title, directly from the mat to the top. The placement was right next to Xan, but somewhat opposite Impulse. Xiong climbed the ladder as fast as he could and began striking Xan with punch after punch. Never one to join in, Impulse tried to interrupt X3's assault and got a face full of green mist for his troubles. The crowd wasn't sure what to make of the tactic from the ninja, especially with him using it on the virtuous Impulse, but who doesn't pop when the mist is spewed? Barely hanging on with one hand, Xander was in trouble. X3 slipped over to a ladder to his left and kicked the propped up ladder as hard as he could at his unstable foe. The ladder slammed into Xan's hand, sandwiching it between the ladder he was hold and kicked ladder. With no way to stabilize himself, Xander fell from the ladder and came crashing down along the top rope. BAM! The pyramid had come down to two, X3 and Impulse. Two men, both viewed as heroic and worthy of backing from the crowd. The ninja was first to grab the suitcase, but he couldn't take it for himself. Impulse had grabbed the opposite side and the two men engaged in a tug of war atop the structure. The crowd rose to its feet, watching closely, wondering what could happen next. Each man tugged with all he had and neither could get the upper hand. Impulse released the briefcase and lit up his rival's chest with a big knife edge and the ninja wouldn't let go of the metal briefcase. WHOO! Again and again he chopped his foe with each chop hyping him up more and more. WHOO! WHOO! WHOO! WHOO! He could feel the number one contender shot in his grasp, as Xiong was starting to lose his balance and grip on the prize. Then, in an unexpected move, X3 fires the suitcase at his opponent, catching Impulse square in the mouth. BANG!!! Impulse's lights were dimmed and he fell to the mat in a heap, leaving the man in the Kato mask all alone atop the pyramid. Mayweather was the only one who had a chance of stopping him, but it was already too late. X3 unhooked the briefcase and it was all over. With the briefcase tightly in his grip, Xiong raised it high into the air and pounded his chest with his fist. His first step to the top was now completed. It was just a small hill before his trek up the mountain. Result: Xin Xin Xiong by obtaining briefcase.
