Countdown '99: 1, 31 December 1999

31 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "When the day is long and the night, the night is yours alone, when you're sure you've had enough of this life, well hang on. Don't let yourself go, everybody cries and everybody hurts sometimes." -REM "Well I jumped into the river Too many times to make it home I'm out here on my own, an drifting all alone If it doesn't show give it time To read between the lines 'Cause I see the storm getting closer And the waves they get so high Seems everything We've ever known's here Why must it drift away and die" -Guns 'N Roses History is a funny thing. It means different things to different people and often it is as fluid as rain, written and rewritten on the whims of mankind. Facts, or things once believed to be solid and constant change over time, and the very meaning of the facts can change until they no longer resemble their origins. Often the facts themselves are forgotten in the heat of the moment, and once thought essential details are lost forever in the excitement of the moment. This was certainly the case for one wrestler in the fWo, a man who was humble in his beginning, and wore a mask not to hide his identity but to define it. Some might consider it fitting that on the great eve of the passing of the century the man who won the honor of being called the wrestler of the millennium would find himself at the center of the future of the promotion itself. One member of the fWo staff had uncovered a fact that had been forgotten... a fact that played into the victory of the Black Panther in the tournament of the ages, and that would provide the fWo with their one hope of salvation. Moving forward As the fWo came to Washington, DC it came with a heavy heart. While the fWo medical staff were highly trained to handle any emergency, by the time they pushed Fallen aside and tended to Joy he had already passed from this mortal coil. Ryan Moore was not rushed to the Chicago Medical center, for it was too late to save his life. He had fallen from the top of the entrance ramp after William Bailey, also known as Demon, pushed him in what was a tragic accident of judgment. The end of the show was chaotic, as David Spencer left the area with Dina Sol and William Bailey sobbed to himself and rushed to check on the man he inadvertently killed. Soon afterward William Bailey was escorted from the arena by police, and while few directly blamed him for his actions, all knew that he would never return to the squared circle again. The woman who had grown close to the wrestler Joy over the past few months, Christina Iom... also known as Glee, rode with the medical officials to the hospital where Joy's body was taken. She too knew that her time as a wrestler or a manager was over. The other player in the drama that had unfolded was John Skyler, better known to fans of the fWo as Fallen, one of the solders in David Spencer's Dark Riders. John Skyler held Ryan Moore's hand as he breathed his last breath, and whatever words Ryan said to John as he passed from life he kept with him. John watched David Spencer leave the area shortly after the accident with his wife, and after shedding a single tear he stood, and vanished silently into the crowd. The backstage area was shaken, and several workers looked for answers out of anger and confusion. Several searched for David Spencer to exact some form of revenge upon him, but with no success. Several looked for signs of the commissioner and owner of the fWo, but Travis Beaven was nowhere to be found, and several suspected that he would not be seen ever again. After leaving the announcing table, Chris Finn arrived backstage and attempted to calm the wrestlers around him, but the wound was deep, and all knew that the next Millennium had been forever cast with a dark shroud. Ten bell salute Fans inside the arena came silently to their feet as the house lights rose, and the fWo roster that was in attendance slowly walked down the metal entrance ramp, heads lowered. This was a smaller than usual fWo contingent of workers, for those that were not scheduled to be a part of the card in some fashion were sent home to be with their families for the turn of the millennium. Among the small crowd that assembled on the entrance ramp stood Aimee Horihito, the Black Panther, Black Quicksilver, Boston Strangler, Briana, Cactus Jon, Chris Universal, Courage, Eron, Fallen, Fear, Havok, Hellrazor, Ice, Jackhammer, Kid Cool, Machina, the Mad Trucker, Micky Maliboo, Mike Bear, Mike Heftel, Primetime, Rain, Rudo, Scott Slugger, Sid Snow, Ultra Violet, the US Marshal, Violet, Vixon, VL and Wretch. Many faces were turned down, several were haggard, and few eyes were dry. In the crowd, hats were removed. The first bell rang, and several wrestlers stiffened at the sound. Nine more times the bell rang, and when the last bell sounded the echo chilled the wrestlers deep within themselves. The lights dimmed once more, and the collection of wrestlers walked silently up the ramp and returned to the backstage area. The final show of the millennium had begun. Black Quicksilver vs. Silver Fox Silver Fox came out onto the ramp, and next to him walked Nikki. The December/March pair walked slowly to the ring, and Silver Fox attempted to get the crowd worked up and into the show, a difficult task after the previous moment. Silver Fox wore a black band around one arm, and as he swung into the ring he stood on the turnbuckle and shouted out to the crowd. As he pointed and shouted Black Quicksilver came out onto the entrance ramp, and slowly made his way down to the ring. Black Quicksilver swung over the top rope and approached Silver Fox, and despite their intense rivalry a handshake took place between the two men. Then all formalities were at an end and they locked up, both men struggling for position. Silver Fox twisted the arm of Black Quicksilver into an armbar, and moved toward the ropes. As the referee checked on Black Quicksilver, Silver Fox grabbed the ropes and used them to apply more pressure to the arm. Black Quicksilver wisely reversed his direction of effort and shoved backward, and Silver Fox tumbled over the top rope. Black Quicksilver ran to the other side of the ring, ran up the turnbuckle, and then ran halfway across the top rope before flying off it and landing upon Silver Fox on the outside. Silver Fox now staggered under this fresh assault, and Black Quicksilver waved one arm in the air to signal a facebuster, and then slammed him downward into the mats that lined the arena floor. Black Quicksilver rolled inside and outside of the ring to break the referee's count, and went back toward Silver Fox. Silver Fox was waiting for him however, and thrust a fist upward into the groin of the masked wrestler, at once stopping his attack and sending him backward into the side of the ring. Silver Fox now moved slowly, still in pain over the earlier attack, and rolled Black Quicksilver back into the ring. Silver Fox paced the ring, occasionally getting in a kick to the head of the masked wrestler. Silver Fox picked Black Quicksilver up, and executed his finisher, an inverted backbreaker slam. The crowd roared as Silver Fox waved to the crowd, and then made a lax cover. Thinking it was over, Silver Fox was surprised when Black Quicksilver kicked free at two and a half. Silver Fox attempted to punch the head on Black Quicksilver, but the masked man blocked it and struck back with hard fists, and then slammed Silver Fox down to the mat. Climbing to the top rope, Black Quicksilver held the pose for a moment, and then dove off with a diving headbutt onto the prone Silver Fox. A cover, and three counts later Black Quicksilver rolled from the ring the victor. As he marched back up the entrance ramp, cameras got word that David Spencer had arrived at the arena... Result: Black Quicksilver by Pinfall No regrets David Spencer entered the cold arena and looked around himself with an expression that was impossible to completely read. He walked with Poison at his side, and if he was concerned about the lack of people that he encountered it did not show on his face. He was determined in his purpose, and walked directly toward the ring entrance, a can of gasoline in one hand and the fWo World Heavyweight Championship in the other. He was at the curtain when a single hand fell on his shoulder, and David Spencer was spun around. David Spencer looked into the eyes of John Skyler, and the moment played out between them with no words spoken. Finally Spencer spoke, but his eyes did not drop from the face of Skyler as he did so. "What happened yesterday was... a shame. But it was not my fault... and it will not deter my mission." Skyler's eyes did not drop at this, but his hands seemed to clench into fists. "I warned you. I told you that you were pushing Will too far... now Ryan is dead... and William is in jail. He won't serve time for what was his accident... but he will never forgive himself." Spencer now looked away, and John was shocked to discover a smirk had appeared on Spencer's face. "I don't give a damn about him... if he can't handle the pressures of what it means to be a missionary of mine then away from everything is where Demon should..." But John broke in then, grabbing Spencer and slamming him against a wall. "William! His name is William you miserable fuck... and god dammit... what he did was an accident... but the way you pushed all of us... and pushed him... you bear more responsibility than anyone! God dammit... a man is dead! Now you drop this... all of this... right now! It's over! Let it be..." David Spencer brushed John Skyler off him, and now an expression of hate was worn on his face. "It's not over Fallen... it will never be over. Sometimes pieces on a chess board are taken... but the game goes on. You dare to strike me? You, who are my servant more than any of them? Poison... teach your husband what it means to be a Dark Rider." John looked away from Spencer just in time to see Poison leap at him, tearing and screaming at her husband. Laughing, Death walked on, parting the curtains and walking down the entrance ramp to finish what he had begun. Burn The crowd fell silent when Death walked to the ring. The usual boos did not at once come, for what they had witnessed the night before changed everything in the eyes of the fans. Death walked up the steel steps in complete silence, and it was not until he was in the center of the ring and had raised the dark red championship into the air that any noise came from the crowd. As Death held the World title into the air the fans began to boo him, angry that a man such as he represented the top of the fWo. Now Death lay the title down on the ground, and he picked up the gasoline and began to pour, letting the noxious fluid saturate the leather strap. The crowd booed louder, and security had to restrain angry fans who surged forward in an attempt to stop the ritual that Death meant to carry out. Death now screamed, "Tonight... after so long... the fWo dies!" He pulled from a jacket a match, and once lit the small flicker of flame burned in the eyes of thousands of fans. He began to knell, and then a voice cut through the boos of the crowd and gave Death pause as he looked up to see who had arrived... A second chance "Not yet, Spencer... not until I've had my chance." The Black Panther stood at the top of the entrance ramp, and behind him was a crowd of fWo wrestlers that looked at the ring with expressions of fury. Spencer paused, and let the match burn out in his gloved hand. He smiled, and pointed at the Panther. "Oh no... you're mistaken. I give you nothing. Not any of you... nothing. It is up to the Commissioner to assign a title shot, and he doesn't appear to be around anymore. I don't have to give any of you anything... and I will not. You do not get a shot at this title... none of you do... that is the law... and the law will not be broken." Death smiled, but the smile wavered when he saw that the Panther also wore a smile under his masked face. "The law, Spencer... the law indeed. Let me give you a little history lesson, shall I? Earlier this year there was a tournament, and a match, where I won the Wrestler of the Year tournament. That victory carried with it a prize... the chance for a shot at the World title in 1999. That title you're about to burn there in the ring." Death now interrupted, spitting with fury at the crowd at the top of the ramp. "And you got that shot! You got it and you lost it! Now get out... get out... get..." The Panther held up a gloved hand, and Death fell silent. The Panther raised a white envelope in his hand and continued to speak. "Yes, I did get that title shot. I got it against Scott Slugger, and in the end I lost... by disqualification. But..." Now the crowd began to rise to their feet and cheer, perhaps already understanding what the Panther was getting to. "But... according to the law, I get a title shot... that shall end in pinall or submission only. That's right... no disqualification. You see, the commissioner then was unwarped by your influence, and he wanted the fans to get a wrestling match... a real match. I didn't get that match... but I will now. For there is only one night left in 1999... and tonight I will get my shot." Inside the ring Death screamed at the Panther, who now, along with the group of wrestlers behind him, laughed. Death screamed and kicked the title in his rage, and then turned his fury up at the Panther. "You want to be sacrificed? You want to suffer the same fate as Joy? Come on then... come on and fall before me!" As Joy's name was spoken the fans began to boo, and the wrestlers at the top of the ramp stopped laughing. The Panther surged forward, but was held back by the wrestlers behind him. The Strangler held fast onto his arm, "Not now... not now... tonight... you get your chance..." Inside the ring Death raised the title from the ground, and laughed, and it was several moments before all were cleared from the ring area so the night could continue... VL (c) vs. Hellrazor Hellrazor came to the ring with Lamont, and he wore a black armband around one bicep as he slid inside the ring. He held a metal pipe in his other hand, and after setting the tabby down at the announcing desk he waited in front of the ring for the Hardcore Champion to arrive. VL came out onto the ramp with the fWo Hardcore Championship belt around her waist, and a black armband around her arm. Originally the match was scheduled to be a skyscrapper match for the Hardcore Championship, but after the accident the night before the match had been changed to a regular hardcore match... out of respect for Ryan Moore. VL got to the ring and shook hands with Hellrazor, and then grabbed the pipe he carried in his hand and whacked him over the head with it. Tossing the title to the announcing table, VL continued her attack by ramming the head of Hellrazor into the steel steps. Hellrazor backed up and circled around the ring to buy him some tile to clear the cobwebs and formulate a plan. Hellrazor picked up a steel chair as he walked and met VL on the other side of the ring. VL swung with the pipe and Hellrazor swung with the chair, and the impact of steel rang out through the arena. The chair was stripped from the hands of Hellrazor as the pipe flew into the crowd. The two now grappled, and spun around in small circles, both trying to gain the momentum. Hellrazor used his upper body strength to push VL down to her knees, and then battered her head with quick, sharp headbutts. VL rolled free as Hellrazor ripped up one of the metal guard rails, and tossed it down upon VL. With VL trapped under the metal Hellrazor climbed back into the ring and climbed up to the top turnbuckle. He signaled up into the air, and then dove off with a flying legdrop onto the guard rail. VL was crushed beneath it, and a scream escaped her lips. Hellrazor too was injured by the attack, and rolled off the steel in obvious pain. VL slowly climbed free from the tangle of metal, struggling to get to her feet but finding it impossible to put any weight on her left leg. She hobbled toward Hellrazor using the ring apron for support. Hellrazor was on his feet as well at this point, but shook his head as if he was disoriented, and fans saw that his forehead had been broken open and was bleeding badly. Hellrazor got his arms up a moment too late as VL raked the eyes, and then punched him solidly in the groin. Hellrazor fell to one knee, and VL also lost her balance and toppled to the floor. The referee was on hand and counted as VL made a loose cover, but only got two counts. Hellrazor, still laying on his back and unable to get up grabbed VL's head and thrust forward with a hard, bloody head butt. VL fell backward, but Hellrazor grabbed the head again and repeatedly rammed her head with his own. VL's eyes now rolled up and she fell backward unconscious, and Hellrazor let her go, apparently confused as to where he was. The referee checked VL, and finding her out cold he called for the bell, awarding the match to Hellrazor by knock out. Hellrazor attempted to get to his feet, but found this impossible, and medical officials came to the ring to assist them both to the back. Result: Hellrazor by Pinfall Revenge The car drove through the night, and even though the streets were lined with people awaiting the New Year's celebration the car showed no signs of slowing. It was somewhere far away from Washington DC, and as a sign was passed reading "Essex: 12km". Behind the wheel was Scott Collins, and by the grin that he wore on his face it was clear that he was near to his destination. Stone was in the passenger seat, and turned around to talk to the people in the back seat. "Quite a bitch getting tickets to England at such a late date... but we are here. Brian... you ready?" War and Plague sat in the back seats of the rental car, and looked anxious. War leaned forward and tapped Scott's shoulder. "You sure you know where you are going?" Collins nodded. "I've got some good directions... we should be seeing his house right about... there." The headlights of the rental car fell upon a large, classical English house. A single mailbox stood at the front of the long driveway, and read in block letters "SPENCER". Collins smiled wider. The car faced the house directly, and looked dark and abandoned, it's owner across the Atlantic and far away from this scene. Collins jammed his foot down on the gas. The car lurched forward and began to speed up the driveway and directly at the house. Stone turned, anxious. "Scott... shouldn't you be braking?" Collins smiled and turned to Stone. "Buckle up, baby." The car spend up the entrance walk and collided with the house in a massive rending of wood and steel. The house seemed to explode inward and shards of board and plaster flew into the night. The front of the house began to cave in, and smoke blew up into the night sky. There was a moment of quiet, and then the four passengers climbed out of the wreckage. Plague held a bag, and dumped it's contents onto the ground. Each of the four picked up a torch, and the fire illuminated the wreckage of David Spencer's home as they set about lighting the remains on fire. They laughed as they went about their work, and soon the bonfire could be seen for over fifty miles away. Collins smiled, and watched the house burn to the ground. "Come on, Riders... let's get the hell out of here. Ryan... that one's for you." Balance Poison walked behind the enraged David Spencer and sobbed quietly to herself. Fallen was nowhere to be seen, and Spencer paced the halls, slamming his fists into the walls and yelling at nobody in particular. He was clearly losing control of the situation, but there was still another factor to consider. The Black Panther wanted the title... but he would not do anything to get it. Death would do anything at all to keep it... no matter what the sacrifice. He continued to scream to himself, and with the amount of noise he was making he did not hear it when Poison was grabbed from behind and drug into one of the empty locker rooms. Dina Sol struggled to get free from the large hands that grabbed her, but an arm was clamped around her waist and a hand was firm over her mouth. Slowly the large figure turned her around, and Poison looked into the eyes of Rain. Rain held a finger to his lips. "Shhhhh. Quiet girl. Whatever happens tonight, it happens... but you're not going to be part of the equation." Rain pinched the side of Dina's neck, and the girl fell limp in his arms. Rain placed her quietly down on the floor, and stood. He looked down at the prone form of the girl, and a sad expression was worn on his haggard face. "This doesn't balance anything out Ryan... but it's a start. Wherever you are... go easy." Rain closed the door to the locker room behind him, and alone he walked out into the night. Scott Slugger vs. Ice vs. Chris Universal The final fWo show of the millennium continued as "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne began to play. The former three-time fWo World Champion Ice made his way out to a mixed reaction from the crowd, and as he reached the ring he stared up into the rafters with concern. Ice showed little emotion; as it was clear the untimely passing of Joy had affected him a bit. Ice awaited his opponents, and out next came the World's Greatest Athlete and former four-time fWo World Champion Scott Slugger. "Back in Black" blared throughout the arena, and Slugger walked out showing little emotion himself. Both he and Ice wore black armbands, and as Slugger reached the ring he looked at Ice with a cold stare. Ice glared right back at him. The lights dimmed and as they did we heard "Do you buy what The Sellout is selling?" and the crowd rushed to their feet. Out came Chris Universal and as the fans cheered him, he walked down the ramp and passed little teddy bears and lollipops to the crowd in memory of Joy. Universal rolled into the ring and as he did both Ice & Slugger began stomping him immediately. Realizing they were working as a unit once again, Ice turned on Slugger and took him down with a clothesline. Ice brought Universal up by the hair and whipped him over the top rope onto the floor. As Universal made an attempt to recover Ice whipped Slugger into the turnbuckles, and when Slugger staggered out Ice took him down with a hard spinebuster. As the referee counted the pinfall, Universal interrupted at two and a half. Universal brought Ice up and slammed his head into the turnbuckle repeatedly, before hitting a reverse neckbreaker. Universal looked as if he was going for a cover, but as he turned around he was met with a double-leg takedown by Universal. Slugger began hammering away on Universal with clenched fists, but Ice pulled him off from behind by the hair and gorilla pressed him high overhead. Ice then tossed him out onto the floor with ease, and as Universal reached his feet near the ropes, he was clothesline over the top rope onto the floor by the Big Blonde Machine. Ice raised his arms as the crowd roared in approval. As both Slugger & Universal reached their feet, they duked it out back and forth, and at the last second caught Ice in the corner of their eye as he leaped over the top rope with a cross-body, taking both men down. The crowd showed their appreciation for the big man taking a risk like that. Ice covered Universal, but Universal kicked out at two. Ice covered Slugger, but Slugger kicked out at two. Ice grabbed his ex-best friend by the hair and brought him up to a vertical base before launching him into the guardrail. Slugger chest turned bright red after hitting the cold steel. Ice brought Universal up and unleashed several reverse knife edge chops, turning his chest bright red as well. He grabbed a headlock and began to head up the ramp, and halfway up Universal shifted momentum in his favor with a belly-to-back suplex. Universal covered, but Slugger leapt in the nick of time with an elbow onto the back of Universal. Slugger covered Ice instead, but Ice kicked out. As the fight continued backstage the former friends and members of The Dynasty reached a dark part of the arena. Slugger used a nearby table for leverage as he went for the Curveball, but Universal launched him into Ice and both men crashed into a bunch of boxes nearby. Universal grabbed the World's Greatest Athlete and hit him with the Cha-Ching! before covering him. The referee counted to three, and as Universal celebrated backstage Slugger attacked him from behind near a boiler room. The room opened and a figure stood in the shadows, and Slugger tossed Universal into the room. Slugger grabbed Ice and tossed him in there as well, and the door slammed shut. The door re-opened moments later as Slugger came out alone... with no hint of Ice, Universal or the mysterious figure in sight. Result: Chris Universal by Pinfall The end The Black Panther came out onto the entrance ramp and the crowd came alive as they began to cheer him in earnest. He walked to the ring and slapped hands with the fans as they went, casting a wary glance at a strange trio sitting at ringside. Heaven, Hell, and Living sat silently at ringside, taking in everything with a strange, hidden interest. The Black Panther climbed into the ring and waited against the ropes, all look of joy off his face now, and grim determination seen beneath his mask. The fans cheered more loudly than ever before, but inside the ring it seemed only silent. The Black Panther watched as the lights dimmed, fire blazed from the top of the ramp, and Death walked to the ring. He was alone, and if there was any crack in his facade this was it. Spencer looked confidant as he carried the title to the ring, but the absence of his group behind him looked strange and man the man seem strangely alone. He climbed into the ring and walked directly up to the Panther, standing face to face with him and smiling a deadly smile. The title was dropped to the mat with a thud. The bell rang and echoed through the ring. As if in slow motion, the two men came at each other, and fists closed. The battle had begun. Alone Backstage John Skyler sat and turned a small silver object over in his hand. He small ring glittered his hand, but the engraving was impossible to read in the dim light. John knew what it read, for he had looked at this ring thousands of times in the last year. This was his wedding band, and he remembered those days with an ache of his heart. It all seemed to simple just six months ago. Then the life of the squared circle was given up freely and happily... with the love between himself as Dina to write the next chapter in his life. On the day that they were wed he thought that he would never be alone again. John Skyler sat by himself in the dark room and looked at the ring. He was become a pariah because of his association of the man Death, and once again he was alone. Ryan Moore was gone. He stood with the others at the start of the show, but he was as alone as Ryan himself now. His choices have dammed him fully. As the Millennium ends, he finds himself back where he began. A mixture of anger and despair filled him, and then he stood. Whatever he began, whatever he promised, he is nothing if he does not finish what he began. Inside the ring Death wrestled in the final match of the year... in the final match of the millennium. His place was there, and let the clips fall where they may. After tonight, Fallen was once again forever alone. Lost for words Outside the arena the protesters continued their marches. Led by several organizations on this final day of the year, they could hear the roar of the crowd as inside the arena the final match began. Penny Pureheart held a sign herself, and her hair was matted down beneath the wet of the rain. This did not diminish her spirit, for what she had seen in the past few months had firmly encased her opinion of the so-called sport of professional wrestling. She looked worn however, and was certainly tired. With only twenty minutes left in the new year, she ached from the cause she supported. She caught a glimpse of herself in the glass of the arena, and sighed at what she saw. She looked damn, worn, and tired. Her pretty appearance was temporarily on hold as this millennium wound down. She sighed at this, but continued to hold her sign high. She did not see Machina behind her, but Machina saw her, and what he saw was a goddess. The night before he won the Tag Team Championship, but he would give it all up if he could only kiss the lips of the woman he saw before him, like usual from a distance. He looked up and saw that only nineteen minutes remained in the new year and clenched his teeth. If he was going to speak with her... he must do so now. He moved forward, and touched her shoulder. She turned, and he was lost. She blinked at him. "Yes?" Machina's mouth opened, then closed, and then opened again. He gulped, and then with his head hung he shuffled away from her, unable to speak. Death (c) vs. Black Panther Death punched the jaw of the Black Panther, and he reeled backward from the blow. The Panther rebounded off the ropes and came back, ducking the arm from Spencer and hooking his arms around Death's head. The Panther dropped to his knees, snapping the head of Spencer and slamming him down hard to the mat. The Panther went for a cover, but could only get a one count. They separated, and the crowd loudly chanted the Panther's name. Death and the Panther locked up once more, and this time Death won the battle of strength and cinched in a side headlock. He applied some pressure now, and used his elbow to batter the Panther's head. The Panther was driven to one knee, and then the other. He fell to his hands and knees as Death began to laugh manically and continued to batter his opponent senseless. The Panther rolled from the ring now, and fell onto the floor. Death did not chase after him and instead screamed out at the crowd. "This is your hope? This is your salvation? Ha! He falls like everyone else... it's over... it's all OVER!" Death climbed out of the ring and walked slowly over to the Panther and placed a heavy boot to the chest of the masked challenger. The Panther rolled from the blow and coughed a hard rope of blood as he struggled to get to his feet. Death laughed again and picked him up, turned him over and dropped him to the arena floor with a hard piledriver. The Panther crumpled, and Death rolled him back into the ring. As Death climbed inside the ring he noticed that the entrance ramp had filled with wrestlers that were slowly advancing to the ring. He pointed up at them, warning the crowd. "You come closer... please... oh please come down. You interfere in this match and it ends... and I keep this title. You stay where you are, or it all ends right now..." The crowd stopped at the top of the ramp, and by the faces of wrestlers like Mike Bear, the Boston Strangler, and others it was clear that they were unhappy with the situation. Inside the ring Death paced the Panther, and attempted to pick him up. The Panther fell to his knees, and Death attempted again. Once more the Panther fell back to the mat, and Death laughed out loud. Suddenly the Panther struck, rolling Death up in a small package. The count was one, and then two... but then Death broke free and rolled away as the crowd loudly screamed in defiance of this. The Panther was not on his feet and dropped one elbow, then two, then three. The Panther grabbed the waist of Death and swung him up and over, crashing him down to the mat with a fury. The referee made a count once more, but again Death kicked out at two. The Panther measured Death and executed a superplex... the Pounce. The crowd popped loudly, and the Panther rolled over David Spencer for the cover. But Spencer had pulled something metallic form his boot, and when the Panther knelt down Spencer jammed the metal into the masked face of the challenger. The metal struck the eye of the Panther and he howled in pain and rolled away. The crowd screamed in anger and the wrestlers on the ramp voiced their concern as now Death got to his feet, laughing. Yelling up into the night Death screamed, "Riders... come to me! Come to me now!" At first nobody came, but then from the crowd came Fallen, and even as audience members struggled to hold him back he tore free and climbed over the metal barrier, and stood up on the ring apron. Death looked at him and smiled. "So... you do come... for you are mine... as you always will be." Fallen looked at Death, and as the crowd held their breath in suspense. Fallen reached upward, and his closed fist raised until it was level with Death's outstretched hand. Fallen's hand opened, and a small silver ring fell from his grasp and into David Spencer's hand. Spencer looked at it in confusion, as Fallen stepped down. "I am not yours Death. Not now... not ever again." Death looked at the ring and then screamed and tossed the object into the crowd. He screamed at Fallen, who now moved back into the crowd. This time the audience parted before him, few wanting to touch or grab Fallen. Death raged, and from behind the Black Panther moved. He grabbed Death, and once more the superplex came as if from nowhere. The Panther fell upon Death... and the count came. Once. Twice. Three. Death screamed, and outside fireworks began to explode... Result: Black Panther by Pinfall The kiss Ultra-Violet sat in his dressing room, patiently watching the blinking lights of a digital clock tick away the minutes toward midnight. Violet sat in Kid Cool's lap in the opposite corner of the room, whispering to him and giggling about some shared joke. Kid Cool seemed quite smitten, fawning over the young lady in front of him. She returned the attention, but cast an annoyed and frustrated look at Ultra-Violet when she could. Ultra-Violet took no note this, instead focusing his attention on the clock. As the time neared midnight, the purple grappler stood up excitedly and began his countdown to the new millennium. "Pi, Hexagon, C, C, Q, 1! Happy new year!" Violet looked up hopefully. Tears of joy swelled in her eyes as Ultra-Violet opened his arms wide toward her. She immediately abandoned Kid Cool to embrace her purple hero. She ran toward him, but stopped mid-stride as Ultra-Violet closed his arms around the sleeping form of Ultra-the-(narcoleptic)-Wonder-Dog. He picked up the startled dog and wished him a good millennium. A look of angry determination crossed Violet's face. She turned back to Kid Cool, grabbed him by the shirt, and hauled him up to her. Casting one final look at Ultra-Violet, she gave Kid Cool a long, deep, passionate kiss. Ultra-Violet fussed with the floppy ears of the purple basset hound and glanced over at his valet and tag partner. Ultra-Violet frowned. In an off-hand, matter of fact way he mumbled softly "Hey." He continued to look at the two. He tilted his head to one side and raised his voice to a normal tone, repeating, "Hey." The kiss continued and Ultra-Violet's sunny face abruptly changed. His brow furrowed in what could only be anger. He yelled. "Hey! That's MY nice lady!" Ultra-Violet reached, grabbed Kid Cool, pulled him away from Violet, and took a closed fisted swing at his jaw. Kid Cool swerved out of the way and ran from the room as fast as he could... carting a very pleased looking Violet with him. Finally Outside the arena people cheered and tossed confetti into the air as the new century began. Penny Pureheart put down her sign and looked up into the sky as fireworks exploded above. Around her people were kissing and singing, and suddenly she felt very alone. She shivered in the rain and looked down at her sign, thinking perhaps that she had made a mistake somewhere, for now the new millennium had come, and she had nobody to share it with. She shivered again, and jumped when a pair of hands draped a heavy coat over her shoulders. She turned, and looked at the man in the mask who had given her the coat. He looked at her, and then smiled. In his hands he held a large pink batch of cotton candy. He held it out to her, and cleared his throat. "Hi nice lady... my name is Ed. And I've... I've got some candy for you." She took the candy and looked earnestly into Machina's eyes, and smiled. "Hi, Ed. My name is Penny. I'm pleased to meet you." The two looked at each other, and then Machina leaned slowly forward, his eyes closed. Penny's eyes fluttered, and she leaned forward as well, and when their lips met there seemed to be nothing else that mattered anywhere in the world. Machina felt the warmth of her lips, and silently he thought two words. "Thanks, Ryan." Fin. Inside the arena the ring had been fluttered with wrestlers. The Black Panther was held up on their arms, and he thrust the fWo World Heavyweight belt up into the air. The fans cheered, and some sang "Auld Lang Syne" as the new Millennium began in earnest. Eron looked around at this and shook his head. "You know... the millennium doesn't actually start until next year. I mean, come on guys..." But few listened to the International Champion as the celebration continued. It was a feeling of great joy and hope inside the arena, and nobody noticed when Death sulked away. All eyes were on the ring as the Panther yelled, "This belt... this is your belt... all of you. This is the fan's belt... and now it will stay the fan's belt.... forever!" Somewhere high up in the arena John Skyler spared a final glance backward, and looked at the celebration that was taking place there. He closed his eyes then, and the cameras faded to black with him. The millennium had come to an end.
