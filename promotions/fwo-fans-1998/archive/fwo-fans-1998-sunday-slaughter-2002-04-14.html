Sunday Slaughter, 14 April 2002

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Slaughter opened up with an image of Chris Universal at his desk, busily going over paperwork. Universal manipulated his pen for a few moments presumably signing something then looked up at the camera. "Good evening, and welcome to Sunday Night Slaughter. As you, the fans of the fWo, know, recently fWo superstar Ric Chronos was involved in an... incident... which ended in the unfortunate expiration of a person attempting to kidnap and cause bodily harm to Mr. Chronos. And, as you have all noticed, Mr. Chronos has not been on fWo television since that incident took place. Well, on Friday, the charges brought against Mr. Chronos were cleared by the presiding court, as it was ruled that Mr. Chronos had acted in self-defense. And, as a result of that, Mr. Chronos has been cleared once again to appear, wrestle, and otherwise work on fWo television programming. Tonight, Ric Chronos returns. And, while I know we've had our differences, you never want to see somebody in the position that Ric was in. So I say, welcome back Ric... but don't expect to get away with anything you wouldn't have in the past. As far as I'm concerned, you're still a manipulative, slightly psychotic man... but you're not a murderer. So, hey... you've got that going for you. With that announcement out of the way, I hope everybody enjoys Slaughter tonight... we've got a huge main event for you, a World Title match a three-way matchup pitting World Champion Ruben Ross against his CyberSlam opponent Erik Kelly, and Ice. It's Slaughter time, everybody... let's get this show on the road." Universal returned to his paperwork. Slaughter went to its opening video and music package, and the Fresno fans began to cheer wildly...
