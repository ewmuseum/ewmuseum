The "new" Sunday Night Slaughter? Chris Universal waited as he tapped a foot on the table in front of him. He was feeling better than he had in weeks, and smiled as he put his hands behind his back. For one thing, the World Championship was back in better hands. Not that he had any particular affinity for the Strangler... but it wasn't in the hands of 3vil anymore, and that was a good thing. Touma had been strange, and Universal still hadn't warmed to him, but he couldn't deny that he was seeing results. Some of the bigger people were... well... nuts... but they did seem to know their jobs. Things were running smoothly, and from what he knew Touma was only about thirty percent complete in his task. Next week he was going to meet the new security guy who Touma had been talking about, and this week he was going to get a presentation from the new music guy, Clemmits. Universal was kind of fuzzy about his ideas... but he had heard that Primetime didn't like him and that was a pretty ringing endorsement in Universal's eyes. A knock came on the door, and Universal grinned. "Yeah, come on in!" He stood as Touma, Urara, and Clemmits came into the room, the later wheeling a small cart with a television on it. Touma beamed. "Universal, it is I, the rising star of western culture employment direction, leader of the neo wrestling movement..." Universal cut him off with one hands. "I know that where you're from neo means something completely different, but you might want to think twice about using it here. That guy isn't exactly the person you want to associate yourself with." Universal noticed Urara's eyes flashing with anger and thought about following that up, but then was distracted by Clemmits and a pumping handshake. "Chris Universal, glad to see you. What you are going to see here is the new direction, the new innovation for Sunday Slaughter, your weekly television program. First I was thinking, Roger, what would you do to symbolize a new style of music and direction in a wrestling program? So I was thinking a musical experience of Cher meets Rob Zombie." Universal blinked. "A... wait... what did you say?" But Clemmits ignored him as he continued on. "But then I remembered that musically the fWo was more about progressive change so I thought, why not a musical combination of Enya meets They Might Be Giants with a cross of Staind." "I..." Universal was stunned into silence. Something about all of the words this man was speaking didn't make sense. "But then..." Clemmits was growing more and more excited as he spoke, while Touma had taken to staring out of the office at some girls from the production staff with his tongue hanging limply from his mouth. Urara was glaring at him. "Then I thought, why not just have a bunch of people shouting to heavy metal music? So I hired this band called Wisk Londry and worked with visual production director Perdido Sanchez..." Universal looked over at Touma. "Perdido? Have I met him?" "Don't know..." Touma shrugged. "He was hired a few weeks ago. Does great work." Clemmits had popped a tape into the VCR in the cart and beamed at Universal as he hit the play button. "Gentlemen, I give you your new Slaughter opening." The screen was black for a few moments, then was suddenly filled with the fWo ring. It stayed there in silence for a few minutes, then fire and explosions hit the ring. A loud, nonsensical rock beat clashed out and between clips of more explosions and fire were clips of a long haired band screaming into microphones. The words were completely unintelligible amidst the rock, as cars, rings and trucks exploded. More fireworks rained up and down as the loud beat built to a climax. Just as it seemed it could get no louder, the lead singer who was stationed on top of an overturned car pointed out at the viewer and screamed louder than ever. This time Universal caught the words. "Sunday Slaughter! GUH BAH! ZI BOO GAI~!" The screen went black, and Universal slowly turned toward Clemmits. Clemmits was beaming. "Brilliant, isn't it?" Universal was speechless. He blinked. He squanders the opportunity by putting his head down, however. Strangler stops short, then hooks Chronos up and drops him with a gutwrench powerbomb. One. Two. Kickout. Chronos just manages to kick out, to the disappointment of the crowd. Wasting no time, the Strangler pulls him up and twists his arm, then pulls him into the Boston Massacre. The crowd erupts as Strangler locks on the feared submission hold. Chronos, knowing the move could end the match at any time, tries a mule kick. Strangler, however, has his legs locked together, blocking the move. Desperate to escape, Chronos uses all his strength to push backward, slamming Strangler into a corner and forcing him to release the hold. Chronos quickly turns and tries a punch, but Strangler blocks it and hits him with one, two, three big rights hands in a row, knocking him for a loop. Strangler then hooks Chronos by the head and runs into the corner, going for a tornado bulldog. Chronos, however, counters it. He drops Strangler on the top rope and quickly hits him with a shot to the gut. Chronos wastes no time in climbing to the top and hooking Strangler between his legs. Then, to the shock of the crowd, he falls back, driving the hurt neck of the Strangler into the mat with an incredible Super-Figure-Four DDT. Strangler rolls over limply, and Chronos lies on his back for moments, getting back his strength. Then, he rolls over and drapes an arm across the Strangler. One. Two. Three. Ric Chronos wins back the fWo World Title, winning it for the fourth time, joining the legendary Scott Slugger as the only wrestlers in fWo history to win the World Heavyweight Title four times. Chronos, sitting, has his arm raised by the ref, then falls back, exhausted. The referee retrieves the belt and hands it to Chronos, who takes it and drapes it across his waist. After resting for a few more moments, Chronos gets up and climbs the turnbuckles, raising the belt high in the air and soaking in the boos. As he does so, Erik Kelly emerges on the entrance ramp. The two lock eyes, and Slaughter goes off the air on the image of these two men who will meet at Fallout, each staring a hole in the other...
