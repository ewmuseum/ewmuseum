Sunday Slaughter, 18 April 1999

18 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Sunday Slaughter hailed from Victoria, BC to a capacity crowd. With only five days remaining to CyberSlam '99 energy in the arena was high, and the fans excited. Before the night was set to begin, fWo announcer Matt Panzer announced that the fWo would be coming to Evansville, Indiana for the big Pay-Per-View in May, Extreme Measures '99: More Extreme Measures. The card would feature the fWo champion Scott Slugger or whomever the champion might be up against the winner of the CyberSlam '99 invitational battle royal. As the fans cheered, fWo announcers Chris Finn and James Armstrong made their way to ringside for commentary. Just as the show was ready to go on the air, a loud crash was heard from the backstage area, followed by screams... Stone's Revenge fWo cameras made their way backstage where a truck had rammed into the side of the limosine carrying Doomsday. fWo medical personel were on hand to aid the shaken members of Doomsday, who appeared mostly unhurt with the exception of Judge Steel, who shouted that he couldn't move his neck. As medical personel tended to the wounded, Stone climbed out of the truck that had been used to ram the limosine, baseball bat in hand. Stone cracked the bat into the head of fWo medical officals on the scene, sending them running for safety. Stone then called over the other members of the bWo, and the four attacked the still shaken members of Doomsday. Stevie Roberts grabbed Brandi by the arm, and quickly fled with Samuel Smooth not far behind. Stone caught the Surgeon by the leg as he scrambled away, and brought the baseball bat down on his head, knocking him unconcious. Fyre meanwhile picked the screaming Judge Steel up and delivered a DDT which further injured the neck of the fWo superstar. Telling the other members of the bWo to follow her, Stone drug the Surgeon by the legs through the backstage area, his head and arms trailing along behind. Stone drug the man to ringside, and shouted at Panzer to hand her the microphone. Stone pointed down at the Surgeon, and instructed Fathom and Wynd to hold him down. Stone then swung the bat into the arm of the Surgeon, and an audible snap was heard by the crowd. As the Surgeon was rolled and dropped out of the ring he held his broken arm, screaming in pain. Stone addressed the horrified crowd by saying, "I didn't want to have to resort to this, but Stevie Roberts... you brought this on yourself. When you attacked Scotty, you signed your death warrant. Stevie Roberts, I'm serving notice right now that you won't be making it to CyberSlam. And as for Brandi... well, at CyberSlam I'm not just going to take your title, I'm going to take your career. And if you don't think I can do it, just look down at the broken pile of crap at my feet." Paramedics rushed in to tend to the Surgeon as the bWo stalked from the ring in search of Stevie Roberts... Apocolyptica vs. Fallen Fallen makes his way out, with a slight limp; still feeling the affects of his leg being injured a week or so ago. "Long Hard Road To Hell" blares loudly over the soundsystem, as Fallen looks depressed and distraught, apparently contemplating something as he doesn't notice a fan spit at him. Fallen climbs into the ring and sulks, as his opponent Apcolyptica makes her way out. She climbs into the ring and as Fallen walks towards her, she takes him out with a reverse legsweep, taking advantage of his already injured leg. Apocolyptica follows it up by bringing Fallen up by his head, and nailing a bodyslam, before following it up with a lightning-fast legdrop. She goes for a cover, but only gets a count of two. She stands up and complains to the ref, and this allows Fallen to recover. Fallen climbs back to his feet with the aid of the ring ropes, and with his back to Apocolyptica, she runs over and dumps him over the top rope, and out onto the floor. Fallen falls in a bad away, landing on his injured leg once again. Apocolyptica sets out to take further advantage of it, waiting a few moments for Fallen to climb back up, before Apocolyptica slingshots herself over with a plancha. She then walks over near the announcers and timekeeper and the rest of the fWo crew, grabs herself a chair, and waits for Fallen to get up, before taking a chair and whacking it into the injured leg once again. Apoco rolls Fallen into the ring and goes for a cover, but somehow, Fallen canNOT and will NOT give up. Apocolyptica decides to go outside the ring, and go up to the top rope, before she flies off with a frogsplash. She misses the attempt, as Fallen musters up enough strength to roll out of the way, and Apocolyptica crashes hard into the mat. This allows Fallen to recover, somewhat, but he still walks with a more noticeable limp. Fallen grabs Apocolyptica by the hair, and brings her to her feet, before he kicks her in the stomach, and goes for a piledriver, but he can't lift her all the way up, so he grabs her by her tights and simply falls backwards, still driving her head into the mat. Fallen goes for a cover, but she quickly kicks out. He staggers back up to his feet and follows the piledriver up with a snap suplex, again avoiding having to hold all of her weight. He goes for another cover, but she again kicks out, this time at the count of two. He goes outside the ring, walks the ring apron up to the top rope, but Apocolyptica quickly meets him at the same spot. As Apocolyptica and Fallen trade blows while on the top rope, she takes the advantage with a punch to the stomach, before following it up with a face-first slam, hitting her finishing moved called "Apocalypse Now" from the top rope. Sensing that the match is finally over, she pins him, and gains a victory. Apocolyptica sits up to her knees, and looks frustrated, despite having won. She throws the referee over the top rope out onto the floor, before going outside the ring. As Fallen still lays diagnol facing the turnbuckle, she positions Fallen and pulls him towards the ringpost, before crotching him into the post. She follows it up by applying a figurefour leglock around the ringpost, and Fallen begins yelping in obvious pain from the damage being done. Apocolyptica applies more pressure, and Fallen is almost screaming because of the pain being inflicted. Apocolyptica slumps down onto the floor, almost forcing her to relinquish the hold. Still angry, Apocolyptica walks over to the nearest set of ring steps, and bringing them into the ring. She begins slamming them into his injured leg, as fWo personnel, security and a medical staff make their way to the ring to stop the carnage. Apocolyptica sees them, and hurries and slaps on a sharpshooter, inflicting more punishment to Fallen. Finally, Fathom makes her way out and she climbs into the ring, concerned about Fallen. Apocolyptica sees her, and walks over to her. The two share a silent staredown, before Apocolyptica smirks and walks out of the ring, proud of the damage she has done. As dozens of people check on Fallen, Apocolyptica walks up the ramp... Result: Apocolyptica by Pinfall Primetime Interview While medical personel tended to Fallen at ringside, fWo cameras and Matt Loco caught up with Primetime backstage. Primetime was asked about his upcoming match with Wolf Fang, and if anything would be settled in this newest encounter. Primetime smirked, and reminded Matt Loco that he had beat Wolf Fang in every other encounter, and even beat his team at Road To CyberSlam when Mitch Wilson got the pinfall on the Goldberg-clone Jackhammer. Primetime told Matt Loco that he would go over Wolf Fang at CyberSlam, the single biggest fWo event of the year, and humilate the little wolf boy in front of the millions of French fans watching from Paris. Primetime then said that after he got through with Wolf Fang he wanted what was coming to him... namely, and fWo title. Primetime said that he and Mitch Wilson wanted another shot at the fWo tag team titles which they so unfairly lost to the Dork Riders a few weeks ago, and he wanted a shot at a singles title as well. Primetime said it didn't matter if he got the Internet title or the International title or even the Hardcore title... all of those titles were fair game and would be making their way back to France where they belonged. If Primetime had to beat Austin Davis, then it was fine by him. If he had to smack around the little girl VL, then that was fine too. As Primetime continued to rant, a bag was tossed onto Primetime from behind, and Ground Zero emerged from the shadows with crowbars. Ground Zero proceeded to tie the bag, and then beat on it with the crow bars, with Wolf Fang yelling "who are you going to beat now, huh?" to the prone form. The Flying Frenchie and Mitch Wilson hit the scene with steel chairs and chased the two away, and tended to their partner in the French Foundation.
