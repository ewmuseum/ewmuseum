Wednesday Warfare, 5 February 2003

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Previously... All roads lead to CyberSlam. For nearly six years the fWo has run shows around the world, but never straying too far from their home, the United States. But recent events have forced the fWo to run operations outside of the United States for an unknown period of time. Entering their second month on the road with no signs of going home any time soon, the wrestlers and staff of the Fans Wrestling Organization have grown tired, exhausted, and disheartened in their very business. In a time when the fWo should be booming with action heading into CyberSlam, wrestlers are dreary, tired, and involved in biter personal feuds. Erik Kelly, the former World Heavyweight Champion desperately seeks revenge against the man who tricked him out of the title, the masked luchador Spyder. Kodiak Vic Creed has the assured title spot, but now a credible complaint from Eron states that he gained a pinfall that was not counted... and that the match was tied all along. Now the fWo finds themselves in Santa Marta, Columbia... and in the midst of a hurricane... Denied As Warfare was about to begin a 2002 Ford Explorer Sport Edition pulled up in the parking lot right outside the backstage entrance. Steven Shadows stepped out of the black sports utility vehicle and grabbed his gym bag. He threw it over his shoulder and headed for the door, but was greeted in an unexpected manor. "Sorry Shadows" said the security guard as he put his hand on his chest. "You're not allowed backstage tonight. So you should just go back to the hotel." "First of all, you better get your fuckin' hand off my chest!" Shadows pushed the mans hand down forcefully. "Second of all, who are you to tell me I should go back to my hotel room. I'm showing up for my job. Now step aside." "Sorry to burst your bubble Shadows, but the fWo isn't allowing you backstage tonight. Especially with Flair already inside the building. Given what happened Sunday before the show, and that lovely riot last Wednesday, the fWo road agents are playing it safe. And since Flair got to the arena before you, he stays and you go." Shadows threw his bag against his truck with anger and rage. "This is bullshit! The fWo is still tryin' to hold me back, piss me off. Why am I the only one being singled out? Takes two to tango buddy, and Flair was in the middle of that shit just as much as I. But he gets to stay cause he was here first!? Fine...I'll make sure to get to the arena really early Sunday. This is the game we're gonna play...then count me in." Shadows jumped in his Explorer and sped out of the parking lot. Shadows was once again denied entrance into an fWo event for the second straight show. And it was all thanks to Flair. A name that was quickly becoming the equivalent to nails down a chalk board to Shadows. Almighty? Long enough he had been silent. Words were cheap, but undirected actions were cheaper. As he sat amidst these Columbian fans, he could not help but find humor in their wantonness. People called him random; unpredictable. Perhaps if they knew his purpose. Everybody knew what was coming next - "Breakdown", and Ragnarok, in some form or another. Tonight, as the fans saw after the fireworks, it was just one member - Scott Slugger. "You know what? This sucks. Absolutely sucks. Something about him just rubbed him the wrong way. He gave the crowd a hang 10 sign, and the crowd responded with a few gestures of their own, none of which were hang 10 or even remotely surf-related. Arden hopped onto the ring apron, but before he could vault into the ring, Scott Slugger leapt at him and knocked him to the floor with a forearm. The bell rang, and Slugger slid outside to go after Arden. Slugger grabbed Arden and whipped him hard into the guardrail. Slugger charged in after Arden and caught him with a knee to the gut. Slugger grabbed Arden by the head and shoved him back towards the ring apron, then slammed his head down into the apron. Arden stumbled back, and Slugger, continuing on the attack, charged at him. Arden saw him coming and rolled aside, and Slugger very emphatically posted himself. Arden grabbed Slugger and rolled him back into the ring, then slid in after him. He brought Slugger to his feet and took him down with a hard snap suplex. Arden floated over for a cover, but only got one. Arden brought Slugger back up to his feet, then took him down with a bodyslam. Arden then dropped a leg and made another cover, this time getting two. Arden brought Slugger up to his feet and whipped him into the corner, then began to unload on him with a series of punches. Arden would have continued his onslut - er, onslaught, but Slugger, being a crafty veteran and unapologetic cheater, brought up his knee into Arden's family jewels. Arden stumbled back, and Slugger came out of the corner. He lifted Arden up onto his shoulders in a firemans carry, then spun him off into a flapjack. Slugger made a nonchalant cover, thinking his night was pretty much over. At two, Arden grabbed Slugger by the head and rolled him into a sort of halfassed inside cradle. One. Two. Slugger, caught completely off-guard thanks to his own arrogance, only barely managed to kick out. He got up, angered, and tried to take Stevie Arden's head off with a clothesline. Arden ducked and kicked Slugger in the gut, doubling him over. Arden then hooked Slugger by the head and lifted him up. He then leapt into the air and drove Slugger to the mat with a picture-perfect piledriver. Slugger was down, and Arden saw an opportunity and decided to risk it all. Arden climbed to the top rope, facing the crowd. Slugger was down and in perfect position, and Arden leapt off. He floated gracefully through the air with a moonsault. Arden came down and connected squarely. The only problem was, Slugger wasn't there anymore, and what Arden connected with was nothing but the canvas. Slugger got to his feet, a grimace on his face, and brought the surfer dude to his feet. Slugger led Arden to the corner, then stepped up onto the second rope. Slugger double-underhooked Arden's head, then flew off. Curveball. Slugger hit the deadly finisher square (hitting it from the corner, in the manner of a traditional swinging DDT, for the first time in perhaps years). Arden was down, and that was that. Cover. One. Two. Three. Slugger got to his feet and the referee raised his arm as "Breakdown" started playing again. Slugger wiped some of the sweat off his forehead and flicked it down at Arden, then rolled from the ring. The faster he was out of this damned place, the better. Warfare rolled on... By his side was Desire, the group's manager, who had developed a reputation for being a formidable force in the ring in her own right. The three figures strode to the ring, very confident that Carson would have no problems dispatching Lance Knight, especially being seconded as he was by both Victor and Desire. They walked to the ring together, taking care not to slip on the rain-slicked rampway. Lance rolled into the ring, immediately finding himself dampened by the waterlogged canvas. As Victor Covington and Desire climbed out the arena floor, Lance and Victor met in the center of the ring and eyed one another. Lance was giving up an inch and roughly twenty pounds to his opponent, but he was confident, having held his own against Covington at BodyCount. As the official stepped in to give the two men instructions, he was somewhat forlorn to see that they continued to stare at one another, paying him very little heed. Chagrined, he nonetheless called for the bell, and the match was underway. Both men quickly took a step backwards and began to circle one another, each looking for the early opening. Knight struck first, diving forward, mindless of the water splashing up from the mat, and took Covington down, dropping him to the mat first with a double-leg takedown, and then with a Greco throw that left Carson on his stomach. Using Covington's back as a pivot, Knight rotated around until he was in front of the prone tag champion and applied a front chancery. Covington, quickly realizing that he couldn't remain facedown on the mat much longer without fear of drowning, powered up to all fours, but what he didn't realize was that he was playing directly into Knight's plan. Covington continued to push forward, but Knight suddenly dropped backwards and locked his legs around Carson's waist, applying a choke sleeper on Covington that left him with nowhere to go. The fans leaned forward in anticipation could this truly be the end of the match already, less than three minutes in? No! Covington's head, slick with the water that was permeating the canvas, managed to slip free from the hold, allowing him to push up and drive his head into Knight's which forced Knight to release his leg scissors as well. Covington immediately got to his feet and dropped a knee into Knight's stomach, keeping the Survivor competitor grounded. He continued his assault by applying a spinning toehold, but when Knight didn't tap after nearly a minute in the hold, Carson tried to spin again, only to have Knight send him half-stumbling, half-gliding across the ring with a boot to the backside. Covington turned and charged, using the slick canvas to aid a baseball slide that caught Knight in the temple just as he rolled to his stomach. Stunned and groggy, Knight still somehow managed to get to his feet, only to find himself quickly wrapped up for a wrist-clutch exploder suplex by Covington. Suddenly angered at the prospect of having a staple of his offense used against him, Knight fired an elbow into Covington's neck, then another, and then finally another before the hold was broken by Covington. Pausing momentarily to gain his footing, Knight spun neatly and delivered a textbook spinning back chop to the side of Covington's neck, which sent the tag champion to one knee. However, he quickly fired back with a knife-edged chop to Knight's chest in retaliation, and it was on... Chop to the neck by Knight... Chop to the chest by Covington... Chop to the neck... Chop to the chest... Chop...chop...chop...chop...chop... Finally, Knight caught one of Covington's chops and spun him into a straightjacket hold. Realizing that he was about to fall victim to the Knightmare 1, Covington took the only option available to him: a rear kick that caught Knight directly in the groin. The referee was out of position to see this, and as a result, the match continued when Covington backed to the ropes and charged, sending a lariat into Knight's neck... ...or so he thought. In reality, Knight had blocked out the pain in his genitals and caught Covington's arm, sending the bigger man to the mat with a head-and-arm suplex. Covington quickly got to his feet, only to find himself snap suplexed out of his boots. Again, Carson popped up, and Knight was waiting, catching him with a double armbar into a suplex. Once on the mat, Knight bridged, turning the hold into a submission, but Covington used the slick surface of the mat to slip out from beneath Knight, twisting his neck painfully and causing Knight to release the hold. In spite of Covington's unusual counter, Knight still retained control, tackling Covington back to the mat and then charging to the ropes. However, as he turned to bounce off the ropes, he felt a presence tugging on his foot, and the fact that he didn't go down could only mean it was one person: Desire. Knight whirled around, trying to get his hands on the culprit, but in the process, he lost his footing due to the slickness of the canvas, and fell hard to his back, knocking the wind out of him. A moment later, he found himself in an even worse predicament, as a particularly stiff knee landed on his forehead, courtesy of his real opponent for the evening. Knight blacked out momentarily, and when he came to, he found himself in a very painful predicament, a stepover toehold with full nelson, a little move Carson Covington liked to call the Painful Attraction. With his vision blurred and every beat of his heart forcing black spots in front of his eyes, Knight found himself with no other option, and he slapped Covington's head three times. The match was over, and it would be small consolation indeed that as Desire climbed to the apron to celebrate, one step behind Victor Covington, Ivy managed to catch her full on the back with a cane shot. She staggered into the ring and turned to go after Ivy, but one of Victor's massive arms kept her in check as Knight rolled out of the ring and the three Rat Pack members headed up the ramp. Suddenly, the lights to the arena crackled and blazed on once more, the power, for the moment, restored. The crowd cheered their approval, and the sudden noise surprised Fallen into loosening the hold just enough for Erik Kelly to flip him up and over... Fallen's back slamming into the ring apron. Fallen clutched his back with his good arm, and Erik Kelly scooped him up and rolled him back into the ring. On the mat, Kelly brutally stomped down, still favoring the back that was aching from the submission hold. Measuring Fallen, Erik Kelly hit the rolling elbow and quickly scooped up Fallen and tossed him into the dragon suplex. The move hit solidly, and the referee went down for the count. 1... 2... But Fallen shot up an elbow, the ring veteren not to be put away so easily. Erik Kelly stayed on the offensie as Fallen favored the arm. Picking him up, Erik Kelly shot Fallen into the air, clutched the neck, and brought Fallen down with a sitting side bomb. Fallen now still on the mat. Erik Kelly walked to the turnbuckle and raised one massive arm to the cheering crowd. Nodding with approval, he climbed to the top rope and slapped the forearm. He measured Fallen, then prepared to leap off the turnbuckle with the flying elbow from hell. From behind him Spyder suddenly came out from the crowd, jumped the ring barrier and shoved Erik Kelly just as he was preparing to leap off at Fallen. Erik Kelly stumbled, fell, and landed hard on the mat. Spyder turned, tapping his forehead with one finger and laughing at the former champion. From the crowd came Neo behind him, still limping slightly from his encounter with Kelly on Slaughter just a few days before. Spyder was clutching the fWo World Heavyweight Championship under one arm, a wide grin from beneath his mask and ignoring the outrage from the crowd. Inside the ring Fallen slowly draped an arm over Kelly, and the referee dropped for the count. 1... 2... But Erik Kelly shoved upward, and Fallen looked in disbelief at the former champion. The Eradicator wouldn't be put away... and so Fallen climbed slowly to his feet, wincing at the swollen and pained arm, and raised Erik Kelly to his feet. Fallen kicked Kelly in the stomach, then went for the DDT... his finishing move. But the damage done to his arm was too great. Fallen couldn't lift the arm in, couldn't hook Kelly, and before he could attempt a counter move Erik Kelly had grabbed him by the neck. Fallen coughed as he was raised up, and a brutal chokeslam followed. The referee knelt for the count as Spyder dove for the ring. 1... 2... 3. Spyder hit Kelly with a double axehandle a moment too late, but the bell was ringing and the match was over. Kelly rolled off Fallen, but right into Spyder and Neo's attack. The two members of the Triple Threat were stomping down on Erik Kelly, grinding him into the mat. Fallen, exhausted and beaten, slowly rolled out of the ring and with his good arm secured the steel chair that Kelly had dropped on the wet floor. The crowd was outraged as he slid the chair into the ring and climbed in after it. Fallen grabbed Kelly's hair and screamed into his face as Neo held him back. Spyder picked up the chair from the mat and raised it over his head for the booing crowd to see. Spyder cocked back the chair, prepared to slam it into Erik Kelly's face... but then the crowd erupted with sudden noise. From the back came Carson and Victor Covington... Triality... the fWo World Tag Team Champions. They ran down the entrance ramp, chairs in hand. The Triple Threat slid out of the ring and jumped into the crowd as Triality slid inside the ring. Standing over the former champion, the Tag Team Champions helped Erik Kelly to his feet, who stared out at the departing Triple Threat with a buring hatred in his eyes. Erik Kelly pointed out at Spyder and screamed, but his words were lost over the roar of the crowd. Spyder, Neo and Fallen looked back at Kelly and Triality, smiling with smug satisfaction. A final reckoning between Erik Kelly and Spyder would come soon... and as Warfare slowly faded to black the question on everyone's mind was not if... but when. Result: Erik Kelly, by Pinfall
