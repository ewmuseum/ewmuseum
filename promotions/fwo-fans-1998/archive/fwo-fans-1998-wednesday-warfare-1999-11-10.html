Wednesday Warfare, 10 November 1999

10 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "Stupid Little Mermaid On Ice... Arial's not skating in a tail... what an f'n rip off..." Matt Loco tossed the remote his was holding at the television, and then turned around to see the Dark Riders entering the arena. Death led Stone, War, and Plague into the arena, stomping as he went and grumbling at ring technicians as he went. "Don't like my cake eh? Well we will just see about that..." Death stomped forward with Stone at his side. He didn't see Megan Dawn approach from behind and tap War and Plague on the shoulders. At first War and Plague growled at the young woman, but then Dawn opened her robe slightly, showing just enough skin so it was obvious that the robe was all she wore. Dawn cooed at the now smiling men, "Come on with me big boys... there's a lot more to see than what've I've shown you so far... or rather, a lot... less." Plague giggled to himself, and War absent mindedly sucked on a thumb with a goofy grin on his face. Waggling a finger at the two men, Dawn led them down another hallway deep into the arena. Death didn't notice that his group had been reduced by half as he stomped forward. Stone noticed, but chose to simply let Death continue to rave until he noticed on his own. Death finally arrived at his locker room, and kicked in the door with a mighty boot. He turned on one heel, still yelling to himself when he noticed that War and Plague were missing. Death's mouth opened and closed without any noise coming out for a few moments, and then he shut his jaw with a clap. Growling from behind closed teeth, Death spat out a dark warning. "Fooling around on the job are they... well not tonight! We've got business here tonight... serious business..." Outside fireworks exploded, signaling that the night was about to begin... Machina vs. Los Latin The lights went to black as strange organ music began to play. A man in a black trench coat came out onto the entrance ramp, holding a baseball bat. He turned to face the crowd, and Machina's face was revealed to the crowd. Machina held up a microphone, and began to speak. "It's showtime! It's... oh hell, that's stupid as all hell. I mean, what's the deal with trying to mix that Crow movie and the Mask? It's stupid I tell you... the only way I could be any dumber is if I glued a bunch of hair to Rudo's back and came out here insulting the Ripper's son because his dad died. God angles are stupid... but hey, they sure are fun to make fun of! Anyway I was thinking about coming down from the rafters on a rope, but it would be a pretty insensitive move on the promotion's part. I mean, what kind of jack ass would do something like that? So anyway, I'm out here booked to wrestle Los Latin, but you know... screw it... I'm going to shoot on you all. No... not that kind of shooting, you can all get out from under your seats. You know... I'm not the smartest worker in the world, hell... I'm not even the smartest worker in the Lone Gunmen, and dammit that's really saying something. A few months ago I got a clever idea where I would piss off everyone in the backstage area, leave in a huff, and then come back in a bold surprise move. Yeah, I know it sounds pretty stupid now, but dammit do you know how hard it is to come up with a gimmick when the whole freakin' locker room thinks you're a god damn joke? A lot of people liked the end result which was me reforming the Lone Gunmen, but backstage the end result is that nobody gives a shit... that's right, I said it... shit... about working a match with me. You know what though? It's my own fault... and whatever happens just happens. But the man I'm booked with tonight is called Los Latin, and he's not in the arena tonight. In fact, he's not going to be in the arena any night from now on. Now I'm not going to go into backstage politics, but dammit Los Latin... I liked your damn angle. And that's why I'm doing this." Machina dropped the microphone and walked to the ring. Once inside, he laid down. The referee looked at him with a confused look on his face. Machina growled up at the ref. "Count me you dumbass... what, do you think I'm taking a god damn nap here?" Shrugging, the referee made the three count, even though the pinning man wasn't even in the ring. Machina rolled out with the count made, and raised one arm in the air. "See you Los Latin... safe travels." Machina walked back up the ramp to the backstage area, stopping only for a moment to look at James Armstrong. He started to say something, but then thought better of it and simply continued backstage. Result: Los Latin by Pinfall The Wreck Backstage a taxi drove up to the arena, parking at the wrestler's entrance. The door to the cab swung open, and the Boston Strangler stepped out, bag in hand. The Strangler took a long look around himself, eyes squinted into thin slits and a devilish grin across his face. Rain fell from the sky and the Strangler looked up at it, letting the mist cover his face. He barely heard the screech of tires as a limousine speed toward him. The Strangler barely had time to note the French flags on the hood of the limousine before he leapt back into the taxi. The limousine struck the side of the taxi, crushing steel and metal and driving the cab backward into the brick of the arena. The taxi driver's head slammed against the glass, a spidery web of cracks appeared where he made contact. Security hit the scene and stood between the wreckage of the taxi and the limousine, where Primetime and the Flying Frenchie were exiting. Primetime and the Frenchie were laughing, and walked into the arena together, leaving the wreckage behind them. Medical officials pushed their way past the crowd of people, using a crowbar to beat the cab door off so they could get to the Strangler. The door free, they looked into the taxi and at once their faces went pale. "My god... is he even alive?" Matthew Zhuk vs. Jackhammer Joined in the progress, the cameras went to the ring where Jackhammer was taking on Matthew Zhuk. Jackhammer seemed in control of the match, but Jackhammer missed a twisting somersault drop. Zhuk attempted to go on the offensive, but was having trouble with his knee. Zhuk attacked with an uppercut, but the punch landed weak and Jackhammer easily grabbed the arm off the rebound and twisted it around. Jackhammer tossed Zhuk into the ropes and battered him down to the mat with chops and kicks, and then succeeded in the somersault drop on the second attempt. Jackhammer hooked Zhuk's leg and went for the cover, but Zhuk kicked out. Jackhammer brought Zhuk to his feet and readied him, but missed somersault drop again. On the attack, Zhuk clotheslined him, and tossed him into the ropes. Zhuk landed a boot in the head, and Jackhammer fell backward into the ropes. Zhuk climbed to the top rope and came off the turnbuckle with a body splash, landing it in the center of the ring. Zhuk made the cover, but Jackhammer kicked out at two and half. Zhuk then tried a superbomb from the second turnbuckle but the move was blocked and switched into a huracanrana. Jackhammer climbed to the top rope and flew off with a diving elbow gaining another nearfall. Zhuk did a death valley driver, and cross arm suplex, but still could not get the successful cover. Zhuk tried to apply a sleeper hold, but Jackhammer punched out and got to his feet. Jumping into the air, Jackhammer dropkicked and landed low, connecting with Zhuk's knee. Jackhammer punched Zhuk in the head, set him up in a powerbomb, and drove him into the mat. Jackhammer covered the former Cruiserweight champion, and got the three count. Result: Jackhammer by Pinfall Deadly woman "Come here boys... come over here..." Megan Dawn curled a finger, and then sucked on it as War and Plague gawked at her. Dawn cooed, and urged the two men further down into the boiler room behind her. A look of concern crossed briefly over War's face, but then he looked at Dawn and a goofy grin passed over his face. "Woman... good." Plague and War walked into the boiler room, letting the heavy metal door swing shut behind them. Dawn stopped against the far wall, and leaned back against it, sliding down to her knees as she went. "Close your eyes, boys..." War an Plague obliged, with Plague actually covering his eyes with both hands an giggling. They never saw the rest of the Dynasty attack from behind, cracking a baseball bat over the head of War and swinging a lead pipe into the back of Plague. The two men let out screams of pain as the Dynasty continued to lay the beat down on the Dark Riders, Scott Slugger laughing as they did so. When the two Dark Riders fell silent and unconscious Chris Universal reached into his wallet and pulled out a pair of one dollar bills, and stuffed them into their mouths. The four men and Megan Dawn laughed, Ice saying "Come on... let's get the hell out of here." Somewhere very far away from this scene of carnage, Death and Stone paced in their dressing room. "Where in the hell are those two? Dammit!" Death picked up a chair and tossed it at the wall, shattering the wooden legs into hundred of wooden shards. Death pointed to Stone. "Find them. Bring them to me. Do it now." Rain (c) vs. US Marshal The US Marshal came to the ring, waving his hat and smiling at the crowd. Before he could get into the ring thunder rolled over the arena, and Rain stepped out onto the entrance ramp. Behind him stood a strange mirror version of the Mad Trucker... and of the Marshal himself. The two men looked darker... different. Rain smiled an insane smile and raised his arms to the air. Lightning crashed overhead, striking the ringpost and sending a massive shower of sparks into the crowd. Rain raised the Internet title, listening to the crowd grow silent in fear and awe. Rain got into the ring and locked up with the Marshal. Surprisingly the Marshal got the upper hand, kicking the chest of Rain and then hitting an inverted DDT. Both men went to the mat, and the Marshal applied a figure four on Rain. Rain flipped himself over, and then crawled to the ropes one hand at a time. The Marshal bore down, but Rain got to the ropes and the Marshal was forced to break the hold. Rain scrambled to his feet, but the Marshal was already up and ran to the ropes. The Marshal hit a shoulder block tackle and then aimed a Texas suplex. Rain blocked it though, and punched the head of the Marshal, opening his lip up. Rain screamed, and climbed to the top rope. He dove off with a flying elbow, but the Marshal rolled out of the way and Rain crashed to the ground. The Marshal went for a cover, but Rain kicked out at two. All offense, the Marshal hit a springboard wheel kick, his massive frame crashing down on the body of Rain. The Marshal went to the ropes again, but his leg was caught by the strange doppelganger Marshal and he fell to the mat. The US Marshal turned and looked down with confused anger at a man who appeared to be himself. Rain struck from behind, nailing the Marshal with a running lariat. Placing the Marshal in the corner, Rain climbed to the second rope and fell over the Marshal, twisting over his body with a death valley drop to the mat. Rain covered the Marshal and hooked the leg, and got the three count. Smiling at the crowd, Rain again raised the Internet title, silently mouthing the words, "Nobody can stop me..." Result: Rain by Pinfall Chaos continues... Stone walked down the hallway looking for her missing companions, and as she turned a corner she didn't see the Dynasty sneak around from behind and head toward Death's locker room. Grinning, Lance Star knocked on the dressing room door, and from within the voice of Death said, "Back already? Good... get in here... we need to discuss what we are going to do to those four fairies and their bitch..." Ice kicked in the door and before Death had time to turn around they were on him, kicking and punching until he could no longer stand. Scott Slugger picked up a chair and broke it over Death's head, breaking open the skin and letting blood flow liberally down his skull. As Death wore a crimson mask of pain, Ice and Universal handcuffed both of his arms up on the wall, putting him into a crucifixion pose. The blood poured from his forehead as the Dynasty stepped back to admire their work. Universal smirked and pulled a can of spray paint from his jacket. Handing it to Scott Slugger, the former World Champion sprayed the number four on the floor in front of the helpless leader of the Dark Riders. "That's our number... don't you ever forget it." Slugger tossed the spray can at Death, and the group left the room with Megan Dawn blowing Death a mocking kiss as they went. Ice turned to Star. "Go on... it's time for you to kick Black Quicksilver's ass..." Lance Star (c) vs. Black Quicksilver Black Quicksilver came to the ring waving Stump in the air and listening to the screaming fans. Black Quicksilver won the right to compete for the Cruiserweight title a week ago, and as Lance Star walked to the ring Black Quicksilver locked his gaze on the champion. Lance Star slid inside the ring, but as Black Quicksilver charged him he slid back outside again and paced around the outside of the ring. Black Quicksilver followed him out, as Lance Star slid back into the ring again. As Quicksilver rolled back into the ring Star kicked down on him, taking the upper hand. Star grabbed Black Quicksilver's leg and did a sukui-nage, which is a judo move similar to a power slam, and then hit a juji-gatame. Star didn't make the cover, and instead raised his arms to the screaming crowd, looking cocky and sure of himself. Black Quicksilver recovered during this show of ego, and grabbed Star's arm from behind. Spinning him around, Black Quicksilver hit multiple kicks into the face of the champion. Star went down as Black Quicksilver went to the ropes. As he came back, Star threw Black Quicksilver with harai-goshi and tried to put him in triangle hold. Black Quicksilver blocked it, and trapped him in an Achilles tendon hold. Star struggled to escape, finally making it to the ropes to break the hold. Black Quicksilver tried to take Star down again, but Star blocked and put him in triangle hold again, this time locking it in tighter. Black Quicksilver escaped to the ropes and executed a belly-to-back suplex, then a kataha-jime to finish the match off. Black Quicksilver made the cover, and in an act of complete desperation Star put one foot on the nearby rope to break the count. Frustrated, Black Quicksilver climbed to the top rope... but didn't see Silver Fox coming out of the crowd. Silver Fox grabbed the leg of Black Quicksilver, causing him to fall groin first into the metal turnbuckle. Black Quicksilver rolled off, and Star quickly took advantage and rolled the challenger up. Star got the three count, as Silver Fox laughed from the outside. Star rolled out of the ring and shoved Silver Fox, warning him that he didn't need his help to win a match. Security hit the scene as the two men were separated, and cameras went backstage to the dressing room of the champion... Result: Lance Star by Pinfall The match is still on... Primetime smiled smugly at James Armstrong, who looked grim. Primetime smirked, "Well, how is he? Hurt? Injured? Dead?" James Armstrong stood and paced the room. "The Boston Strangler is in bad shape Brian. He's been taken to the hospital, and the doctors don't have any clear prognosis. Listen... I've liked you, the Frenchie... I've always tried to support you. But this... this... I don't know what to say." Primetime laughed. "Don't say anything! Or maybe you can say... thanks. Yeah, thanks. I've gotten that nutcase out of here, and now that he won't be able to wrestle the last match with me I guess we can all get out of here a little early. Yeah, thank me James, I've done you a favor!" James didn't thank Primetime, but instead rubbed his head. "Well, it's not that simple. We promised the fans were a World Title match... and a World Title match we must give them or the fWo gets sued. And if the fWo gets sued... I get fired. So Primetime... Brian... you will wrestle tonight, and against an old rival..." Primetime's grin melted into a furious expression. "You can't mean..." James turned on him and walked out of the room saying, "That's right Primetime... lace up the boots. Tonight it's you and Wolf Fang... for the fWo World Heavyweight Championship." Big Mikey Cool vs. Prophet Backstage Mike Heftel watched a tape of the WWF program Raw is War. He shook his head in disgust and folded his arms over his chest. "Damn! Steal my gimmick will he? Well you steal from me and I'll steal from you... Geoff! You got that equipment that I ordered?" Geoff handed Heftel a small box, looking worn and quiet, obviously still disturbed after his encounter with Spencer's Dark Riders. Heftel smiled. "Hit MY music!" Outside in the arena perky rock music began to blare, and after a few moments Heftel ran out onto the entrance ramp. He wore a long wig, wearing daisy dukes and a cut-off blue t-shirt. On the shirt the letters "bWo" were scribbled, and he was announced as 'Big Mikey Cool'. Inside the ring he grabbed a microphone and shouted, "Tonight I form a unit... a unit that will dominate professional wrestling! Tonight marks the start of the blue... world... order!" Prophet's music suddenly blared, and the massive wrestler walked out onto the ramp shaking his head. "You know nothing of the blue Heftel... nothing! Now you die!" Prophet charged the ring and slid inside. Heftel rained punches down on him, but they seemed to have no effect on the large man. Prophet screamed, and launched himself across the ring with a diving spear that pushed Heftel into the ropes. Prophet picked him up and dropped him down into the mat with an inverted tiger driver. Prophet covered him, and put both arms on the fallen man for the cover, still screaming. Prophet stood, arms raised into the air and screaming out at the crowd in attendance. Result: Prophet by Pinfall Discovery Backstage Stone helped War and Plague down the hallway, both of the men limping and holding their heads in obvious pain. "I can't believe you two idiots fell for that little slut's trick... what part of you were you thinking with?" Plague mumbled, "My groin..." War smacked Plague on the head and the smaller man howled in pain. Stone snapped, "Shut up! Both of you... god damn idiots... Death is going to..." She trailed off as she came to their dressing room and saw the door kicked down and shards of wood spread out over the floor. Inside the locker room it was as if an explosion had gone off... mirrors were broken, chairs smashed and tables destroyed. On one wall two handcuffs hung, one snapped in two. They were both coated with blood, and on the floor in front of them a pool of blood lay on the floor, the number "4" sprayed in front of it in sharp, ragged angles. From the pool of blood footprints were visible, leaving the room and heading out into the hall. At first the prints looked like they came from an old or badly injured man, but as they left the room they became more steady... more focused... and more determined. Outside in the arena the Underground's name was announced, and the cameras returned to the ring... Underground vs. Dynasty Fear, Wretch and Mike Bear walked to the ring, enjoying the strong fan reaction they received as they went. Inside the ring Wretch picked up a microphone and spoke to the crowd in strong, confidant tones, something new from the Tag Team Champion. "Tonight we get to fight the Dynasty... but I'm looking forward to Sunday. You see, on Slaughter Mike Bear and I beat two jobbers called Hawk and Animal, and next Sunday we get to embarrass another team. All I want to know is... who's next?" The crowd began to chant Goldberg, and Mike Bear laughed as he took the microphone. "Oh, believe me... nothing would give me greater pleasure than to beat the man who stole Jackhammer's gimmick... but something tells me that Billy boy doesn't have the balls to show up in the fWo because he knows that everyone here will have their way with his jabronnie ass." The crowd began to laugh and Mike Bear held his hands up. "No, no... don't get the wrong idea... because I don't even think Sid Snow would want that ugly bastard. Now Dynasty... or dy-nasty... get out here and learn what the Underground is all about!" As if on cue, Scott Slugger, Chris Universal and Ice came to the ring, flanked by Lance Star. Scott Slugger carried a microphone and shouted, "You all saw what we did to candy boy backstage... now watch us to it again!" Slugger tossed the microphone into the face of Mike Bear, and the fight was on. All six men were in the ring trading punches, and it was some time before the referee was able to get it down to just Fear and Universal. Fear attempted a running lariat, and Universal ducked under and hit a standing kick into his jaw. Fear stumbled back and tagged in Wretch, who ran into the ring and slid under Universal's legs. He came up from behind with a dropkick, and then applied an armbar. Universal chopped out of it yelling, "No armbars! I don't care what the timekeeper wants!" Universal tossed Wretch into the Dynasty corner, and Ice held him as Universal and Slugger took turns punching the face. With Wretch in bad shape the Dynasty taunted him, and Wretch struggled to make the tag. Ice went to the second rope and hit a leg drop, covering Wretch for a two count. Ice went back up, but missed on the second attempt. Wretch made the tag to Bear, and Mike Bear cleared the ring. On the outside the Dynasty strategized, and then all hit the ring at once, bring Star into the ring with them. The four men began to overpower the Underground as the referee called for the bell. Slugger and Ice held down Bear as Universal brought out another spray can, but suddenly the crowd erupted as a bloody and enraged Death stalked to the ring, holding in his hands a lead pipe and screaming. The Underground rallied with the distraction, and officials stormed the ring as Death grabbed the throat of Scott Slugger and the two began to brawl.... Result: No Contest Dreams of the future "In the dream he saw flames, and out from them came four men that he could not clearly see. There was laughter, and a woman with them that screamed and laughed like a lunatic carnival ride. One of the men laughed, but he also cried and his heart ached in silent turmoil. Somewhere before but not near them lay the broken body of a once bright light, now extinguished forevermore. Seas of people wept and looked on in horror, something that that now dead man would have found an unfitting epitaph. men charged and changed as they ran, and as the clock struck down to the end of days the ultimate betrayal came as one man fulfilled his destiny. There were screams, laughs, and finally nothing but darkness closing in on them all. Reality shattered like glass, and all was lost." The man awoke with a start, and for a moment was unsure of where he was. Around him were the cold stones of an alley, and his hands were bloody worn. He stumbled to his feet, but then caught a look of himself in a small puddle. The face that looked back to him looked almost like his own, but it was smeared with face paint and gore. He did not know who it was that looked at him... but if fans had seen him just then they all would have recognized the face of the Grendal. Primetime (c) vs. Wolf Fang Wolf Fang came to the ring, waving to the fans and excited about his World Title opportunity. Wolf Fang looked more fit than he had in past days, and the break from active competition looked to have served him well. It was the main event, and the fans sat on the edge of their seats or stood on their feet in anticipation. Primetime came out onto the entrance ramp, and stood for a moment, drinking in the cheers and boos as he wore the fWo World Heavyweight title around his waist. Primetime walked to the ring confidently, looking at his old enemy with a mixture of amusement and disgust. His expression was torn violently from his face as Wolf Fang leapt from the ring with a diving plancha that hit Primetime head-on outside the ring. Wolf Fang grabbed Primetime by the air and slung him into the ring, quickly making a cover. It was much too early, and Primetime kicked out before the ref could get in position. Still taking advantage of the momentum he had made, Wolf Fang hit Primetime with a series of palm blows, making the chest grow red with the impact. This made Primetime angry, and he attacked back with a forearm smash into the head of the challenger. Wolf Fang wisely escaped to the outside, but Primetime served up some of Wolf Fang's own medicine with a tope. Now on the outside Primetime whipped Wolf Fang into the steel. Wolf Fang held the position, and Primetime hit a missile drop kick. Primetime rolled Wolf Fang back into the ring, and then took a few moments to gloat to the crowd. As he finally rolled back into the ring Wolf Fang caught him, kicking down on the champion and then locking his arms and executing a German suplex, followed by a reverse DDT as Primetime rose. Primetime lay still as Wolf Fang climbed to the top rope, hands outstretched to the crowd. The cameras flashed as Wolf Fang dove, but he missed a diving head butt as Primetime rolled free at the last moment. Both men lay still, trying to get back to their feet. The ref lay on the ten count, but Wolf Fang showed signs of life and got to his feet first. Wolf Fang helped Primetime up, and then dropkicked the champion's knee. Wolf Fang attempted a Dragon screw, but Primetime blocked it. Wolf Fang kicked at the kneecap, and the crowd gasped as the challenger blew out the knee of the champion. Wolf Fang executed the Dragon screw, and then locked on a knee cross hold, going for a tap out. Primetime struggled to hang on, and finally powered out of the hold in the center of the ring. Primetime held his knee as Wolf Fang argued with the referee. Wolf Fang went back to the champion and kept on working on his leg and knee, trying to demolish his vertical base. With Primetime weakened and having trouble standing, Wolf Fang took the advantage and power bombed Primetime to the outside of the ring. Wolf Fang climbed up to the top rope, and then executed a rolling senton from the top turnbuckle to the floor. Wolf Fang removed his elbow pad and threw it out to the ring, finally rolling the champion back into the ring for the kill. Back in the ring, Wolf Fang hit a brain buster on Primetime and went up to the top rope again. Again Wolf Fang extended his arms, but again he missed the diving head butt as Primetime rolled free at the last second. Both men lay still on the mat, until Primetime finally made it to the ropes and used them to stand. Wobbling, Primetime ran to the ropes but on the return Wolf Fang welcomed him with another big palm-blow. Yelling now, Wolf Fang charged and hit a running Fang bomb. Wolf Fang fell upon the champion with a loose cover, and at the last second Primetime got a shoulder up, a split second before losing the World Title to his old enemy. Breathing hard, Wolf Fang picked up the champion and placed Primetime on the top rope, and hit brain buster that hurt both men. After an extended period, Wolf Fang draped an arm over Primetime's chest, and again Primetime kicked out at the last second. Wolf Fang helped Primetime up, and placed Primetime on the top rope again, this time looking for a frankensteiner. Primetime blocked the move, holding onto the ropes, and hitting back with a head butt. Wolf Fang stumbled back, and Primetime acted quickly with a swinging DDT, and then a reverse brain buster. Primetime fell upon Wolf Fang, but the challenger got an arm up at two. Both men got to their feet, and both men ran to the ropes, but it was Primetime who ducked the lariat and hit a dropkick on the return. Primetime pointed into the air, and picked Wolf Fang up and hit a power bomb. Primetime hooked the leg, and Wolf Fang kicked free a second too late. Primetime rolled away, as the fWo World title was placed on his chest. The cameras went off the air with a last look at Primetime and Wolf Fang... both men exhausted and physically spent... Result: Primetime by Pinfall
