Sunday Slaughter, 11 February 2001

11 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction From the looks on their faces, the Underground was not in a happy mood. Mike Bear entered the Pepsi Center first, scowling. Wretch followed immediately behind with a frown on his face. Ruben Ross, Damnation, and Lamont strode behind him, all clearly on the south side of pleased. Eddie was nowhere to be found. Geoff Green, fWo cameraman slash interview, sidled up to Bear with a microphone as Bear walked. "Mike... Mike, what is the condition of Eddie?" Bear continued walking, and for a moment it looked as if he wouldn't answer. Then, still walking, he replied. "Eddie's good enough." Before Green could ask about this enigmatic comment, Bear continued. "Those sons of bitches won't be once we're through with them tonight." Green moved on to Wretch. "Wretch, any thoughts?" "Yeah, Geoff. Sooner or later tonight, A.S.S. will pay. Especially Mitch he better watch his back, because sooner or later we'll get to him." Wretch moved on, and Ruben Ross threw a few words in the direction of Green as he passed. "Tonight, Mitch Wilson pays the price." Damnation and Lamont followed, both silent, and the Underground marched into the arena with bad intentions... Greg Davis (c) vs. Mike Heftel Fireworks explode inside the Pepsi Center, and the fans cheer rabidly at the arrival of Sunday Night Slaughter. The cheers continue as "Down With The Ship" begins to play, and challenger Mike Heftel makes his way to the ring. The cheers immediately turn to boos as "Eye Of The Tiger" plays and Greg Davis strolls to the ring, Cruiserweight Title around his waist. He rolls into the ring and takes off the belt. He then shoves it in Heftel's face while saying to Heftel that that's as close as he'll ever get to it. The referee takes the belt from Davis and holds it in the air, then signals to the timekeeper. The bell rings, and Heftel catches Davis off-guard with a double-leg takedown. Heftel mounts Davis and begins wailing on him with rights, drawing a large cheer from the crowd. Davis quickly rolls to the outside to escape the attack. Heftel slides out to follow him, but Davis slides back in. As Heftel slides back in, Davis begins stomping on him. Davis then brings him back up and whips him into the ropes. As Heftel comes off, Davis clocks him with a big back elbow. Davis brings Heftel back up to his feet and begins hammering away at him. A fist knocks Heftel back into the corner, and Davis stalks in and begins chopping him. The chops ring off of Heftel's chest, and he winces in pain as each one connects. Davis brings Heftel out of the corner and hooks him up, then delivers an exploder suplex. Davis walks over to cover Heftel, but Heftel shocks him by rolling him up. Due to Davis' surprise this gets two before he kicks out. Both men are quickly back up and Davis catches Heftel and takes him over with an armdrag. Heftel gets back up and Davis drops him with a japanese armdrag. Heftel gets back up again and this time eats a dropkick from Davis. Davis makes a cover, but Heftel manages to get a shoulder up at two. Davis mounts the second rope as Heftel gets up, then leaps off. He nails Heftel with a flying clothesline, sending Heftel back down to the mat. Davis puts a foot on the chest of Heftel, who, at the count of two, grabs it and uses it to trip Davis to the mat. Heftel applies an ankle lock, but Davis crawls to the ropes quickly and Heftel is forced to break the hold. As Davis gets up, Heftel grabs him and whips him into the ropes. Heftel leaps high with a dropkick as Davis bounces off the ropes, but Davis grabs the ropes and Heftel slams to the mat. Davis grabs both of Heftel's legs and flips forward into a pinfall. Heftel manages to get a shoulder up at the count of two. Davis brings Heftel back up to his feet once more, grabs him, and drops him with a brainbuster DDT near the corner. Davis leaps onto the second rope and waits for Heftel to get up. When he does, Davis leaps off and takes Heftel down with the Badda Bing. Not satisfied, Davis brings Heftel up and takes him down with the Badda Boom. Though Heftel is clearly out, Davis brings him up again and sets him up for the Davis DDT. Davis lifts Heftel and drops him to the mat, then floats over into the cover. The referee makes the three-count, and Davis successfully defends his title. The ref hands him his belt and Davis makes his way to a camera. "Quicksilver, Sphere, that's just a little taste of what's gonna happen to you at In Misery!" Davis smiles sarcastically as Slaughter heads backstage... Result: Greg Davis by pinfall Unwanted Delivery The locker room of Crusader and Clarisse remained quiet as Crusader laced his boots up in preparation for his tag match with Black Quicksilver against Titan and Sphere. Both of them were quiet around each other as they usually have been in the past couple of weeks. The silence in the locker room - with the exception of the rigid sounds of Crusader lacing his boots - ceased as a knock came at the door. "Get the door," ordered Crusader. Clarisse shook her head at Crusader's assertive attitude, before getting up to answer the door. She saw a man holding a bouquet of flowers. "Are these from you, Cody?" she asked. Crusader looked at her oddly. "Nope, I don't remember sending you flowers." Crusader began to wear a suspicious look on his face, as he finished lacing his boots and stood up to the man holding the flowers. He spoke in a menacing tone. "Who are they from?" The man took a moment to read the tag, before speaking up, "Well, it says they're from Chris Titan here, for Clarisse LaFontaine." The man handed the flowers to Clarisse and walked away. Crusader turned away from Clarisse and fumed in anger, as Clarisse sat the flowers down on a nearby table in their locker room. She hesitated before speaking. "I don't know why he sent them, Cody." He was now pacing back and forth, holding his head, trying to contain all of the angry thoughts that filled his head. Crusader spontaneously kicked a chair across the room, as Clarisse was slightly startled. "Cody, I." He cut her off. "You're coming with me." Grasping her by the wrist, Crusader stalked out of the room like a madman. "Cody, where are we going?" "We're going to find Chris Titan," he said, "and I'm going to give him a pre-match ass-kicking." With that, Crusader stormed out of the room, with Clarisse reluctantly tagging along.
