Survivor IV: Episode Thirteen, 22 July 2004

22 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction There are some things you should know, know in these days before everything would change. To be fair, it was the summer and most eyes were on other pursuits. Dog days of heat and light, and while there was much to be paying attention to, fact was... nobody was. This of course, was exactly what Brian Cage and Frank Smyth, along with their more recent partner Stevie Roberts, had hoped for. For Disney to be enamoured and ultimately bored with the wrestling business for just long enough to scam them out of promotion, money and ultimately much more. There was plenty of danger in waking the sleeping giant. They knew it, and they were counting on the giant to wake at just the right time. The rumors had been frequent, but seemed so far fetched that nobody believed that Brian Cage and Frank Smyth had formed a plan to use the Survivor contest to give a group of young wrestlers national attention, but had no intention of turning them over to the fWo. The rumors that certain copyrights for OCW had been purchased by Cage were dismissed. The talk of someone claiming to represent a new, independent wrestling promotion gathering small venues around the country were seen as fantiful at best. This then, was where things were... with rumors and speculation flying about this so-called new promotion... this OCW. Meanwhile, the giant slept, but it would not sleep for long. Watching the Watchmen One consequence of putting on an air of insouciance and frivolity is that the more intimate details of situations can be glossed over, and before you know it, you're lost. At this point it's normally good form to either drop the charade and work out what's going on, or try to bluff your way through it. If you're so determined to stay true to your character, and your character is like that of Doctor Curiosity, then you're doomed. They say pride comes before a fall, and in that case the good doctor is leaning over the edge of the Empire State Building on a windy day, with a kite attached to his back. With such an attitude, he barged in to the locker room for the Survivor contenders. He was in a fair mood by his standards, which is to say that he was not yet shouting. On a mood scale from Rana Venenosa to Brookes Davidson, he was a Christian Elijah, but without the constant defecation. "Eegor, I have a match tonight! Prepare for me ze curious gadgets zat I vill use to intimidate ze others in to not voting for me." One consequence of being a bit of an idiot is that the more intimate details of situations can make no sense to you, and before you know it, you're lost. At this point it's normally good form to let your brother do the thinking for you, and to listen to his suggestions. "You're immune." The doctor raised his eyebrow at the statement from his assistant. "You're immune Chris... I mean, Master" said Eegor, quickly covering his mistake before he could be censured. The doctor's brain whirred, but the thoughts just weren't coming to him. "You know what that means, right? It means you can't be..." "...pinned in ze match! JAH! Of course I know vhat it means, you buffoon! Remember who is ze doctor, and who is ze assistant round zese parts." The doctor was bluffing, and doing so very, very badly. Of course, once the seed was planted in his head, the Curiosity brand of miracle-grow worked wonders, and a scenario was placed before him as if by magic. "Jah! I shall go in to zis match and vin, ze vay it vas intended! You see, ze people running Survivor finally realised zat I am ze true talent of ze competition, and revarded me in zis vay! I get to beat up ze others losers vith immunity from being pinned!" "Actually, it means that..." "Silence" commanded Curiosity. His mind was working now, and he could see himself hitting all his big moves. The Cheat... The Running Cheat... The Flying Cheat... maybe even The Shooting Star Cheat. Oh, this would be a good day. Eegor watched the doctor as he fantasised about the moves he would hit (most of which involved the use of kitchen utensils, strangely enough), and sighed. Sometimes he felt just like a nanny in charge of an extremely obtuse juvenile delinquent. Sometimes he felt like an effort should be made to stop this. "Look at me. I know you've been acting like this since Collette, but..." That got his attention, much in the way a dagger to the abdomen might. All pretence, all accent and demeanour was lost in a second. "Never mention that name again. She died, and..." "She didn't die, she left you." The man in the lab coat paused, whether in reflection or defence it is hard to say, but when he replied, all traces of Chris Goodman has been removed again; it was Doctor Curiosity speaking. "She is dead to me, jah, and zat is vhat matters. Now, cease and desist your foolishness, as I have a match to triumph in." The doc grabbed a handful of tricks for the match, and proceeded on out of the door, passing Logic as he moved through the wooden portal. "Guten tag, Logic. I look forwards to defeating you via my immunity." The Pensive Punisher stopped for a minute, and looked at Eegor. It was the assistant that broke the silence. "Yes, he thinks he's immune from losing the match. I tried to explain it, but you know how he can be." Logic nodded, and Eegor continued. "I'd best get out there and help him get ready. He's going to need all the help I can give him. Sometimes I wonder how he got that doctorate in the first place." "Maybe you were the brains there too..." suggested Logic. His words hung in the air for a second, and eye contact was made. Though Curiosity and Logic were poles apart, perhaps there was more of a link between the assistant and the 'acqvaintance'. Yet as Eegor hurried off, and Logic considered the match ahead, neither really thought much about the true state of mind of Doctor Curiosity. Neither really believed he could be actually taking things in behind the disguise, behind his carefully constructed fa A Game to Be Played. The entrances were over. The wrestlers were all in the ring. Here the men were, two weeks away from the end of the fWo Survivor Competition. Everyone wanted to prove themselves, and everyone wanted a chance to wrestle. Everyone, that is, except Brookes Davidson. Once again, Brookes found himself in the position of the referee. The method which was used to decide who would wrestle tonight was amazing, even to Brookes who was used to these sons of bitches thinking they were WAY too important. It was just assumed that Rana and Logic would wrestle... then that fucking idiot Doctor Curiosity actually demanded to wrestle claiming some insanity about being destined to win the match thanks to immunity... whatever the hell THAT meant. Brookes eyed all the men standing in the ring preparing for the match to start. He sighed to himself as he began to walk to each competitor one by one to check them over. If there's one thing Brookes did, it was his job when he had to. He may not like being the ref, but he'll damn sure be the best ref you've seen in quite sometime. "Legs apart, freak show," Brookes muttered to Doc as he began to check him over for unauthorized objects. As he began to be patted down, the Doctor started looking rather nervous about something. Brookes' hands got inside Curiosity's coat, and tugged out a rubber chicken. "VER DID ZAT COME FROM?!" Doctor screamed, looking around for the person who planted the poultry in his pocket. Brookes layed a slap across Curiosity's head with the chicken, and threw it out to Eegor on the outside, "Hang onto this, Hump Boy. Try not to choke it." His eyes now turned their attention to Bantam Diablo. He just laughed out loud. He couldn't help it. Just look at the guy! Shaking his head, Brookes walked over to the kid and smiled. "Look, I don't really care if you have a knife on you... I wouldn't DREAM of taking away any advantage you've got. Heh..." Brookes muttered as he walked right past the luchador, and headed straight for the man whom everyone assumed was Bantam's idol. Brookes looked down at his arch nemesis of this competition. He stared right into the eyes of the man who stole his match just a few short weeks ago. The man who came to him and apologized afterwards. The man who was Brookes Davidson's bitch. Rana wasn't one to keep a staring contest up, so his eyes began to find anything else in the arena to look at, but not Brookes'. Not hardly. Brookes just kept on staring at the smaller man. His lip quivered slightly with disgust before he grabbed the luchador by the bottom of his mask and pulled him up the few inches to his face. "You won't win this match." Brookes dumped the little man onto his feet once again, and turned to his last mark. The Pensive Punisher. Holy crap, how gay is that name? Logic had watched what Brookes had done to each of the others, and was ready for some embarassment. He had his chest pushed out, and he was staring hard at Brookes Davidson. Davidson wasn't one to scare easily, though. He walked right up to Logic and returned the burning stare. "You got anything hidden in those big ass trunks of yours, PP?" "Of course not. I fight with hon--" "You don't even have anything hidden in your taste?" "In my what?" Brookes smiled, "I guess there's only one way to find out if you're hiding something in your taste..." A right hand comes soaring through the air at high speeds, and connects with skin and bone as Brookes Davidson slaps Logic right across his face. "... Slap it out of your mouth." Logic surged forward, and almost grabbed Brookes before he remembered that Brookes was the referee and could DQ him for any little touch. Brookes grinned at him, and then grinned at all the other three in order. "You see that, boys? That's how this match is going to go. Exactly the way the competition has gone. Brookes Davidson doing what he wants when he wants, and you stupid fucks don't touch me or you'll find yourselves out of this match AND competition. Just ask Rogers." Logic was particularly upset about that last comment. Why he was upset, it isn't quite clear. Rana felt sort of embarassed for everyone in the ring. He was just glad Brookes didn't have a mic so that the crowd wouldn't get involved in this. Brookes turned his attention to ringside, and motioned for the time keeper to ring the bell. At the end of the evening, four men would be remaining in the Survivor IV contest. The Final Four. And Logic knew he'd be one of them. He could hear Frank Smyth and Brian Cage prattling on about the elimination of Doctor Curiosity, and what it meant to the match. It was inconsequential for the moment though, really. For Logic's plan had worked. The Pensive Punisher was in the final four. Sure, it wasn't official yet, but it would be after the match, once Cage and Smyth gathered them all in the back and gave the eliminated man the bad news. There was one slight problem though, and Logic knew it as he looked out over the crowd in the Milkhouse Arena. "BAN-TAM! BAN-TAM! BAN-TAM!" Or, for the less comedically minded, "RA-NA! RA-NA! RA-NA!" Needless to say, there was no "LO-GIC! LO-GIC! LO-GIC!" accompanying the two aforementioned chants. And really, that was Logic's problem. All of the other members of the contest appealed to the fans. Rana Venenosa was the clear fan favorite. An exciting luchador with moves that could bring the house down. Bantam Diablo had similar ability, but could make a crowd laugh. Brookes Davidson was the "cool" badass, a guy who took no shit from anyone. Doctor Curiosity was the Brain come to life, a comedic would-be ruler who the fans could laugh with and at. Logic was a really smart guy. People tend to not like really smart guys. People find smart guys arrogant. In the event that the much-rumored fan vote came to pass, the odds of Logic surviving would be somewhat low, to say the very least. Even worse for Logic was the fact that he had played the game in order to make it this far. He played it openly and honestly, like no one else had before. The trick to Survivor was alliances; was using people for your own purposes as long as you could. With four men left, that ability was gone. But the four men left WERE allied. They WERE in it together, no question about it. If anyone wanted to know, Logic would be more than happy to tell them the dirty details. But the wrestlers wouldn't blame the "virtuous" Rana Venenosa. They wouldn't even blame the asshole that was Brookes Davidson. Nor would they blame the two men too dim to come up with the idea, Doctor Curiosity and Bantam Diablo. They COULD blame Eegor, but they couldn't really DO anything to him. No, the blame would fall squarely on the shoulders of the most intelligent man in the competition, and Logic knew it. Thus, in the eyes of the fans, he was someone to boo. In the eyes of his fellow wrestlers, he was persona non grata. He was a dead man walking. The thought made him shiver enough that even the fans in the front row could see it. With the exception of what he had done to Reno Davis, he had been honest the entire competition. He had told a chair-wielding Steven Windsor that he had voted to eliminate the Golden Bear, and took the chair shots that followed. He hadn't lied once, and yet...it would be his honesty that would doom him. "...Logic?" The Pensive Punisher ignored the voice. What did it really matter anyway? "One of these...these...cowards will win this contest," he said, under his breath, so that only he could hear it. "I am...finished." Well, not finished yet, he knew. There was one hope left. It shone to Logic's eyes like the Holy Grail. Immunity. "Logic!" For Logic to survive, he needed immunity this week, and in the next challenge. Even then, he would only be in the final two, and would still need a miracle to win. Logic (n)- The study of the principles of reasoning, especially of the structure of propositions as distinguished from their content and of method and validity in deductive reasoning. Miracle (n)- An event that appears inexplicable by the laws of nature and so is held to be supernatural in origin or an act of God. The irony of the situation wasn't lost on Logic. Painful irony. To know that, despite his best laid plans, his victory would be dependant on a miracle... A tear welled up in Logic's left eye. Another formed in his right. What was the point in holding back anymore? David Walter Smith and Logic had damn near become one and the same over the course of the weeks. But now that all seemed lost, there was absolutely no reason to not shed the tears that seemed as if they would come inevitably. And then, just as the calculated exterior of Logic was about to shatter, the voice spoke again. "Logic, get your ass in the fucking ring!" It was, of course, Brookes Davidson. The referee for this match. All at once, Logic remembered where he was. On the apron, in the middle of a wrestling match. David Walter Smith could have his cry later. Both eyes closed. When they opened, the tears had been annihilated, wiped from existence. Logic stepped over the ring ropes, tear-free eyes focused on Bantam Diablo. In one week and a couple of hours, July 30th, 2004 would dawn. The fWo would change forever. And Logic would find out if he would play any part in it, win or lose. For now though, he could only do what he did best. Wrestle. Looking down at his feet when he walked, BD picked out a broken flowering pot next to an antique store. BD titled his head to the side and stood with a queer expression on his face. A single, sunshine yellow daisy with outstretched peddles grew from the moist dirt inside. Minutes from now, BD would be given the news that he was the next contestant voted off. Upon this revelation, he decided to look at a simple flower. I should be bitter. I should be mad, sad, angry, frustrated, somber. Instead, I feel nothing.
