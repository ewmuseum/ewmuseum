First Two Announced

fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE FIRST TWO ANNOUNCED July 17, 2000 In a press conference held at 1pm EST at the fWo entertainment complex, the first two candidates chosen for entry into the fWo Survivor contest were revealed. A beaming Stevie Roberts stood before reporters as the announcement was made. "We are thrilled to reveal the first two wrestlers chosen. Once again, this was an amazingly difficult process as there were literally hundreds of quality applicants that wanted in. For a variety of reasons, these two men were chosen to take part in the contest. Part of it was the interview skill that they exhibited, part of it a dynamic about the character that we wanted to explore... part of it simply how the group was coming together. Remember, we wanted to select twelve candidates that meshed well together and created an interesting team we could work with." The first two candidates were revealed to be "Black Plague" Ruben Ross, an African American wrestler and Arch Angel, a young Caucasian wrestler. The press was informed that there will be more information available on fWoWrestling.com when it comes available, including biographical information and much more. Roberts explained the next step for Ruben Ross and Arch Angel. "These two men are going to be busy getting ready for the first event, which takes place this Friday. We will all see what the tone of the event will be then." When asked when more wrestlers would be announced, Stevie Roberts informed the audience that later today at least two more names would be released to the public. More information will be made available as it develops.
