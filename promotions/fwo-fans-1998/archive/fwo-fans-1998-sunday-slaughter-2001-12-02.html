Introduction Chris Universal talked quietly to himself as he sorted through paperwork and releases for Twilight. It had gotten to be late in the year so quickly, thought Universal. There was less and less time left to get everything done. After Twilight came Countdown, and then there was another long year looming before them. Universal heard a noise behind him and turned, but there was nothing there but the closed door of the office. Universal's eyes narrowed slightly as he tried to remember if he had shut the door when he came inside. He couldn't remember now, but something about it made him uneasy. It would be just like 3vil to try and jump him in his office in order to get an edge up on him. Shaking his head to clear his jumpy thoughts, Universal turned from the door and let out a quick gasp. Fallen stood next to his desk and stared silently down at him. Universal noted in a rush his dark eyes, matted hair, and pale skin. Fallen had always looked worn and strung out, but this time it seemed more severe. Perhaps that was only because it had been more than a year, but Universal didn't think so. Fallen looked more hollow but determined at the same time. It was a look that the junkies got when they were making failed attempts to shrug off the demon that lived on their backs. "J... John. I didn't expect you to be here." But this was a lie and Universal knew it. Last week when he had stripped the title off of him he knew that Fallen would be back sooner or later, more likely on the sooner side. People didn't appear at arenas out of nowhere and go after the champion if they didn't have a long-term plan. Everything about Fallen's manner suggested that he was in it for the long haul. It was Universal's job to figure out what was pushing Fallen this time, calm it, and clear him out of the way before he became more of a problem than he already was. Fallen was standing still, silent. Universal regarded him for a moment, and let the niceties fall off of him. "What do you want, John? In case you didn't realize it, I'm trying to run a business here. Your little stunt at Fallout, no matter how much the end result might have pleased the fans or myself, cost a lot of us a hell of a lot of pain. You understand that, John? All you had to do was to come to us, come to us directly and you could have been put back on the roster if that was what you wanted. We could have worked something out, brought you in this spring or summer. Instead you have to pull some kind of stunt where you just show up out of nowhere. That's not going to cut it... perhaps in Beaven's promotion that kind of half-asses seat of the pants booking can go on, but I run a tighter ship here. In case you didn't notice the wrestling industry isn't exactly the healthiest thing right now. Every time I turn around there are a dozen of wrestling promotions advertising themselves as the next greatest thing while at the same time two dozen are closing up shop. So you will have to excuse me if I try and save this promotion from the kinds of amateur stunts that run feds out of business." Fallen looked silently at Universal through all of this, his expression not changing. Universal quieted, obviously annoyed. If Fallen understood where Universal was coming from he didn't show any signs of it. Finally after long moments had stretched out he turned and walked to the door. Over his shoulder he grumbled, "You're in charge but you don't hold any power here. You can't help me." Fallen opened and walked through the door, then let it close silently behind him. Universal looked at the back of the door, his frown deepening. Frustrated, he slammed his fist into the table. But he had other things to worry about tonight, and he couldn't let some malcontent get to him. Not tonight... not while there was so much to do and so little time. A small spider crawls along the metal until it is positioned directly in front of Grendel's line of vision. The Living Nightmare cocks his massive, masked head to one side and his cold, red eyes stare directly at the tiny arachnid. After a few moments of apparent silence, Grendel extends one huge hand, palm up, to the spider. These two should provide worthy entertainment, for both seem tentative and hesitant, as if neither can truly believe that they are competing in such a venue. The Living Nightmare raises his hand, the same hand that holds his eight-legged minion. The fly alights on the palm, settling down right next to the spider.
