Introduction The Boston Strangler could sense even before he opened the door that something was wrong. He had learned over the years to trust his instincts, and so he readied himself for anything that might present itself after he opened his locker room door. The Strangler pulled the door open. What the Strangler saw inside threw even him (them?) off guard for a second. He had been prepared for a fight or something of the like, but not this. The room looked as if a tornado had gone through it, sat down, had a smoke, and gone through it again. Chairs were smashed. Tables were overturned. A television sat in the corner, smoking, its screen broken in. The room was in chaos. The Strangler looked at the wall. Spraypainted on the wall was a large red crosshair. Symbol of Erik Kelly. Beside it, in blood red spraypaint, a message. "To the top we will go, warriors...To battle we rage...To hell, you will voyage...Your flight from above is soon..." The Strangler took in the destroyed room and felt rage. The battle with Kelly was just beginning. Vengence vs. Kobi Law The Beast' by Fugees brings out the Eradication Inc. member, Vengence. But who is this with him... it is the fWo Tag Team Champions, Ruin! The three huge men walk down the ramp and to the ring and it is pretty obvious why Robert Jensen and Frank Snow are here. They are here to soften up the Laws for SummerSlaughter. 'Push Upstairs' by Underworld brings out the former fWo Tag Team Champions, the East Coast Laws. Many say that they may very well be the future fWo Tag Champs, but two men with attitudes stand in their way and are not backing down. The Laws enter the ring and stare their SummerSlaughter opponents in the eyes. But this gives Vengence an opportunity to sneak behind the brothers and club his current opponent, Kobi, from behind with a massive forearm. Kobi falls to the mat and Juno takes a swing at Vengence, but Vengence ducks the right hand and Ruin charges Juno and throws him to the outside. Ruin drops to the outside to follow up on the attack but the ref threatens to ban them from ringside and they back down. Inside the ring Vengence has Kobi backed into the corner and is pounding away at his skull. Kobi gets the boot up now to halt the attack but Vengence fights back with an eye gouge. Vengence grabs hold of Kobi's arm and whips him to the opposing corner; Kobi crashes hard into the turnbuckles and stumbles out into a sidewalk slam. Vengence back to his feet and drops his massive leg across Kobi's throat and hooks Kobi's leg only for a one count. Kobi gets to his feet now as Vengence does and takes off with a spinning wheel kick from the top rope the connects with Vengence's chest sending him hard to the mat. But from behind Ruin enters the ring and charges Kobi looking for a double clothesline, but Juno tells his brother to duck, Kobi drops to the mat as Ruin overshoots the double clothesline. Ruin charges towards the ropes and rebounds but Juno grabs Jensen's leg on the outside and drags him to the outside as Snow continues towards Kobi and catches a Hurricanranna. Snow hits the mat hard and then rolls out of the ring rubbing his back. Vengence from behind with a reverse waist lock on Kobi. Vengence collapses in a heap to the mat as Kobi quickly covers Vengence for the three count. Vengence kicks out a millisecond too late and can't believe he got pinned by a Cruiserweight. Kobi kicks Vengence in the face and then grabs the big man by his tights and throws him to the outside. Juno enters the ring and stands beside his brother as they call Ruin into the ring to settle this thing once and for all. Ruin drops their belts and slides into the ring with there arms swinging. Juno meets Jensen and Snow meets Kobi. He tries to throw Tempest down by the leg, but she back-flips onto her feet, and ducks under a clothesline. She comes off the ropes and attempts a hurricanrana, but Chronos catches her in a power-bomb position, and flapjacks her backwards to the mat. Chronos manages to lock her legs up in a grapevine, but as he reaches forward for her neck, she squirms forward and tags in Ruben Ross. Ross springs to the top strand, and hits Chronos with a body press as he gets up. Chronos however rolls through the move, staying on top with the lateral press. Tempest springs herself onto the top rope, and comes off with an impacting guillotine leg-drop on Chronos to break up the count at two. This prompts Adam Manson to hit the ring and deliver a cold boot to the face of Tempest. Her head snaps back and she falls to the mat, just narrowly avoiding a permanent neck injury. Before Manson can cause any more damage, Ross takes him by surprise with a dropkick, sending him to the outside. Ross pescadoes over the top rope, but Manson catches him in mid-air and drops him throat-first across the steel guardrail. Chronos looks down at Tempest with a smile on his face, and sets her up for the Figure-Four DDT. He hits it, rendering Tempest unconscious. On the outside, Manson attempts to whip Ross into the steel steps, but Ross holds on, brings Manson in with a knee-lift, and hits a diamond cutter. He heads to the top rope as he sees Chronos making the cover on Tempest, and dives off with a head-butt, breaking the count up at two and a half. Chronos stands up, but he's quickly taken to school with a Spin Cycle. Manson gets up on the apron, but Ross manages to put him back to the outside with a springboard moonsault, as Tempest finds the energy to drape an arm over Chronos for the upset three-count. The bell sounds, and Ross and Tempest are declared the winners with the ref raising their hands. The story is not over, however, as Crucifix comes out from the crowd and blindsides Manson from behind with a steel chair. He continues his relentless attack, striking Manson in the thigh, rib, back, shoulder, and head with the chair. Slaughter drifts away from the carnage to a commercial... Out of nowhere, the legendary Grave Digger monster truck roared into the screen and slammed into the side of the limo. It screamed towards the limo one last time, slamming into it and vaulting over. The driver then turned the Digger and came to a screeching halt in front of the limo. Two windows of the limo busted open simultaneously. The driver crawled out of one, and a bloody and beaten Vic Creed crawled out of the other. Two hands reached out of the window of the Digger, carrying a small bundle. The hands reached up and placed it on the roof.
