Sunday Slaughter, 2 April 2000

2 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction As Perfect entered the arena, he threw his bags to some lowly stagehand and told him to take it to his dressing room. He saw Scott Collins, Stone, War & Plague standing over in a corner, and headed in their general direction. Collins saw him coming, and held his hands up. "Hey look, it's baseball boy." War & Plague laughed, and Stone gave a half grin. Perfect, never one to allow someone to insult him and get away with, shot back at Collins. "Hey look, if it isn't the cake-baking, pussy whipped candy boy." Collins appeared angry, but then looked at his friends. "What a fruit this guy is. Hey Slugger, you up for a match today? Not just any type of match, however. Why don't you recruit some of your girlfriends in that clique of yours and take on me and the other Riders here tonight. You up for it?" Perfect nodded. "You never learn, do you? No matter how many times I use you as my personal punching bag you keep coming back for more. You're like a Timex, you take a lickin' and keep on tickin', only you're not as smart. Alright... I'll find me some partners. Challenge accepted." Perfect shot a glare at Stone, before heading towards the dressing room areas. Outside the arena thunder rolled as a storm moved in... as inside the arena fireworks exploded send thousands to their feet in anxious anticipation... Wretch vs. Hellrazor Slaughter kicks off with the music of Static-X. The crowd begins to boo loudly at former Underground member Wretch, who walks out on the ramp and makes his way toward the ring. He gets in the ring, has a few obscene gestures for the crowd, especially a few in particular who are holding up Mike Bear posters. As he warms up, Machinehead is heard by the crowd, who begin to cheer for Hellrazor. Hellrazor comes out with Lamont, as usual. Also, as expected, Hellrazor is not at 100%, with scars on his back and arm, signs that his battle with VL at Body Count will stay with him forever. He sets Lamont into his pet carrier and manages his way into the ring. Wretch doesn't waste any time, causing an abrupt bell with his kicks to the gut and back of Hellrazor. Razor winces with every kick, but doesn't back off. He starts to hit back with Wretch, but Wretch, having the leverage, keeps him down with knees to the back. Wretch locks in a headlock on Hellrazor on the canvas. Hellrazor, sensing that it is a somewhat weak hold, starts to get up with Wretch having the headlock still applied. Wretch, thinking that the hold will be broken, kicks up his legs and turns it into a crucifix. Hellrazor doesn't care for his move and lets his body crash to the mat, taking Wretch with him. However, Hellrazor forgets about his back, and when he knocks the air out of Wretch, he also takes the full force of Wretch's body on his back. He winces more in pain, but takes it in stride. He covers Wretch, but can only get a two-count. Hellrazor knees Wretch to the head and picks him up, setting him up for a powerslam. Before he can attempt it, Wretch nails a chop to the chest of Hellrazor, and takes off toward the ropes. Hellrazor goes for a clothesline upon his return, but Wretch ducks and stops. As Hellrazor turns around, Wretch nails him with a standing dropkick. The move doesn't fall Hellrazor, but sends him to the ropes. As Hellrazor comes back, Wretch kicks him to the gut and nails him with a rocker dropper, sending Hellrazor to the mat. Wretch picks up Hellrazor and sets him up for a spinning heel kick. As he applies the move, Hellrazor ducks and kicks Wretch to the gut, before hooking him in and landing a double-arm DDT. The move takes a lot out of Wretch as well as Hellrazor. Hellrazor gets up and tries to continue his offensive charge on Wretch, picking him up and landing a gutwrench suplex. This move also takes a lot out of Hellrazor, but he is running on sheer determination now. He picks up Wrech and signals at the top rope. The crowd cheers as they know that Hellrazor will be going for his finisher, No More Pain. He locks Wretch in for the DDT off the top. As he goes for it, Wretch hooks his leg on the turnbuckle, blocking the move. Hellrazor goes for it again, but Wretch punches him in the gut. Hellrazor releases the hold, and Wretch pushes him down to the mat. Hellrazor lands on his back, and Wretch quickly goes for a 450 splash, which he nails. He hooks Hellrazor's leg and gets a quick three count. Wretch celebrates his victory while Hellrazor rolls out of the ring, still nursing his wounds. As Wretch celebrates, Mike Bear appears at the top of the entrance ramp. Wretch stops his celebrating, and starts to laugh at Bear. Bear just stares at Wretch, yelling at him that his time will come. Bear continues his staredown at Wretch as Slaughter heads for a break. Result: Wretch by Pinfall Conversations Heavy breathing filled the air, and Perfect entered the room. Mitch Wilson was doing some crunches on the floor, and although he wasn't scheduled for a match tonight in his hometown of Denver, Colorado... not a day goes by that he isn't preparing or working out; keeping himself in top-notch physical condition. Perfect helped him up off the floor as Wilson finished the last repetition, before handing him a bottle of water. Tilting his head slightly, Perfect commenced speaking. "You're a workhorse, man... I don't know what the FtfWo would do without you. Anyways, I and Collins just sort of booked ourselves a match for tonight." Wilson chugged down the water as Perfect continued. "It's him, his bitch and his two lackey's against myself and three partners of my choice." Wilson' eyes got big. "Count me in." Perfect shook his head. Wilson seemed taken aback, but allowed his friend to explain. "No... it wouldn't be fair if you and I were on the same team. They wouldn't have a snowball's chance in Utah of defeating us." Wilson nodded... "Utah? Don't you mean hell?" A slight pause occurred, before Wilson smiled. "Oh. I just opened up one of my Academy's there... in fact, I should check it out on Wednesday when we're in town there for Warfare." Perfect nodded. "Anyways, we'd dominate them. You know me... I always like a good challenge. And I've got the... perfect... so to speak... teammates." A quizzical expression fell over the face of Wilson... "Oh?" Perfect grinned evilly, before he headed off... Cactus Jon vs. Fallen "One" begins to play, and the fans give a loud but mixed reaction as Fallen and Ric Chronos make their way to the ring. Fallen rolls in and awaits his opponent, and the fans cheer as "Bombtrack" begins and 3-time fWo World Champion Cactus Jon makes his way out to the ring. Jon hops onto the apron and steps in, and the referee gives the signal for the bell to be rung. Fallen runs at Jon with a clothesline, but Jon ducks it and hooks the head of Fallen from behind before driving him into the mat with his own move, the reverse DDT. Jon makes a cover, but Fallen escapes at one. Fallen is quickly back up, but Jon clotheslines him back down. Jon brings Fallen up and whips him into the ropes, before taking him down with a thunderous powerslam. Jon covers, but Fallen kicks out at two. Jon brings Fallen up again, and takes him down with an inverted atomic drop. Jon follows this up with a clothesline that sends Fallen over the top rope and to the outside, and Jon follows him out. Jon grabs the head of Fallen as he hits the floor, and then rams him face-first into the steel ringpost. The referee admonishes him in the ring, but Jon pays him no heed as he procures a chair to use on Fallen, who is slumped against the ringpost. Jon rears back and swings, but Ric Chronos pulls Fallen out of the way at the last moment and the chair strikes the ringpost before bouncing back into Jon's face. Fallen shakes off the cobwebs and immediately takes down the stunned Jon, still holding the chair, with a sitdown faceslam that plants Jon's face into his own chair. Fallen immediately rolls Jon into the ring, and brings him up to his feet. Fallen then positions Jon, lifts him up, and spikes him with a cradle piledriver. Fallen covers, but Jon manages to escape at two. Fallen is undaunted, and brings Jon up before whipping him into the corner. Fallen charges in with a lariat, then begins stomping and kicking away at Jon, causing him to slump down to a sitting position before Fallen rears back and kicks him directly in the face. Jon falls back and clutches at his nose, and Fallen drags him towards the center of the ring. Fallen slowly begins to head for the top, and finally reaches the top turnbuckle, a height to which he is not accustomed. Fallen leaps off, attempting a splash, but Jon rolls to the side and Fallen smashes into the mat. Both men attempt to shake off the beatings they have taken, and both are quickly on their feet. Fallen attempts a right, but Jon blocks it before firing back with a series of stiff rights of his own, and then spinning around and taking Fallen down with a discus lariat. Jon covers, but again Fallen kicks out at two. Jon brings Fallen up to his feet, and places Fallen's head between his legs. Instead of going for his Canadian Crusher piledriver, Jon lifts Fallen up, then smashes him into the mat with a vicious powerbomb. Jon signals that he's about to end it, and begins to head up top. Jon reaches the top turnbuckle, and measures Fallen. He then leaps off the top and smashes onto Fallen with a devastating flying elbowdrop. Jon immediately covers, but the referee is distracted by Ric Chronos who has leapt onto the apron. Jon leaves Fallen to head over to the site of the disturbance, and throws the referee aside before clocking Chronos directly in the face with a right cross. Chronos tumbles from the apron, and Jon heads back over to Fallen. However, Fallen has had time to recover and reaches up to hook the head of Jon and roll him over into a small package. The ref counts, but Jon kicks out at the last moment. Both men get to their feet with alacrity, and Jon launches a wild clothesline at Fallen, who ducks it. Fallen then hooks the head of Jon from behind, setting him up for his Lowfall finisher. Jon twists and struggles to get out of the move, and somehow manages to twist directly into position for a hangman's neckbreaker, and he takes Fallen down with the punishing move. Jon senses that now is the time to go for it, and he brings Fallen up, before whipping him into the ropes. Somehow Fallen manages to reverse it and Jon ends up going into the ropes, but Fallen makes the critical error of putting his head down as Jon bounces off the opposite ropes. Jon immediately hooks the head of Fallen, before lifting him up. Jon holds him in position, before dropping down to the mat with the Canadian Crusher. Jon rolls onto Fallen and hooks the leg. The ref counts one... two... and at the last moment Ric Chronos hits the ring and pulls Jon up from the cover. He immediately hooks Jon's head and takes him down with the devastating Figure-Four DDT as the ref signals for the bell to ring. Chronos helps his friend Fallen to his feet, and Fallen clears his head as Chronos signals to the top rope. Fallen quickly heads up top, and Chronos lifts Jon in a powerbomb position facing the top rope. From the top turnbuckle, Fallen hooks the head of Jon in a Lowfall position. Chronos then falls down in a powerbomb as Fallen leaps off the top, taking Jon down with a combination powerbomb-top-rope-Lowfall. Chronos spits on the body of their fallen rival, before he and Fallen leave the ring and head back to the locker rooms. Result: Cactus Jon by DQ Tom Kwan Do The crowd anxiously awaited the next match when suddenly Tom the Bomb's music began to play, and the man himself walked out onto the entrance ramp. He smiled around at everyone, who loudly booed back at him. As he walked to the ring the SlaughterScreen replayed the disgusting actions of Tom the Bomb during fWo BodyCount which caused the crowd to boo even louder. Tom slid into the ring and wasted no time asking for a microphone. "Now listen up...I have a reason for turning my back on what I thought was my true mentor, The Dark Ninja. You see, I just finished watching Ninja IV: the domination...or maybe it was III, I don't really know...And I realized something, Ninja. You see, I realized that ninjas are EVIL! So my friends, I must now destroy them all. You see, I've been doing some research on ALL the martial arts greats... Bruce Lee, Miyagi, Daniel-San, Leonardo, Donatello, Michaelangelo, Raphael, Shredder, and the Wu Tang Clan.. and using the strength of the Ninja Turtles, the wisdom of Wu Tang, and the speed of Bruce Lee, I've created the ULTIMATE IN MARTIAL ARTS...LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, I GIVE YOU... TOM KWAN DO~!" Tom raised his arms in the air, obviously expecting a loud roar of approval from the crowd. The crowd was largely silent however, and Tom shrugged as he walked back up the entrance ramp to the backstage area. Hiroshima vs. Erik Kelly Slaughter rolled on as "Oh My God" by Guns N' Roses screamed over the speakers. The cameras zoomed in on the stage, before a red light bathed the Pepsi Center. It flashed on and off rapidly as the fans stood on their feet, awaiting the imminent arrival of Hiroshima. Hiroshima walked out onto the stage, the fans booing him loudly. He mocked them as he strolled to and fro to the ring, before climbing into the squared circle. Holding his hands overhead, the boos grew louder, before they turned into cheers. "No Leaf Clover" replaced "Oh My God", as the sounds of the once great group known as Metallica echoed throughout the building. Accompanied by the beautiful Pamela Dee, "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly stepped out onto the stage, the fans cheering him on wildly. He walked down to the ring and pointed at Hiroshima, telling him that this was his last chance to back out of the match. Hiroshima seemed unfazed, as all six-feet and eleven inches of Kelly stepped over the top rope into the ring. Dee took to her corner, before Kelly took his shirt off and threw it into the crowd. The crowd nearly came to blows fighting over the shirt. This caught Kelly' attention for a moment, allowing Hiroshima to attack at once. He blindsided him with a double axehandle, before taking him into the corner and applying an illegal chokehold. He broke it at the count of four laid down by the referee, before measuring Kelly and driving three quick shoulders into the stomach area of the huge frame of Kelly. Continuing on the offensive, he hooked him for a hiptoss and went for it, but Hiroshima simply was not strong enough to complete the move. Kelly blocked it, before reversing it into a hiptoss of his own. He then brought Hiroshima up and whipped him into the ropes, before knocking him down to the mat with a big boot. He brought him up to his feet and whipped him into the turnbuckles, before charging in with a clothesline. As Hiro staggered out, Kelly hooked him for a sidewalk slam, before going for a cover. Hiroshima kicked out at two. Kelly brought him up to a vertical base, before pressing him high overhead, and sending him crashing to the mat, which resulted in an echoing thump which bounced off the arena walls of the Pepsi Center. Another cover, but Hiroshima was not ready to give up so early, and he kicked out at two and a half. Deciding it was now time to "finish things off", he brought Hiroshima up and hooked him for the Eradicator... as he went for it, he didn't yell "powerbomb!", and Hiroshima escaped, falling behind him. He spun Kelly around before going for a lowblow, and then a quick DDT. He covered Kelly, but Kelly pressed out at two and a half. Hiroshima then backed up and drove a knee into the neck, continuing to work on the head area of his opponent. After executing a bevy of chops, forearms and kicks to various areas of the body, Hiroshima targeted him to complete his finisher, the Oblivion. He hooked him for the dragon suplex, but Kelly blocked it, before he got in a shot to the ribs with a knee. He then grabbed him and hooked him, this time yelling out "POWERBOMB!", before completing the Eradication. Hiroshima was no match for this, and Kelly covered him, ending the match and holding his arms victoriously. Suddenly, from behind through the crowd came the reigning fWo World Heavyweight Champion, Dead. He spun Kelly around, before spewing the deadly black mist into his eyes, and then kicking him in the midsection and taking him down with a DDT. Hiroshima and Dead laid into Kelly, attacking the newcomer relentlessly. However, the arena lights dimmed, and "O' Fortuna" blared throughout the arena, signaling the presence of the challenger in the main event at CS2K, The Boston Strangler. The Strangler wasted no time in entering the ring, but Dead & Hiroshima retreated before he could get his hands on them. Dead & Hiroshima backpedaled up the ramp, staring at the Strangler... Dead with evil in his eyes. Strangler and Dee helped Kelly to his feet, Kelly looking at the Strangler with respect in his eyes. Slaughter headed to a commercial, while Strangler and Kelly shared a look of respect. Result: Erik Kelly by Pinfall The Perfect Spot "No! We don't have any spots on the show for you at this time. What... you think you guys can come and go as you please?" Finn shouted, as the threesome known as NXS stood in his office, wondering if any matches were available to them. RottDog scratched his head and stared blankly at Finn, who sighed at the stupidity displayed by the threesome. Just as Finn was about to continue venting, the World's Greatest Athlete entered the picture, and turned to RottDog. "Freak boy! Been a long time!" RottDog stared dumbly at him, before Perfect turned to Cairn and Steel. "Roid monkey! Judge Judy! How lucky can one guy get? Just the three idiots I was looking for. You want a spot on the shows tonight?" Markus Cairn, Judge Steel and RottDog nodded. "Alright. Consider yourselves fortunate to be teaming with the World's Greatest Athlete tonight, because you guys and myself will be taking on Collins and his stooges. Are you up for it? Good. Now... come with me... and if we lose here tonight, this is the last chance you guys will ever receive in the fWo." Finn gave a half grin as the three men followed Perfect out his office. Finn looked over at Armstrong... "Hey, didn't you say you were going to kick Slugger's ass the first chance you got? Well, why didn't you?" Armstrong shrugged. "I'm tired... hey, did we order any bun cake for tonight?" Finn sighed, before Slaughter faded to a commercial... Guano Apes vs. STUMP Squad A sold out crowd stood to it's feet as "We're Not Gonna Take It" played over the PA, bringing out the STUMP Squad, Mike Heftel and the Black Quicksilver for their tag team title shot. As they prepare inside of the ring, "War?" by System of a Down played, and out walked the reigning fWo World Tag Team Champions, Jackhammer and Sphere, the Guano Apes. The Apes pose on the entrance ramp, holding their titles high in the air, as fireworks shoot off inside of the arena. The Apes walk to the ring, and Jackhammer and the Black Quicksilver will start things off. The friends shake hands, and lock up. Jackhammer uses his power advantage to take BQ over in an armbar. BQ uses his technical skills to reverse it into a bridge followed by an arm takedown. Both men pop up, and BQ runs at Jackhammer with an attempted clothesline, but Jackhammer just turns it away, and picks the lighter BQ over his head, and slams him down to the canvas. BQ backs off to his corner and tags in Heftel as Jackhammer decides to tag out to his partner, Sphere. The men lock up in the middle of the ring, and Sphere takes Heftel with a couple chops to the chest. Sphere then whips Heftel into the ropes. Heftel ducks a clothesline, and comes off with a dropkick. Heftel, smelling gold jumps on Sphere with a cover, but Sphere kicks out at two. Heftel brings Sphere up by the hair, and nails him with a couple of forearms, knocking him back into the ropes. Heftel whips him in, and catches Sphere with a back elbow. A quick cover is denied by Sphere, as he kicks out at one. Heftel whips Sphere into the turnbuckle, as he tags BQ. Quicksilver enters in, and begins landing fists to the face of Sphere. He goes to whip Sphere into the other corner, but Sphere reverses it. Sphere runs at Quicksilver, looking for a splash, but BQ moves out of the way, BQ sets Sphere on the turnbuckle, and nails a beautiful huracanrana. BQ runs against the turnbuckles, jumps up, and comes off with a moonsault, but Sphere moves out of the way. Sphere crawls to his corner, and makes the tag to Jackhammer, and as BQ looks to tag in Heftel, Jackhammer is able to grab him by the leg, and pull him back to the neutral corner. Jackhammer lifts BQ over his shoulder, and hits him with a shoulder breaker. Jackhammer covers, but BQ kicks out at two. Jackhammer follows up with a powerslam. Another cover, and another kickout by the Black Quicksilver. Jackhammer begins to get frustrated, and he picks BQ up by the hair. He whips him into the ropes, and BQ ducks a clothesline, and bounces back off the ropes with a flying shouldertackle. BQ runs over, and dives to his corner, tagging in Heftel. Heftel nails a few punches to the face of Jackhammer, before knocking him into the ropes. Heftel runs at Jackhammer, looking for a cross-body block, but Jackhammer catches him. He slams Heftel to the mat, and then he picks Heftel up by the hair. He places Heftel's head in between his legs, and brings him up and over with a powerbomb. Sphere enters, to fend off Quicksilver, and Jackhammer is able to pick up the three count. However, as the Apes celebrate inside the ring, Ric Chronos and Fallen run out, and immediately a brawl between all three teams occurs, and then Cactus Jon runs out, adding to the brawl that keeps going, as Warfare cuts to a commercial break... Result: Guano Apes by Pinfall Rumors A camera picks up in the backlot of the Pepsi Centre in Colorado, to find a black, stretch limousine slowly roll into the arena. Whispers are heard from fans in the arena who are puzzled as to who is to come out of the limousine. The limo comes to a slow stop, and a small-framed man comes out of the driver's seat. The man walks over to one of the side doors, and pulls it open. Fans continue to mutter amongst themselves on who they predict will come out of the limousine. The limo driver calls over some nearby security guards, and they comply. Finally, a Doc Martin shoe-clad foot steps out from the limo, followed by the rest of the body. The man's hair is a light shade of hazel, cropped down the middle in a neck-long style. He has the frame of a cruiserweight, and looks to be a new-coming wrestler. His attire consists of khaki-colored pants, and a fairly tight, dark-blue Ralph Lauren shirt, with a leather coat over-top of it. The man finally brings his head up, and it is recognized as Cody Williams, also known as Crusader in the ring. Gossip amongst the fans grows even larger. Flanked by security guards and the man he was in the limo with, Crusader takes a look at his surroundings, and walks with an attach? case in-hand. Out of nowhere, Matt Loco greets the star that everyone in the arena seems to be talking about. "Mr. Williams! Mr. Williams! Clue me in, clue the wrestlers in, clue the fans in this arena for that matter! You are in an fWo arena tonight, is it true that you have signed with the Fans Wrestling Organization?" Crusader looks at one of the security guards, then straight into Loco, slightly tilting his head, as if he is examining the reporter. Crusader responds, "Signed?" Loco becomes very excited. "Yes! Yes! Have you signed with fWo? We all want to know!" Suddenly, Chris Finn steps into sight, and extends his hand to Crusader, who nods at him and shakes it. "Great to have you in the arena tonight, Mr. Williams," says Finn. "Shall we begin talking in a secluded area?" Crusader takes his attention off of Matt Loco, and responds, "Yes, that would be fine." Chris Finn and Crusader start walking out of the camera's view, but Matt Loco stops Crusader by lightly tugging at his arm. "You haven't answered the question yet, Mr. Cody Williams! Are you or are you not signed with the fWo?" Crusader smirks, then faces toward Loco. "Right... that almost slipped my mind. To answer that question... well, as that ever-so-famous line goes: no comment." Matt Loco is left in the backlot puzzled, with security guards standing in front of him to allow Crusader and Chris Finn to walk off. Riders vs. Perfect & NXS "Burning" starts up, and the fans cheer as Scott Collins leads the Four Riders to the ring for this big elimination match. Collins, Stone, Plague, and War all enter the ring simultaneously from opposite sides, and await their opponents. NXS appears at the top of the entrance ramp, before "Humans Being" begins to play, the lights turn to red, and Perfect appears from behind them, parting the NXS curtain with a shove. A solid wall of fireworks explodes in front of the foursome, and when it dies down, Perfect steps out ahead of NXS and makes his way to the ring, all-business. As they prepare for the match, Perfect turns to the trio and gives very explicit instructions - "Stay out of the ring, and stay the hell out of my way." Perfect then turns to the ring to face Plague, the man who ended his last Cruiserweight title reign a year and a half ago. Plague charges at Perfect, who lifts him up and throws him over his head, causing him to fly head-first into the top turnbuckle. Perfect hooks him from behind for the Perfect Game, but Plague uses the turnbuckles, climbing up them to flip over Perfect. He hooks Perfect from behind for the Dark Driver, but Perfect slides out the hold and takes him down with a DTH onto the first turnbuckle. Plague immediately gets back to his feet, swinging wildly at Perfect with a back elbow, which Perfect ducks. He kicks Plague hard in the stomach, doubling him over, before hooking him in a powerbomb position. He lifts Plague up and smashes him into mat once, before lifting him up again and driving him into the canvas. Perfect lifts Plague up again, before spinning around and dropping Plague into a sitting powerbomb. The referee quickly drops down and registers the three-count for Perfect, and Plague is eliminated. War quickly charges into the ring, and catches Perfect off-guard with a lariat. He brings Perfect up again, before hooking him up for a piledriver. Perfect however uses his momentum to pull himself up to the shoulders of War as War attempts the piledriver, and then double-underhooks the head of War. He then swings around, driving War into the mat with the Curveball. Perfect immediately covers the huge man, and needless to say the referee tolls the three-count on War. Stone then cautiously hits the ring, not wanting to make a mistake. Perfect takes the opportunity to charge at her with a lariat, but Stone ducks and Perfect crashes into the Rider corner. Stone immediately charges in with a clothesline, which she follows up with a series of standing clotheslines. Scott Collins on the apron gets in a few cheap shots as well, and Stone whips Perfect hard into his own corner. Stone charges in at Perfect, but Perfect catches her coming in and lifts her up into the air before driving her down with a flapjack. As he thinks about what to do next, Markus Cairn tags in from the apron. Perfect quickly gets in his face, before thinking better of it and taking his place on the apron, far away from RottDog and Judge Steel. From the apron, he reaches in and slaps Cairn on the back of the head, disorienting him for a moment. Stone meanwhile has recovered, and she kicks the big man low, doubling him over, before lifting him up and driving him into the mat with the Earthquake powerbomb. She covers Cairn, and the referee counts to three. Perfect on the apron parts the ropes for the next member of NXS, and Judge Steel reluctantly steps into the ring. He immediately swings at Stone with a right, which she blocks, then a left, which she blocks, then another right, which is blocked again, before stomping down hard on his foot, causing him to clutch it and hop about in pain. Stone backs Steel into the ropes, before whipping him off into the opposite strands. As he heads back towards her, she takes him over and plants him with a big powerslam. Stone hooks the leg, and the referee again tolls the three-count. Perfect makes a gesture to RottDog, who climbs into the ring, even more reluctantly than did Steel. The 7'0", 360lb. monster cautiously approaches Stone, and she makes a quick move at him. He immediately drops down to his knees and begs off, and Stone and Collins laugh as Perfect slaps himself on the forehead on the apron. Stone takes a few steps back, looking to have mercy on RottDog, but then she charges forward, leaping at him with a seated dropkick that strikes him right between the eyes. She brings the larger man up, and strikes with an inverted atomic drop. RottDog clutches himself in pain, and Stone shoves him into the corner, before hooking up both his legs on the ropes. Stone takes a few steps back, before charging in with the move formerly known as the shattered dreams, which when done well shatters more than dreams. Stone does it perfectly, and RottDog falls forward, still trapped in the ropes, and hits his head on the mat, knocking himself out. Stone unhooks him from the corner, and covers, earning the easy three-count. As she gets up, Perfect charges into the ring with a lariat, which she ducks, before grabbing his lariat arm and whipping him into the ropes. Perfect leapfrogs Stone, and hooks her head from behind, going for the Perfect Game. Scott Collins quickly hits the ring and double axhandles Perfect between the shoulderblades, causing him to release the hold. He slumps to the mat, and Stone sets him up for the Earthquake. She lifts him into position, but he double-underhooks her head as he did to War. She remembers this move however, and throws Perfect off. Perfect lands on his feet and charges at Stone, who throws him up and over her head. Stone turns around to face him, but Perfect has landed on the top rope and flies off, nailing her with his original finisher the Grand Slam. Perfect immediately covers Stone, and the ref counts three. Scott Collins quickly hits the ring, and immediately gets in the face of Perfect. Perfect retaliates with a slap to the face, and attempts to whip Collins into the ropes. Collins reverses the whip and grabs Perfect, throws him up into the air, and catches him coming down with the Death Penalty. Collins covers Perfect, and the ref counts three, giving the win to the Four Riders. Collins is quickly up, and begins to celebrate another one of his many wins over Perfect. However, he does this for a bit too long, and Perfect has time to recover. Perfect quickly gets to his feet and hooks the head of Collins from behind, before driving him down with the Perfect Game. Primetime immediately storms the ring, wielding a chair, and swings at Perfect as he enters. Perfect ducks, and on the backswing Primetime finds himself face-to-face with Mitch Wilson, who has hit the ring from the crowd. He hooks the head of Primetime and drives him into the mat with the Headliner, and Primetime's head strikes his own chair, busting him open. Perfect brings Collins to his feet, and places him on the second turnbuckle facing the ring. As Mitch Wilson heads over to that corner, Perfect brings Primetime up as well and sets him on the top rope, facing the crowd. Result: Riders by Elimination Sex, lies, and blank videotape Ultra Violet headed toward the arena entrance as he got ready for his match. He appeared excited, and was hopping up and down as he walked. This caused his long purple hair to fly in all directions, and it was soon in his face. Blinded, Ultra Violet veered off course and walked directly into a brick wall. A broom that had been laying against it fell to one side, knocking over a light prop that was being used for filming promos backstage. The light crashed into the floor, at once setting a stack of programs on fire. The smoke drifted up to the smoke alarms, and at once the loud beeping of the alarm rang through the arena hallway. The sudden noise caused the caterer to jump, and his tray of fine French champagne was pitched forward, drenching the Flying Frenchie who just happened to be walking in the other direction. Ultra Violet brushed his hair from his eyes and grinned. "Hey Frying Frenchie!" The Frenchie scowled. "Flying. Ze idiot..." Courage vs. Ultra Violet It finally came time for the main event as Slaughter returned from it's final set of commercials. "Rage" by Savatage screamed throughout the Pepsi Center, and out came Ultra-Violet, accompanied by his soon-to-be wife, Violet. UV and Violet both shared a smile as they walked out onto the stage, before smiling at the crowd as they came down to the ring, taking time to sign people on the arm with a purple magic marker. They entered the ring and awaited the arrival of the fWo International Champion and one/third of the French Foundation, Courage. "Reclaim My Place" by Korn rocked Denver, and out stepped the cocky, young champion. His music continued to echo throughout the arena as he made his way down to the ring... Ultra-Violet looked at him curiously as Courage made his grandiose entrance. Courage and Ultra-Violet locked up in the center of the ring, with Courage taking the advantage in a headlock takeover via a pull of hair. UV complained about it, but Courage denied it fully. Courage locked it in, but somehow UV countered out with a head scissors, which Courage rolled through into a pinning predicament. Amazingly, UV bridged out of it, shocking everyone, including UV himself. Once he bridged out of it, he was so proud of himself he released the hold and posed to the crowd, who cheered his efforts. This was a bad move on his part, as Courage snuck up from behind with a quick roll up and a hook of the tights. Ultra-Violet kicked out at two, however, before both men rushed to a vertical base. UV stood with his mouth agape, still in shock of what had just occurred. Courage went for a side kick to the midsection, but UV caught his foot, before pointing at his head as if to say he outsmarted Courage. Courage however countered with an enziguiri, which sent UV to the mat at once. Up until this point neither man had a clear cut advantage, but it appeared Courage would take control from this point on. Courage did just that, bringing Ultra-Violet up and sending him into the turnbuckles, before displaying his aerial ability with a beautiful handspring cartwheel elbow. UV staggered out from the corner, dazed... and Courage continued the assault with a running bulldog. He appeared to be attempting a pinfall, before he balked, instead running and jumping off the second rope into an Asai moonsault. Courage then went up to the top rope, and flew off with an elbow, of which UV moved out of the way. UV rubbed his head, trying to shake off the effects of the previous moves by Courage. He got up to his feet, and then pulled Courage into the corner. He hooked him and hit a spinning DDT out from the corner, before covering him... but Courage kicked out at two. UV then took to the skies on his own accord, climbing to the top rope before flying off with a headbutt. Another cover followed, as did a kickout. Seeking to finish things off, UV brought Courage up to his feet and hooked him for his patented 'Purple Piledriver', and hit it. Sensing things were over, the crowd cheered loudly as he pointed at them... they thought he was playing to the crowd, instead he was telling them about the Cotton Candy vendor in the stands. Suddenly, from ringside, Eron The Relentless slipped into the ring and began attacking Courage relentlessly, before locking in The De-Roguer. Ultra-Violet tried to stop the carnage for some reason, but Eron pushed him off... before he slid out onto the floor, pointing at Courage, and telling Courage that he should make word to the Frenchie that he won't be hiding from him anymore... UV simply looked down at Eron and shrugged, before attempting to cover Courage. The referee informed him he was disqualified, but he didn't understand why... UV invited his future bride into the ring, and they discussed what happened, as Slaughter went off the air. Result: Courage by DQ
