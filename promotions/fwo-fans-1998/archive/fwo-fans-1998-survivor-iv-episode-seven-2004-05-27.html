Introduction On the eve of a promised dramatic shift in the contest, the fWo Survivor members will showcase themselves as individuals to the world for the first time... Prologue "It's past time the world got to know who they are," said Brian Cage, "not as teams, or wrestlers... but as individuals. We're talking about a generation of atheletes that we are expecting to lead the way for the biggest coup in..." "Careful there lad," said Frank, holding up a hand, "let's not let the cat out of the bag just yet. But you're right... this is good. Good for them and good for us. Let them bond with the audence a bit... get a chance to really connect. After all, this is just the start." Brain and Frank watched the monitors, centered on the ring as the show opening finished and the wrestlers headed to the ring. They didn't notice the man behind them, and if they had there was little they could do about it anyway. Stevie Roberts smiled. There was a good balance in this, like the closing of a large circle. He had wrestled with these men in the begginging... and now it seemed like their fates would be tied together once more. He turned, and faced the four men behind him. "Gentlemen, tonight you come out of the shadows." One of them, a man with a voice all-too familiar to the fWo of old spoke. "Including me?" The others did not know who he was either... the mask had always been firmly in place. But Roberts smiled. "Well, perhaps we shall let them guess at it a little longer, won't we?" Invictus Behind the curtain, David Walter Smith, otherwise known as Logic, was somewhat incredulous. He had already been out in front of the fans four times to compete in matches. Given it his all to put on a good show for the crowd. And now, the contest wanted him to introduce himself? Ah well. Perhaps they wanted the fans to connect more with the remaining stars in the contest. Enough worrying about the trivial. It was time to explain himself, explain who and what he was. Cue "Mars, the Bringer of War". Noise erupted from the crowd. Cheers, boos, and general screaming were the order of the day. As Logic made his way down to the ring, he realized the most likely reason for him being sent out here WAS the mixed reaction of the fans. Some people liked Logic, others disliked him, and still others were neutral. "And now, ladies and gentlemen, one of the contestants in the Survivor IV contest, Logic!" The ring announcer hastily exited the ring, jamming a microphone in Logic's hand. The big man scanned the crowd, looking into the faces of hundreds of fans. Hundreds of people, each with their own biases and likes, their own opinions. People that Disney tried to control with glowing "CHEER" and "BOO" signs. Considering that, Logic was proud that the fans thought enough of him to not know what to think of him. And if the Disney suits thought that Logic speaking to the fans would change this, however, they would be sorely mistaken. The Thinking Man's Grappler raised the microphone to his lips, and spoke. "GO LOGIC GO! GO LOGIC GO!" One side of the front row looked at Logic as if he had just grown a second head. The big man continued, with an opposite sentiment. "LOGIC SUCKS! LOGIC SUCKS!" Some members of the crowd began to take up the chant, others continued looking on, confusion on their faces, and still others took up the first chant Logic had screamed out. A giant hand was suddenly raised for quiet, and to Logic's shock, everyone shut up. "Wow. Thank you. I must admit, I did not think you would all quiet down that quickly. Nonetheless...I am sure you are all wondering why I came out here, and started chanting. A fair enough thing to wonder about, and as such, it deserves an answer." Logic exited the ring, and to the great surprise of the crowd, began walking back up the aisleway, still speaking. "Now, I know many of you have never seen a wrestling event before. If you have, please raise your hand." More than a small smattering of hands rose, indicating that some fans had actually snuck past the Disney screenings. Judging by the signs, many of them had been following the contest. Logic nodded out to the crowd, before continuing. "A bunch of you. For those of you that haven't, let me show you something." Logic finally stood on the stage. He sat down on the edge, and pushed himself off to the floor below. He pointed up once he landed, his fingers gesturing to the giant neon sign used to indicate whether fans should applaud or jeer a competitor. The die hard fans in the crowd instantly booed the device, while the tourists in the crowd were clueless. "This is a device that the people who run this place use to control you. When my fellow competitors and I come out, very often this will start glowing, and tell you to either cheer or boo, depending." The jeers of the wrestling fans continued, as the tourists were confused, waiting for one of the lights to turn on. "I came out here to thank you, and to thank the crowds that have been here over the past few weeks...because you haven't let this piece of machinery affect your opinion of me. You have both cheered and booed me. Against Doctor Curiosity, I had your full support...yet against Reno Davis, the support was for him instead. Thank you." The fans in the crowd began to cheer, as even the rube tourists began to get the picture... "You see, I call myself Logic because, honestly, I have a rather high IQ, and I always think things through. I am not a barbarian who spouts catchphrases, frames his elbow, and finishes opponents with a powerbomb, no matter how much anyone would like me to be. But more than that...I am Logic because it is what the wrestling world lacks." David once again looked out into the crowd. His reference had probably gone over the heads of 85% of them. No matter. His point would be proven soon enough. "This is not a Disney movie! No one in this contest is a Disney villain trying to destroy the good virtuous hero. It simply is not the case! Besides...when I watch Disney movies, I LIKE THE VILLAINS! Ursula is far superior to Ariel! But at the same time, some heroes I like. Aladdin is a good man, for example. But even then...those characters are constructed as absolutes. No one in this competition is an absolute! No REAL PERSON is!" Logic took a deep breath, and tried to continue, but his microphone had been cut off. He looked down at the device with disgust, and hurled it into the crowd. As the fans fought over it, the Pensive Punisher charged back to the ring with surprising speed for his size, and snatched another microphone. "All that I ask of you all is that you use the logic that YOU ALL POSSESS! Judge me by my words! Judge me by my actions! But for the love of all you hold sacred, do NOT judge me by a glowing neon sign!" Once again, Logic's microphone was cut off, as the big man began to chuckle. The executives were probably panicking. The neon sign immediately blinked on. It read "BOO", but the fans, to a man, woman, and child, began to cheer. The second microphone found its way into the crowd, as Logic simply began to scream at the top of his lungs. "THIS CONTEST IS CALLED FWO SURVIVOR IV! AND I GUARENTEE YOU, WITH YOUR HELP, WE WILL ALL SURVIVE! DOCTOR CURIOSITY WILL SURVIVE! BROOKES DAVIDSON WILL SURVIVE! STEPHEN HAILOWE! BROCK TYLER! NICK SANTIAGO! STEPHEN WINDSOR! RANA VENENOSA! BANTAM DIABLO! LOGIC! NO MATTER IF WE ARE ELIMINATED, WE WILL ALL SURVIVE IN YOUR EYES!" The front row rose in support. Soon, the second row did as well. Logic was practically shaking, his face red with exertion. His logical nature usually prevented him from showing emotion, but now was one of the moments in which he couldn't help it. He had to tell everyone the truth. He had to let everyone think, for a change. Or at least give them the tools to do so. "THINK FOR YOURSELVES! DO NOT LET DISNEY TELL YOU WHAT TO THINK OF US!" At the top of the ramp, a pair of security guards appeared. Logic wheeled to face them. He knew immediately who had sent them. Before they were even halfway up the aisle, Logic stepped out of the ring, and walked to meet them. "No need, gentlemen. I will go peacefully." The guards flanked Logic as he began to walk to the back. He began thinking of what could possibly be done to him. No severe punishment could be utilized, as it would be a public relations nightmare. Thus, Logic found it very likely that he would walk away with a slap on the wrist. The Pensive Punisher smiled, whispering to himself as he disappeared from sight.
