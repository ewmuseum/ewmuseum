fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 6 February 2000

6 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Fans sat in anxious anticipation of what many had been calling the biggest Sunday Slaughter in the history of the fWo. On their programs it looked like a Pay Per View quality card, several titles on the line, the biggest fWo names involved in the matches... certainly something monumental would happen tonight. The lights dimmed, and the crowd began to cheer. After hours of waiting in line, the night was about to begin. The cheers quickly turned to boos however, as All Star began to play, and out onto the entrance ramp walked Kid Cool. The young owner of the fWo walked down the ramp and waved happily, ignoring the crowd's angry reaction to his presence in the arena. He stepped carefully into the ring, watching the ropes with a narrowed eye to make sure no accidents happened on this, his greatest night. A microphone was pulled slyly from a pocket, and Kid Cool brushed back his hair and addressed the crowd. "Hello, Atlanta Georgia!" Atlanta booed him, feeling anxious about Kid Cool being present and about why he was inside the ring rather than the matches they were expecting. Kid Cool continued. "You know, some of you might not be aware of it... in fact, I'm sure everyone needs a reminder, so let's roll the footage of what happened on Warfare when I took control of this very company." The SlaughterScreen exploded to life as the events that transpired at the will reading were recast, with the audience booing the point where Kid Cool was revealed to be the late Travis Beaven's son. The footage ended, and Kid Cool beamed with pride. "That's right... my father. But take note everyone! You see, I could have abused my relationship with my father to get myself title shots and be booked high up on the card... of course, you're all aware of such things. Remember Watts anyone?" The crowd booed louder and showed signs of revolt. "Easy people, easy! I know you're all anxious to see a wrestling show... it's been forever since Georgia has had any wrestling worth watching, but tonight the fWo will deliver to you... but it won't be that card you see in your programs." Most of the crowd began to chant "Ass-hole", with only a few confused fans chanting "E-C-W". Kid Cool waved for them to quiet down, which they ignored. Kid Cool began to lose his patience, and stomped up and down inside the ring in an attempt to get the crowd to silence. The crowd laughed at him, and sulkily he pulled from one pocket the program for the card tonight. He held it up in the air, and then ripped it in half. "This card was filled with the establishment. Wrestlers making high pay checks but not bothering to go to house shows or show up on time! These wrestlers think they are so great with their million dollar downside guarantees and merchandising deals... well what I want to know is who looks out for the real wrestlers in the fWo? The workers who bust their asses day in and day out to keep this company afloat? What about the wrestlers who never miss a house show, who always take time to talk to the fans, but get jobbed out every chance they get? Well tonight... tonight will be a night for you people! That's right, tonight is going to be filled with title shots... but it's not going to be those complacent lazy bastards getting title shots, it's going to be the workers who bust their butts and get nothing in return!" The card laid out... The crowd was unsure of what to make of Kid Cool's announcement, and backstage Chris Finn and James Armstrong watched the young red-haired wrestler with concern. "He's going to fuck up the Georgia Dome show, Finn." Finn looked at Armstrong sharply. "Watch your mouth! This is one of those backstage segments that gives the audience a glimpse of what goes on in the inner workings of the fWo!" Armstrong rolled his eyes. "He's going to MESS up the Georgia Dome show, Finn." Finn nodded. "I know. We loaded up this card in an attempt to pop a big rating and strengthen our foothold in this territory. If Kid Cool takes the big names off the card all of our planning is going to be wasted." In the ring Kid Cool had pulled a new booking sheet from his pocket, and was going over the new card he had come up with. Finn and Armstrong listened as Matt's match changed from wrestling the Black Panther to wrestling Hiroshima. Finn groaned. "Dammit... Hiroshima doesn't putt asses in seats! The Black Panther is popular down here, we needed him on the card!" "Quiet! Here comes another!," Armstrong hissed. Kid Cool announced that Eron would now face Tom the Bomb and that Courage would face Black Quicksilver. Finn nodded. "Well, that's not as bad... Collins had an ankle injury at the house show on Saturday so it would be good to give him a rest. But Tom? Nobody even knows who Tom is... and we certainly don't have any t-shirts we can sell of him..." Finn looked over at a merchandise stand that was filled with Black Panther masks and Scott Collins candy bars and groaned. Outside Kid Cool continued by saying that the Guano Apes match would be honored tonight and that they would wrestle for the Tag Team Championship belts. Also, Waru's challenge would stand and he would take on Hellrazor. Finn turned around and looked at Mick Foley. "Well, I guess you're off the hook... sorry about bringing you down here." Mick shrugged. "Just as well Finn, I'm not feeling so good anyway... I think I'll just head home..." Mick wandered away as Finn continued to grumble. "What about the main? People were looking forward to seeing the Frenchie go after Davis..." Inside the ring Kid Cool announced that Austin Davis had been given the night off, and the crowd booed. Instead, Kid Cool said, the world title match would be between the Flying Frenchie... and his enemies greatest enemy. Machina, leader of the Lone Gunmen. Finn groaned and dropped his clipboard in disgust. "Machina? Machina doesn't draw shit! God dammit..." Armstrong clamped a hand over Finn's mouth. "Shhhhh! Remember the whole backstage segment business? Finn shook Armstrong's hand off. "Ah, who gives a shit... it's not like there are more than six people reading this..." Matt Douglas (c) vs. Hiroshima Hiroshima is introduced first, coming out to a small heel reaction. He rolls into the ring and silently awaits the arrival of his opponent and International champion, Matt Douglas. "Higher" begins to play as Douglas makes his way to the ring, slingshotting himself in before handing his belt to the referee. The bell rings, and Hiroshima charges at Douglas, who leapfrogs him and causes him to smash face-first into the turnbuckle. Hiroshima backs out, stunned, and Douglas dropkicks him in the back of the head, causing him to again smash into the turnbuckles. Hiroshima falls, and Douglas hits a standing version of his somersault senton finisher. He covers, but Hiroshima kicks out at two. Douglas brings Hiroshima up, and begins to whip him into the ropes, but Hiroshima reverses. However, Douglas coming off catches Hiroshima with a spinning heel kick, knocking him down. Hiroshima quickly gets up, but falls victim to a standing leg lariat. He again quickly gets up, but Douglas leaps onto his shoulders, before taking him over with a quick lariat. Douglas makes his way to the turnbuckles, and leaps backwards onto the second rope before leaping off in a vader bomb. Douglas hooks the leg, but Hiroshima again kicks out. Douglas brings him to his feet, and whips him into the opposite corner. Douglas then backs up, takes a running start and climbs up the chest of Hiroshima before flipping backwards. He then quickly strikes with a hard dropkick that sends Hiroshima up and over the top rope. Douglas plays to the fans in the ring as Hiroshima slowly makes his way up to his feet on the outside. Douglas sees this, and bounces off the ropes, before leaping over the top with a plancha suicida. At the last moment, Hiroshima sees him coming and rolls back into the ring, causing Douglas to fly over him and slam into the guardrail with a sickening sound. Hiroshima rolls outside of the ring, before quickly rolling the body of Douglas into the ring. He rolls in himself, and brings the younger man up. He strikes at Douglas' chest with a hard chop, and chops him repeatedly, backing Douglas into the ropes. He whips Douglas across, and blasts him with a powerslam. Instead of covering, Hiroshima brings Douglas up again, and hooks him in a suplex position. He lifts him up, and holds him vertical to the mat. He holds Douglas for a good twenty seconds, before spiking him with a devastatingly stiff brainbuster. Hiroshima covers, and the ref's count nearly hits three before Matt Douglas manages to kick out. Hiroshima signals that it's over, and brings Douglas up before hooking him in a full nelson from behind. He prepares to take him over in the Oblivion dragon suplex, but Douglas fights for all he's worth, finally freeing himself from the hold and taking Hiroshima down with a back suplex. Both men are down, but quickly get up. Hiroshima winds up a strong lariat, but Douglas ducks it and catches Hiroshima turning around to face him with an ace crusher. Douglas covers, looking to put him away, but Hiroshima manages to kick out at two. Thinking quickly, Douglas rolls out onto the apron, before leaping back in with a slingshot somersault legdrop. He lets his momentum carry him to the opposite rope, which he slingshots over into a standing position. Douglas waits for Hiroshima to get up, and leaps onto the top rope, before leaping off with a springboard swinging DDT. However, Hiroshima holds onto Douglas and sets him down on his feet before kneeing him in the gut and shoving him back into the corner. Hiroshima charges in, but Douglas gets out of his way by again slingshotting onto the apron and catches Hiroshima stumbling out of the corner with a slingshot bulldog. Douglas quickly climbs to the top rope, looking to end it. He leaps off with the Impact, and impacts squarely onto the chest of Hiroshima. Douglas rolls over and covers Hiroshima with a leg hook, and the referee tolls the three-count. Douglas gets back up to his feet and is handed his title, before beginning to celebrate. However, this celebration is cut short when Oracle appears out of nowhere, wielding a steel chair. He blasts the International champion in the back with it, before striking him with it again while he's on the ground. Meanwhile, Hiroshima is beginning to get back up to his feet, only to be put back down when Oracle bends the chair over his head, knocking him out. Oracle raises the chair high in the air before spitting on both men, and heading to the back to the boos of the crowd as Slaughter continues... Result: Matt Douglas by Pinfall Training gear "Okay Tom... if you're going to wrestle ninja style tonight you're going to need some kind of authentic ninja garb. A robe... sandals... any of those will do fine." The Dark Ninja stood before Tom backstage as the large wrestler practiced shadow kickboxing on a nearby wall. Tom's kicks were not looking great, but they were passable and certainly in better shape than they were a few days ago. For one, Tom's shoes no longer flew off when he kicked out. The Ninja rubbed a lump on his head where one of the shoes had flown before Tom had gotten the whole "tying the laces tight" lesson down. Tom stopped, and looked over at the Ninja. "I've got just the stuff in my bag... I was prepared for you saying that. See? I'm going to make a good kung fu guy after all!" Tom grabbed his bag and rushed into one of the corners of the locker room to change. The Ninja mumbled to himself, "Next thing we need to do is work on being more silent." The Ninja waited, tapping one foot anxiously as Tom continued to get into his ninja garb. "Hurry up Tom, your match with Eron is coming up shortly. And you better not be putting on black hefty bags again..." Tom called back that he was nearly done, and in a few minutes sprung up from where he had been sitting and jogged over to the Ninja, his face beaming with pride. "Well? What do you think?" The Ninja looked at Tom and frowned. "When I said Ninja garb I didn't mean Star Wars pajamas, Tom. What the hell does this tag say... 'Wookie Wear'? What in the hell is that?" Tom puffed up his chest stubbornly and stuck out his bottom lip. "Oh fine, these aren't good enough for you. I suppose my Cookie Monster bedtime super slippers are out too?" The Ninja looked down and noticed that indeed Cookie Monster slippers were being worn. He sighed, and wandered out of the room in search of Cactus Jon, someone he was fairly certain wouldn't be wearing fuzzy slippers. Tom watched him go, and then looked down at his slippers. He shrugged. "The look pretty swank~! to me... they even have grip action on the soles..." As Tom looked at his slippers Mick Foley wandered by, searching for the arena exit. Hellrazor (c) w/Lamont vs. Waru Waru is already in the ring as the Slaughter comes back from a near endless barrage of advertising. He taps his foot on the mat impatiently as the hardcore Champ steps out on the entrance ramp. He's cradling Lamont, Hardcore Feline, in his left arm, and has his title belt slung over his right shoulder. As he makes his way to the ring, Waru slides out on the opposite side, procuring a chair. He stalks over to the entrance ramp side of the ring, letting Hellrazor know that he's willing to play his game to help bring some respect to that belt. Hellrazor drops his cat, signalling for Waru to bring it on. Waru obliges, but Hellrazor ducks under the obvious chair shot, then quickly spins around and clocks Waru in the back of the head with the Hardcore title. Waru falls to the floor, giving time for Hellrazor to look under the ring for plunder. He drags out a table, a bat and softball, and a lemon meringue pie. Hellrazor seems to consider the pie, then decides it's probably there for Machina, and gently slides it back under the ring. He then picks up Waru and rolls him back into the ring, buying himself some time to set up a particularly complicated spot. He sets the table up next to the corner of the ring, then grabs the bat and ball, slides them into the ring, and follows. Next, he picks up a still wobbly Waru, and props him up in the corner. He backs up to the middle of the ring with the bat and ball, then lobs the ball into the air. He connects on the swing, and the ball flies straight into Waru's gut. Sure enough, Waru clutches his stomach and falls out of the ring between the ropes, landing predictably on the table. He lies on it while 'Razor climbs the ropes, then leaps to the outside, swinging the bat down in the process. He catches Waru with the full force of the swing, and drives him through the table. The crowd "oohs" and "ahs" in kind, as Hellrazor goes for the cover on the outside. Waru barely twitches, much less attempts at the kickout, so three seconds later the match is officially over. Hellrazor grabs his title and his cat, leaving the clean-up and the collection of Waru to the ring attendants, as cameras head backstage.... Result: Hellrazor by Pinfall Partner search The Chameleon paced backstage, every now and then slamming a fist into the wall in frustration. "Dammit... Kid Cool was all ready to make it a three way dance for the Tag Team titles, but the Eclipse was taken out! Now who is going to be my partner?" The Chameleon didn't hear the footsteps coming up from behind him until it was nearly too late, and he spun around with his fists in the air and ready for a fight. The Chameleon's eyes grew wide under his mask. "You!" The Black Quicksilver stood behind him holding Stump, and looked openly at the masked man. "Chameleon... let me get right to the point. You need a partner, and I've come to realize that so do I. The two of us as a team could revolutionize the Tag Team division and go farther than anyone else can imagine. Now... what do you say?" The Chameleon looked at Black Quicksilver, stunned by the offer that was before him. Slowly, the Chameleon began to grin, and extended a hand. "Black Quicksilver, you've got yourself a deal! You and I... and a shot at the Tag Team gold! Let's go tell Kid Cool that he can get us on the card." Black Quicksilver and Chameleon started off, and then Black Quicksilver stopped and put a hand on the Chameleon's shoulder. "Wait a second... I just remembered something... I don't like you." The Chameleon struggled with comprehending this new bit of information as Black Quicksilver swung with Stump. Stump cracked the Chameleon across the head, knocking the masked wrestler to the ground in an unconscious heap. Black Quicksilver looked down at the prone body and then at Stump. "Well, I guess we still need a partner." As Black Quicksilver pondered this Mick Foley wandered by, searching for the arena exit. Eron (c) vs. Tom "the Kung Fu" Bomb As Slaughter came back from commercial, Tom the Kung Fu Bomb was waiting in the ring, practicing his martial arts forms... badly. Eron the Relentless came to the ring to the distinctive sounds of his entrance music. He entered the ring and faced his opponent with disdain. Tom the Bomb flexed his bicep and shook his entire body as he howled like Bruce Lee. Eron shrugged and hit Tom the Bomb in the face with a superkick, looking to apply his finishing hold and end the match quickly. Tom, however, tried to kip-up, failing miserably and looking like a fool. Eron shook his head, and decided to prolong the match somewhat. As he reached for Tom's hair to pull him to his feet, Tom suddenly shrieked "Shoryuken!" and launched himself an astounding several inches into the air, missing Eron by a good half-foot with his uppercut. Eron, a little angrily, grabbed Tom and irish whipped him to the opposite corner. As Tom ran towards the turnbuckles, he screamed "There is no spoon!" In his mind, he saw himself running up the turnbuckles, vaulting backwards off of them, and surprising Eron from behind in a cool Matrix-esque moment... but in reality he kicked off the first turnbuckle and fell flat on his back, winded and dazed. Eron merely shrugged and applied his De-Roguer boston crab, until Tom was forced to tap out. Result: Eron by Submission The big news "Machina... you're in the main... oh... hello Penny" Rudo called out to Machina, who was standing next to Penny holding a "LOVE DOES NOT WRESTLE". Machina waved at Rudo, and walked away from Penny to talk with him. "I haven't asked her yet... there just doesn't seem to be a good time as long as we are stuck here with all these lesbians in this rally..." Rudo looked around. "Forget about that right now... Kid Cool just booked you in a match... a World Title match! You've got to get inside and suit up so you can get in there against the Frenchie." Machina looked anxious. "I don't know... I was supposed to ask Penny to marry me, and I certainly can't do that if I'm inside wrestling..." Rudo pushed Machina in the direction of the arena. "Just go... I'll cover for you out here. Get in there and bring home the World title for us." Machina looked at Rudo for a moment, and then at Penny. "Fine. I'll go... tell Penny I had a case of exploding bowels or something... you know, something smooth so she won't suspect a thing. Talk me up while I'm gone, will you?" Rudo nodded as Machina ran toward the arena, and then walked over to join Penny. She noticed him, and smiled. "Why, hello Rudo... nice seeing you out here..." Penny put an arm around Rudo's waist and continued to shake her sign in the air. Rudo shrugged. "I've been really busy you know... but hey, I know someone who really likes you..." Penny smiled and blushed. "Well, I like him a lot too." She snuggled closer to Rudo as they continued their protest. Rudo could sense that something wasn't quite right with this, but his head still hurt from the beating he took at the Star Wars convention. Penny continued to snuggle against him as they continued with their protest. From inside the arena Mick Foley looked at the protesters outside through a window, trying to remember why he was needing to get outside. Underground (c) vs. Guano Apes The Guano Apes, Jackhammer and Sphere, make their way to the ring, in anticipation of their Tag Team Title shot. Static X fires up, bringing out the fWo Tag Team Champions, The Underground, to a very positive reaction from an Atlanta crowd not familiar with quality tag team wrestling. Wretch parades to the ring with his Tag Strap lifted high in the air, while Bear strides with the belt around his waist. They enter the ring and hand the belts to the referee, and right away The Guano Apes charge the champs, catching them by surprise. Jackhammer pounds away at Bear, keeping him on the mat, while Sphere chops viciously at Wretch in the corner. The referee manages to drag Sphere out of the corner, and sends him to the apron. Wretch rolls out of the ring, making Jackhammer and Mike Bear the legal men. Jackhammer jerks Bear to his feet and whips him into the corner, following him in with a shoulder tackle. The blow crumples Bear in two, and Jackhammer nails him with a knee to the face, dropping him to the mat. Jackhammer drops down for the cover, getting two prior to Bear kicking out. Both men get back up, and Jackhammer attempts a short-arm clothesline. Bear ducks under it and connects with a quick neckbreaker. Bear gets to his feet and heads to his corner, tagging in Wretch. The Undeground then grab Jackhammer, hitting him with a double-team suplex, and then a double Irish whip into his corner. Sphere tags himself in, and charges at Wretch as Bear steps out onto the apron. Wretch catches the erstwhile Sphere with a leg lariat, and goes for the quick cover, getting two. Sphere gets back to his feet first, whipping Wretch into the ropes. He drops down for a back body drop, but Wretch sees it coming and nails Sphere with a dropkick to the head. Sphere staggers into The Underground's corner, and is met with a hard right from Mike Bear. Sphere's still dazed when Bear is tagged back in. Bear scoops Sphere up onto his shoulder, then plants him into the mat with a powerslam. Bear's eyes light up, and he brings Sphere up onto his shoulders, in position for a DVD, and then heads for the ropes. He backs into the corner, then climbs up to the second turnbuckle. He delivers the DVD from there, driving Surgeon into the mat. Quickly, Bear tags out to Wretch, who covers Sphere, while Bear himself cuts off Jackhammer, preventing a save. Sure enough, the ref's hand hits the mat three times, giving The Underground another successful title defense. The Guano Apes walk away from the ring a bit disappointed, unsure of when they'll get another shot like this. Result: Underground by Pinfall Courage (c) vs. Black Quicksilver Black Quicksilver's theme music hit the arena speaker system and the crowd instantly let loose a flurry of cheers for the fan favorite. Quicksilver steps out onto the entrance ramp with Stump at his side and surveys the fans. Then with a nod and a smirk on his face, BQ took off for the ring at a jog. He slides in and climbs to the second turnbuckle where he plays to the crowd until Courage's music begins. BQ hops down to the mat and waits for Courage who slowly makes his way down to the ring as he soaks up a chorus of boos. The French Foundation member steps through the ropes into the ring before handing off the belt to a ring attendent. Courage stops and shouts a few jeers at BQ, prompting BQ to fire back with a few of his own. Courage takes personal offense and gets up in the face of Quicksilver, then shoves him. BQ flails back into the corner and shakes his head. BQ steps out of the corner and shakes it off, then proceeds to lock up with Courage. Courage twists Quicksilver's arm into an armdrag, mocking the crowd as he goes. Quicksilver goes down to one knee as Courage retains the hold, driving his shoulder repeatedly into BQ's. Quicksilver gives a quick spurt of offense, though, by switching off his arms and hiptossing Courage to the mat. Courage recovers and charges at BQ, but the challenger is to fast and uses a drop toe hold to bring Courage to the canvas. Courage pops back up and tries it again, but this time BQ dropkicks him in the chest. Courage stumbles back against the ropes and comes right back, catching BQ with a big lariat. Courage lobsters up BQ for the quick pin, but only manages a one count before Quicksilver rolles out. Both men charge at each other, but this time BQ comes out on top by hooking a swinging crucifix around Courage and rolling him up. One... two... Courage kips up and out. Both men get to their feet once again and lock up. Courage grabs BQ in another armdrag, but Quicksilver counters right away with a hairpull that sends Courage to the mat with a thud. Courage rolls to his knees as BQ bounces off the ropes and comes back with a dropkick. Courage takes dual boots to the face and is sent flying back under the bottom rope and to the floor. Quicksilver stays right on top of him, though, by launching himself off the top turnbuckle with a flying crossbody that hits Courage dead on. Both men are sent sprawling about the floor, but its BQ who's up to his feet first. Quicksilver whips Courage into the cades and tries to follow it up with a running bodypress, but Couage moves and BQ crashes hard into the guardrailing. Courage takes a few seconds to catch his breath before eye gouging BQ. As BQ stumbles about blindly Courage grabs a chair and solidly clocks him in the back of the head. Quicksilver falls to the floor in pain while Courage climbs onto the apron and awaits BQ. Quicksilver slowly gets to his feet and turns to see Courage come running off the apron. BQ grabs Stump from a nearby table, though, and introduces Stump to a head-on collision with the champion. BQ rolls Courage back into the ring and stomps him a bit before. heading out onto the apron. BQ pulls back on the top rope and launches himself back onto Courage with a slingshot splash. BQ covers, but can only get a two count. Quicksilver lifts Courage by his hair and headlocks him for a bulldog, but as BQ runs Courage heaves him up and crotches him onto the top rope. BQ sits there, stunned by the pain in his groin area, allowing Courage to climb onto the top rope and deliver a back suplerplex to his opponent. Courage rolls BQ up from the resulting position and, while hooking the tights, picks up the three count to retain the fWo Cruiserweight title. Result: Courage by Pinfall Decisions Kid Cool paced backstage in front of Chris Finn and James Armstrong. The two looked at the new owner expectantly, waiting for what next horror he would inflict upon the company. "I've got it!" Kid Cool's pronouncement came suddenly, and both Finn and Armstrong jumped slightly at the sound. Kid Cool sat down suddenly on the arena floor and sighed. "As much as I like being in charge, I just can't give up the life in the ring. I think I've done something good here tonight... I've given some wrestlers a chance to shine when they usually don't get those opportunities. I hope Finn, that you will take the lesson I've taught here tonight to heart. The whole roster is important... not just a select few." Finn spoke slowly, trying to understand exactly what Kid Cool was saying. "So... you're saying that you're resigning as president?" Kid Cool nodded. "I just want to be a wrestler, and a wrestler I will be. Sure, I'll always be a Beaven, but unless there are any other relatives hanging around... or unless Travis comes back from the dead, ha ha... I hereby grant the power of booking and control over to the fWo executive committee." Chris Finn could hardly contain his excitement, and let out a quick "Whoo yeah!" to no one in particular. Kid Cool stood, and walked away from them. Just as he was nearly out of sight he turned. "Just remember what I did today... and how one man, no matter how little or how young, can make a change for the better." Chris Finn and James Armstrong nodded at his words of wisdom, and Kid Cool gave a short salute. He then turned, and ran face first into the brick wall of the arena. His body slumped backward, and he collapsed into unconsciousness. Finn and Armstrong looked at the prone body of the former president and son of the late commissioner and owner of the fWo. They turned to each other and shrugged. "Come on Finn, let's go get us some burgers..." Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Machina "Come With Me" starts playing as Slaughter goes down to the ring for the Main Event of the show. A large "L" appears on the SlaughterScreen, and with a burst of fireworks Machina, not accompanied by any of the Lone Gunmen, makes his appearance on the entrance ramp, before walking to the ring and rolling in. "Juke Joint Jezebel" begins to play, and the fans boo as the fWo World Champion the Flying Frenchie appears at the entrance ramp, belt around his waist. He slowly and confidently makes his way to the ring, flipping over the top rope and into the ring. He hands his title to the referee, and the bell rings for the match. Frenchie and Machina circle, and Frenchie yawns at his opponent. Machina charges, but Frenchie takes him up and over with a hiptoss. Frenchie then covers Machina with one finger, but Machina kicks out immediately. Machina gets up, and Frenchie looks at him, attempting to discern what maneuver he should attempt next. Frenchie thinks, before heading to the corner and perching himself horizontally across the turnbuckles and ring ropes. Machina is not happy with this disrespect, and thus dumps Frenchie up and over the top rope, where he lands hard on the outside. Frenchie gets up, stunned at the fact that he may have underestimated Machina, and turns towards the ring just in time to see Machina send himself over the top rope with a slingshot somersault plancha, taking Frenchie out. Machina quickly rolls Frenchie back into the ring, and goes for the cover. He gets a two-count before Frenchie kicks out. Machina brings Frenchie up, and backs him into the corner with a series of hard chops. Machina quickly takes a few steps back, before charging at Frenchie and nailing him with a leaping leg lariat in the corner. Frenchie stumbles out, and Machina slingshots himself onto the top rope from the inside before leaping off and dropkicking Frenchie in the back. Machina quickly rolls Frenchie over and attempts a cover, but again Frenchie kicks out. Machina brings TFF up, and whips him into the ropes. Machina catches Frenchie coming off with spinning headscissors, taking him down. Machina quickly heads to the top rope, measuring Frenchie before leaping off with Frenchie's move, a guillotine legdrop. Machina covers Frenchie and hooks the leg, but Frenchie manages to kick out just before three. Machina is somewhat surprised, but brings Frenchie up nonetheless and drops him with a hard DDT. Machina quickly drops a leg and again covers, again getting two. Machina brings TFF up again, and sets him up for a belly-to-belly suplex. However, Frenchie reverses it and takes Machina over with a belly-to-belly of his own. Frenchie takes a moment to recover, before bringing Machina up again. He whips him into the ropes, and catches him coming off with a hard leaping back elbow. Machina stumbles, and TFF goes behind him, hooking him up and dropping him with a back suplex before bridging up and holding the move. The ref counts, but Machina kicks out at two. Frenchie brings Machina up and double underhooks his arms, before taking him over in a suplex. Frenchie holds on, and brings Machina up, arms still double underhooked. He then lifts Machina up and slams him into the mat with a tiger driver. TFF quickly hooks the leg and goes for the cover, but Machina manages to kick out. TFF isn't fazed, and leaps backwards onto the second rope, facing the ring and Machina. He waits for Machina to get up, before leaping off with a dropkick to the face. Instead of covering, Frenchie heads to the ring apron, before slingshotting himself in with a splash. He hooks the leg, but Machina manages to kick out at two. Frenchie brings Machina up, and goes for a kick. However, Machina catches his leg. Frenchie, thinking fast, counters this with an enzuiguri directly to the head of Machina, who slumps down to the mat on his back. Frenchie quickly heads to his legs and takes him up and over with an ocean cyclone suplex. Frenchie signals that it's over, and heads to the top rope to finish off Machina. Frenchie measures him, before leaping off with the 450 splash. However, Machina summons up the strength to move at the last minute and Frenchie strikes the mat hard. The fans' and announcers' attention briefly turns to the entrance ramp, where fWo owner Kid Cool makes his way out, followed by a cavalcade of so-called midcarders such as Waru, Hiroshima, Black Quicksilver, Havok, the Guano Apes, Mike Heftel, and of course Machina's friend and partner Rudo along with Sid Snow. They all stand at the top of the entrance ramp, cheering on Machina to win the title for his fellow "midcarders". Machina sees them and waves, before getting up and heading slowly to the head of Frenchie. He hooks Frenchie up and rolls him over in a la magistral, and the ref is down to make the count. He counts one... two... but Frenchie kicks out. Machina is undaunted, and brings Frenchie up. He scoops him up, looking for a michinoku driver. However, Frenchie rolls through before Machina can drive him into the mat. He has Machina in a pinning position before Machina rolls through with the move himself. Machina is on top, and ref again counts before Frenchie somehow manages to kick out at two. Machina quickly brings him up again, hooking his leg and taking him over in a fishermans suplex. The ref counts, but again Frenchie kicks out at two. Machina quickly makes his way to the ring apron, waiting for Frenchie to get up. When he does, Machina springboards onto the top rope, before taking out Frenchie with a springboard somersault bodyblock. Not satisfied, Machina climbs onto the top turnbuckle, before leaping off onto TFF with a moonsault. Machina hooks both legs, but Frenchie somehow manages to kick out before losing his title. Machina is at a bit of a loss at to what to do, but decides to head up to the top rope again. He does so, and as Frenchie gets up, Machina launches himself at TFF with a flying hurricanrana. However, Frenchie holds on as Machina attempts to take him over, and turns it into a diving powerbomb. Machina hits the mat hard, but Frenchie is also down and out from the punishment he's taken so far. The referee begins to count both men down, but both men make their ways to their feet around the count of seven. Frenchie charges at Machina with a clothesline, but Machina ducks and kicks Frenchie in the gut as he turns around. Machina hooks the head of Frenchie and takes him down with a small package. The ref counts one, two, but Frenchie kicks out. Both men quickly get up, and this time it's Frenchie who leaps at Machina, hooking in a crucifix and taking him down. The ref again counts one, two, but Machina kicks out. Both men are again quickly up, and Machina ducks another Frenchie clothesline, going behind and taking TFF over with a german suplex. The ref counts one, two, but Frenchie kicks out before the three. Both men get up, and Frenchie ducks a Machina lariat before dropping him with a back suplex. He then quickly runs into the ropes and leaps off with an Asai moonsault, nailing Machina directly. Frenchie hooks the leg, but Machina just manages to kick out at two to the relief of the large group of wrestlers at the top of the entrance ramp. Frenchie brings Machina up, and attempts a jawbreaker. However, Machina shoves him away and into the ropes, before attempting to catch TFF with a superkick. However, TFF ducks and goes for a superkick of his own, which is ducked by Machina. Machina kicks Frenchie in the gut as he turns around, before picking him up and dropping him with a northern lights bomb. Machina, tasting the gold, heads to the top to go for the Hope He Doesn't Move 450 splash. He measures Frenchie... leaps off... and Frenchie moves as the midcard crew cringes. Frenchie slowly gets up, before bringing Machina to his feet as well. He hooks Machina's leg, before lifting him up and spiking him with a fishermans buster, leaving Machina in perfect position for TFF to finish him off. Frenchie heads to the top rope, before leaping off and hitting Machina with a picture-perfect Guillotine Legdrop. Frenchie covers Machina, and the ref tolls the three-count to the disgust of the midcarders on the entrance ramp. Frenchie slowly makes his way back to his feet, before being handed his title and raising it high in the air as Slaughter goes off the air... Result: Flying Frenchie by Pinfall
