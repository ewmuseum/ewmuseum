FTF House Show, 9 July 1999

9 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mike Bear vs. Wretch Result: Wretch by Pinfall Wolf Fang vs. Briana Result: Wolf Fang by Submission Joy & Glee vs. Primetime & Nikki Result: Primetime & Nikki by Pinfall Chris Universal & Austin Davis vs. Black Panther & Mick Foley Result: Black Panther & Mick Foley by Pinfall Scott Slugger (c) vs. Rain Result: Rain by Pinfall Ground Zero (c) vs. Stevie Roberts & VL Result: Stevie Roberts & VL by Pinfall Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Death Death came to the ring, flanked by the Dark Riders and slowly entered the cage, looking around for signs of how he might achieve victory. The Frenchie came to the ring with his own support, Mitch Wilson, Primetime and Nikki. Inside the ring both men wrestled a bloody and brutal battle, but it was the Frenchie that gained the upper hand as he climbed the cage wall looking for an escape. Death was too quick however, and caught the Frenchie at the top of the cage, and delivered the vicious Death Dealer from the top of the cage. As both men worked to get to their feet the Frenchie found himself able to climb the cage as Death went for the cage door... but Mitch Wilson slammed it shut in his face, giving the Frenchie an automatic win. But the Frenchie deemed it necessary to inflict additional pain on Death and delivered an 850 splash from the top of the cage, which brought the walls down and demolished the outside of the ring. With victory mere inches away, the Frenchie crawled for the win, but was stopped by James Armstrong... who ripped off his mask to reveal that he was the Silver Fox all along. Handcuffing the Frenchie to the edge of the ring, Death fell outside and gained the victory and the title. Laughing and Death and telling him that he owned his title and victory to him... and that he had still failed on his mission to defeat the Frenchie on his own. Enraged, Death chased after the interloper... Result: Death by Pinfall Ultra Violet (c) vs. Cactus Jon Scott Slugger came to the ring and announced that Ice would be the referee outside the ring, and that he would make sure that one man inside the ring won the fWo World Title, fair and square. While this wording seemed odd, it would make sense as the night unfolded. Scott Slugger called Cactus Jon and Ultra Violet down to the ring, and the two at once began a brutal but surprisingly scientific match, with both Jon and UV staying focused on the match at hand. Outside the ring Jon battered the champion, but Ultra Violet reversed his attack and executed a series of brutal suplexes inside the ring. Ultra Violet delivered a hard splash from the turnbuckle and nearly got the three count, but Slugger was out of position and unable to make the count quickly. Jon worked back, and executed a piledriver which nearly had Ultra Violet out, but with the last bit of strength he had Ultra Violet executed a backdrop brainbuster, but struck his own head in the process. Scott Slugger started the ten count, but when he got to nine he quickly signaled Ice inside the ring and covered both Jon and Ultra Violet. Ice made one of the fastest counts in fWo history, and Scott Slugger quickly grabbed the fWo World Heavyweight title and made his way up the entrance ramp, laughing at the scam he just pulled over the fWo. Waving the title in the air, Scott Slugger left the scene while Violet helped Ultra Violet to his feet... but inadvertently crushed a small crystal she was carrying as the cameras and world went off the air... Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall
