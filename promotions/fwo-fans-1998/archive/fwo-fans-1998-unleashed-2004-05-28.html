Introduction The Seventh year of the fWo begins with all eyes on the ring... but it is behind the curtains where the attention should be placed? Prodding the Raver A lot was on Nerva's mind as she stood in front of the curtain. Crusader was still recovering from his injuries suffered from the fall he took at CyberSlam. Now she had no one to lean on and no one to stand behind. She was all by herself now, and it was time to stand alone in the fWo as Brooklyn's Dangerous Daughter. "Shiny Disco Balls" took over the speakers. It was her new theme music for now. She grabbed the microphone from her jeans and breathed in and out. She walked out on stage, rendering an onslaught of boos from the crowd. Nerva walked down the ramp, trying her best to ignore the rude comments directed at her. She slid into the ring and paced around while her music faded out. She looked at each camera around her and then surveyed the crowd. "I'm sure you hear a lot of things about Amy Campbell," she said. "It seems she has a lot of those die-hard fans. Sure, you all heard about how she's been raped. You all know about how she took a bullet for her husband. Hell, you all even know the color of her eyes. Over the past two years she's been with the fWo, you've all grown rather fond and attached to her. You all feel you know her like the back of your hand. You all love her." The fans cheered. Nerva's face was stoic to the crowd response. The camera caught a close-up and it was up on the FanTron. Her eyes were green stones and her mouth was a straight line. When the crowd quieted, she spoke again. "I bet you all loved it when she punked me out last week." Hardly to her surprise, the crowd cheered again. It took a moment longer for them to quiet down. "You won't be loving her for much longer. Let me make that a guarantee. I know something about her, and it's not some childish, harmless secret. Amy walks around like she's got nothing to hide. Like she's the ultimate bad girl who's afraid of nothing. Pfft." Nerva turned to one of the cameras and pointed at it. "Amy, the truth about you has helped me see through it all. I'm going to expose you, you overconfident bitch. Last Monday you showed the world that your business is far from done with me. Well, I'm going to say this to you now: "We're not done for a long, long time. Hell, we've only chipped at the surface of this iceberg. You haven't seen the real me yet. And being frank, these fans haven't seen the real you yet. The truth is coming out, Amy. It's gonna piss you off and I'm going to love every moment of it. Do you know why? Because you've made it my life's passion right now to drive you down to depths you never thought existed." Nerva took a moment to catch her breath and looked around at the booing crowd. She waited and looked to the entrance way. She expected Aimz to arrive on the scene. But there was no such arrival. "Well, I don't hear your music, Amy, so I guess you're not paying me a visit out here tonight. But if you were hearing this - and I hope you were - I'll be in my locker room. I'm looking forward to seeing you again." Nerva dropped the microphone, which cued her music to come back on. She slid out of the ring and walked back up the entrance ramp to the backstage area. Honor It was still early in the event known as Unleashed when the lights seemingly began to short-circuit. Over the speakers, new, foreign sounds began to play- sounds that seemed incredibly haunting and intimidating. The lights continued to go on and off as the music switched between these haunting noises and a pulsing techno beat. Finally, the lights went out for good as the sound of violins filled the arena. This strange piece of music was "Suite [Dragon of Earth]" by Naoki Sato, and as it played the colors of blue and red flashed around the arena. Finally, the spotlights focused on the stage, and instantly the sight made the crowd erupt in boos. Standing to the back with one of his huge arms out-stretched was the seven-foot-tall monster known only as Exel Pure. In front of him and to his right with both of his arms raised in the air was the masked X, his muscular frame adorned by a black-and-red X shirt while his lower body was covered by black tights with the red letter X on them. The man who bore more than a striking resemblance to Xias turned to his left, where standing with her arms outstretched in the crucifix position was the beautiful angel whose beauty was only matched by her viciousness. Her long blue hair flowed downward, past her shoulders. The upper portion of her face was covered by a white half-mask, covering her eyes and most of her nose, but leaving her mouth bare. She wore blue-and-white wrestling attire with one noticeable difference- a Celtic cross adorning her tights on her right pant leg. She was the Avenging Angel. No.....they were all avenging angels. They were the Avenging Angels. And she was their leader. She was Eternity. The trio made their way down the rampway slowly and confidently as the hauntingly beautiful instrumental continued to play over the speakers. The group had no care for what the fans were saying- the fact that the fans of the promotion they hoped to bury underneath the ground were cursing their very names meant nothing to them. They were better than the fWo fans, they truly believed, just as they were better than the fWo. They believed that the entire organization was beneath them- and as they entered the ring on this night, they were dead-set on proving it. The crowd's booing raised in decibel as Eternity was handed a microphone, the music stopped, and the lights returned. She ignored them as she began to speak. "It's been one month since we showed up on the fWo's doorstep." Eternity began. "It has been one month since we first showed the promotion the fury, the pure rage we had to express towards it. But we have not even begun to show it the extent of that rage. Even this past Monday night, when we destroyed a human being inside this ring." The crowd booed as the footage from Monday Night Ruahh~!! was cued up on the fWoTron. Exel Pure, meanwhile, was more concerned with laying a hurting on High Flyer, as he picked the poor man up and rocked him with multiple forearms to the skull, knocking him backwards into the ropes. The seven-foot-tall man whipped him into the opposite ropes and then drilled him with a hard lariat, nearly taking Flyer right out of his boots as he hit the canvas. He motioned to X, who leaned over and picked Flyer up. He hooked Flyer for another move that fWo fans knew very well- the Exploder Suplex '98. The head and neck of Flyer was driven into the canvas by the current finishing move of Xias, and again that fact wasn't lost on the fans. A loud "SELLOUT!" chant had begun to make its way through the rafters, but if X heard them, he ignored them. Eternity, for her part, had recovered and gone outside to retrieve a chair, throwing the poor timekeeper off of his and folding it up. She slid back into the ring as X and Pure picked the United States Champion back to his feet, holding each arm to the side. Eternity snickered as she looked at him, pulled the chair over her head, then hit him right over the skull with an incredibly sick, unprotected chair shot. Thanks to her cronies, Flyer was unable to get his hands over his head, and the result was obvious as soon as X pulled his head back off the canvas- he was bleeding, and badly. This only made Eternity laugh, and she dragged him back up off the canvas, showing no signs of any mercy for the wounded Flyer. Instead, she put him in position for her signature move, starting on the opposite side of him in an inverted DDT position (only with her body facing the opposite direction of Flyer) before leaping over him and driving his head down into the steel chair in one motion. The Heaven's Drop, only twice as painful thanks to the inclusion of the chair. But the group wasn't done yet. No, they wanted to make sure the United States Champion would never be the same again after this night- so they handed him off to the seven-foot-tall monster of the group. Exel Pure took Flyer onto the top rope with him, and as X set the chair up down below, he leaped off from the top turnbuckle with his Exel Bomb, driving the head, neck, and shoulders of Flyer into the cold, hard steel. The man very well may have had permanent damage caused to his head and neck area after such a brutal assault- and finally, the group decided it was enough. "HEAVEN'S DRIVE" began to play as the sickening assault ended, all three raising arms in the center of the ring to the loud, impassioned boos of the crowd. Eternity smiled a sick, disgusting smile as the footage faded. "That man's name is Jack Herman." she began. "I don't know the man personally, but I'm sure he's got people who care about him. I'm sure he even has people who love him. Nowadays, it's almost like all of you wrestling fans have seen so much violence, so much depravity, that it doesn't even effect you anymore, but it effects us. Do you think we enjoyed doing that to him on Monday? Do you think we enjoyed beating him down without mercy until his face was literally covered in blood? Do you think we enjoyed snapping his neck down onto steel in multiple different ways? The man could have been permanently paralyzed. When Pure took him to the top rope and dropped him into the center of the ring, his neck hitting the steel chair and causing a very audible sound on impact, he could have lost all feeling in his legs and never walked again. None of us even know Jack Herman- none of us hate him. That alone should show you how dedicated we are to our cause, how dedicated we are to seeing this promotion that abuses its own employees, that lets them go out and kill themselves for a bunch of uncaring bastards, dead and buried. What makes me the most sick to my stomach is after we did what we did on Ruahh....you people were still able to cheer and enjoy yourself for the Main Event. You people reacted like you just saw a cartoon character be physically dissected inside this ring.....he was a HUMAN BEING, you insensitive assholes! He was a living, breathing person who had to be sacrificed for the greater good, and none of you could even bother to care! All of you make me SICK!" X and Pure nodded their heads in agreement besides Eternity, who paused to collect her thoughts following the emotional outburst. "But perhaps I'm putting too much blame on all of you. After all, you are the fans of an organization who sends a wrestler with multiple previous concussions to wrestle in one of the most physically demanding environments of all time, just to make a few extra bucks. Maybe it isn't your fault that a dishonorable organization has trained you to be uncaring. Perhaps it's the master that we must put the greater fault too. Or perhaps.....perhaps we should turn our attention to its underlings. The wrestlers of the fWo. Now, you might not know this about us, but we possess something called honor." Chris Finn had to keep himself from laughing at ringside on commentary as Eternity said this, then referred to all the times they've attacked people 3-on-1 and caused countless DQs. The fans apparently agreed with him, engaging in a loud "BULLSHIT!" chant. "Now now, I know you probably don't believe me, but hear me out. We have honor because we never back down, and we always let you know what our mission is. We came out here and we told you that it is our goal to end the existence of the Fans Wrestling Organization by any means possible. Any wrestler who steps in this ring knows that they will be going up against not a single person, not a single tag team, but a cohesive unit dedicated to bringing down this entire promotion. The fact is, during our month here, only two wrestlers have stepped up to protect the honor of their company. Both of them, High Flyer and Deacon, have my respect....which is why it will pain me when I have to end High Flyer's career inside this steel cage later tonight, but messages must be sent and things can't be helped. But as I was saying.....only two wrestlers have stepped up to defend this organization's honor against us. All of you fans condemn us for attacking with greater numbers, but to be quite frank, it is not our problem that greater numbers won't assist them. If the fWo locker room really wanted to get off its collective ass, it could send twenty guys out here to attack us, and I would not complain- they would simply be defending the honor of their organization. The fact that only two have bothered to step up proves my point about this organization- and it also proves that the majority its underlings share the promotion's lack of honor. So, I suppose what I am doing now is calling out anyone right now who wishes to fight us. I am giving anyone in the back a chance to defend their promotion's honor. I don't care if there's one of you, two of you, three of you, or ten of you- if you want to prove me wrong, if you want to shut me up, come on out here and try it. Show some honor for once in your pathetic lives. Stand up for your organization and show us that you believe in something. Or perhaps, perhaps I'm right after all. Hmm. What a shock."
