Randall Is Next

29 July 2000

fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE RANDALL IS NEXT July 29, 2000 The man who won the first Survivor battle royal and first immunity challenge was the forth man eliminated in the fWo Survivor contest. Ronnie Randall, the unusually calm performer, was eliminated from the contest by eight of his voting peers, and by his own absent vote. Randall had missed a vote in the past, although due to his immunity it ultimately had no effect on his standing on the contest. Despite Randall being voted out forth, his absent vote was not the one that tipped the scale in his favor like it was for Mike Extreme one week ago. In an unusual change from the normal voting strategy, Randall was voted out largely because of his success inside the ring during the wrestling shows. One wrestler summed up the change in strategy with a shrug. "As the fWo Survivor contest goes on it looks more and more like the immunity challenges are going to be what saves your fat from the fire. Randall won that battle royal, and he's been close in a lot of the other events. If he lasts in the contest the immunity challenges he wins are going to put him right into the winner's seat. Randall... I love you man... so I'm real sorry about this." Randall's voting marks the fourth wrestler to be eliminated, and officially ending the first third of the contest. Spirits in the fWo Survivor contest raised briefly this weekend, as the wrestlers got a day off of voting and doing shows, something many felt was greatly needed. "These people have been pushing themselves really hard... they need a break." Stevie Roberts said. "A few of them are going to be heading home for the weekend, others just relaxing with the nice weather. We are in Texas for two shows next week... so plenty of hot weather and intensive travel is in their future." The votes came as follows: Ronnie Randall: 5 votes, 1 absent vote, 6 total votes Arch Angel: 1 vote Suicide: 1 vote Ruben Ross: 1 vote Ironically, Ronnie Randall had the most diverse spellings of his name of any member in the fWo Survivor contest from people voting for him.
