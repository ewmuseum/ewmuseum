Introduction The Joe Louis Arena was packed. Fans were, as the late Gorilla Monsoon would say, literally hanging from the rafters. As Matt Panzer prepared everyone for fWo Sunday Night Slaughter inside the arena, the fans counted down to what was expected to be a gigantic edition of the show. In the past, it had been an overused cliche. "The biggest moment in the history of our sport." had been used one too many times. But tonight... it didn't seem like a cliche, it seemed like the truth. The countdown reached zero, and fireworks went off at strategically placed locations throught the building. The foundation of the arena was rocked from the ground up. Music blared loudly; as Jeremiah Frankton & James Armstrong prepared to call the action. Panzer stood in the ring ready to introduce the participants of the first match, but he was interrupted. Blue, red & white strobelights flickered off near the SlaughterStage, and out walked three men, as "Christian Says" by Tones On Tail echoed throughout the city of Detroit. Standing on the right, was the brash, arrogant, twenty-two year old Primetime; with one half of the fWo World Tag Team Titles draped over his right shoulder. Primetime looked at the booing fans; and sneered. Standing on the left, was the most recent Grand Slam Winner, The Flying Frenchie; with one half of the fWo World Tag Team Titles draped over his left shoulder. Frenchie looked at the crowd, who were undoubtedly calling him profanity laced names, and he beamed with pride. Standing in between the tag team champions, was the unconceivably victorious Ric Chronos, who won the most coveted prize in the business last night - the fans Wrestling organization World Heavyweight Championship. Chronos was fresh off two wars with Douglas & The Boston Strangler last night, and looked a little worse for wear. These men were the representatives of the fWo. These men were champions. These men... were 3vil. And in unison, they began walking down the ramp as the fans continued to tell them how they felt. But 3vil didn't care. They walked into Bloodbath with a mission, and no matter how impossible that mission seemed going on, they completed their mission. Frenchie walked up to the ring steps nearest him and stood near the ring post before looking at the fans and mouthing off to them in his native language. Primetime took to his nearest corner and began telling the fans they were not worthy to be in their presence. Chronos slowly rolled into the ring, as he was still showing the effects of what happened just twenty four hours ago on pay-per-view. Chronos asked for a microphone, while Primetime & Frenchie joined him in the ring. After the music came to a halt, Chronos stared at the audience (who continued to jeer the three men), before speaking. "Last night at Bloodbath... we reigned supreme! We came into Bloodbath with three tasks... and each of those tasks we completed successfully. Last night... we celebrated in the streets of Cincinnati until the early hours of this morning. What they witnessed last night was nothing short of the beginning of a new era here in the fWo. We, as a group, set new heights for the fWo. We have set the standard here in the fWo - and the standard is excellence. We proved that nobody in the fWo is on our level. Rather, like you the fans... they're all below it." The crowd didn't take too kindly to that comment, and they started up a "3vil sucks" chant. 3vil simply laughed. "Well, if -WE- suck, the fWo must suck a lot more! And that is why... continuing tonight... we will continue to set the standard of excellence here in the fWo. We will continue to raise the bar higher and higher. Last night we achieved the biggest of our goals, but we have more goals. We are not finished. The fWo is still diseased... but we are still going to cure it. In time, we will in fact... bring the entire organization back up to the level it was once at. Last night was only the beginning. The Boston Strangler... a man who you cheer... a man who you singlehandedly chose to represent the organization you follow so dearly and admire... was a defeated & broken man last night. The East Coast Laws... they proved that they are not on the level of these two legends in the ring with me - hell, they're not even in the same league." (Frankton was heard on commentary disputing that fact, feeling like many others that the East Coast Laws could have won the match last night had Chronos not gotten himself involved.) "And Douglas... well, I'll give you credit, but only because you took whatever I threw at you. But even that wasn't good enough; and you failed to defeat me for the Internet Title. But that was last night... and tonight... our domination continues. We're not satisfied... to erase the wrong things that have happened in the past... the injustices that have happened over the last year or so, we must continue. And that's why tonight... Primetime will join the Flying Frenchie as a Grand Slam Champion, because he only has one title left to win, and that's the fWo Hardcore Title. Tonight... Hellrazor will be just like that cat he carries around with him, because Primetime is going to prove that like Lamont, he's just a pussy when compared to Primetime." The fans booed at what Chronos said, because Hellrazor was of course a hero in their eyes, and Lamont? Well, he was like a God. "And The Flying Frenchie here... he will be taking on Monte Burns for the fWo Cruiserweight Title. I know, Monte... I can tell you're already starting to get nervous, knowing that you just won that title, but you will have to deal with it. Just try not to make a mess of yourself... because if you do, we'll be forced to call you the Golden Shower, instead of the Golden Child." 3vil laughed, as the fans continued to boo them and yell unnice things about them. "I, on the other hand, will have the night off. I went through two wars last night, and since I already have the fWo World Title, and the fWo Internet Title... well, there's no reason for me to compete here tonight." Frenchie & Primetime nodded, besides, what would Chronos do? Compete for the Jobberweight Title? Actually... "Do you buy what The Sellout is selling?" ... someone else had something to say about it. And the fans had something to cheer for. 3vil stood in the ring with angry expressions written on their face, while the owner of the fWo - Chris Universal - had an amused expression on his.
