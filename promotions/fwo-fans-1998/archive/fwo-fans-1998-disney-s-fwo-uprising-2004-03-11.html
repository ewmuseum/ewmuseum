Introduction In the land of pain and sorrow there are times when even the greatest of us question our own humanity, and our own soul. It is a question that has begun to circulate more and more in the fWo locker room, for what the members face is beyond simple agression... it goes far deeper. Spousal Forces Re-Aligned Some say that separation brings people closer together. This concept rang true for Jeremy and Shirley Savage, no matter how cliche it might seem. Any argument, any squabble, could be cured with just a little distance...just a little time away from the hostilities of married life. Jeremy's drinking problem was getting to Shirley, causing her to run off with friends for another week-long getaway. Sometimes she wished it would last much longer and in even the most desperate times, wished the Savage separation would last forever. But the slates were clean now. Jeremy was getting his life back in order, and she was feeling more confident about re-entering match work in fWo. Stepping foot in the arena gave Shirley a comforting warmth, like a hug between old friends. Any other day, she would run off and chit-chat with a staffer or fellow superstar; anyone but her husband. At first her avoidance was intentional, but with time grew to a point where she would simply do it out of habit. Tonight, she would eviscerate her vice and track down her husband, who was rumored to be clean of his heavy drinking. She traipsed down the corridor, re-orienting herself to the fWo backstage. She shared smiles with wrestling workers as they scrambled around her to assure Uprising's success. She even caught the attention of VIP fans, who commented on her looks as their focussed eyes tracked her across the room. She looked in the first lockerroom, but did not find her spouse. She did this to about twelve more rooms before pausing for a drink. As she drank from a flimsy paper cup, her eyes surveyed the rest of the hallway. Her mind racing with hypothetical coordinates to where her husband might be located. She dropped her empty cup in a trash receptacle and headed down the hallway, deciding to search the remaining rooms. At the end of the hallway, she found him. He was sitting in a room, all by his lonesome and a great distance away from any human contact. She saw him sigh deeply, and stare blankly at the ground. It seemed as if he were lost, and preoccupied by nothing as he took a long sip from his can of Diet Coke. She snuck up behind him and covered his eyes with her hands. "Guess who." Jeremy grabbed one of her hands and kissed her tenderly on its palm. "Welcome back, Kotter." Jeremy said demurely, holding back his feelings of happiness and joy. However, he couldn't hold it much longer, eventually springing from his chair and attacking Shirley with a tight hug. "So, why are you sitting back here all by yourself?" Shirley asked. Jeremy shrugged in their hug, not really making his answer specific. "Don't worry about me. How was your trip?" Jeremy asked while breaking from the hug and looking her in the eyes. "It was fun, but I wish you were there with me," Shirley said while looking up at him with a shy, smiling face. He countered with a "Me too" before going on... "Well, while you were away, I became an Internet title contender," Jeremy remarked, trying his hardest not to sound arrogant. Shirley seemed impressed and gave him a peck on the mouth. "That's so great sweetheart." "Why are you calling me sweetheart?" Jeremy shot back firmly, "I thought we were divorced!" Shirley looked at him a way befitting a puzzled person. Jeremy flashed a wry smile. "That is the rumor around these parts." Shirley's lips formed a perfectly circle shape; her face hinted surprise. She shook her head before standing on her tip-toes to kiss Jeremy again. "Whatever gave them that idea?" "It beats me. But when you left last time, people thought it was for good. They even had me believing them--" Shirley sharply grabbed his cheeks and pulled his face to hers. "I love you, Jeremy. I would never allow that to happen," She assured before patting his chest and backing up, "I am so impressed at the progress you've made while I was gone. I just wish I were here to experience it with you." "Well, at least you'll get your chance to experience me later tonight," Jeremy flirted. Shirley snickered at his less-than-cavalier advance. The two kissed one last time, hooked elbows, and departed the room. The idea that Tohru was defenseless had now left his head as the more athletic member of Kodane Otaku bounded backwards to a neutral corner. Walden forced himself up to his knees and clapped a bit acknowledging Tohru for his thought process. Tohru responding by nodding to Walden as the 'Taxman' got back to his vertical base and headed towards John Tohru. The two men locked up in a collar and elbow tie up which quickly led to a hip toss. John Tohru was now on his back and Walden was brushing off his pants. Walden casually strode over a bit of arrogance in his step and tagged in Jane Matheson. Matheson grasped the top rope and vaulted over it charging into Johnny T just as he regained his vertical. As if by reaction, Tohru put a hip into the charging Matheson and sent her over his shoulder and onto her back. Shock covered John's face as he realized he had just 'Struck a woman'. John quickly knelt down and helped Matheson to her feet. Her response to his kindness? Jane stepped up on John's bent leg using him to vault her body upwards. As her knee connected with the side of his head and the fans let out a roar of appreciation, Johnny Tohru realized he couldn't beat her if he wouldn't hit her. His answer to such a conundrum? Ichabod... Michaels.. John rolled away from Jane and the middle of the ring and slapped Ichabod on the shoulder. Ichabod turned, showing visible pain just from the slap of the tag. His eyes widened as it set in that he was the legal man. Over the crowds chants the camera captured the words "Ichabod I can't hit a girl." Ichabod turned to his partner, a look of desperate plea on his face. "Then grapple her you big lummox!" John turned back to look at Jane as she grew more and more furious at the delay. Suddenly his face turned flush red with embarrassment. Does the idea of grappling a girl really make a nerd react this way? Oh yes it does... Ichabod shook his head and stepped into the ring for an instant before tagging John Tohru back in. As he stepped back out he yelled at John. "She will kill me!" The two locked grasps and John put a shoulder into her ribs lifting her off the ground. Matheson struggled but to no avail as Tohru carried her over to Walden and tagged him in for her.. Tim laughed as he got in, these two kids were amusing. One wouldn't fight women and the other, he wouldn't fight at all... Tim stepped in and smiled before waving John Tohru in. Tohru responded by sprinting at him only to eat turnbuckle after a Walden drop toe hold. Walden followed that by sliding out of the ring and elbowing Tohru in the jaw. Tohru responded with an open hand and very awkward looking palm strike to Walden. Walden spun on his heel as Tohru popped up and grabbed the ropes a quick vault sent him over the top in a Suicida Plauncha. Both men hit the floor in a lump of body and slowly began to move. Walden pulled himself up first and grabbed Tohru by the hair, sliding him into the ring and following. As both men rose to their feet John again rushed Walden looking for a Yakuza Kick. Walden lowered a shoulder however and raised up before flipping Tohru over in a leg capture suplex. Both men lay on the mat. The match thus far had them both slightly winded and for John Tohru that was more than he wanted as his entire body ached. All the parts of his mind screamed three words.. End It Now... John rolled over with that single thought in his head and as Walden pulled himself up he grabbed Tohru and whipped him to the ropes. But as he lowered his head he telegraphed his move and found himself in a standing facelock. John Tohru had seen his favorite wrestlers do it a hundred times but as he planted his feet and lifted Tim Walden he realized how hard a suplex really was. Both men crashed to the mat but John did not let go, instead he rolled his hips and pulled himself and Walden up again for a second suplex. This second suplex carried more oomph and more snap. As it ended Tohru again refused to release only spinning his hips again and forcing both men to their feet a third time however this time would be differant. Walden struggled as John reached to hook his leg for the Otaku Buster but it seemed fruitless as the slightly larger man locked his hands and picked Walden up. What goes up must come down, Tim Walden was wishing he wasn't coming down on his skull but it was too late for that as John Tohru fell back in the Otaku Buster. John may not have had much experience in a real ring but his time on Fire Pro and hours of puro tapes told him it was time to pin. 1.. 2... 3...
