Fan Board Posting Guidelines (February 2001)

28 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization, Fan Message Board Fan Board FAQ Fan Board Posting Guidelines The following are a few simple guidelines that will help you to avoid having your post removed from this board, and give you an idea of what the fWo Fan Board is all about. The below examples are all reasons why a post can be removed from the board. 1. Duplicate postings. Someone posts the exact same thing multiple times. The last post is kept, the earlier exact duplicates are pulled. At times this can be the fault of the server or your host, and thus an honest mistake. It's certainly not something that we blame you for, unless it's specificly designed to be spam. We don't tolerate spam and hopefully neither does your internet service provider. 2. Flaming/attacks. Specifically, this means spam (intended multiple posts) filled with such wonders as "FUCK FWO FUCK FWO FUCK FWO CAN I JOIN THE FWO" and so on. This *does not* mean questioning something you have trouble with, it means posting material with *no intention* of getting anything answered or changed. It can also mean coming onto the site with the intention of starting trouble with a particular person or the rest of the site and doing little else. Personal attacks with little else to back it up have no place on the boards. Attacks on the fWo don't either. Sure, we welcome constructive criticism and if you don't like a particular angle we ran you're more than welcome to say that, but saying the fWo is "nothing more than eye candy with a domain name" without proof to back it up is not tolerated. 3. Shills for your site. The rules for this are by and large fairly simple. Would you like it if someone use the site you paid for as means to advertise their work? Likely not, unless you gave them permission. That's the general feeling here. We have no problems with posters providing a link to a site IF it comes during conversation about the fWo or other things on the board. However, if you're just here on the site to tell us to visit your site, please don't waste your time. Your post will be pulled by any number of people who have the authority to do so, thus your post likely won't be seen by as many people as you would hope. Not only that, but when the time comes in the future when you would like the fWo's help in some manner... why would we? 4. Insults. When a thread degenerates into nothing but name calling, it's not valuable to be on open boards anymore. That *is* a place for email, but if it has nothing to do with us please don't drag us into it. Messages should be something that anyone can join in on. If you're in a fight with someone else about who is the better roleplayer, nobody will likely care, and the messages will more than likely be deleted. 5. All CAPS. Post your message in all-caps, and away it goes. No, seriously. Away it goes. At this time on the internet, there is no excuse for such insanity in your postings. At the same time, if you want to be taken seriously around here a simple proofread before you post wouldn't hurt. People like to have discussions... and we want discussions here. The discussions are much easier to read though if your messages are. If you follow those five simple rules, and don't ask any questions about joining the fWo or Poser; your posts will more than likely stay up on the board. Thanks, fWoWrestling.com Staff
