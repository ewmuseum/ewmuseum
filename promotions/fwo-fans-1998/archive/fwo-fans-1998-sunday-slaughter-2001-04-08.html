Introduction The whole routine was beginning to become old hat to him, mused the Boston Strangler as he looked down on the envelope on the desk. Open the letter, take the name and the money, and do the hit. Still, he couldn't help but be disappointed. He had hoped this was over. The last hit he could remember for sure having been contacted to do was Vic Creed, and that was a long time ago. There had been other incidents since then, and quite frankly, he had no idea whether he was behind those or not. All he knew was that he did Creed, he didn't do the Brittany kidnapping, and there was another target out there, waiting for him inside that envelope. He picked it up, tore it open. A wad of bills fluttered out, all hundreds no doubt. Inside was a piece of paper. Two words were typed on it. "Chris Universal." Chris... Universal? The Strangler contemplated the consequences of this action for a moment, then decided that they wouldn't really matter. He was already damned anyway. As he turned to go, thoughts of the last Slaughter intruded on his mind. "Chris." The Destroyer had said that name, and seemed to have been choking out another before he passed out. Chris was behind Brittany's kidnapping. Chris... It all clicked home for the Boston Strangler. The harassment from Universal, stepped up recently. The Destroyer's word. The hit he had been assigned to. Chris Universal had orchestrated the kidnapping of Brittany. Why? The Strangler found he didn't particularly care. He set out for Chris Universal's office. Jackhammer vs. Vengence "War?" by System Of A Down hits and Stump Squad's newest member walks out to a mild reaction. The big man made an impressive comeback, after Black Quicksilver's victory at DOA. He turned on a former partner, in Sphere. Now, he's set to face to face a man just as big and mean as himself. A man who goes by the moniker Vengence. A man whose soul was ravaged at a young age, something from which he has never recovered. Walking out to "The Beast" by the Fugees, this big, mean, man intends to make his presence felt. He steps over the top rope and comes face to face with his opponent. Both men stand face-to-face. For one of the few times in his career, Vengence is shorter than his opponent. The two trade blows, back and forth, trying to show who the "bigger" man truly is. Jackhammer blocks a punch by Vengence and connects with his opponent's face, in one fluid motion, sending Vengence to one knee. Jackhammer continues to beat down Vengence, sending him sprawling to the mat. Vengence is quick to get back to his feet and the two start the exchange of punches, all over again. Deciding he's had enough of the all-out brawl, Vengence reels Jackhammer in, and tries to overpower him from a closer range. Vengence goes for a German suplex, but is blocked by the taller Jackhammer whose huge forearm comes down and hits Vengence's back. Vengence pulls his head up, and unintentionally nails Jackhammer under the chin with it, which sends Jackhammer back against the ropes. Vengence walks forward and throws the dizzy Jackhammer against toward the ropes on the other side of the ring. Hitting the ropes, Jackhammer has one goal in mind, to take off Vengence's head. It seems as though Vengence has the same goal, which may be the reason they nail each other with clotheslines at the same time. Both men lay on the mat, and the ref counts... He gets up to seven before either man stirs. At this time, both men get to their knees and the ref stops the count. On their knees they start to throw punches and they continue to do so until they reach their feet. Former Hardcore champion, Vengence gains the advantage and pummels Jackhammer with a series of right hands, that nearly knock the big man off his feet. Vengence whispers something to Jackhammer, something Jackhammer readily agrees to, and the two men lock up in the center of the ring. Both men struggle back and forth, wanting to prove that they're the stronger of the two. But, every time Jackhammer seems to gain an advantage, Vengence pulls back into the strength contest, and vice versa. Finally, realizing that this will take all day, Jackhammer pulls back and boots Vengence in the stomach. Surprised by his opponent's action, Jackhammer leans over in pain, where he's promptly met with a boot to the face. Getting back to his feet, with his face flushed red with anger, Vengence goes after Jackhammer with a clothesline, it sends Jackhammer to the mat. Vengence waits until Jackhammer reaches his feet and tries to kick him in the face - but it appears as though he's forgotten that Jackhammer is bigger than most of his normal opponents, and Vengence catches Jackhammer's foot and throws him down hard to the mat. He then gets Vengence in the HammerFall and nails him. The ref counts to three and the match is over. The crowd cheers, until they see Sphere running down the ramp, toward Jackhammer. He assaults Jackhammer from behind, trying to knock the big man down, but Jackhammer refuses to fall. Finally, Jackhammer turns around and meets Sphere with a couple of quick punches, before clotheslining him over the top rope. Outside the ring, Sphere fumes, and Jackhammer gestures for a microphone. One is thrown to him, and he catches it deftly. He wastes no time in beginning to speak. "Sphere... I'm a simple man, and I'm going to make this real simple. I'm sick and damn tired of you, and I want to teach you a lesson once and for all. You... me... CyberSlam," growled Jackhammer. Sphere chuckled outside the ring, then pointed back and forth between himself and Jackhammer and mouthed "CyberSlam?" A mic was handed to him, and he started to speak. "You're o " "Hold on, boy, I'm not done yet. I want a stipulation. Not just any stip, oh no. I want a... barbed wire match!" The crowd cheered loudly at this, and Sphere gulped. He thought things over for a moment, then shrugged. "What the hell, you're on! And Jack, at CyberSlam, I'm gonna rip you a new asshole... and I mean that in the most literal sense of the term." Sphere tossed the mic away and raised his arms to boos, causing fans nearby to shrink away, holding their noses. Jackhammer shouted threats in the ring as Slaughter went backstage... A rich and powerful voice spoke. "CyberSlam. It's coming. At CyberSlam, men become heroes. Heroes become legends. Legends become immortal. It's coming. It is the one day where everything matters. The one day where every action is etched in history." Greg Davis (c) vs. Wretch As Pearl Jam and Neil Young blasts over the speakers, the fans uproar themselves in cheers. Wretch makes his way out and poses at the entrance ramp, causing a chain reaction of flashbulbs going off. He walks to the ring, pointing at various signs with his name on them. As he warms up in the ring, "Eye of the Tiger" breaks over his music, causing the fans to turn their cheers into boos for Greg Davis. Davis pushes the curtains aside and stands at the ramp pointing at his fWo Cruiserweight Title. He walks to the ring, pointing and laughing at overweight people in the crowd, before climbing up the steps and entering the ring. He hands the title off to the referee, who holds it up in the air and calls for the starting bell. The two start in a grapple, with Wretch going down and tossing Greg with an arm drag takeover. Greg immediately gets up, but Wretch catches him in a headlock and immediately flips him to the mat in a neutralized position. Greg tries kicking out of the headlock with great force, but Wretch keeps it intact. Finally, Greg manages to squirm his way to a standing position and deliver an elbow to Wretch's gut. Wretch doubles over and, before he knows it, falls victim to a wicked knee-lift to the forehead. Wretch staggers back, and Greg chops him across the chest. Wretch fires back with a chop, but Greg hits another one that sends Wretch into the corner. Davis zeros in on Wretch's mid-section with well-placed kicks. Knowing that the wind is knocked out of him, Greg grabs Wretch and whips him into the opposite corner. He charges in at full-speed, but comes out of the corner with a hard spinning heel kick that puts Greg down. Wretch makes the cover, but Greg forcefully kicks out at two. Wretch pounds on Greg with a few stomps and then brings him up. He whips him off to the ropes and manages to catch him with a reverse elbow smash. Greg goes down, but Wretch keeps the pressure on by bringing him up and hooking him up. Seconds later, Greg finds himself flat on the mat courtesy of a suplex. Wretch pumps a fist up for the crowd and then heads to the top rope, before leaping off. The head butt he's looking for doesn't land its mark, as Greg moves out of the way. Seeing Wretch prone, he immediately grabs the arm and tries to lock him in the cross-face, but Wretch proves to be technically sound enough to squirm and roll his way out of the predicament. Before Wretch can get to his feet, Greg hits him with a dropkick. Wretch gets up and falls into Greg's grasp, before being taken over with an exploder suplex. Wretch gets up and ducks under Greg's attempt at a clothesline with a waist lock. Greg sends his elbow back to connect with Wretch's face, and then successfully executes a standing switch. Greg picks up Wretch and drops him with an atomic drop, before quickly heading to the top. As if on instinct, Wretch trips Greg's feet from under him and then proceeds to head up on the top strand beside the perched cruiserweight champion. Before Wretch can attempt anything, however, Greg elbows him in the gut, causing him to trip and be crotched on the top strand. Greg regains his balance and then takes Wretch over with a hurricanrana into the ring. Greg gets down on his knees to make the cover, but takes a second to raise his arms up at the crowd. He eventually makes the cover, but he only scores a near-fall on Wretch. He brings Wretch up and slams him to the mat. As he tries to head for the ropes, however, Wretch trips him down and then manages to tie his legs up in a grapevine. Davis struggles for the ropes, but Wretch keeps him in place by hooking in a three-quarter nelson. Greg screams in pain, but refuses to give in. He reaches out for the bottom rope, but knowing that he's too far, Greg slides himself forward with his free hand and manages to grab it. Angered, Wretch grabs both of Greg's legs and drops him to the mat... right in the middle of the ring. He takes his bandana off and throws it into the crowd, before swinging his arms across. The fans explode. Wretch comes off one end, jumps over Greg to hit the other end... and finally drops a leg drop right across Davis' throat. Upon completion of the Ratings Saver Legdrop, Wretch makes the cover. Two is scored, but Greg refuses embarrassment and kicks out. Wretch brings Greg up for a Wretchinoku Driver, but the crafty cruiserweight slides behind Wretch and takes him down with the Depression DDT Wretch's finisher. The ref slaps the mat twice, but stops when he finds Wretch's leg caught on the bottom rope. Greg brings Wretch back up and tries whipping him into the ropes, but it's reversed. Wretch comes back with a cross body block, but in the process the referee takes a nasty bump. Wretch holds on for the cover, but no ref is there to count. Enter Mitch Wilson, Slade Wilson and Kodiak Vic Creed. Wretch swings a wild clothesline Mitch's way, but Mitch ducks. KVC throttles Wretch and tosses him into the corner, where Slade unloads with sickening and authoritative chops, which immediately welt the youngster's chest. Slade whips Wretch into Mitch, who takes his adversary over with the Headliner. Greg gives the thumbs up to the A.S.S. members and tells them that he has the rest of the match taken care of. The referee awakens, and at that point Greg locks in the cross-face. No response is heard from Wretch, so the ref raises and drops Wretch's hand three times, before calling for the bell. A.S.S. re-enters the ring as Greg is given his belt to gloat, but the party soon finishes as Mike Bear charges to the ring wielding a power drill, the rest of the Underground behind him. A crazed gleam finds its mark in his eyes, as he holds the drill up in the air as he stands over the fallen Wretch.
