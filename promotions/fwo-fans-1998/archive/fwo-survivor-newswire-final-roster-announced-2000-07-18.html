Final Roster Announced

18 July 2000

fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE FINAL ROSTER ANNOUNCED July 18, 2000 And then there were none. At approximately 8PM EST, the final three entrants into the fWo Survivor contest were announced. Stevie Roberts faced interviewers looking unusually serious and somber. "In many ways the fun part has now ended. The fWo staff has selected twelve men out of several thousand candidates and come up with the twelve that you see before you. Now, it's all business." The final three men were not strangers to fans of professional wrestling. The first, a masked wrestler by the name of the Red Dragon, stood in the shadows of the stage, preferring to speak on his own terms and avoid the bright lights and excited questions of the reporters at hand. The second, T-Bone, removed his sunglasses and seemed proud to be on hand. He signed autographs and seemed to bask in the attention the fans were giving him, particularly from the female members in attendance. Finally, the largest man to be entered into the fWo Survivor contest, Kodiak Vic Creed , greeted reporters with a proud sneer. Muscles bulging, Creed insisted that he would be the one left standing when all was said and done. The naming of the last three entrants marked the end of a strange set of days. Attention regarding the fWo Survivor contest has been at a fever pitch, with reporters and hopeful applicants anxious to hear any word of who would be selected. Because of the popularity of the event, rumor has it that the fWo is already looking into a second Survivor contest but no exact date has been named. The fWo Survivor contest has not been without controversy however. Several groups filed lawsuits against the fWo for what was deemed, "Racially intolerant hiring". Specifically, the Latino community expressed outrage at the absence of Spanish representation in the fWo Survivor contest. Spokesman Robert Hernandez had much to say about the Survivor contest. "This is an outrage... we know Latino wrestlers were represented in the application process, but the bigoted wrestling industry once again saw fit to overlook superior athletes." When asked if any legal action would be brought against the fWo, Hernandez said it was "highly likely". Womens organizations also plagued the fWo with complaints after the all-male roster was officially announced. "Women can compete with men, and be superior over them." said Joyce Phillips, a spokesman for WAP, 'Women Against Pro-wrestling'. "I promise you right now, if there was at least one woman at the top of the fWo organization this kind of prejudice would not be allowed to continue." The fWo Board of Directors currently has five members, all male. Several wrestlers who did not make it into the contest also expressed outrage, although on a much smaller scale. Visitors to fWoWrestling.com were forced to listen to the rants of one upset applicant in particular, who insulted those who were chosen as "inferior wrestlers". General visitors to the fWo's web site ignored the comments as the rambling of a deranged lunatic. Not everyone was biter about not making it into the contest however. Dave "The Dynamo" Dirk said, "I didn't make it in, but that's okay. Not everyone could. I'm just going to watch now and see who wins. I'm leaning toward Suicide, I like the kid and think he has a great style. There is Survivor 2 coming I hear, and I'm going to shoot for that. Am I disappointed? Yeah, a little... but I don't see this as a life ending thing. I'm out here to have fun... and frankly I will have more fun watching than worrying about people voting to get rid of me." Similar sentiments were echoed from many of the applicants who were not selected for the first Survivor contest. The final roster stands then with "Black Plague" Ruben Ross, Arch Angel, Mike Extreme, The Don, Ronnie Randall, Juno Law, Nick Allstar, Reno Davis, Suicide, Red Dragon, T-Bone and Kodiak Vic Creed.
