Honor, Respect, and the FWO. “Ten minutes to show time, William.” William looked up from the clipboard and rubbed his eyes. He was starting to suspect he needed glasses. He sighed; he wasn’t ready for glasses. Not in the slightest. The facts were these: the show went well last week. Not perfect; there were always ups and downs with a show that was taping with an aggressive air window. Not perfect; wrestling in general tended to be fraught with random mishaps that quickly turned to disasters. Not perfect… but pretty good. The reviews, while cautious, were positive and the internet seemed to be waking up and paying attention. The lack of movement was new; staying in one arena seemed strange, but it was allowing them all to get their bearings. The cold outside though; he wished he had set up their camp somewhere warmer. San Diego for example; long nights up on the roof of Ivy and the quick access to Mexico wasn’t bad. But the show last week… the connection from past to present… to the future. He couldn’t help but doubt it, but if William Beaven’s father was alive he just might be proud of him. Ivy coming through with Scott was great, and even though it was a one-time thing the fans really loved it. Ivy… he needed to thank her when… His eyes narrowed. He tossed the clipboard to one of the moving crew, attempted to feel roughly a foot taller, and walked down the hallway to the masked man who was leaning against a wall and talking on a phone. “Spyder.” Spyder turned, smirked, and held up a hand. William started to talk, then stopped. He frowned. “Alrededor tres. Sure, sure… I' el ll esté allí. Sí, todos estos americanos son idiotas. With the referee seeing stars, the winded champion down and gasping for air and the momentum finally with her in the once-in-a-lifetime title shot, Karina was given a few too many seconds to think. That’s when bad things happen. Sliding out under the bottom rope, the K-Wolf gestured with a stabbing thumb for the timekeeper to vacate his chair. A thunderous jeer of disapproval rumbled around the arena as the K-Wolf slid into the ring with the folded chair, urging Flyer to get to his feet. Holding the chair out in front of her, Karina readied a kick that would propel the weapon into the skull of the champ… but as the jeers intensified, her grip on the chair’s frame began to loosen. All she had to do was convince herself it had to be done for a few more seconds, and she’d be leaving her second FWO event with the most prized belt in the wrestling world. Just a few more seconds. “Fuck,” vented the frustration at the result of her internal struggle. A relieved, deserving roar then swamped the jeers as Karina flung the chair out under the bottom rope. Grabbing the rising champion while he was still hunched over, Wolfenden hooked his arms as if for a tomikaze, then sharply flipped back over him, letting mechanics take over and snap him over onto the back of his neck as she landed, seated. With the fans at ringside wincing at the C-4’s sickening impact, Wolfenden leant back across High Flyer, hooked the near leg, closed her eyes, and began to count along with the revived referee. “ONE! TWO THREE!” A hush descended upon the arena, fans looking at one another with ‘did that just happen?’ etched across their faces. “NOOOO!” Still a little groggy, the referee lay beside High Flyer, pointing to the wrestling boot perched perilously on the bottom rope. In the space of one second, Karina Wolfenden went from bewildered jubilation to anguished disbelief. Kicking out at the bottom rope, the K-Wolf knocked Flyer’s foot down while at the same time nearly beheading the referee. Trying to compose herself, she urged Flyer to get back to his feet, stalking the champ as he nursed his neck and crawled to the corner. As soon as Flyer had dragged himself up, Kari pounced, hurling herself at him with both knee. Through wincing eyes, Flyer saw her coming, and managed to tumble-roll under the assault. After managing to execute an emergency landing on the middle rope, Wolfenden flung herself back off at Flyer… …only to eat a Locomotive that sent her flipping violently back so that she crash-landed on her face. Still gritting his teeth through the pain stabbing at his neck, Flyer pulled the lifeless Wolfenden away from the ropes and tightly doubled her over, her legs pinning her own shoulders down. “ONE! TWO THREE!” Flyer popped to his feet, and quickly wiped the sweat from his brow. Handed his Heavyweight Championship, he looked at the title and then down to his fallen challenger. He let out a half frown, holding back his disappointment. As K-Wolf fought to her feet, he wiped away an invisible tear, and made his way to exit the ring... The look of shock on Flyer’s face quickly turned to anger. A door slamming shut was heard in the distance as the camera quickly turned, zooming in to see Deacon’s car peeling away from the parking lot. The champ now knew who was to blame for this and he was damn sure that the person was going to pay. The camera faded out.
