Sunday Slaughter, 5 March 2000

5 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Many wrestlers headed toward the worker entrance, and few gave any attention to the large production trucks that were parked just outside the backstage parking entrance. One wrestler did however, and he urged Primetime and Courage to continue on inside while he attended to some business. The Flying Frenchie walked firmly to the production truck door marked "archives" and knocked. He waited for a few moments, cursed under his breath, and then opened the door himself and went inside. Several bleary eyed technicians looked up at the wrestler, at once nervous about why the Frenchie had decided to grace them with his presence. He pointed to the stack of film from last week's Warfare show. "Geoff was film'ng a... private moment of mine. Ze tape of it... I want it. Now." The men in the truck looked around at each other nervously. Finally one stammered, "B... b... but Frenchie... Flying Frenchie... Mr. Frenchie sir... the tape isn't here... you see, someone took it already. Mr. Bear... he came in just a few minutes ago... wanted the same tape sir." The Frenchie looked hard at the tech, and then cursed loudly as he left the truck, slamming the door hard behind him. He walked to the arena, the explosion of fireworks greeting him as Sunday Slaughter took to the air... Lance Star & Chris Universal vs. Underground The Underground made their way to the ring as "I'm With Stupid" by Static-X playing throughout the arena. As Wretch & Mike Bear approached the ringside area, it appeared they were involved in some sort of an argument as they entered the ring. "Do you buy what The Sellout is Selling?" echoed inside of the Charlotte Coliseum and the fans responded with a positive reaction that was overwhelming. Out stepped Chris Universal, which drew a large cheer, before he pointed to beneath the SlaughterScreen and out came his friend and tag team partner, Lance Star. This resulted in an even louder cheer, and Star & Universal walked down the ramp as the fans continued to cheer. Star charged the ring and was greeted with stomps to the head by Wretch, before Wretch took Star to the mat with a snapmare and tagged out to Bear. Bear came in with a reverse chinlock, slowing down the momentum of the match. Star battled back and both men came to a vertical base, before Star escaped the hold with a jawbreaker as he fell to the mat. Star struggled over to his corner and made the tag, and Universal came in a house of fire. He took down Bear with a lariat. Wretch entered and was greeted with a Samoan Drop. Universal followed by sending Bear into the ropes and ducking for a backdrop, but Bear reacted with a single arm DDT. He went for a cover but Universal kicked out. Bear whipped Universal to the ropes and Star made a blind tag. As Universal returned Bear grabbed him by the hair and sent him over the top rope onto the floor. When Bear turned around however he was greeted with a missile dropkick off the top rope by Star, followed by a lightning quick legdrop. Star went for a cover but Bear kicked out at 1. Star followed up with a snap suplex, and then he whipped Bear into the corner. Bear staggered out and was met with a kick to the tender nether regions by Star, followed by an inverted swinging neckbreaker. Star tagged out to Universal who came in and continued where Star left off. An elbowdrop, a kneedrop and a sidewalk slam later, Universal was in position to pin Bear but Bear shouldered out at two. Universal sent Bear into the turnbuckles and then went for a clothesline, but Bear countered into a crucifix. Universal kicked out at two. Bear followed as Universal reached his feet with running shoulderblock, before a Michinoku Driver, of which Universal kicked out at two. Bear went to the top rope and hit a quick moonsault, before a pinfall attempt. Star interrupted with a kick to the back. Bear sensed it was over, and thus decided it was time to go for his patented Whiplash. The former fWo Hardcore Champion quickly took Universal out with the fireman's carry into a Ace Crusher. He went for the pin before he noticed Wretch standing on the apron pleading with him to tag him in. Bear reluctantly did so, but Wretch took his time however. He tried coercing a reaction from the crowd, and never took his eyes off the crowd as he climbed to the top rope. He paused and cupped his hands over his ears as the crowd gave a half-hearted reaction. While he was doing this, he failed to see Lance Star come into the ring and take Bear out with the Shooting Star. Universal moved out of the way and Wretch flew off with a Senton Bomb. He hit it, but he came to and saw that it was his own partner that he had landed on. He acted shock as Star superkicked him right in the jaw, while Universal quickly rolled back into position atop of Bear for the pin. Star & Universal escaped the ring with their first victory under their belts, while Wretch struggled to his feet in the ring, shocked about what had happened. Bear finally recovered and a shoving match ensued, before the other two members of The Underground (Hellrazor and Fear) came down and assured Bear what happened was a simple mistake. Cooler heads prevailed as Slaughter went to a commercial break. Result: Lance Star & Chris Universal by Pinfall Challenge Backstage security rushed to the scene as heated words were exchanged between Eron and Mitch Wilson. The two wrestlers had bumped into each other as they were headed to their respective locker rooms. Mitch yelled and kicked as security restrained him. "Coward... you don't deserve any belt and when I find you... and rest assured that I will... I'm going to snap your neck and rid this federation of your cancer once and for all." Eron smiled, and turned to leave. Mitch Wilson relaxed and allowed security to haul him in the opposite direction, unaware that Eron had been shamming. With the security guards distracted Eron broke free and jumped Wilson from behind, kicking the back of Wilson and knocking him the to ground. Security rushed to regain control, but Eron was already backing away. "Is that right you little whining brat? Well old hoss, you're the one who is going to be gotten rid of once and for all... you asshole, I'll face you tonight, now go pull your panties on and get ready to take a breath... of fresh death!" Eron spat at Wilson and walked away now, much to the relief of the fWo security team. VL vs. Robert Jensen Robert Jensen is introduced first, the crowd's dislike of him cascading down to meet him. He looks to take care of two birds with one stone, sinking his teeth into VL, and in turn earning a taste of fWo gold. Shortly after he emerges however, VL, unannounced, rushes from backstage, the hardcore title strapped yet held in her right hand. She catches an oblivious Jensen completely off guard once he enters the ring, spinning him, and lifting a boot right into his groin area, and has him gasping for air and in a numbing state of discomfort. She brings the gold-plated title into his face. He lurches backwards, the ropes breaking his fall and sending him right back up into a standing position. VL brings the title around once more, yet Jensen renders this attempt futile by deflecting it, and instead brings a right hook around and causes VL's head to snap back uncomfortably. The title falls to the ground, and Jensen lifts it and turns to the crowd, letting them know whose waist will soon harvest the title. However he let images of gold and glory clout his judgment, and upon turning back around, VL is up, and twisting into a spinning heel click, claps the title right into Jensen's stunned face, and causes him to fall in-between the ropes and collapse onto the cold floor below. VL follows him to ringside, and collecting a chair, returns and crinkles it over Jensen's battered cranium. Dropping for a cover, she can only manage to hold Jensen down for two, when Hellrazor, her opponent-to-be come Body Count, hits the scene; 2x4 in hand. Hellrazor comes up behind VL, and jerking her around, looks to clock her. However she instinctively lowers, with a once-standing Jensen feeling the floor give way under him after he is smacked upside the head. The wood does not give way, yet he does after the astounding brunt. VL now rips the 2x4 from Hellrazor, and soon brings it down upon him, splitting loudly into two with a crunch that has Hellrazor laid out. VL now drops on a dizzied Jensen, earning the pin-fall victory and securing another successful title defense. VL leaves the scene, title safely about her waist, and Jensen left to realize that learning is half the battle, as he will have to return to the drawing board the next time opportunity arises. Result: VL by Pinfall Matchmaker Backstage Ric Chronos sat on a chair and looked intently at Fallen. Fallen was sitting on the floor on the opposite end of the room, head sunk and eyes closed. They didn't speak, and in the background the ticking of a clock counted the seconds that passed. Finally, Ric Chronos broke the silence. "You will see. Wednesday. That is, if anyone..." There was a sudden crash of glass as shards of it flew into the room. A brick bounced once on the concrete floor sending small puffs of dust up from where it landed. Ric stood, and Fallen opened his eyes, but remained sitting on the ground, head slumped. A small metal canister crashed in through the hole in the door, and at once smoke began to fill the room. Ric Chronos coughed, and grabbed at Fallen's arm as he struggled to pull the man from the room. Fallen came weakly, more drug than walk. They reached the door and pulled it open escaping the smoke filled room with a harsh gasp. Ric slung Fallen out of the room and fell to his knees, sucking in fresh air. He looked up, and before him stood War and Plague. War looked coldly down at him, and smiled a thin smile. "Challenge accepted." Black Panther vs. Flying Frenchie With the number 20 and 30 slots for the Body Count main event on the line, anticipation among those in attendance becomes addictive, and soon the entire arena is buzzing with excitement. "Killed the Cat" plays first, leading to the arrival of the Black Panther. He is met kindly, focused with sights set on securing the number 20 spot. His music is replaced by that of his opponent, the arena speakers pumping out "Juke Joint Jezebelle". The crowd reacts predictably, jeering at the Foundation member, yet their boos turn to cheers as the match begins, hopeful of one to rival previous meetings dating back to Invasion! Frenchie is first to mix things up, hooking one of the Panther's arms, and dropping to the mat in hope of executing a simple arm drag. Yet Panther's immense frame hardly gives way, and instead, he jerks Frenchie back up, and with their arms still locked, pitches Frenchie into the corner where he smacks into the turnbuckle. He stumbles out drunkenly only to have Panther put a big boot to his face and floor him. Panther looks towards the ropes, and sinking into them, comes back out and drops towards Frenchie with an elbow. He comes up empty when Frenchie rolls away in the nick of time, and this allows both men to catch a quick breather before both regain their feet. Panther comes after Frenchie, yet despite his size disadvantage, Frenchie crouches down almost onto his knees and effectively takes away Panther's center of balance with a Fireman's carry that shakes the ring when he falls to the mat. Frenchie is quick to latch on a headlock, however Panther is not one to go down so easily, and instead rises, even as Frenchie chokes the breath from him. Frenchie is left dangling above the mat, headlock applied yet in a most vulnerable position. A resourceful Panther cannot rid himself of Frenchie, and thus pedals backwards looking to sandwich Frenchie in the corner. Frenchie is able to lift his lower body onto the top turnbuckle right before impact and releases the hold. When Panther turns thinking he will see only Frenchie quivering in the corner, he is taken for a loop when the Frenchie leaps off of the top turnbuckle with a missile dropkick that targets and connects with Panther's throat. Panther crumbles, reaching for his throat and trying to rub out the pain, and this allows Frenchie to wrap him up. At two, as Frenchie has just about secured the number 20 position, Panther muscles out of the pin and keeps his chances alive. Frenchie pins him to the mat with a number of stomps, yet running on adrenaline, Panther simply shakes them off as he rises. Sensing he has touched a nerve, Frenchie hesitates, allowing Panther to deliver a wicked shot to one of his goggled eyes, leaving Frenchie struggling to maintain his composure under an extreme amount of pain. Panther cradles him for a body slam, yet turns it into a turnbuckle smash variation that nearly decapitates the Frenchie on the top turnbuckle. His head snaps back and he cannot defend himself when Panther drags him bit by bit up the turnbuckle and hooks an arm, the Pounce inevitable. Falling off the turnbuckle, Panther extends into a superplex that has Frenchie trying valiantly to escape, however unable to and then thumping off the canvas and slipping slowly from consciousness. Panther rolls over on top of Frenchie, and once a three count is registered, the Panther raises his arms in victory, the 20-slot with his name all over it, while Frenchie must hope for an opportunity to succeed at the 30 spot. Result: Black Panther by Pinfall Trouble in paradise "What in the hell is a paper vs. plastic match?" Rudo looked at the booking sheet for Warfare as Machina dialed. Machina waved him off and worked on pushing the numbers of the phone. He waited as it rang, and then grew sour as the voice mail picked up. He handed heard from Penny in days, and wanted to go over some of the details of the wedding. He was hoping to set it up for an April wedding... if possible on the day of CyberSlam. Now that would be something... of course, he would have to convince Penny that wrestling had nothing to do with it. He looked over at Rudo, and noticed the fWo Cruiserweight title hung over his shoulder. Machina frowned. First singles champion of the Lone Gunmen and it wasn't him? That seemed rather lousy... certainly something would have to be done. Sid walked into the room, smiling. "Good news guys! David has agreed to come along on the bachelor party! Of course, he's going to bring some friends..." Rudo groaned. "They probably will sink the damn ship fat bastards..." Machina smiled and nodded to Sid, and tried again to dial the number... Eron vs. Mitch Wilson "Calling All Stations" begins to play and out to the ring walks Mitch Wilson, representing FTfWo. He rolls into the ring, all business as awaits the Internet champion. Eron's music starts playing and out he walks, holding the Internet title high in the air. He enters the ring and hands the belt to the referee, who signals for the bell to ring. They lock up, and Wilson gains the advantage with a headlock, before being forced into the ropes by Eron and whipped across. Mitch knocks Eron down with a shoulderblock on his way back, and bounces off the opposite ropes quickly, causing Eron to duck down to avoid him. As he gets up, Mitch catches him with a dropkick to the knee, looking to work that leg that he wore down at Trapped. Eron goes down on his stomach, and Mitch immediately heads for his right leg, lifting it up and throwing it into the mat. He repeats this, then turns Eron over and drops an elbow on the knee, before applying a kneelock. Eron quickly reaches the ropes, and both men get to their feet. Mitch ducks a clothesline from Eron, and strikes the Internet champion with a series of hard chops, backing him into the ropes. Mitch sends Eron into the ropes, but Eron manages to reverse it and catches Mitch with a leaping elbowsmash as he comes off, followed by a Russian legsweep. Eron looks the end it early by applying the DeRouger, but Mitch grabs his head and rolls him into a pinning position. The ref counts one, two, but Eron kicks out. Both men are up quickly and this time Eron nails a clothesline, sending Wilson down. He brings him up, bounces off the ropes, and takes Wilson down with a spinning heel kick. Eron covers, but only gets a count of two. Eron brings him up again, and measures him for a superkick, looking to set up his finisher. Eron lets it fly, but Mitch shows his great technical abilities by catching Eron's leg, and taking him over with a dragon screw legwhip. Eron gets up, clutching his knee, and attempts a clothesline, but Wilson ducks and grabs him in a back suplex position. Instead of suplexing him, Wilson lifts him up and takes him down with a kneebreaker, and Eron falls to the mat with Mitch still holding his leg. Mitch sees an opportunity and takes advantage of it, grabbing the other leg and locking Eron into his own move, the DeRoguer. Eron struggles, but cannot escape the move. Finally, with Mitch increasing the pressure on the hold every second, Eron taps out. Mitch releases the hold and holds his arms high in the air, before being handed the fWo Internet Title. He quickly straps it around his waist, but his celebration is cut short as Primetime attacks him from behind, knocking him down. He begins pounding on Wilson, but this is quickly cut short as Perfect rushes the ring to attack his enemy and opponent later on in the show. Perfect and Mitch quickly gain the advantage, and Mitch strikes with an Inverted Russian Legsweep onto the Internet Title. As he stands over Primetime to taunt him, Courage and Frenchie rush the ring and leap at Mitch, and they are followed by Scott Collins who attacks Perfect. FTfWo (with the Internet Title) quickly bail from the ring, and as the Foundation helps Primetime up, they notice Collins and wonder what the hell he's doing there. Shrugging, Primetime heads for Collins and the two begin to brawl, but are interrupted by the voice of Perfect, who has a microphone at the top of the entrance ramp. "Hey, hey, hey! Candyman, Primey, hold up for a second! I've got a proposition for you two goofs, assuming you can stop fighting each other for a moment. Primey, you're no big fan of Mitch, right? And you can't be happy about the fact that I kicked your ass last week and by all means should be holding the World Title right now. And Candyman, I know you don't like me. So how about this - myself and Mitch vs. you two idiots at Body Count? I know both of you want a piece of our asses, so whaddaya say?" Primetime and Collins in the ring both appear to be fine with this, and Perfect continues. "I'll take that as a yes... good luck at Body Count boys, and if you're lucky, we won't break your legs like we did to Ice." Perfect laughs, and heads to the back as Collins and Primetime stare each other down in the ring and Slaughter continues... Result: Mitch Wilson by Submission Love in the air Kid Cool knocked on Chris Finn's door and waited for the announcer to answer. He did finally, and Kid Cool straightened as the door opened. Chris Finn saw who was standing there, and groaned. "Listen... if this is about the title match then you're just going to have to accept that sometimes people lose. You had a... well, a four day reign, and that's better than... well... what do you want anyway?" Kid Cool dismissed the talk of belts and losess with a wave of his hand. "Listen Uncle Chris, I've been meaning to ask you something... about the Warfare Girls?" Finn looked confused for a moment, and then smiled. "Oh... I see... you've become enchanted with one of the young ladies that was dancing the other night. Perfectly understandable... James was in the bathroom for over an hour after he saw their costumes. So which one are you of a mind to get to know better? It's Nip isn't it? Poor girl... she's the talk of the whole locker room." Kid Cool shook his head. Chris Finn scratched his chin. "E. Zee? Well, I was sort of hoping on asking her out so I hope that you..." Kid Cool was again shaking his head Chris Finn now looked stumped. "Well, the other two are married, so I wouldn't recommend..." Kid Cool's face fell. "She's married? Oh no... I thought, well, that she might... well, I can understand where Violet got her looks from. That whole family must be hot. Oh well..." Kid Cool wandered off in the other direction, and Chris Finn watched him go. He thought for a minute and then, "Wait a minute... did he say he liked...?" Primetime vs. Perfect Cameras head to the ring for the next match, where Perfect and Primetime will meet with the winner taking the #1 spot at Body Count and the loser the #10 spot. The arena is bathed in a red light as "Humans Being" begins to play, and fireworks suddenly explode on the entrance ramp, before going down to reveal Perfect who is greeting with a chorus of boos. Perfect makes a comment to the camera about crippling Primetime as he did Ice as he makes his way to the ring, before rolling in and awaiting his opponent. The boos continue as "Blind" starts up and out walks Primetime, who a week ago walked out to face his same opponent as the World Champion. Primetime makes the "title belt" gesture as he walks out, signaling that he believes he should still be the champion. He pauses outside the ring, then rushes in and is met by a series of forearms from Perfect as the bell rings. Perfect attempts to whip him into the ropes, but Primetime reverses it into a short whip spine buster, planting Perfect into the mat. He follows it up with a splash and a cover, which earns a two. Primetime brings Perfect up and goes for a kick which is caught by Perfect, but Primetime quickly turns it into an enzuiguri. Perfect quickly gets up and is taken over by Primetime with a snap suplex. He gets up again and is met with a hard forearm to the face, followed by a kick to the gut and a rocker dropper. Primetime covers again, but Perfect kicks out once more at two. Primetime brings Perfect up and tosses him to the outside, before following him out. He brings Perfect up from the floor and attempts to whip him into the steel ringpost, but Perfect has the presence of mind to reverse it, and Primetime crashes face-first into the post. Perfect quickly heads to where Primetime is near the guardrail and brings him up, before bringing him up. He attempts an Irish whip into the far guardrail, but it is reversed by Primetime, but that is rereversed by Perfect, who turns it into a short whip into a flapjack, which launches Primetime into the air. He smashes down into the French announce table, but the table does not break. The ref, wishing to give the fans a clean finish, elects not to call for a disqualification. Perfect climbs onto the American announce table next to the French table, and slashes his thumb across his throat in the direction of Primetime. Perfect then attempts a moonsault off the American table onto the French one and Primetime, but Primetime rolls off in the nick of time and Perfect crashes through the table. Primetime takes a moment to collect his wits, then rolls Perfect into the ring. He steps onto the apron, and waits for Perfect to regain his footing. When he stumbles to his feet, Primetime springboards onto the top rope, before leaping off and attempting a DDT. However, Perfect out of instinct grabs Primetime as Primetime hooks his head and takes him over with a northern lights suplex. The referee counts one... two... but Primetime kicks out at the last moment. Primetime is up first, and brings Perfect to his feet, before positioning him for the Primetime Bomb. PT spits on Perfect's back before attempting to bring him up. Perfect knows this would spell certain defeat, and struggles to resist, staying on the ground. Primetime attempts to bring him up again, but the larger Perfect again resists. Before Primetime can try it a third time, Perfect drops to a knee and strikes with a low blow, causing Primetime to fall to the mat in pain. As the fWo is not full of wussies, a low blow is not cause for disqualification as it is in other, wimpier leagues, and the announcers point this out as Perfect and Primetime both lay on the mat, attempting to recover. As the ref's count reaches seven, both men begin to get up, and Primetime is up first at eight. He "helps" Perfect up, and again positions him for the Primetime Bomb, striking with a few forearms to the back to weaken Perfect before bringing him up. As Perfect sits on the shoulders of Primetime, he begins to strike with a series of fists to the head, looking to escape the move. He then falls backwards, taking Primetime over with a rana. The ref counts one, two, and Primetime kicks out at the last moment. Both men are up quickly, and Perfect ducks a lariat from Primetime, before scooping him up and planting him with a Michinoku Driver II. He reaches forward and hooks the leg, but Primetime again kicks out at two. Perfect brings Primetime up with him, and sets him on the top turnbuckle facing the crowd. Perfect then heads to the apron and climbs up to the top, before double underhooking the head of Primetime in preparation for an especially dangerous super version of his Curveball maneuver. Primetime fights back with a series of fists to the stomach, and Perfect falls and crotches himself on the top turnbuckle. Primetime quickly hooks him up in the same position as he did last match, and then falls backward, planting him with a top-rope Russian legsweep. Both men again fall with an arm draped over the other, and the referee counts. One. Two. Both men simultaneously kick out. Both men begin to struggle to their feet, and Perfect reaches his first. He waits for Primetime and attempts a lariat, but Primetime ducks it and catches Perfect with a boot to the gut as he turns around, doubling Perfect over. Primetime quickly hooks Perfect for his third attempt at the Primetime Bomb, and lifts Perfect again onto his shoulders. However, Perfect slides free down the back of Primetime, and before Primetime can do anything, hooks his head from behind. Perfect then lifts Primetime up into the air in a reverse DDT position, before dropping him down to the mat in the bone-jarring move known as the Perfect Game. Perfect quickly covers Primetime and deeply hooks the leg. The ref counts one... two... three and Perfect secures the #1 spot at Body Count. He slowly makes his way up to his feet and the ref raises his arm high. Mitch Wilson meanwhile enters the ring from the crowd with a chair, and smashes it over the knee of Primetime. Perfect signals to Mitch, and Mitch places Primetime's knee in the chair. Perfect begins to head to the top rope, but he quickly leaps off and escapes with Mitch through the crowd as first the French Foundation, then Scott Collins rush the ring to prevent the injury to Primetime. From the crowd Perfect lifts first his middle finger, then his index finger high towards the ring to indicate his possession of the #1 spot as the Foundation and Collins eye each other warily from opposite corners of the ring and Slaughter goes to a commercial break... Matt Douglas vs. Courage Courage made his way to the ring accompanied by the last undisputed fWo World Champion, Primetime. The crowd gave these two a mixed reaction, but it was large nonetheless. The French Foundation climbed into the ring and began to work the crowd as Matt Douglas' entrance theme started up. The International Champion made his way down to the ring to a large pop from the crowd who had taken a liking to the young superstar formerly known as Wolf Fang. Douglas climbed to the top turnbuckle before jumping into the ring and mouthing with Courage. The referee separated the two and sent Primetime to the outside as he accepted Douglas' title belt and signaled for the bell. A trio of dings later, and the contest was underway. The two men locked up in the center of the ring and grappled for position, but when neither man found none they broke the hold. Courage and Douglas circled each other awhile more before moving to tie up again. Douglas took the initiative, though, and dropped Courage with a quick hiptoss. Courage smirked as he got back to his feet. The two men moved to lock up again, and this time it was Courage who took control with a quick boot to the stomach of the champ. Courage took Douglas and sent him to the ropes, but upon following him in Douglas sprung onto the top rope and moonsaulted himself over the challenger. Courage bounced off the ropes and nimbly avoided a backdrop from Douglas with a leapfrog. Courage bounced off the opposite ropes and took Douglas down with a pair of spinning leg scissors. Both men scrambled to their feet and Courage floored Douglas for the second time in a row with a stiff lariat. As Matt lay on the ring canvas, Courage climbed to the top rope and came flying off with a moonsault, but Douglas kipped up just in time to avoid the maneuver. Courage, clutching his midsection, slowly got to his feet, only to walk directly into a springboard clothesline from Douglas. Courage scrambled to the outside of the ring where Primetime helped him to his feet. The two began to talk, but were soon interrupted by Matt Douglas planchaing over the top rope and onto the Foundation members. All three men sprawled about the arena floor, with Douglas being the first one to his feet. The international champion hopped onto the apron and waited for Courage to rise, but upon seeing Primetime up, moonsaulted onto the latter. Douglas, a house of fire, then rolled Courage back into the ring and delivered several kicks to him in the corner. Douglas pulled Courage to his feet and executed a Northern Lights suplex, but Courage snuck out at two. Douglas pulled Courage back to his feet and whipped him into the corner. Douglas attempted to follow up with a handspring elbow, but Courage came charging out of the corner with a lariat that nearly knocked the champ's head off. Courage began to showboat to the fans, but as he did so Douglas surprised him with a rollup from behind. Courage kicked out, but upon getting to his feet he was planted into the mat thanks to a DDT. Douglas signaled for the Impact and climbed to the top rope, but just as he was ready to leap Primetime jumped up onto the apron and shoved the champion off. The card dropped again, speeding up now as the stack looked to be nearing an end: You'll hear from us soon The card dropped quickly: And we promise you this: Her smile was one of complete confidence: There ain't a DAMN THING you can do about it! This message shown, she revealed the final card: And now, back to more exciting fWo action! She drops the last card, and smiles at the camera as she crosses her arms in front of her before the camera segways into the next match... Dead vs. Rain vs. Boston Strangler Slaughter returned to a live shot of inside the arena as "Symphony of Destruction" blared throughout the Charlotte Coliseum while the lights began to dim. Fans stood to their feet as Rain walked to the ring with a purpose, greeted by boos from the audience as he did. Rain glared at the fans with a look of annoyance, before climbing over the top rope with ease. He raised his arms as the lights came back on, before the Coliseum returned to darkness. The familiar red light covered the stage as Marilyn Manson' "Beautiful People" shook the arena. Dead walked out onto the stage, and was welcomed with boos, jeers and cusswords from the crowd. Dead neared the ringside area with caution as Rain stared at him, never taking his eyes off of him for a second. Dead returned the glare with a sneer as he removed his trench coat. "Beautiful People" faded and "O' Fortuna" began, and the fans undoubtedly cheered for the man that had defended the honor of their favorite organization since day one. The Boston Strangler emerged from the depths of the backstage area as he stoically approached the ring. Dead was still on the outside of the ring and he looked at both Rain & the madman from Beantown with caution. Deciding it would be a safer bet to go after Strangler, he charged up towards him on the ramp, and both men took it to each other with solid punches. Dead gained the advantage with a cheap shot to the testicular area of Strangler, before taking him down to the ramp with a DDT. He followed by leaning over the Strangler and nailing him with repeated punches, but from behind Rain pulled him off the Strangler by the head. He then whipped him down back first into the ring, and as Dead groggily staggered out Rain took him down with a backdrop. He then rolled him into the ring as Strangler recovered on the ramp. Rain whipped him into the ropes and as he returned Rain took him down with a big boot to the face. Rather than go for a pinfall, Rain grabbed Dead up by the head, before looking to the outside. Strangler was approaching the ring, so Rain quickly thought of a way to take out both men. He pressed Dead high above the ring and sent him to the outside onto the Strangler, before waiting for both men to get up. He charged and dove over the top rope and took the Strangler down with a clothesline, as Dead moved out of the way. Dead procured a chair from a fan at ringside and as Rain got up, Dead smacked him with the cold hard steel from behind. Dead then drove the end of the chair into the neck of the Strangler, trying to choke the life out of him. This only gave time for Rain to recover, and from behind he pulled Dead off the Strangler once more, before grasping Dead by the neck with both hands and tossing him aside. Understandably, this only angered Dead beyond control. Dead plotted as Rain and Strangler traded punches, before Dead removed the guardrail and charged at both men, taking them down with it. He then grabbed Rain by the hair and rolled him underneath the bottom rope into the ring, before covering him. Strangler interrupted from behind with a knee to the head of Dead at two. He then followed up gutwrench suplex. Rain decided to go outside of the ring and search for a new way to inflict more violence upon his opponents. Strangler covered Dead, but Dead kicked out at two. Strangler followed up with a release German Suplex, while Rain found a table underneath the ring and set it up on the outside. Strangler went for The Boston Massacre, but Dead managed to escape out onto the floor. He saw Rain and attacked him with several forearms, before whipping him into the guardrail back first. Rain reversed it however, and instead it was Dead who hit the guardrail with a resounding thud. Rain then saw Strangler charge and bounce off the ropes on the other side of the ring, before he leapt over the top rope at Rain. Dead meanwhile was up on the ring apron, as if he was going to go up to the top rope. Strangler flew and Rain moved out of the way, and Strangler smacked the floor with authority. Something or other caught the attention of Dead near the commentators table, and Dead turned to shout, doing this however distracted him from Rain who climbed up onto the ring apron and assaulted Dead with several shots to the jaw. He then hooked him and turned him towards the table he had previously set up, before running off the apron and powerbombing himself and Dead through the table. Both men crashed through, and shards of wood flew everywhere. Several moments passed by before Rain quickly rolled Dead into the ring, and went for the pinfall. Strangler made one last desperate attempt to break up the pin, but it was all for naught as Rain had achieved victory. Rain and Strangler began to trade blows once more, before Dead joined them in a three-way dance of violence. The trio of men continued to exchange punches and chokeholds and other numerous ways to inflict damage upon each other as Slaughter faded to a off the air, still reeling from one of the more star-studded cards in recent memory. Result: Rain by Pinfall
