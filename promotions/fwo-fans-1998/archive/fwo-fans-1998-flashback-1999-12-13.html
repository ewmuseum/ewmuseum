FlashBack, 13 December 1999

13 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Chris Finn: Hello everyone, and welcome to fWo Flashback. I'm Chris Finn sitting in at the fWo studios here in Redmond, Washington. We've had a couple of big moments recently in the fWo, with the Hardcore Championship changing hands once more. Hellrazor captured his first fWo title at Sunday Slaughter by beating Joy, and we're all certain that this is just the first step to a long and bloody career for the young superstar. But even bigger news than that is the World Heavyweight Title match that went down between four of the greatest wrestlers the fWo has to offer, and all four former champions at one time or another. Ice walked out of the arena with the title, but it was a legendary battle that few have witnessed in the fWo before. Chris Finn: Folks, instead of the usual format for FlashBack tonight we've got a very special treat for you. Earlier today the fWo held a house show in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. The fWo is very proud to take you there now via tape delay to show you what went down at this very special house show in the fWo's "Holiday Hell" tour. Sitting in for commentary are the fWo house show broadcast team, Vince Russell and Ed Ferrari. Take it away Vince and Ed!
