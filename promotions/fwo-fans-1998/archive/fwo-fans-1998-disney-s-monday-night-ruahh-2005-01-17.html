Introduction Previously... The fWo heads to Grand Forks, North Dakota... and find themselves in the middle of a winter storm. But the harsh weather does not deter the newly found fans of the fWo, several of whom were comped to the show after a significantly large marketing campaign by the parent company to the fWo... the Walt Disney Corporation. So braving the storm, the fWo continues on with their road trip... unaware of the dangers just around the corner... Jon Crisp vs. Token Weed The fWo fans instantly began screaming as the cameras began to pan across the arena, a little goading going on from down at the commentators table. They had no idea what would be on next, the fWo had kept the fans in the dark, almost literally this evening. Is everybody in? Is everybody in... ...is everybody in... the ceremony is about to begin... Cue the cheers, cue the lights, cue the pyro... WAKE UP~! He stood on the top of the ramp, his shoulder length blonde and blue hair, his steel gray eyes... He brought his arms up into the air as the crowd let out a mediocre yell. They had watched Vic Creed rip Token Weed apart last week, why would he fare any better against Jon Crisp? Token continued his long walk down to the ring and slid straight into the ring, his eyes glared across the squared circle at Jon Crisp. Slowly Token tilted his head over at Tyson. The two men stood perfectly silent as the bell rang. The two slowly began to circle, the energy in the room was too much for the both of them as they shot in quickly and went for a quick lock up. Crisp grabbed Token's right arm and instantly had Token in a hammer lock. Token thought quickly as he quickly reached over with his free left arm and grabbed the ropes. Sitting in a pointless hole wasn't going to help get both fellows over. Jon instantly took Token back to the ropes and sent him for a little spi. Token came running in and went for a back body drop just a smidgen early, letting Crisp leap over the prone Token Weed. The two each hit ropes again as this time Crisp hopped into the air and drilled Token square in the chest/face area with a perfectly executed drop kick. Crisp rolled to his feet as the fans gave an immense roar. "Craziest crowd ever" Crisp mumbled to himself as he stood silent in the ring. Token on the other hand had managed to roll out of the ring and sat totally silent. Token slowly rolled his way into the ring. The two began to once again, start circling. Once again they ended up in a tie-up. This time Token wasn't budging as easily as Crisp wanted Token to. The sound of skin on skin collection echoed through out the building as Crisp drove his knee deep into Token's stomach. Token quickly doubled over while Crisp was quick to take advantage. Crisp slid around the back of Token and wrapped his arms tightly around Token's waist. Crisp brought Token up and tossed him across the ring with a high release German suplex. "Great" Token mumbled to himself as Crisp was quickly on top of Token for the cover... 1... Token kicked out rather forcefully. Crisp was quick to go back to work on the destruction of the junkie fool. He smiled to himself as he thought of the ring it would have with the fans. The man who ran Token Weed straight the fuck out of town. Crisp brought Token up by his trademark blonde and blue hair. Crisp pushed Token hard, forcing Token to stumble back and bounce off the ropes. He stumbled towards Crisp. *WHAM* Yes kids, Crisp just belly to belly suplexed the fucking hell out of Token. Crisp brought himself over and went for a cover. Crisp seemed satisfied, he figured that this match should be over by now. 1... 2... Token kicked out again. Crisp looked over and started shaking his hand at the ref showing the number 3. The ref shook his head back and forth and held up two fingers. Crisp walked over and slowly brought Token Weed to his feet, he pushed him over into the corner. Crisp took a couple steps back and took off at a full sprint, splashing Token into the corner. Token didn't go down though, he still stood with his head leaning down and touching his chest. Crisp climbed up to the middle rope and began unloading on Token's head. Token stated as he became sturdy. Crisp looked at him with a huge smile. "I think you're a wash-up with no heart, with no guts. You haven't even hit me yet." Crisp said letting out a slight chuckle as he unloaded with a chop across Token's chest. Token took a step or so backwards and looked at Crisp smiling. It was almost as if a fog had been lifted from in front of Token's eyes. Jon Crisp wanted the fighter. He wanted to fight a former fighting champion. Token shot two hard jabs into Crisp's mouth... The fans cheered... Token had been taking a beating for so long they actually cheered for when ever he got some offense in. Token brought his right arm back and connected with a hook to the orbital bone of Jon Crisp. Crisp spun around once from the force of the punch. On his way back around Token thumped him with a left hand to the stomach. Crisp folded in half on impact. Yes kids, the king was back. Crisp didn't have a clue what had just happened. Less than forty seconds ago he was dominating the fuck out of Token, and now Token had ripped him apart brutally in a time that was stunning. Crisp stepped forward and went for a lock up, Token quickly reversed the lock up into a hammer lock of his own. Token smiled as he grabbed Crisp by the back of the head and dropped him into the canvas with a reverse DDT. Token quickly pounced on top of Crisp, assuming a mounted position over top of him. Token began unloading with rights and lefts on the former champion as the ref gave the five count. Token quickly got off of the former World Heavyweight champion. Token reached down and grabbed Crisp by the hair now, pulling him up to his feet violently. He tossed Crisp over into the corner and ran forward, leaping into the air and drilling Crisp with a spinning heel kick. Crisp stumbled out of the corner and to the mid-point of the ropes. Token stalked over to the opposite side of the ring as Crisp slowly stumbled forward, he was in a daze... WHAM Jumping Pump Kick. Mr. Crisp say good night. Token made the pin. 1... 2... .... ...3~! Token jumped to his feet with his arms out stretched as the crowd exploded. Token smiled to them all as Chris Tyson rolled a beaten and bloodied Crisp to the outside of the ring with him. Token stood silent with just a trickle of blood coming down from the corner of his lip. This was the loudest he had ever heard a crowd in his life, and he loved every moment of it. Spyder walked through the curtain, his mask in place, and his chain mail shirt on, just as the fWo faithful had become accustomed. One difference though, he was wearing a Spyder t-shirt beneath the chain mail and cursed in spanish. He was a long way from the US's southern neighbor. He walked to the ring, still spitting curses, and slipped under the bottom rope. Bounding up, he stood on the middle rope and rose his hands to a chorus of boos. He took his chain mail and t-shirt off, rubbing his shoulders until they could adjust. A female fan in the front row held up a sign stating, "I want to kiss Frenchie." Spyder shared a few words, reminding everyone of his role in the Frenchie's 'fluke' win of the World title as well as Spyder's goal of personally lessening the Frenchman of the gold. "Rock Superstar SVJ Remix" by Cypress Remix. The crowd booed instantly as SVJ broke through the curtain. Last week, SVJ made a point that he would be the man in the fWo. While reminding the world of his hatred for Sean Stevens 'XXXStacy', he simultaneously stated that he would climb the mountain of the fWo. As he entered the ring, not even sharing a glance at his partner, SVJ motioned to his waist, letting the whole world know that his goal was to take the belt. The Gregorian chant hit and the crowd continued with their boos. The Deacon, wearing his old monk robe, stepped through the curtain, illiciting a larger heel pop. He hadn't bothered to wear the robe in recent weeks, but with the condition of the arena, he felt it smart to stay warm as long as possible. He strode to the ring, ignoring all the "Faith is Dead" signs. He waited outside the ring for his surprise partner. The chant continued as he stood stalwart, as a giant gargoyle from the netherworld. After defeating Spyder last week, Deacon staked his claim to competing to be the best in the fWo the world champion. He would focus on two things ministering to those in need, namely Craig Miles, and proving to the world that his Faith is not dead... and he'll be carrying the gold to prove just how far it's brought him. "I not know if I could find someone to stand beside me," Deacon said. "In t'e past, I could've called on t'ose t'at t'e fans cheered. To team wit' me now, well... t'at be career suicide, but some men not worry so much about t'ere career. T'ey worry about history, not wit' t'ose t'at see my fait'" "IS DEAD," the fans shouted. The Deacon let them, choosing to use their Pavlov response. "But see me as t'e person I am." "Patiently Waiting" by 50 cent and Eminem blared. The crowd saw the 3 X symbol and knew... they finally had a reason to cheer. Trip came through the curtain, getting a ridiculous pop from the ND crowd. It might've been cold, but Trip couldn't feel anything except adrenaline as they ran from one end of the stage to the other, driving the fans into a total frenzy. He ran down the ramp, not even really paying attention to his partner. It was good to be back in the fWo! Trip hit the ring, Deacon quickly discarding his robe to stay with him, and dashed at Jacobs. They exchanged punches as Deacon and Spyder tumbled through the ropes and to the floor below. The crowd popped huge as the ref called for the bell, choosing to make Triple X and the Superstar the legal men. The Deacon whipped Spyder into the stairs, the luchador leaping on the top before lunging back with a hard cross body. The Deacon spun with the move, performing a concrete jarring powerslam that rocked the fans not watching... Trip and SVJ pounded away on one another, neither man giving an inch. Right hand from Trips. Right hand from Jacobs. Right. Left. Right. Left. The two traded blows until Trip began to get the upper hand, whipping SVJ into the ropes and running against the ropes himself. With a leap, XXX flew with a reverse elbow, sending SVJ to the mat. Trips mounted the Superstar, pounding away with successive lefts and rights as the crowd grew to a feverish intensity. The Deacon lifted Spyder to his feet. With a surge, he tossed Spyder into the railing. Slowly, Deacon stalked the bug. With a thumb to the eye, Spyder had bought himself some time. As Deacon tried to clear his vision, Spyder jumped onto the security railing and leapt into the air, executing a tornado DDT on the concrete. The ref forced Sean to stop pounding on Jacobs so XXX helped up the Superstar before lifting him for a vertical suplex. SVJ, however, had other ideas, slipping out of the hold and flipping, landing on his feet behind Stevens. Jacobs grabbed XXX and sent him soaring with a release german suplex. He crawled over for the pin but only managed a 2 count. Keeping the pace fast, SVJ picked Stevens up and tossed him into the corner. He slowly walked over and repeatedly jammed his shoulder into Stevens midsection. The ref forced the break, which Jacobs gave, before standing in front of Trips and slamming an elbow back, connecting with the side of Trip's face and getting quite a reaction from the crowd. Two refs ran from the back to separate Spyder and Deacon and get the TAG match started in earnest. Both men went to their respective corners, Deacon still clutching at his neck from the earlier blow. Knife edge chops brought a "whoo" response from the crowd as SVJ pounded at Trip's chest. With a cross whip, Trips hit the buckle a moment before SVJ leveled him with a clothesline and then grabbed him, surprising Trip with a Stunner. Trips fired back into the corner and SVJ mounted the middle ropes. "Come on and count, people." Jacobs said before beginning his punches. 1.2. 3. 4. 5. 6. The crowd just wasn't counting? And then Trips began carrying SVJ to the center of the ring, dropping him down with a manhattan drop, getting a large pop from the crowd followed by a larger dropkick from SVJ who had been able to move out of the way of the drop. Trips fell flat as SVJ noticed that his partner was in the corner. He made the tag to Spyder who told Jacobs to lift up and hold Stevens. Meanwhile, Spyder climbed the top rope and soared off, nailing the helpless Stevens with a double axe-handle. He went for the cover, getting another 2 count. Spyder lifted Trip up, grabbed him, and then shouted a Spanish obscenity at the crowd before dropping him with a swinging neckbreaker. The luchador stood up, smiling at the crowd and shouting some more before making the lackluster cover that got an even weaker count barely a one. Spyder yanked up Trip by the hair and placed him for a piledriver. He held him there before cradling and driving Trip into the mat, getting a sizable boo from the crowd. He made an identical cover, but this time it went beyond 1 and beyond 2. Deacon's foot was likely the only thing that could stop the ref's hand from hitting on a 3rd time. The crowd didn't know whether to boo or cheer. Deac had saved Trip, but it was Deac? The ref got in Deac's face as Jacobs entered the ring for a free moment. As Deac argued, Vince whipped Trip into the ropes, Spyder dropped down forcing Trip to leap him, and Vince leveled XXX with a superkick. With perfect timing, Spyder ran into the ropes, leapt on the middle one to perform an in the ring moonsault, clobbering XXX. The ref turned from Deacon to make a cover. Just what Deac wanted. Deacon raced over and picked up Spyder by the mask. In one quick motion, Deac picked up Spyder for the powerbomb but instead of dropping him to the mat, he threw him into Jacobs in the corner, both opponents falling to the floor below. The ref chided Deacon who merely shrugged before making the 'cross pose' and then hitting the far ropes and running and leaping over the top rope My Death is Gain! He landed on both opponents giving his partner a bit more time to recuperate. Triple X slowly got to his feet and then noticed the train wreck outside the ring. The Deacon slowly got to his feet, stumbling out of the way. Triple X smiled, hit the far ropes, and leapt over the top rope My X Gains More... sure, it's not his move, but the crowd loved it anyway! And Deacon stumbled back over to help his partner into the ring followed by a near comatose Spyder. Trips lifted Spyder up for a body slam and then mounted the top rope. The Mute Freak entered grabbing Spyder's head from behind. He dropped down, executing a Fallen One (Reverse DDT) and going for the cover. Only getting a 2 count, Spyder stopping it by placing his foot on the bottom rope. Deacon hooked the leg and tried again, this time getting another 2. Deacon plant with a surprise german suplex that put Spyder on top of his head. The crowd gave a nasty "EWWWW" from the impact and Deacon went for the cover. 1.... .... .... 2.... Save by SVJ.
