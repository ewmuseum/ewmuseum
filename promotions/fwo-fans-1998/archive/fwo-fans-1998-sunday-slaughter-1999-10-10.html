Sunday Slaughter, 10 October 1999

10 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Fans went into one side of the arena, the workers went into the other. The entire fWo roster was on hand, including wrestlers off the active roster, and wrestlers working under conditionals. There were a few notable exceptions however. Several people wondered aloud where Rain had gone, and Apocolyptica was still missing. Perhaps more troubling was the absence of the Speed Demon, who nobody had seen since Wednesday. This aside, there were several booking meetings on plan for the night, but the immediate result was that a very volatile situation was brewing backstage. With such a large roster in a small confined location tempers were running hot. There was an outbreak early in the day between the Blondes and Slugger and Ice which caused several referees and security officials to be on hand to break it up. Amidst all this chaos one man stood atop a table in the center of the backstage area, raised his arms, and yelled, "I am Joy... and tonight will be a night of Joy!" Several pieces of paper, bottles, and chairs flew, striking Joy in the skull and knocking him from the table. On this note, Sunday Slaughter began... Big words Just as Sunday Slaughter's fireworks and theme music began to fade, the lights go out as "The Day the World Went away" by Nine Inch Nails starts to play. Eron the Relentless comes out with a shower of fans booing. He has the International Title on his waist as he walks down the very middle of the ramp, ignoring the fans with a sneer. He swipes a microphone from the announcer and speaks. "Have all you idiots packed your boxes for the Apocolypse in the 21st Century? Have you pulled out your candles for the 2 months of Blackout? Have you, made your computers "Y2K" corrected? I hope not, but of course, you're all too brain-dead to figure that the Millennium, WILL NOT HAPPEN!" The fans boo Eron as he starts to clean off his International Title from the garbage thrown at him, as he walks up on the Turnbuckle. "I am here, as a favor, the ONLY favor you Media-Hyped trash are gonna get from me, and that is, I will stop the Millennium from happening. Yes you heard me, no more 2000 this, 2000 that, no Y2K bull is going to happen. That clock will stop at 1, and it aint gonna move ANY MORE! Now, some of the more people here who dont have "brainrysms" would say "Eron, the greatest fWo Champ of any kind, what will happen to all of time? Will we be in 1999 forever?" the answer, unfortunately, is yes. Now it does have its positives, we wont get to see the 80s come back, Alice Cooper will never have a comeback Tour, George Bush wont be President, but the negatives, we'll ALL have to keep is that Backstreet Boys will still be popular, and that..." But from the backstage area Matthew Zhuk had heard enough, and ran to the ring to accept Eron's open challenge... Eron (c) vs. Matthew Zhuk Eron met Matthew Zhuk as he slid into the ring with kicks to the head, driving the challenger into the mat. Zhuk rallied by grabbing Eron's leg and flipping him over backward, causing Eron to fall on his head. Zhuk grabbed the legs of Eron and attempted a figure four, but Eron mule kicked backward and sent Zhuk spiraling into the ropes. Eron, quick on his feet hit a standing lariat and dropped Zhuk to the mat. Eron made a cover, but Zhuk kicked out at two hard. Eron grabbed Zhuk by the hair and brought him to his feet, but Zhuk hit upward with a uppercut into Eron's face. Eron's nose bloodied, and Zhuk went to the ropes. Zhuk came off and struck with a superkick into the jaw of Eron. Zhuk quickly rolled up Eron, but Eron grabbed the ropes to break the count. Zhuk argued with the ref, which gave Eron a moment to untie the turnbuckle cover and cast it into the crowd. Zhuk turned away from the referee and grabbed at Eron, but Eron reversed his hold and thrust Zhuk's head into the exposed steel. With Zhuk dazed Eron turned him over in the derouger, getting the submission victory. Eron did not release the hold however, and pointed at the crowd, shouting "this is what happens to those who oppose me... this is what happens!" The crowd roared as Courage ran to the ring and slid inside, running after Eron. Eron quickly escaped into the crowd, which Courage in close pursuit... Result: Eron by Submission Ominous words Backstage Cactus Jon roamed from room to room looking for signs of the commissioner. Jon wore an angry expression on his face, and seemed annoyed. Mumbling aloud as he went, Jon didn't see Fallen coming out of a downstairs stairwell. Jon collided with Fallen, and his surprise quickly turned to anger. "You!" Jon yelled, and threw Fallen into the concrete wall of the arena. Jon grabbed the neck of Fallen and started to strangle him, but Fallen only grinned. Jon yelling, "Listen you bastard... I'll deal with you on Wednesday, and there is a hell of lot I have to make up for with you, you damn son of a bitch... who's going to save you Skyler? Huh? Who's gonna save you from Cactus Jon?" Fallen gurgled out a response, and Jon loosened the hold on Fallen's neck to hear what he had to say. Coughing, Fallen said, "Have a care Jon... one may be the loneliest number..." Fallen coughed again, spitting up some blood. "One may be the loneliest... but four... four is the deadliest. Remember that." Jon looked confused, and growling cast Fallen aside as he continued his march down the halls of the backstage area. Fallen watched him go from a distance and wiped blood from his lip. "Four Jon... remember that." Joy vs. Jackhammer Balloons and ribbons dropped from the sky as Joy came to the ring for the first of two matches. Joy waved happily to the fans and grinned, although he rubbed a large bump on his head. Joy got into the ring and attempted to get the crowd to do the happy dance, but the crowd largely ignored him. Jackhammer came to the ring without any kind of entrance or lighting effects, growling and shaking his head back and forth. Jackhammer slid into the ring and Joy began to kick at the head of Jackhammer. Joy backed Jackhammer into the corner and began to pummel the larger man. Jackhammer slumped down, and Joy left him and walked to the center of the ring with his arms in the air. Jackhammer suddenly seemed to snap and sprung at Joy, knocking him to the mat with a spear. Jackhammer then picked Joy up and hit a Jackhammer, dropping him to the mat with a dull thud. Jackhammer covered Joy with two hands on his chest and got the three count. With little fanfare, Jackhammer walked from the ring, asking only "who's next?" Result: Jackhammer by Pinfall Ken Armstrong "We're pleased to have you with us... obviously a man of your size is best used in the ring, but certainly since your injury..." The commissioner shook the Brawler's hand up and down, pumping it with enthusiasm. The Brawler winced a bit, and shrugged. "Well... as long as I'm going something... something in front of the camera. So... when do I start?" The commissioner walked back behind his desk, shrugging as he went. "Any time you want... Flashback could use a good commentator or interviewer..." The Brawler nodded with satisfaction. "Works for me... just one last thing... the name..." The commissioner looked up with a questioning gaze. "The name?" The Brawler nodded. "Yeah... seeing as I'm not wrestling, I'd best go by my real name don't you think? Ken Armstrong." The commissioner smiled, and nodded. "Hey... sounds fine. Just don't let James Armstrong's mail get mixed up with yours... the things he gets... well, I don't need to go into that. See you on Flashback, Ken." Ken Armstrong smiled, as the cameras went back to the ring... Ultra Cool 2000 vs. NXS Back to the ring saw NXS walking confidently down the entrance ramp. Judge Steel, Markus Cairn and Rottdog all boastfully told the fans to go fornicate with a rusty shoehorn, which immediately made the fans boo them out of the building. The fans ovation of boos turned into an ovation of cheers as Ultra-Cool2K made their way to the ring, with the ever good looking Violet. The Purple Ones and Kid Cool climbed into the ring, which Kid Cool still had trouble doing, but appeared to be getting the hang of it. Steel & Rottdog decide they will wrestle the match, while Cairn will watch from the outside. Cairn repeatedly tried to flirt with Violet throughout the match, but Violet appeared to be distracted by a ring on someone' finger out in the crowd. Ultra-Violet surprisingly wrestled a technically sound match, and Kid Cool was in total awe of a simple collar and elbow tie-up in the beginning. UV took Rottdog down with a hiptoss, and followed it up with a dropkick, and a Japanese armdrag. This confused Kid Cool a great deal, as he still hadn't learned to correctly apply an American armdrag, let alone a Japanese armdrag. UV tagged out to Kid Cool to resume the action, and Kid Cool tripped into the ring. This gave Rottdog an open shot, and he brought Kid Cool into the ring and took him down with a piledriver. He went for a pin, but Violet made a distraction outside the ring. She inadvertently began to show her ... upper regions, after a fan convinced her that he would give her the ring on his finger if she did so. This distracted Rottdog and every other male member in the audience, except Ultra-Violet who was wondering why Violet would accept a ring from a stranger but not from UV. UV went to the outside and helped Violet "cover up", and back in the ring Kid Cool snuck up on Rottdog and rolled him up for a pin. Rottdog quicked out before the referee could even count. He jumped to his feet at the same time as Kid Cool. Rottdog went for a clothesline but Kid Cool performed a basic somersault and then popped up in his corner. He turned back to face Rottdog, and UV tagged his back. UV came in and he and Kid Cool double-dropkicked Rottdog into Judge Steel on the ring apron, which in turn sent him out ontop of Cairn on the floor. The crowd promptly begin chanting sexual slurs, and this had to of panicked the fWo front office. Rottdog struggled to his feet and UV took him down with a boot to the stomach, and a DDT. He made a quick pin and the referee and Kid Cool both counted to three. UV was in shock, Kid Cool was in awe, and Violet was hoping a ring would be on the way. UV & KC celebrated, while NXS re-grouped and bickered amongst themselves on the outside, while Slaughter went to a commercial break. Result: Ultra Cool by Pinfall Rain takes control "Get in the ring, dammit... get in the ring!" Rain turned to see Commissioner Ultra Violet pointing up into the ring. Rain looked around himself, staring at a world that wasn't his. Fans booed him, and help up signs reading "Pimpin' Rainman Goin' Down". Commissioner Ultra Violet pushed at the back of Rain then. "Get in the ring, do the job, and the fans cheer Scott Slammer. End of story, you get yourself a beer. Now get in the ring... the fans are starting to wonder what in the hell is going on." Rain climbed into the ring and looked at Scott Slammer, who smiled happily at him. Rain responded by punching Slammer in the face, breaking teeth and bone. The Commissioner yelled in horror, as Rain continued his attack, beating Scott Slammer until he could no longer stand. Rain stood in the ring then, and looked around at the silent crowd. Mouths were opened in surprise or horror, and a smile appeared on Rain's face for the first time in a long time. As he left the ring a masked man broke through the stunned fans and handed Rain something mouthing the words, "You need this... You need it..." The man's hand opened over Rain's, and a small crystal dropped into his hand. Rain grabbed it, and his smile grew wider... Death & Stone vs. Chaos We returned from the commercials to find the team of War & Plague already in the ring, awaiting their former friends Death & Stone. Death & Stone made their way out as the fans jumped to their feet and ran over to the guardrail, trying to touch the duo. Death simply laughed at the little kiddies and told them to go play in the street, while Stone seemed annoyed by their efforts to come close to touching a wrestler. They hit the ring and an all out war took place as the referee seemed to be unable to control the action. He seemed to be on the verge of disqualifying both teams, when things finally resumed to a controllable level. Death tossed Plague around like a football, showing him who's truly "the boss". Death eventually tagged out to Stone who came in and did well for herself, until War tagged out. War' ability to kick people's asses without much effort came into play, especially since he outweighed Stone by a great margin. War & Plague took turns double-teaming Stone, beating her ass within an inch of her life. Death repeatedly tried to intervene, but without making a legal tag which prevented him of being legally in the ring to help Stone out. War proved to be too much for Stone to handle, and made her submit. Death was less than pleased, and chased the two away out of the ring. As he bent over a fallen Stone, he looked up and watched War and Plague walk up the ramp. As they did so, Stevie Roberts made an appearance and appeared to engage in a friendly conversation with them. The fans wondered aloudly what this could mean, and what Stevie's intentions were. The look of hatred, anger and dispair remained etched across Death's face, as he helped up Stone. Result: Chaos by Submission French Foundation vs. Smiletastic Slaughter rolled on as the team of Joy & Glee made their way to the ring. Joy & Glee climbed in, and awaited The French Foundation. Primetime & The Flying Frenchie made their way to the ring, and Frenchie had the fWo Women's Title wrapped around his waist. Primetime was not without gold, as he had the Cruiserweight Title draped over his shoulder. Frenchie beamed with pride as he climbed into the ring, before telling Glee that although she looked like a female, talked like a female, and walked like a female, that did not necessarily mean she would receive a shot at his Women's Title anytime soon. Frenchie then said that he would gladly kick her ass tonight though with glee, before kicking her man's ass with a great deal of pride & joy. Primetime and Joy started things off, and the teamwork of Frenchie & PT proved to be too much for Joy & Glee to overcome in the early going. The match spilled to the outside as Primetime and Glee fought into the crowd, and Frenchie and Joy battled it out in the ring. All hell broke loose as Frenchie went for a superkick, but Joy ducked and Frenchie instead hit the referee. Joy rolled Frenchie up for a pin, but Frenchie kicked out. Joy whipped Frenchie into the ropes and went for a dropkick, but Frenchie held onto the ropes and instead Joy fell to the mat and hit hard. Frenchie then saw Mitch Wilson coming down to the ring... and he proceeded to beat on Joy for a minute, before grabbing Joy. He invited Mitch up onto the ring apron to hit Joy with a chair. As Frenchie held Joy, Wilson took the shot... and missed. Joy ducked, and Frenchie met the chair instead. Wilson stared in disbelief and anger, before Joy forearm smashed Wilson off the ring apron and backfirst into the guardrail. Back on the outside, Primetime disposed of Glee and saw what was going on in the ring. Joy awoke the referee to count the pin, and as the referee was about to smack the mat for the third time, Primetime managed to fly off the top rope and nail Joy with a legdrop to the back of the head. He then argued with the referee as Frenchie rolled over onto Joy, and the referee turned back around to count the pin. Primetime checked on Frenchie as Wilson struggled to his feet on the outside. The French Foundation seemed to be in disarray for a brief moment, before the trio reunited and beat on Joy & Glee some more for fun, before leaving the ring altogether as a unit. Result: French Foundation by Pinfall The return The fans waited for the main event to start, restless and anxious to see Havok inside the ring for the first time. Suddenly the lights went black and fireworks burst through the arena, with a bright blue "L" in a circle blazing above the entrance ramp. The fans rose to their feet with a roar as Machina appeared on the top of the ramp with Billy James and Sid Snow behind him. Machina let the moment carry, and then rose the microphone to his lips. "The Lone Gunmen are back in the fWo... and back in the fWo TO STAY!" The fans cheered once again as Machina looked from left to right with a smirk on his face. "Now the last few months have been nothing but dismal shows, jobbers winning titles... I mean, Eron... what gives there, huh? And then you have the return of NXS? NXS? What you've got there is a giant plate of suck and the fans are having to eat it... well I say no more suck! No more suck! No more suck!" The fans began to chant it, pointing to Machina and the Lone Gunmen as they did. This seemed to disturb Machina, and he continued. "But I'm out here for just one purpose tonight... and that is that the Lone Gunmen JOB NO MORE! We've laid down in the ring for everyone from Melissa Smalls to Bookerman... and that all changed right here and now! The Lone Gunmen lie down for nobody unless they are busty models from Los Angeles, and maybe a dancer from Chippendales for Sid every once in awhile... But the fact remains, no more jobbing! And finally... Scott Slugger. Mr. Baseball. Well champ, Tom Seleck is coming after you for gimmick infringement, but before he does I want a shot at that strap you've got around your waist. Everyone here remembers how I let you take the title shot that was rightfully mine almost a year ago so I could lead this country to greatness... well I'd say you owe my ass that title shot. So balls up, champ... you and me... one week!" With that, the Lone Gunmen left the stage... Havok vs. Chris Universal Sunday Slaughter continued as "Anthem for the Year 2000" began to play inside the arena. After a nifty display of pyros, the newcomer known as Havok was set to make his in-ring debut in the fWo. Havok was welcomed by a chorus of boos by the fWo crowd, and he didn't seem to care as he climbed into the ring and taunted the fans. The fans booed even more loudly as Havok just smirked at them all with confidence. As Havok continued to toy with the fans emotions, we heard "Do you buy what The Sellout is selling?" as out came "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal to an enormous amount of jeers from the crowd. Universal came out in a three-piece suit and looked very dapper; while at the same time he carried a fWo microphone in his hand. He looked at the crowd, and grinned, before looking into the ring at Havok. Universal finally began to speak as the crowd quieted down. "If I paid each and everyone of you to shut up, would you?" The crowd cheered in anticipation of receiving a paycheck for attending a Slaughter. "Right, no, you wouldn't... because you're all morons, just like that rookie in the ring. Havok, The Sellout doesn't have time to wrestle rookies, especially a *Canadian* rookie. The Sellout has money to count, people to make fun of, and movie offers to turn down. The Sellout feels his time would be better spent watching the freshly painted walls of his latest mansion dry than to walk down this ramp, climb into that ring, and layeth the smacketh down on behalfeth of all the people that hate your guts almost as much as The Sellout does. No, The Sellout isn't going to welcome you into the fWo... The Sellout is going to pay someone to do it for him. Why? Because he can. Now, who could he possibly pay that would come down into the ring and kick your ass? Well... last night I placed a call to room number 384 in one of Detroit's better hotels... and I made an offer that he couldn't refuse. He doesn't like working Sunday's, but he hasn't been on tv in quite awhile... so he readily jumped at the opportunity to come down to the ring and give you the wrestling lesson of a lifetime... and trust me, he's going to do just that, and then some. I've found an opponent that is more suitable for a man of your skill... and his name is... Andrew Galactic!" An explosion of pyros bursted all over the place, before a video on the Slaughterscreen began to play, and the word "GALACTIC" appeared. The fans groaned as Mike Heftel' latest gimmick incarnation came out. Galactic was dressed as dapper as Universal, complete with wrap-around shades and an attache case. As Galactic walked towards Universal, Universal gave him an awkward look before handing him the microphone. Galactic walked down to the ring and stepped in, as Havok looked on, not sure what to think. Galactic finally made his way into the ring, and began to speak. "Havok, you're gonna come out here tonight, and you think you're going to make a name for yourself? Against Andrew Galactic? Hardly, little man. See, I know what motivates you. Moola, greenbacks, clams, cash! And that's something I've got a lot of, unlike yourself. So I've got a little deal for you. You lie down on that mat for A.G., and I guarantee you that you'll see more money then you'd ever made working in the bush leagues." Galactic cracked open the attache case, revealing stacks of money, and quite alot of it. Havok looked at the money with a grin on his face, and looked to be considering the deal. Havok came to the conclusion that he'd take the money, and reached for the case. "Whoa whoa whoa, Havok. I said you'd make more money then you'de ever made in Toronto. I didn't say you'd make that much. But I'm an honest man, and I'm going to live up to my end of the bargain." Galactic digged into his pocket and came back with a single quarter. He flipped it at Havok, and told him to lie down, as he agreed to. Havok thought things over for a moment before he dropkicked the attache case into Galactic, and thus spilling some of the money into the ring. Havok then whipped Galactic to the ropes, and took him down easily with a Samoan Drop. He then dragged A.G. into the corner, and went to the top rope as Universal stared at the ring in frustration and embarassment. Havok then hit his patented "Millennium-sault", before finishing A.G. off. Three seconds later, it was all over, and Havok began to put the money back into the attache case, and was about to claim it as his own. Universal came down to the ring and spun Havok around, before going for the 'Cha-Ching!'. Havok blocked it and Universal went flying out onto the floor. Havok ended up leaving with the money, as Universal' plans were obviously foiled. Havok walked beyond the curtain, certainly richer and officially undefeated for the time being. Universal tumbled back into the ring and began yelling at the fallen Heftel, who struggled to his feet. Universal began to berate Heftel, but it wasn't quite clear as to what he was saying. Heftel took off the thousand-dollar jacket he was wearing and threw it into the face of Universal, which somehow 'caused Universal to trip in embarassment. Lance Star quickly came down to the ring and saved Universal from further embarassment, as Heftel seemed to have won the crowd over for a brief moment. Heftel made the journey back into the lockerroom, as Star helped Universal to his feet, telling him not to worry about things... for they had bigger things to worry about on Warfare; as they would face Scott Slugger and Ice in the second round of the tag team tournament. Slaughter came to an end then... with the fans still waiting to see if anything else would develop... Result: Havok by Pinfall
