Wednesday Warfare, 1 March 2000

1 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Primetime spotted Chris Finn walking toward the arena entrance and broke away from Courage and the Flying Frenchie to grab him. Finn was caught by surprise and squawked as Primetime grabbed his collar and lifted him up off the ground. "Some fWo security goons came to my hotel last night and asked for my belt! What in the hell gives? You know I won that match last night, Finn!" Primetime was obviously angry, and Chris Finn waved his hands back and forth to try and diffuse the obviously intense situation. "Easy... easy... listen I know you've got reason to be upset, but we had to do what was fair to both of you... and sadly that meant declaring the title vacant." Primetime growled. "I read Venom's little report on the internet this morning asshole... you think it's right for me to find out what's going on with my title at the same time as thousands of internet geeks get it? That little column of his was being cut and pasted all over the net before I even knew what was going on... you think that's fair?" Chris Finn's feet were suspended about the ground and he waved them wildly. Primetime had enough, and rather than waiting for an answer he tossed Finn against a wall. He started to walk away, and then turned and pointed. "This is bullshit Finn... you know it and I know it. Do the right thing and reverse your decision... because after Courage and I beat those two little goofs that call themselves Money Shit I'm walking out of here with two titles... like it or not." Finn lay on the ground and watched Primetime walk away. He shook his head. It wasn't his decision anyway... it was the decision the booking committee came up with... and it wasn't easily made. As the fireworks exploded inside the arena, Chris Finn stood and dusted himself off. He wasn't looking forward to the rest of the evening at all. The World Championship As Warfare took to the air a taped interview with Chris Finn and James Armstrong began to play up on the WarfareScreen. Before them was the fWo World Heavyweight Championship, glittering quietly on a desk. Chris Finn spoke. "What we have decided is not easy. As some of you may already know by reading fWoWrestling.com, the Heavyweight Championship has been held up and is currently vacant. We have determined that the best thing to do would be to put the championship on the line at BodyCount, the fWo Pay Per View in March. The winner of the main event will not only have the honor of being the first man to win such an event, but will also claim the title of fWo World Heavyweight Champion." Chris Finn shuffled papers, and looked for what he needed to address next. "Obviously something has to be done about the two men who made this all come about, as they both have the most legitimate claim to the title. That is why this week on Sunday Slaughter, Primetime and Perfect will wrestle a singles match, with the winner of the match getting the number one slot for BodyCount. The loser will claim the number ten spot. In addition, we have added a sub main event for Sunday Slaughter. The Black Panther and the Flying Frenchie, who were the last two champions prior to Primetime's reign, will wrestle for the honor of the number twenty and number thirty spot in BodyCount. More information will be released as it comes available." The WarfareScreen flickered, and then went dead as the taped interview came to an end. Fans looked around at each other excitedly, as Wednesday Warfare went to it's first commercial break. Cactus Jon vs. Hiroshima An indication of things to come takes shape in the form of a red glow that soon swallows up the arena. Hiroshima emerges, and is promptly fervently ridiculed and jeered at by those in attendance. Canadian Connection member Cactus Jon, who as of late has not been seen on the fWo scene, then follows Hiroshima. The two lock stares, each as stiff as the other. Cactus is the first to break this spell, leaping upon Hiroshima and grabbing his head. He drills his own forehead into Hiroshima's once, and then wrenching him upwards, lets him fall to the mat. Adding insult to injury, he follows with a series of stomps and then lifts Hiroshima to a standing position. However, Hiroshima is hardly out of it, and when Cactus swings at him, he is able to deflect the attempt elsewhere, and counter with a closed fist of his own that finds its way just below Cactus' right cheek bone. He swings once more yet comes up empty when Cactus ducks. Jon works his way into a waist-lock, yet Hiroshima continues the chain, slipping around into one of his own before awkwardly managing to apply a stretch suplex that tosses Cactus over his head and has him shaken upon impact with the canvas. Hiroshima dives for a cover, forcing Cactus onto his back, but at the count of two, Cactus turns a shoulder and breaks up the pin-fall attempt. Hiroshima is disappointed, and lifting Cactus, uses an Irish whip to send him to the ropes. However, Cactus is able to reverse it at the last moment and instead pitch Hiroshima to the ropes. On the rebound, he catches him, and twisting into a power-slam, cements him to the canvas. Deciding not to cover, he "helps" a susceptible Hiroshima to his feet, and grabbing his tights, lifts him upwards and perpendicular to the mat, in position for the Canadian Crusher Piledriver. Cactus drops downwards, in turn embedding the top of Hiroshima's head into the mat and placing an extreme amount of pressure on a most vulnerable neck. Hiroshima slumps to the mat, and can do nothing as Jon lays on top of him for the cover, which in due time is rung up. Cactus leaves victorious to a mixed reaction as Hiroshima is left motionless in the ring. Result: Cactus Jon by pinfall Meet the Warfare Girls The fans sat restlessly in their seats, anxious to see what would happen next. James Armstrong made his way out onto the entrance ramp, and waved to fans in attendance. They largely booed at him, but if he noticed he showed no signs of it. He climbed into the ring and beamed and the crowd, and signaled for them to quiet. After a few moments they did so, largely because they were bored with James Armstrong's presence at ringside rather than because they were actually interested in what he had to say. James smiled wider and addressed the crowd. "Ladies and gentlemen... you are lucky because you have come to the biggest Warfare we have ever had! In addition to a packed card, you are lucky in that you will see the debut of not just one... not just two... not just three... but four brand new faces in the fWo!" The crowd perked up at this and began to cheer, as several aspiring workers in the stands looked at each other in confusion. "I thought they weren't accepting applications?" Another worker shrugged. "They said they aren't... but I'm just here to learn how to use Poser." Inside the ring Armstrong continued. "Ladies and gentlemen... please give a warm welcome to... the WARFARE GIRLS!" The lights in the arena dimmed, and suddenly hip hop music began to play. Armstrong pointed up the ramp, "Introducing first, from the sunny beaches of Los Angeles... I give you Nip!" A young girl dressed in tight spandex wobbled her way out onto the entrance ramp and waved to the crowd. The crowd cheered loudly as hooting and whistling began. "Introducing next, from Miami, Florida... E. Zee!" Another young girl that bore a striking resemblance to the first bubbled out onto the entrance ramp and blew kisses at the audience. "Introducing next... from a different sunny beach in Los Angeles... it's Chesty!" A third young girl that looked more or less like the other two shook her way out onto the entrance ramp and grinned at the fans with a vacant smile. "Introducing next, from Orlando, Florida... give a warm welcome to Felacio!" The forth girl came out onto the entrance ramp and spanked her own backside for the crowd, who grinned and drooled appropriately. "And finally... from... well, the card says 'I hate you' please welcome... Nightshade!" Nightshade walked slowly out on the ramp with her eyes narrowed. She looked at the other girls and gritted her teeth. The crowd began to chant "Take it off! Take it off!" and the Warfare Girls looked around at each other to see if they should comply. As they pondered this decision, the show went to commercial... STUMP Squad vs. NXS Warfare picks up, and already in the ring await the virtually extinct tandem of Markus Cairn and Rottdog, comprising NXS. They prepare for this rare Warfare appearance, and more importantly for the two men they will oppose. As they waited the Warfare Girls, Nip, E. Zee, Chesty and Felacio danced for the crowd around the ring. Cairn and Rottdog smiled appreciatively at the girls, but frowned at Nightshade, who wasn't dancing. Rottdog yelled for her to shake her ass, and Nightshade lunged at the ring. She was held back by Chesty, whose enlarged breasts thumped into Nightshade's back like twin bowling balls. Suddenly music began to play, and NXS looked up the ramp at their opponents. The two emerged in the form of Black Quicksilver, Stump as always in his grasp, and alongside him, an old face with yet another brand new look. Mike Heftel, his latest rebirth as the wrestler known as Silver Slowback, follows closely behind BQ. In both hands, he cautiously cradles a paper bag with the utmost care. It should be noted that the eyeholes of his mask have been cut exceptionally large, allowing for an unparallel level of visibility while wearing a mask. Once the paper bag and Stump have been carefully placed aside, Black Quicksilver steps in to represent the STUMP Squad first, and Rottdog does the same for NXS. The two circle one another before Rottdog charges at Black Quicksilver. BQ dodges to the side, allowing Rottdog and his full head of steam to go careening by; yet not before BQ sticks out a leg and catches Rottdog, sending him tripping into the ropes. He meets the second rope and is sprung back to his feet for just a moment, before BQ comes at him with a dropkick that connects squarely to his back and has him flipping over the top rope and to the arena floor below. Slowback is quick to grab him and roughly roll him under the bottom rope, pushing him in, and giving BQ a confident thumbs-up. BQ returns this favor, and then turns his attention to Rottdog. Lifting and then whipping him hard into the corner, he uses the ropes to catch Rottdog as he stumbles out of the corner, and floors him with a Bulldog. Slowback calls for a tag, and with teamwork and chemistry top priority, BQ obliges. Heftel, or Slowback, approaches Rottdog, however is met with an elbow to the gut. He cringes and this allows for Rottdog to shake off the effects of the Bulldog, and grabbing Slowback's head, leaps and comes down, slamming it to his knee. Rottdog drags Slowback to the NXS corner, tagging in Cairn, and applying an abdominal stretch, permitting Cairn to sock Heftel in the ribs. Cairn heaves Slowback to the ropes, and on the rebound, meets him and chucks him skywards with a Flapjack maneuver that completely turns the tables in this one. Seeing his teammate in peril, BQ thinks quickly, and grabbing the unexplained paper bag, slides into the ring. From inside the bag he removes two slightly melted cups of vanilla ice cream. As Cairn turns, he is meet by BQ, who extends a cup right into his face, slathering it with Dairy Queen vanilla. Cairn is incensed, however unable to clear the dessert from his eyes. Before either member of NXS realizes what occurred, the bell has been rung, and the referee raises the arms of both BQ and Slowback, the match awarded to them via DQ; by Dairy Queen that is. Chalking up a victory in the win column, STUMP Squad leaves the scene celebrating with the remaining and untouched cup of vanilla, yet to meet defeat since banding together. Result: Stump Squad by DQ Finishers The newest addition to the fWo camera staff Kristen Jacobs discovered an argument backstage, and signaled for cameras to move in closer to catch the discussion on tape. The Ninja appeared to be arguing with Tom, and their words were growing more and more heated. "Tom, it won't work." The Ninja was insistent, and put both hands on his hips. Tom whined back, "But no one will get up from it... I know it Ninja, I just know it!" The Ninja nodded. "Of course they won't." Tom groaned in exasperation. "Well then... what's the big problem, Ninja?" The Ninja rolled his eyes. "Tom..." Tom cut him off, nodding firmly now. "It needs a name. It's the name, isn't it? The Ninja groaned. "No, no. The name has nothing to do with it. It's just not a good finisher, okay?" Tom looked outraged. "What? You've got to be kidding me... it's the best finisher ever!" The Ninja was losing his patience. "Listen, 'back a truck over them' is not a good finisher. It's not even a legal one. You can't just back a truck down to the ring in every one of your matches..." Tom hung his head in despair. Suddenly, his eyes brightened. He started to speak, but the Ninja cut him off. "No Tom, back a bus over them is also not a good finisher." Tom hung his head in despair once more as cameras went back to the ring... Dead vs. Rain As the crowd waited for the next match, the Warfare Girls danced inside the ring, with the exception of Nightshade, who stood sourly next to one of the turnbuckles and glared at the other three girls. The sound of thunder and lightning signaled the immediate presence of Rain, and as his video on the Warfare screen played, Rain walked out onto the stage. Welcomed by only boos, he paid no attention to the crowd, for they were just trying to distract him from his goal of the night. Rain climbed over the top rope and into the ring, and as the lightning still crackled off in the distance, "Symphony of Destruction" began to fade out. It was replaced with the demonic rhythm of "Beautiful People", and the arena was bathed in a light of crimson. Dead stepped out onto the stage and the reaction towards him was no different from any other time he had walked out on that stage since returning to the fWo two months ago. Greeted with jeers and overwhelming hatred, Dead didn't give the crowd any attention either as he neared the squared circle. Dead walked to the ring with a slight look of "pain" in his face, since he was not fully recovered from the war he was in this past Saturday. A slight limp was noticable as well. Rain took off his trenchcoat and prepared for battle, as did Dead. Rain turned around with a look of anger on his face and charged at Dead, the two men locked horns like giant bulls beginning a fight. The referee signaled for the bell, and the match was quickly underway. Rain gained the advantage with a quick side headlock, but Dead backed him up into the corner and began nailing him with shots to the ribs. Rain reversed positions by putting Dead into the corner and returning the favor. Dead tried to protect his stomach, but Rain countered by going for the head. Something had to give here, and the referee intervened, asking for a clean break. Rain appeared to give it by backing out, but we everyone realized it was just a sham as he charged into the corner. However, Dead countered by spitting out the black mist into Rain' eyes, and this left Rain temporarily blinded, giving Dead a perfect advantage to attack. He attacked from behind with a chop block, which only staggered the big man. Dead followed with a kick to the stomach and a quick DDT. A cover ensued, but Rain kicked out at two. Dead confronted the referee about the slow count, and Rain took the opportunity to get the black mist out of his eyes via his shirt. Dead returned to attack with a stomp to the head, followed by bringing Rain up to his feet and taking him down with a snapmare, and following *that* up with an illegal chokehold from behind. The referee reprimanded him, so Dead backed him into a corner and began shouting in his general direction. From behind came Rain with a charge, sending Dead back into the corner, which squashed the referee. Rain then turned Dead around and hit him with the Rainmaker, before going for a pin. He realized there was no referee, and began making attempts to revive him. Dead rolled out onto the floor, his head resting on the mat as he attempted to shake off the affects from the powerbomb. From the crowd behind him emerged The Boston Strangler, who took him to the floor with the Boston Massacre as Rain continued to revive the referee. Neither man witnessed what was going on as it occured behind them. After punishing Dead on the outside with his finishing move, Strangler seemed satisified and began journeying up the ramp with his eyes on the ring. With the referee now revived, he saw Dead on the outside and began to count him out. Ten seconds later, and Rain had literally been HANDED a victory. As Strangler reached the stage however, from behind Perfect shoved him, before going down to ringside. Strangler saw this and advanced towards the ring while Perfect helped up Dead on the outside. Rain viewed all this from inside the ring, before pointing at Strangler & Dead and telling them "it wasn't over." Perfect & Dead escaped through the crowd as Warfare went to a commercial break. Result: Rain by Count Out Friends again "Catsup? Or Ketchup? Hmm... so many decisions..." Machina looked hard at the supermarket aisle, trying to determine which food product to purchase. He was lucky and had the night off, but as he stared at the rows of packaged food he wondered if perhaps this would have been a better job for Sid. He also would need to call Penny when he got back to the hotel... he had such strange dreams the night before, and it would be good to hear her voice. He turned the aisle and nearly ran into the mountain of a man clad in flannel. "Trucker! Funny meeting you here!" The Trucker looked down and smiled. "Well howdy there littl' buddy. You feeling okay? I was a little worried that I might have hurt you in that Bond Match on Saturday. You okay?" Machina nodded, and walked down the next aisle with the Trucker. "Yeah, I'm fine. Listen... I'm sorry about being so pig headed about the whole Bond thing. Friends?" The Trucker smiled. "You bet. I admit I was a little too feisty about that as well. Hmm... peanut butter... peanut butter... ah, there it is!" The Trucker reached for a can of Jif as Machina was reaching for a can of Spiffy. They looked at each other's selections for a moment, and then shook their heads as if to dismiss something. "Well, no more fighting from here on out... we can settle our problems like men, and not in the ring." The Trucker nodded at Machina's words. "You bet. From now on no getting worked up about trivial things." Machina found a box of Ritz crackers and tossed them into his basket. At the same time, the Trucker grabbed a box of Saltines and tossed it into his basket. The two looked at what was in each other's basket, and then shrugged. They walked to the check out line, and Machina stood while the cashier began to rang him out. "Will that be paper or plastic sir?" Machina shuddered. "Plastic! That paper stuff... icky!" The Trucker put a hand on his shoulder. "Hold on there littl' buddy... that plastic bag ain't biodegradable... it's bad for the environment." Machina narrowed his eyes. "Yeah? Well your paper bag is the end result of rain forests being cut down!" The Trucker frowned. "You want to make somethin' of it son?" Machina frowned back. "Maybe I do!" Kid Cool vs. Rudo On the entrance ramp the Warfare Girls continued to dance and flirt with the crowd, with the exception of course of Nightshade, who looked with evil intent at the other two girls on the ramp. A large "L" appears on the WarfareTron as "Dizz-Knee-Land" plays and out walks Lone Gunman Rudo, who walks confidently to the ring to a good reception and rolls in. "All Star" begins to play, and Kid Cool walks out, holding the Cruiserweight Title high. He rolls in, still clutching it, and after a bit of persuading, hands it over to the referee. Cool turns to face Rudo, and the bell rings. The two lock up, and Rudo pushes Kid Cool into the ropes, leapfrogging him over as he comes off and taking him down with a hiptoss. As Cool gets up, Rudo strikes with a dropkick directly to the bridge of the nose, knocking Kid Cool back down again. Rudo runs into the ropes, and leaps onto the ropes, before leaping off with a moonsault onto Kid Cool. Rudo covers, but Cool kicks out at two. Rudo brings Kid Cool up, and takes him down with a back suplex. Cool struggles to his feet, and Rudo whips him into the ropes once more. Rudo puts his head down however, and Kid Cool leapfrogs over him and hooks him from behind for a german suplex. Cool attempts the move, but Rudo flips over and lands on his feet, using the momentum of the move to his advantage. As Cool gets up, Rudo grabs him from behind and pushes him into the ropes, coming off with a rolling reverse cradle. The ref counts to two, but Cool manages to reverse the hold, and the ref reaches two before Rudo kicks out. Rudo is up first and tries to hit a clothesline, but Cool ducks, and chops Rudo hard as he turns around. Cool hits another chop, and backs Rudo into the corner, before whipping him across and into the other. Cool follows him in with a leaping avalanche, and leaps onto Rudo's shoulders as Rudo stumbles out. Rudo has the presence of mind to drive Cool into the mat with a powerbomb reversal, and stays on him in a pinning position. Cool barely manages to kick out at two. Rudo brings him up, and hits a snap suplex, and heads up top, perhaps going for the Toad Splash. Before Rudo can pick a move to attempt, Cool stumbles to his feet and into the ropes, causing Rudo to fall and crotch himself on the top rope, before falling off onto the mat. Kid Cool fails to see this, and is somewhat surprised to see Rudo down on the mat. He shrugs, and bounces off the ropes once... twice... a third time... and finally hits his devastating finishing maneuver, The Elbow Drop. He nails it, and leaps up, celebrating his powerful move. As he jumps repeatedly into the air, one of the Warfare Girls appears dancing at the top of the ramp. Cool looked up at the three women standing there, and suddenly his mouth dropped. He stood staring at the top of the ramp with a slack jawed expression on his face. He is still doing so when Rudo pulls himself to his feet after being struck with such a scintillating move, turns him around and plants him with a DDT. Rudo quickly heads to the top rope, measures Cool, and leaps off, nailing a picture perfect Toad Splash. Rudo hooks the leg, and the ref counts three. Rudo falls back in shock that he has won the Cruiserweight Title, and is handed the belt by the referee. Rudo looked at it with amazement, and then leapt high into the air in celebration as Warfare continues... Result: Rudo by Pinfall Challenge Ric Chronos brushed past security and made his way down to the ring, looking serious and holding a microphone in one hand. He slid into the ring, and paced around it for a moment before addressing the crowd. "I'm here to discuss a little problem we had at fWo Trapped a few days ago. Now I'm new to this promotion, so excuse me if I'm wrong... but aren't partners supposed to help each other in a tag team match? Last time I checked tag team matches didn't involve one member of the team walking away in the middle of the fight. So Fallen... Fallen get out here because it's way past time we dealt with this!" Ric Chronos put down the microphone and waited. A few moments went by, and just when the crowd had given up on anyone responding to Ric's comments, Fallen slowly paced onto the ramp. He looked down at his feet, out at the crowd, and then finally up at Ric. He held his gaze for a moment, then dropped his eyes down and paced the ramp once more. Chronos watched him for a moment, and then began speaking again. "I'm not going to ask you WHY you did what you did at Trapped, because I'm not so sure you'd know the answer. But dammit John, I'm not giving up that easily. I'm not giving up like all the others around here did... so that's why I'm issuing a challenge... right now... to anyone who has the guts to face Fallen and me. Next week, on Warfare... anyone at all... just show up. Fallen and I are going to prove what real teamwork is all about... not like the Panther and Havok, and not like the Underground." Fallen heard this challenge, and looked up at Chronos once again. He caught his eye, and then looked back down at the ramp before walking backstage alone. Ric Chronos watched him go, and then slid out of the ring himself as Warfare went to a commercial. Guano Apes vs. French Foundation Back from commercial, the Warfare Girls continued their dancing display up on the ramp... except there appeared to be fewer of them. In fact, only one girl was dancing, and as she did she looked nervously over at Nightshade, who was the only other Warfare Girl that appeared to be on the ramp. Nightshade wore a thin smile, and looked intently at the remaining girl. Music began to play, and in an unexpected change in the normal way of doing things in the fWo, the champions made their way out first for this tag team title match. Jackhammer Sphere made their way to the ring as Jackhammer tested his guitar's tuning with the old "My Dog Has Fleas" method. The two men stepped through the ropes and handed their belts to the referee when the French Foundation's music played and a cult group of fans exploded. Courage and the arguable fWo World Champion, Primetime, stepped onto the entrance ramp and absorbed both cheers and boos. Without recognizing the crowd the Foundation slid into the ring and began to mouth off at the Guano Apes, who tried their best to counter with the "I'm rubber, you're glue" defense. Eventually the bell rang and the match was officially underway. Sphere and Courage started out with a lockup that saw the bigger Sphere push Courage into the corner. Courage broke the hold with a quick thumb to the eye, though, and began to pound on Sphere. Sphere was backed up to the ropes, where Courage attempted to whip him to the ropes, only to have his hammer throw reversed. Courage returned and was met with a back body toss that planted him in the mat. Courage was back to his feet quick, but was sent right back down with a clothesline. Sphere raised his arms to the crowd and charged Courage for yet another lariat, but Courage ducked as Primetime, who had entered the ring, leveled Sphere with a superkick. Sphere flopped to the mat as Jackhammer entered the ring and began to beat on Primetime. The referee, however, decided that Jackhammer was breaking the rules and ushered him to the outside, turning his back to a double team effort from the Foundation on the tag champ Sphere. Primetime left the ring as the ref turned back around, but was soon tagged back in by Courage. Primetime entered the ring a house of fire and assaulted Sphere with a series of kicks in the corner. Primetime whipped Sphere to the corner and charged after him but was greeted by a big boot to the skull from Sphere. Seeing his oppurtunity, Sphere dived to make the tag to Jackhammer. As Jackhammer charged the ring and cleaned house on both Foundation members a pair of men stepped out onto the entrance way in order to scout the competition. Chris Universal smirked before turning to Lance Star who bore no emotion on his face. Back in the ring, Jackhammer grabbed Courage by the hair and threw him over the top rope and to the outside. Primetime snuck up behind Jackhammer, though, and brought him to the mat with a low blow. Primetime stomped on his opponent a bit before climbing to the top rope for a flying elbowdrop. Before he could jump, though, Sphere pulled down on the ropes and crotched the supposed world champ. Primetime belly-flopped back into the ring as Jackhammer tagged Sphere back in. The two men double delayed vertical suplexed Primetime before Jackhammer left the ring. Sphere picked Primetime up and dropped him with a gutbuster before applying an armbar variation. Primetime soon had the ropes, despite all of Sphere's effort, and the champion was forced to break the hold. Primetime approached Sphere for a lockup, but at the last second delivered a boot to the gut and a DDT. Primetime tagged out to Courage, who slingshot himself onto Sphere with a legdrop. Courage covered, but Sphere slipped out at two. Courage sent Sphere to the ropes, but Sphere reversed it. Courage came back looking for flying headscissors, but Sphere caught him and planted him with a spinebuster. Sphere covered but only managed a two count. Sphere then picked Courage up and lifted him for an inverted atomic drop, but as he hit the move Courage tagged out to Primetime over Sphere's shoulder. Primetime entered the ring, spun Sphere around, and nailed him with a jawbreaker. As Courage rolled to the outside Primetime gave a stunned Sphere a boot to the gut, then executed a perfect Primetime Bomb. Primetime covered, but at the count of two Jackhammer entered the ring and wallooped Primetime with the guitar. The ref called for a bell, and the champions were disqualified. Result: French Foundation by DQ The kiss Violet sat in her dressing room, humming to herself. Ultra Violet had been called to the commissioner's office to go over some problems they were having with the set of Cooking With Ultra Violet. Apparently the insurance company that insures the fWo were insisting that the set and anything that happened within it would no longer be covered by their insurance policy. A knock came at the door, and Violet bounded up, excited to see who was there. She opened the door, and frowned. The Flying Frenchie stood there. He grinned stunningly at her. "Ah, ze Violet... how pretty you are... I trust zat you received ze gifts zat I sent to you?" Violet gasped, and pointed at the Frenchie. "You? You're the one that sent me the candy and flowers?" The Frenchie nodded, and took a step forward. "Oui. And now my mon cher Violet... ze Frenchie claims ze prize." The Flying Frenchie moved closer, grasped Violet around the waist, and delivered a firm kiss directly to her lips. Violet struggled for a moment, and then lost herself in the moment, responding by putting her arms around the Frenchie as he bent her over to hammer home the embrace. Suddenly, Violet remembered who she was and who she was engaged to, and began to struggle once more. She pounded his back as the Frenchie continued with his lip lock on her, when suddenly Mike Bear wandered by. He gasped, and grabbed the Frenchie's shoulder, pushing him away from her. Without the Frenchie supporting Violet she weakly fell to her knees, and the Frenchie smiled a devilish smile before walking away. Mike Bear helped Violet to her feet, and she stammered. "He... he... he kissed me! But... why?" Mike Bear looked grimly down the hall where the Frenchie had gone to. "I'll take care of it... listen, you keep this from Ultra Violet if you're smart. After that whole Kid Cool thing he doesn't need the unnecessary anguish... you let me deal with Mr. Kissy Pants over there." Violet nodded weakly, and walked slowly back into her dressing room as Mike Bear headed for the commissioner's office... Ice vs. Perfect Warfare heads down to the ring for the Main Event of the show, the third ever meeting between former friends Ice and Perfect. As the fans waited for the wrestlers to arrive, they looked quietly up at the top of the entrance ramp, where Nightshade stood alone. She smiled, and folded her arms across her chest with satisfaction. The arena is bathed in blue light as "Crazy Train" signals the entrance of Ice, who has turned the intensity up a notch in recent weeks. Ice is announced at a weight of two hundred and eighty one pounds, and hailing from the well-populated town of Parts Unknown. The mammoth man steps over the top rope into the ring, and stares down the entrance ramp to await his archnemesis. "Humans Being" starts up over the arena loudspeakers, and red lights and lasers fill the arena. Fireworks explode in front of the entrance ramp, and when they go down standing there is Perfect, focused on the ring. Matt Panzer announces him at two hundred forty eight pounds and fighting out of Cooperstown, New York, before adding that the match will be a no-disqualification, no-countout contest and that should Ice lose, he will retire. Perfect rolls into the ring, and quickly takes to his feet. Ice stands there, and Perfect immediately gets into his face. Perfect holds up one finger in the face of Ice, then raises another, then raises a third to signify that he believes he will defeat Ice for the third time. Perfect then slashes his thumb across his throat, signalling that he plans to end the career of Ice. Ice simply stares at him, and Perfect slaps him hard across the face. Ice reels for a moment, then returns the favor with a vicious forearm smash to the face of Perfect, and the bell rings to signal that the match has begun. Perfect stumbles into the ropes from the force of the blow, and Ice quickly whips him across and strikes with a huge boot to the face, knocking Perfect down. Ice quickly brings him up, and sends him into the ropes again, catching him with a thunderous powerslam coming off. Ice hooks the leg, but Perfect kicks out at the count of one. Ice brings Perfect up and whips him hard into the turnbuckles. As Perfect stumbles out, Ice bounces off the ropes and takes him down with a bulldog, ramming his face into the mat. Ice covers, but Perfect again kicks out at one. Ice brings Perfect up again, and shoves him into the corner. Ice backs up a few steps, spins, and attempts a rolling elbowsmash, but Perfect ducks out of the corner and Ice slams hard into the turnbuckles. Perfect quickly grabs Ice and whips him across the ring, running after him and dropkicking him in the right knee as he hits the turnbuckles. Ice stumbles out, and Perfect leaps onto the second rope, before leaping off and dropkicking Ice in the back of the knee. Ice drops down, and Perfect quickly drops a leg on the right leg of Ice. Perfect rolls Ice over, and lifts his leg up into the air before dropping an elbow on it. Perfect brings Ice up again, and backs him into the corner with a series of chops. After a series of kicks to the knee, Perfect again whips Ice across into the opposite turnbuckles. Ice doesn't manage to turn around and crashes sternum-first into the turnbuckles, and never sees Perfect clip him from behind and knock him down onto his back. Perfect quickly rolls onto the outside and grabs the legs of Ice, before pulling him forward and towards the turnbuckle post, crotching him in the process. That is shown as merely insult to injury, as Perfect wraps up the legs of Ice, before locking on a figure-four around the ringpost. This is a perfectly legal action as the match is no-DQ, and Ice screams in pain, just a few taps of the hand away from ending his own career. Perfect relishes this, wanting to make Ice to consciously choose to lose. Perfect pulls back hard on the leg, and at one time is nearly vertical. As they are in that position, Ice reaches deep down inside himself and uses the turnbuckles to shove himself back down to the mat. The momentum of this causes Perfect to fly up from the floor, and he does so with such momentum that his head flies up and smashes into the ring post, opening it up. In severe pain, Perfect involuntarily releases the hold and falls to the floor. Not wanting to lose his submission hold, Perfect quickly pulls himself up and grabs the legs of Ice again, but this time Ice uses what little leg strength he has left to pull Perfect into the turnbuckle post, and he smashes into it again. Ice rolls to the outside, landing gingerly on his right leg. He shrugs it off, and proceeds to smash the stunned Perfect with a hard lariat. Ice limps over to the nearby timekeeper's table, and shoves the timekeeper away before taking his chair. As Perfect struggles to his feet, Ice holds the chair high behind his head, before bringing it forward with incredible strength. It crumples against the skull of Perfect, who himself crumples to the floor on the outside. Ice brings the bloodied form of Perfect up, and quickly clears the French announce table before shoving the carcass of Perfect onto it. He strikes with a few hard elbows to the nose, before slowly pulling himself onto the ring apron. Ice stares at Perfect for a moment, then launches himself off the apron with a flying elbowdrop that puts Perfect right through the table. Shards of wood and monitors fly everywhere, and both men are down. After a moment, a hand reaches up through the wreckage, followed by a body - that of Ice. Ice pulls himself to his feet slowly, making sure not to put much pressure on his knee. He reaches back into the rubble and retrieves the unconscious form of Perfect, before bringing him up and rolling him into the ring. Ice follows, and drapes an arm over the downed form of Perfect. One. Two. Kickout. Perfect manages to lift a shoulder just in time, and Ice rolls off of him. Ice is undaunted, and uses the ring ropes to bring himself up. He reaches for Perfect, but Perfect counters with a leg trip, bringing Ice down. Perfect then pulls himself up, and heads towards the corner. He hops up onto the second rope, still attempting to clear the cobwebs. Perfect waits for Ice to drag himself to his feet, and Ice slowly does so. Perfect leaps off the top rope, looking for the Grand Slam, but Ice counters it by grabbing him and taking him over with a huge powerslam that rocks the ring. Ice hooks a leg. One. Two. Kickout by Perfect. Ice struggles up again, and brings Perfect up with him. Ice shoves Perfect into the corner, and showing tremendous physical and mental strength, sets him on the second rope, looking to hit the Icebreaker and finish it off. However, Perfect rakes the eyes of Ice as he attempts to climb, and Ice stumbles about, blinded. Perfect reaches from the second rope and brings Ice over, before double-underhooking his head. Perfect then leaps off, nailing a picture perfect Curveball. He hooks both legs of Ice, and the referee tolls the count. One. Two. Incredibly, Ice brings his shoulder up at two and three-quarters. Perfect isn't sure what to do, but quickly makes his decision, heading to the legs of Ice and wrapping him up, going for an STF. Perfect takes a moment with the hold half-on to lean into Ice's face to badmouth him, but Ice reaches up and hooks the head of Perfect, rolling back into a small package. One. Two. Kickout. Perfect quickly gets back to his feet, and kicks the leg of Ice as he attempts to get up, causing Ice to fall back to his stomach. Perfect rolls Ice over, and grabs his leg. He then leaps over the body of Ice, attempting to pull his leg up and over his head. Ice clutches at his leg in pain, and Perfect grabs him and brings him to his feet. Perfect grabs the leg of Ice, and takes him over with a dragon screw legwhip. Perfect holds on and brings Ice up again, attempting it once more. Ice reaches back however and strikes Perfect in the face with a pair of forearms to the jaw, causing him to break the hold. The fans begin to rally behind Ice, and Ice shoves Perfect into the corner, before taking a few steps back and charging in with a spin, nailing a rolling elbow on Perfect. Ice steps out of the corner and briefly turns his back, looking to head across the ring and perhaps attempt a running clothesline. Behind him, Perfect stumbles out of the corner and chopblocks the right knee of Ice, and Ice falls back, landing on his shoulders. Perfect holds on and quickly bridges up, attempting to use the move that won his first encounter against Ice. One. Two. Kickout. Ice brings his shoulder up at the last moment, and Perfect is stunned. He brings Ice up again, and places Ice's head between his legs. Perfect shows great strength and lifts the larger man up, before dropping him with a powerbomb. Perfect holds on, and strains to bring the larger man up, before dropping him once more to the mat. Perfect holds on once more, and his muscles bulge under the effort of lifting Ice. Perfect gets Ice up into position, but cannot follow through as Ice falls down onto him in a mounted position, and begins to rain hamlike right fists onto the already cut forehead of Perfect. Ice is getting his second wind, and feels his adrenaline pumping as he brings Perfect to his feet. Ice signals that he wants to end it, and brings his right hand back before wrapping it around the throat of Perfect. Ice takes a moment to stare down his ex-friend, and this costs him as Perfect wraps him up in a small package as he did at Twilight '99. One. Two. Kickout. Ice powers out, throwing Perfect off, but Perfect shows great agility and lands on his feet. As Ice struggles up, Perfect sees an opportunity and kicks him hard in the right leg, following it up with a perfectly legal testicular kick. With Ice doubled over, Perfect goes behind him and hooks his neck, looking to hit the Perfect Game. Perfect attempts to lift Ice, but Ice uses sheer strength of will to bring Perfect up onto his shoulders and toss him over. Perfect quickly gets to his feet, and charges again at Ice. Ice ducks under and grabs Perfect as he attempts a leapfrog, tossing him up onto the top rope facing the crowd. Perfect turns around and is met by a high kick that strikes him in the lower ribs, causing him to fall onto the top turnbuckle and crotch himself. Ice senses a chance at victory and steps onto the first turnbuckle. Despite putting great pressure on his hurt leg, Ice then pulls himself onto the second turnbuckle. Ice then attempts to mount the top turnbuckle, but at first cannot do it. Ice takes a moment to gather himself, then by sheer force of hatred climbs onto the top turnbuckle with Perfect. Ice reaches back and wraps his hand around the throat of Perfect, before bringing him to a standing position. Ice then leaps off and spins, driving Perfect into the mat with a top-rope Icepick. Perfect bounces high off the mat, and Ice uses the very last of his energy to roll himself onto the body of his enemy. One. Two. Three. The ref's hand strikes the mat for a third time, and Ice scores his first-ever victory over Perfect. Ice is too spent from the match to get up, and the ref simply raises his hand in victory as he rolls off of Perfect, his career and livelihood still intact. However, this scene of victory is quickly ruined as Dead and his foot soldiers Hiroshima and Mitch Wilson hit the ring, all wielding chairs. As Wilson helps Perfect back to his feet, Hiroshima brings Ice up and bashes his chair over Ice's head. Hiroshima quickly brings him back up and holds him in position. Dead then strikes with a vicious chairshot to the already-injured knee. Ice crumples in pain, clutching his leg. Perfect then shakes the cobwebs off and quickly converses with Wilson, who places Ice's knee into the crook of his chair. Perfect then climbs up to the top rope, before leaping off with a frog splash onto the chair and Ice's knee, and an audible snap can be heard. Ice isn't moving, and he appears to have passed out from the intense pain. As FTfWo leaves the ring laughing, paramedics rush the ring, quickly stabilizing Ice and placing him onto a stretcher. The stretcher is wheeled to the back as it becomes apparent that Ice may have won the battle, but lost the war, as Wednesday Warfare goes off the air... Result: Ice by Pinfall
