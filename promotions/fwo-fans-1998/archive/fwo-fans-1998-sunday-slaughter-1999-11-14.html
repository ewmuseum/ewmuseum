Sunday Slaughter, 14 November 1999

14 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Sunday Slaughter was scheduled to begin any second across town, but one member of the roster was nowhere near the arena. In a small movie theatre in Baker, Oregon fWo wrestler Joy sat with his mouth agape and hand buried in a box of popcorn. His eyes were wide as he looked up at the screen, mumbling to himself. "This is the most amazing thing I've ever seen..." On the screen Pikachu refused to fight back against his clone Pikachu. The theatre was filled with small children with the exception of Joy, but all faces were glued to the screen with the same dazed expression on their faces. The film, "Pokemon the First Movie" finally came to a conclusion, and Joy stumbled out of the theatre still wearing the dazed expression on his face. "Why didn't I think of this before? Pikachu and Ash have taught me what I should have known all along... that fighting is wrong and that we can all get along! I will take what I have learned today and return to the fWo... before this year is over everyone will be singing the praises of Joy... and nothing can stop me!" Above him the sound of thunder was heard, and as it began to rain Sunday Slaughter officially began... Crucifix vs. Cactus Jack The lights dimmed and screams were heard over the rafters as Crucifix came to the ring carrying a sledgehammer, and tossed it into the ring as he waited for the challenger. Cactus Jack came to the ring without any weapons, and slid into the ring and at once went after Crucifix with swinging punches. Jack grabbed the head of Crucifix and rammed him face-first into the mat and the sledgehammer, opening Crucifix's head up. Screaming to the crowd that he was going to take the title from Death, Foley continued his calculated attack. Crucifix finally got back on his feet and delivered a quick standing kick, taking the air out of him and sending him to the mat. Crucifix jumped onto Cactus Jack and applied a figure four leg lock while reaching for the sledgehammer. Cactus Jack broke the hold and slid outside of the ring, walking over to where a pile of steel chairs sat. Jack grabbed a chair and turned it on Crucifix, who dropped the sledgehammer. Jack continued to bash the steel chair over the head of Crucifix repeatedly, until Crucifix began to scream with rage and grabbed the chair away from Cactus Jack and thrust it into his mouth, cracking the jaw and teeth. Crucifix swung the chair down, dropping Jack and placing it on top of his prone body. Crucifix then delivered a leg drop onto the chair and covered Cactus Jack for the pinfall victory. Holding his arms up into the air, Crucifix screamed that he would once again take the fWo Hardcore title, and that Death should have the balls to face him right then. Crucifix smirked, but suddenly the lights went black and pillars of fire exploded from the turnbuckles... Result: Crucifix by Pinfall Death (c) vs. Crucifix Death came to the ring with Stone, War and Plague by his side. Death looked more angry, more intense, and more focused than usual, and when he got in the ring he walked directly up to Crucifix. Crucifix chopped at the Hardcore Champion, but Death shrugged his blows off and grabbed him by the neck. Death clenched his fist down on Crucifix's throat and screamed at the referee to ring the bell. The timekeeper understood, and struck with fear he did what the champion asked. Death now raised Crucifix into the air, letting his legs dangle feet above the mat. Crucifix was worn from the last match and despite his claims could not have been ready for a Hardcore Title match so quickly after suffering through a grueling hardcore match with hardcore legend Cactus Jack. Unable to hold on, Crucifix succumbed to the choke hold and passed out, going limp in Death's arms. The bell was rung as Death tossed Crucifix to the ground and turned around to face the crowd. Grabbing a microphone from the ring announcer Death screamed out at the crowd in attendance. "You all MOCK ME? YOU DARE TO MOCK ME? Dynasty... I'm going to hunt down and kill each and every one of you starting TONIGHT! Ice, you are going to leave this arena in a body bag... and Scott Slugger, I accept your challenge!" The crowd began to boo now, and toss various personal effects into the ring. Death rounded on him, "SHUT UP! All of you just SHUT UP! You think I won't kick all of your asses too? You think I won't do it? GOD DAMMIT, SHUT UP!" The crowd continued to boo, and now Death swung out of the ring and began to randomly punch members of the crowd as the cameras went to the backstage area... Result: Death by Choke Out Employement opportunity Primetime shouted down the hall at some fWo technicians who were drinking coffee and watching the champion with mild interest. "Hey, I've got my bags over here... pick them up dammit... I'm the World Champion, I shouldn't be doing any damn manual labor..." The techs shrugged and didn't move from their positions. One pointed up as his "Union" badge, and informed Primetime that he was on a break. Primetime kicked at the wall, grumbling to himself. "This is just great... just fricking great. A world champion and I have to lug around my own damn bags..." Suddenly a rather large and chubby man lumbered into the scene and Primetime recognized him as Jake, Courage's former bodyguard. "Yeah fatty? What in the hell do YOU want?" Jake pointed to Primetime's bags, and then to himself. "Primetime... sir... I humbly request to be the person you need more than anything now that you're the fWo World Champion and don't have that Mitch guy around anymore. I want to be your lackey." Primetime looked surprised, and then nodded his head. "Yeah... okay... fine then. Pick those up, and start moving tubby." Jake picked up the bags and walked down the hall. Primetime walked behind him, giving him a kick in the ass from time to time to keep him moving... Eron (c) vs. Kid Cool Kid Cool came to the ring with Ultra Violet and Violet behind him. His partner in Ultra Cool 2000 slapped hands with the fans, but Kid Cool looked strangely serious and somber, climbing into the ring with little fanfare. Kid Cool raised his arms up to the crowd, and only looked sadly down at his feet when the crowd seemed to jeer him. Eron came to the ring next, holding his title over his head as he went. Eron sneered at the crowd, and made some lewd suggestive comments to Violet as he passed her. Eron got into the ring and smacked the head of Kid Cool in a show of mocking disgust. The two locked up, with Kid Cool taking the quick advantage and twisting Eron into an armbar. Eron broke free using the ropes, and then spun and delivered hard kicks, which Kid Cool blocked. Kid Cool followed up with a punch to the jaw that sent Eron backward and earned a warning from the referee. Kid Cool attempted a side standing kick, but Eron caught Kid Cool's leg and held it before applying a sukui-nage, which is a judo move much like a powerslam, and then attempted a juji-gatame. Kid Cool blocked this, and kicked into the face of Eron, opening up a large cut in his forehead that had been bandaged. Both men stood to break the ten count, and faced each other once more in the ring, both men breathing hard. Kid Cool grabbed Eron's arm and kicked upward, one kick hitting Eron's face and doing further damage to the exposed wound. Eron broke free of Kid Cool's grip, and fell down to the mat, rolling toward Kid Cool as he went. Eron grabbed Kid Cool and threw him to the mat with hip toss and attempted to put him in a triangle submission hold. Kid Cool saw it coming and reversed the submission attempted, moving over Eron and switching the hold into an Achilles tendon hold. Eron struggled to get to the ropes to break the hold and managed to do so, clinging to the ropes for support and attempting to stand on his damaged leg. Kid Cool on his feet tried to take Eron down, but Eron blocked by holding onto the ropes and kicked up at the midsection to knock the air from Kid Cool and buy him some time to recover. Kid Cool was hit with the first kick but shrugged it off, executing a belly-to-back suplex, then got behind Eron to apply a modification submission hold that was a mixture of a half-nelson and sleeper hold. Eron again struggled to get to the ropes and was finally able to do so before losing consciousness. Kid Cool now turned to the knees of Eron, striking with quick, calculated kicks to the knee cap. Eron dodged one kick and executed a leg sweep. With Kid Cool dazed, Eron grabbed at the head of Kid Cool and slammed it down to the mat from belly-to-belly position. With Kid Cool on the mat Eron drove his knee into the head of Kid Cool and applied the derouger on the mat. Kid Cool attempted to break free, but finally lost consciousness in the center of the ring, and the match was awarded to Eron. Eron grabbed his title, and sneered again at the crowd as he left. Ultra Violet and Violet entered the ring to support Kid Cool, who had come to inside the ring. Kid Cool was encouraged by their comments, but still looked disappointed... Result: Eron by Submission Last minute changes Black Panther and Havok sat backstage, taping up their fists and getting ready for their match later in the night. Both men spoke friendly enough together, despite the fact that they would be wrestling against each other in the main event. As they spoke together a knock came at the door, and Wolf Fang entered. "This is the battle of the millennium men, huh? Well I don't know if you two guys were aware of it, or if you just chose to ignore it, but I was the millennium man in the fWo before you two ever existed here... so I think it's a real joke that you can have a millennium man match without the original... and that's ME." Black Panther and Havok looked at each other and shrugged. Havok stood, facing Wolf Fang directly. "You want in? Fine by me. The more the merrier. Triple threat match, sounds just fine to me. Might was well give wrestling fans ONE good triple threat match tonight..." Wolf Fang smiled, turned, and left as quickly as he came as Havok walked back and finished lacing up the boots. Black Panther nodded with approval of the last minute change and went back to taping his fists... Micky Maliboo vs. Oracle Micky Maliboo came to the ring dressed in street clothes and carrying a microphone. At his side was the lovely Vixon, and the pair looked nice next to Maliboo as they entered the ring. Maliboo smirked, and addressed the crowd. "Well tonight I was SUPPOSED to wrestle one Oracle. But as some of you might already know, our good friend Oracle has turned tail and left the fWo. Why? Because of one man. Me. Micky Maliboo. The man that kicked Oracle's ass out of the fWo. Remember that! Because tonight there will be no match, and that's because of that coward and his big dumb retard friend. So tonight you don't get the pleasure of seeing Micky Maliboo... because nobody has the guts to face me!" Suddenly the lights went dark and fire exploded up from the turnbuckles. Again Death and his Dark Riders walked to the ring, and Maliboo looked at his arrival with concern. Death got into the ring and grabbed Maliboo before he could escape. He screamed out at Maliboo and the referee, "You want a match you little fly? You gnat? I'll give you a damn match... ring the god damn bell!" Maliboo again tried to get out of the ring but Death grabbed him from behind and suplexed him hard into the mat. Death drove his knee down into the throat of Maliboo, and the young superstar was taken by surprise in this quick and deadly attack. Maliboo got to his feet and tried to get some offense going, but Plague and Stone grabbed his legs and tripped him up. Death grabbed the fallen Maliboo and hoisted him up into the air. Yelling out to the crowd, Death executed a brain buster and made the successful cover. Death grabbed a microphone and pointed down at Maliboo. "How many are left Slugger? How many are left you four little bastards? How much blood will be on your god damn hands tonight?" Result: Death by Pinfall Death (c) vs. Ice Finally having enough, Ice walked quickly to the ring with Scott Slugger, Chris Universal and Lance Star. Scott Slugger grabbed a microphone and pointed at Death, "Don't count on that Hardcore Title match you fool... when Ice kicks your ass tonight you won't have a belt to defend, and there won't be a reason to waste my time with you!" Death had heard enough, and attacked from behind with a jumping knee into that back that sent Slugger out of the ring. Death turned his attention on Ice and took his back, but Ice hit a back elbow and ran to the ropes. Death went into the ropes as well, and both big men criss-crossed inside the ring until Ice hit a hip attack as they passed. Ice tossed Death into the ropes to attempted to hit the hip attack again, but he missed the landing and came down on his leg. Ice struggled to get up, and the referee looked in at Ice's leg to see if he had suffered any serious damage. Death came in from behind and tossed the referee out of the way and attacked the leg with kicks into the shin. The referee Deathned Death not to pull some actions again or he would be automatically disqualified, and Death simply looked at the referee and growled. The referee backed off, and Death went back to attacking the leg of Ice, applying a leglock on the Internet champion. Pulling Ice to his feet, Death executed a perfect Teioh lock and turned it into an STF, but Ice escaped to the rope and forced Death to break the hold. Death punched down on Ice grounding him once more, and went up to the top rope. Before Death could launch himself off the top turnbuckle Ice pushed the referee into the ropes, toppling Death and sending him crotch first onto the turnbuckle. With Death still straddling the turnbuckle, Ice did German suplex from the top rope, sending Death down to the mat hard. Death kicked out at two when Ice attempted a cover, and raked the eyes of Ice, earning another Deathning. Ice meanwhile stumbled backDeathd, and Death caught him with a European uppercut. Death then picked Ice up and hit a powerbomb and followed it up with a diving hip attack, and then powerbombed Ice again. Ice was unaffected by this attack and came back at Death with a hard lariat which sent Death to the mat. Ice prepared a piledriver, but Death lashed out with a closed fist into the injured knee of Ice, toppling the big man. Death followed up but was taken down with a sitting spear by Ice, who then attempted to place Death in a Texas Cloverleaf. Death grabbed the rope to break the hold, and then kicked low when Ice released the hold. Death hit a Yakuza kick, but Ice grabbed the legs and rolled Death up for a pin. Death broke free on the two count, and attacked the weakened knee of Ice again, this time behind the referee's back. Death ground in on the knee, but Ice stood and suplexed Death over. Ice covered Death, but Death managed to use his momentum to his advantage, continuing to roll Ice over and cover him in a small package while applying pressure to the weakened knee. Ice kicked out a moment too late, and Death was awarded the match. The Dynasty swarmed the ring and kicked at Death, who was quickly helped out of the ring by the other Dark Riders. Security hit the scene before things could unravel any further as the cameras went to the back... Result: Death by Pinfall Wrestlers in love Backstage Machina had finished watching Eron's match and looked at Rudo. "Catch all his weaknesses... blind spots... areas I can exploit?" Rudo nodded. "Yes... but there aren't many. Eron is one of the great under rated wrestlers in the fWo and he's going to be hard to beat. Real hard. But his legs are his soft spot. Take away the vertical base and he won't be able to apply many of his submission holds. You keep your eye on that and you... Machina?" Machina was distracted, looking out of the room and down the hallway where James Armstrong was speaking to a pretty young woman with a tape recorder. The woman seemed to be asking Armstrong questions, and Machina's attention was fixed upon her. "So... pretty... so... dammit Rudo, I feel something... a strange pounding in my chest. What is this vile feeling?" Rudo looked down the hall at what Machina had been watching. Rudo turned to Billy James, Brandi, and Sid. "Crap. He's in love." Sid blushed, and batted his eyes. Rudo shook his head. "No Sid... with a woman." Underground vs. Mystery Team Back at the ring the fWo Tag Team champions were in the ring, and Mike Bear held a microphone. "Last week we took two old men out of the old folks home, brought them into the ring, and kicked the all mighty living hell out of them. The whole world watched us give those two losers a bigger beating than most of you in the crowd gave yourselves when Vixon was out here. So what we want to know is... who's going to get their asses kicked next?" Red light filled the arena, and a wolf's howl blared over the arena speakers. Out onto the entrance ramp walked Scott Hall and Kevin Nash, the Outsiders. The two walked slowly to the ring, mocking the crowd and aping for the audience. Hall got into the ring and grabbed the microphone and looked out over the crowd. "Hey yo..." But that was as far as he got before Mike Bear charged him from behind, clubbing him in the back and knocking him to the mat. Bear stomped away on Hall's head as the bell rang, and then applied a Boston crab. Nash entered the ring and made the save with kicks. Bear walked to his corner and tagged Wretch in, and the former Chris Kidman Jr. quickly laid the boots to Hall's head. Wretch drug Hall toward their corner and put Hall in a camel clutch. Hall struggled to hold on, and used his height advantage to grab the ropes and break the hold. Hall tried to come back with punches, but they fell short and went wide. Hall appeared drunk, and it took only one forearm smash from Wretch to knock him down. Wretch climbed the ropes and raised his arms for the crowd, who gave him a good reaction. Hall came from behind and hit a vertical suplex on Wretch and tagged in Kevin Nash. Nash and Wretch exchanged forearm smashes, with Nash's size advantage giving him the edge. Nash body slammed Wretch, and the fWo champion stumbled back and tagged in Mike Bear. Bear entered but was hit by a boot to the head, and Nash delivered a body slam on Bear that sent him stumbling to the concrete floor. Nash put Bear in a Boston crab, and laughed at his partner on the outside. Enraged, Wretch made the save with a kick to the head of Nash. The Outsider tagged in Hall, and Scott Hall quickly followed with the same hold. Nash & Hall continued to attack with double team maneuvers, and attacked with a double-team vertical suplex that did great damage to Bear's back. Hall put Bear in over-the-shoulder backbreaker, but again Wretch made the save. Hall tried to get Bear up for the outsider's edge, but Bear escaped on the back and hit Hall with a lariat. Bear finally tagged with Wretch, and Hall stumbled back in fear and tagged in Nash. Nash went for another big boot, but Wretch ducked and hit a hard forearm. Nash tried to go on the attack again, but Wretch switched Nash's power bomb into huracarana. Wretch covered Nash for the count, but Nash kicked free at two. Nash tagged in Hall as Wretch tagged in Bear. Bear went for a running lariat, but Hall ducked under and hit a blockbuster. The crowd began to chant "Bear!" repeatedly as the fWo champion tried to get to his feet. Hall again went for the outsider's edge, but Wretch entered the ring and hit a running dropkick into the chest of Scott Hall, causing him to drop Mike Bear. Bear grabbed the head of Hall, lifted him up, and slammed him into the mat with a dragon screw. Bear made the cover, and got the three count. Hall and Nash stumbled free of the ring and headed for the back as Mike Bear and Wretch held their arms up for the cheers of the crowd... Result: Underground by Pinfall Flying Frenchie vs. VL The crowd sat nerviously, waiting for the main event to begin. Instead, the Frenchie came out onto the entrance ramp. "Finally, ze Frenchie has COME BACK to ze... where in ze hell is zis again? Zut, no matter... ze Frenchie doesn't care what zis hick town is named. Zis is an insult to ze Frenchie just to be here tonight. But because ze Frenchie es ze fighting champion it is only ze fitting that he es to raise ze buyrate by working. VL... get ze little ass into ze ring!" VL walked to the ring, looking annoyed that she was called out at all. She climbed into the ring, and faced the Frenchie. The Frenchie pointed to a brown paper bag at ringside, and told VL to open it for her challenge was inside. VL ignored the Frenchie and instead decked him with a solid punch and walked out of the ring. The Frenchie stumbled to his feet and held up the Womens Title. "Wait! Zu are not going to ever get zis if you..." VL continued walking, holding up double fingers as she went. The Frenchie watched her go, brushing off his wrestling tights. "Feh, zilly woman... not wanting to be part of zis challenge..." Result: VL losses due to beating Wolf Fang vs. Havok vs. Black Panther When the cameras returned to the ring after commercials, Havok and Black Panther were already inside the ring, and were awaiting Wolf Fang for the triple threat main event match. Wolf Fang came out onto the entrance ramp, and he walked to the ring slowly and with great confidence. Inside the ring the three men shook hands, and agreed that it would be a showcase of their athletic ability and free from cheap shots. The Black Panther and Wolf Fang locked up, and Wolf Fang quickly turned the lock up into an arm bar. From behind Havok kicked into the back of Wolf Fang, and then grabbed the Panther. Havok executed a half- nelson suplex on the Panther, and the Black Panther fell to the mat head first. Havok aimed a power bomb, but Wolf Fang attacked from behind with a standing drop kick that took Havok off his feet, tripping him over the prone body of the Panther. Wolf Fang picked up Havok and hit a quick power bomb, but was attacked from behind by the Panther. The Black Panther executed a Tiger suplex on Wolf Fang, and went for a cover. The Black Panther hooked the leg, but Havok broke the count at two with a kick to the Panther's head. Havok went up to the top turnbuckle, and attacked with a diving shoulder block. The Black Panther kicked his face in the air, and Havok stumbled back into the turnbuckle, mouth bleeding. Up on his feet, the Black Panther chopped and hit a spin kick on Havok in the corner. The Black Panther went for a cover, but Wolf Fang interrupted the count and kicked both men in the head. Wolf Fang drug the Panther up and aimed a standing side kick. The Panther caught it, but Wolf Fang switched into a enzuiguri. Wolf Fang chopped the Panther down, and then locked in the Wolf Bite submission hold that he is known for. The hold was locked in right in the center of the ring, and the Black Panther strained to hold on. Just as he was about to tap out, Havok dropped an elbow on Wolf Fang, and all three men retreated to opposite corners to recover. On their feet once again, Havok charged away from the turnbuckle toward Wolf Fang. The Black Panther caught him in the center of the ring and kicked him in the back. The Panther went for a brain buster, but Wolf Fang grabbed him from behind and suplexed him down to the mat. Wolf Fang hit a jumping kick, but Havok grabbed him from behind and executed a back suplex. Havok hit a kakato-otoshi and a power bomb on Wolf Fang, and went for a cover. The Black Panther broke the cover and grabbed the head of Havok in a forward face lock. Wolf Fang got on his feet and applied a forward face lock on the Panther, with both the Panther and Havok trapped in the holds. The Black Panther struggled to get free, and attacked with a backward head butt on Wolf Fang. Wolf Fang stumbled backward and feel from the ring. The Black Panther continued to apply the face lock, and Havok started to fade into unconsciousness. In a last act of desperation, Havok fell forward and grabbed the legs of the Panther as he toppled over Havok. The referee counted the shoulders of the Panther down and got the three count as Wolf Fang dove on Havok and the Panther a moment too late in an attempt to break the count. The referee awarded the match to Havok, and raised his arm to the crowd. Wolf Fang and the Panther watched this, and then raised Havok's arms as well as the crowd cheered for all three men. The cameras went off the air as Slaughter came to a close... Result: Havok by Pinfall
