29 December 1999

She had taken his hand... Come on, Mary, Don't fear the reaper... -Blue Oyster Cult Wednesday began innocently enough. California was unseasonably cold, but cold weather in California is relative and as wrestlers drove to the arena they took note of fans dressed in shorts cheering them as they arrived. The Pond in Anaheim was a historical arena for the fWo, for it was slightly less than two years ago when the fWo held their first professional show in the arena. Among other things, CyberSlam featured a battle royal to crown the first fWo World Heavyweight Champion. Thirty wrestlers were entered in the competition, and by the time the match was over only one remained. Jim Dudley stood as the first fWo World Champion and would have a successful run with the title that would never be erased from the record books. It was from this day that everything else came, and in all this time nobody questioned the events of that first night. Until recently, nobody had a reason to. But like all things, time has a way of changing things, and even the brightest sun must one day fall and give way to darkest night. The Return James Armstrong stood at the door to the backstage entrance of the arena with a wide smile over his face. It had been a successful two months since he took over the reigns of the fWo, despite a few bumps in the road here and there. A few of the bumps were outside the arena now, picketing with their "fWo Is Porn" signs. Armstrong frowned at them, considering for a moment what the young Penny Pureheart would look like without her top. He shook his head to clear this image with some difficulty and turned his attention to the wrestlers arriving for the show tonight. Spotting Briana with the fWo Womens title, Armstrong waved. "Hey Briana, looking good with the gold baby. Hmm... you're looking a little thick around the middle. Must be all that holiday eating. I'll try and get you a match tonight, okay?" Armstrong turned and didn't see Briana giving him the finger behind his back. A new vehicle was approaching and Armstrong didn't recognize the driver. It was a limousine and it came to a slow stop in front of James Armstrong. He blinked at it. The driver stepped out of it walked to the back door, holding it open with silent grace. A foot stepped out, and then the rest of Travis Beaven followed it. Armstrong gasped. Travis walked toward him, looking around himself and taking in his surroundings. He frowned at the people picketing out front, and Armstrong had a moment to notice that he carried a newspaper before Travis had stopped directly in front of him. Travis unrolled the newspaper, silently looking at the cover story: U.S. FILES ANTI-TRUST LAWSUIT AGAINST WRESTLING PROMOTION. Wrestling organization is a monopoly says Justice Department after recent investigation Armstrong gulped as Travis let the newspaper fall to the ground with a light flutter. Armstrong started to speak, but Travis raised his finger and placed it over Armstrong's mouth with a sudden motion. Travis smiled, and Armstrong seemed to relax. Still smiling Travis said, "James... you're fired." Armstrong's mouth fell open as Travis walked into the arena to resume his role as commissioner of the fWo. Amrstrong didn't notice that Rain had stepped out of the limousine behind Travis, or that the usually clear California sky was starting to gather dark storm clouds. Micky Maliboo vs. Joy Inside the ring Micky Maliboo flexed and motioned to the crowd, all the while backed up by the lovely and scantily clad Vixon. Vixon held up a sign reading "Maliboo World Tour: 3-0". The crowd was anxious for some action, standing on their feet and screaming for nearly anyone who came down the entrance ramp. They settled somewhat as Joy walked to the ring waving and handing out balloons with the help of his busty partner in happiness, Glee. The two walked to the ring, and Joy announced that it was time for him to perform his happy dance. He began to bounce up and down, waving his arms and shaking his butt. Maliboo attacked from behind, quickly grabbing Joy around the waist and flipping him up and over to the mat behind him. Joy landed on his feet, but Maliboo slammed into the side of him with his shoulder. With Joy on the mat Maliboo went for a cover, but the wiley veteran kicked free on two and smiled happily at the crowd. Maliboo kicked at his face and walked to the ropes. Maliboo measured the moment, and as Joy began to rise Maliboo attacked with a running leg sweep. Maliboo dropped a shoulder, but Joy rolled free and Maliboo crashed down to the mat. Joy stood on his feet and raised his arms into the air, and then hit the happy drop onto the skull of Micky Maliboo. The crowd cheered, and Joy wiped a single tear from his eye while whispering, "You like me... you really, really like me!" Suddenly the lights blew out of the arena and a pale blue cast shone down on the entrance ramp. At first it remained empty, and then Plague, War and Stone walked out onto the ramp. They heralded the appearance of their leader, and an angry Scott Collins walked to the ring, on his face the very expression of Death. Inside the ring Joy raised his fists and prepared to battle the intruders, yelling that he and Maliboo could fend them off. Maliboo however had left the ring as soon as the lights went off and Joy found himself inside the ring alone. Collins stepped over the top rope and faced Joy, and with a sudden gesture he snapped his fingers. Plague, Stone, and War attacked Joy, driving him from the ring in a brutal fury. Stone produced a microphone and handed it to Collins, and the leader of the Dark Riders screamed into it. "I've had enough of this bullshit! You hear me in the back? I quit!" Trapped The standoff lasted for a moment as the fans waited to see what was going to happen next. Travis sneered at the leader of the Dark Riders, and held up a paper in his left hand. "Do you know what this is you muscle-bound hick? This is your contract... your fWo contract... and if you think you're walking out of this promotion you've got another think coming." Travis smiled at him, and held up his contract. "I own you, Collins. If I want you to wrestle here, you will wrestle here. If I want you to wrestle those matches in pudding, jell-o, or even gravy you will do that, too. Most people wouldn't be stupid enough to sign a contract like this one... but then, wrestlers have never been brain surgeons have they? Now get out of my ring, Collins... get out and get ready to wrestle a match later tonight." The commissioner got into the ring with the lawyers behind him as the Dark Riders shuffled back to the dressing room. The crowd, who had been uncertain of what to make of the events that were transpiring up until this point, now began to boo loudly. One of the lawyers handed the commissioner another paper, and he cleared his throat and began to read from it. "Now shut up everyone... because I've got more information that impacts all of you. While I was at home and recuperating from my injuries, I had a chance to study a lot of old fWo wrestling tapes, and something that was said last year around this time... the 23rd to be specific... on our Warfare program was of keen interest to me. It was announced that the Flying Frenchie had renewed his visa for another year... and for one more year he would be able to work in this country. One more year... that came to an end on the night of the 22nd of December of this year. Which means that I'm asking the fWo World Champion the Flying Frenchie to come out here right now... and hand over his fWo World Heavyweight Championship." Briana (c) vs. Melissa Smalls The fWo Womens Champion Briana walked to the ring with the Womens Title around her waist. As she neared the ring she asked for a microphone, and several wrestling fans in the audience booed at this development. Briana held up the gold and said, "For months this championship belt was around the waist of not only a man, but a stupid, foolish man. He had no right to wear the belt, but at least now I've taken this belt, and I'm going to restore some dignity to the Womens Championship belt. That means no matches with men... and yes Sid, that includes you. So tonight begins the start of putting this title back on the line against real competition... real women... in real matches." Briana slid into the ring after handing the title to the timekeeper as the music of Melissa Smalls began to play. Smalls slid into the ring as the bell rang, and began to shout at Briana. The two grappled, and Briana turned the hold to her advantage and applied a quick sleeper hold. Smalls broke free, and Briana used the ropes to lock in a juji-gatame. Smalls broke free of this as well and tumbled to the outside. Briana followed, and Melissa Smalls drove her into the steel guard rail. Briana got some revenge by driving Melissa's arm into the iron ringpost, and then her head. Rolling Smalls back in the ring, Briana climbed up onto the apron and then up the metal turnbuckle. She dove off with a flying elbow, but Melissa Smalls rolled out of the way at the last second. On her feet, Smalls rose Briana up for a vertical suplex, but got a one count only on the cover. Smalls argued with the ref, and from behind Briana hit a Northern-lights suplex and then a juji-gatame on the recently returned wrestler. The two brawled around the ring and then went outside again, where Smalls picked up a steel chair and hit a stiff chairshot onto the champion. The two went back to the ring where now in control, Melissa Smalls hit a belly-to-back suplex in the ring. Smalls followed this up with a hard running clothesline and a near fall. Smalls got up and raised her arms for the crowd, not noticing Briana getting to her feet behind her. Briana charged, but Smalls ducked under it and hit an elbow to the back from behind. With Briana wobbling, the challenger hit three missile drop kicks in a row, and then a Northern-lights suplex. Smalls made a confidant cover, but Briana kicked out at two and a half. Angry and wanting to finish the match, Melissa Smalls kicked down at the champion and then climbed to the top rope, but Briana caught her on the way up and delivered a suplex from the top rope that brought Melissa Smalls down to the mat directly on her head. She lay still as Briana hooked the leg for the cover and got the three count. Briana raised the title for the crowd and pointed to herself as the crowd loudly cheered the new champion. The ring announcer made his way to the ring prepared to introduce the next combatants, when the number "six" appeared on the Warfare screen. The number exploded into the word "Living". The close-up revealed exactly who it was, as he walked to the ring with shackles on his wrists and ankles... the chains torn in the middle, so the man could walk around with some freedom. The man walked up the ring steps and climbed into the ring, before walking to the center. He knelt, and a cold darkness blanketed the arena as a spotlight shone on him as he began to pray. The light returned and the man asked for a microphone, before he began to speak. The man looked around with some great trepidation and fear in his eyes, and commenced speaking. "You know, it's been a long time since I was last here.And the last time I was here, I had no personality. I had no life. He knelt to his knees as the arena was covered by blackness again. He looked up to the sky as the spotlight shined, before he started yelling again and rolling around on the mat. Security rushed out from the back and tried to contain "Living" from hurting himself, but he fought them off. Suddenly, the stage bursts into the flame like it had twenty-four days earlier, and the number "six" burst onto the screen. It exploded into the words "Heaven" with "Living" below it. The flames were eventually dispersed by some backstage personnel with fire extinguishers. Inside the ring, Living continued to roll around as security brought out a straightjacket, finally restraining the crazed individual... At his side was Fallen, and behind him stood Poison and Demon. Demon was bleeding from the mouth, and on hands and knees repeatedly struck his head into the steel of the ramp. Poison sobbed to herself as the four made their way to the ring, but as they neared the commissioner pointed to Death's accompaniment. "Not them... they aren't allowed here. This match is between you and Rain... no hangers on." Death smiled, and nodded to Fallen, who led Poison and Demon back up the ramp to the back. Death turned. "That's all fine little man... they are needed elsewhere after all. Now come Rain... this is the endgame." Rain charged Death, who was standing on the ring apron. Death was struck and flew backward into the metal guard rail and flipped over it into the crowd. Rain climbed out of the ring as the referee began to lay on a count. The commissioner stopped the referee, urging him to let them go at it and not count them out. In the crowd Rain shoved fans from side to side to get to Death, grabbing a steel chair as he went. Rain swung with the chair, and Death got an arm up to deflect the chair a moment too late. The chair struck the side of his head and Death went down to one knee. Rain swung and hit Death a second time in the head, dropping him to the floor. Smiling, Rain nodded with satisfaction. "All too easy." Rain carried Death back toward the ring, but as they neared the guard rail Death punched Rain until he dropped him down to the floor with a thud. Rain looked at the downed Death and reached for him, but Death grabbed the outstretched arm and pulled Rain down into the steel guard rail. Both men injured, they climbed back over the railing and back toward the ring, finally rolling inside on opposite ends. Death climbed to one knee, still fazed from the earlier blows. Rain looked in better shape, and as he got to his feet he glared down at his opponent. Rain measured Death, and then quickly dropped an elbow into the big man's skull. Rain cursed and spat, and then brought Death up, going for a piled river. He started to pick him up, but Death struggled and regained his footing. Death pushed upward, bringing Rain up and over with a back body drop that sent the big man crashing to the mat. Death stumbled back into the ropes, watching Rain roll to the outside with a crafty eye. On the outside the commissioner hurried to Rain's side and handed him what looked to be a taser of some kind. Rain nodded as he accepted the weapon, and rolled back inside the ring. As Rain moved toward Death, the crowd suddenly came to their feet as a new arrival made his presence known on the top of the entrance ramp. Gasping with surprise the former commissioner and owner of the fWo Travis Beaven stood at the top of the ramp, looking down at the events that were transpiring below with disgust. The Travis at ringside looked stunned, and yelled up to Rain to alert him to the new arrival. Rain heard and looked, and his brow bent with concern as he saw that the real commissioner had arrived at the arena. In his distraction he never saw Death coming, and the leader of the Dark Riders struck from behind with a kick to the back, and a DDT. As the Travis at ringside screamed Death covered Rain, and the counts later the fWo had a new World Heavyweight Champion. Result: Death by Pinfall Closing The crowd booed and began to toss objects toward the ring as Death retrieved the title and held it into the air with a sneer. He then signaled to the top of the entrance ramp, and his Dark Riders appeared, grabbing the Travis that had just arrived and holding him fast. Meanwhile Death grabbed the commissioner at ringside and hauling him inside kicking and screaming. Death lifted the commissioner into the air and tossed him into a turnbuckle, watching as the crystal he held in his fist fell from his hand. Death smiled an evil smile, and before Rain could grab the dropped crystal Death snatched it up. He held it up and turned it over in his hand, smiling at the strange translucent glow that came from the center. Rain struggled to get to his feet, and Death attacked with a hard kick to his ribs that sent the wrestler coughing backward. Death looked back at the crystal again, and then leaned over Rain. "You've used this to build yourself quite a little empire my friend... but the responsibility of this is far to great for one like yourself." With that Death crushed the crystal in his hands, bits of glass and smoke flying from his closed fist. Rain screamed as he did it, and looked in horror as the commissioner he had brought to the arena ceased to exist. On the entrance ramp the Dark Riders brought the true commissioner with them toward the ring, shoving him inside where he stood on hands and knees before the new champion. Travis got to one knee, and then to his feet. He looked up into the face of Death with hatred, and then satisfaction. The crowd roared with anger as the owner and commissioner of the fWo raised the arm of Death into the air. As the cameras faded to darkness, a voice could be heard screaming out at the booing fans as Travis yelled, "I give you... my new champion... Death!"
