They spoke quickly to one another, knowing that if they couldn't figure out a fight plan, a way to take out as many of these yellow bastards as possible before they fell, would be the next best step. Once Vox Nihili were pinned, the killjoy descended like vultures. The killjoy who attacked Flyer before caught Alias in the midsection with a gut punch, and went right after his bad eye, digging his thumb in as Alias fought him off. Alias screamed. It was blood curdling. K-Wolf rushed over to stop him, but was quickly passified with black mist, shot directly into her eyes. Karina fell, blind (although not as much as Alias was at the moment), swinging wildly at the five remaining killjoys surrounding Vox Nihili. Alias, doubled over, was picked up by killjoy. One quick twist, and the killjoy, a snapmare driver, was nailed on the Pulp Hero on the outside of the ring. As K-Wolf bounced around, swinging blindly, quickly and without hesitation, the killjoys went in six opposite directions, each finding their own way out of the arena through the shocked Seattle crowd. When suddenly, we lost power. Murphy's Law, folks. “Lights! DAMNIT GET THE LIGHTS!” could be heard in the background as the announcers position picked up various background noise. People scattered to salvage the main event of the ReAction before Cyberslam! Some rumbling was heard inside the ring. It had already been an exciting, intense no contest. Something that you would definitely expect one week before Cyberslam. Cameras started to flash throughout the arena. The fans were getting restless trying to see what was going on in the ring. The lights flickered for a few seconds, revealing the impossible, and then stayed on revealing the carnage. Bodies littered the floor around the ring. But the crowd gasped at what was inside the ring. Both Deacon and High Flyer were bound to separate crosses in the middle of the ring, and standing in the middle absorbing the crowd's jeers was the enigma known as Killjoy. Or was it? The fans had watched earlier as several killjoys surrounded the ring... and then left through the crowd. This Killjoy was different, statuesque as he held the FWO World Heavyweight Championship high in the air. It was no longer missing. What was lost was now very much found. So was killjoy the man that locked Flyer in the closet and stole the title weeks ago? Was this the killjoy that viciously assaulted Craig Miles? Was this the killjoy who had waged a bloody and brutal war of stolent moments of violence with Vox Nihili, in the weeks leading to Cyberslam? The man asked for a microphone as he placed the World Title over his right shoulder. Killjoy reached into his jacket and pulled out a clipboard with a piece of paper on it. The masked man looked around at his sacrifice in the middle of the ring. He walked over to Flyer and smiled, rather unlike killjoy, and then slapped Flyer in the face. He then turned his attention to Deacon and whispered something in the big man’s ear. Both men started to stir, the crowd stirring more as he reached behind his head and yanked the mask off of his face. The crowd went silent as the man that stood in the ring was none other than… Superstars ‘Superstar’ Vince Jacobs The loudest jeers of the night came from the crowd as SVJ had returned to the FWO. Jacobs slowly made his way over to the World Champion. “Remember me, Jack? The man you screwed three years ago. Not only out of his title, but out of his place within HIS company. You didn’t think that your past sins were going to stay buried did ya?” Jacobs moved in closer to Flyer, almost nose to nose. “I warned you for the past month that you were going to have to pay for your sins. I told you I was going to seek retribution. So I thought to myself where would be a good place to do this… hmmm… Cyberslam.” Jacobs said with a smirk. Flyer started to yell something back at Jacobs. Jacobs held the clipboard up to Flyer’s face. “Oh you mean this contract? I received this contract from a good buddy of mine. You and Deac may know him. He’s the new writer in FWO that's been hanging around backstage. What did you call him Deacon? Napoleon? Conrad? Xeno?” Deacon startled at that last name. "I thought that would have been a huge tipoff, there. But none of those names were his, and if you weren't such a ... mute freak, then you would have talked to Beaven to find out he didn't even hire a new writer. I did. Xan Scott worked for me, and you let him lead you around like a deranged shepherd. By the way, where is Chris Shepherd?" Deacon struggled against his bonds, screaming at Vince as his smile broadened. "Oh, you'll get your hands on me soon enough. I'll see you," And Jacobs' smiling face got within inches of Flyer. "And especially you, at Cyberslam." Jacobs took a pen and signed his named to the contract and threw it on the mat before taking the World Title off his shoulder and placing it on Flyer’s cross. “See ya Saturday.” Jacobs turned to the crowd. “Ladies and Gentlemen, YOUR GOD has returned to the FWO.” Jacobs dropped the microphone, and turned looking at the wrestlers starting to stir on the floor. Alias was the first to stagger to his feet, locked on this site within the ring, the long history between he and SVJ causing another rush of expectation across the arena. Alias looked at Vince in shock garnering a smirk from the Superstar. Looking at Alias, Vince Jacobs pointed at his own left eye. He had left that scar on the Original Pulp Hero. The Superstar had left many scars in the weeks leading up to this moment, he reveled in that fact, as he turned to his World Heavyweight Championship match opponents. High Flyer seethed. Deacon roared. 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs... outstretched his arms, basking in the angry reaction of the crowd and smirked. Cyberslam, it would be the return of more then just the show. So much more.
