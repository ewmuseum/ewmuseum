The Fans Wrestling Organization is synonymous with playing host to a plethora of great champions, who later become legends in the world of sports entertainment. After over a decade of wrestling superiority, the FWO has created men whose names are tantamount to greats such as Ric Flair, Hulk Hogan, Stan Hansen, and other name-worthy legends of the sport. Men such as the Flying Frenchie, Mike Bear, Mitch Wilson, Scott Slugger, and others made their name internationally known by capturing the FWO’s most coveted prize – the FWO World Heavyweight Championship; a championship that demands respect, honor, admiration and congratulations from its champion’s peers. The reasons behind such pride come as a surprise – there stands a man who loathes the very existence of the federation he now represents, there stands a man who was the poster-child for everything that was Anti-FWO; yet, there he sat, in the center of the FWO’s ring, smiling as he proudly held the FWO World Championship above his head to a jeering crowd. He brought the title back down over his lap and, for the first time since signing on the dotted line to the FWO, smiled at his glorious reflection in the FWO World Heavyweight Championship’s golden decorated buckle. From his other hand, the Satanic God lifted the microphone to his mouth and low, amused chuckles expelled from his joyous disposition. ”I believe the old saying goes – Anything can happen in the world of wrestling,” he said as he pulled the championship onto his shoulder and looked out towards his ‘adoring’ fans who shot boos, middle fingers and other ‘positive’ reinforcement his way. “Never has that statement been truer than right now.” Krow brought the title off his shoulder and towards his face. He stared into its hypnotic glow and cracked a sly, arrogant grin towards it. ”At one time, I would’ve spat upon this very championship and gladly burned it into disrepair; but now, as I finally hold this very championship; now that I see the reaction it garners from being in my possession…I realize that actually holding it as it’s rightful owner…” his smile grew wide. “…is so much more fun.” ”And not because I take joy in representing this company, but because I truly enjoy the reaction’s on every single face I’ve encountered since last week.” “The Deacons, the Vince Jacobs, the Rana Venenosas, the Keith Scott Zimmermans, the Jeff Garvins; they all have been trying to attain such success through various ways.
