Introduction Previously... As the fWo moves to the end of their improptu Canadian tour, all eyes are on the fWo Pay Per View event Body Count taking place in just a few short days. For it is BodyCount that will shape the organization for the rest of the year, because the winner of a brutal 40-man main event has an automatic title shot at CyberSlam and whoever the opponent might be. With all eyes on the upcoming show, the question remains who will rise to the occasion and stake out a place for themselves in history?
