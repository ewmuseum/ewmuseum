Introduction Previously... Several months ago the fWo manager and owner, Silver Fox, was attacked by Ray Chavez and put into a coma. The fWo fell into the hands of the Disney Corporation, who over the months have attempted to enforce their will on the product. Many in the fWo longed for the return of Silver Fox, alive and well, to take the promotion back into the hands of those who loved wrestling... but mysteriously Silver Fox checked out of the hospital without anyone in the fWo knowing, and has not been seen since. Meanwhile, Brian Cage and Frank Smyth have worked together to put the biggest hoax the industry has ever known into effect through the national contest "fWo Survivor". With millions watching, Cage and Smyth have built national names for a small roster of virtual unknowns... and hold the clout of a newborn promotion in their hands. Across the sea, the CWL continues to run shows in Europe, but the owner, Terry Nodar, has been restless of late, his stress brought on by the condition of his wife, Penny. Searching for answers that are not there... Terry struggles through the days wishing that his friends the Lone Gunmen were once more by his side. Finally, somewhere in the lost highways of America the man known as Fallen sits and waits for his chance to get revenge on the final object of his insane rage... Ric Chronos... Priorities High Flyer waltzed around backstage, a bit bruised from the beating last week that was driven down onto him by his arch rival, Kellen Kinkade. Since he had his opponent down, Halo figured it was time to get the plunder, as he hopped out of the ring and began to dig underneath it. He pulled out a trashcan and tossed it into the ring, hitting Dog in the back of the head as he attempted to get up. He then slid in two trashcan lids, not stopping to think why there were two lids, but only one trashcan underneath the ring. Halo re-entered the ring right as Dog got to his feet and grabbed the trashcan. Dog heaved the trashcan at the Halo, but Halo ducked and ran toward Dog. He jumped into the air and it looked like he was going to attempt a hurricanrana, but Dog threw him over his head. Unbeknownst to Dog, however, Halo landed right on the top rope, and he waited for Dog to turn around before he flew off with a corkscrew somersault bodyblock. The protege of Black Quicksilver then grabbed the trashcan lids and waited for Dog to get up. *CLANG* No one knows WHY Dog is known as "Dog"...Dog probably doesn't even know why he's called "Dog". But at this moment in time, he could've sworn he saw a bunch of little chihuhuas running in circles around his head. He didn't know why he saw chihuahuas. He was pretty sure he doesn't have any Mexican heritage. Dog continued to just stand there, dazed from having a trashcan lid hit him on either side of his head at the same time. He didn't have much time to recover before Halo grabbed the trashcan, placing it over his head and upper body. Dog stumbled around with his arms trapped at his side, unable to see where he was going. Halo sent a dropkick right into the trashcan, causing Dog to bounce off the ropes, and it was then that Halo took him down with a drop-toe-hold. He made sure Dog was in position and climbed to the top rope, delivering the Road To Ruin, crushing the trashcan that was still covering Dog. Halo shook off the small amount of pain he felt from hitting the trashcan, then removed it from Dog, who was easy prey for a three-count. Halo stood up and had his hand raised in victory, but he knew it wouldn't be this easy at Unleashed when he meets Lindsay Troy for the Hardcore Title.
