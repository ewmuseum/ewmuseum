Wednesday Warfare, 8 December 1999

8 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "I am the champion... I AM THE CHAMPION," sang Joy as he wandered down the backstage hallway of the Cancun arena. Several wrestlers were arriving at that moment, and nearly all looked annoyed at the high pitched whine of the new fWo Hardcore Champion. Closer inspection revealed that Joy had spray pained daisies on the Hardcore belt, a fact that caused a nearby Mike Bear to shake his head in disgust. Bear nudged Wretch to take a look at the belt mentioning, "Man... you thought that title went in the crapper when Collins got it... man, now look at it." Joy continued to parade around the locker room, beaming with pride at his new acquisition and getting several pats on the back from Glee. On another side of the locker room, Havok pointed to a newspaper article with dismay. "You see what this reporter is writing about us, Fang? 'fWo is Porn'... what a load of crap! They have all these quotes from Armstrong... man, someone is screwing us on the inside... hurting our name. Who would do such a thing?" Wolf Fang shook his head. "Well, I'm sure James will have something to say to whoever is responsible... he can have quite a temper you know. Armstrong wouldn't let some moron tell some reporter all these things about us." Havok nodded in agreement as Eron walked into the room, followed by Stinky. Stinky was loaded up with his bags and looked rather unstable as he trudged through the room, but Eron wasn't paying attention. Eron's gaze was focused on the members of NXS, all of whom appeared to be mocking him. Steel stood, and grabbed at his crotch. "Hey Eron... I've got your taste of death right here!" Eron's eyes narrowed, and he started over toward the small group before fWo security got between him and the three members of NXS. Austin the Giant and John Willis both strained to hold him back, and loud words were shouted. Kid Cool wandered through the room, followed closely behind by Violet. Kid Cool had an angry expression on his face, and several fWo stars noticed that he wasn't wearing his fWo Tag Team Title, something that he hadn't parted with since he won the belt a few weeks earlier. Violet was close behind Kid Cool, and the young lady was dressed in a very provocative silk dress that was cut high up the leg. Violet was beaming at Kid Cool, and winking at him. Kid Cool didn't seem to notice, and appeared to be lost in his anger as he exited from the other side of the room. Frankton and Relgan wandered into the room, and were accompanied by Lynn, who held a clipboard with the night's matches posted on it. Lynn posted the matches up on the wall, and several workers crowded around to see who would be wrestling that night. As they noted their places on the card Ultra Violet wandered into the arena loading area with a perplexed look written across his face. He held a bottle of water, and looked at it suspiciously. "Now is it DON'T drink the water, or drink as much of it as you can... hmmmm." Deciding he was thirsty Ultra Violet opted for the later, not noticing that a hush had fallen over the wrestlers in attendance. Things grew more quiet as Rain made his appearance, accompanied by several strangely familiar fWo superstars in his entourage. The fWo roster gave Rain a wide berth as he came, still uncertain about the fWo Internet Champion's newfound friends. Rain took in the uneasiness of the wrestlers with a smile, confidant that he had unnerved those in his vicinity. Some arguments came from the office of the acting commissioner, and upon further inspection it was revealed that it was Cactus Jon making the most of the noise, accompanied on either side by Tom the Bomb and the Dark Ninja. Jon was shaking a form in front of James Armstrong's face, and the commissioner was trying to keep his composure in front of the pretty Penny Pureheart who sat in a chair next to him. James sighed, and signed the form Jon was waving at him. Jon nodded, and left the room with the talent contract forms for the Ninja and Tom signed and approved under his arm. James turned to Penny Pureheart and said, "Man, first the fWo gets slandered in the press because some damn fool in the organization can't keep their mouth shut to the reporters and now this... more Canadians. Can you believe it?" Penny Pureheart pressed the record button on her tape recorder and smiled a questioning smile. James Armstrong continued, speaking with the air of someone who hasn't given what he is about to say much thought. "I mean... sure it could be worse... we could have an influx of Polish wrestlers... I mean, did you ever hear the one about how many Polish wrestlers it takes to buy a vial of steroids? Aw... sure you haven't... but I suppose it could be worse, we could get feminazi lesbian wrestlers in here, am I right, toots? Yeah, I'm right... hey, want to go get a hotel room and see the fifteenth wonder of the world?" Penny blinked in surprise, and raised the tape recorder closer to James. James didn't notice it, and shook his head. "Nah... on second thought I better not. Not with some damn fool leaking things to the press and me needing to keep an eye out for who the idiot is. Have to take a rain check on that honey..." Outside of the commissioner's office and across the large arena entrance room stood Machina, looking through a pair of binoculars. He was obviously looking through them backwards, and his mouth was open in horror. Nudging Rudo, Machina pointed to the binoculars. "My god... they've gone all fuzzy!" Inside the arena loud explosions were heard, and wrestlers groaned. Wednesday Warfare had officially began. Hiroshima vs. Boston Strangler The arena lights turn blood red as Hiroshima makes his way to the ring. Walking purposefully, he climbs the ring steps and waits in the corner for his opponent. The Boston Strangler comes out next, to a strong response from the fans. Ignoring it, he marches to the ring and climbs unto the apron, pausing to stare down Hiroshima. Hiroshima keeps his eyes locked on the Strangler, and the stare down is maintained even while the Strangler climbs through the ropes. The bell rings, and they lock up. Strangler takes the early advantage with a hammerlock into a belly-to-belly suplex. He stays on top of Hiroshima, raining punches down on him. Hiroshima rolls him over, then starts letting loose with some palm strikes. The Boston Strangler boxes Hiroshima's ears, then grabs his head and starts butting it, taking control once more. He stands back up, bringing Hiroshima up with him. Grasping Hiroshima's throat with both hands, he deftly lifts him into the air, choking him. Hiroshima's legs dangle beneath him as he struggles for air, and the ref begins his count to break the blatant choke. The Strangler drops Hiroshima at four, then glowers over him while he rasps. The ref checks on the older wrestler, finally deciding that the match can continue. Hiroshima slowly gets back to his feet, walks towards the Strangler, as the men thump chests into each other. Hiroshima slaps the Boston Strangler, who shoves Hiroshima backwards in return. BS charges Hiroshima, opening himself for the dreaded DTH. Hiroshima covers for two, but the Strangler kicks out in impressive fashion. Back up, Hiroshima dishes out some brutal knife-edge chops, forcing BS into a corner. He continues chopping away until the Strangler turns the table, trapping Hiroshima in the corner. He wails on him with forearm shots to the head, then with one hand chokeslams him. He picks him up without covering, and darts behind him to lock in the Boston Massacre. Instinctively, Hiroshima tosses Boston Strangler with a Judo throw before he can cinch it in, then falls back into the corner to get a breather. The Strangler gets back up and grabs at Hiroshima, but is met with a chop to the throat. Hiroshima then bursts out of the corner and levels the Strangler with a lariat, then slips on a full nelson on the mat. He rolls the Strangler into a pinning situation, earning two before the Boston Strangler breaks it with a kick to Hiroshima's head. The Strangler gets back to his feet and darts behind Hiroshima, then locks in the Boston Massacre successfully. Hiroshima fights the urge to quit for all of ten seconds before the excruciating pain of the hold forces him to submit. The Strangler releases the hold as a wry smile creeps across his face. He motions across his waist, calling for his title shot. He mouths the name "Primetime" a few times, then leaves the ring, heading backstage. Result: Boston Strangler by Submission Chapter One: Surprise guests Micky Maliboo was prepared backstage for his interview, and the lovely Vixon stood temptingly by his side rubbing his shoulder. Micky started to walk out of the arena entrance but was surprised as a man in a leather jacket and a dirty crop of blond hair pushed past him. Micky got a better look at who it was, and at once instructed Vixon to get someone from security. The crowd settled and looked with surprise as Oracle walked down to the ring, dressed in street clothes and carrying his own microphone. Oracle slid into the ring and turned, looking at the capacity crowd around him. The crowd booed Oracle, remembering the disparaging comments he made about the fWo roughly a month ago before he claimed he was leaving for good. Oracle started to speak, stopped, and waited for a moment as the crowds continued to boo the former Tag Team champion. Finally, Oracle raised the microphone to his lips and began to speak in loud, commanding tones. "You boo me? Huh? Is that the way it's going to be tonight... you boo me? Listen... I gave everyone in this promotion everything I had for months upon months... and this is the way you treat me? Listen up fans... and all you clowns in the back had better listen up too. I got screwed by the fWo, I got screwed because they didn't bother to find out if I was doing the things they accused me of. It's just taken on the word of one little bastard in the back that I'm missing shows and fighting too stiff... well that's bullshit and you all here know it. Oracle delivered a year ago and was responsible for one of the moment memorable feuds in the history of the promotion with a man you all might know... the Boston Strangler. Yeah, I was getting screwed back then too... remember that straightjacket match? Yeah, that didn't go the way you and I know it should have gone, but that's water under the bridge. I was ready to forget all of that if it wasn't for one man badmouthing me in the back... I think you know who I'm talking about too. The man who stole the push that was supposed to be mine, all because he convinced the writers in the back that I was over the hill... that he represented the new generation." Oracle paused for dramatic effect, noticing that the crowd had stopped booing him and were now listening in with interest. "Eron... get your ass down here and bring the title that should be mine with you!" The crowd began to chant Eron's name, and it wasn't long before the fWo International Champion made his appearance at the top of the ramp, surrounded with fWo security. Eron pointed down at Oracle, telling them that he shouldn't be here, and that he wasn't even under contract. Security moved in, and Oracle laughed, "Oh, so this is how it's going to be? You've got all the guts in the world when you're badmouthing me to the writers, but now all of a sudden you've got to have twenty guards backing you up? And where's you're little bitch without the noise tonight? He backstage warming up your bed?" Eron's eyes narrowed, and as the security guards entered the ring and took Oracle peaceably, Eron reached over the guard rail and closed his fist around a steel chair. Security cuffed Oracle, and marched him up the entrance ramp to the backstage area, and past Eron. As the passed the International champion, Eron pushed on the guards out of the way and swung with the steel chair, connecting with Oracle's skull. Oracle crumpled, and Eron tossed the chair at him as he began to shout. "You think that's funny? You think you're a god damn comedian old man? I'm going to beat your ass like I did in the old days... quick and easy and there's not a damn thing you can do about it grandpa... so suck it up and you come on back to the ring... and I'll give you a good breath... of fresh death! Got me gramps? Got me?" Eron continued to shout, but his words were lost in a chorus of boos as Wednesday Warfare went to a commercial... Black Quicksilver vs. Wretch vs. Chameleon December 8, 1999 The cameras headed to the ring for the next match, pitting Chameleon, Black Quicksilver and Wretch against each other in Triple Threat Rules. Chameleon was already in the ring, and without the support of Total Eclipse and Silver Fox. "We're Not Gonna Take It" erupts from the sound system, bringing out a Stump-wielding Black Quicksilver. He wastes no time going after Chameleon, alternating between punching him with his left hand and letting Stump attack him. The ref had no choice but to ring the bell before Wretch had arrived. Black Quicksilver slides to the outside and sits Stump on the Timekeeper's table, then slides back into the ring to continue his assault on Chameleon. Wretch finally gets to the ring as Black Quicksilver is stomping away at Chameleon in the corner. Black Quicksilver and Wretch look at each other, then look at Chameleon, and apparently have an idea. The two of them bring Chameleon back to a standing position, then Wretch lifts him on his own shoulders as Black Quicksilver springs to the top rope. Black Quicksilver nimbly turns around before flying off the top and catching Chameleon with a hurricanrana off of Wretch's shoulders. Both men roll out of the ring after this maneuver, but Wretch keeps the referee from counting them out. On the outside, however, Black Quicksilver and Chameleon start brawling up the entrance ramp, and the referee just disregards Wretch's pleas and starts laying the count down on them. At about five, Silver Fox and Total Eclipse come down the entrance ramp to enter the fray. Black Quicksilver holds them off for a bit, before succumbing to the numerous foes as the referee finishes his count, and awards the match to Wretch. Wretch leaps outside the ring and grabs Stump, then flings him to Black Quicksilver. Obviously, the advantage is given back once more to Black Quicksilver, who, along with Stump, drive Chameleon, Total Eclipse, and Silver Fox to the back. The crowd, noisy as the superstars made their way back to the ring, did not pay even the slightest notice to the Chameleon or Silver Fox, who stood near the ring and were engaged in what, if anyone was watching, appeared to be a very significant conversation. "I'm tired of this, I really am... I'm going to get out of the business... become a trainer. Perhaps out in California..." The Chameleon looked at Silver Fox, who had just uttered these words, with an eyebrow raised under his mask. "Well old friend," said the Chameleon, smiling ever so slightly at the irony of it all, "tonight is my last match as well. For I have planted the seeds I need to plant here, and done all that I needed to do. Now I will wait, bide my time, and come at this another way, and from another angle." Silver Fox smiled. "Always full of schemes, aren't you? Well, just make sure you don't get me wrapped up in your crazy plans." The Chameleon nodded and clapped his friend on the back. Under the mask it was always amazingly easy to lie. "Of course not old friend, of course not." Result: Sid loses by no woman kissing Eron (c) vs. Flying Frenchie As Eron's music began to play, the Frenchie took the time to pose and grin arrogantly on the second turnbuckle, thinking he already more or less has the title won. Eron enters for the second time in the night to a loud chorus of boos. He holds the International Title high, smirking at the fans as he rolls into the ring and attempts to start the match quickly by hammering Frenchie with the title. Frenchie waits for Eron to get up, then slingshots himself over the inside of the ropes and to the outside, landing on the second rope and leaping off with an Asai moonsault, taking Eron back down. Frenchie gathers Eron up and tosses him unceremoniously into the ring. He kicks Eron in the back of the head, causing him to sit up, and then runs the ropes and knocks Eron back down with a seated dropkick directly to the mouth. Eron rolls to the outside and retrieves his title belt, and rolls back into the ring. Eron gathers up his title and leaves as well, leaving the Marshal alone and wondering where his handservants are. Havok brought Ice near the ropes and placed him neck first on the top rope, before bouncing off the ropes on the outside, and returning to land on his back with full force. Havok tagged out to Wolf Fang, and Wolf Fang came in and applied a sleeperhold. Wolf Fang maintained the move for a few minutes, before the referee raised Ice' arm twice. On the third, Slugger came in and dropkicked Wolf Fang, which propelled him and Ice into the referee. The referee went down, and both men were out of it for several moments. Ice got to his knees and crept over, and instead of tagging in Slugger, he tagged out to Heftel. Heftel came in and was surprised to even be competing, but went ahead and did so anyways. Heftel went for a big kneedrop and Wolf Fang moved out of the way. Heftel hit the mat hard and grasped his knee in pain. Wolf Fang tagged in Panther, and Panther came in and pressed Heftel high above the ring and dropped him quickly. He hit the mat hard, and then Panther looked at Havok and seemed as if he had something up his sleeve. With the referee still out, Ice came in and attacked Panther from behind, but Wolf Fang joined in the fray. Wolf Fang and Havok fought to the outside with Slugger & Ice, while Panther positioned Heftel for his patented superplex. He hit it, and Heftel was out cold. Havok escaped from the brawl and climbed up to the top rope, before he came off with his patented Millennium-sault. He had him pinned as Panther and Slugger mixed it up on the outside, but the referee was still out of it. Havok nudged the referee several times, to no avail. Universal came in from behind and spun Havok around and nailed him with the "Cha-Ching!", before placing Heftel on top of Havok. Universal then grabbed some money out of his jacket and placed it into the referee's pants pocket, before waking him up. The referee counted the pin, and Universal went to the outside. He grabbed a chair and whacked Panther from behind, and Slugger broke free. Ice, Slugger and Universal walked up to the stage, while Wolf Fang checked on his fallen partners... the crowd remained stunned that they had just witnessed an actual pinfall from Mike Heftel. Universal looked into the cameras and said "Money can buy anything. ANYTHING!", as Warfare went to a commercial... Result: Dynasty by Pinfall Chapter Four: Layers upon layers Death limped into the arena, one leg bandaged along with heavy bandages wrapped around his head. His eyes were circled with bruises, but an angry glare burned from his eyes. At his side was Stone, and behind her walked Plague and War. Unlike the Dark Riders the fWo fans had come to know, these Dark Riders looked grim and completely devoid of humor. Stone spotted James Armstrong and grabbed him before he could run away. "Spencer... where is he?" James stammered, unsure of what to say to this obviously angry group of wrestlers. "I... I... I haven't seen them show up... t... t... they weren't scheduled to be here tonight... none of them..." Death leaned forward, wincing in pain as he did so. "You're the commissioner, Armstrong. Sign them to matches next week. Make sure that they are here... and we will take care of the rest." Death leaned closer, glaring hard at Armstrong. "Don't... forget... Armstrong." Death released him, letting James Armstrong stumble back weakly into the arena wall. The Dark Riders stepped back, and began to talk amongst themselves. None of them looked up, but even if they had the sight of a security camera would not have seemed very strange. They had no way of knowing that on the other end of the camera, evil watched them with a menacing smile drawn across his face. David Spencer sat in the center of a dark room, with Fallen standing next to him. The two were illuminated only by the bluish glow of the security screen. Spencer smiled as he watched the Collins and his group of Dark Riders on the screen, and turned to Fallen. "So... Scotty thinks he can put us into a trap. How interesting... what do you say, friend?" A figure hidden in shadows just beyond the glare of the television moved slightly, and cleared his throat. A familiar voice floated up from the darkness, and Spencer smiled wider as the shadowy figure spoke. "I say we show him exactly what a trap is, Spencer..." Primetime (c) vs. Courage Time for the main event came as Warfare returned from the commercial break. Out came the challenger, Courage, who walked out onto the stage with his arms outstretched. He yelled out "C'mon, baby!" and proceeded to prepare himself for his big world title match. Courage ignored the chants of "Go, Jericho Go!" and rolled underneath the bottom rope. "Bawitdaba" began to play and out came the reigning fWo World Champion Primetime, and the crowd let out an ovation of boos as Primetime strolled down to ringside. Primetime climbed into the ring and the match was on, as Courage speared him into the turnbuckles so hard the title popped off the waist of Primetime. Courage followed by whipping him over the top rope onto the cement floor. Courage continued his attack after gaining some momentum and jumping over the top rope onto Primetime with a flying elbow to the face, which hit Primetime square in the jaw. Courage brought Primetime over by the commentators table and slammed him face first into it, before attempting to whip him back first into the stairs... Primetime reversed that however, and instead Courage' bare flesh hit the cold steel. Primetime rested, before rolling into the ring and playing up to the booing crowd. The crowd let him know just how they felt about him, by throwing cups of soda and empty nacho containers into the ring. Primetime asked for a microphone and said he would stop the match because he is the fWo World Champion, and he has that kind of stroke around here, since he is friends with Frankton and Ralgin. The crowd continued to throw objects, and one fan even grabbed a small kid nearby and tossed him several rows forward. Primetime said he had enough of the fans and their trash so he was leaving. He went to the outside and grabbed his fWo World Title, but Courage grabbed part of it and a struggle for the belt ensued. Primetime got the better of it and Courage went backwards, stumbling over the ring steps of which he just hit into. Primetime began to walk up the ramp before he looked back one last time at Courage who crept to his feet. Primetime held his title in the air and boasted about being the champion, but as he turned around he bumped into the Boston Strangler. Primetime dropped his title in fear and walked backwards, as Strangler looked at him with an evil smile. As Primetime reached the ring, Courage grabbed him from behind and rolled him into the ring. Courage went up to the top rope and waited for Primetime to get up, before Courage flew through the air with a high cross bodyblock. The momentum resulted in Primetime being on top of Courage instead of vice versa, and the referee counted the pin. At the count of two, Courage kicked out. He got to his feet and stomped Primetime's head repeatedly, as Strangler picked up the World Title and stared at it, not taking his eyes off of it for several minutes. The match continued as Primetime ended up going for his Primetime Bomb, but Courage reversed his way out of it into a hurracarana. He hooked the legs, but Primetime kicked out at two. Primetime and Courage got to their feet and charged at each other for a clothesline, and both men hit. The referee laid down a ten count and at the count of eight, Courage got to his feet. Primetime did the same, and he charged at Courage, who backdropped him over the top rope onto the floor. Courage then waited for Primetime to get to his feet again, and Primetime grabbed a steel chair. He went back towards the ring and Courage baseball slid into the chair which slammed him right in the face. Courage went to the outside and grabbed Primetime in a waistlock, but Primetime did a go-behind and waistlocked Courage. He then spun Courage around, and kicked him in the stomach. He then power bombed him through the ringside announce table, and shards of glass from the monitors went flying everywhere. Pieces of wood flew into the crowd, and Primetime looked shocked at the result. He rolled into the ring and demanded for the referee to count Courage out. As the referee reached eight, The Boston Strangler climbed into the ring and slammed Primetime in the back with the fWo World Title. The referee called for a DQ, and Strangler attacked Primetime relentlessly. First with the title, and second, he applied his famous "Boston Massacre" Crossface Chickenwing. The referee awarded the match to Primetime since it was Primetime that was attacked. Courage struggled into the ring from behind the Strangler, and whacked him over the head with a steel chair. Courage then helped Primetime to his feet, and the two proceeded to pound the Strangler relentlessly, including a double-team powerbomb on the steel chair. As Warfare came to a close, Primetime and Courage shook hands and spit on the Strangler, before leaving. They walked up the ramp and shared some laughs, as Jake came out to greet them. Courage and Primetime ignored him, and walked backstage as Wednesday Warfare faded to black... Result: Primetime by DQ
