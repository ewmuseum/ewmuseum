fWo — Card

Wednesday Warfare, 26 April 2000

26 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Upping The Ante Dead turned a corner, before reaching several doors. He walked down the hall, until he found the door he was looking for. He knocked. For several moments, there was no answer, until the door creaked open, and there stood the man he would be up against in a mere seventy two hours: The Boston Strangler. Dead smiled evily as The Strangler glared at him. "This... is not enough." Dead said, pointing to the fWo World Title. "I want to up the ante... I want more than this to be on the line." The Strangler hesitated. "We've already put our career on the line before, Davis... and we won." Dead interrupted him, something very few could do without having their teeth smashed down their throats. "No, Paul... I've changed my mind. I can end your career Saturday by simply giving you an injury that you wouldn't recover from... I want the stakes to be higher than your career... I want the stakes to be higher than this strap of gold... I want it all. You know what I want." The Boston Strangler nodded... not taking his eyes off of the man he didn't trust as far as he could throw (which is pretty damn far). "Yes... we know. But that decision is not up to us. We're afraid you're stuck with what you've been dealt with, Davis." Dead would not take no for an answer. "I always get what I want, Paul. Always. You know who to speak with about this situation. Go to them. Get this done." "And if we don't, Davis?" The Strangler shot back. "There will be no match this Saturday." Before the Strangler could reply, Dead stormed off in another direction. The Strangler stammered, kicking his lockerroom door off it's hinges. Without hesitation, he walked away. Searching for what Dead had requested. Meanwhile... in another section of the arena... In Training, Part One Sweat poured off of his face as he pressed the weights above his chest. He laid on the weight bench, and in a rare moment, we saw what he looked like out of character. Nobody knew exactly what his character was, since he changed it all the time, but for awhile he had a niche in the fWo as Silver Slowback. That all changed when something got into some of the regular tag teams the fWo had, and 'caused them to betray their respective partners. The Stump Squad relationship was different however. They not only had broken up, but they got back together after a match. And after that, they broke up again. And now they're having another match, or so Finn and Armstrong informed them earlier today before Warfare began. Finn recommended that they train for it, as to not screw up on the biggest day of the year for the fWo. Heftel took this suggestion very seriously, because he's Heftel, and he takes everything seriously. Well, almost everything. Kinda. Sorta. Okay, he takes very little seriously, but that's beside the point. For once in his life, he was taking something seriously: CyberSlam. He continued to bench press the heavy weights above his chest with a look of satisfaction on his face as he breathed. The camera shifted to a different view that revealed Heftel wasn't lifting very much. In fact, it could be said that smaller men could lift more with ease. A mere forty pounds resided on the bar... but to Heftel, he was lifting the weight of the world... off of his shoulders. Ric Chronos vs. Hellrazor vs. Sphere vs. The Future Frenchie Finally, Warfare headed inside the Arco Arena for the first match of the evening. Beats from "Christian Says" by Tones On Tail are heard all-over the arena, which brings out Ric Chronos, greeted by a warm POP from the crowd. Accompanied by Fallen, Chronos slaps a few hands before entering the ring. His music is faded out, and then replaced by Result: The Future Frenchie by pinfall Monumental Request Chris Finn & James Armstrong sat in their office, conversing about the huge event to occur in three days. "You know, we're a pretty popular organization, James... but I have a feeling about Saturday. I think... this show... could really put us on the map." Finn half-smiled. Armstrong nodded. "Yes, but with a huge main event, and a solid line-up overall... well, with the exception of everything that doesn't include the Foundation... what do you expect?" Finn' half-smile turned to a full blown sigh. "You're obsessed..." He was interrupted by a knock. Finn welcomed them in. It was The Boston Strangler. Just his mere presence made Armstrong cower in fear in the corner, not saying a word. "Come in, Paul... what can I do for you?" "You heard Davis, did you not?" The Strangler answered the question with a question. Finn seemed taken aback... "No. I didn't. Why? What has he gotten us into now?" The Strangler' head looked at the ground. "He wants it all." The native of Boston, MA replied with a sigh. "Wants what?" The interim owner of the fWo asked quizically. "Control. He wants it all. He wants control of the fWo. He has asked us to request that you put it on the line on behalf of us. If he wins, he gets control." "And if he loses?" Finn asked with a puzzled look on his face. "He didn't say. But we're sure something can be arranged, Finn. We're confident in our ability that we will be victorious this Saturday, Finn. You've had faith in us before. Please do not lose that faith now." The Strangler stated boldly. Finn shook his head negatively. "We can deny his request. What can he do about it?" "He said the match would not go on. You know about the contract he has... the minimal amount of control he has right now simply isn't enough for him. He wants more. He wants to own... us. We're not referring to just ourself, either. We're referring to the entire company." Finn sighed. He looked at Armstrong, who had his ability to speak taken away the instant the Strangler entered the room. "Okay. Tell him we'll do it. But he has to give us something in return. We will comply with his request, and all four of us will sign the contract later this evening. I'll have the lawyers print up the proper paperwork. See to it that Davis gets word of this." A relieved look fell over the Strangler. "Thank you, Finn. We promise you that you will not be disappointed." The Strangler exited the room, as Armstrong came out from hiding. "What a nut." Threats As Primetime entered his lockerroom, he noticed a tv stand which in addition to the tv on it, had a VCR below it. Primetime glanced down and noticed a tape in the VCR. A label on the tape read Brian. He pushed the tape in the VCR, and the VCR immediately began playing. Onto the screen came an image of a glass door, with white lettering that read "Wilson Training Academy. Sacramento, CA". Primetime grimmaced, before the video continued playing. We were taken from the door of the facility to inside the facility itself. Inside sat a wrestling ring, where Mitch Wilson (the owner and creator of Wilson Training Academies throughout America) was demonstrating to some newer students some punishing blows. He stretched them out with various submission moves, and the camera zoomed in on one particular student, who writhed in pain because of a leglock that Wilson had cinched in. The camera then focused on Wilson, who stared directly into the camera and calmly spoke. "This is what is going to happen tonight... and more importantly; this is what is going to happen at CyberSlam. Any questions, Brian?" The tape faded to black, before static flashed onto the screen of the tv. Primetime felt like pounding his fist through the screen, but remembered that wouldn't be very smart. Instead he pushed it off of the tv stand, before removing the VCR from the stand, and tossing it to the ground, smashing it into a thousand pieces. Primetime then looked at the camera and spoke as calmly as Wilson did. "When I get done with you at CyberSlam, Mitch... your body is going to be in a thousand pieces, just like this VCR is, punk." Primetime walked out of the lockerroom, as Warfare went to a commercial break. Rain vs. Eron The Relentless Warfare is brought back from commerical with a match involving two former Internet Title champions. "Physical" by Nine Inch Nails sends Eron the Relentless out toward the ring. He is swallowed up in a sea of boos as his cocky smirk tells the crowd what they can do what their noises. He slides in the ring and awaits the man from whom he took the Internet strap at Invasion, back in January. Megadeth blasts over the speakers, and a much warmer reaction is given to Rain. Rain understands that this reaction is due to the fact that he is wrestling perhaps the most hated man in the sport. Rain climbs onto the apron, and his 7'2" frame begins to lumber over the top rope. Eron begins to rush in for an attack even before the bell rings. Rain, however, has anticipated this from the beginning, and catches Eron with his hand around the relentless one's neck. He picks up Eron, and tosses him over the top rope, chokeslamming him to the mats below. Eron struggles to his feet, and sees a rather interesting-looking fan. He is wearing a beret, and is swearing at Eron in some sort of language. Eron can only make it out to be some sort of Spanish or choppy Turkish, but it's all the same to him. He plucks the beret off the man, and proceeds to spit into it. He then takes the man's orange soda and pours it into the beret, which is then set back onto the man's head. The crowd boos even louder at this. Eron smirks and turns about to climb back into the ring. However, Rain has other ideas. He leaps off the apron with a double axe-handle to the head of Eron, knocking him to the ground. The crowd cheers, and Rain drags Eron into the ring. Rain begins to pick up Eron; Eron decides to get the upper hand by low-blowing Rain, sending him to the mat. The crowd boos, and Eron could care less. Eron decides to work on the back of Rain. He achieves this by stomping and dropping elbows repeatedly to Rain. He picks up Rain and sends him to the turnbuckle. Eron follows this by clotheslining him up against it. He sends Rain to the other turnbuckle, and follows with another clothesline. Eron watches Rain stumble out of the corner, and proceeds to bulldog him to the mat. Eron turns Rain over and goes for a cover. He gets two before Rain kicks out. Eron glares at the ref, complaining of a slow count. He goes back to work on Rain, splitting the legs of the big man. He asserts himself into the rather personal life of Rain, giving him a headbutt to the groin. The fans boo at Eron's attempt to disrupt Rain's ability to procreate in the future, and Eron responds by delivering another headbutt to the groin. Then a foot to the midsection. Then a boot to the head. Eron picks up Rain and delivers a dropkick to his back. Rain staggers, but shall not fall. Eron hits another dropkick; Rain does not fall again. Eron gets angry, and hits a Russian leg sweep. He splits Rain's legs again, and this time gives him a wishbone. Apparently, Eron does not wish for the Rain family tree to continue. Eron gets up and takes off for the ropes. He leaps and hits a springboard moonsault off the second rope. Eron goes for another cover. At two-and-a-half, Rain kicks out. Eron glares at the ref once again, before picking up Rain. He works on the back once more, delivering a snap suplex. Eron picks up Rain again, and this time lands consecutive German suplexes. Another cover by Eron, and another two-count. Eron can't stand this, and he pushes the ref. The ref pushes back. Eron pushes the ref again. The ref pushes back, this time sending Eron to the mat. The crowd erupts in cheers, and Eron gets flustered. He picks up Rain and gives him repeated shots to the head, before setting him up on the top turnbuckle. He ascends to the top rope, and goes for a DDT. No sale. Rain has a foot on the rope, blocking it. Eron goes for it once more. Again, nothing doing. Rain picks up Eron, and sends him to the mat with an atomic drop. This is payment for earlier in the match. The fans cheer in delight, as they'd rather see the Relentless family tree become stunted. Rain picks up Eron, hooks the head, and hits a devastating vertical suplex. Unfortunately, it's devastating to both Eron and Rain. Both crash to the mat with no signs of getting up any time soon. The ref begins to count both men out. As the word "Seven" is yelled out of the ref's mouth, both wrestlers begin to show signs of life. Eron gets up first, and takes after Rain with a shot to the head. Rain blocks it, and delivers one of his own. Eron takes another shot, and again not only is blocked, but receives one of his own. Eron takes off the ropes. When he hits the ropes, he turns and feels Rain's hand on his neck. Rain followed him the whole way. Rain lifts Eron up and holds him up for about fifteen seconds...and drops him to the mat with a devastating chokeslam. Rain picks Eron up and drops his head down for his finisher...the Rainmaker. Rain lifts Eron up and nails the jacknife powerbomb. He covers Eron and gets the pin. Rain has won the match, much to the delight of the crowd. The fans begin to toss confetti to the ring, as many of them paid to see Eron get the bejesus beat out of him. Rain stands in the middle of the ring, taking in these cheers. He knows they're not completely for him, but he'll take what he can get. Meanwhile, Eron gets to his feet, and exits the ring. He grabs a chair, folds it up, and climbs back into the ring. He walks over the Rain, spins him around, and nails him over the head with a chair. On the way down, Eron takes another shot, and connects. Eron spits on Rain and hops out of the ring. The confetti turns into soda cans, hot dogs...even Eron t-shirts. Eron dodges these articles purchased at the event, flips the crowd the bird once more, and exits the ringside area, as Warfare hits to another break. Result: Rain by pinfall Last Minute Scheduling Finn & Armstrong sat in the office as a printer finished printing up some papers. Finn looked at Armstrong, who asked... "Well, Chris... are we set for CyberSlam?" "No... I've got to go tell a few people they're booked for the show. And then... we need to get the papers signed. After that, we'll be set. I've got to go now do all this now... so you make sure the catering is all set so you can have your damn bun cake that you like so much." Armstrong was looking down at some magazines (the contents of which are unknown...), before his head bobbed up in preparation to snap at Finn, but he noticed that Finn was grinning. After two years of knowing each other, they finally shared a somewhat sentimental moment. This lasted all of fifteen seconds before Armstrong exclaimed... "Yeah, and I'll make sure they have a stack of your porno's handy for CS when the matches not involving the French Foundation occur." Finn sighed, before exiting the room. Upon exiting, he made a long journey down a hall before he came across two wrestlers, both newcomers to the fWo. Erik Kelly & Crusader. Finn proceeded to tell them how pleased he was with their entry into the fWo, and the success both men had achieved thus far. He continued to tell them that he was sorry for the late notice, but they would be performing at CyberSlam, against each other. Kelly & Crusader seemed taken aback by this a great deal, but were happy to be present on the biggest card of the year in some fashion. They wished each other luck as Finn moved down another portion of the Arco Arena. He came across Jeremiah Frankton. The two men exchanged greetings, before Finn asked Frankton to do a favor and inform the Guano Apes, Hellrazor & Fear, Ric Chronos & Fallen, and Courage & The Future Frenchie... er... Lance Star... er... whatever, that they would be competing in a four-way dance for the fWo World Tag Team Titles on Saturday. Frankton obliged, before Finn asked him to find VL Tha Murdera to tell her that she would be defending the fWo Hardcore Title in a three-way dance against Robert Jensen and Frank Snow at CyberSlam as well. Frankton replied with a sly grin.. "Is that all, Chris?" Finn smiled slightly, before nodding. "Yep... CyberSlam is now official. Eleven matches. Or twelve. I don't remember. Whatever. I'm off to have these papers signed. Tell the workers I'm sorry for the last minute notice on their matches... take care, Jer." Frankton and Finn walked off in opposite directions, before Warfare went back to a gym elsewhere in the building. In Training, Part Two Sweat poured down his back as he ran on a treadmill. The treadmill wasn't at a high impact level, or whatever you'd like to call it, so the work wasn't very difficult. Judging from his wrestling attire, it was Black Quicksilver. He breathed heavily as he watched the tv in front of him, which ironically, was showing him. He thought that was odd, but he remembered he was watching Warfare... and suddenly, he realized it was live. He looked over at the other treadmill next to him that wasn't functioning, but someone was on it anyways: Stump. Stump sat there, doing it what it does best. Sitting. But this only frustrated the Black Quicksilver even more. He yelled at it. "Stump! Stump! Don't you realize we have a very important match Saturday? We have to train for it! We can't just sit on our... butts... hey, yes I realize you don't have a butt but that makes no difference at all! Get up and run! Run Stump, run!" BQ sighed as Stump continued to sit on the treadmill, while Warfare headed in another direction... Making It Official The Boston Strangler & Chris Finn arrived at the lockerroom of the fWo World Champion; carrying with them three packets of papers. They knocked, and were welcomed in by Dead. Finn gave Strangler a copy of the contract, and Dead one as well. The fWo World Champion scanned his copy. The number one challenger to the title carefully checked over his copy of the contract as well. Dead looked concern as he came across something of importance on the contract. "Wait a minute... I didn't say anything about my career on the line, Paul." Finn interjected on the Strangler' behalf. "Davis... you've been a thorn in our side for too long. All you've wanted to do is damage the fWo, we can no longer allow it. If you lose Saturday, you're gone. For good." Dead hesitated, before nodding. "Alright... you accepted my terms... I'm confident enough to accept yours." Dead checked over the contract one last time. As he did, The Strangler started to play mindgames Dead. "Are you ready, Davis?" Dead shot back an evil smile. "You know I am, Paul. These papers look copasetic to me. Let's do this." The Strangler gave a wry, confident smile. "Indeed. Let's." Finn gave Dead a pen, and Dead looked over the paperwork one more time, before signing his name to it. The Strangler proceeded to do the same. Finn looked at their signatures, before adding his. Everything was official. If The Boston Strangler defeated Dead at CyberSlam, it would be the end of the wrestling career for Dead, and a new World Champion in the Boston Strangler. However, if he is unable to defeat him... the ownership of the fWo changes... with Dead in complete control. One thing is certain... after Saturday... the fWo will never be the same again. Dead flashed a smile at Finn, before pointing out that if after he wins Saturday and has control, he'll still keep Finn & Armstrong around to call the action if they would like. Finn looked somewhat concerned, before remembering there weren't very many capable announcers nowadays in wrestling anyways... Warfare took to a commercial, with Dead heading towards the FTfWo lockerroom. Dead, Perfect & Mitch Wilson vs. The Boston Strangler, Scott Collins & Primetime Warfare returned from the previous set of commercials to the ring, where the ring announcer (Matt Panzer) stood, ready to introduce the combatants of tonight's hyped six-man tag team match. As he did, the theme of the FTfWo began to play. "Oh My God" by Guns N Roses echoed throughout the Arco Arena, site of this Sunday's big game between the Sacramento Kings and the Los Angeles Lakers. A red strobe light flickered throughout the arena, and out onto the stage stepped the three men representing the FTfWo tonight. On the left stood Perfect, the only four-time fWo World Champion to ever exist, and the man who held the fWo World Title for forty five percent of the last year of the last century. In the middle stood Dead, the reigning fWo World Champion. On the right stood Mitch Wilson, the present fWo Internet Champion, and former member of the French Foundation. The music continued to pulsate, as the trio of men headed to the ring. Climbing in, they were greeted with boos as Dead held his fWo World Title high above, and Wilson did the same. Perfect smirked, prompting more boos from the crowd. Their music faded as "Burning" by Manowar replaced it, and outstepped Scott Collins onto the stage below the Warfare Video Wall. Collins tentatively walked to the ring, while his music was replaced with "Blind" by the somewhat overrated band, Korn. Primetime walked out, and wasted no time as he joined his one-time tag team partner at Body Count, Collins, in the ring. However, the FTfWo took advantage of the three-on-two situation, and began to attack. With Dead & Perfect working over Collins, Wilson took it to Primetime... as the situation developed, "O' Fortuna" pounded throughout the Arco Arena, and the Boston Strangler came rushing to the ring. He and Dead immediately collided, both men brawling inside and out of the ring. As Wilson took Primetime out onto the floor, Perfect garnered a near-fall on Collins inside the ring after a beautiful Samoan Drop. Collins shouldered out at two, and as Wilson/Dead were instructed to their corner, Primetime & Strangler converged to their corner. Perfect brought the leader of the Four Riders up to his feet before shooting him into the turnbuckles, as he came out Perfect took him down with the dreaded drop-toe-hold. From there, the World's Greatest Athlete took Collins and brought him up to a standing position, before a shot to the midsection area, and a snapmare takeover. Perfect followed it up with a standing dropkick, which nailed Collins square in the facial area. Perfect then tagged out to Mitch Wilson, who continued the onslaught. Wilson continued from where Perfect left off, first bringing Collins up to his feet and hitting him with a barrage of forearms, before backing him into a corner, and unloading with a plethora of reverse knife edge chops to the chest. Wilson brought Collins out to the center of the ring and prepared for The Headliner, more commonly known as the inverted Russian Legsweep. Collins however countered with an elbow to the head of the current fWo Internet Champion, and another, before charging into the ropes and returning to the center of the ring with a flying shoulderblock. He then tagged out to his partner at Body Count last month, Primetime, who came in a house of fire. Looking to avenge what had taken place this past Sunday at Slaughter, and after having spent a night or two in jail, Primetime brought Wilson up to a vertical base before taking him to the canvas with a sudden snap suplex. Primetime brought him up once more, before applying a reverse neckbreaker, again taking Wilson to the mat. A cover ensued, but Perfect broke it up by interfering with a kick to the neck. The referee instructed the co-leader of the FTfWo back to his corner, and Primetime proceeded his attack on the man he would face in three days at CyberSlam 2000. Primetime shifted directions and after taking Wilson out with a Northern Lights Suplex, he went up to the top rope, showing his Cruiserweight ability... and flying off with a variation of the shooting star press. He went for a cover, but this time Wilson shouldered out of it on his own at the count of two and a half. Primetime tagged out to Collins, and he and Collins performed a unique double-team maneuver. As Collins hit a Death Valley Driver, Primetime flew off the top rope yet again with a frogsplash. Primetime rolled out onto the floor, but before Collins could attempt a cover, Perfect came in and backhanded Collins on the back of his head. Collins attention was now focused on Perfect, as the referee backed the superstar formerly known as Scott Slugger into his corner. With the referee and Collins both focused on Wilson, Dead saw his opportunity. He quickly procured a chair from nearby before sliding into the ring. Not paying attention, Collins was whacked from behind on the back hard with the weapon. Dead then tossed the chair to the mat, before sliding out onto the floor as the Strangler intervened in the ring. Looking out at Dead who now stood in the crowd, he passionately glared at the World Champion. Dead dared him, challenging him to come out onto the floor and do something about it out there. The Strangler waited though, albeit impatiently, waiting for his opportunity to get his hands on his main rival. The referee instructed everyone back to their corners, and Wilson and Collins began to struggle to a vertical base. Both men did, before each of them shared the same thought. Charging for a clothesline, each man hit it, and now were both on the mat. A ten count ensued. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Neither man showed any signs of movement. Six. Seven. Eight. Collins raised his arm, prompting the crowd to cheer. Nine. His arm draped over the fWo Internet Champion. The referee switched from a ten count, to a three count. One. Two. Wilson escaped defeat, as Perfect flew off the top rope, coming down on the man he would meet at CyberSlam. An elbow drop broke up the near fall. Wilson struggled to make a tag. Crawling to his corner, he finally reached up, and his hand met the hand of Dead. Dead came in looking focused on one thing, and that was inflicting pain on anyone he could find. He brought Collins up and quickly hit a DDT. Primetime had enough, and charged to the ring, but he was met with an overhead belly-to-belly suplex on the behalf of Dead. Dead then brought Collins up and took him back down with a fallaway slam, before completing a spike piledriver (assisted by Perfect). Dead then turned his attention to the Strangler, and this time Strangler could take no more. He came into the ring, looking to have a one-on-one battle with his opponent in the main event of the first CyberSlam of the new century. Instead, however, as Perfect grabbed the referee and diverted his attention, Wilson procured the chair that remained in the ring off of the mat, and charged at Strangler, taking him down with a shot to the neck. Strangler fell immediately, as if the injury was much more severe than a regular chair shot. Dead laughed maniacally, before Wilson took the chair and set it in the center of the ring. He brought Strangler up and quickly hit the Headliner. Wilson then came over and he and Perfect distracted the referee agian, before Perfect left the scene of commotion. He slowly brought Strangler up, and looked to go for the Perfect Game, but it appeared he was taking too much time. Instead, Collins valiantly fought back into the ring, so Perfect went after him. Dead looked at his partner in crime curiously, but quickly took the Strangler and set him up for the Crucifixion. He hit it, before he went for a cover. Wilson released the referee from his hold, allowing him to count the pin. Not knowing what had occured, he did so. Wilson kicked Primetime, who struggled back into the ring. Three seconds later, and Dead had achieved victory. Strangler clutched his neck, however, and it looked like severe damage had occured. As the scene of chaos continued, only one question loomed overhead... would the Strangler make it to CyberSlam physically able to wrestle in a match that would decide the fate of the fWo? As the announcers asked the fans at home just that, Hiroshima... the other member of the FTfWo, came out to the ring. Quickly he brought the Strangler up, before taking him out with the Oblivion. A four-on-three continued, until... Result: Dead, Perfect & Mitch Wilson by pinfall CyberSlam Collision Match: The Flying Frenchie, Ultra-Violet, Erik Kelly, Crusader, Mike Bear, Havok, Wretch, The Black Panther, VL Tha Murdera, Robert Jensen, Frank Snow, Courage, Jackhammer, Fallen, Fear, Black Quicksilver, Mike Heftel, Matt Douglas, Ru ... the man who recently garnered a pinfall victory over the self-proclaimed "most celebrated wrestler" on the fWo roster, Erik Kelly, stormed to the ring. Finding himself on the onslaught of a quick attack on the part of Dead, Wilson & Hiroshima, he went to the mat in a heap. A barrage of kicks occured, while Perfect & Collins brawled to the back. A medical team scurried to the ring and helped the Strangler from the ring, who appeared to favoring his neck quite noticeably. Dead scoffed at this, as he, Hiroshima and Wilson kicked at the fallen Kelly. However, in an unlikely scenario, the man Kelly would face in three days quickly stormed the ring to aide him. Crusader fought off Wilson and Hiroshima, while a horde of security personnel and officials escorted Dead to the back. Kelly joined the fight against Wilson & Hiroshima, and the referee rung the bell, signaling the beginning of the CyberSlam Collision Match. The four men fought to the backstage area as well, and it appeared their fight would not end anytime soon. Seeing as how Kelly & Crusader had not returned in an allotted amount of time, the officials ruled them out of possibly winning the match. Matt Panzer took to the ring to announce the entrances of the participants, but before he could, a brawl began to make it's way out onto the stage. It seemed as if the participants of the fWo Hardcore Title Match could wait no longer to begin, and Frank Snow suplexed VL Tha Murdera onto the stage from the backstage area. As he did however, he was met with a foot to the groin by Robert Jensen, and the trio waged war inside the ring. It wasn't long however before they were joined by Black Quicksilver and Mike Heftel, who up until recently... at least twice... comprised the Stump Squad. Mike Heftel walked backwards out onto the stage, with Stump in his hands. He taunted BQ to join him in the ring, so he and BQ could settle their differences. BQ followed him as he ran to the ring. Heftel stood in the ring holding Stump above his head in celebration as BQ came in as well. Before BQ could do anything though, VL came up from behind with a spinning heel kick to the back of the head. This put Heftel on the mat, and now BQ was incensed that VL took his opportunity to waylay Heftel on his own. BQ charged at VL, but was only taken down with a hip toss into a table which had been set up by Jensen and Snow in the ring. Jensen and Snow looked at one another, incensed that VL had ruined their spot. They charged and took her down with a double clothesline, before celebrating... Jensen then took Snow out with a russian legsweep, and now only Jensen remained on his feet. His premature celebrating was interrupted, as "Killed The Cat" began to play, and out came the giant stature of a man in The Black Panther. The Panther charged down to ringside. He spun Jensen around, and took him down with a chokeslam. BQ got up, and he took him down as well with a chokeslam. Snow got up, and again, he was taken out with a chokeslam. Panther bragged about it, taunting the three men he had just taken out. Heftel struggled to his feet, and had propped himself up in a corner. VL saw her opportunity however, and she flew off with a missle dropkick, which propelled Panther chest first into Heftel, squashing him in the corner. Panther fell backfirst to the mat, however... and Heftel wobbled out from the corner, before falling headfirst, planting his hard cranium into the crotch of the 1999 Wrestler of the Year. Panther gasped, and since Heftel was still on the Panther, a referee sprung into action, sliding underneath the bottom rope into the ring, and counted the pin. Panther was eliminated, joining Kelly & Crusader on the sidelines. BQ was now back on his feet however, and he brought Heftel up, before taking him out with a wicked Cubic Zirconium Cutter. He then pinned his former partner, and Heftel joined the other three men previously eliminated. Panther held his head as he walked up the ramp, with Heftel following a ways behind him now. BQ celebrated, but from behind came Jensen and Snow, who took him out with a double team DDT. Jensen went for a cover, but Snow became jealous, and brushed him off BQ, before going for a cover himself. At the count of two, Snow moved just in time, because VL flew off with a springboard moonsault from the middle rope. Three seconds later, and Black Quicksilver was eliminated. Three people remained in the ring, but not before "Surfacing" by Slipknot began, and outcame a former International, Cruiserweight and Tag Team Champion. Courage rushed to the ring, quickly procuring a two-by-four from underneath the ring. He awaited VL to reach her feet, before taking her out with a shot to the face, perhaps in retaliation for VL attempting a variation of his finisher. He pinned her, and she was now officially eliminated from the match. However, Courage was joined in the ring by three members who were representing the three teams Courage would be involved in a four-way match for the tag team titles with this Saturday. Fallen (accompanied by Chronos), Jackhammer (accompanied by Sphere) and Fear (accompanied by Hellrazor) joined the action. Jackhammer went after Courage, while Fallen and Fear found themselves fighting in a corner. Jensen and Snow rejoined the action, and Snow took Jensen out with a spinebuster, before sliding out to the floor, and grabbing himself a chair. Rather than use it how most people use it (to sit, dammit), he brought it into the ring and placed it on the face of Jensen, before quickly going to the top rope, and coming off with a elbow. Hurting his arm in the process, he sprung up to his feet, and was taken out with a reverse DDT on the part of Fallen. Fallen covered Snow and eliminated him, and Fear did the same to Jensen. Courage & Jackhammer continued to square off, and Fallen & Fear resumed their rivalry. Jackhammer was getting the best of Courage, until "Juke Joint Jezebel" began to play. Everyone expected the Flying Frenchie, but instead out came the Future Frenchie, also known as Lance Star. Star came running down to the ring as Courage escaped from the corner with a thumb to the eye. Star tossed Courage a beret, and Courage had little idea of what to do with it. Jackhammer turned around however, and Courage threw the beret at his face, catching him off guard. Courage then quickly took him down with a hurrancanrana, before eliminating him from the match seconds later. Sphere attended to Jackhammer on the outside, while Courage grabbed Fallen off of Fear and spinning him around, before taking him down with a Implant DDT. He went for a cover, but Fear pulled him off and went for a chokeslam, but from behind came Courage with an axehandle to the back, sending Fear into a corner. Fear then staggered out and Star took him down with the Shooting Star, before Courage quickly covered him. Fear was now eliminated, but Fallen grabbed Courage off of him, and taking him out with a belly-to-back suplex. However, both men had their shoulders to the mat, and both men were now eliminated from the match. The four-teams (Guano Apes, Damnation, Chronos/Fallen and Courage/Star) brawled to the back, and now the ring was devoid of any more participants of the CyberSlam Collision Match. This soon changed however, as "Macarena" began to play, and out walked the current Cruiserweight Champion Rudo, accompanied by Sid Snow & The Total Eclipse. The three men converged to the ring and began to dance, as the lights dimmed. Their dancing was interrupted however, as "Paint It Black" by Gob (whoever the hell they are) started to play. The trio in the ring turned their attention to the stage, and outstepped Scott Hart, looking goofy as ever. Hart looked around mischeviously, before waving to the back. Out came Wretch, who looked around, perhaps for any signs of his former partner, who had been acting nuts as of... well, ever. Wretch held his International Title high above the ring, as the announcers noted that Havok would not be able to compete tonight, as he and The Black Panther had gotten into a backstage skirmish as the Panther was met backstage. Havok was taken to the hospital and was now being treated for minor injuries. As Rudo looked at Wretch suspiciously, he wasn't paying attention as Matt Douglas came in through the crowd. Douglas waited for Rudo to turn around, before he took him down with a high crossbody. Douglas was looking for a pin, but the momentum of both men shifted Rudo on top of Douglas. Sid Snow reached in and held the leg of Douglas, as the referee counted the pin. He came in quickly and now was leaving quickly, as Rudo picked up the pin, eliminating Douglas from the match. Rudo stood to his feet and began jumping for joy, but he was blindsided with a shot to the head from Wretch' International Title. Wretch covered Rudo with one foot, but decided that wasn't good enough. Instead, he invited Hart into the ring, and he had Hart help him eliminate Rudo from the match via throwing him over the top rope onto the floor, directly into Sid Snow and Total Eclipse. Rudo' momentum knocked Snow down, but not the Eclipse. Instead, the Eclipse caught him and looked at him, before contemplating consuming him for dinner. Deciding against it however after remembering about the lifetime supply of Twinkies he had backstage, he set Rudo on his feet and slapped him on the back, before the Lone Gunmen exited to the backstage. Wretch and Hart thought Wretch had won the match, and began celebrating. Instead, screeches of a drill were heard throughout the arena, and outstepped Mike Bear onto the stage. He charged the ring, and Wretch & Hart escaped. Wretch headed towards the crowd, with Hart headed in the same direction. Wretch turned a corner however, and headed back into the ring. Bear chased Hart into the crowd, and Wretch retrieved his International Title, before holding it high overhead. He then pointed at his head, reminding the crowd that he was much more intelligent than he appeared. However, it was all for naught, when Mike Bear came back through the crowd into the ring. Spinning Wretch around, he took him out with the drill. He then prepared for the Whiplash, as Ultra-Violet began to make his way down to the ring to the sounds of "Rage", by Savatage. UV slid underneath the bottom rope and watched Bear take Wretch out with a fireman's carry into an Ace Crusher, before pinning him and eliminating him from the match. Bear then grabbed the drill and spent the next several moments trying to literally screw drill bits into Wretch' head. Ultra-Violet saw this, and thought it was funny. He laughed for a few moments, before stopping, not remembering what he was laughing at. "Juke Joint Jezebel" began to echo throughout the Arco Arena, and this time, rather than a (rather bad) impostor, the true Flying Frenchie appeared. Carrying with him a set of trimmers, he quickly paced to the ring, and was met with a flying bodypress over the top rope on the part of Ultra-Violet. The referees determined that Ultra-Violet was now eliminated from the match, while some security members hurried down to the ringside and tried to prevent Bear from doing any further damage to the mental capabilities of Wretch. The referees informed Ultra-Violet of what had occured, but like every other battle royal Ultra-Violet had been in, he didn't understand, and assumed he had won. He jumped around before heading backstage to tell Violet the good news. Frenchie recovered, and thought he would just deal with the evil purple one this Saturday. With Bear' back to him, Frenchie quickly found a chair, before sliding into the ring and whacking Bear from behind, as the officials escorted Wretch to the back. Frenchie then went up to the top rope and flew off with the 450 splash, before pinning Bear, and winning the match. He then went outside the ring and grabbed the trimmers, before coming back in. Threatening to shave off Bear' hair in an example of what UV could expect in a mere seventy hours, the show went off the air, with the announcers reminding everyone that CyberSlam 2000 would take place this coming weekend. Fade to black. Result: The Flying Frenchie by pinfall
