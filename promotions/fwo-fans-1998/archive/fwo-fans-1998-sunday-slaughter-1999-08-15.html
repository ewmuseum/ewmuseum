Sunday Slaughter, 15 August 1999

15 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Slaughter opened the small community of Pensacola, Florida to a capacity crowd, on hand to see their favorite fWo superstars. Many fans carried signs for the Boston Strangler, still excited after his dramatic return at Hotter Than Hell II. Behind the arena cars and limos pulled into the backstage area, as wrestlers arrived at Slaughter. The backstage area was a hectic center of activity, with wrestlers rushing to their locker rooms and security officials holding back frantic fans who wanted to take a photo or get an autograph. Amidst all this activity, few saw the strangely familiar trio of men wander into the arena, their motives and purpose a mystery... Oracle vs. Lance Star Lance Star came to the ring with Chris Universal, and the two motioned to the crowd that they would soon be the fWo Tag Team Champions. Universal took the microphone and told the crowd that on Warfare he and Lance would face the former champions Ground Zero, and that it was just one more wall to knock down on their way to being on top of the fWo. Oracle ran to the ring before his entrance and leapt on Universal, battering away with hard rights and lefts. There was no sign of Prophet, who in recent weeks seemed to be Oracle's partner once more. Oracle hit a drop toe hold and followed it up with a drop elbow, but he was unable to get this quick pinfall victory. Star responded to the quick attack by whipping Oracle into the ropes and hitting a devastating lariat. As Oracle got to his feet Universal tripped him up from the outside, and Oracle turned to face his outside attacker. Universal backed up from the ring, shrugging as if he had done nothing wrong. Oracle turned his attention back to the ring, but the distraction had served it's purpose, as Lance Star hit his modified Urange Suplex and covered Oracle for the quick pin. Star and Universal celebrated outside of the ring, but failed to notice Prophet running in from behind. The big man tackled Universal to the mat, as Oracle got to his feet and went after Star. Referees and security ran to the ring to forcibly drag the men apart as Slaughter went to a commercial break... Result: Lance Star by Pinfall Pick me, UV Backstage the cameras found Kid Cool outside Ultra Violet's dressing room, hanging a large banner in front of the door. On it were large letters reading "MENTOR ME, UV!", and the banner was hung directly over the door. Kid Cool stepped back for a moment to admire his work, nodding with approval. "This should do the trick..." said Kid Cool, and then he wandered off in search of some kind of snack food item. The camera stayed on the door to Ultra Violet's dressing room for a few moments with no activity, and then the door knob began to rattle. The door tried to open, but the banner prevented the occupants of the room from exiting. The door knob continued to rattle, as the voice of Ultra Violet came from behind the door. "Darn it... I think I've forgotten how to work this dang thing again..." Wretch (c) vs. Black Quicksilver "Breathe" played throughout the arena as the challenger Black Quicksilver made his way out to the ring waving Stump and getting a large reaction from the crowd. Wretch came to the ring fresh off his victory against Ultra Violet with the Falcon and Mike Bear behind him and the Cruiserweight title slung over his shoulder. Wretch stormed into the ring and took control early on with a standing forearm to the head of Black Quicksilver. He whipped BQ into the turnbuckles, Black Quicksilver rebounded and caught a kick attempt by Wretch, and countered with a beautifully timed enziguirri kick. Black Quicksilver whipped Wretch into the ropes and followed it up with a dropkick, and then attempted a cover for a count of two. Black Quicksilver brought Wretch to his feet and nailed him with a bevy of punches, before whipping him to the ropes and attempting a hurracarana. Wretch blocked it though, and countered it into a powerbomb, and then took a few moments to recover from the past onslaught. Wretch grabbed Black Quicksilver by his mask and whipped him into the ropes, and when he returned he followed it up with a hotshot... making sure that his throat hit the top rope, stunning him. Wretch then maintained his attack; using a running powerslam from his arsenal of moves. Receiving only a two count, he nailed a quick DDT, before apparently getting ready for his finishing move. Black Quicksilver grabbed Wretch's legs and tripped him to the mat, but Wretch quickly pulled BQ's head around and pulled him tight into an inside cradle for the three count. Wretch rolled from the ring where he was congratulated by Bear and the Falcon as Black Quicksilver watched from the ring, looking angry at the reversal victory by the champion. Result: Wretch by Pinfall Rain vs. Cactus Jack The lights dimmed and thunder was heard over the rafters as Rain came to the ring carrying a sledgehammer, and tossed it into the ring as he waited for the challenger. Cactus Jack came to the ring without any weapons, and slid into the ring and at once went after Rain with swinging punches. Jack grabbed the head of Rain and rammed him face-first into the mat and the sledgehammer, opening Rain's head up. Screaming to the crowd that he was going to take the title from Mike Bear, Foley continued his calculated attack. Rain finally got back on his feet and delivered a quick standing kick, taking the air out of him and sending him to the mat. Rain jumped onto Cactus Jack and applied a figure four leglock while reaching for the sledgehammer. Cactus Jack broke the hold and slid outside of the ring, walking over to where a pile of steel chairs sat. Jack grabbed a chair and turned it on Rain, who dropped the sledgehammer. Jack continued to bash the steel chair over the head of Rain repeatedly, until Rain began to scream with rage and grabbed the chair away from Cactus Jack and thrust it into his mouth, cracking the jaw and teeth. Rain swung the chair down, dropping Jack and placing it on top of his prone body. Rain then delivered a legdrop onto the chair and covered Cactus Jack for the pinfall victory. His victory didn't last long however, as Mike Bear ran to the ring and attacked Rain from behind. Soon after Crucifix made an appearance, and assisted Rain in the ring. Mike Bear drug Cactus Jack free, and challenged Rain and Crucifix to a Hardcore tag team match one week from tonight... at Sunday Slaughter. Jack seemed to consider for a moment, then shrugged and said that it sounded better than other offers he had for the night as Warfare went to a commercial... Result: Rain by Pinfall The Mad Frenchie wises up The Mad Frenchie whistled a happy tune as he walked down the fWo halls, stopping from moment to moment to sign autographs or kiss small babies that were held out to him. He entered his dressing room, stopping for a moment to chat with Primetime and Mitch Wilson. As he looked for a marker to use to sign more autographs, he stumbled across a videotape. Whistling he stuck it into a VCR and gasped in surprise at what he saw there. On the screen he saw the Surgeon shooting Primetime, Wilson, Cookie and Alice with some kind of strange ray. "Sweet mother Minerva! If the Surgeon was responsible for that then that must mean that I..." Before the Frenchie could finish his statement Chris Universal knocked him in the head with a wrench, knocking him out and dropping him to the floor with a thud. "Well, that oughta fix him..." said Universal, as he left the dressing room in the same direction as he came. Los Latin vs. Brawler "Hotel California" played over the speakers as the lights in the arena dimmed, and a host of Spanish matadors walked out to the ringside area. Los Latin made his way to the ring next, waving a Mexican flag and sneering at the crowd. He grabbed a microphone and told the crowd, "Yes you pathetic fat American fools... it is I. Your physical, intellectual, complete superior, Los Latin! You all want to be like Los Latin... you all want to make love to Los Latin... well think twice and call me in the morning for it won't be happening! You all should be happy that I even think to come out to see you gringos up in my area! So sit back and watch me dispose of one of your treasured American heroes!" Los Latin dropped the microphone as the crowd booed him and tossed garbage into the ring. The Brawler was wheeled out in a wheelchair, still injured from his match with Death many weeks ago. The Brawler began to protest that he should have to wrestle in this condition, but Los Latin dove from the ring with a flying plancha that took out the Brawler, still in his wheelchair. Los Latin rolled the immobile and completely helpless Brawler into the ring and covered him for the pinfall victory. He then held up his arms and whipped his brow as if he had wrestled a tough match. Grabbing the microphone again he said, "One American slob down... nineteen to go!" The crowd continued to boo, and a fan hit Los Latin with a lucky shot to the head by a beer bottle as Slaughter went to commercial. Result: Los Latin by Pinfall Meltdown drawings Chris Finn came to the ring to announce three more teams for Meltdown. Addressing the crowd, he said, "The FIRST team to announce tonight for Meltdown in less than two weeks is the team of TWITCH and... well, this is strange... THUMPER! TROUBLE is the first team! The SECOND team to announce tonight is the team of the CHAMELEON and ORACLE! The THIRD team to announce tonight is the team of MACHINA and the BOSTON STRANGLER! This week on Warfare the final three teams will be announced... don't miss it!" With than, Finn walked from the ring as the Slaughter crowd got ready for the main event... Doomsday (c) vs. Trouble Trouble came to the ring, Twitch back from a near-career ending injury and looking fit and ready for action. The fWo Tag Team Champions came to the ring, Stevie looking cocky and VL walking smugly behind him. The two raised their titles up into the air for the crowd, but were quickly attacked by Twitch and Thumper from behind. The challengers stayed on the attack, double teaming Stevie as VL rolled to the outside for safety. The ref finally was able to get Twitch out of the ring, as Thumper attacked with a brutal snap suplex that sent Roberts to the mat with authority. Stevie attacked back with a blow to the groin, and hit a rocker dropped on Thumper as he stood dazed. Roberts tagged in VL, and then hoisted Thumper atop his shoulders inside the ring. VL delivered a jumping lariat from the top rope, and went for a cover. Twitch broke the count, and VL tossed him from the ring for his interference. VL then hit a DDT on Thumper, and covered him in the center of the ring. Before the three count could be made, Ground Zero came to the ring and distracted VL from the outside. VL stood to yell at them, and the ref attempted to get Ground Zero to go back to the backstage area. While they were distracted, Twitch rolled into the ring and urged Thumper to roll out, pretending to be dazed. VL turned to make the cover on who she thought was Thumper as Ground Zero left the ringside area, but Twitch quickly hooked her tights and rolled her up in an inside cradle. The ref counted to three, and the crowd was shocked as Trouble was announced the winners of the match and the new fWo Tag Team Champions. VL and Stevie looked on incredulously, and then attacked the ref and tossed him from the ring. VL leapt for Thumper who held the fWo Tag Team title out mockingly, but Stevie held her back. Stevie then yelled at Thumper that their victory was cheap, and that he expected a rematch in one week at Slaughter. Thumper and Twitch only laughed, and said that they would "think about it" as the cameras went off the air and the show came to an end... Result: Trouble by Pinfall
