With one hard shove, Stevens sent Lightning into a full rotation, which allowed him time to back up two steps and then fire off the sweetest looking superkick in the business. The Factor X. Dropping down for the cover, Stevens could only pray that Knight would be able to prevent any attempt by Dew to break the count, but such thoughts proved to be unnecessary, as Dew could only slide into the ring as the referee's hand hit the mat the third time. Enraged, Dew charged at Stevens, but his momentum was halted by a hard kick to the ribs from Knight, who then applied a full nelson on the slightly larger Element. Calling to his partner, Knight demanded another Factor X, and as Stevens connected with the button of Dew's chin, Knight launched him backwards, releasing his full nelson at the peak of the throw and sending Dew to the outside with a release dragon suplex. And while it hadn't been pretty, Team EQ had managed to do what they had set out to do: pick up another win en route to hopefully securing another shot at Triality. As Stevens and Ivy supported Knight as they made their way up the ramp, leaving the Elements to collect their bruised pride in the ring, one thought was common amongst the trio: would such a match be what it took for Triality to finally relent and give them their well-deserved title shot? They could only hope so... and with that Sunday Slaughter faded to black for another week. Result: Team EQ (Stevens pinned Lightning)
