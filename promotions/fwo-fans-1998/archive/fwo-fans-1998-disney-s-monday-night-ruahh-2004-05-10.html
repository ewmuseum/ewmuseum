"Early Bird... 3:00 P.M. The garage was a quiet, sleepy place after the historic Cyberslam VII event. The staff and wrestlers were forced to return to the daily grind, however, none could be found in the bowels of the desolate underground lot. An automobile rapidly approached in the distance, growing from a little black toothpick to a full stretch limousine in a matter of moments. The speeding car screeched to a halt at the door and the engine was killed immediately. The driver poured out of the car, and marched a half-circle around the limo to arrive at the back seat. His body language was devoid of any morsel of finesse. With haste he grabbed the handle and threw the door back to reveal his passenger. Two petite feet, wearing blue thong sandals, slowly emerged and touched pavement. The driver graciously took the extended hand and helped the person out of the vehicle. The newly crowned champion, Shirley Savage, stood upright with a smile. Her newly acquired jewelry, the golden Cruiserweight belt, hung loosely on her slim shoulder. "Thanks," she said, detachment in her voice. Her eyes squinted, though never deviated from the door to the backstage. A great deal was on her mind. Jeremy was beginning to drink again. He adamately denied it, but Shirley knew better. She knew the putrid stench of his breath and the blank glaze over his eyes too painfully well. He was falling fast, but she couldn't catch him. She had tried everything to keep the bottle from his hands but nothing seemed to work; he couldn't see that it was the bottle that would cripple him worse than Sphere did. Another helping that had been slapped on her full plate was the unexpected fWo Cruiserweight title victory. Instead of scrapping for matches and notoriety, she realized that soon people would be coming after her. Almost a dozen fWo superstars wanted what she had, which was the reason she had arrived at the arena so early. She would plan, and scheme, and do whatever it took to keep her new prized possession as long as possible. "Would you like me to safely escort you inside?" The young, handsome man enquired. Shirley looked around her and gave him a puzzled expression. The garage was completely empty; the only threats to her safety were probably the cracks in the ground below. "Thank you for your generosity, but..." she trailed off to the attentive driver. She looked at his wide eyes and genuine face. For a quick second, the driver reminded her of Jeremy...before his world fell off it's axis and his life turned into a whirlpool of sorrow. She stroked imaginary whiskers on her chin, embroiled in thought. She would be incredibly lonely without her husband, preferably sober, by her side. Shirley needed a companion so she could maintain her sanity in the upcoming evening of uncertainty. She glanced up from her thought and noticed the driver was returning to his door. "How would you like to be my escort for the entire evening?" Shirley asked honestly. His face brightened; he couldn't refuse such a beautiful customer. His face then turned to a stern glare. "Hey lady, I don't do the 'escort' thing," he said, making imaginary quotation marks in the process. He obviously interpreted her offer as sexual. Shirley rolled her eyes. "Sorry, I'm married," Shirley said matter-of-factly, "so you don't have to worry about me coming on to you." The act of flirting with him was making bile rise in the back of her throat. "C'mon, I don't want to be alone tonight." The man raised his hands and quickly dropped them. "I was never much of a wrestling fan," the man said with a smirk, "since it's all fake." Shirley stomped around to his side of the car and firmly planted an open hand on the side of his head. His neck promptly jutted to the side with the forceful blow. "Now did that feel fake?" she asked in a soft, tender manner. The surprised man rubbed his throbbing temple and shook his head in the negative. Shirley shook her head; so much for her request. She adjusted the title belt on her shoulder and walked up to the door. The idiotic male suddenly realized that he was turning down the best-looking woman in sports entertainment, if not the world. After a short encounter, the man could clearly see that she was not only beautiful, but spunky and brash. Before Shirley could get inside, the man called out. "Yes?" Shirley asked as her neck turned and her long black hair blew at will. The man didn't have the chance to continue, as his ears caught static coming from his dashboard communication system. He grabbed the receiver and barked to his superior. "I'm having engine troubles!" he shouted. Shirley smiled. The voice on the end was startled. "Okay, Banion, just get it taken care of!" "Okay! OVER AND OUT!" He bellowed before spiking it into his drivers seat. He took a deep breath, locked the doors, and joined Shirley at her side. The crowd began to boo wildly, and the referee called for the bell, awarding the match to the Dark Riders by submission. Winters raised his hands in victory, and Primetime jumped up onto the apron and stepped into the ring. Death continued to apply the Death Trap, refusing to let it go, and Winters and Primetime began to go to work on Ice, stomping away on his head and exposed upper body. Winters saw Brand Frontier begin to stir, and quickly brought him up and placed him on his shoulders, then drove him down to the mat with the Eleven as the crowd continued to express their displeasure. Not satisfied with that, Primetime brought Brand up and set him up in the corner. He then drove Brand down with the Fall From Grace, hitting the high-impact move with full force, sending Brand falling down to the mat, unconscious. Death still had the Death Trap applied, and now he shouted to Primetime, who rolled out of the ring and began to dig underneath it. He emerged with a red canister and a small square something that he held in his palm. Death finally released the Death Trap, and he took the red canister from Primetime. As the announcers shouted for help, Death poured the contents of the canister over Ice. A strong smell, easily identified, began to rise from the ring - gasoline. As the announcers shouted, Primetime passed Death the small square, which Death opened, identifying it as a matchbox. He took out a match, then gestured for Winters and Primetime to exit the area, which they did. Death stood above Ice for a moment. Outside the ring, security forces began to run down to save Ice, but they were intercepted and knocked down by Primetime and Winters. Death struck the match... looked at the flickering flame that was dancing its way down the small wooden stick... then casually dropped it. WHOOSH! Ice went up immediately, and his screams rang through the arena as Death laughed evilly over him, then rolled from the ring as it became unsafe. The Dark Riders stood at ringside to watch the bonfire - and as they watched, a man carrying a fire extinguisher sprinted down the entrance ramp, jumping the fallen bodies of security guards, then rolled quickly into the ring. As the Riders stood, cackling, knowing that it was too late to prevent Ice's suffering severe, life-changing damage, Black Quicksilver triggered the fire extinguisher and sprayed it on Ice, putting out the fire before it could grow too big to be contained. At the foot of the entrance ramp, the three Dark Riders grinned and laughed, and BQ turned to shout for medical help for Ice. A group of EMTs began to sprint to the ring, and the Riders let them pass. As the technicians began to rapidly attend to Ice behind him, BQ shouted down at the Dark Riders, who stood silently, grinning at the carnage they had caused. With a final image of these three evil, despicable men, who had already done so much damage in one night... Ruahh faded to black. Result: Dark Riders by submission
