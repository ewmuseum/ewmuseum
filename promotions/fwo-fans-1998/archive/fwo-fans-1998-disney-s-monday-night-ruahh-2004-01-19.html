Brand threw the chair down furiously and called Kinkade back to the ring but it was too late, the screwjob had been completed and now he could only wait and see if he would indeed meet The Jersey Devil as the man himself promised. Result: Kellen Kinkade by Disqualification The plot thickens. A bloodied Kellen Kinkade staggered through the backstage halls before finally reaching a destination he'd intended on arriving at long before participating in his match with Brand Frontier. "Did you see all of that Mr. Michelle?" Kinkade spluttered from his bloodied mouth as the Marketing Manager turned his attention from the monitor on the wall to the man standing before him. "I did indeed, absolute disgrace. I can't believe this man has been allowed to climb the ladder so high in this company, are you alright?" Carl replied, putting a hand on Kinkade's shoulder. "I think I may be concussed, he brought that chair down pretty hard. That didn't hurt as much as the fact that he wouldn't shake my hand though, to think the woman I love is shacked up with him makes my blood run cold." The Jersey Devil staggered to the side for a moment before leaning against the wall. "I really don't know what to say Kellen, you have it your all out there but as long as he continues to cheat like that... as I understand he can't lose his belt via disqualification. I'm completely lost with regards to what advice I might give." Michelle put his hands on his hips and sighed as Kinkade's face suddenly turned deadly serious. "There is... one thing I had in mind." Kade gasped as though he was exhausted "One more chance, that evil man relies on cheating his opponents and being disqualified to retain his championship gold. Lets take that option away from him... give me the opportunity to face him in a no disqualification match at Bodycount Mr. Michelle and I swear I won't let you or Disney down again." "Very well." Michelle nodded "I'll speak to the necessary people and make it so, the sooner this man is taken off his pedestal the better. We can't have those kinds of antics on our shows, punches, shots below the belt and foreign objects... I have a good mind to call the police." "No need for that Carl." The Jersey Devil sneered "At Bodycount I'll be the judge, jury and executioner." With that, Kinkade turned his back on Mr. Michelle and walked away... wearing on his face the twisted grin of a man who'd just placed down the final piece of a sinister jigsaw puzzle.
