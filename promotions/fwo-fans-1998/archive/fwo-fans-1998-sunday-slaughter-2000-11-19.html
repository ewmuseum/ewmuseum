Sunday Slaughter, 19 November 2000

Fallout: The Day After The opening image of Sunday Night Slaughter appeared on the fWo SlaughterScreen inside the arena. Slaughter hadn't officially began, but as the fans hurried to their seats, they were given a glimpse of backstage activities. The soon to be most talked and written about wrestler in professional wrestling, Ric Chronos, entered the Continental Airlines Arena with a duffel bag. Chronos walked down the arena's entrance and inside the arena, before he turned a corner. There, he ran into Crusader, who was nursing injuries from the previous night of competition. Chronos and Crusader exchanged a handshake, before again congratulating each other on a hard fought match. Chronos then walked past the Flying Frenchie, who congratulated him on tearing Primetime a new one in the semi-finals of the WOTY tournament. Chronos thanked him, before telling Frenchie he had a very important with Armstrong. Frenchie nodded, and the two men parted ways. The Flying Frenchie then began to walk to the other end of the hall, before he came to a door, and knocked. A response quickly came, in the form of Mike Bear. Bear opened the door fully, and greeted the Frenchie with a hello. The two exchanged small talk, before Frenchie got to the point of why he was there, and asked Bear if he would mind putting off his owed shot at Primetime a little bit longer... so the Frenchie could face him at Twilight. Frenchie reminded that they were scheduled to meet there last year, before a four-way match ruined those plans. Bear contemplated it, before Frenchie enticed the offer, saying that if he defeated Primetime, he'd get an impromptu World Title the night after Twilight. After more contemplation, Bear agreed, considering he had at least one upcoming tag team title defense tonight. Frenchie thanked Bear, before he turned to leave. There, he bumped into Hellrazor & Fear accompanied by Lamont. Frenchie exchanged greetings with the former three time tag team champions, before walking away. Damnation entered the room, and after the door shut, all that was heard was utter chaos. Not in the form of physical abuse, but verbal. A few "YOUR MOM~!"'s were exchanged. The camera man followed The Flying Frenchie, who walked to another room with the door closed. Frenchie initially began knocking, before hearing some loud moaning sounds. "James, are you in zere?" Frenchie exclaimed. The moans stopped, before Armstrong opened the door, looking a little... preoccupied at the moment. "Oh. Pierre. Can you wait a minute? This will only take a second anyways." The door shut once again, before some more moaning and yelling was heard. The door re-opened a mere sixty seven seconds later, and an attractive female walked out of the room. She grinned at Frenchie, who wasn't amused. James Armstrong welcomed Frenchie into his office, before asking him what he needed. Before anyone could hear, the door shut, as the cameras followed the female that left Armstrong' office. She turned the hallway, and walked past the current fWo Hardcore Champion, Scot Hartt, who was accompanied by the other reigning fWo Hardcore Champion, Tha Mutha Fuckin' Abusah Of Yo Momma. Tonight, both men weren't dressed like The Dudley Boyz like they were last night, instead they were dressed like Edge & Christian. The camera eavesdropped in on their conversation. "Being the Hardcore Champion rules!" exclaimed Hartt. The Abusah appeared to be in agreement. "IT... DAYRE EYE SAY... WREAKS OF AWSUMNESS!" Abusah & Hartt gave each other high fives, before Hartt had an idea. "You know, Edj, I say we come up with an excuse so we don't have to wrestle tonight." Abusah smiled. "YAH! THAT WOOD BE KEWL! BUTT, WAT EXCUS COOD WE YOUZE?" Hartt pondered the question. "I know! We can say we were lifted via a forklift and dumped off the side of a building!" Abusah disagreed. "KNOW! THATZ LAYME... HOO WOOD EVER BELEAV DAT?" What's this? Abusah actually being logical? Hartt conceeded. "Yeah, you're right. That's about as absurd as falling off the roof of Cobo Hall and coming back to wrestle a half hour later. Well, we've still got a few minutes to come up with something... boy, I love my hardcore title, it rules!" Abusah must've disagreed with that statement. "WAT? UR KNOT DA HARDKORE CHAMPEEN... EYE AM! YA BIG REEKAZOYD! U LYKE... TOTALEE LAK IN KEWLNES!" The duo continued to argue, while Slaughter faded to it's opening montage. Challenge. Sunday Night Slaughter began inside the Continental Airlines Arena, with the usual fireworks exploding throughout the arena nonsense. Loud sirens and music blared, while The Flying Frenchie began walking down the shiny entrance ramp to an abundance of cheers. Frenchie acknowledged the cheers briefly with a beam, before commanding a more serious expression as he entered the ring. Frenchie took to a corner and raised his arms in the air, then asking for a microphone as it was clear he had a very good reason to be out here tonight. The bells and whistles of the program came to a halt, as the former multiple time fWo World Champion began to speak. "Wit' ze blessing of my close friend Mike Bear, who has t'ankfully agreed to delay his owed title shot a few more weeks. Zat said, I am out here for one reason and one reason only - to challenge Primetime to a match at Twilight for ze fWo World Title. Long time fans may remember zat last year, he and I were originally supposed to face each ot'er for zat title at Twilight. Since t'ose plans were t'rown out of wack due to a four-way match zat was arguably one of ze greatest of all time t'anks to me no less, I t'ought that it would be more than appropiate for us to face each ot'er this year. One on one. Wit' a few stipulations... zat I will reveal if Brian accepts zis challenge. Rest assured... come Twilight... Je gagnerai." With the challenge made, Frenchie was embraced by the fans with cheers, while Slaughter quickly went to it's first commercial break of the evening. Perhaps he was clearing something off of them that was disturbing him, or perhaps he was literally biting at the chomps to get ahold of his number one enemy. Either way, we would find out momentarily. Primetime walked down the ramp, taunting the fans as he did. He looked in the ring and saw Chronos, and saw a man who was hellbent on winning the title tonight. But in the mind of Primetime, it wasn't going to happen. We would all soon find out. Primetime made his way down to ringside, and continued to talk trash with some fans. With his attention momentarily distracted, Chronos saw his opportunity. He sprung to the top rope immediately, and flew off with a corkscrew elbow to the face of the champion. Both men crashed to the floor. Primetime again found himself in a situation where the match began outside the ring for the second consecutive night. Chronos stood to his feet and stared at the sea of fans, and knew his time had come. He grabbed Primetime by the head and brought him up, slamming him head first into the ring, and then the guardrail. He then backed him up against a ringpost, and chopped him across the chest hard. Sounds of flesh against flesh echoed throughout the arena. Chronos then Irish Whipped him towards the guardrail, but Primetime reversed it. Chronos however reversed it again, but instead, hip tossed him over the guardrail. Fortunately, there were no fans around, and instead Primetime smacked the pavement back first. Chronos then grabbed a nearby steel chair. Since the match hadn't officially started, he wouldn't be disqualified. Chronos came back over to where Primetime was located, and as he climbed up off the cement floor, Chronos swung downwards, and cracked the chair across his head. He connected. Chronos then climbed over the guardrail, and instructed the fans to back away from the action. They complied, and Chronos winded up for another chair shot. Instead, Primetime ducked, and Chronos connected this time with a security guard. The guard fell to the ground in pain. Chronos attempted to apologize, but this gave Primetime an opportunity. Primetime grabbed him from behind with a waistlock. Chronos knew what was coming, and had to avoid it. Somehow. Someway. The Backdrop Driver. Had Primetime hit that move, the match would have never started. Instead, Chronos escaped with a go-behind, and once he was there, he grabbed Primetime by the head, and pulled him in. Lowfall. Chronos looked down at Primetime, and told him that was for "John Skyler". Chronos then brought Primetime up and sent him flying over the guardrail. Chronos followed. Primetime got up, and Chronos rolled him underneath the bottom rope into the ring. Chronos again followed. With both men in the ring, the referee signalled for the bell. The match was now officially underway. Chronos immediately went for a cover. One. Two. Primetime got his foot on the bottom rope. Not discouraged, Chronos brought Primetime up once again, and sent him to the ropes. Primetime returned, and found himself falling asleep shortly thereafter. The Hourglass. Chronos had the sleeperhold locked in, but not enough. Primetime back suplexed out of it to break the hold, and then took a moment to recover from all that he had endured so far in the match. That break didn't last long. Chronos was ready to rock and roll, and Primetime knew he had to do something to stifle his momentum. Primetime brought Chronos up and sent him to the ropes. Chronos returned and leapfrogged the ducking Primetime. When he returned, Primetime grabbed him and went for a hip toss. It was blocked, and then countered into a hip toss. That was also blocked, and countered into a hip toss. Finally, with both men near the ropes, Primetime hip tossed Chronos to the outside. But Chronos held on, and landed on the ring apron. Chronos then dashed over and climbed up the turnbuckles. He flew off with a flying bodypress, but the momentum caused Primetime to end up on top of him for the second pinfall attempt. One. Two. Kickout. Both men jumped to their feet, and Chronos brought Primetime off his feet with a quick legsweep. Chronos then followed it up with a rolling pinfall attempt. One. Two. Primetime bridged out of it into a backslide. One. Two. Chronos broke free. Both men were now on their feet, and Primetime charged. Chronos took him down with a Japanese armdrag. And another. Chronos then backed him into the ropes, and sent him across. Primetime returned and was taken down with a backdrop. Back to his feet, Chronos again sent the champion to the ropes. But Primetime reversed it. When Chronos returned, Primetime had ducked for a backdrop of his own. But Chronos telegraphed it with a swinging neckbreaker, followed by another cover. One. Two. But Primetime kicked out. Undaunted, Chronos brought Primetime up, and dropped him again. This time with an inverted swinging neckbreaker. Another cover. But again, Primetime kicked out. Continuing on a red hot pace, Chronos decided to take another high risk. Going up to the top rope, he waited for Primetime to reach his feet. Once he did, Chronos leaped off the top rope with a spinning heel kick. Primetime, however, moved out of the way, and Chronos hit the mat. Finally having caught a break, Primetime rested on the mat for a few seconds, before he saw Chronos coming to. Near the ropes, Primetime charged and clotheslined the 'Man Of The Hour' over the top rope onto the floor. PT followed him out, only he stopped at the ring apron. He waited for Chronos to get up, before jumping off with a double axehandle... however, Chronos countered with a shot to the stomach. Seeing as how they were close to the ring announcers tables, Chronos prepared for a big time move. A move that could possibly end the match. A move that won him the Wrestler of the Year tournament... from the top rope. The Figure Four DDT. He had him locked in position, but Primetime blocked, escaped, and fought free with a lowblow. The referee questioned Primetime's dirty tactics, but Primetime denied anywrong doing. With Chronos bent over, Primetime saw another opportunity - everyone knew what was coming. Primetime grabbed him by the head and pulled him in. Primetime Bomb. However, Primetime found himself flying through the air via a Chronos backdrop. Staring at the ceiling, he crashed through the Spanish announcers table. The crowd roared loudly as it appeared Chronos was well on his way to ending the match soon. Chronos paced himself as the referee checked over Primetime, and of course, the ring announcers. Chronos walked over and moved the referee aside, before he knelt over Primetime, and unloaded with fists of vengeance. Undoubtedly, this was payback for what Primetime had attempted to do three months ago. Chronos continued to assault Primetime viciously, before the referee waved him off. Ric brought Primetime up and rolled him into the ring. For good measure, he hit a Michinoku Driver for the pin. One. Two. But Primetime kicked out. Flabbergasted about Primetime's abundance of stamina, especially twenty four hours after a grueling match at Fallout, Chronos seemed bewildered about what to do next. Seeing as how it was going to take a big time move to finish the champion off, Chronos hooked him... and ran towards the corner. Chronosphere. But Primetime escaped and pushed Chronos out of it in mid-air. Chronos collided with the referee, who unfortunately happened to be standing in the way. Primetime fell to the mat after in exhaustion, and now all three people in the ring were down & out. Moments passed, before Primetime crawled over and laid an arm over Chronos. But there was no referee. Seeing as how the referee was out, Primetime sought to finish things off through devious means. He retrieved part of the table he had crashed through moments before, before bringing it into the ring. Chronos stood and Primetime nailed him with it. To add more salt to the wounds, Primetime brought in a monitor, and laid Chronos out with that. Finally, he followed it up with the Primetime Bomb. The match had to be over. Primetime covered Chronos, but the referee was still out like a light. Angry, Primetime shouted to ringside attendants to call for another official. Time elapsed, but another referee charged down to ringside. He climbed in, and counted. One. Two. Thre - no. Chronos somehow, someway kicked out. The arena erupted in cheers. Primetime stood to his feet, and looked around the arena. He knew he had to do something to end the match. But what? As a semi-conscious and now bloody Chronos stood to his feet, he turned around, and said hello to a boot courtesy of a Primetime superkick. Chronos went limp. One. Two. Thre - no. This time, Chronos got a shoulder up. Primetime was more than angry, he was more than pissed, he was furious. The reigning champion stood to his feet, and went to the top rope. There, he flew off with a legdrop, perhaps to mock his hated enemy the Flying Frenchie, who was surely following the action closely in the back. Having connected, Primetime thought that wasn't enough. Instead, he pushed the referee aside, and sent Chronos' limp body flying over the top rope to the floor. Primetime then methodically stalked him outside the ring. As Chronos got to his knees, he slammed him head first on the American announcers table repeatedly, before bringing him up, and laying him on his back on the table. He then told him he was going to finish him off. Punching him in the face a few more times, he then went to the ring apron. He decided to get a running start. As the fans watched intently on what Primetime was going to do next, Primetime decided to take things to a new level. He slowly climbed to the top rope. He looked out at the fans, and without hesitation - without any trepidation, he flew off. Guilotine Legdrop through the table. Jeremiah Frankton & Stevie Roberts ran for their lives. Pieces of the table were scattered throughout the Continental Airlines Arena. Monitors were out. The arena was in awe. They did not think Primetime would do something like that... but they soon found out they were wrong. Time passed, and finally, Primetime got to his feet first. He knelt over Chronos and punched him a few times, just as Ric had done to him earlier. The referee told Primetime to take it back to the ring. Primetime did... and then attempted a pinfall. One. Two. Two and 99 ?/100ths. Chronos got his foot on the rope... but just barely. Primetime was furious. He jumped to his feet, and began mouthing off to the referee. Backing him into a corner, he unleashed a verbal barrage of obscenities and such profane language that could not possibly be printed here. He began putting his finger into the referee's chest, continuing to yell unkind things at him. But while he was doing this, PT forgot about one thing. The match. From behind, Chronos grabbed him, and took him down with a rolling suplex. He brought him back up, and hit another. He brought him up for a third time, but Primetime escaped with a go-behind. Primetime had him in a waistlock, but again - Chronos escaped that, then grabbed him by the head. He hooked him, and charged towards the corner. Chronosphere? Connected. Primetime was out of it, and Chronos knew it was over. The problem was, all the life was taken out of him. All the strength. But tried as he might, he crawled over laid his arm over the champion. The crowd went silent. If the referee's hand hit the mat three times, it would be safe to say that the arena would've exploded. It didn't. At two and a half, Primetime kicked out. But the war was not over. Chronos brought Primetime up, and again hooked him for the Chronosphere for the third time in the match. Instead, however, Primetime escaped by draping Chronos on the top rope... crotch first. Primetime backed up to recouperate, before deciding the time was right. He walked over, grabbed him, and hooked him for a suplex - instead he turned into the slingshot Michinoku Driver. Snapshot. Primetime then covered him for the pin. One. Two. Three. It was over. Chronos had put up a tremendous fight, but could not escape defeat. Primetime stood in the ring victorious, although just barely. The referee recovered the fWo World Title for him, and Primetime realized just how lucky he was to win this match. It looked as if Primetime knew that he was quite fortunate... and might've considered congratulating Chronos on the match if he wasn't such an evil bastard. No matter. Before he got the chance; the lights went out... Result: Primetime by pinfall Final Terms ... and "Juke Joint Jezebel" by KMFDM began to play. Out walked The Flying Frenchie. Primetime stood in the ring with his mouth agape. Hoping that Frenchie wasn't out to start anything physically, Primetime locked his eyes on the man he hated more than any other human on this planet. Frenchie stopped on the stage, and waited for his music and the large amount of cheers to die down. Finally, it did. And Frenchie had something to say. "Your career... my career... our careers... on ze line at Twilight? Is zat really what you want, Brian? If zat is what you want, I will be more zan happy to end your career in a few weeks. Under a few ot'er stipulations. First of all, the match must end wit' a pinfall or a submission. Secondly, ze match can have no interference whatsoever. Any interference will result in indefinite suspension to ze offenders, even if zey are interfering on my behalf. If you can agree to zose rules... zen I will see you at Twilight. Are we in agreement, Brian?" Primetime dropped his title to the mat, and looked down at it. Sweat dripped off his forehead, as referees helped the bloody Ric Chronos from the ring. A look of anger came over the face of Primetime, and he looked out at the crowd. He then looked at Frenchie... and at that moment, realized he could not turn back on his answer. But that was fine with him. This was one situation he had to take care of himself. With his eyes focused on the face of what was once a close friend, Primetime shook his head yes several times to make sure there was no confusion as to what his "final answer" was. Frenchie beamed, before he told Primetime in French that he was going to receive an early ass kicking. Frenchie slowly began to walk towards the ring, and Primetime braced himself. As Frenchie reached ringside, it appeared all hell would break loose between two men who despised each other with every fiber in their body. Instead, however, a face emerged from the crowd. "Kodiak" Vic Creed. From behind, Creed spun the World Champion around, and just as he had done to Ross last night at Fallout and earlier tonight, nailed Primetime with a Hangman's DDT. Creed then stood over Primetime with a smirk, before looking out at Frenchie, and asking him if he wanted some too. Before Frenchie could respond... Slaughter faded to black.
