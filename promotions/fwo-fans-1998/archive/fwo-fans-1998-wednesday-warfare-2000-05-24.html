Wednesday Warfare, 24 May 2000

24 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Arrival A bright yellow taxi-cab pulled up onto the grounds of the Fleet Center in Boston, Massachusetts. The driver told the passenger how much was owed, and the passenger quickly paid. He exited the vehicle and watched it ride off, before walking closer to the Fleet Center. He looked at the structure and sighed, at the same time a menacing expression forming upon his face. "Boston... what a hellhole. Certainly... nothing... like Liverpool at all. But what better place to dethrone the fWo's savior... their Champion... than in his home. Yes... tonight... The Boston Strangler... is mine." The man let out an evil laugh, as he continued to walk into the arena where fireworks were already going off, signaling the beginning of the May 24th edition of Wednesday Warfare. Matt Douglas (c) vs. VL Tha Murdera he sounds of "Shook Ones, Part II" by Mobb Depp begins and out walks the queen of hardcore, VL Tha Murdera. VL walks to the ring with a look of confidence on her face, despite the fact that this is her first non-hardcore action in quite awhile. She steps into the ring and awaits her opponent of the night, the fWo Cruiserweight Champion. VL' music fades and "Higher" by Creed begins. Matt Douglas is greeted with cheers as he begins the long walk down the ramp, the Cruiserweight Championship strapped tightly around his waist. As he reaches ringside he's distracted by some nearby fans, which gives VL an opportunity to get an early advantage. VL baseball slides out onto the floor, spins Douglas around and kicks him in the stomach, before lifting him for a powerbomb. Douglas escapes and spins out of it, landing on his feet. He reaches and grabs VL by the neck before taking her down with a snapmare takeover. Douglas takes off his belt and hands it to the referee, who takes it over to the nearby announce table. Douglas goes up to the ring apron, and waits for VL to get up to her feet. Douglas charges and flies off, taking VL with a flying hurracanrana. The fans go crazy over this high risk maneuver. Douglas now stands nearby and waits for VL to get up again. As she does, Douglas charges with a flying forearm but VL ducks and Douglas hits the nearby ringsteps. Douglas slumps against the stairs in a sitting position, and VL charges at him before nailing him flush in the face with a seated dropkick. VL makes her way to her feet and rolls Douglas back in, before making a cover. The ref counts two, but Douglas manages to get a shoulder up before he can reach three. VL brings Douglas to his feet again and whips him into the ropes, but Douglas reverses it. He makes the mistake of putting his head down, and VL hooks his head as she bounces off the ropes, taking him down with a swinging DDT. VL brings Douglas up again and takes him over with a snap suplex. She then runs into the corner and leaps onto the second rope before leaping off with a vader bomb. VL hooks a leg of Douglas' and the referee makes his count, but Douglas again kicks out at two. VL brings Douglas to his feet and whips him into the ropes again, this time completing the move successfully and superkicking him over the top rope. Douglas on the outside stumbles woozily to his feet, and VL charges and leaps over the top rope with a plancha, taking out Douglas. After taking a moment to recover from the high-impact move, she rolls Douglas back into the ring and climbs onto the apron. VL slingshots herself into the ring with an elbowdrop, before rolling over and making the cover. Somehow Douglas finds the strength to kick out at two, and VL brings him up once more. She lifts him up and takes him down with a standing flapjack, before slashing a thumb across her throat and heading for the top rope, facing the crowd. She leaps off, and crashes down with a moonsault onto the back of Douglas, flattening him into the mat. VL immediately rolls Douglas over and hooks his legs, and the referee makes the count. 1... 2... and Douglas somehow kicks out again. VL is beginning to get frustrated, and heads up top once more. She reaches the top and prepares for the Illegal Activity, but Douglas makes a last-ditch effort, staggering to his feet and slamming into the ropes, causing VL to fall from her perch on top. Douglas seems to get a second wind at escaping his predicament, and quickly climbs to the top rope. He reaches the top turnbuckle and promptly leaps into the air, taking VL off with a hurricanrana. Douglas brings VL up and hooks her from behind, before taking her over with a german suplex and bridging up... but his shoulders appear to still be on the mat with VL's. The referee counts 1... 2... 3... and signals for the bell. Douglas jumps up, celebrating a tough win, but the referee refuses to raise his hand. VL stumbles up and approaches the ref, but he refuses to raise her hand too. With the crowd, wrestlers, (and referee) in confusion, Warfare continues with the official result of the match still unknown... Result: ??? It's all about the ratings... Chris Finn sat in his office looking over paperwork. As he mumbled to himself, a slight knock on the door was heard. Finn called the person into their office. It was James Armstrong. Armstrong had a stack of papers, and tossed it onto Finn' desk. "How the hell do you explain that?" Finn looked over the paper. "Hmmm. That's odd. Our rating for Sunday Night Slaughter was down... that's not good, considering we're coming off a pay-per-view and all." Armstrong nodded. "Yeah... but, there was that Heat/Knicks game..." Finn nodded as well. "And we had an early start time..." Armstrong shook his head up and down. "Yeah, and we were going up against another wrestling organization's pay-per-view..." Finn concured. "And it was our first show back in control. You know, no more of the FTfWo regime that ran things here previously." Armstrong agreed. "Yeah, and that pay-per-view happened to be put on by the WWF. And we all know that Nielsen families are traditionally from New York and are bought off by the WWF." Finn shrugged. "Ah well. So, how have you been doing, James?" Armstrong seemed taken aback that Finn was actually inquiring about his well being. "Well... the damnedest thing happened the other night. I was walking out of the Skydome after Slaughter and some nutso fan was out there with a gun. I immediately called for security, and they were there in seconds, just like the main event players they are. So, anyways... they arrest the guy and he starts going nuts saying that *I* told *him* to shoot me in the freakin' head! Can you believe that?" Finn blinked. He thought things over about two seconds, before replying. "Well, you did say that if you rooted for Slugger again for someone to shoot you in the freakin' head." Armstrong backpedalled on his comments. "Well! That doesn't mean they have to take me seriously!" Finn sighed. "This is the first time ANYONE has ever taken you seriously." Armstrong, who had been chewing on a piece of gum, blew a bubble. It popped. He now had pink gum all over his face. "So?" Finn shook his head. "Nevermind..." Until It Sleeps Warfare returned from the commercial break, and the cameras focused on the stage as the light faded into obscurity. "Paranoid" by Black Sabbath began to play, and a hush fell over the crowd. A video began to play on the Warfare Screen above the stage. It showed the vibrant fWo logo on the screen, before the logo burst into flames. The logo slowly burned from the inside out, until all of the logo remained was ashes. The ashes fell, before being swept off the screen by one word. Death. The cameras now focused on the entry onto the stage, and out walked the man himself, David Spencer. The fans began booing, and it could be said that no ovation of boos could be compared to the one Spencer was now receiving. Point blank, however, he didn't care. He walked to the ring with pride and determination, a look of hellbent anger written across his face. As he reached the ring he was given a microphone, and had to wait several moments for the crowd's frustration to pass before he spoke. "I... am... back. But the only way I will be back for good is if I win the fWo World Title here this evening... tonight... and I... most certainly will. If I don't... I'll walk away from the fWo for good, and you will never hear from me again. Last December when I was going to burn this promotion to the ground... my task was thrown off course by The Black Panther. We all know he was just a pretender, and he was not the true savior of the fWo. The true savior of the fWo was... your hometown hero... The Boston Strangler." The fans cheered loudly at the mere mention of his name. Death continued. "Your cult icon... the man who dethroned Davis this past Saturday... now has to get by me. And he won't. You know why? Because I am Death... and I am here... to be the proverbial roadblock in your quest to go down as the most heralded World Champion of all time. Tonight... I stand in your way. You get by me however... and I'm gone... for good. See you later, Paul... don't be late..." Spencer let out a maniacal laugh, before his music once again played. The fans resumed booing the vile & disgusting man, as Warfare went backstage... Conversations. The Mutha Fuckin' Abusah Of Yo Momma entered the arena with pride, still wearing the same attire he wore this past Sunday at Slaughter. As TMFAYOM reached a greeting area where the wrestlers are welcomed by members of the fWo staff, Lance Star emerged from the picture. As he did, he greeted TMFAYOM, who was startled. "AGH! WACH WEAR UR GOIN, BIOTCH!" Lance tried not to laugh, he had no idea why TMFAYOM chose to yell so much. Lance cut right to the point. "You know, Abusah, I've watched you for the last couple of weeks... and I think you have potential." "POTENSHUL?", TMFAYOM said quizically. "It means I think you have some talent... but you need to break away from that Scott Hart loser... we all know he's just trying to hold you back." "IZ HE? I HAD KNOT NOTISED". Lance grinned. "Come on... let's have a little chat." TMFAYOM shrugged. "OK, I WIL TALK TO U." Lance and TMFAYOM walked off, while Warfare went to a commercial break... Erik Kelly (c) vs. Frank Snow Godsmack blares across the PA system, and the crowd shows little appreciation for Frank Snow, who makes his way down toward ringside. He brings with him a grocery cart full of things, such as the COOKIE SHEET OF DOOM, and the BROOM OF DOOM, and then ROLLING PIN FROM HELL. As he is pointing to his cart, trying to make fans jealous of his housewares, "No Leaf Clover" by Metallica hits the PA system, and the crowd jumps to their feet at the sight of the massive Hardcore Champion, Erik Kelly. The crowd loves their hometown hero, and he shows his appreciation by taking it all in. He is wearing his "Eradication, Inc" blue-collar shirt. As usual, the lovely Pamela Dee makes her way to ringside with the champ. Erik Kelly looks at Snow's cart, pulls out the ROLLING PIN FROM HELL, and tosses it at Snow, who has his back turned. The cooking utensil hits Snow in the back of the head, and he falls to the mat. Kelly slides into the ring, and picks up Snow. The thing Kelly didn't realize was that Snow had a lead pipe in his hand. Snow nails Kelly with the pipe, sending Kelly to the mat. Snow takes the pipe to Kelly's head repeatedly and goes for a cover. At the count of two, the ref realizes that Kelly's foot is on the ropes. Snow hops down and picks up the BROOM OF DOOM, and climbs back into the ring. He picks up Kelly, and whips him into the ropes. Upon Kelly's return, Snow hits him in the gut with the butt end of the stick. He then breaks the BROOM OF DOOM over the back of Kelly. Kelly falls to one knee, and Snow runs outside again. This time, he picks up something worse than the broom of doom: THE GARBAGE CAN OF NEW YORK CITY! Snow picks up Kelly and tosses him to the outside, Snow picks up the COOKIE SHEET OF DOOM, and nails Kelly with it. A cover and a two count later, and we're back to Square One. Snow runs Kelly into the guard rail, and then charges at him with the ORDINARY STEEL CHAIR OF HECK!; Kelly sees this and trips Snow. Snow falls and hits his head on the flat ORDINARY STEEL CHAIR OF HECK!, knocking him senseless. Kelly picks up Snow and throws all of the contents of the grocery cart aside, and tosses Snow into it. However, Snow, being heady for a guy who just got knocked senseless by the ORDINARY STEEL CHAIR OF HECK!, pulled Kelly in with him. Snow gracefully flips out of the cart and runs it up the entrance ramp. He takes aim, and tosses the cart, Kelly inside, off the entrance ramp. The cart hits the ground and flips over, landing on Kelly. With Erik Kelly down, Snow walks down toward ringside to grab some more goods to use on Kelly. Pamela Dee runs by him to check on Kelly, which gives Snow an idea. Snow leans down and grabs his lead pipe, and then grabs Dee, and picks her up. The entire arena can hear her screaming and see her kicking in failed attempt after failed attempt to break free. Snow walks over to the opposite end of the entrance ramp. He places Dee down, and then grabs her again. He picks her up, and threatens to powerbomb her onto the table that rests upright on the ground below. As he tosses her up around his head, he gets a wide smile across his face, which generates loud boos from the crowd. Snow holds her for a second, and then gets tapped on the shoulder. Snow, not wanting to turn around, tosses Dee aside (luckily, she lands on her feet). He first bends down and picks up his lead pipe, and then turns around. He sees Erik Kelly, who has a much deadlier weapon than Snow's lead pipe, the BROOM OF DOOM, or the ROLLING PIN FROM HELL: THE SHOVEL OF HARRISBURG, PENNSYLVANIA. Snow looks in horror as the SHOVEL OF HARRISBURG crashes with his skull. Kelly picks up Snow, and yells a familiar cry to the crowd. "POWERBOMB" is heard among the arena, which brings massive cheers to the crowd. The Eradication is coming Kelly dives off the entrance ramp, and drives Snow through the table. A cover and three seconds later, and Erik Kelly remains the Hardcore Champion. A woozy Kelly runs up to check on Pamela Dee. After he finds that she is alright, they begin to hobble up the entrance ramp. However, Frank Snow, who is coming to after the Eradicaton, gets up and takes off after Kelly. The two begin to brawl again through the entrance curtin, as Warfare heads to a commercial break. Result: No Contest. Damnation vs. Stump Squad vs. Ice & Mitch Wilson vs. Primetime & Lance Star Warfare returned from commercials to the sounds of "We're Not Gonna Take It" by Twisted Sister, signalling the arrival of the STUMP Squad, for the 4 way elimination tag match for a shot at the fWo tag team titles. They step into the ring, and "From This Day" starts up. The team of Damnation steps out onto the entrance ramp to an enormous amount of cheers. As they step into the ring, "Calling All Stations" plays, and out steps "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson, and the big blonde machine himself, Ice. The team named Bad Attitude walks to the ring, Ice towering over the Black Quicksilver in the middle of the ring. However, before they begin fighting, "Last Resort" by Papa Roach starts up and Lance Star, and the fWo Internet Champion Primetime, 2/3 of the Future Foundation step out and bask in the jeers from the crowd. The two men continue to the ring, and both step onto the apron. Primetime makes a yawning gesture, as if he could care less about the match, and the bell rings. Ice and BQ lock up, and Ice uses his enormous power to throw BQ into the corner. A barrage of punches follow, and BQ falls to the mat. Whip into the ropes, and a big powerslam by Ice. Ice with a cut-throat gesture, and follows that up with the Icepick. The three count is academic, and the STUMP Squad has quickly been elminated. Star and Primetime look at each other, and then PT lets out a "Why the hell not.", before stepping into the ring. Ice and PT lock up, and PT gains the advantage with an armbar. PT lets go of the armbar, before stepping over to Fear and slapping him in the face, saying "Tag, you're in.". Fear sneers at PT before stepping into the ring. Fear and Ice begin trading punches in the middle of the ring, before Fear whips Ice into the ropes. A big backbody drop by Fear brings Ice over to his corner, and Ice decides to tag in Mr. Main Event himself, that being Mitch Wilson. Collar and elbow tie up, and a whip by Fear. Fear drops down, and Wilson leaps over. Wilson comes back, and Fear looks for a hiptoss, but it's blocked by Wilson, who takes Fear over in a hiptoss of his own. Near the Future Foundation corner, Wilson locks on a bow and arrow, but Star touches Fear, for a tag, which is seen by the ref and not Wilson. Star gets in the ring, behind Wilson, and runs the ropes. He comes back with a dropkick to the face of Mitch, sending "Mr. Main Event" aback. Fear steps out of the ring, and Star stays on top of Wilson. Star with a scoop slam, and he heads to the top ready to fly. He goes for a guillotine legdrop, but Wilson moves out of the way. Wilson with a death valley driver, and a cover, but Star kicks out at two. Wilson lifts him up up, and whips him in. Star ducks, and stays behind, looking for his own version of the Headliner. However, Wilson reverses it, and gets behind Star. He drives him into the mat with a Headliner, and covers. PT steps in to break up the count, and he is brought down with a huge clothesline. The three count is recorded, and immediately, Hellrazor gets in and jumps on Wilson, with a barrage of punches. As Star and PT walk to the back, Havok and Scott Hart come out, and the two teams begin brawling. The teams trade punches all the way to the back, and the cameras follow them. Out of nowhere comes TMFAYOM with a chair. He smacks Scott Hart in the back, and Hart falls over with a giant thud. TMFAYOM laughs before going to give Lance a high five. He misses, but his dirty deed has been done. Back in the ring, Hellrazor has nailed Wilson with a DDT. Cover, but Wilson gets out at two. Hellrazor puts Wilson on his shoulders, and signals for Fear to go on the top. However, Ice gets Fear at the bypass, and pushes him off of the ropes. Fear goes tumbling down, and Wilson escapes from Hellrazor. Waistlock from behind, and Wilson runs to the ropes. He goes for a pinning combination, but Hellrazor reverses it and ends up on top. The ref counts, 1, 2, 3, and Damnation picks up a clean win over Bad Attitude, and a shot at Chronos and Fallen. Result: Damnation by pinfall Soon Matt Loco scrambled backstage, putting on his clip-on tie as the cameras went live. "Hello, I'm Matt Loco. I have some inside information about "Xavier", and when this person will make their presence felt in the fWo. I'm told that this Sunday on Slaughter he will be there to make an appearance." Ironically, as Loco was saying this, a high pitched sound echoed throughout the backstage area, and a loud pulsating sound was heard throughout the arena. Back in the arena, "SOON" flashed onto the Warfare screen, and Loco' teeth chattered in fear. Mike Bear (c) vs. Scott Collins Warfare continued on as "Burning" began to play, and out walked the challenger for tonight's fWo International Title Match. Scott Collins came out to a nice warm welcome from the crowd, and he nodded at the fans as he reached the ring. Collins raised his arms which drew more cheers, but seconds later his music was replaced by "Dragula" by Rob Zombie. The fans arose to their feet and applauded the fWo International Champion Mike Bear, who stepped out onto the stage. Bear walked down to ringside and pointed at Collins, telling him that if he wanted a clean fight he was going to get one. Bear handed his title to the referee as he reached the ring, and the referee held it in full view for everyone. The referee then handed it to a ringside attendant, before signaling for the bell and the start of the match. Bear and Collins met in the center of the ring and exchanged pleasantries, before locking up. Collins overpowered Bear into side headlock, and after several moments, a headlock takeover. Collins worked away at the neck of Bear in the early going of the match, before Bear slowly regrouped and although he was still in a side headlock, he maneuvered it to where both men were at a vertical base. Bear went for a belly-to-back suplex, and hit it. Bear then bounced off the ropes as Collins recovered from the move quickly. Bear charged at Collins but Collins sent him into the other set of ropes. When Bear returned he had to hop over Collins, who went to the mat chest first. Bear returned to once more and Collins took him down with a shoulderblock. Collins then bounced off the ropes, and Bear turned over onto his chest. Collins hopped over Bear, bounced off the ropes again and when he returned Bear took him down with a hip toss. Bear then brought Collins up to his feet and whipped him to the ropes. Bear went for a backdrop but Collins showed some surprising agility and leapfrogged the backdrop, then spun Bear around and kicked him in the midsection, before going for the Scotty Dropper. Bear pushed out of it and Collins was sent into the ropes, and as he came back Bear hooked him at first for a Samoan Drop, and then it looked like he was going for the Whiplash, but Collins escaped and fell behind Bear. He waistlocked him, then charged into the ropes. Bear held on and Collins bounced off him and went tumbling backwards. He somersaulted out of it backwards on the mat, and as Bear turned around Collins charged and both men went up and over the top rope onto the floor as a result of a Scott Collins' lariat. The fans responded with cheers and were in a complete frenzy as they toppled onto the floor. Collins got up first and whipped Bear into the guardrail. Collins charged at Bear but Bear quickly grabbed him, and then hit a belly-to-belly suplex on the floor. Bear then rolled Collins underneath the bottom ropes into the ring. Taking a breather, Bear paced around the ring before climbing onto the apron, and then into the ring. Bear brought Collins up to his feet and then hit a Death Valley Driver, before attempting the first pinfall of the match. Collins kicked out at two. Undaunted, Bear brought Collins up and whipped him into the turnbuckles, before charging in and hitting a powerful elbow. Collins neck snapped back, and Bear took advantage by propping him up on the turnbuckles so he was facing the crowd, and then hitting a reverse neckbreaker. He then covered him again, but Collins shouldered out again at two. Bear sent him off into the ropes and ducked for another backdrop, this time however Collins stopped and hooked him for a double underhook suplex. Collins then took a moment to regroup, before continuing with a few stomps to the back of the two-time fWo International Champion. Collins then waited for Bear to get up, and as Bear did, he was sent back down by a Collins' superkick that damn near detached his head from his body. Collins then brought him back up and hit the Scotty Dropper, before going for the pin. Bear kicked out at two, and Collins was frustrated. Thinking he went for it too early, he tried it again. He hit it, but as he celebrated the authority of which he executed the move with, he saw a disturbance up on the ramp. It was Erik Kelly & Frank Snow, who had still not finished their brawl from moments earlier in the card. Collins became frustrated by this, wondering why the two were still fighting, and out on the stage. This distraction allowed Bear to recover, and Bear quickly rolled Collins up for the pin. The referee counted to three, and Bear had retained his fWo International Title. Kelly and Snow' brawl headed backstage, while Bear and Collins exchanged some words in the ring, of which nobody could hear... meanwhile... Result: Mike Bear by pinfall. Attack Backstage, Primetime was about to cut a promo when Havok appeared on the scene. "You know what, Primetime? I'm sick of you. I'm sick of you, Courage, and that punk ass Lance Star. You guys are so cocky, so arrogant... that it's enough to make me sick to my stomach. Well, I'm not going to sit here and bitch about it, I'm going to do something about it. That's right, Primetime. You and me for that Internet Title of yours at Critical Condition. We'll see if you have the guts to face the REAL future of the fWo." Primetime smirked, and was about to answer, when Mitch Wilson arrived on the scene wielding a chair. From behind he whacked Havok, sending him to the ground. Primetime moved towards "Mr. Main Event", but Wilson took him down with a drop-toe-hold. He then brought Primetime up to his feet and took him out with the 'Headliner', and then followed suit by doing the same to Havok. "I'm sick of both of you claiming you're the future. I'm the main event, I'm Mitch Wilson, and at Critical Condition, I'm going to be the fWo Internet Champion when the night is said and done. That's right... let's make it a three-way dance, boys." Wilson then spat on Primetime, before picking up the Internet Title that he once held, and walking away from the scene of chaos. The Boston Strangler (c) vs. David Spencer Warfare returned from it's final commercial break of the evening to an already darkened arena. "Paranoid" played once again, and out came David Spencer, also known as Death. Spencer was greeted with a deafening ovation of boos, but he paid no attention to what the fans thought. Spencer climbed into the ring, and awaited his opponent of the evening, the fWo World Champion. The lights remained dimmed as "Paranoid" was replaced by "O' Fortuna". The hometown hero stepped out from the backstage area, with a focused expression. He stared deeply into the eyes of Death as he walked down to the ring. With the fWo World Title draped over his shoulder, he reached ringside, and Spencer came out onto the floor to meet him. The two engaged in a slugfest, exchanging left and rights as The Boston Strangler' music continued to blare throughout the arena. Spencer got the upper hand early after a vicious right to the jaw, before he whipped him into the ring steps. Strangler reversed it however, and Spencer went into the steps back first. Strangler then walked over and grabbed him by the head, before executing a snap suplex. Strangler rolled Spencer under the bottom rope into the ring, and the referee signalled for the bell and the official start of the main event. Surprisingly, the World Champion climbed to the top rope, and as Spencer reached a standing position, he was taken back down with a flying lariat off the top rope by The Boston Strangler. The Strangler went for an early cover, but Spencer kicked out at two. Strangler continued, bringing the insane individual from Liverpool up to his feet, before unloading with a barrage of reverse knife chops. The crowd replied with "whoo!" in unision, before Strangler prepared to Irish whip Spencer. Instead, Strangler brought him back and scooped him up onto his back, before hitting a Samoan Drop. Another cover, but this time Spencer kicked out at two and a half. Undaunted, Strangler continued on the offensive. He began stomping away at the chest of the original Death, before bending over and bringing him up once again. He pushed Spencer into the corner, and unleased some forearms, before attacking the body yet again. He then whipped Spencer into the corner, but Spencer reversed it. Strangler went flying in back first. Spencer charged but Strangler was unfazed. Strangler charged out and took him down with a solid clothesline. Spencer hit the mat hard, but bounced back up. Strangler then took him down with a gutwrench powerbomb, of which Spencer kicked out of at two. Strangler was frustrated about how much punishment Spencer had taken, considering Spencer hadn't wrestled in over three months. Strangler again brought him to his feet, hit another chop, then a forearm, then an elbow to the stomach. He then whipped Spencer into the corner, and charged in for a clothesline. Spencer ducked out of it however, and then turned Strangler around. He kicked him in the stomach, before setting up him for a suplex. Instead, he propped Strangler on the turnbuckles, before hitting a superplex. He went for a cover, but Strangler kicked out at one and a half. Spencer backed up and drived several knees into the forehead of the Strangler, he then did a slight signal as if to say this match was over. Spencer brought Strangler up for the Apocalypse Piledriver, and hit it. He went for a cover, but Strangler pressed out of at two. Clearly frustrated, Spencer brought Strangler up and hit another piledriver. He went for yet another cover. This time, Strangler had more trouble, and barely got out at two and a half. Finally, Spencer went for one more... but Strangler countered by backdropping out of it. Spencer hit the mat. Strangler then stood behind Spencer and waited for him to get up, before locking in the Boston Massacre from behind. After several moments, Spencer tapped out, signalling the end of the match and his wrestling career. Strangler stood to his feet as the referee handed him the fWo World Title. Strangler had overcome two of his most hated rivals in a span of four days. Spencer was helped from the ring, presumably to be never seen or heard of again. Nobody would miss him. As the Strangler celebrated, Eron The Relentless came in through the crowd. Strangler turned around, and was greeted with a steel chair to the head. Strangler didn't fall down after one, it took two shots to get the World Champ down. Eron then locked in his finisher, the De-Roguer. Strangler was unconscious from the shots to the head, but an expression of pain was obvious on his face. Eron kept the hold locked in for several moments until finally he released it. He turned around and saw the World's Greatest Athlete standing behind him with a baseball bat. Eron and Slugger circled each other and Slugger pointed at Eron with the bat. This continued for several moments, until Eron stopped. He began pleading with Slugger not to hit him, but the former four-time fWo World Champion wanted no part of it. He had promised to make that scar on Eron' face bigger at Redemption, and although he won the grudge match between the two, he didn't get the job done. Now was his chance. Slugger prepared to take some batting practice, and send Eron' head off his body with a single swing. Slugger swinged the bat, but Eron moved out of the way. Instead of hitting Eron, it hit a groggy Strangler. Strangler went down immediately, as Slugger looked at his mishap in shock. A deceptive smile came over the face of Eron, before he quickly exited the ring. Slugger bent over to help Strangler up, but it was of no use. Strangler was officially out cold. Slugger looked down at himself in anger and frustration, before checking on the World Champion further. Warfare went off the air in chaos and confusion, all because of Eron. Result: The Boston Strangler by submission.
