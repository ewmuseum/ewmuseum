Sunday Slaughter, 11 July 1999

11 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The scene is black and white, and it opens on a slightly darkened room with a single chair in the center. It's hazy and the picture is slightly out of focus. There are no signs as to exactly where the room is, and a light smoke wafts up from the floor, which is out of the current picture. A man in a suit walks onto the scene, his long hair tied back in a simple pony tail. His face is obscured by smoke and the angle of the shot, and he stands facing the wall for a long time, thinking unknown thoughts. Finally he turns slightly, and the viewer catches a slight glimpse of a worn and tired face. The man raises a single gentlemen's smoking pipe and lights it, washing the Ripper's face with a brief glare of light. Taking a slight puff of the pipe, the Ripper smiles at the unseen camera, and smiles. "Oh, hello. Didn't expect to see me here, did you?" Sitting down on the chair and making himself comfortable, the Ripper looked directly at the camera and began to speak, softly at first, and then more steady. "Well, some really strange things have been happening in our little place we like to call the fWo. Yes, yes... I know what you're thinking... I'm dead. Well, that's quite true. You see, professional wrestling can be a cruel mistress that can overtake your mind if you aren't careful... and well, I wasn't. But that ain't why I'm here exactly. You see, while I'm certainly no longer alive I do live on in the minds of those people that are still very much a part of the living. In their dreams, in their conscience... you know, maybe a few of the wrestlers don't push themselves past the safe parts because of what happened, and that's quite alright. But it's all about dreams that's the reason I'm here right now. You see, a few months ago this cameraman called Geoff Green had this little dream after a long and hectic show, and man it was one hell of a dream. Things were all backward and mixed up... and nobody was quite who they were supposed to be. Well, this dream was so powerful that not all of it was brushed away when ol' Geoff man woke up. Yesser, some of that dream... well, it sorta lingered if you catch my meaning. Some of that dream got stuck into some kind of physical symbol, which in this case happened to be a small crystal necklace like thing. As luck would have it, that goofball Ultra Violet done got himself that necklace by accident, and he's been carting it around ever since. It's actually a wonder something hasn't happened to it before now. But at the end of his match on Friday well, his lady friend went to check up on him 'cause she's not-so-secretly in love with the purple haired fool... not that he realizes it mind you... but she helped him up and that crystal thing, well it broke up pretty damn good. And, well, that's why I'm here right now. Sure, sure... you're all wanting to know what is happening back in the real world with 'ol Scott Slugger and that World Title he stole... but the real world... well, it's pretty much on hold at the moment. So, make yourselves comfortable, and sit back, because things are going to be a bit strange for awhile until somebody sets things right again. Who will do that you ask? Well, I'm sure I don't know... but maybe we can find out together, okay?" The light dimmed in the room as the Ripper finished smoking his pipe, and the viewers were taken down to the ring, where the show was getting ready to begin... The commissioner addresses the cameras Shiny Slaughter took the air as the crowd stood on their feet for Q hours of excitement. It was announced that the fWo World Title would be on the line, and that the fWo Pet of the Year would be in the ring to give an interview. As this lineup was announced the cameras switched to the fWo commissioner, Clarence Chadwick. The cameras caught him cleaning his glasses, and when he noticed that he was being filmed he cleared his throat and began to speak. "Salutations my fellow homo sapiens. Indubitably tonight's broadcasted fare shall confound and stimulate the cranium in a plethora of manners." As Mr. Chadwick continued in this manner, the cameras panned sideways for a shot of his loyal assistant and coffee brewer Malcom, who prepared a cup of coffee and dusted off the United Way display in order to entice more charity. Smiling broadly, Malcom deposited his check into the United Way jar and continued on with his cleaning and dusting. Meanwhile, Mr. Chadwick continued without noticing that the camera had strayed from him. "This eve we shall witness an athletic contest between two newcomers to the fWo, Angry Angus MacGregor and Logan MacGregor. Well, I'm certain that it will be a... *cough* "barn burner" ranking at least C or % stars in the "star rating" category. Personally, I cannot fathom why the uWo would need such charlatans in their roster, but ever since that Korean fellow was elected president we've been forced to hire the most unusual people." Suddenly, the door burst open and steam filled the room. Mr. Chadwick gasped in horror, but soon sighed a breath of relief as Violet entered the room, holding a warm plate of brownies and dressed in her traditional full dress and bonnet. Violet smiled broadly and tilted her head to one side, cheerfully saying, "I've got some brownies ready dear... would you like a taste?" Mr. Chadwick laughed and looked back at the camera. "Ah, dear 'Violet'. Always by my side while asking no affection or anything else in return... you're the perfect partner, aren't you?" Violet smiled even more broadly, and offered the brownies up again more insistently. Laughing, Mr. Chadwick sent the cameras down to the ring for the first match... Dorkness vs. Chris Unicorn Chris Unicorn waited in the ring along with Morning Dawn, his trusted pretty lady. Unicorn had a lot of money with him, and like a good wrestler began giving it out to hungry children in the front row. The lights dimmed, taking the shiny glare off the entrance ramp as Dorkness walked to the ring looking all big and scary-like. Inside the ring some holds and things were applied, but not for long because the audience began to get bored with their lack of big moves. Dorkness finally captured Unicorn in a choke hold, and prepared to slam Unicorn to the mat... but at the last moment thought better of it and put Unicorn safely down. Dorkness then announced that he had made a new friend, and brought Joyness to the ring. Dorkness announced that from now on he would be known as Happiness, and would dedicate his life to fighting evil. During this, Unicorn had gone to the outside to get a steel chair, and tossed it into the ring so Happiness could sit down and make himself comfortable. Unicorn then walked up the entrance ramp, but was meet by the Dark Panther. Unicorn and the Panther stared at each other for a few moments, until the Panther removed his mask and revealed that he was actually Unicorn's father. Unicorn stood in shock for a moment, but then hugged his father and raised both of their arms to the cheering crowd. As they walked back to the backstage area, Fast Driver Guy and Plaque passed out free candy and coloring books to the audience... Result: No Contest Quickjack Blacksiver & Charmeleon vs. Pluto & Ambiguously Gay Wrestler The Lone Ninjas came to the ring for their match against the strange mask people. Quickjack Blacksilver came to the ring with Charmeleon and the Pillsbury Doughboy, and somewhere lurking behind Silver Lox watched the actions with interest. While Macadamia and Pluto stood on the ring apron, the Ambiguously Gay Wrestler locked up with Quickjack. They wrestled for awhile, and tagged in their partners in random unison. This continued for some time, with one wrestler nearly going over the top rope and winning the match right then and there. Finally, as Charmeleon had Pluto on the ropes, Err-Ron appeared on the top of the entry ramp and made his way to the ring. Sliding into the center, Err-Ron stood between the two wrestlers and held up his hands, bidding them to stop. Asking for the microphone Err-Ron said, "I say there chaps... why all of the bloody this and that? You've got to control your anger I say... you can't be punching each other in the gulliver right here and there, you understand right?" At first it looked like Err-Ron was about to be attacked by six men, but then they seemed to come to an understanding and all shook hands. They left the ring together, shaking hands and patting each other on the back to commemorate their newfound friendship. Result: No Contest Pestylance & VaL vs. French Dressing vs. Ground Hero vs. Frank Castle's Guys Pestylance along with VaL walked to the ring, fWo Tag Titles in hand. On the way to the ring, VaL attacked Pestylance, but then seemed to have a change of heart and helped him up and walked him to the ring. Once inside she suplexed Pestylance, but then was concerned for his safety and helped him to his feet. French Dressing came out from the backstage area next, with Taking It To The Primetime and Just Mitch. As they two teams waited inside the ring, Ground Hero came out to the ring as the third team in this four corners Tag Title match. Goldberg carried Wolf Thing to the ring, and as the pair entered all three teams looked at each other uncertainly. Finally, Baseball Man and Icy walked to the ring, representing Frank Castle's Guys. Once inside the ring, a pier Q brawl broke out, with all eight wrestlers attacking each other. VaL took shots at all of the other teams and her own partner on occasion, but always seemed to make up with Pestylance soon after attacking him. Finally Goldberg stumbled over someone's foot and fell out of the ring, thus becoming the victor and taking the Tag Team belts. The action didn't seem to stop however, and the fighting continued inside the ring until Decal came to the ring with his crystal ball thingie and a large bag. Decal climbed into the ring and bid all of the wrestlers to stop, and reached into his bag pulling out six more Tag Team titles. Happily, he announced that everyone was a winner and passed out the belts to the happy wrestlers. Everyone patted each other on the back, and went to the locker rooms for ice cream. Result: Everybody! Mystery Revealed! Cameras went backstage, where Mr. Chadwick paced the backstage area troubled. Despite a warm plate of fresh sugar cookies being held under his nose by Violet, Chadwick remained troubled. From a distance, the names of Angry Angus MacGregor and Logan MacGregor were announced, followed by loud Scottish music. "I just don't comprehend the nature of it Violet... why would dear Scooty turn to the dark side like that? I mean, Scooty and I were close chums from the old days... I just don't know what kind of traumatic event would have turned him like that..." Suddenly, Scooty Cool, AKA Dooth walked into the room with a large club-like instrument and went to attack Chadwick. He was stopped however, by a mysterious figure cloaked in shadows, who grabbed Dooth and delivered a Scooty-dropper onto the hard concrete floor. With Dooth laid out, Chadwick strained to see who had saved him. The figure stepped out of the shadows, revealing none other than Scooty Cool holding a large bag of purple Skittles. Chadwick gasped, and exclaimed, "Scooty! But... if you're you, then who is that on the floor?" Scooty Cool looked serious for a moment and explained, "It's a doppelganger, Clarence." Slapping his forehead, Clarence Chadwick shouted, "Of course! That makes perfect sense... after all, we were best friends, a heel turn by you would be completely illogical! Say, how's Stoned anyway?" Scooty smiled, "Just fine, Clarence, now let's go get some Skittles, what do you say?" Clarence slapped Scooty on the back and said, "I say let's do it, but first I've got to take care of a little business in the ring." As Clarence walked to the ring, loud noises were heard over the P/A system as the match between Angus and Logan continued... with Angus finally landing a move called the Caber Toss and shouting "Och aye!" as he picked up the victory. Pleasant Truckerman (c) vs. Frying Frenchman As the main event was about to begin, Proctor Steel came to the ring with a microphone to announce the crowning of the fWo Pet of the Year. Proctor Steel announced that despite entries from around the globe, including Sherry, Flame, Breeze, Wet Dream, Apocobanana, and Briannie, the fWo Pet of the Year was none other than... Scrappy the Wonder Dog... a healthy basset hound. The crowd all cheered, pleased that a small dog had won out against several bikini-clad beauties. Proctor Steel then called out the challenger to the fWo World Heavyweight Title, the Frying Frenchman. The Frenchman came to the ring with the flag of Hungary, and saluted the crowd pleasantly. His opponent, the Pleasant Truckerman, the fWo World Heavyweight Champion, walked to the ring and the match was at once on, with several really keen wrestling moves being used and even a hold or two. As the Truckerman had the Frenchman in a big strong drop thing, Chadwick ran to the ring and stood between both men. Explaining that they didn't need to fight, because wrestling wasn't about fighting anyway, the Truckerman and the Frenchman shook hands and hugged. Chadwick then invited down other members of the fWo roster that we had seen or heard from earlier, and also wrestlers like Risen, Al Blow, Mauler, Cacti, Crucifax, Rhane, Falcor, Starvation, Fred Slow, Mat F**k, Michael Bare, Profit, The Other Nicer Ninja, The Godfather, The Man With 1,004 Gimmicks, D-High, Scoot Heart, Doctor Strange, Bomb the Tom, Tweezer, Twizzler, Stumpless, Battle, Letch and Lion-O to the ring. They all looked at each other, and began to sing in a chorus line, "Good morning, sunshine... I'm leaving today..." As this song continued, a loud voice came out over the P/A system. Clarence Chadwick looked up in confusion as the voice said, "THIS IS THE STUPIDEST THING I'VE EVER SEEN... AND I'VE SEEN YOUR MATCHES, ULTRA VIOLET." The speaker of these words came out onto the entrance ramp, but before we could see who it was a fan held up a large sign, obscuring the view. Mr. Chadwick sighed, and said, "Oh... it's you. You're always trying to ruin everything..." But no sooner did Clarence utter those words than the world began to fade away into darkness... Result: No Contest The end? Somewhere in an undisclosed location a small glint of light appeared, and then allowed the room to fade back into darkness. A small clinking noise was heard, and a form masked in shadows moved to see what caused the disturbance. The man cloaked in shadows reached down to pick up the object and looked at it for a moment. "Well... this is certainly an interesting trinket... now how can I use it to my best advantage I wonder..." Picking up the crystal, the man placed it into his pocket, and walked slowly over to a table, where a series of fWo contracts lay, all with Austin Davis' name written on them... and the date July 23rd written in dark ink on each.
