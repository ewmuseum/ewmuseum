fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE ONE MORE GONE July 27, 2000 For the second time in three voting rounds, absence played a part in the final elimination. However this time it was of less importance than Round One and the elimination of Mike Extreme, as the absent vote only added to what was already inevitable. Wrestler T-Bone was the latest victim of fWo Survivor. With nine wrestlers voting, it was clear that T-Bone's recent interviews worried several of the fWo Survivor members, causing them to mark the young and energetic wrestler as the next to be eliminated. One wrestler who requested not to be identified said, "T-Bone is sort of one of those diamond in the rough characters. When he first arrived I didn't see him as a huge threat, but he's adapted well to the Survivor environment and as a result I'm going to have to cast my vote his way. I don't doubt for a second that he won't be picked up somewhere else... and fast. I vote for T-Bone." Another wrestler had a very different reason for voting for T-Bone. "I rolled a ten sided die. The number nine came up, sorry 'Bone." Yet another wrestler explained his reason for voting T-Bone with a shrug. "I like everyone in the Survivor contest. I don't like rap though. So T-Bone... I guess that leaves you. Sorry man." Most wrestlers were not happy with casting their votes, and as the fWo Survivor contest wears on a feeling of nervousness sinks deeper and deeper in to the competitors. Several wrestlers, who were jovial coming into Survivor are now more weary, jaded, and subdued. The locker room which was talkative after the first show, is now silent as wrestlers prepare for matches quietly and with little emotion. Stevie Roberts has seen the change, and spoke openly about it. "It's the result of the numbers shrinking. You think you know what you're in for when you join, but when Mike Extreme went out everyone realized that the contest is more brutal and more harsh than it looked like from the surface. Everyone involved deserves a big round of applause... because this is tough. Likely the toughest thing many of these people have ever done." The final votes were as follows: T-Bone: 6 votes, one absent vote, 7 total votes Nick Allstar: 1 vote Reno Davis: 1 vote Suicide: 1 vote More news as it develops.
