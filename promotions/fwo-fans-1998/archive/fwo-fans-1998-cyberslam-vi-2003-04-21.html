Introduction Six years ago the fWo held an event called CyberSlam. It introduced the world of professional wrestling to a cast of characters they had never seen before. Names like Scotty Cool, Ultra Violet, Scott Slugger, the Flying Frenchie, Machina, the Dark Riders, Black Quicksilver, Cactus Jon, Austin Davis and Jim Dudley were instantly made legends, and over the years have told countless stories and wrestled before millions of people. Six years ago the fWo was a small promotion, struggling to gain a foothold in the delicate niche that was professional wrestling. Unknown by all, the fWo has grown over the years as more and more fans have tuned in to watch the progress of wrestlers they made superstars. Never more evident than now, the fWo is the Fans Wrestling Organization... built by the fans to what it is today. For several months the fWo has been banished from performing within the United States. Traveling the world, the fans have slowly lost interest; unable to follow the fWo in person they have looked to other feds. Opportunistic promoters have sought to bury the fWo while it has shown weakness, using the predicament of the fed to their own selfish interests. Touring the world the wrestlers and agents of the fWo have gained a new perspective of the cultures and events that surround and encompass them... and with that understanding have made the decision to go out on top, rather than with a long, prolonged decline of popularity. The fWo returns to New York City to celebrate six years in operation, six years of popularity and six years of devoting themselves to the fans. In the most famous arena in the world, Madison Square Garden, the fWo has come to make their final stand in front of a showcase of millions... Last Stand "Madison Square Garden... it's a great place to go out." Silver Fox nodded to Samuel Smooth and looked up at the arena. Beside them, Thumper and Twitch nursed beers and sat silently. "It is." The air was still cool in New York. Springtime coming on strong now, but the nip in the air could still be felt. Overhead the sky was still light, the daylight moving to darkness would happen later while they were inside the arena. "It's going to be a good show." Twitch nodded, "A grand show. We could go out on a lot worse." Silver Fox looked down into his beer and sighed. "Did I do the right thing? Nobody backstage trusts us anymore... me least of all. Why should they? In the end I lied to them, just like all of the promoters I swore that I'd never be." "Nah, mate." Thumper shook his head. "You did it for the right reasons... which is more than what most could say. They will forgive you, they understand. There are a lot of scared people there now, scared about their livelyhood and their jobs..." Samuel Smooth nodded, "Besides, I've... been talking to some of the boys in the back. They understand deep down. We're golden, so don't you worry none. We did, and you did, what you had to do." "Better by far," said Twitch, "to go out with your head held high than dragging on the ground. Some dogs keep moving after it's time to be put down. We've all seen it. This was the right way to go, and it will be one hell of a show." Silver Fox sat still, his mind tired of racing around the same old problem. But it was no use. In coming back here they violated the law, and if he was lucky all that would happen was that he would be out of a job. Head held high... it sounded good. But sounding good and being good were two different things. Very different things. Cars honked horns, and from where they sat they could see fans filtering into the arena. A few of them knew... thanks to Martyr the news had leaked onto the internet despite Kid Cool's best efforts to prevent it from happening. But they were coming anyway, and for that reason and that reason alone the show must go on. Silver Fox stood and looked back at his partners, and yes, his friends. "Let's go. We've got a show to do." Smiles and nods and determined expressions all around. To fight the fight when the outcome is certainly against them... what more could he ask for? What more could anyone ask for?
