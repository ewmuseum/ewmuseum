Introduction Previously... All roads lead to CyberSlam. As the fWo struggles to maintain morale during a tour of the world that may never see them return to the United States, the promotion finds itself in South America. Faced with a fierce storm on Warfare, the roster is pleased to see that the enviornment is in better spirits this Sunday... but other problems are in store. Transportation on short notice is difficult, and the fWo is about to find itself several key members shy of an ideal operating roster. But eclipsing problems with travel and transport... Ruben Ross. Inside the locked bathroom, Vic sat on the toilet and leaned against the sink. He'd given up trying to stop the trickle of blood flowing from his nostril, letting it run down his face and drip onto the floor. With every beat of his pulse, his head throbbed so badly that it made him feel nauseous. His skin was filmed with a cold, clammy sweat that drastically contrasted with the burning sensation in his joints. Creed would never be mistaken for the poster child for clean living. In his career, he'd ingested on injected enough drugs into his body to start his own pharmaceutical company. Some of it was for social reasons. Some of it was to get that edge he felt he needed in the cutthroat business of professional wrestling. Some of it was to mask the pain caused by the first two reasons, as well as general wear and tear. After all, you don't wrestle in two or three promotions at a time and not take something to help you work through the bumps and bruises. Cracked collarbone? Shoulder tear? Lower back pull? Knee ligament damage? Suck it up or someone else is taking your place on the card. Vic did whatever he had to do to succeed. He wouldn't be the failure his father thought he was. He'd make his daughter proud. Sarah. Vic's eyes turned up from the floor. He hadn't spoken to her in a week. With Darryl Vishanti floating around, he tried to keep in touch with his daughter as much as he could. But it was hard to do with Vic travelling internationally. On top of it all, he was getting a World Title shot at Cyberslam. The number one pay per view of the year, and Kodiak Vic Creed was getting his shot. The pressure of his business and his personal life weighed on the Main Man's shoulders, which only amplified his pain. "Vic? It's getting to be that time," shouted Matt from the other side of the door. "Yah, yah." Vic's tongue felt like it filled his mouth, one of the side effects of the T2 synthetic steroid. He'd taken to calling the drug 'Crash', because that's what happened when the drug wore off. That's where he was at right now, crashed and crashed hard. He tried not taking it for a couple of days, but he received a special delivery package from Vishanti. It was a picture that Sarah had drawn at school. She hadn't even realized the picture was gone. Somehow, he knew Vic hadn't been taking it. So it was back on the poison. It took all of his energy to lift his huge arm to the sink. His hand opened, knuckles scarred from the numerous fights he'd gotten himself into while out on the town, and closed around the small vial of T2. He flexed his legs, just to make sure that they were still there, and struggled to stand. The pain in his shoulder throbbed fiercely. He felt like an arthritic sixty-one year old, rather than an athletic and powerful thirty-one year old. But all that would change with one injection. Matt continued to pace, stopping only to look at his watch. "Things aren't running that well tonight. I don't want to cut things too closely, in case there's a problem." The door flew open. The two ticking time bombs were about to go at it again. But this time, Shadows brought a little something extra with him. Flair was the first to take a punch but Shadows ducked it. As he ducked the punch he pulled out a lead pipe from his boot and swiftly took it to Flair's rib cage. Flair bent over gasping for air as Shadows stood up and brought the pipe over his head and sent it crashing down into Flairs neck with a thunderous thud. Shadows brought it crashing down again for good measure. He then stood over Flair in a victorious position with his arms raised in the air, but it takes a lot more than that to keep the 'king of extreme' down for long. Flair saw an opening and his took it, a quick punch to Shadows' family jewels. Although some people will tell you he has none. Shadows immediately grabbed his crotch and the pipe fell to the ground. Flair picked it up, but still obviously in pain from the two previous shots to the back of the neck, and got in a few shots of his own. It was getting violent, and fast. Several wrestlers from the back rushed out and broke up the fight before it spilled over into the crowd preventing yet another potential riot. Flair broke free from ones grasp and charged at Shadows, Shadows was on one knee and saw Flair coming and hit him with a spear. The two exchanged punches as the fWo logo flashed on the television screen and faded to black.
