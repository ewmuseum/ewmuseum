Disney's Wednesday Warfare, 30 November 2005

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The 11/28 Warfare Report Crazy night of Warfare once more, its late and I don't have much time so let's get this started. An "Untouchable" Carson Nash promo played on the TravisTron. It showed him standing outside his "future home" and panned out to show the new Professional Wrestling Hall of Fame building being built in Latham, NY. At the end of the promo, Nash held up a replica plaque of himself saying that one day, it would hang above all others. Underneath his name read: "The Single Greatest Professional Wrestler Ever." A good segment that had the crowd hot the whole time. (1) Rana Venenosa def. Caden Brocc in a first round WOTY tournament match. Nobody had heard of Brocc leading into the match, so there was some curiosity. Wasn't a long one, so can't really judge him yet...Venenosa goes over with the Frog Splash. (2) Internet Champion "The Mecca" Alex Creed beat "The Golden Child" Monte Burns in a first-round, non-title WotY tournament match. A most entertaining match, pitting the two-time Internet Champion, Alex Creed, against a three-time Cruiserweight Champion in Monte Burns. The tempo of the match started out fairly quickly, but before long it was the veteran Burns who took control and tried to keep the Mecca restrained. Despite being the smaller of the two, Alex showed his surprising strength and used his explosive high-risk style to counter Burns' more methodical approach. When Burns had seemingly gotten the upper hand by countering a Mecca Driver into an Electric Chair drop, he called for the Establishment--sadly for him, Alex countered it with a dropkick and Burns landed awkwardly. One Discus Lariat later, the match was over. They shook hands after, and while Burns did hold on to the back of his neck on the way out he was walking out without help and waving and everything. Backstage, Coral Avalon, he who will work twice tonight, tried to smooth things over with Michelle Masters. The CW champion, not known for her emotional stability in spots, just gave him the silent treatment. A frustrated Coral eventually just departed after Ruben Ross was more than happy to fill the communication void by mocking Coral unmercifully, including liberal use of "Where's my royalties check?" (3) Sphere beat Dr. Curiosity in a first-round WotY tournament match. The two tag team partners met in the center of the ring before the opening bell, and Sphere uncharacteristically extended a hand for a pre-match handshake. Curiosity accepted, only for the referee to find a pair of brass knuckles in his possession moments later. As the referee took the foreign object away and discarded it from the ring, Sphere produced another foreign object from his tights, striking Curiosity in the throat before placing the unseen object into his boot. Most of the match saw Sphere only getting ahead through cheating, while Curiosity tried to counter with basic wrestling maneuvers such as the bodyslam and vertical suplex. Midway through the match, Mega Job, along with the Entourage of Scott Falk and Robert Falk, came down to ringside. They began to film the match and do a report on it, claiming that it is such a rarity these days to see two tag partners and best friends throwing their alliance out the window for singles glory. It was then that Mike Heftel began to make his way through the crowd. He had some difficulty...ALOT of difficulty...but eventually made it over the guardrail. Beef egged him on, claiming that he once saw him win a match clean. Heftel tried to attack, but was stopped by Robert Falk, who promptly used him as human javelin. A short while later, the match ended with Sphere countering a sunset flip by sitting down on Curiosity and grabbing the ropes over the referee's head. Mike Heftel cut a promo from the trainer's room as he was getting his head bandaged. Evil-Lyn was nearby, yelling at the trainers to work faster (except for the female one, whom she talked to rather softly). Heftel went on about how even though he came away practically unscathed from his run-in earlier in the show (he claims to only have dislocated a shoulder and found a kneecap floating around somewhere in his ribs), and the reason he's getting bandaged up is because he ran into a low ceiling on the way to the locker room. He once again blamed it on Mega Job, and said that next week they *would* pay for what "they" did to Jon Crisp several weeks ago. (4) Michelle Masters (w/Rob T) defeated Coral Avalon by DQ in what I assume was a fWo Cruiserweight Title match. Not even a match, as it ended like a minute in with a run-in. Michelle slapped the everloving N'awlin out of Coral to start. A lockup was offered and shoved out of. A second lockup (since Coral's a no-women-hitting wuss) was more successfully, but Coral's DDT attempt failed and Michelle dropkicked him headfirst into the buckle for his trouble. And then...... As Rob T watched the proceedings from ringside, something grabbed his attention. A man wearing a hooded sweatshirt and a ski mask has jumped over the barricade on the other side of the ring. He turned to look... ...only to get hit in the back of the head with a forearm. The perpetrator of that crime was dressed identically, but a little taller than the first man, who slid into the ring and speared Michelle after she whipped Coral into the ropes. So much for the match. The crowd's boos told Coral that something had happened, and as he ran over to help Michelle, a spinning clothesline from the masked man who'd took down Rob put him on the canvas. The other man picked Coral up, hooked his head, then ran to the ropes and jumped off backwards, driving Coral back into the mat with a springboard reverse DDT. Together, the two men pulled Michelle up to her feet and whipped her into the ropes. As she came back, one of them dropped to his knees and swept her leg out from under her. At the same time, the other one ran towards her, grabbed her by the neck, and snapped her backwards, bending her in half and sending her to the mat. At that, the booing changed into a general murmur of confusion and speculation. That move... it couldn't be who they thought it was, could it? Then the shorter of the two men pulled his sweatshirt and ski mask off, and the mop of dirty blonde hair sent the place into shock. Then the other man removed his ski mask and that confirmed it. Tim Logan. Vince Pulaski. Sabotage was in the fWo. Tim yelled at the ringside attendant to give him a microphone over the new chorus of boos. "It's about damn time we got here." Sabotage walked over and knelt down next to Michelle. "C.T.U., Michelle? 'Chelle Terrorizing Unit? Come on, you're better than that," Tim continued. "Not by much, but... although you are still collecting has-beens like the black Mario wannabe this guy's running with collects Shines," Tim said, pointing to the still-fallen Coral Avalon. "Seriously, though, look at you. Michelle Masters. From an absolute nobody two years ago to the biggest stage in the business. Cruiserweight champion of the fWo. And I know this looks bad, but really, Vince and I couldn't be prouder. "So we just thought we'd drop in, sign a couple of fWo contracts.
