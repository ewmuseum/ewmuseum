Sunday Slaughter, 25 February 2001

25 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Outside the Bradley Center, a cold wind blew. The temperature was well below freezing, and the wind itself certainly wasn't helping. John Willis patrolled the premises, looking for anything that appeared to be out of place. He had found nothing, and began to walk back towards the building. Willis stopped at the door to the arena and began to reflect. He was a long-time veteran of the security business, and had been with the fWo for nearly all of its near three years. He had learned on the job to trust his gut, and his gut was telling him that bad shit would soon be going down. Even the wind seemed to support this theory. It howled and gibbered like a maniac monster, swirling around Willis and making him shiver. It was an ill wind, and it didn't bode well for tonight. Willis shook his head and stepped inside the arena. Slaughter was about to begin. MUD (c) vs. Carnage The entire arena was silent, as the lights dimmed and the cell was lowered to the ring, and hanging from it's ceiling is the knife that Carnage used for several violent acts in his life, including two stabbings of Toad. Finally, the cage was on the ground, as the ring attendants checked to make sure and then they instantly charged backstage. The violent match, was moved from last night as the Minnesota State Athletic Commission denied the match from happening just prior to the event. But the thought was washed out of the fans minds, as "Guilty Conscience" by Eminem plays over the sound system bringing the former two time fWo Hardcore Champion, Carnage, out to the arena. Focused and determined, Carnage ignored the hecklers in the ground, as he entered the cage through the open door, and he looked around examining the cell. As he walked around the ring running his hand against the cage, he noticed the ladder that stood next to the ring post. As Carnage reached forward to touch the ladder, his music cut and "My Name is Mud" by Primus shot over the PA system which brought Mud out to the ring like a bullet out of a pistol charging down the ramp for vengeance against the man who killed his buddy Toad. As Carnage turned from the ladder, he watched Mud find his way into the cage through the open door. And the steel cage was shut and then locked by the outside referee. But the cage door was the least of the worries of either of the wrestlers. Slowing his pace, Mud swiftly removed his Hardcore title and sent it crashing to the floor. The two opponent's eyes locked, and their gazes remained liked for several moments, but then something was set off causing both Mud and Carnage to charge forward simultaneously at one another in a car less game of chicken. Neither man veered off his path, but Mud wins as he leaps into the air lambasting Carnage with a flying lariat. And the hardcore champ didn't stop with knocking down his opponent, grabbing Carnage by the hair he leads him to the steel steps and begins to ram his head against it violently, yet consistently. With each slam of the head, the more the tiny slit on Carnage's head opened, and from that slit came a red river flowing down the bridge of his nose. Carnage's body simply slumped to the ground, but Mortimer Ulysses Dredrick wasn't done yet, as he lifts his opponent off the ground by the back of his shirt, and propels the Crazy Corteia hard into the steel cage. As a result the body of Carnage simply bounced back off the cage, which caused him to bang the back of his head on the steel steps. Mud was enraged already from the previous occurrences between he and Carnage, and finally he knew that he could finish all the problems right here and now. Mud scooped up his nemesis and slid him into the ring. Taking his time, Mud slowly walked up the steel steps entering the ring over the second rope, and he eyed his current victim who was pushing himself to his feet, Mud taking the opportunity to send him in the other direction as he stomped on the back of Carnage. Mud then changes his mind, lifts Carnage to his feet and sends him hard into the ropes with an Irish Whip, waiting Mud goes for a DTH but as Carnage arrives he leaps over the attempt following up by bouncing off the opposite ropes and charges forward nailed Mud with a baseball slide to the face. Carnage then brings Mud to his feet, only to follow it up by sending the Hardcore champ to the ropes, and he follows up with a splash in the corner. Staying on the attack, Carnage stood on the first rope and sent a fury of fists to the face of Mud, and then as he stopped he went to bite the forehead, and as he did so Mud's screams were unleashed. Carnage looked up towards the see of fans and spit out what seems to be a piece of Mort's skin, and he started his maniacal laugh as he showed his yellowed teeth that are now covered in a slight crimson covered liquid. The laughs turned to gasps, as the little Carnagies were violated as Mud lowblowed his opponent, Mud then grabbing onto the legs of Carnage simply throws him to the outside where he smashes into the standing ladder causing it to fold and slam into the cage wall! But Carnage didn't move, as his leg was caught between the spaces between the steel rungs, seeing this Mud got to the top and dived off with a plancha directly onto Carnage. The ladder fall from it's leaning position on the cage, it simply dropped down to the ground hard, causing the very bodies of both Carnage and Mud to bounce off. Quickly after Mud rebounded, and was on his search under the ring as he threw the fWo logo embroidered ring cloth up, and soon after he spotted what he was looking for as he yanked out a table. And he held it in both arms ready to set it up but what he wasn't ready for was Carnage who speared him from behind sending Mud down to the ground directly on top of the metal bottom of the table. The hardcore champion cringed in pain and rolled on his back. Taking up where Mud left off, Carnage lifted the table and set it up. Nowhere near finished, he grabbed onto to the back of Mud's head and set him up on the ring apron. Carnage with a rear grapple on Mud, setting up for the first half of Maximum Carnage, but as he attempted to nail the Snap German Suplex, Mud shot back a mule kick to the groinal area of Carnage. Mud then turned and grabbed onto Carnage's head, and he jumped off and nailed a Tornado DDT! And both men crashed through the table, in a pile of wreckage. Both men slow to respond, until Mud fought his way to his feet. Slowly he walked, until he reached the ladder, Mud grabs onto the ladder and slid it into the ring. And after he rolled, as he got in and he unfolded it setting it up directly under the knife which delicately hung from the ceiling. Mud slowly climbed the ladder and reached up grabbing the knife, but the moment he gained control of it the ladder came crashing down. The knife dropped out of Mud's hands, and there became a mad dash for the weapon, as Carnage reached down grabbing his knife. And he walked on uneven ground as he approached Mud, a crazed glare in his eye and he stabbed forward at Mud who in attempt to avoid Carnage jumped backwards and tripped over the ladder. Stalking like a hunter towards its prey. And the closer Carnage got the more Mud pushed back with the palms of his hands and finally he reached forward grabbing the ropes and as Carnage approached he was hit hard by the feet of Mud which was sent to the midsection of his opponent. Once again the knife dropped to the ground, and this time it bounced on the ground, and Mud reached down and grabbed it. Carnage arms open and charging towards Mud, leaving an opening... and Mud took it as he stabbed Carnage's abdomen and he pulled the knife out. Covered in blood, he stared towards Mud, his legs shaking more than ever, blood dripping down his lips, as they curled to show that Carnage was laughing... by now the bell had rang and the announcer announced that Mud was still the Hardcore champ, but he had to stop the laughing as he thrusted his arm forward yet again catching Carnage again. And at that point, Carnage slumped to the ground encircled by a puddle of blood. Mud took the knife into his pocket, as his music once again played as he exited but pushing passed him was the paramedics to take Carnage to the nearest hospital. Result: MUD by stabbing State Of The fWo Slaughter returned from commercials to the sight of a freshly-cleaned ring and the sound of Jeremiah Frankton and James Armstrong reminding the viewing public that MUD and Carnage had gone into the previous match voluntarily, and updating them that Carnage appeared to have suffered only superficial flesh wounds and would require stitches but nothing more. After that was finished, the crowd rose to their feet as they heard "Do You Buy What The Sellout Is Selling?" Chris Universal, dressed typically slickly, made his way to the ring, microphone in hand. Universal climbed up the steps into the ring and addressed the crowd. "Hello Milwaukee!" The crowd cheered at the mention of their hometown. "Now, I have plenty of important news to get to, so I'm not going to waste any time. First is the condition of Ruben Ross. As reported earlier by Dick Venom, Ross has suffered multiple back and neck injuries, and he will miss at least a month, possibly more. I'd like to send out my best wishes to Ruben. As a result of his injury, I have no choice but to vacate the Internet Title. Thus, there will be a sixteen-man tournament held to crowd the new champion, beginning tonight and culminating at Dead Or Alive! The brackets are as follows. Tonight, Havok will face Juno Law; Fear will face Chris Titan; Robert Jensen will face Hellrazor; and Erik Kelly will face Crusader. Next week, the Boston Strangler will face Vengence; Mike Heftel will face Sphere; Frank Snow will face Black Quicksilver; and the Black Panther will face an opponent who will make his debut tonight against Sphere, next! On the March 11 Slaughter, the quarterfinals will take place; on the March 18 Slaughter, the semifinals will take place; and the finals will be held at Dead Or Alive!" Universal paused, mentally moving on to his next subject, then continued. "As everybody knows, last night at In Misery Mitch Wilson and Wretch tied in an Iron Man match to determine who would face the fWo World Champion at CyberSlam IV. I spent last night and tonight's pre-show thinking about what I would do to rectify that situation, and I've come to a conclusion. At Dead Or Alive, *both* Wretch and Mitch Wilson will receive a World Title shot against Mike Bear in a triangle match!" The crowd cheered loudly at this. "Whoever walks out of that match as champion will be guaranteed a spot in the CyberSlam main event. They will have the luxury of facing a mystery opponent at CyberSlam in the opening match. What's the luxury there, you ask? In that match, the fWo World Title won't be on the line. The two people who don't win at Dead Or Alive will meet at CyberSlam in a one-on-one match, with the winner facing the titleholder regardless if he defeats the mystery opponent or not!" The crowd cheered again, and Universal smiled. "Oh, and one more thing. Since last night's match was inconclusive, I feel it's only fair that, in this very ring tonight, Mitch Wilson will face Wretch in a one-on-one rematch!" The crowd popped huge at the announcement of this gigantic main event. Universal continued. "With all of that in mind, enjoy tonight's Slaughter... if you buy... what the Sellout... is selling!" Universal finished his address off with his trademark catchphrase, and left the ring to a shower of cheers. Cameras left Universal to return to the ring where the next match was about to take place. Mystery Opponent vs. Sphere "Back To School" by the Deftones emanates from the speakers, and Sphere, hot off a phenomenal match for the Cruiserweight title yesterday at In Misery, makes his way down to wait for the Mystery opponent. A feminine voice replaces Sphere's entrance theme, and it whispers "You can't deny Fate." "Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson begins, and Sphere starts waiting anxiously in the ring. Meanwhile, Eddy Fate, jumps over the guard rail, wearing a high necked, sleeveless, black spandex suit, with green stripes down the sides, and his trademark leather jacket and Foster Grant sunglasses. Eddy slides in under the bottom rope, and walking up behind Sphere, taps him on the shoulder. Sphere turns, swinging, and Eddy ducks under, retaliating with a European uppercut as the referee signals for the bell to start the match. Eddy immediately pushes Sphere into the ropes, and shoots Sphere off, and Eddy tries for an overhead belly-to-belly. Sphere lands on his feet, however, and catches Fate from behind, and tosses out an atomic drop. Eddy turns just in time to be slammed down with a superkick, and Sphere quickly springboards to the ropes, impacting with a moonsault. He only gets a two count, and as he pulls Fate up, Eddy counters with a single leg takedown, then drops his leg into the midsection of Sphere. Eddy pulls Sphere into a double-underhook, and then hits a painful suplex, floating over for the cover. Sphere kicks out at two, and Eddy brings his opponent up again, and steps behind into a waistlock. Sphere reverses, then grabs a full-nelson, and starts to lift. Eddy struggles, then breaks the hold, and twist behind Sphere again. Eddy lifts hard, and hits a German suplex, but maintains his grasp, hitting another. As he goes for the third, Sphere tries to elbow his way out, but Eddy ducks under, and slams Sphere down with a northern lights suplex. The count breaks at two, and Sphere cuts off Fate with a rake to the eyes. Sphere spins Fate around, then drops for a backslide, but again only gets a two count. Eddy starts up, but Sphere grabs the newcomer's head, and introduces it to the top turnbuckle. Sphere levels in a chop, then another, and decides to toss Eddy to the other corner. Fate reverses, however, and the impact tosses Sphere out into a spinning wheel kick. Eddy brings Sphere up and lifts him onto his shoulder, then sets him on the top rope. Eddy climbs up and positions himself, then leaps and hurricanranas Sphere to the mat. With the Twist Of Fate hit successfully, Eddy covers Sphere for the three count, garnering a hard fought victory in his debut fWo match. The crowd cheers Fate as he celebrates his victory, while Slaughter goes backstage... I actually have to defend the tag team titles with him. I don't think this can get any worse. As Crusader finished taping up his wrists in his locker room, a backstage worker poked his head in quickly. "Your match is next," he called out, before moving on. Crusader took note of it and slipped on his elbow pad. "Crusader versus Erik Kelly... it sounds like CyberSlam 2000 all over again." She looked into the far right-hand corner of the mirror, and saw an image that she wished she hadn't. It was the reflection of Chris Titan.
