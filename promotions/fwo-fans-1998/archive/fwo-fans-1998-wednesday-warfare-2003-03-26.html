Introduction With the world stage still absorbed by war, the fWo heads toward CyberSlam on a collison path with their own destiny. This night the fWo finds themselves in France, controversial for the nation's stance on the war in Iraq. But for some in the fWo the journey to France is a homecoming, and as one man celebrates his return to homeland another fears for his life. Silver Fox, the chief fWo road agent has been taken hostage by Richard Moore, the deranged father of Ryan Moore... a wrestler the world belives is dead. But Ryan Moore is alive and well under the guise of Fallen, information that has just been given to Richard Moore. His reaction may very well prove to be his most unpredictable act yet. But conflict is a way of life in the fWo, as Erik Kelly and Kodiak Vic Creed continue their path to Cyberslam with the fate of the fWo World Heavyweight Championship hanging in the balance... Vitriol Samuel Smooth paced backstage. The hope amongst the staff had been that once they'd reentered the Western World, they'd leave some of their troubles behind. Slaughter, for instance, had been fine. Paris was shaping up to be altogether something different. Matt Panzer tore through the curtains marking the entrance to the stage, fuming. "This isn't going to work. That crowd is livid." "They always boo you, Matt," interjected Thumper. "Cute. No, it's not that. You've seen the signs. You've heard the chants. "I'm not." Silver Fox shifted his weight against the crude ropes that bound him. Richard Moore sat backwards on the chair leaning over the back and regarded him with what looked like a mixture of disgust and mistrust. "Prove it." Silver Fox slumped back against the wall. Moore had drug him into a dank Tel-Aviv hotel. They had been planning to make the cross and travel into Europe proper, but their conversation had taken a turn and now all of Moore's attention was upon him and what he had said. Silver Fox second guessed himself for the hundredth time since he had let it out that Ryan Moore was alive and well. He had hoped it might cause his captor to falter, to sink with the revelation and he might be able to slip away. But the opposite seemed to be the truth... Moore was more calm, more still and looked more withdrawn than ever. "Prove it? Prove that your son is alive?" Silver Fox sighed. "How do you prove the truth to a blind man... or a mad man... someone who denies the truth when it stares them in the face..." Moore blinked slowly, and drew the pistol he carried up until it was leveled at Silver Fox's head. "Prove it." Silver Fox closed his eyes and thought, desperately thought, of a way out of what seemed more and more like a hopeless situation...
