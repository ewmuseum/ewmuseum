Introduction Previously... The fWo continues their world tour in Tokyo, Japan... but while in the land of the rising sun evil deeds are afoot back home... Don't Mess With Me Coral Avalon was bored. I know that's not the best of openings, and I'm aware I'm smacking the fourth wall with a giant mallet that's engraved with the words "THIS MALLET BELONGS IN THE FOURTH WALL'S FACE", but bear in mind that I follow the law known as "simplicity is the mother of invention and the father of hardcore". Yeah, it should be "necessity". Shut up. Anyway, Coral Avalon was bored. So, the logical conclusion from this is to ask "why is he bored? He's a professional wrestler for God's sakes!". And the logical answer? He's got NOTHING tonight. No big debut, no amazing thing to do... he just had a Silver Screen segment with Sonny Silver, combining two things he really doesn't like: In-ring promos and Sonny Silver. He especially despised the latter, after an incident on a tSC Tuesday Night Wrestling program where Sonny made Coral's (somewhat emotionally weak) girlfriend, Annabelle, cry. Mainly just because Sonny thought that was hysterical. He had half a mind to just walk out there and deck Sonny just for continuing to exist. However, his train of thought was soon derailed, skidded off course, and crashed into traffic at a crossroads when the Codemaster came from behind, turned him around, and shoved him into a wall. Coral was knocked into a seated position from the impact, and attempted to get up. SMACK! That was the Skull Hammer barely missing Coral's head, impacting the wall just above him. Coral, however, didn't seem to be that nervous, "I see that anger management hasn't worked for you very well." "Shut up." the Codemaster said, "Or the next shot won't be intentionally missed." Coral sighed and slowly got up anyway, keeping an eye on the Codemaster and his hammer while getting up, "Let me guess. You're still ticked off from last week and want to take it out on the guy who said it was going to happen." The Codemaster grunted, but said nothing. "Look," Coral said, "I didn't lose the title for you, I just predicted you might lose it. I'm pretty sure you don't want to pay any more fines for assaulting a wrestler, do you?" The Codemaster grimaced. His referee-bashing, set-destroying tantrum last week had cost the Codemaster a few thousand dollars and certainly set back any hope of remodelling the video game store he operated in Utah. He probably didn't want that to happen again. However, he pointed at Coral, "Don't mess with me, Locke. I ain't in a very good mood. I'm liable to do anything." "Yeah. Sure." Coral said, before he turned around and walked away. However, he turned around and looked at Codemaster, "Although, hopefully, most of that anything you're liable to do will revolve around getting better at Soul Calibur 2," he said, before he winked at him, "Last I checked, I'm still better at that." With that, Coral left, leaving the Codemaster to tighten his grip on his hammer. "You'll get yours." he said, through clinched teeth. Preparing for WAR!! "Don't you understand what they've done?" Carson Nash's voice rang out. It was two weeks in a row that he had been scheduled for a tag-team match only to have the match cancelled as he and Chris Small were about to walk out on stage. The reason they heard were the tag team name "Victoria 69ers" was unappropriate and that since they were short on time, the fWo creative team decided to can their debut tag match. However, Carson Nash was smarter then that. He knew that the crowd would rather watch him wrestle than sit back and watch Keith Scott Zimmerman do his spotty 8 moves per match. "They've declared war on us." Chris Small sat back and watched his tag team partner pace around the locker room. "Maybe we should just change our tag team name," Small suggested. The look that came across "Untouchable" Carson Nash's face was that of completely disgust. "WHO THE FUCK ARE YOU? DO NOT QUESTION MY DECISIONS!" Nash screamed back at his tag team partner. Small hung his head, obviously annoyed, and began to focus on tying up his gear. "What the hell do you think you're doing?" Nash asked. "They're not showing me any respect, I'm refusing to get into that ring tonight." "I know you are, you told me that already, which is why I went and got myself booked in a match with Mike Ragnal for my debut." You felt "Untouchable" Carson Nash's heart drop. He was hurt... Just kidding, he was pissed. "FUCK YOU!" Nash screamed and stormed out of the locker room. Small continued to lace his boots and smirked. "Well, that went better then I thought it would have..." The scene almost faded to black when the door swung open. "WHY ARE YOU TALKING TO YOURSELF?" Nash screamed and slammed the door closed again. NOW the scene faded to black... Rana Venenosa vs. Sphere A chill runs up your spine And crawls into your brain A freezing touch of fear... Sure, those feelings no longer take over the average person's body upon Sphere's entrance, but it's still a kickass song, so why not just let him use it as his entrance theme? The American Psycho (who's still American yet hasn't really been acting like a "psycho" lately) appeared from behind the curtain and made his way to the ring, once again not looking too enthused to be competing here tonight. He climbed into the ring and simply leaned against a corner, paying no attention to...well, anyone, really. Soon after his music died down, "The Cage" began to play. Rana Venenosa stepped out onto the entrance ramp, getting a much more positive reaction than Sphere. Hell, he got more of a reaction PERIOD. He made his way toward the ring, focused for his first match since Meltdown. The Poisonous Frog entered the squared circle, keeping his eye on the now-only-occasionally devious Sphere as he did some last minute prepping for this bout. His music died down, shortly before the referee signalled for the bell. Rana and Sphere circled each other before meeting in a collar-and-elbow tieup. Rana easily won, as it didn't look like Sphere was trying to get any type of early advantage, and the Poisonous Frog slapped on a side headlock. Rana wrenched on Sphere's head until the American Psycho was able to shove him off and into the ropes. Sphere slowly stood straight up, only to have Rana floor him with a shoulderblock. Rana looked down at Sphere, then ran toward the ropes behind him. He bounced off and noticed that Sphere was still on his back, so he went for an elbowdrop...and connected. Yes, Sphere made no effort at all to evade the maneuver. Rana then pulled Sphere up and rocked him with several forearms before whipping him into the ropes, catching him with a hurricanrana once the American Psycho reached the middle of the ring. Sphere decided now would be the best time to roll underneath the bottom rope and take a breather on the floor, but didn't have much time to think, as Rana came flying over the top rope with a somersault dive, taking Sphere down on the thinly-padded mats surrounding the ring. Rana immediately hopped up to his feet, visibly energized by the early advantage he's been able to gain thus far. He bent down and grabbed Sphere, picking him up off the floor and rolling him back into the ring. Venenosa re-entered the ring himself, picking up Sphere, only to put him back on the mat with a scoop slam. He signalled that he was ready to go up top, then he stepped out onto the apron and began to climb the turnbuckles. Once he was up top, ready to fly again, Sphere shuffled out of the way and rolled underneath the bottom rope, heading out to the floor once again. Of course, he was sure to keep his eye on Rana, making sure he wasn't in harm's way this time. The American Psycho took a short walk around the ring before stepping up onto the apron, telling Rana to "stay back", and also telling the ref to "make" Rana stay back. He climbed between the ropes and pantomimed that he was ready, and then circled around the ring while Rana did the same. Right before the two could meet in a collar-and-elbow tieup, Sphere caught Rana by surprise with an eye gouge, then secured a side headlock while Rana was stunned. "HA!", Sphere laughed rather loudly, proud of the fact that he was able to trick Rana into a false sense of security. He wasn't laughing several seconds later, however, when Rana lifted him up and dropped him backwards to the mat with a back suplex. Shortly after flopping up into the air and onto his stomach, Sphere once again rolled underneath the bottom rope... ...or at least tried to. Rana was able to catch him this time, pulling him up to his feet and laying in a hard knife-edged chop to his chest. Sphere grabbed himself and stumbled into a corner, where he ended up having to endure MORE chops. Now was the time he regretted not leaving his t-shirt on before he came out for the match. After all, it's 2005. If someone wants to wear a t-shirt while they wrestle, then apparently they CAN. I mean, it's not like they want the people in the crowd to think they're actual athletes and entertainers and such. Maybe that's Sphere's mistake. He wants to be a wrestler, and as we all know, wrestlers suck when they're in the big-time promotions. Working the knee to set up a leg submission? Who wants to see THAT? Rana pulled Sphere out of the corner, only to send him into the corner across the ring with an Irish whip. Sphere hit the turnbuckles and remained there, giving Rana the opportunity to charge. Sphere got his legs up before Rana could make contact, though, maneuvering himself between the ropes and ending up on the apron. Rana was able to put the brakes on and keep from crashing into the turnbuckles, but Sphere leaned forward from his position on the apron, catching the Poisonous Frog with a forearm to the jaw. He then ran across the apron and and climbed up to the top rope, wasting no time in leaping toward Rana, apparently going for a cross-body-block. Rana was ready, though, as he caught Sphere with a dropkick to the midsection as the American Psycho was still in mid-air. It wasn't that long after both men hit the mat that Rana dove on for a pinfall attempt... 1. 2. ..... ..... Kickout. Rana made it to his feet, pulling Sphere with him and going for another Irish whip. Sphere managed to reverse this one, however, and caught Rana with an inverted sitout powerbomb that planted him face-first into the mat. Instead of going for the cover, Sphere hit the ropes and...well...casually walked over to Rana's head and dropped a knee on it. It was *then* that he decided to go for a cover... 1. 2. ..... ..... C'mon, do you think a lazy kneedrop is gonna beat Rana Venenosa? Although it'd be funny if it did. Seriously, it'd be a total burial. Then again, it's not like anyone would know or be talking about it or whatever. Purple monkey dishwasher. We should get back to the match...don't worry, it's almost over. The ending's the best part, right? Especially if the "right guy" goes over? Sphere lifted Rana off the mat and nailed him with several European uppercuts before sending him into the ropes, perhaps making the mistake of putting his head down. Rana flew over, attempting roll Sphere up with a sunset flip, but the American Psycho stood his ground. He tried to keep himself from falling backward, even reaching out in an attempt to grab the referee. With the official out of his reach, Sphere simply shrugged and fell back. 1. 2. ..... ..... Sphere got a shoulder up to escape the pinfall attempt, then rolled backward and shot up to his feet, kicking Rana square in the face. This was most likely the first time in FOREVER (aside from that match with Dr. Curiosity a while ago) that Sphere got a positive reaction, as apparently REALLY HARD KICKS TO THE FACE go over very well in Japan. Rana held his face, and rightfully so, since HE JUST GOT KICKED REALLY HARD IN THAT AREA. Sphere pulled him off the mat and teased to the crowd that he would try to lift Rana for the Six Feet Under, but instead just tossed him over the top rope and to the floor. Sphere yawned and simply stood there in the ring, waiting for Rana to get up on the outside. Once the Poisonous Frog was on his feet, Sphere began to signal that he was going to take flight, perhaps as a receipt for the dive that Rana hit him with in the opening moments of the match. He hit the far ropes, charged across the ring, and...HOLY CRAP! He stopped. He just leaned against the ropes and looked at Rana, then began to clap and act as if he were cheering on his opponent, "begging" him to get back in the ring before the ten-count. Rana reached for the middle rope and pulled himself up on the apron, while Sphere backed up and just, well...let him get back in the ring. Rana looked at Sphere, not sure of what was going on, but Sphere held his arms out, as if to say "Hey, I'm a nice guy!", only to lunge forward and attempt a clothesline. Since clotheslines don't feel that good, whether they're coming from a "nice guy" or not, Rana ducked, then turned around and connected with a few well-placed forearm shots to the face of Sphere. One could tell they were well-placed since Sphere said "Ow." after each shot, see. Rana then went for an Irish whip, but Sphere put the brakes on and pulled Rana in, setting him up for a Russian legsweep. The ref saw Sphere's foot on the bottom rope, RIGHT before Sphere himself pulled it away. Yes, that's right.
