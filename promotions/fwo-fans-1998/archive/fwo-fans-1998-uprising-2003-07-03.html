Introduction The impact of the fWo Unleashed: Battle Bowl special is still being felt as all eyes turn to Meltdown and the possibility of supreme victory... Epidemic I. fWo Uprising did not open with a token fireworks display. Instead a single word written out in plain white text sat ominously on the pitch black video wall. Disease. Slowly but surely the letters bled into the next words. The Bubonic Plague. The words remained only for a few seconds before fading away to be replaced by the sickening image of thousands upon thousands of rats all crawling amongst themselves in a dark confined space. And then the deep voice that wouldn't have been out of place in a movie trailor spoke up. "If only the rodents had been the all that the black plague affected. Transmitted through the fleas that plagued their bodies the disease quickly infected the people of China in the early 1330's before the outbreak spread through Western Asia and Europe. The disease struck and killed people with terrible speed and in less than five years 25 million people died between 1347 and 1352. From this we learn that diseases can develop in any time or place under any circumstance leaving nothing immune. Not even the fans Wrestling organization. At Meltdown, a new disease develops and the epidemic begins." The footage faded to black and the fWo returned to regular programming. Defined Uprising began in earnest inside the Tacoma Dome with thunderous fireworks and a wild, cheering Washington crowd. fWo cameras panned the crowd, catching jumping, shouting fans, and various signs including "Slugger + Mariners = greatness", "Triple XXXias", and "Heftel Is High". The commotion continued as the cameras settled on the ring, and the announcers welcomed the crowd to Uprising. The Tacoma Dome went dark. Earth-shattering fireworks erupted on the entrance ramp. "Back In Black" by Fistfunk started to play. The roof of the Tacoma Dome blew off in the general direction of the Pacific Ocean, turning Tacoma's most famous venue into an open-air stadium. And Scott Slugger stepped out from backstage. The World's Greatest Athlete, wearing a Joel Pineiro jersey and jeans, walked down the entrance ramp, with fireworks shooting up behind him with every step. As he neared the ring, the fireworks died down and Slugger began to slap hands with the fans. He then jumped onto the apron and stepped into the ring, then quickly made his way to the ropes. Slugger climbed onto the second rope and leaned over towards the crowd, raising an arm to the Tacoma fans, who continued to cheer deafeningly in response. Fireworks shot toward the ceiling from each of the four turnbuckles. Slugger leaned against each rope, and each time, pyrotechnics went off. Slugger hopped down from the final rope and gestured for a microphone. One was thrown to him, and he caught it deftly. Slugger stood in the middle of the ring, waiting for the cheers from his (near-) hometown fans to die down. Finally, they did, and Slugger lifted the microphone. "Damn... does it feel good... to be back... in the GREAT NORTHWEST!" The crowd cheered. It wasn't a mention of their city by name, but it was good enough. Slugger stood silently for a moment, seemingly thinking about what he was to say, then lifted the microphone again. "A long, long time ago... I can still remember... when the World's Greatest Athlete was the World Champion!" More cheers. "In fact, I can still remember the day I lost it. My last day as fWo World Heavyweight Champion. It was September 24, 2000. I lost it to Wretch... a fine and deserving champion. Since that day, it's been nearly three years - two years, nine months, and nine days to be exact. Since that day, my life has changed in ways I couldn't possibly describe. I've gone through a lot... I left the fWo, came back, left again, and came back again. I've done things I never thought I was capable of, both good and bad. It wouldn't be a stretch at all to say I've been through Hell and back, and that the Scott Slugger standing in this ring today is not the Scott Slugger who stood in the ring on September 24, 2000. But in some ways... I am the same Scott Slugger. I'm still the World's Greatest Athlete... I'm still a legend... and I'm still the best this company has seen and ever will see!" Wild cheers from the Tacoma crowd. "And oh yeah... I still die a little every day I don't have that World Title belt. Let's face it... when you think of Scott Slugger, when you think of the WGA, what do you think of? World Heavyweight Champion. Hell, back in the day, there was a time where I had been World Champion for over half of the fWo's history! For a long time, I was the longest-reigning World Champion in fWo history. I'm the only five-time World Champion in fWo history. I've main evented CyberSlams for the World Title. Everything I've accomplished, everything I'm known for, it all traces back to the fWo World Title. It defines me. But the thing is, over the past three years, I've been redefined. And that new definition hasn't included the World Title... and that kills me. I've seen twenty World Champions since I lost the title, and that's twenty too many. The WGA is done watching other people carry around my title. Tonight, it comes back home." The crowd erupted at this proclamation and its implications. "Vic Creed... I'm not asking you. I'm telling you. You and me... tonight... for the fWo World Heavyweight Championship." Slugger appeared to be ready to continue speaking, but he was interrupted as "Walk" hit the Tacoma Dome speakers. The crowd exploded with boos as the mammoth fWo World Champion, "Kodiak" Vic Creed, parted the curtain and appeared in front of them. The title belt was strapped around his massive waist, and its gold gleamed and shone against the bright lights. Creed had a microphone in hand. "So tell me, Slugger... who do you think you are? You think you can just order around Vic Creed?" "As a matter of fact, yes." "As a matter of fact, NO! Now, listen closely... I might've given you a shot at me tonight, if you'd asked nicely, but since you were a punk-ass bitch about it, here's how it's gonna be. You're gonna have to earn a shot at me... and if you earn it, then you get a shot. Monday." The crowd booed at this turn of events. "What if I say no?" "Then you don't get any shot. Take it or leave it - it's not like you have a choice, and I'm happy either way. You say no, I don't have to deal with you. You say yes and win, I get to kick your worthless ass once and for all." Slugger thought about it for a moment. "Creed... you've got a deal." The crowd cheered. "Now, as I understand it, your stooge left you a while back... so I gotta ask, how do I prove it? I assume it's to be a match... but against who?" "You'll find out, son, when the match comes around. Until then, you can just sit around and think about it. You got it?" "Yeah. I got it. You get whoever you want, you get a whole army, you get the by-God Oakland Athletics, it won't matter - because I'm coming for my title, Creed. And when I get to you... It won't be your pleasure." Slugger threw the microphone aside, and "Back In Black" played again. Slugger and Creed stared each other down, and Uprising went to commercial with a huge main event looming... Temerario vs. Dog Temerario came to the ring slowly, fist in the air and looking around at the crowd through the slits in his mask. On the FanTron behind him scenes from his match with James Renshaw played as 'Man Without Fear' raged over the arena speakers. Temerario slid into the ring, nodding at one half of the Animals, Dog, who was already in the ring and testing the ropes. Temerario leapt up onto a corner turnbuckle, pumping his fist in the air and wincing slightly as he watched the fans around him. The majority of them looked nonplussed, interested in seeing the higher-up members of the roster make their appearance and not particularly interested in the masked daredevil from Mexico. Temerario sighed. He had been killing himself in this industry... and for what? To be yawned at in Tacoma? Sighing deeper, he leapt off the ropes as the bell rang, and surveyed his opponent. He had been warned to work a more mat style and avoid risky dangerous spots. Frowning, he circled Dog, ignoring Kat and Rat on the outside. The match started slowly, with Temerario testing Dog with several quick grabs. Jumping forward, hit a quick chop to the neck and then unleashed a flurry of spinning heel kicks to keep Dog off his feet and confused. As Dog rose again, Temerario hit a quick bodyslam that drove the bigger man to the mat. Temerario continued on with a series of sliding leg drops, keeping Dog off of his feet. Grumbling, Dog dodged one of the sliding leg drops and attacked with a quick uppercut. Dog then, moving slowly but with force, trapped Temerario in a modifed backbreaker submission hold. The crowd began to boo mildly, and chants of 'boring' began to pop up throughout the arena. Dog, equally annoyed, taunted the crowd by yawning, and continued to batter Temerario into the mat. Dog grabbed Temerario by the mask and slammed him face-first into the mat. Stepping back, Dog flexed for the crowd, who responded with more chants of 'you suck' and 'boring'. Giving up on them, he turned back to the masked Temerario. Dog put Temerario into a standing camel clutch, but Temerario was getting his second wind and began to rally. Trying to stay on the attack, Dog slammed Temerario to the mat with a gut wrench suplex and applied a legscissor hold. Temerario quickly reversed into a ankle-lock submission hold, and, giving up on the matwork, went to his quick aerial attack. Temerario climbed to the top rope and hit a quick jumping shoulder block, then working quickly slammed Dog to the mat with a double-arm suplex. Then Temerario got onto his feet and landed a spinning heel kick, punting Dog into the corner. Running forward, Temerario flipped, hooked his legs around Dog's head, and flipped him with a standing huricarana. Pumping a fist into the air, Temerario climbed to the top rope and, with arms extended, leapt from the top rope with a swan dive spinning corkscrew plancha that connected on target. Temerario hooked the leg, and the referee dove for the count. 1... 2... 3. The bell rang, and for the second match in a row Temerario was once again victorious.
