Sunday Slaughter, 24 September 2000

24 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Doubts? The Key Arena. Seattle, Washington. Tonight, the home state of the fans Wrestling organization headquarters would play host to one of the biggest shows in it's near three year history. Coming off a heated pay-per-view just twenty four hours ago in Sacramento, California - the world waited with anticipation running through their veins as tonight the winner of Meltdown just one month ago would receive his fWo World Title Match against five time fWo World Champion and certain fWo Hall Of Famer, Scott Slugger. The World's Greatest Athlete. The World Title would be up for grabs as Slugger would defend his title against up and coming star, Chris Kidman, Jr. Kidman Jr.' goal in wrestling is to entertain the fans first, and win titles second. He's been entertaining the fans in some form or another since he debuted, and tonight would be his chance to compete for the top prize in sports entertainment. Coming in, he is the fWo Internet Champion, and unquestionably has a bright future ahead of him, if not the brightest, than any other athlete on the roster within the same age frame as he... Wretch. The Ratings Saver. Heading in, Wretch pinned Slugger last night in the main event of the night, and certainly would be the fWo World Champion if the title had been on the line. Fortunately, for Scott Slugger, it wasn't. But tonight, Wretch would get his chance. With the setting in place, and the competitors known, Slaughter took to the air with a shot of the fWo World Champion himself, arriving at the arena. Outside the arena, a legion of fans stood chanting one of his many nicknames - "WGA" - repeatedly. As he arrived inside the Key Arena, Kristen Jacobs approached him with a camera crew at her side, and a microphone in hand. "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to fWo Sunday Night Slaughter. I'm here with five time fWo World Champion Scott Slugger, and Scott, I must ask - last night, Wretch pinned you cleanly. Are you worried that he may do the same tonight, especially when it appears you have had some doubts the last twenty four hours?" Slugger paused, before removing his expensive sunglasses, and putting them in his jacket pocket. "Doubts?" Jacobs nodded. "Kristen, you haven't been around long enough to know this, but it's common knowledge. The WGA never doubts himself - he doesn't doubt tonight, injured arm or not, concussion or not, that he will walk out of here with the fWo World Title around his waist, on his shoulder, in his hands, wherever the World Title is, the WGA will be right beside it. Because it is his, it always has been his, and it always will be his. And until the next time you interview me Kristen... just as it is now, it'll be your pleasure." Slugger flashed his trademark smirk, before walking further inside the Key Arena. Now The fans inside the arena waited with baited breath as Matt Panzer counted the fans down to the introduction of fWo Sunday Night Slaughter; dated September 24, 2000. Multiple fans held up their signs in show of support of their favorite superstar(s) or angle(s), hoping that it would be caught somehow on television tonight. They looked to the stage where fireworks shot off, and the fans began to cheer as a result. Music echoed of the walls of the Key Arena, formerly known as the Seattle Coliseum before they renovated it a few years ago. The music died out, as Slaughter was offically live. Jeremiah Frankton & Stevie Roberts welcomed everyone at home to the show, while on the stage, "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson appeared, while his music now played. Wilson was greeted with boos and jeers from the crowd, but ignored it all with a smirk across his goatee clad face. Wilson rolled into the ring, before walking over towards the general direction of the announcing position, and asking for a microphone. He was given one, and immediately signalled for his music to stop playing. "First of all... what happened last night was an absolute travesty. I mean, the referees have the nerve to allow the Frenchie & Primetime 'survive' until the final match? What kind of crap is that? The rules stated that whomever the final survivor or survivors were on ONE of the teams would reach the final match. Not the final survivor or survivors from both teams. What would be the point of that? I kicked both their asses... twice in one night, I might add, and they still reach the final match? The referees here sicken me, and that's why I think that James Armstrong should look into hiring some new officials. Officials who will call things right down the middle and favor Ice & I whenever possible. But enough about how the fWo referees suck... I'm out here for one reason, and one reason only. I can't believe I'm about to say it - but Primetime, I want that Women's Title of yours, and you know why. So go ahead, save us all the trouble, come out here, let me carry you to a five star match just like I did from March until July, and just like I will tonight... I'll pin you, you can go back to your little fling with Pierre, and everyone will be happy." Wilson tossed the microphone aside, and a referee headed down to the ring, before "Snap" began to play. Primetime walked out to a bigger ovation of boos than what Wilson received, but rather than ignore them, he played to the crowd, and taunted them by showing the fWo Women's Title to them, which they didn't like. He then paid no more attention to the crowd, and stormed the ring, where the referee signalled for the bell... Primetime (c) vs. "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson Primetime charged at Wilson with the belt still in his hands, and swung. Wilson ducked, and as Primetime turned back to face him, he was met with a glancing blow to the facial area. Three consecutive punches later, and Primetime relinquished the title, as Wilson backed him against the ropes. He unleased several reverse knife edge chops, before sending Primetime to the ropes. The brash Buffaloian returned and was taken down with a hip toss. PT came to and rushed a standing Wilson, who then took him down with a Japanese Armdrag, before locking on an armbar, and working over the arm area. Primetime came to his feet, and rolled through on the mat, before getting back up and reversing the armbar into an armbar of his own. Wilson reached back in attempt to escape, but Primetime locked in the hold, applying more pressure. Primetime then shifted gears and went into a hammerlock. Mitch tried to escape by bending over, reaching back for one of the legs, but Primetime applied more tourqe to the hold. Wilson however escaped with a go-behind into a waistlock, and then lifted into a German Suplex attempt, but Primetime escaped into a roll up for a pin. The referee counted to two, but Wilson escaped. Both men rushed back up to a vertical base, before PT backed Wilson into a corner, and measured him by driving his shoulder into the rib area repeatedly. Primetime then brought him out from the corner and tried a submission move to the arm he had previously worked over. PT then got him grouned on the mat and winded up and drove his knee into the arm, before bringing Wilson up, and sending him to the ropes. Wilson returned and Primetime grabbed him by the injured arm and sent him over the top rope onto the floor. PT joined him on the outside, and procured himself a chair. He waited for Wilson to get up before taking the chair and smacking it across that injured arm a few times, in particular the shoulder area. Primetime then hooked him seemingly for a 'Headliner' on the cement floor, but Mitch countered out of it with an elbow. Unfortunately, he used the injured arm, as that was the only way he could escape. He held his elbow now, and Primetime grabbed him by the hair and sent him into the ring. He then brought Wilson up and hit a pumphandle slam, before going for the cover. Wilson kicked out at two, and PT continued to work over the arm, this time with a Fujiwara Armbar. Wilson writhed in pain, and PT applied yet more pressure to the hold. The referee asked Wilson if he wanted to call it a night, but Wilson responded with an emphatic 'no'. Incensed, Primetime released the hold, went to the floor, and brought in the previously used chair. He slid it into the ring, and took his time entering. The referee now had the chair, and prepared to put it outside, but Primetime disagreed. He wanted to use it. He walked over and turned the referee around, before grabbing the chair from him. The referee said something he shouldn't have, and PT threatened him with it. PT then turned to whack Wilson with it as he got to his feet, but the referee grabbed it from behind. Primetime was now enraged. The official went to put the chair on the outside, but PT stopped him again. However, from behind came Wilson with a shot to the little PT's. PT bent over holding his family jewels, and this gave Wilson an opportunity to take control. Wilson grabbed PT and pulled him in for a pedigree. Primetime however escaped by knocking his legs out as he was bent over. He grabbed the legs, and prepared to send him flying into the air with a teeter totter type move. Instead, he pointed to his head, and turned it over a Boston Crab. He cinched it in, and continued to work Wilson over with submission moves. The referee again asked Wilson if he wanted to give it up, but Wilson said 'hell no', and instead crawled inch by inch over to the ropes, and grabbing them. PT had no choice but to release the hold, and it wasn't fast enough to the referee's liking. He threatened to DQ Primetime, but Primetime laughed it off. Wilson rolled out to the floor to catch his bearings, and shake off the cobwebs. Primetime saw it differently, and instead charged and leaped over the top rope with a somersault plancha. Mitch couldn't move in time, and PT landed on him, and now both men found themselves on the floor. Primetime got up and played to the crowd for a moment, before grabbing Wilson and rolling him into the ring. Wilson slowly got up inside as PT took to the top rope, flying off in a sunset flip attempt. PT didn't have enough momentum, and Wilson reached down, brought PT up to his feet, and then caught him off guard with a knee to the solarplexis, followed by an inverted swinging neckbreaker. Wilson then caught his breath, and worked PT over with jumping kneedrops to the chest area. Wilson then brought Primetime up and sent him into the turnbuckles, where now he took the opportunity to drive repeated shoulder after shoulder into the ribs of Primetime. Wilson then did it again, this time charging and driving the shoulder in. This time it appeared he hurt the arm however, and tended to it while the referee checked over Primetime. Wilson walked back into the corner and Primetime caught him with a kick to the midsection, before lifting him up and hitting a Michinoku Driver. Primetime couldn't follow through on the eventual pinfal, instead falling back to the mat to recover. The referee began a ten count, and at six, both men began to show signs of life. Primetime charged at Mitch but Mitch took him down with a drop toe hold, before bringing Primetime up, and hitting a Mitchinoku Driver II. Wilson then went to the top rope and flew off with a frog splash. He hit it, further injuring the ribs, but at the same time, it appeared as he hurt his arm as well. Wilson had enough left in the tank to drape his arm over PT for a pin, but it received only a two count. Wilson was undaunted, laying on his back, wondering what to do next. He brought Primetime up and hit a Superstar Suplex, and had just enough in him to turn it into a bridge, but that garnered only a two count as well. Frustrated, Wilson brought PT up and went for the Headliner, but Primetime countered the same way Wilson did earlier. An elbow to the face, but his elbow was much stronger, and didn't injure his arm. Wilson went backwards and collided with the referee, and as Primetime turned, Wilson staggered towards him. Primetime grabbed him and hooked him for the slingshot Michinoku Driver, and hit it. The referee was out, however, and there was no referee to count. Suddenly, a referee from the back emerged. He charged to the ring, but as he reached ringside someone else emerged from the crowd and whacked him with a chair in one motion. The Flying Frenchie. With the second official out, Frenchie had his chance. Primetime stood to his feet and dared Frenchie to come into the ring. Frenchie did, and Primetime charged with a swinging punch. Frenchie ducked, turn around, and whacked him with the steel chair, just as he did the referee. Frenchie then waited for a dazed Wilson to get up, before taking him out as well. Frenchie then nudged the referee several times, before exiting, and watching the action unfold. Both men were out, until Wilson crawled over and draped his arm on Primetime. One. Two. Three. Primetime was defeated, and we had a new fWo Women's Champion for the second time in seven days. Frenchie retrieved the Women's Title, before climbing back into the ring and dropping it on Wilson, and welcoming him in advance. Frenchie then stood over Primetime and spoke to him some unpleasantries. However, trouble was on it's way in the form of Courage & Lance Star, Primetime' cohorts in the Future Foundation. They charged the ring and began to attack Frenchie, before helping Primetime up, and all three unloaded on him. Meanwhile, Ice came down to the ring and helped the victorious Wilson to the back. As The Future Foundation pounced on Frenchie continuously, help came in the form of The Underground. Mike Bear & Damnation. They cleared the ring, as the Foundation exited the ring to a chorus of boos. Frenchie was helped to his feet, and thanked the Underground for their noble cause, as the Foundation disappeared into the back. Frenchie was about to exit the ring as well, when Mike Bear stopped him... Result: Wilson by pinfall Offer Mike Bear now had a microphone, and welcomed Frenchie back to the center of the ring. "Pierre, I know we've had our differences. But it seems like just lately, we've meshed really well together. The fans are behind you as much as they are behind us." Bear turned to the crowd who supported this statement with cheers. "They want to see you kick Primetime's ass in a singles match just as much as we do. And we both know that you're not going to get that opportunity as long as you've got guys like Mitch Wilson interferring in your business, and the Kings to deal with as well. We've been thinking about it, and we think it would benefit both of us if you..." Bear turned to Damnation, who nodded in approval. "Joined The Underground." Frenchie looked at Bear in disbelief, before putting his head down, and contemplating it. Bear enticed him by offering him all the amenities the rest of the Underground had... like secks with immoral women and alcoholic beverages twenty four hours a day. Frenchie passed on that, but did like the opportunity of having one of his favorites - Lamont, the Hardcore Feline - in his corner. Frenchie turned to the crowd for guidance on his decision. They cheered, before chanting "Join The Underground!" repeatedly. Frenchie smiled, and almost beamed, thinking back to when this same crowd didn't like him some seven months ago. Frenchie nodded, and shook the hands of Bear & Damnation, resulting in cheers from the crowd. Bear and Frenchie raised each other's arms, before preparing to leave, but Frenchie stopped Bear, and asked for the microphone. "Hold on just a moment, Mike. I 'ave a big tag team match next week on Sunday Night Slaughter... for the only title zat has eluded me in my entire career 'ere in ze fWo. Isn't it ironic zat I will likely 'ave to face Fear and Razor 'ere?" Bear nodded, before Frenchie continued. "Let's make it more ironic, zen. Why don't you join ze Flying Frenchie... and be his tag team partner." The fans cheered quite loudly, and Bear looked at Damnation, who shrugged, as if to say 'what the hell, go for it'. Bear stook out his hand, and nodded. An eruption from the crowd occured as a result, and Frenchie and Bear raised each other's arms in the air, as fireworks shot off throughout the Key Arena. Slaughter however went to a much needed break, some thirty five minutes into the telecast... Erik Kelly, Juno Law & Wolf Fang vs. Vengence & The Thrill Killers Result: Vengence & The Thrill Killers by pinfall Grand Slammed Backstage, Slaughter continued on as James Armstrong sat in his office, eating a piece of bun cake. He'd wondered what everyone thought about last night's pay-per-view, and thinking of last night reminded him of the alleged 'photos'. Armstrong didn't know much about the photos, all he knew is that they were fake. Didn't exist. Certainly, the alleged situation did happen, but as far as Armstrong was concerned - the photos didn't exist, and thus, it was just an unfound allegation. Armstrong sighed, before scarfing down some bun cake. Suddenly, his office door swung open, and Armstrong nearly coughed up the piece of cake into his lap. Mitch Wilson was now in his room, and didn't look happy. "Uh... Mitch... could you mind knocking before you come in next time?" Wilson smirked. "What, do you have a guy underneath that table pleasuring you this week instead of a female?" Armstrong shook his head negatively. "No, no... I'm not like that!" Wilson smirked, and Armstrong just asked him to get to why he was there. Wilson explained that when he lost to Wretch on August 27th, he lost the fWo Internet Title, and never received a rematch. Continuing, he asked Armstrong what he planned to do if Wretch defeated Slugger tonight for the fWo World Title. Armstrong laughed, spitting bun cake all over his table. "Kidman? Beat my boy Slugger? Uh huh... and the photos of me and Finn with hookers in Vegas really doexist! Please, Mitch, get serious. Wretch couldn't defeat Scott if his life depended on it..." Armstrong went back to eating his bun cake, before noticing the silence. "Okay, okay... if Wretch won tonight, I'd probably vacate the Internet Title. Why do you ask?" Wilson smiled, as that's what he wanted. "But, I'm still owed a rematch - against Wretch." Armstrong nodded. "So you'll get your rematch next week on Slaughter." Wilson shook his head. "No, because if Wretch is the World Champion.. he won't have the Internet Title. And I'm still owed a rematch - against Wretch. So that means, I face Wretch - if he wins - next week for the fWo World Title." Armstrong shook his head in a manner Wilson did not expect. "No, no... how do you figure? Look, what does it matter anyways. He's not going to win." Wilson persisted. "James, I'm owed a shot at Wretch - if he's not the Internet Champion, than that means I face him for the World Title." Finally, Armstrong gave in. "Okay, Mitch... if you lay down for me next week for that chick belt, you've got it." Wilson smiled. "No problem. I lay down for you for the chick belt next week - and Wretch wins - I face him next week for the World Title?" Armstrong nodded. "Well, you know what that means, then." No, Armstrong didn't, as he was oblivious to the obvious 90% of the time. "Uh, no, what does it mean?" Wilson grinned deviously. "It means I now have the same opportunity to become the first Grand Slam Champion that Primetime & Frenchie do." Armstrong was in shock now, but then remembered.... "Uh huh... yep, and the Mariners are going to win the World Championship of baseball this year... just as Wretch will win tonight. Please." Armstrong went back to his bun cake, while Wilson went to his locker room... The Abusah vs. Scot Hartt Result: Hartt by submission Complaints Slaughter headed backstage where Armstrong continued to eat his bun cake, and from the looks, it was his second plate of the night. "Gotta love bun cake... gotta love bun cake..." Slaughter went a shot of Ruben Ross who was in a nearby dressing room lacing up his boots. "What a nut." Suddenly (as moments before) Armstrong' office door swung open, and in came the furious Juno Law. "This is crap!" Armstrong hadn't yet seen him as instead he was staring at his plate. "No, this is some good stuff... wanna try so..." as Armstrong looked up and saw the young rookie in front of him, but didn't quite remember his name. "Juno, right?" Law nodded affirmatively. "Can I help you, Juno?" Law pushed over a nearby chair. "Yeah, you can help me by booking me against Rave next week on Slaughter!" Armstrong sighed. "What's with you and all this demanding stuff? And who's Rav... oh, Erik Rave. Right. Well uh..." Just then, another visitor entered the room in the form of the fWo Survivor Winner, "Black Plague" Ruben Ross. "Are you whining again, Juno? Is that all you do is whine? Here a whine, there a whine, everywhere a whine whine?" The two men came close to blows when Armstrong halted them. "Is there some tension in this room?" asked Armstrong - who again, was 90% oblivious to the obvious. Ross & Law shook their heads, before Armstrong reached a conclusion. "Alright, then you guys can settle this matter next week in the ring on Slaughter - understood?" The two men shooke their heads, before Law informed Ross that next week he would be "just fierce". Ross retorted by telling Law that he would be "just complaining again after losing to the Black Plague in seven days." Slaughter went back to the ring for another highly touted bout... Crusader vs. "The Ego" Greg Davis A hush falls over the fWo hometown crowd, as footage from Unstable last night starts playing, highlighting in particular Crusader Result: Davis by pinfall Two Birds With One Stone Slaughter continued, again in the office of James Armstrong. This time he was on the phone, with nobody knowing who was on the other end of the conversation. To be certain, however, it was not Pierre Delacroix - also known as The Flying Frenchie, as he just entered the room, and took a seat. Armstrong welcomed Frenchie in with a tilt of his head, before asking the person on the other line if he may call them back. They give him the good word, and he bids them farewell, before saying hello to his good friend, and asking him what he wanted. Frenchie stated that he wanted to kill 'two birds wit' one stone', and Armstrong puzzedly asked him what he meant. He elaborated on that, telling Armstrong that one way or another, he wanted a match with both Mitch Wilson & Primetime at the next big fWo pay-per-view spectacular - emanating live from Boston, Massachusetts on October 28 - Halloween Hell. Armstrong said that his request would be met, and that the original French Foundation would do battle in a three-way dance at Halloween Hell... Slaughter went back to the ring, with apparently one match signed already for the next pay-per-view. Damnation vs. The STUMP Squad The Seattle area fans are pumped for the upcoming contest, a tag title match between STUMP Squad and Damnation. Result: Damnation by pinfall Bragging Slaughter went backstage for the final time of the night, where the aforementioned victorious Greg Davis stood in an interview area, with Clarisse at his side. The Ego winked at Kristen Jacobs, who continued her increased schedule of interviewing the athletes of the fans Wrestling organization. "I'm standing alongside Greg Davis, who apparently has secured the valet duties from Clarisse for the remainder of the year. Greg, how do you feel about this?" Davis smiled goofily, before answering the question. "How do I feel about it? Ask Clarisse how she feels about it. She couldn't wait to get away from Cru. She couldn't away to get from that idiot. She wanted to be with a winner - and for those of you keeping score at home - The Ego is undefeated in his meetings with Crusader, be it here in the fWo, or in Tornado Wrestling, or on the planet Pluto. Yes, if we lived there, I'd beat him there too, because I am The Ego..." before Davis could finish, Jacobs interrupted him. "Wait a minute... you know that had Clarisse' interference on behalf of Crusader didn't backfire, you would be a defeated man tonight... you know that, Greg." Davis smiled again. "Yeah, whatever redhead. You know that Clarisse interfered on MY behalf - because she couldn't take anymore of that loser... Cru-loser! That's what his name is. Cruloser. Isn't that right, Clarisse?" Davis turned to Clarisse, who smacked away his arm, before exiting the area. Davis smiled. "She obviously can't wait to get back to the hotel. And as for you... if you want a piece of the Ego, you know where to find me. Here's the key to my room, but if there's a 'do not disturb' sign on the door, do not enter, if you catch my drift. Later, Chrissy!" Davis stormed out of the area in a huff, while Jacobs sighed, and Slaughter went to it's final commercial break of the evening, with the big World Title Match mere minutes away. Scott Slugger (c) vs. Wretch Slaughter headed to the ring for it's main event of the evening, the gigantic fWo World Title Match between the fWo Internet Champion Wretch, and the fWo World Champion Scott Slugger. Slugger had been on a roll as of late - defeating Primetime, Austin Davis & The Boston in successful fashion during the past month. Wretch had been active as well, defeating Ice and settling some tension between them that had existed for more than a year. That was the past however, and right now, one of these two men would lead the fans Wrestling organization into the future. Big Wretchy, The Ratings Saver. Scott Slugger - The World's Greatest Athlete. The time for talking was over. The time to 'get it on' was now. "Paint It Black" hit the speakers inside the Key Arena as Slaughter returned from it's commmercial break, and the crowd exploded in an ovation of cheers. The fWo Internet Champion walked out onto the stage, and for the first time in recent memory, used his title as an air guitar. The fans roared with approval, and laughed at the same time. Big Wretchy walked down to the ring talking about being the man who created wrestling, the man who put asses in the seats, and the reason everyone was there tonight. Wretchy entered the ring and held the fWo Internet Title above the air for everyone to see, before giving it to the referee, and doing some poses in the ring. During the middle of a pose, he was interrupted. "Back In Black" replaced "Paint It Black", and out walked the WGA, Scott Slugger. Carrying the fWo Internet Title over his right shoulder, he was greeted with a standing ovation, as he was wearing a Seattle Mariners shirt, proud that they happened to be winning in a game with division rivals the Oakland A's. Slugger entered the ring and as Wretch stood in a corner, the two exchanged pleasantries, and Wretch noticed that Slugger was still nursing that injured arm. Slugger posed as fireworks shot off in the ring, as the referee showed Wretch what was on the line. The fWo World Title. As if anyone needed to know. Wretch held it for a moment, before giving it back to the referee, who handed it to an outside official. The fireworks stopped, the music stopped, time itself, stopped. Wretch did some warm up exercises in the corner, and Slugger removed his Mariners shirt, tossing it into the crowd. The referee signalled for the bell, and Wretch & Slugger met in the center of the ring. Face to face. Eye to eye. Toe to toe. It was time to get it on. Wretch swung first, but Slugger blocked it, and unloaded with three consecutive punches. Slugger backed Wretch into a corner, Irish Whipped him into the other side, and as Wretch came out Slugger drove a knee into his stomach, sending Wretch to the mat. Wretch quickly got back up, and Slugger took himself and Wretch out of the ring with a clothesline. Both men were now on the floor, but it appears Slugger was just as hurt. Running on pure adrenaline, he had forgotten about the injured arm. He placed it on the mat and looked it over, as a part of it had turned somewhat blue, with red meshed throughout. This gave Wretch enough time to get to his feet, and Slugger charged in his direction. Wretch - thinking quickly - took him down with a drop toe hold. The injured shoulder of Slugger was rammed into the steps, and now Wretch apparently had the advantage. Wretch grabbed Slugger and whipped him into the steel steps on the other side, again shoulder first. Wretch posed for the crowd, before grabbing Slugger and sending him into the ring, before going up to the top rope. Slugger slowly climbed to his feet, and Wretch flew off with a high cross body, but his momentum was so strong that it sent Slugger ontop of him. One. Two. Easy kickout for Wretch, who then went back to work on the injured arm. He brought him up and hooked in an armbar, and twisted it several times for good measure. Sensing the fans wanted to see more action than pause, and Wretch was always known to want to please the fans, turned things up a notch and whipped Slugger to the corner. Slugger crashed in hard, and Wretch went in and executed a monkey flip. Somehow, the WGA landed on his feet, and as Slugger charged Wretch quickly combatted him with a superkick. Wretch went for another cover, but Slugger kicked out. Wretch brought Slugger up and took him down with a snapmare, and as a somewhat dazed Slugger sat on the mat, Wretch hit a sitting dropkick. A cover by Wretch, and Slugger kicked out just before the two count. Wretch brought Slugger up and sent him to the ropes, and ducked for a backdrop. Slugger returned and had just enough to hit a swinging neckbreaker, but didn't have anything left in the tank to do much more. Wretch & Slugger were both dazed on the mat, and the referee began a ten count. At six, Wretch got to his feet, and staggered back to the ropes, and as Slugger got up on the other side of the ring with help from the ropes, Wretch walked up from behind and dumped him over the top rope onto the floor, where Slugger crashed in a bad way. Wretch then climbed to the top rope, and waited patiently for Slugger to get up, before turning and facing the other way. He flew off with a moonsault, and barely caught Slugger. The fans started up an fWo chant, and now both men were out of it on the outside. The referee began a ten count, when suddenly, two figures emerged on the stage. Mitch Wilson & Ice. Ice climbed up onto the ring apron, and the referee saw him. Stopping his ten count, he went over and told Ice to leave the area, without noticing Wilson. Wilson waited for a groggy Slugger to get up, and Slugger did. Wilson charged and hit him with his newly won Women's Title, sending Slugger sprawling onto the Spanish Announce Table. There, Wilson took him up on the table, and all hell broke loose. The Headliner. Both men went crashing through the table, and Wilson looked to be somewhat injured as a result. Since he quickly had to exit the area, however, he ignored the pain, and brought Wretch over near Slugger, laying him near him, so it would look like Wretch did the deed, and not Wilson himself. Wilson then joined Ice, and informed the referee of what had occured over near the Spanish Announce Table. The referee went to the outside, and as Wilson clutched his ribs, he high fived Ice, before they slowly walked backwards up the ramp. The referee began a ten count, and at six, Wretch finally got up, and pulled Slugger into the ring. Nearly a minute and a half had passed since Wilson planted him through the table, but Wretch was determined to make the cover. He did, but didn't hook the leg. One. Two. Two and a half. Two and three quarters. Thre - no. Somehow, someway, Slugger got his foot on the bottom rope. The crowd cheered, although Wretch had his fair share of supporters, who started up a 'Wretchy' chant. Wretch acknowledged the fans, before in a dazed, he went to the top rope, and prepared to fly again. This time, he flew off with a senton bomb. But Slugger dug deep down inside, and moved out of the way. Wretch hit the mat, and the fans started up a 'WGA' chant. Slugger fed off the crowd's energy, and got to his feet. He went upstairs, and flew off with a Grand Slam. Cover, with a hook of the leg. One. Two. Kickout. The fans were torn between the two popular superstars, and now Slugger had to make a big play if the title were to stay in his grasp.
