Wednesday Warfare, 26 July 2000

26 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Arrival of Team Ratings Wednesday Warfare splashed onto your television screen with the image of Wretch and his two lackey's making their way inside. If you recall, last time this happened his two lackey's were kidnapped. Not this time. Nuh uh. Wretch had a plan. A plan so devious and so intelligent it's a wonder that any human could conceive of it. He had the lackey's walk in front of him so he could keep his eyes on them at all times. Brilliant. Wretch whistled softly to himself as Abusah and Scot Hartt playfully jabbed at each other, before Abusah "swoorved" Hartt and gave him a hard jab. Hartt took offense, but Abusah told him not to hit him because tonight was "KNOW HITTIN ABOOSAH NITE". Hartt reluctantly withheld the jab he was about to make, as the trio entered their nearby lockerroom. As they did, they realized that list Wretch compiled a few weeks ago had finally been secured. Their demands had been met. Wretch removed a copy of the list he had made before he gave it to Finn, and checked things off. "#1 - A bicycle helmet full of four hundred and twenty peanut buttered M&M's, but only of the green, brown and red colors. Check. #2 - Four hundred and twenty mouse pads. Check. #3 - a Britney Spears poster spraypainted with the words "I LUV ABOOSAH". Check." Wretch stopped reading the list momentarily, and looked over at Abusah, who now appeared to be fornicating with the aforementioned poster. A censor covered his portion of the screen. "Let's see here... #4 - A poster of David Lee Roth autographed personally for Scot Hartt that reads "Hott For Teacher". Check." Wretch stopped reading the list momentarily, and looked over at Hartt, who now appeared to be fornicating with the aforementioned poster. A censor covered his portion of the screen... and Wretch was barely visable. Wretch continued anyways. After reading items 5-37, he stopped and looked around the list. "No... Moolah." Wretch shrugged. He then read items 37-68, before getting to item 69, which ironically happened to be the last item on the list. "#69 - The Fabulous Moolah. Live... and in person. I... don't see her... where is she? Dammit, I wanted the Fabulous Moolah to be here!" Hartt & Abusah didn't care. They were too busy doing... well, whatever it is they were doing. Wretch sighed, and told them to stop. They were afterall making the ratings drop... and that wasn't a good thing. They were here to save the ratings, not kill them. That was the Future Foundation' job afterall. Wretch threw his hands up in the air, and starting shouting. "This is great. Just friggin' great. Where is she? I may not be on the card tonight, but you guys are. Let's go tell Finn that you guys aren't wrestling until Fabulous Moolah is HERE... and I don't care if Warfare is delayed twenty four hours. I really don't! Finn won't put on the show without his three biggest..." Wretch slyly looked at Hartt. "Two biggest rating draws..." Hartt shook a fist in his direction, before Abusah got mad. He speared his statue of gobstoppers that formed a... well, we don't know what it was, but now the gobstoppers were all over the place. Hartt grabbed his personally autographed by Pee Wee Herman jar of Vaseline and threw it against the wall. The trio stormed out of the room. Finn was a dead man. Lance Star vs. Hellrazor Cameras headed to the ring, where Future Foundation member and one half of the California Kings, Lance Star was stretching against the ring ropes in preparation for the first match. Fireworks explode, and resound off the Reunion Arena' walls, which get the crowd in a frenzy. After the Warfare entrance on the Warfare screen ends, the image of Lance Star in the ring prompts the crowd to react differently. The crowd Result: Hellrazor by pinfall Ridiculous Demand "NOCK NOCK!" screamed Abusah. "Who's there?" An unknown voice responded. The trio laughed quietly amongst themselves as they stood outside the office of the owner of the fWo. Finn opened the door and much to his dismay three men he knew he needed in his company to stay afloat stood in front of him. Wretch. Scot Hartt. And The Almighty Mutha Fuckin' Abusah Of Yo Momma. Team Ratings. Rateings 'R Us. Big Wretchy. The nicknames are endless, as are the possibilities of the ratings which have been heading upward since Wretch proclaimed he and his cronies the saviors of the ratings. "What do you guys want?" Wretch smiled, before producing the list of demands from his pocket. "This... item number sixty nine." Finn groggily wiped his eyes, as it appeared he hadn't slept in several days, or even... weeks. Finn looked at the item and sighed. "Impossible. We got every other item on your list except this one. Her asking price was too much." Wretch grinned. "Well, see, that's where you're going to have to pay the price no matter what. Tell me what she wanted..." Finn shrugged. "I don't recall. It hit the six figure mark I believe." Wretch grinned widely. "You see... a mere one hundred grand will pale into comparison to us leaving the shows. See, I'm pulling a power play here. I wanted Fabulous Moolah... and by god, we're going to get her. My boys won't wrestle until she's here... and if we don't wrestle, if we refuse to work, you ratings drop. And when your ratings drop, so do your sponsors. And then your buyrates... eventually merchandise... your top talent begin to get disgruntled... this happens many times, Chris. Look at wcw. They're going to lose eighty million dollars this year alone. Now, the fWo is worth more than a mere eighty million I'm sure... worth a lot more than that southern indy fed undoubtedly. So, you think this over. We'll begin tonight... Abusah and Hartt won't wrestle in their handicap match with that... thunder... lightning... weather messer upper guy. And Saturday... I won't wrestle in your little iron... man.. Black Sabbath... gone in sixty seconds whatever match. I won't... you can count on that. And when the fans become aware of this... they'll back out of ordering the pay-per-view. They will cancel. And that won't look good for your precious little fWo now will it? So I suggest you get Moolah here... or else your company will go back to the direction it was headed in before Big Wretchy came along... and that is down." Finn pondered this mess, before trying to reach a compromise. "Look, it'd be absurd for you to demand that Moolah gets here now. But, let's reach a compromise. I can promise you she'll be at the pay-per-view... or at Slaughter. Deal?" Wretch and his boys huddled up. They eventually hit heads from being too close to one another. Wretch grabbed his as ultimately he was the only one out of the three with any ounce of intelligence within him. Abusah and Hartt continued on like it was nothing... and it was. Finally, the three men reached an agreement. "Hey Finn... you've got a deal. But if she's not at one of those two shows, we're walking. For good." Finn gulped, before shaking Wretch' hand. Wretch asked when his lackey and his lackey's match was scheduled. "Right now..." Wretch sent his boys out of the room, before reminding Finn that Wretch equals ratings... Rain vs. Rateings R' Us "Connection" by Elastica was heard throughout the arena, which brought out the tandem of Scot Hartt, a noted PhD., and The Abusah of Yo Motha Fuckin Result: Rain by pinfall How'd He Do That? Warfare returned from commercials to find Wretch smiling as he stood backstage, while Wretch, Hartt & Abusah began to head from the stage into the unknown. Obviously, it was a videotape of Wretch holding an eight by ten glossy of the Fabulous Moolah that distracted Rain. "Well, well, well. Looks like Mr. God of Thunder and Lightning just got himself fooled. Well, I bet your God's are saying to themselves right now... "Why did we choose this idiot?" That question is a mindboggler unto itself, but you should be asking yourself this, Rain. This Saturday at SummerSlaughter when Big Wretchy hits the Depression on you... and assuredly, he'll make you say hello to wood at some point in the match.... you've got to ask yourself... "Why am I so damn boring?" And that question will be just as mindboggling as the aforementioned question of the God's choosing an idiot like yourself. And you're an idiot because... you're still watching this video... while I'm in the back snacking on gobstoppers and jellybeans, baby!" Rain stood up and gave the Warfare screen an angry glare while tending to his injury 'caused by the wood, before Warfare headed to commercials once again... Courage vs. Fear Warfare returned from a short commercial break once again to find Result: Fear by disqualification Underestimated. Warfare went backstage to find Mike Bear sitting on a chair in his lockerroom lacing up his boots. The image went from color to black and white, as sweat already poured off his face. He paused after lacing one of the boots up to address the camera. "In a few moments, we will see a preview of SummerSlaughter... four men in one match... two from the main event, two from the first ever four-way Iron Man match. Mitch Wilson is a great wrestler. Primetime is a great wrestler. The Boston Strangler is a great wrestler, that's why he's the champion of this promotion today. But I think people are overlooking and underestimating me. And that's what will be their fatal mistake. Don't underestimate me, Primetime. You may have accomplished a lot in the fWo... but on a night like tonight, accomplishments mean squat when you're in there across from the ring from me. Don't underestimate me, Wilson. You may be the best technical wrestler this promotion has ever had on it's roster, but tonight... if you wanna be technical, go ahead. Because tonight I will prove to you and everyone else that I am ready for the next level. Don't underestimate me, Strangler. You may be the most feared man this sport has ever seen, but Mike Bear is fearless. Mike Bear is hungry. And tonight... whether some think I have a chance or not, Mike Bear is a winner. Tonight... four men will enter... one will walk out with their arm raised. On Saturday... it'll be just you and I in there, Paul. And that's when the games will stop... because this isn't a game. This is the World Title. And I'm willing to bleed.. I'm willing to sweat... I'm willing to endure a mountain sized box of punishment, and then some... and in seventy two hours... if you underestimate me... your prized possesion will be nothing more than a memory." Bear proceeded to begin lacing up his other boots as he prepared for the main event... but first... back in the ring... Scott Slugger & Ric Chronos vs. Eron The Relentless & The Black Panther "Physical" by Nine Inch Nails welcomes us back to Warfare, and a chorus of boos as the most hated man in the fWo walks out. Eron the Relentless appears to ignore the crowd as he stands at the top of the ramp, then turns as the near 7 foot masked man called the Black Panther joins him. Both men walk to ringside with a purpose in their step. As they enter the ring, AC/DC's "Back in Black" takes over the airwaves and the crowd gets to its feet as out walks Ric Chronos alongside Scott Slugger. Both men nod to each other, as they walk down to ringside, and high five fans. Chronos appears a little annoyed as he gets to the ring, casting an icy stare at Panther. As the wrestlers get themselves settled, Eron and Slugger decide they will start the match. Both of the competitors in the upcoming Hell in a Cell match taunt each other to open the match. Eron executes an armbar, then slaps Slugger across the face. Slugger gets flustered, and chases Eron to the ropes, where Eron gets in between the ropes and shoos Slugger away, with the help of the ref. Upon locking up again, Slugger gets an armbar this time, and returns the favor to Eron.However, when Eron attempts to closeline, Slugger positions himself for a FLAWLESS DROP TOE HOLD. (DTH, bay-beeee!) Slugger then hits the ropes, Eron drops, Slugger bounces to the other side and returns with the Grand Slam (Flying Lariat). Slugger tries to end it quickly, but barely gets a 1 count. Scott puts Eron into a headlock, and decides to execute a standing bulldog on Eron. Picking up Eron again, Slugger goes for a suplex only to get blocked and lifted in the air by Eron, then dropped into a front face suplex. Grabbing Slugger viciously by the hair, Eron decides Scott needs his face rearranged, and attempts to help by punching it about a half dozen times. He finishes the punches with a spinning heel kick, knocking Slugger into the turnbuckle. Eron goes over, puts Scott up on the top turnbuckle, then gets on top himself and pulls off an impressive Frankensteiner. Eron covers Slugger, but only gets a 2 count. Picking Slugger up, Eron sets him up and winds up the superkick...only to miss by Slugger ducking, turning around, and giving Eron a reverse neckbreaker that takes alot out of both men. Chronos begins to clap to get the audience into the action, as the ref begins to count both men down on the mat. At about 5, both men look around, glazed look in their eyes, as they look for their tag partner. Both knowing they can’t give anymore without a breather in this fast paced match. Eron starts to inch his way towards his corner, where the massive Black Panther leans over as far as he can, wanting in on the action. Meanwhile, Slugger shakes the cobwebs out, locates Ric Chronos, and crawls in his direction. Eron reaches Panther, tags him in, and Panther runs to grab Scott Slugger just before he can tag in Ric Chronos. Panther succeeds by grabbing Scott's left leg, causing Scott to stand up and hop on one leg. Suddenly proving why he's the World's Greatest Athlete, Slugger jumps in the air on his free leg, plants it firmly on Panther's chest and executes a dropkick with little effect, but enough force to push Slugger close enough to tag Ric Chronos in before Slugger lands face first on the canvas. The crowd cheers the display, as well as the entrance of the very pumped up Chronos. Panther looks around at the crowd, then at Chronos, assessing how to get into the situation. Chronos looks rather agitated, glaring at the tall masked Panther. Chronos exchanges words with Panther...and for moments as the crowd starts to die down in sound noise, both men do nothing but exchange "pleasantries". Finger pointing ensues...angered expressions and shouts exchanged, then both men are at it. And apparently the rulebook went out the window as well. Panther uses his larger frame to push Chronos back into the ropes, and both spin around on the ropes until they get into a turnbuckle. At the same moment, Eron decides now is a good time to sneak around the ring and attack Slugger who was once again getting to his feet. Eron grabs Slugger's feet, trips him up, and pulls him to the outside. Chronos and Panther have turned for the worst, as both place chokeholds on each other. The ref attempts to restore order, and starts counting for both wrestlers to break the hold. On the outside, Slugger gets the upperhand on Eron, and a huge pop takes place as Slugger shows major leaguers how a 'Curveball' should look. Curveball on the cement floor, with the aid of the stairs, and Eron is out. Slugger is down, having taken alot of punishment in this match. Back in the ring, the ref had counted to 5, yelled that he was gonna disqualify both wrestlers, and unfortunately does so as neither Panther or Chronos cooperated. The crowd boos this action, as fWo Security begins to storm the ring. Fighting through kicks, scratches, punches, and finger pointing, security breaks up Panther and Chronos. When it looks like order is restored, Chronos breaks free and jumps on the pile containing Panther, and the two brawl and strangle each other some more. As they get broken up again, Chronos looks towards the ramp to find his tag partner, Fallen, standing there. The man known as John Skyler looks somber, and the only action he displays caught on camera is his head lowering in sadness. Chronos looks dumbfounded, as does Panther now seeing what Chronos sees. Warfare decides to head to commercial now after this most recent development. Result: Double Disqualification Crucified (VII) We went backstage to find Lance Star humming to himself with a towel around his waist as he approached his lockerroom. He had an icepack on his head, still nursing to his minor injuries that he incurred as part of his match with Hellrazor. "Good God... I'm glad it wasn't one of those Landmine Exploding Barb Wire Thumbtack Flaming Tables With Pirahna's Around The Ring Hell In A Cell type things... I'd never wrestle in one of those. I can't believe that crazy bastard went through that stuff earlier in the year." Lance turned a corner and found his dressing room door shut. "That's odd. Hmmm, what was I saying again? Oh yes... Hellrazor. What a nut. And that cat of his. His cat gets drunk. Only in America would a friggin' cat get drunk... then again, America sucks..." Lance opened the door to find a bottle of ether sitting in the middle of the viewable portion of the room. "Oh man... no..." Lance entered the rest of the room and saw Courage tied to a cross. Lance went through this, so he could sympathize with what Courage went through. "Jeez, you live for a minute to go soak in a jacuzzi, you come back, and your partner ties himself to a cross..." Obviously, Lance wasn't too bright... did he think Courage did this to himself? Who knows... Warfare proceeded to the ring for it's final match of the evening. The Boston Strangler vs. Mitch Wilson Genesis, unfortunately for those in the arena who do not enjoy the serene sounds of Phil Collins, brings us to the main event for the evening. Mitch Wilson makes his way out to the entrance ramp, his face hard and stern, his expression unmovable. Even a few Sprite cans that fly in his direction do not sway him. He slides into the ring and awaits one of his three opponents for the evening. Papa Roach, the lastest flash-in-the-pan that the younger fWo fans can identify with, hits the speakers next, and Primetime makes his way out toward ringside. Along his way down, he spots a fan wearing a beret and yelling "Zut! Must Zee TV...zut is zis filth?" He held a MUST SEE TV = POTTY BREAK sign. Primetime grabbed the sign, and ripped it up immediately. He then grabbed the beret and walked toward the fWo Announcers' table, where he dropped it on James Armstrong's head. Armstrong, not knowing what to do, put it on Jeremiah Frankton's head. He stayed away from the ring until his next opponent came out. "Dragula" by Rob Zombie hits the speakers next, and Mike Bear, the next man to get a shot at the coveted fWo World Title, makes his way out toward the ring. He walks down and cautiously slides into the ring, ahead of Primetime. O' Fortuna hits the speakers, and the fans, who were cheering for Bear, cheered even harder for the Boston Strangler, the fWo World Champion. He slides in the ring, and occupies a third corner. Primetime finally slides in the ring, and takes up the last remaining corner. All four men look at each other. The bell rings. The men charge the center of the ring. The battle has begun. Primetime and Mike Bear lock up. The two don't have much of a history, aside from Bear having issues with the Flying Frenchie in the beginning of the year as well as the recent skirmishes seen between the two men. Bear gets the best of Primetime, with a headlock. PT counters with a back bodydrop. Bear gets up quickly, however. He realizes, along with his competitors, that this is only the beginning. It will take a lot more than that. The Boston Strangler nails an uppercut on Mitch Wilson. Like the aforementioned PT and Bear, these two do not have much of a history. Save for a match recently, these two have been strangers to each other. On this night, they have a chance to get acquainted with one another. Just how much is the question. Wilson gets a quick legsweep on TBS, and then covers him with a one-legged Boston Crab. TBS gets out quickly, and these two have realized that they have quite a battle on their hands. Primetime throws Bear into a corner. He charges him, and takes off for a splash. Bear moves, and PT eats some turnbuckle. He turns around, and Bear nails him with a DDT. Bear gets up, and Wilson, who has kicked Strangler in the gut, nails a DDT of his own on Bear. Meanwhile, PT has slid out of the ring. The Strangler is heading towards Wilson. The ref, meanwhile, is having ulcers trying to keep up with all of this. Wilson grabs the ref and turns him around. The Strangler looks strangely at this. He looks at the way Wilson has distracted the ref, and knows something is up. Primetime is nowhere to be found. The Strangler takes a step back when he turns around, thus to avoid a collision with a foreign object. Primetime, who was ready with a chair, looks at the Strangler. Wilson, who has been watching this out of the corner of his eye, slyly gives Strangler a little "push" with his foot while talking to the ref. Strangler unexpectedly pushes forward, and right into the steel of Primetime's chair. Primetime goes for a cover and yells at the ref. The ref, obviously tired from Wilson's conversation, runs to make the cover. At two, Mike Bear makes the save ahead of Wilson. Wilson looks at Bear with a bit of vengence in his eye. He grabs the head of Bear and hammers away on him. He picks him up and hits a snap suplex, followed by a Camel Clutch. The ref asks Bear if he wants to give up, but Bear won't give. There's plenty of fight left in him. Wilson gets tired of holding the move, and picks up Bear. He whips him toward a corner. Bear nails the corner at full speed, and drops to the canvas. Immediately, Wilson leaves the ring. He motions for Primetime, who has the chair with him. PT walks over, and Wilson holds Bear's right arm out. Primetime starts to take some vicious chops at it. Bear yells out in pain, and PT looks at Wilson with a smile. Wilson smiles back, and then stops for a second. Wilson and PT both scratch their heads...and then, realizing what they are doing, stop it. PT takes a whack at Wilson with the chair, but misses. Wilson kicks PT in the gut, making him drop the chair in front of him. Wilson immediately grabs the neck of PT and nails him with a DDT. Wilson picks up PT and starts to look at the announcers' tables. Which one would inflict the most damage? There's the Danish Announcers' table, which, due to a lack of ratings in that country, has been reduced to a card table. There's the Bangladesh announcers' table, which has a picnic table cover in the shape of a bandanna on it. Why this is is unknown...it's believed that James Armstrong tried to make our friends of the middle east feel "at home", or that, should they get hungry for bun cake, they could just walk over there and eat away...either way, it didn't work. So Armstrong just gave them some of Finn's porn, which sparked their interest. While Wilson ponders how American pornography and picnic blanket announcer table-bandannas are somehow shaping the wrestling world, another object grabs his concentration. Primetime nails Wilson in the groin with a boot. Wilson hits the floor; his midsection is a very delicate area (this is a major reason why he listens to Genesis. 94% of all men with delicate groins listen to Genesis. It's a fact). Primetime couldn't care less about why he listens to Genesis. He knows that the task at hand is to beat him so badly that he can never hear the 80s group again. Primetime lines up the head of Wilson, and boxes his ears. Wilson shoots upward; the pain of that is almost unbearable. However, Wilson hits a very important counter-move. As he shot his head upward, he nails PT is HIS groin. Primetime hits the mat with a resounding thud. Meanwhile, the Boston Strangler is up and watching all of this. He looks down at the mat. Mike Bear is beginning to stir, his arm a wreck. The Strangler begins the waiting game. Wilson begins to get up. He cannot hear a thing, and that doesn't matter. He picks up PT and drags him toward the regular fWo announcers' table. He picks PT up, and the two begin to brawl. They trade rights and lefts. PT nails Wilson in the gut, and grabs his neck. A suplex on the table is coming. He lifts Wilson up; however, Wilson used his inertia to carry himself past PT; PT turns around, and is met with a kick to the knee from Wilson. PT bends down, and Wilson grabs him. He goes to pick him up. However, this is met with a block by PT. PT counters with a suplex of his own. However, at the top of the move, Wilson shifts his weight so that he's on top of PT. Primetime, who now has a bum wheel, cannot hold the added weight, and falls. Wilson lands on top of him, thus knocking the wind out of Primetime. Wilson picks up Primetime, and sets him up on the table. He grabs the beret from Frankton's head, and put it on Primetime. He sets him up for the Headliner, his finisher. He locks him in, and nails it on the table. The table is destroyed, and, it seems, so are Mitch Wilson and Primetime. However, the fans have another event to watch. A preview of SummerSlaughter's main event is about to take place. Mike Bear has gotten into the ring. Strangler starts to nail Bear with lefts. Bear counters with a left of his own. Strangler goes for a knee in the gut, which is countered beautifully by Bear. Bear grabs Strangler, and seemingly out of nowhere picks up Strangler for the Whiplash. However, Strangler, having a lot of his energy, blocks it. Strangler, instead, hits an inverted DDT on Bear. He goes for a cover, but only gets a two. Bear kicks out, and Strangler decides to go for the Massacre. He tries to lock Bear into it, but Bear counters with a solid kick to the sternum. Strangler flies backward, and Bear gets up. Bear charges Strangler. Strangler charges back at him, and hits him with a shoulderblock to Bear's right side. Bear falls and clutches his arm. Meanwhile, Mitch Wilson begins to stir. Strangler walks over toward Bear. Bear seems to be very injured, and Strangler senses it. However, Bear does have one trick up his sleeve. As Strangler is picking him up, Bear falls to one knee. Strangler bends down, and Bear grabs him. He lifts him up, and, this time, and definitely out of nowhere, he nails the Whiplash. He covers. The ref counts. One. Two. Kickout at the last possible milli-second. The Boston Strangler has kicked out of the Whiplash, although it's not a Whiplash at 100%. The two men lay on the ground, both dazed. Meanwhile, Mitch Wilson, who just a minute ago was getting up, stumbles into the ring. When he does this, he makes the horrible misfortune of walking over the Strangler. The Strangler pulls a trick out of his own hat and grabs Wilson, and rolls him up into a small package. The ref counts. At the count of two, Wilson uses this move like a cup of coffee, as it instantly wakes him up. He kicks out, and the two men begin to get up. They're not the only ones. So does Mike Bear. Bear sees these two men next to each other by the ropes. He charges them, arms extended. The Strangler, seeing this coming ducks. Wilson, not seeing it, gets decked by Bear. Wilson flies over the top rope, and lands on his head on the mat below. Bear turns around to find Strangler's boot in his midsection. Bear bows over, and Strangler sets him up for a piledriver. However, Bear backdrops out of it. Strangler, however, holds on, giving a chance for a sunset flip. Strangler is so close to succeeding when Bear pulls out one final trick out of his hat. He drops to his knees, reaches back and holds both legs. The Strangler has no place to go. One. Two. Three. Mike Bear has secured the upset win. The Strangler cannot believe it. Primetime, who has since gotten up, grabs the beret off his head and rips it in half. Wilson begins to walk to the back, holding his head and neck. The Stranger sits in the ring, watching as Mike Bear celebrates one of the biggest wins in his career. Bear, clutching his arm, celebrates with the crowd. Meanwhile, the announcers remind viewers that SummerSlaughter sits just seventy two hours away, and the cameras fade to black. Result: Bear by pinfall
