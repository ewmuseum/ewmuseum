Introduction Sigh The scene opened up in the back parking lot and a figure wearing a black shirt and wrestling tights was already approaching the coliseum. The bright purple NS letters on the front of the shirt signified it was Nicky Santiago, unless of course it was just someone wearing a shirt of hers. However, it truly was Nicky Santiago. So anyways Nicky Santiago was seen walking with her brother/manager Pablo Santiago. The two walked across the parking lot with gym bags in their hands and walked up to an entrance door. "Well, here we are again Pobs, making that famous walk." Nicky said smiling with curiously. Pablo looked down at her and laughed. "Their's nothing famous about these first walks to debut shows arenas." Pablo paused. "Trust me, if it was famous, we'd be in a limo." Pablo said with a glare in his eye that almost looked like he had imagined himself pulling up in a limo. Nicky nodded. Then Pablo spoke once again. "You know, I once pulled up in a Chevy truck once." He said smiling with pride. "Chevy truck?" Nicky said with a questioning look. "What the hell is so special about that!" She exclaimed. Pablo's face turned to that of a person who had just been asked something as simple as "What is one plus one." .... Pablo then spoke. "How could you not know how special that is, I swear, your becoming more American every day." "What? Excuse me!" Nicky said becoming angry. Pablo huffed. "Practically everyone arrived to the arenas in beetles in Mexico so to pull up in a Chevy truck back then was something." Pablo said this time raising his chest up high. "Well whatever, let's go inside." Pablo said now getting frustrated. "What? What!" I understand, really." Nicky said apologetic like. The two then walked in and Nicky nodded at the security guard who noticed the two and the wardrobe they were in and assumed they were wrestlers as he let them past. Nicky then walked around a corridor with Pablo as the two neared the snack table. "You know Pobs, during Survivor and all during tSC there were never any tamales or nacho's or even burritos for that matter on the snack table." Nick said. "A shame." Pablo chimed in. "And look before us, OCW, they truly provide!" Nicky said excited like. And before the two luchadors stood a snack table which included mexican food, tamales, burritos and even gorditas could be seen. "Chuo kno I dwelly dink dis is gobeing next tig ding." Nicky said with a moutful of burrito. "Huh?" Pablo said back loudly confused. "You know, I'm really thinking this is going to be the next big thing." Nicky said now without a mouthful of burrito. "It just might be." Pablo said. Pablo walked around looking from left to right as stagehands ran by trying to complete before show tasks and what not. "Lets check out this coliseum." Pablo said demandingly as he walked forward and pushed a curtain aside. Pablo's mouth dropped as Nicky then pushed through the curtains with her free hand as the other was still tightly grasping a burrito. "What the hell is this? It looks like an elementary school's gym!" Nicky blasted out as her face dropped. Pablo laughed. "Yeah, I expect BIG things." He then chuckled some more before walking back into the main corridor. "At least they got a good snack table." Nicky said in a quiet voice as if she was trying way too hard to cheer herself up. Opening Speech "Ladies and gentlemen...welcome to OCW." The wrestlers in attendance in the makeshift conference room stared at Phillip J. Roth III, the majority partner in the OCW promotion, standing in front of the room, with Brian 'War' Cage, Frank 'Famine' Smythe and Stevie Roberts seated on either side of him. There had been a month long delay since the chaotic events of Meltdown, where a schism had created a new promotional entity, mainly due to attempted injunctions by Disney. However, the injunctions had been successfully stopped in place, partially due to the man speaking in front of the roster at this moment. Roth, a former practicing attorney, still wore the classy-looking pinstripe suit which he had worn while winning a number of class action lawsuits against the tobacco industry a number of years ago. "I wish to explain the circumstances of this monumental event to everyone in this room..." trailed off Roth, as he calmly adjusted his gold cufflinks, "Tonight, we will be running the inaugural event, which will show to the world why the OCW is a force to be recognized in Professional Wrestling." stated Roth, as the hand of one of the OCW's staff members went up. "Yes, Mr. Strong?" asked Roth to Ryder Strong, former News Reporter turned OCW Backstage Reporter. "Mr. Roth, why exactly are we running our debut event in such a small venue? I mean..." trailed off Strong, as Roth interrupted himself, quickly brushing his hand against his slowly greying hair. Roth's quick tongue and even quicker thinking on his feet was presenting itself to the roster. "My reasoning for this is to establish our market base. I know we have a number of marketed individuals, such as our United States champion, Kellen Kinkade..." trailed off Roth during his almost PR explanation, motioning to Kinkade, who smirked devilishly wtih the United States championship draped across his shoulder, "Reno Davis..." trailed off Roth again, motioning this time to Davis, who nodded, "And others. However, given that no one was every successfully promoted as the primary drawing card of their promotion and inherited an established large fanbase, we are stepping into unknown territory in terms of our drawing strength." stated Roth bluntly, as he clasped his hands behind his back, and began to pace around. "It's better to fill up 5000 seat venues now than to half fill 10000 seat venues, and be indebted at the start. Once we establish our market strength, it will justify us using the major promotional venues, as well as paying for Pay Per View time. But for now, we are grounded to being a nationally growing promotion, attempting to form our own niche." Roth expressed calmly, as Strong nodded his head in affirmation at the cool and calculated rationale for the smaller sized venue usage. "Now, tonight, I want to see everyone try their best out there, presenting their best possible work and make us proud..." trailed off Roth, as Cage said 'Hell Yeah!' and Roberts and Smythe both nodded their heads in agreement, "And soon enough, we will become a true force that will make people take a good look at all of us. Now..." trailed off Roth, pointing to everyone at the meeting, "Let's get this show off the ground." Bantam Diablo vs. Logic While other promotions worried about their hair and sequence tights, the dogs of war were unleashed in OCW. To a passing eye, it seemed like another start up promotion with intentions on overthrowing the crown. To others it was a big fuck you to Disney and sports entertainment in general. We welcome you. Mars, the Bringer of War, composed by Gustav Holst cued up over the crackling speakers. Close followers of Survivor IV boo'ed as Logic came down the narrow hallway. A few slap happy fans reached out and patted Logic on his glistening shoulders. He ignored them, his eyes rest sternly on the red, white, and blue ring ropes as he climbed onto the foot worn mat. Mars, the Bringer of War was replaced by AC/DC's Thunderstruck as BD made a charismatic entrance along with the lovely Diz, and red-headed Bink as ringmates. BD happily reached out to eager hands, and even signed a few autographs. Bink and Diz were busy handing out various signs to the crowd in attendance. 'BD For President' 'BD=Better Than Your Mom' 'I Love Little Girls...AND BD!' They were but a few... BD entered the ring and spun around to the crowd with his arms open. He received a nice welcome from the small crowd as Logic continued his cold steel stare from the corner of the ring. The bell rang and signified the real start of OCW. Yes I know...a real tear jerker. Logic made a quick move out of his corner while BD was caught a bit off guard. BD and Logic paced around the ring in circles teasing the crowd before they went for a tie up. Logic bore down with his 6'9" frame for a collar and elbow. BD countered with a quick as a cat (meow) strike to the midsection. Logic doubled over gasping for air as the crowd gave a small pop as BD waved his finger to the crowd. Logic may be smart, but BD is sly. Crack! BD was folded in half on the mat as Logic pushed through on a stiff clothesline sending an "Ouch!" through the crowd. Logic picked up BD and Irish whipped him into the ropes before lifting him straight up in the air and back down on his face with a nice vaulted gorilla press slam. A front face headlock turned into a twisted, over the head pin for the first time in OCW history. 1... 2... BD kicked out and rolled back over onto his stomach. He slowly got up before Logic whipped him hard into the red ringpost. BD stumbled out of the corner and Logic ran with a full head of steam. He leapt into the air and grabbed hold of BD's head and executed a textbook bulldog from out of the corner. Well...I mean, if BD didn't shrug him off that is. Logic landed ass on the mat and nothing in his cradle. BD adjusted his mask before running off the multi-colored ropes and aiming both heels on the crown of Logic's head. Logic neck sprung forward and came back to zenith as BD rolled away from his opponent before stalking over and applying an under-hook dragon sleeper. Logic thrashed out in pain even more when BD buried his knee into the small of his back to increase the potency of the submission move. The referee gained position and asked Logic if he wanted to give up. The look of a raging bull told him it was a stupid question. Logic wasn't the only person that was taking a toll because of the submission hold. It took all that BD had to keep a man almost 90 pounds bigger than him at bay. Before the end, it was BD that gave. A glass shattering jawbreaker to be exact. Both Logic and BD now lay on the mat as the referee administers the standard ten count. It was around 7 or 8...I can't remember, when BD climbed to the ropes. He was looking to put it away early. The crowd popped as BD raised his arms slowly in the air. On the mat, with a bullseye on his chest was Logic. GFC !!!! (Generic Cruiserweight Finisher for all you less knowing folk) BD HIT IT! The mat that is. Logic crawled over to BD and covered. The referee slid into position and like a true man, he slapped that mat! 1... 2... BD kicked out. The all forgotten Bink and Diz finally came to life and tried to get the crowd into the match by chanting 'BD 4-LIFE.! BD 4-LIFE! BD 4-LIFE!'. The chanting didn't phase The Pensive Punisher. He picked up BD and again whipped him into the ropes. Coming off the ropes he grabbed BD around the waist and went for a German suplex. BD gained his bearings mid-revolution and grabbed hold of anything as he was slamming towards the mat. Little did he know it would be Logic's neck. During the process of the German suplex, Logic also delivered a reverse DDT on himself. What a sucka!!! Around the count of 6, Logic swiveled around to the top of BD's body. He braced his hands on BD's back and delivered pendulum drop knee breakers to BD's shoulders, wearing down his arms and putting strain on his neck. After each drop, BD screamed...like a girl. Bink and Diz on the outside cringed at the site BD was making. He was losing fans by the second with those school girl shrieks. Feeling satisfied, Logic picked up BD and spun him around so his back was facing him. He placed his hands under BD's arms and lifted him high in the air. He walked around the ring and smirked at the crowd, generating many boo's from the crowd. BD, held in a crucifix postion looked lifeless. Logic grunted loudly while he muscled BD over his head and dropped him into a Crucifix powerbomb pinning combination. Immediately the referee began the 3-count. 1... 2... BD gave a weak kick out. Logic sighed knowing only the inevitable would come. Logic helped BD into the ropes and on the return gave a spinning elbow. Before gravity could even be a factor, Logic had BD rolled up again with a nice small package. 1... 2... KICKOUT AGAIN! Like you didn't see it coming. Diz pounded the mat as Bink made side bets against BD. Logic tied BD up in a full-nelson. Before he could do anymore damage to the upper body, BD made a sloppy attempt at a headbutt. Logic loosened his grip momentarily. Just enough for BD to make a last ditched effort into the ropes. He came barreling off the ropes but it was too little, too late. Logic caught him again and executed a sidewalk slam. Scratch that. BD countered with a Hurricanrana. The crowd cheered as a smile developed underneath the mask of Bantam Diablo. Can you spell comeback? BD picked up Logic and rocked Logic into the ropes with a few right handed punches. BD locked wrists as he went to send Logic into the turnbuckle. The follow-through was anything but what BD expected. Logic squeezed down with his own hand, not breaking the wrist lock. Instead, the over powering 290 pound powerhouse pulled BD in with a boot to the breadbasket. Logic cradled BD's head. BACKDROP DRIVER! PROOF POSITIVE! This match is over folks! The referee makes the count. 1... 2... 3! NO! I MEAN YES! In OCW's inaugural match, Logic wins out of nowhere! Result: Logic via Pinfall Loading... Loading... Loading... Covered by the shadows, one of them turned to help the other get his suitcase out from in-between the automatic doors. They then walked into the light. Getting his baggage caught in the arena doors was just the beginning. Conor, on the other hand, wore the lime attire. He was, as said, the younger of the two. At 21 (13 in VG years), Conor was the fuel of the team. He was the crazy, uncontrollable second player, that yearned for the chance to one day become the first, although that would never happen... but who said you can't dream? Tyler observed the arena walls, while Conor kept trying to adjust his blue shirt. "You know... we don't have to wear these things wherever we go." Conor complained, but Tyler didn't bother to reply. The Fuse Bros. turned the corner and came across their locker room. Tyler unlocked the door with the key he was given by Roth (Tyler thought Roth was a cold individual, someone who might be into stealth video games. A sliver of yellow light glowed brightly beneath its wood ceiling. As the camera climbed, as did the streaks that ran parallel on the oak door. The ridges formed intricate and ornate patterns, weaving and zigzagging past the brass doorknob and up to the nameplate that hung loosely from the door. The gold engraving gleamed in the astringent white light that pestered overhead. The camera panned, allowing only a letter to creep into frame as it inched along. P-H-I-L-L-I-P--J--R-O-T-H A commotion was heard on the other side of the door, gradually increasing in pitch and timbre. The golden doorknob jostled, and the cameraman stood back to await the arrival of the one departing. The door crept open just enough so the person inside could see if anyone was there. Obviously, there was. The man's identity was neatly concealed by overwhelming shadows from the dimly lit office. He looked back, seemingly out of apprehension. He was consoled by the dry voice within. "Go ahead, son. You're a part of the OCW family now." The man lowered his head and opened the door wide and stood in the direct center of the doorframe. He was clothed in an OCW t-shirt; his original one hanging in neglect on his left shoulder. Still, only the contours of his face could be observed as the monstrous bill of his had effectively hid his identity. However, the fans watching from within the arena saw this, and he began to hear a distant rumble of cheers and applause. They had recognized him, remembered him, appreciated him. He lifted his head then, allowing his mouth to emerge into the light and produce a sneer for the closeup camera shot. He then backed up and hurried down the corridor, the camera lingering to watch him decrease in size as he receded into a pool of frenzied media personnel and OCW superstars. The camera, losing the man's position eventually, promptly faded out. Well, if it aint the masked rat! Song 2 by Blur hit the loudspeakers and echoed around the arena. Followers of the fWo Survivor contest knew exactly what this meant. Taylor Rogers stepped out into the arena wearing an all yellow tights, pads and boots combination, accompanied by his manager the devious Benjamin Davis. Rogers stared at his surroundings with a determined look on his face, before moving his focus to his manager Davis, who simply nodded. It was time for Rogers to compete in accordance to his manager's game plan. He was about to do whatever it took in order to win. "Cocky" by Kid Rock. The music blared as "Potential Personified" Chris Moliano stepped out into the arena to a mixed reception. The crowd remembered him from his time in the Survivor contest despite his early exit from the competition, being only the third man eliminated. Chris was decked out in blue trunks and knee high blue boots with both wrists taped up and an expensive pair of sunglasses on. He walked with a distinct swagger about him as he smiled arrogantly. Cocky was not only his theme song - it was his personality. As he stepped into the ring he stared at the crowd those lucky enough to be here to witness what was sure to be his first ever win, and an impressive one at that. Ding Ding! The bell sounded and the two combatants circled the ring with their eyes locked firmly on one another, until Moliano dived in looking to lock up with Rogers who side stepped and grabbed Chris in a waistlock. Rogers pushed Chris into the turnbuckle over in the far corner, holding him there until the referee stepped in and ordered he broke the hold due to Moliano being in the ropes. Rogers appeared as though he was going to give a clean break, before he gave Moliano a cheap punch to his spine causing him to cry out in pain. Rogers looked over at Benjamin Davis on the outside who applauded his action with a beaming smile on his face. Moliano was visibly angered by Rogers' reluctance to break cleanly and charged full steam ahead towards him. Rogers was caught off guard, perhaps still thinking about his controversial game plan, and was knocked down to the canvas with a hard clothesline. Moliano was never one to play out a tactical match; he went in as the aggressor each and every time and was determined to make an impact on the debut of OCW. Chris was already pumped up and called for Rogers to get back up to his feet, and when he did Moliano greeted him with a picture perfect dropkick that drew approval from the crowd. The match had just begun but Moliano was already up ahead of steam and leapt up to the top rope searching for a high impact move. As Taylor again climbed back to his feet Moliano took off with a cross body but Rogers regained his senses in time to counter it and bury Moliano into the mat with a thunderous powerslam. Rogers held on for a cover... One... Two... Moliano got the shoulder up in the nick of time. He knew right away that he had made the mistake of getting too worked up and going for risky moves way too early in the match. He had promised himself he would not make any more mistakes, but as Taylor Rogers picked him up by the hair to a vertical base and began firing away with vicious right hands he knew he had already made a huge error. Taylor Rogers on the other hand had recovered from the impact of the clothesline and dropkick and set to work on the torso of his opponent with some right hands, each one hitting harder than the last. Rogers whipped Moliano into the ropes and when Chris came back off the ropes a high knee met him square in the jaw. Rogers did not waste a second as no sooner was Moliano back up was he being hooked in a snap suplex that shook the ring. Rogers leapt up and Moliano followed only to be hooked in another suplex, this time it was a German suplex with a bridge for a pinfall attempt for which the referee was a little slow getting down to make the count... One... Two... Moliano was just able to roll out of the pin but he was really feeling the effects of those suplexes on his spine. Rogers was quick to realise this and capitalised by walking around Moliano and punching him right in the lower spine for the second time in the match once again causing Moliano to yell out. Rogers had the strength advantage over the slightly smaller Moliano and charged toward him shoulder first driving his shoulder into the spine of Moliano with a spear. Rogers knew he was in complete control and could see Moliano struggling and holding his back with a grimace on his face. CRUNCH! Rogers picked up Moliano without too much trouble and drove him hard into his knee with a crunching back breaker. This game plan was simple and more pleasing to Taylor was completely legal select a body part and wear your man down. Chris held onto his lower back gingerly as he sucked in the air whilst Rogers was recovering from the suplex. Taylor was soon up and went for Moliano who stepped aside and took Rogers down to the canvas with a drop toe hold and followed through locking in a sleeper hold in an attempt to wear down Rogers while his back recovered. Taylor sprung up and tried to fight Moliano off but his vice-like grip was too tight and the big man was soon back down to one knee, and then to the mat completely as Moliano's sleeper took effect. His mind was brimming with ideas of what to do next; Moliano was by no means an experienced competitor and his noticeable lack of any kind of win hinted not towards a lack of talent, but a lack of knowledge. His mind raced as he held the sleeper on tightly and the referee dropped the arm of Rogers... Once... Twice... Not the most picturesque one you will see but the impact was there and Rogers certainly felt it. The crowd began to stir and Moliano was again getting worked up, he saw an opening and had to take it he could ill afford any more mistakes. Chris made up his mind and ran off the ropes looking for a clothesline only problem was Taylor had regained his bearings in time and had attempted the exact same thing. The resulting double clothesline had both men down on the canvas and facing a battle against the count of the referee... One... Two... Both men remained shoulders down on the mat. Three... Four... Rogers began to stir as he looked over to his corner and saw Benjamin Davis smacking the edge of the mat to try and encourage him back to his feet. Five... Moliano looked over at Rogers who had begun to move. Six... Seven... Rogers grabbed the bottom rope and tried to pull himself up. Noticing this and seeing that Rogers was about to beat him back up to a vertical base Moliano gained a sudden burst of adrenaline and began to climb back to his feet. Both men were up almost simultaneously to a roar from the crowd as they stepped into the centre of the ring and began exchanging blows. Moliano held on for a pinfall attempt... One... Two... Kickout! Rogers shook his head to try and rid himself of the ringing in his ears, but was not allowed much time to do so before Moliano was back on the offensive with some more stiff chops. The crowd was rising as they could sense the momentum shifting firmly in Moliano's favour much to Benjamin Davis' fury. Chris signalled that he was about to scale the turnbuckle and climb to the top for the second time only this time he knew it was the right moment. Chris Moliano climbed steadily to the top and tapped the end of his elbow before taking flight with a picture perfect diving elbow as cameras around the arena flashed throughout the crowd. "Come on Chris...this is it baby." This was his chance to show the world that wrestling was what he does. He was about to prove the doubters wrong, his ambition was about to become reality and Christopher Moliano was finally going to break his du... Nope. Sorry folks but this isn't a fairy tale.
