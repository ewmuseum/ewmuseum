Wednesday Warfare, 29 March 2000

29 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Ultra Violet whistled to himself as he watched wrestlers enter the arena in preparation of Wednesday Warfare. It was going to be a good show... or so the fWo officials were telling the local media. Ultra Violet picked up his bag that had been laying next to the arena entrance. It seemed heavier somehow, but Ultra Violet didn't worry about it. He walked past Mike Bear, who was being coached by James Armstrong as to the promo he would cut later in the night. Ultra Violet walked to his dressing room and walked inside still whistling to himself. He tossed the bag onto a bench and began to dig through it looking for his lucky boots. Suddenly his hand came over something hard and square, and he frowned. What could this be? Ultra Violet pulled it out and looked curiously at a videotape. It had a small note attached to the front of it. "PLAY ME" was written in a tight French script. Ultra Violet shrugged and took the videotape out of the locker room in search of a video player. He didn't see the electrical cord laying on the floor and tripped on it, dropping the tape. The tape flew through the air but before it could smash upon the hard concrete floor it was caught. "Hey... be more careful! You almost wrecked this!" War tossed Ultra Violet the tape and Ultra Violet waved thanks. He spotted a video player across the room and headed for it, not realizing that he was dragging the tape against the side of the wall as he walked. It scratched and chipped the case, threatening to crack down the middle. Just as it was about to snap Ultra Violet reached the player and stuck the tape into it with a grin. He pressed play and waited. Static filled the screen, and nothing more. Ultra Violet scratched his head, wondering why anyone would put a blank tape into his bag. Shrugging he went back to his room and continued to dig through the bag. He pulled out shirts, paint cans, and his lucky giant magnet. Outside, the fireworks exploded, heralding the start of Wednesday Warfare... Primetime vs. Eron "Physical" by Nine Inch Nails brings out Eron as Warfare returns from commerical. The dark superstar accepts the jeers of the crowd, and takes exception to the few extreme French Foundation fans throwing things at him. Security gets between Eron and the fans, and escorts Eron to the ring. Eron looks back toward the entrance ramp as "Blind" by Korn begins to play, bringing out Primetime to a negative but loud reaction. It is, however, warmer than Eron's. Primetime stops at the ramp and sneers at Eron. He will go no further. The Flying Frenchie and Courage come out and the three converse on the ramp. Primetime grabs a mic from backstage and begins to speak. "Listen you rogue freak, as much as I would like to carry your no-talent ass to your first watchable match in much, I'd rather not put my body in danger by actually having to touch your grotesque body and contact a disease." Eron, not surprised, grabs a mic and replies back. "Fine. It doesn't matter to me how I get a win. I could kick your ass now, or you can consider your ass kicked already. Either way, you're gonna lose. So go ahead and go stick up the backstage, like all the rest of the French." TFF looked at Primetime and pointed at Eron, saying "Kick ze putain's behind." Primetime nodded in agreement and took off toward the ring. Eron met him as PT climbed in the ring and the two started brawling. Primetime kicks Eron in the gut and goes for the Primetime Bomb. However, before he can lift him, Eron reverses it into a back body drop. Eron starts stomping away at Primetime, and then picks him up and lifts him for a bodyslam. He connects on it, and picks PT up again, and bodyslams him again. Eron picks up Primetime again, and takes off toward the ropes. Primetime senses this and runs behind Eron, catching him with a clothesline, sending Eron over the top rope. Eron crashes to the floor, and Primetime follows him out, and picks him up. Eron clubs Primetime in the gut, knocking the wind out of him. The two start to brawl again, and Eron gains the upper hand with a kick to the gut and a DDT on the ramp. Eron then begins to walk toward the announcer's table, grabbing Stevie Roberts' chair. He folds the chair up and nails Primetime with it. He grabs hold of Primetime and tosses him back into the ring, followed by the chair. He climbs back into the ring and grabs the chair. The ref blocks Eron's attempt to further damage PT. However, this only ticks Eron off more, and he nails the ref with the chair, knocking him out. That doesn't stop him from hitting him again to make sure he's out. He grabs the fallen Primetime and locks him in his finisher, the De-Rouger. As he has the Boston Crab locked in, TFF and Courage make their way to ringside. Eron's back is turned toward the ramp, so he is unaware of their arrival. They grab the chair that Eron used on PT and walk up behind Eron. "Ze Frenchie stinks, eh?" TFF asks as he wacks away on Eron's skull. Courage grabs another chair as TFF continues to swing away on the now-unconscious Eron. The two lace into Eron until he is cut open, as Primetime begins to come to. He picks up Eron and nails the Primetime Bomb, as TFF wakes up the ref. Primetime makes the cover, and gets the easy three count. The French Foundation takes a couple of more shots at Eron, and then climb out of the ring to some cheers from the crowd, mainly due to the beating they administered to Eron. The three starts walk up the entrance ramp as medics arrive at ringside as Warfare heads to a break. Result: Primetime by DQ Boston Strangler vs. Hiroshima Warfare resumes as the cameras head to the ring to a rematch from the December showdown between The Boston Strangler and Hiroshima, who was respresenting the FtfWo. "Oh My God" by Guns 'N Roses began blaring throughout the Worcester Centrum, and Hiroshima is greeted by a mammoth amount of jeers from the crowd. Hiro strides into the ring and awaits his opponent, the man he assisted Dead in burying a week ago on Warfare, the Boston Strangler. Strangler returned to the fWo this past Sunday night as the show was ending. After the show had stopped, the fWo cameras kept rolling and captured a verbal confrontation between Dead & Strangler, before Hiroshima came in from behind. However, the Strangler saw him out of the corner of the eye, before whacking him with a shovel. This resulted in a giant "klong" that echoed throughout the United Center. Just as the Strangler was about to gain revenge, Dead escaped from the ring, taking Hiroshima with him and retreating to the back. That has led us to tonight's match between the two, and as Hiro awaited "O' Fortuna" jarred throughout the Centrum, before the man from nearby Boston made his way out to a huge positive reaction. Strangler methodically stormed the ring, and Hiro gained the early advantage by pummeling him immediately with stomps and kicks to the head. Hiro brought Strangler to his feet and sent him into the ropes, but Strangler reversed it, and when Hiro returned he was met with an unpleasant charging elbow to the facial area. Hiro was sent to the mat, and the nearby hometown favorite brought Hiroshima up, before taking him down with a sidewalk slam. A cover ensued, but Hiro muscled out at one and a half. Strangler's onslaught continued, as he dished out a gutwrench powerbomb, a snap suplex and a tombstone piledriver, mocking Hiroshima' colleague Dead. None of those however were enough to put his opponent away. Strangler sent Hiro into the ropes and as Hiro returned he was taken down with a drop-toe-hold, and then a camel clutch. Hiroshima tried to escape, but it was all for naught as the Strangler held his guard. However, the arena turned to darkness just as Hiroshima was about to tap out... and on the Warfare screen, an image of Dead appeared. A giant bulldozer was behind him, and this captured Strangler' attention at once. Strangler released the hold, before standing at the edge of the ring and listening to what Dead had to say. "Paul... while you are being troubled by my cohort Akira tonight... and since we're in what essentially could be referred to as your hometown... I thought I'd do a little digging. And what I found out is... where I'm standing... is where your parents are gone and buried. Not only them... but your mentor. The man who's funeral you couldn't even bring yourself to attend. I'm sure he's unhappy about that... so I thought I'd do some more digging tonight... and let you say hi to them once again. Let them see what's become of their son. Let you... come face to face with your fear. Have fun with Akira... since you're tied up at the moment... I'll tell your mom and dad you said hi. I'll let old what's his face know how you're doing... I told you once, Paul. I am the master of mind games... well, tonight... it's time to play." Dead let loose an evil maniacal laugh, before the image disappeared from the screen. Strangler was about to exit from the ring when Hiroshima snuck up from behind with a lowblow, before turning Strangler around and attempting to hit the Oblivion. As he went for it, the Strangler somehow escaped, and upon doing so he immediately slapped on the Boston Massacre, which prompted the fans to cheer wildly. Hiroshima soon tapped out or blacked out... maybe a combination of both, before Strangler stormed from the ringside area victorious... and nobody knowing where he was headed. Result: Boston Strangler by Submission Frank Snow vs. Robert Jensen Warfare returns from commerical break to the sounds of "Bleed" by Soulfly, bringing out Robert Jensen for this Hardcore number one contenders match. He has his barbed wire baseball bat in one hand, and with the other he is steering a wheelbarrow full of weapons to the ring. As Jensen sets up a table, Godsmack's "Whatever" plays, and Frank Snow runs down the ramp, and immediately attacks Jensen with fists to the back. Snow grabs Jensen by the hair, and rams him headfirst into the ring post. With Jensen stunned, Snow tips out the various weapons from the wheelbarrow. Snow takes the empty wheelbarrow, punches Jensen in the head a few times, and sets him in the wheelbarrow. Snow sends the wheelbarrow into the guardrail, and Jensen tips over into the crowd, knocking over a few innocent fans. Jensen tries to recooperate, but as he is stepping back over the guardrail, Snow comes at him with a chair. Jensen thinks quickly, and blocks the chairshot with a punch to the gut. Jensen finally makes it over the guardrail, and grabs the chair. He nails Snow in the back with the chair, before rolling him into the ring. Jensen rummages through the weapons on the ground before producing a 2x4. Jensen keeps the 2x4 in his hands, as he sends Snow into the ropes. As Snow comes back, Jensen gets the 2x4 into Snow's gut. With Snow keeled over, Jensen hammers him in the back with the 2x4. Snow crumbles to the mat, and Jensen covers, but only for a two count. Jensen picks Snow up by the hair, as he sets up the 2x4 in the middle of the ring. Jensen whips Snow into the ropes, and goes for a clothesline. Snow ducks under, and Jensen turns around, right into a Snow spinebuster right on the 2x4! The crowd erupts into an fWo chant, as Snow goes for a cover. However, Jensen kicks out at two. The crowd is amazed, and Snow decides to perch Jensen on the turnbuckle. He goes for a superplex, but Jensen blocks, and pushes Snow off. The back of Snow's head hits the 2x4 which was still in the ring, and it knocks Snow out. Jensen climbs down, and to the outside, where he grabs the barbed-wire baseball bat. He brings it into the ring, and clears the 2x4 from the ring. He picks up Snow's lifeless body, and sets him up for the DDT on the barbed wire bat, otherwise known as "Make You Bleed". However, Snow blocks, and pushes Jensen back into the ropes. Jensen shoots off, and walks right into his own finisher, a DDT on the barbed wire bat. Blood begins to pour from Jensen's face, as Snow grabs a chair, and places it on Jensen's face. Snow climbs the turnbuckle, and delivers the Snowfall, the top rope elbow drop. Snow covers, and gets the three count, earning a shot at the still-injured VL, and the Hardcore Title. Result: Robert Jensen by Pinfall Grand Theft Auto A car screeched out of the arena at over seventy miles per hour. A camera caught the glimpse of whose car it was, and it closely resembled the Dodge Viper owned by the World's Greatest Athlete, Perfect. As a backstage worker walked over to examine the skid marks, Chris Finn walked by, gathering paperwork as he went. The backstage worker asked Finn if he saw who it was. Finn nodded... and said "Strangler. He's gone nuts... and well, he just accomodated Scott Slugger's car." The backstage worker nodded, not knowing what to say. Finally, he looked at Finn and said "Oh yeah... Mike Bear wants to meet with you." Finn replied... "About what?" Answering the question, he responded with... "Well, he mentioned something about CyberSlam.. and killing Wretch... god, he's gone nuts." Finn replied in agreement. "Everyone has. I'm not sure what has 'caused all this... but hey, it's going to be a wild thirty-two days to CyberSlam... well, thanks for passing that along." The backstage worker nodded, heading off in an opposite direction... before Finn mumbled to nobody in particular... "Damn Bear... I wonder what has gotten into that nut." Fallen vs. Erik Kelly "One" starts up, and the crowd gives a mixed but loud reaction as Fallen, accompanied by Ric Chronos, stalks to the ring. Fallen silently rolls in under the bottom rope before pulling himself to his feet, while Chronos elects to stay on the outside to second Fallen. "No Leaf Clover" starts up, and the crowd reacts with a loud pop for (near) hometown hero and fWo newcomer Erik Kelly, who storms down to the ring while his valet Pamela Dee (dressed in a very revealing evening dress) follows behind him. The monstrous Kelly mounts the apron and steps over the top rope, before charging towards Fallen and taking him down with a hellish clothesline as the bell rings. Kelly quickly brings Fallen up and sends him into the ropes, before shaking his spine with a big powerslam. Kelly quickly hooks a leg, but Fallen kicks out at two. Kelly brings Fallen up again, and sends him into the ropes before catching him with a big boot. Kelly once more brings Fallen to his feet, and hooks him up from behind, before slamming him down to the mat with a pumphandle slam. Kelly is pumped up, and takes the opportunity to yell at Chronos at ringside. Chronos hops onto the apron to get into Kelly's face, and the ref attempts to separate the two, not noticing Fallen coming from behind Kelly with a low blow. Fallen quickly spins the larger man around and hits him with an inverted atomic drop, keeping him doubled over. Fallen quickly takes Kelly down with a russian legsweep, and covers. Kelly powers out of the cover at one, and Fallen brings him up once more. He hooks the head of Kelly, and spikes him with a hard DDT. Knowing it'll take more than this to put Kelly away, Fallen brings him up once more and throws him across the ring into the corner. Fallen quickly heads into the corner and unleashes his full fury on Kelly, lashing out at him with rights and lefts in a rapid-fire manner. Finally Kelly has had enough and shoves Fallen away, but Fallen rolls up onto his feet and charges into the corner with a leaping forearm that catches Kelly on the jaw. Kelly staggers out of the corner, and Fallen whips him into the ropes, catching him coming off with a rolling elbowsmash. Kelly hits the mat, and Fallen quickly covers and hooks a leg. The referee makes the count, but Kelly kicks out again at one. Fallen brings the larger man up and attempts to take him down with a piledriver, but the strength of Kelly is too great and he back body drops Fallen over. Fallen quickly is up and dropkicks Kelly, sending him back into the ropes. Fallen charges at him and slams his knee into Kelly's stomach, before hooking him up and whipping him across the ring, following him in with another knee to the stomach. Fallen attempts to do this once more, but Kelly reverses the whip and catches Fallen coming off with a sort of spinebuster/bearhug front slam. Kelly quickly brings Fallen up again and sends him into the ropes, before taking him down with a tilt-a-whirl sideslam. Kelly brings Fallen up again and whips him into the corner, but Fallen reverses it and sends Kelly in. Fallen charges in, going for a leaping body attack, but Kelly catches him in mid-air by the throat, before driving him down to the mat with a hard chokeslam. Kelly quickly brings Fallen up again and positions him, and shouts "POWERBOMB!" to the crowd. Kelly then lifts Fallen up and drives him nearly through the mat with his Eradication finisher. Kelly quickly hooks the leg and the referee registers the three-count. Ric Chronos immediately hits the ring as the ref raises Kelly's hand, and helps Fallen to his feet. Kelly sees this out of the corner of his eye and turns to stare down the duo, who stare right back at him. After a moment, the standoff ends and Kelly rolls from the ring to join Pamela Dee in walking to the back, victorious. Result: Erik Kelly by Pinfall CyberSlam Chris Finn entered his office, and much to his shagrin James Armstrong was standing in a corner, snooping around. Finn's entrance caught Armstrong offguard, and Armstrong couldn't think of any other way to reply but "Damn catering! I was wondering if you had any... er... bun cake. The nutjobs who booked the caterers for tonight didn't order any bun cake. Yeah... bun cake..." Finn smirked, before reminding Armstrong that it was the two of them who booked the caterers for tonight. Armstrong sighed, before taking a seat, as Finn sat behind his desk. The two spoke for awhile, about stuff leading up to CyberSlam, before Finn asked... "Hey, did you see Strangler steal Slugger's car?" Armstrong nodded, and mentioned that it served that punk right. "I still don't understand what happened. One minute we're friends... he's playing the Shane to my Vince... the Luger to my Flair... the Russo to my Bischoff... and the next minute..." Before Armstrong could continue, Mike Bear entered the room. Finn started things off... "Hey, I don't want any trouble for teaming you with Havok... I know he's an egotist, but you..." "That isn't my concern, Finn. My concern is CyberSlam. You know what I want. Book it." Bear responded. Finn nodded, saying it was already a done deal. Bear thanked him, before shooting a glare in Armstrong' general direction. Bear exited the room, and Finn sighed. Armstrong looked over... "Good thing we got that taken care of. You never know when that nutball will try and whack someone's head off... or actually try to screw someone in the head..." Finn nodded, before the scene faded and Bear prepared to go team with Havok. Mike Bear & Havok vs. Black Panther & Wretch "Dragula" signals the arrival of Mike Bear, who powerwalks to the ring, getting a good pop and looking to be all business. He quickly rolls in and awaits his partner Havok, who enters to the sounds of cheers and "When Worlds Collide", before heading to the ring and leaping over the top and in. "I'm With Stupid" starts up, and the crowd begins to boo as Wretch makes his way out onto the entrance ramp, looking arrogant and repeatedly gesturing to himself. He waits on the top of the entrance ramp as "Killed The Cat" begin playing, and the Black Panther enters to a shower of boos. Both men soak up the negative reaction, before quickly heading to the ring. They roll in, and the referee quickly separates the two teams before anything can happen. The bell then rings, and the ref lets them go. Black Panther and Mike Bear start out for their teams, and quickly lock up. Panther shows his superior power by shoving Bear hard into the corner, and Bear eyes him warily from the corner. Panther charges in, going for a lariat, but Bear moves and Panther strikes the turnbuckles. Bear quickly hooks him up from behind and takes him down on the back of his head with a belly-to-back suplex. Panther gets back up to his feet, and is met by a quick enzuiguri to the head. Bear quickly covers, but Panther kicks out at two. Bear brings Panther back up, and strikes him with a stiff forearm right to the mask-covered nose, which sends Panther back into the ropes. Bear quickly whips him across, and catches him with a ring-rocking powerslam. Instead of covering, Bear brings Panther up again, and attempts to whip him into the corner. However, the Panther reverses the whip and sends Bear in, and charges after him. Bear grabs the top rope on his way in and uses his momentum to lift himself up and over the charging Panther, before twisting in mid-air and rolling up the Panther in a version of a sunset flip. The ref's count reaches two before Wretch hits the ring to break up the count. As the ref escorts Wretch back to his corner, Bear heads to his and tags in Havok, who climbs onto the top rope, waiting for Panther to get up. As he does so, Havok leaps off with a cross-body block, but Panther catches him in mid-air and slams him down to the mat. Panther quickly tags in Wretch, who mounts the top rope and flies off, nailing an elbowdrop to the throat of Havok. Wretch covers, but Havok kicks out at the last moment. Wretch brings Havok up to his feet, and hits a dropkick which sends Havok into the corner. Wretch backs up a few steps, and charges into the corner, nailing a huge leaping body splash. Havok collapses out of the corner, and Wretch runs into the ropes, before leaping off the second strand with an Asai moonsault that hits its target perfectly. Instead of covering, Wretch tags in the Black Panther, and they both bring Havok to his feet. Panther holds Havok, and Wretch bounces off the ropes, going for a spinning heel kick. However, Havok slips out of the Panther's grasp and Wretch nails his first-time partner, knocking him down. Wretch quickly charges after Havok, but Havok eludes his grasp and makes a diving tag to partner Mike Bear, who rushes the ring, smashing his former partner Wretch with a stiff clothesline. As the Panther gets to his feet, Bear takes him back down with a superkick. Bear quickly covers Panther, but Wretch manages to break it at two. Bear quickly gets up and blocks a punch from Wretch, before clotheslining him up and over the top rope. Bear quickly scoops up the Panther, before taking him down with a tombstone piledriver. In their corner, Havok wants to be the one who makes the pin on his ex-friend, so Bear tags in his semi-recovered partner. As Bear heads out to the apron, Wretch nails him from ringside with a chair, out of the sight of the referee and Havok, who is heading to the top for a moonsault. Wretch quickly leaps onto the apron and shakes the ropes, causing Havok to crotch himself on the top turnbuckle. Wretch quickly mounts the top rope and takes Havok down with an incredible top-rope Depression. Inside the ring, the Black Panther quickly rolls over and onto Havok, and the ref tolls the three-count. The victorious Panther and Wretch quickly head to the back, arms raised high in tainted victory. Result: Black Panther & Wretch by Pinfall Mitch Wilson vs. Scott Collins The fans inside of the sold-out Centrum rose to their feet, in anticipation of the Warfare main event. With the fans on their feet, Manowar's "Burning" filled the arena, and out walked Scott Collins, a man who has had recent problems with the FTfWo. Collins steps into the ring, and prepares mentally for his Internet Title shot as a red light comes over the Centrum, and "Calling All Stations" began to play, bringing out "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson, clad with Internet Title. Wilson lets the red light radiate upon his body, before stepping into the ring. Wilson hands over the title belt, and the bell rings. Immediately, the two men begin circiling each other, which is a common part of the feeling out process. After a few seconds of this, the men lock up, and Wilson takes Collins into an armbar. Collins reverses it into a release german suplex, but Wilson lands on his feet. Collins turns around, and Wilson goes for a clothesline. Collins ducks under, and comes off of the ropes with a flying forearm. It connects, and both men pop up. Collins takes Wilson over with a japanese armdrag, and he holds onto it in an armbar. Wilson reverses, and gets behind Collins, wrenching the arm back, while having a boot placed right on Collins' back. Eventually Wilson gives up on this, and gets a better idea. With Collins lying on the mat, he hooks his legs within Collins', leans back, and executes a surfboard. Collins' back begins to feel the pain, however, Wilson can no longer hold Collins up there, so he releases the hold. Wilson picks Collins up by the hair, and takes him over with a snap suplex. Wilson covers, but Collins gets a shoulder up at 2. Wilson lifts Collins up, and sends him into the ropes. Collins ducks a shortarm clothesline, and responds with a boot to the gut. The stronger Collins lifts Wilson up and takes him down with a sitdown crucifix powerbomb. Collins' pinning combination is kicked out of at two, so he goes back to the drawing board. He sends Wilson into the ropes, and catches him in a tilt-a-whirl backbracker. Another cover by Collins is escaped by Wilson, and Collins takes out his frustration by simply throwing Wilson to the outside. Collins gives chase, and whips Wilson into the guardrail. With Wilson stunned, Collins takes out more frustration by lifting up the stairs. Collins runs at Wilson with the stairs in hand, but Wilson gets a foot up, knocking the steps into Collins' face. Wilson throws the stairs to the side, and rolls Collins back into the ring. He nails a few forearms to the head of the Riders leader, and eventually backs Collins into the corner. Wilson nails a backhand chop to Collins' chest, and Collins responds with a fronthand to Wilson's chest. Wilson makes like he is going to respond with another backhand, but instead he slaps Collins right into the face. Wilson begins backing up, and Collins walks towards him. Wilson backs up all the way to the other corner, where he suckers Collins in, and a drop toe hold brings Collins' face right to the bottom turnbuckle. Wilson taunts the crowd by pointing to his head before placing Collins on the turnbuckle. Wilson steps outside of the ring, and climbs up. He lifts Collins up, and comes off with a super brainbuster. Wilson taunts the crowd, and goes for a cover. Collins kicks out at 2 and a half, proving that Wilson's cover was done too hastily. Wilson perches him on the turnbuckle once again. Wilson tries to lift him over with a superplex, but Collins blocks it with a few punches to the stomach. Collins stands up on the turnbuckles, and hooks Wilson's head between his legs. A super-powerbomb does the trick, and Collins makes the cover. One, two, but Wilson gets a shoulder up once again at two. At this point, we turn our attention to the entrance ramp, where we see Perfect stroll out, chair in hand. Back in the ring, Collins runs at a groggy Wilson, and plants him with the Scotty Dropper. However, Perfect sneaks to ringside, hiding the chair behind his back. Collins notices this, and he and Perfect exhange words. Perfect then smacks Collins with the chair. Collins stumbles back, right into an awaiting Wilson, who plants him with the Inverted Russian Legsweep. Wilson covers, and a three count later, he retains his Internet Title. Warfare appears to be ending, as we see the image of Wilson holding up the Internet Title, alongside Perfect, mocking Scott Collins... but... on the Warfare screen... a picture that could not be described appeared. Result: Mitch Wilson by pinfall Aftermath Dead stood triumphantly over the graveyard, drinking a damn fine bottle of Chardonay, before tossing the bottle to the side. He prepared to climb into the bulldozer... one has to wonder why Dead didn't begin digging the bodies up earlier in the night. However, a loud screeching sound echoed throughout Worcester, before the camera focused in on a Dodge Viper. Out from it stepped not Perfect as Dead was expecting... but the Boston Strangler. Dead saw him and the two men who would face at CyberSlam 2000 for the fWo World Title came to blows, exchanging in a deadly game of fisticuffs in a cemetery. Dead threw Strangler into the bulldozer, before he gained the advantage.. pounding away at the Strangler from a mounted position. Strangler found the bottle of Chardonay, before cracking it over the head of Dead. Crimson began to slowly trickle near Dead's eyebrow down his face... it was a small cut, but a cut nonetheless... and the blood took Dead's eyesight away temporarily. Strangler fought him off, before they fought out of the cemetery, and near a bridge nearby. They continued fighting... back and forth... before suddenly... Strangler went for a clothesline... and he hit it. But the momentum sent both men up and over, and all we heard was a splash that echoed for miles and miles. The two men disappeared into the murky waters... and Warfare faded to black.
