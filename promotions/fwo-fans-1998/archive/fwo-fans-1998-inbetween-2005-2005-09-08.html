Introduction Once upon a time there was a small promotion, formed by wrestlers and people who loved the profession. It had one primary goal: to entertain and enlighten the fans. It was named the Fans Wrestling Organization for this reason; to reflect the value of the fan. To reflect how much the workers valued the people who came to the arena and who spent their time and money to watch these athletic specimens perform. This was the Fans Wrestling Organization... and it grew into a powerhouse in the professional wrestling and entertainment world. After some time, it was aquired by the Walt Disney Corporation, and after a time of adjustment, it grew bigger and better than ever. Loyal fans flocked and filled the arenas... and while the performers came and went, the story and the promotion lived on. This was the story of the fWo, the story everyone knew on the surface. But under the surface all is not well. 114:08:45:00... Lately I've been looking at myself, in the shirt, in the mirror and I've been asking myself why it isn't me in the ring. The human body can withstand amazing punishment. While some say that life is fragile and easily broken I know better because we all know better. Ten hits to the face, blood broken and skin sloughed from the face, my eyesight gone crimson from nerve capsules bursting and flooding the pupil with viscous, red fluid. But with enough concentration the knees can hold, fists can remain steady and I can stand and look my opponent directly in the face. Is that all? When I see them, on television or lately in person, as I've been traveling with the promotion for some time... I find myself bored by the inevitable. Which is that sooner or later one of them will lay down, not stand up, and the bell will ring. It isn't a symbol of victory for the winner, it's a sound of cowardice for the loser. Because the body can take it. Take much more, so much more that only the complete shattering of the ribcage, heart and mind, to go cold, dead in the ring would be valid. That's why I find myself thinking, why isn't it me in the ring? Then I remember that the ring is a shell, just the focal point. But the focal point for now. The cameras are going to turn sooner or later, and then I will have my chance to stand and show what standing means. This isn't about toughness, it's about certainty. My shirt has almost gone gray with use. Black and taunt once upon a time, my skin has been roughened, bruised and battered. Too many fights, too many chances to stand. On the center, the white circle and the F. This is our uniform, and I see more every night. I haven't been to my job in sixteen days. I'm sure by now I wouldn't be allowed in. The strange thing is, with enough fury and beating, with enough slow rises to your feet the world becomes an ocean of sound. Life is muted, like you are underwater, but before the pressure cracks you the light comes on and you're completely aware that this time it's you who are free and it's everyone else moving in slow motion.
