fWo: In-between, Part Three (August 2002)

23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization fWo: In-between, Part Three In which we meet kids with too much time on their hands "So in conclusion, not only do I find Man U to be a superior soccer team, I also find Ginger to be a sub-par actress at best, with the work of Savannah still being superior in nearly all forums, at least when the budget was in the right place and she was allowed to work with the flexibility that benefits an actress of her caliber. There. Send." Lights were dim and the blue haze of the computer screen lit the basement with the healthy glow that one associated with college and late-night Dungeons and Dragons sessions. The boy sitting in front of the computer screen didn't mind, and if the low sounds of the Ramones coming from his headphones bothered his on-screen concentration he didn't say anything. On the table in front of him were the relics of the computer junkie: wrappers of candy bars, a bag of half-eaten Cheedos, several toys of his favorite Simpsons characters and a pile of papers and bills. He typed furiously, a small grin crept around his face and his hair neatly tucked beneath the baseball cap he wore on his head. The faded logo of Mr. Cranky's film review site was centered on his hat, facing backwards for maximum visibility as he surfed and scanned the 'net. Just another night of adventure in the home of the computer junkie. Sounds of footsteps on stairs announced the arrival of his roommate, led by the small terrier who had detected the smell of the Cheedos and was moving in with a glazed, interested and drooling stare. The collar jingled, and the silver tag reading "Tim" clattered against hairy legs. The boy dropped the bag of Cheedos to the floor and Tim dove in, at once dusting his muzzle with the orange powder that was the signature of the snack. Content, the terrier barked happily and curled up next to the boy's feet to rest. "You're still online? Jesus... your skin is going paler than it was. Don't you think you should lay off it a little?" The boy waved a hand dismissively. "Nonsense... I haven't been on that long. Besides, I've just got two... no C... I mean, three sites left to go. I bookmark the message boards so I can get around them faster. You know, if I don't show up even for a couple days these people forget about me. That's the internet for you, everyone is so fickle. But I'm getting there, my name is getting out there." His roommate rolled his eyes. "Yeah. Getting your name out there. A handful of other people with no lives will remember you right up until the next Star Trek movie comes out and they leave their little bat caves in search of tickets and Big Gulps. Yeah, there's a legacy..." But the boy wasn't paying attention, and his roommate leaned over to look at the screen. "Who is this JDW and why are you so angry at him?" "Why?" The boy exploded. "He's a moron... he doesn't know anything about wrestling. He's a snob who just thinks he knows everything there is to know about the industry even though he's never worked in it. So I like to taunt him by coming online and bitching that over the hill wrestlers should be made champions. Right now I'm talking about how Barry Windham should be brought back... I've been using Barry for months. Anyway, it makes him so mad... it's great! You can also get this guy going by talking about what a lamer the Frenchie is." His roommate's attention had begun to wander as he scanned the room for signs of something more intelligent to talk with. He wished his assistant was still around... he missed her and she usually had good things to talk about. He was starting to settle on the couch as being a good alternative. "But I thought you liked the Frenchie..." The boy shrugged. "Well sure, I mean, the guy did do a lot for me. Anyway, it ain't that big a deal and I really don't care one way or another. It's just so cool to get online and get these people all frustrated. Anyway, only two more sites to do and then I'm done. Oh, and I'm going to hack into the fWo site again... really screw with people's heads." His roommate slumped down into a chair. "Jesus Christ... why in the hell would you want to do that? Hacking the fWo? Isn't that illegal?" "Ha!" The boy typed furiously on the computer, easily navigating the shoddy security system put into place on the fWo site. "You want illegal? How about a board who is illegitimate at best using their power to terminate the rightful fWo wrestlers from the active roster? So Kelly gets injured and a couple people talk about maybe the possibility of getting a wrestler union in place so the fWo can offer health care. You know, like every other business in America. Most of the fWo roster didn't even know the request was made. But this board... this joke of a board... just terminates everyone and brings up scabs and people who can't work for shit! Did you see the results of the house show on the internet? Lamers got crucified! You've got Samuel Smooth putting himself over one of the legitimate stars in the promotion, freaking Joseph being jobbed out in under a minute... this is a disaster. The sinking of the whole ship, man. That's no way to treat the fWo, and I'll tell you this right now, my father wouldn't have let this shit go down. Neither would Universal, but see what happens when you let businessmen run wrestling? God damn... so I'll just let them know that a few of us out there are watching them closely. Make them know that their shit isn't going down in private. So let's see... let's see... username 'thefwo'... Jesus, CWL copiers... password... password... ah. The password is 'password'. God, what a bunch of idiots. So I'll just send this file up here..." His roommate rolled his eyes and flicked on the television. "God, you really are a lamer Buckley. You know what?" Buckley took off his hat and shook out his red hair. The sweat mark from the hat was still visible on his forehead. "Hey, maybe so, but at least I never had some girl after me for like, what, three years and you never picked up on it? You're a lot more clueless than me, Tounma... and besides, you never got yourself one of these." Buckley lifted his shirt and patted the very dusty and very moldy cardboard and plastic replication of a title belt around his waist. The words "BONI" could be read in faint marker, and just below that "Property of Kid Cool" was scribbled in ink pen. Tounma seemed unimpressed, and went back to watching television. Smiling, Kid Cool went back to surfing the internet, and would continue until far too late at night. But hey, there were things to discuss all over the internet... and he certainly wasn't alone out there. Hell, most of the people he was talking to were certainly just like him.
