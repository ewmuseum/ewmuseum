Introduction "Jeebus, Eli... you look dead on your feet. Did you actually sleep ONCE this week?" Poison Ivy hasn't been in his presence an hour, and she's already raggin' on how he looks. Which is not to say she doesn't have reason - Eli's got dark circles under his eyes, he's unshowered, still wearing a mesh shirt and leather pants from the rave he and Angel went to the night before. He's there for one reason, one reason only... the fact that everyone is required to be at TV. "Not now, Ivy.... I need to find Universal, put in my five minutes, and collapse in a corner for a few hours." Eli opens the arena door for Ivy, letting her in, in front of him. She looks to be in decent spirits regardless of the tragedy she's just endured - and while she's promised to tell him the story over a cup of coffee at a later date, when he's a little more awake. And after she takes care of another pressing matter. There's still a little while before the show starts, and Ivy seems a tad impatient for her pressing matters. "Sean is off tonight, isn't he?" "I think so, but he'll be here. I'm gonna find Universal, but if I see him I'll tell him you're looking for him." Ivy turned and stopped him. "No, don't. I'll find him myself, I've had enough of this passing notes back and forth between classes sort'a thing." Eli laughs despite himself, and puts a friendly arm around Ivy's shoulders, squeezing hard. Maybe, he thinks, she'll finally do it. But their walk into the heart of the building is interrupted by a tapping on Eli's shoulder. "M-Mister Flair?" They both turn around, to see the fWo Cruiserweight Champion, Tempest, standing there with a guilty look on her face, but a small smile, to boot. "What can I do for ya, Tempest?" "I need to talk to you about something important... got a minute?" "I---guess so. Ivy?" "Go on," replied Ivy, "I'll catch up with you later. Eli and Tempest went off in one direction, leaving Ivy behind. She looked around for a moment, and, almost at random, picked a direction and headed off in it. Lance Knight vs. Jon Crisp The fans turned their attention to the rampway as "Elite" by the Deftones hit the arena PA system and Lance Knight stepped out from backstage, raising his arms into the air as pyrotechnics went off, as did the fans in attendance. Being showered with cheers from the crowd, Lance walked down the rampway towards the ring, and slapped hands with a few lucky fans. As Lance rolled into the ring, "Elite" was replaced by "High and Dry" by Radiohead played, the fans turning their cheers into boos as Jon Crisp stepped out from behind the curtain, a sneer on his face. He stepped down the rampway, being pelted with boos, as well as the occasional piece of garbage. Sneering at the fans, Jon eyed his opponent, before he climbed into the ring. As the bell rang, Lance crashed Jon Crisp with a hard forearm, sending him stumbling into the ropes. Lance then grabbed Jon, and whipped him across the ring. When Jon returned, Lance executed an head and arm suplex. Then, Lance went for the pin, knowing Crisp would kick out, and got a quick one count for his troubles. Lance shook his head, and lifted Crisp up. Crisp, however, did not appreciate the fact that Lance had been on the offensive, and hit Lance with a solid low blow. Then, Crisp took Lance into the corner with a series of hard neck chops that sent him flying backwards. The fans booed as every neck chop connected with Lance. Eventually, Crisp lifted his boot, attempting to choke the life out of Lance Knight. The referee began the disqualification count, and at four, Crisp got off. Crisp then pummeled Knight with a series of swift and brutal kicks to the abdomen region as the fans booed. The referee pulled Crisp away, and Crisp stared at the referee for a few seconds, before anger overtook him, and he threatened to attack the referee if he dared to touch him again. When Crisp turned around, Knight hit him with a hard uppercut, sending him stumbling back. Lance whipped Crisp into the ropes, and then grabbed him, lifted him up and dropped him onto his neck and shoulders, and therefore completed a Tiger Suplex without an upper bridge. Lance then got up, and lifted Crisp again, whipped him into the ropes, and once again executed a Tiger Suplex without an upper bridge. The fans cheered as Lance went over to the prone form of Crisp, and put a foot onto Crisps' chest, attempting to embarrass him. Lance got a quick two, but Crisp managed to lift an arm up, ending the pinfall as the fans began to boo. Lance grabbed Crisp, and lifted him up. He then whipped Crisp into the ropes, attempting for a third Tiger Suplex, but it never happened. Instead, Crisp propelled himself off the ropes, and executed a flying lariat, taking both himself and Lance down at the fans began to boo. The referee began the knock out count, waiting to see which of these men were fit to continue, and which were out of it. At the count of six, Crisp crawled over, and draped an arm onto Lance, only getting a one count for his efforts. Crisp, angry that Lance was able to kick out so quickly, lifted Lance's head up, and locked on a Dragon Sleeper as the fans began to boo. Crisp applied more and more pressure to the maneuver, hoping to make Lance submit in one of the submission moves he knew. Lance waved his arm, but refused to tap out, and got weaker and weaker by the second as the fans booed. Suddenly, Lance went limp as the boos from the crowd continued, and Crisp smiled. The referee checked Lance, and lifted his arm once, seeing it drop. He lifted the arm again, and saw it fall once more. However, on the final time, the arm did not drop, and the fans in attendance went wild as Lance got the strength to get up, and elbowed Crisp in the gut several times. Once Crisp let go of Lance, Lance spun around and quickly kicked Crisp in the gut. He then grabbed Crisp's head, and drove it into the mat with a powerful DDT. The fans cheered as Lance stomped around the ring a bit, charging himself up for the final offensive against Crisp. Lance stared at Crisp, and smiled. He walked over to Crisp, and lifted him up by his head. Then, he hooked Crisp up and stared at the fans for a second. Lance then nodded, and lifted Crisp up, and executed the Knightfall, his finisher which was a Waterfall Suplex. The fans cheered insanely as both men fell down to the mat, the fans going wild, as the referee counted both out. At the count of six, Lance crawled over, and weakly covered Crisp. The fans counted as the referee's hand fell down three times, and the bell rang. "Elite" hit the PA system once again, as Lance had scored a victory over the veteran superstar, Jon Crisp. Though Crisp put up a valiant effort, Lance was the one who managed to roll out of the ring, his arm raised in victory by the referee. Things looked good for Lance Knight. Just as soon as they wished for something to happen, "Jump Around" by House of Pain cued up, and the fans exploded into cheers as Wretch stepped out from behind the curtains, smiling at the fans in attendance. He walked down the rampway slapping the hands of some lucky fans. As soon as Wretch entered the ring, however, his mind was set for the moment on his upcoming match. As "Jump Around" faded out and the lights faded out as "Everything's Cool" by Pop Will Eat Itself hit the PA system. As soon as the lights were off, they were back on, and a surprised Wretch was met from behind with a large double axehandle by the mammoth monster Grendel, sending Wretch crashing to the mat as the fans booed the cheap shot. Grendel growled at the fans, lifted up Wretch, and quickly took down Wretch with a hard neck chop. The fans booed Grendel even more as he circled Wretch, motioned for Wretch to get up, as the fans tried yelling at Wretch not to get up in Grendel's direction. However, Wretch had been knocked senseless, and turned towards Grendel's direction as the monster raced towards Wretch, attempting to spear him. At the last second, however, Wretch stumbled out of the way, allowing the mammoth Grendel to fly into the turnbuckle as the fans cheered Wretch. Wretch, though was ready for this, and reversed the move into an enzuiguri, sending the giant falling down to the mat. Wretch saw Grendel was down, and smiled. He did a quick look in both directions, making sure that no one was at the ropes, ready to attack him again, and when he saw that, he raced to the ropes. He got to one end, and whipped towards the other end, only to spot Grendel being up and fully awake. The beast sneered at Wretch as Wretch headed towards him. He was not a man to take lightly, no matter how goofy and stupid he acted like, and Grendel quickly lifted up Wretch. Wretch suddenly sprung into action, and he executed a thumb in the eye of Grendel. Grendel, who was known for almost obsessive cheating in matches, had himself just had a cheat shot performed on him. Grendel tried to clear up the temporary blindness in his eye as Wretch climbed the turnbuckle. When Grendel turned in the direction of Wretch, he was caught off guard by a jumping swinging DDT. The fans exploded with cheers as Wretch drove Grendel into the mat with a swinging DDT, known commonly as the 'Tornado DDT'. Wretch climbed up the turnbuckle, and stared down at Grendel. He measured Grendel, before he launched off with a Shooting Star Splash as the fans exploded with cheers. Wretch connected with the unmoving form of Grendel, and hooked the leg for the pin. At two, Grendel kicked out as the fans booed Grendel for getting out of the pin. Wretch stared at Grendel in shock, before he shook his head, and lifted Grendel, who hit his own low blow on Wretch, a shot to the groin. Grendel whipped Wretch into the ropes, and when Wretch returned, he belly to back suplexed Wretch to the mat. Grendel then placed a foot on Wretch's chest, trying to embarrass him. Wretch powered out an arm at two, however, and the fans cheered Wretch on, as Grendel shook his head in disgust and anger. He began to choke the life out of Wretch as the referee began the count to eventually disqualify him, but Grendel let go at four, and lifted up Wretch. He grabbed Wretch by the throat, and attempted to choke slam him to the mat, but fate intervened. Wretch managed to hit the mammoth in his gut with a hard elbow, allowing Wretch the chance to escape Grendel's wrath for now. Wretch hit another thumb to the eye, making Grendel stumble back in pain. Wretch then climbed the turnbuckle, and the fans cheered as Grendel stumbled blindly into the directly was in, and Wretch was ready to deliver a surprise. When Grendel was close enough, Wretch launched off the turnbuckle with a big missile dropkick, crashing into Grendel's face, and sent the big monster down. Wretch then ran to the ropes again, hoping to execute his finisher, the Rating Savers Legdrop. However, fate once again conspired against Wretch as Grendel managed to trip Wretch as he came back. Grendel got up, and locked on an ankle lock as the fans began to boo. Grendel applied more pressure to the move as Wretch gritted his teeth in pain, and moved closer and closer to the ropes. However, Grendel pulled he back, and applied more pressure. What Grendel didn't notice, however, was the fact that he was touching the rope on the other side of the ring. The referee broke up the ankle lock hold, as Grendel was unfairly using the ropes for his own advantage. Grendel began to yell at the referee as Wretch weakly grabbed Grendel from behind, schoolboyed him for a quick two count before the mammoth of a man kicked out. The fans booed as Grendel stood up, infuriated at Wretch, and stared down at Wretch. Grendel lifted up Wretch, and whipped him quickly into the ropes. When Wretch returned, Grendel executed a gutbuster onto Wretch, and hooked the leg. Wretch kicked out at two, and Grendel began to yell at the referee again, not noticing Wretch getting up. Wretch measured Grendel quickly, and executing a drop kick to the back of Grendel's knee's, sending Grendel crashing down to the mat. Wretch then climbed the turnbuckle, and measured Grendel, preparing to launch off with another aerial manuever. However, Grendel, in a slightly out of whack state, pulled on the top rope weakly, sending Wretch falling onto his crotch in pain, as the fans began to boo. Grendel shrugged off the daze, and saw Wretch defenseless, and smiled, as he climbed the turnbuckle. Grendel wrapped his arms around Wretch, and used all of his momentum and force to lift Wretch up into the air, making him crash onto the mat with a hard superplex. The Art of Self Destruction, Phase IX (External, I) The parking lot was darkening already, the streetlights beginning to hum as they flickered on in the brisk evening air. Headlights spanned across the crowded lot, throwing contorted shadows against the vacant cars, and a checkered taxi pulled up to the back door of the arena. The door swung open, and Mufanga Kagos climbed out of the car's backseat, a tall black man dressed in jeans and a heavy sweater, his long dreadlocks tied back into a loose ponytail. He used to love coming in the front door of the arenas, meeting the fans as they pushed against the red velvet ropes, signing autographs, shaking hands, posing for photos. But that was before Bodycount, and that was back when he still considered himself worthy of the attention. At Bodycount, he was eliminated by the Boston Strangler, and never eliminated a single wrestler before he was sent packing. It was an utter disaster, and he had no one to blame but himself. He had a chance to join this company and go to CyberSlam to face the champion all with the first few months, and all he had to do was win the Body Count Battle Royal. One match, and he would be an instant legend. He could be another Slugger. Another Strangler. Another Creed. But now, here he was slinking in the back door of the arena like a coward, afraid to face the crowds. Mufanga Kagos needed redemption. He needed a quick fix of glory. Something to erase the utter failure he suffered at Bodycount. A quick win to turn some heads and make people realize that this young Kaogs character was more than a freak in a loincloth. A quick win to show that he deserved this chance in the fWo... He needed a match, and he needed a match with some weight. Pushing through the glass doors of the arena, Mufanga was deep in thought about this much-needed match as he stepped in the bustling action of fWo backstage activity. Donaven Winters stalked slowly around the corner as Kagos bursted into his path. Both men fell to the ground from the collision. Winters gave the newcomer a disinterested look before getting up and trying to walk away. Kagos spoke first, grabbing Winters by the arm. "Sorry about that, Donaven." Winters stared at Kagos. He was staring a hole, right through his head. "Watch where the hell you walk." Kagos frowned, "I said I was sorry... sorry." Winters mouth turned into a sinister smile.
