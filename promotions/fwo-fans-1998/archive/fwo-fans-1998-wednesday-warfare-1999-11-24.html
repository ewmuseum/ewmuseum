Wednesday Warfare, 24 November 1999

24 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Primetime exited the limousine and walked with a heavy duffel bag in one hand and the fWo World Heavyweight Championship in the other. Next to him walked the Flying Frenchie, holding the fWo Womens Title next to his waist and waving off attendants and security as he went. "Non... non... non autographs for any 'o zu slobs... non... I am in ze hurry." fWo personnel watched them pass silently, trying to stay out of the French Foundation's way as they walked. They were approached by Jake and James Armstrong, with the later holding the former by the ear. "You've got to watch where you leave your fat boy Primetime... I caught him eating food out of my locker room. Let it happen again and I won't keep letting you du... I mean, wrestle easy matches. Now listen... both of you. I'm showing this young lady around the backstage area... got me? She's a reporter, and she's doing a story on professional wrestling... and tonight I'm going to see if I can't take her into my dressing room and show her me sleeper hold if you know what I mean..." The Frenchie and Primetime exchanged a quick glance. They understood what Armstrong meant, but it was a safe bet that Armstrong didn't. Armstrong continued. "Listen... you two do me a favor and run things back here tonight, okay? I haven't been able to get hold of Scott Slugger all night and after him... er... I mean of course I thought of you two first." Armstrong patted the Frenchie and Primetime on the back and wandered away from them. Shaking their heads in amused disgust, Primetime and the Frenchie continued forward to their dressing rooms. Primetime entered his, and didn't notice the shadowy figure lurking just inside the room. Primetime walked over to put his bag down, and the figure slammed the door shut, locking it and trapping the both of them inside. Primetime wheeled and faced the intruder. "You..." Chapter One: Threats and challenges... Chris Universal, Scott Slugger and Ice walked down the arena corridors, baseball bats in hand. Scott Slugger tapped the walls with the bat, a grim expression on his face. He yelled as he walked, letting his voice echo down the hallways. "COLLINS! Come out you miserable bastard! You think you can injure one of our own? You think the Dynasty is going to let you get away with it?" Coming the opposite direction, the US Marshal had the misfortune of wandering into their general area while reading the booking sheet for the night. "Hiroshima? Oh man..." But before he could get any farther he bumped into Slugger, and looked up with first annoyance and then a mixture of apology and concern. Slugger didn't hesitate as he swung with the bat, knocking the Marshal against the wall. His head struck the steel with a hard smack as he connected with the Frenchie's dressing room door. The Frenchman wandered out into the hallways to see what the commotion was and spotted the Dynasty. "Zut... trouble! Prim'time! Prim'time! Come out of ze room quick!" There was no answer, and the Frenchie tried the door handle. Discovering it locked, the Frenchie stepped back and charged the door, knocking it down and barging into the room. There he spotted Courage, choking Primetime out with an unturned chair. "You steal what was mine? I'll get you for this punk... I'll shove that title down your throat!" The Frenchie grabbed Courage and pulled him free, and the intruder backed out of the room with a snarl. Back in the hallway Courage spotted the Dynasty, and blew them off with a wave of his hands. Ignoring the insult for the moment, the Dynasty picked up the booking sheet that the Marshal had dropped. Universal read slowly, "Collins is in the second to last match of the night... but I can fix that... after all, money talks." Universal produced a handful of bills and went off in search of Frankton. Slugger crumpled the sheet up in one fist. "You're not leaving here alive, Collins..." They stomped off, leaving the Marshal on the floor, rubbing his head with a strange gleam in his eye... Waru vs. Hiroshima The lights dimmed, and a red spotlight shone on the top of the ramp. Hiroshima walked to the ring, eying the fans in attendance with contempt. Inside the ring he raised his mighty fists in the air, and screamed out at the fans. After some delay, Waru was shoved out onto the entrance ramp, obviously a substitute for the Marshal, injured at the hands of the Dynasty. Waru walked slowly to the ring, looking annoyed that he had to be there at all. Picking up a microphone, Waru pointed out at the crowd. "Why am I here? Answer me this now, because I'm tired of trying to figure it out! In Japan I was one of the most respected wrestlers in the world. The world! But you Americans and you fWo groupies just want your sports entertainment crap... and I'm sick of trying to please you freaks. First I had a mask... but that wasn't good enough because people can't get over in masks they say. Then I am given a stupid angle where my stupid hair is stolen by a cruiserweight who has no business even cleaning up a wrestling ring! Then I go hardcore, but I get injured by one of your sloppy American so-called workers! What about work rate? Wrestling moves? Psychology? The time honored tradition set forth by Misawa~! The stiff fighting of Kawada~! The swanky moves of Wild Pegasus~! The fWo is like a giant flaming BFI truck of suck... and I'm not going to take it anymore, so screw off!" Waru made as if to leave, but Hiroshima grabbed him from his hair and yanked him into the ring. Waru shouted at Hiroshima that he wasn't wrestling, but Hiroshima responded with a punch to the face. Waru reeled, and Hiroshima grabbed his neck and delivered a mighty choke slam. Waru dazed, Hiroshima covered him for the three count. Hiroshima screamed at the crowd as Waru lay prone, and the cameras quickly zipped backstage for a look at the Warfare Girl tryouts... Result: Hiroshima by Pinfall Chapter Two: Girls and Guts Camera footage from the last few weeks of the tryouts were shown in video montage. The excitement of becoming a Warfare Girl were in the eyes of young "Trout" as she explained that being a Warfare Girl would help her feed the homeless of Chicago though dance and song. Nobody could forget the spunky "Bazookas" and her impressionistic dance to honor the memories of the fallen veterans who died in the Faulkland Islands. Or what of "Beaver" and her quest to climb all of the great mountains of the Eastern United States to raise awareness of the horrors of penal implant surgery gone awry. Perhaps most dramatic where the shots of "Fuzzy Clam" at her local church, helping senior citizens to once again protest the evils of network television. A few of the shots in the montage featured a less cheerful girl, dressed all in black with an angry frown on her face. Her arm and fist swung at the camera, and the video went dark for a few moments before it resumed with some of the other Warfare Girl hopefuls. At the end of the video montage, a reminder was placed for all the people at fWowrestling.com to vote for their favorite Warfare Girl in the Fan Zone... Primetime (c) vs. Death The lights dim once again tonight, as a scratchy "4" appears on the TravisTron. Scotty Collins, Death, strides towards the ring with his Hardcore Title strapped around his waist. He's visibly anxious about this opportunity to regain the title he lost to Ultra-Violet five months ago, and goes over to the announcing table to yell at Chris Finn. Death wondered why his match was suddenly bumped up on the card, and grabbed Finn by the neck as if to slam him. Just as he was about to strike Finn for the indignity, "Bawitdaba" brings out the fWo World Heavyweight Champion, all by his lonesome, and limping slightly. In the ring, he stands proud with the title hoisted high above his head. He kisses the belt, then hands it off to the referee, who displays it for the crowd. Death enters the ring, and the match starts. The lock up in a test of strength, and Death takes control, bridging Primetime to the mat. The ref drops to the mat to count, but Primetime gets out of the predicament with a monkey flip on Death. Both men get back up, and Death charges Primetime with a spear, which PT counters with a DDT. PT drapes his arm over the Hardcore champ, and gets two before Death kicks out. Primetime lifts Death for a running shoulderbreaker, and connects with that move. Death rolls to the outside for a breather, but Primetime catches him with a baseball slide. On the outside, Primetime scoops Death up and drops him sternum first no the apron. PT kicks at the challenger, then returns to the ring, taunting the Boston fans. He enjoys this immensely; so much, in fact, that he doesn't notice when Death staggers back into the ring. Death spins around Primetime and locks in a small package, nearly getting three before Primetime kicks out. Primetime springs to his feet, bewildered, and shoves Death to the mat. Collins rolls back to his feet and hits a fisherman's suplex, earning another near-fall. Primetime breaks out and rolls to the corner in an attempt to break Death's momentum. It doesn't, however, as Death starts pounding away at the cornered World Champion. With Primetime properly dazed, Death lifts him to the top turnbuckle, and prepares to hit a top-rope Death Penalty. The Dynasty rushes the ring before it can happen, though, possibly saving Primetime from the same fate that Lance Star suffered. In the process, they do give Death the match by disqualification, a fact that makes Scott Slugger even angrier as he shouts, "Ahead of me? You?". While the Dynasty beat on Death, Primetime tries to escape through the crowd, only to be accosted once again by an irate Courage. Courage screams at Primetime about stealing his fat man, while war ensues inside the ring, where Chaos and Stone have joined the battle. Security valiantly try to hit the area to break things up, but only succeed in keeping War and Plague restrained. Laughing, the Dynasty pull Death away with them, promising evil toward the leader of the Dark Riders... Result: Scotty Cool by DQ Chapter Three: Mixed Signals Somewhere deep within the bowels of the arena James Armstrong pressed the "play" button on a home stereo that was piled atop some crates. Barry White floated out of it, and James Armstrong grabbed two Styrofoam cups molded in the form of wine glasses and filled them with punch. His shirt was off and he wore a loose fitting robe, and gray chest hair and gut were allowed to roam free into the room as he approached Penny Pureheart. He smirked at her, and leered at the ample amount of cleavage that lay beneath the silk business suit the woman wore. Penny looked up at James uncomfortably. "So... Mr. Armstrong... you say there is something big that we need to discuss?" James Armstrong leered again, showing teeth and fillings. "Oh yeah baby... I'm not just a one-Slugger man you see... and I think you and I should work... closer... on this story you're going to write. Maybe... real close... maybe do some... under cover work... maybe some... deep exploration..." Penny Pureheart shook her head in confusion. "Deep... what? I don't follow you... what is this big thing you want to discuss?" James Armstrong's leer grew wider as he let his robe fall completely open. Penny gasped, and then quickly removed a camera from her bag and took a picture at the naked acting commissioner of the fWo. Armstrong yelped, and sprung backward. "What was that for?" Penny grinned nervously. "Er... just something to remember for later. Um... I've got to like... go... now... right now. So... um... I'll see you later, okay?" Barry White continued to sign as Penny left the room, leaving James Armstrong confused and still holding the punch. Scratching his belly with a pinky before letting it roam up to his armpit where it began to curl the hair there, James Armstrong shook his head. "Women... always sending mixed signals..." War vs. Ultra Violet vs. Plague The arena lights dim as the team of Chaos, War and Plague of the Dark Riders, make their way to ringside. They head to separate coroners, awaiting the arrival of the third man in this three-way match. The crowd explodes as "Rage" comes over the sound system, bringing out Ultra-Violet, all by his lonesome. The cameras cut backstage, where Violet and Kid Cool are watching the pretty purple pyros explode on a monitor. Back in the ring, Ultra-Violet has been attacked by both Plague and War. Having trapped him in a corner, they take turns stomping him to the mat, taking delight in the damage they're doing to one-half of Ultra-Cool 2000, a team that will be vying for Tag Team Gold along with Chaos at Battle Bowl. With Ultra-Violet lying in heap on the mat, War kicks him flat, then military presses Plague on top of him. The ref hits the mat to start the count, but Plague releases the cover and starts headbutting Ultra-Violet. This causes UV to stir a bit. Plague and War shrug at this unexpected development, and double-team suplex the valiant purple warrior. They pick him back up and whip him to the corner, and both men follow up with avalanche splashes. UV stumbles out of the corner and is met with a powerslam by War. Again the ref goes to count, and again the cover is broken. The ref admonishes them, and warns them that he'll throw the match out if they don't make a serious attempt to end the match themselves. War simply shoves the ref away, and they wishbone UV. He grimaces, and is scooped up onto War's back for a crucifix powerbomb. Plague positions himself so as to hook UV's neck, and Ultra-Violet gets dropped by a crucifix powerbomb/neckbreaker combo. Rather than going for the cover, Chaos hits a series of knee- and elbow-drops on the prone Ultra-Violet. Realizing that neither man has the intention of ending the match, the ref rules the bout a no-contest, and struggles to get UV in the clear. War is clearly irked by this, and levels the ref with a mighty right hand. While this happens, Kid Cool dashes to the ring and kicks Plague off of his partner. Plague goes to attack, but War restrains him, forcing him back up the ramp. Kid Cool stands over his fallen friend, swearing revenge, as we head to a very extended commercial break... Result: No Contest Chapter Four: Dangerous Developments Warfare returned to strange hand held camera footage in black and white. The picture was jumpy, and grainy, and the way the background blurred by it was obvious that the scene was coming from a moving vehicle. The camera moved up, and Scott Slugger's face blurred into view behind the wheel of an automobile speeding down the Boston turnpike. From the sounds of laughter, it was clear that Ice was the one filming. The shot remained on Scott Slugger, and the former fWo champion turned toward the camera with a smirk. "Welcome to D-TV and unlike the imitators you will see some violent shit on here tonight. You see, we've got a very special guest with us tonight... candy boy himself, Scotty Cool. Well Mr. Cool, do you have any words for the fans watching at home?" The camera bounced and swung to the back seat of the car, where Death lay tied and gagged. He struggled, but was unable to free himself. Laughter filled the car, and the camera swung back to Slugger. "Well, we wish Chris could be here, but it seems some internet geeks picked him to win some match, so he's back at the arena and can't be a part of tonight's festivities." The car came to a stop, and through the light of the headlights the camcorder showed what appeared to be a lake in front of them. Slugger opened the car door and stepped out, and at the same time so did the cameraman, Ice. Slugger leaned inside the car and released the clutch, letting the car slowly roll forward toward the lake. Slugger waved at it. "Death, tonight you're going out Susan Smith style... see you later you miserable bastard... and remember, I'll be the first to 60. Count on it." The camcorder filmed the car slowly rolling into the lake... and into the murky depths. Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Brandi Back at the arena the Flying Frenchie was walking to the ring, fWo Womens title in hand. He was met with the usual boos and jeers that seemed to accompany his matches of late, and when he got in the ring he held the title up as if to ask where his applause was, and was met with tossed cups and plates of food. The Frenchie shrugged it off and asked for a microphone, waiting until one was found and handed to him, and then pulled his own out of his tights. On the side of the microphone it read "Frenchie Warfare" and he placed it near his mouth as he began to speak. "Well... zis is many weeks since I won ze Wom'ns title match in ze very difficult and hard match for me. I know... it is very hard to defend zis title with such ze busy schedule as one such as this one zat is m'self does... but ze Frenchie abides. Non... I am beginning to wonder if zere is such a thing as non good wom'n in ze fWo to carry zis title. Perh'ps ze Frenchie must as you say, retire zis belt. But for later date is zis thinking of mine. Tonight ze challenge is for ze Brandi... come down wom'n!" Brandi walked to the ring, looking warily at the Frenchie and holding up her fists, ready to fight. By her side was her husband Rudo, who also eyed the Frenchie with suspicion. The Frenchie looked at them both, and then began to speak again. "Brandi... zis question is for you and it is ze easy one. If you can answer it... I will give zis title to you. Ze question is zis... how is it zat you know who ze Death was ze first time anyway? You never explained zis, and we all want to know." The crowd fell silent, suddenly very interested in the answer to this age-old question. Brandi started to speak, stopped, and then shook her head. "I... don't remember, Frenchie." The crow groaned, and the Frenchie shook his head sadly. "Do not remember ze key to ze angle? Ah... Americans..." The Frenchie stopped looking at Brandi and looked directly at the camera. "If only ze fans had paid more attention to ze angle, they might know... alas, it was not to be." The Frenchie left the ring with the title, another challenge gone unrivaled. Result: Brandi loses by bad answer Chapter Five: Old Friends, Better Partners Backstage Matt Loco caught up with Chris Universal. Loco stuck a microphone in his face, and asked the member of the Dynasty bluntly, "Universal... you won the fWowrestling.com match poll and will face the Underground in tonight's match. My question for you is this... with Lance Star out of the picture, who will you select as your tag team partner?" Universal paused for a moment, as if he was deciding if he should tell or not. Finally he nodded and said, "You know, six months ago at CyberSlam I turned on my mentor and friend Mick Foley. And now, six months later my best friend is paralyzed. I've thought about what I did, and I've apologized to Mick... because tomorrow is Thanksgiving, and I wanted to bury the hatchet between us, and become friends again like old times because I'm thankful for you the fans, I'm thankful for Lance Star being alive, and I'm thankful to be a friend of Mick'. Kinda mad that he didn't mention me in his book, but that shouldn't keep all of you from buying his book... it's a great book, if you buy what The Sellout is selling. Anyways, one person sprung to mind that could be my partner tonight, and that's Mick. No matter if he's Cactus Jack, Mankind or Dude Love, he's the hippest, nicest hardcore legend the world's ever known... and tonight, he and The Sellout are going to make sure The Underground doesn't have a nice day! Buy the book!" Just then Ice and Scott Slugger entered the room, still laughing over their earlier exploits. Universal slapped hands with Slugger, smiling. "Good work man... know go out there and kick the Panther's masked ass..." Black Panther vs. Scott Slugger We return to the ring, where Black Panther is already located. "Back in Black" blares across the arena, bringing out Scott Slugger. A serious look lies across his face, as he no doubt is filled with worry and anger over the fate of Lance Star. Slugger pulls himself onto the apron and flips into the ring. He heads to a corner and stretches against the ropes, before turning to face the Panther. He jaws with the Panther, and slaps him. Slugger threatens to do it again, but is met with a fierce palm strike to his nose. Panther heads to the ropes and comes back with a lariat, flipping Slugger upon the impact. Panther lifts Slugger off the mat with a waistlock and hits a stunning gut-wrench suplex, earning an early two-count. Slugger gets back to his feet and knocks Panther backwards with a forearm, then dropkicks him to the outside. As Panther gets back to his feet, Slugger sails over the top rope with a pescado, knocking Panther back to the arena floor. Both men take their time getting back up, and when they do Panther has a whip reversed, sending him into the steel post. Slugger charges Panther, knocking him into the post a second time. He grabs Panther's mask and attempts to ram his head into the post, but Panther resists, pivots, and catches Slugger with a jawbreaker. Panther brings Slugger to his feet and lifts him in position for a piledriver, but Slugger counters with a golotta. Slugger rolls in Panther, then heads to the top rope. He comes off with a twisting splash that misses its mark as Black Panther rolls out of the way. Panther bring Slugger up for a piledriver, and this time Slugger doesn't fight it, and gets dumped on his head in the proper fashion. Panther goes for the cover, but Slugger inexplicably gets his foot on the ropes for the break. Panther rolls away from Slugger, trying to figure out what to do next. While he contemplates his next move, Stone, War and Plague hit the ring and attack Scott Slugger en masse, giving him a win via disqualification. Stone screamed at Slugger, asking him where Collins was. Panther, ever the upstanding wrestler, tries to pry them off of his opponent, but ends up being attacked himself. The Riders make short work of the two competitors, until Ice, Chris Universal, Wolf Fang, and Havok make their way to the ring to break up the attacks. As the Riders flee, the Millennium Men and the Dynasty stare each other done, each side just barely refraining from starting the battle that will take place at Battle Bowl. Slowly, cooler heads prevail, and each team tends to their fallen allies. The cameras head backstage Result: Scott Slugger by DQ Chapter Six: No Going Back With War, Plague and Stone occupied in the ring and Collins nowhere to be found Scott Taylor snuck through the hallways of the fWo arena, looking around himself in suspicion and worry. Taylor glanced over his shoulder as he approached the door to the incinerator room, saw nobody behind him, and snuck inside. It was dark in the room, and steam coated the floor. He tried to walk quietly, but his shoes still clacked on the hard concrete. He didn't see Fallen until he was nearly on top of him, and Fallen rose from the floor and the mist like an unearthly demon from hell. Fallen looked coldly at Taylor. Taylor stammered out a response, mostly a whisper. "I... I... I come to... seek an audience with your master... with Death." Fallen kept his gaze locked on Taylor and grabbed him by the neck. As Fallen was about to toss him into the mists and pipes of the room, a voice boomed out behind him. "Skyler... let him pass... let this one come." Fallen released and Skyler crept forward, eyes wide. "David... I mean Death... yes... the real Death... I come on my knees..." Taylor noticed he was not on his knees and fell to them at once. "I come to beg you... to let me serve you once again. I am strong with power in the fWo offices... I can help you..." Death looked at the man, and a slow smile crossed his lips. "My dear boy... you were once a valuable ally. A link into the upper power of the fWo. But boy... your time has come and gone. When you choose to follow the imposter... you sealed your fate. And boy... I'm much beyond you ever could be now..." Taylor looked puzzled at these cryptic words, and then jumped as Death's voice began again. "Poison... this one is yours." Smiling and weeping, Poison advanced on Taylor, and he screamed as everything went blissfully dark... Underground (c) vs. Chris Universal & Cactus Jack The fWo World Tag Team Champions came to the ring, holding their belts up for the crowd and flanked by the massive Fear. They were picked to face the Hollywood Blondes by the Fan Zone on fWowrestling.com, and both looked excited and ready for their match at BattleBowl. Chris Universal came out without Ice or Slugger, and walked to the ring looking arrogant and quite lucky with the stunning Megan Dawn at his side. He waited by the ring as familiar music played, and Cactus Jack came to the ring dressed in his familiar ring attire. Bear and Wretch looked nervously at each other as the hardcore legend walked to the ring to stand next to his old friend. Universal and Jack looked at each other for a moment, and then shook hands and slid into the ring. Inside the ring Cactus Jack started the match with Mike Bear, and the two paced each other before locking up. Cactus Jack won the test of strength, and pushed Bear to the mat with a hard thud. Cactus Jack then delivered a legdrop on Bear, and climbed to the top rope. Jack was going for a splash, but Wretch interfered with a jumping kick on the ring apron that sent Jack down hard into the metal turnbuckle. Bear hit a big boot and aimed a chokeslam, but Universal entered the ring and interfered. Universal was kicked by Wretch, who joined the three men in the ring as the referee desperately tried to maintain order. Cactus Jack hit a running lariat on Bear and went for a cover, but Wretch made the save. Wretch was tagged inside, and Jack at once went on the offensive and chopped Wretch. Cactus Jack went to the ropes for a running lariat on Wretch, but Fear punched at his back as he hit the ropes. Wretch then hit a jumping kick on Jack. On the attack now, Wretch executed a belly-to-back suplex. Bear was tagged in and the Underground did a double-team powerbomb, and Bear went for the cover. At the two count Universal entered the ring and made the save. Bear aimed for a chokeslam, but Jack powered out and whipped him into the Road Warrior corner. Universal punched at Bear's head as the referee was tied up with Wretch, who wanted to save his partner. Weakly now, Bear powered out and attempted a German suplex, but Cactus Jack reversed it. Cactus Jack covered but Bear powered out at two. Bear hit a DDT in the center of the ring, and both men crawled to make tags. Wretch and Universal came in, and Wretch ducked a clothesline by Universal and hit a body splash coming off the ropes. Universal was dazed, and with Bear keeping Cactus Jack busy on the outside Wretch executed a perfect Ligerbomb inside the ring and rolled Universal up for the three count and the successful title defense. The Underground escaped the ring weary, but still Tag Team Champions. Universal and Jack watched them go, and then saluted to the crowd and walked to the back together. Universal stopped on the way up the ramp and grabbed a "Lance Star" sign and pointed to it, sending a shout out to his injured partner... Result: Underground by Pinfall Chapter Seven: The Betrayal Backstage Machina whistled to himself as he strode down the hallway. Even his earlier loss couldn't break his bad cheer, and the masked man couldn't help but skip a little bit as he thought of the woman he had become so infatuated with in earlier weeks. He suddenly stopped whistling as he turned a corner and spotted the girl herself, standing at a telephone booth and talking to someone on the phone. His heart began to beat faster as a dreamy look crossed over his eyes. The woman was magnificent, and knowing it probably wasn't right, he leaned close to try and hear what she was saying on her end of the conversation. "That's right, NAKED. Oh, don't worry... I got the photo. After three weeks here I think it's the crowning jewel in our story. Yeah, I know. Yeah... couldn't have been better if we had planned it ourselves. You're right... this is going to look great on the front page. I can practically read the headline now... 'wrestling president drops shorts in another example of the vile filth professional wrestling spews out'. I tell you, not a sponsor one will support their shows after we are done with them. Professional wrestling will go off the air, and we can all be happier! What's that? The wrestlers? Who cares... all a bunch of brain dead retards... every last one of them. Deserve to get tossed on the street I say... none of them are bright enough to get a job anywhere else. Yeah... I'll see you next week. I'm going to be at the Pay Per View... plenty of filth there to cover I'm sure. Yeah, tell them all Penny says hi. Bye." Penny Pureheart hung up the phone and walked quickly away, heels clacking. From his hiding place just around the corner, Machina's face was pale. Outside, the sounds of the main event could be heard... Death vs. Cactus Jon Cactus Jon heads to the ring for this next match, to determine Primetime's challenger at this Saturday's Battle Bowl '99. Accompanied by the Black Ninja and Tom the Bomb, he waits on the outside for David Spencer, Death. Spencer walks to the ring, defiant, and surrounded by his Dark Riders, Fallen, Poison, and Demon. They enter the ring as a group, and mock the Canadian superstar and his entourage. As Demon and Fallen appear ready to attack, the referee quickly rules that if the Riders do not return to the locker room, David Spencer will forfeit the match and the World Title opportunity will go to Cactus Jon. Spencer is outraged, demanding that if his men go, then so should Jon's. The ref ignores him, and starts a ten-count for Spencer's group to comply by. Spencer quickly shoos away his men, but glowers viciously at the official. Jon enters the ring, and the match is on. Jon quickly powers Spencer into a corner, and hiptosses him to the mat. An elbow drop gets nary a one-count, as Spencer easily kicks out. A snap suplex brings Jon to the mat, and Spencer floats over for a two-count. As both men retake their vertical bases, Spencer kicks Jon in the knee, bringing him back to the mat. Death flips Cactus Jon onto his stomach and drives Jon's knee into the mat repeatedly. Tiring of that, he rolls Jon over again and locks in a figure-four. Jon makes the ropes to break the hold, but the damage is already done. Jon hobbles back to his feet, and is tossed between the ropes to the outside. Death follows up with a suicide dive, which Jon ducks, sending Death crashing into the guardrail. As Jon recuperates on the outside, Tom props Death up against the rail and starts punching, while the referee opts to ignore the blatant interference. Death blocks Tom's next flurry of punches and returns with some of his own. The Black Ninja goes to superkick Death, but Death pulls Tom the Bomb into the path of the kick, and he's knocked cold. Ninja stares down at him in disbelief, and gets caught with a nasty lariat that sends him crashing head-first against the ring steps. Death slides back in to avoid a count-out, and Jon drives his elbow into Death's back, keeping him grounded. Jon locks in a headlock, which the fans applaud with much vigor. Minutes pass while he cinches the hold in tighter, drawing more and more response the entire time. The ref checks Death's arm, and it drops twice. The third time Death's arm stops shortly before hitting the mat, and he fights his way back to his feet. Death catches Jon with a pair of elbows, then shoves him into the ropes and schoolboys him. Jon breaks the pin with a headscissors, cutting off more and more circulation to Death's head. Death squeezes out of it, then kicks Jon forcefully in the lower abdominal region, causing the entire arena to gasp in unison. Pleased with the effect, he drops an elbow on the already afflicted region, and covers for two before the lights go out. When the lights come back on, Death is lying motionless on the mat, and Grendel stands above him. The ref asks Grendel if he knows what happened, and Grendel motions to Jon. The referee is suitably pleased with the explanation, and Cactus Jon crawls over Death for the pinfall and the World Title match at Battle Bowl. Grendel points at the fallen Death, and smirks, when the lights go out again. When they come back, Grendel's gone, and Cactus Jon celebrates his victory with the Black Ninja and Tom the Bomb on the outside as Warfare goes off the air for another week. Result: Cactus Jon by Pinfall
