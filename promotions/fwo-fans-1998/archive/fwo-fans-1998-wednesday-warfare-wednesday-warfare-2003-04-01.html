Introduction Previously... The fWo has been forced from the United States. With Cyberslam VI looming only a couple weeks away, the fWo finds itself desperate to return home. A brutal road trip has taken its toll on the talent in the fWo, and nerves have been stretched tighter than ever. With the threat of Richard Moore finally put to rest, the fWo must now focus themselves on their primarily goals: getting home. It is with this important issue on the minds of the wrestlers and ring crew that finds the promotion in the posh Cumberland Hotel in downtown London. As the roster waits for tomorrow's Wednesday Warfare, not knowing that moments from now bad news is about to be delivered via the curtous yet brisk front desk receptionist and her telephone routing skills... First Strike Ivy McGinnis wasn't your typical all American girl. In fact, she was the farthest thing from it, she wore 'fishnets' over regular stockings, boots over heels, highlights over a ponytail, because for the most part, all of that stuff was just to bland. Where was the originality? What set you apart from the next girl? When she first entered the wrestling business, promoters thought they'd be getting another Teri Melton - a business suit, wearing sidekick. What they got was a loud mouth, Singapore Cane swinging, Psycho Bitch that not only commanded respect; she went out and earned it. She had been through so much throughout all her years in the wrestling business, she had seen even more than that. Poison Ivy honestly thought there was nothing left on this planet that could surprise her... ...boy was she wrong. Her hotel room door opened, as Amy Campbell, aka Aimz entered. She noticed Ivy's swollen eyes immediately, and began to realize something wasn't right. "Aimz.... come in, close th'door behind you, I don't want Sean or Eli hearing us." Ivy began. If Aimz wasn't worried before, she sure as hell was worried now. She sat down beside Ivy, rubbing a tear from her cheek, trying to reassure her, before she ever really figured out what the problem was. "What's happened?!" Amy'd given a rather speedy response to the situation in front of her, but quickly thought back... "And before you go on, this'd better not be some kind'a April Fool's joke you didn't get to play yet." "Oh yeah... because I can *so* cry on command. April Fools, Amy." Sarcasm was usually one of Ivy's strengths, but tonight for some reason... the jovial mood was noticeably absent. The redhead blinked for a second, studying carefully. Ivy was somewhat like Amy herself in a respect - she was either incredibly energetic or very, very silent... this certainly wasn't the norm for the Psycho Bitch. ''Sorry...'' She cringed. ''But..uhh...what is wrong?'' Ivy blinked, wiping the remaining tears out of her eyes, "I..." She laughed. "It's the same whether I say it quick or slow... I'm late. And I mean late."
