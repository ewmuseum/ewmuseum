fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE NIGHT OF THREE July 28, 2000 It was a triple threat night. Nine wrestlers remain in the fWo Survivor contest after Thursday's vote of T-Bone. With only nine wrestlers in the contest, Survivor officials had a special night of action planned for the immunity winner. A mini-triple threat tournament. "What else can you do with nine people? We thought it would be a good test of endurance to see how wrestlers fared wrestling not only twice in one night, but with the added difficulty of triple threat rules in each match." Stevie Roberts explained. "We want to test various skills of the wrestlers before this is all done... that way the immunity winner should be different each time, or at least give everyone a fighting chance at victory." Survivor Round Four saw four matches. First three qualifying matches, where Reno Davis, Arch Angel, and the Don respectively won their individual bouts. Then at the end of the night the three men came together for one final match where the Don managed to put victory literally from the jaws of defeat. When asked for comment on his victory, the Don had little to say and simply lit a cigar before closing the door on his locker room. The Don now holds immunity, which means that during the next round of voting he is except from elimination. As the roster in fWo Survivor continues to shrink, immunity becomes more and more valuable. Another interesting event took place during Round Four, where for the first time interference was seen in a match. Not once, but twice wrestlers came to the ring in order to disrupt and likely cost a wrestler the win. First, Kodiak Vic Creed attacked Red Dragon during his match, leading directly to his loss. The Red Dragon avenged this interference with interference of his own, directly costing Creed the victory in his match, which Creed was all but certain to win. Neither man would give a statement regarding their actions. For Round Five of the fWo Survivor tournament fWoWrestling.com has already learned that it will have two matches. The first will be a four team, eight man tornado style elimination tag team match. Then the winning team will separate and wrestle in a singles match to determine immunity. The contrast of trusting your partner and then being forced to wrestle him for the ultimate prize in Survivor will likely cause further dissention within the ranks. More news as it develops.
