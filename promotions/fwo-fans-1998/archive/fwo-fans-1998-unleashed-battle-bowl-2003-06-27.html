Introduction The night has come. Tonight, on the special NBC produced "Unleashed: Battle Bowl" the fWo will learn the randomly selected teams in the Meltdown main event. To win the event means the greatest glory the fWo can offer... a shot at every championship the fWo offers. But to get there teams that have likely never worked together must form a tight bond and evade the burning hatred that threatens to boil over and take them all... Meltdown Announcement Fireworks exploded from the top of the stage. It was the fWo's return to NBC after being unceremoniously removed from their schedules less than two months ago. At the top of the FanTron a countdown blazed into life... it would be a mere thirty one days before fWo Meltdown, the Pay Per View event of the summer. But tonight was Unleashed, a regular special on the NBC network that would not only kick off the build to Meltdown, but would also pay homage to the traditional Battle Bowl pay-per-view events with a huge main event spectacular. As the fireworks settled, "'Round" pumped over the arena speakers as fWo staff Silver Fox, Samuel Smooth, Twitch and Ted Tallison made their way down to the ring. The four road agents had become synonymous with the leadership of the fWo, despite their status as road agents and product managers rather that the true guiding force behind the promotion, which, depending on who you asked was anyone from the Board of Directors, the Disney corporation or the wrestlers themselves. Tonight however, was not about leadership or responsibility, but about announcements. Namely, the announcement of the twenty randomly selected teams in the fWo Meltdown contest.
