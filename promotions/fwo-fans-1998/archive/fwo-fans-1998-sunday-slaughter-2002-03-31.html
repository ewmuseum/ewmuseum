He was in street clothes, faded baggy jeans and a fWo sweatshirt. He always believed in scouting out his enemies... living as they do, trying to see the world as they do... but this was too much. This was torture. "He flows like a harpoon? What the hell is that supposed to mean?" Mufanga muttered in disgust as he rose from the sofa and paced around the tiny windowless dressing room that the fWo set aside for green rookies like him. Hell, he had to go bother the janitor for a stereo just to listen to this worthless Vanilla Ice CD. Not like the veterans, like this Ice character. He's a veteran, so he can come back to the federation with a silly gimmick, and get top billing? After making fun of Mufanga's honorable gimmick? His honorable loincloth? But Kagos was used to living on less. And he was knew it was only temporary. Once he beat Ice in the ring, one on one... then his name would be made. Then his stock would go up. Last week he almost walked away with the Internet Title, in a match he had never asked for, but welcomed just the same... any match that put him in the same ring with Ice, with a chance to show the fans he could go toe to toe with the puffy-panted behemoth, was a good one. Kagos could be champion now... he had hit Triple X with the Wildive, he was the better man in the match, he was going to pin Triple X for the Title. And then Ice threw him off the pin, and over the top rope. Another blown chance. Just like his entry into the fWo, he blew his chance at the Bodycount Rumble. shortly after that, he blew a chance to win the Hardcore Title from Donaven Winters. And last week, he blew a chance at the Internet title. "Hell," he said to himself. "All I need to do is lose to Ross for the World Title, and I'm a Grandslam Loser... all within my first month in the company." "Ah couldn't've said it betta, chump." A voice from outside the doorway rang out in a familiar false accent, startling Mufanga into dropping the To the Extreme CD. "Yo Tarzan, Check out this beat... Quick to the point, to the point no faking/ I'm cookin' rookies like a pound of bacon." Mufanga burst across the room, a streak of ebony as he crossed the room to the closed door. A viscious snarl appeared on his broad features as he pounced on the door like a wild animal. There was another *click*. The door was locked. From the outside. "Yo, rookie..." Ice yelled from the hallway. "Tonight this MC is goin' solo. Peace out." Tempest (c) vs. Sphere Slaughter heads to the ring with music blaring and fireworks a-blastin'. The SkyDome record crowd of 68,238 (one more than WrestleMania 18, ha ha) is going absolutely insane.. "Battle Flag" by the Low-Fidelity Allstars blasted over the PA system as Tempest stepped out from behind the curtains. She waved at the crowd as her music played in the backround, and the fans in attendance continued to cheer her on. Tempest rolled into the ring, and got up, beginning to remove her Cruiserweight title. She handed it to the referee, as the final notes of "Battle Flag" played. As soon as "Battle Flag" ended, "Anarchy in the U.K." by Megadeth blasted over the PA system. The fans began to boo as Sphere strode out onto the entrance way, sneering at the boos of the crowd. Sphere then trotted down the rampway, before turning into a full jog, sliding into the ring as the bell rang. Sphere was met with a hard double axehandle by Tempest as the fans cheered her on. Tempest laid in a few hard kicks into Sphere's ribs before lifting him up, and whipping him into a turnbuckle. Tempest ran towards Sphere, who lifted up a boot, knocking the High Flying champion down to the mat. Tempest was then lifted up by Sphere, who scooped Tempest up, before driving her into the mat with the Tony Blair Driver, getting a quick two count before Tempest kicked out. The fans booed as Sphere lifted Tempest up, and whipped her into the ropes. As soon as Tempest returned, however, she executed a hurricanrana on Sphere, driving him into the mat. The fans exploded with cheers as Tempest climbed the turnbuckle, and executed a Moonsault onto Sphere, hooking the leg for the pin. At two, Sphere tossed off Tempest to the anger of the crowd. Tempest got up first, and ran towards Sphere. She grabbed Sphere's head, and drove it into the mat with the Rottweiler. However, Tempest didn't go for the pin, as she feared Sphere still had the strength to kick out of a pin attempt. Tempest climbed up the turnbuckle, and eyed the crowd for a second before waiting for Sphere to get up. Tempest then executed her second finisher, the System Crash, driving Sphere's head into the mat. Tempest hooked the leg, and three seconds later, she had quickly retained her Cruiserweight Championship against Sphere. She picked up her Cruiserweight title, and showed it to the crowd. Meanwhile, Sphere, angered at losing to a woman, grabbed Tempest's head from behind, and executed Feels Like The Ukraine as the fans booed him on. Then, Monte Burns stepped out from behind the curtains. Burns stared for a second, thinking about helping her, but then deciding against it. While Burns returned backstage, Sphere dragged Tempest to the center of the ring, and pointed at the fans. He then climbed up the turnbuckle, and then drove into Tempest with his finisher, the Holy Grail, a shooting star press. As "Anarchy in the U.K." cued up, the fans knew one thing: Burns had turned his back on Tempest. Result: Tempest by pinfall Meeting "What a dump," Crucifix muttered as he looked around the small room. "This place is no bigger than a storage closet." "It is a storage closet," David Cross said as he moved a mop off to the side. "You wanted your own locker room, and all the private locker rooms were spoken for. Besides, this place isn't so bad." Crucifix tapped the single light bulb hanging over his head. "It'll do, I suppose. I don't need the frills that others desire. I don't need the limo's, the hotel rooms. I'm self-sufficient. All I need is revenge." "Well, a nice hotel room wouldn't hurt. I think someone stole my wallet last night at the Y," said Cross as he searched through a gym bag. "You you you. It's all about you, isn't it?" said Crucifix as more of a statement rather than a question. "I'm doing this for you, stupid. I'm doing what you don't have the stomach to do." Cross opened up one of the metal folding chairs and sat down. "I wanted to talk to you about that," he said. "I know what you're planning for Frontier and I..." David looked down, "I don't want any part of it." Crucifix picked up a folding chair of his own. "Your problem is that you don't know what you want. You don't want any part of Frontier's dismantling? That's fine with me." David raised his head, a look of relief on his face. "I'm glad you underst-" Unfortunately, David couldn't complete his sentence. Crucifix completed it for him, via a folding chair across Cross' face. David fell back, flipping out of the chair and landing prone on the floor. Crucifix stood over him, then brought the chair down across his back. Again. And again. And again. "I'm glad that you're glad," said Crucifix as he tossed the chair aside. "As usual, your pain... has been my pleasure." Brand Frontier/Lance Knight vs. The fans in attendance stomped their feet in anticipation as they awaited the next match, which would be a tag team match pitting two sets of foes against one another. Several seconds after the chants began, "Last Man Standing" by Biohazard blasted over the arena PA system, and Brand Frontier stepped out from behind the curtains to a loud pop from the crowd. Brand stepped down the rampway, slapping some fans hands, while eyeing for Crucifix surprise attacking him anytime along the way. Luckily, however, Crucifix never appeared anywhere, and Brand climbed up the ring steps, entering the ring shortly afterwards. As "Last Man Standing" lowered in volume, a new song replaced it: "Elite" by the Deftones. The fans cheered as Lance Knight stepped out from behind the curtains. Lance raised his arms into the air for a second, receiving a cheap pop from the crowd as he stepped down the rampway as did Brand several seconds ago, slapping some fans hands. The fans cheered both men on as Lance made his way to ringside. Lance paused for a second, before rolling into the ring, and eyeing Brand. Then, the men shook hands in a sign of unity. The unity was cut short as Grendel and Crucifix both double back attacked Brand and Lance. Grendel executed a powerful power clothesline from behind Lance, while Crucifix executed a dangerous Asai Moonsault, knocking both himself and Brand Frontier to the mat. The bell rang, and the referee ordered Crucifix and Lance Knight to exit the ring, and stay on the apron. Meanwhile, Grendel lifted up Brand, and put his hand around Brand's throat as the fans in attendance began to boo Grendel on. Grendel sneered at the fans as Brand used this temporary distraction to his advantage. Brand elbowed Grendel in the head a few times as the fans cheered him on, and then shoved him into the ropes, knocking his partner Crucifix off the apron. The fans went wild as Brand used all of his strength and pain resistance to grab Grendel on his way back and executed a powerful T-Bone suplex. Both men lied in the ring, writhing in pain. At four, however, Grendel managed to get up first, and went over to Brand. Grendel smirked and kicked Brand hard in the face, knocking him back into the mat as the fans booed. Grendel lifted up Brand, and using his might scooped him up into the air. He held Brand in the air for several seconds before dropping him hard onto his chest with a powerful Military Press Slam as the fans booed. Grendel eyed Brand for a few seconds before tagging in an eager Crucifix, who executing a top rope Moonsault onto Brand, going for the quick pin. At two, Brand managed to kick out of the pin attempt, which angered Crucifix. Crucifix began calling the ref names as the referee yelled back at him. Meanwhile, Brand got up from behind Crucifix as the fans cheered Brand on. As soon as Crucifix turned around, Brand hit Crucifix with 'The Hammer of God', which caused Crucifix to sink to his knees, and then the ground. The fans exploded with cheers as Brand climbed up the turnbuckle, and stared at the crowd for several seconds before diving off with 'The Paragon Bomb'. After several seconds, Brand connected with the move successfully, and hooked the leg for the quick pinfall. At two and three quarters, however, Grendel broke up the pin, costing the potential victory for him and Lance Knight. Lance Knight began motioning to be tagged, and Brand relented before tagging Lance in. Lance raced into the ring, and threw a hard right, taking down Grendel. The fans exploded with cheers as Lance Knight quickly took Crucifix down with another hard right. Lance Knight then lifted Crucifix up, and whipped him into the ropes. However, this time Crucifix jumped onto the top rope and dove off. Crucifix attempted to impale Lance Knight to with a flying headbutt, but Lance moved out of the way, making Crucifix bounce off with a sickening thud. As the referee turned to Crucifix, Lance was side sweeped by Grendel using a steel chair to the back of his head. Grendel then tossed away the blood stained chair, and lifted up Lance Knight before driving him into the mat with a hard chokeslam. Brand tried to enter the ring, but the referee yelled at him. Meanwhile, Crucifix weakly tagged in Grendel, and Grendel lifted up Lance Knight, and in another show of disgust, sit down powerbombed Lance Knight onto a chair that Crucifix brought in, as the fans booed them on. Crucifix pulled out the chair, and Grendel yelled at the referee. The three count was quickly made before Brand could stop it. "Everything's Cool" by Pop Will Eat Itself hit the PA system as Grendel and Crucifix stared at Brand, considering to attack him too. However, they relented, and rolled out of the ring. Meanwhile, Brand lifted up Lance, and the two men eyed their cheating opponents who snatched an unfair victory, and smiled as they backtracked to the backstage area of the arena all the while. Scott Slugger (c) vs. Jon Crisp fWo Internet Title Scott Slugger (c) vs. Jon Crisp WINNER: Scott Slugger by pinfall Slaughter heads to the ring for a one-on-one matchup, for the Internet Title. The Toronto crowd cheers wildly, ready for this next big matchup. "High and Dry" hits, and the crowd boos as Jon Crisp makes his way to the ring, a sneer ever-present on his face. Crisp rolls into the ring and rolls his eyes at the crowd, then commences stretching, readying himself for this big matchup. "Forever" plays, and the crowd explodes as Scott Slugger heads out from backstage, fWo Internet Title gleaming around his waist. Slugger walks to the ring with fireworks exploding behind him, and slaps the hands of the fans all the way. Slugger finally climbs up the ring steps and vaults into the ring. He unstraps the Internet Title and hands it off to the referee. The ref raises it in the air, then passes it along to the timekeeper. The ref then signals for the bell, and the match is on. Slugger and Crisp lock up, and Slugger gains the advantage. He kicks Crisp in the gut, then whips him into the corner and charges in, following him. Slugger nails Crisp with a leaping bodysplash, taking the fight to the Cult Icon early. Slugger backs up and begins unloading with a series of chops, knifehanding the chest of Crisp and raising large red welts. Slugger brings Crisp out of the corner and backs him into the ropes, then sets to whip him across. Crisp, however, reverses. Slugger comes off and (baseball) slides between the legs of Crisp, who whiffs on a lariat. Slugger kips up to his feet and lets his momentum carry him forward. It carries him right into Jon Crisp, who is turning around, and Slugger follows through with a clothesline, knocking Crisp straight to the mat. Slugger then runs into the ropes. He leaps onto the second strand, then leaps off of it, nailing Crisp square-on with an asai moonsault. Slugger holds on, reaches forward, and hooks the leg. Crisp kicks out at two. Slugger brings him back up to his feet and hooks his head, then lifts him up into the air. He then brings Crisp down, driving him into the mat with a brainbuster. Slugger floats over into another cover, and once more gets two. He brings Crisp up and this time drives him into the mat with a double-arm DDT, followed by a third cover. This still fails to get the three, with Crisp kicking out after two. Slugger pulls Crisp to his feet by the hair, but Crisp catches Slugger with a thumb to the eye. Slugger stumbles back, and now it's Crisp on the offensive. Crisp grabs Slugger and takes him to the mat with a snap suplex. Crisp holds on and brings Slugger back up, then executes a traditional suplex. Finally, he holds on and brings Slugger up one last time, executing a delayed suplex. Crisp makes the cover, but Slugger manages to get a shoulder up after the count of two is registered. Crisp brings Slugger up and kicks him in the gut, then runs into the ropes and executes a swinging neckbreaker, taking Slugger down to the mat. Crisp then heads to Slugger's legs and braces himself, then lifts his legs high in the air and brings one down knee-to-knee on Slugger. He repeats this, then does so again. Crisp then grabs Slugger and brings him to the ropes. He places Slugger's leg on the bottom rope and leaps into the air, coming down on the knee of Slugger. Crisp drags Slugger away from the ropes, and applies a half crab, still working over that right leg and knee of Slugger, surely in preparation for a later Sharpshooter attempt. Crisp wrenches back on the leg, trying to do further damage and maybe even secure a submission victory. Slugger uses his forearms to crawl forward, trying to reach the ropes. Slugger reaches out a hand, going for the ropes... and makes it. Crisp is forced to release the hold, and finally does so after a count of four. Snarling, he brings Slugger up and dropkicks him in the leg, knocking him down. Crisp brings Slugger right back up and whips him into the corner, then follows him in with an elbow to the face. He then wraps Slugger's foot around the bottom rope and pulls forward, breaking the hold as the count of four is reached. He leaves Slugger's foot in place and walks across the ring to the other corner, preparing to wreak more carnage upon Slugger. Crisp runs forward and baseball slides into the corner, going for the trapped leg and foot of Slugger, but Slugger frees himself and uses his arms to lift himself up. Crisp follows through on the slide, and his momentum carries him under the bottom rope and to the outside where he lands with a considerable splat. Slugger takes a moment to collect himself, then rolls to the outside to take the fight to Crisp. Slugger brings Crisp up to his feet and runs him into the ringpost. Crisp's head bangs off the steel, and he collapses woozily to the ground. Slugger still clearly feeling the effects of Crisp's attacks brings him up and tosses him back into the ring. Slugger lifts Crisp to his feet and lifts him onto his shoulders. However, Slugger's leg gives way, and he drops Crisp. Crisp quickly double-leg trips Slugger, then sets up to apply a Sharpshooter. Slugger, however, reaches forward and grabs Crisp by the head, rolling him up into a small package. This gets a two, then Crisp kicks out. He once again sets to apply the Sharpshooter more carefully this time and succeeds, wrapping Slugger up in the devastating submission hold. Slugger scowls in pain, and Crisp yanks back on the hold, sure that his first fWo Internet Title is mere moments away. Slugger tries to fight his way out of it, but cannot. His only choice is to crawl for the ropes. Slugger drags himself toward the ropes, trying to force the break. He reaches out his arm... but cannot reach. He reaches out again... and Crisp yanks him back towards the middle of the ring. Slugger now has nowhere to go, and he prepares one last-ditch effort to break it up. Slugger puts all his energy into rolling his body. He succeeds, and the momentum and movement of the maneuver forces Crisp to break the hold to cheers from the crowd. Slugger slowly gets to his feet, and Crisp gets up as well. Crisp wastes no time in going for the kill, kicking Slugger in the gut and hooking him up, preparing to execute The Icon.
