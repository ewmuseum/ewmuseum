Night of Pranks, Part I The Boston Strangler walked into his locker room and looked around briefly before heaving his bag onto a nearby bench. He stretched a bit and then reached out and opened a locker. Suddenly a tumult of balloons poured out in red, blue, green, yellow, purple, and about every color one could imagine. Strangler jumped at the unexpected deluge and batted them away. He looked at them suspiciously until he realized Chronos lacked the style to use this as a trick of some sort. In the midst of the balloons a single flashy silver one hung a bit lower than the others with a card attached to it's string and he picked it up to examine it. Just as he did, the door opened and James Armstrong walked in. He laughed aloud and said, "Oh this is cute. I was about to ask about the match with Chronos, but I guess I should ask whose birthday it is instead." Strangler's color rose slightly as he said, "No one's. It appears to be a... well, an exuberant form of thank you note from Tempest." Armstrong raised an eyebrow and said, "Tempest? Ooh, so you have an admirer. What's she say in there, huh?" Strangler's gaze toward Armstrong held a note of warning as he tossed the note at him and said, "Read for yourself. It's quite innocent really." Armstrong opened the card and read, "Dear Strangler, Thanks so much for the save last week. My ribcage and I appreciate it even if you were just trying to get your hands on Chronos. For your help, you'll be happy to know that for tonight you have diplomatic immunity from pranks. No marbles, I promise. Of course everyone else is screwed. Sincerely, Tempest " He grinned maliciously and said, "Aw, isn't that cute," as Strangler stood and started toward him. "We think you'd better be going," he said. Armstrong shrugged, playing innocent and said, "What? C'mon, I never it was a bad thing. I mean she's a hottie and all, so..." "Leave," said the Strangler flatly as he opened the door. Armstrong chuckled as he stepped out into the hall and said, "Aw c'mon, it could have been worse. At least she didn't send ya flowers or something." *POW!* The sarcastic sneer on Armstrong's face was obliterated by whipped cream and a good bit of flaked coconut as a pie sailed down the hall and caught him full force. A smile started to creep across Strangler's face as a peel of high pitched laughter came from the end of the hall, just before a small dark-haired shape zipped around the corner. "You're right James, it is cute come to think of it. Enjoy the show," said the Strangler as he closed the door with a chuckle and left Armstrong spluttering uselessly in the hall.
