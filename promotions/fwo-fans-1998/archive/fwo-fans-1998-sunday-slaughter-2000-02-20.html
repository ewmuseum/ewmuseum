Sunday Slaughter, 20 February 2000

20 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Memphis was all activity as Sunday Slaughter got ready to take to the air. Cars had arrived at the Pyramid all throughout the day, with wrestlers hefting bags into the arena over shoulders. Scott Collins arrived with Stone, and wheeled his bag in on the little bag wheels that came on the bottom of his bag. Fallen arrived alone, and as he walked into the arena people at the gate noted that he had gotten deep into his character, often to the point that he didn't answer to John Skyler any more. Geoff Green was particularly saddened by the change, as John was one of his closest friends prior to the accident in December. As Geoff was considering all of this Kid Cool tapped him on the shoulder. "Hey Green... you hear if my challenge was accepted? You know... the three way?" Geoff blinked. "Three way? Have you been hanging out with Nikki again?" Kid Cool nodded happily. "Yeah! Man, she's the greatest... did you know she was...," Kid Cool lowered his voice and looked around to see if anyone was listening. Satisfied he turned back to Green. "She said I was her first! Isn't that cool Geoff? I finally got to have sex and it was with a virgin! How perfect is that?" Green smiled a thin smile. "Oh yeah... that's pretty... unbelievable... Buckly. Anyway, yeah. Your match was approved... Courage, Ultra Violet, and you for the Pay Per View. Good luck." Kid Cool grinned and wandered away in search of someone else to tell his tale of sexual conquest to. Geoff Green shook his head with amusement. "Yeah... good luck. You're gonna need it." Inside the arena the explosions of the opening fireworks sounded... and Sunday Slaughter took to the air. Ric Chronos vs. Chaos "Time" plays over the arena's sound system, bringing out a deliberate looking Ric Chronos. He heads straight to the ring, ready to take on both members of Chaos. The lights dim, and the scratchy "4" appears on the Slaughter Screen, as War and plague make their way to the ring. Plague slides right in while War heads to the corner, and the match is on. Plague scrambles to his feet and drives his shoulder into Chronos, taking him to the mat. Plague straddles over the newcomer, raining down lefts and rights upon him, then starts to headbutt him. Woozy, he relents, then heads to the corner and tags in the monstrous War. War brings Ric to his feet, then whips him into the corner. He follows Chronos in shoulder-first, finding only the steel ring post after Chronos falls out of the way. A schoolboy on the dazed War earns only two for Ric before War gets his shoulder up. Ric grabs War's leg and drags him into the middle of the ring, then rolls War over and slips in an armbar on the mat. Ric pulls at War's arm, trying to separate his injured shoulder, but his attempt is put to an end when Plague enters the ring and breaks it up. The hold broken, War joins his partner in putting the boots to Chronos, until finally the referee brings Plague back to his corner. War lifts Chronos up onto is shoulder, and places him on the top turnbuckle. A belly-to-belly superplex follows, and War goes for the cover. Remarkably, Chronos gets a shoulder up at two and a half. War covers again, this time hooking Ric's leg, but again he only gets two. Frustrated, he brings Ric up, and whips him to the ropes. Chronos ducks under a lariat, and comes back with jumping DDT. Plague enters the ring once more, but is caught with a DTH as he charges Chronos. Ric Chronos kicks Plague out of the ring, then props War up in the corner. Unleashing his anger against the Rider, he gives him ten punches in the corner. He descends, and pulls War slightly out of the corner, then grabs his head and nails The Chronosphere. The resulting cover gets three. Ric Chronos stands triumphant in the ring, gaining a measure of revenge for his friend Fallen, as Slaughter heads backstage. Result: Ric Chronos by Pinfall Training The scene went a segment taped earlier in the week from the dojo of the New Ninja somewhere in the wilds of Canada. The Ninja paced the wooden floor, thinking of the right way to begin with the next lesson. Tom sat in front of him, obviously having difficulty controlling his excitement. From time to time he struck out at the empty air, perhaps to demonstrate his skills of karate or perhaps because he was being plagued by invisible insects. The Ninja spoke, breaking the silence. "Alright Tom-San... you did... -cough- great there on Slaughter. But you're not going to get so -coughluckycough- all of the time. So Today I will be teaching you..." Tom leapt to his feet, a goofy grin across his face. "Wax on, wax off? The Ninja sighed. "No Tom-San, it's..." Tom was now jumping from foot to foot, excited. "Is it sand the floor? Paint the house? Ooh... ooh... ooh... crane kick? It's crane kick isn't it? Oh boy oh boy oh boy..." The Ninja smacked him over the head with the broad side of his hand. This seemed to calm Tom somewhat, but he was still looking far too excited for the Ninja's taste. "No. It is how to keep quiet and listen to your sensei, dammit!" Tom began to sulk. "That doesn't sound like much of a lesson to me..." The Ninja's face began to grow visibly red. "Tom, if you don't shut the f..." Tom cut in, "Tom-SAN." The Ninja exploded. "Dammit!" Tom shrugged, "Damn what?" The Ninja screamed and tore at his mask in frustration as the camera faded to black... Frank Snow (c) vs. Robert Jensen Robert Jensen, carrying a chair, steps out onto the entrance ramp to a negative reaction from the fans in attendance. Paying it no attention, the challenger to the fWo Hardcore Title shrugs his shoulders and takes up a position just next to the entrance way. Then, as Jensen's music quiets down, the crowd lets out a chorus of boos for Robert's dirty tactics. Frank Snow's music starts up, and Snow steps onto the ramp and right into a chairshot from Jensen. The chair dents to the point of uselessness, so Jensen throws it off the side of the elevated ramp. Snow, championship belt still around his waist, holds the back of his head in pain and staggers down the ramp. Jensen decides to go after him with conventional methods and begins to bludgeon the champ with stiff forearms and fists. Jensen peppers Snow as both men continue down the ramp at a slow pace. Jensen grabs the head of Snow and attempts to ram it into the railing, but Snow blocks it and elbows Jensen in the gut. Jensen doubles over, allowing Snow more than enough time to hook him and suplex him onto the steel rampway. Jensen arches his back in pain upon landing. Snow finally undoes his title belt and holds it in the air for a few moments in order to admire it, then proceeds to bring it down on the head of Jensen over and over. Snow ceases the pounding walks down the ramp to the ring where he hands off the belt to a ring attendant and begins searching underneath the ring. Finding his booty, the hardcore champion pulls out a table and sets it up at a slanted angle against the side of the ring. Robert Jensen, meanwhile, has gotten to his knees. Snow grabs Jensen by the head and brings him over to the ring, where he throws him headfirst into the table. Jensen slumps to the arena floor before the table while Snow retrieves a chair from the crowd. Snow then sets a dazed Jensen's head up against the table and catches him between metal and wood with a wicked chairshot. Jensen pops out of his stunned state in extreme pain, holding his head and rolling about the ringside floor, while the crowd oohs and ahhs. Snow shows no mercy, however, and brings Jensen to his feet. The champion then irish whips Jensen right into the table, shattering it. A beaten and bloody Jensen drops to the floor where Snow makes a cover. The ref gets down to make the count but Jensen gets a shoulder up just at a 2 3/4 count. Snow pulls Jensen back up and rolls him into the ring, then enters himself. Jensen gets a seconds wind and scampers to the corner, begging off all the while. Snow looks to the crowd, who pop, and then lays into the challenger with kick after kick. Snow heaves Jensen to his feet and whips him to the corner, where Jensen hits with such force that he bounces back out into the center of the ring. Snow intercepts him with a lariat that turns Jensen end over end. Snow raises his arm to the crowd and yells, and the crowd cheers in return. Snow then lifts up Jensen once again and drops him with a sit-down powerbomb. Jensen's head bounces off the canvas as the ref makes the count, but Jensen gets the shoulder up in the nick of time. Snow rolls his opponent off of him and slides to the outside where he sets up a table for the second time tonight. Then, Snow heads back inside and lifts Jensen into a back suplex position, but deposits him backwards on the top turnbuckle. Snow steps through the ropes onto the apron, and then climbs the turnbuckle from the outside and hooks Jensen for a superplex through the table. Snow hooks his challenger, then heaves him back... but Jensen blocks it. Snow tries again, and again it's blocked. Jensen then lifts Snow and dives off the top turnbuckle, landing Snow stomach first across the barricade and driving himself through the table which is demolished. Jensen ceases all movement, as does Snow who remains hung across the railing. Then after about a half a minute, Snow slowly sides off the railing and onto his feet as Jensen gets to a knee. Snow throws back his hair and attempts to climb over the barricade, but as he does a chair is brought down across his head. Snow falls to the floor as Hellrazor stands there, mocking him. Jensen moves to attack Hellrazor, but Double S, Sid Snow, nails him with the fWo Hardcore Championship belt. Jensen goes down as well. Hellrazor, meanwhile, piledrives the champion onto the chair and concrete to a negative response from the crowd. The ref throws the match out following these attacks, and Hellrazor and Double S leave the scene. Both Jensen and Snow remain on the floor, however, and soon EMTs are all over them... Result: No Contest Tom the Bomb vs. Mystery Challenge Tom comes out for a singles challenge. He continues to proudly point at his white belt in kung fu. "Do you know what this means boy? It means that my hands and feet are considered somewhat dangerous weapons... and you'll know this when I beat you about the head and chest area! So anyone with the guts...That's right, you can take TOM-THE-KUNG-FU BOMB'S CHALLENGE!" After a few moments of silence, "Guilty" begins playing over the arena's loudspeakers. An angry looking Stone stalks down the entrance ramp and without a word rolls into the ring. As Tom the Kung Fu Bomb carefully enters his basic martial arts stance, Stone calmly walks over to him and grabs him by the neck. With a wrenching motion, she takes him to the mat. Tom the Kung Fu Bomb racks his brain for what to do from this position, but his scant training is telling him nothing. With a lurch, he finds himself being lifted by his neck, and then just as quickly, Stone's dropped him back-first over her knee. Tom clutches the small of his back... which lets Stone drive her thick black shoe directly into his face. Tom yelps and tries to cover his face, so Stone kicks him in the groin. Tom's eyes go wide and buglike, and his face reddens... and Stone picks him up to his feet. Tom wavers a bit, before Stone lifts her knee into his stomach, doubling him over. With a wrench about the waist, Stone lifts him above her head, and spins once before dropping him to the mat in her finishing move, the Earthquake. Hooking her ankles over his arms, Stone holds Tom down while the referee counts to three. Then, just as abruptly as she came, Stone leaves as her music plays. Result: Stone by Pinfall Eron (c) vs. Boston Strangler "O Fortuna" can mean only one thing; that being the arrival of none other than the Boston Strangler. Once it begins, the crowd responds as if on cue with a huge ovation, showing their devotion for the ever-cryptic Strangler. In stark contrast to this reception, the fWo Internet title holder, Eron the Relentless, follows yet finds himself the target of much heckling and jeering. Indifferent as ever, Eron only scoffs at them, cocky as usual, confidence accompanying him with every step. Once both Eron and Strangler are prepared to do battle, the bell is rung, and there is no turning back for either man. Before any punches are thrown, Rain, the one responsible for the backstage mugging of Strangler just one Slaughter ago, emerges and simply comes to a halt on the ramp, watching on silently. Meanwhile, inside the ring, an over enthusiastic Eron lunges to lock up, yet Strangler side-steps him and watches as he drops to the mat. Keeping him helplessly grounded, Strangler delivers a chain of stomps to Eron's back. Now lifting him and pitching him to the ropes, Strangler meets him on the rebound, lifting him into a Spinebuster, before capping it off by drilling the champion into the canvas. Then bringing him to a standing position once more, Strangler sends him to the ropes for another time as he himself heads off the opposite ropes and as they near in the center of the ring, Strangler leaps into a cross body that levels Eron. However, in this train wreck, only Strangler escapes unscathed, so he lifts Eron and works his way into a back breaker drop. He covers yet manages only a two count. As he begins to lift Eron and has him on his feet, Eron staggers Strangler, boxing his ears with a Bell Clap maneuver. Strangler's ears wringing, Eron's shoves him into the corner and smothers him with lefts and rights, looking to wear down the man before him. The referee interjects, squeezing in between and pleading for Eron to bring the match out of the corner. Eron hisses angrily, however allows the referee to step aside before lashing out with a precise kick to the gut. Then positioning himself correctly, he carries Strangler into a side Russian Legsweep. Eron looks for the cover, yet as the referee brings his hand down upon the canvas for a third time, Strangler kicks out just in time. Eron berates the referee, irate at his incompetence. Nevertheless, he looks to continue, whipping Strangler to the ropes. Strangler latches onto them, preventing himself from rebounding off the moment Rain decides to alter the outcome. Eron sees this and thus begins to distract the referee, tiptoeing the thin line between a heated disagreement with the referee and disqualification, anticipating Rain to get the job done for him. Strangler is caught off guard when Rain grabs his ankle and tears him from the ring. Rain makes good on his name and rains down with a torrent of punches. Strangler is unable to fire back, and pays for this as Rain muscles him to the floor, leaving him senseless. Eron finds an easy way out as Rain heads backstage. The referee sees Strangler, down for the count, and upon reaching the count of ten, officially awards the match to Eron by countout. Result: Eron by Count Out Looking for a partner Backstage Black Quicksilver put his hands on his hips and evaluated what was in front of him. Cairn stood there, scratching himself quietly. Black Quicksilver grunted in a noise that signaled neither approval or disapproval. He turned to Stump. "So what do you think? Team material?" Stump considered, and remained quiet. Black Quicksilver turned back to Cairn. "You say your partners are missing. Steel and Rottclog." Cairn held up a finger. "RottDOG actually. He got his name after some people in the AWA caught him having relations with..." Black Quicksilver cut him off with a wave of his hand. "That's unimportant. Listen Cairn, obviously you're not my first choice. I was hoping that Barry Bladerth would be available, but he's in deep soccer training. This brings us... to you. Now I've set up a little match for you with Hellrazor to test your worth to me as a potential partner. So if you will proceed to the ring?" Cairn nodded, and flexed. Under his breath he began a silent chant. "Dontblowthis. Dontblowthis. Dont..." But as Cairn reached the arena entrance he stumbled, and rolled out of the backstage door and down the entrance ramp. As he rolled he began to gain speed, bumping and tumbling down the entrance ramp until he finally stopped in a heap at the base. Black Quicksilver looked from behind the curtain as fWo medical staff arrived to haul away Cairn on a stretcher. He was still shaking his head in disgust when Hellrazor arrived behind him and looked down at the medical staff wheeling Cairn away. "Is that my match being wheeled away?" Black Quicksilver nodded. Hellrazor grunted. "Well, wheeled away before the match or wheeled away after the match... same end result." Black Quicksilver sighed. "Back to the drawing board. Say... how's Lamont?" Hellrazor shrugged. "It's all good. I just bought him one of the little fuzzy dangly balls." Black Quicksilver nodded, "Yeah... I was thinking about getting one of those for Stump." Hellrazor looked over at Stump, and then back to Black Quicksilver. "You know, you're just not right." Machina vs. Mad Trucker The crowd shifted nervously, anxious for the next match to begin. Suddenly, the lights dimmed. Blue light began to flash, and smoke came from the top of the entrance ramp. "Come With Me" began to play, and the "L" of the Lone Gunmen blazed onto the SlaughterScreen. Machina stood at the top of the ramp, dressed in a Coke t-shirt, arms outstretched, and a bottle of Coke in each hand. As he walked to the ring fWo attendants suspended a can of Coke and a can of Pepsi on opposite corners of the ring, getting things ready for the upcoming match. Machina had made it to the ring, and was just starting to swing into the ring when the sound came. Ripping from the arena speakers like a message from God, fans rose to their feet and began to cheer. "TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" The Mad Trucker came out onto the entrance ramp, and he wasn't alone. By his side was the adorable young girl who was known to Pepsi fans everywhere. With pigtails on each side of her head swaying, she walked cheerfully down to the ring with the Trucker. Machina watched them coming, his glare never leaving the face of the Trucker. On the Trucker's part he too was all business... eyes locked on Machina's and his usual jovial expression one of intensity and anger. For on the Trucker's part this was about Cola, and not a matter to be taken lightly. The Trucker neared the ring, and suddenly Machina's stoic form was broken as he ran across the ring and slid to the outside, catching the Trucker in the face with both boots. Machina shot an angry glare at the young girl at ringside who was looking at him stubbornly and then returned to the Trucker with a hard boot to the head. Signs of ring rust were obvious, as the Trucker was working to get back on his feet and get back into the smash mouth style of wrestling in the fWo. Machina looked up at the Pepsi can dangling high above the ring and considered going after it. He kicked at the Trucker once more and began to climb, just inches away from the prize, honor, and the match won. But before he could reach the can the Trucker was on his feet, on the apron, and had grabbed his leg, gripping it. Machina fought with him for a moment, both men high above the floor. Then Machina lost his grip, and the Trucker tossed Machina backward from the post and into the announcing table. The announcing table shattered, and Machina lay broken on the floor. Medical officials ran to ringside and worked to quickly get Machina's mask off to see if the blood coming from his mouth was the sign of anything more serious. The Mad Trucker looked on with some concern, for the moment forgetting the match at hand. They loaded Machina onto a gurney and began to take him away, the crowd cheering what they had just seen. The Trucker looked around the arena to see what he should do next, when the crowd began to make noise. Machina had gotten to his feet, snot coming out of his nose, and was limping his way back to the ring. The Trucker hopped off the apron and meet him at ringside with a big boot to the head that sent Machina spiraling backward. The two continued to brawl on the outside, the little girl cheering on the Trucker as they did. Machina, barely able to stand, held onto the metal apron for support as the Trucker climbed onto the ring apron. The Mad Trucker bellowed, and pitched himself from the apron toward Machina. But at that moment Machina noticed that his shoe had become untied during the crash through the table and he bent down to correct the problem. The Trucker hurtled over him, colliding with the metal barrier and falling to the ground in a heap. Machina looked over at him and smiled. He looked up at the can of Pepsi. All was clear for him, and he moved toward his goal. Suddenly he felt a small hand grabbing his leg, and he turned around. The little girl stood there, bottom lip stuck out stubbornly. Machina tried to shake her free, but she had a strong grip. "C'mon you little brat... get off'a me!" The little girl glared at Machina and her words came out in a deep throated growl. "Eat shit you bastard." Machina's eyes grew wide as the little girl grabbed him with her other arm as well, and then suplexed him off the ring apron and down to the arena floor. The little girl moved over to the steel steps and picked them up effortlessly, raising them above her head and moving over to Machina. Machina yelped, "Holy crap! Quick... bring out the bears! The bears now!" From the backstage area a growling was heard, and the two large Coke polar bears appeared at the top of the entrance ramp. Machina pointed to the young girl with the steel steps, and the polar bears loped toward her with jaws snarling. They fell upon her, ripping and tearing flesh from bone and sending a spray of blood out to the crowd. Machina got to his feet. "Ha ha! Take that you little bitch!" The bears heard him, and turned their attention to the strange masked man who was making strange loud noises. The bears growled and leapt toward him, and Machina quickly ran back up the entrance ramp for the backstage area, the two polar bears closely behind. By this time the Mad Trucker had regained his bearings, and got to his feet. Shaking off the cobwebs he looked down at the carcass of the young girl and shrugged. Rolling into the ring he climbed slowly up the turnbuckle post, and grabbed the Coke can, securing the victory. The crowd rose to their feet with loud cheers, and the Trucker triumphantly tossed the Coke can to the mat and stomped on it. "The Joy of Cola" played over the arena speakers, and the Trucker basked in the glow of thousands of adoring fans as Slaughter went to a commercial break. Result: Mad Trucker Underground (c) vs. Guano Apes Slaughter comes back from commercial to the image of the ominous, evil, satanic, etc. steel cage slowly being lowered from the roof of the arena. It finally settles over the ring, and the music of the Guano Apes begins to play as the announcers tell the fans that this match can be won either by escaping the cage or by pinfall or submission. The Apes enter through the door, and test out the cage by rattling it. The music of the Underground starts playing and Wretch and Mike Bear make their way to the ring to a huge ovation from the fans. Bear and Wretch elect to enter the cage by climbing up the side and over, and they stop at the top of the cage to raise their belts high into the air before throwing them down to the timekeeper and continuing down the cage into the ring. The bell rings and Jackhammer and Bear start. Bear immediately charges at Jackhammer who takes him up and over with a huge powerslam. Bear gets up, but is met by a huge foot to the head by Jackhammer, knocking him back down. Jackhammer covers, but Bear kicks out at two. Jackhammer brings Bear up and whips him into the ropes with extra authority, causing Bear to slam hard into the cage as he hits the ropes, before stumbling back towards Jackhammer, who lifts him up in a gorilla press. Jackhammer then gorilla presses Bear back-first into the cage, and Bear falls off the cage, striking his throat on the top rope as he comes down and landing on his back. Jackhammer takes a moment to pump up the crowd, and covers Bear again. The ref counts, but Bear manages to kick out at two. Jackhammer tags in his partner Sphere, who teams with him to whip Bear into the ropes and catch him with a double clothesline. Jackhammer then hits a standing splash on Bear as Sphere drops the leg. Jackhammer exits the ring as Sphere covers, but Bear again kicks out. Sphere brings Bear up, and hits a snap suplex, taking him over. Sphere then mounts the second rope and leaps off, driving an elbow into the throat of Bear. He covers Bear, but Bear again kicks out. Sphere brings Bear to his feet again, and spikes him with a DDT. Sphere this time elects to attempt to exit the cage via the door, but Wretch quickly enters the ring and back suplexes him, drawing in Jackhammer, who hits a stiff clothesline on Wretch. Jackhammer lifts Wretch in a gorilla press, then throws him face-first into the cage. Meanwhile, Bear has gotten back to his feet, and Sphere is struggling to his as well. Bear measures Sphere, and clocks him with a superkick. Bear quickly covers Sphere, but Jackhammer breaks the count at two. At this, the referee escorts first Jackhammer then Wretch back to their corners, and Bear and Sphere make their ways to their feet. Sphere attempts a right hand, but Bear blocks it and lifts Sphere up in a Death Valley Driver, before dropping him to the mat. Bear is getting his second wind and elects to climb the cage as Sphere regains his senses. Jackhammer reaches and stretches for the tag from Sphere... and gets it, not bothering to enter the ring in favor of climbing up with Bear. As Jackhammer is fresh, he manages to reach Bear as Bear straddles the top of the cage. Jackhammer grabs ahold of Bear, who attempts to fight him off with punches. Jackhammer counters with punches of his own, and Bear nearly topples over and wins from the force of one, but is grabbed and pulled back over by Jackhammer. Jackhammer puts Bear's head between his legs, and signals that he's about to end it. He then lifts Bear up and falls backwards off the top of the cage, nailing a fallback powerbomb and landing right in the middle of the ring. Both men hit hard, and Bear bounces from the impact of the move. Fortunately for Wretch, he lands right in the Underground corner, and Wretch reaches out and tags his partner's barely-conscious body. Wretch quickly climbs to the top rope, and leaps off with a 450 splash onto the prone body of Jackhammer. He hooks the leg and the ref begins to count, registering the three-count just a split-second before the weary Sphere breaks it up. Wretch quickly gets up and pulls his partner Bear to his feet, and they celebrate as the cage is raised. Sphere slowly manages to rouse Jackhammer, and the Guano Apes prepare to leave the ring after being defeated. Bear stops them as he retrieves a microphone. "Nice match you guys. That really could have gone either way. I'll tell you, Jackhammer, that powerbomb hurt like hell. You guys'll get another match some time down the road." The two teams shake hands and the Guano Apes leave, at least content they'll receive a rematch. Bear & Wretch stay in the ring, and Bear gets on the microphone once more. "Well, it seems like we have another tag team to deal with. Havok, Panther, we can't wait for Trapped, so how about on Wednesday we have a little tag match..." "Killed The Cat" begins palying, and out from the back come Havok & The Black Panther. Bear doesn't look happy that they interrupted him, but before he can say anything Havok begins to speak. Before he can, however, Bear interrupts him."Oh wait, did I forget to mention, this'll be a six man tag match. We just so happen to have a new member of The Underground waiting in the wings, so you go backstage and see if you can get anyone to team with your overconfident asses and we'll see you on Warfare." Panther grabs the microphone from Havok's hands, which Havok doesn't appear to be happy about but listens as his partner begins to speak. "Mystery partner, huh? Buddy, I can think of a couple dozen people backstage looking to kick your ass. It's a deal." Slaughter goes to a commercial break as the Millennium Men and The Underground continue to argue off-camera in the ring... Result: Underground by Pinfall Making up Machina rubbed his shoulder where the polar bear had scratched him with it's claws. It hurt some, but Machina wasn't that concerned. He sat on a bench and sighed. His Coke sponsorship was gone for sure. The Coca Cola representative who packed the polar bears back into their cages wouldn't even speak to him, and despite the mauling of their competitor's figurehead they were obviously not happy with him. With that disappointment under his belt he went in search of Rudo, and couldn't find him. Sid was in their dressing room, shouting into a telephone at his lame boyfriend David. Likewise Penny was strangely absent from the usual picketing that was going on outside. He was certain he would catch up with her sooner or later, after all they had a wedding to plan. Machina was hoping the wedding would happen before Ultra Violet's wedding. As far as he knew UV hadn't set a date, so Machina had a good change of beating it. Machina sighed again. He was about to lay back on the bench and catch a couple of winks before the show wrapped and everyone started heading off when a knock came at the door. "Come in..." The door opened, and the Mad Trucker entered. "Hey Ed." Machina waved, "Yeah what do you want Thomas? Come to gloat?" The Trucker sat, and offered a hand. "Nah, just came to say thanks. That was a great match. Listen we have our fights out there, but when I saw that little girl get mauled it occurred to me that these cola wars aren't really a big deal. Friends?" Machina looked at the outstretched hand carefully, and then smiled and shook it. "Yeah, you're right Thomas. It's just cola after all... it's not worth getting all angry about it. Sure I lost some money... but hey, easy come, easy go. Here... let's get some real drinks." The Mad Trucker stood. "Yeah... yeah, I'd like that." They walked over to the catering table and Machina reached for two cups. He handed one to the Trucker and picked up a bag of tea and tore it open. He steeped the tea into hot water, whistling as he did it. The Trucker watched this with a look of disgust on his face. "What in sam hill is this? You some kind of nancy boy?" Machina looked confused. "What? It's just tea... I always drink tea. Why... what do you drink?" The Trucker pointed to the canister of coffee. "Coffee! Coffee of course you little hoo-ha! You means to tell the Trucker that you're some kind of tea drinkin' nancy?" Machina glared at the Trucker and stabbed a finger into his chest. "Listen here you big lummock, tea is better for you, better tasting, and better all around. That black swill kills your sex drive, gives you bad breath, and isn't good for your heart!" The Trucker was aghast. "You... take... that... BACK!" He grabbed the canister of coffee and swung it at Machina, who ducked at the last second. Machina lunged at the Trucker, and fWo security quickly hit the scene to separate the two men. Machina screamed, "This isn't over fatty! This isn't over!" Trucker struggled against the security team holding him back. "I'll teach you what no good tea will do you no good nancy boy!" As the two men struggled against the men holding them back Slaughter went back to the ring... Perfect & Mitch Wilson vs. Flying Frenchie & Primetime vs. Havok & Black Panther Time for the main event had come as pryotechnics shot off near the stage, and "Killed The Cat" began to play, which signalled the entrances of The Millennium Men. The Black Panther & Havok walked out onto the stage and received a big response from the crowd as they walked down to ringside. Upon reaching the ring, Havok took off his trenchcoat while Panther did some stretches in the corner, preparing for the match at hand. While they did, the Panther' music discontinued playing and replacing it was the music of Van Halen' "Humans Being", and as it played a red spotlight bathed the stage as the rest of the building darkened. Perfect & Mitch Wilson of the FTfWo made their presence felt, the fans inside the arena jeering them resoundingly. Perfect and Mitch ignored them at first, before giving them a double-finger salute, making their reaction of jeers earsplitting. As they walked to the ring, their music was cut off by "Juke Joint Jezebel", signalling the entrance of the fWo World Champion TFF & Primetime. Perfect & Mitch heard this and turned around, waiting their presence. They didn't have to wait long as Frenchie & Primetime ran out from the back furiously. The two men representing the French Foundation and the two men representing the FTfWo began slugging it out furiously, as Frenchie & Perfect went at it while Primetime & Wilson exchanged blows. In the ring, Havok & Panther looked on as their four opponents went at it. Frenchie & Perfect brawled into the crowd as Frenchie attempted extracting revenge for the crime that occured this past Wednesday on Warfare. Closer to the ring, however, Primetime & Wilson went at it. While this went on, Panther and Havok discussed something before Panther pressed Havok high above the air and launched him into Primetime & Wilson on the outside. Frenchie & Perfect ended up on the "safe" side of the guardrail as Havok took Wilson & Primetime down, before grabbing Primetime by his dark mane of hair and rolled him into the ring. He then climbed to the top rope as the referee signalled for the bell. Panther took to his corner, while Wilson recovered and went to his corner as well. As Primetime struggled to get up, Havok leapt off the top turnbuckle with a graceful missle dropkick, slamming both feet into the chiseled jaw of Primetime. Frenchie and Perfect discontinued struggling with each other on the outside, each taking a corner as well. Havok continued his offense in the ring, whipping Primetime into the ropes and ducking for a backdrop. Primetime leapfrogged it and bounced off the ropes on the opposite side, before returning and being taken down with a flying headscissors. Both men sprung to their feet and Primetime charged at Havok. Havok took him down with a hiptoss, and once again Primetime got up and this time Havok took Primetime down with a Japanese Armdrag. Primetime backed off into the corner with an aching back, and tagged out to Frenchie. Frenchie glared at Primetime since Primetime had difficulty with Havok, and Frenchie came in. He looked at Havok as Havok prepared to take him on, but Frenchie didn't want Havok. He wanted revenge. He wanted.. Perfect. Havok reluctantly obliged and tagged out to the World's Greatest Athlete. Perfect came in with a cocky smile and Frenchie quickly charged and took him down with a spear. Frenchie pounded him relentlessly with lefts and rights. From behind, Mitch came in and pulled Frenchie off of his partner by the mask, before slapping him. This infuriated the proud Frenchman, and he gave chase to Wilson. From behind however came Perfect with a double axehandle, before hooking the Frenchie for a snap suplex. Perfect brought Frenchie up and whipped him to the ropes as Mitch returned to the corner. As Frenchie returned with Perfect ducking for a backdrop, Frenchie grabbed Perfect by the hair and slammed him head first into the mat. Frenchie continued, kicking at Perfect continuously. He brought Perfect up and chopped him across the chest several times, before taking him down with a snapmare takeover. He followed this with a sharp kick to the back, which undoubtedly sent a chill up the spine of the only four-time fWo World Champion in the history of the fWo. Frenchie continued by bringing Perfect up and whipping him into the corner near Havok & Perfect, and Panther made a blind tag. As Perfect staggered out Frenchie took him down with the dreaded drop-toe-hold, before applying a hammerlock while Perfect was on the mat chest first. From behind came the massive stature of the Panther, and he pulled Frenchie off by the hair, before turning him around and gorilla pressing him high to the mat, and as Perfect rolled out to the floor to recover, Panther slammed him to the mat with ease. Frenchie was quick to recover however, and he charged at Panther, but Panther took his massive right hand and slapped the chest of the man he lost to at Invasion! in January. Panther followed by wrapping his hand around the throat of the Frenchie, and chokeslamming him to the mat. He followed this up by going for a suplex, but rather an ordinary suplex, he took him to the corner and set him up for the Pounce. Sensing victory, he climbed up to the second rope and hooked Frenchie for his patented superplex. Moments later, both men flew through the air and hit the mat. Panther was about to go for the cover, when Havok requested that he be tagged in so he could cap things off with the Millennium-sault. Panther obliged after some hesitation, and Havok took his time climbing to the top rope. He flew off the top rope for it, but the Frenchie moved out of the way and Havok hit the mat. Frenchie slowly but surely crawled over for the pin, and with some help from Primetime on the outside holding down both of Havok' legs, they picked up the win and thus eliminated the Millennium Men from the three-way battle. Havok rolled to the outside and Panther confronted him as Frenchie struggled to his feet with aide of the ropes on the inside. Panther & Havok appeared to get in an argument on the outside, but cooler heads prevailed as Wilson came in to face off with the Frenchie. Panther & Havok headed up the ramp and upon getting backstage we saw them get in a confrontation with the Underground, which was quickly subdued by security. Back at ringside, the two men (Frenchie & Mitch) first talked plenty of trash, probably debating on "who made who". After some heated debate Frenchie slapped Wilson across the face. Wilson put his hand to his face, before smirking almost as if to say "alright, two can play this game." Wilson and Frenchie circled each other before locking up, and Frenchie gained the advantage with a hammerlock, but Wilson countered into hammerlock of his own. Frenchie countered this with a waistlock, but Wilson escaped with a soft elbow to the jaw, followed by a fireman's carry takeover. Wilson then hit five consecutive kneedrops, before bringing Frenchie up and taking him to the mat with a fallaway slam. He followed by going for the Fujiwara Armbar, but Frenchie quickly escaped to the floor. Seeing this, Wilson got some momentum as Frenchie recovered on the outside by bouncing off the other set of ropes and baseball sliding out onto the floor into a headscissors takeover on the outside. Wilson wasted no time and slid the Frenchie into the ring, before going up top once more and coming off with a flying elbow drop. He covered the Frenchie, but Primetime came in and interrupted the pin. Wilson tagged out to Frenchie and the two men sent Frenchie into the ropes. He returned and both men went for a kick to the midsection, followed by a rarely seen (and perhaps never done before) Death Valley Driver. Perfect covered Frenchie, but Primetime came in and pulled him off by the leg. Perfect seemed disturbed but continued on the offensive. He brought his opponent up by the hair and hit him with various forearms to the chin, before whipping Frenchie into the corner. Frenchie reversed it and Perfect slammed into the corner. Frenchie followed in slowly and Perfect nailed him with a shot to the jaw, followed by a kick, but Frenchie managed to catch the kick in time, grasping ahold of his foot. Perfect countered with an enziguiri, but Frenchie ducked. Perfect crumpled up on the mat before Frenchie let go of his foot and in exhaustion began to walk over to Primetime... Wilson came in and this distracted the referee, and Frenchie made the tag. Primetime came in a house of fire and ready to kick some ass, but the referee backed him into his corner. Perfect reached up and tagged out to Wilson, who came in and grabbed Frenchie and whipped him to the ropes. Frenchie returned and Wilson went for a tiltawhirl backbreaker and hit it. Sensing Frenchie was almost out of it, Wilson tagged out to Perfect and the two men sent Frenchie to the ropes. Frenchie returned and Wilson & Perfect went for a double-team clothesline but Frenchie ducked and returned for a clothesline of his own before falling to the mat and making the long crawl towards Primetime. He finally tags out, and Primetime gets his chance. He nails Wilson with a fury of punches, before taking both Wilson and Perfect and slamming them head first into each other. Primetime took control by sending Wilson over the top rope onto the cement floor, and upon seeing this the fWo Internet Champion Eron The Relentless hopped over the guardrail and took his opportunity, slamming a chair into the back of Wilson before dumping him over the guardrail. Primetime and Frenchie took control in the ring by whipping Perfect to the ropes and as Perfect returned Frenchie kicked him in the stomach, before Primetime took him down with a swinging neckbreaker. Eron & Wilson brawled into the crowd while inside the ring Primetime set Perfect up for the Primetime Bomb while Frenchie went to the top rope for his coup de grace. Primetime hit the powerbomb while Frenchie flew off with the 450 splash, and Primetime covered Perfect for the subsequent pinfall and victory. Frenchie & Primetime celebrated as Scott Collins emerged from the shadows backstage headed towards the ring. As the Frenchie held his coveted Championship high overhead, Collins entered the ring cautiously preparing to pound on Perfect. Frenchie jokingly asked Collins if he had tasted any good cakes lately, but got no response as Collins went after Perfect. Frenchie & Primetime left the ring victorious as Collins brought Perfect up and hit the Death Penalty. Collins raised his arms as the crowd was very pleased by this, but from another direction in came Ice, who spun Collins around and took him out with a chokeslam. He then made a mock pinfall attempt on both men, his hand hitting the mat three times in mocking fashion. Ice celebrated with his two rivals out cold on the mat below as Slaughter went off the air. Result: Flying Frenchie & Primetime by Pinfall
