Introduction Previously... In 2003 the fWo rose to new heights as they battled internal struggles, a global shift from their normal operating area, and finally succeeded in becoming a mainstay promotion in the world. At Countdown, dreams were realized as after long last Brand Frontier bested Kodiak Vic Creed and took the World Heavyweight Championship for his own. But all was not well, and in a moment of horror it was revealed that Ryan Moore, often known as Fallen, was responsible for a rash of murders that culminated in a deadly killing spree which claimed the lives of Spyder, Neo, Twitch, Samuel Smooth, John Willis and Temerario. Worse, Silver Fox suffered a heart attack and critical injury at the hands of Ray Chavez, who revealed himself as the destructive Mr. X. With the management in disarray and only semi-outsider Ted Tallison with any ability to lead the promotion while battling his alcohol addition, 2004 opens on bleak news and an uncertain future... Never What It Seems Wednesday, December 31, 2003 Ric waded his way through the smoke, coughing bits of it back out as he progressed. His eyes stung, his chest burned, and the treacherously slick ground beneath him threatened to spill him onto the arena's concrete floors at any moment, but he dared not stop. One of his few remaining virtues was his dogged loyalty to his friends, and he could not dream of leaving while a friend might be in danger. The haze parted for a moment, and Ric brushed past one of the firefighters trying to clear the scene. The man tried to interject, tried to prevent Ric from moving forward, but Ric was having none of it, brushing the man off as if he didn't even exist. A few minutes before word of the fire had spread, Pierre had left to speak with Silver Fox... and Fox was last seen in the vicinity of the fire. As Ric continued breaking past people and rescue workers, he realized for the first time that he hadn't even finished getting dressed, and that the sock on his left foot was sopping wet, not improving the tenuous traction Ric had on the floors, which were still wet from the usage of fire hoses. The smoke and the spray combined to cling to his bare chest, making it appear as if he had been exerting himself, and his jeans were beginning to take on a grimy complexion. Normally, he would have waited a few moments, finished dressing, and been content to stay a casual observer. Today, however, was not a normal day, and a gut feeling propelled him recklessly forward. Avoiding yet another person trying to counsel Ric to stay back, he broke through into an area where the wounded were being triaged. Before him, lying on a stretcher, was Pierre Delacroix, the Flying Frenchie. EMT's had placed an oxygen mask on Pierre, and Ric could already see the signs of minor burns forming. Relatively, though, Pierre looked like he was just passed out, maybe from too much smoke - he looked like he was going to be fine, and Ric sighed in relief. Then Ric's heart sank. As the EMT's lifted Pierre's stretcher to transport him, Ric saw the bloody, mangled remains of Pierre's knee. Although the EMT's were bandaging the ruined joint to the best of their ability, it was obvious to even a casual observer that this wasn't something that would be easily healed. The tears came without warning - here lie his closest friend, one of the few people who were there for him during one of his darkest periods, and Ric hadn't been there. Frenchie had asked him to come along, but Ric was more worried with getting a quick call in to Lyra, and so had opted to stay behind and start changing into street clothes. Now, Frenchie was in a bad way, and Ric knew that he could have done something to prevent it. The medics were pushing Pierre towards a waiting ambulance. One of them tried to intercept as Ric moved to join them, but the glare that Chronos shot the man let him know that there would be no denying Ric on this day, and the medic wisely stood aside. Another medic acknowledged Ric with a nod of his head. "Going with us?" the EMT asked. "Try stopping me" was Ric's solemn reply. Ric reached for the stretcher, and noted for the first time that he still had his cell phone in hand, and that the line was still open. Steeling himself, he placed the receiver up to his ear. "Uh, Lyra?" "God damn it, Ric! I've been screaming at you for five minutes! Are you OK?" "I - I'm OK. It's Pierre." "Oh my god. What happened?" "I'm not sure, but it - it doesn't look good. Um, I'll call you from the hospital, OK? And uh, could you call Jasmin? I think she'll want to... she'll want..." "She's here with me, Ric - we were getting things together for Pierre's birthday party tomorrow. I'll tell her, and I love you." "You too." With that, Ric hung up the phone. They had reached the ambulance, and Ric climbed aboard behind the medics. There was only one thing certain in Ric's mind as he watched the various IV's and painkillers get administered to his longtime comrade. This was going to be Pierre's worst birthday ever. The Aftermath Thursday, January 1, 2004 Hidden deep in the shadows of the Madison Square Garden entrance, Ray Chavez looked down at the fallen owner of the fWo.
