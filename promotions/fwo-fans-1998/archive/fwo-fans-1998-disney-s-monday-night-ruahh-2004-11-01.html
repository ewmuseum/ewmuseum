The Crisp fans in the crowd let out another "OH SNAP!", and the fans who had begun to support Wilson mixed in some partisan booing of their own, not happy at all with this disrespectful display. Crisp went back to his earlier rant. "You're facing the man who is owed a shot at the title. And I don't mean Brand Frontier. "I mean ME." The crowd, knowing this was coming, still roared in anticipation. And Crisp beamed. "So, in one week, at about this time, you'll be staring at the REIGNING WORLD CHAMP. We will have switched places. And you'll get to go have fun going back into the shadows, namely your father's, where you feel more comfortable. Because I want what's mine... what's been owed to me since your little asstag relay friends, the Supertards, did to me a few months ago." One week, Bitch. Take that title EVERYWHERE. Treat it like the goddamn Stanley Cup, and take as many pictures of it you can... because, in seven short days, the title comes home to the Icon and his fans." Some of the crowd began chant "ICON!" while Mitch burned with anger. A "WILSON!" chant rose up from the opposite side, trying to drown out the noise of the other half of the crowd. Crisp, meanwhile, had one more thing to say. "Oh, and since your dominatrix Death took Black Quicksilver's defining moment at CyberSlam, I'm taking your biggest night to revel in the glory of my return, and to the fWo's return to five-star matches and 10-point ratings. Just the start of payback to you and the Supertards, and a reminder of what you really are... a B-list wannabe in a Superstar's shadow. "And next week, you'll be in the shadow of a champion... because the fWo World Champion is JUST THE KIND OF GUY I AM!" "Gonna Fly Now" hit the fWo speakers for the first time since early summer, and the crowd loved ever second of it, as Crisp played to the crowd, temporarily stopping to give Mitch Wilson some rather rude and unsutable hand gestures. In fact, they were so rude, they cannot be printed, but would be pointed out to the FCC the next day. Eyes blazing, Mitch stood in the ring and pointed at his title, then pointed at the sky, then at Crisp. The implication was clear. Crisp had come out, destroyed the moment where Mitch meant to pay respect to the man who had made him what he was, and verbally pissed on a moment that was reserved for the memory of a great man... and a father. For no other reason than to promote himself. For the memory of Slade Wilson... it would not stand. Crisp, meanwhile, pointed out that he didn't care by closing his hand and waving it up and down, causing the FCC to look at more fWo footage the next day. And so, RUAHH~! faded to black, admist not one, but two big shocks to close the show...
