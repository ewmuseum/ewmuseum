fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 25 November 2001

25 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "Twenty four hours ago a battle raged that few had matched before. Erik Kelly and the fWo Champion Ric Chronos did battle in the squared circle for the greatest prize in the industry: the fWo World Heavyweight Championship. In a fierce battle Ric Chronos somehow managed to hold onto the title, but it was not a fight that left the foreman on 3vil unscathed. In the end it was not Ric Chronos who left the arena with the title, nor the members of the fWo that 3vil challenged from that back. A mysterious masked man entered the ring from the crowd, but as Chronos prepared to teach the intruder a lesson he hesitated with recognition. A moment later, the mysterious man had escaped out of the ring with a pinfall and the fWo title. Chronos had recognized something that nobody else had... the mysterious man was his former partner from years back, and now Fallen is champion." The crowd rose to their feet as the screen faded once more to black, the narration fading to silence. The energy in the arena was high... just twenty four hours ago the fWo event Fallout had been broadcast and nobody had anticipated the events that had unfolded. With many questions unanswered, the fans waited anxiously for answers. When the first man appeared at the top of the entrance ramp, they roared with approval. Chris Universal walked out onto the entrance ramp, microphone in hand and a grim expression on his face. He walked silently down the entrance ramp, but halfway down he turned, looking anxiously back up the ramp. He wore an expression that was difficult to read, but fans nearby noticed his taut face and white knuckle hands. Universal waited in the middle of the entrance ramp, but he did not have to wait for long. The lights dimmed, and the music of Ric Chronos spread out through the arena. Through the haze of smoke and lights three men appeared, and unlike their appearance the previous night there was no signs of arrogance on their faces. 3vil looked determined, angry, and strangely confidant. The Flying Frenchie, Primetime and Ric Chronos walked down the entrance ramp toward Chris Universal, who instead of waiting for him simply turned and headed toward the ring. With Universal leading the way, the four men approached and entered the ring as the cheers from the crowd turned to loud boos and occasional chants. If 3vil heard them, they did not dignify their actions with any kind of response. They walked to the ring, and reaching it climbed inside and looked pointedly at Universal. Chris Universal looked out at the crowd, strong emotions clearly raging through him. As he raised his microphone, the crowd fell silent. "Ladies and gentlemen... thank you. Thank you for coming tonight to this presentation of Sunday Night Slaughter. This is a very special show, with several key matches as we set the stage for Twilight, our..." But Chris Universal was cut off by Ric Chronos, who jerked back on him by the back of his shirt and shouted something that wasn't picked up by the microphone. Universal's eyes flashed and he glared back at Chronos with a tight snarl escaping his lips. The two glared at each other, and the moment played out as the Frenchie and Primetime looked on anxiously. For a moment it looked as though Universal was going to remove his shirt and fight Chronos right here and now, but then Universal's eyes dropped and he cursed. He turned and raised the microphone again to his lips. "Last night we saw an epic battle between Erik Kelly and Ric Chronos. While Chronos emerged the victor..." Universal turned and with his eyes narrowed glared at Chronos, "... I'm certain that Kelly will demand a rematch very soon. But for now Ric Chronos emerged as champion. But then, in a display of excessive arrogance and stupidity, 3vil opened up challenges to wrestlers in the back. Douglas, Black Quicksilver, the Boston Strangler and last night's tournament winner Ruben Ross all came to the ring, but it was clear that the challenge was phony... a scam put on by cowards. After attacking like a pack of dogs, another challenger showed himself. This one masked to hide his identity..." At this point the crowd burst into applause and chants of "Fallen" wove through the crowd. Chronos looked around himself and shouted at the crowd to silence, as the Frenchie held his hands to his ears. Universal held the microphone down, smiling as he watched 3vil's reactions to the crowd. As they raged, Chronos pointed back at Universal, yelling at him to get on with it. Universal's expression soured, and he turned back to the microphone and held his hands up for silence. "As you know now, this challenge was former fWo wrestler Fallen. None of us expected him to return, and none of us even knew where he was. We have not seen him in the fWo for more than a year and a half, nor on television for almost a year. When he appeared, he humbled this man here by not only taking him down in this very ring, but pinning him and capturing the fWo World Heavyweight Championship!" The crowd roared with approval, but quickly silenced as Universal held the microphone up to his lips again. "However, it is my sad duty to inform all of you and John Skyler... Fallen... that as he is not a member of the fWo roster, I cannot sanction or approve his title victory. You are not a member of the fWo roster, and as such cannot compete in an fWo without special permission, and certainly not for an fWo title. Despite my regret that I cannot crown you fWo World Heavyweight Champion, I must ask you to return the title and championship to the rightful fWo World Heavyweight Champion Ric Chronos." The boos rang out through the arena, and as quickly as Universal had finished his comments trash began to rain through the air toward the ring. The members of 3vil ignored this however, nodding with approval at Universal's words and raising their hands in the air as if they had won a major victory. Universal stood silent, clearly torn by conflicting emotions. Anger burned on his face, but he was determined on the path he had chosen. Holding the microphone to his lips once more, Universal pointed up the entrance ramp. "So Fallen, I know that you are in the back, and I ask you to come out right now." The crowd turned anxiously toward the entrance ramp, and their boos turned to cheers as Fallen strode out onto the entrance ramp. He had not appeared on the entrance ramp for the fWo in a long time, but little had changed in his demeanor. He looked quiet and sullen, eyes staring out through strands of long and wild hair. The only change was the gold title he had slung over his shoulder, the fWo World Heavyweight Championship... and despite Fallen's dark and moody appearance the title suited him. He walked down the entrance ramp, taking his time and looking down at the ramp and his feet rather than the ring. Inside the ring 3vil looked down at him, the Frenchie and Primetime sneering and laughing at his shabby appearance. Ric Chronos did not join them however, and looked down at Fallen with fury. His former partner not so long ago, there was no love lost between them now. As Fallen reached the ring and rolled under the ropes Chronos stood his ground, and the tension between the two men could be cut with a knife. "John, I'm sorry, but you..." But as Universal began to speak Fallen snapped his neck and glared at him and Universal fell silent. Fallen returned his gaze toward Chronos, still silent, and moved forward until the two men were standing face to face with mere inches between them. No words were spoken, and neither man looked down. Slowly, Ric Chronos raised an arm and reached for the title over Fallen's shoulder. Before he could grasp it, Fallen grabbed his arm, and pushed it to one side. Chronos bared his teeth, but Fallen stood his ground. Even more slowly than Chronos, Fallen reached up and pulled the belt off of his shoulder. The crowd booed as he held it, then looked slowly to one side at it. Turning his attention back to Chronos and locking his gaze with his, Fallen dropped the title on the mat between them. The sound echoed through the arena, and for a moment the crowd was silent before the boos began again. Then Fallen turned completely away from Chronos, giving Universal one final stare before heading for the ropes. Chronos looked down at the title, then sneered and nodded to Frenchie and Primetime. The two sprang into action, rushing forward and grabbing Fallen from behind. Before he could make a move to defend himself, Primetime and Frenchie jerked him back and slammed him down into the mat. Chronos directed the two, holding back Universal with one arm as the Frenchie and Primetime continued to execute Fallen with calculated strikes. Unable to defend himself, Fallen was pounded by Frenchie and Primetime who punched and stomped the helpless man on the mat. Finally Primetime and Frenchie stood and held back Universal as Chronos stepped forward. He glared down at Fallen, then shouted down at the helpless man. "I ran you out of this fed... I ran you out you son of a bitch... and you're never coming back!" Chronos quickly scooped up Fallen from the mat and power bombed Fallen hard, dropping him hard rather than finishing the move. Screaming at the crowd and Fallen, Chronos picked him up a second time and power bombed him again, then went for a third. On the third, Chronos moved Fallen toward the ropes when he had him up in the air and power bombed him over the ropes and down to the steel ramp below with a shattering crash and thud. Fallen screamed in pain as Chronos screamed in victory, and Fallen lay still. Chronos returned to the center of the ramp and raised the championship belt up in the air. The crowd responded with boos, and Chronos snarled around at them. He turned to Universal and grabbed his microphone while shoving him backward. Screaming into the microphone, the feedback nearly overcame his screams. "You son of a bitch... this is my title, my championship, and nobody is ever going to take it away from me! Not you, not Kelly, not the Strangler... not anyone! You go back and crawl under whatever rock you came out from... you're not good enough to be in my ring... none of you are and none of you ever will be! This is my ring, 3vil's ring! This is my world championship... I own this title and I'll do that to anyone who steps in my path!" Ric Chronos threw the microphone to the ground, gave one final glare to Chris Universal, and rolled out of the ring with the Frenchie and Primetime closely behind him. They stopped for a moment at the body of Fallen, laying unconscious and still on the steel entrance ramp. Chronos sneered at him, then turned and walked up the ramp and into the back. Inside the ring Chris Universal held his head in his hands, clearly devastated by what he was forced to do. He looked at Fallen outside of the ring and shook his head in sadness. "I'm sorry John... God, I don't know what you were thinking... you had to know it would go this way..." Universal directed the medical staff to help Fallen to the back, and then he too walked up the entrance ramp to the back. Inside the arena the crowd screamed their outrage... but their words would have no effect. For the moment Chronos was on top of the promotion once more... and they were helpless to stop it. As 3vil stood in the ring, trash continued to be thrown into the ring by enraged fans. The trio of men basked in the hatred of the crowd. The fans continued to show their displeasure... but their reaction of outrage and anger changed. "Slave To The Grind" by Skid Row hit the speakers, and Black Quicksilver appeared on the stage with STUMP in his hands. A burst of cheers rang out from the crowd, and as fireworks shot off throughout the stadium, Quicksilver charged towards the ring. Chronos charged towards Quicksilver with a lariat, but BQ ducked and when Chronos turned around, he was met with a STUMP to the head. Frenchie & Primetime attempted to advance on Quicksilver, but the referee stood in between them and Quicksilver, ordering them out of the ring as he signaled for the bell. The match was now officially underway. fWo World Champion Ric Chronos vs. Black Quicksilver w/ STUMP Quicksilver placed STUMP in the corner as Chronos returned to his feet. The fans cheered wildly as Frenchie & Primetime went apeshit outside the ring, spewing profanities at the referee and the fans themselves. Quicksilver rushed towards Chronos with a spinning heel kick, and hit his impending target. Chronos dropped to the mat and Quicksilver was eager to go for the early pin. Chronos kicked out with authority, and as both men got back to a vertical base Chronos struck BQ with a kick, and followed it up with a DDT attempt. Quicksilver quickly reversed it into a Northern Lights suplex, with a bridge. One. Two. Chronos kicked out, and both men rushed to their feet once again. Chronos painted Quicksilver' chest a crimson color, using reverse knife edge chops to accomplish the task. He followed it by sending BQ to the ropes, and when BQ returned Chronos went for a sidewalk slam, but Quicksilver somehow turned it into a pair of tiltawhirl headscissors. Chronos went down to the mat but quickly got back up. Quicksilver attacked with a kick, followed by a Cubic Zirconium Cutter attempt. Chronos pushed him towards the ropes and when BQ returned Chronos attempted a lariat. BQ went to the mat and slid in between the legs of Chronos on the mat. Chronos turned around and was met by a dropkick by BQ that sent him stumbling backwards into a corner. Quicksilver charged and hit a handspring elbow. As Chronos groggily staggered out from the corner, the former six-time fWo Cruiserweight Champion charged and laid him out from behind with a bulldog. From there, he hit a standing moonsault. Feeling this sequence of moves was enough to take a shot at winning the match again, Quicksilver covered and hooked the leg. One. Two. Two and a half. Chronos raised his shoulder up, and Quicksilver held his head in frustration, knowing how close he was to winning the match. Quicksilver turned and stared out at the sea of fans, before he looked back towards Chronos. Chronos was now on the outside, and Frenchie & Primetime walked over to help him formulate a gameplan to gain the advantage from BQ. Quicksilver had a lot of momentum and by taking this break from the action it allowed Chronos to save him some time. The referee, meanwhile, followed the rules of the match and began counting to ten. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. Seven. The face of Quicksilver turned to an emotion of worry, and he wasn't going to stand for this any longer. With a head of steam, Quicksilver charged and went over the top rope with a somersault plancha. Like a bowling ball being rolled down the alley towards a couple of pins, Quicksilver hit Chronos & Frenchie, but somehow Primetime escaped harm. The referee stopped the count and joined the four men on the outside. Frenchie attempted to strike at Quicksilver, gritting his teeth and showing outrage at what Quicksilver had done. With the referee occupied at holding Frenchie at bay, Primetime reached inside the ring and grabbed STUMP. When Quicksilver turned around, he fell victim via Primetime, as Primetime hit him with STUMP. Primetime stood over Quicksilver and smiled, before jawing with some fans at ringside. Chronos was now back to his feet and he quickly began stomping away at the head and neck area of the challenger, before reaching down and pulling the padding that covered the arena floors. He exposed the concrete, before he got down on his hands and knees and grabbed Quicksilver, bringing him over to the concrete. Hatred and disdain ran through every fiber in Chronos' body, and he slammed the head of Quicksilver several times onto the exposed floor. Chronos stood and snarled at the fans, before he looked down at Quicksilver and snickered. He grabbed Quicksilver and placed his head between his legs, before hitting a piledriver on the concrete. Chronos rolled Quicksilver into the ring, before following him in. Confidently, Chronos placed a single boot atop the chest of the challenger. Chronos yelled at the referee who was still preoccupied with Frenchie. The referee entered the ring and his hand hit the mat once. Then again. He raised it a third time, and came within a inch of hitting the mat. Quicksilver kicked out. A furious Chronos began verbally assaulting the referee, calling him every name in the book and telling him that he would fire him if he had the power, especially after what occured last night. Chronos directed his attention back to the challenger. He stomped away at the head of Quicksilver again, before bringing him up and piledriving him again, this time on the cold, unforgiving canvas. Quicksilver' body collapsed in a heap, and Chronos followed it up with another pinfall attempt. One. Two. Somehow, someway, Quicksilver lifted his shoulder off the mat. Chronos swore in frustration, but continued, desperately trying to put BQ away. He backed up and drove a knee into the head of Quicksilver, continuing to focus on the head and neck area of BQ. Bringing Quicksilver up to his feet he planted a knee in the ribs, before hitting a reverse neckbreaker. He went for another cover, but Quicksilver kicked out. Chronos brought him up once more and whipped him to the ropes, but Quicksilver reversed it. Chronos saw BQ ducking, and telegraphed it with another neckbreaker, this time one of the swinging variety. Chronos rushed over and pinned Quicksilver once more, but he would not stay down. Chronos swore out in frustration again, before looking at his colleagues in disbelief. A dejected Chronos looked down at Quicksilver, then at the fans, and back to Quicksilver again. He brought him up and whipped him to the ropes, this time Quicksilver couldn't reverse it, and when he returned he found himself trapped in the Hourglass, or as James Armstrong affectionally called it - the sweaty armpit submission. Chronos applied the dragon sleeper, increasing the pressure in attempt to put him out for good. Chronos smirked at Frenchie and Primetime, pleased that Quicksilver appeared to be fading quickly. The referee checked over Quicksilver for any signs of life, before preparing to raise his arm. Suddenly, Quicksilver grabbed Chronos around the waist and planted him into the mat with a belly-to-back suplex. The other members of 3vil stood in disbelief, as it appeared Quicksilver was fading into unconsciousness. The referee stood above both competitors in the match and again began counting to ten. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. Seven. Eight. Quicksilver rolled over onto his stomach and crawled towards Chronos, laying an arm on the champion. The referee went to the mat and counted. One. Two. No. The fans counted along with the pin, and were frustrated that the referee didn't count to three as they did. That was the fault of Chronos however as he somehow managed to get his foot on the bottom rope. However, momentum was once again on the side of Quicksilver. He brought Chronos up and executed a reverse layout DDT, followed by a skytwister press. Quicksilver hooked the leg for a cover. One. Two. No. Primetime reached inside the ring and pulled the referee to the outside. Primetime and the referee engaged in a verbal conflict, as Quicksilver turned and looked out at Primetime and Frenchie with a look of frustration. Chronos was back to his feet however... and was in a corner, still reeling from the onslaught by Quicksilver. Quicksilver backed up and charged again as the referee returned to the ring. He looked back at Primetime once more giving him a stern warning. Unbeknownst to the official, Quicksilver attempted another handspring elbow. The crafty Chronos however pulled the referee into the corner, and instead of hitting Chronos, BQ hit the referee. The referee immediately collapsed to the mat. Quicksilver turned and saw what he had done. From the side, Chronos charged and speared Quicksilver through the ropes to the outside. The crowd sat in amazement at what they had just seen. Chronos and Quicksilver remained on the mat for a few moments, before finally Chronos reached his feet. A look of desperation had come over the face of Chronos, but he would not be sidetracked. Chronos peppered Quicksilver with a few punches, before grabbing the challenger in a headlock, and running up the stairs. From there, he planted Quicksilver through the Spanish announcers table with the Chronosphere. Shards of wood and glass splattered about, while Jose & Pepe scurried for their lives. Frenchie & Primetime looked on in approval. The referee still remained out cold in the ring. Meanwhile, a quiet whisper was played throughout the arena. "Let the bodies hit the floor." Through smoke and fog, 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly appeared on the SlaughterStage. Frenchie and Primetime turned to each other in a look of amazement, as they figured Kelly would not be around again for some time after what occured last night. Battered and bruised, Kelly began stomping towards the ring like a man hellbent for vengeance. Primetime rushed towards him on the entrance ramp. He immediately collapsed as the size eighteen and a HALF boot by Kelly left a dent in his face. Frenchie advanced on Kelly as they came face-to-face. Frenchie attempted a right hand, but Kelly blocked it before grabbing him by the throat with both hands and tossing him aside. Chronos was now getting up. He saw 'The Eradicator' coming. There was not a damn thing he could do about it. Kelly grasped his throat, his huge hand masking Chronos' neck easily. Kelly prepared to lift him with a chokeslam, but Chronos elbowed out of it and managed to escape. Chronos nailed Kelly with a forearm, but that had very little effect. Kelly kicked him in the stomach, and as Chronos bent over, Kelly had him at his mercy. Kelly grabbed him and pulled him in, before shouting "POWERBOOOOOOOOOMB!" The fans shouted it along with him, and Kelly decimated the table where the aforementioned James Armstrong and Jeremiah Frankton were sitting with a full force powerbomb. Chronos was eradicated. Kelly reached inside the ring and began to nudge the referee to awaken him. He looked down at Chronos and smiled, telling him that he was fortunate enough to have walked out of Fallout... but tonight he would not walk out, he would leave on a stretcher. The referee came to in the ring and saw both Chronos & Quicksilver laying on the floor, both covered in television monitors and the remains from the tables. The referee immediately began counting to ten. One. Two. Three. Frenchie & Primetime were back up, and they quickly began attacking Kelly. Hammering away with forearms and kicks, they brought Kelly to two knees. Four. Five. Six. Suddenly, the lights dimmed somewhat, and "Rage Against The Machine" hit. Seven. Eight. Bulls on Parade. Ruben Ross appeared and joined the fight. The referee reached nine. Just as he was about to count to ten, the fight spilled into the ring, and the referee' count was interrupted. The referee attempted to restore order, but there was too much of a pier six brawl to contain. Frenchie & Ross went at it in a corner, as did Kelly & Primetime. Before long Kelly and Primetime had fought up the ramp backstage, while Ross & Frenchie were doing the same. In the ring came Chronos, who began yelling at the referee. The referee appeared confused at first, but then Quicksilver rolled Chronos up for a pin from behind. The referee counted. One. Two. Chronos shouldered up. The World Champion came to his feet once again, infuriated at the official. From behind came Quicksilver, who turned Chronos around. Quicksilver received a kicked to the stomach, followed by Chronos attempting the Figure Four DDT. Somehow, Quicksilver backdropped out of it. Chronos held on in attempted sunset flip... but Quicksilver simply fell to his knees and hooked Chronos up for the pin. One. Two. No. Chronos inexplicably avoided defeat. Chronos reached his feet, and again began going on a tirade directed at the official once again -- but when he turned around he was wiped out via a SuperQuick Silverkick. Quicksilver followed it up with another pin. One. Two. No. With Chronos laid out, Quicksilver turned to a corner and began ascending. He looked out at the fans, who urged him to jump. Never one to disappoint the fans, Quicksilver leaped off with the SilverSplash. And missed. Chronos moved out of the way and Quicksilver hit the mat. Chronos again began berating the official, who pointed outside the ring. Chronos brushed him off before turning his attention to Quicksilver. He swiftly brought the challenger up, before going for the Chronosphere again. As he charged towards the corner, Quicksilver deftly countered it by placing Chronos on the top turnbuckle. Quicksilver followed him up and attempted a belly-to-back suplex, but Chronos escaped and landed on his feet behind him. He reached up and used his raw neck strength, placing Quicksilver atop his neck. He walked out to the center of the ring, and prepared to launch a devestating move, but Quicksilver turned it into a victory roll for the pin. One. Two. No. Chronos again escaped defeat, and the crowd sighed in frustration, knowing that Quicksilver was mere inches away from getting the win and becoming World Champion for the first time in his storied near four year career with the fWo. Putting that aside, as Chronos came to his feet Quicksilver caught him offguard with a kick, before pulling him in for a Cubic Zirconium Cutter. Unlike earlier, this time he was able to connect with the move. Without hesitation, without reconsidering what happened just moments prior, Quicksilver ascended to the top rope once again. SilverSplash. He nailed it. Quicksilver quickly hooked the legs as the fans counted along with the referee. One. Two. Three. The fans simultaneously stood to their feet and rejoiced in a standing ovation. The cheers were so loud the building began to shake. Chronos laid on the mat... distraught and dejected... while Quicksilver went outside the ring and retrieved the fWo World Title. Quicksilver raised it into the air, the fans continuing to shower him with respect and adulation. He had finally done it. Black Quicksilver was the fans Wrestling organization World Champion... or was he? Chronos finally came to in the ring, and was not happy. He kicked the ropes... and as Quicksilver quickly went backstage to enjoy the moment with friends, Chronos began destructing the place around ringside. He grabbed chairs and threw them into the ring. He attempted to destroy the tables at ringside even further, but failed. He looked underneath the ring and found his trusty sledgehammer. He used it to dismantle a pair of steel steps at ringside, and also threatened a shot at Frankton. The referee remained in the ring... which was not good for him. Chronos hit the ring and cornered him, threatening to use that sledgehammer to beat him within an inch of his life. Chronos was understandably infuriated at what had occured... and emotions of rage and anger filled his body as his blood boiled over. Chronos raised the sledgehammer into the air and prepared to unleash his fury. From behind however came his colleagues Frenchie & Primetime, and perhaps they were doing him a favor: Had Chronos gone through with what was in mind... wrestling might not be in his future. The members of 3vil discussed the situation while the referee remained in a corner... frightened. Perhaps frightened for his life. Slaughter went to a commercial break, with fans hinging in anticipation of what would occur next. Result: Quicksilver by pinfall? Error "You all... can quit celebrating... right now!" Chronos yelled as he glared at the fans. Chronos stomped around the ring with his sledgehammer in hand. "Chris Universal... get your ass out here right now. Because it's happened again. The same official who made the three count last night... the same official involved in that debacle last night at Fallout... is the same official involved here tonight!" Chronos' voiced boomed throughout the arena, while the fans appeared confused as to what Chronos meant. "Universal... I'm not fooling around... get your ass down here to the ring!" Universal' music cued up, and Universal walked out onto the stage for the second time this evening. He approached the ring with caution, and largely brushed off the reaction the crowd came him, positive or not. Chronos pointed up to the large SlaughterScreen that towered over the stage. "You guys in the truck... cue up the footage of this referee... this supposed paid fWo official who obviously can't do his job right..." Chronos pointed to the referee in question, before turning back to the SlaughterScreen, formerly affectionally known as the TravisTron. "Look at this... both I and Quicksilver... through tables outside the ring... and you're obviously counting both of us out of the ring... but for some reason... you stop right before ten... because Erik Kelly & Ruben Ross don't know where to keep their big noses where they belong!" Footage showed that Chronos was correct. The referee stopped the count as the brawl hit the ring. "There should've been a double countout, and I tried numerous times to explain that to the referee... but did he listen? No! He let the damn match continue!" The fans booed, partly because they knew Chronos correct, and partly because they hated Chronos with a passion. Universal made his way into the ring and helped the official to his feet. More footage played of incidents where Chronos was telling the referee that the match should not be happening, that it should be a double-countout. "Black Quicksilver is not the fWo World Champion. *I* am the fWo World Champion, and I always will be the Champion! It's my title... that title doesn't belong to anyone else but me! That title... the most prestigous, most coveted title in ALL of wrestling... it belongs to me, it belongs to 3vil, and it automatically makes us the most elite organization in wrestling!" Chronos turned to Universal. "You may be the owner, but after what happened last night, Universal... this jackass should've been fired on the spot. He doesn't deserve to be employed anymore. He doesn't deserve to be able to wear that striped refreee's shirt anymore. He should be fired... right here... right now... that's the only way this matter can be truly resolved." Universal somewhat nodded with him. "Now, the only way to rectify this situation... is to have Quicksilver and I continue our match later tonight. No interference, no one allowed at ringside, and certainly... no incompetent jackass as the referee! There's no way I want the match to end on a double-countout... but there's no way that I was going to let that huge... monstrous mistake that this incompetent jackass made stand. Two nights in a row... you make two huge, gigantic mistakes... you don't deserve to be employed anymore... get the hell out of this ring. Get the hell out of... OUR... ring." The referee bowed his head, before Universal stopped the official and pulled a microphone out from his jacket pocket. "You know what, Ric... I and I'm sure many of these fans are really getting sick of you." Universal paused as the fans overwhelmingly applauded his statement. Chronos snickered at Universal. "You run around here, acting like you own the place... and while I admit, you are correct in your assessment of the situation... that mistakes like this should not happen... and thus... his contract is terminated as of now. I wish you the best and thank you for serving the fWo for the many years that you've been employed." The referee turned and exited the ring, bowing his head in shame as he walked up the entrance ramp and disappeared backstage for the final time. "But that brings me back to you, Ric. I'm tired of this stuff. I am the owner of the fWo... and you and your little cohorts run around here acting like you own the place, like you can do whatever the hell you want!" "That, Universal... is because we ca - " "Shut the hell up right now." Loud cheers from the crowd rang out, as Universal took off his suit jacket and set it in a corner. "I'm not finished. I purchased the fWo last December, and immediately I owned all rights of the company. From television deals... to merchandising... to pay-per-view... to sponsorships... and so on and so forth. With that purchase, I became the... the... the man who calls the shots around here. And yes, I realize I made a mistake when I put up booking power on the line, but believe you me, I'm not going to make that same mistake again. Perhaps... perhaps it was an overslight on my part. He's fine and ready to do the shows, if that's what you're worried about." "Well, Tom... actually... it's about him actually BEING on the shows..." Universal continued, still fidgeting with the pen. "You see... a few of the animal rights groups have been calling our sponsors about us involving a cat in the matches. Until recently, we were able to push them aside with the notion that nothing bad would happen to the cat... but with what just happened with Neo, and the puppy incident before that, and the Creed incident a couple of months ago..." "Go on." Hellrazor said gruffly, his eyes flickering a bit. His grip on the hardcore belt was like a vice. "Some of the sponsors are ready to pull if we don't comply. I'm afraid you're going to have to leave Lamont back at the hotel, or in the care of someone else. If you need it, I've already taken the liberty of looking up our stops for the next 6 months, and have a list of 5 star kennels where we can reserve a spot for him. I've been assured that he'll receive the best care, and..." "Just a God Damned second." Universal glanced at Hellrazor, noting the look on his face... and not liking it one bit. Suddenly, being alone in the office with the Hardcore Champion had taken a turn towards being dangerous... "First, let me tell you something. What happened to Lamont with Creed... do you remember that night? No, of course not. You were busy checking what the gate was for the night... seeing what the preliminary buyrate was. I was busy walking the streets of Boston... by MYSELF... trying to find a place to keep Lamont alive." "Now hold on, Tom. There's no reason to get upset..." "Upset? Did you spend a night in jail? Did you? I DID. Were you worried that the only companion you had on the road was dead and gone? I sure as hell did..." "Look, it's not that we don't like having him around..." "Shut the fuck up." "Excuse me?" Universal's face began to contort with anger. "How dare you..." "I said shut the fuck up. I wasn't done talking. You said your piece, now I'm gonna say mine." Universal's grip on his pen tightened as he struggled to keep his composure. The damned cat, meanwhile had taken to playing with his coffee cup. "Fine. Go on, please." "You sit there, talking about what's happened to Lamont, and acting like the fWo cares... you couldn't give a shit about me, or about Lamont. It's painfully clear right now. So a sponsor wants to pull their advertising... it's just money. We've been here for damned near every show you've ever had. While other guys were milking injuries, sitting at home on the company dime... we were here, me and Lamont... doing whatever we needed to help the fWo. I fought with broken ribs, burns, stitches...concussions... God Damn it Chris.. I fell off of a fucking scaffold and defended the next night. But that doesn't matter to you, does it? It's all about the money...the ratings. God forbid you put the workers before the bottom line." "Tom... I do my best to make sure that the fWo provides a safe working environment..." Universal managed to stammer. "The hell you do!" Hellrazor yelled, slamming his fist down on the desk. "Look at my face! Look at my arms...my back.... look at the scars! Does this look safe to you? Does it? You're damned right it doesn't. It's not safe... but that's not what my gripe is. I chose to fight the way I do... I've got no one to blame for these but myself. What happens in the ring is all good...because I've ended some careers and messed up a few photo ops in my time. No...what I'm talking about is what happens outside of the ring. Creed damn near killed my cat... and you let it pass. Neo killed Willis' puppy. John Willis, who had been with the company since the beginning, protecting the fWo employees, protecting YOUR ass, doing security detail... and you couldn't offer him one bit of safety. You hired the lunatic back... and worse yet...you kept him on. John Willis, the guy who used to go and volunteer at the homeless shelter on Thanksgiving and Christmas... you chose that racist, psychotic hillbilly over him. Neo.. He took my cat... did God knows what to him...for WEEKS... and you sat behind your desk, doing nothing. NOTHING. You just sat here, in your fucking ivory tower, watching people bleed, watching people hurt inside and out... and why? Because it made you even more money, you selfish son of a bitch. You had a chance to do something. Any other promotion would have fired Creed and Neo. Any other promotion would have brought animal cruelty charges against them, which may I remind you, is a felony. But somehow, the inquiries were dropped. And why? The ratings were too good, weren't they, Chris? I'll bet they were...and that's why you kept Neo and Creed around... and that's why you continue to let things happen around here. Shit, you're so busy looking at your bank account that you had the booking of the company stolen right out from under your nose! And you didn't do a damned thing about it.... because the money was still good. But what would I expect out of a guy who has to replace the S's in his name with a friggen dollar sign?" "You don't even know what you're getting yourself into..." Universal began, his eyes narrowing. "With one signed order I could fine you so much, you'd be lucky to cash your paycheck for a cup of coffee at one of those roadside diners that you frequent... I make the choices that I make, because it's good for the fWo. it brings in revenue, it pays my salary... but it pays yours as well. I'll admit, for the past few years, Lamont has brought in quite a pretty penny, and has been a good marketing symbol. But weighing that against our sponsors... it's a sound financial decision." "But it's the wrong decision" Hellrazor glared back at him. "...and you know it." "Regardless. I have responsibilities to the fWo, as the owner and CEO. Responsibilities that you will probably never have to deal with. Your responsibilities are to go out and defend that Hardcore Title as necessary, and tow the company line. Your cat stays elsewhere. Besides, he'll be safer that way...right? You're a champion in this company, and one of the elder members. Now act like it, or you might find your position changed very quickly..." "Is that so?" Hellrazor's jaw clenched up and he balled his hand into a fist. "Don't force my hand, Tom. If you go head to head with me in this one, I guarantee you'll lose." All 3 fWo employees in the office froze in a standoff. Hellrazor locking eyes with Universal, while Lamont eyed both of them warily... ...then in an instant, it happened. Lamont nudged the coffee cup, and it tipped dangerously over Universal's financial statements before spilling and pouring hot coffee everywhere. Universal swore, and swore again as the pen that he had been clenching suddenly burst, shooting ink all over his shirt as the coffee spread across the desk. Reacting quickly, Universal reached into his drawer, and grabbed a handful of napkins, wiping ink and coffee alike, trying to clean up the mess... until he saw the belt in front of him. Hellrazor had placed the belt on the desk, and stood over Universal, holding Lamont under one arm, with a scowl on his face. "You want me to choose between this piece of scrap metal...and my friend? Is that what you're getting at? Well then... Fuck you Chris. I quit." Hellrazor turned and stormed from the office, leaving a speechless Universal alone to deal with a sudden void in the Hardcore division. Universal felt guilty. Guilty because nearly every word Hellrazor said was true. And it hurt him. But as a wise man once said... the truth hurts. Universal glanced down at the table in front of him and sighed. He had the fWo World Title he appeared to have won earlier in the night, and as he rolled into the ring he regrettedly handed it to the official, knowing for the time being it didn't belong to him. But that's what this match was for. Quicksilver greeted the fans warmly, and they in turned reacted with a large ovation for the most recognized member of the STUMP Squad. However, those cheers soon turned to boos. "Christian Says" by Tones on Tail filled the arena, and out walked the reigning fWo World Champion, Ric Chronos. He was booed loudly, but he stared at the ring with focus and determination. Chronos reached the ring rather quickly. Pleased by the recent turn of events, but angry for what had occured earlier in the evening with Fallen, Black Quicksilver himself, and of course the errors made by the referee, Chronos had some frustrations to take out on his opponent. Both men were already exhausted from their previous match, so it was quite understandable that Chronos wanted to end it early. Quicksilver, however wasn't going down that early.
