Sunday Slaughter, 5 November 2000

5 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Primetime vs. War Slaughter explodes inside the Hartford Civic Center, and the sounds of Metallica's "Through the Never" blasts throughout the overpriced hellhole. This means one thing, and one thing only, that it was time to Cheat like Bitches! War, Plague, and Stone take to the ring to have War face off against Primetime in the second round of the Wrestler of the Year tournament. Suddenly, the lights dimmed, and Slipknot's "Snap" started up. The fWo World Champion came out, to a negative reaction from the fans. Primetime points to the World Championship that he holds up for all to see, seemingly to remind the fans that he is after all, the World Champion. Primetime enters the ring, hands the belt to an attendant, and the bell rings, signailing the start of the matchup. War immediately charges with a clothesline, which the world champion ducks. Primetime nails a few punches that back War up into the ropes. He sends him off to the other side. Primetime leapfrogs, and goes to the other side, where he's tripped up by Stone. He gets up, turns around, and grabs Stone by the hair, but War comes from behind with a double axhandle. War then shoots Primetime off into the ropes, and nails a huge press slam. War jumps on with a quick cover, but Primetime kicks out at two. Plague gets up on the ring apron to distract the referee, and Stone hands War a bag full of powder. War grabs the powder, and goes to throw it at Primetime, but PT ducks and the powder flies into the air, not hitting anybody. Stone lets out a "damnit", on the outside, and Primetime sneaks behind War with a reverse DDT. He covers, but at two, Stone nails an unseen slap to the face of Primetime. At this point, the world champion has had enough, and goes to the outside to chase after Stone, but at a bypass, he is cut off by a chair wielding Plague. The steel chair smacks with Primetime's head, and Plague manically laughs, and then rolls Primetime into the ring. The referee, who was being distracted by Stone, sees the cover of War and begins to count. One. Two. Primetime gets a foot on the bottom rope, and Plague lets out a "DaMMiT!?". War brings Primetime to his feet and picks him up for a vertical suplex, but Primetime turns it into a rollup. One, two, and Stone with another bag of powder, catches Primetime in the eyes with it, and War reverses into a rollup of his own. At two, however, Primetime kicks out. He tries to shake off the effects of the powder, but War nails a quick poke to the eyes, and a quick full nelson bomb. War covers again, and once again Primetime finds a way to kick out at two. Stone slaps the mat in disgust, and War brings PT to his feet and sends him into the ropes. Primetime ducks a clothesline, and stays behind, catching War in the jaw with a superkick. He covers, but this time, Plague pulls Primetime's leg, pulling him off of War, and thus off of the cover. Primetime turns around to cuss out Plague, and War sneaks of from behind, catching PT in a waistlock, and then over with a huge german suplex. War covers, and at two Primetime kicks out. Stone slaps the mat in disgust, motions to Plague, and then goes to distract the referee. Meanwhile, Plague pulls out his secret weapon from under the ring, a video camera. Plague enters, and goes to hit Primetime with it, but PT ducks, and he nails War. Plague stands over War with an "UhOH", and Primetime grabs Plague by the hair, and throws him from the ring. He goes over to Stone, and with a right hand, knocks her off the ring apron. PT then brings up a dazed War, and nails the Snapshot. War is no match, and three seconds later, Primetime advances in the Wrestler of the Year tournament. Meanwhile, Slaughter rolls on... Result: Primetime by pinfall Mike Bear vs. Hellrazor Rob Zombie's "Dragula" helps to bring the viewers back from a commerical break, and brings the fans back to ring action. Mike Bear makes his appearance on the entrance ramp, something that prompts a standing ovation from the crowd. Many of them are puzzled as to why he is coming out first, however. He slides into the ring and warms up. Machinehead hits the speakers with "From This Day", and the crowd stays on its feet for Hellrazor. Those puzzled fans realize why Bear was the first one out now, as Hellrazor is bringing Lamont with him to the ring. Hellrazor climbs into the ring, and shows Lamont to the crowd, before opening up his pet carrier. Lamont climbs in, and he is set on the outside of the ring. The bell rings, and Hellrazor loosens up a bit, before extending a hand to Bear. Bear takes it in kind, prompting a cheer from the on-hand audience. They then lock up, with Hellrazor getting the advantage. Bear reverses with a armbar, and Hellrazor reverses it. Bear brings Hellrazor to the ropes, and whips him across the ring. Bear falls onto his stomach to prepare for Hellrazor running over him. However, Hellrazor stops as he nears Bear, and hits him with a splash. Hellrazor lifts Bear up, and locks him in for a sitdown tombstone. However, Bear breaks out of it with an elbow to the sternum. Hellrazor bends over in pain, and Bear nails a sitdown DDT as a result. Bear covers him, but can only get a two, before Hellrazor kicks out. Bear picks Hellrazor up, and whips him across the ring. Hellrazor bounces off the ropes, and sees Bear beginning a dropkick. Hellrazor ducks, and keeps running, catching Bear in mid-air with a diamond cutter. Hellrazor immediately covers Bear. One counted for. Two registered. Three is so close, yet so far, as Bear gets a shoulder up. Hellrazor picks Bear up. However, Bear hooks Razor into a small package, which prompts another count. One. Two. ThreNo. Razor kicked out of it. Bear picks up Razor, and whips him into the corner. Bear grabs Razor, and whips him across the ring into the opposite corner. However, Razor leaps to the second rope, prompting Bear to think that he was going to try to hit him with a crossbody. Bear hits the deck, which buys Hellrazor more time. He climbs up one more rope, to the top. He signals for the Lamonsault, and the fans go crazy. Bear looks up to see what all of the ruckus is about, when Hellrazor takes off. In something that in theory is fluid, yet when pulled off by a 6'6" man isn't quite as much, Hellrazor did a nice job in the move. However, he forgot the most important thing, which is make sure your opponent is down for sure. Bear easily moves out of the way, and Hellrazor falls flat on his face. Bear decides to go for a cover, and gets two and three quarters before Hellrazor can kick out once again. Bear picks Hellrazor up, and whips him into the ropes. Bear tries to clothesline Hellrazor, but Razor ducks it. Bear turns around and is met with a foot by his gut. Bear sidesteps it, and takes Hellrazor down with a russian legsweep. Bear holds onto Hellrazor's legs, and puts him into a sharpshooter. Hellrazor, in the middle of the ring, has no chance. He is writhing in pain. The ref asks him if he wants to submit. Hellrazor replies in the negative. Bear locks it on more, hoping to make Hellrazor submit. Instead, Hellrazor begins to get onto his hands, and tries to bring himself to the ropes. He starts to make some headway, and Bear begins to shake his head. Bear locks it in even tighter, prompting Hellrazor to move even quicker now. Hellrazor, with one final grasp, reaches the ropes. Bear immediately lets go, and moves toward the corner. He climbs to the top, hoping to catch Hellrazor off-guard. He, too, signals for the Lamonsault. He lines Hellrazor up, and gets ready. He takes off, but does so awkwardly. As a result, the leap isn't clean, and Bear nails the ropes, before bouncing off them and landing on the ring apron. Hellrazor now has his chance. It's all or nothing. He picks Bear up, and signals for the No More Pain. He takes him to the corner, and climbs up. He locks Bear in, and takes off. He's got it. Or does he? Bear decides otherwise. Bear somehow reverses it, nailing a Whiplash out of the No More Pain. He falls on top of Hellrazor, prompting the ref to make the cover. One. Two. Three. Bear moves on. Both men, obviously exhausted, lay on the ground for a few minutes. Finally, Bear gets up, and picks Razor up with him. Razor immediately shakes Bear's hand, congratulating him on the win. Bear doesn't celebrate here, but does hold his hands in the air. Both Hellrazor and Bear walk up the entrance ramp together, as Slaughter heads for a break. Result: Bear by pinfall Fear vs. "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly Machinehead rattles the arena as Fear makes his way down toward ringside, effectively bringing Sunday Slaughter back from commercial. Fear hops into the ring, and awaits his opponent in the Wrestler of the Year tournament. Not much longer, the wait is over; Metallica hits the sound system, bringing out Erik Kelly with Pamela Dee. Both figures bring massive cheers from the crowd. Kelly, a little groggy from last week's attack, holds his hands high in the air as he walks down the ramp. He climbs into the ring, and nods at Fear. Fear nods back, and the bell hits, beginning this match. Fear and Kelly lock up, with Kelly getting the early advantage. Kelly brings Fear into a corner. However, Fear uses a bit of momentum to whip Kelly's head into the turnbuckle, sending him sprawling backwards. Fear quickly hops onto the second rope, and nails a double axehandle off of it. Kelly falls to one knee. Fear quickly kicks Kelly in the head, causing him to fall down. Fear goes for a quick cover. At two, Kelly kicks out. Fear gets up, and picks Kelly up with him. He whips Kelly into the ropes. However, Kelly reverses it, and sends Fear into the ropes. Fear bounces off the ropes, and catches Kelly off-guard (although momentarily) with a crossbody splash. Kelly stumbles a bit, but he manages to catch Fear, before slamming him hard to the mat. He picks up Fear, and whips him into the ropes. He catches him by the neck, promptly lifts him up, and chokeslams him to the mat. This causes Kelly to cover Fear, which garners two and a half before Fear kicks out. Kelly picks up Fear, and whips him into the corner. Fear stumbles out, and Kelly makes way for the ropes. He lunges off the ropes and catches Fear with a bulldog. Another cover, another two-plus count for Kelly. Kelly picks up Fear and whips him into the ropes. Fear bounces off, and is caught by a big boot from Kelly. Kelly goes for another cover, which gets another two and a half, before Fear can get a shoulder up. Kelly brings Fear up, and nails him with a Bearhug Front Slam. He covers him, but pulls up at one. He know what time it is. The fans know what time it is. And Fear, subconsciously, knows what time it is as well. Kelly brings Fear to his feet, and locks him in for the Eradication. "POWERBOMB!" is heard by Kelly, as well as the minions and minions of Kelly's fans. He brings Fear up, and drives him to the mat with The Eradication. A cover and three seconds later, and Erik Kelly has moved on in the Wrestler of the Year tournament. However, as he celebrates, another man wishes to crash the party. Vengence, complete with a garbage can. He moves toward Kelly. Dee gets Kelly's attention, prompting him to turn around. "Say hello to the garbage, jackass," Vengence yells at Kelly before attempting to stuff the can over his head. However, Kelly blocks it, and kicks Vengence in the gut, prompting him to drop the can. He locks Vengence in, and immediately a "POWERBOMB!" is heard throughout the arena. Kelly lifts him up, and another Eradication has been felt on this night, this time on a garbage can. Kelly raises his arms up high, celebrating his killing of two birds with one stone. However, as is the norm, things travel in threes. This information has beenn either forgotten or ignored by Kelly, but not by another man: The Boston Strangler, who has excused his way into the ring. He picks up the can from behind, and nails Kelly with it. Standing over Kelly with evil in his eyes, he looks over at Pamela Dee... with a gleam in his eye, as Slaughter goes elsewhere. Result: Kelly by pinfall Ric Chronos vs. Jackhammer Slaughter heads to the ring for yet another Wrestler Of The Year Tournament match, and the fans are ready for action. "Step Into The Light" starts to play, and the fans cheer as Jackhammer makes his way to the ring, looking typically goofy. He steps over the top rope and waits for his opponent. "Christian Says" then hits, and the fans pop as the Man Of The Hour, Ric Chronos, begins to powerwalk to the ring, looking intense and focused. He steps up the ringsteps and into the ring, before looking over his opponent once. Chronos and Jackhammer then lock up, and the ref signals for the bell. Jackhammer gains the advantage due to his superior strength, and whips Chronos into the ropes. As Chronos comes off, Jackhammer levels him with a big boot to the face. Jackhammer drops down and makes the cover, garnering one and a half. Jackhammer brings Chronos up and whips him into the ropes again, but this time makes the mistake of putting his head down. Chronos leapfrogs over, before grabbing Jackhammer by the back of the head and dropping him to the mat with a reverse DDT. Chronos brings Jackhammer back up and whips him hard into the corner, before following him in with a hard lariat. Jackhammer stumbles out, and Chronos takes him down with a bulldog. Chronos makes the cover, but only gets a count of two. He then mounts the second rope, before flying off with a traditional elbowdrop that finds its target square. He covers, but again gets only two. Undaunted, Chronos brings his opponent back up, before lifting him up and dropping him down with an inverted atomic drop. With Jackhammer doubled over, Chronos grabs him by the head and spikes him into the mat with a hard DDT. Chronos then signals that he wants to end it, and brings Jackhammer back up before placing his head between his legs. Chronos then hooks his ankle and drives Jackhammer into the mat with the Figure-Four DDT. With Jackhammer down, Chronos lifts his head up, goes behind him, and locks on the Hourglass. Jackhammer spasms in pain, then begins frantically tapping out. The ref rings the bell, and Chronos releases the hold. The ref raises Chronos' arm in victory, certifying that he shall move on to Round 3, and Chronos basks in the loud cheers of the crowd as Slaughter moves on... Result: Chronos by pinfall Wretch vs. Ruben Ross Slaughter returns from commercials to the sounds of Prodigy's "Firestarter". The Black Plague, Ruben Ross steps onto the entrance ramp, with his recently won Cruiserweight title in his hand. Ruben continues to the ring, until Gob's "Paint it Black" starts up. The ratings saver, Wretch, steps onto the entrance ramp, without Ratings R Us at his side. Wretch plays to the fans, and then he enters the ring. He and Ross begin circiling each other as the bell rings. Ross sweeps Wretch's legs from out under him, but while Wretch is on the ground, Wretch gets Ross into a legsweep, taking him to the ground. Wretch tips up, as does Ross. Wretch nails Ruben with a dropkick, and quickly covers. At one, the cruiswerweight champion kicks out, but Wretch stays furiously on the attack. A series of backhand chops to Ruben's chest are followed up by a whip into the ropes. A savate chop is ducked by Ruben who comes back with a swinging neckbreaker. Thinking quickly, the fWo Survivor winner covers Wretch, but he too kicks out at one. Ross brings Wretch up in position for a tombstone piledriver, but Wretch reverses that. Ross reverses his attempt, and then finally is able to execute the tombstone. Ross covers, and at two Wretch kicks out. Ruben goes to whip Wretch into the turnbuckles, and succeeds. Ross looks for a monkey flip, and executes it, but Wretch lands upright. Ross turns around, and walks right into a big superkick by Wretch. Wretch jumps on for a cover, and at two, Ruben kicks out. Wretch pounds on the back of Ross, and whips him into the ropes. He looks for a tilt a whirl backbreaker, but Ruben turns it into a huracanrana. Both men quickly up, and Ruben charges. Wretch dumps him over the top, and seemingly to the floor, but Ruben lands on the ring apron. He grabs Wretch and pulls him down by the hair, and then uses the ropes to execute a gullotine legdrop across Wretch's throat. Ruben quickly covers, but as is the norm for this match, Wretch kicks out. Ruben brings Wretch to his feet, and sends him into the ropes. Wretch ducks a clothesline, and quickly hooks Ruben for the Depression, Ruben, thinking quickly, escapes into a go behind, but Wretch counters that with a mule kick, followed by a facebuster. Wretch covers, and at two, Ruben kicks out. Wretch brings Ruben up, and perches him on the top turnbuckle. Wretch follows after, and looks for a superplex. Ruben blocks it, nails a punch to Wretch's face, and then lifts him up, and executes a big top rope death valley driver. As the fans burst out in cheers, Ruben covers. One. Two. A nanosecond before three, somehow, Wretch places his leg on the bottom rope. Ruben goes nuts, as he almost had the match won, but came up just short. Ruben brings Wretch up, and looks for the Plague of Darkness, but this time it's Wretch getting out of the finisher, and he looks for a small package. One, two, Ruben reverses the package. One, two. Wretch reverses that, one, two. Finally, the package is broken and Wretch charges with a clothesline. Ruben ducks, and takes him down with a belly to belly suplex. Ruben heads to the top rope, and flies off with a guillotine legdrop attempt, that comes out short. Wretch brings him to his feet, and nails a Wretchinoku Driver. One, two, somehow Ruben kicks out. Wretch brings Ross to his feet once more, hooking him for a german suplex. Ross rolls it forward for a victory roll, but at two Wretch powers out. He sends Ross into the turnbuckles face first, and as Ross stumbles back, he walks right into the Depression. A three count later, and Wretch advances in the Wrestler of the Year tournament, but none will forget the effort that was displayed by one Ruben Ross. Result: Wretch by pinfall "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson vs. Scot Hartt Result: Wilson by pinfall Crusader vs. Juno Law Result: Crusader by pinfall The Flying Frenchie vs. Carnage Slaughter heads to the ring for the main event, a match in Round 2 of the Wrestler Of The Year tournament. "Guilty Conscience" starts playing, and the fans boo as fWo Hardcore Champion Carnage begins to make his way to the ring, belt around his shoulder. He rolls into the ring and tosses the belt aside, before awaiting his opponent. "Juke Joint Jezebel" begins, and the crowd goes insane as The Flying Frenchie begins to make his way to the ring, looking cocky as ever and sporting his trademark beam. Frenchie hops onto the ring apron and slingshots into the ring, before meeting the glare of Carnage with one of his own. Carnage then charges, and Frenchie counters with a drop-toe-hold, driving Carnage's throat into the bottom ring rope. The ref signals for the bell, and the match is on. Carnage quickly gets back up, and eats a right from Frenchie. Frenchie peppers him with a series of rights, knocking him into the ropes, before whipping him across. Frenchie leaps high into the air and blasts Carnage with a dropkick, catching him directly on the nose. Frenchie makes the cover, but only gets a two. As he brings Carnage up, Carnage drops down and low blows him, doubling him over. In a flash, Carnage is up and rides Frenchie's face into the mat with a rocker dropper. He brings Frenchie up and hurls him to the outside, before stepping onto the ring apron. As Frenchie gets up, Carnage leaps off and takes him back down with a flying lariat. Wasting no time, he grabs the nearest chair and walks back over to Frenchie. Carnage swings, but Frenchie rolls to the side and Carnage's chair slams into a monitor on the Spanish announce table, sending a shower of sparks in all directions. Carnage turns and swings again at Frenchie, who rolls away from the blow and comes up against the ringpost. Carnage winds up and sends a whistling shot at him that audibly slices the air... and Frenchie moves a third time. The chair slams into the ringpost, then richochets violently back into the face of Carnage, knocking him backwards. Carnage keeps ahold of the chair, and Frenchie sends a bullet-like superkick in his direction, slamming into the steel and driving it into Carnage's face a second time. Carnage is knocked to the ground with the chair over his face, and Frenchie drops a quick leg onto the chair, driving it into his face once more. Frenchie then brings Carnage up and rolls him into the ring, before making an arrogant cover, figuring, along with the crowd, that the match is over. To Frenchie's great surprise and consternation, the Hardcore Champion escapes at two. With a scowl on his face, Frenchie brings Carnage up and shoves him into the corner, before unloading with chops to the chest. Frenchie follows it up by measuring Carnage, then driving an elbow into the throat. Frenchie steps back, and Carnage falls face-first from the corner. Frenchie quickly hooks him up and rolls into a la magistral cradle. The ref reaches a count of two, but again Carnage kicks out. Frenchie is visibly annoyed, and brings him up once more. He takes him down with a hard DDT, before heading out to the outside. Frenchie then begins to climb the ropes, looking for either the 450 or the Guillotine, but Carnage brings himself to his feet and throws his body into the ropes, dropping Frenchie down and crotching him on the top. Frenchie then flips down to the mat, and Carnage takes his place on the turnbuckles. Frenchie slowly gets to his feet, and Carnage hooks his head. He then flies off and spins, before spiking Frenchie with a tornado DDT. Carnage rolls onto Frenchie and hooks a leg. He gets two before Frenchie raises a shoulder. Carnage signals that he wants to end it and earn the upset win, and he brings Frenchie up and goes behind him. He hooks Frenchie, then drives him to the mat with a snap german suplex. Carnage then begins heading to the outside, looking to complete the Maximum Carnage. He reaches the top rope, measures Frenchie, and flies off. His flying elbowdrop... misses. Frenchie manages to roll away in the nick of time, and Carnage smashes into the mat with a splat. Frenchie quickly heads to the outside and springs to the top, not wasting any time. He then flies off to an explosion of flashbulbs, soaring through the air with a majestic Guillotine Legdrop. It connects fully on target with the throat of Carnage, and Frenchie makes the cover. Three seconds later, Frenchie earns the victory and moves on to face his hated rival Primetime in Round 3 of the WOTY Tournament. Speaking of Primetime, he sprints into the ring from the crowd as Frenchie's hand is raised in victory, and takes down Frenchie from behind with a lead pipe shot to the Guillotine leg. As he repeatedly bashes the downed Frenchie in the kneecap, knee, and other leg-like areas with the pipe, Mitch Wilson strolls slowly to the ring, a smile on his face. Primetime finally finishes bashing Frenchie and raises the pipe high in the air, recieving a shower of boos for his troubles. As he does so, Mitch leisurely rolls into the ring and walks up behind him. Mitch, smile still glued onto his face, taps Primetime on the shoulder, and Primetime turns. Mitch then lightning-fast grabs Primetime by the head and drives him into the mat with a Headliner. Mitch then unstraps the World Title from its place on Primetime's waist, unstraps his own International Title, and steps between the bodies of Frenchie and Primetime. Mitch raises the two titles high in the air, drinking in the hatred of the crowd, sardonic smile on his face. Slaughter's cameras goes off the air on the image of Wilson, standing triumphant over his enemies... Result: Frenchie by pinfall
