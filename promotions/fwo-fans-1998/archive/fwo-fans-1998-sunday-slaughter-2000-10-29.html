Sunday Slaughter, 29 October 2000

29 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Never Pinned Inside the Worcester Centrum, the fans sat patiently on their seats, waiting for the second when the first wrestler came through the entrance. When that happened, they would react accordingly. If it was someone they disliked, the arena would be full of jeers. If it was someone they liked, the arena would be full of cheers. In ten seconds, the arena was certainly going to be one thing: Loud. fWo Ring Announcer Matt Panzer began counting down. Ten. Nine. Eight. Seven. Six. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. The Slaughter theme began to play, the cameras began to roll, and the fans rose to their feet. Fireworks screeched throughout the arena, shooting off from strategically placed into the rafters of the Centrum. The Slaughter theme faded. Its sounds were replaced by 'Last Cup Of Sorrow', by Faith No More. "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson. Until last night, the fans Wrestling organization World Heavyweight Champion. The fans booed, and booed loudly. Wilson came out onto the stage with a microphone in hand, before he walked down to ringside. Stepping into the ring with a smirk on his face, Wilson looked at the crowd with contempt. He looked around the arena, before signalling to the guys in the back to cut his music. They obliged. Wilson immediately made it known why he was here. "Brian Wisniewski, I'm calling your ass out right now." Wilson looked to the stage and waited rather impatiently. "Snap" by Slipknot began to play, and out came the current fWo World Heavy Weight Champion. Primetime. Primetime looked at Wilson cockily, walking down the ramp with the title he had held two times before last night around his waist. Primetime cautiously entered the ring, and stood across from Wilson. Primetime's music faded, and Wilson addressed Primetime. "Brian, I know we're not on the best of terms right now, but deep down inside, we both can understand each other. We both can relate to each other." Primetime looked at Wilson in confusion, but asked him to continue. "Last night at Halloween Hell, I lost the fWo World Title without being pinned in the middle of this ring. Last night, you won that fWo World Title not by beating the champion, but by beating another participant in the match. That, my friend, is not right. And let me tell you why. There's an old saying that all of us are familiar with. And that saying is "to be the man, you've gotta beat the man". Whoever has that World Title certainly is without a shadow of a doubt - the man. Last night, you beat a French frog... you didn't beat the man. You didn't pin me for that fWo World Title. Last year, Ice didn't pin you for that title. Earlier this year, you lost the title in a screwjob double pin finish. Dead went on to win the title, but he didn't beat you. That, Brian, is how we can relate. And all I'm asking out of you, is to give me a shot at that title tonight. Give me my rematch. Tonight." Primetime looked around the arena at the fans who urged him to give Wilson the shot, simply because they wanted to see a huge fWo World Title Match tonight. After several moments of contemplation, Primetime finally shook his head. "Okay, Mitch. You're right. You'll get a fWo World Title shot." The fans roared in approval, but they should've known better. "Not tonight, however. At Fallout. I'm going to make you wait. Besides, I'm booked tonight. See, a few weeks back I had the unfortunate privelege of carrying your ass once again in a tag team match against Mike Bear & Wretch. And tonight, I have the same unfortunate privelege... except I'm not going to be carrying your ass, I'm going to be carrying Pierre's ass. And after carrying both of your asses to a five star match of the year candidate last night, my back is already somewhat sore." Primetime chuckled as the fans booed loudly. Mitch interjected himself. "I should've known better, Brian. You're just afraid. You're afraid that for once in your life, you're going to lose that belt the way it should be lost - by getting pinned in that ring. No one has ever pinned you for that title... but you've got to remember just a few weeks back I beat you in the center of the ring, and that's why you don't want to face me again. But that's okay. You can go ahead and have your little tag match, because at Fallout, I'll be waiting." Wilson looked as if he was done speaking, but he wasn't finished. He had more to say. "Oh, and another thing Brian, until that happens, I'm going to go after a title that I feel is as equally as important as the one around your waist. The fWo International Title." Wilson paused, and Primetime began laughing. Wilson smirked at this, and his former tag team partner asked him why he felt that way. "Why? Why do I feel it's equally as important? Let me educate your New York raised ass for a minute, Brian. It's all a matter of meaning. International... World. They have the same meaning. They mean the same things. And while you're busy wrestling in that tag match tonight, I'll be occupying myself by putting another title around my waist. One as equally as important as yours, in my eyes, Brian. Hellrazor - if you have the balls, just bring it tonight in this ring, because I'll prove that I'm just too damn good for your own damn good." Wilson raised his arms as his music began to play, while Primetime just sat there with an expression of disbelief on his face. Wilson went to the back as the crowd booed loudly, while Slaughter went backstage... It's *My* Shot ... and backstage, James Armstrong sat in his office with a young female sitting across from him. "Well, you're not the brightest crayon in the box, but for tonight, you get the luxury of being my personal assistant. Now, go get me some bun cake, eh?" The young female stood up from her chair quickly, and bounced up and down with a cheerful laugh. Her hair - among other things - bounced as well as she nodded, and Armstrong looked quite pleased. She opened the door to find someone standing outside the door, and that someone didn't look happy. It was the rightful number one contender to the fWo World Title. Mike Bear. She quickly moved out of his way as he entered the room, staring her down while he did. She exited quickly, and that gave Bear a minute to address Armstrong. "James, this is crap. You booked that match at Halloween Hell last night so there would be a number one contender, right?" Armstrong nodded. "So why is Wilson facing Primetime again at Fallout?" Armstrong' jaw fell open. "He what?" Bear repeated it. "Weren't you watching? Wilson just secured himself a shot at Fallout against Primetime!" Armstrong didn't look pleased, but there's nothing he could do. "Well, technically Mike, it's his right. He did lose last night, but he wasn't pinned - and he is owed a rematch. So why don't you wait, and you'll face the winner of the match, okay?" Bear didn't look pleased, but he couldn't do anything about it, either. "Okay, James. I'll wait. I've waited patiently since my last title shot - I can wait some more. But let me tell you something James. If I don't get that title shot soon, asses will be kicked. And since we're being technical - that means yours!" Bear gave him a stern look and Armstrong nodded, before Bear quickly exited the room, off to prepare for his match later tonight. Meanwhile, Armstrong finished the segment, quietly mouthing the following to himself, but loud enough for the camera to pick it up. "Wilson and Primetime AGAIN? Okay, but only with a 'gimmick'... hmmm..." While Armstrong thought about what rules the match would be contested under, Slaughter went to a commercial break. Juno Law vs. Red Dragon Slaughter comes back onto the scene at the Worcester Centrum, and the fans begin to react as "Battle of the Mound" begins to play, calling out the Red Dragon for his WOTY match against Juno Law. Dragon looks about at the crowd, and the camera catches the sign of a Juno Law mark, with the comments 'DRAGON SCREWED LAW!'. But just as the camera returns to follow Dragon, an image is seen running in his direction down the ramp. That image is of Juno, who lays out Red Dragon with a football tackle from behind. Not wasting any time, Law jumps up and proceeds to 'stomp a mudhole' into the Dragon. With the match not officially started yet, Law picks up Dragon walks him over to the side of the ring and irish whips Dragon hard into the stairs. In another calculated move, Law goes over to the table with the ringbell, steals the timekeeper's chair (and it's a shock the fWo still PAYS a timekeeper...despite the fact no one ever uses him anymore), and returns over to Dragon. With ferocity, he lays the chair into the small of Dragon's back. And again...and again, before the ref says he's had enough of this pre-match friendliness and wants this match to get in the ring. Law obliges and dumps Dragon into the ring. The bell FINALLY rings, and the match is underway. Law whips Dragon into the ropes, and swings a vicious knife edge chop into the chest of Dragon. Dragon winces at the stinging effect. Law whips Dragon into the ropes again, and attempts a clothesline. However Dragon ducks, bounces off the other rope, and springs into the air with a flying forearm that connects. Dragon lands on Law, and the ref drops to count. He only gets 2, though. Dragon gets up, kicks Law in the stomach, and sets him up for a vertical suplex. He connects, but Law no-sells it as he is up immediately on pure adrenaline. It appears last night's events at Halloween Hell infuriated Law quite a bit. Law pulls an SCSA vertical body tackle on Dragon, and punches his lights out. Then, in a pure rage, he starts choking his opponent, almost to the brink of disqualification. Law jumps right into the ref's face, and stalls long enough to allow Dragon to get his senses enough to small package Law. The ref drops, but only counts 1 1/2 before Law breaks the hold. Dragon knife chops Law, Law yells and returns the chop. Dragon returns the favor, Law yells some more and returns the favor. The two play 'Tag' for about 30 seconds, until it appears Law gets the advantage, whipping Dragon into the ropes and performing everyone's favorite move. The D....T....H! Dragon falls forward, and Law takes this opportunity to set Dragon up and perform the Mexican Surfboard. Dragon yells in pain, but refuses to call it quits. After about a half minute, Law breaks the hold, picks up Dragon and executes a devastating Manhatten Drop. Law wastes no time, as he leaps to the top turnbuckle, turns to face Dragon, shows a thumbs down in Dragon's direction, and leaps into the air. Just Fierce. Juno yells "IT'S OVER!", and right he is. The ref makes the three count, and Law stands victorious over the man who turned on him last night. They were never friends, but it was still sweet revenge from last night. Law gives a couple more quick kicks to Dragon before the ref tells him to leave. Law again obliges the ref's request, and heads back up the ramp, all the while sneering in Dragon's direction. After Law disappeared to the back, Dragon got up, and something in the crowd caught his eye... the cameras faded to a commercial with the Dragon obviously distracted by something in the crowd. Result: Law by pinfall Rules For The Main Event Of Fallout, Part One Slaughter returned backstage from the commercial break, where the acting owner of the fWo sat in his office, consuming his favorite treat. His personal assistant for the evening asked him if he would like more coffee, but Armstrong shook his head no. "No... one cup is enough for me. I have to think of a 'gimmick' for that damn main event at Fallout, and coffee - much like yourself - would only make me think of something else." Armstrong shot a charming smile in her direction, and she giggled in response. "Now, let's see here... chain match... no... cage match... overdone... strap match... no... those suck... Texas Death match... no... those suck too..." Armstrong continued to contemplate what kind of rules the main event of the next pay-per-view would be, while Slaughter itself returned to the ring for the final first round Wrestler of the Year tournament match. Crusader vs. "The Future" Lance Star The familiar sounds of Result: Crusader by pinfall Autographs & Introductions Near the Worcester Centrum exit, the mysterious Red Dragon walked, looking displeased about his earlier misfortunes against Juno Law. Dragon looked to the right and saw a few tables sat up. Sitting behind one of them was former multiple time fWo Cruiserweight Champion Black Quicksilver. On the table in front of him was a stack of papers and a few pens, and it looked like he was in the middle of signing autographs, or at least taking a break from it, as no one stood before him to get his signature. To the left of BQ sat his compadre in the STUMP Squad, Mike Heftel. Heftel too, was a former Cruiserweight Champion himself (for a record short three days, just two days shorter than the original holder of that record, the Flying Frenchie back in 1998), and like BQ, Heftel had nobody in front of him. To the left of Heftel sat STUMP himself, in all his glory. However, in front of him a lengthy row of fans were gathered, anxiously wanting to get a piece of memoribilia signed by one of the more popular... personalities in the fWo. BQ & Heftel sighed, before BQ asked Heftel "Man, how does Stump do it?" Heftel shrugged, and then thought it would be keen if he asked Stump himself. "Stump, how do you do it? How come you're so popular?" Stump sat there with a blank expression, before responding. "..." Heftel smiled. "Oh, so that's how!" BQ rolled his eyes underneath his mask, while Dragon looked at his now fellow STUMP Squad members. Shaking his head in amusement, Dragon then was distracted by something... something that seemed familiar to him. Something that he saw earlier tonight. Dragon walked over to the row of fans waiting to get their Stump signature. Amongst the fans was someone who obviously worked in the arena, judging from their attire. Dragon smiled. "Hello. I believe I saw you earlier this evening in the stands after my match. Weren't you selling hotdogs?" The female - who was obviously somewhat shy - smiled. "Yes." Dragon smiled. "I thought so. My name is Jamie." The two shared a brief handshake, before Dragon reached inside his bag. "Here... would you like to join us next week at Slaughter? This will get you backstage." Obviously laying the charm on pretty thick, he handed her the pass. The female nodded. "Yes. I'm a big, big fan of the fWo. I would enjoy that." Dragon smiled. "Would you like to join me for dinner after you get done here at the arena? I was going to leave, but once I saw you again, I had to ask." The female smiled, accepting the invitation. Dragon appeared pretty happy, but BQ & Heftel looked down about things. "Sheesh. He gets the chicks, Stump gets to sign autographs, what do we get?" Heftel smiled. "We get to job to the dashboard!" BQ once again sighed, while Slaughter headed elsewhere... Rules For The Main Event At Fallout, Part Two Consumption of his favorite treat (bun cake) now complete, Armstrong leaned back in his plush office chair, obviously in deep thought. "Hell In A Cell... as much as Frenchie would like to see them toss each other off... no, we had one of those... Falls Count Anywhere? No, that match is overdone too..." Armstrong continued to think out loud about what rules the next Primetime/Wilson encounter could be wrestled under, but his personal assistant of the evening had other intentions. "Mr. Armstrong... is there anything I can do for you?" Armstrong obviously didn't hear her... but she giggled again. "Silly you... you need to take your mind off things. And I know just the thing that'll do it." The personal assistant got up from her chair and positioned herself behind him, as he continued to sit in deep thought. Slowly she began to rub his shoulders, and that somewhat got his attention. "I know! A Ladder Match! No, we just had one of those... and I... er... wait a minute..." Now, not only was she rubbing his shoulders, she was nibbling on his ear. "Hey, I kind of like that... what's your name again?" Before she could answer, the cameras faded and headed back to the ring. Hellrazor (c) vs. "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson Back in the ring, Mitch Wilson was in a corner doing some light jumping jacks, his music fading. Wilson was coming off of a grueling match last night at Halloween Hell, but he still appear to be in tiptop condition. Wilson continued to do exercises in preparation for his match, as it was apparent that Hellrazor had accepted the challenge. Before long, Faith No More was replaced by the sounds of 'From This Day' by Machinehead. The current fWo International Champion & one half of the World Tag Team Champions walked out onto the stage, with Lamont in a petmobile and the fWo International Title around his waist. His tag title resided over his shoulder, and the popular Hellrazor took to the ring. Placing Lamont in the corner, and handing his International Title to the referee, Hellrazor did some light exercises as well in his corner. Lamont wished him good luck through his special ESP abilities, and as Hellrazor thanked him, Wilson attacked from behind with a double axehandle to the back. The referee signalled for the bell, and the evening's first title match was underway. Wilson now chopped Hellrazor in the corner, before attempting to bring him out with a hiptoss. Razor blocked it, and instead hiptossed Wilson to the mat. Wilson got up, and Hellrazor took him down with an elbow. Hellrazor now grabbed him and snapmared him to the mat, before continuing with a rolling necksnap. Hellrazor laid his arm over the challenger, and the referee counted. At two, Wilson got a shoulder up. Razor brought Wilson up and sent him to the ropes. Wilson reversed it and then ducked for a backdrop. Wilson leap frogged him, and then when he returned Hellrazor took him down with a drop toe hold. Hellrazor got back to a vertical base, and as Wilson did the same, Hellrazor charged and took him down with a spinning heel kick. His weight made the move have that much more of an impact, and Wilson staggered backwards and fell out onto the floor. Wilson got up and held his back, and then he turned to the ring, only to see two feet coming at him with incredible velocity. Wilson went flying back onto the American announce position as a result of the baseball slide. Hellrazor brought him back up to his feet and chopped him, before grabbing him by the head and getting ready to place him back in the ring, however Wilson caught him offguard with an elbow to the ribs. Wilson then grabbed Hellrazor and slammed him head first into the table, and then again, and a third time with incredible force. Wilson then rolled him into the ring, and joined him in there. First he tauned Lamont, and then the crowd. As Hellrazor got up, Wilson went for a kick. Hellrazor caught his boot, but then caught something else: kick to the head enziguiri style. Hellrazor fell to the mat, and Wilson followed it up with a pin. After a two count, Hellrazor got his foot on the rope. Wilson continued the attack, bringing him up and sending him to the ropes. Hellrazor returned and Wilson hit a high knee, before going for a pin. At two, Hellrazor got a shoulder up. Wilson signalled to the crowd he had this one wrapped up, and brought Hellrazor up again. This time, he hit the Superstar Suplex, which garnered another two count. Deciding to take a high risk, Wilson went to the top rope over where Lamont was located. He looked down and taunted Lamont some more, but this bought time for 'Razor. Razor stumbled backwards into the ropes, and Wilson now found himself crotched on the top rope. Razor groggily walked over and climbed to the second rope, before hooking Wilson for a DDT. He hit it, and of course everyone assumed it was over, as 'Razor just executed the 'No More Pain'. Both men were out of it, and that was good news for Wilson. Razor rolled over and laid an arm on Wilson, and at two and 999/1000ths, Wilson got a foot on the ropes. The crowd oohed and aahed at the near fall, and Hellrazor found himself trying to come up with something else to finish the match. Hellrazor got to his feet and brought Wilson up and nailed him with some forearms, before hooking him tombstone style. He hit the sitout tombstone, and the fans roared in approval. Wilson was now further away from achieving his goal of winning the fWo International Title, a title he held in the same class as the World Title (at least now he did, anyways). Hellrazor crawled over for the cover, and somehow, someway, Wilson got his shoulder up. Frustrated, Hellrazor looked at Lamont, and again through his special powers - Lamont told him what he had to do. But it was no matter. Hellrazor knew what he had to do. Hellrazor went to the corner directly above Lamont, and faced the crowd. He climbed up to the top rope, and flew off with the most feared move in the sport. The Lamontsault. Wilson, however, had the tenacity to move out of the way, and instead Hellrazor smacked the mat. Wilson got up to his feet and looked around the ring. Realizing that Hellrazor had given him more of a match than he had anticipated, Wilson stomped at him on the mat. Wilson now brought him up to his feet, and sent him into the turnbuckles. There, he placed him on the top rope, before chopping him across the chest. The referee moved in and started his traditional five count to break it since Hellrazor was on the ropes, but Wilson didn't care. He went out to the ring apron, and from behind he brought him to a standing position. Since both men were on the ropes, they shaked somewhat. Wilson had intentions of planting him on the mat with the Headliner, but their loss of balance prevented that. Wilson quickly decided it was either he or Hellrazor that was going off, and he chose Hellrazor. He pushed him off the ropes and inadvertently 'Razor crashed into the referee. The referee now found himself knocked out as a result. Wilson remained on the ropes, and noticed that Hellrazor landed in position for a move that he was certain would piss someone off if he executed it. The 450 Splash. He flew off, but Hellrazor somehow got his knees up. Wilson crashed, and now both men lay on the mat exhausted, along with the referee. Moments passed before 'Razor finally crawled over and laid his arm atop Wilson. However, there was no referee. Pissed, Razor again turned to his friendly feline for advice. This however gave Wilson the opportunity to get back in the match. From behind, he took Hellrazor out. Headliner. But again, there was no referee. Wilson tried to revive him, but it was of no use. He was for sure out. Wilson decided to take matters into his own hands. He went to the outside, and grabbed the title they were fighting for. Slinging it into the ring, he waited for Hellrazor to get up. Finally, the cat lover did. He stood on spaghetti legs, and was obviously quite groggy. Wilson charged with the belt and struck. Razor fell to the mat, Wilson nudged the referee again. No luck. Instead, a referee from the back emerged. He slid into the ring, and Wilson covered Hellrazor. One. Two. No. Instead, Razor got a shoulder up. Pissed at this, Wilson began arguing with the new referee. He pushed him, but the referee didn't back down. Instead he responded with a push of his own. Barely moving Wilson, Wilson laughed, and pushed him back. This time the referee went flying to the mat. He didn't get up. Wilson turned and saw 'Razor coming at him. Wilson ducked the lariat, and when he bounced off the ropes and came back, Wilson hip tossed him into a Michinoku Driver. To be more specific, the self proclaimed Mitch-inoku Driver. Wilson knew it was over, but he decided to rub it in. Walking over to the corner, he found Lamont in the petmobile, and waited for 'Razor to get to his feet. Thirty seconds passed, and he did. Smack. Wilson slammed it into his head, before going over to the first referee, and this time completely reviving him. He covered him for the pin, and three seconds later, Wilson found himself with a belt around his waist once again. Wilson retrieved it and held it high above the ring, proud of his latest accomplishment. The fans booed in disgust, and the first 'Grand Slam' winner walked to the back, proclaiming to the camera that as a result of having not been pinned last night for the title and because of the International Title having the same meaning as the World, that he was the true World Champion... Slaughter went to a commercial, with Hellrazor attempting to recover from a brutal and exhausting match. Primetime & The Flying Frenchie vs. Mike Bear & Wretch Cameras returned to the ringside area, and zoomed in on the stage. "Paint It Black" by Gob started up, and the fans stood to their feet. Former fWo World Champion Big Wretchy, The Ratings Saver walked out onto the stage, flanked by his lackeys on either side. They reached the end of the stage, before Wretch turned back to them, and directed them to go to the back - he was taking care of this one by himself. Wretch greeted the fans with high fives as he reached ringside, before entering the ring, and waiting for his tag team partner of the evening. His partner was no stranger, as he had teamed with him twice in the last month or so (at Unstable, and on the October 8th Slaughter). In addition to that, these two men were part of the original Underground - and were the fWo World Tag Team Champions on several occasions. As the theme of Wretch stopped, the theme of Mike Bear began to play. This kept the fans on their feet, as "Dragula" by Rob Zombie pulsated throughout the Centrum. Bear appeared on the stage with Brittany at his side, and raised his arms to the fans, who responded with more cheers. Bear joined Wretch in the ring and Brittany wished him luck, before heading outside the ring. The former best friends stared holes through each other, as it was quite obvious neither man thought highly of one another at the moment. Bear climbed up in the corner and raised his arms again, before his theme was replaced by one of his fellow Underground member's music. "Juke Joint Jezebel" started up, and as the sounds of KMFDM blared throughout the arena, The Flying Frenchie walked out onto the stage. He stopped for a moment to take in the cheers from the fans, before beaming proudly. Frenchie began walking down the ramp, but as he did, he was greeted rather rudely by his partner from behind. Primetime nailed him with the World Title, and as Bear looked at Primetime's assault in confusion, his own partner came up from behind and nailed him with the Depression (reverse DDT). Wretch then stood above Bear and verbally abused him, while on the outside, Primetime grabbed Frenchie and tossed him over the guardrail. He joined him and the two men brawled throughout the crowd. Back in the ring, the referee admonished Wretch, and backed him into his corner. Wretch stood on the apron in amusement at the brawl Frenchie & Primetime were engaged in. They slammed each other off of whatever they could. Punch after punch, kick after kick, and the crowd loved every minute of it. Frenchie decided to attempt to decapitate Primetime on the floor with a piledriver, but somehow Primetime backdropped out of it. Then he waited for Frenchie to get up... Frenchie slowly did after having his back slam off the pavement. Primetime snuck up from behind and grasped him, apparently for the dreaded backdrop driver. Frenchie however knew what was coming, and went for an elbow while still in hold. Primetime ducked. Frenchie tried again. PT ducked again. Frenchie struggled and escaped with a go-behind. But PT countered with a go-behind of his own, and then he nailed Frenchie with a low-blow. From there, he hit a running bulldog on the concrete, before telling Frenchie off, and heading back towards the ring. Wretch watched Primetime walk back, but with his attention elsewhere, he didn't realize Bear had recovered. Bear stood in the center of the ring, shrug off the reverse DDT, and then charged. Wretch saw something coming out of the corner of his eye, but before he could react, it was too late. He found himself on the receiving end of a superkick. It caught the end of Big Wretchy' jaw, and was forceful enough to send him flying off the ring apron. Unfortunately for Wretch, he was standing near the announcers tables. Now, he found himself in a pile of wood and monitors. Commentators Jeremiah Frankton & Stevie Roberts were in shock at the carnage, and of course, Roberts likely found a way to turn it into a joke of sexual nature. Bear smiled at what he had done, and the crowd booed, as they didn't want to see Bear & Wretch go at it. With Bear' attention focused on what he just did, in from behind came Primetime. Primetime waited for Bear to turn around, then kicked him in the stomach. There, he set him up for a Primetime Bomb. Instead, Bear backdropped out of it. Primetime ended up on his feet on the ring apron. Bear reached out and grabbed him, bringing him in with a vertical suplex. The referee finally signalled for the bell, and the match was on. One minute it in, Bear covered Primetime, for only a two count. Bear brought PT up and sent him to the ropes. As PT returned, Bear ducked for a backdrop, but PT grabbed him and hit a swinging neckbreaker. He then went to the ring apron, and climbed up to the top rope. But before he could capitalize, he was distracted. Frenchie had returned, and got his attention. Primetime looked over at Frenchie, and this allowed Bear to take advantage. First, he crotched Primetime on the top rope, by knocking his legs out from underneath him. Then, he joined him up there, and hit a overhead suplex. Primetime smacked off the mat, and Bear went for a cover. One. Two. Instead, Bear was now on the floor as a result of Frenchie reaching in and pulling him underneath the bottom rope to the floor. Bear asked what Frenchie was doing, and Frenchie beamed, obviously telling Bear he didn't want to lose the match. Bear shrugged it off and went back into the ring, while Frenchie took to his corner. Wretch finally recovered, and was now on the ring apron. WIth each man in position, the match was finally set, a good seven minutes into action. Bear brought Primetime up and nailed him with a few haymakers, before whipping him to the ropes. Primetime reversed it however, and when Bear came back, Primetime went for a sleeperhold. He cinched it in, but after about forty five seconds of being in the hold, Bear dropped to the mat for a jawbreaker. Bear then got up and looked like he was about to tag a groggy Wretch in, but instead, flipped Wretch off. He went back into action. Bear brought Primetime up and worked him over in the corner, before sending him to the ropes. Instead, he brought him back and hit a short arm clothesline. He worked him over some more with some stomps, before sending him into another corner. He followed in, but Primetime used the corner to press himself up (kip up) and fell behind Bear, who rushed into the corner sternum first. Primetime then grabbed him with a waist lock, and hit a release German Suplex. Both men were now dazed on the mat, and Primetime slowly began to climb over to his corner. Primetime looked up and saw his partner, and was reminded of how much he despised him. Rather than tag out, he looked up and mouthed an audible "Fuck you!" before spitting in his direction. This enraged Frenchie, and he came in, but the referee backed him into his corner. Primetime stood up, and when he turned around, Wretch grabbed him and hit a Wretchinoku Driver. Wretch then resumed his place on the apron, before getting the crowd into the match by stomping his feet and clapping his hands. The fans were clearly into it now, as Bear fought to his feet. Bear was groggy, and he fell backwards towards his corner. There, Wretch tagged himself in. He came in a house of fire. He nailed Primetime. He would've nailed Frenchie, but Frenchie remained on the apron, inviting Wretch to kick Primetime's ass some more. Wretch shrugged, and did so. Wretch brought Primetime to his feet and whipped him to the ropes. Primetime returned and Wretch hit a spinning heel kick. Wretch then pointed to the top rope, and he went to the ring apron. He flew off with the headbutt, before going for the pin. One. Two. But the count was interrupted. Frenchie came in and kicked his back, thus puzzleing Wretch, who thought that Frenchie just gave the okay to kick Primetime' ass. Wretch went after Frenchie, but the referee got in between them, and directed Frenchie back to his corner. As Primetime stood up in the middle of the ring, Bear came in and hit a Death Valley Driver, before rolling out to the floor. Wretch came back over and went for another pin, of which Frenchie again broke up. This time, Wretch got to him before the referee could split them up. Bear meanwhile retrieved a piece of the announcers table on the outside, before rolling it into the ring. Wretch charged at Frenchie, and Frenchie backdropped him over the top rope to the floor. Wretch hit hard, and then Frenchie went to turn back towards the ring. Primetime was now on his feet facing Bear, who had the piece of the announcers table. He charged and swung, but Primetime dropped. Instead, Bear hit one of his close friends and fellow Underground member in the back. The referee didn't see it as he was checking on Wretch, but he turned back around. Primetime grabbed Bear by the hair and sent him flying over the top rope on the floor, before he joined him out there. Frenchie was dazed in the ring, and Wretch slowly climbed back into the ring. Primetime slammed Bear on the cement floor, before telling him that he sucked, and Brittany did too. Primetime then grabbed Bear & sent him flying over the guardrail, while back in the ring, Wretch now stood over Frenchie. Realizing that Frenchie had backdropped him out onto the floor just seconds ago, he brought him up and hit second Wretchinoku Driver of the night. The referee counted, but Frenchie kicked out at two. Undaunted, Wretch took to the sky once again, looking to beat Frenchie... With his own move. 450 Splash. Big Wretchy flew off with the 450, while Primetime verbally assaulted Brittany on the outside. Primetime grabbed her by the hand, and appeared ready to give her the kiss of death, after having just disposed of Bear over the guardrail. Back in the ring, the referee counted. One. Two. But Frenchie somehow got his foot on the ropes, right as the referee's hand was about to touch the mat for a third time. Realizing there was only one way he could beat the fWo legend, Wretch dragged him to the middle of the ring. He lightly kicked the left arm of Frenchie, while Primetime was about to give Brittany a big, wet sloppy kiss. Wretch took off the bandana and tossed it into the crowd. He pointed back and forth, before charging to the ropes. He came back and leaped over the prone body of the Frenchie. He returned once again, and there instead of hitting the biggest ratings drawing move on the history of the sport (Ratings Saver Legdrop), he found himself on the receiving end of a kick to the midsection by Primetime. Primetime then hooked him and hit the Primetime Bomb, before grabbing his hated rival the Frenchie, and laying him atop Big Wretchy. The referee had no choice but to count. One. Two. Three. Primetime & The Flying Frenchie had done it, even though they would've enjoyed teaming with Canadians than each other once again. Primetime retrieved his fWo World Title from ringside, and backed up the ramp, proud of what he had done. The camera's never captured if he ever did kiss Brittany, but it was forgotten by now. As the camera's prepared to fade to black, Primetime walked backwards right into the man he would meet in just over twenty days at Fallout. Mr. Main Event. Primetime turned around and before he could react, Wilson nailed him with a forearm, and then another. Mitch then grabbed Primetime by the hair and sent him underneath the ring. Instead of joining him though, Wilson remained on the floor. Wilson smiled, and Primetime got to his feet. He shouted obscenities at Wilson, but Wilson just shrugged them off. Thinking he was safe, Primetime turned towards the center of the ring. Instead, he found himself meeting the wrath of someone else. Not Pierre Delacroix. Not Chris Kidman, Jr. Not even Michael Baer. Instead, Lance Walker. Better known to everyone else as Lance Star. Star hooked him and planted him into the mat with the Shooting Star. The crowd erupted, as Lance looked down at Primetime and unloaded with a barrage of harsh words that cannot be printed here. After the cheers died down, Lance went to the floor, where he joined Mitch Wilson. The duo looked at each other, and shook hands, as Slaughter faded to black. Result: Primetime & Frenchie by pinfall
