fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 13 May 2001

13 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Workers backstage shuffled about as Slaughter prepared to go live. Several wrestlers were gathered in an area outside the arena, as a long white limosuine pulled up. Seconds passed as the limo sat. It was not yet night, but it was twilight, dusk, and the cool air blew with a slight breeze. The purple sunset reflected its rays off the top of the limo, bouncing them off skitter-skatter around the lot outside the Phillips Arena. Then, the door opened. And out stepped Chris Universal. Universal breathed deep of the Atlanta air it was invigorating, like a natural high. He looked around him at the workers, going about their business, and smiled. The fWo was alive, the fWo was well, and the fWo was his. He could feel it... it was going to be a great year. Universal shut the door of the limo behind him, then walked into the Phillips Arena and the beginning of fWo: Year Four. Slaughter faded into its opening sequence, which viewers noted had been replaced, featuring new highlights and theme song, to represent the new year for the fWo... Monte Burns vs. Havok The sound of Powerman 5000 brings out one of fWo's newest signees. Monte Burns is set to make his first scheduled appearance in an fWo ring, and the fans aren't sure what to think. His confident swagger is that of an ex-champion and he stands idly by while Havok makes his entrance to "Awake" by Godsmack. He still looks a little worn out, after that spectacular match on Slaughter last week. The bell rings just as Havok steps in the ring and the match is quickly underway. The two tie up and it's Monte Burns who manages to quickly gain the advantage. His speed seems to have gained him it over the weary Havok. A chop by Burns sends Havok sprawling backwards where he bounces off the ropes. Seeing an open opportunity, Burns hits a spinning heel kick on one of fWo's seasoned veterans. Without wasting a moment, Burns begins to stomp away on Havok. There's no way he's going to let Havok gain the advantage. But Havok quickly turns the tables on the eager Monte Burns, and upends him, by grabbing his foot and basically throwing his opponent to the mat hard. Both men spring quickly to their feet, where they meet each other with openhanded slaps. The exchange lasts mere seconds before Havok comes back with a boot to the midsection, which he quickly follows up with a hard, straight kick, which is planted square on Burns' nose. Burns' body makes a thump as it hits the canvas and Havok makes a cover, which he knows probably won't win the match. The referee's hand slaps the mat twice, before Burns desperately kicks out, to save the match for himself. He wants to make a lasting impression, but that definitely wouldn't have been the way he wanted to make it. Burns climbs to his feet to meet Havok, and surprises him with a hurricanrana out of nowhere. The fans simply look on in awe, after that spectacular move puts Burns back in control of the match. Grabbing Havok by his long hair, Burns puts him in a better position for him to do what he wants - and to make sure he gains a victory over the former partner of both Wretch and Black Panther. He thinks an asai moonsault will do it. Climbing the top rope slowly, but surely, Burns keeps Havok in view, carefully observing him to make he doesn't avoid one of Burns' trademark moves. Burns flies through the air and executes the move perfectly, to a loud gasp in the arena. The fans' reaction seems to fuel Burns, because he doesn't go for the cover on Havok, but instead continues the offence. A side russian legsweep puts Havok back on the mat, his face wrenching with pain. A double-armed DDT nearly does Havok in, but he manages to escape a very close count. The ref's eyes focus in on the action as Havok begins a comeback. Frantic blows by Havok nearly put him back in the match, but a straight-leg kick puts him back on the mat, where the ref begins another count. Havok won't stay down, though. He showed in his match against Panther that he is not one to be taken lightly and today is no exception. Burns seems to realize this, as he steps up his game. Chop after chop on Havok seems to wear him down. Burns is just too much for him tonight, but Havok does his best to fight back. But after another spinning heel kick by Monte Burns, Havok just can't recover and lies on the mat, not moving a muscle. With his opponent in a very helpless state, Burns decides to finish the match off. The Establishment signals the end for Havok. All that remains is for it to become official. With the ref slamming down his hand for the three, if does so. Monte Burns' arm is raised in victory, and "Nobody's Real" sounds over the speakers. An impressive debut by Monte Burns will not be forgotten. The man has a tremendous career for ahead of himself in fWo. Tonight was only the beginning. Result: Monte Burns by pinfall The Offer Vengence knew full well he wasn't alone in the weight room. At this point, he had neither patience for not time to be interrupted, and he wasted no time in dropping the weight on the floor, where it landed with a loud clank, then turning to face the intruder. "Loco." "That's me," said Matt Loco. "Listen, Keene, I've got an offer you can't refuse." "I doubt it." "Maybe, but listen to me anyway. CyberSlam proved to me that I need somebody beyond Erik watching my back. Erik's great, but he can't be everywhere at once. With your... history... with him, I figured you'd be a perfect candidate to be my bodyguard." Vengence considered. Aligning himself with Loco and Kelly could be a good chance to move up the fWo ladder. "Your proposal is acceptable." "Great, great. You start as of now, of course." "Fine. I have a match now, Loco... let's go." Vengence started toward the door, and Loco followed him, rubbing his hands together in glee at having acquired the services of another one of the fWo's true monsters. Vengence vs. Sphere "The Beast" hits the speakers as Vengence, along with Matt Loco, makes his way down to the ring. The fans boo the two men as they make their way down. Vengence climbs into the ring and begins to warm up. Meanwhile, The Deftones hit the speakers, bringing out Sphere to another negative reaction. He runs down towards the ring, slides in, and immediately goes after Vengence. He clocks him with a left-hand, and then a right. Vengence counters with a couple of rights, and then a boot to the midsection. Sphere bends down, but spears Vengence in the process. Sphere picks up Vengence, and tosses him into a corner. Vengence bounces out and charges Sphere. He nails Sphere with a Clothesline from Hell. Sphere hits the deck, and Vengence immediately climbs to the second rope. He takes flight, and nails Sphere with a knee to the face. Vengence picks up Sphere, and whips him into a corner. Vengence lines him up, and nails a flying spinning elbow. Sphere lumps out of the corner, and Vengence picks him up. He nails a Sidewalk Slam, and then goes for a cover. One. Two. No sir. Sphere kicks out at two and a half, and Vengence gets annoyed. He converses rather harshly with the referee, and then picks Sphere up. He points at the ref, and then lifts Sphere up with a Press Slam. He nails it, and then looks at the referee. He points at the ground, and then covers Sphere. The ref counts to two, and Sphere kicks out. Vengence, looking rather ticked off, picks up Sphere, and lines him up for a Stargazer. Vengence goes for the superkick; however, Sphere ducks it, and dropkicks Vengence. Sphere, still a bit groggy, picks Vengence up, and headbutts him four times. Vengence falls into a corner, and Sphere nails him with a dropkick to the midsection. He quickly gets up, and Vengence, wincing from the pain of a dropkick to the midsection, continues to bow down, in order to take care of the pain. As he gets up, Sphere greets him with a superkick of his own. However, Vengence ducks that move, and grabs his leg. He nails a Russian legsweep, and has Sphere in line for a figure-four leglock. He locks it in, and Sphere yells out in pain. The ref checks on Sphere; meanwhile, Vengence uses Loco for more support, for more leverage, and for more pain. The ref looks up, and Loco quickly releases Vengence's arms. The ref checks back on Sphere, and Loco grabs Vengence's arms once again. The ref looks up, and quickly yells at Loco, before kicking his arms away. Meanwhile, Sphere tries to muster up the power to reverse the hold. He begins to do so, despite Vengence's attempts to block it. Finally, Sphere does, and Vengence quickly grabs hold of the ropes to break the hold. Sphere breaks the hold, and is a bit slow to get up... so slow, in fact, that Vengence beats him to it. Sphere gets up, gets his bearings, and looks towards Vengence. However, all he can see is a big black boot coming at him. Vengence nails the Stargazer. Happy with the recent turn of events, he immediately sets up for the Eclipse. He picks up Sphere, and begins to set him up. Meanwhile, while he does this, a figure begins to make his way out of the crowd. Apparently, this man wants some better seating, because he brought his chair with him. He climbs into the ring, his bald head shining against the lights. As Vengence picks Sphere up with the Eclipse, the man, known to fans all around the world as Jackhammer, nails him in the head with the chair. Sphere falls on his feet, and turns around to find out what happened. Immediately, he is met with a chair shot, and tossed out of the ring. Jackhammer tosses Vengence out of the other side of the ring, and grabs a microphone. "Hey, jackasses, I've got a little proposal for you," he says to both men. "I want a little piece of this action as well, and the only way that can be achieved is if we have a little three-way hardcore dance at Redemption." The crowd cheers at this, and Jackhammer looks at Sphere. Sphere nods, accepting his end of this challenge. Loco grabs a microphone, and speaks on behalf of Vengence. "You're on, Austin, or Goldberg, or whatever your name is." Jackhammer's eyes peer down at Loco, as he calmly steps out of the ring. Loco, realizing what he may have done, immediately bolts for the back. Jackhammer stays on the apron, looking down at Vengence. Meanwhile, Slaughter moves on... Result: No Contest Leaving The fWo World Heavyweight Championship Title Belt. It's twenty pounds of gold, attached to a pair of brown leather straps. It's what every wrestler strives for, and when you finally have it in your possession, you know you've made it. It was currently around the waist of Mike Bear, who is sitting on a chair in the Underground locker room. Bear's face was touched with a bit of sadness, as he looked across the room at his best friend Wretch. "I know, Mike, but I have to go," Wretch said. "I've had a lot of fun here in the fWo, and I'm not leaving for good. But I have to go, man. I've got the papers all set up with Universal. And I've got a movie offer on the table... Maximum Overdrive 2! And we all know how successful sequels with wrestlers are. Bear nodded, conceding the point. "Alright, Chris... if you think you've got to go, then go. Just, make sure you come back, OK?" Wretch smiled. "You bet." Bear got up off his chair and stuck out a hand to Wretch. Wretch took it, briefly shook it, then pulled Bear into a hug. The two separated. "Good luck, Chris." "Same to you, Mike." And with that, Wretch left the room. Seconds later, the door opened up again, and fWo Internet Champion Ruben Ross stepped into the room. "Hey, Mike." "Hey, Ruben. You hear about Chris?" "No, what's up with him?" "He's leaving." "Leaving? Man, that's too bad. Especially for you, considering you two are scheduled to tag against Kelly and Chronos tonight." Bear's eyebrows raised. "What?" "Yeah, it's all set up. You two against them two. I'd offer to tag with you in his place, but I'm already booked with 'Razor." "Ah, hell... the one time I don't check the card when I get here... I gotta go, Ruben." "Later." Bear sprinted out the door and down the hall. He threw open the door to the parking lot, just in time to see Wretch's vehicle speed away. "Shit!" Bear stood alone in the doorway, utterly without a partner... Champion? Walk' by Pantera began to sound, signifying the arrival of the fWo Hardcore Champion Kodiak Vic Creed.. The Hardcore Title draped over his massive shoulder, Creed walked down the aisle and stepped into the ring. With a smug look on his face, Creed raised the title overhead with one hand and held a mic with the other. "What you people are looking at right now, is the most dominant champion the fWo has. I've faced three men at once, four at once. They still couldn't get this strap from around my waist. I mean seriously. Look at the champions you have right now. You've got the World Champion. Mike who? Please! The guy's the poster boy for horseshoe luck. And does anyone even remember who the Internet champion is?" The fans started chanting the name of Ruben Ross, but Creed ignored them. "That's right. Not a clue. And don't even get me started on the Tag Chumps! Face it peope: if you were to look up the word 'champion' in the dictionary, assuming any of you could read, you'd see a picture of the Main Man, Kodiak Vic Creed!" 'Nobody's Real' by Powerman 5000 signified the entrance of one of the fWo's newest recruits, "The Golden Child" Monte Burns. Monte stood in the aisle and shook his head. "If you're what passes for a champion in the fWo, then I got here just in time." Creed leaned on the rope and frowned as Burns continued. "Ever since I started my career as a professional wrestler, I've proven that I've got the talent and skill to back up everything I say. That's the difference between a superior wrestler, such as myself, and a superior idiot, such as yourself. I mean, how much intelligence does it take to hit someone with a chair? What kind of skill do you need to slam someone through a table?" "Why don't you get your ass in here and find out," Creed growled as he held the ropes open. "You know something Creed?" Monte's grin was wide. "I just might take you up on your little offer. Maybe I should come down to the ring, unmask you as the fraud you are, and do the world a favor by tossing that worthless belt in the trash where the rest of you 'Hardcore' wrestlers belong." Vic stepped between the ropes and hopped to the floor. He tossed the title to the side as he charged at Burns like a bull. Several referees scrambled out of the back to restrain the rampaging Hardcore champ. Monte simply smiled and walked away as Creed struggled to free himself. "When I'm done with you Burns," Creed hollered. "you'll wish you'd never signed that fWo contract!" Mike Heftel vs. Jon Crisp "Down with the Ship" hits the speakers, bringing out Mike Heftel to a rather nonchalant reaction from the crowd. He walks down confidently, until he gets to the ring. He moves to slide into the ring; however, he loses his momentum as his legs refuse to go with him. Rather, they hang down over the apron. Eventually, he falls over, back onto the mat. He decides that the steps are the real way to go, and he uses them. As he warms up, "My Hero" hits the speakers, bringing out fWo rookie Jon Crisp for his debut. The fans, unsure of what to think of him, give him a rather mixed reaction. That is, until he climbs into the ring and grabs a microphone. "Atlanta, Georgia...home to the 1998 NFC Champion, Atlanta Falcons!" The fans erupt in a cheer, at least for a few seconds. "It just shows that the NFC's decline didn't start with the Giants. The Falcons sucked, too." The fans begin to boo, somewhat. Some cheer, realizing that he probably is correct. "This place is also home to the Atlanta Braves, which resembles what was WCW. A lot of old farts, over the hill, with the delusion...wait, wait...I'm in a 'Double-digit IQ Zone', that's right...delusion is probably a word you've never heard...ok, MAKE-BELIEVE game that they are still good. Yeah, that sounds about right." The fans begin to boo him like crazy. "And, what big city wouldn't be complete without their very own minor league basketball team! How about them Hawks!" The fans' boos get louder and louder, and Crisp smiles. "Have no fear, idiots... er, fans! You're about to see a five-star match, the first-ever of my jobber opponent's career." The fans continue to boo him. Meanwhile, Crisp turns to Mike Heftel, and hands him the microphone. "Here, this thing is heavy. Hold it with both hands." Heftel, a bit unsure of this, does what he's told. When he does, Crisp kicks him in the gut, and nails a quick DDT. He picks him up, and whips him into the ropes. Crisp takes aim with a flying clothesline, and nails it. He covers Heftel; at two, he picks him up. The ref warns him, and Crisp laughs it off. He picks Heftel up, and sets him up on the middle rope, facing the corner. Crisp climbs to the top rope, with Heftel's head locked tightly into his arm. He picks Heftel up, and nails an inverted superplex. Another cover, another two count, and another pickup by Crisp. Crisp lets Heftel go, and he walks around in a daze. Crisp lines Heftel up, and nails a bulldog from behind. Then, Crisp drags Heftel into the middle of the ring, and sets him up for a sharpshooter. He locks him in, and applies the move. Heftel cries out in pain, ready to tap. However, as he begins to do so, Crisp lets go of the hold. Then, he locks him in again. Heftel, again ready to tap, feels the pressure relieved by Crisp releasing the hold. Finally, Crisp picks him up, and locks him in for a Gutwrench Powerbomb. He lifts him up, and nails the devastating move. He picks up Heftel again, and signals for his finisher, The Icon. Crisp locks Heftel up, with his head to Crisp's side. He lifts Heftel up, so that Heftel is face-down. He takes off from the corner, and leaps towards the middle of the ring, nailing the inverted sitdown powerbomb. Heftel falls sickly and awkwardly onto the mat, and the crowd is hushed. Crisp covers, and gets the three count. He holds his arms above his head, proud of what he has done. The fans jeer him and boo him crazily. As Crisp celebrates his first win in the fWo, Slaughter moves to a break. Result: Jon Crisp by pinfall Meeting "Come in." Fear vs. Black Panther "Bullet the Blue Sky" by P.O.D. takes to the speakers, as Fear storms down the ramp with an angry expression worn across his face. With 80% of the oxygen in his body escaping and Fear not holding up one bit on the move, Panther uncharacteristically taps out, awarding the match to Fear. Before Panzer can proclaim Fear as the winner, however, the former Underground member exits the ring and reenters with a steel chair intact. As Panther coughs and tries to regain his breath on the mat, Fear drives the end of the chair straight into his chest. He continues his attack by stomping at Panther, and completely ignores the perpetual sound of the bell. He lifts the chair up for a final strike on the Black Panther, but is stopped as he sees the cavalry of the Underground slide into the ring Mike Bear, Ruben Ross, and Hellrazor. Reluctantly, he throws the chair down and eyes each of his ex-friends, before exiting the ring and heading to the back. Panther comes to his feet and shares a stare with Mike Bear for a moment, but the contact ends at that, as he too exits the ring and follows Fear to the back.
