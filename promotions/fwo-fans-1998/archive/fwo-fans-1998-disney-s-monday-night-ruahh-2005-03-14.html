Introduction Previously... Eight years ago it was Jim Dudley who survived a battle royal on the first televised night of the fWo to win the fWo World Heavyweight Championship. Fighting against names like Cactus Jon, Austin Davis, Stevie Roberts, Scott Collins, Ultra Violet, Machina, the Boston Strangler, David "Death" Spencer and others, Jim Dudley's current standing with the promotion could not be any more different. A pawn of the mysterious John Smith, Jim Dudley is the current acting commissioner of the fWo. A job he holds with grudging responsibility, Dudley's motivations for being part of Smith's game has been to this moment hidden and unknown. But as the fWo gears up for a milestone in the promotion's history, Cyberslam VIII, some things are just going to have to break...
