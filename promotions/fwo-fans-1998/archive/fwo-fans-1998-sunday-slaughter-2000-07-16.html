Sunday Slaughter, 16 July 2000

16 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Team Rateings Is Here... Big Wretchy The Ratings Saver arrived inside the Mryiad accompanied by Scot Hartt & The Mutha Fuckin Abusah of Yo' Momma, and were immediately greeted by fans who had been turned away, unable to get a ticket to attend tonight's already sold out three weeks in advance edition of Sunday Night Slaughter. As the trio walked through the entry, they walked past several dozen members of security, medical personnel and backstage helpers... while this happened, Wretch began to address them as they walked several steps behind him, although he never turned around to actually look at them. "Tonight, men... we're going to go to war. That's right. We are Team Ratings... and you two are going to begin a conquest to conquer the tag team division; while I... get myself a shot at that coveted shiny silver lookin' piece of treasury... the Internet Championship. We're going to introduce Mitch Wilson not only to wood, but to charisma... because we all know that Big Wretchy, The Ratings Saver is the most charismatic person the fWo has ever employed... and well, Mitch could learn a lesson on charisma... and who better to teach him than me!" Big Wretchy gave a serious looking grin as he passed more people backstage, and continued to talk. While we could hear the sounds of two men giving struggle, we could not see them... Hartt & Abusah were no longer behind Wretchy, and Wretchy himself was too busy giving (well, he thought he was) a pep talk to his lackey's. With Hartt & Abusah no longer present, and as Wretchy finished up what he was saying, he saw they were no longer behind him. "Guys? HEY! Guys... get back here! Where did you guys go?! I told you Finn doesn't bring his stash of porn to the shows anymore..." Wretchy looked around and still couldn't see any signs of them, so he simply shrugged and asked for directions to his lockerroom. "Lockerrooms rule. You know, for Warfare, I'm going to demand an obscure list of items I want to have at the show... or else... I won't show up... and Finn won't like that because his ratings will go down! Yeah! Obscure items rule!" Wretchy pumped his fist in the air as Slaughter took to the air inside the arena... Challenge Red fireworks blasted into the air, while fans rejoiced as the pyrotechnics obviously signalled that Sunday Night Slaughter was about to begin. The cameras zoomed inside the Myriad in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma and found a sea of fans pumping their fists in the air as Slaughter began. It wasn't long before they had something to cheer about, as "O' Fortuna" began to play, and the fans immediately rose to their feet and began cheering. Their cheer hit a fever pitch as the reigning fWo World Champion since May 20 of earlier this year walked out onto the SlaughterStage. He took no time in walking down the ramp and reaching the ring... before walking up the steps and making the slow walk onto the apron. He climbed inside before asking for a microphone and receiving one rather promptly. The fans cheered as he acknowledged their presence, before the music finally faded, as did the cheers. The arena was now silent. The champion began to speak. "We haven't... been the most talkative lately... the unrelenting schedule of being the fWo World Champion takes it's toll... many champions can attest that one you hold this title... it is your focus... you aren't motivated to share your thoughts with everyone as you once did... your life is consumated by keeping ahold of this belt... and never relinquishing it. But tonight... we're going to speak, and we're going to speak for one reason only: Mitch Wilson. Mitch, everyone knows that what occured on Warfare was the biggest accomplishment in the history of your career... which to date comprised of winning the Internet Title, being a surfer a long time ago, and standing in the shadows of the Frenchie. We're not going to let you make a career... off of us. And we're not going to wait long to prove that... although you are an accomplished wrestler, you're certainly not... good enough to pin us for this fWo World Title. We want a match... and we want it... now." The Strangler tossed his microphone to the floor, before giving the fWo World Title to a referee who was present as he had thought the first item on the agenda tonight would be a Cruiserweight Title match... he was obviously mistaken. Strangler stared intently at the stage, waiting for a sign of the man who claimed to be "Mr. Main Event"... The Boston Strangler vs. "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson As Strangler stands in the ring awaiting Wilson, "Calling All Stations" suddenly hits and out walks Mitch Wilson, licking his chops for his first World Title shot - ever. Wilson tosses his Internet Title aside, slides into the ring and his eyes meet the gaze of Strangler, before the two lock up. The bigger Stranger gains the advantage and locks Wilson into an armbar, but Wilson rolls onto the ground, flips up, and flips Strangler onto the ground with a reversal of the armbar. Wilson immediately drops down into a cross armbreaker, but Strangler quickly breaks and makes it to the ropes. Both men quickly get up and Strangler ducks a Wilson clothesline before hooking him up into a released german suplex. Strangler rolls onto Wilson and makes the cover, but Wilson escapes at two. Strangler brings Wilson up and positions him for a powerbomb. Wilson counters by double leg tripping Strangler and front flipping into a pinning combination, which scores him a two count. Wilson and Strangler make their way to their feet and Wilson immediately leaps high into the air and blasts Strangler with a leg lariat. Wilson covers again and hooks a leg, but Strangler kicks out at two. Wilson is undaunted and brings Strangler up, before whipping him into the ropes. Wilson spins and nails Strangler with a rolling elbowsmash as Strangler comes off the ropes, and Wilson again drops down immediately and makes the cover. Not willing to give up his title just yet, the Strangler lifts a shoulder at the count of two. Wilson brings him up once more and whips him into the corner, before charging in with a leaping bodysplash. Wilson then hammers Strangler with a chop, and another, and another, and one more. Wilson lifts Strangler up and places him on the top turnbuckle in a seated position, before climbing up and hooking his head, preparing him for a superplex. Wilson tries it, but Strangler has hooked his foot in the ropes and Wilson crashes to the mat. Strangler takes a step onto the top rope, before leaping off and crashing down with a flying elbowsmash. Strangler makes the cover... and Wilson kicks out at two. Strangler takes a moment to clear his head, then brings Wilson up again and whips him into the ropes. He makes a rare mistake by putting his head down, and Wilson takes full advantage by hooking him and rolling him into a small package. The referee counts one... two... and Strangler reverses the small package into one of his own. One... two... and Wilson shoulders out in the nick of time. Wilson is up to his feet first and catches Strangler as he gets up with a thunderous clothesline. Wilson brings Strangler back up again and drops him with a gutwrench suplex before rolling straight through into another cross armbreaker. This time, the weakened Strangler cannot escape and Wilson can smell the title victory. Strangler writhes in pain, but Wilson won't let go of the hold, actually pulling back harder in his attempt to dislocate the arm of the Strangler. Finally, the Strangler focuses all his energy on one thing - reversing the hold. The larger Strangler slowly rolls over, grabbing onto the surprised Wilson and lifting him up into the air in a show of incredible strength. Strangler slowly makes his way to his feet, before dropping Wilson with a ring-rocking powerbomb variation. Strangler cannot make the cover immediately however, and only does so after taking precious moments to recover. As a result, Wilson kicks out just at two. Strangler brings Wilson to his feet again and attempts to whip him into the ropes, but Wilson reverses the whip and locks on a crossface chickenwing of his own on the Strangler. Strangler counters by immediately dropping down into a jawbreaker, sending Wilson stumbling back. Strangler gets up and charges at Wilson, but Wilson ducks and the lariat Strangler intended for him strikes the referee instead, knocking him out. As Strangler surveys the situation, Wilson spins him around and hits the Headliner. Wilson covers and reaches out with one arm to attempt to revive the ref, but it's to no avail. As he attempts to wake the ref, Primetime storms the ring, weilding a chair. He enters and blasts Wilson on the back with the chair. Primetime then lifts Wilson up, positions him, and plants him with a Primetime Bomb on the chair. His mission accomplished, Primetime spits on Wilson and rolls from the ring, before heading to the back with the chair held high. Moments pass, before the referee finally awakes and sees the two unconscious wrestlers. He begins a ten-count. One... two... three... four... five... six... and Strangler rolls over and drapes an arm over Wilson. The ref drops down and makes the count. One... two... thre - and Wilson shocks the crowd by lifting a shoulder. Strangler drags himself to his feet, and Wilson grabs the ropes and uses them to pull himself up too. He sees the Strangler and charges at him, but Strangler catches him and plants him with a rotating spinebuster. Strangler starts to cover Wilson, but Wilson reverses miraculously into a Luminary Lock on the arm that was earlier in the cross armbreaker. Strangler realizes this and reaches the ropes before Wilson can lock in the hold enough to do damage. Both men make their way to their feet and Wilson strikes first, lashing out and kicking Strangler, possibly low. He hooks the head and leg of Strangler, and drops him with the Superstar Suplex. Wilson reaches back, hooks the leg, and bridges up. One... two... and Strangler kicks out at the last possible moment. Wilson, normally unflappable, begins to get frustrated. He brings Strangler up and, deciding that he should waste no more time, hooks his head for the Headliner. Wilson wraps his leg around that of the Strangler, in order to add momentum to the force that will drive Strangler's head into the mat and win him his first World Championship. Wilson allows himself a small grin to savor it... and Strangler twists free. Before Wilson can react, Strangler goes behind him and locks on the Boston Massacre, before scissoring Wilson's body with his legs and dropping down to the mat, leaving it impossible for Wilson to reverse with a back mule kick or jawbreaker. Wilson screams in agony, not wanting to give up his chance... then finally the pain is too much and Mitch Wilson taps out, much to the delight of the crowd, who give a hearty cheer to their conquering champion, the Boston Strangler. Strangler is handed the belt by the referee and holds it high above his head as Slaughter heads backstage... Result: The Boston Strangler by submission Making A List And Checking It Twice... Slaughter played on a nearby television set, but that was not of his concern right now. Right now, he was making a list of demands to give to Chris Finn. He looked at his list of demands thus far, and began to speak out loud. "#1 - A bicycle helmet full of four hundred and twenty peanut buttered M&M's, but only of the green, brown and red colors. #2 - Four hundred and twenty mouse pads. #3 - a Britney Spears poster spraypainted with the words "I LUV ABOOSAH". #4 - A poster of David Lee Roth autographed personally for Scot Hartt that reads "Hott For Teacher". #5 -" as Wretch was about to read item number five, he looked at the nearby television set and saw Rain... and nothing but the face of Rain. Wretch sets the list aside, before grabbing his crotch. "Say hello to woo..." before Wretch can finish, he sees Hartt & Abusah... obviously being held hostage by Rain. They were both ducttaped to some wheelchairs, and appeared rendered helpless by the big man. Suddenly, Rain pushed the camera man who was videotaping the scene over, before the camera crashed and static overcame the tv screen. The director in the satellite truck quickly switched over to Wretch, and Wretch was now looking at himself on the tv. "This is pretty damn cool!" Wretch then remembered his lackey's were being held hostage... and hurried out of the room with the list and a two-by-four in hand. Hellrazor obviously caught Chronos off-guard, for his is trying to catch some breath. Hellrazor covers Chronos, but only gets two and a half. Hellrazor picks up Chronos and nails him with a kick to the head, followed by a snap suplex. Hellrazor tosses Chronos into Damnation's corner, and tags in Fear. Hellrazor holds Chronos, while Fear climbs to the second turnbuckle. He jumps off, and nails Chronos with a blast to the head. Fear whips Chronos into the ropes, and tags back Hellrazor. Chronos is met upon his return to Fear by a back bodydrop. As he is flipping over, he feels Hellrazor catching him. Hellrazor nails him with a powerbomb from the back bodydrop. A quick cover by Hellrazor would get a three, if it weren't for Fallen breaking it up. Fallen gets up, and he and Fear begin to brawl, before the ref breaks that up. Hellrazor picks up Chronos and locks him in for a gutwrench suplex. He nails it, and tags in Fear. The two pick up Chronos, and whip him into the ropes. Fear catches Chronos, and the two hit a modified version of the 3D. Another cover, and again Fallen breaks it up at 2 and just under 3. As Hellrazor walks back to his corner, Fear begins to pick up Chronos, and again whips him into the ropes. This time, Chronos grabs the neck of Fear, who was bending down for another back body drop, and hits a swinging neckbreaker. The two lay on the ground amid the cheers of the crowd. The ref begins to count the two out. At the count of four, Chronos finally starts to move. At five, Fear begins to stir. At seven, Chronos is at one knee. When he hits seven, the ref stops counting. Now, the tension was on...who would be the first to tag his partner? Chronos appeared to have the advantage, but he's also running on empty. Fear has just gotten to his feet, and he lunges for Hellrazor. The tag is made, and Hellrazor charges at Chronos. As he gets a few feet away, he grabs his leg. However, he can do no more, as Chronos tags in Fallen. Fallen comes in with fists-a-fire, and he nails Hellrazor with a punch to the head. Fear comes charging at him, and he too gets nailed by Fallen. Hellrazor charges Fallen once more, and again he gets nailed. Fear takes off toward Fallen, and he gets tossed over the top rope. Chronos hops down off the apron and begins to brawl with Fear. Meanwhile, Fallen picks up Hellrazor, and whips him into the corner. He charges Hellrazor, and leaps at him for a splash. At the last possible second, Hellrazor moves, and Fallen nails the turnbuckle. Hellrazor moves quickly, and picks up Fallen, and moves to the top rope. He sets Fallen up, and hits the No More Pain off the top. He goes for a cover and gets the three. Chronos slides in the ring, but it is too late. Damnation has won the match, and retain the tag titles. Fear gets in the ring, and the three men look down on Fallen. Fallen gets up after feeling the DDT off the top, and the two teams begin to congratulate each other on a hard fought match. As they are doing this, the three remaining members of the Dark Riders make their way down to ringside. War has a camera in his hands, and he begins to attack everyone he can. Plague has the film that was in the camera, and he begins to strangle the participants of the match. Meanwhile, Stone watches with a careful eye, despite the smirk on her face. However, what she cannot see is what's behind her. The Black Panther runs from behind her, then right past her, carrying a chair. He slides into the ring, prompting the members of the Riders to slide out. Their deed done, they walk back up the ramp amid boos from the crowd. The Panther helps up Fallen, and then moves toward Chronos. As he tries to help him up, Chronos shoves him away. Panther frowns at this; however, before any action can spread, Fallen gets between the two wrestlers. As they sort out this mess, Slaughter cameras head backstage. Mitch Wilson (c) vs. Primetime vs. Wretch Slaughter heads to the ring for the main event of the show, a huge three-way match for the Internet Title. "Paint It Black" by Gob hits and Big Wretchy The Ratings Saver makes his way to the ring to loud cheers from the crowd, and it is obvious that out of the three, the crowd is on his side. His music is cut off by the excessively annoying "Blind" and Primetime appears on the entrance ramp to a chorus of boos. He walks to the ring, rolls in, and engages in a staredown with Wretch. Both men's attention is diverted to the entrance ramp as "Calling All Stations" starts to play and "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson appears on the entranceway, the Internet Title strapped around his waist. Wilson looks somewhat worse for wear after his tough World Title match against the Boston Strangler earlier on, but still looks as intense as ever. He stalks to the ring as the boos continue, before unstrapping his belt and handing it to the timekeeper. Wilson rolls into the ring, and the bell rings to begin the match. Primetime and Wretch both go after the champion Wilson, striking with fists and chops and looking to wear down the titleholder first. Wilson fights back with punches and chops of his own, and finally knocks Havok away before blocking a punch from Primetime and throwing him into a corner.
