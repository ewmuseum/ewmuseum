Introduction The fWo arrives in Israel distracted and distant from their normal concerns. For having found themselves plucked into the middle of a region at war, all eyes are on the world stage and the actions even now being decided in office buildings thousands of miles away. The end of their Middle East trip in sight, the next few days will test all of them in ways they can't possibly imagine... Arrival Silver Fox stepped out of the dirty cab and looked out on the wasteland where a city had been built up and around. Ashdod, Israel. Perched on the harbor, Ashdod had spread out over the years, not as hampered by strife as neighboring Tel Aviv. But the faces were hardened against the war that they had lived through. Scorch marks from bombings and attacks over the years stood as reminders of the ongoing struggle the region had survived through. Silver Fox sighed and looked at the arena. He didn't want to be here. Frankly, none of them did. The United Nations were still bickering over what to do with Iraq, and the region was in turmoil. Tonight's show would be bad... Sunday's show would be worse. He could feel it... they all could. He walked out of the car and toward the arena, taking in the armed solders that patrolled the streets. The hungry faces of the Palestinians... mired in poverty in this region their cause had been picked up by martyrs and violent activists. No place was fully safe. Silver Fox waited for Samuel Smooth, Twitch and Thumper to arrive and sighed, breathing in the harsh air tinged with a salty aftertaste from the sea. Survival was a strange thing, and often not recognized until the head was firmly planted inside the noose. He wasn't a religious man, but in this holy country he prayed... for his safety and for the safety of those around him. Now it simply remained to be seen if God was listening. Fuck the World FIGHT!
