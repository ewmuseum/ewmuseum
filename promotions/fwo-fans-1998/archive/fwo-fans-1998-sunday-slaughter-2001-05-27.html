fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 27 May 2001

27 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Hellrazor strode through the backstage halls of The Township, Lamont walking at his side. As it turned out, Lamont hadn't been supplied the proper amount of Tender Vittles in his locker room as required by his contract, and he and Hellrazor intended to do something about. Universal would get a tongue-lashing for this, and, if he wasn't lucky, the hissing of his life. Hellrazor looked down at Lamont, then stopped cold as he ran into a living brick wall. Crucifix. Crucifix looked down on Hellrazor, scowling. He wasted no time in explaining his actions. "Last week I asked you for a match. This week I'm telling you. You will give me a match... or else." Hellrazor scoffed. "Not likely, freak-boy." "Is that a fact?" Crucifix lowered his face into Hellrazor's. Hellrazor returned his angry glare. "You damn straight it is." Crucifix then swung at Hellrazor, nailing him. Hellrazor fought back and blasted Crucifix with a right that staggered him. He looked around for a weapon of some sort, and his eyes settled on a chair. He grabbed it, and measured Crucifix. Crucifix saw him coming and simply stood there, arms stretched out. "Go ahead... do it... it's in your nature... it's who we are." Hellrazor hesitated a second, then his decision was made for him as John Willis grabbed the chair from behind him. Willis restrained Hellrazor, and other security members stood between he and Crucifix. Hellrazor growled and released the chair to Willis, then turned and walked off. Lamont snarled at Crucifix, then followed Hellrazor. Crucifix watched him go. "He... looks like a man in desperate need of a wake-up call." Jackhammer/Monte Burns vs. Sphere/Vic Creed "Nobody's Real" by Powerman 5000 brings in one of the smaller competitors in the following match, Monte Burns. "War?" by System of a Down brings out his partner, Jackhammer, who is also the man that pinned Monte Burns at last weeks Slaughter. "Back to School" by Deftones brings out one-half of their opponents, Sphere, but by the size of this man's partner he is more like one-fourth of this team. "Walk" by Pantera brings out Sphere's Tag Team partner, Kodiak Vic Creed. The bell sounds as all four men make their way to their corners, the lighter of the four, Sphere and Burns, will start this one off. They circle the ring and then lock horns, Burns overpowers Sphere and backs him into the ropes and then delivers an open hand chop to the chest. Irish whip, Sphere rebounds off the ropes and runs into a spinning heel kick from Burns. Sphere quick to his feet but is put back down with an arm drag. Sphere up again and charges at Burns but Burns hits a hurricanrana to take the hardcore wrestler down into a pinning combination but only gets one. Burns lifts Sphere back to his feet, double underhook backbreaker. Burns lifts Sphere to his feet once more and wrenches the arm and makes a tag to Jackhammer. Jackhammer climbs to the top ropes and drops the double axe handle on the exposed ribs of Sphere. Jackhammer then follows it up with lefts and rights and then an Irish whip across the ring and Sphere runs right into a sidewalk slam from the big man. Jackhammer to his feet and drops an elbow to the inside of Spheres leg and then begins to wrench it. Jackhammer back to his feet and begins to stomp at the leg of Sphere. Jackhammer now drags Sphere back to his corner by his leg and sets it on the bottom rope. Jackhammer now steps over the leg and then lands on it with his backside, and then does it once more before tagging Burns back in. Burns now leaps over the ropes and drops the elbow across the chest of Sphere and makes a cover, two. Burns lifts Sphere to his feet and hits a double arm DDT and makes another pin attempt. Two count. Sphere fighting back now with closed fists but Burns gouges his eyes and then hits a side Russian leg sweep and makes a cover. But Creed has seen enough and storms the ring and stomps Burns. Jackhammer goes to stop him but ends up on his back when Creed hits a back body drop. Burns goes to meet Creed but Creed hits a military press into a powerslam. The ref now orders Creed out of the ring as Jackhammer slowly rolls out of the ring and Burns is on the mat. Sphere now to his feet but doesn't even know what happened and makes a cover on Burns, only gets a one count. Sphere now slaps on a choke and the ref breaks it and gives Sphere a warning. Sphere backs Burns into the ropes, Irish whip, Burns ducks a superkick from Sphere, rebounds of the ropes but ends up on the receiving end of a headbutt. Sphere felt that headbutt as much as Burns did and looks shaky but makes the tag to Creed. Creed coming to finish what he started last week as he cockily struts over to Burns. Burns tries to crawl away but Creed grabs Burns' tights and pulls him back to the middle of the ring. Creed picks Burns up in a Gorilla Press and drops him ribs first onto the mat after doing some repetitions of course. Creed lifts Burns up again and now plants him into the middle of the ring with the famous Creedbomb! Creed still doesn't go for a cover as he now picks Sphere up again and throws him into the turnbuckles and begins to drive his shoulder into Burns' gut several times. Burns can't take much more of this and needs to make a tag to Jackhammer. Creed then follows it up by launching Burns to the middle of the ring with a T-Bone Suplex. Creed drops to the mat and goes for a cover. One, two, Creed lifts Burns' shoulder up because he still isn't done. Burns now crawling to his corner but Creed places his boot on Burns' back and forces him to the mat. Creed picks Burns up and hits a massive Tiger Suplex and almost breaks Burns' neck. Creed doesn't bridge the move and doesn't make a pin attempt as he now lifts Burns' up for one of the most devastating moves in Creed's arsenal. The reverse Death Valley driver he likes to call the Alaskan Avalanche. But no, Burns is squirming and is able fall on his feet behind Creed. Burns runs towards the ropes and performs an Asai moonsault from the second rope onto a standing Creed which is a mistake because Creed easily catches him over his shoulder and then proceeds to drop Burns throat first across the top rope. Burns falls to the outside holding his throat but Creed follows him and throws him into the guard rail like he's a rag doll. Inside the ring Jackhammer and Sphere are now going at it in a mad brawl. On the outside Creed just threw Burns into the third row and then they continue to brawl into the crowd. Jackhammer just a chokeslam on Sphere and now lifts him up again by his hair and goes for an Irish whip. Sphere runs across the ring and ends hitting the ref and knocking him through the ropes to the outside concrete. Sphere stumbles backwards into a spinebuster by Jackhammer. Creed and Burns are once again at ringside and Creed is dominated the Golden Child. Jackhammer decides to finish Sphere off with the Pumphandle Powerslam he likes to call the HammerFall. But from out of the crowd comes Vengence and he hits Jackhammer with a steel chair. Then Sphere charges him but Vengence puts him down with the Stargazer. Vengence now looking at Creed from the ring and becomes greedy as he attacks the Alaskan from behind. Vengence and Creed are now brawling on the outside, as Burns is able to slip away back to the ring. Burns hops to the top rope and hits the Swanton Bomb he calls 'The Establishment'. The ref sees the cover and slowly makes the count, one, two, three! The team of Monte Burns and Jackhammer wins this one but Burns isn't done as he grabs the chair used on his partner earlier and heads to the outside. Burns is now behind Creed who is beating the shit out of Vengence. Burns then calmly taps Creed on the shoulder and then whacks Creed between the eyes with the steel chair as he was turning around. With Creed down, Burns begins hammering away at his knee, looking for revenge on behalf of his own shoulder. He then grabs a microphone. "Vic... that's what we in the biz call 'payback'. It's a bitch. You wanna hear something else that's a bitch? I'll tell ya. It'll be a bitch for you after I win that title at Redemption and retire, ridding the fWo of the sorry Hardcore division that's bringing it down, forever!" Burns smirks, then tosses the microphone aside and leaves, Creed clutching at his knee the whole time. Result: Jackhammer/Monte Burns by pinfall No-Show Chris Universal looked at his gold Rolex watch. Looked up. Looked at his watch. Looked up. Nothing. He had scheduled a meeting with the East Coast Laws' agent to discuss plans for Redemption, but the Laws were nowhere to be found. As a matter of fact, he hadn't seen them since they had lost the tag team titles. Universal sighed, turned on the television. ESPN was showing highlights from the LA/San Antonio game earlier. The goddamn Flakers had won again, and that simply compounded Universal's bad mood. He hated the Flakers. Universal looked up at the door one last time. He willed it to open, the Laws to appear, ready to sit down and talk... Nothing. Dammit. They were a no-show. Frank Snow vs. Mike Heftel "My Ruin" by Sevendust hits the speakers and Frank Snow walks out, expecting an easy victory. After all, he's facing Mike Heftel. Following closely behind Snow are Robert Jensen and Aimee Horihito. All three stand patiently at ringside, awaiting Mike Heftel's arrival. The fWo's perennial jobber makes his entrance to "Down With The Ship" by Scatterbrain. Snow cracks a rare smile at Heftel's entrance. There's no way in hell he's going to lose this match. Who loses to Mike Heftel, anyway? Snow and Heftel climb in the ring at almost exactly the same moment. Heftel tries to throw the first punch. It's not a good move, on his part. Snow unloads punches on Heftel, sending him to the ground, screaming in pain. The man, who for a long time thought he was Steve Austin's brother, grabs the ropes to get the ref to step in between the two wrestlers. The ref looks back, after moving away Snow, to see Heftel still hanging on to the ropes. He stares at Heftel, who has his eyes closed, and waits for him to release the ropes. But Heftel refuses to let go. Heftel opens an eye slowly, to see an arena full of people staring at him. Finally, with a groan, he lets go of the ropes, and gets to his feet. He doesn't stay on his feet for long. Snow comes crashing toward him and lays him out with a flying lariat. Moaning, in pain, on the mat, Heftel seems ripe for the pickings to Snow. He tries for a cover, but the ref only gets to two, before Heftel surprises everyone in the arena in kicking out. Heftel even looks surprised, himself. Snow pulls Heftel up by his hair, and does his very best hits him with a DDT. Another cover results in another kickout. The ref's not exactly sure what to make of this. He's refereed more than a few Mike Heftel matches and he's never seen Heftel kick out with such intensity. The crowd is stunned into silence, when Heftel comes up with two offensive maneuvers in a row. A kick to the midsection is followed up with a DDT. It seems like, for once, Mike Heftel might be in charge of a match. That feeling doesn't last for long. Snow gets back to his feet and starts hammering away at Heftel. No longer just content in getting a win over one of the biggest losers in fWo history, Snow wants to demolish Heftel. Heftel takes punch after punch, until it seems like he's been knocked through the ring. Then, he takes another blow. At this point, there's no doubt in anyone in the arena's mind that Heftel will lose yet another match. An inverted piledriver from Snow seems to detach Heftel from his head. Snow quickly follows that up by hauling him to a corner, and setting up a devastating move the bottom rope piledriver. When Snow gets him in the position he wants him, he hits the move. Some fans turn their heads in horror and only hear the thunderous thwack of Heftel's head hitting the mat. Snow just looks around for a minute, with no fear of Heftel, who's down and out. Suddenly, shock hits Snow's face. A groggy Heftel quickly rolls up Snow, and the ref jumps to the ground, where his arm smacks the canvas three times. The match is over. Mike Heftel won. In an unbelievable turn of events, the jobber beat Frank Snow. Ruin, however, isn't too happy with the way things turned out. Robert Jensen joins Snow in the ring and the two of them attempt to start beating on Heftel. Heftel, luckily, rolls out of the ring, away from the oncoming assault. With a look of embarrassment on his face, Snow gestures for a microphone. One is thrown to him. He speaks slowly and methodically, "Heftel... We want a piece of you, Mike Heftel. You caused me unbelievable embarrassment tonight, something that I soon won't forget." "That's right, Heftel," continues the other Ruin member, Jensen, "you don't get away with embarrassing Ruin." Heftel quickly grabs a make to respond to the two men standing in the ring. "Even though it seems a bit rushed, and I already have plans for the pay-per-view, how about you two guys take on me...and a partner of my choice." The Ruin members look at each other with small smiles and nod their heads. "Yes," reply the two in unison. "Good. Because my partner will be... Stump!" A roar from the crowd and laughter from Ruin interrupts Heftel, but he doesn't mind, and walks toward the back with a huge grin on his face - he's just won a match. At Redemption, Heftel and Stump vs. Jensen and Snow, be there or be square. Result: Mike Heftel by pinfall Trifecta Slaughter continues on as "Slave To The Grind" brings out Black Quicksilver, rumored leader of the STUMP Squad. He is wearing the fWo International Title around his waist, and has the fWo Women's Title slung over his shoulder. Also of note is a brown sack he is carrying, and he is soon in the ring, where he is immediately handed a microphone shortly after setting the brown sack in the middle of the squared circle. "Hello, hello! For those who keep forgetting who I am, my name is Black Quicksilver, the current reigning fWo International Champion, as well as the current reigning fWo Women's Title...two of the most important titles in this promotion for sure. Tonight, I'm out here for two reasons. One...this." BQ bends down and reaches into the sack, pulling out... "That's right. The fWo European Title is back, and you know what that means. Right again! I am the first person in the history of the fWo to hold three titles at once, and to that I say BOO-YA!" BQ looks to the crowd for approval, and gets confused because most of the fans in attendance are actually cheering him on. "Now, the second reason why I'm out here...I now have three titles, and I think I should defend one of them. Now...which title should I defend?" BQ takes turns looking at each of the titles he owns before coming to a decision. He tosses the sack and all three titles out of the ring and begins to speak once again. "I think I'll defend the Women's Title tonight...against ANY woman in the back. Now...who wants to touch me?" AFI's "Sacrifice Theory" hits as BQ pulls out "The Official Guide To fWo Entrance Themes" and can't seem to find the song that is currently blasting over the PA system. After giving up, he looks up at the ramp to see who's coming down to challenge him for the title. "Oh crap." Black Quicksilver vs. Andrea Andrea, also known as Claire's arch-enemy, slides into the ring and spears BQ out of his boots as a referee also enters the ring. She unloads on BQ with a few closed right hands to the temple before BQ rolls over on top of her. BQ now has the advantage, and it looks like he's got a good idea. He grabs Andrea and continues rolling, a big smile on his face all the while. That smile turns to a look of pain seconds after Andrea manages to send a knee into his gonads. Andrea shoves BQ off of her and retreats to a corner, where she feverishly brushes herself off. BQ, meanwhile, has taken the time to put his boots back on, although he's still noticeably in pain. The crowd pops rather loudly after BQ gets to his feet, because they've noticed Mike Heftel running down to the ring, with Stump in hand. Heftel reaches the ringside area and tosses Stump to BQ. Heftel gives BQ a "thumbs up", and BQ returns one of his own, as it looks like he has another good idea. He takes out the ref with Stump. BQ jumps for joy before tossing Stump back to Heftel and giving another thumbs up. Heftel and Stump leave as Andrea is dumbfounded. BQ urges Andrea to "come get some...no really, come get some, please!". Andrea simply walks over to BQ, kicks him in the gut, and takes him down with a snapmare. She kicks him in the head a few times, before bending over to taunt him. Not surprisingly, Claire sprints down to the ring, slides in, and grabs Andrea by the hair. She runs over to one of the corners and starts banging Andrea's head into the top turnbuckle. The crowd counts along as Andrea's head meets the turnbuckle ten times, and Claire lets go, but Andrea's head is still throwing itself into the turnbuckle. Claire shrugs and shoves Andrea to the mat. She walks over to the semi-conscious BQ and drags him on top of Andrea before waking up the ref. One three-count later, and BQ has actually won a singles match. "Slave To The Grind" hits as Claire helps BQ "snap out of it". BQ becomes ecstatic when he realizes he was on top of a woman, albeit an unconscious one, for about three seconds, and grabs all of his belongings before leaving with Claire. Result: Black Quicksilver by pinfall Burning Again The lights go down and a blue strobe flashes in time to the opening beats of the Lo-Fidelity Allstars "Battle Flag". At the top of the ramp a shape much smaller than those the crowd is used to seeing emerges and is silhouetted against the backdrop of flashing blue. The curving feminine lines swing into motion as the heavier part of the song kicks in and the lights come up to reveal Tempest smiling as she trots to the ring and waves, her vivid blue eyes sparkling with mischief and energy. She scales the turnbuckles and raises her hands to polite cheers, and she grins as the lights glint of her shimmering blue PVC bodysuit. She flips back into the ring and lands gracefully then tosses her head, flipping her long coal black locks from her eyes. She calls for a mic and is handed one as a few whistles and whoops are heard from the crowd. She laughs and waves playfully and then speaks. "Hello everybody! You know I've recieved such a warm welcome here in the fWo that my first weeks here have been a real thrill. But ya know, I just don't understand guys like Fear." She shakes her head and a few people laugh, recalling her pranks on Fear, while a few murmur nervously about why the tiny Tempest would be picking a fight with someone so much larger and more dangerous than herself. "He says he's gonna get someone who doesn't mind beating up on women, but hey, he can call in the sixth fleet for all I care! I'm not scared!" She hops back and forth, shadow boxing with a fierce look on her face and the crowd laughs at her antics. "I'll pulverize 'em! C'mon Fear, whatcha scared of huh? Why's it matter who fears you and who doesn't? I mean if you're really such a great wrestler then it shouldn't even matter how you present yourself, you should be able to..." As Tempest is speaking, the monstrous Fear appears on the entrance ramp. He merely stares down at Tempest. The only female wrestler in the fWo can only return the stare, confused as to what he wants. Had Tempest studied the wrestler's handbook, she would've remembered to look behind her, as that is where the attack always comes from. And this time it comes with a pipe. While Tempest stares up at Fear a woman appears out of the audience and leaps up into the ring with the aforementioned pipe. Most audience members have no idea who she is, a small amount recognize her as one of the founding members of FTfWo, Heaven, while an even small amount recognize her from such failed stables as the bWo and that one that was lead by Silver Fox as the woman named Fyre. It would appear given the uniform that she's back to Fyre again. Fyre continues to wail on Tempest for a minute or so, before picking up the mic that one of the now two female fWo wrestlers dropped. "If you'd like to continue this you pathetic excuse for a wrestler, that can be arranged at Redemption." Fyre leaves the ring and walks toward Fear. As she approaches, Fear gives an approving nod, but Fyre just walks right past him. Fear turns his head slightly, giving a confused, frustrated look in Fyre's direction before turning and following her. In the ring, Tempest is just getting up. She reaches for the microphone. "Oh, I see, sending a woman after me? Too scared to fight me yourself huh Fear? Well...whatever your name is, I'll see you at Redemption!" Tempest bounds out of the ring and back up the ramp as audience members smack their foreheads, thinking about how this new star has bitten off more than she can chew. Hellrazor vs. Robert Jensen "From This Day" hits and Hellrazor is welcome with a warm reception, from the fans in Columbia, South Carolina. Hellrazor still walks enough to the right, that there's room enough for another person to be walking beside him. He makes it to the ring just in time to turn around and see Robert Jensen's entrance. Sevendust's "My Ruin" plays and most fans are not happy about it. Jensen is accompanied by teammate Frank Snow, and Aimee Horihito. Fans are anticipating a brutal brawl between two of the toughest wrestlers in Hellrazor and Jensen, despite Hellrazor's claims to have given up the hardcore lifestyle. Jensen climbs into the ring and immediately gets into Hellrazor's face. The bell rings at the exact moment Jensen lets go of a hard punch. Hellrazor feels the blow, and reacts accordingly, by stepping back and glaring at Jensen for his cheapshot. Hellrazor decides to take the match slowly and ties up with his opponent. Given his height and weight advantage, it comes as no surprise to anyone, when Hellrazor wins the battle of the brawn. He throws Jensen hard to the mat, but Jensen is back to his feet nearly instantaneously. Jensen charges at Hellrazor, trying to use his speed to his advantage, but Hellrazor sidesteps him and he flies against the ropes behind him. Bouncing back, he's hit by a cross-body. With both men down, but Hellrazor on top, the ref manages to count to two, before Jensen manages to get his shoulder up. With both men back on their feet, Hellrazor tries to kick Jensen, but his foot is caught, and Jensen is happy to have his opponent in such a precarious situation. His joy doesn't last for long, though. An enzuiguri by Hellrazor, pretty much wipes away any fun Jensen has been having. Following that up, Hellrazor hits Jensen with a gutwrench suplex, to stay in control of the match. Feeling the end is near for Jensen, Hellrazor tries to setup his finisher with a Sitout Tombstone. It works, and the adrenaline comes rushing through Hellrazor. He pulls Jensen toward the corner... With no one paying them any attention, on the outside, Frank Snow and Aimee Horihito are knocked down and out by a chair...in the hands of Crucifix. The man who wants to reunited Hellrazor with his hardcore ties, continues his assault by bashing Hellrazor over the head with the chair. He follows that up with fierce shots to both Jensen and the referee. With everyone out, Crucifix hauls Hellrazor over to the side of the ring, where he's quickly wrapped in the ropes. Shot after shot from the steel chair leave Hellrazor in worse and worse condition. A man can only take so much, and Hellrazor appears to be at that point, with blood flowing down his injured face. A maniacal smile appears on the face of Crucifix, as he continues to beat away on Hellrazor. Finally, Crucifix decides to throw away the bloody chair. He reaches down and touches Hellrazor's bloody face. He dips his finger in the blood rippling out of the wounded Hellrazor, then touches it to his own face. Fans in the arena struggle to see what it is he's drawing. After Crucifix moves out of the way, it comes into plain sight. A crucifix has been drawn in Hellrazor's blood, on Crucifix's face. With his devilish smile still plastered on his face, Crucifix leaves the ring area, where he has left five people laying, and continues toward the back. Result: No Contest Respect Mike Bear didn't expect any special to happen when he opened the door of his locker room. All he wanted to do was go out to the weight room and get some work in before his title defense tonight. He knew he'd be going against Chronos as well as Manson, and it would serve him well to be as ready as possible. When he exited the room, he nearly ran straight into the Black Panther, who had been reaching for the doorknob himself. Panther and Bear briefly looked each at each other, then Panther spoke. "Michael... I've been looking for you." "Because?" "I have to talk to you. Last week was about more than returning favors... it was about respect. It takes a lot to earn my respect, but you did so when you saved me from Fear. It may not have much meaning to you, but, as their number is so few, I thought you would be well served to know that you are among the number that has earned the respect of the Black Panther." Bear considered this a moment, then studied the Panther. Silently, he turned and went back into his locker room. Seconds later, he emerged with a Heineken in hand. Bear held it out to the Panther. Panther looked Bear in the eyes, then took the Heineken. Panther became puzzled as Mike Bear's face changed from acceptance to alarm. Before he could pop open the Heine, he was clobbered from behind by Fear and Fyre, working, for the moment, together. They then fell on Bear, who fought back against both of them with wildly swinging right hands. Panther got up, popped the top of the Heineken, took a quick swig, then spun Fear around and spat the beer in his eyes. Fear stumbled off, blinded, and Fyre took a quick look at her opponents before electing to join Fear. Fear shouted, pissed, "You think you're so damn tough?! I'll show you what tough really is! You and me, Panther, at Redemption!" Panther looked him over. "I'll see you there." Fear and Fyre stormed off, and Bear and Panther turned to walk off as well. As they did, Bear only had one question. "Way to fight them off and all, but did you really have to waste so much Heineken?" Fear/Insane Wonder vs. Black Panther/Eddy Fate "Sugar" by System of a Down starts up, bringing out the first of four competitors. Insane Wonder comes down and climbs into the ring. "Bullet the Blue Sky" by P.O.D. brings out Wonder's tag team partner this evening, Fear. The big man climbs into the ring and taps fists with Wonder as the music changes to "Du Hast." From behind the curtain appears the former Wrestler of the Year, Black Panther. He pauses at the entrance, knowing enough not to rush down there without his partner. "You Can't Deny Fate!" echoes through the arena, and "The Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson soon follows. Out walks the fWo Cruiserweight Champion Eddy Fate. Together they walk down the ramp, eyeing their opponents carefully. They climb up onto the apron across the ring from Fear and Insane Wonder, and Panther climbs into the ring. Fear steps out onto the apron, and the bell is rung. They circle each other for a few seconds until Insane Wonder charges in, tackling Panther and showering him with punches. Panther manages to shove Wonder off of him, and as they get to their feet Panther takes him down with a clothesline. Wonder is back on his feet in an instant only to be met with another Panther clothesline. Panther brings Wonder to his feet and sends him into the ropes, but Fear slaps his partner on the back as Wonder rebounds. Panther goes for a third clothesline, but Wonder ducks under and Panther gets a faceful of Fear's boot. Eddy Fate doesn't see the tag and climbs in. The referee yells at Fate to return to his corner, but Fate persists. Meanwhile, Wonder and Fear send Panther into the ropes, and after kicking him in the stomach simultaneously they deliver a wicked double-DDT. The Cruiserweight Champion decides to step back out, bringing the ref's attention back to the match at hand. He forces Wonder to leave, but Fear continues the beating. He brings Panther up, and takes him over with a towering suplex. He goes off the ropes and attempts a leg drop, but Panther rolls out of the way and scrambles to his corner where he tags in Fate. Fate rushes in and dropkicks Fear, then surprises Wonder on the apron with a punch. Turning his attention back to Fear, Eddy Fate climbs the ropes and comes off with a hurricanrana that takes the 7-footer down. Fate puts in some well-placed kicks before Fear can rise again. Fear stands, and throws Fate into the nearby corner. He rocks Fate's head back with a few elbow shots, and picks him up and places him on the top rope. He starts to climb up after him, but Fate recovers and punches back. Fear steps back onto the mat, and Fate hooks his head. Fate leaps off, nailing a picturesque Tornado DDT. He walks back over and tags in the Black Panther, who notices Fear has rolled out of the ring. Panther takes advantage of the situation, nailing a springboard moonsault onto him. As they rise, they both start brawling until Fear gorilla presses Panther into the fans. He climbs over the barrier as well, and stalks after him. The referee yells at them to come back, meanwhile Insane Wonder grabs his can of tuna. Eddy Fate comes back into the ring, figuring he's the legal man now, and as he turns to find Wonder he gets a face full of tuna. Wonder tosses the can to the side and makes the pin. The referee, confused, makes the three-count and leaves, without raising Wonder's hand in victory as Fear and Panther brawl well into the upper rows of the ringside area. Result: Fear/Insane Wonder by pinfall Six Days (III) Sunday Slaughter came back from commercial to a black screen, and the opening strains of Rage Against The Machine's "Testify" could be heard. One word appeared. REDEMPTION A voiceover began. "In six days, the fans Wrestling organization presents its May pay-per-view, Redemption." As the voice recapped the various matches for the show, highlights of the events leading up to the matches were shown. "Andrea meets Claire with Mike Heftel as the referee. Andrea showed up after CyberSlam, focused only on hurting Claire. Claire has finally had enough, and she gets her chance to fight back... at Redemption." "Jackhammer, Sphere, and Vengence meet in a triangle match. All three men are looking to move up the ladder in the fWo, and their paths have crossed over the past month with explosive results. It explodes one last time... at Redemption." "Ruin defends the fWo World Tag Team Titles against Mike Heftel and Stump. Heftel embarrassed Ruin, and now Ruin looks to lay waste to Heftel and his inanimate partner. Heftel tries to survive... at Redemption." "Tempest faces Fyre. Tempest, notorious for her love of harmless but funny pranks, went a step too far when she pulled a series on Fear. Fear then brought in his secret weapon from out of the past... Fyre. Boston Strangler/Ruben Ross vs. Erik Kelly/Greg Davis Survivor's "Eye of the Tiger" hums throughout the arena, and Greg Davis makes his entrance to an enormity of jeers. He slides into the ring and taunts overweight fans ringside as a loud cry of "POWERBOMB!" rocks over the speakers, followed by Limp Bizkit's single, "Break Stuff." An enthusiastic Matt Loco leads the intimidating Erik Kelly to the ring as the crowd continues with the jeers. Kelly enters the ring and gives a nod of recognition to Davis, before leaning over the ropes and raising a fist to the booing crowd. The song "Bulls on Parade" by RATM suddenly replaces his music. 'Black Plague' Ruben Ross appears at the entrance with his Internet Title belt clutched tightly in his right hand, and he waits there for his tag team partner. O' Fortuna is the song that brings out the last competitor in this match, the Boston Strangler. Strangler appears through the curtains to a large rise in cheers and stalks to the ring with Ruben Ross beside of him. They simultaneously enter the ring, and the bell firmly sounds. Ross pats Strangler on the chest and tells him that he will commence the match, and the Boston native agrees. Greg assures Kelly that he will face off against Ross, and the seven-footer nods and exits to the apron over the top rope. The bell sounds. Davis and Ross proceed to engage in a grapple, with Greg using his height and weight advantage to push Ross to the ground. Ross gets up and locks with Davis in a collar-and-elbow tie-up once more, this time taking him over in a headlock. Davis picks his opponent up and drops him right onto his knee with an atomic drop. He gets behind him with a waist lock and attempts a German suplex, but Ruben lands on his feet and schoolboys Davis up. At two, Greg escapes the predicament. Both men stand up, and Ruben Ross charges at Davis. Greg takes control with an arm-drag takeover and reaches out to Kelly, who tags in with his monstrous hand. Greg holds Ross still, while Erik bounces off the ropes and elevates his knee right into Ruben's ribcage. Ross slumps to the mat gasping for air, and then makes an effort to get to his knees. Kelly clasps his hands together and drops down a hammer-like double axe-handle across Ruben's upper back. Ross fights back with a chop to Kelly's broad chest, but the big man shrugs it off and pushes Ross into the corner by the face. Kelly grabs Ross by the arm and shoots him off into the other corner. Ross hits with such impact that he stumbles out and falls right into Kelly's grasps. The Eradicator picks up the man who defeated him at Cyberslam in a military press, and then drops him face and stomach first to the mat. Ross bounces off the mat like a basketball, and Matt Loco stands and laughs sadistically at Kelly's weaker opponent. Kelly bends over to pick Ross up, but the Internet Champion comes out of nowhere with a forearm shot to the face and then takes to the ropes. He ducks under a big boot from Kelly, turns him around and dropkicks him into the ropes. Ross quickly heads up to the top rope and bulldogs Kelly face-first into the mat. Ross quickly runs to bounce off the ropes, but Greg hits a well-placed kick to his back through the ropes. Immediately, Ruben Ross turns around and nails Davis with a right. As he turns around, however, he is met with a huge hand around his throat. Kelly attempts to lift Ross up, but Ross counters with a shin kick and then a spinning heel kick. With a great amount of energy, Ross rolls forward and tags into the Strangler, who enters the ring to a wild pop from the crowd. Kelly is up to one knee before he can even realize what is happening, and the Strangler takes him down with a big boot to the face. Kelly gets up from the move, but gets booted to the mid-section and taken over with a gut-wrench suplex. Strangler comes off the ropes and leaps high in the air, before dropping down with an elbow drop... that misses. Kelly gets to his feet and reaches out to Davis, who reluctantly makes the tag. Erik throws Strangler into the corner and pins his foot against his throat as Davis lashes in hard rights to the Strangler's mid-section. Ruben Ross flies over the top rope with a clothesline that hits Kelly, and proceeds to brawl with him at another one of the corners. Davis attempts an Irish whip on the Strangler, but Strangler holds on and pulls Greg in, locking him in for the Boston Massacre. Kelly turns to break the hold, but Ruben Ross uses that distraction to take and hold him down with a drop-toe-hold. Greg feels no choice but to tap, and the ref calls for the bell, awarding the match to Strangler and Ross. The crowd cheers as the referee raises the Strangler's arm, declaring he and Ross the victors, and Slaughter goes to the final commercial of the night. Result: Boston Strangler/Ruben Ross by pinfall Mike Bear (c) vs. Adam Manson Slaughter heads to the ring for the main event of the night, and the crowd is jacked. "Burning Blood" hits, and the crowd boos as Adam Manson makes his way to the ring, looking as fearsome and intimidating as always. He climbs onto the apron and steps over the top rope into the ring, then waits for his opponent. "Engel" hits, and the crowd goes nuts as Mike Bear makes his way to the ring, the fWo World Title belt wrapped around his waist. Bear climbs up the ring steps and into the ring, then unstraps the belt and hands it to the referee, who raises it into the air. He then hands it off to the timekeeper and signals for the bell. Manson and Bear lock up, and Bear takes Manson down with a drop toe hold, dropping him flat on his face. Manson quickly gets back up and charges at Bear with a clothesline, but Bear ducks and, as Manson comes off the ropes, nails him with a clothesline of his own. Bear brings Manson up and hooks him up, then drops him with a snap suplex and makes the first cover of the match. Manson easily kicks out at one. Bear brings Manson up to his feet, but Manson lashes out with a low blow, doubling Bear over. Manson then leaps and clocks Bear with an axe kick, taking him down to the mat. Manson covers, but Bear kicks out at two. Manson brings Bear to his feet and whips him into the ropes, then catches him coming off with a spinebuster throw. Manson covers once more, but again Bear kicks out at two. Manson brings Bear up again and grabs him, then drops him with an inverted chokeslam. Cover. Two-count. Manson brings him up again kick to the back of the leg, diving inverted DDT. Cover. Two-count. Manson heads to the top rope. Bear gets up. Destiny Hammer. Cover. Two-count. Manson shouts in frustration, a series of high-impact moves having done little to drop Bear. He decides it's time to bring out the heavier artillery. He brings Bear up, then goes behind him. He sets him up in a german suplex position and goes for the Screaming From The Sky but Bear counters with a reverse kick straight to the testes. Manson falls back, holding his crotch, and Bear advances on him. Bear picks Manson up, positions him, and spikes him with a piledriver. Not satisfied yet, Bear brings Manson up and shoves him into the corner, then unloads on him with kicks and stomps. He then takes a few steps back, charges forward, and blasts Manson in the corner with a superkick. Manson slumps into a sitting position, and Bear backs into the opposite corner, then charges in with a seated dropkick to the face. He brings Manson out of the corner and shrugs him onto his shoulders. Bear goes for a death valley driver, but Manson falls off of him and lands behind him. Bear turns, and Manson clenches his hand around Bear's face. Exhale The Vile. Before Manson can slam him to the mat, Bear kicks Manson below the belt, causing him to release the hold. Bear grabs Manson and whips him, then pulls him back in a short whip, looking for a lariat. Manson ducks under, however, and twists Bear's arm. He then climbs the turnbuckles onto the top rope, looking for the Rope-Walk DDT. Instead, Bear dives into the ropes, causing Manson to fall on the top rope, again hurting his thrice-abused crotch. Bear then shrugs Manson onto his shoulders, setting him up perfectly for the Whiplash. Bear sets him up, but Manson slides down his back. Bear turns, and Manson grabs him and whips him into the corner or tries to. Bear reverses, and sends Manson flying into the corner. Unfortunately, the referee is caught and sandwiched between Manson and the corner. Manson stumbles out, and the referee falls to the mat, unconscious. Bear catches Manson, lifts him onto his shoulders, and hits the Whiplash. Unfortunately for Bear, he never sees Ric Chronos come out of the crowd, slide into the ring behind him, and tap him on the shoulder. Chronos then kicks him in the gut, doubling him over, then hooks his head and drives him into the mat with a Figure-Four DDT. Chronos, using the logic that his rightful title will be much easier to get if it's in the hands of a friend, rolls Adam Manson onto Mike Bear and revives the ref. He then slides from the ring and heads to the back, never looking back. The referee slowly makes the count. One. Two. No. Bear just gets a shoulder up, and the crowd goes nuts. Both men struggle to their feet, and Manson takes a wild swing at Bear. Bear ducks it, and, for the third time in the last five minutes, lifts Manson onto his shoulders for the Whiplash. For the second time in the last five minutes, he hits it. Bear then makes the cover. The ref counts. One. Two. Three. Mike Bear retains his title going into Redemption. He doesn't have any time to celebrate, however, as Ric Chronos rushes back to the ring from the back and grabs Bear. He then takes him down with the Chronosphere. Chronos rolls to the outside of the ring and retrieves the fWo World Title. He slides back in and stands over the fallen Bear, then raises the title high into the air to the thunderous boos of the crowd. The boos suddenly turn to cheers as the Underground Ruben Ross, Hellrazor, Black Panther, Eddie, and Lamont rushes the ring. Chronos tosses the belt aside, grabs the semi-conscious Adam Manson, and exits the ring, leaving into the crowd, and looking back into the ring with a look of rage, defiance, and hate on his face...
