Sunday Slaughter, 9 January 2000

9 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Chris Finn walked slowly into the New Haven arena, his head hung. He patted at his thinning hair with an absent gesture and sighed inwardly to himself. He looked tired, but there was an edge of panic that underlay his movements. He patted his jacket pocket, and pulled a fresh carton of cigarettes free. He lit one, and coughed as the smoke filled his lungs. This was a new experience for him, and one that his body wasn't accepting easily. His coughing filled the halls, and Geoff Green heard the noise and walked toward him. "Finn... jesus man, you look like shit..." Chris Finn nodded and half sat, half fell into a studio chair set in the receiving room. "Green I... you know, I always thought that Armstrong would be the one to sink us." Green shook his head, confused. "I'm afraid I don't know what you mean..." Finn shook his head absently. "Never mind. Tell security to be in full force tonight. We're going to have problems." Green wandered away and Finn sighed and rose to his feet. He padded down the hallway in no particular direction, and didn't see Chris Universal until he was nearly on top of him. Finn collided with the wrestler and dropped the papers he was carrying. Finn stood and Universal growled, "Watch where you're going you little bitch." Finn collected himself and mumbled an apology. Universal started to head off, and then turned. "What in the hell is wrong with you anyway?" Finn was walking away then, and talked over his back. "It's not your concern... just a mistake I made... a big mistake." Universal laughed. "Yeah, no kidding. You should have left that French bastard in France... bringing him back was a real stupid mistake." Finn was farther away now, and paused for a moment to look over his shoulder. "No... not him. Davis." Micky Maliboo vs. Fear "Another One Bites The Dust" hit the loudspeakers, and a pumped up looking Micky Maliboo made his way down to the ring accompanied by the voluptuous Vixon. The mood quickly changed from there, though, as the towering man known as Fear made his way to the ring minus his Underground comrades. Fear stepped over the top rope and waited for the match to begin. Once the bell rang, Fear made a few attempts to lock up with Maliboo, but the smaller Micky seemed hesitant, unsure of how to fight a man who nearly had an entire foot over him. Fear, getting frustrated, finally had it and rushed Maliboo, but Micky sidestepped Fear and sent him stomach first into the turnbuckles. Fear stumbled out and slowly turned to meet the charging form of Micky Maliboo. Maliboo leapt for a cross body block, but was caught by Fear, only to have Micky slip out on top and land on the top turnbuckle behind Fear. Maliboo quickly grabbed the head of Fear and executed a flying bulldog, driving Fear's head into the ring. As Fear held his head, attempting to recover, Micky climbed out to the apron and slingshot himself back into the ring and into Fear with a flying shoulder block. Fear went down and Micky made the quick cover, but Fear heaved Maliboo off of him at the count of two. Fear rolled to the outside, trying to slow down the pace of the match, but Maliboo slid out after him and attacked Fear from behind. Maliboo attempted to whip Fear into the ring post, but Fear reversed it and sent Micky face first into the cold metal. Maliboo dropped to the floor, but was rolled back into the ring by Fear. Fear, looking rather angry, literally threw Maliboo into the corner, who bounced out and right back into the arms of Fear. Fear took Maliboo and, once again, chucked him into the corner, but this time as Maliboo came out Fear caught him with a big boot to the face. Maliboo hit the mat hard, and Fear began to choke out his opponent with his boot. The ref counted to four and Fear let go, then once again reapplied the choke. Maliboo was able to get the ropes this time, though, and the ref stepped in between Fear and Maliboo in order to separate the two. Fear ignored the referee, though, and jumped onto the back of the Maliboo, who's neck was bounced across the bottom rope before he was able to pull himself onto the apron. Fear turned around and said a few more words to the ref before focusing on Maliboo, who had by then gotten to his feet on the apron. Fear decked Micky with a solid right hand, and another, and another, but he would not go down. Then, in a burst of energy, Maliboo grabbed Fear by the neck and dropped down, hotshotting him across the top rope. As Fear staggered back, Maliboo slid back into the ring and began to wail on his foe with a series of fists to the chin. Fear fell against the ropes, and as he came to his senses Maliboo climbed to the top rope and flew into Fear with a moonsault bodyblock, rolling him up for a two count. Maliboo was on fire, and as he Fear to his feet he went to lift him for the Mellon Collie, but Fear blocked Micky's efforts with a sharp elbow to the back of his neck. Maliboo staggered backwards, gripping his neck, but Fear pulled him into a pair of standing leg scissors and dropped Maliboo with a huge fallaway powerbomb that looked to have knocked Maliboo out cold. Fear signaled to the crowd for the Thantophobia, who responded with a mixed reaction. Fear grabbed Micky and lifted him up in the air with a gorilla press position, but instead of dropping him into a stomach breaker Fear made his way towards the apron. The ref got in the way of Fear and warned him not to throw Maliboo to the outside of the ring, which angered Fear. Fear then threw a prone Maliboo directly into the ref, causing both men to go down in a heap. Fear smirked as the bell rang, signaling his disqualification, but he didn't care. Fear picked up Micky in a gorilla press position once again, but this time executed the Thantophobia. As Maliboo whimpered, holding his gut, Fear lifted Maliboo once again, and dropped him into a second Thantophoba. Finally, fWo officials rushed the ring and pulled Maliboo out. As security tried to get Fear to leave the ring, Slaughter broke for a commercial... Result: Micky Maliboo by DQ Mistakes were made Geoff spotted Finn pacing in front of the small booking office and nodded to him. "No problems yet... no problems at all. What were you expecting anyway?" Finn pointed to the main arena, and ignored Green's question. "And the announcers? Who's on desk?" Green looked frustrated now, "Roberts and Loco... dammit, Finn... we can't solve anything if you don't tell us what in the hell is going on." Finn looked hard into the face of Green, and then down at his feet. "Even though he was retired last July he was always under contract... we were paying him, paying him for nothing. So he just couldn't return to the in the ring until the 23 of this month... but with all the stuff going on, it seemed like the right way to go. He always was a draw, and with Travis vanishing, Armstrong... So I phoned him up, asked him to come in... asked him to come by. All well and good, right? We talked, and he seemed genuine enough... seemed like the right guy for the job... he said... well, he said to trust him. So I did. I... had no idea what his motives were. He never said anything about wanting to kill the fWo... but..." Finn shook his head, and some understanding began to dawn on Green's face. "Oh, christ..." Briana (c) vs. Nikki "Laid" by James played, signaling the introduction of tonight's challenger for the fWo Woman's Championship, Nikki. As Nikki made her way to the ring she was followed by a slightly different looking Silver Fox, perhaps due to his humbling at the hands of Black Quicksilver. Next came the champion herself, Briana, who held her newly won belt in the air, perhaps as a way of getting under the skin of Nikki, who had been the last woman to hold the belt previous to the French Foundation's run with it. The bell rang, and the two competitors immediately locked up. Briana pushed Nikki back into her own corner and delivered a series of hard chops to the chest, then grabbed Nikki's hair and threw her into the center of the ring. Nikki landed with a loud thump and held her back in pain, but Briana stayed on the attack with a dropkick to the face of her sitting opponent. Briana dropped for the quick cover, but Nikki snuck out well before a two count and slid to the outside. As Nikki consulted with Silver Fox, Briana hopped onto the second turnbuckle and played to the crowd. Once the referee hit the nine count Nikki reluctantly rolled back into the ring, only to be met by Briana charging for a clothesline. Nikki ducked, though, and back dropped Briana over the top rope and to the outside. As Briana hit the padded floor, Nikki dropped to her knee, yelling in pain as she held her ankle. While the ref checked up on Nikki, Silver Fox grabbed a folding chair at ringside and absolutely clobbered Briana with a vicious shot to the back. Briana went down hard, and Nikki was more than happy to point her prone body out to the ref who laid a ten count down. By the time the ref had counted to seven Briana had yet to move and, thinking her easy prey, Nikki signaled to Silver Fox to roll her inside. Fox obeyed, and Nikki, who's ankle had made a miraculous recovery, covered Briana. 1... 2... 2 1/2... 2 3/4... Briana got the shoulder up in the nick of time! Nikki looked to Silver Fox in shock, almost as if asking what to do now. Fox shrugged, and an angry Nikki pulled Briana to her feet and tried to whip her to the ropes, but Briana countered and sent Nikki into the ropes, instead. Nikki came running back and right into a huge power slam by Briana. Briana quickly popped up to her feet as the crowd got solidly behind her, and began to scale the turnbuckles. Nikki still had yet to move, and Silver Fox attempted to defend his woman by distracting Briana. Briana, though, simply launched herself at Silver Fox with a flying cross body block that knocked the wind out of him. Briana then rolled back into the ring where Nikki was just getting back to her feet. After measuring her up Briana darted in and jumped, connecting directly to the chin of Nikki with her spinwheel kick. Nikki went down in a heap and Briana made the easy cover for the pinfall victory. As Nikki was rolled out of the ring by Silver Fox, Briana celebrated inside with her belt. Result: Briana by Pinfall Morale Backstage several wrestlers sat talking quietly amongst themselves, and comparing notes. The Black Panther sat next to Havok, and the two of them talked about the recent changes to Matt Douglas. "Well yeah, I suppose he looks a little like the Hardy boy... but you know Douglas... quite a different style really. He's more solid... his moves are targeted, where the other boy is more flash, less substance. You know how it is..." Havok nodded, and then pointed to the door where Chris Finn now stood. Finn looked around the room, taking in the twenty or so workers that now looked up at him expectantly. The Boston Strangler now stood, and with one hand on his hip looked hard at Finn. "We want to know what Davis is doing here... he's trouble. We had enough trouble with him last time... we don't need him around again." There was nodding in the room, and Finn started to speak, stopped, and then started again. "Listen... a mistake was made. A big mistake... and it was mine. I'm looking for ways to correct it now... but remember, I'm just an announcer. I'm acting in this position now... coordinator... and I'm doing as good as I can. But I'm not an owner or a leader. I don't know where Travis went... and somehow I gather than none of you really want him back anyway." There was some mumbling agreement then, and the wrestlers looked around at each other in agreement. Finn continued, "But things are going to be bad for the next few weeks... real bad. Hang in there, everyone... and we can get through this. Bit things are going to be a mess... a real mess." Black Panther & Havok vs. Underground The music of the Underground starts to play, and the former tag team champions Bear and Wretch come out to a great ovation from the crowd. Looking intense due to the level of competition, they stretch in the ring as "Killed The Cat" begins to play and out walk fWo Cruiserweight Champion Havok and the fWo World Heavyweight Champion, the Black Panther. They get a great ovation from the crowd, and both hold their gold up in the air to the fans' approval. Finally, both men make their way into the ring and eye their opponents. As a show of respect, the men starting the match, Black Panther and Mike Bear, shake hands before circling as the bell rings. They lock up, and the World Champion gains the advantage, twisting Bear into an armbar before turning it into a hammerlock. Bear reaches back, looking for a reversal opportunity, but cannot find one. Panther drops down, taking Bear down with a dth. He switches to a front headlock, and brings Bear to his feet, before taking him down a DDT. Panther covers, but Bear kicks out at two. Panther brings Bear to his feet, and takes him over with a double underhook suplex. Bear gets back to his feet, and Panther attempts to whip him into the ropes, but Bear manages to reverse it. However, he is caught by Panther coming off with a swinging neckbreaker as he lowers his head. Panther covers, but Bear kicks out at two. Bear gets up, and backs slowly into his corner, looking for a tag. Panther allows him to do so with no attack, and Bear tags in Wretch. Wretch slingshots in and as Panther charges at him, baseball slides under the champ's legs. He hits a quick dropkick to the back of the head, sending Panther into the Underground corner, where Bear gives him a forearm shot for good measure, sending him right back into a back suplex from Wretch. Wretch quickly rolls to the apron, and comes back in with a slingshot legdrop. He covers Panther, who kicks out at two. Wretch brings the larger Panther up, and sends him into the corner. Wretch charges in, and takes Panther out of the corner with a monkey flip. Panther gets up quickly, however, and as Wretch attempts a hurricanrana, Panther catches the smaller Wretch and plants him with a liger bomb. The ref counts, but Wretch kicks out at two. Panther gets up, and brings Wretch up with him. He backs him into the ropes with a series of rights, and whips him across. Wretch manages to reverse, and puts his head down for a back body drop. However, Panther leapfrogs, and grabs Wretch's head from behind. He then drops down with a standing bulldog. Instead of going for a cover, Panther tags in his partner Havok as Wretch begins to get to his feet. Wretch is up by the time Havok is in, and they go at it in a frenzy, trading rights and lefts back and forth. Finally, Havok, being the fresh man, gains the advantage, and backs Wretch into the ropes. He starts to whip him across, but instead turns him back around and plants him with a short spinebuster bomb. Havok quickly heads up top, and prepares a massive frog splash. However, Wretch moves and Havok strikes the mat hard. Wretch quickly covers, but Havok kicks out at two. Wretch gets up first, and waits for Havok to get up as well. When he does, Wretch grabs him and hits a michinoku driver on the Cruiserweight champion. Quickly, he heads to the top and measures his downed opponent. Wretch then leaps off with a big flying headbutt. Instead of covering, he tags in his partner Mike Bear and the Underground prepares a double team move. Bear grabs the legs of Havok in a boston crab position, but lifts him up horizontally. Wretch then grabs the head in a falling reverse ddt position. They then drop to the mat. Bear quickly covers, but the count is broken up at two by the Panther. As the ref ushers Panther from the ring, Bear locks Havok in a boston crab. Wretch then leaps in with a slingshot somersault legdrop to the back of the head. Bear releases the hold, rolls Havok over, and covers. To his amazement, Havok kicks out at two and ninety-nine one hundredths. Bear brings him up again, and shoves him into the corner. He unloads with a series of chops. Bear then puts Havok on the second rope. He climbs up with him, and prepares a superplex. However, Havok fights back with a series of punches, and manages to turn Bear to face the mat. He then hooks his head and leaps off with a flying bulldog. Both men are out, and it's a race to the tag. Bear gets it first, tagging in Wretch. However, immediately after, Havok tags in the World Champion Black Panther, who clocks Wretch with a clothesline as he enters. Wretch gets back up, but is powerslammed down by Panther. Panther covers, but Wretch kicks out. At this point, Mike Bear has recovered enough to get up, and sees Havok getting up with the help of the ropes. Bear charges at Havok, and takes him over the top rope with a clothesline. Meanwhile, the Black Panther has set Wretch on the top rope and is preparing to hit the Pounce. Bear walks up beneath him, and grabs him in a doomsday device position. Wretch then leaps off the top rope with a spinning heel kick as Bear falls, and Bear quickly covers Panther and hooks both legs. The ref counts three, as Panther kicks out a second too late, and the Underground picks up the win. Bear and Wretch celebrate their somewhat-upset win, as Havok rolls back in and gives Panther a hand to get back to his feet. They roll out to the outside and collect their titles as Slaughter continues... Result: Underground by Pinfall Protesting "But I don't WANT to wear the shirt." Machina looked distastefully at the shirt that the large woman was holding out to him. It read "I WAS SEXUALLY HARASSED BY WRESTLING". The large woman thrust the shirt at him, and Penny Pureheart patted his shoulder from behind. "It's okay Ed... don't be ashamed of it... we need to tell the world just how evil professional wrestling is." Machina took the shirt and looked at it grumbling, "Man, this sucks..." Penny's brow furrowed and she looked hard at Machina. "What did you say?" Machina rolled his eyes under his mask, and put on the shirt. "I said that wrestling sucks. Now come on... let's get in position in front of the arena and get this over with." Penny nodded, and Machina followed behind the group of protesters. Rudo walked up next to him and looked at the shirt, and then began to laugh. "Oh man, that's the most pathetic things I've ever seen... and I've seen the Shocker." Machina grunted. "You know, that Penny has a nice ass and all, but this protesting stuff really blows. I've got a match coming up next week and I guess it's the Bruce Springsteen mask for me... but man, I wish I could just tell her the truth and she would shut her yapper and accept it. What is it with women anyway? Why can't they just get in the kitchen and make us all dinner? Hey... speaking of dominating women, what's up with you and that whipped Collins?" Rudo shrugged. "I've got to cut a promo on him... should be a good match... if Stone lets him wrestle that is. Man, just be glad you're out here with these nuts instead of in a relationship with that wench." Chris Universal vs. Living "Guerilla Radio" by Rage against the Machine heralds the entrance of Chris Universal. True to form, the self-proclaimed 'Franchise' of the fWo arrogantly struts towards the ring. He scoffs at the crowd, sneering at them as he passes towards the ring. He reaches the apron and hops the top-rope. Universal puts on a mock expression of fear as "Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson begins signaling the arrival of Living. A man unhinged from his surroundings and even himself, he mutters zealously to himself and casts suspicious glares at all around him. With a ring of froth forming around his mouth he begins shouting loudly to himself and seemingly is unable to prevent himself from clawing and scratching at his face. Slithering under the bottom rope he crawls towards Universal with a look of sheer lunacy. Universal simply puts a hand to his mouth, patting it as if yawning. Without warning he leaps into action and begins to stomp Living till he is cowering beneath Universal's foot. Universal laughs and parades around the ring imitating the unusual behavior of Living. He turns but finds Living who promptly wipes the cocky grin off of Universal's face with a fist that connects square between the eyes. Living shoves Universal into the ropes and when he comes back, power-slams him deep into the mat. He stands over Universal, shaking violently. After a series of stomps to the chest, Living lifts Universal but Universal sneaks a quick low blow past the referee and brings Living to his knees with numbing pain as he grabs for his midsection. Universal kicks him in the face, lifts him to his feet, and uses a swinging neck-breaker to gain the advantage. Universal falls on Living with an overconfident and lazy pin. Living escapes easily at 1 and Universal frowns and continues the match. He looks to whip Living into the turnbuckle, yet has the move reversed and he himself is sent into the corner. Living charges and leaps looking to take the wind out of his opponent, yet Universal rolls out of the corner and out of danger and Living hits only air and then crashes down upon the turnbuckle. With Living clutching his ribs, Universal slaps him stiffly across the face. Living turns back rabidly and looks ready to pounce, yet Universal takes the air out of Living's attack and gouges his eyes. Grabbing Living's head, Universal proceeds to smash his head into the turnbuckle. Taking Living into the center of the ring, Universal executes a somewhat weak belly-to-belly suplex on Living. He covers him up, once more with a lackadaisical pin that Living is able to turn a shoulder on at 2. An irritated Universal hops to his feet and begins to berate the referee, urging him to count faster. He turns, but once more finds Living on his feet and feels as both of Living's hands clasp his neck and then lift him upwards, straining Universal's neck. Living continues to choke an elevated Universal, but Universal reaches an arm out and gouges Living's eyes once more. Living drops him and Universal stumbles backwards looking to regain the breath that Living had squeezed out of him. As a light-headed Universal turns, Living comes from behind and rolls him up with a schoolboy maneuver. Universal attempts desperately to escape the pin, yet Living uses his size advantage to keep the wrestler down, and once the referee brings is hand down to the mat for the third time, the bell sounds, and Living picks up a shocking upset victory. He slips away and under the rope, and leaves, showing no signs of any emotions save madness. Universal just remains on his knees, mouth open however for once with nothing to say. Not flexing a muscle, Universal watches as a man he had anticipated walking over, disappears backstage. He only looks on in shock. Result: Living by Pinfall Return of the storm Rain stood backstage, getting ready for the next match where he would have to defend his Internet title. To Rain everything seemed very silent... the cycling noise and insanity of other worlds had left him, and he felt... very alone. Rain wrapped the straps around his hands, and looked at the curtain, ready to go out. Ice's music began to play, and Rain turned to see the big man, his opponent walking up behind him. Ice stopped and looked at Rain, sneering at the champion. "You crazy bastard... you thought you had everyone fooled didn't you? Well know sucker... it's payback time. I'm going to take your belt... and then I'm going to take a little bit of you. I owe you for all that shit you did... and now it's time to collect." Ice walked through the curtain, and Rain watched him go silently. Outside storm clouds sounded, and Rain looked skyward. A thin smile passed over his face like water, and then he turned his gaze to the curtain. It was a new year, and things are new for the Internet champion... but some things never change. Rain strapped the Internet title around his waist and flexed his arms. Time to prove what he was made of, all over again. Rain (c) vs. Ice The unmistakable sound of "Crazy Train," by Ozzy Osbourne, resounds throughout the New Haven Coliseum as the mammoth frame of Ice takes shape on the ramp. He begins his walk down to the ring and slowly climbs to the apron and then ducks under the second rope. Adjusting his arm-pads he turns to await his opponent as "Symphony of Destruction" begins to pour out of the arena speakers. The equally as intimidating Internet champion Rain emerges. Unhurried, he deliberately and methodically grips Ice with an ominous glare. Circling the ring he hands off his Internet title to the official and begins to mount the ring steps before finally entering the ring opposite the challenger. The bell is rung and the match underway as both men begin to circle one another and than lunging forward clash in the center of the ring where they lock up face to face. Rain uses leverage from his 7'2" self to weaken Ice and bring him to his knees. He wraps his immense arms around the waist of Ice yet is taken aback with an elbow to the chin. Ice shoots off the ropes and spins with an elbow into the skull of Rain. Whipping the champion into the ropes he looks to telegraph a back body drop, but a quick reacting Rain foils his plans and plants him with a swinging Neckbreaker maneuver. Rain drops on top of the fallen Ice, yet the referee only gets as far as 1 before Ice kicks out. Rain follows up with a flood of stomps to the neck and head of Ice before rolling him over and bringing him to his feet. He lifts him and slams him down before bouncing off the rope and looking for an elbow; Ice is able to roll away as Rain finds only mat. Ice looks to keep Rain on the mat with authoritative hammerlocks to the lower back area. Lifting him up from behind by his hair, Ice uses all his strength to lift all 360 pounds of Rain and bring him down with an Atomic Drop. As Rain arches backwards reaching for his back he falls forward into the ropes and on the bounce back is met by a back drop from Ice that has him laid out. Rolling away and under the bottom rope, Rain takes a second to recover as Ice calls for him to return. Rain reaches under the bottom rope, and clasping Ice's ankle, takes his right leg out from under him and drags him to the outside. After a series of stiff punches, Rain sends Ice into the ring steps. Rain executes a fireman's carry and then lifting Ice quickly applies an arm-wrench. Ice reverses this move into an arm-wrench of his own and then connects it with a hip toss that brings Rain down hard on the concrete. Rolling him back in, Ice follows and after a flurry of stomps and kicks, lifts him and sandwiches him between himself and the turnbuckle with a bear hug, using the ropes for leverage. With his force crushing the ribs and chest area of Rain, Ice backs off and puts the sole of his leather boot right onto Rain's neck and applies pressure. He lets go and Rain stumbles forward only to be caught by Ice and whipped forward. At the last second he reverses and in turn sends Ice headlong into the turnbuckle. Ice takes a few steps forward after colliding with the turnbuckle but is knocked to the ground by a clothesline from Rain. Dropping to the mat, Rain wraps up his opponent, yet once more Ice escapes victory and is able to turn the tables and roll Rain up. Rain kicks out at 2, and both men take a moment to gather themselves. As they both stand once more and Ice doubles over Rain with an uppercut to the abdomen, the crowd's attention turns elsewhere and a chorus of boos begin as the man formerly known as Scott Slugger, Perfect, briskly approaches the ring with a steel chair in one hand. Sliding the chair and than himself under, Slugger shoves Ice aside and through the ropes to the floor. As Rain lifts his head, Perfect molds the chair over Rain's head with a crackling shot that sends Rain from a standing position to his back in one instant. Sluggers raises the chair high and turns to the enraged crowd as the referee signals for the bell, thus awarding the match to Rain by DQ. An irate Ice dives back in yet Perfect keeps his attack at bay with stomps to Ice's vulnerable back. He continues this even as the referee looks to stop him. Had Rain not risen to his feet, it might have gotten uglier. Yet Rain and Ice both put differences aside and head in pursuit of Perfect, chasing him backstage as Slaughter goes to break. Result: Rain by DQ Attacked David Spencer walked into the arena next to Poison. He was surrounded by hired security guards, and ask he walked through the hallways of the arena it was clear as to why. fWo wrestlers that saw the pair passing lunged at the group, cursing, spitting and throwing wild punches in an attempt to get at Spencer. The security team surrounding Spencer did their job, and Spencer laughed at the wrestlers who attempted to attack him. They escorted him to a dressing room, and opened the door for Spencer and Poison. The men lined up outside the door, and wrestlers passing by looked angrily at the room. Inside the locker room David Spencer sat down on a bench, and pointed to the door. "You... girl... get out of here... I would be by myself." Poison looked at the door with alarm, and then back at Spencer. "But... but David... they will hurt me out there..." Spencer laughed, "I don't care... get out! Out!" Poison went, and Spencer looked around the quiet room with satisfaction. He never saw the vent above him open, and feet dangle out, and then drop to the ground. He was seized from behind by Living, Heaven and Hell as Austin Davis descended from the vent and looked into the face of David Spencer. Spencer growled at Davis, and he laughed. "You pathetic old man... you had your day, and you blew it. Now I'm taking you out of the game... for good." Davis swung with a hard fist, and the sound of it cracked out inside the small room. Spencer was gagged before he could scream, and the beating continued. Matt Douglas vs. Eron "Higher" by Creed begins to play, and making his debut in his new persona, Matt Douglas walks out to a fairly loud ovation from the crowd, remembering his days as Wolf Fang. He rolls into the ring and raises his arms to the crowd, being cheered. The cheers turn to boos as Eron's music begins to play, and the long-time International champ walks out, reveling in the crowd's hatred. The announcers make note of the match's non-title status as the bell rings, and Matt Douglas quickly takes down Eron with a dropkick. He hits another, and whips Eron into the ropes. He catches Eron with a leaping leg lariat, taking him down. Douglas drops a quick leg, but before he can cover, Eron rolls to the outside to regain his senses. As he pauses to clear his head, Douglas runs into the ropes and launches himself over with a somersault plancha, taking out Eron. Douglas takes a moment to bask in the cheers of the crowd, then rolls Eron back into the ring. Douglas covers, but Eron kicks out at two. Douglas brings Eron to his feet, and whips him into the ropes. Eron leapfrogs over Douglas as he comes off, and as Douglas turns around, hooks him for a DDT. However, Douglas counters by leaping and raising his body vertically in the air, before falling back and letting his momentum take him into a northern lights suplex. Eron kicks out at two, and quickly gets up and charges at Douglas. Douglas sidesteps and shoves Eron off the ropes, but as Eron comes off, he leaps backwards and wraps his legs around Douglas, going for a forward roll. However, Douglas holds on and tosses him backwards in an ocean cyclone suplex, and rolls through into a pinning position. Eron kicks out at two, and gets up quickly again, but is caught by Douglas with an armdrag, then another. Finally Douglas goes for a rana, but Eron steps back and Douglas falls to the mat. Eron immediately goes to apply the deroguer boston crab, but Douglas reaches forward and flips him into a pinning position. Eron just manages to kick out at two, but flattens Douglas as he gets up with a clothesline. Eron drops a quick elbow, and brings Douglas up. He whips Douglas into the ropes, and slaps on a sleeper as Douglas comes off. He then drops down into a sitting position, nearly snapping the neck of Douglas. Eron quickly rolls over into a cover, but Douglas kicks out at two. Eron brings him up, and hooks him up in a russian legsweep. He rolls into a cover again, but Douglas kicks out.
