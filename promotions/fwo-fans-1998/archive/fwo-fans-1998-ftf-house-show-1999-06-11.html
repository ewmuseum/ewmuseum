fWo — Card

FTF House Show, 11 June 1999

11 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Smoke wafted slowly up from the hard street. Light rain spattered the ground, and over the dark and cold pavement floated even darker storm clouds. It had been lightly raining all day in fact, and the city had an unusual calm. The steel mills looked like large sentries standing a silent watch over their charges, the city proper. All of this was unimportant however and Geoff Green, fWo head cameraman, was sternly reprimanded for filming the bloody scenery when there was a big fWo show in town. The lines of fans stretched out of the arena and scalpers walked up and down the mass of humanity attempting to make an additional buck or two at the expense of Ticketmaster. Behind the arena limousines, rental cars, and bicycles arrived at the arena, with each carrying different fWo wrestlers... depending on their status in the company. As bag boys and security personnel helped the wrestlers to their locker rooms, a large UPS truck pulled into the wrestler's parking lot and a large crate was unloaded and dropped on the ground. Mr. Willis, fWo security head signed for the box, and looked curiously at it before wheeling it inside. The box made a strange ticking sound, and Mr. Willis, wanting to protect the contents from the rain, wheeled the box into the arena and onto the entrance ramp for safe keeping. As he left the arena, Stone came out onto the entrance ramp wielding a baseball bat and flanked by the Dark Riders. The fWo Womens title hung over her shoulder, and she demanded that the Women's Battle Royal match happen right NOW. With Chris Finn and James Armstrong behind the announcing table, the bell sounded at the night officially got underway. Fyre, Nikki the Valley Girl, Wynd, Sid Snow, VL, Briana, Apocolyptica, Aimee Horihito, Melissa Smalls Stone and the Dark Riders stood near to the ring and watched the match introductions unfold. As James Armstrong slowly began to cycle up into a state of sexual frenzy, women began to march down the entrance ramp and head to the ring. Briana came out first, dressed in well-fitting black tights with her hair tied back behind her. She wore a pair of leather gloves, and high shootfighting boots. A simple leather jacket was worn as well, with her name spelled out in metal barbs on the back. Briana climbed to the top turnbuckle and saluted the crowd, who responded with enthusiastic cheers. Next came Apocolyptica and Melissa Smalls, walking down the entrance ramp in unison and showing solidarity for their tag team unit. Nikki wandered down next, looking dazed and confused and wearing a Primetime t-shirt, which appeared to be on backwards. As she walked to the ring, Joy intercepted her, handing her pamphlets about abstaining from lovemaking before marriage. Nikki looked at him with an expression of confusion, and then broke out into peels of laughter, calling him a dork and encouraging the crowd to chant the same. Faced with this humiliation, Joy waved his fist and retreated to the backstage area. VL came to the ring next, dragging behind her a barrel of weapons, and what appeared to be one dead monkey. Wynd came to the ring quietly, looking meek and without a place now that her team the bWo has dissolved utterly. Aimee Horihito came out onto the entrance ramp but before she could make her way to the ring proper, Waru and two large Japanese businessmen approached her. The trio pointed to the ring and observers nearby could overhear them speaking in Japanese, offering Aimee some sort of deal. They came to some form of agreement, and the four walked backstage, ignoring the match at hand. Fyre came out onto the ramp next, and strolled to the ring with a cocky expression on her face which matched her new attitude in and out of the ring. With all of the women in the ring the match was about to begin... but the lights dimmed and pink strobes began to flash and all women turned to see who was coming out onto the entrance ramp. Dressed in a tight thong and a large feather boa, Sid Snow sauntered out onto the entrance ramp, and began to do his version of "the grind" to the stunned audience. Annoyed with this turn of events, VL grabbed a steel chair from ringside and ran up the ramp. VL swung and connected with Sid's head, sending him down to the ramp with a dull thud. The crowd chanted for more, and VL obliged, decking Sid a few more times for good measure while Chris Finn reminded the viewers at home that VL's actions were in no way representative of the fWo's official policy on alternative lifestyles. During this, VL hammered away while the battle royal officially began inside the ring. Smalls and Apocolyptica worked together on Wynd, who fell easily from the ring with the double attack. Fyre attacked Briana from behind with high swinging punches to the back of the head, sending her into the ropes. Fyre quickly grabbed hold of the leg of Briana and attempted to flip her out and over, but Briana hooked an arm and managed to hold on. VL, through with Sid, began to walk back to the ring, but then caught sight of Stevie Roberts at the top of the entrance ramp and quickly chased after him, all thoughts of the match gone. Inside the ring Apocolyptica was working on Nikki, and had her nearly out and over, until Fyre rushed from behind and scooped them both up and over the top rope. Apocolyptica was caught by surprise and tumbled over, but Nikki saw the rush coming and slid under the bottom rope without going outside. Nikki smacked backward with a bitch slap, catching Fyre in the center of the ring and knocking her to the mat. Nikki continued to pummel Fyre as Briana and Melissa traded hard blows in the opposite corner. Briana got the upper hand with a spinning heel kick, and tossed Smalls into the corner for repeated punches. Working together, Nikki swung Fyre and Briana swung Smalls toward the center of the ring where the two collided. Briana picked up Smalls and tossed her from the ring as Nikki did the same with Fyre. With only two left in the ring, Briana and Nikki squared off as Plague climbed onto the ring apron. Handing Plague a chair, Stone pointed to Briana, telling him to make certain that Nikki won the match so she would have an easy victory. Plague waited, and swung the chair as the girls neared, but accidentally hit the wrong girl, and Nikki toppled into the ropes. Briana delivered a hard lariat, sending her from the ring. Distressed by his mistake Plague started to apologize to Stone, who only looked grimly at him and pointed to Death. Looking fearful, Plague moved near to Death, who grabbed him by the neck and drug him to the backstage area. Officials arrived at ringside and pointed to Stone, telling her it was time to get into the ring to face Briana. Stone balked, telling the officials that she was in a good mood today, and would give Briana one week to recover. The two argued about this, until Stone grabbed her title and escaped into the crowd, telling Briana that she had better watch her back if she wanted to survive until next week. As the chaos around ringside subsided, "I Touch Myself" by Divinyls began to blare out over the PA system... Result: Briana Falcon vs. Stevie Roberts As rainbow colored strobes began to flash around the arena Stevie Roberts appeared on the entrance ramp dressed in a very tight pair of British colored shorts. Roberts danced his way to the ring, asking women as he passed if he made them "randy". Many of the ladies laughed at the concept of shagging with the swinging leader of Doomsday, but swooned as Roberts thrust his crotch and buttocks at them. As Roberts continued to work his mojo, the Falcon's music began to play, and the Falcon accompanied by Chris Kidman came slowly to the ring, looking with distaste at the display Stevie Roberts was putting out. The Falcon took a look around the arena for signs of the other Doomsday members, pausing briefly in his scanning of the stadium to look at the large crate sitting atop the entrance ramp. The Falcon continued to the ring, grabbing a camera from a cameraman as he went and dispatching Kidman to see to the crate. The Falcon walked up to the gyrating Roberts and broke the camera over his head. Shards of glass sprayed the ringside area as Roberts collapsed in a heap, bloody and broken. The Falcon tossed the remains of the camera to the side and sent Roberts rolling inside the ring. The Falcon followed, and methodically paced around him stopping every few moments to kick the downed wrestler in the ribs. Kidman meanwhile had gone back up to the top of the entrance ramp, and began to pry at the crate in an effort to discover it's contents. With the noise he was making in an effort to discover what it contained he failed to hear approaching footsteps. From behind came Judge Steel, the Surgeon and Samuel Smooth, and the three made quick work of the distracted Kidman. Binding him with chains, the three slowly made their way to the ring where the Falcon was making short work of their leader. The Falcon was having no trouble with Roberts, and had locked him in a figure four in the center of the ring. Roberts was struggling to hold on when the other members of Doomsday climbed up onto the entrance ramp and called out to the Falcon. Seeing them there, the Falcon pushed aside the referee and took a hard punch at Judge Steel, downing him. The Surgeon tossed a rope around the Falcon's neck while Samuel Smooth attacked his face. The Falcon struggled to take down both men and finally succeeded in tossing the Surgeon backward onto the steel barrier. He then attacked Smooth with a head butt, taking down this man as well. But in this distraction Stevie Roberts had climbed to his feet and launched himself at his opponent with a flying dropkick. The Falcon crumpled, and Roberts stomped down, taking advantage of the distraction to it's fullest potential. Roberts continued his attack, when suddenly the crowd erupted with noise. VL appeared and ran down the entrance ramp, wielding a bat and heading for Roberts. She slid into the ring and threatened Roberts with the bat, while helping the Falcon to get to his feet. The Falcon did so, and thanked VL for her assistance. His thanks was short lived however, as VL turned and decked the Falcon with the bat. The Falcon fell, and VL bid Roberts to cover him so the match could be over. The cover made, Roberts and VL left the ring, arm in arm and laughing at the destruction they have wrought with the rest of Doomsday following closely behind. Turning back briefly to the Falcon, Roberts gestured at VL and the rest of Doomsday, and laughed at the Falcon... telling him that he would never be able to beat all of them with just one boy by his side. At this the Falcon grinned a thin smile and watched the group leave, hatred burning in his eyes. Officals gathered to escort him from the ring, as the music of the Black Panther began to play... Result: Stevie Roberts by Pinfall Chris Universal (c) vs. Black Panther The Panther walked down to the ring quickly, looking from side to side and slapping the hand of the occasional Panther fan as he went. He flipped himself over the top rope and into the ring, looking silently around the arena and pacing the ring. His eyes narrowed as the music of Chris Universal played, and the man himself ventured out onto the entrance ramp. He was followed by Megan Dawn, who held up the Internet Title high above her head for the crowd. Universal sneered at the Panther, and paced the outside of the ring, not wanting to climb inside. The Panther waited quietly, as Universal protested having to enter the ring and face an opponent he considered a lesser man. Tiring of this delay, the Panther ran against the ropes and dove to the outside, but it was just the moment Universal was waiting for, and he stepped to the side and shoved the flying Panther as he went, sending him crashing into the steel. Laughing at the trick, Universal picked up the bell at ringside and cracked it over the head of the Panther, laughing again at the loud sound it made. As the ref pleaded with Universal to get into the ring, he finally rolled the Panther inside and climbed in himself. Universal methodically dropped elbow after elbow onto the helpless Panther, and then applied an ankle lock submission hold. Universal refused to release the hold when the Panther grasped the ropes, and the ref finally had to pry him off himself. Universal backed the ref into the corner protesting the action, and then dismissed him. Universal climbed to the second rope and came off with a flying leg drop, doing further damage to the challenger. Universal rolled the man up, but could not get a three count. Angry at this Universal rolled to the outside and stomped around the ring, kicking at chairs and the steel steps. He got into an argument with a fan at ringside, and spit in the fan's face, telling him that he could buy and sell him fifty times over. This momentary distraction allowed the Panther to get to his feet, but Universal saw the revival taking place and quickly climbed in to begin the attack once more. Universal hammered down, and brought the Panther up in a piledriver... and dropped him to the mat. Universal laughed, and made a loose cover... but this proved to be his undoing. The Panther, who had more strength than he let on, reversed the cover quickly and rolled Universal up in an inside cradle. Universal struggled, but kicked out a millisecond too late, and the Panther was declared the victor. The Panther rolled out of the ring, holding his ribs and the Internet Title, a thin smile behind his masked face. Universal's demeanor was far less pleasant, as he grabbed the referee and threw him from the ring in disgust. Universal shouted out at the arena that this wasn't the last they had seen of him tonight... Result: Black Panther by Pinfall Mad Trucker vs. Darkness Mick Foley, dressed in his referee Mankind garb walked to the ring and waved to the crowd, thrusting a man clad in Socko up at them. As he got into the ring, the arena was pitched into darkness and fans began to scream in terror. A massive man appeared on the entrance ramp, and walked to the ring with purpose, bringing with him fear and despair. He climbed into the ring and cast his gaze over the crowd, who froze within his stare. Darkness himself, the living embodiment of the night watched as fWo officials removed the ring ropes and replaced them with deadly barb wire... finally bidding the Mad Trucker come. With that, a loud TOOOOOOOOOOOOOT was heard, and the lights in the arena rose. The Trucker walked out onto the entrance ramp, and waved to the crowd, who responded with loud cheers of approval. The Trucker walked confidently to the ring, stepping inside and not backing down from the huge form of Darkness. The Trucker looked around at the barb wire strung around the ring, and wiped his forehead with dismay, looking nervous. Darkness had no qualms and quickly locked up with the Trucker, who momentarily was taken off guard. The Trucker backed nearly into the wire, but then dug his heels into the mat and struggled back. The two continued in this fashion, locked in a deadly grasp with both inches away from severe pain and destruction. The Trucker looked for an edge, finally getting the better of Darkness and backing him nearly into the corner turnbuckle. Darkness kicked out then, catching the Trucker in the groin and causing him to lose his grip. Darkness laughed an evil laugh of victory, and grabbed the Trucker by the neck, casting him into the barb wire. Darkness ground his head into the barbs, bloodying his face and weakening the large man. The Trucker howled in pain, as Darkness backed up and charged, driving the Trucker harder into the steel barbs. The Trucker blindly lasted out, getting lucky with a punch and driving Darkness back. The Trucker charged the large man, but he stepped aside and cast him into the corner made of steel and barb wire. Darkness paced slowly, methodically attacking with blows to the face. The Trucker's face was bloody, blinding him and taking his ability away as Darkness continued his assault. Darkness grabbed at the barb wire, taking a handful in his glove and again grinding it into the flesh of the Trucker. Skin and even the hard leather of Darkness' glove tore and frayed as the attack continued, until finally a blind and desperate Mad Trucker screamed out to the crowd, "Remember kids... Mad Trucker says don't make Mad Trucker bleed!" With that the Trucker let out a massive bellow and began to pick Darkness up, finally getting the big man over his head. The Trucker then dropped him on the wire, slicing into his midsection and ripping his costume to ribbons. Blood poured from Darkness as he struggled to free himself from the wire, but found it difficult as the wire had burrowed into his skin. Darkness finally gave up, and setting himself on his feet on the outside of the ring bid the Trucker to come on. The Trucker obliged, diving from the ring and landing on the barb wire covered form of Darkness. The two became stuck together as they traded blows on the outside, Cactus Jack making a slow count. Blood poured from them as they battled, and Foley got to ten and declared the match a no contest. The combatants did not care, and their lust of battle continued up the ramp, leaving a dark, bloody trail as they went. Finally out of sight, sounds of the battle were heard even from far, and wrestlers and officials alike struggled to get out of the way of the battle. Walking carefully through the mess of wire and blood, Mick Foley exited the ring and began to walk up the entrance ramp. He was met at the top by Chris Universal, who walked over to him and began to pressure him about something as the two walked backstage. Result: No Contest Al Snow (c) vs. Black Quicksilver Wielding Stump and the WWF European title, Black Quicksilver came to the ring waving to the crowd. Black Quicksilver got into the ring and sat still in the corner of the ring, waiting for his opponent. Al Snow came out next, trotting quickly to the ring wearing the Cruiserweight title and with Head tucked solidly under an arm. He slid into the ring quickly, and leapt upon the Black Quicksilver, hammering away with Head. The title was cast aside as Al Snow went about his work, striking quickly and driving Black Quicksilver from the ring. Quicksilver ran around the ring to avoid Snow, who chased after him. Quicksilver leaned against the metal guard barrier and kicked up as Snow came forward, catching him in the face. Snow wiped a thin ribbon of blood away from his mouth, and came after Black Quicksilver as well. Black Quicksilver punched out, but Snow grabbed his arms and drove his head into Black Quicksilver repeatedly, and then cast him inside the ring. Snow climbed in after, but the Quicksilver rebounded off the ropes and attacked with a baseball slide into his face. Snow was hurled backward against the metal barrier, and Black Quicksilver dove from the ring with a flying plancha. It landed solidly, and Snow's head was cast hard against the steel. Quicksilver rolled him into the ring and attempted a cover, but Snow kicked free. Quicksilver climbed to the top turnbuckle and signaled to the crowd that he was about to executed a 450 splash. He dove, but Snow rolled aside and Quicksilver met the mat hard, wounding himself. Snow stood on shaky feet, looking around at the crowd and grabbing Quicksilver by the neck. He looked around himself once more, and grabbing Quicksilver firmly by the head signaled for the snow plow. But as he began to executed it the ring apron rustled, and the Chameleon slid out from under the ring. At the same moment the Total Eclipse emerged on the entrance ramp, bellowing at the referee and Al Snow. With the two distracted by this large arrival, the Chameleon snuck up behind Snow and delivered a firm blow to the head of the champion. The damage done, the Chameleon left the ring watching Black Quicksilver extend a tired arm over the chest of the downed Snow. The referee turned and quickly made the three count, declaring Black Quicksilver the victor. Smiling, the Chameleon walked up the entrance ramp to meet the Total Eclipse, looking over his shoulder as he walked. Black Quicksilver caught sight of the pair and looked up at them confused, as the Chameleon pointed and called out, "You owe your title to us Quicksilver... remember that." The Black Quicksilver looked on troubled and confused as officials tended to Snow. Finally leaping from the ring, Black Quicksilver gathered his two titles and Stump and walked backstage, passing Ice and Scott Slugger as he passed. Result: Black Quicksilver by Pinfall French Foundation (c) vs. Scott Slugger & Ice The crowd came to their feet as Ice and Scott Slugger made their way out onto the entrance ramp. Slugger posed, flexing and sneering at the crowd while Ice stood behind him a giant shadow. The two walked to the ring quickly, not stopping to slap the outstretched hands of the fans who cheered them. Scott Slugger had the Hardcore title draped over his shoulder, and held it up for the cameras, raising two fingers and pointing to Ice and himself. The music of the French Foundation began to play, and Mitch Wilson along with Primetime and Nikki came to the ring, titles on their shoulders. The two looked confidant as they walked to the ring, and Primetime looked pleased as he groped Nikki all the way to the ring. Handing her his title, he stepped into the ring with Wilson and at once began badmouthing Scott Slugger, finally slapping him in the head and laughing as he stumbled backward. Slugger responded by charging and taking Primetime off his feet with a tackle, starting the match proper. Primetime got to his feet and ducked a clothesline, picking Slugger up as he came back and slamming him to the mat. Primetime dropped a knee, but Slugger rolled and swatted backward with a chop to the head. Slugger followed it up with a back body drop, staying on the attack with a series of suplexes and finally a European twisting arm drag. Shouting that he had no right adopting his stupid American mind to a European move, Mitch Wilson entered the ring and broke the pinfall that Scott Slugger was attempting. Slugger slowly backed into his corner and made the tag to Ice, who came in to face the much smaller Mitch Wilson. Ice grabbed for Wilson's neck, but he ducked quick under and took the large man down with a drop toe hold. Wilson then grabbed the arm and began to work it over with quick chops to the shoulder and the solar plexus. Wilson turned sideways, and began to thump Ice in the head with his boot, until the ref warned him to cease. Wilson stepped back and went to the second rope, hopping off and dropping the elbow into the back of Ice's head. Ice shrugged this off and grabbed the leg of Wilson as he began to rush away, causing him to drop face-first to the mat. Ice picked him up from the back of the head and slammed him down face first to the mat. Ice then hoisted the smaller man up, and swiveled him around for a piledriver. Before Ice executed it he thought better of the move, and sent his body into a backward momentum position for a sitting piledriver rather than the much more dangerous one that might cause injury rather than simple pain. Ice covered the man, but Primetime rushed into the ring to break the cover. Primetime was tagged in, and dropped Ice with a quick boot to the head, and followed it up with a European uppercut. This sent Ice staggering back, and Primetime continued his attack but failed to notice the blind tag out to Scott Slugger. Slugger entered the ring with both fists swinging, catching Primetime off guard and sending him reeling. Primetime struggled to his corner to make the tag to Wilson, who entered the ring and rushed Slugger. Slugger ducked under and hit a DDT, and quickly went for the cover. But as he knelt down Wilson grabbed the neck and leg and rolled him up in a quick reversal. Wilson was only able to get a two count however, and Slugger powered out. Slugger ducked a lariat and attacked with a kick and an inverted neck breaker, and covered Wilson... putting both feet on the ropes for added leverage. Despite Primetime's objections, the ref made the count and declared Ice and Scott Slugger the new fWo Tag Team champions. Raising their arms in victory, Primetime, Nikki and Wilson slowly walked back up the ramp... only to be met by Joy. Joy wagged a finger at Primetime and Nikki, telling them that none of this would have happened if they had only waited until marriage before engaging in PDA's... Public Displays of Affection. Annoyed, Primetime chased Joy back up the entrance ramp with Nikki and Wilson following close behind. They passed the Flying Frenchie, who was on his way to the ring for his next match... Result: Ice and Scott Slugger by Pinfall Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Death The Flying Frenchie's name was called, but nobody appeared on the entrance ramp. After a few moments, his name was called again, and the Frenchie walked to the ring quickly, slapping hands with the fans and wearing the International Title around his waist. He raised his arms up for the crowd, and shook his head at them as the cheers came. The lights dimmed then, and the Dark Riders came out onto the entrance ramp. Death came first, and after looking down at the Frenchie sent the other Dark Riders to the back, telling them that he wanted to play fair and didn't need their help. This confused the Dark Riders, but they did as their leader asked and returned backstage leaving Death to walk to the ring alone. With both men in the ring, the two paced each other, and locked up... but there was a sudden shout of "STOP" over the arena speakers. The Silver Fox appeared on the entrance ramp holding a microphone, and came quickly down to the ring. The Silver Fox asked the two men if they planned to do all their fighting by themselves, and if they wouldn't mind sharing their fun with himself. The Silver Fox told them that it would certainly be a shame to have to sully his hands by dealing with the two ruffians, but that he would do it all 'for the fans'. With that he swept his arms out to the crowd, who responded by booing him loudly. Death and the Frenchie agreed to this, and welcomed the Silver Fox into the ring by holding the ropes. With all three men inside the ring the bell was rung, and the Silver Fox looked from one side to the other, trying to determine which he should attack first. Finally, he walked up to Death and delivered a soft chop. Death was devastated, and fell backward in agony. Turning his attention to the Frenchie, Silver Fox looked for an opening, and finally simply blew in his general direction. This felled the champion, and Silver Fox drug his body on top of Death's. With one pinky, Silver Fox pinned both men, and was declared the victor by the confused referee. Silver Fox held the title above his head as Fyre came out to congratulate him, and the crowd sat in stunned silence. Silver Fox continued to pose for the fans, when suddenly a loud explosion was heard from the backstage area. Smoke billowed out from the entrance area, and Death, dragging chains behind him walked out onto the entrance ramp looking furious. He walked to the ring as the crowd began to stir from the other end of the arena, and the Flying Frenchie came into view, handcuffed and ripping at his bonds. The two came at the ring from opposite ends, and Silver Fox quickly jumped from the ring and made a quick getaway through the crowd along with whoever the fake "Death" and "Flying Frenchie" were. He left the International title behind, and when Death got to the ring he quickly picked it up and slung it over his shoulder. The Frenchie got into the ring as well, and ripped it off Death's shoulder, taking it for himself. Death turned on the Frenchie, and the two began trading blows in the ring as security stormed the area. It took several minutes to clear the chaos in the ring, and somewhere high above the arena the laughter of Silver Fox was heard... where he stood with Fyre, the Chameleon, the Total Eclipse, and the two masked men... Result: No Contest Ultra Violet (c) vs. Rudo Sirens began to wail as the main event was announced. The announcer introduced the challenger first, and Rudo walked onto the entrance ramp wearing a long black trench coat. Rudo was flanked by the Shocker, Sid Snow, and Billy James, the later of which was wearing a new suit and smoked a cigar. Rudo stopped his men as they started to walk down the entrance ramp, and told them to go back to the backstage area because he had all the help he needed on his feet and under his coat. Puzzled by this cryptic statement, the three walked back to the locker rooms shaking their heads and mumbling small words about how with Machina gone the Lone Gunmen had a fool for a leader. Rudo got to the ring and asked for a microphone and a folding chair, and when given both items sat down and waited for the champion. Purple strobes pulsed as Rage by Savatage blared over the speakers and Ultra Violet ran out onto the entrance ramp. Ultra Violet was flanked by Malcom and Violet, and held the World Title up, backward, for the crowds. They cheered him and the back of his title, and a thousand flash bulbs burst as excited fans took photos of the champion. The sudden lights surprised Ultra Violet and he fell backward, hitting his head on the steel of the ramp. It seemed to have little effect and Ultra Violet popped back up with a grin on his face, and shouted "WHOO YEAH!" to the fans. His excitement was quickly halted as Rudo began to talk, and said that he was going to offer him a special "one night only" deal. Putting on his best thinking face, Ultra Violet looked on intensely as Rudo continued to talk. Rudo pointed up to the large crate, and told Ultra Violet to look it over. Ultra Violet did so, and he and Violet both became absorbed with the large crate as slack-jawed expressions overtook their faces. Rudo told Ultra Violet that they could fight... but that wrestling was overdone these days and he had a better idea. He would offer Ultra Violet a trade... his fWo World Title for whatever was inside the huge box. This caused a stir amongst the crowd, and on the entrance ramp as Violet quickly hopped up and down and pointed to the box, telling Ultra Violet that something really big and shiny must in there, like a giant engagement ring or a huge diamond proposal ring. Ultra Violet looked unsure at these, and stared down at his hands frowning. Suddenly, a light went on in his eyes and he asked Rudo if the giant crate contained candy. Rudo told him that it certainly might, and Ultra Violet quickly offered him the World Title. Seeing these events unfold with dismay, Malcom quickly snatched the title and grabbed Ultra Violet's arm, telling him to get into the ring and wrestle. Violet meanwhile tugged on his other arm, telling him to take the box filled with bridesmaid dresses and engagement presents. Ultra Violet was tugged in both directions and became transfixed with the shiny entrance ramp as he was fought over. Finally, Malcom gave up and began to sob as Violet pulled Ultra Violet to the large crate. Rudo told Ultra Violet that the deal was made, and that he would take his title for what was in the crate. Ultra Violet waited excitedly as the crate was lifted into the air, revealing... nothing. Ultra Violet frowned at this turn of events, and turned back to Rudo claiming that he had cheated. Rudo told Ultra Violet that cheating him was child's play, but if he wanted a beating he was welcome to one. Ultra Violet then ran to the ring as the bell sounded and slid into the ring to face Rudo. Unfortunately Ultra Violet slid too far, and went out the other side of the ring and tumbled to the floor. Rudo waited while Ultra Violet got into the ring and dusted himself off. Ultra Violet then prepared to fight, but Rudo told Ultra Violet that he had fallen into his trap and would now feel the wraith of his pocket monsters. Ultra Violet gasped in horror and looked at Rudo's waist in dismay. Rudo flipped open his trench coat and grabbed at his waist, bringing out several small red and white balls. Ultra Violet looked confused by this, but Rudo seemed confidant as he hurled the balls at Ultra Violet and shouted "Charmander, Squirtle, Bulbasuar... I choose you!" Ultra Violet stared at the small plastic balls as they bounced off the mat and came to a stop near his feet. They simply sat there. Ultra Violet looked up at Rudo, down at the balls, and then up at Rudo again. Using his foot, Ultra Violet kicked the small plastic balls out of the ring and lunged at the challenger. Ultra Violet caught Rudo by the head, and began to repeatedly slam Rudo into the canvas yelling, "You... shouldn't... lie... about... CANDY!" Rudo scrambled free, and rolled to the outside of the ring. There he came face-to-face with Violet, who grabbed him by the head and slammed him repeatedly into the steel barrier yelling, "You... shouldn't... lie... about... ENGAGEMENT RINGS!" Rudo stumbled away from this new attack, and directly into the path of Malcom. Rudo stumbled into Malcom, causing the manager to trip over and dirty his suit. Righting himself, Malcon looked at the dazed Lone Gunmen, and raised a single eyebrow. He slowly pulled a single pen from his jacket pocket, and wrote out something on a small piece of paper. Speaking softly, Malcom informed Rudo that he had written out a summons and would see him in court for his assault. Rudo vomited in horror and fled up the entrance ramp screaming. fWo officials met him at the top, and informed him that he must continue the match or risk banishment from the fWo, and loss of all his Denny's coupon privileges. Grimly, Rudo walked back to the ring and slowly rolled inside, facing Ultra Violet who was staring out into the crowd mindlessly. Rudo attacked with a drop toe hold, bringing the champion down to the mat with a dull thud. Rudo then got to his feet and jumped up and down on Ultra Violet's back, shouting "WHO'S DA MAN" over and over again. Annoyed with Rudo's lack of proper English, Ultra Violet began to bitch slap the Lone Gunmen, and then picked him up, signaling for a pile driver. Because Ultra Violet was unaware of proper pile driver etiquette and the hurt vs. injure argument, he simply dropped Rudo on his head, knocking the Lone Gunman unconscious. Victorious, Ultra Violet placed a single foot atop the prone body of Rudo for the pinfall victory. Pleased with the win, Ultra Violet walked up the entrance ramp where Cactus Jon was waiting. Walking up to Ultra Violet Cactus Jon tapped him on the shoulder and asked for when the title shot he was promised would occur. Ultra Violet smiled and patted Jon on the back, telling him that he would make sure the match was signed the next time he spoke with the commissioner, and he remembered to bring it up. With this business completed, Fight the Future officially drew to a close as the cameras went off the air... Result: Ultra Violet by Pinfall
