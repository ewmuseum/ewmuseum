Wednesday Warfare, 31 May 2000

31 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ratings, ratings, ratings... Chris Finn stormed into his office. Jeremiah Frankton sat in a chair, awaiting the owner of the fWo so he could go over some last minute things with Finn before the show took to the air. Finn grasped in his right hand a sheet of paper, while in his left hand resided a bottle of water, looking to be half empty... or in some people's opinion, half full. Finn sat down in his chair and sighed. Frankton looked concerned, but didn't know if now was a good time to talk. Finn began the conversation. "Ratings went down a bit again. What the hell is going on?" Frankton shrugged. "Well, there was that Laker/Blazers game, but that was well before Slaughter." Finn nodded. Suddenly, the door opened to Finn' luxorious office. It was James Armstrong. Armstrong was looking quite dapper. "Why are you so happy?" Finn asked. Armstrong said "Because I've figured out why the ratings were low for Warfare & Slaughter!" Finn acted happy. "Oh yeah?" Armstrong nodded. "Yeah! See... Monday... I was on this helicopter tour... and we were near a pylon... we saw the remains of a stork... a piece of it's nest... and a damaged set of insulators. Later that day, ratings came in... and they were lower than expected! So obviously... it was this bird's nest that affected our rating!" Finn' face turned bright red, not in an embarassed fashion, but in a more angry tone. "James... you've gotta be [censored] kidding me! It was obviously... er... what the hell was on tv Sunday night?" Armstrong shrugged. Finn' fist pounded his desk. Frankton sighed. And Warfare began... Six Man Four Corners Match To Determine The Number One Contender vs. vs. vs. Fireworks and pyros shot off as the Marine Midland Arena braced themselves for a sure-to-be humdinger edition of Warfare, just two days after America celebrated their beloved fallen soldiers on Memorial Day. "Paint It Black" by Gob began to play, and out walked Wretch & Scott Hart, two of the six competitors of tonight's six man four corners match to determine the number one contender to the fWo Cruiserweight Title, currently held by Matt Douglas. Hart and Wretch entered the ring, and awaited their other opponents. "We're Not Gonna Take It" by Twisted Sister began to play, and out came the beloved leader of the Stump Squad, Black Quicksilver. He carried Stump with him to the ring, before climbing in and raising Stump to a giant ovation. Wretch looked at Stump curiously, before wondering if Stump had ever had secks before. Hart scratched his head, before posing for the benefit of those with flash photography. "Down With The Ship" by Scatterbrain, whoever the hell they are, replaced the theme from one of the many 80's bands who were nothing more than one hit wonders, and out walked everyone's resident psycho, Mike Heftel. Heftel came out to a favorable reaction, and joined his co-conspirator in crime in the ring. "Switchback" by Biohazard played, and seconds later arrived the up and coming newcomer from New Brunswick, Canada - Crusader - walked out onto the stage to a very large reaction of cheers. He joined the other four men in the ring, and they awaited the sixth and final participant of the match, "The Future" Lance Star. "Guerrilla Radio" by RATM played, and out came the man who has long claimed to be the future of professional wrestling, Lance Star. Star entered the ring, and the referee chose two men to start things off, and it would be Star & Crusader. Wrestling fans would note that it was Star & Crusader who tore the house down just eleven days ago at Redemption, and looked for them to do so tonight. Crusader eyed Star wearily, remembering that Star was the first man who was victorious over Crusader, albeit by a lot of cheating. Crusader and Star locked up, before Crusader grabbed the arm of Star and twisted into an armbar. Star went to escape, but as he did he was met with a superkick to the face. Crusader then whipped Star into the ropes, before somersaulting on the mat up into a clothesline, taking down one third of the Future Foundation. Crusader then bounced off the ropes and charged at Star, taking him down with a forearm. He then tagged out to Black Quicksilver, who flew off the top rope with a missle dropkick. He hit Star square on the jaw, before tagging out to his tag team partner. They whipped Star to the ropes and took him down with a double-elbow. Heftel posed for the crowd, but this only allowed Star to escape. He quickly tagged out to Hart, who wanted no part of it. He tagged out to Wretch. Wretch came in and charged at Heftel, who took him down with a hip toss. Heftel then tagged out to Black Quicksilver, who came in off the top rope again for a high crossbody, but Wretch countered into a powerslam after catching him. Wretch then tagged out to Hart, and Hart would only come in once he saw that BQ was down and out. Hart flew off the top rope with a diving headbutt, before going for a cover. BQ kicked out at two. Hart softened BQ up again with a Death Valley Driver, before going for a Texas Clover Leaf. He had it locked in and it looked as if BQ was going to tap out, but in came Lance Star. Star grabbed Hart and planted him into the mat with the Shooting Star. Star then placed BQ on top of Hart. As he did this, the referee positioned to count, but at the same time Abusah (TMFAOYM) came running down to ringside. The referee reached two, and Abusah pulled Hart underneath the bottom rope onto the floor. BQ stood up and looked at Abusah in shock, and in from behind came Star, who planted BQ with a Shooting Star, before telling BQ "that was for costing me the chance to win Meltdown last year! Hoo-ha!" He then pinned BQ, before standing up and bragging. With one down and five to go, in came Heftel. Heftel looked at Star, before poking him in the eye, and running outside the ring. Star looked at Heftel increduously, and in from behind came Crusader, who rolled Star up for the pin. With two out of the match and four to go, Crusader and Wretch locked up in the center of the ring. Crusader got the early advantage, but Wretch reversed momentum to his side after hitting a Tornado DDT. Wretch then directed Abusah to trip Crusader when he whipped him to the ropes, but any idiot could see that Abusah would screw up. Wretch whipped Crusader to the ropes, but Crusader reversed it. Instead Abusah tripped Wretch because he wasn't looking at the ring, instead he was boasting to the fans about something or the other. Wretch stumbled into the ring, and Crusader scooped him up for a small package. Wretch went outside the ring and argued with Abusah, and tried to make sense of the situation. Hart joined them, but back in the ring, Heftel charged at Crusader and Crusader took him down with a tiltawhirl backbreaker, before signaling for the Gravity 101. He went to the top rope, and flew off, in a move that could not be described by words. He hit it, before he eliminated Heftel. It was now down to Crusader and Hart, and with the referee still on the outside arguing with Wretch, Hart and Abusah, Crusader looked out suspiciously. Abusah walked away from the skirmish as Wretch & Hart argued about whether Abusah had a brain in his head or not. Abusah procured a chair from ringside, before climbing into the ring. He nailed Crusader with it from behind, before exiting. Hart saw his opportunity. He grabbed the referee by the shirt, and directed him into the ring. Hart then locked in The Final Exam. The referee tried to see if Crusader wanted to give up, but Crusader offered no response. He was out. The referee raised his arm three times to make sure, before awarding the match and shot to Hart. Hart was elated as he went out to ringside and met with Abusah, while Wretch walked away from the duo with a angry expression on his face. Warfare went backstage while Hart and TMFAOYM celebrated... Result: Scott Hart by pinfall Don't need 'em, don't want 'em... Eron The Relentless sat near a catering table looking at all the other wrestlers scarf down food and drinks. Eron turned his head slightly, and noticed something out of the corner of his eye: Chris Finn, the owner of the fWo. Eron stood up from his chair and stopped Finn as he tried to walk back, obviously trying to avoid a confrontation at the lunatic resident from Koshyn Falls, whereever the hell that was. Eron angrily demanded an answer from Finn. "What the hell are you doing scheduling me for a tag team title match? I don't have a partner. I don't need a partner. I don't want a partner. What part of don't... don't you understand?!" Finn sighed. "Look, Eron. I'm in charge around here, and while I am, you play by my rules. You did some damage to The Strangler last week on Warfare, and tonight I took care of three birds with one stone. One, this match allows him to gain some revenge on you. Two, I made a main event with an aura of mystery surrounding it... and uncertainty. Nobody knows who your partner will be, not even you. And three... this allows Chronos & Fallen the opportunity to prove themselves worthy against some of the well known stars in the fWo, and prepares them for the match at Critical Condition with Damnation. Now what part of that... don't you understand?" Eron gave Finn a menacing look, before stammering. "Well, all seven of those other assholes in the ring are going to experience one thing tonight. They're going to experience... a breath... of... fresh.. death. And when they're all in the hospital tomrorow, you'll have one person to thank: ME. Me! Not you, not anyone else... me... me... Finn... me..." Eron motioned towards Finn, but Finn quickly walked away as fast as he could, while Eron laughed maniacally. It appeared that Eron was enjoying tormenting the fWo a lot... and sought out the opportunity to do so whenever possible... meanwhile... back in the ring... Mike Bear (c) vs. Courage Result: Courage by pinfall Straight Talk, Hardcore Style. It had been a long time since we had seen this man, since CyberSlam in fact. Back without an explanation of where he had been, but who was going to ask this man where he had been? He was cranky, and had a chip on his shoulder to boot. He stood slumped up against the wall, looking at various wrestlers as they walked by and scoffing in their general direction. One person in particular that walked by was Erik Kelly. Kelly was talking to his manager - Pamela Dee - about a couple of vignettes he had been asked to shoot. As he walked by, Neo scuzzed him off, and gave a disgusted look. Kelly paid no attention, he had other things to concentrate on, but as the camera focused in on the man known only as Neo, he began to speak. "What a buffoon. He enters the fWo and thinks we're supposed to be impressed by his size. Well, we all know size doesn't matter. It's not how big your pencil is, it's how you write your name. I wouldn't be surprised if he couldn't spell his name let alone write it. How the hell did that six foot eleven walking talking pile of crap get in the fWo? And what's up with that ridiculous hair of his? It looks like a bird sat on his head and decided to dump out six days worth of feces. His name is just as ridiculous. The Eradicator? More like the masturbator if you ask me. Despite that woman... if you can call her a woman... of his, I'm sure he hasn't been laid in years. I'm sure he knows what it's like to have a few consecutive lonely nights in hotel rooms with a bunch of Penthouse Letters and a jar of Vaseline. And this is the guy the fWo has as their Hardcore Champion? Erik Kelly likely doesn't know the definition of hardcore... but sooner or later... I'm going to show him what hardcore is all about. And that's your opinion... because I just told it to you." Neo pushed the camera man out of his way as he walked off in another direction, while Warfare ironically remained backstage for another segment... Watching... Erik Kelly sits in his locker room, watching Warfare take place. His large 6 foot, 11 inch frame sits in a comfortable leather sofa...although he's been edgy tonight and walking all over the arena. He strokes his goatee as he carefully studies the events unfolding this evening. There's only one match at the moment he has interest in...it's up next. The Number One Contenders match for the Hardcore Title. Erik excuses himself from Pamela's company, stating he needed to "use the facilities"....although his version is a little different than what he wanted her to know. As he makes his way down to the back area, he distances himself from the entry way to the TravisTron. Seeing the competitors to tonight's Hardcore matchup...Robert Jensen's still quite busted up. Kelly grins at the poor bastard. Frank Snow appears ready to go tonight...no brain, no pain. But then one man catches Erik's eye...Jason Xavier. The man formerly known as "Prophet". He hasn't noticed Kelly yet, as Kelly remains in the shadows, somehow keeping all of his size out of view. Perhaps Xavier is just too busy thinking about his match. He goes out into the ring as his entrance is called...and Erik decides that he'll hang out here and watch how things develop... "Perhaps Mr. Dickhead needs to know how it feels to have a match interrupted...". As Erik watches a monitor of the ring backstage contemplating what to do, the camera segways back to the action of the match. Xavier vs. Robert Jensen vs. Frank Snow "Whatever" by Godsmack blares over the PA system, bringing out Frank Snow. Many fans boo him; however, these same fans have purchased the Frank Snow BROOM OF DOOM (available at www.fwowrestling.com). Snow comes down and pulls out his BROOM OF DOOM, and tosses it up in the air. The fans laugh when he fails to catch it. As he glares and talks trash to the crowd, Soulfly hits the speakers, and Robert Jensen, the former Hardcore Champion, makes his way down toward the ring. He comes to the ring with nothing, unlike his counterpart Snow. The two have an intense staredown as the lights go out, and a blue streak runs across the arena. Out comes Xavier. He looks at the crowd with a nodding satisfaction, then makes his way down to ringside. He slides into the ring, and Snow and Jensen finish their staring contest and go to town on Xavier. Snow takes off out of the ring and goes to his cart. The fans divert their attention away from Jensen's dropkick to Xavier as they want to see what Snow will pull out of his cart. Snow fishes around and then decides on his GODFATHER'S HOE. Snow yells for Jensen to bring Xavier toward the ropes so he can hit him with his GODFATHER'S HOE. Jensen whips Xavier into the ropes, and Snow connects with such force that the HOE breaks in half. One half flies into the crowd, and nails a woman as she was about to flash the crowd. The crowd now boos even harder against Snow and Jensen; they have just been deprived of some very nice looking puppies. Snow fishes back into his cart o' goodies; this time he pulls out the IMAC OF BAD IDEAS. Snow tosses it to Jensen. However, Xavier, who has risen up (the GODFATHER'S HOE didn't have much of an effect), intercepts it and nails Jensen with it. The fans cheer, as it is the first time they have seen this horrible piece of machinery put to good use. Snow fishes around and then puts on a Dominic Hasek jersey. The fans cheer once again. Is this a new Frank Snow? He then pulls out a hockey stick and yells to the crowd "This is the STICK OF A WASHED-UP GOALIE!" The fans boo him like it's their job, and they're not gettin laid-off any time soon. He slides into the ring, and looks at Xavier, who still has the IMAC OF BAD IDEAS with him. Xavier throws the IMAC OF BAD IDEAS at Snow. Snow fends it off with the STICK OF A WASHED-UP GOALIE!, then looks behind Xavier. Xavier turns around, and Snow nails him with the piece of equipment. Snow goes for a cover on Jensen, instead of Xavier. After a 2-count, Jensen kicks out and stares at Snow. Snow, meanwhile, goes for a cover on Xavier. At a count of 2, Jensen hits Snow with the butt end of the STICK OF A WASHED-UP GOALIE. Snow rolls over in pain, and Jensen hops out of the ring. He looks into Snow's cart and pulls out a very unique item: the BUFFALO WING SAUCE FROM HELL. It is only fitting that this would be in the cart, seeing as how this is the place where this delicacy originated. Jensen walks into the ring, and eyes up Snow. Jensen throws the BUFFALO WING SAUCE FROM HELL into Snow's face. It catches him in the eyes, thus burning the bejesus out of Snow. Snow grabs the vat from Jensen and tosses whatever is left of the sauce into his eyes. Jensen shrieks in pain; he didn't know how much this stuff could sting! Xavier, meanwhile, has eyed them both up. He grabs both men by the neck. They had no idea where he is; their faces are flooded with tears from the sauce. He raises them up, and then hits a double chokeslam, otherwise known as Blue Rising. A pin on both men and a three-count later, and Xavier has won his fWo debut. Meanwhile, Erik Kelly makes his way down to ringside, amid massive cheers from the crowd. He climbs into the ring, and he and Xavier have a staredown. Security officials run into the ring (some with napkins for Snow and Jensen), and break up any melee before it gets started. Meanwhile, Warfare heads to a break. Result: Xavier by pinfall Aftermath... Erik Kelly walks back into his locker room after the match. Pamela looks at him with a look on her face. Erik decides to ignore it for now. He did what he had to do...and that was send a message to Xavier. He may be a big man...but he's not THE Big Man around here...and tonight he didn't prove a damn thing. Erik sees the camera, and sends a brief message. "Hey Jason...not bad...if you were facing Sid Snow, that is. You got a long way to go....but since you wanted my attention, you got it, Clone!" Erik motions his arms to have both perpendicular to each other, forming his trademark crosshairs. "Consider yourself targeted...and if I were you, I'd have eyes in the back of your head...Because there's a big red crosshair on your back, and the message says 'Eradicate Me'!...It will happen, and there ain't a daaaaaaaamn thing you can do about it, Bitch!" Erik walks off, directing Pamela towards the door with his gear in tow....apparently interested in making it an early evening in Buffalo. As they depart, a commercial for Critical Condition airs. Primetime vs. Havok Warfare returns to the arena formerly known as the Marine Midland Arena, now known as the HSBC Arena in Buffalo, New York to the sounds of "When Worlds Collide" by Powerman 5000. One of the three participants in the Internet Title match at Critical Condition, Havok, steps out onto the entrance ramp, to a mixed reaction of cheering and booing. He steps into the ring, and suddenly, "Blind" by Korn hits, and Primetime steps out onto the entrance ramp. As the Buffalo drunks come running out of the Headlines sports bar located inside of the arena, Primetime grabs the microphone. "You know, a few years ago, when I came back to this town right after I joined the ill fated French Foundation, I gave this town the name of "Buffalo, France". As we all know, the French Foundation is out, and the future is now. So, seeing as the future is now, I thought, "Why not shed that France name from the great town of Buffalo?" However, then I realized that Buffalo isn't great. Buffalo sucks! Look at this place! The waterfront is horrible! Hamister's so pissed that he can't bring an ABA team here because of financial backing, and I say "good for you, Hamister!". I mean, look what you people did when he brought an arena football team to Buffalo..correct that a *losing* arena football team to Buffalo. They lose, and you leave! You people have no allegiance to anything. Hell, look at your damn Sabres. One year they go to the Stanley Cup finals, the next year, their booted in the first round! And what happens? Attendance plummets! And the worst thing? I thought that the women of France were bad, hell, nothing compares to the cows that you have here! No wonder they say "people don't have secks in Buffalo"! There's only one good looking woman in this whole damn town, and that's MY girlfriend Jenna. Any problems with that? Well, too damn bad! I denounce this town as my hometown, and now for the people in my ex-hometown, I'm going to stroll to that ring, and let you catch one last glimpse of the fWo Internet Champion, as he beats the living hell out of Havok!" Primetime throws the microphone down, and runs to the ring to an enormous amount of booing from the crowd. He slides into the ring, and immediately, Havok meets him with fists to the face. A whip into the ropes by Havok, and PT ducks a clothesline. PT catches him with a boot to the gut, and sets him up early for a Primetime Bomb. However, Havok is able to grab PT by the legs, and slingshot him back into the ropes. PT bounces back, right into a huracanrana by Havok. The ref counts, but PT is able to get out at two. Both men get up, and Havok runs at PT, who proceeds to dump him right over the top rope and onto the floor. With Havok rustiling about near the barricade, PT climbs the turnbuckles, and comes off onto Havok with a plancha. Both men hit hard, and then PT decides to whip Havok into the railing. However, Havok reverses it, and PT goes flying over the barricade and into the stands. As security moves the fans out of the way, Havok runs at PT, and stands on the barricade, he jumps at PT, but somehow, PT gets Havok up and over with a backbody drop, that sends Havok hard to the floor. PT follows after him, and throws Havok over the barricade. This time, it's PT who decides to stand on the barricade, and jump at Havok. However, once again, a reversal is done, and Havok sends Primetime to the floor with a press slam. Havok picks Primetime up by the locks of his hair, and rolls him into the ring. Once Havok gets him in the position that he wants, he begins to climb the turnbuckles. Havok comes off with a frog splash, and he connects. He covers, but Primetime is able to get a shoulder up at two. Havok picks him up, and looks for a slam, but PT shimmies out of it, and grabs him from behind. He nails him with a german suplex. He rolls through with that with another. One more time, and after this suplex, PT pops up, and picks Havok up by the hair. PT nails him with a boot to the gut, and levels him with a Primetime Bomb. However, instead of covering, he lifts Havok by the hair, and looks him straight in the eye saying "You want to be high on the cards? You want to be the Internet Champion? Well, this is what you're going to have to deal with, punk!" He then sets him up for another Primetime Bomb, but from the crowd comes "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson, and Ice. Wilson grabs the Internet Title, as Ice distracts the referee. Wilson climbs into the ring, and nails PT in the back of the head with the title before dropping the belt on him, and spitting on it. He then picks PT up, and levels him with a Headliner. He drags Havok's limp body, and puts an arm of Havok's over Primetime's body, and exits the ring. Ice also moves away from the referee, who is able to record the three count, and Havok picks up the win. Slaughter cuts to commercial, as we see Wilson smiling at what he has done. Ric Chronos & Fallen (c) vs. The Boston Strangler & Scott Slugger vs. Ice & Mitch Wilson vs. Eron & ??? Warfare heads to the ring for the main event of the night, a huge four corners match to determine the fWo World Tag Team Champions. The crowd reacts with loud boos for the duo of Ice and Mitch Wilson, who enter to the ironically titled "Bad Attitude" by Deep Purple. After they reach the ring, "Back In Black" begins to play and Scott Slugger makes his way to the outside of the ring to loud cheers, staring in warily at his two enemies. "O Fortuna" suddenly begins playing and the cheers intensify for the fWo World Heavyweight Champion, the legendary Boston Strangler. The Strangler joins Slugger at ringside, unstraps his title, and the two slide into the ring and begin brawling with Bad Attitude, Strangler with Wilson and Slugger with Ice. They all suddenly stop as "Physical" begins playing and the fWo's most hated man, Eron The Relentless appears at the top of the entrance ramp and, in a rare lack of judgment, rushes the ring. He slides in and gets to his feet, only to be met by the gigantic right hand of Ice, which wraps itself around his neck. Ice lifts Eron up and plants him with an Attitude Adjuster chokeslam. As Eron twitches on the mat, Mitch Wilson drags him up and takes him down with a Headliner. Bad Attitude shuffles off to the side, leaving the carcass of Eron to Slugger and Strangler. Strangler brings Eron up and whips him at Slugger, who takes him down with the Curveball. Slugger brings Eron up and shoves him to Strangler, who immediately locks in the Boston Massacre. As he cinches in the hold, the lights suddenly go out. The crowd buzzes in anticipation, and suddenly a purple glow engulfs the arena. "My Hero" by Foo Fighters begins to play and out walks none other than the Black Panther, making his first appearance in a good long while. The crowd gives the returner a mixed reaction, and both teams back off in the ring as he methodically approaches, wary of what he may plan to do and deciding to play it safe. The Panther rolls into the ring and brings his apparent partner Eron up, before lifting him up and slamming him out of his boots with a pumphandle slam. Satisfied, Panther heads to the back again, and Scott Slugger, ever the opportunist, makes the cover. The ref counts three as "Christian Says" begins to play throughout the arena, and Ric Chronos and Fallen finally make their entrance, tag titles around their waists. The fans cheer, both at the arrival of this fan-favorite team and at the actions of the returning Panther. On his way back, Panther walks astride the tag team champions, and the three men share a wary glance before Panther continues to the back and Chronos and Fallen continue to the ring. Inside the ring, Mitch Wilson and the Boston Strangler have paired off, and from their team, Ric Chronos joins the fray. Wilson swings at the Strangler with a lariat, but the World Champ ducks and Mitch's momentum carries him into Chronos, who spikes him with a DDT. Chronos brings Wilson to his feet and shoves him to the waiting Strangler, who attempts to lock on the Boston Massacre and does so. However, Ice quickly hits the ring and breaks the hold, which draws in Scott Slugger, which draws in Fallen as well. The match quickly degenerates into a clusterfrickish brawl, and somewhere amongst the chaos, Scott Slugger reverses an attempted Attitude Adjuster from Ice with a testicular kick, before grabbing Ice and whipping him straight into his partner Mitch Wilson. Ice strikes Wilson hard, and Wilson stumbles back into the waiting arms of the Boston Strangler, who this time locks on the Massacre. After fighting for a moment, Wilson finally decides he can take no more and signals to the referee that he quits. Strangler releases the hold and kicks Wilson out of the ring, while on the other side of the ring Scott Slugger sits on the turnbuckles and laughs as Chronos and Fallen combine to double suplex the huge Ice over the top rope to the outside, where he lands with a splat. Ice quickly makes his way up and shouts threats, but is forced to head to the back with Mitch Wilson as the referee informs him that he has been eliminated. On that side of the ring, Slugger suddenly leaps off the turnbuckles at Ric Chronos, attempting the Grand Slam. Chronos sees him coming out of the corner of his eye and catches him, planting him with a powerslam. He hooks a leg and the ref's count reaches two, but the Strangler breaks it at the last moment with a boot to the back. The referee finally decides to seperate the two remaining teams, and Slugger and Chronos are designated the legal men, forcing the Strangler and Fallen to their corners. Chronos brings Slugger to his feet and hooks his head for the Chronosphere, but Slugger fights out and drops down into a reverse neckbreaker variation of his own. Slugger drapes an arm across Chronos, but Chronos kicks out at two. Slugger brings Chronos to his feet and kicks him hard, doubling him over. Slugger attempts a rocker dropper, but Chronos moves his head and catches Slugger coming down in a variation of a pumphandle slam. Chronos quickly makes his way to his corner and calls in Fallen, and the duo performs a double snap suplex. In his corner, the Boston Strangler desperately calls for Slugger to tag in, and Slugger appears to hear him, lunging to get to his corner. Fallen attempts to stop him by grabbing him in a sleeper from behind, but Slugger drops down into a jawbreaker before rolling into his corner and making the tag. Strangler rushes the ring and knocks down Fallen with a huge lariat. Ric Chronos attempts a lariat of his own, but Strangler catches his arm and locks in the Boston Massacre. Chronos is close to the ropes, but cannot reach them. As he struggles, Bad Attitude makes their way back, starting an argument outside the ring with Scott Slugger. The referee sees this and slides outside to split them up, never seeing Eron rush the ringside area. He pulls Strangler out and bashes his arm with a lead pipe, before bringing up Strangler and dropping him on his head with a brain buster on the floor. Not satisfied, he grabs the ring steps and drops them on Strangler's arm, before grabbing a chair and hammering down on the steps, driving them onto Strangler's arm. Not seeing any of this, Slugger rolls back towards the ring, where he is grabbed by a recovered Ric Chronos and lifted into the Chronosphere, which he cannot escape this time. Despite his questionable legality, the three count is tolled on Slugger and Chronos and Fallen win the match. They roll from the ring and grab their belts, before heading to the back. From the outside, Bad Attitude strikes like vultures, hitting the ring and attacking a wounded Slugger. Outside the ring, Eron pulls the Strangler up and, in the insult of all insults, slaps on the Boston Massacre on the Boston Strangler. Inside the ring, Bad Attitude's clubberin' continues until the Black Panther returns from the back, charging the ring and causing Bad Attitude to bail. Panther stands threateningly inside the ring as Warfare ends in chaos... Result: Chronos & Fallen by pinfall
