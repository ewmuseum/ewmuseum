Wednesday Warfare, 16 June 1999

16 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The show opened with cameras trained backstage catching the arrival of an fWo star not seen in active competition in many months. The Texas Kid stepped out of a limousine and was escorted into the arena by fWo security. As he walked through the halls a faint smile graced his face beneath his mask as he recognized old faces, and met people for the first time. Suddenly sounds of a scuffle were heard, and the Flying Frenchie burst onto the scene. He grabbed the Texas Kid by the neck and brought him closer to himself, ripping at his mask as the Texas Kid attempted to get away. The Frenchie was restrained as he shouted at the Texas Kid, "You're him aren't you? I know it's you Silver Fox... that mask isn't fooling anyone! Unhand me, zut!" Security ushered the Flying Frenchie out of the area and back to his dressing room as the Frenchie attempted to calm himself. The sounds of Warfare exploded over the arena speakers as the cameras returned to the ring, where the action was starting... Scott Hart vs. Surgeon "Hot For Teacher" by Van Halen began playing, as newcomer to the fWo - Scott Hart - made his way out to the ring. The crowd was dancing as a hit from the 80s was actually being used by a 90s wrestler. Hart climbed into the ring where the Surgeon already awaited. Hart made quick work of the Surgeon, utilizing a swinging DDT, a reverse neckbreaker, and a diverse plethora of other moves. Hart appeared to be toying with the Surgeon, who appeared to be no match for the newcomer Hart. Hart defeated Surgeon using "The Final" (Texas Cloverleaf)... Surgeon submitted rather quickly, and Hart exited the ring looking determined, and all business, as his career in the fWo has finally started. Wasting no time with the fans, he walked up the ramp and back into the locker room... Result: Scott Hart by Submission Everybody has a price, even Mr. Socko We return from the commercial break to see green and gold fireworks shoot throughout the arena. "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal makes his way out wearing a 3 piece suit and the gorgeous Megan Dawn at his side. Noticably present is the Internet Title, of which Universal lost this past Friday after a near two month run as champion. Oddly enough, Universal carries with him a purple bag... which looks very much like the one he gave his former friend Ultra-Violet as a birthday present a few months back. Universal climbs into the ring and as the dollar $ign pyrotechnic prepares to lower in place, Universal signals for it to stay up hanging above the ring because he presumably doesn't feel like posing. Universal orders Megan Dawn to stand in the corner, and Universal begins to address the crowd on hand, the television audience at home, and one Mick Foley. "Mick Foley, you better bring your ass on out to the ring because I've got a very lucrative offer for you and I know you won't refuse". Foley makes his way out with Mr. Socko wrapped already around his fist, and he climbs his way into the ring; receiving a nice greeting from the crowd. As Universal begins to speak, Foley interrupts him: "Hold on there, Chris. I've talked it over with Mr. Socko here, and Mr. Socko has told me that before even hearing your lucrative offer, he will accept it. It's time for him to move on, he needs someone elses smelly, corn infested, toenail ripped off foot to rest on. He needs a new home. And seeing as how you're the richest man in the fWo and all of wrestling, he thinks you have a better home than mine. He thinks that since you have a lot of money and I don't, you're able to afford a good doctor to give you the proper medications to get rid of your corns, and hang nails, and ingrown nails, and things of that nature... where as I cannot afford things like that. So, he will accept your offer under one condition... and that is, when you and I get it on in a First Blood match at Numbered Days, we have a special guest referee calling it like he sees it. We have a special referee that won't end the match at the mere sight of crimson. We have a special referee that won't puke when blood comes gushing out of my forehead, or I get thrown through a table and blood comes out of my shoulder, or my ear gets torn off, and blood rushes down my body like the water rushing down Niagra Falls". As Foley was getting ready to say who the special guest referee was going to be, Universal readily accepted... without knowing who it would be. Foley then told Universal he was stupid for accepting so quickly, but without further adu, he would tell him who the special guest referee was: "Baad Ass" Austin Davis. Universal went crazy, ripping pieces of his suit off of his own body, nearly beside himself... not believing that he just made a foolish mistake. Finally, he demanded that Foley hand over Mr. Socko... in exchange for the purple bag... which was what Foley accepted in return for Mr. Socko. Foley said he would, but not until after he kissed Mr. Socko, and gave it a going away present... a date with MRS. SOCKO! Foley pulled Mrs. Socko out from his underpants, put it on his free hand, and rubbed his two hands together... as if the foot apparel was "getting jiggy with it". Universal grew angrier, and charged at Foley... who then shoved Mr. Socko down his throat again, like he did weeks ago on Slaughter... Universal begged for Megan Dawn' help, who ran towards Foley as well... but she was greeted with a taste of Mrs. Socko. As Foley controlled the fWo's richest couple in the palms (?) of his hands, the arena lights turned blue, and out came Death... Death climbed into the ring and grabbed Foley, ripping him off of Universal and Dawn, and threw him to the ground. He then stomped him repeatedly, as everyone wondered aloudly if Death was Universal' mystery partner for this Friday. It grew apparent that he was, as he helped up his longtime friend Universal to his feet. Universal then laughed at Foley, as the stunned crowd watched. As Universal continued to mock Foley, "trust me" echoed throughout the arena, and out came Austin Davis! Davis walked out onto the stage as Universal hid behind the massive Death, who stood there, taunting Davis. Davis motioned for someone to come on out, and *finally*, Ultra-Violet made his way out almost crying. Davis explained to UV that if he wanted his purple bag back, he had to go down there, kick some ass and get it back. UV didn't seem to understand, and as Davis grew frustrated... Violet came out to ease his mind. Violet explained in simple terms what UV had to do, and UV grew excited... he charged down to the ring with Davis, but tripped at first. Davis helped him up, brushed him off, and the two continued up into the ring. Davis struggled with Death, but UV made quick work of Universal... before helping Davis get Death out of the ring. That left Dawn, and Davis told Dawn that "this Friday, you'll be my bitch". Dawn continued to back up, but she backed up right into the arms of Violet, who tore the upper part of her dress off, and threw her out of the ring. Universal scattered quickly to cover up the almost naked Dawn... as Davis simply laughed. Finally, Universal, Death & Dawn left the ringside area... as UV petted his purple bag, happy to have it back. Davis simply smirked, and as the camera came closer to him, Davis said "See UV, I'm not a big meanie afterall...trust me." With that, we all soon realized that this Friday it would be Davis & Ultra-Violet vs. Chris Universal & Death. If Universal pins Davis, Davis must retire. If Davis pins Universal, Dawn becomes his for thirty days. Darkness vs. Mad Trucker As "TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" rang out throughout the arena, the Mad Trucker walked out looking angrier than ever. Trucker has been waiting for this moment for a long time, and the moment has finally arrived for him to seek revenge. He climbs into the ring and as Darkness charges towards him, he lifts up the big boot and Darkness meets the leather soul. Trucker is just getting started, as he lifts up Darkness with relative ease and drops him throat first on the top rope. Trucker brings Darkness to his feet, and whips him to the ropes. As Darkness returns, Trucker plants him into the canvas with a spinebuster. Trucker with a cover, but only gets a count of two. Trucker kicks him in the stomach repeatedly, just abusing the former multiple fWo champion. Trucker is hellbent, a man on a mission, a man using violence with a purpose. He grabs Darkness by the mask and brings him to his knees... he grabs him by the throat and begins choking him repeatedly, showing no mercy in his quest to destroy Darkness. The referee makes Trucker stop, who shows no patience with the referee. Trucker grabs the referee and throws him over the top rope out onto the cement floor, before grabbing Darkness and doing the same thing to him. The referee is out cold on the cement floor, and Trucker is just warming up. He grabs Darkness and slams him head first into the guard rail. He then thinks of a better idea, and whips Darkness into the guardrail. Darkness goes flying into it, which collapses the guardrail and makes Darkness a vegetable in the process. Trucker grabs the ring steps, and waits for Darkness to get up. Darkness slowly gets up, and as Trucker swings the ring steps towards Trucker, Darkness ducks and nails Trucker with a lowblow. This catches Trucker off balance, and he cannot hold the ringsteps anymore, so they more or less fall upon his chest and lower half of his body. He falls to the ground, and Darkness takes over. Darkness grabs a chair, and drives one end of it into the throat of Trucker. Trucker gasps for every last breath of air as Darkness continues his assault. Darkness brings Trucker up to his knees, and as Darkness' attention is diverted to clearing stuff off the ringside announcer table, Trucker catches him with a lowblow. With Darkness bent over, Trucker grabs him and power bombs him through the table with unrelenting fury. As the crowd sits in stunned silence, dozens of fWo officials come down to the ring and clear the area, as well as helping the original referee for the match... the match ends in confusion, with no decisive winner... as Warfare goes to a commercial break reeling from what just occured. Result: No Contest Mike Bear signs a match Shots of the fWo executive committee were shown signing Mike Bear's first match against Chris Kidman Jr. next week at Wednesday Warfare. A brief press conference was held to announce Bear's offical entry into the fWo, with Cactus Jon and the commissioner of the fWo Travis in attendance All seemed well, but when asked for comment Mike Bear grew angry for seemingly no reason and began to run amuck. He kicked aside the podium and grabbed an interviewer, slamming his camera to the floor and leaving the room. Cactus Jon and Travis exchanged a glance, and then shrugged, deciding that given Bear's unstable nature, things actually went fairly well. The fire alarm then sounded, as smoke from the camera set off the automatic sprinkler system. Water sprayed from the celing as Travis gave off another long sigh. Scott Slugger (c) vs. Oracle Oracle came to the ring and grabbed a microphone, shouting to the crowd that in one week he would be forever ridding the fWo of one Eron the Relentless. Eron told the crowd that nobody in the fWo wanted Eron around, a comment that brought boos from the crowd. Eron tossed the microphone at them in disgust, shouting to the cameras that one Prophet had better referee the match the way he was supposed to, or Oracle would take them both out. Scott Slugger came to the ring with a title slung over each shoulder, and Ice walking behind him. Slugger got into the ring and at once grabbed for Oracle's face. Oracle ducked under, and grabbed Slugger by the waist and sent him flying into the corner. Seeing this develop, Ice got into the ring and began to attack Oracle with high punches, sending the smaller man to his knees. Slugger got to his feet and continued the two on one attack, the ref doing little to stop it besides quietly recommending Ice "play fair". The rules of the Hardcore Title match cite no holds barred rules, and Ice was technically legal inside the ring. Suddenly Prophet ran to the ring, rolled inside, and attacked Ice from behind, evening the score. The crowd suddenly erupted as Eron ran through the audence, angry at the obvious teamwork shown between his opponent and the referee at Numbered Days. A full scale brawl broke out in the ring, with Scott Slugger finally sliding out of the ring and giving Oracle, Prophet, and Eron the finger as he left. Slugger smirked as he walked up the entrance ramp, but suddenly a dull roar was heard from the rafters and rain began to fall from the celing. Slugger looked up, his face wet from the rain, and held the title up in the air, shouting "You're never going to get this... whoever you are..." Result: No Contest Joy's challenge and the unvieling of a new title Joy came to the ring unannounced carrying a large bag, and climbed into the ring as fans began to boo him. Joy looked around, obviously hurt by the reaction from the crowd. Grabbing the microphone, Joy announced that after many days of trying to think of just the right stipulation should he lose at Numbered Days to the fornicator Primetime. Joy told the crowd that late at night the idea came to him... if Joy were to lose, then he would hand over his most prized possession. With that, he reached into his bag and pulled out a championship belt. It looked normal enough, until it was turned over and a large happy face was revealed to be the main part of it. Joy held the title in the air, and announced that from this night forward, the fWo would recognize his title... the Happiness Championship Belt. The crowd sat stunned, as Joy left the arena, still holding the title. Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Briana The main event was announced as the Texas Kid made his way to the ring, waving at the crowd and wearing a referee's shirt over his ring tights. The Kid climbed into the ring, and announced that the match was for the fWo International Championship. He introduced Briana, who came to the ring wearing a tight black leather outfit, complete with shootfighting boots, a tight sports bra, and her hair tied in a tight knot on the top of her head. Briana swung into the ring and awaited the Frenchie, who came to the ring slowly, looking around himself for signs of Silver Fox. The two began to wrestle an even match, with the Frenchie using his high flying experience to dodge attacks from the challenger. Briana swung fiercely, but the Frenchie always managed to stay one step away. As the Frenchie attempted to dodge another attack from Briana, he collided with the Texas Kid, causing himself to fall down and Briana to seize the advantage with hard shots to the head. The Frenchie escaped the attack and grabbed the Kid, shoving him into the ropes. As the Kid and the Frenchie argued, the Chameleon came quietly to ringside and stood observing the action. The Frenchie spotted the Chameleon, and prepared to go after him, but was caught and rolled up by Briana, who scored a two count. The Chameleon hopped onto the ring apron and patted the Kid on the back, which seemed to confuse the Texas Kid. The Frenchie seeing this, grew enraged, grabbing the Texas Kid and suplexing him to the mat. Certain that the Kid was actually Silver Fox in disguise, the Frenchie began attacking him inside the ring, as Briana attempted to pull him off. The Kid called for the DQ, and an angry Briana kicked at the back of Frenchie's head for the result of the match. As the cameras went off the air, the Chameleon laughed and walked up the ramp to the backstage area, where the Silver Fox, Fyre, Total Eclipse and two men hid in shadows were waiting... Result: Briana by DQ
