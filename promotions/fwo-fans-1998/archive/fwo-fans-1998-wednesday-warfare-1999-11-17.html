Wednesday Warfare, 17 November 1999

17 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The Flying Frenchie walked into the arena parking lot, small tote bag rolling behind him on tiny wheels. He carried with him the fWo Woman's title, and attempted to show it off just enough so it was obvious that he carried some gold, but not which gold. The Frenchie spotted some attractive groupies standing outside the arena entrance and approached wearing his most confidant French leer. "Hello ladies... zey women like ze French food? Yes?" The girls giggled, but informed the Frenchie that they were actually waiting to see Ultra Violet who was scheduled to arrive at any moment. The Flying Frenchie looked confused by this, and stepped away slowly with a scowl written across his face. "Bah... zese Canadian wom'n are not right in ze head... per'aps zere moth'rs drank ze vine when ze were preg'ant or some zuch nonsense..." Geoff Green walked up to the Frenchie holding a clipboard marked with the logo of the fWo FanZone. "Frenchie! Frenchie! The results of your match are in... it was close, but..." The Frenchie put a hand up, stopping Geoff from continuing. "Wait. Let me guess of this. Ze man I will face in ze ring is none o'her zan my own partner Primetime. Isn't zat correct?" Geoff nodded, somewhat confused. "Gosh Frenchie, that's dead on! How did you know that?" The Frenchie shook his head, dismissing the idea that it could have been someone else as though it was pure nonsense. "Of course ze Primetime won. Even zese Canadian fans do not want to be bored with ze likes of ze baseball pla'er or ze scary bear man. Very well zen, it will be ze mat' of ze year. Ze Frenchie against his own brot'er Primetime. For ze World 'Eavyweig't title no less..." Geoff Green shook his head. "Nah... that ain't right... it's not for the World..." Primetime cut him off again and walked away toward the dressing room. "For ze World 'Eavyweig't title no less..." Inside the arena the crack and boom of fireworks rocked the arena as Wednesday Warfare went on the air... Fear vs. Rudo Wednesday Warfare took the air with a bang as the lights in the arena dimmed and a large "L" formed on the fWo WarfareScreen. Rudo came out onto the entrance ramp, joined by his wife Brandi, and the pair walked to the ring, smacking hands with the fans and getting charged up for the opening bout. Inside the ring Rudo stood on the turnbuckle and awaited his opponent from the Underground, Fear. The massive Fear came out onto the ramp with Mike Bear and Wretch, but then waved them to the back, explaining that he could finish this man off on his own. Fear charged the ring and slid inside where he was met by kicks from Rudo. Rudo acted quickly to keep the big man off his feet and dropped an elbow. It hit, and Rudo went for a quick and early cover. Fear easily kicked out, tossing Rudo into the air as he pushed out. Fear grabbed Rudo by the neck and tossed him down to the mat with a face buster. Fear climbed to the top rope, held his position for a moment, and then dropped his elbow on Rudo from the top. Rather than go for the cover Fear picked Rudo up and hit a German suplex, but did not let go of Rudo's waist. Fear picked Rudo up to a vertical base once again, and executed a death valley driver. Fear made a cover, but Rudo got a foot on the ropes at two. Fear stood and went to the ropes, but on the return Rudo ducked and pitched him over the ropes to the outside. Stumbling backward, Rudo waited until Fear was on his feet on the outside and hit a baseball slide to the outside. Rudo rolled Fear back into the ring, and the big man stumbled to his feet as Rudo climbed inside the ring. Rudo kicked at Fear, but the big man caught his foot. Before he could toss him, Rudo hit a spinning heel kick that took Fear down to the mat. Rudo signaled up to the crowd and climbed to the top rope, diving off with a moonsault that hit it's mark. The referee went down, and Fear kicked out a second too late. Rudo stood, arms raised, and looked amazed that he had actually won the match. Fear, angry with the decision, struck from behind with a fist to the head, knocking Rudo down. Spitting on him, Fear walked to the back as cameras went there as well... Result: Rudo by Pinfall Machina's passion Matt Loco stood holding a microphone up to Machina's mouth. "Machina... in a little over a week you face fWo International Champion Eron in a match that many are saying will be a classic..." Machina looked at Eron with a raised eyebrow. "A classic? Oh come now, Loco... you've got the interview, stop stroking me off here. Eron, the fact of the matter is this. Tonight I go up against Bill Goldberg in what should be a... wait... that's not right... I'm facing the bigger, dumber, more hairy version of Goldberg tonight... Jackhammer. I want you to take note because what I do to him won't be pretty. In fact, it might be a little ugly. It might be leg drop in orange tights ugly. But it's just a preview of what I'm going to do to you at BattleBowl. Now another thing, I..." Machina trailed off as he spotted James Armstrong walking with a lovely young reporter. Machina's eyes glazed over as he saw her, and Loco had to remind him of where he was before Machina's attention was back on him. Machina started to get back into the interview, but then shook his head and put an arm on Loco's shoulder. "Loco... who is that woman?" Loco motioned down the hall to where Armstrong and the young lady had gone. "The woman with James Armstrong? Oh, that's Penny Pureheart... she's doing an expose for 20/20 on what it's like to be in a wrestling promotion. She's real talented too... I mean, she's a close personal friend of mine and all, but I..." Loco continued to talk, but Machina wandered away from him, hoping to steal another glimpse of this young angel... Smiletastic vs. Chaos As Joy and Glee pass out Pikachus and Poliwhirls to the younger children in the audience, Chaos approaches the ring, with grim looks on their faces. Plague slides into the ring and stares at Joy, still on the outside, while War simply steps over the top rope. Joy enters the ring for Smiletastic, and quickly gets beat down by Chaos. Plague steps out of the ring eventually, leaving War in there with Joy. A gut-wrench suplex by War almost puts Joy away, but he kicks out by instinct alone. Without warning, the entire scene turns purple. The camera cuts back, revealing a monitor in Ultra-Cool 2000's locker room. UV and Kid Cool are trying to study Chaos for their upcoming match at Battle Bowl, but are failing miserably as they both stare in astonishment at Ultra (the Narcoleptic Wonder Dog)'s amazing prowess at lying down. The cameras cut back to ringside, where Plague has tagged in and is taking his turn destroying Joy. A succession of headbutts forces Joy to the mat, and Plague follows up with vicious eye gouges. While the ref tries to part the two men, the scene shifts to the Underground's locker room. There, the tag champs observe the match, and in a much more analytical way than UC2K. Again the scene cuts back to the ring, and Glee has found her way into the match. Plague whips her into the ropes and takes her down with a hair-pull face buster. The subsequent cover gets three for Chaos, who walk out of the ring with nary a look at Smiletastic. Joy and Glee, meanwhile, tend to their wounds with a cheery round of "It's a Small World". As the crowd begs them to stop, the cameras go backstage for an interview with the man who was voted to face the Flying Frenchie later in the night... Result: Chaos by Pinfall Interview with the champ Geoff Green stood with Primetime, and excitedly informed him that he had won the fWo Fan's Match of the Week at fWowrestling.com. Primetime didn't look surprised. "Hey, when you're the best wrestler in the whole fWo it's no surprise that the fans would want to see you in action as often as possible. And going against a real legend like the Frenchie has always been a dream of mine. The Frenchie is easily one of the greatest wrestlers in the whole fWo, and if this title was on the line I don't know if I would be walking out of here the champion. Luckily for me the belt isn't, so..." Geoff cut him off, speaking quickly into the microphone. "But Primetime... the Flying Frenchie, your partner in the French Foundation has already informed us that it will be for the fWo World Heavyweight Title..." Primetime scratched his head. "Nah... the Frenchie is just kidding you. Yeah... just kidding you. Listen, I'm done talking about that. What about BattleBowl? Now that the Strangler is off somewhere in the hospital... boo flippin' hoo... I guess I get the night off. Is that about the extent of it Green?" Green shook his head. "No... the fWo promised a World Title match and a World Title match they will deliver... James Armstrong is still looking at wrestlers for the..." Primetime cut him off. "Shut up Green. Listen here, Armstrong. It doesn't matter who you put in the path of Primetime... because I'll run over anyone you put in my way. Get it?" Primetime took a last look at Green and snorted before walking into his locker room and slamming the door. Death (c) vs. Lance Star Lance Star makes his way to the ring for this next bout for the fWo Hardcore Title. The Cruiserweight Champ holds his title high in the air, screaming at the fans about how much better he is than everyone else. In the ring, he heads to the corner, and awaits Death. The lights dim, and the ubiquitous "4" appears on the TravisTron, but there's no Death in sight. Star advances in the ring towards the entrance ramp, and is thus caught off guard when Death comes in from behind and blasts him with the Hardcore Title. Lance crumples to the mat while Death rants over him, kicking and stomping the entire time. Death picks up Lance and piledrives him square onto the title, busting him open. Collins heads up top and comes off with an elbow, which Lance Star just barely rolls out of the way of. Both men stumble back up to their feet, and Star hits a wicked spinning elbow. Star into the ropes now, and a flying forearm takes Death off his feet. Lance grabs Death's legs and turns him over into a Boston Crab, buying himself some breathing time. Death powers out of the hold in short order, however. Back to his feet, Death whips star to the ropes, and follows up with a lariat to knock him out of the ring. Death slides out and grabs a chair, but Star catches it when Death swings away, then boots him in the gut. Star tosses the chair to the side, and rolls Death back in. Judo throw by Star sends Death to the mat, and is quickly followed up by a decidedly lackadaisical STF. Death makes the ropes, which would normally result in a forced break, but not in a Hardcore match. Realizing this, Death uses his upper body strength to pull himself to the top rope and break the hold. Star goes to small package Death, but Death shoves off the bloody Cruiserweight Champ and takes him down to the mat with a hard punch to the jaw. He lifts Star and sets him up on the turnbuckle, and looked to hit a top-rope Death Penalty. Death seemed to come off the ropes slightly wrong however, and hit Star from a strange position. Star crumpled backward, and his head hit the mat with a hard thud that send his neck into a strange angle. Death looked around for a moment, suddenly uncertain of what to do. Death finally covered Star, and the ref counted quickly to three as Star remained still. Death rolled off Star and said something, and then seemed to get angry. Death rolled from the ring and grabbed up the steel steps, looking to go after Star with them. Before he could, the rest of the Dynasty make their way out to put a stop to it. Death escapes into the crowd before the Dynasty can get to him, while Chris Universal calls out for some EMTs for Star, who's in a bad way Result: Death by Pinfall Dark words Matt Loco spotted Rain walking down the halls, looking with a smile over his shoulder at empty air. Loco asked for a word, and Rain seemed to consider it, and then nodded with the grin not leaving his face. "Rain... tonight you face the Chameleon who will no doubt be escorted to the ring by the big man... the Total Eclipse. You can't look past that match, but tell us about BattleBowl where in just a few days you will step into the ring with a man that many are calling the next fWo Internet Champion... Fallen of the Dark Riders. We all know that Fallen will be backed up by David Spencer and his band of lunatics... but you've had some rather strange help yourself lately." Rain slowly grabbed the microphone away from Loco, and pushed him away so that the camera saw him and only him. Rain smiled and talked in a soft yet commanding voice. "The Chameleon? That's not even a match. But Fallen... you have your friends with you, but so do I. Many of them. In fact, I've got an entire universe... no... universes to choose from. All of you... you think you understand what it's all about but you've seen nothing yet. You don't yet understand the true nightmare which I have at my disposal. But I'm not out to create nightmares... no, I'm not the villain here. I'm just here for control... and it's control that I'll have. Getting this title was the first step, but it's not the last. Before I'm done you won't be able to trust who you are... who you're wrestling... and what will happen next." Rain suddenly blew into the room and the lights exploded... casting the room into darkness. Machina vs. Jackhammer Jackhammer walks out of a haze of smoke, slapping himself in the head. Grunting, he slides into the ring, and turns to face the entrance ramp. As the smoke dissipates, the arena lights lower, and a giant blue "L" in a circle appears on the TravisTron. Out comes Machina, leader of the Lone Gunmen. He gets the camera's attention and pulls out a magazine, with a blurry image of a scantily clad woman in it. Machina blushes through his mask and tosses the skin mag to a crowd of lonely wrestling fans, then produces People magazine. He opens it to a hastily added page declaring Machina the sexiest masked wrestler four years running, and proudly continues towards the ring. Jackhammer tries a spear right from the start, but it's side stepped by Machina, so Jackhammer sails into the ringpost. As he reels out of the corner backwards, Machina connects with a neckbreaker and covers for one. Jackhammer gets back up, but his suplex is countered into a small package, again for one. Machina brings Jackhammer to his feet and whips him to the ropes, catching him with a spin-heel kick upon his return. He then quickly climbs the turnbuckle and connects with a 450 splash. It only gets two, as Jackhammer barely kicks out. Machina barks at the ref, allowing Jackhammer to schoolboy him for two. Jackhammer scoops up Machina and slams him to the mat, then drives his knee into Machina's cranium. Machina yelps and runs to the outside to collect himself. Just then, Eron, the International Champ and Machina's opponent at Battle Bowl '99, comes down to ringside. They exchange words on the outside before Machina, distracted, rolls back into the ring, and gets speared by Jackhammer. Jackhammer roars, and powerbombs Machina. Jackhammer gets the pin and leaves, while Machina simply twitches on the mat. Eron enters the ring, holding his belt above Machina's face, telling him that's the closest he'll ever be to it. Eron grimaces as he walks away, and Warfare heads backstage Result: Jackhammer by Pinfall Heat Backstage Scott Slugger, Chris Universal and Ice helped arena EMTs wheel Lance Star to an ambulance, where they loaded him up and strapped him down. As the ambulance was starting to pull away the Millennium Men; Black Panther, Wolf Fang and Havok approached, wanting to know what the status of Lance Star was. Universal started to throw a punch at Havok, but Ice restrained him. Panther stepped forward. "This isn't about a fight... or our match at BattleBowl. Lance Star is a wrestler like all of us... we just want to know his condition. Is this a minor injury or more major?" Short tempered and angry, Ice let go of Universal and charged the Panther, knocking him off his feet as the two men brawled on the pavement. Universal and Wolf Fang tried to break it up as Slugger shouted at Havok. "You think this is funny? You want to come in here and pretend that you give a damn? You little bastards... I'm going to kill Death first, and Ice and Universal are more than enough to kick your ass at BattleBowl... you hear me? You get it?" Security rushed the scene and desperately tried to keep the men apart, with little success. The cameras spiraled back to the ring... Rain (c) vs. Chameleon The cameras return to the ring for the next match-up, this one for the Internet Championship. Total Eclipse lumbers towards the ring with the challenger, Chameleon, riding on his back. Chameleon climbs off of his partner and steps onto the ring apron, drawing in jeers from the crowd. He thumbs his nose at them, and waits patiently in the center of the ring. The arena darkens, and thunder crackles, bringing out the awesome Rain, Internet Champion of the fWo. He strides confidently towards the ring, alone at first, but soon joined by a man in a Death mask. Clearly neither Collins nor Spencer, this one doesn't even resemble Rain's partner from Mob Rules, aside from his Cruiserweight build. Rain pays no heed to him, and steps into the ring against Chameleon. The bell rings, and Rain staggers Chameleon with a hard right. Rain continues the assault, backing Chameleon into the corner. There, he hits a flurry of high knees on Chameleon, and then tosses him to the mat. Rain bellows to the crowd before following up with a knee drop, which Chameleon avoids. Both men back up, and Chameleon kicks Rain repeatedly in the thigh, then dropkicks his knee. Rain falls to a knee, and then falls victim to a rolling neck snap. Chameleon climbs to the top, while Rain takes to his feet again. A cross body off the top is caught and countered into a spinning sidewalk slam. Chameleon rolls out of the ring and into the arms of Total Eclipse, who looks concerned. Rain follows to the outside and takes issue with Total Eclipse, charging him with a big boot. Chameleon, who'd still been in the arms of TE, falls on top of him, and rolls under the ring. His opponent gone, Rain starts smashing the bejeezus out of Total Eclipse, laying him out with mighty stomps. Chameleon re-emerges with a chair in hand, and wraps it around the back of Rain's head. With a bit of effort, he rolls him into the ring, and then takes a second to pat himself on the back. This proves to be his undoing, as the masked Death superkicks Chameleon, knocking him out but good. Death rolls him into the ring, then unmasks, revealing the handsome masked face of Black Quicksilver. BQ laughs at Chameleon's misfortune as Rain makes the cover and gets the three. Rain looks down in disgust at Chameleon, then grabs his title from the referee and heads backstage. Result: Rain by Pinfall Boxing or ass? The scuffle completed backstage, James Armstrong handed Havok a clipboard with an assignment written on it. "What's this... a match? Against..." Havok trailed off and then looked up at Armstrong with wide eyes. "Gunn? What do you mean 'Gunn'? I'm wrestling a 'Gunn' tonight?" Armstrong nodded. "That's right... we were very lucky to get him actually..." Havok tossed the clipboard back at Armstrong. "Look, I'm all for a good match, but what gives? I mean... what Gunn is this? The ass boy or the boxing boy?" Armstrong smiled to himself and started to walk away. "Oh... you'll see. Don't you worry about it." Havok watched him go with a look of annoyance on his face. "Can you believe that guy? I mean... what is this 'Gunn' business anyway?" Wolf Fang and the Panther shook their heads as the cameras quickly tried to catch up with Armstrong... Warfare Girl Problems As Armstrong approached the room where the tryouts were being held he heard sounds of a scuffle. With a leer on his face he ran the rest of the way to the room, and then stopped with a shocked expression on his face. The room was a complete mess. Shards of tables were scattered around the room, and chairs were bent against the walls. Pink material lay in torn shreds all around the room, and hung from the lights on the ceiling. Several attractive models looked to have been beaten, and lay strewn around the room in small heaps, buttocks and breasts jutting out from the piles of unconscious women. An fWo official was laying on the ground, his head bleeding from where a clipboard had been smashed. Sitting in the middle of all of this on a small chair and sipping a cup of coffee was Nightshade. Armstrong gaped at her. "What in the hell happened here?" Nightshade regarded Armstrong coldly. "They wanted me to dance. So I did." Armstrong's mouth hung open wider as Nightshade continued to sip her coffee amidst the wreckage. The cameras went back to the ring. Havok vs. Tommy Gunn Havok waited in the ring for his opponent, the Black Panther and Wolf Fang standing on the outside. The lights dimmed slightly, and Italian music began to play. A man walked out onto the entrance ramp dressed in a pin stripe suit, an oversized fedora, and carrying a large violin case. He had a long cigarette holder that held a smoldering chic cigarette, and walked to the ring with a slow swagger. Over the PA system he was announced as "Tommy Gunn", but the fans recognized him clearly as Mike Heftel. Gunn got to the ring and asked for a microphone, threatening the ring announcer with the violin case as he did so. He looked out at the crowd, and spoke in a poor replica of the Italian language. "Hey-a... you a want to see me a-fight? Well-a I am a gonna do that right-a now... because-a Tommy the Gunn-a doesn't back a down from a nobody. Well-a Havok... I'm a gonna givea you a chance to walk away right-a now... and sonny... this is a offer you can't a refuse." Havok answered Tommy Gunn by kicking him in the head from the ring and rolling to the outside. Havok punched Gunn in the head, and then asked for the violin case. Sighing, Gunn handed it to him and Havok knocked him over the head with it. Havok rolled Gunn inside the ring and brought him up into a vertical position. He drove him to the mat, and then signaled that he was going to the top rope. He dove off with the Millenium-sault, and covered Gunn for the pin. As Wolf Fang and the Panther congratulated him on his victory, the cameras went backstage... Result: Havok by Pinfall A match is made After the chaos in the fWo Warfare Girls tryout room, James Armstrong looked sweaty and tired as he spoke to Matt Loco. "Mr. Commissioner..." Armstrong stopped him. "Call me... Mr. President." Loco nodded. "Mr. President... the world is anxiously awaiting who will face Primetime at BattleBowl. We know that the Boston Strangler is out on an injury... and we wanted to know who would be stepping into the number one contendership role." Armstrong nodded. "Well Matt, right off if you're asking who the number one contender is, you've got to be thinking Scott Slugger. I know I do... all the time. But my lawyers have informed me that Scott Slugger in that match is impossible... so what I've done is created a number one contenders match for this Sunday Slaughter. The winner of this match will go on to face Primetime at Battle Bowl. I've chosen two wrestlers that I think are worthy of such a position in the fWo. First we have David Spencer... a former fWo World Heavyweight Champion. He will face another former fWo World Heavyweight Champion... Cactus Jon. I've spoken with Cactus Jon, and he will be returning to the promotion after some initial discussions were worked out. So this Sunday, on Slaughter, it will be a battle of former champions to determine the number one contender. Now go down to the ring... it's time for the main event." Primetime (c) vs. Flying Frenchie The Flying Frenchie walked to the ring looking nervous and slightly confused about how he would wrestle the next match against his partner and friend. Once inside the ring the Frenchie whispered something in the referee's ear and passed him what looked like a large handful of Francs, and the referee nodded. The ring announcer was spoken to, and the match was announced to be for the fWo World Heavyweight Championship. Primetime came out then, looking somewhat angry. At his side wobbled Jake, carrying his title and jacket. The pair marched to the ring and Primetime started to argue at the Frenchie, who tried to calm him down. The two came to some form of an agreement, and shook hands, albeit reluctantly. As the Frenchie turned away to go to his corner Primetime charged, knocking him down in a show of force. The Frenchie was angry at this and quickly went for a high kick, but Primetime ducked it. Primetime grabbed his leg and drug him to the mat and took control. Both stood up, and the Frenchie got on the advantage, hit a high kick and landed a near perfect STO. The Frenchie attempted to follow it up with a juji-gatame, but Primetime slid down and escaped to the ropes. The Frenchie ran forward and landed a driving knee, knocking Primetime in the face and causing him to bleed. Sensing weakness, the Frenchie executed a vertical suplex and went for the juji-gatame once more. Primetime blocked it and kicked the back of the Frenchie with a high spinning heel kick. The Frenchie went to the mat and Primetime attempted a cover, but was slapped in the face for his efforts. Both men stood once more, and Primetime chopped Frenchie in the chest and neck. The Frenchie choked under the hard shops to the neck, and staggered backward, opening him up for a backward suplex. The Frenchie was slammed down to the mat, and Primetime hit a swinging kick from the second rope that send both men down for a few seconds. Both men slowly got to their feet once more. Primetime went for another powerbomb, but the Frenchie blocked it. The men traded punches until Primetime landed a hard punch to the jaw, and spinning hit a heel kick on the back of the head. Primetime attempted a German suplex, but the Flying Frenchie switched it into knee bar and pushed down into the back of Primetime. The ref checked for the submission, but Primetime refused to give up. Straining, Primetime escaped to the rope. Standing with his back to the Frenchie, Primetime was battered from behind with quick rabbit punches to the back of the skull. The Frenchie went for a brainbuster but Primetime blocked out by hooking the leg. Primetime chopped in the neck, taking advantage of the earlier injury and sent the Flying Frenchie crashing down to the mat. Primetime hit a spinkick, and scooped the Frenchie up for a piledriver. The Frenchie blocked it, and dropped Primetime with a DDT. Frenchie made the cover, but on the two count Primetime reversed and rolled the Frenchie into a small package. The Frenchie got a foot on the ropes at two, and as Primetime released the cover the Frenchie lunged off the ropes to put him into a small package. The momentum carried him to far however, and Primetime used it to cover and bear down on the chest of Primetime. The Frenchie kicked out a second too late... and Primetime retained the title. Primetime stood with his arms raised as he was handed the title, and the Frenchie sulked. Finally sighing hard, the Frenchie stood and helped raise the hand of the champion, pointing to his friend and partner. As the cameras went off the air the Frenchie could be heard saying, "Hey... I still have zis chick's belt right?" Warfare came to a close with a reminder to log onto fWowrestling.com and vote for next week's Fan Match for Warfare. Result: Primetime by Pinfall
