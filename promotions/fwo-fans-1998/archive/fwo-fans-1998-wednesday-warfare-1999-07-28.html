fWo — Card

Wednesday Warfare, 28 July 1999

28 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Behind the arena fWo trucks pulled into the lot, unloading crates, camera equipment, and other materials that are typically found at a wrestling show. Ring attendants remained hard at work as the sounds of fireworks came from inside the arena, signalling that Wednesday Warfare had offically began. Suddenly, a few attendants yelled in warning to members of the fWo staff that were unloading a nearly truck as a limosine pulled sharply into the area. Machina exited quickly, and surveyed the scene around him with smug satisfaction. Pointing at one of the attendants, he said, "You! Get over here and open the door for your new president!" The ring attendant raised an eyebrow at the recently returned masked wrestler, and then went about his duties, ignoring Machina's request. After waiting a few moments to make certain that the man wasn't going to comply with what he asked for, Machina stomped into the backstage area, opening the door for himself, and calling backward, "You idiots had better be more respectfull to your new president next time I come through here, or I'll fire the whole mess of you!" With Machina gone, the workers went back about their duties as if little had happened, a few of them shaking their heads in astonishment over the masked wrestler's words. One worker turned to another and said, "You see, since he beat Davis he thinks he's the new president of the fWo." The second worker nodded in understanding and said, "Oh! So that's it! Didn't he read the contract carefully?" The first worker just raised an eyebrow and pointed at the arena. "You remember who we are talking about right? The man who wasted four months trying to be president of the United States?" The second worker nodded again, and lifted up a large box, sighing, "What an stupid asshole." Inside the arena, the sounds grew louder, and the ringing of a bell signalled that the first match was about to begin... Kid Cool vs. ??? "All-Star" by Smash Mouth begins to play loudly throughout the arena, and out comes Kid Cool wearing his favorite Ramones t-shirt. Cool high fives some fans on the way to the ring, before climbing up into the ring. He stumbles into the ring, obviously he wasn't properly trained into the art of climbing into the ring correctly. Kid Cool walks into the middle of the ring and begins yelling about his challenge. Not realizing that only few people can hear him, the referee tells him to wait a second, and he'll get a microphone for him. Kid Cool thanks him, and the referee hands him the microphone. Kid Cool begins to speak, saying he's made a challenge and if nobody has the guts to answer the challenge, then he's gonna sit in the middle of the ring all night until someone comes out and forces him to leave. Kid Cool then sits Indian style in the middle of the ring, until we hear sounds of thunder and lightning, until out walks the former Hardcore Champion Rain. Rain points at Kid Cool as he walks down the ramp and tells him "I'm going to rip that Ramones shirt off of your ass so hard the actual Ramones will feel it!" Rain climbs up onto the ring apron and steps over the ring. Rain stands there looking at Kid Cool, who stands up. Cool stares at Rain with a look of horror on his face, before running and bouncing into the ropes on the other side of the ring. He returns and nails Rain with a very soft hit to the stomach. He then looks up at Rain, he reaches down and grabs Kid Cool by the throat with one of his massive hands. He lifts him up into the air and chokeslams him in the middle of the ring. He then brings him to his feet and whips him to the ropes. Cool returns and Rain takes him out with a spinebuster. He then tosses Cool over the top rope and onto the floor. He props Cool up against one of the ring posts, so Cool is laying on the ringsteps with his back to the ringpost. Rain grabs a chair and slams it up against Cool's head with a ton of impact. He then grabs Kid Cool by the throat and chokeslams him on the cement floor. Rain then rolls him into the ring and pins him with his pinky. He then grabs the microphone and says "I only have one thing to say: Crucifix, if you have any guts, you'll give me a re-match for the Hardcore Title. I don't just want a re-match, I'm demanding one. I know you're in the back, and I demand an answer before the end of the night or there will be hell to pay." With that, Warfare went to a commercial break as Kid Cool lay in the ring, being checked on by fWo medical personnel. Result: Rain by Pinfall Juvenile antics We return from the commercial break to find Chris Universal walking down a hallway with a camera man with him. Universal walks up to a door and knocks on it. On the door, reads a tag that says "Black Panther". Universal continues to knock on the door. Universal then looks at the camera and says "Looks like nobody is home." He then attempts to open up the door, and does so. He tells the camera man to wait outside. A few moments pass by after Universal closes the door. Universal returns and the camera man asks him what he was doing in there. Universal replies and says "None of your damn business; but you might want to inform the Masked Retard that I just urinated on his mask." Universal smiles confidently before walking out of view. Chameleon vs. Briana Briana made her way to the ring to the cheers of the fans, and waved to them from the top of the rope. The Chameleon's entrance was far less pleasant for the mysterious wrestler, and he slid into the ring to a chorus of boos from the fans. The pair locked up in a collar and elbow tie up, with Briana getting the advantage early. Briana hit two quick kicks, but as she went for the third the Chameleon grabbed the leg and threw her to the mat. The Chameleon applied a reverse chinlock, and kept Briana in the center of the ring to dictate the tempo of the match. As Briana grew weary, the Chameleon went for a cover, but to no avail as he got only a one count. Briana followed up for another kick, and then attempted to hit a lariat, but the Chameleon ducked under and grabbed her neck, snapping her to the mat with a reverse DDT. Staying on the attack the Chameleon picked up the female competitor and nailed a body slam before hooking the leg for a cover. This got a near far, as Briana kicked out at the last second. The Chameleon punched at Briana, attempting to wear her down with hooks to the chest and jaw. The Chameleon then picked Briana up for an airplane spin, but Briana slid out from behind and ran to the ropes for a quick clothesline. Climbing then to the top rope, Briana executed a perfect flying dropkick. The move paid off for Briana, but she was only able to get a two count when the Chameleon found the ropes with his foot. The Chameleon then blocked a punch and executed a single arm suplex on Briana, setting her up for the kill. The Chameleon attempted a cover, but it was Briana that found the ropes with a boot, stopping the cover. The Chameleon chopped at Briana, but she was energized now and punched back, driving the Chameleon to the mat. Briana then set the Chameleon up for a somersault neck snap, but then missed with a reverse roll attempt. The Chameleon attempted to come back, but it was Briana who moved more quickly, delivering a chop to the jaw and then executing a single arm suplex on the Chameleon. Yelling to the crowd, Briana executed a backbreaker on Chameleon, and let him drop weakly to the mat. Briana went for the cover, but the Total Eclipse and Fyre ran to the ring to stop the count. This earned Briana the DQ victory, as the two wrestlers and a revived Chameleon stomped away on Briana. Outside of the ring Silver Fox yelled orders from a wheelchair, until the crowd erupted as Wolf Fang, Jackhammer, and Black Quicksilver ran to the ring to fend off Briana's attackers. As Warfare went to a commercial the four stared down Silver Fox's men on the outside. Result: Briana by DQ Panther has a request ... we return from the commercials to find The Black Panther, with his mask intact, outside the commissioner's office with Travis. The Panther says that he knows that the traditional way to win cage matches is to go over the top of the cage or out the door... but he wants the chance to pin Universal cleanly in the cage match as well. He asks the commissioner to grant his request, and the commissioner does so. As Panther is about to leave, the camera man asks him if he heard what Universal did. Panther says "I did... I saw the masked covered in his urine... what, does the idiot think I'd only bring one mask to the arena? I don't think so. Nice try, Universal... I know that was just a desperate attempt so I wouldn't be able to show up for our match because I'd have to reveal my face, but I'm still going to show up for the match to kick your ass and take my title back." With that, we went back to the ring.. D-Lo Brown vs. Flying Trucker D-Lo Brown came to the ring holding his newly won European and Intercontinental Titles in the air, and yelling at the crowd that they should recognize this. D-Lo tossed his titles into the ring and then stood on the ringpost, shaking his head and shouting out to the fans. Pointing at the fWo logos, D-Lo shouted that an fWo title was next. The sound of a horn played then, as the Flying Trucker came to the ring waving at the fans. The Trucker looked smaller than normal, and walked a little unsteadily as the fans were unsure of if they should boo or cheer this new Trucker. The Trucker signaled to the crowd, and shouted "Le TOOOOOOOOOOOOT! Er... I mean... TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" to the fans, who became even further confused. Annoyed with this silliness, D-Lo dove to the outside catching the Flying Trucker on the ring apron. D-Lo then rolled the small athlete into the ring, and went for a cover. Only able to get a two count, D-Lo picked up the Flying Trucker and went for a pumphandle slam. The Trucker was able to duck out of it however, and delivered a hard kick to the chest of the WWF superstar. The Flying Trucker then went to the turnbuckle, but seemed to become unsure of what to do next. He started to climb to the top rope, but then looked at his feet uncertainly and climbed down again. He started up to deliver the 450 splash once more, but then finally stepped down to the mat again and walked over to D-Lo. He attempted a powerslam, but it was easily blocked by the much heavier D-Lo, who instead picked the Flying Trucker up and executed a perfect sidewalk slam. Climbing to the top rope himself, D-Lo executed a frog splash, and hooked the leg for a successful cover. D-Lo walked away from the ring leaving the Flying Trucker confused as to what had just taken place. Talking slowly, the Flying Trucker said, "This is all Darkness' fault... I guess ah still aren't back to one hundred percent after those matches with him... well, better go for ze... I mean, the hair of the dog that bit me. Darkness, I'm callin' you out for le... for A match next week!" With that, Warfare went backstage... Result: D-Lo Brown by Pinfall Skyler arrested... or is he? Backstage John Skyler lay on a small cot holding his head and moaning slightly. His eyes looked sunken and his skin was pale, as several doctors monitored him from a distance. Speaking to each other, the first doctor said, "We are going to have to turn this matter over the police... it's too dangerous to keep him at these shows if what we suspect is true." The second doctor nodded. "I've called them... they should be here any moment." As if on cue, a knocking was heard on the door and a large uniformed officer opened the door. Looking at the doctors, the policeman said, "That him? That Skyler?" and pointed to the resting superstar. The doctors nodded, and the policeman walked over and cuffed him, hauling him to his feet and practically carrying him from the room. The doctors conferred with each other once again. "Was that the right thing to do?" The second doctor nodded again. "It was the only thing we could do. John Skyler looks to have murdered his wife in cold blood..." Cameras followed the policeman carrying Skyler to his squad car, and watched him load him in from a distance. As the car pulled away, lights flashing, careful viewers noticed the back of a third head in the forward passenger seat of the car... a decidedly female head... Crucifix (c) vs. Plague With Plague already in the ring... the fWo Hardcore Champion as Crucifix makes his way down to ringside. Crucifix climbs into the ring, and Plague charges at him. Crucifix simply grabs him by the head and throws him over the top rope onto the cement floor. Crucifix climbs over the top rope and waits on the apron. Plague climbs and Crucifix jumps off with a flying axehandle. He then grabs Plague and slams him on the entrance ramp, before draping him over his shoulder and carrying him up the ramp into the backstage area. He walks up to a door that reads "DO NOT ENTER", before he opens the door and takes him in there. The camera man begrudgingly follows him in before Crucifix slams Plague head first into an electrical box. He then finds a table with a bunch of empty beer bottles scattered on top of it. He power bombs Plague through the table of beer bottles. Glass goes flying everywhere, and one of the shards of glass goes flying into Crucifix' eye. Crucifix holds his eyes for a few minutes which allows Plague to recover. Plague spots a door leading out of that room and he slams Crucifix head first into it. He then opens the door which leads out to an area where wrestlers are associating. The wrestlers scatter out of the way as Plague brings the giant Crucifix near an fwowrestling.com broadcast position, where Warfare is being broadcasted. Matt Loco and several other lackeys run amok as Plague grabs a computer monitor and slams it into the chest of Crucifix. Plague finds a cartfull of janitorial items such as glass cleaner, a vacuum, a mop and a broom. He takes the glass cleaner and sprays it into the eyes of crucifix, before taking the mop and braking it over his head. Crucifix falls backwards through some boxes, before Plague takes the vacuumed and jams it into the crotch of Crucifix. Plague kindly sweeps up the carnage with a broom for several moments, before bringing Crucifix to his feet and tossing him into the cart. He positions the cart and charges full speed ahead... Crucifix goes flying through the fwowrestling.com broadcast position, which sends computers and monitors everywhere. He stands Crucifix up, while steel in the massive cart, and suplexes him out of the cart through a buffet table. Crucifix is covered with hot dogs and cans of carbonated drinks. Plague then climbs up into the cart and waits for Crucifix to stand up. He flies off and Crucifix catches him, before tossing him away with a fallaway slam onto the hard concrete. Crucifix takes Plague outside where people are having an outside barbecue to celebrate Warfare being in their hometown. Crucifix tells them to get out of his way. He clears off a wooden table before slamming Plague head first into a cars windshield. He grabs a container of gasoline and squirts it all over the table, before lighting a match and dropping it onto the table. The referee tries to get him to stop, but Crucifix tells him to get out of his way or HE will go through the table of fire. The referee reluctantly obliges, before grabbing Plague and crucibombing him through the table of fire. He covers him for the pin and retains the title. Suddenly Rain arrives on the scene and attacks Crucifix. He slams him head first into the hot barbecue grill which was cooking hamburgers. Rain shoves Crucifix into a passing by Mike Bear, who was heading into the arena. Mike Bear looks at Rain, and screams, before attacking him. About fifty or so security officials along with several referees and backstage attendants flood the scene, as Plague sits in a pool of blood and charbroiled flesh. The officials try to split things up as the area is run amok with chaos. Result: Crucifix by Pinfall Punk Brit vs. Joy Loud music from the Clash blared over the arena P/A system as a man dressed in heavy leather, chains, and with a spiked mohawk walked out onto the entrance ramp. He sneered at the fans in attendance, and flipped them off as he walked to the ring, nursing his left leg. As the man, who was introduced as "Punk Brit" got into the ring and grabbed the microphone, it became quickly obvious that it was actually Mike Heftel. Punk Brit said, "Gor! This is the bloody worst lot of droogs I er' seen! Where's my opponent tonight? Get down 'er sunshine so I can tear your bloody skull in, right!" Nobody walked out onto the entrance ramp, and Brit Punk looked around incredulously. "What now, is 'ere nobody with the man dangles to face me 'ere? C'mon pumpkin, let's get started!" The lights suddenly turned yellow, as Joy walked out onto the entrance ramp and headed for the ring. Joy slapped the hands of the fans as he went, and dove into the ring with a smile. Punk Brit wasted no time delivering a hard shot to the face of the "new" superstar, and laughed as Joy held his jaw tenderly. Punk Brit took Joy down quickly with a drop toe hold, but as he moved quickly to cover Joy his fake mohawk came unattached, and it tumbled to the mat. Yelping in horror, Punk Brit went after it, but it was Joy who came from behind to quickly roll the "British" superstar up for a cover. Joy got the pinfall victory, and walked from the ring shaking his head... telling fans nearby that Punk Brit just wasn't happy enough... and perhaps should try listening to music other than Morrissey or the Smiths. Result: Joy by Pinfall Meltdown's first two teams The commissioner walked to the ring, wielding a microphone and a small slip of paper. The commissioner waved to the fans in attendance and said, "Thank you all for coming to the show tonight, we here at the fWo appreciate your support. Now... before I get to the reason I came down here, I'd just like to express my thanks to Machina for riding the fWo of Austin Davis... who I positively, completely, one hundred and ten percent guarantee will never been seen in the fWo again." The crowd seemed unsure of what they thought of this statement, and so simply waved their signs at the cameras. The commissioner continued, "Now, I'm out here to announce the first two teams that will be competing in the fWo's August Pay Per View spectacular... METLDOWN! As you know, there will be twenty teams made up of forty fWo superstars, each team chosen at random. The match will start with two teams in the ring, and a new team will enter every two minutes until all forty teams have come to the ring. This match has battle royal over-the-top elimination rules, and once one member of a team is eliminated the other member must then leave the ring as well. When the match gets down to only one team, the remaining two men will fight it out inside the ring with normal one on one rules in effect. The winner will receive a guaranteed FULL SWEEP title shot... that's one a title shot for EVERY title in the fWo, with normal gender and weight restrictions temporarily waved!" The crowd cheered as they took this bit of information in, and the commissioner continued. "Entrance will be chosen at random one hour before the event, but over the next four weeks we will introduce all twenty teams, starting with these two. The first team drawn at random for Meltdown will be the team of the FLYING FRENCHIE and CHRIS UNIVERSAL!" The crowd cheered in response, as the commissioner continued with, "And the second team announced today and drawn at random for Meltdown will be the team of the BLACK PANTHER and MIKE BEAR!" The crowd cheered again, as Travis left the ring and the show went to it's final commercial. Chris Universal (c) vs. Black Panther ... we return from previews for "Meltdown" which is the fWo's next big pay-per-view event. It looks like it's time for our main event, as we hear "Do you buy what the $ellout is selling?" before out comes "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal, carrying his beloved fWo Internet Title. Universal walks down the ramp quickly, before joining the commentators at ringside. Universal says "I'm not going to wrestle this match because the Black Panther will be coming to the ring wearing the mask that I urinated on. I'm not going to touch that guy's head. I don't know if someone took a dump after I urinated on it. He's covered in filth, obviously, and I'm too rich to touch filth like that." As Universal continues to ramble on... the Black Panther begins walking down through the crowd, behind the commentator's position. The Black Panther climbs over the guardrail and walks over to Universal, who was talking to Chris Finn, sitting to his left. Panther grabs Universal by the neck, and brings him to his feet. He sends him into the cage with an Irish Whip. Universal bounces off the cage and staggers out towards Panther, who backdrops him through the commentators table behind them. Universal goes crashing through and Chris Finn runs for his life. Panther then grabs Universal and slams him head first into the cage, immediately busting him open. Panther brings the action into the ring, and the referee rings the bell and the match finally begins. Panther goes for a cover, but Universal somehow kicks out. Panther whips Universal to the ropes and takes him out with a big boot to the jaw. Panther brings him to a vertical base once again, and whips him to the ropes. Universal returns and Panther slaps on an Abdominal Stretch, and Universal writhes in pain. The referee tells Panther he could be wasting his time because you cannot win this match by submission... the rules state (for this match) that you can win by pinfall, or go through the cage door or over the top of the cage and out. Panther reluctantly releases Universal, who walks into the corner holding his back. Panther turns him around and Universal drops to his knees and nails him with a lowblow. Universal sees his chance to escape. Universal quickly heads towards the door and Panther follows, despite the pain. Panther is obviously unable to shrug off the shot to the groin, as he grabs hold of Universal tights from behind. He pulls them down somewhat and shows off Universal' bare naked ass to the crowd. Universal looks embarrassed and quickly reaches back with his leg and nails him with a shot to the groin, using the mule kick. Panther drops to the mat and clutches his hurt... um yeah. Universal laughs at Panther, and then says "I'm going to retire you just like I retired Austin Davis a year ago!" Universal walks over and looks down at Panther, who quickly rolls him up with a small package. Universal kicks out. Both men rush to their feet and Universal charges at Panther, taking him down with a flying forearm. Universal follows it up with a snapmare. He then climbs up to the top rope and waits for Panther to get up. Panther does and Universal flies off with a perfectly executed missile dropkick to the head. Universal with another cover, but Panther kicks out again. Universal bounces off the ropes and comes back, dropping the point of his elbow into the chest of Panther. Another cover, and another kickout at two. Universal tells Panther to "say his name, bitch!" before kicking him in the head. He brings Panther to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckles. Universal casually walks in and Panther grabs him by the neck, and tosses him into the turnbuckles. He follows it up with kick after kick after kick into the solar plexus. Panther then looks at Universal and says "YOU say MY name, BITCH!" before whipping him into the turnbuckles. Panther bends over for a backdrop, but Universal staggers out and hooks him for a reverse ddt... no, he turns it into a nicely done inverted swinging neckbreaker. A cover, but only a two. Universal decides it's time to exit the cage. Universal takes his own damn sweet time in doing so... and as Universal attempts to escape from the cage, Panther climbs up to the top rope and barely stops him. Plotting carefully, Panther grabs Universal and hooks him for a superplex off the top of the cage. Both men go flying through their air, and smack the mat hard... a few minutes pass by while both men lay on the mat. Finally, Panther drapes his arm over for a cover. The referee climbs into the ring and makes the count. 1... 2... but somehow, Universal kicks out. Panther brings Universal to his feet, and attempts to Irish Whip him head first into the cage. Universal reverses at the last minute and instead, Panther smacks the steel. As Panther staggers backwards, Universal spins him around and kicks him in the midsection. He then hits the "Cha-Ching!". Universal is too exhausted to cover him at first, but eventually does. Panther kicks out at two. Universal decides since he can't beat him with his finisher, he'll attempt to climb over and out of the cage. Universal takes too long, and by the time he gets to the top of the cage, Panther has recovered and is on his feet. Panther stops Universal, slams him head first into the cage as Universal hangs halfway in, and halfway out of the cage. as Universal continues to hang, Panther hooks his arm, and hiptosses him from the top of the cage onto the mat. Panther finally drops down from the top rope... and attempts to pin Universal. Somehow, Universal drapes his foot across the bottom rope. Undaunted, Panther eventually decides to walk out the door. The referee opens the door and Panther walks out down the ring steps. He then shuts the door and is declared the winner. With his back to the ramp/stage, he raises his arms as the referee walks over to grab the Internet Title to award it to him. As he basks in the cheers of the crowd, a man appears on the scene and attacks him from behind. He throws the Panther face first into the steel cage several times, before opening up the cage door and sliding him in. Universal is finally back up to his feet. Megan Dawn, who was noticeably absent throughout the match, followed the man who attacked Panther into the ring. Universal and the man whip Panther to the ropes, Universal kicks Panther in the stomach, and the man hits a 'rocker dropper' on the Panther. Universal is handed a chair from Megan Dawn. The man brings Panther to his feet and Universal nails several steel chairs to the head. Panther falls to the mat. They bring Panther back to his feet. Universal climbs to the top rope... Dawn hands him the chair as Universal' "friend" holds the Black Panther. Universal flies off with a chair shot to the head of the Panther. Megan Dawn reaches inside her bra and pulls out a pair of handcuffs, and hands them to the duo. They handcuff Panther to the cage in "crucifixion" style fashion. Universal then asks for a microphone and receives one, and he begins to speak. "Well, well, well... I hate to say I told you so, but I fucking told you so. I told you I had friends, and I told you that you would meet one of them tonight on Warfare. I don't lie, because here he is right before your very eyes. Not only are you meeting him, Masked Retard, the whole f'n world is witness to the newest addition to the fWo roster. Let me cordially introduce to you my newest tag team partner and co-conspirator in crime... you're looking at the future of professional wrestling, because he IS "The Future" Lance Star! And together, we combine to make a cohesive unit that from this point on you may call the Hollywood Blondes. That's right. We are the Hollywood Blondes. We do what we want, when we want, wherever we want and there's not a damn thing anyone can do about it. And the first thing we want to do is to serve notice to each and every tag team on the fWo roster. From that Austin Powers wannabe and his hardcore bitch that call themselves the champions, to Ground Zero, to the French Foundation... and on down the line, nobody will be safe because we are here for one reason. And that reason is to take over the fWo's tag team division, and to put the thing back on the f'n map. We want to be the fWo World Tag Team Champions and I guaranfriggintee you that we will be the fWo World Tag Team Champions before we're done here. Let the destruction of the Panther tonight be a precursor to anyone who wants to screw around with the Hollywood Blondes. Thou shalt not fuck with the Hollywood Blondes... and those who do, will pay the price. Because each and every person on this planet has a price, but we're the only ones who can afford it... if you BUY..." Universal hands the microphone to Star, who finishes it off; "... what the Hollywood Blondes are selling." With that, Warfare came to a close with the Panther still handcuffed to the cage. Result: Black Panther
