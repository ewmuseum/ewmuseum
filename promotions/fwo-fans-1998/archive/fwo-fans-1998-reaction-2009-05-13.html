ReAction’s broadcast did not open with extravagant pyrotechnics; nor did the broadcast open with the ignition of fanfare, traveling spotlights, fast panning shots of the crowd, or even the FWO’s ritualistic B-Roll theme music. Rather than the usual excitement that follows the opening of FWO’s weekly show, fans are given nothing but darkness and silence. 4-weeks ago he was supposed to feel excited as well. In his mind, there was supposed to be a grand jubilation represented by bright colors, a fast heartbeat and a feeling of self gratification; but rather the usual excitement that follows a championship victory, this one man felt nothing but darkness. Krow sat upon a lone steel chair in the dead-center of the square circle. He had been seated there for quite some time prior to the live feed, and had garnered a few “BORING” chants to boot. However, these mere threats at insulting fell upon deaf ears, for Krow was not concerned with those around him. After all, only one spotlight shined…and it was on him. The rest of the world was dark and shadowed off, maybe for dramatic effect, or maybe because they just didn’t matter to him. Krow’s head was lowered and his eyes blankly stared at the glimmering golden strap that rested at his feet. The glorious FWO Hardcore Championship, a title that has just as long a history as the World Championship, is redeemed as a championship that tests the pain endurance, the physical strength, and the shear insanity of any of those who hold it. It is considered an honor to hold. Great names such as The Flying Frenchie, Austin Davis, Mike Bear, Hellrazor, Erik Kelly, Kodiak Vic Creed, and Graphic Violence allowed their career to soar to heights never before seen once holding a championship stained with blood and sweat of fallen warriors. But despite its magnificent record and previously mentioned accolade, Krow looked upon its shimmering golden laced in silvery strips in shame. ”Twenty-eight days,” he began with a solemn tone. “Twenty-eight days I have been forced to hold this championship and claim to be its keeper. But for 28 days I have felt nothing but that.” Krow lifted his head some and stared impassively at the abysmal darkness before him, his eyes showed signs of mental fatigue and physical drain through, ironically named, ‘crow’s feet’ and haggard darkened rings of sleep deprivation. ”In those 28 days, I’ve tried many things to feel right about holding this belt. I’ve annihilated a worthless piece of waste in the form of Fairview Reed, thinking that obliterating his spunk would make this championship feel right around my waist – but it did not. I’ve destroyed an FWO Legend’s legacy in the form of Tim Olsen, thinking that his pain, suffering and injuries would bring butterflies into my stomach – but it did not. I’ve even taken the pure innocence in the form of Tony Davis and ripped it to pieces, thinking I would feel a sense of orgasmic-esque pleasure from his agony – but I did not,” Krow said softly, unfazed by the mental and physical torments he placed upon innocent victims. ”And every week I am left with the question of: Do I deserve to be Hardcore Champion?” Krow rhetorically asks himself. “And during my self-interrogation, the F.W.O. roster decided to take matters into their own hands.” Krow lifted his eyes towards the abysmal darkness in front of him and pulled a white sheet of paper from his denim pocket. He rested the microphone onto his lap and unfolded the sheet of paper which was titled ”FWO Petition”, and below it was a slew of different wrestler’s names. Krow presented the paper towards the crowd for a moment, before bringing to his own face and lifting the microphone up with his free hand. ”What I present to you is exactly why I left this god forsaken business 5-years ago,” Krow began, his tone still soft and his face still expressionless as he read the contents of the paper. “What I have before me is something that I noticed on the door of one Ivy McGinnis on my way to the Key Arena tonight. This is a Petition signed by various FWO wrestlers; not a petition to have me fired or banned – for that would be cowardice,” Krow stated in a mocking tone. “Instead, this petition has posed a similar question to that of my own. But rather than questioning my legitimacy as a champion, the FWO has decided to question the legitimacy of the championship itself.” Krow lifted the paper up above his head. ”This is F.W.O.’s Petition to abolish the FWO Hardcore Championship.” The crowd fell into a mixture of cheers and jeers at the news, as Krow assured them that this Petition was absolutely real. He shook his head in disbelief as he read the names on the petition to himself, leading fans to lip-read in an attempt to reveal the petitioners to the masses. ”This, my friends, is what I have been dealing with for many, many years. While I go through a path of self-realization, that may or may not cause suffering to others, the F.W.O. responds the same way Action! Wrestling responded, the same way tSC responded, and the same way LoC responded,” Krow said, taking on a tone that mocked those who petitioned. “We aren’t trying to get rid of Krow…we are just trying to get rid of the division he primarily wrestles for.” ”In laymen’s terms, this is their “non-cowardly” way of trying to get rid their lives of impending pain and suffering; a way to get rid of me,” Krow commented, as a wicked grin began to form on his face. “Ironically enough, one of these names has played directly into my most recent revelation,” the Satanic God said as he lowered the petition to his side. “Last week, through my demolition of one Tony Davis, I was placed into a very intriguing situation with a certain champion.” The crowd immediately erupted in excitement, rumors of a Champion versus Champion contest ringing in their ears as the name “High Flyer” came up more than once within the sea of FWO fans. ”I have tried many things to make my championship feel at home in my grasp, but it wasn’t until I looked deep into the eyes of this champion that I realized that I was looking for self gratification in all of the wrong places.” “This champion took such pride in his rank as World Champion. He took such honor in being the “top dog” of this promotion; and when I looked into his eyes, I envied his pride. I envied his pride so much so that…” Dramatic pause. “I wanted it all to myself,” Krow’s voice grew rougher as he continued his speech. His eyes narrowed angrily as he snapped his head threateningly towards the entrance ramp. “I found that the only way I can feel pride like a champion…is to take it from another champion. And who better than the very individual who signed this paper?” The crowd erupted in excitement, as Krow’s voice grew threatening as he peered towards the entrance ramp. ”So if said champion wishes to abolish this championship, than I pose the question: Why not do it yourself?” The crowd buzzed. For a few seconds - an eternity in TV time - Krow sat and looked towards the entranceway, a small scowl on his face. And then a voice piped up from backstage. "It would be MY PLEASURE." Key Arena's decibel meter went into the red, and the fans got to their feet. Then it hit. "Marie-douceur, marie-colere." Were they angry at the bait and switch? You see, the world doesn't want you gone." Zimmerman suddenly leaned in and seemingly every single one of his teeth was showing, making Krow sneer at him. "JUST ME." You let the two biggest jobbers on the roster get two-counts on you and you beat up an old, fat and retired EX-wrestler. Then you wonder why you feel empty? Pond scum finds its own level. It's why you got these sheep to cheer you for a minute, after all." With his free hand, Keith gestured around them, and the arena bubbled up with jeers. "Save it, jackals. So, you're no champion. And you're damn sure no wrestler. Trash cans, tables, sheets of glass, steel chairs--that's how you got over. That's the only thing separating you from the rest of the PIW trash probably working the graveyard shift at Wal-Mart or whatever it is these sad people do with their lives when they're not pilliorying my good name." Keith leaned back once again, as Krow continued to scowl at him, and then Zimmerman put his feet right up on Krow's prize. The former PIW World Champion's eyes went wide, if only for a bit. "You see, you aren't even the reigning litmus test for hardcore around here. I've registered three awesome matches in what you call a division, and I've won them all. Hell, I won one with TWO MOVES. Any idiot with weaponry and a firing brain cell can do what you do. And as for the little petition there, well..." Keith gestured to Krow, who was still holding it in his free hand, and Krow's arm extended. Keith took the paper, after a bit of resistance. "...huh. Other people signed this. I didn't even pull my favorite Keith Scott Fong scam off, this is other people on the roster. Interesting!" Keith went over the paper, smiling as he did so. "You know what the difference between you & High Flyer is? He has something I want. Something of value. Something that belongs to me and by all rights should be mine, Krow. You see, when I win the Organization's crown jewel, it's going to be a career-defining moment, and the final nail in the career of that washed-up has-been Harmen." BOOO! Keith rolled his eyes at the incursion. "But you? This FOOTREST I'm so currently enjoying? Nothing to me. LESS than nothing, to be honest. Over a division who's style went out goddamn years ago and keeps the rest of the roster from having THEIR hardcore matches mean more?! You see, YOU didn't kill Action: I did. You didn't file the work for Craig to get his foot in the door and get Disney out, to snuff OCW before they could even spread their wings: that was ME. And you damn sure didn't see to it a boring chant got started during a Sonny Silver match one night, thus starting his downward spiral towards irrelevancy. What I find most galling about your ways, Krow?" Krow looked bemused and gestured to Keith, his much larger boots occupying space next to Zimmerman's. "They're SO 20th century. Sometimes, when I do something evil, it takes months, and even years to be fully executed. And yet." Krow grabbed his microphone. "And yet WHAT?" KSZ shook his head, offered a small smile. "And yet when I set out to do it, my enemies fall. And the result comes back once again: Zimmerman prevails." Keith stood up, causing Krow to do the same, and he spared a downward glance before looking back up at the man he loathed. "So, tonight, I'm going to solve every problem you've got, and I'm going to do it with panache. I am going to take you up on your offer for that Hardcore Championship match, and we're going to come back out here later, and you're going to have your first real title defense." Some boos. Some cheers. All noise. "This little pity party comes to a crashing end. There's going to be a rebirth of RESPECT for wrestling. MY wrestling. And if knocking Creed unconscious won't settle it...if nearly taking Bear's ear off is doomed to be subsumed by a goddamn time limit draw the very next week...if having a championship with the Frog's blood on it doesn't move the jackals...ohhhhh, Krow. OHHHHHHH, KROW!" Someone was getting downright jolly. "THIS WILL. I'm going to take everything bad you're feeling and I'm going to turn it into fuel. Fuel for YOU. Because starting next week, you'll be on a quest for vengeance and it'll never be achieved. Never to be made whole. You're going to be known as the catalyst for the most shocking moment in ReAction history: this night, May 13th, 2009, when Keith Scott Zimmerman pinned a battered, bloody Krow--" Krow had been almost smiling. "--and in a final act of spite...burned the Wo's HARDCORE CHAMPIONSHIP INTO NON-EXISTANCE." WHOA! Speechless announcers. Stunned crowd. And in the eye of this maelstrom stood one man, the man who'd started the show. Keith Scott Zimmerman reached down, grabbed the title, and-- --put it over Krow's shoulder. "Ashes to ashes, Krow. Dust to--" And just as Zimmerman went to close his speech, a monstrous and pale hand snapped up and covered the microphone violently. Zimmerman attempted to snatch it back, but Krow’s mighty grip, fueled by unmitigated anger, refused his request. The Internet Champion was ready to give one last, powerful yank to retrieve his mouth piece, but the amused chuckle of the Satanic God distracted him and attracted his ire. “That’s all it’s been with you, Keith,” Krow said, as he kept his eyes on the Hardcore Championship rested upon his shoulder. “That’s all it’s ever been with you, and the rest of the rats that crawled from the festering holes called tSC and Action. All you’ve ever done is complain about me.” Krow lifted the petition up a bit and extended it towards Keith Scott Zimmerman. ”And this proves it. If it weren’t for my challenge, Keith, you and the rest of the tSC rejects would simply hide behind legal petitions and backstage politics, just like you’ve been doing for the last half-decade. Let’s be honest, Keith,” Krow said as he slowly and menacingly lifted his head to express his amused glee towards Zimmerman, who scowled in defiance. “If I hadn’t called you out, you would’ve just hid behind this piece of paper and that big mouth of yours; just like the Jeff Garvin’s, just like the Max Danger’s, just like the Joey Malone’s, just like every moron who can apply a 3-minute headlock, cut an angry promo and work over a single body part for 20 minutes. You’d just complain, and complain, and complain.” Some jeered, some cheered; within the crowd there was a battle of PIW fans and tSC fans. The mixture of the crowd was never so apparent then when chants of each respective company began to do battle amongst the FWO crowd. KSZ and Krow, however, took no notice of their rants and instead kept their daunting stares fixated on one another. ”But I’ll give you credit, Keith. You are the very first to finally approach me,” Krow nodded slightly, blinking his eyes ever so softly. His face no longer showed the expressions of anger or rage, but rather of amused satisfaction. While his hatred for Keith Scott Zimmerman was immense, it only reminded him that such a match could fulfill his insatiable void of being champion – not only could he take the pride from a champion, but save his own. ”So I thank you, Keith. I thank you for finally coming forward as a representative of all my nay-sayers. I thank you, Keith, for coming forward as a challenger. I thank you, Keith…,” Krow’s grin dissipated a bit to one of a calmer nature as he slowly extended his pale hand towards Zimmerman, who retracted cautiously. “…for being their sacrifice.” ”And you and I both know that there is much more on the line than just the Hardcore Championship, Keith,” Krow said coldly as he hand extended further towards Zimmerman, who cautiously accepted it, making sure to share a tight grip with the sadistic fuck in front of him. Krow accepted the masculine gesture with one of his own, pulling Zimmerman in tighter and spreading his smile across his face in an almost Joker-ish manner. “Your pride is on the line as well.” The crowd stirred into hysteric cheers as Krow dropped his microphone to the ground. Cameras focused on the tension between Keith Scott Zimmerman and Krow, as their respective championships glistened in the spotlight. Krow’s smile had disappeared and was replaced by a daunting scowl. However, unlike other FWO competitors, who’d cower a bit in fear and self preservation, Zimmerman returned the scowl of discontent and tightened his grip even further. There was no fear between them, for hatred was a far stronger emotion than cowardice. Especially hatred of astronomical proportion. Since he had won the Silver Pyramid match, his mind was solely on one thing, finding a way to win the cruiserweight title. The conversation with Ruben Ross only made things worse for him, as he was sure that he couldn’t just avoid the champion and have her make a big enough mistake for him to pin her for the title. She was too smart and too talented for that. Plus, getting in the ring with her meant that there was a chance he could have a steel chair wrapped around his head again. That was definitely something he didn’t want to happen again. Who knows what screw could be knocked loose with another shot. It had been days since he had a good night’s sleep or was able to eat a decent meal. He couldn’t get the situation out of his head and his walk around the back was nothing more than an attempt to do so. He tried everything, but the usual methods of clearing his mind just weren’t working. Not even boobies were helping and that’s when he knew he was in it deep. He finally reached the locker room and pushed the door open. All that walking and thinking were for nothing, as wasn’t one Michelle Masters waiting for him once he entered the room. “You gotta be shitting me,” he grumbled to himself.
