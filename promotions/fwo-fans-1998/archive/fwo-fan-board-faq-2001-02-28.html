fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization, Fan Message Board Posting Guidelines Fan Board FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions Frequently Asked Questions About Joining The fWo Can I join the fWo? Currently, we are not bringing new members in, although look to around November or December. The waiting list has closed officially today on November 3rd, 2000. People that joined the waiting list before today and made the deadline will be brought in before anyone else. After each application of the waiting list has been reviewed, and we've decided who to bring in, the fWo will bring in people on an as needed basis. Frequently Asked Questions About Poser Can you help me with Poser? No. We simply do not have enough time or energy to help anyone; and a lot of people send us mail asking us that. Please note that the Poser character meshes of the fWo wrestlers currently on the roster were created by someone with over three years worth of experience working with Poser, and several years of training in art. For any help on Poser we recommend trying the following sites: PLANET 3D http://www.planet-3d.com/ ADDING CUSTOM CLOTHES TO POSER CHARACTERS http://www.ruku.com/posercls.html LESSONS ON POSER http://www.3dtotal.com/ffa/tutorials/tutorialsposer.htm POSER TUTORIAL AND FORUM http://www.iguanasoft.com/poser/tutor.htm INDEX OF POSER HELP SITES http://www.probe.net/~lannie/poser4.htm They will certainly be able to help you out more than we can. It may not be much but it's a start, and more than we can do at this present time. Please do not send us mail about Poser. If you do, and you are on the waiting list or later ask to be on the waiting list, you will either be taken off the waiting list or not added if you do later ask. Will you help me with my site in any fashion, such as designing art for my site? Again, sorry, but we can't. We simply put too much work into the fWo and that is sometimes more than we can handle. Other Frequently Asked Questions Will the fWo ever start a minor league? Likely not. Can I write a column for the site? We get this question a lot. Generally, we have no problem with it if you have exceptional writing skills and your columns are written professionally. Please see our current columns for a good idea of how we would like for any columns to appear. Can I write matches for the fWo? At the present time we are not looking to add any new members to the fWo writing team. This can change at any moment however, and we are looking to add new members we will make a note of it here on the fWo Fan Board. Can you help me with my site? Sorry, our main priority is keeping the fWo up and running, and that consumes a large portion of our time. However, we refer you to http://www.kayfabe.com, where there you can inquire about having help for your site. Now that we have all of that covered feel free to ask any questions on the Fan Board that we haven't covered. We've covered Poser & joining the fWo in depth, so please do not use the fan board to ask about it, nor e-mail us about it. Any e-mails regarding joining the fWo or Poser will not be answered, so don't waste your time.
