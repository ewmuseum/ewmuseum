Sunday Slaughter, 23 April 2000

23 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Outside the Arrowhead Pond, site of CyberSlam I, a large black limousine pulled up aside the arena. Small French flags decorated the limo and flapped in the wind as the back door of the limo opened. Out stepped the French Foundation, looking ready for their matches tonight. As Frenchie began heading towards the arena, he wondered aloud, "What exactly is wit' zat Lance Star, anyhow?" Courage shrugged. "He's a moron." Primetime nodded and adjusted the Internet Title he wore around his waist. The trio walked on, but they were suddenly stopped by a platoon of what appeared to be armed Anaheim PD officers. "Zut! What is the meaning of zis?" The officers suddenly parted to let through none other than Mitch Wilson, who turned to Primetime. "Hey, Brian. Nice to see you again." Primetime glared, and Wilson continued. "If you've been wondering where I've been the past week... well, I've been working on an agreement between Utah, Arizona, Nevada, and California law enforcement that... well, let's just say it's not a good thing for you." To the side of Primetime, Frenchie snorted. "What ze 'ell are you talking about, putain?" Wilson turned an icy stare at Frenchie, who returned it, before turning back to Primetime. "Well, as of right now, Brian, you're under arrest." The officers moved forward, some separating the rest of the Foundation from Primetime, and the others cuffing the superstar. "You'll spend tonight in jail here in Anaheim... then in the morning you'll be extradited to Utah where you'll face charges of vandalism - my Academy. In the week or so after that you'll visit Arizona where you'll face charges of theft - my title - and then Nevada, where you'll face more vandalism charges. Good luck making it to CyberSlam." The officers finished cuffing Primetime and lead him to a nearby squad car. Courage protested, but Primetime shrugged it off. "No biggie... just make sure you guys remember to post bail for me and it'll be fine." Mitch Wilson turned to the cops and congratulated them. "Good job, men... you've ended this hooligan's three-state crime spree. Oh, yeah... I think one thing has been forgotten, though." Mitch turned to Primetime and addressed him with a sneer. "The belt, please? Oh wait, you can't give it to me... you're cuffed. How stupid of me to forget." Wilson quickly walked over to Primetime and unsnapped the title. "Book him, boys." The officers ushered Primetime into the squad car, which drove off into the night as Wilson buckled the Internet Title around his waist. A few of the officers remained to seperate the Foundation and Wilson, and both headed off to seperate entrances, the Foundation fuming and Wilson smirking. Inside the arena loud fireworks exploded as Slaughter went to the ring for the first match of the night... Courage & Future Frenchie vs. NXS Slaughter returns from commercials, as air raid sirens play throughout the arena. Judge Steel, and Markus Cairn, collectively known as NXS. Steel and Cairn continue to the ring, then "Surfacing" by Slipknot starts up, and out walks the cocky French Foundation member, Courage. Courage taunts the crowd, when "Juke Joint Jezebel" starts playing, and for the second straight event, Lance Star steps out onto the entrance ramp, dressed as "The Future Frenchie". Clad in beret, goggles, and everything else that Frenchie wears, Star makes his way to the ring, alongside Courage. Courage starts the match off, up against Steel. Courage gains control, with an irish whip. Steel bounces off, and Courage leapfrogs. Steel comes back, once again, and this time Courage takes him over with an armdrag. Steel bounces up, and Courage plants him with a dropkick. Steel recovers to his corner, and tags out to Cairn. Cairn challenges Courage to a test of strength, and Courage obliges. Instead, Cairn nails a boot to the gut, and sends Courage into the ropes. Courage comes back, and is met by a Cairn clothesline, followed by a quick legdrop. He taunts the Future Frenchie, and then whips Courage into the ropes, Courage ducks a clothesline, and then nails Cairn with a flying shouldertackle. Courage then goes over, and tags in Star. Star comes in, and nails Cairn with a few right hands, before taking him over with a snap suplex. Star whips him in, and goes for a huracanrana, but he can't get Cairn over. Cairn plants Star with a sitdown powerbomb, right into a cover. The ref counts, but the Future Frenchie gets a shoulder up at two. Cairn decides to tag out to Steel, who goes right to work on Star. He whips Star in, and nails him with a back elbow, that sends him reeling. He picks Lance up for a powerslam, but Lance shimmies out of it. Lance grabs Steel in a waistlock, and takes him over with a nice looking German suplex. However, when he bridges for a cover, Cairn comes in and nails a boot to the stomach of Star. This allows Steel to be able to tag out to Cairn. Cairn comes in and whips Star into the ropes. Cairn catches Star by the throat, and looks to plant him with a chokeslam. However, Star nails a boot below the belt, and then runs at Cairn for a clothesline. He connects, but at the same time, Cairn tried his own clothesline, and also connected. Star is the one who recovers first, and slides to his corner. He tags in Courage, and at the same time Steel steps into the ring. Courage comes in, a house of fire, and nails both Steel, and Cairn, the legal man. Courage takes Cairn down with a spin-wheel kick, before running to the other side. He comes off with the springboard asai moonsault, commonly known as the Courage Required. However, instead of going for a cover, he points up to the rafters, and gives a knowing look to Lance. Star climbs the turnbuckles, and comes off with a 450 splash. He lands perfectly on top of Cairn, and the referee makes the three count. Star celebrates, and eventually, Courage goes over, and shakes his hand, and the team walks up the entrance ramp--together, as Slaughter cuts to a commercial. Result: Courage & Future Frenchie by PInfall Cactus Jon vs. Ric Chronos "Christian Says" by Tones On Tail took to the speakers, which brought out The Man of the Hour, Ric Chronos. The fans greeted him with cheers, as he took his time walking down the ramp. Chronos slapped a couple of hands before sliding into the ring. The beats from Tones On Tail were faded out, and replaced by Result: Ric Chronos by Pinfall Wretch vs. Crusader Biohazard blasts over the PA system as Crusader walks his way down toward ringside. The newcomer is cheered loudly by the crowd, something that pleases him as he slides into the ring. He awaits his opponent, whose music, "Paint it Black" by Gob hits, and boos begin to ring throughout the arena. The International Champion Wretch comes down toward ringside. He isn't alone, and it's not just Scott Hart that's walking with him...a full set of riot police are by his side as well. He climbs up onto the apron and taunts Crusader, before climbing inside for this non-title match. The bell starts, and the two immediately begin to go at it. They trade blows, as Wretch sends Crusader into the ropes. Wretch goes for a clothesline, but misses. Crusader ducks and continues toward the ropes. Wretch follows him, hoping to hit a cross body block and send Crusader over the ropes. However, Crusader anticipated it, hearing the footsteps of Wretch, and drops to the mat as Wretch takes off. Wretch flies over the top rope and flies onto the announcers' table, crushing it to the ground. Wretch takes a minute to get up, before yelling at the riot police, telling them that they should be "taking out this chump." The riot squad look at each other in confusement. Meanwhile, Crusader sees an opportunity, and climbs to the top rope. Scott Hart sends Wretch's attention to the top rope, where Crusader comes flying off with a missile dropkick. Crusader gets up and tosses Wretch back into the ring. Crusader gets up and prepares to set himself up for a springboard moonsault over the ropes. However, Scott Hart grabs the leg of Crusader, and sends him crashing to the mat. Wretch gains his senses and gets up at the same time Crusader is...Wretch sends a dropkick to the knees of Crusader, dropping him back to the canvas. Wretch picks up Crusader and sends him to the ropes. Crusader returns and receives a kick to the gut. Wretch then delivers a devastating swinging neckbreaker. Wretch goes for a cover, but only gets a two. He gets up and picks up Crusader, walking him over to the turnbuckle. He sets him up on the second rope, and positions himself for a hurricanrana. He locks it in and hits it. Wretch immediately goes for another cover, but gets two-and-a-half before another kickout. Wretch glares at the ref, and motions to the riot squad to do something about him. The squad stand around, confused once again at why Wretch is talking to them. Wretch just throws his arms in the air and walks back over to Crusader. Wretch hits a swift legdrop over the chest of Crusader. He then picks him up and hits a backbreaker to Crusader. Wretch senses that this match is his, and picks up Crusader for the Depression, his finishing reverse DDT. As he locks him in, something comes up on the SlaughterScreen. Wretch drops Crusader to see the image of Mike Bear on the screen. Bear is walking around the back, apparently toward the ring. Not only that, but he seems to be carrying a drill with him. Laughter can be heard echoing the halls of the arena. Wretch looks at the riot squad and tell them to secure the area, so that Bear cannot get around him. The riot squad, not exactly the coldest ice in the tray, start to walk up the ramp, obviously confused by what Wretch has told him. Wretch starts to scream at them, and doesn't realize that Crusader is crawling towards him. Crusader grabs the legs of Wretch, and rolls him up in a small package. The ref, in perfect position, counts to three, and Crusader grabs a surprise victory over Wretch. Wretch cannot believe this, but doesn't have time to complain. He and Scott Hart take off through the crowd as Crusader celebrates in the ring. Meanwhile, Slaughter heads to a break. Result: Crusader by Pinfall Flying Frenchie vs. Erik Kelly "No Leaf Clover" sends the fWo cameras to the entrance ramp, where Erik Kelly is walking toward the ring. Alongside him, as always, is the lovely Pamela Dee, who is wearing an overcoat. They are accompanied by the cheers of the crowd, save for one man at ringside in a beret shouting (in full French accent) "Zut! You stink up ze place, you silly Kelly. I pass gas in your general direction!" Obviously, this man is awaiting the sounds of "Juke Joint Jezebel", which immediately begin to play over the speakers, and cheers turn to a more negative sounding reaction as the Flying Frenchie makes his way to ringside. He has a cocky look on his face, and is confident that success will prevail for him in this match. He slides in the ring, gives a sneer to Kelly, and a smile to Pamela. The bell sounds, and the match starts. The two lockup, with Frenchie getting the upperhand with a swift headlock. Kelly uses his power to break out of it, and turns it into an armbar. TFF flips out of it and the two lock up again. Again, TFF gets the upperhand, and Kelly manuevers himself to the ropes, and sends the Frenchie across the ring. TFF hits the ropes and sees Kelly getting ready for a shoulderblock. TFF slides under the feet of Kelly and sets up for a drop kick. However, as the Frenchie goes for it, Kelly sidesteps it, and catches TFF. He hits a bodyslam on TFF, knocking the wind out of the French Foundation leader. Kelly stands up and moves toward the ropes...a legdrop is coming. Kelly jumps and hits it. He picks up the Frenchie and sends him toward the ropes. Kelly goes for the Clothesline from Hell, but TFF ducks it. Kelly turns around and receives a super kick from the Frenchie. TFF goes for a cover, but only gets at two. TFF yells at the ref about a slow cover, but decides to continue his offensive onsluaght. He picks up Kelly and kicks him in the gut. He takes off for the ropes, and nails him with a bulldog upon his return. He goes for another cover , and gets two and a half. He again yells at the ref, but it's a moot point. He picks up Kelly and sends him to the ropes once more. As Kelly comes back, TFF hits him with a cross body block. Another cover, and another two-count. TFF is visably upset at this development. He picks up Kelly and sends him to the ropes. Kelly gets his bearings a little bit and goes for a clothesline. TFF ducks it and looks for another superkick; however, this time he's caught with a hand around his neck. Kelly wants to go for a chokeslam. TFF, thinking quickly, kicks Kelly to the gut, and hits a swinging neckbreaker. The Flying Frenchie takes off for the turnbuckle...time for the 450 splash, a prelude to the Guillotine Legdrop. TFF positions himself on the top turnbuckle and takes off. He nails it, and the crowd goes wild, in appreciation of such a move. TFF signals to the top once more, this time for the legdrop. As he climbs the turnbuckle, Pamela Dee gets up on the apron. Dee motions toward her overcoat, and TFF stops his train of thought. His eyes focus on Dee, who is starting to pull at her overcoat strap, which would undo the coat. TFF seems very interested in this, and pretty much forgets what else is going on. Pamela Dee continues to visually tease TFF, who is extremely distracted by the beautiful woman. Unfortunately for TFF, he doesn't realize that Erik Kelly has risen and is coming his way. Kelly grabs TFF, who suddenly comes back to earth. Kelly grabs TFF around the neck and pulls him off the turnbuckle. He nails the chokeslam from the top rope, crushing TFF to the mat. Kelly looks around and signals for his finisher, the Eradicator. He picks up the Flying Frenchie, and sets him up for the powerbomb. He picks up the Frenchie, yells "POWERBOMB!", and slams TFF to the mat. He covers TFF, and the ref counts the three, thus making Erik Kelly the winner. Kelly and Dee celebrate on their way up the entrance ramp. Meanwhile, TFF has gotten up and has started to get in the ref's face about a fast count. The ref ignores TFF's words...unfortunately for the ref, he can't ignore TFF's blows to the head. TFF gives repeated shots to the head of the ref, and sends him directly to the canvas. TFF goes up to the top turnbuckle, and immediately hits a Guillotine Legdrop. He goes up for another as security and officials run down to the ringside, breaking up the mini-melee...and Slaughter heads back to a commercial. Result: Erik Kelly by Pinfall Dead vs. Eron The final Sunday Slaughter before CyberSlam returns from it's final set of commercials, to the sounds of "Physical" by Nine Inch Nails. This means only one thing, "Mr. Screwjob" himself, Eron the Relentless steps out onto the entrance ramp, basking in the jeering of the crowd. The read-headed individual continues to the ring, for his World Title match with Dead. Then, Marilyn Manson's "Beautiful People" starts up, and clad in black trenchcoat, the fWo World Champion, Dead, steps mythodically onto the entrance ramp. He holds up the fWo World Title with one arm, and walks to the ring. He steps up onto the ring-steps, and removes his trenchcoat. The bell rang. The fWo World Title is on the line. Dead paces around the ring for awhile, before he and Eron lock up. Dead uses his power, and throws Eron into the corner. A punch to the face brings Eron down, and Dead begins using those punches more and more, knocking Eron down to the mat. He lifts Eron's limp body, and sets him in the tree of woe. Dead walks to the other corner, then runs at Eron, nailing a knee right to the man's sternum area. Eron begins coughing, but this doesn't stop Dead. Dead unhooks Eron, and whips him into the ropes. Eron ducks a clothesline, and comes back with a flying forearm. This is unable to bring Dead down, so Eron tries again. A second forearm, and once again, Dead will not go down. Eron runs at Dead again, looking for a clothesline. This time, Dead is able to catch Eron by the throat, and brings him down with a violent looking chokeslam. Dead laughs, before covering Eron. One, two, but somehow, Eron gets a shoulder up. Dead goes back to the drawing board, with an irish whip. Eron comes back, and is caught with a big ring-shaking powerslam. Instead of covering, however, Dead gets an idea. Dead slides out of the ring, and grabs a steel chair. He sets it in the middle of the ring. He goes back over to Eron, and perches him on the top turnbuckle. Dead climbs the turnbuckles himself, and gets behind Eron. He turns Eron around, and places him over his shoulder. Dead comes off with a perfect top-rope powerslam. The back of Eron's head hits the chair, and Dead climbs on top for the cover. One. Two. Instead of picking up the win, Dead lifts Eron up, looking to inflict more punishment on the already battered Eron. Dead throws Eron's limp body to the outside of the ring, and then gives chase. He whips Eron into the guardrail, and follows that up by picking up the ring steps. Dead uses the steps to his advantage, and nails Eron in the head with the edge of it. Eron begins flowing blood, and Dead just stares at him, and laughs. Dead rolls Eron into the ring, and once again, instead of covering, he goes to work on Eron a little more. Dead places Eron on his back, and drops him with a nicely executed Samoan Drop. Once again, he lifts Eron up by the hair. He plants him with a single arm DDT. Dead covers, but at 2, he lifts Eron up by his red locks. This time, Dead plants Eron with an inverted hangman. Again, Dead avoids a cover. He perches Eron on the turnbuckles. A superplex follows, but somehow, Eron lands on top with a cover, but Dead easily kicks out at one. Dead picks Eron up, and nails him with his own version of a sky-high. Dead decides to take Eron back to the top once again. This time, a top-rope brainbuster does the trick, and Dead connects. Dead picks Eron back up by the throat, and chokeslams him to the mat. Dead picks Eron up AGAIN, and plants him with a piledriver. Dead grabs the mic, and announces "I'm done playing games. I'm done fucking around with this no-talent hack. Hey Paul, just think, Paul, that in 6 days, this will be you. You Paul. You." With that Dead picks Eron up, and sets him up for his finisher, the Crucifixion. Suddenly, from the crowd, the Boston Strangler comes, and grabs Dead by the legs. Dead turns around, and meets a Strangler chairshot. Dead hits the mat, and Eron awakens, seeing that he is about to become the World Champion. Eron covers. One. Two. No, the Strangler enters with the chair, and wallomps Eron over the head. Eron falls to the mat, and Strangler places Dead's arm over Eron, and the referee makes the three count. The Strangler gets down by Dead, and tells him "It's the last one you're ever going to get. Tonight you leave with that belt... because we let you", as Sunday Slaughter goes off of the air. Result: Dead by Pinfall
