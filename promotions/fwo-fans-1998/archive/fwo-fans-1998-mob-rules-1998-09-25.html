Mob Rules 1998, 25 September 1998

25 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jackhammer, God's Gift, Wolf Fang & Black Quicksilver vs. Primetime, Shocker, ShowStopper & Scott Slugger The match began with Black Quicksilver coming out with the team instead of the rumored Surgeon. On Primetime's team, Scott Slugger was the mystery partner. The match progressed with Jackhammer's team being the men on top, until Primetime was able to score a pinfall on God's Gift, who went insane and attacked his own team before leaving enraged through the crowd. A weakened Jackhammer and Wolf Fang fell to the Shocker and Showstopper respectively, but were eliminated by a fresh Black Quicksilver and Stump. However, before Black Quicksilver could do any more damage, Scott Slugger and Primetime were able to put the man away and be the winners and survivors of the match. Result: Primetime & Scott Slugger Briana & Lone Gunmen vs. Flying Frenchie, Falcon, VL and Dark Angel In a surprise move, Darkness did not come to the ring, and was replaced by the Falcon, much to the dismay of the Flying Frenchie. On the other team, Queen Bee was nowhere to be found, and the team was at once at a disadvantage. Briana was able to rally her team however, and put the Dark Angel away with some assistance from the Lone Gunmen. VL eliminated Rudo, and Briana seemed to be making a comeback by eliminating VL, when Queen Bee entered, as part of the Dark Riders. Queen Bee attacked Briana and cost her the match. The Falcon proceeded to work over Machina, and he and the Flying Frenchie used strong teamwork to keep the Lone Gunman at bay. However, the Flying Frenchie then nailed the Falcon with a chair, causing Machina to eliminate him. The Frenchie quickly entered the ring and eliminated the very weakened Machina to become the sole survivor. Result: Flying Frenchie Ultra Cool, Stevie Roberts & Chris Universal vs. Brawler, Billy James, Great Waru & Austin Davis The match started strong, with Stevie Roberts and Ultra Cool doing away with nearly all of the Dark Riders, except for the mystery partner, who was a large masked man. Roberts was first eliminated by teh masked man, and then Chris Universal entered the ring and unmasked the Dark Rider to reveal Austin Davis. Davis pinned Universal, and left the ring, causing himself to be disqualified. Death came out to congradulate Austin Davis, who attacked Death, and then attacked Jim Dudley, proving that nobody could trust the superstar who was thought to be retired from the fWo. Both members of Ultra Cool survived the match via count out. Result: Ultra Cool Ultra Cool vs. Flying Frenchie, Primetime & Scott Slugger A weary Ultra Cool were already at a disadvantage, given that they had just come off of a tough match. The Flying Frenchie was first eliminated after the Falcon entered and hit him in the head. Ultra Violet scored the pinfall quickly without realizing what had occured. Scott Slugger pinned a weary Ultra Violet, and tagged in Primetime. Scotty Cool rallied and pinned both Primetime and Scott Slugger to become the sole survivor, and would go on to face Jim Dudley at the next event.
