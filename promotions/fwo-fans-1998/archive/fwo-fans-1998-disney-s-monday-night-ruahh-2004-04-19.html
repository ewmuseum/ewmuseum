She sent an important message to Aimz earlier tonight, a message that drove their rivalry deep into the skin. Crusader nodded to her. "It was a good night," he said. "We're going to celebrate tonight." The copyright notice appeared on the screen as Crusader and Nerva exited the ring and walked up the ramp as the winners tonight. Result: Crusader and Crisp via pinfall; Crusader pins Black Quicksilver
