fWo — Card

FlashBack, 15 November 1999

15 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The cameras opened on Chris Finn, sitting inside fWo studios with a large row of monitors behind him, all showing highlights from Wednesday Warfare and Sunday Slaughter. "Hello everyone and welcome to fWo FlashBack for November 15th, 1999. I'm Chris Finn and we've got a lot to talk about this hour. First off I'd like to wish a very happy birthday to Melissa Smalls, who had her birthday a roughly a week ago on the 7th, and to the Black Panther who celebrated his birthday yesterday. The Black Panther worked on his birthday, putting on one of the best triple threat matches the fWo has ever seen. Before we get started I'd like to address some phone calls we got last week. fWo FlashBack is currently a bi-weekly program, meaning that it hits the air every other week. We hope to bring you a weekly show in the very near future, but for now it's every two weeks. Well, it's been a rather stunning two weeks in the fWo as we head into the top of the month and BattleBowl just around the corner. We're going to be announcing some names for the special BattleBowl battle royal tonight, as well as two new matches for the card. As always you can surf onto the web at fWowrestling.com for all your fWo needs, and to keep up to date on the latest events in the fWo. We've got a lot of news tonight, a very special interview, some other surprises, and right now a very special Classic fWo match. Tonight's match comes from the 1998 BattleBowl event, and it was a special attraction tag team match between Darkness and the Boston Strangler teaming up to take on Oracle and Prophet... Darkness & Boston Strangler vs. Oracle & Prophet Darkness and the Strangler came to the ring separately, looking uncertain of each other and of their teaming. Oracle and Prophet came out to a smoke filled arena and blue flames shooting from the ring posts. Oracle and Darkness started off the match, with the larger Darkness dominating the smaller but faster Oracle. Oracle tagged in the large and mysterious Prophet, who fought back with a double axe handle and stiff chops in the corner. Reluctantly, Darkness tagged in the Boston Strangler, who took the big man off his feet and applied various submission holds to keep the large man grounded. As the Strangler was sent into the ropes, he accidentally hit Darkness, who was watching a strange man in the top balcony unroll a large sign. An angry Darkness struck out at the Strangler in the misunderstanding. Oracle was tagged in and attempted to use the distraction to pin the Strangler, who was able to kick out. Darkness came into the ring after an unfriendly tag, and proceeded to once again maul Oracle and the Prophet. As Darkness put Oracle in a sleeper hold, Oracle used the ropes to kick himself backward, and sent Darkness sprawling into the corner, where he bumped the Strangler to the floor. As the Strangler stood and yelled at Darkness, Oracle attacked from behind with the crystal ball, unknown and unseen by the ref. Oracle pinned Darkness as the Strangler, annoyed, walked away from the ring, claiming that he was his own best tag team partner. Result: Oracle & Prophet by Pinfall Comments from Darkness The fWo returned from the Classic fWo match from BattleBowl to find Chris Finn on the phone with fWo superstar Darkness. Chris Finn asked, "Darkness, that night we saw you and the Boston Strangler team up in a very unlikely tag team combination. Could you tell us a little more about what that night meant to both of you?" On the speaker phone the dry and always ominous voice of Darkness was heard. "Well Finn, the Strangler and I were trying this tag team thing then but as history showed us it didn't work out for long. The Boston Strangler and I had some of the most brutal battles the fWo has ever seen, and I assure all of you that before it's all said and done we will meet in the ring again. That night Oracle and Prophet got very lucky... but it wouldn't happen again. As you remember I got my revenge on Oracle, and the coward never faced me again after that." Chris Finn nodded on the other end of the line. "Rumor abounds that Oracle has left the fWo, along with his large on again, off again partner Prophet. Do you know anything more about it?" Darkness paused for a moment, and then answered. "Finn, I don't have any idea what those two are up two and frankly I don't care. I'm taking a much needed vacation from the fWo right now and I'm not scheduled to return until late this year... a few days before the end of the year. Oracle and Prophet never seemed to have a clear idea of what they wanted in the fWo, and nobody is sad to see them gone. Now as for the Strangler... I understand he's in a hospital somewhere thanks to the fWo World Champion Primetime. Well I can't imagine a more stupid move than that... when the Strangler gets his hands on Primetime... and he will... you can bet that there is going to be blood... lots of blood." Chris Finn signed off and addressed the camera once again. "Thanks to Darkness for talking with us. We will have an update as to the Strangler's condition later on tonight, but right now we want to take you to a match taped exclusively for FlashBack on last week's Wednesday Warfare." VL & Stevie Roberts vs. Smiletastic The former fWo Tag Team Champions Doomsday came to the ring, Stevie looking cocky and VL walking smugly behind him. As they entered the ring Joy and Glee offered to shake hands, but Steve and VL simply ignored them and continued to pose for the crowd. From behind Joy and Glee attacked, punching weakly at their backs. The challengers stayed on the attack, double teaming Stevie as VL rolled to the outside for safety. The ref finally was able to get Joy out of the ring, as Glee attacked with a brutal snap suplex that sent Roberts to the mat with authority. Stevie attacked back with a blow to the groin, and hit a rocker dropper on Glee as he stood dazed. Roberts tagged in VL, and then hoisted Glee atop his shoulders inside the ring. VL delivered a jumping lariat from the top rope, and went for a cover. Joy broke the count, and VL tossed him from the ring for his interference. VL then hit a DDT on Glee, and covered him in the center of the ring. A quick three count was made, and VL and Stevie left the ring the victors. Inside the ring Joy commented that Glee hadn't been smiling enough, and next time they wrestle just turn their frowns upside down and everything will be super peachy keen. Result: VL & Stevie Roberts by Pinfall fWo Ranking When the cameras returned to the studio Matt Loco was sitting next to the rankings for the week. "Hello fans! I'm Matt Loco, and I've got the fWo rankings for the week. Remember, the way this chart works is that every wrestler who won a match moves up three notches on the chart. A loss moves the wrestler down three notches. If the wrestler wasn't on the chart, they start at number 20. If the wrestler didn't wrestle, they move down one notch. At the end the numbers are adjusted to make a twenty count again. Remember, we had four shows to go over, so let's get to it." Loco held up the chart, which read: 1. Death (Collins) (c) 2. Mike Bear (c) 3. Flying Frenchie 4. Primetime 5. Scott Slugger (c) 6. Black Panther 7. Wretch (c) 8. Machina 9. Eron (c) 10. Boston Strangler 11. Rain 12. Chris Universal 13. Mad Trucker (c) 14. Cactus Jon 15. Jackhammer 16. Ultra Violet 17. Havok, 18. Lance Star (c) 19. Black Quicksilver 20. Fallen Over: Courage, Micky Maliboo, Crucifix, Hiroshima, Poison Matt Loco looked back at the cameras. "Join us in two weeks when I'll go over the rankings again. Thanks and good night!" The cameras faded off into another segment... Match of the Week Upon returning to the control room Chris Finn was seated with recently returned fWo wrestler Wolf Fang. Chris Finn noticed that the camera was filming, and addressed the fans. "Folks, thanks for sticking with the fWo and FlashBack. I'm here with Wolf Fang, and we're about to show the fWo Match of the Week, which saw Wolf Fang step up to the plate and take the Boston Strangler's place against Primetime. Wolf Fang, thanks for joining us. Now, you were gone for some time from the fWo... can you tell us where you were?" Wolf Fang glanced at Finn, and nodded. "Finn, I'd been in the fWo for over a year, and during that time I had little time to improve myself inside the ring. The fWo has a brutal schedule for their wrestlers, and I was in need of going out, getting some training in some new arts of the squared circle, and that's what I did. I've always been a strong technical wrestler, but I got the chance to go to Japan and train with some wrestlers there. I'd like to shout out to Toshiaki Kawada, who trained with me while I did a short All Japan tour. Good training man, and you can see it put to good use in this match." Finn nodded, and rolled film of the fWo Match of the Week, from the November 9th Wednesday Warfare. Primetime (c) vs. Wolf Fang Wolf Fang came to the ring, waving to the fans and excited about his World Title opportunity. Wolf Fang looked more fit than he had in past days, and the break from active competition looked to have served him well. It was the main event, and the fans sat on the edge of their seats or stood on their feet in anticipation. Primetime came out onto the entrance ramp, and stood for a moment, drinking in the cheers and boos as he wore the fWo World Heavyweight title around his waist. Primetime walked to the ring confidently, looking at his old enemy with a mixture of amusement and disgust. His expression was torn violently from his face as Wolf Fang leapt from the ring with a diving plancha that hit Primetime head-on outside the ring. Wolf Fang grabbed Primetime by the air and slung him into the ring, quickly making a cover. It was much too early, and Primetime kicked out before the ref could get in position. Still taking advantage of the momentum he had made, Wolf Fang hit Primetime with a series of palm blows, making the chest grow red with the impact. This made Primetime angry, and he attacked back with a forearm smash into the head of the challenger. Wolf Fang wisely escaped to the outside, but Primetime served up some of Wolf Fang's own medicine with a tope. Now on the outside Primetime whipped Wolf Fang into the steel. Wolf Fang held the position, and Primetime hit a missile drop kick. Primetime rolled Wolf Fang back into the ring, and then took a few moments to gloat to the crowd. As he finally rolled back into the ring Wolf Fang caught him, kicking down on the champion and then locking his arms and executing a German suplex, followed by a reverse DDT as Primetime rose. Primetime lay still as Wolf Fang climbed to the top rope, hands outstretched to the crowd. The cameras flashed as Wolf Fang dove, but he missed a diving head butt as Primetime rolled free at the last moment. Both men lay still, trying to get back to their feet. The ref lay on the ten count, but Wolf Fang showed signs of life and got to his feet first. Wolf Fang helped Primetime up, and then dropkicked the champion's knee. Wolf Fang attempted a Dragon screw, but Primetime blocked it. Wolf Fang kicked at the kneecap, and the crowd gasped as the challenger blew out the knee of the champion. Wolf Fang executed the Dragon screw, and then locked on a knee cross hold, going for a tap out. Primetime struggled to hang on, and finally powered out of the hold in the center of the ring. Primetime held his knee as Wolf Fang argued with the referee. Wolf Fang went back to the champion and kept on working on his leg and knee, trying to demolish his vertical base. With Primetime weakened and having trouble standing, Wolf Fang took the advantage and power bombed Primetime to the outside of the ring. Wolf Fang climbed up to the top rope, and then executed a rolling senton from the top turnbuckle to the floor. Wolf Fang removed his elbow pad and threw it out to the ring, finally rolling the champion back into the ring for the kill. Back in the ring, Wolf Fang hit a brain buster on Primetime and went up to the top rope again. Again Wolf Fang extended his arms, but again he missed the diving head butt as Primetime rolled free at the last second. Both men lay still on the mat, until Primetime finally made it to the ropes and used them to stand. Wobbling, Primetime ran to the ropes but on the return Wolf Fang welcomed him with another big palm-blow. Yelling now, Wolf Fang charged and hit a running Fang bomb. Wolf Fang fell upon the champion with a loose cover, and at the last second Primetime got a shoulder up, a split second before losing the World Title to his old enemy. Breathing hard, Wolf Fang picked up the champion and placed Primetime on the top rope, and hit brain buster that hurt both men. After an extended period, Wolf Fang draped an arm over Primetime's chest, and again Primetime kicked out at the last second. Wolf Fang helped Primetime up, and placed Primetime on the top rope again, this time looking for a frankensteiner. Primetime blocked the move, holding onto the ropes, and hitting back with a head butt. Wolf Fang stumbled back, and Primetime acted quickly with a swinging DDT, and then a reverse brain buster. Primetime fell upon Wolf Fang, but the challenger got an arm up at two. Both men got to their feet, and both men ran to the ropes, but it was Primetime who ducked the lariat and hit a dropkick on the return. Primetime pointed into the air, and picked Wolf Fang up and hit a power bomb. Primetime hooked the leg, and Wolf Fang kicked free a second too late. Primetime rolled away, as the fWo World title was placed on his chest. The cameras went off the air with a last look at Primetime and Wolf Fang... both men exhausted and physically spent... Result: Primetime by Pinfall More commentary The match concluded, the cameras returned to the fWo studios with Chris Finn and Wolf Fang. "An impressive match, Wolf Fang... obviously your time off did well by you. Now, on Slaughter we saw another match that you wrestled in that also happened to be the main event... this time with the Black Panther and Havok. Now is there some sort of unit there? And what about Ground Zero, the team you were in with Jackhammer?" Wolf Fang paused for a moment, and then spoke. "First off... Jackhammer and I are still friends and we will still team from time to time. Jackhammer is currently involved with Black Quicksilver and Briana chasing Silver Fox and those guys... meanwhile I've found a couple of kindred spirits in the Black Panther and Havok. The Panther has been a legend in the fWo so far, and Havok's a great athlete as he proved on Slaughter. The three of us have formed a bit of a unit... you see, the bad guys in the fWo have had their way with the promotion because of their gang tactics. The three of us are going to change all of that. We watch each other's back... make sure people like the Dynasty and Dark Riders... both sets of 'em... keep things fair." Chris Finn nodded, and followed it up with another question. "The fWo has grown to be a massive promotion in the last year... a lot of people want in. Do you have any advice for them?" Wolf Fang nodded quickly. "Don't be a prick. I've seen tons of people in the fWo come and go... and the people who stick it out have a right to be commended. You've got people like Death or Scott Slugger... I don't like them but you've got to give them credit for sticking it out. The people who come and go... they make it harder for new people to step up to the plate." Chris Finn nodded. "Thanks Wolf Fang... and best of luck to you in the future. Folks, we've got a special match taped at last Sunday's Slaughter show... let's take you to that now." Hellrazor vs. Hiroshima Hiroshima met Hellrazor as he slid into the ring with kicks to the head, driving the challenger into the mat. Hellrazor rallied by grabbing Hiroshima's leg and flipping him over backward, causing Hiroshima to fall on his head. Hellrazor grabbed the legs of Hiroshima and attempted a figure four, but Hiroshima mule kicked backward and sent Hellrazor spiraling into the ropes. Hiroshima, quick on his feet hit a standing lariat and dropped Hellrazor to the mat. Hiroshima made a cover, but Hellrazor kicked out at two hard. Hiroshima grabbed Hellrazor by the hair and brought him to his feet, but Hellrazor hit upward with a uppercut into Hiroshima's face. Hiroshima's nose bloodied, and Hellrazor went to the ropes. Hellrazor came off and struck with a superkick into the jaw of Hiroshima. Hellrazor quickly rolled up Hiroshima, but Hiroshima grabbed the ropes to break the count. Hellrazor argued with the ref, which gave Hiroshima a moment to untie the turnbuckle cover and cast it into the crowd. Hellrazor turned away from the referee and grabbed at Hiroshima, but Hiroshima reversed his hold and thrust Hellrazor's head into the exposed steel. Falling backward, Hellrazor was helpless as Hiroshima picked up Hellrazor and drove him head first into the mat. Hiroshima covered the fallen man, and made a quick cover for the victory. Hellrazor watched Hiroshima leave, looking angry and with a gaze that appeared that nothing was settled... Result: Hiroshima by Pinfall Finishing comments The cameras returned to the fWo studios. Chris Finn sat, with the monitors behind him pointing to fwowrestling.com. "Folks, before we run out of here we have to give you word about the Boston Strangler. The man who will wrestle for the fWo World Title when he recovers is stable, and an early MRI shows that he suffered a fracture of the left rib and has a slight concussion. We hope for the best from this great athlete. Also, we are announcing that at BattleBowl we have signed two new matches. First, the fWo Hardcore title will be on the line when Death faces Scott Slugger of the Dynasty. But the rest of the Dynasty will be in action as well, as Chris Universal, Ice, and Lance Star will take on Black Panther, Wolf Fang and Havok... the Millennium Men. Folks, don't forget about BattleBowl itself... a twenty man battle royal. This battle royal will boast that the winner of the match will get a title shot the next night on Sunday Slaughter. This battle royal will feature several fWo superstars... and we've learned that the Dark Riders... Spencer's team of Dark Riders... are already entered in the event along with the US Marshal, VL, and Stevie Roberts. Folks, that's all for today, but we hope you join us next time for fWo FlashBack. Also don't forget to go to fwowrestling.com for the latest news updates and area for the fans of the fWo to give their comments. Don't forget to vote for who you want the Flying Frenchie to face on Warfare, and don't forget to go to the Fan Zone on fwowrestling.com later in the week for information on how you can vote for the next Match of the Week. For the fWo, I'm Chris Finn saying goodnight!"
