Hello...Hi...Hey there...Hello Again Waylon stood there backstage and did not move an inch, his eyes wandering from side to side watching the traffic of crew members move back and forth. They quickly scuttled from side to side preparing for the show that would await them. Waylon wore a trench coat and a suit underneath, both plain as you could get. The coat was a faint brown and the suit a lighter tone of black...if there is such a thing. The tie that hung to his shirt was bright red in color however he felt as though it really accentuated his chest. He had to look good for Sara, as for today would be the first time he would see her in almost seventeen years. He took a brave step forward and motion outward with his right hand, "Excuse me," he said out loud to an ongoing crew member who walked right past him without even acknowledging Waylon's presence. Withdrawing back into an almost defeating motion Waylon suddenly grew intimidated, thoughts of failure and doubt flooded his mind as fear that he would be able to find Sara sunk in. This was all that he knew, until he heard the most beautiful voice in the world drift into his ears from behind him. "Mr. Perceval will you ever learn?" He turned slowly to stare at Sara Sundae, the love of his life. She smiled back at him with a wicked grin, "You gotta be a man!" She seemed to shout it, however in reality she stated it in a normal tone. Waylon was hush-hush as he stared at her blankly for what seemed to be an endless amount of time, before his body suddenly kick started his mind and he flung himself forward and clung to her. He held her tight and smelt her hair, the same smell he had always remembered....hers. "It's great to see you Waylon," She whispered softly in his ear, "Friend," is what she followed up with. Waylon gripped her tighter before letting her go. Now they stood face to face, "Sara," he mouth moved but no voice came out. "So Waylon," She continued, "You are in the industry? With your physique I would have never guessed." He cut her off, "No, no, I met a guy." Her left eyebrow cocked, "Really? You met a guy?" Knowing what she was insinuating he was quick to respond, "No, not that way. I mean, I manage a guy." "I thought you were involved in-" "Things change." "Yes they do." "Yes..." "So where is he?" "Who?" "Your guy." "What guy? Oh yeah! Pierce is here, but...uh not with me now." "Why?" "Well he likes my management skills but almost hates me personally....er...yeah. We are never in the same spot, he says I'm annoying." Sara paused for a moment to contemplate the validity of that, before smiling, "I can see that," She said and squeezed Waylon's arm with her hand. Waylon looked back to her again, "So, I can go get him." "That would be great, tell him to follow the left wing to section 43, my office is back there. And afterward perhaps you and I can go get some coffee?" Another wonderful smile was followed by her soft voice. "Of course, " Waylon jumped as he spoke and then turned fast to the opposite direction, "I'll go grab him...do you know where the washroom is?" She nodded and pointed to his right, "That way." "Thank you Sara," Waylon turned and headed off to the washroom and Sara followed suit by leaving to head for her office. As they took about ten steps Waylon whipped back in her direction, "Sara..." She heard him and turned.... "Yes Waylon?" He smiled, "Its great to see you again." "You too Waylon." Death vs. Vince Jacobs Ruahh opened up after its traditional beginning video package with a wide panning shot of the crowd, cheering wildly, waiting for the beginning of tonight's edition of fWo Monday Night Ruahh. Signs were waved, most baring the names of their wavers or somebody who their wavers thought sucked. Massive fireworks exploded on the entrance ramp, firing the crowd up even more. With the preshow festivities over, Ruahh headed to ringside for Chris Finn and James Armstrong to announce the early lineup for the show - The lights went out. The fans immediately started booing, well aware that the show was about to start on an unpleasant note. Fog, low to the ground, drifted in across the entryway. A gravelly voice spoke. "And behold a pale horse: and his name that sat on him was Death, and Hell followed with him." Thunderous fireworks exploded in front of the entryway, and a red glow bathed the arena. A huge skull appeared on the FanTron. Pyro started to spray up from the area in front of the entryway, and "Ether" hit. Death, clad in a heavy leather trenchcoat, appeared through the smoke and fire, and the intensity and volume of the crowd's booing increased. Death stood there for a moment, then stepped forward, looking extremely intimidating as he made his way to the ring for his match on this, the final show before his World Title shot at Meltdown. Death reached the ring, the lights came back up, and "Ring Superstar" began to play, heralding the arrival of Death's opponent, the former fWo Internet Champion, "Superstar" Vince Jacobs. The booing decreased and gave way to a sort of mixed reaction (but a loud reaction nonetheless). The fans were no fans of Jacobs', but given a choice between him and Death, they'd pick him every time. Jacobs made his way to the ring looking confident and focused for this big matchup... surely a victory here, which would rank as a slight upset, would catapult Jacobs to a whole new level in the fWo. Jacobs stepped up the ring ropes into the ring, and as he stepped through the ropes, Death leapt upon him, hammering down on him with clubbing forearm blows. Death stood Jacobs up as the ref called for the bell, then sent him into the ropes. As Jacobs came off, Death leapt into the air and caught him with a leg lariat, sending Jacobs down to the mat. Death made a cover, but Jacobs quickly kicked out. Death brought Jacobs up to his feet again, then sent him hard into a corner, sending Jacobs slamming into the ringposts. Death followed him in, connecting with a big bodysplash. Jacobs stumbled out of the corner, and Death hit the ropes, then came off and drove Jacobs down to the mat with a lariat. Jacobs set Death up and whipped him across into the ropes. Death leapfrogged Jacobs coming off, then stopped behind him and applied a waistlock, going for perhaps a german suplex. Evil laughter erupted from the vile leader of the Dark Riders as the fans boos escorted them from the ringside area. Questions hung in the air now, heavy ones. Things happen quickly on this battlefield, but had Brand Frontier really laid into Quicksilver with the Hammer of God by accident, or was he taking his shot at weakening the reigning title holder before his chance at the gold? What exactly was Death's intention this night? With his force now fortified with the addition of the masked Balaam, what force must gather to stop them? And... who of these men would walk out of Meltdown as the World Heavyweight Champion? Result: Brand Frontier via Pin fall The return The show was long over. Stagehands milled around the backstage area, relatively calm despite the events that had occured earlier in the night. It was the proverbial calm before the storm, the slight break in the clouds before the destruction than few knew were coming. Men carried boxes and crates, tearing down the set so that other shows could take the place of the venue, and loading trucks to prepare for the long journey to Meltdown... and the first away from home event in a few months. Footsteps came on the concrete, but few heads turned to look. Those who would have might have glimpsed a familiar face beneath slick, silver hair. The man walked in a dark green suit, crisp and unusual on the body of a gentlemen that all of them knew quite well. The man turned, humming a slight tune under his breath, and showed no signs of recognition of the people around them. They were not strangers, but they were not his purpose tonight. It felt good to be back amoung them all the same... but now was not the time for old memories. It was the time for actions and decisive movement... there would be time enough for the other later. Perhaps. He turned down a hallway, and nearly bumped into Ted Tallison. Tallison turned to excuse himself, and felt the words in his mouth before they were spoken. "You..." Silver Fox smiled, and clapped Tallison on the back. "Me. I'm back Ted, back to take care of some business. It's time I set things right around here... and I intend to do it."
