fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE AND THEN THERE WERE FOUR August 9, 2000 The fWo Survivor contest entered the final four a day later than usual, as Survivor wrestler Arch Angel after defeating the Don in an earlier sudden death match was narrowly defeated by Ruben Ross during Round Nine. Arch Angel and Ruben Ross tied during the voting period, leading to the sudden death match. Arch Angel was a favorite among both Survivor members and fans watching the event. The young cruiserweight came into the contest with little background or information about him, but over time proved his abilities beyond the wildest expectations of onlookers. "Arch Angel was a good competitor. He was generally well liked, and his interview skills seemed to improve day by day." said fWo Survivor coordinator Stevie Roberts. During a press conference following the event, Arch Angel thanked the other members of the Survivor contest, the staff, and the fWo for letting him participate. Joking, Arch Angel said, "I came I saw I got voted out, ha ha ha ha ha!" But later became somber and told reporters, "Many people have tried to do what we are doing but they have not accomplished it, we are a special breed of wrestlers, we are Survivors!" The rest of round nine featured a quick tag team match followed by a singles immunity match between the winning team. Due to the incredible match he wrestled earlier in the night, Ruben Ross helped lead his team to defeat at the hands of Kodiak Vic Creed and Reno Davis. Davis surprised onlookers by tricking Creed into a victory, and thus winning immunity. This is the first immunity challenge won for Reno Davis, and it ensures that he will be among the final three left in the contest. With round nine complete, Reno Davis, Suicide, Kodiak Vic Creed and Ruben Ross will vote to see who enters the final leg of the contest. fWo officials claim that the wrestler voted out will be announced tonight, rather than on the following day. With round nine complete, the Survivor members now get an extended weekend. Round ten takes place on Monday the 14th and will feature a three way contest between the remaining wrestlers. More news as it comes available.
