Introduction Previously... Three days ago the fWo ran a show in the war-torn nation of Israel. Mere miles from the Gaza Strip in Tel-Aviv where conflict and bloody rebellion is a way of life, the fWo attempted to provide two hours of wrestling entertainment to the excited fans in the area. But a night of entertainment was not to be, as the actions of a terrorist Palestinian suicide bomber brought the evening to an abrupt and horrifying halt. With casualties mounting and fWo wrestlers and personel caught in the conflict, nobody was safe. The horrors of an unstable nation crashed down upon them all, bringing forever to a halt the illusions of peace and safety. Throughout the chaos, the head road manager Silver Fox was abducted by Richard Moore, the estranged and unbalanced father of Ryan Moore who he believes to have perished three years before. But Ryan Moore lives on in the guise of Fallen, as unaware of his father's actions as his father is of his own existance. Now, with Silver Fox missing and in deadly peril, the fWo turns to Turkey... trying to move forward as they shake off the bloody horrors of Sunday, and the uncertain fate of a world at war... A roar of rage heralds Kelly's approach as he careens towards Brand, elbow ready to deliver another vicious blow. Only Kelly feels the print of Frontier's sole as Brand manages to get a foot up clocking the champion in the jaw. Erik stumbles and with unsure footing receives a heavy clothesline that takes him quick to the mat, end over end. Neither man giving an inch Kelly tries to regain a vertical stance but the man known as Brand Frontier charges in and drop kicks the champion in the face. Kelly goes to his back and Frontier springs in the air and much to the shock and delight of the crowd executes a perfect roll through leg drop. Hooking a leg Brand strains to hold the pinning attempt. Kelly pushes back against the Paragon and the ref doesn't even get in a position for a pin attempt before he kicks out with authority. Brand now back to his feet reaches for Kelly again but gets planted in the face with a hard right. Then a left. Then a right. Kelly now with the advantage begins to toss Brand again to the corner. This time though he holds on and pulls the dazed hero back to him. Over the head powerbomb. Brand bounces off the mat with the impact and the crowd is in shock. Kelly locks in under Frontier's leg and lies on top, trying to end things early. One... Two... Erik can hear the screams of the fans before he even knows what happens. First clue that their 'hero' kicked out at the last second. Frontier was still in the game. Kelly rises to his feet as Frontier kips up. The fans show their approval to both men's actions, not sure of who exactly to root for more. Brand chargers in first, hungry for the gold that has been around Kelly's waist for much of the past year. His charge though results in a swift and powerful... bearhug. Kelly wrenches down on Brand's lower back. Letting loose only long enough to yank Frontier back to him, forcing the air out of Brand's lungs and putting pressure on the Survivor's bruised ribs. Long seconds pass and Brand begins to show signs of fatigue. The lack of oxygen has started to takes its toll. Kelly's head whips around and peers out in to the audience again. This time to fully realize that he is getting jeered. Taking the public outcry as an indication to change things up a smirk shows on the champion's face as he squeezes in on Frontier's ribs again. Brand cries out and they lock eyes. Moments later Brand's feet leave the mat and someone breaks a slab of concrete over his back! Or rather that's what it felt like as the mighty warrior crashes to the mat courtesy of the Defcon 1!!! Kelly again grabs Frontier's leg and rolls his massive 336-pound frame on top of him. One... Two... Three!!! NO!!! Frontier powers out, drawing strength from within. He pulls himself up gasping for air as Kelly slams his hand to the mat, glancing to the crowd again this time seeing a little boy with a bandage over his arm. Distracted Erik reaches for Brand but his hand is knocked away as Brand lands a solid left to his jaw. It staggers Kelly back; enough that Brand clutches his wrists and tosses him to the ropes. With the rebound Brand takes full advantage and spears the mammoth to the mat. Springing back to his feet Frontier points to the turnbuckle before ascending to the top. The fans cheer wildly as the two hundred and seventy odd pound man flies. Turning head over heels The Paragon Bomb connects with devastating results... ...to Brand Frontier. The World Champion rolled out of the way at the last minute and avoided certain doom. Clutching his aching abdomen Frontier barely makes it to his feet as Kelly stalks towards him. Cocking his left hand behind him Frontier waits. When Erik reaches for his opponent Brand lets loose with a left handed punch, almost strong enough to send himself off his feet. Kelly felt it upon impact, his heart a split second later. He stumbled back, his massive frame crushing the referee in the corner. The Hammer of God. And the crowd erupted. Kelly collapsed, so did the ref. So did Brand. All three lay motionless in the ring for a few seconds as the fans in attendance started to stamp their feet. Suddenly a chorus of jeers rang out as the U.S. Champion, Pete Borst ambled to ringside. He dragged his title belt absently behind him as a freshly lit cigarette dangled from his lips. Sidling up next to the ring Borst cocked a curious look to the three in the ring. Sliding in he inched close to Frontier before taking his lit cigarette and mashing it on the chest of the Paragon. Frontier writhed. The burning flesh smell filled his senses and Brand rolled to his feet, a look of a man possessed washing over his face. Borst threw a wild haymaker but it was blocked, another attempt followed but it was blocked again. Brand was having none of that. Wiping away the ashes from his chest Brand dove at Borst yanking the United Stated title from his grasp. Brand tossed the now tarnished title to the mat before stalking Borst. Kelly began to stir. Borst trash talked the hero seeing the champion behind him beginning to rise to his feet. With lightning like reflexes that surprised the fans as well as Frontier Borst dove at Brand and pushed him, back in to Kelly. Kelly spun around, seeing Brand he planted a massive boot barely above the waistline that bent the Paragon over. Taking his cue Borst rolled out of the ring. Kelly pushed Brand's head between his legs and screamed the phrase that normally signaled the end of his challenger's chances of winning... POOOWERBOOOOOMB!!! Brand bounced off the mat a good foot from the impact. In his daze he could have sworn he heard something crack. Erik Kelly knelt down to pin the mighty hero when a chorus of jeers again rung out from the crowd. He caught Borst out the corner of his eye. With a cocked eyebrow Kelly hooked the leg as an afterthought as he noticed the ref still unconscious. The pain in the back of his head made his vision blur. Something had just struck him from behind. Why in the hell would Pete Borst stop him from pinning Brand Frontier? It didn't make sense and as Kelly clutched the back of his head and concentrated through the throbbing he saw that it didn't need to make sense. It wasn't Pete Borst. Obviously the boo's from the crowd didn't punctuate the Eradication on Brand Frontier it signaled the arrival of a new player in this skit. "The Main Man" Vic Creed stood over Erik Kelly, a long and twisted length of logging chain coiled around his gargantuan fist. Brand had had time to recover at this point and took a few extra seconds to size up the situation. Pete Borst and Vic Creed, twin brothers from different mothers. Both evil in their own way. With Kelly dazed and confused Brand had to play his cards right. He began to stand. With weak knees at first he finally managed a vertical position, holding the ropes for support. He and Creed simply stared at each other. Brand Frontier then stepped forward closing the gap between Creed and himself. As the crowd roared Frontier stood defiantly between the Alaskan Juggernaut and the World Champion. No words were exchanged as Creed snarled. His grip tightened around the chain, knuckles turning white. Vic turned slightly swinging the chain in circles, bearing down on Brand. He growled at Frontier and motioned for him to get out of the way. The fans waited with baited breath as the two latest survivors of the recent horrors stood opposite each other. Their wait was short lived however as Pete Borst used the distraction brought upon by Creed and had snuck back in to the ring. Charging at Frontier Borst planted a stiff fist to the side of his head sending the Paragon reeling. Creed took the distraction and used it for all it was worth descending again on his Cyberslam opponent. Another swing of the heavy logging chain connected, only with the mat this time however, as Kelly had recovered enough to defend himself. Lunging at Creed with his arms outstretched Kelly knocked in to KVC causing him to lose grip on the huge chain. The two behemoths rolled on the mat, exchanging blows nearly causing Brand Frontier and Pete Borst to separate from their mutual death hold on each other. Borst rocked Brand in the side with a stiff punch only to have his jaw jacked by a quick left handed jab courtesy of Frontier. The two men finally pulled apart long enough to dive once again at each other planting one thunderous punch after another. Creed threw Kelly in to the corner and followed in with a massive splash that took the breath from the champion. Gloating and pushing his finger in his chest Creed was knocked off balance by Frontier. Borst had sent the Paragon sailing in to the mountain of man with a surprising Irish whip. Vic pushed back at Frontier and they finally tore in to each other, as Borst couldn't resist the turned away champion as a target any longer. A heavy boot found it's way to Kelly's groin and Borst simply laughed. The laugh subsided as he realized Kelly was a thinking man and a sports cup had absorbed much of the blow. With is adrenaline on high Erik washed away the rest of the pain from the cheap shot and dove on top of the United States Champion. The ref, frail as he was, had finally began to recover. Through clouded vision he could have sworn he was seeing double. A few blinks later had pushed back the haze over his eyes and he realized that yet again a match ended by outside interference. Seeing the, for lack of a better overstatement, carnage the referee called the match. The bell rang. Pete Borst hammered in to the side of Kelly before diving on top of Frontier. Brand rolled off of Creed and the Main Man turned his attention to the World Champion just in time to feel the pain of an enraged Erik Kelly Clothesline. He tumbled over the top rope and barely landed on his feet as officials swarmed from the back. Frontier tossed Borst to the ropes and as he turned to rebound Brand cocked back his left hand. Seeing this, Borst dropped to his knee, killing his own momentum and rolled to safety. The fWo World Champion, Erik Kelly and the Survivor II Champion Brand Frontier stood triumphantly in the ring as Vic Creed and Pete Borst rethought their strategies. With a stalemate for the time being this battle and this Warfare... ended. Result: No Contest: Erik Kelly retains the fWo World Championship.
