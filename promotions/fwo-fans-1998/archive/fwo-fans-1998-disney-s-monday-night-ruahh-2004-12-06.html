Introduction Previously... The fWo returns to Baltimore, a city left untouched by the promotion for nearly three years. Within the promotion ratings and attendence have slowly increased, and the trust in the fWo locker room has slowly grown toward their Disney owners. But as the promotion heads to the end of the year, all wonder what shocking events could occur that would once again shake up the status quo... Early Smoke Baltimore, Maryland the city of the Inner Harbor, Fells Point and Federal Hill. Home to exquisite television programming (unlike this script) such as 'Homicide' and 'The Wire.' Featuring the feared Ravens and laughable Orioles sports franchises. The last time Craig Miles set foot in this city was when he was the Commissioner of New Frontier Wrestling their first year in business, operating out of the Vault; a little known nightclub in the heart of Charm City. Miles breathed in the cold air just outside the entrance door to the arena, "It's good to be back," he said has he exhaled a waft of visible breadth. Of course, Miles' run in Baltimore ended when fire marshals closed down an NFW event due to an oversold capacity and wrestlers with a penchant for lighting themselves and any graspable objects on fire. Even then, the Dean of Thermodynamics was experimenting. So, it wasn't a surprise to see a few more fire trucks parked at the arena tonight. Miles smirked at them, his silver sunglasses reflecting the parking lot lights. With a quick flip, a Newport entered his lips, subsequently followed by a light of his Zippo and a deep, satisfying inhalation of Vitamin N. "Mind if I bum one off you?" Miles smirked, recognizing the European accent, and without bothering to turn to face the inquirer, flipped a cigarette at him, which Pierre Delacroix, the Flying Frenchie, deftly caught. "Merci, Miles." Pierre had a cheap Bic lighter, but by cupping his hand over the flame, was able to light the cigarette without too much hassle. He took a drag, and started talking aloud, as much to himself as to Craig. "I gave zese up years ago, but on nights like zis, ze cold, ze wind, all zat merte, it's just right." "I was hoping to catch up wit' you, apart from your friends and mine. A few t'ings on my mind, if you don't mind." "Contrary to your countrymen's opinions, last I checked this was a free country," Miles said with a smirk and exhale. Pierre returned the sarcastic smile. "I know better zan to belive you actually care, but for what it's wort', I don't have a single problem wit' zis Soldiers deal you've got. Saving ze FWO from itself, from Disney, whatever, it's been done, but far be it from me to deny such simple pleasures to ot'er people." Another inhale, and Pierre calmed down a bit. It's the magic of tobacco, which everyone, especially all you impressionable youths, should use. "If zat's all you were up to, I'd have no qualm. A few years ago, you'd probably fine me an ally, again, knowing full well zat you don't much care for such details." Frenchie flicked his wrist, sending ash to the concrete of the parking lot. "You've got a pretty good set-up, as well, which as memory serves was given to you by a very good friend of mine. Zese last few mont's you've gone so far as to label him part of ze problem, and have taken what could only be described as glee in tormenting him." "I trust you can see, zen, where my problem lies wit' you." Miles nodded, the statement didn't anger him. Naturally, the moment Frenchie started, the Soldier General knew something about Chronos would be said. "Y'know Pierre," Miles inhaled and exhaled a smoke ring, "I'm not here to save anything. There was a time in your country when anger and poor conditions led to a revolution. Back then it was about freedom, and truthfully, all the Soldiers are is an expression of the very same concept. By that same token, there are always casualties in these wars...there's always bloodshed." Miles inhaled and exhaled, looking out to the street lights for a lost moment, before continuing, "I betrayed Ric. Truthfully, its the one thing I regret and certainly something I remorse over. At the same time, Ric was the only key for a door that Disney had locked. Chronos could've let bygones be bygones and join me...you as well could've joined me." Miles' silver sunglasses turned towards Frenchie, "but Ric chose not to. Ric seeks vengeance and you and I both know that..." Inhale... Exhale... "Remember, he never suffered the attacks Frontier and Creed did. I'm not tormenting him, Pierre, he's walked into the fire on his own will. He's run out on my open challenges, taken part in Dudley's bounty. While I've escaped his clutches more than once, I only do so with glee knowing its never as easy as it may look." Miles smirked for a moment before continuing, "However, as long as Ric seeks vengeance, I will not take that away from him and neither should you." Miles exhaled a smoke ring, before flicking the cigarette away, "And perhaps, it'd be wise to consider the fact that you shouldn't give him any greater cause," the silver sunglasses came off, Miles' steel blue eyes looking directly into Frenchie's, "...if you catch my drift." Pierre shook his head, and took a last puff from his cigarette before tossing it to the ground and stepping on it. Ultimatum A brooding Jim Dudley sat in his chair, fingers laced, and watched the two men standing in front of his desk fidget nervously. Alex Creed, the self-proclaimed "Mecca", had been out of the fWo for a number of months after a hideous beating from the Soldiers of the Fifth Seal. He trained hard for his return to the ring, only to have Jim Dudley pull the rug out from underneath him. Dudley's wiggle room on the subject had been strangled after Mob Rules, which left Alex to anxiously wonder what he was doing here. Powerhouse, however, was quite uneasy about being here. Only a week earlier, he'd helped dupe the Commissioner into resigning Kodiak Vic Creed to the fWo. Now, Dudley requested to see him in his office. He wasn't officially a part of the fWo, so what did Jim Dudley want with Powerhouse now? "You're both probably wondering why I've asked you to come here." Dudley looked up at them and studied their faces. Alex almost started to smile; Powerhouse didn't even want to make eye contact. He pointed a finger at the masked muscleman. "You lied to me. You deceived me and now Kodiak Vic Creed is free to roam the halls of the fWo. You could have had a bright future with us, but you threw it away for him. For him." "And you," Dudley said as he looked at Alex. "Let me tell you something, Boy. I've been in this business for years and I've seen more talent in curtain jerkers than I've seen in you. If it wasn't for your Daddy pulling some strings, you'd be wrestling in bars and bingo halls. It takes more than a set of abs to make it in this business." He grabbed the folder with Alex's name on the label and shook it at the Mecca, fax sheets falling onto the desk. "These evaluations don't impress me, and they aren't worth the paper they're printed on in my opinion." Alex felt his temper start to swell. He wanted to speak, but slowly drew in a breath and chose to remain silent. Dudley's disposition clouded over. "I want you both to know that this isn't my decision. The word has come down from Disney that they're both still interested in having you as part of the fWo." The tension between the two dissolved and Alex and Powerhouse couldn't help but smile. Dudley didn't share their jubilation. "Don't start celebrating yet. I still have the final say in this, and while nepotism and deception should not be rewarded, I still have a decision to make." "Alex, I've seen your medical clearance. I've seen all the paperwork from your trainers and specialists. You seem ready to return. But until you take a few bumps, I have reservations about you being ring ready. I've taken the liberty of booking you in a match tonight to test you physically. We'll see how ready you are to make your comeback. If you win your match, you will be reinstated to active status with the fWo. However, should you lose your match " "I won't lose." "Do you mind? As I was saying, if you lose your match you will remain on the injured list indefinitely." The Commissioner turned back to Powerhouse. "And you. Yes, in the few matches you had with us you managed to somehow impress those higher up in the company. They feel you should be given a chance, another chance," he corrected himself, "to become a permanent part of the fWo family. So I've booked you in a match as well. If you win your match, we'll start you off on your fWo career. Lose and you can go back to whatever hole in the wall you used to wrestle in." A second chance. It was more than the big man could have hoped for. All he wanted was to provide for his family, and an fWo contract would guarantee his family's well-being. Powerhouse choked back his enthusiasm though, not wanting to come off as too eager. "Fine," he said simply. Alex stood with his hands on his hips. "So who are we wrestling?" Dudley smirked, and in the back of the Mecca's mind he could tell what the answer was going to be. "I would have thought it'd be obvious by now. You're wrestling each other." "What?" Powerhouse asked. "After tonight, I'll only have to deal with one of you. Now get out of here. You both have a big match to prepare for." Powerhouse and Alex Creed left Dudley's office in silence. Neither one spoke to the other as they went their separate ways. For one of them, their future in the fWo was about to start. For the other, it was going to come to an end.
