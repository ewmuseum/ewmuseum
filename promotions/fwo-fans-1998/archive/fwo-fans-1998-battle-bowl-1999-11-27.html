Battle Bowl 1999, 27 November 1999

27 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Somewhere backstage, nearly everyone was serious in going about their jobs and getting ready for the Pay Per View. Behind one door however, things were different. "So that's the deal Rudo... she's not a reporter at all... she's a plant by an organization with political aspirations to nullify wrestling in the United States. I know I should be angry... maybe even a little hurt... but I just think of her face and it makes me feel all goosey inside. So... what should I do?" Rudo considered, scratching his chin. Sid sat behind him, obviously bored. Mumbling to himself, Sid looked over the booking sheet. "Am I even on this card tonight? God, what lousy promotion... geez Machina, I don't know why you're getting all gooey over this dame... have you seen her thighs? Humph." Machina and Rudo ignored him while Billy James numbly chewed a stick of Wisconsin cheese. Brandi rubbed Rudo's back, trying to relax the Lone Gunmen and make it easier for him to think. "I've got it!" Rudo suddenly snapped his fingers and stood up. Reaching into a coat pocket Rudo retrieved a small case, and handed it to Machina. Machina opened it, and looked at a pair of unassuming glasses. Rudo smiled. "She's a reporter, right? Simply wear these glasses when you go to the ring... she will never recognize you under those glasses, and you can continue to court her at will." A smile spread over Machina's face, and he put the glasses on and raised his hands to the air in triumph, chanting as he did so. "USA! USA! USA!" In the distance, the noises and explosions signalled that Battle Bowl had begun...
