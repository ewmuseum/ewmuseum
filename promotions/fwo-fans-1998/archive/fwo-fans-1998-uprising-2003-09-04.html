Introduction Touring through the Southwest, the fWo comes to Farmington, New Mexico... a growing town with a very excited wrestling fan base. Tonight will be more than a usual night of wrestling however... it will be a night where the Wrestler Of The Year tournament brackets will be announced, and these brackets will determine the shape and scope of the biggest wrestling tournament of the year. With the mandate that everyone must participate, it means that sixty four wrestlers are on edge... nervous... knowing that to get a good start in the tournament can make all the difference between being an also-ran... or a legend. With Friends Like These... James Black looked down at his watch. They were late. He turned his head and looked to his supervisor Edgar, an elderly man of an unknown age. Edgar had been with the Disney company in a variety of forms for what seemed like forever, at least 50 years. The man had to be pushing 80 years old. If not the stress and high pressure environment of working for one of the most demanding corporations in the world had really taken its toll on his body. Edgar twitched in his wheelchair and looked back at James, offering a concerned and annoyed look that Mr. Black interpreted perfectly. The two seemed to know what each other were thinking at all times. One man, however, had absolutely NO idea what EITHER man was thinking. Scotty J. Bowman. Bowman sat at the other end of the gray room in an uncomfortable chair, often adjusting his posture in a vain and futile attempt to find a relaxing position. There was nothing relaxing about this meeting. Black and his old associate had been suspiciously quiet when talking to Bowman about his position with Disney and their plans. One couldn't help but think that Scotty J's situation was bleak. He was still the fWo Cruiserweight Champion, but instead of the dominating force he once was in the division his name had been tarnished (in Disney's eyes) by a series of pinfalls at the hands (or paws) of one man. Mittens. Mittens T. Cat. Even the thought of that psychopathic delusional maniac made Bowman grit his teeth in disgust. That cat had almost cost him his belt and good reputation in a matter of weeks. Some might call these noble men and women... Bowmaniacs. The sooting tones of "Around the World" covered by Naked Round the Block accompanied a short message on the Fantron. "A Star is Born." The Bowmaniacs set free their inhibitions and went wild, unleashing a booming chorus of approval - a pop that matched if not surpassed the arrival of Mittens. It was clear that this upcoming Cruiserweight title match would involve two of the most popular and beloved members of the division. Bowman himself appeared before his legion of fans and stood on the ramp that led to the ring. He paused for a brief dramatic moment and removed his fWo Cruiserweight title belt from around his waist and held it high above his head with both arms extended, allowing the spiraling aqua lights to beam and reflect off it. After the moment had set in Scotty J made his way towards the ring where his furry opponent stood tall, awaiting his arrival. Bowman progressed with the usual bounce in his step, taking time to make eyes with the ladies and slap hands with the men. Both wrestlers stood face to face in the middle of the ring as the referee poised himself and the bell rang. The match started slowly with Bowman casually approaching Mittens and made small talk with him. "You don't even belong here." He said as if he was busy searching for his REAL opponent. Mittens held his ground, mimicking Bowman's words and laughing an obviously sarcastic laugh. "Oh, oh, you don't even belong here, you don't even belong here, heh heh hehehe!" Mittens bobbed his head in the air and clapped the fingers of his paw open and shut, as a representative of Bowman's ever flapping jawbone. Bowman, more than tired of the cat's nonsense and tomfoolery, drew his fist to the air and threw it toward his feline adversary. However, the champion's assault was anticipated and met by a firm paw. Mittens held the champ at bay and quickly lifted a boot that hit it's mark in the middle of SJB's gut. Bowman convulsed and opened himself to a DDT that sent the base of his forehead bouncing off the canvas. Mittens remained relentless, driven by the taste of gold and glory, he knelt over Scotty J and pummeled him with a barrage of hard fists landing directly on the blonde cruiserweight's jaw. The fans in the audience remained split, with many cheering the furry challenger as he raised his arm in the air and many more booing him. Bowman drove an elbow into Mittens's side, then locked his arms firmly around the cat's waist and lifting him high into the air.
