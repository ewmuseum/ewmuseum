Introduction The next fans Wrestling organization pay-per-view was one month away. Body Count. The event began back in 2000, when fWo officials at the time came up with the unique idea of having a pinfalls only battle royal. It didn't matter if you were the last man remaining in the ring... Body Count required you to pin as many people as possible. The person who pinned the most participants won. The Flying Frenchie had a chance to tie the number of opponents pinned set earlier in the night by Austin Davis, but ultimately was screwed by Eron. If Eron had been pinned by Frenchie... fWo history may have changed drastically... but instead, Eron left the ringside area and the honor went to Davis. Davis won Body Count as well as the vacated fWo World Championship, and went onto successfully defend it at CyberSlam that year against the Boston Strangler. How things change. Last year, in 2001, the fWo World Title wasn't the prize. Instead, going onto CyberSlam and facing the World Champion was. And one year ago today, Body Count had a tie. Mitch Wilson and Wretch won the event... and eventually Wilson won the World Title and ended up losing it at CyberSlam to Mike Bear. A year later... neither Wilson nor Bear are on the current fWo roster. How things change. On February 23rd, the third Body Count will undoubtedly set the stage for this year's CyberSlam. Thirty participants will enter... and only one will go on... to the spectacular, epic event. Who will go on to CyberSlam? Who will be World Champion?
