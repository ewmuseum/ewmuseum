Result: Frenchie by pinfall Remembering The Past Neo whistled softly to himself as he used chalk to draw pictures on the concrete outside the arena. Wrestlers had been coming and going all day with little attention paid to Neo, and this suited him fine. He was absorbed by the pictures he was sketching; rainbows and unicorns and creatures from the Lord of the Rings movie he had seen that had sparked his imagination. Every now and then he rubbed his head and frowned, still feeling the lingering remains of a bump on his temple. It had shrunk considerably in the last week, and was now barely noticeable. Neo felt it only as an afterthought, but had already forgotten exactly how he had gotten it. Wrestlers, but mostly attendants for there were far more of them, gave Neo a wide berth. Some of them still remembered the angry lunatic who had bullied and threatened the majority of them. Some had heard about Neo's "accident" with Ruben Ross and were cautious of disrupting whatever strangeness was causing him to act in this passive manner. Some suspected that this was all just a game that Neo was playing for some sick amusement, but those people were dwindling in number as the days wore on. All knew that Neo was anything but a patient man and the thought that he would control himself to this degree and for this long seemed very unlikely. Neo's sense of humor had been fairly basic; this seemed to be a very involved joke where the only person feeling the brunt of the humor was Neo himself. This seemed unlikely at best from a man who had punched the elevator buttons in rage when the door wouldn't close fast enough. Most believed something messy was going on, and like Universal suspected that if Neo's mental condition had been radically changed it would be short lived at best, and a massive lawsuit at worst. Physical injuries were covered by the contracts they had signed to protect the promotion from lawsuits over things like broken arms or legs. Mental injuries were certainly not covered by any such clause, and it would be an easy manner to take the promotion to the cleaners over it given the amount of apparent change in Neo's personality. Yet while the majority gave Neo a wide berth, one man frowned and walked directly up toward him. "You think this is real funny, don't you hillbilly?" Neo glanced up from his chalk drawing and saw Hellrazor standing in front of him. Hellrazor was frowning, and Neo smiled to put him at ease. "No... these aren't funny drawings, these are..." Hellrazor smacked Neo's hand and caused the chalk to fly several feet from him. Neo gasped in surprise, and Hellrazor jerked him to his feet with a growl. "You freak. You sick freak... you may have everyone else fooled with this little goody two shoes act, but I don't buy it. Do you hear me? I don't buy it for a second. You may have everyone else fooled, but you don't... fool... me." Neo's expression was mild surprise and confusion. Hellrazor grunted in frustration and dropped him to the ground in disgust. Neo sat, collapsed, his mouth open and his eyebrows high. His face was a perfect expression of confusion. Hellrazor marched away, then stopped and turned with an angry glare in his eyes. He reached into his pocket, pulled out a long band of cloth and tossed it at Neo. "And I don't want your stupid hillbilly scarf either. You understand me freak... you stay away from me or I'll break a lot more than you arm. Hear?" Hellrazor stomped away, and Neo looked down at the scarf he had knitted for Hellrazor. He sniffed, and his lip trembled. Several feet away around a corner from the building Urara watched the scene with confused, conflicting thoughts. She had been watching Neo closely for the last week, trying to understand what had happened to the man who had terrorized her for months. Could it really be as simple as a knock on the head giving him a kind of amnesia? She found it hard to believe... yet... was it any harder to believe than believing her own feeling of pity that Neo now sat alone on the dirty floor? Certainly he deserved this... yet... Outside the ring Creed grabbed Slugger and pressed him into the air, before dropping him throat first on the guardrail. Slugger gasped for air, and Strangler joined them on the outside. Ross & Frontier saw this and moved to retaliate. The referee separated them from Creed & Strangler however, who worked away on Slugger with kicks and chokes. The two men rolled Slugger back into the ring and Davis went for a cover, but only got a two count. Frustrated, Davis brought Slugger up and hit a piledriver. He continued to work over the head & neck area. Davis locked in a standing Cobra Clutch on Slugger, literally sucking the life out of the WGA. Slugger attempted to escape, and after several moments he finally did with a mulekick. Davis bent over and attempted to block out the pain. Slugger charged towards the ropes and returned, but Davis was waiting for him and took his head off with a high knee. Fans booed loudly, thinking that Slugger had made his comeback. Davis went for another cover, but Slugger kicked out. Davis was really angry now, and glared at the official for a moment before executing a throat slash... signalling for the end. He brought Slugger up and hit the Crucifixion. It was, by all rights, over. One. Two. No. Frontier entered and with a stiff kick broke up the pin. Davis stared at him with fury, before walking over and tagging in the Boston Strangler. The Strangler came in and Davis whipped Slugger to the ropes. Slugger returned and Davis kicked him in the stomach, before hitting a gutwrench powerbomb for a two count as this time Ross broke it up. Angry, Strangler brought Slugger up and went for the Crossface Chicken Wing, but Slugger escaped onto the ring apron. Davis walked over and Slugger pushed him, sending him flying off the ring apron to the floor. As Strangler walked over, the WGA nailed him with a shoulder to the ribs, before leaping over with a sunset flip. One. Two. No. Dazed, Slugger staggered around the squared circle, and as Strangler got up Slugger grabbed him and went to a corner. Coming up next was a move that very few walked away from the match victorious, their arm raised instead of Slugger's. Swinging. Doublearm. DDT. The Curveball. No. Strangler stopped him in the middle of the ring and hit a Northern Lights Suplex in return. He elevated his legs for the bridge. One. Two. No. Both men got up and Strangler ducked the swing from Slugger, before hitting a German suplex. And another. And another. Another. Another. Five German suplexes later, Strangler rolled him over for the cover. One. Two. NO. Somehow, someway, Slugger got his shoulder off the mat. The fans yelled in approval while Davis looked on in shock and horror. Davis was clearly frustrated and now his partner called for a weapon. Davis retrieved one of their World Tag Team Titles. He tossed it to his partner, and Strangler clutched it, preparing to strike. As he did the referee grabbed it from him and the two began a tug of war. From behind as Strangler won the battle, he fell backwards and Slugger rolled him up for a pin. One. Two. Davis broke up the pin, and as a result, a pier6brawl broke out. Davis & Slugger battled. So did Strangler & Frontier. As did Ross & Creed. Moments passed and in the ring Slugger hit the Curveball on Davis. The referee lost track of who was legal and who wasn't. He counted the pin. One. Two. Creed came in and grabbed Slugger, before hitting the Hangman's DDT. He went for a cover. The referee again counted. One. Two. Ross pulled Creed off of Slugger. The two fought back and forth, before Ross got the advantage with the Plague of Darkness. Ross covered Creed. One. Two. Strangler pulled Ross off of Creed and locked in the Boston Massacre. Ross reached for the ropes and was close to tapping out. The fans booed as it appeared the World Champion was about to give in to the pain. Ross reached for the ropes and the referee counted and a reluctant Boston Strangler released the hold at four. He prepared to lock it back in again, but from behind came Frontier. A kick to the stomach followed by a running powerbomb into the corner was what occured next. The Divine Right. Frontier went for a cover. This was the same move he beat Davis with last week. One. Two. No. The aforementioned Davis was like a snake, returning to the picture out of nowhere. He smiled before he grabbed Frontier and pulled him outside the ring. He clocked him with a title shot to the head. Ross was now up and he turned and staggered right into a kick to the stomach, followed by one of the most devestating moves in wrestling history. A crucifix powerbomb DDT. The Dead End. Davis hit the move and as he went for the cover, Slugger charged and speared him into the mat, unleashing several punches. But behind him, Creed was back up and was perched on the top rope. Creedzillinator. He hit it. He covered and Slugger turned and dove at the last second, but it was too late. Good had battled evil and on this night, evil was too much to overcome. The team of Davis, Strangler & Creed were victorious, and as they celebrated outside the ring, Frontier returned holding his head, blood trickling out of the corner of his eye as he stumbled in confusion. Slugger checked on Ross, and Frontier as well. Words were exchanged by the five men, and with Ross out on the mat, Creed challenged Ross to a match next week on Slaughter... and should Ross accept the stakes would be unquestionably higher than any previous encounter by the two men before. The World Title would be on the line... but would Ross accept? Slaughter faded to black... the image of good succumbing to evil remaining on the screen. Result: Creed, Strangler & Davis by pinfall
