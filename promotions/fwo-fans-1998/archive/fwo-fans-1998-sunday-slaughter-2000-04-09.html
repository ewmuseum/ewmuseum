fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 9 April 2000

9 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Slaughter opens up with a shot of a limousine pulling into the parking lot of the E Center, as the sun settled far off to the west on a pleasant spring day in Utah. A trio of small French flags waved atop the limousine. As the limousine stopped, a driver exited from the front of the limo and came to the back, before opening up one of the doors. Out stepped The Flying Frenchie, Primetime & Courage, who of course compromised the French Foundation. They made their way underneath the concourse into the two year old building, but failed to notice two figures emerge from the shadows. The figures in question were the World's Greatest Athlete, Perfect and the reigning fWo Internet Champion Mitch Wilson, who were wielding weapons of mass destruction. Perfect carried a sledgehammer, while Wilson had on his person a jackhammer. Perfect began hitting the limousine repeatedly with the sledgehammer, while Wilson started up the jackhammer, before climbing onto the hood, and using the jackhammer to drill a hole inside the large, plush limousine. However, a female and three males stepped into the picture from the inside of the arena, and it was Stone, along with Scott Collins, War & Plague. A small brawl erupted as Hiroshima arrived on the scene, while Stone watched from afar. Collins and Perfect duked it out while Wilson & Hiroshima went at it with War & Plague. A sly grin developed onto the mouth of Stone, as Slaughter headed into the arena, the custom French Foundation limo obliterated from front to back. Damnation vs. STUMP Squad The sounds of Machinehead bring the fans' attention back to the entrance ramp here on Slaughter, as Hellrazor and Fear, two members of The Underground that have teamed up to comprise "Damnation", make their way down to the ring for this tag-team match. They walk down and greet the fans, all-the-while keeping their seriousness on the task at hand...winning their match. As the two check out the ropes for stiffness in the ring, Twisted Sister hits the speakers, and the STUMP Squad, consisting of Black Quicksilver and Mike Heftel, make their way out to ringside. They are accompanied by the fans' cheers, as well as Stump. They get in the ring and ask Hellrazor about the ropes. He just stares at them and then at Stump, which promptly gets an odd look by the hardcore legend. The bell rings, and BQ and Heftel manage to get the Underground members to stand in the middle of the ring. "All right you two, time to decide who starts first," says Heftel. From there he proceeds to play a rousing game of "Train Train Number Nine." Hellrazor and Fear look at each other, not knowing what to do...but they decide to join in the game. The first man selected is Hellrazor. Amazingly (but not coincidental), Fear is selected as the second man. BQ and Heftel try to wish them good luck on having to pound each other while they watch, but Razor and Fear begin to pound away on the two stunned stars. Fear takes Heftel to one corner, while Hellrazor takes BQ to the opposite corner. A few chops and blows to the head, and Fear and Hellrazor whip the STUMP Squad members toward each other. However, both BQ and Heftel reverse it and send Fear and Razor toward each other. Before they crash into one another, Fear makes an adjustment on his destination, and the two run by each other and clothesline BQ and Heftel to the mat. Heftel makes his way to the outside, and Hellrazor walks over to double-team the fallen BQ, who is taking kicks to the back of the head by Fear. The ref comes in between Hellrazor and makes him go to his corner, thus bringing order to the match. Fear continues to kick at the head of BQ, before bringing him up and sets him up for a suplex. However, he lowers BQ onto the ropes, using them as a springboard for a more devastating maneuver. He goes for a quick cover but only gets two. Fear picks up BQ and takes him into the Underground corner, where he tags in Hellrazor. The two begin to pound away at BQ. Fear kicks BQ to the gut and tucks his head in for a power bomb. As he does this, Hellrazor sets up his next move by the ropes. As Fear nails the power bomb, Hellrazor runs off the ropes and nails BQ with a body splash, which produces a cover. The ref gets in position and gets two-and-a-half. Hellrazor, somewhat surprised by this, starts to knee BQ in the gut, making sure he doesn't get his wind back. He picks up BQ and tags back in Fear. Hellrazor brings BQ back to the middle of the ring, while Fear waits in the corner. Hellrazor applies an atomic drop to BQ, who jumps right into the waiting arms of Fear, who catches him with a choke slam in the making. He hits it, and goes for another cover. However, this time, Heftel breaks it up at two-and-three-quarters. Heftel tries to fight off the both Damnation members; however, he cannot even fight off the referee, who assists him back to his corner. As he protests, Fear and Hellrazor pick up BQ and hit a double-arm choke slam...not once, but twice. Hellrazor goes for the cover. However, he only gets a two-and-a-half count, as the ref couldn't get in position in time to make the three. Hellrazor begins to complain to the ref for a second, and doesn't see BQ somehow getting up. BQ grabs Hellrazor, who nails him back to the mat and continues complaining to the ref. BQ gets up AGAIN, and rolls Hellrazor up in a small package. This gets a two count, as the stunned Hellrazor took a second to kick out. He gets up and tries to hit BQ, who ducks every shot. He kicks Hellrazor to the gut and hits a modified X-Factor. He picks up Hellrazor and hits a standing dropkick. This prompts Fear to hit the ring, which brings Heftel (with Stump) inside as well. BQ ducks Fear's charge, sensing that Heftel would hit Fear with Stump, and would allow for a quick rollup pin. However, Fear sees Heftel's run at him, and ducks it...BQ is not as fortunate. Heftel nails Black Quicksilver with Stump, knocking him to the mat. Fear kicks Heftel, who falls out of the ring, and covers BQ. Three seconds later, the Underground is your winner. Heftel gets back up into the ring and gets BQ to his feet. Quicksilver, not happy with the outcome, begins to argue with Heftel as they walk up the ramp. Damnation continues to celebrate in the ring as Slaughter heads to a break. Result: Damnation by pinfall Black Panther vs. Scott Collins "Killed The Cat" starts up and the fans begin booing lustily as the Black Panther makes an appearance, soaking in the boos with a smirk under his mask. He arrogantly heads to the ring, and as he enters "Burning" begins to play and the boos more or less turn to cheers as Scott Collins heads to the ring, flanked by the rest of the Four Riders. Collins rolls in and immediately takes it to Panther, clobbering him with forearms. Collins quickly whips Panther into the ropes, and catches him coming off with a clothesline that knocks the larger man down. Panther is quickly back up to his feet, and Collins clotheslines him over the top rope and Panther splats on the outside. Panther gets up, and begins to gather himself on the outside. As he clears his head, Scott Collins bounces off the ropes and dives through the middle rope with a suicide body block. It turns out to be just that, as Panther moves out of the way and Collins smacks his head into the guardrail. Panther brings Collins up, and drops him throat-first onto the guardrail. Panther then grabs the head of Collins from behind and runs forward, ramming his forehead into the steel ring post. Panther then rolls Collins back into the ring after being admonished by the ref, and pulls Collins up into a piledriver position. Panther hits it, and covers Collins, who kicks out at two. Panther brings Collins up once more, and whips him into the ropes. Panther catches Collins as he comes off with a ring-rocking power slam, and hooks the leg. Collins escapes at two, and Panther is undaunted as he pulls Collins to his feet. Panther backs Collins into a corner, and whips him across. However, Collins reverses and Panther hits hard in the corner. Collins clears his head, then charges in with a clothesline which is countered by the Panther with a boot to the head. Collins staggers out of the corner, and Panther runs up behind him and bulldogs him down. Panther rolls Collins over and covers, but Collins again kicks out at two. Panther brings Collins up once more, and sets him up for a power bomb. Panther attempts to lift Collins up once, but Collins shifts his weight in mid-move and back body drops Panther out of the hold. As Panther gets to his feet, Collins serves him up a leather sandwich in the form of a super kick right to the mouth. Panther falls back into the corner from the impact, and Collins, getting his second wind, charges into the corner with a lariat that nearly decapitates Panther. Collins then rears back and blasts away at Panther with a series of lariats that knock the masked man down to a sitting position. Collins takes a few steps back, then charges in and kicks Panther right in the face because he can. Panther slumps back, and Collins lifts him up and sets him on the turnbuckles. Collins climbs beneath Panther and goes for a super Death Penalty, but Panther grabs onto the ropes and Collins flies off and hits the canvas himself. Panther then leaps off and drops an elbow onto the throat of Collins, but Collins moves. At this, Havok begins to make his way to the ring, wielding a chair and looking to extract vengeance from his former friend. Panther, ever the opportunist, points this out to the ref as he makes his way to his feet, who turns to confront Havok on the apron. Panther then pulls a pair of brass knucks from his pants and puts them on, before turning to confront Collins. He swings, but Collins ducks and grabs his head for the Death Penalty. Panther shoves him off into the ropes, and catches Collins coming off with a knuck shot flush to the forehead. Collins drops to the mat like he was shot, and Panther throws the knucks aside. He yells at the ref, and the ref turns and counts the three for Panther. A second after the ref's hand hits the mat for the third time, Havok's chair strikes the back of the covering Panther. Panther rolls off and struggles to his feet, only to be met by a chairshot to the top of the masked head that sends him tumbling from the ring. Havok raises the chair high in the ring to the approval of the fans as Slaughter continues... Result: Black Panther by Pinfall Courage vs. Wretch Warfare returns from commercials, and an ad hyping Cyberslam 2K to the sounds of Static-X's "I'm With Stupid". Wretch, Scott Hart, and Mike Bear's girlfriend, Brittany appear on the entrance ramp. Hart has Brittany by the wrist, and Wretch poses on the ramp with a condescending grin on his face, before continuing to the ring. Hart leads Brittany to the outside, as the almighty Korn's "Reclaim My Place" echoes throughout the E Center. Courage appears on the entrance ramp, International Title firmly wrapped around his waist. Courage raises his arms and soaks in the jeers from the crowd. Courage steps to ringside, looks at Scott Hart and laughs, because, well, he is Scott Hart, and steps up on to the ring steps. Anxious to start the match, Wretch begins pummeling Courage, before he can even get his title and jacket off. Wretch rips the jacket from him and throws it into the crowd. Courage finally is able to unstrap the International Title Belt, and Wretch sends him into the ropes. Wretch leapfrogs, and when Courage comes back, he drops down. Courage jumps over him, and when he bounces back, Wretch takes him over with an arm drag. Courage pops up, and Wretch hits him with a dropkick that sends him to the outside. With Courage stunned, Wretch runs against the ropes, and looks for a springboard plancha, but when he comes off, Courage grabs him, and sends him over the guardrail. Courage follows Wretch into the crowd, and they begin brawling up the stairs in the E Center. Eventually, Wretch gains the upper hand, and with a punch to the jaw, Courage rolls down the stairs, and eventually ends up leaning up against the guardrail. Wretch runs at Courage, but Courage is able to back body drop him over the guardrail and onto the concrete floor. Courage rolls Wretch into the ring, and climbs the turnbuckles. Courage comes off with a flying elbow drop that would make Randy Savage and Shawn Michaels jealous, and connects. He gets on top of Wretch for the cover, but the former Underground member kicks out at two. Courage contemplates what to do next, and after a few punches to the face of Wretch, he places him on the top turnbuckle. Courage looks for a huracanrana, but he can't get Wretch over. Wretch hangs on, balances himself up, and comes off with a top-rope liger bomb. Wretch keeps it into a pinning situation, but Courage nails a double boot to the head to escape the pin. Wretch lifts Courage up by the hair, and takes him over with a snap suplex, putting him in prime position. Wretch climbs the turnbuckles, facing the crowd, but Brittany steps over, with salt in her hand. She throws the salt, but at the same time, Wretch put his head down, to get into position, and Wretch doesn't even notice that Brittany tried to cost him the match. Wretch finally gets into full position, and comes off with a moonsault. However, he took too long setting up, and Courage gets out of the way. Wretch hits face first, and Courage recovers, before picking Wretch up by the hair. He places Wretch on the top turnbuckle, and takes him over with a superplex. However, Wretch rolls it over, and lands on top of Courage in a pinning predicament. However, before Wretch can get the three count, and the title victory, Courage is able to kick out. They both pop up at the same time, and Wretch runs at Courage with a short arm clothesline. Courage ducks, and is able to plant him with a float over DDT. Courage taunts the crowd, before lifting Wretch up again. He hits Wretch with a boot to the gut, and then a power bomb. He keeps the power bomb locked, and lifts him up again. However, he can't quite get Wretch down, but Wretch is able to take it right into a pinning huracanrana. Courage is able to get out of it at two. On the outside, we see Scott Hart get a chair, but inside of the ring, Wretch whips Courage against the ropes. Courage ducks a clothesline, and stays behind. He plants Wretch with a power bomb, and sets him in perfect position for the Courage Required. Courage runs against the ropes, but Hart takes the chair, and whacks Courage right in the face with it. Courage hits the mat, and Wretch crawls over to where he's laying and drapes an arm over him. Three seconds later, Wretch has captured the fWo International Title. As the referee awards him his title, he looks into the camera and says "I've got gold, I've got your girl, life is good.", obviously as a shot to Bear. Warfare cuts to commercial, as Hart and Wretch celebrate inside of the ring, while Brittany looks on. Result: Wretch by Pinfall Mitch Wilson vs. Erik Kelly "No Leaf Clover" begins to play, and the fans pop as Erik Kelly, accompanied by Pamela Dee, steps out of the entranceway and heads to the ring. Kelly steps up and over the top rope into the ring as Dee takes her place outside. "Calling All Stations" starts up with its trademark intro guitar riff, and the fans begin to boo as "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson begins making his way to the ring, Internet Title wrapped securely around his waist. As he reaches the ring, Wilson unstraps the belt and hands it to the timekeeper, before rolling into the ring and warily eyeing his massive opponent. The bell rings, and Kelly immediately charges at Wilson for a clothesline, but Wilson ducks and hooks Kelly in a waist lock from behind. Wilson attempts to take him over with a German suplex, but the size of Kelly is too great and Kelly breaks the hold with an elbow to the side of the head. Mitch staggers back, and Kelly immediately knocks him down with a big boot to the face. Kelly covers, but Wilson kicks out at two. Wilson makes his way to his feet, and Kelly quickly grabs him and whips him across into the ropes, before lifting him up and planting him down with a bear hug spine buster. Kelly brings Wilson up again, and whips him into the ropes again. Kelly catches Wilson coming off, and slams him hard into the mat with a tilt-a-whirl sideslam. Kelly quickly hooks a leg, but the Internet Champion kicks out at two. Kelly brings Wilson up to his feet again, but Wilson strikes him on the way up with a low blow that evades the attention of the referee. Kelly is doubled over, and Wilson takes him over with a gut wrench suplex. Wilson covers, but Kelly easily kicks out at one, throwing the smaller Wilson to the side. Kelly makes his way easily to his feet, but he is met halfway up by a Wilson dropkick to the head that knocks him back down to the mat. Wilson now brings the larger man up himself, and sends him into the ropes. As Kelly comes off, Wilson leaps into the air and strikes with a leaping leg lariat that catches Kelly right on the throat. Kelly is staggered, and backs into the ropes. Wilson charges at him, and smashes him right on the jaw with a flying forearm, but still Kelly doesn't go down. Getting frustrated, Wilson quickly makes his way up the turnbuckles to the top, and leaps off at Kelly with a double axhandle. However, Kelly blocks the axhandle blow with his hands and wraps his right hand around the neck of Wilson, looking for a choke slam. Wilson struggles for a moment, but then slips free of the hold, dropping down and tripping Kelly with a form of a DTH that sends the larger man neck-first onto the second rope. Wilson quickly slides to the outside and begins pulling down on the neck of Kelly, choking him. The ref gives Wilson a five-count to break it, and Wilson does so at four. Wilson slaps Kelly in the face to add insult to injury before rolling back into the ring and bringing Kelly up to his feet. Wilson whips Kelly into the ropes, and leaps at him as he comes off. Kelly catches Wilson in mid-hurricanrana, but Wilson fights back with a series of fists that strike Kelly right between the eyes, and then quickly takes the larger man down the with the hurricanrana and hooks both legs. The ref's count reaches two, but Kelly manages to kick out in time. Wilson brings Kelly up to his feet again, and bounces off the ropes. Wilson spins and attempts a rolling elbow, but Kelly ducks it. Wilson turns and sees Kelly winding up for a clothesline from hell, and Wilson ducks as well. As Kelly turns to face Wilson after missing his move, he is met by the elbow area of Wilson smashing into his chest. Kelly staggers, and Wilson winds up again, before smashing Kelly with a second rolling elbow, this one augmented by a leap that causes it to smash Kelly directly in the face. Wilson covers Kelly, but Kelly again kicks out at two. Wilson brings Kelly once more to his feet and attempts a whip into the ropes, but Kelly reverses it and maneuvers Wilson into a pumphandle position, before lifting Wilson up and slamming him into the mat with a ring-rocking pumphandle slam. Kelly brings Wilson up once more and lets the crowd know that he plans to win the Internet Title with a shout of "Power bomb!". Kelly sets up Wilson for the move, but tosses him aside when he sees that on the outside of the ring, Dead has grabbed Pamela Dee and is threatening her. Kelly quickly slides to the outside and charges at Dead, who shoves Dee aside and, as Mitch Wilson distracts the ref inside the ring, spits the black mist into the eyes of Kelly. Kelly is blinded, and Dead DDTs him on the outside before rolling him into the ring. Wilson lets the referee go and heads over to Kelly before bringing him to his feet facing a corner. Wilson leaps onto the second rope, before leaping off at Kelly, hooking him in mid-air in position for the Headliner, and spinning him around in the manner of a swinging DDT and planting him into the mat with a Headliner. Wilson hooks the leg of Kelly, and the referee tolls the three-count and registers the win for the retaining Internet Champion Mitch Wilson. As Dead rolls into the ring and he and Wilson begin to put the boots to Kelly, "O Fortuna" begins to play and the fans pop. The lights suddenly black out, and after about 15 seconds of this, they come back on to reveal Mitch Wilson and Dead laid out in the ring with a hangman's noose draped over both. Erik Kelly stands tall in the ring with Pamela Dee, holding the Internet Title high, before throwing back at the body of Mitch Wilson and exiting the ring. Slaughter rolls on with the fans at a fever pitch due to this mysterious development... Result: Mitch Wilson by Pinfall Guano Apes vs. Ric Chronos & Fallen Warfare returns as the sounds of Anthrax's "Time" rocks the E Center in West Valley City, Utah. Ric Chronos and Fallen, the challengers, step out onto the entrance ramp and revel in the jeers from the crowd. Fallen and Chronos step into the ring, and await the arrival of their opponents, the Guano Apes. Suddenly, "War?" by System of a Down starts up, and the reigning, and seemingly unstoppable fWo Tag Team Champions, Sphere and Jackhammer, the Guano Apes step onto the entrance ramp. As fireworks shoot off into the upper parts of the arena, the Apes continue their trek to the ring. As they step in, Sphere and Jackhammer play paper scissors rock to determine who will play first. Sphere can't quite handle the game, so he forfeits, letting Jackhammer "win" the honor to start off the match with Fallen. Jackhammer meets with the deranged individual in the middle of the ring, and they lock up. Jackhammer, who has the obvious power advantage in this matchup sends Fallen into the ropes. Fallen ducks a short arm clothesline, and bounces back off of the ropes with a flying shoulder block. Fallen follows it up with an arm drag, turned into an armbar. Jackhammer looks for a way to shimmy out of the move, and he does. Jackhammer uses the go behind to take Fallen over in a release German suplex, a move that the big man doesn't use often, but it worked effectively there. Jackhammer climbs on for a cover, but Chronos steps in, and nails a boot to the head, to break up the count. Chronos flees from the ring, but Jackhammer looks enraged inside. Jackhammer orders Fallen to go over and tag Chronos, because he wants a piece of him. Fallen seemingly obliges, and the "Man of the Hour", Ric Chronos steps into the ring. The two big men go face to face in the ring, before Chronos nails a punch to the jaw of Jackhammer, that sends the big man reeling. Chronos follows it up with a whip to the ropes. Jackhammer comes off, and Chronos plants him with a power slam. Chronos covers, but Jackhammer kicks out violently, sending Chronos halfway across the ring. Chronos backs off to his corner, and tags in Fallen, as Jackhammer decides to bring Sphere into the matchup. Fallen immediately runs at Sphere, taking him down with a nicely executed spear. Fallen pummels Sphere, before bringing him up, and taking him down with a snap suplex. Fallen climbs the turnbuckles, and sets himself up. Fallen comes off with a guillotine legdrop, but Sphere rolls out of the way. Sphere runs, and takes Fallen down with a clothesline, and then he runs over to where Chronos is situated on the ring apron, and takes him down with a punch. Chronos goes flying, and hits the guardrail. However, Fallen has recuperated from the clothesline, and he gets behind Sphere. Fallen gets him with a rollup, but Sphere gets out at two. Both men reach their feet at the same time, but a double clothesline happens, and both men are down. As they struggle to get to their feet, Chronos and Jackhammer await the tags. Both men reach their corners at the same time, and tag in their respective partners. Chronos and Jackhammer hit the ring, and begin trading fists. Chronos takes the advantage, and whips Jackhammer into the ropes. He nails Jackhammer with a boot to the gut, before setting him up for a DDT. However, Jackhammer nails a few punches to the ribs of Chronos to break the situation. With it broken up, Jackhammer nails a boot to the gut, followed by a power bomb. He covers, and three seconds later, the Guano Apes have retained their titles. The Apes gather their titles, and across the ring, Chronos and Fallen stare at them, along with Fear and Hellrazor, who are standing on the entrance way. Result: Guano Apes by Pinfall Flying Frenchie vs. Eron Slaughter was about to conclude as the cameras headed to the ring for the main event of the evening. "Physical" by Nine Inch Nails began, and out from the backstage area emerged Eron The Relentless. Eron entered the ring and slumped in a corner, before awaiting his opponent of the night, and the man he prevented from winning Body Count a mere two weeks ago. "Juke Joint Jezebel" began to boom throughout the E Center, and the Flying Frenchie stepped out onto the ramp, directly below the SlaughterScreen. Frenchie began making the long walk down the ramp. Both men were not liked by the fans, but if the fans had to choose someone to cheer for tonight, the unanimous favorite would be the Frenchie. Frenchie strolled into the ring, before taking off his beret. Sitting atop his mask was a package of breath mints, of which Frenchie offered to Eron. Eron looked at Frenchie in disgust, as a cocky smile formed on the mouth of the former three-time fWo World Champion. Eron remained slumped in the corner, and Frenchie looked at Eron confidently. Frenchie held the package of breath mints in one hand, before charging at Eron and trying to stuff it down the throat of Eron. Eron quickly slid out onto the floor, holding his mouth, as Frenchie did get a shot in. Eron began to walk away from the ring, before he headed towards the ramp. Seeing this, Frenchie baseball slid out onto the floor, before turning Eron around and slapping him across the face. Frenchie then kicked him in the stomach, before hitting a swinging neck breaker on the floor. Frenchie grabbed Eron by his red hair and slid him into the ring. He climbed up onto the top rope before waiting for Eron to get up, and then he flew through the air before hitting a missile dropkick right into the face of Eron, which sprawled him onto the mat like so. Frenchie got up and went for a cover, but Eron kicked out at two, promptly. The Frenchie continued, waiting for Eron to get up, before using his thumb to stab Eron in the eye, which is of course patented and trademarked by the superstar from Toulouse. Eron held his eye as he staggered around, before Frenchie whipped him to the ropes, and taking him to the mat with a hurracanrana. A cover ensued, but Eron kicked out at two. Frenchie brought Eron up quickly and laid him out with a Michinoku Driver, before going up to the top rope... he went for the Guillotine Legdrop, but somehow Eron moved out of the way, and Frenchie hit the mat with a sickening thud. Frenchie' leg was in serious pain, and Eron took advantage of it. As Frenchie pulled himself up, Eron went after the injured leg with a dropkick to it, which sent Frenchie to the mat. He then went up to the top rope, and flew off with a knee into the upper thigh of the Frenchie. ETR continued on the offensive, this time bringing Frenchie up and hitting a beautifully executed hanging vertical suplex. He then locked in a sharpshooter, before working him over with it. Frenchie struggled to get out of it, but it was of no use, Eron had it locked in. Frenchie writhed in pain, but he crawled closer and closer to the ropes to break free, and finally he did. Eron reluctantly let the hold go, before he taunted the Frenchie, and brought him out to the center of the ring. He locked him in the De-Roguer, and continued to obliterate the Frenchie. Primetime began walking to the ring quickly to help the Frenchie, but he did not want to get his friend disqualified. He sat at ringside before Mitch Wilson came down to the ring, and from behind he turned Primetime around. Now face to face, they exchanged pleasantries, before engaging in a game of fisticuffs. Inside the ring, however, Eron saw this going on, before he released the hold. Wilson threw Primetime into the guardrail, and Primetime hit back first into it. The guardrail collapsed and Primetime ended up in a heap from behind. Eron however snuck up on Mitch from behind with a chair, before laying him out. He stood over Mitch and looked at him in disgust. Primetime came up and began kicking Mitch on the floor, but Eron took him by the side and hip tossed him onto the ground. Perfect came down and he and Eron began trash talking, but from behind came Scott Collins, and the two began to brawl. Their brawl spilled near the crowd, and from behind Eron came and slammed his chair into both of their backs. He stood over them, looking at them cockily, before looking at the booing crowd, and the destruction he had inflicted. The referee was unaware of how to call the match, as Frenchie pulled himself up in the ring. Eron quickly used his chair with Frenchie near the ropes, before slamming it face first into him. Frenchie spilled back in the ring. Eron seemed happy at what had occurred. Ultra-Violet charged down to ringside, and slid inside the ring, before he used some purple spray paint to spray paint "UV" on the back of the Frenchie's shirt. UV seemed pleased at what he had done. Eron tossed the chair into the ring, and then headed backstage... after having knocked everyone silly with a chair, he surely would now be public enemy number one among the fWo. UV saw the chair, and used it to sit down on it and sing songs, as Slaughter came to a close. Result: Flying Frenchie by DQ
