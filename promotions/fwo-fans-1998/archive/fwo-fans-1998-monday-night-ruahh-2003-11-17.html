Introduction In a land far away, the fWo struggles through holidays away from home, and the impending doom of Countdown... Scratching the Surface An interrogation had already begun. The person being interrogated had absolutely no clue and that made it all the more interesting. The mysterious cigar-smoking individual in the brown fedora made himself present yet again. He had a job to do and nothing was about to stand in his way. Nobody knew this man, yet he knew himself all too well. Every movement of his head only brought his face further into darkness as he questioned Marcus Harrington about the dilemma fWo was currently facing. Each question brought about a clueless look. This staff member obviously knew nothing about what was going on, yet he the figure still haunted over him, drilling him with questions about the night in question. "I.. I.. I have no clue. I just sign the documents for the shipments we get. I maintain the inventory and that's it. I want no trouble. I got a wife and kids at home." Another puff of that dreadful cigar. "Listen son. I'm just curious that's all. We've got our order too you know. The C & Y Trucking Company takes pride in our shipments. That's what we're all about. When police start questioning us about the contents, we tend to get a little upset. We're stuck in the middle right now. I just want to know what you know so I can contact my superior." The staff member took a deep breath and let it all out. "Alright then, I don't see why I can't help you out a little bit. After all, it's not your fault this mess happened. Well, let me start off by telling you that we've been getting these shipments for two years now. Of course, that's when we signed the contract with you guys. The buzz going around is that the contents of the box were some type of drug or something. "Those wouldn't be Cuban would they?" Another puff. "Nah." Marcus would continue. "Well, whatever was in there sure pissed of Silver Fox. He's been in so many meetings lately. I hear that the agents are just itchin for us to get back to the states so they can tear fWo a new one," he finished. "Whose idea was it to move fWo televised shows out of the U.S.?" Marcus scratched his head while looking up at the lights on the roof. He searched for an answer. "I hear that it's already been planned for some time now. fWo is solid in their scheduling. I'd say it was a coincidence. A fortunate one at that." Another puff that engulfed both of them in thick grey smoke. "Anything else funny about the night of the DEA raid?" A sudden thought popped up that seemed of some importance "There was this one guy there. He was kinda big and pretty mean looking. He was questioned only to quickly retaliate from any answers. He disappeared as soon as the bust." The final puff. "Thanks for all the help. I'll be sure to let my supervisor know." Marcus walked away confident with the answers he'd given. He continued walking down the hallway while the silhouette of a man looked on. Too bad he didn't work for the truck company. Too bad he wasn't who people thought he was. Within a second, Aimz was standing beside him, before she flew over the top rope with a Slingshot to drop a leg across the back of Elliot's head, guillotining him across the middle rope. Elliot dropped onto his back in the ring, while Aimz pulled herself up on the outside and made her way to the corner. Still slightly favouring her right knee, Aimz climbed to the top and balanced herself while Elliot staggered up to his feet. The crowd knew what was coming, and responded accordingly, cheering in expectation of the Dead Aim. Seeing her on the top, and also knowing what was coming, Elliot reacted quickly and flung himself towards the ropes, yanking on the top stand to make Aimz lose her footing... Or she would of, if she hadn't seen him make the lunge and dropped back down to the apron. Elliot turned, looking shocked that she was standing on the apron, waving with a smile before Springboarding onto the top rope, then off into a Corkscrew... ...Catching a shocked Elliot's head with a DDT and spiking him into the mat. Elliot didn't move a muscle as Aimz screwed her face up, the move putting a lot of load through her right knee, but the win was there and she dropped her body over Elliot. 1..... 2..... 3!!! She then knew what it was like to retain the fWo United States Championship for the first time, and despite a nagging pain in her knee, it felt pretty damn good. The fans rose from their seats and applauded the win as the official held her arm aloft, then presented her once again with her belt. With the slightest of limps, Aimz headed to the corner and pulled herself onto the middle rope to hold her title aloft for the cameras, leaving Elliot on the mat, still not moving... ...and having to wait a little bit longer for his fame. Result: Aimz via Pinfall : Retains US Title
