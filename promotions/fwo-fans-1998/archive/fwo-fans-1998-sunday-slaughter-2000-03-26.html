fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 26 March 2000

26 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Last night... in one of the most brutal contests the world of professional wrestling has ever seen, VL became an eight time fWo Hardcore Champion... a record that will likely never be surpassed. But at what cost? VL and Hellrazor will never be the same again. Last night... two former champions were winners once more, as Primetime and Scott Collins defeated their arch enemies Perfect and Mitch Wilson. But fate is unkind and nothing was settled. All four men are on a collision course that can only end at CyberSlam. Last night... in their final match the Mad Trucker had the spirit of Elvis on his side and despite some help from the pop icon band of the last century, Machina was unable to defeat him. With his wedding mere days away, what is next for the man called Machina... Last night... Mike Bear fought alone against one of the most celebrated men the fWo has ever seen... and was victorious. Despite his greatest efforts, the Flying Frenchie was thwarted. The question remains... what does the Frenchie have up his sleeve next... Last night... in a match that many thought would be an easy victory for Lance Star and Chris Universal, the Tag Team Champions the Guano Apes firmly established themselves as the tag team of the new millennium as they retained their titles. But their rest will be short lived, for challengers lie in wait around every corner... Last night... thirty men entered the ring with the greatest prize in the entire wrestling industry at stake. The fWo World Heavyweight Championship. Wrestlers fought with everything they had, but in the end it was the selfish actions of one man that condemned the fWo to the dire title reign of Dead. Now, with things looking their darkest, the fWo prepares to enter their third year... with everything at stake. CyberSlam is thirty five days away. Everything at stake The crowd sat nervously as they awaited the beginning of one of the biggest shows in fWo history. The ring announcer began to count from ten down as Slaughter was set to begin. He reached one, and a sudden explosion of pyros burst throughout the arena. Fans stood to their feet and cheered as the beginning of Slaughter rapidly unfolded. As the announcers welcome the fans at home to the show, "Do you buy what The Sellout Is Selling?" blasted throughout the place where Michael Jordan and the Chicago Bulls celebrated six championship victories, twenty three thousand simultaneous cheers echoed throughout the United Center. Chris Universal walked out onto the stage, and the deafening cheer grew louder as he approached the ring very business like. Universal quickly retrieved a microphone, before he commenced speaking. "I am here for one reason and one reason only... last night I came to the realization that Lance and I aren't quite good enough to become the fWo World Tag Team Champions, and we may never be. We've had countless opportunities, and each and every time we've failed to get the job done. I don't blame it on him, I don't blame it on anyone else, I blame it on me. If I'm as good as I say I am, I should be able to keep up my end of the bargain and help us win a championship. But, I haven't done that. And I'm not good at accepting failure, I never have been. When I do fail, something happens, and it's as if something takes me over and I can't recover. Well, unless I accomplish something here tonight, and unless I achieve my goal, I will never recover. All of my life, I've had a goal. A dream. Not only to wrestle, not only to get rich while wrestling, but to become the World Champion of a major organization. I guess you could compare me to my mentor Mick Foley... he wants to main event a WrestleMania, well... The Sellout wants to main event a CyberSlam. And history shows that the World Title is defended in the main event, and you cannot be in the main event unless your defending or competing for that very title. Well, I'm assuming this year will be no different. I want to be in the main event of CyberSlam, and by God, if I have anything to say about it, I will be in the main event at CyberSlam if you buy what The Sellout is selling!" The fans cheered, while Universal acknowledged their response before continuing. "Since I began my career in the fWo, it seems as if from that very moment; I've had a thorn in my side. That thorn was Austin Davis. That thorn is now known as Dead. And that thorn is now the fWo World Champion. Well, he says that he's going to ruin CyberSlam, and no offense to anyone else in the locker room, if there's one person that can prevent him from doing that, it's *me*. We've battled for nearly two years, I know him like the back of my hand... well, I'm not going to let him ruin CyberSlam. I'm not going to let him even reach CyberSlam. At the same time, I know he's not going to just come out and give me a World Title shot; but I would like the ugly bald headed son of a bitch to get his angry ass out here so I can speak to him face to face and man to man!" On cue, a red light showered the SlaughterStage, and "Beautiful People" echoed throughout the United Center as fog eminated from from fog machines throughout the arena. The fans booed loudly as the newly crowned fWo World Champion walked out onto the stage. He looked at Universal with disdain as Universal cut him off before he could speak. "I don't want to hear but one word from you and when I wish to hear that word from you I will ask for it. Got it? Good. I'm sure you've heard what I've said so far, and despite the fact that you may not agree with me, you must understand where I'm coming from. All I've got to say is this. Tonight, you and me. Put that fWo World Title on the line. But... let's raise the stakes. Let's not stop there. Few may remember this but last year at CyberSlam, I said I would put my career on the line and I did. If I lost, I was going to retire. But I didn't... and I won. At Trapped, you put your career on the line, you said if you lost, you would retire. SummerSlaughter proved that when it comes to being a man of your word when you put your career on the line, you are a man of your word. You put it on the line, you spent the duration of the six month time frame outside of the ring before you could return to active wrestling. Since we have both put our careers on the line in separate matches, let's take it just one step further tonight. fWo World Title on the line. My career on the line. Your career on the line. One of us walks away the fWo World Champion and is in the main event of CyberSlam, the other may or may not walk away, but they leave the sport *tonight*. All I want to know is, do you accept? Say yes or no, and I'll walk away. One word, that's all I want to hear." The fans cheered as Dead contemplated the situation presented to him. After not even ten seconds, he responded with a resounding "Yes." Universal raised his arms and the crowd cheered loudly, before Slaughter went to a commerical break reeling from this shocking development. Scott Collins vs. Hiroshima As we move on to our next match, Hiroshima begins to make his way methodically down to ringside. The crowd shows their hatred for the Japanese legend with loud boos, and chants of 'you suck'. He reaches the ring, and stands with his arms raised high, just in time to have a cup full of soda strike him in the head. Hiroshima wipes the soft drink away from his face without so much as a change from his stone-carved expression. Turning towards the entrance ramp, he is just in time to spot the number 4 appearing on the TravisTron as 'Burning' by ManOWar beings to play throughout the sound system. The crowd surprisingly bursts into cheers as Scott Collins appears on the ramp, his arms folded and a confident look on his face. Collins points down at Hiroshima who is beckoning him to the ring, before pulling his shirt over his head and charging headlong towards his opponent. Collins slides under the bottom ropes, and the bell rings to start the match. Immediately, Hiroshima begins to lay the boots to the North Carolina powerhouse, attempting to keep him off of his feet. Hiroshima drapes Collins' throat on the bottom rope before driving a knee into his neck, causing him to roll about the canvas clutching at his larynx and gasping for air. Sensing he has the match under control, Hiroshima grabs Collins by the hair before clamping his hand around his throat, and whispering something into the leader of the Riders' ear. He then tosses Collins into the corner like a rag doll and charges in after him with a massive shoulder block... only Collins has the sense to slide out of the way, leaving Hiroshima caught halfway over the top turnbuckle. Acting quickly, Scott grabs the temporarily stunned Hiroshima under the chin and snaps off a painful hangman's neckbreaker. As both men get to their feet, Collins tackles Hiroshima to the mat and unleashes a furious barrage of punches to his face, drawing a trickle of blood from the FTfWo strongman's nose and lip. At the 4 count of the beating, the referee grabs onto Collins' arm, only to be shoved aside prompting a stern warning, and a shouting match between the two. During this time, Hiroshima has rolled over, and hits a low blow on Collins and a quick roll-up which gets a one count. Both men reach a vertical base once again and lock up with the advantage going to Collins as youth wins over experience for the moment. He whips Hiroshima into the ropes and catches him in a press slam, roaring in anger as he launches him 2/3rds of the way across the ring with a crash. A running elbow drop and a two count quickly follow, before Collins once again sends Hiroshima across the ring, catching him with a Hot Shot across the top rope on his way back, perhaps as payback for the early going in the match. As Hiroshima lays motionless on the mat, Collins stands over him talking trash and emphasizing each word with a kick to the face. He pulls Hiroshima to his feet and hits a T-bone suplex with authority. He moves to lock in a standing figure four on the large frame of Hiroshima, and in no time has it cinched in right in the center of the ring. As Hiroshima slails attempting to escape the move, Collins looks up towards the ramp and his face contorts in anger. Perfect and Mr. Main Event Mitch Wilson have begun to stroll down towards the ring mocking Collins who continues to bear down on the submission hold while Hiroshima begins to fade out from the pain. Just when it appears victory is within his grasp, the two members of the FTfWo make it to the ring apron and jump up prompting the ref to run to keep Perfect from interfering in the match. Clearly upset by this turn of events, Collins releases the hold and launches a superkick at Perfect which sends the former fWo champion flying from the ring apron and onto the announce table with a resounding crash. The ref however, has also caught some of the blow, having been pulled into the way by Perfect at the last second and now is on one knee in the corner attempting to clear the cobwebs from his mind. Undaunted, Collins reaches for a hobbling Hiroshima and snaps him into position for the Scotty Dropper. As he turns to hit the move that would most certainly end the match, he has no time to react before Mitch Wilson and 10 pounds of Internet Title gold blast him in the forehead with a flying strike from the top rope. Collins drops to the mat like an anchor, and Wilson slips out under the bottom rope like a snake, taking his Internet title with him. The ref has recovered by this time and sees Hiroshima with a small package on Collins, even pretending that Collins is struggling to get out of it, and dives into position to make the count. A triple slap of the mat, and Hiroshima is the winner. With a scowl he pulls his hand away from the ref, and rolls to the outside to help Wilson who is busy trying to get Perfect back to his feet. The three men begin to make their way back up the ramp, but not before Collins, still groggy from the belt to the head, pulls himself up by the ropes and points to the trio yelling that this one is far from being over. Result: Hiroshima by Pinfall Traffic jam Wretch looked at the masse of cars and trucks in front of him. He sighed. He picked up his car phone, before dialing. It rang several times, before a familiar voice answered on the other end. It was his close friend Mike Bear. "Hello?" "Hey Mike, this is Chris. I'm on my way to the United Center now, but I'm stuck in traffic. I'm sure you've heard about how bad the traffic is here in Illinois... well, I'm from New York, and I've never seen anything this bad." Bear replied. "Hey, no big deal. When do you think you'll be here? Soon? We've got that huge tag team title match tonight... will you be here soon?" Wretch gathered his thoughts quickly, before responding. "Yeah. I should be there in the next half hour or so. If I don't make it... well, how is Hellrazor? Is he doing any better?" "Nah... he's probably still in the hospital, I ought to call him to see how he is doing. He went through a war last night... but, as you were saying?" "Well, I was going to have him take my place if I can't make it... what about Fear, is he around?" "No, Fear wasn't scheduled to be here tonight, so I don't think he's here... I hope you hurry, man. Tonight is our night. Tonight is the night we reclaim our titles." "Indeed. Well, traffic looks to be picking up a bit, so I'll be there soon. See you in a few, Mike." "Yeah, later buddy.... hey... about that chairshot last night..." Before he could go on, Wretch had hung up. Bear sighed, before pacing in his lockerroom... STUMP Squad vs. Chaos "We're Not Gonna Take It" hits the PA system as Slaughter returns, bringing the STUMP Squad down to ringside. Black Quicksilver and Mike Heftel come to the ring to cheers from the fans. As they warm up in the ring, Metallica blares, and the team of Chaos, War and Plague, come out to a somewhat mixed reaction (despite more boos). They hit the apron and stare down their opponents in this tag-team contest. Heftel and Quicksilver discuss who will start the contest, and when War goes back through the ropes onto the apron, BQ is chosen as the STUMP Squad. The bell rings, and the two competitors circle each other before locking up. An armbar by Plague is reversed by BQ, which is reversed by a somersault by Plague. BQ returns a somersault of his own, and hits Plague with a spinwheel kick to the head. Plague hits the mat, but has enough wits about him to trip the feet of Black Quicksilver. As BQ falls to the mat, Plague hits a quick ankle-lock submission hold. However, they are right by the ropes, and BQ quickly grabs them, causing Plague to drop the hold. Plague rises to his feet, and sends BQ to the ropes. BQ reverses the whip and sends Plague into the ropes. BQ kicks Plague in the gut, and goes for a rocker dropper. However, as he puts his foot up, War (who was tagged in when Plague went into the ropes), grabs BQ by the neck and chokeslams him to the mat. War picks up a seemingly lifeless BQ and hits an inverted powerslam. War tags in Plague and they start to double-team the helpless STUMP Squad member. Mike Heftel tries to interfere, but the ref stops him. As the ref is tied up by Heftel's interference, Plague chokes BQ in the ropes, while War grabs a chair and nails the head of BQ, causing a chorus of boos throughout the crowd. BQ falls back onto the mat, and Plague rolls him up. 1, 2, and BQ kicks out just in time. Plague picks up BQ and sends him to the ropes. Plague drops his head for a backdrop, but BQ, for a split second, wakes up and hits a swinging neckbreaker. Both wrestlers are down on the mat, as the ref beings to count both men down. At about the seven count, both wrestlers show signs of life. The crowd starts to chant "HEF-TEL! HEF-TEL!", as BQ crawls his way toward his corner. Meanwhile, Plague reaches his corner, and tags in the big War. As War starts to come into the corner of the STUMP Squad, BQ tags in Mike Heftel, who comes in with fists-of-fire. BQ returns to action and begins brawling with Plague. Heftel hits War once, but with no effect. Twice, and again, no effect. Heftel remembers something he sees on the WWF, and spits his fist. He hits War again, but War doesn't go down. Heftel ponders why he doesn't go down...and while he is doing this, War kicks Heftel in the gut. War sets up Heftel for his finisher, the Exploder. As he begins to put him in the reverse powerbomb, Plague hits BQ, who runs into War. War falls, with Heftel on top of him. The ref counts to three, and the STUMP Squad is victorious. Chaos just looks at the two wrestlers and leave, as BQ and Heftel begin to celebrate their victory. As this continues, Slaughter heads to a break. Result: STUMP Squad by Pinfall Demands The Frenchie barged into Chris Finn's office without knocking. Chris Finn and James Armstrong looked up from the documents they were going over and Chris Finn nodded. "Frenchie. I suspected I would be seeing you today. Have a seat." Chris Finn pointed to a chair, but the Frenchie stood where he was. His eyes narrowed. "I am not here to demand ze match. I am not here because I want ze pile of crap in ze ring. Quite ze contrary. What happened last night was ze most pathe'ic crap I have ever seen... and I 'ave seen Scott Slugger wrestle. Now I am not requesting... ze Frenchie is ordering. Eron must be fired. Now. Tonight." Chris Finn looked at James and the two sighed. James walked slowly toward the Frenchie and put an arm around one shoulder. "Listen Pierre... I know you're angry right now, and who could blame you? Chris and I agree... what Eron did is... totally unacceptable. But we can't just fire him. He's got a contract... and like it or not, he is a draw for this company." The Frenchie looked hard at James. "Ze draw. Ze man who single handedly make ze main event ze joke. Fine." The Frenchie turned and started to walk out of the room, but as he neared the door he turned and looked back with an expression that caused James to take a step backward in its pure hatred. "But my my words... leaving Eron in zis promotion is a mistake, and I refuse to have any part in it." The Frenchie then turned and walked out of the room, leaving Chris Finn and James Armstrong to ponder the rocky future that was before them... Courage vs. Havok Slaughter comes back with the music of Havok, who receives a great reaction from the crowd. He warms up in the ring for this chance at some fWo gold. As he does this, "Reclaim my Place"hits and Courage, the fWo International Champion, comes out on the entrance ramp. The fans stand to address him, whether it be with cheers or boos. The only thing that is certain is that it is a large reaction. Courage walks down to the ring, holding his belt high in the air. He hits the apron and looks at Havok, before climbing to the second turnbuckle to showcase his championship belt. Courage turned and faced Havok, and the bell is rung, starting the match. Both wrestlers circle each other, before going to lock up. As Havok makes an attempt at locking up, Courage ducks and trips Havok to the mat. Courage laughs at his opponent and plays to the crowd for a minute, as Havok gets up. Havok looks to lock up again with Courage; however, before he does this, he delivers a kick to the gut of Courage. He then grabs Courage by the head and suplexes him to the mat. Havok picks Courage up and hits a standing dropkick, sending Courage through the ropes and onto the mat. Havok starts to get momentum off the ropes opposite where Courage is beginning to get up. He returns and flies over the top rope, nailing Courage with a moonsault. Both men hit the outside, and Havok gets up and begins to pound on Courage. Havok picks up Courage and throws him into the steel steps. Courage bounces off of them and crumples to the mat. Havok grabs a chair and uses it on Courage's head, not once, but twice. Courage seems out of it, and Havok tosses him back into the ring, and goes for a pin and the championship. Courage, however, has other plans, and kicks out at two. Havok is somewhat surprised, but knows what he has to do to win the belt. He picks up Courage and starts to attack his right knee, kicking out the leg repeatedly. Courage hits the mat, where Havok begins to wear down the knee with elbows and shots with his own knee. Havok sees that he is in the middle of the ring, so he decides to put Courage into a figure-four. As he starts to do this, Courage somehow grabs Havok's tights and rolls him into a small package. It gets a two-count, and is broken by Havok reversing it, putting Courage's shoulders to the mat in the small package. A two-count is gotten before yet another reversal, which gains another two-count. Havok and Courage both get up, and Havok runs to the ropes. Courage tries to clothesline him upon his return, but Havok ducks it and whips into the opposite ropes. Courage takes off to the ropes as well. As both of them meet, they clothesline each other. The ref begins a ten-count. At five, Courage gets up. Limping over to Havok, he picks him up and hits a suplex on him. He picks him up again and delivers a chop to Havok's chest, knocking the wind out of him. Courage takes off once again for the ropes. He dives into Havok, except it's not Havok. Havok ducked Courage's diving lariat. Instead, it is the ref who suffers the blow from Courage. With the ref knocked out, Courage and Havok trade blows, before Havok hits a quick DDT. He senses victory, but does not know the ref is out. He puts Courage into a sharpshooter and tries to make him submit. Courage begins to tap, but it does not matter; the ref is out. Havok continues the hold, before switching to a figure-four leglock. Courage is withering in pain as Havok continues to apply pressure on the injured knee. Seeing that he has the match won, he breaks the hold and starts to wake up the ref. What he doesn't see is the 6'10" frame of the Black Panther running down the ramp, chair in hand. He hits the apron and sneaks up behind Havok. He pokes Havok on the shoulder. Havok turns around and is floored by a vicious chair shot by the Panther, which brings a massive negative reaction. The fans sense that this incredible match by the two superstars will not have a clean victory to it. Panther picks up Courage and drapes an arm over Havok, and then picks up the ref, waking him in the process. The ref counts... 1...2...and 3, and Courage retains his title once again. Panther looks down at Havok, spitting on him and laughing before walking away. Medics arrive to tend to Courage's knee as Slaughter heads to a commercial break. Result: Courage by Pinfall Internet rumors Backstage Matt Loco spotted Chris Finn stepping out of an office. Putting on his best face, Matt Loco approached with camera raised. He snapped a photo of Chris Finn as he turned to see who was coming, and Chris Finn blinked back against the glare of the flash bulb. "God dammit Loco... what did I tell you about doing that?" Loco stammered an apology, but it led directly in to the question he had on his mind. "Chris... Chris... can you tell us anything about the rumors?" Finn's eyes narrowed, and he looked suspiciously at the reporter. "Rumors? I'm afraid you will have to give me more to go on than that..." Loco looked incredulously at Finn and continued. "You know what I'm talking about... it's been all over the internet! Cody Williams... the Crusader... is it true that he is negotiating with the fWo?" Finn's expression turned sour. "Look... you can trust that trash you read on the internet. No comment... Loco... no comment." Finn walked away from Loco, leaving nothing answered... Guano Apes vs. Underground Slaughter returned from the commercial break and the cameras took to ringside. "War?" by System of a Down began to play and out came the reigning fWo World Tag Team Champions The Guano Apes. The crowd applauded them favorably as they were victorious last night in basically upsetting Chris Universal & Lance Star, the odds on favorites to win. Sphere and Jackhammer reached the ring with the gold wrapped tightly around their waists, before handing them to the referee who held them in full view for the fans to see. As their music died down, "Dragula" by Rob Zombie blared throughout the United Center, before Mike Bear came out to an overwhelming reaction from the crowd. Obviously, since only Mike Bear was present, Wretch had not yet made it to the arena, further proving why the traffic system in Chicago sucks. Mike Bear looked around, as he had to go it alone in the Underground's bid to regain the fWo World Tag Team Titles. As he neared the ring, suddenly his music came to a halt. The lights blacked out, and the SlaughterScreen came to life. The scene opens up to a small, dark room again, a now familiar scene. The camera pans to show Wretch sitting Indian style in front of the set just like the numerous times before, the same expression of a brainwashed child on his face. As he sits in the room he watches a videotape of Mike Bear confronting him about the recent brainwashing scandal. A shadow once again emerges from the darkness, and rests his hand upon Wretch's shoulder. The unfamiliar voice spoke... "Wretch... I've told you time and time again all the problems with Mike Bear. How he can't let you win, how he needed to hold on to *your* coat-tails to make it here, how he makes more enemies than allies, how he's held you back... but do you know the major problem with him is?" Wretch looked up, for the first time with a smirk on his face. Wretch began speaking... "How damn gullible he is?" Wretch laughs and stands up as the fans at ringside as well as Bear begin voicing their displeasure with the arrogant young man. "Ah... Mike Bear. You are quite possibly thee *dumbest* human being in the fWo ... like I actually needed to be brainwashed to see that you are nothing without me." Suddenly, someone merged from the shadows. It was ex-fWo wrestler Scott Hart, which prompted a chorus of loud boos from the crowd. He carried with him a paper, presumably a script, or plans that Wretch had given him over the last several weeks. Hart spoke... saying only "Uh, Wretch... can I have the money for the "Shadow Guy" bit now?" Wretch nodded and laughed, before removing a large stack of money from his pocket and handing them to Hart. Wretch continued... "There... go make yourself useful... as for you, Bear... it's my time. Ah... do you know how good that sounds? *MY*... TIME. My time for the titles, my time for the checks... my time to be the man. Not being forced to carry your ass because 'The fans like us'. Because Bear, *I* don't like you. I never have, and I never will. And why the people like you is one of thee biggest mysteries I've ever been faced with. To think, TO THINK, that you sat before me and actually said that *we're* a team. No, Bear... *I'm* the team. *We* were not the most popular stable... *I* was the most popular stable. When *I* felt like winning... we won. When *I* felt like losing... we lost. Honestly... Wretch unable to defeat the Guano Apes? Come off it, not even the *fans* are the stupid... Wretch... unable to land a super-kick? Wretch... unable to hit the right guy with a senton bomb? The facts kept adding up... and yet Bear, you *still* didn't see it. But now... maybe you do. Don't you see Bear? You're nothing without me... and it's going to be proven tonight that without me... you can't win. For tonight, you go it *alone* against the Guano Apes. Have fun, Bear... I know I will..." Wretch laughed arrogantly at his former friend and partner, who stood near ringside absolutely livid. His face turned bright red... you could almost picture his anger seeping out through his skin. He yelled, before charging up the ramp. He didn't care about the tag team titles anymore, he was presumably off in search of his former friend... Bear was hurt... and it appeared he not only intended to confront his ex-friend, he likely intended to kill him. The Apes stood in the ring with the referee, who decided to go ahead and award them the match. Jackhammer appeared angry, screaming something about the lack of competition around here lately. Result: Guano Apes by Forfeit Farewells "You ready?" Machina turned from the curtain that looked out at the main arena, and Rudo thought that he saw a tear in his eye. He started to ask, but Machina was already speaking. "Yes... I'm ready... the limo is here?" Rudo nodded. "It's out front... set to take us to the airport. We fly to Orlando, get on another limo that drives us to the cape. We get on the boat... and it's the bachelor party of your life! We were lucky to win that contest you know... it's gonna be great." Machina nodded, and smiled. "It's going to be... nice to be married. Penny is... well, she is what I've always dreamed of. We will be... very happy together I think." Machina didn't notice Rudo looking away at this and shuffling his feet. He had no way of knowing about the relationship that Rudo had been having with Penny over the last few months. Had he known, things might have gone differently... but he did not know. Machina had turned back to the curtain and was looking out at the crowd. "The only thing is Rudo...," and Machina sighed in a strange way that Rudo had never heard before. "This only this is... I've got this funny feeling... that I'm never going to see all this again." Dead vs. Chris Universal It was time. The show began with these two men and the show would end with these two men. Two wrestlers. One championship belt. One man would walk away the Champion, the other would never wrestle again. Chris Universal won the first battle. Austin Davis won the second. Chris Universal won the third. Dead won the fourth. Tonight would be the fifth and final battle, as this match would *never* *ever* happen again. "Do you buy what The Sellout is Selling?" is heard for the second time this evening, and Chris Universal walks out to an enormous reaction from the fans. With the reaction nearly deafening and certainly earsplitting, Universal is mobbed by the fans as he makes his way to the ring. Fans wish him the best, as this could in fact be the final time he ever wrestles. Suddenly, the house lights dim and fog rolls in through the entrance ramp as "Beautiful People" begins. The first forty seconds of the song build up to the familiar "repeated jarring", before the crescendo reaches a fever pitch. Finally, a trenchcoat sporting figure makes his way out to the stop of the stage, surrounded by fog as the familiar red spotlight bathes the arena for what could be the final time. He stares around the arena for several moments, Universal notes that Dead has decided to bring a friend tonight as a bag is slung over the right shoulder of the World Champion, with the fWo World Title hanging over the left. Undoubtedly a snake is likely inside the bag, which must have Universal concerned if not worried. Dead walks up the ring steps slowly, before ducking quickly into the ring, tossing the bag aside, and staring down his long-time enemy Universal. The lights slowly begin to come back up and Dead and Universal come face to face, verbally berating one another as only these two individuals can. Both men stop the talking and get down to business. Dead with a right, blocked by The Sellout. Dead with another, again blocked. Dead with a third, and the third time isn't the charm, as it is blocked again and this time returned with a solid left hook. Universal now grabs Dead by the collar before sending him to the mat by his trenchcoat, and at the same time removing it from his body. He then tosses the coat over the ropes out onto the floor, where a ring attendant picks it up and carries it to the back. Universal waits for Dead to rise to a vertical position, before charging and spearing him into the turnbuckles. The impact is so devestating that both men end up in the middle of the ring trying to come to, which leaves the referee only one choice but to begin a ten count. The crowd quietly boos as the referee reaches two, and then four, with the boo growing somewhat louder. Finally, Universal is able to drape one arm over Dead, and with the crowd cheering feverishly, the referee gets in position for a count. Dead shoulders out at two, and the match continues. Universal struggles up to a vertical base, and Dead does the same. The two men exchange punches in the ring, neither man getting a clear-cut advantage over the other. Dead finally gains the advantage and backs Universal into the ropes with more punches, before quickly whipping him across. Dead catches Universal with a lariat, knocking him down with tremendous impact. Dead capitalizes; quickly going after the neck with a hanging vertical suplex, letting the flow of oxygen & blood quicken to the brain before finally completing the move. Dead gets up to his feet, grimmacing at the crowd confidently, before he brings Universal up and sends him into the turnbuckles. Universal crashes in sternum first, and from behind Dead grabs him by the hair and takes him down with a reverse ddt. A cover ensues, but Dead only garners a count of two. Dead verbally absuses the referee to a point where the referee considers disqualifying him, but realizes the ramifications of this match are so important that he shouldn't. This gives Universal time to recover, and he charges in with a double axehandle, sending Dead to the mat. Universal takes a moment to catch his breath, but as Dead gets up, Universal quickly maneuvers himself into a postion where he can pummel him on the upper chest with hard forearms, backing him into the ropes. Universal goes for a whip, but Dead reverses. Dead then pulls Universal back for a short-arm clothesline, but Universal ducks and hooks Dead, bringing him down with a Shooting Star, perhaps in tribute to his good friend Lance Star. The ref counts, but Dead kicks out at two. Dead gets up quickly, but Universal quickly takes him back down again with a swinging neckbreaker. Dead gets up quickly again, and Universal charges at him, taking him over the top rope with a 'Cactus Jack' style clothesline, perhaps in tribute to his mentor and longtime friend Mick Foley. Universal waits for Dead to get up, before launching himself over also with a slingshot bodyblock, taking Dead down. The Sellout resumes a vertical stance, getting into the face of Dead and screaming at him before bringing him back up again. Universal backs up a few paces, before charging at Dead and smashing him in the face with a rolling elbow, which sends Dead sprawling near the Canadian announce position at ringside. Universal hops up onto the ring apron, before running at Davis and delivering a hard elbowdrop, again perhaps in tribute to Foley. Universal continues his attack, before he rolls onto Dead and peppers him with a multitude of rights and lefts from the mounted position. Dead is dazed and confused, and rightfully so since Universal has unloaded an assortment of offense over the course of the match. Universal yells in his face once more, before bringing him up and slamming his face repeatedly into the Canadian announcer's table. Universal shoves Dead onto the table, before driving a plethora of elbows into his chest, deep into his cold black heart. The bald headed madman reaches for his chest while Universal rolls back into the ring via underneath the bottom rope, before taking his time climbing up to the top rope. He measures Dead; before finally Universal leaps off the top rope with a legdrop, hitting it. The velocity of both men going through the table is absolutely amazing, the table crushing upon the impact of both men... succumbing to the overwhelming impact. Universal is up to his feet first, before he grabs Dead by the head and sends him into the squared circle underneath the ropes. He climbs in and slowly makes a cover. One. Two. Dead drapes a foot over the bottom rope. Nearly ten minutes have gone by in the match so far, and neither looked ready to stop wrestling any time soon. Universal pointed at the Spanish announcing position, before grabbing Dead by his bald head and preparing to whip him over the ropes out onto the floor, but Dead caught him offguard with a lowblow, before sending him through the ropes to the floor. This allowed Dead to rest for a moment, before he rolled to the outside. Broken, battered and bruised, Dead plopped Universal up onto the Spanish announcer's table where Pepe and Jose were located, perhaps praying for their lives. Dead climbed up onto the table and brought Universal up, before taking him down with the deadly Dead Death Drop, also known as the 'X-Factor' performed by X-Pac in the wwf. Both men crashed through the table. Bottles of water, shards of wood, glass from the monitors and papers scattered everywhere. Slowly, both men get to their feet, before Dead rolls Universal into the ring. He attempts a pinfall. One. Two. This time, Universal gets a shoulder up just as the referee's hand were to hit the mat for a third and final time. Dead is undaunted, but has no choice but to think of another way to end it. This time, he points to the English announcer's table, and brings Universal out to the floor once more. Propping him up on the table, he climbs up on it as well. The two men battle, exchanging roundhouse rights and lefts, before they each begin choking each other with both hands. The loss of oxygen causes both men to pass out if only briefly for a moment, causing both men to go straight down through the table. The referee, deciding that he could no longer let it go on, decided to begin a ten count. Counting fast, he reached seven before somehow Universal came to and brought himself to his feet with some assistance from the guardrail. He staggered over the virtual warzone, before bringing Dead up and into the ring. He staggered around in the ring for a bit, before woozily he fell back first to the mat. Coincidentally, his arm ended up draped over Dead, who at the same time reached back and draped his arm over Universal. Having no other choice, the referee counted once more. One. Two. Both men quickly kicked out, neither man knowing what exactly was going on, but sheer experience and gut instinct triggered both men to kick out. Some time elapsed as Universal and Dead struggled to their feet, not knowing where to go from here. Dead staggered towards Universal, who quickly went for the kill. Kick to the stomach, and an attempt at the Cha-Ching occured. Dead countered by pushing Universal into the turnbuckles sternum first. As he came out from the corner backwards, Dead kicked, before hitting the DDT, which was once known as the "Baad Ass Bottom Line" and which was his former finishing maneuver. He went for a cover. One. Two. Only two. Universal shouldered out, and Dead appeared to be on a second wind. Recovered from the earlier punishment, he decided to go for the kill. However, instead he motioned to the back, almost as if he were requesting assistance. Dead quickly hooked Universal for The Crucifixion (sitdown tombstone pildriver), and hit it. Hook of the leg. Cover. One. Two. Kickout. Suddenly, Wilson & Hiroshima appeared on the scene, rolling out a dumpster. They rolled it just down to ringside, before quickly heading backstage. Dead whipped Universal over the top rope onto the cement floor, before exiting the ring himself. Dead came out onto the floor where he pointed at the dumpster, reminding fans that last month at Trapped the two men took a huge spill into the dumpster, and it was amazing that one man was able to walk away from it let alone both. Dead went to whip Universal into the dumpster, but Universal reversed it. Dead smacked into the cold hard steel, and Universal charged. Dead responded by opening his mouth and spraying that deadly black mist into his face. Universal covered his face, before staggering backwards up against the ring, and falling to the floor. He held his face before Dead got behind the other side of the dumpster, and pushed it up against him, crushing him with it up against the ring. Dead moved the dumpster back, before he looked at the body of his rival, crumpled in a heap. He rolled him into the ring, where he completed yet another Crucifixion for good measure, before finally ending the war. One. Two. Fans hoped Universal would kick out, but this time, he couldn't. Three. He lay motionless in the ring, as Dead sat there looking at the booing fans with satisfaction on his face. The referee handed him the fWo World Title, and he quickly got to his feet and held it high above the ring victorious, before Mitch & Hiroshima made their way down to ringside once more. The two men put Universal into the dumpster, before pushing it backstage as "Beautiful People" hummed throughout the arena, which had almost turned completely silent. Perhaps it was due to the fact that one of their favorites would wrestle again. Perhaps it was due to the fact that the FTfWo had the World Title and promised that the fWo would come to an end at CyberSlam 3. It could have been either of those reasons. The fans had nothing to cheer for. A hero had fallen, and an entire organization would be brought down to it's knees in just over a month. Suddenly, from the back, a figure began to emerge. His long hair had chunks of dirt scattered throughout it, and he carried a body bag over one shoulder and a shovel in his free hand. His face was pale, he looked rather disheveled... and all considered, he looked happy. An evil, sadistic smile was worn from ear to ear. Dead finally took notice of the individual. He thought he had disposed of him, but it was only temporary. The Boston Strangler was back, and it didn't look like he had been in any hospital whatsoever as Finn told everyone during the play-by-play of Body Count. The Boston Strangler had come back for revenge. He had come back to take back what was his, something he was owed since last November. A shot at the fWo World Title. Dead knew his intentions... but there was nothing he could do about it. The Strangler tossed the body bag into the ring, before entering with the shovel. Their eyes locked on one anothers, but before a battle could ensue, Slaughter faded to black... with the Strangler back looking to take out his number one enemy... and the fans showing their reaction by welcoming him back with a jarring positive reaction. Result: Dead by Pinfall
