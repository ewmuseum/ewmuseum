Introduction With an Unleashed special just around the corner, the fWo travels to Salt Lake City, a destination that has been fatefull to the fWo in the past for the final show before the outdoor arena special. But will any surprises be in store for the fWo roster as they gear up for the big night? Pro Wrestling: Keeping ToCad America Inc. in business. As the scene fades in we are taken directly to the ring for Monday Night Ruahh~! No fanfare, no fancy cutaway graphics over produced by the techno-nerds. Nothing except a lone figure occupying said ring, much the reason for the continuous and loud ovation from the fans in attendance. Standing in the ring is none other than the Paragon Brand Frontier. This is the same man who has fought off the likes of the twisted bastard Donaven Winters and the members of Brotherly Love on his quest to reach the top of the Fan's Wrestling Organization. Or, truth be known, on his quest to reach the man that holds that top spot in the federation. Looking none the worse for wear, all things considered, Frontier simply takes up space in the ring, a shiny metal briefcase laying on it's side at his feet. After the ovation has begun to fade, thanks in part to the overwhelming curiosity of the audience, Brand retrieves a microphone from his waistband. "I've come out here tonight, without the music, without the fancy sparklers, without the grand bandstand to make a point. I think it's a point lost on the one man that should understand it the most. It's a point that I believe everyone watching has known even if they have just recently started following this career of mine." The words were hushed, almost reserved. Frontier has broken from the trademark 'hero voice'. For the first time on fWo television it was as if Brandon Roche spoke for the Paragon. It's hard to believe that one simple and straight forward sentence could illicit such a response but as Brand utters it the crowd erupt once again, "I want Vic Creed." "I don't know how much more to the point I have to be. From the day I set foot on 'fWo soil' he has been my sole purpose." The disgust was plainly evident, Frontier's disregard of Vic Creed oozed out of every utterance. "Retiring the Boston Strangler was a career milestone, some say a passing of the torch if you will, and as great as it was in a sense for me it was still a roadblock on my way to Creed." The Strangler chant came on strong, and Frontier nodded his admiration. Continuing though through the chant Brand would not be diverted from the task at hand. He spat again at the microphone as if it was Creed's face, "Spending months locked in struggle against Ric Chronos sharpened me, honed me to be the warrior I am today. That too was a roadblock, it only made me better but it wasn't Vic Creed." "Standing 'gainst the plague threatening the fWo known as Pete Borst and my subsequent run as the United States Champion will be looked at as some of my proudest memories, but for everything it was there was one thing it wasn't... my shot at Vic Creed." "And here we are tonight. I stand in this ring two weeks removed from the Alaskan Juggernaut all but dismissing me as he put $50,000 on the line in an effort to keep me away from him yet again. There in lies the point I said the one man that shouldn't be missing it the point, was." The strength in Frontier's voice had begun to grow over the past few minutes and now, now it was like the fans remembered, strong, resolute, absolute, undeniable, all things that defined the mighty hero himself. "The point is that there isn't a force on this planet that will stop me coming after you Creed. Not Brotherly Love, not Donaven Winters, not threats to my loved ones, not even fifty-thousand dollars." With that said Frontier leaned quickly and retrieved the strong aluminum briefcase from the mat, holding it high, "And that's exactly what I have here Creed. Right here, in this case, the exact amount of money that defines how afraid you are of me. Fifty-thousand reasons for you to prove you aren't the same low-life punk that you were back in the Elite Wrestling Association days..." Just then the crowd rain down a chorus of jeers reserved for the likes of Saddam Hussein himself, or Hitler, or Vince Russo. Brand turns toward the entrance way, half expecting a new bounty hunter to be storming the ring. Instead he found a 100 foot face of the fWo World Heavyweight Champion splayed across the FanTron. The smirk on his face spoke volumes, defining in one mere look, six years worth of contempt for Brand Frontier. "Wait Brand," Creed's voice boomed, "You're the one that rode my coat-tails to the big show and you call me the low-life? It was a small price to pay for what he been waiting six years for. Both men smirked, each thinking they had played the other into their hands. The whole thing seemed like bad taste to fans, who continued to jeer Joy all the way to the ring. Joy slid inside and walked at once toward Temerario, beaming and holding out his bunch of flowers. Temerario looked down at them, confused. In his wrestling career this had never happened before, and he scratched his head in confusion at the gesture. Joy attempted to reverse the hold, but Temerario easily escaped the reversed hold that Joy obviously had little experience in using. Temerario at once went back to various neck holds in an attempt to keep Joy grounded. Joy attempted several times to reverse or escape the holds, but was always brought back to the mat by Temerario where a new neck hold would be applied again. Joy rallied in an attempt to gain any advantage, but Temerario easily dodged his attempts and delivered a running drop kick to the mush which sent Joy toppling over the ropes and out of the ring. Joy regrouped on the outside, stomping one foot and shouting 'darn' over and over for the generally laughing, mocking crowd in attendance. Joy used this advantage to knock Temerario to the outside, where the masked man fell badly on the barely padding floor. Finally getting caught, Temerario managed to escape as the referee argued with Joy about using an illegal hold. As Joy finished this conversation he turned and charged at Temerario, who ducked under and sprung up from behind with a swinging vertical neck breaker that dropped Joy to the mat. Temerario slammed a knee into Joy's face, then climbed to the top rope and executed a perfect plancha from the top. He knelt for the cover as the referee dove. 1... 2... 3! Temerario rolled off Joy as his music played and he looked quickly to the rafters and motioned the cross on his chest. He smiled... it felt great to be back in the ring again. Dragon lifted up up Knight, and yanked up his arm, then put his left leg over Knights' neck, putting on a variation of a cerrajera, a modified octupus hold. Dragon was nodding his head as Knight appeared to be in great pain, and seemed like he would tap out at any minute. The referee asked Knight if he wished to give up, but Knight shook his head, refusing to give in to the powerful submission that Dragon had locked on. Instead, Knight went for the nearest leg Dragon had available to grab, and grabbed it, yanking Dragon off his feet, and on to the mat. Dragon grabbed his neck, as Knight grabbed his, then stared at his opponent with a look of pure anger. Knight grabbed Dragon in a waistlock, lifting him up, and dragged him into the center of the ring. Once there, Knight hooked his leg into a standing scissors around Dragons' leg, and quickly slapped on an inverted face lock, completing one of the most feared submission moves in wrestling, the Stretch Plum. Dragon was crying out in pain, and Knight kept on applying pressure to the hold. The referee asked if Dragon was going to quit, but Dragon screamed "NO!", and Knight continued maintaining the hold. Dragon began losing consciousness fast, and after about thirty seconds, the referee noticed this too. The referee lifted up Dragons' free arm once, and dropped it, seeing it fall. The referee lifted up Dragons' arm a second time, and dropped it, seeing it fall.The referee lifted it up a final time... And saw it fall. The bell rang, as Knight let go of the hold, and stumbled into the ropes, holding his neck in obvious pain. Though Dragon did not win the contest, he nearly had the United States champion beat on a number of occasions. But in the end, the champion retained successfully, and had his arm raised in victory. Dragon slowly came to, and saw the referee awarding Knight the United States title, and got up. Knight and Dragon stared at each other for a few moments, before Dragon extended his hand, and Knight took it, shaking hands with his formidable foe. The fans cheered as Dragon lifted up Knights' arm, then left the ring to allow Knight to shine in his glory. Backstage, a figure stared at the monitor and smiled to himself, knowing it was time to finally confront Great Dragon after scouting him out for the last few weeks. The figure walked away from the monitors, as Dragon made his way up the rampway, and Ruahh~! faded to black... Result: Lance Knight via Submission; Retains United States championship
