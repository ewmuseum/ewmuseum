fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 17 October 1999

17 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Sunday Slaughter's music filled the air as fireworks exploded from the rafters. A capacity crowd filled the arena with their shirts, banners, and signs. Chris Finn and James Armstrong sat at the announcing table, with James Armstrong waving a US flag. The noise died down, and suddenly the lights died. The crowd popped in anticipation, but the lights stayed off and the noise died down once more. Suddenly blue fireworks exploded from the entrance ramp, and a large "L" filled the screen with a circle surrounding it. Machina stood below the Slaughter Screen, arms held wide and flanked on either side by Billy James and Sid Snow. Machina held up a microphone and began to speak to the surprised crowd, "Welcome to the house that the Machina built! But this is not a happy house, and all of the damn lollypops in the world from that goofy goof goof Joy can't help it. And Machina asks the fans why this must be? The show has just started and you are already bored beyond belief. Well I can't blame you, for at one time this was supposed to be the greatest Sunday Slaughter of all time... but blondie boy spoiled it for all of you! Instead of the leader of the single most charismatic organization in the history of the sport putting the boots to baseball boy you will instead see pretty boy and sports lad touch and feel each other all over in a display that quite frankly makes Machina sick. Well take heart fans, for on Wednesday Warfare I... the Machina will climb into the ring the brave challenger and leave the ring the fWo World Heavyweight Champion! Finally, a wrestler that you can be proud of... a wrestler that you can cheer... a wrestler that will not be denied his destiny! No longer will you chant 'loser! loser! loser!'..." At this the crowd began to chant "Loser! Loser! Loser! at Machina, which caused the leader of the Lone Gunmen to stomp his feet on the ramp. "No! You will not chant loser, because from this day forward Machina will not lay down for anyone e... e... e... EVER... AGAIN! Now start the show, because the sooner it's over with the sooner I become champion!" With that the fireworks again sounded, and Machina raised his arms to the crowd. Finally settling down from this introduction, the fWo started with their first match... Underground vs. Ultra Cool 2000 The audience in attendance is treated to a video package showing the events from Warfare leading up to this match. The Underground, Mike Bear, Wretch, and Fear, appear on the top of the entrance ramp, much to the approval of the fans. Mike Bear sends Fear back to the locker room, telling him that they wouldn Result: Double Count Out Dark Future The cameras went backstage to a dark corner of the boiler room, where Grendal sat with the hardcore title draped in front of him. Grendal spotted Geoff Green and the camera crews, and looked deadly into the camera. Green started to ask Grendal about his plans now that he held the fWo Hardcore title, but Grendal cut him off with a simple gesture... a raised hand. When Grendal spoke his voice sounded gravely and muffled and oddly calm. "I don't know why I have come to this time, at this place. I have inflicted such pain on an innocent soul by my coming... a soul that knew not of the legend of the Grendal. This has come to pass however, and thus I have to be what I am. A dark power brought to the fWo by a purpose of which I do not know. These are not the ramblings of a lunatic... far from it. This is like it always has been and always will be. Something happens to bring me, the balance between order and chaos is disrupted and the Grendal awakes in the body of a vessel. There are three signs... the bringing of honor, which has been this fWo Hardcore title. The comes the sacrifice in fire and blood... and finally comes vengeance. Vengeance of the Grendal. Now go... lest the sacrifice be yours man of the camera." Geoff Green stepped back, obviously shaken by this as the cameras went back to the ring... New Wave vs. Trouble Slaughter continues with what promises to be an intriguing match-up as Stevie Roberts and Plague make their way out, before stopping and pointing behind them as VL & War make their way out. Everyone appears to be all smiles, as they walk down to the ring. Stevie looks into the camera and says "Hey Collins... it isn't our fault that nobody likes you, you big baby." before they climb into the ring. The tag team of Thumper & Twitch, collectively known as "Trouble", are already in the ring awaiting New Wave. Roberts and Twitch start things off, and the action picks up as it goes onto the outside. Mid-way through the match, Death makes his way out screaming and shouting. Not everything is audible, but what we do hear as security and officials make their way out to stop him from getting to the ring, is Death saying "Oh, great, so I'm to blame? I'm to blame for everything, aren't I? Well, you can take that blame and roll it up in a ball and shove it up the top of your legs! You hear me? The top of your fucking legs! I'm not to blame!" Nobody understands quite what he means, but admist the confusion Roberts rolls up Twitch for a quick pin while Death continues to rant and rave on the outside. Death pushes officials out of his way, and grabs a microphone. "I'm not to blame! Do you understand me? Don't push me over the edge because I'll come in there and piss in that very ring! You can all just go to hell! Leave me alone!" Officials finally refrained Death and physically forced him backstage, as Roberts and the rest of the crew just laughed in his general direction. Roberts, War, VL & Plague just laughed it off as Slaughter went to a commercial break... Result: New Wave by Pinfall Announcements for Warfare... Backstage Mike Bear, Wretch, Fear, Ultra Violet, Violet, and Kid Cool all stood surrounding the commissioner of the fWo, Commissioner Travis. Travis was holding a clipboard and waving his arms, trying to explain what he had planned due to the double count out end to their match. Bear was in the Commissioner's face, yelling that this was crap, and that they should advance over those two goofs. He pointed at Ultra Violet, who was looking up at the flickering fluorescent lights with a blank expression on his face and a string of drool slowly descending from his lip to the floor. Kid Cool looked at Ultra Violet, the light, and the door and scratched his head, trying to decide if he should emulate this or not. Travis finally got Mike Bear back a few feet, and explained that the tournament would not have a bye, and that the match would be replayed on Wednesday Warfare. The winner would advance, and the match tonight would be stricken from the books. Bear and Wretch seemed satisfied with this, and paced away with Fear behind them. Suddenly, Joy and Glee entered the scene and confronted Ultra Violet and Kid Cool. Joy got Ultra Violet's attention by waving a match in front of his face and said, "Hey there! Ultra Cool 2000... you guys are the only ones I can trust to help bring my message of happiness to the world... so I'm making an offer to ya right here and right now... the four of us at Mob Rules against the biggest collection of meanies we can find. What do you say?" Ultra Violet seemed ready to answer, but suddenly the sounds of an Ice Cream truck came wafting into the room, and Ultra Violet quickly ran to find it. Joy and Glee ran after him, with Kid Cool and Violet chasing behind. Standing alone in the room, the commissioner pointed to the camera and said, "This still on? Well fans... this comes from me to you, and it goes like this. This Wednesday, on Warfare, you can count on one thing... it's going to be the biggest show in the history of the fWo. I'm telling you right now... you miss this, and you will regret it... count on that." With that the cameras went back to the ring, the promise of the commissioner still rippling through the arena... Fallen and Poison vs. French Foundation Fallen and Poison make their way out to a plethora of boos from the crowd. Poison is crying again like she's been known to do in recent weeks, while Fallen remains unfazed by the fans response. Out comes The Flying Frenchie and Primetime, who both hold some gold of their own and are looking to gain some more by winning this tournament. Fallen charges at Frenchie as he climbs in, and spears him through the middle ropes onto the floor. Fallen is beating on Frenchie when Primetime comes over and pulls him off by the hair and hits him a few times, before rolling him into the ring. PT then hops up to the top ropes and flies off with a missle dropkick, knocking Fallen damn near out of his pants. PT goes for a cover, but Fallen kicks out. Frenchie gets back up on the ring apron and watches, although noticably in pain. Late in the match, Primetime hits a Primetime Bomb on Fallen before going for the cover. Fallen kicks out at the last possible second, and Frenchie is frustrated. Frenchie spots Mitch coming to the ring with a chair, and Frenchie begins to distract the referee. PT holds Fallen, and Mitch finally climbs in. Mitch swings and Fallen ducks, and Primetime falls back first onto the mat. Fallen kicks Mitch in the stomach, before hitting a DDT. Fallen covers PT, but Frenchie refuses to let the referee go count the pin. Poison still stands on the ring apron crying, not even paying attention to the match. Fallen walks over and grabs the referee from Frenchie, or tried to. Frenchie has an arm and Fallen has an arm and it's a virtual tug of war as both men fight for the referee. Finally, Frenchie lets go and Fallen pulls the referee ontop of him, thus knocking him onto the mat. PT is back up from the chair shot, he moves the referee off of Fallen, and a second later Frenchie flies off with a 450 splash. PT instructs the referee to count before he helps up Mitch. PT and Mitch begin a shoving match while the referee counts the pin. Frenchie comes over and tries to keep things under control, but Mitch says something he shouldn't have and this infuriates the Frenchie. PT tells Mitch that that was strike two... but before he could finish that sentence, everything turns to dark as the mysterious masked man makes his way out dragging Demon. All three members of the French Foundation scatter out of the ring and through the crowd. The masked man shouts obsecenities and other things and rants and raves about not getting his hands on the Foundation as an explosion occurs from the turnbuckles. Poison finally comes into the ring and is still crying as Fallen comes to. The announcers give their word to keep their eye on this situation as Slaughter goes to a commercial break. Result: French Foundation by Pinfall Tag Team offer Backstage Crucifix had grabbed Geoff Green by the shirt and was repeatedly slamming him against the cold wall of the arena. Crucifix was screaming at Green to tell him where Grendal was so he could go murder him and take back what was his. As Crucifix continued to scream at Green, Jackhammer hit the scene and spotted Crucifix against the wall. Looking serious, Jackhammer approached Green and patted him on the back. Jackhammer said, "Brother, you and I need to get them back for what they did to us last night... you and I, we can go after Hart and Steiner together. It's up to us to show them who is next... and it can be them if you and I team up. Come on, man... you and me... Hart and Steiner... it will be the greatest Slaughter in the history of our sport!" Crucifix looked at Jackhammer with a confused expression on his face, and shouted, "What in the hell is wrong with you?" before stalking away. Jackhammer followed after him, his arms spread wide in a questioning motion. As they walked away security ran through the area shouting, "We've got two maniacs outside trying to get in... quick, call the police!" One security guard asked, "Who are they? I've never seen them before... but that one guy is as big as a mountain!" Inside the arena the cameras went back to the ring... Eron (c) vs. Courage Courage ducks several paper cups and shoves Jake into the path of others as he makes his way to the ring. Eron comes out next, threatening to smack several fans next to the rail. He was attended by Stinky, his new bodyguard, but after taking a look at Courage inside the ring Eron shook his head and sent Stinky to the back, telling him he didn't need him for this joke of a wrestler. Eron enters the ring and slowly removes the International Title from his waist. He shouts at Courage, telling him that the belt is going home strapped around the same waist it came in on. The ref is handed the belt and displays it for the crowd, before handing it to a ringside attendant and calling for the bell. Courage and Eron lock up, and Courage cinches in a side headlock. He applies some torque and slaps the back of Eron Result: Courage by Pinfall Reunion "Felt good, didn't it?" Stevie Roberts clapped Plague on the back as the two got into the limo with War and VL. Plague grinned sheepishly back at Stevie. "Yeah it did... it's been... what? Three years? Felt right though. New Wave is back and it's where it should be. We can do the job to you on Warfare... cover your back through the rest of the tournament so Doomsday gets the titles back, and then it's the start of a new dynasty." Stevie smiled, but then looked troubled. "But what about Collins? Do you have any regrets?" Plague shook his head and laughed. "Regrets? Nah... Collins was never our real leader. He's a second rate Death, trying to fill shoes that David Spencer built. But not even Spencer could keep me from my friend. You, Stevie." The two got into the limo, but before it could pull away a truck collided into the side of it. From out of the truck stepped Mountain and Angus, who were at once swarmed by security. Mountain roared, "The fWo are full of puny men... wimps! Will nobody have the guts to accept our challenge? Nobody?" Security struggled to get them out of the area as medical personnel tended to Doomsday inside the wrecked limo. Cameras returned to the ring, where the final match of the night was getting ready to begin... Scott Slugger (c) vs. Lance Star As we come back from commercial, "F-ck Off" by Kid Rock begins to play, and Lance Star, an intense look in his eye, walks down to the ring to a great deal of boos. Unlike Roger Clemens, he shrugs them off and focuses on the ring, rolling in and bouncing off the ropes to warm up, before turning to the entranceway and awaiting his opponent. After a moment, "Back In Black" by AC/DC starts and the fans boo even more as fWo World Champion Scott Slugger steps out, holding the World Title high over his head. Fireworks explode behind him as he walks arrogantly to the ring, and tosses the World Title nonchalantly to the timekeeper. Slugger leaps onto the apron, and hops over the top rope before posing on the nearest turnbuckle. Slugger turns to Star as the bell rings, and the two warily circle each other. They grapple, and Slugger breaks it off. Now Star grapples with Slugger, and hits a go-behind, and leaps onto Slugger's shoulders, before rolling forward with a victory roll. Slugger however grabs the legs and Star is in a pinning position. The ref counts to two, but Star rolls through and now has Slugger in a pinning position. This garners a two-count as well, before Slugger throws off Star, who rolls backwards onto his feet and charges at Slugger with a crucifix. Slugger holds on however, and nails a Samoan Drop, and holds onto the leg for a pin. This gets a two, but Star rolls onto his side and Slugger is now being pinned. The ref counts two before Slugger kicks out. Slugger charges at Star as he gets up, but Star leapfrogs and Slugger bounces off the ropes, and baseball slides under Star as Star goes for a clothesline. Star turns around and charges with a lariat again, but Slugger catches him and has him in position for Lance's own Shooting Star finisher, but Star breaks this with a series of elbows to the head. Lance Star goes for a superkick but Slugger ducks and as Star turns around tries for a superkick of his own, which is also ducked. Slugger and Star look at each other for a moment, and both simultaneously extend hands for a shake in a show of respect of the series of counters and reversals. Each man seems somewhat surprised at the actions of his opponent, but takes the hand anyway. Slugger and Star then simultaneously strike out with kicks to each other's midsections. Star's seems to have more effect, however, and he kicks Slugger twice more in the gut, backing him into the ropes. Star whips Slugger into the opposite ropes, and catches Slugger coming off with a standing leg lariat. Star quickly covers, but only gets a one-count. Star is up first, and as Slugger makes his way to his feet Star charges at him and slams him with a back elbow. Slugger goes down, and Star drops down with a senton, slamming into Slugger. He covers, but only gets two. Star brings Slugger back up now, and hooks him in position for a vertical suplex. As he does this, Matt Panzer announces that 5 minutes of the 20-minute time limit have elapsed. Star holds...and drops Slugger hard on his back. Star signals to the crowd, and climbs up to the top rope. He then flies off, nearly decapitating Slugger with a flying elbowdrop. Star covers, sure he's got the win, but Slugger kicks out at two. Star is surprised, but quickly brings Slugger to his feet. He uses a short whip on Slugger, and lifts Slugger onto his shoulders for a Death Valley Driver. However, Slugger slides down his back with a sunset flip. This garners two, but as Slugger gets up he is taken back down by a hard lariat from Star. Star pulls Slugger onto his feet again, and nails Slugger with a simple eye rake, disorienting him. Slugger stumbles into the corner as Star climbs up top and double underhooks the head of Slugger, yelling that he's going to beat him with his own move. Star leaps off, but Slugger reverses in mid-move into a front-layout suplex, smashing Star face-first into the mat. This appears to bust Lance open. Slugger crawls over to Star and makes the cover. Star kicks out at two however, and Slugger is shocked. Slugger brings Lance up as blood drips down his face, and Slugger hits a kick to the midsection, followed by another, and then one more which is immediately followed up by a Rocker Dropper on the doubled-over Star. Slugger, with Star down, bounces off the ropes and nails a quick legdrop on "The Future". Slugger then climbs on top of Star and begins raining down punches on him, opening up the cut even further. Slugger reaches back and hooks both legs, but at the count of two, Star suddenly rolls forward and Slugger is in a pinning predicament. This only gets a two-count before Slugger boxes Star's head with his legs. With Star disoriented, Slugger grabs him by the head and delivers a falling reverse DDT, dropping Star hard. Slugger then goes to the legs, and looks to apply an STF. However, Star once again reverses Slugger's move, this time into a small package. This gets two before Slugger kicks out. As he reaches his feet, he very audibly yells a not-suitable-for-television word at getting each of his moves reversed. Star is slower to get up, however, and when he reaches his feet Slugger nails him with a spinning heel kick. Slugger covers and hooks the leg, but once again only gets a two-count. Undaunted, Slugger brings Star up again, and hooks him for a hiptoss. The bloody Star reverses this, but Slugger reverses again, and now both men go flying over the top rope and land hard on the outside. Slugger gets to his feet first, and climbs back onto the ring apron. He then leaps off, going for a flying legdrop, but Star moves and Slugger hits hard on the outside. Star slowly gets to his feet, and pulls Slugger up to his feet. Star then whips Slugger into the steel ring steps, and Slugger goes head over heels over the ring steps and lands hard on the concrete floor. Star walks over to Slugger and begins to trash-talk him, slapping him in the face. Star then grabs a nearby chair, and gives Slugger two successive concussive shots that bust open the World Champion. As he does this, Matt Panzer announces that 10 minutes have gone by. Star then pulls Slugger to his feet by the hair, and drags him to the Spanish Announce Table. Jose and Pepe scatter, knowing what's likely to come next. Star takes a moment to gloat to the crowd, and Slugger takes advantage with a low back kick, doubling Star over. Slugger then rolls Star onto the table, and begins to slowly climb back into the ring. Slugger then starts to climb to the top turnbuckle. He measures the prone Star carefully, and then leaps off with a flying somersault legdrop, and he crashes into Star and through the table. The crowd begins to chant "fWo! fWo! fWo!" at this, and the ref finally realizes what his job is and begins to count both men out. The count reaches 7 before Star stirs, as does Slugger. Slugger gets to his feet first, and rolls into the ring at 9, breaking the count. Star follows him back in, and Slugger begins stomping away at Star as he rolls in. Slugger brings Star to his feet, and bounces off the ropes. He leaps at Star with a jumping lariat, but Star instead catches his arm and in one fell swoop nails the Shooting Star on Slugger. Star is still exhausted, though, and many seconds pass before he drapes an arm over Slugger. 1...2...Slugger kicks out, and Star cannot believe it. He holds his head in his hands, but slowly drags Slugger to his feet. Star whips Slugger into the ropes, but as Star drops his head for a back body-drop, Slugger double underhooks it and nails the Curveball on Star in the middle of the ring. Slugger too is dazed, and it is a quite a few moments before he drapes an arm on Star. The ref counts 1...2...but Star kicks out, and now it's Slugger's turn to stare in disbelief. Matt Panzer at ringside announces that 15 minutes have gone by, and Slugger, determined to win, brings Star to his feet again. Slugger hooks Star in position and delivers a brainbuster. This gets just a two-count, and Slugger is pissed. Slugger picks Star up, and sets him backwards on the top rope. Slugger grabs the neck of Star and drops down with a top-rope neckbreaker. With Star down, Slugger heads to the outside, and performs a slingshot split-legged moonsault on the downed Star. Sure this is it, Slugger places one finger on Star. At two, Star reaches up and pokes the eyes of Slugger, and Slugger reels backwards. Star stumbles to his feet, but grabs Slugger and nails a float-over DDT. The dazed Star doesn't bother to hook the leg, and Slugger kicks out at two. Star brings Slugger to his feet, and goes behind for a tiger suplex. He nails it, but Slugger somehow manages to get free at two. Panzer announces that 18 minutes have elapsed, and two remain. Star brings Slugger to his feet, and whips him into the corner. Star charges in with a corner splash, and Slugger is flattened. Star goes back to the well one more time, but Slugger moves and Star nails the steel ringpost, and stumbles right into Slugger, who trips him up and covers Star by leaning on both legs. At two, Star flips Slugger over for a two-count, but Slugger rolls forward for two, then Star rolls forward and gets two on Slugger. Slugger gets out of it and kips up to his feet, and charges at Star with a lariat. However, Star hooks the arm and gets Slugger in a backslide as Panzer announces that 19 minutes have passed. This only gets two, and both men run at each other and clobber each other with a double clothesline. The ref begins to count, and at 7, Slugger makes it to his feet, and pulls Star up. With 45 seconds left, Slugger hooks Star for an exploder suplex, and nails it. He covers, but only gets two. With 35 seconds remaining, Slugger gets Star in position for a powerbomb, but as he lifts him, Star counters with a hurricanrana, and gets two. 25 seconds now remain, and as both men get to their feet, Slugger nails Lance with a low blow. Slugger makes a "home run" signal to the crowd, and starts climbing up top. Slugger measures Star, who is standing, and it becomes apparent that Slugger is going for his old finisher, the Grand Slam. Slugger leaps off with 15 seconds left, but flies right into the arms of Star. Star turns Slugger around into the middle of the ring, and plants him with another Shooting Star. Knowing that one couldn't put Slugger away before, Star pulls him up as Panzer begins counting down- 10, 9, 8, 7, Star hooks Slugger, 6, Star nails Slugger with Shooting Star number two, but appears to hit his head on the mat on the way down, 5, Star is dazed from the impact, 4, 3, he places an arm on Slugger, 2, the ref counts one, 1, the ref counts two, and the bell rings to signal the end of the match! The ref's hand nearly pounds the mat a third time, but he pulls it up and both men have gone to a time limit draw! Slugger, blood staining his tank top, struggles to his feet with the help of the ropes, as Star uses the turnbuckles to pull himself up. As he does so, he gestures for the microphone, and Panzer tosses it to him. Star says, "Scott Slugger...congratulations...you survived 20 minutes with me. But the question...is...can you survive...25?" Slugger grabs the microphone from him and asks, "You...want 5 more...minutes? The WGA says...you've got your 5...more minutes!" The crowd cheers at this, appreciating the effort both men have put forth and happy at the prospect of seeing more of this superb match. The ref agrees to this, and signals to Matt Panzer, who announces that there will officially be a 5-minute overtime period. The timekeeper rings the bell, and the crowd spontaneously begins chanting "fWo! fWo! fWo!" once again at the efforts of these mighty warriors. Both men begin the match as they did earlier, circling each other wearily. They lock up, and Slugger gains the advantage, tossing Star into the ropes and catching him coming off with a flapjack. Slugger then goes to the legs of Star, and applies the STF he tried once previously. This time it works, and Star howls in pain as Slugger cinches in the hold. Star tries desperately to make it to the ropes, but the bigger Slugger holds him more or less in place. Finally, after nearly half a minute in the hold, Star begins inching forward. He goes forward...forward...and finally reaches the ropes. Slugger briefly locks the hold in even tighter, then breaks it at 4. As Star gets to his feet, Slugger kicks at the leg of Star, and Star stumbles. Slugger bounces off the ropes, and nails a seated dropkick on the leg as Matt Panzer announces only 4 minutes remain. Star goes down, and Slugger stomps away at the leg of Star. Slugger brings the limping Star to his feet and tosses him into the corner, and begins to stomp away at the leg. Star, out of reflex, strikes out with a kick that nails Slugger low, and Slugger stumbles away. Star walks up from behind Slugger, and with a great effort, lifts him onto his shoulders. Star, looking to win the way he did their first encounter, climbs to the first rope...then the second...but his leg buckles underneath him and Slugger drops harmlessly behind him, and quickly mounts the turnbuckles, hooking the head of Star. Not wasting time with signaling, Slugger went for the Curveball at the 3 minute mark, but Star once again reversed into a Michinoku Driver. However, this time the Champion rolled through with the move, and Star was in a pinning predicament. The ref counted two, but Star escaped. Slugger and Star both got to their feet simultaneously. Both men went for running clotheslines, but ducked each other. As they bounced off the ropes, both men went for cross-body blocks, each nailing the other. Each landed with his arm on the other, and the ref counted both men down to two before each simultaneously lifted a shoulder. Star got to his feet first, and nailed a drop toe hold on Slugger, before leaping over him and locking in a front facelock. Star then turned the move into a DDT and Slugger bounced off the mat as Panzer announced 2 minutes left. Star covered, but Slugger again kicked out. Star fell backwards in exhaustion, but slowly got to his feet, and dragged the near-unconscious Slugger up with him. As he did so, Slugger's friend Ice began to slowly make his way to ringside. Star whipped Slugger off the ropes, and criss-crossed off the side ropes, catching Slugger in the middle of the ring with an inverted swinging neckbreaker. Before the ref could count the pin, Ice jumped onto the apron to distract the ref, and surreptitiously slips a pair of brass knuckles into the ring. Slugger kicks out in the ring, and rolls over, finding the knuckles. Slugger slowly gets to his feet, and as the previously-standing Lance Star walks toward him, Slugger nails him with the knuckles at the one-minute count. Ice lets the ref go at this, but from the back comes Chris Universal with a briefcase, presumably full of money, who dashes past the slower Ice and clobbers first the ref, then Slugger, who had covered Star, with the briefcase with 45 seconds left. Ice slides into the ring, and gets into it verbally with Universal. A replacement ref begins sprinting down to the ring, and clears both Universal and Ice from the ring by threatening to DQ their respective partners if they don't leave the ring as Panzer announced 30 seconds remaining. Both Slugger and Star begin to stir woozily, and both reach their feet with 20 seconds left. Slugger goes for a wild clothesline, but Star ducks, and picks up Slugger for a back suplex. Slugger flips backwards onto his feet, however, and strikes from behind with a low blow as Panzer begins counting down again- 10, 9, Slugger gets a waistlock on Star intending to run out the clock, 8, 7, 6, 5, Star suddenly reverses and goes behind on Slugger, 4, 3, Star hits a German suplex on Slugger and both men's shoulders appear to be down, 2, the ref counts one but Star's knee buckles and Slugger lands on top, 1, the ref counts two, and the bell rings again! Slugger can't believe it, and falls down to a sitting position, before slowly getting up. On the outside, Ice grabs a chair and rolls into the ring. He bashes the still-down Star in the injured knee with the chair once, then twice, as Chris Universal frantically opens the briefcase on the outside, exposing a great deal of money. Slugger begins stomping away as well, as Universal grabs a huge wad of cash with both hands and rolls into the ring. He holds out the cash to Slugger and Ice...and both take the money with smiles on their faces. Universal has a thoughtful look on his face as the cameras go off the air for Slaughter... Result: Time limit draw
