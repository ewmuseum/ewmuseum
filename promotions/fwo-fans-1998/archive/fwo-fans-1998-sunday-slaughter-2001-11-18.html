Sunday Slaughter, 18 November 2001

18 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Flying Frenchie vs. 'Total Elimination' Eli Flair Fireworks explode and music blares inside the arena as Slaughter begins here in Omaha. The crowd cheers loudly, welcoming the fWo back to Nebraska. The fireworks stop, and the music dies down. "No More Mr. Nice Guy" begins to play, and after a moment of confusion, the fans begin to cheer as Eli Flair makes his way to the ring with Poison Ivy. Flair looks focused and intense for this match he thought he'd be having last week, his official (as opposed to unofficial) fWo debut. Flair steps into the ring and waits for his opponent. "Juke Joint Jezebel" hits, and the crowd erupts in boos as the Flying Frenchie makes his way to the ring. Frenchie looks disdainfully into the ring, then turns to make a pass at Poison Ivy. She slaps him, and Frenchie is turned around, right into the fist of Eli Flair who has left the ring. Flair hits another shot to the head of Frenchie, and another as the bell rings. Flair grabs Frenchie and whips him into the guardrail. Flair then charges in and hits a huge lariat on Frenchie. Frenchie slumps to the floor, and Flair lays in a series of stomps. Flair brings Frenchie up and tries to whip him into the ringpost, but Frenchie reverses it. Flair slams face-first into the ringpost, then gets bulldogged to the floor by Frenchie. Frenchie brings Flair up and tosses him into the ring, then hops onto the apron. He then slingshots himself in with a legdrop. Frenchie makes the cover, but Flair kicks out at two. Frenchie brings Flair up to his feet and whips him into the corner, then backs up and charges into the corner. He does a handspring and a flip, then clocks Flair with an elbow to the face. Flair stumbles out, and Frenchie nails him with a smooth dropkick to the back of the head. Frenchie covers again, but once more only gets two. Frenchie brings Flair up and goes for a suplex, but Flair blocks it. He then lifts Frenchie up and hits a hanging suplex, driving Frenchie into the mat. Flair brings Frenchie up to his feet and whips him into the ropes. As Frenchie comes off, Flair catches him and slams him into the mat with a thunderous powerslam. Flair hooks the leg, but Frenchie manages to get a shoulder up at two. Flair is undaunted and brings Frenchie up. He lifts Frenchie into the air, then drives him into the mat with a brainbuster. Flair, not done by a long shot, runs into the ropes and drops a leg across the throat of Frenchie. Flair covers for a two-count. Flair brings Frenchie to his feet and hooks him by the head, then spikes him with a DDT, driving Frenchie jarringly into the mat. Frenchie flops over onto his back, and Flair makes the cover. He gets two, then Frenchie kicks out. Flair brings Frenchie up once more, but this time Frenchie fights back with a devastating eye gouge. Flair stumbles off, and Frenchie leaps onto his shoulders and, in an incredible display, takes the big man over with a frankensteiner. Frenchie then runs into the ropes, leaps onto the second strand, and comes off with an asai moonsault. He hooks a leg, but Flair gets a shoulder up after two. Frenchie backs off and waits for Flair to get up, measuring him. When Flair gets up, Frenchie lets fly with a superkick that hits Flair squarely on the chin. Flair falls to the mat, and Frenchie covers. To his displeasure, Flair kicks out after two. Frenchie brings Flair up again and takes him down with a hard DDT, then begins to climb to the top rope. He signals that he's going for the 450 splash, then cocks his head as the fans begin to cheer. Sure enough, the cheers are not for him, but for the Boston Strangler, who is rushing to the ring. As the referee conveniently checks on Flair, Strangler hops onto the apron. He blocks a punch from Frenchie, then sweeps his legs out from under him, crotching him. His job done, Strangler heads back to the back. Flair gets up to his feet and sees Frenchie in trouble, and climbs up to the top. He hooks Frenchie for a superplex, but Frenchie counters with a shot to the kidneys. He then shoves Flair off to the mat, where he lands with a crash. Not wanting to waste any more time, Frenchie sets for the Guillotine Legdrop. He measures Flair... leaps... ... and misses. Frenchie crashes to the mat as Flair rolls away. Flair makes his way to his feet as Frenchie grabs his back, in deep pain. He slowly gets to his feet, dazed. From behind, Flair hooks him by the head, then drives him into the mat with the Fallen One. Flair makes the cover. One. Two. Three. Eli Flair takes the victory, and the crowd cheers. Primetime immediately rushes the ring to help Frenchie and attack Flair, but Flair sees him coming and bails, joining Poison Ivy on the outside. The popular duo heads out through the crowd as Frenchie pounds the mat, clearly frustrated. Slaughter, meanwhile, heads elsewhere... Result: Flair by pinfall Boiling Point Hellrazor taped his fists, eyes closed into narrow slits. A grim expression was worn on his face, and he looked not at all like his usual self. Dark and brooding, Hellrazor glanced down at the contract laying on the bench next to him. He had thought this issue would have been settled by now. Neo wasn't worth wasting his time on, but the fool had gone too far. Far too far. Footsteps sounded outside of his locker room, and the door creaked open as 'Black Plague' Ruben Ross poked his head into glance at Hellrazor. "Kick his ass on Saturday." Hellrazor didn't respond, lost in his own world of rage and hatred. He could see Neo inside of his mind, laughing and sneering at him. But blood poured from his eyes and his skin began to crack and split in festering sores. The skin boiled and ran off his bone in bloody streams leaving bleached white behind. Soon nothing but a dry and dusty skeleton remained, and with one strong fist Hellrazor reached out and crumbled it into dust. Hellrazor came back to himself, alone in the dressing room. Frustrated at the open air he swept the contract aside with one large hand and took to his feet. He slammed his fist into the bench and let out a frustrated grunt of rage. This would have to end... this would have to stop. He could not tolerate this any further... vengeance would be had and he would kill any mention or sight of the man called... "Neo!" Hellrazor's head snapped to the sound behind his locker door and his teeth were bared. Someone outside was shouting, and it was a name he was interested in dealing with now. Six days were far too many. He took a step toward the door, listening through a red cloud of hatred. "Neo's out here and he's gone crazy! Someone... someone help!" Had Hellrazor been more clear of thought he would have responded with more caution to this call, but Hellrazor was lost in his fury and he only had one thought as he stomped toward the door. One thought that was similar to his dream. Neo's body breaking down into dry dust in his fists. Neo's head splitting like a ripe melon. Neo's blood flowing freely from his body. Hellrazor hit the door and shoved it open, bursting into the hallway with wide eyes and bared teeth. He turned a moment too late, and the bat swung toward him with a metallic rush of air. It struck Hellrazor's body with a wet thud and the air rushed from his mouth in a gasp. Quick as it struck, the bat was swung backward in an arc and came around again into the side of the body. Hellrazor sunk to one knee, eyes still burning with hate over this latest act of cowardice. Neo couldn't fight him like a man... Neo couldn't beat him like a man... Neo didn't care. Neo tossed the bat to one side, a wide smile on his face. "You're stupid boy... stupid like I knew you was. You're trash son, just trash. Wrestlin' is a sport for real men, for smart men... not for cotton pickers like you boy... not for trash like you boy..." Neo grabbed up Hellrazor and ran him forward toward one of the glass windows that separated the hallway from the executive offices. Neo spotted Chris Universal sitting behind a desk in one of these offices and his smile widened. Universal hadn't noticed the people running out of the way in the hallway as his attention was locked on a stack of paperwork for the pay per view. A second later the glass had shattered as Hellrazor was rammed into it, falling forward amidst the shards of glass. Chris Universal leapt to his feet, alarmed and with an expression of horror on his face as he looked down at the bloodied Hellrazor. Eyes blazing he looked at Neo and with a wave of revulsion found him grinning at the carnage he had caused. Neo's drawl came out surly and stubborn. "You don't want to have a problem with me, rich boy." Neo glanced down at Hellrazor with a sneer, then nodded his head. "But this glass here has given me an idea... keep you eye on the monitor, rich boy... I'm not finished yet." 'The Golden Child' Monte Burns vs. Eddy Fate vs. 'The Fallen Angel' Donaven Winters The fans in Omaha seem riled up for the next contest, not surprising in light of the match's stipulation, with the winner receiving a shot at the fWo Cruiserweight Champion Tempest at Fallout. The sounds of "Nobody's Real" are heard first, as "The Golden Child" Monte Burns makes his way out being accompanied by Monica. With a look of pure disdain on his face, the former Cruiserweight Champion walks slowly down the entrance the ramp, followed closely by the young woman. As he reaches the ring, Burns begins to stretch out the ropes before his music ends. "Somewhat Damaged" starts up next, as the crowd gives a fairly warm reception to "The Fallen Angel" Donaven Winters. Making eye contact with his hated rival, Winters strides confidently down to the ring, glaring at Monica as well. Monica looks back at Donaven with an evil smile, feeling that her mere presence at ringside could make a difference. Winters and Burns stand in opposite corners shooting holes through one another as they await the third person in the match. Their wait ends as "Disconnect" blasts into the arena and the crowd explodes with cheers for Eddy Fate. Also a former Cruiserweight Champion, Fate runs out from the back with his wife Destiny and walks proudly down the ramp, waving his hands appreciatively to the crowd. Fate climbs into the ring in another corner, looking back and forth between Winters and Burns. All three men begin to step toward the middle of the ring, a little bit of talking going on between Winters and Burns. Fate watches on as the referee rings the bell to officially begin the match. Burns walks up close to Winters, their faces nose to nose as they exchange words. Fate steps forward as well, saying something in Monte's direction. Burns now turns his attention to Fate and gives him a shove. Fate responds with a shove of his own that sends Monte stumbling backwards. Burns starts to charge at Fate but attacks Winters with a hard right instead. "The Golden Child" sends Winters staggers backwards, but Fate grabs him from behind with a belly to back suplex. Fate goes for an early cover, but Donaven breaks it up before the ref can start to count. Fate and Winters exchange blows before Winters gains the advantage with a high knee. Winters whips Fate hard into the ropes and kicks him in the stomach then drops him with a stalling brainbuster. Just as he gets to his feet and turns around, Burns, who has recovered and perched himself on the top turnbuckle, leaps off and nails him in the jaw with a missile dropkick. Monte makes the cover, but Winters kicks out just in time to avoid the pinfall. Burns brings Winters up to his feet and pulls him into a powerbomb position. Instead, Monte lifts Donaven up and drops him across his knee with a double underhook backbreaker. Before Burns can make the cover, however, Fate spins him around and hits a northern lights suplex with a bridge for a very close two count, with the crowd gasping at the near fall. As both men get to their feet, Burns tries a punch, but Fate blocks it and hits him with one of his own. Burns staggers back, only to be hit with a punch from Winters this time. The two men continue to bounce Monte back and forth between them before double clotheslining him over the top rope to the outside, much to the delight of the fans. With Burns out of the picture, Winters tries to take advantage by nailing Fate with a german suplex, but Fate elbows his way out of the hold. Fate pounds on Winters with a couple of martial arts kicks to the ribs before taking him down with a dropkick. Fate makes the cover, but Winters kicks out at two. Undaunted, Fate gets up quickly, keeping Winters on the mat with a couple of kicks to the head. Fate walks over to the nearby corner and exchanges a smile of approval with Destiny as he heads to the top. But Monte has gotten to his feet on the outside and reaches up to grab Eddy's foot, crotching him on the top turnbuckle. This gives Donaven time to recover, and "The Fallen Angel" heads to the same corner and climbs up to the top as well. Winters grabs Fate into a front facelock and hits a superplex on Fate from the top. Winters makes the cover, but Burns runs in to stop the count at two and prevent Winters from getting the title shot. As he lifts Winters up from behind, Burns kicks Fate on the ground before whipping Donaven into the ropes. Burns catches Winters with a spinning heel kick to the jaw and makes a cover. Fate breaks up the pin at two, kicking Burns in the side of the head to return the favor. Fate lifts up Burns and knocks him back against the ropes with couple of knife edge chops that send the crowd into a "WHOO" frenzy. Eddy now whips Burns off the ropes and hits him square in the jaw with a wheel kick. Fate makes the cover, but Winters breaks up this pin attempt at two. Fate and Winters come to blows, with Fate taking the advantage and sending Winters into the ropes. Fate lands a kick to the gut and starts to go for the Twist of Fate, but Winters pushes Fate away into the ropes before nailing him with The Waste. Winters covers, but Fate is able to kick out at two. As Winters starts to yell at the referee in frustration, the recovered Burns comes up behind and drops him with a side russian legsweep. Fate charges up behind Monte, but "The Golden Child" sees him coming and uses Fate's own momentum to send Eddy flying over the top rope. Burns looks over at Monica and nods. before walking to the nearby corner and climbing to the top. Monte looks down at Winters on the canvas, then out at the fans in attendance as well. Finally, Burns leaps off with The Establishment... Unfortunately for Burns, Winters moves. As Winters rolls away, Burns comes crashing down hard on his beck to the mat. Winters starts to get up and lifts Burns up for what appears to be an exploder suplex. But Monte manages to block the move, instead hitting Winters with a high knee and setting him up for a double-arm DDT. However, this time Winters blocks the move and backdrops Burns. Winters turns around and waits for Burns to get to his feet before lifting him up and sending him crashing to the mat with the Shattered Society, one of Donaven's patented manuevers. Winters starts to make the cover as Burns lays out on the mat... For some reason, Winters gets up from the cover. Standing on the apron is Monica, yelling something at Donaven that has distracted him from the match. Monica quickly drops off the apron, but Winters follows her to the outside. Meanwhile, Fate rolls back in the ring, surveys the situation quickly, and then heads to the top turnbuckle once again as the crowd stirs in anticipation. Fate holds his arms up and then jumps off, nailing the fallen Burns with a picture perfect senton bomb in the middle of the ring. Three seconds later and Fate has won the match to get a title shot against Tempest at Fallout. Fate takes to a corner and begins celebrating. Meanwhile, Burns climbs to his feet, clutching his ribs after having the wind knocked out of him. Burns spots Winters and Monica arguing, and immediately attacks from behind. He spins and kicks Winters in the stomach, before taking him out with a swinging neckbreaker. Winters slides out to the floor, and Burns and Winters continue to brawl around ringside. The brawl eventually reaches the crowd, and up onto the stairs. As Fate continued his celebration in the ring, the fans reacted negatively to the appearance of Ric Chronos on the stage. Fate had his back turned to the stage, and did not know why the fans were booing. "Eddy... I wouldn't celebrate so soon if I were you." Fate turned around sharply, a knee jerk reaction to Chronos' voice. Fate stared a hole through Chronos. Chronos shot an icy glare back in the direction of Fate, before he continued. "You want to know what evidence I've got over Tempest' head? Well, you are going to have to work for it. Since you've earned your match with her next week at the pay-per-view, I'm going to give you that opportunity. You two are going to fight in a ladder match. High above the ring... held in place by a cable... the fWo Cruiserweight Title will be placed. But not only that... in addition to that... alongside the Cruiserweight Title... there will be a folder. And in that folder will be the so-called 'evidence'. Should you obtain the evidence... you'll find out *everything*." Chronos smirked. "Enjoy the match... break a leg. For me?" Chronos smirked again as Fate sneered in his direction. The stakes were set. Fate knew what he had to accomplish. But the task would not be easy.
