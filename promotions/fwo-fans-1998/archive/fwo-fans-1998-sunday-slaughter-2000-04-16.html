fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 16 April 2000

16 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction As fans poured into the arena a camera was picked up backstage, and entered the locker room of Crusader, to find him ready for his match in his wrestling attire, sitting down on the bench. He was just finished taping his hands and wrists up. His hair was damp and neatly slicked back, as he made sure that his boots were laced tightly. Crusader vs. Matt Douglas "Higher" plays and out walks Matt Douglas, with a smug, confident look on his face. The crowd cheers, as Douglas' pyro shoots off. Douglas jumps over the ropes, into the ring, and prepares. Then "Switchback" by Biohazard hits up, and for the first time in fWo history, superstar Crusader walks out onto the entrance ramp, to cheers from the crowd. Crusader continues to the ring, and steps in. Inside of the ring, the two men meet face to face, and as a sign of respect to one another, they shake hands. Then, they lock up, and Douglas immediately slaps on an armbar. Crusader is able to flip out of it and turn Douglas over with an armdrag. Crusader bounces against the ropes, and runs at Douglas. He attempts a huracanrana, but Douglas catches him. Douglas flips Crusader in mid-air, and brings him down to the mat, face-first. Douglas climbs on with a cover, but the newcomer gets a shoulder up at 2. Douglas punches Crusader, knocking him into the turnbuckles. With Crusader stunned, Douglas steps to the outside. Douglas climbs the turnbuckles, and sits himself on the stunned Crusader's shoulders. He turns it into a huracanrana attempt, but Crusader holds him up there, and takes him down to the mat with a running power bomb. Instead of covering, Crusader grabs Douglas by the hair, and throws him to the outside. Crusader climbs the turnbuckles, and comes off with a corkscrew plancha. It connects, and both he and Douglas go flying into the barrier at ringside. Crusader ends up on top, and pounds Douglas with a barrage of punches. Crusader picks him up, and looks to whip him into the other guardrail, but Douglas reverses it. Douglas sends Crusader into the guardrail, and then runs at him. Crusader backbody drops Douglas over the rail, but Douglas lands upright. Douglas balances himself on the guardrail. Douglas jumps on Crusader's back, and takes him over with a huracanrana. Douglas rolls Crusader back into the ring, and climbs the turnbuckles. Douglas comes off with a perfectly executed guillotine leg drop. However, instead of covering, Douglas climbs the turnbuckles once again. Douglas comes off with the senton bomb, The Impact, but Crusader is able to get out of the way at the last second. Douglas pops up, and walks into a sky-high. Crusader runs against the ropes, and then hits Douglas with an Asai Moonsault. Finally, Crusader points to the skies, before climbing the turnbuckles. With Douglas in a vulnerable position, Crusader connects with the Gravity 101. Crusader covers, and a three count later, has made a successful fWo debut. Crusader rolls out of the ring, and as Douglas recovers, Cruiserweight Champion Rudo appears on the entrance ramp, pointing to the Cruiserweight championship belt. Result: Crusader by Pinfall Trouble brewing Cameras picked up Mitch Wilson arriving at the arena and looking from side to side for his hated rival Primetime. As security guards scrambled around him Wilson walked toward the arena entrance, brushing aside Hellrazor and Fear as he went. Wilson the reigning Internet Champion looked grim and annoyed, an expression that was not unusual for him. He walked to the ring, brushing off fWo camera crews that attempted in vain to tell him that his segment was not planned and that a match was supposed to begin at this moment. Wilson slid into the ring and wasted no time in explaining his presence. "Primetime... you lousy pile of shit... I don't want to wait until CyberSlam... so you get your ass out here right now and we can settle this like men... careers on the line you bastard, I just don't care!" The SlaughterScreen blazed into life as a close-up of Primetime appeared. The member of the French Foundation smiled, and urged the cameraman to back up. As the camera panned back it revealed the wreckage of a room torn to shreds. Tables were crashed, weight lifting equipment had been tossed into walls, and the entire room was in a state of general disarray. Primetime grinned as a sign reading "Wilson Training Academy" fell from hooks on the wall and crashed to the floor. Primetime shrugged. "Looks like I've done it again... except this time I think it's gonna really cost you to get this repaired." Primetime laughed as the SlaughterScreen blinked from life, and Wilson cursed inside the ring. Enraged, Wilson swung from the ring and marched up the entrance ramp to the backstage area... Courage & Lance Star vs. Damnation Slaughter resumed as the fans inside of the MGM Grand stood to their feet, anxiously anticipating the next match. "Sad But True" by Metallica shook the arena, and out came Damnation onto the stage. Cheered loudly, the duo stepped into the ring, raising their arms to more adulation from the crowd. They awaited their opponents as their theme faded away, while "Reclaim My Place" by Korn jammed over the sound system. From the backstage area came Courage & Lance Star, teaming again, although Courage appeared reluctant to do so. Courage and Star weren't walking closely together, but both appeared focus on the task at hand, and tonight that was defeating the undefeated tag team of Hellrazor & Fear. The two slid into the ring, and a staredown ensued between them and Damnation. The bell rung, and Courage motioned Star to the apron. Star protested, but to no avail. Courage looked at Hellrazor, who would be starting the match for Damnation. The two locked up, which resulted in a headlock by Hellrazor. Courage reversed it with an armbar, which was reversed again by Hellrazor. To break the hold, Courage executed a back flip, and caught Hellrazor with a kick to the jaw. Hellrazor staggered back to the ropes, at which Courage charged him. Hellrazor ducked and tossed Courage over the ropes. As Courage hit the ground, Fear caught him with an elbow off the apron. Courage gasped for air as Fear tossed him back into the ring. A tag by Hellrazor brought Fear into the match. As they did this, Courage ran over and tagged Star on the shoulder. Star looked at Courage, who told him to get in the ring. Star climbed in the ring and dodged a lock up with Fear; instead, as Fear turned around, Star caught him with a drop kick. Star picked up Fear and whipped him into the ropes. Upon Fear's return, Star hit with a clothesline. He picked up Fear and hit a suplerplex. At the count of two, Hellrazor broke the cover with a kick to the head. As the ref brought Hellrazor back to his corner, Star went to his corner. As he went to tag in Courage, Courage hopped off the apron and walked over to the railing, where a fan was holding an UV sign. Star looked at him in disbelief; Fear looked at him with opportunity. Fear ran over to Star, who was not keeping track of his opponent, and spun him around. He kicked him to the gut and nailed him with a DDT. He picked up Star and walked him over to Hellrazor; a tag in, and Hellrazor began to climb the ropes. Fans knew what this meant, and they began cheering for the No More Pain. Fear picked up Star and gave him to Hellrazor. Hellrazor maneuvered Star into his finisher, and nailed the DDT off the top rope. He rolled up Star; as he did this, Courage hit the ring. However, as the ref counted, and Fear charged Courage, Courage stopped and watched the count. The three count was made, and Damnation were your winners. As they celebrated, Courage stood over Star. He picked up Star, and looked annoyed at him. Star awoke out of his daze and tried to talk to Courage about another match. All the fans can hear was "Dude, you suck." Star grabbed Courage and asked for another match. Courage just shrugged his shoulders, saying "Whatever...", and walked to the back. Star just stared at him walking up the ramp as Slaughter hit a commercial break. Result: Damnation by Pinfall Erik Kelly vs. Rain "Symphony Of Destruction" begins to play and the fans begin to boo as the massive former Internet Champion Rain makes his appearance, walking slowly and menacingly down the ramp and stepping over the top rope into the ring. His music is cut off by "No Leaf Clover" and the fans pop as the equally mammoth Erik Kelly makes his way to the ring, accompanied by the lovely Pamela Dee. Blue lights flash as the huge man makes his way to the ring and steps up and over the top just as Rain did. The lights fade back to normal, and the bell rings. Rain quickly bounces off the ropes and shoulder blocks Kelly, but Kelly doesn't move. Rain attempts the move again, and Kelly is staggered. Rain comes off the ropes once more, but this time Kelly nearly decapitates him with a hellish clothesline that sends Rain down to the mat. Kelly covers, but Rain kicks out at two. Kelly charges at Rain as he gets up and knocks him over the top rope with a big boot to the face. Rain lands on his feet and attempts to shake off the cobwebs, but Kelly suddenly runs and launches himself over the top with a breathtaking no-hands plancha. Kelly lands on top of Rain and begins raining down fists to the head, before pulling him up. Kelly tries to whip Rain into the ring post, but Rain gathers his strength and reverses the whip, sending Kelly face-first into the steel and causing Dee to visibly wince at ringside. Rain walks over to Kelly and calmly brings him up, before grabbing him by the hair and running over to the guardrail, which he attempts to slam Kelly's head into. Kelly blocks with a boot to the guardrail, and slams Rain's head himself. Rain staggers back, and Kelly clotheslines him down to the floor. Kelly then brings Rain up and rolls him into the ring. Rain slowly makes his way to his feet. After Kelly enters the ring, he charges at Rain, but Rain sees him coming and reverses into a powerslam that hits with a thunderous impact. Rain covers and hooks the leg, but Kelly kicks out at two. Rain brings Kelly up to his feet again, and whips him hard into the corner. Rain charges in with a lariat, before turning to face the opposite way and repeatedly striking Kelly with a series of alternating back elbows. Rain walks away and Kelly slowly staggers out, only to eat a big superkick from Rain. Kelly crashes to the mat and Rain makes a cover, but Kelly kicks out at two again. Rain brings Kelly to his feet and spikes him with a DDT, before covering and again only getting a two-count. Beginning to get frustrated, Rain brings Kelly up once again and attempts a whip into the ropes, but Kelly reverses. As Rain comes off, Kelly lifts him up and throws him down with a bearhug spinebuster variation. Kelly is beginning to get his second wind, and he brings Rain up again, before scooping him up. Kelly, in an amazing display of strength, holds the 7'2"/360 Rain in a fall away slam position for a good ten seconds before hurling Rain over his back. Rain slams to the mat, and Kelly signals that it's time for the kill. Kelly brings Rain up and attempts to whip him into the ropes, but Rain reverses. Rain makes the critical mistake of putting his head down, however, and Kelly coming off positions him for the Eradication with a mighty yell of "Power bomb!". Kelly then lifts up the giant Rain and powerbombs him to the mat with a ring-rocking impact. Not satisfied, Kelly brings Rain up once more and repeats the combination of yell and power bomb. Wishing to send a message to the entire fWo, Kelly once more drags the battered Rain to his feet and yells "Power bomb!", before Eradicating Rain for the third time to the cheers of Pamela Dee. Kelly then drops onto Rain, not bothering to hook a leg, and scores the three-count. The fans cheer as Dee enters the ring and the victorious Kelly lifts her high onto his shoulders and Slaughter rolls on... Result: Erik Kelly by Pinfall Trouble in Tom-dice A still image revealed to the viewers that they were being taken to the dojo of Tom "The Kung Fu" Bomb. "RULE 228!", he yelled out, to which the class quickly responded "OBJECTS IN MIRROR MAY BE CLOSER THAN THEY APPEAR!" Tom nods, and then yells out "319!". The class responds "NO FOOD OR DRINK ON THE POOL DECK!" "RULE 65!" "WE ARE WATCHING FOX!" Suddenly, a student's hand shoots up. "Mr. Tom-San Sensei Dude, what does anything have to do with Karate of any sort?" Tom frowned. "Are you questioning the mighty Tom Kwan-Do?" The student shuffled one foot. "Well...uh, yeah, it kind of...sucks." THE DOJO students gasp and begin to surround the defiant young man. They all make "Whaaaaaaaaaaa" karate sounds, before the student yells out, "Look, the Foot Clan's in your backyard!" Alarmed, all of the students and Tom rush out of the basement to go search for them. Alone, the lone student laughs, mumbles "Idiots," and grabbed himself a Bruce Lee poster before leaving. STUMP Squad vs. Wretch & Scott Hart Slaughter heads to the ring and the crowd erupts with boos as Scott Hart and International Champion Wretch, holding the polished silver belt high above his head, begin to make their way to the ring to the sounds of Gob. Wretch and Hart roll into the ring and await their opponents. "We're Not Gonna Take It" begins playing, and the crowd cheers as Black Quicksilver and Mike Heftel in the guise of Silver Slowblack make their way to the ring. The two walk to the ring slightly separated after the events of Warfare. Inside the ring the referee rings the bell and the teams decide that Hart and Slowblack will start for their teams. Hart charges at Slowblack, but Slowblack ducks his lariat and kicks him in the stomach before setting him up for a piledriver. Slowblack plants Hart into the mat, and covers. Fearing an early loss, Wretch breaks up the pin at two. Slowblack brings Hart up once again, and takes him over with a snap suplex. Slowblack then mounts the second rope and comes off with a driving elbow to the throat of Hart. Slowblack covers, but Hart manages to kick out at two. Slowblack brings Hart to his feet and whips him into the ropes, not seeing the blind tag made by Wretch. After Slowblack plants Hart in the middle of the ring with a spinebuster, he fails to see Wretch springboard into the ring and dropkick him in the back of the head. Hart rolls from the ring as Wretch goes to work on the man formerly known as Heftel. Wretch shoves him into the corner and strikes with a series of chops, stinging the chest of Slowblack. Wretch then whips Slowblack across into the opposite corner, before charging in with a leaping body splash. Slowblack stumbles out and Wretch floors him with a clothesline before covering him. Wretch only gets a two-count as Slowblack escapes at the last moment. Wretch brings Slowblack to his feet again and attempts a go-behind into a German suplex, but Slowblack reverses it into a go-behind of his own. Wretch counters with a reverse kick that escapes the notice of our typically incompetent referee. With Slowblack doubled over, Wretch picks him up and spikes him with a Wretch-i-noku Driver, before reaching forward and hooking the leg. The ref counts two before Black Quicksilver hits the ring, making the save in the nick of time. The referee escorts Quicksilver from the ring and Wretch takes the advantage to double-team with Hart. Hart in the corner chokes away at Slowblack while Wretch stomps a mudhole in him, and proverbially walks it dry. As the referee turns away from Quicksilver Hart stops choking and Wretch tags him in. Hart grabs Slowblack and DDTs him out of the corner, before going to the legs to set up his Texas cloverleaf finisher. He hooks the legs, but Slowblack rolls him back into a small package for a near-fall. Both men are quickly to their feet, and Slowblack ducks a clothesline before taking Hart down with a stiff back suplex. Both men are down and begin to crawl to their corners for tags. Both men tag out simultaneously and Wretch charges Black Quicksilver only to be met with a slingshot forearm shot. Wretch staggers back and Quicksilver charges at him with a spinning heel kick, which is ducked by Wretch and Quicksilver hits this partner Slowblack who was just getting to his feet. As Quicksilver checks on his fallen partner, Wretch rolls him up from behind and hooks the tights, scoring a flash pinfall. Wretch jumps up and down in the ring as if he just won the World Title, and rolls Scott Hart from the ring before sliding out after him. Hart slowly pulls himself to his feet and follows Wretch as Black Quicksilver and Silver Slowblack begin to argue in the middle of the ring. The situation quickly blows over, but there is still a small amount of tension between the men as they leave the ring, walking past Wretch and Scott Hart, who are still celebrating. Wretch's celebrating stops suddenly as he is spun around by Mike Bear on the entrance ramp. Bear immediately strikes with a fist to the face, and super kicks down Hart as well. The brawl begins to escalate before officials quickly break it up to boos from the crowd, who wants them to fight. Slaughter rolls on with the refs struggling to maintain order... Result: Wretch & Scott Hart by Pinfall Closer A shaky camera manned by Kristen Jacobs desperately attempted to keep up with Mitch Wilson, who was running down the Las Vegas strip in the direction of the Wilson Training Academy located in the city of sin. "You took the time to take off your damn clothes, not keep up with me you little bitch or I'll give some of what I'm gonna give to Primetime's ass to you." Wilson rounded a corner and spotted the training academy. The door appeared to be hanging from hinges and Wilson screamed aloud as he charged the building. The camera bounced along behind him and when it caught up to Wilson it found the reigning fWo Internet Champion swinging wildly at Primetime who appeared to have been caught in the act. Wilson was fighting with fury and had overpowered the member of the French Foundation, but Primetime blindly groped for a chair and swung, connecting with Wilson's head. As Wilson lay dazed Primetime got to his feet and laughed. Primetime grabbed the Internet Championship he had stolen from the floor and made a quick escape. Wilson got slowly to his feet, his face wearing a mask of red rage. Scott Collins vs. Flying Frenchie Slaughter returns from commercials as "Burning" plays. Scott Collins steps onto the entrance ramp, below the SlaughterScreen as the crowd cheers. Collins walks to the ring, when "Juke Joint Jezebel" starts up, bringing out the leader of the French Foundation, the Flying Frenchie. Clad in his "Why Must Ze Frenchie Be So Handsome?" shirt, the Foundation leader walks to the ring, with a concentrated look on his face. The Frenchie steps into the ring, and immediately, Collins attacks him with a barrage of fists. Collins continues the attack with a whip to the ropes. Frenchie ducks a short-arm clothesline, and comes off with a flying shouldertackle. Frenchie follows it up with a few fists that send Collins reeling into the turnbuckles. Frenchie grabs him in a collar and elbow tie up, and moves Collins away from the buckles. Frenchie climbs them, and comes off, looking for a Tornado DDT. However, Collins hangs on to the move, and Frenchie can't get him over. Collins plants Frenchie with a German suplex, and then goes for a quick cover. Frenchie gets a shoulder up at two, and Collins has to go back on the attack. Collins nails some solid forearms to the masked head of the Frenchman, sending him reeling into the ropes. Collins with an Irish whip, and he catches Frenchie with a perfect tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Once again, Collins goes for a quick cover, and once again, Frenchie gets a shoulder up. Frustrated, Collins throws Frenchie to the outside. Collins grabs a chair from ringside, and runs at TFF with it. However, Frenchie is able to dropkick the chair back into the face of Collins. Frenchie grabs the chair, and nails Collins in the back with it, before rolling him into the ring. Frenchie covers, and this time it's Collins getting the shoulder up. Frenchie picks Collins up by the hair, and sends him into the ropes. Collins ducks a clothesline, and nails a boot to the Frenchie's gut. He sets him up for a power bomb, but Frenchie grabs Collins by the legs, and slingshots him into the ropes. Collins bounces back, and the awaiting Frenchie catches him in a huracanrana. Frenchie covers, but Collins gets out at two. Frenchie climbs to the top rope, looking for the 450, but suddenly "Rage" by Savatage hits and Violet leads Ultra Violet to the ring. UV looks at Violet inquisitively, saying "There aren't any balloons out here!", but then UV spots TFF, and exclaims "It's that big meanie! Let's get him!" before running to the ring. A camera flash along the way blinds UV, and he stumbles blindly, knocking TFF off of the turnbuckles. Collins has recovered, and looks at this, but from behind, Perfect attacks Collins, and levels him with a steel chair. With both men out, and Perfect standing in the ring, the referee has nothing to do except to declare the match a no contest, as Slaughter cuts to commercial break. Result: No Contest Dead & Perfect vs. Boston Strangler & Eron Slaughter heads down to the ring for the main event, and the crowd is absolutely jacked for this huge tag-team matchup. The arena lights slowly dim to black, then an explosion rocks the arena as "Oh My God" begins playing. The lights turn a crimson red and lasers blast throughout the arena as one-half of FTfWo makes an appearance on the top of the entrance ramp - four-time World Champ Perfect and current and three-time World Champion Dead, belt strapped securely around his waist. These two titans of FTfWo make their way to the ring, reveling in the boos, before rolling in. Perfect mounts the turnbuckles, giving the "up yours" sign to the crowd, while Dead remains stoic in the ring, awaiting the arrival of their opponents. "Physical" begins playing, and the boos continue as the king of the screwjobs Eron appears on the entrance ramp, before walking down to the ring. Not wishing to be in the ring with both Dead and Perfect, he begins to harass the fans outside the ring. He is interrupted as the lights fade out once more. The thundering beginning of "O Fortuna" begins playing, and a bank of fog rolls out across the entrance ramp, partially concealing the menacing visage of the Boston Strangler. The Strangler slowly and confidently walks to the ring, but instead of entering, goes after his partner Eron who has resumed his heckling. Eron never sees the Strangler walk up behind him and throw him to the floor with a released German suplex. Eron clutches his neck in pain as a result of the Strangler's assertion that he needs no partner. Strangler rolls into the ring and warily eyes Perfect and Dead. The bell rings, and Perfect immediately charges Strangler, who ducks and shoves him into the corner before hammering away with rights and lefts to the jaw. The Strangler winds up with a big right, and the force of the blow knocks Perfect to a horizontal position draped across the two turnbuckles. The Strangler takes a few steps back, then runs towards the corner with a field goal kick that strikes Perfect right between the shoulderblades, causing him to roll off the turnbuckles and splat to the mat. The Strangler covers, but Perfect kicks out at two. Strangler brings the World's Greatest Athlete to his feet, and whips him hard into the opposite corner. Strangler charges in, but Perfect moves and Strangler slams into the turnbuckles sternum-first. He staggers out, and Perfect hooks him from behind for the Perfect Game. Strangler struggles and manages to reverse it into a go-behind, and he clamps on the Boston Massacre. Perfect immediately counters with an inverted testicular kick, doubling Strangler over. Perfect leaps up and rocker droppers the Strangler down, before covering and hooking the leg. Strangler kicks out at two, and Perfect brings him up once more, throwing him into the corner and striking with a series of chops. Perfect then begins stomping away at the Strangler until he is slumped in a sitting position, before backing up, running forward, and kicking him right in the nose just because he can. Strangler falls backward, slumping against the turnbuckles, and Perfect charges back in with a seated dropkick to the face. Perfect then pulls Strangler to his feet and brings him to the FTfWo corner, where he tags in Dead. Perfect holds Strangler open for Dead, and Dead hammers Strangler right in the kidneys with a clenched fist. Dead then blasts Strangler in the face with a right, which seems to anger the big man. Strangler fights back with a right of his own, then another, and another, and finally backs Dead into the ropes before whipping him across. Dead reverses the whip however, and ducks under Strangler coming off. Perfect then knees Strangler in the back as he hits the ropes, leading him to stumble out into a Dead DDT. Dead covers and hooks the leg, but Strangler kicks out at the count of two. Dead brings Strangler up once more and lifts him up in a vertical suplex, before dropping him down with a brainbuster. Dead tags out to Perfect, who mounts the top rope before coming off with a picture-perfect (no pun intended) splash to the Strangler. Perfect hooks the leg as Dead mounts the turnbuckles and cackles in apparent victory - 1... 2... and Strangler kicks out at the last moment. The fans erupt in cheers for their hero, and Perfect sneers as he pulls Strangler to his feet and gestures to Dead, who hops down from the turnbuckles and assists Perfect in double-whipping Strangler to the ropes. They attempt a double-clothesline, but Strangler ducks it and when they turn around, FTfWo is met by a decapitating double-clothesline from Strangler. All three men in the ring are down, and outside the ring Eron is beginning to stir. Eron gets to his feet and begins to roll into the ring, but a hand pulls him back. The hand belongs to the Flying Frenchie, and Courage is by his side. The Foundation begins to brawl with Eron, and suddenly from the back comes an outpouring of fWo wrestlers, led by Damnation, Fear and Hellrazor, who are bringing Lamont. Eron begins to back away, but the large group of wrestlers angry at his recent action pounces on him and Eron is figuratively laid to rest by the huge posse of ass-kickers. The Foundation gets in a series of shots, but moves aside to allow Damnation through. As Hellrazor and Fear converge for a double-team on Eron, they carefully demonstrate what it's like to stomp ass to hell and back. This attack is highlighted by a devastating Lamont-Sault from the fWo's favorite feline. This beating continues on the outside as Strangler, Dead, and Perfect begin to stir inside the ring. Dead is up first, followed by Strangler. Dead swings wildly at Strangler for a lariat, but Strangler hooks his arm in mid-move and locks him into the Boston Massacre to a monstrous pop. Dead immediately begins to tap, but the referee can't do anything as he is not the legal man. The legal man is Perfect, who makes his way to his feet, shakes off the cobwebs, and stalks up behind the Strangler. With Strangler's attention focused on Dead, Perfect hooks his head from behind, lifts him up, and drives him into the mat with the Perfect Game. Perfect covers Strangler and three seconds later, FTfWo escapes with a lucky victory. They immediately bail from the ring and Dead gathers his belt and mocks the crowd. However, inside the ring the Strangler is stirring and Dead never sees him pounce on him from the ring apron. The two begin brawling through the crowd while Perfect, still upset at Warfare's results, joins in the ass-kicking party of Eron as Slaughter goes off the air... Result: Dead & Perfect by Pinfall
