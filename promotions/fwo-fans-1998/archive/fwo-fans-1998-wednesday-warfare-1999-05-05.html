Wednesday Warfare, 5 May 1999

5 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction As Warfare opened up, we saw "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal making his entrance into the arena via a limosuine, and he climbed out and stood on top of it holding his fWo Internet Title high in the air, acting as if he owned the place. He then shouted "I'm the king of the world!" repeatedly, as Megan Dawn climbed out of the limo. Suddenly, Austin Davis made an appearance, first scaring Dawn, and then throwing her into the limo. He then climbed on top of the limo and brawled with Universal. Universal at first gained the upper hand with a rake of the eyes, and then went for the "Cha Ching", but Davis nearly pushed him off the lengthy end of the limo. Universal gathered himself and turned around, but was met with a kick to the stomach, and then Davis hit a modified version of the "Cha Ching" of his own on Universal. Universal' body bounced off the limo and onto the ground, and Davis then reposessed the Internet Title and took it into his own possession. As Davis was about to leave, he smirked at Universal and gave him the middle finger, yelling "fuck you!" and then climbed into the limosuine. Universal grabbed a chair and threw it at the limosuine as it sped off, apparently with Davis *and* Dawn inside. Universal then saw Slugger & Ice and shouted profanities at them, and as the viewing audience saw this, one can only assume that they were wondering how Davis & Universal would co-exist as a team against Slugger & Ice at Extreme Measures... and, wondering where Davis was taking Dawn... Primetime vs. Mitch Wilson The three members of the French Foundation make their entrance, with their leader The Flying Frenchie in front. Frenchie carries with him the flag of France, as Mitch Wilson & Primetime follow behind. They climb into the ring and Frenchie asks for a microphone, and as the fans hatred for the Foundation continues, Frenchie begins to speak. Frenchie sez zat it iz a travesty for ze fWo to expect two friends like Wilson & Primetime to wrestle because ze outcome of ze match could tarnish zeir friendship. Frenchie continued, saying zat being ze two outstanding competitors that Mitch & Primetime are, zey would *wrestle* and give ze unworthy American fans a match to remember. Frenchie then gave the microphone to Wilson, who said that the match would be a lesson for the Panther, and he should watch and learn. Primetime and Wilson then shook hands, and wished each other the best of luck. Frenchie did the same, saying that their friendship will overcome any fierce competition between the two in the end. The bell then rang, and the match began. Both men start off with a collar & elbow tie-up, before Wilson grabs an arm bar. Wilson twists it into a hammer lock, but Primetime quickly counters into a snapmare takeover. Wilson gets back up to his feet quickly; and the two men enter a collar & elbow tie-up once again. Primetime quickly with the upperhand into a sideheadlock, and then a headlock takeover. Primetime working Wilson over, before Wilson fights it up back to a vertical base, and uses his upper body strength to send Primetime into the ropes. Primetime returns and Wilson takes him down with a shoulder block. Primetime back up to his feet and Wilson hits him with a hip toss, which sends PT flying underneath the bottom rope onto the floor. Frenchie, lends a helping hand, helping PT up to his feet. PT pushes him off at first, but then realizes Frenchie is only trying to help him up. PT climbs back into the ring and pushes Wilson furiously, and Wilson is somewhat confused. Not backing down, Wilson pushes him back. Primetime takes offense, and slaps Wilson. Wilson' face turns to anger, and Wilson shoves him so hard that Primetime bounces off the ropes, and comes back to Wilson, who lifts him up onto his back and hits him with a Samoan Drop. Wilson then gets in his face and slaps him, beforeWilson goes for a cover, but Primetime is able to kickout at the count of two. Undaunted, Wilson brings Primetime to his feet, whips him to the ropes and goes for a clothesline, PT ducks, returns off the ropes from the other side and as Wilson ducks down for a backdrop, Primetime hits a DDT. PT with a cover, but Wilson kicks out. PT brings Wilson to his feet and hits him with a kick to the stomach, before following it up with a lightning quick snap suplex. PT brings him to his feet again, and whips him into the turnbuckles. PT goes in, and nails Wilson with an elbow to the side of the face. Wilson staggers out and PT follows it up with a running bulldog, however, as PT brings his legs in front of him Wilson uses his own free arms to catch his legs, and counters into a back suplex. Both men are out of it now, and the referee begins a ten-count. At six, Wilson returns to his feet to stop the count, and waits for Primetime to get up. PT does, and Wilson takes both he and PT over the top rope out onto the cement floor with a flying clothesline. Wilson gets up first, and grabs a chair from the timekeeper. Frenchie goes to stop him at first, but then Wilson tells him to get out of his way. Frenchie obliges, and Wilson says "this is for shoving me", and he cracks the hard steel over PT's head as he was attempting to get up. Frenchie goes to grab the chair from him again, and as they struggle, PT recovers. PT gets up and nails Wilson with a dropkick to the back, which sends the steel chair flying into Frenchie's head. Frenchie falls to the floor, and both Wilson and Primetime look shocked. Wilson turns, and says "this is for slapping me!", he goes to take PT' head off with the chair, but Primetime ducks, and when Wilson turns around, PT dropkicks the chair back into Wilson' face. PT then checks on his friend Frenchie, as this match continues to heat up. Wilson rubs his face, feeling the affects of the chair shot. Wilson sees Primetime being a compassionate human being, and scoffs. As PT is bent over, Wilson walks over from behind and pushes him into the guardrail face first. Wilson then checks on Frenchie, and helps him back to his feet. Wilson then walks over to Primetime and kicks him in the stomach, before executing a perfect gutwrench suplex on the cement floor. Wilson follows it up by rolling PT into the ring. Thinking it is over, he goes for a cover, but PT gets his foot on the bottom rope at the count of two... or did Frenchie put it there? Nevertheless, Wilson goes back on the attack, first bringing PT to his feet, and whipping him into the turnbuckle. Wilson walks over and chops (whoo!) the chest of Primetime not once, not twice, not three times, but ten times in succession. Primetime staggers out and falls face first down, and Wilson rolls him over for a cover, but garners only a two count. Wilson brings Primetime to a vertical base and whips him into the corner hard. Primetime goes flying in, and up and over, ala Ric Flair some 10 years and 45 pounds ago. As Primetime walks across the ring apron now, Wilson meets him half way and locks on a sleeperhold. As he does this, Primetime drops to the floor, and Wilson' neck snaps across the top rope. Primetime thinks its time to finish him off now, and he climbs back into the ring and brings Wilson to his feet. Primetime kicks him in the midsection, before placing Wilson head between his legs, obviously going for the powerbomb. He attempts, but Wilson blocks. Another attempt, and another block. Wilson then uses his neck strength to bring Primetime up; apparently going for a backdrop, but Primetime holds on. Primetime now upside down as Wilson contemplates what to do next as PT is on his back. Wilson simple falls backwards, and he falls back first onto Primetime. Wilson stays on top of PT in this fashion, attempting to pin him using his back. One, two, but PT recovers and bridges up out of the pinfall, then twists Wilson around, but he is stopped before he can go any further, and Wilson brings Primetime up onto his back, and follows up with a Death Valley Driver. As the time limit approaches, Wilson goes for a pinfall attempt, and gets it, garnering the victory and the International Title shot against the Panther this Friday. As Frenchie climbs into the ring, he raises Wilson' hand, before congratulating him on the victory. Wilson & Frenchie then help Primetime to his feet, and he and Wilson shake hands, congratulating each other on a hard fought match. As the three men raise their arms in unison, and most of the crowd applauding a great wrestling match, the Black Panther appears on the screen and says; "Wilson, if you want the title as bad as you say you do, you're going to have to earn it. And you can "just wrestle" all you want, the fact of the matter is NOBODY can recover from the Pounce. And like a panther stalking its prey, this Friday, I'm going Pounce. See you then, bitch, I mean Mitch!" As Wilson looks on frustrated and angry, PT and Frenchie attempt to calm him down as we step out for a commercial break... Result: Mitch Wilson by Pinfall Backstage arrivals Backstage cameras found the Dark Riders entering the arena. War, Plague, Stone, Scott Taylor and Death all exited the limo and shoved cameramen and fWo officals out of the way as they entered the arena. As Stone left to get ready for her match, a single delivery man appeared before Plague and handed him a small silver rose. Plague looked at it somberly, and then grabbed the delivery man by the neck and drove him into the wall. At once security arrived and began to pull Plague off the delivery man, but Plague would have none of it. War began to toss security men off of his tag team partner, when suddenly they were attacked from behind by two men weilding garbage cans. Dressed in colorful clothing with sports jerseys reading "13" and "69" the two grabbed War and sent him crashing to the hard floor with a double powerbomb. Death charged in at the two who made a quick escape. As Death checked in on War and Plague, fWo cameras and Cactus Jon caught up with the two strange men who attacked the Dark Riders. "I don't know who you are, but after what you just did to the Dark Riders you are friends in my book!" The two men laughed at this, and grabbed Jon by the head and flung him into the brick wall of the arena. "We're trouble idiot... and we don't care about you or the fWo. We want those Tag Team titles, and don't try to get in our way!" With that the cameras took viewers to the fWo rewind, featuring the end of the Mitch Wilson/Primetime match. Frank Snow vs. Sid Snow Sid Snow came to the ring first, strutting down the ramp and preening his hair on the way down. Snow climbed into the ring and politely asked for a microphone, and then waited as "Leatherman" Frank Snow made his way down to the ring. Snow climbed into the ring and dwarfed over the smaller, petite Sid. Sid stops Snow from attacking him first, saying he had something to say first. Sid said that he noticed something that he and Frank had in common, and that was they both had the same last name. With that said, Sid suggested that they become "blood brothers". He then asked Frank what he thought, and Frank simply gave him the middle finger, before charging at him and clotheslining him over the top rope out onto the floor. Frank continued from there, first lifting Snow up and dropping him chest first onto the commentators table. Then, Frank through Sid shoulder first into the ring steps; before finally piledriving him on the cement floor. He then threw Sid back into the ring, waited for him to get up, before flying off the top rope with a flying body press. Subsequent pinfall only gathered him a two count, so Frank continued. He throws Sid into the ropes, and takes him out with a sidewalk slam. From there, Frank hits a beautiful Fisherman's Suplex, which Sid kicks out of at the count of two and a half. Finally, Frank tells Sid to beg for mercy, because he's going to end his career. A look of horror comes over Sid' face as Frank throws him through the middle ropes onto the outside. He then takes the steel stairs and places them near Sid' leg, before taking a chair and hammering it alongside Sid' leg and ankle. As Sid screams in pain, Frank laughs, before rolling him back into the ring, and slapping on an anklelock submission. Sid begins to cry in pain, and tears stream down his face. The referee asks him if he wants to give up, but Sid is crying uncontrollably and you cannot understand his slurred speech. Finally, Sid' head slumps into the mat, apparently passing out from a loss of... tears? At any rate, the referee awards the match to Frank, who finally releases the hold, laughing hysterically at what he just saw. The referee attempts to raise Frank's hand, but Frank shrugs it off, before heading up the ramp as Warfare goes to a commercial break. Result: Frank Snow by Submission Fathom Prepares Backstage Fallen is spotted going into Fathom's dressing room, and offering to escort her to the main arena entrance. Fathom looks worried, and Fallen assures her that he will be watching closely so nothing very bad happens. Fathom seems energized and nods in approval. She stands to her feet and says "Let's do it!" before marching out of the locker room. Fallen trails along behind holding a small box in one hand and looking anxious and confused... Stone (c) vs. Fathom "Burning" plays over the arena soundsystem, and Stone makes her way out to an ovation of boos. Nobody likes the Queen of the Dark Riders, apparently. Stone carries with her the fWo Women's Title, which she won at CyberSlam 2 back on April 23. Stone slides into the ring and awaits Fathom, her former friend and prodigy from the bWo. Fathom makes her presence to a nice reaction from the crowd, and she eagerly runs down to the ring. Her and Stone lock up immediately, and Stone gets the upper hand and throws her to the mat by her hair. As Fathom gets up, Stone elbows her in the midsection, and then takes her down with a reverse Russian legsweep. Stone brings her back to her feet quickly and follows it up with a snapmare takeover, and then a legdrop. Cover, but only a two count. She throws her to the outside and waits for her to get up, before both of her feet slam into Fathom's face with a beautiful baseball slide. Now on the outside, she throws Fathom into the ringsteps. However, with her back turned to the ramp, Wynd & the rest of the bWo come to the ring, along with Fallen. Wynd pushes her first from behind and Stone goes flying into the ring guardrail. Stone comes back and turns around and Fyre takes her down with a hiptoss, and Stone hits hard against the floor. Wynd slams her head first into the ring steps, and Fallen follows suit by rolling Stone into the ring, before walking over and helping up Fathom as the others take care of Stone inside the ring. Wynd & Fyre hold Stone as Fathom climbs into the ring and slaps Stone HARD, before Wynd & Fyre chuck her over the top onto the cement floor. Fathom, not done yet, waits for Stone to get up before taking Stone out with a baseball slide of her own, before coming back into the ring and hugging Fallen... even though Fathom lost the match, a moral victory should satisfy... Result: Stone by DQ Backstage Action In the backstage area Death is spotted grabbing television monitors and tossing them at the wall, sending hundreds of pieces of glass spraying into the room. War and Plague are also demolishing the room, smashing chairs, tables, and anything they can get their hands on. Suddenly an fWo security guard goes flying into the room, followed by Cactus Jon, Scott Slugger, and Ice weilding metal crowbars. At once a brawl ensues, with the Dark Riders taking on members of team fWo. A hard metal bar strikes flesh as Scott Slugger lands a vicious shot to the back of the fWo World Champion Death, who shrugs it off and tosses a table at Slugger. fWo security arrives to break up the massive fight, as Jon, Ice and Slugger take a break in the corner. Ice looks around the room and asks "Hey... where is Stone?" Revenge Cameras cut to the hallway outside Fathom's dressing room, where Fathom and Fallen, along with the rest of the bWo, make their way to her room. There is a feeling of excitement and perhaps vindication after the Women's title match, and spirits are high. Telling Fallen and the bWo to give her just a second, Fathom enters her locker room to change. Closing the door behind her, she doesn't notice Stone, standing just behind where the door swings open. Stone quickly locks the door and shoves a chair beneath the doorknob as Fathom turns in shock. Stone grabs a rag that smells of ether and lunges at Fathom's face, trapping her on the ground as the door begins to rattle. Fathom loses conciousness as Stone laughs and now pounding can be heard on the door. Suddenly serious Stone leans over the barely aware Fathom and whispers "this is for holding me down..." before grabbing Fathom's forearm and snapping it with a hard twist. Fathom screams as the door is finally broken down, and Fallen and the bWo rush into the room. They see Fathom, her left arm crooked and bleeding and miss Stone, who escapes out from behind them. Paramedics are quickly called, but the damage is done... Wild Thing vs. Cairn Markus Cairn makes his entrance first, acting cocky as ever. He stops near a camera and speaks into it, saying that he is "the master of doing the Wild Thing, er, that didn't come out right", before shrugging and heading down to the ring. He climbs in and the crowd doesn't know how to react to him, other than to give him obscene gestures with their hands. Nevertheless, Cairn waits as The Wild Thing makes his way out onto the stage with his pack of wolves, his wolves wait as Wild Thing walks down to the ring. As he does, Cairn gets on the microphone and says "Wild Thing, you make my eyes strain. You make everythang... ugly. Wild Thing, you know, you really stink". Cairn begins to laugh; and walks over to the ropes to hand the microphone to one of the fWo staffers. The fWo staffer is too lazy to get off his ass, so Cairn climbs out onto the ring apron, and reaches, handing the microphone over. He turns around and waits on the ring apron, and watches Wild Thing slithers underneath the bottom rope into the ring. Somehow, this strikes Cairn as humorous, and he laughs even more. As he does, Wild Thing charges at him at full speed and dives through the middle & top rope, *spearing* Cairn out onto the broadcast table at ringside. The crowd oohs and aahs at the impact, and Cairn already appears to have his work cut out for him. Wild Thing shakes some of the debris from the table and other assorted items off of himself, before pulling Cairn up. He hammers him in the face repeatedly, with open fists. Deciding that that isn't enough, he grabs a monitor from ringside, and slams it into the face of Cairn, which busts him open immediately. Wild Thing is hungry for blood, and it streams down Cairn' face, he looks at it with pleasure. Wild Thing brings Cairn to his feet as the ref watches the action, and decides he still isn't finished inflicting damage & pain. He brings Cairn over to the other broadcast table, and tosses him up onto it. Wild Thing then goes up onto it himself, and kicks Cairn in the stomach, who managed to get to his feet quickly. Wild Thing then grabs a front facelock, and then turns it into a swinging neckbreaker, and both men go flying through another broadcast table! Debris flies all over the place once again, Wild Thing seems to have been affected very little, however. Wild Thing finally takes the action into the ring, rolling Cairn in. He covers him like a predator eating its prey, and the ref counts to three quickly, as Cairn is a bloody mess. Thing simply smirks, showing some emotion, before heading back up to the ramp warily. Slowly, but surely, he reaches the top to his pack of wolves. Before Warfare has a chance to go to a commercial break, Cairn groggily stands up and still bloody, asks for a megaphone. The referee tries to tell him that they don't have a megaphone, but Cairn ignores him. The referee finally gets him a microphone, and Cairn says that Wild Thing attacked him from behind, and it wasn't fair. He says that he knows he can beat a unicorn, and Wild Thing is no different, because Wild Thing has horns too. Cairn says that he'll put his career on the line and if Wild Thing can defeat him, he'll retire. Wild Thing; who had turned to listen, simply nods and agrees to the match. Cairn laughs before tripping over his own feet and falling outside the ring, as Warfare *finally* reaches that commercial break... Result: Wild Thing by Pinfall Ultra Violet Interview After nearly two weeks of not being seen at an fWo show, Ultra Violet arrives in a small room backstage to give an interview with Matt Loco. Matt asks Ultra Violet about his feelings on Scotty Cool and the formation of the Dark Riders once again, on the anniversary of the fWo. Ultra Violet shakes his head in dismay, and explains that he wishes that he only had some way of meeting Scotty in the ring one-on-one. Matt is confused as he reminds Ultra Violet of the battle royal results at CyberSlam. Ultra Violet seems even more glum as he explains that he's never lost a Battle Royal before and he figured he had better get to the gym and train harder in the future. More confused Matt Loco tries another angle of questioning regarding the team fWo match against the Dark Riders on Friday at Dark Days. Ultra Violet shook his head happily and explained that Dark Days was being held in the same city as the 1999 Toy Fair, and he would be there. Matt asked if Ultra Violet meant the match or the Toy Fair, to which Ultra Violet responded by becoming absorbed with the shiney camera. Sensing the interview was at an end, Matt took the viewers back to ringside. Mad Trucker w/Darkness vs. Stevie Roberts The Trucker makes his way out accompanied by Darkness, and Trucker looks evil... and scruffy... and doesn't look quite the fun loving guy we all used to know. Trucker & Darkness climb into the ring, before Darkness whisper in Trucker' ears as "HOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOONK!" continues to play over the arena soundsystem. Darkness then climbs outside the ring and waits on the floor, looking up at the ramp, waiting for Stevie Roberts. Trucker is also looking up at the ramp, and Roberts comes into the ring from the crowd, behind Trucker. Roberts slides in and as he does, he accidentally hits Trucker' leg, which only bumps the huge man. Trucker turns around and lifts him up with both arms, choking him with his massive hands. Trucker then tosses him aside like a pissed off newspaper boy delivering the the newspaper at 6am in the morning. Trucker follows it up with a huge big boot, which sends Roberts sprawling up and over the top rope onto the cement floor. As Trucker distracts the referee, Darkness walks over and grabs Roberts, choking him like Trucker did moments ago, only with one hand. Darkness then follows it up by piledriving Roberts on the ring steps, and Roberts head hits hard against the steel mesh. Finally, Darkness rolls Roberts into the ring, and Trucker follows suit. Trucker brings him to his feet, and in succession, repeatedly, gives Roberts three more big boots. Trucker is hell-a-over confident, but nevertheless, continues his attack. Trucker whips him into the corner and goes for a back drop, and somehow Roberts musters up enough energy to turn it into a sunset flip. However, Trucker simply sits down, and his knees pin Roberts. The referee counts, one, two, and at two and a half, Roberts uses his legs to counter and finally makes a sunset flip. One, two, three, and Roberts pulls off the upset victory. Darkness is furious, and he climbs into the ring. He and Trucker double-team Roberts, first with a double-team powerbomb. Finally, they whip him to the ropes and Darkness catches him belly-to-belly, before pushing him up words into the air and falling backwards, right into a Trucker diamond cutter. Roberts is finished, and security, officials, scatter the ring, trying to get the man they call Mad Trucker & the evil Darkness out of the ring as the show goes off the air... Result: Stevie Roberts by Pinfall
