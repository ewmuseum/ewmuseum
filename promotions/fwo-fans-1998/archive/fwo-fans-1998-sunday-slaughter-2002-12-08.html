Sunday Slaughter, 8 December 2002

8 December 2002

Introduction Previously... The fWo returns to Sunday Night with Sunday Night Slaughter... on the air once again after spending the majority of the air off television. In one night the fWo will return to shows twice a week... a new fWo United States Champion will be crowned... and a mysterious secret behind the funding of the fWo and what dangerous new ties have been created will be revealed... Welcome to Slaughter "Ladies and gentlemen... welcome to Sunday Night Slaughter!" Matt Panzer looked around at the crowd, beaming at the crowd faces he saw around him. There were empty seats, but these days in wrestling there were always empty seats. Those feds that claimed otherwise were lying, papering the books, or just insane. Still... The red and black set for Sunday Night Slaughter did look imposing, and it was nice to see one of the flagship shows back in production and back on the air... but the whole look did seem a bit like it belonged back in the nineties rather than in this century. Panzer wasn't any kind of artist, but these things did seem somewhat obvious. Black, red and silver as a color scheme? Panzer sighed and caught himself. God, even his thoughts were boring. Perhaps he should try a hobby during the off moments of the night... knitting or something. Coming down the entrance ramp were the Slaughter announcing team of Scott Taylor and Mathew Zhuk... the later wearing a tight, sleeveless shirt and wearing a new cropped haircut. Zhuk hadn't been on the fWo roster for years, and Scott Taylor blinked at the bright lights of the arena. Taylor had been an announcer once upon a time, then became a manager for the heel stable the Dark Riders before finally moving into a promoting job for one of the developmental leagues down in Orlando. Now he was back, and Taylor looked more nervous than anyone. At least the crowd didn't recognize him from his heel days and start throwing bottles at him. "Tonight... tonight as we welcome you back to fWo Sunday Night Slaughter we do so with a special treat for you, our loyal fans." Panzer was back to talking to the crowd again, reading over the scripted comments printed on his card. "Tonight we will introduce the new fWo United States Championship belt!" The crowd cheered at this, and Panzer raised his hand to silence the crowd. They did so, but unwillingly. "In an attempt to remind viewers of the tradition of the fWo, this title will be determined in a manner reminiscent of the original fWo World Championship debut in the fWo... in an over the top rope battle royal featuring many of the top fWo superstars currently here tonight, and backstage anxiously awaiting their opportunity. But that's not all ladies and gentlemen... in addition the fWo will crown the vacant Cruiserweight Championship, right here, tonight, in this very ring, and coming up right now! Ladies and gentlemen... I once again welcome you to the fWo, and to Sunday Night Slaughter!" The music blared up over the speakers again, the lights flashed... and the night officially begun. Areumna'derin Rahl vs. Great Dragon "The following contest is scheduled for one fall, and is to crown the fans Wrestling organization World Cruiserweight Champion!" exclaimed the ring announcer, as the hyped match between Areumna'derin Rahl and Great Dragon for the Cruiserweight championship was going to take place on this first edition of Sunday Slaughter in nearly eight months. The ring announcer then began announcing the arrival of the first opponent, as "43" by Mushroomhead cued up on the PA system. This signaled the arrival of the first participant in the match, Areumna'derin Rahl, who came out to a mixed reaction from the crowd, mainly due to the poor matches he had recently. Rahl ignored the fans though, and proceeded to walk to ringside. Rahl climbed the ring steps, and entered the ring, eyeing the fans briefly along the way. The referee inspected him quickly, making sure there weren't any weapons on him as the last notes of "43" played over the PA system. As the referee finished checking him, "Reclaim my Place" blasted over the PA system, and Great Dragon walked out onto the rampway, Laura once again not present. The ring announcer began announcing Dragon's statistics as he walked down the rampway, slapping the occasional fan's hand as they cheered him on. Dragon walked down to ringside, eyed his opponent, and rolled into the ring. Dragon kipped up, and the referee went over to check him. Once the referee was satisfied, he signaled for the bell, and the match began. Dragon circled the ring, as did Rahl, and the two measured each other up. Almost simultaneously, Dragon and Rahl both reached out, locking up in a collar-elbow tieup, with Dragon getting the advantage. Dragon used the advantage to execute an armdrag on Rahl, who quickly kipped up. Rahl then ran towards Dragon, who grabbed his arm again, and flipped him over for another armdrag. However, Rahl grabbed Dragon's own arm, and took him over as well. Both men fell to the mat, and quickly kipped up, as Dragon eyed Rahl. Dragon ran towards Rahl, and swung his arm in a Lariat fashion. Rahl, however, ducked the Lariat, and came up right behind Dragon. Rahl jumped onto Dragon's back, and tried to lock on the Katahajime submission hold, as Dragon couldn't stand the weight of his larger opponent. Dragon fell backwards, landing over Rahl in a pinning predicament. At two, however, Rahl managed to knee Dragon in the back, breaking the pinning situation, and getting up. Rahl lifted up Dragon, and pointed to the crowd, before he hooked his head under his arm, and drove it into the ring with a DDT. Rahl hopped onto the top turnbuckle, and quickly sat down, executing a picture perfect Split-legged moonsault onto Dragon. Rahl got a two and a half on Dragon, but he managed to power out. Rahl slapped the mat in frustration, and got up, lifting Dragon up with him. He hooked Dragon's head, and this time began lifting him into the air. Almost at the top, however, Dragon managed to reverse the situation, flipping out of the setup behind Rahl. Rahl was caught off guard, and Dragon hooked his arms around Rahl's waist. Rahl tried to fight free, but it was too late. Dragon used all of his strength to lift Rahl up into the air, and then drove him into the mat with a German Suplex. Dragon then lifted both himself and Rahl up again, and executed another one. This time, however, Dragon bridged the move, but got a two and a half only, one half of a slap away from winning the belt. Dragon slapped the mat in frustration, and proceeded to grab Rahl for another move, but Rahl pulled him into a small package. Rahl got a quick two before Dragon kicked out, and kipped up onto his feet, eyeing Rahl, and sneering at his opponent. Dragon waited as Rahl got up slowly, a bit disoriented from the force of the pin breakup. Dragon then buzzsaw kicked Rahl in the face as the fans went wild. Dragon made a knife-like motion with his thumb across the throat. Then, he climbed up the turnbuckle, and turned towards his opponent. Rahl lied on the ground, barely moving, and Dragon smiled for a brief second. Then he hopped off, and flipped into midair flawlessly. Dragon came crashing down onto Rahl's chest with his 630 Splash as the fans went wild, and the referee made the academic three count, securing the victory. "Reclaim my Place" cued back up over the PA system, as Dragon was handed the fWo World Cruiserweight Championship. He got up onto his feet, and had his arm raised by the referee, and then lifted the belt into the air, posing with it for a few seconds before he rolled out of the ring, and headed up the rampway. Rahl, in the meanwhile, was informed of the loss to Dragon, and sunk his head, as once again he had lost, especially when it mattered. Though he may have put up quite the fight, in the end, it's not the effort that counts, but whether or not you came out the winner. And tonight, he came short.
