Introduction Previously... It is the end of the year, and throughout the fWo spirits are high. From a three month overseas trip which saw not only a new fWo World Heavyweight champion crowned, but the expansion of the fWo's product and empire. But back home all is not well, and as the fWo moves to the end of the year and Countdown, ominous clouds loom on the horizon... Coral stood up and frowned, not really happy with the fact that of all the people who were in peril in this match, it just had to be a close friend in Michelle Masters. Nevertheless, an early end would be the better end, and he quickly made a lateral press on Michelle. One. Two. Kickout. Michelle kicked out, so Coral pulled her into the Rogues' side of the ring and made the tag to the Blackest Brother in the Hyrule Kingdom in the Codemaster. Said Blackest Brother came storming into the ring, and immediately made with the stomping on the fallen Firey Thunder Girl. The fans booed the Codemaster as he pulled Michelle to her feet and whipped her across the ropes. Michelle ducked the Codemaster's clothesline, but couldn't duck the Codemaster's followup when she rebounded back. TRIFORCE CLOTHESLINE. The discus clothesline knocked down Michelle, and the Codemaster arrogantly put a foot on Michelle's chest while simultaneously pointing at Ruben Ross, who had now taken Brandon Youngblood's place on the apron, shouting something about Ross being less black than he was. One. Two. Yeah. Right. Like Michelle was gonna job to a foot in the chest. Who do you think she was? Randy Savage? The Codemaster grumbled, but soon grabbed Michelle by the hair and yanked her to her feet before making the tag to Coral Avalon. Coral entered the ring, and both men hooked Michelle up for a suplex, before snapping her over with a double snap suplex. Coral then floated over and made the cover, hooking Michelle's leg. One. Two. Shoulder. Not happening today. Coral didn't seem frustrated and pretty much knew Michelle was resiliant enough to kick out of this sort of thing. He pulled Michelle to her feet and whipped her into the ropes. Coral then caught her with what appeared to be a hip toss, but appearances can be deceiving when you're facing Coral Avalon. Hip toss backbreaker. Michelle landed right on Coral's knee, bounced off, and held her back in pain. She rolled on her stomach to avoid any further pain, but this just opened her up. Coral placed his knees on her back before rocking himself backwards and into the bow-and-arrow backbreaker... a backbreaker that was a submission hold. It was also Rob T's "Agony of Defeat" submission hold, a fact that would've made Rob point at Coral and go "HEY!" if he were actually still there. Coral elected to ignore him. Coral held on to the hold, but meanwhile, the Codemaster climbed up to the top rope unannounced. The referee started his five count just as the Breaker of Kingdom Hearts did something mean. GUILLOTINE LEGDROP! Yes, while Michelle was still trapped in the bow-and-arrow backbreaker. You can say "ouch" now. Coral released the hold pretty much by necessity, and glared at the Codemaster as he exited the ring. Coral sighed to himself, and then rolled over and made a cover on Michelle. One. TWO. THRE-SAVE! That save came courtesy of Ruben Ross. Ruben, not bound by "rules", stayed in the ring and pounded on Coral with some kicks. He pulled Coral to his feet and sent him into the ropes, but Coral grabbed the ropes and stopped his momentum. Ruben charged, but Coral ducked and back dropped him to the floor. With Ruben properly disposed of, Coral pulled Michelle to her feet and whipped her into the ropes. He attempted, again, to catch her with another tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. But Michelle caught him with a headscissors takedown which put Coral on the second ropes. Michelle took the opportunity to phone it in. 619. This time, there was no Scott Falk to stop her. She connected with the swinging kick and landed out on the apron. With Avalon down, she quickly went for the springboard, looking to Drop The Dime. She was denied because of two factors. 1) The Codemaster ran into the ring and was subsequently stopped by the official. 2) Scott Falk, the motherfucker he was, once again put a stop to any of Michelle's offense by grabbing her ankles and yanking her down. However, in spite of the interference, Michelle landed on her feet and then blasted Scott Falk in the face with an elbow smash, knocking him down. Michelle then hopped back up onto the apron and looked to exchange her dime dropping with a little... WEST COAST--BACKBREAKER! In midair, Coral caught her and then drove her down onto his knee with another backbreaker. Coral scooped up Michelle's legs and tried to get victory off of that. One. TWO. THR-NO. Michelle barely eeked out a shoulder. Meanwhile, Scott Falk collected himself on the floor, muttering curses after Masters' elbow strike to his face, "That motherfucking bitch. She should be at fucking home, pregnant and barefoot and making her fucking husband some god damn strudel to suck on." A mother subtlely covered her son's ears while Scott was coming to, all while giving Scott a death glare. Scott, rubbing his jaw, simply asked of the mother, "Stop staring at me, or you're going to have every square inch of the deity in my pants rammed down your fucking throat." Scott narrowed his eyes, "While your son watches." The mother quickly whisked her son away before any more brain damage could be done. Speaking of brain damage, the Codemaster, who was freshly tagged, wished to do some of that to Michelle Masters. As Coral left the ring, the Codemaster put Michelle in a waistlock, before snapping her backwards with a brutal German suplex, complete with a bridge. One. TWO. THRE-NO. A close near-fall, but Michelle managed to squeak a shoulder out. At around this time, Ruben Ross was back up after getting brutally backdropped over the top rope and to the floor, and he was pissed, let me tell you. He slid into the ring, and caught the Codemaster from behind with a hard forearm shot. Michelle managed to weakly roll into the ring, where she fell near Mega Job were. "Dude. Hot chick just fell out right in front of us." Beef said, with a grin on his face. "Yuuuup." Janito said, adjusting his tie. Beef's grin was wiped off his face as soon as Brandon Youngblood came over to try and help out Michelle. Predictably, the two taller members of Mega Job fled, putting themselves between Steve and Scott Falk. Unfortunately, Ruben Ross had about enough of them too. That's why he took a running start and FLEW over the top rope and into both men with a springboard crossbody, which took both adult-sized members of Mega Job down for probably the rest of the match. Ruben stood up, relatively unscathed, and watched Brandon Youngblood sneak into the ring. The Codemaster grumbled. "I thought WE were supposed to be the evil ones who came in without tags." he muttered, before he looked at the referee and frantically pointed in a random direction, "Look! An eagle!" Unfortunately, this referee wasn't as stupid as the last few he had pulled that stunt on, and the referee simply crossed his arms, "You've done that to me for the last time, Brown." The Codemaster winced and looked at the oncoming bullet train known only as Brandon Youngblood's arm. LAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRRIIIIIIIIIOOOOOOOOOOTTTTTTTTTTTOOOOOOOOOOOO~! The Codemaster was a grease spot on the L-And-N. Brandon Youngblood wondered what that fly he just swatted was. Then he went for the cover as he was apparently the legal guy. One. TWO. THRE-SAVED! This time by Avalon, who broke up the three count by diving on top. Despite not being legal, let's just not care about rules any more, because they're homogay. Especially CTU's. Coral caught Brandon as he got up with a kick in the gut, and then whipped him into the ropes. Brandon, however, reversed this and sent Coral in instead. When Coral came back, Brandon looked for the Spinebuster. Not happening, though. Coral caught him with a DDT in midair and drove him to the canvas. Brandon rolled out of the ring and flopped to the floor, while the Codemaster left to deal with a recovering Michelle. The referee, who's lost all control of the match, tried to get Codemaster and Michelle to stop fighting and return to their corners. Ross re-entered the ring at the same time as Coral Avalon recovered from the DDT. He booted Coral in the gut. Ratings Spike? No way. Coral knew that move just as well as Ruben Ross did, and quickly turned over into his own inverted facelock. But Ross dropped to his knees, shrugged off the inverted facelock, and proceeded to catch Avalon in the nuts with an "accidental" low blow that still got cheers from the crowd. Wouldn't you know it? Scott Falk tried to run into the ring, but Ruben Ross casually showed him the other side of the ring. With no more distractions, Ross went to put this match in the books. He slowly tried to pull Coral back to his feet, but Coral wasn't being very cooperative about it. All the while, a single man had leapt out of the crowd on the referee's blind side and jumped into the ring. This man simply proved beyond a shadow of a doubt that the referee's "blind side" was fucking ridiculous, because this man stood at six feet and nine inches, and weighed at three hundred and eleven pounds. Though he had evidently been dressed in a business suit earlier, he had torn off the jacket he was wearing, revealing a blue tie. Ruben realized that unless there was an indoor eclipse, there should be no reason for the giant shadow behind him. So, he turned around. And ate some BLACK HOLE SLAM for his troubles. The ring shook from the impact, and the giant of a man quickly exited the ring to attend to Scott Falk, who laid out on the floor where Ruben Ross dumped him. Scott, however, had some words to say to him, "God fucking dammit, Robert. Why couldn't you fucking show up sooner?!" "Traffic." the man known as Robert Falk, Scott Falk's brother, said simply. As Robert helped Scott up to his feet, Coral Avalon warilly got to his feet and went to a nearby corner before hopping up to the top rope. And in honor of Eddie Guerrero, Coral flew off with the intent to flatten Ross even flatter than he already was. FROG SPLASH. The referee, who had turned around to find out the source of the impact he had heard, saw the frog splash and quickly slid into the ring to make the count. One. TWO. Youngblood attempted to interfere, but he was stopped by STEVE of all people. THREE. The Blue Rogues retained. The Codemaster ran away from Michelle Masters as soon as the bell rang, pulled Coral from the ring, and got the hell out of dodge. The other Rogues collected themselves and headed for the aisle, though Scott had thoughtfully collected the Rogues' tag titles on his way out. Michelle and Brandon entered the ring to check on Ruben, while staring holes through the still-champions. The newest addition of the Blue Rogues and their Entourage, Robert Falk, had secured the victory for the fWo World Tag Team Champions. But how long will their luck last?
