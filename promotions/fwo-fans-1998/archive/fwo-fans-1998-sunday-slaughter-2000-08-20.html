Sunday Slaughter, 20 August 2000

20 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Questions Sunday Night Slaughter was about to begin. Mike Bear was pacing in front of the entrance back and forth, waiting for the music and fireworks to go off before entering. He couldn't wait any longer. Just as the fireworks were about to explode, Bear called for his music to be played, and stormed into the arena. As the sounds of Rob Zombie's "Dragula" blared throughout the eight year old facility known as the America West Arena, Bear walked down the ramp briskly. His appearance brought a rise out of the fans, who loudly cheered at his arrival. Bear walked up the stairs into the ring, and as the fireworks now exploded on the stage, the fans didn't turn their attention to that. Instead, they paid close attention to Bear, who looked very upset about something. Bear asked for and received a microphone, before waiting for all the smoke to clear and the noise to die down. "I once knew this man... right... he was the embodiment of what all the boys in the back could be proud of. A man we all respected and admired. He was the leader of the boys... he had his detractors, but he paid no attention to them. We all knew that he was a natural leader. Earlier this year, he and I stood side by side and fought off the naysayers in that group known as the FTfWo. Well, the other night changed all that. I came to the realization... that none of us knew this man like we thought we did. What he did was not acceptable. It was heinous. It was... one of the most disgusting things I've ever seen. And thus far I haven't been able to get a straight answer out of him about why he did it. I'm not sure I want to know why; but I need to know why. I know he's in the back watching this, and right now, I'd like for the Boston Strangler to come out here and explain himself." A murmur began in the crowd as the haunting sounds of "O' Fortuna" started up. The former fWo World Champion walked out onto the ramp to an ovation of boos from the bothered Phoenix fans. Strangler methodically paced down the ramp and climbed into the ring, before approaching Mike Bear cautiously. Confusion had set in on the Strangler, and he had no idea what Bear wanted. As he stood around three or four feet away from the Strangler, Mike Bear looked at Strangler sincerely, and asked him one question. "Why?" The Boston Strangler looked out into the sea of fans, confused about what Bear wanted. Moments passed, and Strangler could not come up with an answer. Bear and all twenty thousand plus fans waited anxiously for an answer. Strangler looked around some more, and was in deep thought. Just when he was about to say something, Bear could no longer take it. He slapped The Strangler. A hush fell over the crowd as the Strangler' eyes rolled back. He then felt his face... and felt the mark had been made. Suddenly, The Strangler grabbed Bear by the arm he just slapped him with, and slapped on The Boston Massacre. He pulled and pulled back some more, and Bear writhed in pain. Intense angry screams bellowed out from Bear, before referees hit the ring and began to try and pull Strangler off of the leader of the Underground. Strangler stood up and pushed the referees aside, before exiting the ring in a masse of confusion. He looked back at Bear... and something wasn't right inside the mind of the former World Champion. He felt his face again... and knew nothing. A sharp pain began in the back of his mind, and as he staggered to the back in disbelief, Slaughter went to a commercial break. Greg Davis vs. Wolf Fang Greg Davis walks down the ring to a mixed response. His music, "Eye Of The Tiger" by Survivor, blares over the entire arena. He waves to the crowd heartily as he makes his way down, and slides into the ring. As he warms up for this match, Creed hits, and the crowd goes crazy for Wolf Fang, as he makes his way down toward ringside. He slaps the hands of fans and slides into the ring. He eyes up the newcomer Davis, whom he had faced at the Ryan Moore Memorial Show. The bell rings, and Davis goes to shake hands with Wolf Fang. Fang looks around, and goes to return the shake. When the hands come together, Davis pulls Fang closer, in a show of "homeboy love". He brings Fang's body to him; however, while he does this, he seems to slip a bit, and he raises his leg to catch himself. However, he seems to catch Wolf Fang in the ribs with his knee, knocking the wind out of him. Davis, seeing this, decides to capitalize on this misfortune. He picks up Wolf Fang and whips him into the ropes. He picks up Wolf Fang upon his return, and twists him around for a suplex. However, Fang uses this momentum to grab the neck of Davis, and he hits a mid-air DDT. Davis, stunned at this, is slow to his feet. Douglas, however, beats him to it, and picks him up. He puts Davis into a headlock, and brings him to the ropes. Davis whips him across the ring, and falls when he does so. Fang leaps over Davis as he crosses the ring again. However, Davis appears to be getting up, and he quickly lifts his head...right into the midsection of Wolf Fang. Wolf Fang falls instantly, and the fans boo Davis. Davis looks around, obviously not able to comprehend why the fans are booing him. He picks up Wolf Fang, and picks him up for a suplex. He lands the suplex, and goes for a cover. This garners a two-count before Wolf Fang kicks out. Davis immediately gets up, and runs toward the turnbuckle. He hops onto the second turnbuckle, and leaps off for a moonsault. Wolf Fang moves, however; but Davis anticipated this. He lands on his feet; when Wolf Fang turns around, Davis dropkicks him. Another cover, and another two count. This time, both men rush to their feet. Davis looks at the ref. Meanwhile, Wolf Fang comes up to him from the right. Davis points down where he had the pinning combination; as he does this, he pokes Wolf Fang in the eye, seemingly unintentionally. He looks at this, and then looks at the fans. They are booing him mercilessly, and he doesn't understand why. He walks toward Wolf Fang, who can't see clearly. Davis grabs the head of Wolf Fang, and uses the turnbuckle for momentum. He hops off, and nails a DDT off the turnbuckle. He goes for a cover, but again, only gets two. He looks at the ref. This time, he stomps at whines about not getting the three. He stomps three or four times. However, his foot continually lands on Wolf Fang's head. He looks down and mouths something, as if to say "My bad." However, that's all the sympathy he gives Wolf Fang, as he picks him up and sets him up on the second rope. He climbs to the top, and gets ready for a superplex off the top. However, Wolf Fang blocks him. He then counters by lifting Davis high into the air, and hitting an inverted suplex from the second rope. He goes for a three, but gets two and a half. Wolf Fang, looking all business, picks up Davis, and then drops him. Primetime has made his way out onto the entrance ramp. Wolf Fang seethes at his mere appearance; and momentarily forgets about Davis. Davis sees this, and grabs Wolf Fang. He locks him in quickly and nails the Davis DDT. He quickly locks the leg and gets the pin. Primetime begins to laugh as he walks backstage, and Davis begins to celebrate as if he had won the Super Bowl. Wolf Fang shakes his head as he comes to; while sitting on the mat in confusion. Davis gives a small salute to Wolf Fang as if to thank him for the match, before exiting the ring... Result: Davis by pinfall Resistance Slaughter headed backstage for the first time this evening, to find a horde of medical officials checking on the injured arm of Mike Bear. Bear was furious, and sat still as they looked him over. As they did, thoughts of why Strangler could do such a thing filled his head, and ultimately, he was just as confused as the Strangler appeared to be. He thought about the issue more and more, which enraged him. Bear has long been known to throw tantrums, and this one was no exception. He stood up and pushed people aside right and left, telling them to get out of his way. He had snapped just as the Strangler did... although he knew clearly what he was doing. He turned his attention to the cameras near by, and told them to go tell Strangler that later tonight, he'd be waiting in that ring for him; and regardless of what Strangler is going through now, he won't be confused by the ass kicking he's going to receive later tonight. Slaughter headed elsewhere backstage... Additions To The Roster Chris Finn and James Armstrong sat in their office backstage shootin' the bull, and talking about nothing in particular. That was, until Armstrong brought up last week and.. the Vegas incident. "How could I have known that guy was from NBC? At least we have enough money to make the fWo run until December." Finn shrugged. "Yeah, I guess you're right. We lose Warfare... but perhaps it's a good thing. Perhaps all our workers will be more motivated with only having to do one tv show per week. You never know." Armstrong nodded. "Hey, who got in from Tornado Wrestling?" Finn grabbed a nearby list, before reciting off the names of Greg Davis, The Red Dragon, Juno Law, Erik RAVE & Carnage. "Davis starts tonight, though. We were very impressed by him, and we came up with good ideas for his character. The ball is in hands now, what he with it rests on his shoulders." Finn looked around the room as Armstrong responded. "Nice, nice. Weren't Dragon & Juno Law in that Survivor contest we had?" Finn nodded. "Yep... I was very impressed with them. I was impressed with everyone in the Survivor contest, with the exception of a few... but other than that, I'll likely look into offering at least two more Survivor participants contracts in the near future. Or not... it just depends on how our roster looks in a month or two." Armstrong nodded, before suddenly, the team of Damnation barged in the door. Armstrong rolled his eyes, before nervously moving out of their way. Following in behind them was something on the floor, dressed in a sombrero, sunglasses, a red cape, and a big moustache. The individuals appearance likely gave it away who it was, but Fear & Hellrazor seemed oblivious to the true identity of their "new" friend. "What in the world..." asked Finn, as he stood up and looked down at the tag along. Hellrazor spoke first. "Hey, Finn... we were just wondering if we could get our new friend here a shot at the fWo Cruiserweight Title?" Fear added in that their new friend was well below the weight limit, and was very experienced with flying around and doing... "hurraycanarana's" and "tope don cilo whatever's". Finn shrugged. "What's his name?" Fear smiled proudly. "Senor Apoclypse the 3rd! He's great, you really ought to see him in action. Would it be okay if he faced Heftel tonight for the Cruiserweight Title?" Finn shaked his head negatively. "Sorry, guys... I'm putting Heftel in there with someone who thinks he's... well, all that and a bag of chips. Just to see if this guy is as good as he and his supporters claim." Hellrazor looked quizically. "You're putting him in there with me? But I'm not even in the Cruiserweight division..." Finn rolled his eyes. "Right, you're not even in Cruiserweight Division... and besides, you're facing Team Rateings and Chaos tonight and the winner will go onto Meltdown to face the Kings." Hellrazor looked somewhat disappointed, but then asked Fear if he was ready to 'stomp ass to hell and back'. Fear nodded, although he let Finn know he was disappointed that Finn wouldn't allow Senor Apocolypse the 3rd to face Heftel. "Well, maybe for Warfare... it is the final show." Hellrazor' disappointment changed to confusion. "Wait... no more Warfare? You've gotta be kidding me!" Finn nodded. "Not a joke. You can thank Armstrong." Armstrong quickly ran out of the room, with Damnation urging Senor Apocolypse the 3rd to 'get him some'. Slaughter headed back to the ring for another match. Crusader (c) vs. Jon Spratt Rage Against the Machine hits, bringing out "The Legend" Jon Spratt. Spratt walks down the ramp and slides into the ring. As he warms up, Biohazard hits the speakers, bringing out Crusader, the International Champion. He gives some fans a high five as he walks down the ring. He slides in, and immediately the two go at it. Spratt nails Crusader with a knee in the gut, followed by a quick gutwrench suplex. He picks up Crusader and tosses him into the ropes. Spratt follows him to the ropes, and, as soon as Crusader hits them, Spratt nails him, sending him over the ropes and spilling to the ground below. The Canadian-born Spratt follows the Canadian-born Crusader out to the outside. He picks up Crusader and whips him into the steel steps. Another pickup, and another whip, this time into the steel rail. Spratt charges Crusader; Crusader, seeing this, falls down out of desperation, and Spratt runs full-speed into the guardrail. Crusader limps up and climbs onto the apron, and then the ropes. He ascends to the top rope, where he eyes Spratt getting up. He lines him up, and flies off with a 450 splash. He flies on top of Spratt, who takes the major part of the fall. However, the toll has been taken on Crusader as well. The two lay seemingly unconscious outside of the ring while the ref inspects the carnage. He decides that a ten-count is in everyone's best interest, and begins the count to ten. However, a woman flashing the crowd stops his count at five. He forgets about the count, and stares at the woman. When the ref finally remembers what to do, Crusader has gotten to his feet, and then picks up Spratt. He tosses Spratt into the ring, and slides in. He picks him up and nails an inverted scoop piledriver. He goes for a cover, and gets two and a half. He stands up and picks up Spratt, before sending him to the ropes. Crusader gets ready to hit a hip toss on Spratt; however, Spratt sees this coming, and counters, grabbing the neck of Crusader and nailing a neckbreaker. He picks up Crusader and hits a shoulderbreaker, before going for a pin. He gets two and a quarter, and glares at the ref for a "slow count". He stands up and begins to pick up Crusader. However, Crusader manuevers himself, before using a modified leg-sweep to take the weight out from under Spratt. Crusader uses his quickness to grab the legs of Spratt. He quickly locks in a figure-four leglock, sending pain throughout the body of Spratt. The ref wants to see if Spratt will give up. Spratt promptly gives the ref a sign (hint: rhymes with "fiddle dinger")...he won't give up. Crusader applies the move tighter; however, it doesn't sway Spratt. Finally, Crusader gives it up, and picks up Spratt. He sends him to the ropes, and Spratt returns. Crusader follows with a hip toss, and then a dropkick as Spratt returned to his feet, and then another hip toss out from the corner. Crusader then went up to the top rope, and flew off with a high crossbody. Spratt caught him over, and then went for a fallaway slam. Somehow, the athletic & agile Crusader landed on his feet instead, and when Spratt turned around, he was taken down with a crucifix. Spratt kicked out at two, and both men rushed up to a vertical base. Spratt grabbed Crusader by the arm and sent him to the ropes, but Crusader reversed it. Crusader ducked for a backdrop, but Spratt went for a sunset flip. Crusader however dropped down, reached back and grabbed Spratt' legs. At two, Spratt escaped into a pinfall of his own, but Crusader kicked out. They rushed up again, and Spratt chopped Crusader across the chest, before sending him into the corner. Jon rushed in, but Crusader put his foot up. Spratt stopped and grabbed it, before bringing him out of the corner. Spratt then spun him around, pulled him in towards his body, and went for an atomic drop. Crusader slammed into the turnbuckles sternum first, and then Spratt ran and jumped up onto his shoulders for a victory roll. Crusader had the presence of mind to just fall backwards, and then slammed Spratt into the mat. Crusader then went to the ring apron groggily, climbed to the top rope, and hit the Gravity 101 for the pin & win. Crusader celebrated briefly with the title, before exiting the ring as Slaughter rolled on. Result: Crusader by pinfall Blackout Backstage, The Boston Strangler sat in his lockerroom, with his heads slumped in his hands. He was in deep thought.. and everything was so hazy as of late that he often couldn't remember stuff that happened ten minutes ago. He saw what Bear said earlier on... but had no idea what problem Bear had with him. As he sat in thought.. and pondered what was going on... Matt Loco entered the room. Strangler looked at Loco confused... before Loco informed the man who held the fWo World Title for a record tying eighty nine days that Mike Bear wanted him to be out in the ring later tonight. Strangler stood up, and thought about it. "Tell him... we'll be there. Although we don't know why he has a problem with us." Loco nodded, before Slaughter went back to the ring. Damnation vs. Rateings R' Us vs. Chaos Result: Damnation by pinfall Accusation Backstage, Matt Loco knocked on the door of a dressing room. He didn't get an answer, before finally, Crusader opened the door. Before Crusader could say hi, or ask Loco what he wanted, Loco quickly motioned for the camera to go on. "I'm standing by with one of my close, personal, long time friends Crusader, and Cru, I was just wondering your thoughts on new comer Greg Davis? You know, he beat you in Tornado Wrestling a few weeks back, but you eliminated him in the battle royal. Any thoughts?" Crusader gathered himself, before speaking. "Well, since you asked, before I state my opinion, I'm going to state a fact. Greg Davis is a heckuva competitor, and will fit in fine here in the fWo. But I saw what happened earlier tonight, and I'm not pleased. It is clearly obvious that Greg used the distraction of Primetime to win his match, and well, that's not the Greg I know." Loco looked somewhat bewildered. "The Greg you know?" Crusader nodded. "Yes, in Tornado Wrestling, Greg has always been a fan favorite, the crowd clearly loves him. But thus far - and I'm not saying he can't change, or perhaps I am mistaken - he's been the opposite. Tonight, in my opinion, he wouldn't have defeated Wolf Fang had Primetime not been involved somehow. Is that all you wanted to know?" Loco nodded, before thanking him for his time. Crusader shut his lockerroom door, and Slaughter went elsewhere... Ice (c) vs. Erik Kelly Cameras head back to the ring, where the referee looked around the America West Arena and it's sea of fans. He appeared in deep thought, but the screeching sounds of "Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit changed all that. "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly and his valet Pamela Dee walked out onto the ramp, and the popular duo received a round of applause from the fans. As they walked towards the ring, someone jumped over the guardrail. They had some form of gear covering their face, and appeared quite... tall. They moved Dee out of the way, before attacking Kelly, and assaulting him with lefts & rights. Kelly tried to fight back, but he was caught offguard. Dee watched on in horror, before several members of security rushed out and began to take away the unknown individual. It was rather difficult given his size, but they managed to calm him. Kelly was incensed, but checked Dee to see if she was safe. Slaughter went to a commercial with this bizarre segment fresh in our memory. Result: No Contest; Match Never Started Bedridden Sunday, 3:00pm Cameras cut in to the Ravenswood Medical Facility in Chicago, Illinois. Eddy Fate and Daniel Longshadow, the Black Panther, are nervously sitting in the waiting room, while Fallen is pacing back and forth, waiting to hear word on the condition of Ric Chronos. A short doctor with greasy black hair appears, and as soon as he appear, Fallen and Fate approach him. "Hello, I'm Dr. Rusux. You three are the ones here with Mr. Chronos?" Fallen quickly nods his head in an affirmitive. "Well, I've got some bad news, gentlemen. Mr. Chronos is suffering from a severe concussion, two cracked vertebrae, and a torn trapezius. He's in a lot of pain, so he's been fading in and out of consciousness for the past few days, while we were trying to get an accurate assessment of his injuries. He's going to be here for a few weeks, at least. He'll be experiencing some minor paralysis of his right arm from the damage, and it's going to take him a month or two to recover. Is there anyone we should contact?" Fate glanced at Fallen, before looking back at the doctor. "Not really," states Fate. "We're pretty much all that's left of his family. If you need any billing or insurance information, I've got that." The doctor looks at Fate raising a skeptical eyebrow. "Well, just make sure you give all that information to the head nurse, Pam Deblows. I'm going to call in a few specialists, and he should be ready to see visitors tomorrow morning. In the meantime, if you have any questions, just eave a message for me." The doctor walks off, and Fate turns back to Fallen. "So, John, what do we do now?" Fate queries. Fallen's eyes take on a sadistic glint. He gestures towards Panther, who stands up and walks over to the duo. "First, you stay here with Ric. As for me, I'm getting a ticket to Utah for Warfare. Primetime will pay." Fallen's voice sounds like ice, as the scene fades to black. Denial Cameras returned backstage, where Matt Loco continued his increased interviewing duties. He came upon another dressing room, and knocked. The door quickly opened, and there stood (one of) the fWo's newest acquistion, Greg Davis. Fresh off a controversial victory in his debut, he smiled cheesily at the camera, and waved at it somewhat, before Loco introduced himself. "Hello, Greg. I'm Matt Loco, and I'm here to interview you. I was curious if you saw Crusader' comments earlier, about how you possibly may have cheated to win in your debut match here?" Davis looked appaled at the accusation, but addressed it nonetheless. "I'm absolutely... appalled at such an accusation. Oh, and by the way, hello Matt. Nice to meet you. Anyways, yes, I did see Crusader' comments.. and I'm not pleased. Cru, obviously, I have to jump to the conclusion - and I hate doing that - that you're jealous. You're jealous because... we faced each other a few weeks back, and I came out on top. So, basically, you've gotta be jealous of the success a man like Greg Davis achieves. You know, I come in on my first night in the fWo, and beat a top talent like Wolfie Fangie, and you... have the nerve to accuse me of cheating? It was a clean victory, Cru... and I thought I knew you better than that. Obviously, I don't. I'm... I'm not happy, Matt. Could you excuse me?" Davis was obviously being sarcastic, but Loco nodded, and walked away as Slaughter rolled on. Mike Heftel (c) vs. "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson Things heated back up in the ring, as "Scatterbain" by Down With The Ship started up, and out walked everyone's favorite jobber, Mike Heftel. He walked out with the Jobberweight - er Cruiserweight Title around his waist, and rolled into the ring, awaiting his competition of the evening. "Last Cup Of Sorrow" by Faith No More bellowed over the arena sound system, and Heftel' jaw drop. He knew that his opponent of the evening was obviously someone he didn't want to face, and as the fans stood up, they saw the reigning fWo Internet Champion, Mitch Wilson. Wilson stood on the stage with a cocky smile, and Heftel quickly assumed he had no chance in hell of winning this one. Wilson had been on a roll as of late, and hadn't lost in singles competition since early July. Wilson entered the ring and handed his prized fWo Internet Title to the referee, before doing some quick exercises to prepare for the match. Heftel looked at Wilson confused, not knowing what Wilson was doing. Taking into consideration of the success Wilson had enjoyed since he returned earlier this year, Heftel began to do the same exercises. Wilson looked over and saw this as the referee signalled for the bell. Heftel started to do some jumping jacks, and Wilson walked over looking somewhat mad, somewhat amused. Heftel looked at Wilson and offered out a hand, before Wilson pulled him over and went for a Superstar Suplex. He hit it, but before he could bridge for the pin, "Paint It Black" hit, and out walked Scott Hartt & TMFAOYM. They charged down to ringside, and Scott Hart jumped onto the ring apron to distract the referee. Abusah entered, and as Wilson stood, he went for the SPEER. Instead, Wilson moved, and Abusah went flying into the referee, who knocked Hartt off the ring apron. Abusah laughed as it looked like two dominoes following over, but as he turned around Wilson grabbed him by the hair and sent him flying over the top rope. Wilson then turned and saw Heftel standing up groggily. Wilson came up from behind and grabbed him, before hooking him for the Headliner. Upon completion of the move, his first reaction was to cover Heftel, but saw the referee was essentially knocked out by Abusah' failed attempt at the spear. Suddenly, someone walked out onto the ramp with a referee's uniform on. Wilson assumed it was a referee, so he went back over and attempted the cover. The crowd popped, as the person tore off the uniform, to reveal a yellow & red "Wretch-A-Mania is Running Wild" tanktop. Obviously, it was The Wretchster himself, but the fans didn't know that until the referee's shirt was gone. Wretchster climbed onto the ring apron, and pointed at Wilson, who backpedaled. Wretchster shook his finger and head back and forth simultaneously, before entering. Wilson dropped to his knees and 'begged' for forgiveness, and Wretchster prepared to unload with punches. With Wretchster looking for the crowd's approval, Mitch took a cheap shot with a low blow, before grabbing him by his trunks and sending him crashing out onto the floor. With the actual referee now seemingly recovered, Wilson walked over and finally pinned Heftel, and the referee counted to three. Wilson held both his newly won fWo Cruiserweight Title and his fWo Internet Title high overhead, before walking up to the stage, where Ice was now waiting for him with a title of his own, the fWo Hardcore Title. The duo of Bad Attitude celebrated with all the gold they had accumulated recently, while Team Rateings gathered themselves in the ring, not looking pleased... Result: Wilson by pinfall Meltdown Announcements Slaughter went backstage for the final time, where Chris Finn stood with a paper in his hands. The camera man cued him, and Finn prepared to speak. "Meltdown is just six days away, and everyone already knows that the winner of the big battle royal will go on to win a full sweep of title shots that will occur over a one month duration of time. I'm here to announce that in addition to the main event, we're pleased to announce the signning of some matches. They are as follows. As stated earlier on Slaughter, the California Kings will meet Damnation in a ladder match for the fWo World Tag Team Championship. In addition, the fWo Hardcore Title will be defending by Ice against Erik Kelly, since that match did not go down earlier tonight. Also, it appears that Mitch Wilson will be pulling double duty as he will have to defend the fWo Internet Title & the fWo Cruiserweight Title in two separate matches. In the match for the fWo Internet Title, he will face Wolf Fang, who won himself a shot at that title before his injury in a match with Primetime. In the match for the Cruiserweight Title, he will defend against the winner of a four way dance between newcomers Greg Davis, Juno Law, The Red Dragon & the winner of "fWo Survivor", Ruben Ross. And I've been asked when teams for Meltdown will be announced, well the answer to that is tonight, I will go home and draw some random teams with the exception of The Boston Strangler who will be assigned a partner, and announce those random teams on Warfare. Good luck to everyone, and we hope you all tune into the final edition of Wednesday Warfare which will be live this Wednesday from the E Center in West Valley City, Utah." As Finn finished that sentence, Slaughter headed back to the ring for the assumed main event of the evening. The Boston Strangler vs. Mike Bear As Slaughter returns to the ring for the final match of the evening, the crowd murmurs in anticipation of what may very well be a war. The earlier actions of The Boston Strangler and Mike Bear have caused tempers to flare on both sides, and these two men will now attempt to settle it in the ring. Suddenly, the fans erupt on cue as "Dragula" blasts over the sound system, followed by the pyros and the appearance of one furious Mike Bear. He stalks to the ring, but it's obvious that he's not alone. The baseball bat that he carries over one shoulder makes his intentions pretty clear. Someone is going to get hurt tonight.
