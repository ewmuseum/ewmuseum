Monday Night Ruahh~! started with the challenger for the FWO World Title in the center of the ring, mid-sentence. "...since Cyberslam has led up to this point. Black Quicksilver had better enjoy these next few days as Champion, because they'll be his last." Death let the boos wash over him, completely in his element. And with the belt around my waist for the sixth time, I can assure you we'll see some changes around here. Death was cut off, however, by "Juke Joint Jezebel". His arrogant smirk disappeared into the scowl of a man who really, strongly dislikes being interrupted. The Flying Frenchie bounded down the entrance ramp, all smiles. He slid into the ring, and started talking into his own microphone. "Scott, I'd say it's good to see you, but I'm not zat good a liar. "You see, ze World Title is a great accomplishment. No joke, no punch line, it's ze trut'. But as valuable and highly honored as zat title is, zere can be no greater accomplishment zan winning ze Wrestler of ze Year tournament. Only four men have held zis accolade." Frenchie balled up his right fist, lifting a finger as he listed the previous Wrestlers of the Year. "Black Pant'er. Ric Chronos. Ruben Ross. Jon Crisp. Four World Champions. It's as good a collection of talent as any you'd find. Zis year, zey'll add one more, and all due respect to Ric, ze best of zem. Moi." Death held up his hand to shut up Pierre. Death grinned, glad at having annoyed Frenchie, but the grin disappeared, as this little party had one last guest. Black Quicksilver, FWO World Champion. "Frenchie, I wouldn't get so upset. The way I see it, I've had some trouble getting matches these days. You just got yourself a title shot." Death looked apoplectic in the center of the ring. Brand Frontier, Eli Flair, Deacon, and the Flying Frenchie, all four men ready to make their mark and take their place next to the others who had achieved the greatness that was the Wrester of the Year, collectively looked up the ramp to the fWo World Champion. Pierre even broke out his trademark smile as he did his best to look steely and resolved even though he knew his record against the champ was less than impressive. Black Quicksilver smiled and waved "hello" dismissively, holding the camera as Ruahh~! took its first commercial break. Issues Jim Dudley casually thumbed through the stack of papers on his desk, trying to busy himself until the man he'd sent for arrived. The two hadn't really communicated much since Dudley took over the lead position in the fWo, and up until now that had suited both parties fine. Jim sighed to himself, knowing that this meeting wasn't going to go very well at all before it even started. Few meetings ever went well when the party involved "You wanted to see me?" asked Kodiak Vic Creed as he strolled into Dudley's office and plopped himself into one of the chairs in front of the desk. Dudley fought to suppress his growing irritation at the big Alaskan. "Nice to see you still like to make yourself at home wherever you go." "Yeah," Vic said and leaned back. "I'm actually glad you sent for me. I wanted to talk to you about something." The list of issues a man like Creed would want to discuss skimmed through Dudley's mind. "Oh? Well let's get your business taken care of first. Maybe we've got some common ground to work from." Vic sat up and leaned forward, resting his huge arms on Dudley's desk. The veins bulged out from the biceps pouring out from his t-shirt sleeves, almost resembling bluish purple cables. "There's actually two things I wanted to talk about. The first has to do with my boy Alex. What's the deal with him not getting any bookings?" Dudley leaned back in his chair, putting some space between himself and those veins. "He doesn't have suitable medical clearance, and the clause in his contract prohibits him from wrestling until he gets it." "That's bullshit." Vic's arm twitched. "He's fine. The kid's gone out of his way to get some extra training under his belt with Lindsay Troy. You've got money there. Since when does the fWo not want to make money?" An amused look crossed Dudley's face. "It's so strange, hearing you speaking so highly of someone other than yourself." Vic ignored the jibe and continued to stare at the fWo commissioner. "Look Vic, I know you helped set up your son's contract. But after your own contract fiasco, I would have thought you'd have learned your lesson." Two direct hits, and Creed still didn't seem to be biting. "He's only pay per appearance. If he doesn't show up, he doesn't get paid. Until I see some hard documentation clearing him to wrestle, Alex won't be appearing in an fWo ring in the foreseeable future. It's the best thing for all parties involved." "Again, bullshit." "Get me paperwork. If he doesn't have medical clearance, he doesn't wrestle." Dudley shuffled the stack of papers on his desk and set them to the side. He leaned forward, feeling more confident in his position, and closed the gap between himself and the Main Man. For a brief second, he saw something in Vic's eyes disbelief, discomfort before the hard stare returned. "Now what else did you want to discuss?" "Legends. Why don't I have a match yet?" "As you know Vic, a Pay Per View broadcast has a set time limit that we can squeeze matches into. I wanted to make sure our marquee talent gets airtime before I fill the other match slots," Dudley added with great satisfaction. Kodiak Vic Creed was a headache for anyone in a position of authority, and it felt good to be able to put him in his place. Creed, of course, was obstinate. "I'm Kodiak Vic Creed, Jim. I am marquee talent." "You're still a draw Vic, but let's look at the facts. What have you done lately?" "Oh, you mean other than butt heads with the Soldiers of the Fifth Seal?" Creed snapped sarcastically. Dudley sighed. "Yes, other than that. Vic, you should know better than anyone that it's a struggle to hold a top spot in the fWo. Last time I checked, you weren't even listed in the fWo's top ten. You haven't been for months." The commish watched his words hit the Main Man harder than any fist could. Creed's retort came with a mutter. "I've been busy." Dudley thought he caught a hint of resentment in his tone. Vic sat up. "So the Fifth Seal boys are getting airtime ahead of me, is that what I'm hearing?" Something clicked inside Dudley's head and he tried not to smile at the thought of killing two birds with one stone. "You know their situation. My hands are tied," he said with a serious expression on his face. Creed frowned and stood up. "Well mine aren't. Give me one of them." He paused. "Give me Gianni." "If that's what you want," Dudley commented with a touch of indecision. Vic shot the commissioner a dirty look. "What's that supposed to mean?" "You don't exactly have the best track record against him, and he's already pinned you in the Wrestler of the Year tournament." Abusing Creed's ego like this was almost therapeutic. Dudley watched Creed's anger brewing. "I think you're forgetting who you're talking to. I think it's time to remind the fWo who the Main Man is around here." Dudley didn't say a word. He just held his serious expression and watched the Juggernaut storm out of his office. As soon as the door closed, he breathed a relieved sigh and thought about the match that had just been signed. He had no love lost for either party involved, and already knew it was going to be a very physical confrontation. "Good, they deserve each other." He reached for his papers and suddenly realized that he never got a chance to discuss what he wanted to see Creed about. Jim just shrugged. Things had worked out better than he'd expected. 'Radio Friendly Unit Shifter' Nirvana As soon as the charging guitar riff began, the crowd moved their hands to cup their mouths to amplify the venom they had for the man walking out from the entrance curtain wearing a brown leather jacket, faded blue jeans, silver sunglasses and no shoes... ...and naturally, a Newport dangling precariously through his lip.
