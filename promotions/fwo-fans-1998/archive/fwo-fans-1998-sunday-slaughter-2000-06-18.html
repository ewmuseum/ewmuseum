fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 18 June 2000

18 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction fWo Sunday Night Slaughter exploded inside the Saginaw Civic Center, where the fans anxiously awaited what was expected to be a tremendous night of action. The Sunday Night Slaughter theme blasted throughout the arena, while fireworks shot off in various directions, while the fans rapidly cheered the arrival of one of the top programs on television despite the dropoff in ratings as of late. As the Sunday Night Slaughter theme continued, the fireworks and eye candy filled show stopped, and the show began. On the SlaughterScreen above the SlaughterStage, a video began to play. The next level. The barrage of text bursts onto the screen as the fans begin to cheer loudly. In order, fifteen of the sixteen participants began walking to the ring. First, former fWo Internet, Hardcore & Tag Team Champion Mitch Wilson, followed by Mike Heftel. Behind Heftel came Wretch, a former Cruiserweight, International & Tag Team Champion. Shortly behind Wretch came Havok, a former Cruiserweight Champion. Ric Chronos followed Havok, carrying his fWo World Tag Team Title with him. Walking a few steps behind him was The Black Panther, who had accomplished a lot in the fWo. Many questioned why he was in the tournament, being that he was a former fWo World Champion. It could be explained that since the Panther never really dedicated himself to the fWo after his world title reign, that this would be his first chance to prove he belonged at the next level. Behind the Panther came a man who stood six feet eleven inches, and had already unleashed fury & vengeance upon the fWo itself like no newcomer had done before: "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly. Although a newcomer to the fWo, this man proved he had the skills and desire to take his game to the next level. He had already put Hiroshima on the retired list, and his match with Rain left Rain packing. His first round opponent did not appear, since his surprise was being a well kept secret within the fWo. Oddly, Neo was already at ringside, he just refused to join his colleagues in the ring, instead joining in with Jeremiah Frankton & Stevie Roberts for commentary, and offering his opinions of his "competition", Xavier. Xavier came out to a nice little reaction with the fWo Hardcore Title, and walking a few steps behind him was a sure crowd favorite, perhaps one of the most popular entrants in the Next Level tournament, Crusader. The fWo Cruiserweight Title strapped tightly around his waist, this young newcomer took to the ring with determination, and although he had befallen some bad luck as of late, he looked focused and ready to show he had all the ingredients necessary to raise his game up a notch and compete with the main eventers of the fWo. Behind Crusader came Hellrazor, who carried with him Lamont, the hardcore feline. The fans roared in approval, chanting the name of Lamont, which echoed off the arena walls. As Hellrazor joined the other competitors, out came "The Future" Lance Star, and he was booed loudly. Star was joined by his partner in the Future Foundation, Courage, who carried the fWo International Title, and confidently grinned at the crowd, as well as the other participants in the ring. "Dragula" began to play and the fans cheered loudly, as out came a former Hardcore, International & Tag Team Champion came out in the form of Mike Bear. His tattooed figure appeared ready that this was his opportunity to reach the highest pinnacle of his career in the fWo, a shot at the fWo World Title. Bear rolled into the ring and stared at his competitors, before the lights went out. Suddenly, some crappy pop music began to play, and anyone could ascertain it was the band known as Third Eye Blind. The lights came back on, and on the ramp stood Machina. Machina received a thunderous welcome of applause as he charged the ring, doing two or three cartwheels down the ramp. The crowd looked at him dumbfounded, before cheering loudly as he reached the ring and raised his arms. His music stopped, and finally, "Epic" by Faith No More played. Out came the owner of the fans Wrestling organization, Chris Finn. Finn, who would be facing Eron The Relentless later in the ngiht, came out to a mild reaction from the crowd. Finn joined the wrestlers in the ring, and procured a microphone from his jacket pocket, before beginning to speak. "Ladies and gentlemen, these are the combatants whom have been selected to take their game... to the next level. One of these fifteen participants, as well as the mystery entrant, will compete in a ten day tournament to be held beginning on next Sunday, at Sunday Night Slaughter, and culminating with the semi-finals and finals of the tournament on Wednesday Warfare on July 5th, live from the Allstate Arena, which was once proudly known as the Rosemont Horizon, before some corporate bastards decided to change it. The winner of this tournament will receive one prize, and one prize only, and that is a shot at the fWo World Title sometime during the month of July, most likely at the pay-per-view spectacular known only as SummerSlaughter, which will emanate from the sold out Alamodome in San Antonio, Texas. This is your chance, gentlemen, to seize the day, and take advantage of the opportunity that is being given to you. Last year's SummerSlaughter featured an epic match-up for control of the fWo, or retirement, between Austin Davis, now known as Dead, and Machina. Machina came out on top in the end, just as the present fWo World Champion did at Redemption, The Boston Strangler. This year's SummerSlaughter will be no different. The stakes are very high, and I wish you all the best of luck, as the fWo approaches the next level next Sunday!" The fans roared in approval as fireworks once again shot off near the stage, before Chris Finn made an exit backstage. Neo, meanwhile, was the first to follow Finn... presumably to head outside and wait for his fight. Primetime vs. Matt Douglas Sunday Slaughter returns from commercials to an empty ring, as the sounds of Creed's "Higher" blast throughout the arena. The song brings out the young superstar known as Matt Douglas. Douglas, a hard-working individual, jumps into the ring, and prepares to face his arch-rival of nearly 3 years now, that man being the current fWo Internet Champion, Primetime in a non-title match. Suddenly, the lights dim, and "Blind" by Korn plays. As frontman Jonathan Davis screams "Are you ready?!", the fWo Internet Champion Primetime steps out to a chorus of boos from the crowd. PT nails a few punches to the ribs, and breaks up the DDT motion. He gets behind Douglas and nails him with a wicked German suplex that drops him right on his head. PT covers, but Douglas once again gets a shoulder up at two. Primetime lifts Douglas up and looks for the Primetime Bomb. He lifts Douglas up, but once again, Douglas fights out of it. He gets out of it, and ends up behind Primetime. He then gets a quick victory roll, but that is reversed into a Primetime victory roll. Douglas is able to shoot Primetime out at two, and into the ropes. Primetime bounces back, and Douglas lifts him up. PT gets out of that, and nails Douglas with a reverse DDT. Cover by Primetime, and Douglas is out at two once again. Primetime looking astonished here at the tenacity that Douglas is displaying, perches him on the middle turnbuckle. He goes for a superplex, but Douglas pushes him off the turnbuckles, and onto the mat. Then, Douglas climbs to the top turnbuckle, and flies off with the Impact. He hits, and immediately jumps on Primetime with the cover. 1....2...and no! Primetime kicks out of the finisher of Matt Douglas, the Impact. Now, this time it's Douglas with the astonished look on his face. Douglas brings PT up to a vertical base, and hits him with some stiff kicks to the side, and ribs. A quick whip into the ropes, and Douglas hits PT with a back elbow. This brings the Internet Champion down to the ground, and Douglas quickly climbs to the top ropes. He looks to come off with a guillotine legdrop, but Primetime is able to roll out of the way. PT gets up, and nails Douglas with a blow where he picked him up, and draped his throat over the top rope. With Douglas' head still draped over the ropes, PT climbs the top, and nails a wicked gullotine legdrop onto Douglas' head. PT falls to the outside, and Douglas' head snaps back violently, and then falls back into the ring. PT jumps back onto the apron, and climbs the turnbuckles once more. This time, he comes off with a big splash, but somehow, someway, Douglas gets out of the way. Douglas jumps on PT's back, and tries to reverse it into a huracanrana. However, PT holds on to Douglas, and has him in prime position for the Primetime Bomb. However, before PT can drop Douglas down to the mat, Douglas shimmies out. Quickly, he catches PT offguard with a small package. One, two, three, and Matt Douglas picks up a huge win over the current fWo Internet Champion. Douglas scurries up the ramp, and Slaughter goes to commercial to the picture of a furious Primetime, stunned in the middle of the ring. "Epic", by Faith No More, the Wednesday Warfare theme plays and the valiant if slightly crazy owner of the fWo, Chris Finn makes his way to the ring to cheers, clad in a white fWo logo t-shirt and jeans. Finn rolls into the ring and awaits his opponent, Eron The Relentless. "Physical" starts to play and the fans react with boos as he makes his appearance. Eron makes his way to the ring and rolls in, and the bell rings. Eron gestures to Finn, saying that Finn can have the first shot. Finn eyes Eron warily, before walking up to him, winding up, and unleashing the fury of a devastating right fist. Eron yawns in response, and Finn takes a few steps back before charging at Eron with another punch. Eron grabs his hand mid-punch and twists, bringing the owner of the fWo to his knees. He then lashes out with a kick to the face, knocking Finn down. Eron brings Finn to his feet and whips him into the ropes, before catching him coming off with an elbow shot to the face. Valuing his life, the ref does so. Three seconds later, Chris Finn has defeated Eron.
