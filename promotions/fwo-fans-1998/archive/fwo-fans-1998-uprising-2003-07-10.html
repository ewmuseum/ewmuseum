Introduction Some days feel like an eternity... for the fWo these four days will be some of the longest of their lives... Epidemic III. fWo Uprising did not open with a token fireworks display. Instead a single word written out in plain white text sat ominously on the pitch black video wall. Disease. Slowly but surely the letters bled into the next word. AIDS. The word remained only for a few seconds before fading away to be replaced by the suited individuals standing next to one another in a line, each sneezing one at a time until the third did so and dropped dead. And then the deep voice that wouldn't have been out of place in a movie trailor spoke up. "Imagine a world where the common cold can kill you and you have an insight as to what AIDS can do. Thought to have spread from monkeys to humans between 1926 and 1946 although recent research indicates that it could have spread from chimpanzees to humans as early as 1675. AIDS breaks down the immune system and as yet there is no cure, it killed 3 million people last year and the numbers continue to grow. From this we learn that diseases can develop in any time or place under any circumstance leaving nothing immune. Not even the fans Wrestling organization. At Meltdown, a new disease develops and the epidemic begins." The footage faded to black and the fWo returned to regular programming. Temerario vs. Rat Lights pumped in the arena and "Man Without Fear" began to blare over the speakers. Temerario pushed the curtains aside and tossed his t-shirt to the crowd as he stood at the top of the entrance ramp with sparks exploding all around him. Inside the ring was Rat, one half of the fWo tag team The Animals, with the lovely and always present Kat on the outside. One week ago Temerario had beaten Dog, the other half of the Animals, and tonight he was slated to see if he could continue the sweep against the other member of the team. Rat was running back and forth against the ropes, warming up and sparing lusty glanced down at his scantily clad manager with the fuzzy cat ears and tail. Temerario came to the ring slowly, pacing himself and staring up at Rat from the outside. Temerario circled the ring, then rolled inside as the bell sounded. Wasting no time, he began at once to trade quick chops in the center of the ring with Rat. In little time Temerario got the upper hand, but Rat reversed a clothesline and tossing Temerario spinning into the corner post. Both men rolled quickly to the outside, with Rat staying on the offensive as he proceeded to drive Temerario into the corner steps. Temerario clutched at his back, surprised by the quick attack from the small tag team member. Rat slammed Temerario's head into the announcers table, and then hard into the metal ring divider gate before rolling a slumped masked Temerario back into the ring. Rat continued to use quick body shots to keep Temerario on the defensive. As Rat charged Temerario, the high flying masked man ducked down and Rat tumbled wildly to the outside. Temerario slid out once more, and followed him out into the crowd. Fans clapped them on the back as they continued their battle, and one grabbed at Temerario, excited to touch an fWo superstar. This was all Rat needed, as he regained the advantage and once again slammed the distracted head of Temerario into the guardrail. Back inside the ring, Rat continued to attack with high punches with an occasional assist by Kat, who clutched at Temerario's foot as he fell into the ropes. Rat missed with a flying lariat as the masked man ducked, and Temerario took control with a quick belly-to-back suplex. Rat bounded to his feet, but Temerario blocked a punch and used a snap samoan drop to drive Rat down the mat. As Temerario looked for a pinfall, the other half of the Animals, Dog, ran to the ring. Grabbing Temerario's boot as the referee was down for the three count, Dog shoved Temerario off his partner. Temerario stood in the ring, glancing down at Dog on the outside with anger. Pointing at him, Temerario began to make fun of Dog's stomach, then kicked Rat in the groin as the referee was distracted in his attempts to get Dog to return to the back. Temerario slowly climbed to the top rope, then dove off with a flying corkscrew moonsault that hit directly on target. The referee dove for the count. 1... 2... 3! The bell rang, and Temerario bounded to his feet and climbed the corner turnbuckle, one fist in the air. The show was just beginning, but Temerario smiled. The night, for him, was far from over...
