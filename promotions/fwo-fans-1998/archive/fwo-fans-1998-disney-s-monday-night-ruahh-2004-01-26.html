But tonight wasn't about Jack Lundin or the new face that's gotten in Dynamo's business, Zero. Tonight was about stopping the title bid of the Blackest Brother in the Hyrule Kingdom by any means necessary. Tonight was about putting one's foot down and proving just why they're the Hardcore champion. And hey, if it meant drinking the Haterade and knocking the black man on his ass to get things done, then so be it. Dynamo wore the Hardcore title around his waist as he stomped up the steel ring steps and stepped through the ropes. Upon entering the ring, Dynamo tore the Hardcore title from his waist and held it high up, for all to see. That belt was what it was all about tonight, and the Codemaster saw that. The referee was handed the belt, and he raised it up for all to see. He handed the title belt off to the timekeeper and called for the bell. This match was on, bitch. Dynamo had a very definite size advantage over the Codemaster. He was six feet and five inches and was nearly two hundred and eighty pounds. Whereas the man occasionally known as the Real Superstar of Mario & Luigi's Saga gave up seven inches and seventy pounds to the three-time Hardcore champion. Dynamo was also far more used to the fWo's Hardcore enviroment than the Codemaster was, and was also a proven champion. However, what the Codemaster lacked in size, he made up for with a huge amount of speed. His abilities with high flying, while not totally proven, were more than a match for Dynamo's power advantage. On top of that, the Codemaster had a noticable expierence advantage over Dynamo, with at least a year and a half over the Hardcore champion. Oh yeah, and did I mention that the Codemaster was generally more prone to cheap, dirty tactics than Dynamo was? Yeah, I thought so. Anyway, the two men slowly circled one another, neither wanting to just jump right into this. Dynamo had no real idea what the Codemaster was all about, while the Codemaster, being a smooth operator, would rather take his time and pick his shots than actually leap blindly into the arms of a man who was significantly larger than he was. But, essentially, that was what the Codemaster ended up doing as he entered the collar-and-elbow tieup. Dynamo simply shoved him backwards, landing the Codemaster back-first to the canvas. The Codemaster rolled backwards until he was to one knee, and looked up at Dynamo. He knew that Dynamo was powerful, but probably didn't have too much of a conception of it up until now. Neverthless, the Golden God of Golden Sun got back to his feet and went for another collar-and-elbow tieup. Only this time, the Codemaster was smart and instead of actually locking up, he ducked Dynamo's grab, did a go-behind, and did a double leg trip. The Codemaster soon floated over and hooked the Dynamo in a front facelock, trying to get an early advantage going. However, Dynamo soon powered his way to his feet, lifted the Codemaster off of the ground, and tossed him into the corner. The Codemaster impacted the turnbuckles with his back, but he quickly moved out of the way before Dynamo could crush him in the corner. The Codemaster managed to kick Dynamo in the gut, before he unleashed an open-handed chop. CHOP! "WHOO!" And a second one. CHOP! "WHOO!" The Codemaster pulled Dynamo to the center of the ring and managed a fireman's carry takeover, before he latched on a top wristlock on the fWo Hardcore Champion. He was trying to weaken the tremendous power that Dynamo possessed. However, Dynamo soon maneuvered himself to his feet and twisted himself around, so that the Codemaster only had an armbar applied and they were face-to-face. Then the Dynamo used his free arm to grab a handful of the Codemaster's tights and power him off of the canvas. Dynamo set the Codemaster into a seated position on the top rope, forcing the referee to make the Codemaster break the hold. So, of course, as soon as the Codemaster broke the hold, Dynamo reared back and slammed his fist into the Codemaster's face, knocking him from the top rope, down onto the apron, and down to the ringside floor. The fans liked that and cheered Dynamo heavilly as he looked around, either looking at the fans for reacting for him, or maybe keeping an eye out for Zero. Neverthless, the fWo Hardcore Champion soon stepped between the top and middle ropes and walked out on the apron. When the Codemaster managed to get to his feet, but he, himself, was still hunched over in the attempt to steady himself, Dynamo leapt off of the apron and slammed his elbow down on the back of the Codemaster's head. This, of course, sent the King of Kirby pratfalling back to the floor. Dynamo shook his arm a bit to regain a little feeling in it, before he grabbed the Codemaster by his head and pulled him to his feet. Dynamo grabbed him by the wrist and whipped him towards the steel steps, making sure to pull the force downward. THUD! Thus, making sure that the Codemaster slammed into the steel steps back first. The Codemaster laid on the arena floor, his face a mask of anguish, and his body writhing in pain. The Hardcore champion casually walked over and pulled the Codemaster back to his feet, before rolling him back into the ring. Dynamo soon rolled under the bottom rope and re-entered the ring. He pulled the Codemaster back up and hit the backbreaker, before he quickly made the first cover of the contest. One. Two. Shoulder. But the Codemaster, the wily (like Dr. Wily, baby) veteran, had managed to get his shoulder up off of the canvas to stop the three count. Neverthless, Dynamo pulled the Codemaster to his feet and went to the Irish Whip, sending the Codemaster into the ropes. Dynamo went for a clothesline, but the Codemaster ducked it. Then he went for the back body drop, but he put his head down too soon, allowing for the Codemaster to kick him in the face. Dynamo stood up straight and held his jaw for a moment, before the Codemaster simply shrugged and kicked Dynamo right in the nuts. Predictably, Dynamo doubled over and collapsed to the canvas, while a good 95% of the fans booed. The Codemaster non-chalantly shrugged and held out his arms, as if to say, 'Hey, anything goes'. The Codemaster went to one of the corners and picked up one of the weapons he had brought with him, which happened to be the Koopa Shell. The Codemaster set the shell in the center of the ring and pulled Dynamo to his feet again. Another open-handed chop hit with a loud smack, which was followed up with a "WHOO!". Then the Codemaster shoved Dynamo into the corner and stomped away at Dynamo's midsection with a Steve Austin-like series of stomps, until Dynamo was half-sitting, half-laying against the corner. That's when the Codemaster moved back over to where the Koopa Shell was, before he picked it up. Smiling an ear-to-ear smile, the Codemaster did a hop forward before he under-handedly pitched the somewhat heavy Koopa Shell at Dynamo. The male members of the crowd gasped and groaned in sympathy pain when the Koopa Shell hit Dynamo right in the nuts, the second nutshot that the fWo Hardcore Champion has suffered in just the last minute. The Codemaster, himself, mimicked the reaction a man would have if they got hit in the nuts, only he was doing it mockingly, and he was getting showered with more boos(and a few small cheers) because of it. Neverthless, the Codemaster saw that Dynamo had rolled out of the ring, and was only somewhat vertical because he was holding on to the apron. Naturally, the Codemaster's initial reaction could have been to baseball slide(or, in his terms, Charge Kick) him. But the Codemaster knew that this wouldn't be enough... at least until he noticed that the Koopa Shell was positioned right in front of where Dynamo's head was. Grinning, the Codemaster waited for Dynamo's head to poke above the apron, before he ran towards the Shell... and Dynamo. THUD! The Codemaster's Charge Kick had shoved the Koopa Shell violently into the face of Dynamo, causing him to tumble backwards onto his back. The Koopa Shell was deposited at ringside, but the Codemaster didn't need it any more. Instead, he simply pulled himself out to the floor, grabbed Dynamo by his hair, and tossed him right back into the ring. The Codemaster leapt onto the apron before he slingshot himself up and over the top rope, and down across Dynamo's neck with a catapult legdrop. Quickly, the Codemaster spun over into a cover on Dynamo. One. Two. THR-Shoulder. The Codemaster wasn't frustrated or anything. He simply pulled himself to his feet, and then walked over to another corner before he picked up his second weapon, the Casey Bat. That would be the baseball bat with "CASEY" written on it. The Codemaster took a test swing with the weapon, then looked at it with a grin on his face. He waited for Dynamo to get to his feet, before he took a swing for Dynamo's head. The only problem was that Dynamo leaned back far enough to avoid the wild swing. The Codemaster had absolutely no reaction time to evade Dynamo's kick in the gut, which caused him to drop his baseball bat. Dynamo quickly picked up the bat, and proceeded to blast the Codemaster in the gut with it. The Codemaster held his stomach in pain, but he got the surprise of his life when Dynamo whirled the bat around and caught the hunched-over Codemaster in the face with a second shot. THUD! The Codemaster flew off of his feet and landed on his back, the blow having busted open the Blackest Brother in the Hyrule Kingdom. The fans gasped in shock, and then cheered the Dynamo, who dropped the Casey Bat onto the canvas and spat on it in disgust. Dynamo quickly went into the lateral press, hoping that the explosive shot with the Casey Bat would be enough to put away the Breaker of Kingdom Hearts. One. TWO. THRE-KICKOUT! Dynamo was bewildered as to how the Codemaster managed to kick out of that, but he didn't pay it any mind, and instead, pulled the Codemaster up to his feet again. Dynamo started to hook the Codemaster up for a powerbomb, but when he lifted the Silencer of Shy Guys up onto his shoulders, that's when he fought back, raining down fits on Dynamo's forehead, trying desperately to NOT get powerbombed. Finally, the Codemaster managed to slip through Dynamo's arms and land on his feet in front of Dynamo. Sure, he was dizzy from the skull-crunching shot with the Casey Bat, but he was still fighting back. The Codemaster finally spun around, looking for possibly the Triforce Clothesline. The Dynamo stood opposite Zero, backstage at the event. The sweat still coated his body from the match earlier with Codemaster. Zero shifted in his stance, not removing his eyes from Dynamo. Zero had cut him off backstage for a purpose. Violence was no intention at this moment. He only carried a message, and he wanted to be sure Dynamo knew the extent of it. "What do I want from you?" Zero paused. "Is it not obvious?" The Dynamo clutched at his title. "You can't have this." His glare cut through Zero. The Dynamo was a man bound to the object he carried. Zero cocked his head, then lowered his gaze to the title in Dynamo's arms. "And I don't want you to give it to me." The Dynamo took a step forward, oblivious to the possibility of another encounter with Zero right at that moment. "Then what do you want?" His voice hissed at the end of his words. Zero stepped back to return to the space he and the Dynamo shared. "Just a chance." From his shirt pocket, he retrieved a single cigarette. "Just give me a shot at that title this Friday at Bodycount." He clicked a lighter and lit his cigarette. Dynamo slung his arms to his side, the title dangling from his right hand. The words incited him. "Since when do you deserve a shot?" The brow on his head tightened. Zero blew out a patch of smoke and raised his cigarette hand. "How about..." His eyes narrowed. "...Ever since I decided that I am gone from your life after it ends." The Dynamo took another step toward Zero. "After Bodycount, you will be gone?" Zero met his step forward with a step of his own. "Sure," he said darkly, as smoke wiggled through his teeth. The Dynamo inhaled and exhaled with a visible force. "How can I trust you?" He maintained his ground with Zero. "I don't expect you to," replied Zero. "But I will give you my word, regardless." Zero opened his eyes, allowing the Dynamo to look deep within his soul. A moment passed before a word was spoken between the two. Finally, after a bout of internal warfare, Dynamo broke the silence. "So Friday is your last day with the company?" Zero blew a patch of smoke through his nose. "It will be. Provided that..." He inhaled from his cigarette. The Dynamo turned his head slightly and cocked an eyebrow. "Provided by what?" Zero blew the smoke into the Dynamo's face. "...That you win." The smoke in his face incited the Dynamo, but he did not react. He stood his ground and was determined to be the better man in this situation. "I will." "You had better hope so," said Zero. "Because a champion can't simply give up his title right after he wins it. You understand that." "I do. And I'm not going to worry." The Dynamo stepped into Zero's personal space and stood nose to nose with him. In a low voice, he added, "After Friday, I'll walk out still as the Hardcore champion, and I will be the man who has finally retired you. No more Zero. It's back to wherever for you." Zero stepped back and thumped his cigarette against a far wall. "In time, I am positive of that. But will it be after Friday?" He turned to walk away from the Dynamo. "We'll have to wait and see," he said over his head. "You better be ready," hollered the Dynamo. As he was rounding a corner in the hall, Zero stopped and looked back at the Dynamo. "And I hope the same for you."
