Speed Is Critical

fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE SPEED IS CRITICAL July 25, 2000 With the third show of fWo Survivor just around the corner, Stevie Roberts was on hand to explain to reporters what the tone of the third show would be like, and what the immunity challenge would look like. "Basically the job in round three is to get the job done as quickly as possible. Match times are going to play a critical role in determining who gets immunity. The wrestler to defeat his opponent in the shortest amount of time will gain immunity and have a free ride for the next round of voting." Immunity will become more and more important as the weeks go on. Certainly wrestlers who were voted for during the second round of voting will be doing their best to obtain immunity. Nick Allstar, T-Bone and Reno Davis all will be looking to pick up their win and hopefully secure their place in the fWo roster... at least for a few more days. This will not be an easy task. T-Bone faces Reno Davis in the first match of the night. Both want to get the quick win and set the pace for the rest of the competitors. Nick Allstar takes on The Don, Arch Angel takes on the Red Dragon, Kodiak Vic Creed takes on Ruben Ross and Suicide takes on the last immunity winner, Ronnie Randall. Most wrestlers will be looking to put the immunity to greater use than Randall did during round two. Randall never voted in the second round of the fWo Survivor contest, making his immunity winning go at least partly to waste. This mistake isn't likely to be made a second time by the wrestlers, as they will move into round three with two of their number already voted out. Of the two, Mike Extreme has been enjoying moderate success in the IWO and in the talk show circuit discussing his time in Survivor and how he felt he was viewed as the biggest threat by other members. Recent cast away Juno Law has a successful Tornado Wrestling career, and based on his impressive interview skills in the Survivor contest is likely to be offered a contract in other promotions soon if not already. Controversy surrounding the Survivor contest continues to swell, with several wrestling organizations now getting involved in the protesting of the contest. [name withheld], owner of XIW [Xtreme Internet Wrestling] made several comments on public message boards regarding the cruel nature of the Survivor contest, and noting that neither himself nor any member of his organization was chosen for the contest, thus proving the bias of the owners. Battle Wrestling commissioner [name withheld] sent several angry letters to fWo offices demanding a spot on the Survivor roster or a lawsuit would be filed. When asked for comment, fWo Survivor coordinator Stevie Roberts had this to say: "Screw off." More news as it comes available.
