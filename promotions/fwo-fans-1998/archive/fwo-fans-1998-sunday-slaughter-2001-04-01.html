Sunday Slaughter, 1 April 2001

1 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The Boston Strangler arrived at the Phillips Arena with nothing in particular on his mind... he wasn't scheduled for a match, nor any type of segment, and nobody was on his hit list at the moment. For the Strangler, he was simply fulfilling a contractual obligation. Strangler, who as was his custom arrived at the arena in his ring attire, opened a door from the back hallway into the main backstage area. Various crew and a few wrestlers were backstage. The Strangler scanned them all uncaringly they could drop dead, quite frankly, and he wouldn't care less. One of them in particular caught his eye the Destroyer. Who knew why he was there maybe he thought the fWo would book a WrestleMania-style gimmick battle royal and he would be lucky enough to be involved in it. Whatever his reasoning, the Strangler didn't particularly care. He had been feeling that a lot lately, but that was beside the point. The reason the Destroyer caught his eye was the large scar around his right eye. The Strangler flashed back to just over two months ago Body Count. Frankton: Somebody in a mask and some type of jersey is dragging her out of the arena! Brittany struggling... and she scratches her attacker's eye! Armstrong: Ouch. It all clicked home for the Strangler Destroyer had kidnapped Brittany at Body Count. Destroyer had kidnapped Brittany at Body Count? True, it was a bizarre conclusion, but the Strangler knew it was true. He didn't know why, or particularly care... that he would leave to Bear. In the Destroyer, the Strangler saw a piece of his very sanity conclusive proof that he had not been involved in Brittany's kidnapping. The Strangler stepped forward, snarling silently. "Hey, Strangler. Long time no see. What's u " The Strangler cut the Destroyer off by grabbing him around the throat and lifting him off the floor. "You... are coming with me." The Strangler shrugged Destroyer over his shoulder and set off in the direction of the Underground locker room. Sphere vs. Rottdogg "Pressure Zone" by Beck hits the speakers, bringing out Rottdogg to a general yawn from the crowd. He throws his arms up in satisfaction, and makes his way down the ramp. Meanwhile, Sphere runs in from the crowd, and slides in to the ring with chair in hand. Rottdogg doesn't see him, and continues to celebrate his presence in the ring. Sphere lines him up, and brings the chair down towards Rottdogg's skull. However, Rottdogg, who is against the ropes, notices that his shoelaces to his boots are untied. He bends down to tie them, and the chair hits the ropes, only to bounce off them and nail Sphere in the face. Sphere falls backwards, the chair flying out of his hands. Rottdogg looks behind him, and looks at the ref. "Is this a hardcore match?" Rottdogg asks. The ref shakes his head, and Rottdogg sighs. "Those were the days," he says to himself. He gets up, forgetting to tie the other shoe, and walks towards Sphere. He picks up Sphere, and whips him into the ropes. Sphere, however, reverses it, and sends Rottdogg flying into the ropes. Rottdogg bounces off the ropes, and trips on his shoelaces. He falls first-face on the mat, and Sphere moves in for a quick ankle lock submission move. Rottdogg winces in pain, but he won't give. Sphere applies it tighter, and Rottdogg yells out. Finally, Sphere lets go, and picks up Rottdogg. Sphere gets such momentum from picking up Rottdogg, that he actually picks him up from a sitting position, and nails a suplex on him. Sphere gets up and grabs the legs of Rottdogg, trying to part him like a wishbone. Rottdogg tries to fight it, and finally, he gets a leg free. He begins to kick at the air, and Sphere stops, trying to figure out what is going on. He finally decides to give up on whatever mind tricks Rottdogg is trying to implement, and takes off for the second rope. Then he realizes what he's doing, and gets back down. He walks over to Rottdogg, and picks him up. Then......he headbutts him. Rottdogg shakes out the cobwebs, and headbutts Sphere back. Sphere, obviously surprised by this, headbutts Rottdogg harder. So Rottdogg takes his knee, and plants it perfectly in the crouch of Sphere. Sphere doesn't seem affected by this, and instead, takes HIS knee and plants it in Rottdogg's crotch. Rottdogg, who doesn't seem affected by this, headbutts Sphere. Sphere, to counter this, FAKES the headbutt, and gives him a knee in the crotch. This sets Rottdogg off, as it appears that the war of concentration. Rottdogg rolls around the floor, and Sphere decides that enough is enough. He picks up Rottdogg, and lines him up for the Spinal Retraction. He picks him up, and lands the vertical DDT. He covers Rottdogg, and three seconds later, Sphere picks up the victory. He celebrates in the ring for a moment, before someone interrupts him. Jackhammer makes his way into the ring via the crowd, and attacks him with some barbed wire. Jackhammer nails repeated lefts and rights with the barbed wire, which cuts Sphere wide open. Jackhammer picks Sphere up, and drops him over the top rope, and down to the mat below. Then, he begins to celebrate with the wire in his hands, as Slaughter moves on... Result: Sphere by pinfall Intentions Nothing seemed the same with Crusader as he entered the arena through the back door. His head was tilted low and the normal air of confidence that he used to have was now gone. As he lugged his bag over his shoulder and walked through the hallway, Kristen Jacobs who was waiting around for someone to interview quickly jogged to catch up to him. In a matter of seconds she was behind him. "Crusader!" she called out. "Crusader... I'd really like to get a few words with you." He paused, but continued his walking again. Right now wasn't the best time for an interview, but this was something that an amateur reporter such as Kristen couldn't understand. In Crusader's mind, *nobody* could understand him at this point in time. Kristen followed him, disregarding the fact that Crusader was indirectly being annoyed. "I just want a few words, Crusader. Just a few words..." He turned to open the door to his locker room and immediately, Kristen knew what was coming next. He was going to slam the door in her face and yet another chance for a good interview would slip out of her hands. But the door didn't close before her. Instead, Crusader left it open as he walked in and set his bag over on a coffee table. He took a seat on the couch, and leaning with his arms resting over his knees, he looked up at Kristen. She figured that this meant that he'd spare a few words for her. Upon nearing Crusader, Kristen unfolded a metal chair and took a seat, before calling in the cameraman. "Crusader, I along with many fans suspicions that it was you that sent Clarisse those dead flowers last week. Is this true?" She realized that the question seemed rhetoric. He answered her with a simple nod of the head. "Okay then," she said as she twirled the microphone around for a second to come up with another topic to speak about. "That brings us to tonight. According to the program sheet, you're currently NOT booked in a match. What are your plans for this evening?" Crusader lifted his bag upside down and out spilled a vast array of cutting weapons and utensils. Kristen was taken aback from the sight and suddenly felt uneasiness in her stomach as Crusader stared at her. Not wanting to continue the interview, Kristen ran out of the room. Aimee was in a leather recliner, while Juno sat to her right in a loveseat while Kobi was to her left, sitting on his friend the steel chair. "So," she said to Juno, "you're an April Fool's joke master?" "Oh, you better believe it. Of course, they're usually on Kobes here, right bro?" Kobi grunted. "Don't worry, you'll get me one of these days," Juno said with a grin. He then turned back to Aimee, who was smiling interestedly. "You should've seen the one I did last year... all I can legally say on the air is that it involved glue, chili powder, and a shitload of cockroaches. He was totaled." "Totally?" asked Aimee sweetly. "Totally," agreed Juno. "I once played an April Fool's joke on Juno," butted in Kobi. "Is that a fact," asked Aimee, clearly not interested. "Yeah, it is," continued Kobi. "He was sitting on a " He was unceremoniously interrupted by John Willis poking his head through their locker room door. "Kobi, your match is next." "Great," replied Kobi, his tone conveying the exact opposite emotion. "Hey, why are you telling me anyway? You're security, right?" "Well... yeah." "So, go secure. Or whatever the hell it is you guys do." Willis hung his head, then turned to go. Kobi turned back to Juno and Aimee. He had just burn~!ed Willis, and he was sure they'd congratulate him for his top-notch diss. To Kobi's dissatisfaction, they seemed far too interested in their conversation to care. "Alright guys, I'm going," he said, hoping for a reaction. "Good for you," said Juno, paying less than no attention to him. Kobi snorted in disgust and turned to go. Black Panther vs. Kobi Law With "Du Hast" playing, a 6'10," 375 lbs monster steps into view of the fans. Hidden behind his mask, Daniel Longshadow currently has one thing on his mind - victory. He grows impatient waiting for his opponent and warms himself up, staring down the entrance ramp. When "Push Upstairs" by the Underworld hits, Black Panther knows the time is growing near. In a few short minutes Kobi Fojune Law will be in the ring. Then Black Panther will be able to destroy the Japan native and take one more step toward the tag titles that he so richly deserves. Kobi, surprisingly, is making his way down to the ring without the company of his brother but with his title belt around his waist. He hops up on to the mat, tosses the belt to a ring attendant, and the match is about to start. With the ring of the bell, Panther wastes little time attacking his opponent, hammering him with those huge fists of his. Kobi puts his arms over his face to protect himself, but that does little good and Panther starts to lambaste Kobi's kidneys with vicious shots. Crawling back, Kobi hangs on to the ropes and the ref steps between the two. Hoots from the crowd, calling Kobi a coward, turn him red, and he goes after Panther with a vengeance. But his vengeance is nothing compared to Panther's yearning for the tag titles, and Panther's forearm sends Kobi tumbling to the mat, once again. His eyes narrowed and red with intensity, Kobi picks himself up. Realizing he has no chance to get in a battle of strength with the powerful Panther, Kobi decides to take advantage of something Panther's lacking-speed. He bounces off the ropes and ducks under Panther's lethal arm, hitting the ropes before the Native American can even turn around. By the time Panther does turn around, he's found himself on his back. With Kobi laying on top of him, going for a cover, Panther kicks out, or more appropriately, throws Kobi clear off of himself. Fans cheer, because they've grown more and more disgusted with the Laws with each passing week. Kobi gets back up seconds before Panther, but they're seconds he uses to his advantage. By the time Panther reaches his feet, a dropkick sends him flailing back against the ropes. A spinning heel kick brings the groggy Panther down to the mat. Kobi climbs the top rope and nails Panther with a Senton bomb. He follows the move with a cover, hooking his opponent's leg. The ref gets to two, before Panther finds a way to power out again. Not nearly as strong, Panther regains his footing with the intent of dismantling Kobi. Kobi has the same plan in mind for the Nevada native, and tries to hit him with a monstrous DDT. Panther has other plans, however, and he holds Kobi Law in the air, with his arm around Panther's head. Kobi tries his best to squirm out of Panther's strong grip, but it's to no avail. Panther doesn't let go until he sees fit, and then Kobi goes crashing to the mat. Panther doesn't waste any time before continuing his attack. He hits Kobi with a double underhook suplex that fans erupt upon seeing the impact of Kobi's body meeting the mat. Panther follows it up soon after with a fisherman's suplex and covers Kobi before he can reach his feet. The ref gets to two and his arms on the way to three when Kobi raises his left shoulder, saving the match, for the time being. Kobi makes his way to his feet where Panther meets him and puts the smaller man under his arm. He runs toward the corner... bulldog. A face print can be seen of Kobi, and some fans are even forced to cringe at the move. Most, though, cheer, thinking there's no way one-half of the tag team champions will kick out of that move. Even the ref doesn't think so, as he goes down for the count. The shock on his face is evident, when Kobi manages to kick out at two-and-a-half. Surprising most of the arena more, Kobi shows his great resiliency in getting back to his feet. Panther hits him with a couple of punches, before stepping back to get a good run at Kobi. Kobi ducks what he feels is an impending clothesline...only Panther doesn't try to hit him with a clothesline. Instead, Panther throws his entire body on top of Kobi. Making his way back to his feet slowly, Panther goes for a move that will keep down Kobi forever - The Killer Instinct. There's no stopping the three count, and Rammstein plays, signifying another victory for the Millennium Men over the East Coast Laws. Panther smiles, knowing he's just earned a tag title shot next week, and he exits the ring and heads to the back. As he does so, he passes Juno Law (carrying a microphone) and Aimee Horihito, heading to the ring. Panther shoots them a menacing look, but they ignore him and keep on walking. Finally they reach the ring and roll in, and Juno helps Kobi to his feet. Kobi shakes his head, dazed after his beating. "Whoa... bro... that sucked," he said. Juno nodded, chuckled, and spoke. "Yeah, that did, bro. Unfortunately, I've got more sucky news... brace yourself, bro. This is Bad." Kobi's eyes went wide in anticipation of some ghastly horrible news. Juno spoke, enunciating each word clearly. "They... canceled... Big Wolf on Campus!" Kobi's eyes bulged. His favorite television show, the wacky, aimed-at-kids yet filled with semi-sophisticated humor and buried on Fox Family 30-minute sitcom... canceled? "NOOOOOO! Oh, merciful fate, why!" Juno stared blank-faced at his brother for a few moments, then began to break into chuckles. "April Fools, bro!" Juno and Aimee began to laugh loudly, and Kobi began to steam. "Very... funny." "Oh, c'mon, bro... I only embarrassed you on national television by outing the fact that you like a children's TV show. No big deal, right?" "Right," Kobi said, emotionless. As Juno and Aimee continued to laugh over their April Fool's joke, Slaughter moved backstage... He then plants him with a back suplex that jars Ross' spine as he slams into the mat. Wilson then heads to the corner and leaps onto the second turnbuckle. He measures Ross and leaps off, driving the point of his elbow into the throat of Ross. Wilson makes the cover, but at two, Ross catches him off his guard and rolls him into a small package. Wilson barely manages to kick out at two, but is quickly back on his feet. He waits for Ross to get up then charges at him with a kneelift, but Ross side-steps and Wilson continues into the ropes. As Wilson comes off, Ross leaps high into the air, wraps his legs around Wilson's head, and takes him over with a hurricanrana. Wilson is quickly back up to his feet but staggered. Ross charges in his direction, but Wilson catches him and throws him to the mat with a spinebuster variation. Wilson then lifts Ross up from behind and cradles him with one arm while using the other to pull the tights. The ref counts one... two... but at the last second, Ross kicks out. Wilson is stunned, but being a consummate professional, he doesn't allow it to rattle him and he brings Ross back up again. He goes behind Ross and sets him up for another back suplex. Ross, however, flips over the shoulder of Wilson and lands behind him. He hooks Wilson up, then drops him with a picture-perfect Paint It Black. The ref makes the count - one... two... but Wilson manages to get his shoulder up at two and nine-tenths. Ross brings Wilson back up once more and lifts him into position for the Plague of Darkness. Wilson, being a technical master, shifts his weight, hooks Ross' head, and rolls backwards from the suplex position into a small package. The ref counts, but Ross gets a shoulder up right before the three-count. Both men quickly get back up, and Wilson charges and goes for a clothesline. Ross ducks, then makes Wilson eat leather with a beautiful superkick. Ross then heads to the outside and climbs up to the top rope. He flies off, and nails a picture-perfect Blackout. Ross makes the cover, but Wilson just manages to get a shoulder up. Ross has Wilson teetering on the edge of an upset, and he can sense it. Ross brings Wilson up and hooks him up. He then plants Wilson with a Spin Cycle. Practically tasting the gold, Ross reaches out and hooks a leg. The ref counts one... two... and somehow Wilson kicks out. Ross brings Wilson back up again, but Wilson reaches up and blasts him with a low blow. Ross stumbles off, and Wilson spins him around and hooks him up for the Headliner. Ross fights out with a series of elbows to the head, and Wilson staggers away. Ross charges at him, and Wilson counters by backdropping him over the top rope to the floor. Wilson spots a mammoth shape making his way to the ring and immediately engages the referee in an animated discussion, likely about how much the Texas Rangers will suck this year. Erik Kelly reaches ringside, then levels Ruben Ross with a charging big boot. Kelly brings Ross up and tosses him back into the ring, and Mitch Wilson brings him to his feet. In one fluid motion, Wilson hooks Ross up and drops him with the Headliner. Wilson covers, and three seconds later, scores the victory. Outside the ring, Erik Kelly is stalking towards his title belt, but from the back the Underground floods ringside. Mike Bear, Wretch, and Damnation begin smacking down Kelly, and Bear and Wretch branch off into the ring to help Ross up and fight off Wilson. Wilson sees the trio coming, shoots them an up yours sign, and bails through the ropes. Wilson grabs his title and heads to the back, meeting up halfway with Greg Davis, who has made his way out from backstage. Wilson raises the belt high in the air, making sure Bear and Wretch know that he's still the champ, and Slaughter goes off the air with the image of both Mike Bear and Wretch leaning over the top rope, staring out at Mitch Wilson and the fWo World Heavyweight Championship belt...
