Sunday Slaughter, 4 February 2001

4 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The door to the locker room swung open, and in the doorway stood none other than Mike Bear, leader of the Underground and reigning fWo World Heavyweight Champion. Bear stepped into the room and was followed by Wretch and the rest of the Underground, entering in order fWo Internet Champion Ruben Ross, fWo World Tag Team Champions Damnation, Lamont, and Eddie. Bear took a seat on a large leather recliner, while Wretch walked over to a stereo system and inserted a CD. As "She Bangs" played, Damnation headed to the small refrigerator in the corner of the room. Hellrazor pulled the door open, and gasped. Inside was a large bucket filled with ice and nothing else. Fear clutched his heart as if wounded and stuttered, "No... H-H-Heines?" "Noooooo!", shouted Hellrazor. Ross in the background chuckled. "It's no big deal, guys. Isn't that what Eddie's here for?" Bear added, "He's got a point, you know." Hellrazor turned to the ex-cabbie. "Eddie, I'm sending you on a very important mission. Your mission, should you choose to accept it " Fear chimed in. "That means 'whether you like it or not'." "- Is to bring to us, at any cost, a six-pack of Heinekens. The fate of the entire Underground depends on you, Eddie! Run! Run like the wind!" Eddie nodded and sprinted out of the room. Wretch spoke up in the background. "Guys, have I asked Eddie yet if he thinks Ricky Martin is bad *ass*?" As the Underground pondered this question, Slaughter headed to the ring for the first match of the night... Black Quicksilver vs. Erik Kelly "Slave to the Grind" hits the PA system, as Black Quicksilver comes to the ring with STUMP in hand. The crowd cheers, as BQ raises STUMP in the air, and then places him in the corner. The cheers quiet, and then trademark "POWERBOMB!!" roars over the speaker system, and unlike prior months where the fans would cheer the oncoming wrestler, they instead horribly boo him. And like always "Break Stuff" follows the roar as Erik Kelly makes his way to the arena lead by Matt Loco. With each step, Loco fills the powerful Kelly with words of wisdom for the match, and the big man acknowledges them all before he steps onto the apron. Kelly then steps over the top rope and stares Black Quicksilver in the eyes, which causes the former cruiserweight champion to gulp and glance a nervous look towards STUMP for some guidance. STUMP takes the opening to say "...", but the knowledge doesn't help much as Kelly simply levels BQ with a vicious clothesline. The bell rings as the match officially starts. Kelly lifts Black Quicksilver back to his feet and shoots him towards the rope with an irish whip. As BQ returns he is launched high in the air with a back body drop; his actions show a failed attempt at a sunset flip as he all he touches is air before his back slams violently against the mat. BQ's back arches on impact and he grabs it while giving a grimace that's visible under his mask. Erik Kelly eyes BQ as he brings him back to his feet, and just as quickly he throws him out to ringside, and slowly he follows eyeing BQ just like your fat Uncle Larry eyes the last piece of turkey on Thanksgiving. He jumps down to the ringside padding, grabs BQ, and lifts him up by the back of his shirt. Kelly then sends Black Quicksilver violently into the steel railing. Due to circumstance, Quicksilver's legs are trapped between the rails, and his arms are folded behind the top bar suspending him off the ground. Slowly Kelly walks up, absorbing the orders yelled by Matt Loco. Kelly then proceeds to nail BQ with knife edge chops to the chest, each one causing Quicksilver to wince. Kelly then reaches and rips the sleeveless t-shirt off Black Quicksilver. Kelly reaches back again and nails him with five more chops, reddening the chest of BQ. Finally Erik Kelly grabs onto BQ and simply shoves him down from his position in the railing. As a reaction, BQ shoves off the padded flooring trying to get to his feet but he drops back down nearly banging his head on the steel steps. Kelly stays in control, as he pulls BQ up by his mask and he stands Black Quicksilver up, and then lifts him into a two handed choke, and the big man doesn't even feel the effects of the rampant kicks of BQ which all nail him in the midsection. Kelly then simply drops Black Quicksilver, causing the STUMP Squad member to plummet and bounce off of the protective mats. Kelly then rolls Black Quicksilver into the ring, and he follows by entering the ring over the top rope. Black Quicksilver forces his way back to his feet and stares towards Erik Kelly with glazed eyes. Valiantly, he bounces into the ropes and charges forward narrowly missing a clothesline attempt by Kelly. BQ charges back forward and jumps for a bulldog. He hooks onto Kelly's head, but the big man doesn't budge as he breaks it and then nails him with a sidewalk slam. Kelly stands up and looks down upon Black Quicksilver, but his attention pans away from BQ as he sees a table slide into the ring, and he hears the words of Loco ringing loud and clear. Kelly sets up the table, and pulls Black Quicksilver to his feet only to level him with a right that causes the STUMP Squad member to slump back down on the mat. Erik Kelly positions the table in front of the corner, grabs onto BQ and lifts him up, and follows it up by ascending to the top rope. Kelly lifts BQ up and nails the Eradication from the top rope through the table. The ref just looks down with his jaw dropped. Kelly shoves away the shards of broken table and thrusts his foot on the chest of BQ. The intimidated ref counts three giving the win to Erik Kelly. He gets ahold of Kelly's hand to raise it, but Erik Kelly just shrugs it away as he exits the ring with "Break Stuff" playing loudly throughout the arena. As Kelly walks to the back with Matt Loco, Sphere exits the crowd and then enters the ring. His arrival is answered by hellacious boos from the crowd. Sphere starts to lay the boots to BQ. Sphere then grabs onto Black Quicksilver and starts to go for the Vertical DDT. The crowd starts to cheer as from behind Mike Heftel enters the ring holding STUMP. Heftel then uses him on the back of Sphere's head. Sphere drops BQ down to the mat, and starts to flee as Heftel chases him out of the ring. Heftel then lifts BQ up and helps him to the back as the crowd cheers the two. Result: Erik Kelly by pinfall Out For Blood Carnage stepped out of the bathroom stall and walked towards the sinks. After all, despite his violent tendencies, he still had enough dignity to wash his hands. He looked down at the knobs and turned on the hot water. He then looked up. MUD stood in the mirror behind him, eyes completely blank. Without hesitation, MUD screamed and rushed toward Carnage, who was caught off guard and had no time to pull his knife. MUD grabbed Carnage's head and slammed it forward into the mirror, shattering the glass and slicing open Carnage's forehead. Carnage fell to the tiled bathroom floor, and his blood ran off his forehead in a stream that flowed across the tiles. MUD began raining down fists on his face, beating Carnage in a mad rage. Seconds later, the door flew open and fWo security flooded the bathroom. They pulled MUD off of Carnage, and he lashed out violently at them. MUD briefly broke free of the guards and pounced on Carnage again. The guards pulled him off as he shouted in animal rage at Carnage. The guards dragged MUD out of the room, and one shouted for medical help for Carnage as Slaughter went to commercial... Wretch vs. Kodiak Vic Creed The crowd rushes into a roar of cheers as the sounds of Pearl Jam and Neil Young takes to the speakers, bringing out the young and charismatic Underground member, Wretch. The much-adored ratings saver excitedly makes his way to the ring, before entering and striking a pose for the crowd. His moment of pre-match glory is cut-short, however, as "Walk" by Pantera shifts the reaction of the crowd to a chorus of boos for the infamous Kodiak Vic Creed. KVC nonchalantly makes his way to the ring, taking his sweet time flexing his triceps for the broads at ringside. KVC climbs up the steps and onto the apron, before flexing one last sweet time. This however proves to be a mistake, as Wretch pinpoints a dropkick to the back of KVC and sends him sternum-first onto the steel guardrail. KVC turns around, not so much hurt from the move, but rather annoyed. KVC challenges Wretch to come at him, and the relentless youngster flies out with a Pescado, but the large Vic Creed catches him and drives him spine-first into the steel ring post. Wretch holds his back in pain and tries to find a support to help him get up, but KVC cuts him short with a clubbing forearm shot to the back, before rolling him back into the ring. Creed cracks his knuckles and steps into the ring, ready to inflict heavy punishment on Wretch. Vic stomps away at Wretch a few times, before bringing him up and putting him back down with a short arm clothesline that leaves Wretch gasping for air. Still showing fight, Wretch gets up and charges at KVC, but KVC blocks him off by ringing his neck. Wretch finds a way out by sticking his thumbs between the space of his neck and Creed's hands, and then proceeds to take a bite out of his 'bird' finger. KVC releases the hold to tend to his fingers, but Wretch takes another smart shot by stomping Creed's foot. Holding his foot now in pain and annoyance, Creed stumbles about the ring, and Wretch bounces himself off the ropes, coming back with a dropkick to the back of the knees. Wretch makes a cover, the referee gets to two, but KVC shows his awesome power by practically bench-pressing the cruiserweight off of him. As Creed gets up, Wretch kicks him twice in the mid-section, and then locks him in a headlock. Wretch bounces off the turnbuckles with his feet and attempts a tornado bulldog, but Creed simply shrugs himself out of the move, causing Wretch to hit the mat hard. KVC approaches Wretch and sends him into the corner, before following in with a monstrous splash. Creed hooks Wretch up in the corner and delivers a huge T-bone suplex, before taking a few moments to make comments about Wretch's manhood. KVC stops his talking and got down for the cover, but Wretch shows his toughness by kicking out. KVC stands ready to deal more offense, as he brings Wretch up and tosses him into the ropes. Wretch returns and gets sent up high and back down to the mat with a back body drop. Wretch cringes and holds his lower back in pain, but still insists on getting up as soon as he can. Wretch crawls a couple feet towards Vic Creed and reaches up to deliver a few shots to his mid-section, but the Alaskan Juggernaut merely bellows a laugh, before bringing his large forearm across the head of Wretch. Wretch goes down, but KVC brings him back up by the back of his Underground T-shirt, and sets him up for a suplex. KVC stalls for a few seconds to show off... and finally brings Wretch to the mat with a thunderous stalled suplex. Vic Creed lies back atop of Wretch with a half-hearted cover, and Wretch manages to get a shoulder up at the ref's count of two. KVC brings Wretch back up and tosses him into the corner, before following in with some hard kicks to the mid-section. After lacing in a few hard chops, KVC slaps on a blatant chokehold. Wretch desperately gasps for oxygen, but the muscular monster releases at the ref's count of four, and then steps back a bit, only to charge in with a clothesline. Wretch luckily evades it, and traps Creed in the corner with lightning-fast stomps to the mid-section. Wretch fires him up with a few chops, before mounting atop and raining down the fists. The fans count along to ten, and Wretch dismounts. KVC stumbles forward, and Wretch tries picking him up for a slam, but the bigger Creed simply pushes him off. KVC tries for an elbow drop, but Wretch moves out of the way and mounts atop Creed with hard right hands to the side of the head. Creed pushes his opponent off of him, and charges at Wretch, but Wretch takes him down with a drop-toe-hold. KVC gets to his feet again, but Wretch has him scouted with a well-placed spinning heel kick. Wretch goes to the top rope, and flies off with a diving head butt that finds its mark. Wretch hooks the leg and covers with all of his might, but Creed gets out at two. Wretch knows he has to take chances to win this match, and so he heads to the top rope again... but Creed doesn't let this chance prove worth it, as he trips him and powerslams him from the top rope. Wanting to end this, Kodiak sets Wretch up between his legs and lifts him up for a Creed Bomb. Wretch however wriggles free and counters the move into a face-jam on KVC. KVC manages to get to his knees after the move, but Wretch gets right behind him and drops him to the mat with the Depression, before making the cover and just barely securing the three-count on Creed. KVC takes a moment to recover from the move, as Wretch runs out of the ring and heads up to the ramp, soaking in the cheers from the crowd to the sounds of his music once again. Result: Wretch by pinfall Punishment fWo cameras went to Chris Universal's office, which was done up as tastefully as ever. The fWo owner sat behind his desk in a large leather swivel chair. Universal sported a Versace suit, Oakley shades, and a million-dollar no pun intended smile. "Ladies and gentlemen, it appears that the fWo has a situation. Or, more accurately, two situations. Number one, last week Black Quicksilver become the number one contender to the fWo Cruiserweight Title on the same show, he signed a contract to face Sphere at In Misery. It is my decision that these two matches will be merged at In Misery, Greg Davis or whoever is champion will defend against both Black Quicksilver and Sphere in an elimination triangle match!" Universal paused a moment, cleared his throat, and continued. "Furthermore, after I specifically warned that there would be punishment for interference in the fWo Internet Title match last week, Havok still felt it necessary to interfere. Obviously, something must be done. Havok! Panther! Come in!" The door to Universal's office opened, and Havok stepped in. The Black Panther then stepped in, a good distance behind him. Both men took chairs on opposite sides of the front of Universal's desk, and traded hard stares. Universal addressed them. "Havok, I have no choice but to punish you for your actions last week." In his chair, the Black Panther chuckled. To his surprise, Universal wheeled on him as well. "And you, Panther. Why are you so smug? You've been running around, screwing with people's matches, interfering as you see fit I'm not going to stand for that. As far as I'm concerned, you both deserve punishment for your recent actions. Thus, I propose this the reunion of the Millennium Men. You two must team together, at In Misery, against... Damnation!" The crowd cheered loudly at this. "Good luck, guys. Who knows, maybe you'll get along well enough to win the tag titles. That'd be fine with me. But I seriously doubt that'll happen. More than likely, you'll both get your asses kicked and that's fine with me too." "I can't believe you're doing this to me!" shouted Panther. Havok chimed in. "Yeah. I don't want to team with that bastard." "Too bad, boys. You made your beds, now you have to lie in them. And oh yeah, I forgot to tell you you've got a match together tonight, too. Against the East Coast Laws. And oh yeah... that's happening... next, if you buy what the Sellout is selling!" Havok got up and stomped out of the room, and Panther followed, also clearly angry. Universal leaned back in his chair and turned toward his monitor to watch the proceedings... East Coast Laws vs. Millennium Men Puff Daddy hits the speakers, and Juno and Kobi Law, the East Coast Laws, come out to ringside. They received a mixed reaction on their way down to ringside. They slide into the ring and begin warming up. Then, "Du Hast" hits the speakers, and the mixed reaction immediately turns to hate, as The Black Panther, now FORMER Internet Champion, makes his way down towards the ring. He climbs over the ropes, and walks into the ring. Juno Law stares at him as he walks by. Panther takes one of his massive hands, palms Juno's head, and pushes him backward, into Kobi. The two stare at him with narrowed eyes as Godsmack hits the speakers, bringing Havok to the entrance ramp. The fans cheer hard for him, and he replies in kind, although he does seem a bit peeved that he's out here for this. He walks down towards the ramp. Meanwhile, TBP begins to fight with Juno Law, causing the match to start. Havok, rather pissed that he didn't start the match, hustles a bit more to the ring. TBP manhandles the smaller Law, until he whips him into the ropes. Law tags in his brother without TBP noticing, and then slides underneath TBP. TBP turns around, and Juno leaps up towards TBP's neck. He grabs his head, and TBP tries to fight him off. However, Kobi rolls the legs out from underneath the Panther, and he falls right into an X-factor. A cover from Juno generates a two before TBP kicks out. Havok, meanwhile, is looking elsewhere. Kobi picks up TBP; TBP then picks up Kobi, and tosses him aside. He then walks over to his corner, and slaps Havok in the head, which, technically, tags him in. Havok looks at TBP with a menacing look. Meanwhile, TBP drops down on the mat, and walks over to the announcers table, taking their pitcher of water. Havok, miffed at this, doesn't see Kobi leaping towards him. Kobi greets him with a dropkick, and Havok hits the mat. Kobi picks him up and tags in Juno, who climbs to the top rope. Kobi holds onto Havok, and Juno gets ready for a missile dropkick. He takes off; only problem is, Havok is gone. He dropped, and rolled out of the way. Juno nails Kobi, and Kobi hits the deck. Havok looks to his corner, but TBP is still getting water. He turns around, and Juno nails him with a spinning heel kick. Juno goes for a quick cover, and gets two, before Havok kicks out. Juno picks up Havok, and nails him with a hurricanrana. Juno quickly takes off for his corner, and tags in Kobi. Juno leaps to the top rope, and takes off with a guillotine legdrop, which connects. This is immediately followed by a 360 splash by Kobi, which furthers the damage on Havok. Kobi covers Havok, and the ref counts. One. Two. Three. Or was it? Havok got his shoulder up just in time. Kobi looks rather upset at this, and he picks up Havok, and nails him with a vicious chop to the chest. Havok continues to try to gasp for air, and Kobi capitalizes, whipping Havok into the ropes, and following up with a spear. Kobi picks up Havok once again, and whips him into the ropes. Kobi lowers his head, something Havok notices as he comes towards him. Havok grabs the head of Kobi, and nails a neckbreaker, putting both guys on the ground. Juno pulls for his brother, and the ref begins to count both men out. At five, Havok begins to get up, as does Kobi. Havok begins to go toward his corner, but finds nobody there. Panther laughs at him, and Havok gets fired up. However, as this happens, two other men make themselves known. Ruin, the team consisting of Robert Jensen and Frank Snow, run down towards ringside. Jensen grabs a chair, and Snow distracts the ref. Jensen lines up Juno, who has his back turned, arguing with Snow. Jensen decks Juno from behind, and then turns toward Kobi and decks him twice, causing him to fall off the apron. Panther sees this as Jensen hops out of the ring. He climbs into the ring, and covers Juno. Snow points the ref towards the cover. The ref turns around, looks confused, but makes the count anyway. Panther gets the three, and the former Millennium Men are your winners. The fans boo the Panther's pinfall rabidly. Panther gets up, and walks over towards Havok. The two get in each others faces, and a tussle begins. However, security makes their way down to ringside to break up the tussle before it becomes a melee. Meanwhile, Slaughter steamrolls forward.... Ruben Ross (c) vs. Sphere The Deftones hit the speakers with "Back To School", bringing out Sphere, ready to take the first crack at new Internet Champ, Ruben Ross. He hops into the ring and warms up as "Firestarter" hits the speakers. The fans cheer hard for Ruben Ross, the newest in the long line of Internet champions. He walks confidently towards the ring, and slides in, showing everyone his new belt. The bell rings, and Ross hands it to the ref. Then, he turns around, and receives a boot in the midsection from Sphere, followed by a DDT attempt. However, Ross pushes out of it, causing Sphere to fall down, thanks to that wonderful thing called intertia. Ross uses his momentum to carry him to the ropes, which he uses to get more momentum. Sphere gets up, and immediately receives a clothesline from the champ. Sphere gets up again, and this time sees a pair of boots coming at his head. However, he gets out of the way of Ross' dropkick attempt, and catches him in midair, dropping him with a powerslam. Sphere goes for a cover, but Ross kicks out at two. Sphere picks up Ross, and whips him into a turnbuckle. Ross flies in, and has the wind knocked out of him by the collision. Meanwhile, Sphere takes aim for the ropes, gets some momentum, and takes flight with a bulldog on Ross. Ross, however, gets his steam, and catches the weight of Sphere, knocking him back for a backdrop. This catches Sphere off-guard, knocking the wind out of him. Ross uses this opportunity to go for a cover. One. Two. Uh huh. Sphere gets a shoulder up. Ross picks up Sphere, and whips him into the ropes. Sphere bounces off, and stops. Ross is nowhere to be found. Sphere scratches his head, and turns around. Immediately, he finally greets Ross' boots to his cheek, as Ross punishes him with a missile dropkick. Another cover generates two and a half, before Sphere can finally kick out. Ross immediately grabs the head of Sphere, and locks him in for a vertical suplex. However, in mid-air, he turns it into a spinning tombstone...the Sit The Fuck Down (because, hey, he always wanted a move to be called that). Ross contemplates a cover here, but decides against it. He picks up Sphere again, and this time lifts him up for a powerbomb. However, he drops Sphere's head onto the top rope. Sphere hits the turnbuckle, and then spills onto the mat with a sickening thud. Ross looks at Sphere, and decides that he is still ok, at least for one or two more moves. Ross lifts up Sphere, and sets him up on the top rope. Ross climbs up, and locks his arm around Sphere's head. He signals to the crowd that something big is about to happen, and the crowd responds in kind. Ross picks up Sphere, and locks him up for a superplex. He looks at the crowd once more, and releases. He takes flight, and turns it into a SUPER SIT THE FUCK DOWN~!~! The crowd erupts, absolutely amazed at the move. Ross hooks the leg, although he might not need to do so. Three seconds later, Ross' first title defense is successful in a major way, and the crowd leaps to their feet to cheer Ross. He holds up his title once it is presented to him, and plays to the crowd a bit. Ross then gestures for a microphone. One is tossed to him, and he begins to speak. "Erik Kelly, get your ass out here!" The crowd cheered as Ross called out The Eradicator, and seconds later "POWERBOMB!" was heard, followed by "Break Stuff", and Erik Kelly and Matt Loco made their way onto the entrance ramp. Loco had a microphone in hand and spoke into it. "What the hell do you want?" "I'll tell you what I want. What I want is Erik Kelly's ass, at In Misery!" The crowd popped, and Ross continued. "I am sick and damn tired of you trying to punk me out. I don't know why it hasn't gotten through your thick skull that that chairshot at Body Count was an accident, but as far as I'm concerned, if you won't figure that out yourself, I'll be damn happy to beat the knowledge into you!" Kelly snarled, and Loco handed the microphone to him. "Ross, I don't give a good goddamn if it was an accident or not. All I care about is taking you out permanently. Me and you, for your belt, at In Misery? You're on! But Ross, realize this right now. Sooner or later, I'm gonna get to you. Whether it's in the next few weeks or at In Misery itself, your ass is mine. And there ain't a daaaaaaaaaaamn thing you can do about it!" Kelly threw the microphone and stalked off with Loco, while Ross held the belt high inside the ring and pointed to himself. Slaughter, meanwhile, headed backstage... Crusader vs. Chris Titan Two figures appear at the entryway, and the fans begin to react loudly when they see that those two figures are Crusader and Chris Titan. Titan seems to have the upper hand so far, as he delivers a hard right forearm to Crusader. Crusader fires back with a chop, but Chris Titan cuts him off with a kick to the mid-section, followed up with a suplex right on the steel ramp. Crusader manages to get up, but Chris Titan charges in and smacks him with a knee to the face, causing Crusader to fall back and roll towards the mats. Chris Titan brings Crusader up and attempts a whip into the steel steps, but Crusader reverses it, causing Titan to collide with the steel steps shoulder-first. After taking a moment to catch his breath, Crusader brings Titan back to his feet and rolls him back into the ring.
