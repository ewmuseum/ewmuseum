Uprising, 2 October 2003

2 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The Wrestler of the Year tournament continues... with one of the most highly anticipated matches of the contest... Freebird The fans were beginning to get restless, as Unleashed came back from a commercial break with no real activity. Finally, the curtain was thrown back and three figures emerged: two men, formerly known as Team EQ, and their manager, the aptly named manager of champions. Lance Knight, "Triple X" Sean Stevens, "Poison" Ivy McGinnis. Each wore a black armband, to commemorate the loss that they were feeling, both as professionals and as people. And while Ivy had nearly died a month earlier, and Lance and Sean had both lost good friends within the past twelve months, this loss was perhaps the hardest to deal with. Not because they couldn't understand why someone in his prime had been taken from them, but precisely because they understood it completely. And while they'd all been in the business for different lengths of time, they had one thing in common: they'd all been changed for the better by Eli Flair and they liked to think that they'd helped him to change for the better as well. And now? Now he was gone, choosing his love for his family over his love for the business. Ivy, Sean, and Lance all knew that Eli had made the most heart-wrenching decision of his nine-year career, and while they all secretly harbored their own hopes that he would return, he had made it clear to each of them that this decision was, more than likely, final. And it was for this reason that they had gathered in the ring on Uprising. In one way or another, Eli had brought them together, and they meant to honor him together as well. Without saying a word, the three turned towards the FanTron as the sounds of Lynard Skynard's "Freebird" filled the arena. On the FanTron, a still image of Eli Flair's face, that focused and stoic look that he had worn so many times. If I leave here tomorrow Would you still remember me? (CUTTO Eli Flair coming to the ring dressed as Deacon, with Deacon in the ring dressed as Eli Flair.) For I must be traveling on, now, 'Cause there's too many places I've got to see. (CUTTO the final few moments of the Barbed Wire match against Donaven Winters, where Eli won his first fWo Hardcore Championship.) But, if I stayed here with you, girl, Things just couldn't be the same. (CUTTO backstage footage from CSWA's Battle of the Belts XVII, where he leaned against a wall talking to CSWA Commissioner Chad Merritt, his wife Angel pacing in the foreground.) 'Cause I'm as free as a bird now, And this bird you can not change. (CUTTO the final moments of the December 31st, 2002 'Last Man Standing' match against Ruben Ross) Lord knows, I can't change. (CUTTO a bloody Eli Flair and an equally bloody Kevin Powers, twisted and wrapped up in barbed wire) Bye, bye, it's been a sweet love. Though this feeling I can't change. (CUTTO One of many shots of Eli Flair kneeling down by the ropes, listening to one of Poison Ivy's words of wisdom) But please don't take it badly, 'Cause Lord knows I'm to blame. (CUTTO Eli Flair, bleeding, standing on Troy Windham's left hand inside the cage.) But, if I stayed here with you girl, Things just couldn't be the same. (CUTTO the staredown between Eli Flair and Kodiak Vic Creed) Cause I'm as free as a bird now, And this bird you'll never change. (CUTTO the scene following Eli Flair vs. Hornet at CSWA Anniversary 1999, where he held the CSWA World Title above his head) And this bird you can not change. Lord knows, I can't change. (CUTTO Eli Flair and Triple X following their match at the HEW Tribute show, shaking hands in the middle of the ring.) Lord help me, I can't change. The video package faded out much as it had begun, only this time, instead of Eli Flair's face, it showed a shot of him and his family, and the look on his face was one of contentment and happiness, a look that he had seldom worn "at work" since the birth of his daughter. And as the song ended, the hush that had overtaken the crowd was replaced by a soft, respectful clapping. The three somber figures in the ring made their way down the ring steps and back up the entrance ramp, looking very much like a funeral procession as they marched, head down, to the back. Title Shot A long black stretch limo pulled up outside the Raleigh Sports Arena. The limo stopped as the door swung open and out stepped 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs wearing a blue Armani suit and Gucci sunglasses. Vince took a deep breath and starting to cough. "This place stinks." The crowd in the arena started to boo from Vince's comments. The driver handed Vince his bags as he walked toward the arena's entrance door. "Tonight in Raleigh, North Carolina, Vince Jacobs is going to become the new fWo Heavyweight Champion. Tonight Vince Jacobs: Pro Wrestling's Phenomenon will make history." Vince walked into the arena turning his nose up to people in the backstage area. He was looking for his dressing room, nothing more nothing less. SVJ walked past a kid backstage and stopped him. "Hey kid, you do know who I am right?" Before the kid could answer, Vince put his hand up in the kid's face. "Of course you do kid. SVJ is the man that will lead the fWo into the future. I am the future; you know a Superstar, The Ratings Grabber. It all starts tonight. Watch a Phenomenon in action later tonight." Vince walked off, as the kid looked at him stroll down the hallway. Spyder vs. Kodiak Vic Creed One of the most highly anticipated matches for the evening had to be the contest that was about to begin. Hyped by the performers and promotion alike, all eyes were now locked on the top of the entrance ramp as one man made ready to make his appearance. The black and white countdown appeared on the FanTron, then the pumping music of Rammstien heralded the arrival of the masked man. Only a few days had passed since his return, but Spyder was easily as hated now as he ever had been. As he walked to the ring, a smirk on his face and his dark eyes shining brightly, the screen behind him showed highlights from his brutal ladder match with Temerario. Fans had been hoping, perhaps believing without basis, that the villainous Spyder had turned a new leaf and decided to play on the side of the angels. This hope was dashed however, as Spyder repeatedly assaulted his fellow countryman with a steel chair, literally battering him into unconsciousness. The audience was informed that after the match Temerario was rushed to the local hospital where he suffered a severe concussion and required dozens of stitches. Temerario would be out of action for a couple of weeks, and as Spyder glanced over his shoulder and saw the footage for himself he laughed long and hard. Spyder hit the ring and swung himself inside, the nimble masked rudo luchador smirking as he grabbed the microphone from the timekeeper. "Ola... idiot fans! Why do you boo me? Because I am who I am, esse? It was you who believed I was something other than my nature... you who had me pegged as this great savior and great superstar. Well hommes, you were right about one thing, I am a great superstar. But the day esse, the day I start fighting for all of you idiot gringos is the day I hang up my boots and they get some jobber in the back to pretend they are the Spyder." Spyder laughed a long, hard laugh and tossed the microphone back at the timekeeper. The fans all around them were booing louder than ever, but as the music began for the fWo World Heavyweight Champion, the boos seemed to only intensify. Arguably the two most hated men in all of professional wrestling we about to go at it in the center of the ring. Kodiak Vic Creed, the Alaskan Juggernaut, the man who had withstood every challenge since he faced Erik Kelly more than six months ago, made his way slowly out onto the entrance ramp. The fWo World Heavyweight Championship secured tight around his waist gleamed under the lights and the fireworks that exploded around him. Through his dark sunglasses he surveyed the fans around him, smirking slightly as he made his way slowly down the ramp to face his loud and obnoxious challenger. This was a Wrestler of the Year tournament match, and not for the championship. But it made the contest no less important... both wrestlers were favorites to win the tournament, but one must be eliminated in the very first round. Fans booed and jeered as scenes from Unleashed and Creed's match against Brand Frontier were played behind him on the FanTron. Scenes of the brutal battle and Creed's questionable victory flashed on the screen, but the big man paid no heed and kept his eyes locked on Spyder, a slight snarl to his lips. He climbed into the ring, head down as if daring Spyder to rush at him. But Spyder kept his position and merely glared back at the World Champion, a position that Spyder held not so very long ago. Creed moved in until he was mere inches from Spyder. The two men glared at each other, neither a fan favorite but both carrying a severe dislike for one another. The bell rang, marking the start of the match official, but neither moved. Spyder looked up into Creed's eyes, still hidden from the sunglasses he wore on his face. Creed glared at Spyder, then smirked and turned his head away as he laughed at the sight of his opponent. Spyder's face flushed under his mask and he struck with a hard chop to the face that sent the sunglasses flying off of Creed and they shattered against a ring post. Spyder stepped back, satisfaction on his face. "Who's laughing now esse... or should I say... bitch?" Kodiak Vic Creed looked at the glasses, then slowly turned back to Spyder. A low growl was building from deep within him, and he suddenly sprung forward with a stiff forearm that caught Spyder in the neck and drove him spinning off of his feet. Spyder hit the mat with a wet thud as Creed turned, picked him up by the head and lifted him high up into the air. Creed gorilla pressed Spyder in the air, then let go and let the smaller man drop some eight feet down to the unforgiving mat below. Spyder groaned as Creed flexed and paced the ring, staring in disgust as his opponent. He walked up to Spyder, intending to pick him up once more, but Spyder shot up a boot and caught Creed hard in the groin. Creed groaned in pain and stumbled backward as Spyder leapt up to his feet, wincing slightly at the pain in his ankle. He advanced on Creed, shoving the referee aside as he moved forward. Spyder laced hard chop after hard chop into the chest of the champion, quickly lacing his chest with red stripes. But Creed's distraction was only momentary, as the big man shoved Spyder back and using his powerful arms began to strike back with hard, closed fists into Spyder. The referee moved in, warning Creed against using the closed fist. As he admonished the Alaskan Juggernaut, Spyder reached into his tights and retrieved a pair of brass knuckles. Slipping them quickly and quietly onto his fist, he moved in, shoving the referee aside and clocked Creed in the jaw. Creed fell back at once, and as the referee moved in to check on him Spyder quickly tossed the weapon out of the ring, the smirk back on his face. "All too easy, hommes." Spyder moved in, laughing at the streak of blood now coming from Creed's jaw and lay down for a casual cover. The referee moved into position as Spyder laughed. 1... 2... But Kodiak Vic Creed was not done. With no effort he tossed Spyder off of him and the growl in the back of his throat became a roar. Spyder looked at Creed with wide eyes, aware now that all he had done was make the big man, the world champion, furious. Creed came forward, absorbing several chops and grabbed Spyder by the face. He shoved him back into the corner, and as Spyder hit hard and came stumbling forward he picked him up and planted him with a belly to belly suplex. The referee moved closer, but Creed pushed him aside and picked Spyder up again. Holding him horizontally, Creed slammed Spyder down on his knee, but did not release him. Instead he spun him around and delivered a belly to back suplex that caused the entire ring to shake beneath the sheer impact of the power move. Spyder choked in the middle of the ring, coughing and completely dazed by this brutal, relentless attack. But Creed simply shot a knee down into Spyder's neck, then flipped him onto his chest and applied a stiff camel clutch submission hold. They were in the center of the ring, and Spyder was far, too far from the ropes and any hope of breaking the hold. The crowd suddenly roared as Neo emerged from the top of the entrance ramp and sprinted toward the ring, a steel chair in hand. But Creed saw him coming and as Neo stepped up onto the ring apron the champion connected with a hard right hand that sent the former Hardcore champion toppling from the side of the ring and into the steel barrier with a resounding crash. The steel chair lay on the padded floor where Neo had dropped it, and satisfied that Neo had been taken care of Creed turned his attention back to his masked challenger. As the referee moved over to shout to the time keeper to get security down to ringside to retrieve Neo, Kodiak Vic Creed turned back to his opponent. But Spyder had gone, rolled out from under the ring ropes and scurried over to the fallen steel chair. Creed spotted him on the outside and quickly jumped onto the ring apron. But Spyder had reached the chair and brought it up and onto the Alaskan Juggernaut's head. A resounding crack filled the arena, followed by Spyder's laugh as he tossed the chair onto the floor and moved over to the fallen champion. Creed was on one knee, dazed and trying to shake his head out. Spyder struck at once, grabbing the big man's head and slamming him up against the steel steps. Inside the ring the referee had turned his attention back to the match and was starting the long ten count for the two wrestlers to return to the ring. Spyder however had grabbed Creed's head and began to repeatedly bash it against the steps. With every blow a hollow metallic boom filled the arena until as the voice of the referee shouted out 'eight!' Spyder finally withdrew his attack and rolled the big man into the ring. Setting Kodiak Vic Creed up near the center of the ring, Spyder screamed out to the crowd in Spanish and climbed the ring post, clearly signaling for his big moonsault. But as he climbed Creed seemed to come back to himself, and groaned for the referee to come closer. The referee moved in to check on Creed as Spyder launched himself off of the top rope toward Creed. As the last second Creed pulled the referee onto him, and Spyder dove directly onto the referee who was shielding the big man from his deadly aerial move. Spyder groaned in pain as he slammed into the referee, and rolled off of the men clutching at his chest. Creed pushed the now limp and unconscious referee off of him and climbed slowly to his feet. The referee was out cold, and Creed looked down at him for a moment with a nasty smile and then rolled him out of the ring. Turning around, he then turned his attention to Spyder. Ripping off one of the turnbuckle pads, Creed picked Spyder up by his head and began to grind the masked man's face into the exposed steel of the turnbuckle. The referee, completely out and oblivious to this, made it possible for Creed to continue to work the masked man over until blood began to pour liberally out from under the masked man's mask. Suddenly the crowd erupted once more, but this time with resounding cheers. Running down from the entrance ramp was Brand Frontier, a serious expression on his face. Creed turned to see him coming, and ran over to the side of the ring to meet his old foe. Brand Frontier was up on the ring apron, shouting at Creed and warning him. As the audience screamed, Kodiak Vic Creed grabbed Brand Frontier by the shirt and cocked his fist back as if to hit him. But from behind Spyder had obtained Creed's coveted fWo World Heavyweight Championship. Brand Frontier dropped off the ring apron and Kodiak Vic Creed shouted at him further, but behind him Spyder had stumbled back into the ring and now stood crouched and ready. As Creed turned back to what he thought was his helpless opponent, Spyder charged forward and slammed the gold belt directly into the big man's face. Creed fell like a rock, and Spyder tossed the belt into the corner of the ring and lay down on him for the cover. Meanwhile, Brand Frontier grabbed the referee and shook him to revive him, then rolled him into the ring. Still dazed and barely aware of what was happening, the referee began a slow and tortured count. 1... 2... 3! The referee collapsed again as the bell rang, and Spyder got up from his fallen opponent smirking at how the match had gone in his favor. On the outside Brand Frontier watched the events with his arms crossed and a frown on his face, not liking how Spyder had won the match but pleased that Kodiak Vic Creed had been put out of the contest. Spyder picked up the belt from the corner and looked at it, smiling as he held it up for the crowd. The crowd booed, but Spyder ignored them. "Soon muchacha... soon you will be mine once again. This I promise you." Spyder draped the title belt over Creed's body and rolled from the ring. He grinned at Brand Frontier and pointed toward him. "Thank you esse, I owe you one. Perhaps you would fit in with my Triple Threat... eh, hommes?" Brand Frontier scowled. "You disgust me. Don't think I did this for you." "Oh?" Spyder said, a smile playing across his face. "Well esse, time will tell, won't it? There may come a day yet where you beg me to help you. Indeed hommes, who knows what the future may bring?" Spyder laughed and slowly made his way up the entrance ramp, leaving Brand Frontier frowning in his wake, and Kodiak Vic Creed, the fWo World Heavyweight Champion, eliminated in the first round. "Fucking public apology," he said while the words on the cards ran through his head. "I'm the one who deserves the apology around here." Along the corner he saw Chris Titan and immediately put the cue cards in the breast pocket of his blazer. He followed Titan to the coffee table. Overcoming Handicaps Thomas Harper's eyelids crept tentatively closer as he nestled in his chair. Despite the hard plastic of the seat, Thomas found comfort, and stealthily drifted off to sleep, a gentle snore rumbling immediately. His head dropped backwards, and his jaw fell open. In his newfound slumber, the younger of the Harper brothers wasn't awoken by the elder entering the room, deliberately quietening his motions when he saw Thomas' state. "Typical," Elliot scoffed under his breath. Harper's dark Hazel eyes scanned the room, and locked menacingly on a thin metal tray. Sneakily, he tip-toed over towards it and quietly slid it off the surface, brandishing it with both hands. "Time for your wake-up call," he chuckled in his usual sinister way. Light on his feet, Elliot was standing beside Thomas in a matter of seconds, tray held high and ready. Then, with a graceful yet furious swing, Elliot sent the tray crashing against the wall, resulting in a deafening tinny explosion of sound. Thomas' eyes shot open, startled, and his body flayed, off-balancing the chair and sending the younger Harper toppling backwards where he thumped against the cold tiled floor. "Ugh," Thomas expelled as his head bounced off the hard surface, and his eyes blinked rapidly. Seemingly not caring about his brother's physical condition, Elliot quickly grabbed the plastic chair and placed it over Thomas' downed form. Straddling the chair, with it's back facing Thomas' head, Elliot snarled down at his brother. "So this is what you do while I'm away," Elliot seethed, his brows angled so they met just above the bridge of his nose, "Is it?" Still stunned, Thomas couldn't formulate a sentence in his defence. "I.... Er... I..." "After all your fuck-ups, all your lies to me about making it big, you didn't think to... I dunno, warm up?" Elliot bellowed down at his brother, adopting a domineering stance as he leant over the chair, "Maybe get prepared so you don't become an even bigger disappointment?" Thomas had no vocal come-back, his lip just quivering to convey his thoughts and feelings. "Tonight is your last chance, Thomas," Harper then decreed, with rasping sincerity, "If you lose this handicap match tonight, I'm going to rid myself of my own handicap... ...YOU!" Taking in the severity of his brother's words, Thomas finally mustered up some words of his own. "I'll beat 'em, Elliot... I'll beat 'em good.... For you," Thomas nervously assured his elder sibling, "I won't be no disappointment no more..." A sneering Elliot just shook his head. "Oh, you won't just beat them... No... Tonight you're going to show just what you can do. No holding back," he programmed into Thomas, "I want you to DESTROY the Animals, I want you to DISMANTLE Rat and DECAPITATE Dog... I want them waking up tomorrow morning in pain, questioning if they EVER want to get back into the ring again..." Still slightly startled from recent events, Thomas nodded along to Elliot's venomous words. "I don't want you to pin them until there isn't a single ounce of fight left in them... If they submit, I want you to carry on anyway," Elliot ordered, with a madman's hint to his shadowed eyes, "The world will watch in fear of you, the locker room will sit up and take notice... And I'll be there at the end, hailing you as my brother... ...Do you understand, Thomas?" Thomas nodded, not that he had any other choice. "Good," Elliot announced, rubbing his hands together in glee, "Don't let me down." Aimz vs. Yukio The cameras shot back to the ring, as Aimz and Yukio were receiving last minute instructions from the referee regarding their first round Wrestler of the Year tournament match. The referee quickly rang for the bell, as Aimz and Yukio quickly locked up. Aimz gained the advantage, and hooked Yukio's head under her arm. Aimz fell backwards, and spiked her head onto the mat with a DDT. Aimz quickly fell on top, and got a quick two count before Yukio kicked out. Yukio held her head as Aimz shook her head in frustration, and lifted up Yukio. Yukio tried to shove Aimz away, but Aimz quickly rammed a knee into the abdomen of Yukio. Yukio keeled over as Aimz put her head between her legs, and lifted her up, spiking her onto the mat with a piledriver. Aimz quickly covered again, but once again only got a two count from Yukio. Aimz shook her head again, and quickly got up, laying in a few kicks to Yukio's face, and quickly lifted her up again. Aimz lifted up Yukio, and quickly kicked her in the abdomen again. Aimz put Yukio's head between her legs once more, and quickly flipped Yukio into the air, spiking Yukio onto the canvas with a snap powerbomb, ramming her head onto the mat. Aimz quickly covered once again, but once again, Yukio managed to kick out, this time at two and quarters. Aimz shook her head, and quickly punted another kick into Yukio's face, hopping onto the turnbuckle as Yukio slowly got up. As Yukio began to stand up, albeit slowly, Aimz flipped backwards into the air, twisting around, and grabbing Yukio's head, spiking it onto the mat, and completing her Dead-Aim. Aimz quickly hooked the leg, and the referee made the standard three count, finishing this squash of a contest, and allowing Aimz to advance on in the Wrestler of the Year tournament. The referee lifted Aimzs' hand up in the air in victory, as the crowd cheered her on. Result: Aimz via Pinfall; Advances in WOTY tournament to 2nd round Goodbye Stranger It was that time again, a time that he had missed for so long. It had been ages, or so it seemed since Douglas had slid his tights back on. Feeling the texture of them grabbed a hold of his skin. Sliding on his boots and his feet fitting firmly into them, it felt right, and it made him strong. Now he was stronger. Not mentally, but physically. Douglas had harnessed some form of strength by taking flesh from other men. After consuming pieces of their heart immense energy flowed through his veins and he felt alive. He felt more alive than he had ever felt, and he knew why. "Of course you do." His friend spoke to him, his friend had been speaking to him for a long time now. In fact his friend knew everything about him, even to what he was thinking. They would talk, and an understanding would be made. Douglas didn't inform anyone of who his friend was, and his friend would share secrets with him that only ancient men before him knew. Douglas was preparing for war, a war to be started by him. The world, it needed cleansing, it needed to be clean again... a world that loved him, but was dirty. So dirty it tortured him! Made him the FREAK he is NOW! TURNED ON HIM! HURT HIM! IT WAS DIRTY!..SO..Fucking dirty. His friend told him of a way to help cleanse the world. Douglas had taken the first step, taking strength from his enemies, consuming their soul. But his face... so scarred... so ugly... unable even to cry. Douglas smashed his fist hard into the glass mirror, leaving a substantial crack in the mirror. "Ah now, don't be emotional. It's just skin." Douglas looked up at the mirror and touched his scarred face, "But you don't understand... my face..." "I do understand. Do not lose focus." Douglas began to nod, "Your right, I'm so sorry." "Excuse me Mr. Douglas?" Douglas turned to face a young man standing in his door way, the young man's eyes suddenly shifted and Douglas responded, "Yes?" The young man took a few moments to speak, "Silver Fox wants to see you." Douglas nodded and walked past the young man. The young man watched Douglas walk down the hallway and disappear around the corner but just before he left, he looked back into the room Douglas was in. Looking for another man, or at least whoever Douglas was talking to. The young man paused, "Freak..." then continued on with the rest of his duties. Thomas Harper vs. Rat & Dog In the ring, the duo of Rat & Dog, the Animals, awaited their opponent... And his manipulative brother. TheyCameIn... The Cowardly Survivor & the Innocent Pawn stepped out onto the stage, to be met with a barrage of jeers, the fWo fans despising the un-sportsmanlike antics of the Survivor III pair thus far. Elliot proudly displayed his brother, an un-realised mass of raw power, and let everyone know that it was he - Elliot Harper - who unleashed him on the wrestling business. When they reached the foot of the aisle, Elliot ordered Thomas into the ring, where the Animals adopted an attacking stance, ready for it to begin. Elliot walked around to his corner, finding a cameraman on his way to spout off predictions of destruction to. The bell rang, and the Animals, knowing Thomas' mission, attacked right away, subduing Harper with their best forearms. Their onslaught didn't even drop Thomas to one knee, so a change in strategy was called for. Taking a bulky wrist each, they whipped the muscled frame of Thomas into the ropes and went for a double Clothesline... But Thomas negated it by exploding off the ropes with a Spear to each man, driving his shoulder into their ribcages. Rat and Animal curled up on the mat, grabbing their stomachs, winded. Thomas looked to Elliot, who simply gestured towards the Animals and drew his hand across his throat. After clamping his giant hand around Rat's throat, Thomas yanked the Animal off the mat, then seamlessly lifted him overhead in a Gorilla Press. Showing no strain whatsoever, Thomas walked around the fallen Dog with his partner held aloft, then brought Rat down with a devastating Slam. As Rat grabbed his back and arched off the mat, Thomas went for Dog, pulling him up by the back of his tights. Brutally, Harper whipped the Animal into the turnbuckles, stunning him... Before charging in with a Corner Clothesline, nearly taking Dog's upper-body apart. A hand around the throat stopped Dog falling to the mat, and with ease he was lifted up onto the top rope. Seemingly enjoying the methodical destruction, Thomas then threw Dog from his seat with a One-Handed Choke Toss. Dog crash landed beside Rat, and both began the battle to get back to their feet... ...A battle which Thomas ended by pulling Rat off the mat with a crude gutwrench. Thomas didn't release the hold, and kept the Animal aloft with an Inverted, Upside-Down Bearhug, shaking Rat like a rag doll. With Rat roaring out in pain, and his limbs flailing about due to the move, it was impossible to tell whether he was trying to give up or not... But that was all elementary as Thomas hoisted Rat up higher, then roughly threw him down with a Ganso Bomb, drilling the Animal into the canvas with vicious power. Rat was out cold, the impact to his neck and shoulder cutting his body's power off, but Dog was slowly crawling to his feet. Face etched in pain, Dog staggered towards Thomas, determined to put up a fight, but was met with the sole of Thomas' boot right in the face. Dog stumbled back against the ropes upon impact, leaving him seated on the middle strand... A sitting duck for Thomas Harper. At Elliot's command, Thomas charged at the defenceless Dog, Spearing him through the ropes with brute force, so that their bodies crashed down on that mats beside Elliot. With the sound of his brother's applause cutting through the jeers of the crowd, Thomas got to his feet, looking down at the wheezing Animal. Showing no mercy or hesitation, Thomas pulled Dog up by the neck and lifted him over the guard-rail... Before dropping Dog down ribs-first across the steel. Dog grabbed his stomach and tried to heave in the air that had been knocked out of him, but before any kind of recuperation could be achieved, Thomas again pulled him off the mats and tossed him casually back into the ring. Thomas turned to Elliot, who was nodding in appreciation of his brother's raw display of power. Into the ring Thomas went once more, and observed both the down Animals, deciding which one to go for next... With Rat being the unlucky choice. With the Animal holding the back of his sore neck, Thomas dragged Rat up to his feet and sent him into the ropes, catching him on the way back with a Charging Lariat, knocking Rat off his feet and spinning down onto the mat, unconscious. With Rat no fun to play with anymore, Dog was in Thomas' sights. Still struggling to breathe, Dog fought to get to one knee, then his ascent was aided by Thomas who pulled him up into a Bearhug, again shaking him harshly for extra punishment. Feeling his body being crushed, Dog went to tap, but Harper didn't even give him the chance to before he was crushed against the mat with a Belly to Belly Slam. Both Animals were out cold... Broken, beaten and bruised in a scary display of efficiency. Thomas was smiling, finally seeming to enjoy the brutal power that was his to command, and finally feeling he was making Elliot proud.
