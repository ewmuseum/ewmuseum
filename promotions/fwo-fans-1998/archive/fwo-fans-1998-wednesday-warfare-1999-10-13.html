Wednesday Warfare, 13 October 1999

13 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Backstage Crucifix leaned against the wall, his Hardcore Championship title slung over one shoulder. He looked fixedly down the halls of the arena, a mild smirk on his face. Spotting Bookerman walk by, Crucifix yelled, "Hey, Y2B boy... you got the guts to get in the ring with me? I won't hurt you... too far beyond recognition... how about it?" Bookerman fled away from Crucifix, and the darkly dressed champion laughed. Jackhammer entered the room and spotted Crucifix, looking at once shy and apologetic. He started to walk over to Crucifix, stopped, and then stepped up next to him. Jackhammer put his hand on his head and scratched the bald skin mumbling, "Aw shucks Crucifix... I just wanted to say that I'm sorry, you know? I didn't mean to beat you that way... it's just that I didn't want Hogan to interfere. You know how he is, right?" Crucifix looked at Jackhammer with a flat expression on his face and shook his head. "I have no idea what you're talking about baldy... now beat it." Jackhammer shuffled one foot and looked glum. "Yeah, I know how it is man... I'm sorry...," Jackhammer said, before retreating away toward his locker room. Crucifix shook his head in disgust and confusion. Just then the door leading in to the backstage area opened, and Rudo and Brandi stepped inside, looking back and forth to see if it was safe to enter. Crucifix raised an eyebrow at Rudo being in the arena, and Rudo pointed at the Hardcore champion. "I wasn't here... get me?" The couple quickly made their way into one of the dressing rooms, with Rudo holding a bag in one hand and a small green-colored rod in the other. Outside in the general seating area of the arena, fireworks exploded as the fWo began broadcasting Warfare live to televisions across the country. Crucifix shook his head in disgust once again. "There's nobody in the fWo that has the guts to face me... guess this is going to be an easy night..." He walked down the hall to his dressing room, not noticing a figure in the shadows behind him, who watched his every move... Fear the Underground The fans in the arena got ready for their first match, but were surprised when an unfamiliar song came over the house speakers. Chris Finn and James Armstrong looked at each other, unsure of what was happening since this did not appear on their program notes. The crowd looked around in confusion, until The Underground emerged from under the Travis Tron. Mike Bear asked the crowd if they liked his new music, which he said was Sanitarium by Metallica. The crowd cheered and then Wretch motioned to the back, and three masked men dressed in black emerged from the back behind the tag team. The five men walked to the ring, slapping hands with the crowd and getting a fairly large cheer since they hadn Ultra Cool 2000 vs. Dreamers The tag team tournament continued as Oracle made his way out without his scheduled partner, The Prophet. Oracle stood in the ring and awaited for Prophet to make his way out, but despite being announced three times, Prophet didn't. Oracle looked suspiciously around him, before Prophet came out through the crowd and into the ring. The two immediately began arguing about having to team with each other. Oracle began doing some trash talking, and perhaps said something he shouldn't have, as Prophet didn't take any more verbal thrashing from Oracle and nailed him with a kick to the tender nether regions, before following it up with a jumping DDT. Prophet then slid out onto the floor and brought in a chair. He piledrived Oracle on the chair several times, before leaving the ring with Oracle in it a bloody mess. Ultra-Cool2K made their way to the ring next, accompanied by Violet. The two wore huge leather pads on their shoulders and arms, with plastic spikes sticking out into the sky. Both wore modified Hocky goalie helmets, and Kid Cool appeared to have his on backward, and kept bumping into Violet as he walked. Ultra Violet turned to help his partner, and his balance shifted radically under the heavy pads, causing him to fall backwards and hit his head on the ramp. Kid Cool, who could see nothing, tripped over his partner and rolled down to the ring in a heap. Untangling themselves from the gear, Ultra Violet and Kid cool climbed in the ring and asked the referee why Oracle had fallen asleep. The referee told them what happened, and this confused Ultra-Violet even more. Ultra-Violet wondered aloudly why Prophet would attack his friend. Ultra-Violet then promised Kid Cool he would never attack him and leave him in the ring asleep, because friends don't let friends fall asleep during Warfare. Since Ultra-Violet was being so friendly, he told Kid Cool he could see what it's like to actually pin somebody during a match. He let Kid Cool do the honors, whlie waiting out on the ring apron. Kid Cool was so excited he forgot what a pinfall was, and had to have Ultra-Violet come back into the ring and explain it to him. Ultra-Violet told the referee to ring the bell, so he could show Kid Cool how to do a pinfall. Ultra-Violet covered Oracle and showed Kid Cool how to do it. The referee counted to three, and UC2K advanced to the second round of the tag team tournament. Ultra-Violet seemed befuddled why the referee declared them the winners, when all he was doing was showing Kid Cool how to execute a pinfall. The referee told them they won, and should take the win. Ultra-Violet almost got angry, before smiling, and remembering he never gets angry. Kid Cool seemed disappointed that he didn't get to make the pin, but was happy nonetheless because they won again. Warfare rolled on to a commercial break... but it should be noted that Micky Maliboo was backstage watching all that occured very closely... Result: Ultra Cool 2000 by Pinfall Not taking "no" for an answer Outside the office of the commissioner John Willis stood guard. It had been a quiet day, and much more calm than on Slaughter. Willis brushed at a sleeve, almost bored with the lack of activity. "Maybe that dumb JOY campaign is working after all...," Willis said, just before a steel chair flew into his head. Willis crumpled down to the floor in a heap as Scott Slugger and Ice stepped over his body and knocked at the door. Slugger grinned to Ice and said, "Damn, I could have been a pretty good pitcher too..." before the door opened. Commissioner Travis looked down at the fallen body of John Willis and then up at Scott Slugger. The Commissioner closed his eyes for a moment, hoping that when he opened them again Scott Slugger would be gone. This did not work, and Slugger grabbed the Commissioner by the shirt and pushed him up against a wall. "Now listen here you pencil pushing asshole...," Slugger shouted into the face of the Commissioner. "You get out that booking pen of yours, you call your little writers into a meeting, and you give Ice and I the Hollywood Blondes match tonight. I'm not waiting another four days to shove this boot up the asses of those two pretty boys, you sign the match tonight, or the beating I gave you last time is going to look like a damn nap." The Commissioner gulped, and Scott Slugger lowered him back to the ground with a smirk. Slugger and Ice slapped hands and left to go back to their locker room, as the Commissioner looked on with dismay. Finally walking back into his office he picked up the phone, dialed, and said into the receiver, "Get the writers up here... we've got to make an addition to the card..." NXS vs. Underground NXS strut their way to the ring for their match, another first round Tag Team Tournament match-up. Steel and RottDogg slide into the ring and pose while Cairn stalks the ringside area. The Underground are out next, and Wretch slaps hands with the fans while Mike Bear keeps his eyes locked upon their opponents. The massive new member of the Underground, Fear stands at the top of the ramp, looking down with the grim expression of an enforcer. They have a short discussion on the outside before Bear enters the ring and Wretch takes his place on the apron. The bell rings, and Bear knocks both Steel and RottDogg down with a double-clothesline. RottDogg roles to the outside, and Bear drags Steel back to the middle of the ring. Once there, he drops a leg across his sternum, and goes for the cover. Steel kicks out before two, so Bear headbutts him and covers again. This time the ref slaps the mat twice, but Steel still gets out. Bear brings him to his feet and whips him hard into the neutral corner, then catches him with a backbreaker when he staggers out. Bear heads up top and drops an elbow, but Steel rolls out of the way in the nick of time. Steel makes it to his corner and brings in RottDogg, who goes straight for the Bear. He lifts Bear up for a chokeslam and plants him in the middle of the ring. He goes for a cover, but Bear gets the shoulder up before even a one-count is applied. Both men up, and Bear brings RottDogg down with a death-defying Drop Toe Hold. Bear hooks in an STF, but RottDogg quickly grabs the ropes to break the hold. Bear brings RottDogg to his feet and applies a side headlock, and then he tags in Wretch. Bear executes a quick Russian leg sweep to bring RottDogg down, and Wretch comes off the top with a twisting moonsault for two. Wretch and Bear whip RottDogg back into his corner, where he makes the tag to Steel. Steel foolishly rushes The Underground, and is caught with a double team hiptoss. Steel gets back to his feet and is hit with a double team suplex. A double team powerbomb follows, and Wretch locks in a camel clutch on Steel. Trapped in the middle of the ring and unable to power out of the hold, Steel submits while Mike Bear wards off both RottDogg and Cairn. The decision rendered, "Big Daddy" Markus Cairn enters the ring along with RottDogg, but dual dropkicks from the Underground clear the ring. NXS make their way back to the locker rooms in shame, while The Underground play up to the crowd, triumphant. Result: Underground by Submission Trouble in France? Backstage the sounds of a heated argument were heard up and down the halls of the arena. Matt Loco grabbed a microphone and went to investigate, and discovered that the argument was coming from behind the door of the French Foundation locker room. Slamming tables and chairs could be heard, along with angry shouts. One of the voices was obviously the Flying Frenchie, and the other sounded very much like Mitch Wilson. Just as Matt Loco had gotten up the courage to enter the room to get the story, Primetime and the Boston Strangler walked onto the scene. The Strangler shoved Loco backward with a simple, easy gesture and the pair entered the room to break up the argument. The shouting stopped, as the door was slammed shut again. Matt Loco strained to listen, but could hear nothing more from outside the French Foundation dressing room... Cactus Jon vs. Fallen Warfare continued as Cactus Jon made his way out. Cactus Jon wasn't alone however, as he brought along some of his "boyz" from TCW to watch his back. The Foreign Phantom, "Big" Ben Welsh and Havok were with Jon, who walked to the ring with a purpose. Jon climbed into the ring and awaited Fallen, who stormed to the ring. These two wasted no time in going at it, as they traded blows back and forth. Meanwhile on the outside, the guys from TCW went over to the broadcast position and began picking on poor James Armstrong. Back in the ring, Fallen seemed more annoyed by the punches than anything, as he again appeared to be unaffected by them. Fallen got the upperhand after a rake to the eyes... and then a kick to the midsection. He followed it up by whipping Jon to the ropes, and ducking for a backdrop, but instead Jon returned and hit a fisherman's suplex. The referee began to count, but Poison & Demon appeared and pulled the referee out of the ring. The referee immediately signaled for the bell. Poison and Demon then climbed into the ring and helped Fallen up, before beating on Jon. The TCW'ers stormed the ring and cleaned house, before helping Jon up. Fallen, Demon & Poison stared into the ring at Jon and his boyz, and they dared them to come into the ring and fight. The arena suddenly went to darkness, and the mysterious masked man made his way out onto the top of the ramp. The masked man made the turnbuckles burst into flames, and nearly scathed the face of Jon, who stared at the masked man spitefully. Fallen, Demon & Poison began to walk up to the top to meet the masked man, as Jon and his comrades vowed revenge. As they left the ringside area James Armstrong shook his head in disgust of the Canadian's performance, and vowed that his dream team would show up next week on Warfare... Result: Cactus Jon by DQ Rain contemplates his future Rain sat somewhere cold and completely foreign to the real world. A dusty leather jacket covered him, and in one hand he turned a small crystal over and over again, looking at all sides and facets. He could almost see things inside of it... worlds upon worlds of reality, now all under his control. Rain thought about this for a moment, trying to best determine what to do next. He would never have to return to the real world again if he choose not to... but this was not something he thought he could live with. Besides, with this object in his possession he could enter and leave any reality whenever he choose to. But the question was, could others? Rain thought about this silently, turning the crystal over in his hand as he did so. It was a small, glassy crystal...diamond shaped and cold to the touch. As Rain turned it over in his hand he didn't notice it start to change... until when he finally looked at it again he was surprised to see that it had taken on the shape of a raindrop. Rain smiled and closed his fist around it. He could travel anywhere and anywhen he wanted to... but could others? Could, for example, he bring someone from one reality to the next? And if he could... wouldn't he have full control over them, since he determined how and when they could come and go? Rain's smile broadened. There was only one way to find out... Micky Maliboo vs. Matthew Zhuk When we return to the ring, Matthew Zhuk is already in the ring, informing everyone who's willing to listen, along with those who aren't, that he is, in fact, Great Lookin'. Micky Maliboo, making his re-debut in the fWo tonight, dashes down to the ring and slides right in. Zhuk takes advantage from the get go, stomping the back of Maliboo before he could get up. Zhuk then slipped in a camel clutch, hoping that what worked for Wretch minutes ago would work just as well for him. It didn't, as Maliboo powered out of the hold in short order. The two men return to their feet, and stand face to face for the first time in this match. Maliboo swings at Zhuk, but Zhuk ducks and dropkicks Maliboo's knee, bringing him down again. Zhuk leaps to the top, and nails a senton splash on Maliboo. Maliboo kicks out of the cover, and springs back to his feet. Micky grabs Zhuk's long hair and tosses him to the outside. When he tries to go outside himself, the referee holds him back, admonishing him for his blatant cheating, going so far as to advise Micky to be more subtle next time. Zhuk steps onto the apron, and orders the ref to move Maliboo out of the way. The ref attempts to comply, but when he turns his back to move Maliboo, Zhuk springboards over the ref's head and nails Maliboo with a dropkick. The resulting cover gets two, so Zhuk hits a standing senton/moonault combo, which also earns a two. Zhuk brings MM back to his feet and goes for a hurricanrana, but it's reversed into a powerbomb by Maliboo. Maliboo's not finished yet, as he hoists Zhuk back up, and hits a running powerbomb. Maliboo stands over Zhuk, who's nearly folded in half, and screams out that this is what's going to happen to anyone else that steps into the ring with him. Maliboo goes for a cover, but before the ref can count to three, Prophet runs into the ring and breaks the count. He nails Maliboo with his patented "Blue Rising" chokeslam, the entire time berating "Mali-blue" for not respecting him or the power of Blue. Before any further damage can take place, Oracle comes to the aid of Micky, catching Prophet with a bulldog and kicking him out of the ring. Oracle and Prophet exchange words, as Micky Maliboo comes to. Result: Micky Maliboo by DQ The return of New Wave Down one of the hallways in the arena War, Plague, Stevie Roberts and VL stood. Stevie and VL looked wary, but War and Plague remained relaxed. Stevie shook his head as if he didn't believe what he had just heard. "Let me get this correct... you're going to lay down for us next week?" Plague nodded and said, "Listen, if that's what it takes for you to trust us then consider it done. Besides, you two are former Tag Team champions..." VL leaned forward murmuring, "Two time World Tag Team champions." Plague nodded, understanding. "Right, right... two time champions. You guys have what it takes to go all the way, and we are good at helping keep things fair. I mean, do you think Death could have won any of the titles he got if he didn't have us? Come on... War and I carried that team by watching his back. And we can watch your backs much better than Death and Stone's... because we respect you man." Plague put out a hand to Stevie. "Come on Stevie... trust me on this one... and let's let New Wave be born again." Stevie paused for a moment, and then grasped Plague's hand. Stevie looked moved, and with a crack in his voice said, "It's good to have you on my side again Johnny..." Dressed in a full black costume and a mask with an "X" across the face, a mysterious new competitor made his way to the ring. Announced as "WRESTLER X, Masked Superstar", the wrestler thrust up his arms for the crowd, getting a mild response. His opponent, Courage, came out onto the ramp, but after looking at the wrestler in the ring with mild amusement he sent Jake down to the ring instead saying, "I don't have time for Heftel's goofiness". Jake waddled down to the ring looking mildly alarmed that he would be facing WRESTLER X, and rolled into the ring to confront him. The two locked up, and WRESTLER X put Jake in a quick chin lock, and then drove a knee into his back. Jake yelped and dropped down, turning the hold into a jaw breaker and escaping. Jake ran back to the corner of the ring looking for an escape, but WRESTLER X was after him, and delivered hard punches into his midsection. These seemed to have no effect, merely squishing off the large belly of Courage's assistant. WRESTLER X used a head butt instead, dropping Jake quickly and sending him to the outside. Jakes shuffled around the ring as WRESTLER X came after him, but sensing that his assistance was needed Courage grabbed WRESTLER X from behind and turned his mask around. Blinded, WRESTLER X stumbled into the turnbuckle head-first. Jake re-entered the ring as WRESTLER X wandered away into the announcing table, and then into the metal guard rail. Courage grabbed WRESTLER X and rolled him into the ring, where Jake tripped him and kicked at his head. Jake then climbed to the second rope and started to go higher, but the metallic groan from the turnbuckle warned Jake that this might not be a good idea. Jake fell back off the second rope with the "Fat Guy Splash" and lay on WRESTLER X for the three count, winning the match. Courage was busy watching this from ringside, and didn't notice Eron coming up from behind him. Eron swung with the International Title, striking Courage in the head and dropping him to the mat. Laughing, Eron made a quick escape to the backstage area with his International Title in tow. Jake looked after Courage, who was enraged at being attacked. Warfare went to a commercial as Courage got to his feet and gave chase... Result: Jake by Pinfall Trouble in JOY land Backstage Joy was busy passing out buttons which had his JOY message printed on them. Joy wasn't getting very far with his campaign, as wrestlers and backstage personnel passing by him ignored his pleas for happiness or actually punched Joy in the face as they went. As Glee dabbed at Joy's swollen eye with a damp cloth Joy expressed frustration. "Gosh darn it Glee, I've been trying to get everyone to go along with my JOY campaign and it's just not working. Only one loser on a web site is actually using the JOY in his messages, and I'm just darn-diddly-arned about what I'm supposed to do next." Glee patted his head and gave him a kiss on the cheek. Joy winced from the pain the contact made, and dropped his bright yellow JOY buttons. He knelt down to pick them up, and as he did so four wrestlers walked past him. Fallen, Demon, Poison, and... "Oh my gosh!" Joy gasped as his face turned white. Glee looked struck with horror as he put an arm on Joy's back to comfort him. Joy turned to Glee wide eyed and white as a sheet. "Did you see who that was? Oh no... this is bad... this is very, very bad..." Crucifix (c) vs. ??? Crucifix makes his way down to ringside, the Hardcore title slung over his shoulder and a microphone in his hand. He grabs the top rope and pulls himself onto the apron, then steps over the top rope. Crucifix lifts the microphone to his face, and begins to speak. "People, I've held this (raising the Hardcore Title) for over a month now, and no competition has stepped forward. Maybe they're afraid of me, maybe they're afraid of the title. Who knows? But I've been willing to step into the ring and defend it against anyone who wanted a shot. Now the powers-that-be, they've decided that someone's getting a shot at my belt, but they don't think it's necessary to tell me who? What kind of crap is this? Listen, whoever you are, I think it's time you let your presence be known." Crucifix tosses the microphone out of the ring defiantly and faces the ring entrance, waiting for his opponent. The arena goes black suddenly, and then grows very quiet. When the lights come back on, Crucifix is standing face to face with Grendal! The referee signals for the bell, and the two monsters trade stiff blows in the center of the ring. The larger Crucifix forces Grendal to the ropes and lariats him to the outside. Crucifix follows that up with a suicide dive as Grendal regained his footing, taking the fight to the arena floor. Both men back up, and now they're fighting in the aisle, knocking each other senseless. Grendal stuns the champ with a high knee, and then beals him over the guardrail and into the crowd. Security holds back the fans as Grendal delivers an axehandle to Crucifix, doubling him over. Grendal locks in a double under-hook, but the suplex attempt is blocked by Crucifix, who backdrops Grendal onto the hard cement floor. Crucifix picks up the challenger, and sidewalk slams him onto a row of previously occupied chairs. The impact crushes them, and Grendal is left lying in a pile of mangled debris. Crucifix drops a knee on his skull, busting Grendal open in the process. The champ brings Grendal to his feet and whips him hard into the steel barrier. Grendal flips over the rail upon the collision, and lies motionless in the aisle. Crucifix mocks the newcomer openly, and takes his time getting back to the match at hand. By the time he reaches the guardrail, Grendal is back on his feet, and he rocks Crucifix with a hard right before dropping his throat on the barrier. Grendal jerks the section of rail loose and brings it down on the back of Crucifix's skull. He goes for a cover, but the ref informs him that he won't count the fall anywhere but in the ring, so he picks Crucifix up and places him in position for a running powerslam. He carries the 310 lb. man to the ringside area, and performs a running powerslam onto the steel steps. Crucifix rolls off the steps while clutching his back, and Grendal picks up the steps. He swings them down towards Crucifix, but the champ catches the steps with his feet, driving them back into the face of the challenger. Crucifix returns to his feet slowly and rolls into the ring. He backs into a corner for a breather as Grendal climbs back into the ring as well. The two look each other over from across the ring, before they both charge with lariats. Both men go down, and the ref starts to count them out. Crucifix is up at 5 and drops another knee to Grendal's skull. He lifts Grendal and locks in a sleeper hold. The ref checks Grendal, but his arm stays up on the first try. Grendal butts the back of his head against the Crucifix's face and drives Crucifix into the corner. He sets Crucifix up on the turnbuckle, and hits an overhead belly-to-belly superplex! The ring shakes at the impact of 600 plus pounds hitting it so much that the ref is knocked off his feet. Fortunately, it puts him in perfect position to count the cover as Grendal drapes his arm across the champ's body, earning the pinfall, and becoming the new Hardcore Champion. The referee hands Grendal the title, who seems pleased. The lights go out again, and when they come back only Crucifix remains in the ring, still recovering from the grueling battle. Hollywood Blondes vs. Scott Slugger & Ice It was now time for our main event and the first match of the second round in the continuing tag team tournament. As we all heard "Do you buy what The Sellout is selling?", out came The Hollywood Blondes. Universal & Star walked out onto the stage and looked upon the masses of bodies shouting their proverbial lungs out with a massive ovation of boos, before walking down the ramp and cockily taunting the fans. Universal had a handful of c-notes and told the fans that it was the most money they'd ever see in their lives. As the duo stepped into the ring, Universal ripped off his three-piece suit rather quickly, revealing a pair of black trunks with gold lettering on the back that read "The Franchi$e". Star & Universal awaited their opponents, and seconds later, we heard "Back in Black", which signalled the presence of the fWo World Champion Scott Slugger and his close friend Ice. Slugger came out with the fWo World Title wrapped around his waist, and Ice stared intently at the ring, seeking revenge for the comments made by the Blondes about Ice being Slugger' "big blonde boyfriend". Slugger & Ice charged the ring and immediately began brawling with the Blondes, Slugger & Universal went at it as did Ice & Star. Slugger & Universal traded punches, while Ice manhandled the smaller Star. Ice tossed Star from the ring as Slugger whipped Universal to the ropes and then ducked for a backdrop; Universal returned and hopped over him with a leapfrog, and ran right into Ice. The Hollywood Blondes re-grouped on the outside, while Slugger and Ice stared them down from the inside. Ice backed his partner up against the ropes, and sent him in the Blondes direction with an Irish Whip. Slugger went flying out feet first towards both men, taking them both down with a baseball slide. Slugger rolled Star into the ring, and then slammed Universal head first against the steel steps on the outside, which sent him reeling. Star got up staggering, and when he turned around he was taken down with a kick to midsection, followed up by a ddt. Ice went for a cover, but garnered only a one and a half count. Ice continued to work over Star, as Slugger and Universal each went to their respective corners. Ice brought Star to his feet and whipped him to the ropes, Star returned and Ice pressed him high into the air, but Star escaped and fell behind him and rolled him up into a pin. Ice quickly kicked out, and he charged at Star and Star took him down with a Japanese armdrag. Both men hopped to their feet and Star hit Ice with a bunch of reverse knife edge chops, the seemed to have an affect on Ice. Star backed Ice into the ropes and whipped him across. As Ice bounced off the ropes, Slugger slapped his back and tagged himself in. Ice returned and Star took him down with a single leg Boston Crab, but couldn't keep it locked in for long as Slugger came in and took Star down with a fisherman's suplex. At the count of two, Universal came in and broke up the pin. Slugger brought Star to his feet and as Star returned he went for a tiltawhirl headscissors, but Slugger reversed it into a tiltawhirl backbreaker. He brought Star to his feet and hit him with some forearm smashes, before whipping him into the turnbuckles. Slugger followed it up as Star staggered out with a sidewalk slam, before bouncing off the ropes and following it up with a kneedrop. He went for another cover, but again Universal interrupted things. Finally, Slugger brought Star to his feet, and told him to tag in Universal. Star obliged, and Universal came in. The fWo World Champion and the self-proclaimed fWo franchi$e exchanged greetings, before Slugger hauled off and hit Universal. Universal returned the favor, and this continued before Slugger gained an advantage with a knee to the stomach, followed by a pumphandle slam. Slugger went for a pinfall, but Universal kicked out at two. Slugger tagged in Ice and they whipped Universal into the ropes, before following it up with a double-backdrop. Ice picked up where Slugger left off, taking Universal down with a submission type nervehold to the neck. After lasting in the hold for several minutes, Universal appeared as if he couldn't take any more punishment, and was about to pass out. Star came in and kicked Ice in the back of the head, forcing him to release the hold. Both men made a move towards their respective corners to tag out, and finally did so. Star & Slugger came in, and after fighting back and forth, Slugger got the advantage. Slugger hit a piledriver, and went for a cover, but only got a two count. Slugger hit a Samoan Drop, went for a pin, but again got only a two count as Star show off his resilliency. Finally, Slugger decided he needed to finish him off. Universal and Ice brawled onto the floor, as Slugger signalled for the Curveball finisher. Slugger locked Star for the hold, and jumped up onto the second rope... he turned in a motion to complete the hold, but Star reversed it and took him down with a Michinoku Driver off the second rope. Star went for the cover, and Slugger kicked out just a split-second too late, as the Blondes picked up the victory. Both teams were exhausted, and Slugger was in absolute shock. Star did indeed pin Slugger like he said he was going to, as Slugger & Ice stood in the ring staring at the Blondes who were now outside the ring. Slugger became angry, and even pushed Ice out of his way at one point, clearly pissed off about getting pinned once again. Slugger asked for a microphone, and began to speak. "Lance Star... I'll give you credit. You did pin the World's Greatest Athlete like you said you were going to, but I believe it was a total fluke. You're still nothing, a nobody... you can hang onto this cheap victory again like you have for the past month, but it doesn't mean squat until you pin me for the fWo World Title. So, what do you say... Sunday Slaughter, you and the WGA, twenty-minute time limit. If you beat me, you're the new champ. If I beat you, you must admit the other two victories were an absolute fluke. Deal?" Star motioned as if he agreed, and Slugger gave a confident grin, as Warfare went off the air... Result: Hollywood Blondes by Pinfall
