Introduction In 1998 the fWo launched Sunday Night Slaughter, a weekly program than spanned six years of programming. Through the course of the show, the fWo saw new champions crowned, love bloom, and hatred kindled into blazing infernos. Over the course of time all good things must come to an end, sometimes so that a new chapter and new beggining can be written. What the future will hold, none may say... but at least one ending, the ending of Sunday Slaughter, will be written tonight. 13-Sep-98 Roberts came to the ring with all of the Dark Riders, and it was shown that Taylor had security hold off Ice, the Strangler, Ultra Cool, Cactus Jon and other fWo stars would wanted to aid Dudley. As the bell rang, Roberts unexpectedly laid down in the ring and allowed Dudley to quickly pin him. Roberts then stood and tore off his Dark Riders shirt, revealing an fWo shirt beneath. The angry Dark Riders hit the ring as Roberts and Dudley escaped into the rafters with the aid of Brandi, who lowered a harness to the ring. Taylor angrily ran to the ring and announced that the decision be reversed and Dudley be fired from the fWo. At that point comissioner Travis arrived on the entrance ramp, and announced that the only person being fired from the fWo was Scott Taylor, and that not only would Dudley get a title shot at the next Paranoid show, but that it would be a sealed cage match to ensure that no Dark Rider interference take place. "It's just one'a those days, when ya don't wanna wake up. Everything is fucked, everybody SUCKS... Ya don't really know why, but'cha wanna justify ripping someone's head off!" Cheers turned to boos turned to a mixed reaction as James Renshaw, the Lord of the Hardcores, headed to the ring with purpose. "No human contact, and if you interact your life is on contract! Your best bet is to stay away, muthafucka! It's just one of those days!!" He looked more determined than before, but he also had nothing to compare it to. Renshaw slid under the bottom rope and cracked his neck, waiting for his opponent. "I will never bother you... I will never promise to... I will never follow you... I will never bother you..." He didn't have to wait long. "Never speak a word again, I will crawl away for good!" The blue-green strobe kicked up as the fans popped huge at the sight of Eli Flair and Poison Ivy's scowling faces over the FanTron. Eli and Renshaw, no stranger to each other, circled with suspicion. They locked up, and Eli landed a knee to Renshaw's gut. He doubled over in pain as Eli crashed his knuckles into his face, bringing him straight up again in time to eat a hard clothesline. Eli grabbed him by the throat and pulled him to his feet, shoving him into the corner as hard as he could. He ignored the referee's call to take it out of the corner and pounded the helpless Renshaw with fists from his head to his chest. A cross- corner whip, and a HARD spear that flattened the youngster against the turnbuckle. Eli hooked his head and dragged him to the middle of the ring, where he was planted with a stiff DDT. He waited... just a moment... and spiked him with a one- handed chokeslam. As the music kicked in again and the referee raised Eli's hand in victory, the King of Extreme shook his head in exasperation... this was not the kind of match he'd wanted to have tonight. As he headed back up towards the backstage, he tried to slap hands or knuckles with every fan with a set outstretched.... very much unlike Eli Flair, but the fans weren't complaining. After all, how often do you have tickets to the final Sunday Slaughter of all time? Result: Eli Flair via submission Coincidence? Lance Knight looked up when he heard the door to his locker room open. Poison Ivy stuck her head in. "Alex," she began, "remember I told you about the local radio contest, and the winner gets to meet you?" "Yeah, Ivy, I remember," Lance replied. "Is it time?" "It's time." He stood and waited. He didn't really want this need this right now, but public image and publicity were a known hazard of the business. From the other side of the door he heard Ivy introducing herself, probably to some pimply-faced prepube who'd get a boner meeting his hero in person. When the door opened, however, Lance could only stare. "Hey, I heard I was going to meet a wrestler! Is this the guy who's going to take me to him?" Luke Starkey stood in the doorway, a grin plastered across his face. Behind him, Ivy looked confused. Lance laughed and walked over to shake Luke's hand. "What're you doing here?" asked Lance. "I'm the big contest winner!" Luke answered. "Do you two know each other?" Ivy was clearly perplexed. "Yeah, Luke here saved my life a couple of weeks ago," Lance said casually. Ivy was instantly concerned. "What!?!" Luke smiled disarmingly. "Actually," he said, "I just didn't want the truck driver to mess up his shiny grill." Luke and Lance laughed easily while Ivy stared, not quite believing what she was hearing, and the fact that she was hearing about it for the first time. "Well then," she said, "since I was only here for introductions, I'll leave you boys alone. But Alex, I want to hear more about this later." "Yes, ma'am," Lance answered dutifully, but his smile belied the serious tone of his voice. Ivy sighed, rolled her eyes, and closed the door behind her as she left. Lance gestured to a chair and took a seat himself. Luke, looking around the Spartan locker room, took the offered seat. "So this is the world of professional wrestling, eh?" Luke asked. "Not quite as glamorous as I'd imagined." Lance snorted in amusement. "It's actually just like this: sitting alone in tiny room, waiting until they call your name. Pretty boring, most of the time." "And you love it." "And I love it." Luke suddenly leaned forward, looking intently at Lance. "Listen, Alex... I didn't have any idea that I was going to meet YOU when I won this contest - they just told me it would be an fWo superstar. And Ivy opened the door and you're standing there..." Lance shrugged his mighty, muscular shoulders. "Weird. Coincidence?" Luke stood up. "I don't believe in coincidence." Lance stood as well and the two stood, looking at each other silently. Luke broke the awkwardness as he clapped Lance on the arm. "Well look, I've never been backstage at a big wrestling extravaganza before, and this pass gives me All Access, so I'm going to poke around for while." "Thanks for dropping by, Luke. It was really good to see you again," Lance said honestly. Luke laughed. "Hell, I wasn't about to pass up a chance to meet a real wrestler," he said, "Are there any around?" Lance smiled at the jest and watched Luke leave. It was nice to see a friendly, smiling face, he thought, especially since lately friendly faces around him seemed to be in short supply. Aimz(c) vs. Kellen Kinkade "In my eyes, indisposed In disguise as no one knows Hides the face, lies the snake The sun in my disgrace." BOOM. The blinding firework explosion bled into the rest of "Black Hole Sun" by Soundgarden as the Immortal champion stood at the top of the aisle, belt aloft in the air and ready for a bout which he'd planned for meticulously over the past month or so. Tossing himself willingly into the cauldron of abuse before him he ambled down the aisle, title belt slung over his shoulder and glistening under the bright lights of the ringside area. There was a confidence about the challenger... a confidence that seemed far too assured. As he clambered into the ring "Going Under" by Evanescence hit and the crowd hit the roof, Aimz appeared from behind the curtain with the hardcore title raised in one hand and a Singapore cane in the other. She wasn't wasting any time either... the hardcore champion rushed toward the ring with the confidence of a competitor who had nothing to fear. A competitor who knew nothing about a rib injury that she'd been lied to about... Aimz was far from 100% and the only person that truly knew it was the man poised to attack her in the middle of the fWo ring. Aimz dropped the title belt just outside the ring and slid under the ropes as Kinkade dropped his Immortal title... she drew back the cane and took a massive swing but The Jersey Devil was wise to it and ducked. His rebound against the ropes brought him straight into Aimz... ... with a Killing Spree. It was at that very moment that she realized she was far from fully fit; it hurt... it hurt like hell and his choice of attack had been far too convenient. Kinkade had made a delicate incision and now he was going to attack the open wound with everything that he had. Aimz lay back on the canvas in burning agony... her ribs on fire as Kellen Kinkade got to his feet a pleased man. "I don't tell people to fuck themselves because they can wait, for nothing." He barked at the downed champion before smiling insanely and picking up the Singapore cane that she'd dropped "Because as the old saying goes... good things come to those who do, you probably realize now that you were lied to about your ribs, so sad... too bad and too late for that matter. Welcome to hell, slut." "Screw you!" Aimz snarled defiantly through clenched teeth as she tried to stand... something that she achieved for a split second. SNAP. The horrible sound of the cane whipping across he ribs... Aimz fell straight back to her knees in agony again as Kinkade paraded around her, the weapon raised triumphantly in the air. He didn't waste much time however... quickly threading the cane under her body as she gasped for air and using it to apply what could only be described as a highly modified Boston crab, sitting across her back before pulling the stick upwards into her ribs. The crowd shared their feelings with a chorus of booing as Aimz desperately tried to drag herself across the canvas, pulling the weight of Kinkade with her as she did. A huge roar from the crowd erupted as she clasped a hand on the bottom rope... urging the referee to prise Kinkade away from her, giving Aimz enough time to drag herself under the ropes to temporary safety. Temporary being the key word. Kinkade was quickly back upon her... shoving the official to the canvas and stepping through the ropes onto the canvas, drawing back the cane and measuring yet another shot to Aimz as she tried to stand. Kinkade leapt but this time it was Aimz who had his number... bringing up a forearm straight into his groin as he landed. Another explosion of cheers went through the arena as Kinkade fell to the arena floor in agony. "Now there's a surprise... there is something down their!" Aimz snarled as she staggered up to her feet and snatched the cane from Kinkade's hand. The Immortal champion slowly staggered up to his feet still clutching his nether regions and grimacing as Aimz drew back the cane. "And now we find out it there's anything up..." CRACK. "THERE!" Aimz cried out, blasting Kinkade across the skull with the cane and snapping it in the process. Kinkade fell back on the cold arena floor as a steady flow of crimson began to pour from a now open wound in his forehead. Aimz looked at the broken cane, frowned and then tossed it to the floor before moving a few steps to locate her Hardcore championship... if the cane was no longer useful, a title belt to the skull would certainly leave a lasting impression. Lifting the belt from the floor... Aimz took a grip on it and prepared to level the bloodied Kinkade as he once again struggled up to his feet but as had occurred in the match already, the tides were once again about to turn and momentums were about to shift. Aimz rushed forth but Kinkade spun to the side and grabbed a handful of her hair along with the waist of her pants... using the momentum she'd gathered and his weight to drive her rib first into the ring apron! "Ohhhhhhhhhh!" The crowd exclaimed as Aimz felt the brunt of the crushing blow... the bleeding Kinkade wasn't finished however, dragging her back before launching her into the steel ringsteps with authority. CLANG. Aimz hit hard... again rib first she connected with the steps and flew right over them to land on the arena floor in a heap as the steps clattered down beside her. Kinkade took a moment to wipe his own blood away from the one good eye he had before making his way around to Aimz and dragging her up by the hair once again. Just so that he could toss her rib first into the guardrail. the squeamish in front row turned away in disgust because on impact... Aimz slumped over the steel rail with blood dripping out of her mouth, a telltale sign that her rib damage had lead to bleeding internally. Kinkade grabbed her by the hair again and tossed her under the ropes, after rolling across the canvas she'd left a trail of blood behind her. Kinkade lifted up the ring apron and much to the dismay of those attending produced several objects... none of which painted a pretty picture as he tossed them into the ring. A chair. A table. Then finally... ...a roll of barbed wire. Satisfied, he slid back under the ropes as Aimz dragged herself toward the ropes in an attempt to get to her feet. As she did Kinkade went to work setting up the next five minutes of fun, he placed the table horizontally in one corner of the ring, before wedging the chair between the middle and top ropes of the other corner. But the main attraction was the one that had the crowd booing him more heavily than ever. He again snatched Aimz up... before holding her high above his head in a gorilla press and bringing her sickeningly down across his knee. Speaking of sickening, as Aimz lay flat out on the canvas... Kinkade went to work wrapping the coil of barbed wire around his own arm. The crowd watched on, many stunned into silence as he clambered up the ropes and positioned himself on the very top... pausing for a moment to flip everyone off before leaping through the air and connecting with a horrific barbed wire laced elbow drop from the top. Aimz was bleeding instantly as Kinkade got up to his feet and raised the barbed arm in the air... much of Kinkade's past was a mystery but there was no question that at some stage he'd become more than familiar with the concept of extreme wrestling. He continued to prove the point... leaving the champion with no time to recover as he lifted her up and draped her against the vertically leant table in the corner. Taking a few steps back... he rushed forth to deliver what would surely be the end of the match, another Killing Spree. CRASH. But if it was going to end anyone's match it would be Kinkade's, Aimz used the little strength that she had remaining to throw herself aside as Kinkade's momentum threw him not only shoulder first through the table, shattering it... but straight into the ringpost as well. "Holy Shit! Holy Shit!" The crowd exclaimed, as the bloodied Aimz lay face down on the canvas and the even more bloodied Kinkade hung a mangled wreck amongst table and ropes... the two remained down for several moments, neither showing signs of life until surprisingly Kinkade started to pull himself from the debris. Aimz had crawled across the ring, her sights set firmly on the steel chair stuck between the ropes. As she reached it Kinkade turned and spotted her... rushing forth again in an attempt to find a third Killing Spree but once again the result rendered was not that expected, Aimz caught him with a drop toe hold and his head cannoned into the chair... leaving a bloody imprint upon it. The challenger fell back to the canvas completely still, as Aimz saw her chance to shiny... wiping blood from her mouth she yanked the chair out from between the ropes and struggled up to the top rope with her, throwing herself off and positioning it under her leg in mid-air. CRACK. The sound of the steel chair coming down on Kinkade under the weight of Aimz... the two lay still on the canvas once more before Aimz finally mustered the strength to roll over and pin her newfound nemesis. 1! 2! 3! NO! NO!? The crowd sat in disbelief... Kinkade had somehow, someway kicked out of the pinfall... Aimz couldn't believe it either, pounding the canvas furiously with her fist before pushing up to her feet and grabbing the chair once more. "Get up you son of a bitch!" She roared as Kinkade struggled up to his feet in an extremely dazed state... taking aim, she unleashed a mighty chair shot which unfortunately provided no purchase whatsoever. Kinkade dropped to his knees and whipped his barbed wire wrapped wrist across her ribs... changing the complexion of the match once more. Aimz doubled over and Kinkade snatched the chair from her arms... before drawing back and snapping it into her hair with a merciless clatter, busting the seat panel right out of it and bloodying the Hardcore champion even further. She lay on the canvas a wreck... bleeding internally and externally and completely still. If Kinkade made the cover now it was over, which was why the crowd watched on in further shock as he fell to the canvas and rolled under the ropes, snatching up a microphone. "They say that only women bleed!" His manic voice roared as he stumbled back up the steps "But as you can see it isn't true... the stab wound in my hand, the deep cut on my head... visible proof. Tonight I put the theory to the test by busting every object within a mile radius across slutbag's head... women aren't the only ones that bleed but they do bleed none the less." It looked as though Kinkade had something more to say but he was quickly stunned as were those watching the events unfold... Aimz came from nowhere, call it instinct, call it desire, call it what you will. The hardcore champion got to her feet and rolled up Kinkade from behind... putting both feet up on the ropes. 1! 2! 3! "Going Under" by Evanescence hit and the crowd erupted like never before... somehow their champion had retained her title, but the celebrations were short lived. "Cut this female Linkin Park bullshit!" Kinkade roared as he staggered back up to his feet "Look at you idiots... jumping up and down with your deluded perceptions of victory! Look at her... LOOK! You call that victory? She sneaks up from behind and holds me down for three seconds and you consider that to be success? Rose tinted sunglasses folks... you all seem to be wearing them, there's an old saying about battles and wars that you could all do with looking into." The Jersey Devil ranted, berating the crowd maniacally. "Pathetic." He scoffed. A few moments of pacing around the downed champion followed, as Kinkade flicked back his blood-soaked hair and knelt beside Aimz. "I hope you can hear me bitch." He growled "Because I want to make something abundantly clear, these trials and tribulations are not about the stupid title belt that I could've taken from you two minutes ago, they aren't about winning wrestling matches or wars of words... they're about you... ...just you." Kinkade got to his feet, throwing the microphone into the crowd furiously before rolling under the ropes and snatching up both the Hardcore and Immortal title belts... he tossed the Hardcore strap over the ropes and it landed with a thud next to a champion who'd been victorious on the night but perhaps defeated in the bigger picture Kellen Kinkade had been painting. The Jersey Devil stomped furiously up the aisle leaving a furious set of fWo fans behind to look on at their downed heroin. It seemed as though the worst was yet to come.
