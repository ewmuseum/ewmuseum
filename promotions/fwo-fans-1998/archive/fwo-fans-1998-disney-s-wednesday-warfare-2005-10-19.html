TWO... TH---- NO!! That was close as Lindz was a half of a second away at becoming the new fWo World Heavyweight Champion. Lindz knew it was close as she stood to her feet and looked down at Vince. Jacobs slowly struggled to his feet as Troy looked on like a cobra ready to strike. SVJ turned around and Troy hooked the champ driving him into the mat with a double underhook face breaker. Lindz went for another cover on Vince. ONE... TWO... THR--- NO!! Another kickout by Vince, this time it was closer than the last time. Lindz stood up as the crowd cheered her. She knew one big move would end this match. Troy picked up Vince for a suplex attempt but Vince reversed it landing behind Troy. The champ quickly hooked Troy and drover down with the Falling Star. Now both the champ and the challenger were lying on the mat. Jacobs and Troy started to make it to their feet. Suddenly Codemaster came running down the ramp way with the Skull Hammer in hand. He hopped up onto the apron where Troy was getting to her feet. Jacobs saw what was happening out the corner of his eye as he held the referee in front of him. Codemaster took a swing at Lindz but she ducked and nailed CM off the apron with a big forearm shot. Jacobs sprung to his feet and was standing in the middle of the ring waiting for Lindz to turn around. She turned around and got caught with the Star Struck. Jacobs rolled over and hooked her leg for the pin. ONE... TWO... THREE... Jacobs pulled out the victory and retains the fWo World Heavyweight Title with a little help from CodeMaster who got involved. Lindz will be a little perturbed at what just happened.
