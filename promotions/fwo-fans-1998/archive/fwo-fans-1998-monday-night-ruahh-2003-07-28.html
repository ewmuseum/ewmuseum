Introduction With Meltdown over the roster of the fWo must now rebuild, reorganize, and try to build from the ashes. The chaotic battle royal has damaged friendships and once strong allies, built new and angry rivals, and in some cases formed disturbing new alliances. Kellen Kinkade has beaten the odds to win the main event, giving him an automatic title shot for every championship belt the fWo has. The fWo Survivor III contest came to a surprising end with the winning team the Uprising having a strong showing in the event... and the return of Donaven Winters has shocked fans and wrestlers alike.
