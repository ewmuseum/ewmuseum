Wednesday Warfare, 1 September 1999

1 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction It was late afternoon in Fort Myers. So this is how it is, this is how it always happens in the late afternoon. Screw your late afternoon. With my last 50 lira I purchased some true and honest Martinni; I took a pull from the bottle. It was good. It burned my mouth and felt good and warm going down my esophagus and into my stomach. From there it went to my kidneys and my bladder, and was good. I remembered then when I last saw Hemmingway who was still a damn fine writer. It was in Toulouse, France and we looked out the windows at the dark and gloomy streets and drank Martinni in the late afternoon. It was late afternoon and had been late afternoon for some time. Soon, the show would begin. Mad Trucker vs. Flying Frenchie vs. Surgeon The arena in Florida shook as fireworks exploded from the rafters, signalling the opening of the popular fWo Wednesday Warfare program. As the lights strobed and the music blared through the arena, a fifteen-feet high steel cage was lowered to the ring. While this happens, we take you back to two weeks ago where Surgeon "fixed" things and made everything copacetic; except there was one thing he couldn't fix... the aangry emotional feeling the Frenchie & Trucker had towards him. We return to the ring and The Surgeon begins to make his way out to a negative reaction from the crowd; as the cage is still being lowered in placed. He pauses midway down the ramp and grins cockily at the fans, but little does he know that Mad Trucker has snuck up behind him. Trucker spins him around, and kicks him in the midsection. Trucker scoops the smaller Surgeon up, and drapes him over his shoulder. Trucker charges at a semi-quick pace down the ramp, and rams the head of Surgeon into the cage several times. He then brings him up into the ring; and as Trucker does this, the familiar music of The Flying Frenchie begins to play. The Frenchie walks out beaming, and wastes no time in coming down to ringside. Trucker scoops Surgeon up, and powerbombs him. As he does so, The Flying Frenchie begins climbing up one side of the cage. Just as he reaches the top, he looks back at the crowd, and does a "throat slash" with his thumb. He then, as steadily as possible, stands on top of the cage. Trucker places Surgeon in the corner, and then gives the "go ahead" to Frenchie. Before Frenchie does, Trucker yells out "Kids, PLEASE don't try this at home!" as Frenchie prepares. Frenchie finally dives off from the cage, hitting a 450 splash and damn near going through the Surgeon and the ring. Frenchie clutches himself, showing signs of some pain as the crowd remains silent in awe. Trucker scoops Surgeon up with ease, as Frenchie just took the air out of him... literally. Trucker waits for Frenchie to get up, before he and Frenchie toss Surgeon over the top of the cage into the crowd. The referee signals for the bell by default, as Surgeon wins. Frenchie and Trucker don't mind however, as they finally received revenge for what Surgeon did the last few months. Frenchie and Trucker eye each other carefully as Trucker leaves first. A loud, "TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" is echoed through the crowd as Trucker shakes hands, kisses babies and makes friends on his way to the back. Frenchie, meanwhile, waits in the ring, beaming of his latest accomplishment. It seemed however, that Frenchie had something of greater importance on his mind.. and he appeared to be in deep contemplation about the matter. Result: Mad Trucker and Flying Frenchie Lego Machina's Ego In a taped interview, Matt Loco caught up with traveling superstar Machina for a few words in the San Francisco airport. Matt Loco asked Machina how it felt to be considered one of the best talents in the world. Machina looked at Matt Loco for a moment with distain, and then began to speak. "The term 'best' is misused all too often in wrestling... in fact, so misused that I believe it is perhaps the biggest problem in the game today. People are obsessed with becoming the best. They want the glory, the fame, the respect. They wrestle me, they try and get a rub... but what does it get them? An oversized ego... a cocky attitude that makes them believe that they are the best, and thus they deserve the best. Look at me for example. I'm Los Latin, the greatest wrestler who ever lived. Why, my skills alone make all these pitiful American slobs look like children in a man's game. But I'm not egotistical about it. Why, you could say that I'm the most humble man that has ever lived... and some people have. In fact, I've gotten several awards for being the most humble. But I don't say I'm the best... because what does it mean? I mean, certainly I'm the biggest innovator of moves and holds in the world, but I don't brag about it. Those that do have egos... it goes to their head. When I was awarded the honor of being the... excuse me... EIGHTY FIFTH best tag team around... well... that just goes to show what an incredible talent I happen to be, but I don't have an ego about it. I mean, that's just wrong." Matt Loco scratched his head at this, as Machina and recently reunited Sid left the interview to get on a plane. Matt shrugged and murmured, "Man... what an asshole." Mike Bear, Falcon, Wretch vs. Black Quicksilver, Wolf Fang, Jackhammer The Underground came out led by Falcon, who appeared to be contemplating something and not really concerned with the fans. Mike Bear and Wretch took time to exchange pleasantries with the fans, however. Falcon slid into the ring and as his name was announced, he rolled his eyes before slumping into the corner. He then sat back up and told Wretch he could start out the match, that he wasn't feeling well. Falcon went and sat on the ring apron as Black Quicksilver (w/ STUMP!) and Ground Zero made their way out. Wolf Fang and Jackhammer shook hands with the fans, while Black Quicksilver shook STUMP in the air and started up a STUMP chant. BQ slid into the ring and he and Wretch started things off. BQ & Wretch literally tore down the house, with Wretch showing off some offensive skills, executing a tiltawhirl flying headscissors, a hurracarana from the top rope, and a missle dropkick. BQ got the advantage and tagged in Jackhammer, who came in and used his size and strength advantage to work Wretch over. Wretch tagged out to Bear, who came in and brawled for a bit with the big man. Jackhammer was forced to tag out to Wolf Fang after a shot to the midsection by Bear, however. Bear tagged in Falcon, and Falcon came in looking disinterested. Wolf Fang, shrugged, and "laid down" for Falcon... but Falcon had no idea what was going on, and refused to pin him... before tagging out to Bear. Bear came in and went for a cover, but Wolf Fang wrapped him up into a small package for the surprising three count. Falcon, Bear & Wretch left as a unit, with Bear & Wretch wondering what was wrong with Falcon. As Jackhammer and Wolf Fang faced the crowd in celebration, BQ shook STUMP high above the air in the middle of the ring. Courage came out from behind through the crowd and attacked BQ. He chopped blocked him, before whipping him into the turnbuckles, and hitting him with a very weak reverse knife edge chop. Courage then spat at BQ, and left the ring in a hurry while Jackhammer and Wolf Fang finally turned around to check on BQ. Warfare went to a preview for "Fallout", before proceeding to a commercial break. Result: Black Quicksilver, Wolf Fang, Jackhammer by Pinfall Scott Slugger Wants Answers Backstage, Scott Slugger stormed the arena, looking for the commissioner of the fWo. Behind him was Ice, who carried both the fWo World Heavyweight and Internet titles in his massive arms. They found the office of the commissioner, and pounded on the door until it swung open. Inside sat Travis, tired and weary, looking over a large stack of papers. Slugger started to speak, but his voice soon advanced to a scream. "I'm tired of this shit, Travis! Who in the hell was that last week? Huh? Three men, three names. I want them... and I want them NOW!" The commissioner looked up at the fWo World Heavyweight champion and held his arms wide. "I have no idea of who they are. We haven't signed any new talent... and if anyone who isn't signed with the company shows up they will be removed from the arena at once." Slugger stared hard at the commissioner from another moment, and then nodded and left the room as violently as he entered. Travis watched them go, and smiled a wry smile. "That's right... haven't signed any NEW talent..." Waru vs. Chameleon The cameras came back to the ring where the Chameleon stood, dwarfed by the massive slab of man-meat behind him, the Total Eclipse. The Chameleon flexed for the audience, who responded with bored booing, and the Total Eclipse belched, shaking tiles from the roof and causing them to fall on members of the crowd. Even angrier, the crowd began to toss garbage into the ring. The Chameleon, unthwarted, grabbed a leaf blower from under the ring and blew the garbage back at the crowd, laughing. Waru came out of the crowd, ducking soda bottles and boxes and dove into the ring. Waru quickly ducked to the side as the Chameleon attempted a spear. Waru punched downward into the back of the Chameleon as he passed, and picked the Chameleon up from the side and snap suplexed him over the back and down to the mat. Waru hung back and waited for the Chameleon to get to his feet, and then charged with a running drop kick. Landing it, Waru attempted a cover, but was only able to get a two count. Waru got to his feet and grabbed the wrestler in a side headlock. Waru worked the headlock in tighter, trying to cut off the oxygen of the wrestler. The Chameleon began to fade, and finally dropped down to the canvas helpless. The ref checked the am of the Chameleon, but Waru stopped him, mentioning that he didn't want the match to end that way. Waru then climbed to the top rope, and dove backward with an inverted moonsault that found its target. Waru covered the Chameleon, and easily got the three count. Pointing at the cameras, Waru told the crowd that Wretch was next.... Result: Waru by Pinfall Number 7 in the dark Out in the crowd cameras found former Denver Bronco and two-time Super Bowl winner John Elway sitting in attendance. Wearing a "UV" t-shirt and helping his boy with a soda, John Elway seemed to be enjoying himself. Matt Loco neared to get a word with the celebrity, and John Elway stood to greet him. John Elway explained that he was a big fan of the fWo, and that he had been talking with the fWo executive committee about making some kind of special guest appearance in the ring at some point in the future. Before Matt Loco could follow things up, Darkness appeared on the scene and grabbed John Elway by the shirt. Threatening him, Darkness had to be restrained by fWo officials before he did further damage to the former Super Bowl champion. As the scuffle was contained, Wednesday Warfare went to a commercial... The Choke Artist vs. Boston Strangler The arena goes dark, and dark, foreboding music starts playing. A man clad in black leather slowly makes his way to the ring with a microphone. Upon entering the ring, the lights come back on, revealing Crazy Mike Heftel. He speaks: "The world.....is a dark place. And I....am the master.....and the ruler of the darkness. Many men.....in the f....W....o....claim to be evil....claim to be dark....but in truth....none may compare....to The Choke Artist! Tonight....I'm going to teach somebody a lesson....and I can think of no better place to start....than Scott Hart. C'mon out here." "Hot For Teacher" starts playing, but no one comes out. After a minute or so of waiting, the Travis-Tron reveals Scott Hart laid out backstage. The lights go out again, and this time "O Fortuna" plays, much to the delight of the crowd. The Boston Strangler rushes the ring, and immediately Heftel retreats to the corner, his hands in the classic "not in the face" position. The Strangler looked down at Heftel, and extended a hand to help up the frightened competitor. Heftel grasped the hand, and the Strangler at once flung him to his feet and began to beat on Heftel using his own arm. Heftel squacked in horror as the Strangler continued to beat on him, pounding his torso and head. Finally the Strangler picked Heftel up, and held him over his head for an extended period of time... and then dropped him to the ground with a dull thud. Placing a foot on his chest, the Strangler got the three count and left the ring with the same maniacal grin he had when he entered. He started up the entrance ramp, but then stopped in his tracks when he spotted the Flying Frenchie and Mitch Wilson standing at the top of the ramp. The Frenchie and Wilson observed the Boston Strangler for a short time, and then vanished into the backstage area... Result: Boston Strangler by Pinfall Fallout Announcement Chris Finn got into the ring, and addressed the crowd. He first congratulated Primetime on his victory at Meltdown, and then announced that the next fWo Pay Per View was fWo: Fallout, and that it would feature the final and semi-final matches of a 36-man tournament to determine the first-ever winner of the fWo "Wrestler of the Year" tournament. This tournament would be not only important because it was the first of it's kind, but also in that it would crown the "Wrestler of the Millennium". Chris Finn then unfolded a piece of paper, and announced that the following wrestlers had been selected to participate in the tournament: Black Panther, Boston Strangler, Black Quicksilver, Cactus Jon, Chris Universal, Crucifix, Courage, Darkness, Eron, Fallen, Flying Frenchie, Ice, Joy, Lance Star, Kid Cool, Machina, Mad Trucker, Matthew Zhuk, Mike Bear, Oracle, Primetime, Rain, Scott Slugger, Death, Speed Demon, Stevie Roberts, Stone, Ultra Violet, VL, War, Wolf Fang and Wretch. Chris Finn announced that the tournament brackets would be released on September 2nd on fWowrestling.com. With that, the crowd readied themselves for the main event... Cactus Jon vs. Black Panther Cactus Jon made his way out carrying a ladder, but still looked quite bruised after the attacks by Fallen at Meltdown & Slaughter. Jon slid the ladder into the ring under the bottom rope, before rolling underneath the bottom rope on the other side of the ring. Jon then propped the ladder up in the corner, and awaited his opponent, the former fWo Internet Champion. The Black Panther made his way out to an ovation of cheers by the fWo crowd, and Panther looked like a man on a mission as he made his way to the ring. Panther asked for a microphone as he got down to ringside, and received one. He then informed Jon that "whatever is going on between you and Fallen, is just that, between you and Fallen. Don't bring me into your mess. I only want two things, to win here tonight and to get back my Internet Title. Right now, you're the only thing in the way of me getting what I want. I'd like to keep it that way. Tell Fallen now to stay out of our business." Jon complied, and the match began. After several minutes of wrestling, Panther was the first one to use the ladder. Panther hit a nicely executed spinebuster in the middle of the ring, and then Panther scooped up the ladder, and dropped it on the arm of Jon. Panther followed it up by propping the ladder back up into the corner, and then Irish Whipping Jon into it. Jon however reversed it, and Panther went crashing chest first into the steps. Panther fell back onto the mat, and Jon took control. He drove the top of the ladder into the knee of the Panther, before gorilla pressing the ladder, and simply dropping it onto Panther. Panther writhed in pain as Jon looked down at him in pleasure. Jon continued his assault, until finally he climbed up onto the top of the ladder. Jon flew off with a splash, but Panther moved out of the way. Panther placed the ladder in the corner while Jon laid sprawled out on the mat, rolling about clutching various body parts. Panther brought Jon to his feet, and then hooked him for a suplex. Instead of suplexing him, he propped him up in another turnbuckle. He then, climbed up to the second rope, hooked Jon for the superplex, and stepped up to the top rope after launching Jon into the air. He and Jon flew off into the air and both men crashed onto the mat, but Panther still wasn't done. Panther took the ladder and positioned it for a suplex. He then lightning quick-like suplexed the ladder onto Jon, before pinning Jon underneath the ladder, with the weight of his body holding Jon and the ladder down. The referee counted, and Panther walked away the victor. Panther slid out of the ring and up into the crowd, celebrating the victory with his fans. Meanwhile, Jon attempted to recover from the match. Suddenly, Dina came out dragging Fallen on a leash. Fallen looked as if someone had literally reached inside him, grabbed his emotions and pulled them out of his body before hiding them. A blank stare was across his face, until Dina instructed him to attack Jon. Fallen slid into the ring and begin clawing at Jon, and biting at Jon' fingers. He then stomped at Jon' head repeatedly, before grabbing the ladder and driving the top of it into Jon' crotch. He then lifted Jon up and piledrived him on the ladder, before several officials convinced Fallen to back off. Dina restrained Fallen with a look of pleasure written in cursive across her face, and she appeared to be almost beaming. Fallen sulked back into a blank stare, with no emotion whatsoever. It appeared he hadn't slept in days, and wasn't going to for some time. Dina took Fallen up onto the stage before patting his head one more time, for good measure. As Warfare went off the air we were left with the image of dozens of kind people from the medical staff checking on Jon's condition... Result: Black Panther by Pinfall
