Inside the ring, Mitch and BQ had stopped at the sound of the fireworks, and now BQ snuck up behind Wilson and caught him with a low blow, doubling him over. BQ hit the ropes and came off with a bulldog. Down the ramp, Jon Crisp made his way to the ring, then hopped up onto the apron. On the opposite side of the ring, Ice was up and on the apron as well, and the matchup was finally underway. BQ made the cover on Wilson, but got just a count of one. BQ brought Wilson up to his feet again and caught him with a forearm, then another. BQ set Wilson up for a whip into the ropes, but Wilson reversed it. BQ came off and ducked underneath an attempted running clothesline from Wilson. Wilson let his momentum carry him into the ropes, and as he came off, Ice slapped his back, making a blind tag. BQ came off the ropes and leapt, then cracked Wilson with a leaping forearm shot to the jaw, knocking him down. At ringside, Slade Wilson grimaced. BQ got up - and was spun around by Ice. Ice picked him up in a gorilla press position, then held him in the air... held him... held him... Then was chop-blocked to the knee by his own partner, Mitch Wilson. Ice hit the floor, and BQ came down on top of him, immediately hooking the leg for a cover. One, two - Mitch broke up the count with an elbowdrop. Mitch shoved BQ aside, then brought Ice up to his feet and nailed him with a leaping leg lariat. There was no incentive for Wilson and Ice to work together in this match, as they were essentially competing for a title shot, and that lack of incentive was quickly coming into play. Wilson brought Ice up, and Ice shoved him backwards. Ice lashed out with a martial arts kick - And at the same time, BQ caught Wilson from behind with a spinning heel kick. The two shots connected with Wilson's head at the same time, causing him to crumple to the mat and causing Jon Crisp to grimace in the corner in physical sympathy. Wilson rolled from the ring, groggy, and Ice quickly caught BQ and shoved him into a corner. Ice unloaded on BQ with a knee to the gut, then another, another, another, driving the air out of BQ. Ice then set him up and sent him hard across the ring into the corner. Ice charged across - And BQ moved. Ice slammed into the corner, then stumbled out, facing across the ring. BQ charged up behind him and took him down with a Cubic Zirconium Cutter. BQ rolled Ice over - but made the mistake of being within reach of his partner. Jon Crisp tagged himself in. BQ made the cover anyway, making a mistake by leaving himself in range of a legdrop to the head from the World Champion, drawing a big pop from the crowd. Crisp picked Ice up and gave him a stiff forearm, then another. He then hit the ropes. Crisp tried to come off with a lariat - Ice ducked underneath it. Crisp turned - and Ice nearly took his head off with a kick. Ice grinned, then dropped down to make the cover. One. Two. Broken up - from two angles. BQ caught Ice with a seated dropkick, and at the same time, Mitch Wilson springboarded into the ring and caught Ice with an elbowsmash on the way down. Mitch brought Ice up and went to work on him, and BQ did the same on Crisp. The two pairs of enemies began to brawl, and the referee quickly lost control of the action as the so-called "partners" tried to beat the living hell out of each other. In one corner, Ice drove a shoulder into Wilson's gut, then backed up and went for another. Wilson used the ropes to lift himself up, then leapt forward, landing behind Ice. Ice turned - and just like that, found himself on the receiving end of a Headliner. The crowd popped for the finisher. On the other side of the ring, Jon Crisp chopped the hell out of Black Quicksilver. James Sunderland, who had returned to the ringside area, leapt onto the apron, drawing the referee's attention with his protest of what had been done to Ice. Slade Wilson saw the opportunity, gesturing to Mitch and withdrawing a pair of brass knucks. Mitch nodded and grabbed Crisp from behind by both arms. He pulled Crisp towards the ropes, and Slade Wilson stepped onto the apron and swung. Crisp freed himself - and Slade's knucks found his son's face. Crisp caught Slade with a shot to the head, knocking him from the apron and sending his knucks flying. Crisp raised his hands in triumph to the crowd, which cheered - then gasped. Black Quicksilver had caught the flying knucks - and he used them to nearly decapitate the fWo World Champion. Crisp fell to the mat, and the crowd erupted in boos. BQ grabbed Crisp and dragged him to his corner, then stepped out onto the apron and shouted at the ref. The ref turned, and BQ reached in and tagged himself in. He then sprinted across to the opposite corner, which put him in perfect position across from the legal man, Ice. BQ stood on the top rope... then leapt. Silver Splash. Connected. As the crowd rained down boos, BQ stayed on top of Ice and hooked the leg. The ref made the count. One. Two. Three. More boos, and BQ's hand was raised. He grinned beneath his mask, having pulled one out of nowhere to retain his rightful title shot. The crowd continued to jeer and express their displeasure - then the jeers became cheers. The FanTron had come to life. And Scott Slugger's face was plastered on it. Slugger let his lips jerk into a slight smirk as he saw BQ's reaction in the ring, with BQ's not-fit-for-the-FCC thoughts being clear to anyone who could read lips. Slugger waited for the crowd reaction to die down, then spoke. "BQ, my man. Way to go - you just screwed three guys at once. I'd say that it's probably not the first time, but gay jokes are just so passe. Anyway, I hate to rain on your little parade of jobber-to-the-stars joy at keeping your title shot, but the WGA has an announcement to make that's vastly more important than anything that could ever issue out of your pointy little head. As we all know, between now and CyberSlam is a little show called... Unleashed. It's Unleashed: Final Fight this time, and that's just a little bit appropriate, because you see, Unleashed is going to be Jon Crisp's final fight as fWo World Champion. Why, you ask? The answer's quite simple... At Unleashed, Jon Crisp will be defending the fWo World Title... Against me." The crowd erupted at this proclamation, and BQ threw up his hands in disbelief. Slugger let the full smirk out this time, and as the noise finally dropped to a level where he could be heard, he continued. "You see, the fWo management saw that I did lose a #1 Contender match to BQ over there - yes, that's true - but they realized that there were extenutating circumstances. The WGA had already been booked that night, and he had already wrestled against both Vic Creed and Mitch Wilson. Now, I know that the more Scott Slugger there is in one broadcast, the better it is for everyone. The better for ratings, for buyrates, for the fans in attendance, for the men at home who wish they could be me... for the women at home who wish they could be with me... the better for everybody! But as great as the WGA is, it's a tough task for even me to wrestle twice in a night. So the fWo decided that it would be perfectly fair to give ME the World Title shot at Unleashed. And frankly, I couldn't agree with them more. It's been too long since I've had the big gold belt around my waist... and it's been too long since I've been where I truly belong. The main event at CyberSlam." A huge pop. "So Jon Crisp... I'll see you at Unleashed... And it will not be your pleasure! Oh, and BQ... sucks to be you, my man." Slugger continued to smirk, and the FanTron faded to its usual view of the goings-on in the ring. At that particular location right now, Black Quicksilver's eyes were bulging practically out of his mask. He rolled out of the ring and grabbed a chair, then slammed it into the ring apron, did it again, did it a third time, screaming in disbelief and frustration.
