Sunday Slaughter, 21 October 2001

21 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Survivor II Fans shuffled in chairs nervously, anxious about the upcoming show. It was the last Slaughter before the big October pay-per-view Halloween Hell and fans expected a nerve racking show filled with the usual twists and turns that always came before the big events. It was like the calm before the storm, and the feeling inside the arena was electric. Above them on the overhead monitors images of the events leading up to tonight's Slaughter and the pay-per-view streamed endlessly over the screens. The crowd was just starting to get frustrated with the lack of action when the lights dimmed and ring announcer Matt Panzer slowly made his way down the entrance ramp to scattered applause. As he climbed into the ring and swung beneath the ropes the crowd fell silent, anxiously awaiting what would come next. "Ladies and gentlemen," Matt's voice boomed over the crowd and he nodded with appreciation as the fans in attendance gave him their full attention. "Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome at this time special fWo goodwill ambassador Stevie Roberts!" The theme from the first two Cyberslam events played as Stevie Roberts walked slowly out onto the entrance ramp, beaming and waving to fans in attendance. Stevie was dressed in a new tuxedo which looked slightly more classy than what fans were used to seeing him in, but Stevie appeared comfortable as he neared the ring, slid inside and clapped Panzer good naturedly on the back. Taking the microphone from him, Stevie again waved to the fans and beamed at the cameras and lights. "Groovy! Well, hello fWo fans, and so glad to see everyone tonight. As you all know, we are just six days away from October's big Halloween Hell pay-per-view event. This event promises to be bigger than you can imagine, with plenty of shocks and surprises... including the return of two mystery legends from the fWo! Yeah, I know... you're all hoping it's going to be old Stevie here, but I'm sorry to disappoint you ladies out there... I'm retired. You're going to see some big title matches, a few faces you thought you would never see, and much, much more." Stevie glanced up at the overhead monitors with a wry smile and shrugged. "But you already know that, right? You've seen the ads, and you've already ordered the pay-per-view, right?" The crowd cheered at his words in response, and Stevie smiled while he waited for it to settle down. "Okay, okay... but as exciting as that is, I'm here for another, greater reason tonight. You might have heard about this little contest we were running... a little contest involving sixteen people?" The crowd cheered again as several signs were waved in the crowd with the word 'Survivor' written on them. Stevie nodded toward them, and pointed them out for the cameras. "That's right, we are just one week away from the second fWo Survivor contest, and unlike that other contest out there we aren't going to be asking anyone to boil water, hunt pigs or fall into fire. No, we're going to take sixteen lucky wrestlers and give all of them a chance to go for the glory... to be a Survivor winner like last year's winner Ruben Ross. The winner will get a chance to get into the fWo... but will also have the bragging rights of making it through one of the most brutal and intense contests in history. So after receiving thousands of applications and going through each and every one of them... I'd like to thank all of you for taking the time to apply. You're all winners, baby!" The crowd cheered more loudly yet, and Stevie clapped along with them. As the noise began to settle down, he withdrew a slip of paper from his pocket, unfolded it, and held it out in front of him. "But now it is time to announce the sixteen finalists. You sixteen have an awesome responsibility before you. You carry the torch for every fan out there who wanted to wrestle in the fWo. You carry the burden of voting your comrades out one by one. You must endure the nervous torture of each elimination round. You must all work together, yet sacrifice each other out. Without teamwork you will fail, but without self preservation you will be eliminated. I envy all of you... but at the same time I wouldn't want to be in your shoes right now. So with that, I will announce our finalists." Stevie cleared his throat as the crowd surrounding the ring fell deathly silent. The large screens fell black as they waited the first few names to be called. "The first team of eight will be represented by the color of crimson. They will bear the team name The Riders in honor of our first large stable in the fWo. While the Dark Riders were a heel faction, the Survivor team will fight to win contests and stay alive through teamwork and unity. This team is represented by the following: Brand Frontier, The Magi, Matt Rivers, Lance Knight, Shen Lung, The Wildman of Lagos, Jessica Arrows and Martyr. Good luck to you all, and make your fellows and all of us proud of you." A large round of applause followed this announcement as the names of each of the Surviving team members appeared on the overhead screens. Several signs waved, and Stevie nodded to each in turn. "The second team of eight will be represented by the color of slate blue. They will bear the team name Gunmen in honor of one of the longest and most beloved teams in the fWo. While the Lone Gunmen were a group of misfits drawn together by a common cause, so too shall these individuals need to bond in order to survive a series of intensive challenges from within and without. This team is represented by the following: Golden Glen Miller, Aren Archer, Xaxon, John Mammoth, The Great Dragon, Graphic Violence, Grendel and... er..." Stevie turned to Matt Panzer and pointed to a name. There was some whispering as Stevie raised an eyebrow at the final name on the list. Matt nodded and Stevie shrugged. "And the final man in the Survivor contest, a Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman. Good luck to you, and live up to your namesake." Stevie cleared his throat, folded the piece of paper back into his pocket and smiled out at the crowd. "These sixteen participants will now be given one week to get their affairs in order, prepare for the contest, and ensure that all necessary information has been submitted to the fWo. We will be contacting you all tonight with additional information. Once again good luck to you all, and let the game begin. After a short break now, Sunday Slaughter will get underway. Enjoy the show, all of you!" The screen flashed the sixteen names once more as Stevie left the ring. Brand Frontier, The Magi, Matt Rivers, Lance Knight, Shen Lung, The Wildman of Lagos, Jessica Arrows, Martyr, Golden Glen Miller, Aren Archer, Xaxon, John Mammoth, The Great Dragon, Graphic Violence, Grendel and Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman. The crowd cheered and applauded loudly, anxious for the show and the contest to begin in earnest. Cheese & Crackers A sudden knock at Donaven Winters' door roused him from his weary contemplation and he opened it to find Tempest standing there, with a bright smile and a plate full of various kinds of crackers. He looked down at the plate of crackers and the frown across his face deepened. "What are these for?" he asked, already knowing the answer. "The cheese!" Tempest said in a bubbly tone. "What a great snacking opportunity. You are gonna share aren't you?" She gazed at him with a puppy eyed glance. Winters looked down on her smiling face and slowly pushed the plate of crackers onto the floor. "The cheese is gone, go find the dumpster out in St. Louis if you want to have snacks. I'm sorry Tempest, but I'm not really in the mood for anything tonight." Tempest looked at the crackers, which Trouble was now happily scarfing down and then pouted back up at Winters. "But... come on, haven't you ever heard that expression about making lemonade when life hands you lemons? Well, when your enemy hands you cheese...bring crackers!" She waved her hands in exasperation. A small smile cracked across Winters' face. "Yeah... I know," he began. "Maybe some other time, all right? The past few weeks just haven't been going very well for me. It's just not the best thing for me right now. I'm sorry." Tempest chuckled and said, "Oh all right. But I still say you'd have an easier time of things if ya learned to laugh once in awhile. I think it's the only thing keeping me sane right now." The smile faded from Winters' face and he looked right into Tempest's eyes. "I've always learned never to get my hopes to high," he stated. "That way, when I get let down, it doesn't bother me, I move on. If I laughed a little more, I'd be taking things too seriously, know what I mean?" Tempest scratched her head. "I don't think so. All I know is that no matter what they try to do to me... and they've been trying a lot lately... I have to laugh. If I stop, they win." Winters put a hand on Tempest's shoulder. "Whatever works for us," he said reassuringly. "I hate to break this off, but I need to prepare for my match with Burns. It... it was nice talking to you." Tempest nodded and said, "Good luck. Hey, just act like you're gonna pick his pockets. That'll throw him off." She started off down the hall and wheeled briefly, laughing and said, "Haha! Made ya smile!" Creed entered the ring and grabbed Fate by the neck, before taking him out with an impressive one handed chokeslam. Creed looked down at Fate; before telling Destiny that "was for her." Creed went for the cover, but from behind Ross attacked with a dropkick from behind. Creed went toppling into the turnbuckles backfirst. Ross backed up, and sprinted... jumping first over Fate' body, then over the fallen Chronos, he leaped with a leglariat, and connected. As Creed staggered out from the corner, Ross grabbed him and took him out with a brainbuster. He followed it with a pinfall attempt. One. Two. No, Chronos broke up the pin, driving an elbow into the back of Ross. He then grabbed Ross and disposed of him over the top rope, before turning his attention to Fate, who was coming to in the ring. Chronos kicked him in the stomach, but Fate caught his foot, spun him, and pushed him into Creed, who was attempting to get to his feet. Chronos & Creed collided, and when Chronos came back out Fate kicked him in the stomach, before going for the Twist Of Fate. This time, Chronos pushed him into the referee. The referee and Fate collided; with the referee taking most of the damage and flying through the ropes to the floor. Chronos grabbed Fate and took him out with the Chronosphere. Chronos had the cover, but the referee was out. Not knowing that he had taken the referee out, Chronos stood and looked around the ring, before spotting him on the floor. Chronos went out to wake him up, as he had the match won in the ring. Chronos slapped the referee a few times lightly, attempting to revive him. From behind however came Ross, wielding a chair. Chronos turned, and Ross introduced him to the steel. Ross then slid into the ring, and as Creed was preparing to inflict damage on Fate, Ross leveled him with a chair three or four times, but Creed wouldn't go down. A fifth time sent Creed to the mat. Pleased with his effort, Ross hit an asai moonsault. The referee began to regain consciousness, and was climbing into the ring. Ross hooked the leg. The referee struggled... to slowly... count the pin. One. Two. Three... No. Creed amazingly got a shoulder up, and a stunned Ross brought Creed to his feet. He swung him to the ropes, but a now bloody Creed reversed it. Creed ducked for a backdrop, and Ross stopped and positioned him for a piledriver. Fate began climbing to his feet, and as Ross went for the move, Fate decided to help him, payback for the earlier chokeslam. Fate grabbed Creed by the legs, and together they drove KVC into the mat with a spike piledriver... ironically, on the chair. Chronos was now coming to and as he climbed up the stairs, Fate saw him. Fate charged and connected with a spinning heelkick, which sent Chronos off the ring apron. Chronos hit the floor, but quickly got back up enraged. Chronos should have stayed down, as Fate came once again, this time over the top rope with a suicide plancha. Fate & Chronos rolled around on the floor, exchanging fists of hatred, while back in the ring, Ross had Creed covered for the pin. The referee began to count once again. One. Two. No. Creed shouldered out, and Ross wondered what he had to do to put the big man away. Ross fought to get the title from underneath the big man. He then kneeled over Creed and forearmed him several times, before backing up and driving knee after repeated knee into the face of Creed. Finally, he positioned him in a corner, before laying the chair atop the Internet Champion. Ross signalled he was going up top, and assuredly, this could mean only one thing. Black Sabbath. How appropiate. Ross connected with the shooting star frogsplash. Although he was now bleeding out of his mouth after hitting the move, Ross didn't care. One of them would be bleeding next Saturday at Halloween Hell, and he was going to ensure it wouldn't be him. Ross quickly crawled over and hooked the leg, while Chronos & Fate were now fighting on the ramp. Back in the ring, the referee counted. One. Two. Three. The fWo had a new Internet Champion, and Ross was no stranger to the title as he was the longest reigning INET Champion in history. The referee handed Ross the title, and he stood over a bloody Creed... a bitter, yet proud champion. Ross raised the title into the air, and just as a new Survivor was beginning, this Survivor was determined that no matter how many Survivor's the fWo had, he would always be the original. Ross took to a corner and held the title, while Chronos & Fate disappeared into the back, embroiled in a bitter fight. Fireworks exploded throughout the arena, and 'Black Plague' was once again... champion. Slaughter appropiately faded to black with this image. Halloween Hell II was six days away.
