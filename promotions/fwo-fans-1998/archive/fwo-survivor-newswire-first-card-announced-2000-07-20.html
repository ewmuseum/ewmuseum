First Card Announced

20 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE FIRST CARD ANNOUNCED July 20, 2000 The card which had been widely speculated to be released exactly one day before the first fWo Survivor wrestling event was released to news outlets at 3PM EST. The card features all twelve of the wrestlers in single pairings and has no immediate surprises. fWo Coordinator Stevie Roberts confirmed the validity of the released card. "This is the first week and first show of Survivor. What we wanted to do is to put out an exhibition style card featuring everyone. This is only the first show, so as I reported earlier there will be no immunity gained from this card. Next show the immunity challenge is officially up for grabs in what will be a more unique card." When pressed for details about what the second show would hold, Stevie Roberts refused comment. Many speculate that the second card will feature a battle royal between the remaining eleven members, with the winner being the first to have immunity from the voting process. While this has not been confirmed, fWo officials are warning the San Jose audience that the second show which will be broadcast from their location will feature only one match. Thus the conclusion has been that the one match will in fact be a Royal Rumble or Battle Royal style event. The wrestlers involved in the Survivor contest are simply happy to see the next step moving closer. Several expressed relief at finally seeing the card, stating that they had been "running blind" in terms of challenges and interviews. One wrestlers involved in the contest who did not wish to identify himself said, "It's about time. I didn't know who I was facing, and when the stakes are this high I like to have more information. All of us would. You're going to see some of the initial stress evaporate now as people focus on their first match". While wrestlers may be feeling more secure with the knowledge of the upcoming card, most are still nervous about the first voting period. Voting will take place directly following the event, and the wrestler voted out of the contest will be notified around 5PM EST the following day. "It's going to be rough to be voted out, but the person who is first out has a lot to be proud of." said Stevie Roberts. "The person voted out first was voted out because the other wrestlers saw him as a threat. That means the first voted out should be the one people fear the most. If you're going to go out, I can't think of a more ego boosting way." Several protest organizations are rumored to be on hand in Seattle to protest the fWo and the Survivor contest. Some groups feel that the nature of the contest is unfair emotionally and will cause damage to the self esteem of wrestlers involved. Other groups are protesting the event due to a lack of gender and racial balancing. More news as it comes available.
