Introduction A few short days ago, the fWo Survivor IV contest made history by putting on an original show dedicated only to Survivor. Both teams battled hard, but in the end the Gold Team was sent to the elimination chamber. Tonight, one member of the Gold Team will be eliminated and immunity is once more up for grabs... Prelude "They ready for this? To lose one of their own?" Frank shrugged and continued to look through a series of documents. He wasn't finding what he was looking for, and Brian's concerns were little more than an annoying hum at this point. "We've been over this before," said Frank, "it doesn't matter if they are ready or not. They are in the situation, like it or not, and tonight is the first test to see if they understand the bigger game or if they are going to be the usual whining fools and walk away when the future is right within their grasp. Some of them watched the last show I'm sure... they at least know that there is always a chance that they can get brought back with..." "I thought the Black group was..." But Frank looked up sharply and cut off Brian with a flick of the hand. "Quiet. Not yet. Besides, that's just the near target, not the end goal. Remember that. Yeah, one of them gets voted out tonight. One of them is going home... or isn't. That's up to them, isn't it? But make it clear that it's the winner that gets the contract into the fWo. We aren't running a fucking charity case here, we're running..." "...something bigger," finished Brian. "You're getting repetitive." "In this business," said Frank Smyth, "you have to be." Terror-Stricken It was only a few days ago when the first Survivor IV show aired and to 'The Fan' Brock Tyler, it was the worst night of his life. For just about everyone else, everything seemed fine. However, Brock was not happy, in fact, he was still humiliated, and was scared to go out into public. This was the reason he found himself sitting in front of a television set, in a small hotel room, watching Countdown two, from 1999. But before the video could start, there was a knock at the door. Worried, Brock slowly peaked out the window. He let out a deep breath after realizing it was Rana Venenosa, or as he knew him, Mathew. Brock removed the chain from the door and unlocked the deadbolt, just as he opened the door Matt pushed his way through, and quickly slammed the door shut behind him. "Whew, can't get any piece and quiet anywhere these days. The press are hounding me for a story." he said walking over to the bed and lying down, "Mind if I just relax here for a little while, I was needing to write my mom a letter anyways." Brock walked over to the television and picked up the remote, "No man, I don't mind at all. I was just watching Countdown two from 1999." "Thanks." The Fan nodded, "Anytime." Matthew rolled over on his side, facing the opposite direction. He pulled out a pen and a sheet of paper from a small drawer sitting in-between the bed he occupied on and the empty bed across from him. Brock hit the play button on his remote and Countdown two began to play. He sat there for a good while, watching old fWo superstars such as The Boston Strangler, Primetime, Mike Bear, Wretch, Wolf Fang, Machina, Briana and Havok. They were all standing in a circle, with serious looks crossing all their faces. But as he continued watching, his mind was led elsewhere. He began to think about the first survivor show, the beginning, and the end. The Fan was curious of to what Bryan and Frank had going on. Were they planning to ruin Survivor IV? That was only one of the many thoughts that crossed his mind. Brock was curious and at the same time, worried. He wanted to know what was going on, but he had no way of finding out. An hour or so had past and something caught Brock's attention. [I]"Fallen, get rid of him!"[/I] It was the voice of Joy and it was the end of Countdown two. Fallen, Joy, Death, and Demon were having a few quarrels with one another. And as Brock continued to watch, he witnessed the death of Joy as he fell off the entrance ramp, and landed on his head. A tear began to form in Brock's right eye, but he wiped it clean before Matthew could look over and spot it. Brock stopped the video. He now sat in a bundle of silence and merged all his thoughts into one, he was worried about his future, worried about Survivor IV, and overall, worried about becoming a professional wrestler. Beer's a way of life! Beer's MY way of life! Drink Drink Drink! Reno's stunt last week wasn't ignored. He'd pay for it eventually, but the suits in the fWo were just waiting for their chance to strike him properly. After all, they weren't sure if he really was drunk, or if it was an act for the show. Either way, they had their eye on him, which concerned him greatly. It he screwed this up, he'd be a two-time Survivor loser and even more of a laughingstock. With Disney's new ownership over the fWo, Reno knew that there would be many changes from the days of old, but it was much worse than he could have imagined. The backstage politics between wrestlers alone was enough to make him cringe, but Brian Cage, or War, or whatever the sketchy leader of the Silver team called himself was what really made Reno weary. He didn't seem concerned with Survivor, rather, he had other business. The Survivor 4 allowance check was much more than Reno's from the original contest. Interestingly enough, it was spent twice as fast. Davis got the shakes around the same time as every other day. Noon time strolled around and his worries had made the urge double in seriousness. In a blink of an eye, he found himself at the liquor store throwing his money away for substance. Time slowed as he entered his car, bottle of Royal Reserve already in hand, and cracked open the cap. He could feel the warmth from the bottle, the soothing smell of peace emanating from the whiskey. The first drop coated his tongue and a chill of patience and comfort followed the liquor down to his stomach and throughout his limbs. With a deep sigh, he relaxed back in his seat. Blink. Davis kicked open the Silver Team's locker room door, with literally a crate of alcohol in one hand, his hardly full bottle of R&R in the other, and a stench of booze, reminiscent of the dirty old man who used to live down the street from you, following right behind. "PARTY!!" The Unwanted Angel hollered to what he then realized was a bathroom. He chuckled to himself, "Elijah? You in here? I got hooch galore for ya, you human laxative!" No answer. Davis checked out his watch and saw he was still 2 hours early of being early to the show. "Beer's a way of life!" Reno drop kicked open another door, hoping for results. "Beer's MY way of life!" He realized he got the right room this time as he saw the Silver team banner hanging on the wall. Davis got up to his feet and gazed around the empty room, realizing he'd be partying by himself for a while. Fine with him, he thought and then continued to sing his drinking song, "DRINK DRINK DRINK! FIGHT FIGHT FIGHT! I Just wanna get drunk tonight!" He eased the precious crate onto the floor near the door and slumped himself over a bench. A few swaggles of booze later, Reno realized he was a boring guy to party with alone. If he were schizophrenic, he could summon some wicked cool friends to party with him, but he wasn't so he realized that was a stupid idea and drank until the bench would take its clothes off. The door to the Team Silver locker room suddenly opened, and Davis brightened. Finally, someone to party with, Maybe it was even a hot chick who had gotten lost in the building. Unfortunately, as the Unwanted Angel turned, he came face to face with the man least likely to join him in drunken debauchery. "Party's getting started!" Reno stood up to meet his fellow drowner of sorrows. "Reno Davis, exactly what do you think you are doing?" Logic. Lovely. "Oh, hey. You know," Reno sat back down, knowing this guy wasn't about to party. He was more the type who cried at a party, or whined about being the designated driver or other wimpy and childish things, "I'm just...just drinking. You want something?" Davis reached into his crate, pulled out a bottle of Crown Royal and tossed it to Logic. Davis figured he'd like that sissy Crown Royal stuff if he liked anything. The Pensive Punisher caught the bottle, glaring at Reno. Suddenly, Logic smiled. Davis was taken aback. Would he actually take a sip? A drunken Logic was an amusing thought to Reno. Unfortunately, it was just that, a thought. "No thank you." Logic whirled, and swung the bottle, crashing it against the door. The pained look on Reno Davis' face seemed to indicate that, if not for the bottles in his hands, he was ready to lap the liquor up from the floor so as not to let it go to waste. "I was a bouncer in bars in New York City for three years," Logic said, voice rising as he crossed the room and grabbed Davis by the collar. "I've seen what this does to people. I will not have you sullying our entire team with this garbage. There may be much to worry about, but I do not NEED liquid courage to find solace!" Davis twitched as he looked from the murdered bottle of alcohol, then back to Logic. "Acohol.....Abuse...." Davis murmured. Logic didn't quite understand, "Pardon me?" "ALCOHOL ABUSE!!!!" Reno leapt from his seated position and flew at Logic in a drunken fury. Logic sighed and swatted the tiny Davis out of the way, sending him against the wall in a slump. He moaned and rolled onto his side, "Feel my rage...feel it!" Logic shook his head at the sight, Davis was an almost unbeatable fighter when he was focused, and sober. When booze was added to the mix, it was a sad sight of what the poison could do to a person so talented. Sickened, Logic just stared at the incoherent Davis on the floor. "And you would be our leader, Mr. Davis? You would lead us? You cannot even lead yourself, cannot even lead your own life. Until you gain control, I suggest you cease your delusions. You may be able to convince the rest of the team to drown their sorrows in cheap ale, but I am not one of them. Goodbye, Mr. Davis." The door slammed behind Logic as he left, leaving a stunned Reno alone in the Team Silver locker room. Fucker. Reno gathered himself to his feet, staring at the mess of booze by the door. As much of an arse as Logic was, he had a point, Reno needed to get his shit together or else he'd lose his shot at everything he's worked so hard for. Reno found his hand around the bottle of whiskey again and without a second thought, took a swig. He needed help, he thought, taking another swill. He had to stop drinking. This was his demon. Davis took another drink. Is there hope? He pondered over another sip. Hopeless. Reno drank until the thought of cure was out his mind. His self-realization became another drunken smile as he waited for more people to party with. Are you having fun? Fun and Games. The sounds of children laughing and venders peddling merchandise echoed through Taylor Rogers' ears as he walked aimlessly through Walt Disney World. Since his early childhood he had always wanted to visit the so-called "most magical place on earth", but now, as an adult, his pale face told the story of someone who just wanted to go home. A group of children ran past him in a line, each yelling louder than the next. The biggest child, who couldn't have been more than seven or eight years old, stood proudly in front. It was this one who caught Taylor's eye. He knew, as the child in front began to hop on one foot, consequently causing those behind him to do the same thing, what game they were playing. He couldn't help but wonder, as he watched the one child command the rest, if he was playing the same game. From this the questions started rising from his heart to his head. He hated thinking so much, but he just couldn't control it. How long had he been a pawn? Was he a pawn at all? And if he was, whose pawn was he? An annoying beeping interrupted his train of thought and he felt something vibrating from inside of his pocket. Sighing, he thrust his hand down into its depths and pulled out a cellular phone. Holding it in his hand the same hand he had used a few days before to injure the foot of Brookes Davidson he glanced at the caller ID with his bloodshot green eyes. Benjamin It blinked across the phone's screen over and over again, taunting him like an eight letter bully with nothing better to do than make his life miserable. He didn't want to pick up, but he knew in the pit of his stomach that he had to. After all, he practically lived with this man. Ignoring him would certainly make that a bit difficult, wouldn't it? Pressing a button on the keypad, he made his decision. "Hello?" He said after raising the phone to his ear, awaiting response from his manager. "You want me to come to the lot now?" He asked, no doubt repeating what Benjamin Davis had just told him. "But why Seven Bottles of Liquor on the Wall "DRINK DRINK DRINK! FIGHT FIGHT FIGHT! I JUST WANNA GET DRUNK TONIGHT!" Logic was practically running down the halls in the bowels of the Walt Disney Sports Complex, trying to get the screaming voice of Reno Davis out of his head. It was ridiculous, no, beyond ridiculous. Downright childish, even. Davis wanted to lead Team Silver, while he could barely lead anything but his mouth to the neck of a bottle containing alcohol. The thought of it had Logic shaking his head. "He is such a fool. A pity, really." He knew what Reno could do. It was all documented in his files. Reno Davis. The Unwanted Angel. Survivor II finalist, and former boyfriend of current fWo superstar Amy Campbell, also known as Aimz, who was herself a former fWo Hardcore and United States Champion. Logic kept similar files on all those in the competition, whether on his own team or the opposing Gold squad. To say he was the most paranoid man in the contest would be an understatement. Yet to Logic, paranoid, fear, and worry were all good qualities at this stage of the game. "Ah, thank heavens, a machine which will dispense some liquid that won't poison my mind or body." The Thinking-Man's Grappler slid a dollar inside a nearby drink machine, and a bottle of water popped out. Logic grabbed the bottle with his large hand, easily unscrewed the top, and took a long, refreshing sip. He temporarily entertained the idea of running back to the Team Silver locker room with more bottles for those who would show up, but it was an illogical idea. Between alcohol and water, most of Team Silver would choose alcohol. That fact alone made Logic smile. He pulled out the tape recorder from his pocket. By now, he was on his fourth or fifth cassette, after having filled the others with notes and thoughts, no matter how seemingly insignificant. "Reno Davis is still a drunken fool. He is sitting in the locker room right now with enough alcohol to kill four men. The other Team Silver members should be arriving soon, and they will most likely join him in imbibing." Every second they spent imbibing was a second less they had to analyze their situation. This contest was a mystery, a gigantic puzzle. The leaders of each team were former fWo stars, War and Famine, each promising to pull off the biggest con in the history of professional wrestling. On top of that, the fWo itself was under chaos. Disney's control was seemingly tested on a daily basis. The superstars, from what he had seen from the television shows, had been on edge. Everything was building to CyberSlam, and Logic would be watching. That was the big man's advantage. He paid attention to everything. While others like Doctor Curiosity moped about losing, he was watching his competitors. While Reno Davis ran his mouth and got drunk, he was taking notes. And unless Christian Elijah was engaged in deep thought while on the commode, he was no mental threat either. Another sip of water passed down Logic's throat. Inconspicious was Logic's favorite word in this competition. Let Bantam Diablo and Reno Davis argue over who was the leader. Let Doctor Curiosity rant and rave. He could read them all like books. Well, all except Windsor, who spoke out even more rarely than he did. While they all focused on the individual challenges, Logic watched the game as a whole. He analyzed the situation and made decisions. He had hated to swat Reno Davis as he did, but he had done so in self-defense. Besides, Reno was so out of it he would likely never even remember it happened. He would be everyone's friend. Agree with his team when he could, disagree when he had to, but always respectfully. He would stay in the game long enough to figure out what Frank Smyth and Brian Cage were trying to pull. He would figure out how it involved him. The Pensive Punisher continued wandering the halls. He heard the faint sounds of screaming in the distance. More of his team had probably showed up. He ducked into a small alcove, sitting down in the corner, alone. He enjoyed people alone, enjoyed letting the thoughts, the worry, and the fear wash over him. It kept him going. If he wasn't worried, wasn't thinking about this 24/7, he knew he would lose. For the pen truly was mightier than the sword. Could Logic defeat Reno Davis in the ring? Maybe, maybe not. But could he beat Reno mentally, intellectually, logically? The answer was an emphatic yes. Any other answer would be simply illogical. You've Got A Friend In Me It had literally been seconds since Taylor Rogers had walked back onto the lot where the newly created Survivor shows took place and already he wanted to leave again. The reasons why, however, were unclear. This was probably due to the fact that everything having to do with what takes place on the lot was unclear to everyone involved with the competition, except of course to those who were controlling it. And Taylor, who was billed as the simple monster from simple Iowa, was certainly not in control of anything, whether it be his own career or the contest that would make or break it. He had heard the rumors. Everyone had heard the rumors. Yet, one could never be too sure if there was any truth to any of them, especially since the nature of the contest usually led to people embroiled in it to having trust issues with those around them and vice versa. It was a big mess and nothing could be cleaned up and returned to the place it originally started. Not one of the wrestlers knew exactly what they were supposed to be doing. Not one of them was on the right path. Even worse, not one of them even knew what the right path was or what it would lead to. Taylor Rogers opened a door in a hallway that seemed to be completely lined with them. Was this the right direction? Probably not. Yet, a sliver of hope within him kept him thinking that someone would provide a map. "Taylor!" An all too familiar voice exclaimed. "There you are!" Maybe it would be Benjamin Davis. "Sit down champ! We've got many things to discuss!" Taylor obliged and took a seat on a steel folding chair that rested near the wall of whatever room it was that he and his manager were inside of. Sitting down, he sized up Benjamin with eyes that were once widened, but now blinded, by the prospects of becoming an fWo wrestler. Why was he smiling? "So what's up?" Taylor asked innocently, prodding for an answer. "I just got finished going over the tape from last week's match." Benjamin replied. "Seems like you got winded a bit too easily. I'm thinking we're going to intensify some of your work out. Wouldn't want to lose anymore matches or worse! The contest!" Taylor's heart sunk. "Yeah, about that." He said meekly, Davis' eyes now doing the sizing up. "I'm started to get freaked out by all this secrecy, Ben. And I know you can handle it, but..." "...If you know I can handle it then why are you worried, T.R.?" Benjamin asked, almost sounding offended. "I've gotten you through worse than this!" Taylor thought back to his days of addiction, when Benjamin locked him in a room for days on end as he suffered through the pains of want and withdrawal. Somehow he felt comforted. "Yeah, I guess you're right." Taylor said, seemingly convinced. "Of course I am!" Benjamin exploded happily with a hearty, Santa Claus-like chuckle, pulling a white document out of the inside pocket of his blazer as the warmth of his laugh soaked Taylor like the sun. "Now, I want you to take a look at this new training schedule. I think it might eliminate some of the problems you had last week..." His words faded away, almost like the fear and paranoia that a few minutes ago had been boiling hotly inside of Taylor 'the Terrible' Rogers just had. All the things he had questioned. Brian Cage. Frank Smyth. The contest. The rumors. The lies. The secrecy. Benjamin made it all disappear, at least in Taylor's mind. In the real world, though, all of those things existed and would exist until the final Survivor stood at the top of a mountain that the Fans Wrestling Organization may very well be plotting to blow up right after. Soon, Taylor Rogers would have to realize this or else he'd simply die beneath the rubble. It came as no big surprise to the people closest to him; they knew he wouldn't really be able to trust his teammates or anyone in the fWo for that matter. For a game that relied on trust and mystery, The Golden Bear wasn't doing so badly. Steven Windsor quietly walked through the backstage hallways taking it all in for the first time, really. The previous times he had been to the Walt Disney Sports Complex he hustled to his destination to be on time and get things going. For the first time he was a few hours early to the building and figured it wouldn't hurt to have a look around. Upon entering the next hallway in his path he noticed Logic sitting in a folding chair in a corner all by himself. Steven wasn't really sure of the other big man who also seemed to not like to talk much to his teammates. Steven figured he'd leave the investigating and chit chats for later, if there was a later. He continued to walk right on by Logic and left the big man to his bottle of water. Steven figured he might as well head toward the Team Silver locker room and see if he and Logic were the only ones in the building already from the team. He was hoping this was the situation because he wanted a little alone time to himself, but it that thought wasn't meant to be, which he soon realized when he opened the door to the locker room and found Reno Davis half passed out on a bench with a crate of liquor sitting on the floor next to him. Steven Windsor wasn't the man Reno Davis was looking for, figuratively speaking.
