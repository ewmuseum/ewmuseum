Introduction Everything must come to an end. Epidemic V. fWo Uprising did not open with a token fireworks display. Instead a single word written out in plain white text sat ominously on the pitch black video wall. Disease. Slowly but surely the letters bled into the next word. Typhoid. The word remained only for a few seconds before fading away to be replaced by a shot of an individual exiting a bathroom. Hands glowing brightly to illustrate the areas that hadn't been washed properly the individual moved into a kitchen area before slowly preparing food, the glowing colours passing from hands to that being handled. And then the deep voice that wouldn't have been out of place in a movie trailor spoke up. "Ever wonder why people are so persistant about washing your hands? That's because if you don't you might be spreading a disease that killed 13 thousand British men compared to only 8 thousand battle related deaths during the war between Britain and South Africa in the late 19th century. Typhoid fever is characterized by the sudden onset of sustained fever, severe headache, nausea, severe loss of appetite, constipation or sometimes diarrhoea. Severe forms have been described with mental dullness and meningitis and if left untreated the disease can be fatal. From this we learn that diseases can develop in any time or place under any circumstance leaving nothing immune. Not even the fans Wrestling organization. At Meltdown, a new disease develops and the epidemic begins." The footage faded to black and the fWo returned to regular programming. Faith on a stage It was over, that's what they had told him. Deacon had faced Areumna'derin Rahl, not because he wanted to, but because he had to. Rahl had attacked multiple people in the name of the Deacon. He had even attacked Chris Shepherd when all either wanted to do, was get Rahl to a safe place where he could receive help. But you can't give help where it isn't wanted. But all of that was unimportant... it was over now. As the lights went out directly after the opening of Uprising and the Gregorian Chant began, the crowd knew instantly that something was definitely not over, something concerning the Deacon. He was led through the curtain by Chris Shepherd who was sporting a bandage from the past Ruahh. Deacon did not bother to 'dress' the part. He had the night off, and from early internet reports, it was due to a mild concussion from the chair shot that Crucifix had given him. However, Deacon would perform, if not in the ring, then in his personality. Chris and Deacon made their way to the ring, stepped inside, and then Chris took the microphone, taking his role as spokesperson for the two. "Tonight, I'm not here to spend time talking about getting a title shot." Chris began. "God willing, those belts will come with time and effort, both of which we have never been lacking." "I'm not here to talk about Timothy Veneno, the man you call Areumna'derin Rahl." With that name mentioned, the crowd went into a frenzied booing. Chris waited for the crowd to calm down. "We did our best, both of us, but we can't do everything to help others. God has gifted some to help Timothy and those with similar problems. We're not among them. Deacon is a wrestler, not a psychologist, and the only wish I had was that he would've used whatever influence he had on Timothy to get him to see a professional sooner. We might have been able to do more." "We're not here to talk about Brand Frontier or Aimz." Chris said, getting an easy pop from the crowd. "What we are here for... is Crucifix." Those cheers turned to boos in a nanosecond. "I've never understood you, Crucifix. You were placed with Deacon - granted, neither you nor Deacon wanted that tag team situation, but it was there. Deacon tried to make the best of it, not only for his own good, but for your good, and the good of the fWo. And to be honest, you guys might have went far had you had your head screwed on straight. Instead of helping, you spent your time throwing digs Deacon's way, driving a wedge between the team, and generally being an irritant in the side of the Deacon. And why? What did he do that was so bad?" Chris paused for a few moments to collect his thoughts. "I don't see anything he did, but I see plenty that you did, and are still doing." Chris said just as the ViewTron came to life. It showed the end of the Deacon vs. Rahl match, the part where Crucifix came in and hit Deacon with the chair. As Crucifix headed up the ramp, the video picked up what he said... "Save me, Deacon," Crucifix began. "Save me from myself before I do something, that I'll likely enjoy watching you suffer from." The viewtron went back to darkness as Chris put the microphone back to his lips. "I see that it is time... that we stop the backstabbing... the cheapshot attacks. And we let you face The Deacon in the ring... one... on ... one." With that announcement, the crowd responded with a deafening cheer. "This isn't a sneak attack. We're not hiding, waiting for you to not notice us to spring some attack. The lights aren't being turned out. We're challenging you to a match at Monday's Ruahh. We're challenging you to stop testing our patience... and begin testing our faith. Because on Monday, you're going to see that faith thrust into your face. "Is the Evidence." The fans that knew and understood helped him chant that phrase that had defined much of his work in Greensboro's CSWA.
