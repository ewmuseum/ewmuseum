Introduction Previously... With the events of the last several weeks weighing on their minds, the fWo travels to Sevilla, Spain and more peaceful territory after their harrowing Middle East tour. With the world's eyes on the war with Iraq, fans have gathered en masse to the arena in the hopes of enjoying a nice distraction. But within the locker rooms of the fWo there is still worry and concern. Kodiak Vic Creed's quest for the title has taken a deadly turn, and the relucatant father figure to the fWo locker room, Silver Fox, is missing and presumed dead. But every story has a turn, and events are even now closing in around them all that will once again change their fate... Dead Man Walking The San Pablo arena stirred irritably as "Walk" by Pantera began to thump throughout the building. Many rose to their feet and began to boo as Kodiak Vic Creed stepped out from behind the curtain.
