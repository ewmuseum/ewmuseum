Sunday Slaughter, 18 February 2001

18 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction It was a dark and cloudy night outside the Municipal Auditorium. A familiar figure stood in front of the doorway to the interior. A shock of blond hair capped his head, and his face bore an expression that was pleasant yet undeniably stupid. "Wretch? Wre-etch? I got your letter that despite being written in handwriting that did not appear to be yours told me to meet you here! Where are you, Wretch?" called Scot Hartt. He looked around, puzzled. There was no sign of his old buddy. "Well, crap." He kicked at the ground, clearly annoyed, and started towards the door. Maybe he was inside. He twisted the doorknob and pulled the door open. He was yanked out of sight by an unseen hand. The door creaked closed, slowly. It shut, sealing off the inside of the arena from the cold air of the night. Practice Makes Perfect In the owner's office, the owner himself - Chris Universal - sat in a five-wheeled leather chair, fiddling around with a Rubix cube. After getting all sides with the same color, he smiled at his small accomplishment, but was cut short by a knock at the door. "Yep, come in," he called out. Clarisse nervously entered, looking to have something on her mind. "Ah, Clarisse. A pleasant surprise as always. Anything I can do for you?" She took a seat in the chair in front of Universal's desk. "Well, Mr. Universal, I'd just like to talk about your decision to put me as the referee of Cody's and Chris' match at In Misery. I know that it sounds good on paper, but really, I have no experience in refereeing, and I know that I'll screw up." Chris Universal nods and shrugs, seeming to be in agreement. She continued, "Plus, I'm technically NOT under contract to the fWo, so I don't have to do this. I really don't want to be in the middle of this." At this moment, Universal interjected, "What you don't want is sometimes what you need, Clarisse. You *are* in the middle of all of this - you're engaged to Crusader yet still have some sort of fling with Titan, or what have you. You see, this isn't about contracts - it's about choices. Adding you as an element to this match will hopefully put an end to the situation between Crusader and Chris Titan, because you should know first-hand that it's been hectic lately, and it's even affected both Crusader's and Titan's concentration in the ring." Looking down at her clasped hands over her crossed legs, Clarisse took a moment to process what was said to her. This time, she shrugged her shoulders and nodded in agreement. "But what about my lack of experience? I'm not even a wrestler, Mr. Universal. Sure, Mike Bear would make a fair referee with his experience in the ring, but I hardly know any of the rules." Universal sat back in his chair for a moment, holding his chin in his hand. "Well, you know what I think? Why not get some practice tonight?" Clarisse wore a confused face. "What do you mean?" Reaching under his desk, Universal pulled out a small-fitting referee's shirt and tossed it in Clarisse's direction. "Here tonight on Sunday Slaughter, you have officiating duty. Your first match - Crusader versus Sphere, and your second match - Chris Titan versus Mike Heftel." Holding the referee's shirt in her hand, Clarisse tilted her head. "Are you sure?" Universal nodded. "Of course. Just ask one of the off-duty officials tonight to help you out with the basic rules of the ring, and then later on you can apply them in the matches you referee. You'll do just fine. Now get to your locker room and get dressed!" Universal winked at her, as she took a deep breath and walked out of the office. MUD (c) vs. Vengence "The Beast" by the Fugees takes over the sound system in the Municipal Auditorium, as the beast known as Vengence makes his way to the ring. With each step the crowd continues to boo the monster. He steps into the ring and waits for the Hardcore Title shot he has asked for for so long. His wait is stopped as "My Name Is Mud" by Primus surfaces over the speakers, and the crowd cheers for the man who was moved from his friends' bed side to defend his Hardcore title. MUD charges to the ring. He throws his belt to the ground as he slides into the ring. Vengence charges over to start to stomp on MUD, but with each attempt MUD rolls out of the way. The rolling stops as MUD rolls out of the ring and to his feet. He starts to search under the ring. Under the table, he finds a conveniently placed box full of weapons. MUD pulls the box of weapons from under the ring and shoves it under the bottom rope. As he does so, he fails to notice the mammoth Vengence, who gets behind him and nails him in the back of the head with a clothesline. MUD drops to the ground much like his friend did when stabbed by his now arch nemesis Carnage. Vengence grabs onto the shirt and pants of MUD, then simply hurls him at the announce position where MUD lands on top of James Armstrong causing the former fWo owner by default to fall on his back, which draws a positive response from the crowd. Slowly MUD pushes himself back to his feet, only to get hit with a vicious boot to the midsection by Vengence. MUD is doubled over, and Vengence grabs ahold of MUD and lifts him up for a powerbomb. MUD fights back with rounds of fists into the face of Vengence causing the big man to lose his grip on MUD resulting in the Hardcore champion to be dropped on the table, but the announce table remains in good condition with no break. But because of the lack of the break, the blow hurt even more especially MUD's lower back. Writhing in pain, MUD tries to get back to his feet off the table but the moment he was on his feet he slumped to the ground in pain, holding his lower back. The referee charges in asking if MUD was okay enough to continue, but reminding the ref that it was a Hardcore match, Vengence grabs him from the back and throws him into the side of the ring knocking the ref out! Booing nonstop, the crowd watches as Vengence folds a chair and stalks towards MUD. Slowly and painfully MUD gets to his feet. The moment he lifts his head he meets a friend named steel chair. MUD is knocked back out cold, and now blood leaks down his forehead and down onto his nose as his head rests on the padded mats surrounding the ring. Vengence grabs onto MUD and throws him back into the ring, and slowly MUD crawls - but he is stopped at the fWo logo in the center of the ring as Vengence applies a vicious stomp to the back of the champ. MUD isn't done - he keeps crawling. He reaches for the bottom rope of the ring and then he reaches up, grabs the second, and then the top. MUD is back on his feet, but his legs are like spaghetti wobbling every which way. This causes the Hardcore Champion to rely on the ropes for his balance. MUD turns his arms and almost falls. He slams his back onto the ropes and, as he tries to get up to continue his fight, Vengence takes that opening to nail a horrendous big boot to the face of MUD sending him flying over the top rope and down hard onto the ringside mats. Vengence reaches down into the box full of weapons and pulls out a kendo stick. He holds it tight in his right hand. Vengence jumps out of the ring and heads MUD's way. Vengence starts to walk faster and faster as he gets closer to MUD, and when he gets there the champ is on his feet. The big man swings the kendo stick but MUD ducks and sends his shoulder into the midsection of Vengence doubling the big man over. MUD gets back to his feet, looks at Vengence and spits directly into his eyes. MUD, running on an empty tank, grabs onto the head of Vengence and runs up the ring steps and nails an acid drop! As a result of the move, both men lay motionless on the ringside mats for moments on end. The referee finally comes to, gets back to his feet, and he enters the ring. He walks over to the ropes and sees both MUD and Vengence out cold. MUD slowly gets to his feet and slides into the ring. He gets up to the top turnbuckle and steps up on top of the ringpost. He stays on his perch like an owl as he waits for his prey to begin to move. Just moments later, Vengence gets up to his feet and the moment he looks up MUD is airborne. The two collide, causing a huge wreck outside the ring. On their feet cheering, the fans start supporting MUD with chants of his name, and he is back to his feet. This time he heads to the box of weapons and pulls out the trash can. He charges towards Vengence who is back on his feet, but the extremely large man attempts to send a big boot to MUD's face. MUD ducks, only to resurface and throw the trash can at Vengence, who catches it. MUD follows up with a spinning heel kick that sends the trash can back into Vengence's face, rocking the big man back and sending him into the steel railing. MUD reaches up, grabs onto Vengence, and forcefully slides him into the ring under the bottom rope. MUD follows him in and sets the big man up against the ropes. MUD hits a series of knife hand chops onto Vengence. The champ then changes Vengence's position and nails a Tornado DDT. MUD grabs onto Vengence again, but the big man grabs onto him and nails a brainbuster. Vengence brings MUD back to his feet and shoots him in for the ropes. He telegraphs a back body drop but as MUD returns he nails a swinging neckbreaker. Blood flowing down his forehead and all, MUD reaches into the weapons box, grabs Playstation, and nails Vengence across the head with it. It shatters. MUD goes back into the box and pulls out a stop sign which he places on Vengence's chest. MUD climbs up to the top and jumps off, nailing the MUD Stomp. MUD hooks the leg, and the ref counts 1! 2! 3! MUD remains the fWo Hardcore Champion. "My Name is Mud" began to play, then is hastily shut off. MUD looked up the entrance way and saw Carnage with the mic in hand. "Nice showing Mortie, just about as good of a showing as Toad had when I stabbed my knife into his chest.. hahaAHAHahA!!" MUD attempted to get out to fight Carnage, but the referee held him back as Carnage continued. "Mort you were never ready for me, you'll never be ready for me, all you are right now is the weak member of a pack waiting on the fringe waiting to be plucked away and murdered viciously. I am the predator, who will take you and lacerate your body leaving the shreds of red flesh and bones in the middle of the grasslands. You're story ends next Saturday Mortie, that's if you're willing to put that Hardcore title on the line against me at In Misery in a no-holds-barred Knife match!" The crowd remained silent as MUD stares Carnage in the eyes and nods his head, acknowledging the matchup. Carnage smiles his yellowed smile, happy that the match has been signed. "hahaAHAHahA! Don't worry MUD, you're already In Misery with your friend Toad ready to die, on Saturday. I'll put you out of it! Don't die before Saturday, Mort, please don't Die! HAahahahahahahAHAHah!!" Carnage continued to laugh as Slaughter went backstage... Result: MUD by pinfall The Necklace At the water cooler, Clarisse poured herself a cup of water, now donning the referee stripes with black spandex shorts on. She took a drink and fixed her hair into a ponytail, just as Chris Titan came into the scene. This startled Clarisse slightly, as she dropped her cup of water. She tried to walk away from the man she made love with six years ago, but Titan held her by the arm. "Please let me go, Chris," she said... but Titan didn't let go of his grip. Instead, he held onto her and presented her with an item wrapped in a white cloth. "You forgot this," he boldly said. Puzzled, she accepted it. Unraveling the cloth, Clarisse found the necklace that Chris had given her those years back at the beach. The beautiful engraved stones. It didn't have a monetary value, but certainly did have an emotional value to Clarisse. Before she could even say anything else, Titan had left. Suddenly, Crusader had arrived to the area, and Clarisse slightly jumped again. She turned around and concealed the necklace somewhere in her bra. "What are you doing?" Crusader interrogated. Clarisse turned to face Crusader. "Oh, umm... nothing. I was just adjusting my... erm... my bra." Not caring, Crusader moved on to what he wanted to talk to her about. "About my match with Sphere next," he began, fixating his eyes straight on Clarisse. "You'd better call this one straight down the line. Don't get in my way." Clarisse nodded, as Crusader turned to walk away, disappearing from her view. Sphere vs. Crusader Nervously, Clarisse makes her way to the ring - wearing a referee's shirt bundled at the bottom into a belly-top - ready to referee the following match. She's greeted by some whistling men at ringside, but pays no attention to them. Sphere kicks out a second before three, and Clarisse holds two fingers up to show Crusader the result. Annoyed, Crusader catches Sphere with a kick to the mid-section, before lifting him up and dropping him straight to the mat with a brainbuster suplex. Clarisse jumps back from the impact of the move, and Crusader immediately yells, "Get out of the way!" Sphere stumbles around and receives a Flipside, and Crusader holds on for the cover. The crowd counts three, but Crusader swears as he sees Clarisse knocked out on the mat.
