Silver Fox smiled as he looked at the arena... three more shows and they were done for the year. Things were certainly looking better now... and if they could survive this silly 'Countdown curse' then all would be well. "Over here... we need to get into the building on the side entrance." Samuel Smooth nodded, and behind him Twitch and Ted Tallison... who looked tired and worn. Silver Fox frowned. Tallison had been growing worse in the last few weeks, not better. He had been drinking far too much... and it was starting to show signs of serious wear on him. "Ted," Silver Fox began, but he stopped as he noticed a pair of policemen striding up to them. His mind at once flashed to what the problem must be... a fight that involved fans perhaps? Another drug bust. Silver Fox sighed, and he felt his momentary good cheer collapse around him. "You in charge here?" One of the guards nodded to Silver Fox, who nodded in return. The two policemen exchanged a look, then stepped forward. "We have something... of a sensitive nature to discuss. Would you mind some privacy?" Silver Fox glanced around at his friends. "No, whatever you have can be said in front of them. I'm technically in charge, but we run this promotion together. What's wrong?" "Maybe nothing," said the first policeman, "maybe something. We've gotten credible information that one of your former wrestlers, John Malcolm, may be in the area." Samuel Smooth groaned, and Twitch looked at once nervous. Silver Fox closed his eyes, feeling the overwhelming sense of it all crashing down on him. "I see," he said, opening his eyes, "what do you want me to do." The policemen exchanged another glance, then the man who had been speaking to Silver Fox reached into his pocket and withdrew a silver pistol. He held it out to Silver Fox, who looked down at it with surprise. "Take this," said the policeman, "and this card. It's not procedure, but we feel that if this Malcolm comes after anyone, it's likely to be you. Don't use it unless it's serious. Otherwise, call the number on this card if you hear anything. Anything... got it?" "I do," said Silver Fox, and he took the gun with no small amount of trepidation. "and I will." The policeman nodded, and motioned that they were going to be patrolling the area. Silver Fox smiled as best he could and wished them well, feeling the weight of the handgun in his pocket. He looked at Smooth, Twitch, and Tallison, and tried to remember the good feeling he had mere moments ago. Countdown had begun... 2:00:00... Neo looked at the door, taking in only for a moment the broken lock that hung like a decayed tooth. It was snowing lightly and New York looked nearly magical in the night. The lights for the holiday were out, blinking softly all around him, and for a moment the part of him that was still John Malcolm mourned the loss of his innocence and of the opportunity to have a holiday by the fire with family and children. "Not all roads lead to bliss," he mumbled to himself, and pushed the door open. Madison Square Garden was empty and silent. Crates had been delivered for the Countdown show on Wednesday, but for now they stood silent on the packing floor. Shadows loomed all around them, and the gloom of the place gave him pause. He was not alone here. Slowly, a smile creased over his face, and Neo slid a hunting knife out of his sleeve and into his hand. "Come out, come out... I know you're here." There was no response, and Neo walked further into the dark and deserted room. His footfalls made loud echoes on the concrete floor and he made no effort to be silent. Dust hung in the air and it was cold, all too cold in the arena that was famous the world over. Neo came to a large hallway with a high ceiling and wide walls. It was more of a rectangular room than anything else, and Neo stopped at one end of it. Beyond he could see the dim glimmer of the arena itself, faint shadows of the seats and the rigging. In two days this area would be filled with people on the eve of the New Year, but for tonight it was only the two of them. "Neo." Neo nodded and let the dim light play off the blade of the knife. "Fallen." Neo grinned, his teeth gleaming in the light. "You're not going to leave this place." Neo stepped forward, his body tensed. At the other end of the hallway Fallen stood, barely more than a siloutte against the end of the hallway and the edge of the arena. The growl began low in Neo's throat, then rose until his roar and scream made harsh echoes in the hallway. Then his body snapped and he began to run forward, the knife held tight in his hand. He ran forward toward Fallen, cutting the distance between them quickly. Fallen waited for him to come, their final battle begun. History Countdown 3 opened with a view of the Continental Airlines Arena earlier in the day...before any of the fans arrived at the building...even before most of the wrestlers arrived at the building. The camera panned across the front of the arena, until a single man came into view, standing alone. Black Quicksilver stood there in his black hooded sweatshirt, hood up and hands in pockets...and looked off to the side. "Countdown. 1999. I was involved in a bitter feud with Silver Fox, and on December 31st...it was over. I defeated the man who helped me start my career in this business and then tried to end that same career. Four years later...that man is my boss. *I* have to answer to *him*. Again." BQ looked down at the ground. "Countdown. 2000. A man in a mask entered the fWo, threatening to destroy the STUMP Squad. Once again, on December 31st...I defeated my opponent. This man revealed himself to be Chris Titan, who tried to take one of my best friends away from me a few months later. He also got more opportunities than I did during that period. Opportunities he didn't deserve. It wasn't long before he tucked his tail between his legs and ran away, only to return just a few months ago. He was given more opportunities, and once again he disappeared." BQ looked up at the sky. "Countdown. 2001. I faced "Kodiak" Vic Creed two nights in a row. I was the only guy in the locker room with the balls to do this. Countdown 1 came...and it looked as if I was finally going to be a superstar. I went after the Flying Frenchie. That feud propelled me into matches with Ric Chronos, the fWo World Champion. I even defeated Chronos on more than one occasion...but then the fWo closed. My home was no more." BQ took his right hand out of his pocket and rubbed the top of his head. "Countdown. 2002. The fWo had re-opened, and Team Lightning was going to ascend to the top of the tag team division...but by this time of the year...one-half of that team was gone. Then...on the 31st...Jon Crisp needed a tag team partner, and with this partner's help, he would drive Damnation out of the fWo. *I* was that partner. We were supposed to become a tag team...but we weren't a team. It was just Jon Crisp and his partner. Jon Crisp won the Tag Titles. Jon Crisp won the Wrestler of the Year Tournament." BQ pulled his hood down and looked directly into the camera. "If history has taught us anything, it's that, while you may end the year on a high note...there's only one place to go from there during the new year. DOWN. I've dropped down the ladder of success four years in a row, and I'm tired of it. Tonight...Wretch finally crawls out from under that rock, and after I dispose of him...I'm coming after YOU, Crisp. I'm ending the year on a high note and I'm staying there in 2004. Come this time next year...no one will remember who Jon Crisp is." Offering Times were tense in the fWo. Lance Knight could feel the tension as he walked through the corridor lining the belly of the arena. Everyone he passed by felt like they had a burden on their shoulders that they couldn't shake. Even he felt a weight on his back that wasn't sitting right with him. He noticed one of the utility doors opening and paused to see who was coming through. It was more than likely one of the workers coming in from the parking lot, taking a back way in to avoid the fans. But if it was someone who shouldn't have been backstage, Lance had no choice but to usher them right back out of the building. Tonight wasn't the night for someone to be wandering around looking for autographs in places they shouldn't be in. Who knew whom the fan might run into - for better or worse? At first glance, Lance was sure it wasn't a fan coming in. The man was far too big to be a casual fan. He seemed to fit into that "larger than life" mold that so many sports entertainers seemed to fall into. Broad shoulders bound by rugged, black leather. Tight cropped, ivory white hair. Six o'clock shadow. Eyes hidden behind a pair of four hundred dollar sunglasses. Lance felt a little awkward as Kodiak Vic Creed stepped through the doorway and closed the door behind him. There had always been stories about his personal life, a life he kept very well-guarded. People let slip certain tales they'd heard from a friend of a friend. Even Brand Frontier, a man whose life had crossed paths with Creed's over the course of his career, had stories about the Main Man he wouldn't talk about. Word had spread through the locker room that Vic had lost his daughter over the holidays. For a Father to bury his child was a terrible, terrible thing. Yet here was Creed, ready to go to work. Lance wasn't sure if that was a good or bad thing, but here he was with the World Title in its traveling case. "Uh, hey Vic." Lance wasn't quite sure why he said anything as Creed walked past. Professional courtesy maybe. Giving acknowledgement to someone who had suffered a painful loss during what should have been one of the happiest times of the year. Sure, maybe Creed didn't really deserve any kind of courtesy. It didn't hurt Lance to be human, even to a person who was somewhat less than human. Creed actually came to a stop, which surprised Lance a little. He expected Creed to just walk by as if Knight wasn't even there. He didn't turn, didn't face Lance at all. He just stared ahead through those jet-black sunglasses. "Lance." Knight was stuck with a whiff of stale whiskey breath, and guessed that those sunglasses were hiding a pair of bloodshot eyes. Clearly Creed was hurting. "Look, I know we've never really been that tight." Lance didn't know what to say. "I'm just... well I just wanted to offer my condolences -" Maybe Lance was half-expecting it, which was how he managed to get his arm up to block Creed's right hook. The power behind the blow still managed to knock Lance off-balance and sent him staggering into the wall. His back hit the concrete and he braced himself for another punch. Vic didn't have another punch in him. Not right now. "You can offer whatever you want after our match tonight." Lance stepped up to Creed. "Don't sweat it Chump. The only thing I'm offering you now is an ass-kicking, and I'll be happy to serve that up in spades." "Can't wait," Creed shouted over his shoulder as he continued down the corridor. Lance watched the big man disappear around the corner. What he couldn't see was that, while Vic's eyes were bloodshot behind his sunglasses it wasn't from alcohol. "Can't wait," he whispered aloud. Countdown To Tag Gold? Stormwindz walked towards the Brotherly Love locker room at a fast and direct pace. Countdown would be the moment that both herself, and Brotherly Love, had the chance to prove themselves... For rather different motives. The Harpers, especially Elliot, carved the fWo tag team titles. They were the next rung on the ladder of fame that Elliot was ruthlessly climbing, while Thomas would do anything to make his elder sibling's dream come true, as payback for all he's cost him. For Stormwindz, however, the goal was less direct, and a lot more complicated. In 2 days time, she could be the manager of the fWo tag team champions, having taken from the mid-division rookies to the pinnacle of tag wrestling. Fulfilling their potential in such a short space of time would give her stock a raise, but being successful was nothing if she couldn't be herself. For too long she'd been Stormwindz, and every success she took on under the moniker, in the costume, seemed to lose it's glory when it finally filtered through to Anna herself. But the name was protective, it save Anna putting herself out in front of the world. Soon, she'd have to decide which she wanted, finally; Success under someone else's name, or glory for herself... Glory for herself and all she is. "You're set," she purposefully stated, striding through the door and handing a schedule to Elliot. "3 nights, 3 matches... Minimum, given the Gauntlet." Elliot browsed the sheet, and nodded, happy with what he saw. "Walden & Matheson tonight, the Villanis tomorrow, and then the big one to round off the year," he pondered, already thinking ahead to the title match, or matches. "We get to clear up a little un-finished business as a warm-up, then take our rightful place at the top of tag team wrestling." Despite his selfishness, Stormwindz couldn't help but break a slight smile at the pure drive Elliot had. To get to the place she wanted, she had to find a man who didn't just want what she needed, he craved it. There were plenty of better wrestlers than Elliot Harper, and about 6 billion better human beings, but he had what she needed... And he also had Thomas. Seemingly lost already in the frantic rush to the new year that is Countdown, Thomas was switching his gaze between Elliot and Stormwindz. "Is he ready for this?" she asked Elliot, nodding her head at the dumb mass of muscle in the corner. "He understands?" Ignoring Thomas, Elliot's reply was confident. "He doesn't need to understand, he only has to do... And do he will." "Thomas knows that we're going to go into 2004 with those belts," Elliot continued, while Thomas perked up, nodding along with Elliot's boast, "And he knows that it'll go a long way towards making things up to me, don't you, Thomas?" Still nodding limply, Thomas answered. "I'll beat 'em all for the belts," he blurted out, "Do it for Elliot, Thomas will." With a focussed Thomas ready to unleash, Elliot knew that all he had to do was get his sibling psyched, get him worked up. There was a monster stirring inside his docile brother that was remaining contained, unable to fully break out. There had been glimpses of it, of the raw power a furious Thomas held, but an something that was so rare held little value to Elliot in the long run. "You'd better go," a serious Elliot then suggested to Stormwindz, "I need to have a little 'talk' to Thomas, he's still not quite... Prepared yet." Sensing it might be best that she leave, Stormwindz made her exit and left Elliot to fulfil his end of the bargain. Watching his sibling's face carefully from his corner, Thomas sensed something was up, and just looked right at Elliot. "Okay, Thomas," came the low, almost sinister voice, "We need to have a little talk..." Thomas said he'd do whatever it took to make things up to Elliot, and now his elder brother was going to make sure the offer was fully taken up. A dumb brother with plentiful power but a rarely used animal ferocity was no good to Elliot anymore. It was enough to get him to this point, enough to get them to the position of challengers... But to stand tall atop the tag division, he'd need a brother who'd attack as instinct, who'd stop to think when an opponent couldn't put up a fight. Elliot was willing to sacrifice all that was good about Thomas; his trusting nature, his playfulness, and his unfaltering inquisitiveness... All those would be replaced by a 300-pound mass of paranoid hate, and Elliot wouldn't bat an eyelid. "You know, Thomas... Mother never liked you, and as for Dad? There was a reason he used to tell his friends I was his only son..." Thomas face seemed to fall as Elliot gleefully pummelled the words right into him with an unrelenting stare. As Elliot moved closer, the view darkened, and Elliot's words were lost in the distance. "They let you stay at a school where you were bullied EVERY day, don't forget that, don't forget the times you stayed behind, crying, afraid to walk home by yourself... ...But I was always there for you, wasn't I?" Jeremy Savage vs. Dexter Pratt Jeremy Savage stood backstage, a little nervous about the match that was about to take place. It was his first wrestling experience in a major pay-per-view, and he could tell by the sound of the arena's crowd that there was no room for mistakes. The atmosphere revived memories of his deceased father, who played crowds of that magnitude with inspiring non-chalance. It was time for him to raise the bar, and go after his fathers fame. Hopefully, he could erase it from the industries' memory. Tonight, it was time for him to take a big step forward and pound fresh craters into the Savage legacy. It was time for him to make new footprints in the sand, and push onward to bigger and better things. He looked down at Shirley, who appeared nervous about the forthcoming "2 out of 3 Falls" match. She had every right to be. Her humanity was stripped from her; she was reduced to a mere trophy of the outcome, a piece of meat that would be auctioned off to whomever pulled the hardest punches.
