Sunday Slaughter, 30 September 2001

30 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "Terry... good to talk with you." Chris Universal held the phone to his ear as he looked out over the skyline and felt the autumn breeze. It was certainly getting cooler now... the summer was gone and another winter was on the way. Universal hated the winter and hated the colder weather. Things seemed to always go worse in the winter, both inside the ring and out. Certainly this winter wasn't starting off on the best of terms... Chronos, Primetime and the Frenchie were going stronger than ever and a lot of other things seemed to be falling apart. Thousands of miles away Terry Nodar sat behind a desk filled with paperwork. The desk never seemed to shrink and he was always astonished that the majority of work a wrestling promoter had to do had nothing to do with wrestling or wrestlers. Contracts, forms, legal releases... the paperwork was a never-ending flood of busywork. While working for Tort carried advantages in terms of the pay, there were times that he longed for the old days when he was simply Rudo Insano and flirting with Melissa Smalls. "It's good to talk with you too, Chris." Terry smiled... who would have ever thought that the two of them would wind up as the leaders of their respective promotions? "I hear the Frenchie is causing you problems over there. Keep your head up, things get better. Once Culligan stepped aside and we started running shows in Europe exclusively we got back in control. So don't worry... things have a way of coming around." Universal nodded. "Thanks Terry, and I'll work past that... somehow. But that wasn't why I was calling you today." "No?" On his end of the phone Terry raised his eyebrows. "Well, I'm still heading over to the states for the Christmas party you're throwing. Is that what you wanted to talk with me about?" "No, no..." Chris sighed deeply and pulled a small notebook out of his pocket. "It's about staff. You see, we've got a number of contracts that recently expired. For a lot of reasons we didn't jump on the ball and get some of these removed... and then with the recent downturns in wrestling a lot of our staff has moved onto Hollywood or the networks. We're operating tonight with pure contractors, but a lot of those people have never seen the show before and won't be around longer than a couple weeks. We need a dedicated staff... and since you had to practically build one from scratch..." "I see." Terry nodded. "Well, our situations were slightly different, but I think I have the number of someone who can help you. He's a talent recruiter who goes by the name of Touma... good guy, works very quickly... er..." Universal had started writing the name down when he heard Terry's hesitation. "Problem?" "Well... no..." Terry scratched his head as he searched for the right words. "Not exactly. Touma is a good guy and very good at what he does. He's really the best person I can recommend. But... well... he's a tad eccentric..." "Eccentric?" Universal frowned as he finished copying down the name. "I'm not sure I like the sound of that." Terry nodded to himself in the room. "I know... but he really is the best and given your situation I'm not sure anyone else could get the job done in time. Let me give you his number..." Chris Universal nodded as he copied down the phone number Terry read to him, and then smiled into the phone. "Well, given this crowd I'm sure we won't even notice. Listen Terry, the show's about to begin so I'd better head down to the floor. You take care of yourself, and I'll see you later this year. Bye." Chris Universal shut his phone with a click and took another long look over the city skyline. "Touma... well, I'm sure we won't even notice a little eccentric personality..." Somewhere in the distance thunder rumbled in the clouds. Universal glanced over toward it, a nervous feeling slowly creeping over him. If he was a more superstitious man he might take it as a bad omen... but Universal was not a superstitious man. With a final look at the city he walked into the arena and toward his destiny. We're Taking Over! Inside the arena, just about 24 hours removed from Unstable, Slaughter begins. The usual pyro and fireworks go off, shortly before a familiar theme song began to blast over the PA system. It was the theme song that could only be known as "BUM DA BUM BUM BWAMP!" Out walks the STUMP Squad, with Black Quicksilver leading the way, playing air guitar with the Wominternopean Jobberweight Title. Every member of the STUMP Squad is wearing a black t-shirt, with "pWo" written on them...in purple, of course. The STUMP Squad climb into the ring, and God help us all, each and every one, because Mike Heftel has a microphone. "Hey yo. Would you like to take a survey?", Heftel asks his adoring public, who has no idea what he's talking about. "Um...well...I'm gonna ask you stuff anyway! Did you come here to see...the fWo?" The fans in attendance "pop", before Heftel asks another question. "Or...did you come here to see...the pWo?" Heftel points the mic toward the crowd, and is met with a faint applause. "One more...for the good guys! Or...is that the bad guys? What are we supposed to be again?" BQ and Heftel both shrug, and Heftel hands BQ the mic. "That's right, the pWo is here! And now, allow me to introduce ourselves...I am the fearful leader of this fine organization, Hollywood Quicksilver! See, if you abbreviate that, you get "HQ"! Clever little pissant, aren't I?", BQ says, before pointing to Heftel. "This...right here...is...Da Job Guy!" Heftel poses for the crowd, before he removes a toothpick from behind his ear, and attempts to flick it into the crowd. Since it's Heftel, the toothpick bounces off a turnbuckle, and comes back at him, hitting him right in the eye. BQ continues, pointing to Jackhammer. "This tall drink of water...is...Big Ugly!" Jackhammer rolls his eyes as BQ turns his attention to Claire, who is holding Stump. "And rounding out the group is Claire...yup, it's just "Claire"...and Stumpp! For those wondering, that is not, I repeat, NOT, a typo!" Most of the fans note that Stump is wearing sunglasses and a headband, while BQ happens to look down at his shirt. "Wait. You know what I just realized? Our logo sucks! Who drew this crap? Mike?" Heftel has mysteriously come in contact with another microphone. "I don't know, but it looks like someone just spraypainted it on." "Yeah, it kinda does!", BQ replies. "But we're not supposed to be gang members...who's the graffiti artist? Kevin? I mean...Big Ugly? As you can see, I'm looking in your general direction." "I haven't the slightest clue.", answers Jackhammer. It seems that BQ heard a different answer from Jackhammer. "What's that? You said graffiti isn't just a style...you can draw it, too? Well, I don't care what you think. We don't have time for this arguement, as it will prove to be useless...just like us." BQ "adjusts" himself before speaking again. "We came here to say this...we're here for one reason, and one reason only...well, actually...two reasons. The first thing I wanted to say is that the Wominternopean Jobberweight Title is no longer on the line 24/7. It was just too damn tiring for me, and everyone knows how lazy I am, and therefore, I'd be nothing but a lazy person who's tired all the time. Alright, now that I got that out of the way, here's the biggie..." BQ lowers the mic, looks to the left, looks to the right, looks to the ring, points toward the ramp, and brings the mic back up. "We're takin' over!" "Christian Says" hits. The STUMP Squad scatters. "They're in my hair, they're in my hair!", Jackhammer shouts, as he is the last member of the STUMP Squad to hop the guardrail and head for higher ground, as 3vil make their way to the ring. The fWo World Champion and fWo World Tag Team Champions enter the ring, with the fans inside the Bradley Center booing them wildly. It'd been awhile since the fWo had been in Milwaukee, and with fall approaching that meant winter was right around the corner, and winters in Wisconsin were known to be very bitter and cold. The music of 3vil faded, and as Chronos attempted to talk, the loud boos wouldn't die down. 3vil seemed rather annoyed that every time Chronos attempted to speak, the boos were so loud it made it impossible for him to say anything and anyone inside the arena would hear it. Finally, Chronos raised his voice and spoke above the crowd. "We stand before you tonight... victorious. Last night, at Unstable, we were victorious!" Chronos spoke with authority, neglecting to mention that 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly had won the Unstable 'Survivor' Match and is now someone he would be facing at Halloween Hell. "The fact of the matter is, we proved that the fWo... is inferior to our faction. The three of us... along with the Alaskan Avalanche... set an example. An example that we continue to rule the entire promotion... with an iron fist!" The boos picked up a bit, and the trio in the ring laughed confidently. "And tonight, our rule will continue. Wretch... was the best mystery partner you guys could come up with? Well... Creed already put him back on the shelf, and I anticipate we won't be seeing him for awhile. Douglas... you were one of the so called 'best' the fWo put in the ring with us. You suffered the same fate the rest of your team did. Tonight, you will suffer the same fate as you will go against the Black Panther. And beware... after last night, I suspect he's not in a very good mood. As far as Ruben Ross goes... we suspect he won't be seen for awhile either. With the way the 'Main Man' has been manhandling him as of late, it wouldn't be a very good idea for him to show his face. And finally, that brings me to the Boston Strangler... earlier today, we were doing some talking... and we've decided that you will be facing someone that might prove to be your greatest challenge to date. Yes, that person... with one kick... could very well put you out of action forever. That person... is Tempest. Good luck with her Paul... she's a real bitch." Chronos laughed along with Frenchie & Primetime, before finally, Chronos ended things. "Given our performance at Unstable last night... I think the three of us will be taking the night off. No... nobody deserves a shot at my World Title this evening... and there isn't a tag team out there that can hang with Brian & Pierre here. We deserve a bit of a break..." The fans cheered, knowing that 3vil wouldn't be in the ring tonight. "... and we feel we are owed it." Frenchie asked Chronos for the microphone, and Chronos gladly passed it over to him. "W'at Ric here is trying to say... is zat none of you bastards in zis hellhole Wisconsin deserve to see ze t'ree of us put on a magnificent show for you all!" Frenchie laughed, before handing the microphone to Primetime. "Yeah, instead, you get to watch a bunch of untalented nobodies... really, I feel sorry for all of you people... you know, Pierre & I would really like to get in the ring with someone tonight and beat their asses like two redheaded stepchildren, but the fact of the matter is, nobody on the roster right now deserves to be in the ring with any of us!" A smirk came over the face of Primetime, while Frenchie beamed. Chronos attempted to talk again, but each time he did, he received more loud boos, and simply decided not to say anything else. 3vil began to exit the ring, while Slaughter went backstage... Meeting "Come in." Universal set a notebook aside, before Juno Law entered the room. "Thank you for flying out here tonight. I hope you brought your gear." Law appeared confused. "My... gear?" Universal nodded. "You see, you guys have been out of action since Bloodbath, and I understand you've had your problems... and new things have come up. But you have to wrestle. If you recall, I gave you guys time off back in March." Juno agreed. "Yeah... well... Kobi isn't here..." Universal shrugged. "I'm afraid you'll just have to go it alone. And I may have to release Kobi." Juno' jaw fell open. "Now... if you'll excuse me, Juno, I have some phone calls to make. I don't know who your opponent will be, but go talk to 3vil. I'm sorry, but they will be giving your responsibility for the evening. I really wish it was me... but, well, things don't always turn out the way you expect them to." Juno nodded, and thanked Universal, before exiting the room. Commissioner Sphere? Slaughter went backstage, although, more specifically, to Sphere's locker room, which has been turned into an office of sorts. Sphere sat behind the desk, looking at some paperwork, but was interrupted by Crucifix, the new fWo Hardcore Champion. Upon entering the room, Crucifix spotted Sphere, and realized he had made a mistake. "Oh, I'm sorry. I have the wrong room..." Crucifix started to turn around, but Sphere stopped him. "No, no, that's quite alright. I've been meaning to talk to you about that Hardcore Title, there. As the commissioner of the fWo, I've signed a match for you tonight...you'll be defending that title against every member of Doomsday." Crucifix was confused. "Oh, and it'll be a cage match as well. Now, bugger off." Crucifix left the room, and shut the door behind him. "Doomsday was a group that unsuccesfully tried to take over the fWo two years ago...and that guy isn't even the commissioner! Good thing I caught that, or else there'd be people wondering why I didn't face Doomsday tonight!" Crucifix walked off as Slaughter went elsewhere. The Black Panther vs. Douglas Returning from the first commercial break, "Du Hast" begins to play throughout the arena, and it is accompanied by a loud chorus of boos from the capacity crowd. Those same jeers intensify as Daniel Longshadow, The Black Panther, makes his way out onto the entrance ramp and heads down towards the ring, oblivious to the crowd and focusing on the task at hand. As vicious and feared of a competitor as The Black Panther is, he knows that his opponent, while still a relative newcomer to the fWo, is also considered a dangerous man in his own right... On cue, "Underdog" by Testeagles and an exploding pyro signal the arrival of that very man: Douglas.The crowd erupts as Steve Douglas wastes no time in getting to the ring, running full tilt and sliding under the ropes to lunge at the Black Panther. Immediately he and the Panther go at it tooth and nail, trading vicious punches while the ref signals for the bell to begin the match. After a series of punches, elbows, and chokes by both men that cause the fight to roll about the ring from corner to corner, a swift kick to the midsection by Douglas is followed up with a clothesline that knocks the masked giant off of his feet. Seeing this brief window of opportunity, Douglas drops a knee across the forehead of the Panther, and begins swinging away, trying to open up some crimson under the mask while also releasing some of the frustrations he has endured over the past few weeks. A knee to the groin by the Black Panther changes Douglas' perspective momentarily, as he crumples to the mat in agony. Never one to pass up a chance to inflict pain, the Panther pulls Douglas up by the hair and whips him into the ropes, following up with a back elbow that catches Steve flush in the mouth. Douglas spits out some blood from his mouth as he tries to get to his feet, checking to make sure he hasn't lost any teeth on the exchange. He turns just in time to be caught in the grip of the Panther, who drops him to the mat with a huge spinebuster, knocking the wind right out of him. The Panther half smiles, feeling the match in his control and heads off the ropes, dropping a forearm across the sternum of his opponent. Seizing the moment, the masked predator tries for a pinfall, but is rewarded by a one count and a hard kickout by Douglas for his efforts. He pulls Douglas to his feet, and goes for a suplex, which is blocked by his opponent. Again he tries, and to his surprise finds that Douglas not only blocks the move a second time, but follows it up with a suplex of his own that takes the big man down hard. Still trying to spit the blood from his mouth, Steve Douglas whips the Panther into the turnbuckle, catching him with a powerslam after watching him stagger back out of the corner. A quick pinfall attempt is met with a two count, as the Panther uses his massive strength to power right out of the predicament. The two grapplers roll to their feet, and jump right back at each other, to the joy of the crowd which roars it's approval. Again, they fight into the corner, with Douglas grabbing hold of the Panther, mounting the first turnbuckle, and headbutting him repeatedly as the crowd counts along. Steve jumps from the tunbuckle and backs up, waiting for the Panther to come to him. Still somewhat stunned by the effects of Douglas's skull striking his, the Panther stumbles forward just in time to be hoisted in the air by the 6'6" powerhouse. The crowd cheers, knowing that one move is coming... The Douglas Driver. However, before he can connect with his coup de grace, Douglas notices something out of the corner of his eye, heading towards the ring area... towards him. Two men, still unknown in what exactly they want with the fWo...but well known in their intended target. A.D. and Kenny Rock have arrived from the Asylum, heading straight for Douglas through the crowd. The fans begin to boo, and one even manages to toss a soda, striking A.D. He turns and sneers at the fan, wondering if that's the best he could do while Kenny Rock simply grins at Douglas in the ring, with one thing on his mind: Trouble. Douglas immediately drops the Panther to the mat with a thud and heads towards the ropes, ready to rush at the two fighters should they make a move towards the ring. Of course, both men are toying with him... neither makes an advance from behind the safety of the barricade and instead stand grinning and waving at Douglas. Steve leans over the ropes, shouting things at them that aren't quite fit for the audience to hear... and assuring them that they are next once he finishes with the Panther. Douglas turns to where he left the Panther laying, prepared to finish the match off... but realizes too late he has made a cardinal mistake: he turned his back on his opponent, and was distracted long enough for him to recover. The Panther takes full advantage of the situation that Douglas has allowed himself to be put in, connecting with a swift kick and the pump-handle slam... known as "The Killer Instinct"... while the ref calls for security to come down to ringside. The Panther quickly drops down to cover, and for good measure he grabs a handful of tights while rolling up a struggling Douglas for the pinfall. The ref records the three count, and the Panther slides from the ring victorious. He shoots a suspicious glance over at Rock and A.D. while he stalks up the entrance ramp, but they pay him no attention, focusing only on Douglas who by now has gotten to his feet and is quite angry. Steve pulls himself out of the ring and to the floor and heads towards the two Asylum competitors... but is stopped and held back by fWo security who have arrived just in time to break up the impending fight. Three members of the security force push Douglas up the ramp, talking him down all the way to the back while the remainder of the security team assists Rock and A.D. in finding the quickest exit out of the building. Heh, but now that I think about it... you can have rematches." "What do you mean?" Winters questioned. "Well, Einstein," Monte said sarcastically. "A series of matches, maybe a best of seven. That way I can prove to you... and the world that last night was a complete fluke. And the winner of the series gets my Cruiserweight title." Winters stroked his chin for a moment, deep in thought. He looked back up at the grinning face of Monte Burns. "Fine, deal. If I can knock you senseless repeatedly, it's a deal," Winters remarked. "You've made a mistake, Burns." Winters turned and left the dressing room. Monte sat back down and laughed. "Oh, don't be so naive," he said. "Just wait until you see what I've got up my sleeve." The scene faded to black and Slaughter went elsewhere. Interruption "What the hell do you want?" Juno rolled his eyes once again, before walking away. As he did, Frenchie & Primetime laughed behind his back... Spoiled Plans Tempest looked high and low for him. Leaning up against the wall, she whistled to past the time away. Suddenly, the person she was looking for entered the Bradley Center. A bag slung over his shoulder, he almost walked past her without a word. The Boston Strangler. But Tempest stood in his way. "Where do you think you're going?" she asked delightfully. Not knowing what she was up to, the Strangler replied and prepared to keep walking. "To our lockerroom." Tempest stood in his way still. "Well, I have something to tell you... That saves my whole groveling for my life routine I was gonna do here. But... it wouldn't hurt to at least try." Strangler seemed confused, until Tempest runs up and rapidly punches him in the stomach. Nearly smiling, Strangler yawns and pops her on top of her head. Tempest plops down on her butt, rubbing her head gently. Frowning, Tempest stands and leaps on a table nearby, before jumping off. Strangler catches her. Tempest evaluates her situation, before she frowns. "Put me down!" Never one to treat a lady poorly, Strangler sets her down. Tempest doesn't look happy... but all Strangler can do is shrug. Tempest grabs a nearby chair, and leaps and taps Strangler very lightly on the head. Strangler immediately falls down like a ton of bricks. Tempest smiles with delight. Strangler gets to his feet. "Don't know why our so called World Champion has so much trouble with you... you seem nice to us." Tempest smiled and gave him a friendly little hug. Elsewhere... "God dammit!" Chronos pushes the monitor off the table. It crashes with a thud, shattering into pieces. "They can't do this!" Chronos pounded his fist into a locker furiously. "No, they're not going to get away with this... Strangler is still going to wrestle tonight, against Erik Kelly. We'll see how Strangler goes from fighting a woman... to fighting a fucking monster. I saw Kelly' eyes last night, and he's determined. Strangler will pay... if he thinks he can get away with this... well, he's got another thing coming." Frenchie & Primetime agreed. "W'at if ze Strangler and ze Eradicator... well, w'at if zey don't wrestle ze match?" Chronos sneered. "Well, I'll make sure that they will... or else there will be consequences." Primetime nodded. "Such as? We can't fire them... unfortunately, we don't have that authority." Chronos shrugged. "I'll think of something... dammit! I'm just so... enraged right now. I need to take this anger out on someone. In a match. No titles of course would be on the line, as nobody deserves a shot at my title tonight anyways." Frenchie & Primetime glanced at one another, before smiling. "We will find you a worthy opponent." Frenchie nodded. "Yes... zey will be a top notch, established competitor. Someone that can give you a real challenge." "I don't care who it is... I just can't believe this. And Tempest, she's going to pay... in fact, I have an idea. If I'm fighting tonight, so are you guys. And your titles will be on the line... against Tempest... and Eddy Fate." Cheers resounded throughout the arena as they watched on the SlaughterScreen. Frenchie & Primetime seemed purturbed. "Zut?" "Yeah, what?" Chronos elaborated. "Don't worry about it, I have an idea... I know you guys will win the match." The boos, however, continue loudly as the fWo Cruiserweight Champion "The Golden Child" Monte Burns stands at the entranceway, smirking at the crowd with the title around his waist. Burns smile mockingly at a few fans as he walks down the ramp. Winters doesn't feel like waiting, however, and heads to the outside to start the match early. The two men meet at the bottom of the ramp, trading punch after punch as their emotions take control. Burns gains a quick advantage with a thumb to the eye and slams Donaven's head into the railing. After finally handing the Cruiserweight belt off to the ref, Burns connects with a pair of knife-edge chops that send Winters stumbling over to the apron. Burns charges at Winters, but Donaven hits a quick back elbow to stop Monte. Winters grabs Burns and slides him into the ring before following inside. The bell sounds to officially start the match as Winters whips Monte into the ropes and catches him with a flying clothesline. Winters makes an early cover, but Monte is up just as the referee hits two. Winters now has Burns set up for a suplex, but Burns blocks it with his leg and drops Winters with a suplex of his own. Burns holds the back of his head before quickly leaping to the top and flying off with an elbow. Winters was ready for the move, though, and rolls out of the way just in time. Monte is holding his arm in pain, but Winters shows no compassion, however, by dropkicking Burns right in the injured arm. Burns falls to the mat in pain as Donaven makes another cover that results in a two count. Winters remains on the attack, though, working on Monte's injured arm with an arm-bar submission hold. Burns is screaming in pain as the referee checks on him. Donaven wrenches back on the arm, but Monte refuses to submit and finally reaches out with his free arm to grab the bottom rope. Winters releases the hold and gets up to inflict more pain on Burns. The champion, however, grabs Winters's leg and rolls him up into a small package that almost gets three. Both men get up quickly, with Burns ducking a Winters clothesline and hitting a superkick that dazes the challenger. Burns grabs Winters and whips him into the ropes. Monte tries a spinning heel kick, but Winters catches his foot and then drops Burns with a single-arm DDT, doing more damage to the injured arm. Winters drops a leg across the back of Monte's head before climbing to the top rope. Winters leaps off with a shooting star press and connects this time on the fallen Burns. Winters covers, but Monte's leg is under the ropes. Now, Winters lifts Burns up and whips him hard into the corner. Burns is leaning back against the turnbuckles as Winters stands in front of him. Winters grabs Monte's jaw and looks him right in the eyes, telling Monte just how much Winters hates him. Winters now picks Burns up and sets him on the top turnbuckle before climbing up as well. Winters is starting to pick up Burns, looking as if he's going to try the Shattered Society from the top rope... Burns has this move well scouted, however, and manages to block Donaven from the attempt. Burns hits Winters with two rights to the head and a back elbow, as "The Fallen Angel" falls back to the canvas. Burns now steadies himself on the top turnbuckle, while Winters gets back to his feet. Monte leaps off with a missile dropkick to the jaw that has both men down on the mat. Burns is the first to his feet as he stumbles over to Winters and kicks him in the stomach. Fighting through the pain in his arm, Burns somehow lifts Donaven up and hits him with a double underhook backbreaker before making the cover. Winters is up at two, though, and Burns continues his assault by stomping Winters in the face. Burns watches as Winters slowly gets to his feet, still slumped over a bit. Then, Burns attacks, sending Winters violently backward with a side russian legsweep into another pin attempt. Again, Winters gets a shoulder up just in time. Burns lifts Winters up, staring his opponent in the eyes before whipping him into the ropes. Winters is able to duck a clothesline by Burns, but he misses a back elbow attempt and Burns kicks him in the gut and drops him with a swinging neckbreaker. Burns starts to make the cover, but decides instead to head into the corner and climb to the top. Burns looks down at Winters before looking out at the crowd, hating both equally. However, Winters has had time to recover and moves just in time to avoid The Establishment from Burns. Again, both men struggle to their feet with the aid of the ropes, meeting in the center of the ring in a flurry of punches. This time it's Winters who gains the advantage, doubling Monte over with a high knee. The third time, Kelly caught him for the chokeslam, and he hit it. His arm was injured, but he had just enough strength to complete the move. Kelly crawled over for the pin. There was no way Strangler would get up. But Chronos wouldn't count. Kelly realized what Chronos was doing. A brief confrontation ensued, but nothing but words were exchanged. From behind, Strangler rolled Kelly up for a pin. But Chronos wouldn't count. Realizing this, both men rushed to their feet, and both began heading towards Chronos' direction. Chronos didn't back down. They were falling into his trap. If they both resorted to physical harm on Chronos, both would be disqualified, and neither would get a title shot. So they gave Chronos an ultimatum. Either call the match down the middle, or they would risk being put in prison to ensure he would never walk again. "Touche." With that in agreement, the match continued. Kelly & Strangler exchanged lefts and rights, before Kelly sent Strangler to the ropes. Strangler returned and flattened him with a big boot. Kelly went for a cover. Well... Chronos lied. Frustrated, Kelly brought him up once more and sent him to the ropes. Kelly went for the chokeslam, but Strangler grabbed his arm, and went for the Massacre. Kelly broke free, before shoving Strangler back into the corner. He came back out and this time he ducked a clothesline, but when he turned around Kelly wrapped his massive hand around Strangler' throat and planted him into the mat with another chokeslam. Rather than going for the cover, he brought Strangler up, and positioned him for the Eradication. "POWERBOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMB!" echoed off the arena walls. Kelly brought him up, but Strangler spun out of it and landed on his feet when Kelly attempted to follow through. A kick to the stomach and a DDT followed, and then a cover. But Chronos... wouldn't... count. Strangler confronted Chronos in the corner, and that gave Kelly time to recover. Kelly charged and unleashed all three hundred and thirty six pounds into both of them in the corner. Strangler staggered out dazed, and Chronos seemed to have had the life knocked out of him. Chronos fell to the mat, and now Kelly decided that he was going to ensure that he would get a World Title shot. Regardless, Strangler would as well if Kelly won... and it'd be better that both of them got that shot, than one... or not at all. Kelly brought Strangler up, and this time completed the Eradication. But to ensure that Strangler stayed down, he repeated it yet again. Kelly made sure Chronos was revived to count the pin. Perhaps if Chronos wasn't so groggy, he would remember what he was doing and not count the pin. However... he just got crushed by well over six hundred pounds of Kelly & Strangler. Primetime & Frenchie rushed out from backstage and attempted to notify Chronos of what he was doing, but it was too late. One. Two. Three. Kelly would be facing Chronos in some form at Halloween Hell. Strangler would be getting a shot as well. Primetime & Frenchie neared the ring, and retrieved their colleague from the ring, as fireworks exploded throughout the arena. Kelly smirked at 3vil, knowing he had gotten the best of them on this night, and that finally, the good guys had pulled out some big wins. Kelly helped a semi-unconscious Strangler to his feet, and as 3vil stood on the stage, they looked in the ring and realized that Kelly & Strangler were two forces that even the three of them might not be able to overcome... And with that, Slaughter faded to black.
