Slap On The Wrist A very meek Cabbot Wilson stood in the dimly lit office, shadows engulfing most of the room. The light, what there was of it, barely illuminated Ted Tallison's face as he sat behind his desk. Being the smallest and weakest in a family of professional wrestlers, Cabbot knew that feeling all too well. He'd stood there for almost a minute before Tallison addressed him. "Last week, your client didn't show up for work." Cabbot decided not to tell Ted that Vic had disappeared for a better part of the week. He couldn't reach Creed at all, and had no idea where he was.
