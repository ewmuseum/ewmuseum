"I remember when we, Deacon and I," the mention of Deacon's name increaseed the 'heel' reaction tenfold. "When we first began, people called us backwater, old-fashioned, religious freaks. They would do skits showing Deacon as some 'Deliverance' banjo player. But they didn't know he was from a country far from the land of 'hollers and criks'. Deacon was from here." With that statement, the crowd went ballistic with boo's that few had ever received in the fWo. "I remember when a young upstart, who claimed what in the US is referred to as, 'an alternative lifestyle', had a problem with Deacon. He would never understand us as long as our teaching would not condone his choices in life. What he didn't get, was that our teaching was our life and our choice. And just like he didn't care whether we agreed or not with his choices, we didn't care whether or not he agreed with ours. And we choose Christ." Chris had never been so bold in the fWo, but if they were going to boo him and Deacon, he would give them what he believed they most needed the truth. "When Deacon came out of Egypt, he never thought he would return. He knew if he did, the people of ... 'his' land would hate him. Even as .One talks of how Chaos has led him to the fWo to bring about destruction. Even as Spyder has cheated his way to the championship, or as it were, attempting to cheat himself back into the championship. Even as two video game junkies cheat to victory after victory and one foul mouthed Borst does all that is considered wrong by any society in this world. You would rather hate one of your own. Maybe it's because of what we say, or believe, or think. Or maybe it's because we're right in the midst of you. We're reaching out with the love of God and showing you that we have the faith to believe that no matter what evil you do, God will use those of us among you to reach out to you as friends, and show you a real living savior in our lives. Maybe it's the fact that we're in the midst showing you that..." Chris paused for a moment as- - The TravisTron cut to a blank screen. The Gregorian chant built again, but this time it was a different ancient song this time it was in Arabic. With the crescendo in music, the boo's got louder to keep it in check. A single spotlight brightly shined above the ring, illuminating it and allowing the crowd for the first time to see that a robed Deacon, pointing to the east, had been in the middle of them all the time. Several people tossed trash at the Deacon, and when the first piece actually hit 'him', it passed right through. The crowd let out a collective gasp. The eastern walls of the coliseum illuminated, and standing atop one of the walls was a man in a robe. The figure of Deacon was in a prayer position. "Fait' - -" The unmistakable Middle-Eastern accent of Deacon could be heard echoing throughout the stadium. The figure took off it's robe to reveal that the real Deacon was standing there. He looked at the people, his people, and scanned the entire Coliseum. "- - is t'e EVIDENCE!" Deacon shot his arms out into a cross position, at the same time, multicolored fireworks shot in the air to explode, sending the red and blue and green shimmering sparks falling from the cool, night sky above. Deacon stood there, holding the crucifix pose as the lights continued to brightly show him. The TravisTron shot to him live atop the walls. Deacon stared into the camera and then looked to the heavens. The camera cut, and the lights went out. The Deacon had brought his faith to the scariest stage of his life. Erik Kelly vs. Nick Brandish As 'Ironman' by Black Sabbath filled the air, the fans in attendance weren't quite sure who was making their way to the ring. Even after 'Nasty' Nick Brandish stepped out into the cool evening air, the fans still were somewhat less than enthusiastic to see the big man lumber to the ring. After all, Brandish was a relative unknown to not only the fWo fans, but to wrestling fans in general. Wearing his street clothes, Brandish stood in the center of the ring, defying his opponent to come out and dance with the Grim Reaper. The music faded, replaced by the dull silence of the crowd. That didn't last long as "Bodies" by Drowning Pool cued up. As 'POOOOOWERBOOOOMB!' echoed into the heavens, the fans were on their feet. The fWo World Champion, Erik Kelly, charged out from the back. Brandish didn't budge, only watched as Kelly slid into the ring. As soon as Kelly's head cleared the bottom rope, the referee signaled for the bell. Apparently Nick Brandish waited for the bell before punching the time clock. No sooner had Kelly entered the ring than Brandish exploded on him, driving his knee into Kelly's face with his full four hundred-pound frame behind it. Kelly staggered backward and tumbled over the top rope. Brandish rolled out after him, pulling the World Champ to his feet by his hair. He scooped up the Eradicator and drove his back into the ringpost, then bodyslamming him to the ground. Brandish stomped Kelly's head several times before walking over to the barricade. Erik tried to shake the cobwebs as he grabbed the ring apron. He'd never seen Brandish wrestle before. He knew nothing about his style, other than he was one of the few men in the fWo who was actually heavier than the Eradicator. Erik had the height advantage and would have to start using his reach to Further thoughts were stopped as Nasty Nick crushed him against the side of the ring. Clubbing forearm after clubbing forearm drove Kelly down to one knee. The referee reprimanded Brandish, telling him to get the match back in the ring. Nick stepped away from Kelly. He took a moment to suggest to the referee that he take his head and cram it into his own rectal cavity. A moment's distraction. A moment to recover. A moment was all Kelly needed. As Nick turned back to continue his assault, he was introduced to the bottom of Erik Kelly's foot as the champ tried to kick Nick's head into the fifth row. The big boot took Brandish off his feet, and now it was Erik Kelly's turn to stomp a mudhole in Nasty Nick's carcass. Brandish rolled onto his stomach and tried to crawl under the ring. Kelly shook his head and dragged Nick to his feet, rolling him into the ring. The match, as they say, was on. Erik sent Brandish into the ropes. He charged forward with a clothesline and caught Nick across the chest. Nasty Nick didn't budge. He frowned and pointed to the ropes, daring Kelly to charge again. Kelly came off the ropes and struck Brandish across the chest again. Again, Nick didn't budge. Erik nodded. "Okay tough guy, let's see what you've got." Nasty Nick obliged and came off the ropes with a clothesline of his own. Kelly ducked the blow, allowing the momentum to send Brandish into the opposite ropes. This time Kelly nearly jerked him out of his boots as he planted him with a spinning powerslam. The fans cheered, clearly impressed with Kelly's power and style. Erik continued his attack, sending Brandish into the turnbuckle and following him in with a shoulderblock. With the big man trapped in the corner, Erik leaned against him and drove his elbow into the side of Brandish's head. The assault continued. Lefts and rights from the Eradicator, peppering Nick's head and thick body. After a warning from the referee, Kelly switched to knife-edge chops. He was wearing a T-shirt, but the smacking sound Erik raised from Nick's chest could be heard throughout the arena. That seemed to wake Brandish up, as Nasty Nick started firing back with chops of his own. The two big men worked their way to the middle of the ring, each hitting the other harder than their previous blow. Ugly red welts started growing on Kelly's chest, and even though Brandish's chest was covered he was showing the effects of the Eradicator's attack. Kelly reared back for another chop and accidentally elbowed the referee in the head. The official stumbled backward, which gave Nick the chance to kick Erik Kelly in the groin. Hard. Field goal hard. Kelly doubled over and fell to the mat. With the referee still out of the picture, Brandish slid out of the ring and grabbed a chair. He quickly re-entered the ring and sized up the World Champ, measuring him for a devastating chair shot to the back. Kelly clutched his back in pain as Brandish tossed the chair out of the ring. The referee pulled himself to his feet as Brandish splashed Kelly with his four hundred pound mass. He rolled Kelly onto his back and hooked his leg for the pin. 1... 2... Kickout. Nick couldn't believe it. He came off the ropes with another splash, this one to Kelly's ribs. He hooked the leg and went for the pin a second time. 1... 2... Kickout... again. Brandish pulled Kelly up and whipped him into the ropes. Kelly reversed the whip, clutching his ribs as he did so. As Brandish came off the ropes, Erik grabbed him by the throat. A chokeslam? On a four hundred pound man? Nick grabbed Kelly by the throat and punched him in the ribs, forcing Kelly to release his grip. It was Nick Brandish who delivered the chokeslam. "It's over!" he announced to the fans as he hooked Erik Kelly for what looked to be a vertical suplex... the setup for the Lifespike. Nick Brandish looked confident as he lifted the World champion into the air. But there was still fight left in the Eradicator, and with a mid-air adjustment he landed behind Nasty Nick. Nick turned and caught a stiff kick to the stomach. He almost turned green as he doubled over. Kelly stuck the big man's head between his legs. "POWERBOOOOOMB!" he yelled before bracing himself. Nasty Nick went up... Nasty Nick came down with a thunderous Eradication. Erik cringed and clutched his ribs as he went for the pin. 1... 2... 3. Erik Kelly had extracted a measure of revenge. The fans were on their feet, celebrating alongside the fWo World Champion. Everything seemed fine. Then the TravisTron came to life. Result: Erik Kelly via Pinfall Stalking Your Prey Erik Kelly and the rest of the outdoor audience looked to the TravisTron as it came to life with the image of Kelly's biggest rival, who also happens to be the #1 Contender he will face at Cyberslam. Kodiak Vic Creed. Creed was outside somewhere, his trademark sunglasses worn even late at night. He grins in his non-chalant manner as he gets a chorus of unheard boos from the Egyptian audience. Kelly puts his hands on his hips, not knowing what to make of this. Creed hadn't been seen for a few shows, and apparently no one knew where he was. "Yo, Kelly. I'm figuring you just took Nicky for a ride. That's alright. The lug probably had a fifth of vodka before facing you. No matter. I just wanted to send you a little message to let you know we're all fine here." The camera went to Kelly, who's puzzled expression said it all. The crowd was fairly silent as well. "What...don't look so stupid with that look, Kelly. You should know. You've always known. While you've been too busy paying attention to your job, I'm busy paying attention to the one thing you don't have anytime for. And I'm taking care of "her" real well." Erik's face started to lighten up in disbelief. "Oh...you are as dumb as you look then. Well, since you apparently think I'm lying to you..." The camera zooms away slowly from Creed. Creed stands in front of the entrance door of a hospital. A sign comes into view. Chicago Hope Medical. Erik Kelly's heart sunk. In one swift moment, an enraged 'Eradicator' jumps out of the ring and runs up the entrance and out of the arena. He doesn't hear Creed's last words. "Don't worry, Kelly. I'll keep your bitch warm for you." The camera catches Erik Kelly out back. Running for his locker area, the camera catches a glimpse of Kristen Jacobs with a bag in hand holding Erik's things. She exchanges it, and yells 'Go Go Go!' to a quick departing Erik Kelly out the back entrance. Erik finds his rental car, and still in his wrestling gear, jumps into the vehicle, gets it running, and drives off. Slaughter goes off the air as the car disappears from view.
