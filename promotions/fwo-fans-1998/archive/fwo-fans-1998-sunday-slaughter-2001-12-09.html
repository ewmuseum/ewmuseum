Introduction "Doc? You in here?" Eli Flair opened the dressing room door, hoping to find Dr. Lovejoy. They had parted company after his promo at the hotel, and he was hoping to see her once or twice more before her assignment was over. "Eli?" The voice was a familiar one, but not the doctor, "In here." Eli entered the room to see Poison Ivy in full "Psycho Bitch" attire - red cotton dress, black fishnets, and knee- high combat boots. She'd been dealing with some family affairs all week, hence her absence from Eli's talks with the doctor. "Hey," she said, "You're late. Dr. Lovejoy waited around as long as she could, but she had to go." Ivy looked into her bag for a moment and withdrew a sealed envelope with Eli's name written across the front. "She left this for you, she said you were going to be waiting for it." Eli stared at the envelope, reaching out for it but afraid to, at the same time. He was never interested in what anyone else thought about him, but for some reason, he had to know. HE HAD TO KNOW. Vengeance The room was decorated opulently only the best for the dastardly trio known as 3vil. Primetime was lounging on the leather couch with a wine glass in hand, watching as the Seattle Seahawks were repeatedly raped by NFL officials. The Flying Frenchie was sitting on a plush chair, furiously pressing buttons on a controller, no doubt looking for his own special brand of revenge after last week's debacle. Well, it would just be a damn shame if one of our opponents... couldn't make it, now would it? Melodies Of Life, Coda Eli opened the letter carefully, withdrawing two folded pages. "Dear Eli, I spent the week before meeting you familiarizing myself, not with your life, but with your work. It became apparent to me very quickly that this would not be an assignment I'd relish, for how am I supposed to analyze a man who drops another man headfirst into a pile of glass to thousands of cheers, then opens a beer with him after the show? I was never so glad to be wrong in my life. I've come to realize that your parents play a significant role in your ethics of dealing with the rest of the world. They both died at very young ages, leaving you as their only legacy. Rapidly approaching the age that they passed, your thoughts are slowly becoming overcome with those that wonder how you will be remembered - if at all. Your stance toward telling your fans how they can leave their mark on the world if they simply try to, is indicative of your constant reinforcement of your own parents as 'statistics' - and your own predication toward that life seemed to be enough of a deterrent. Yet you continue to downplay and avoid situations where you will have to act like a celebrity. This little mental roadblock is the cause of a good deal of your anxiety. You also have trouble committing to a relationship. This is no big secret or surprise, considering your track record. However, after spending a limited time with Angela, I've come to the conclusion that she's tough enough to survive whatever you've got in store for her. You said that Ana Maria killed herself because you were never there. You said that Emily left because you were never there. Some women cannot handle a lover who spends his life on the road. Angela is a singer. She's a twenty- six year old woman who has spent the last nine years of her life on the road. She can handle it. People call professional wrestlers showmen, and a dozen other things. You and your friends fight for what you believe in. You are warriors. You all somehow manage, regardless of the circumstances, to never lose your dignity. The most stable elements in the periodic table are roughly between iron and silver. Look deep within yourself, Eli.... and ask yourseif if those who left you behind would be proud of you. If your parents would think you carried on their name with the dignity it entails. The very name, FLAIR, is one of dignity. If your wife, Alicia, would be happy with the way you've continued to live, after her loss. Those who have passed on already, Eli, can no longer influence how you live your life. You must be your own man, and not wonder whether or not those who have already gone before you would be proud. They already are. Between iron and silver, Eli.... I think that suits you. All my best, Dr. Jennifer Lovejoy." Confrontation Ric Chronos moved down the hallway with all of the confidence of a man who was firmly in charge of his surroundings. He sneered at cameramen and others in the production staff and patted the fWo World Heavyweight Championship that was slung over one shoulder. He made sure to lock eyes with people as he passed, turning the championship belt slightly as he went so there was no mistaking what he had over his shoulder. If Ric Chronos was worried about the match coming up with Ruben Ross it didn't show on his face. Chronos moved like a man filled with the assurance that he was in no danger of losing his spot any time soon. Spotting a cameraman attaching a battery pack to a digital camera, Chronos moved toward him with a hard, intense gaze. "You. Chump. Get over here." The cameraman's head snapped up and a clear expression of panic swept over his face. He glanced frantically around him in the hopes that Chronos was addressing someone else. No such luck, all of the other members of the production crew had quickly departed when they saw Chronos coming. It figured... he was so into his equipment that he didn't see the danger until it was too late. Slowly and with great apprehension the cameraman walked toward Chronos, leaving his camera behind on a metal storage case. "No you idiot... bring the equipment! You think I'm calling you over here for the pleasure of your company? Little puissant, get your damn camera!" The cameraman froze, then retrieved the camera and came forward more slowly still. Chronos seemed to be growing more and more frustrated, and the cameraman hoped he would escape this encounter without needing to go to the hospital. Chronos barked orders with the aggressive nature of a man who was used to being on top. "I'm going to make your career puissant... pick up the camera and take some shots of the most dynamic force ever to hit a wrestling ring. Your shots will be on the cover of the fWo magazine, so do it right or it's your ass. Get it?" The cameraman blinked, then looked around himself. "Wait... are you sure about this? Just back here? Don't you want... I don't know... some kind of setting?" Chronos grabbed out and clutched the cameraman around the neck. He squeezed, and the cameraman gagged and began to turn red. "You saying I need props? You saying I need god damn props? You're looking at Ric Chronos chump... you're looking at a god damn star! You take the picture before I shove that camera up your ass chump!" Chronos released the cameraman and tossed the fWo World Heavyweight Championship on a metal crate behind him. He flipped his hair back and fixed his face in a stern expression as the cameraman snapped pictures. Ric Chronos crossed his arms, glaring for the camera. More pictures were taken, and Chronos reached behind him for his championship belt. His hand brushed against the metal of the crate, empty. Chronos blinked, and turned in place expecting to see that the belt had fallen off of the crate onto the floor. Fallen stood behind Ric Chronos, the fWo World Heavyweight championship held in one hand. His face as expressionless, and Chronos was surprised into silence at seeing him there. Fallen glanced at the title he held, weighing it in his hand and looking at the tainted reflection he made in the steel. Chronos found himself and took an angry step forward. "You. You're not supposed to be here ant. I took you out once and I'll do it again. Now get your grimy hands off of my belt... that isn't yours and never will be. Hand it over so I can get started kicking your ass." Fallen looked at the belt again, but didn't hand it over. "Looks clean. Looks like you've spent time polishing your belt Ric. That how it is? That still how it is? You always cared about little pieces of metal Chronos. I guess it's gotten you pretty far..." "Farther than you, ant," Chronos injected, lunging for the title in Fallen's grasp. Fallen took a step backward and shook his head. "Not just yet Ric. We were partners once... we've got some catching up to do." Ric Chronos sneered. "I carried you to a few victories. That's all it ever was. We don't have anything to say to each other." "No?" Fallen glanced down at the title in his hand again. "Then let me catch you up to some things Ric. This title here? Well, this title is mine. I beat you for it and maybe you hold enough power to worm your way out of accepting your own incompetence, but you know that I beat you and that belt rightfully belongs to me. You think about that when you get in the ring with Ross. You remember that you were beaten and the only reason you're the champion is because you've wormed your way into a little bit of power. Without your power and puppets you're nothing Ric. You think about that." Chronos lunged forward and this time Fallen released the belt. Chronos snapped it up over one shoulder, furious to the point where his face was becoming red. "You're right about one thing, chump... I do have the power. I am the power. Not just in the fWo, but in all of wrestling. Everyone in this industry answers to me and they always will... but you don't have to worry about that. I'm going to toss you out of here now hump, and you're never coming back." Fallen turned, his hair down over his face. There was a smile there, but beneath the hair it wasn't visible to Chronos. "That's fine Ric. Toss me out. You're certainly powerful enough to move someone out of a building... not powerful enough to keep me from beating you and not powerful enough to keep me from taking your belt, but I suppose powerful enough to use a telephone to call security and have me removed from a building." Chronos extended an arm and grabbed Fallen by the neck. He pulled him forward until Fallen could smell Chronos' breath and see the angry sharpness of his eyes. "I can beat you any time I want, John. I can take you out any time I want, John. You want me to put you into the hospital or the grave, John? Fine. Get out to the ring and I'll do it right now." Fallen stared into Ric's eyes. Had it really been so long ago that they were partners? That they depended on each other? Looking at Ric now... it seemed to have been an eternity. "I don't work here, Ric. You have the power, you made sure of that." Ric Chronos and Fallen stared at each other in silence. A thin smile crept over Ric's face and he released Fallen as he stepped backward. "Ah, John. You're not near as subtle as you believe. I am no fool, and I understand your little game today. You're right, I do have the power. And you're right, by my own power I can't give you the beating you deserve tonight. You want me to reinstate you, is that it? Well John, your little game has backfired on you. I know your plan and I'm not a fool. But as anyone will tell you I am a fair man, and I'll give you a chance to prove you're worthy of getting your head handed to you by the best wrestler in the world. You want back in the fWo? You can fight for it John. You win, I'll consider letting you back in the fWo. Or you can just walk away... you're no stranger to that, are you John? You've done that before, haven't you John?" Fallen was silent, his face impassive. Ric looked frustrated with Fallen's lack of response, and grunted his disgust. He was turning to leave when Fallen spoke. "When." Ric Chronos turned slowly, the smile back on his face. "Show up at Twilight, John. I'll arrange everything else." Fallen stood steady, his face still impassive. "Who." Ric Chronos smiled wider. "Oh, you don't get to know that, John. Don't get to know who or how. You just show up and I'll take care of everything else. Or don't show up and walk away like you've done before. Doesn't really matter to me either way, chump." Fallen didn't show any signs of acknowledgement, but glanced at the title belt hung over Ric Chronos' shoulder. "Keep it shined up, Ric. It won't be yours for long." Before Ric could respond Fallen turned and vanished around the corner of a hallway. Ric watched him go, the smile fading into a deep frown. He heard a coughing noise behind him and turned to see the cameraman picking up his equipment. His eyes narrowed and he lunged forward, grabbing the cameraman by the neck and slamming him up against a wall. The cameraman gasped in pain, then slumped down in a tangle on the ground. Chronos picked up the camera and snapped it in his hands, tossing the broken plastic down on top of the limp body of the cameraman. Growling to himself, he patted the fWo World Heavyweight Championship belt and paced away, resuming his search for Ruben Ross.
