Sunday Slaughter, 12 August 2001

12 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Philadelphia. The so-called City Of Brotherly Love. Slaughter was a few short hours away, and it didn't appear like there would be any 'love' at all tonight in the city of Philadelphia. With 3vil running around hurting everyone, and seemingly everyone in the promotion have a grudge with someone, tonight, Philadelphia might as well be the city of pain. Nevertheless, Slaughter began outside of the First Union Center. A familiar vehicle pulled up outside, and a familiar face got out. It was the face of fate. Eddy Fate, to be exact. Eddy thanked the person for the ride; and said he'd see them later, before walking into the First Union Center. But as he got inside, little did he know, the person that dropped him off pulled the car into a secluded area. They got out of the vehicle & shut the door. The person was someone Eddy Fate knew, and knew well. The person was about six feet tall, with red hair, and a very curvacious body. The person was his wife. The person was... Destiny. With that out of the way, Slaughter proceeded to its opening montage and introductions... Giving In? Slaughter opened inside the arena with the usual fanfare. Fireworks were blasting, the crowd was cheering, and everyone was looking forward to another night of fWo action and excitement. Just then, the opening sequence was ground to a halt, and music begin to blare over the loudspeakers... ...'O Fortuna'. The Boston Strangler appeared at the top of the ramp, wearing a neck brace due to the injuries at the hands of 3vil. Without so much as looking at the cheering crowd, the Boston Strangler made his way down the ramp, tossed his belt over the top rope, and slid into the ring after it. Demanding a microphone, he iwas tossed one from Matt Panzer and stood staring down at his belt, still in the middle of the ring. "This... has gone on long enough." The crowd went silent, the anger in his voice hushing them in order to better hear him. "Week, after week... we come to the arena, hoping to defend our title. Week after week, we're put into a tag match of some kind. And week after week... we're laid out with a chair, or a ring bell, or a 3 on 1 beating. And why?" The Strangler bent down and lifted up the Heavyweight Championship belt. "Because of this. Because of a worthless trinket, we have 3 men trying to end our career week in and week out. But no more... we're tired of being mugged at the end of every show, we're tired of going to the hospital... we're tired of fighting a battle that we cannot win. You win Chronos. We give up. Come and take your damned belt... it isn't worth our career." A loud chorus of boos accompanied Ric Chronos as he stalked to the ring, a small smile on his face while "Christian Says" by Tones on Tail plays in the background. Chronos ducked between the ropes and circled the Strangler who merely stood in the middle of the ring, holding the belt out in his hand, not even bothering to look up. Finally, Chronos also procured a microphone, and pointed at the Strangler with a grin. "You disappoint me, Paul. Truly, you do. Oh, don't color me surprised that you gave up. Only a damned fool wouldn't have given in to the three of us... but I was hoping for a little more fun before I moved in for the kill. But no... not you Paul. You didn't even try! How does that make you feel?" The Strangler didn't answer him, and instead just dropped the belt to the canvas in front of Chronos, who shrugged and begun to speak again. Well, suit yourself then. It just makes life easier for both of us anyways, right? Let me get this out of your way for you so you can get your sorry self out of *my* ring..." With a chuckle, Chronos stooped over to pick up the belt. But before his fingers could touch the gold that he has been pursuing, something stopped him. A gutwrench powerbomb by the Strangler. As Chronos rolled about the mat, having been dropped squarely on the belt, the Strangler snatched the title from the canvas, just in time to give a taste of the gold to the Flying Frenchie and Primetime who have rushed down to the ring to their friend's aid. He then flopped out of the ring between the ropes, taking his title with him, and leaving a message for the members of 3vil who have begun to get to their feet. "If you three actually believe that we would give in to you... that it would be that easy... then your chances of survival at Bloodbath just got slimmer... Chronos, if you want this belt so badly... at the pay-per-view... just try and take it from us." Having said this, the Strangler walked to the back, holding the title high as the crowd cheered.... and 3vil stood in the ring, plotting their next move. Protest Monte Burns stood in the hallway, a cell phone against his ear and an unhappy expression on his face. Whoever was on the other end, it was clear that he was commiserating with Burns over his latest predicament "I have to team with Black Quicksilver tonight. Black Quicksilver! The little idiot that dumped that damn belt on me... yeah, one and the same. And that frigging Universal... the nerve of him, to actually tag me up with that loser. I swear, I oughta buy his ass out or something... I'm sure I have more money." Pause. "Yeah, good point I know I wouldn't want to own this place with 3vil running around. I guess I'm just gonna have to take care of this myself. Damned if I want to tag with BQ, but if you want something done, you gotta do it yourself. I'll pin one of the morons we're facing shouldn't be hard, considering it's only the STUMP Squad and dump the title on them once and for all." As Burns spoke, Black Quicksilver walked up from behind him and slapped him on the back. Burns, startled, jumped, throwing the cell phone forward onto the ground where it hit with a shatter. "C'mon, Monte, we're up next! Let's go!" BQ strolled off, humming a happy tune, and Burns snarled. The sooner this was over, the better... Monte Burns/Black Quicksilver vs. STUMP Squad "War" hits, and the crowd cheers politely as the STUMP Squad, Jackhammer and Mike Heftel, make their way to the ring. They converse and choose Heftel to start out. "Slave To The Grind" plays, and the cheers intensify a good deal as fWo Cruiserweight Champion Black Quicksilver makes his way to the ring and hops up into his corner. Finally, "Nobody's Real" plays and the cheers turn to boos as Monte Burns slowly walks to the ring, sneering all the way. Burns unstraps the Jobberweight Title and tosses it carelessly aside. BQ, meanwhile, hands his title off to a ring attendant, who takes it to the timekeeper. Burns turns to BQ and tells him in no uncertain terms that he, Monte, will be starting off the match. The bell rings, and Burns and Heftel lock up. Burns gains the advantage with a quick kick to the midsection. With Heftel doubled over, Burns hooks his head and spikes him to the mat with a double-arm DDT. Wanting to get it over as quickly as possible, Burns makes the cover. Heftel kicks out at two. Burns brings Heftel to his feet and hooks him up from the side, then drives him to the mat with a russian legsweep. With Heftel down, Burns runs into the ropes, leaps onto the second strand, and leaps off with an asai moonsault. He covers and hooks the leg, but again only gets two. Burns brings Heftel up once more and whips him into the corner. Monte backs up, then charges in with a big spinning heel kick that clocks Heftel. Burns lands on the apron and steps back into the ring, then begins to unload on Heftel with chops. Completely focused on losing his title, Burns brings Heftel out of the corner and lifts him up in a suplex position. Heftel, miraculously, shifts his weight and rolls Burns back into a small package. He gets two. They both get up, and Heftel ducks a Burns lariat and rolls to his corner. He tags in Jackhammer. Burns sees Jackhammer charging and catches him with a knee to the gut, doubling him over. Burns runs into the ropes. As he does so, BQ slaps him on the back, tagging himself in. Burns, focused, ignores it and catches Jackhammer with a swinging neckbreaker. He covers, but the ref doesn't count. BQ perches on the top rope and yells at Monte to move so he can hit Jackhammer. Burns is puzzled, and the ref explains that a tag has been made. Burns stomps the mat and curses. He walks through the ropes into his corner, taking special care to, out of spite, make sure the ropes jiggle enough for BQ to fall and crotch himself. Jackhammer gets up and presses BQ off the top rope to the mat. He covers, but BQ kicks out at two. Jackhammer brings BQ up and positions him for a powerbomb. He lifts BQ up, but BQ rolls down his back and takes him down with a sunset flip. It gets two. Both men get up, and Jackhammer nearly decapitates BQ with a clothesline. He then tags out to Mike Heftel. Heftel hits the ring and runs into the ropes. As he comes off, Jackhammer picks him up by the legs. He turns to drop Heftel on BQ, but Monte grabs BQ by the boot, then pulls him aside in time. Heftel splats into the mat, and Burns drags BQ back into his corner. He then "tags" BQ's prone body and hits the ring. He catches Jackhammer with a leaping forearm, catching him off-guard and knocking him from the ring. He then hooks up Heftel and drops him with a double-underhook backbreaker. Burns climbs to the top rope and leaps off, nailing Heftel with the Establishment. Burns makes the cover and secures the 3-count. He pumps his fist, thinking he's lost the Jobberweight Title. BQ, recovered, joins Burns and raises his hand for a high-five. Burns just glares. BQ smiles through his mask. "C'mon, Monte, be happy! Even though you won, you're still the Jobberweight Champion!" Burns frowns. What's going on? BQ continues. "It was a tag match, not a singles title match, so the belt's still yours! Isn't that great?" The referee hands the belt to Burns, who looks at BQ, looks at Heftel, looks at the ref, who is also holding BQ's Cruiserweight Title, looks back at BQ, then reluctantly takes the damn Jobberweight belt. He sulks his way out of the ring and heads backstage as Slaughter goes, well, backstage... Result: Monte Burns/Black Quicksilver by pinfall Punishment The door to Chris Universal's office opened, and in walked the three men who had made life for the fWo a living hell since they had joined forces weeks ago. Primetime. The Flying Frenchie. Ric Chronos. 3vil. Universal got up from his chair, clearly pissed. "I'd going to advise you three right now, and I'd suggest you take my advice. Sit the hell down, shut the hell up, and listen the hell to me. Got it?" 3vil exchanged bemused looks. Chronos looked at Universal. "And you called us in here... why?" "I'll tell you why. I'm not going to stand for the kind of crap you pulled last week. You damn near permanently took out the World Champion, the Tag Team Champions, the ring announcer, the security staff, and you better bet your ass you're not just going to walk away from that. Not as long as Chris Universal's running this place." Primetime laughed. "Is that a fact? Who's to say we won't just corner your pathetic ass right here and now, cripple you for life, and then go out and finish the jobs we started last week?" Universal stood his ground. "Because if you do, I'll sure as hell as plenty of grounds to fire all three of your asses, won't I? Hell, you oughta be polishing my shoes right now, thanking me that I don't just fire you anyway after last week." 3vil backed down, but didn't back off. "Tell us, zen..." said the Frenchie. "If you're so willing to just fire us wit'out a second thought after last week, why haven't you done so?" "Because I've got something better in mind, that's why. Namely the three of you getting your asses kicked all around the First Union Center." "Really?" sneered Chronos. "You're damn right. For example, I think it's be a very fitting punishment for you, Pierre, to face the East Coast Laws in a Handicap Match. After all, you want those tag titles so bad, so here's your chance to weaken the champs before the pay-per-view... if they don't put you out of action, that is." Frenchie snarled. "Eat merde and die, putain." "No, I'll pass. Moving on to Primetime, I'm going to give you a World Title shot tonight." Primetime's face briefly showed confusion, but he shrugged and smiled. Universal smiled as well, and Primetime's smile began to die down. "Of course, I can't forget there'll be three stipulations. Number one, it's No-DQ. Number two, the rest of 3vil is banned from ringside and oh yeah, that goes for Frenchie's match too. And number three, there'll be a special referee... Douglas." "You'll pay for this, Universal." "Actually, I think not. But, while we're speaking of Douglas, I've got some news for you too, Ric. First, you've got tonight off. Of course, if you get a week off, I'm going to have to even up your schedule... say, by having you work two matches at Bloodbath. And since you seem to like beating up Douglas so much, you'll get another shot to do so when you face him in an Internet Title match at Bloodbath." "You can't do that to me!" "Oh no? I can't? Looks to me like I just did. Now, if you don't mind, get your asses out of my office. Now." 3vil glared at Universal, then turned to go, muttering threats the whole way. Universal sat back down in his chair. He was taking a big risk putting Douglas in such a position, but that came with the territory. He closed his eyes and hoped the move wouldn't backfire... A Thought Monte Burns stalked backstage, quite pissed. He had failed, once again, to lose the Jobberweight Title. He had to figure out how to get it off his waist, some way, somehow... As he walked, Black Quicksilver appeared from behind him and threw an arm around his shoulders. "Monte! Good to see ya!" Burns stopped short, calmly grabbed BQ's arm, and removed it from his shoulders. "What. Do. You. Want." BQ began to speak, turning randomly and gesticulating as he did so. "Well, somebody's a grumpy Gus. I just wanted to tell you that after our match, I got to thinking. Sure, you won, but still, I can't help but that think that you'd make a great member of the STUMP Squad! You've got everything it takes except for that whole winning part. But we can work on that, right Monte?" When there was no reply, BQ turned around. Burns was nowhere to be found. "Monte? Monte! Hey... where'd you go?" BQ shrugged, then wandered off in a random direction, probably heading to his locker room... or something. Eddy Fate vs. Jon Crisp Slaughter rolls on as the sounds of "My Hero" by Foo Fighters begin. This brings out former amateur wrestler Jon Crisp, who was greeted with familiar sounds . While Eddy Fate was in a war with the Black Panther, the self-proclaimed 'Icon' Crisp was caught up in his own games with 'The Fallen Angel' Donaven Winters. Crisp climbed into the ring and raised his arms; soaking in the boos of the crowd. Crisp' attention was immediately diverted when his music stopped playing, and instead we heard 'Disconnect' by the Henry Rollins Band. Fans immediately rose to their feet and cheered as former fWo Cruiserweight Champion Eddy Fate made his way out, appearing to be wincing as he walked down the entrance ramp. Fate rolled into the ring and immediately Crisp pounced, much like a lion... or a panther. Crisp began kicking and stomping away at Fate, and the referee signaled for the bell to start the match. Crisp brought Fate to a vertical base, before whipping him to the ropes. Fate reversed it and ducked for a backdrop, but Crisp countered with a swinging neckbreaker. Crisp stood to his feet and plotted his next move; as Fate appeared to be in more pain than usual. Crisp brought Fate up once again, before hitting a lightning-like quick snap suplex. Crisp attempted a pinfall, which garnered only a two count. Feeling he should've won the match right then, Crisp brings Fate up and whips him into the turnbuckles, before following him and unleashing a bevy of chops, which brightens the chest of Fate to a bright red. Continuing his offensive assault, Crisp brings Fate out with a hip toss, and as Fate sits up naturally, Crisp walks around and unloads with a sitting dropkick to the face. Going for another cover, which results in another two count. Crisp brings Fate up and whips him to the ropes and this time it's Crisp who ducks for the backdrop, but like Crisp did to Fate, Fate makes Crisp pay for it by countering with an attempted Twist Of Fate - but Crisp has it scouted, and instead counters by pushing Fate into the closest turnbuckles. Fate hits chest first, before coming out backwards into Crisp, who goes for a German Suplex. Instead, he goes for a released version of the move, but Fate counters out by flipping out and landing on his feet. Crisp is caught off-guard by Fate's move, and he turns to charge at Fate. Fate counters with a solid spinning heel kick, before slowly rising back up to his feet. Fate wipes some saliva from his mouth. Fate brings Crisp up and Irish Whips him to the ropes, and Crisp returns to be taken down with a drop-toe-hold. Fate then proceeds to go up to the top rope, and it appears he has a high risk maneuver in store. With Crisp on his back, Fate leaps off with a senton bomb, but Crisp had that move scouted as well, and instead rolls out of the way. Crisp quickly goes for a cover with a hook of the legs, but Fate again kicks out. Crisp is frustrated, but won't be deterred in his quest to win the match. Deciding he should end the match, Crisp brings Fate up and whips him to the nearest turnbuckles, but Fate reverses it, and instead it's Crisp who finds himself in a corner. Fate comes in with arms raised for a double-axehandle, but Crisp counters with a kick to the stomach. He then pulls Fate by the head; and positions him for his finisher, The Icon. He spins him up powerbomb style, and prepares to run, however, Fate leaps off and lands onto the turnbuckles behind Crisp. The fans cheer wildly. An angry Crisp turns and spots Fate leaping off, but it's too late. Before he knows it, he finds himself taken down into a pinning position with a hurracanrana from the top rope. One. Two. No, Crisp escapes. Both men come back up to a vertical base, and exchange punches, before Crisp is sent to the ropes once again. Crisp returns and Fate kicks him in the stomach, before this time hitting the Twist Of Fate. However, Fate is worn out, and is unable to make an immediate pinfall attempt. Once he can make one, it's all for naught, as Crisp is able to shoulder out at two. Surely, had Fate pinned him sooner, the match would be history. However, Fate is undaunted, and goes to the nearest turnbuckles, deciding to take another risk. He again leaps off with a senton bomb, and unfortunately for Fate, again misses. Crisp moved out of the way just in time, and now Crisp is in control. Deciding he better put Fate away, he does. The Icon is hit, and three seconds later, Crisp is declared the winner of the match. Crisp' arm is raised by the official, but the 'Cult Icon' pushes him away, knowing he needed no help to win this match... Crisp walks backstage, while Fate sits up in the ring, wincing in pain... Result: Jon Crisp by pinfall Attack Slaughter returned from commercials as Jon Crisp walked down the corridor, and towards his locker room. He had an aggravated look on his face, something that had come to be the norm in Crisp's life, at least the portions shown on fWo television. "That moron Hartt," Crisp said. "Idiot brings the Abusah here, and then they don't show up for the show? Dammit, I needed Parka here tonight. I haven't even heard from them since Wednesday. What the hell are they doing?" Crisp walked into his locker room, where he heard his cell phone ringing. He picked it up. "Hello?" "Do you remember what I said?" A strange voice, not unfamiliar to Crisp, was on the line. Crisp, perturbed at what he saw as a waste of time, responded. "Who the hell is this?" "Do you remember what I said?" "Maybe if you tell me who this is, I could answer you." Then, there was silence. Crisp tried to get another answer, and then tried screaming into the phone. Then, he felt something cold come into contact with his neck. The cold feel of a wrench struck him in the neck, and Crisp fell down in a heap. He never saw Donaven Winters come in the door, as his back was turned. Winters continued to lash into Crisp with the wrench until he was out cold. Then, Winters laid into Crisp with rights and lefts until he was covered with a crimson mask. Winters smiled, stood up, and turned off the cell phone he used to call the Cult Icon. "I said, watch your back this Sunday. You're just like the Ego, Icon. You overestimate yourself. If you had not, you wouldn't be in this predicament. Learn from the Ego, Crisp. Those who don't remember history are bound to repeat it." Winters hit Crisp one more time, and left, into the shadows. Meanwhile, Mr. T walked in with Mike Heftel. "N THEN WE TOOK HIS LUNCH MONEY, SUCKA!" "T, are you trying to tell me that Face was a wuss?" "FACE WAS A NANCY, SUCKA!" "Face was NO nancy." Then, both men saw Crisp. Heftel turned to T. "I bet Face did that." "FACE IS HERE, FOO?! I SQUASH THE FACE BUG!" Then, a voice came from inside the room. A bad British voice. "You bloody wankers. Do you forget the words of Coldplay? It was all yellow, you filthy bastards." Sphere rolled around on a chair, into the light. T and Heftel looked at him, and T replied. "WHAT YOU TALKIN ABOUT SUCKA?!" "That bloke Winters did that. He'd a good chap, ya know." "Why didn't you stop him?" Heftel asked. "Why should I, you filthy yank?" Sphere replied. "You yellow bastards are ALL goin' down, you know that, you filthy sailors? Don't think us Brits are just gonna let one filthy loss 225 years ago bring us down, you filthy football Americanos." "WHAT HE SAYIN, SUCKA?!" "I can't tell, T," Heftel replied. "He's more confusing than Stump and Parka in a rousing game of charades." Sphere sighed, and sipped some tea. Crisp still laid on the floor, not receiving any help from T, Heftel, or Sphere, due to the recent conversation. Meanwhile, Slaughter rolled on... Insane Wonder/Adam Manson/Tempest vs. Ruben Ross/Hellrazor/Crucifix "Sugar" begins to play, and Insane Wonder and Adam Manson make their way to the ring, walking together. The fans boo, and the duo shrugs it off as they hit the ring. "Battle Flag" begins, and the boos become cheers as Tempest reluctantly makes her way to the ring to join her two partners. She steps into the ring and eyes them warily. "Beautiful People" begins, and the crowd reacts quizzically as a lighter, smaller Crucifix than they're used to makes his way to the ring. He waits outside of the ring, glaring in at Adam Manson. "From This Day" plays, and the crowd pops as fWo Hardcore Champion Hellrazor makes his way to the ring, looking focused. He looks Crucifix over skeptically, then shrugs. What the hey, you make do with what you've got. "Bulls On Parade" then hits, and the crowd pops big-time for former fWo Internet Champion Ruben Ross, who makes his way to the ring looking confident as ever. He joins his partners, and they take their corner. Before the match can begin, Crucifix grabs a microphone. Clearly looking to make an impression in his "debut", Crucifix turns to his partner Hellrazor. "Hey, 'Razor! I know we're partners and all tonight, and hey, I'm all for beating the ever-loving hell out of Marilyn Manson and his little freakboy over there. Although the chick isn't bad hey, baby, I'll give you my number later." Tempest's face tells the story not likely. Crucifix continues. "But anyway, I was thinking... once we're done with these guys, howzabout you giving me a Hardcore Title shot at Bloodbath?" Hellrazor looks at Crucifix, quite skeptical. Is this little guy serious? Crucifix takes this in. "Hey, you don't have to answer now, but take your time, think it over, and get back to me, OK? OK." Hellrazor looks at Ross, who just shrugs. The two briefly converse as Crucifix tosses the mic aside, and they determine that Hellrazor will start the match. The bell rings, and Insane Wonder charges at Hellrazor. He leaps, but Hellrazor catches him in mid-air and throws him into the air, bringing him down with a fallaway slam. Hellrazor brings him up and whips him into the corner, then charges in with a lariat. He follows it up with a series of clotheslines, nailing Wonder. Wonder stumbles out of the corner, and Hellrazor takes him down with a hard back suplex before making a cover. Wonder kicks out at two and crawls to his corner, where he tags out to Adam Manson. Manson hits the ring, fury flashing in his eyes. He swings wildly at Hellrazor, who ducks. Hellrazor goes for a kick, but Manson catches his foot. Manson pulls Hellrazor toward him, then clobbers him with a clothesline. Manson brings Hellrazor back up to his feet and angrily whips him into the ropes. As Hellrazor comes off, Manson catches him with a spinebuster and hurls him to the mat. Manson drops an elbow, then another, and another. He makes the cover, but only gets two. He brings Hellrazor up, but Hellrazor blocks a punch and absolutely clocks Manson. Manson is dazed, and Hellrazor goes to his corner and tags out to Ruben Ross, who springboards in with a dropkick that nails Manson. He covers, but Manson kicks out at two. Ross brings Manson up again and pokes him in the eye. Manson stumbles about, and Ross takes advantage by hooking Manson by the head and dropping him to the mat with a brainbuster. He covers, but again only gets two. Ross brings Manson up and whips him into the ropes, but Manson reverses it. He makes the mistake of putting his head down, and Ross stops short. He lifts the larger man up, then spikes him with a cradle piledriver. He covers, but Manson gets a foot on the ropes after two. Ross brings him up and hooks him up, then drops him with a beautiful Spin Cycle. He reaches forward and hooks a leg, but again Manson kicks out just short of three. Ross tries to bring him up again, but Manson nails him with a low blow. Manson crawls toward his corner and reaches out blindly for a tag. Wonder fails to notice, busily arguing with Tempest, who has extended her hand as an afterthought. Manson finds Tempest's hand instead of the intended tag to Wonder. Tempest leaves her argument in mid-sentence and hits the ring. She runs into an armdrag from Ross, but she lands on her feet. She catches Ross with an armdrag of her own, but he also lands on his feet. He charges at her with a clothesline, but she ducks. He bounces off the ropes, and Tempest leapfrogs him as he comes off. Ross comes off the opposite ropes, and Tempest, still facing the opposite direction, leapfrogs over him again. Ross comes off a third time, and slides between Tempest's legs. He gets to his feet behind her, and as she turns, they both try dropkicks. Neither hits, of course, and both land in a defensive position. The fan applaud, and Ross extends a hand. They shake, as Wonder and Manson roll their eyes in their corner. Ross ponders his next move, but as he does so, the voice of Crucifix pesters him to tag him in. Ross shrugs and does so, and Crucifix hits the ring, flailing wildly. Tempest easily dodges his blows and catches him with a superkick. As she makes the cover (only getting two), Vic Creed (with a chain) and Vengence, clearly working separately, make their way to the ring. With the ref's attention on the action, the two behemoths pull Hellrazor and Ruben Ross off the apron. Vengence takes Hellrazor down with an Eclipse, while Creed drops Ross with a Bodybreaker. He then begins opening up on Ross with the chain. They begin laying in stomps. Inside the ring, Tempest hits an Asai moonsault. As her momentum takes her up to her knees in her corner, Adam Manson reaches out and tags himself in with a threat of "I took out the first one, and the second one's mine too." He brings Crucifix up, grabs him, and drops him with an Exhale The Vile. Manson repeats the move, then puts a foot on Crucifix's chest for the pinfall. Manson begins attacking Crucifix with chokes and stomps, and Wonder joins in. Tempest protests, but they ignore her. Finally, she takes matters into her own hands and nails Wonder with a spinning heel kick, knocking him from the ring over the top rope. Adam Manson sees this and charges at her, but she ducks his rush and spinning heel kicks him as well, sending him through the ropes. Wonder gets up and extends a hand to Manson, and they give Tempest the evil eye as, on the other side of the ring, security removes Creed and Vengence from their victims and Slaughter heads to commercials... Result: Insane Wonder/Adam Manson/Tempest by pinfall Finishing What He Started Slaughter returned from commercials to find The Black Panther already walking down the entrance ramp, the fans booing his arrival, and 'Du Hast' by Rammstein blaring throughout the First Union Center. The Panther paid no attention to the fans; as he had a task before him to complete. Panther leapt to the ring apron with catlike agility, then climbed over the top rope with relative ease. The 1999 Wrestler of the Year demanded a microphone, as he had something to say. "Two weeks ago on Sunday Night Slaughter... Eddy Fate, I did the same thing I did to Mike Bear & Fear at SummerSlaughter. I took you like a sack of feathers, and tossed you right off that stage" - Panther points to the SlaughterStage "to the floor! Without hesitation. And believe me, if I get the chance, I'll do it again. That night, you were nothing more than fresh meat in my claws... and tonight, I intend to clean my claws. To finish what I started. In other words... I'm calling you out." Panther turned his attention to the stage and waited about ten seconds for Fate while the fans jeered his words. Fate didn't appear, so Panther once again invited him to appear. "Fate, I know you're here tonight, I saw you have a match earlier with Crisp... and surely, I like to think I played an indirect part in the reason you lost. What's the matter, Eddy?
