Introduction The fWo moves into a new era, with new partners. With the assembled Board of Directors moving to push the fWo into bigger and better territories, as well as with mandate to compete at the top of their industry, deals have been made to find the fWo corporate sponsorship and an exclusive network to distribute their shows.
