Sunday Slaughter, 24 February 2002

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pre-Slaughter The fWo remained in Seattle, twenty four hours removed from the first pay-per-view of 2002, Body Count. Last night it was Safeco Field, home of Major League Baseball's Seattle Mariners. Tonight, it would be the Key Arena, home of the Seattle Supersonics. Ric Chronos arrived at the arena early in the afternoon, with a small gym bag slung over his right shoulder. He appeared tired and angry... the bags under his eyes reflecting what little amount of sleep he had gotten last night. Eddy Fate ruined his plans, and Chronos spent most of the night being upset over it. Ric was leading Body Count with four pinfall eliminations... that is until a certain individual by the name of Erik Kelly returned. Beyond that... Fate knew what he was doing. He knew by laying down for Kelly in the match it allowed Kelly to even the score with Chronos... and of course an exhausted, beaten and worn Ric Chronos was no match for Kelly and a powerbomb off the top rope. Thus negating Ric' quest to main event CyberSlam and win back his fWo World Title. And thus... putting the stipulation that if Ric didn't win Body Count, he must face Fate instead. Chronos passed backstage workers as camera man Geoff Green attempted to catch up with him; along with Kristen Jacobs. "Ric... last night you were close to winning Body Count..." Chronos stopped and looked at Jacobs with a sneer. "Were you saying something?" Chronos snapped at her. Jacobs was taken aback, and prepared to answer the question... but she was interrupted. "Let's just say this... Eddy always talks about how Fate cannot be denied." Chronos moved towards the camera, his eyes narrowing and his teeth bared. "Tonight... he can be." The former four-time World Champion paused and looked down at his gym bag. "And tonight... he will... be." Chronos stammered off in another direction while Jacobs & Green looked at each other. Slaughter was a few hours away... but one could easily ascertain that Chronos had a plan. A plan to deny Fate... Double... Or Nothing? Live inside the Key Arena, Matt Panzer counted down the seconds until Sunday Night Slaughter was set to begin. The thirty seconds passed, and as the Slaughter music blared throughout the arena ("Sunday Slaughter! Sunday Slaughter! GUH BAH! ZI BOO GAI~!"), fireworks also shot into the atmosphere from strategically placed locations in the former Seattle Coliseum. As the last Slaughter in February took to the air, Jeremiah Frankton & James Armstrong welcomed viewers at home and recapped what happened last night at Body Count. Tempest won the Cruiserweight Championship for a second time, and Monte Burns revealed himself as the secret admirer. Donaven Winters won the fWo Hardcore Title, which had been vacant for three months. A tragic incident happened at the end of the fWo Internet Title Match, as Kodiak Vic Creed mysteriously collapsed after clutching his chest. Slugger picked up the win, and won the title. And in the fWo World Title Match... Austin Davis took a commanding lead, but the champion Ruben Ross came back and in the final seconds tied Davis; before winning the match a few seconds before time would expired. And then, the battle royal. Monte Burns rushed out of the gate with two pinfalls. Brand Frontier got a couple of pinfalls as well. But it was Mitch Wilson who led a majority of the match with three eliminations, until Ric Chronos surpassed him with four. It appeared Chronos would win... when Erik Kelly returned to the fWo and pinned everyone that had remained in the ring, thus passing Chronos and thus earning him the right to go onto CyberSlam V. Tonight was the beginning of a new story, as the fWo was now on the road to CyberSlam. After completing the run down of the night prior; Frankton & Armstrong informed viewers of what they knew had been scheduled thus far. Ruben Ross was scheduled to defend the fWo World Title against Ric Chronos & Eddy Fate in a three-way dance. The Flying Frenchie & Black Quicksilver were scheduled to meet in their match that was supposed to occur at Body Count, but didn't due to an injury suffered by Frenchie that mysteriously prevented him from competing until the Body Count battle royal. Ice would return to fWo action to team with Austin Davis against Scott Slugger & Erik Kelly, while Donaven Winters would defend his newly won fWo Hardcore Title against Monte Burns. And with all that out of the way, "O' Fortuna" echoed throughout Key Arena and darkness blanketed the ringside area. The fans rose to their feet and began booing, as fWo World Tag Team Champion The Boston Strangler appeared on the stage and wasted no time in walking towards the ring. Strangler rolled into the ring and gestured for the lights to come back on and his music to stop, before he retrieved himself a microphone. Focusing on using the right words, the former World Champion began to speak. "Brand Frontier..." Strangler was caught offguard by the rousing ovation of cheers the mere mention of the fWo Survivor 2' winners name. He sneered at the fans before continuing. "You've come to the fWo with a lot of the spotlight on you... just as we did four years ago. We chose to step up and face all that was evil... and defend all that was good. And it worked for awhile... until we realized we were being used. Used by Finn... used by Beaven... used by Universal. Used by the fWo as it's human shield. And we realized our mistake and corrected it. We realized that we shouldn't have been fighting against men like Austin Davis... or David Spencer. We realized that we had more in common with them... than we had thought. We redeemed ourselves." Strangler paused before he continued. "We see that you studied your history, Brand. That's fine... it's rare that a wrestler is able to beat us on his own. Which is how we explain your fluke of a pinfall in the Body Count battle royal... you resorted to covering us after the legend in his own mind... Scott Slugger... hit us with his finisher. We consider that to be two flukes on the same night. And tonight we're out here to make sure that nobody ever remembers your two flukes... they will only remember that when it came down to the Boston Strangler & Brand Frontier in an fWo ring... that the Boston Strangler... stood... alone. Victorious." Paul Revere Adams thrusted his fist into the air, which drew an ovation of boos from the crowd. "Your opinion matters nothing to us." Strangler stared an icy hole into the faces of the crowd. "Frontier... the rules of the match are simple. No disqualification. No countout. You either win by pinfall or submission... inside this very ring. If we lose again... we will walk away. We will walk away from our ring... we will walk away from the company that owes everything to us... we will walk away." The fans cheered loudly, a deafening reaction that rocked the Key Arena. "Win or lose... however... we guarantee you won't. You know where you can find us... and if you accept meet us out here... man-to-man... in the ring." Strangler dropped the microphone, and it boomed throughout the arena. Strangler began walking to the back, but paused to look at the fans one last time. "Redemption begins tonight." Strangler said under his breath, before disappearing into the back as Slaughter went to a commercial break. Knight-time As the Deftone's "Elite" filled Seattle's Key Arena, the fans began to cheer for a man who had put on a surprisingly strong showing in last night's BodyCount match, eliminated Grendel from the competition before being unfairly eliminated by Graphic Violence. It was unfair because the aforementioned Grendel, who was already eliminated, had held down Knight's foot while GV made the cover, thereby preventing Knight from kicking out. However, none of the turmoil that would normally have been apparent on Knight's young face was visible, and it looked like he had reconciled himself to the happenings of the night before as he slid into the ring and called for the microphone. Panzer tossed the mic to him, and Lance easily caught it and brought it up to his lips. "Seattle, Washington," Knight began, but then stopped, both overwhelmed by the roar of the crowd and slightly guilty for the way he had gone about getting it. As the fans' roar died down slightly, he continued, "Thank you for the warm ovation. As I'm sure many of you saw last night, there was a strange turn of events that led to me being eliminated from the BodyCount match, but that's not what I'm out here to talk about tonight, actually." When the fans showed their disapproval, Knight used to his free left hand to motion for them to calm down. "Don't worry, Grendel will get his." The decibel level was increasing again, but Knight continued unabated. "However, after the BodyCount match, I'm told Graphic Violence was going around backstage telling other members of the fWo, both wrestlers and technicians, and, from what I understand, basically anyone who would listen to him, that 'I was still his (censored).' Shadows, if that's the case, then why am I the one with the fWo contract, while they just call you in when they need another body to fill some space in a match? If that's the case, then why couldn't you beat me without help from that reject from "The Brotherhood of the Wolf"? If that's the case, then..." Before Knight could finish, a figure crept out from beneath the ring, moving with the stealth of an animal, and before Knight could even sense that he was there, the man-beast Grendel shambled across the ring on all fours before diving at Knight's knee. Lance toppled over backwards and an fWo official came running out to ringside. As Grendel pulled Knight to his feet, the official slid into the ring and called for the bell, making this an official contest in spite of its impromptu start. Lance Knight vs. Grendel Seemingly uncaring of whether or not it was an fWo sanctioned match, Grendel continued his attack, apparently only pulling Knight to his feet so that the furry monster could jerk his leg back out from beneath him. As Knight began to topple over backwards, Grendel wrapped his arm around Knight's knee and fell backwards, delivering a DDT to Knight's leg that caused intense pain to shoot through not only the Pittsburgh native's leg, but the whole way up his spine. With a smile, Grendel watched as the momentum of the move carried Knight forward, further aggravating the pressure on his captive leg. Satisfied, Grendel released Knight's leg and rolled the smaller man over. He again reached for Knight's wounded leg, only to receive a kick to the face from the healthy one. Unfortunately for Knight, this seemed to have no effect on the Fallen Angel, who continued to advance upon him, his leather mask revealing no emotion. Using both legs this time, Lance delivered another kick to Grendel's face, and this slowed the big man down long enough that Lance could roll backwards and get to his feet. However, he wasn't there for long, as his injured knee gave way and sent him crumpling awkwardly to the mat. Moving slowly, Grendel advanced on his opponent, looking for all the world like a lion stalking a downed antelope. Knight struggled to pull himself to his feet with the aid of the ropes, and this time he was successful. As Grendel approached, Knight feinted left and then hobbled right, actually building up a half-decent head of steam as he charged to the ropes. As he turned, it looked as if it would be all for naught, as Grendel rose up out of his crouching run, completely intent on delivering a whiplash-inducing lariat on Knight. Much to the fans' approval, Grendel never got the opportunity, as Knight ducked beneath the huge meaty arm that was aimed at his neck. He continued to run as best he could, reaching the far ropes and rebounding once more. This time, however, it was his turn to strike - taking care to plant his good foot for more push, Knight leapt forward, taking a surprised Grendel to the mat with a cross body block. The official dove into position to make the cover, but Grendel easily pressed Knight off of him before dumping Lance back to the mat. Both men got to their feet, Knight still clearly favoring his ravaged left knee. Grendel, still confident, moved forward, envisioning Knight as nothing more than wounded prey. Thus, it was a shock to Grendel when he found himself caught in Knight's surprisingly powerful grasp and then tossed through the air to the outside of the ring, suddenly becoming the victim of one of Knight's vast array of suplexes, this time one of the head-and-arm variety. As Knight turned to check on the whereabouts of his opponent, Grendel got to his feet and leapt forward, catching his fellow Survivor 2 competitor by his left ankle and once again pulling Knight off of his feet. Without warning, Grendel pulled Knight to the corner, where he savagely wrapped Lance's leg around the ringpost. As the crowd booed, Grendel once again slammed Knight's knee off of the cold steel of the ringpost. The official slid out of the ring and warned Grendel about the consequences of continuing his plan of attack, but a snarl from the bestial Grendel backed the official away and allowed him to ram Knight's knee into the ringpost a third time, drawing even more boos from the crowd. Satisfied, Grendel slid back into the ring, the match official following close behind. The Fallen Angel got to his feet and advanced on Knight, who was clutching his knee in the corner. Grendel hooked his hand inside Lance's armpit and pulled Knight to the center of the ring. From there, Grendel wrapped a furry paw around Knight's throat. Showing his tremendous strength, Grendel hoisted the 250-pound Knight into the air with a single arm. He apparently thought about delivering a chokeslam, but instead simply held Knight above the canvas. After several seconds, as the cords in his arm began to tighten, Grendel simply released Knight. Much to the crowd's disapproval, Knight landed on his feet, but his left knee quickly gave way, leaving him lying on the mat and writhing in pain. Further displaying his sadistic streak, Grendel dropped an elbow onto Knight's afflicted knee, eliciting a groan of pain from the downed competitor. Apparently satisfied with his handiwork, Grendel grabbed Knight's left ankle and cinched in a figure four leglock. Knight held on, first trying to stretch to the ropes, but that option quickly left his mind, as he was in the middle of the ring with no ropes anywhere even remotely within reach. In desperation, Knight attempted to turn the move over, reversing the pressure of the hold and applying to the force to Grendel's knee, but this too proved to be a dead end as he was unable to maneuver the bulky Grendel over. Slamming the mat in frustration, Knight finally ceded the victory to Grendel to prevent any further damage to his knee. Result: Grendel by submission Return of the Eradicator Before the camera could return to the arena, a familiar whisper was heard. "Let the Bodies hit the....FLOOOOOOOR!" The Key Arena almost crumbled in the wake of that soundbyte, and the followup "POOOOOWERBOOOOMB!" roared by the thousands of marks aided in the cause. 'Bodies' by Drowning Pool continued to play, and a lone figure emerged in the entrance way. Blue jeans, black t-shirt from his 'Say Hello to Block' merchandise collection, and a black biker jacket. His hair appeared longer than last time we saw him. The trademark chin goatee still apparent. If anything appeared different on this near 7 foot, 330+ pounder...was a gold band on his left ring finger. One thing for sure, though. He looked really pissed off. 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly was back. And as he made his way into the ring by stepping over the top rope, he looked around as the camera caught his entrance video behind him up on the SlaughterTron. As the laser lights died down, and the arena lights came back up, Kelly nodded to Matt Panzer, took a microphone in hand, and prepared to speak. Before he could say a word, the crowd noise built up again with a "Kelly! Kelly! Kelly!" chant. Erik, his face still a picture of rage, looked around the Key Arena a few moments more, until the sound lowered enough that he could be heard. "Did you forget about me?!?!" he roared into the microphone. The crowd continued their chanting and support for the returning fWo superstar. "Did you forget about my rage? Did you not expect me to come back and claim what will rightfully belong to me come CyberSlam V?!?!" A flurry of cheers, and some mixed boos from the hardcore Ross fans in the audience. "Apparently every single individual in BodyCount did...and like the hunter always says: Know when your ass is targeted for Eradication!" Another pop for Kelly's comments. A sign was caught in the crowd from an attractive brunette. The sign reads: 'Kelly, can I be your mistress? I won't tell!'. Another sign read: 'Chronos can't beat Kelly cuz Fate is on his side!'. The camera returned to Kelly, as he leaned forward on the top rope and stared into the camera. 'I've had some time to step away and see what's going on. And low and behold, I go get married..." Brief pop at this announcement. "...and this place starts looking like fu*CENSORED* WCW!" Lots of hoots and hollars at the mention of the defunct organization. "Making this short and sweet...everyone's on notice that their future is going to be told. And it's a messy future for them. Austin Davis..." The crowd reacted very negatively to the mention of the name. "...I don't know what the fu*CENSORED* Universal was thinking letting your ass back in here. Every moment you've spent in this organization...you've made every wrestler's life a living hell...going back to CyberSlam 3, right here in Seattle, Washington..." Crowd reaction for mentioning their city name. "...to Countdown to Oblivion. And Strangler..." Erik seemed to froth at the mouth mentioning Paul Revere Adams'. "...Paul...you did the unthinkable...you sided with the one man who stood for everything you don't...you turned your back on everyone...the fans...the other guys...especially me, Paul. If anyone hated Austin Davis more than me was yourself." Erik lowered the microphone as the crowds' boos built up momentarily. "So let me be the first to announce that both of your days are numbered...as I will give all fWo fans the one late Christmas gift I can give them....your asses permanently rearranged and kicked out of here!" That Kelly chant began again. One woman tried to catch Kelly's attention with a sign that read 'I'm a virgin...Eradicate me!' Kelly started to pace about in the ring in a methodical powerwalk motion. "Let me leave one final thought to our illustrious champion, Mr. Ruben Ross." The crowd popped briefly for Ross as Kelly continued. "Davis has something to fear...Strangler has something to fear....but you, my friend, will have the biggest fear of them all. The fear of each second passing by...as Cyberslam, the greatest spectacle in our sport, draws closer. The day where we will both pop a 20 in the Main Event! The day all your signature moves and 90 word long catchphrases will be meaningless...the day you learn that Bitchville is destined for Eradication!" A solid pop from the crowd in anticipation of the main event. "The message is clear to anyone who gets in my way...I will not stop until I have MY World Title around my waist. Get in my way...your ass won't just be targeted for Eradication...you won't just say hello to block...you'll become just another Clone in my world...where I am the King...I am the Master...I AM THE POWER of the fWo..." Some crowd participation begins to chime in with Kelly. "...and there ain't a daaaaaaaaaamn thing you can do about it!" Erik threw the microphone down as 'Bodies' played once more. He jumped up on a turnbuckle, and very emotionally played to the crowd, making hand gestures indicating he wanted that belt, then stretching his arms out as he roared his trademark "POWERBOMB!" that was drowned out by the music. After repeating this in all corners of the ring, Erik made his way out of the ring, hi-fived a few of the fans, and proceeded back up the ramp. The camera caught a glimpse of what looked like a man who wore the focused look of intensity, of desire, of raw adrenaline. In the coming weeks, things appeared to be building up to a boiling point...and he looked ready to be at the very center of all that chaos. Meanwhile, Slaughter cut to a commercial break... Zero vs. XXXstasy Inside of the arena, "Renegade" by Jay Z/Eminem blasted throughout the stands. The fans' disapproval was made known instantly for the man who was about to appear. He was Sean "Triple X" Stevens. With an arrogant step, he appeared from behind the curtain. The chorus of boos that he gathered didn't seem to slight him one bit. Confidently, he made his walk down to the ring. In his hand, he held a microphone. Stepping through the ropes, he let his introduction to the fans carry on for just a moment longer before he cued for his music to stop. He smiled. "Look at me. I'm a beat up man, but tonight I hold my head up high! Sure, I didn't win the hardcore title. But a saving grace came later in the evening during Body Count." Triple X paused for a moment to let the fans take it what he said. "Yes, it was a saving grace. Luckily, or shall I say by talent, the Blue Eyed Badass managed to remove Zero from Body Count with only a single stroke of effort." The fans' boos lit up the arena. Triple X only marveled in their one-track minds. Sure, they didn't know what was good for them, but Stevens knew exactly what had happened. "Not only," he continued, "did I eliminate Zero before he even managed to get a pinfall in the match, I was also the first person to attack him in it. He didn't stand a chance. In fact... I beat him with his own move! As pathetic of a move that it is, I'm surprised he wasn't able to get up from it. In fact, even if he were here tonight, I'd do it to him again just to show how easy it is to put this guy away. But of course, he's not. He's back home, resting his body from the beating that..." Suddenly, "Infected" started up inside the arena. Stevens' eyes blared out to the entranceway. He wasn't supposed to be here tonight. But as the sound of the fans' pop rose, the curtain to the backstage slowly moved. Out stepped Zero from the backstage. In his own hand, he held a microphone. So tonight, the two would face off with the war of words. It seemed to work everywhere else. Zero slowly walked towards the ring. The after-effects of the previous night were visible, as he had a cut on his face from the hardcore title match at Body Count. Stepping into the ring, he looked over at Stevens. He was slow to reply. He merely looked at his opposite. Stepping in closer to Triple X, Zero slowly raised the microphone up to his mouth. "...Are you sure?" was all that he said. His eyes narrowed and dug into Triple X's head. His body stiffened as he awaited Triple X's reply. But as Triple X raised his microphone to reply, Zero quickly tossed his right into Stevens' face. It hit and bounced off, sending a large thud throughout the arena. With the distraction, Zero came in with a swift kick to Triple X's stomach and then followed it with a forearm to the side of Triple X's head. The bell rang to signal a match. Breathing heavily, Zero had led off the abrupt match between Sean "Triple X" Stevens and he. Triple X stumbled back as he tried to catch himself. The sudden attack had caught him off guard. He managed to raise his head, only to see Zero lunge at him. Triple X put up his hands to block the attack, but Zero crashed into him, sending both down to the canvas. They rolled on impact, giving Triple X the advantage as he came out on top of Zero. But a quick shove to the left sent him back down on his back. Zero quickly got to his feet and grabbed the top rope in front of him. With a quick jump, he put both feet on the middle rope, then sprang against them with a leg drop in Triple X's vicinity. But as he landed, Triple X rolled out of the way, sending Zero crashing flat onto the canvas. He clutched his back at the impact, only to have a shin slam into his chest. As Triple X got back to his feet, his kick into Zero's chest was almost instinct. Seeing Zero stunned, Triple X immediately grabbed Zero's head and pulled him back up to his feet. He latched onto his head and snapped him over with a suplex. No one was going to attack him and think that they could benefit from their actions. Triple X was in control. He pulled him back up to his feet and swung a right hand in to connect with Zero's face. It caused Zero to stumble back. Feeling his momenteum growing, Triple X reared back and again swung a haymaker. It hit Zero in the side of the head, forcing him to turn to the side and take another step backwards. Acting quick, Triple X returned to the well to deliver another punch. He swung out, but this time, Zero was waiting for him. Seeing the punch coming, Zero threw his arms out to block the punch with his forearms. The impact caught Triple X off guard. It was the opening that Zero needed. He quickly stepped forward and placed his leg behind Triple X. With a hard forward thrust, he slammed his hands into Stevens' chest and pushed backwards. With the speed of the move, Triple X fell back over the leg behind him. Crashing to the ground, Zero was quickly on top of him with the right hands. Triple X threw up his hands to protect his face. The blows came in from the left and right. Then, as quickly as they came, they ended. He looked up only to see Zero getting back to his feet over him, then falling back down onto him with a hard slamming forearm to his throat. Stevens instantly reached for his throat after the move, coughing to regain the air that had depleted him. Seeing Triple X fight to regain his breathing, Zero got back to his feet and paced to the ropes. With a quick slide, he went down to the outside. From there, he just started grabbing things. The first was a ringside chair. He didn't even stop to unfold it as he threw it over his back and into the ring. The second thing was another chair. He tossed it inside of the ring just as he had done before. It bounced a few times, landing right on top of Triple X's feet. The third thing Zero grabbed was a table that rested just inside of the ring apron. He pulled it out and propped it against the bottom ring rope. Triple X felt the chair land against his feet. He raised his head slightly to see the metal weapon. Then, with a thud, his head fell back down to the mat. On the outside, he could hear the rattle of things being dug through and grasped. Turning his head, he caught a glimpse of Zero moving a table. Zero looked at the two chairs inside of the ring and the table outside. Sensing enough, he climbed back onto the ring apron. Looking into the ring at Triple X, he decided a different avenue of reentering the ring. Moving to his left, he grabbed hold of the turnbuckles and began making his way up to the top one. Getting there, he squatted to judge his distance from Stevens. Gaining it, he stood up and readied himself. In the distance, he could hear the fans' excitement through the noise that they were generating. With a thrust, he plunged towards Triple X. Looking up to see Zero jumping off the turnbuckle, Triple X acted fast. He had hooked the end of the chair with his foot. At just the right moment, he kicked it up to his hands. As Zero came down towards him, Triple X hurled the chair in Zero's path. With a sudden, loud thud, he had found his target. The chair struck Zero in midair. The chair hit him in the side of his head. Zero's landing was less than perfect as he came crashing down on his side. The chair, having rolled around his head, landed on top of him. He reached for his head and could feel the exact spot where it had hit him. He tried to lay there for a moment to regain his senses, but a sudden chair strike into his back told him that wasn't going to happen. Triple X had made it to his feet and clutched the chair in his hands. With a violent overhead delivery, he slammed the chair across Zero's back. Feeling the power running through his hands, he began to smile. The crowd thought less of his actions, but his approach didn't care. This was something that he liked doing. No one could beat him. He had beat everyone. To his doubters, there he was, chair in hand, beating someone who was supposedly going to put an end to his ways. Tough chance. Kicking the chair that laid on Zero over onto his head, Triple X pulled the chair in his hand back for another strike. He held it up in the air for a moment, before again violently slamming it down onto the other steel chair. The metallic sound was deafening. And beneath it all laid Zero. Triple X tossed the chair down to let Zero lay in the mess of steel. His eyes were drawn more towards the table on the outside that sat propped against the bottom rope. Taking long, slow steps, he walked to the ring ropes and grabbed onto the table. Pulling it slowly, he managed to pull it halfway through the ropes before, from behind, a chair slammed into his skull. Behind him, Zero watched as Triple X fell down to the mat. Slamming the chair that he held in his hand to the side, his eyes narrowed at Stevens. His head only shook to the left. The throbbing in it was enough to make even the shaking of one's head seem like an eruption. Stepping over Triple X, Zero grabbed the table and finished pulling it into the ring. Sitting it on its end, he picked it up and pushed against the top rope with it. Feeling the give, he slid it down in front of it, then began to push the middle rope in the opposite direction. Managing to get it in, he pushed the bottom rope away as he placed the table down in front of it. With a solid knock on the table, he had managed to lace it into the ropes. Triple X was slowly getting back up to his feet. Just as he had managed to get up, Zero grabbed onto his body and slammed the two forward with a forward Russian leg sweep onto the chairs in front of them. Triple X's face hit the seat of one of the chairs hard. Zero slammed into them, but managed to keep his face up to avoid any direct hit while performing the move. Wanting to act quickly, Zero got back up to his feet and pulled Triple X up along with him. Triple X was managing a comeback, though. He slammed a knee into Zero's midsection. Zero buckled over, but threw an uppercut back into Triple X's jaw. Stevens rocked back from the move. Sensing the opportunity, Zero quickly latched onto Triple X's arm and slung him with an Irish whip. Triple X felt his body being hurled towards the ropes. Just as he saw what he was heading into, it was too late to suddenly stop. With a loud crash, his body slammed into the table that had been placed amongst the ropes. His momentum cracked the center of the table and caused the top part to flip forward from the force of the ring rope. Because of the impact, Triple X's rebound was very slight off of the ropes. Just as he threw him into the ropes, Zero trailed behind Triple X and went into the ropes, just a few feet from the tables. Triple X's impact had broken the table and let the ropes have the rebound that they needed. It was all that was required. He bounced back and leaped forward. He placed his hands on Triple X's shoulder and twisted himself in midair. Grabbing Stevens' head with his shoulder as he made a 180, he clutched tight and maintained his body to land on his stomach. Triple X only felt an arm grab his head from behind. The next thing he knew was that he was being slammed forward, and in a hurry. With an impact on the steel chairs, he hit the mat. The force of the move caused him to bounce onto his back. Zero had performed a floatover Level Zero. Sensing victory in sight, he quickly rolled onto Triple X and waited for the ref's count... 1... 2... It was then that the ref saw Stevens' foot on the rope. In the moment of action, Stevens had managed to roll towards the ring ropes instead of away and towards the center of the ring. Zero sat on his knees and just looked at the situation. When he had finally decided to try something different with his finisher, something that wasn't lifted the night before, it fell to the grasps of the ring rope. Frustrated, he grabbed Triple X by the head and pulled him back up to his feet. But Triple X, as he was being lifted, felt a single hope. His fingers grasped onto the steel chair that laid on the canvas. Just as Zero pulled him up, he slammed the edge of the chair into Zero's midsection. Zero folded over with the unexpected strike.
