FTF House Show, 16 July 1999

16 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction From a distance the roar of the crowd could be heard, as fans stomped their feet in anticipation of the final Fight the Future house show. Backstage, camera man Geoff Green found Mike Bear pounding on doors, looking for a partner. One door opened to reveal Eron the Relentless, looking agitated for being disturbed. Mike said, Chameleon vs. Jackhammer The music of the Chameleon began to play, and the mysterious masked man walked carefully out onto the entrance ramp, flanked by Silver Fox. The two made their way carefully down to the ring, with the Chameleon walking smugly in spite of the fans, who loudly booed him. Jackhammer Result: Jackhammer by Pinfall Black Panther vs. Chris Universal The Black Panther walked to the ring alone, clothed in a jacket and shrouded in mystery. He carried with him a hammer, and climbed into the ring smugly. Chris Universal walked to the ring with the Internet title, and held it up to the crowd, before smirking at the Black Panther and telling him that, Result: Black Panther by Screwjob The experiment, part three Backstage, the Frenchie walked determined, looking for Primetime and Mitch Wilson. He suddenly discovered that his mouth was dry, and veered off course to get a cup of coffee. He stepped up to the coffee machine, and obtained a cup, sipping it slowly. His brow furrowed, and suddenly his eyes grew bleary and he toppled backward with a single, Stone (c) vs. Apocolyptica Melissa Smalls came to the ring with large handfuls of money, and began to toss the cash out to the crowd. Climbing into the ring, she announced that she was here to stand by Apocolyptica Result: Melissa Smalls by DQ Death (c) vs. Brawler Standing inside the ring, Death looked around at the other Dark Riders, stopping his gaze on the babbling Plague. Death punched him in the skull, toppling the small man to the outside, and screamed out the Brawler Result: Brawler by DQ The experiment, part four Somewhere backstage in a small room, the Trucker swept an arm across his forehead. He was alone in the room, and looked around for something to drink, and spotted a single glass of water on the counter. He reached for it, but the Speed Demon walked into the room just then, and grabbed it first. Ever the gentleman, the Trucker allowed him to have it despite his thrust, and the Speed Demon walked out of the room holding it. The Trucker continued to sit alone, and from outside of his room here was the sound of a thud, and then the clink of a glass. Then silence. The Surgeon then entered the room, holding the glass of Mike Bear & Mystery Partner vs. Falcon & Wretch The Falcon came to the ring, along with Wretch and the dog Muffin on a leash, and stood in the center of the ring. The Falcon mentioned to Bear that, Result: Mike Bear and the Foreign Phantom by Pinfall Austin Davis vs. Briana Briana came to the ring, led to the ring by the commissioner, and exercised in the ring, waiting for Davis to arrive for his match. Austin Davis Result: Briana by Pinfall Backstage horror... Geoff Green and the fWo camera crew investigated a disturbance in the dark parking lot, where a woman was found stripped down to only her panties and tied to a wooden cross. A bar covered her top, and she had badly bruised arms and legs. She also wore a black plastic garbage bag over her head, and as the camera crew grew nearer, Geoff Green ripped the bag from the woman Mad Trucker vs. Flying Frenchie The Flying Frenchie Result: No Contest Ice vs. Ultra Violet & Cactus Jon Ice came to the ring with Scott Slugger, who escorted him to the ring. Scott Slugger took the microphone, and told the crowd that he felt Ice was powerful enough to defeat both slobs. Scott Slugger then told the two that under no uncertain terms, even though Ultra Violet and Cactus Jon were signed to meet him at Summer Slaughter in a three way match, they would get their asses handed to them without any trouble on his part. Ultra Violet Result: Ultra Violet & Cactus Jon by DQ
