Chris Finn Announces Survivor

13 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE CHRIS FINN ANNOUNCES SURVIVOR July 13, 2000 During a press conference at 1pm EST in the fWo entertainment complex in Times Square, Chris Finn announced the debut of fWo Survivor. fWo Survivor, a four week program, will test the mettle of superstars from around the world who have dreamed of a chance of being a part of the fWo. Said Finn to reporters, "All of us in the fWo are very excited by this revolutionary concept in wrestling. With the recent popularity of 'reality TV' we decided that the time was right to debut our own product based on the same principles that made the current CBS series Survivor so popular. The big difference is that in ours the lucky survivor will in fact be granted a coveted spot on the fWo roster... making this the perfect opportunity for literally thousands of anxious competitors." fWo Survivor is scheduled to begin on July 21 and will run until August 16. During these four weeks, twelve lucky wrestlers will attempt to do the impossible... showcase their talents while attempting to survive a vote held by their peers. Concept developer and program manager Stevie Roberts explains further. "Basically you have twelve wrestlers selected from a pool of entrants. We will select the twelve based on a sample of their interview ability and enter them into the fWo Survivor contest. Now, every week we hold three small shows... with every wrestler involved in some fashion. At the end of the show, the twelve wrestlers are forced to vote one wrestler out of the contest. The voting is confidential, but we expect alliances to form and cliques to get involved in whittling the number of wrestlers down. However, the problem with strong alliances will be that eventually it comes down to just a few people... and that is where all bets are off." During the month long period, wrestlers will work on interviews, match hype, and much more in an attempt to prove their worth not only to their fellow wrestlers and thus attempt to gain favor, but also to the viewing audience, who will have a final say in who survives... and who goes home. "The best thing about this is that even if you aren't the survivor it is a big shot in the arm for the aspiring wrestler to be a part of this contest. A wrestler who doesn't survive but does show good teamwork and interview skills is likely to be picked up by someone, if not the fWo, eventually." Steve Roberts explained. When asked if there would be any crossover with the regular fWo roster, Stevie Roberts was quick to answer. "No. The fWo Survivor contest is strictly an internal contest. However, you can bet that the fWo superstars will be watching with keen interest... because one of these twelve survivors is going to be a part of their roster when it is all over... and because he or she survived in such an extreme environment there is going to be a lot of justifiable bragging going on." The fWo Survivor tournament officially starts Friday, July 21 with the first Survivor show. More news as it develops.
