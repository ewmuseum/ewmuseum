Donnie Daze, Neko Case, and Osyrus were all formidable competitors...but all obstacles could be overcome. What concerned Logic the most was the mystery man. The mystery former fWo World Champion. The Pensive Punisher had consulted his files and the fWo website, scouring every bit of data he could for information to figure out who this mystery man was. Eyeballing the man's proportions, he had narrowed his search down to four men. Scott Collins. Death in the Dark Riders. Just as David Spencer had once been, and Scott Slugger was now. Mike Bear. One of the fWo's earliest champions. Other than that, Logic didn't know much, but the fWo World Title said all that he needed to know. And then, the wild card guess...the longshot. The Boston Strangler. A man who had not been heard from in some time. But the proportions fit. And in a contest in which the unexpected was commonplace...Logic wouldn't be shocked to hear "O Fortuna" strike up in the near future. Who it was exactly Logic did not know. And it didn't matter. For tonight, Logic had requested to face this man, this former champion. Whomever it was would push Logic to the limit, something that Logic knew well. And with one Proof Positive, Logic planned on shoving Team Black and all of its mysterious plans squarely up the rear end of one Stephen Roberts.
