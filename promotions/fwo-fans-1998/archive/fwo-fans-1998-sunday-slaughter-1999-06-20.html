Sunday Slaughter, 20 June 1999

20 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction As Mick Foley enters the arena carrying his bags, Matt Loco approaches him for an opening word about the First-Blood match between Foley & Chris Universal this coming Friday at Numbered Days. Foley's response was: "You know... there's a song called Sunday Bloody Sunday, or something like that... I can't remember who it's by, but on the way to the arena tonight, it reminded me of what I'm going to do to Chris Universal tonight. Before tonight is over... before it's time to go catch a plane to go on to Warfare this Wednesday, I'm going to make you bleed, Chris. I'm just doing you a favor. I'm helping you prepare for Friday when you bleed like a woman on her menstrual period... if you smell what the sock is cooking! Have a nice day!" With that, Slaughter officially was off and running. Plague vs. Silver Fox With Plague already in the ring, Silver Fox came out to a chorus of boos from the attending crowd. Fox walked down the silver carpet laid out down the ramp, and a silver spotlight shown only on him, and him alone. Fox walked up the special stairs made just for him, before climbing into the ring, pointing at Plague, and laughing. Plague didn't get the joke, and charged at Fox. Fox sidestepped him and Plague went flying into the turnbuckles sternum first, and then Fox back suplexed him into the canvas. Fox went for the cover, but Plague quickly kicked out. Fox followed it up with several leg drops, and went for another cover... and again, Plague kicked out. Fox finally grabbed Plague by the hair and threw him out onto the floor. Plague hit into the floor hard, and bruised up his right knee pretty badly. The referee began a ten count; but then realized that Plague was seriously hurt and went onto the floor to see if he could do anything to help. Suddenly, the unlikely duo of Death & The Flying Frenchie came storming down to the ring, unbeknownst to Silver Fox and the referee. Death climbed up into the ring, turned Silver Fox around, and kicked him in the stomach... before nailing the "Death Penalty" on Fox and then dragging him into the corner, where Frenchie flew off the top rope with a 450 splash. Frenchie & Death exited and watched up near the ramp, as if they were... awaiting something. Plague quickly jumped up to his feet, and climbed into the ring... where he then easily covered the Silver Fox for the pinfall. A rope began to hang high above, and it was being lowered by some device. The rope came down near Death and Frenchie, who then directed it up into the ring as it continued to hang from the ceiling. Plague brought Silver Fox to his feet, and then Death & TFF climbed into the ring and helped Plague tie the noose around Silver Fox' neck. They then hung him high over the ring, and the crowd applauded the efforts, as they do not like SIlver Fox for whatever reasons. With Silver Fox hanging high above the ring, Slaughter stepped out for a commercial break with the awesome image fresh in our memory. Result: Plague by Pinfall Hide and Seek As we return from a word from our sponsors, "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal is shown trying to hide in the boiler room of the arena. As Green records the action, we can hear Universal muttering something about "losing it", as well as saying "I'm not going to bleed. The only way I'm donating blood tonight is if I get paid to..." Universal walked throughout the boiler room, before a small mouse scared him up some stairs where he tried to find another hiding spot... meanwhile, back in the ring... a match was getting ready to take place. Joy (c) vs. Eron the Relentless Eron came the ring, escorted by fWo security, who ushered him quickly to the ring. Eron resented the escort, and held up his arms to the fans, who cheered his arrival. Joy came out onto the ramp to the tunes of "Happiness Pie" and held the Happiness World Title up for the crowd, who responded with laughter. Joy walked to the ring, handing out suckers to the audence and skipping as he went. Inside the ring Joy told Eron that this match wasn't scheduled to be a happiness title match, but that if Eron asked very nicely he would put the title on the line. Eron responded by smacking Joy in the face and knocking him to the match. Smiling, Eron continued the beating on Joy, who attempted to remain pleasant himself so he would not lose the match. Both wrestlers smiled widely as they pasted each other with hard lefts and rights. Eron grabbed the midsection of Joy and delivered a hard backward suplex, and Joy rolled to the outside for safety. As the ref made the count on Joy, Oracle ran in from the backstage area weilding a steel chair, and connected with the back of Eron's skull. Eron fell as Joy re-entered the ring, and Joy told the ref to ask Eron if he was happy. The unconcious Eron was not able to respond, and Joy was declaired the victor. Pleased, Joy skipped from the ring, but was attacked by Primetime and Nikki on the top of the entrance ramp, and the pair had to be restrained by security as Slaughter went to a commercial... Result: Joy by Happy Darkness speaks Backstage Darkness was spotted by Dick Venom, who asked the massive superstar if he regreted what he did to the Mad Trucker. Footage from Fight the Future II was shown, where Darkness purposely attacked and fractured the leg of the Mad Trucker. Post-show footage was shown of the Trucker being loaded onto an ambulance, and taken to the local hospital. Doctors confirmed what was already suspected, that the leg of the Trucker was fractured and he would likely be out of action for some time. Returning to the live footage of Darkness with Venom, Darkness told the reporter that he didn't care if the Trucker was alive or dead, and the rest of the fWo locker room had better beware. His wraith was just starting to be released. With that Darkness grabbed a passing Billy James, and rammed him face-first into the brick wall. Fearing for his life, Dick Venom fled quickly. Rain vs. Jackhammer Jackhammer came to the ring with Wolf Fang, who held the new fWo Cruiserweight Title above his head as they walked to the ring. Jackhammer stood quietly in the corner of the ring, growling softly and waiting for the debut of the mysterious Rain. The lights dimmed, and the sound of thunder filled the arena. A shadowy figure stood on top of the entrance ramp, and made his way slowly to the ring. He stepped slowly into the ring as Jackhammer charged, but quickly ducked under and dropped Jackhammer with a drop toe hold. Rain climbed to the second rope, and dropped a fist into the face of Jackhammer, opening up his face and spraying blood onto the canvas. Not going for a pin, Rain attempted to suplex the big man, and actually got him up, before dropping him directly down in a vertical driver. As Jackhammer strugged to his feet, Rain kicked him in the midsection and delivered a vicious powerbomb, called the "Rainmaker". Rain covered Jackhammer for the pinfall victory as Wolf Fang hit the ring. Rain laughed at the smaller wrestler, and rolled to the outside. As Rain walked up the ramp, Waru came out and stared down the big man, as the cameras went to commercial. Result: Rain by Pinfall Black Quicksilver attacked Cameras followed Black Quicksilver from the fWo parking lot, to the backstage area. He held Stump under one arm, and discussed Russian politics as they walked. Suddenly, the Total Eclipse wandered out in front of Black Quicksilver, and created a distraction by pretending that he was "dead sexy". Black Quicksilver reeled in disgust, as the Chameleon snuck up behind him. The Chameleon attacked Black Quicksilver with a steel chair and quickly grabbed Stump. Laughing, the Chameleon waved Stump and Black Quicksilver, and made a quick escape... Lost and Found Finally, after a near half hour of chasing after Universal, Foley found him getting ready to climb into his limosuine preparing to make a quick exit from the arena. Foley grabbed Universal by his shirt, and pulled him out, before nailing him with several punches. He then brought him around the limo and threw him head first into the limo, which busted him open immediately. Universal began bleeding like a pig, and Foley delivered on his guarantee to make Universal bleed before the night was over. Foley then grabbed Universal and brought him up onto the very top of the limo, before piledriving him on top of the limo. As Universal continued to bleed, Foley pulled Mr. Socko out of his tights and slipped it onto his hand, before applying the mandible socko on Universal and rendering him unconscious. Suddenly, Austin Davis came out of the limo and climbed up onto the limo. Davis was sporting a referee's shirt, preparing for this Friday when he referees the First-Blood match between Foley & Universal. He slapped the limo three times as Foley pinned Universal, before climbing off and going over to Ford Explorer with large tires, and climbing in. He then started the vehicle, and as Foley got off the limo and left, Davis sped right ahead into the limo with the Ford Explorer... crashing into the limo and bumping Universal off the top of it. Davis then sped out of the arena area in the damaged Explorer, while Warfare went to a commercial break... Ultra Violet (c) vs. Stevie Roberts Stevie Roberts walked out onto the entrance ramp with VL next to him, and the two walked to the ring in stride. Roberts saluted the crowd, then kneeled on one knee and kissed VL's hand with a swoop. Ultra Violet walked to the ring with Violet, who eyed VL with some suspicion. VL held up both arms and shruged, telling Violet that she was just going to stand at ringside and not do anything to interfere in the match. Ultra Violet stumbled into the ring and stared at Stevie questioning. Stevie gestured to his crotch, and UV looked down at his tights with some embarrasement to check his fly. Stevie attacked with a running lariat that flipped Ultra Violet to the outside. VL stood back from him as Violet ran around to make certain that everything was going well. Roberts ran to the opposite end of the ring and rebounded off, flying over the top rope and striking Ultra Violet on the outside. Roberts rolled him back into the ring and took a moment to leer at Violet, and then kiss VL deeply. Violet grumbled and walked to the other end of the ring as Roberts re-entered the ring. Roberts attacked with quick kicks, but the delay had given Ultra Violet a chance to recover and he grabbed the leg of Roberts and flipped him down. Both men back on their feet, Roberts charged but Ultra Violet ducked under and grabbed Roberts by the head. Ultra Violet struck Roberts in the head, and then prepared him for the piledriver. As Ultra Violet was about to strike, VL entered the ring and kicked him from behind, earning a quick DQ victory for Ultra Violet. Roberts and VL continued their attack until Violet called to the backstage area, and the US Marshal ran to the ring. Violet and the Marshal both entered the ring and chased VL and Stevie off, who laughed at the trio from the ramp. As the cameras went off the air, Violet and the Marhsal helped UV to his feet and the crowd cheered... Result: Ultra Violet by DQ
