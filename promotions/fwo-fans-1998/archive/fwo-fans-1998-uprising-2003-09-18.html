Introduction The Wrestler Of The Year tournament rolls on... Choices Do you believe in fate? Or chance? How about coincidence? I do. It does exist. I cannot ask you to believe what I say as I am sure you don't know who I am, or what I have experienced, what I have seen or felt. Trust is something that is tossed around these days, no longer is trust such a strong feeling in which a sense of comfort follows. However, fate does exist. Coincidence is here for a reason, as people as meant for something. Some people are meant to do great things, and some are meant to do horrible things. Although it is also important to remember that we do have a choice, that no matter how much life is laid out for us, and our fate is somewhat pre-determined. We make the final decision that opens the door into the next phase, we make the final call. As that is how it is, and how it will always be. It is the final test, the test in which we are given to prove where our heart lies, and defy all those scientific facts only to rely on one true mystery, the human soul. Choices are made everyday, and from them consequences. We all make our choice eventually. In the Shadows The camera pans to see Steven Shadows' dressing room. Suddenly a familiar figure walks up to the door and opens it. This figure was obviously not Steven Shadows but in fact it was Vince Jacobs. Vince walked into Shadows' room and looks around. He sees a fruit tray on the table in front of the sofa. Vince grabs a grape and pops it in his mouth and sits down on the sofa. "So this how the fWo treats its regulars." Vince thought to himself Vince propped his feet up on the coffee table and laid back relaxing. Obviously Vince was a very brave man to be waiting in the lion's den sort of speak. Vince didn't care.. He wanted Shadows as much as Shadow wanted him. The door swung open as Steven Shadows walked in with a cane and Lisa behind him carrying his bags. Shadows stopped dead in his tracks to see Vince Jacobs sitting on his sofa in his dressing room. Shadows wanted to pounce on Vince for his actions two weeks ago when Vince actually had the audacity to come to Steven Shadows' house and attack his girlfriend and him. Vince slowly stood to his feet as Lisa shut the door behind her. Vince smiled looking at both Steven and Lisa. "What the fuck are you doing in my room?" Shadows said inching his way toward Vince Vince held his hand up in Shadows' direction. "Whoa there cowboy... I am not here to fight. I am just here to talk." "You got five minutes to say what's on your mind Vince." Vince motioned for Shadows to have a seat. Vince sat in a chair across from the sofa as Lisa and Steven sat down. "You don't understand what I have to do to reclaim my previous glory. People automatically want to jump on me and make a name for themselves because they think they can beat SVJ." Vince said Shadows and Lisa looked at Vince not showing any signs of remorse... "Just because I was the former CWL Heavyweight Champion, promoters want SVJ on their roster so they can push their wrestlers to the top off my name." "You know Vince... I am tired of hearing this sob story. If you have nothing pertinent to say then get the hell out of my room." Shadows said with a stern look on his face Vince stood up and started to walk toward the door. He turned around and peered back at Steven Shadows. "I thought you of all people would understand how I feel. I'll go now." Vince turned around and sunk his head as he walked out of the door. Vince closed the door behind and looked up with a sick grin on his face. Inside the room Steven turned to Lisa. "I don't believe anything that son-of-a-bitch just said." Lisa looked at Steven. "Even I could tell he was lying." Preparation "This is really fun, Jordan," RAM said while walking towards his tag team partner who was lacing up his wrestling boots. He was wrestling in the Wrestler of the Year tournament just like RAM was. RAM seemed just happy to be involved in such a prestigious competition, Jordan on the other hand, seemed completely serious. Jordan looked pissed off, as usual when he stood up and locked eyes with his tag team partner. SMACK! RAM stood standing in front of Jordan X Shabazz, breathing heavily. Jordan had a sadistic look on his face as he began to speak, "GET SERIOUS!" he screamed. "We may not be tag teaming our way to the top at the moment, but both you and I know that as Survivor winners we have to prove ourselves to be versatile." RAM continued to breathe heavily as Jordan continued to eyeball him deeply. "I've got my own match to worry about," the self-proclaimed Black Superman started. "Get your mind correct." "I'm going to catering." And with that, Jordan was gone. RAM stood there, in the locker room looking like a crazed madman. He walked over to a mirror and looked himself in the face... CRACK! The sound of RAM's head smashing against the mirror reverberated throughout the empty locker room as blood streamed down his forehead. The self-inflicted wound of anger seemed to excite the baby-faced giant. RAM laughed to himself as he walked out of the locker room and made his way to the ring, it was time that he proved himself to Jordan X Shabazz has a competent and trustworthy tag team partner. RAM vs. Sphere (w/Evil-Lyn) "Son of a Bitch" by Nazareth blared throughout the speakers as Sphere slowly made his way down the ramp with Evil-Lyn on his arm. The crowd seemed rather indifferent to the former Tag Team champion as he laughed his way down the ramp. He rolled himself into the ring and began to bounce back in forth in preparation as Evil-Lyn took her rightful place in his corner. "What If I Was White" blasted over the PA system as RAM stepped through the curtains with his blood streaked face showing nothing but discontent for his opponent. The crowd jeered one-half of the fWo Survivor III champions and former fWo Hardcore champion as he made his down the ramp. RAM rolled himself into the ring as official Tom Renner signaled for the ringing of the bell. Sphere immediately began to stomp on the back of the big man as he shook off the kicks. RAM got to his feet and grabbed Sphere by the throat. He tossed Sphere carelessly into the corner before stalking in slowly looking to hit some rights and lefts, Sphere used his agility advantage to slip out from under RAM and immediately hit him with two kicks to the ribs. Sphere backed up into the center of the ring where he posed cockily after making RAM look like a fool. This angered the big man and it showed as he lumbered across the ring looking to take Sphere down, Sphere sidestepped him and embarrassed him again with a drop toehold. The crowd cheered Sphere as RAM got to his feet with a look of anger on his face. He ran right at Sphere only to be taken down with yet another drop toehold. RAM got to his feet again before being hit with a drop kick. He rolled to the outside of the ring where he tried to gather his thoughts. Before he could even get a clear thought through his head, he was hit in the shoulder with a baseball slide from Sphere. Sphere attempted to take it to RAM on the outside of the ring by hitting him with a two forearms to the side of the face. After those two, Sphere tried a third, which was promptly blocked and got him hit with a head butt from RAM. Sphere hit the protective mats with a thud as RAM appeared to be pissed. Instead of trying to capitalize on his first real offense of the night, RAM made his way over to timekeepers table and shoved him out of his seat. The crowd became silent in anticipation as RAM grabbed the blue steel chair behind him and folded it up. Sphere got to his feet as RAM gently tapped the chair on the mat. RAM smashed the steel chair across Sphere's face, causing it to crumple up. Tom Renner immediately signaled for the bell as the Wrestler Of The Year match was ended in less than five minutes. RAM slammed the chair onto the mat as he realized what he had done. He had ruined his chances of advancing due to the simple fact that his anger had taken control over him. Evil-Lyn tended to Sphere as RAM screamed in anger. The crowd was upset and disappointed at the length of the match and how it turned out. Sphere advanced. RAM began his slow trek back up the ramp to a chorus of jeers from the crowd. And the heavy weight of shame hanging over his head. Both men were interrupted by the sound of thunder in the background, Fox moved toward the window and looked out, it seemed as though a storm might be on the way. The Arch Factor The past couple months have gone anything but right for the Lunatic. He stood there, in the bathroom set off for the fWo wrestlers, suited up and ready to go out there and entertain, only for his name never to be called. There was a lot of that going around. With Wippit and Mufango and Padrona all missing in the course of a month, Flyer had been left with no one in the division to really work with. Bowman and Mittens were set to be together, the two golden children from Disney's point of view. Where was Flyer in all of this? More importantly, did they even care anymore? But it goes before that. It goes to when he was in aWc and the promotion folded shortly after he dropped the Heavyweight strap due to financial problems, just when they were hitting their stride creatively. It goes back to when he joined Action! and failed to recapture the crowd in a larger venue setting. Maybe he should have just stayed in the small bingo halls, the gymnasiums and the places were wrestling was thought of as nothing more than a spectacle by those renting out their buildings to it. Where he was respected and cheered for what he had done in the past. Not what he was doing now. The world has become filled with people asking "What are you doing for me now." And the only thing Flyer did tonight... ... was punch a hole into the tile wall of the fWo's male restroom. Shards of tile fell to the ground while the dust flew into the sky. His knuckles, just a bit bloody, but the frustration made the pain invisible to the eyes of his mind. He was determined to make the fWo do something with him. He was determined not to become the next Sabu or Abdullah the Butcher. Veteran wrestlers respected for what they've done and watched as a side show act like they were tigers jumping through flaming hoops in the circus. And if he won the Wrestler of the Year tournament... maybe he'd be out there in the ring with Brand Frontier and Lance Knight, Deacon and Eli Flair. And it wouldn't be for what he's done. It'd be for what he's doing. It's great to be on the upswing of a career, heading to the top on a wave of success. But eventually, that wave, that roller coaster, has to come back down. Gravity, the physics behind an arch. All these weighed down on the reality of life. So let's break the cycle. Let's create a new arch. A new starting point. Let's create the Wrestler of the Year. Elliot Harper vs. Jordan X Shabazz As "They Came In" hit, Elliot Harper did not look best pleased as he was apparently shoved out through the curtain, and out onto the stage. Without his bulky brother for back-up, he would be ripe for the picking in the 1st round of the Legend's tourney... With the picker being Jordan X Shabazz. One week ago they were team-mates, both on the winning side, but tonight... Well, tonight he was going to get his ass kicked. Into the ring Elliot rolled, looking every bit like the wrestler in patterned tights, elbow pads and intricate wrist tape to go with his chiselled physique... But he wasn't acting the part, as the sweat that moistened his brow was from fear, not preparation. As Harper's gaze darted around, "What If I Was White?" started up, and Jordan X Shabazz made his way from the back, punching the air before him with purpose. Both men were devoid of their much larger and stronger tag team partners... But Shabazz had his experience and skill to make up for it. He was famous even before Survivor III, thanks to NTICW, PCW and A!... As for Elliot, he was just riding the coat-tails of his brother... Leeching off his success. But tonight it seemed as if he'd finally be at the mercy of someone, without Thomas to bail him out. Jordan slid into the ring, and Elliot decided if he was going to walk away from this match, he had to do something... That something being a Baseball Slide into Shabazz's ribs. The bell rang and Elliot pounded away with forearms, that Shabazz was shrugging off as he rose to his feet. Looking worried that Jordan wasn't being subdued, Elliot bounced off the ropes, an awkward act for the small man, but was met with the stiff arm of Shabazz, who nearly Clotheslined his head off. A cheer went out as Elliot crashed down flat on his back and blinked up at the arena lights... They crowd might have hated both men, but they wanted to see Elliot beaten black and blue too. A dazed Elliot patted his lip for blood, before Jordan went pull him up... To which Harper responded by roiling away and scurrying to the outside, something the fWo fans didn't appreciate. Both Shabazz and the referee called for Elliot to re-enter the ring, but Harper just fobbed them off, telling the referee to count while he tended to his sore mouth. 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... 7... 8... 9... Just as it seemed Elliot would take the count-out loss, he rolled back into the ring to break the count. Hopping back to his feet, the cowardly Harper showed off his toned physique before urging Shabazz to come for him. The nerves that plagued him pre-match seemed to be fading as Elliot prepared for Jordan's attack.. But cut him off with a boot to the ribs... ...Only he didn't, as Shabazz caught Elliot's foot in mid-air. Elliot hopped on his left foot and pleaded with Shabazz, who was getting ready to nail the Harper brother with a big right hand. In the nick of time, Elliot exploded out with an Enziguri, only for Jordan to easily duck and watch Elliot crash front-first down onto the canvas. With Elliot sprawled out below him, Shabazz quickly hit an Elbow Drop to the back of his head, then locked in a tight Headlock. Flailing his arms around, Elliot tried to break free of Jordan's tight hold, something he finally achieved by reaching the ropes and clinging to them for dear life. Jordan broke, and Elliot again went right to the outside, grabbing his burning ears that were hurt by the tight grip of Jordan. The referee had no choice, and again he began his count. 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... 7... 8... 9... To the frustration of the crowd, Elliot again took until the last split second before rolling back into the ring... Where Jordan was waiting for him. Shabazz went to pull Elliot up, but the smaller Harper shot between his legs to escape. It didn't take Jordan long to get upright and turn around, just in time to block a forearm from the sneaky Elliot... Then land his own. With a thud, Jordan rocked Elliot's chin, dropping him immediately. Again, Elliot went to scramble to the outside, but Jordan grabbed his foot. Desperately trying to escape, Elliot kicked his feet out violently, eventually freeing himself, and allowing passage back to the outside. Another jeer from the pissed off crowd was all that met Elliot outside, as he wiped the blood from his bottom lip. Sighing, the referee again made the count he was used to by then. 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... 7... 8... 9... Repeating the formula, Elliot slid in at 9... Only for Shabazz to run and catch him with a kick to the ribs on the way, sending a winded Elliot dropping back out again. With the fans popping, Jordan dropped to the outside and pulled up a squirming Harper, before tossing him into the guard-rail. Elliot yelled out as he collided with the steel, but was grabbed by the hair right away by Jordan, then send flying into the steel steps. The fans were loving it, as Elliot's haven on the outside was shattered. Groggy and swinging his arms madly in all directions, Elliot was then scooped up by Jordan... before being dropped down across the guard-rail with a Hot-Shot. Elliot grabbed his throat and lashed around on the floor, gasping for breath, but the only sympathy Shabazz gave him was to roll his body back into the ring. Instinctively, Elliot crawled behind the referee, trying to use him as a shield, but Shabazz simply shoved the referee out of the way and stomped Elliot's throbbing head. With Elliot squirming, Jordan pulled him up off the mat and whipped Harper into the ropes... Only for Elliot to grab them in order to prevent rebounding and again go to scramble out of the ring, but Shabazz rushed to cut him off. Elliot was halfway out of the ring with Shabazz straining to pull him back in as the referee went to call for a break, seeing as Harper was in the ropes. But, seeing Jordan was focussed on dragging him back in, Elliot quickly pushed himself in and took a swing at Shabazz... Which the Black Superman ducked, but the helpless referee didn't. On the mat, the referee grabbed his jaw and screwed his face up, while Shabazz yanked Elliot fully into the ring by his tights and began kicking away at his flailing form. Desperately trying to block the kicks, Elliot curled up into a ball, but the crowds mixed noise suddenly turned jeer-heavy as Thomas Harper's huge frame removed itself from under the ring. With the official down, Thomas slid in behind Shabazz who was too busy putting the boots into his brother to notice. Suddenly, and roughly, Thomas spun Jordan around and locked in a Mandible Claw. Jordan couldn't fight the paralysing hold, and was hoisted up shortly before being spiked down with Thomas' Mandible Slam. As soon as Jordan's body impacted, Elliot un-curled himself and covered... Waiting for the referee to come to. But before the official came to his senses, Jordan's tag team partner, RAM, came sprinting down the aisle... He had suspected the Harpers would pull something. Into the ring RAM slid, hopping up in time to catch a charging Thomas with a Powerslam. Grabbing hold of the younger Harper's long hair, RAM then pulled him right back up again and heaved him into the air for a Chokeslam... Before dumping the timid monster over the rope, Harper thumping against the apron on his way down. RAM turned, to be met with the sight of Elliot still covering Jordan, praying that the referee would have come to while RAM was dealing with Thomas... He wasn't so lucky. The cowardly Harper slowly stood and begged RAM off, but his words were ineffective. Closer and closer RAM got to Elliot, who's pleading fell on death ears... A point proven by RAM's boot shooting up and catching Elliot under the chin and sending him staggering out through the ropes, his limp body landing at the foot of the aisle. Then, the bell rang. Rubbing his eyes with one hand, the referee signalled to the time keeper with the other... The first thing he saw after his eyes opened being RAM's boot connecting with Harper. Elliot's humiliation had turned into the cheapest of cheap and stolen wins. As RAM bellowed down at the dazed official, Thomas stumbled around the ring and helped his brother to his feet, carrying his unconscious form up the aisle over his shoulder. A totally undeserving Elliot Harper was through to the 2nd round. And it was disgusting. Result: Elliot Harper via DQ Confrontation *SMACK* Prod slammed his right fist into the palm of his left hand. He stood in his dressing room, looking hard at his reflection into the mirror. Nodding his head slightly he grinned, "This is your chance Prod, you'll be on top by the end of the night. Prod immediately sneered and then pointed his finger at Reily, "You," Reily took a step back and Prod a step forward, Prod spoke in a stronger more aggressive tone, "I thought I told you to stay away from me." Reily jumped up to his feet, "No listen to me Levi-" "HEY!" Reily look to his left to notice Detective Hughes standing at the opposite end of the hallway, Hughes screamed down at Reily, "Noah Reily DO NOT MOVE! You are under ARREST!" Reily's eyes shot back to Prod, "Levite look at me." Prod's eyes caught Reily's immediately and Reily now spoke softly to him, "If I could tell one thing that would save your life, that would be run. Run Prod, run." Prod was speechless, for the first time, what Reily said really hit him hard and an overwhelming sense of belief overcame Prod. In the next instant Reily was gone, running from a now chasing Detective Hughes. As Hughes ran past the room, Prod snapped back into reality. He had a match. The Dynamo (c) vs. Levite Prod The fans had piled in for the latest fWo Uprising, their noise alerting the entire town of their presence. They were excited, ready and waiting for the next match. It was a hardcore match, but they knew little of the first competitor. He was a newcomer to the fWo's scene, and they didn't know what quite to expect. The one thing they expected however was insanity. So far the man had brought a dead body with him, and stabbed everyone's favorite tag team, the Animals. Now, he was to bring his extreme style to a hardcore match against a man they knew all too well; A man who was familiar to anyone who watched Uprising. That man? The fWo Hardcore Champion. His name? The Dynamo. So when the unfamiliar sounds of Radiohead filled the arena, nobody really knew what to expect. The dark claps of percussion and slow hum filled the arena. Then the words of the cult band's frontman were heard, In pitch dark I go walking in your landscape Broken branches trip me as I speak. Just cos you feel it doesn't mean it's there Just cos you feel it doesn't mean it's there The haunting echoes of the music blared as Levite Prod made his way down the ramp to the ring. His eyes were bloodshot, focused only on each step before him. He slid into the ring under the bottom rope, and looked at nothing. He merely slammed one fist into the other, mouthed a few words to himself, and then waited. The fans, relatively quiet and nervous around the giant man, got relatively louder, exploding for the words that next came over the TravisTron. "HARDCORE... CHAMPION" filled the screen, and the guitars of the Vines hit in the arena. The familiar words were heard, and even sung by the audience. I'M GONNA GET FREE! I'M GONNA GET FREE! I'M GONNA GET FREE RIDE INTO THE SUN! The Dynamo walked out, the belt glowing around his waist. Scars slowly healing on his body, his knee visibly bandaged with black tape, he kindly slapped hands with the fans as he walked down the ramp. He smiled brightly, and then looked at Prod, the smile fading to reluctance. He jumped up onto the apron, and hesitantly ducked under the rope. Throwing his arms high in the ring, he smiled as the music cut. He held out a hand to Prod, only to receive a cold glare, and then he felt the vice grip of the massive hand before him. Dynamo shook Levite's hand, and then handed his belt to the official. The bell rang and Dynamo stared into the eyes of his opponent. He circled to his left, as Prod did the same, the two of the tracing the mat with their boots. Prod stood taller than Dynamo, putting the champion at a disadvantage, and took the liberty of attempting to strike first. Levite lunged at Dynamo, who sidestepped the blow, and threw a hard kick into Prod's stomach. Prod doubled over, and Dynamo followed up with a hard axe handle to Prod's neck and head. Levite shook off the blow pretty easily, surprising his opponent. He quickly grabbed the Dynamo's arm, and pulled him forward, then hit him with a hard uppercut. Dynamo spit blood already as he went flying back into the ropes, and Prod was there to slam his boot into the hero's face, sending him over the ropes to the outside. Somehow Dynamo was able to land on his feet, and he quickly stepped away towards the announcer's tables. Prod had climbed out of the ring, and pointed at the champion. Dynamo clenched his fists hard, and stepped towards Prod. Like De La Hoya, he threw quick fists of fury repeatedly to Prod's head. Levite's slow reaction caused him to cover his face late, as one blow got him hard in the eye. He backed up, and Dynamo took the opportunity to dropkick him back into the ringpost. Hitting the back of his head against the metal beam, he clutched his skull as the Dynamo stepped to his feet. The fans cheered their hero as he threw a knee into Prod's stomach, then DDT'd him to the ground. Prod clutched his head once more, and rolled on the ground. Dynamo jumped up, smiling, and slid into the ring. He looked up to the ramp, checking for any sign of Lundin or something familiar, but found nothing. He turned back, only to see Prod sliding in after him. He then saw something shine from the spotlights on Prod's waist. The momentary distraction was all Prod needed, and he slammed a fist into the champion's gut. Fiercely gripping Dynamo, he locked his arms around his body. He then used his 310 pound frame to pull Dynamo backwards, hitting a hard T-bone suplex. Dynamo's body hit the mat with a hard thud that echoed throughout the arena. Prod then stood up, and began to lay hard boots into the wrestler. Dynamo jolted with each blow as they left welts on his chest area. Picking up Dynamo, Levite kicked his knee hard, the bandages signifying the remnants of the match against High Flyer. Buckling under his own weight, Dynamo went down onto his bad knee, only to have Prod grab him by the neck, lift him up, and then quickly let go to grab his waist and whip him down with a spinebuster variation. The fans let out a collective "OH!" as Dynamo's spine cracked against the ring. Prod then wasted no time and once more slammed boots into the body of the hardcore hero. Levite Prod stood up after throwing five hard boots, and threw his arms high. He felt glorious. He had something to prove tonight. To Fox, to Hughes, to Reily, they didn't matter. Tonight was his night. "PROD! PROD! PROD!" he screamed. A few fans near the ring chanted with him, but others countered back with their "DY-NA-MO!" chant. Soon, the entirety of the arena was engaged in the dueling chants. Prod just smiled, and then lifted Dynamo up. He grabbed Dynamo around the waist, and squeezed him in an enormous bear hug. He then took the large hero and lifted him higher again, and then fell backwards, dropping Dynamo onto his face and the mat beneath him. Dynamo was out cold. This was Prod's chance. He went for the pin, the clean victory. He knew it, he felt it. The referee ran around and slid down to the mat to get the count. 1... 2... ... Prod jumped up, but not in victory. The referee pointed to the ropes, and Dynamo had gotten his foot there somehow. Angered, Prod lifted him up once more. Dynamo harnessed his energy, and shot back with a hard fist to Prod's face. His former strategy of trying to use any sort of speed against the monster was now gone. He merely wanted to survive. Dynamo hit Prod again, this time harder, and then grabbed his arm. He whipped Levite to the ropes, and then tried the clothesline. Prod ducked the blow, and bounded off the opposite ropes. Dynamo lifted his foot for the big boot, but Levite caught it, and then threw his leg back towards him, sending his force and Dynamo's weight onto the champion's bad knee. Dynamo crumpled to the mat, and Prod was there. Prod began to choke Dynamo. Choking him hard. Choking the fight from him. Dynamo felt himself passing out. He was simply being overpowered with that same stiff style he had debuted since winning his title. Prod then lifted Dynamo high, and clotheslined him down hard. The head of the Dynamo hit the mat, and bounced back. Prod then pinned once more, slamming all his weight onto Dynamo's body. The referee counted again, Prod making sure no foot was falling on the ropes this time. 1... 2... 3... No. The Dynamo got a shoulder up just in time, and Prod cursed aloud. He needed this win, and he was going to get it. So as he lifted Dynamo once more, he began to reach for his belt, but then hesitated. Once more hesitation caused a turn in the match. Dynamo was all over that split second, and threw a forearm into Prod's chest, sending him backwards. Dynamo followed up with a dropkick that sent Prod to the outside apron, barely gripping the ropes. Dynamo stood up, then kicked Prod's hand, sending him crumbling to the floor below. Dynamo slid out himself, reached under the ring, and searched for a weapon. What he found was a shovel. More than one in fact, dozens of shovels. He cursed Lundin's handiwork, and then found himself compelled to use the tool. Prod began to groggily stand, and Dynamo was there, bringing the shovel down over the skull of Prod. Prod now was out cold. Dynamo then lifted the gardening device over his head, and like a gravedigger he slammed the tip into Prod's back. He then raised it once more, and slammed it flatly into Prod's spinal column. Prod screamed out in pain, coming to his senses. Dynamo looked at the shove, the tip now bloody having drawn blood from Prod's back, and he felt anger surge through his body. He looked at Prod and then the shovel and reeled back, and the cameras zoomed into Dynamo's face, showing the obvious fear that now melted his hardened glare. He threw the shovel away back under the ring, and turned away from Prod. He began to speak to the referee, but before words came out, he felt searing pain in his foot. Prod had gotten him, and the fans were shocked at what they saw. It was something glittering in the light, like the flash of a piranha in the ocean.
