Wednesday Warfare, 22 September 1999

22 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Heroism. To overcome even the greatest obstacles and surpass expectations. To make an impression that will last forever. The road to the fWo Wrestler of the Year finals has not been an easy one. Thirty two wrestlers began the journey weeks ago and now only six remain. By the time Warfare has come and gone, two more shall fall by the wayside leaving but four athletes to enter the final phase. Tonight the most decorated wrestler in the fWo, the Flying Frenchie will attempt to earn his spot in fWo history when he goes up against the man who is like a force of nature onto himself, the mighty Rain. Both men have defeated powerful opponents to get this far but when the dust settles only one's hopes will remain alive. Tonight the established takes on the newcomer as Scott Collins better known the world over as Death, leader of the Dark Riders will battle Courage, a man who has shown an unstoppable urge to win. For Death, his very reputation and standing with the Dark Riders is at stake. To lose against a newcomer is to lose face. For Courage it is the chance to continue forward on his winning streak and take down another fWo name. The ultimate glory is but two days away... and the stakes have never been higher. Welcome to Warfare. Crucifix (c) vs. VL Fireworks explode overhead as VL saunters down to ringside, dragging behind her a cart full of weapons. She sorts through it and settles on a chain as the lights lower. Lightning strikes the entrance ramp, and Crucifix walks down the aisle, the Hardcore Title slung over his right shoulder. As he approaches the ring, VL catches him with a plancha that sends both him and his belt to the floor, and the bell rings. VL strikes the downed Crucifix with her chain-wrapped fist, bloodying him within seconds. She relents, and rolls back into the ring, taunting Crucifix. Enraged, he slides into the ring, but VL starts stomping away before he can take his feet. She changes strategies and starts whipping the Hardcore champ with the chain until he rolls out of the ring. He walks over to her cart, and grabs a baseball bat. Armed, he steps onto the apron, swinging wildly to back VL off. He steps over the top rope, and the two stare down. The tension breaks as Crucifix smacks the bat against her skull, dropping her to the mat. He attacks her ribs and her legs with the bat before hurling it out of the ring. Crucifix hits several kneedrops to her ribs, and picks her up. He whips VL to the ropes and hits a 180 spinebuster, rocking the ring. Grinning maniacally, he makes the cover, but VL gouges his eyes and reverses, earning herself a two count. Both competitors back up, and Crucifix angry now. A kick to the gut bends VL over, and a knee lift sends her to the mat. Now she rolls out of the ring to regroup, and Crucifix follows. The two trade punches on the outside, but Crucifix's blood is starting to blind him, and he slows down enough to give VL the edge. She leads him to the ring steps, and performs the dreaded DTH onto them, opening up Crucifix's forehead further. She rolls him into the ring, and heads up top. Reverse frogsplash only gets two, and VL heads back out for more toys. This time she grabs a broomstick before heading back in, and she catches with a flurry of shots to the back and the head. The final shot to his skull breaks the stick, sending half of it into the crowd like a lethal souvenir. She uses the remaining half to choke out Crucifix, but even with her weight on top of him, he drags himself to the ropes. He pulls himself to his feet using the ropes, with VL still on his back, and topples over the top rope to the floor. He lands on top of VL, who seems to have smacked her head against the thin mat. After lying there for a while, Crucifix takes his feet, and rolls VL back into the ring. He lifts the KO'd VL up for a Cruci-bomb, and lands it on his much smaller opponent. The ref counts to three and hands Crucifix the Hardcore Title. Crucifix leaves the ring somberly, just barely still the champ. Wednesday Warfare prepared to go to commerical, but suddenly Scott Slugger's music played, and the fWo World Heavyweight Champion walked out onto the entrance ramp... Result: Crucifix by Pinfall Scott Slugger has his say Scott Slugger looked around himself at the top of the entrance ramp, and removing the fWo World Heavyweight title from his waist he slung it over his shoulder and walked to the ring. Chris Finn offered him a microphone, but Slugger only sneered and pulled his own microphone from the pocket of his leather jacket. Slugger began to speak, and then stopped and looked around at the crowd. The crowd booed him, and Scott Slugger nodded appreciatively at them. "You can make all the damn noise you want... but when you're the fWo World Champion, you can do any damn thing you please. So you keep making noise, because I don't give a damn." The crowd booed harder, and Scott Slugger began to polish his World Championship belt with one sleeve, a cocky smirk never leaving his face. The crowd settled when it was clear that Slugger was going nowhere, and he began to speak again. "Last week on his program I had the easiest damn match I ever had... and remember, I've wrestled RottDog before. I brought the so-called owner of this promotion to the ring, I kicked his ass, and I showed him who the real man in command is." More booing from the audience as Slugger continued. "But last week was special for another reason as well... hey, Scotty... you hear me back there? First to fifty, Scotty. Remember that dead boy, first to fifty right here." Slugger smiled more broadly and tapped his title again. "Know what? I did a little checking and it seems as though I've held this title more than anyone else in the fWo by a pretty good margin, so you might just say I'm feeling just a little lucky. And dammit, when you're as successful as I am, you try and give the little people a rub... being that so many of them suck and all. And who sucks more than NXS?" Slugger held the microphone out to the crowd, who at once shouted, "NOBODY!" Slugger smiled, and went back to speaking. "That's right. Nobody. So I'm a nice man, a kind man...a man who is looking to give something back to his industry. That's why I'm putting my fWo World Heavyweight Championship up against not one man... no, not two men... but all three men in that little SUX group or whatever the hell those three jabronies are called. This Friday, fWo Fallout... what I'm offering is a Gauntlet match. Me against CrapDogg, Judge Suck, and Markus Can't. And count on this... I'm going to knock you three losers right out of the park. Now, go to commercial... the owner is done." Slugger slung his title over his shoulder and vaulted over the top rope as the Warfare producers obliged, and went to commercial... Joy vs. Mike Bear Coming back from the break, the first thing viewers see is a giant smiling mouth. The camera backs up, and the mouth is shown to belong to Glee who waves and smiles at the crowd, accompanying Joy as he pushes a cotton candy machine down to ringside. He makes a few sticks for the ringside fans before entering the ring and taking a microphone. "Mike Bear, what's wrong with being happy? I'm happy. Glee's happy. The whole crowd is just so gosh-darned incredibly happy they could sing! But not you, oh no. What you need, Mike, and what I'm going to give you when I win this Happiness Match, is a new perspective in life, a happier one, and changing your name to Teddy is a good start. You'll thank me for it later." Joy hands the mic to a ringside attendant while fans throw garbage, chairs, and the elderly at him. He takes it all in stride, a bright smile on his face. Mike Bear appears on the entrance ramp, and there seems to be something glistening at the sides of his mouth. As the cameras zoom, we see that he's safety-pinned his bottom lip to his cheeks, forcing a smile upon his face. Joy appears momentarily shocked, and then simply tells Mike that that's one of those things that's going to change, and soon. Bear levels him with a right hand, starting the match. Joy bounces back to his feet, and takes Bear down with a reverse jawbreaker. He follows that with his Super Happy Slamtastic, a move that would earn him the win in a regular match, but not under happiness rules. Joy goes to the outside, where he reclaims his microphone. "Something's got to disturb you, Mike. Hey, I know, I'm getting some all the time! With her! Doesn't that make you furious?" Bear heads to the outside in pursuit, the macabre grin still on his face. He catches up to Joy and slaps him like a little girl, eliciting a huge response from the cynical wrestling fans in attendance. He grabs Joy and brings him to the cotton candy machine, shoving his face into the bowl, just above the arm that spins the sweet sugar strands around. Joy reemerges gasping for air, his face covered in pink wisps of yumminess. Bear lifts Joy for a suplex, turns, and brings him down with one of the front-layout variety. He then picks Joy up, and whips him into the cotton candy machine, crashing it to the ground. Joy looks momentarily distraught, but before the ref notices he makes an "aw shucks" motion with his arms and puts on a happy face. That doesn't last long as Mike Bear DDTs Joy on top of the fallen machine. Joy seems to be near tears and the ref calls for the bell, declaring Mike the winner via Happy. Bear pulls out the safety pins and starts to walk away, but Joy gets back on the mic again. "Alright, you beat me Bear. But don't you think it would be nice if you'd change your name to Teddy Bear anyway? C'mon kiddo, work with me here." Mike shakes his head, and approaches Joy. He grabs the microphone away from him. Courage was screaming at Black Quicksilver, who was trying his best to break free and throttle the newcomer to the fWo. With Black Quicksilver fully restrained Courage began to mock Black Quicksilver, reaching over and slapping him on the head. Black Quicksilver struggled harder yelling, "Courage? Coward is more like it... you can't beat me unless you've got that fat tub of lard helping you cheat! You're not enough of a man to face Stump, let alone me!" Courage's mocking smile turned serious, and he pointed at Black Quicksilver and began to yell himself. "You want me to kick your ass one more time? Fine... bring it mask boy! You leave your piece of disgusting smelly scalp back and I'll leave fat bastard here. I'm already scheduled for one match that night after I kick Death's sorry butt, and I've got enough to take your ass to school and still not break a sweat. The fWo wants Courage to bump up their buy rates? Fine by me. See you Friday you pathetic pile of crap... Courage will show you what it means to be a real man." Black Quicksilver struggled free and lunged at Courage, and the two fell to the cold arena floor throwing lefts and rights. fWo security struggled to separate the two as cameras went to the ring... Rain vs. Flying Frenchie The cameras returned to the ring as the mighty Rain strode confidently into the arena. Cameras followed Rain's progress to the ring, and thunder rippled through the upper levels of the arena. Rain parted the ring ropes and stepped through, taking a long hard look at the crowd as he went. The referee came over to check on Rain, and Rain grabbed the referee's head and looked into his eyes. Rain appeared satisfied with whatever he saw, and released the referee and turned to the entrance ramp. Mumbling, Rain said, "Well, let's see which one comes out..." The Flying Frenchie's music played, and he appeared, carrying a large French flag with him. Flanked by Primetime and Mitch Wilson, the Frenchie strode confidently to the ring, waving to fans that by the reaction of the crowd were obviously not in attendance. Rain backed up to let the Frenchie enter, and the Frenchie rushed him, attacking fiercely. With Rain off guard, the Frenchie hit several running drop kicks in succession, trying to get the big man off his feet. The Frenchie went for another running drop kick, and Rain grabbed his legs and swung him into the turnbuckle. The Frenchie struck his head on the steel, but Rain did not let go of his legs. Using his upper body strength, Rain lifted the Frenchie up by his legs and swatted him face-first into the mat. Rain then swung the Frenchie out of the ring, and he landed in a heap at Mitch Wilson's feet. Rain went to the ropes and executed a tope to the outside, but Wilson pulled the Frenchie out of the way and Rain crashed into the steel barrier. Getting to his feet, the Frenchie picked Rain up and executed a German suplex from the apron. The Frenchie rolled Rain back into the ring and attempted a cover, but Rain kicked out at two. The Frenchie quickly scrambled to his feet and hit a running ligerbomb The Frenchie went to a second rope and attempted a flying dropkick, but Rain rolled out of the way and got to his feet. Screaming, Rain charged and speared the much smaller Frenchie, knocking him backward against the turnbuckle and sending him splattering to the mat. Rain went for a cover, but then seemed to spot something in the crowd. He got to his feet and looked intensely out at the audience, and thunder rolled and crashed in the upper levels of the arena. He rubbed his eyes, and went back for the Frenchie, but when he made a loose cover the Frenchie quickly reversed into an inside cradle. The three count was made, and the Frenchie quickly scrambled out of the ring and ran for the backstage area, Wilson and Primetime behind him. Inside the ring Rain sat quietly in the center and looked quietly out at the crowd, deep in thought. Warfare went to commercial, leaving viewers to ponder this development... Result: Flying Frenchie by Pinfall Jack's back. The commercial turned to a man sitting at a desk before a computer. The man looked worn and tired, and he slumped forward in his desk. His head appeared to be some kind of giant white ball with a hat. The phone rang and the white ball headed man picked it up. Death vs. Courage Death came to the ring with Stone, but not with the other Dark Riders War and Plague. Matt Loco ran up to him and asked for a comment, but Death shoved him out of the way, sending him toppling over the metal guard rail. Courage came out onto the entrance ramp with Jake and at once started mocking the Result: Death by Pinfall Tag Team Situation The commissioner came to the ring with a notebook in his hand and a briefcase in the other, and addressed the crowd. Chris Finn entered the ring with a microphone and asked what this impromptu visit was for. The commissioner opened the briefcase and removed the two fWo World Tag Team titles. He began to speak, when suddenly the music of Doomsday sounded, and VL and Stevie walked onto the ramp. Stevie pointed to the commissioner and yelled, "You've got something of ours boss man... and if you think that beating that Slugger gave you was bad, just try us. VL will punch your teeth out and ram 'em up your ass. Now give us our belts..." Before the commissioner could answer, the music of the Hollywood Blondes began to play, and Chris Universal and Lance Star came out of the crowd. Universal held a microphone and said, "Your belts gay man? You're not fooling anyone with your sexual act, and you're not fooling anyone by saying that you deserve the belts. If anyone deserves those belts it's me... I mean us... so hand those belts over right now." The commissioner looked flatly at all four men, and then said the one word that would get him out of the jam. "Security?" fWo Security hit the scene, and as the two teams were restrained the commissioner continued to speak. "I'm here to announce one thing to all of you... starting at the next Sunday Slaughter we are going to kick off a sixteen team tag team tournament to determine the undisputed Tag Team champions... and you can choose to compete..." The commissioner looked down at the two teams being held back by security. "...or you can simply forfeit your places right now. The choice is yours... gentlemen." VL spit at the commissioner, who wiped his face with the back of one hand. "... and Ladies." The commissioner walked to the backstage area with the titles in hand as Warfare went to another commercial break... Fallen vs. Job Van Dam The crowd milled around the arena until Matt Panzer suddenly announced, "Ladies and gentlemen... introducing at this time a star from EXTREME CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING..." The crowd looked over to the ring with slight interest and Panzer continued, "...or a reasonable facsimile. Ladies and Gentlemen... coming to the ring at this time Mr. Wednesday Night... Job Van Dam!" Mike Heftel came onto the entrance ramp wearing a tie die wrestling outfit, and carried with him a microphone. Addressing the crowd Van Dam said, "Folks, when you look into this ring, maybe you see a glassy-eyed slacker with a bad case of the munchies, but you're not seeing everything! I, Job Van Dam, am the driving force of the fWo! I am "Mr. Wednesday Night"! I am "The Quarter-Hour of Power"! I am the "Whole F'N Segment", Job Van Dam. Now bring out my next victim, and I'll show you what an egotistical basta...er, excellent wrestler I am!" Job Van Dam walked to the ring amidst confused and booing fans, but no opponent showed. "What's the matter? Doesn't anyone have the guts to face the most powerful drawing power in the wrestling world?" Suddenly the lights dimmed and a large masked man walked onto the entrance ramp. In both hands he held dog leashes, with each attached to Fallen and Poison. Poison struggled at hers, and the large masked man jerked up on the chain, choking the young woman. The masked man grunted, and released Fallen, who at once snapped to life and ran down the ramp at Job Van Dam. Fallen slid into the ring and began to rip at Van Dam, punching, beating, and biting the "Whole F'N Segment". Van Dam fought back, suplexing Fallen over and getting some distance. Job Van Dam hit a few flying kicks, finally turning the tide on the match. Fallen struggled back, but Van Dam suplexed him to the mat. Job Van Dam then climbed to the top rope and flew off of it, but missed Fallen by a good distance. Fallen sold the move anyway, and Van Dam dropped the leg, but wound up hitting Fallen in the groin. Fallen grunted in anger as Van Dam continued to blow spots, and finally grabbed the leg of Van Dam and punched at the knee, dropping him. Fallen backed up as Van Dam obtained a chair. Job Van Dan tossed a chair at Fallen, but Fallen failed to catch it and it collided with his skull. Fallen looked at Van Dam with annoyed confusion, and decided to just punch him to the mat. Fallen began to beat and batter Van Dam, opening him up and causing him to bleed all over the mat. Van Dam was beaten senseless by Fallen, and finally was covered. Fallen got the three count and rolled out of the ring, but the large masked man shook his head and pointed back at the ring. Fallen snapped to life again, re-entering the ring and mauling Heftel more. Fallen grabbed Heftel's leg and drug it over to the apron, and began battering it with a chair. Fallen screamed and hit himself with the chair as well, and was finally drug off by fWo security. fWo Medical staff came to the ring to tend to Mike Heftel, and carried him away on a stretcher, bleeding and in bad shape... Result: Fallen by Pinfall NXS makes their mark James Armstrong suddenly burst into a panic as he sent the cameras to the backstage area. fWo security was on hand as Scott Slugger was found laying face down on the concrete in a pool of his own blood. Medical staff tended to Scott Slugger, who had been obviously beaten and jumped by a group of people. As security began to speculate as to who would do such a thing, RottDogg, Judge Steel and Markus Cairn entered the scene and shoved the medical staff out of the way. The three began to mock Scott Slugger, slapping his head and asking him if he had a "boo boo". Dogg turned to the cameras and began to speak with a smirk on his face. "Hey fWo fans out there in TV land... my name is the D-O-dubba-G and the Rott is hot and on top. I punked out this little bitch along with NXS because he doesn't deserve to step into the ring with super heavyweights like the Cairn and Steel... speaking of which..." Dogg moved aside so Judge Steel could get in front of the camera, and Steel carried with him the same smirk that RottDogg had. "Oh all my little bitches out there in television land... you've got to learn who the men are, and you damn sure that it's us. You want to step up and slap some smack down on our hood? Bitches, you gots another think coming, 'cause we three are the biggest and baddest you bitches done ever see. I'm called steel because of what I got baby... so move aside and get in line to climb this big mountain!" Finally Cairn moved into view of the camera and said simply, "Slugger... this Friday one of us will be wearing your belt... but that's not your big problem. You see... win if you can... survive if we let you. And bitch, we won't. NXS is here again, and here to stay." The three left the scene as fWo medical staff desperately tended to the injured fWo World Heavyweight champion... Mad Trucker (c) vs. Primetime "Bawidtaba" brings out Primetime for our next match, closely shadowed by the Boston Strangler. Primetime is proudly brandishing his fWo Cruiserweight title, and telling all of the fans at ringside that he's going to bring home the Internet Championship tonight. A loud "TOOT!" is heard over the sound system, introducing the current Internet Champion, the Mad Trucker. He has both arms lifted high in the air, one displaying the Internet Title, and the other making a horn-pulling motion. Trucker enters the ring, and the two square off. PT starts off with chops to the Inet champ, but they're not very effective. Not discouraged, he hits the ropes and hits the Trucker with a flying shoulder block, but it barely moves him. Primetime gets back to his feet, dusts himself off, and goes to the ropes again. Mad Trucker tries to lariat him down, but Primetime baseball slides underneath the larger man, springs to his feet and dropkicks the back of his knees. With the Trucker down, Primetime applies a Paris Crab, which the Trucker easily powers out of. Back to his feet, the Trucker whips Primetime to the ropes and nails him with a big forearm. PT kips up, and Trucker forearms him again. Trucker goes for an elbowdrop, but PT rolls out of the way, only to roll back for a lateral press. It just barely gets the one, as Mad Trucker bench presses Primetime off of him and through the ropes to the floor. As the ref checks on PT on the outside, the Strangler enters the ring behind Mad Trucker and connects with a reverse neckbreaker. He rolls out just as PT heads to the top and hits a twisting senton. He tries another cover, this time getting a two, but Mad Trucker just isn't hurt enough. Up again, and this time a scoop and a slam from Trucker puts him back in control. Trucker drops a knee to Primetime's skull and covers him for two. He lifts the French superstar back up and locks him in a bear hug. The crowd, enthralled with the psychology, sets all of their signs down and remain absolutely silent, showing respect for the skill of these two men. Primetime breaks the hold with an ear bite, and the crowd resumes its rowdy nature. Trucker whips PT to the ropes again, and this time Primetime comes off the ropes with and Asai moonsault. The Trucker catches him, however, and lands a shoulderbreaker. The Trucker into the ropes, and a huge splash just about flattens Primetime, but he squirms out of the cover at two. Primetime goes to the corner to recover, as Trucker and the ref discuss whether to eat bratwurst before or after hotcakes. Just then, the ring apron rises and Ice steps out from underneath the ring. He clocks Primetime with a lead pipe, and as PT stumbles out of the corner Mad Trucker breaks off his conversation to hit a modified 18-Wheeler for the pinfall and title retention. Ice asks for a microphone, but before he can get it the Strangler attacks him. They trade blows, neither getting the advantage, while Trucker heads to the back. Primetime follows suit while the Strangler takes a slight advantage in the brawl, but is met on the entrance ramp by Nikki and Speed Demon, who get their kicks in while the Strangler is distracted. Mitch Wilson runs in from the back to chase off the two, and calls the Strangler over. Ice and the Strangler break apart and stare each other down, before the Strangler reluctantly makes his way to the downed PT and assists him to the back. The cameras fade... as Warfare goes off the air for another week and fans are reminded to tune in to fWo Fallout... Result: Mad Trucker by Pinfall
