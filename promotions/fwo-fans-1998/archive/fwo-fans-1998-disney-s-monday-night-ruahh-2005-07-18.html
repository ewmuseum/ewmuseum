Introduction It was summer hot, fall humid. Dog days of July, and people walked wearily from bus stations to their points of destination. Time ticked, slow and plodding, and even the excitement that was in the air was a hot, edgy excitement. John had been a member of the fWo camera crew for six years. He was retiring from it tomorrow, something that the wife had been asking him to do for years. The pay was awful, the travel worse... but it was for neither of these reasons that he was quitting. Something had changed. Not in the arena, but out of it. John felt it in the air, and it made him nervous. He was far from a brilliant man, but his gut had steered him right since he was a child. His parents had called it a super instinct, and that was exactly what it was. On pure instinct he had dodged trouble in college, found the love of his life, and avoided countless mishaps that had claimed the futures of his once friends. Now, the same instinct told him that his time with the fWo needed to come to an end. He watched as fans walked into the arena, and couldn't shake the feeling. It had gone wrong somehow. All wrong.
