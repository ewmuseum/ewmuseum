The Mecca landed back-first on the canvas, painfully, while the Codemaster rolled to the ropes and used them to get to his feet. When he got there... his right arm pulled down the left strap. The left arm pulled down the right strap. Game on. The Codemaster measured Alex, looking for the victory, in the only manner he knew how. When Alex got up, the Codemaster spun him around and grasped his head. MAGNUM FANG! The move that won him the title. The move that won him countless other victories. It was all over, as the Codemaster went for the cover. One. TWO. THREENO. Shoulder! The Codemaster, sitting up, could only glare at the referee in shock. He looked up at the lights, wondering if gravity was working, or if the second law of thermodynamics had just been violated... or if some jackass really did put in a Aerith Is Not Dead code in Final Fantasy VII. He told the referee, "That was THREE, YOU ZEBRA COLOURED SPAWN CAMPER." But the referee held up two fingers, prompting the Codemaster to get to his feet. He stalked the referee, slapping his left hand onto the palm of his right hand three times, before holding up three fingers. But the referee saw his three fingers and raised him two. The Codemaster kicked the bottom rope, frustrated that he couldn't yet put away young Alex Creed. He called Alex to get to his feet, and when he wasn't getting up so soon, that's when the Codemaster hunched over and tried to pick him up. Inside cradle. One. TWO. THREE! Fans were in shock. So were the announcers. And ESPECIALLY was the Codemaster, who was trying to register in his brain what the hell just happened. He held his shoulder in pain from the match, but when he heard the following announcement, he was livid. "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match, and NEW fWo Internet Champion... ALEX CREED!" Instantenously, the pain in the Codemaster's shoulder faded, as "Step Up" hit again. He popped up to his feet, and yelled at the referee. It was clear that the referee thought he didn't make a mistake. The Codemaster, hands on his hips, turned away, and contemplated what just happened. All of his hard work was gone in a month, based on one quick pin. He turned back to face the referee... before killing him DEAD with the MAGNUM FANG. "Step Up" stopped. The Japanese fans in attendance were stunned, and were even more stunned as the Codemaster chased away the timekeeper, who was about to come into the ring and give Alex Creed his newly won title. Nobody knew what the Codemaster was going through. He was starting to show a side of himself that he had never shown before, and it was evident as soon as he went to a corner and pulled out the Skull Hammer that the Codemaster wasn't messing around any more. He was done playing the easy mode. And he was done playing the hard mode. He was done playing, in general. SMACK! This was the sound of the Codemaster, with the Hammer in both hands, clocking Alex in the forehead with the handle of the hammer as the new champion rose to his feet. A few referees came from the back to try and stop this madness, but as soon as the first one came in, that first referee lived to regret it. CRACK! A shot with the Skull Hammer was directed towards the leg of that referee, which caused the other referees to suddenly stop. Then the Codemaster simply picked up Alex Creed, before unceremoniously tossing him over the top rope and to the outside, near the referees. The timekeeper was about to hand Alex the title, when the Codemaster slid to the outside and blocked his path. A punch in the timekeeper's gut allowed the Codemaster to take the Internet title from him. He looked at it for a while, while the referees tried to get near him and convince him to stop his madness. That only caused the Blackest Brother in the Hyrule Kingdom to simply FLING the Internet title into the face of one of the referees, knocking him down. CRUNCH! That was the sound of the Codemaster kicking loose one of the steel steps. Setting aside his Skull Hammer for a moment, he picked up that staircase and simply THREW it in the direction of the American broadcasting table, nearly collapsing it and sending the announcers scattering. Then he picked up the Skull Hammer and hit the timekeeper's table like it was an oversized golf ball being hit with a hammerheaded golf club, tossing it into the crowd. The angry former Internet champion did the same to the steel steps, though they didn't move quite as far, before he repeatedly SMASHED the Spanish table with multiple hits with the Skull Hammer until it was a pile of wood. A golf club-like swing was then directed at one of the monitors. CRASH! The hammer's head lingered there briefly, before the Codemaster simply kicked it off and it went sailing over his head and into the crowd, where it landed with a harsh sound. He returned to the ring, Hammer in tow, whereupon he noticed that the referee that he had struck in the leg with his Hammer was wobbling around, trying to get to his feet. The Codemaster dropped his Hammer. MAGNUM FANG. The referee was down. The Codemaster picked his Hammer back up, put a foot on his chest, and raised his arms in the air in "triumph". If he had turned to his right at any time, he would've seen Alex Creed walking away... holding the newly acquired Internet Title high over his head. But he didn't. He was looking at the camera with eyes that wrought such intensity and frustration as to make a brave man weep. The Codemaster lost his title and his pride... and the last straw might have just broken over the Dodongo's back as a result. And therefore, the last scene of Warfare was a scene of chaos. Result: Alex Creed via pinfall; NEW Internet Champion
