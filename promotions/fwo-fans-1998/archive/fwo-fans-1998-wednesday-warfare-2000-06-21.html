Wednesday Warfare, 21 June 2000

21 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Something was in the air. Whatever it was, wasn't very good. This was afterall Michigan, and anyone who's been to Michigan knows it sucks. Especially Detroit. Why anyone would want to live there is beyond comprehension. The air was smoggy, and as Neo sat outside, he wondered who would have the guts to show up tonight. The dampness of the air led one to believe danger was near. But it was more than the air itself, or what was in the air. The Joe Louis Arena had an aura about it. Tonight... wasn't a good aura, or a bad aura. It was a strange & mysterious aura... and as the night began one could determine that very odd things were about to occur. Courage vs. Rudo Warfare heads into the arena to the sounds of "Dizz Knee Land" by Dada. Fireworks shoot off all over the place, and the announcers welcome everyone at home to the June 21 edition of Warfare. This brings out member of the Lone Gunmen, and a former cruiserweight champion, Rudo. This upcoming match will be Rudo's first match since losing to Matt Douglas at Cyberslam. Rudo enters the ring, and stretches, when "Surfacing" by Slipknot starts up. This brings out the man who just recently lost his fWo International Title to Mitch Wilson, one third of the Future Foundation, Courage. The arrogant superstar struts to the ring, showing no signs of losing any confidence with the loss to Wilson. Courage steps into the ring, and the bell rings. Immediately, Courage takes the quick offensive, with a barrage of fists to the Lone Gunman. Quick whip into the ropes, and Rudo ducks a clothesline. Rudo comes back, looking for a flying body-press, but Courage ducks down, and Rudo goes flying over him, hitting the mat. Courage jumps up, and takes Rudo over in a german suplex. He bridges, but Rudo kicks out of it at two. Both men reach a vertical base at the same time, and Courage drops Rudo with a violent clothesline that knocks Rudo down to the mat, hard. Courage lifts Rudo up by the hair, and into a Death Valley Driver. Courage connects, and covers again. However, Rudo manages to get a shoulder up at two, once again. Courage lifts him up, and goes for an irish whip. Rudo reverses, and when Courage comes back, looks for a leapfrog, but he jumps too early, so Courage is able to catch him, and bring him down with a very good spinebuster manuever. Courage pops up, and decides to go to the top rope. He connects with a huge guillotine legdrop, and covers. One, two, but Rudo gets out of it. Courage brings Rudo up again. He lifts him on his shoulders, but Rudo reverses that into a pinning attempt. However, he didn't hook it well enough, and Courage gets out at one. Rudo, showing obvious signs of ring rust, tries to jump on Courage for a huracanrana, but Courage holds Rudo up there, and brings him down with a powerbomb. Courage, simply laughing at Rudo's display of ring rust, but he doesn't realize that Rudo is really trying hard in this contest. Courage launches Rudo into the ropes, and when he comes back, he walks right into a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Courage, lets out a "C'mon, baby!, before running over Rudo. He runs back the other way, facing the ropes, and comes off with the Courage Required. The three count is nothing but a formailty, and Courage picks up an easy win over the former cruiserweight champion, Rudo. Result: Courage by pinfall Ratings aren't everything, they're the only thing. Chris Finn & James Armstrong walked around backstage with grins on their faces. With the looks they were giving people it could be easy to ascertain that were both in very good moods. This could mean that the ratings went up, or at least they didn't drop. One could easily note that we would likely find out later in the show. Finn & Armstrong exchanged greetings with several of the wrestlers as they journeyed further into the depths of the arena. Finn & Armstrong stopped to talk with several wrestlers about their roles in the coming weeks. Notably present were Havok, Black Quicksilver, The Guano Apes, Matt Douglas and several others. As Finn & Armstrong continued to converse with the wrestlers Warfare went back to the ring for it's next match. Ric Chronos & Fallen (c) vs. Damnation Warfare returned inside the arena, and the fans waited with anticipation the arrival of the number one contendes to the tag team titles, Damnation. As the announcer introduced them and their theme echoed throughout the arena, several fans held up signs that praised the duo who represented the Underground. "FOSTERS = AUSTRALIAN FOR FEAR!" was proudly written on one sign, while "LAMONT IS THE TRUE HARDCORE BEER DRINKING FELINE" read another. Their song continued to play, but there were no signs of Damnation for one reason or another. The referee instructed the announcer to introduce Ric Chronos & Fallen, and he did. "Christian Says" by Tones on Tail begins to play, and the fans begin to cheer, waiting for their arrival with a large degree of eagerness. Fireworks shoot off, thus exciting the fans even more, but the champions never arrive. Since neither team have made an appearance, the referee calls the match a double count-out. As the announcer makes this announcement to the fans, the fans boo loudly, before Warfare heads to a commercial break. Result: Double-Countout Crusader (c) vs. Havok Result: Crusader by pinfall What the... Finn & Armstrong turned a corner, and Finn wasn't pleased by what he saw. In his office sat four men in the form of Ric Chronos, Fallen, Hellrazor & Fear. A variety of different brand beer cans were scattered throughout the place, and as Finn entered, he gave the four men a disgusted look. "You guys... WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU GUYS DOING IN MY OFFICE?!" Finn shout at them with anger and bitterness. Chronos looked at Finn... "What? I'm not ducking frunk... dog gamnit... you're... you're out of your mind... Chad... what's your name again... Finn?" Finn looked at Chronos as if he was about two seconds away from being released from his fWo contract. "And look at you two!" Hellrazor burped. "Don't put me in any more exploding barbed wire kingpin nail fighting death fire matches! Please! I just want to drink... sleep... and be merry... or even better... sleep with Mary! Yeah!" Still angry, Finn pointed at Lamont, who was sitting in a corner vomitting all over a suitcase. "Jesus Christ! Your damned CAT is even drunk! He just upchucked beer and tender vittles all over my five hundred dollar pristine leather attache case!" Hellrazor pointed at Finn... and almost forgot what he was going to say... before putting himself together, or at least, trying to. "No way... man... I... told youse... we don't... d... drink... on the job... Lamont jusht had a hairball... or shomethin'." Chronos tried to help the matter. "Indeed... I'm... all for putting away a few beers... or... a couple hundred after the show... but these... allega... allug... allig... claims... of a drinking contest are..." Chronos paused to take a very long burp, before finishing. "... unfounded." Finn shook his fist, before telling the men to clean up and get out. "Just... just get out. All of you. And while you're at it, go get ready. You guys have a match. You're going to wrestle each other whether you like it or not. And that's that!" Hellrazor burped, and Chronos farted. The two men laughed, while Finn grabbed Lamont and gently tossed him outside the door. "Out. NOW!" Neo vs. Whoever had the guts to be kicked in the nuts like RottDog. Another night, another fight. Neo leaned against the brick wall of the Joe Lewis arena. He face was battered and marred with the dark stain of dried blood. His body was bent as he could not stand upright without severe pain moving up through his groin and into his chest. Neo suspected he had a broken rib, or at the very least a bruised one. But his eyes were alive... they burned with a fire that Neo found was turning him into a new man. A stronger man. But now he was alone again, outside with the dull Detroit air burning in his lungs and the vast majority of the fWo roster inside the arena taking part in their little wrestling matches. Hardcore? Please. Hardcore was outside in the street... hardcore was Neo. But then footsteps came, and Neo smiled with anticipation. Wrestling... tournaments... none of them mattered now. The only thing that mattered was the fight. Steel,Cairn, and Rottdogg walked around the corner of the building. Steel carried a chain, Cairn held in his hands a crowbar, and Rottdogg limped behind them. He wore a neck brace, and his arm was bandaged in a loose sling. "That's... cough... him... that's the bastard." Rottdogg smiled, and the facial expression caused enough pain that he clutched at his throat, "You're going to pay you little bitch... you're going to pay hard." Neo's face showed amused determination. "You don't get it boy, this isn't about me kicking your ass... even though it wasn't exactly a tough job... it's about changing things. It's about this soft world with all these people lulled to sleep under the spell of god damn hip hop music and Jerry Springer. People who aren't even alive, they are just sleepwalking from one pathetic crap moment to another. Right here, this is real. This is what I'm talking about, and if you three little freaks want a piece of me you're going to remember who you took on." Cairn spat. "Shut up... you god damn little freak... this is wrestling. It's not boxing, not street fighting... it's wrestling. You went too far... and you're gonna pay for your shit." But Neo was finished listening, and he lunged forward and caught Steel in the jaw with a surprise sucker punch. Steel's jaw crunched and popped out of position as he dropped to his knees, the chain forgotten as it clattered on the ground. Neo picked it up as Cairn moved forward. Cairn kicked at his head, popping Neo's nose out of position and opening a torrent of blood that at once drenched the front of his shirt with the thick red liquid. Neo grunted and swung with the chain, his vision starting to waver as he caught Cairn across the face with the steel of the chain. Flesh tore under the movement, and Cairn stumbled backward with a yelp of pain. "You... you son of a bitch! You ruined my god damn face!" Neo ignored him and moved forward, wrapping the chain around Steel's neck and hauling backward with all of his weight. Neo punched forward at the back of Steel's head, the pressure causing his face to turn blue. Neo was weakening though, and the pounding in his ears was growing larger. He never heard Rottdogg come up from behind him, and swing with forgotten crowbar at Neo's skull. Metal connected with bone, and Neo slumped forward unconscious. He lay still as Rottdogg slowly helped Steel to his feet. Steel clutched at his neck where the chain had bruised the flesh beneath. "That... cough... god dammit! That bastard was trying to kill me!" Rottdogg pointed to the downed Neo. "No he wasn't... the crazy asshole wanted a fight. Well... let's give him one." They moved forward then, Cairn joining them as they slowly surrounded the unconscious Neo. Sounds of fists striking flesh could be heard from some distance away... the slap of fists into ever growing wet meat continued long into the night... Result: Obviously, not Neo... Primetime & Lance Star vs. Sid Snow & Total Eclipse As Warfare continued, 'I Know You Want Me' began to play and out walked Sid Snow & Total Eclipse, back in action for the first time in a couple of months. Eclipse had crumbs of taco shells and hamburger buns in one of the rolls of his stomach, and noticeably had a milkshake stain on his mask as he walked to the ring. Apparently, Eclipse had prepared for the match by eating... a lot... at Taco Bell & Burger King earlier this afternoon. Snow pranced to the ring as the fans applauded their entrance, before the arena lights switched off. "Last Resort" by Papa Roach echoed throughout the Joe Louis Arena, while the fWo Internet Champion Primetime & "The Future" Lance Star walked to the ring. Lance Star had a can of Sprite in his left hand, and after sipping a beverage, he spat the mist into the depths of the crowd. Wrestlers tend to do this often, and Lance thought since he was afterall a wrestler, it'd be cool if he did it too. Star didn't consider that the fans may not like this, and it showed as they shouted angry obscentities in his general direction. Star & PT rolled into the ring, and after some pre-match posing, they took to their respective corners. Star and Snow would start off first, while Eclipse took a seat at ringside, already winded from the long walk to the ring. Star and Snow locked up, and Snow took to the advantage with a side headlock. Star countered into a waistlock, before a quick roll-up attempt. Snow kicked out at two. At this time, four men made their appearance on the stage. Chronos, Fallen, Hellrazor & Fear were... there. Oddly enough, Fallen was dressed in Fear' wrestling gear, while Fear was dressed in Fallen'. Although a little tight, Fear couldn't notice, as he was obviously drunk. Chronos didn't even realize it was Fear, and not Fallen, and he held up his fist to signify some sort of kinship. Fear returned the favor, although he didn't know quite why. Suddenly, Hellrazor tripped and passed out on the ramp, while Fallen was signing autographs. After the show, a fan would later show that Fallen signed the autograph as "Rush Limbaugh". Back to the match, Eclipse was waddling his fat ass up the steps to try to make the tag to Snow, who had taken advantage of the match and had Star in prime position for Eclipse to drop a... something or other on him. Eclipse waddled in and dropped a big leg drop, but Star moved out of the way, and Eclipse crashed to the mat. Star tagged out to Primetime, and the two men bounced off the ropes, before returning for a double-sitting dropkick which nailed Eclipse in the face. He rolls of fat jiggled as he fell backwards towards the mat. Primetime took over and flew off with a frogsplash, but Eclipse kicked out at two. Primetime then hit an elbow, went for another cover, but Sid came in and broke up the pin. Finally, Star went up top and flew off with a moonsault, but Snow rolled Eclipse out of the way just in time. However, this meant Primetime would hit Snow instead, and he did just that. Primetime smacked into Snow, but the way he hit also injured him. PT clutched his ribs, while Snow clutched other parts of his body... so to speak. With PT down and out in the ring, Mitch Wilson stormed into the ring and brought Primetime up for the 'Headliner', while the referee had his back turned to the action. He tried to get Eclipse out of the ring, but his skinny frame was not powerful enough to. Wilson nailed the Headliner on Primetime, but as he turned around, he was met with a crescent kick from Lance Star, which sent Wilson flying out of the ring. Star then hit the Shooting Star on Sid Snow, planting him into the ring with authority. He then placed Primetime on Snow, before tapping the referee on the shoulder, and directing him back to the action. The referee counted it, and Primetime & Lance were victorious. As Lance was looking to help Primetime up, he noticed something on the Warfare screen. The Sprite logo - to be exact. Underneath it in text it said "Sprite sucks... and your mom does too!" This image then faded, and Wretch stood with his lackey Scot Hartt behind him, and Hartt' lackey - The Mutha Fuckin' Abusah Of Yo' Momma - standing next to him. Wretch smiled, before the image faded altogether, and Warfare proceeded to a commercial break with Star disgruntled in the ring. Result: Primetime & Star by pinfall Ric Chronos & Fallen (c) vs. Damnation Warfare returned to the arena, where the camera's picked up Fear waking up Hellrazor. Chronos & Fallen continued to talk with the fans, with Chronos undoubtedly telling stories about his pleasant experience at the Brian Pillman Memorial Show which occured last month. Chris Finn came out to the ring and instructed all four men to have their match, and the four men reluctantly (without their own intent to, mind you) headed to the ring... visably drunk off their asses. Hellrazor nearly fell down, but he was helped into the ring by Fear. Chronos & Fallen took to their corner, although Chronos couldn't understand why Fallen was wearing what Fear usally is attired in and why Fear is attired in what Fallen usually wears. Chronos laughed at this, before Hellrazor hauled off and hit Fear, and then tagged in Fallen. Fallen stumbled around, before charging at Hellrazor. Hellrazor moved, and Fallen fell over the top rope onto the floor. Hellrazor stumbled backwards, laughing at the amusing site, before nearly throwing up in a corner of a ring. Instead, he fell through the ropes to the floor too. Chronos & Fear shrugged and tried to help their partners up, to no avail. Instead, the referee counts both teams out. Chronos & Fear laugh at the bell sounding, not realizing that tomorrow morning all four men would have pounding headaches. And Lamont? Lamont was passed out in the dressing room, likely with a pounding headache of his own to wake up to in the morning. Result: Double Countout, again. Xavier (c) vs. The Black Panther Warfare returns to the sounds of the Fighters of Foo playing "My Hero", as the crowd jumps to their feet awaiting the arrival of the Black Panther. Shortly afterwards, Panther prowls onto the stage acknowledging the crowd. As he makes his way to the ring, the 6'10" touches hands with members of the crowd, and stops to take a photo with a youngster wearing a Black Panther mask. As he climbs the stairs to enter the ring, "Bleed" by Godhead starts to play, and out walks the fWo Hardcore Champion Xavier. Wearing sunglasses and a long leather jacket, the near 7 foot monster looks about at his surroundings, almost ignoring the boos as he makes his way to the ring. Instead of going into the ring, he lifts the apron to search for objects. Panther wastes no time to slide under the ring and thwart Xavier's actions by giving the champ an uppercut and a boot to the groin. Panther quickly takes advantage of his offense by grabbing Xavier and throwing him shoulder first into the steps. Looking under the ring to see what is there, Panther grabs a video camera. He positions it as if he's doing his own home movies by using Xavier as his target. Panther makes motions for Xavier to wave to the camera...but the champ is dazed. Panther gets upset with his subject not cooperating, and proceeds to smash the camera over Xavier's head, further knocking Xavier senseless. The front row crowd roaring its approval of the carnage, Panther poses quickly for someone who had the benefit of flash photography, then grabs Xavier and throws him in the ring. Taking his time, Panther finally gets in the ring only to find Xavier waiting, who from his knees throws some powdery substance in the face of Panther. Panther violently reacts to this action, and staggers backwards, falling on the ropes. Still a little shaken up by the previous attack, Xavier slowly gets to Panther and irish whips him into the ropes. Panther rebounds off the ropes to find himself almost ten feet in the air, thanks to a high backdrop from the champion. Finally able to remove the leather jacket, Xavier grabs what appears to be a lead pipe out of one of the pockets before dropping the jacket on the outside where a few fans attempt to steal it, only to have security leaves their tries in vain. With a swift stroke to the abdomen, Panther crumbles to the fetal position in the ring. Xavier takes the pipe and crashes it over Panther's lower back, returning the Panther to a painful position on the mat, back arched in the air. Xavier lifts Panther's head up, and with a snarl about his face, jams the pipe across Panther's neck, and pulls towards him...choking the former World Champion. Panther now very slow to move around slumps back to the ground, as Xavier drops the pipe nearby, stands and look around soaking in the boos from the crowd. Finally having enough of the crowd, Xavier returns to Panther by picking him up off the mat, then proceeding to execute a double-underhook suplex. Xavier goes for the pin, only to get a 2 count. As Xavier glares at the ref, Panther appears to be able to see again. Panther gets up at the same time as Xavier, and gets the upperhand with a rake to the eyes, followed by a clothesline. Picking the Hardcore Champion up, Panther irish whips Xavier into the ropes, and proceeds to thoroughly powerslam him upon returning. Panther pins, hooking the leg, and barely gets a 2 count. Quickly taking initiative again, Panther positions Xavier for a fisherman's suplex...once again only getting a 2 count. A little frustrated, Panther kicks Xavier a few times, picks him up and proceeds to throw him HARD into the turnbuckle, following up with a BIG splash...where Xavier dances outward with a big dazed look on his face, and pulls a classic Ric Flair face plant. Panther senses the momentum, and begins to pick up Xavier. Suddenly, the crowd erupts...with various chants of "POWERBOMB!" scattered throughout the Joe Louis Arena as Erik Kelly runs down to ringside...with a cinder block in hand. Panther is oblivious to this, as he is positioning himself for his finisher, The Killer Instinct. When Kelly starts to enter the ring, the crowd gets louder. Panther stops, allowing Xavier to stand upright. As Panther turns around, Kelly is on the charge, cinder block aiming in the direction of someone's head...who's is uncertain. However, Xavier doesn't intend to find out if it is meant for him, as he pushes Panther hard towards Kelly, his head squarely hitting the cinder block, and the rest of him crashing to the mat, unconscious. Xavier however takes the low road and leaves the ring. Kelly glares at the Hardcore Champ, drops the cinder block, and moves his hands around his waist in the traditional "I want your belt and your ass" fashion. Xavier starts to head up the ramp staring back at Kelly. Xavier decides to grab his crotch to acknowledge Kelly, but unfortunately didn't pay attention to Robert Jensen who clotheslines Xavier with a chair. The ref says "Fuck this match, it's over!", and starts to leave the ring. Kelly watches with an intense look about his face, as he stands on the second turnbuckle and rips off his "Eradication is Upon You" t-shirt, throwing it into the crowd. His immense upper body flexes as he points at Xavier, then to the crowd. Erik Kelly is now out of the ring, cinder block raised over his head to the acknowledgement of the crowd. Xavier glares up the ramp at his opposition at Critical Condition, a snarl forming on his lips as Warfare heads to a commercial. Result: No Contest Announcement about Critical Condition Machina walked to the ring, a smile worn on his face, a heavy jacket worn, and a microphone dangling from one hand. He walked steadily to the ring, and slapped the occasional hand as he made his way to the ring. He was alone this night, with none of the usual surrounding that occurred with his partners the Lone Gunmen. But Machina looked confidant, and as he slid into the ring he smiled at the cheers that went up from the crowd. Pacing now, Machina strode around the inside of the ring, waiting for just the right moment to begin. "Lance... Star. Funny... I could have sworn that you signed with some small promotion out of Atlanta, but here you are getting in my way andthinking that you can outsmart... me. Well Mr. Star...you certainly did book this match here tonight, but I'm thinking I still came out just fine tonight. After all, this so called... Future Foundation just wasted valuable ring time... valuable endurance... valuable training... with three men who aren't even in this Next Level tournament. Let's face it Mr. Star... I'm going to win that tournament, and there is nothing that is going to stop me. Not you, not your little friends, not anyone else that the bookers here try to throw in my way. When I returned to the fWo, I did it for one big reason. The money. But the second next biggest reason was to one day be announced as the greatest champion in the fWo... and hold that most valuable of prizes that this promotion has to offer. That's right... I'm talking about the International Championship. Now maybe... maybe... this tournament isn't for that prize, but it's my stepping stone to the top. If you think that a little mystery opponent is going to stop me... well... you're dumber than I thought. For you see... there is more than one way to influence the bookers here... and there is more things that a wrestler can accomplish than simple match making. For it occurs to me that in a few days... well, the fWo is holding one of them Pay Per View like things. What is that name again? Critical Condition? Well... you're going to discover that name is more appropriate than you think Mr. Star. Because you see, I just happened to have that night off... and you know how much television sucks on Saturday night. So I figured... why not volunteer my services?" Machina slid the jacket off his shoulders, and smiled. Beneath the jacket was a referee's shirt. The crowd gasped as Machina kept talking. "So I figured... why not become... a referee for the night? After all, if the Lone Gunmen are about anything, it's being fair. So I asked the bookers to grant me the opportunity of being the special guest referee for a very important match. Mike Bear against Scott Collins. But then I realized, 'What am I thinking? Why referee a match I could care less about when I could instead screw up your chances at walking into the Next Level tournament?' So I asked... and lo and behold, I got what I wanted. Lance Star... Wretch... say hello to your referee at Critical Condition." Machina dropped the microphone and smiled a wide smile filled with the smug confidence of a man who has gotten what he wants. His moment of glory held for only a moment however, as Lance Star himself walked out onto the top of the entrance ramp. Star wasn't smiling as he raised the microphone to his lips. "Masked freak... you think you can get in the middle of a score that I've got to settle with Wretch? Boy, you're not going to even make it to Critical Condition after tonight." Machina simply laughed at this, and picked back up the fallen microphone. "Yeah, yeah. Listen Starry... why don't you just trot your little mystery man out here so we can get this done with, okay? You're taking up valuable air time." But now a smile did cross over Lance Star's face, and he spoke more clearly, and with more confidence. "Mystery opponent? I'm afraid that's not quite correct freak. It's not... plural enough." Machina vs. The Guano Apes "Plural? What in the hell?" Machina looked surprised, and then the music of the Guano Apes started up. "Hey... just wait a minute!" But Machina was cut off by Lance Star, who now stood at the top of the entrance ramp with Sphere and Jackhammer behind him. "Now, now... when I told the booking committee that you were trying to get rid of all the ring rust you had acquired during your... ah... vacation... and freak, you do look like you've put on some weight. You've got them weird man tits thing going you're so chubby. Well, they agreed with me that what better to get you into shape than a handicap match... and what better than that but a no-DQ handicap match? Enjoy pal... oh, and by the way... the Guano Apes here are a little... upset... that your goofs ruined their title shot last week. Have fun, guys!" But as Lance Star retreated from the entrance ramp the Apes were already running to the ring. Machina dropped the microphone, "Oh... shit." Jackhammer hit the ring first, and wasted no time going for a quick clothesline. Machina ducked under, and turned around to stick his tongue out and wiggle his fingers next to his ears. But from behind Sphere grabbed Machina's legs and drug him out of the ring. Machina squawked and attempted to crawl into the audience, but Sphere grabbed him and held him fast. He signaled up to Jackhammer inside the ring, and the big man climbed up onto the turnbuckle. With a mighty bellow Jackhammer dove from the top turnbuckle to the outside, connecting with the trapped Machina with a mighty body splash. Machina lay still on the outside, and Jackhammer and Sphere considered their next move. Jackhammer shrugged, and the two walked to the guard rail and began signing autographs while they waited for Machina to recover. The masked man showed signs of life some five minutes later, and Sphere tugged on Jackhammer's arm to get his attention. "Hey... hey, he's finally getting up!" Jackhammer ignored him as he flirted with the girl that was watching the show from the second row. "Hey baby... want to go to the back and learn how to apply a figure four leg lock? Or maybe... a body press?" Sphere tried again to get Jackhammer's attention, but when he turned back to look at Machina's progress he gasped in surprise. "Hey... where is he?" Sphere looked right and left for signs of the masked man, but no signs of him could be found. Sphere and Jackhammer walked back to the ring and looked under the mat. Nothing. They looked under the announcer's table. Nothing. They looked in the first few rows... and again nothing. Jackhammer and Sphere shrugged, and slid into the ring to pose. The crowds cheered the tag team, but one member in the crowd wasn't cheering at all. Machina sat in the row just beyond the first few rows with a disguise he always kept hidden in his tights. Sunglasses and a fake beard. He crept closer to ringside, taking great pains to slide over the guard rail undetected. He crawled toward the ring, and as he moved he drug behind him a steel chair... and a candy bar he had bought from one of the vendors in the crowd for later. He waited until Sphere was close to the ropes, and then swung upward with the chair, connecting with his head. Sphere toppled backward, and Jackhammer looked at him with a confused expression on his face. Machina climbed into the ring and ripped off his disguise. Jackhammer gasped. Machina laughed. The Jackhammer punched Machina in the head, and the masked wrestler crumpled in a heap on the mat. Jackhammer looked at the unconscious wrestler for a moment, shrugged, and placed a single foot on his chest. The referee made the count, and the Guano Apes were declared the winners. Jackhammer helped Sphere up, and the two walked toward the back as fWo officials worked to revive the leader of the Gunmen. This appeared to be a difficult task, so Wednesday Warfare wisely went to a commercial. Result: Guano Apes by pinfall Crucified A prone body hung to a cross, which was placed up against a wall. Robert Jensen was the person in question, and although he wasn't liked in the lockerroom, he didn't have a lot of enemies either. You could assume that Xavier was the one who did it, but Xavier had already left the arena after his match, since there was no reason for him to stay any longer. Jensen was unconscious, and as a secretary walked by the room, she hollered for help. Medical personnel arrived, and helped untie Jensen, before trying to revive him. All was not well as Warfare went to a set of commercials.. The Boston Strangler vs. Ice The Warfare cameras cut to the entrance ramp, and boos and "Bastard" by Motley Crue fill up the atmosphere as Ice makes his way down to ringside. He sneers and intimidates the crowd as he walks down to ringside, minus Mitch Wilson. He looks toward the entrance ramp as O' Fortuna begins to play. The fans stand on their feet and cheer hard for the fWo Heavyweight Champion, the Boston Strangler. Paul Adams comes out and acknowledges the fans' reaction, and holds the fans' belt in the air, so everyone can see. He slides in the ring, and, before the bell can ring, Ice goes to work on Strangler with hard kicks to the abdomen. He picks Strangler up and whips him into the ropes. Ice goes for a clothesline, but BS ducks it, and turns around. He finds Ice has a slower reaction time, and he gears up. Ice turns around, and Strangler decks him in the left temple. Ice hits the mat, and Strangler goes for a quick cover on the stunned wrestler. At one-and-a-half, Ice comes to and kicks out. Strangler picks up Ice and tries to manuever him into a suplex. Strangler goes into this corner face first. Again, he comes out wobbly. Ice sees this and grabs the waist of the Strangler. He lifts him up, and nails him with a back-bodydrop. Another cover, and two and a half second later, Strangler kicks out. Ice looks at the ref again; his patience is wearing thin. He picks the Strangler up, and whips him into the ropes once again. This time, Ice charges hard at him and nails him along the ropes with a knee to the gut. Ice lifts the Strangler up, and drives him to the mat with a gutwrench powerbomb. Ice goes for the cover, and holds the leg (not to mention using his legs on the ropes for leverage). At two-and-three-quarters, the ref sees the legs and discontinues to the count. Ice scowls at the ref. One more mishap by him, and Ice would have him looking eye-to-eye with his intestines. Ice grabs the Strangler and throws him into the ropes. Strangler comes back, and Ice grabs him around the neck. Time for the Attitude Adjustment. However, Ice seems to have lost his balance. This is due to the fact that Strangler, being the champ that he is, has found a counter; a Russian legsweep. He whips Ice off of his feet. This has bought him some time, although a limited amount. Ice gets to his feet and charges the Strangler. He goes for a right; it's blocked. He goes for another right; blocked again. He goes for a headbutt. Strangler moves, and in the process, wraps his arm around the neck of Ice. Strangler DDTs Ice, sending him to the mat. Strangler looks down; now is the time to go for the Massacre. He drops to one knee. He looks at the ref, making sure he's in the right position. However, he's not. Strangler looks around, and then looks behind him. He sees Mitch Wilson, who has come down to ringside, is up on the apron. Strangler, disgusted at this turn of events, walks over to the ref. He looks down for a second to get his balance. When he does, Wilson tosses something into the ring; it's a crowbar. Luckily for Wilson, Strangler does not see this, as it has flown over his head and lands next to Ice. Strangler walks over to the ref and Wilson, and starts to argue with Wilson to get down off the apron. Wilson says he'll move, but puts his arm around the ref for some "words of advice". Strangler remembers his mission, and turns around to finish off Ice. Then...everything goes pitch black. No, not in the arena...but in the head of the Strangler. Ice wails on him with that crowbar. Immediately, blood starts to fill the wound, and then spread over his face. Ice tosses the crowbar away, and grabs the neck of the Strangler. Attitude Adjustment coming...and Ice nails it. Ice picks him up once more. He grabs Strangler's neck, and hits it again. Ice covers the fallen champion. Wilson allows for the ref to turn around. The ref sees the blood, and looks at Ice. Reluctantly, he counts the pin, and Ice is the winner. Wilson hops into the ring, and he and Ice celebrate as if they had just won the fWo title. They then spit on the Boston Strangler, and hop out of the ring. Medical personnel, complete with stretcher, rush to the ring to attend to the Boston Strangler, as Warfare heads toward a commercial break. Result: Ice by pinfall Ratings, baby, very ratings. The office of Finn & Armstrong was finally clean, although the smell of vomit, beer and tender vittles reeked in the air. Armstrong appeared hungry, and had mentioned earlier he was, but wasn't about to eat now with that foul smell residing in the office. Finn looked at Armstrong and grinned, but then looked at the monitor and saw that his biggest star - The Boston Strangler - was hurt. "This isn't good." Finn noted, and Armstrong concurred. Finn frowned, before sending an assistant to go check on him. "Hope he's alright... anyways... Slaughter' ratings didn't drop from the previous week, so that's good." Armstrong nodded. "I still think that the key to our ratings going back up is the return of the Frenchie, Finn." Finn rolled his eyes. "You heard him. Now is not the time. As much as I'd like to have him back on the shows, now is not the time. Anyways... I'm hoping this tournament of ours goes over as smoothly as possible. It seems to have gotten people talking about us again, and not in the 'Hey look, their champion is a celebrity who sucks ass' kind of way, but a 'hey, this tournament should be cool' kind of way, you know? Ah well... some people say using wrestling to draw viewers isn't creative... but who the hell are they to say that? We are a wrestling promotion, aren't we?" As Finn said this, he was reminded of Lamont throwing up in his attache case earlier. Finn sighed, before continuing. "This tournament... I think if everything plays out properly... this could really start something for us. Ignite even more interest in the fWo." Armstrong agreed, but still... "Ah, it might, but a whole tournament? Why not just bring back Pierre, damnit." Finn rolled his eyes one more, before we headed back to the ring for our final match of the evening, and quite possibly also Scott Collins' second to last match of his career. Scott Slugger vs. Eron The Relentless "Physical" By Nine Inch Nails kicks off the main event on Warfare, and the fans chant some rather interesting things at Eron as he walks down the ramp.
