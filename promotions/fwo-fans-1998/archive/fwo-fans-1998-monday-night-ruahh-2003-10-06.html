Introduction The Wrestler of the Year Tournament continues as the First Round is completed... Ratings The lights dimmed, and the arena was lit up with images of massive explosions on the Fantron. Destruction. Mayhem. Carnage. All of it, embodied in one man. His anthem - "Walk" by Pantera. Kodiak Vic Creed stepped out from beneath the Fantron, illuminated by flames and mushroom clouds. Like him or hate him, most people choosing the latter, Vic Creed's swath of mayhem couldn't be ignored. He walked to the ring with the swagger of a man whose confidence had come at the cost of others. Even in the darkened arena, he wore his trademark sunglasses. People couldn't see his eyes, and many wouldn't want to. If the eyes were the windows to the soul, then Creed's eyes were best left covered. Man or Woman, Adult or Child. None of it mattered to the Main Man. TheJuggernaut UNcaring UNbeatable UNstoppable Creed didn't wait for the booing to subside as the house lights came up. He simply started speaking, quite loudly, over the noise. "Last Thursday marked an evening of great significance in our business. Seems like our competition pulled in the lowest ratings they've had in a long, long time. Meanwhile, the fWo put up tremendous numbers as usual. I wonder why that was? What happened last Thursday that was so damn special?" Vic slowly paced around the ring with the World Title over his shoulder. For a moment he seemed lost in thought. "Was it the touching memorial that opened the show?" The crowd cheered, recalling and respecting the superb career of Eli Flair. Creed's expression showed he didn't share the fan's view. "Nah, I doubt it. I'd consider it an option if it was for someone significant, but after all it was Eli Flair we're talking about". It wasn't so much boos that followed Creed's comment. It was more like general loudness, hoping to drown his voice. They didn't want to hear anything Vic Creed had to say on the subject, but as long as he had the mic they didn't have much of a choice. "Hey, don't get me wrong. The guy had a great run. Thanks to me, he got to go out on the top of the card rather than the nickle and dimer he was before he crossed the Main Man's path." The crowd's volume toned down a notch as they tried to decide if Creed was somehow paying Flair a compliment. Vic tried to reassure them. " I even took one of those black armbands they were giving out. I think I still have it too." An "Eli! Eli!" chant started high up in the arena, but became quiet as Kodiak Vic Creed reached down the front of his jeans. He fished around before slowly pulling his hand out, clutching the black memorial armband. "Yeah, there it is. I used it to soak up ny nutsweat while I was working out earlier today. Still a little ripe by the smell of it." So much for quiet. The fans were hot at Creed's blatant disrespect, which mattered to Creed almost as much as the price of tea in China judging by the bored look on his face. "Eli left to spend time with his family. More importantly, he left because he knew I was right. I told him a long time ago to do just what he ended up doing. If he would have listened to me from the beginning, he could have saved himself a few ugly beatings along the way. He's a slow learner, but he finally got the message. Better late than never I guess. If that Memorial was for the passing of Eli Flair, consider this my way of pissing on the grave." Vic tossed the armband out of the ring, well short of the fans in the front row who stretched to reach for it. Vic stood in the center of the ring. "So if that wasn't it, what could it be?" He thoughtful expression twisted into a cocky smirk. "I've got it. Not once, but twice, the sheep - just like you meatsacks - tuned in to see YOUR fWo World Champion do what he does best - kick the living Hell out of people. Boo all you want, but people can't help but watch me do what I do. It's like turning on the news and watching a tornado rip through a trailer park. It's destructive, it's horrifying, and it's something you just can't get yourself to turn away from." "First, they watched me manhandle the resident insect Spyder in what had to be the most anticipated Wrestler of the Year match in the entire tournament. Personally I can't see why. To the uneducated, it may have looked like I came up short in that match. But really, do I have to win some tournament to be considered Wrestler of the Year?" He jerked his thumb toward the World Title resting over his shoulder. "I've got this, which means I'm the best. Not just in the fWo, but in the business. Period. Plus, I've held the belt for most of the year so far, and nobody's come close to taking it off me. That carries a lot more weight than winning some single elimination tournament. So best of luck to whatever waste of skin ends up winning the whole thing. Enjoy your trophy, because the only gold that matters around here is still right where it belongs... around my waist." "After I finished that warm-up match with Bug Boy, I had to face the former CWL World Champion, Superstar Vince Jacobs, in a match refereed by none other than Brand Frontier." The fans finally had something positive to react to, and react they did at the mere reference to The Paragon. "Now I know Brand can count to three. After all, he's heard that count a few times when he's faced me. But the sweetest thing about winning that match was watching Brand practically swallow his tongue when he had to award me the victory." Creed chuckled. "Now that was sweet. He figured he could screw me over, but once again the Main Man got the last laugh." "All in all, it was a great night for me. Not only did I beat SVJ, not only did I one-up Brand Frontier, but I also gave Vinny Mac one helluva black eye in the ratings. Tonight's going to be no different. I'm going to come out and dominate, Juggernaut-style." For the first time since stepping through the curtains, Kodiak Vic Creed held up the World Title with one hand, letting the Gold dangle along his massive arm. "The belt showed up with me tonight, and it's leaving with me as well. This is my gold... this is my world... and everyone else is just living in it." Explosions fired on the Fantron. "Walk" cued up again, and the Main Man took his leave. In a matter of minutes, Creed had shown his utter lack of respect for anyone and everyone that was not him. He felt the entire population of the planet was beneath him. Would he be put in his place tonight? Everyone in attendance, and more than a few people in the back, hoped the answer to that would be a resounding yes. A battle of singles who normally maintain strong tag team alliances, this match would find Conor Fuse of the always strange and unusual Fuse Bros. taking on Carraba... a Survivor III allumni who wrestled as part of the EMO Kids. Carraba rolled Fuse into the ring, and the bell rang to signal the offical start of the match. Conor waits for him to rise, then hits a quick hiptoss, and a dropped elbow into the neck of the EMO Kids wrestler. Smiling, Conor struck a quick superhero-esc pose with one fist arcing high into the air. If a power-up was possible, surely now would be the moment that it would take effect. Fuse bounced off the mat, and as he rose he was nailed by a furious crossbody off the ropes by Carraba. It was over. 1... 2... 3. Carraba rose, pleased at himself and his performance. Fuse however, was wondering if a restart button was nearby... and what it would take to get it pressed. But restart or no, the match was over. Carraba had won... and would be advancing on.
