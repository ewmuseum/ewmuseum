Wednesday Warfare, 12 January 2000

12 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The crowd shuffled in their seats as they waited for Wednesday Warfare to take to the air. Suddenly the lights dimmed, and fireworks exploded through the arena. Large banners rolled down on either side of the WarfareScreen. They were black, and deep red writing burned the cloth with five letters. "FTfWo" Austin Davis walked out onto the entrance ramp, and the crowd at once began to boo. He was followed by Scott Slugger, and behind them the triad of Living, Hell, and Heaven. They walked to the ring amidst the boos and calls of the crowd, and close inspection saw that Austin Davis wore a tight, thin smile. He climbed into the ring and looked around himself with satisfaction and then raised the microphone to his mouth. "Fans of the fWo... behold your commissioner!" More boos followed, and Austin Davis laughed at them as he waited until he was ready to speak again. "They needed someone to save them... and who better to trust than the dead? Austin Davis was there for Finn during his moment of weakness, and now Dead is here for all of you. I watched like all of you in disgust as the owner of the very promotion you all claim to love betrayed all of you in his bid for power. I watched him rise... and I watched him fall. Chris Finn came to me needing someone to enforce the rules and bring order back to the fWo... well order and rules I shall bring. But they are my rules... and this is my order. The fWo is my plaything now... and each and every one of you will suffer beneath my command." The crowd booed, and now tossed garbage into the ring. Davis batted garbage aside, and continued. "Last week I took care of Spencer. I haven't forgotten the past... and your torment will continue and continue until I will it to end. But for tonight... well, tonight I have to book myself a card. And who will perform on my Wednesday Warfare?" Davis paused for dramatic effect, and then continued. "Primetime... you don't give a damn about me... well I don't give a damn about you. Tonight, you will meet another participant in a stairway to hell match here tonight. A steel chair will be hung high above the ring tonight, and you will climb up to it by the means if a ladder. Your opponent? Well, I see Wretch has a ladder match coming up at Invasion against Courage & Havok... and since Wretch is a god damn moron that should never have defeated me back at the first CyberSlam, and would need some help climbing a ladder, you guys can battle it out here tonight. And if you don't give it your all, if you don't beat the hell out of each other, I'll see to it that you will be fired right here tonight... trust me." The crowd booed but Davis went on, ignoring them. "A couple matches were already signed. Rudo and Collins can go ahead and kill each other for all I care. VL and Hellrazor was already filmed, so we can show that crap too. Hellrazor, you said I would be Dead... well I am Dead. I hope you had fun killing yourself. We'll let Lamont live... and I won't piss on your grave at the funeral out of the kindness of my heart. I also hear that Sid Snow has a little crush on Maliboo. Well I figure we can just let Sid come down here and sodomize any fool he wants, and if it's Maliboo then all the better, because he's got the dumbest god damn name I've ever heard. Chris Universal... to sum it up, I hate you. You're completely pathetic. So tonight, I say you will wrestle Cactus Jon... another piece of crap. This brings me to the World Champion of this so-called promotion... the Black Panther. Panther... you're a tall drink of water and nothing more. You need a spot on the card, so tonight in the main event, you'll team with the man that you'll be facing at Invasion for the fWo World Title, the Flying Frenchie. I know you two don't like each other, so that should make the match much more enjoyable to watch. We all know how much Frenchie matches tend to suck, so I'm sure you can carry him. Anyways, your opponents will be two men who hate each other but nobody gives a rats ass about it anyways, Havok and Courage. We'll see if you guys can keep from killing your teammates or your friends, seeing as how Frenchie and Courage have a relationship going on in the Foundation, and the Panther and Havok claim to be Millennium Men or some crap like that. Oh, since wrestling is all about gimmick matches these days, let's make this a gimmick match with a bunch of stipulations so people will stick around to watch this piece of crap match. Frenchie, if you're pinned by either Havok or Courage, your title shot will automatically be stripped from you and will be given to me. Panther, if you get pinned by either Havok or Courage, your title will be stripped from you and will be given to Perfect so he can enjoy a fifth reign as champion. Havok, if you are pinned by Frenchie or Panther, well, we all get to come down to the ring and kick your ass. Courage, if you are pinned by Frenchie or Panther, we all get to come down to the ring and kick your ass. Sound fair enough? No? Tough. Those are the rules. Obey them or else. If I have to fire each and every one of you... well who gives a damn." Austin Davis tossed the microphone into the crowd and stalked up the entrance ramp as the boos of the crowd escorted him to the back... Micky Maliboo vs. Sid Sid Snow made his way down to the ring to little or now reaction, depending on how well your hearing is. But, soon the methodical beat of "Another One Bites the Dust" was thumping throughout the arena, and the crowd popped as Micky Maliboo, accompinied by Vixon, made his way to the ring. The bell rang, and both men locked up, testing each other. Maliboo wrenched back on the arm Sid Snow, who waw angered by this and layed into Maliboo with a vicious slap that stunned Micky. Stepping back, Maliboo frowned as he looked at Snow. The two locked up again with Micky getting the first move off by whipping Snow to the ropes. As Snow rebounded, Micky bounced off his own ropes and made for Sid, but the Gunman lept frog over Maliboo. Each man returned, and Snow took Maliboo off his feet with a shoulderblock. Maliboo grunted in frustration and rolled to the outside where he began consulting with Vixon. After a few moments Maliboo climbed back into the ring and, once again, locked up with Snow. Micky gained the upperhand and sent Snow into the ropes as Vixon hopped up onto the opposite apron. As Snow rebounded Micky hit the mat, allowing Sid to jump over him and stop dead in his tracks to prevent himself from ramming into Vixon. Then, to Sid's surprise but the fans delight, Vixon slowly began to unzip her top.. while Maliboo crept in for the rollup. But, before Micky could get there, Snow shrugged and turned right into Micky. Checking Maliboo up and down, Snow decided that THIS was much rather what he wanted... but Maliboo took his momentary lapse of concentration for his advantage and began to wail away on Snow with lefts and rights. Maliboo whipped Snow into the ropes one more time and took him down o nthe way back with a flying elbowblock. Snow quickly got back to his feet, but was in turn whipped into the corner turnbuckles. Micky tried to follow up with a running splash, but as he did Sid dived out of the way, allowing Micky to crash headfirst into the top turnbuckle. Maliboo staggered back and right into a spinebuster slam by Snow, who immediately sprang to his feet to deliver the Snowjob. But, as he dropped down, Maliboo rolled out of the way, and Sid's head kissed the mat hard. Maliboo recovered himself as Snow slowly got back to his feet and scooped up his opponent, dropping him into the Mellon Collie. 1... 2... 3, and it was all over. Scott Collins vs. Rudo A large "L" appeared on the TravisTron, and soon the crowd were on their feet in markful appreciation as "Come With Me" played, signaling the arrival of the Lone Gunmen. Insano Rudo and his wife Brandi stepped out on the ramp, brandishing a deadly looking pair of plastic whiffle bats. The two jogged down the aisle and slid in the ring, only stopping to point out a "Funk Fears Rudo" sign. As the Gunmen posed and played to the fans "Burning" began, and soon Scott Collins had emerged on the entrance ramp to a mixed reaction, despite the much loved assortment of weapons he pushed in front of him in a wheelbarrow. Alongside him was Stone who still retained her somewhat tainted smirk of Dark Rider days. Before the two Riders progressed any further, though, Rudo grabbed the microphone and shouted, "Hey hey hey Collins! I was just doing some thinking before the match and, ya know buddy, I don't think that pimp... err.. woman of yours is any good for you." Collins' eyes steadied on Rudo who had slipped through the ropes and was now walking up the ramp. "So, what I did was go out to the best escort service in town and find you the hottest chick I good." The faces of Stone and Collins looked a bit perplexed, but all questions were answered as they turned around to see Sid Snow emerging from the entrance ramp with a sign that stated "Collins was the Sexy Death." Collins, perhaps caught by surprise, took a few steps back, walking directly into Brandi and Rudo who bulldozed the Rider's leader with a double clothesline. The two then began to viciously and unremorsefully beat on Scott with their whiffle bats as an angry Stone leapt at Snow, ripping at him with nails. Snow immediately dropped the charade and ran to the back where Stone seemed more than happy to give chase. The couple continued their assault, but despite the immense amount of punishment Collins was taking the former Death was able to make it to his feet and absorb each bat blow, one at a time. Both Gunmen seemed hesitant to continue their attack, as each looked at each other nervously. Finally, Brandi nodded and the two dropped their bats and made a move for another double clothesline, but Collins was one step ahead and blasted them both with a huge double lariat. Brandi dropped to the steel ramp, holding her head, while Rudo was staggered back. As Collins stalked Rudo, the Gunman continued to walk backwards before bumping into the ring apron. Collins sensed his chance and charged, but Rudo pulled himself up with the middle ring rope and gave Collins a boot square to the face. Collins staggered back, but by the time he had regained his wits Rudo had already pulled himself into the ring and had begun to showboat. Scott was enraged and quickly slid into the ring, where he once again began to resume stalking his prey. As Collins approached Rudo backed up into a nearby corner and raised his hands to shield his face, goading in Collins. With Scott taking the bait Insano Rudo flung out his leg and delivered a solid kick to the stomach of the former world champion. Collins was staggered, and another three or four kicks had the wind completely knocked out of him. Rudo propped himself onto the middle turnbuckle and signaled for an elbowdrop but took to long, allowing Collins to grab Rudo by the head and tights and throw him off the turnbuckle with an overhead toss. Rudo hit the mat with a loud thump, but Collins showed no mercy and followed up by kicking his opponent to the outside. As Insano staggered about on the floor Collins rolled out after him and selected a steel chair from the ringside scenery. Rudo turned to face Scott, but was met by a loud chairshot for this action. As Insano stumbled backwards some more, Scott wound up and took another shot, again solidly connecting with the skull of the Gunman. But, as Collins raised the chair above his head for a third time, Brandi came diving over the top rope with a suicida plancha... unfortunately for Brandi, Collins was about three feet to the right of where she landed. Scott looked back and shrugged before once again turning to Rudo, who snuck in a low blow to bide some time. Rudo then grabbed the head of the dazed Rider and smashed it against the ring apron a few times, causing Collins to slump to one knee. Collins tried to get back to his feet, but Rudo hopped onto the apron and came flying off with a nicely executed Asai moonsault. Both men collided and sailed back into the guardrail, where Rudo was the first to make it to his feet. Brandi was also on her feet by now, and both Gunmen began to give Collin's a taste of their respective boots. Rudo then tapped Brandi on the shoulder and pointed towards the TravisTron. As Brandi nodded, Rudo fled the scene, allowing Brandi to carry on the battle herself. Brandi pushed much larger Collins and rolled him into the ring, where she scooped him up and planted him with a slam near the corner. Brandi then waved a hand to the crowd and climbed up top for the coup de grace, the 450 Splash. But, as Brandi flipped, Collins pulled up both knees, introducing Brandi to a huge amount of pain in her abdominal area. Collins struggled to his feet and, ignoring Brandi, scanned the area for Rudo. Scott spotted him rolling his very own wheelbarrow of goodies towards the ring, and slid out to meet him. Rudo stopped rolling the barrow and began fishing through it for a weapon, but as he did Brandi came soaring through the air with a tope con hilo, intent on hitting Scott Collins. As Rudo dived out of the way, however, so did Collins, and Brandi ended up landing directly on top of the wheelbarrow, spilling the contents everywhere. Collins sadly shook his head at Brandi as he scooped up a mailbox from the wreckage of the wheelbarrow and made his way towards Rudo. Rudo dodged the first swing by Collins, allowing the mailbox to strike the entrance ramp, splintering parts off. Quick fans at home were able to catch a glimpse at the name plate under the address, which read "Spencer." Collins reared up the mailbox to swing again, but Rudo dived in and began laying into him with lefts and rights. Collins dropped the box and staggered back, tripping over a toaster that had been knocked out of the barrow by Brandi's dive. Rudo picked up the toaster and swung, cracking it against the side of Collins' head. Scott staggered back and collapsed on the guardrail, where Rudo proceeded to clock Collins upside the head three more times with the roaster. Collins looked almost completely out of it as Rudo drag him back into the ring and went for the cover. One, two... kickout! Rudo then signaled for the end and climbed to the top rope for the Frog Splash, but before the Gunmen could jump he accidentally tripped, perching himself atop the ringpost. Collins got to his feet and approached the prone Rudo, then climbed out to the ring apron. All questions regarding his motives for this were answered when Collins reached up and grabbed the back of Rudo's neck, then delivered a Death Penalty from the apron to the floor, bouncing the head of Rudo against the apron, but with his legs locked under the second turnbuckle Rudo was left sitting in a somewhat awkward position on the top. As the Matt Loco screamed something from the announcer's table about Rudo being dead, Collins climbed back into the ring grabbed his opponent by the head, intent on bringing him down for the easy pin and win. As he pulled Rudo off, though, Brandi reached under the bottom rope and pulled out Collins' leg from under him. Collins fell backward, and a barely conscious Rudo hooked the leg, getting a surprising and shocking 1, 2, 3. Brandi quickly slid into the ring and grabbed Rudo, dragging him to the outside as an enraged Scott Collins looked on. As the couple sped to the back, Collins shook his head while he exited the ring. Chris Universal vs. Cactus Jon On key with "Guerilla Radio", Chris Universal emerges onto the entrance ramp. With a smug look on his face he 'looks down his nose' at the ramp-side fans before descending the ramp and rolling into the ring. Once inside however, he is all business as he looks to rebound off of an upset loss at the hands of Living a few nights prior. Stretching inside and awaiting the arrival of his opponent, Universal turns his attention as "Bombtrack" begins. A trace of uneasiness can be seen across his face as Universal is somewhat out of his element in this hardcore rules, anything goes affair. Cactus Jon comes forth from the backstage area brandishing a wooden baseball bat, and equally as focused as he too looks to get back on the winning track. He strides towards the ring and right before reaching ringside, sprints towards the ring and dives under the bottom rope. Universal anticipates this and meets him, keeping him grounded with stomps to the back. Finally lifting Jon by his hair Universal turns him and in one fluid motion flings him though the ropes and to the arena floor below. Universal waits and then shoots off the opposite ropes and comes back, as Jon turns, and rams his feet into the chin of Jon, snapping his head back and knocking him to the ground once more with a baseball slide. Universal continues the assault by using the opposite ropes again and this time vaulting over the top rope and crashing down upon Jon's prostrated body. This maneuver seemingly takes more out of Universal than Jon, and after a short delay, Jon is the first one to his feet. Lifting Universal he whips him hard into the ring post. Universal drops to the ground clenching his shoulder that collided with the post. Jon lifts him once more and once more whips Universal; this time into the ring-side guardrails. Universal pounces on the bat, and begins to take vicious hacks at Jon, smacking Jon's left arm and side. With each blow comes a resounding 'thwack.' He drops the bat and wraps up Jon, but Jon kicks out at a questionable 2 count. Universal shakes his head at the referee's count, but stays on task and picks up the bat. Universal elevates Jon to his feet and begins to stab Jon in the gut with the short end of the stick. He raises the bat high above and brings it down over Cactus Jon's head with tremendous velocity, the lumber crackling and splintering into a million shards of wood. Jon rears back, a gash opened in his forehead, and a steady stream of blood beginning to gush out and mask his face. The Canadian hardcore legend stays true to his name however, and with a crazed look in his face lunges at a stunned Universal and tackles him. Coming down on top, Jon begins to grab Universal by the hair and pound his forehead into Universal's cranium with barbarous head-butts. Jon lifts Universal by his neck and alternates knees to Universal's now bloodied face. Universal topples backwards only to be met by a Cactus Jon clothesline that sends him over the top rope. Universal manages to grab the top rope as he goes over to hold himself up on the ring apron. He doesn't stay there long as Jon cuffs him on the top of the head and sends Universal helplessly soaring down onto the announcer's table ringside; the table does not give way and Universal rolls off of it to the floor. Jon slides under the ropes and to the outside where he lifts Universal and sets him up for a Powerslam. He executes it and heads towards the ringside official, effortlessly tossing him aside and folding the chair he had until recently sat on. Jon heads back to Universal poised to hammer in Universal's skull. He follows through with two powerful shots to the head in a row that leave Universal hanging by a thin thread in this one. He pitches the distorted chair aside and rolls a spent Universal into the ring. Lifting the Warfare tarp at ring-side, he hauls out a folded table. He slides it under the bottom rope and climbs into the ring after it. Setting it up diagonal to one corner, he sets Universal up for a Piledriver and carries out the maneuver with an authoritative Canadian Crusher. Universal's head bounces off the mat at an awkward angle and he falls limp. Cactus Jon drags Universal to one corner, and props him up on the turnbuckle before clambering up as well and preparing to further wreak havoc on Universal's already damaged body with a Superplex through the table. As he extends upwards, Universal gathers strength from somewhere deep inside and holds strong. Jon tries once more but still Universal provides enough counter pressure to prevent being tossed up and through the table. A frustrated Jon looks to try once more but Universal uses a free arm to punch Jon's stomach. Cactus Jon begins to wobble on the second rope and Universal takes advantage of this weakness and uses a hammerlock to the chest to send Jon backwards and crashing through the table, breaking it cleanly in two. Jon twists in pain, but Universal inflicts even more pain with a top rope elbow to the jaw. Still feeling the effects of the Canadian Crusher, Universal uses the last of his energy to bring a dazed Jon to his feet and hit a running Neckbreaker; the Cha-Ching that flattens Jon and allows Universal to lift one arm and bring it down upon a fallen Cactus Jon.
