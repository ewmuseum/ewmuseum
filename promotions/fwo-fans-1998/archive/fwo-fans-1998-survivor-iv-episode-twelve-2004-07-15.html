Introduction There are some things you should know, know in these days before everything would change. To be fair, it was the summer and most eyes were on other pursuits. Dog days of heat and light, and while there was much to be paying attention to, fact was... nobody was. This of course, was exactly what Brian Cage and Frank Smyth, along with their more recent partner Stevie Roberts, had hoped for. For Disney to be enamoured and ultimately bored with the wrestling business for just long enough to scam them out of promotion, money and ultimately much more. There was plenty of danger in waking the sleeping giant. They knew it, and they were counting on the giant to wake at just the right time. The rumors had been frequent, but seemed so far fetched that nobody believed that Brian Cage and Frank Smyth had formed a plan to use the Survivor contest to give a group of young wrestlers national attention, but had no intention of turning them over to the fWo. The rumors that certain copyrights for OCW had been purchased by Cage were dismissed. The talk of someone claiming to represent a new, independent wrestling promotion gathering small venues around the country were seen as fantiful at best. This then, was where things were... with rumors and speculation flying about this so-called new promotion... this OCW. Meanwhile, the giant slept, but it would not sleep for long.
