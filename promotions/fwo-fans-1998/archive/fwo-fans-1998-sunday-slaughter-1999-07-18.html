Sunday Slaughter, 18 July 1999

18 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Sunday Slaughter opened with the usual fireworks and explosives, most of which were lit by the fWo ring crew and not the audience. Cameras spiraled down to deep down in the backstage area of the arena, where the commissioner sat next to Chris Finn. "Well, just five days away now. Damn Davis... I can't believe what I'm about to do." Chris Finn nodded, and patted Travis on the back. "Well, I'm sure Briana will do just fine... she beat him on Friday right? And if she does it again, then he's gone! Out!" Travis looked at Chris Finn with a wry smile on his face. "Briana? Briana face Davis? Well, Briana did a great job to be certain, and I'm sure that something good will happen to her down the line... but this is too important, you know? Can you imagine if Davis were to win? He would own this company! Austin Davis would be in charge of everything... writing the matches, setting up the Pay-Per-Views... I can't give him any kind of edge. And, as good as Briana did, having her face Davis would give him that edge. He can study her moves, and know what he's in for. No, to take care of Davis I've got to surprise him... shock him... keep him off guard. That's why... well, as much as I hate to do it I have to rely on a man who hasn't been around in a long, long time. A man who is a proven champion... a man I certainly am no great friend of... a man who has beaten Davis before. But more importantly... a man who wants revenge." Chris Finn looked puzzled, and then understanding came over his face. "Travis... you don't mean... you can't mean... him?" Travis nodded, as footsteps grew near. Both men looked up, and shock passed over Chris Finn's face. On the commissioner's face was a different emotion... a mixture of anger and certainty. As the cameras focused on black boots and slowly moved up the mystery man's body nearing his face, feed turned to static, and the cameras went back to ringside... Stevie Roberts & VL (c) vs. Melissa Smalls & Apocolyptica When Melissa Smalls and Apocolyptica's names were called, only Smalls came down the entrance ramp. Asking for the microphone, Smalls told the crowd that due to Apocolyptica's accident on Friday, they would not be able to wrestle tonight, but hoped that Stevie and VL would be honorable enough to grant them another shot at the titles in a few weeks. Smalls then told the crowd that she was in search of whoever performed such a dastardly deed on her partner and friend, and would track them down no matter the cost... Result: Stevie and VL by forfeit A challenge... Unexpectedly, Jackhammer and Briana came to the ring and grabbed the microphone. Addressing the crowd, Jackhammer told them that after Friday he knew that drastic measures had to be taken to wake Black Quicksilver up, and thus he was challenging him to a tag match at Summer Slaughter. Black Quicksilver and Chameleon, against himself and his new friend Briana. The Chameleon and Black Quicksilver appeared on the entrance ramp, and the Chameleon quickly accepted the challenge. Darkness vs. Famine Famine made his way to the ring, booed by the crowd despite being gone from the fWo for some time. As Famine neared the ring the lights in the arena went out, and screams were heard through the audence. Darkness ran to the ring, and grabbed Famine before he had a chance to enter. He tossed Famine into the steel of the ring steps, and then delivered a wicked powerbomb which saw Famine landing back-first on the steel railing. Screaming in pain and begging for mercy, Famine attempted to crawl to safety... but Darkness rolled him into the ring and applied a cross arm-breaker, making Famine tap out in a rare submission style victory for Darkness. Walking backstage calmly as security and medical personel rushed the ring, Darkness said only, "You're ALL next." Result: Darkness by Submission The Surgeon tries to make things right... Backstage the Surgeon was seen lamenting his earlier mistake which appeared to have actually done something very unusual to the Mad Trucker and Flying Frenchie. Looking down at the strange genetic scrambler gun in his hands, the Surgeon knew that one blast should repair any damage he had done, and set off in search of the Trucker and the Frenchie. He spotted the Frenchie's dressing room, and from inside could hear loud arguments. Grinning to himself in satisfaction and mumbling to himself that "everything can be salvaged yet..." the Surgeon tossed open the door and fired a strange translucent goo inside, coating the room's occupants. Grinning as he hosed the room, the Surgeon's smile quickly turned to a look of dismay as he realized that he had just fired on Primetime, Mitch Wilson, Cookie, and Alice... and that the Trucker and Frenchie were nowhere to be found. Gasping in horror, the Surgeon stammered for a moment, and then simply dropped his weapon and fled. Inside the room the four occupants shook their heads as if to clear them, and began arguing again. Finally, Cookie exclaimed, "Enough arguing! You stupid Americans just can't understand anything!" Cookie and Alice stomped out of the room, with Alice wondering aloud where Nikki was. Primetime and Wilson exchanged glances and shrugged to each other. Primetime asked, "Want some pie bud?" Wilson nodded, and they left the room as well... Black Panther vs. New Ninja The New Ninja came to the ring after some time of being unseen in the fWo. The Ninja looked bigger and stronger, and marched to the ring with purpose and determination, slapping the hands of the fans and vaulting himself into the ring. The Black Panther came to the ring alone, and motioned at his waist that he would soon be champion. The Ninja attempted to turn the match into a submission-style contest, but the Panther stayed on top of the action with a series of technical take-downs followed by power moves and holds. The Black Panther finally locked in a neck hold, but the Ninja dropped to his knees, turning the hold into a jaw breaker. The New Ninja then tossed the Panther from the ring, and jumped onto the top turnbuckle for a plancha onto the outside. The move missed however, and the Panther rolled the dazed Ninja back into the ring. Grabbing the Ninja by the midsection, a quick suplex was executed, and a cover was attempted. The Panther got the three count, and yelled at the cameras that on Friday Universal was next... Result: Black Panther by Pinfall The mystery of John Skyler Two unknown fWo doctors consulted each other, both looking nervously over at Fallen, who was sleeping peacefully on a small cot set up in the corner of the room. "He doesn't know where he has been for the last three weeks. He's suffering from severe exposure, and there is still the matter of the blood coating his hands and arms... blood that isn't his. The blood type matches someone else we know though..." The second doctor shook his head nervously. "Do we know where Dina is? Can anyone get hold of her?" The first doctor shook his head sadly. "No. We've been trying to find her, but she appears to have vanished... or been hidden." The two doctors looked over at John Skyler once more, and now his eyes fluttered slightly as a low moan escaped his lips... Death (c) vs. Scott Hart Scott Hart came to the ring alone, and held up his arms for the crowd, who cheered him in kind. Next came Death, who came to the ring with the Dark Riders... Stone, Plague, War and Scott Taylor. Death quickly jumped on Hart, but Hart managed to flip Death to the outside of the ring, and attacked with a flying splash to the outside. Scott Hart then landed a flying elbow and drug Death back into the ring. As Scott Hart made the cover Death grabbed the leg of the ref and drug him to the ground before delivering a hard shot to the jaw of the ref. As the ref lay out cold, Death removed the turnbuckle and smashed Scott Hart's head into the steel, opening the forehead up. As the ref came to and signalled for the DQ, Deaht continued his attack until several security guards physically drug him off Hart. As Death was drug away, he yelled to the camera that every other superstar that was put against him would go to the morgue... and that the Dark Riders would make everyone pay... Result: Scott Hart by DQ Gloating An excited James Armstrong climbed into the ring to interview the fWo champion, Scott Slugger, who was flanked by the massive Ice. Slugger told the crowd that, "Last Friday I warned Ultra Violet and Cactus Jon that they should learn to obey their betters... Ice and I. As those two losers sit and home and nurse their wounds just remember who it was that put you in the condition you're in right now... me. This Friday, at Sunday Slaughter, only on Pay-Per-View, I'm going to finish the job that Ice started... namely, taking you two children to school. I can't blame you if you didn't bother to show up... because tell 'em what will happen to 'em if they do Ice." The microphone was handed to Ice who said, "It would be bad." Slugger grabbed the microphone back from Ice asking, "Now, I'm a little confused as to this whole good/bad thing..." Ice took back the microphone and told the crowd, but before he could go on young Kid Cool came out onto the entrance ramp holding a microphone of his own. He interupted the pair and said, "Hey! You two did something really wrong last Friday, and I'm here to collect! I want both of you... in the ring... against me, Kid Cool, this Wednesday on Warfare! I'm not afraid of either of you two... and I'll teach you both a lesson in respect!" Ice and Slugger looked at each other, and then began laughing hysterically before accepting this handicap match and walking back to the backstage area. Austin Davis vs. Crucifix The crash of music over the arena PA system signaled that Austin Davis was on his way to the ring. Davis failed to appear on the entrance ramp, but a roar of an engine was heard over the music. Davis emerged from the backstage entrance riding a large Harley, and drove it down to the ringside area, slamming it into the apron and skidding it out of control into the metal barrier as Davis leapt off and climbed into the ring. Davis flipped off the crowd, and told them that on Friday he was going to be "the boss", so get ready for hell. Crucifix emerged from the backstage area and began to walk to the ring, but strange storm clouds began to gather over his head immediately. Crucifx turned to look back up the ramp, and a tidal wave of water rushed down the ramp in his direction. From overhead, lightning stuck the ramp, and the storm raged. Crucifix stood his ground and looked up the ramp to spot Rain, standing amidst the clouds and thunder, the two joined in battle as Davis watched from the ring. As the cameras went off the air, Davis smirked, telling fans that he would show up on Warfare, and take care of the commissioner a few days early... Result: No Contest
