Introduction Previously... Last week Jon Crisp obtained the fWo World Heavyweight Championship after a shocking return... but will Crisp be able to hold onto the belt, or is another, even more powerful force waiting in the wings to hold the industry's top prize? Kick Off The arena was plunged into darkness, and then, for the first time in nearly a year, a familiar tune began to play over the arena loudspeakers. Ein. Zwei. Drei. Explosions. Rammstien. Blazing red lights and extreme fireworks exploded as one man, a man who in the last three weeks had been making his impact known once more in the fWo, strode onto the steel entrance ramp. The former fWo World Heavyweight Champion, the masked luchador and technical master... ...Spyder. Spyder extended his arms to either side, and ignored the fans who had at once began to boo, hiss, and scream in disgust. He laughed, dipping his hand into his tights and pulled out a microphone. He flicked it on with a quiet press of the button which was not heard over the shouts of the audence and spoke into it with the familiar, mocking, cocky tone that had returned to the fWo and already began to wear out its welcome. "Vuelvo de nuevo a la arena, vengo de nuevo abajo de la rampa de la entrada y las televisiones de Am Spyder vs. Lindsay Troy Greased and slicked down fine, groovy leather trim I like the way you hold the road, mama, it ain't no sin Trampled Underfoot. The lights blazed gold as smoke filled the top of the entrance ramp. With a determined look on her face and her arms outstretched for the crowd she came through the smoke. Lindsay Troy. Former fWo Hardcore Champion, and currently her eyes were set on taking down the man with the big mouth in the ring. For a moment the mouth in question hung open in surprise. Then he snapped it shut with a pop and smirked. He shook his head slowly as Lindsay Troy entered the ring, weaving under the ropes and standing mere feet from the masked luchador. Spyder slowly rose from the chair and pushed the gutair out of the ring, then moved the steel chair aside with one foot. He did all of this slowly, as if making a big show of his efforts. Finally, after this was done and the crowd had begun to boo and chant loudly, he turned to face Troy. "Muchacha... please. Now, perhaps you were not here back when I ran this promotion, but you need to understand something muy claramente. This sport? This is a man's sport muchacha. A year ago I came into this very ring, and I spanked little Aimz muchacha until her little behind matches that red hair of hers. Some say I turned her off of men after that night, my performance to the muchacha so powerful that it made her unable to be with any other gringo man. But no matter... the point is muchacha that I do not wrestle muchacha girls. Not tonight, not tomorrow, not next month. So unless muchacha, you are going to make me a taco salad and..." Spyder leered and slowly paced Troy, who stood still, an eyebrow raised, and slightly amused. Her hands were tight though, but this danger was lost on Spyder. "...well muchacha... perhaps I have another use for you back in my hotel, eh? So why don't you just slide out of this ring, get into my limo in the back and show me what you've got?" Troy smiled, and Spyder smiled back. Then Troy reared back and connected with Spyder's jaw with a loud and audible crack. Spyder stumbled backward as Troy grabbed the fallen microphone with something of satisfaction. "Ring the bell, I'm going to show this little fool what I've got." Spyder came off the ropes rubbing his jaw as the bell rang, his eyes narrowed. He spat on the ground and stomped a foot, then moved forward and delivered a heavy chop into Troy's chest. The impact made her stumble backward, and Spyder snorted with satisfaction. Then his eyes went wide. A now quite furious Troy charged at Spyder, but the masked man managed to duck out of the way at the last moment and pushed Lindsay Troy toward the ropes. Troy leapt up and sprung off the second rope, backflipping over Spyder. Spyder was caught completely unawares, and for the second moment of the night he found his mouth open but completely speachless. Spyder regained his composure and charged at Troy, who quickly leapfrogged over him and dropped down onto her back, throwing her feet in the air. Spyder came off the ropes again but stopped short, grabbing Troy by the feet and rolling her backward, causing her to come quickly to her feet. Spyder put Troy in a waistlock and ran her into the ropes, but the Queen of the Ring held on as Spyder flopped backward with a thud. Spyder quickly scrambled to his feet and managed to duck a jumping roundhouse kick and slid down with a grin for a leg sweep. But Troy jumped over Spyder's foot and timed her next move to catch Spyder as he came to his feet with a heavy clothesline. It dropped Spyder to the mat, and Troy went back into the ropes for another. She came forward, but this time the masked man ducked and caught Troy in waistlock. Now far more serious, threw her overhead, attempting a German suplex. But Troy landed on her feet and grabbed Spyder by the shoulder to whirl him around. Troy quickly grabbed Spyder in a waistlock and went for a German suplex of her own, but Spyder also managed to land on his feet. As he did he let out a laugh of victory... which Troy silenced with a hard punch to the face. Spyder stumbled backward, and fell out of the ring. Spitting on the floor, he pointed up at the ring and began to curse. But at just that moment noise erupted from the crowd and he turned to see the Flying Frenchie and Ric Chronos charging down the entrance ramp. "Hijo de una perra..." Spyder decided to completely forego the match and instead leapt into the crowd, shoving audence members aside as he ran for safety. The Flying Frenchie and Ric Chronos chased after him, but had a disadvantage as Spyder quickly weaved in and out of fans. He turned halfway up the stands and laughed, pointing at the ring. "Too slow, hommes! Too slow! But good timing, because I was just about to finish that muchacha off... some other time muchacha! Ha ha, some other time!" Inside the ring Lindsay Troy watched, hands on hips as Spyder vanished from sight. Beside her the referee made the count to ten, then raised her arm as the bell rang, signalling her the victor. She frowned, feeling little satisfaction in the victory. But there would be other days, and other matches... and as she raised her arms for the crowd she knew that sooner or later she would get Spyder back into the ring and finish things just as easily as she started them. James Renshaw vs. Powerhouse Coming back from commercial James Renshaw stood in his corner and watched the muscular masked man make his way to the ring. He knew this was the guy's try-out match but didn't know much else about him.. He did know that the guy's name used to be Strong Guy, but because of some pending court case Disney had him change his name, and his outfit, to Powerhouse. Listening to his intro music, a dangerously close rip-off of the Superman movie anthem, Renshaw squeezed the ring rope in frustration. Those Disney people were hovering around Powerhouse just before the match, swooning over him like he was Brad Pitt or something. The marketing potential for someone like Powerhouse was huge, especially with Disney's demographic. If he could make a good showing here, Powerhouse would mean big bucks to the corporation. But once again, James Renshaw was being left out in the cold. Well this marketing wet-dream wasn't going to get a free ride tonight, he decided. The referee called for the bell. Powerhouse cautiously moved to the middle of the ring and extended his hand to James Renshaw. Renshaw paused before he shook it, then sucker punched the masked man with a stiff left hand. He drove a knee into Powerhouse's stomach and sent him into the ropes. Renshaw stepped up and drove a shoulder into the big man, but Powerhouse didn't budge. "Do that again," Powerhouse said and pointed to the ropes. All pretence of friendliness was gone from the big man's voice. Renshaw, no small man himself, charged and shoulder blocked the big man a second time. Again, Powerhouse didn't budge. Renshaw went back to what did work, and that was a knee to the stomach. He grabbed Powerhouse by the wrist and whipped him into the turnbuckle. Rather, he attempted to. Powerhouse stood his ground and didn't move an inch. Renshaw yanked again, and still had no luck. Powerhouse yanked his arm back and clotheslined James Renshaw, spinning him in midair. "What the Hell?" James muttered as he shook his head. Powerhouse yanked him to his feet and sent him into the turnbuckle. Renshaw watched helplessly as the masked man charged at him and drove a huge reverse elbow into the side of his head. Powerhouse then effortlessly hip-tossed him three quarters of the way across the ring. The fans popped at the power display. Renshaw wasn't as thrilled as he stood up. He watched the masked man with a wary eye before they locked up in the center of the ring. James grabbed Powerhouse by the back of the mask and used it to move him into the corner. The referee called for the break, and Renshaw broke clean. But before the big man could step out of the corner, Renshaw wound up and chopped him across the chest, kneed him in the stomach, and punched him in the mouth. "How do you like that, Golden Boy?" Renshaw whispered before jabbing both thumbs through the eye holes in the mask. With the crowd booing and Powerhouse staggering blindly out of the corner, Renshaw clotheslined the masked man over the top rope. He followed behind and pulled Powerhouse to his feet by the mask. He twisted the mask, rendering Powerhouse completely unable to see. He was helpless as Renshaw bounced his head off the ring apron, kicked him in the stomach, and suplexed him gut-first across the ringside stairs. The referee continued to count until James rolled back into the ring. The fans were booing, and fans in the front row could see blood trickling from a nasty cut on Powerhouse's right arm. The masked man slowly rolled off the stairs and held his ribs as he made his way back into the ring. He slid under the bottom rope, and Renshaw commenced with the dance of the injured ribs. "There are no heroes anymore!" Renshaw announced to the crowd as he lifted Powerhouse limply to his knees. He ran off the ropes and charged at the masked man with a boot to the head. The foot caught Powerhouse in the face and snapped his head back. Renshaw leaned against the ropes and smirked at the crowd, proud of what he'd done. The crowd started to cheer. Renshaw looked down at the front row and told them "He's done." He turned around to admire his handiwork. Instead, he quickly noticed two things. The first was that Powerhouse was standing right behind him. The second was that Powerhouse's standing lariat, the Powerline, was very effective in rendering a man unconscious.
