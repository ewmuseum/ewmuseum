fWo — Card

Wednesday Warfare, 14 April 1999

14 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction LIVE from Fayetteville, North Carolina the fWo presented Wednesday Warfare in front of a capacity crowd. All eyes were in the rafters where a large sealed cage loomed high above the ring. Many fans had called the fWo hotline and knew Austin Davis had demanded the cage be placed there, but it was still unknown as to what the cage would be used for. Chris Finn and James Armstrong were on hand for announcing duties, and it was announced that backstage Stevie Roberts had been reunited with his Doomsday team members. Matt Loco was present, but Stevie refused to say why he was released, and mentioned only that he had to train for Friday and what has now become the most important match of his life. Briana (c) vs. Matthew Zhuk "Unforgettable" played throughout the arena as the challenger Matthew Zhuk made his way out to the ring. Zhuk got a mixture of positive and negative responses from the crowd, as he made his entrance. Briana came out looking like a woman on a mission with the Cruiserweight Title in her hand. Briana stormed into the ring and took control early on. She whipped Zhuk into the turnbuckles, Zhuk returned and caught a kick attempt by Briana, but Briana countered with a beautifully timed enziguirri. Briana whipped Zhuk into the ropes and followed it up with a dropkick, and then attempted a cover for a count of two. Briana brought Zhuk to his feet and nailed him with a bevy of punches, before whipping him to the ropes, and attempting a hurracarana. Zhuk countered into a powerbomb, and then took a few moments to recover from the onslaught by Briana. Zhuk grabbed Briana by her hair and whipped her into the ropes, and when she returned he followed it up with a hotshot... making sure her throat hit the top rope. Zhuk then maintained his attack; using a running powerslam from his arsenal of moves. Receiving only a two count, he nailed a quick DDT, before apparently getting ready for his finishing move, the "Here's looking at you, kid" superkick. As Briana got to her feet, she noticed Zhuk lunging towards with a foot of whipass ready to take her head off her shoulders. At the last moment, she ducked, and took Zhuk down with a schoolboy for a surprising three count. Briana made a quick exit after retaining the Cruiserweight Title in a evenly contested match-up, and as she got to the top of the entrance ramp VL appeared on the monitor taunting the champion... Result: Briana by Pinfall Backstage with Black Quicksilver Backstage, Black Quicksilver watched the match between Briana and Matthew Zhuk unfold, arguing with Stump, who from the conversation on Black Quicksilver's end seemed to be giving Black Quicksilver pointers. Black Quicksilver grew further and further annoyed, and finally placed Stump in a corner of the room where he told it to "think about what it had done". Black Quicksilver continued to rant into the empty locker room that he was going to show Stump that he was just as good as it was this Friday. The Stump had no response to this. Oracle vs. Chris Kidman Jr. Chris Kidman Jr. came to the ring with his other misfits, and circled ringside several times before entering, slapping hands with the fans and signalling to the fans holding Kidman signs in the crowd. As Kidman entered the ring the lights dimmed and Oracle came out onto the entrance ramp. Oracle carried with him his crystal ball, and let it roll it down the ramp to the ring. Oracle climbed into the ring slowly, and surveyed the scene before him. Kidman charged with a running dropkick, but Oracle easily ducked aside and Kidman was sent into the ropes. Kidman became tangled there, and Oracle stomped away at the helpless Kidman while the ref struggled to free him. Kidman finally got free, but Oracle powered him into the corner and suspended him on the turnbuckle in a tree of woe. Oracle stomped further, and then ran to the opposite end of the ring to charge Kidman. Kidman managed to roll free at the last moment, and Oracle was sent crashing into the turnbuckle. High in the rafters wolves called at this, and Kidman looked up into the rafters nervously. Oracle got to his feet, but was taken down again by a quick snap suplex by Kidman, who then picked Oracle up and executed the Kidman Driver in the center of the ring. Signalling to the crowd, Kidman went to the top rope and executed a perfect 450 splash onto Oracle. This was only able to get him a two count however, and while Kidman argued with the ref Oracle dropped to the outside of the ring. Oracle retrieved his crystal ball, and when Kidman ran to the outside to confront his opponent Oracle smashed the ball over the head of Kidman. Kidman dropped, but the ref saw this happen and called for the DQ. Oracle looked back out over the crowd and sneered, holding up the shattered remains of the crystal ball. Result: Chris Kidman Jr. by DQ Machina's search for intelligent life Outside the arena Machina was spotted talking to someone on a pay phone. Machina was growing annoyed as he asked for Fear's number. The operator seemed not to understand this, and continued to ask Machina who he wanted to speak to. When Machina insisted "Fear" again, the operator tried scaring Machina by shouting "boo" into the phone. This had the opposite effect, as Machina began to smash the phone into the wall of the phone booth. Machina was shouting that he was just looking for someone to kick Ultra Violet's ass on Friday when an fWo security guard arrived on the scene to inform Machina that he had called the police after seeing Machina's rampage. As Machina ran away from the scene, the security guard told Machina that he didn't need to worry anyway, because the New Ninja had been signed to face Ultra Violet on Friday. Machina didn't hear this as he jumped into his limo and asked the driver to take him to China. VL (c) vs. Cactus Jon Newly crowned Hardcore champion VL came to the ring carrying a chainsaw, a large sledgehammer and a Canadian flag. As she entered the ring she began to smash at the flag with the sledgehammer, causing very little damage to the cloth. Seeing this, Cactus Jon at once ran to the ring and began to trade hard blows with the champion. Jon finally grabbed the hair of VL and rammed her face-first into the mat. Screaming to the crowd that this would be what would happen to the Dark Riders on Friday, Jon continued his calculated attack. VL finally got back on her feet and delivered a quick standing kick to the champion, taking the air out of him and sending him to the mat. VL jumped onto Jon and applied a figure four leglock while straining to the side of the ring where the chainsaw lay. Cactus Jon broke the hold and slid outside of the ring, walking over to where a pile of steel chairs sat. Jon grabbed a chair and turned it on VL, who dropped the chainsaw onto and into the mat, causing severe damage. Jon continued to bash the steel chair over the head of VL repeatedly, until the lights went out. Jon looked up at the entrance ramp where War and Plague began to make their way to the ring. Cactus Jon smiled, and urged them on. Meanwhile, Ice exited the backstage area behind them, and charged the Dark Riders from behind. Ice beat both men to the ground, as Cactus Jon joined the attack. With this interference, the ref declaired the match a no contest, and as VL retrieved her title the Warfare Screen came to life, showing the Flying Frenchie standing outside the arena with a baseball bat. The Frenchie smiled, and announced that tonight would be another popular edition of "demolish VL's car" night. The camera then panned back to show the Frenchie standing next to VL's newly reparied Camaro. As VL watched, growing more and more enraged, the Flying Frenchie proceeded to demolish the car. Result: No Contest Slobberknocker Backstage While VL's car was being demolished cameras found fWo security racing backstage to the scene of the French Foundation locker room, where Wolf Fang and Primetime were locked in a heated brawl. As Jackhammer and Mitch Wilson looked on concerned Primetime and Wolf Fang traded hard punches, having to eventually be seperated by fWo officals. Standing in the background was new fWo star Nikki the Valley Girl, who looked innocently at the camera. Nikki told the camera crew that she just wanted to see if either of these two big men would be interested in taking her back to her hotel room after the show, and that for some strange reason they began fighting. Nikki then walked away from the scene, but first winked at Mitch Wilson while giving Jackhammer a light slap on the butt. Meanwhile fWo officals had Wolf Fang and Primetime seperated and were locking them into their own dressing rooms for their own safety. Scotty Cool vs. Samuel Smooth Samuel Smooth came to the ring with three attractive women in various states of undress, and Doomsday. Stevie Roberts trailed along behind the group, looking badly shaken and haggard. Stevie was mumbling something to the effect of "we will just give them what they want and everything will be fine..." As Samuel Smooth got into the ring and began yelling at the crowd to "shut the hell up" Scotty Cool made his way to the ring to a huge ovation from the crowd. Cool slapped hands with the fans and eyed the large Doomsday group nerviously as he stepped into the ring. Cool at once locked up with Smooth, and easily overpowered the smaller man to the mat. Cool whipped Smooth into the turnbuckle, then picked him up on the rebound and press-slammed him into the mat. Cool attempted a cover, but was only able to get a two-count. Cool wound his arm back as he prepared to give another punch to the Smooth one, but Judge Steel entered the ring and caught Cool's arm from behind. As Cool pushed Steel back to the outside, the Surgeon handed Smooth a steel insturment of some kind. When Cool returned his attention to Smooth, Smooth smacked him in the face with the object and quickly rolled him up for the pinfall victory. The rest of Doomsday entered the ring then, and rushed Cool. As they all began to attack Cool with kicks and stomps Ultra Violet and Stone ran to the ring armed with a chair and a large candy cane. This drove Doomsday away from the ring, and Stone exchanged heated words with Brandi. Result: Samuel Smooth by Pinfall Lucky 15 Announced Chris Finn entered the ring along with the Commissioner of the fWo, Travis Beaven. Both eyed the large steel cage suspended over the ring, and then addressed the crowd. Travis held a sealed envelope, and announced that inside were the 15 lucky entrants into the Battle Royal at CyberSlam, and that these 15 would be the last 15 wrestlers announced for the card. Travis then read the list, which contained these 15 wrestlers: 1. New Ninja 2. Mathew Zhuk 3. Ultra Violet 4. Cactus Jon 5. Rudo 6. Fallen 7. Chris Kidman Jr. 8. Mitch Wilson 9. Crucifix 10. Samuel Smooth 11. Plague 12. Briana 13. Wild Thing 14. Black Panther 15. Mystery Entrant With these names announced, Finn made his way back to the announcing table for the final match of the night, looking up again at the ominous sight of the suspended cage... Black Panther (c) vs. Crucifix Crucifix came to the ring first; and his ominous presence was felt early on as he stared down some fans at ringside. Crucifix stepped over the top with ease as he prepared for this all-important International Title match between he and The Black Panther. The Panther made his entrance to a nice response from the crowd, and he quickly entered the ring. Panther and Crucifix had a silent staredown in the middle of the ring as the bell was rung. Panther started things off with a right hand to Crucifix, which barely affected him. Crucifix responded with a punch of his own, and the two traded punches as the match progressed. Crucifix got the upperhand and sent the Panther flying up and over the top rope with a hiptoss, and then Crucifix came out and followed it up with a chokeslam on the ramp. Crucifix took the International Champion back into the ring, and continued to dominate the Panther. He whipped him to the ropes and damn near took his head off with a big boot. Crucifix went for a cover, but somehow the Panther was able to kick out. Suddenly, the Oracle made his way out to watch this match with a keen eye at ringside. As Crucifix complained about the slow count, Panther glanced outside to see the Oracle at ringside. The match progressed as Crucifix brought Panther to his feet, grabbed him by his neck, and motion for a chokeslam, but Panther blocked it, nailed him in the midsection with a kick, and then followed it up with a powerbomb. Panther continued his offense with a legdrop, and then whipped Crucifix into the turnbuckles. He followed it up with a huge clothesline, and several fists to the side of the head, before using all his strength and energy to lift Crucifix up onto the top turnbuckle, setting him for "the pounce". As Panther was ready, Oracle held his leg and Crucifix ended up landing on top of Panther. The referee saw this and immediately distracted Crucifix, before Oracle left up the ramp in quick fashion. Crucifix was very angry and had a staredown with the Panther, before chokeslamming the referee and exiting the ring. Panther shrugged it off and walked back to the locker room with the International Title still around his waist after a hard-fought battle. After this, we saw Chris Universal watching in the lockerroom area on a monitor; with interest AND the Internet Title in his possession... Result: Black Panther by DQ Davis Showdown With the final match of the evening completed, the fWo Commissioner Travis Beaven returned to the ring and began to speak, telling Davis that "we've complied with your request to have the cage built and hung above the crowd here tonight, now you comply with your end of the deal and bring me the fWo World Title". "Trust me" is heard throughout the arena, as Austin Davis came out to a huge ovation of boos from the crowd. Davis still had the World Title with him intact around his waist, even though he did not defeat Scott Slugger on this past Sunday Slaughter. Davis climbs into the ring with his mad at the world attitude always present. Davis grabs the microphone and looks up at the ominous cage hanging high above the ring, which he demanded to be built and present at Warfare, or he would not give the World Title back to Scott Slugger. Davis said: "First of all, I said I would give the World Title back to Scott Slugger, not you so you can go ahead and get the hell out of here if you'd like, I don't give a damn. Second of all, in order to give him back the World Title, he must be present, and obviously he's not here. So, Slugger... why don't you come on out here, and I'll give you the World Title... just like I gave you the victory on Sunday; because we all know you cannot defeat Austin Davis without help". A few moments passed by before "Back in Black" began to play, and out came the rightful fWo World Champion. Slugger came out looking rather angry, and walked to the ring quickly with the intentions of getting the fWo World Title back around his waist. Slugger climbed into the ring and Davis handed him the fWo World Title; like he said he would. Slugger simply smirked and turned to walk off, and as he did Davis stuck out the middle finger in his direction. Slugger climbed outside the ring and held the fWo World Title up for all to see, before receiving an ovation of cheers from the crowd. Slugger then put the World Title on his shoulder, and exited up the ramp. Davis then spoke some more: "Beaven, I said I wouldn't touch you and I'm keeping my damn word. I've got something else up my sleeve right now, and if you get injured I'm responsible, and then I'm suspended for one year. So, if you could be so kind to exit the damn ring ASAP, I'd be forever indebt, or some mushy shit like that". Beaven simply shrugged, and exited the ring up the ramp. Davis then demanded for the cage to be lowered, which took a minute or so, then he continued: "Thank you very much. Now, all of you may be wondering why this big damn cage is here. Simply put, in case you've been living under a rock the past couple of days, the fWo bookers have decided to put me and Cactus Jack in a closed-cage match - also known as a hell in a cell - to end it once and for all this Friday at the final Road to Cyberslam show. Well, if you know anything about me, you know that I'm a very impatient man. I want to have the match right this very minute, right here, right now on Wednesday Warfare, because I want the world to see the demise of Mick Foley on live national TV. So Jack, I know you're in the back, if you have any sack, come on out here and I'll kick your ass off the top of that cage back to Connecticut where you belong!" Davis waits a few moments as the crowd cheers wildly at the possibility of a match like this happening tonight. Finally, we hear "Do you buy what I'm selling?" and out comes Chris Universal with Davis' fWo Internet Title wrapped around his waist. The crowd explodes as Universal makes his way down the ramp. As he does, the mysterious purple masked man makes his way out from behind and attacks Universal. He and Universal trade punches as Davis looks on from the ring with a surprised look on his face. His attention is diverted by this; and he doesn't see Cactus Jack repel from high above the ring using a cord to the top of the cage. Jack quickly removes the cord and yells down toward Davis, who finally sees Jack. Davis exits the cage, grabs a chair and throws it up on top of the cage. Davis finally climbs up the cage, and reaches the top. Jack takes the chair and swings at Davis, but Davis ducks the attempt, and kicks Jack in the midsection. He and Jack begin wildly trading punches as Universal and the masked man fight down the ramp and into the ring. As Universal and the masked man brawl inside the ring, Jack & Davis fight on the cage above it. Davis & Jack fight towards the location of the broadcast tables, and Davis began to get the upper hand. As Davis grabs Cactus Jack by the shirt and begins to toss him over the edge of the cage the cameras go off the air as Warfare concludes...
