Introduction The fWo Survivor contest, through the request of organizers Frank Smyth and Brian Cage have taken over the Thursday timeslot... but on their first prime time night they have gotten less than favorable news. They will be wrestling... Disney's way. Charming? "Please tell me this is some kind of sick joke." A very unhappy looking Logic stood in the halls of the Walt Disney Sports Complex. The reason for his discomfort could be seen in the match assignment he had in his left hand, and the costume that he held in his right. He read over the assignment one more time, to make sure he wasn't hallucinating. "Sleeping Beauty Match, Doctor Curiosity versus Logic. The winner is the first man to kiss a sleeping princess who lies atop a ten foot platform. The Disney Corporation will be providing music and costuming for this match." Logic disgustedly tore the paper in half. "This is not pro wrestling! This is an eighth grade play!" He unfolded the costume, and immediately recognized it as being from the Disney classic Cinderella. They wanted him to dress up as Prince Charming. Logic was not Prince Charming. He was not a cariacture like the characters in Disney movies. He was a real person. A real person that the Disney Corporation wanted to prostitute his dignity for the sake of attracting a younger fan base. He was about five seconds away from tearing the outfit to shreds and taking out his anger on the nearest fWo or Disney employee he could find, before he took a deep breath and calmed down. Sure, the outfit was silly. It would be embarrassing to compete in front of people wearing it. The match itself was an embarrassment to professional wrestling. But that's what Smyth and Cage were trying to prevent. This contest's purpose was to put one over on the Disney Corporation. And equally importantly...the match was against Doctor Curiosity. The self-proclaimed genius that had been pestering Logic at every turn, week after week. This match, silly as it was, would allow him to settle the score. He would take care of Curiosity, kiss the princess, endure the embarassment, and head to the back. There, he could see which team would have to make the fifth elimination of the contest. For now, his ultimate goal in the contest was clear. He would go to the bathroom now and put on the Prince Charming outfit. He might not like it, but he would do it. Hopefully, he could last in the contest long enough to stand beside War, Famine, and even Pestilence if he could be convinced to do so. And at that moment, Logic would become Death. The death of the Disney Corporation in professional wrestling forever.
