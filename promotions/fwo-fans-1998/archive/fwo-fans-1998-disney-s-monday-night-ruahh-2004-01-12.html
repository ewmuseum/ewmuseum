Introduction The fWo begins 2004 in uncharted waters as, through a series of unfortunate events, the Walt Disney Corporation has seized control of the promotion for as long as the fWo's president and owner remains comatose. Now comes the time to adjust to their new arena, for the promotion will no longer tour, but will instead operate out of the Disney Orlando complex, and the fabled Milkhouse Arena. As always with change comes new faces, and new players in the game... Nemesis January 12th, 2004 Orlando, Florida 6:48 PM. "What the fuck?" Still unsure what was happening on RUAHH tonight, Ivy McGinnis was at the arena at 9:30 AM, on the dot. She contacted all of the wrestlers who wanted their matches booked for the evening, worked out a rough schedule for the night, and posted it on the Board. Basically, she was Silver Fox for the day. Get the show put together, get a rough schedule worked out, and figure out where they could insert the unexpected. This company meant the world to Ivy and she felt responsible for its continued success. She left the arena at three and returned a few minutes ago - showered, dressed, and with a fresh (so to speak) thermos of coffee. And the board has been completely rewritten. Not only that, but there appeared to be far too many people just milling about. Who are these people? "Hey!" said Ivy, as she grabbed a passing Kristen Jacobs by the arm, "What's going on?" Kristen rolled her eyes. "Disney staff." "Are you kidding me? What happened to the board?" "Disney staff." Ivy shook her head. "Where's Ted?" "No idea. Listen, Ivy... there's a bunch of people here that are apparently in charge... I think you need to talk to them." She looked over Ivy's shoulder. "And I have to run." "What? Why?" "MY new boss," replied Jacobs, "Talk to you later." Jacobs hurried along towards a brunette woman standing outside a door labeled "PRODUCTION." IVy's eyes narrowed. The way the woman stood. The tap-tap-tapping of her nails on the door. The way she was dressed, and the disdainful glare she shot Jacobs. Ivy looked at her and her nine years' experience boiled down into a burning pit of rage that could only understand one word. T E R I This woman reminded her of Teri Melton in every way. Teri Melton was a mainstay in the CSWA, who had - among other things - slept her way into a top level CSWA front office position, and had slept with Poison Ivy's then - boyfriend, Hornet, during a lapse in their relationship. While Ivy got her status, power, and respect through hard work and sleepless nights, Teri earned every bit of her own slice of the pie on her back. And something about this woman - the name on the door below 'PRODUCTION' read "Janet Blanchard" - that reminded her of the woman formerly known in the CSWA as 'Miss Hooters.' Ergo, she was very likely to find herself on the receiving end of Poison Ivy's Singapore Cane one day. For the moment, Ivy's attention turned back toward the training room. On a hunch, she peered inside to see Ted Tallison speaking with Matt Panzer - thankfully still employed. He looked like he had been drinking tonight, but was in control of himself sufficiently to the point of doing his job with the same competence he historically enjoyed. She waved at him and headed in his direction - they had a tribute to plan, a show to run, and a wrestling promotion to save... from a damned mouse. Bad Omen "Sorry, kid. fWo employees only." He hadn't had a televised match for weeks, but he showed up at the Sports Complex, willing to do anything, even if it was just another dark match. The young rookie turned around with his head hanging, and when he looked up, he spotted a figure that he was quite surprised to find standing there, watching him. "What...what do you want?" Meet Mark Lively Mark Lively got out of the cab and looked at the Milkhouse arena with only the slightest expression of distain. Ten Bell Salute Fans in the arena stood as the lights began to slowly dim. One by one, the majority of the fWo locker room walked slowly and solemnly down to the ring, gathering around the outside of it. Inside the ring, now illuminated by the only lights in the arena, were a single pair of wrestling boots, a luchador's mask and the ring attire of the legends who had passed away at Countdown. Most of the fans knew what this was about, for it had been in the news... in the papers... it seemed everyone knew about the tragic events that had taken place in New York. While there were some confused fans, obviously members of the audience who had come to the show as part of their Disney vacation package and had no real sense of what wrestling was about, they stood with the others and were silent in respect for the mourning that was taking place in front of them. The first bell of the ten bell salute sounded, echoing throughout the arena. Many eyes of the wrestlers around the ring were not dry, and some looked upward. None of them expected to be starting the year in such a fashion. The second bell rang, all silent in the arena. No sound, no movement. The third bell, and some of the workers around the ring wondered how many more of these there would be... how many more fallen comrades would be saluted in such a fashion. The forth bell, and still it was silent. The arena gleamed in the darkness, the moment held locked in time. The fifth bell. Many wrestlers closed their eyes, broken by the grief. The sixth. The seventh. The eighth... and then a new sound. The fWo FanTron flickered to life, and several of the wrestlers turned at the interruption. The audience turned as well, and the heavy rusting sounds of movement broke the silence in the arena. On the FanTron, a tiny light was flickering around, as if one of the members of the audience was using a lazerpen to trace an invisible picture. But then the glowing dot grew as it came closer. Then, with a bright sparkle of light and a flash, the dot zoomed to the foreground of the FanTron, and an animated Tinkerbell smiled and beamed to the audience. She glanced down at the silent audience and wrestlers, who were stunned by this bizarre invasion into their grief. Tinkerbell meanwhile smiled and preened to the audience, then flew around the screen in a quick whirl and pixie dust spread all around the screen. As the burst of light grew, fireworks exploded around the stage, and loud pounding music began to slam over the Milkhouse arena speakers. "Let's get the party started! Let's, let's... let's get the party started!" Through the fireworks on the stage came the characters, first Goofy, then Donald and Daisy, Aladdin, the Beast, Chip and Dale and more. They came down the ring ramp, dancing to the music and waving at the fans in the audience. Around the ring, the fWo roster looked at one another in stunned horror and disbelief as Buzz Lightyear began to fire off fWo and Disney t-shirts into the crowd with a propelling gun. The fans around them had mixed reactions... obvious wrestling loyalists began to boo loudly, but other fans smiled and began to clap along with the music. Several parents held their children out to the huge Disney characters, who shook hands and signed autographs as they continued to dance to the music. Finally at the top of the stage, Mickey and Minnie Mouse appeared, along with a beaming blonde man with a shockingly bright Disney dress shirt. "Okay everyone... are we all ready to party?" Cheers and boos from the audience, who were decidedly conflicted about all of this. Around the ring, several of the wrestlers had vanished, heading through the crowd or just quietly making their way past the entrance ramp and into the backstage area. At ringside, Poison Ivy stood with Ted Tallison. Ivy's face was dark and locked in a tight scowl, while Tallison held an expression of stunned disbelief. The Disney characters were dancing around them, and some had gotten into the ring and were performing mock wrestling moves on each other. Ivy's eyes were narrowed into slits. "Oh, hell no.
