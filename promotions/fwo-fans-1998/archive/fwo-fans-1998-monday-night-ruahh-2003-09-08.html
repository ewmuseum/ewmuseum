Monday Night Ruahh~!, 8 September 2003

8 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction It begins here. The Wrestler Of The Year tournament. 'nuff said. The Gathering As the pumped up audience waited in anticipation after a thrilling episode of a man shooting fWo merchandise out of a plastic cannon, they were greeted by unfamiliar music, and an unfamiliar lighting arrangement. A strobe light began to flicker through the entire arena as a screeching noise from the music came into a harsh drum and guitar riff that was accompanied by fireworks. As the guitar continued to play, the words on the FanTron gave away who's spectacular entrance this was: "The Rogues" Bad Blood by Ministry continued to play as Eron, eGG NOG, cHEESE and YoGuRt came down to the ring. The crowd showed their appreciation by trying to drown out Al Jorgeson's Ministry out with loathing boos, something that simply fuelled the Rogues as they entered the ring. The music came to its head as the strobe ceased, and the three men stood side by side inside the ring, Eron with a microphone. "Tonight... is a night of gathering. The gathering of The Rogues, together in this very ring. Myself, NOG, cHEESE." Eron stopped, looking over at YoGuRt. YoGuRt gave Eron a puppy face, as Eron looked back to the front of the crowd. "And I guess YoGuRt. It seems as if this is the way things will be, for The Rogues. I had other plans, but I of all people have to understand that sometimes, the roads do change on you. My plan was for Voran Xias to realize his fate, and walk step by step with myself as a member of the Rogues. My victory against him was supposed to be his wakeup call. But instead, it seems Voran decided to take an optional road. The road out. And it is a tragedy, a tragedy he decided to take the road that should not be travelled! But I guess, in my most sympathetic tone, I hope the tragedies he faces comes to an end, and his road leads him back to here." The crowd was pretty silent, unsure of what to take of what Eron was saying. Voran did seem to be gone, but this wasn't anything they heard of. Eron then continued. "So Silver Fox and Sammy Smooth have announced the Wrestler of the Year competition, to come to a head at fWo Legends. The Wrestler of the Year... I remember when this the build up for this, was a respectful competiton! A battle of the greatest, a battle to see who just is the greatest wrestler not just in the fWo, but in the world... and then Black Panther won it. And no-one cared anymore. But then they worked it up, made the whole world believe that this was it, this would be justice, this would be the Greatest Tournament Ever... and Ric Chronos won it. Once again, no-one cared anymore. The company seemed to have one last shot. Let the deserving man win the Wrestler of the Year, let the one who truly did work the hardest, give it to the man or woman who showed infinite amount of respect, credibility and talent night in and night out. Instead... they gave it to Ruben Ross! What the hell is this?" Eron took a dramatic pause, allowing the crowd to give their opinion on Eron taking a metaphorical dumb on past, respected fWo stars. "And now, hey, they decided to do it for a fourth time. Hell, the guys who nearly put this place out of business were smart enough not to repeat this farce in 2002, but hey, lets try it one more time? How am I supposed to expect to win a competition when the man who deserves to win, never wins? And I have to face WGL in the first seed match. That's World's Greatest Luggage, by the way." The crowd booed, as any normal human being would from comments against a legend like Scott Slugger. "Here is some trivia for you people, did you know that if it wasn't for me back in 2000, making him look like a hero once again? He went from Austin Davis' luggage man, to being carried by me, house show in and house show out. And if it wasn't for him being crafty in our Hell in a Cell match, he'd be working overseas in the CWL Retirement Home right now!" the crowd continued to boo as Eron simply smiled. "But you see, like I said before, the contest always fumbles. The contest is always just a ploy to give to those who can't do it on their own. But then again..." Eron smiled, pausing for a moment. "I love to break cycles. I love to ruin what is supposed to be right. And in the Wrestler of the Year Tournament, I am going to win. I won't just win. I will slaughter, I will maim, and I will crucify if 2000 year old execution methods are needed. That fWo World Heavyweight Title is the one thing, the one pedestal I still haven't stood upon, and it is time I do it. "And I will do it, at fWo Legends. At. All. Costs." The fans began to boo a bit louder then regularly at Eron's comments, but their noise tirade was ended by cHEESE taking the microphone from Eron. cHEESE holds the mic, looking at Eron. "Umm... Eron, something you forgot. Me and NOG are also in the first seed matches. I could become the fWo World Heavyweight Champion." Egg NOG then grabbed the mic from cHEESE. "Me too! egg NOG... CHAMPION OF THE WORLD!" Eron looked over at both of them, and shrugged his shoulders, taking the microphone back. "You know what, I understand. I'm not going to argue, because we all have personal goals to go with our personal intentions. cHEESE," Eron paused to look at him, putting a hand on his shoulder. "If you become the fWo World Heavyweight Champion, I will still stand side by side with you. And egg NOG," Eron once again turned to the NOG. "If you become the fWo World Heavyweight Champion, I will still stand side by side with you. This isn't a faction of jealousy. This is a faction of Rogues, willing to take what comes to them." NOG and cHEESE nodded their heads, in agreement. "However... you two, as the Legion of Dairy, are a tag team. And matches, like tonights with the Fuse Bros., should be where your attention lies. As for me... tonight is my night to think of one thing: fWo Wrestler of the Year." "Bad Blood" by Ministry once again played, as the three men shook hands and began to walk to the back, YoGuRt following not too far behind. Take us to it? It was just a short time before their videogame would be fully loaded, and the Fuse Bros. would be playing against one of the newer fWo tag teams in the Legion of Dairy. But as Tyler Fuse walked the hallways of the Stadium of New Mexico, and Conor Fuse followed closely behind, the Fuse Bros. didn't care about that part of the videogame right now. Right now, there was something else on their minds. Something that had been there for more than three weeks now. The Fuse Bros. hadn't played the fWo videogame since that time three weeks ago, yet, they were back again... with a better memory than most of the other "gamers" in the fWo. Tyler turned the corner in the hallway, and arrived at a locker room door. The scene panned upwards, showing that this locker room door was none other than Pretty Fly's. Tyler knocked. And then he waited. Inside, Nate and Steve-O were just about to smoke up, but Nate sighed as he rose from the bench and opened up the door. "Hello... goombas." Tyler greeted with a smirk. "Hey dude!" 'Narly' Nate replied, looking for a high five. Yet, Tyler was not accustom to this new type of "slang"... so he just stood there, and stared directly at the 'Narly One'. "Uh... so, what's up dudes?" Nate asked, once again looking for a high five. Oh, the life of a pot-head... it was a good life to have. Once more, Tyler just stared directly at Nate. "Yeah, anyways..." Nate said, drifting his voice off into nothing. "Yes." Tyler inquired. "We're here to bother you guys... do you remember three weeks ago?" Three weeks ago!? Was Tyler kidding!?!? Nate and Steve-O couldn't even remember ONE MINUTE ago! "Not... sure..." Nate's voice once again drifted. "Okay." Tyler said as he looked back at Conor, who held his 'tough man' look upon his face. "Let me remind you goomba's then." "Uh... okay dude!" Nate said. "Well, you two basically interrupted our match against The Uprising with this 'warp tunnel' promo. Conor and I must admit that we were rather captivated by this package... so, we'd like to know, DO you really know of this warp tunnel?" Nate nodded with excitement. "Yeah dude. We do! We have it in our house!" Opening the door a little bit wider (so that Steve-O could be seen), the brother of the 'Narly One' spoke. "We've been in it... how many times now Nate?" "Oh, I don't know..." He tried to count, but could barely be able to. "One... uh... how ever many this many is..." Nate flashed his ten digits about fifteen or so times. Tyler nodded as he looked back at Conor. "Yes. Go on." Nate didn't. "That's like... it... dudes!" Once again, Tyler nodded his head slightly. "Would we be able to visit this warp tunnel with you guys?" About to say yes, Nate then realized something. "Hold on a second..." He pondered. "You're the Fuse Bros. right?" Nate wasn't sure. I mean, he saw a lot of things in his day... and when the walls talked to you often enough, you got confused here and there as to who was who, and what was what. "Yes." Conor spoke up for the first time. "Not... not good dudes." Nate shook his head. "Why's that?" Asked Tyler. "You were the guys who beat us up that once... with chairs." So pot-heads DO have three week memories!? Steve-O cut in. "Twice... I think, if that comes after one." Pretty Fly wasn't sure. "I think three does." Nate said. "So three times." Conor began to count on his fingers, but he was stopped by his brother. "No. That's alright Conor... you don't have to do that." He then looked back at Nate. "What would it take for us to make that up to you guys?" He said. Nate pondered again... as he glanced back at Steve-O and shrugged. "Dudes, you guys wrestling tonight?" He asked. Tyler nodded. "Against the Legion of Dairy, yes." "How about... uh... you win your match, and we can see dudes?" Nate said. Actually agreeing, Tyler and Conor Fuse affirmed. "Okay. We can do that." 'Narly' Nate smiled with stoned enough delight, as he turned back to Steve-O and brought out his roll. "Time to get hhhhhiiiiiiggggghhhhhhh..." Tyler looked at Conor. "Weird, weird goombas." Survivor Challenge Cue the music. "Walk" by Pantera. No prompting required. Boos. Enter the Main Man, wearing a while lotta gold. Namely, the fWo World Title. Kodiak Vic Creed casually strolled to ringside, his face reflecting his recent gruelling match against Brand Frontier. Occasionally he'd clearly favour his knee slightly, but those moments of weakness would pass quickly. Yet his expression was strangely uplifting, considering he'd recently lost fifty thousand dollars. "I know," Creed started to say as he adjusted the huge gold belt draped over his massive shoulder. "I know that Brand Frontier is one happy guy. He's got fifty thousand dollars that he probably thinks he earned. Well Brand, enjoy my money. Enjoy it while you can. But buddy boy, all the money in the World doesn't come close to having this." Creed patted the belt. No music. No fanfare. "Creed." The fans exploded as Brand Frontier stepped out from the back, mic in hand. "How's the knee Vic? How's the big bad Juggernaut holding up after I kicked his ass?" "I'm still here, and I still have the belt." "Only because you've spent more time dodging me than you have facing me." Brand shook his head in disgust. "For a man who wants to keep me under his thumb, you sure are spending a lot of time staying away from me." "Let me tell you something about control." Creed's expression was serious. "You got into the fWo by winning the Survivor contest. You think that makes you special? Hell, I was in the first Survivor. I didn't win it and look where I'm at today - World Champion. I'm even employed, which is more than I can say for just about everyone else in the first Survivor." "I wasn't surprised when you won Survivor 2." That cocky smirk returned to the Main Man's face. "I rigged the whole damn thing." Brand's expression was one of shock and mistrust. The fans were simply stunned. "When I found out you were in, I knew it was my chance for a little payback. I made sure that you won your fWo contract. Or rather, I won you your fWo contract. So you see Brand, I started owning you the second you walked through the doors." "You think I believe anything you tell me Vic?" "Oh I made a believer out of Ana, night after night." Creed chuckled at his comment. "You didn't really survive anything. The contest was a joke, just like you." Brand started down the aisle. The fans cheered their approval. Vic waved him back. "Woah woah woah Brand. If you want a shot at the belt, you're going to have to jump through my hoops." Frontier slowed his steps. "The fWo Survivor might have been a joke, but I've got a real contest for you: the Kodiak Survivor Challenge. If you can win three KSC matches, I'll give you your title shot at Unleashed." Brand paused in thought. "You're on." Vic smirked. "See Brand? You take orders quite well from your superiors." "Three matches Vic. That's all that stands between us. At Unleashed, you're going home a little lighter." Now Brand chose to smile. "One gold belt lighter. And there's only two things you can do about it..." The smile turned to a smirk as Frontier watched Creed scowl. "Nothing and like it." "Thor" began to play and Brand Frontier disappeared into the back, leaving Kodiak Vic Creed clutching his World Title. Would Brand survive Creed's challenge? And if he did, would Creed survive another Brand Frontier match as World Champion? Fuse Bros. vs. LoD "Monkey vs. Robot" by James Kochalka Superstar... and out walked the legenDAIRY (hahaha, get it? LegenDAIRY!? I'm a comic... a comic!) duo known to the Survivor III contest, the fWo, and all of the wrestling and fighting world in general as the Legion of Dairy. The LoD. cHEESE and egg NOG walked out to what was basically a mixed reaction. Some people liked them for their over-all talent they displayed... whereas others could not stand the Legion of Dairy. Past comments about how wrestling sucked... or how the Asylum had a better tag team division than the fWo still played in their minds... and they weren't about to cheer them on because of it. The LoD entered the ring, as they began to go over their wrestling strategies for what was said to probably be one of the weirdest and most odd tag teams of all time. The Fuse Bros., Tyler and Conor. Cue up, as always, the world one, level one theme song to Mario Bros. one, the classic video game for the Nintendo Entertainment System... providing the public with countless unforgettable titles, including Metal Gear, Rad Racer, and lots of other well enjoyed games that will remain a part of our heritage for a very long time. Out marched Tyler and Conor Fuse to boos from the Crowd. Conor held their trademark powdered blue question mark box gingerly in both of his hands, as he made sure that it would not fall on the ground and lose all of its contents. They would need it to play against the former Survivor III gamers. Conor was damn sure of that. On the other hand, Tyler had a firm grasp of the Cheat Codes in his... all two pages of them, with a size three font, and everything and anything allowing them to be able to use chairs in a match, to even a bulldozing crane if need be. And even though Tyler and Conor weren't sure how to get a big giant crane into the Arena just yet... they would find a way when that time would come. Tyler took out a roll of tape from his tights, as he began to stitch the Cheat Codes up against the nearest ring post. Meanwhile Conor just placed the powered blue box underneath his corner as he waited for Tyler to finish plugging the papers into the system... and for the game to actually begin. Tyler was, after all, the first player... and as 'Fuse A' entered the ring, egg NOG was set to be the first player for the gamers under the title of LoD. The bell sounded. The game had begun. Tyler circled around egg NOG for a couple of rounds... as both tried to gain the upper hand by finding a good chance to strike. That didn't happen. The Fans began to grow restless, as Tyler turned to the Mushroom Kingdom to see what the problem was... and as he did that, he was instantly floored to the mat. egg NOG laughed for a split second before he bounced off the ropes, and sent Tyler Fuse flying up in the air, over the top rope, and to the padding below via a clothesline to the side of his head. Conor Fuse grumbled at this... as egg NOG brushed his hands together as if that took little to no effort at all. And it hadn't, so egg NOG was allowed to do so. But Tyler Fuse was resistant to say the least. He pulled himself right back up off the mat, and rolled into the ring. There he was met by numerous egg NOG boots to the side of his head, as one half of the former Asylum Tag Team Champions picked Tyler Fuse up, and snap suplexed him to the mat. Yet Tyler had used the ropes and got right back up. egg NOG just smiled, DDT'ing 'Fuse A' to the canvas, as he walked over to the ropes, and bounced off of them. A leg drop to the neck of Tyler made sure that the Fuse Brother was not going to have an easy time breathing. egg NOG then rose, only to go off the ropes and do the same thing all over again. If Tyler couldn't breathe after the first one, well, now his trachea was totally crushed (but not in a Triple H type-of-way). egg NOG picked Tyler Fuse up, as he rifled him into the ropes and waited for him upon return. Tyler was slow in doing so, too, as his legs shook a little, all from the effects of the double-leg drop curiously of egg NOG. SLAM! Powerslam. egg NOG looked for the pinning combination. One. Two. Boot to the side of the head. Conor Fuse had entered the ring, and kicked egg NOG square in the face. Of course, by now Conor's so-called "cop-out" was to exclaim that the Cheat Codes allowed him to do this... and the Referee would just stare Conor down, and point him back to his corner. egg NOG rolled around the mat, clutching his head as he reeled in deep pain. Conor's kick had caught him right in the temple... and now it was beginning to pulsate through the rest of his skull. But the former Asylum Tag Team Champion wasn't a Champion for nothing. He had learned to carry a thick skin on his body... and as egg NOG rolled into the ring ropes, he stuck out his right hand, and pulled himself up. Tag. egg NOG had tagged in cHEESE. cHEESE marched in, picking up Tyler Fuse as he took hold of his right arm, and brought it back down to the mat, along-so throwing the rest of his body against it. cHEESE then went to work. Was the same reply, only louder this time through. And cHEESE enjoyed what he was doing here... so, just for good measure, he did it once more. "AH~!" Picking up Tyler, cHEESE hurled him into the ropes, and shoulder blocked the same side (right) of Tyler as he fell to the canvas. With Tyler once again down, cHEESE looked to finish off the match. He grabbed Tyler by the right arm, and tucked it behind his armpit... in a version of a 'Rings of Saturn' maneuver, just done with one arm. In came Conor Fuse once more, as he blasted cHEESE straight across the face with his boot. cHEESE fell back, as Conor had now made both members of the LoD taste his rock hard wrestling boot that he happened to sport. Conor Fuse exited, while this allowed for Tyler to get on his feet, bounce off the ropes, and land a leg drop of his own. Whack. This time it was cHEESE whom felt the cold wrath of Tyler Fuse's leg. Conor roared in, quickly nailing cHEESE with a clothesline and then another as he got right back up.
