But as the Survivor II Champion moved in, Pierre went straight to the eyes and slapped on an obvious chokehold. Brand slapped Frenchie's hands away in anger, a clothesline from the Heavens brought the Frenchman down hard. Dropping down on his opponent, Brand began to lay in shot after shot of hard lefts and rights. When he was convinced his opponent was dazed and confused the hero lifted Pierre up and tossed him into the ropes. Frenchie was unable to avoid the huge dropkick that followed and fell back into the ropes once more, the cage that was on the other side actually holding him in to the ring this time. Brand leapt up with a Heart Kick that sent the Frenchie over the ropes, once more against the cage. Frenchie stumbled a bit on the outside before beginning to fall, catching himself on the second rope as he did. Pierre pulled himself back in the ring and tumbled over and on to his back. The former United States and World Champion went to the top then, the crowd exploded as Frontier took flight. The Paragon Bomb! Brand rolled off with the impact but scampered back and hooked Frenchie's leg. One! Two!! Three!!! Brand Frontier had just defeated the Flying Frenchie to rightfully earn his title shot, or he would have, but Frenchie had enough presence of mind to drape his arm over the ropes. Brand was already in the midst of celebrating when the referee finally got his attention and pointed back to Frenchie, who was now rising to his feet. Pierre's trademark smile once again spread across his face, he was not through yet. He might not be the Wrestler of the Year this year, but the honor of beating the man that did have that title was driving him now, adrenaline had left him five minutes ago. The crowd began to jeer now, spitting out disgust in what they saw, it made both combatants look around surprised. Even the ref halted and curiously looked out in to the masses. A second later all three men realized what had drawn their ire however. Spyder had arrived, stomping to the ring with determination on his face. He stopped at the chain link fencing, grabbing and pulling at it before making his way to the door. The ref on the outside, holding the keys, tried to block the recently resurrected villain only to receive a punch to the throat that brought him to his knees. A swift kick from Frenchie's nemesis felled the official and allowed Spyder the time to seize the keys. He came to the top of the steps on the outside as the fans changed from jeering to cheering. Ric Chronos bolted from the back... Making a beeline to help his comrade, he spun Spyder around, catching the masked man off guard, yanking the keys from his hand before tucking them in his trunks. The two rivals immediately laid in to one another, the bigger Chronos getting the upper hand at first before a strike to the eyes gave Spyder a brief respite. In the midst of the chaos Brand charged in and turned Pierre around quicker than Carson Convington on a first date. A German suplex sprawled Frenchie out, but he broke free, not allowing a chain reaction to occur. Frontier countered quickly however and an Implant DDT drove the Frenchman to the mat. Brand grabbed a leg and laid back... One! Two!! But once more Frenchie shot a shoulder up. The Frenchman had fought through the odds to bring him to the finals of the tournament a few weeks ago. In his heart he believed, had it not been for Spyder, the man that now battled his friend Ric Chronos on the outside, he would have been the Wrestler of the Year and not his opponent this night. Frontier brought him to his feet again and made for a Powerbomb attempt, pointing to the corner as he did, but Frenchie slipped out and backed up, and hit a standing clothesline that took Brand by surprise. Frontier stumbled back and Frenchie grabbed on and connected with a Frenchie Driver. Setting the paragon up now, Pierre went for a fireman's carry, but Frontier slipped out the back and went for a DDT. Frenchie blocked the attempt however and brought Brand up and around with a back to back whip suplex that drove the Irishman into the mat. Once more the Flying Frenchie headed to the top for the finish, diving off with an amazingly graceful headbutt, often imitated but never duplicated, but Brand rolled out of the way at the last second much to the fan's delight and the Flying Frenchie connected with nothing but unforgiving canvas. Frenchie rolled on the mat but Brand Frontier rose defiantly. The runner-up in the 2004 Wrestler of the Year tournament came to his feet as well. Both men having gave it their all the arched toward the other. Brand stepped to the side as Frenchie lunged, the Frenchman spun around, looking to counter whatever the hero had to offer. But counter he couldn't, the Flying Frenchie had spun around and right in to an exploding chest via the... Hammer of God! With the fan's on their feet and somehow Spyder half way up the side of the cage Brand Frontier hooked Pierre's leg as the official leaped over the two of them, landing again in mid count... One! Spyder reached the top of the cage. He steadied himself, taking aim. Two!! Ric Chronos had given chase however and had grabbed Spyder's leg. The masked man tried to pull away. Three!!! Spyder's interference might have made a difference... might have, but no one would know. With Ric Chronos holding on to his enemies boot, keeping him from interfering Brand Frontier had defeated the legendary Flying Frenchie to keep his deserved World Heavyweight Championship title shot. The crowd erupted once more as the announcer made the official call. Spyder finally kicked free of Chronos' iron grasp and lept down on to the top turnbuckle. There, he carefully waited. Frontier, now celebrating his win, not hearing the jeers of the fans over his theme music, did not see what had just occurred. He stepped away from Frenchie for a split second, raising his arms in the air as Spyder took flight. However, in a split second Brand spun, catching Spyder in mid-air before leaping in the air himself and driving the man that had tarnished his Wrestler of the Year win into the canvas with a twisting powerslam. The impact rocketed them both off the mat, Frontier bounding back to his feet in one swift motion. Spyder arched his back as he writhed on the mat. Chronos, during this time, had slid down the outside of the cage and made his way to the door, using the keys from earlier he unlocked said door and stomped into the ring. Spyder began to get to his feet, realizing the predicament he found himself in he scampered to a corner, holding a hand up in the air, begging off an irate former World Champion in Ric Chronos. Frenchie now made his way up, groggily, and realized the events that had unfolded. As Ric stalked towards Spyder, Frenchie stepped in front of the Mighty Hero. The two locked eyes and amidst the stare down neither man saw what was coming from up the ramp way. Finally, the two jutted their hands out, agreeing with the outcome of the match... seconds before Result: Brand Frontier via Pinfall, retains World Championship Title Sho
