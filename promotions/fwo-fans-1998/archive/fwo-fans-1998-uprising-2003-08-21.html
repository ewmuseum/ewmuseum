Introduction The fWo comes to the city of broken dreams, Las Vegas. With the Unleashed special just eight days away, the roster finds themselves weary from traveling in the dog days of summer... Laying the Groundwork Silver Fox settled himself behind a small coffee table and frowned as he glanced at the front page of the paper. Israel again... the place hadn't settled since they had been there earlier in the year. For a while it appeared as though progress would be made, but here they were, some six-odd months later and it was still bad, very bad. Around him were the noises of the coffee house. It was hot, ungodly hot. Whose idea had it been to run a tour of the Southwest in the summer? Despite the flooding that had taken place earlier in the week the town was still what it was... a dry, desperate hole for the guilty and the hopeless. Las Vegas... it was what it was. Silver Fox was handed his coffee and wondered, not for the first time, why Disney had not set up a stationary location for them to run shows from. Certainly something in Orlando would work... the Disney complex was there and he was certain that they would have no troubles selling out 10,000 seats every week. The road trip, the traveling wrestling show... it seemed like such an old school mentality. Like the old carnivals from where the business sprung from. But the road takes it's toll on a man. Always. He winced... a cold, shooting pain traveled up his left arm. He bit it back, struggling while his leg shook involuntarily. He closed his eyes, waiting it out. After a few minutes, the pain had faded away. He opened his eyes, worried. This wasn't good. He didn't have time to look into it further, not now... not when there was so much work to do. Don't make me break out the old Janitor's catch phrase for your pasty 'land of the cheap manufactured children's toys' self!" Koji continued to walk. "Hey JERKY, I'm talking to you! Yes, I know it's a first since people never talk to you, because you can't talk back it seems, but I am." Flyer lowered his head. "I thought we were working together." Flyer sighed. "I thought we were friends." And with that, Koji parted the entrance curtain and returned backstage. The boos around the arena began to swell, and Flyer began to jump up and down in the ring in frustration. He calmly collected himself, trying to take in everything. "What are you people looking at? Huh?!? Go rent a freaking movie! Why don't YOU jog a little!" Flyer said, pointing to a portly man in the audience. "And YOU!? I don't know what happened to you when you were born, but it's gotta be a funny story with an ugly ending." Flyer paced around the ring, unable to collect himself. The one person that had stayed in the fWo and had become Flyer's friend, had slowly turned his back. "Fine, you know what I'm going to do? I'm going to leave, right now! No match for me tonight, no more of seeing my athletic GIFT inside this very ring! You want to boo me out of here, then I'm going to leave! Right now!" Flyer stayed in the ring a small bit, tapping his foot. And stayed. And stayed. "Okay, I was just lying before, but if you really want me to go, I'll be on my way to the next defunct city on this tour of stupidity." Flyer threw down the microphone in frustration, going out through the middle ropes and tugging at the apron as he landed on the floor. He walked slowly up the entrance ramp, taking enough time, and it seemed that he wiped a single tear from his eye before leaving the line of sight. Kodiak Vic Creed(c) vs. Kellen Kinkade "Black Hole Sun" by Soundgarden arrived but it didn't bring with it the usual display that had those in attendance spitting with rage. There was no firework explosion or triumphantly posed Kellen Kinkade at the top of the aisle, instead pacing rather briskly down the aisle was an edge, nervous looking Jersey Devil. Somewhere between the several glances over his own shoulder nervous rubs of the back of his neck, Kinkade managed to acquire a microphone and clamber up into the ring. Taking care to shoot a glance around front row to make sure nobody was about to jump it and attack him. "I've had about enough of this shit." Kinkade sighed as the crowd made a sarcastic groan of sympathy "I don't expect you people to understand what it's like to be stalked, to have an itch that try as you might just can't be scratched. Over the last few weeks Lance Knight has hampered my every move but tonight all of that comes to an end." Kinkade paused as a "Knight! Knight! Knight!" chant erupted in the crowd, The Jersey Devil turned quickly to the aisle but the fact that nobody was there further had the crowd mocking the Immortal champion. "Come on cue ball, the fun and games are over... get your ass out here." The Jersey Devil growled with an angry scowl upon his face. The eruption of "Walk" by Pantera had him looking even more nervous, as the fWo World Heavyweight Champion stepped out onto the aisle with the title belt strapped firmly around his waist. Creed for once had a little backing from the crowd who wanted nothing more than to see the canvas being mopped with Kinkade's head sometime in the near future. Creed didn't waste time; he was down the aisle and towering above Kinkade within a couple of seconds. The face of the Main Man remained cold and emotionless behind his silver reflective sunglasses as Kinkade scratched his head "I guess we still aren't clear, why don't you go and sit in front row for a few minutes while I sort out my little issue. Eat some popcorn, drink some soda, make some friends." Unimpressed, Creed remained still as Kinkade started to look irate "Alright then, maybe the message will get through better if I look you in the eye while I say it." The Jersey Devil stepped forth and proceeded to do something that left the crowd completely silent. He took off Vic Creed's sunglasses. Snapped them in half. And threw them out of the ring. "Fetch." Creed quickly whipped off his title belt before tossing it to the official and dragging Kinkade up by the hair, one huge gorilla press later he dumped the challenger insultingly over the top rope and to the arena with a hefty thus, stopping to dust off his hands and remark "Why don't you fetch instead?" toward The Jersey Devil as he got to his feet humiliated. Creedzilla clambered out of the ring and caught Kinkade as he tried to scamper away, clutching him by the wrist and whipping him toward the steel steps with true authority. Still aware of his position Kinkade was able to leap right over the steps and turn, lifting them out of their position and tossing them into the hands of Vic as he advanced. A spinning heel kick from the lower section of the steps saw Kinkade drive the upper half that he'd thrown right into the face of Creed, with first blood of the match drawn as the champion bled onto the arena floor from the big impact. With Creed down and temporarily stunned, Kinkade swiftly hoisted the steps above his head to bring them down on The Main Man with a sick thud. But in a moment that showed just how edgy he was, a fan patted him on the back and had him dropping the steps and spinning around fists clenched and ready. "Knight! Knight! Knight!" The fans chanted jubilantly as a furious Kinkade rued a missed opportunity, his own paranoia had cost him a good shot at Creed and he paid for it as The Alaskan Juggernaut got to his feet and smacked Kade with a hard knee to the ribs, manhandling him once again by gorilla pressing him up and dumping him over the top rope and back into the ring. The sound of a steel chair snapping soon followed, the bloodied champion was about to give his opponent a payback that'd see him losing vital fluids too. Creed had been insulted beyond the point of caring about being disqualified and he was soon in the ring and measuring up a shot to the man the fans called Judas as he scrambled up. Vic made a mighty swing but The Jersey Devil managed to duck it... leaving Creed to follow through toward an unfortunately placed official. Fortunately, Creed was able to slam the brakes on and avoid beheading the referee with the chair but fortunes were changing like the wind. Kinkade arrived from nowhere, catching Creed in the ribs with a Killing Spree as he turned and drilling him into the official... knocking both men into the corner and crushing the life out of the man in the stripes shirt as part of the process. The official slumped to the canvas in a heap as Vic staggered out of the corner gasping for air and clutching his ribs, Kinkade wasted no time in picking up the chair Creed had dropped and delivering a shot across his spine that had him arching his back in agony. A second drilled edge of the chair went right into Creed's ribs and doubled him over for the grand finale, a blazing chair shot to the back of the head as Kinkade leapt from the second turnbuckle. The Main Man was down and hurt, blood staining the canvas from the step shot and brains scrambled from the third chair shot.
