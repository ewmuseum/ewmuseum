She disappeared and reappeared in the ring as quickly as she did for weeks at a time on actual programming. The conflict in their strategies was apparent - Shadows would strike hard, and god only knew when Amy would. They grinned at one another. That was half the fun. Shadows stood proudly across from Aimz, who felt mocked by his confidence and that gleam holding strong in his eyes.
