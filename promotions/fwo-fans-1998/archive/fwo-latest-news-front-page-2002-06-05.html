Check out the Columns Archive! Check out the Interviews Archive! Tickets go on sale for fWo events in July. June 5, 2002 The fWo will hold 15 events during the month of July. Action will begin on July 1 and 2nd, and then wrestlers will have five days off to celebrate Independence Day with their families and loved ones. fWo Unleashed coming this Monday on the USA Network. June 5, 2002 The action begins at 9pm Eastern/6pm Pacific time. The fWo is about to be 'Unleashed' once again on live, national television on a new night, on a new channel. fWo Unleashed will be two hours of programming you will not want to miss out on. Hell In A Cell tradition will continue this year at SummerSlaughter. June 5, 2002 Participants have yet to be determined. While speculation persists that the main event of SummerSlaughter will be an fWo World Title Match between fWo World Champion 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly & 'Kodiak' Vic Creed, it is unlikely that a HIAC match would occur between the two big men.
