fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 24 October 1999

24 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Sunday Slaughter began with the cameras in the backstage area. It was a sight that was rare in the fWo, nearly the entire roster was gathered in the concrete room, waiting for word on what was to come. Wrestlers who had not been seen in some time, wrestlers who we active competitors... that night nearly all of them shared the same room. There were some notable exceptions, those being both pairs of Dark Riders, whose absence spoke volumes. With the exception of Stone, not a one was to be seen. Chris Finn walked into the room, and the fWo roster looked in his direction as the conversation died down to a hush. Chris Finn looked at them, suddenly nervous all over again. Clearing his throat, Chris Finn began. "Thank you all for coming. Obviously..." Chris Finn was cut off as Ice and Cactus Jon stepped forward, both looking angry. Ice spoke first, "Shove it Finn. The last time this asshole was around I suffered third degree burns... I had a shoulder blade fractured and the whole fWo went to shit... Jim Dudley got rid of that bastard and I want to know why he's back." Jon pushed Ice out of the way and stepped up into Finn's face. "The Commissioner. What's to be done, eh?" Finn stepped back, trying to get some room. "Listen up everyone... nobody likes this situation less than I do, but we are stuck with it... at least for now. The Commissioner is in the hospital, and is currently listed in critical condition. He's lapsed into a coma, and at this point we are unaware if he will be returning to the fWo or not. According to the bylines of the promotion, a new representative must be appointed no more than two weeks after the vacation. The board will be making their decision on who the new Commissioner will be after the Mob Rules event on Saturday. Now, onto business at hand. The fWo is badly understaffed in security, and for that reason we are not requiring anyone to stay at the arena, for your own safety. John Willis is ramping up security and we will have a secure force in place by this Wednesday. I'll be assisting with this effort, and thus there will not be a taping of FlashBack this week. We have two tag team tournament matches tonight, after they are over you are all free to go." The fWo roster looked around at each other, and some members did get up and leave. Before they were out the door, the Black Panther stood and looked around at the wrestlers in the room. The Black Panther was usually a silent man, and when he spoke everyone stopped to listen. "I wasn't here when the Dark Riders caused trouble the first time. But if I had been I would not have run. We are many, and they are few... feeding upon fear. I don't run. I fight." The fWo locker room looked around at each other, slowly starting to nod. From the outside the opening music of Slaughter sounded, and the wrestlers left the room in the direction of the dressing room. Finn turned to Willis, pointing at the doors. "Watch for the Dark Riders... don't let them in the building tonight. We don't need any more trouble..." Finn turned to go to the announcing table, failing to notice the shadowy pair of men creep into the room, headed in the direction of the ring... Hollywood Blondes vs. French Foundation Kid Rock's "Bawitdaba" bring out the French Foundation for a Semi-Final match, to determine who will face The Underground at Mob Rules for the vacant Tag Team Championship. Primetime and Frenchie taunt the crowd, while The Boston Strangler follows silently behind them. Primetime and Frenchie simultaneously flip into the ring as blue, white, and red pyros go off around the ring. Kid Rock plays again, this time "F*ck Off", and The Hollywood Blondes, the team that forced the vacating of the titles, march to the ring, pointing at and mocking the Foundation. They enter the ring and stand chest to chest with the Foundation, neither team backing down. Finally Primetime and Universal head to their respective corners, and the match starts. Frenchie shoves Star to the mat and spits on him, adding to the animosity that these two teams have built up in recent days. Star charges Frenchie, who sidesteps the attack, mocking Star even more. Star locks up with him, but Frenchie breaks and slips between the ropes, causing the ref to move Lance out of the way. Frenchie yells out that Star isn't worth his time, and tags out to the Cruiserweight Champion of the fWo. Primetime locks up with Star, and forces him to the corner. Star turns in the corner, reversing their positions. The sound of his sharp chops echo throughout the arena, and he sets Primetime up on the turnbuckle. He climbs the corner for a superplex, but its blocked by Primetime. The two exchange blows up top, leading to a dazed Primetime finally being superplexed to the mat. Star tosses Primetime into the Blondes' corner, and then heads to the Foundation's corner to mouth off at Frenchie. While the ref separates those two, Universal gets some kicks in on Primetime, and then steps onto the apron. Primetime is propped up against the 'buckles in the Blondes' corner, so Lance charges with a shoulderblock. PT drops out of the way, causing Star to crash shoulder first against the steel ring post. PT grabs Lance and suplexes him to the center of the ring. He drops a couple of knees onto the injured shoulder, and then nails a shoulderbreaker on him. Star is curled up in pain in the corner while Primetime tags in the Frenchie. The Foundation hits an assisted Primetime Bomb on Star, but the cover by Frenchie is broken up by "The Franchi$e". Frenchie connects with a neckbreaker on "The Future", and a springboard moonsault gets two. Frenchie misses with a slingshot legdrop, and the two grapplers get to their feet at the same time. Lance slaps Pierre repeatedly, asking if he's good enough for him now. Star applies a small package for a near-fall, and Frenchie reverses it for another. Star releases to break the pinning situation, then heads to his corner for the tag. Universal meets TFF with a float-over DDT, and then locks in a chinlock. After berating the French superstar enough, he lets go and lets Frenchie return to his feet. He goes for a kick to the gut, but Frenchie catches his foot and swings him around, following up with a bulldog. Frenchie kips back up and tags in Primetime, who connects with a slingshot kneedrop to Universal's back. Primetime slips on a ? nelson for two, which Universal kicks out of. Primetime whips Universal to the ropes and ducks under a clothesline, locking in an abdominal stretch. Universal hiptosses PT to break it, and then locks in one of his own. In the maneuver, he elbows Primetime repeatedly in the ribs, and then turns the hold into a pumphandle slam. Chris tags in Lance, and then throws him off the top rope with a Rocket Launcher. That gets two for the Blondes, but PT gets his foot on the rope before the three. Ready to finish it off, Star hits the Shooting Star on Primetime, but the count is broken by a Guillotine Legdrop from Frenchie to the back of Lance's head. Lance rolls off of Primetime, and Universal enters the ring and nails the Cha-Ching on TFF. PT drapes his arm over Star while Universal throws Frenchie out of the ring, unaware of the pinning situation. Universal turns, sees what's going down, and drops an axehandle on Primetime just a second too late, as the ref's hand has already hit the mat for the third time. The Blondes look ready to dismantle Primetime until the Strangler intervenes, warning them that it may not be a very good idea. The two men shrug and head to the outside to beat on Frenchie, which doesn't seem to bother the Boston Strangler in the least bit. Mitch Wilson emerges from backstage and slowly makes his way to Frenchie's aid, who'd been destroyed by the Blondes on the outside. The Blondes back off, and The Foundation head back to their locker room, the entire time enduring the jeers of Chris Universal and Lance Star, who can be heard swearing that this won't be the last they hear from them... Result: French Foundation by Pinfall Aftermath... The Flying Frenchie and Primetime walked to the backstage area, slapping hands and smiling about their victory. The turned a corner and nearly ran into Mitch Wilson, who was coming in the opposite direction. Wilson looked angry for a moment, and then softened. "Yeah?" The Frenchie and Primetime exchanged glances and then the Frenchie looked hard at Wilson. "What is ze problem with you? Where were you on Warfare? Zis... not showing up... what is zis?" Wilson's expression soured, and he snorted as he stepped around the Frenchie and walked away. The Frenchie and Primetime watched him go, concerned about what was happening with their alliance... Chaos vs. Underground The Underground, Mike Bear, Wretch, and Fear, make their way to the ring, looking very focused. Fear in particular seems on edge, knowing that he'll be responsible for keeping things on the outside in control. The three of them huddle in their corner discussing strategy, while the arena darkens. A static "4" burns on the SlaughterScreen, and it brings out Collins' Dark Riders. Death, Stone, War and Plague walk side by side, taking the entire width of the entrance way, flanked by armed security guards on either side. Death started to wander away from the security force, but was blocked by the police force. Stone stares maliciously at Fear as Chaos slide into the ring, ready for this Semi-Final match. Chaos take it to the Underground with hard, closed fists, pummeling both Bear and Wretch down to the mat. War tosses Bear from the ring, and then hits Wretch with a spiked piledriver. War makes the cover, but the referee refuses to count until Plague leaves the ring. The ref gets a chokeslam for his reticence, and Chaos continues punishing Wretch in the ring while Death and Stone attack Bear on the outside. Fear grabs a chair to ward off Death and Stone, and then he brings Bear back to his corner. Another referee has made it to the ring, and restores a bit of order, as Plague stays in the ring with Wretch. Plague batters him with a barrage of headbutts, a macabre smile across his face as both he and Wretch start to bleed about their foreheads. He scurries over to his corner to bring in War, who steps over the ropes before dropping an elbow on Wretch's head. He flips Wretch up onto his shoulders for a backbreaker submission hold, and starts to squeeze away. Wretch looks to be out, so the ref lifts his arm. It drops twice before Bear makes it into the ring and clips War, releasing Wretch from the hold. The ref scolds Bear and escorts him to his corner, as Chaos double teams Wretch again. Bear sees this, and struggles to get by the ref, but all he accomplishes is buying more time for Chaos. War leaves the ring, leaving Plague in there with Wretch again. He whips Wretch into the neutral corner, and stomps him down to the mat. Plague covers Wretch nonchalantly, and Wretch reverses it for two. Plague breaks out, furious, and he kicks Wretch from the ring. Fear is right there to prevent shenanigans from the other Riders, and he assists Wretch back to his feet. Wretch enters the ring in his corner, and tags in Bear. Bear hiptosses Plague over the ropes, then rushes War in the corner with a clothesline to knock him to the floor as well. Death slides into the ring and charges him with the Hardcore title, but a backdrop deposits him to the floor. Stone stands on the apron and draws Bear to her while Plague sneaks on him from behind, but Bear senses the sneak attack and knocks Plague silly with a roundhouse right. Stone grabs him from behind, choking him, until Fear cracks his chair against her back. She crumples to the arena floor, and Bear covers Plague. Plague kicks out at two, and delivers a hard elbow to stun Bear. He tags War back in, and a double team suplex follows. War powerbombs Bear, following that up by placing a foot on him for the cover. Bear easily gets a shoulder up, and brings War to the mat with a rolling ankle-lock submission. War grabs the rope to break the hold, and Bear leaps to his corner for the tag. A bloody Wretch climbs the turnbuckle and hits a hurricanrana on War for two. Both men back up, Wretch ducks under a War lariat and kicks him in the kidney. War drops to a knee, so Wretch dropkicks him in the back of his skull. He locks in a camel clutch, which War powers out of. Wretch is whipped to the ropes and gets caught with a high knee. War tags out, and Plague stomps away on Wretch. Plague DDTs Wretch and slides across him for the cover, but Wretch kicks out. Wretch crawls to his corner, but Plague grabs his leg and drags him back to the middle of the ring. An elbow drop by Plague misses as Wretch rolls out of the way, and Wretch hooks him in a La Magistral for the pinfall. The Underground have just advanced to Mob Rules, which angers the Riders to no end. As Death began to protest the security force surrounded him, insisting that he walk with them to the backstage. Result: Underground by Pinfall Threats... Slaughter returned from the commercial break and almost immediately as the show went back to live action, we heard "Do you buy what the $ellout is selling?" and out came The Hollywood Blondes. Chris Universal carried with him a briefcase with the contents remaining unknown, for now. Universal & Star appeared to be all business as the duo climbed into the ring. They paid no attention to the boos or overwhelming negative taunts from the crowd, as Universal was handed a microphone. Both men appeared to be very angry over what transpired earlier on in the night, and they seem focused on getting whatever they needed to off their chest. "We're out here for one reason and one reason only. For some reason, Travis Beaven has had his head surgically implanted up his ass and has not booked the greatest tag team to ever grace this business on the second annual Mob Rules. Well, we're about to change that. You see, we weren't booked for a match at Fallout and we all saw how ridiculously horrid that pay-per-view was. We're not about to let another pay-per-view become a disappointment. Over the last few weeks, we've formed a certain amount of respect for the fWo World Champion & his friend Ice. It's not very often when we wrestle another team and feel they are close to OR are at the same level as the Hollywood Blondes. As you've all seen we've been developing a rivalry with the French Foundation, and somehow, earlier tonight, they got LUCKY and defeated us. That will NEVER... and I mean NEVER happen again. We want a chance at payback and we want a chance at revenge and we're not going to wait for it. Now, as you all know in 6 days at Mob Rules, Slugger & Ice will be facing Frenchie, Primetime, Mitch Wilson and the Boston Strangler. Slugger & Ice have insisted that it be a four on two battle, but we think that we'll be able to change their mind. I know they're in the back, and I know they're listening. So... guys, you know what's in this briefcase. A lot of money, and you can consider this a "signing bonus" because there will be plenty more come Saturday when we step into the ring with you guys to face those French communist, non armpit shaving feminazis at Mob Rules. What we're proposing to you guys is that we'll give you a lot of money, you accept it, and you let us be on your team so we can send those ass backwards commies back to France where they belong... if you buy... what The Sellout... " before Universal could finish, "Back in Black" began to play and out walked the fWo World Champion and Ice. Both men wore smirks across their face and Slugger appeared confident. Slugger and Ice glanced at each other and spoke a few inaudible words, before Slugger held the microphone up to the World's Greatest Mouth. "You two... want to team with us?" Universal and Star nodded, and Slugger and Ice spoke to each other again, with the words remaining unheard. Slugger spoke again... "We'll think about it." Slugger and Ice then exited, and Universal was furious. Star was trying to calm him down, but Universal would not pay attention. "You're not going to make us wait, you bastards! You hear me? We don't wait for ANYTHING! You want to try us? You should learn that nobody and I mean NOBODY shuns the Blondes! You got that? Everybody has a price... and I guarantee you WILL PAY!" Star tried to calm down Universal as did several other members of security as Universal continued to rant and rave... Boston Strangler vs. Hellrazor The cameras got back from commercials to find Hellrazor already inside the ring, flexing and ready for a fight. The lights dimmed, and the Boston Strangler came out onto the entrance ramp, head down and a wide, evil grin etched over his face. The Strangler ducked between the ropes and climbed into the ring, and Hellrazor instantly attacked him. Hellrazor beat down the Strangler with strong axehandle shots to the back, and followed it up with an uppercut to the jaw. Hellrazor kicked at the leg of the Strangler until he went down on one leg, and then applied a sleeper hold in the center of the ring. The Strangler went for the ropes, and then simply broke the hold outright and chopped fiercely at the chest of Hellrazor. Hellrazor screamed and chopped back, both men trading blows inside the ring. The Strangler's knee gave out and he went down to the mat, and Hellrazor went to the ropes. He missed a drop elbow, and the Strangler quickly got to his feet and his a standing lariat. The Boston Strangler hit a back suplex, and then climbed to the second rope to execute a flying elbow drop. The Boston Strangler covered, but only got a two count. The crowd began to chant "Strangler", and the Strangler looked around at the fans with a wild, maniacal grin on his face. Hellrazor rose, and ducked a clothesline attempt, and then hit a missile drop kick. The Strangler kicked out of the cover, and came back with a heavy dropkick. Motioning up at the rafters, the Strangler picked up Hellrazor and delivered a vertical driver spike into the mat. The Strangler covered Hellrazor, and the count was made. The Strangler rolled Hellrazor from the ring and grinned at the crowd with a smile that looked evil and dangerous. The cameras quickly went backstage where Eron was screaming... Result: Boston Strangler by Pinfall Eron cheated... "Where are you going?" Eron shouted at Courage, who was leaving the arena with a bag slung over one shoulder. Courage had Jake running behind him, carrying his title and trying to grapple with several larger bags. Courage looked at the bag he was carrying, and tossed it at Jake to carry. Courage continued to walk, ignoring Eron as he signaled to the limo driver to open the door. Eron slammed the door shut that had been opened for Courage and shouting into his face. "Where are you going you coward? You've got a match with me tonight! For that title!" Courage smiled a condescending grin at Eron, and patted him on the shoulder. "You heard what Finn said... we are all free to go. So I'm going. It's a shame about your title shot and all... but that's the way the business works Eron old boy. But don't worry... I'll give you your title shot... but, well, my schedule is pretty full, so it might be awhile... a long while." Courage laughed as he and Jake got into the limo, leaving Eron fuming in the parking lot. Eron turned on one heel, cursing. He spotted Joy, handing out balloons to children. Eron walked over and smacked Joy in the head, giving him the finger as he stalked into the arena. Waru vs. Bookerman Jackhammer came to the ring, putting a steel chair near the ring and folding his arms as he sat in it, watchful for signs of trouble. Waru came to the ring, arms held above his head and ready for his match. Waru was ready to climb back up the ladder to the fWo championships, and readied himself in the ring as "Y2B" filled the screen. Bookerman walked to the ring, shouting "tonight I'm booking a victory!" Waru wanted none of this, and dove from the ring with a diving plancha to the outside, striking Bookerman with one flying shoulder. Waru rolled Bookerman back into the ring, but missed the dropped leg as Bookerman climbed to his feet and attacked with a rake to the eyes. Bookerman grabbed the head of Waru and quickly hit a swinging DDT. Bookerman climbed to the top rope, but suddenly the crowd erupted. Two large men suddenly made their way through the crowd, shoving fans out of the way as they went. A scream bellowed up from one of them, shouting "Och-aye!" Angry Angus and Man Mountain climbed over the guard rails, entering the ring. Mountain picked up Bookerman and tossed him from the ring, sending the smaller man crashing through the wooden announcing table. Angus picked up Waru and held him for a spiked piledriver, and then shoved him from the ring. Jackhammer stood, his fists raised in defense of the smaller men. Angus picked up the bell off the time keeper's table and with deadly accuracy flung it at Jackhammer's head, knocking him to the ground with a loud thud. Mountain grabbed Chris Finn and tossed him from the announcing table, threatening James Armstrong as well. Armstrong quickly fled as Mountain and Angus pulled large "cWo" banners out of a bag and hung them over the ring aprons, covering the fWo and Sunday Slaughter logos. Mountain grabbed a microphone and bellowed, "This is now OUR SHOW! Quarrymaster couldn't be here tonight, but from this moment on you are all living in fear of the men who will kick your ass! fWo, you cowards... face us!" The cameras went to the back as the two men continued to reign havoc at ringside... Result: No Contest Havok interview Geoff Green was backstage, and caught Havok walking by on his way to the dressing room. Green shouted out for Havok to give the fWo fans some comments, and Havok shrugged. "I'm new to the fWo, and I don't understand half of this crap... but I'll tell you one thing, the Black Panther impressed a lot of us tonight. The fWo has a lot of strong personalities, and many of them were running scared after this whole Spencer and Collins mess. Panther put it in perspective. Obviously everything is messed up tonight, but I'm making a challenge to David Spencer or Death or whatever that clown wants to call himself for Wednesday Warfare. Let the millennium man be the first one that painted up goon faces back in the fWo, and I'll prove to all of you that Havok is here to stay... and prove to the Black Panther that I can be a partner he can be proud of. Spencer? Bring your ass to the ring... because all havoc is going to break out on Warfare and you're going to suffer the consequences." Havok paced away as the Frenchie's music was heard... Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Stone The Frenchie came to the ring, waving at the fans and flanked by Primetime and the Boston Strangler. Inside the ring the Frenchie waved a French flag and saluted the crowd, who resoundly booed him. From outside the ring Angus and Mountain threatened the Strangler, who glared back at them. The Frenchie ignored the invaders, but also stood on the other side of the ring as he began to speak. The Frenchie held up the microphone and said, "It is ze big week for ze Foundation... ze Foundation will fight at Mob Rules, Primetime will beat ze Death for ze Hardcore title, and in one week Primetime will beat zat baseball buffon for ze World title. But all is not ze roses I fear, for tonight I may lose zis pathetic girlie title. Stone? Get ze little buttocks to ze ring and bring ze cake." Stone's music played, and Stone along with the other Dark Riders came out onto the entrance ramp. Security quickly held back Plague, War and Death, and Death cursed as they were forced backstage again. Stone walked to the ring, face red as she held a cake in one hand. Stone growled as she handed the cake up to the Frenchie, who leered at her ample cleavage that was revealed as she leaned over. The Frenchie sampled the cake, and looked surprised. "Zut! Zis is ze good cake! Primetime must try some of zis..." Primetime sample the cake, and showed his approval at the cake, giving it the thumbs up. Stone reached for the womens title, and the Frenchie jerked it back. "Non! Zis is not for you until all of ze Foundation say it is trey bien." The Frenchie handed the cake toward the Strangler, who didn't take it. The Strangler continued to glare at Mountain and Angus, and whispered only, "I'm not here to play your games Frenchie... I'm here to do a job, and eating cake isn't part of it." The Frenchie grinned, and then mocked an expression of sadness toward Stone. "Oh! I'm so sorry chere... ze Foundation has not given all ze approval so ze title is not yours. Too bad... so sorry... thanks for playing... goodbye." The Frenchie quickly rolled out of the ring with Primetime as Stone rushed the ring, looking angry and ready for a fight. The Strangler slowly backed away, never letting his eyes drop from Mountain and Angus, who now climbed into the ring... Result: Stone loses by bad cake Brawl Mountain and Angus got into the ring, and pulling a can of spray paint from their jeans began to spray "fWo SUCKS" on the canvas. Angus grabbed the microphone and yelled, "ACH-AYE!" and handed it over to Mountain who shouted, "So... none of the fWo wrestlers had the danglies to come and face us like men! Well I'm not surprised... but at Mob Rules there will be four of us and if we have to kick all of your asses then that's exactly what we will do!" Suddenly Mike Heftel ran down the ramp toward the ring, but was quickly taken out by Mountain and Angus, the later holding him down while Mountain delivered a big splash. Silver Fox and the tag team Trouble ran to the ring, but they too were quickly taken out by the angry pair. Now laughing, Mountain and Angus urged more wrestlers to come to the ring. Suddenly the Black Panther showed up on the entrance ramp, and flanked on each side stood Havok, Ultra Cool 2000, the Lone Gunmen, the Underground, the French Foundation, Cactus Jon, and Joy. The wrestlers ran to the ring, and a complete brawl broke out. Suddenly the crowd erupted as the Blondes ran to the ring, attacking the Foundation. Scott Slugger and Ice showed, and quickly went after Primetime. More wrestlers poured out of the backstage area as Slaughter went off the air, fists still flying from the center of the ring...
