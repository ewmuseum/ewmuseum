Introduction Once upon a time there was a small promotion which wanted to make a huge impact on the world of professional wrestling. Through a multitude of wrestlers, events, and personal sacrifice the fWo stood on the edge of the world and found there was no room left to climb. It was then that they began to realize that when you have climbed to close to the sun, the only way left to go... was down. Hyrule's Ascension The Codemaster. Mere words don't describe him, but that's mainly because we prefer to use sentences. The Breaker of Kingdom Hearts was in his white Nintendo singlet, not even bothering to put on his black robe and cape for this evening's event. His hands were still bandaged from last Friday's Unleashed, and while the lack of the presence of his Megaton Hammer was glaring, it was obvious that he was a man on a very important mission. We don't know what his mission is, but what we did know was the fact that the Codemaster looked every bit as annoyed and angry as he did three days ago on Monday Night RUAHH~!. Apparently, Disneyworld had already gotten old for the Codemaster. He was marching down the hallways of the Walt Disney Sports Complex, looking for someone, something, or some place to be. Finally, as he rounded the corner, he found the door he was apparently looking for and barged in. No knocking or nothing. What? He's the Codemaster. He had the right to be rude. As he entered the locker room, he pointed at the individual who occupied the room. "Hey, Mega Man, hope ya don't mind I walk in... I have this tendency to just barge into people's rooms." the Codemaster told the man who had just now stood up to get a look at the man in the white Nintendo singlet who seemed ready for combat. A guy with a striped shirt. A referee. "So, how about it, Mega Man?
