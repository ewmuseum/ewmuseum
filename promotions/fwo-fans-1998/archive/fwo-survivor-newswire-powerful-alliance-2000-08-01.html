fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE POWERFUL ALLIANCE August 1, 2000 The alliance is alive and well. Some fWo Survivor viewers had begun to speculate that with the results of the last two votes the alliance that had dominated in the early days of the contest had dissolved into a feeding frenzy of common interests and mutual enemies. When Mike Extreme was expelled, it was clear that if not for his absent vote the alliance that Mike Extreme was a part of would have voted out Juno Law. The alliance still accomplished their goal on the following round, voting Juno Law out of the contest by enlisting the aid of others under the fear that a TW alliance had formed. However, as the days followed it became clear that the TW wrestlers, Juno Law, Red Dragon, and Kodiak Vic Creed had not been voting as an alliance at all, and perhaps because of this made Juno Law an easy target. The following rounds saw votes cast for T-Bone and Ronnie Randall, both men being voted out of the contest as a result of their interview or wrestling skill, and both being voted out by considerable margins. This led some analysts to believe that the alliance had dissolved when it was clear that the TW wrestlers posed no clear threat in terms of banding together to vote, but now it appears that it was all just a ruse. This week saw the expulsion of Red Dragon, which shocked and surprised several followers of the Survivor contest. "Many people had picked Red Dragon to win the contest. His interview skills were solid, his wrestling good... as he had already won one immunity challenge. Many of us were shocked by this development." said Tom Wilson, odds maker in Las Vegas, Nevada. Others had less ambiguous things to say about the alliance. "This is a complete load of [explicative]. Today's vote has nothing to do with protecting your place, it's about a couple lousy cheaters banding together to boot someone out for kicks. The alliance members should be ashamed of themselves." said one fan on an eWrestling Usenet board. "fWo Survivor is a good idea, but it's people like this that are ruining it for future shows." Speculation runs high as to who the alliance members might be. Speculation ranges from wrestlers Suicide and Reno Davis being the core alliance, while others feel it is wrestlers Nick Allstar and Arch Angel. Some blame Kodiak Vic Creed for the Red Dragon's ousting, reasoning that if the Red Dragon and Creed had worked together in round five he might have won immunity, or at least had a better chance. The vote for the Red Dragon comes at a time when previously voted out members of Survivor have been discussing their future plans as it relates to the contest. As was explained in the Survivor rules, the final two members remaining in the Survivor contest will be voted on by the eliminated ten members of the contest to determine who is the ultimate winner. One wrestler who had previously been voted out said, "Being voted out wasn't fun... but at least I will get some satisfaction back. I know who the alliance members are because one of them approached me to try and get me to join. They pretended to be my friend, then stabbed me in the back. Needless to say, I'm going to make damn sure every eliminated wrestler knows who the alliance members are so they can stick it to them in the final round. For now, all I can say is Plague... watch you back. You too, Don." Red Dragon was eliminated while in the middle of a heated rivalry between himself and Kodiak Vic Creed. Many speculate that Creed and the Dragon's feud will continue on some level in the TW federation. Final results were as follows: Red Dragon: 5 Suicide: 2 Nick Allstar: 1 More news as it develops...
