Sunday Slaughter, 19 September 1999

19 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction fWo fans were high in attendance in the St. Louis arena, excited and ready for a night of fWo action. On the video monitors clips were shown explaining the card tonight, and the events leading up to the matches that the fans would be seeing inside the arena and at home. The Wrestler of the Year tournament reaches the quarter finals tonight with two matches that will determine the face of fWo Fallout. First, the Black Panther will attempt to maintain his winning streak as he takes on the evil Darkness. Chris Finn noted that the two have not faced each other before, and that both have been on winning streaks going into this match. Finn also noted that Darkness had demanded a rematch following the No Contest ruling at Hotter Than Hell. The second match is one of rising stars, first from the winner of Meltdown and member of the French Foundation, Primetime. Tonight he faces Wretch, who has surprised several people by making it this far into the tournament. In tag team action the Mad Trucker and Joy join forces to fight their current enemies Mike Bear and Ice. All four men have been scheduled for matches at fWo Fallout, and tonight's match will allow them to test their opponents for the first time. The fWo World Championship will be on the line as Scott Slugger faces the man he took the title from, Cactus Jon. Cactus Jon has not been seen in some time, and many have been wondering where his mind is currently at. Also, the threat of NXS still hangs in the air. Fireworks sounded, and the night offically began... Los Latin vs. Samuel Smooth Backstage Samuel Smooth was found aruging with fWo officals about the match he had signed. Still wearing a neck brace, Smooth aruged that he was in no condition to compete. While the fWo officals wondered aloud why Smooth was signed for a match, Los Latin entered the room and grabbed Smooth by the neck, delivering a mighty lariat that took the injured man off his feet. Latin then drug Smooth to the ring, with the former member of Doomsday screaming the entire way. Los Latin rolled Smooth inside the ring and climbed to the top turnbuckle. Having plenty of time given that Smooth was all but incapacitated, Los Latin saluted the crowd, and the gave them all the finger, yelling that he was about to deliver the pride of Mexico. He dove off the top rope with a moonsault, causing further pain to Smooth on the mat. Rather than go for a cover he picked Smooth up, and again yelled to the crowd, this time telling them that he was going to deliver the honor of Mexico. A spinebuster followed, and Los Latin picked Samuel Smooth up a third time. Yelling to the crowd again, Los Latin told them that he was now going to deliver the vengence of Mexico, and attacked with an inverted DDT. Now Los Latin made the cover, and withno trouble at all got the three count. Los Latin taunted the crowd, telling them that he had bested another of their finest American warriors, and that none could stand before the onslaught of mighty Mexico. Result: Los Latin by Pinfall Everybody in Straightjackets We cut to commercial from Sunday Slaughter to show a group of teenagers standing with their backs to the camera. Just then "Rapper's Delight" by the Sugar Hill Gang begins to play and the teens start to breakdance. As they squirm, wiggle, and spin about the floor, flailing away in the true idiocy of breakdancing, it becomes apparent that they're not dancing, they're strapped into straightjackets and attempting to free themselves. The song continues to play and a large teenager spins out of control, loses his balance, and knocks over an old lady who was busy rapping with a mangy dog. As the old lady's 4 inch thick glasses fall to the floor, they land in the direct glare of the hot studio lighting. This sets off a chain reaction of concentrated light beaming into a cheap backdrop, smoke, and then full blown fire. As the models scream and try to electric boogaloo their way out of the collapsing studio, The Boston Strangler steps into full view of the camera. With a wide grin on his face he says "EVERYBODY in straightjackets!", before turning to laugh hysterically at the chaos of big pants, crying, and running mascara as the dog runs by yelping, followed by the old lady who is blindly bumping into everything in her path. The screen fades to white with the words "Old Navy: Everybody in Straightjackets" before we move back to Sunday Slaughter... Joy & Mad Trucker vs. Ice & Mike Bear Happy music began to play as Joy and Glee walked to the ring and began to pass out candy to fans in the front row. Joy waited in the ring for his partner, the Mad Trucker. The Trucker came to the ring with the fWo Internet title, and looked more stern than usual after the attack on his good friends Cookie and Alice. The Trucker stood outside the ring, and as Ice appeared at the top of the entrance ramp the Mad Trucker charged, spearing him up at the top of the ramp. Ice and the Trucker began to brawl near the arena entrance as Joy watched on from the ring. He failed to see Mike Bear come out of the crowd from behind, and Bear struck quickly with a DDT from behind. Bear kicked at Joy's head, and left the ring to search for weapons. Bear grabbed a steel chair and re-entered the ring, but by this time Glee had entered and attempted to shield Joy with her body. Bear tried to toss Glee out of the way, but as he did so Joy got an uppercut in that caused Bear to drop the chair. Joy picked the chair up and swung it at Bear, but it collided instead with the head of Glee. Glee fell down as Bear used the distraction to suplex Joy, and Ice and the Trucker continued to brawl at the top of the ramp. A referee attempted to get the match in some kind of order, but was backhanded by Ice, and fell to the arena floor in a heap. Officials signaled for the double DQ as order was desperately attempted to be maintained... Result: Double DQ Challenges made for fWo SlamTasim An interview was taking place. Geoff Green stood before Rain, who was dressed in a bright silver shirt and had two scantily clad women at his side. Rain was telling Geoff Green that next week at fWo SlamTasim he would take Everybody's Friend Austin Davis to the mat, work over his neck and pin him to the mat clean before going to the hotel with all of his fine, fine hos. Geoff Green told Rain that Austin Davis had the power of love behind him, and that he would be tough to beat. Rain simply pointed at his chest and explained how he had the power of pimping behind him and that Everybody's Friend Austin Davis would be going down on his road to the fWo World Championship US Super Belt. Geoff Green nodded and the interview ended. From a distance, mostly hidden by the row of lockers, Rain took all of this in with grim concern. Things were unraveling faster than he suspected, and something had to be done soon... Black Panther vs. Darkness The Black Panther came to the ring quiet and determined. His earlier interview where he told Geoff Green that losing was not an opinion was again played as he waited inside the ring for Darkness. The lights went out, and the deadly Darkness came to the ring, looking evil and determined as he stepped over the top rope. The two locked up, but Darkness attacked with a blow to the chest and whipped the Black Panther into the ropes. Darkness hit a hard lariat and the Black Panther attempted to roll out of the ring to recover. He was caught by Darkness who placed the Panther on the apron with his neck out, climbed out of the ring, and then hit elbow smashes from the below. Black Panther came back, and took Darkness' back, attempting to put him in a cross body submission. Darkness kicked in Black Panther's knee, which had been injured during the last match the Panther had. Darkness worked on his knee, attempting to ground the wrestler. Black Panther used his other leg to kick Darkness and placed him on the top turnbuckle. The Black Panther hit a powerslam off the top and attempted a cover. The Black Panther's momentum carried him over and Darkness used this to put him in cross knee scissors. After working the leg, Darkness put the Black Panther in cross arm scissors, but Black Panther escaped to the ropes. Darkness German suplex'd the Panther, but his adrenaline was up and the Black Panther shrugged it off. Moving quickly, the Black Panther stood up and hit a Tiger suplex. Black Panther attempted a spinning heel kick but Darkness caught his leg and hit a Dragon screw. Darkness went for a cover, but Black Panther reversed it and rolled him up in a small package. Darkness escaped a moment too late, and Black Panther walked away with a victory. As this match came to a close, Primetime walked out onto the entrance ramp, holding a microphone... Result: Black Panther by Pinfall Primetime vs. Wretch Primetime addressed the crowd b saying, "So I'm facing Black Panther, huh? Well, looks like Primetime is going to go two for two at Pay Per Views, because all of you losers are looking right now at the Wrestler of the Millennium! I'm going to win that title at Fallout, I'm going to kick Nikki and Speed Demon's ass, and I'm going to take it all back to France, a country that doesn't suck like this one! Now Kidman, or Wretch, or skinny little freak, get your butt out here so I can whip your ass!" Primetime got into the ring and waited for Wretch, who walked to the ring solidly, looking more than a little nervous. When Wretch got close to the ring Primetime dove over the top rope to meet him with a diving plancha. Both men fell to the floor, and Wretch kicked up at Primetime's face to get back on the offensive. Wretch grabbed Primetime's neck to deliver a swinging neckbreaker, but Primetime hit a jaw breaker to escape. Primetime rolled Wretch into the ring, but when Primetime fell on him for the cover Wretch put his thumb into Primetime's eye. Both men stood, but Primetime held his eye where he was jabbed by Wretch. Wretch then hit a powerslam and went to the top rope. Attempting a diving splash, Primetime rolled out of the way at the last second and Wretch hit only the mat. Primetime nailed a diving clothesline, a covered for a two count. Frustrated, Primetime went to the second rope and executed a diving elbow drop, but the cover again only got a two count. Picking Wretch up, Primetime shouted that he was going to hit the move that he made famous, and executed a perfect neck breaker suplex. Primetime went for another cover, but Wretch reversed it in an inside cradle for a two count. Both men stood, and Wretch hit a standing lariat. Wretch drop-kicked Primetime and executed a somersault drop before climbing to the top turnbuckle. Primetime stood and grabbed Wretch, aiming a superplex but Wretch blocked and kicked the face of Primetime. Primetime fell back, and Wretch quickly hit a diving clothesline. Covering him in a crucifix, Primetime kicked out a second too late and the match was awarded to Wretch. Primetime looked at him in disbelief, and made a threat to Wretch that he wouldn't ever forget this day... Result: Wretch by Pinfall fWo: The Music Volume 2 Slaughter went to an ad for the upcoming fWo: The Music Volume two. Shots were shown of the entrances of several fWo superstars, as the release date for the collection was broadcast: October 5th. Matt Loco went over the track list for the compact disc and the cassette, which includes themes for Primetime, Kid Cool, Eron, Rain, Joy, Crucifix, Lance Star and many more. The disc has a total of 19 tracks, and is expected to once again sell very well. Scott Slugger (c) vs. Cactus Jon Cactus Jon came to the ring with the Canadian flag raised above him, and got a strong reaction from the crowd... with the exception of one man, sitting in the third row. It was Los Latin, and he booed the Canadian superstar and waved the flag of Mexico in return. Jon ignored this, and went to the ring, focused on the match at hand. Scott Slugger emerged with the fWo World Title over his shoulder and none the worse for wear despite the brawl that marked the end of Hotter Than Hell IV. Scott Slugger started this match with two quick clotheslines that took Cactus Jon off guard. Standing up he offered a test of strength. The two grappled, and Cactus Jon turned the hold to his advantage and applied a sleeper hold. Slugger broke free, and Cactus Jon used the ropes to lock in a juji-gatame. Slugger broke free of this and tumbled to the outside. Cactus Jon followed, and Scott Slugger drove him into the steel guard rail. Jon got some revenge by driving Scott Slugger's arm into the iron ringpost, and then his head, busting the champion's forehead open. Rolling Slugger back in the ring, Cactus Jon came up next and was rewarded by a series of chops from the champion. Slugger rose Jon up for a vertical suplex, but got a one count only on the cover. Slugger argued with the ref, and Cactus Jon hit a Northern-lights suplex, then a juji-gatame on the distracted champion. The two brawled around the ring and then went outside again, where Cactus Jon hit some stiff chairshots on Slugger. The two went back to the ring where Scott Slugger hit a belly-to-back suplex in the ring. Slugger followed this up with a clothesline. Slugger held his arm that had been driven into the steel, and flexed it in an attempt to get some feeling back. Slugger didn't see Jon get to his feet, and the challenger hit three missile drop kicks in a row, and then a Northern-lights suplex. Jon made a confidant cover, but Slugger kicked out at two once more. Jon got to his feet and hit four successive missile drop kicks and rolled up Slugger, but the champion kicked out a hair before the three count. Cactus Jon then climbed to the top rope, but Scott Slugger caught him on the way up and delivered a suplex from the top rope that brought Cactus Jon down to the mat directly on his head. Jon lay stunned as Scott Slugger got the three count. Slugger left the ring victorious, announcing that he would have a big challenge on Warfare. The show ended with clips from Hotter Than Hell Four, and a brief preview of Fallout... Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall
