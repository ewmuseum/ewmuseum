Sunday Slaughter, 4 March 2001

4 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction A large crowd had gathered in the office of Chris Universal. All of them wore black fWo logo t-shirts and jeans the unofficial uniform of the fWo security officer. Standing at the front of the group, holding his head, was John Willis. "You alright, John?" inquired the snazzily dressed Chris Universal from behind his desk. "Yeah, I'm OK boss. Just got a bit of a headache." "And that's more or less exactly why we're here. Somebody's been making attacks ever since Body Count. As a result, I want you guys to be extra careful from now on. Call in backup on anything that looks suspicious. Just... watch your backs, OK? Let's find this guy and put a stop to it, OK?" Some of the group members nodded. Others voiced their assent. "Alright. Let's go, guys. Good luck," finished Universal. The security officers left, and Universal leaned back in his leather chair. "Let's see... Eraser is on tonight, isn't it? Yeah, yeah it is. Now where'd I put that remote?" Universal scanned the room and saw nothing. Shrugging, he opened up a drawer on his desk. "Son... of a bitch." Universal reached inside and pulled out not a remote, but a steel bar. The trademark weapon of the person behind the attacks. Universal gazed at it for a moment, then threw it angrily aside. Sphere vs. Mike Heftel "Back To School" by the Deftones hits the speakers, and Sphere comes out onto the entrance ramp. He gets a not-so-favorable reaction as he makes his way down the entrance ramp. He hops in the ring and awaits his opponent for the Internet Title Tournament, who happens to be Mike Heftel. As the announcers remind the public of this, Scatterbrain replaces the Deftones, and Heftel walks out to a decent response. A bit surprised by this, he begins to...well, to "Hulk up". He takes off for the ring, and gets ready to slide into the ring. However, the plans are changed when he trips on a piece of carpet, and flies through the ropes onto the mat. The fans, knowing that he did try his best there, continue to applaud. However, Sphere, who sees this unfolding, decides that the best move here is an ankle lock submission maneuver. He locks it in o Heftel, who immediately begins to moan in pain. He seems ready to tap out when the bell rings; however, once the bell does finally ring, Sphere lets go, and allows Heftel to get up. Sphere begins to walk towards the ropes, with his back turned towards Heftel. Heftel, being a bit skeptical at first, lays low. Then, he decides that Sphere really will let him up, and he begins to do so. It's about that time that Sphere leans up against the ropes, and charges Heftel with a bulldog. Heftel, however, looks down at a nickel he dropped on the floor, and leans down to pick it up. Sphere totally misses his head, and flies across the ring. Heftel, completely not noticing this, looks at where Sphere was, and scratches his head. He takes a look at where Sphere IS, and is puzzled. He looks where he was again, and now where he is. It's possible that he flew, Heftel thinks to himself. However, how could he get the mechanism on his body? What in the world is he using to propel himself in the air. Why, it could be possible that he gives new meaning to the term "high-flyer". Sphere gets up, meanwhile, and walks over to Heftel, who's very deep in thought by this time. "Hey, did you drop this quarter?" Sphere asks, holding out his hand. Heftel breaks his train of thought, and looks into the hand, which is empty. "There's no..." Heftel begins to answer. However, he's cut off by a kick to the gut from Sphere, followed up with a DDT. Sphere rolls Heftel over and covers him. At two, Heftel kicks out, which irritates Sphere. Sphere decides to give Heftel a bit more to think about, and so he tries to lock Heftel into a sharpshooter. But Heftel, who isn't partial to that particular move, quickly begins to kick his way out of it. Sphere turns it into a figure-four leglock, something that Heftel doesn't seem to mind as much as the sharpshooter. However, the pain is very intolerable, and Heftel doesn't seem to enjoy it. He's on the verge of tapping out when he flails his arms about in pain...and grabs hold of the ropes. The ref orders the hold broken, and so Sphere does, after a five-second count. Heftel struggles to get himself back on his feet. While he does, Sphere calculates his next move. Heftel begins to back away from the ropes, when Sphere charges past him, springboards off the second rope, and nails a springboard spear. This move sends Heftel sprawling to the mat. Sphere, in the mean time, covers him. At two and a half, Heftel manages to kick out, which infuriates Sphere, who felt that move should have finished him. Nontheless, he decides that Heftel hasn't received enough punishment. Sphere points to the top rope, and the fans boo as he does so. Showing that he could care less about what they think, he does it anyway. He climbs to the top, and lines Heftel up for a moonsault. He takes flight, and nails the move. He contemplates another cover, but decides to end it with the Spinal Retraction instead. He sets Heftel up, and nails it. A cover and three seconds later, the match is over, and Sphere moves on in the tournament. He throws his hands up to the crowd, who reacts negatively to him. Sphere simply shrugged it off and gestured for a microphone. One was thrown to him, and he caught it deftly. "Ah yes, another victory for the Spherester! Y'know, life sure is good for me... I'm in the quarterfinals of the Internet Title tournament, I'm bitchslapping the STUMP Squad at will, Claire still wants me... things couldn't be going much better, could they?" As he spoke, Black Quicksilver and STUMP began to make their way to the ring to help Heftel. As they approached, Sphere took notice of them. "Look... it's the brainless inanimate object... and his STUMP!" Quicksilver rolled Heftel out of the ring and started towards the back. "Man... I haven't seen so many horses' asses since the last time I went to the zoo!" On the ramp, Quicksilver stopped. He turned back towards Sphere and shouted something at him. As he did so, Heftel whispered in his ear. Adept lip-readers could make out BQ saying, "You're right... it would help if I had a microphone, wouldn't it?" BQ sprinted back towards the ring, took a mic from a ring attendant, and then sprinted back to Heftel as Sphere shook his head in the ring. Heftel whispered in his ear again. "You're right again... I could've just done it from there," said BQ to Heftel. Then he shrugged and lifted the mic to his lips. "Hey! Stupid! First off, they don't have horses at the zoo... those are zebras. And second, I'm getting pretty damn sick and tired of you. As far as I'm concerned the fWo would be a better place with you massively injured, so I'm challenging you right here to a No-DQ match at Dead Or Alive! You got the little Spheries?" Sphere chuckled. "You better believe it, boy. You're on... No-DQ, Dead Or Alive. I'll see you there, pal." He tossed down the microphone and exited the ring before heading backstage a good distance from the STUMP Squad as "Slave To The Grind" played. Slaughter, meanwhile, went backstage... Result: Sphere by pinfall The Rose Matt Loco sat across from his prot Black Quicksilver vs. Frank Snow "Slave to the Grind" echoes throughout the arena and out walks Black Quicksilver with STUMP in hand to cheers. After making it down to the ring, he places STUMP in the corner and stares down the ramp, awaiting his opponent for the Internet title tournament match. As "Whatever" by Godsmack hits the speakers, Frank Snow appears from behind the curtains and the crowd erupts. Reaching the ring, Snow steps above the top rope. Knowing everything that's on the line, BQ wastes no time introducing himself to his opponent, as he flies off the ropes and nails Snow with a flying forearm. Reeling back, Snow has to grab the ropes to keep himself in the ring. Not wanting to lose his momentum, BQ steps back and nails Snow with a stand-sidekick. Snow falls over the top rope and hits the floor hard, where he lays motionless. With the opportunity in front of him so early in the match, BQ takes a gamble. He goes for the somersault plancha. The gamble doesn't pay off. Snow manages to stumble to his feet and dive out of the way, leaving BQ with nothing to hit except a 2 inch thick mat, which did little to protect him from the cold, hard concrete underneath. With the advantage in his corner, Snow wastes no time throwing his smaller opponent in the ring, where he still lays in pain, gripping his back. Reaching his feet as Snow re-enters the ring, BQ moves back. But not in time. Snow pummels him with his razor-like fists. He beats BQ down to his knees, where a hard blow to the head knocks the smaller man over. Picking BQ up, Snow attempts to toss him into the ropes but BQ reverses it, surprisingly, Snow re-reverses it and BQ goes flying into the ropes. Standing firm, Snow nails his opponent with a thunderous clothesline, which the fans "ooh" at, as Quicksilver is flipped in the air and falls harshly on the mat. Kicking his opponent in the midsection, Snow decides he's weak enough for a leg drop. It turns out to be a big mistake, as BQ rolls out of the way, and Snow yelps in pain from the impact. Both men fight to get to their feet first, but it turns out to be an even battle, as the two get to their feet at nearly exactly the same time. Frank Snow takes to the offensive first and lays in a few punches on his feisty opponent, but BQ fights back with the strength of a man twice his size. Going toe to toe, it's evident that BQ won't be able to last as long as the bigger man, and he quickly realizes it too. Nailing Snow stomach, BQ backs off, as Snow lurches forward. With his opponent crouched over, BQ delivers a quick dropkick which snaps Snow's head back with a huge amount of force. The resilient Snow manages to stir a bit, even after such a devastating move. Helping Snow to his feet, BQ throws him in the corner to set him up for something special. Handspring elbow time. The crowd's excitement is evident as BQ's elbow nails Snow in the head, and Snow thumps against the canvas. BQ follows that up by kicking away at Snow which gets the referee to intercede. Snow gets time to recuperate as the referee chastises BQ. Snow reaches over the referee to smack the unsuspecting BQ who careens back in surprise. The ref squirms out of the way, as Snow's size and strength advantage begins to show. Throwing punches like there's no tomorrow, Snow does his best to bash BQ's skull in. Picking up a limp BQ, Snow hits a hard suplex which rocks the canvas. Sensing the end is near, Snow goes for a cover. A quick kickout at two stops Frank Snow from advancing on in the Internet Title Tournament. Feeling the chance at the title within his grasp, Snow scales the turnbuckle. He goes for the Snowfall, but as he's set to go airborne BQ staggers to his feet grabs the ropes and shakes them hard. Snow falls straight down crushing a part of him no man wants harmed. His face doesn't hide nearly unbearable pain, as BQ gets back to his feet. Getting Snow to his feet, BQ leaves him there as he climbs the turnbuckle. Wasting little time, he nails a super Cubic Zirconium Cutter and covers Snow. Skid Row plays to signify the end of the match, as Snow lies with his back on the mat. Result: Black Quicksilver by pinfall Identity Crisis At first, Chris Universal didn't believe his eyes. Striding through his door were two MUDs and a Toad. Well, not really striding one MUD was unconscious and being dragged by the other. Universal rubbed his eyes. Yeah, this was real. "Rain?!" he called out hesitantly. No answer, as expected. "Oh well... so much for that semi-obscure joke. What can I do for you, guys?" A MUD stepped forward. "This bastard isn't me." Universal looked from MUD to MUD. "Yes... I noticed." "No, what I mean is he's saying he's me, but he's not. This guy you've been running out on your shows and calling him MUD." Universal shrugged. "And I'm guessing that's not entirely correct, correct?" "Yeah." "So what do you propose I do about it? And aren't you dead?" Universal gestured to Toad. "N-n-no, not r-really." "Oh. OK." "What I want, Universal, is a blood test. You test me, you test him. Then you'll see what I mean." Universal turned this over in his head. What the hell, it couldn't hurt. "OK, sure. I'll set something up for you guys tomorrow, I'll accompany you two to the tests, and in a conveniently convenient coincidence, the results should probably arrive to me in time for next week's Slaughter." "Alright. I'll be waiting. Let's go, Toad." Toad and a MUD turned and left the room, dragging the other MUD behind him. "Huh. Well, that was bizarre, wasn't it?" Boston Strangler vs. Vengence "The Beast" signals the entrance of a beast in his own right, the 6' 10" monster known as Vengence. Vengence heads straight down to the ring, an icy glare in his eye and a chip on his shoulder. A loud, but unmistakably mixed, reaction welcomes the other competitor as "O Fortuna" fills the arena and the Boston Strangler makes his way down the ramp, sizing up the larger Vengence. He climbs in and a stare down ensues, continuing even after the bell rings--these two enigmatic men have a history here in the fWo, after all. The Strangler and Vengence circle one another before Vengence simply reaches in and rakes the eyes of the Strangler. Vengence looks to capitalize, swinging a powerful clothesline the Strangler's way. The Strangler manages to duck behind the swing, grabbing Vengence in a rear waistlock and pushing him into the ropes before rolling him up in a pinning predicament. The Strangler almost gets the best of the larger man with the roll up, but Vengence manages to power out at two. Vengence is up quick and looks angry.
