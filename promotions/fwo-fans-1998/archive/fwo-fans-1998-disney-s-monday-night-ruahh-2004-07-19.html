Introduction Previously... Famished Brand Frontier stood outside the office of Gary Schroeder. He paced restlessly, awaiting the arrival of the general manager. After the events of the last few weeks, especially since the last Unleashed, Frontier itched to get in the ring and vent some frustration. "Mr. Frontier, you're waiting to see me?" The jittery voice echoed a bit in the near empty hallway. Brand turned to see Gary finally arriving, small entourage in tow, a bundle of coffee stained papers in one hand, a large mug in the other. "Yes sir, I want a match tonight. I'm tired of being booked for the big events and being left off television otherwise. Put me against someone tonight, it doesn't matter who. I need to get in the ring. Last week I wasn't even scheduled to be on the show, hardly anyone was. Last week's card was an embarrassment to the company, and you know it. You need to do you're job and take your head out of Juan Valdez' ass." Frontier seemed rather perturbed and as Gary is known to do seceded to Brand's demands. "Fine Brand... fine." He took a sip of his ever-present liquid diet before continuing, "You pick who you want, as long as they're not booked, they're yours." "Anyone?" "Anyone." "Then I want Death." The spit take was priceless. "D...Death... again? Why in the hell would you want him again?" Gary rolled his eyes. "Because it's not finished. Even with your limited exposure to the business Mr. Schroeder you should be able to tell that." Gary countered, "I... well, what I meant was, you can have anyone in the ring tonight but him." Brand frowned. Before he could counter though Schroeder's office door exploded outwards and out came the Dark Riders themselves. All three of the evil bastards had been laying in wait for Frontier all this time. Donaven Winters, Pestilence, was the first to make contact, driving the surprised hero to the floor. Death followed close behind with a series of kicks as Primetime threw the general manager out of the way. Once Frontier had the wind knocked out of him he was pulled to his feet by both Pestilence and Famine. "You don't deserve to be in the ring with me Frontier. You failed at Extreme Measures and you've failed every time you've had the chance to stand against me face to face. Paladin was right in one thing, there is no one that can stop us, not without becoming us in the process... either way hero, we will always be." Brand spit in the face of Death, not the first time he had done so either. All it did this time though, was raise the ire of the dark haired embodiment of evil. The former WGA struck out with a hammering right hand, straight in to the temple of the captive hero. "Insolent dog..." Death wiped at his face, "Do it." He instructed his minions. With those two simple words, Winters lifted Frontier up, kicking him in the stomach in the process and tucking his head under his arm. Primetime lifted Brand's legs and added to the momentum as his head thudded against the concrete thanks to the implant DDT. Brand convulsed on the floor as Primetime got on all fours in front of the paragon. He taunted the hero, calling out words that threw the censor button into a frenzy. Death stood back and grinned as Winters continued the assault, driving a knee in to Frontier's ribs over and over, recalling the damage Brand had sustained to them over the winter months against Vic Creed. Frontier's eyes bulged as he rolled to his side. Death finally motioned the two to back away from his nemesis, stepping over the downed Gary Schroeder as he did. "This will end tonight hero. Just like you wanted... but not. Like I said pup, you had your chance at me, now your soul will starve, now you will be left an empty husk as Famine deconstructs you in the ring..." The three Dark Riders looked down, both literally and figuratively, at the man called Brand Frontier, laughing at the damage done as the show traveled to the opening montage. Give 'em hell, kid Black Quicksilver, fWo World Heavyweight Champion, had just entered the building. He headed straight for the room where tonight's card was posted. Yes, while some matches are made *during* the show, there are still a few that get signed *before* it goes on the air. He skimmed down the board, and saw what the last match was. World Title. Black Quicksilver. vs. High Flyer. vs. Alias. He nodded, then turned around and left the room. He went to find his locker room, as tonight's match would definitely prove to be a challenge heading into Meltdown... Dance Dance Revolution- The favorite rhythm game of angry minori Once again, Eternity's left foot was on the bottom rope- this time, however, it was only because X had recovered in time to put it there. The referee only saw her foot on the rope and stopped his count just before three. Aimz, who had seen the entire thing, was obviously less than thrilled. Again, showing her newfound aggressiveness, the Red Raver stood up and got in the face of the referee, pointing angrily out at X and yelling at him. She didn't notice Eternity getting back to her feet, but she did hear her bouncing off the ropes behind her, and Aimz stepped to the side just in time to avoid a flying clothesline by Eternity. The referee, however, was not so lucky, and was wiped out by the flying clothesline from the Cruiserweight Champion. Eternity turned around just in time to take a boot to the stomach and a DDT, dropping her headfirst to the canvas. She leaned over and hooked Eternity's leg this time for the cover, but of course there was no referee to make the count. Angrily, Aimz got back to her feet, and walked over to the downed referee, leaning over and attempting to revive him. She turned away from the downed referee just in time to duck a clothesline attempt by X, then catch him with a standing side kick to the chin, knocking him down. Aimz pointed down at him then raised her arms in the air to a huge ovation....before she was turned around and nearly had her head taken off by the third and final member of the Avenging Angels, the seven-foot-tall Exel Pure. Yes, the numbers had finally caught up to the Red Raver. Pure picked the dazed Aimz up to her feet, taunting her as he put one massive hand around her throat. Before he could make good on any of his threats, however, Jacob Winters came running down the rampway. He grabbed the fWo Cruiserweight Title belt the referee had kicked to the outside earlier, and slid into the ring with it. Pure noticed him and dropped Aimz just in time to turn right around into a leaping shot with the belt by Winters, wiping out the seven-footer. Winters then turned to see Eternity getting back to her feet, and ran forward, slamming her own title belt right into her masked face, dropping her to the canvas. Aimz climbed back to her feet as well.....just in time to see the referee call for the bell before collapsing again. Aimz looked at Winters, and beginning to piece things together, realized the referee had seen his interference.....but not the interference of the Angels. She had just lost this match, via disqualification. Winters dropped the title belt and raised his hands in defense as Aimz walked towards him. The Red Raver seemed receptive to his words, even thankful that he had come down to the ring to save her from the 3-on-1 assault of the Avenging Angels. At least, until she swiftly kicked him in the gut and then dropped him head-first onto the Cruiserweight Title belt with a DDT. Now, the fans were giving a much different reaction to Aimz as she raised her arms in the air. Instead of all cheers, it was a far more mixed reaction. But it didn't matter. Because on this night, Aimz had shown the entire world that, from now on, no one was allowed to stand in her way. No one. Aimz vs. Jacob Winters "Blue" by Yoko Kanno picked up as the Crush of the Week faded from view, bringing out the former Cruiserweight Champion Jacob Winters. So new to the fWo and having already gotten a brief taste of fWo gold, Jacob wanted a shot to put the Cruiserweight Title back around his waist. To do that, however, he'd have to go through a very focused Red Raver.....and he knew that wasn't going to be an easy task. However, the fact that he had revenge on his mind after Aimz laid him out after he came to her aide last week was certainly going to help motivate him, and that DDT remained on his mind as he climbed up the apron and stepped through the ropes. He raised his arms in the air as he entered the ring, drawing a decent pop, then awaited the arrival of his opponent. "Thoughtless" by Evanescence soon replaced Blue, bringing on the red lights and a loud reaction from the fans. However, unlike most times in the past, the reaction was decidedly more mixed than it had ever been- it was obvious to the fans that Aimz was showing them a new side of her, and it wasn't a side that they all necessarily liked or respected. The reaction of the crowd was the last thing on Aimz's mind as she made her way down to the ring, the same focused, intense look that had been on her face the previous week. Aimz knew in her mind that she was ready for this, ready to take down Eternity and put some fWo gold back around her waist at Meltdown. What she wasn't ready for, however, was when Jacob Winters suddenly ran backwards, bounced forwards, then leaped through the ropes and crashed into Aimz with a tope just when she reached the bottom of the rampway! The crowd let off a surprised cheer as Jacob slowly picked himself up before picking Aimz up and dropping her throat-first across the barricade. Jacob was out to show that he was every bit as aggressive and focused as Aimz tonight, and he had done a good job of doing just that so far. He picked Aimz up and tossed her into the ring, then followed in after her as the referee called for the bell. Jacob remained on the attack, quickly bouncing off the ropes and catching Aimz with a seated dropkick right to the face as she sat up. He hooked the leg behind her for a pinfall attempt. ONE! TWO! Aimz, however, was out of that pinfall attempt just as quick. When both of them stood back up, she ducked another kick attempt by Winters, before catching him with a hard kick right to the jaw! Jacob collapsed to the canvas, then backed up into the corner. Aimz wasn't going to relent, however, and followed him in, before rocking his upper-body with several hard kicks. She kept at it before picking Winters all the way up and then Irish whipping him into the opposite turnbuckle with all the strength she could muster, nearly falling over as she did it. It paid off, as Winters' upper chest hit the top turnbuckle hard. He felt all the wind in his body get knocked out of him as he stumbled backwards.....right into an Aimz school boy for her first pinfall attempt of the match. ONE! TWO! Winters kicked out of the pinfall attempt, while still trying to regain his ability to breathe. This pin attempt was a lot closer than the last, but again Winters kicked out before the count of three. Aimz stayed on him and leaped onto his shoulders as he stood up. She then leaped forwards and took him down with her, into an inside cradle. ONE! TWO! THR-NO! Another close one for Winters, but again he managed to escape the pinfall attempt just before the count of three. This time, when they both got back up to their feet it was Aimz who tried a standing sidekick, but it didn't pay off as Winters managed to duck underneath it. Aimz turned around just in time to see Winters bounce off the ropes and then level her with a hard flying lariat! With Aimz down, Winters climbed to the top rope, trying to finish her off while he still could, and leaped off with a 450 Splash..... .....that missed, as Aimz rolled out of the way. All Winters got was canvas, and he landed hard on the mat, smacking it with his upper body and face. As he raised his head in pain, Aimz bounced off the ropes and caught him with a seated dropkick to the face, paying him back for the earlier one, that appeared to knock him out for good. She turned over the motionless Winters and hooked his leg for a cover. ONE! TWO! THR-NO! An angry Aimz stood up and shoved the referee down, obviously not happy at his call of the rope break. The referee yelled at her as she turned around....and was met with a forearm to the sternum. Jacob Winters was on his knees, and was attempting to fight back to his feet. He threw more forearms until Aimz was reeling backwards, and leaning over. He hooked her leg for a cover as soon as they landed, and the referee, perhaps a little pissed from getting shoved down by the overly aggressive Raver, seemed more than willing to give him a faster count than usual. ONE! TWO! THR-NO! Even with the fast count, however, Aimz wouldn't stay down, and thrusted her shoulder up just before three. Winters smacked his hands on the mat in anger, then looked at the referee, who didn't have much to say other than shrug. The angry Winters picked Aimz up.....and got hit with a low blow for his troubles. The ref admonished Aimz, who ignored him. Before she could follow up on the move, however, her eyes caught a pair of suspicious characters running down the rampway. Namely, the masked servants of the fWo Cruiserweight Champion- X and the seven-foot monster Exel Pure. Aimz walked forward just as X slid in the ring, and tried to catch him off-guard with some stomps to the back of his head. Winters was back up and attempted to fight as well, catching Pure with a standing enziguri, but as Pure stumbled backwards X turned Winters around and hooked him for the Exploder Suplex '98, dropping him right on his head and neck to the boos of the crowd. X and Pure turned to the referee, who just backed up and rolled out of the ring, soon out of sight. It appeared this match wasn't going to continue. With both of her challengers down, finally the fWo Cruiserweight Champion emerged from the back. Pure and X just looked up at her from inside the ring, then turned back to the two challengers. Pure lifted Aimz back to her feet, then placed one hand around her throat and dropped her to the canvas with a vicious chokeslam. The eyes of Deacon opened and quickly squinted from the light pouring in from a bright halogen lamp. For nearly a week now, the former fWo Internet Heavyweight Champion had been the subject of a silent kidnapping case in Orlando, Florida. That same amount of time, the Mute Freak had also lost his sense of sight. The powerful rays emitted from the halogen lamp easily overpowered any vision his mind would attempt to piece together. Currently, that was another problem. Deacon's mind. It did not feel normal, heavily altered...acute, but sedated. "I will never make you suffer for the sins of history. Only for the sin of pride, for it is ugly and self-righteous." Elijah tried to locate where the voice was coming from, but its tone resembled a vinyl record being played slowly. The Egyptian knew he was drugged, and started to realize he was bound to a cold table as well. The surface metallic and sending chills along his bare back. He felt a hand grab ahold of his... "But for now Elijah, I need to know the truth..." Elijah felt something injected into his forearm... "I want you to think long ago...before you became a man...to when you were a child..." Deacon's world started fading into a black, sleepless state. He felt himself losing inhibition, all control and discipline leaving his body. "Who... the words were a struggle for Elijah. "...Craig, is t'at you?" The question lingered for a moment, Deacon's world becoming a darker shroud each moment. Finally, Elijah could make something out of the distorted soundwaves entering his ear. Quiet laughter. "Yes." Deacon revealed himself moreso than he could consciously reveal his own past to Chris Shepherd himself. Xias (c.) vs. 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs "Ring Superstar" cued up, bringing the fans on their feet with boos for the man about to make his entrance. The Ratings Grabber looked focused as he climbed into the ring, ready to challenge for fWo gold once again. The sirens of "Ironside" cut off "Ring Superstar" as the lights dimmed and then flashed. Xias walked up the steps and entered the ring, all the while continuing the staredown with his opponent. The ref handed the belt off to a ringside attendant and called for the bell, officially starting this match. The three circled each other, waiting to see who would make the first move. Flyer lunges forward and nails BQ with a forearm, then goes for a back elbow on Alias. Alias ducks and chops Flyer, but is then kicked in the gut by BQ. BQ follows it up with a forearm, then turns around and chops Flyer. The champ grabs Flyer and goes for an Irish whip, but Flyer reverses it. Flyer ducks down as BQ hits the ropes, but the masked man puts on the brakes and holds onto the ropes. He didn't count on Alias charging over and clotheslining him over the top rope, however. BQ landed feet-first on the apron, holding onto the ropes so he wouldn't fall to the floor. Flyer stands up and charges at BQ, but the champion ducks down and connects with a shoulderblock to Flyer's midsection. BQ slingshots back into the ring, somersaulting over Flyer's back, and finds himself on the receiving end of an Alias big boot. Alias doesn't have much time to celebrate, though, as Flyer nails him with a spinning heel kick. All three eventually make it to their feet...Flyer still holding his stomach and the other two a bit dizzy. Flyer and Alias both go to chop BQ at the same time, but the champion ducks, turns around and leaps into the air, throwing a front dropkick, each foot connecting with one of his opponent's chests. Both hit the mat, and Flyer rolls out of the ring. BQ pulls Alias to his feet and unloads on him with forearms before attempting an Irish whip. Alias reverses it and sends BQ toward the ropes, but BQ ends up flying over the top with a somersault plancha instead...right onto Flyer. Both men are down on the arena floor, and it isn't long before Alias leaves the ring. He walks right over to BQ and picks him up, whipping him into the guardrail with such force that the masked man goes flying right into the crowd. Alias then picks Flyer up and whips him into the steel steps, before rolling him back into the ring. Alias followed him back in and picked him up, only to put him back down with a bodyslam. Alias made sure Flyer was in position and headed up top, where he flew off with the Glasgow Kiss. Alias held his own head for a few seconds, then went for the cover, but Flyer managed to kick out after two. All this while, BQ has climbed back over the guardrail and is making his way toward the ring. He climbs up on the apron as Alias picks Flyer up and whips him into the ropes. Alias catches Flyer and lifts him up, then falls back, dropping the former U.S. Champion throat-first across the top rope. Flyer collapses to the mat and rolls out of the ring, holding his throat, while Alias turns over and goes to get up, but he can't see BQ slingshotting himself back into the ring. The champ flies through the air and catches Alias with an Oklahoma Roll, getting a two-count. BQ lifts Alias to his feet, hitting him with several forearms and knife-edged chops before attempting another Irish whip. Alias manages to stop his momentum and turn around, nailing BQ with a hard chop of his own. He then rocks the masked man with a few European uppercuts before whipping him toward the ropes. Once again, BQ sees Flyer on the outside, so he attempts a baseball slide, but Flyer sees him coming this time, so he simply sidesteps the move. BQ ends up sliding underneath the bottom rope, and he finds himself standing right in front of Flyer. Flyer, not wasting any time, goes for a right hand, but BQ blocks it and hits Flyer with a right hand of his own. He then jumps up on Flyer's shoulders, looking for a hurricanrana, but Flyer stands his ground and manages to powerbomb BQ right on the arena floor. Flyer re-enters the ring and ducks an Alias clothesline, grabbing the Original Pulp Hero in a waistlock. He runs Alias into the ropes, and lets go, backflipping into the middle of the ring as Alias holds onto the ropes. Alias charges, but Flyer leapfrogs over him and drops down. Alias hits the ropes, hops over Flyer, and hits the ropes again. By this time, Flyer's already hopped up to his feet, and Alias is easy prey for a hurricanarana. Flyer quickly makes the cover, but only gets a two-count. Flyer pulls Alias to his feet and hooks him for Hypothermia, but Alias manages to fight out. He grabs Flyer by the wrist and pulls him in, going for a short-arm clothesline, but Flyer ducks, goes behind Alias, turns him around, scoops him up, and drives him down with the FlyerDriver. During this exchange, BQ had rolled back into the ring, and seeing that Alias was down and Flyer was in the midst of getting to his feet, he climbed to the second turnbuckle, right behind Flyer. He waits for Flyer to turn around, and then jumps off the turnbuckle, catching Flyer with a swinging DDT that gets two. All three men are down, trying desperately to pull theirselves together and continue the match. They all start to get up at the same time, and BQ is the first to make a move, attempting to clothesline Flyer. Flyer ducks and turns toward BQ. It looks like he's going to go for a clothesline of his own, but Alias comes from behind with a running big boot, nailing him in the back of the head. Flyer stumbles forward, walking right into the Wasted Years. BQ covers, but only gets a two-count before Alias grabs him by the leg and pulls him off. BQ gets up and immediately goes for a kick, but Alias catches his foot. BQ then goes for an enziguri, but Alias ducks. Still, BQ lands on his feet, but Alias turns him around, hooks him, then takes him over with Pulped. He makes the cover, but High Flyer breaks it up with an axhandle at the count of two. Alias, obviously not too happy with the fact his pinfall was broken up, gets to his feet and throws a knee into Flyer's midsection, then whips him into the corner. He picks BQ up and whips him into Flyer, then charges in as BQ stumbles out, nearly taking the champion's head off with a lariat. Alias keeps running, squashing Flyer in the corner with an avalanche. Alias sends Flyer into the corner across the ring, but Flyer manages to leap onto the second turnbuckle, then jumps off, twisting around and looking for a cross-body, but Alias sees him coming and turns the maneuver into a gutbuster. Alias makes the cover, but BQ is able to break it up after two. BQ pulls Alias up and hooks him for a suplex, but it gets blocked. Alias then goes for a suplex of his own, turning BQ upside down, but BQ manages to get free and lands behind Alias. He hits the ropes as Alias turns around, then Alias ducks down. BQ executes a sunset flip, but Alias keeps rolling backwards and ends up on his feet. BQ hops up to his feet and gets in a kick to the midsection, followed by several kicks to Alias' ribs and legs. Finally, BQ hooks Alias for a suplex again, and this time he's able to get him up. During the entire last exchange, Flyer had climbed up to the top rope, and once he saw BQ turn toward him, holding Alias up in the air, he leaps off, connecting with a cross-body on Alias. Flyer stays on for the cover, but he only gets two before BQ breaks it up. BQ and Flyer trade blows in the center of the ring, and Flyer manages to get the upperhand, sending BQ into the ropes with an Irish whip. BQ comes back and Flyer goes for a tilt-a-whirl, but the champion lands on his feet and goes for a clothesline. Flyer ducks, grabs BQ, and hits the Lunatic Bomb. He makes the cover, but Alias pulls him off after a two-count. Alias pulls Flyer to his feet and goes for an Irish whip, but Flyer reverses it, sending Alias into the ropes and nailing him with a dropkick. Flyer turns his attention back to BQ, picking him up off the mat and putting him in the Gory Special. The ref checks to see if BQ wants to submit as Alias gets up. He goes over to Flyer and kicks him in the chest, trying to get him to release the hold. Flyer keeps it on, so Alias kicks him again. Flyer still has BQ in the hold, so Alias goes for another kick, only to have Flyer turn around, thus nailing BQ with the kick instead. Flyer drops BQ and goes for a roaring elbow on Alias, but Alias ducks and hooks Flyer in a full nelson, then forces him to the mat, holding him in a camel clutch, as well. Alias keeps the hold on, and once BQ gets up, he climbs up on Alias' shoulders and gets his legs around the Original Pulp Hero's head, then leans back, grapevining Flyer's legs with his arms. Both Flyer and Alias refuse to give up, and the holds are broken once Alias releases Flyer and shoves BQ's legs off his shoulders. BQ and Alias get to their feet, where Alias catches BQ with a forearm to the jaw. Alias then whips BQ toward a corner. *THUNK* BQ hits the turnbuckle sternum-first and collapses to the mat. Alias grabs Flyer, who's having a little trouble getting up, and takes him over with an Exploder suplex. Alias picks up BQ and attempts to take him over with the same move, but BQ hops out, spins Alias around, kicks him in the gut, and plants him with the Temporary Refund Adjustment. He makes the cover, but Flyer is able to break it up after a two-count. BQ gets to his feet at the same time Flyer does, and the two being to trade blows again. BQ gets the best of it this time, backing Flyer into a corner and sitting him on the top turnbuckle. BQ climbs up to the second turnbuckle, seemingly trying to get to the top, where he would take Flyer off with a hurricanrana...but Flyer fought back, keeping BQ from climbing any higher. BQ gets knocked off the turnbuckle, but lands on his feet, then throws three headbutts into Flyer's midsection. BQ falls to one knee, dizzy from the headbutts, but he slowly stands back up and climbs back onto the second turnbuckle, where he hooks Flyer for a suplex. He probably would've succeeded in executing the move had Alias not come from behind and hit him with a hard forearm to the back. Alias then ducked down and managed to take BQ off the turnbuckle and hold him in a powerbomb position. Before he could drive BQ down, though, Flyer stood up on the top rope and leapt off, catching BQ with a somersault neckbreaker at the same time Alias went for the powerbomb. Since Alias was the closest, he was able to make the cover, but Flyer JUST managed to break it after two. Alias and Flyer are on their feet, and Alias gets the upperhand, rocking Flyer with several forearms. He hits the ropes and goes for a running big boot, but Flyer ducks and grabs Alias from behind, taking him over with a German suplex. BQ sees Alias land right near him, so he drapes an arm over his chest, but Flyer stomps BQ on the back of the head after a count of two. Flyer pulls BQ to his feet, only to take him down with a back suplex. Flyer makes the cover, but Alias is there to break it up after two. Alias pulls Flyer up and sends him into the ropes, but Flyer slides through his legs upon his return. Flyer hooks Alias from behind and gives him an atomic drop, then hits the ropes again as Alias turns around, nailing him with a roaring elbow. Instead of going for the cover, Flyer decides to head up top, but he takes too much time in climbing, as Alias is already up. He goes over and crotches Flyer on the top turnbuckle, then joins him there. The two duke it out as BQ pulls himself up in another corner. Alias gains an advantage, and then throws Flyer off the top rope with the Big Fat Kill. Both men crash to the mat, Flyer right behind BQ, who's now on his feet. BQ seizes the opportunity, climbing to the top rope. He looks back and sees that Flyer is still there, so he takes off. Silver Splash. Cover. 1. 2. Alias pulls him off. Alias pulls BQ to his feet and kicks him in the gut, then lifts him up for a high-angle powerbomb, but BQ slips out, landing on his feet right in front of Alias. BQ gets in his own kick to the midsection, then bends Alias over, hooks him, and turns him upside down, before hooking his legs in front of Alias' arms. Silver Lining. Right on top of Flyer. BQ, still holding onto Alias, rolls off of Flyer and holds Alias down for the pin. 1. 2. 3. BQ has just retained his title, defeating *two* men. The ref hands him the belt and raises his hand, but would he be doing the same at Meltdown? Result: Black Quicksilver by pinfall
