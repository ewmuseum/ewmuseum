Nameless... zero Yesterday... I hide my arms under black leather and cloth. It will protect me, from attack and from eyes. My hair cropped short. This is not unusual for me, life under a mask is difficult and most masked wrestlers wear their hair neat and trim. My face clean, my body aching for the release of the ring. Boots, tights, shirt. They are not traditional wrestling gear, they are black protection against the ever else. I do not do this for bravado... some foolish wrestlers believe that wearing a torn shirt and jeans make them appear more powerful, more tough. I know better. The clothes do not make the man, the man makes the man. My mask is black and simple. It serves its purpose well, for it is not meant to be flashy or impressive. I believe that if I am successful, if my path to revenge is carried out against the man who changed my life... then I may return to those days of old when style and substance were what I was known for. But that is getting ahead of myself, and it is a long road from here to there. It is one man I see in my nightmares. One man who haunts me... who made my dreams crumble into dust. One man who I blame for it all... who brought me here and who made me suffer such great pains. But no more... no longer. No more. My mask fits well, the mask of a luchador. The mask of a warrior. On my table I glance at paperwork authorizing the medical clearance of Temerario. But my name is not Temerario... and it never was. I am Nameless... for one night more.
