Sunday Slaughter, 10 February 2002

10 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The Veterans Memorial Coliseum would be playing host to Slaughter, with the show set to begin in a few moments. Tonight was the second Slaughter in the month of February, and miles away other events were taking place such as the 2002 Winter Olympics in Utah or the National Basketball Association's All-Star Game in Philadelphia. Outside the arena workers piled in. Long lines of rental cars and limosuines arrived, with wrestlers and celebrities getting ready to enter the arena. There would be an epic World Title match this evening between fWo World Champion Ruben Ross and Ric Chronos, the man he beat for the title at the end of last year at Countdown To Oblivion 1. Cameras took in the action and filmed the arrivals. Other arrivals included the significant others of wrestlers, including the World Champion's. Valentine's Day was later this week and suffice to say some couples were going to be preparing for the occasion earlier. Last night however was another reason for Andrea & Ruben Ross to celebrate. Ross turned age twenty-three yesterday, and Andrea was showing up to the arena to surprise Ruben. As Andrea entered the Coliseum, a vistor watched her from afar, taking in her every action. Every breath she took... he was watching her. Every move she made... he was watching her. He lurked in the shadows as she moved closer to her intended location. And then he stopped watching... and started following her. Andrea stopped as if she knew someone was near... she looked around and came face-to-face with the man her boyfriend would be facing in two weeks. Austin Davis. Davis smiled before grabbing her by the arm and muttering under his breath. "Don't say a word. Don't scream. I'm not going to hurt you... if you come with me, I promise you I won't lay a hand on your body. Trust me." Terrified, Andrea did what she was told. Davis and Andrea walked down several halls and apparently out of sight. Tag Teaming? As he walked into the back area of the arena, Zero grabbed a sheet that listed the booked matches for the night. He was aware that he had a match for the night, but was never told specifically what it would consist of. He held onto the sheet and walked on towards a locker room. He would look at it once he had a chance to sit down and relax. Opening the door to a nearby locker room, he stepped inside and dropped the bags down beside a chair. Taking a seat, Zero looked at the booking outline for the night. As he scanned down to his section, the match that he was in struck him as rather odd. He'd be teaming up with Donaven Winters to face Eli Flair and Sean "Triple X" Stevens. ...Teaming? The mere arrangement of the match was something that held questions. Why would he face Eli Flair? Why would he team with someone who he didn't even know? His sights were set in on Sean Stevens, not any other person involved in that match. There was also the simple fact of he being apart of some team. If he didn't even know Winters, how could he know that he could be trusted as a partner? Quite simply, he didn't. And that fact didn't sit well with him. Tag teaming was not something that he just jumped into. In the past, it would only be through a series of weeks and events before he would tag with someone. Those moments were few and far between. The last tag-team that he was apart of was nearly two years earlier. In a customary sort of way, it had abruptly ended as he and his partner, Blade, had a collapsing of a mutual respect that had formed between the two. He didn't tag in wrestling. It just wasn't something that he done. He looked back down at the sheet in his hand. He didn't like the concept that spoke back to him. But, there wasn't a whole lot he could do about it by that point. Over in the WWF, many of their workers were already talking about having new people coming in who had a history of altering booking arrangements in their favor. He was not like that and didn't want to start. If he had to be in a team for the night, he could get through it. He didn't like the idea, but the conclusion of it all would come soon enough, so he could return to his normal routine that he had become accustomed to throughout the years past. The first thing he had to do was speak with Donaven Winters. The two had never spoken before. Zero only knew who he was. If that was his partner for the night, he had to at least have prior conversation with him. He would still not be comfortable with the sudden teaming, but it could help bridge a gap that would have been previously left void. Leaving his bags where they laid, he walked out of the locker room and down the halls. The arena was large in the back area, but Zero was sure that he would find Winters eventually. Taking a random guess in direction, he started down the corridors. The Dark Side Of The Moon, Prelude 1 "Goddamnitt, now what?" Poison Ivy was getting irritated. She had been pacing the lockerrooms for nearly an hour, trying to decide what to say to Triple X when she saw him. After the night they spent together, and missing last week's Slaughter due to prior committments, he was owed some form of explanation. But how does Ivy explain her feelings when she doesn't even understand them? And how can she try to understand them when her telephone keeps ringing? "Hello?" Her face goes from annoyed to concerned in the span of three seconds. "Brian? Calm down. No - CALM. Tell me where you are, tell me what's wrong." Her face.... goes from concerned to panicked.... in much less time. "WHAT?!? Is she okay?" .... "No - no. Don't worry about that, you just go with her. I'll catch a cab to the airport and be there tonight. Just stay with her, understand?" Ivy looks like she doesn't know what she's doing for a moment - she collects her things and throws them into her bag. She catches a glimpse of herself in the mirror; the folks at the airport will simply have to deal with her being in her 'business attire' of black vinyl pants and furry black tanktop. Hastily writing a note for Eli Flair, she leaves it on the bench and hurries out the door. Right into the path of Triple X, who had been looking for her. "Ivy--" he began, "We need to--" "Not now, Sean," she interrupts, hurrying away. Triple X simply stands there, watching her go. "Not now, huh? Not now, not ever. Whatever." Monte Burns (c) vs. Eddy Fate As the first sounds of "Disconnect" by the Henry Rollins Band fills the arena, the Jacksonville fans erupt in cheers for one of their favorites, Eddy Fate. The two-time former fWo Cruiserweight Champion strides out from the back looking focused for the upcoming match. Fate jogs down the entrance ramp, waving to the fans as he makes his way down to ringside. Just as Fate slides into the ring, his music is replace by Powerman 5000's "Nobody's Real." With the crowd's boos resonating throughout the arena, "The Golden Child" Monte Burns walks out slowly from the back, the fWo Cruiserweight Championship belt draped over his shoulder. Burns steps slowly down the ramp, sneering at the fans yelling obscenities and flashing unwelcome gestures. Nearing the ring, Burns hands the belt off to an official, while Fate paces around in a circle in the ring. Burns starts to climb into the ring, but he stops and tells the official to keep Fate back. As the referee tries to get Fate back, Burns jaws with a fan at ringside who seems to have made an unflattering remark about Monte's sexual orientation. Fate sees this and takes off running towards the ropes. Just as Monte turns back around, Fate wipes him out with a somersault suicide plancha that makes the crowd go BONZO GONZO~! Fate gets back to his feet and lifts Burns up as well, as "The Golden Child" looks out of it already. Fate slides Burns back into the ring and the referee finally calls for the bell to officially start the match. Fate tries for the cover immediately after the bell rings, but Burns kicks out before the ref can make a count. Staying on the offensive, Fate brings Burns up and knocks him back against the ropes with hard knife edge chops that send the crowd into a fit of "WHOO! WHOO! WHOO!" before whipping Burns across. Burns manages to duck a clothesline attempt, but Fate catches him with a hurricanrana on the bounce back. Not wasting any time, Fate runs to the ropes and nails a springboard onto the fallen Burns. Fate makes the cover and hooks the leg, but Burns kicks out at two. Again, Fate pulls Burns up and starts to whip him into the ropes. This time Burns reverses and sends Fate into the ropes instead. However, Fate ducks a Burns clothesline attempt and goes for a boot to the midsection. The sly Burns sees this coming and catches the foot, wagging his finger in Fate's face with that cocky grin. Fate smiles back, but his smile is accompanied by a kick to the head as he drops Burns with an enzuigiri. Fate quickly gets back to his feet and climbs to the top turnbuckle. He looks down at Burns and then leaps off and connects with a 450 splash. The crowd counts along with the cover, but Burns kicks out again at two. Fate lifts Burns up and starts to go for a suplex, but Burns slides down the side to escape the move. Before Fate can react, Burns drops him with a side russian legsweep, ending Fate's momentum and drawing the ire of the crowd. As Fate lies on the mat, Burns stomps away at his head repeatedly. Burns heads quickly over to the corner and climbs to the top rope. As Fate struggles up to his feet, Burns leaps off and connects to the jaw with a missile dropkick. Burns makes a cover, but Fate's the one to kick out this time. After glancing at the referee, Burns picks Fate up by the hair and drops him across his knee with a double underhook backbreaker. Now Burns goes for his own asai moonsault, but Fate gets his knees up to block the move. The crowd roars with delight, as Burns clutches his ribs in pain and Fate gets to his feet. With the crowd on its feet, Fate grabs Burns in a side headlock and starts forward for a running bulldog. Burns, however, manages to push Fate forward and send him crashing chest first into the turnbuckles. As Fate stumbles backwards, Burns spins him around, kicks him in the midsection, and spikes him into the mat with a double-arm DDT. Burns quickly covers and hooks the leg... One... Two... Thr-Kickout by Fate! Burns slams the mat in frustration at the two count, as the fans erupt in cheers for Fate. After shaking his head in disgust, Burns pulls Fate up and whips him into the ropes. Burns goes for a spinning heel kick, but Fate rolls through to duck the move and takes Burns down with a yakuza kick. Fate lifts Burns back up and whips him hard into the ropes. He catches Burns with a kick to the stomach and goes for a DDT. However, Burns is able to escape and drop Fate with a swinging neckbreaker instead. Burns hurries over to the far corner and climbs to the top, looking out at the sea of booing fans along the way. Once on top, he leaps off with a flying elbow drop. Fate moves at the last possible moment and Burns lands with a thud. As Burns holds his elbow in pain, Fate lifts him up and whips him into the ropes. Fate catches him with a vicious wheel kick to the jaw and makes the cover... One... Two... Thr-NO! Burns kicks out just in time to avoid losing the title back to Fate. The crowd looks just as shocked as Fate, who hesitates for a moment before going back to work with kicks to the head. Fate lifts him back up and goes for an irish whip, but Burns manages to reverse again and send Fate in instead. Burns goes for a clothesline, Fate ducks. Burns tries for a hurricanrana as Fate bounces back through, but Fate catches him and drives him into the mat with a powerbomb. The crowd goes into a frenzy, as Fate rushes over to the corner and climbs to the top turnbuckle. As Burns lays there out on the mat, Fate nails a senton bomb to the heart of Burns. He reaches back over and makes the cover as the referee counts... One... Two... Thre-NO! NO! Burns gets a shoulder up in the nick of time, causing the crowd and Fate to have a collective heart attack. Fate sits up on his knees for a moment, obviously shocked that he wasn't able to get the win there. Fate finally shakes out of it and lifts Burns up, hoping to put the nail in the coffin. He tries for the Twist of Fate, but Burns shoves him off into the corner. Burns goes for a clothesline as Fate turns around, but Fate ducks the clothesline and hits it. The Twist of Fate. As he starts to go for the cover, he notices the referee slumped down in the corner. Unfortunately for Fate, when Burns pushed him into the corner he collided with the referee. Fate walks over to the corner and tries to revive the official, as the crowd begins to boo mercilessly for some reason. One look at the entrance ramp tells why. Ric Chronos is on his way to the ring. Running down the ramp, Chronos slides into the ring at full speed and immediately goes for Fate. Not realizing that Chronos is there, Fate finally gives up on the referee and turns around. At that moment, Chronos smirks with evil intentions and lands a stiff kick to the groin. With Fate doubled over, Chronos easily sets Fate up and drives him into the mat with a Figure Four DDT. Chronos stands over Fate's fallen body, laughing as the crowd boos with hatred. As Chronos exits the ring, Burns slowly makes his way over to the corner and climbs to the top. He looks down for a moment at Fate, then to the crowd, before leaping off and connecting with The Establishment. The referee, still groggy from the collision in the corner, crawls over and makes the slow count... One... Two... Three! Much to the dismay of the crowd, Burns gets the victory and retains his Cruiserweight Championship in a hard match with Fate. The official wearily gets to his feet and raises Monte's arm in victory, as another official hands "The Golden Child" his title belt. As Burns heads to the back, the crowd voices its displeasure at the contest's result. Result: Monte Burns by pinfall Mind Games The room was dark and cold. Andrea sat in a chair in a center of a room somewhere inside the arena. Davis paced around, circling her while playing various mind games with her. "You know... I said I wouldn't touch you..." Davis leaned down and his voice was nothing more than a whisper. His breath was warm, and Andrea was frightened by his mere presence. "... but it would be so easy for me to..." Davis brushed his hand slightly up against her face. Andrea pushed it away and Davis snickered. "... do whatever I wanted to you right now. Nobody would ever know... and the sad thing is, Andrea... nobody would ever care. Not even Ruben... that ten pounds of gold means more to him than you do. Why do you think he blew up at you? Not because of the pressure that he was going through... but because it's the truth." Davis smiled and brushed his hand through her hair. A tear trickled down the side of her face. She didn't believe a word Davis was saying, but Davis often had a way of saying things that would make anyone hurt. Davis chuckled a little before standing up and walking around, kneeling down so he was face to face with her. "You thought it would be nice to surprise Ruben... you thought it would be nice if you showed up and offered some encouragement for him in his match tonight with Ric... well... surprise, surprise. Plans have changed. I'm going to say happy birthday to Ruben in my own way... but that's for later. You see in that bag over there? That black bag on the floor? You know what's in there? Do you?" Andrea didn't offer up a response. She was a strong-willed woman but Davis was having his way with her. When Andrea didn't respond Davis grew angry. His voice went from a whisper to a shout, and echoed throughout the room. "There are ten poisonous snakes in there... all hungry. Hungry to snack on something... to inject their poison into the veins of your so-called man. I don't want just the title, Andrea... can I call you 'Dre?" Davis chuckled at his attempt at humor, and Andrea closed her eyes. "I want to... I want to make Ross realize who he's dealing with here. I'm not Ric Chronos... I'm not going to stand behind cronies and allow them to do the dirty work for me. Anything I've ever wanted done I did it myself... and what I want to do is make your little boyfriend know that I'm his worst nightmare. It's a shame his first World Title reign won't last more than a few defenses on television and a single defense on pay-per-view... because I'm going to take the title from him." Davis paused. "And by the end of the night... he will be stripped of all his pride, his dignity, and every fiber of good left in his body. I don't care about the glory of being a champion... I care about making people hurt. And when I see Ross in pain... it makes me feel good. And once he finds out that I'm causing you some mental pain here... it'll make me feel even better. And when that title is away from him.. it's going to be the lowest moment of his career, because of me." Another tear slid down the cheek of his "hostage". "Tearing others down to make yourself feel better... you're an insecure asshole!" Davis stood up and looked down at Andrea. He considered striking her but thought better of it. "You're nothing more than a pawn in my game of chess, bitch." Davis turned and looked at the bag of snakes. The bag moved and twitched slightly, but not much. Then, "Defy You". Monster pop. Ruben Ross appears, the fWo World Title gleaming around his waist. Ross heads to the ring, taking in the cheers of the crowd as he goes. He vaults into the ring and heads right over to Chronos to talk trash. Chronos gives it right back, but Ross blows him off and heads to the ref to hand over his title. The ref raises it in the air to signify that it's on the line, then hands it off to a ring attendant to pass to the timekeeper. The bell rings. It is, as they say, go time. Ross and Chronos lock up. Ross gains the advantage, armdragging Chronos to the mat. As Chronos gets up, Ross makes him eat a dropkick right to the kisser, sending him back down. Ross brings Chronos to his feet and whips him into the corner, then charges in after him. He blasts Chronos with a spinning heel kick that nails him squarely. Ross lands on the apron, and Chronos stumbles out. Ross leaps onto the top rope, and goes for a dropkick. Unfortunately for him, Chronos turns, sees him coming, and sidesteps him. Ross crashes to the mat, and now it is Chronos' turn to lay on the pain. Chronos brings Ross to his feet and whips him into the ropes. As Ross comes off, Chronos catches him and slams him to the mat with a powerslam. With Ross down, Chronos runs into the ropes. He leaps high and comes down on Ross' neck with a kneedrop. Chronos makes the cover, but only gets a count of two. He brings Ross to his feet and takes him down with a snap suplex. He brings Ross back up, still holding onto the suplex, then takes him down with a delayed vertical suplex. Finally, Chronos completes the trio of suplex moves. Brainbuster. Chronos rolls over and makes the cover. One. Two. Ross kicks out. Chronos slams the mat in frustration, then brings Ross to his feet. He grabs him by the head and runs him towards the ropes, then tosses him over. Chronos slides out himself, but is caught by Ross, who slams his head into the apron, dazing him. Ross then slides back into the ring and runs into the ropes. As Chronos regains his senses, Ross leaps. No-hands plancha. Misses. Ross slams into the German announce table, shattering it and sending Hans and Franz scattering. Chronos smirks at this cruel twist of fate, then sets about the task of removing Ross from the wreckage. He pulls Ross up and shoves him back into the ring. Chronos rolls in after him and brings him to his feet. He hooks Ross by the head and runs into the corner. Chronosphere. Chronos hits the Chronosphere, and Ross is down and seemingly out. Chronos makes the cover, sure that this is it. One. Two. Ross kicks out. Chronos can't believe it, and gets to his feet to shout at the ref. The ref defends his count, and as such, Chronos never sees Ross crawling up behind him. Schoolboy. One. Two. Chronos kicks out. Chronos barely manages to get a shoulder up. Both men get up to their feet, but Ross has had time to recover and regain his senses. Chronos goes for a clothesline, but Ross ducks. He then hits Chronos with a leaping leg lariat. Ross brings Chronos to his feet and executes the Spin Cycle, driving Chronos to the mat. Ross hooks a leg. One. Two. Kickout Ross wastes no time in bringing Chronos up, and he goes behind him, setting up the Paint It Black. Instead, Chronos executes a mule kick, dazing Ross and sending him stumbling backward. Ross is doubled over, and Chronos puts him between his legs. This is it. Chronos prepares the move that will surely win him back the World Title, the Figure-Four DDT. Ruben Ross backdrops out of it. Chronos lands hard on the mat, and Ross quickly brings him back up. He hooks Chronos up and prepares to end it. Sit The Fuck Down. Cover. One. Two. Three. Ruben Ross is still your fWo World Heavyweight Champion. The crowd cheers wildly as Ross is handed his belt, and he raises it high in the air and heads towards the turnbuckles, which he climbs and lifts the belt high in the air. As Ross climbed down from the turnbuckle he looked at the World Title in reflection of all that had occurred since he won the title. He had been successful in title defenses against Vic Creed, Neo, Scott Slugger and now Ric Chronos. He had an amazing run so far and he didn't want it to end. There would be more title defenses and more obstacles. More challengers would challenge... and he as a champion would have to stand up to the trials and tribulations that undoubtedly would come before him. The crowd continue to cheer, but the cheers turned to boos once the fans saw someone else arrive on the scene. Austin Davis was running down the entrance ramp wielding a steel chair while carrying a large bag. Davis slid into the ring... and as Ross turned Davis immediately used the chair and smacked him in the face. Ross collapsed in pain, and was unable to defend himself. Blood immediately began pouring out from his forehead and into his eyes. Davis continued to attack like a rabid dog, in an attack that came out of nowhere. Davis kicked Ross in the face, and Ross rolled onto his stomach while attempting to wipe off his crimson covered face. Davis reached for a bag and pulled out a smaller bag, before untying it and emptying the contents. Shards of broken glass spilled out onto the mat, and the fans gasped as Davis shot a sneer in their direction. Davis looked down at the canvas and upon further inspecting found a piece of glass that wasn't that short, and had a very sharp end. Davis reached for it and then bent down above Ross and began using the sharp end to stab Ross in the back of the neck several times. A small cut opened and soon Ross was more bloody. Davis smiled before grabbing Ross and slapping him across the face, and bringing him up. As Frankton & Armstrong described the horrific scene Davis lifted Ross into the air onto his back, and extended his arms outwards. In one rapid motion he brought Ross down with a crucifix powerbomb DDT onto the glass. The Dead End. The fans exploded with boos but literally were in awe of what Davis had done. Little did they know, he wasn't finished. Davis reached inside the large bag he brought to the ring with him... and this time he pulled out something nobody had expected. A rope. Murmurs spread throughout the crowd before it turned into one loud reaction of boos. Davis drove a knee into the back of Ross, before tying the rope around the neck of Ross. He lifted the near lifeless body of Ross into the air before running and letting Ross drop... but he leaned back on the mat before eventually sitting indian style. He was hanging Ross... and the expression on the face showed he loved every minute of it. Trash poured into the ring as Davis sat back, continuing to choke the life of Ross out. Davis looked into the audience with a crazed look in his eyes, which was easy to separate from his normal expression of anger and hatred. Davis then closed his eyes and began saying something quiet at first... until it grew louder. The fans quieted enough that it could be heard throughout the arena. "Happy... birthday to you... happy birthday... to you... happy birthday, dead Ruben... happy... birthday... to you." Security rushed the ring with a group of officials and medics en masse. Davis had gone too far. As Ross gasped for his life Davis continued to sing... while security tried to pry him off the helpless champion. Davis finally released his grasp and Ross fell to the floor, his face completely red... and covered in blood. Davis walked over and grabbed the World Title, a title he had held on numerous occasions but not since May of 2000. He looked at his reflection and liked what he saw... which was him. Just being him. Davis walked over to near the edge of the ring and looked down at Ross... before saying one word. "Checkmate." Slaughter came to a close, the image of a man that was the epitome of evil standing in the ring with the World Title in his hands leaving a lasting impression... and fans wondering if their champion would be able to walk out of Body Count with the gold around his waist. Result: Ruben Ross by pinfall
