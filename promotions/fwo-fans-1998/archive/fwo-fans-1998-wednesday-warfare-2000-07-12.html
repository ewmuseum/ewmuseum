Wednesday Warfare, 12 July 2000

12 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Playing Games With A Politician? "Three weeks ago, I left the fWo and quite frankly, I didn't know if I was going to come back. And the reason I didn't know if I was going to come back or not, is because from day one, that I've been in the fWo, I've done nothing, nothing but deal with the good looking, Dodge Viper driving, egomaniac of the politics behind that curtain. The fact of the matter is I have a wife and three kids and I really don't need this shit. But let me tell you the reason why I did come back, I came back for everyone of those guys in their lockeroom who week in and week out, bust their ass for the fWo! I came back for the Mike Bear's, I came back for every single guy or gal in the Lone Gunmen, I came back for the Erik Kelly's, Ric Chrono's, Wretch, Eron, the guys behind that curtain who give a shit about this organization, and let me tell you who doesn't give a shit about this company, that god damned politician Scott Slugger, what happened out here tonight, all day long, I'm playing politics with Scott Slugger, because Scott Slugger wants to play his creative control card, and to him, that meant that tonight in the middle of this ring, he knew it was bullshit, he'd beat Eron, well guess what? Slugger got his wish, and got his belt, and he went home, I promise everyone here on my god damn grave, you will never see that piece of shit again! I know you paid good money to come here tonight, and no one is going to be ripped off tonight. So Scott Slugger now has the fWo belt..and Scott, let's refer to that as the Scott Slugger memorial belt, because from here on out, that belt don't mean shit! There will be a new fWo belt, and as far as I'm concerned, that belt still belongs to the one guy who busted his ass week in and week out, and you can love him or hate him, but he doesn't screw anyone backstage, and that's Eron The Relentless... Eron is still the official fWo champion, but he will defend that title, in this ring.. and he will defend that title, against a son of a bitch back there who for 108 years has been busting his ass in fWo and can't get a god damn break because of Scott Slugger... and I'm talking about... Barry Bladerth! Barry Bladerth and Eron The Relentless are the two reasons why I'm in this god damn ring to begin with, so tonight, those two guys will compete for the new fWo Heavyweight Title... and Slugger, you good looking, Dodge Viper Driving egomaniac... KISS MY ASS!" We went backstage to find a shot of Chris Finn disgusted at Jeremiah Frankton in the ring wearing a backwards Yankee's cap and looking like he hadn't shaven nor slept in several weeks. Finn exits the picture, before we see Frankton throw down the microphone in the ring. Suddenly, the view goes to Jeremiah Frankton (announcer on the fWo pay-per-views and on Wednesday Warfare) who is sitting inside an office with his head slumped on the desk. The sound of the booming microphone awakened him, and he looked at the clock with an expression of frustration... and worrysome. He wiped the groggyness away, before looking at his notes and realizing he had a show to call. Putting his jacket on, he walked out of the office, still shrugging of the affects of a horrible, nasty... nightmare. Crusader (c) vs. Scot Hartt The sounds of Elastica bring us inside the Omaha Municipal Auditorium, for the start of Warfare. Fireworks explode, and Scot Hartt walks his way down to ringside. As usual, his lackey, TMFAOYM, which would be spelled out for you had it not been for a lazy transcript writer, walks down with him. He appears to be carrying two signs, or, as TMFAOYM says, "SYNES~!", with him. One reads "JUPA MI PENGA" or some spanish, and the other one reads "BK BIOTCH! 2 4 2 HOE! SAV MUNNEY! SAV CHIX! EET MEET!". This is obviously an attempt to save the chickens, as he had done on Slaughter. Biohazard directs the fans' attention away from the two men in the ring, and Crusader makes his presence known in the fWo world for the first time this evening. He walks proudly with his Cruiserweight title over his shoulder. He slides into the ring, and acknowledges the crowd's delight with him. He looks at Hartt and then at TMFAOYM, and then chuckles. TMFAOYM growls at him, and then says "SEY HAY, WOULD!" Crusader looks at him again, and TMFAOYM gets out of the ring with his arms raised. The bell rings, and Crusader and Hartt lock up. Hartt gets in an armbar, which is reversed by Crusader. Hartt reverses that, which prompts Crusader to flip out of the move. Crusader then jumps up and catches Hartt's head with his feet, before flipping him over. The crowd leaps to their feet in appreciation of this display of athleticism. Crusader takes off to the ropes, and then hops over Hartt and flies to the other side. Hartt stands up, and Crusader runs into him. However, the bigger Hartt flattens him with a shoulderblock, and then a quick elbow. Hartt gets in a headlock. Crusader gets himself to one knee, and then to one foot. He then gets to both feet, and looks at a corner. He begins to lead Hartt there. As they near the turnbuckle, Crusader leaps and lands both feet on the top turnbuckle. He uses that momentum to turn both their bodies, where Crusader flies toward the ground, landing a bulldog on Hartt. Crusader goes for a cover, and gets a two before Hartt kicks out. Crusader leaps to his feet and streaks toward the ropes. He hits both feet on the middle rope, and goes for a springboard moonsault. As his body flies toward Hartt's, Scot gets up his knees, catching Crusader in the gut. With the wind knocked out of Crusader, Hartt has the advantage. Hartt picks up Crusader, and locks him in for a gutwrench suplex. He lands that, and goes for a cover. At two and a half, Crusader kicks out. Hartt gets up and looks at the ref, obviously displeased with that count. He picks up Crusader and puts his arms around his waist. This show of affection, however, turns ugly, as Hartt hits a belly-to-belly suplex to Crusader. Hartt picks him up again, and again hits a belly-to-belly suplex. He goes for another cover; again, at two-and-a-half, Crusader kicks out. Hartt grunts at the ref, and then picks up Crusader. Hartt decides to set up Crusader for his finisher, the Final Exam. Hartt grabs the neck of Crusader, and locks in a vertical suplex. As he begins to pick him up, Crusader blocks it. Hartt picks him up agian, and again it is blocked. Hartt finally does get him up; however, Crusader uses his momentum to land behind Hartt. Hartt turns around, and is met with a standing dropkick. Hartt crashes to the floor. Crusader runs toward the ropes, and again tries the springboard moonsault. This time, he nails it. Crusader picks up Hartt, and whips him into the ropes. Hartt flies off the ropes and right into the hands of Crusader, who nails him with an inverted scoop piledriver. Crusader signals to the crowd, and the crowd anticipates one of the greatest moves in the fWo...Gravity 101. He climbs the turnbuckle, and then flies off. The flashes of cameras are seen all over the arena, as Crusader nails his finishing move on Hartt. Three second later, and Crusader is still your champion. TMFAOYM flies into the ring, and charges Crusader. He spears him, which barely fazes the champ. He looks at Crusader in terror, and yells "EET MEET!", and then exits the ring. The crowd begins to cheer Crusader again, as the cameras head somewhere other than the ring... Result: Crusader by pinfall Ratings Continue To Improve Backstage in a secluded office sat the brains behind the fWo - or lack thereof - of Chris Finn & James Armstrong. The humidity was unbareable, and all they had to compensate was a small fan that barely worked. "We really need to use our budget more wisely, Finn." Finn rolled his eyes, because Armstrong was the one ordering things they didn't need... like bun cake. "Yeah, perhaps we can cut back on your bun cake addiction or something, James. Anyways, ratings are up for the... sixth show in a row. The buyrate just came in for Critical Condition, and we did well there. We can go back to our old budget, but c'mon, stop ordering so much damn bun cake for the shows! You're the only one who likes it..." Armstrong disagreed. "Not true, Finn. Total Eclipse likes it too, damnit. Why, before he got hooked up to that symbol... er... cross... whatever last week on Warfare, he chowed down on about four of them!" Finn sighed. "You're forgetting that Eclipse will eat anything, James." He had him there. "Well, you're right... but still, I was just saying, I'm not the only one who eats bun cake around here." Another sigh from Finn, before he reached for a cell phone. "I need to call Loco and see if the Gunmen have shown up for their promo yet. Excuse me for a moment." Armstrong nodded and allowed Finn some silence. "Yes... Matt? Have the Gunmen arrived yet? No? Well, let me know when they get here. I'd like to put their promo on soon... I was informed they wanted to lay a challenge out to the Foundation for Slaughter... yeah, if they're not here by the tag match, we'll skip it." Warfare returned to the ring. California Kings vs. Stump Squad Warfare returns to the sounds of "We're Not Gonna Take It" by Twisted Sister. The song brings out the STUMP Squad, Mike Heftel and the Black Quicksilver, without their "manager" Stump. As soon as these two men enter the ring, "Last Resort" by Papa Roach hits and the California Kings, Lance Star and Courage, who are also two thirds of the Future Foundation. Star and Courage play to the fans, hearing a chorus of jeers. Star and Courage continue to the ring, and "The Future" Lance Star enters the ring, as Courage takes to the outside, to lock up with the Black Quicksilver. BQ starts things off with an irish whip, and as Star comes back he lifts a knee, and it connects right to the gut of Star, and brings him over. However, Star, using his head, grabs BQ, and rolls him up for a two count. Both men reach a vertical base, and Star runs at BQ, looking for a lariat. BQ ducks, nails a kick to the gut of Star, and brings him down with a facebuster. BQ covers, but Star kicks out at two. With that, BQ tags out to Heftel, who comes in with a barrage of punches to the face of Star. Heftel goes for an irish whip, but it's reversed by Star. Thinking quickly, Heftel tries to go for a cross-body block, but Star, thinking even more quickly ducks the manuever, and Heftel goes flying to the outside. Star tags out to Courage, who immediately climbs to the top rope. Courage flies off with a plancha onto Heftel, who was still located on the outside. Courage lets out a "C'mon baby!" before rolling Heftel back into the ring. With Heftel in prime position, Courage climbs to the top, and looks for a guillotine legdrop. However, Heftel rolls out of the way, and Courage hits the mat. Courage pops up, and walks right into an inverted russian legsweep by Heftel. Heftel covers, but Courage gets out again at two. Heftel lifts Courage up, and looks for a DDT, but Courage holds on, and turns it into a suplex with a bridge. However, Heftel kicks out of this, and Courage goes over, and tags in Lance Star. The Future enters the ring with a barrage of boots to the fallen Heftel, before bringing him to his feet. He whips him into the ropes, and looks for a backbreaker, but Heftel somehow turns it into a huracanrana. With having Star brought down, Heftel goes over, and tags in BQ. BQ enters with a bang, using the ring ropes to his advantage, and bringing Star down with a springboard dropkick. BQ quickly covers, and Star gets out of it. BQ brings Star up with some forearms that back Star into a corner. BQ then climbs up for the 10 punch count, but at 7, Star grabs BQ, and takes him down with a running powerbomb. Star, instead of covering, then points to Courage, and tags him in. Courage runs, hits the ropes, and flies off with the Courage Required. He covers, and Star enters the ring to fend off Heftel's count break up. 3 seconds later, and the California Kings pick up another win within the tag ranks of the fWo. Result: Kings by pinfall Request We head backstage once again to find Fallen knocking on the door of Finn & Armstrong. Seconds later the door opens... Finn greets him, before congratulating him on his number one contendership to the fWo International Title. He gets thanked in return... before Fallen gets down to the reason he's there. "Yeah... Chris... remember what you said about you'd do whatever it took to help Chronos out as long as he remained on the shows?" Finn nodded. "Yeah, I've noticed that he's gotten worse lately, and I'm willing to help in anyway I can. Got anything in mind?" Fallen nodded. "Well... the reason I'm here is I know I'm now the number one contender to whoever wins out of the match tonight... but I was wondering... I think Chronos seemed hurt from the lost, thinking it would be easy... little did he know I was in a competitive mood on Sunday. Well, I was just wondering if it would be out of the question to ask if we could both be number one contenders... and face the champion at SummerSlaughter in a three-way dance?" The owner of the fans Wrestling organization contemplated the scenario laid before him for a few moments, before deciding to give it a shot. "Sure, I don't see why not. Ric did fairly well in the Next Level tournament, and I guess giving him the same opportunity that you've gotten would likely lift his spirits... sure, what the hell. Pass Ric along the message, and tell him I wish him the best of luck and if he needs anything to let me know." The thankful Fallen nodded, before heading off in another direction of the arena... Wretch (c) vs. The Black Panther Warfare returns to the sounds of the Foo Fighters' "My Hero", which brings out Daniel Longshadow, more commonly known as the Black Panther. Panther strolls to the ring, with a determined loom showing through his mask. Suddenly, "Paint it Black" by Gob plays, bringing out Next Level finalist, and the reigning fWo International Champion, the ratings saver himself, Wretch. Wretch, who is not accompianied by his lackey, and his lackey's lackey, strides to the ring, with a smug look on his face. Wretch hands the International Title over to the referee, and the bell rings. They lock up, and Wretch wrenches in a side headlock. Pantjer shoots him off into the ropes, and then Panther drops to the mat. However, instead of jumping over, Wertch lands his own version of the people's elbow on to the back of the Panther. Wretch brings BP up to his feet, and sends him into the ropes. Panther bounces back, and walks right into a side suplex by the champ. Wretch covers, and Panther powers out easily at one. Wretch, preterbed by this, runs at Panther with a clothesline. Panther ducks, turns around, and plants Wretch with a uranage. Panther opts not to go for a cover, and instead brings Wretch up to his feet. He looks to give Wretch a vertical suplex, but Wretch slides out, and hooks Panther from behind, and takes him over with a release german suplex. Wretch jumps on with a cover, and Panther reaches a shoulder up at two. Wretch brings Panther up, and looks to hook him in for the Depression, but Panther counters it with a belly to back suplex. Panther gets up, and a stunned Wretch walks right into a fisherman's suplex. Panther gets a two count, but that's all he gets as the Next Level finalist gets a shoulder up at two. Panther brings Wretch up, and looks for a swinging neckbreaker, but Wretch counters with a few fists to the ribcage, which breaks the hold. Then, Wretch takes Panther over in a belly to belly suplex. Wretch brings Panther up, and sends him into the ropes. A back elbow is ducked, and then Panther comes back with a flying lariat of his own. Panther quickly lifts Wretch up, and brings him down with a head and arm suplex. Panther covers, and Wretch kicks out at two. Panther brings Wretch back up, and sends him into the ropes. He looks for a back body drop, but somehow, Wretch lands on his feet, behind Panther. He then immediately drops the Panther with the Depression, lets out a Kurt Angle-esque "Whoo!", before climbing the turnbuckle to soak in the adoration of the crowd. However, as he stands on the turnbuckle, flashes of lightning, and the roar of thunder is heard, and on the TravisTron, Rain's image appears. He says a simple "When it rains...it pours." before his image fades from the screen. Wretch's face becomes one of fear, but as soon as you know it, the Panther is up, and from behind, he hits Wretch with the Killer Instinct, a pumphandle slam from the top rope. Panther jumps on with the cover, and 3 seconds later, the fWo has a new International Champion. Warfare heads to commercial with the image of the Panther holding the title high, for all to see... with noted fWo historians remembering that very title was the one Panther made a career out of just last year in the mid-part of 1999. Result: Panther by pinfall No Gunmen Yet Backstage, Finn made another call to Loco to find out if the Gunmen had arrived yet. "Yeah, Matt... it's me again. Are they here yet? They're not? God damnit... you know, I love the Gunmen and all, but I'm getting a little sick and tired of their antics... coming and going... shoot angles... Machina told everyone about the shit he still received in his bags for that one... you know, Sunday was a flight problem... tonight is strike one. Two more, and they're outta here. What? Of course I can. It's my company. If they want to play like this, I can play this game too... I don't care about their large fan base. If they want to risk it, they can be my guest... I'll find someone else who wants to be around here." Warfare returned to the ring once more for a gigantic tag team match... The Boston Strangler & Erik Kelly vs. Bad Attitude Warfare heads to the ring for a tag team match of superstars, and the fans are pumped. The begin to boo heartily as "Bad Attitude" starts up and the hated duo of Mitch Wilson and Ice head to the ring, Ice wearing the brand new "Ice: Hide Your Children" shirt, on sale now. The gigantic Ice steps over the rope into the ring as Wilson hands his Internet Title to the timekeeper. The two await in the ring as the lights dim and "O Fortuna" hits to a monstrous pop. The Bostonian duo charges the ring as the lights go up, Strangler unstrapping the World Title and tossing it to the timekeeper as he slides in. Wilson and Ice strike with a series of stomps to the entering duo as the slide in, and quickly both members of Bad Attitude bring Kelly and Strangler up, Wilson on Kelly and Ice on Strangler. They back up the pair of gigantic Masschusettians into the corners and Wilson strikes Kelly with a ringing chop that connects simultaneously with Ice's hard side kick to the chest of Strangler. Bad Attitude gestures to each other, before simultaneously whipping Strangler and Kelly out of their corners and at each other. Much to Bad Attitude's dismay, Kelly manages to reverse the whip and sends Wilson flying towards Strangler, who leaps high in the air and blasts Wilson with a forearm to the face. Strangler immediately drops down into a cover, and Erik Kelly catches Ice attempting to make the save, clocking him with a big boot. Wilson escapes the pinfall of Strangler by the skin of his teeth, which is all the more impressive since teeth don't have skin. The referee finally decides that now is the time to restore order and after a moment, Strangler and Wilson are in the ring and designated the legal men. Strangler lifts Wilson to his feet and hammers him with a forearm to the face, knocking Wilson back. Strangler charges at Wilson with a clothesline, but Wilson ducks, spins, and catches Strangler with a rolling elbowsmash. Wilson makes the cover, but Strangler kicks out at two. Wilson brings Strangler up and whips him into the ropes, but makes the mistake of putting his head down. Strangler stops in front of Wilson, hooks his head, and drives him into the mat with a DDT. Strangler heads to his corner and tags in Erik Kelly, who lifts Wilson to his feet and lifts him up into a press slam position. He then throws Wilson far across the ring, where he lands with a crash. Kelly brings Wilson to his feet again and whips him into the ropes, before catching him coming off with a spinebuster. Kelly hooks a leg, but Wilson kicks out at two. Kelly lifts Wilson up and tags out to the Strangler, who mounts the second rope as Kelly holds him open. Strangler leaps off, nailing a double axhandle on Wilson. He brings Wilson to his feet and goes behind him for a german suplex, but Wilson counters with a mule kick that doubles over the Strangler. Wilson quickly reaches back and double underhooks the arms, before driving Strangler into the mat with an impaler. Wilson quickly rolls to his corner and tags in Ice, who hits the ring charging, only to be brought down by a drop toe hold from the Strangler. Both men are quickly up, and Strangler ducks a clothesline from Ice before winding up and nailing him with a right cross. Strangler then hooks Ice up and throws him across the ring with a release northern lights suplex. Strangler quickly heads to his corner and tags out to Kelly, who wants a piece of Ice. Kelly hits the ring and brings Ice up, before whipping him into the ropes. Ice reverses however, and Kelly goes into the ropes instead. In an impressive show of power, Ice catches the seven-footer coming off with a powerslam that shakes the foundations of the ring. He hooks a leg, but Kelly escapes at two. Ice brings Kelly to his feet and nails him with a charging side kick right to the face. Kelly hits the mat and Ice goes into a mounted position, before raining down punches on the face of Kelly. This only serves to enrage Kelly, who rolls over and begins hammering on Ice. Kelly finally gets off of Ice and brings him to his feet, before backing him into a corner. He unloads with rights and lefts, and Ice strikes back with a series of punches of his own. The two trade shots, and finally Kelly gains the advantage. He whips Ice into the ropes, where Ice makes a blind tag to Mitch Wilson. Kelly grabs Ice as he comes off the ropes, and lifts him up in a gorilla press position, before dropping him face-first onto the mat. As he does so, Mitch Wilson charges at him and blasts him with a forearm, knocking him back into his own corner where he alertly tags out to the Strangler. Strangler hits the ring to a big pop wearing a maniacal grin, and advances slowly on Wilson, who backs away. Wilson then decides to try a different tack and charges at Strangler, who ducks his attack and hooks him up from behind, before dropping him with a release german suplex. Meanwhile, the previous brawl between Ice and Kelly has spilled to the outside, where the two are brawling next to the Australian announce table. Ice finally gains the advantage with a low blow, and grabs Kelly by the throat before hitting the Attitude Adjuster, driving Kelly through the table with a crash. Inside the ring, the Strangler has brought Mitch Wilson to his feet and quickly locks on the Boston Massacre. Shockingly, Wilson twists out of it and hooks the head of a surprised Strangler before immediately driving him into the mat with the Headliner. Wilson quickly hooks a leg and goes for the pin, and scores a surprise three-count. Bad Attitude scores the somewhat upset win and quickly leaves the ringside area to a shower of boos. Inside the ring, the Strangler is up and outside of the ring, Erik Kelly has dragged himself to his feet. Both wear looks of extreme anger as Warfare rolls on... Result: Bad Attitude by pinfall Team Ratings In Disarray? Warfare went backstage one last time to find the trio of Big Wretchy The Ratings Saver, The Mutha Fuckin' Abusah Of Yo Momma, and Scot Hartt. The trio stood in their lockerroom with mad looks on their faces... nobody was speaking, but their looks said it all. "Wretch... what was that? I thought you had that one locked up.. The Black Panther is just a kitty cat! Meow!" Abusah chimed in with his own thoughts. "MEOW! KITTY KAT! EET MEET!" Wretch rolled his eyes. "You guys, I was obviously distracted by that Rain guy... he came on with one of his stupid videos again... and... well, it doesn't matter, I'm owed a shot at the Internet Title anyways, so I might as well go after that. But what about you two!?" Hartt looked at Wretch puzzeled, and Abusah spoke on their behalf. "US TOO? WAT R U TALKING ABOUT?" Wretch rolled his eyes. "Guys... if you're going to be part of this revolution... if you're going to be part of Team Ratings... and be responsible with me for taking the ratings out of the crap... and putting them back on the map... then you guys need to get your act together! You guys need a role... you guys need a direction... you guys need to become the most rockingest tag team the fWo has ever seen with the exception of the Underground!" Hartt sighed. He didn't want to team with Abusah. Abusah was dumb, and it's likely they'd never win a match. Abusah must've been reading his mind. "WAT? WE CANT BE A TEEM. SCOTT TRYS TO STILL THE SPOTLITE AND I DONT LIEK THAT!" Hartt looked at his lackey with disapproval. "Steal the spotlight? Excuse me, who's the one always swerving us all the time?" Abusah chuckled in a dumbfounded sort of way. "ME! I SWURVE YOU ALL, BIOTCH! SWURVE! SPEER!" Hartt sighed. But Wretch wasn't about to give up. "You guys... just give it a shot. What do you say, eh? Finn is on my side now... he knows all about the mystical power Big Wretchy possesses and stuff. He's like... bound to dig the idea of you two as a team. I'll get you booked for Warfare. So, c'mon, how about it?" Hartt started to like the idea. "Yeah, I guess that's not a bad idea afterall. But what about a name?" Wretch shrugged. "I don't have any ideas off the top of my head. You?" Hartt shook his head. "Not really..." But Abusah... he did. "I HAV AN EYEDEA! HOW ABOUT THIS GUYZ... HOW ABOUT RATEINGS R US! GET IT? RATEINGS R US!" Wretch looked at Hartt, and Hartt looked back. The two men couldn't exactly see each other... all they saw was gold. A golden opportunity... with that, Warfare proceeded to it's main event of the evening... Mike Bear vs. Scott Slugger vs. Eron The Relentless Warfare heads to the ring for the main event of the show. During the previous commercial break, a giant unenclosed cage has been lowered over the ring, and the fans are in a frenzy of anticipation for the fWo's first cage match since CyberSlam 2K. After Matt Panzer announces that the match will be pinfalls only, "Physical" starts to play and the fans boo loudly as Eron appears on the entrance ramp, grinning an evil grin. He walks slowly to the ring, and enters through the door before testing the cage by rattling it. His music is cut off by "Back In Black" and the boos turn to loud cheers as Scott Slugger begins to make his way to the ring. He rushes the ring and quickly enters through the door, before charging at Eron with punches. Eron is unprepared for this quick attack and Slugger pounds on Eron. He grabs Eron by the hair, runs across the ring with Eron in his hands, and slams him face-first into the cage. Slugger then slams Eron's face into the cage once more on the opposite side of the ring. Slugger brings Eron up, but Eron strikes with a low blow and hits a double leg takedown, before locking on the De-Roguer. As he completes the hold, "Dragula" rings out through the arena and the fans go crazy as Mike Bear charges to the ring. Bear begins scaling the cage outside of the ring, and quickly reaches the top. He yells down to the ring at Eron, who hears him and releases the hold before turning to face him. As he does so, Bear leaps off the top of the cage to a flurry of flashbulbs and nails Eron with a flying clothesline. Bear makes the cover on Eron, and gets a two count before Slugger, always an opportunist, shoves him off and covers Eron himself. This also gets a two before Bear breaks it up. Slugger replies by opening up on Bear with a right, and Bear replies with a right of his own. This punch staggers Slugger, and Bear whips him into the ropes before catching him with a back body drop that sends him high into the air. Bear brings Slugger up again and whips him hard into the corner, before charging in with a hardy boyz-style leg lariat. Slugger stumbles out of the corner, and Bear mounts the second rope, climbs to the top rope, and leaps off, before driving Slugger into the mat with a flying bulldog. Bear makes the cover on Slugger, but Slugger just escapes at two. Bear brings Slugger up again, but his move is cut off as a recovered Eron picks him up and drops him with a back suplex. Eron heads to the corner and leaps onto the second turnbuckle, looking to drive the point of the elbow into the throat of Bear. However, Scott Slugger has other ideas and grabs Eron, before press-tossing him out of the corner, where he lands on Bear with an unintentional version of a somersault senton. Seeing his chance, Slugger quickly makes the cover on Bear, but Bear manages to escape after a two count. Undaunted, Slugger brings Bear back up to his feet again and kicks him in the stomach, before taking him down with a rocker dropper. Out of the corner of his eye he sees Eron getting up behind him, and quickly grabs Eron and whips him into the ropes. Slugger makes the mistake of putting his head down, and Eron takes him down with a sit-down facebuster. Instead of going for the pin, Eron rams the face of Slugger into the cage, before grinding it against the steel, drawing blood from Slugger and boos from the crowd. Eron then hooks Slugger up and takes him down with a russian legsweep. He pauses to gloat over the fallen Slugger when Mike Bear swings him around from behind and lifts him onto his shoulders to a massive pop, as the crowd knows what's coming next. As Bear spins Eron however, Eron slides down his back and quickly hooks him and takes him down with a reverse DDT. Eron chuckles evilly, then brings Bear up. Eron then bounces into the ropes, and takes Bear down with a spinning heel kick. He makes the cover, but Bear kicks out at two as Scott Slugger drags himself to his feet a few feet away. Eron isn't upset however, as he has a plan. He brings Bear up to his feet, takes a few steps back, and unleashes a superkick. Bear sees it coming and ducks however, and as Eron turns, Bear lets a superkick fly. Eron ducks this as well, and it hits Scott Slugger instead, knocking him into the ropes. Slugger bounces off, and tries a superkick of his own on Bear, who ducks and Slugger nails Eron instead. Bear then clocks Slugger with a forearm to the face, before kicking him in the gut and executing a piledriver. Bear makes the cover, but it's broken up at two by Eron. Eron quickly drags Bear to his feet and positions him, before powerbombing him into the turnbuckles. Bear is out, and Eron drags him to the middle of the ring and begins climbing the cage, looking to finish off Bear with a high-risk maneuver. He finally reaches the top and measures Bear, but his plan is derailed when the bloody Scott Slugger begins to shake the cage from below. Eron falls and crotches himself on the steel, before falling backwards. Instead of crashing through a table, he is saved by the fact that one of his feet are hooked in the cage. Seeing an opportunity to inflict major punishment on his arch-rival, Scott Slugger begins to climb the cage. He quickly reaches the top, before straddling the cage, reaching down, and pulling Eron's face up to his. He then blasts Eron with a punch, causing Eron to fall back down and slam his head on the cage. Slugger does this again, and goes for it a third time as Mike Bear begins to get to his feet below them. Before Slugger can punch him again, Eron grabs him by the hair and flips him over his head, and the crowd is stunned as Slugger flies from the top of the cage and through the French announce table, which is turned into a pile of kindling with Slugger inside the wreckage. Eron laughs at this, before beginning to work on freeing himself from the cage. When he finally gets himself free and up on top of the cage, he sees none other than Mike Bear, right there waiting for him. Bear grabs Eron, positions him, and leaps off with a Whiplash from the top of the cage, causing thousands of cameras to go off and evoking chants of "fWo!" and "Bear!" from the shocked crowd. Bear covers Eron and the three count is academic. The referee raises Bear's hand inside the ring as teams of paramedics hit the ring, for both Slugger and Eron. Bear begins to scale the cage again, and Warfare goes off the air with the image of Bear, standing victorious on top of the cage... Result: Bear by pinfall
