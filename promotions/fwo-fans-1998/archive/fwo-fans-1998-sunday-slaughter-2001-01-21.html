Sunday Slaughter, 21 January 2001

Introduction Slaughter faded in directly to the ring, but all was not as it usually was. The ring was clearly a scene of chaos and mayhem. Chairs were scattered about, and one figure stood in the middle of the ring. The figure hurled a chair out of the ring angrily, then gestured for a microphone. Mike Bear wasted no time in speaking. "I want to know three things, and I want to know them right the hell now! Number one, where is Brittany?! Number two, who did it?! And number three, why?!" Bear paused for a moment, stomped across the ring in anger, and continued. "As for who, I do have a few guesses." Bear paused. "First, Scott Slugger." The crowd cheered at the mention of the name of the five-time World Heavyweight Champion. "After all, it was a Dodge Viper. But I seriously doubt it was him. Scott doesn't have to pull shit like that to get a shot at me, or to play mind games with me. He talks the talk and backs it up - in the ring. I'm going to take an educated guess and say that the people who did this may have attempted to frame Slugger. That brings me to my second guess... the Boston Strangler." The crowd reacted, loud but mixed. "After all, he's been suspected of all kinds of things in the past few weeks. He's nuts. I have a feeling that he was behind it. So Paul, get your ass out here right the hell NOW!" "O Fortuna" began to play, and the Boston Strangler appeared on the entrance ramp with a microphone. "Mister Bear, I will tell you right here and now that I was not involved. Think about it for a moment. How did it benefit me? It didn't lead to me as champion. As a matter of fact, it cost me my shot." Bear thought it over. "You're right. Not only that, I'm a fair guy. Whenever you want a rematch, you'll get it." "I better get it and I better get it tonight. Not only that, I want there to be no chance I get screwed again like I did last night. No countouts, no disqualifications." The crowd cheered at the prospect of seeing this huge match. "You've got it." "Good. Tonight, Bear, you will suffer the same fate as you did at SummerSlaughter. I'll lock in you in the Boston Massacre so tight that you'll never be able to escape... and then I will become the fWo World Champion once again." Bear smirked. "Is that a fact?" "It certainly is." "We'll see about that. If you ask me, tonight... asses will be kicked!" The crowd cheered as Bear hit his catchphrase, and Bear and Strangler shot matching glares at each other as Slaughter went to commercial... Kodiak Vic Creed vs. Havok Slaughter returns from commercial to the sounds of "Walk", and the fans boo loudly as Kodiak Vic Creed walks to the ring. Creed chuckles at the boos as he steps into the ring and waits for his opponent. The boos become cheers as "Awake" hits, and Havok makes his way to the ring, fresh of injuring arch-enemy Primetime at Body Count. Havok slides into the ring and shoots a look at Creed, who returns it right back. The bell rings, and they lock up. Creed gains the advantage with a stiff shot to the stomach, then blasts Havok with a back elbow to the fact that knocks Havok into the corner. Creed charges in with a clothesline, but Havok dodges out of the corner. As Creed staggers backwards out of the corner, Havok dropkicks him in the back of the head, knocking him back in. Havok then grabs Creed from behind and takes him down with a stiff back suplex. Havok rolls over and makes a cover, but only gets two. Havok brings Creed back up to his feet and whips him into the ropes, but Creed reverses it and takes Havok down with a thunderous powerslam. Creed brings Havok back up and whips him into the ropes again, then takes him down with a huge clothesline. Creed covers, but Havok kicks out at two. Creed brings Havok back to his feet again and hooks him up. He then takes him over with an overhead belly-to-belly suplex. Creed covers again, and again gets a two-count. Creed brings Havok up and shoves him into the corner, then charges in with a clothesline. He steps back, then clotheslines Havok in the corner again. And again. And again. And again. Creed steps back, and Havok slumps to the mat. Creed drags him away from the corner and locks on a camel clutch. Havok screams in pain, but cannot go anywhere as he is locked in by the huge arms of Vic Creed. Creed has the hold locked on, and Havok finally begins to sag in Creed's arms. The referee lifts and drops his arm twice. He tries it a third time, but Havok's arm stays up. Havok begins fighting back to his feet, and finally stands up. Creed remains on his back, and Havok falls backwards into the corner, slamming Creed against the turnbuckles. Havok steps back and shakes it off, then charges into the corner. Creed lifts up a boot and Havok slams into it, cutting his rally short. Creed then explodes out of the corner with a lariat that plasters Havok. He brings Havok back up to his feet and wraps a hand around his throat, then chokeslams him to the mat. Creed covers, and nearly gets three before Havok kicks out. Creed brings Havok up to his feet and positions him for a Creed Bomb. Creed sets to lift Havok, but Havok shifts his weight to counter and comes down on his feet. He grabs Creed by the legs and trips him down to the mat, then rolls forward into a pinning combination. The ref nearly reaches three, but Creed kicks out. Both men are quickly back up and Creed goes for a clothesline. Havok ducks, then dropkicks Creed as he turns back to face him. Havok brings Creed back up to his feet and whips him into the corner. The referee is caught in Creed's way, and Creed slams into him, sandwiching the ref between Creed and the corner. The second the ref hits the mat, Black Panther sprints to the ring with a chair in hand. He slides into the ring and swings at Havok, but Havok dodges the blow. He lashes out with a superkick that knocks the chair back into the face of the Panther and knocks him from the ring. As Havok yells at Panther, Vic Creed comes up from behind him. He lifts Havok onto his shoulders, facing the ceiling, and then drops him with an Alaskan Avalanche onto the chair. Creed then kicks the chair from the ring and grabs the ref. He drags him over to Havok and makes the cover. The ref slowly counts one... two... three and Creed scores the victory over Havok. The crowd roundly boos the result as Slaughter heads backstage... Result: Kodiak Vic Creed by pinfall Welcome Back "Man... it's good to be back." Robert Jensen stood inside a door to the Thomas & Mack Center, in a large wide hallway. He surveyed his surroundings. It was a far cry from his bouncing job. He had never really liked that job anyway he always craved a chance to return to the bright lights and big shows of the wrestling business, and now was his chance. Now he just had to go find Frank and discuss plans for their tag team. They had originally been christened the Hardcore Heroes, but had quickly dumped that name in favor of Ruin. "Yeah... Ruin. That name rocks." Before Jensen could further pontificate on their chosen name, he was interrupted by an approaching Juno Law. "Hello, Mister Jensen." "Hey yourself. Juno Law, right?" "Correct. I just wished to tell you that it will be an honor to work with a legend of your caliber." Jensen chuckled. "Legend, huh? I like the sound of that." "It's certainly fitting." The two began to walk and talk. "So Juno, odds are sooner or later I'll be meeting you in the tag team division. Where's your partner anyway?" "Oh, he's right... there." From behind, Kobi Law clobbered Jensen. Jensen crumpled to the ground, and the East Coast Laws immediately began stomping on him. After a while, they stopped. "Dude, what a loser!" "Totally." The two bashed their forearms together in the style of the early 1990s baseball Bash Brothers, then walked off, chuckling all the way. Vengence vs. Wretch The crowd erupts in a chorus of boos as ELO's "Fire On High" heralds the arrival of the six foot, ten inch monster called Vengence. He stalks about the ring, and as soon as "Keep On Rockin' (In The Free World)" begins, his eyes narrow to slits as he watches Wretch coming down to ringside with the roar of the crowd. Wretch steps between the ropes, and Vengence charges. Wretch quickly sidesteps, and as Vengence turns, Wretch meets him full on with a dropkick. Vengence is only knocked back a step, so Wretch decides for a second try, with only a little more effect showing. Frustrated, Wretch hits the ropes, and with the running start to this dropkick, is able to knock Vengence over the top rope and to the outside of the ring. Vengence looks livid, and gets back into the ring, running full bore right into a drop toehold by Wretch. The Long Island, New York, native takes only a half second before he floats over, placing Vengence in side headlock. Vengence works his way up, forcing Wretch to his feet, and softens Wretch up with a few blows to the midsection. Vengence pushes Wretch off, and after hitting the ropes, Wretch goes for a shoulder block that has him bounce of the mountain known as Vengence. Wretch quickly picks himself up, and hitting the ropes again, comes off with a hurricanrana that successfully takes the big man down. Vengence pops back up, but Wretch uses a spinning heel kick to keep Vengence off balance and slightly stunned, knocking him back into the corner. Wretch attempts to shoot Vengence into the opposite corner, only to find it reversed. Vengence charges in after Wretch, but Wretch jumps to the top rope, and launches himself at Vengence with a high cross body, but only gets a two count on the pin. Vengence is looking a little winded, not used to having an opponent take so little punishment at this stage in the match. As he gets up, Wretch goes for a superkick, only to watch as Vengence ducks, grabbing Wretch from behind in an attempt at a full-nelson spinebuster. Wretch thinks quickly, and in a great display of agility, kicks up and over Vengence, coming down with the behemoth in the reverse DDT position. Signaling to the crowd, they pop as Wretch hits the Depression. With his foe down, Wretch hits the Ratings Saver Legdrop, and three seconds later, has his hand raised in victory. As he celebrates, he fails to notice Mitch Wilson slide into the ring from the crowd. Turning, Mitch meets him with the Headliner, knocking Wretch senseless. Wilson taunts the prone Wretch, and Slaughter heads backstage. Tonight, disqualifications or count outs that would send the match into disrepute are NOT present. For tonight is a rematch for Strangler a second chance. His known theme music, O' Fortuna, takes to the speakers, which causes a loud yet mixed reaction from the crowd. The Boston Strangler appears at the ring entrance and begins slowly walking towards the ring. The fans aren't an issue on this night for him ... the World Title is. He enters the ring and paces about, waiting for Mike Bear. "Engel" by Rammstein causes Strangler's head to jolt straight to the ring entrance, where he his opponent will appear. Mike Bear walks down the ramp to a large ovation from the crowd, with the coveted fWo World Title around his waist. He keeps his eyes straight on Strangler in the ring, as fans reach out to pat him on the arm, back or what have you. Bear cautiously makes his way up the steps and enters the ring through the ropes. After removing his belt, Bear holds it up high for the fans to see, which causes a great wave of cheers. Bear then hands it over to the referee, who holds it up as well to signify that this was indeed for the gold. The bell sounds, and Mike Bear stands toe-to-toe with the Boston Strangler. Almost at the same time, the two go straight towards each other and meet in a collar-and-elbow tie-up. Using his strength, the Strangler bends his legs slightly and springs up, causing Bear to land in the corner. The Strangler charges in with a clothesline, but the defending champion moves out of the way and corners his challenger with hard boots to the mid-section. Bear brings the edge of his hand smacking the chest of the Strangler with a knife-edge chop. He follows it up with two more, before sending the Strangler into the other corner. The Boston Strangler hits the corner hard and feels more punishment on him in the form of a Mike Bear corner splash. The Strangler staggers forward out of the corner, and falls victim to a hip toss by Mike Bear. Strangler manages to get to his feet, but Bear nails him with a dropkick. Seeing the Strangler prone, Bear quickly follows up with a snap suplex and made the cover. One. Two. But a kick-out by the Boston Strangler ruins Bear's early-winning plans. Things seem to be going OK for the champion so far, as he now decides to go back to the basics with a wristlock. Strangler pats his shoulder a couple of times, before rolling through and reversing the move into an arm-drag-and-twist. Bear holds onto his shoulder, trying to find some way to reverse the Strangler's reversal, but the Boston native interrupts his thoughts by yanking the arm almost out of its socket. The Strangler pulls Bear forward and strikes him hard in the shoulder blade with a forearm. The Strangler continues his onslaught by driving the point of his elbow into the shoulder of Bear, and then places his arm between his legs. Bear gets viciously taken down to the mat with a cross-armbreaker, leaving the referee to see if Bear wants to submit. Profanity comes out of the mouth of Bear, but no words requesting submission. Bear kicks forward with his feet, and manages to slip out of the hold. Bear manages to get up to his feet via the ropes, and then takes a wild swing at the Strangler. Strangler blocks it and hits Mike Bear hard with a head butt that sends him into the ropes. Upon return, Strangler picks up his adversary and slams him hard to the mat. He comes off the ropes and drops a leg drop, and he continues his assault with two consecutive elbows to the sternum of Bear. Now it comes to a vicious chokehold by the Boston Strangler. The referee can't do anything but watch, until the Strangler finally releases and brings Bear to his feet. Strangler hooks him up in a headlock, kicks off the ropes and hits a tornado bulldog. Bear manages to get up, but Strangler boots him in the mid-section and drops him to the mat with a double-arm DDT. Strangler makes the cover. One. Two. Bear kicks out. Strangler waits on Bear to get up, and flattens him with a clothesline. Strangler picks Bear up in a suplex... but Bear drops behind and grabs Strangler's head, dropping him to the mat with a reverse DDT. Cover. One. Two. Strangler kicks out. Bear now waits on Strangler to get up, and then charges in with a clothesline. Strangler seems prepared, and manages to flip Bear up and over the top rope. Bear, however, lands on the apron, and as Strangler turns around, he's met with a shoulder-block through the top and middle ropes. Bear hooks Strangler up in a suplex, and takes him over right onto the steel ramp. Knowing full well that this match is indeed no disqualification, Mike Bear stalked around the ring and found Panzer. Bear ordered him to surrender his chair, and Panzer did so quickly. Bear turns around with the chair to go back to the Strangler, but the challenger manages to stay one step ahead of Bear by slamming the steel steps right into his face. The Boston Strangler picks up Bear, and drops him right onto the French announce position, stomach-first. Picking up the chair, the Strangler aims at Bear's shoulder, and then strikes him hard. Bear roared in pain as he rolls off of the table and tries to regain his footing. Seeing Strangler coming at him again with the chair, Mike Bear sends his foot out in desperation with a superkick that connects and sends the chair right back into Strangler's face. Mike Bear staggered about and saw a ringside fan with a beer bottle. After getting a demand for the bottle, the fan holds his hand out and begins drinking, as Bear tells him to hurry up. Time is indeed running out, as Strangler gets back to his feet. Upon finishing the contents of the beer bottle, the fan surrenders the bottle to Mike Bear, who then smashes it over the head of the Strangler. Shards of glass fly everywhere, and blood draws from the forehead of the Strangler. Bear quickly rolls the Strangler into the ring to follow up. Strangler gets to one knee, as Bear grabs him by the hair. Strangler fights back with right hands to avoid any more offense. Bear takes each shot, as Strangler quickly takes to the ropes. Bear manages to pick him up... and drop him to the mat with a tombstone piledriver. Bear hooks the leg and says a prayer. One. Two. ... Strangler kicks out. Frustrated and wanting to end this match A.S.A.P., Bear brings Strangler up and shoots him off to the ropes. A back body drop is telegraphed, which enables Strangler to drop the face of Bear over his knee. Bear stumbles forward into a drop-toe-hold by the Strangler, who then follows it up with an STF lock. Bear manages to elbow his way out of the hold. As Bear gets up, the Boston Strangler gets behind him and takes him over with a release German suplex. Bear manages to get up from the move, but he gets a boot in the mid-section by the Strangler, and then a gutwrench powerbomb. Bear's lights go out, and Strangler makes the cover. One. Two. Those lights weren't out for long, however. Bear kicks out with authority and keeps a look of resilience in his eyes. But that resilience begins its dismemberment, as Strangler comes in from behind with the Boston Massacre the crossface chicken wing. Bear struggles and fights in arguably the most devastating submission hold in the fWo, but all of his efforts make matters worse, as he begins fading away. The fans try rallying behind him... but it's no use to Bear, as the Strangler takes more and more life out of him. All of the softening holds that Strangler used are now paying off, and Bear is realizing it all now, as his eyes begin to close. But hark the rise in cheers as Brittany runs down the ramp! Seeing the very woman who keeps his sanity in check each and every day... Mike Bear's eyes widen and he finds a never-before-seen adrenaline rush within him. Strangler locks in the body scissors, but that's no issue for Mike Bear. Brittany lends words of encouragement Mike's way, and he manages to grab hold of the bottom rope. The ref can't do anything about it, though, but inside Mike Bear knows he can. Bear uses as much strength as his right arm allows him to roll under the bottom rope, and successfully escape the Boston Massacre. Bear holds his arm in pain and tries to regain his mind into the game, but an enraged Strangler flies over the top rope with a plancha. Brittany tells the Bear who is still groggy from the Massacre to move, and he does so, causing Strangler to hit the ramp hard. Bear rolls the Strangler back into the ring, hell bent on retaining his title. He picks Strangler up into a fireman's carry, and calls out to the crowd. Whiplash. Bear makes the cover. One. Two. Two and 99/100ths. Strangler gets the shoulder up... showing Bear that the fight isn't going to be over just yet. Mike Bear shows no signs of frustration now... as he picks Strangler up again in a fireman's carry, this time taking him down with a Death Valley Driver. He heads to the top rope... and executes a move that makes the entire fans feel as if they got their money's worth. The Lamontsault. Cover. One. Two. Three. The fans in the Thomas & Mack Center erupt in cheers as Rammstein resurfaces over the speakers. Mike Bear is awarded the fWo World Title belt as the referee holds his hand up in victory. Brittany enters the ring to celebrate with her significant other in life, as the Strangler rolls out of the ring in disappointment. Holding his belt up high for the fans to see once again, Mike Bear soaks in the cheers, as he knows he ended Slaughter with a bang. Result: Mike Bear by pinfall
