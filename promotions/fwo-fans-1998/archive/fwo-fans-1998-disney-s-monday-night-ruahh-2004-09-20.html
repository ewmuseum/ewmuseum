He looked back at Venenosa with a cold stare that the Survivor IV winner found to be almost intimidating. Within seconds, he also nodded in agreement of the proposed matchup. "Well then, looks like we..." Aimz tapped BQ on the shoulder. "Oh, yeah. Almost forgot. Aimz...how 'bout you?", BQ asked as he held the mic toward her. "Rana...", she said, putting a hand around the microphone and still trying to catch her breath. "This Friday...Unleashed...and all things will BEGIN." Aimz took her hand off the mic and stared up the ramp at Venenosa. BQ looked out at Death, and this edition of RUAHH~! came to a close...
