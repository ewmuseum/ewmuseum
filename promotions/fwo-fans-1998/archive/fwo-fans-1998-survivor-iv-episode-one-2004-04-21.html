He paced up the hallway, smiling as he heard the light cheers and applause from outside. The first Survivor television show. It was really amazing if you thought about it. Walking down the hallway, he stopped outside the makeshift office where Frank and Brian had set up their material and knocked. No answer. Frowning, he checked the door and found it unlocked. He swung it open, and shrugging decided to wait inside. Stevie pulled out a chair and slumped into it, sighing. Long days, and the Disney ownership troubled him. There was no security anymore... not that there was any in the wrestling world anyway, but it still seemed... His eyes fell on a blue folder and it pulled it over to him. Bored, Stevie began to flip through it. Comprehension came over his face, and his tan complexion went white. "Oh my God..."
