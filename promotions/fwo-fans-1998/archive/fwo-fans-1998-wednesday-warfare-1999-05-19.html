Wednesday Warfare, 19 May 1999

19 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction As Warfare opens up, a limosuine pulls into the arena bay. The driver gets out of the white stretch limo, walks to the back and opens the door. Outsteps the fWo Internet Champion "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal. Universal holds the door open for Megan Dawn; who climbs out wearing a skimpy outfit. Universal smiles and holds the Internet Title up for the camera to see, and says "I am YOUR Internet Champion. Whenever you web geeks go online, go to [detail withheld] and see what a REAL web site is supposed to look like. Hit the music! Er... sorry about that..." Universal then smirks and he and Dawn walk into the arena. Shortly thereafter, another limosuine pulls up, and this time the French Foundation get out of it. Out first comes Mitch Wilson, then Primetime, then Ze Flying Frenchie. Wilson glares at the camera and he and Primetime say "Chaos, we're going to do one thing to win our match tonight: Just wrestle." Frenchie then beams, and glances at the camera man, but realizes he has nothing to say, and walks off with his co-conspirators in crime. Finally, another limosuine pulls up and out steps Rudo Insano, Sid Snow, Billy James and the rest of the Lone Gunmen. As the camera focuses on the inside of the limo, waiting for Machina to step out, it soon realizes that the limo is empty. Quickly, the super-crafty Geoff Green tells Rudo & James that Machina is not in there. A shocked look comes over Rudo's face and he says "Oh, shneikes, what are we gonna do?" The camera glances over at James, and you can almost tell that a lightbulb went off inside his head just by the look on his face. James says "Hey, since Machina isn't here, can I wrestle his match? Can I? Please?!" Rudo & Snow simply give him a scowl and say "NO!"; and that quickly dampers whatever good mood James was in. As Rudo & Snow ponder where their fearless leader has gone to, a black truck pulls up and "Baad Ass" Austin Davis steps out. Davis tells the camera man he has a surprise for Green, and he takes Green to the back of the truck... where inside several dozen snakes hiss and slither about. Green drops the camera and as the camera smacks the ground, the Warfare opening begins... Chaos vs. French Foundation The French Foundation came to the ring, with the Mitch Wilson and Primetime wrestling for the evening. Suddenly the lights went off, and fire erupted from the entrance ramp. Plauge and War made their way to the ring with Scott Taylor, and from behind them Death and Stone could be seen standing just beyond the entrance door. The Dark Riders both leapt into the ring and forced the Foundation from the ring. Plague began to scream and drive his own head into the mat, hitting it repeatedly until blood began to freely flow over his eyes. War started the match with Primetime, using his strength to bat the smaller man from post to post. Primetime used his speed and agility to escape a running tackle and jumped on War's back, where he attacked with rapid rabbit punches until War was sent to his knees. The Wilson and Plague were tagged in, and Plague at once began to bite and claw at Wilson, laughing histerically. The ref attempted to get this stopped, and Plauge smacked him to the mat with a hard chop. Plague began to deliver multiple head butts to Wilson, but Wilson quickly hooked the leg and scored a surprise three count. Death and Stone then hit the ring as fWo security and officals hit the ring to get the Foundation to safety as the Dark Riders looked on from the ring. Plague began to scream, but was interupted by silver roses falling from the celing. Plague looked up in horror, and began to claw at his eyes... Result: French Foundation by Pinfall False Alarm As Warfare returns from a commercial break, Rudo & James are standing outside the arena looking for Machina, as rumor quickly spread that Machina had arrived. A limo pulls out, and out comes Machina... or is it? The masked man who looks like Machina but probably isn't walks over to Rudo, and kicks him in the stomach and DDT's him on the cement. James looks shocked, and the masked man yells "Boo!" and James runs off. The masked man grabs Rudo and throws him into the side of the limo, before smirking and saying "Happy fucking Birthday, you piece of trash!" before Warfare goes to another commercial break... Scott Slugger & Ice vs. Mad Trucker & Darkness Slugger and Ice came to the ring together, and looked more like a unit than they have in years. The two posed inside the ring, looking over cockily at the crowd and daring them to taunt them. To a chorus of boos, Darkness and the Mad Trucker came to the ring, with the Mad Trucker smacking the heads of children as he walked. While the ref was checking the boots of Scott Slugger, Darkness struck Ice from behind with some kind of foriegn weapon, causing the big man to fall. From there Darkness and the Trucker teased the helpless Ice, keeping him isolated in their corner and beating down on him with hard kicks. Suddenly, the lights in the arena dimmed and all eyes turned up to the entrance ramp, where Cookie and Alice stood, holding a hubcap from Bertha. The two pointed down at the Trucker, who seemed unsure of this development. Yelling at the Trucker to stay inside the ring, Darkness went after Alice and Cookie on the entrance ramp. The Mad Trucker looked on as this drama unfolded, allowing Ice to tag in Scott Slugger. Slugger entered the ring and removed a turnbuckle cover, then surprised the distracted Trucker with a hard lariat into the steel of the turnbuckle. The Trucker went down, and Ice and Slugger got the pinfall victory. Upon hearing the bell ring, Darkness turned back to the ring, and began demolishing everyone in his path... including the ref, Scott Slugger and Ice. fWo security hit the ring to restrain the big man as Cookie and Alice made a quick retreat... Result: Scott Slugger & Ice by Pinfall False Alarm II Sid Snow is sitting inside his dressing room when his private telephone rings. A woman's voice speaks saying "Mr. Snow?", and Snow replies with "This is them". "Er, huh? Nevermind. Mr. Snow, I've been informed that you have a secret admirer and I'm here to deliver the flowers from that very person. Would you like to accept them? I will be outside waiting, oh, and I've been told that Machina just arrived..." Snow says he will "be right there", and he climbs up from his chair and exits his room. He makes a long jog down the hallway and out of the building, where Megan Dawn is waiting with flowers... just as Machina walks by... or is it? Snow eagerly grabs the flowers and spots Machina; who then grabs the flowers and shoves them in Snow's face, before throwing him all over the place and beating the hell out of him. Machina hands a $1000 bill to Dawn and says "Keep the change", as Dawn appears to have taken a liking to... er... Machina. Machina walks off and at the last moment, he pulls his mask off to reveal a shiny bald head... Chris Universal (c) vs. Austin Davis vs. Flying Frenchie vs. Machina "Juke Joint Jezebel" begins to play, and out comes Ze Flying Frenchie, accompanied by Mitch Wilson & Primetime. Frenchie carries a French flag and walks to the ring proudly, occasionally taking the time to "beam" accordingly. Out next comes Austin Davis, who carries a black bag with him to the ring. Davis climbs into the ring and Frenchie quickly exits onto the outside. Davis holds his arms up in the air as his music blares over the arena soundsystem, with some fans approving for his arrival. "Do you buy what the $ellout is selling?" plays and out comes Chris Universal, with the fWo Internet Title draped over his right shoulder. Megan Dawn accompanies him, although somewhat begrudingly. Universal climbs into the ring and Davis backs off, letting him in. Finally, "Come with Me" begins to play as we all await the arrival of Machina. As he's introduced by that fine fellow Matt Panzer, we all soon realize that Machina is not coming. Davis grabs the microphone from Panzer and shoves him down, and says "Well, since that Korean bastard or whatever nationalization he claims to be isn't here... I thought I'd take this time to provide some entertainment for you all tonight. I came with the full intentions of kicking Chris Universal's ass and taking the Internet Title back where it belongs, but you see... theres this thing... or things, we could say, that I can't take my mind off of. Megan Dawn... since that bitch Debra won't show us her puppies, why don't you do us all a favor and show us yours?" Dawn looks very reluctant, but then Davis continues... "Megan... put it to you this way. If you show us your puppies, I'll call it a night and I'm sure Frenchie will too, and Universal can walk home the Internet Champion". Dawn smiles, and begins to unbutton her shirt, before pulling it off as the crowd goes crazy. Dawn is getting ready to loosen her bra, when Universal begins to stop her. She continues anyways and with the bra fully off, Universal uses his Internet Title to cover it up. The crowd begins to boo and suddenly, we hear Machina' voice saying "Screw the puppies... we wanna see the kitten!" Machina begins to repel from the ceiling upside down, as Universal "accidentally" drops the Internet Title and Dawn's naked breasts are shown. Davis smiles as Machina lands on the floor on the outside, and Rudo Insano begins to make his way down limping. Frenchie and Machina begin to brawl on the outside, as Dawn quickly covers herself up and Davis attacks Universal. The match progresses all throughout the arena, with Primetime hitting on Dawn from time to time. Moments pass by before Davis throws Frenchie up onto the American commentators table, while Machina and Universal are up on the Spanish commentators table. Suddenly, Davis hits the "Baad Ass Bottom Line" DDT on Frenchie and simultaneously, Universal hits the "Cha-Ching" on Machina on the other table. All four men go crashing through before Davis gets up and enters the ring. Davis raises his arms in the air before Universal grabs a chair, and comes in from behind, before hitting Davis in the back with it. While this goes on, Rudo walks over and rolls Machina into the ring, thinking he's doing him a favor by keeping him from being counted out. With his back turned to the ring; Universal quickly pins Machina and grabs his Internet Title, exiting with Megan Dawn in the midst of boos from the crowd. Primetime & Wilson check on Frenchie, who is buried in debris from the table, as Rudo checks on Machina. Davis sees Universal exiting with the Title, and gets up. He looks over at Rudo and grabs him, hitting the Davis Death Drop on him, before saying "Happy fucking Birthday, you piece of trash!" and exiting to a nice response from the crowd, as Warfare went to a commercial break... Result: Chris Universal by Pinfall Death vs. Cactus Jon Cactus Jon came to the ring to a warm ovation from the crowd, and grabbed the microphone from the ring announcer. Cactus Jon then told the fans that he was going to introduce them to some greats in the world of wrestling, and invited down Frank Snow, Mike Bear, and Tom the Bomb. Cactus Jon told the crowd that since the Dark Riders were into gang attacks, he was going to keep things even for this match. The lights went out then, as Death walked to the ring along with Scott Taylor. The rest of the Dark Riders slowly formed a line at the top of the entrance ramp, looking down at the ring stoicly and quietly. Jon and Death locked up, with neither man getting the edge. Jon swung Death into the ropes, but Death ducked under and hit a stiff lariat that sent Jon to the ground. Death then attempted to drop an elbow, but Jon rolled free and scrambled into the ropes. Jon charged Death as he got to his feet, and knocked him from the ring. As Death tumbled to the outside, Bear, Snow, and Tom all set on Death, attacking him on the outside.
