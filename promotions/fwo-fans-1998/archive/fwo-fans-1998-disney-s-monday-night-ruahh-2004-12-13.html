Introduction Previously... For several months, good wrestlers have fought bad wrestlers. At times bad wrestlers have wrestled bad wrestlers. Occasionally, good wrestlers fight good wrestlers, but usually because of some plot of a bad wrestler. They fight in a ring with ropes, pads, and in front of people. This... is the fWo. Heads I Win, Tails You Lose Jim Dudley sighed and rubbed his temples, resting his elbows on the desk. This scene had been played out too many times in the past few months, and the thought made his head hurt. Broadcast after broadcast, Pay per View after Pay per View, one of them managed to disrupt the show in some form or fashion. After having his ear chewed off for the entire day, Dudley was looking forward to doing a little chewing of his own. He frowned and stared at each member of the buffet standing in front of his desk: Kodiak Vic Creed. Jonathan Gianni. Nick Brandish. Alex Creed. "Gentlemen," Dudley started as he looked from face to face. "I am aware that you're having problems dealing with each other. But it is becoming very clear that your involvement with each other is starting to affect business. That, gentlemen, has repercussions beyond this office. You have been at each other's throats for months. You've interrupted matches, disrupted Pay Per Views -" Alex cut him off. "Hey, I don't know what you're talking about. I haven't been on -" "You interrupted me. Don't do that again." He picked up a small, portable DVD player and set it on the desk so the monitor faced the four men. "As far as your pleas of innocence, I'd like to direct you to this security footage taken at Twilight." The black and white footage began to play. A corridor, empty for the moment. Soon Kodiak Vic Creed and Nick Brandish, both in each others grasp, appeared in view followed by several security officers. Creed slammed Brandish against the wall. Nick shoved him back and drove an uppercut into Creed's head. Two men grabbed Vic by each arm and pulled him away, while four men struggled to restrain Brandish against the wall. In the background, Jonathan Gianni approached the mob scene. He looked frustrated, angry. As soon as he saw Kodiak Vic Creed, he made a beeline through the crowd and drove a fist into the Main Man's face while security held his arms. Before anyone could react, Gianni had peppered Creed with several blows and didn't appear ready to stop anytime soon. From the bottom half of the screen, Alex Creed charged and dove over the first row of security to tackle Gianni. Vic pulled himself free and charged at Brandish, slamming him back against the wall. Security tried in vain to pull the wrestlers apart, their exasperation showing clearly on their faces. Blood spattered onto uniforms. Clothing tore. Heads were driven into the floor. Anarchy. Chaos. Carnage. Dudley turned the movie off and looked up at Alex. "You were saying?" "Uh, nevermind." "As I was saying before," Dudley continued, "your actions are attracting attention in the company beyond this office. Word has come down that this must stop. Mangle each other on a daily basis and nobody would care. Do it on company time and start causing a loss of revenue, and steps must be taken." "I've got no problem ending this once and for all," Brandish growled. Vic turned to the Monster. "I'm right here Slappy. If you're feeling froggy, start jumping." "Start anything in my office and it's the last thing you do in this industry," Dudley threatened. "Even people who think they can't be fired can be punished in other ways." He waited until Vic slowly turned away from Brandish before resuming. "Countdown is approaching. End of the Year, end of old business. You will conclude your business with each other at Countdown." "And I suppose you have a plan for that too, right?" Gianni asked. Dudley smiled. "Of course. That's why I'm on this side of the desk, and you're on that side of the desk. Now, next week the four of you will show up with a type of match in mind. I don't care what kind as long as it starts and ends in the confines of the arena. None of this parking lot brawling garbage. I will flip a coin for you two," he motioned to Vic and Brandish, "and you two," indicating Alex and Gianni. "The winner of the coin toss will wrestle his opponent in that type of match at Countdown." "You're gonna flip a coin? That's it?" Vic inquired. "I've wasted enough production time on you four. I'll splurge a quarter to determine your Fates. Nothing more." With a wave of his hand, Dudley motioned for them all to leave. They would return on Monday, and with one flip of a coin lives were going to change. Careers could potentially be shortened, or ended. The thought brought an extra wide smile to Jim Dudley's face.
