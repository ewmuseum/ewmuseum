fWo — Card

Wednesday Warfare, 21 July 1999

21 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction As Warfare inside the arena opens up, "Baad Ass" Austin Davis makes his way out while his music plays in the background. Whispers of "trust me" begin at first, signalling the entrance of Davis. Davis walks out looking as mad as ever, and appears somewhat nervous, as Friday might well be his last day under fWo contract. Or, it could be the day that he rules the entire wrestling world. Davis climbs up the ringsteps and has a microphone in hand. Davis climbs into the ring and pauses as the excitement builds. The lights above shine off Davis' shaved head, as Davis begins to speak. Davis says; "Tonight could be the last time you see me again on a Wednesday Warfare. Friday could be the last time you ever see me again in an fWo ring. I don't intend for either tonight or Friday to be the last time, but I am the one who made the stipulations for the big match this Friday. I'm the only one who ever had the balls to put their career on the line in order for control of the company. Steve Austin has never done it. Hulk Hogan would never do it. Goldberg won't wrestle because of some damn contract disputes, let alone put his career on the line. Be that as that may, I could not live with myself unless I faced Chris Universal one more time since my career could be over in 48 hours. So, Chris... we've had our history. We're bitter rivals, and we hate each other more than any two people have ever despised each other in the history of mankind, let alone wrestling. I'm calling you out. You and me, one more time, for old time's sake. No title on the line. No disqualification. No countout. No rules, just a referee to count the pinfall in the RING like it should be. If you have the testicular fortitute as our old friend Mick Foley would say, be a man, and accept the challenge". As Davis concludes and prepares to make his exit; Universal appears on the fWo Big Screen above the stage area. Universal says "I'll accept your challenge... because when Austin Davis and Chris Universal go one-on-one, you can bet your happy ass that you'll go home even happier having known that you got your moneys worth from the best damn wrestlers in the world. Each time you and I step in this ring, it's magic. Of course, it's because I carry your ass like no one else can, but that's besides the point. Tonight, you're going to go one-on-one with the $ellout for perhaps the final time... and the Sellout will give you all the credit in the world for being stupid enough to put your career on the line this Friday, but tonight, you're going to go down for the 1-2-3, just like you did in our first match. Trust me, if you buy... what the Sellout... is selling!" With that, Warfare cut away for previews for Summer Slaughter. Silver Fox vs. US Marshal Silver Fox comes to the ring first, and noticably absent from his side are his cronies in crime. The spotlight shines brightly on him, and as always, he gets the red carpet treatment through and through. Silver Fox climbs up into the ring and demands the referee hold the ropes open for him next time, or else he'll suffer the consequences. The US Marshal makes his way out to a nice ovation from the crowd. After a short match which featured both men hitting their favorite moves, US Marshal caught Silver Fox offguard with the Lone Star Stunner. However, Silver Fox kicked out, leaving US Marshal to resort the "Hangman's Noose" for the victory. However, Death hit the ring and through Marshal over the top rope onto the cement floor. He then brutalized Silver Fox, before leaving him flattened in the ring after the "Death Penalty" not once, not twice, but three times before fWo officials and other medical personnel convinced him it might be best to leave. Result: US Marshal by Pinfall Up a notch As Warfare came back from commercial, "Push It" by Static-X played, and Falcon strode to the ring trailed by Wretch. They both entered the ring, and Falcon took a microphone, attempting to talk over a loud chorus of boo's. "Well....I have to say, I'm a bit disappointed." Falcon faked a despondent look, and Wretch peered his head over the mic. "How come?!" "*sigh* I was looking forward to kicking the living crap out of Mike Bear in the back tonight....but he landed himself in the slammer. Kids, you can learn a lesson from Mikey" Falcon points his finger to the camera and makes an informative facial expression. "Crime does not pay." "Seeing as how I have no opponent for tonight, Warfare, or even this Friday's Pay-Per-View event....I'm giving myself a 3 week paid vacation!", but the music was cut short, and out walked fWo Commissioner Travis Beaven with a mic in his hand. "God damn it, Falcon, I have enough to worry about without having to deal with you. Falcon, I make the rules here, not you, and I have a match for you at Summer Slaughter. And here comes your opponent..." At that moment, Psycho Man started playing and the two men in the ring looked like they were trying to exit through the crowd. However, they were intercepted by a man in a TC F'N W t-shirt and a long beard. The man knocked out Wretch with a steel chair, but Falcon ran away and got to the entrance ramp. The man proceeded to sling Wretch over his shoulder, roll him into the ring, and then follow him in. The man grabbed a mic from Matt Panzer. "Falcon, I'm sure you know who I am by now," the man said, ripping off the fake beard to reveal Mike Bear underneath. "That's right, Travis bailed me out of jail. Since I had a lot of time to think in that cell, I came up with a the perfect match. You see Falcon, Summer Slaughter is taking place in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Wisconsin, and more specifically Milwaukee, is famous for two things: beer...and cheese. So, to give the fans a taste of Wisconsin's finest, we're going to be having the very first creamery/brewery match. One across the street from the other. And when I win, I get Muffin back, and we settle this once and for all." Falcon, still carrying his mic, said back, "What? Why in the hell do you think I'd agree to those stipulations? What's in it for me?" A big smile spread across the face of Bear. "Well, Falcon, you don't have much of a choice, given that Travis has already signed your ass for the match." Falcon's face went from angry to worried. "Oh but don't worry Falcon, I'll make this interesting. If I win, I get the dog back, if you win, well, you can have whatever you want from me." A big smile again spread across the face of Falcon. "Anything? I can have anything? Fine, if I win, you never show your face in the fWo again. No six month deal, nothing. You leave the fWo forever." "Done. Travis, write that into the contract. Falcon, get ready to go to hell." Travis walked to the back, a confused look on his face, most likely wondering why Mike would agree to that stipulation. Falcon followed, laughing all the way. Mike remained in the ring, looking determined as Warfare went to commercial. Wolf Fang (c) vs. Scott Hart Scott Hart came to the ring alone, and received a nice reaction from the crowd. His opponent, the reigning fWo Cruiserweight Champion Wolf Fang came out joined by his partner Jackhammer, whose ominous presence surely had to concern Hart. Little did Hart realize that Jackhammer would play a big part in the match later on. The two began a technically sound match, with both men hitting hip tosses, various armdrags, and other old school style moves. With Wolf Fang out on the floor, Hart climbed up to the top rope and prepared to descend off with a cross bodyblock. However, Jackhammer moved in front of Wolf Fang and caught Hart. He then slammed him back first into the ring post, and rolled him in the ring. Fang then joined Jackhammer in the ring, and the referee signalled for the bell, disqualifying Fang. Fang shouted at Jackhammer for interferring, and the two almost came to blows before cooler heads prevailed. As they were preparing to leave, Jackhammer grabbed Hart and squeezed the life out of Hart with a massive bearhug. This once again ticked Fang off, who literally had to pull his bigger partner off Hart... the two argued up the ramp and into the backstage area, as Fang blamed Jackhammer for costing him the match... Result: Scott Hart by DQ The trap In the back of the arena, the rented car of Briana arrived and Briana exited, stopping to retrieve her bags from the back of the car. Briana was dressed in a leather trenchcoat and knee-high boots, and had her hair pulled back in a loose fitting ponytail. Briana walked slowly to the arena VIP entrance, but halted when the door opened and Fyre stood blocking her path. Raising her arms in confusion, Briana warned Fyre to get out of her way. Fyre spit at Briana, and the young woman charged the valet of Silver Fox, taking the girl off her feet and drove her back into the steel door. As Briana continued to attack the woman, she failed to notice footsteps behind her until it was too late. The Chameleon, Total Eclipse, and Silver Fox attacked her from behind, tossing her into one of the nearby cars and shattering the windshield in a spray of glass and blood. The Brawler stood nearby, one arm in a sling and brace, as the rest of Silver Fox's men continued to batter the weakened and helpless Briana. As fWo security arrived on the scene, Silver Fox's crew left laughing, the damage already done... Jackhammer vs. D-Lo Brown Jackhammer came to the ring, ready for action and seemingly oblivious to what just happened to Briana backstage. Inside the ring Jackhammer grabbed the ropes and stretched out, preparing himself for the match. His opponent, D-Lo Brown, came to the ring after many weeks of absence in the fWo, and grabbed the microphone. D-Lo informed the crowd that this Sunday he was booked to get the REAL European belt back around his waist, and that Black Quicksilver could keep the stinky crappy one he wore. D-Lo then attacked Jackhammer with a shot to the jaw, and flipped him up and over for a standing back body drop. D-Lo stayed on the attack by attempting to control the pace of the match with an armbar, but Jackhammer's size allowed him to reverse the hold, and toss D-Lo to the mat. D-Lo came off the ropes with a flying splash, but was caught by Jackhammer and slammed to the mat. Jackhammer attempted a cover, but at that moment footage from Briana's entrance was shown on the screen. Jackhammer, who had been walking to the ring when the attack happened looked up at the scene for the first time, and was distracted by the shots of his partner being assaulted. D-Lo took the distraction to best benefit, landing a DDT and then the low down for a successful pinfall victory. Jackhammer, worried about the status of his partner for Friday night ran to the backstage area... Result: D-Lo Brown by Pinfall Who's watching John Skyler? From an unknown location, mysterious figures watched a television screen, showing a direct feed from one of the offices in the depths of the arena. On the screen, John Skyler was seen, lying prone on a cot while doctors and psychologists studied the young man. Skyler appeared to be sleeping, but suddenly his eyes began to flutter and he bolted upright where he lay and began to scream, "SHE'S DEAD! SHE'S DEAD!" Doctors rushed into the room and administered a shot to the arm of Skyler, and after a few tense moments he feel back down again, sleeping a harsh and solid sleep. A feminine hand reached out and shut off the set, chuckling as she did so. Just out of sight, a mysterious figure said, "He's lost his mind... good work, Dina..." Prophet vs. Primetime Rock music and fireworks signaled the arrival of Prophet, who walked to the ring from the crowd, slapping hands and smiling as he went. Inside the ring Prophet flexed for the audience, as Primetime's name was called. The French Foundation member walked to the ring, dressed in a heavy denim jacket and slapping the hands of the fans as he went. Primetime was nearly to the ring when Nikki ran up behind him, asking him why he didn't come to her hotel room the night before. Primetime blushed and rubbed the back of his head, explaining that it was ten 'o clock by the time he got in town, and that it wasn't right for him to come calling so late. Nikki looked confused by this answer, and Primetime told Nikki that he would make it up to her by taking her to the carnival tonight so they could ride the bumper cars. Nikki retorted that she would rather bump him in bed with the lights off, and Primetime stepped back aghast. Telling Nikki that she needed to eat with that filthy mouth, Primetime swung into the ring as a very confused Nikki walked backstage, a small tear in her eye. Primetime extended a hand to Prophet, who shook in gladly before locking up. Primetime attacked with a side Russian leg sweep, and then followed it up with a drop elbow into the back of the larger man. Primetime went for a cover, but was easily thrown off by the big man. Prophet ducked a lariat attempt from Primetime, and caught him on the return with a spinning heel kick. Another cover proved futile, as Primetime began to gain the advantage with hard chops to the chest. As Primetime went for his finisher, Prophet raked the eyes of the French Foundation member, causing him to stumble back in a daze. Prophet quickly hit a swinging neck breaker and covered Primetime for the surprise victory. Annoyed at this turn of events, Primetime explained to the ref that cheating was wrong, and that by allowing this kind of behavior from Prophet they would be setting a bad example for the children. Result: Prophet by Pinfall Something fishy going on Matt Loco wandered backstage, looking for someone to pester. As he turned a corner, he discovered a man dressed in a strange gray plastic costume with a fin protruding from his back. Matt Loco scratched his head, confused at this sight, and he cleared his throat to gain the man's attention. Mike Heftel turned around, and waved happily has he placed a gray skull cap over his head. Loco took a step back in surprise of this revelation, and said, "My god, Mike... what's going on with you man?" Heftel smiled and waved a hand, which had a strange gray glove on it that shimmered in the light. He responded, "I'm not a man, I'm a fish! EEEE EEEE EEEE!" With that Heftel wandered away, and Loco called out after him, "But wait! A dolphin's not a fish. It's a mammal...." The Dolphin vs. Cactus Jon Tranquil sounds of the sea filled the arena with the occasional burst of Dolphin speak, as the ring announcer introduced the debut of "The Dolphin". The Dolphin came to the ring wearing an gray body suit with a skull cap and a fin, wiggling from side to side as he walked, simulating swimming. When he reached the ring apron, he dove up at it, trying to "flip" himself inside. The Dolphin ran in circles inside the ring, "swimming" in circles for the crowd. The music of Cactus Jon began to play, and the Canadian made his way to the ring shaking his head at having to be in a match with such a pathetic wrestler like this one. Climbing inside, Jon told the Dolphin to have some dignity and just go now. The Dolphin responded by spraying water from his mouth at Jon, and then "swimming" in circles around him. Jon, growing ever increasingly annoyed, grabbed the Dolphin by the neck and delivered a hard choke slam. Jon covered the Dolphin, but because of the fin on his back the Dolphin's shoulders would not hit the mat, and thus could not be pinned. Frustrated, Jon worked at ripping the fin off, as the Dolphin delivered multiple head butts at the wrestler. Unable to get the fin removed, Jon kicked the Dolphin to the mat and applied a Sharpshooter submission hold in the center of the ring, and the Dolphin quickly tapped out. Jon removed the hold with disgust, telling the cameras that "Scott Slugger was going to look even more pathetic than crazy Mike Heftel here on Friday." Inside the ring, the ref helped the Dolphin get to his feet, and out of the ring. The fin kept getting stuck on the ring ropes, and additional fWo officials hit the scene to assist... Result: Cactus Jon by Submission Eron's foul mood Backstage, the US Marshal was getting ready to leave the arena, excited about the prospect of his Internet Title Shot at the next fWo House Show, Hotter Than Hell I. The Marshal had finished packing up his bags and was ready to go when Eron the Relentless walked by, and grabbed the hat off of the Marshal's head. Laughing at the large wrestler, Eron punched a fist through it, and tossed the remains back at the Marshal. The Marshal turned and began to scold Eron, which only served to further anger the superstar. Eron spit at the Marshal, and then charged him, spearing him back against the concrete wall. fWo officials arrived on the scene and pulled the two men apart, as Eron yelled, "You want a piece of this? Huh? Come get it cowgirl..." Rain (c) vs. Ice Ice walked to the ring with Scott Slugger, looking huge and menacing as he walked. Grabbing the microphone, Scott Slugger told the crowd that tonight both of them would have titles... he the World Heavyweight title, and Ice the Hardcore title. Ice flexed his muscles and awaited Rain, who entered the arena amidst swirling rain and lightning. Rain walked slowly to the ring, and inside walked directly up to Ice, standing nearly eye to eye with the big man. Rain and Ice locked up, with Ice taking a quick shot to the groin with one knee. This put Rain on the defensive, and Ice methodically pounded Rain in the corner with hard lefts and rights. Rain ducked under one of the punches and came up from behind, delivering a deadly belly-to-back suplex that sent Ice crashing to the mat. Rain dropped an elbow and then went to the second rope, measuring and flying off with another elbow. Rain covered Ice, but Scott Slugger climbed onto the entrance ramp and distracted the referee. Rain left Ice and walked over to Slugger, threatening him on the ring apron. Just then, the crowd came to their feet and Crucifix walked to the ring holding the Hardcore title up for the crowd. Spotting his nemesis, Rain pointed up at Crucifix and the weather began to turn foul once more. Rain ran up to meet Crucifix, but as he got close Crucifix pulled a small dagger from his pocket and plunged it into the side of Rain, who at once collapsed in agony. Rain had managed to duck to one side so the knife entered the skin indirectly, but he still began to bleed badly, and with the rain falling from the rafters the blood pooled and mixed with the water to cause a red stream that coasted down the ramp. Yelling, Crucifix threw the Hardcore title down at Rain, telling him that this Friday he would be taking it back for good... Result: Ice by Count Out Just three moves Ice and Slugger began to leave the ring, but suddenly Kid Cool appeared on the entrance ramp, dressed in his wrestling attire, and asked the pair where they were going and if they were scared of facing him. Ice and Slugger exchanged a glance and then laugh, bidding Kid Cool to step into the ring. Holding the microphone, Slugger asked Kid Cool how he expected to beat the two, and Cool responded that he knew all three of the key wrestling moves and was the technical expert in his wrestling school. Kid Cool entered the ring and quickly ran to Slugger, hitting him with a weak chop that was barely heard in the arena. Laughing, Slugger allowed Kid Cool to toss him into the ropes, and took a clothesline from the young man. Cool quickly went for a cover, but Slugger tossed the boy off with little trouble. Now worried, Kid Cool backed up into the ropes as Slugger took the microphone again. Incredulously, Slugger asked, "That's it? That's all you know? A chop, a clothesline and a pinfall? What about this third move of yours?" Stammering, Kid Cool answered that the Pinfall WAS the third move, causing Ice and Slugger to laugh loudly once more. Together, Ice and Slugger grabbed Kid Cool, and Ice delivered chokeslam after chokeslam as Slugger counted to ten. Leaving the boy laying in the ring, Ice and Slugger walked backstage again, laughing loudly as they went... Chris Universal vs. Austin Davis Warfare returns from the commercial break as The fWo Internet Champion "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal makes his way out to a rousing ovation of boos from the crowd. Some cheers are heard when Megan Dawn comes out and appears at his side. Universal is announced as standing 6'3, weighing in at 245 pounds and hailing from Miami, Florida. Universal climbs up the ringsteps and holds the ropes open for Dawn, who carries the Internet Title for him. Universal is handed the Internet Title by Dawn, and Universal holds the Internet Title high above his head while the dollar $ign pyrotechnic lowers into place. Green and gold fireworks shoot all over the arena, while Universal poses. Universal hands the Internet Title to Dawn who knows her role and goes outside the ring. For the second time tonight, we hear the eerie whispers of "trust me" before "Baad Ass" Austin Davis makes his way out to a mixed reaction from the crowd, although mostly jeers and boos. They announce Davis as from Death Valley, Arizona and weighing in at 250 pounds, while standing at six-foot, two-inches tall. Davis (sporting his usual black vest) climbs into the ring and stairs at Universal. The two come face-to-face, nose-to-nose and chest-to-chest in the middle of the ring. Davis and Universal exchange verbal pleasantries as they talk trash with each other back and forth. Universal takes off his gold necklace and throws it into Davis' face. Davis catches the necklace before it falls to the ground. He looks at it, and spits a chunk of saliva on it. He then clinches it in his right hand, and hauls off and hits Universal with a hard right hand. Universal reacts with a right hand of his own. The two continue to exchange punches back and forth. The two begin rolling on the ground exchanging solid punches... brawling all over the ring. Davis gets the upper hand with a shot to the jaw... he then brings Universal to his feet. Davis kicks Universal in the stomach and whips him into the turnbuckles. He follows it up by walking into the corner and nailing Universal with a bevy of punches. Davis takes his vest off and wraps it around the neck of Universal, choking him. He then uses both hands and throws Universal across the ring, using the vest in his advantage. Universal gets back to his feet a few seconds later and Davis damn near takes his head off his neck with a clothesline from heaven. Davis grabs Universal and throws him over the top rope out onto the floor. Davis climbs through the ropes and waits on the ring apron. Universal eventually gets up, and Davis dives off with an axehandle into the head, knocking Universal back onto the ground. Davis brings Universal to his feet, grabs him by the arm, spins around and pulls Universal into him and takes him down with a short-arm clothesline. Davis brings Universal back to his feet and sends him into the ring steps with an Irish whip, but Universal reverses it at the last moment and Davis goes flying knee first into the steps. Universal jaws with a fan and then tells the fan to get his ass off the chair... the fan says no at first, but Universal raises his hand as if to backhand him, and the fan obliges. Universal grabs the chair and waits for Davis to get up. Limping, Davis finally does and Universal slams the end of the chair into the leg of Davis. The referee is out on the floor and is attempting to get the action back into the ring, but can do nothing more than that because this is a no-dq, no count-out match. Universal lifts the chair up, and strikes it back down into the thigh of Davis. Universal brings Davis over by the guardrail and slams him head first into it. Universal then takes him over near the ring post and slams him head first into it. Universal then whips Davis back first into the guardrail and the whole guardrail topples over into the crowd. Universal stomps at the head of Davis several times, before bringing Davis up to his feet and dragging him up into the crowd. The two take turns slamming each other into various parts of the arena walls, and exchanging punches. Davis attempts a piledriver on a staired portion of the arena, but Universal backdrops him and Davis lands on part of the stairs with a sickening thud. The crowd 'pops' as a result. Universal brings Davis back to his feet and down back near another part of the arena... he tosses him up and over the guardrail. Universal climbs up onto the guardrail and waits for Davis to get up. Davis eventually does, and Universal flies off with a double-axehandle... but Davis counters by ducking out of the way, and nailing Universal with a shot to the stomach as he lands on the floor. Davis follows that up by bringing Universal to his feet and hitting a swinging neckbreaker on the floor. Davis rolls Universal into the ring, and soon thereafter follows him in. As Universal lays stomach-first on the mat, Davis stomps at the back of his head several times. Davis then brings Universal to his feet and throws him into the ropes. Universal returns and as Davis goes for a hiptoss, Universal counters into a float-over ddt. A few moments pass by, before Universal gets back to his feet and applies a Boston Crab to the back of Davis. It appears as if Davis will have to submit, but he musters up some strength to crawl over to the bottom rope. Universal finally releases the hold, but pushes the referee to the mat afterwards. Universal brings Davis to his feet and hammers him with some shots to the midsection... as Davis is bent over, Universal bounces off the ropes on the other side of the ring and as he returns, Davis reaches up and grabs him by the hair and throws him over the top rope onto the floor. Davis then gives Universal the double finger, and tells him politely to "go to fucking hell". Davis climbs outside onto the ring apron, and waits for Universal to get up. Davis flies off with a clothesline, and hits it. Davis then goes over by the announcers tables and grabs a chair. He slides it into the ring, before going back over to Universal and throwing him shoulder first into the ringsteps, which literally knocks the ringsteps out of position. Davis goes over near the main broadcast position where Finn & Taylor are located and he mouths off them. Davis mentions to Finn about Beaven, telling him that "he's next". Meanwhile, Universal staggers about up onto the ring apron. Universal is back-first to the ring ropes, and facing the crowd. While Universal is trying to pull himself together, and is obviously exhausted, worn out, and delirious from this all out battle, Davis grabs a part of the electrical cords used for microphones, monitors, and other items. He brings it up into the ring and sneaks up behind Universal. He carefully manipulates the cord around Universal' neck, and begins choking him as Universal continues to face the crowd. Moments pass by before Universal reaches up and grabs Davis by the head. Universal drops down to the floor while continuing to hold onto Davis' head, which brings Davis throat first down on the top rope. Universal, with the help of Megan Dawn, relieves himself of the electrical wiring down onto the floor. Davis is dazed on the mat back in the ring... Universal plots out his next move. He grabs Davis by the legs and pulls him over to the ring post. Universal pulls Davis crotch first into the ring post. Universal then goes back into the ring. He brings Davis back up to his feet, and hits a side-Russian legsweep. He goes for a cover, but only gets a two. Universal bounces off the ropes and hits a perfectly executed elbowdrop, and goes for another cover. Davis barely lifts his shoulder up off the mat at the last second, and this leaves Universal frustrated. Universal brings Davis to his feet and whips him back first into the turnbuckles. Davis hits into the corner with a thud, but stays in the corner. Universal follows it up by running at full speed ahead, attempting a shoulder block into the midsection of Davis. Davis moves at the last moment however, and Universal goes flying into the ringpost shoulder first. He hits hard, before dropping out onto the ring apron and trying to hold on using the middle rope. This buys some time for Davis to recover. Davis does, and he reaches out and grabs Universal by the head. He prepares for a vertical suplex, in an attempt to bring Universal back in to the ring. Instead, however, he props Universal up onto the top rope. Davis climbs up onto the middle ropes. He nails Universal with a shot to the head. He follows it up by hooking Universal for a superplex. Both men go flying into the air onto the mat, but Universal feels a majority of the blow. Several moments pass by before Davis musters up enough strength to put his right arm over Universal. One, two, but somehow, Universal is able to kick out! Davis struggles up to his knees, and plots his next move. He grabs Universal and dumps him over by the commentators tables out onto the cement floor. He waits for Universal to get back up to a vertical base, before bouncing off the ropes on the other side and baseball sliding onto the mat out underneath the bottom ropes with a pair of boots to the head, which sends Universal sprawling back up onto the American announcers table. Universal is sprawled out backfirst on the table now, and Davis climbs back into the ring. He goes up onto the top rope and beings talking trash down at Universal. Davis then flies off with a an elbowdrop, but Universal moves and Davis goes crashing down through the table by himself! Finn and Taylor are soon covered in monitors, and other assorted items that were once on their table. Megan Dawn comes to the aid of Universal, making sure he's okay. Universal appears to be alright and he reaches down and slaps the face of Davis. He follows it up with another slap. He then brings Davis to his feet and slams him head first onto the Spanish announcers table. He props him up onto the table and follows him up. He kicks Davis in the stomach, before hitting the "Cha-Ching!" finisher through the table on Davis! Shards of wood, glass, and monitors end up all over the place as Pepe and Jose run for their lives! Universal is still in control here, and Davis has gone through two tables here tonight just as Davis put Universal through two tables back at CyberSlam. Universal slaps Davis across the face, before rolling him into the ring. He goes for a cover now... one... two... no! Somehow, Davis gets a shoulder up and Universal is frustrated. Universal spots the chair that has been lying in the corner for quite awhile, and he brings it over. He waits for Davis to crawl back up to his feet. Slowly, but surely, Davis does. Universal prepares to take Davis' head off with a charging chairshot to the head, but at the last minute Davis pulls the referee in front of him and instead, the referee receives the chairshot. The referee goes down instantly, and is out cold on the mat. Universal looks down at the referee, with a look of concern on his face. Meanwhile, Davis sneaks up from behind, and nails him with a lowblow. Universal drops the chair on the mat. Davis spins Universal around, and nails a Death Valley Driver on the chair. The referee remains crumpled in a heap on the mat as Davis looks up at the stage. Quickly, a second referee comes charging down to make the pinfall. Davis goes to cover Universal... and at the count of two, Megan Dawn comes into the ring and rakes the back of Davis. This stops the count, and Davis gets up and looks at Dawn. He methodically stalks her into the corner. He reaches out and grabs her by the hair as she begs for his mercy, but Davis will give her none tonight. Universal, however, has something to say about that and snakes up and rolls up Davis for a pin. One, two, but Davis kicks out. Davis is back to his feet first, he waits for Universal to turn around. Davis with a kick to the testicular area, and he follows it up with a fisherman's suplex. One, two, but again, Megan Dawn interferes with a kick of her own to testicular area of Davis. It affects Davis greatly because she was wearing high heels. Davis is now clutching himself on the mat, writhing in pain. Universal follows it up by bringing Davis up, and hitting the "Cha-Ching!" in the middle of the ring. Universal goes for a cover, but the Black Panther runs down to ringside and grabs Megan Dawn. Universal spots this and relinquishes the cover. He walks over and as Panther has Dawn in his clutches, he begins mouthing off at Panther. Panther moves Dawn out of his way, and kicks Universal in the stomach. Davis is back on his feet, and he turns Universal around, and hits the "Baad Ass Bottom Line" DDT on the chair. One... two... but Universal again somehow kicks out! Meanwhile, Panther scoops Dawn up, and drapes her across his shoulder. He hauls her out of the ring and back up the ramp, much to the pleasure of Davis, who had the match won several times but didn't because of her. Davis brings Universal to his feet and whips him to the ropes. Universal returns but ducks the Davis clothesline... Universal bounces off the ropes and returns again. Kick by Davis. "Cha-Ching!" by Davis. Pinfall attempt by Davis. Three seconds later, and Davis has won the match! Davis rolls off Universal and sits on the mat. The crowd is applauding the efforts of both men here... the place literally looks like a warzone, with pieces of guardrail flattened on the floor, and all of the commentators table smashed into a million pieces. Davis raises his arms and the crowd applauds him as he and Universal put on a hellofamatch. Suddenly, the lights go out. The arena is filled with complete darkness, and nobody can see what is going on. The lights come back on and Scott Slugger & Ice are in the ring. A look of shock comes over Davis' face. The two throw Davis into the ropes, and as Davis bounces off, the duo bend over for a double-backdrop. Davis returns and nails both of them with a ddt. The lights suddenly go out and the arena is in complete darkness again. Moments pass by before the lights come back and Commissioner Travis is in the ring. Travis shouts at Davis before Davis charges at him and spears him into the mat. The lights go out again. Nobody can see a damn thing as it appears as if arena officials didn't pay the bills this month. Lights come back on once again, and a masked man wearing a tye-dye shirt carrying a cane is in the ring. Davis looks at him and although you can't hear it, you can clearly see Davis saying "what the fuck?" before the man canes Davis. Blood immediately rushes from his forehead and Travis, the masked man, Slugger & Ice tie Davis up into the ropes. Suddenly, Ultra-Violet & Cactus Jon appear on the stage. Slugger & Ice spot them and make their way up there. The four men brawl on the stage for a few seconds before the brawl escalates backstage. Travis laughs at Davis while the masked man continues to cane Davis, and Travis points at the masked man, telling Davis that "this is what awaits you on Friday... and after Friday, your career will be over". With that, cameras go off the air as Warfare comes to a close... Result: Austin Davis by Pinfall
