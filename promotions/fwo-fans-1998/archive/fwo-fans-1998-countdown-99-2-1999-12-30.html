Countdown '99: 2, 30 December 1999

30 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "Off through the new day's mist I run Out from the new day's mist I have come I hunt Therefore I am Harvest the land Taking of the fallen lamb." -Metallica "Glory days well they'll pass you by Glory days in the wink of a young girl's eye Glory days, glory days." -Bruce Springsteen Chi-town is known for cold weather in the winter. The sun was starting to set as the skies became a dark hue of gray, and despite it being still early cars ran with their headlights on. Fans waited in line to be let into the arena, shivering slightly in the cold and talking quietly among themselves. Betrayal was hardly anything new to wrestling. Many Chicago residents remembered events that had transpired on the second of September during the previous year. Scott Taylor had been appointed the commissioner of the fWo then, and of course had betrayed Cactus Jon and the rest of the fWo by aligning himself with the Dark Riders. It is written that those who do not learn from history are destined to repeat it, and certainly this seemed to be the case as the Millennium drew to a close on the fWo. With the alignment of the owner of the promotion and the greatest enemy the fWo had ever faced, things appeared darker than ever before. But even in the darkest times things can get worse. Joining forces Backstage the atmosphere was dark and quiet. The usually jubilant atmosphere faded moments after the owner of the promotion and the commissioner of the fWo joined with Death... and turned his back on the wrestlers he once helped lead to stardom. This night all was silent, and only a handful of security personnel and technicians wandered the halls, going about their jobs with a silent demeanor. A pair of workers carried a large camera case into the arena, but were surprised by the opening fireworks of the show. They dropped the case, and a large white envelope that had been tucked into the case fell free and drifted to the ground. The technical staff cursed themselves and picked the case up again, and one of them kicked the envelope under a nearby locker room door. The door had been locked, but the space beneath the door was wide enough for the envelope to slip under and it skidded across the tile floor until it hit the boot of Havok. The wrestler ignored it and continued to focus his attention on the other workers in the room. It was an odd collection of wrestlers, untied by a common threat. The Boston Strangler, Primetime, Mike Bear, Wretch, Wolf Fang, Machina, Briana and Havok stood in a small circle, and by the grim expressions on their faces it was clear that serious business was afoot. The Strangler spoke, "We've got a problem... and unless we work together with all of you then we will fail." Havok nodded. "I think you speak for all of us here... and many more that aren't here now. Nobody likes what has become of this promotion, and as long as Travis is aligned with that bastard we've all got to work toward a common goal." Wolf Fang nodded, but then turned and addressed Havok. "You're right... but sadly this isn't a fight that I can fight with all of you. Wolf Fang works alone... and as of right now I'm leaving." Wolf Fang exited the room and a confused Havok followed him out, unsure of why his partner in the Millennium Men was abandoning them. Machina cleared his throat, and the other wrestlers in the room turned with a frown toward him. Bearing a wine bottle Machina said, "Listen everyone... I realize that many of you might not remember me ever since Bruce Springsteen appeared in the fWo, but I've got another problem... Billy James is missing. Ever since that crystal thing was smashed... I can't find Billy anywhere. It's like... it's like he just disappeared." Mike Bear waved Machina aside and moved into the center of the room. "Listen everyone. If we cut off the head we solve the problem. Going after the Riders won't solve anything... we must stop the problem at the source. Our target is Beaven... and we've got to get to him." Primetime clapped Bear on the shoulder. "Bear's right. We've got to put aside our other problems until this is solved... or until we are all out of a job. I'm going to find the Frenchie and bring him back to the states... the rest of you have to get to the commissioner." The wrestlers in the room nodded, and the camera went to the ring... Courage vs. Austin The Giant Jake walked to the ring next to Austin the Giant, and the pair swung into the ring with a flair. Jake held his tubby arms high above his head and jumped up and down, his rolls of flab shaking and bouncing in the air like a massive bowl of jelly. Austin the Giant removed his jacket and flexed against the ropes, ready for action. The music of the French Foundation played, and Courage walked to the ring with the French flag waving above him. Nearing the ring, Courage circled to the announcing table and picked up a microphone. Pointing at Jake he said, "You just sit tight tubby... I'll deal with you and your freak friend in a second, but right now I've got a little message for the so-called commissioner of this promotion. I don't know if it was you or some stupid trick of Rain that got the Frenchie deported... but Primetime and I aren't going to rest until he's back in the fWo where he belongs... and with the World title around his waist. You or your four clowns of the apocalypse want to get in our way? Please do... nothing would give me greater pleasure than kicking your ass." With that Courage slid into the ring and at once went after both Jake and Austin, trading punches between the two. Jake tumbled from the ring as Courage turned his attention to Austin, and pitched him into the ropes. Courage leveled the big man with a clothesline, and then urged Austin to get back to his feet. Austin did, but ducked under Courage's running lariat and grabbed him from behind executing a quick, tight suplex. Austin removed the strap of his singlet and picked up Courage, pressing him up into the air and letting him fall to the mat with a hard thud. Austin went for a cover, but Courage kicked out at two. Austin went to the second rope and jumped off with a flying forearm, but Courage ducked under and executed a standing kick from behind. Austin tumbled into the ropes and was quickly tangled up inside them. Laughing, Courage methodically struck with punches to the head and gut of Austin who was helpless to defend himself. The referee finally succeeded in releasing Austin and the big man tumbled to the ground. Pointing at Jake on the outside, Courage climbed to the top turnbuckle and signaled for his finisher. He leapt off with a springboard moonsault and hit Austin square on, and covered him for the three count. Courage stood and again cursed at Jake on the outside, when suddenly the lights went out... Result: Courage by Pinfall Reasons As Courage, Jake and Austin left through the crowd, fireworks exploded at the top of the entrance ramp. Demon appeared at the ring entrance, and cackling to himself he slunk down to the ring, followed closely behind by a nervous looking Fallen. Poison came next, but the eyes of the fans were at ringside, where Demon had grabbed a cameraman and began to claw and tear at him, screaming as he did so. Fallen pulled him free, looking more and more uncomfortable by the second as he restrained his partner in David Spencer's Dark Riders. The man himself now made an appearance at the top of the ramp, and the new fWo World Champion Death made his way to the ring, standing next to the owner and commissioner of the fWo, Travis Beaven. The pair walked silently to the ring, and once there joined the other three Dark Riders inside the ring. The five made an unlikely grouping, and the fans loudly booed this new faction. The fWo World Heavyweight title around Death's waist appeared to have been crudely stained dark red, and as this was noticed the champion removed the belt and held it up for the booing crowd with a sneer. Death reached for a microphone, but before it was handed to him the commissioner snatched it and addressed the crowd. "Last night marked a change in the way this company works... and... listen, shut up all of you, or there won't be another god damn show in this town ever again! This is my company, you are all my fans, and if I want to show you nothing but interviews from the Chameleon all night long, that's exactly what you all will get! Now as it just so happens, I've run this company from nothing to the biggest promotion the world has to offer. There are hundreds if not thousands of workers just waiting and begging for the chance to hold my microphone... and what has all of this success gotten me? Respect? Hardly. The workers that I helped bring to stardom mock me... they don't respect me... and they don't give a damn if I live or if I die. You can blame this alliance you see before you on the workers you cheered. Scott Slugger? A miserable excuse for a worker that has climbed to the top of the wrestling world because I deem it so. The Flying Frenchie? The only reason he's not busting his ass in a coffee house is because I willed it otherwise. Austin Davis? Well, you all saw what happened when he crossed me once too often. When I was brutally attacked by this man..." Travis pointed to David Spencer, who was towering behind him with an unreadable expression. "When I was attacked did the fWo workers rally to my side? Did any of them avenge my legacy? Hardly. So I did what I had to do... and that was do something that none of your heroes and wrestlers had the strength to do... and that's bring this man... bring Death... under my control. Under my rule. Now this title that I have given so much meaning to is mine to control the way it was always meant to be. This champion that I have selected will defeat all those who stood in my path, and will restore the proper respect to the promotion... and it's owner... that it deserves. Only I choose who gets a title shot... and nobody else will ever get close to this title, ever again." With this speech concluded, Travis stood in the face of the boos and calls of disgust by the thousands of fans in attendance. A smirk was on his face, but it faltered when David Spencer grabbed the microphone away from him with a wide and deadly smile... Things change "How delightful for you to give all these fools a history lesson... but you're wasting your time, Beaven." Death held the fWo World Heavyweight Championship in one mighty first and the microphone in the other, and by the confused expression that the commissioner wore it was clear that what was happening was not according to plan. Death looked around the arena, and the smile was still worn on his face. "Perhaps it is time to remind all of you of our... charter. I formed the Dark Riders a long time ago... back before this promotion existed. We went from territory to territory... and those that stood in our path soon feel. We came, we took what we wished, and when he left there was little more than an empty husk that remained. But so consuming was this task that we set ourselves to that I failed to notice that the men that I had selected to be my missionaries had grown weak and pathetic. The cracks first showed themselves in Stevie Roberts... but soon War and Famine showed themselves to be the pathetic fools that they are. My goal to destroy this promotion was at risk... and thus I left to search for the cause once again. The fWo went on, and it was obvious that everyone had gone their own way... and I resigned myself to the fact that evil will stop here, my mission complete. Retired and forgotten, I faded into obscurity... that is, until a young woman came to me asking to be shown what it means to hold power. Young Dina Sol rekindled the evil that had fallen asleep within, and my mission to destroy the fWo was reborn. Thankfully, you've now helped me achieve that goal... and now that you have... your usefulness is at an end." Fallen and Demon grabbed the commissioner from behind, and restrained him against the ropes as Death held the World Title in front of him. Baring his teeth Travis yelled, "Dammit... you're my champion... this wasn't part of the deal!" Death laughed a deep, guttural laugh. "I have altered the deal, pray I don't alter it any further. You remain alive... it is only your promotion that is coming to an end. Tomorrow night I burn this title... and I burn this arena to the ground. The next thousand years will remember only one thing... and that is misery. Now take him... and remove him from the arena of Death." Travis was drug away kicking and screaming as the laughter of Death rang out through the arena, as the fans continued to boo in disgust... Havok (c) vs. Wretch Inside the ring stood Wretch, with Fear and Mike Bear standing on the outside. Cameras turned to the backstage area and picked up Havok exiting the locker room he had been inside earlier with the fWo Cruiserweight Championship over his shoulder. He looked from side to side as he walked, and did not notice the white envelope that he kicked out into the hallway. The envelope spiraled down the hall and out of sight as Havok turned to the arena entrance and walked down the entrance ramp. He looked at the fans and held the world title up as he walked, patting it as he went. As he slid into the ring he exchanged words with Wretch and Bear on the outside, and cameras picked up him asking the two if they had seen what had just transpired between Travis and Death. They nodded, and as the bell rang Mike Bear and Fear walked to the head of the entrance ramp and stood guard there. Wretch and Havok shook hands, and then locked up in the center of the ring. Wretch took the upper hand and trapped Havok in a head lock, attempting to keep him in the center of the ring. Havok struggled, and rolled himself under Wretch and grabbed him from behind. Havok held the neck and hip and worked Wretch into a side submission hold. The hold was too weak however, and Wretch easily made it to the ropes to break the hold. The two men separated, and they circled each other. Wretch reached out first and caught Havok's head, but Havok reached up and grabbed his arm and pulled him toward a standing lariat. Wretch ducked under the arm and pulled Havok's arm up, pushing him into a armbar. Havok struggled, and finally simply fell backward, crushing Wretch between him and the mat. Havok rolled free and caught his breath as he watched Wretch slowly get to his feet. Havok launched himself in a running spear, and both men were carried by the momentum to the outside. They continued to battle around the ring, and now the quick attack and counter nature of their battle had distracted their attentions, and before the wrestlers realized it they had been counted out. Upon hearing the bell to signal the end of the match Havok and Wretch took notice of the referee and where they were, and turned slowly to each other. Nodding they shook hands, and Wretch growled, "This isn't over... remember that." Havok nodded. "No... it's not." The group of wrestlers walked back to the backstage area, in search of their allies... Result: Double Count Out More mysteries Cameras located Scott Slugger somewhere deep in the arena nodding. It was dark around the former champion, and he wore a bandage over his head where it had been driven into the monitor. He rubbed the area now, wincing slightly at the pain. Around him were the common objects found in a boiler room, and wafts of steam floated up from the floor. He was speaking to someone, but it was impossible to tell exactly who it was through the smoke and natural darkness of the room. Something was said that was inaudible, and Slugger nodded. "You're right... they will never know what hit them." Before the mysterious figure could respond to this, the cameraman bumped into one of the pipes that was protruding from the cold floor. Slugger turned and spotted the man, and a frown crossed over his face. He moved to intercept the man, and as Slugger's fist flew through the air the image quickly turned into loud static... Rain (c) vs. Ice Security stood in front of Rain's locker room with metal batons and rapped loudly on his door. When no answer came they broke the door down, and found Rain sitting alone in the center of the silent room. Rain looked up uneasily and when the security men came toward him he reached out and grabbed one of the men as if to make sure they actually existed. The security man batted away the hand and grabbed Rain up, escorting him out of the room. Rain looked confused as he was drug deep into the arena, and finally tossed inside the boiler room. Rain stood alone in the dark room and jumped when the bell rang from inside the arena. The dark shadows hid much of the room, and Rain looked around himself until he heard approaching footsteps. Ice came out from the darkness, and on his face he wore a dark smile. "You're being punished boy. We both are. We've both been locked in this room... and we are supposed to kill each other. You owe me a great deal Rain... and I don't see your little army of ghosts around to help you now. Only one of us is leaving this room..." Ice bellowed and charged, and Rain was driven backward into the concrete wall. Rain guarded his face as Ice began to lob heavy blows to the face of the Internet Champion, and finally was able to duck away as Ice's fist rammed into the wall with a spray of dust and plaster. Rain backed away and reached for a weapon, finding one in the form of a steel pipe leaning against one of the large boilers. Rain swung and Ice ducked and ran forward. Rain's blow swung into simple air as Ice grabbed his midsection and carried him backward into the steel. Ice continued to punch at Rain as the Internet champion struggled to get away. Rain kicked upward into the groin of Ice, and he collapsed in pain as Rain wiped blood from his lip and looked down at his opponent. Unsteady on his feet, Rain paced the room and picked up a crate to toss at Ice. It shattered over Ice's back, and the challenger let out a gasp of pain. Rain walked to the door to the boiler room on unsteady feet, but before he could grasp the handle Ice attacked from behind. Down on his hands and knees, Rain struggled to get out of the way as Ice kicked into his midsection. Ice carried Rain toward one of the large boilers, and began to pick him up for a piledriver on the hard concrete floor. As he started to get Rain up, Scott Slugger stepped out from behind the boiler and swung with a steel chair at Ice. Ice fell as Slugger and a mysterious shadowy man continued to attack him as Rain walked away and escaped out the boiler room door. Slugger looked up at him with his eyes narrowed, but the mysterious man put an arm on one shoulder. "Let him go... time for him later." They went back to their assault on Ice, but from the open door of the boiler room Chris Universal arrived and jumped at Scott Slugger. The mysterious man sunk back into the shadows and Chris Universal took notice him. Universal started after the man, but Ice caught his foot from behind. The three men continued to brawl inside the dark room, perhaps a preview of what was to come the next night... Result: Rain Trouble We returned from the commercials to find Living and Heaven already in the ring. About six hundred candles formed the number "six" in between them, with each candle strategically placed. Each wick burned slowly as Living began to speak first, thinking what he was about to say, before proceeding. "She is Heaven, I am Living... and together, we give you... Hell." The lights dimmed, and the number six burst onto the video screen. A bell tolled about six times, and on the sixth time, inside the ring, a spotlight shone on the circular part of the candles forming the number six. Suddenly, someone emerged from underneath the ring. He climbed up from below, before reaching his feet. As he did, he outstretched his arms and with his head looking down, he climbed out. The man known only has "Hell" now stood next to "Living" and "Heaven"... and it was almost certain that "Hell", after further observation, was the man once known as "Famine", formerly of the Dark Riders. Hell, Heaven and Living stood with their arms outstretched, crucifixion style, before they bowed their heads. "Six days until the second coming" appeared on the video screen before those words transformed into "Dead" as we went onto a commercial... Losing it Bruce Springsteen stumbled down the hallways of the arena, the empty bottle dangling from one hand. "Billy... gone... Billy? Five dollars? Get out of here..." He stumbled into one of the walls, obviously drunk. As he did so he kicked a white envelope that had been laying on the arena floor, and he picked it up with mild interest. On the cover of the envelope he read a single name. "Black Panther? Whys they Panther got to be leaving his envelope on this here floor here? That doesn't make any..." He drifted off as he spotted an interesting spot on the wall and leered at it. "Hey baby... you're no Penny Somethingheart but damn, you've got a butt that won't..." Suddenly Fallen, Poison and Demon walked by, hauling with them a kicking and screaming Commissioner Travis. Demon spotted him and lunged at him, snapping his teeth and gibbering wildly. Fallen grabbed his neck, and from behind him Death spoke in a deep and laughing tone. "Let him have his fun Fallen... the weak deserve what they get." The Riders walked away, but Bruce Springsteen waved the envelope at him. "Hey misster... Black Rider... this envelope is for you..." Bruce tossed the envelope at Death, who caught it in one hand. He looked at it, and a frown crossed over his face. "Nothing you have interests me, worm. By tomorrow you won't even have a job anymore. Enjoy your wine while you can... tomorrow everything burns." Death tossed the envelope back at Bruce, and it bounced off of him and hit the ground. The Riders walked on, and Fallen moved closer to Death. "Listen... Spencer... I have my own reasons for wanting the fWo to come to an end after all of the pain it's caused me... but Demon... Demon's lost his mind, man. We've got to get him out of here before he kills himself." Death stopped and turned to Fallen, and his face wore an expression of deep, smoldering anger. "Don't you ever, ever, tell me what to do. Demon stays... and if you don't want to be dealt with like we are going to deal with him..." Death hitched a thumb at the commissioner. "Then you will learn to be silent. Now all of you take him down into the darkness... and leave him there so we can go back to the ring to finish tonight. You're going where I came from Travis... and nobody is going to see you... ever again." Ultra Cool (c) vs. Lone Gunmen Backstage Rudo found Bruce Springsteen slumped against a wall. "Dammit, Machina! You're drunk! You're in no condition to wrestle!" Bruce Springsteen tossed his bottle against a wall and pointed at Rudo. "That sounds like a challenge to me... come on!" The music of the Lone Gunmen blared over the arena speakers, and the familiar voice of Machina with the appearance of Bruce Springsteen appeared on the entrance ramp. "OH YOU DIDN'T KNOW? Well your ass had better call somebody... and if it can do that, it's the most talented ass in the woooooooooooooooooooOOOOOORLD! Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, other people who appreciate the announcing of Michael Buffer... the Lone Gunmen proudly give to you this finely made, solid steel entrance ramp! Made in the heartland of America by some of the best trained steel workers in the business, this entrance ramp is a modern day marvel... thirty feet of solid, galvanized steel that rises at it's highest apex a good thirty feet into the air..." Before Bruce Springsteen could continue Kid Cool ran from the top of the entrance ramp wielding his sixteenth of one of the fWo Tag Team titles. Bruce Springsteen saw him coming however, and quickly dropped to all fours. The bell rang, and Rudo jumped in the air in surprise of the start of the match. Because of the incline of the entrance ramp, Kid Cool could not stop himself in time and he tumbled over Bruce Springsteen and rolled down the entrance ramp until he hit his head on the metal guard rail at the bottom of the ramp. Kid Cool yelped a high pitched whine and fell unconscious as Ultra Violet made his appearance at the top of the entrance ramp, proudly wearing the other sixteenth of Kid Cool's half of the fWo Tag Team belt. Behind him stood Violet, and when she saw Kid Cool laying motionless at the bottom of the entrance ramp she quickly ran down to check on him. Ultra Violet meanwhile had produced his bucket of purple paint and began to sign autographs, but was finding the full bucket of paint and steep incline of the entrance ramp too much to navigate at once. Seeing Ultra Violet having trouble, Bruce Springsteen told Rudo to go help him by holding his paint can while he signed autographs for the fans. While Rudo did this Bruce Springsteen stumbled down the entrance ramp to check on Kid Cool. Halfway there he forgot where he was going and instead grabbed the microphone he had dropped earlier. "I want everyone to know, that I love each and every one of you... especially Briana but especially Penny Pureheart. That's right, I love Penny Pureheart... and I'm not afraid to show it I... hey, whatta want over here... go'on..." Rudo had now left Ultra Violet but was still carrying his can of paint and was attempting to keep Machina from embarrassing himself any further. Bruce Springsteen batted the microphone at him as Rudo tried to take it away, and it made odd thumping sounds as it collided with Rudo's head. Rudo finally grabbed the microphone and tossed it away, and it happened to strike Kid Cool on the head, who was showing signs of life. Kid Cool went out again, and Violet patted his head while darting glances up at Ultra Violet to see if he was watching her. He wasn't, and instead was looking at Rudo who still held his can of purple paint. Ultra Violet reached for it, but at that moment Rudo turned around and the two collided, splashing purple paint everywhere. Both men were drenched in purple paint, and Bruce Springsteen turned to see the two purple painted men before him. He looked confused, and scratched his chin as he looked from Rudo to Ultra Violet and back again. "Crap... I can't tell them apart... I've got to get something to wash these two off so I can tell which one to pin..." Rudo and Ultra Violet looked at each other as Bruce Springsteen ran backstage to search for some kind of solvent. Ultra Violet looked down at Kid Cool, and as he did so Violet began massaging Kid Cool's shoulders with more passion. Ultra Violet smiled and waved to her and then turned back to Rudo, and Violet grunted and dropped Kid Cool's head back to the floor with a thud. Ultra Violet shrugged. "Why doesn't he just go down there and pin Kid Cool? I mean, he's out and everything..." Rudo shrugged back and said, "I've learned not to think about things too much." Meanwhile Bruce Springsteen had found a bucket of gasoline and ran back out onto the entrance ramp. He splashed the gasoline on Ultra Violet and Rudo, who at once began gagging as the purple goop ran from them and trickled down the entrance ramp. Rudo yelled, "Dammit Machina..." Bruce Springsteen shook his head with an expression of horror under his mask, and Rudo rolled his eyes. "Fine... dammit Bruce Springsteen, that's gasoline.... we are freezing now!" Bruce Springsteen sighed with relief. "Is that all? Here, I can solve that..." Bruce Springsteen walked backstage again, and Rudo and Ultra Violet exchanged a long glance. Ultra Violet grinned foolishly at Rudo, and Rudo shook his head with dismay. "Yes, Clarence... go back there and find him before he finds the blowtorch and lights both of us on fire." Ultra Violet wandered away in search of Machina as Rudo looked around for one of the beer vendors. Backstage Ultra Violet walked past Joy and waved cheerfully as he went. Ultra Violet finally spotted Bruce Springsteen down one of the halls looking for the men who were in charge of the pyrotechnics. Ultra Violet grabbed him from behind and wheeled him around, causing the Bruce Springsteen mask to fall off. Machina yelped and punched Ultra Violet in the head. "My disguise!" Ultra Violet stumbled backward and bumped into the emergency sprinkler system, and at once water began to spray down into the halls and onto the fans in the arena. Ultra Violet looked around as Machina yelped again. "You've made it rain inside! Demon boy!" Machina reached around for a weapon, moving his hand over a steel chair, baseball bat, large knife, and a rifle. Finally his hand settled on a Sheryl Crow album and broke it over the head of Ultra Violet. Ultra Violet fell backward and Machina made a cover, determined to end the match quickly before Penny Pureheart spotted him without the Bruce Springsteen disguise. No referee was in sight, and Machina lay on Ultra Violet for nearly thirty seconds before realizing this fact. Machina stood and addressed Ultra Violet. "Wasn't this supposed to be a falls count anywhere match?" Ultra Violet scratched his head. "I think so... either that or it was supposed to be a balls mount anywhere match... but I suppose that doesn't make any sense... you know, since we are in Chicago and all." Machina scratched his chin. "So where is the referee?" Ultra Violet shook his head. "I dunno... out at the ring?" Machina snapped his fingers. "Of course! At the wrestling ring! Why didn't I think of that? So how are we going to count pinfalls?" Ultra Violet thought for a moment, and then brightened with an idea. "I know! If you pin me I'll count to C, and if I pin you, you'll count to C. There's only one problem... if I'm counting to C then how will I kick out?" Machina patted him on the back. "Don't worry... I'll do that for you. Okay?" Ultra Violet smiled. "Okay! Shall we get back to the match where we left off?" Machina nodded. "Let's." Ultra Violet lay down on the ground and Machina made the cover. Ultra Violet counted slowly to C, and Machina leapt up and down with excitement as he won the match and the fWo Tag Team Championship belts. Ultra Violet looked confused. "Wait a minute... why didn't I kick out?" Machina shrugged and patted him on the back saying, "Well, you have my leg hooked while you were counting the pinfall and I was trying to kick out... you're a technical master, UV!" Ultra Violet beamed and said, "Yeah I am... well, enjoy the titles!" Machina shook his hand, and the two went off in opposite directions. Result: Lone Gunmen by Pinfall The white envelope Backstage the Boston Strangler, along with Mike Bear, Wretch, Briana, Havok, Hellrazor and several others marched down the halls of the arena. The Strangler carried with him a bat, and as he passed locker rooms he swung the bat into the door, looking for signs of the Dark Riders or the commissioner. "We will find them... they can't hide forever..." Mike Bear looked nervous, and scratched his chin. "What do you suppose they did with Travis?" Briana scoffed, "Who cares? Bad riddance to bad rubbish as far as I'm concerned... if we find him then he's going to learn what it means to betray the wrestlers that gave their all to help him... dammit... on the road we are a family... we are the only family we've got..." Hellrazor grunted, "Yeah... perhaps... but all the same... I mean... I just can't believe that Travis would do something like... well... I just don't know what to do. The guy gave me another shot in this promotion... I just have a hard time understanding why he would do something like this..." Havok waved at both of them. "Guys... don't you get it? The problem is the title... Death has it... and according to the deal Travis set up he doesn't have to give anyone a title shot if Travis doesn't allow it... if Travis doesn't okay the match... it doesn't happen! Now that Death has gotten rid of Travis... he can do anything with that title that he chooses!" The Strangler suddenly came to a stop, and his brow furrowed as he spotted something on the ground. "We see something... hold..." The Strangler reached down, and brought up a single white envelope. He opened it, and reached inside and pulled a single paper. He read it quickly and a small smile crossed his face. Turning to the others he said, "Where is the Black Panther?" Endgame The lights dimmed as the Dark Riders returned to the entrance ramp, David Spencer leading Fallen, Poison, and Demon to the ring with the fWo Heavyweight Championship around his waist. The crowd at once began to boo again, loudly showing their displeasure at the return of the group. Demon looked disturbed by the reaction from the fans, and tried to leap over the guard rail and go after several of the people at ringside. Fallen grabbed him by the hair screaming, "Dammit William... get hold of yourself... this isn't you!" Hearing this Death swung and struck Fallen on the side of the head, bringing the Dark Rider to his knees as he began to cough ropes of blood out onto the floor. Death laughed as he watched Fallen on his knees, and Poison began to laugh along with him, cackling and tearing at her own hair as she did so. Death climbed into the ring and held the championship up into the air, which caused another wave of boos to drift out from the crowd. "Shut up! Recognize this... this title is mine... and now that the weak fool who dared to make a deal with the devil has been... dispatched... it will remain mine... forever. The Dark Riders... after two years... have finally won. This fWo... this promotion... and all of you... will remain in darkness... in misery... forever." Death laughed out at the booing fans, and now the fans began to throw objects toward the ring. An arc of bottles, chairs and small projectiles flew through the air toward the ring, and Death's laughter suddenly cut out into static as the announcing table and production stand was smashed from a flying chair. Death turned slowly, the buzz and hum of the audience making everything seem slow and warped. A smile crossed over his face, and Death knew that with every bottle that was thrown... with every chair that was tossed... the fWo spiraled further and further down into dark madness. The fans had turned on the promotion... and the end of it all was near. He flipped over the top rope, and as the garbage continued to fly through the air the Dark Riders made their way back up the entrance ramp. They were nearly out of the arena and the camera was starting to fade out when a single voice came over the arena speakers... One moment in time "You... need... to... stop... being... UNHAPPY!" Inside the ring stood Joy, who had come out through the crowd and looked angry for one of the few times in his life. The Dark Riders turned, and when Death saw who it was who had challenged him he began to laugh. Joy smiled, and nodded. "That's right... that's better... you big meanies with all your 'Oh, I'm going to make everyone miserable and bring you all into darkness, grumble, grumble, grunt'... you need to just get that sad stick out of your wazzoo and replace it with a pretty old candy cane!" Death stopped laughing and looked down at Joy for a moment, and then raised his middle finger. Joy gasped, and the Dark Riders turned and started to walk away. Joy stomped his foot and started up the entrance ramp after the Dark Riders. He reached Death just as he was starting to duck under the curtain. Joy grabbed Death's shoulder and spun him around, and then pasted a happy face sticker onto Death's chest. Death raged and grabbed Joy's arm and tossed him aside growling, "Fallen, get rid of him!" Fallen started, and then backed off. "There's no point... he's just a harmless nut... come on, let's just go. Tomorrow it all ends anyway..." Death looked at Fallen for a moment, and then slapped Fallen with a backhanded blow that took Fallen down to his knees. Death pointed to Demon, and the wrestler leapt at Joy. Demon kicked Joy in the head, and grabbed him and began to repeatedly punch Joy in the skull. Fallen screamed, and the fans once again began to pelt the wrestlers with garbage. Death laughed a deep laugh, and Poison cried... as Fallen reached for Demon. A wildly screaming Demon meanwhile picked up Joy and ran toward the edge of the entrance ramp, releasing Joy's limp body as he neared the side. Joy tumbled over, and things seemed to suddenly get very quiet. Joy fell... and one arm bent to protect himself as he tumbled. Demon had stopped screaming and fell silent, and Fallen reached out into empty air. Joy fell head first, and when he landed he lay still. Demon screamed now, but this was a very different scream... a scream of anguish. Fallen quickly ran down the ramp, but even as medical personnel flooded the area and John Skyler reached his one time partner and friend, he already knew it was too late. The cameras went to black... and everything was silent.
