Sunday Slaughter, 21 April 2002

21 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction (earlier today) "I'm serious, Eli! Put the bat down and think about this for a second!" Slaughter isn't beginning for another twenty minutes, but Eli Flair has barely been inside all night long. Earlier today, Donaven Winters shoved his girlfriend, Angel, into the side of a bus during an altercation outside their hotel. Eli is taking it personally. "So whaddaya gonna do, huh? Bash Winters in the head? What's that gonna accomplish? Wait until Saturday, wait until CyberSlam, when you've got the wire!" exclaimed Poison Ivy, pacing in a circle one step behind Eli himself. "You don't understand," he retorted, "Angel's not a part of this. She never was and never will be. I promised myself she wouldn't suffer because of my career... that shouldn't have happened." Ivy stopped him, putting a hand on the bat. "You're right, it shouldn't've happened. But it did. Angel was really hung over and didn't feel a thing, Eli. She wasn't hurt. Let it go." "No fucking way." "Listen to yourself," pleaded Ivy, "Or better yet, for once, listen to ME! Wait until Saturday. Get your hands on the wire and shred the bastard. Remember, put your hands on him on Saturday and it's a highly- anticipated match on a pay- per- view event. Put your hands on him in the parking lot and it's assault." Eli seems to consider this for a moment. "Go inside," he says to her, "and get me a camera." Or not. Crucifix vs. Monte Burns Slaughter heads to the ring for a cruiserweight matchup pitting two of the fWo top high-flyers against each other. "Beauitful People" plays, and Crucifix makes his way to the ring to boos. He hops over the top rope into the ring. "Nobody's Real" hits, and the fans give a still-mixed but tending towards positive reaction as Monte Burns makes his way to the ring. Burns steps into the ring and the match is underway. The bell rings, and Burns and Crucifix lock up. Crucifix gains the advantage with a rake of the eyes, then whips Burns into the ropes. As Burns comes off, Crucifix hits him with a leaping leg lariat, taking him down to the mat. Crucifix covers, but only gets two. Crucifix brings Burns to his feet and whips him into the corner, then charges in with a handspring elbow. Burns stumbles out of the corner, and Crucifix takes him down to the mat with a bulldog. Crucifix runs into the ropes, going for an asai moonsault, but Burns raises his knees and Crucifix slams down on them, cutting his momentum off quite effectively. Burns gets to his feet and brings Crucifix up, then tosses him through the ropes. Burns backs up, then charges into the ropes. He leaps, sending himself through the middle of the ropes as Crucifix gets to his feet. Burns nails Crucifix on the fly with a forearm smash, sending both men down to the floor, hitting hard. Burns makes his way to his feet and brings Crucifix up, then whips him into the guardrail. Burns charges in and nails Crucifix with a spinning heel kick, causing Cruci to stumble out and Burns to land amongst the crowd. Burns immediately gets to his feet and leaps onto the guardrail. He then flies off with a dropkick, hitting Crucifix squarely on the back of the head. Burns brings him back up and tosses him into the ring, then makes the cover. Crucifix kicks out at two. Burns brings him up to his feet again and sets him up, then takes him down to the mat with a snap suplex. Burns holds on and brings Crucifix back up to his feet, and takes him down again, this time with a traditional suplex. Finally, he holds on again and brings him up, then takes him down with a delayed suplex. Burns floats over into the cover, but Crucifix kicks out. Burns brings Crucifix up again, but this time Cruci gains the edge with a kick below the belt, stunning Burns and causing him to stagger off. Crucifix shoves Burns into the corner and begins hammering on him with fists to the face, stunning him. He sets Burns on the top turnbuckle, sitting, and climbs up. Crucifix prepares to rana Burns off of the top rope, but Burns senses it coming and shoves him off. Crucifix lands in perfect position for Burns to end it all. He measures Crucifix... And leaps. The Establishment. Burns connects squarely, and makes the cover. One. Two. Three. Burns gets a victory and some momentum heading into CyberSlam, and his arm is raised by the referee. While Burns celebrates, Crucifix gets up, appearing to have not taken this loss very well. He rolls out and procures a chair, then rolls back in and blasts Burns from behind to boos. Crucifix brings Burns up to his feet, then DDTs him on the chair. He brings Burns up again, but from the back Tempest rushes to the ring to cheers. She slides in and knocks Crucifix from the ring with a spinning heel kick. Crucifix decides to take his leave, and Tempest walks over to where Burns is slowly (very slowly) getting to his feet. She doesn't offer him a hand to help him up, and as he stands, the two begin to stare into each others' eyes. Their locked gazes don't go anywhere else as Slaughter, well, does... Result: Monte Burns by pinfall Human Shield (part one) Black Quicksilver entered the bathroom and headed straight for the urinal. A zipping noise was heard, despite the fact that he was wearing his wrestling tights, and he turned toward the camera. "Do you MIND?" Shortly after BQ's statement, the door to one of the stalls flew open, and out stepped Ric Chronos, who did a double-take as he headed for the sink. "YOU!" "Yipe." BQ tried desperately to zip up before running away, and Chronos went to grab him. "Whoa! Ain't you gonna wash those first?" Chronos decided that he may as well wash his hands before destroying BQ, even though he'd probably have to wash them again afterward. This gave BQ ample time to run away, as he still tried to zip up his imaginary zipper. Chronos darted after him when he realized it was just a trick, although, after all, it WAS BQ, so who REALLY knows? Chronos rounded the corner and came to a sudden stop, as he saw BQ. But there was one problem standing in between him and BQ. Jackhammer. Chronos backed off, prompting BQ to shout at him as he hid behind Jackhammer. "I'd like to see you just TRY to get past someone who's left leg is bigger than your entire body! Yeah, that's right, I know it makes no sense to have a former multi-time World Champion back off from JACKHAMMER, but it's, like, the principle that matters, you know?" Chronos turned and walked away, making a vow to himself that he'd eventually catch up to BQ later in the night. Phone Call Chris Universal sat down at his desk, and moved around some of his paperwork, so that it looked like he was getting it done. "If only I could just say... " Universal began. However, a phone call interrupted him. He picked it up, and heard a rather interesting voice on the other line. "Hello, Chris." "Jon?" Universal asked. "Damn straight," said the Cult Icon, Jon Crisp, on the other end. "Guess where I am?" "Not at the arena..." "Right again, you sorry-ass excuse for a cross between Don Johnson and Linda McMahon. And guess what? You're not going to see me tonight." "And you have a match tonight, that four-way with Wretch, Ice, and Kagos... " Universal replied. "That's a damn shame. Guess I'm no-showing." "Jon, come on, what gives here?" Universal was getting rather impatient. "It's simple, Chris. You've screwed me, and not on a fluke. Not just once. You've screwed me a LOT. Austin Davis screwed me. The Boston Strangler screwed me. Hell, if I had to run an angle with Bastion Booger, that would end up screwing me too. Well, now it ends. "Now, I'm... screwing... you." "With one night?" Universal asked, rather sarcastically. "Jon, it's going to take a lot more than one night to screw me over." "What is tonight, Chris?" "April twenty-first." "No, shithead. In fWo terms, what is it?" Universal thought about it, and began to see what Crisp was getting at. "The final buildup for CyberSlam V." "Correct. So, tonight, you're going to get to promote your funbag o' matches, B-list celebrites, and a roster full of has-beens, never-will-bes, and never-get-laids. You'll get to promote all of that, and Alf's celebrity appearance. But you won't get to promote what will make CyberSlam special. It will be the Icon's first CyberSlam. "Or, should I say, *was to be* the Icon's first CyberSlam." Universal's ears perked up at that last comment. "What was that?" "You heard me. Fuck CyberSlam. Fuck Wretch and his movies. Fuck Austin Davis and his inability to do the right thing and hand the belts to Winters and I. Fuck the fWo for fucking me. And fuck you for being a fuckhead. Go ahead and fill the time with a midget hardcore match, and watch the buyrates go down. Because, through all of the crap you've put me through... from wrestling with Jackhammer to wrestling with Heftel to being hit with concrete whoppers... through all of that, I've STILL brought the ratings, and I've STILL made you money. That's something that can't be said for nearly every single other so-called performer on your little viagra-craving roster. "The biggest buildup to Cyberslam, the famous Wretch/Crisp feud, the one that was getting the press, the buyrates, the 'holy shit let's buy this to see these two go at it'... will have no payoff. You fucked that up last week. You fucked up for the last time with me." "So, what is it you want Jon," Universal replied, "... more money? More exposure? A title? I assume that's what you're getting at here." "Respect, Universal," was Crisp's reply. "You've shown none to me. So why should I show any to you?" "Because there's something called a contract," Universal replied. "If you don't show up next week, you're fired. And I guarantee that nobody, from the WWF to the WWA... nobody will hire you if you pull this little stunt." There was some dead-air, and then Crisp spoke up once more. "Fine. If you want me to show up, I'll show up. But you'll regret it. CyberSlam's going to be a lot shorter than it should, and you'll have only you to thank. Got that, Moneybags?" Before Universal could respond, Crisp hung up the phone. Meanwhile, Slaughter proceeded on. Scott Hart vs. Mike Heftel Slaughter headed to the ring. "Connection" played, signaling the entrance of Scott Hart, along with La Parka and Claire. The trio entered the ring and waited for Hart's opponent. Mere moments later, "Down With The Ship" hit, bringing out the reigning fWo Wominternopean Jobberweight Champion Mike Heftel, who puts the title on the line for the first time tonight. He's accompanied to the ring by Abusah, who's wearing a dog collar, and Evil-Lyn, who is leading him to the ring by a chain connected to the dog collar. It's not because she wants to show everyone who's in charge, but rather it's because Abusah thinks the dog collar-and-chain look is rather fashionable. Heftel reaches the ring, and after everyone except for him and Hart have left the ring, the bell sounds. Right away, both start to wrestle invisible men. Heftel gets taken down with an armdrag, Hart with a hiptoss. Both men get to their feet and both men are taken down by snapmares, before receiving kicks to the back of the head. They decide to retreat to the outside of the ring, hoping to find some "weapons" that will help them overcome the invisible men. Hart finds a banana, and Heftel finds a Nerf football. Hart throws the banana at one of the invisible men, but he misses. Heftel throws the football at the other invisible man, but the invisible man catches it and throws it back at Heftel, nailing him right in the forehead. The football bounces off Heftel and flies into the waiting arms of La Parka, who spikes it and shimmies. Hart helps Heftel up, and tells him that maybe they should save the real creative stuff for CyberSlam and finish this match. Heftel agrees, at almost the exact moment Abusah gets loose from Evil-Lyn and starts charging towards La Parka. La Parka takes him down with a roundhouse kick and shimmies, while Abusah sees little birds flying around his head. Abusah then tries to eat the birds. Back in the ring, Heftel and Hart desperately want to finish their match, but the invisible men keep interrupting. Hart takes a superplex/flying splash combo, and then Heftel is hit with a double-dropkick. As all this is going on, Evil-Lyn casually walks by Claire, gives her hair a tug, and keeps walking. Claire goes to run after Evil-Lyn, but doesn't see La Parka ducking down near the ringpost on the other side of the ring. Evil-Lyn runs by La Parka, and La Parka jumps up to clothesline Claire, but Claire ducks and hits La Parka with a clothesline of her own when he turns around. Meanwhile, in the ring, Heftel and Hart have finally come up with a plan. They both approach an invisible man, and Heftel allows one of them to DDT him, as Hart lets the other one give him a bodyslam... right on top of a prone Heftel. The referee gets down and makes the three-count, awarding the match to Hart, meaning Heftel retains his title. Right after the bell rings, Sphere and Mr. T hit the ring, and then they climb into it. They somehow manage to scare away the invisible men. Sphere waits for Hart to get to his feet, and he plants him with the This Was Your Mum Last Night, and then gives Heftel the Besmircher. Sphere and Mr. T celebrate in the ring, and then "War?" hits, bringing out Sphere's tag team partner at Cyberslam... Jackhammer. For some odd reason, Abusah and La Parka have both decided to get into the ring. Big mistake, as Jackhammer chokeslams Abusah once he gets into the ring, and gives La Parka one seconds later. Hart gets to his feet and spins around right into the third chokeslam. Jackhammer looks over at Sphere and Mr. T, but remembers that he can't touch Sphere until Cyberslam. So he grabs Mr. T and *tries* to chokeslam him, but SOMEONE doesn't want to jump, so Jackhammer just pushes him aside and leaves the ring, knowing he'll finally be able to get his hands on Sphere at CyberSlam, when they team up to take on Hart & La Parka and Heftel & Abusah at CyberSlam. "How am I supposed to highlight your hair then?" BQ shrugged as he sat in a chair in the makeup area, with Claire standing in front of him. Donaven Winters vs. Tempest Slaughter heads to the ring for a rare champion vs. champion matchup although neither title is on the line. "Piggy" plays, and the crowd boos as fWo Hardcore Champion Donaven Winters makes his way to the ring. Winters climbs up the ringsteps into the ring and waits for his opponent. "Battleflag" hits, and the crowd cheers as fWo Cruiserweight Champion Tempest makes her way to the ring. She slides in and looks across at Winters, who returns the gaze. The bell rings, and the two lock up. Winters gains the advantage and whips Tempest into the ropes, then catches her coming off with an elbow to the face, knocking her down. Winters drops an elbow, then another, then makes the cover. Tempest easily kicks out at two. Winters brings Tempest to her feet and whips her into the corner, then charges in, hitting a clothesline. Winters brings Tempest out and sets her up for a suplex that hits squarely. Showing off some of his little-used flying ability, Winters runs into the ropes and hits an asai moonsault that connects squarely with Tempest. Winters then covers, but once more the referee only manages to reach a count of two. Winters brings Tempest back up and whips her into the ropes again, but this time Tempest reverses it. As Winters comes off, she leaps and nails him with a leg lariat, knocking him to the mat. Tempest immediately brings Winters up and dropkicks him down to the mat. With Winters down, Tempest mounts the second rope, then comes off with an elbowsmash. Tempest makes the cover, but Winters kicks out at two. She brings him to his feet and hits a bodyslam, then drops a hard leg across the throat. Tempest covers once more, and again gets a count of two. She brings him up and hooks him by the head, then runs across the ring and drives his face into the mat with a bulldog. Tempest waits for Winters to get to his feet, then runs into the ropes. She leaps onto the second strand, then comes off with a spectacular move an asai somersault neckbreaker. The crowd pops, and Tempest heads to the top rope, sensing that this is the time to take advantage of her opportunity. Winters, however, gets to his feet and throws Tempest off the top to the middle of the ring, where she lands with a crash. Winters immediately brings her back up and runs into the ropes. He then blasts her with a rolling elbow, knocking her down to the mat. He brings Tempest back up and hooks her by the head, setting up the Reflection. Tempest, however, fights her way out of it with a series of elbows, then charges at Winters. She leaps into the air and his shoulders, going for a hurricanrana to take him down to the mat. Winters, however, uses his superior size and strength to hold on. He then drives Tempest down into the mat with a powerbomb. With Tempest down, Winters brings her back up to her feet. He sets her up, looking to finish off the mat. Winters lifts Tempest up into the air and brings her down with an orange crush bomb, also known as the Broken Dreams. Winters then makes the cover. One. Two. Three. Winters comes out on top with a victory, and the crowd boos as his hand is raised. He responds by giving them all the finger as he heads to the back, title in hand... Result: Donaven Winters by pinfall Not With a Whimper - part 2 With the match over, the video wall cuts to static, and fades back out with Eli Flair in the parking lot, vaguely illuminated by the streetlights. He's still got the bat in his hand. Winters looked up at the screen with interest. "Winters!" Flair shouted, "I'm here, you bastard! You didn't want to wait for CyberSlam earlier today, so why wait now? GET OUT HERE." You raised the stakes, killer, when you proved to me that this was MUCH MORE than a simple wrestling show." "Don't... DO NOT... make me come in there after you." Unable to stick with this any longer, the Slaughter producers cut away from this to commercial, preparing for the next match.
