Wednesday Warfare, 19 January 2000

19 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Fireworks explode inside the arena. The fans erupt as they prepare to brace themselves for what is about to occur before them tonight. Several of the fans wave a flag representing their country of France. As the theme for Warfare plays, several "FTfWo" banners unfurl from the ceiling, prompting the fans to change from a chorus of cheers to a loud ovation of boos. The FTfWo logo bursts onto the screen, before "Humans Being" begins to play. A red spotlight covers the stage, while fog eminates from the below the two figures who step out. The ovation of boos grows even louder once the fans recognize the two men on the stage. Dead & Perfect. Dead & Perfect taunt the crowd as they walk towards the ring quickly. Both men enter the ring and Dead sticks out his arms in a crucifixion pose, while Perfect looks at the crowd with a smug look on his face. Dead produces a microphone from his trenchcoat and prepares to address the onlooking audience when he pauses as the fans to began to chant something. The chant grows louder, and louder... before it becomes apparent that everyone is chanting ze Frenchie's name in unison. Dead looks them with a smirk, before he shouts... "I want you all to shut up or I'll fire the son of a bitch!" The crowd discontinues chanting his name, but begins to boo again. Dead continues by telling the crowd that this Sunday at Slaughter he will take care of the Boston Strangler and his reign of power will not end. Perfect takes the mic. "La bienvenue vers la France, terre de sucent! Maintenant, je sais que tous vous des grenouilles pouvez comprendre l'anglais, et si vous can't- trop mauvais. Now, despite the fact that the fWo is in France tonight, tonight will be perhaps the greatest Warfare in fWo history! For FTfWo, anyway. For the rest of ya- well, I send my condolences. Actually, no, I don't. And with that in mind, let us get to the ever-so-fun task of booking matches for our personal amusement, shall we? Number one, the hero of all you idiots- which is just another example why France sucks- the Flying Frenchie." The fans respond to this with cheers. "Now, now, I would suggest all of you shut the hell up, unless I accidentally tear up that contract of his and leave him to rot in his home country. Lord knows the fWo'd be better off without him...now, if you'll remember, the Frenchie lost the title he stole a couple weeks ago after being reported and deported. Who pointed this out to the fWo and Travis and convinced him to strip good ol' TFF? Why, Rain, of course. I'm sure that didn't sit well with Frenchie, so, as a favor, I will put him in a match two days before his big World Title match with the 7-foot 2 360 pound monster known as Rain. Enjoy, Frenchie. I'll be rooting for you, really! Next, we have a tag team match scheduled tonight. Since even French people need bathroom and snack breaks, Mike Bear and Fear will face Rudo and Sid Snow. In fact, the refreshment and condiment providers of France should thank me for this match, as it will increase sales of food tonight by at least 103%! Then, just in case that bathroom break runs long, we will also see Eron and Kid Cool do battle. After all, what makes more sense? Kid Cool sucks. Eron sucks even more. What better place for a sucky battle of suck than the capital of suck known as Suck-Land...err, France? Then, the Cruiserweight Title will be on the line. Havok, your match tonight is being scheduled for two reasons. Number one, you insulted Dead. Oops. Wrong move. Number two, you're closing in on my record for longest reign as Cruiserweight champ, and I will not stand for a piece of crap such as you even nearing my legendary and incredible record. Thus, you will face your fellow girly-game player, Wretch. He's shown he can get lucky and win every once in a while, plus he's also shown that he's no threat to break my record- thus, he gets the Cruiser title shot. Have fun, Hav, and don't expect to be going down in fWo record books any time soon. Now, since every show needs a little T, and a little A, I see no reason not to have a women's match here tonight. However, since the fWo roster consists of ugly hags, neither quality T nor A will be on display tonight when Briana faces Aimee Horihito. And finally, a big ol' four-man match. Of course, three of them will be on the same side, but that's irrelevant. Because I am facing this man in two days and have no qualms about seeing his ass kicked, David Spencer will be the one in the match. His opponents will be number one, Scott Collins- yes, the legendary Candyman and perennial bitch of Stone. Number two, a guy who has proven in his long and illustrious career just how many jabronis one guy can beat to pad his record, Mr. "32-0-7 against jobbers and 0-8-0 against talent", the Boston Strangler. And number three, the man who says that he is nobody's puppet, Ice. Well, guess what- whether you like it or not, I'm pulling your strings tonight. You are the third member of that team, and if you don't show, I will fire your ass! Understood?...Now, from the quality bookermen at FTfWo, enjoy Warfare everybody!" With that said, the crowd begins booing again, which is met by a quadruple-finger salute from Dead and Perfect, who walk to the back to the boos of the crowd... Scenes from Marseille Scenes showed on the Warfare Screen of the fWo wrestlers arriving in France, and touring the country during the day. Throughout France there seemed to be an air of jubilation as the fWo wrestlers mingled with the citizens of France, who were all eager to get their photos taken with the wrestlers. Outside of the airport a huge crowd had gathered to cheer on the arrival of their heroes, the French Foundation. Primetime, Courage, and the Flying Frenchie himself were all guests of honor in a huge celebration that rocked the bustling town of Marsielle. The Frenchie addressed his fans, pledging to win the fWo World Championship from the Black Panther and go on to have the largest party in the history of France. Indeed, Primetime and Courage were also both hailed as heroes, despite not being French natives. The fans embraced the two comrades of the Frenchie, and presented both members with an honorary French citizenship. The other members of the fWo received a warm welcome, if not the same ecstatic approval that the French Foundation did. Rain talked to several French locals at a popular bistro, and shots were shown of the Underground taking in the sights and sounds of the Stade Velodrome, home to Olympique Marseille and location of tonight's Wednesday Warfare. In the streets of Marseille several fWo wrestlers toured the town and took photos and signed autographs, but none did their job with as much enthusiasm as Ultra Violet. Carrying his bucket of purple paint, Ultra Violet happily plastered dark purple hand prints on the walls of buildings, on fans, and on the occasional auto. Fans seemed overjoyed with the purple haired wrestler, and thanked him for his autographs with a beret that he wore jauntily on his head. Ultra Violet was having a great time until Kid Cool came out of a nearby store and attacked Ultra Violet from behind with a high kick, sending him spiraling into the glass window of a local coffee shoppe. Kid Cool grabbed the beret from Ultra Violet's helpless form and put it on his own head before yelling, "Everything that was yours is mine... because they all like me better! So nyah nyah nyah!" Kid Cool then dumped Ultra Violet's purple paint into the gutter before running away, presumably to train some more in the local French gyms before going to the show tonight. The scenes ended with this display, but Kid Cool was cheered despite his evil actions, largely because the fans in attendance had met Kid Cool days earlier when he arrived at France early. Kid Cool had been training in one of the gyms owned by the Flying Frenchie, and the fans were receptive to the young American trying to do things right by learning from their hero. The crowd remained rowdy and excited, anxious for the first match to begin... Underground vs. Lone Gunmen A large "L" appeared on the TravisTron, and a collective cheer filled the arena. That's when everybody's favorite jobbers, the Lone Gunmen, made their way out onto the entrance ramp. Sid Snow walked to the ring and was soon followed by Rudo who took a moment or two to pose with his fWo Tag Title belt. As the two prepared for a match that would be sure to foreshadow the results of Machina & Rudo versus Mike Bear & Wretch at Invasion! in France, the Underground's theme music blared. Mike Bear and Fear, representing their stable tonight, made their way down to the ring to a heated reaction from the crowd. Upon entering the ring both Fear and Sid Snow stepped onto the apron, making it clear who the starters would be. The bell rang, and Bear and Rudo began circling each other. The two tied up and levied for position for a bit before Mike Bear had Rudo up against the ropes. The ref demanded a clean break, but Bear decided to whip Rudo across the ring instead. Rudo rebounded off the far ropes and returned right into the elbow of Bear, sending his head bouncing off the mat. Rudo, however, immediately popped up back to his feet and the two men returned to lobbying for position. Another collar n' elbow tieup, but this time Rudo took the advantage with a knee to the gut of his opponent. Mike Bear doubled over as Rudo bounced off the ropes, but the Underground member moved out of the way of Rudo's attempted elbow, allowing the Gunman to fall to the mat. Mike Bear quickly ran against the ropes himself, and delivered a massive lariat to Rudo as he got to his feet, sending him to the outside. Fear jumped down from the apron and headed towards Rudo, prompting Sid Snow to enter the ring. Bear shot Snow a look as if to say "What the hell are you thinking?", and followed this thought up with another lariat. Snow dropped to the mat and Bear made a quick cover, but the ref refused to count since Sid wasn't the legal man. As Bear argued with the ref Snow rolled to the outside, where Fear had been beating on Rudo. Fear whipped Rudo towards the ringpost, but Rudo reversed it and sent Fear headfirst into the steel. As Fear stumbled back Rudo slid back into the ring, where he was greeted by stomps from Bear. Rudo covered his head, but Bear continued until he felt satisfied. Then, with a slight grin on his face, Bear signalled to Fear. As Fear dropped to the arena floor once again, Bear dropped a knee onto the back of the head of a still downed Rudo. Bear then allowed Rudo a few seconds of reprieve as he barked a few more orders at Fear who was sitting up a table next to the ring. Rudo took this time to help himself up using the ropes and attack Bear from behind, who was staggered by Rudo's forearms across the back. Rudo then whipped Bear into the opposite ropes and backdropped him over the ropes, but the former tag team champion was able to hold onto the top rope and stay on the apron. Rudo turned around to gloat, but was met by a fist to the face from Bear. Rudo stepped back as Bear made the tag out to Fear, who charged Rudo with a big boot to the face. Rudo fell to the mat and tried to crawl his way over to tag Snow, but Fear pulled him back into the Underground's corner while taunting him. Fear scooped up Rudo and slammed him to the mat with authority before dropped a big leg across his throat and making a cover. 1... 2... kickout by Rudo. Fear grabbed Rudo by his hair and helped to his feet, but brought him right back down again with an inverted neckbreaker. Fear tagged back out to Mike Bear, and the two executed a double suplex onto the tag champ. Rudo slowly got to his knees, where Bear stepped over the back of Rudo and began to pepper his forehead with powerful blows. Rudo took each one full on and slumped over upon being released. Bear then ran across the ring and sent Sid Snow sailing into the guardrail with a shoulderblock. As Snow reached tears on the outside, Fear stepped back into the ring to handicap the situation on the inside. Bear then, to the excite of the crowd, pointed to the table on the outside. Fear nodded in agreement, and the two brought Rudo over to the ropes. Bear stepped out to the apron as Fear set up Rudo in standing leg scissors, and together the two used Rudo to obliterate the table with a spiked powerbomb to the outside. Rudo crashed through with a thunderous force and lay still. Bear shouted something about "Wait til' Invasion!", which is when Sid Snow valiently attacked the Underground team. Snow delivered a series of chops to the chest of Bear, staggering him, then tried to whip Fear to the turnbuckles. But, the seven footer was to much and reversed the throw with one of his own. Snow hit the corner with such force that he bounced out, and right into the waiting arms of Fear. Fear scooped up Snow and dropped him in the Thantophobia, then quickly made the cover and got the pinfall victory for his team. As Fear and Mike Bear raised each others hands and Rudo pulled himself up to a crawling position, Machina came running down the aisle, chair in hand. Machina slid into the ring and whacked Fear in the back with it, but as he attempted to hit Mike Machina tripped over himself and fell to all fours. Bear began brutally kicking Machina in the ribs until Machina pulled himself to his feet, where Bear whipped him into the far ropes. As Machina returned Mike grabbed his head and chucked him over and to the arena floor where, after recovering, Machina hightailed it out of there with Rudo and Snow. Result: Underground by Pinfall Discovery Shot a day earlier fWo cameras picked up on David Spencer, whistling to himself as he drove over the grounds to his England estate. Spencer seemed to be in an unusually good mood, which looked odd with the face paint that he wore. In the backseat of the English auto lay Poison, gibbering and whimpering to herself. Every now and then Spencer would frown, and jab a hot cigarette lighter back at the young girl, inciting screams of pain and then silence. Spencer whistled to himself some more as he fought with the wheel on the windy English roads. "It will be good to see the old house again," Spencer thought. David Spencer had been away from his home for over two years, and remembered fondly the manor in which he spent his childhood torturing rats and other small woodland creatures. In the study to the manor he had several pictures of his dear mum, along with some fingernails he pulled off a homeless rag in East Rutherbrook. The manor always had a richly smoked scent to it, and as he neared his home he could nearly imagine that he smelled the scent of the manor already. He came around the last bend whistling, and then the tune died on his lips as the car rolled to a slow halt. There seemed to be an unusual lack of house where it ought to be. Spencer scratched his leg and groin as he let his eyes wander over the country side, which included the wonderful flowers and vines he remembered from his youth. He looked at kept grounds and tulybrook, the stables, the burnt down remains of his manor, and the old well. Something seemed wrong. Spencer frowned as he worked with it. The vines were there, check. The stables also seemed to be in order. Sounds of a horse came from one and Spencer dismissed it as he looked on. The well seemed normal enough, perhaps a bit rusty. The house of course had been burned to the ground, right... was there something wrong with the brook? He didn't think so, but perhaps he had best get a look at it. He walked toward it, tripping briefly over something. He looked down. It seemed to be the picture of his mother. Odd, it wasn't in the house. It also appeared to have been urinated on. "That shouldn't be," thought Spencer, as he picked it up. "This should be in the den next to the firepl..." Suddenly it occurred to David Spencer that his house ought to be standing right in front of him rather than be burned down. He dropped the photo. What followed was a string of explicitives in nearly a dozen languages. Inside the car, Poison continued to sob to herself. "Anthem for the Year 2000" begins, and the fans cheer the entrance of the champ. As he reaches the ring, he unbuckles the Cruiserweight belt and gives it to the timekeeper before leaping onto the apron and slingshotting into the ring. The ref signals for the bell to ring, and Havok and Wretch shake before circling each other. They lock up, and Havok gains the advantage. He twists Wretch into an hammerlock, then releases and hooks the head for a bulldog. He runs forward, but Wretch suddenly shifts weight and has Havok in a back suplex position. However, Havok carries himself over and lands on his feet behind Wretch, who turns to face him and is met with an armdrag. Wretch gets up quickly and catches Havok off guard with an armdrag of his own. They get up and Havok shoves Wretch into the ropes, but Wretch leaps onto them and asai moonsaults over Havok's head. Havok turns around and is kicked in the gut by Wretch, who goes for a rocker dropper. However, Havok overpowers him and flips him sideways, where he lands on his feet. Both men attempt a dropkick, and hit the mat simultaneously. They each quickly get up to one knee, warily eyeing each other, and the crowd begins to cheer for the technical exhibition. They both get up and, after acknowledging the crowd, lock up once again. Wretch hits a pair of hard knees to the gut, and follows up with a stuff european uppercut. He backs Havok into the corner, and strikes with a series of hard chops. Wretch then whips Havok across the ring and follows him in, but Havok uses the turnbuckle to leap up horizontally and twists into a sunset flip, taking down Wretch. Wretch kicks out at two. Wretch gets up first, and leaps onto Havok as he gets up, taking him down with a crucifix. Havok kicks out at two, and gets up quickly, only to be met by a kick to the gut by Wretch, and a thunderous Liger Bomb. The ref counts, but Havok manages to kick out at two. Wretch brings him up, and whips him into the ropes. He catches Havok coming off with a knee to the gut, and hooks him up and drops him with a russian legsweep. Wretch then runs off the ropes and leaps onto Havok with a somersault senton splash. He covers, but again only gets two. Wretch brings Havok up, and sends him hard into the corner. Havok stumbles out, and Wretch charges at him. He attempts a swinging bulldog, but as he comes down off the turnbuckles, Havok throws him off, and his momentum takes him half way across the ring. Wretch gets up slowly, and is taken right back down again by a superkick with so much force it could be heard in Germany. The referee reaches nine, and both men suddenly take notice and attempt to sprint for the ring. However, as they do, both men are suddenly yanked down by the hair by a pair of fans wearing long sweaters with hoods and sweat pants as the ref counts ten for a double countout. Security rushes to them, looking to escort the unruly fans out, but step back when one fan leaps onto the guardrail and moonsaults off it onto Havok. The fans pop incredibly when the fan pulls down the hood to reveal Courage, and he rips off the sweater to reveal the French Foundation t-shirt.
