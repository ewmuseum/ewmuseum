Sunday Slaughter, 29 April 2001

29 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Last night... one of them actually won a match. Tonight... they take over Slaughter. Next week... back to jobbing for all of them. But, anyway, back to tonight... the STUMP Squad presents... the STUMPies! Biggest Girly Man Cheesy award show music plays, bringing out alleged STUMP Squad leader Black Quicksilver. He steps out from behind a curtain and onto a stage. BQ: Hello, and welcome to the STUMPies. I am your host and presenter for the evening, and for those of you who either don't know or care, my name is Black Quicksilver. You may know me better as "That Guy Who Carries Stump Around", "That Guy Who Gets Carried Around by Mike Heftel", "That Goof With Jackhammer", "That Masked Guy With Claire", "That Guy Who Won Five Cruiserweight Titles But Didn't Do A Thing To Deserve Them", or "That Guy Who's Been In The fWo Since The Beginning And Has His Career Going Absolutely Nowhere". Awards will be presented in ten different categories. As you can see on the podium in front of me... BQ notices that he's not standing behind the podium, but instead to the far right of it. He notices this mistake and walks over to the podium. BQ: As I was saying, you'll see exactly what a STUMPy looks like if you feast your eyes on this little statue in front of me. BQ shows off the statue, which is an award shaped like Stump. BQ: To all those people who can't get it right... the STUMPies are named after the worst stable in the history of the fWo, the STUMP Squad. They are not named after Stump, as he does not capitalize his entire name. Go back to school, Mr. Lee. Well, now that I've gotten that out of the way, let's get on with the show! The first category is "Biggest Girly Man", and the nominees are: Crusader (shot of Crusader brushing the hair out of his face) Havok (shot of Havok brushing his hair in front of the mirror) Wretch (shot of Wretch using some hairspray) Greg Davis (shot of Davis getting some blush applied to his face) Erik Kelly (shot of Kelly giving a menacing, yet feminine-looking snarl) And the winner is... Crusader! Ladies and gentlemen, Crusader could not be here tonight, so accepting the award on his behalf is Matt Douglas, a man who is not only blonde, but straight as well! Matt Douglas walks onto the stage from behind a curtain and accepts the award. He then prepares to make a speech. Douglas: Wow, this... isn't really an achievement on my part... so I guess I'll go pawn this or something. Uh... RUAHH~! Douglas leaves as BQ pulls out another STUMPy and places it on the podium. Person Most Likely To Have Their Picture Sold At A Rather High P BQ: Moving right along to the second category... the recipient of this award is the "Person Most Likely To Have Their Picture Sold At A Rather High Price On E-Bay"! And the nominees are: Brittany (shot of Brittany throwing her hair back) Vengence (shot of Vengence scaring away Mike Heftel) Eddy Fate (shot of Fate celebrating his win over BQ) Claire (shot of Claire smiling at the behaviour of BQ and Heftel) Cult Icon (shot of Cult Icon getting headbutted to the ground by Mr. T) And the winner is... Claire! Ladies and gentlemen, before I bring out the winner, I'll remind you that if was YOU, the viewing public, who selected the winners. So if you like Brittany more than Claire, tough nuggets, you should've voted. Claire, come on out! Claire comes out from behind the curtain and accepts her award. Claire: Um, I'd like to thank everyone who voted for me... I know it probably wasn't a whole lot of people, but then again, not a whole lot of people voted for the other nominees, either... but, oh well. Claire gives one more smile and walks back behind the curtain, BQ looking at her the whole time. BQ: Man, I hate to see her leave, but I love to watch her go! BQ looks around, and then presses a button on the side of the podium. Canned "BOO!" sounds are heard. After a few seconds, the "booing" stops, and BQ places another STUMPy on the podium. Sickest Bastard BQ: Anyway... onto the next category... and I assure you, the winner will NOT be here, because I'm one of the biggest wussies in the fWo. Ladies and gentlemen, the nominees for "Sickest Bastard" are: Sphere (shot of Sphere trying to break a gold chain) Chris Titan (shot of Titan throwing Stump off the ramp) Clarisse (shot of Clarisse turning on Crusader) Carnage (shot of Carnage stabbing "Toad") Erik Kelly (shot of Kelly powerbombing BQ through a table, from various angles) BQ: Yipe. Oh, and the bastard is... Chris Titan. Yay. BQ throws the STUMPy over his shoulder. BQ: Like I said, the winner isn't here, since people would actually tune in if they knew your's truly was going to get the crap kicked out of him. The next award is certainly one of the most prestigious. BQ pulls out another award and places it on the podium. Best Mic Skills BQ: Ladies and gentlemen, the nominees for "Best Mic Skills" are: Mini-Rottdog (shot of Mini-Rottdog stuttering) Lamont (shot of Lamont purring) Stump (shot of Stump saying "...") Famine (shot of Famine swearing) Robert Jensen (shot of Jensen proclaiming that he's "hardcore") And the winner is... Stump! Stump rides across the stage on a cart, grabs his award, and exits as quickly as he entered. BQ: Well... you'd think that the recipient of the award for "Best Mic Skills" would have just a teensy bit more to say in their acceptance speech. Anyway... next category is... BQ produces another award. Best Entrance Theme BQ: Why yes, it's "Best Entrance Theme". For you neanderthals, or "Ices", as I like to call them, "Best Entrance Theme" is another name for "Best Song That Plays When A Wrestler Comes To The Ring". The nominees are: STUMP Squad, "We're Not Gonna Take It" (shot of the entire STUMP Squad walking to the ring) Jackhammer, "War?" (shot of Jackhammer sprinting to the ring) Black Quicksilver, "Slave To The Grind" (shot of BQ getting dizzy from headbanging as he runs to the ring) Mike Heftel, "Down With The Ship" (shot of Heftel stumbling down the ramp dressed like a gigantic robot) Greg Davis, "Eye of the Tiger" (shot of Davis walking to the ring shortly before a victory over BQ) And the winner is... Black Quicksilver. Geez, that guy? Wait... yes, I'm being informed over my earpiece that I did not know was in my ear until now... that... I... am... Black Quicksilver! And since I'm Black Quicksilver, that means I win! Um, yay for me and everything! Claire? Claire comes back out on the stage. BQ: Claire, could you be a sweetie, and... oh, wait, what am I saying? You already ARE a sweetie! Claire rolls her eyes but still manages to crack a smile. BQ: Um... so... anyway, could you take this to the back for me? Claire smiles and takes the award for BQ. She starts walking to the back, and BQ is once again watching her, but she turns around and sees him looking at her. Claire: Ahem. Turn around. BQ turns around, sighs, and puts another STUMPy on the podium. Look, I'm A Gimp And/Or Dead BQ: For this next category, some say I must not value my life a whole lot. Those people are right. I mean, I'm a frickin' jobber in the world of professional wrestling. But still, alot of people told me not to present this award. So here are the nominees for "Look, I'm A Gimp And/Or Dead"! Scott Slugger (shot of someone wearing a Seattle Mariners jersey, obviously not Slugger, getting hit in the back of the leg with a baseball bat by a masked assailant) Ric Chronos (shot of someone dressed like Chronos, but obviously not him, bumping his ankle on the leg of a table) Ice (shot of someone dressed up like Ice, but obviously not him, tripping over his own two feet) Jackhammer (shot of a car getting dropped on Jackhammer's leg Boston Strangler (shot of BQ and Heftel wondering why they're standing in front of an open grave) And the winner is... Boston Strangler! Good ol' BS couldn't be here tonight, lucky for me since I haven't fully trained for a trip down an elevator shaft yet, so accepting the award on his behalf... Jackhammer! Jackhammer limps out onto the stage from behind the curtain, and accepts the award. Jackhammer: I hope no one minds, but I've prepared a little speech, just in case I ended up winning this award or was called out to accept it on someone else's behalf. Jackhammer pulls a piece of paper out of his back pocket, unfolds it, and starts to read it. Jackhammer: I've been in the fWo for quite a while. I'm a former four-time Tag Team Champion, but that fact is only brought up when I lose to one of the new guys, making it seem like defeating me is a huge accomplishment. Sure, I got a car dropped on my leg a few months ago, but who hasn't? Now, I'm back, and... Jackhammer pauses, grabs a pen, and makes a correction on the piece of paper in front of him. Jackhammer: Now, I'm back, and even though I didn't win this award, I will still do my best to rip the collective head off of all who are thrown into my path. Sure, I may be a member of the STUMP Squad, but that doesn't mean I can't try to win a match every once in a while. BQ shrugs and give a "makes sense" nod. Jackhammer: So, in closing, if the Boston Strangler can come back from the dead, then surely the same can be said about my career, right? Thank you. Jackhammer leaves to BQ's applause. BQ goes back to his place behind the podium and gets another STUMPy. Dream Match BQ: Jackhammer, ladies and gentlemen. Now, onto the next award, which is what the voting public selected as their "Dream Match". The nominees are... Black Quicksilver vs. "Black Plague" Ruben Ross vs. Black Panther (obviously doctored shot of Panther and Ross standing over a fallen BQ, each with a foot on his chest... or is that picture NOT doctored?) Frank Snow vs. the fWo World Champion (obviously doctored shot of Snow raising the fWo World Title above his head) Claire vs. Kristen Jacobs (obviously doctored shot of Claire and Kristen in a close staredown, with their hands on their hips and heads poking forward, their faces just inches away from each other) Stump vs. Lamont (obviously doctored shot of Lamont doing the "crane" pose as Stump prepares for battle) Sphere vs. Damnation (obviously doctored shot of Hellrazor and Fear each ripping an arm off of Sphere) BQ: Before I announce the winner... that shot of Claire and Kristen Jacobs was as far as we could go without getting in any more trouble. On a side note, I hope you people were smart enough to pick that as your dream match. Now... the winner is... butter nuts! It's Stump vs. Lamont. Stump rides back onto the stage on his cart to accept the award. BQ: Since we've only got one STUMPy here, and Lamont is too big of a pussy to show up here tonight, Stump will be presented with the award! Stump? Stump: ... Stump leaves with his award. BQ: My, some strong words from Stump after winning his second STUMPy tonight! Now, for the next award! BQ places another STUMPy on the podium. Favorite Bathroom/Homework Break Match BQ: And the nominees for "Favorite Bathroom/Homework Break Match" are: Boston Strangler vs. Eron, June 14th Warfare (various shots of the referee taking the title away from both men) Mitch Wilson vs. Primetime vs. Rain vs. Wretch, 60 Minute Iron Man Match from Summer Slaughter (shot of numerous people in the crowd sleeping) Jackhammer vs. Ric Chronos, November 5th Slaughter (shot of Jackhammer looking up at the lights while laying on his back) Primetime vs. Courage vs. Lance Star, December 3rd Slaughter (shot of garbage getting thrown into the ring during the match) Black Quicksilver vs. Sphere, Dead Or Alive (shot of Crusader doing some of his math homework) And the winner is... Black Quicksilver vs. Sphere! Since we've only got one award, and Sphere is a bastard, albeit not the sickest bastard, but a bastard nonetheless, I will be taking this award home. I don't want Claire to catch me looking at her again, so I'll just put this aside for the time being, as I get set to present the next award. BQ gets two STUMPies out from underneath the podium. Match Of The Year BQ: Well, what do you know, there's two awards for this category, the category being "Match of the Year". The nominees: Sphere vs. Vengence, December 10th Slaughter (shot of Sphere driving a forklift into the crowd) Damnation vs. Acolytes, September 10th Slaughter (shot of Lamont hitting the Lamontsault on Faarooq) Scot Hartt vs. Abusah, November 19th Slaughter (shot of Hartt and Abusah running into each other and bumping heads) Black Quicksilver vs. Mike Heftel, Cyberslam 3 (shot of a graphic reading "WE WILL NOT SUBJECT YOU TO WATCHING FOOTAGE FROM THIS MATCH") Machina vs. Guano Apes, June 21st Warfare (shot of Jackhammer and Sphere signing autographs while Machina hides in the audience) And the winner is... Black Quicksilver vs. Mike Heftel! Mike, get out here! We won! Hurry! Mike Heftel runs out from behind the curtain and onto the stage. He trips and falls onto the stage looks around to see how that happened, shrugs, gets up, and runs over to the podium, where he grabs his award and celebrates with it as BQ does the same with his award. Heftel: Wow... um, this... this is totally unexpected. Two wins on back-to-back nights? I mean, if there's one guy who's more of a loser than me and Sean, it's Abusah... no wait, maybe that Belleshop guy, too. But, as I was saying, I thought for sure that Abusah had this award won, but thanks to Sean carrying me through that match, and getting paid even less for agreeing to lose to me, we prevailed! BQ and Heftel high-five. BQ: You know what, Mike? Why don't we present the final award together? Heftel: Really Sean? You mean it? BQ: Hmm, I don't know. Do I? Heftel: I don't know... do you? BQ: Hmm. Heftel: Hmm. BQ: Ah... yeah, of course I do! Heftel: Alright! Heftel grabs the last award from underneath the podium. Wrestler Of The Year BQ: The nominees... for "Wrestler of the Year" are: Heftel: Black Quicksilver (shot of BQ looking shocked as he is handed the Cruiserweight Title). BQ: Mike Heftel (shot of Heftel looking shocked as he is handed the Cruiserweight Title). Heftel: Frank Snow (shot of a brick flying into Snow's head). BQ: Markus Cairn (shot of Cairn getting powerbombed by Jackhammer). Heftel: Judge Steel (shot of Steel getting powerbombed by Jackhammer). BQ and Heftel, together: And the winner is... Both open the envelope. BQ: Oh my God! Heftel: It's... it's a tie! A tie between... Black Quicksilver... BQ: And Mike Heftel! Confetti rains down from the ceiling as BQ and Heftel raise the award for "Wrestler of the Year" together, and the rest of the STUMP Squad walks onto stage. Each STUMP Squad member takes turns hugging each other. BQ hugs Claire noticeably longer than anyone else, while, moments later, Claire jumps and lets out a little shriek shortly after she congratulates Stump. After all the celebrating is done, BQ steps back up to the podium, holding Stump. BQ: Well, we've... Suddenly, a wheel on Stump's cart moves slightly toward Jackhammer, and makes contact with his foot. Jackhammer: AGH! Jackhammer goes down in a heap, while, at the same time, a lighting fixture drops on Heftel from above. Heftel: Um... ow? BQ surveys the damage as a figure dressed in all black, with a mask, even, jumps out of nowhere, directly onto Claire. BQ watches the two women roll back and forth, before deciding to close the show. BQ: Well, what do you know, someone who seems to be a fan of mine... or a fan of Black Panther's... is now wrestling with the only female member of the STUMP Squad. Thanks for nothing, and goodnight! Claire and the masked figure fall off the stage while rolling, and right before the STUMPies go off the air, the masked figure can be seen getting up and running away from an angry Claire.
