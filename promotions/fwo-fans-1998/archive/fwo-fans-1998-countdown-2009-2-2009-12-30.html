Ruben Ross vs. Ric Chronos Ruben Ross paces backstage, shuffling back and forth, making sure he's stretched and limber. Chronos vs. Ross. A title on the line. A rematch from Countdown several years ago, where Ruben Ross beat Ric for the biggest prize in the FWO. "Christian Says" hits. Chronos steps out. Ross and Chronos step face-to-face, for the second night in a row - Ric extends his hand. Ross looks at it, but only just before he smacks it with a quick acknowledgement. They weren't strangers to each other, but Ross felt this time... this time it was sincere. The circling begins, each man gauging the other. Ross seems slightly slower than normal to Chronos. Ric looks a little stiffer, less fluid in his movements to Ruben. Here comes the lockup, and the two tie up, Ric pushing Ross back into the corner, dropping back for the clena break. Reaction times are gauged, calculated, and recalculated. The innate comprehension of the physics of brutality possessed by most wrestlers worked overtime in these two men, veterans of some of the most devastating battles the FWO had ever seen. Plan, counter, possible counter, reversals all played out in the fraction of an instant, ideas discarded almost as quickly as they came to life. Simultaneously, wrestlers came to the same conclusion - this wouldn't be settled by thought and strategy - they knew each other's limits too well.
