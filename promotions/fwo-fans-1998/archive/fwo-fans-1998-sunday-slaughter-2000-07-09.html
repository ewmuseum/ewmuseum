fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 9 July 2000

9 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Coveted. It may seem like just a bunch of leather with gold, but to some, it is the pinnacle of sports entertainment. The fWo World Title. Held by few, coveted by many. Tonight, The Boston Strangler sits atop the mountain, with the gold firmly around his waist for nearly a full two months now. Four men will be vying for a shot... Wretch. Mike Bear. Scott Slugger. Eron The Relentless. Wretch will take on Mike Bear in the finals of the Next Level tournament, and the winner will receive a shot at the fWo World Title at SummerSlaughter. Scott Slugger & Eron - who have been in a never-ending rivalry since late March - will collide with the winner receiving their fWo World Title shot this Wednesday on Warfare. Four men. Two World Title shots. One champion. And it all starts... Tonight. "The Future" Lance Star vs. Sid Snow The fans inside the Mark of the Quad arose to their feet, holding their signs and screaming wildly at the top of their lungs as fireworks exploded inside the ring, and on the SlaughterStage. This obviously signalled that the July 9th, 2000 edition of Sunday Night Slaughter was about to take place... and that it did. With "Last Resort" blaring throughout the arena, Slaughter officially began with "The Future" Lance Star walking out onto the stage with a microphone. Greeted with mainly boos and a few scattered cheers, Lance grinned as he walked down the stage. His music faded and Lance addressed the crowd. "You know, the great city of Illinois is known for a number of things... like, their sports. Now sure, the Chicago White Sox hold the best record in baseball... but, c'mon. Let's get real. They suck. Frank Thomas sucks. And every other player on their team sucks." The fans did not take kindly to this, and began booing even louder. "And then there's... Da Bears. Who? They suck too. When was the last time they had a winning record? See, you guys can't even remember!" More boos came from the crowd, and Lance scoffed at this. "You guys boo me only because I speak the truth! But, let's not stop there. The Cubs? Who? Sammy Sosa who? Actually, his name should be SAM-ME Sosa, because we all know Sosa would leave this hellhole overnight if he could and go play for a REAL team, like... the... well, baseball sucks period, so who gives a crap where he would go! And finally, that brings me to your..." Lance prepared his voice, before continuing. "as your announcer Ray Clay would say... SIX TIME NBA WORLD CHAMPION... CHICAWGOOO BOOLS! They're under the salary cap, they drafted some pretty good players, they might get Me Myself and Tracy McGrady, who doesn't know if he's going to play in Toronto this week, or Orlando, or Chicago... or Bombay, Indiana... but all in all, you know as long as they don't have Mike, they're going to suck!" This was the topper, and not only was Lance receiving boos, he was receiving middle fingered gestures from the crowd, and three minutes into the show, and the Slaughter ring was cascaded with a bunch of trash. Lance grinned proudly, and finally ended his diatribe. "And I bet you're all wondering what that has to do with Sid Snow. Well... we all know Sid Snow sucks, just like this hellhole of a city known as Illinois!" The crowd continued to boo, before finally, 'I know You Want Me' began to play, and the fans looked to the stage for the arrival of Sid Snow. Uncharacteristically the fans cheered his impedning entrance wildly... but a few moments passed, and Sid had not came out. Lance looked for his opponent, and even laid down, offering a chance for Sid to come out and immediately pin him, but still there was no signs of Sid. Finally, the referee told the ring announcer that he was going to start the match, and if Sid wasn't out here by the count of ten, Lance would win via forfeit. Ten seconds passed, and Sid hadn't arrived, so Lance received a notch in the win column. Lance looked disappointed, before getting on the microphone and proclaiming himself the king of forfeits. The fans booed slightly, but this time they were more annoyed by his presence than what he had said. Lance walked backstage grinning proudly, before Slaughter went backstage... Result: Star by forfeit Fate Backstage, the cameras quickly encountered a 2000 Dodge Viper pulling up into the garage of the Mark of the Quad, with everything being witnessed inside the arena on the SlaughterScreen. Obviously, it was the man who had a fetish for driving Viper's... Scott Slugger. Slugger turned off the vehicle, before exiting as Matt Loco rushed to get a word with the World's Greatest Athlete. "I'm Matt Loco, and tonight fans we are backstage to get a word with Scott Slugger, who has just arrived. Scott, tonight you face Eron The Relentless in a match that will unquestionably determine who will receive the fWo World Title shot this Wednesday on Warfare against the Boston Strangler. Chris Finn informed me earlier that if it is you that wins, it will be inside a fifteen foot high steel cage to prevent any chances of any outside interference occuring in the match. How do you feel about that?" Slugger smirked as he took off his $500 sunglasses, before tossing them into the driver's seat of the Viper. Pausing, he reflected on everything, before finally responding. "Fate. It is fate that the WGA will take home the fWo World Title, be it on Wednesday, be it a month from now, be it a year from now, be it in the year three thousand with me walking around via the aide of a walker... it doesn't matter. The fWo World Title will be in my possession again... and when it happens, it will be MY pleasure. Now, the WGA wishes to speak about Eron. It is no secret that Eron isn't even in the same league as the WGA. Scott Slugger may be looking at the Boston Strangler who is on top of the mountain with my fWo World Title, but Eron is down at the bottom of the mountain, looking up... and tonight, when you look up, you will see one thing, Eron. You will see my arm raised high above... and you will know that it wasn't your pleasure, it was mine. And Matt... until next time, the pleasure has been all yours." Slugger moved Matt aside as if it were nothing, before walking into the arena preparing to face his opponent of the night to determine the true number one contender... Damnation (c) vs. Bad Attitude Slaughter returned from the fWo "True" ad to the sounds of "Bad Attitude" by Deep Purple. Ice & the fWo Internet Champion Mitch Wilson walked out to nothing but boos, and showing off his charisma Wilson looked at the fans smugly. Ice looked all business, ready to compete with Damnation for the fWo World Tag Team Titles. Wilson held his fWo Internet Title above the ring, before placing it in a corner of the ring. Wilson then stretched out while Ice simply stood and awaited for Damnation to make their entrance. "From This Day" echoed off the arena walls, and out walked the fWo World Tag Team Champions. Hellrazor & Fear were greeted with an enormous positive reaction from the fans, who cheered for Lamont. Lamont was not present, but that didn't matter, as the fans looked forward to seeing Damnation dominate this match. The two teams took to their respective corners, the referee signalled for the bell, and the match was underway. Hellrazor & Wilson felt each other out, before locking up in a collar and elbow tie-up. Wilson got the advantage into a side headlock, but Hellrazor sent him to the ropes. Wilson returned and charged into Hellrazor who took him down with a shoulder block. Wilson got up and charged again and this time Hellrazor took him to the mat with a scoop slam, followed by a lariat that sent Wilson over the top rope to the floor. Ice came out to check on Wilson, and with the two men distracted Hellrazor tagged out to Fear. The duo backed up, before charging and sliding underneath the ropes with a simultaneous baseball slide. Wilson was met with two boots to the face as was Ice, who toppled over the guardrail into the crowd, prompting a frenzy of cheers from the audience. Fear then grabbed Wilson and brought him back into the ring, sending him to the ropes. Wilson returned and Fear held the fWo Internet Champion high above with a gorilla press, before dropping him and moving out of the way. Wilson smacked the mat hard, but popped back up and walked right into a chokeslam attempt on the part of Fear. With his hand firmly grasped around the neck of Wilson, Fear prepared to plant him into the mat with authority. However, the wily veteran nailed Fear in the face with an elbow, before bouncing off the ropes and staggered him with a flying forearm shot to the forehead. Struggling to maintain his balance, Fear staggered before Wilson grabbed him and planted him with a gutwrench suplex. Ice finally made his way back onto the ring apron, still groggy from the baseball slide that 'caused him to get up close and personal with the fans. Wilson tagged out to Ice, who went to the top rope and dove off with a diving headbutt. Hitting right on target, Ice went for a pinfall attempt, but Fear was able to kick out. The Big Blonde Machina brought Fear to his feet and backed him into a corner with a bevy of forearms, before chopping him across the chest. For added affect, Ice pulled the two straps of Fear' tanktop down, and chopped him some more, before hip tossing him out of the corner. Ice then methodically taunted Fear some more, adding insult to injury by saying he would eat his children if he had to. As Fear got up he was met with another forearm, before he was sent to the ropes with an Irish Whip. Fear returned and was met with two hundred and eighty one pounds of a powerslam. Another cover, but Fear kicked out. Ice tagged out to Wilson, before snapmaring Fear to the mat. Wilson took to the air with a frogsplash, but Fear kicked out of that as well. Appearing very confident and looking to finally end it, Wilson brought Fear up and hit a fisherman's suplex, or as Wilson dubbed it the 'Superstar Suplex'. He hooked the leg of fear, but Fear again escaped defeat. Still confident as ever, 'Mr. Main Event' stood to his feet, before tagging out to Ice. Wilson then whipped Fear to the ropes, and as Fear returned he found himself clutched in the large arms of Ice with a bearhug. Wilson wasn't done yet, and he charged with a clothesline. Feeling that it was over, Ice went for a cover... but the referee was backing Wilson out of the ring, telling him that he was close to being DQ'ed for being in the ring longer than five seconds. This allowed Hellrazor to break up the pin. Ice anticipated this, and he charged at Hellrazor as he entered with a clothesline. Hellrazor ducked, and Ice went into the corner instead, but no damage was done... until he turned around and was taken to the mat with a belly-to-belly suplex. Hellrazor exited the ring before the referee could look, and saw Fear crawling over to cover Ice with the arm. One. Two. Kickout by Ice, and now we had both Fear and Ice crawling to their respective corners to tag out. Fear' hand slapped the hand of Hellrazor to a massive pop, and Wilson was tagged in. Hellrazor came in a house of fire, taking Wilson down with a lariat. Wilson spung back up and Hellrazor sent him to the ropes, and when Wilson returned Hellrazor took him down with a drop-toe-hold. He then readed himself to tag out to Fear to complete the 'Final Judgment'. Fear came in and pressed Wilson as he did earlier, while Hellrazor reached the top rope preparing to fly off. A groggy Ice could do nothing but stand and watch as his partner is about to be decimated as he was on Warfare. However, out onto the stage walked Stone with her video camera, as well as War & Plague. War slid into the ring and chopblocked Fear, who essentially dropped Wilson ontop of himself. Plague pushed Hellrazor by the leg and Hellrazor flew into the ring, smacking into it hard. The referee witnessed it all and called for the bell, and Ice looked pissed that War & Plague ruined his chances of taking himself home some gold tonight. Ice came in and exchanged punches and forearms with War, while Plague darted around the ring kicking anyone he could. The referee tried to make the melee come to an end, but he was discouraged by the daunting task of emptying the ring of six large individuals. Suddenly, two-thirds of the Future Foundation joined in the brawl. Primetime went after Wilson directly, as he still had unfinished business with him. Primetime and Wilson did battle, while Star took a chair and smacked anyone he could with it, resulting in boos from the crowd, who still were not going to forgive him for what occured earlier on in the show. Noticeably absent was Star' partner Courage, but it was widely known that Courage had other matters to deal with tonight. As the pier6brawl continued, Slaughter went to a commercial break with more questions than answers concerning the tag team division... Result: No Contest Me Ruined The cameras found Eron backstage in his lockerroom, lacing up his boots with his hair barely cover his forehead, and part of his eyes. For dramatic effect this was filmed in black and white, and it essentially picked up in the middle of Eron's dialogue. "All your hopes and dreams... ruined in an instant. It's a situation you should know well, Slugger. Last week... with the help of some friends... I ruined your chances of winning the fWo World Title for a fifth time. And tonight... it will be me... without friends... who will ruin your chances yet again. Yes... tonight, in a few short hours... I will ensure that you walk away with blood on your hands... your blood... and not a World Title shot. It will be me, Scott. It WILL be ME... who faces the Strangler on Warfare. And it WILL be ME... who locks in the De-Roguer on Mr. Adams... and forces him to relinquish his grasp of that coveted prize. But so you don't feel left out, Scott... it WILL be YOU... who will takes a BREATH... of FRESH... DEATH. It WILL be YOU... it WILL be YOU..." Eron repeated that phrase several more times, before pushing the camera man over, and exiting the room. Slaughter rolled on... "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly vs. Rain We resumed action back in the ring as Rain walked down the entrance ramp while thunder and lightning cackled in the background. The mysterious man who stood at near seven feet tall climbed into the ring with ease, and his presence made the ring seem very small compared to his size. On the SlaughterScreen we saw Erik Kelly, who stood folding his arms... he outstretched them, and looked up at the ceiling, before 'Break Stuff' by Limp Bizkit began to play... obviously, what appeared on the screen was just a video, as the true master of Eradication, walked out to a massive greeting from the fans. The fWo Hardcore Title resided over his shoulder, and he entered the ring with a look of vengeance on his face. Kelly stepped over the ring ropes as easily as Rain did, and almost immediately, the match began as Rain charged and clothesline Kelly over the top ropes to the floor. Kelly landed on his feet however, before tossing the Hardcore Title aside and reaching up with his large hand. He grasped the ropes and pulled himself to the ring apron, before climbing back in. Rain had turned to boast to the crowd, and Kelly charged at him from behind and nailed him in the back with a double-axehandle. This staggered Rain, and the two bulls locked horns after that, neither man getting a clear cut advantage as they tried to gain some leverage with shots to the chest and face. Kelly backed into the corner and nailed Rain with some slaps to the chest, of which barely fazed the big man. Rain moved Kelly into the corner and hammered him with slaps to the chest as well, and Kelly went unfazed by the shots. Finally, Rain took Kelly out with a hiptoss, but Kelly blocked it and attempted a hiptoss as well. Rain blocked it before poking Kelly in the eye, which 'caused Kelly to be blinded temporarily. Using the advantage, Rain quickly went for a powerbomb, but Kelly blocked it, then reached up and nailed Rain in the little Rainies with a shot to the groin. Rain grimmaced in pain, although an expression of hellbent anger resided on his face as well. Kelly then charged and took him down with a lariat. Looking to dominate early, Kelly brought him up and sent him to the ropes. Rain returned and Kelly took him down with a big boot. Kelly then backed up and measured him, before dropping a massive legdrop to the chest of Rain. He went for a cover, but Rain kicked out at two. Kelly continued to dominate, sending Rain into the corner and clotheslining him there as well, before finally hiptossing Rain. Kelly waited Rain to get up, before taking the opportunity to yell "POWERBOOOOOOOOOMB!". Looking to finish him off, Rain reached up and returned the lowblow from earlier. With Kelly now in pain; Rain took over with a sharp DDT. He rolled him over and went for a cover, but Kelly kicked out. Taunting Kelly, he waited for him to get up, before charging and nailing him with a spear. He planted him into the mat, and then unloaded with a barrage of punches to the sides of the head. He finally covered him again, but Kelly kicked out at two. Looking displeased, Rain complained about the slow count. He sent Kelly into the ropes and as Kelly returns Rain sent him into the mat with a spinebuster. He finally raised his arms, resulting in boos, but thunder and lightning again resounded throughout the arena. He brought Kelly up and then prepared to take him out with a Rainmaker, but Kelly blocked it and backdropped out of it. Rain tried to hold on for the sunset flip, but Kelly reached the ropes and pulled himself out of his grasp. He then turned and as Rain got up, Kelly charged with a big boot to the face as well. Kelly then grabbed him by the hair and sent him over the top rope onto the floor, and Rain smacked hard. The fans cheered as Kelly climbed outside onto the ring apron, and waited for Rain to get up. Rain did, and Kelly took a big risk, by flying off the ring apron into a lariat. He missed his mark, and instead crashed into the guardrail and hitting the floor as well, as the crafty, more experienced Rain was able to move out of the way. Rain then waited for Kelly to get up, before charging and knocking him to the floor with an impressive elbow to the chin. The war of the big men continued, as Rain sent Kelly shoulder first into the ring steps. The referee began a ten count, since it was obvious he would be unable to control the two big men. Rain told the referee to piss off, before he prepared to send Kelly into the other set of steps again. Kelly however reversed it, and instead it was Rain who said hello to the ring steps. His shoulder weakened, Kelly fought it off, before finding his hardcore title, and nailing Rain in the forehead with it as he got up. Kelly then rolled Rain into the ring, which stopped the ten count at eight. Looking to take another risk but this time make good on it, Kelly climbed to the top rope and flew off with a lariat, this time hitting it as Rain crashed on the mat. He went for a cover, but at two, Rain was able to put his bottom foot over the rope to break up the pin. Frustrated, Kelly moved the referee aside before bringing Rain up and atomic dropping him, and Rain went flying into the corner. He hit it sternum first, before staggering out backwards. Kelly grabbed him and appeared to go for a belly-to-back suplex, but instead he placed him on the top turnbuckle. He then went to the outside on the ring apron, and reached up and nailed Rain in the jaw several times, weakening Rain some more. Kelly then prepared to do the unthinkable, and "Eradicate" Rain from the top rope with a powerbomb. He hooked him, and then he hit it. It took a lot out of Kelly as well, and he wasn't able to immediately cover the big man, but eventually he did, picking up the win. An exhausted and battered Kelly raised his arms to the delight of the crowd, before asking for a microphone... it appeared as if Kelly had something to get off his chest. "At Critical Condition... I defeated a fellow big man named Xavier to win this fWo Hardcore Title... the day after, I had a minor setback, but I had the daunting task of facing a mystery opponent... and that man was Rain. Of course, he defeated me after hitting me with a cinderblock at the beginning of the match... but tonight, I came back, and redeemed myself with an important victory. And with that... the Eradicator... self proclaims himself... the best big man... in this damn sport today!" Kelly finished that sentence with a yell, and the fans applauded his words, obviously agreeing with the big man. Kelly was about to continue, when suddenly from behind he was met with a shot to the head... by a block of dry ice. The dry ice crumbled, and Kelly was out cold in an instant, when just moments ago he was victorious. Ice obviously had taken offense to his self proclaimation of sorts... and decided to take matters into his own hands. He stood over Kelly with a look of anger, before giving him a middle finger gesture... and although the unconscious Kelly could not see it, he most certainly... felt it. Ice exited the ring with the sound of boos surrounding his departure as he headed backstage. A few moments passed before Pamela Dee and medical personnel rushed the ring to help Kelly out of the ring... and as they did, Rain began to get up as well. Rain saw what was going on and wasn't pleased, but he had no idea of knowing what happened after the match. He saw the dry ice, and saw Erik Kelly out... but was confused. Rain saw the referee, and demanded an answer. Before the referee could offer up an explanation, an impatient Rain grabbed him and powerbombed him to the mat, resulting in more boos from the crowd. Rain glared at the crowd with anger. His back to the stage, he had no idea what was about to happen. Wretch came out onto the stage with Abusah, and down on the floor Scot Hartt appeared. Wretch appeared to be giving directions to Abusah, who had a two-by-four in his hands. Abusah charged the ring, and waited as Hartt told Rain that he was about to say hello to wood. Looking confused, Rain turned and was met with a shot to the head by Abusah. Shards of wood flew everywhere, before Abusah stood above Rain with a big ass smile on his face. "SAY HELLOH TOO WOULD!" shouted Abusah, while Hartt joined Wretch on the stage, laughing and joking about what occured. Abusah exited the ring to a large pop, and he and his friends exited, with the ring looking like a car crash on I-15... Result: Kelly by pinfall Focused. Mike Bear sat in his lockerroom, resting after doing two hundred consecutive sit-ups. It's obvious from his top peak physical condition that he is ready... and ready to go to the Next Level. However, his former best friend Wretch stands in his path... and although the two are on some friendly terms right now, Bear doesn't care. "Tonight... one of us... will go to the Next Level. I've proven I am ready for it... by advancing this far in the tournament, and by ending the career of fWo legend Scott Collins a few weeks ago at Critical Condition. I've won the fWo International Title several times, I've won the fWo Tag Team Titles several times... I was the Hardcore Champion, and I carried that division for a lengthy period of time. Tonight, I am ready... nothing is going to stop me from winning tonight. Not a friend like Chris... wouldn't matter if my own mother was across from me... I'm going to the Next Level, and that is all there is to it. Whoever the champion is at SummerSlaughter better keep that belt nice and warm for me.. because come the 29th, I'm going home with that title around my waist... and everyone will fear... the Underground." The expression on the face of this athlete is one of determination, focus... and pride. The Next Level awaits. Ric Chronos vs. Fallen Slaughter returned to action as this was a match to determine the number one contender to the fWo International Title. The Black Panther would finally receive the shot he garnered nearly a month ago this Wednesday on Warfare; while tonight's contest would determine who would face the champion at SummerSlaughter. "Christian Says" by Tones on Tail began to play, and out walked Ric Chronos & Fallen, the former fWo World Tag Team Champions of nearly two months. Chronos & Fallen were greeted with cheers from the crowd, including support from a masked man sitting in the front row... The Black Panther. Chronos entered the ring and took to a corner to raise his arms to the crowd, while Fallen did the same in the other corner. The referee signalled for the bell, and Chronos looked out at the fans, ready to make the most of his opportunity. However, he glanced at the Black Panther... who caught his attention. Chronos snickered, before he and Fallen shook hands, and locked up. Chronos got the early advantage into a headlock, but Fallen sent him to the ropes. Chronos returned and leapfrogged the backdrop attempt, before he bounced off the other set of ropes. Rebounding, he came back and went for an elbow, but Fallen ducked that. The two men turned and faced each other, Fallen charged and Chronos took him down with a Japanese Armdrag. Both men rushed to their feet, and Chronos hit another one. Fallen slid out onto the floor to pace himself, and received a wish of good luck from the Black Panther. Fallen entered the ring and he and Chronos locked up again, this time Fallen takes the advantage into a headlock. Chronos countered into a waistlock, before going for German Suplex with a release. Showing signs of agileness, Fallen flipped out of the move and landed on his feet. Chronos turned expecting to see Fallen on the mat, but Fallen was on his feet and he took him down with a superkick. Fallen then brought him up and sent him into the ropes, and as Chronos returned Fallen took him down to the mat with a hiptoss. Fallen then brought Chronos up and hit a modified Death Valley Driver, before going for a pin. Chronos kicked out at two, and the match continued. Fallen took a risk by going to the top rope, and he flew off with a missle dropkick, but Chronos moved out of the way and watched Fallen hit the canvas. Chronos then decided to take over, first hitting a double-arm DDT, and then a inverted swinging neckbreaker. He went for a cover, but Fallen kicked out. Chronos applied a reverse chinlock, preparing to wear Fallen down for the Chronosphere. Moments passed and then Chronos brought Fallen up and sent him to the ropes... Fallen returned and Chronos slapped on a sleeperhold. He had the hold really locked in, and looked to wear Fallen out... a minute or so later, and Fallen was obviously groggy. The referee asked him if he could hear him, but no response could be heard. The referee rose his arm once, then twice, and the crowd began to cheer, wanting Fallen to be able to continue the match. Panther continued to offer his support as well. The referee rose his arm a third time, but this time Fallen left his arm up, and the crowd responded with a round of applause. They cheered him on some more, and finally Fallen broke the hold by reaching up and bringing Chronos over to the mat with a snapmare takeover. Exhausted Fallen braced himself via use of the ropes, and waited for Chronos to get up... Fallen was in the corner, and Chronos got to his feet and walked over and hooked him for the Chronosphere. He went to the corner, but Fallen escaped by pushing out and sending Chronos back towards the center of the ring. Chronos hit the mat hard, but recovered seconds later... dazed... from behind, Fallen hit the Lowfall. He quickly covered and hooked the leg, before getting the pin seemingly out of nowhere. Shocked, Fallen raised his arms in celebration, as he would be receiving an fWo International Title shot. The Panther gave him a thumbs up... but Fallen then realized that his celebration only 'caused more to dampen the mood of Chronos, which hasn't been good for several weeks. Fallen went over and consoled Chronos, who appeared upset, but happy that Fallen will be facing the International Champion at SummerSlaughter... from ringside, Panther looked on closely... Result: Fallen by pinfall Say Hello To The Next Level Backstage, Matt Loco tried to get a word with Wretch, the other finalist of the Next Level tournament. "I'm standing here backstage with Wretch..." his attempt to speak was only drownded out by the laughing from Wretch, Abusah and Scot Hartt. "Did you see his face? Absolutely priceless..." remarked Scot. Wretch joined in. "Yeah... Abusah, you showed him..." Abusah laughed. "I MAD HIM SAY HELLOH TOO WOULD!" Wretch and Hartt laughed... before Loco interrupted them. "Gentlemen, please, a little decorum wouldn't hurt right now... I'm wondering your thoughts on the finals of the Next Level tournament, Wretch?" Wretch paused, before responding. "Matty, the name is Wretchy, Big. And the fWo knows they couldn't possibly pass up the opportunity of having Big Wretchy The Ratings Saver facing The Boston Strangler at SummerSlaughter, because the shows leading up to it would have ENORMOUS ratings, and the pay-per-view itself... well, buyrates would explode... but enough about that... Rain, if you think that you scare me with your thunder and lightning... well, we've got two words for you... YOUR MOM~!" Wretch smiled, before Abusah chimed in with a catchphrase of his own. "YAH, BIOTCH! AND I HAV PROOVED DAT I AM THA MUTHA FUCKIN ABUSAH OF YO MOMMA... AND SOON, SHE IS GOEENG TO SAY HELLOH TO WOOULD!" Loco wore an expression of discomfort, and motioned to cut away from this scene of laughter... and although Wretch was in a laughing mood, he knew his match with Mike Bear later tonight was no laughing matter. The Next Level awaited. Scott Slugger vs. Eron The Relentless Slaughter heads to the ring for the first of two huge matches on the show, the other being the Next Level finals. This match will pit Scott Slugger against Eron The Relentless, a battle of two men who have gone from mere antagonists to raging enemies in the space of months. Tonight, the prize that will go to the winner is a shot at the fWo World Heavyweight Championship, the pinnacle of the industry. Matt Panzer announces that the match will have a 20-minute time limit. The fans first begin to boo as "Physical" plays and smoke bomb fireworks signal the arrival of the despised Eron, who wears an insidious grin on his face. Eron slowly and arrogantly strolls to the ring, taunting the crowd. He rolls in and signals that he wants da belt. The boos quickly turn to loud cheers as "Back In Black" starts to play and a legend of the fWo, Scott Slugger appears on the entrance ramp. Slugger walks to the ring, soaking in the cheers. Slugger turns an icy gaze on Eron, before rolling in and striking a pose on the ropes. Fireworks explode behind Slugger, creating a brilliant backdrop. Finally, the fireworks and music die down and the two turn and stare face-to-face. The bell rings and they have no reservations about immediately hammering away on each other with right hands. First Eron strikes with a stinging right, then Slugger counters with a jaw-jacker of his own. This continues until Eron gains the advantage with a kick to the gut. Eron reaches down and takes down Slugger with a double leg trip and holds on, but Slugger quickly flips Eron over and onto his side, not wishing to be put into the De-Roguer, let alone this quickly. Eron quickly makes his way to his feet and charges at Slugger, but Slugger ducks a clothesline and leaps high at Eron, before planting him with a rocker dropper. Slugger quickly covers, but Eron kicks out at one. Slugger brings Eron up and backs him into the ropes, before whipping Eron across the ring and into the ropes. As Eron comes off Slugger leaps up for a hurricanrana, but Eron catches him and plants him with a Liger bomb. Eron holds on, but Slugger kicks out at the ref's count of two. Eron briefly gets in the face of the ref, before an idea strikes him and he brings Slugger up. Eron hooks the leg of Slugger and wrenches it into a painful dragon screw. Slugger screams in pain, but Eron simply chuckles soullessly and drags him to his feet before repeating the move. Eron then lifts up the leg of Slugger and measures him, before dropping a hard elbow on the joint of the leg.
