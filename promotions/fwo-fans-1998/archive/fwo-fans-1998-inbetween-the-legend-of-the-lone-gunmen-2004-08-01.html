Introduction Once upon a time there was a group of individuals who called themselves the Lone Gunmen. Led by their masked leader Machina, they made an impact on the wrestling world that few would deny, or in many cases, remember. They have vanished from the world of professional wrestling in recent days, but their tale and adventures live on. This is their story... Sid and the Eclipse, too, had decided to move to England and help Terry through it all, and Kid Cool decided that there was a lot of Ireland to explore for elves. Slowly, moment by moment, it began to feel like old times again, although there was a weight that had settled over them that would never fully be lifted. The weather had grown sunny when Machina walked into Terry's office with a trace of a smile on his face. He was carrying a stack of papers under his arm, and he dropped them on the desk where Terry sat with a smile on his face. "Two years of research... I've been reading it." Terry nodded. He recognized the papers, of course... for he had seen Penny pouring over them for hours on end. "She did good work..." Machina grinned, "She did great work... and it's work that needs to be finished. So I've decided to stay and do it... to bring Penny's work to life. This promotion has a great history... a history you're still writing, but these last hundred years must be given back to the fans. I aim to do it." Terry smiled, a weak but grateful smile. "All by yourself?" "Of course not," said Machina, extending his hand and helping Terry to his feet. "I'll have the help of my friends." Terry looked down at the papers, then up at the face of his friend. "And you always will... because great dreams never die."
