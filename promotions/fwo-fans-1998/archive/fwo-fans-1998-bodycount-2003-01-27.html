Introduction Body Count first started in March of 2000 as the Pay Per View which held the first ever Body Count match. Though these days the Body Count match is to determine the number contender for the fWo World Heavyweight Championship at CyberSlam, the first BodyCount had the World Championship itself actually on the line. In a classic ending, Eron the Relentless screwed the Flying Frenchie out of winning the World title by walking out of the end of the match, allowing 'Dead' Austin Davis to win the belt, and subsequently led to his 'takeover' the next month at CyberSlam. Another classical match that night was Mad Trucker going against Machina, which was a Pop Icon match, with Trucker coming out as Elvis, and Machina as Paul McCartney. Another classic was VL vs. Hellrazor for the Hardcore title, which saw VL win the belt for the eighth and last time in her fWo career. Finally, the 'Tape' matchbetween Mike Bear and the Flying Frenchie is still remembered by some hardcore fans of the fWo. The following year, more classic matches came out of Body Count. There was the scaffold grudge match between MUD and Carnage for the Hardcore championship. In addition, the headlining Body Count match ended with a tie between Mitch Wilson and Wretch. Black Quicksilver not getting his full revenge on Chris Titan, A three way Cruiserweight title match between Lance Star, Ruben Ross and Crusader, and the World title match between Mike Bear and the Boston Strangler also helped round out the show. In 2002, several matches rang out. First was the Cruiserweight title match between Tempest and Monte Burns, where Burns admitted he was Tempest's secret admirer. Then, the Boston Strangler losing the war to Brand Frontier, who would temporarily retire him the next night. The four way Hardcore championship match was also a classic in its own right, with a new side of Donaven Winters coming out. The infamous Creed versus Slugger match was also a classic, with Creed's heart attack costing him the title. Ruben Ross's iron man match with Austin Davis was another unforgettable match. And finally, the Body Count match which saw Erik Kelly taking five pins in the last few minutes to win the match.
