Sunday Slaughter, 3 December 2000

3 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
31: Countdown To Oblivion December 2000 for the fans Wrestling organization kicked off in Washington, DC at the MCI Center. Inside the arena, fWo Commentators Jeremiah Frankton & Stevie Roberts walked down the entrance ramp to some cheers, although it should be noted that Roberts' cheers were much louder than Frankton's. The fans welcomed them to ringside, and they took their seats in preparation to call tonight's action. Inside the ring, fWo Ring Announcer Matt Panzer told the fans that we would go live in twenty seconds. The fans cheered loudly, and finally after the twenty seconds had passed - fireworks began shooting off on the stage, and music blared loudly throughout the arena. The fans rose to their feet and cheered, as finally the show that had been sold out since mid-October had taken to the air. With a surprise, it seemed. "Beautiful People" began blaring throughout the MCI Center, and a red strobe light flickered on and off which of course was the entrance for one of the most hated - if not the most hated - men in fWo history. Austin Davis. Dead. The fans began booing immediately at his mere presence. They had no reason to like him - back in January, he attempted to kill the fWo. In April, he became the official owner of the fWo, and shut it down for three weeks. In August, he began playing a series of mind games with The Boston Strangler, and it is safe to say that Davis is a big reason for the latest change in attitude of the Strangler. Nevertheless, Davis looked at the fans, and snarled. He didn't care what the fans thought. Why should he? He was a three time fWo World Champion without the help of the fans. He didn't need them, and he certainly didn't want them to be in his corner. Davis now stood in the ring with thousands of hostile, angry fans around him - booing loudly. Without hesitation, Davis gave them the middle finger, before reaching into his black trenchcoat and pulling out a microphone. As his music came to a halt, and the arena went back to it's usual status, Davis began to let everyone know just exactly why he was here. "For those of you who don't quite remember... earlier this year, before CyberSlam... back in January, I was in control. I don't like to admit defeat... but The Boston Strangler took the power I had away from me... twice. When I was in control in the beginning, it was from a different standpoint. Not an ownership standpoint... but a booking slash commissioner type standpoint. If you remember... with that power... I ensured that I and Scott Slugger - who was just another person I used to get what I wanted - would receive title shots if we lost our said power... at any point in the future. When we decided... we would each receive a shot. We both knew that after what we had done to this company, there was no way that Chris Finn & James Armstrong would give me or him a title shot. That changed for Scott, obviously... as he kissed a little ass and got back in their good graces. But now he's paying for it... injured... at the hands of The Boston Strangler. Back to my point... and just to back all of this up, if you would all take a look at the SlaughterScreen... I'll have those fWo technical geeks play the evidence of this." Davis stopped talking, and pointed everyone's attention to the SlaughterScreen, where a videotape of the 01.23.00 Slaughter played, proving that what Davis was saying was true. "Oh, one last thing, should the Boston Strangler do the impossible and defeat me here tonight, and let's face it, the chances of that happening aren't very good... should this be our last night of power, well... we're going to take out an insurance policy for ourselves. That's right. At some point in the future, whenever WE decide, we both will receive a shot at the fWo World Title. And at any point in the future, we may put this into affect... whether you like it or not." The videotape stopped, and Davis resumed speaking. "Well, I've decided that the time is right... not tonight... but at the final televised show of this year - Countdown To Oblivion 1 - I will be facing whomever the fWo World Champion is. Be it Primetime... be it The Flying Frenchie... be it Mike Bear... be it anyone else, they will face me. And just as Jim Dudley did... just as Stevie Roberts did... just as the entire fWo did... they will suffer extreme pain and punishment... they will be crucified. The countdown has just begun. Prepare for oblivion. Trust me." Davis outstretched his arms in a crucifixion style pose... and the fans again booed him as they had done earlier. This time, Davis completely ignored all of it, and exited the ring. He was then mobbed by the crowd, as he decided to officially exit near the fans. Slaughter had officially began with an unexpected visitor... and while the shock was still setting in, Slaughter took to it's first commercial break. 'Black Plague' Ruben Ross (c) vs. Crusader Slaughter returned to the sounds of Biohazard's "Switchback". A former Cruiserweight Champion, and the runner up in the fWo Wrestler of the Year tournament, Crusader, steps onto the entrance ramp, and he will soon get a chance to regain that Cruiserweight championship. "Firestarter" by the Prodigy starts up, and the Black Plague, Ruben Ross steps onto the entrance ramp. He holds the title high for all to see, and then slings it over his shoulder, continuing to the ring. He hands the title over to the referee, and the bell rings. The men begin circling each other, and then lock up. Ruben takes Crusader over in a side headlock, and shoots him into the ropes. Crusader comes back, and Ruben leapfrogs. Instead of hitting the other side, Crusader stays behind, and drops Ruben quickly with a reverse DDT. Crusader hits the ropes, and nails an Asai Moonsault, as that wacky fan who seems to be at every fWo show holds up his "ASAHI!" sign. Crusader covers, but at two, Ruben kicks out. Crusader brigns Ruben up for a michonoku driver, but Ruben gets out of it. He spins Crusader around, and nails a boot to the gut, followed by a nice powerbomb. Ruben hooks the leg on the cover, but Crusader kicks out. Ruben nails a scoop slam, and then decides to head to the top rope. He flies off with a guillotine legdrop, but Crusader moves. Crusader then perches himself up on the top rope, and awaits for Ruben to get up. When he gets up, Crusader takes him down with a missle dropkick. Crusader then covers, and at two and a half, Ruben kicks out. Crusader brings Ruben up, and sends him to the outside. Crusader decides to go flying once more, and waits for Ross to get up. He flies off looking for a splash onto Ruben, but Ross moves, and Crusader nails the guardrail face first. Ruben brings Crusader up and into the ring, and nails a facebuster. He covers, but at two Crusader kicks out. Ruben brings Crusader up once more and sends him into the ropes. Crusader ducks a chop, and comes back looking for something, but Ruben grabs him and drops him throat first across the top rope. Crusader then bounces back into a cradle piledriver from Ruben. Ross covers and this time it would have been over, had Crusader not gotten his foot on the bottom rope. Ross then brings Crusader up, and nails the Plague of Darkness. One, two, and the referee is distracted by Stone, who had come out to ringside from the back. Ross looks over at her, and shouts obscenities in her direction, while Plague comes in and drops Ruben from behind with a German Suplex. Meanwhile, War comes in wielding a chair, and Plague stands Ruben upright. War swings the chair, but Ruben ducks, and the chair cracks Crusader over the head. Crusader falls in a heap, and Ruben disposes of War and Plague. Meanwhile, Ruben sees that Crusader is out cold, so he covers, and easily records the three count. Slaughter goes to a commercial break, with a shot of Stone on outside of the ring looking very displeased about what had just occured. Result: Ross by pinfall Reign Of Confusion Slaughter resumed from the commercial break to find The Boston Strangler entering the MCI Center. A camera crew and Kristen Jacobs sought to catch-up with the native Bostonian. Strangler walked swiftly, as if to avoid them. Jacobs, however was too quick, and despite the camera crew being several feet behind her, she finally caught up with him. "Hello fWo fans, I'm standing alongside the Boston Strangler... and Mr... er... Strangler, I was just wondering if you had anything to say about your recent feud with Erik Kelly?" Jacobs held the microphone up to his chin and prepared for him to say something... but no words came out. The Strangler stopped, and with his eyebrows raised but his eyes nearly shut, he waved her off. Being a true reporter, Jacobs persisted. She continued to follow him trying to get a word from him... about something, about anything. "Well... if you don't have anything to say about that, what about the fact that you're scheduled against the Flying Frenchie next week? I find out earlier tonight that you would be facing the man from Toulouse, and it's quite possible that it could be his second to last match as a member of the fWo roster. Your thoughts?" Strangler again stopped... and it appeared he did have something to say, but couldn't. Instead, he shrugged it off, and walked away. He bumped into Ruben Ross who was now backstage after his win over Crusader just moments ago. Ross took a swig of his bottled water as Jacobs instead tried to get a comment from him. "Ruben! May I have a word?" Ross nodded, since he always enjoyed talking to her. Jacobs had the camera crew set up their equipment, as it was clear they were tied from chasing after the Strangler and hauling around the expensive machinery. With that, Jacobs prepared to interview the reigning Cruiserweight Champion, in the meantime, Slaughter went back to the ring for a Hardcore Title Match. Carnage (c) vs. "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly vs. Vengence ELO hits the arena speakers, bringing out Vengence for his chance at fWo gold. The hardcore title is now up for grabs, and the fans are beginning to stand up, anticipating a very violent matchup. Vengence, instead of going down to the ring, takes out his lead pipe and attempts to hide himself under the security of the entrance ramp. Meanwhile, Metallica hits the speakers, bringing out the Eradicator, Erik Kelly. Kelly, however, doesn't come out. Vengence waits for a minute, and then peeks his head out to see if he's coming. Kelly, meanwhile, seeing the entire time what Vengence was doing, comes out from the crowd, and grabs the head of Vengence, before driving it into the corner of the entrance ramp. Vengence turns and finds a right hand turned into a fist, headed right for his nose. Vengence blocks it, and tries one of his own. Kelly blocks that, and headbutts him, causing Vengence to fall against the ramp. Meanwhile, the fWo Hardcore Champion, Carnage, comes flying out to the ringside area, and sets himself up on the sound equipment, out of the eye of the brawling Kelly and Vengence. He eyes up the situation, grabs a speaker, and takes flight with a splash onto both men. Vengence nails Kelly with a right hand, causing him to fall back. However, Vengence hasn't moved. This basically means that Carnage took out Vengence with the speaker. In the aftermath, Kelly grabs Carnage, and tosses him into the steel guard rail. Carnage hits the floor, and Kelly senses an upper hand. He picks up Carnage, and runs him toward the ring, before tossing him in. He then grabs a table, and tosses that in as well. Kelly turns around, and is met with a big boot by Vengence, who is wearing part of the speaker around his neck. He grabs that part of the speaker and clobbers Kelly upside the head with it. Kelly swaggers to one knee, and Vengence climbs into the ring with a chair. Meanwhile, Carnage, who has gotten up, begins charging Vengence by bouncing off the ropes. Vengence doesn't quite see it coming, until he sees Carnage's feet coming at his head. He throws his arms up to block it. However, the chair is attached the hands. As a result, Carnage kicks the chair, which nails Vengence in the head. Vengence falls onto the apron, then to the mat below. Carnage begins to set the table up in the middle of the ring, when he sees Kelly beginning to get back into the ring. Carnage walks over, and goes to pick Kelly up. However, Kelly, having other ideas, grabs the leg of Carnage, and pulls him to the outside. Carnage and Kelly begin to brawl, with Kelly getting the upper hand with repeated rights. He then grabs Carnage, and picks him up. He runs toward the guard rail once again, and gives Carnage a good toss. Carnage lands on the guard rail, causing it to fall backwards. Kelly starts to stalk after him; however, Vengence, still with that chair, nails him in the back. Kelly turns around, and Vengence nails him on the top of the head. It seems that Vengence was using the first chairshot as an attention-getter, and a successful one at that. As Vengence walks over top of Kelly, he is suddenly greeted by a very undesired guest; Kelly's boot in his groin. Vengence drops the chair in front of him and begins to bowel over; Carnage, meanwhile, has been stumbling to his feet. He sees what is happening in front of him and makes a charge towards Vengence. He grabs the head and DDTs him onto the chair. Carnage gets up, still wobbly, and is met with a hand around his neck by Kelly. Kelly lifts him up and chokeslams him through the second rope, and into the ring. Kelly grabs Vengence and tosses him into the ring as well. Kelly climbs into the ring, and notices a small problem. Two people to mess with. Sensing the possible trouble it would cause him, he grabs Carnage once again, and throws him back out of the ring. He picks up Vengence and sets him up for a chokeslam through the table that is set up in the ring. He lifts him up, and plants him in the middle of the table, which buckles and is destroyed under his weight. The fans go crazy, as this very crazy match will result in a new champion. Kelly covers Vengence, and the ref, a forgotten member in this match, makes the count. One. Two. Three. Or...maybe not. As the ref's hand was on the way down for that final three, Carnage flew back in, lead pipe in hand. Carnage struck Kelly in the back of the head, causing Kelly to go out cold, and the count to be broken. The ref makes the clarification while Carnage climbs to the top rope. He signals for (half-of) the Maximum Carnage. He climbs to the top, and lines Kelly up. He takes flight, and nails the elbow. He quickly covers, and gets the three count, thus escaping with the title. Meanwhile, Vengence climbs to his feet. He grabs Carnage and tosses him out of the ring, before picking up Kelly. This jolts Kelly, who pushes Vengence. Vengence begins to push back, and a brawl ensues. Security comes down to break up the brawl, which is beginning to get out of control, as Slaughter pushes on.... Result: Carnage by pinfall Backstage Confrontation(s) Backstage, cameras caught up with Juno Law, who casually walked backstage. Heading in the opposite direction fresh off his interview with Kristen Jacobs was Ruben Ross. Knowing the history of these two men, this wasn't good. However, maybe we would get lucky and this upcoming confrontation would be more of a verbal one than one of physical nature. Law and the fWo Survivor bumped into each other on accident, but you could feel the tension building between these two as they got into each other's face. Ross broke the silence. "How is your therapy coming? I was commenting to Kristen that at least someone around here has a Mental Health Plan." Ross smirked as did Juno, but Juno clinched his fists in preparation for a fight. However, Ross would soon find out that Law could insult with the best of them. "Things are going very nicely, Ruben. Thanks for asking. By the way, when's the fWo going to have a doctor nitpick that brain of yours? It won't take much time, since we all know there isn't much there to begin with." Ross again smirked. "Good comeback Juno, but this isn't grade school... this is the big time, and this right here..." - Ross pointed to his Cruiserweight Title - "separates the winners from the losers." Juno had enough of Ross' comments and appeared to be ready to come to blows, but instead he kept his cool... as if he knew something that Ross didn't. "Keep talking your trash, Ross. It's not all about poppin that twenty share, you know? It isn't all about the gold, Ross... remember that. It all comes back around." Juno left the conversation with the final words. Ross wondered what Juno meant, and wondered how he was able to stay calm when in the past he wasn't. Ross shrugged it off, and turned a corner. There, he bumped into another arch nemesis. "Kodiak" Vic Creed. Ross stared up to his massive six foot three inch frame, and Creed laughed. "How's the weather down there, Rossco?" Ross smirked, before telling Creed off. "The weather is great, Vic. I've got gold... I've got charisma... and I've got twenty shares week after week. What do you have?" Creed ignored it, and pushed Ross out of his way. Creed went to walk away from Ross as if he meant nothing to him, before Ross mouthed off some more. Creed asked Ross if he was ready to back up his trash talk by agreeing to a match at Twilight. Ross not only agreed to it, he welcomed the challenge. And so another match had been declared official for Twilight. Two of the three fWo Survivor finalists would be competing against each other come December 16th. Creed & Ross headed in separate directions. And fortunately for us, two backstage confrontations had occured in the same segment... and nothing too physical happened. Ric Chronos (c) vs. The Black Panther Sunday Slaughter returns to the sounds of the Foo Fighters "My Hero". The crowd immediately begins to jeer as the Black Panther steps out onto the entrance ramp for his Internet Title shot. Soon enough, "Christian Says" by Tones on Tail starts, and the reigning fWo Wrestler of the Year, and Internet Champion, Ric Chronos sprints to the ring. He throws the title down and slides in, immediately brawling with Panther. Panther takes over and sends Chronos into the ropes nailing a lariat as he comes back. Chronos falls down but gets up immediately, and Panther picks him up and nails a sidewalk slam. He quickly covers, but at two, Chronos kicks out. Panther brings him up and naisl some quick chops across Chronos' chest, and he sends him into the ropes again. Chronos ducks the lariat this time, and comes back with a flying shoulderblock. The move sends the Panther all the way to the outside. Taking a risk, Chronos flies over the top with a suicide plancha, which connects with the Panther. Chronos stays on top of Panther firing lefts and rights which connect with his former friend. He rolls Panther into the ring and then heads to the top rope, which is something that he doesn't do too often. Chronos looks for a flying elbow drop, but the Panther rolls out of the way. Panther gets up and hooks him for the pumphandle suplex, but Chronos reverses into a go behind. With a mighty lift, he nails a german suplex complete with bridge. At two, however, Panther powers out of the bridge. Both men reach their feet, and Chronos charges. Panther ducks, and stays behind. He nails a belly to back suplex, and then covers Chronos. At two, Ric kicks out, but Panther stays on the attack, lifting him up by the hair. Panther nails a few right hands to the face, before sending Chronos into the corner. He looks to drive at him with a splash, but Chronos moves out of the way. Panther nails the corner face first, and then Chronos rolls him up, but he only is able to muster up a two count. Both men to their feet, and Chronos hooks Panther from behind, and he nails a reverse DDT. Chronos quickly jumps on for the cover, but at the last possible second, Panther kicks out, as the crowd gasps in astonishment. Chronos complains to the ref about a slow count, but there's nothing he can do about it. Meanwhile, Panther recovers, and spins Ric around. He nails a boot to the gut, followed by a HUGE ax kick to the back of the head. Chronos falls to the ground in a crumpled heap, as the Panther celebrates. Panther perches Chronos on the top rope, and then comes down. Panther pulls the referee to the side, as the crowd wonders what the hell he is doing, but it all becomes clear as the reigning fWo World Champion, Primetime, runs to the ring yielding a chair. He jumps on the ring apron, and nails Chronos in the head with it, knocking him even more loopy than he previously was. As Primetime exits from the ring apron and goes ringside, the Panther then goes to the top, and nails the Pounce. He covers, and at a count of three, the fWo has a new Internet Champion in the form of the Black Panther. Panther and Primetime celebrate inside of the ring, meanwhile stomping Chronos, until the Flying Frenchie hits the ring. The heels flee the area, and celebrate on the ramp, while Frenchie checks on Chronos. Meanwhile, Slaughter heads elsewhere... Armstrong appears to be in deep thought looking at the local Washington Post, when someone knocks on the door. Before Armstrong can even ask who's there, the door swings open. Armstrong quickly folds the paper up, and looks at the visitor with a deep "gulp". The visitor is six feet ten inches of pissed off hostility - "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly. Armstrong politely asks Kelly what he wants, but Kelly is not in the mood for politeness. "Armstrong... I don't care what you have to do right now, just schedule Vengence and I for a match at Twilight. This feud needs to be settled. I need to teach him what happened in the past is the past... and there's no better way to end things than at Twilight." Armstrong nodded. "Alright. You get your match. And as an added incentive... to entice things and make it interesting, the match will be wrestled under hardcore rules. Also - whoever wins the match will face whomever holds the fWo International Title later on the same night as an added bonus. Fair?" Kelly thought it was extremely fair, especially considering all that he wanted was a match with Vengence. Kelly nodded, and exited the room. As he went to leave, someone else was there... ready to enter. Chris Universal. Mr. $ellout. Kelly acknowledged Universal and welcomed him back to the fWo. Universal thanked him, and told him that Kelly had become one of his favorite performers during his time away from the ring, and in the future they should "team up". Kelly nodded, before fully leaving. Armstrong sighed as Universal entered the room. "Come in, Chris. What can I do you for... er... do for you?" Universal stayed approximately five feet away from Armstrong, as he made it a personal rule for himself way back when since Armstrong is known to take his obsession of wrestlers a little too far. "Well, James... it's pretty simple. I heard earlier that you booked Frenchie against the Strangler next week." Armstrong nodded. "Pierre wanted one last match with one of his longtime rivals. I put all of the names of his rivals into a hat... and out came Strangler'. Hope Pierre doesn't kill me." Universal shrugged. "Whatever. I don't care how you set it up. I was just wondering if you could put me in that match, seeing as how last week could be the last night Frenchie is in action here. How much more people does he have to injure before you finally suspend him?" Armstrong quickly defended himself. "Hey, I've been under a lot of stress lately, and I've had a lot on my mind. Cut me some slack, okay? I'll make it up to you... somehow... I promise." Chronos chuckled, as if he didn't believe him. "Yeah, sure. You must have had a lot on your mind. How in the world could you allow Mike Bear & Wretch decide who they would be defending the tag team titles against at Twilight?" Armstrong seemed unaware of this, and what he said verified that. Chronos nodded, before asking Armstrong if he wasn't really going to make things up to him. "Ric, a lot of people think I'm a buffoon... that I'm incompetent... that I'm a fool. They may be right to a degree. But I'm a good man deep down inside, and I will make it up to you somehow. Maybe I'll send some blackjack and hookers over to the hotel later, okay? On second thought, just some hookers." Chronos smiled, and left the room. Finding himself left alone, Armstrong grabbed a 'post-it' note from nearby, and wrote something down on it.
