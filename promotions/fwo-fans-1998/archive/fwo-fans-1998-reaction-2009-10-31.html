That was why, when Spike and Alias locked up again, Spike lifted him up and threw him backwards into his home corner. The Wolf reached for the tag, but Alias stepped out of it. A third lock up, and Alias drove the tip of his boot into Spike’s shin! The big man’s center of gravity shifted, and Alias used the opportunity to flip him over with an armdrag takedown that landed him on the mat! Alias ran to the opposite ropes as Spike climbed to his feet and hit a dropkick just as the giant was coming off one knee, and sent him partway between the top and middle rope! Tag to Karina Wolfenden, who climbed the top turnbuckle and dropped down on the back of Spike’s head with a modified clothesline, while Alias had his foot on the big man’s back! K-Wolf landed outside the ring and immediately slid back in, and tagged Alias again! The former Tag Team Champions double – whipped one of the people who took the belts from them into the ropes, and the Wolf bounded into the adjacent side! Fist to the stomach by Alias! Scissor kick by the Wolf! Spike went face first into the mat! Alias rolled him over, ONE… TWO… Kickout! Another scoop, and another tag to the K-Wolf. Karina climbed to the top turnbuckle as Alias set Spike up—SPIKE WITH A REVERSAL! HE JUST SHOVED ALIAS INTO THE CORNER! K-Wolf nearly lost her balance, but held onto Big Bad’s shoulders! Spike with a handful of K-Wolf’s hair, and he just tossed her to the mat! Alias up to the bottom rope, and he just jumped onto Spike’s back with a sleeper – slash – chokehold! Caine with a count on Alias, but Spike just backed him into the corner! Elbow to the side of his head! Spin and a punch to Impulse’s face! Impulse just flew off the apron! Xander Scott with a shot, but Spike with a headbutt! XS off his feet, but he held onto the middle rope, just barely! K-Wolf with a dropkick to the back! CHEESE JUST HIT THE RING! EGG NOG FOLLOWED! The Legion of Dairy grabbed K-Wolf from behind and double – slammed her to the mat! Spike pulled Alias out of the corner and dropped him with a short – arm clothesline. He ignored Donald Caine’s insistence that he let Whiskey Jack leave the ring, just as the Legion of Dairy was ignoring the same warnings. Speaking of, the LoD pulled The Wolf back to her feet again and each held an arm, and gestured to Spike to finish her off. For a few steps, at least. Just as Spike was about to attack, cHEESE let go of Karina’s right arm and pointed. Too late, though, as Xander Scott came off the top rope with a dropkick nearly halfway across the ring, which caught Spike on the back of the head and between his shoulder blades. Spike fell forward into the referee, who fell backwards into cHEESE! K-Wolf punched egg NOG in the face, turned around, and kicked Spike in the ribs! Callie Urban just hooked Karina from behind! T-Bone Suplex! Xander pulled Spike to his knees and measured him for another kick, but cHEESE blocked it and kicked him square in the crotch! Spike wrapped a hand around XS’ throat, and hoisted him up one handed! Chokeslam! SPEAR BY ALIAS! Scoop by cHEESE! Whip into the ropes… Alias reversed it! egg NOG with a German Suplex! IMPULSE WITH A SLINGSHOT OVER THE TOP ROPE! SUDDEN IMPACT ON CHEESE! He dropped down for the cover, but Donald Caine was still out! It’s just as well, he would’ve gotten to about one half before Callie Urban grabbed him by the top of the head and shoved him backwards into the corner, where she landed a series of kicks to his ribs and chest. Karina Wolfenden and Spike Saunders were both to their feet at about the same time, and surveyed the damage – egg NOG was pounding Alias with right hands, Xander Scott was climbing to his feet, cHEESE was out cold, and Impulse and Callie Urban were in the corner. K-Wolf gestured to Spike to bring it, and the seven foot monster stepped toward her. Karina backed up a step to center herself – YoGuRt on the apron, he grabbed a handful of hair! Spike with a boot against Karina’s throat in the corner! Suddenly, YoGuRt lost his grip! Impulse Classic was on the floor with one of his boots in their hand, and was pulling hard! YOGURT FELL TO THE FLOOR BELOW! Spike with a knee to K-Wolf’s stomach, and he wrapped both hands around her neck and pulled her out of the corner! He was choking her, forcing her down! Callie whipped Impulse across the ring, and he hit the opposite turnbuckle with his chest full force! Alias caught one of Nog’s fists and twisted his arm and shoved him away, giving him enough time to roll to his knees! Donald Caine stirred the slightest bit! Urban ran her hand across her neck and grabbed Xander Scott from behind as he was steadying himself on the ropes! She spun him around, elbowed him in the face – GENTRIFICATION! Still no referee! Karina Wolfenden was turning red, but she still struggled as much as she could against Spike’s grip! Her guts and defiance were cause for respect and enough for the fans to rally behind as they chanted her most recent nickname with a passion: AS-TRO-POP! AS-TRO-POP! IMPULSE CLASSIC JUST CAME OFF THE TOP TURNBUCKLE! LANDED ON SPIKE’S BACK AND SWUNG AROUND TWICE! MODIFIED TORNADO DDT! Karina dove to the right as the grip on her airway broke, and Impulse Classic rolled through like a cat! The impact was enough to catch Callie Urban’s attention, and she stepped toward the masked figure! Spike, likewise, was dazed by the blow, though on his feet in a hurry! Impulse Classic tugged at the mask and pulled it off— —to reveal a head of shoulder length, auburn hair, and the face of a young girl. Very young, couldn’t have been more than twenty, twenty one years old. Eagle eyed fans would recognize her as the girl that sometimes appeared in Impulse and Xander Scott’s backstage segments. What was her name again? Rose. She locked eyes with Callie Urban, who looked like she hesitated for just a second, before both she and Spike stepped toward her. That second’s hesitation was all it took. SUDDEN IMPACT on Callie Urban! Spike’s attention twisted to Impulse, and Rose took the opportunity to slide under the bottom rope to the floor! The seven footer rushed the Marathon Man, who sidestepped! Spike nearly ran chest – and ribs – first into the corner, but stopped himself at the last second. Impulse jumped onto Spike’s back and wrapped his arms around his neck in what looked to be a sleeper hold! Spike worked at his grip for a few seconds, but, realizing that he could pretty much walk around without any difficulty with the one hundred and eighty eight pound Impulse on his back, turned around and rammed backwards into the corner! Impulse’s grip loosened, but held tight! Another ram! Alias ducked under an egg NOG right hand and hooked him, and PULPED him! cHEESE was finally up, and he ran at Big Bad! Drop toe hold by Karina Wolfenden! Spike flipped Impulse over his shoulder and grabbed Alias by the back of the head! ONE… TWO… THREE! Donald Caine called for the bell, and everyone froze! The fighting stopped, and all eyes drew to the far ropes, near the middle of the ring, and Xander Scott with a single arm draped across Callie Urban’s chest! That was pretty much all it took for the six standing and semi – standing competitors to start fighting again. Rose gently pulled Xander Scott out of the ring, and pulled Callie out on a different side, and then ducked out of the way as three more referees ran down to help quell the riot! The Legion of Dairy had sent Karina Wolfenden over the top rope, and Alias and Impulse both slid out at their first opportunity to check on their respective tag team partners. Stevie Roberts had returned both tag team belts to Spike Saunders, who raised one in each hand above his head and pointed at Callie, who was resting her chin on her arms on the mat outside the ring, and the fans booed! Until Donald Caine, in the aisle, grabbed Xander Scott’s hand and raised it, and XS, in turn, raised Impulse’s, and pointed toward the Entourage, and the fans cheered! “The winners of this bout,” said Stevie Roberts, “The team of Vox Nihili, and Excess Impact!” Impulse subsequently grabbed both Big Bad and the Wolf’s hands and raised them as well, to an even larger ovation. The two members of Vox Nihili looked at each other as if they weren’t entirely sure what to make of the way the match came to an end, but Alias still patted Impulse on the shoulder and Xander on the back as the former Tag Team Champions left ringside first. reAction’s credits rolled, and while Ivy McGinnis’ plan to put the offending teams against each other in hopes that they’d beat some sense into each other may have raised some eyebrows, it was clear that nothing was solved here. Except for Excess Impact earning a title shot. Technically, they were due to receive the next shot. Technically. Nothing about the World Tag Team Championship had been simple in the past few months. Result: XSI and Vox (XS over Callie, XSI earns title shot)
