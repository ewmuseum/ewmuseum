Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Wrestlers slowly filed out of the arena, some still in ring attire with towels or jackets and some dressed in casual attire. It had been a long night in Florida, and the workers were tired and ready for a long night's rest. As they filtered through the backstage area they passed Geoff Green, who sat sullen on a pile of crates and watched them go. Joy and Ultra Violet spotted him there, and walked over to comfort the fWo cameraman. "Hey Geoff, don't look so glum... it happens to everybody!" Ultra Violet patted Geoff on the back, and Geoff looked up with dismay. "Happen to everybody, Clarence? I sent a camera crew out to the ring without any damn tape in the cameras! I took two hours of dead air! Tell me just how that can happen to 'anyone', huh? Now what are we going to broadcast?" Ultra Violet shrugged, and mumbled something about how he didn't know tape was supposed to be in those cameras anyway. Joy thought for a moment, and then snapped his fingers. "I know Geoff! Just air the script!" Geoff looked up in confusion. "The script? I... you think that would work?" Joy nodded. "Sure it will! I'm sure everyone has always wanted to see what the script looks like for fWo wrestling shows anyway, and I'm sure nobody will mind after so many great cards that you've produced." With that Joy stared directly into the camera and looked right at the audence. "That's right, nobody will mind." Geoff seemed to brighten, and scurried away to find the script so he could get it aired. Ultra Violet looked over at Joy with some confusion. "Who were you speaking to just now Joy?" Joy looked back into the camera, staring directly into the eyes of the viewers. "It's not important Ultra Violet, just a bunch of people who are going to hit refresh in a moment after reading this to make sure they have all of the card. Now c'mon... let's get you that Ice Cream you wanted." Joy and Ultra Violet left the arena as the cameras went dark, and the show script offically was run.
