Wednesday Warfare, 5 April 2000

5 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The Warfare Girls grinned and giggled happily as they were shoved off the bus in front of the backstage entrance to the arena. Four of the five Warfare Girls were there, although due to recent mishaps with the youthful and energetic dance group. Nip led the four, who were rounded out by Chesty, Laigh and Hummer. This was Laigh's first wrestling show, and the air of danger and intensity perversely made Laigh's nipples harden, like that Magic Shell stuff on a bowl of ice cream. From inside the waiting area came a sudden shout of excitement, and Kid Cool ran toward them. He tripped over a pebble that was laying on the asphalt and came down hard on his face. He picked himself up and walked forward more closely, looking from side to side for his favorite Warfare Girl. Recognizing Nip, he smiled happily. "Hey ladies! Any of you might know where the beautiful and heavenly Nitro Girl Nightshade is?" Nip looked mindlessly at Kid Cool. Her blazing eyes danced like Astaire and Rogers, but since they were crossed, it was an ocular tango, and his eyes had to foxtrot just to maintain eye contact. Nip finally realized that Kid Cool was expecting something from here and smiled nervously. "Okay, if you say so! Tee hee!" Kid Cool scratched his head. Something wasn't working properly in this conversation. Inside the arena loud fireworks exploded in an earsplitting burst, much like John Goodman's butt after a chili cookoff. Warfare had begun. Mike Bear vs. Havok "When Worlds Collide" starts up, and the crowd cheers as Havok makes his way to the ring and vaults over the top and in. The cheers intensify as "Dragula" starts up and Mike Bear makes an appearance, heading to the ring as his world-famous entrance video plays on the WarfareScreen. Bear rolls into the ring, and Bear and Havok shake hands as the ref rings the bell, remembering their recent teaming. They then circle, and lock up, with Havok gaining the advantage with a hammerlock, before taking Bear down with a leg trip. As Bear gets up, Havok strikes him full-on in the face with a dropkick, and Bear staggers back into the ropes. Havok quickly heads over to him and whips him across, before leaping up onto the shoulders of Bear for a hurricanrana as Bear comes off. Bear catches him in mid-move, and drops down with a Liger bomb. The ref counts, but Havok kicks out at two. Bear brings Havok up again, and begins a whip into the ropes, before pulling Havok back towards him with a short whip and lifting him onto his shoulders for a death valley driver. Havok slips free, and hooks Bear in a waistlock from behind, before taking him over with a German suplex. Havok bridges up, but Bear kicks out at two. Bear is immediately up and swings at Havok with a lariat, but Havok ducks and hooks Bear again from behind, before taking him down with a back suplex that drops Bear right on the back of his head. Havok is quickly up and drops a leg, before covering Bear. Bear kicks out at one, and Havok brings him back to his feet again. Havok whips Bear into the ropes, but Bear reverses it. However, Bear makes the fatal mistake of putting his head down, and Havok hooks him up before driving him into the mat with a devastating corkscrew piledriver. Havok immediately covers Bear and hooks the leg, but Bear somehow escapes after a two-count. Havok again brings Bear back to his feet, before whipping him into the ropes. Havok then catches him coming off with a leaping leg lariat that takes Bear down. Havok begins to climb to the top rope, looking for his original finisher the Millennium-Sault, but as he leaps off Bear moves and Havok plants himself into the mat. Bear quickly gets up and brings Havok up as well, before setting him up for one of his past finishers, the Drop To Hell. Bear lifts Havok up, but Havok flips over and lands on his feet. Havok hooks Bear, looking for his inverted brainbuster, dubbed the Headache, but Bear also flips over and lands on his feet. He grabs the head of Havok and drives him down into the mat with a bulldog, and Havok's face smashes directly into the mat. Bear brings Havok up and hooks him up, before driving him into the mat with a tombstone piledriver. Bear hooks the leg of Havok, but Havok manages to kick out instants before the ref's hand hits the mat the third time. Bear once more brings Havok up, and attempts a whip into the ropes, which is reversed by Havok. Bear ducks the attempted short clothesline from Havok, and as Havok turns, Bear pastes him in the face with a stiff superkick that jacks his jaw. Havok crumples to the mat, and Bear quickly covers. However, the referee is not there to make a count, and Bear sees that this is because he is on the apron, arguing with Wretch who has made his way in from the crowd to stop the pin. Bear heads over to the apron and shoves the ref out of the way, before clocking Wretch directly in the nose with another superkick. Wretch flies off the apron and strikes the steel ringpost, knocking himself down and nearly out. Bear turns back to Havok and hooks his leg, but Havok has had time to recover and hooks the head of Bear before rolling him over into a small package. Bear has nowhere to go, and the referee tolls the three-count, giving the win to Havok with an assist from Wretch. Both men are quickly up, and Bear stares down Havok for a moment, somewhat upset with him for taking the tainted win, but after a moment they shake once more, each more concerned with their respective enemies than each other, as Warfare continues... Result: Havok by Pinfall Harsh words Backstage Geoff Green spotted the Dark Ninja and shrugged. It wasn't the leading story, but several fans had asked about it so Green felt obligated to learn what he could. Green walked up to the Ninja and tapped him on the shoulder. "Ninja? Yes, Geoff Green here. Last week on Sunday Slaughter we all heard the comments that your former partner and student had to make. How do you respond to Tom, and what do you have to say about the Tom-Kwan-Do style of fighting?" The Ninja regarded Geoff and then rolled his eyes. "Tom-Kwan-Do? Please. I have no idea what nonsense Tom thinks he's gotten into but I'll say it once and I'll say it a million times... Tom is an inferior wrestler and a bad student. As far as I'm concerned he can take that Tom-Kwan-Do and shove it up his ass because when I get hold of him he's going to wish he was never born." Geoff gulped as Warfare went to a commercial break... Jackhammer vs. Cactus Jon vs. Ric Chronos vs. Black Quicksilver The bright lights inside of the Delta Center in Salt Lake City, Utah shined as "Bombtrack" by Rage Against the Machine played. Out walked lately disgruntled fWo legend, Cactus Jon. Jon stepped into the ring, and awaited his opponents. "Time" by Anthrax played, and Ric Chronos walked to the ring, with Fallen following closely behind. Chronos steps in, as he orders Fallen to the outside. Then suddenly, a burst of fireworks comes about, followed by Twisted Sister's "We're Not Gonna Take It", bringing out the Black Quicksilver, walking side by side with Mike Heftel. BQ slides into the ring, and begins brawling with Chronos, as Jon joins in. The four men brawl as "War?" by System of a Down plays, bringing out Jackhammer, accompanied by Sphere, his fellow tag team champion. Jackhammer enters the fray, and all four men brawl. As they do, Hellrazor & Fear make their way out onto the stage to scout the competition. Eventually, the men pair off, and Chronos begins fighting with Jackhammer, as BQ goes at it with Jon. Over in the ring corner, Jon is choking a fallen BQ with his boot. Jon picks him up, and whips him into the corner. A few back elbows follow, and Jon perches BQ on the top turnbuckle. Jon takes the Quicksilver over with a suplex, and Jackhammer breaks away from the cover to jump on top of his friend with a cover. Chronos picks Jackhammer up off of BQ by the head, and whips him to the outside. Jackhammer comes crashing down, as BQ whips Jon into the ropes. Jon comes back, and BQ catches him with an amazing tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Jon kicks out of a quick cover at two. Meanwhile, outside of the ring, Chronos comes at Jackhammer with a chair, but Sphere grabs the chair from Chronos. Chronos turns around, and pushes Sphere to the ground. Chronos stands over Sphere, taunting him, when suddenly out of nowhere, BQ comes off with a plancha, onto Chronos, as Jon lays on the mat, after taking a moonsault when the whole ordeal outside of the ring. BQ pounds Chronos with punches, as Sphere runs off. Jackhammer notices a little opening, so he takes advantage of the fallen Jon, by sliding into the ring with a cover. However, Jon had a little too much time to recover, and he kicks out at two. On the outside, Chronos takes the advantage, and nails a powerslam on the concrete, before sending BQ into the guardrail. In the ring, Jackhammer sends Jon into the ropes. Jon ducks a clothesline, and comes back with a dropkick. Jon follows up with a quick snap suplex. He covers, but Chronos slides into the ring, and nails a boot to the back of Jon's head to break up the count. Chronos lifts Jon up into the air, and hits a powerbomb. Meanwhile, BQ, who has gotten up, climbs the turnbuckles. Chronos goes to a cover, but BQ jumps off with a guillotine legdrop to the back of the head of Chronos to break up the count at two. BQ now jumps on Chronos with a cover, but Jackhammer gets up and breaks up that cover. Jackhammer and BQ brawl, as Fallen goes to slide a chair to Chronos. He is unsuccessful in getting the chair to him, as Sphere and Heftel go over to brawl with him. The three way brawl erupts, but meanwhile, Jon whips Chronos into the ropes and catches him with a boot. As he turns around, Jon is met with a crushing clothesline from Jackhammer. BQ gets up, and climbs the turnbuckles. He nails Chronos with a flying headbutt, but when he makes the cover, Chronos kicks out at two. In the other side of the ring, Jackhammer plants Cactus Jon with a chokeslam. At the same time, BQ comes off of the turnbuckles looking for the Silver Splash, but he misses. Jackhammer is about to go for the cover, but he sees Fallen, who has broken away from the brawl on the outside, get up on the ring apron. They jawjack, but meanwhile, Chronos picks up the fallen Jon, and plants him with the Chronosphere. Jackhammer nails Fallen, but it's too late, as Chronos already picked up the three count for the win. Result: Ric Chronos by Pinfall Team of the world Backstage Matt Loco caught up with Black Quicksilver and Mike Heftel, the Silver Slowback as they were heading to the locker room after Black Quicksilver's match. "Hey... you guys! My two close personal friends! Have I shown you my photos of Jenna Jameson and me hanging out at my huge beach house in Malibu? Anyway... listen... you two have a tag team match coming up on Sunday Slaughter. How are you guys going to take on the massive hardcore team in Fear and Hellrazor?" Black Quicksilver stopped and looked down on the reporter like a webhead looking down on an AOL user. "Listen Loco... first off keep your dirty little photos to yourself. Second, the two of us are just starting to really click as a team. Why, there is nothing that can stop our rise to the top of the fWo... nothing! I defy anyone to prove that we are going anywhere but to the top of the tag team ranks in the fWo. This Sunday it's the two Underground goofs. Next it's maybe time man and depressed lad. But one way or another, the two of us are here and here to stay." They walked off, leaving Loco standing alone and wondering who to bother next... Matt Douglas vs. Wretch Gob hits the speakers as the fWo cameras hit the entrance ramp. Boos also ring throughout the arena as the cocky Wretch comes out, minus Scott Hart. He looks at the crowd and points to his head, telling them that he's got something up there that they don't. He climbs in the ring and awaits his opponent in this match, which will determine that #1 contender to the International Title. "Higher" by Creed hits the speakers, and Matt Douglas makes his way out to a warm reaction. He makes his way down the ramp quickly, and slides into the ring. The two stare down one another, as the bell sounds. The two lockup, and Wretch locks Douglas into a headlock. Douglas reverses it, and Wretch reverses it once more. Douglas picks up Wretch and turns it into an atomic drop. Wretch bounces off of Douglas' knee and into the ropes. As Wretch comes back, Douglas catches him and lays in a hip toss. Wretch gets up quickly and gets hit with another hip toss. After a third hip toss, Wretch makes way for the outside. As he rethinks his strategy, Douglas garners some speed off the ropes and dives over the top rope and onto Wretch. With Wretch laid out on the ground, Douglas picks him up and tosses him back into the ring. He goes for a cover, but gets only a two. He picks up Wretch; however, as he is doing so, Wretch lays in a cheap shot to the midsection, bringing down the momentum of Douglas. Wretch, now gaining momentum, clubs Douglas in the back. Another hammerlock and Douglas is floored. Wretch lays in a quick leg drop to the back of the head of Douglas. He gets up and hits a small dropkick to the head. Wretch picks up Douglas and locks in an abdominal stretch, and then throws him into the turnbuckle. As he goes to splash him in the corner, Douglas moves, causing Wretch's head to go right into the top buckle. Douglas kicks Wretch in the gut and hits him with a suplex. Douglas, gaining some momentum, goes for a pinfall, but only gets two. As he gets up, Wretch, thinking quickly, grabs Douglas' trunks and rolls him up in a small package, but only gets a two. The two starts get up and start lacing chops into each other's chests. Douglas gets the best of Wretch and locks in a headlock once again. However, this time, he turns it into a bulldog. Wretch, groggy from having his bells being rung, tries to get up. Douglas helps him up and takes him up to the top rope. He positions Wretch and hits a hurricanrana. He is sensing victory, and goes up to the top for a senton bomb. However, at that time, Scott Hart comes down to ringside with a guitar at his side. He leaves the guitar on one side of the ring and climbs the apron on the other, getting the referee's attention. Douglas sees this and starts to yell at the ref. He climbs down off the top rope and walks over to the ref. He argues with Scott Hart and tries to get the ref back into the match. He finally gives up and goes back to finishing off his opponent. Only his opponent is up, and has a guitar in his hand. Wretch wastes no time in nailing Douglas with the guitar, laying him out. He clears the debris out of the ring and covers Douglas. Hart turns around the ref and makes him see the cover. A three count ends it for Wretch, giving him the victory. Hart climbs into the ring and they take a couple shots at Douglas before leaving, celebrating their victory. Douglas starts to come to as Warfare hits a commercial break. Result: Wretch by Pinfall Destruction Warfare continued inside the arena as the announcers went over what was coming up next on the show. However, their discussion was halted as on the TravisTron a video began to play. Mitch Wilson with a camera crew walked down the streets of downtown Salt Lake City, Utah. Wilson had just opened this brand new Wilson Training Academy several weeks ago, and hadn't yet gotten around to stopping by to see how things were running. Wilson and the crew entered the Academy, to find a receptionist sitting behind a desk... almost frozen to her chair. Behind her on the white wall read in blue & red spray paint "CyberSlam 2000 - I Quit Match"... Wilson screamed, before grabbing a chair and throwing it across the room. "What else did he do, dammit?" The receptionist jaw chattered, nearly unable to speak... "He... he... trashed the place... he beat up one of our newer trainees... and then he told me that when you got here... to make sure you saw this." She pointed up at the message written on the wall, before Wilson looked at the floor, contemplating of what to do or say. He screamed an obscenity that no doubt echoed throughout the Wasatch Front, and likely was heard just down the street by the religious folks at the Salt Lake City LDS Temple. Wilson didn't care if anyone heard him. Primetime had done an unspeakable act, and now he would have to pay the price. Wilson looked over at the camera.. "Brian, you think you can do something like this to me and get away with it? Not a chance. I accept your challenge... at CyberSlam 2000, you and I are going to get it on. And one way or the other, come hell or high water, without a shadow of a doubt... insert every other clich? you can, I will make you say it. I will make you scream it. Because I am not a quitter, and on April 29... I will make you scream "I Quit"... count on it, Brian. And tell Pierre he better find a new replacement for you in the Foundation, because after April 29, your career will be over." Wilson ordered the receptionist to get someone in here to "clean this place up", before he stalked out of the Wilson Training Academy with the camera crew in tail. Geoff Green asked Wilson where he was going, and Wilson only muttered out - "The Delta Center." The camera crew followed Wilson as he sped off... VL vs. Frank Snow "Whatever" by Godsmack begins to play and Frank Snow begins to head to the ring, dragging a cart of weapons behind him. As he nears the ring, Snow begins digging in the cart, producing a chair, a 2x4, and a bottle of Surge~!, all of which he throws into the ring before entering himself. "Shook Ones Part II" begins, and VL immediately rushes the ring, sliding in and taking it to Snow with a series of rights. She whips Snow into the ropes, and catches him coming off with a hiptoss, which he reverses. VL re-reverses it, and in the process hiptosses Snow up and over the top rope. Snow splats on the floor, and VL grabs the chair that Snow had tossed into the ring earlier and slides outside with it. As Snow gets up, VL cracks the steel over the top of his head. Snow crumples back down, and VL shoves the timekeeper away and grabs his seat. She approaches Snow with a chair in either hand, and as Snow makes his way to his feet again, VL swings both chairs, and sandwiches the head of Snow in between them. Snow falls to the floor, but VL shows no mercy and brings him up again. She picks up the larger Snow and drapes him over her shoulder, before charging forward and ramming him back-first into the ringpost. VL rolls Snow back into the ring, and covers him. Snow somehow manages to lift a shoulder, and VL brings him back up to his feet. She shoves Snow back-first into the corner, and picks up the 2x4 lying in the ring. VL rears back and takes an Alex Rodriguez cut, but Snow moves and she whiffs harder than a batter facing Pedro Martinez. The 2x4 strikes the top of the steel ringpost, and it shatters in half. VL turns to face snow wielding the half-board, but is met by a kick to the stomach and a quick sit-down gutwrench powerbomb by Snow. Snow grabs the 2x4 fragment that VL dropped on impact and turns the splintered, sharp end of it away from him, before dropping down and driving it straight into the forehead of VL, busting her open. Snow grinds the wood against the flesh, before hooking the leg and making a cover. VL kicks out at two. Snow brings VL up again and whips her into the ropes, but VL reverses it. As Snow comes off, she attempts to tilt-a-whirl him into position for an inverted piledriver, but the larger Snow shifts his weight in mid-move and flips into position for one of his own, and he hits it, driving VL into the mat. Snow quickly grabs the chair and begins to head up top, looking to hit the Snowfall and win his second Hardcore Title. As he climbs, Robert Jensen rushes the ring and rolls in, grabbing the Surge on the way in and uncapping it and swigging it down in one motion. He quickly turns to face Snow on the top rope and grimaces at the taste of the drink, involuntarily spitting it out and right into the eyes of Snow, who drops the chair to the outside. Seeing what he has done, Jensen shrugs it off as being as effective as anything and extracts a roll of barbed wire from the pocket of his jeans before laying it on the mat. He then climbs up to the top rope, hooks the head of Snow, and falls back, hitting a top-rope variation of the Make You Bleed. Jensen quickly rolls VL onto the body of Snow, and the referee tolls the three-count. VL retains her title, and Jensen heads to the back, snickering at his actions... Result: VL by Pinfall Kidnapped Warfare approached one of it's final commercial breaks, as Mike Bear' girlfriend Brittany arrived at the arena. "Can anybody tell me where Mike is?" Several backstage workers pointed her in the direction of where he could be located. "Thanks... I was at the Hilton nearby and I saw he lost tonight to Havok, and thought I'd come console him." Brittany walked off in search of her boyfriend, before suddenly Scott Hart appeared from the shadows. "Whoo-hoo-hoo! Today's your lucky day... you and me should meet at the pay-per-view and marry! What do you say?" Brittany quipped. "Screw you, asshole." Hart' smile turned to a frown. She slapped him. "Move out of my way, you jerk." From behind, Wretch grabbed her, and threw a blanket over her head. She struggled, even kicking Hart in the testicular area, but Wretch overpowered her. Hart complained loudly about the pain, but Wretch told him to shut up. They took Brittany away... making sure she could not scream for help. Ultra Violet vs. Eron "Physical" by Nine Inch Nails blared throughout the Delta Center, prompting the fans to boo loudly, as it sounded like one of those bands that eat chickens and preach devil worshipping, and they don't take kindly to people like that around here. Eron The Relentless stepped out underneath the huge Warfare screen, and the boos only became louder. Eron slid into the ring, taking off his trench coat as he reached a corner, before slouching in it and awaiting the arrival of his opponent of the night, the man he cost the chance of winning the fWo International Title this Sunday, Ultra-Violet. Ultra-Violet was not accompanied by Violet for one reason or another, and as his trademarked them of "Rage" shook the Delta Center, he walked down the ramp smiling and cheery. As he rolled into the ring, Eron met him and took him down with a quick DDT, before going for a cover. UV managed to struggle out at two, before Eron continued his attack, not letting UV recover for a moment. Eron whipped UV into the corners, before charging in with an elbow to the head, and after UV staggered out to the middle of the ring, Eron took him down to the canvas once again with a side Russian Legsweep. Another cover and another kick out, and Eron seemed undaunted. Deciding he wasn't going to waste any more time trying to pin UV, he decided to go for his finisher early. He locked it in, and UV writhed in pain and cried for help, but instead of help... the lights went out. As lunatic fans at ringside shouted "ARMS!" and "Hellfire and Brimstone!"; red, blue and white lights flickered on and off throughout the arena. "Juke Joint Jezebel" began to play, and out from underneath the TravisTron stepped the evil and diabolical Flying Frenchie. He had a sarcastic expression painted on his face, and despite the fact that the men inside the ring hated him immensely, Eron kept the hold locked in as Frenchie began to speak. "Eron? You wish to have a match wit' ze Frenchie? Consider it done, putain. Zis Sunday at Slaughter, I will end your career in ze fWo and zat will bring much delight to everyone. Go ahead and release ze hold, zis is an A and B conversation between I and ze evil Purple One zere, so you can see your way out of it." Eron did release the hold, happy that he would be facing Frenchie at Slaughter... he exited the ring up through the crowd, and the fans jeered him some more... Frenchie turned their attention back to him, however. "I should have known zat only a moron like yourself would 'ave been able to screw something so simple up. But, being ze intelligent and well endowed man zat I am, I had an ace up my sleeve. I had a back up plan, so to speak. And... if you look behind me on ze TravisTron, you will finally see what I have been tryin' to show you for ze last mont' or so. Take a look, Purple One." Ultra-Violet shrugged, and decided to go ahead and watch. Frenchie looked at him suspiciously, hoping his attention span would be long enough to view what he had to show him. The lights stayed in the dim phase as Frenchie pointed to the TravisTron. A video began to play, and it was certainly not the infamous tape of Pamela and Tommy Lee from a few years back. It was... "The Kiss". Ultra-Violet watched the events unfoled. He watched as Violet answered the door, frowning at who she saw. Ultra-Violet frowned too... who wouldn't? It was the Frenchie... and he was grinning. Ultra-Violet listened to the video, as the Frenchie spoke. "Ah, ze Violet... how pretty you are... I trust zat you received ze gifts zat I sent to you?" UV' mouth fell open, as he continued to watch the video. He watched his future wife gasp, and point at the evil Frenchie. He listened as she spoke in the video. "You? You're the one that sent me the candy and flowers?" The Frenchie nodded, and took a step forward. "Oui. And now my mon cher Violet... ze Frenchie claims ze prize." The Flying Frenchie moved closer, grasped Violet around the waist, and delivered a firm kiss directly to her lips. The video continued to play, as Violet struggled for a moment, and then lost herself in the moment, responding by putting her arms around the Frenchie as he bent her over to hammer home the embrace. Suddenly, Violet remembered who she was and who she was engaged to, and began to struggle once more. The video deceased playing, and Ultra-Violet seemed really, really angry. He stomped his foot in the ring, looking at Frenchie with a "I'll get you for this." look from ear to ear. Ultra-Violet was quickly given a microphone, and he spoke. "Hey, you big meanie! I'm going to teach you that it's not nice to go after other people's significant other! Why don't you bring your evil butt down here, so I can open up a can of purple paint on ya, and walk it dry!" The Frenchie grinned, knowing that he had the Evil Purple One right where he wanted him. Frenchie walked down the ramp just a bit, for dramatic effect. "Non! Non, I will have none of zat. Tonight doesn't work for ze Frenchie, I'm afraid. I 'ave more important t'ings to do, like watching a tape of my greatest matches, or showing o'ther women what it is like to spend a night wit' ze Frenchie. However, if you truly want to face ze Frenchie, zen you will accept my challenge. How about you and I... at CyberSlam... in Seattle." Ultra-Violet interrupted him... "Where?" Frenchie chuckled. "Seattle... perhaps you've heard of it. Then again, I guess I shouldn't have given you zis much credit. Only, if you want to settle zis matter so badly, then surely you will accept ze following. CyberSlam... you and ze Frenchie. Zis will be no ordinary match, non, ze Frenchie will have none of zat. Ze Frenchie wants zis t'ing done his way, ze Frenchie way. Zat purple hair of yours has haunted ze Frenchie far too much, I cannot let it continue. So, you put up zat prized hair of yours... and I put up ze one t'ing zat gives a little mystery to ze aura of ze Frenchie. My mask. Hair versus mask... at CyberSlam. Ei'ter you walk away wit' a bald head, or I walk away without my precious mask." Ultra-Violet pondered things, before Ultra-Violet accepted. "Yes, you big meanie! I accept your challenge... hot-damn-diggity-dog-and-a-half! At CyberSlam I'm going to pin you 1, Q, C or my name isn't Ultra-Violet! Whoo-Yeah!" Frenchie balked at this, grinning evily, his plan working like a well-oiled machine thus far... Warfare headed for a commercial break, with another match now set for CS2K. Result: No Contest Signing The fans in the arena divert their attention to the Travis-tron, which brings up an image with the heading "fWo presents...". The screen fades out, then fades in with a close-up shot of a document, which is indeed a contract. The camera's view quickly skims through some contractual obligations, length of time, etc. The fans in the Delta Center seem to be quite confused, but immediately grow into an ocean of gossip when the see the camera scan over the word "fWo" and the name "Cody 'Crusader' Williams." A close-up face-shot of Crusader himself, who reveals a short grin on his face. He diverts his attention down to the document, and the camera follows his attention. The shot now being viewed is a Mont-Blanc wielding hand, which signs the ever-so-famous "dotted-line." The pen is put down, and the hand is moved, which shows a hard-to-read, yet professional-looking signature. Cheers start to echo slightly amongst the crowd, as the Travis-tron goes black. Moments later, the first-time beats of Biohazard's "Switchback" is heard throughout the arena, and no one else but Crusader appears at the top of the ramp, wearing black shoes, blue jeans, and a leather coat, zipped-up. Crusader zips his coat down a bit, and pulls out the document that he had just signed. After looking around at the thousands of fans in anticipation, Crusader fully unzips his coat fully, then peels it off of himself, revealing a t-shirt with the well-known 'fWo' logo printed on it. Gossip, chatter, and whispering amongst the crowd ends, as they finally realize what these moments have lived up to, and give a strong support of cheers for Crusader. With his right hand wielding the contract, and his coat in his left hand, Crusader lets his head fall back a bit, as an incredible display of fireworks go off. Crusader brings his head down, and begins walking down the fWo ramp for the first time in his life. It is indeed a long walk, as Crusader looks around at the different fans, supporting their respective favorite wrestlers. His coat is slung over his shoulder, and he hangs it upon the top rope of the fWo ring. In what is yet another great moment for Crusader, he mounts the stairs, and enters the fWo ring. The fans in attendance are continuing their appreciation of Crusader, as he looks around yet again at the fans, from the dedicated, front-row fans, to the less-fortunate ones at the top deck. Crusader bears a look of approval on his face, then pulls a microphone out of his back pocket. "There's nothing else I'd like to start with..." Crusader begins, as the fans are settling down a bit. "... than to say: It's great to finally be here." The fans are already taken with Crusader's respect of the Fans Wrestling Organization. "This document right here does NOT represent the empty promises, lies and unfair treatment that exists too much in the world of professional wrestling. Yeah, I've had a lot of those. But this right here is about *opportunity*." At this point, Crusader begins pacing a bit. "People dream. People fantasize. I do both. However, another thing I do, is I *live*. Living your dreams and fantasies isn't impossible, just ask anybody who has made it this far. So all I ask for now, is a small welcoming." The fans applaud Crusader's comments once more, as he looks around at the crowd, and finally kneels down a bit to place the microphone on the floor. He exits the ring, taking his leather coat off from the rope, and heading back up the ramp way. He is breath taken, and lets out a sigh of relief before disappearing behind the curtains. Dead & Hiroshima vs. Erik Kelly & The Boston Strangler The fans rose to a vertical base as "Beautiful People" began to echo throughout the ten-year old Delta Center, and the familiar red strobe light flickered on and off. Two figures emerged from backstage onto the stage underneath the TravisTron, and they were obviously representatives from the FTfWo. Dead & Hiroshima looked around the packed crowd of 19,911 as the fans booed them generously, but the two men did not care how they felt about them, because it was damn sure the feeling was mutual. The two men stepped into the ring, and raised their arms... as they did, "O' Fortuna" began to play, and out stepped The Boston Strangler. Strangler stepped out onto the ramp, but he was alone. He looked back underneath the giant structure behind him however, and out walked all six feet and eleven inches of eradication. Erik Kelly was accompanied by his valet Pamela Dee, and the trio walked to the ring as Dead removed the trench coat from his body and Hiroshima stretched out in a corner. Strangler and Kelly entered the ring, before discussing who would enter the ring first. Dee watched from their corner, as Dead & Hiroshima conversed in their corner as well at who would be in combat in the beginning of the match here tonight. With those out of the way, Strangler decided he would open the match... and it appeared Dead was going to as well, before he decided not to for some reason. Instead, it was Hiroshima who would kick things off... Hiroshima and Strangler met in the center of the ring, and they exchanged pleasantries. A deadly game of trash talk unfolded, before Dead began to sneak into the ring... the referee saw this however, and cut him off, pushing him back to the corner. With Strangler looking over Hiroshima at Dead, who was behind Hiroshima, Hiro quickly pulled some powder out from his trunks, and whisked it into the eyes of the Strangler. Catching him completely off guard, Hiro smiled at the booing fans, as the referee returned to action. Strangler bent over, clutching his eyes, and Hiroshima came up from behind with a double axe handle directly into the spine of the Strangler. Hiroshima continued the assault, taking the Strangler in a headlock before hitting a running bulldog. He then brought Strangler up and unloaded with a bevy of lefts and rights, and the Strangler still did not have his vision restored. Hiroshima whipped Strangler to the ropes, before catching him and hotshotting him onto the top rope. He went for a cover, but Strangler kicked out at two. Hiroshima brought the Strangler up by the hair, before tagging out to Dead, who came in looking to take out the man who would be competing against him for (what he felt was) the FTfWo World Title at CyberSlam. As Strangler struggled to his feet and continued to rub his eyes, Dead snuck up from behind with a chopblock to one of his legs. He then methodically took over, looking to systematically take apart the Strangler piece by piece. He targeted the left leg of the Strangler, first measuring him, then dropping repeated elbows. He then dropped a knee into it, inflicting as much damage as he could upon his most hated rival. Dead brought Strangler up by the hair, before whipping him out onto the floor. Out there, he found a knee and drove it into his leg, before whipping him towards the ringsteps... Strangler however reversed it, and instead Dead smacked the steps hard. Strangler rolled into the ring, his vision now restored, before he began a crawllike journey to the corner so he could tag out to his partner for the evening, Erik Kelly. Strangler was nearing the corner, when Dead seemingly recovered from the blow he was dealt with, and rolled underneath the bottom rope. He tagged out to Hiroshima, before Dead dove and grabbed the leg of the Strangler as he reached the corner, and Hiroshima came in and dropped a big legdrop into the back of the leg of the madman from Boston. Hiroshima went to work, picking up where Dead left off. Hiroshima pulled Strangler into the center of the ring, where he continued to drive his leg into the mat repeatedly... knee first time in and time out, Hiroshima continued... however, he paused and looked at the crowd for a moment, and basked in their boos. This only allowed the Strangler to escape, and he turned forward and was now facing Hiroshima, and as Hiroshima bent over to grab his other leg, Strangler put his foot into the chest of Hiroshima, before pushing him backwards into his corner. Hiroshima hit back first into the turnbuckles, before he fell down onto the mat, his head hitting into the boot of the Strangler. Strangler turned over onto his stomach, and began crawling over to the corner, before he made the hot tag to Kelly. Hiroshima recovered, and was back in the game, he charged at Kelly, who grabbed him by the throat and held him into the air with both hands, before tossing him to the mat like so. Dead entered, and Kelly nailed him with a few punches. Hiroshima recovered, and he walked over dazed towards Kelly, who took both men by the head and slammed them together. Dead spun over the top rope onto the cement floor, and Strangler limped to his feet. Kelly whipped Hiroshima into the corner, and as he walked out he was met with a foot to the face, before a Kelly chokeslam. Kelly tossed Hiroshima in the direction of the Strangler, who took him down with, before he and Strangler tossed Hiroshima over the top rope onto the cement floor. However, from behind came Dead with a steel chair as the referee looked out of the ring at Hiroshima, seeing if he was okay. Dead bashed a chair into the leg of the Strangler from behind, and as Kelly turned around he was met with a steel chair to the head. Dead then tossed the chair to the mat, and picked Strangler up, before going for the Crucifixion, his patented sit-down tombstone piledriver... this time, on a chair. He hit it, and he had Strangler pinned, but the referee was still outside the ring checking on Hiroshima. He called for the referee's attention, and the referee slid back into the ring. He counted as Dead covered the Boston Strangler, but at the count of two, a loud "powerbomb!" shout echoed throughout the arena, which scared the hell out of the referee so much he stopped the count. Dead stood up and saw Kelly standing behind him, and Dead moved towards him but Kelly kicked him in the stomach with the thrust reminiscent of a mule kick from an actual mule, before grabbing him and going to powerbomb him. Strangler moved off the chair, and Kelly power bombed Dead onto it, completely eradicating the World Champion. Kelly covered him, and the referee counted the pin. Three seconds later it was all over, and Kelly helped Strangler to his feet, but he still had a severe limp. Kelly and Strangler had a slight embrace, as Kelly was helping him stay on his feet... Dee entered the ring and raised both their arms, as Warfare came to an end, with the good guys winning... Result: Erik Kelly & The Boston Strangler by Pinfall
