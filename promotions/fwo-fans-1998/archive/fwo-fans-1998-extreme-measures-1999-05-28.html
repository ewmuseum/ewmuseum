fWo — Pay-per-view

Extreme Measures 1999, 28 May 1999

28 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction As the show began to air, the fWo roster was escorted onto the entrance ramp for a ten bell salute to recent fWo star Owen Hart. It was a somber way to start off the event, but a touching tribute to the great wrestler. As this tribute ended, the stars returned to their dressing rooms, and a slightly shaken Chris Finn joined James Armstrong at the commentary desk. Al Snow (c) vs. Stevie Roberts Roberts came to the ring without Doomsday, telling his group that he wouldn't have need of their services to rid the fWo of the crazy moron called Al Snow. Nodding at this, Doomsday left the area in search of Nikki the Valley Girl, as Al Snow ran to the ring. Both men locked up quickly, with Al Snow catching both of Roberts arms and repeatedly butting him in the head. Roberts rebounded with a shot to the groin, and a quick drop toe hold. Roberts held the leg as he repeatedly droped the knee into the midsection of Snow. Roberts then grabbed the head of Snow, and flipped backward and over Snow in a laying neckbreaker. Unable to get the three count, Roberts went to the outside to look for a weapon. As Roberts searched for something to attack Al Snow with, the Falcon emerged from the crowd to shove Roberts into the steel stairs, and then piledrive him on the outside. The Falcon then rolled Roberts back into the ring, and left for the backstage area. With Roberts out cold, Al Snow easily got the pinfall victory. Result: Al Snow by Pinfall Black Quicksilver vs. D Lo Brown Black Quicksilver came to the ring with Stump, and got a strong reaction from the crowd as he lifted it up for them. Black Quicksilver then asked the crowd if they would rather have Stump, or rather have Head. The crowd seemed uncertain, and D-Lo Brown ran to the ring before their decision could be made. D-Lo dove into the ring, but right into Black Quicksilver's hands, as he proceeded to stomp down upon D-Lo. D-Lo finally caught one of the legs of Black Quicksilver and flipped him down to the mat. D-Lo dropped a leg, and then climbed to the top rope. Black Quicksilver showed signs of life, so D-Lo exited the turnbuckle and returned to the center of the ring to deliver an uppercut. D-Lo then returned to the turnbuckle and delivered a frog splash. Unable to get the three count, D-Lo returned to the top turnbuckle, but Black Quicksilver quickly rose to his feet and tossed D-Lo off the turnbuckle and down to the mat. Black Quicksilver did not go for the cover at once, and D-Lo reversed the cover when it was finally applied. D-Lo achieved the three count, and an angry Black Quicksilver argued with the ref, and then kicked D-Lo out of the ring. Going under the ring, Black Quicksilver produced the European Title that he stole from D-Lo many months ago, and shouted that it would remain his, and he would be defending it from now on. Result: D Lo Brown by Pinfall Mad Trucker w/ Darkness vs. Mad Trucker w/Cookie & Alice The Mad Trucker came to the ring with Darkness, laughing and spitting at the fans. Both in the ring, the Mad Trucker asserted that he was the real Mad Trucker, and that Cookie and Alice would be unable to produce a Mad Trucker. With that the lights went out, and a loud "TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT" was heard. A pale purple light shone out over the arena, and Cookie and Alice walked out onto the entrance ramp, with Cookie weilding a large hubcap, that was used to reflect the light into the crowd. The crowd then erupted as the truck of the Mad Trucker rolled out onto the entrance ramp, with the Mad Trucker the fans know and love behind the wheel. The Mad Trucker then stood on top of the truck, and saluted the crowd with a loud "TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" The Trucker then ran to the ring, ducked a lariat, and delivered a hard kick to the midsection of the Evil Trucker. The Good and Pure Trucker then picked up the Evil Trucker, and delivered several Eighteen Wheelers, finally pinning him after he was severely pummled. Darkness began to enter the ring, but the US Marshal ran to ringside, and prevented Darkness from entering the ring. As the US Marshal cleared the ringside area, the Mad Trucker grabbed the microphone, and delivered another "TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" to the crowd. The Trucker then thanked Cookie and Alice, and revealed to the crowd that the Evil Trucker was actually Evil Roy Slade, escaped from the pen. The Mad Trucker revealed that Darkness was behind his abduction, and that he would get his revenge at the next fWo House Show, as he would wrestle Darkness in a barb wire match, with Cactus Jack as the guest referee. Result: Mad Trucker by Pinfall Scott Slugger & Ice vs. Austin Davis & Chris Universal Scott Slugger and Ice came to the ring as a strong unit, as it was announced that the winner of the match would wrestle the fWo Tag Team Champions at the next house show. Austin Davis came to the ring next, by himself, and began to attack Scott Slugger and Ice. The bell rang, as Chris Universal made his way to the ring slowly, not caring about how fast he got to the ring. Slugger and Ice both attacked Davis in tandem, as Davis struggled to rebound. Davis attempted to tag out several times, but Universal continued to dodge the tag. Davis and Ice rolled to the outside, with the French announcing table taking the brunt of their fury. Back inside the ring Chris Universal attacked with a cheap shot, but a returning Mick Foley dove into the ring to prevent Universal from capitalizing on his cheap shot. Foley delivered a shot to the head of Universal, and Scott Slugger quickly took advantage with a cover. The ref counted to three, as Mick Foley told the cameras that he would face Chris Universal at Numbered Days... the next fWo Pay-Per-View. Austin Davis looked at Universal with disgust, and walked back to the locker rooms as Scott Slugger and Ice celebrated. Result: Scott Slugger & Ice by Pinfall Oracle vs. Eron the Relentless Oracle and Eron began their fight on the entrance ramp, before either had been introduced. The two brawled around the ringside area, with Oracle tossing a steel chair at Eron. Eron caught it before it struck him in the head, but Oracle delivered a quick running drop kick into the steel chair, and into Eron's face. Bleeding badly, Eron was shoved into the ring by Oracle. Oracle then tossed in a steel chair, and strangely the ref in the match seemed not to care. Eron was picked up by Oracle, and dropped on the steel chair repeatedly by Oracle. Oracle did not go for the pinfall, and continued to wreck havok on Eron. When Eron protested to the ref in the match, the ref spit on him and turned his back on the proceedings. Oracle waved to the back, inviting other fWo stars to come out and attack Eron, but while his attention was diverted Eron grabbed Oracle and rolled him backward in a tight cover. Frustrated, the ref counted to three and Eron quickly escaped from the ring as Oracle and other fWo stars chased after him. Eron delivered the finger to the men chasing him, and laughed as he ran, much to the delight of the crowds. Result: Eron the Relentless by Pinfall Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Falcon The Falcon came to the ring gloating over his involvement in the Cruiserweight Title match earlier in the night, and awaited the Frenchie inside the ring. The Frenchie came out to a lavish entrance, with several members of the French Legion waving the French flag on the entrance ramp. The Frenchie held his International Title high above his head for the crowd, and strode smugly to the ring. Inside the ring the Falcon and the Frenchie locked up, with the Frenchie getting the advantage after a kick to the groin. The Frenchie delivered a back suplex, but the Falcon scrambled away and exited the ring, looking to regroup. The Falcon entered the ring on the other side, and ducked a clothesline. He delivered a quick back body drop, and then followed it up with a leg drop. The Falcon went for a cover, but the Frence kicked out hard, sending the Falcon off of him, and to the outside. As the Falcon got on his feet and got ready to climb back into the ring, hands from under the ring grabbed the ankles of the Falcon and brought him down to the arena floor. Stevie Roberts emerged from under the ring and began to pummel the Falcon, causing him to be counted out. The Frenchie looked to see what had caused the count out, and then shrugged and left the ring, belt held high. As Roberts continued his attack, Chris Kidman leapt from the crowd and pulled Roberts off the Falcon, suplexing him into the rubble of the French announcing table. Kidman and the Falcon then left to go to the backstage area. Result: Flying Frenchie by Count Out French Foundation (c) vs. Ground Zero Ground Zero came to the ring angry, with Jackhammer and Wolf Fang both carrying baseball bats. The Foundation came to the ring carefully, looking warily at Ground Zero and the bats. When the ref took the bats away from Jackhammer and Wolf Fang, Primetime and Mitch Wilson slid into the ring and attacked Ground Zero with their tag titles. As the match started the French Foundation double teamed Wolf Fang, leading the ringside announcers to speculate that he could use an energy burst of fresh SURGE. A bottle of SURGE was passed into Wolf Fang, who drank it and at once was energized, and began attacking both members of the French Foundation, sometimes with his fists, and sometimes with bottles of refreshing SURGE. Wolf Fang cleared the ring, and Primetime and Mitch Wilson determined that discretion the better part than valor, and left the ringside area, allowing themselves to be counted out. Upset at the faliure of getting the tag team titles, Ground Zero used the bottles of SURGE to attack the ref and ringside crew, before tossing bottles of SURGE to happy fans. Result: French Foundation by Count Out Death (c) vs. Ultra Violet Ultra Violet walked to the ring with some confidance, seemingly aware of what was going to occur that night. Death walked slowly to the ring, staring down his old partner. Inside the ring, Death extended a hand to Ultra Violet, who looked around uncertainly. Finally, trusting soul that he is, Ultra Violet met the hand, but was greeted with a cheap shot by Death. The larger man continued to dominate the smaller challenger, who rebounded from every attack, unwilling to give up to pinfall or submission attempts. Both men made their way to the outside, where James Armstrong was felled by a wicked shot, leaving Chris Finn alone at the announcing table. Back inside the ring, Death attacked with several hard shots, finally leaving himself open to an attack by Ultra Violet. Death fell to the outside at one point, and Violet, angry over the recent attacks upon Ultra Violet, attacked Death with fury. Death escaped the wraith, but was off his game, allowing Ultra Violet to take the upper hand. Ultra Violet applied a head lock, but Death was able to power out. With neither man taking a firm advantage, both men lay still on the canvas, prompting the ref to call for a ten count. Ultra Violet and Death both made it up, and Ultra Violet capitalized first, scoring the pinfall victory over his one-time friend and now biter rival. Exhausted, Ultra Violet seemed uncertain of his victory, and could only celebrate with Violet in the center of the ring as Death slunk away into the shadows. Result: Ultra Violet by Pinfall Wedding of John Skyler and Dina Sol From that scene fWo camera crews took the viewers to a small chapel on the opposite end of town. Dina Sol and John Skyler were wed in front of a large crowd, who appauded the new couple as they were pronounced man and wife. The bouquet was thrown after the ceremony, with Sid Snow being the lucky "bride-to-be". The couple stood at the reception greeting guests, as various friends, family, and associates of the fWo. Various fWo wrestlers shook hands with the two, but Machina slipped Dina a small package amist the hand shaking. The cameras faded to black, as Extreme Measures came to a close...
