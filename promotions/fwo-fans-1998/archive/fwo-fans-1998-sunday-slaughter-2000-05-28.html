fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 28 May 2000

28 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Chris Finn entered the Providence Civic Center with a confident look on his face. As he reached his office, James Armstrong was sitting in a chair with a frown on his face, and papers firmly grasped in his hand. Finn sighed. "What's the matter, James?" The expression on Armstrong' face remained in a frown. "Well... ratings for Warfare came in, and it doesn't look good." Finn' face went blank. "Well... that could be for a number of reasons. We were on at a different timeslot again, right? And... we... had... we were... was basketball on? No? Uh... hey! Dawson's Creek was on, and EVERYONE loves that show. They had the most romantic same-sex kiss in history, didn't they? Wasn't it their season finale? Uh... Wednesday... uh... what else is on Wednesday? HEY! Spin City was on... and they... er... who's in that show again?" Armstrong rolled his eyes. Finn scrambled for more reasons. "Well... I figure it's due to... aw crap. Blame it on the old regime... it'll likely be awhile before we bring the ratings back up because of that shmuck." Armstrong nodded, before we went to the ring for the first match of the night. Ric Chronos vs. Hellrazor Slaughter explodes inside the sold out Providence Civic Center to the sounds of "From This Day" by Machinehead, signaling the arrival of one half of the team that would be challenging the fWo tag team champions at Critical Condition, Hellrazor. With Fear close at his side, the former fWo Hardcore Champion steps into the ring, and awaits the arrival of his opponent, one half of the tag team champions, Ric Chronos. "Christian Says" plays and Ric Chronos, with Fallen at his side strut to the ring, with the fWo tag team titles secured around their waists. They continue to the ring, and Fallen takes his place directly across of Fear on the outside. The bell rings, and the match is underway. Lockup, and Chronos with a quick whip into the ropes. Hellrazor ducks a clothesline, and comes back with a dropkick of his own. A quick cover is kicked out of by Chronos at one. Both men reach a vertical base, and Hellrazor runs at Chronos with a clothesline. Chronos ducks under, and Hellrazor turns around right into a quick DDT by Chronos. Instead of covering, Chronos goes to work with some hard backhand chops that send him aback into the corner. Chronos perches Hellrazor on the top rope, and brings him over with a superplex. Chronos covers, but Hellrazor kicks out at two. Chronos lifts Hellrazor up and slams him right back down to the mat, in preperation of his next move. Chronos climbs to the top, and looks for an enormous elbowdrop, but Hellrazor is able to get out of the way just at the right moment. Hellrazor lifts Chronos up, looking for a DVD, but Chronos slips out of it, behind Hellrazor, and then plants him with a reverse DDT. Cover by Chronos, and another kickout by Hellrazor. Chronos picks Hellrazor up, looking for a powerslam, but this time it's Hellrazor shimmying out of it. He pushes Chronos into the corner, and Chronos comes back, unknowing that Hellrazor is right behind him. Hellrazor hooks him, and brings him over with a german suplex, complete with a bridge for a pinning combination. However, Chronos is able to get free at about 2 1/2. Both men reach their feet, and Chronos goes for a lariat. Hellrazor ducks it, and goes for a powerbomb. Somehow, however, Chronos is able to turn it into a facebuster(X-Factor). Cover by Chronos, kickout by Hellrazor again. Chronos, looking astonished tries to think of something to do, hooks him for a suplex, but Hellrazor turns it into a small package. The ref is able to record the three count, and the team of Damnation gets one up on the fWo tag team champions with this one on one win. As Hellrazor and Fear celebrate inside of the ring, Chronos gets up, and Fallen enters, and the two teams engage in a staredown as Slaughter cuts to a commercial. Result: Hellrazor by pinfall "The Future" Lance Star w/ Abusah vs. Scott Hart Sunday Night Slaughter returned from commercial to the sounds of Result: Star by pinfall Plans. Finn laid on his office couch, tossing a squish ball into the air repeatedly. Armstrong looked off into space. "Hey, Finn... do we have any bun cake?" Finn shrugged. "Hungry again, James?" No response. "You know, you've been packin' on the pounds lately." Armstrong glared in Finn' general direction. "I'll have you know, Finnster, this is no beer belly. This is the fuel tank for my secks manchine!" Finn chuckled. "Yeah, yeah..." Armstrong looked at Finn in a serious manner. "Speaking of sex... any plans for the chicks division?" Finn sat up from the couch, and looked around his office. "None. We haven't done anything with the women's division for months, but we haven't been able to. The lack of willing competitors and a small fan base just doesn't merit a full push for that division. I think it's time we put it on the shelf for awhile, until more women join up." Armstrong agreed. "Yeah, that sounds like a plan..." Finn stood up from his couch and walked over to a nearby desk, before sitting down and looking at his laptop computer. "I've got to make some calls. If you'll excuse me, James." Armstrong got up and grinned. "What, don't trust me?" Finn gave Armstrong a response he didn't want to hear. "Well, not exactly." Armstrong look disturbed, before stomping out of the office. Finn rolled his eyes, before reaching for his phone. Matt Douglas (c) vs. VL Tha Murdera "Shook Ones, Part II" by Mobb Deep sounds, which brings out VL, who comes to a mixed reaction. She calmly walks down the ramp way, and slides under the bottom rope, taking a corner. Result: Douglas by pinfall Phone Calls Back inside the office, Finn began dialing a number. The phone rang several times before a voice answered on the other end. "Jon?" asked Finn. "Hey... this is Chris Finn. I know you haven't been the most active lately but I'm willing to give you another chance. Are you up for it? .... You are? Great. Warfare is booked, but we've got room for you at Slaughter. Can I trust that you'll be there? ... Superb. I'll have the scheduling department get back to you tomorrow on your opponent, and we'll ship some tickets your way as soon as possible. Is your visa in good standing? ... Great. Great to have you back, Jon." Finn said goodbye to the voice on the other end, before finding another number. He dialed the number, and repeated the said process of earlier. Someone answered, and Finn began. "Hey... Daniel. This is Chris Finn from the fWo. I was looking at your contract earlier today and you still have about eight months left on your original deal. I've came up with a role for you if you'd like to make a return to the fWo... would you? .... You would? That's great news. I'd like for you to be at Warfare if possible... I have a spot for you on the card immediately. Is this do-able for you? ... It is? Great. Warfare is from Buffalo... I'll make sure the scheduling department get your tickets in the next forty eight hours, and I'll call you again before Tuesday to go over your role on the card for Warfare. Sound good? ... Great. I'll talk to you tomorrow." Finn hung up thep hone, before putting his fist in the air to signify he was very happy about the latest turn of events. Erik Kelly (c) vs. Frank Snow vs. Robert Jensen vs. Hiroshima Boos bring us back from commerical, as they have been exposed to "Whatever", by Godsmack. It isn't the music that they are so upset about; rather, it is the appearance of the man called Frank Snow. Snow makes his way down to ringside with a cart of goodies that would make even VL jealous: among the items were the BROOM OF DOOM, ELECTRIC DRILL OF TERROR, and the ever-so-fearsome SNOW CONE. This was actually Snow's lunch, and he ate it on his way down to ringside. KMFDM blasted on the PA system next, which was to bring out Hiroshima. However, loud boos for the former FtfWo member quickly turned to cheers as Erik Kelly ran down toward ringside with a familiar object: THE ORDINARY STEEL CHAIR OF HECK!. Kelly slid into the ring, and immediately Snow and Kelly revisited the good times they had on Warfare. Kelly swung the ORDINARY STEEL CHAIR OF HECK! at Snow, and missed. Snow countered with a superkick to the chair, which smashed into Kelly's head. Kelly stumbled backwards over the ropes. Snow hops down off the apron and grabs another of his plethera of accessories, the ROLLER SKATES FROM THE 70S, from his cart and charges Kelly. Snow misses Kelly and runs into the guard rail, sending the ROLLER SKATES FROM THE 70S flying into the crowd. At this time, both Hiroshima and Robert Jensen make their way toward ringside. Then they notice that they are running down together. Jensen trips Hiroshima, who, along with Jensen, is bandaged along his torso. Both men have souveniers of Redemption: injuries to the head and back. Jensen pulls the BRASS KNUCKLES OF DESTRUCTION out of his tights and starts to pound away on Hiroshima. Meanwhile, Kelly has pulled out a rather devastating weapon from Snow's cart: MICHAEL JACKSON ALBUMS FROM THE 80S. He takes the extremely successful "Thriller" album and smashes Snow over the head with it. Snow, realizing that this was a collector's item, frowns at Kelly's actions and clotheslines him. Obviously, Michael Jackson will have as much effect on this matchup as he does in pop culture today. Snow pulls out a rather unassuming item out of his cart: a simple walkman. He then grabs the ORDINARY STEEL CHAIR OF HECK! and nails Kelly in the head with it. He hits him two more times and then places the headphones over the ears of Kelly. What fans don't realize is that Snow has just implemented the deadly SHERYL CROW TORTURE METHOD. He sets the volume method to "7" and hits play. "Strong Enough" hits Kelly's ears...this is something he cannot take. Slowly, Snow moves the volume level up to "8". Kelly is seen trying to grab the headphones off his head. But he cannot, for his arms are pinned down (he is on his stomach, and therefore cannot be counted for a three). The volume then moves to "9" as "All I Want To Do" begins to play. Kelly starts screaming; no man should ever be forced into this. As he can take it no longer, Snow gets nailed in the head by Jensen, who is carrying the SHOVEL OF HARRISBURG, PA. Kelly begins to recuperate as Jensen tosses Snow into the ring. Jensen then swings the curtain under the ring open. He pulls out a rather interesting item and tosses that into the ring: THE PATRIOT MISSILE. Nobody knows why this would be underneath the ring, but then again, this is not an ordinary match. Jensen slides into the ring and picks up THE PATRIOT MISSILE. He nails Snow in the head with it. Snow is knocked out cold. Meanwhile, Kelly has recuperated enough to notice that Hiroshima is out cold, his face a bloody mess from the repeated shots of Jensen and his BRASS KNUCKLES OF DESTRUCTION. Kelly picks up Hiroshima and throws up onto the stage. He searches underneath the ring. Immediately the crowd starts to chant "WE WANT TABLES! WE WANT TABLES!" They remember watching Redemption, and what Kelly did to Hiroshima there. Kelly remembers this as well, and as a result, he grabs a table and takes it up on the stage. Then he finds two sheets of glass and sets them on the table as well. He looks around the area some more, and finds one more piece of glass. As three sheets of glass lay on the table, Kelly picks up Hiroshima, the crowd rises to their feet. Meanwhile, Robert Jensen, who has pulled out the BARBED WIRE OF THE DAMNED, begins to set up Snow (still knocked out from THE PATRIOT MISSILE shots he took) for the Make You Bleed. Kelly yells "POWERBOMB" to the crowd, which brings much delight to the fans. He picks up Hiroshima and takes off toward the table. He hits the Eradication, sending glass deep into the back of Hiroshima, as well as all over the entrance ramp. He places his boot on top of Hiroshima, and waits for the ref to count the three; however, there is no ref. He is down in the ring, awaiting Jensen's cover of Snow. Jensen has hit the Make You Bleed, and is three seconds away from becoming the new Hardcore Champion. In a flash, Kelly takes off toward the ring. He hops onto the apron as Jensen makes the cover. He takes a leap onto the top rope at the count of 1. He lines up Jensen as the ref counts 2. He takes off and connects on a leg drop, breaking the cover as the ref was about to slam his hand down for the three. Kelly picks up Jensen and hits a powerbomb. He then picks up Snow and does the same. He then places a foot on each of them and awaits the ref's count. However, the lights go out. Suddenly, a blue streak runs across the arena, and the word "XAVIER" runs across the SlaughterScreen. A very tall man appears from the entrance ramp. He takes a look down at Hiroshima, who is a bloody mess on the stage. He walks over him and makes his way toward the ring. He steps onto the apron and steps over the top rope. He looks down at Snow and Jensen, and then looks Kelly eye-to-eye. The two begin to jaw at each other as the lights come back on. Security personnel rush to the ring to break everything up. Medical personnel attend to Hiroshima. The match has apparently ended, thanks to this man known as Xavier. As the chaos ensues, and the medics turn their attention to Snow and Jensen, Slaughter heads to a commercial break. Result: No Contest Shocker. Slaughter came back from commercial to find Deep Purple's "Bad Attitude" playing and the duo of Mitch Wilson and Ice making their way to the ring under blue lights to boos from the crowd. Ice had recently announced in a promo that he had an announcement regarding Scott Slugger that would be revealed on Slaughter, and some of the less bright in the audience wondered aloud if this would be it. Wilson, walking to the ring in front of Ice, rolled into the ring and mounted the turnbuckles, taunting the crowd as Ice stepped over the top rope and into the squared circle. His usually angry and/or stoic face now showed traces of amusement as he retrieved a microphone from a ringside attendant and addressed the crowd. "Hey, Sluggsy! I told you I had a little surprise for you today... well, here it is. Roll that tape, boys." On the SlaughterScreen, scenes from last July's Fight The Future III house show, live from Tacoma, Washington, began to play. Onscreen, Cactus Jon worked back, and attempted to execute a piledriver which surely would have finished Ultra Violet. With his last bit of strength however Ultra Violet executed a backdrop brainbuster, but struck his own head in the process. Scott Slugger started the ten count, but when he got to nine he quickly signaled Ice inside the ring and covered both Jon and Ultra Violet. Ice made one of the fastest counts in fWo history, and Scott Slugger quickly grabbed the fWo World Heavyweight title and made his way up the entrance ramp, laughing at the scam he just pulled over the fWo. Back in the ring, Ice laughed, a chilling sound. "You see, I was one of two special referees for the match. And when I came back, I checked on something - the validity of my referee's license. Turns out, Travis never had time to revoke the thing, Armstrong was too busy dicking around with the promotion, and Finn just plain forgot... so as of right now, I am still an officially licensed fans Wrestling organization referee. And guess what, Sluggsy? As of now, I am the special guest referee for the main event at Redemption." The crowd reacted to this revelation with shock, and Ice sneered as some fans wondered aloud why Finn didn't simply come to the ring and revoke the license. "And for those of you who are wondering why Finn doesn't just revoke it now... well, once a referee is announced for a match, he can't be removed. Bad break, Sluggsy, but that's the way it goes sometimes. Hell, you should know considering your team throws Jose Mesa out there every other game." Ice chuckled again. "As for the other two guys in the match? Eron... ya bug me, kid. But hell, better you than Slugger. And Strangler? I got nothing against you. Remember the fWo Honor Guard? Me, you, and two midcarders. And you took down FTfWo... which gets you big points in my book." Ice paused a moment, then continued as Mitch Wilson appeared momentarily dismayed in the background. "Yeah, they pretty much sucked, save for Mitch here." Wilson's face cleared, and Ice continued. "But all in all, I can only promise one thing. I will be a fair and impartial referee, and I'll call this one right down the middle... except for when I'm trying to screw Slugger." Wilson chuckled in the background, and Ice continued. "So Sluggsy... I'll see you at Critical Condition. Don't be late." Ice tossed the microphone aside as "Bad Attitude" began to play again, and Ice walked backstage. Wilson awaited in the ring however, for the arrival of his opponent. Mitch Wilson vs. Havok "Last Resort", the theme of the Future Foundation, begins to play throughout the arena as Foundationer and fWo Internet Champion Primetime makes his way to the ringside area to provide commentary, with his belt conspicuously absent as it is around the waist of Mitch Wilson, who is already in the ring. Wilson's opponent for the night, Havok makes his entrance to "When Worlds Collide", rolling into the ring and shooting an icy glare at Primetime, who returns it from the announcer's table. Wilson unstraps the belt and sets it aside, before turning to Havok as the bell rings. The two lock up, and Havok gains the advantage, armdragging Wilson over. As Wilson gets up, Havok takes him back down with a dropkick. Havok quickly covers and hooks a leg, but Wilson easily kicks out at one. Both men make their way to their feet, and Havok grabs the arm of Wilson and whips him into the ropes. As Wilson comes off, Havok leaps high into the air and hammers into Wilson with a leaping leg lariat, taking down Mr. Main Event. Havok then runs into the ropes, before leaping onto the second strand and leaping back with an elbowdrop. Havok covers, but Wilson kicks out at two. Havok brings Wilson up again, and stings him with a chop that knocks him into the corner. Havok follows this up with another chop, reddening the chest of Wilson. Havok takes a few steps backwards, then charges into the corner, leaping in with a body splash that misses badly as Wilson rolls out of the corner. Havok hits the pads chest-first and stumbles out backwards, right into a back suplex with a bridge from Wilson. Havok kicks out at two, and Wilson brings him up and takes him back down with a small package. Havok again kicks out at two, and Wilson brings Havok up once more and takes him over with a hard snap suplex. Wilson holds on and brings Havok up, before spiking him with a brainbuster. Wilson continues to hold on to the suplex position, finally bringing Havok up again and taking him over with a fishermans suplex. The referee makes the count, but Havok somehow manages to raise a shoulder at the count of two. Wilson disputes this with the official for a moment, allowing Havok to roll him up from the mat in a schoolboy. The ref nearly reaches three, but Wilson shoulders up and out at two. As Havok gets back up, Wilson clobbers him with a clothesline, re-taking the momentum. Wilson brings Havok to his feet once again and whips him into the ropes, before catching him coming off with a tornado variation of the traditional backbreaker. Wilson covers and hooks a leg, but Havok again manages to stay alive with a kickout. Wilson brings Havok up and whips him hard into the turnbuckles, before charging in and jacking Havok's jaw with a leaping forearm shot. Havok stumbles out, and Wilson bounces off the ropes and bulldogs him. The crowd begins to go into a frenzy, but quickly remembers that nobody in the fWo does the worm and thus resumes their normal level of sound. Wilson, paying them no mind, drops a leg on Havok's arm, followed by another. He then brings Havok up and twists his arm into a hammerlock, which is followed up by a hammerlock bodyslam. Wilson brings Havok back up to his feet and grabs his arm again, before dropping straight down to a sitting position, attempting to pull Havok's arm out of his socket. With Havok down and clutching his arm, Wilson takes the opportunity to unleash a stream of of words that George Carlin would likely find offensive in the direction of Primetime, who takes exception and begins to leave the announcer's table as Wilson heads back over to Havok and lifts him up. Wilson slaps on a standing armbar on Havok, but Havok has had time to recover and launches a simple clenched fist to the nose of Wilson, who grabs his face in pain and releases the hold. With Wilson doubled over holding his face, Havok takes him down with a rocker dropper. Sensing he has the momentum, Havok brings Wilson up once more and whips him into the ropes, but Wilson manages to reverse. Havok attempts a clothesline coming off, but Wilson sees it coming and takes Havok down, locking on one of his two main finishers, the Fujiwara armbar. Havok screams in pain, close to tapping, and Wilson pulls farther back on the arm. However, his attention is diverted when he sees Primetime at ringside, collecting the Internet Title. Wilson releases the hold and charges at the ropes, sliding down with a baseball slide that knocks the gold back into the face of the unsuspecting Primetime. With his mission accomplished, Wilson turns back to Havok, who is still on the ground, and attempts to reapply the hold. Before he can drop down, however, Havok kicks upwards, hitting with what may or may not be a sort of testicular kick, but in any case it leaves Wilson doubled over and in pain. With Wilson in pain, Havok brings himself to his feet and hooks Wilson from behind with his non-worked left arm. He then lits up and drops down, spiking Wilson with The Headache. Havok rolls Wilson over and covers, and the referee tolls the three count. Before Havok can celebrate, a recovered Primetime hits the ring and waffles Havok with the Internet Title. He then brings Wilson up and begins to hammer away on him. Wilson, still out of it, takes the majority of the blows, and Primetime continues to hammer on him before Havok begins to recover and leaps onto Primetime, striking him with closed fists. Primetime shoves him aside into Mitch Wilson, who lashes out indiscriminately, clocking Havok. With that, the brawl between the two is on and Primetime rolls outside, grabbing his belt on the way and heading backstage. Inside the ring, Wilson and Havok continue to brawl with no signs of stopping as Slaughter heads backstage... Result: Havok by pinfall Closer (to Critical Condition) Eron The Relentless sat in his lockerroom, looking at the television set with a disgusted look on his face. As he saw the camera enter the room, his expression changed into a confident smile. "So, Ice wants to make himself the referee for our match at Critical Condition? I don't care. I don't [censored] care. I'm Eron The Relentless and I'll do whatever the [censored] I want. I don't care if Ice tries to keep me from doing it, because I'll still do it. And just like baseball boy and our paper champion... if Ice tries to get in my way, the only thing he'll get is a breath... of fresh death!" Eron laughed, before whispering "breath... of fresh death" over and over and over... before standing up and pushing the camera man down. Mike Bear vs. Scott Collins The fans react with a respectful ovation as "Burning" plays and the recently embattled Scott Collins makes his way to the ring, looking especially focused and intense for this match, which he considers one of his last chances to regain his position as a feared superstar. He rolls into the squared circle and waits for Mike Bear to head to the ring, The tattooed warrior enters, International Title around his waist, looking as focused as he usually does. Bear rolls in and unstraps the belt, handing it to the referee. As he does so, the bell rings. Collins charges him with a clothesline, but Bear ducks and unloads on Collins with a thundering windup right. Collins' head jerks back and he flies backwards, crashing into the corner. Bear stalks in and hammers him with another right, followed by another, another, and another, rocking Collins' world. Bear grabs the arm of Collins and whips him across into the opposite corner, where Collins crashes hard into the turnbuckle and stumbles out, right into a clothesline from Bear. Bear drops down and makes a cover, but Collins kicks out at two. Bear brings Collins up again and attempts a whip into the ropes, but Collins reverses it. Bear ducks Collins' attempted short lariat and grabs Collins as he turns around, pulling him into a short powerslam that plants Collins into the mat with a ring-rocking impact. Bear covers Collins and hooks a leg, but Collins powers out at two. Bear brings Collins up again and goes for a superkick, but Collins ducks out of the way and blasts Bear's head with his foot in a superkick of his own. Collins takes a moment to shake off the cobwebs, then covers Bear, hooking a leg. Bear shoulders up at two, and Collins brings him to his feet again before taking him over with an overhead belly-to-belly suplex. Collins rolls Bear over and applies the Scotty-La-Magistral-Pinfall-Maneuver, but Bear just kicks out in the nick of time. Sensing that much more damage must be done to Bear, Collins brings him up and chops him hard on the chest, knocking him into the corner. Collins steps in and strikes Bear in the head with a series of elbows. Collins begins to frame Bear with his hands before delivering the final one, but then remembers that people who do that suck and simply smashes Bear on the head with the elbow one last time. Bear stumbles out, right into a northern lights suplex from Collins. The referee counts one, two, but Bear kicks out a heartbeat from losing his International Title. Collins brings Bear up again, and drops him down with a brainbuster. Collins heads to the corner and mounts the second turnbuckle, before leaping off and driving the elbow into the throat of Bear. Collins covers, but Bear again shows his resiliency by kicking out before the three count is tolled. Collins brings Bear up once again and attempts to take him over with a double-underhook suplex, but Bear fights desperately out of it and backdrops Collins over his head. In the process, he seem to get a second wind. As Collins gets up, Bear hiptosses him up and over, followed by leaping into the air with a beautiful dropkick that strikes Collins flush in the face. Collins makes his way to his feet again and is met by a superkick which knocks him against the ropes. Bear then charges at Collins, taking both men over with a Cactus clothesline. Both men crash to the outside, and after a moment, Bear reaches his feet again and rolls Collins in. He brings Collins up and attempts the Whiplash, but Collins slides down his shoulders and hooks his head on the way down for the Death Penalty. Bear holds his ground however and shoves Collins off into the ropes. He catches Collins coming off, lifts him into a fireman's carry, and wastes no time in hitting the Whiplash on his second attempt. Bear makes the cover, and the ref counts one... two... three, and Bear retains his International Title. The referee goes to the outside and receives Bear' strap, before bringing it back in the ring and handing it to Bear. Bear holds it for the crowd to see, which draws a large cheer. Bear straps it around his waist and exits the ring, and begins walking backstage. As he does however, Collins asks for a microphone and is given one... Result: Bear by pinfall Do Or Die "Hey, Bear. Turn around. I'm not done yet. I'll give you your due. You beat me. But I want one more chance. One more chance to prove to you, the rest of the world, and most importantly myself that I have what it takes. I want to prove that I still belong around here. I've been the Internet Champion, I've been a Tag Team Champion, I've held that very belt that is around your waist right now, I've been a Hardcore Champion... and I've done what I'm sure you'll do someday Bear, and I've held the most prized posession this industry of ours has to offer: The fWo World Title. I've held it not just once, but twice. And I was the flagbearer for this company for many months, so I know I still have what it takes. I just want to prove it to myself. So, here's what I'm proposing to you. I want another match at Critical Condition... between you and I. Your title doesn't have to be on the line, because this isn't about a title to me right now. Don't worry about that. But I just request one more match between you and myself. And if I don't win... if my arm isn't raised at the end of that night, then I shouldn't be in the ring. And that's not an offense to you, because you'll be able to brag that you were the one who retired the great Scott Collins. So, what do you say, Bear? One on one at Critical Condition. If I can't win... I'll leave the fWo." Collins put his hands near his hips as he waited for a response from the International Champion. Bear considered the offer for several minutes before shaking his head, and pointing at Collins, telling him he had a deal. Collins smiled and held a fist in the air, before telling Bear that victory would be his come June 24th. On that note, Slaughter came to a close... with the crowd hoping that June 24th would not be the last we all saw of Collins.
