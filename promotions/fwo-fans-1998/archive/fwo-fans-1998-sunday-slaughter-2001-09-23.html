Introduction Within the arena fans looked around the arena with speculative glances. For many of these people, it was the first time they had been in a large public gathering since the attack less than two weeks ago. There were fear in some glances, defiance in others. American flags waved silent sentinels above murmuring voices. This was not an overly patriotic crowd, but it was a gathering that was somber, reflective, and pained by the recent past. Few within the arena had been personally impacted by the attack, but all felt the weight upon their shoulders. Much like sixty years ago, September 11th would be a date that would forever live in infamy. What would come tomorrow caused nervous glances around the arena with fans taking special note of the exits and escape routes. What would come tomorrow prompted people to rub their temples with fingertips, hard lines of worry etched into their foreheads. What would come tomorrow led mothers to hold their children closer and fathers to contemplate the destructive force of war against in many ways an unknown enemy. An old patriot sitting high in the arena stands lowered his head to the ground as the events of the recent past washed over him, and with the knowledge that events had been set in motion that called for destructive, consuming vengeance. The ring, which would be the center of their attention this night looked strangely empty and alone. The lights in the arena were subdued, and the announcement that came caused several in attendance to jump nervously. "Ladies and gentlemen, if you will please stand and focus your attention to the arena entrance." They came slowly from the back, both the wrestlers that they all knew and the workers who normally were hidden from sight. Ric Chronos, John Willis, Vic Creed, James Armstrong, Primetime, Geoff Green, Black Quicksilver... at the moment alliances were put aside as one by one they filed slowly out onto the entrance ramp. Still more came, until the ramp was filled with the staff of the Fans Wrestling Organization. Shoulder to shoulder they stood, some in costume and others in the simple attire that they came to the arena with. Many looked tired, but all looked haunted. In turn, the fans in attendance stood as one. The sounds of chatter had died away and now there was only the sound of scuffling feet and the nervous gesture of smoothing clothing and furtive glances. One by one their view held upon the high flag that hung from the rafters. At the moment it was not a symbol of the country they lived in, but a symbol of the lives that had been snuffed out in one violent moment, lives that would go no farther. With no speeches or announcement, the loud sound of the bell rang. The noise echoed through the arena where only silence met the sound. . . . . . . . . . . The bell rang ten times, and then fell silent. The flag high above hung still, unwavering... but still. Some fans saw the wrestlers slowly return to the back. Some continued to look at the flag as a tide of emotions battled within them. Some looked to God, others looked within. Some cried, some held still. All looked to the future, an uncertain and often frightening future. But the future would come, and humanity would go on. He went for another cover, but Ross somehow kicked out. The match continued as both men continued to pummel one another. Creed swung Ross to the ropes and ducked for a backdrop. Ross stopped and went for a reverse neckbreaker but Creed countered directly into a suplex w/ a piledriver -otherwise known as the 'Kodiak Krippler'. The fans grimmaced, fearing the match would be over. Creed went for a nonchalant cover, but Ross kicked out at two. This time, for good measure, Creed hooked the leg. One. Two. No. Ross again kicked out. Creed = pissed off. "Fuck!" Creed yelled out, as the fans took delight in his frustration. Deciding that he was going to put an end to this match once and for all, Creed went to the top rope. He flew off, but Ross put his foot up at the last minute, nailing Creed in the jaw. Creed went flying backwards into the corner, and Ross struggled to his feet. He turned around as Creed walked out from the corner, Ross speared him backwards - into the ladder. The ladder came toppling down onto both men, but moreso on Creed. With both men out, the referee began a ten-count. One. Two. Three. Four. Ross showed signs of life as his arm seaped up through the mass destruction in the ring. Five. Six. Seven. Eight. Nine. He draped it atop the ladder. The referee stopped the ten count, and went directly into a three count. The match appeared over. One. Two. No. Creed pushed the ladder off with all his might, and then grabbed the nearby ropes as blood poured down his face. He began pulling himself up with aid from the top rope, before he turned around only to meet a kick to the stomach, followed by a double-arm DDT from Ross. Ross then charged and leaped off the middle ropes with an Asai moonsault. One. Two. No. Creed shouldered out, and now Ross knew he had to take another high risk to win the match. He decided to escape over the top of the cage and out. He began climbing up, but Creed reached up and grabbed his left foot. Ross kicked back several times, before pushing Creed backwards. Ross was now on top of the cage. But instead of exiting out, he was now standing. He flew off with a high crossbody, but Creed caught him in the air, and releasing him into a pile of chairs behind him with a fallaway slam. Deciding the match was almost over, he grabbed one of the chairs, and placed it on Ross. He began climbing up to the top rope, and again flew off with a frogsplash. The Creedzillinator. He covered Ross. It was over. One. Two. No. Ross got his foot on the bottom rope. "Fuck!" Creed was more pissed off than before. But what could he do... he slowly crawled over and grabbed a chair, before looking through the steel mesh to the outside. The fans called him names and booed him, but he didn't care. He was going to make Ross taste the steel chair and like it. But Ross was grabbing one of his own, and Creed didn't know that, as his back was to Ross. The two men stood up, turned towards one another, and swung. They connected. Both men went to the mat, and the referee began another ten count. Blood dripped profusely off the faces of the champion & challenger. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. Seven. Eight. Nine. Creed got to his feet first, and he grabbed every chair in the ring and made sure that none of them were near Ross. He then positioned a ladder in the corner, and made sure that Ross had no quick access to any weapon... or so he thought. Creed grabbed a chair, and prepared to lay waste to Ross once again. A groggy Ross climbed to his feet, and as he turned, Creed swung. But this time Ross ducked. Creed swung again, but Ross somersaulted on the mat and grabbed the ladder. Creed swung and Ross pushed the ladder into Creed. The ladder hit off the chair and into Ross, and then backwards into Creed. It hit off the massive structure of Creed, and fell on the mat near Ross... who fell backwards and found himself tangled in the ropes. Ross struggled to get free, but Ross couldn't escape -- his body was very sore, especially his ribs. And Creed wasn't in the best of shape either, his shoulder a virtual mess. But Creed wasn't hooked in the ropes, and thus he had a chance to win the match. The referee helped Ross as much as he could to get free... Creed accused the referee of favoritism but it was natural for the referee to help a wrestler stuck in the ropes. Creed spit some blood towards Ross, before he told Ross it was over, and he'd see him at the pay-per-view next Saturday. Creed slowly climbed towards the corner, before helping himself to a vertical base with the ropes. He climbed outside the ropes, and headed towards the door. As he did, Ross broke free, and grabbed the ladder. Ross screamed at Creed. "PREPARE TO DIE, MOTHERFUCKER!" He positioned the ladder as Creed stopped in his tracks right in front of the steel cage door. Ross used the ladder as a battering ram and nailed Creed in the ribs, and sent him packing... unfortunately for Ross, Creed hit the door and broke them off the hinges, before he landed outside the ring. Creed' feet hit the floor, and the officials both in the ring and outside the ring agreed -- Creed was the winner. Ross dropped the ladder, realizing he had essentially helped Creed win the match. Creed grabbed his shoulder, and Ross fell to the mat, knowing that tonight, he was a defeated man. Suddenly, from backstage came the reigning fWo World & Tag Team Champions. Ric Chronos. The Flying Frenchie. Primetime. Collectively known as "3vil". They charged the ring and one by one entered. Ross fended Primetime off with a head and arms suplex. But he turned and fell victim to a Frenchie superkick, and then the three men preyed on Ross like a pack of wolves on their final meal. Fist into flesh echoed throughout the arena as the steel cage began raising to the ceiling. The Black Panther, followed by Ruin & Matt Loco, entered the ring. All men got their shots in on Ross, as Loco tended to Creed outside the ring. Creed scowled at Loco, knowing that Loco had a certain... sexual preference. From the back, however, came The Boston Strangler, Erik Kelly, Hellrazor, Eddy Fate & Tempest. Kelly & Hellrazor each went after Ruin, while Fate took care of Panther just as he did earlier. The Strangler took out Frenchie, while Tempest... got a parting shot in at Chronos as she nailed him with a jumping thrust kick, sending him over the top rope to the outside. There, Strangler charged and speared him into the guardrail... and both men toppled into the ringside area. Smart fans got out of the way in time. Dumb fans remained there as the two men collided in them, but afterwards got up and started up an absurd yet appropiate "fWo!" chant. This chant continued as the show faded to black... and Frankton & Armstrong reminded everyone to tune in this Saturday. Unstable was mere hours away.
