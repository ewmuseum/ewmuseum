Introduction The fWo lives in strange times. With the promotion returned to the United States after several months of international exile, the effects of their lapse in activity has taken its toll. The first and biggest effect is about to decend on the promotion, with Wednesday Warfare and Sunday Slaughter coming to an end in one week. Tonight, with only one more event remaining in the flagship series of the fWo, all eyes are on a very uncertain future... State of the Promotion address "We're ready." Silver Fox nodded to Geoff Green and looked at the camera. This wasn't being broadcast out to the arena or into the homes of fans around the world, but it was going out on closed circuit to the wrestlers in the back... all of the backstage monitors, box sets and into the production truck where it would be taped for the Board of Directors and for media outlets. Silver Fox nodded to the camera, waiting for the red light to come on and let him know that they were going. Geoff signaled him. They were rolling. "Members of the fWo staff, wrestlers, backstage hands, production assistants, and others. Members of the board, investors, and our partners in the media. My name is Grant Mitchel. I wrestled under the name Silver Fox for this fine promotion several years ago. Today, I work with Samuel Robinson Fitspatrick, Theodore Thompson and Ted Tallison as the managing road agents for the fWo. For years we have produced events, these events have then been carried on paid order programming, specifically our flagship events, as well as on weekly live television programs. In the past we have pursued both network, cable and syndication options for our programming, and have enjoyed a relationship with NBC and FOX that have spanned several years. Due to changing market conditions this relationship has come to an end. Using their contractual option, NBC and FOX have elected not to renew our programming. While this is an unfortunate severing of a lengthy relationship, it also opens the doors to new opportunities." Silver Fox cleared his throat and glanced at his notes. If he had a choice, it would be that he would rather be taking stiff clotheslines from Kodiak Vic Creed, the fWo World Heavyweight Champion, than do this. "The series finale of our flagship program Wednesday Warfare will occur on May 21, next week, and the series finale of Sunday Slaughter will occur a few days earlier on May 18. After this date, the fWo will not appear on a weekly basis on either NBC or the FOX network. The historical importance of this move should not be lost on anyone... for years the fWo was defined by Wednesday Warfare and Sunday Slaughter, but with the closing of the past we must look forward to the future. While the wrestling industry has suffered recently and we have seen the purchase of such large properties as World Championship Wrestling, as well as the closure of such national level independent promotions like Extreme Championship Wrestling, there is still a great future available in the sports entertainment industry. With this in mind, I have an announcement concerning the fWo's future." Silver Fox glanced at Geoff Green, and Green nodded for him to continue. "Beginning May 26, Monday, the fWo will enter a new era with strong expectations for the future. Our investors and board of directors have urged us to look at a more national and ultimately more global level. I am pleased to announce that as of one 'o clock PM, the Fans Wrestling Organization has secured a three year media contract with the American Broadcasting Company and their parent company the Walt Disney Entertainment Group. As part of this agreement the fWo will being broadcasting on Monday nights on the ABC network, and on Thursday night on the ESPN subsidiary network. This is part of a broader plan to compete directly with the World Wrestling Entertainment company, and we have the full support and encouragement of the Board of Directors to make this attempt a reality. Let me be clear: big wins can only be obtained with big risks. This is a big bet, but it is a bet that our board, as well as our staff, believes that we can win." Silver Fox looked nervously around him. Several wrestlers were standing around the area, watching this announcement first-hand. All wore expressions of concern, hope and anticipation. It really was the end of one era and the start of another. "In addition to our new television schedule, there are changes in our broadcasting strategy for paid programming. We will broadcast four events on premium pay channels on Sunday Nights. These events will be supported by broad advertising and marketing campaigns from our parent media company. In addition, we will still hold our year-end special event, Countdown, as a premium purchased event. During months where we do not have a premium event scheduled, as well as in the month of December, we will hold a special monthly program on the FOX network as part of the network's deal to buy out the remainder of our contract. This event, titled Unleashed, will be announced in June on an undetermined night that has yet to be negotiated." Wiping his brow, Silver Fox came to the end of his notes. "I hope that you join me in embracing this new opportunity for all of us. This will not be an easy task. We are asking all of you to step up to the plate and help take the promotion, as well as yourselves, to a new level. With your help I am convinced that we can succeed. On behalf of the fWo Road Management Crew, I thank you." The red light flicked off. Silver Fox glanced around at the collection of wrestlers, road crew, and other staff that had assembled to watch his speech. He nodded to them. Slowly, they began to clap... and Silver Fox smiled.
