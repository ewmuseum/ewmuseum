As the referee jumped out of the way and in to position the Paragon saw exactly who they had directed their anger at. 'Cocky' Craig Miles, the mastermind behind the near destruction of the Deacon was speeding full tilt down the ramp way towards the ring. The ref counted. One! Craig Miles leaped. Two!! He slid under the rope and gained his footing and dove on top of the hero with a pointed elbow. All the while Brand never let go of his fallen enemy. Three!!! The hand hit the mat milliseconds before Miles hit Frontier. Frontier absorbed the attack. Still holding on to Winters as the ref jumped to his feet... Calling for the bell. Brand Frontier had just vanquished the Omega of the fWo... and now he was going to pay. The 'pointed elbow' had crashed against Frontier's already damaged nose and this attack served to tear Frontier up, not allowing him to see what happened next. Miles retrieve an instrument for the Paragon's destruction from ringside. He pulled the metal object back as far as his arms would take it and then brought it to bear on the back of the head of the mightily in trouble hero. The sickening thud broadcast over the speakers by the nearby camera echoed in the arena, only to be drowned out by another... and another... and another... It would make no sense to keep count at this point actually as the officials backstage stormed the ring, attempting to stop the slaughter. That's when the fans found hope yet again as they cheered the arrival of... Black Quicksilver!? Racing to the ring, another chair in hand, the Number One Contender entered the Lion's Den. He swung the chair as if it was mighty Mjolnir calling a maelstrom. Winters and Miles both exited the ring, but as they did yet another evil emerged from the darkness. Like a snake from a pit Kellen Kinkade had followed Quicksilver to the ring, a few seconds behind. Quicksilver knew nothing of what was behind him until he was spun around by an arm, catching a boot to the gut as he did. A second later BQ inhaled The Dying Breath, the sit-out DDT specialty of the Immortal One flattening Quicksilver's face to the mat. With that cue, Miles and Winters reentered, content smiles of evil awash across their faces. They circled around to Kinkade's side eyeing him warily as he stared a hole in the soul of the prone Frontier. As if following a predetermined plan of attack, Kinkade pounced on his nemesis smashing thick fists sickly across the brow of Brandon Roche as Craig Miles and Donaven Winters began smearing the legendary Black Quicksilver across the mat. Again the fans cheered. Yes, cheered. Turning to look to the entrance way because of the unusual reaction by the sheep in attendance Winters noticed yet another pawn in the game integrating himself in to the fray. The fWo World Heavweight Champion had arrived! Jon Crisp, big gold belt in his hand stormed the ring himself, swinging the big prize as his weapon of choice Winters and Miles scurried from the ring. With that brief respite, Quicksilver began to stir. Crisp had interfered quickly enough to 'save' his adversary for Cyberslam from suffering any lasting effects. Kellen paused shortly and as if that was all he had been awaiting Brand Frontier stood in one fluid motion tossing Kinkade off of him. Brand Frontier was enraged, blood now covering most of his face, his sudden rebirth however had energized the faithfuls once again and they cheered. Kellen staggered back and a now revived Black Quicksilver crouched and waited. Time slowed. A newly arrived Jon Crisp, the one true championship tightly in his grasp, also waited. Kellen Kinkade turned, halfway between to two of them. Quicksilver lunged. Jon Crisp swung. Kellen Kinkade ducked. SuperQuick-SilverKick. Right in to the World Heavyweight Championship belt, this drove the twelve pounds of gold into the face of the current champion. Jon Crisp went down like a sack of bricks. The fans booed as Miles and Winters now bum rushed the startled masked man, squashing him in to the corner, delivering stomps after punches after lowblows until the fight was driven from the leader of Karma. Kellen Kinkade had now turned his sights on the rejuvenated Brand Frontier. He struck out like a venomous snake, Brand countered, he lashed out like a madman, and Frontier shouldered the brunt. Long seconds passed as the two went toe to toe, neither man giving an inch. Brand Frontier was fighting on reserves now and with Winters holding Crisp down with the edge of a discarded steel chair and Quicksilver laid unconscious by the two man assault it looked like time was not on the Paragon's side. Indeed it wasn't. Frontier's last punch was weak. The loss of blood and odds he and the other heroes had fought against was too much. He fell to one knee suddenly and Kinkade vultured on to the faltering warrior. It was all over now. Evil had sank it's teeth in the jugular of the mighty and nothing could stop them now. The Gregorian chant echoed out as if a clarion call to the unwavering. Again the out of breath and exhausted faithful in attendance were reinvigorated. A savior had arrived. Seven feet of heart and faith fortified steel. The Deacon had emerged, his large legs powering him to the ring. Not succumbing to the machinations of Craig Miles over the months Deacon had shown, weary from the battles the previous weeks but not willing to stand by as such atrocities festered. The Mute Freak careened over the top rope as quickly as he could. The fear in the eyes of the evil was evident. A wild swing from the large arm of Deacon sent Winters sprawling over the top rope and to the floor. This gave Craig Miles the time he needed. He leaped forward, leading with the steel chair, aiming for the soft back of Big D's head. Instead it bounced of the massive battler's back. Miles stumbled back from the vibrations as Deacon turned. He grabbed Miles by the throat and sent the 'Cocky One' spiraling over the ropes, following the same path Donaven had just taken. Jon Crisp and Black Quicksilver both began to regain their sense from the brief rest Deacon had given them. Brand Frontier kneeled at the feet of Kade and Kellen smiled. That is, until he felt the large palm of Deac's right hand slam down on his back, nearly flattening him to the mat. He used the momentum of the strike however to carry himself through the ropes and to safety. Kinkade began to back out of the battefield, once again following the path Miles and Winters had already taken. They seemingly waited for Kinkade, all staring in to the ring with hatred. Brand Frontier was now to his feet, groggy as he was. Crisp and Quicksilver had managed to shake away the cobwebs themselves from the brief but dangerous battle. Now these two men stared at each other. Suddenly breaking the silence however Crisp threw a pointer finger in to the face of his pay-per-view opponent both began arguing back and forth between each other, consumed with the battle lines they had drawn for each other these last few months. Deacon and Brand Frontier looked onwards into the heart of true evil however as Kinkade, Winters and Miles exited to the back. The war had truly began this night, one they were now wondering if they had the fortitude to wage. Result: Brand Frontier via Pinfall
