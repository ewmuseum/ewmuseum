Wednesday Warfare, 22 December 1999

22 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The Flying Frenchie entered the arena and all was well with the world. Just three days before Christmas, the fWo backstage area was strangely quiet and uneventful. Despite the concerns for the coming Millennium, wrestlers were relaxed and even chatted to each other in a friendly fashion. Nearly every member of the fWo roster, past and present, were milling around backstage, exchanging gifts and complimenting each other on the matches over the last year. In one corner of the large entry room, Scott Slugger and Machina sat in front of Geoff Green's camera for the special year-end Sunday Slaughter that would air the night after Christmas. James Armstrong was hardly a competent man, but he knew enough to give the wrestlers a few days off for the holidays, and after Twilight many of them wouldn't be working any shows until the 29th. There was some idle chat about what everyone would be doing for the holidays, and few noticed the smoke that slowly started to fill the room. Those that did see the wafts of smoke assumed it was steam... or simple fog. By the time that the first scream came it was nearly too late. Choking and gasping for air, floods of fWo wrestlers, production assistants, and security fled the room, doors slamming shut behind them. "It's that bastard Spencer... it's got to be...," gasped Tom, breathing in big gulps of air outside the arena. In the smoky room all was silent for a long minute, and then a large form emerged from the clouds. Rain surveyed the area with a thin smile on his face. From behind him more shadows drifted into human form... first three, then six, then twelve, then thirty. As more forms assembled behind Rain, the fWo Internet Champion extended his arms and looked up toward the heavens. "This is all mine... now, all mine." Boston Strangler vs. Machina The Boston Strangler came out onto the entrance ramp, the opening fireworks of Warfare exploding around him and sending a spray of sparks onto the red haired warrior. The Strangler grinned an evil and dark smile, and walked slowly down the entrance ramp with his eyes fixated on the ring. The Strangler would meet Primetime in a mere two days, and as the big man's fists clenched and unclenched, it was clear that he looked forward to that meeting. Inside the ring the Boston Strangler lay back against the ropes, his hair blown over his face and his eyes peering between strands at the arena entrance. Born In The USA began to pump through the arena, and Bruce Springsteen came out onto the entrance ramp. Bruce Springsteen made his way to the ring, slapping the hands of fans and scratching at where his mask chafed his neck. He noticed Penny Pureheart standing at ringside with a number of picketers, and watched with dismay as fWo security and the local San Jose police force escorted them out of the arena. He didn't notice the hand of the Boston Strangler reaching from the ring and grabbing his neck until the hand was clamped around his head. Bruce was drug into the ring and the Strangler at once began battering the masked man with hard lefts and rights. Grunting "Born To Run was a stupid song," the Boston Strangler continued to pound the head of Bruce Springsteen. Bruce got a leg up on the ropes, and the referee forced the Strangler to back away. Bruce staggered to his feet, and got in a karate stance. The Boston Strangler walked up to him and punched him in the nose, sending the Bruce Springsteen mask spinning around his head and finally landed facing the opposite direction. Bruce staggered around, and the Boston Strangler easily placed the crossface chickenwing submission hold on him and locked it in. Quickly tapping out, the Strangler released Bruce and sent him falling to the mat. Turning to the camera, the Boston Strangler whispered, "Primetime, you're dead. Friday... and tonight." Result: Boston Strangler by Submission Chapter One: Eight. Backstage Matt Loco stood interviewing Black Quicksilver. Behind the masked superstar stood Briana, Jackhammer, and the newest remade member of the fWo, Sphere. The ex-Surgeon looked in great shape, and appeared to have spent his time off getting himself in better physical conditioning. "So Black Quicksilver, my close personal friend... you've assembled a strong group of wrestlers behind you... so what do you hope to accomplish by gathering these people together?" Black Quicksilver laughed, and grabbed the microphone out of Matt Loco's hand. "Loco, isn't it obvious? I've been one step ahead of Silver Fox all year... and the new year isn't going to be any different. Silver Fox, you've got Nikki... not a big surprise there... after all, she's made her way around the rest of the locker room so she was bound to come around to you sooner or later... easy Jackhammer, I'm sure you will get your turn sooner or later... anyway, you've got the tramp, and you've got lizard man and fat ass. That makes four... and looking around behind me... yes, there are four people here. Four... and four. Hmmm... what can that mean?" Black Quicksilver scratched his head in mock thought. Jackhammer appeared to be in real thought, and leaned forward to say, "Eight?" Black Quicksilver looked up at Jackhammer with a bemused expression on his face, and started to continue, but Matt Loco had noticed the Black Panther walking down one of the hallways. He ran after him yelling, "Panther! Panther! I've got something amazingly important here for you! Hey, come back! Hey... don't close that door behind you... hey... Panther?" Courage & Primetime vs. Jake & Austin the Giant For the second time tonight the fans in attendance were treated to the hard beat of the fWo Wednesday Warfare theme, playing as the entrance music for the odd looking team of fWo security guard Austin the Giant and the former bodyguard of Courage, Jake. Both men walked to the ring to a modest pop, and immediately began prepping for their upcoming battle. "Bawitaba" hit the speakers next, followed by a chorus of boos. "Rock N' Roll", the team composed of French Foundation members Primetime and Courage, confidently strolled down the aisle, mocking Jake as they go. Sliding into the ring, Courage dashed at Jake, but withdrew back to his corner when Austin stepped in his path. One bell ring later, and the match was on. Courage looked as if he was going to start for his team, but tagged out to Primetime once it became apparent that Jake had no intention of stepping into the ring. Primetime moved to lockup with his opponent, whom held a one foot four inch height advantage over the former world champion, but was quickly sent to the mat with a sharp fist from Austin. Primetime held his head, slowly getting up, and turned right into another powerful shot from the 500 pound plus giant. Primetime charged back to his feet this time, full of rage, and began to lay into Austin, though having very little effect. Austin simply grabbed Primetime by his hair and flung him into the corner and laughed. Primetime, now getting very frustrated, decided that he had had enough for now and tagged out to Courage, who seemed hesitant to step into the ring. As he did, though, he surprised Austin by charging into him with a dropkick, stumbling the big man. One more, and Austin was teetering. Courage turned to gloat to Jake, but Austin leveled him from behind with a huge clothesline. Austin offered the tag to Jake, but the former bodyguard refused. Shrugging, Austin lifted up Courage and planted him with a huge body slam. Courage arched his back in pain, but Austin showed no mercy as he lifted him up for another... when, out of nowhere, Primetime leapt off his corner's top turnbuckle, dropkicking his own partner in the back. The force was enough to knock Austin to the mat, and Courage was right there for the cover, but only got a two count before being, literally, thrown across the ring. Austin shook his head as he got to a knee, but before he could go any further both Foundation members were on him, stomping away. The big man still managed to pull himself up though, where Primetime and Courage hooked him for a big double suplex. They tried once... No! Again... Still not enough! One more time... and Austin reversed it with a huge double suplex of his own! Both Courage and Primetime hit the mat with a thud, and Courage quickly rolled to the outside. Primetime slowly got to his feet, but was once again put to the mat with a mammoth headbutt. As Primetime laid on the mat Austin bounced off the ropes, and dropped a massive elbow on top of him, followed up by a cover... the ref, however, was currently paying attention to the scene outside the ring, where Courage had backed Jake up to the guardrail, taunting him. Grabbing him by the shirt collar, Courage was ready to heave Jake into the crowd when someone jumped over the guardrail between the two. Courage backed up in fear, and it became apparent just who this "fan was"... none other than the Boston Strangler. The Strangler wielded a metal ball bat, and pounded it threateningly against the palm of his hand. Courage cautiously backed up against the apron, until Primetime came flying over the top rope with a cross body plancha on Strangler. Both men sprawled back, colliding with the barricades. At that moment Jake made his break for the ring, and Courage followed. But, as Courage slid in he ran smack into the chest of one Austin the Giant, who planted the newest member of the Foundation with a belly-to-belly suplex. Austin then accented this with a splash off the ropes, knocking whatever Courage had left out of him. Austin then pointed to Jake, and then to Courage. Jake did as told and made the cover, getting the pinfall as Strangler and Primetime just began to regain their wits on the outside. Primetime recovered first, and decided to live to fight another day as he helped Courage back to the locker rooms. Result: Jake & Austin the Giant by Pinfall Chapter Two: Christmas Spirit Ultra Violet smiled to himself as he looked at the Christmas Tree the arena had set up in the lobby. There were bright lights strung around the branches, blinking on and off in pretty and hypnotic patterns. Around his waist was one half of the one half of the fWo Tag Team title. It had been obviously cut with some sort of sharp and jagged instrument, and seemed to be attached poorly... for it was dangling precariously and looked ready to drop off at any moment. With all of the bright lights and excitement of the holidays, it would be easy for the title to drop off Ultra Violet's waist without him noticing it. Ultra Violet continued to look at the tree, admiring the globes that hung from the branches. They were bright and reflective, and looking closely at one he could just make out Kid Cool and Violet's reflection. The looked happy, obviously in touch with the Christmas spirit... and by the look of things, each other. Violet rubbed Kid Cool's back, and then Kid Cool rubbed Violet's back. Ultra Violet smiled. It was nice that his pretty lady was getting along so well with the new kid. Ultra Violet made a mental note to bake her some kind of cake... or at least get Scotty Cool to bake it for him. The mental note lasted in Ultra Violet's head nearly ten seconds before it was replaced with the miniature train that ran around the tree. Oracle vs. Scott Slugger Oracle's music begins to play, and the blond-haired Oracle makes his way to the ring to a loud reaction from the fans. Due to the beating received from Eron, Oracle is slightly limping as he walks. After a moment, Eron's music begins to play, and the International Champ makes his way to the ring, rolling in and confronting Oracle. The two are about to exchange blows when "Back In Black" begins to play, and the crowd boos as Scott Slugger begins to make his way to the ring, gesturing to the crowd that the World Title will soon be his. As Slugger reaches the ring, Eron begins to exit, stopping only to give Oracle, who is facing Slugger, a vicious kick in the back of his injured leg. Oracle staggers, but turns to face Eron and grabs at him, missing by inches. Slugger takes advantage with a dropkick to the same leg, and Oracle goes down hard. Slugger immediately begins to stomp on the leg of Oracle, laughing as he does so. As Slugger stands over the legs of Oracle to taunt him, Oracle lifts his good leg and nails Slugger in his little Sluggsies. Slugger hunches over, and Oracle quickly grabs his head and rolls him into a small package. Slugger kicks out at two, and Oracle beats him to his feet. Oracle strikes with a running clothesline, followed by another, and finally nails Slugger with a hard leaping back elbow, knocking Slugger down. Oracle covers again, but only gets two. Slugger gets up, and Oracle backs him into the corner. Oracle strikes with a series of chops, each drawing a "whoo!" from the crowd. Finally Slugger begins to slump down in the corner, and Oracle backs off, only to charge back in with a mick foley-type corner charge. Slugger drops to the mat, and Oracle waits for him to get up. As he does, Oracle plants him with a hard DDT, spiking him into the mat. Oracle goes down to the mat, and applies a cross armbreaker on Slugger, looking for a submission. However, Slugger powers over to a position facing Oracle, and grabs him by the waist. In a surprising show of power, Slugger lifts Oracle into the air and blasts him with a falling powerbomb. Oracle is out, but Slugger has to take a moment to recover from the earlier beating, and so when he covers only gets two. Slugger, undaunted, brings Oracle up, and grabs him by the leg. Slugger then twists over and stretches the leg of Oracle with a dragon screw. He repeats this, and after the second time, drops down into a legbar. Oracle comes close to submitting as Eron laughs at him from ringside, but does not. Finally, Oracle manages to reach the ropes...only to have his hands knocked away by Eron before the ref can see this. Oracle appears to be in even more trouble when Slugger grabs his leg and rolls over, lifting up and into a single leg Boston crab. Slugger is working the leg unmercifully, and Eron is enjoying every moment of it. Gathering up what little energy he has left, Oracle stretches desperately for the ropes...and makes it, the ref turning around to see this this time before Eron can knock his hands off. The ref tells Slugger to break the hold, and Slugger does so, thinking that he's won the match. Slugger heads to the turnbuckles and raises his fists high to the boos of the crowd. However, he doesn't notice Oracle slowly getting up and limping towards him. As Slugger poses, Oracle puts his head between his legs and grabs Slugger by the waist, and drives the unsuspecting Slugger to the mat with a powerbomb from the turnbuckles. Slugger is out, but Oracle's leg won't allow him to make it to Slugger to cover. Finally, he does so, and Slugger kicks out at two. Slugger gets to his feet first, and brings Oracle up to his level. Slugger hooks Oracle up, and delivers a powerful rocker dropper onto the mat. With Oracle down, Slugger rolls to the outside as Eron begins to distract the ref. Slugger grabs a chair from ringside, and tosses it into the ring. He places it on Oracle's leg, and begins to climb to the top rope. Despite having the match well in hand, Slugger takes the opportunity to try to cripple his opponent by leaping off with a flying somersault legdrop onto the chair on Oracle's leg. He quickly tosses the chair from the ring, and Eron leaves the ref alone as Slugger applies a figure-four leglock to Oracle. The ref sees this, and notices that Oracle's shoulders are down, and begins to count. One, two, three, and Scott Slugger gains a victory over Oracle. Slugger calls for a microphone, and addresses the crowd. "In case you didn't know, I think it's real clear now- if I did *that* just to make sure I beat Oracle- just think what lengths I'll go to at Twilight to make sure I walk out of there with my *rightful title* around my waist. And after I beat Ice for that belt-", Slugger takes the time to point to a fan at ringside in a Flying Frenchie shirt, "I'm gonna cripple good old Pierre Delacroix. And that's a promise..from the WGA." Slugger throws the microphone aside and begins to make his way back to the locker room to boos, as Eron takes the opportunity to roll into the ring and repeatedly stomp at the leg of Oracle, laughing as he does it. Satisfied, Eron rolls from the ring and leaves as well, leaving only Oracle, who, out of sheer force of will, pulls himself to his feet with the help of the ropes and stares menacingly at Eron... Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall Chapter Three: Strange days Scott Slugger worked his way back up the entrance ramp after his match and ducked past the curtain. There was a small man sitting on a stool behind the curtain, and when he noticed Scott Slugger looking at him he angrily shouted at the World Champion hopeful. "Dammit! Pay no attention to me!" Scott Slugger shrugged, and continued on into the backstage area. He headed to his locker room, and as he walked he heard some strange voices drifting up from down a flight of stairs. Slugger nearly passed the stairs by, but then he heard what sounded like his name from the sound of the voices below. Slugger turned, and after a short pause walked slowly down into the dark of the boiler room. The scene grew darker and darker as he walked down the stairs, and Slugger struggled to see through the dark. He turned a corner on the flight of stairs and came to the base. He found himself before a large room, and in the darkness he recognized a familiar face. Slugger's eyes narrowed. "You." Many minutes passed, and Slugger one again climbed up the stairs to the main arena hallway. Passing Geoff Green in the hallway, Slugger looked at him thoughtfully. "You know Geoff... he's right." Geoff shook his head, not understanding what Scott Slugger meant by his cryptic comment. Whistling he opened a door to the main arena entrance, and his voice sunk to an inaudible whisper. Inside the room stood Rain, surrounded by hundreds... or thousands... of familiar but twisted wrestlers. Walking toward Geoff, Rain carried the Internet title over his shoulder. Geoff stammered in a harsh whisper, "My god... this is madness." Rain smiled beneath dark glasses. "Oh yes. Yes it is." Rain walked past him, and headed for the ring. Rain (c) vs. Black Panther The music of the Millennium Men begins first, as Black Panther makes his way to the ring to a loud ovation from the crowd. He mounts the turnbuckle and raises his fists, getting a very positive reaction. "Symphony Of Destruction" begins to play, and the cheers turn to boos as Rain enters the arena, Internet title around his waist. He is accompanied by many alternate fWo wrestlers- the Millennium Men, Eron, Rain, Scott Slugger, the Flying Frenchie, Cactus Jon, and the Underground. This ominous procession follows Rain's slow march to the ring, and Rain takes a microphone as he reaches the squared circle. "Black Panther, take heed. The Gods of the Storm have declared that this shall be a lumberjack match, and as it is their will, so it shall be." He tosses the mic away, and the ref shrugs, signaling that it indeed shall be a lumberjack match. Rain rolls into the ring, and Panther attacks quickly with a series of stomps to the head and back, before bringing Rain up. He tosses Rain into the ropes, and catches him coming off with a fist to the midsection. Panther goes behind Rain, and sends him down with a russian legsweep. Panther gets one before Rain kicks out with authority. Rain gets up, but is met by a series of stinging right hands from the Panther, sending him back into the corner. Panther takes a few steps back, and charges in with a spear, flattening Rain. Rain collapses from the corner, and Panther covers, but only gets two. Panther gets up and drops a quick elbow on Rain, before mounting the second rope. He leaps off, driving the point of the elbow into the throat. He covers for two. Panther brings Rain up, and bounces off the ropes, sending Rain down to the mat hard. Panther heads up top, and prepares a top-rope splash. However, unbeknownst to the ref, the alternate Slugger shoves him from behind and Panther crashes into the mat, hitting hard. Rain covers, and gets two before Panther kicks out. Rain brings Panther to his feet, and throws him into the ropes. He catches Panther coming off with a big powerslam, and lifts him to his feet once more. Rain goes behind Panther, and hooks him up, delivering a huge pumphandle slam, driving Panther into the mat. Rain covers, but only gets two. Panther begins to slowly get back to his feet, and is shoved into the corner by Rain, who begins choking him with a boot. At the ref's count of 5, Rain breaks the hold, and begins arguing with the ref. At this, the alternate Underground jumps onto the apron and begins pounding on the Panther with clubbing forearm blows. They quickly jump down when Rain disengages himself from the ref and returns to the corner to ram a knee into the gut of Panther. He tries this again, but Panther, out of desperation, rolls to the side and pulls Rain into the corner by the tights, slamming him shoulder-first into the steel pole outside the ring. Rain staggers back, and Panther quickly strikes from behind, grabbing Rain by the arm and executing a painful fujiwara armbar, threatening to snap the arm of the Internet champ. Rain screams in pain, unable to break the hold. He uses his extreme power to inch towards the ropes...but finds that he can't make it far enough to reach the ropes. Panther grinds down on the arm, looking to become a 3-time Internet champion. Rain is on the verge of tapping out...but the alternate Fang and Havok jump onto the ring apron, diverting the attention of the ever-so-stupid ref. As he tries to get them down, the alternate Jon produces a string of barbed wire, and begins to wrap it around his fist. He then slams the Panther in the forehead with the barbed wire, causing him to release the hold. It is clear that Panther has begun to bleed from the mask, and the alternate Frenchie quickly rolls into the ring and rolls Rain onto Black Panther. At this, the alternate Millennium Men hop off the apron, and the ref turns to see Rain covering the Panther. He counts one...two...but at the last moment, Panther lifts his shoulder and kicks out. Panther begins slowly getting up, and so does Rain. Rain clears his head first, and charges at Panther, who ducks a clothesline and applies the armbar again, but Rain quickly maneuvers out before Panther can lock it in, and grabs the still-on-the-mat Panther from behind, lifting him up from the mat and spiking him with a release German suplex. Rain quickly covers, but Panther kicks out at two and two/thirds. Rain can't believe it, and brings Panther up to his feet, setting him up for a Rainmaker powerbomb to finish it off. He lifts, but in mid-air Panther shifts his weight to land butt-first onto Rain, and reaches back quickly to hook a leg. Rain just manages to kick out at the count of two, and he is the first to his feet. As Panther gets up, Rain picks him up in a sidewalk slam position, holding him in the air and charging into the corner, ramming Panther in back-first with incredible force. Unfortunately, the referee is in the way, and gets sandwiched between Panther, Rain, and the turnbuckles. Meanwhile, Rain slashes his thumb across his throat, and sets Panther up on the turnbuckles, looking for a top-rope Rainmaker. However, as he climbs up, Panther hits him with a low blow, causing Rain to fall backwards off the turnbuckle. Panther prepares to fly off onto Rain, but at that moment, the alternate Panther crotches him on the top turnbuckle, and quickly climbs up. The alternate Panther hooks up the real one, and drives him into the mat with the Pounce, a devastating superplex. The alternate Panther quickly picks up Rain and drapes him over the Panther, and begins to revive the ref. The ref slowly comes to, and begins to count- one...two.....three, and Rain has retained the Internet title. He struggles to his feet, supported by the alternate wrestlers, and is handed a microphone by the ring attendant. "Now..you can see...that with this great power behind me...I am unstoppable. Mark my words..before this year ends...the earth will see...destruction on a grander scale than any have ever seen before." Rain throws down the microphone and raises the Internet title to the boos of the fans, and begins to slowly walk to the back as the Millennium Men make their way to the ring to tend to their fallen partner and Warfare continues.. Result: Rain by Pinfall Chapter Four: Girls being girls "Okay girls... we got smoked out of the main staging room, but we can practice in here. Okay... everyone got your costumes on?" The girls jumped up and down vacuously, bells attached to their puffy pink boots jingling. The dance director smiled, and raised his hands in the air. "Hoo-ray! Okay Warfare Girls, let's sound off!" The first girl bounced forward, her blond hair tussled up in a blond explosion from the back of her head. She beamed a white beaming smile and shouted "We are the Warbear Girls! I'm Kiotus!" She shuffled to one side as a large chested red-haired girl came bounding into the front. Saluting and obviously very cold, the girl sang, "My name was... hee... I mean IS... Feellatia!" She retreated as a brown haired girl skipped into the front and winked at the dance director while slapping her own butt. "Hi! I'm I.B. Ezee!" The three girls continued to dance up and down on each leg, bobbing and shaking. A door slammed from the back of the room and the sound of jingling bells came nearer. These bells sounded angry, and were preceded by stomping feet. Nightshade came into view and glared at the dance director. "I'm Nightshade. I hate all of you." The dance director clapped excitedly and gathered the girls together. "That's great, all of you! We are going to debut on Warfare next week, so just be ready to perform your little heart out. Now, Kiotus, work on your bounce. Feellatia... try your hair up. Ezee... have you looked into those puffy armbands I mentioned? Oh... and Nightshade... have you considered getting some bigger breasts?" Outside the Warfare Girls tryout room various wrestlers walked by on their way to various parts of the arena. Joy was headed for the ring when he heard the screams coming from the Warfare Girls tryout room, and looked over with surprise. "Man... those girls really give it all they've got..." Joy vs. Darkness Joy was already in the ring, trying to lead the crowd in a sing-a-long from one of the turnbuckles, when Warfare returned from it's commercial break. The arena dimmed as a whole, and the sounds of soft voices speaking filled the void. Slowly emerging onto the entrance ramp - to a huge reaction from the fans... good or bad, it's hard to tell - is the man known as Darkness, making his return to the fWo. He slowly stalks his way to the ring, never losing focus of his opponent. Stepping through the ropes, Darkness stands dead center of the ring, arms crossed, with a maniacal smile crossing his face. The arena fully lights up again, just in time for the ref to read off the instructions to both men. Once this is settled, the bell rings, and both men stand there, Joy with his trademark grin, and Darkness with a puzzling smirk. Suddenly, Darkness lunges into Joy with fists and boots, getting the loss. But the bell sounding doesn't stop Darkness, as he continues his rampage. He beats Joy back into a turnbuckle where he proceeds to kick him in the face over and over. Grabbing Joy by his tights, Darkness heaves him back into the middle of the ring. Following up, Darkness lifts Joy into a northern lights suplex, but flips him over at the high point, dropping him in a modified version of a northern lights bomb. Joy lands hard on the back of his neck, and ceases all movement. As the ref announces Joy the winner, Darkness makes his way back to the locker rooms, satisfied with his showing. Result: Joy by Happy Lisiado Guerrero vs. Death Strange Spanish music began to play, and a man dressed in an elaborate luchador costume walked out onto the entrance ramp. He wore a mask with giant horns, and some kind of red and green fins lined his back. He flipped into the ring and raised his arms, being announced as "Lisiado Guerrero". He breathed some kind of mist, and did cartwheels around the ring. Careful fWo viewers recognized the new luchador as Mike Heftel, and shook their heads at this latest effort. One fan turned to another, whispering, "Hey... wasn't he supposed to get some kind of title match against Havok and Chris Universal? Whatever happened to that storyline?" The other fan shrugged, "Who the hell knows man... I hear the booker comes up with this crap in his car on the way to his real job." Suddenly the lights in the arena blew out, and Death came to the ring, followed by Fallen, Poison, and Demon. The four marched to the ring, and slid inside, fires blazing from the ringposts. Lisiado Guerrero backed up against the ropes and launched himself at Death with a flying plancha. Death caught him, and snapped him up and around, dropping him on his head. The other Dark Riders fell on him, attacking with a clumsy viciousness that had not been seen since the last time Steve Williams was in town. The bell rang, and the match was awarded to Death via "severe beating". As the Dark Riders left the ring Death pointed toward the camera and laughed, "Six-a days.... only six-a days now." Inside the ring Lisiado Guerrero weakly asked the referee why he didn't get the DQ decision. The referee shrugged, and reminded him that he was a luchador as the cameras went to the back... Result: Death by severe beating Chapter Five: Fights Scott Slugger walked down the arena hallway, looking thoughtful and reflective. A door to a broom closet opened, and Scott Slugger moved to avoid it. In doing so, he was forced directly into the path of Ice, and the two men looked hard into each other's eyes. Ice cocked a fist back and swung, punching Scott Slugger in the head. Slugger toppled back into an arena wall, and gave a shout of surprise. He rebounded and went after Ice, both men swinging and shoving in their anger. Chris Universal appeared on the scene and attempted to separate the two, with little success. Ice and Slugger grabbed Universal and swung him into a COKE machine that was nearby. The brawl continued until fWo security physically separated the two, pushing them apart and backward. Slugger spat at Ice, shouting, "That's it you big stupid retard... the Dynasty is dead... hear me? Dead! You just made it personal you big ass... this Friday that little belt of yours is mine bitch, so..." Scott Slugger continued to rant, and Ice was so focused on trying to break free of the security guards to go after Slugger that he didn't notice Chris Universal get to his feet. A look of rage on his face and a stream of blood running from one lip, Universal lunged at Ice, clocking him with a solid punch to the face. Universal continued to hammer on Ice as fWo security attempted to deal with this new threat. Scott Slugger was shoved away by security, and as he looked at the his two former teammates brawling with each other he gritted his teeth. "Fools... none of this is going to matter in a few days anyway... when I'm champion." Slugger shuffled away, and in doing so failed to notice the Frenchie who was watching the scene from the doorway of the broom closet. The Frenchie smiled next to the door that he had opened to divert Scott Slugger's path. "Zis is excellent... zese Americ'n fools are easier to manipul'te zan can be... non... zis is like ze pie. Easier zan ze pie. Yes. Ze pie. Hmmm... pie... Frenchie would like ze pie..." The Frenchie wandered off in search of pie as cameras went to the ring for the main event... Underground vs. Canadian Connection With a shot at the fWo Tag Team titles hanging in the balance, the sealed cage that was hanging from the rafters slowly descended to the arena floor. The sight of the steel monstrosity enclosing the ring filled the arena with a sense of excitement and dread, and when the music of the Canadian Connection began to play the crowd stood on their feet and gave them a massive reception. Cactus Jon led Tom and the Ninja to the ring with the Canadian flag flying overhead. Jon stopped to shake hands with some fans in the front row that waved a Canadian flag of their own, as the Connection climbed inside the ring and readied themselves for the coming war. As if on cue, the howl of the Underground theme came into the arena, and Mike Bear, Wretch and Fear came out onto the ramp. Lights shone on the trio, and a thin wall of fire erupted from the head of the entrance ramp. The three walked through it, steam and smoke drifting off them as they made their way to the ring. Mike Bear entered the ring with Wretch close behind, and before the door was locked and chained the battle was on. Wretch grabbed the head of the Ninja and planted him face first into the mat, as Bear attempted to sling Tom into the steel behind the ropes, but a quick thinking reversal by the member of the Canadian Connection sent Bear face-first into the steel instead. Tom followed up, trapping Bear between the ropes and the side of the cage, and moved in with methodically timed punches. Wretch meanwhile was having his troubles with the Ninja, who had applied a chokehold on the member of the Underground. Cactus Jon rattled the cage from the outside, and the referee inside the ring checked to see if Wretch was still conscious. Still trapped between the ropes and the cage wall, Mike Bear began to climb his way up the side of the cage, attempting to battle back from the educated kicks coming from Tom. Bear got to the top of the cage and hung from the cage ceiling, and began to lash out with wild kicks to the head of Tom. Only a few hit their target, but it was enough to take Tom off the offensive. Reeling backward from this attack, Tom stumbled toward the ropes, and Bear locked his legs around the neck of the Canadian. Bear screamed, his upper body showing great strain and he continued to choke out Tom with the hanging leg scissors. Wretch had meanwhile reached the cage wall, and flipped the Ninja up and over his back so the former Hardcore champion collided with the cage wall. Wretch stepped backward, trying to get a breath of air and clear his head from the earlier hold. The Ninja came at him with quick jabs, but Wretch went to the mat and grabbed the legs of the Ninja. The Ninja toppled, and Wretch quickly stood and went to the second rope, coming off with a dropped elbow to the back. During this Tom was nearly unconscious, and in a fit of desperation he grabbed the legs of Mike Bear that were wrapped around his neck. Holding fast to his legs, Tom dropped to his knees. Bear held for a moment, but then the strain was too great and his fingers tore free from the cage and he toppled to the mat on top of Tom, head snapping on the table of body and mat. Bear reached up to crack his jaw back into place, and left a bloody handprint on his chin. He looked at his hands, seeing wide ribbons of blood from where the cage had torn into flesh. Tom struggled wearily to his feet and began to kick at the head of Bear, stomping him down to the mat. On the other side of the ring and the cage Fear shouted encouragement to Wretch, who was climbing to the top turnbuckle, and then up the cage until he could climb no farther because of the cage ceiling. Wretch timed his jump, waiting until the Ninja started to climb to his feet. Wretch pitched himself from the side of the cage, but the Ninja saw him coming and drove a fist upward into the throat of the member of the Underground. Wretch reeled backward, clutching at his throat and gasping for air. Wretch began to convulse, and the Ninja paced to the other side of the ring and helped Tom batter Mike Bear. Outside the cage the crowd came alive as the Dark Riders made a second appearance, Death leading Fallen, Poison and Demon to ringside. Fear and Jon bravely went after them, but doubled up they were no match for the Dark Riders who bore steel weapons in their grip. The Dark Riders surrounded the ring, and Death directed Fallen and Demon to attach metal hooks to the side of the cage. The hooks were attached to a chain, and Death looped this around a hitching post on a equipment truck. Sensing it was time to finish things, Tom covered Mike Bear as the referee started the three count. They were interrupted by the fire of an engine, and then the rending sound of steel, as one side of the steel wall crumbled and then tore free from the cage. Laughing at this victory, the Dark Riders stormed into the cage, at once attacking Tom and the Ninja. Fallen and Demon picked up the Ninja and spiked him into the mat as Poison punched at the groin of Tom. Cactus Jon had stood on the outside and now attacked Death from behind, and the Dark Riders moved to once again naturalize this threat. Still choking, Wretch fell upon the prone body of the Ninja, and the frightened referee quickly made the three count and ran from the scene. Wretch helped Bear and even his opponent Tom and the Ninja to their feet, and then moved to help Cactus Jon on the outside. As they moved to attack the lights in the arena dimmed, and Scott Collins and his assortment of Dark Riders ran to ringside and at once fell upon Spencer. A massive brawl broke out at ringside, and attempts by fWo security to contain the situation proved futile as they were quickly dispatched by the wrestlers at ringside. Primetime, Courage and the Flying Frenchie walked to the ring to get involved in the fight, but were met by the Boston Strangler, and soon after Jake and Austin. Scott Slugger ran from the back to attack the Frenchie, and Ice and Universal were soon involved as well. Eron, Oracle, the Black Panther and most of the fWo locker room emptied out to ringside, with all of the wrestlers exchanging punches and kicks, and the fans at ringside running for safety out of fear for their lives. As the battle waged onward, rumbling sounds came from the back, and several wrestlers turned their attention to the entrance ramp. Rain appeared alone at the top of the ramp, and he threw his head back and uttered a deep laugh that bordered on complete madness. From behind him came hordes of the strangely familiar doppelgangers, and as screams of horror broke out from the crowd the cameras faded to black... Wednesday Warfare coming to a close with a final reminder that fWo Twilight was live on Pay Per View this Friday... Christmas Eve. Result: Underground by Pinfall
