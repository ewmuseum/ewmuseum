Sunday Slaughter, 1 August 1999

1 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction A limosine pulled up to the back of the arena, and out stepped Scott Slugger and Ice. The pair carried their bags over their shoulders, and looked ready to conduct some serious business. Pushing fWo officals aside as they passed, Slugger was overheard saying that he was there to get his match signed, and get his title... and that was all. Slugger then looked at Ice and mentioned that the fWo still owed him seventy five grand... and he intended to get that tonight as well. As the pair entered the arena, Machina walked up to them, and tossed his bag at Ice. Ice caught it with a surprised look on his face, and Machina pointed down the hall... telling him that his dressing room was number thirty four. Machina then looked at Slugger, and pointed to his belly. Machina told Slugger that if he wanted to continue working for the promotion, he needed to stay in shape. Slugger started to advance on Machina, but he had already turned and was heading in the opposite direction... yelling at some fWo officals to clean up the hallways. Slugger looked annoyed, but then he and Ice continued on their original path to their dressing room. Ice tossed Machina's bag into a nearby garbage bin as they walked. Darkness vs. Mad Frenchie The lights went completely dark, and fans looked hard for him on the entrance ramp. He didn't come from there however, he came from the crowd, tossing fans from side to side and cracking the heads of children as he made his way to the ring. Inside he bellowed loudly, shouting and raising his fists into the sky. The Mad Frenchie made his way out onto the entrance ramp, dressed in his dark clothing and beret. He cursed in french as he walked, and backhanded a few fans as he went. Inside the ring Darkness looked at the Mad Frenchie with a smile on his face, and cocked his head to one side in mild amusement. The amusement quickly ended when the Mad Frenchie spat in his face and delivered a deadly double axehandle to the top of his head. The Mad Frenchie then followed it up with an uppercut, striking again and again at the head of the large masked man. The Mad Frenchie then went to the top rope, but it took a long time to get there, and Darkness was on his feet after him before he got to the top. Darkness grabbed the Mad Frenchie on the top rope and tossed him to the floor, slamming him into the steel barriers. The Mad Frenchie struggled to his feet, but Darkness was on him, slamming his head again and again into the steel steps. Darkness then ripped the beret off his head, yelling at him to get old of himself, and rolled him back into the ring. Darkness attempted a legdrop, but the Mad Frenchie easily avoided it and went back up to the top rope. He went for the 450 splash, but was too large and too slow to execute it successfully, and fell to the mat. Darkness capitalized, piledriving the Mad Frenchie and stalking from the ring after the count. Looking at the camera, Darkness told the viewers at home that there would be no rest until all of the fWo... was in darkness. Result: Darkness by Pinfall Trouble. Backstage Matt Loco opened his mail, pausing to look at a large package. Opening it, a videotape slid out, and Loco looked at it curiously. Loco popped the video into a nearby VCR, and for a moment static filled the screen. Then the screen was dark, and for a moment Loco thought there was nothing on the tape. As his finger was about to hit the STOP button, he heard the low moans. Looking back at the screen he could make out a faint, shadowy shape in the distance. It appeared to be seated, and tied down. The moans came from this shape, which as the camera drew closer was obviously male. Suddenly a click was heard, and a small bulb hanging from a string on the roof came alive with light. For an instant the room was visable, a small, dirty room with bloody streaks on the walls. In the middle of it sat John Skyler, bound with rope and blindfolded. Then the light was hit with something, and it began to spin wildly around the room, sending John into bright light and total darkness with each swing. In the brief moments when Skyler was visible, a form was seen to draw closer... and closer. Whoever it was it was impossible to see in the brief light provided... but it his or her hands were a large pair of steel hedge clippers. As the form got closer the clippers were raised toward John's neck... and then the light was pitched into total darkness. Then the screams began. Shaking, Loco pressed the STOP button, not able to see any more. Speaking softly first, then yelling, Loco screamed for Willis, leaving the tape in the VCR. As he left the room, an unseen figure entered, grabbed the tape, and left silently... Machina vs. Falcon "Come With Me" begins to play, and out walks Machina. Machina tells the fans that they are in for a helluva show, as he, the president of the fWo, has booked a good card for them to enjoy. Machina makes his way up onto the ring apron before telling the referee to hold the ropes open for him. The referee ignores him and Machina tells him "next time you disobey me, I'll fire your ass faster than you can count to 3." Machina tells the referee he wants him to call it right down the middle. Falcon makes his way out onto the stage, and looks at the crowd mightily, before making his way down the ramp. He rolls into the ring and stares at Machina, who has a cocky smile on his face. Machina asks the referee to get him a microphone. The referee refuses, so Machina tells him he's fired. He then goes and gets a microphone himself, and fires the ring announcer. The announcer and the referee just shrug, as Machina instructs Falcon to lay down for him. Machina says he's the new boss around here, and what he says goes. Machina demands that Falcon lay down for him and put him over, like any other good employee would. Machina says that if Falcon doesn't put him over, he'll fire him and the rest of the Underground. Falcon sighs, before laying down. Machina smiles cockily, and says "You see that? I have power. You don't." Machina then prepares to cover Falcon. As he pins him, he instructs the referee to count. The referee counts, 1... 2... but Falcon quickly grabs Machina' head and rolls him over into a cradle. Machina kicks out at two, and both men quickly get to their feet. Machina charges at Falcon with a spinning heel kick, but Falcon ducks. Machina crashes into the mat, and Falcon pulls him back to his feet. He nails him with some side kicks, before whipping him to the ropes. Falcon ducks for a backdrop, but Machina stops and says "That's it! You're fired! You didn't put me over, so get the hell out of here... and take that stupid dog Muggsy with you!" Quickly, Mike Bear & Wretch make their way down to ringside. Machina goes outside the ring and begins arguing with them, as the referee lays down a ten count. Machina tells them they're both fired, and they should clean out their lockers immediatley. Before he knows it, he's been counted out. Falcon goes outside, and tells Machina "you need to get off the prozac, man." before he and the rest of the Underground leave. Machina angrily goes back into the ring and pushes the referee around, before jumping up and down throwing a bit of a temper tantrum. He then asks "where did my posse go? WHERE DID THEY GO?! I want ANSWERS!" Everyone simply ignores him, and Slaughter continues on as usual... Result: Falcon by Count Out Signing The commissioner came to the ring, holding in his hands a series of contracts. Inside the ring the commissioner called out Scott Slugger, and then Cactus Jon. The two wrestlers entered at the top of the entrance ramp together, uncomfortable being in close proximity to one another and Slugger eyeing the World title consideringly. Inside the ring the commissioner handed Scott Slugger the contract, and Slugger looked it over carefully. Looking up at the commissioner with a clever look in his eye, Scott Slugger took out a pen and added something to the contract, and then signed his name to it. He handed it back to the commissioner, who read the addition and raised an eyebrow and then handed it over to Jon, telling Slugger "only if he agrees to it". Jon read the contract, and smiled a smug smile at Slugger. He signed his name and said, "I just called your bluff you stupid bastard. Hotter than Hell... the champion Cactus Jon and the challenger Scott Slugger... lumberjack match." The crowd began to cheer, and Cactus Jon locked his gaze with Scott Slugger, with neither man giving any quarter. Rain vs. Mike Bear As we return from the commercials, Mike Bear appears on stage, already making his walk down the entrance ramp. "Psycho Man" blares in the background, as Bear, minus Muffin or the rest of the Underground, climbs into the ring. Bear waits as his opponent Rain makes his way out. The massive Rain walks out onto the stage, and he points his finger at Bear. Rain motions for Bear to "come get some", and Bear has no problem doing so. Bear climbs through the ropes, and yells, before charging at Rain. He takes Rain down with a double-leg takedown. He then begins hammering on Rain furiously. Rain gets a shot in edgewise, and then Rain goes on top of Bear and hammers him. The referee tries to get the action in the ring but Rain pushes him out of the way. Rain presses Bear high overhead and drops him throat first on the guardrail. Bear staggers out and Rain takes him out with a reverse knife edge chop. Rain repeats the process two or three more times, before propping up Bear on his back and hitting a Samoan Drop on the ramp. A thud is heard throughout the arena and possibly in another state. Bear appears to have been damn near crippled as he lays in pain, clutching his stomach. Rain gets on his feet and then grabs Bear, and brings him down near ringside. He throws Bear back first into the ring, but Bear reverses it. Rain hits the ring instead, and as he staggers out, Bear kicks him in the midsection, and then hits a swinging neckbreaker on the cement floor. The referee continues to try to get the action back in the ring, but it appears these two rivals won't have any part of it. Bear tosses the referee aside. Bear grabs the ring steps and drives them hard into the head of Rain. Rain is busted open immediately, and Bear just laughs as he continues to dish out more punishment. Bear rolls Rain into the ring, before reaching underneath the ring and pulling out a table. He slides that into the ring as well... before climbing into the ring himself. He props the table up in the corner. Bear throws Rain into the opposite turnbuckles and prepares to Irish Whip him into the corner with the table. As he's about to, he spots the referee standing in front of the table, as if he's not going to allow Bear to do that. Bear changes his mind, and kicks Rain in the stomach several times... before hitting a diamond cutter on Rain. Instead of going for a cover, he goes outside the ring and grabs a chair. He throws it into the ring. He grabs another chair, and throws it into the ring. Both chairs land by each other... Bear mouths off to the fans for a moment, before rolling back into the ring. Rain is attempting to get to his feet, and does a few seconds later. The referee attempts to pick up the chairs, but Bear grabs one and he and the referee have a tug of war over it. Rain attempts to grab the other chair. With both men pulling on the chairs, the referee has no choice but to let go. Both men have chairs in their possession, but the referee tells them that if they use them, they'll be disqualified. Rain and Bear look at each other, and both of them swing the chairs... but at the referee. Both steel chairs hit the referee square in the head, and the referee falls to the mat immediately. Bear then hurries and nails Rain with a shot to the head, before taking the big man and whipping him into the turnbuckles once again. He then prepares to whip Rain into the corner with the table... he does so, but Rain reverses it. Bear reverses it with a counter into a hiptoss, and Rain does the same so Bear goes flying into the table. The table splits in half and Rain looks pleased. Rain throws Bear out onto the floor and the two take their fight into the crowd... both men pummel each other with punches, until they make it so far up into the crowd they're near several skyboxes. They fight into an actual skybox, and several people run for their lives as the action continues. Suddenly, Rain throws Bear through the glass through the skybox, and Bear goes crashing into a group full of fans! A small "fWo" chant erupts as Rain, satisifed by his action, turns around to exit the skybox... but Rain is nailed by an awaiting Crucifix with the Hardcore Title! Crucifix looks down upon Rain, before exiting the skybox quickly as Slaughter... goes to a commercial break in an attempt to restore order. Result: No Contest Another challenge Kid Cool came to the ring, holding his own microphone. Officals started to block his path, but Cool motioned them aside telling them that it was time for him to win the quarter hour ratings. Cool stepped into the ring, and called out to the fans in the arena. "Who wants to see Kid Cool wrestle? Well... it's not going to happen tonight... but I'm offering a big challenge. Death? Some people backstage seem to think that you're a big badass... but not me. So I'm offering this challenge for Wednesday Warfare. You... and me. In the ring. Just to make it fair I'll have one arm tied behind my back. Still not fair you say? Well how about this... any of your Dark Riders can interfere in the match at any time. Sound fair? Then show up at Warfare punk... you and me." With that, Cool left the ring, leaving the audence laughing at the prospect of the upcoming match. Stone (c) vs. Nikki the Valley Girl Nikki the Valley Girl came to the ring looking sad, asking the cameras from time to time where her Primetime was. She got into the ring and did some clumsy stretching excercises. Tiring quickly, Nikki reached inside her bag and began painting her nails, and then spraying her hair. Stone came to the ring, and quickly got inside, grabbing a microphone as she went. She yelled out to Kid Cool, screaming, "Hey kid! We accept your challenge... and I'll kick your ass myself!" She was obviously enraged, and spun backward to face Nikki. Nikki was spraying her hair and as Stone turned she bumped Nikki's arm, sending the hairspray into her eyes. Stone reeled backward, and tripped, falling backward. Nikki reached for the bottle of hairspray, and tripped herself, falling atop Stone. The ref made the count, and awarded the Womens Title to Nikki. Nikki seemed happy with this new bit of jewelry, and walked from the ring asking where Primetime was, because they needed to celebrate. Primetime didn't show however, and Nikki walked sadly back to the dressing room alone. As she got to the top of the entrance ramp, the Speed Demon appeared holding flowers. He congratulated her, and the pair walked backstage together. Inside the ring, Stone began to scream, and grabbed the referee, raking his eyes and bloodying his face, and then driving his head into the steel turnbuckle again and again... as Slaughter went to commercial. Result: Nikki the Valley Girl by Pinfall Meltdown draws Inside the ring Chris Finn held a small card, and called out to the audence. He announced that he would be giving out three teams tonight for Meltdown, and there were what they were. First, there was the team of RAIN and PLAGUE. Second, there was the team of ICE and the MAD TRUCKER. Finally, there was the team of WRETCH and VL THA MURDERA. With these names announced, Chris Finn left the ring, and the fans prepared themselves for the final match of the night... Doomsday vs. Dark Riders Slaughter returns to a totally dark arena, before Death & War make their way out. The crowd jeers the two massive men, as Death tells the crowd that tonight they're taking home the fWo World Tag Team Titles. Death raises his massive arms and the lights come back on, before he laughs maniacally as he and War climb into the ring. Death and War taunt the fans from inside the ring, before the fWo World Tag Team Champions Stevie Roberts & VL Tha Murdera make their way out. Roberts tells the crowd that he's going to "shag the dark riders like they've never been shagged before." Roberts holds the ropes open for VL, and as soon as the duo makes their way into the ring, Death & War attack relentlessly. Death clotheslines Roberts over the top rope, but also follows him out with a version of the 'cactus clothesline'. Meanwhile, War grabs VL by the neck and prepares to chokeslam her. He lifts her up into the air and slams her hard down into the mat. He hooks the leg for a cover, but gets a two count only. War brings her to her feet and whips her into the turnbuckles, as Death finally makes his way to his corner. VL staggers out and War takes her down with a powerslam. War doesn't hook the leg, and VL kicks out at one and a half. War, realizes his mistake, and tries again... this time with a hook of the leg. Kickout by VL at 2. War hammers VL with some punches before bouncing off the ropes and hitting a legdrop. War drags VL into the corner and slams her head first into the turnbuckles, then tags in Death. War and Death swing VL to the ropes... VL returns and War backdrops her into the waiting arms of Death, who then catches her and powerbombs her. A cover by Death, but Roberts runs in and delivers a shot to the head of Death, which breaks up the pinfall. The referee escorts Roberts out of the ring, and Death shrugs off the headshot. Death brings VL and whips her to the ropes. VL returns and Death kicks her in the stomach and goes for a vertical suplex. VL, however, escapes and falls behind him and takes him out with a reverse ddt. VL is still reeling from the effects of the punishment dished out, and she crawls as quickly as possible to tag in Roberts. Roberts comes in and stomps Death in the midsection area. Roberts repeats the process, and then bounces off the ropes and nails Death in the head with a sitting dropkick. A cover, but a kickout by Death. Roberts brings Death up and rakes him in the eye, before piledriving him in the center of the ring. A pinfall attempt, but War comes in and pulls Roberts off by a leg. Roberts gets up and shouts at War, who attempts to kill him. The referee steps in the way and tells War to get out of the ring. Meanwhile, this gives VL & Stevie a chance to double-team Death. They take him down with a double-team brainbuster in the middle of the ring. This enrages War, who comes back into the ring again. Stevie & VL whip Death to the ropes, and when he returns, they duck for a double-backdrop. He counters it, DDTing both of them into the mat. Death attempts to get up... but VL holds Death by the leg. Death reaches back and nails her with a shot to the jaw, before crawling over into the corner... he makes the tag to War. VL & Stevie get to their feet and War takes them down with a double-clothesline. War brings Stevie to his feet and throws him over the top rope. He then gorilla press' VL into the air... and chucks her in the direction of Stevie, who catches her. Both of them go flying down onto the floor. War then turns around and checks on Death, who is in the corner attempting to make a recovery. Suddenly, The Hollywood Blondes come down to ringside. "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal jumps up on the ring apron and distracts the referee. Lance Star climbs into the ring, he waits for War to turn around, before nailing him with The Shooting Star (rock bottom) in the middle of the ring. Universal helps VL up into the ring, and Star places her ontop of War for the victory. Universal tells the referee to turn around as a pinfall is occuring... the referee counts the pinfall, and VL & Stevie retain the titles. The Blondes leave as quickly as they entered, and Roberts comes in and helps VL up... the two leave to the back still champions as Death climbs to his feet. With a look of disgust on his face, he stares at War as he stands above him. He demands War to get to get up off his ass. Death & War exchange words before Death slaps him, and tells him to know his role. Stone, Plague & Scott Taylor come down to ringside and make sure cooler heads prevail... the Dark Riders exit the ring as a unit... but the question remains, why did Universal and Star cause them to lose? Result: Doomsday by Pinfall
