Introduction Returned from their trip on the islands of paradise, the fWo journeys to the old west, Arizona, New Mexico, Utah... often overlooked by professional wrestling, the fWo will celebrate summer with outdoor venues and big road shows. But the threats and dangers always loom ever closer... The Unforseen Areumna'derin Rahl pulled on the car door handle and exited his vehicle. Tonight was a big night. Tonight he looked for a second chance, another opportunity to make the most of his spot on the fWo roster. He walked along the ridge of the back parking lot where the pavement met a patch of grass. The spot they gave him was so far away from arena the thing looked like a toy. Even in the middle of August, it was getting a little chilly after nightfall. He put his hands in his pockets and began his walk. He was arriving a bit later than the other performers so the place was quiet and unpopulated, and it gave Rahl some time to think. He was down, but he wasn't out. In the recent past he had been little more than a bit player in the fWo in his own mind. But he knew he could change that. He knew that if he could get another shot, he'd make the most of it. He was now daydreaming, but he was jarred from his fantasies as he ran his hand across his left shoulder and realized that something was missing. Rahl turned around and began walking back to his car to retrieve his forgotten gym bag. He had been so concerned with how Silver Fox would respond to his plea for another shot at singles glory that he had left his wrestling attire behind. As he meandered about the parking lot a second time nothing looked familiar. He looked back to the building and at the cars around him. For another minute he walked farther and farther from the arena, and was starting to get annoyed. Had he made a wrong turn? Was his car even parked in this lot? "Son of a bitch..." he muttered under his breath. He felt angry, he felt lost, he felt frustrated. And then he felt a hand reach across his face from behind. Rahl's head jerked backwards as he fainted, falling in a slump on the concrete pavement. He was down... and out. To HELL with wrestling... Earlier in the day... Silver Fox had been at the arena for hours. First, a meeting with Samuel Smooth that, went fairly well, he'd later say, then production, pyro checks ... soundcheck. It was pushing six o'clock, yet he was already exhausted. How he'd make it through the night's show was beyond him, but he knew he had to. Had to lead by example, show these youngster's what work ethic really is he mumbled as he entered his office and fell into his chair. His phone rang. "Ah hell," he quietly cursed, as he went to answer it. "Hello..." "Fox," it was Triple X, Silver Fox recognized his voice. "Just thought I'd let you know that I'm not wrestling tonight, I won't be at the arena tonight ... and, depending on how things are, where I'm going, I may miss Monday's show as well." Fox was startled, but then calmed as he knew exactly what this was all about. "Sean... Ms. McGinnis is a tough girl. A fighter... tougher than most of the men on the roster. If those bullets were strong enough to kill her, and her fighting spirit, I don't think you of all people showing up there will stop it." "Did I ASK you for your opinion on what showing up would do?" He didn't even give him a chance to respond. "No. ...I'm going to where she is, you don't understand, I have to be there. ...there's so many unresolved issues ... so many things we need to say that we haven't said. Maybe it's cool for somebody like you to just brush her off to the side, but--" Fox interrupted. "--you can't be implying that I don't care about the well being of Ivy, because I, just like you, do. But, it'd be impossible for me to cancel the entire show to be at her bed-side ... you must understand the position I'm in." "I do. ...but, you've gotta understand the position I am in. I'm sure the rest of the roster wants the night off, too. Ivy was friendly to everyone, save Erik Kelly. But, I'm ... not ... everybody." "You're right, Sean. You're Sean Stevens ... Triple X. Ivy's ex-boyfriend." That hit hard, Stevens wanted to say something, perhaps something inappropriate. Where did he get off talking to him like that? Who did he think he was? Sean swallowed hard, kept his cool, and spoke slow, and calm, into the receiver. "Fox ... you can fire me. You can strip me of my title. Do your thing. I won't be in the arena tonight, and that's that." Trip was about to hang up, Silver Fox sensed that, "OKAY ... okay! That's fine. You can have the night off, the rest of the week even. But, I need you at the arena on Monday. The office has big plans for you and the Internet Title. We've been impressed how you've stepped up as a champion, and want to give you bigger, better opponents to showcase your talent. How's that sound?" "I really don't care right now," Sean said. "You've got Deacon next Monday. We need you here." Fox tried again. "Sean. ...we need you here." Click... Silver Fox stared at the receiver, before hanging it up and sighing heavily. Ted Tallison shook his head upon hearing this comment from Black Quicksilver. "No, look, I sweat your match will happen tonight. "Yeah...okay, I'll see what I can do." "Alright, I gotta get going. Good luck on the match.", Tallison tells BQ, before walking off. "Yeah...luck." Fuse Bros. vs. Martyr & Rahl He didn't want to be doing this. "43" by Mushroomhead. Areumna'derin Rahl hesitated before he walked out from behind the Uprising curtain. He rubbed his brown hair as Rahl shifted his eyes from Fan to Fan sitting throughout the jam packed Arena. A part of him wanted to be happy that he was given this second chance... but knowing what's at hand, as Rahl rolled into the ring, he couldn't help but kick the bottom rope in frustration. This wasn't what he asked for. This wasn't a Match in the Cruiserweight division. This wasn't a Match in the Hardcore division. This wasn't even a Match in the Singles division. This was a tag team Match, as "I'm a Monster" by Ours began... calling out the one known to the fWo as the Martyr, and Areumna'derin Rahl's teammate. Martyr walked down the ramp, staring at an unimpressed Rahl in the center of the ring. He then entered, but even before Martyr could go over some pre-match thoughts with his partner, the theme song to their opponents cued up. The videogame had loaded. The Fuse Bros. were on. Tyler and Conor marched out to boos from the Mushroom Kingdom. Tyler, holding the Cheat Codes in his right hand, and Conor, holding the Fuse Bros.' signature powered blue question mark box in his left. The Fuse Bros. walked down the ramp, smiling to the good people in the Mushroom Land as they approached the apron. Martyr attempted to slide out of the ring just then, hoping to catch Conor in the face with a baseball slide... yet, just as the Martyr went off the ropes, Rahl had already dove over the top, and missed Conor Fuse completely. So much for the element of surprise. Tyler quickly finished taping the sheets to the ring post, as he lightly pushed his Brother out of the way so that he could hammer Rahl down with countless boots to the face. Martyr sighed, and bounced off the ropes for good this time, diving through the top and middle as he tackled Tyler Fuse to the floor. The Referee didn't know what to do at the moment. He just stood there... watching, as Conor Fuse picked up the Martyr and hurled him into the ring post. THUD! Martyr's head smacked right off the hardwood. Ty picked up Rahl and positioned him in a headlock... before flying into the ropes and sliding through Rahl's legs on the rebound. But Rahl was far from done just yet... and as Conor Fuse and the Martyr started to head to their respective corners, order was restored in the game... and now it was Rahl's turn to do something. The first player crushed Tyler Fuse across the jaw as they both began to rise. This sent Tyler into the corner, as Rahl rushed in, slamming his shoulder straight into the stomach of his opponent. Tyler gasped for air... as he fell to the floor and started to roll around. Rahl grinned... for only a split second though. He was feeling the affects from Tyler's falcon arrow suplex, a move in which Ty had perfected so well. In mere seconds, he would be standing on top of the enterance ramp for the first time in what felt like forever. The music ended his thoughts and shoved him harshly back into reality. Behind the swaying curtains, the fans were getting louder, though not as loud as they had one been for him. Tonight was not a night to look back, however. Tonight, he was looking toward the future. A future where fans would be louder than ever before. But Brand Frontier stood in the ring and watched Kodiak Vic Creed back his way up the aisle. The bile climbed in in his throat as he watched Creed smirk and kiss the World Title. "You'll never get this," Creed yelled out to Brand Frontier. "You'll never get me." Frontier scowled. "We'll see about that Vic. We'll see." Result: Kodiak Vic Creed via Pinfall
