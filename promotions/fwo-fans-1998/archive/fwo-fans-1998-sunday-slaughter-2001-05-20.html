Sunday Slaughter, 20 May 2001

20 May 2001

Introduction James Armstrong walked through the halls backstage at the Municipal Auditorium, smiling as if he owned the world, which he probably thought he did. A few feet behind him stumbled Black Quicksilver, overloaded in bags which covered his face, causing him to be unable to see. Armstrong stopped in front of a door with a giant gold star sticker pasted on it and "Armstrong" written in black marker on the star. He opened the door. "Put 'em in here, mask guy." Quicksilver stumbled around, not sure where 'here' was. Annoyed, Armstrong walked behind him, carefully turned his shoulders so that he was facing the open door, then kicked him in the butt, causing him to stagger through the door and throw the bags onto the floor, where they landed with a crash, a shatter of glass, the blaring of an alarm, and the screech of a cat. "Here's your bags Mr. Armstrong! Although... you're just a commentator...why do you have all this stuff with you?" asked Quicksilver. "Need to know! Move along!" "Um... I believe you were going to give me something?" "Oh yeah... that's right." Armstrong reached into his wallet, but only found a handful of one dollar bills. "Hmm, I don't seem to have a five dollar bill on me... unless..." "Unless what? Do I have to go out there and job to Kelly again for the money?" Armstrong pondered that thought for a moment before deciding against it. "Nah... he's kicked your ass too many times anyway, and besides, I can't book matches anymore, if you haven't forgotten. Tell ya what, kid..." Armstrong reached into one of his bags as the 26 year-old BQ had a hard time figuring out why Armstrong just called him a "kid". "Here ya go. Take that instead. You probably need it." "Wow! The Women's Title!" exclaimed BQ, stunned. "Have a ball.... kid." Armstrong slapped BQ on the back, before getting his bags and leaving the area, headed out towards the broadcast booth. "This is awesome! But...why'd he have the Women's Title with him? He never brings it to the shows... that doesn't make any sense at all!" As Quicksilver pondered the total lack of sense in the situation, Slaughter cut to its intro package... Vengence vs. Mike Heftel Slaughter continues on as "The Beast" brings out Vengence and his newly acquired manager Matt Loco. The two step into the ring as the fans in attendance boo them tremendously. The boos turn to cheers seconds later, as "Down With The Ship" brings out Mike Heftel. Noticeably odd is Heftel's choice of ring attire this week. He's wearing a striped referee shirt, black dress pants, and black dress shoes. He steps through the ropes while he pulls a microphone out of his pocket, surprising many with the fact that he did two things at once without falling. He begins to speak as his music stops. "Ahem. That's right. I just said "Ahem". I'm sure most of you have noticed, but for those who have not, you know, the ones too busy screaming about workrate and the such, I'm wearing an fWo referee's outfit. See, there's an fWo logo on the shirt". Heftel points to the fWo logo on his shirt as Vengence talks over strategy with Loco. "The reason I'm wearing this is because...well, in the past few weeks, there's been some crooked referees here in the fWo. Yup, there's been some refs who have caused me and Sean...that's Black Quicksilver to you people...to WIN matches, and that's going to stop, for tonight, *I* will ref my own match! Thank you!" Loco steps out of the ring as Heftel tosses the mic and calls for the bell, then turns to face Vengence. Stargazer. Eclipse. Splat. Vengence goes for the cover on instinct, then realizes what he's doing. Right when he's about to get up, however, he sees Heftel's hand rise off the canvase. Heftel's hands hits the canvas, rises up again, hits the canvas for a second time, rises up yet again, and hits the canvas for a third time. Heftel manages to call for the bell, signaling the end of the match. Vengence gets up as Loco enters the ring, and the two celebrate the win, but not too much, as it's just Mike Heftel. They don't have that much time to celebrate, though, because Jackhammer rushes the ring, clotheslining Loco en route to Vengence. The two monsters duke it out in the ring as Heftel sits up, and a clothesline from Jackhammer sends the two over the top rope and onto the arena floor. They continue their brawl as Sphere is seen coming out from the audience. He gets on the ring apron, and ascends to the top rope, but he doesn't spend much time there, as he leaps on both Jackhammer and Vengence. Several officials run down to the ring to seperate the three men and send them to the back, but we're not ready for a commercial yet... Adam Manson vs. Eddy Fate Six Feet Under hits the speakers, bringing out Adam Manson for this hardcore rules, non-title contest with Eddy Fate. He comes out with numerous weapons, including a dead squirrel, two wooden baseball bats, and a cattle prod. He tosses those items into the ring, as "Beautiful People" hits, bringing out the Cruiserweight Champion, Eddy Fate. Fate runs down towards the ring, quickly grabs the ring bell, and runs into the ring. He charges Manson, and swings with the bell in his arms. Manson ducks it, and kicks Fate in the neck. Fate drops the bell into Manson's hands, who clocks him in the head with it. Fate drops, his head already opened up. Manson looks at Fate, and picks him up. He grabs the bat and the cattle prod. He takes the bat, and pushes it into Fate's side. Fate, despite being groggy from the bell, knows when something odd is happening. He looks at the bat pushing into his side, and then at Manson. Manson shrugs his shoulders, and proceeds to take the prod over his shoulder, and he nails Fate with it on the head. Fate drops again, and Manson begins to shock Fate with it. Fate moves around like a fish out of water with each shock, and the ref tries to break it up. Manson replies to this by shocking the ref. The ref flies back, clutching his chest. Meanwhile, Manson takes the bat, and puts it up against Fate's neck. Manson drops down and chokes Fate with the bat, almost to the point of suffocation. Manson finally lets up, but not for long. He picks up Fate, and drags him hair-first to the apron. Manson positions Fate's head over the edge of the ring, and then he positions himself against the ropes. Manson decides to leapfrog the ropes, and he lands full-force on Fate's head, causing his neck to snap back. He quickly gets up, clutching his neck. Manson uses his opportunity to nail a neckbreaker back over the edge of the apron. Manson grabs a trash can from underneath the ring, and tosses it back into the ring. He also grabs a chair, and sets it up in the ring. He picks up Fate, and drags him to the top rope. He locks him in, and nails an In Death's Embrace, a DDT off the top onto the chair. You'd think the man just won the goddamn lottery or somethin'. So, I've got a plan for Mister Greg Davis." Ross led Universal offscreen. "It goes a little something like this..." Insane Wonder vs. Greg Davis "Sugar" by System of a Down begins to play over the sound system, and the Insane Wonder, plus his can of tuna, makes his way out to the ring. He makes his way down there rather quickly, and sure of himself. He hops into the ring, and waits his opponent. "Eye of the Tiger" begins to play, bringing out Greg Davis. Davis walks confidently down to ringside. He slides into the ring, and warms up. Before the bell can ring, another song hits the speakers. "Beautiful People" hits, bringing out Cruiserweight Champion Eddy Fate. The crowd cheers, and Fate responds in kind. He has with him a microphone, and he begins to speak. "Blokes, for your showing for me earlier, I'll tell you what I'll do. The winner of this here match will be the #1 contender for my belt. This is for your helping me out earlier, mates." The two nod at Fate...and as they do, another piece of music hits the speakers. That would be the music of Ruben Ross. Ross comes out wearing a referee shirt, and he has a microphone of his own. "You know, I was talking to Mr. Chris Universal in the back, and we both thought it would be a good idea if I were the special referee for this match. So that's just what I'm gonna do." Ross walks down to the ring, much to the fans' delight. Davis smiles, as he thinks he'll have some fun with this one. As Ross climbs into the ring, Ego walks over to him. "I see...you'd rather count the three FOR me, then have your shoulders on the mat against me, eh?" Ross doesn't say a word, and starts the match. Davis and Wonder lock up, with Davis getting the upper hand early with a headlock. Wonder whips him into the ropes, and Davis replies to that by not letting go of the headlock. Rather, he nails Wonder with a bulldog. He goes for a cover, and gets a two-count before Wonder kicks out. Davis looks at Ross, getting upset for "holding a grudge". Ross stays silent, and continues to call the match. Davis picks up Wonder, and whips him into the corner. He follows up with a hurricanrana, and Wonder hits the mat. Davis goes for another quick cover, and Wonder kicks out at two. Davis shoots another look at Ross, and picks Wonder up. On the way up, Wonder locks Davis in for a small package...he gets two and half before Davis can kick out. Wonder picks up Davis, and whips him into a corner. Davis hops up onto the top rope, and fakes taking off. Wonder doesn't bite for the fake, and he charges Davis. Davis flips off the top, behind Wonder. Wonder, meanwhile, hops onto the top, and nails a dropkick as Davis lands. Davis lands back on his neck, and holds it as he is slow getting up. Wonder walks over to him, and picks Davis up. Wonder tries to pick Davis up, but Davis blocks it. Rather, Davis picks up Wonder, and suplexes him to the mat. Davis begins to taunt Ross again, getting up in his face. Ross just looks at him, and nods. Davis takes this as a sign of weakness, and continues the taunting. Meanwhile, the Insane Wonder has an idea of his own. He has his tuna can. He grabs it, and walks over to Davis. He pokes Davis on the shoulder; however, Davis continues to taunt Ross. Ross just nods, and Wonder pokes him again. Davis finally turns around, and Wonder nails Davis with the can. He looks at Ross, and Ross allows it. Wonder goes for the cover, and gets a rather quick three count. The Insane Wonder begins to celebrate, as he is in line to go to Redemption as the number one contender for the Cruiserweight title. Ross holds Wonder's arm in the air, and then hops down. Davis gets up, and fumes at Ross. Meanwhile, Slaughter goes to a commercial break. Result: Insane Wonder by pinfall Boston Strangler vs. Ric Chronos Various images of the Boston Strangler's intimidating and enigmatic face surface on the Slaughter-Screen as O' Fortuna sets the forbidding atmosphere of the match. The Strangler appears on the entrance ramp to an ovation of cheers from fans that have been for and against him in the past. He walks to the ring with his eyes looking at nothing in particular, but rather visioning his aspiration for the night defeating Ric Chronos. After he enters and makes his presence known to the crowd, the sudden shift in music causes a sudden shift in reaction. "Christian Says" by Tones on Tail echoes as a song to the fans of betrayal and two-facedness. Ric Chronos makes his entrance, pushing the curtains aside. He raises both fists to the crowd, orchestrating a rise in jeers. Strangler watches his opponent on in the corner with his elbows resting on the ropes. Chronos walks up the steps and enters the ring while keeping an eye on the Strangler. Strangler cracks his head to either side as Chronos contracts his arms out. They circle about the ring, and lock up. Chronos draws first offense with an arm-drag takeover and immediately follows through with a cross arm-breaker. Strangler kips up out of the move and snaps an elbow across Ric's chest. Chronos turns over to hold his chest, and Strangler takes control with a half-nelson/hammerlock combination hold. With both of his arms disabled, Chronos uses both legs and a surge of aggression to jockey out of the predicament and grab his opponent in a basic headlock. The counter hold does not last for long, however, as Strangler presses Chronos against the ropes and shoots him off to the other side. Strangler swings a wild clothesline, and Chronos ducks it. From behind, Chronos chop blocks Strangler's legs from under him and wraps him in an abdominal stretch. Strangler is trapped in a seated position with Chronos' leg draped over his left shoulder and with his own arm being stretched back. When Strangler refuses to give up, Chronos becomes enraged and sinks a right hand into Strangler's ribs. Strangler cringes from the pain and clenches his teeth. Chronos' eyes light up with fire when he does not hear a submission, and he drives another right hand into the right side of Strangler. Knowing that his opponent will not give up, Chronos unloads with rights into Strangler's ribs, and then reverts to using his elbow. Chronos wrenches back more on the hold and demands Strangler to give up, but still receives no response only a physical comeback. Strangler wriggles his shoulder free from Chronos' leg and gets to a standing position in the abdominal stretch. Chronos twitches at the Strangler's power, and Strangler uses this distraction to reach back with his left hand and pull Chronos' right leg from under him. Chronos lands on his back, and Strangler immediately applies the figure-four leg-lock. As Strangler accelerates the pressure by pushing up against the mat, Chronos pounds his body up and down like a fish on land. Strangler looks straight on at his struggling opponent, and shows no signs of compassion. The fact that Chronos' knee is ready to snap in a matter of seconds, remains in his mind as an after-match award. The skilled technician Chronos, however, manages to flip the move over and reverse the pressure. Strangler manages to slip his ankle out of the weak lock and eyes Chronos trying to get up. He hooks him up from behind and drops him knee-on-knee. Chronos limps about, and Strangler gets behind him again, only to drop him this time with a German suplex. He makes the first cover of the match, and Chronos makes the tough kick-out with his damaged leg. Strangler heads to the outside and attempts a mount to the top rope, but Chronos cuts him off with a flying back elbow that sends him spine-first across the steel guardrail. Random fans take the opportunity to pat Strangler on the back, but the man in pain can hardly even acknowledge the fact that fans exist in the area. The only thing he sees is Chronos coming to the outside, and immediately he fights back with a boot to the mid-section. Chronos absorbs the shot and smashes his forearm across the bridge of Strangler's mouth. Surveying the scene, Chronos spots the timekeeper's bell beside of Matt Panzer, and immediately grabs it from him. He lifts it up to crash into the Strangler's head, but the referee comes to the outside and pulls it away from him, much to the delight of the crowd. As Chronos argues with the referee, Strangler hooks him up from behind and looks to the rear to see the announce position. As he takes this time, however, Chronos back elbows him and sends a leg back, straight into Strangler's jewels. Chronos grabs a handful of red hair and tosses the Strangler back into the ring. He rolls back into the ring and stands up to have Strangler at his feet. The dominant World Title contender sets Strangler between his legs, and hooks him up in a triangle the prelude to the figure-four DDT. In a dramatic twist of fate, however, Strangler counters and back drops Chronos. With irony in the air, Chronos counters Strangler's counter with a sunset flip. The ref gets down to the mat and counts, but the Strangler kicks out at two. Chronos immediately grabs a hold of Strangler's arm and tries whipping him into the turnbuckle. Strangler reverses, however, and Chronos lands in the corner with a whiplash effect on his previously injured neck. He proves to be OK, however, as he dodges a corner-splash from the Strangler, and hooks his head up from behind. A reverse DDT later, Chronos is over Strangler in a lateral press. One. Two. No dice. Pounding the mat violently with two bare hands, Chronos roars at the referee's count. He does not listen to the ref's response, but rather hooks up the Strangler and runs up the ropes, looking for the Chronosphere. He kicks off the ropes, and the crowd cheers wildly as the Strangler shifts the move straight into the Boston Massacre. Chronos uses his few seconds of full energy to back Strangler into the corner, where coincidentally the referee is. As a result, the ref is downed. Strangler lets out a primal scream and tightens the hold to the point where Chronos' body goes practically lifeless. With his free hand, Chronos decides against looking for a counter and taps at one of Strangler's body-scissoring legs. This sign of surrender however takes no effect on the Boston native, and the only sound that will stop him is the bell. With no referee to end the match, Chronos' career remains in question. Erik Kelly runs down the ramp wielding a steel chair, much to the relief of Ric Chronos. Chronos yells out to him to hurry up, and Kelly eventually enters the ring and lifts the chair up high above his head. He drops it once across the Strangler, but the former World Champion remains unfazed and keeps the hold intact. Two monstrous chair shots later from Strangler's fellow Boston native, the move gets broken up.
