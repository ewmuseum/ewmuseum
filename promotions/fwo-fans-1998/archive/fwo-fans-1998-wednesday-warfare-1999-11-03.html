Wednesday Warfare, 3 November 1999

3 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction A limousine pulled into the backstage parking area, with fWo security officials waving personnel and fans hoping to get an autograph aside to make room. Chris Finn, James Armstrong and John Willis stood at attention, waiting for the occupant to get out. The door swung open, and Scott Slugger, Ice, Chris Universal and Lance Star exited the vehicle looking smug and confidant. Finn sighed and relaxed, walking quickly to the main arena floor with James Armstrong obviously annoyed that it was not the new fWo commissioner. Slugger carried the fWo World Title around his waist and walked toward his locker room. He was stopped by Primetime, who was being held back by fWo security. Primetime shouted, "That belt is mine Slugger... tonight's the end of your reign Slugger... you're not going to have all those goons inside the cage!" Security strained to hold Primetime back, as the champion only grinned at the challenger, raising a single finger in response. Primetime struggled harder, as behind him the Boston Strangler had words with the Frenchie. "We are not pleased about the way things are going Frenchie," the Strangler said. "You promised us a shot at a title of Primetime's... and right now he has none. If he does not get a title tonight, we will be very angry..." The Flying Frenchie grinned and tried to calm the Strangler down. "Zut... do not worry zus... everything will be just fine..." The Strangler stalked away as Frenchie's expression turned from confidence to concern. The Strangler passed Courage and Jake, and the former International Champion was loudly yelling at his chubby bodyguard. "Tonight is it you little... excuse me, fat excuse for a human being... you mess up tonight and it's back to the streets with you... got it?" Jake gulped and waggled his head up and down quickly, not noticing Machina and the Lone Gunmen striding into the arena. Machina wore heavy black rimmed glasses with yellow lens, and spotted Geoff Green. Machina grabbed him by the shoulder and motioned down a hall. "Come with me, Green... you're going to want to get this on tape..." Inside the arena, loud fireworks exploded signaling that Wednesday Warfare had officially taken the air... Hiroshima vs. Steel Hiroshima came to the ring amidst a spray of red fireworks, looking grim and menacing as he stomped to the ring. Inside the ring already was Steel from the team NXS, and Steel flexed his sizable muscles for the crowd. Steel watched as Hiroshima slid into the ring, and began to mock the big man by walking around the ring like Frankenstein. Steel asked for the microphone and walked up until he was standing near nose to nose with the large man. "Hey, Hiro-cleana... yeah you big dummy I'm talking to you... you want to get a piece of Steel? Huh? Come on you big walking log... you got anything to say besides just grunting at me? Did the radiation over in your homeland melt your brain?" Steel tapped a finger on Hiroshima's chest for emphasis with each word he spoke, and then laughing turned around and motioned toward his crotch at the crowd. Hiroshima grabbed his head from behind, raising him up into the air and then dropping him unceremoniously to the mat. Hiroshima ran back to the ropes and delivered a heat butt into the chest of Steel. Steel writhed in pain as Hiroshima went to the ropes again. This time Steel rolled out of the way and tried to escape out of the ring, but Hiroshima grabbed his leg and pulled him back inside. Steel clawed at the mat and whimpered, trying to get free from Hiroshima's grasp. Hiroshima picked Steel up with ease, and in a simple fluid movement drove Steel into the mat with a piledriver. Hiroshima covered Steel and got the easy three count, and then stood up and screamed. He pounded his chest, and left the ring the victor. Steel held himself, still whimpering and moaning in agony... Result: Hiroshima by Pinfall Uninternational Champions Machina led Geoff Green into a small locker room, where a map of the world had been pasted on the wall. Machina motioned for Geoff to sit down, and Machina cleared his throat. "In the history of mankind we have seen many leaders. Alexander the Great... Napoleon... Boris Karlof... all these men have one thing in common, and do you know what that one thing is Geoff?" Geoff shook his head, looking confused and somewhat annoyed. Machina continued. "Of course you don't. You're a fool... a man who actually thought that those ITT tech commercials were a good opinion for advanced schooling. But what they didn't teach you in that community college of yours is that all of these men were international in nature. Now, Geoff, I ask you... what constitutes international? Is it... a flavor of coffee? A more expensive hooker? No! It's being born in a country that is not our own. Namely, somewhere else." Geoff looked confused at this point, as did the other Lone Gunmen in the room. Geoff started to get up to leave, but Machina pushed him back into his chair. He pointed again at the map of the world. "Yes, an entire world full of people who are international, and yet we still find ourselves with an uninternational International champion. Eron." Rudo raised his hand. Machina nodded to him. "Actually sir... I believe Eron is from Canada..." Machina scoffed. "Canada isn't a real country! Come now, it's just the backyard of America! The fact of the matter is this. I am dedicating my life to seeing the International title on a real international wrestler. Someone with style, grace, or myself." Machina made a shooing gesture at Green. "That's all... go on now, you're dismissed." Green still looked confused and started to say something, but then thought better of it and simply left the room. Rudo turned to Machina. "You think they bought it?" Machina shrugged. "Hey, it's better than running an angle about disliking candy..." Mad Trucker (c) vs. Rain A loud "TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" echoed throughout the arena as Mad Trucker made his way out to a huge ovation of cheers from the crowd, as it was one week ago we saw Trucker lying in a pool of his own blood. The fWo Internet Title was draped over his shoulder, and he climbed over the top rope and held the title into the air with one hand as the crowd cheered with approval. Trucker gave the belt to the referee and awaited his opponent, who came out to the sounds of thunder and lightning. Rain stepped out through the backstage area to a mixed reaction from the crowd, and strolled down to the ring. He rolled into the ring and the match immediately began, as the two traded punches back and forth to start things off. Rain got the upper hand and slammed Trucker' head into the turnbuckles in succession, before working the Trucker over with forearm smashes. Soon thereafter he sent Trucker into the opposite corner with a furious Irish Whip, and as Trucker walked out he ducked a lariat by Rain, and as Rain turned around he was met with a big boot to the face. Trucker picked up Rain and sent him to the ropes, and as Rain returned Trucker took him down with a spinebuster. Rain kicked out at two, and Trucker continued the offensive assault as he worked Rain over with stomps to the head, before a kick and a stunner out of nowhere. Another cover and another two count. Trucker followed things up with a legdrop of doom, but Rain moved out of the way just in the nick of time. Rain slid out onto the floor and pulled Trucker split-legged first into the ring post, before slamming the right leg into the post repeatedly. On the third attempt, Trucker mustered up enough strength in his lower body to stop the move from happening, and kicking Rain hard so that he went over the guardrail behind him. Trucker rolled out onto the floor and shook off the pain, and as he bent over to pull Rain up, Rain grabbed him by the neck and brought him throat first onto the cold metail of the rail. Trucker staggered out and Rain snuck up from behind and took him down with a bulldog on the floor. He then rolled Trucker into the ring, and went for a cover. Kickout at two and a half by the Trucker. Rain decided to stray off his usual course of action by taking a high risk maneuever, and he went to the top rope on the outside. He flew off with an elbowdrop, and hit it as Trucker attempted to roll out of the way. Another cover, but another kickout. Rain brought Trucker to a vertical base and hit him with several reverse knife edge chops, which echoed throughout the crowd, before he whipped him into the buckles. He charged in, but Trucker sidestepped it and Rain slammed into the turnbuckles. Trucker waited as Rain staggered out, and Rain scooped him up over his shoulder for the 18-Wheeler. He then went for it, but Rain slipped behind him. Trucker turned around, and was kicked in the stomach, and then powerbombed as Rain hit his finisher. Feeling that would not be enough to finish Trucker off, he scooped Trucker up and decided to go for it all by finishing Trucker off with his own finishing move, the 18-wheeler. He hit it, and then pinned Trucker, becoming the new Internet Champion. As he held the title high in the air, Warfare cut to the backstage area. We saw Lance Star pacing backstage with the Cruiserweight Title over his shoulder, and Chris Universal nearby staring at the television intently... keeping a close eye on the new Internet Champion. Result: Rain by Pinfall Break time With Warfare on break James Armstrong wandered backstage, whistling quietly to himself as he went. He spotted some doughnuts on a table and walked over to them with a smile. In front of the doughnuts was a small card that read: HANDS OFF! DOUGHNUTS FOR WARFARE GIRLS APPLICANTS James Armstrong grabbed the card and crumpled it up, tossing the remains on the floor as he began to gorge himself on doughnuts. He ate quickly, looking over one shoulder from time to time to make sure he wasn't being observed. As he was about to leave he spotted a dark manila envelope. On the front of it was written: PRIVATE! James Armstrong took another look around himself and then opened the envelope, letting the paperwork slide out. Armstrong read quickly, getting crumbs all over the front of the papers as he ate. Suddenly he stopped in mid-bite and his eyes went wide. He looked around himself again, this time with more urgency. He heard footsteps coming up the hall, and quickly pulled out a pen from his pocket. Working quickly, he scribbled something out on the paperwork, and wrote a new addition in. He stuffed the papers back into the envelope and laid it back on the table before quickly scurrying away. A second after he vanished down another hallway, a series of fWo lawyers and officials entered the room. The chatted noisily and one commented, "So that's where I left this!" as he picked up the manila envelope. The group of men left the room, still chatting... Courage & Jake vs. The Millenium Men The arena is overcome by booing as Courage and his loyal but round bodyguard Jake approach the ring for the next match. Courage berates Jake, telling him that he'd better not screw up again, and then heads to the apron. Out next come The Black Panther and Havok, a team christened by fans on the Internet "The Millenium Men." The fans respond as expected to the wildly popular Panther, and the positive response seems to be spreading to Havok as well, especially after his match last week against Death, David Spencer. The Panther starts off in the ring, and quickly takes Jake to the mat with a single-leg take down. Sticking with the leg, The Panther turns Jake over and applies a half-crab. The pain is overcoming Jake, and he's on the verge of giving up when Courage flips into the ring and breaks the hold with a kick to the back of Panther's skull. Panther gets back to his feet and knocks Courage to the outside with a lariat, before tagging in Havok. Havok drags Jake into the center of the ring and quickly locks in a figure four. Again, Jake's pain is very obvious, and again the hold is broken by Courage, this time coming off the top rope with an elbow to Havok's knee. The referee leads Courage back to his corner while Jake and Havok lie on the mat, each man holding their respective legs. Jake crawls over to his corner and brings in Courage, who fails in preventing Havok from tagging back out. Panther catches Courage with a series of punches and kicks, then whips him to the ropes. A Panther lariat sends Courage to the mat, but the cover gets merely a one-count. Both men back up, and this time it's Courage that takes control. He forces The Black Panther into his corner and stuns him with a high knee, then goes to Havok's corner to taunt him. Havok is drawn into the ring, which in turn brings the ref's attention to him. Meanwhile, Jake is tying the Panther's shoelaces together in the corner. Courage goes back to attack him, then whips him out of the corner. Naturally, the Panther stumbles and falls to the mat, knocking himself out. Courage goes for the cover but the Panther somehow manages to kick out at two. The Panther tried to stand, but found he couldn't with the laces tied. The referee spotted this, and looking in the direction of Jake, who was pantomiming to the crowd how he tied the Panther's bootlaces together. The referee looked disgusted and called for the bell, earning the Panther and Havok the DQ victory. Courage yelled at the referee, then at Jake, and then at the referee again. The referee shrugged and pointed down at Jake. Courage rolled from the ring still screaming at the fat bodygaurd, and yelled at him all the way up the entrance ramp. Result: The Millenium Men by DQ Plans and schemes Somewhere in a deep and dark area of the area, David Spencer sat on a heap of crates and discarded garbage. The material had been piled up in a throne and Spencer looked over the people before him with a dull, dead gaze. When he spoke, it came from a whisper deep within himself, and sounded full of cold fury. "Above me I hear the children laughing, the fans enjoying themselves. This... bothers me. I want to hear the screams of the dammed as I pluck the skin from their bones. Tonight should be a massacre, all rejoicing in the glory that is I... the lord Death who rules his Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse with an iron fist. Nay, tonight will not be a night for joy, it will be a night struck at the begging of the end... for when the clock strikes zero the millennium of Death is at hand." Spencer pointed slowly to Fallen and Poison and bid them stand. "You two... go forth and realize my dream. Nobody can stop you... for you are my Dark Riders on the hunt of evil. Teach those two another lesson in what it means to oppose me... go forth... and finish them off." Poison began to sob, but the tears rolled down a face already stretched wide with a gaping, hollow smile. Fallen smiled as well, but his eyes burned dark with unknowable schemes. Death looked down at Demon, who lay on the cold ground bound at the legs and arms. "They all laughed at you... even that little slut girl. Well boy... you will get your revenge. Oh yes... you will get your revenge..." Oracle vs. Prophet The arena is bathed in blue light as Prophet walks towards the ring, his eyes full of madness. He stands in the middle of the ring awaiting Oracle, who rushes out from the entrance and slides into the ring. Oracle unleashes on Prophet, venting out his frustration on his former partner. After gaining the advantage, he scoops up Prophet and slams him hard to the mat, and then follows up with a blatant choke. The ref breaks it, but not easily, as Oracle seems to have had enough from his former partner. Oracle breaks free from the ref's grasp and stomps away at him ferociously. The ref starts laying down a five-count for Oracle to stop, and he does at four. The ref checks on Prophet, and deems him able to continue. Prophet returns to his feet, and paces around the ring, dodging subsequent attacks from Oracle. After a minute or so of stalling, Prophet challenges Oracle to a test of strength. They lock hands, and Prophet forces Oracle down to the mat. Prophet runs to the ropes and lands an elbow drop square on Oracle's sternum, and goes for the cover. He gets two, and then tries again, this time hooking the leg. Again only a two-count, so Prophet relents and slides out of the ring. Oracle follows him, and the two brawl near the announce table. Finn and Armstrong scatter, but the table is spared as Oracle whips Prophet hard into the ring post. Oracle grabs Armstrong's chair and whacks Prophet hard with it, knocking him out but good. He rolls Prophet into the ring before following him in. Rather than going for the pinfall, he intermittently yells at and stomps on Prophet. He brings Prophet back to his feet and props him up in the corner. Oracle charges him with a shoulder tackle and drives him backwards into the turnbuckles. He then places Prophet onto the turnbuckle, but Prophet simply falls forward, landing on Oracle and bringing him to the mat. With both men momentarily out, Mickey Maliboo comes to ringside and slides into the ring going right for Prophet. Seeing this, the referee has no choice but to disqualify Oracle and give the match to Prophet. Oracle gets to his feet and confronts Maliboo about this, obviously irked that he was cost a victory he felt he had in the bag. Maliboo simply decks Oracle and walks away, as Warfare cuts to commercial. Result: Prophet by DQ The path of a Warfare Girl, part one Backstage a series of bouncy young women stood grinning and giggling to each other. All looked excited to be backstage at an fWo event, and giggled at each other in their very short and skimpy skirts. A man walked into the room with a clipboard and addressed the young women. "Okay, thank you all for coming... I'm glad all of you signed up to be in the Warfare Girls tryouts... but wait... we are missing someone..." The door crashed open and a fWo makeup artist stumbled inside. She collapsed on the floor as more sounds of scuffling were heard. Finally security shoved Nightshade into the room, who looked around at the girls present with a devastating glare. The man with the clipboard checked off her name, and beamed happily. "Okay! Great! I'm glad all of you are here, and like I said, not all of you will get to be Warfare Girls, but we are all glad you've decided to come down for the tryouts. Okay, now remember, the most important part of being a Warfare Girl is to make the crowd think that they have a chance of sleeping with you... even though we all know that's never going to happen." One of the girls raised her hand. The man with the clipboard nodded, and pointed to her. "Yes Bubbles... you have a question?" Bubbles giggled and started to ask, but then seemed to forget what she was going to say. Finally her eyes brightened and she said, "Um, like... do we have do... like, math and stuff?" The man with the clipboard shook his head. "No, this job does not require math. Okay, are there any other questions?" Nightshade raised her hand, and the man nodded to her. Locking the man's gaze with a deadly stare, Nightshade said, "Do you want to die from blood loss or oxygen loss?" The man smiled and said, "Oh Nightshade... you've got such a great sense of humor. But girls, Nightshade does bring up an excellent point. Since you will all be getting rather sizable breast implants... yes, even you Ms. Peaks... you make experience some dizziness. If this happens, just smile wide and thrust out your chest... works every time. Okay, now girls we all need to move to the parking lot where we will have some dance practices... let's go... chop, chop! Nightshade, put down that hammer..." Lone Gunmen vs. Dark Riders Warfare continued as Fallen and Poison came out as the fans welcomed them by booing them loudly and tossing things in their general direction. Fallen seemed miffed by the fans and while Poison appeared to have her mind on other matters. Poison headed towards the ring apron, but Fallen stopped her and the two awaited their opponents instead at the bottom of the shiny entrance ramp. A huge "L" contained in a circle appeared on the Warfare screen, and out came Billy James and Sid Snow. Sid Snow checked out Fallen, while Billy James seem to be disturbed by Poison' outer beauty. Billy slapped Snow and reminded him that this was a wrestling match, and if Snow wanted a piece - so to speak - of Fallen; he would have to do it on his own time. Snow sighed and they continued to the ring, and Fallen and Poison attacked. Fallen hit a quick reverse DDT on the outside, before rolling Sid Snow into the ring for the pin. James fought off Poison on the outside and pulled Fallen onto the floor at the count of two, and he and Fallen began to brawl. Poison and Fallen double-teamed James while Sid Snow recovered. Snow actually attempted a wrestling move, but it was all for naught as he flew off with a crossbody and Fallen caught him and disposed of him over the guardrail with a fallaway slam in one fell swoop. Fallen rolled James into the ring and he and Poison worked James with various maneuvers to ensure them the victory, for Snow was too busy on the outside worrying about a broken nail. Fallen finished things off with a spike piledriver after some assistance by Poison, before garnering the win and the three count. Fallen and Poison exited to the ring and broke another one of Snow' nails for good measure, which left Snow crying profusely as Warfare faded to a commercial after a preview for the next big pay-per-view, Battlebowl, coming your way live on November 27th... Result: Dark Riders by Pinfall A little firing... Backstage everyone could hear Courage screaming at Jake, and tried to stay out of the area but remain close enough to hear the verbal trashing take place. Los Latin and Bookerman hid around a corner, laughing to themselves as Courage continued his tirade against his chubby bodyguard. "You are the biggest, most worthless... pile of giant fat lard I've ever seen! I'd have better luck bringing a big sack of flour to the ring than you... because at least the sack of flour would have more intelligence! You've been living off Courage for years... well no longer fat boy! You're fired! Get out of my face, I never want to see you again, and I'm docking you this week's pay! You hear me Jake? You get the message through that fat head of yours? You're fired! Fired! Now get out of my sight!" Jake scrambled away, tripping over a cord that was laying on the ground. Courage gave Jake a kick in the ass to get him going again, and watched scowling as Jake vanished down a hallway... Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Fyre The Flying Frenchie came to the ring, holding the fWo Womens Heavyweight Title in the air and a paper bag in his other hand. The crowd booed him, but the Frenchie seemed not to notice as he stepped into the squared circle and was handed a microphone. The Frenchie signaled for quiet, but the crowd instead began to chant 'asshole'. The Frenchie paid it little attention and began to speak. "Zut... I am... very sorry for zis show zat you are having to zee tonight. You zee, I for one thought zat ze fWo would have better womens to be offering for zis special challenge of mine. Sadly zis is not to be. It is one worthless woman after another... zis cannot be, zo let us all hopes zat tonight it will change. Fyre... come to zis ring right now." Fyre came out onto the ramp, with her partner and romantic interest Silver Fox close behind her. She walked suspiciously to the ring, and neared the Frenchie, but the masked superstar motioned for her to stop a few feet away. Behind her Silver Fox looked nervously for signs of Black Quicksilver. "Zo... you have came. Good! Zis is one part of ze challenge, already complete. Already you are better zan zat moron Stone and her chubby partner. Because I tire of zis belt, you get ze easy challenge. Only ze complete buffoon would not master zis. Are you ready?" Fyre nodded, looking more sure of herself. The Frenchie grinned an evil smile, and pulled from the bag a large kielbasa sausage. "Zis is your challenge. All you must do is zis... you must swallow ze whole sausage down ze throat. It is ze skill any French woman can do." Fyre took the sausage and marveled at how big it was. She started to swallow, but quickly gagged and started again. She repeated this for several attempts, never able to get more than one third of the sausage down her throat. Finally, the Frenchie shook his head in disgust. "Zut! Zis is horrible! Only zat much? Well, masseur Silver Fox... I suppose we know more about you zan we wanted to, eh?" Silver Fox at once blushed bright red and rushed the Frenchie, but he was already out of the ring, still carrying the title. Looking back at the two in the ring he said, "Well... better luck next time... I will bill you for ze sausage, so keep it as ze token of ze failure zat is you." Inside the ring Silver Fox and Fyre looked angry, and did not see Black Quicksilver slide into the ring from behind. Black Quicksilver attacked with Stump, driving both the Silver Fox and Fyre out of the ring. Still screaming, security attempted to hold Black Quicksilver back as Fyre and Silver Fox were escorted to the backstage area... Result: Fyre loses by bad swallowing The Tag champions speak Backstage Geoff Green caught up with Mike Bear and Wretch, the fWo Tag Team Champions. Green asked the champions what they had in mind for the future of the fWo, and who exactly they would be facing on Slaughter. Mike Bear responded saying, "Green... the Underground has the gold, and now it's time for a little recruiting. So what I want you to know is that the Underground will be adding two new members to our ranks on Sunday Slaughter... making us the single most dominant force in professional wrestling today. And Falcon... if you're listening to this then we want you back buddy... so get your head together and get back. As for who we are facing... Wretch?" Wretch took the microphone and looked directly into the camera. "Green... we don't know who we are facing. The Underground went through every team the fWo had to offer, so the challenge isn't here anymore. That's why we are sending out a challenge to all the promotions around the world to show up, get your ass kicked by the Underground, and become another victim on our highway. Come on Bear... let's go." The champions walked away from Green, as the cameras went back to the ring... Black Quicksilver vs. Eron Eron came out with the International Title once again around his waist as the crowd let him know how they felt about him, saying unnice things and doing unnice gestures, and throwing unnice objects at him. Eron ignored all that and stepped into the ring as pyros sounded off. Eron handed his coveted title to the referee and awaited the Black Quicksilver, who came out to a gigantic greeting of cheers as the fans all chanted "STUMP~!" in unison. Quicksilver shook stump in the air, obviously still excited about the match despite Eron' title not being on the line, before he charged the ring. Eron catapulted himself over the top rope with a thrusting dropkick, which sent BQ back onto the floor. Eron then grabbed stump and rubbed his ass on it, before tossing it aside and rolling BQ into the ring. The referee rang the bell as Eron went up to the top area and flew off with a somersault legdrop, before making a pinfall attempt. BQ got a shoulder up at two, and Eron continued his assault with a variety of offensive moves. Eron took BQ down with an inverted swinging neckbreaker, before following that up with a senton bomb from the top. BQ rolled out of the way and Eron hit the ground hard, and BQ took him down with a crucifix for a pinfall attempt. Eron kicked out at dos, and BQ decided to take things onto the floor. He threw Eron through the middle ropes onto the floor, before he waited for Eron to return to his feet. Eron did so, and BQ did a baseball slide into a headscissors which took Eron by surprise onto the ground. BQ went up to the ring apron and flew off for a hurrancanrana, but Eron countered it into a running powerbomb on the floor. Both men remained out for a few moments before Eron rolled BQ into the ring at the count of seven, and then Eron went into the ring. Eron went for his De-Rogeur, but BQ countered out of it into a surprise pinning combination for the pin. Eron kicked out of it one second too late, and BQ got the upset victory. Afterwards, Eron became incensed at what happened and pounded BQ with a chair repeatedly, before adding insult to injury by slamming BQ in the head with stump several times as referees struggled to restrain Eron. With the ringside area a lost in a sea of officials, Warfare rolled on... Result: Black Quicksilver by Pinfall And the commissioner is... Inside the ring stood John Willis and Chris Finn, along with a host of fWo lawyers and officials. Chris Finn addressed the audience. "Fans... like all of you I am sorry about the awful events that befell our commissioner two weeks ago. It's been a rough time in the fWo, and we thank all of you for your support, and your well wishes. It is with some sadness that I report that we must select an acting commissioner who will oversee booking and planning for the promotion while Travis recovers from his injuries. With that said, may I see the envelope please." One of the lawyers handed Chris Finn a manila envelope, and Chris Finn opened it. A momentary look of confusion crossed Finn's face as crumbs fell out of the envelope. Finn pulled out the papers and read, "On this day of November the third, I am proud to announce that I will..." A crease marked Finn's brow as he read down the paper. "What the fuck?" The fWo censor sprang into action bleeping the word as lawyers leaned over to see what was written. Finn held down the microphone and shouted at them, pointing to the paper and jabbing it with his finger. The lawyers shrugged. Finn shouted, "This isn't right! What in the hell is this?" Finn tossed the paper to the ground, and one of the lawyers picked it back up, handing it back to Finn with a shove. Finn looked at the lawyers again and started to protest, but the lawyers only shook their heads, saying that what was on the paper was legally binding. James Armstrong climbed into the ring to calm Finn, and Finn swung at Armstrong with a small fist. Security officials drug Chris Finn away, as James Armstrong was handed the paper. Clearing his throat, James Armstrong announced, "Ladies and gentlemen, it is with great pleasure that I announce myself acting commissioner of the fWo. During my tenure I promise to be a fair commissioner, giving all wrestlers in the fWo a fair shake. Yes, it is due to the great wisdom of the executive committee that I be named commissioner... and I'm certain that you will all agree that I'm the best man for the job. Why, my father once told me..." Chris Finn escaped the security officials and charged Armstrong again, knocking him to the mat. Security drug Finn off again, and Armstrong gave a final salute to the crowd as he accompanied Finn away. Matt Loco and Dick Venom came to ringside to announce the final match of the evening... Scott Slugger (c) vs. Primetime We head back to the ring for our main event, a match two months in the making. Primetime strides to the ring along with The Boston Strangler and The Flying Frenchie as the cage lowers from the rafters. It lands, and when the ring crew seem satisfied with it, the three superstars check the cage to make sure it's not going to fall apart mid-match. It must have passed their test, because Primetime steps into the cage while Frenchie and the Strangler head back to the locker room as per the referee's order. "Back in Black" blasts over the loudspeaker, bringing out the World Champ Scott Slugger, along with Ice, Chris Universal, and new fWo Cruiserweight Champion Lance Star. The Dynasty attempt to enter the cage en masse, but the official prevents it, and sends everyone but Slugger to the back. Slugger rags on Primetime a little before handing the World Title to the referee, who in turn holds it in the air above him, displaying it to the crowd. The referee leaves the cage and locks the door, starting this match. The two competitors size each other up for a second, then lock-up. Slugger takes control with a side headlock, and Primetime counters with a push-off. Slugger pivots to face Primetime again, and turns an attempted lariat into a backdrop. Slugger drops a leg on the downed Primetime, and heads for the cage wall. Before he can start climbing Primetime catches him and sends him to the mat with a german suplex. Primetime grabs Slugger's arm and starts wrenching away at it, compounding the damage inflicted at Mob Rules by the Boston Strangler. Slugger manages to flip out of the hold, but he's obviously the worse for wear. Primetime nails Slugger with a dropkick, and races to the cage wall. He gets about halfway up before Slugger grabs his ankles and whips him down to the mat. He follows that with a headbutt to PT's groin. Slugger stands defiantly over the challenger, then runs to the ropes and drives his elbow into PT's chest on his return. He lifts Primetime and suplexes him, but PT blocks it. Slugger tries again, and again it's blocked. Primetime knees Slugger in the gut, and then puts him in position for a shoulder breaker. Slugger wriggles out of it, and connects with a reverse DDT. Scott picks up Primetime and whips him to the ropes. Primetime rolls under an attempted superkick and meets Slugger with a cross-body coming the other way. Primetime connects with a guillotine legdrop next, which leaves Slugger gasping for air. Primetime takes the opportunity to scale the cage, and gets a good head start on Slugger. He's almost over the top by the time Slugger gets up and reaches the wall. Slugger scrambles up the cage and manages to get a handfull of Primetime's hair, forcing Primetime back near the top of the cage. Slugger then nails a superplex off the top of the cage, and both men feel the effects of that one. Slugger's up first, and he heads towards the cage door. The ref unlocks and opens the door, but Primetime's up and spins Slugger around before hitting a hurricanrana. The door is closed again, and Primetime picks up Slugger. He whips the World Champ into the ropes, and backdrops him into the cage wall. The cage shakes, but thankfully doesn't give. Slugger returns to his feet, only to have Primetime rub his face into the cage wall. Blood starts trickling down Slugger's face, and Primetime only worsens it with rabbit punches to the cut. Primetime lifts Slugger into position for a shoulder breaker and connects this time. He picks the champ back up and hefts him up for a Primetime bomb, but Slugger starts punching away with his good arm while seated on Primetime's shoulders, forcing PT back into a corner. From there, Slugger lets loose with the traditional ten-punches in the corner. Slugger drops back down to the mat, and stalks off to the opposite turnbuckles. He tears free the turnbuckle pad, exposing the joint of the ring ropes, and then walks back to Primetime. An Irish whip into that corner is reversed by Primetime, and Slugger sails shoulder first into the exposed metal. Slugger stumbles out of the corner, and is met by a face-buster from Primetime. Primetime walks to the wall and takes a bit of a breather, then starts to slowly climb the cage. Slugger stirs and heads towards the wall, trying to head Primetime off at the pass. He catches up with Primetime near the top of the cage, and the two men trade chops, hoping the other one succumbs and falls. It doesn't happen, though. Both men hang onto the cage, 10 feet above the ring, until Primetime grabs Slugger's good arm and lets go of the cage. Now Slugger is supporting both men's weight with an injured arm, and the strain is taking its toll. Finally, Slugger has no choice but to let go, and both men fall back to the mat. Primetime lands on his feet and rolls out of the way of Slugger's less controlled fall. Slugger appears to be out, so Primetime walks to the door, waits for the ref to open it, and steps out of the cage to become the fWo World Heaviweight Champion. Primetime grabs the title and walks towards the entrance ramp, where The Flying Frenchie and The Boston Strangler have appeared to congratulate Primetime on his accomplishment. Primetime embraces Frenchie, and then turns to the Strangler. The two eye each other, and then the Strangler extends his hand. The two men shake hands, and the Strangler raises Primetime's hand in victory. He then turns to Frenchie, and says, "Our contract is now fulfilled, is it not?" Frenchie nods, and is quickly taken down by a hard right from the Strangler. The Boston Strangler lifts Primetime by the neck one-handed, then continues: "For two months, we've done our part. We've stayed out of the limelight that was rightfully ours, and it has become worth it. We want our shot, Brian. And if we don't get it by Battle Bowl, you will come to regret ever entering into a deal with us." The Strangler drops Primetime, and grabs the World Title. He holds it in his hands, admiring it, then simply tosses it on top of Primetime, before leaving to the back. Primetime groggily gets up collects the title and the Frenchie, and heads backstage as well. Meanwhile, back in the cage, Scott Slugger begins to stir. He composes himself and heads to the door, but finds it surprisingly locked. Undaunted, he starts climbing the cage, even though he's hampered with the injured arm. As he nears the top, a ceiling type structure lowers, sealing off the cage for good. Slugger drops to the mat, confused as to what's happening, when the lights go out. Suddenly, the cage bursts into flames, and 4 Dark Riders, led by Collins, step out onto the ramp. They collectively mock Slugger until the other members of the Dynasty erupt from behind them, and a large brawl ensues. Slugger is still trapped in the flaming cage as Warfare goes off the air Result: Primetime
