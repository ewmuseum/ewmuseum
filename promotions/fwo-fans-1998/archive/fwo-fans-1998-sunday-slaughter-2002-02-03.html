Introduction The excitement filled the room. The tension was palpable. The anticipation was... well, high. A large group of workers both wrestlers and backstage personnel had gathered around the television, where a familiar voice could be heard calling the events. The throng stood in silence, breathlessly waiting to see what would happen. "They line up... The kick... It's good!" The group erupted in a multitude of emotions some were elated, others were dejected, others, something in between. One voice was heard between the shouts it could have been any of them. "Holy shit!" The reactions continued until another anonymous backstage worker walked up to the group. "Uh, guys... we're on. It's time to get going." The group died down. Another voice spoke up, again indistinguishable. "What kind of moron would book a wrestling show on Super Bowl Sunday anyway? Oh well. At least we didn't do it at halftime." The rest of the group nodded as one, and the various workers broke up and headed to their places as Slaughter headed to its opening video package... Jon Crisp vs. Mufanga Kagos "High and Dry" begins to play, and the crowd boos as Jon Crisp begins to make his way to the ring. Crisp takes in the booing with a smirk, then climbs up the ring steps and into the ring. He does a few stretches as he waits for his opponent. With Jon Crisp already in the ring, the driving beat of "Warriors' Call" by the Yunabi Drummers hits the PA system and out of he back marches fWo Survivor participant, Mufanga Kagos, much to the delight and anticipation of the crowd. Kagos hits the ringside area, and is set to slide beneath the ropes for his fWo debut, when Crisp rushes over in attempt to start things early, hoping to get in a few stomps before the bell. Kagos is wise to ploy and pulls up short, and looks to the referee for a little help, which he receives in the form of the official admonishing Crisp to his corner. After a brief protest, Crisp obeys the referee's command, and Kagos slides into the ring. The ref calls for the bell. Crisp smiles slyly at the rookie Kagos, licks his lips, then holds his hand up high, challenging the newcomer to a test of strength. Kagos obliges, and reaches up high, only to find himself chopped across the Adam's apple with a vicious backhanded forearm. Kagos clutches his throat and gasps for air, but there is no rest for the weary here in the fWo, and Crisp punctuates that statement with a textbook European uppercut, which drives Kagos into the ropes. Crisp is quick to follow up, nailing Kagos with a series of right hands, and then whips him across the ring into the ropes. Kagos bounces of the ropes into a waiting Crisp drop toehold. Crisp then locks on an ankle submission hold in an attempt to end this one early. Kagos screams out in pain. The referee asks him if he wants to quit, but Mufanga Kagos refuses to go out so quickly in his fWo debut. After an agonizing four to five seconds, Kagos uses a burst of adrenaline to scurry to the ropes, grabbing the bottom rope. The ref orders Crisp to release the hold, but he refuses. After another stern warning to Crisp releases the hold and backs off of Kagos. Kagos climbs to his feet, and turns to Crisp. Crisp nods and smiles, telling Kagos that the fWo is a far cry from the bush league indy. feds he came from. Kagos ignores Crisp's trash talk, and begins to circle. Crisp shrugs out to the crowd, who boo in response, and once again raises his hand to challenge Kagos to a test of strength. Just as he did earlier, Kagos accepts the challenge and raises his hand up high. Crisp, almost predictably, goes for the same chop maneuver, but Kagos gets an arm up and blocks it. Crisp goes for a chop with his other arm, which Kagos also blocks, then follows it with a kick to the gut of Crisp. Crisp doubles over, which leaves him wide open for the axe kick to the back that follows. Crisp goes down to the canvas hard, but before Kagos can capitalize, he rolls out of the ring, to regain his composure. The crowd boos. From the outside, Crisp looks into the ring at Kagos with a look of disbelief. Avoiding the opportunity to gloat, Kagos merely stares back out at Crisp. Crisp rubs his back and stalls for time in the ringside area. The referee begins to count which forces Crisp back into the ring. The two men circle, then lock up. Crisp quickly slips behind Kagos with a waist lock, but the tribesman shimmies loose and reverses it, moving behind Crisp. Crisp, is a veteran, however, and as he looks down at the Kagos' arms around his waist, he notices something of interest... ...a bare foot. Without a moment's delay or hesitation, Crisp stomps down firmly upon the unprotected foot of Kagos, causing the tribesman to howl in pain. Wasting no time, Crisp propels himself off the far ropes, and catches the still howling Kagos on the rebound with a swinging neckbreaker. Kagos goes down and goes down hard. Crisp quickly rolls him to his back and makes the cover... One.... Two... Kagos kicks out, showing he still has plenty left in the tank. Frustrated, Crisp grabs a fist full of dreadlocks and pulls the African warrior to his feet. SLAP! A stinging knife-edge chop sends Kagos into the corner. SLAP! Another. SLAP! A third knife-edge chop causes Kagos to slump in the corner. Sensing the momentum is with him, Crisp tangles Mufanga's arms in the ropes, despite the referee's objections. Convinced Kagos is securely snared, Crisp heads to the opposite corner of the ring. Kagos jaws out to the crowd a bit, before charging across the ring, towards the entangled Mufanga Kagos, when in actuality the proper way to describe Kagos would be as the "apparently entangled Mufanga Kagos", because just as Crisp is about to make impact with the mid section of Kagos, the warrior springs upward, using the ropes as leverage, causing Crisp to crash headlong into the ring post. The crowd roars in approval. Crisp staggers wildly around the ring, drunk from the collision of his skull and the steel post. Kagos shows little mercy against Crisp, as he rushes to the ropes, leaping from the canvas to the second rope, then springboarding off. Turning his body in mid air, Kagos catches by the head and bulldogs him to the canvas. Crisp is down and in trouble. Kagos goes for the cover... One... Two... Crisp kicks out. Kagos gets to his feet and stomps down upon Crisp with a flurry of quick stomps. The crowd loves it. Crisp is completely out of it. Kagos gets to his feet and lets out a warrior's call to the crowd, who responds with howls of delight. He quickly scurries to the corner and ascends the turnbuckles, until he is on top. He looks down at Crisp in the center of the ring, and lets out another call to the crowd. Once again the crowd responds. Then Kagos leaps from the top rope, attaining incredible heights. Flashbulbs go off from around the arena. Soaring through the air with his arms and legs held wide, Kagos crashes down upon the prone and exposed form of Crisp. Kagos covers... One... Two... Three. Mufanga Kagos secures a victory in his first fWo match, and the fans cheer wildly as his hand is raised. Slaughter, meanwhile, heads elsewhere... Result: Offer As fWo owner Chris Universal sat in his office, momentarily enjoying a brief respite from the normal chaos of a wrestling show, a knock came at the door. Universal sighed softly and shuffled some paperwork into a pile on the corner of his desk. "Come in," Universal requested, trying his best to muster a friendly tone for his voice. As the bald-headed visitor opened the door and stepped into Universal's office, a brief smile crossed Universal's face. "Lance," he said, moving from his seat to stand and shake Knight's hand. "So nice to see you. Have a seat, please." Knight obliged, a look of concern coming onto his face as his eyes darted around the room, finally settling on the pile of paperwork that was arranged on Universal's desk. With a sigh of relief, Knight realized that there didn't appear to be a single punitive document in sight. Universal swiveled in his chair and grabbed a nearly-full pot of coffee from the adjacent counter surface. He poured a cup and offered it to Knight, who politely shook his head. Universal withdrew the proffered cup and mixed in a small container of creamer. "Lance, we need to talk," Universal stated before taking a small sip of his coffee. Once again, a look of tension crept across Knight's face, but Universal apparently didn't recognize the trepidation in the other man's face, and he continued to speak, saying the last words Knight expected to hear after his confrontation with fWo employee Fallen last week. He had been an invited guest of the fWo; however, he still wasn't a part of the roster, and that gave no small amount of worry to the Pittsburgh native. "How'd you like to work for the fWo?" Universal stated, leaning back in his chair. This was a nice change of pace from all the recent turmoil he had seen while the fWo was touring Europe. As a stunned Knight simply stared at Universal with his mouth slightly agape, Universal continued. "Also, I'd like to apologize for Fallen's actions last week. He had no idea that you were a guest of the fWo, and as such, he was simply acting in self-defense. It's my sincerest hope that you won't pursue any legal action in this situation." Knight's face immediately morphed from shock to relief. "Oh, no worries there, Mr. Universal. I was more afraid of the opposite happening, in fact." Universal pondered this for a moment, and then waved his hand as if to dismiss the idea as nothing short of trivial. "Us... sue you? Out of the question. You were our guest at the show, and we take spare no expense in ensuring that our guests both enjoy themselves and are treated with the utmost respect." Universal paused to take another sip of his coffee. "So, I've lain my offer out on the table; what is your decision going to be?" Knight was overjoyed as he answered Universal's query. "Of course I'd love to work for the fWo; who wouldn't? From what I've seen, it's a well-run organization with no small amount of respect in the industry. Hell, if I didn't want to join the fWo, I would've never auditioned for Survivor." Universal's smile was as broad as his new employees. "Excellent. Feel free to spend the rest of the night backstage, and we'll sign the paperwork later this week. Sound good to you?" Knight's reply was immediate and delivered with enthusiasm. "That sounds great to me." As Knight stood and made his way out the door, Universal smiled, "Have a great night, Lance." But his closing fell on deaf ears, as Knight had already made his way hurriedly down the hall. "Well, that went a lot better than I expected." Fashionably Late The parking lot outside of the Ice Palace the exclusive parking lot, not the one used by the general public was almost full. Still, there were spots available for those who chose to arrive fashionably late as the latest arrival did. The blue Dodge Viper pulled into the parking lot at high speed, showcasing the powerful engine. It took a quick turn around a corner and pulled easily into an open spot one right next to the building, as it worked out. As expected, out of the driver's side stepped Scott Slugger. Rather than enter the building, Slugger walked around to the other side of the car and opened the passenger door. Stephanie Griffith stepped out Slugger's significant other, who had last been seen on fWo television at Halloween Hell. Slugger stepped aside with a flourish as he opened the door. "And who says chivalry's dead?" Stephanie said with a smile as she stepped out of the car. Slugger chuckled. "Oh, it is. I'm just doing this because chicks dig it." "I thought we dug the long ball." "Oh yeah, this too. After you." Slugger opened the arena door and Stephanie stepped through. Slugger followed. "I present to you, the Ice Palace. Sure, it looks like crap from the outside, but hey it looks even worse inside!" The door closed behind him. Seconds later, the driver's side door of the car next to Slugger's opened. It banged against the side of the Viper, leaving a dent. The car's window was rolled up. And Vic Creed stepped out with a smile on his face. Out steps the Boston Strangler, one half of the fWo World Tag Team Champions, who walks to the ring with a purpose. Fans at ringside yell harsh obscenities, but Strangler only glares at them on his way to the ring. As Strangler hands his tag belt off to an attendant and steps into the ring, "O Fortuna" fades out and the crowd prepares to erupt. "Jump Around" by House of Pain starts up and the fans explode with cheers. Running out from the back is Wretch...Big Wretchy...Wretch Daddy...the Wretchster...the, well, you get the idea. With a slight grin on his face, Wretch jogs down the ramp, slapping hands with some of the fans along the way. Strangler sneers down at Wretch, who stands next to the ring wondering how to enter. Wretch starts to slide in, but Strangler runs over to cut that off. However, Wretch reaches out and grabs the feet of Strangler, taking him down to the mat and pulling him to the outside. Wretch goes to work with knife edge chops that back up Strangler, then whips him hard into the steel ring steps. Wretch pulls Strangler back up to his feet and slams his head against the ring apron. With Strangler stunned, Wretch slides him back into the ring and climbs in himself, as the referee calls for the bell to start the match. Strangler struggles up to one knee, then Wretch starts to kick away at his ribs. Wretch grabs the Strangler and pulls him up to his feet, before dropping him with a running bulldog. Wretch goes for the cover but only gets a two count. After the two count, Wretch springs to his feet and runs over to the nearest corner to climb to the top turnbuckle. Brand glances around to Lance and the new arrival of the fWo is taken aback by what he sees. His former Rider teammate who always seems so stoic and unaffected by the events of the sport they participated in had broken down. Tears had streamed from his now red bloodshot eyes. They had started to dry now though as rage had built a dam blocking that show of emotion. He places a heavy hand on Knight's shoulder as he looks one final time at the monitor. One final time at a new monster that had stepped in front of the old. Rage filled his eyes as he stepped away. Looking now into the camera as if looking into the heart of the Boston Strangler he spoke. His thunderous voice shaken by the events he just witnessed it still resonated power and commanded respect. "You son of a bitch, you're dead." Frontier stomps through the gathered masses. Off once again to battle against that darkest hour. Ruben Ross (c) vs. Vic Creed Slaughter heads to the ring for a huge main event, a World Title match pitting two old enemies against each other. These two have met more than once, and more than once a title has changed hands as a result. Will history repeat itself? It's time to find out. "Walk" begins to play, and the crowd boos lustily as Vic Creed appears from backstage, a chilling smile on his face. Creed, oddly enough, sports a black eye. Creed sneers at the taunting fans, then heads to the ring, putting the crowd out of his mind and getting ready to inflict pain, vengeance, and general un-niceness. Creed slides in and feints toward Matt Panzer, who stumbles back. Creed chuckles as Panzer pulls himself to his feet. Creed then unstraps the fWo Internet Title and hands it to a ring attendant, who takes it to the timekeeper. For a few moments, all is silent. There is no sound but the buzzing of the crowd, eagerly awaiting the arrival of the fWo World Heavyweight Champion. Then, it's showtime. "Defy You" hits, and the roof blows off the place as Ruben Ross appears from backstage, looking all kinds of pumped up. Ross exhorts the cheering crowd, and their decibel level rises even higher. Ross makes his way to the ring and walks up the ringsteps. He points a finger at Creed, who points another finger in the direction of Ross the middle one. Ross chuckles, rolls his eyes, and steps into the ring. Ross takes his spot across from Creed and unstraps the World Title. The referee takes it and lifts it high it's up for grabs as of now. He hands it off and it makes the attendant-timekeeper route. Ross and Creed begin to circle. The bell rings. They go for a lockup, but Ross slides between Creed's legs. Before Creed can react, Ross is up and dropkicks Creed in the back of the head, knocking him sternum-first into the corner. Ross charges in and bodysplashes Creed, driving him further into the corner. Ross grabs Creed and whips him across the opposite direction, then runs in. He clocks Creed with a spinning heel kick, hitting him squarely. Ross lands on the apron, and Creed stumbles out of the corner, clearly in a daze. Ross springboards onto the top rope. He leaps off and bulldogs Creed to the mat, then makes a cover. It only gets a two-count, as Creed kicks out hard and with authority he's far from beaten. Ross brings Creed back up to his feet and attempts to lift him for the Spin Cycle, but Creed crashes down a meaty forearm across the back of Ross, staggering him. Creed then bull-rushes Ross and plasters him with a clothesline, knocking him to the mat. With Ross down, Creed drives the point of his elbow into the throat and makes a cover. This also gets two. Creed has the momentum firmly in his corner however, and brings Ross up again. He whips Ross into the ropes and catches him coming off with a thunderous powerslam, driving Ross hard into the mat. Creed hooks a leg and beckons the referee to count, but Ross kicks out at two. Creed brings Ross up and goes for another whip into the ropes, but Ross reaches down and reverses it. As Creed comes off, Ross side-steps and pushes Creed forward, sending him flying from the ring. With Creed on the outside, Ross shakes off the cobwebs and prepares to run the ropes. As Creed gets up, Ross runs, then leaps over the top with a no-hands plancha. Creed, however, catches him using his enormous strength. He holds Ross for a few moments, then pitches him with a fallaway slam, sending him over the guardrail into the crowd. Creed gets up and reaches over, grabbing Ross, then suplexes him back to the ringside area. Creed then lifts Ross to his feet and hefts him high overhead with a gorilla press. Creed begins to do reps with Ross, then drops him to his shoulder and drives him into the thinly-padded floor with the Powerdriver. Ross is down and maybe out, and Creed rolls him into the ring. Creed rolls in himself and makes the cover. One. Two. Kickout by Ross. Creed slams the mat in frustration and gets up to get in the face of the referee, complaining of a slow count. The ref disagrees, and Creed cocks back a fist to clock him. From behind, however, Ross rolls Creed up. The ref quickly drops down to count. One. Two. Kickout. Creed, caught off-guard, barely gets a shoulder up. As the two men get up, Creed charges at Ross again, going for a clothesline, but Ross sees him coming and ducks. As Creed bounces off the ropes, Ross leaps high into the air and nails Creed with a leaping leg lariat. With Creed down, Ross runs into the ropes and leaps onto the second strand, then leaps off with an Asai moonsault. Ross covers and hooks the leg. One. Two. Kickout. Ross, undaunted, brings Creed to his feet. As he does, none other than Austin Davis strolls out from backstage, a smirk on his face. He walks up to the ring and waves at Ross, who shoots him the bird in response. As the action continues inside the ring, Davis pulls up a chair and takes a seat at ringside to watch. Back inside the ring, Ross goes behind Creed and sets him up. He then executes the Paint It Black, driving Creed to the mat in an impressive show of strength. Rather than hold on and go for the cover, Ross decides that he needs to put on some finishing touches. With that in mind, he heads to the top rope. With Creed down, Ross measures him. He then leaps, going for it all. Blackout. Connects. Ross makes the cover and hooks the leg. One. Two. Kickout. Ross is pissed he really thought he had him there. Nonetheless, he brings Creed back up again and gooks him up, preparing for his finisher the Plague of Darkness. Creed, however, uses his remaining strength to block it. He then lifts Ross up and executes a version of his own the Kodiak Krippler. With Ross down, Creed takes a few moments to gather himself, then rolls onto him for the cover. One. Two. Kickout. Creed curses, sure that he had him. With no other choice, he brings Ross to his feet. Creed kicks Ross hard in the gut, doubling him over, then sets him up. He then lifts Ross up and drives him to the mat with a Creedbomb. Creed knows he's in position now. Ross is down, and Ross may be out. It's time to end it it's time for Vic Creed to execute the final maneuver on his way to becoming fWo World Heavyweight Champion for the first time. Creed climbs up to the top rope. He looks down at Ross with disdain. At ringside, Austin Davis gazes on interestedly. Vic Creed leaps. Creedzillinator, a move that has put Ross away before... Misses. Ross rolls to the side, and Creed splats to the mat, landing with a faceplant. The crowd goes wild, their hero still alive. Ross pulls himself to his feet, knowing that he may not get another opportunity this good. He brings Creed up and hooks him up. He lifts Creed into the air. Sit The Fuck Down. Ruben Ross makes the cover. One. Two. Three. Ruben Ross retains the fWo World Title, and the crowd goes wild. Ross is handed the belt and lifts it in the air to the delight of the fans. As he does so, Scott Slugger rushes out from backstage. As Slugger approaches the ring, he stops behind Davis shrugs and pushes Davis' chair over with Davis still in it then resumes sprinting towards the ring. He brings Creed up and begins wailing on him, hitting him with extremely stiff shots, much stiffer than the fans of the fWo are used to seeing. Slugger knocks Creed through the ropes and follows him out, then knocks him into the crowd and follows again. The two stars continue brawling off through the crowd as Davis collects himself and grabs a mic. Inside the ring, Ross chuckles at Davis' misfortune. Davis snarls. "I'm sure that's really funny now, Ruben, but we'll see who gets the last laugh at Body Count... assuming, of course, you're suicidal enough to accept my challenge." Ross gestures for a mic. One is thrown to him. "You're damn right I accept your challenge, you bald idiot. I'll see you at Body Count, Davis... and in fact, I think I might just make you the new Mayor of Bitchville!" Davis sneers. "Will you? That's not gonna happen, Ross. You spend these next few weeks enjoying that title to the fullest... because at Body Count, it belongs to me. Trust me." Davis tosses the mic aside, and "Down With The Sickness" begins to play. Davis and Ross continue to trash-talk, sans mics, as Slaughter goes off the air... Result: Ruben Ross by pinfall
