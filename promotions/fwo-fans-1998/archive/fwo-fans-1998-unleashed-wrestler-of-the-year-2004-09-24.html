Ric Chronos vs. Nameless vs. Wippit Guud It was time for the first elimination match of the night... the match that would offically kick off the 2004 Wrestler of the Year tournament. All remembered how last year Jon Crisp defied the odds to make it to the top of the roster and etch his name in fWo history as the wrestler of the year... and now the waiting was over. Tonight the challenge would begin again and one man would once more rise to the top of the wrestling world. Ric Chronos entered first, fists raised high and smiling at the reaction from the fans. A former fWo World Heavyweight Champion, Chronos was a legend in the industry already... a true master of the sport and mainstay member of the promotion. He made his way to the ring, his gold and black wrestling tights a familiar sight to fans who were interested to see if he could once more rise to glory. As Chronos entered the ring, the fWo Wrestler of the Year tournament brackets were displayed, and Ric Chronos looked up with the rest of the fans at the matches that would be on display tonight. Craig Miles vs. Deacon vs. Dynamo. Vince Jacobs vs. Alias vs. Chelsea Vice. James Renshaw vs. Flying Frenchie vs. Lance Knight. And of course... No music, but smoke and flickering spotlights signalled the next wrestler was about to make his way to the ring. The most mysterious one... Nameless, the masked wrestler who all knew to be once called Temerario. A legend from Mexico, a string of injuries and personal issues had prevented him from rising to the same level in the United States. From the look of determination Nameless wore under his mask, it was clear that tonight he intended to correct that in a big way. Nameless swung into the ring and shook hands with Ric Chronos. Chronos, still wary of the masked man, was nonetheless pleased that an ally of sorts would be in the match with him. It made things easier... and perhaps it would lead to a quick victory and entrance into the final eight members of the tournament. Chronos nodded to Nameless, and the two turned to stare up the ramp and the final member of their three-way match. Wippit Guud shoved the curtains aside and locked eyes with the wrestlers in the ring. Guud, a legendary cult wrestler for the last several years had been in his share of tough moments and hard matches. Having gained a following in other promotions, Guud was still waiting for the right opportunity, the right moment, to really explode inside the fWo. Tonight could be... should be... that moment. Taking off his shirt as he headed to the ring, Wippit Guud tossed it carelessly into the crowd, never letting his gaze drop from his opponents in the ring. He swung himself inside, and the crowd fell silent. They looked at one another. The rules were simple. No time limit, single elimination. One pinfall meant all the difference in the world. Somewhere in the distance, the bell rang. Guud smiled. Showtime. The fans cheered as the quick chain wrestling turned more dangerous... as Guud struggled to maintain his grip on Chronos for a release German suplex.
