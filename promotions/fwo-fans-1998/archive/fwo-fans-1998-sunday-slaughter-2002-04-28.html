fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 28 April 2002

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Sunday Night Slaughter began in September of 1998, and now, after three and a half years, Slaughter's run is over. CyberSlam was twenty four hours ago, and now the fWo rolls into Las Vegas for what will be the final broadcast. The fWo will cap everything off with a historic match as 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly defends the fans Wrestling organization World Championship against 'The World's Greatest Athlete, Scott Slugger. Las Vegas is the city of gambling, and Kelly is undoubtedly rolling the dice by facing a man who has been champion on five previous occasions. Slugger has defeated the legends of the fWo, toppling stars like Scott Collins, Austin Davis, Primetime, The Flying Frenchie, Machina, Jim Dudley, and others. Tonight... the two will do battle in the final match in Slaughter history, as an era comes to a close. Press Conference Photographers had their cameras ready. Several microphones stuck out of the oak podium. The fWo logo curtain hung behind it. Chris Universal stepped up to the podium and ignored the flashes from the star-hungry photographers. "Ladies and gentlemen, I'd like to introduce Cody 'Crusader' Williams, who has a statement about his current physical state. Please welcome him." More flashes went off as Crusader came out dressed in a navy suit. Twenty-four hours changed his mood from ecstatic to somber. He cleared his throat and looked out at the reporters with their tape recorders pointed at him. "Due to the events that occurred last night," he said, and then paused. "Due to the confrontation between Chronos and myself," he said, and then paused. "Due to the attack," he said, and then paused. "Due to," he said, and then paused. Reporters were nudging one another and shrugging their shoulders. Crusader looked across the room, breathing heavily. He took one large gulp. "Ric Chronos's attack on me last night at CyberSlam has rendered me incapable of being a physically active wrestler on the fWo roster. My doctor has told me that the result was a concussion and refused to clear me to wrestle. When I will be OK again is a pending matter. I would like to apologize to my fans and fellow workers of the fWo for this. Most importantly," he said, turning towards Chris Universal. "I would like to apologize to Mister Universal for failing to live up our verbal agreement. The future is uncertain, and I can only hope. Thank you very much." Before reporters got the chance to fire questions away, Crusader walked away from the podium. 'The Cult Icon' Jon Crisp & Mufanga Kagos vs. Not surprisingly, the Las Vegas crowd rises to their feet with anticipation. Fireworks explode on the stage, and a loud roar from the crowd rocks the MGM Grand on this post-CyberSlam edition of Slaughter. "High and Dry" blasts through the arena, and the boos begin to rain down heavily for "The Cult Icon" Jon Crisp. While he seemingly always has a pissed off look on his face, Crisp looks especially angry tonight after losing to Wretch at CyberSlam. Crisp stalks slowly down to the ring, just shaking his head at the fans. As Crisp nears the ring and climbs inside, his music is replaced by "Warrior's Call" and the boos are replaced by cheers for the "Wildman of Lagos" Mufanga Kagos. Jogging down to the ring, Kagos eyes Crisp cautiously before sliding into the ring. The two men stand toe to toe in the ring, each obviously not pleased with having to team with the other. Kagos' music fades out, and for a few moments the only sounds in the arena are the heated words between the two reluctant partners. Then "Nobody's Real" by Powerman 5000 starts up, and something different happens: the fans cheer wildly. "The Golden Child" Monte Burns walks out from the back with a big grin on his face, obviously happy to see the reaction that he's receiving from the crowd. Burns waves his arm in the air to acknowledge the cheers and waits at the top of the ramp. The music fades away, but the cheering remains just as loud as "Battle Flag" by Lo-Fidelity All-Stars signals the entrance of his partner. With the fWo Cruiserweight Title still strapped around her waist, Tempest walks out with a bright smile as well, winking at Burns as she stands next to him. Burns and Tempest make their way to the ring together, soaking in all of the crowd's cheers. As they step up on the apron, Crisp makes it clear to Kagos that he'll be starting the match. Burns does the same in the other corner, and the referee calls for the bell. Crisp steps forward in the ring and looks at Burns, calling for Monte to bring it on. Burns shrugs his shoulders and runs forward, taking Crisp up on the offer. Crisp swings wildly for a clothesline, but Burns ducks that and connects with a back heel kick that dazes Crisp. As Crisp turns around, Burns bounces off the ropes and leaps with a clothesline to take the bigger man down. Burns makes the early cover, but Crisp kicks out at one. Up quickly, Burns makes the tag to Tempest, who leaps over the top rope into the ring. Burns and Tempest grabs Crisp's wrists and whip him into the ropes. The pair try for a double clothesline, but Crisp ducks that and bounces off the other side. Unfortunately for him, Burns and Tempest recover quickly and catch him with a double dropkick as he bounces off. Tempest covers this time, but Crisp makes it out at two. After kicking Crisp in the head a couple of times, Tempest rushes over and climbs to the top of the nearby turnbuckle. As Crisp gets to his feet, Tempest leaps off for a top-rope hurricanrana. This time, however, Crisp is ready for the high flying move and catches Tempest on his shoulders before driving her to the mat with a stiff powerbomb. Burns winces on the ring apron as he watches the impact, and Crisp continues the punishment with boots to Tempest's head. Crisp pulls her up and whips her hard into his corner, following her in with a lariat in the corner. Finally getting a chance to enter the action, Kagos slaps Crisp on the back and tags himself in. Crisp looks over at Kagos with a hard stare but exits the ring. Kagos lays in a couple of knife edge chops in the corner, before pulling Tempest out and whipping her into a neutral corner. Kagos charges in and connects with a vicious shoulder block to Tempest's midsection. Pulling back for a moment, Kagos dives forward with another hard shoulder block before grabbing Tempest by the waist and hitting a northern lights suplex out of the corner. Kagos bridges for the pinfall, but Tempest escapes at two. Both of them get to their feet, and Kagos whips Tempest into the ropes. Kagos goes for a superkick, but Tempest ducks the attempt. Tempest keeps running forward and leaps onto the middle rope, springboarding off the ropes with an asai moonsault that connects just as Kagos turns back around. She's unable to make the cover, however, and both competitors are left on the mat, struggling to reach their respective corners. Burns and Crisp are leaning over the ropes, trying to stretch as far as they can to make it easier for their teammates. Kagos reaches his corner first and makes the tag, allowing Crisp to come charging into the ring. Just as Tempest is about to reach Monte, Crisp runs over and hammers Monte, sending Burns flying off the apron. Crisp turns his attention back to Tempest, pulling by the hair back to the center of the ring. Burns gets back up and tries to get at Crisp, but the referee holds him back, while Crisp taunts him by holding Tempest by the hair. Crisp pulls Tempest up to her feet and knees her repeatedly in the midsection. As Tempest folds over in pain, Crisp grabs her around the waist and plants her with a gutwrench powerbomb. Crisp pulls back up again and lifts Tempest up in the air with a vertical suplex. He holds her up in the air for what seems like forever before finally sending her crashing back down to the mat. Feeling he's done enough, Crisp makes the cover, but Tempest kicks out at two. Kagos calls for the tag on the outside, but Crisp pays him no attention as he lifts Tempest up and sets her on the top turnbuckle of a neutral corner. Crisp starts to climb up the ropes, but Tempest starts to fight him off. Crisp fires back, but Tempest catches him with a hard elbow to the nose that stuns him and knocks him off. Before Crisp can recover, Tempest leaps off and spikes him into the mat with a bulldog. As Tempest crawls towards her corner, Crisp rolls over towards his corner, not really knowing where he is. Once again, Kagos seizes the opportunity and reaches down to slap Crisp on the arm and tag himself in. On the other side, Tempest finally reaches her corner and makes the tag to Burns. As Kagos runs forward, Burns catches him with a series of right hands before going for a whip into the ropes. Kagos reverses the whip and sends Monte in instead, looking for a back body drop. Burns sees it coming and kicks Kagos square in the jaw, then drops him with a side russian legsweep. Knowing exactly what to do next, Burns runs towards the ropes and nails an asai moonsault on Kagos. Burns rolls back over and covers... One... Two... The pinfall attempt is broken up by Crisp, who dropped an elbow across the back of Monte's head. Before Crisp can do anymore damage, he's wiped out by a missile dropkick from Tempest, who had used all of her remaining strength to climb to the top. Meanwhile, Burns pulls Kagos back up by the hair and starts to lock him in for a double-arm DDT. But Kagos manages to fight out of the hold and pushes Burns back against the ropes. As Burns bounces off, Kagos lands a boot to the midsection and drives him into the mat with The Headshrinker. Kagos rolls back over and drapes an arm across Burns... One... Two... Thr-NO! Kickout by Burns just as the official was about to hit three. Kagos smacks the mat in frustration, probably realizing that a better cover would have possibly meant victory. Still, Kagos gets to his feet and pounds his chest to cheers from the crowd, before heading over to the corner. Kagos climbs to the top and looks out at the fans, then leaps off with the Wilddive... But Crisp pulls Burns out of the way. As the crowd looks on in confusion, Crisp pulls Kagos up by the hair, yelling in his face about how he's the one who's supposed to win the match. Before Kagos can do anything in retaliation, Crisp tosses him over the top rope to the floor below, still yelling in his direction. Unfortunately for Crisp, the recovered Tempest stood waiting for him as he turned around. Tempest lands a kick to the stomach and grabs Crisp in a side headlock before running towards the corner and nailing him with the Rottweiler. Having just gotten to his feet, Burns immediately heads for the top turnbuckle in the same corner and raises his arms to the crowd. With Tempest smiling, Burns leaps off and hits the Establishment before making the cover... One... Two... Three! The official signals for the bell, and the crowd erupts for the victory by Monte Burns and Tempest over Mufanga Kagos and Jon Crisp! Tempest helps Burns back up to his feet, and the official holds both of their arms high into the air, as "Nobody's Real" plays again. On the outside, Kagos makes his way to the back, holding his side in pain from the fall. Tempest and Burns start to leave as well, with Burns holding the ropes for Tempest to leave. Burns starts to step through as well, but something stops him. Jon Crisp. Back up to his feet, Crisp grabs Monte from behind by the hair before tossing him across the ring with an overhead belly to belly suplex. Tempest turns to see what's going on and slides into the ring immediately. She starts to go after Crisp, but he turns around just in time to catch her with a hard right to the jaw. Crisp picks her back up again and sends her crashing over the top to the outside. Back to his feet, Burns charges after Crisp and spears him to the mat as soon as he turns around. Burns unloads with a flurry of punches on Crisp, obviously upset about Crisp's treatment of Tempest. Burns starts to pull Crisp up from the mat, but Crisp sneaks in a low blow uppercut that stops Monte in his tracks. Crisp gets up to his feet and sets Burns between his legs before lifting him up and nailing the Icon in the middle of the ring. Crisp stands over Monte's fallen body and glares down at him, showered by boos. But he's still not done yet. Crisp pulls Burns back up to his knees once more and looks to be setting him up for another Icon. This time, however, Crisp lifts Monte up in a traditional powerbomb position. With Burns on his shoulders, Crisp runs forward towards the side of the ring where the entrance ramp is located. As he reaches the ropes, Crisp launches Burns forward, sending him flying to the ouside and crashing to the hard concrete floor below. A hush quickly comes over the crowd, as everyone in the arena looks at Monte's prone body laying on the floor. Crisp just stands motionless in the ring, watching as a stream of officials run from the back to attend to Burns. Tempest hobbles over to Burns as well, still clutching her side in pain from her own fall to the outside. As two EMTs rush out from the back with a stretcher, a few of the officials rush into the ring and escort Crisp to the back. As he walks by, Crisp looks over at the scene with a crazed look in his eye, still not saying a word. Slaughter proceeds to the first commercial break of the night, with the EMT's still looking Burns over... Result: Tempest & Burns by pinfall Announcements Backstage, Chris Universal sat in his chair behind a desk and looked at the papers in front of him. It had been a long night thus far, even though things were just really getting started. The situation with FOX had been quite stressful for him, and it seemed as if he hadn't sleeped in days. He looked at his watch, and realized the last time he got more than fifteen minutes of sleep was on Thursday evening. A deep sigh followed shortly thereafter, and Universal straightened out his suit jacket and tie while cameras prepared to begin rolling. Seconds passed and Universal was given his cue. "Good evening. We hope you all enjoyed CyberSlam last night in Anaheim. The event was a tremendous success, but tonight we have more pressing matters. First, I must announce that Ruben Ross will not be appearing on Slaughter tonight due to injuries sustained from his match with Erik Kelly last night, and due to overall wear and tear his body has endured in the past couple of months. Secondly, tonight's World Title match will go on as planned. Scott Slugger has assured me that he is ready to take on Erik Kelly tonight, despite the amount of punishment dealt to him by Vic Creed and Mitch Wilson last night at CyberSlam. Third... many of you know about the situation, with tonight being the final Slaughter ever. Because of that, the June pay-per-view that was scheduled months ago has been cancelled. Our next pay-per-view will happen in August, and it will be SummerSlaughter. A date and a location will be announced the next time you view fWo programming on the air. It has been a tremendous ride thus far, but unfortunately, Slaughter is no more. I hope you enjoy the rest of the night's proceedings, and look forward to the next time fWo programming is live on the air. Please continue to visit fWoWrestling.com for the latest news and information regarding that and much more." Being There He was tired, he was beaten up, and he was jet-lagged, but dammit, Lance Knight was there! He had managed to catch a flight from Detroit, Michigan, to Las Vegas, Nevada, just so that he could be backstage for the final fWo Sunday Night Slaughter. Parking his rental car in the talent lot, Lance gets out and walks to the talent-only entrance, flashing his backstage pass and making his way into the MGM Grand. No sooner than he opens the door than he bumps into Eli Flair, the larger than life specimen who had outlasted Donaven Winters in a brutal contest for the Hardcore Title last night. Backing up in awe a step, Lance smiles at Flair, hoping he hasn't angered the giant of a man too much. Instead, he is met with a smile and clap on the shoulder from Flair, who is a good half foot taller than Lance and outweighs him by fifty pounds. "Good show out there last night, kid." "Yeah, same to you, Mr. Flair. You and Winters stole the show." "Call me Eli. Where you headed now?" "Oh, I don't know, I wasn't scheduled for anything tonight, so I was just gonna hang out back here and soak in the atmosphere. This was just one of those nights I couldn't miss." "Tell ya what - c'mon with me, we'll send this shit off right. How'zzat sound?" "Sounds great, Mr. Flai... Eli." With another clap on the shoulder, "Total Elimination" Eli Flair walks down the hallway, Lance Knight in tow, until they reach a door with Flair's name on it. When the door opens, Lance nearly faints upon seeing who is inside. Black Quicksilver vs. 'Kodiak' Vic Creed Slaughter returned to the ring once again, and as the crowd talked amongst themselves, their conversations were obviously cut short as "Slave To The Grind" by Skid Row played over the speakers. Fans jolted up out of their chairs and turned to the stage, holding up their signs in hopes of getting them seen on camera. And as Black Quicksilver appeared on the stage, the roar of the crowd welcomed the perennial STUMP Squad member out to the ring. BQ darted down the shiny entrance steelramp and rolled into the ring, before raising his arms -- and then pausing, remembering whom he was up against tonight and deciding that he shouldn't get his hopes up. BQ acknowledged the crowd with a wave before taking to a corner and waiting. The cheers from the audience transformed into passionate hate, as "Walk" by Pantera brings 'The Main Man' out onto the stage. Creed puts his arms at his sides and glances at the crowd, and automatically comes to the conclusion that he's better than every single person in attendance. Creed scowls with disgust at the audience one last time, before making his walk towards the ring and focusing on his opponent. As Creed approached ringside, BQ began to realize he had to take a different approach to the situation. BQ begins walking towards Creed. Creed seems amused by this as he nears the ring. But amusement turns to shock as BQ catapaults him over the top rope with a pescado. Creed shows his lightning quick reflexes and catches him. He smiles, before shaking his head and looking for a place to dump BQ. Creed realized that the entrance ramp was a good location, and began walking in that general direction. As Creed reached his destination, the Alaskan Juggernaut turned back and faced the ring, before dropping BQ with a fallaway slam. Or so he thought. BQ managed to land on his feet, and as KVC returned to a vertical base, he glanced at the crowd and noticed a few of them were cheering. Not used to this reaction, he knew something was up and turned around to find BQ, standing. A bewildered Creed began walking to BQ who as Creed swung, jumped up onto the shoulders of the main man, and went for a victory roll on the ramp. The two men rolled down the ramp to ringside, and BQ landed on top. Creed snared and shot BQ off of him with a push. BQ went backwards into the ring apron, where he climbed up to and waited for Creed to get up. Creed did, and BQ leaped off with a flying forearm. This stagged the longest reigning fWo Internet Champion in history, and BQ again pummeled him with a forearm. BQ grabbed Creed and rolled KVC into the ring, but Creed had other plans. He caught Quicksilver with an elbow, before grabbing him and sending him into the nearest set of ringsteps. Creed cracked his knuckles, and then his neck, before walking over and picking up what remained of BQ, before slinging him over his shoulder and walking up the ring stairs. Creed reached the top and onto the ring apron, before snapmaring him over the top rope into the ring. Quicksilver landed with a thud, and Creed climbed in before telling the referee to do his job and ring the bell. The referee obliged as Creed backed BQ into the corner and fired off shot after shot. Lefts and rights dazed BQ, and KVC wasn't finished. He brought BQ up and whipped him into the opposite corner, before charging in shoulder first. All three hundred plus pounds pounded the rib area of BQ, and Creed was now hitting a groove. He grabbed BQ and brought him up, before taking him down with a Northern Lights suplex. Creed continued to dish out punishment, this time using his foot to carve a niche in BQ's mask covered skull. Creed laughed before taking BQ and doing something that damn near could have killed BQ. It didn't even appear if Creed cared about winning the match -- after last night, he was in the mood to hurt someone. Badly. Creed threw BQ like a dart, over the top rope to the floor. Quicksilver' body landed with a dull thud, and Creed smiled as the crowd reacted with jeers. Creed looked to the outside to see if BQ was moving, but he wasn't. Creed waited as the referee began to count BQ out. One. Two. Three. Four. Creed glanced down again before looking at his wrist as if he was looking at his watch. "Ten seconds doesn't last this long." He told the referee to count faster. Creed had other things on his mind and better things to do. He didn't care about pinning BQ, just maybe hurting him and winning the match by any means necessary. Seven. Eight. Nine. The crowd slowly began to cheer. BQ was standing. The ten count stopped as BQ fell back down to the ground. "No fucking way." Creed glared and joined Quicksilver outside. Creed grabbed BQ and set him face first against the ring steps. He quickly looked for a chair. Creed found one and walked back over. "He ain't getting up after this", Creed thought. He raised the chair into the air and prepared to knock BQ out cold. But the referee grabbed the chair from behind. An incensed Creed snickered and turned around. Creed pushed the referee, and the chair fell to the ground. Creed grabbed it and prepared to deliver a sickening chairshot to Quicksilver. But the referee snatched it again. This time Creed' massive hands grabbed the neck of the official, who immediately began signaling for the bell. His face turned bright red as Creed squeezed the life out of him. "Stay out of my business." His business or not, the match was over -- BQ apparently would win by countout. Creed tossed the referee aside before looking down at BQ and deciding to spare him. "I've got bigger fish to fry." Creed began walking up the ramp as Matt Panzer announced Quicksilver was the winner by disqualification. The crowd cheered at this, intending to get under KVC' skin, but he didn't care. He didn't look back. Slaughter went to a commercial break, as referee's and other personnel came down to look over BQ and the referee. Result: BQ by DQ The Era Of Eradication Begins The world had witnessed the beginning of an era last night, as the most coveted prize in our sport changed hands. The fWo World Championship went one from one fan favorite to another, and either way, regardless of the result, the fans would have been happy. Last year, Mitch Wilson & Ric Chronos had held the title for a majority of the year... and to have this year begin with the longest reign in fWo World Title history by Ruben Ross, and the new champion being someone fans equally liked, this was a welcome change. The lights turned to darkness, but two spotlights shined on the entrance ramp. The sounds of 'Drowning Pool' brought the crowd to their feet. 'Let The Bodies Hit The Floor' began to whisper throughout the MGM Grand. The spotlights moved back and forth on the stage, making the stage the only place visible in the arena. Fans in the crowd held lighters in the air and as cameras showed an overhead shot of the crowd, it made for quite a visual effect combined with the sweeping spotlights. Finally, the spotlights stopped on one place on the stage, in front of the entrance. "POWERBOOOOOOOOMB!" roared, and out from the curtains appeared the new fans Wrestling organization World Heavyweight Champion. 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly was here. The new shirtless champion sported the gold around his waist, as the jean clad warrior stopped on the stage. The spotlights shone on him, and him alone. Kelly inhaled and began walking down the stage as other spotlights began panning down the ramp with him. Kelly reached ringside and leaped onto the ring apron, before turning and looking out to the crowd. The crowd was at a fever pitch, letting out it's largest reaction of the night. "This feels damn good", Kelly thought, before climbing over the ropes with ease. Kelly raised his arms before walking to one corner and looking out at the fans. He had waited for this moment for so long, perhaps you could say he even lusted after this very moment in time. To be champion. Kelly yelled out at the crowd, his emotions taking over, and not being able to control what was building up inside of him. This was a good emotional outburst, as Kelly had been waiting to do it since he defeated Ross last night in the ring. Kelly was given a microphone and as the lights returned to show the entire arena, Kelly stopped in the center and took in the scene. Seventeen thousand, one hundred and fifty seven fans were standing. Clapping. And cheering their new champion. Kelly' music faded and Kelly looked around. A "Kelly!" chant had broken out. Kelly expected a huge reaction, but not one of this magnitude. It finally quieted down enough for him to speak. "The Era... Of Eradication... Has Begun!" Kelly growled, his voice booming throughout the arena as the fans cheered his every word. "Last night, at CyberSlam V, in front of the entire world, I defeated Ruben Ross right here in the very center of this ring for this." Kelly began removing the title from his waist before holding it into the air. "The fWo World Championship!" The fans cheered wildly again. Kelly draped the title over his shoulder, where it would remain. "And now, after all this time... after years of being in the fWo and being doubted by the critics, and the cynics, The Eradicator stands alone on the top of the mountain, with a sea of clones looking up!" No matter what Kelly said right now, the fans would cheer. Kelly probably could have said, "I think you all should kill yourselves, right now!" and the fans likely would have reacted positively and proceeded to do it. "Make no mistake about it. The match could have gone either way last night. Ross very well could have defeated me, just like he did last year in Miami." Kelly paused to reflect. "But last night, was an entirely different story. My story. The story of a man who proved everyone who ever doubted that he could do it, that he could win the big one. Well, guess what, armchair critics? I DID IT!" "And now... now that I'm on top, I don't plan on going down anytime soon. Tonight, on the final broadcast of Sunday Night Slaughter..." the fans booed as Kelly nodded in agreement. "... I have the unenviable task of taking on the World's Greatest Athlete, Scott Slugger. He's held this five times... and while that's nice, I'd rather hold it once for a long time, like maybe *five* years. And that's exactly what I plan on doing. Tonight, it starts with Slugger. And it will continue. The Era Of Eradication has only begun..." Kelly grabbed his title and raised it into the air once more. "And there isn't a damn thing anyone can do about it!" The fans agreed with this statement and stood to their feet, applauding Kelly. Kelly gave the cameras a look of confidence, before his music once again started up. Kelly took to a corner and played to the crowd, while backstage... someone was watching, and he was highly sure he was going to do something about it. Rage Cameras quickly went backstage where Vic Creed watched on a backstage monitor, obviously unimpressed. "There isn't a damn thing anyone can do about it... is that so, Kelly? Well... I think you're wrong. In fact, I know you're wrong. If I have anything to say about it... The Era Of Eradication would be over tonight. Slugger gets the shot... when I beat him within an inch of his life last night at CyberSlam... that's the biggest crock of shit I've ever heard. It should be the 'Main Man' getting that shot, I know it, Kelly knows it, Slugger knows it, and Universal knows it." Creed pushed over the monitor which had other pieces of equipment hooked up to it. The monitor crashed and stagehands were startled by the commotion. Creed began pushing things over and throwing things when security and referee's arrived on the scene. Creed pushed them out of his way before he saw Universal coming down a hall. "Universal... what the hell kind of crap is this?" Universal seemed unaware of what Creed was talking about. "I should be the one facing Kelly tonight... not Slugger!" Universal put his hands on his hips. "Vic, you lost last night. And according to the stipulations, the winner would face Kelly tonight. You didn't win." Creed pushed Universal against the wall and security began trying to pry the 'Main Man' off of him. Creed and Universal yelled at each other as Universal ordered security to escort Creed to his dressing room and make sure he stayed there, a task even Universal wouldn't do himself. Security complied but knew it would be difficult. Slaughter proceeded to a commercial break, and Universal headed off in another direction as Creed continued to cause trouble. fWo Hardcore Champion Eli Flair vs. Grendel Fresh off a controversial loss to Lance Knight at CYBERSLAM, Grendel entered the arena to the sounds of "Everything's Cool" by Pop Will Eat Itself. He ignored the reaction of the fans and simply headed to the ring, taking but a moment to raise his hand to a fan that got a bit too close. "Phucking Phreak" by Velvet Acid Christ broke the mood, as the word "ELI" appeared on the video wall, signalling the arrival of the new fWo Hardcore Champion, Eli Flair. He appeared on the ramp with a somewhat better mood etched on his face than Grendel, but with an arm wrapped in athletic tape, and a definite limp in his step. Behind him, Poison Ivy raised the Hardcore Championship belt high above her head. The bell rings, and these men lock up. Grendel, one of the few men in the business to have a height and weight advantage over Flair, coupled with still- healing injuries, easily sends the new Hardcore Champion into the corner. He riddles Flair's abdomen with hard punches, knocking the wind outta him. He pulls Flair out of the corner by the hair, and rakes his back with those claws! He hooks Flair around the waist and attempts to pull his opponent up for a powerbomb, but Eli is able to catch him behind the knees and lift, dropping Grendel to the mat with a backdrop. Eli, however, favors his left knee at this move, as it is obviously still tender from his war the night before. He's able to be on his feet before Grendel can take advantage, and he circles his challenger. Grendel throws a fake punch to get Eli off guard, but he doesn't fall for it. Eli is able to catch Grendel's arm, fully extended, and maneuver behind his opponent. Grendel fires an elbow backwards that catches Eli on the temple, breaking the armlock. Grendel whipped Eli into the ropes, but Eli ducked the resulting clothesline, coming off the other side to hit Grendel with a clothesline of his own that dumped both men over the top rope and onto the floor. The fans cheered the change of pacing, as Eli pulled himself back to his feet, his back to Grendel. The Living Nightmare had liberated the top set of ring steps, and was measuring the champion for a definite match- ending shot. Eli, turning, saw him out of the corner of his eye and was at least prepared to take the blow, if not outright block it. Grendel caught him with the stairs on his bad arm, but he was able to roll with the impact, staggered back to the announce table.
