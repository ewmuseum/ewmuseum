Countdown '00: 3, 29 December 2000

29 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "I watched you change into a fly I looked away you were on fire and I watched a change in you it's like you never had wings now you feel alive I've watched you change I took you home set you on the glass I pulled off your wings then I laughed and I watched a change in you now you feel alive now you feel so alive I've watched you change like you never had wings I look at the cross then I look away give you the lungs to blow me away" "Change" by Deftones Introduction Friday began innocently enough. The weather in Orlando was quite usual for this time of the year, with temperatures making for a atmosphere best described as nice and warm. Whereas everywhere else, snowstorms raged on. Roads covered in black ice. Power outages. Absolute hell. Not in Florida. And I bet the wrestlers were thankful for that. Backstage in the venue formerly known as the Orlando Arena, workers such as Mike Bear, Fear, Hellrazor, Black Quicksilver & Erik Kelly watched James Armstrong walk by from afar. If you haven't been keeping score at home, Armstrong is the acting owner of the fans Wrestling organization, and has been looking to sell the promotion since mid-September of this year. One thing noticeable about Armstrong is that several men in suits were following him, and the tension was apparent as they walked by. The workers gawked as they walked by, and wondered just loud enough amongst themselves so only they could hear each other. "I wonder what Finn is thinking about this." Kelly said, to nobody in particular. BQ nodded. "I bet he's probably really mad, n' stuff." Bear however wasn't so sure. "Well, what if Finn doesn't even know? I mean, have any of you heard from him in months?" BQ shrugged. "I don't even think Finn knows who I am. I know he knows who STUMP is, though. Speaking of STUMP, I gotta go talk to the guys. See ya later." Fear burped loudly, before saying goodbye to BQ. A few moments of silence passed, before a foul smell arose from below. "Hey, who farted?" Hellrazor wondered loudly, while Countdown To Oblivion went elsewhere... The Meeting A camera took us to an office, and Armstrong and the men who followed him into the arena entered it quietly. The men began looking around the office, even to the point of snooping. This caught Armstrong offguard. "Hey, I don't have any S&M porn in here! I don't take that stuff on the road anymore, and I'd appreciate if you'd quit snoopin' around!" One of the men had a sympathetic smile on his face, almost as if he felt sorry for Armstrong. "We're not interested in that, James." Armstrong scratched his head. "Oh, well, what are you interested in?" Another man spoke up. "Cameras. We'd appreciate it if our meeting wasn't filmed, you know, for security reasons." The man gave a small pen over to one of his colleagues, who barely glanced at it, and handed it back. "Looks like we've found a hidden camera. James, do you know of anyone who'd be interested in our meetings?" Armstrong pondered the question, as if it wasn't obvious. Everyone employed by the company he runs on behalf of Chris Finn just might be interested, well, at least one would think. Instead, Armstrong couldn't think of anyone. "Nah, I doubt anyone would want to know the details of our deal. I mean, people don't understand legal mumbo jumbo like that, right? Not smart people anyways, and let's face it, we're not in a state full of smart people. They can't count for crying out loud!" The men looked at each other, and slightly chuckled at Armstrong' attempt at humor. Either way, we wouldn't find out what happened next - instead, we faded to black as a result of the camera being dismembered. Crusader vs. Plague (w/ Stone & War) Countdown To Oblivion began inside the TD Waterhouse Centre with a bang, as "Paranoid" by Black Sabbath started out. Boos scattered throughout the arena, while fireworks continued to shoot off on the stage, where Plague, War & Stone (The Dark Riders) now appeared. The Riders entered the ring, and the fans boos turned to cheers, as "Switchback" by Biohazard began. This brought out Crusader, who was of course accompanied by Clarisse. They entered the ring, and Clarisse and Stone exchanged glares, before the referee forced everyone out of the ring except the participants of the match. The bell rang, and the match was on. Crusader hit a few signature moves early in attempt to win the match quickly, but Plague countered with interference from Stone & War. And it was interference that cost Crusader the match at the end, even though he had it clearly in his grasp. Crusader went to finish things off with the Gravity 101, but War hopped up on the ring apron, and distracted both he and the referee. The referee did his job and tried to get War to get off, and this gave Stone her opportunity to interject in the match. She pushed Crusader off towards the center of the ring, and Crusader hit with a thud. Plague then brought him up and finished things off with the Dark Driver for the one-two-three. The Dark Riders celebrated, while Crusader tried to tell the referee he was cheated. The referee however said he saw nothing, and his decision was final. Meanwhile, Stone shot a sarcastic grin in the direction of Clarisse, who was checking on Crusader. Clarisse returned the grin with a glare, while Countdown To Oblivion rolled on elsewhere. Result: Plague by pinfall Demands Backstage cameras caught up with the reigning fWo Hardcore Champion, Carnage. Carnage had some papers in his hand, although it wasn't clear to everyone else what was written on those papers. Carnage came to a door and knocked. James Armstrong appeared, but before he could actually speak, he was spoken to. "I demand a match with Mud! HAHAHAhahaha!" Armstrong looked back to his office, and said he'd be right back. Armstrong closed the door behind him, and gave Carnage a blank expression on his face. "I... er... what was your name again?" Carnage glared at him, before walking towards him, which pushed Armstrong up against his office door. "Look, you haven't said one word to me since I've been here - but I want a match with MUD. Tomorrow night. Countdown 2." Armstrong raised his eyebrows, and nodded. "Okay, but who's MUD? Look, sir, there's no MUD on the fWo roster, sorry to tell you." Carnage snapped, and began yelling. Something about promises of violence if Armstrong didn't comply with his demands. Armstrong finally calmed him down, and set the match. After Carnage left the area, Armstrong had noticed the papers Carnage was holding had been discarded to the ground when Carnage turned angry. Seeing as how Armstrong already didn't like Carnage, he grabbed the papers and crumpled them into a ball, and threw them into a wastepaper basket nearby. Armstrong then re-entered his office, while C3 continued elsewhere... Rock Scissors Paper "Look, I think I should face STUMP's kidnapper tonight. I mean, STUMP likes me best, doesn't he?" said Mike Heftel, looking at his STUMP Squad teammates with sincerity. BQ frowned. "Yeah, but STUMP is my best friend. Well, besides you guys. But there would be STUMP without me! I found him, he's mine!" Dragon pointed out that anyone could have found STUMP, and BQ was just in the right place at the right time. BQ disagreed. "And I suppose I won the Cruiserweight Title five times by pure luck, too?" Dragon and Heftel looked at each other with devious grins, and nodded. BQ frowned. "So!" Finally, Heftel offered up a solution to their problem. "Okay, we'll play a game of rock scissors paper. Whoever wins, will face STUMP's kidnapper tonight, no questions asked. Alright?" BQ & Dragon nodded, before the game began. BQ & Dragon went first, and BQ's rock smashed Dragon' scissors. Now it was down to BQ & Heftel. BQ again went for the rock, while Heftel revealed paper, and covered BQ's rock... like so. Heftel had won, and he shouted out as such with glee. "I won!" BQ frowned, while Countdown rolled on... More Speculation The discussion about the uncertainty of the fWo continued between The Underground and Erik Kelly. "A lot sure has changed in the last four months since Finn left." Fear noted. Hellrazor offered up his thought. "I still don't know who farted." Everyone else rolled their eyes, before Hellrazor got serious. "No, man, things change. It's the inevitable. Bosses come and go, but the people, they never change. We haven't changed. We may have lost a few people on the way, but the fWo is still the same." Bear concurred. "Yeah, even though Armstrong is a buffoon and has screwed things up beyond belief, the fWo is still the same. At least the attitude is. I mean... we could be in WCW, right?" Everyone chuckled. "Yeah, you're right." said Kelly. "But... why are you so optimistic? You've been kept from the fWo World Title for the last two months. You should've faced Primetime at Fallout, not Mitch. You shouldn't have given your shot to Frenchie, either." Bear shrugged. "Can't change the past... just hope for the best in the future. I don't know what I'll do if I don't win tonight. Ric' on a roll, but I've got a bad feeling about tonight." Kelly looked at Bear as if he knew something everyone didn't. "Mike, do you know something we don't?" Bear shrugged. "All I know is I have a bad feeling about tonight, that's all. Something's not right. All the same, I'd rather be here than anywhere else. Even if our acting owner's a total retard." Everyone chuckled, before Pamela Dee arrived on the scene to tell Kelly his match was coming up. Kelly said goodbye to the others and wished Bear luck in his long awaited match with Chronos, before heading towards his lockerroom with Dee... while Countdown headed back to the ring for another match. 'The Ego' Greg Davis (c) vs. 'The Future' Lance Star Inside the arena, the fans prepared themselves for another match, as "My Generation" by Limp Bizkit brought out the cocky & arrogant Lance Star. The self-proclaimed future of professional wrestling entered the ring, and did some light exercises, before waiting for his opponent to arrive. As one of the latest Bizkit hits faded, it was replaced by an old time hit - "Eye Of The Tiger" by Survivor. The fWo Cruiserweight Champion Greg Davis walked out onto the stage, and like Lance, was greeted by boos from the crowd. Rather than cheer for someone in this match, the fans in attendance urged for both men to kill each other, as it was clear nobody liked 'em. The referee held the Cruiserweight Title in the air, reminding everyone what was on the line in this one. He signalled for the bell, and the match began with a transition of mat holds. Each man tried to get advantage, before Davis decided to get the advantage through underhanded means with a low-blow. He followed it up by going for the 'Davis DDT', but Lance turned it into a nearfall with a Northern Lights Suplex. The match continued at a fast pace, and the end came with Lance attempting to position Davis on the top rope for the Super Shooting Star... but Davis countered into a 'Davis DDT" from the top rope for the pin. Davis celebrated, but his celebration was cut short as Lance attacked him from behind, and demanded another rematch for tomorrow night's show... Countdown went to a commercial break in the meantime. Result: Davis by pinfall Taking Out The Trash Coming back from the commercial break, cameras continued to wait outside the office of James Armstrong, hoping for a glimpse, a split second of footage of what was going on in the meeting. One of the arena janitors came by, and found some containers of trash nearby. Including the wastepaper basket that now contains whatever papers Carnage was holding in his confrontation with Armstrong from earlier. The janitor emptied it into his large trash bin, before getting on his walkie talkie and telling whoever answered it on the other end that his job was done, and he would dump the trash he had gathered outside the arena in a dumpster. On that strange note, Countdown headed back to the ring... Mike Heftel vs. STUMP's kidnapper Countdown continued in the arena, as a figure emerged from backstage. Upon closer inspection, the person was dressed with a black shirt, blue jeans and wore a black mask to cover their face. The shirt they wore had some text on it, that read "I STOLE STUMP!" on it, and had a "MISSING" picture of STUMP on the back, complete with STUMP's description. The fans realized that he was STUMP's kidnapper, but couldn't recognize him, given the fact he had covered up his true identity pretty well. As he walked down the ramp, from behind came Mike Heftel, and he tried to attack STUMP's kidnapper from behind - but he didn't know how. He was going to tap him on the shoulder, but decided that he should just attack him unannounced. He prepared to do a double axehandle, but the kidnapper turned around and kicked Heftel in the stomach, before laying him out with a DDT on the ramp. The kidnapper then rolled Heftel in the ring, and asked the referee to ring the bell. He then covered him for the pin, and it was evident that the STUMP Squad had two more chances to find out his identity and get STUMP back - or STUMP would never be seen again. STUMP's kidnapper got on the microphone and reminded Dragon & BQ of this fact, and laughed evily. The fans booed, while Countdown went to a commercial break, not so shocked that Heftel had lost... Result: STUMP's kidnapper by pinfall Strange Visitor The master of eradication and his valet made preparations for Kelly' match in the lockerroom. Kelly finished lacing up his other boot, while Pamela warmed him up by massaging his shoulders. Kelly then stood up and did some stretches, before asking Dee to retrieve his fWo International Title for him, while he excused himself and exited to the nearby bathroom. For some... odd... reason, the cameras followed him in, but videotaped him from behind so they couldn't see him relieving himself. Kelly finished up, before walking over to the sinks, and washing his hands. Kelly showed that if nothing he had good manners and took care of himself. At least he wasn't pissing all over the bathroom floor like most people in this godforsaken world do. Anyways, Kelly prepared to leave, before he heard something. A voice, it seemed. "Just a minute." Kelly looked around the room, and looked towards the other stalls. All of them were empty except for one. "Identify yourself", the person inside the stall exclaimed. "It's Erik. Erik Kelly", the Eradicator stated. The person inside the stall sigh... not of relief, but not of worry, either. "You could be doing so much better than that piece of tin you've got now." Kelly smirked. "Oh yeah? Do tell, oh wise master of the porcelain." The unidentified visitor wasn't pleased by Kelly' sarcasm. "Don't be a wiseass. I can give you advice - I'm great at advice. I've been in this business a long time, Erik. I've seen it all and done it all. You want to succeed, here's some advice. You're six foot eleven. You should be dominating matches, and not with the help of a cinderblock, either. You're smarter than most men in this business that are your size. You want to do better, you become a monster. An uncontrollable monster. Nobody can stop you, but they can contain you. And if they can contain you, if they can capitalize on what few weaknesses you have, you're beatable. Don't expose your weaknesses, and you're unstoppable." Kelly seemed interested in what he had to say, but he did afterall have a match to prepare for. "Look... you're probably right, but I've got a match to prepare for. Why don't we talk later tonight, after the matches? Who are you, anyways?" The man rejected his attempt to conversate later. "No... I'm leaving after your match. But I will be at Countdown 2." Kelly reached for a towel and wiped his hands off. "Okay - let's meet up. How may I be able to recognize you?" The man coughed. "My voice. Once you hear me, we'll talk." Kelly seemed bewildered by this statement, but he threw his towel in a nearby bin. "Fair enough. See you then - or hear you, rather." Kelly exited the bathroom, and Dee asked who he was talking to. "I don't know, Pam. Let's get on deck." Kelly seemed rather upbeat than usual, and this surprised Dee. Countdown headed to the ring, for the introductions of our next match. 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly vs. 'Black Plague' Ruben Ross vs. 'Kodiak' Vic Creed "Walk" by Pantera brought out the self-proclaimed 'Main Man', otherwise known as 'Kodiak' Vic Creed. Creed was greeted with boos from the crowd, and Creed passed it off as the fans being jealous of his incredible physique and overall looks. Creed reached the ring and ripped his brand new "America's Superior Specimen" shirt off his Greek God-like body, while his music was replaced by "Firestarter" by Prodigy. This brought out the fWo Survivor winner and former Cruiserweight Champion 'Black Plague' Ruben Ross. Ross was welcomed with loud cheers from the crowd, who were obviously quite fond of the man who claimed could pop that twenty share. Ross entered the ring and immediately began going at it with Creed. These two men were no strangers to each other, as they had battled each other just twelve days ago at the most recent fWo pay-per-view, Twilight. Creed eventually got the upperhand with a spinebuster, before 'No Leaf Clover' by Metallica started up. Out onto the stage stepped the fWo International Champion, 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly. Creed immediately turned his attention to the stage, and beckoned Kelly to get out there so they could settle their differences. This was by all accounts the third live show in a row they had some type of physical altercation with one another. Kelly & Creed went at it, and Ross joined the fray. The match was probably the sleeper match of the night, and featured an all out brawl in the crowd, before heading back to ringside. Creed rolled Ross into the ring, while Kelly struggled to regain his senses in the crowd. Creed hit the Hangman's DDT on 'Black Plague' and prepared to pin the match to win his first title in the fWo, but before he could, Kelly entered the ring and grabbed Creed from behind and without stopping, threw him over the top rope to the floor on the otherside. Kelly then brought Ross up and chokeslammed him, before signalling to the crowd that this match was over with a yell of "POWERBOOOOOOOOOOMB!" that echoed throughout the former Orlando Arena. He then sought to make good on his claims, bringing Ross up and then back down with the Eradication, and covering him for the pin. Ross had suffered a Hangman's DDT, a chokeslam and the best powerbomb in the business, and prior to that was on the receiving end of a double-team belly to back suplex in the crowd in a rare moment of teamwork by Creed & Kelly. Although he had lost, it was somewhat of a moral victory for Ross, as it took quite a lot to put him down for the count. Kelly celebrated his win with Dee, before 'Mr. Main Event' Mitch Wilson appeared on the scene, and laid Kelly out from behind with a chair. Wilson then said he wanted his rematch tomorrow night at Countdown 2, or things would be too damn bad for Kelly' own damn good. Countdown 3 headed quickly to it's second to last commercial of the night... Result: Kelly by pinfall Not Wrestling Tonight Countdown 3 returned from the commercial break, to find Primetime & The Black Panther talking backstage. The cameras zoomed in and captured the video and the audio of the encounter as well. "Don't worry, I'll take care of this. I wasn't happy when I found out I was booked against you either, but don't worry - I'll fix it." Panther nodded, before Primetime took a walk down the hallway, to the office of Armstrong. Primetime knocked, before the obviously exhausted James Armstrong appeared. He sighed, as Primetime was the last person he wanted to see right now. Before he could say a word, Primetime did the talking. "Look, Daniel and I have decided we're not wrestling tonight. I don't want the Internet Title... I'm a freakin' World Champion. I deserve a rematch with Time-Boy, and I don't care who've you've promised title shots to, or awarded title shots to. Wretch, Austin Davis, Mike Bear, little red riding hood, I deserve a rematch, and I better get one on the next two shows. You hear me? I'm not wrestling tonight, and you can't make me." Armstrong was furious. "What? That's a breach of contract - I could sue you, or worse, just release you on the spot right now. You're lucky you're not one of the superstars I was more or less forced to release at the request of the potential new management. I would've done so in a heartbeat, Brian. You may not be wrestling tonight, but tomorrow night, since I'm still the boss around here, since I'm still in charge, you're going to wrestle tomorrow night against the Panther. And if you and he don't actually wrestle, if you decide to just lay down for him or whatever, I will do whatever I can to convince the new management that you're a liability to this company. You've ended careers, you've ruined big moments, and you're not going to get away -" Armstrong was rudely interrupted. "Listen here, baldo. You can sit here and be anal about this all you want. But you more or less just threatened me - and you know what happens when people threaten me. No, I'm not going to do something to you, but I'm going to prove to the new management that I'm the only star around here. It's all about me. It's all about Primetime. And that little World Title match you've got coming up in a few seconds? I'm going to prove that neither of those two jackasses are in my league. So you go tell the new management that they can go fornicate with a rusty fork for all I care - I'll be able to find work in this business, because I'm a star. I'm the star. People pay to see me cripple people because they thrive on violence. You may not realize that, but I do. And this company would be nothing without me. So you go back to your little meeting, you tell the new management that if they're going to keep anyone around in this company, it's going to be me." Primetime moved Armstrong out of his way with a nudge, before exiting. Armstrong slammed his fist against the wall, and regretted it, as his hand was now in immense pain. "God damn Brian. Sometimes I wonder what Pierre saw in that punk." Cameras took us elsewhere... Discovery For some odd reason, our cameras took us outside the arena, where a row of dumpsters sat, with heap upon heap of trash inside them. Suddenly, one of the dumpsters opened with a single hand from the inside. Another hand was there, and the two pushed the lid of the dumpster up. It was MUD & Toad, two men who weren't yet officially in the fWo. Their resumes spoke for themselves, as if anyone doubted their credentials. They had most recently wrestled in the now closed Tornado Wrestling, and fared quite well there. No matter. The cameras revealed that MUD & Toad had been sleeping, as they both stretched and yawned at the same time. "Let's find something to eat, little buddy." MUD & Toad delved into the dumpster to find something, anything. They found several plates of uneaten buncake. They prepared to consume the food, before they each dropped their paper plates. The buncake crashed in the dumpster, in amongst the other trash. "At least we can still e-e-eat the crumbs!" exclaimed Toad. MUD nodded, and they reached inside, before spotting something. A crumpled paper ball. MUD decided to investigate, and he opened the letter. "What is it?" Toad asked. MUD inspected it closely. "I can't read it too well, but it looks like someone was going to try to kidnap someone... wait a minute, they were going to try to kidnap YOU!" Toad' jaw fell open. "M-m-me?! W-w-hy?!" MUD shrugged. "I bet it was... Carnage." Toad nodded, while Countdown went to it's final commercial break. The fWo World Title match was up next. Ric Chronos (c) vs. Mike Bear Countdown To Oblivion 3 returned from the commercial break, and fans inside the arena anxiously awaited the main event. Although they had been cheated out of a match thanks to the decision on the part of Primetime & The Black Panther not to wrestle tonight, they knew that their favorites Ric Chronos & Mike Bear would more than make up for it. Matt Panzer introduced the challenger of the night, Mike Bear, who entered to his usual music. "Dragula" by Rob Zombie roared throughout the arena, 'causing the fans to go nuts over one of their favorite superstars. Bear rolled into the ring and struck a brief pose, before partaking in some light warm-up exercises in preparation for his match two months to the day in the making. His music faded, and was replaced by the more obscure theme of the champion. "Christian Says" by Tones On Tail started up, and out walked the fWo World Champion to a deafening ovation. The 2000 Wrestler Of The Year sprinted to the ring, and rolled in. He gave the fWo World Title to the referee, before walking over to the nearest corner, standing to the second rope and raising his arms in the air. This prompted the fans to cheer more loudly, before Chronos resumed to a standing position on the canvas. Chronos walked to the center of the ring, as did Bear. A brief staredown ensued, and then both champion and challenger shook hands, before telling the referee to ring the bell. The bell rang, and the first Countdown main event was on. The two men locked up in the center of the ring with a collar and elbow tie-up, but not until after circling each other several times, to feel each other out for the match. Chronos took the advantage with a side headlock, but Bear countered into a hammerlock. Chronos tried to escape in the traditional fashion, but couldn't. He went back up and countered with a go-behind into a hammerlock of his own. Bear couldn't counter in the traditional sense either, and instead countered with some quick elbows to the side of the head with his free arm to break the hold. Bear then charged towards the ropes, and returned with a flying forearm, which took the champion down. Bear then attempted the first pin of the night. One. Two. No. Chronos kicked out. Both men sprung up to a vertical base, and Bear charged at Chronos. Chronos took him down with a Japanese armdrag, and then once on their feet again, Chronos took Bear down with a lariat. Chronos then attempted a cover, but Bear kicked out. Bringing Bear up, Chronos sent the Philadelphian into the turnbuckles, before charging in. Bear quickly escaped and hopped onto the ring apron, before charging towards the other corner. Bear executed a beautiful springboard dropkick, which took Chronos down to the mat. Bear then rolled Chronos out onto the floor, and went back out onto the ring apron once again. Bear flew off with a double-axehandle as Chronos reached his feet, but the 'Man Of The Hour' saw it coming, and countered with a punch to the midsection at the last second. With Bear now slumped over, Chronos took control with a bulldog from behind on the outside. Chronos then rolled Bear into the ring and went for the pin. At two, Bear got a foot on the bottom rope. Undaunted, the champion brought the challenger up and irish whipped him to the ropes. Bear returned, and Chronos hooked him with the Hourglass. Bear tried to fight it off, but Chronos locked the hold as tightly as he could. Seconds passed and Bear began to show signs that he was drifting off. The referee raised his arm once, and it fell. He raised it again a few moments later. Fans of both men were torn, and didn't know what to think. The referee raised it a third and final time... if it fell like the two previous times, this match was over. It didn't. Bear held it up and countered the Hourglass into a belly-to-back suplex, and now both men remained out of it on the mat. Moments passed before finally Bear lay a single arm on Chronos for the pin. One. Two. No. Chronos kicked out, and now Bear had to turn it on to see if he could pull out the win. He first brought Chronos up and hit a Death Valley driver, which garnered a two count only. Deciding to take another high risk, Bear went up top and flew off with a senton bomb. He hit it, and went for another cover, but again, Chronos kicked out. Seeing this, Bear decided to go for his coup de grace. He brought Chronos up onto his shoulders, but Chronos escaped and fell behind him, before hitting the Reverse DDT. Lowfall. Both men remained on their backs on the mat, and the referee started up a ten count. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. Chronos began to show signs of life. Seven. Eight. Nine. He draped an arm over the dazed Mike Bear, and the referee positioned himself to count the pin. One. Two. No. Chronos decided to go for a high risk maneuver of his own. The Figure Four DDT was a dangerous hold, and could possibly injure Bear. This match wasn't about injuring Bear, it was about winning the match and retaining his fWo World Title. Chronos went up top, and prepared to fly off with some sort of high risk move. Isntead, Bear staggered to his feet, and crotched Chronos on the top rope. Rather than join him up there, Bear faced the center of the ring, and reached back to retrieve Chronos. He had him in position for what appeared to be a running powerslam, instead, however he turned it into a sitdown tombstone piledriver. The match appeared to be over, as the move essentially took the life right out of the crowd, not to mention Chronos. Exhausted, Bear finally positioned himself for the pinfall attempt. One. Two. No. Chronos got a shoulder up at the last possible split second. Bear was flabbergasted. He knew there was only one thing left to do. He tried the Whiplash, and Chronos countered. Now it was time to go for it all. Lamontsault. Bear flew off, but Chronos moved out of the way, and Bear hit the mat with a sickening thud. Chronos didn't have much left in him to make the cover immediately, and thus, the referee began another ten count. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. Chronos finally came to, and staggered to his feet. Bear did the same. Bear charged at Chronos, but Chronos caught him offguard with a kick to the stomach, and then hooked him for the modified Acid Drop. Also known as the Chronosphere. He ran towards the corner, and hit it. The same move that he defeated Primetime just twelve days ago for the fWo World Championship. Everyone in the arena assumed it was over, but as everyone knows, it isn't over until the fat lady sings. One. Two. No. Bear kicked out. The fat lady had not yet sung. Chronos brought Bear up and whipped the challenger to the ropes, but he reversed it. Chronos returned and spotted Bear going for a backdrop. Although he had decided not to go for the move earlier, he decided to this time. Figure Four DDT. No. Instead, Bear countered with a backdrop out of the move that starts out in a powerbomb type position. Chronos ended up on the ring apron. Bear thought he had backdropped the champion out of the ring, but turned and saw that Chronos caught himself and was now on the apron. Without hesitation, almost on instinct, he quickly went for a superkick. It connected... but what happened next was likely not the intended result of Bear. Chronos went flying off the apron and landed through the American' commentator table, where the sole commentator Jeremiah Frankton tried to move out of the way. Fortunately, he did. Chronos landed with a splat. Bear hung his head in apologetic form, as he didn't mean to have it happen like that. The referee quickly went to the outside and began checking on the champion. The fans remained in awe. Bear stood in the ring breathing heavily, in awe himself. He didn't mean for it to happen, but it did. And he wondered what was going to happen next. He never saw it coming. Literally. Down the ramp came Primetime, who had screwed Chronos on December 3rd, he had screwed him at Twilight, and now he was about to screw someone else. Mike Bear. Primetime entered the ring with a cordless drill, and when Bear turned around, Primetime leveled him. Primetime then brought him up and hit a Primetime Bomb, and then the Snapshot, to ensure that Bear wouldn't get up. Chronos & the referee never saw it happen. Primetime fled the ring, and as Chronos miraculously came to, he crawled into the ring and saw Bear out of it. He assumed Bear must've been too exhausted to capitalize on his opportunity, and fell to the mat. Chronos climbed in, and covered him. One. Two. Three. Chronos had retained the title, but he didn't know that Primetime had helped him do so. Chronos crawled over to the nearest corner and slumped, as his body was not in any shape to celebrate. A half minute passed before Bear got to his feet, and walked over to the ropes, telling Chronos that Primetime helped him. Chronos looked at him in confusion, and tried to talk to Bear, but Bear wouldn't hear any of it. He asked for a microphone, and was given one. Nobody knew what he was going to say, but we were about to find out. Result: Chronos by pinfall The Aftermath Chronos was getting up as Bear began to speak. "I've been in this company for over a year and a half, and I've never refused to do anything the company didn't want me to. They asked me to parody everyone in the company, and I did. They asked my friends to go beyond limits to embarass others and we did, even if it meant embarassing ourselves in the process. They wanted me to work with certain people I didn't want to work with, but I didn't refuse to. I just did my job. You know, as a wrestler, we're given special priveleges, or we can get away with stuff that you people can't get away with in the regular workforce. I've always thought of myself as a representative of you guys, that I was the guy who worked hard, no matter what the circumstances, even if I didn't like them. When other guys in this company were refusing to work with others, I wasn't. When they refused to do their jobs, I wasn't refusing to do mine. I just did it. To see Primetime refuse to wrestle Panther tonight was sickening, and only a incompetent fool like James Armstrong could allow something like that to happen. I was owed a title shot for two months and I never brought it up. I knew it would come to me eventually. I even allowed one of my closest friends a shot before me. Chronos got one before me *twice*. Mitch Wilson got one before me. And I didn't speak about it until now. Tonight, my opportunity came. And we all just saw Primetime screw me out of the title. I've been screwed a lot in my career. Here in the fWo, and in other wrestling companies. And quite frankly, I've never been so bitter. After all I've done for this company, the acting owner acts like it meant nothing. And it essentially meant nothing because Primetime won't be punished for his actions here tonight, if you know anything about Armstrong. And I know a lot about him. I know he's a bald old timer that doesn't care about this company. He wants to cash in. He's wanted to since the night Chris Finn gave him the responsibility of running this company. And he's ran it into the ground. I won't be around to see the new management, but I hope they do a damn better job of running it than he did." The fans and Chronos wondered what Bear meant by his comment, but Bear would soon reveal. "Armstrong, get your ass out here. I have something to say to you." Thirty seconds or so passed, before Armstrong crept out onto the stage. The fans booed. Bear shot a glare in his direction. "Beaven treated me well. Finn treated me well. Hell, even Davis treated me well to a certain degree. You? You've forgotten about everything I've done for this company... and that's something that doesn't sit well with me. And I'm not about to risk the sale of the fWo falling apart. Knowing you, you'll screw that up too. So I have two words for you, Armstrong. I quit." Bear tossed the microphone to the mat, and it made a resounding thud that echoed off the TD Waterhouse Centre walls. The fans looked on in shock as Bear exited the ring. Armstrong walked down the ramp quickly in attempt to convince Bear to change his mind, but Bear shoved him to the ramp. He gave him a cold stare, while Chronos struggled to come to in the ring, still in shock of the nights developments. Armstrong laid on the ramp in embarassment, as Bear exited into the depths of the arena. The referee handed Chronos his fWo World Title, but Chronos motioned as if he didn't want it. Chronos exited the ring and walked towards the stage, but Armstrong got up and tried to keep him from going after Bear. He tried to talk to him, but Chronos grabbed him by his suit, and moved him aside. The cameras took us backstage and Chronos looked around for Bear. He first went for his lockerroom, where Fear, Brittany & Hellrazor stood confused. Chronos asked them where Bear had gone, and they said he left. He wouldn't talk to them. Chronos quickly ran towards the TD Waterhouse Centre parking lot, and saw Bear' vehicle speed away. Chronos didn't know what to think. All he wanted to do was to re-start the match, since Primetime played a part in the outcome, and we all know what Chronos thought about Primetime. But it didn't matter at this point. Bear wasn't around. Bear was gone.
