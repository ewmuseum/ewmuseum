Wednesday Warfare, 7 July 1999

7 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The cameras found the commissioner's office moments before Warfare took the air. Sadly, he was not inside of it, and Geoff Green along with the fWo camera crews paced the halls looking for things worth filming. Coming to a slow stop outside Scott Slugger's dressing room, Geoff could hear shouting. Looking inside, the cameras found the commissioner of the fWo alongside Chris Finn arguing with Scott Slugger. Slugger pointed at his jaw and yelled, "You see what kind of chaos is going on in the fWo these days? Listen here, Beaven... when I came to the fWo over a year ago it was an honorable promotion... someplace that you could be proud to work in. Now look where we are! On Friday I'm going out to kill myself against some guy we know nothing about just for this title... the Hardcore title. What is that anyway? A hardcore title? You've got to be kidding me... but now I have a chance to legitimize the biggest prize in the game, the fWo World Heavyweight title... and dammit... nothing is going to stop me from bringing the people the match they deserve." Travis nodded, and looked down at something he was holding, pausing only a moment to smile at the slight bulge that was developing on his bicep. Travis cleared his throat and said, "So what you're proposing is a system to eliminate outside interference by posting Ice at the entrance ramp to act as an outside referee. Is that correct?" Slugger nodded, and pointed at the clipboard again. "...and no amount of Canadian freaks or lawyers, valets, or moron wrestlers are going to stop us from doing our job. Ice and I will ensure for you a first-rate, no-interference match between two great stars for the greatest title in the game. No bald headed jerks running in to disrupt things... and I know that must sound pretty good to you, right?" Travis nodded slowly, and signed the paper. "Very well Scott. Your authority in the ring is final, and I'm signing Ice as the special outside referee. Should you become too injured during the Hardcore match earlier in the night, Ice will assume your duties as referee, this Friday, and Fight The Future. Now... if there is nothing else?" Slugger shook his head, and shook the commissioner's hand to seal the deal. Finn and Travis exited the locker room on their way to the commissioner's office. Speaking directly to Finn Travis asked, "Speaking of Davis... is he still incarcerated?" Finn nervously shook his head and answered, "Uh... no sir...Mr. Universal posted bail yesterday for him, and well... it cleared, and workers out front just told me five minutes ago Davis is on his way here." Angry, Travis reached into his coat pocket, pulled a small paper from his pocket, and crossed off a single name from it. "Universal is helping Davis? That bastard was on a very, very short list Finn... but no longer. You make certain he explains himself to the fWo and to me before this night is over... now best get out there with Armstrong before the Frenchie becomes the guest announcer again." Finn went on his way, and Travis looked at the list once more and shook his head slowly. As Travis walked he caught a reflection of himself in the glass of the arena halls and shook his head, suddenly looking slightly more confidant... Mike Bear vs. Rudo Rudo came to the ring, by strangely alone and not with the rest of the Lone Gunmen. Stopping him on his way to the ring, Chris Finn asked Rudo where his partners were. Rudo started to answer, but then burst into tears and climbed into the ring. Rudo asked for the microphone, and announced that tonight was his final match. Rudo said, "I've been looking for an identity for a long time in the fWo, and finally found it with Machina and the Lone Gunmen. When Machina left, well... it's just not the same. Billy James has already retired from the fWo, and will be working in Hollywood. He told me that he has had dog days in the past, and that someday he might return to the road once more... as another person perhaps, but for now his fWo time is done. Sid Snow... well, Sid was going to follow his heart and open a bathhouse in San Francisco. The Shocker... well, the Shocker is still in the fWo, if anyone cares. As for me... well, I'm going to try and find myself again. I'd like to thank all of you for cheering me, Melissa Smalls for those wonderful nights many months ago, and I'd like to thank Ultra Violet for giving me some of the best matches of my career. Until we meet again... goodbye, fWo." With that, Mike Bear's music played, and the wrestler came to the ring ready to fight. Rudo shook his hand as he entered, and lay down on the mat, wrestling one final match "Lone Gunmen style". With a small tear, Rudo left the ring to a standing ovation from the crowd. Bear saluted him, and told the referee to rule this match for Rudo... because he deserved to go out not a loser, but for this last time, a winner. Bear then cleared his throat, and addressed the crowd... Result: Rudo Mike Bear with a challenge After the crowd noise had died down, Bear started speaking with a intensity rarely seen from the newcomer. Bear said, Black Panther vs. MOTYC, Ruler of the nth dimension Strange techno music began to play, and weird blue lasers began to shine out from the entry ramp. Smoke poured out of the backstage entrance, and a strange form emerged. A man dressed in an unearthly bodysuit emerged, waving what appeared to be six arms at the crowd. As the man walked to the ring, people in the crowd discovered that the four extra arms on each side of his body were attached by a wire, and the man shook them at small children, attempting to frighten them. Clumsily he climbed into the ring, but one of the arms got caught on the ring ropes, and the referee had to assist him inside the ring. Announced as "MOTYC, Ruler of the nth dimension", fans quickly were able to see that it was Mike Heftel under the weird bodysuit and makeup. The music of the Black Panther played, and the former International and Internet champion walked to the ring looking confused and dismayed that he was about to wrestle the newest gimmick of "Crazy Mike Heftel". The Black Panther slid into the ring and paced the strange man, unsure of how to lock up exactly with the six-armed man. MOTYC made the decision to wrestle easier by attempting to spear the Panther, but got tangled up in the arms and tripped into the arms of the surprised Panther completely helpless. Shrugging, the Panther delivered a face-first DDT to MOTYC and quickly covered as many of the arms as possible. Unable to move and more less break the count, MOTYC was quickly pinned by the Panther, who slid out of the ring still looking dismayed that he had to wrestle in such a match. Meanwhile, MOTYC was unable to get to his feet and thrashed around with his arms, which only succeeded in smacking himself in the face and knocking himself unconscious. Sighing, the ref called for fWo officials to come to the ring and help drag away the heap of Heftel, ruler of the nth dimension. Result: Black Panther by Pinfall Austin Davis arrives "Trust me" begins to play and an ovation of boos starts up before Austin Davis even walks out from behind the TravisTron. The boos grow louder as Davis walks out wearing his "Davis 384 hours" t-shirt and a pair of black levi shorts. Davis already has a mic, he rolls into the ring and wastes little time speaking his mind. Davis says "As you may or may not know, that dumb son of a bitch Chris Universal posted the bail for me again. What he fails to realize is he just made the biggest mistake of his fwo career. Why, Chris, why? Why did you bail Davis out of jail? I think that's the question here on everybody's mind here tonight... so if you got the grapefruits to come on out here and give me an answer, by all means... go for it!" Moments pass by before we hear "Do you buy what the $ellout is $elling?"; and out walks the Internet Champion. Universal says; "You want to know why, Davis? Because I'm a business man. And like any smart business man, he protects himself. He makes sure he has a back-up plan, he makes sure he has an insurance policy, and he makes sure he has a way to cover up his ass. Well, in case you've forgotten, you and I are scheduled to team up on Friday against that masked retard and that psycho-freak Foley and if we lose, I gotta face the masked retard next week. So, before your bald ass asks... er, I mean "baad ass" asks me "what's in it for Austin Davis?", I'll tell you. If you and I win on Friday night, you'll get the Internet Title shot, and he won't... and I'll also throw a little money your way so you can find a nice place in a retirement home... if you BUY what the SELLOUT is SELLING!" Before Universal leaves, Foley appears up on the TravisTron. Foley says; "Hey Chrissy! You've got it all wrong! You see, because I'm a business man as well, and I have a plan. Austin Davis... I'm not sure if I can wait until Friday to extract revenge for you costing me a shot at the Internet Title... so if YOU have the grapefruits to accept a match with me here tonight... well, I'll go home happy knowing that I indeed got revenge on your sorry, balding ass two days early! And if I don't go home happy knowing that I indeed got revenge on your sorry balding ass two days early, well that would mean you went home almost-happy knowing that you beat me for the millionth time! How about it, Davis? You and me, in a no-dq, falls-count anywhere in this great city of Charleston match here tonight on Warfare for all of the world to see?" Davis simply smirks and says; "I've never backed down from a challenge... and tonight, you're going to feel the wrath of Austin Davis. Trust me!" With that, Warfare went to a word from our sponsors... Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Mitch Wilson vs. Primetime Warfare returned to live action as all three members of the French Foundation made their entrance together, rather than separately, showing how tight this group really is. Normally, when friends face each other they make separate entrances... but not in this case. Frenchie led the charge, as he, Wilson & Primetime made their way into the ring. The crowd was really anticipating this match-up, as the group had been touting it for several days as a great match. The referee rung the bell and we were off... until Silver Fox and his cronies made their way out. They ran down to the ring and attacked the French Foundation, throwing Frenchie over the top rope and tying up Primetime in the ropes. They handcuffed Wilson to one portion of the bottom rope. They went on the outside and Brawler & Black Quicksilver put Frenchie through a table. The Total Eclipse then did a big splash on Frenchie, knocking the wind (and more) out of him. Silver Fox then spoke into the cameras, saying "let this be a warning... nobody wrestles in a great match except the Silver Fox!" The referee was then thrown out of the ring, and security hit the ring in attempt to restore order. The cameras suddenly went backstage to a darkened room, where Death and the other Dark Riders stood hovering around a single monitor. Sounds of dogs barking in the background were heard, and Death smiled and spoke softly. "Enjoy the moment while you can Frenchie. This Friday, your title is mine. But tonight... oh, tonight... Silver Fox... Brawler... you shall come to understand what evil really is..." Result: No Contest Backstage life's a blast Backstage, cameras found Jackhammer and Wolf Fang arguing backstage. Unable to pick up their exact words, Geoff Green moved the cameras in closer but before he could get close the shouting grew louder, and Wolf Fang stalked from the room. Shouting over his shoulder, Wolf Fang exclaimed, "I don't want to be a part of this, I'm busy as it is". Inside the locker room, Jackhammer looked glumly on, mumbling to himself, "Well... now what am I going to do?" In another location of the backstage area, Eron the Relentless shouted at some personnel to hurry up and film a promo for him. Disgruntled by Eron's new attitude, the fWo staff didn't hop to his commands, and an impatient Eron grabbed one of them and slammed them into a wall. As Eron's reign of terror continued, the US Marshal emerged from behind him and grabbed one arm. Telling Eron that "enough was enough" the Marshal protected the fWo personnel so they could get to safety. As Eron and the Marshal began to come to blows, security arrived to push them away from each other. The French Foundation was caught on film, still grumbling after the finish of their match. As Nikki ran up to meet Primetime to welcome him to the backstage area, she smoothed out the ruffled back of her outfit. Nikki and Primetime kissed, and were at once shouted at by Joy and Glee, wielding bibles. The Frenchie and Wilson pulled Joy and Glee back, trying to keep an incident from breaking out. As the Frenchie struggled with Joy, a masked man snuck up behind him and used a pair of tweezers to pluck a single hair from his head. The Frenchie shouted, but as he turned to see who had committed the act of thievery the masked man had already gone. With this behind them, the cameras looked for other action, and quickly found it... Austin Davis vs. Mick Foley We returned from the commercial break to find Davis & Foley already fighting backstage. Foley charged at Davis and Davis backdrops him through a table with an assortment of hot coffee and other drinks on it. Some of the coffee scarred the forehead of Foley, but this man has no limits as to the pain he can receive. Davis grabs Foley up by the hair and spots a limosuine. He throws Foley back first into the limosuine, and then grabs a two-by-four off the ground. He runs and swings at Foley, but Foley ducks out of the way and the two-by-four smacks the window into a thousand pieces. Davis turns around and swings at the left leg of Foley, and takes him down on the ground. He then slams Foley onto the hard concrete. He chokes him out with one of the camera man's cords, and then climbs up ontop of the limo. Davis flies off with reckless abandon, attempting to do the famous 'Cactus elbowdrop' that Foley is famous for. Foley moves out of the way and Davis crashes into the concrete hard. fwo security have finally directed a referee over to where the carnage is taking place so we can have a pinfall counted. The official makes his way over, as Foley looks at the limo, then looks over at the camera man, looks down at Davis, and over at the referee... he then yells out "bang bang!" and tells the camera man to get in. He tells the referee to get in as well. He then picks up Davis and throws him in too. Foley instructs the driver of the limosuine, who turns out to be the Black Panther, to "take us on a ride!". Panther obliges and the limo speeds out of the arena. The camera man's footage isn't all too clear at this point, but we can tell Foley and Davis are battling it out in the limo. Minutes go by before the limo stops, and Foley instructs everyone to get out as he continues to beat on a weakened Davis. Foley then throws Davis out by the head onto the ground. The camera man's focus still isn't too good at this point, but it is somewhat clear that they are at a trainyard somewhere in Charleston. The two men fight back and forth throughout the yard, using chains and various other items to beat on each other with. Finally, Foley hits the double-arm ddt on Davis on a section of tracks. Foley climbs up one of the ladders on the trains to the top. He then yells; "Hey Davis, let me show you how it's done! Bang bang!" Foley gets a running start and comes off with the 'cactus elbowdrop', hitting it right into the chest of Davis. He is too dazed to make a full attempt at a cover, but with his arm draped over Davis, the referee counts anyway. Three seconds later, it's over, and Foley will go happy knowing he finally got a win over Austin Davis in his own kind of match... Result: Mick Foley by Pinfall Trouble vs. Black Quicksilver & Chameleon The music of Trouble began to play, and Twitch and Thumper came down the ramp... but not alone. The two tag team partners carried with them a large cardboard spinning wheel, and pitched it into the ring. Smiling broadly, Twitch grabbed a microphone and pointed to the wheel, now standing upright in the ring. Written on the wheel were words like "No Holds Barred", "Slime", "Water", "Hot Wild Sex", "Screw It, Let's Eat Chicken" and "Honest Wrestling Match, No Cheating". Twitch announced to the crowd that they were going to revolutionize the sport of wrestling with the "wheel of fate", and would be spinning it before every match. Twitch then motioned at Thumper, and he spun the wheel, causing the cardboard to shake as it turned. During this the Chameleon and Black Quicksilver walked to the ring, ready for their match. Sadly for them, the wheel landed on "Screw It, Let's Eat Chicken", and Twitch and Thumper looked at each other with a mixture of hunger and depression. Taking the wheel with them, Twitch and Thumper patted the Chameleon and Black Quicksilver on the backs and forfeited the match. Somewhat annoyed by this tomfoolery, Black Quicksilver grabbed the microphone and told the "fools in the crowd" that he wanted a real match... next week, on Warfare... Ground Zero, or Stevie and VL... whoever the tag team champions happened to be. Black Quicksilver then laughed, and ripped at his mask, revealing the Chameleon beneath it. The Chameleon then pulled at his mask, revealing the Black Quicksilver beneath. Still holding the microphone, the Chameleon told Ground Zero and VL and Stevie not to feel confused... just bring the titles for them. As they walked out of the ring, the music of Scott Slugger began to play, and the crowd rose to their feet... Result: Black Quicksilver & Chameleon by Forfeit Chris Universal (c) vs. Scott Slugger Scott Slugger came to the ring, smiling. He wore a referee's shirt, and was accompanied by the massive Ice, who followed him to the ring. Backstage, Chris Universal was shown arguing with some fWo referees about Ice being at ringside, and Scott Slugger's Hardcore Title not being on the line. As Universal argued, the Commissioner walked into the area and fixed Universal with a cold glare. "You have your own people to bail you out Universal... you don't need the fWo. Get to the ring, your match is starting." Universal, angry stormed to the ring, tossing the Internet title in at the ref and attacking Slugger from behind while he was talking to Ice. Universal knocked Slugger down to the mat, and grabbed one of his legs and began whipping it into the mat repeatedly. As the ref attempted to pull Universal backward he batted the ref away and continued to work on the leg of Slugger. The ref warned Universal that he needed to back off, or risk disqualification. Universal shouted at the ref, and turned back to Slugger, who had not gotten to his feet. Slugger waited until Universal drew close and delivered a hard punch to the midsection of Universal. As Universal stumbled backward, Slugger got to his feet and landed chop after chop into the chest of Universal, drawing red marks on the skin of the champion. Slugger grabbed Universal, and flipped him up and over in a full standing suplex. With Universal on the mat, Slugger went for a cover, but Universal quickly reversed it, and kicked at the weakened leg of Slugger. The ref count to three, and Slugger failed to break the count... although he seemed not to fully try either. Smug, Universal grabbed his title and swung himself over the ring, leaving some angry parting words at the referee and Ice. Ice entered the ring and helped Slugger to his feet, not noticing the slow darkening above them. As the two felt drips of water they turned their heads skyward to see dark storm clouds over the ring, signaling an ominous future for Scott Slugger. As Slugger and Ice neared the ropes to leave the ring, they spotted Crucifix at the top of the entrance ramp, pointing silently down at Scott Slugger. As the two stared off, a single bolt of lightning struck from above, arcing on the ringposts and sending a shudder through the audience. As Scott Slugger stared grimly at the signs of Rain and Crucifix, he mumbled quietly to Ice that this Friday he would need full control to keep things fair... and perhaps some luck to keep his own title. As Ice nodded, the cameras went black as Warfare drew to a close... Result: Chris Universal by Pinfall
