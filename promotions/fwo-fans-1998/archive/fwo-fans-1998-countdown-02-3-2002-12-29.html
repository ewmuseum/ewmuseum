The scars covering the tattoos. The nonchalant way he crossed his arms over his chest as the referee gave them their instructions like his opponent was anyone BUT Erik Kelly. Further undeniable proof on both ends that these two men, fundamentally the same, would forever pursue their goal of entertaining the fans and being the absolute best they could possibly be will forever be reached by vastly different means. You had to respect that. The referee finished giving the two men his instructions and called for the bell, but neither wrestler moved. As the fans started to cheer the staredown, the moment of tension was broken by Eli Flair, offering his hand to Kelly. "Might as well shake it before you bleed," said the King of Extreme with a wry smile. Much to his surprise, Kelly took his hand and did just that. "Good luck," replied the Eradicator, "You'll sure as hell need it." Neither man had moved yet, and the match already had an unforgettable moment. At last it broke, and the two men retreated to opposite sides of the ring to drop off their respective championship belts. Just as quickly, their attention returned to each other. They locked up as the match officially began, both looking for the advantage, both looking for the early edge. After several seconds of force, they mutually broke the hold. The first lockup... and neither man budged. Take two? They locked up again, and slowly, painfully, Erik Kelly backed Flair up into the corner. As the referee called for the break, both men slowly let go and fired a right hand almost in sync at each other. Eli Flair's was a split second faster, and it showed. Another right hand. A third. Erik Kelly staggered back toward the center of the ring as he saw the fourth fist fly toward his face. "BLOCK!" Hello. As the Champ blocked the fourth right hand with his wrist, he closed his eyes and headbutted Flair, knocking him back against the ropes. Kelly wasted not a second as he followed through with a clothesline that half pounded, half- shoved Flair over the top rope. He'd have tumbled all the way to the floor if he hadn't held onto the top rope, limiting his descent to the outside of the ring apron. Erik Kelly grabbed him by the hair and pulled him to his feet, his head hooked. Suplex attempt... blocked. Another... blocked. The arena collectively stood to see if Eli Flair could take advantage and get the big man over the top rope that would definitely be a challenger's advantage. However, while Kelly might not have been able to get Eli Flair over the top rope in suplex form, he had a distinct advantage in that he had less of a distance to fall. He leaned back as much as his three hundred and thirty- six pounds would allow him, and quite effectively dragged Flair over the top rope. As tens of thousands of people gasped at this display of strength, Kelly simply used his opponent's awkwardness at being so manhandled and finally lifted Eli Flair up for that suplex... and held it there. He waited... waited... waited... and finally dropped it. A hook of the legs. ONE... TWO... KICKOUT. Flair rolled away from Kelly as he regained his breath. Lighting on one knee, he barely had the chance to stand before he was whipped into the corner. Kelly followed up with a boot to the neck, breaking just as the count reached four. A knee to the midsection. Another. An elbow to the side of the head slumped Eli in the corner. Somewhere, off in the distance of the upper deck, a faint "Let's Go Eli!" chant had begun. Kelly put another boot into Flair's neck, cutting off his air. As the referee began a five- count, Eli moved his hands from trying to separate Kelly's boot from his neck to bracing himself against the corner as he picked both feet up off the mat and kicked out at Kelly's sole vertical leg, catching him on the knee. Kelly began to lose his balance as the pressure came off Flair's neck, and he grabbed the Champ's foot before it got too far away and twisted it around, locking in his ankle. Before he could get the submission hold properly cinched, Kelly turned into it and kicked back, catching Flair in the stomach. He nipped up with an agility that defied his size and delivered another boot into his opponent's stomach, and hooked him. "POOOOOOOWERBOOOOOOOMB!" cried the arena, drowning out the man himself. Any and all doubts about the strength of the World Champion were smacked down as he lifted the three hundred plus pound King of Extreme up in the air and turned with him away from the ropes. And any and all doubts about the awareness of the King of Extreme were smacked down as, once in position, he landed a fist into Kelly's head. And an elbow. And a pair of thumbs to the eyes. Disoriented, Kelly lost his balance and fell backwards, with all three hundred pounds of Flair landing on his chest. Eli reached back and hooked the leg, hoping for the disorientation to last. ONE... TWO... Kelly hooked his other shoulder with his other leg and flipped him. ONE... TWO... Eli rolled through and back, pinning Kelly's legs down. ONE... TWO... Kelly kicked out and back, and Eli was once again slingshotted into the ropes. Off the opposite side, and gave Kelly time to return to his feet. SHOULDER BLOCK... and nobody moved. The "Let's Go Eli!" chant had gotten marginally louder, as had a "Kelly Rules!" chant from somewhere in the middle of the lower level. Eli bounded off the ropes again and attempted another shoulderblock... with the same result. He circled the stationary Champion for a moment, looking for an opening as Kelly's eyes dared him to find said opening. With the cunning of an amateur- style mat wrestler, Eli Flair faked a dive for the legs and brought his hands up, catching Kelly directly under the chin with a reverse axe handle. He followed up with a knee to the gut and a quick hook- of- the- head- DDT. The leg was hooked, ONE... TWO... TH Result: Erik Kelly via pinfall (Retains Championship)
