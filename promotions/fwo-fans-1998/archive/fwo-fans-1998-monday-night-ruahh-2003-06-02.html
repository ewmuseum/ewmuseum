Introduction The fWo explores new venues and new business as the push to grab firmly hold of an international entertainment spot continues on. He took a half second to lock it in, and chimney-braced his way back to his feet. The two men braced against each other, Collins suddenly threw his feet out from underneath and dropped both men to the mat. Eli's waistlock suddenly broke as his chin bounced off Collins' shoulder. He backed up from his opponent and spit a bloody mess out of the ring - he'd apparently bitten his tongue. The fans applauded again at the hold, the improvisation, and the escape. A fourth lockup, and Collins spun behind Flair with a hammerlock. Eli looked over his shoulder and countered with an elbow to the temple. Collins let go of the hold as Eli spun around and dropped the former Champion with a clothesline! The cover, ONE... TWO... KICKOUT! He picked Collins up by the hair... Scoop and a slam! Eli cracked his neck and scooped Collins again... POWERSLAM! The cover... ONE... TWO... KICKOUT! Eli shot up to his knees, his hands rested on his hips. As he scooped Collins up, the former Champion reached for his own hair and pulled, rolling Eli up! ONE... TWO... KICKOUT! Eli sprung backwards toward the ropes as Collins rolled in the opposite direction. They rushed each other and locked up, with Collins shoved into the corner! He ducked a forearm and slinked around Eli, and shoved him chest- first into the turnbuckle, and hooked him from behind! German suplex! ONE... TWO... TH-KICKOUT! Collins stayed on top of Eli, with a double underhook as the King of Extreme climbed to his feet... double underhook suplex dropped Eli flat on his back! He leaned forward... ONE... TWO... Kickout! Eli pushed backwards, and sent Collins into the ropes! He rebounded off as Eli ducked down, and slid underneath his legs on the second reversal! Eli turned around just in time to take a boot to the gut, and was dropped with a DDT! The cover, ONE... TWO... THR--KICKOUT! Eli propped himself up on one elbow as soon as he kicked out - the adrenalin flowed through his veins with gusto. Collins backed up and waited... waited... SUPERKICK! ELI CAUGHT HIS ANKLE! REVERSAL! It happened in fractions of seconds... Scotty Collins backed up and waited for Eli to stand. As he started to turn toward the former World Champion, Collins fired a superkick near his chin that, if it didn't outright end the match, would've brought it damned close. Eli probably didn't notice. What he saw was something flying toward his face, and instinctively reached out to catch it. Upon his secured grip, he knew what he had, and wrenched Collins down to the mat in an anklelock! To make matters worse, Eli knelt down on his other leg, which effectively cut off his most obvious escape. "EEEEEEEEEEELLLLIIIIIIIIII......" "COL-LINS! COL-LINS!" "EEEEEEEEEEELLLLIIIIIIIIII......" "COL-LINS! COL-LINS!" Divided, the fans were... but it was the same people cheering for both men. They wanted to see Collins tap. They wanted to see Collins escape. They wanted to see one man come out the winner. They wanted to see both men come out the winner. Gritting his teeth, Collins planted both hands on the mat and dragged his body forward. Again. And again. He looked around, deflated, as he saw he had moved a scant few inches; with the King of Extreme's weight on his ankle it was a wonder he'd even made it that far. Still, the ropes were just out of reach. Another attempt to pull himself forward... another inch. But Eli could see that he was inching closer, and as long as he knew the ropes were within his ability to grab, Collins would never even think of giving up. He backed the two men to the center of the ring-- --and it was that opening that Collins hoped to get. The second he felt Eli's knee lift the slightest from his ankle, he pulled it away and rolled across, catching the King of Extreme square in the jaw with a vicious kick. Eli fell backwards, the anklelock submission hold broken completely. The two men got to their feet in rapid succession, but as Eli took a step towards him, the long- delayed plan came to fruition. SUPERKICK. Eli didn't go down, though he came damned close. He got a foot behind him just before he would've fallen and staggered backwards, finally rested on the ropes; which looked like the only thing that held him up. The cheers for Collins resumed in full force as the former Champion leaned into the opposite side and ran at him, clothesline armed and ready. Until Eli reached forward and caught him by the neck. He used Collins' own momentum against him to chokeslam/choke toss his opponent clear over the top rope! Collins landed in a heap on the outside as Eli continued to catch his breath. The referee began to count as Eli slid outside the ring as well. Poison Ivy shouted instructions at him, the memory of Thursday's war with Lance Knight still fresh in both their minds. Eli pulled Collins to his knees and delivered a vicious right hand to the face! A hand wrapped around his throat and he sent Collins back into the ring before returning himself. As Collins returned to his feet, Eli slid into the ring underneath the bottom rope. Collins delivered a kick to the side of his head, but as he went to pick him up, Eli drove a shoulder into his gut from his crouched position! A hook around the waist, and a gut- wrench suplex! The cover, ONE... TWO... THREE! KICKOUT! Eli slapped the mat angrily as he pulled Collins back to his feet. Whip into the ropes, and a fist to the midsection doubled Collins over! Eli set him up for a Russian Legsweep, but Collins grabbed him by the hair and reversed it into a face- first slam of his own! He rolled away to catch his breath, but Flair didn't move. Collins hooked the leg, ONE... TWO... THREE! NO! KICKOUT! He kicked out at the last possible second, to the chagrin of Scotty Collins, and the amusement of the entire audience, who remained on their feet, cheering for the efforts of both men! Down to the wire, Collins looked around with exhausted eyes, and signalled that it was time to finish things off. He picked Eli up and hoisted him over his shoulder... but the King of Extreme suddenly came to life! He struggled against his opponent, and managed to drop backwards onto his feet! Collins had studied Eli Flair's moves before, and knew this was exactly where he did not want to be, but before he could do anything about it, Eli dropped backwards. FALLEN ONE. A normal man would've been shopping for a wheelchair. But the King of Extreme simply fell to the mat, unconscious. Kodiak Vic Creed slowly unravelled the length of chain from around his ham hock fist and stared coldly at Ivy, almost daring her to step into the ring. Creed stepped through the ropes and began walking up the aisle. People jeered him, threw drinks and garbage at him. They shouted insults at him and called him names. But for the Alaskan Juggernaut, they could have been throwing money and compliments for all he cared. Because deep down, he didn't care. Not anymore. He'd given up everything to become World Champion. Nothing else mattered to him. Nothing. And the sooner people realized that, the safer they would be.
