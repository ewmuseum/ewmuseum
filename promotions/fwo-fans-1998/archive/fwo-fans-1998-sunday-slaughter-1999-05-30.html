Sunday Slaughter, 30 May 1999

30 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Fans were welcomed to the fWo's presentation of Sunday Slaughter with clips from the Pay Per View on Friday Night, Extreme Measures. A voice over reminded the viewers of the events that occured at Extreme Measures in 1998, and then updated fans with the results from Friday Night. Fans were welcomed to the show by Chris Finn and Matt Loco, the new Sunday Slaughter announcing team. Fans were promised an interview with the new champion, as well as an update on Dina and John on their Honeymoon. Fans were then reminded that Slaughter was a family show, and that *those* clips from the Honeymoon would not be shown. Just as the night was about to begin, the lights dimmed, and a strange silver light shone out over the arena... The Arrival of Silver Fox As the crowd looked on in confusion, silver lights flashed and a lone man dressed in a silver suit made his way slowly onto the entrance ramp. As he walked silver roses fell from the rafters, and he paused from time to time to kiss young female fans on the cheek. He looked at the ring with distain, and snapped his fingers. Instantly, a group of men came down the entrance ramp with silver carpeting, which they proceeded to roll out in the ring. Finally, with the ring covered in the plush silver carpet and the steel steps brushed with a light feather duster, the man stepped inside calmly, ring ropes held far apart to avoid them touching his person. He looked at the ring microphone, and with a look of disgust pulled a new silver one from his jacket pocket. He informed the crowd that they may call him "Mr. Silver Fox", and nothing else. The crowd responded by chanting "fairy boy". Slightly ruffled, he continued by asking the crowd if they were aware of why the great lover was here, in this damp, dank, crappy arena. He told the crowd that a very long time ago he had the privilege of... tasting... a young woman that was someone else's property. He said that the owner had gotten over it, and even had carved a new identity in another land... this land. He said that he knew that the lunatic has fooled several... but has not fooled him. He told the crowd that Plague was just a little boy who is scared of losing his property once more, and the Silver Fox is here to collect. But then Silver Fox paused, looking out at the crowd in confusion. Silver Fox told the crowd that sadly, Plague had nothing... and that he had nothing that could be taken. Silver Fox brightened, mocking the action of suddenly getting an idea. Silver Fox told the crowd that since Plague had nothing to take, he would simply have to take something of his master's. With that, Silver Fox gestured to the entrance ramp, where Stone stood. Stone walked down in a silver gown, and joined Silver Fox inside the ring. The laughter of Silver Fox rang out over the arena as Stone embraced him in a passionate embrace. Falcon vs. Judge Steel With the events of Extreme Measures still fresh on the minds of the members of Doomsday, Stevie Roberts was shown backstage giving Judge Steel some encouragement for his upcoming match. Judge Steel came to the ring with wrists and forearms taped, and carrying a baseball bat. Getting into the ring, he yelled at the ref to declare the match a "hardcore" match, and then asked the crowd if the Falcon was too much of a pansy to wrestle that kind of match. The Falcon responded by coming to the ring with a metal crow bar, and sliding into the ring. The two men paced, and finally swung at each other. The metal crowbar sliced the bat in half, leaving shards of wood scattered inside the ring. The Falcon swung again, and Judge Steel ducked just moments before the steel bar cut the air above his head. Judge Steel, determining that he had enough of the match, slid out of the ring and began to trot up the entrance ramp. The Falcon moved slower, rolling calmly out of the ring and grabbed the bell from the timekeeper. The Falcon watched Judge Steel come to a stop at the top of the entrance ramp, and taunt him and the crowd. Aiming carefully, the Falcon hurled the bell at Judge Steel, who saw it coming a moment too late, and was cracked in the skull by the flying metal. Taking his time, the Falcon strolled up the entrance ramp to where the bloody body of the Falcon lay, and made a relaxed cover on the top of the entrance ramp. The ref, still in the ring, quickly made his way up the ramp to make the pinfall count. Before he could get to the top of the ramp, Stevie Roberts, Samuel Smooth, and the Surgeon rushed the Falcon from the backstage area. The three swung heavy chains at the Falcon, who desperately attempted to get to his feet. Roberts stuffed a bag over the head of the Falcon, as the Surgeon and Samuel Smooth cuffed the overwhelmed Falcon. With the other members of Doomsday holding down the Falcon, Judge Steel was laid on top of him, and the ref made the count. Laughing, members of Doomsday kicked the bound Falcon as they made their way to the backstage area, but cameras close to the Falcon picked up the faint sound of muffled laughter. Result: Judge Steel by Pinfall Backstage Arrivals Cameras turned to the backstage area, where several limos and rental cars signalled more arrivals at the arena. Many fWo stars were quickly wisked away by fWo officals, or production crews, who routed the wrestlers to makeup, costuming, or meetings for upcoming promos, angles or matches. Despite the hustle and bustle of the backstage, wrestlers were treated with respect... with the exception one arrival. Eron the Relentless pulled up to the backstage area already in his wrestling uniform, and exited the cab. Eron, at the show to tape several promos, wandered backstage looking for the fWo officals in charge of routing him to his proper destination, but fWo officals payed him no heed. Eron began to grow frustrated with the lack of attention, and kicked several crates, causing a large ruckus as fWo officals arrived to clean up the mess. As this was going on, a limo pulled sharply up to the curb and Death exited, along with Scott Taylor and War. As other Dark Riders began to exit from the limo, Death grabbed the camera man filming the arrival and shoved him to the ground, cutting the feed and sending the transmission into mute static. Black Panther vs. Primetime The Black Panther made his way to the ring in preperation for the Internet Title match he will have at the next fWo House Show. The Panther warmed up in the ring, stretching and waiting for the French Foundation member. Primetime came to the ring with one of the fWo Tag Team titles, and waved it at the crowd, who responded with boos and jeers. Primetime flipped himself up in the ring, and charged the Panther, catching him with a european uppercut. Primetime then kicked the leg of the Panther, grounding him with a sharp blow to the side of the knee. The Panther worked to get to his feet, but Primetime continued to exact calculated attacks on the left knee of the Panther, snapping the leg with a dropped fist and then applying a modified figure four. The Panther, close to the ring ropes, easily grabbed hold to force Primetime to break the figure four. Black Panther attempted to get to his feet, but found that his leg could not support his weight, and fell to his knees once more. Primetime mocked the Panther, making fun of his inability to stand. Primtime attempted a cover, but the Panther was still fully able to kick free of the pinfall attempt. Primetime went back to mocking the Panther, as the crowd suddenly stirred with excitement. Nikki the Valley Girl came down to the ring skipping, and stood up on the ring apron. The ref at once worked to get Nikki off the ring apron, but Primetime pushed the ref to the side and leered at the loosely clad young woman. Nikki seemed pleased with the attention, and began to slowly pull her top down for the French Foundation member. Absorbed with this, Primetime failed to see the Black Panther come up from behind, pulling Primetime down and over and applying an ankle lock submission hold. Caught by surprise, Primetime had no method of escape and quickly tapped out. The Black Panther had to helped from the ring by officals, while Nikki concerned over her ill-fated effect on the match entered the ring to care for Primetime. Primetime seemed confused, but not completely upset with the attention he was recieving. Result: Black Panther by Submission Honeymoon Report James Armstrong reported from Maui where John Skyler and Dina Sol were relaxing and enjoying their honeymoon. With beautiful weather and warm water making the locale a tropical paradise, James Armstrong interviewed John Skyler, who was happy and relaxed explaining that he had finally put the nightmares behind him. John Skyler explained that Dina's arm was healing, and ultimately he had put the losses in the past behind him. John explained that Dina and he had been talking and had come to the decision that they would not be returning to active competition in the fWo. John mentioned that he had been talking with the commissioner of the fWo about both of them returning as announcers, or reporters, or commentators... something in an offical capacity, or perhaps as trainers in the fWo training camp. John looked into the sky and told James Armstrong that the days of Fallen were forever behind him, and that he would no longer be alone. James then interviewed Dina, who seemed relaxed and happy with their union. Armstrong asked Dina if she harbored any ill feelings toward Stone, and she shook her head no. James then asked if she will miss wrestling in the fWo, and she replyed that her new life with John was all that she needed. As the interview ended, Dina and John thanked James for interviewing them, and Dina gave James a suitcase to take back to fWo headquarters for her, with the instructions to hand it to the Commissioner personally. James seemed confused by this, and after John and Dina left the area attempted to open the suitcase, but discovered it firmly locked. Grunting with displeasure, James and Geoff took the feed back to the states. Black Quicksilver & Al Snow vs. Chris Kidman Jr. & D Lo Brown Prior to the match, footage of Al Snow and Black Quicksilver talking were shown, with Al Snow promising Black Quicksilver a shot at the Crusierweight title if he were to be the one to make the winning pinfall. As the two walked to the ring, it was clear that they were not pleased to be teamed together, and they entered the ring in different locations. When Kidman and D-Lo were announced, only D-Lo came to the ring, looking angry and worried at the absence of his partner. D-Lo entered the ring alone, and signalled the ref to start the match without Kidman, as he could not be found. D-Lo locked up with Al Snow, who was able to trap him in a head lock and tag in Black Quicksilver. The two continued to double-team D-Lo inside the ring, until Al Snow attempted a cover. Before the three count could be made Black Quicksilver entered the ring and tossed Al Snow off the man, making the cover himself. Al Snow broke the count himself, and he and Black Quicksilver began trading blows inside the ring. D-Lo took the advantage to roll Al Snow up, but Black Quicksilver grabbed Stump and used it to knock first D-Lo in the head, then Al Snow. Kicking Al Snow from the ring, Black Quicksilver rolled D-Lo up and got the three count. Black Quicksilver then shouted that he would see Al Snow and his title next week. Result: Black Quicksilver and Al Snow by Pinfall Backstage Chaos Backstage members of Doomsday were seen cheering each other on, pleased with what they accomplished earlier. Suddenly, the lights in their small dressing room went dark, and screams began to fill the darkness. Cameras jumped outside the dressing room, where the Falcon and Chris Kidman stood blocking the exit door. As the Falcon held the door Kidman quickly nailed a board across the surface, blocking any attempt at an escape. Kidman looked less polished than usual, with greasy hair and a sneer. With the board in place, the Falcon reached for a can of gasoline, and poured it over the surface of the door, and then produced a lighter. Yelling "payback is a bitch" to the trapped members of Doomsday, the Falcon lit the door ablaze with the lighter. As the Falcon and Kidman left the area laughing, fWo officals quickly worked to get the fire put out, the coughing screams of Doomsday heard in the background. In another area backstage cameras found Silver Fox and Stone, laughing together as they watched the earlier footage of the Dark Riders arriving at the arena. Silver Fox turned to Stone, telling her that it appears that they had really gotten under their skin. Before the words had fully left his mouth the door to their dressing room exploded in shards of wood as Death, War and Plague entered the room. Stone quickly got to her feet and ran, but was caught by another Stone, who grabbed her by the neck and ripped the black wig from her head, revealing Fyre beneath. The real Stone delivered a hard chop to the face of the imposter, as Death and War held Silver Fox down while Plague delivered punch after punch to his head. His hands a bloody mess, Plague stood and whispered that he was "just getting started hurting you" as the Dark Riders left the room a ruined mess. From the floor, Silver Fox began to laugh, and pulled at his neck... removing a latex mask and revealing the Chameleon beneath. From the shadows of the room, the real Silver Fox stepped out, smiling and holding a bottle of champagne... Chris Universal (c) vs. Matthew Zhuk Matthew Zhuk came to the ring quickly to a large reception from the fans. Zhuk raised his fists in the air as Chris Universal appeared on the entrance ramp, and along with Megan Dawn made his way to the ring. Universal stepped into the ring and grabbed a microphone before the match could begin, and told the crowd that after his disposed of this jobber tonight, he would go on to defeat and unmask the Panther, and then expel Cactus Jack from the fWo forever. Chris Universal continued, but Zhuk, who had heard enough, attacked from behind and shoved him from the ring. Zhuk then climbed to the top turnbuckle and launched himself at Universal in a diving somersault plancha. Zhuk picked Universal up by his hair, and sent him crashing into the steel steps. As Zhuk moved in for more damage, Megan Dawn stepped in his way, blocking further attacks on Universal. Zhuk attempted to move her, but the distraction was enough for Universal, who picked up a chair and swung it at the head of Zhuk, connecting with flesh and skull. Universal then rolled Zhuk into the ring, but as he attempted a cover Zhuk quickly used his momentum to spin him up and over into an inside cradle. Universal kicked out at two and a half, and kicked at the head of Zhuk, which had begun to bleed badly. Universal went to the second rope and dropped the leg, but Zhuk rolled free of the impact spot and quickly lined up Universal for a lariat. Universal got to his feet quickly, but was taken down by a standing kick by Zhuk. Feeling the tide turning in his favor, Zhuk grabbed the head of Universal and executed a quick piledriver. Zhuk attempted a cover, but Megan placed Universal's foot on the ropes. As the ref warned Megan of this act, Universal lashed out with a headbutt and rolled up Zhuk, grabbing a good amount of his tights for the cover. The ref, unable to see this, made the three count and declaired Universal the victor. Universal kicked at Zhuk after the match, punishing him for doing as well as he did. Universal yelled at Zhuk that he just made his ass famous, and to think twice before signing another match with the greatest champion of all time. Universal then left the ring, as Sid Snow made his way out. Sid, still dressed in the bride's maid dress and weilding the bouquet he caught at the wedding of John Skyler and Dina Sol, looked after Matthew Zhuk. Determining that Zhuk was in need of CPR, Sid prepared to administer aid, but Zhuk, coming to his senses saw the face of Sid looking down at him and punched upward. Zhuk then made his way from the ring, looking behind him in a mix of anger and revulsion. Result: Chris Universal by Pinfall Interview with the champion Ultra Violet, along with his valet Violet and pleased looking manager Malcom came out onto the entrance ramp for an interview with Chris Finn. Ultra Violet was wearing the fWo World Title, albit backwards, and was beaming in pride. Ultra Violet explained that he thought he would never get the fWo World Title after losing his first battle royal, but that thanks to the millions and millions of the little UVs out there he finally had something to show for it... a new can of paint. Ultra Violet held up the paint for the crowd, who looked uncertain at this development. Violet whispered in his ear, and understanding passed over the face of the champion. He held up the fWo World Title then, and the crowd roared with approval. As Ultra Violet was getting ready to continue, Machina arrived behind him, flanked by Billy James, the Shocker, and Rudo. Machina told Ultra Violet that he was tired of waiting for an answer, and wanted to know if Ultra Violet was going to grant his request for a title shot. As the two men stared at each other, the cameras went off the air leaving viewers to wonder what would happen next...
