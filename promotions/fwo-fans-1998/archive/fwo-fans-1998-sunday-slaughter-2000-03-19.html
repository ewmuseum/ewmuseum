Black Panther vs. Dead Slaughter kicks off as "Beautiful People" begins to play. The Gund Arena transforms into a visual of a typical rock concerts, with fog and smoke covering the stage, while a red strobe light flickers throughout the arena. Dead makes his way out, holding his arms overhead. The crowd welcomes him with boos and jeers; but Dead pays them no attention, walking to the ring with an evil look painted across his face. Dead enters the ring and raises his arms once again, the crowd responding again negatively. "Killed The Cat" starts playing, replacing the screaming of Marilyn Manson. The Black Panther strolls confidently out onto the stage, his daunting image on the giant SlaughterScreen behind him. He rolls underneath the bottom rope into the ring, but this just gives Dead an early advantage. Dead cowers over him as if he were his prey, stomping him in the head repeatedly before taking off his trench coat and using it to choke the Panther with it. Dead relinquishes the hold as the referee warns him with a five count; had Dead not released his hold of the Panther he would have been disqualified. Dead brings Panther up and takes him down with a kick to the stomach, followed by the Dead Death Drop. A cover follows, but the Panther is able to kick out at two. Dead follows it by going up to the top rope and flying off with an elbow, but the Panther moves out of the way and Dead hits the canvas of the squared circle. The Panther goes on the offensive and whips him into the corner, before charging in with a clothesline. He then brings Dead out with a sidewalk slam, before covering him. Dead gets a shoulder up at two, but the Panther makes sure the momentum doesn't swing in his direction. He brings Dead up and takes him down with a gut wrench suplex, before hooking him for a double underhook powerbomb. Panther follows with another cover, but Dead kicks out at two. The Black Panther brings Dead up to his feet by his bald head, before punishing him once again with a hanging vertical suplex. Another cover, but Dead is able to get his feet on the bottom rope. Panther is furious, feeling it was a slow count, and this only enables Dead to get the advantage with a lowblow, followed by a DDT. He goes for a cover, but as the three count is to be completed, Dead pulls Panther up by the mask, and goes to hook him for the Crucifixion. However, The Boston Strangler makes his way near ringside through the crowd, and with some help from the weight of the Panther, Strangler pulls Dead by one leg, enabling the Panther to fall onto Dead for the impending pin. Three seconds later, and Panther escapes with a victory. He slides out onto the floor and looks at the Strangler with a look that essentially said he didn't need his help, but the Strangler doesn't care. He and Dead exchange stares, with Strangler looking at Dead with a hint of sarcasm, while the bald headed man returns the favor with a glare of his own. The Panther leaves the ringside area with a victory, while the staredown continues... a dozen of officials make their way down to separate the two, preventing the situation from escalating any further as Slaughter rolls onto a commercial break. The Trucker looked at Machina. "Hey... you're right! This is a lot easier than warning them ahead of time..." Hellrazor vs. Flying Frenchie Slaughter comes back from commercial with the music of the fWo Hardcore Champion, Hellrazor. As is the norm during his title reigns, he holds the title in one hand and Lamont in the other. He raises Lamont into the air for the crowd's delight. As he gets in the ring, he opens up the door to the pet carrier that security provides for Lamont. The hardcore feline climbs in, and promptly goes to sleep. As Hellrazor gets ready, Juke Joint Jezebel hits the speakers, and The Flying Frenchie makes his way down to ringside, rubbing his head from the earlier clobbering. The reaction is somewhat mixed, leaning toward boos, however. The Frenchie gets in the ring and has a microphone with him. "Zut, what is zis? Stupid bookers. Nobody wants to see this silly Undergound. Nobody wants to see zis silly match. Everyone knows that hardcore "wrestlers" can't wrestle. Zat is what WCW is for. If ze ratings go down, it's not my fault." As the Frenchie tosses the microphone out of the ring, Hellrazor grabs the Frenchie and puts him in a headlock. He turns it into a suplex, but Frenchie gets right back up. Sneering, the Frenchie locks up with Hellrazor, and gets the upper hand with an armbar. Hellrazor reverses it. Frenchie counters by doing a flip and catching the hardcore champ with a dropkick. Frenchie quickly grabs hold of Hellrazor's neck and begins to apply pressure for a sleeper. Hellrazor, unamused at this attempt to put him out of commission, begins to stand up. He nails Frenchie in the gut twice to get him to release the hold, and then nails him with a DDT. Hellrazor, forgetting for a second what kind of match this is, goes to get a chair, before the ref reminds him that it cannot be used. Hellrazor climbs back into the ring and quickly clotheslines the Frenchie, who had gotten to his feet but was still woozy. Hellrazor continues his attack, stomping away at the Frenchie's right knee. He grabs his right leg and drops an elbow onto the knee. Hellrazor picks up both of the Frenchie's legs, and spreads them apart, before giving him a wishbone. This causes the Frenchie to yell out in pain. Hellrazor goes for a quick pin, but he only gets one and a half. Hellrazor picks up the Frenchie and tosses him over the top rope to the outside. Hellrazor slides under the bottom rop and picks up the Frenchie again. He wraps Frenchie's legs around the turnbuckle pole, and climbs back into the ring, where he proceeds to put him in a figure-four leglock. The Frenchie yells out in agony, but will not submit. The Frenchie, as if by luck, is close to a crazy superfan of the Foundation, who gives him a cold soda. The Frenchie flips off the lid and throws the soda into the face of Hellrazor. The cold ice and soda causes Hellrazor to release the hold. The Frenchie slumps to the ground, almost unable to get up. He somehow climbs to his feet and slides slowly back into the ring, where Hellrazor is waiting. Hellrazor grabs the right leg of the Frenchie and begins to pound away at it once more. As he is doing this, a commotion at the top of the entrance ramp is going on. Down the ramp come Primetime and Courage. Primetime has Ultra-the-(narcoleptic)-Wonder-Dog in his arms, and a big grin on his face. The dog is asleep, unaware of what is going on. Ultra Violet and Violet are behind the two Foundation members, as UV is trying to give them a leash and telling them not to take him into Stone's locker room, because he had a few "accidents" in there already. Violet saw who was wrestling and quickly took UV's attention away from Ultra. Primetime and Courage ignore the purple-haired star, and go up to Lamont's pet carrier. Hellrazor sees what is going on and begins to yell at Primetime to get away from the cage. Primetime ignores Hellrazor and begins to try to wake up UV's sleepy K-9. Ultra opens an eye and sees Lamont. He barks at the cat, as if he's trying to scare it. Lamont, irritated as he has been woken up, takes a swipe at Ultra's nose. Ultra jumps back and begins to growl at Lamont. The scene outside the ring has irritated Hellrazor, as he leans over the rope and continues to yell at the Foundation. The Frenchie, meanwhile, has staggered back to his feet. He sees the situation at hand, and sees an opportunity. He seizes it, surprising Hellrazor with a crucifix. He grabs hold of his tights and scores the victory. Hellrazor, angered by the outcome of the match, begins to swing at the Frenchie. Primetime puts Ultra on the ground and hops up onto the apron with Courage. Before they can climb in, Mike Bear and Wretch come down to ringside, although Wretch seems like he's not in any hurry to get there. Frenchie hops out of the ring, and the Foundation members begin to taunt the Underground as they celebrate their victory. Frenchie slaps the back of Ultra Violet and winks at Violet on their way back up the ramp. Bear starts to yell at UV, but cannot get his attention as he and Violet head up the ramp behind the Foundation. Hellrazor checks on Lamont, who fell back asleep, as Slaughter heads to a commercial break. Result: Flying Frenchie by Pinfall Frustration ... Before "Beautiful People" can even begin to play, Dead charges down to ringside. He rolls into the ring, but before he can get his hands on Cool, Cool flees from the ring. Dead continues to charge after him around ringside, but the smaller & more agile Kid Cool remains free from his grasp. Kid Cool sees this, and quickly grabs him and rolls him into the ring, before hitting an elbow drop. Kid Cool covers Dead, and Dead kicks out a moment too late after what appeared to be a very quick count. Kid Cool jumps to his feet and celebrates, jumping up and down wildly. He then goes to the outside and grabs a chair, before bringing it in and smacking Kid Cool in the head repeatedly. Wretch rolls to the outside, and he and Bear get into an argument. Meanwhile in the ring, Chronos goes to congratulate Fallen, but Fallen blows him off, and walks away on his own.... There was a heavy white man standing before it trying to gorge himself on nearly everything on the table. Machina frowned. "Hey you... get your fat ass away from there! Who are you anyway?" The man looked up, a handful of noodles dangling from one pudgy jowl. "Ums Ksdirk Kadweasde." The Mad Trucker pointed to him. "With your mouth empty, tubby!" The fat man swallowed. "I'm Kirk Kadden." Machina frowned. "Well get out of here... and even though I have no good reason to do so and despite the fact that I don't know or like you I think you should come to my bachelor party which will be in almost one week on a boat called the Ritanic." The large man waddled away as Machina turned back to the Mad Trucker. "Hey, that was really great how we fought off those people together... we should do that again... maybe go for the tag team titles!" The Mad Trucker nodded. "I agree... and to confirm our new relationship have one of these sausage patties." Machina gasped. "Patties? What are you... mad? Sausage links are clearly the only way to eat sausage! You don't mean to tell me that you actually eat your meat in patty form?" The Mad Trucker frowned. "Sausage links are an abomination to the very soul brought about by commies and the Hollywood industry." Machina screamed in rage and grabbed the catering table and overturned it. "I will NOT hear these foolish words!" Security officials hit the scene to break up the two men, as Sunday Slaughter went to the final match of the night... Mitch Wilson vs. Scott Collins vs. Perfect "Burning" starts up, and the fans react favorably as the muscular form of Scott Collins makes his entrance from the back, looking to be all business. Collins rolls into the ring and mounts the turnbuckles, raising both fists high, before hopping down and rolling from the ring. Collins retrieves a chair, and rolls back in, awaiting his opponents. The arena lights turn to crimson and lasers flash as "Humans Being" hits, and Perfect appears on the entrance ramp. Instead of heading to the ring, Perfect waits on the entrance ramp. The music changes to "Calling All Stations", and the arena lighting treatment remains as Mr. Main Event Mitch Wilson appears on the entrance ramp, Internet Title around his waist. The two FTfWoers stand united on the entrance ramp for a moment, soaking in the loud boos, then both stride purposefully to the ring, intent on hurting Collins. Perfect leaps onto the apron, but Collins swats at him with the chair, causing him to jump back down. Mitch quickly rolls in from the side as the ref attempts to detain Collins from using the chair. Collins gains the advantage and pulls away the chair, and turns to face Wilson, who leaps up and dropkicks the chair into the face of Collins. The chair goes flying, and Wilson quickly covers Collins as the ref counts. Collins kicks out at two as the announcers inform the crowd that this match will be contested under Triple Threat Rules - two men in the ring at once, first pinfall wins the match. Wilson brings Collins up again, and chops the living hell out of him with a backhand that produces a smack heard throughout the arena. Wilson repeats this, and sends Collins across the ring. As Collins comes off, Wilson sticks out a knee into the gut of Collins, who flips in the air. Wilson leaps high into the air, and comes down with a kneedrop to the leg of Collins. Wilson, shooter instincts kicking in, zeroes in on the leg and decides that it will be the target for the match. He brings Collins up, and kicks repeatedly, viciously at his leg. Wilson shoves Collins violently into the corner, and charges in with a dropkick to the knee. Wilson immediately retakes a vertical base, and wraps Collins' knee around the ropes, before pulling it in towards the ring. The referee gives him a five-count to break, and Wilson breaks at four. Wilson frees the leg of Collins, and slaps him in the face before turning him around to face the crowd. Wilson quickly tags in Perfect, who leaps down from the apron to the outside. Wilson takes a few steps back, gets a running start, and charges toward Collins before chop blocking him in the back of the knee. Collins collapses down, and Wilson slides to the outside due to his momentum. On the outside, Perfect pulls Collins towards him, crotching him on the post, and wraps him up in a ringpost figure-four. Wilson quickly rolls back into the ring and gets into the face of Collins, laying down the badmouth on the two-time world champ. The referee meanwhile administers two five-counts - one to Wilson to exit the ring and one to Perfect to break the hold. Both do so at four, and on the outside Perfect grabs the leg of Collins and slams it hard into the ringpost. He repeats this, and does it again before rolling into the ring. Perfect drags Collins out to the middle of the ring, and positions him the way he wants him. Perfect quickly leaps to the second rope from the inside, and climbs up to the top. He then leaps off at Collins, hitting a frog splash on the targeted right knee. Instead of covering, Perfect brings Collins up again, and grabs his leg before taking him over with a dragon screw. Collins clutches at his leg, and Perfect begins stomping on it, repeatedly, not stopping. Collins is in extreme pain, and Perfect turns him over and places one of his legs below Collins' right knee and one above. Perfect stands up, leaps, and crashes down, trapping and snapping Collins' knee in between his. Perfect gets up and hobbles around the ring, clutching at his right leg, mocking the pain of Collins. He laughs uproariously at himself, and cockily smirks down at Collins. He then returns his attention to the knee, and twists it, setting him up for an STF... but Collins grabs his head and rolls through into a small package. The ref counts one, two, but Perfect kicks out at the last moment. Perfect is stunned, and Collins has time to use the ropes to pull himself up. He hobbles toward Perfect, who is just getting to his feet, and prepares a clothesline, which Perfect counters by sweeping the leg of Collins out from under him and quickly dropping an elbow on it. Taking it a bit more seriously, Perfect quickly heads again to the legs of Collins, and this time slaps on the STF, locking it in tightly. Collins howls in pain, and uses all his strength to keep from submitting. Perfect simply cinches in the hold more tightly, and Collins is trapped with nowhere to go. He appears to be on the verge of tapping out... when, out of the line of sight of the ref, Mitch hops down from the apron and pulls Collins towards the ropes, placing his arm over the bottom rope. The ref quickly notices this, and cautions Perfect to break the hold. He does so, and immediately gets into the face of Wilson on the apron, wondering what the hell he did that for? Wilson replies that it was only business, and Perfect is less than pleased. They continue arguing, and the referee steps in to break it up, never noticing Collins crawl up from behind Perfect and nail him with a low blow. Perfect falls backward, in immense pain, and Collins uses the last of his energy to reach up and slap Wilson. The referee rules this a tag, and Wilson is forced to get into the ring. He does so, and instead of going for Perfect, attacks Collins with more stomps to the knee. As he does so, Perfect pulls himself up, and walks up behind Wilson, before spinning him around and getting into his face. Wilson shoves Perfect, and Perfect shoves back. They appear to be about to come to blows, when suddenly both turn and attack Scott Collins, who again had pulled himself to his feet, with a double clothesline that sends him up and over the top rope. Perfect and Mitch turn to one another again, their anger placated for now, and stare each other down... and shake hands. They take a few steps back, then lock up, and Mitch grabs the arm and twists through into a hammerlock, before Perfect reverses into one of his own. Mitch reaches up, looking for purchase, and grabs the head of Perfect, taking him over with a version of a snapmare. Perfect holds on, and Mitch goes over with him, but stops halfway over, bridging up and pinning Perfect shoulder to shoulder. The ref counts to two before Perfect breaks the hold. Both men are quickly up, and Mitch immediately takes Perfect over with an armdrag. Perfect is up immediately, and armdrags Mitch over. Mitch gets up, and leaps over a legsweep attempt from Perfect, grabbing Perfect's head on the way down and charging forward for a bulldog. Perfect reverses the momentum and attempts a back suplex on Mitch, but Mitch flips over. Perfect turns, and both attempt a dropkick. They both quickly take a knee to face each other, and the crowd, despite not liking either man, applauds this show of wrestling prowess. As this happens, Scott Collins comes out of nowhere with a left-leg superkick to the back to the jaw of Wilson, and he strikes Perfect as he gets up with a stiff lariat that sends Perfect for a 360. Perfect slowly gets up, and Collins grabs his head for the Death Penalty. Perfect shoves him off into the corner, and as Collins stumbles out, facing the crowd, Perfect rolls onto his stomach and takes down the right leg of Collins with both of his own legs, attempting to roll through into a leg lace, but Collins rolls through that onto his feet, and grabs the legs of Perfect, before dragging him around to face the turnbuckles. Collins falls back and Perfect goes flying into the air with the slingshot, and smashes his forehead into the steel ringpost beyond the turnbuckles. Unfortunately for Collins, he also sandwiches into the referee, who slumps down. Collins gets up, still wary about his knee, and grabs the head of Perfect, before taking him straight down with a Death Penalty. He realizes there is no ref to count, and goes after Mitch. He brings him up, and doesn't spot Primetime coming in through the crowd, wielding a chair. Collins begins hammering at Wilson, and Primetime rolls into the ring. Not wasting any time, he immediately swings at Wilson... who drops down from a Collins punch. Primetime's chairshot bashes Collins directly in the head, and Wilson reaches up to low blow Primetime. Primetime holds onto the chair, and Mitch quickly takes his feet and hooks the head of Primetime, before driving him down with a Headliner onto his own chair. With Collins down, Mitch works to revive the ref, who slowly comes to. Wilson quickly covers Collins before Perfect can recover, and the ref tolls the three-count. Wilson quickly is handed his title by the timekeeper, and helps his FTfWo partner Perfect to his feet. Despite his wanting the title, Perfect raises the hand of Wilson, and points to him. FTfWo stands victorious in the ring as Slaughter goes off the air... Result: Mitch Wilson by Pinfall
