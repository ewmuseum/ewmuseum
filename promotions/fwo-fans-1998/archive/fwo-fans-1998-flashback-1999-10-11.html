FlashBack, 11 October 1999

11 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The show opened on a shot of the fWo studio production room. Television monitors lined the walls and Chris Finn chatted with Geoff Green about a promotional piece in development for Mob Rules. Noticing that the cameras were on and rolling, Finn smiled and addressed the fans. "Hello and welcome to fWo FlashBack. It's been a busy week in the fWo, with tempers hot and a lot on the line. Let's put it into perspective. Right now the fWo is right in the middle of a Tag Team Tournament to determine the new World Tag Team champions. The titles have been vacant for a few weeks now, and this situation has brought teams literally out of the woodwork. With the first round finishing up on Warfare, there are already a few favorites in the fWo offices. Obviously the former champions Doomsday are in the running, along with the team that caused this situation to begin with, the Hollywood Blondes. Now folks, more on this later in the hour but we've also got a very special interview that new fWo reporter Ken Armstrong, who you all know better as the Brawler, had with Scott Slugger. This interview was recorded earlier in the day, and you won't believe the things that the fWo World Heavyweight Champion had to say. Folks, it's a big night on FlashBack and right now we want to take you back in time over one year ago to a match on Wednesday Warfare. It was June 17th, 1998, and this is our Classic fWo Match of the Week... Machina & Black Quicksilver vs. Flying Frenchie & Rage It was a tough time in the fWo, with Scott Slugger holding the fWo Cruiserweight title. He had just won the title from Ultra Violet a few days before, and cruiserweights were lining up to get their shot at the belt. The Flying Frenchie who would win the title nearly a month later had chosen the unlikely ally of the Empire, Rage as his partner against two other up and coming Cruiserweights. Black Quicksilver and Machina, both masked wrestlers joined forces for this match only against the Frenchie and Rage. Black Quicksilver and Machina didn't like each other, and it was obvious from the poor teamwork that existed right from the start of the match. The two had separate ring entrances, allowing Machina to suffer the double team attack before Black Quicksilver arrived at the ring. With a weary Machina starting the match against the wily Frenchie, the French superstar had little trouble dominating the match. The Frenchie delivered a devastating cross-body splash that sent Machina to the outside of the ring, where Rage drove his head into the steel barrier. Black Quicksilver protested this, and caused the referee to be distracted as the Frenchie and Rage battered Machina on the outside. The Frenchie rolled back into the ring as Machina recovered, using the guard rail for support. Machina managed to toss a chair into the ring, which the referee quickly kicked to the ring apron. The Frenchie, tired of Machina's stalling hit a flying plancha to the outside. Rolling him back in, the Frenchie got a two count and argued with the referee. During this Rage picked up the chair and used it to level Machina, but the referee caught it and quickly called for the disqualification. Their damage done, the Frenchie and Rage left the area as Black Quicksilver climbed into the ring to check on Machina... or so we thought. Once Machina was helped from the ring, Black Quicksilver hit a quick kick to the midsection and delivered a hard piledriver on the cold concrete. Shouting to the cameras, Black Quicksilver demanded his title shot. Result: Machina & Black Quicksilver by DQ Black Quicksilver comments on the match The scene returned to the fWo studios, where Chris Finn was talking with Black Quicksilver about the Classic fWo match that had just aired. Chris Finn asked, "So what do you think came as a result of that match Mr. Quicksilver?" Black Quicksilver scratched his head through his mask and laughed a little. "First off... my name isn't really Black Quicksilver, so I'm not 'Mr. Quicksilver', okay? You know... that match certainly wasn't one of my bigger matches, but it did show you the kind of attitude I had back then. Face it, in wrestling the bigger men traditionally do better, so I was a bit bitter about that... and I wanted my strike at the gold..." Chris Finn broke in with, "You would win that title a few months later, after winning a tournament for the belt when it was declared vacant... isn't that right?" Black Quicksilver smiled. "Yeah... Plague... that goofy bastard... had this idea that he was going to give the belt to David Spencer. Except Spencer was over the weight limit, so he couldn't keep it. I beat Fallen in the final round of the tournament, which was a big deal because nobody really knew what to expect of Fallen back then... nobody really knows what to expect of him now I guess. I've held the title four times, and yeah... I'd like to make it a fifth time. Depends on if Primetime is man enough to give me a title shot. I think that the Cruiserweight title is one of the most honorable ones in the promotion, and it's really being wasted right now." Chris Finn thanked Black Quicksilver for his time, and took the show to a commercial break. Joy needs your help Bright yellow filled the screen as the message "PAID FOR BY THE CITIZENS WHO WANT EVERYONE TO BE HAPPY 'N STUFF" appeared. This faded out to a shot of Joy, sitting by himself on a stool in a cold and empty room. Joy looked pleadingly at the camera and said, "Fair citizens of this country... we find ourselves at the very edge of a dark and gloomy ravine filled with icky bad feelings and anger. Too many people today are walking around unhappy... afraid to let their true giggling nature bubble free. Why, just take a look at this man..." Joy held up a photo of former WCW President Eric Bischoff. Joy shook his head sadly, "We shouldn't have people that look like this in our world. That's why I'm asking all of you to join my on my 'Road To Smiles 2000'. You can help spread the message of good cheer when you write letters, do homework assignments, file reports and tax returns, or even post messages on the internet. At the end of your paper, or post... just type the three little letters "JOY". Through this grass roots campaign we can put at end to sadness in our country. So help me, or soon we will all look like this." Joy held up the photo of Eric Bischoff once more, with a tear coming to his eye. Scott Slugger Interview (part one) We come back from commercial to see a large brick wall, and seated on a bench in front of it in street clothes, former fWo wrestler Ken Armstrong, aka Brawler. Large block letters on the brick wall inform us that this is "Ken Armstrong's Dojo". Armstrong faces an unknown person off camera, and now turns to the camera. Armstrong: Welcome back to fWo FlashBack, everybody, I'm Ken Armstrong, and some of you might recognize me as the Brawler. Well, since my arm was broken, I'm not gonna be back in the ring anytime soon, and instead, I'll be hosting my own interview segment on fWo FlashBack- "Ken Armstrong's Dojo". This week, we have an exclusive interview with none other than fWo World Champion Scott Slugger. The camera now pans back to reveal Slugger, sitting across from Armstrong, in a leather jacket and sweat pants with the fWo World Title over his shoulder. Armstrong: Welcome to the show, Scott. Slugger: Now, before we get started, I have one question- you're not related to James Armstrong, are you? Armstrong: No. Slugger: Good. If you were, I would be running very fast in the opposite direction right now. Finn: Of..course. Well, Scott, you've been in the fWo for a long time, and have held a lot of titles, and you've always had no shortage of opponents. But now, with that gold around your shoulder, you've been a wanted man in the fWo as of late- even more so than ever before. Slugger: You're an observant one, aren't you? Armstrong: Well- Slugger: Shut up. Armstrong seems nonplused by this, but lets Slugger continue. Slugger: First off, let me address the latest so-called opponent to challenge the WGA. That being McKinney, or whatever that little masked dude's name is. Machine-head, you're obviously another of the countless little people in the fWo looking to get a rub from the WGA. And knowing his...preferences, I'm sure your "little buddy" Sid wants one too. Well first off, the WGA doesn't swing that way, pun intended, and Sid can go back to San Fran for all I care. Second, Ghost In The Machine-a, you want a title shot? You got it. I've got nothing better to do Sunday, so I figure a 15-second sparring session couldn't hurt. I'll see you in the ring. Now, K to the A, let me tell you what this big ol' piece of gold I have right here means. It means that I am the best damn wrestler this industry has to offer, let alone the fWo. And when everybody knows that you're better than they are, obviously they're gonna go after you. Whether it's Machina or anybody else, that doesn't mean that they stand any more than a small chance in hell, but that's beside the point. I could be temporarily exhausted from the non-stop party life that is the WGA's and they could get a one-in-a-million upset. A fluke win could really elevate their career, you know, which they'd never be able to do on their own so-called merits. (cough)LanceStar(cough). Armstrong: Speaking of Lance Star- Slugger: Who was speaking of that no-name? Armstrong: Well, you were, for the record. Slugger: Whatever you say, Kenny, whatever you say. Armstrong: But speaking of Lance Star, you and Ice have really seemed to have some issues with the Hollywood Blondes. You're currently scheduled to face them in Round 2 of the World Tag Team Title tournament very soon. Any thoughts? Slugger: Of course I have thoughts, you idiot. If I didn't, I wouldn't be here, and I'd let this new show sink to the bottom of the ratings pile instead of gracing it with my ratings-assuring presence. *But*- back to the matter at hand. Lance Star, you score one BS pin and you start spouting your mouth off about how you're the uncrowned World Champion and all this crap- give me a freakin' break. You see, you and Universal are the Hollywood Blondes- and what comes out of Hollywood? Movies and TV. Fake programming designed to take the place of reality. That, my friends, is exactly what you are- you're both nothing, nobodies, never-will-bes, *fakers*. And you can keep shooting your mouth off, but it won't take the place of reality- which is Scott Slugger beating your ass 1-2-3 whenever he damn well feels like it. Armstrong: More with Scott Slugger after this... fWowrestling.com The scene was of a simple bright gray room, painted brightly and filmed so no visible corners could be seen. A man stood in the center of it, holding a newspaper next to a bus stop sign. A blind man entered the picture, tapping his way along with a cane and heading toward the man reading the newspaper. His cane bumped the leg of the man reading the paper, and the man looked up expectantly and with an expression of slight annoyance. The blind man adjusted, and passed in front of the man reading the paper. The man with the paper stopped reading, and put his arms on the blind man's shoulders. He turned the blind man to face the camera, and patted him on the back to set him on his way. The blind man waved to thank the man with the paper, but the man with the paper kicked at his back, sending him flying into incoming traffic. A loud crash and bump was heard as the man with the paper chuckled to himself and went back to reading. A voiceover announced "fWowrestling.com... we go that one step farther than the competition!" The commercial then came to an end. Scott Slugger Interview (part two) Armstrong: Welcome back to fWo FlashBack, I'm Ken Armstrong talking with the fWo World Heavyweight Champion Scott Slugger. Another person that's recently been, as you would say, "shooting his mouth off" has been the Flying Frenchie, who claims that you're ducking him. Any comments? Slugger: Of course I have co- ah, forget it Ken, you're hopeless. Coincidentally, that also describes good ol' TFF. No hope of getting a shot at me, let alone beating me. You see, I fail to see the logic that says he deserves a title shot. He broke my ankle once, 9 months ago. Big fat hairy deal. You haven't done a damn thing lately that says you deserve a shot at me, and until you do, you can kiss my- wait a minute. Wait just a minute here! I think I just had an idea. Mob Rules is coming up, right? How about this- if you, the Foundation, and the Strangler can beat me and Ice, I'll give you your shot. Otherwise, no deal. Armstrong: But doesn't it have to be 4-on-4? And why would you put a title shot on the line in a match where you're at a numbers disadvantage? Slugger: First off, if the WGA says it's 4-on-2, it's 4-on-2, period. What the WGA says goes, and that's a fact, Kenny. Second, I seriously doubt we're at a disadvantage here. Look at our competition for pete's sake! We've got some moron from Bahh-ston- which speaks for itself. We've got one guy who somehow won Meltdown, then proved he can't handle the spotlight by doing absolutely nothing afterwards. We've got another guy who claims to be the best *wrestler* in the biz, but who hasn't even stepped in the ring in a good 4 or more months, and we've got the Flying Frenchie- which also speaks for itself. Put that against myself and Ice, and I think you'll realize who's really at a disadvantage here. And besides, Big K, who cares if we lose? We're not going to, but if by some small chance we did, I already owe a title shot to one worthless French Foundation member. One more is no big deal. Of course, that's assuming they win at all, which quite frankly won't happen. Armstrong: If you say so... Slugger: What was that? Armstrong: Nothing. Well, we're starting to run out of time here, so I think we've got time for just a couple more questions. Slugger: Shoot. Finn: You're obviously a big baseball fan- Slugger: Like I said, you're very observant. Finn: - and I want to know, who do you see in the World Series? Slugger laughs for a second, then answers. Slugger: Always have those hard-hitting questions ready, huh K-Man? Well, I'm gonna totally disregard stats and team quality, and I'm gonna go on a hunch here- I say we see the Mets and the Red Sox, and the Sox finally break the Curse. If somehow Cleveland beats the Sox, then I say we see a Subway Series, and the Mets pull it off just because I don't like the Yanks. But my first and foremost prediction is the Red Sox over the Mets. Finn: Well, Scott, we're running out of time here, so any closing thoughts? Slugger: Just one. Anybody- and I mean *anybody*- who steps into the ring with me, there's just one secret to a match with Scott Slugger. And that is to be ready- because you're guaranteed to be handed a 1-way ticket on the Curveball Express. Got it? With this, Scott Slugger smirks at the camera as we hear Armstrong. Armstrong: There you have it folks, Scott Slugger, an arrogant man, but he's got some good reasons to be. This is Ken Armstrong, and this has been the first installment of "Ken Armstrong's Dojo". Stay tuned, folks, for more fWo FlashBack. Matt Loco's Rankings The scene turned to Matt Loco, who sat by a chart labeled "fWo FlashBack Ranking system". Speaking quickly, Matt said, "Hello fWo fans! I'm Matt Loco, your close, personal friend here with the fWo Rankings of the week. These rankings are done simple style and chart the biggest movers and shakers of the fWo, based on the winners and losers for each week. What I've done here for the first week is set up the top twenty wrestlers based on my own opinions of the promotion, and next week we will see who moved up and down the ladder. For the week of October 11th, the rankings look like this." Matt Loco held up the chart. 1. Scott Slugger (c) 2. Machina 3. Black Panther 4. Primetime 5. Mad Trucker (c) 6. Death 7. Chris Universal 8. Flying Frenchie 9. Mike Bear 10. Eron (c) 11. Boston Strangler 12. Jackhammer 13. Cactus Jon 14. Courage 15. Crucifix (c) 16. Ultra Violet 17. Ice 18. Lance Star 19. Rain 20. Black Quicksilver "Obviously, this list is only loosely based on current conditions, and will change radically next week when we have hard data available. See you then!" Matt Loco beamed as his segment came to an end. Closing Comments The scene returned to fWo studios, where Chris Finn sat next to a large screen monitor. "Folks, I hope you've enjoyed FlashBack tonight... don't forget to come back next week when we bring you an interview with the man who won Meltdown, and the man that many say will be the next fWo World Champion, Primetime. We will also bring you an inside scoop on the fWo invaders. Who are these people? Where did they come from? What is with the grudge they bring to the table? More of Matt Loco's rankings for week two, Classic fWo, and much more! For the fWo, and all of us in the fWo studios... I'm Chris Finn saying goodnight!" With that, FlashBack came to a close.
