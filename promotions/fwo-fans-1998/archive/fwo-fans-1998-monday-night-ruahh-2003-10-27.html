Just For the Fans As Ruahh came back from a commercial, it found the tag team champions in the ring, and the World Peace Organization didn't appear to be as happy as one would think two-time tag champions should be. Perhaps Ric Chronos and the Flying Frenchie felt that their roles within the company had been unfairly diminished, or perhaps they were simply upset about being in Florida, the vacation choice of every middle-class American family in the world. Or maybe it was something else entirely... "Y'know, we've tried to offer you people a solid alternative to the mindlessly boring fare that passes for competition in the other divisions in the fWo," began the Man of the Hour, "and yet you still cheered for that team that beat us, Tempe or Flagstaff or something like that." "And to make matters all zat much worse, you putains can't even give us a decent theme park to spend our off time at give me EuroDisney over this weak American ripoff." Surprisingly, the people of Florida booed the Frenchie more than Chronos, perhaps because Florida was synonymous with Disney, and the Floridians couldn't take a Disney employee like Frenchie disrespecting their pride and joy. "However," Chronos began, taking over the microphone from his partner, who merely flashed his trademark smile at his "adoring" fans, "in spite of your best efforts to make us stop caring about whether or not we keep this division from stagnating like the rest of the fWo has, we're out here tonight to file a formal complaint against the fWo." "Ze fWo has failed to give us ze level of competition zey promised us, and tonight was yet another night with no opponents for us to crush. So we came out to issue ze open challenge to any team zat thinks zey can match our multitude of abilities." And when nothing happened, Chronos took the microphone back from his partner and looked towards the top of the entrance ramp. "I guess I shouldn't be surprised...but I'd hoped that at least one of the rookie Survivor teams would be dumb enough to come out and find out what survival is really all " But before the master of the Chronosphere could finish his thought, the arena lights dimmed down and an unfamiliar song began to play: Gangstarr's "Skills". And as Premier laid down the beat and Guru discussed the subject of the song, the FanTron lit up with five words that brought the crowd to their feet and raised the decibel level of the fans immediately. For some reason, though, the World Peace Organization seemed much too happy about facing off with competitors of the caliber that they'd be facing. Yukio vs. Neo Fans chanted and clapped for the action to begin as the Ruahh camera crew scrambled into position. On the FanTron, scenes from Uprising where Yukio challenged Neo to a match were shown. Fans cheered as they saw the familiar faces on screen, then turned their attention to the entrance ramp where Yukio was shoving past the curtains and making her way to the ring. An on but mostly off wrestler over the last year, Yukio had been put out of action many months before by the actions of the then triple threat; Spyder, Fallen and Neo. Tonight, perhaps as retribution for that action or because of more recent events, Yukio had convinced Neo to meet her in the ring... no holds barred. She waited in the ring, eyes focused and breathing hard. She was heavy on ring rust, and shook out her arms, dressed in casual jeans and holding onto a steel chair that she grabbed before swinging into the ring. Neo's music began to play; Scum of the Earth, and he strode out onto the stage holding a barb wire 2x4 and wearing a sadistic grin. He made a kissing gesture toward Yukio, then leered and began to stride swiftly forward, tapping the baseball bat in one gloved hand. Scenes from Neo's brutal 'Worst of Five' series with Fallen began to play on the FanTron, and Yukio caught her breath as she began to regret what she had gotten herself into. As Neo approached, Yukio dropped the chair and ran to the ropes. She hit a baseball slide into the chair that catapulted it out into Neo's face as he approached. The crack of steel on teeth made Neo stumble back and spit blood as in the distance the bell rang. Yukio headed to the outside, determined to do damage quickly, but Neo turned the tide with a shot to the midsection from a heavy fist. He had dropped the bat when the chair hit him, and now focused on stiff European uppercuts. Yukio, down on one knee, drove a heavy fist into Neo's groin that caused the long-time fWo star to howl in pain. Getting to her feet, Yukio grabbed Neo's head and sent him hard into the steel stairs. With a loud metal clang, the audience got to their feet and began to cheer, pleased that one of the most hated superstars was getting some much-deserved revenge. Yukio went back into the steel railing, prepared to ram a running knee into Neo, but in a desperate moment Neo found the fallen barbed wire 2x4 and thrust it up as she came forward. Face first into the barb wire, Yukio screamed in pain and tore herself away, forearms and face bleeding with ragged cuts. Neo hit a quick mafia kick, still clutching his groin, and then drilled Yukio with the barb wire 2x4. It stuck on her back, and he ripped it free with a wet, tearing sound. Neo rolled Yukio into the ring, the match heading there for the first time since it began. Grinning now, Neo suspended a limp Yukio in the corner and moved slowly, deliberately to the opposite side of the ring. Holding the 2x4 up, he charged forward, but Yukio got up a weak boot, hitting the barb wire 2x4 and driving it back into Neo's face. Neo howled in pain and dropped the bat, feeling the cuts that had ripped into his skin. Yukio stumbled forward, her face a crimson mask, and hooked Neo's neck. She moved to DDT him down into the bat, but Neo, who outweighed her by more than a hundred pounds, easily blocked it and flipped her up and over with a suplex into the ropes. She bounced off and back, and Neo nailed a swinging DDT that drove her face into the barb-wire 2x4. Smirking, Neo rolled her over, not bothering to pull the barb-wire 2x4 that was caught on her skin off. He covered her loosely, and the referee slide down, horrified by the pool of blood, to count. 1... 2... 3. It was elementary. With such a weight advantage and playing into Neo's sick, hardcore tendencies there was no hope for Yukio who was trained in the Japanese style of lucha libre. Neo stood, started to leave the ring, and then paused, smirking. He shoved the referee who was checking on Yukio out of the way and pulled the 2x4 free from her torn and battered body. Fans held their collective breath, horrified at what Neo might have in mind... Kevin Masters trudged into his locker room. His face looked tired and worn. His bag was carelessly dragging on the floor behind him. He hadn't slept in days. Gripping the shovel tightly in his bandaged fist, he slammed it against the mirror. "He's dead." If you asked around, many in the sport would say it was the one, true, World Title nowadays. Brand Frontier knew this. He also knew that at this point in time the strap meant next to nothing to him. All of his energy was focused on the man carrying the belt, the man that claimed he was the be-all-end-all of professional wrestling, Vic Creed.
