Sunday Slaughter, 8 July 2001

8 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Sunday Night Slaughter hit households all across America with fireworks, which were rather appropriate for the 4th of July just past. Then, from sights of the fans cheering, the cameras panned to the entrance ramp, where Chris Universal was standing. He nodded to the crowd, then proceeded to walk down the entrance ramp. He climbed into the ring, and took a microphone from a ring attendant. He looked at the crowd for a moment, then began to speak. "Ladies and gentlemen of Providence, welcome to Sunday Night Slaughter!" The fans erupted as Universal smileed, and then continued. "We are just a short time away from SummerSlaughter, which promises to be one of the greatest events in fWo history. In fact..." As Universal began to bring up the main event for SS, someone interrupted him. That someone was the Cult Icon. "My Hero" hit the speakers, bringing out Jon Crisp. Behind him are Mr. T, La Parka, Sphere, and Scot Hartt. Hartt had toilet paper around his forehead, made into some sort of bandana. Sphere was wearing macaroni gold chains, with one gold chain including a watch, a la Flavor Flav, from Public Enemy. T nodded in appreciation of Sphere's newest wardrobe, which also included a 1-800-COLLECT T-shirt. Crisp looked at Sphere, and prepared to speak. He shot another look at Sphere, this time in disgust. Universal glared at the Icon. "What do you want?" he asked. "What do I want? What do I want?" responded Crisp. "I want some REAL opponents. That's what I want." The fans booed Crisp, and Universal pondered this. "Well, you DO have real opponents. Jackhammer. Mike Heftel. Those are real opponents." "Come on, you know what I'm talking about," Crisp answered, "Heftel reminds me of this state, or town, or village... whatever Rhode Island is. Heftel, like Rhode Island, is small, weak, and would be the first to go in any altercation with another country." The fans began to boo Crisp even louder as he said that, although some fans just kind of sigh, realizing that Rhode Island isn't quite the banner state of the union. "I mean, what idiots would name this place an ISLAND, when it's a peninsula. What a bunch of morons. But these people are much like these idiots who I seem to be wrapped up with lately. I want no part of these jackasses. I mean, look at what they caused T and Parka to do." Universal took a look at T with Sphere, and Parka with Hartt. Parka tried to eat some applesauce during this, and it ended up going all over his shirt. Hartt took off his toilet paper headgear, wiped the applesauce off, and put the headgear back on. Universal smiled, and replied to Crisp. "It appears that this crew appears worthy of fighting the STUMP Squad." The fans went crazy in applause, and Crisp begun to protest. However, before he could do so, Universal continued. "But, for tonight, I have some opponents in mind that might be more to your liking." "Opponents, plural?" Crisp asked. Universal nodded, and Crisp smiled. "Fine by me. Those idiots, whoever they are, are about to receive their first-ever five-star match, courtesy of me, the Cult Icon." "Oh, I think these guys have had their share of top-notch matches." Universal replied. The Icon looked at him in an odd way, and nodded. "That's quite alright. After I'm through with them, their so-called 'top notch matches' will look like the Great Waru's highlight reel. And that's YOUR opinion, not because I told it to you, but because you need it told to you, jackass." Crisp raised his arms in the air, and began to leave. Meanwhile, La Parka began to shimmy, and Hartt followed in kind, before he tripped and fell off the entrance ramp. Meanwhile, Slaughter rolls on... Nice Bike Frank Snow and Robert Jensen walked just outside the Providence Civic Center. They had their gym bags over their shoulder, and their Tag Team Championships protruding from the bag. "Seriously, we don't need that Horihito bitch, Frank." Jensen stated as the camera got in close enough to hear their conversation. "We're better off without her. We're still the Tag Team Champs, and we've got Loco in our corner. We're part of the most dominant force in fWo with Vengence and Erik Kelly. Things are smooth, man!" Frank pondered these thoughts in his head, then responded. "I guess you're right. Those guys have been watching our back, and we've been keeping our end of the bargain up. I was just surprised you felt the way you did." Robert looked at Snow funny. "What are you talking about? I'm from the frickin' military! You think I want some Japanese bimbo in my corner?!?!" Frank blinked for a moment. "I guess I never thought about it that way. Maybe you're right, man. We're part of Eradication, Inc. A collection of the best hardcore wrestlers ever in fWo history. And we're the Tag Champs. To quote a couple morons... "We reek of awesomeness!" Jensen shook his head. "Yeah, something like that." Robert then stopped, nudged Snow, and pointed. "Hey, check it out." The camera turned to reveal a motorcycle. A familiar motorcycle. "Nice bike, eh?" The camera turned back to catch Snow nodding in agreement. "C'mon. We gotta go talk business with the others." With that, the Tag Champs exited to the camera's left as the camera focused on the motorcycle. He brings BQ up to his feet and takes him down with a double-arm DDT, spiking him to the mat. Burns heads to the top rope and measures BQ, then leaps off with a flying elbow. BQ rolls out of the way. BQ takes a moment to recover, then kips up to his feet. Burns gets up at the same time as well, and BQ leaps onto Burns' shoulders and takes him down with a rana. BQ reaches back and hooks the legs, but Burns kicks out at two. Both men get up, and BQ ducks a Burns lariat. He then takes Burns down with a reverse layout DDT. BQ then nails Burns with a standing moonsault, and reaches forward and hooks the leg. Burns just gets a shoulder up at two. BQ brings Burns up and throws him to the outside. As Burns gets up on the outside, BQ runs into the ropes and leaps over the top strand with a somersault plancha, knocking Burns back down. BQ brings Burns up, but Burns nails him with a low blow. Burns takes BQ, then whips him hard into a guardrail. He then hooks BQ up and drives him into the guardrail with a russian legsweep. Burns rolls BQ back into the ring and heads to the top rope. He measures BQ, the leaps off with the Establishment, nailing his target squarely. Burns covers, and gets the 3-count. Burns snatches his title away from the ref, then the Jobberweight Title as well. The ref snatches the Jobberweight Title back and explains to Monte that you can only win it by losing. Burns dismisses him as a moron and heads to the back, title raised high. Inside the ring, BQ is awarded with his title, and astute observers may notice a gleam in what we can see of his eye, as if he has an idea, as Slaughter goes to commercial... "I just got Monte! Tee-hee!" "Oh...really? What'd you do?" "I slipped a guinea hen into his laptop! Isn't that great?" "Hmm... hen, ay?" "Mike!" "Mike, ay?" "Mike!" "Huh?" Tempest rolled her eyes at Heftel's behavior, and got up from her seat to show Heftel the door. "See Mike, there's the door." "Oh... yeah... looks really rectangulary." "Okies, I think you have to go now. Toodles!" "Guh-zund-heit!" replied Heftel. Tempest smiled and closed the door, as Slaughter continued on...
