Disney's Monday Night Ruahh~!, 7 June 2004

7 June 2004

Introduction In fourteen days, their trials will get far, far worse... Good Odds Nick Brandish, looking no worse for wear after his Unleashed match, stormed down the hallway. Arena employees quickly stepped out of the big man's way as he passed by. He wasn't wearing a tie and the top two buttons of his shirt were open. Not quite his usual business attitude, but what Nick wore wasn't really of great concern to him. Finding the person he was looking for, was. Revelations - Alpha and Omega - each snagged a bottle of water from the concession table which had been set up in the back. It was early in the evening and the stress level in the back hadn't gone up yet. The rapid approach of Nick Brandish was about to change that. Nick stopped in front of the two men. "Hey, you two are tight with that Troy broad right?" Both men frowned. "What's it to you?" Alpha asked with a great deal of disdain in his voice while Omega folded his arms in front of him. "Do me a favour. Tell the bitch I'll be prying the Hardcore Title from her cold, dead hands the next time I see her." Nick almost started to smirk. "I could use some easy gold, and tearing her tits off would just cap off the title win." There was a moment of hesitation as the shock of Brandish's absurd statement sunk in. Once they realized that Nick was actually serious, Alpha and Omega decided to continue the conversation in the international language of fisticuffs. Punches and kicks from the duo drove the big man against the wall. He retaliated by swatting at them like a bear trying to knock fish out of a stream. The first Security member arrived on the scene and briefly considered breaking up the fight. He quickly came to his sense. "I'm gonna need more people here," he said as he pulled out his radio. "Alot more." A right uppercut knocked Alpha into the opposite wall. Omega drove his shoulder into Nick's stomach. Alpha came back quickly and grabbed Nick's huge arm, pulling him into a short-arm clothesline that didn't knock the big man down but did clearly hurt him. Finally an adequate mob of Security arrived and rushed to separate the huge combatants. Revelations fought to compose themselves as Security ushered them back. Nick Brandish, however, was not pleased. "You two think you can take me? Fine. Tonight I'll see you bitches in the ring." Alpha and Omega silently agreed and smiled to themselves. Did Brandish have any idea what he'd just agreed to? Sometimes when a man's mouth writes checks his body can't possibly cash, there was a general feeling of apprehension for his well-being. But in the case of the Monster... it couldn't have happened to a better guy. Contender Brand Frontier looked in to the camera as he exited the General Manager's office. The trademark 'wink and nod' to the fans was gone, replaced with a look of grim determination. You would think he had just been given the worst news imaginable. He strode down the hall, past the only remaining "tag team" in the fWo, Revelations, past High Flyer who was just now arriving, past the concession stand and a few lucky fans with backstage passes. He ignored their pleas for an autograph, it seemed he was a 'man on a mission'. Soon his long stride brought him to a heavy door. He paused briefly before knocking hard. The camera swung around a bit in an attempt to see exactly whom this paragon has business with. That answer came though as the door swung open and there stood... The fWo World Heavyweight Champion. Black Quicksilver. As is always the case when the champion is seen by anyone, he was ready for the nights activities already presenting himself in his wrestling gear. It seemed he might be expecting to defend... the... title... With that thought hanging in the air Frontier spoke first. "Quicksilver." "Brand." A long pause followed before BQ finally stuck a hand out and they shared a handshake of mutual respect. "I wanted you to know, from the horse's mouth so to speak, that I didn't ask for it." Quicksilver squinted, "It doesn't matter if you asked for it or not Brand, however it happened it's you and I tonight..." He held up the most prestigious championship in the sport for all to see. "... for this." There it was, what many had guessed by this meeting. Tonight on Ruahh~! it would be the former United States and World Heavyweight Champion, Brand Frontier against the current and deserving champion, Black Quicksilver. The twelve pounds of gold would be on the line. BQ continued. "I just think it's interesting that while Sco... Death wants the power the belt is supposed to possess you end up getting booked for this match right after everything that happened at Unleashed." Brand interjected, "Well, just know that even though I didn't ask for it, now that the chance has been given to me I can't just walk away from it. Whether I need the belt or not to get the Riders to come after me is a moot point right now. When I step in to the ring with you tonight, friend, it'll be with the intent of leaving as the World Champion." "Just as I'm sure you know that I have no intent of letting you leave as World Champion. I might applaud this war you've declared on Collins and his flunkies but right now this belt is mine, and it's going to take everything and some more to get it away from me." BQ responded, more focused than many thought he could be. "I respect that. In the ring it is then." The two looked on to each other with stoic gazes as they both nodded to each other. Frontier backing out of sight of the camera as the show moved on. 'Nasty' Nick Brandish vs. Alpha & Omega Question One: What do you get when you put three men in one ring together who are over six-foot-five and have a total mass of over nine hundred pounds? Answer: A train wreck. Question Two: What do you get when two of those men are on a mission from a higher authority to protect the current Hardcore Champion from any threat by any means necessary, and the other man is a said threat and has expressed his intentions with no hidden meaning? Answer: A very, very personal train wreck. "Suffocate" by Motograter heralded the in-ring return of "Nasty" Nick Brandish, and the fans didn't miss a beat by booing the hell out of the red-eyed maniac from Thailand. You think Brandish gave a damn? You would have thought wrong. But Revelations, who very much gave a damn and who very much wanted to pound the crap out of Nick Brandish for his mocking threats against Lindsay Troy earlier in the evening, weren't even going to wait for Sammy Hagar to start the opening lines to "Seventh Seal" before they charged out from the behind the curtain and attacked Brandish from behind, much to the crowd's delight. Nick went down to one knee but before he could recover, Alpha and Omega grabbed ahold of him and tossed him unceremoniously underneath the bottom rope and into the ring. Both men then hopped up onto the apron and Omega entered the ring after Nick, prompting the bell to ring. Omega sent clubbing forearms onto the upper back of Brandish before grabbing Brandish by the arm and pulling the monster toward him, only to drop him down to the mat with a short-armed clothesline. Omega followed with an elbow-drop to the trachea and a cover. 1... Tw... A forceful kickout by Brandish. Omega looked to bring him to his feet, but Nick responded with a hard jab to Omega's throat. The taller of the two Revelations members coughed for a breath while Nick got to a vertical base. With Omega incapacitated for the moment, Brandish turned his attention to Alpha. Brandish motioned for Alpha to try his luck, and the hothead from Nevada quickly took up the challenge. The two exchanged punches, rights after rights after lefts until Brandish got the upper hand. He whipped Alpha across the ring and caught him on the rebound with a spinning spinebuster. Brandish mounted Alpha and began to rain down calculated punches to his temple area. Alpha tried to cover up, but Brandish just responded with hard body shots. Omega, having recovered, saw his partner in trouble and charged over, driving his boot into the side of Brandish's head. Nick rolled off Alpha and Omega dragged him to his feet. He sent Brandish into the far ropes and spiked him to that mat with a fireman's carry brainbuster. 1... 2... Kickout. Alpha stalked over and assisted Omega in stomping at Brandish's chest cavity. After being unrelenting for several moments, they toss Brandish to the outside of the ring near the ramp. Alpha and Omega look at each other and nod. Alpha then charges against the far ropes to gain momentum then runs across the ring and leaps into the air, looking to land on top of a stunned Nick Brandish. Instead a very unsurprised Nick Brandish caught Alpha... In the stomach... With a lead pipe. The crowd groaned as Alpha rolled around the outside of the ring, clutching his midsection. Brandish looked down and spit on Alpha before looking back to the ring to see an irate Omega charging for him. Brandish quickly wound up and hit Omega below the belt with the pipe before winding up and cracking him upside the head, prompting the bell to ring for a disqualification. Nick, however, wasn't done. With Omega on the mat, bleeding, Brandish took the opportunity to inflict more damage. He climbed over the top rope back into the ring, dropped to his knees, and proceeded to choke Omega with the pipe. The timekeeper rang the bell continuously, and the referee's commands for Brandish to get out of the ring went unheeded. Omega might have had a severely bruised windpipe if a roar of cheers from the crowd didn't signal the arrival of the Chairwoman of A One Entertainment and the Hardcore Champion of the fans Wrestling organization. Lindsay Troy came charging down the ramp with a steel chair in hand, throwing caution to the wind as she slid headfirst into the ring to confront Nick Brandish. Brandish, upon hearing the mood swing of the crowd, had gotten a good look at who was running at breakneck speed towards him. He rolled off Omega and stood up, appearing to back off Troy after she had gotten to her feet. Troy stood over Omega and began swearing in two languages at Brandish, who waved his hands in apology. He lowered his hands as Troy held the chair in one hand and began pointing at him. Nick dropped his hands lower and let go of the pipe... ...right onto his foot. Brandish kicked the pipe up and it caught Troy square on the collarbone. She dropped the chair and went down to one knee, but Brandish sent her onto her back with a knee to her head. The crowd throws heated boos at Brandish as he exits the ring. He strides past Alpha, who is getting to his feet with the assistance of the guardrail. He gets halfway up the ramp before he turns around to look back to the ring to see Troy helping Omega to his feet. If looks could kill, Brandish would be dead a hundred times over. With a sinister laugh he throws a sneer back to Troy in the ring. "You're already dead, bitch!" he yells to her. "You just don't know it yet." They could see in the dark because they lived in it, ate and drink it, breathed it. Donaven Winters. Primetime. Death. The Dark Riders. Winters' voice came out of the darkness first. You've done nothing that requires it... if you had, you would have known it before you had a chance to open your mouth. No, the blame for what transpired at Unleashed falls on the shoulders of one man... Brand Frontier. It was Brand Frontier who gave my opponent time to escape my clutches... it was Brand Frontier who attempted to cost me the match... and finally, it was Brand Frontier who, with a chair, did cost me the match. And... the World Title. The so-called paragon... the self-proclaimed, self-righteous champion for that which is good and right. Is it right that I should be unfairly denied what is mine? Is it good?" When Death didn't continue for a moment, Primetime ventured a response, guessing (correctly) that one was expected. "Of course it isn't... it's an outrage!" Death chuckled deeply. "Indeed it is... an outrage that Brand Frontier would dare to step into my territory as he so did. He had been nothing more than prey, foolishly challenging me and waiting for me to move in for the kill as I did to Ice and Paladin... but at Unleashed, he crossed the line. By thwarting me he stepped into my domain and tried to make it his own. I warned him before not to make this personal... and he ignored my warning. Now, he has no choice but to suffer the full extent of my wrath. He will know my name... he will know what I stand for... and he will wish he had stayed ignorant. Tonight, I take my price from his body. But soon..." Death paused for a moment. "Soon?" asked Winters. "Soon..." repeated Death, and suddenly his face emerged from the darkness, still trapped in shadows but visible, staring into the camera with flaming eyes. "Soon I will take my price from his soul." Death disappeared into the blackness, and the only sound that could be heard was the maniacal laughter of the three Dark Riders... Could've Been a Contender "Flair!" "He blew it, Schroeder! Why's he gettin' all the favoritisim?" Eli stopped and turned toward the new voice. "James. How's the wife and kid?" The two wrestlers stared deep into each other's eyes with hatred flaming between. "Great idea!" Just as suddenly, they both looked toward Schroeder. "What the hell are you talking about?" asked Eli. "James Renshaw," replied Schroeder, "gets an Internet Title shot. Picture it, Eli... an underdog!" He put an arm around Eli's shoulder. Eli looked at it, and it was quickly removed. "So... because this guy who hasn't won a match in two years says, he's in and I'm out?" "Yes!" said Renshaw. "No!" said Schroeder, "You both get a shot. Deacon is an overpowering champion, can he fend off two challengers at once?" He walked away, with a dazed look in his eyes. "That boy ain't right," said Renshaw. "Amazing," replied Eli, "we finally agree on something." Jon Crisp vs. Steve-O RUAHH~! continued as "Dig Up Her Bones" hit, bringing out the entire International Drainage Commission. Sphere was dragging Steve-O down the ramp by his shirt, as Evil-Lyn, Mike "Big Daddy" Heftel (with Animal on his shoulders), and "Narly" Nate followed. Result: Jon Crisp by pinfall The Modern Day Messiah Coming off of a major loss at Unleashed, Jacob was in a bad mood and it was noticeable, as he drug his duffle bag behind him. Winters couldn't believe that he had suffered a loss to Shirley Savage, but on top of that he had lost the one thing he had ever achieved the fWo Cruiserweight Title. Slowly Jacob turned a corner and continued his somber steps. It had been his, the panicle of his career. Years of work tossed down the drain in one night by one person. He had a plan though; he wasn't done with Shirley Savage quite yet. His title was going to come back to him if it was the last thing he did. "Why would you do this to me," Jacob said under his breath as he looked towards the heavens, as if he was questioning God himself. Honestly, he more than likely was questioning him. Jacob set his eyes back to the normal plane and continued walking, letting his eyes dart back and forth a few times before catching Kristen Jacobs. She was standing quietly with a microphone in hand and a smile on her face. Winters was about to put part one of his plan into action. Allow the prey to know they are being hunted. There's no fun in war if parties involved aren't fully aware of the intentions of those engaging in battle with them. Jacob dropped his duffle bag onto the floor and marched over to Jacobs and tapped her on the shoulder. "Turn that thing on, I have a message for Shirley Savage," Winters hissed at the woman. "She and the rest of this tainted world must hear and heed the words I'm about to speak." Kristen flipped on the microphone and prepared herself for the impromptu interview that had just been forced upon her. "Well Mr. Winters, you suffered a staggering loss at Unleashed do you have any feelings about that?" Jacobs asked, trying to make this as professional as possible. Winters just stared at her, allowing her to realize that the question she had just poised was more then a mistake to be asking. She took a step back and held the microphone out for him to speak, her hand trembling a bit. "There was a loss at Unleashed, but it was because there was a fast count. I couldn't be beat by a woman and I'm not going to allow her sinful acts to continue on. I must free her soul and take back what is rightfully mine." "So, you're not quite done with Shirley Savage?" If looks could kill, Kristen wouldn't have continued standing there. Winters thought he had made his point clearly enough. "No and I won't be done until she's no longer giving her blasphemy and putting a black mark on this company. I will not stand for the injustice of a second rate woman like Shirley Savage, or Nerva or Aimz to allow women to think they can compete in a man's world. In my world. Shirley Savage is just the first of many people I'm going to wake up from this dream." Jacob smiled and chuckled to himself. "Today is the day the war starts and on this day you will find your modern day Messiah and savior," Jacob screamed, as he turned and walked away from Kristen. He had begun his war. The Modern Day Messiah had arrived. Passion As the Slam of the Week faded from view, the opening chords of the instrumental "Unmei (Guitar Version)" began to play, and the crowd cheered as it brought out the arrival of Enforcer. Enforcer, the sister of Eternity who hadn't been seen since Body Count 2004, returned at Unleashed to lend a hand in the fighting against the Avenging Angels. And as one might imagine, we were about to find out why. The battle-scarred athlete slapped the hands of some of the ringside fans before climbing up the ring steps and entering the ring. He reached for a microphone as the guitar beat died down. "Warriors." Enforcer began. "Many of us in the fWo are warriors. But what makes a warrior, anyway? What makes a normal man stand up and fight for something, whether it be a philosophy, a feeling, a paycheck......or vengeance? What makes a normal human being become a warrior? There's dedication. There's fighting spirit. And as my sister said, yes, there's honor. But there's one more thing, one more thing that makes a normal person a true warrior. Passion. You need to have passion for what you believe in, whether it's having a nice home or having the blood of your enemies staining your hands. If you don't have any passion, you're not a true warrior. You're just a two-bit hack who's going to get sent home with some broken limbs. Believe me, from experience." Enforcer paused before continuing, looking out at the crowd. "So why am I back? I've noticed a decided lack of passion in the fWo. I've noticed that no one here ever seems to show the passion that makes a warrior a true warrior. When I was here, and I thought I was fighting for my sister's honor, and I put my own body on the line against Xias to do it, I showed passion! When I was fighting for revenge at Body Count, basically 2-on-1, I showed all the passion my beaten-down body could muster. And when I stepped into that ring at Unleashed, swinging for the fences, I showed that the passion was back, and that it spelled trouble for the Avenging Angels." The crowd cheered that, of course, which made Enforcer chuckle. "Some of you may think I am here for revenge again, or that I hate my sister. Some of you may even think I'm here because I care about the fWo. Well, you're wrong. I love my sister, and to be quite frank I don't give a damn what happens to the fWo. But my sister is waging a war on the fWo the likes which have never been seen, and as far as I'm concerned, I'm here to protect her. How am I protecting her by fighting her, you might ask? Well, if she has the passion to destroy this place, to go up against the best the fWo has to offer, she'll have no trouble dispatching a retired, beaten-down old brawler like me. But if she doesn't have the passion to do it, if she can't show that she's not just an angel, but also a warrior, then I'm going to take her down before she gets herself seriously hurt. It isn't hate that fuels my passion, Eternity. It's love. Because I-" But Enforcer was suddenly cut off as the lights went black and the strange opening noises of "Suite [Dragon of Earth]" by Naoki Sato could be heard all over the arena. The crowd loudly booed as the blue and red spotlights started to flash, and when the noises gave way to the beautiful violins, out from the back stepped Eternity, her arms raised in the familiar crucifix position. What happens in the next few seconds can only be classified as gruesome. Brand Frontier was assaulted, nearly maimed, beaten and bloodied in short order. The rabid fans now began raining down garbage in to the ring. A few security guards had begun to usher away the brave few who had started the barrage of waste, but soon the tide had grown and they had lost control. The guard railing bulged under the weight of quite a few disgusted onlookers. Someone even managed to toss a chair nearby the ring. The announce crew had been escorted away at this point. On televisions across the nation all that could be heard was the heartbeat of hatred that the Riders had brought to life. Death looked around and smiled wildly as a sense of chaos enveloped the home of the Fan's Wrestling Organization. Finally he knelt down beside the bloody and battered hero, grasping the microphone like a weapon he drove it against Frontier's forehead, echoing the sick thud around the already frenzied arena. He finally brought the blood splattered stick back to his mouth, growling out and bring an eerie silence to the maelstrom. "Brand Frontier. The Mighty Hero. Walk away from this, if you can, and know that every step you take, every footfall you leave in your wake is my doing. You exist because I let you exist. Our shadow has cast itself over your crusade, knight, you have been undone before you ever started. You are not the hero to stand against us, but whatever you are, you are because of me." The words might well have been delivered to deaf ears, as if Brand could hear any of it from the beating he had just endured. Finally Winters and Primetime released him from their hold and he crumpled to the mat. Death now picked up the bent steel chair for his earlier assault. Closing in on the now rising World Champion. BQ looked up through the eyeholes in his mask and saw the last thing he'd see for awhile. A quickly descending weapon of singular destruction wielded by a madman. Quicksilver's mask grew warm as he lost consciousness. The crowd roared against the actions of the Dark Riders as the evil took hold in the building and Ruahh~! was finally and mercifully brought to an end, the screen fading to a deserving all consuming blackness. Result: Black Quicksilver by Disqualification
