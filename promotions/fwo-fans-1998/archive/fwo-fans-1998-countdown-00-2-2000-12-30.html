Countdown '00: 2, 30 December 2000

30 December 2000

The acting owner of the fWo not seen outside his office since 12pm; about nine hours earlier. He had been in negogiations with the potential new management since last night at Countdown 3, and apart for a few hours, those negogiations seemingly hadn't ended. Wrestlers wondered about their status, now that Mike Bear was no longer part of the fWo. Primetime & The Black Panther entered the arena, and the other wrestlers showed their disapproval for Primetime with cold stares. Afterall, he was essentially partially responsible for the departure of Bear, a man who was respected and admired by his colleagues and peers. Primetime & Panther ignored them, and headed into their section of the lockerroom. Fear & Hellrazor couldn't stand being near the duo, and exited the room. The cameras followed them out, and James Armstrong was seen walking down a hallway nearby. Cameras rushed to catch up with him, it appeared that he hadn't slept since Thursday night, and his overall appearance was less than stellar. Apparently he had been wearing the same suit for twenty four hours, and he no longer was bald, as parts of very short hair had sprouted amongst his face and head. Armstrong grabbed a bottle of water and took a drink, before asking the fWo technical crew to get his music ready, as he was going to make an announcement. He squirted some water on his face, before wiping it off with a hankerchief, and then walking up to the on deck in preparation to make his entrance. Countdown To Oblivion 2 was officially underway it seemed, and with that, the opening montage began with music from "Pardon Me" by Incubus... Announcement Inside the USF Sundome in Tampa, Florida the fans waited impatiently for some signs of life from backstage. There had been no "dark matches" prior to the actual show, and so when such simpletons as members of the ring crew or even Jeremiah Frankton came out, they got rather large pops. Finally, signs of life emerged from backstage, and music started up. "Pretty Fly For A White Guy" by Offspring began, which brought out the acting owner of the fWo, James Armstrong. A huge portion of boos greeted him, and Armstrong seemed rather disenchanted by the boos. He persisted, and ignored them as he entered the ring. After the music quieted down and the fireworks stopped shooting on the stage, Armstrong began speaking. "I know I'm not a very popular man right now so I'm going to make things go as smoothly as possible out here. First off, Mike Bear showed up tonight and made his departure from the fans Wrestling organization official. He is no longer a part of the fWo. That is all I can say about the situation as it's legally complicated and my lawyers have advised me not to divulge any more than that. Second of all, tonight, there will be an fWo World Title match. As a result of Wretch and the aforementioned Bear getting along in their tag team match with Damnation at Twilight, Wretch is owed a title shot, and tonight he will be facing Ric Chronos right here in this ring in Tampa, Florida!" Armstrong said that last part with some enthusiasm, thinking it would get him on the good side of the fans, but it didn't. Disappointed but his ego not yet harmed, Armstrong continued. "The winner of the Wretch/Ric Chronos match will be facing Austin Davis tomorrow night at Countdown To Oblivion 1. Other titles will be on the line tonight as well. Erik Kelly will defend the fWo International Title against Mitch Wilson, Greg Davis will defend the fWo Cruiserweight Title against Lance Star, ummm... Carnage? Carnage will defend the fWo Hardcore Title against someone... I'm not sure who yet. In other action, Crusader and Plague will meet up once again tonight... and the fWo Internet Title will be on the line as The Black Panther will defend it against Primetime. Like I said last night, if Primetime and Panther don't actually wrestle, they will face major consequences. And of course, I'm sure you're all aware of the potential sale of the fWo now..." The fans booed and booed loudly. They were not pleased that the fWo would have a new owner. Armstrong had done a pretty crappy job but at least he had some type of history with the fWo. They didn't want some new guys coming in taking over. And they'd likely bring all the older overpaid stars back like Slugger, Ice, Davis, Machina, Spencer and Collins and the like. Not good. Armstrong nodded at their frustration. "I know you aren't happy about it, but I do not wish to run the fWo any longer. At any rate, negogiations have gone rather well, and I expect to announce something tonight about the future of the fWo. Thank you all, and I hope you enjoy tonight's event." Armstrong quickly exited the ring, as fans began to throw empty containers of trash. A bottle of water nearly hit Armstrong, and although nobody could hear him, Armstrong mumbled "Damn fans." under his breath before heading backstage... Countdown To Oblivion 2 went to a commercial break in the meantime. Carnage (c) vs. ? Countdown To Oblivion 2 rolled on as "Guilty Conscience" by Eminem (the popular white boy rapper, not the popular candy) echoed throughout the arena, bringing out the fWo Hardcore Champion Carnage. Carnage was not-so-welcomed into the arena with a chorus of boos from the Florida crowd. Carnage rolled into the ring and looked around the arena, before noticing a skirmish going on in the crowd. A homeless man and someone that appeared to be a midget were trying to 'bum-rush' the ring so to speak to get a piece of Carnage. Security held the two people back, but Carnage launched himself off the ring apron at the two men, taking down the two men and the security in the process. Carnage then grabbed the homeless man and threw him over the railing, before throwing his midget companion over as well. It was apparent the two men were MUD & Toad, two people that Carnage had a major beef with since they stole his knife. The match didn't reach the ring for a good three or four minutes, as the two men brawled all over. Carnage and MUD took turns attempting to injure each other, and Carnage succeeded at least temporarily. In the end it was Carnage who was victorious with the Maximum Carnage to retain his fWo Hardcore Title. At the end, Carnage noticed something in the corner. While Toad helped MUD come to in the center of the ring, Carnage retrieved what apparently was his knife and he pocketed it, before heading backstage, promising MUD that it wasn't over between them... Result: Carnage by pinfall Making Plans Backstage, cameras caught up with Primetime & The Black Panther, who were in the midst of a conversation while consuming some coffee near a table of catered food & water. "Armstrong is such a jackass. I mean, he thinks by putting me in a match with you I'm being punished." Primetime said. Panther concurred. "I don't know what his intention there is. He hasn't always been the sharpest knife in the drawer." Primetime nodded in agreement. "Nor the brightest crayon in the box." Panther nodded as well. "The lights on but nobody's home..." The joke had gotten old so Primetime didn't continue it. "You know what, screw James. What's he going to do? He can't fire me. He'd only fire me if the new management wanted me gone, and they obviously don't want me gone. You know, I've got a plan..." Primetime noticed that their conversation was being videotaped, and told the camera crew to scram. The director in the truck took the cue, and sent things back to the ring for another match. 'The Ego' Greg Davis (c) vs. 'The Future' Lance Star Countdown To Oblivion 2 rolled on, as for the second time in two nights, "My Generation" by Limp Bizkit began to bring out the 'The Future' Lance Star. Star ignored the fans jeers, and instead entered the ring, and awaited his opponent. Tonight he would challenge 'The Ego' Greg Davis for the fWo Cruiserweight Title once again, as he did so last night. Star came out on the short end of the stick last night, was determined not to let that happen again here tonight. Star did some exercises in the ring, while his music was replaced by the champion' music. "Eye Of The Tiger" brought out one of the most confident, cocky & arrogant men in the sport, Greg Davis. Davis walked down the ramp, positive that he would not lose in tonight's contest. As he reached ringside, Star decided to start the match on the outside. He charged and slid out onto the floor, taking down the champion with a flying headscissors takeover. The action continued on the outside for a few more moments, before it reconvened inside the ring. Star attempted to win the match early with the Shooting Star, but Davis countered it into an Inverted Russian Legsweep, garnering only a two count. Late in the match, Davis went for the 'Davis DDT' as he did last night, but Star countered it into a Northern Lights Suplex. Instead of going for the bridge, he went to the top rope, and flew off with the Shooting Star Press. He hit it, but got only a two count. Davis got the advantage and hit the Davis DDT, but miraculously, Star got his foot on the rope at the last possible second. Davis decided to take a high risk, and it appeared he was going for a moonsault. Instead, Star crotched him on the top rope, before joining him up top, and planting him into the mat with the Super Shooting Star. Star finally covered him for the three count, garnering himself the win and a third reign with the fWo Cruiserweight Title, although the last time he officially held the title he only held it for twenty four hours, losing it to Crusader the night after he won. Time would tell if that happened again, although not until tomorrow night at Countdown 1. Star exited the ring victorious, while Davis made up for the loss by beating the referee up after the match in the ring.... Result: Star by pinfall Friendly Discussion Backstage, cameras captured "Team Ratings" walking around backstage talking amongst themselves. Team Ratings was officially comprised of the man who would face Ric Chronos tonight the fWo World Title (Big Wretchy The Ratings Saver), and the lovable lackeys known as Rateings R' Us (Scot Hartt & The Mutha Fuckin' Abusah Of Yo Momma). "Man, things just won't be the same without Bear around." Hartt said. Wretch agreed. "Yeah, I mean... I'm glad I get a World Title shot tonight, but man, one of my goals was to finally defeat Bear, and no offense to Chronos, but I would rather be facing Mike tonight for that title." The Abusah seemed rather confused. "WAT HAPPEND TWO MYKEE?" Wretch sighed. "Don't you remember? He quit last night." A lightbulb seemingly went off inside the mind of Abusah. "O RITE! BARE IS A KWITTER! KWITTER BEAR!" Just then, Fear & Hellrazor appeared on the scene, scaring the damn near bejeezus out of Abusah. "HAY! U GUYZ SMELL LYEK BEER! IS THAT..... SHLITS? U GUYZ SMELL LYKE SHLITZ!" Fear sighed. "Wretch... you better tell him that we drink Heineken and ONLY Heineken..." Hellrazor interjected his opinion. "But if there are no Heiny's available, will drink just about anything." Wretch shrugged. "You know how he is... once an idiot always an idiot." Countdown meanwhile went back to the ring... The Black Panther (c) vs. Primetime ... where fWo ring announcer Matt Panzer stood and prepared to introduce the participants of the next match. "Snap" by Slipknot began to play, and Panzer introduced Primetime like he normally would, but Primetime never appeared. Panzer re-introduced him a second and a third time, but again, Primetime never came out. He decided to introduce the fWo Internet Champion instead. "Du Hast" by Rammstein played, and Panzer introduced the champion, but he never appeared either. As he did for Primetime, he introduced the fWo Internet Champion two more times, but again, nobody ever saw the Panther. Fans sat restless, booing the lack of appearance of the two men. Meanwhile, the referee and Panzer discussed what to do, and the referee decided to call the match a no-contest. The fans booed once again, and wondered where Primetime & Panther were. Countdown To Oblivion 2 would continue, but first... a commercial. Result: No Contest Strip Poker? We returned from the commercial break to find ourselves inside the confines the lockerroom, where the STUMP Squad sat around playing poker. For some strange reason, Red Dragon was wearing nothing except his mask, and ditto for Black Quicksilver. One could assume they were playing strip poker to decide who would face STUMP's kidnapper tonight. Last night it was Mike Heftel and he lost. Badly. Speaking of Heftel, he was down to just his underwear, so that was another clue they were playing strip poker. Thankfully, anything that is not acceptable on network TV on a Saturday night was covered up by blurs. "Alright, I've got four of a kind" said BQ, "How about you guys?" Dragon had a pair of aces, while Heftel didn't have anything. That meant Heftel was out. Rather than strip himself fully, he decided to just concede and leave the room. "Er... I lost guys. It's out of you two now." Dragon & BQ smiled. "So much for that old saying that guys who wore masks weren't any good at strip poker, eh BQ?" BQ smiled. "Well, you're about to lose, buster. Deal." Dragon shuffled the cards, before dealing BQ a hand. BQ kept two cards, and asked for three. Ditto for Dragon. "Read 'em and weep, big guy." Dragon revealed his hands. Five aces. Five aces? BQ frowned. "I only have... er... a Go Fish card and a King. Hey, I demand a recount! There's only four aces in a deck of fifty two cards - you rigged this!" But by the time BQ had finished saying that, Dragon had already began getting his wrestling gear on for his match, which ironically would be up after this next match... Crusader (w/Clarisse) vs. Plague (w/ Stone) It was do or die time for Crusader. If he didn't win this one, then Plague would get the best of him in a best two out of three series. "Switchback" by Biohazard started up, bringing out Crusader & Clarisse to the delight of the crowd. Cheering wildly and waving their "Crusader rules!" signs as high as they could, the fans welcomed the couple with open arms. Crusader did some warming up in the ring, and the fans cheers turned to boos as War, Stone & Plague made their way out. The Dark Riders trio entered the ring, and the referee decided that War would not be allowed at ringside tonight, and demanded he go backstage. War complied reluctantly, and the match was on. Tons of nearfalls early on in the match, and both men easily could've won. In the end, while the referee checked on Plague after a high spot that saw Plague apparently take the worse end of, Stone climbed up onto the ring apron and began reaching for something. It was apparent it was the old salt up her sleeve trick. She prepared to throw it at Crusader, but Clarisse decided to help Crusader out. She jumped onto the ring apron and a struggle ensued. Crusader watched hopelessly, before Stone got the upper hand. Somehow, the salt ended up going into the eyes of Clarisse instead. Stone then took care of her with a stiff kick, and she fell off the ring apron. Stone climbed down and began verbally abusing Clarisse. Meanwhile, Plague recovered and grabbed Crusader from behind with a waistlock, but Crusader quickly countered with a go-behind into a roll-up for the pin and win. Stone saw this and was not pleased. She entered the ring and verbally berated the referee, while Crusader went to check on Clarisse. Countdown To Oblivion in the meantime headed for another commercial break... Result: Crusader by pinfall Strange Visitor, Part Two The commercial break ended and we found ourselves in the men's bathroom, where "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly stood in front of a mirror, making sure that his chiseled physique was still in peak form. He had a big match with 'Mr. Main Event' Mitch Wilson tonight for the fWo International Title. Kelly was confident, especially when you consider that he defeated Wilson thirteen nights ago at Twilight. Nevertheless, Kelly reached for a towel and dried his damp hair off, when he heard a voice from behind him in one of the stalls. The same voice he heard last night. "Kelly, is that you?" the unknown voice spoke. Kelly nodded. "Yeah, it's me, oh mighty porcelain god." Rather than take offense like he did last night, the voice chuckled. "Good. I was beginning to get impatient. You can only take sitting on the john for so long." Kelly chuckled. "Yeah... but why are you so secretive?" The voice from beyond said that he didn't wish to have Kelly know his true identity at this time, but told Kelly he would find out in due time. "Fair enough. So what's your advice for me tonight?" The unknown visitor gave Kelly some advice alright - some advice he knew Kelly wouldn't like. "You want my advice? Well kid, I don't sugarcoat anything. I'd drop the chick, she's no good for you." Kelly threw the towel up against a nearby wall. "What? What the hell are you talking about? She's one of the best things that never happened to me. You think I'm going to drop her?" The unknown visitor once again confirmed that's what his advice was. "Get rid of her. She's bad for you. You can do much better, and I don't mean in the relationship department - just the management part. You know, if you had someone else, you'd be the World Champion by now." Kelly had heard enough. "Look, if you're going to talk like that, you can keep your advice to yourself. I'm outta here. I got a match to wrestle. You're just lucky I don't put my foot through that stall and kick your ass." Kelly exited the room in a huff, while the unknown visitor laughed. "Sure. Fortunately I'm protected, and there ain't a damn thing you can do about it." Kelly ignored him as he exited the restroom, still incensed... Red Dragon vs. STUMP's kidnapper Countdown To Oblivion 2 continued at ringside, as Red Dragon made his way out to a warm reaction from the crowd. He had slowly been getting more fans as his popularity increasened thanks to his association with the highly popular STUMP Squad. Dragon climbed into the ring and acknowledged the cheers from the crowd, before the cheers were replaced by boos. STUMP's kidnapper came out, dressed in the same fashion he did last night. As he reached ringside, Dragon decided to start the match on the outside, and flew off the top rope with a springboard plancha. The referee signalled for the bell, and the match was on. Near the end of the match, Dragon went for his Dragonsault, but STUMP's kidnapper moved out of the way, which in turn gained him control of the match. STUMP's kidnapper finished the match off with the victory, and soon, everyone knew that tomorrow night would be the STUMP's squad last chance. STUMP's kidnapper laughed as he went backstage, while BQ & Heftel joined Dragon in the ring, consoling him on the tough loss... Result: STUMP's kidnapper by pinfall Barred Countdown To Oblivion continued backstage, where James Armstrong stood outside of his office with Kristen Jacobs. Jacobs was given her cue, and promptly began interviewing the acting owner of the fWo. "Mr. Armstrong, the question on everyone's minds is, what is the status of the fWo? Is a sale possible? And if so, when?" Armstrong instead ignored the issue at hand. "I'll comment on that after the next match. On the other hand, the *potential* new management and I have decided to do something about Primetime. Since it was clearly his idea for him and The Black Panther to not wrestle here tonight, Primetime will be punished tonight - whereas the new management will punish the Black Panther accordingly if the sale of the fWo goes through. If not, you can be sure that I will lay down the law on the Black Panther when the time is right. As for now, I have decided that Primetime be barred from the building. He is already being escorted thanks to some of Tampa's finest here tonight, and Primetime will not be present the rest of the night. We feel this will prevent such a screwjob from happening here again tonight, and hope for a good clean match between Wretch & Ric Chronos." Jacobs nodded, before asking Armstrong another question. "Would it be fair to say that your decision on Primetime was due to your personal feelings about him?" Armstrong nodded. "Yeah, I thought that was pretty obvious. That and the fact that he was partially responsible for Bear quitting last night, and thought he should be held accountable. The potential new management and I agreed that he be barred from the arena from this point on." Jacobs nodded again, before thanking Armstrong for his time. Meanwhile we went back to the ring for our second to last match of the evening. 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly (c) vs. 'Mr. Main Event' Mitch Wilson The show continued as 'Mr. Main Event' Mitch Wilson came out to a huge chorus of boos from the crowd. Wilson looked ready to wrestle, as that was obviously his mantra - to just wrestle. Wilson climbed into the ring and did some stretches, as his music by Faith No More died down. It was replaced by 'No Leaf Clover' by Metallica, which brought out the fWo International Champion, 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly. He stormed out of the backstage area with Pamela Dee at his side, and the fWo International Title in his clutches. He threw the title into the ring and climbed over the top rope with ease. Wilson charged and Kelly stuck up a big foot, and Wilson ran right into it. He collapsed in a heap on the mat. Kelly attempted to use the belt to take Wilson out, but the referee stopped him. This allowed Wilson to nail Kelly with a low-blow, before taking Kelly out with a DDT. The referee gave the fWo International Title to a ringside attendant, before signalling for the bell. The match was on. Wilson dominated for a good portion of the match, but Kelly came back. He had the match won when 'Kodiak' Vic Creed came down to ringside and began checking out Pamela Dee. A verbal war ensued between Kelly & Creed. Creed (unbeknownst to Kelly) threw a pair of brass knuckles into the ring to Wilson. The referee nor Kelly saw this, but Dee did, and tried to tell Kelly. Kelly turned around and found himself on the receiving end of a punch by Wilson. Kelly fell to the mat, and Wilson went up top. He flew off with an elbowdrop, before bringing the big man up and swinging him into the turnbuckles. Kelly hit and staggered out, and as quickly as possible, Wilson hit the Headliner for the pin. Wilson had won back his fWo International Title, thanks partially to Creed. Wilson & Creed scoffed at Dee before making their way backstage. In the meantime, Dee checked on Kelly... while CTO2 went backstage once more... Result: Wilson by pinfall Confirmation A shot of James Armstrong (still looking quite tired and not in the best shape at the moment) sitting behind his desk was what we saw. As the cameras slowly zoomed in, Armstrong commenced speaking. "Ladies and gentlemen, with some regret and sadness, I have been given the go-ahead to at least announce that I have completed a deal with several investors that have been interested in purchasing the fWo for some time, and the fans Wrestling organization will be sold tomorrow morning." Fans inside the arena who watched this on the CountdownTron booed loudly upon hearing this news, almost to the point that you couldn't hear what Armstrong said next if you were in the arena. "We have come to an agreement, and I believe the deal will be best for both you the fans and the people who work for this company. I will no longer be with the company in a management role after tomorrow afternoon, and will be kept on as an announcer to replace Stevie Roberts as a color commentator." Fans again booed, as this news was not pleasing. "The paperwork is being written up as we speak, and all that is needed is a few signatures, obviously mine. The deal has been agreed to verbally, but both parties understand that a deal could potentially fall through tomorrow morning if things aren't ironed out to both of our liking. Most verbal deals would hold up in a court of law, but given the complications of the deal, it will not hold true in this case. Essentially that means if I were to get a better offer at the eleventh hour tonight, I am more than able to take it into consideration and/or even agree to it without prior notification. However, and I would never lie to you, our great fans. I feel the fWo will continue to live up to the standards it had been risen to by it's predecessors, and do just as good of a job if not a better one than most of our previous owners." Fans still were not pleased, and trash was thrown into the ring. Chants of "Bullshit!" rang out, and Armstrong frowned, but continued to talk backstage. "Terms of this deal will not be disclosed until Tuesday January 2nd, and for further details, be sure to check out fWoWrestling.com, as our web site should have some news for everyone in the morning. We will keep you all updated on the situation via the world wide web. That said, I'd personally... from the bottom of my heart, to thank each and everyone of you for supporting the fWo through good times and bad." The fans inside the arena scoffed at this, knowing how often Armstrong had insulted them in the past. Total chaos ensued in the arena, and security charged down to the ring to clear it for the final match of the night. The shot of Armstrong disappeared on the CountdownTron, and all that remained was static. Countdown To Oblivion 2 then cut to a commercial break... it's final one of the night. Ric Chronos (c) vs. Wretch Countdown To Oblivion 2 returned to find security and janitors still cleaning up the ring. "Keep On Rockin' In The Free World" by Pearl Jam & Neil Young began, and that brought out the challenger of tonight's fWo World Title contest, Wretch. Wretch was joined by his lackey and his lackey's lackey, that being Scot Hartt & The Abusah. The fans showered the trio with cheers, and this undoubtedly proved that Wretch was still among the most popular stars in the fWo. The referee instructed Abusah & Hartt to take a corner, while Wretch stood in the ring, waiting for the fWo World Champion. "Christian Says" by Tones on Tail begins and brings out the champion; a man who has overcome alcoholism & a severely injured neck within the span of six months, who steps out on the entrance ramp to a large reaction from the capacity crowd. Chronos walks down the ramp and enters the ring. He hands the World Title to the referee, who holds it high above the ring, showing everyone what tonight is all about. The referee signals for the bell. Wretch & Chronos shake hands. The match is on. The fans are into the match from the very beginning, as Wretch & Chronos lock up. Chronos gets the upperhand with a side headlock, but Wretch sends him to the ropes. Chronos reverses, and upon returning, Chronos looks for a clothesline. Wretch ducks and nails a few fists to the face of Chronos, before sending him into the ropes. Chronos comes back and walks into a sidewalk slam attempt by Wretch, but Chronos turns it into a tiltawhirl headscissors takeover. Chronos gets up and drops a quick legdrop onto Wretch, and then goes for a cover. At two, Wretch kicks out. Chronos brings Big Wretchy up and sends him into the ropes. Chronos charges in and Wretch decides to hop out onto the ring apron. Chronos reaches out and hooks him for a suplex, but Wretch counters into a suplex attempt of his own. Chronos blocks it, and as both men struggle, Wretch grabs him by the head and falls to the outside, and in the process snaps Chronos by the throat across the top rope. Concerned about Chronos, but more concerned about winning the match, Wretch goes to the top rope, and leaps off with an elbowdrop. He hits it, and goes for his first pinfall attempt. One. Two. Chronos gets his foot on the bottom rope. The match continues at a fast pace, with Wretch bringing Chronos up, and chopping him several times, before whipping him to the ropes. Wretch ducks for a backdrop, but Chronos counters with a double-arm DDT. Chronos struggles up, bounces off the ropes, and drops an elbowdrop. After connecting with that, Chronos brings Wretch up and hits a series of rolling vertical suplexes. On the third one, Wretch blocks it, and turns it into a fisherman's suplex. He completes the hold with a bridge. One. Two. Chronos escapes defeat. Both men get back up and Wretch backs Chronos up against the ropes, chopping him once again. The crowd responds with "whoo's!" in kind. Wretch then attempts a hip toss, but Chronos counters into one of his own. This continues two more times, before Wretch hip tosses Chronos out onto the ring apron, but Chronos lands on his feet. Chronos then slides between the top and second rope, driving his shoulder into the stomach of Big Wretchy. Wretch bends over, and Chronos catapaults himself over with a sunset flip. One. Two. No. Wretch escapes defeat as well. Both men get back up and Chronos shoots Wretch to the ropes. Wretch returns and Chronos goes for a sleeperhold. The Hourglass. He had yet to win a match with it, but it was one of his more effective holds. He used it to wear down his opponent mentally, and while it never lead directly to victory, it was partially responsible. Wretch began to wear thin, but Abusah & Hartt urged him on to make a comeback, as did the entire crowd. Wretch reached up and grabbed Chronos with a freehand, before falling to the mat. This 'caused Chronos to go jaw first into the top of the head of Chronos, which sent him springing backwards. Both men now found themselves looking up into the rafters of the USF Sundome. Fans urged both men to get up as the referee began a ten count. One. Two. Three. Four. Chronos began showing signs of life in one corner, while Wretch did the same in the other. Five. Six. Both men back to their feet. Wretch rushed, and Chronos caught him into a powerslam. Seconds passed, before Chronos went for a pinfall attempt. One. Two. No. Wretch escaped defeat again, this time laying his foot on the bottom rope nearby. Chronos decided to go for a high risk. He went to the top rope, but Wretch recovered, and staggered into the ropes. Chronos fell, and found himself crotched on the top rope. Wretch joined him up there, and connected with an impressive superplex from the top rope. Both men again found themselves on their backs, looking up at the lights. The match hadn't been going on for that long, no more than ten minutes, but already, both men were exhausted after the impact of each move they had endured thus far. The referee began another ten count. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Wretch and Chronos began to get up at the same time. Six. Seven. Eight. Finally, they reached their feet, and charged at one another, thinking the same thing. Clothesline. They both hit it, but in the process, took each other down. Another ten count ensued as a result. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. Seven. Eight. Nine. Somewhere between nine and ten, Chronos rolled over and laid his arm atop Big Wretchy. One. Two. Nope. The match rolled on. Wretch and Chronos got back up, and exchanged forearms with one another, before Chronos whipped Wretch to the ropes. Wretch returned and a superkick attempt by Chronos, before kicking him in the midsection, and hitting a Wretchinoku Driver. The referee quickly position himself for the count. One. Two. No. Wretch decided to go upstairs. Abusah & Hartt continued to cheer him on. He was a former fWo World Champion, but the last time he and Chronos met, he was not victorious. He was determined to be victorious this time. Wretch flew off with a senton bomb, and barely nailed Chronos at the end of the move. Deciding that wasn't enough, Wretch brought Chronos up and sent him into the corner. Chronos reversed it, and when Wretch came back out after smacking the turnbuckles, Chronos kicked him, and then hooked him for his finisher. The modified acid drop. Chronosphere. He hit it. It was over. One. Two. No, it wasn't. Somehow, someway, Wretch kicked out. Frustrated, Chronos brought himself to his feet, and plotted out his next move. He brought Wretch up and sent him to the ropes. Wretch reversed it, and when Chronos returned, he saw Wretch bent over for a backdrop. He decided to go for the Figure Four DDT, but Wretch backdropped out of it. Chronos held for the sunset flip, but Wretch broke his attempt at that up, and then charged to the ropes. He returned, and Chronos went for a clothesline... Wretch countered it by ducking, and when he found himself behind Chronos, there was only one thing left to do. The Depression. After connecting with the hold, Wretch contemplated going for the cover, but he knew that it was going to take a lot more to put away Chronos, considering the hot streak Chronos had been on. Everyone knew what was coming as Wretch stood above the champion. However, someone had other intentions in store. The Black Panther. He emerged from the backstage area, with a chair in hand. As he came down to ringside, Hartt saw him and charged. Panther dropped his chair and grabbed him by the neck, and chokeslammed him on the ramp. The Abusah hopped onto the ring apron and tried to tell Wretch what was going on, but the referee walked over and began arguing with the illiterate lackey. Meanwhile, Wretch grabbed his bandana and threw it into the crowd, before waving his arm back and forth. He charged one direction and bounced off the ropes, before returning and hopping over Chronos. Waiting for him was the Black Panther, who caught him offguard with a kick to the midsection. Panther then brought Wretch up and hit the Killer Instinct pumphandle slam, before rolling Chronos atop Wretch and leaving the ringside area. Fans sat in confusion, but booed, as they seemingly had been cheated out of a conclusive ending to a World Title match for the second night in a row. Panther went backstage, his plans apparently accomplished, although his intentions were very mysterious at this point. Abusah finally got down off the ring apron to go help up Hartt, while back in the ring, the referee turned and saw the pinning predicament. One. Two. Three. Although Chronos retained the fWo World Title in his second title defense, he had done so in screwjob fashion for the second straight night in a row. Unbeknownst to him much like last night, he had won tonight after someone interferred on his behalf. Abusah helped Hartt up, and they entered the ring. Chronos slowly climbed up in the corner, bewildered as to how he won the match. Abusah & Hartt then helped Wretch up, but Wretch for some reason pushed them aside. Wretch then went to the outside and grabbed a microphone. He, like Bear, had something to say about what just happened. It was obvious he wasn't very happy. Result: Chronos by pinfall The Aftermath "You guys know I'm generally a fun loving guy, but I can't take this anymore. The way this place has been run the last four or five months... has been... embarassing. Armstrong has no clue of what he's doing, and it shows. Thank God he's selling the fWo because he has no clue about how to run a wrestling promotion. He should never be allowed to run a wrestling promotion as long as he lives", the fans cheered at this statement, as they were in complete agreement. "Primetime & Panther both refuse to do their job again tonight, and his solution? He'll prevent one of them from being inside the building. Not a bad idea, but logic dictates that thieves run around in packs. Armstrong of course, has no idea what logic is. He has no clue of what he's doing, and thank God he's selling the fWo, because The Abusah - no offense, Abusah - could run a wrestling promotion better than Armstrong. You know, I only had one more goal left in my career here in the fWo. I've won the fWo World Title... and while I would've liked to have won tonight's match, it ultimately wasn't that big of a deal if I won or not. The way I lost, however... and how that could have been prevented is what irks me. Both Primetime & Panther didn't show up for their match tonight, so James punishes one of them? I had the match won. The match was mine. Nobody gets up from the most... ratings saving move in the history of sports entertainment... nobody. Not even the man of the hour, Ric Chronos - no offense Ric. But Panther sticks his masked ass into my match.... my moment... and it clearly could've been prevented if Armstrong had barred both Primetime AND Panther from the building." The fans clapped loudly about this statement, as Wretch continued. "Instead, Armstrong, being the dumb bald jackass that he is, bans one of them. Earth to James, come in James, we have a problem James, you don't have a brain James. Well screw Armstrong... I'm not going to wait around, because you just know James will screw up that deal tomorrow. As much as I'd like to see a new boss around here, it just ain't gonna happen if James is behind the deal. You all want to know my final goal here? My final goal here was to beat Mike Bear, and now that's impossible. Bear left last night, so I have nothing left to do here. If Mike's gone... I'm gone too. I have one last thing to say to you, James. Your mom. I never thought I'd hear myself say this, especially after what I've endured here... people holding me back, other wrestlers refusing to trust me because what former friends had done to them in the past... bad gimmicks... horrible angles... " Wretch paused, before continuing. "But... I... I quit." Wretch dropped the microphone, and exited the ring. Abusah & Hartt chased after him, but Wretch told them he had made up his mind - he was leaving. Team Ratings disappeared to the back, while Chronos remained in the ring with the fWo World Title before him, laying on the mat. As he wondered if this was all worth it, he didn't know what was coming next... nobody did. Through the crowd came a masked individual, and they grabbed Chronos from behind with a waistlock. Chronos tried to escape, but after the grueling battle he had just endured, his resistance... was futile. The individual took Chronos and dropped him literally on his neck, and we had all seen this happen to Chronos before. The Backdrop Driver. Time essentially stopped as fans sat in awe of what they were witnessing. Everyone now knew who the masked individual was... and began booing. The person stood up, and took his mask off. It was obviously one person. Primetime. Primetime looked down at Chronos with a sickening smile. Fans threw trash into the ring as they had earlier in the evening. Primetime looked at the crowd and took in the moment. He had taken out Chronos - again. Chronos wasn't moving. Primetime grabbed the fWo World Title, and put it on his shoulder, before he stood near Chronos, and looked down at him. With a snicker, he spit down at him, before holding the fWo World Title up in the air with one hand, and placing a boot atop the chest of Chronos. Countdown To Oblivion 2 faded to black... with an image of evil cemented in our minds forever.
