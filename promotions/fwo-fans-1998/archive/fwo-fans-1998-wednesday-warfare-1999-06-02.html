Wednesday Warfare, 2 June 1999

2 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction fWo cameras circled the arena in Detroit, Michigan where Wednesday Warfare was being broadcast live. In back of the stadium hundreds of excited fWo fans stood along the street where fWo wrestlers and officials drove past in order to enter the arena. Fans cheered as the Black Panther arrived at the arena and waved to the crowd in attendance. As he waved, he was suddenly attacked from behind by Chris Universal, who laid him out with the fWo Internet title. Chris Universal continued to berate the fallen wrestler, as fWo security drug him off and restrained him. More fWo security was present at the entrance to the backstage area, as Silver Fox arrived in his silver limo, along with the beautiful Fyre dressed in a silver gown. As the pair exited the limo, fWo security stood guard in the event of a Dark Rider attack. Silver Fox called fWo cameraman Geoff Green closer, and informed him that tonight he would show the world that the Silver Fox still has what it takes to be the fWo champion... and sent a warning out to Ultra Violet that his belt would soon be his. Meanwhile, fWo officials caught up with VL, who was seen driving her jeep up to the side of the arena. VL carried with her a metal bat, and when asked about the tag match she was in tonight, VL only responded that she was hunting bigger prey tonight... Stevie Roberts, and didn't know if she would be at the match or not. As she was speaking, the Falcon arrived on the scene, and told VL that if she was hunting Roberts then he could help. As the Falcon continued to talk, Chris Kidman appeared and grabbed the camera from Green, tossing it to the ground and ending the transmission. Inside the arena, fireworks were lit as the opening credits of Warfare rolled, and James Armstrong along with Scott Taylor sat in for commentary... Scott Slugger (c) vs. Shocker Action began in the ring, as the Shocker out onto the entrance ramp wearing jeans and wielding a baseball bat. The Shocker was accompanied by Rudo, Sid, and Billy James, and it was announced again that Rudo was the new leader of the Lone Gunmen, and that he would be facing Ultra Violet for the World Title at the next fWo house show. As the Shocker got to the ring he argued with Rudo, who yelled back at him and told him to get into the ring. The Shocker obliged, as Scott Slugger walked out onto the entrance ramp. Slugger held up a wooden bat in one hand and the fWo Hardcore title in the other to a huge ovation from the crowd. Sneering at this, Scott Slugger walked down to the ring, letting his bat hit the heads of fans as he walked by. With Slugger in the ring, the Shocker prepared to fight to regain the title, but Rudo called him over for some last minute instruction. Not waiting for this, Slugger took advantage of the distracted Shocker and cracked the bat over the Shocker's head. Slugger continued to use shards of the bat to work over Shocker, and then pulled out a lengthy piece of wire fencing from underneath the ring. Slugger announced he was going to have batting practice, and proceeded to wrap Shocker up in the wire before beating him senseless with the wooden bat. Slugger then stuffed a garbage can over Shocker's head and obtained a chair from outside and began to whack the can. Slugger continued the mayhem by saying he was going to play a game he used to play as a kid, called "kick the can". He proceeded to kick the can repeatedly as the bound and helpless Shocker lay on the ground with the wire wrapped around him and a garbage can stuffed solidly onto his head. Finally, Slugger appeared to get bored... and he finished the match off by DDTing the Shocker, who was still wrapped in the fencing onto the can. He then took off one of his boots, and pinned Shocker with his smallest toe to get the win, a first in the history of our great sport on the greatest show in the history of Warfare, no doubt. As the Slugger left the ring, Sid Snow ran up behind him and shoved the champion, calling him a big meanie. Slugger began to go after Sid, but then thought better of it and walked backstage. As Sid pouted, Rudo yelled at the Shocker outside the ring, who became frustrated with the new leader of the Lone Gunmen, and walked away from him cursing. Warfare then faded to a preview of Slaughter, before coming back to live action... Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall Law comes to the fWo Cameras caught up with Joy, who was wandering out backstage with a can of yellow paint and a brush. Joy was wildly painting the backstage area in bright colors, including slow-moving camera men and wrestlers that were asleep or resting. Joy sang while he painted, which endeared him to several fWo officals who shouted obscenities in his general direction. Joy neared the ShowStopper's dressing room, who came out with a bang, shoving Sid Snow to the ground, who happened to be passing by. As Joy attempted to continue on his merry way, the ShowStopper grabbed him by the back of the head, and drove him head-first into the floor. The ShowStopper conitnued to assualt the helpless Joy, who refused to fight back because it was "happy day". As women screamed for help, the coffee spilled, and desperate men dialed 911, a rugged man appeared on the scene. Flipping back the brim of his trusty weatherbeaten hat, the US Marshal took a look around himself and sprung into action. With a quick move, the US Marshal stepped in and took action, quickly picking up and tossing the ShowStopper into the corner. From there he quickly secured a metal bar, and wrapped it around the ShowStopper, rendering him helpless. He then quickly tipped the coffee maker upright, dusted off the top, helped the women and Sid Snow to their feet, and offered a helping hand to Joy. As Joy thanked the US Marshal, he quickly turned and looked into the sky, knowing that there was injustice that had to be righted elsewhere... and was off. As Sid Snow asked who their hero was, Joy answered... "That's the US Marshal, law of the fWo!" Fyre, Wynd & Briana vs. Melissa Smalls, Apocolyptica & VL Melissa Smalls and Apocolyptica came to the ring together, a sign of their new unity as a tag team, and perhaps an ill omen for the fWo tag division in the future. Smalls got into the ring and cornered the ref, appearing to offer him a bribe of some kind. The ref looked startled, and flatly refused the money offered, warning Smalls and Apocolyptica back to their corner. The team waited for VL, who failed to come out when her music was played. The Warfare screen flashed to life then, showing scenes of VL armed with a steel pipe searching for Stevie Roberts. Angry at this, Melissa Smalls told the ring announcer that they would wrestle a handicap match, and that their opponents should get their slutty asses to the ring. With that Wynd came to the ring, looking concerned and not accompanied by her usual tag team partner and former bWo member Fyre. Briana ran out to meet up with Wynd, and the two discussed the absence of Fyre. Not willing to wait for the meeting to come to any kind of conclusion, Apocolyptica and Melissa Smalls jumped from the ring and attacked the two out on the entrance ramp. Smalls grabbed Briana by the waist, and drove her to the mat in a standing spear while Apocolyptica had secured a steel chair and bent it over the head of Wynd. Dragging their bodies back to the ring, Smalls held down Briana while Apocolyptica climbed to the top of the turnbuckle, and executed a flying splash, but missed Briana, who broke free of Smalls grasp, and hit Smalls with the splash. As the ref attempted to get the winded Smalls back to her corner, Briana grabbed the head of Apocolyptica and drug her to her corner, where she and Wynd took turns attacking her in their corner. With the ref distracted by Smalls, Wynd held the arms of Apocolyptica while Briana applied a standing body press. As the ref came over to check, Wynd let go of Apocolyptica and Briana flipped her over in a modified european standing suplex. Briana went for a cover, but the ref was slow in counting, and Apocolyptica was able to easily kick out. Briana then tagged in Wynd, but Smalls entered the ring as well. Despite no tag the ref allowed it, and Smalls ducked the running lariat of Wynd and hooked the legs and arms in a grapevine takedown. Smalls applied a cover, and the ref quickly counted to three. As Briana and Wynd complained about the fast three count, Smalls and Apocolyptica left the ring... and handed a large wad of bills to the ref as they went. As they walked up the entrance ramp the two told the cameras that they didn't need VL, and were going to find her and show her exactly what it meant to no-show the Conglomerate. Result: Smalls & Apoclyptica by Pinfall French Taunting... or Manipulation? fWo camera crews pick up the Dark Riders, stalking the backstage area in search of Silver Fox. Leaving a path of destruction as they pass, the Dark Riders demolish cameras, monitors, and helpless people. The group of four turn a corner, and Death stops suddenly, his face turning red with rage. Posted on his dressing room was the simple number "42". Grabbing the paper and ripping it up, Death grabbed a table and began battering the door the paper was pasted too, finally breaking into the Flying Frenchie's locker room. Surprised by this invasion, the Frenchie, along with Mitch Wilson and Primetime joined the battle with the Dark Riders, as complete chaos broke out in the French Foundation locker room. The Dark Riders had the advantage with an extra member, until from out of nowhere Nikki the Valley Girl arrived, and jumped on the back of Stone, shouting "leave my Primetime alone!" As fWo officals stormed the area and attempted to stop the fighting, the viewpoint suddenly switched, and viewers were watching the scene from a monitor from an undisclosed location. Panning backward, Silver Fox, Fyre, the Total Eclipse and the Chameleon could be seen. Silver Fox watched the chaos unfold on the small monitor, laughing a slow evil laugh, and rubbing his hands together. Silver Fox then pointed to the Chameleon telling him to go to phase two of the plan. Turning back to the screen, Silver Fox whispered "excellent" as the Dark Riders and French Foundation continued to battle each other... Wolf Fang vs. Frank Snow Wolf Fang stormed to the arena entrance for his match, walking past Twitch and Thumper as he went. Seeing Jackhammer follow Wolf Fang, Twitch and Thumper began to laugh, pointing at the guitar hung on Jackhammer's back. Suddenly, an idea passed over Twitch's face, and he grew angry. Twitch whispered in Thumper's ear, and he grew angry as well... mumbling something about "dirty hippie". Wolf Fang meanwhile had cleared the backstage area and stormed down to the ring, with Jackhammer close behind. The ref quickly jumped from the ring and ordered Jackhammer back to the locker rooms, claiming that his presence at ringside constituted a distraction in the match. Jackhammer protested, but finally walked backstage as Frank Snow walked to the ring. Both men paced each other methodically, each extending tentative arms and finally locking up. Snow got the advantage in the lock up, but Wolf Fang slid under his legs to break the hold. Wolf Fang then delivered a standing drop kick from behind, sending Snow down to the mat. Quickly grabbing the arms, Wolf Fang flipped back onto his back, sending Frank Snow up in an inverted bow and arrow submission hold. Snow screamed, but finally managed to shake his way loose, and roll to the outside of the mat. Wolf Fang ran to the opposite end of the ring, rebounded off the ropes, and dove from the ring with a diving plancha. With both men down on the outside, the ref applied a ten count, but Wolf Fang managed to get to his feet first and rolled in and out of the ring to break the count. Wolf Fang then picked the weary Frank Snow up, and suplexed him onto the steel entrance ramp. Snow held his back as Wolf Fang picked him up and rolled him into the ring. Wolf Fang then went for a steel chair at ringside, but the ref refused to let him into the ring. By the time Wolf Fang made it back into the ring Frank Snow had recovered to some extent, and met Wolf Fang with a hard punch to the stomach of Wolf Fang. Frank Snow then grabbed the arms and turned Wolf Fang around, finally latching onto his neck and dropping to his knees, turning the hold into a reverse DDT. Wolf Fang held the neck as Snow paced around the ring, dropping an occasional fist on the still prone Wolf Fang. Frank Snow finally went for cover, but Wolf Fang got a shoulder up and rolled up Snow with an inside cradle. This did not get the three count either, and both men retreated to neutral corners. Sensing an opening, Frank Snow charged Wolf Fang, but Wolf Fang leapfrogged over and executed a perfect running drop kick on Snow. As Wolf Fang prepared to go for the pinfall, the Warfare screen suddenly came alive with a shot from backstage. The screen showed both members of Trouble, Twitch and Thumper smiling. At their feet was Jackhammer, the guitar broke over his head. Twitch pointed at the screen and told Wolf Fang that he was next, and that if he wanted to save Jackhammer additional punishment he should get his ass backstage. Wolf Fang started out of the ring to rescue his tag team partner, but this distraction allowed Frank Snow to come up from behind, and trap Wolf Fang in a cross face chicken wing submission hold. Wolf Fang struggled, but was unable to escape. Finally he tapped out, with Trouble laughing on the screen during the entire hold. As Frank Snow was declared the victor, Wolf Fang quickly ran backstage, limping slightly, in search of his partner and Trouble... Result: Frank Snow by Submission Puroresu Invasion? Backstage Commissioner Beaven was shown meeting with fWo officals about promoting the upcoming Numbered Days Pay-Per-View. Seeing Geoff Green and the fWo camera crew in the room, the Commissioner addressed the crowd and informed them that he had an important announcement about the special attraction match at Numbered Days. Before he could get any farther, the door to the room buckled and finally exploded inward. Several large Japanese men stood at the entryway, and then parted as Waru and two other unknown individuals entered the room. Waru grabbed the Commissioner, and yelled in his face... screaming that he had been overlooked for too long, and would now take over the promotion and run things his way from now on. John Willis hit the scene and restrained Waru, who glared at Willis, and then ordered the large men accompanying him to eliminate the security guard. They moved quickly, grabbing Willis and holding him down, as Waru delivered a hard standing kick to the arm of the security captain. An audible snap was heard, and for the first time in his life John Willis screamed in pain. During this the Commissioner and fWo officals fled the room, but Waru seized Geoff Green and pulled him closer. Staring directly into the camera, Waru told the camera that Slaughter would run red with blood, as the dawn of Seppuku was here. Waru then told the viewers that the definition of Hardcore would be defined with a no rope barbed wire spider net glass crush death match... and Slugger had better beware, for whoever held the title when he came for it would be destroyed. Silver Fox vs. Trouble The crowd, still stunned from what they had just seen, rose to their feet as the lights in the arena dimmed and a silver light shone down. The music of Silver Fox began to play, as Silver Fox made his grand entrance to the ring, Fyre next to him dressed in a silver gown. Silver Fox neared the ring, taking great care to avoid the fans at ringside, who reached out at him with their dirty, plague-filled hands. Silver Fox finally got to the ring, and stared at the steel steps in disgust. Snapping his fingers, fWo crews came to the ring with vacuums and feather dusters and dusted off the ring apron and steps. Silver Fox pulled a white handkerchief from his pocket and whiped the steel step to inspect it, and found it meeting his expectations. Silver Fox then and only then entered the ring, and held up his arms from the crowd, who soundly booed him. Sneering, Silver Fox urged his opponents to come forward and take their beatings. The music of Trouble played, and Twitch and Thumper came to the ring slowly. They looked out of sorts, and climbed into the ring carefully. When the bell rung, Silver Fox quickly took Twitch down with a drop toe hold, and then dropped a knee into the back of the wrestler. Urging Thumper into the ring as well, Silver Fox delivered a spinning european uppercut and took the man down. He then lay one member of Trouble over the other, and pinned them both with one pinky. Silver Fox beemed as the crowd booed, until Plague and Stone hit the scene. Plague and Stone fell upon Silver Fox, attacking with lefts and rights... but both members of Trouble had gotten to their feet and had grabbed steel chairs from ringside. Twitch and Thumper leveled Stone and Plague with the steel chairs, and continued to beat on them as Silver Fox crawled to saftey. Thumper then ripped at his face, revealing Silver Fox beneath. Silver Fox ripped at his face, revealing the Chameleon. As the crowd gasped, Silver Fox dressed as Thumper, pointed at Twitch and asked the crowd if they wanted to see who was beneath the mask. Before he could reveal who the imposter was, War and Death ran to the ring. Silver Fox, Fyre, the Chameleon, and the imposter Twitch ran from the ring as Death and War looked after their partners. As Silver Fox ran, he yelled at Plague that he was just getting started... and that he would be questioning who he was before long. Result: Silver Fox by Pinfall Stevie Roberts Departs Stevie Roberts was shown leaving the arena along with Doomsday. As he stepped into the limo fWo camera crews asked him what had happened with VL, who was looking for him during the entire match. Roberts shrugged, and said that he hadn't seen VL all night, and that he and the rest of Doomsday were heading back to the hotel. Roberts told the camera crew that he was going to shag all night long, and show the ladies of the town that while Space Mountain is nice for beginners, it's Everest that the Detroit ladies would be climbing tonight. Giggles came from inside the limo, and Roberts corrected himself, saying that "I'm going to get my mojo working right now baby, and I'm going to shag until I can't shag any more!" Roberts then stepped into the limo and it drove off, with the Falcon and Kidman running out to the backlot moments too late. The Falcon said he would get Roberts sooner or later, and that he would find VL and tell her that he had left for the night... Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Cactus Jon With the chaos cleared from the ring, Cactus Jon walked to ringside with the Canadian flag waving high on the TravisTron in a video package; and was greeted by a mixed response from the attending crowd. Jon climbed into the ring and saluted the flag proudly, before Ze Flying Frenchie & his cronies Primetime & Wilson make their way to the ring. The Frenchie did not seem to have the International title with him, but did carry a leather attache. He also appeared to be badly limping from the attack earlier in the night. The Frenchie then got into the ring, and slowly unlocked the case. He took from it the International title, and beaming with an abundance of pride, he held it into the air as the fans booed him. He ignored their jeers with a sly smirk across his face, before handing the International Title over to Primetime & Wilson. Frenchie and Jon locked up in the center of the ring, but was not long before the match spilled to the outside. Jon dominated in the early goings of the match with a bulldog on the chair; and then used the chair on Primetime & Wilson. The referee allowed the contact with the steel on the outside of the ring, but when Jon attempted to use it inside the referee warned him of a DQ loss. The Frenchie finally gained control and had the match won; but Jon reversed things by moving out of Frenchie's moonsault attempt. Jon was going to finish things off with the Canadian Crusher (piledriver), but after connecting Primetime pulled the referee outside of the ring during the two-count on the subsequent pinfall. Primetime and the referee continued to argue as Wilson climbed into the ring and handed Frenchie a small object. Meanwhile Jon left the Frenchie to go to the outside and pushed Primetime to the floor. Frenchie had the object as Jon returns... Frenchie swung, but Jon ducked and kicked the Frenchie in the stomach, and then hit him with a single-arm DDT. Jon then went up to the outside, and prepared to leap off the top rope. Frenchie however, pulled the referee in the way... and Jon collided with the referee. The referee, although hurting, quickly signaled for the disqualification as Frenchie escapes the ring... his title intact. Frenchie, Primetime and Mitch Wilson headed up the ramp in celebration. Jon helped up the referee, and apologized for the mistake as other referees came down to help carry off their fallen colleague. As the show went off the air, Scott Taylor announced that a rematch has been announced for the next house show and that it WILL be for the fWo International Title... Result: Cactus Jon by DQ
