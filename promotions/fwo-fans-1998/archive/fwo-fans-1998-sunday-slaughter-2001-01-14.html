Sunday Slaughter, 14 January 2001

14 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction A long white stretch limo pulled up in front of the Alamodome. In the background, it could be seen that the limo had a hot tub in the back. Seconds passed, and a door opened. Out stepped Chris Universal, snazzily attired in a dress shirt and slacks, along with clearly expensive leather shoes. Though the sun presented no problems of visibility, Universal's eyes were still shielded by Ray-Ban shades. Universal, emanating coolness, smiled. "Hello San Antonio!" He removed the sunglasses and shot a smile at the camera. "Welcome to Sunday Night Slaughter, ladies and gentlemen. Before we get started, I'd like to inform each and every member of the fWo fanbase exactly what we have in store for you at our upcoming pay-per-view... Body Count." Universal paused, then began to go over the show's card. "As determined last week, Mike Bear will defend the fWo World Heavyweight Championship against the Boston Strangler. The Black Panther will defend the fWo Internet Championship against Erik Kelly if he retains his number one contendership tonight, or against Juno Law should he defeat Kelly. In a Grudge Match, Primetime will meet Havok. Lance Star will defend the fWo Cruiserweight Title against Crusader and Ruben Ross. Damnation will defend the fWo World Tag Team Titles against Chaos. Black Quicksilver will face Chris Titan. And, the Body Count match. Thirty men will participate." Universal took a deep breath and rattled off the list. "Wretch will enter first. Primetime will enter tenth. Mitch Wilson will enter twentieth. Also in the match will be... Black Quicksilver, Black Panther, Carnage, Chris Titan, Crusader, Erik Kelly, Fear, Frank Snow, Greg Davis, Havok, Hellrazor, Jackhammer, Judge Steel, Juno Law, Kobi Law, Lance Star, Markus Cairn, Mike Heftel, MUD, Plague, Red Dragon, Robert Jensen, Ruben Ross, Sphere, Vengence, Vic Creed, and War." Universal paused, took another deep breath, and finished. "Now, enjoy Slaughter... if you buy... what the Sellout... is selling!" With that, Slaughter cut to its intro, then the arena... War vs. Fear Fireworks explode inside the arena, and the fans cheer wildly. "Through the Never" by Metallica, those Napster (and everyone else on the face of the planet, for that matter) suing bastards, begins. The song brings out one half of the Dark Riders, War, along with manager Stone. Stone and War bring about an arousing chorus of jeers from the capacity crowd. Soon enough, however, their music fades out and gives way to "From This Day" by Machinehead. The 7 footer, Fear, steps out and the crowd pops for one half of the tag team champions. Fear continues to the ring, slides in, and he and War begin brawling. War takes control early, and sends Fear into the ropes. He lifts the knee up and it catches him in the gut, keeling him over. War hits the ropes and nails a huge ax kick which causes Fear to crumple to the mat, early in the match. He covers quickly, but Fear kicks out easily at two. War brings Fear to a vertical base, and lifts him up for a suplex. Fear gets out, and hooks in a waistlock from behind. War looks to elbow out, but Fear ducks that elbow, and then takes him over with a german suplex, complete with bridging action. At two, War kicks out of the bridge, and both men quickly get to their feet. Fear charges with a clothesline, but it's ducked by War. War stays behind and drops him with a belly to back suplex. War decides to go to the top rope at this point, so he does so. He flies off looking for a top rope elbow drop, but Fear moves out of the way at the last second. Fear brings War to his feet, and hooks him from behind. He then drops him with a vicious reverse DDT, before covering him. One, two, and War kicks out. Fear brings him up once more, this time looking for a big powerbomb, but War nails some punches to the face of Fear as he's in the air, causing him to be dropped to the ground. He then nails a boot to the gut, hits the ropes, and catches Fear with a swinging neckbreaker as he comes back. War covers but Fear is able to kick out at two. War brings Fear up, and places him on his shoulders. He looks for a death valley driver but Fear gets out of it. He spins War around and grabs him in a two handed chokehold, before lifting him in the air with a big slam. He covers, but again War is able to kick out somehow. Fear brings him up and sends him into the ropes. He looks for a big clothesline, but War ducks down as he's coming back. However, as War comes back from the other side, he walks right into a gorilla press. Fear then turns it into a big powerslam, and covers. One, two, and finally, three, Fear wins the match between one half of the Dark Riders, and one half of Damnation. Slaughter cuts elsewhere as Fear celebrates inside of the ring. Result: Fear by pinfall The Porcelain God Must Be Crazy (Or Loco) Erik Kelly and Pamela Dee walked down the hallway backstage. Kelly had a slightly pained look on his face, but it was quickly replaced by a look of happiness as he spotted... "A bathroom! I'll be right back." Pamela nodded, and Erik pushed the door open. He stepped up to a stall, while fWo cameras stopped well away from being able to see Kelly. Seconds passed, and a voice came from one of the stalls. "Hey, Erik." The voice was answered by Kelly's own deep voice. "The Porcelain God himself. Long time no see." "Yeah. A whole week. Anyway, I think the time is right to reveal myself." Kelly grunted. "Well, wait a second." A zipping noise was heard. "OK." Kelly stepped back into view, and walked over to the sinks. He commenced washing his hands, and behind him, the stall creaked open. Kelly looked up, and saw in the mirror... "Matt Loco?!" "The one and only." Kelly shook his head in mingled surprise and amusement. "Didn't I drive you out of here?" "Briefly, yes." Kelly turned to face Loco. "But that doesn't matter. I see something in you, Erik. You've got a monster inside. You just need to unleash it. Even if you don't want to admit it, you know I'm right." Kelly smirked. "Maybe, maybe not." Loco continued. "Listen. You've got a match tonight with Juno Law. If you lose, you lose your number one contendership. What could it hurt to have me in your corner maybe giving you a different perspective on things? I can't do worse than Miss Pamela, am I right?" Kelly pondered for a moment, then shrugged. "What the hell. One match, Loco. One match to try your philosophy." Loco nodded. "You won't be disappointed." Kelly looked the little man over. "I'm sure." With that, he headed to the door. Loco followed close behind. Outside, Pamela Dee saw the two exit. Loco moved off to the side as Pamela approached Erik. "What's with Loco, Erik?" "Basically, he's got some ideas for me. I agreed with him to let him manage me tonight." Dee looked confused. "What about me, Erik?" "Well, you're still my manager, y'know, officially... I'm just checking out the options, playing the field. Hey, I'm sure he'll suck." A few feet away, Loco rolled his eyes. Dee looked dubious. "Alright, Erik... but just tonight." "Just tonight, of course. Listen, we gotta go, alright?" "OK." With that, Kelly moved off with Loco. Dee sighed, looking puzzled... Lance Star vs. Ruben Ross The following match wasn't a title match, but it certainly was an opportunity nonetheless for 'Black Plague' Ruben Ross to move back up in the ranks of the fWo cruiserweight division. His music by Prodigy turned the fans attention towards the ramp, where he appeared and soaked in the cheers of the fans. They fans were quick to begin booing as "My Generation" by Limp Bizkit began to play throughout the arena. 'The Future' Lance Star charged to the ring and carelessly tossed his Cruiserweight Title to a ringside attendant and rolled in. Ross tried for a clothesline, but Lance ducked it and kicked Ruben a few times in the stomach, before following it up with vicious chops to the chest. Star tried whipping Ross to the other corner, but in mid-move Ruben stopped dead in his tracks and held onto Star's arm. BPRR tried taking Star over with a hiptoss, but Star landed on his feet and twisted Ross' arm into wristlock. Ross used his technical expertise as he flipped out and reversed the wristlock, quickly bringing Star in for a knee to the gut. Ruben Ross attempted a standing rocker dropper, but Star flipped him over with the back of his head. Ross landed on his feet, but Star took him down with a vicious clothesline. After mocking the fallen Underground member with obscene gestures, Star brought Ross to his feet and whipped him off to the ropes. Lance made an uncharacteristic mistake by telegraphing a back body drop, and this enabled Ruben to drop him straight to the mat with a facejam. Not yet pleased, Ross went to the top rope and flew off with a missile dropkick that connected with Lance Star. BPRR covered Star, but only two was counted. Lance Star managed to get to his feet, but Ross met him with a series of forearm smashes, finished off with a right hand to the kisser. After shouting some smack-talk Lance's way about being "punked out," Ross attempted a baseball slide. Lance wisely moved out of the way, but Ross persisted in his offence with a right hand, which was blocked by Star. Star delivered a hard boot to the gut of BPRR, and picked him up bear hug style, before ramming him spine-first into the steel ring post. As Ross tried making his way up holding his back in pain Lance Star called him a spineless cruiserweight and rolled him back into the ring. Star got up on the apron and sprung himself over with a legdrop on the fallen Ruben Ross. Lance hooked the leg and tried ending it all for Ruben, but a kick out at two spoiled those plans. Lance laced in a few rights to the head of Ruben Ross, and then brought the former Cruiserweight Champion to his feet. Star tried whipping Ross into the ropes, but a reversal ensued. BPRR attempted leapfrogging too early, and this enabled Star to bring him down to the mat with a high-impact sit-down powerbomb. Star held on for the cover, but Ross kicked out at the last second. Lance picked up Ross and took him over with a hard suplex, before heading to the top rope. Star flew off with a moonsault, but caught nothing but canvas, as Ruben evaded the move. Seeing an opening, Ross hooked Lance up for the Plague of Darkness, but Star slid down the back of 'Black Plague' and attempted a roll-up. Ross thought quickly and rolled through, ending the position with a rear waistlock on Star. BPRR brought Lance Star up and down to the mat with a German suplex, holding on for the cover. The ref counted two, but Lance rolled over to get a shoulder off the mat. Ross held onto the move and attempted another German suplex, but Star swung around with a wild right backhand. Ruben Ross ducked it and now had Star hooked in a front waistlock. A Northern Lights Suplex from San Diego's own brought Star's shoulders to the mat and as Ruben Ross bridged his body for intensified weight pressure. Star however grasped the waist of Ruben Ross, and turned the move over in a mimic of 'Hogan-Steamboat' spot at two. Now in the dominant position, Star took Ross over with a gutwrench suplex. Lance Star then headed outside to grab a Sprite beverage, bringing it up to the top rope. After taking a long swig, Star whipped the bottle into the ring. As the referee went to go kick it out of the ring, Crusader appeared out of nowhere and pushed Star off the top rope, enabling Ruben Ross to hook Star up and drop him to the mat with the Plague of Darkness. Ross quickly covered and pulled the three count over the reigning fWo Cruiserweight Champion. As "Firestarter" played through the arena, Crusader wore a sly grin and headed to the back as Ruben celebrated his non-title victory. Result: Ruben Ross by pinfall It Was Who? fWo cameras went backstage, into the locker room of the STUMP Squad. In the background, the Black Quicksilver stretched in (very) early preparation for his match. STUMP sat in a chair beside him, apparently recovered from its massive injuries. It sported a Band-Aid on its head. Quicksilver complained to STUMP, who he appeared to think was working him too hard. "STUMP, I don't care what that girl in Wichita could do, I'm not that flexible!" STUMP shot back "...". "Yeah? Same to you!" Elsewhere in the room, Red Dragon, Claire, and Mike Heftel sat together. Heftel rolled his eyes. "He never shuts up." Dragon questioned "BQ or STUMP?" Heftel answered. "Both." Black Quicksilver overheard, and interjected. "So why exactly are you guarding her anyway?" Dragon answered. "Because, last week, Sphere tried to get his freak on with her." Quicksilver's mask furrowed in confusion. "Sphere's out with an injury. Remember what happened during the battle royal? He took a nasty bump over the guardrail." Claire shook her head. "No. That was definitely him. He tried to hit on me." Heftel interrupted. "Well, I gotta go guys. Got a match." "Against Titan?", asked Dragon. "Yeah." Quicksilver chuckled. "Good luck!" Heftel, oblivious, replied. "Thanks." STUMP chimed in. "..." Heftel frowned. "I am not a jobber!" Heftel paused. "Wait a second... I am. Oh. Never mind, you're right. Well, later guys." "Later", chorused the rest of the group. Heftel got up and walked out the door as Quicksilver resumed stretching... Chris Titan vs. Mike Heftel "Down with the Ship" by Scatterbrain plays over the PA system introducing Stump Squad representative, Mike Heftel to the Alamodome. Cautiously he walks down the ramp, peaking over his shoulder for his opponent who could possibly attempt to strike him from behind. Heftel slides under the ring standing and looking towards the entrance way, as "Voices" by Disturbed gave way to former Tornado Wrestling superstar, and assaulter and kidnapper of Stump, Chris Titan. Chris Titan strolled to the ring cockily, and he arrived and looked into the ring at his opponent where he started to laugh. Heftel full of rage charges and leaps over the top rope with a plancha, but smoothly Titan simply sidesteps the aerial manuever causing Heftel to crash violently into the guardrail, the smile vanished from Titan's face, as he lifted Heftel up and began to ram his head into the railing, finally the onslaught ended as Titan rolled Heftel into the ring. Titan followed him into the ring, stalking his opponent. Titan lifted Heftel to his feet and Irish Whipping him into the ropes, as Heftel returns Titan surprisingly misses his clothesline attempt, as Heftel ducks taking the opportunity to follow up with a go behind rear grapple. Heftel attempted to lift his opponent, but his attempts were cut short as Titan sent a violent mule kick to the wrestler formerly known as Silver Slowback. Doubling over, Heftel left himself open for Chris Titan's bulldog! Obviously out cold, Heftel was covered by Titan and the official got down to count one..two..thr.. no! Titan lifted the limp body of Heftel to his feet. Chris fired Mike Heftel back to the ropes yet again, and as he returned Titan scooped him up onto his shoulders and he nailed him with his finisher, Titanium, a reverse Death Valley Driver. The unconscious state of Heftel wasn't helped by Titan's efforts, as the superstar jumped down hooking the leg of the Stump Squad member getting the count from the referee. And his theme replayed over the sound system, allowing for Titan to leave after a match that took almost as much time to wrestle as it took for him to arrive to. Result: Chris Titan by pinfall Sick And Tired Chris Universal sat behind his desk in his office, which was decorated similarly to last week. Universal's monitor appeared to be tuned in to ESPN and Sportscenter, where highlights aired of the day's earlier football games. Universal groused as, onscreen, the New York Giants put the proverbial boots to the Minnesota Vikings. "Damn Giants... oh well. At least the Raiders lost." Universal shook his head in mild disgust. The door to his office suddenly crashed inward, and two bodies tumbled through it Carnage and MUD. The two appeared to have been brawling for a good deal of time, and just happened to throw each other through the door to the office of the commish. Universal sighed, got up, and put himself in between the two rivals. "Alright, what's the deal here?!" MUD spoke first. "I'm sick and tired of this son of a bitch and I want a match! Hardcore Title and Toad on the line, Body Count." Universal nodded. "That can be done." He turned to Carnage. "How about you?" "Fine, I'll put my best friend on the line, and my title... but I've got an IDEA! How about a SCAFFOLD MATCH?!" Universal turned to MUD. "You game?" "I'm game." "OK then, it's set." "I'll see you AT BODY COUNT!" "You damn well will." "Alright you two, that's enough. If you have to brawl, do it away from the expensive art and electronics. Carnage, go." Carnage glared at Universal, then shuffled off. Universal held MUD at bay for a few moments, then backed off. MUD walked out. Universal returned to his monitor, and pressed the power button. "Well, that should keep those two away from each other for a good fifteen seconds or so. I think, in any case, it's time to call it a day." Universal gathered up his portable personal belongings from the desk, and walked towards the door. Universal stared at the hole where the door used to be, then slowly looked down at the shattered door on the ground. "Hmm. I suppose slamming the door is out of the question." With that, he walked through the hole and out of sight. Plague vs. Hellrazor Slaughter bursts back from commercials to the sounds of "Paranoid" by Black Sabbath. The wacky, zany, and downright goofy Plague steps out onto the entrance ramp along with his manager, Stone. Plague lurks around ringside, calling a few fans "BiTChEs!" He enters the ring, and his music gives way to Machinehead's "From This Day". Hellrazor steps out to a humongous pop from the crowd, as they appreciate one half of the tag team champions work. Hellrazor continues to the ring, sans Lamont, who is shown watching on a backstage monitor. Hellrazor enters the ring, and immediately Plague charges maniacally and attacks Hellrazor with a barrage of punches, before quickly sending him off into the ropes. Hellrazor bounces back into an elbow to the face by Plague. Plague then hooks him and takes him over with a quick suplex. Plague covers, and at two Hellrazor easily lifts a shoulder up. Plague brings him up and whips him into the corner. Plague charges, but Hellrazor lifts a boot up that catches him square in the jaw. Hellrazor perches himself on the second rope, and then grabs Plague and brings him over with a tornado DDT. He covers, but at two Plague manages to lift up a shoulder. Hellrazor continues the attack, by taking Plague over with a fisherman's suplex. Again, at two, Plague manages a kickout. Hellrazor decides to place Plague on the top rope, and Hellrazor climbs after looking for a superplex. However, Plague blocks the first attempt, he once again blocks a second attempt, and on a third attempt, he pushes Hellrazor from the top rope. Hellrazor falls to the mat, and Plague flies off with a big elbow drop that connects with Hellrazor straight in the heart. Plague covers, but, my friend, at two, Hellrazor manages his shoulder to be lifted. Plague brings Hellrazor up once more, and looks for a DDT, but Hellrazor counters into a northern lights suplex. He bridges, but at two Plague kicks out. Hellrazor brings Plague back up to his feet and takes him down with a hard DDT. Hellrazor covers, but Plague barely manages to kick out at two. Hellrazor brings Plague back up to his feet and sets him on the top rope. Hellrazor climbs up, setting up for the No More Pain. At this, Stone gets up on the ring apron. The referee begins to argue with her, and War charges out from the back. He slides into the ring and walks up behind Hellrazor, then puts him on his shoulders. He sets up diagonally to the corner, then drops down with an Exploder, the force of which causes Hellrazor to bounce over onto his stomach. War rolls out of the ring, and Plague leaps off the top rope with a frog splash. Stone hops off the apron, and the ref turns back to the ring. He sees Plague covering Hellrazor, and registers the three count. Plague pulls off the upset, and War and Stone roll into the ring and begin stomping on Hellrazor. Plague quickly joins in. This doesn't go on for very long before the entire Underground (Mike Bear, Wretch, Ruben Ross, Hellrazor's partner Fear, Eddie, and Lamont) sprints to the ring. Stone and Plague bail, and War backs off, but finds himself against a corner. Bear steps forward and grabs him by the hair, then throws him to Fear, who grabs him in a double choke. He then lifts War up and slams him down with a two-handed chokeslam. War rolls from the ring and joins up with his partners as the Underground helps their comrade up to his feet inside the ring. Slaughter, meanwhile, heads backstage. Result: Plague by pinfall Exeunt Universal As Chris Universal approached the limo, he could sense something was wrong. What, however, he wasn't sure. Then he saw it. A flash of black against the white of the limo on his peripheral vision. Universal walked cautiously to the back of the limo. To his surprise, the hot tub's water had been blackened. It was completely jet-black. "What the hell?" Universal shook his head, then walked towards the front of the limo. He opened the passenger side door and found the driver. Tied up. A bouquet of black roses sat by the driver. Universal stepped back to assess the situation. One thought ran through his head again and again. It could only be one person. "Strangler." Erik Kelly vs. Juno Law "Victory" hits the speakers, and the crowd boos as Juno Law, accompanied by his brother Kobi, begins to make his way to the ring. Erik Kelly strides to the ring, with Matt Loco behind him. As Kelly reaches the ring, he steps onto the ring apron. He leaps high and hits a forearm to the face, but Kelly only slightly staggers. Law backs up and charges again, but this time Kelly catches him as he leaps. He then hurls Juno over his shoulder with a fallaway slam. Kelly brings Juno up and backs him into a corner, then whips him across the ring into the other corner where he hits with a thunderous impact. Law stumbles out, and Kelly takes him down with a hellacious clothesline. On the outside, Loco claps accordingly. Kelly drops down and makes a cover, but only gets two. Kelly brings Law up once more and lifts him up in a gorilla press, then drops him straight down to the mat on his face. Law stumbles back up to his feet as Kelly waits, then charges at Law and takes him down with a back elbow. Kelly brings Law back up and whips him into the ropes. Kelly sets up for a spinebuster front slam, but Law slides between his legs. Before Kelly can react, Law reaches out and low blows Kelly. Law quickly grabs Kelly by the head and bulldogs him down to the mat. Loco on the outside looks annoyed. Law leaps onto the second turnbuckle, then leaps off with a moonsault that connects squarely with Kelly. He hooks a leg, but Kelly kicks out powerfully at two. Law waits for Kelly to get back up to his feet, then hits a standing dropkick to the chest that knocks Kelly into the ropes. Law backs up, then superkicks Kelly up and over the top. Kelly lands with a thud on the outside, and Kobi Law immediately begins putting the boots to him. After a few moments of this, Kobi brings Kelly back to his feet and throws him into the ring. Loco looks furious, but knows enough to keep his distance from Kobi. Inside, Juno brings Kelly back to his feet. He begins chopping away at Kelly, finally backing him into a corner. Law walks back about halfway across the ring, then charges at Kelly. He leaps, but Kelly catches him in mid-splash. Kelly reaches down to bring Law up, but Law grabs him and rolls him up into a small package. This gets two before a caught-off-guard Kelly kicks out. As they get up, Kelly grabs Law and whips him into the ropes. As he comes off, Kelly powerslams him to the mat. Kelly brings Law up once more, and hooks him up. He then lifts him high into the air and holds him in a suplex position. He holds him... holds him... holds him... then finally drops Law to the mat. Kelly floats over and makes a cover, but again Law kicks out at two. Kelly brings him back up and whips him into the ropes, but Law ducks underneath Kelly's attempted big boot. He kicks out at Kelly as Kelly turns around, but Kelly catches the foot. Kelly spins him around and goes for a lariat, but Law ducks under it. As Kelly turns around, Law kicks out again, and again Kelly catches him. This time, Law leaps and hits an enzuiguri to the shoulder of Kelly, staggering him. Law quickly gets back up, runs into the ropes, and leaps. He nails a leg lariat, knocking Kelly to the mat. Law makes the cover, but Kelly kicks out at two. Law heads to the apron as Kelly gets up, and begins climbing the turnbuckles. Kelly reaches his feet as Law reaches the top turnbuckle. Law leaps off, and nails a flying dropkick. He covers Kelly. Kelly grabs the ropes and stops himself short. Law, who has put his head down, looks up - and finds Kelly's hand wrapped around his throat. Kelly lifts him up and chokeslams him to the mat. Mike Bear/Wretch/Boston Strangler vs. Primetime/Black Panther/Mitch Wilson Slaughter heads to the ring for its huge main event, and the crowd is jacked. "Last Cup Of Sorrow" begins, and Mitch Wilson makes his way to the ring to loud boos. He rolls in and waits for his partners. "Du Hast" starts, and the boos continue as the Internet Champion Black Panther makes his way to the ring, belt around his waist. He hands it off to the referee and stands a few feet away from Mitch. "Snap" starts, and the boos intensify as Primetime makes his way to the ring, grinning arrogantly. He rolls into the ring and joins Panther, paying little to no attention to Wilson. His music is replaced by "O Fortuna", and the fans give a loud if mixed reaction for the Boston Strangler, who smartly stops in front of the ring rather than enter with the three inside. "Rockin' In The Free World" hits, and the fans cheer loudly as Wretch makes his way to the side of the Strangler. Finally, "Engel" starts, and the crowd goes nuts as fWo World Champion Mike Bear makes his way to Strangler and Wretch. Bear unstraps the belt from his waist and throws it to a nearby ring attendant, and the trio rushes the ring. The heels meet them with stomps as they enter, but they quickly make their way to their feet and begin brawling. Bear begins fighting Panther, Wretch pairs off with Wilson, and Primetime and Strangler go at it as well. The faces appear to be winning the brawl, and Strangler and Bear hurl their opponents from the ring. The trio then advances on Mitch Wilson, who, deciding discretion is the better part of valor, attempts to back off. He has nowhere to go however, and eats a big right hand from Strangler. The right sends him to Mike Bear, who clocks him as well. Wilson staggers off in the direction of Wretch, who winds up mightily and then pokes him in the eye. Wilson stumbles about, blinded, and Wretch goes behind him and back suplexes him as Strangler and Bear head to the corner. Wretch bridges up, and the ref counts two before Wilson kicks out. Wretch brings Wilson up to his feet and whips him into a corner, then charges in with a lariat that finds its mark. Wilson stumbles out, and Wretch dropkicks him in the face, knocking him down. Wretch quickly brings him back up to his feet and takes him down with a hard bodyslam. Wretch heads to the second rope and leaps off with a legdrop that finds its mark. Wretch covers, but Wilson kicks out at two. Wretch brings Wilson to his feet once more and whips him into the ropes. Wretch goes for a big boot, but Wilson ducks under it and stops himself. Wretch turns around, and Wilson spins and blasts him with a forearm. Wilson grabs Wretch and hiptosses him into a Mitch-inoku Driver. He reaches forward and hooks a leg, but Wretch kicks out at two. Wilson tags out to the Black Panther, who enters the ring and begins pounding on Wretch with forearms as he gets up. Panther whips Wretch into the ropes and powerslams him to the mat. Panther covers, but only gets two. Panther brings Wretch up and whips him into the corner, then follows him in with a back elbow. Panther measures Wretch and begins unloading with elbows. Finally, Panther stops and drags Wretch out of the corner. He lifts Wretch up in a gorilla press and then slams him to the mat. Panther then tags out to Primetime, who brings Wretch up to his feet and takes him down with a snap suplex. Primetime brings Wretch back up and takes him down with an inverted atomic drop, which he follows up with a leaping clothesline. Primetime covers, but once more only gets two. He brings Wretch up and holds him by the hair as Wretch attempts to crawl over to his corner. Primetime then yanks Wretch back and up to his feet. Primetime grabs him in a suplex position and brings him up, but Wretch suddenly shifts his weight and DDTs Primetime to the mat. He takes a moment to shake off the cobwebs, then brings Primetime up and eschews the opportunity to tag out in favor of a chance to do some damage on Primetime. He whips Primetime into the ropes and doesn't see him make a blind tag to Mitch Wilson. Wretch nails Primetime coming off with a big boot to the face, knocking him down. Wretch goes to the head of Primetime and throws his bandana to the crowd. He is then spun around by Mitch Wilson, who takes him down with a Headliner. Wilson covers, and Wretch is eliminated. Mike Bear hits the ring immediately and catches Wilson getting up with a superkick, knocking him back into his corner where he tags out to Panther. Panther hits the ring charging, but becomes the victim of a Bear DTH. Panther stumbles back up to his feet, and Bear lifts him up and takes him down, in an amazing display of strength, with a death valley driver. Bear covers, but only gets two. He brings Panther up, but Panther hits a cheap shot to the gut that may or may not have been low. Panther hooks Bear up and takes him down with a vertical suplex, then brings him right back up. He whips Bear into the ropes, and catches him with a hard clothesline. Panther covers, but only gets two. He brings Bear back up and whips him into a corner. Bear stumbles out, and Panther runs into the ropes. He then bulldogs Bear down to the mat. He covers, but Bear manages to kick out at two. Panther brings Bear up and tags out to Primetime, then holds Bear open for him. Primetime slingshots himself over the top rope and enters with a punch to the kidneys. Bear stumbles off, and then takes a superkick from Primetime. Primetime covers, but again gets just a two. He brings Bear to his feet and whips him into the ropes, then catches him with a leaping leg lariat. Primetime brings Bear back up to his feet, then tags Panther into the ring for a double-team move. They whip Bear into the ropes, but he ducks their double clothesline. As they turn, Bear, coming off, leaps and clotheslines the duo to the ground. He shakes off the cobwebs and brings Panther up. He lifts Panther onto his shoulders, then takes him down with a picture-perfect Whiplash. Three seconds later, the teams are even at two. Bear is immediately blindsided by Primetime, who is quickly joined by Mitch Wilson. This brings the Strangler into the ring as well, and the referee begins to lose control. The four warriors brawl wildly, with Strangler and Wilson going at it as well as Bear and Primetime. Wilson throws a punch at Strangler, but Strangler grabs his arm and twists it into the Boston Massacre. Primetime attempts to throw a superkick at Bear, but Bear sees it coming and grabs his foot. He then spins Primetime around, and lifts him onto his shoulders. Bear takes Primetime down with the Whiplash. Bear makes the cover, and the ref, saying screw the principles of who is the legal man and who isn't, drops down to count. One. Two. Before he reaches three, Mitch Wilson cries out that he quits. Three. Bear and Strangler simultaneously eliminate Primetime and Wilson, and the duo survives the match. Slaughter's cameras go off the air on the image of Mike Bear and the Boston Strangler, standing victorious, just days before they will meet for the World Title... at Body Count. Result: Bear/Wretch/Strangler (Bear & Strangler survive)
