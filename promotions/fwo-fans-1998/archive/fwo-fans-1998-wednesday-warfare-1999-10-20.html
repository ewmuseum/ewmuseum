fWo — Card

Wednesday Warfare, 20 October 1999

20 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Lights flashed as the ambulance screeched into the secured parking area, waiting for the bay door in the back of the arena to be raised to allow entry. The driver was on edge, nervous and sweating. fWo security staff got the entry cleared and waved the driver inside the building, running alongside the ambulance as it pulled inside. Backstage was in chaos. Technicians and medical staff frantically ran in seemingly random directions, obviously on edge. The ambulance drove through the mayhem, passing a group of workers who were watching from the sidelines. Angel stood next to Silver Fox and Fyre, and all three looked pale at what they had just witnessed. The ambulance came to a stop, and the medical staff burst from the doors, grabbing a gurney and large bags of medical equipment as they went. They had to push their way through the crowd that had gathered there, but had little success as technical staff stayed stiff with horror. Ken Armstrong and Tom Deeth were near, and began to push people out of the way so the medical staff could get to their destination. As people parted, the scene before the medical crew was made clear. One turned and attempted to contain the urge to vomit as another went deathly white as the color visibly drained from his face. John Willis sat on the ground with Melissa Smalls, attempting to protect the body of the commissioner from the crowd. Commissioner Travis lay in a pool of blood, his eyes puffed closed and a large gash running down the side of his face. The hair on one side of his head appeared to have been removed and the bloody skin and bits of skull could be seen through the gore, as if he was driven into the concrete and then drug across the hard surface. One arm was bent in an strange direction and a bit of the bone was seen broken through the skin. He was obviously unconscious, and his breathing came in short rasps that contained a small click of his throat as he exhaled. Willis let the medical staff get to him, and then turned and drove his fist into the wall, punching a hole and creating a small cloud of plaster and dust. Screaming at everyone and none at once John yelled, "Dammit... why couldn't I have been there... Who did this? Who did this dammit! I'll kill whoever did this..." Somewhere far away from this in the dark recesses of the arena Grendal sat, his eyes closed in still contemplation. Suddenly his eyes fluttered open, and murmuring softly said, "The second sign... already..." An offer made... Away from the chaos witnessed earlier Geoff Green walked next to Samuel Smooth and Briana. Geoff was explaining how the cameraman can actually be the most important tool in developing a proper push for a wrestler, given the fact that it is through the eyes of the cameraman that the rest of the world views what is happening in the ring. Geoff explained that people who were in good with the cameraman got the better angles and color quality. As Geoff was about to continue he spotted Havok talking to someone in hushed tones down the hall. Geoff winked at Hellrazor and Smooth, telling them that it was time to go to work and he paced quietly down the hall toward Havok, his camera raised to one shoulder. He flipped the record button, and then gasped with surprise as he saw Havok talking to the Black Panther. Havok was saying, "Listen... I know I came in at the end of your match at Fallout, but I've got to make a name for myself like everyone else in this promotion. Now all I'm saying is this... you and I aren't booked for tonight, and I'm thinking that instead of fighting each other we should team up to take someone on. With you, the wrestler of the year, and me... the wrestler of the millennium... we could really do some damage together. What do you say?" The Panther looked skeptical and started to walk away, but Havok put a hand on the Panther's shoulder and stopped him. "Hold up masked man... I understand why you might be skeptical, so I'll give you one week to decide. Just remember, you and I... the millennium men... we couldn't be beaten, you know what I'm saying?" The Panther now seemed to be considering the offer, as Geoff Green slowly backed away. From the main arena sounds of a bell were heard, and the cameras went to the floor for the first match... Hiroshima vs. Micky Maliboo Inside the ring already was Hiroshima, who waved the flag of Japan in the air proudly, much to the annoyance of the crowd. Hiroshima ignored the boos and cat calls from the crowd, and instead raised a solid fist in the air in defiance. Micky Maliboo walked to the ring with the lovely Vixon behind him, and Maliboo slapped the hand with some of the fans as he went, seemingly oblivious to the amount of attention that was being paid to his attractive valet, who was dressed in a tight American print bikini. Maliboo swung into the ring and pushed his hair back with one hand, and then mocked surfing on one turnbuckle. The crowd yelled warnings to Maliboo as Hiroshima rushed at him from behind, but the big man struck with a punch to the back and then picked him up for a suplex. Hiroshima attempted a quick cover, but Maliboo kicked out easily at one. Mailboo went running to the ropes for a lariat, but upon impact the big man remained on his feet. Maliboo whipped sweat from his brow and went for another lariat, but again Hiroshima stayed up. Maliboo went to the ropes for a third, but Hiroshima caught him and brought him up in a big bear hug. Hiroshima bore down on the hold, trying to drive to oxygen from the lungs of the surfing superstar, and Maliboo began to fade. The ref checked Maliboo's arm once... then twice... but on the third attempt Maliboo pumped his arm up and began boxing the ears of the big man. Hiroshima staggered and released Maliboo, and Maliboo quickly went to the ropes and rebounded off with a big drop kick that took the big man down to the mat. Maliboo quickly covered, but before the two count could be made Prophet slid into the ring from nowhere and began to kick down at Maliboo. As the referee called for the DQ Prophet continued to stomp away on Maliboo, until Oracle ran down the entrance ramp carrying a baseball bat. Oracle swung with the bat, breaking it over the head of Prophet and sending the big man staggering backward. Maliboo got to his feet, and he and Oracle executed a double clothesline that sent Prophet from the ring. Prophet shook his fist at them as the stare down commenced, but the cameras were quickly called backstage where the World Champion was arriving... Result: Micky Maliboo by DQ Arrivals... A long white limousine pulled into the backstage area, honking briefly at the Surgeon, who was talking with the New Ninja about his future genetic plans. The Ninja looked bored and annoyed at once, and stepped away from the path of the limo, leaving the Surgeon standing in it's path as he continued to rant about the virtues of gene splicing. Having given one warning already the limo drove forward, colliding with the Surgeon as it went. The Surgeon yelped and ran out of the way, which generated laugher from Twitch and Thumper who were watching the scene with interest. The limo pulled to a stop, and Matt Loco quickly crowded next to it, trying to see through the tinted glass. A door opened, and the fWo World Heavyweight Champion Scott Slugger exited, followed by Ice. The two stretched, and then looked with satisfaction at the limousine they drove to the arena in. The license plate read "Slugger1" and the limo appeared to be brand new. Matt Loco quickly shoved a microphone in the champion's face, asking where he had come by such a nice vehicle. Slugger grinned cockily and said, "It's apparently a gift little man... now get out of Slugger's way, won't you? I've got to relax a little more before I squash that masked bug in the main event... after all, I've got another win to rack up... you hear me Collins? Now Fritz... Magee... hurry up and get Slugger's bags you miserable pieces of crap under Slugger's boot..." The two ring attendants snapped to attention, quickly grabbing the bags of Ice and Slugger and walking to their dressing rooms. Slugger gave the fatter one a kick in the ass to get him moving faster, and laughed as he left the scene, leaving a bewildered Matt Loco to look on at the limo with envy... Courage makes a challenge... Back in the arena the music of Courage suddenly played, and the man himself followed by his hefty associate Jake stepped out onto the ramp. Courage grinned widely, and raised the fWo International Championship high above his head. Fireworks exploded on the ramp as Courage raised a microphone to his mouth and began to speak in commanding tones to the crowd. "Have no fear... Courage is here!" The crowd mildly cheered for this, although there were more boos than cheers as the fans waited for what he would say next. "Finally one title in the fWo has a small amount of dignity! After being traded from loser to pathetic loser in the history of this title, the International belt is finally in the hands of a deserving champion! A champion that you can cheer! A champion that makes you get down on your knees and thank God that wrestling was ever invented! And what better title for Courage to hold than the biggest, most important title in fWo history... the International Title! In other countries this title is given the respect it deserves... the respect of being the global... nay... the International recognition of a belt that you can respect and honor! I am your champion, and I have determined that the biggest Warfare of the year would not be the biggest anything if Courage was not represented! So I've scoured the backstage area to bring you an opponent worthy of the skills and honor that I bring to the ring! Who deserves the political rub that even being in the same room with the fWo International Champion provides! Jake, carry this belt and get your fat ass to the ring so I can show the billions and billions of Courage fans out there why I am the greatest champion in the history of the world!" Courage walked to the ring as all eyes turned to the ramp to see who his opponent would be... Courage (c) vs. DJ Thickee Thick Loud rap music blared over the arena speakers and the lights began to flash as a man in an oversized baseball jersey, pants hanging down below his ass, fWo boxer shorts visible, and oversized sunglasses covering his eyes beneath a Oakland Raiders stocking cap. He carried with him a microphone, and began to rap along with the music. He sang: "Hey hey hey! Yo yo yo! I'm facin' Mr. Rock 'n Roll. He ain't not'in special, just anot'er punk. Now I'm gonna trash him like a hunk o' junk. I'm world reknowned, Hugely endowed. Rounder than that Jake, Talkin' bout my snake. I know you ain't quick, like DJ Thickee Thick, What I'm tellin' y'all is I've a really big dick. Hey!" The crowd groaned as mumbling of "Heftel again" ran through the crowd. DJ Thickee Thick shuffled down to the ring and began to wave his arms to the crowd, who responded by tossing their bottles, chairs, and small children at him. Thick ducked this, and climbed up to the ring apron with some difficulty. The pants had dropped down still farther until they hung around his knees, restricting his movement and showing off more of the fWo boxer shorts he wore. He attempted to step over the ropes, but the pants prevented this so he slid in underneath. The pants began to twist around his knees, restricting his movement still farther. He faced Courage despite this, and the fWo International champion struggled to maintain a straight face. Thick took a swing at Courage, catching him in the jaw with a quick rabbit punch. Courage recoiled, holding his face and looking annoyed. Courage reached out and pulled the jersey up over Thick's head, and began to punch him through it as Thick stumbled around blindly. Courage left Thick and climbed to the top rope to execute a springboard moonsault which connected on the still blinded through heavy apparel DJ Thickee Thick. Courage covered him for the successful pinfall and then stood up and held his arms out from the crowd. Jake clapped for him and held up his International title, but the crowd still booed the champion. Ignoring them, Courage walked up the entrance ramp with Jake. He did not see Eron coming, who carried with him a metal trash can. Eron swung the can into the face of Courage, denting the can and making a loud gonging sound that rang out through the arena. Courage stumbled backward, and Eron executed a superkick from the top of the ramp that sent Courage flying out off the edge of the ramp and down to the crowd with a thud. Eron grabbed the International title and tossed it down at Courage yelling that if Courage had any balls he would show up with the title on Slaughter to get his ass kicked by the greatest International Champion there ever was. Jake tended to Courage who appeared to be hurt on the arena floor as cameras shifted backstage once more... Result: Courage by Pinfall A present between friends... Plague looked at the box with some confusion. "What's inside?" Stevie Roberts smiled. "Just open it Johnny... I think you'll be surprised." Plague looked skeptically at the present he held in his hands, and ripped off the wrapping paper slowly, looking at Roberts carefully. Roberts smiled, and nudged VL beside him, who rolled her eyes at War. War smiled down at her in the dressing room as Plague continued to open his present. Tossing the wrapping paper on the floor, Plague pulled out a pair of wrestling tights, and a spiky leather jacket. On the back of the jacket the words "NEW WAVE" were written and Stevie slapped Plague on the back with a large grin. "You see? It's your old wrestling outfit! I've got mine too! Since we did so well on Slaughter I thought we should make it more official! That's why I've gone to the fWo and told them that starting after Mob Rules New Wave is back, baby!" Plague grinned, and looked up at War. "That's great Stevie! I've been really looking forward to that, and it's pretty swell that you got me this gift. Tonight we've got our tournament match, War and I will do the job while looking out for that big meanie Death... or should I say Dork... and then it's smooth sailing to the end of the tournament." Stevie beamed at this and extended his arms to give Plague a hug. "Come out Johnny... give me a hug... I know you wanna!" Plague's smile vanished off his face, and with effort he put it back on again. Looking up at War, Plague submitted to the hug as Stevie continued to beam. After breaking the hug Stevie wanted to make certain that his friends knew that he wasn't light in the loafers, so he groped VL and delivered a deep kiss to her. Plague looked up at War. "Come on big man, let's go get ready for our match." The two left the room, passing by the Total Eclipse, who was attempting to eat the entire contents of a vending cart. The Chameleon sat on top of him waving instructions to technical crews in a harsh, clipped tone. Plague shook his head as the cameras returned to the ring... Primetime (c) vs. Joy Joy came to the ring with Glee, tossing out candy and balloons to the crowd as he went. Joy asked the crowd if they thought the French were meanies enough to be the team he faced at Mob Rules. The crowd responded by tossing heavy objects into the ring. One struck Glee in the head, and she toppled out of the ring. Joy didn't seem to notice, but James Armstrong stood up from the announcing table and told Joy that the French team wouldn't be facing any stupid happy team, but rather the evil Canadians under his guidance. Joy pouted as Primetime came to the ring with the Boston Strangler behind him and the fWo Cruiserweight title strapped around his waist. It was announced that Primetime would get his shot at the Hardcore Championship next week on Warfare, and Primetime walked not to the ring but to the announcers table. Primetime yelled at Armstrong, asking him what in the hell he was talking about. Armstrong looked sheepish, and Primetime rolled into the ring. Once there Joy extended a hand, and asked how things were going with Nikki. Primetime grabbed his arm and twisted it around in an armbar, and then walked to the corner with Joy in tow. Primetime used his agility to climb to the top of the turnbuckle, still holding the arm of Joy. Once there Primetime dove off the turnbuckle in a quick rolling flip, causing Joy's own arm to bend around and strike him in the face. Primetime found this amusing and grabbed Joy's arm again. Yelling, "Don't punch yourself! Don't punch yourself! Don't punch yourself!" Primetime used Joy's arm to clobber him in the head as the happy superstar began to whine and cry. Primetime spat in disgust and kicked at the back of Joy, sending him skittering across the ring. Primetime grabbed the arms again, and drug Joy up and over in a modified European Bow and Arrow submission hold. The referee checked to see if Joy would submit, but Joy was already unconscious. The referee called for the bell, and Primetime rolled from the ring still the fWo Cruiserweight champion. The Strangler walked behind him as the two went back up the entrance ramp to the backstage area. As they walked they passed Steel, RottDogg and Cairn, who began to mock Primetime and the Strangler. The two picked up crates that were sitting backstage and flung them at NXS, dropping them down to the floor with little effort. One of the crates skidded over and bumped the leg of Hellrazor, who looked up in surprise and then anger. Hellrazor began to shout, "Hey! Boston boy! You're nothing but a little French bitch, do you hear me? You want to mess with Hellrazor? I'll take that damn rope o' yours and shove it up your ass, you hear me boy?" The Stranger simply grinned a wide and shark-like grin and stepped forward to meet Hellrazor's challenge, but security were quick on the scene to prevent another situation like they were witness to at the start of the show. Somewhere in the distance however, more shouting could be heard... Result: Primetime by Submission Wrestlers run amuck... Jackhammer was shouting at Crucifix, who looked confused and annoyed at the same time, but looked ready to start fighting as well. Jackhammer tore off his shirt and continued to yell, shouting, "You've turned bad brother! You've turned real bad! What happened to the dream, man? What happened to the man who went out and gave it all for the fans? You're in with them, aren't you? Well brother, you're next!" Crucifix leapt at Jackhammer, but more fWo security guards held him back, trying to restrain the big men from going at it. Jackhammer punched at his head and screamed, again trying to get at Crucifix. Medical staff arrived with some sleeping aids for Jackhammer as Crucifix was half pushed out of the room. Crucifix slammed his fist into some nearby lockers, and shouted for someone to tell him where Grendal was. There was no answer, but down one hallway Crucifix spotted several former fWo wrestlers who were there to pick up royalty checks. Screaming Crucifix grabbed a chair and swung, striking Scott Hart in the head and sending him splattering into the wall. He punched at the Showstopper and Barry Bladerth, knocking them to the ground and reaching for Famine. Security hit the scene as the Texas Kid kicked at Crucifix with Rage and the Dark Angel, all trying to calm the angry giant down. Crucifix lobbed one more punch in the direction of Fury, striking the masked man in the head and knocking him back into a picture of the Ripper, which had been hung in memory of the fallen superstar. Security again pushed at Crucifix to restrain him as the cameras again went to the ring... Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Stone The Flying Frenchie came out waving a French Flag and carrying the fWo Womens title in one hand. The Frenchie grinned at the crowd, and bowed to them despite the loud boos. The Frenchie climbed into the ring and asked for a microphone, which James Armstrong readily gave him. The Frenchie said, "First off... to you Masseur Armstrong... zat is ze first I had heard of ze French Foundation fighting ze rotten Canadians... so don't make your plans zo quickly unless zu have more to offer zan your petty salary. But zat is not why I am here in front of all ze Frenchie's fans. No, tonight zis is about ze Women's Championship. As you know zis title is beneath me, but zis does not mean zat I can just hand zit over like ze piece of trash that Glee proved herself to be made of. Zo I am here tonight to offer another challenge to ze lucky lady in ze fWo, to see if zey are worthy enough to carry this... girlie strap." The Frenchie paused for effect, looking around himself with satisfaction. "Stone? Come to zis ring right now... ze Frenchie commands it!" Stone's music played, and the Dark Rider appeared, looking skeptical at this challenge. She walked to the ring and climbed inside walking up to the Frenchie and putting up her fists to fight. The Frenchie shook his head and laughed. "No, no... silly woman! Zis it not ze fighting challenge at all, zis is ze challenge more designed for... ze weaker, more pathetic sex. Like you!" Stone rushed at the Frenchie with a snarl escaping her lips, but the Frenchie ducked out and held the title up between them. The Frenchie waggled a finger in front of her face. "Non, non... zis is not ze way to be ze champion!" Stone calmed, but continued to seethe in the ring. The Frenchie continued, "Now... your challenge is this, a challenge zat strikes at ze heart of French culture and pride. You are to bake ze Frenchie ze best cake you can! Yes, ze best cake zat you can cook for ze Frenchie. When you bring ze cake down to ze ring I and ze other honorable members of ze French Foundation will taste ze cake, and if it is a satisfactory cake zen you will win ze championship belt. Now, run along little girl, and start ze baking. I will be on Slaughter to taste ze cake you make... so do not make ze Frenchie wait for you!" The Frenchie rolled from the ring quickly as Stone charged him again, yelling and screaming at the French superstar. The Frenchie escaped to the back with the title as Stone shook with anger inside the ring. The cameras went backstage once more... Result: As yet undetermined... Ready for Y2B... "You call this a show?" Bookerman tossed the script on the ground and put a foot on it as if to emphasize this point. Bookerman stood nearly alone backstage, as workers and other fWo personnel walked past him without paying much attention to his shouts. "This show stinks! I, master booker of the world and best writer should be writing the biggest Warfare of the year! Those two dumb writers Frankton and Ralgin couldn't write a good show if it came and bit them on the ass... which it would never do because they stink! I, Y2B am the master booker and writer of the world, and this is how I would have this show end! Hey, Eron!" Eron and Stinky walked by, and gave Bookerman the finger as they went. Bookerman cleared his throat and nodded to the Shocker, who was sitting near Bookerman with an excited look on his face. Bookerman continued, "First I would have all the wrestlers in the fWo be in the ring for a big battle royal. Everyone would be fighting, and that's when the Shocker would come out and beat everyone up. New champion!" Bookerman looked back at the Shocker, who gave him the 'thumbs up' sign. Bookerman continued, "Then I would have Bookerman come to the ring and beat the Shocker up, and take the fWo World Heavyweight title... no... make that all the titles. Yeah, all of them. Then I'd belt all the titles together in a big lump of metal and make a crown. I'd be King Bookerman!" The Shocker, not pleased with this turn of events in Bookerman's plan got up and grabbed Bookerman by the mask. Bookerman swung the clipboard down on the Shocker's head, knocking him out cold. Bookerman smiled, and started to walk away. Thinking better of it, Bookerman went back to the Shocker and took his wallet. He then spotted an attractive dark-haired woman in the distance and whistled appreciatively. Bookerman strolled up to her, and began to massage her back. The woman turned, and Bookerman screamed as Nightshade grinned at him. The cameras returned to the ring as screams of horror were heard throughout the backstage area... Grendal (c) vs. Death The lights sprialed into darkness and then loud fireworks explode as the Dark Rider Death makes his way to the ring. The fans boo and jeer and shout naughty things at him, to which he just shrugs off and taunts them with an evil smile. Death slides into the ring and breaks into a pose, and waits as the fWo Hardcore Champion is about to come out. The arena darkens and "For Whom The Bell Tolls" begins to play. Out came the mysterious Grendal, who looked a little beat up from last week. Grendal walked with a purpose to the ring and paid no attention to the fans. Grendal dropped the title before he brought a table out from underneath the ring. He set it up on the outside, before turning and facing the ring. As he did so, Death sprinted and slid on the mat and baseball slid right into Grendal's face, which knocked him back first into the guardrail. Death then grabbed him and whipped him shoulder first into the ring steps. Grendal clutched at his arm in pain, before Death snagged a chair from somewhere near ringside and took a shot at his arm. Death took a swipe at the head which busted Grendal open, and blood began to flow freely. Death finally rolled him back into the ring. Death whipped him into the turnbuckles with authority, and as Grendal walked out Death scoops him up and tossed him aside with a fall away slam. Death went for a cover, but Grendal kicked out. Death brought Grendal to his feet and applied an armbar, working over Grendal's injured arm. Grendal looked in pain as Death switched from an armbar to a hammerlock and continued to work him over. A few moments went by before Death hammered Grendal in the back repeatedly, punishing Grendal, but Grendal decided to counter with a mule kick. This made Death bend over and clutch his tender nether regions in pain, as Grendal attempted to recover. Grendal turned around and as Death is still bent over, Grendal took him down with a DDT. Grendal rolled him over for a pin, but only a two count. Grendal brought Death to his feet and whipped him to the ropes, as Death hits the ropes Grendal charged at him and clotheslined Death and himself over the top rope through the table on the outside. Both men go crashing through, and the crowd cheered in amazement of what they just saw. Grendal may have hurt himself even more as he's now on the floor clutching his arm, while Death rolled around holding his back. Grendal was to his feet first, but he was still dazed... Death got to his feet as well, and both men charged at each other taking each other down with a simultaneous clothesline. Both men out again, and the referee begins to lay down a ten count. As the referee approached seven, Grendal rolled his arm onto Death for the pin. One, two, but Death grabbed him by the arm and locked on a submission move. Grendal writhed in pain as Death had a maniacal look on his face. Death continued to work on the arm but Grendal broke free with a thumb to the eye. Grendal climbed to his feet and rolled Death into the ring. He whipped Death to the ropes and went for a Samoan Drop, but Death countered into a crucifix for the pin. Kickout at two. Death jumped up to a vertical base and whipped Grendal to the ropes. Grendal reverses it and ducked for a backdrop, but Death stopped and nailed him with a knee to the face, and then a cradle piledriver. Death went for a cover, but Grendal kicked out. Death followed it up with a snapmare, and several kicks to the shoulder blades. He brought Grendal up and went for a suplex, but Grendal blocked it and somehow got Death in position for a tombstone, and hit it. Suddenly, he gripped his head as blood began to trickle from his ears. He then exclaimed "Oh god... it's going to happen tonight..." before bringing Death up for a choke slam. He began to lift Death up, but his arm gave out. He clutched his arm and Death quickly hit the Death Penalty, taking advantage of him. Death went for the cover and became the new Hardcore Champion, marking the second time in 8 days the title changed hands. Death held the title in the air for everyone to see, and left the area. Grendal lay still inside the ring, holding his head and shaking. Crucifix came out of the crowd, security officials still hanging on to his legs as he moved to the ring. Crucifix grabbed the head of Grendal and began to hammer away, but more security arrived to break this up as Warfare went to Matt Loco backstage... Result: Death by Pinfall Interviewing the Blondes... Backstage Wild Thing chased after a wolf, shouting at it to obey. Both of the Blondes laughed and the cameras returned to the ring... Mad Trucker (c) vs. Mitch Wilson "Satisfaction" starts playing, but Mitch Wilson fails to come to the ring. After a minute or so, Matt Panzer announces Mitch Wilson again, and again no one emerges from the ramp. Deciding to give him more time, Matt Panzer announces the Internet Champion, the Mad Trucker, for this non-title match. The Trucker gives a loud "Toot!" for the crowd, waiting patiently for Mitch. Suddenly, the TravisTron comes to life with images from the French Foundation locker room. The Flying Frenchie approaches the camera, and begins to speak. "Monsieur Trucker, I em not so pleased to tell you zat Mitch Wilson is nowhere to be found. But since we'd like to see you get ze beating you so rightfully deserve, would it be alright for us to send a substitute?" Mad Trucker quickly agrees to fight any nancy they've got. Frenchie walks towards the ring, rather unhappy with the situation, and slides in to face the Trucker. The bell rings, and Trucker wastes no time in dominating Frenchie with his powerful arsenal. After several minutes of taking a severe beating, Frenchie turns the tide with an eye gouge, and a low blow, every heel's best friends. He goes to work on Trucker's hamstring with a series of kicks, and eventually gets the big man down on the mat. He stomps away on the back of Trucker's head, before heading up top. As he climbs the turnbuckles, the World Champion Scott Slugger and Ice make their way to ringside.
