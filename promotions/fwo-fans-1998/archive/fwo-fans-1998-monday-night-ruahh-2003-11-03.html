Ice followed him - and Slugger suddenly reached forward and grabbed Ice by the seam of his pants, pulling him into the corner where he hit the turnbuckles. Slugger then rolled out towards his corner and the tag - But Ice grabbed him from behind and yanked him into the middle of the ring. Ice lifted Slugger up to his feet and went behind him, then prepared to apply a hold he rarely if ever busted out - the chicken wing submission. Ice lifted Slugger up off the mat to apply the hold, pulling back with incredible pressure on the arms, especially the injured right one. Ice paraded around the ring with Slugger, finally reaching Slugger and Jacobs' corner, holding Slugger just out of reach of a tag - Or so he thought. Slugger had been saving his energy for this one moment, and he used all his energy to try to pull an arm free. He succeeded, and drove his body forward, stretching out his arm. "Gonna Fly Now" hit the speakers. The fans exploded, their patience finally paying off. The Cult Icon, Jon Crisp. Check that. The Wrestler of the Year, Jon Crisp. Nice ring to it. Crisp did something he was never accustomed to: he soaked in the cheers. He slowly walked down to ringside, wearing a sweatsuit. He was not here to wrestle on this evening. He was too banged up for that. Rather, tonight, he was here to... "Hi!" Crisp said, giving the fans more reason to cheer. They started their ICON chant, one that has become quite a custom in recent shows. He let THAT soak in, too. Tonight was HIS night. "Friday night, Legends...I took on two men worthy of winning the prestigious Wrestler of the Year title. I took on Deacon, who pushed me to my limit. And I took on Kellen Kinkade, who took advantage of what Deacon had started earlier in the evening. They pushed me to limits I didn't think were possible...or, at least, I hadn't seen in quite a long time. And, in the end, I stand before you. YOUR Wrestler of the Year. YOUR Hero. YOUR Icon." There will be WRESTLING...and damn fine wrestling at that. People you never thought could have five-star matches will have them. I will not do what my predecessors did to me. I will not ignore them. I will own the top two belts in the company...the United States Title, and, of course... ...the Heavyweight Title. I will own them...at once."
