fWo — Card

FlashBack, 18 October 1999

18 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction One night... in a small town in the Pacific Northwest of this country. Many didn't see it coming. With Mob Rules just over two weeks away the fWo has been preoccupied with the Tag Title Tournament and the formation of four-man teams for the elimination event. But ominous winds are blowing in the fWo. Two title changes in two nights. The introduction of an unstoppable monster, and the return of several fWo stars. Dark storm clouds loom over the fWo, and on Warfare the storm will finally break. On Wednesday Warfare everything you thought you knew about the order of wrestling will change. This is the biggest Warfare for the year, and the repercussions of what will happen in 48 hours will be felt for months to come. Four title matches. One night only. FlashBack Introduction With the promo for Wednesday Warfare played, fWo cameras turned their attention to the fWo studios, where Chris Finn sat wearing an fWo shirt behind a giant poster for Mob Rules. Chris Finn smiled, and addressed the fans with an excited tone in his voice. "Fans, welcome to fWo FlashBack during what has become a very interesting time in the fWo. You heard the announcement from the commissioner on Slaughter, he is promising a monumental Wednesday Warfare this coming Wednesday from Washington. Folks, the internet is on fire with rumors of what the card could contain that has the fWo. Some are saying that we could see a big return of a huge star and others are saying that we will see several title changes. Folks, four titles are on the line at Wednesday Warfare that we know of. Two tournament matches, and the card is changing and evolving as we speak. That's forty eight hours away, and we've got some big material for you here tonight as well. We've got a handful of interviews, we've got a classic fWo match, some fWo rankings, and much, much more. But right now I'd like to announce..." Suddenly Machina's music played in the small studio, and Chris Finn looked around himself in confusion. A door swung open and Machina entered, wearing a camouflage jacket, hat, and holding an empty six pack of beers. Machina walked up to Chris Finn and looked him dead in the eyes, shaking his head in disgust. Chris Finn started to speak, but Machina cut him off and said, "You run your little mouth about Wednesday Warfare and making your little guesses as to why the show will be the biggest, well I'll tell you why it's the biggest and that's because Machina is going to win the title from that little baseball boy and that's all I got to say about that! Now you want to antagonize Machina right here in this room well I just have to say UH UH that ain't gonna happen!" With that Machina grabbed Chris Finn by the neck and started to deliver a reverse DDT, but then Machina seemed to waver, and fell backward, releasing Finn as he went. Laying on the floor on his back, Machina murmured, "Oh... should have saved the beer for after the interview..." Machina passed out as Chris Finn regained his composure and told the production crew to go to a commercial break... Nutcracker The commercial opened on a cozy looking living room, decorated for Christmas time. All was well, and some holiday music played in the background. Several small green army men walked across the living room floor, armed with matches, a rope, and a firecracker. The army men moved in tight formation toward a wooden solider, and looked as if they were about to launch some sort of assault on it. Just as they were about to swing into action, Joy bounded into the room, knocking the green army men off the table where they stood in his bounding. Looking around at the trees and presents with his eyes wide, Joy began to giggle with delight. Joy continued to turn, seeing presents in every direction. His eyes grew wider and wider until he finally passed out with an overload of pleasure, a wet stain marking his trousers. The wooden statue looked wisely at this, saying only "Video games suck." Ultra Cool vs. Scott Slugger, Flying Frenchie & Primetime Classic fWo this week was brought to the fans by "KATBD, 'Kill the Taco Bell Dog'. This classic fWo match featured one half of the double main event at last year's Mob Rules Pay-Per-View, originally airing on September 25, 1999. The match featured the survivors of the previous elimination matches that aired earlier in the evening. On one team were Ultra Cool, the team of Ultra Violet and Scotty Cool. This team had yet to capture the fWo Tag Titles, and were still relatively untested inside the ring. On the other side were the team of the Flying Frenchie, Primetime, and Scott Slugger. It was largely because of this match that the Frenchie later sought out Primetime to be his partner in the French Foundation, and it was also a match that helped stoke the fire between Scott Slugger and the Flying Frenchie's rivalry. All five men wanted the win, because the winner would go on to wrestle the fWo World Champion for the title. Ultra Cool were tired, having just come from their match, but it was the other team that would taste the first elimination when the Falcon entered the ring and attacked the Frenchie with a steel chair to the skull, fracturing the head of the French superstar and causing his beret to fall off. The Frenchie fell, and Ultra Violet fell on top of him for the pin. From there Primetime and Scott Slugger cut the ring in half, isolating Ultra Violet and slowly wearing the former Cruiserweight champion down. Scott Slugger climbed to the top rope as Primetime held Ultra Violet down, and executed a perfect splash from up high, and rolled up Ultra Violet for the pinfall. A rested and anxious Scotty Cool ran in and took down Scott Slugger with a lariat, who made a hot tag to Primetime. Scotty Cool picked up Scott Slugger for a running powerslam, but Primetime attacked the leg with a spear. Primetime continued to hammer away on Scotty Cool, who hit upward with a shot to the jaw and rolled Primetime up for a pin. Scott Slugger came in and at once stomped away on Scotty Cool's head, opening him up and causing Scotty Cool to yell in pain. Scott Slugger went for a DDT, but Cool blocked it and hit the Scotty Dropper out of nowhere, getting the quick pinfall. Cool stood tiredly inside the ring, ready to face then champion Jim Dudley for the fWo World Heavyweight title one week later. Scotty Cool didn't win that match, and wouldn't win the championship until the next year. This match was a cornerstone in all five men's careers however, and this year's Mob Rules will likely be no exception. Result: Scotty Cool Classic fWo comments Classic fWo ended and the cameras returned to the fWo studio where Chris Finn was sitting with the winner of the 1998 Mob Rules event, Scott Collins, Death. Chris Finn looked nervously over at Collins, who sat smiling in his dark leather suit. Finn cleared his throat, and said, "So... Mr. Collins, thank you for being with us today..." Collins looked over at Finn and smiled, and said, "Thank you Mr. Finn. I'm glad to be here." Death grinned wider, showing plenty of teeth. Finn nervously cleared his throat again, and started to ask Collins a question. Collins instead grabbed the microphone out of his hands and shoved him hard into a set of monitors to the left, and began screaming into the microphone. "Are you watching this Slugger? I kicked your ass back then and I'll do it again you little bitch... I'll do it again! That was a match for ME that night Slugger, a 'W' in my column and a 'L' in yours! And yeah, you may have a few more wins that me right now, but I'll be the first one to 75 Slugger! Me! You can't beat my record, because I fight dirty... and I've got a woman with me! You hear that? A woman!" Collins calmed down for a moment, and seemed to remember something. He started talking again, this time more calmly. "Now as for Plague and War... well you will get what you're owed on Warfare, and I'd tell you exactly what will happen, but it just so happens that I've got to buy some flowers for Stone to make her happy. But I'll see you both on Warfare." Death stood up, looking over at the crumpled form of Chris Finn. Death turned back to the cameras, smirking. "Better go to a commercial." It's about the beer The commercial showed a desolate and deserted terrain, with harsh conditions obvious. No signs of life could be seen, save a pair of vultures circling overhead. A semi truck drove into the area, and Black Quicksilver stepped out of the cab. Black Quicksilver looked around himself, and then nodded his head. He opened the truck, taking out a single bottle of beer. He opened it, and poured it out onto the dirt. A magical thing happened Primetime Interview Armstrong: Hello fans, and welcome back to Flashback. My guest at this time is the winner of the second annual Meltdown... Primetime: *interrupts* and the man who retired Wolf Fang Armstrong: Primeti.... Primetime *interrupting again* Don't forget the future fWo World Champion. Armstrong: Primetime.....Primetime, glad to have you here today on Ken Armstrong's dojo. Primetime: The pleasure is all yours. Armstrong: Anyway, let's start out hot and heavy. Primetime: What the hell does that mean? Armstrong: Nevermind...anyway, recently on fWo television we have seen some dissention within the ranks of the Foundation...would you care to comment on that? Primetime: Listen, Brawler boy, last I checked you aren't a Foundation member. Last I checked, none of those peons at home are Foundation members. What is going on within the ranks of the Foundation is *strictly* Foundation business. Armstrong: I'm sure you've heard the comments that the Black Quicksilver and Havok have made regarding the Cruiserweight championship.... Primetime: Yeah...I have. They both want to go one on one with the.....oh wait, that's somebody else's material....damn that guy's gotta get some new catchphrases. If that guy says something about cooking and smell or something like that, I might have to go down to Stamford and personally kick his ass.....anyway, I'm off subject. BQ, you claim to say that the Cruiserweight Title has lost prestige since I've had it. Quite frankly, that's a bunch of crap. HaVoK....the millenium man? I hear Mr. Jericho wants his royalty checks..that's Chris Jericho c/o World Wrestling Federation Entertainment. Since you both want a piece of a real *wrestler* I've got a little suggestion. BQ, Havok, and Primetime...2 weeks from now. Wednesday Warfare. Crusierweight Championship. Speak up, boys. Armstrong: Speaking of the Cruiserweight championship, this Wednesday on Warfare, you defend that championship against Joy. Thoughts? Primetime: I recollect a few months back beating Joy match after damn match, and beating him and Glee with Nikki the Valley Whore. So, the way I see it, he can take his happy face, lube it up real nice, turn it sideways...and...damnit I'm doing it again! I'll just kick Joy's happy ass...it's that simple. Armstrong: Well, I hear that we are running a little short on time...however, I'd like to cover this last thing. Last week, on the dojo, Scott Slugger laid out a challenge to the French Foundation and the Strangler, would you like to comment on that? Primetime: Here's a comment: I always thought Slugger was an idiot. Challenging the Foundation and the Strangler to a 4 on 2 match just proves it. If you and that big block of Ice want to fight *us* in a 4 on 2 match, so be it. However, I feel that you, my friend have an ulterior motive. You don't want to face Primetime one on one, face to face. You fear Primetime. So, by throwing 4 on 2 you want to get somebody else in the ring with you, and not Primetime. 4 on 2...you want it? You got it. Armstrong: Well, that's all the time we have...any final thoughts? Primetime: fWo fans....you are looking at the next fWo World Champion...and That's the Bottom line cause Primetime said....damnit! Those silly, stale catchphrases! Amrstrong: Folks, I'll be back next week with the Black Panther, winner of the Wrestler of the Year tournament about his future fWo plans. fWo Top Twenty The cameras returned to the studio where Matt Loco was sitting looking down at Chris Finn, who was nursing a head injury. Matt Loco smiled, and said, "Folks, it's time again for Matt Loco's fWo rankings. We have seen a lot of movement on the chart this week, and let's remind you of what we had last week." Loco held up a chart, which read: 1. Scott Slugger (c) 2. Machina 3. Black Panther 4. Primetime 5. Mad Trucker (c) 6. Death 7. Chris Universal 8. Flying Frenchie 9. Mike Bear 10. Eron (c) 11. Boston Strangler 12. Jackhammer 13. Cactus Jon 14. Courage 15. Crucifix (c) 16. Ultra Violet 17. Ice 18. Lance Star 19. Rain 20. Black Quicksilver Matt Loco put the chart down and continued. "Now the way this chart works is that every wrestler who won a match moves up three notches on the chart. A loss moves the wrestler down three notches. If the wrestler wasn't on the chart, they start at number 20. If the wrestler didn't wrestle, they move down one notch. At the end the numbers are adjusted to make a twenty count again. With that in mind, this is what we have for you now." Loco held up a new chart, which read: 1. Primetime 2. Machina 3. Scott Slugger (c) 4. Black Panther 5. Chris Universal 6. Flying Frenchie 7. Mike Bear 8. Mad Trucker (c) 9. Cactus Jon 10. Death 11. Courage (c) 12. Boston Strangler 13. Eron 14. Jackhammer 15. Lance Star 16. Ultra Violet 17. Crucifix 18. Rain 19. Ice 20. Black Quicksilver over: Kid Cool, Wretch, Maliboo, Jake, Grendal (c), Plague, Stevie Roberts The cameras returned to Matt Loco, who put down the chart. "That's it for this week, I'll see you next week with an updated chart." Ending The cameras returned to Chris Finn, who rubbed his neck and looked as if in great pain. "Folks, that just about does it for this edition of FlashBack. Next week we are going to bring you an interview with the Black Panther, winner of the Wrestler of the Year Tournament. As if that wasn't enough, we will be bringing you another Classic fWo match, this one the second match in the double main event of Meltdown. Matt Loco will be back with the updated chart... and something new to FlashBack. We will start presenting some of the dark matches from Warfare and Slaughter, two matches on the show featuring some of the hot new talent of the fWo that you usually are not able to see on fWo television due to time constraints. Folks, make sure you tune into what many are calling the biggest Wednesday Warfare of the year this Wednesday night, and come back for FlashBack next week for all of that and much, much more." With that the show ended... or so we thought... Ominous words The screen went black and smoke filled the screen. Slowly, red words appeared on the screen reading only, "With smoke comes fire". The words faded out, as FlashBack ended for real...
