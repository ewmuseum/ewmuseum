Kinkade stumbled back, and the bell was rung. Outside the ring, Scott Slugger smiled, an unexpected twist in his favor having occurred. Creed grabbed Kinkade and whipped him across the ring into the ropes. Kinkade came off, and Creed took him up and over with a big powerslam. Creed brought Kinkade back up to his feet and shoved him into the corner, then quickly began to unload with a series of shoulders to the gut. At the second, Slugger saw his chance. He slid into the ring and snuck up behind Creed. On the fifth shoulder, Slugger dropped behind Creed and schoolboyed him to the mat. One. Two. Creed kicked out with authority, sending Slugger bouncing backwards and into the ropes. Slugger came off, and Creed, still on his back, stabbed out his feet and kicked him in the gut. Slugger dropped to a knee, and Creed got up. He grabbed Slugger by the head, setting him up for a suplex. As he did, Kellen Kinkade blasted him from behind with a forearm shiver, causing Creed to lose his grip. Kinkade quickly hooked Creed up and took him to the mat with a back suplex. With Creed down, Kinkade came off the ropes and dropped a knee to the throat. He then turned his attention to Scott Slugger, who was back up. Kinkade hit him with a series of punches, staggering him, then lifted him up suplex-style. He dropped Slugger towards the mat, head-first, turning the suplex into a brainbuster. Kinkade quickly floated over into a cover. It got two before Slugger kicked out. Kinkade wasted no time in bringing him back up to his feet. Kinkade set to whip Slugger into the ropes, but Slugger reversed, sending Kinkade across instead. Slugger dropped down as Kinkade came off the ropes, and Kinkade hopped over him - directly into a thunderous clothesline from Creed. Kinkade dropped to the mat as if he'd been shot. Creed got up, but any opportunity for him to celebrate was short-lived, as Scott Slugger ran towards him and hit him with a lariat of his own, one that hit with such impact that it knocked Creed backward and up and over the top rope to the floor. Without missing a beat, Slugger hopped onto the second rope and flipped off, back towards Kinkade. He connected perfectly with an Asai moonsault, nailing Kinkade squarely, and hooked the leg. Kinkade kicked out at two. Slugger brought him up and whipped him into the corner, then charged in, following him, and leapt into the air with a corner splash that connected fully. Kinkade fell from the corner to the mat, and Slugger mounted the second turnbuckle. He then came off and drove the point of his elbow into the throat of Kellen Kinkade. Before Slugger could cover, Vic Creed grabbed him from the outside of the ring, tripping him. Slugger fell face-first to the mat, and Creed dragged him to the outside. Before Slugger could fight back, Creed grabbed him and posted him, knocking Slugger down and, for the moment, out. Creed quickly rolled back into the ring and brought Kinkade to his feet. Creed lifted up Kinkade above his head - no small feat, with Kinkade being a large man himself - then dropped him to the mat, where he splashed down face-first. Creed made the cover, but Kinkade kicked out after a two-count. Wasting no time, Creed brought Kinkade up again and went behind him, hooking him up for a german suplex, presumably with a release. Before he could execute the move, Kinkade countered with a mule kick to the gonads, which, whether or not they may be shriveled for steroidal reasons, still cause Creed enough pain to force him release the move. Kinkade turned and went behind Creed, then applied a Cobra Clutch, looking to wear the big man down and, perhaps, turn it into a DDT. Kinkade held on to the move, wearing Creed down. Whatever Kinkade hoped for, however, it was not meant to be, as Scott Slugger slingshotted himself onto the top rope, then came off with a dropkick that connected squarely with the back of Kinkade's head, causing him to release the hold. Slugger quickly grabbed Kinkade and threw him to the outside of the ring. With Creed still struggling to take in oxygen after having it choked out of him, he was dazed, and Slugger was prepared to take full advantage. Not wasting any time, Slugger approached Creed as he got back up to a knee. Slugger grabbed Creed by the back of the head and used all of his strength to lift him up, then drove him to the mat with the Perfect Game. Not done, Slugger brought Creed right back to his feet and stood him up, still dazed, in the middle of the ring. Slugger bounced off the ropes, leapt, and double-underhooked Creed's head. Curveball. Slugger drove Creed to the mat, and the fans held their breath. It appeared as if a new man would be heading to CyberSlam. Cover. One. Two. Thre - No. Kellen Kinkade grabbed the referee by the leg and pulled him from the ring. The referee immediately yelled at Kinkade, threatening to DQ him, but backed down when Kinkade stared him down. Slugger, agog at being screwed again, leaned over the top rope and shouted down at Kinkade. Kinkade just smiled back at him. Why? Well, Vic Creed was a powerful man... powerful enough to recover quickly from even the most devastating moves. He had crawled behind Slugger, and immediately nailed him with a low blow. Slugger dropped to the ground and rolled out to the outside, dazed and in pain. Creed was still on his knees, and Kinkade reached into the ring and blasted him in the face with a forearm, knocking him back. Kinkade rolled back in and grabbed Creed, bringing him back to his feet. Kinkade crouched and waited for Creed to get up to his feet. When Creed was up, Kinkade blasted forward out of his stance and charged madly towards Creed. The Killing Spree. Creed was down, and Kinkade quickly made the cover, sure that, after a Perfect Game, Curveball, and Killing Spree, Creed had the proverbial fork sticking out of his back. The referee made the count. One. Two. Thre - No. Scott Slugger reversed Kellen Kinkade's actions, pulling the referee from the ring himself. The ref didn't bother to argue, but simply sighed and rolled his eyes. Kinkade was getting to his feet, and Slugger slid back into the ring and began to trade punches with him. Right from Slugger. Left from Kinkade. Left from Slugger. Right from Kinkade. Slugger gained the advantage, and knocked Kinkade back towards the ropes - And from behind, Vic Creed grabbed Scott Slugger. Lifted him up in a gorilla press. Dropped him to his shoulder and drove him to the mat with the Powerdriver. Slugger was down, but Creed knew he wasn't done. Kellen Kinkade ran at him, but Creed effortlessly ducked the attempted clothesline. When Kinkade turned, Creed kicked him in the gut, doubling him over. He then lifted him up. Hangman's DDT. Cover. One. Two. Three. Just like that, Vic Creed had pulled off an impressive victory of two of the fWo's top stars, and cemented his claim as the man who deserved to be going to CyberSlam. Creed's arm was raised by the referee, and he raised his other in celebration. Times were good for the Main Man. Slaughter, meanwhile, rolled on... Result: Vic Creed by pinfall
