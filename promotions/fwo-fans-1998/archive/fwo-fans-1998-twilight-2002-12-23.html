Introduction It is an event that closes the year and sets the stage for Countdown. Fueds are resolved, enemies come together for what might be the last time, and epic battles are waged between hated rivals. fWo Twilight has traditionally been the final Pay Per View of the year, spanning five years over a long and sorded fWo history. The fans Wrestling organization's final Pay Per View of the year, Twilight has been a Pay Per View with many memorable moments. From Primetime and Flying Frenchie's war that culminated into a Retirement Match for the World Title to Austin Davis joining forces with Chris Universal to defeat 3vil, only to turn his back and attempt to destroy fWo once again, this Pay Per View is one that should not be missed. With Countdown, three shows that often change the face of professional wrestling looming just days away, Twilight is sure to shake the status quo in ways few can expect.
