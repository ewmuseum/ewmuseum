Sunday Slaughter, 7 April 2002

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction A buzz courses through the crowd. Excitement has built over the last 45 minutes as the Molson Centre has filled nearly to capacity. The ring announcer has just told the live audience that they have one minute until another historic fWo Sunday Night Slaughter goes on the air! The rabid fans stand on their feet with their signs held high making noise for the passing camera as the Fox Channel shows a live commercial for the upcoming evening. The fans know they could be witnessing one of the most awesome spectacles in wrestling as they are here and only two weeks removed from Cyber Slam V! Two weeks away from ???????? As the camera clicks on the wrestling fanatics in attendance are shocked by the cold open to the show as no pyrotechnics go off and Brand Frontier simply steps from the entrance way. A stunned silence greets him at first until the entirety of the arena, almost in unison; realize that The Paragon has made his presence known. They pop and they pop big! Frontier looks out to the masses as he paces a bit. Walking to the edge of the arena he raises a closed fist into the air and beyond reason the crowd grows louder. Brand reaches behind him and pulls a microphone from the band of his trunks and knowing something must be up the crowd starts to quiet down. Hesitantly Brand raises the microphone to speak. He seems to be gathering his thoughts or is mildly distracted. A look of anger washes over his face but that soon passes. It seems as if he is trying his damn-dest to keep himself in check. "All hail The Paragon!" The usual loud pop from the crowd is followed by a sustained Par-a-gon chant. As he talks over the chant it begins to subside so he continues, finally being able to be heard over the noise. "...and Frontier is here tonight for one reason. That reason has a name of course. That reason is more or less a human being. That reason is of course the hardcore legend Crucifix!" A chorus of boos halts Brand from continuing. As it dies down he continues. "It's getting mighty old that every week I try to come out and do what I am paid to do, wrestle, and have to fend off maniacs like the mad luchador instead. It pisses me off that I was minding my own business and for whatever twisted reason Crucifix thinks that I will be the message bearer for his new reign of terror. Well, I'm sorry to say but the only thing I will be a message bearer for is the fact you shouldn't do certain things in life. See, I don't care if you pull on Superman's cape and I don't wear a mask like the Lone Ranger. I could even care less if you spit in the wind, but one thing is for certain Crucifix... you don't [bleep] with Brand Frontier!" The crowd erupts in favor of what the Paragon is saying. Not paying attention to them as he normally does he continues on. It seems that the events of recent weeks has lit something in him that most have not seen before. "I can stand out here all night and insult the Hardcore Luchador or I can do what I normally do and make my point in the ring. You see, I don't have to jump people from behind or use weapons to do the job. I am the Paragon and quite simply that is more than enough. Crucifix, you masked bastard come out later tonight and meet me in this...very...ring. Leave your little barbwire wrapped ass in the back if you are as much talk as I suspect you are. Or, come out here and wrestle me. Now before you get to rolling yourself up in tar and razorblades or whatever notice what I said. I said... wrestle." "I really don't think you can do it. You hide behind this hardcore style because you couldn't handle it straight up. Tonight, I'm going to prove that. The challenge is here Crucifix, I have a lot of frustrations to get out and you seem like the perfect punching bag. You want to send a message tonight's the night for you to do it..." The arena speakers squealed and sputtered as Brand tossed the microphone to the edge of the ring. Brand leaned over the top rope and let the momentum of his body carry him over. As he stomped up the ramp way he mainly ignored the fans. Now consumed with whatever demons he had in him it showed on his face. Most anyone who looked him in the eyes could see as the show faded to it's first commercial break that it was just beginning. Tempest (c) vs. Triple X Slaughter heads to the ring for a Cruiserweight Title match, the latest in a series of defenses by the current champion Tempest. "Renegade" begins to play, and the crowd boos as Triple X, XXXstasy, Sean Stevens makes his way to the ring, looking cocky (of course). Triple X rolls his eyes at the crowd, then climbs up the ringsteps and steps inside the ring, clearly ready for this big matchup. "Battleflag" plays, and the boos become loud cheers as fWo Cruiserweight Champion Tempest makes her way to the ring, belt strapped around her waist. She slaps the hands of the fans as she goes, then rolls into the ring and looks across at her opponent, who favors her with a cocky glare. Tempest unstraps the Cruiserweight title belt and hands it off to the referee, who raises it into the air to signal that it is on the line. He then passes it along to a ring attendant, who takes it to the timekeeper. Tempest and Triple X begin to circle, and the ref calls for the bell, signaling the beginning of the match. They lock up, and Triple X uses his strength (and over hundred-pound) advantage to shove Tempest into the corner. He charges in after her, but she moves and he slams into the turnbuckles. He steps out and Tempest locks up with him again, then goes behind and applies a hammerlock. Triple X reverses into a hammerlock of his own, but Tempest counters it herself. Triple X heads to the ropes to break it. Tempest steps back, and Triple X tries to take advantage by bursting up and out with a clothesline, but Tempest ducks it. As Triple X turns, she leaps and nails him with a dropkick, hitting him squarely in the chin. Triple X is knocked to the mat, and Tempest takes advantage by running into the ropes and executing an asai moonsault that nails Triple X perfectly, hitting him across the chest. Tempest reaches forward and hooks a leg, but Triple X manages to kick out at two. Tempest wastes no time in bringing Triple X back up and whipping him into the corner. She runs into the corner as well, following him, then climbs up his chest and flips back towards the center of the ring. She runs back into the corner, but Triple X explodes outwards, blasting Tempest with a clothesline that sends her for a flip. Triple X measures her, then drives the point of the elbow into the throat. He drapes himself across her for a cover, but Tempest quickly kicks out. Triple X brings Tempest to her feet and hooks her up, then drives her into the mat with a snap suplex. With Tempest down, Triple X backs into the corner, then runs out, rolls on the mat, and leaps up, coming down with a splash that hits squarely. Triple X hooks the leg and makes the cover, but Tempest manages to get a shoulder up at two. Triple X brings Tempest up again and throws her over the top rope to the outside, where she lands hard. Triple X taunts the crowd for a few moments, then waits for Tempest to get back up to her feet. As she does, he takes a few steps back, looking to give himself room to gain momentum when he runs. He then rushes forward. Triple X leaps over the top rope to the outside with a plancha, sending himself flying like a missile at Tempest. Tempest sees him coming and has enough composure to dodge out of the way, rolling to one side. Triple X's momentum carries him all the way to the guardrail, and he slams chin-and-jaw-first into it, jarring himself and knocking himself hard back towards the apron of the ring. With Triple X down after taking one risk too many, Tempest gets up and shakes off the proverbial cobwebs. She heads over to Triple X and brings him up, then rolls him back into the ring. She brings him up and pushes him back into a corner, then begins to attack with a series of martial arts kicks, pounding Triple X. She then hooks him by the head and signals to the crowd that she's about to execute a bulldog. Tempest runs out of the corner with Triple X's head in her grip, then leaps and drives him face-first to the mat with a good old-fashioned rodeo bulldog, leaving Triple X dazed and confused. Tempest brings him back up and hooks him by the head, preparing to hit a DDT. Triple X, however, fights out of it with a series of fists to the midsection, causing Tempest to lose her grip. He then lets fly with a superkick, aka the Factor-X. Tempest, however, ducks and Triple X's effort is all for naught. Tempest immediately grabs him by the head and runs into the corner, then comes off with the Rottweiler, driving Triple X into the mat with the devastating finisher to a big pop. Tempest immediately makes the cover, hooking the leg. One. Two. Three. Tempest secures the victory, once more successfully defending her Cruiserweight Title, and her hand is raised as the crowd cheers. The title belt is handed to her once more as Slaughter heads backstage... Result: Tempest by pinfall Caught The fWo cameraman slowly pushed the door open. It was already open a crack, and in the fWo, that was as good as a sign on the door that said "hey, asshole, open this door." The room was dark, but the cameraman could hear the sound of breathing. He cautiously turned his camera around the corner. Grendel was standing in front of a desk with his fingernails and them only soaking in a jar on the desk. This could be seen because the jar was filled with a green liquid which was glowing enough to slightly illuminate the scene. The cameraman jerked his camera back in surprise. Inside the room, Grendel jerked his head up at the noise. He slowly removed his fingernails from the liquid and walked out towards the corner. He looked around. Nothing. Grendel grunted, then stomped out of the room, presumably heading for the ring. Around a corner in the hallway, the head of the cameraman peeked out. Sometimes, this job really sucked. Lance Knight vs. Grendel The fans at the Molson Centre watched the video display over the entrance way as "Elite" by the Deftones hit the PA system. A good number of fans in attendance cheer and shouted "Knight! Knight! Knight!" as Lance Knight stepped out from behind the curtains. He stared around at fans in audience, and smiled to himself as he knew the cheers grew when he exited from backstage. "Nice crowd here in Montreal..." said Lance to himself as he made his way down the rampway and slapped some fans hands. He body still ached from the injuries inflicted in the last few matches against Grendel, but he knew this would be the time he would have to make a stand against the man-beast. As the final notes of "Elite" played, Lance climbed up the apron, and entered the ring. As soon as Lance entered the ring, "Elite" was replaced by "Everything's Cool" by Pop Will Eat Itself. The fans began to give a smattering of boos as Grendel the Living Nightmare stepped out from behind he curtains. His huge frame and disturbing appearance frightened many of the younger fans as he stepped down the rampway, ignoring the boos from the crowd. The former Tornado Wrestling World Champion paused, and eyed his opponent for several seconds, making sure he wouldn't attack him when he entered. When the referee ordered Lance away from Grendel while he entered, Grendel smiled to himself. Grendel entered the ring, and then the referee moved away, signaling the start of the matchup. Lance and Grendel circled one another for several seconds, waiting for the other to open up. Tired of waiting, Grendel suddenly locked up on Lance, and hip tossed him to the mat. Grendel then lifted up a temporarily stunned Lance Knight, and throat chopped him hard. Lance stumbled back as the fans booed Grendel on. Grendel continued his barrage of hard throat chops, sending Lance stumbling back in surprise and shock. When Lance was at the turnbuckle, Grendel grabbed Lance, and whipped him towards the other turnbuckle. However, Lance managed to reverse the whip, sending Grendel instead into the turnbuckle on the other end. When Grendel crashed into the turnbuckle, the fans exploded with cheers. As soon as Grendel stumbled out backwards from the turnbuckle, Lance was ready, and grabbed Grendel's head. Lance then drove Grendel's head into the mat with a reverse DDT. Lance then rolled the massive Grendel over, and wrapped his arms around Grendel's throat, locking on a Kata ha jime, known in the professional wrestling industry as a 'Tazmission'. Lance sneered as the fans cheered him on. Lance wretched the Kata ha jime tighter, making Grendel lose conciousness slowly. The referee checked on Grendel, who was still fighting the dangerous submission hold as the fans watch Grendel in amazement. Lance tightened the hold, and after several seconds, Grendel went limp. The referee lifted Grendel's hand once, and it fell. The fans began cheering as the referee lifted the hand for a second time, and it fell. However, the third time the hand stayed up, and the fans began to boo as Lance realized what was going to happen. Grendel smiled as he began elbowing Lance in the gut several times, making him break the hold, and allowing him to get up. While Lance got up, Grendel lashed out with a cheap low blow. Lance keeled over, and Grendel grabbed Lance, whipping him into the ropes, when Lance returned, Grendel executed to perfection a Belly to Belly overhead suplex, making Lance crash onto the mat with a hard thud. Some of the fans even became sickened when Lance cursed out in pain, as that move reopened some pains from the last few weeks that he had thought he eliminated. Grendel lifted up Lance, and whipped him into the ropes. When Lance returned, Grendel grabbed Lance by his throat, and executed a one hand chokeslam in one quick and powerful movement. The fans exploded with boos as Grendel smirked down at Lance. Grendel lifted Lance up, and eyed the fans in the arena, before deciding an appropriate way to punish Lance. Grendel lifted Lance into the air, appearing to be executing a Military Press Slam. However, in the last second, Grendel grabbed Lance's neck and legs tight, causing more pain for Lance, before executing a powerful Death Valley Driver to the canvas. Lance was driven into the mat as the fans in attendance booed, and Grendel smiled. Grendel hooked the leg, but only got a two count. Grendel slapped the mat in frustration, before lifting up Lance, and going for a headbutt. However, Lance was ready, and forearmed Grendel in the face. Grendel stumbled back, and Lance grabbed Grendel by his arms, executing a Northern Lights suplex, but only getting a one and a half count. Lance turned to the crowd, being distracted by the fact Grendel had kicked out, and was unsure of what to do. Grendel got up quickly, and raked Lance in the back with his sharp fingers. The fans booed as several long and thin gashes appeared on Lance's back, and Lance collapsed onto his knees. Grendel then kicked Lance hard in the back of the head, making him fall onto the mat. Grendel looked at the booing fans in attendance, and smiling to himself hooked on his submission finisher, Pain. Lance wretched back in pain as the aptly named finished caused him to be blinded with pain. Grendel smiled as he wretched back. However, while Grendel wretched back, he didn't notice he had touched the ropes, forcing him to break the hold. Grendel got off Lance, and began to yell at the ref. While Grendel yelled at the referee, Lance got up from behind Grendel, and yelled at Grendel. As Grendel turned around, Lance scooped Grendel up, and completed the Knightfall, knocking the wind out of Grendel as the fans exploded with cheers. Lance hooked at the leg, and three seconds later, "Elite" cued back up on the PA system as Lance rolled out of the ring. Lance held his neck and back in pain, but smiled to himself, turned around and faced his defeated opponent. "That was for last week, asshole..." said Lance quietly, engulfed by the cheers from the crowd, as he headed backstage, while Grendel stirred to, enraged that he lost the match to Lance Knight. Before he could harm the referee, however, the referee rolled out of the ring. Grendel stared at Lance for several seconds, before rolling out of the ring, and heading to the back on his own free will. Result: Lance Knight by pinfall Payback Kristen Jacobs walked the back hallways of the Molson Centre. She turned around a corner, and saw the individual she wanted to find. 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly. Kelly was walking down the hall, his back to Jacobs. He wore his black 'Say Hello to Block' t-shirt, with his trademark pose and logo on the back of the shirt. He wore faded out Old Navy jeans that fit comfortably around his 6'11", 336 lb. lower frame. Kristen called out to Erik, and he turned while taking a chug on his Poland Spring water bottle. He waved to Kristen and headed into her direction. The two exchanged a brief friendly hug, and Kristen asked if she could have a brief interview with him. Erik nodded, and Kristen began. "Erik, last week on Slaughter, you showed up after Ruben Ross' main event victory over Ice in front of a 68,000+ crowd and hit Ruben Ross with a chair. Why were you out there?" Erik scratched his dirty blond goatee on his chin as he responded. "Kristen, I'm doing what I promised I'd do for Ruben a few weeks back. I'm watching our champ's back. Ice got up, and wasn't going to be a good sport about losing to Ross and his 'Plague of Darkness'. So I went out there to make sure that Ross would be alright. Yeah, I grabbed a chair, and yeah I meant to hit Ice. But I made a mistake. As Montreal Expos' Third Baseman Geoff Blum's hair stylist said, "Oops. My bad!" A mild chuckle could be heard from the Montreal crowd, while Kristen continued. "You didn't seem to be too sorry to hit him. In fact, you looked amused by the whole event." Erik grinned. "Yeah, I admit I got some pleasure out of it. The instant it happened, I immediately thought of BodyCount 2001... January 20th, 2001 in Las Vegas, Nevada. We were fighting it out with Victor Creid, bless his roided heart. I had KVC in my grasp, and Ruben grabbed a chair and swung for the fences. Unfortunately, I learned that day why Ruben is a wrestler and not a baseball player... his aim sucks! Took one square on the chin, had several stitches on my upper lip for 10 days because of him. But... last week, it just happened to be that the roles were reversed, and Ice was the target. So I missed. Hey, even Barry Bonds doesn't hit homers all the time! So Ruben, sorry about messing up that cro-magnon look you've got going there, buddy. It won't happen again. And don't worry... I've still got your back. You're in good hands with 'The Eradicator'." Erik turned to look back to Kristen, while she asked her next question. "Tonight, you and Ice will tag for the first time ever as you face the World Champion and Mufanga Kagos. Your thoughts about this match?" Erik shrugged. "I tell ya, Krissy, I don't know what to make of it. I gotta face Mr. Ego and Kamala, Jr. tonight, and to make matters worse... I've gotta tag with Ice. I tell ya, when I put his ass through a block of ice in 2000, I think I did some permanent damage or something to that boy. Oh, by the way, Vanilla... 1990 just called, they'd like their shitty dance moves back." Erik looked down one of the hallways a moment, his eyes rolled back into his head as he pondered this evening's match. "If Ice starts trying to dance, he'll get Eradicated so hard, he might just be back in style in 2012." Erik took a quick sip of his bottled water while Kristen asked her final question. "Cyberslam is around the corner, on April 27th. What are your thoughts on your main event matchup?" "Krissy, it's simple. Cyberslams haven't been my best time to shine in the past. I've never won one, but I'm not worried about it. This is different now. There's a different focus in me, a different drive in me. I will not stop until I become the undisputed World Heavyweight Champion... until I become 'The Man'... 'The Master'... 'The Ruler of the World'. Tonight's warmup against Ruben is just another chapter in our history. We've brought the house down many times, and we'll do it again. There's just one thing that will be different this time. We're not talking about International Titles...we're not talking about Hardcore Titles...we're talking about the grandest prize in the game, at the biggest pro-wrestling event in the world. At Cyberslam V, Arrowhead Pond in Anaheim, California... in 20 days, Ruben... the end of your reign will take place. A new era is dawning... 'The Eradication Era'. And while I find this a hard pill to swallow...here in Montreal, Quebec Canada... " Brief crowd pop took place while Erik waited for it to subside. "...the Montreal Canadians are the most storied and decorated franchise in NHL history. Like them, Ruben....on April 27th, I begin my story... my history, as the most celebrated... and most dominant World Champion the fWo or any wrestling company has ever seen. Why?" Erik folded his arms over his chest, as he positioned himself in front of Kristen to do his trademark pose. Kristen was no longer in view. "Because I am the Power of the fWo... and there ain't a daaaaaaaaaaamn thing you can do about it!" Erik began moving to his left, away from the camera. He took one last look, and lightly patted Kristen on the shoulder as he walked away from the interview. Kristen watched him leave, as the camera ended the interview and Slaughter went elsewhere. Conversation When everything was going right, when all the performers were on, the crowd was jazzed, and the backstage crew were nigh invisible, diligently doing their jobs so well that you didn't notice them at all, that's when running a wrestling promotion seemed worth it. Tonight was one of those nights, and Chris Universal was grateful for it. He'd finished up the vast majority of his paperwork already, and now all he had to do was deal with the talent, and brainstorm for CyberSlam. Sitting at his desk, stretching a bit, he even has time to speak with an old acquaintance, the trainer who'd provided some young talent for the evening's dark matches. "Bon soir, madamesoille. I want to commend you on tonight's batch. You brought a good group with you, Jasmin." Jasmin Desjardin, standing across the desk from Universal, offers him her hand, which he dutifully kisses. "I'd like to t'ank you for ze opportunity. It's an excellent teaching tool, a chance to show zem what it's really like to perform before a large crowd, rat'er zan some hundred or so people in an armory or rec center. However, zere is anot'er matter I'd like to discuss wit' you, if you could." "Of course. You mentioned something on the phone about Pierre?" Jasmin nods. "Zis is all somewhat embarrassing, but I haven't heard from him in a while, and I haven't seen him on ze broadcasts for ze last few weeks. Is zere some kind of problem?" Universal chuckles. "Aside from the usual problems with Pierre? Not really. He had been quite upset about Body Count and the following Slaughter. He really wanted a win against Quicksilver, but that wasn't the right way to end it." Jasmin nods again, knowing this to be the truth. "Not to mention losing to Wasner just a short time later. It was hell just getting the two of them to agree to be in the ring together again. After that, he said he needed to take some personal time, get some of his affairs in order. I told him to take all the time he wanted. While there will always be room in the fWo for a perfomer like Frenchie, the more of these long time personalities we have off the air just helps us push some of these newer, fresher talents. He made a few appearances for us at event ticket sales and what not through the first half of March. Haven't heard from him since. Which reminds me that I've actually got a question for you. How long has he been married? I've never heard anything about that." "Zat's ze most ridiculous t'ing I've ever heard. He's not married, he never has been." Still, the very suggestion of this seems to rock Jasmin, who wears an incredulous look on her face. "Oh, please, no reason to get upset. Perhaps I misheard him when he said he needed to finish his divorce." "Yes, zat must be it. I apologize for taking your time. I must go find my students now, before zey get into any trouble." "Right. Good seeing you again, and next time we look into roster additions, I'll give you a call." As Jasmin leaves, Chris can't help but feel that he'd just put his foot in his mouth. Hell, wouldn't be the first time. His office empty again, he stretches, waiting for the next emergency. There was always a next emergency. Result: Double Countout Words The lights dimmed a bit in the Molson Centre, as "Nobody's Real" by Powerman 5000 began to play and signaled the impending entrance of "The Golden Child" Monte Burns. The crowd gave a slightly mixed reaction to those sounds, some having grown to respect Burns as a great competitor and a caring guy, others feeling that he certainly had some ulterior motives in his pursuit of Tempest. As Burns made his way out, however, the crowd was surprised to see him walking out wearing khakis and a long sleeve shirt instead of his normal wrestling attire. With his long hair tied into a ponytail, Burns calmly made his way down to the ring and grabbed a microphone from the timekeeper's table. Taking a look at the sea of humanity in the crowd, Burns climbed the stairs to get into the ring and paced back and forth a few moments before lifting the microphone to his lips. "Over the last few weeks, I've tried my best to show you, the fans of the fWo, and Tempest that my feelings towards her were completely real. I put my feelings out there in front of the entire world. I put my reputation on the line to show Tempest that I cared about her," he said while looking down at the mat. Burns slowly lifted his head and then looked back up to the entrance. "But I guess that just wasn't enough. It wasn't enough that I expressed my feelings right after losing a hard-fought match. It wasn't enough that I alienated myself from friends and family. It wasn't enough that I changed a great deal of the way I handle myself." Burns once again paused as the last word left his lips, looking out at the crowd. The fans closest to the ring could see the look in his eye, and if you had asked them their thoughts about Monte's speech, they would have sworn he was telling the truth. "Well, I'm to take one last shot at proving myself to you, Tempest. I'm going to take one last shot at showing you exactly why I feel you're the woman for me and I'm the man for you. Tempest...I'm challenging you for a rematch at CyberSlam...a rematch for the fWo Cruiserweight Championship!" The crowd let out a cheer at that point, obviously realizing what kind of match would be in store for the fans at CyberSlam with a Tempest/Burns rematch.
