fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE FINAL SURVIVOR August 18, 2000 On August 18th, 2000 the first Survivor contest officially drew to an end. Votes from many of the previously voted out wrestlers came in at various points over the past two days, with only a small handful not reporting in. Ruben Ross, the high flying technical African American wrestler was chosen as the final survivor by his peers in a narrow victory over wrestler Reno Davis. Ross, whose catchphrase, "I'm outta here like I stole somethin'" was highly regarded by many of the viewers of the contest and unlike several wrestlers who came before him was not heavily implicated in the infamous alliance. Ross, who voted for many if not all of the wrestlers who elected to vote him in, was said to be elated with the news. The margin of victory for Ruben Ross over Reno Davis was slim: only one vote put Ross over Davis for the victory. With seven of the ten wrestlers electing to vote one way or the other, the final tally was 4 votes for Ruben Ross and 3 votes for Reno Davis. The announcement of Ruben Ross as the final Survivor comes after nearly four weeks of wrestling, interviews, and dramatic twists and turns through the voting process. Several other organizations are already planning similar contests, and the jury is still out as to if there will be another Survivor contest. The fWo wishes to express their deepest thanks to all twelve wrestlers who made it into the Survivor contest, and to the thousand of wrestlers who submitted applications.
