Sunday Slaughter, 2 July 2000

2 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction A coin toss. It's used at the beginning of football games to determine who will have possession of the ball first, thus giving them the first opportunity to score. It's used at the beginning over the all important overtime period as well. Sometimes it's used to determine other trivial matters in every day life, and sometimes it might determine more important matters like who to give a promotion to. Either way, tonight it would play a very big part in the fWo, and the future of the fWo. The fate of the fWo could be decided with the flip of a coin. If it lands the way seemingly everyone wishes it would, Scott Slugger would receive a fWo World Title shot tonight, and undergo the overwhelming task of defeating The Boston Strangler. Doing so would make Scott Slugger a five-time fWo World Champion. Nobody else has won it more than three. Three of the men who did it aren't even active in the fed right now. David Spencer (Death), The Flying Frenchie & Dead. The other two? Well, Cactus Jon is trying to make a big comeback... and Ice let his opportunity slip away with his loss to Slugger a week ago on Slaughter. If the coin however lands on another side, it will be Eron The Relentless who receives his third opportunity in the last eighteen days to win the fWo World Title. If Eron does, it would perhaps be a slap in the face to every other man who has held the title, with exceptions being the two men who did not care for the title other than to simply use it as a tool for bragging rights: Dead & David Spencer. Thirteen men have held the fWo World Title, and if Eron were to become number fourteen some believe it would be the darkest night in the history of the fWo. That night may be upon us. That night... could be tonight. Welcome to Sunday Night Slaughter. Xavier vs. Crusader Pop. Pop. Pop. Those are the sounds we hear as fireworks explode throughout the Five Seasons Center. Sunday Night Slaughter has arrived in full force. With a rabid crowd on hand to watch the action, "Bleed" starts playing and Slaughter heads to the ring for a Next Level tournament quarterfinal match, pitting Xavier against Crusader. Xavier makes his way to the ring first, ignoring the boos of the crowd. He steps over the top rope and into the ring. His theme is replaced by "Switchback" and the fans cheer as Cruiserweight champ Crusader appears on the entrance ramp. Crusader makes his way to the ring, hands his belt to the timekeeper, and catapults himself over the top, soaking in the cheers of the crowd. The bell rings and Crusader and Xavier circle, before Xavier locks up with Crusader. Crusader goes behind and leaps onto Xavier's shoulders, before rolling down into a victory roll. Xavier escapes at one, and both men are quickly up. Crusader leaps up and jacks Xavier's jaw with a dropkick, sending him stumbling into the ropes. Xavier stumbles back out, but manages to catch Crusader's leg on a kick. Undaunted, Crusader leaps up and slams his foot onto the side of Xavier's head with an enzuiguri. Xavier topples to the mat, and Crusader covers. Xavier kicks out, however, at two. Crusader runs into the ropes as Xavier gets up, leaps onto them in an Asai setup, then leaps off, spinning in mid-air and clocking Xavier with an Asai forearm shot as he gets up, knocking him back down. Crusader doesn't cover and instead runs into the corner and leaps up, before dropping back down with a vader bomb style body splash. He hooks a leg, but Xavier escapes at two once more. Crusader takes a few steps back as Xavier gets up, then leaps up and attempts a hurricanrana. The larger, stronger Xavier however still has enough to catch Crusader in mid-air. He holds him in position, before dropping down with a Liger bomb. The ref makes the count, but Crusader lifts a shoulder at two. Undaunted, Xavier brings Crusader up and whips him into the ropes, before picking him up and powerslamming him with a thunderous impact. Xavier gets up and doesn't go for the cover, instead deciding to bring Crusader up as well and whip him into the corner. Xavier then charges in with a clothesline, winds up, and begins repeatedly clotheslining Crusader in the corner. Xavier takes a few steps back, charges in again, and tries for a big boot. Crusader rolls out of the corner however, and Xavier crotches himself on the top rope. Crusader rolls to the apron and sets up as Xavier frees himself from the corner. Crusader then springboards onto the ring ropes and hits Xavier with a springboard Lou Thesz press. Xavier is too big and strong however, and he holds on to Crusader, before swinging him from one arm to the other and planting him with a front slam. Xavier covers, but Crusader escapes at two. Xavier brings Crusader up and lifts him up in a gorilla press position, before pressing him over his head repeatedly. Satisified with his power show, Xavier tosses Crusader down hard. Xavier places a foot on Crusader, sure it's over. Crusader disagrees, kicking out at two. Xavier begins to get angry, and brings Crusader up before wrapping a hand around his throat for a chokeslam. He lifts Crusader up, but Crusader hooks the head of Xavier with his legs and converts the move mid-move into a hurricanrana takeover. Crusader reaches back and hooks the legs, and Xavier only barely kicks out at two. Xavier is up first and blasts Crusader with a lariat, sending him for a flip. He brings Crusader up and lifts him up in a vertical suplex, before holding it. He continues to hold Crusader vertical in the air for nearly half a minute, before deciding it's time to plant Crusader. Crusader again has other ideas and shifts his weight, hooks the head of Xavier on the way down, and blasts him with a high-impact DDT. Crusader is too beaten to make the cover immediately, and it costs him as the delay enables Xavier to escape just before three. Crusader is beginning to get a second wind however, and he runs into the ropes and hits Xavier with a spinning heel kick as he gets up, knocking him back. Crusader then hooks the head of Xavier and lifts the larger man up, before dropping him with a brainbuster suplex. Wanting to put the finishing touches on him, Crusader heads to the top as Xavier drags himself to his feet. Crusader leaps off with a missile dropkick, but Xavier sees him coming and moves to the side, causing Crusader to crash into the mat. Xavier wants to finish Crusader now and not risk another comeback, and he positions Crusader before lifting him up for a powerbomb. Much to Xavier's dismay, however, Crusader drops down, hooks the head of Xavier, spins and plants him with a version of the tornado DDT. Crusader quickly heads to the top rope and measures Xavier, before leaping off with the spectacular Gravity 101. The referee tolls the three-count and Crusader picks up the victory, advancing to the semi-finals of the Next Level tournament. Slaughter continues as Crusader celebrates his win... Result: Crusader by pinfall Arrivals In one portion of the arena entered Eron The Relentless, who walked swiftly. His attire consisted of a leather jacket, an Eron t-shirt, and his wrestling tights. Eron looked more focused than ever, and on a night like tonight, that does not bode well should he be the lucky one chosen to face The Boston Strangler tonight for the fWo World Title. In another entrance, the World's Greatest Athlete, Scott Slugger, entered the arena with an expression of determination etched across his face. He nodded at some of his fellow wrestlers, acknowledging their presence. Several wished him good luck in the coin toss that would be occuring shortly. A motorcycle pulled up near the back door of the Five Seasons Center, and a man who had been champion for nearly six weeks hopped off the bike. He carried his bag into the arena, methodically walking as his long hair blew in the wind. He removed his sunglasses, before placing them elsewhere in his jacket. He continued to walk with purpose as the cameras took to another section of the arena, to find Chris Finn & James Armstrong making their entrance. Finn walked with a hop in his step, clearly looking forward to tonight. He tossed a coin his right hand repeatedly into the air, although lightly, ensuring he would catch it each time it fell. He looked at Armstrong who was all smiles, before loudly boasting "I told you we should have had Wretch at ringside for every match all along!" Finn rolled his eyes, but didn't appear angry or annoyed. Nothing was going to take him out of the mood he was in tonight. Nothing... ...except the realization that Eron could achieve what nobody wanted him to in a few short hours... and that was to become the World Champion of the fans Wrestling organization. Ric Chronos vs. Rain Sunday Slaughter returns from commercials to the sounds of "Christian Says" by Tones on Tail. This song brings out Ric Chronos, former tag team champion for the second round next level tournament match. Then, the lights go out, raindrops begin to fall from the ceiling, and "Symphony of Destruction" by Megadeth plays. As the song plays, the raindrops stop, the lights come back on, and Rain is in the ring, standing behind Chronos. He attacks Chronos with a forearm to the back of the head, which stuns the Man of the Hour. He sends Chronos into the ropes, and catches him in a big sidewalk slam. He covers, quickly, but Chronos gets a shoulder up at two. Rain brings him back up to his feet, and nails a few quick punches, before sending Chronos into the ropes once again. Rain ducks down, looking for a backbody drop, but Chronos meets Rain with a boot to the face, followed by a quick DDT. Chronos jumps on with a cover, but Rain powers out at two. Chronos brings him back to his feet, and sets him up for a vertical suplex. However, Rain slips out of it in the air, and lands behind Chronos. He hooks Chronos' arms in a full nelson position, and then slams him down to the mat. Rain covers, but once again, Chronos kicks out. Rain brings him back to his feet, and hooks him in a chokeslam position, but Chronos nails a boot below the belt, and takes him over with a fisherman's suplex. He bridges, but Rain unhooks the bridge, and kicks out at two. Both men up, and Chronos ducks a Rain short-arm lariat. He stays behind, nails a boot to the gut, and somehow powerbombs Rain to the mat. Chronos jumps on top with a cover, and Rain easily powers out at one. At this point, Mitch Wilson appears on the entrance ramp, and takes a look at the match, doing a little scouting. Chronos, looks astonished that Rain just sat up like that on his powerbomb, tries to kick Rain back to the mat, with a few boots, but Rain just won't stay down. Rain eventually reaches an arm up, and grabs Chronos by the throat, bringing him to the mat with a chokeslam. However, instead of covering, Rain brings Chronos back to his feet. He throws him over his shoulder, and then throws him throat first on top of the top turnbuckle. From there, Chronos blindly walks into a Rain press slam. Rain brings Chronos back to his feet, and then plants him into the mat with a huge powerslam. Rain then brings Chronos up, and plants him, finally, with a Rainmaker. However, he brings Chronos up again. Another Rainmaker, and another time, he brings Chronos up to his feet. He plants Chronos with another Rainmaker, and he picks him up one more time. However, instead of an ordinary Rainmaker, Rain brings him up and gives Chronos the Rainmaker, where the back of his head and neck hit the top turnbuckle. He hits it, and Chronos falls to the mat in a crumpled heap. Rain drags Chronos to the middle of the ring, and places one foot on his chest, as the ref records the three count. Satisfied with the win, the lights go off, and Rain dissappears. Wilson looks at the fallen Chronos, and then continues back into the backstage area. Result: Rain by pinfall Coin Toss Backstage, several wrestlers gathered around as Chris Finn paced back and forth, waiting for the arrival of three men, who were heading to where Finn was located. The Boston Strangler tossed his bag in his dressing room, not caring where it landed, or what resulted because of it smacking the pavement. Eron removed his leather jacket and tossed it into his dressing room, before beginning the long journey to where Finn was located. And finally, the third of the trio, walked promptly from his dressing room not stopping for even a moment to sign an autograph or exchange greetings with some fans. It's not that Scott Slugger was a jerk, it's just that even the most bitter individual must understand this was his day, this was his moment. Number five. Five World Title reigns. Few have ever done it in any promotion and if they have, it's likely a few of their reigns were undeserved. Not Slugger. When the "race" started back in 1998, Scott Slugger paced himself, while others shot right out of the gate to glory. Four of those five men were hardly around anymore... and one didn't look to be back in the fWo World Title picture soon after what occured last Sunday. Slugger not only caught up, he surpassed everyone else, and still remains firmly in the lead. Ironically, it was Slugger who reached Finn first. He greeted Finn, before giving a stern look to a man he most certainly did not have any positive feelings for, Eron. Eron glared at him before simply standing by and waiting, and finally, the champion arrived. He exchanged pleasantries with Finn & Slugger, but didn't say a word to Eron; he didn't have to. This picture would speak louder than any words... an image of Strangler giving that maniacal cold stare to Eron said it all. Finn asked the gentlemen if they were ready... and they all nodded. Finn stated that the coin toss would be determined like this; Finn would do the honors and flip it. If it landed on heads, Scott Slugger would be the winner. If it landed on tails, Eron would be the one who would receive the opportunity to win the World Title later tonight. With all of this hanging in the balance, Finn nodded, and prepared to flip it. Asking the men for further confirmation if they had any impulse to back off, Slugger & Eron looked at Finn if he were an idiot... and well, he was. Finn shrugged, before finally, he tossed it. It landed. Heads. Slugger would face The Boston Strangler. And this match wasn't booked to sell out an arena two months in advance... the crowd was already here. The arena had been sold out for six weeks. They didn't need to run this to draw. It was going to happen to settle things once and for all, although it should be noted that Eron too would receive a World Title shot forthcoming eventually... the coin toss was to determine who would receive their shot first. Tonight, Slugger. Soon, Eron. Eron looked at Finn, Slugger & Strangler with vengeance and perhaps a hint of revenge, while Slugger told Strangler he would see him later. Strangler nodded, and quietly said "The pleasure will be ours.", while Eron stormed off in another direction. Although he no longer had a purpose to be here any further, something in the air indicated that Eron would be back... somehow. Finn wished his champion good luck, while Slaughter returned to the ring. The Lone Gunmen vs. The California Kings Semi Charmed Life began to play, signaling the arrival of the Lone Gunmen, Double S Sid Snow and the massive Total Eclipse. The two walked to the ring without signs of Machina or Rudo, and fans noticed that Sid carried with him a folding table, tucked casually under one arm. The duo got into the ring, and Sid reached for a microphone. Fans rolled their eyes in anticipation of what the well-oiled member of the Lone Gunmen had to say. "People of Cedar Rapids... let me be the first to let you all know that tonight, in this very ring, you will all be witness to a little slice of Lone Gunmen history. For you see, since the Lone Gunmen returned to the fWo some three weeks ago, we have been right in the center of things. In the action so to speak... and I know that you all know that Double S here loves... action. But we got to talking backstage, and the Eclipse and I have decided that just being in the center of the action isn't quite enough. We want something that lives near the waist of the two rednecks called Damnation. Yes, yes... I think you all know what I mean." Sid dropped the arm holding the microphone down for a moment and smiled out at the booing crowd. He continued after a lengthy pause, "That's right... the fWo Tag Team titles... what did you think I meant? Please... everyone here knows that I'm saving myself for a very special baseball player on the roster... isn't that right Eclipse?" But the Eclipse's attention had wandered to one of the food vendors that was canvassing the audience. Sid continued with a sigh. "But tonight is just one step on our route to the top... because you see, getting completely and utterly humiliated by Machina wasn't enough. No, little Lance Star and Courage have challenged us to a match right here tonight. So... what is your cute little team name again? California Kings? Well boys, get your asses down to the ring and prepare to be spanked... in a way only Double S can." The music of the California Kings hit and the duo made their appearance at the top of the entrance ramp. The cocky smile sometimes seen on the faces of Lance Star and Courage was gone now, replaced with an expression of pure rage. They walked quickly toward the ring and once at ringside began tossing chairs inside onto the mat. Alarmed, Sid and the Eclipse dodged the flying steel objects as best they could, and met Star and Courage as they slid into the ring with kicks. As the bell rang the Kings made no attempt at starting a regular tag team match, with both men hammering away on the Gunmen... much to the dismay of the referee. Star grabbed Sid's arm and tossed him into the ropes, then charged after the helpless Sid and nailed a hard clothesline that send Sid toppling to the outside and crashing down hard on the arena floor. Star again went to the ropes, rebounded, and leapt to the outside with a flying plancha that devastated both men out on the cold arena floor. Inside Courage was punching away at the Eclipse, but to little avail as the Eclipse simply laughed as Courage's fists disappeared within the doughy mass of his stomach. Bellowing, the Eclipse laughed, "Silly man! Nothing can move the Eclipse!" Frustrated, Courage spotted a vendor three rows back in the crowd and pointed toward him. "Look Pillsbury doughboy... free hot dogs!" The Eclipse turned, already drooling at the prospect. Courage quickly snatched up a steel chair from the mat and bent it over the head of the turned Eclipse, sending the fat man reeling toward the corner. He landed with a loud thud, and the ring creaked ominously under the massive weight of the big man. Courage ripped off the t-shirt he had been wearing and signaled to the crowd that he was about to hit his super kick into the face of the big man. But on the outside Sid saw Courage setting up for the move and quickly grabbed a steel chair laying on the arena floor. He tossed it in to the Eclipse, and as Courage moved forward the Eclipse swung and decked Courage sideways with the steel before he even had a chance to raise his leg up for the move. Sid raised his fist in victory, but missed Lance Star moving in from behind with a spear that drove Sid backward into the steel of the steps. A loud thud rang out through the arena, followed shortly by the bell as the referee threw the match out with a double disqualification ruling. fWo security hit the scene as Lance Star continued to kick down at the helpless and battered Sid, and the Eclipse grabbed up Courage and put him into a massive bear hug in the center of the ring. The brawl didn't stop there, War & Plague hit the ring to a mixed reaction and joined in the melee. War & Plague attacked whomever they could get their hands on. Meanwhile, on the stage stood Stone, who watched the action proceed with baited interest, capturing it all on a camcorder. As fWo security worked to get the three teams separated, fans knew that the feud between the Kings & The Gunmen was far from over. Result: Double Disqualification Questions... Slaughter rolled on as "Creed" blasted inside the arena, signalling the arrival of Matt Douglas. When we last saw Matt, he had taken his own step to the next level by defeating the fWo Internet Champion (at the time) Primetime in non-title action. The crowd reacted warmly to the presence of Douglas, who speedwalked to the ring looking focused. Douglas rolled into the ring and asked for a microphone. As his music slowly faded, he began to speak. "You know, two years ago, I came to the fWo looking to make a name for myself. I believe I've done that, and if you look back to just two weeks ago, I defeated Primetime who was the Internet Champion at the time, in a non-title match. Well, he went on to lose to Mitch Wilson, who is undoubtedly a great competitor, but I still haven't received my title shot. All I want to know is..." while Douglas continued, Primetime rushed the ring from behind to a barrage of jeers. Douglas turned around and ducked an attempted superkick, and when Primetime turned around Douglas took him out with a dropkick. Douglas then brought him up and snap suplexed him, before heading to the top rope. He flew off with the 'Impact', but Primetime rolled out of the way at the last moment. Douglas smacked the mat, and then Primetime shook off the affects of the dropkick and snap suplex. Primetime waited as a dazed Douglas staggered to his feet, and from behind Primetime hooked him. For the second time in eight days, Primetime looked to put another star out of action. Last week, Havok. Tonight, Douglas. Both of them victims of the most dangerous move in wrestling, the backdrop driver. Douglas smacked the mat with authority, and a hush fell over the crowd. Fans clutched each other as Douglas' neck snapped, although he showed signs of life, it didn't look good. The fWo medical team stormed the ring, a few accompanied by a stretcher. Primetime exited the ring to boos, as several fans took the liberties of throwing an assortment of items at him. Primetime shrugged them off as the medical staff quickly checked over Douglas while Slaughter went to a commercial break... Team Meeting Slaughter returned from another set of commercials. Backstage, Big Wretchy The Ratings Saver paced back and forth while Scot Hartt sat in a chair looking through the latest issue of the Pro Wrestling Torch. Hartt' lackey sat indian style on the floor with several pieces of posterboard and some crayola magic markers. Wretch addressed the Abusah, who looked at him confused. "Alright, Abusah... I want you to make some signs for me tonight..." Abusah' raised an eyebrow. "SINES?" Wretch nodded thoughtfully. "Yes, some signs. You know, with me being the ratings draw of the fWo, I think I'm allowed to have my own signs... and well, I think that tonight is a good night to... play some mind games with Mitch Wilson." Abusah looked at him more puzzled than before. "MYND GAMEZ?" Wretch again nodded, before instructing Hartt to work with him on some of the ideas for the signs. Hartt prepared to, before asking Wretch a rhetorical question. "I don't believe this... I see news about this wwf fed, I see news about wcw, I see news about ecw... I see news about the WERCRAP fed... but do we see any news about the fWo? I mean, what's up with that? We're popular enough..." Wretch concurred, before simply shrugging and exiting the room... Machina vs. Mike Bear The Next Level tournament rolled on as Machina made his way out to the ring. Surrounded by the Lone Gunmen, Machina stopped when he reached ringside and asked for a microphone. Speaking clearly, he addressed his partners. "Rudo... Sid... Eclipse... I know you all want to be out here to help me go all the way in this thing... but listen, I've got to do this one on my own. You've got to go back now, head on back to the locker room. Go on... head out." The rest of the Gunmen looked at each other questioningly, then Sid nodded and the trio began to walk back up the entrance ramp. Machina shook his head with confusion and continued speaking. "Hey! Hey wait a minute! This is the part of the dialog where you say, 'no, no... we can't possibly leave you out here alone... we're staying'! And then I say, 'Are you sure about that guys?' and then you all say, 'Yes, yes! And even though you say you want to win this match on your own, we are going to help you win at every possible opportunity by using various cheating tactics.' You see how this is supposed to work guys?" The other Lone Gunmen looked at each other in confusion and then shrugged and walked back to ringside. Meanwhile the blaring sounds of Rob Zombie began to pulse over the arena speakers and Mike Bear made an appearance at the top of the entrance ramp. He walked to the ring escorted by the cheers of the crowd, but his gaze was intense and strongly focused on Machina. Mike Bear slid inside the ring and walked up to Machina, speaking at first softly then rising in volume until he was screaming into the face of the masked man. Machina swung back a fist as the bell rang, but Bear blocked it with a quick forearm and brought the other elbow up and over to make hard contact with his face. Machina stumbled backward, and Bear exploded from the center of the ring with a running forearm smash. Machina hit the mat hard, and Sid climbed up onto the ring apron. Mike Bear beat the referee over to the Lone Gunmen, and with a heavy hand punched Sid off the ring apron and back onto the steel barrier. The referee was over at the scene now, warning the Eclipse who was kneeling down to check on his fallen partner. Mike Bear raised his fists to the crowd, who responded in kind. But they did not see Rudo sneak around to the other side of the ring and pass a heavy metal object into Machina, who quickly took it and hid it within his closed fist. As Mike Bear walked back over to finish off his opponent, Machina struck upward with what could only be a pair of brass knuckles. The crack of steel on bone echoed through the arena and Mike Bear fell backward, his lip now split and bleeding. Machina quickly tossed the foreign object out of the ring and walked carefully around Bear. He delivered a few sharp kicks into the solar plexus of the downed Bear, and then signaled that he was going to end the match with his "Hope He Doesn't Move" finisher. Machina climbed to the top turnbuckle, extended his arms out to either side, and launched himself off in a perfect 450 splash from the top. It connected, and Mike Bear gasped as his breath escaped him in a rush. Machina grinned widely as he made the cover, but Bear at the last second got a shoulder up, breaking the count. Machina looked down surprised, and then grim determination took over as he once again got to his feet. Machina drug Mike Bear closer to the turnbuckle and climbed to the second rope, this time dropping a quick hard elbow into the chest of Bear. Bear's head lolled, and Machina again signaled for his finisher. He climbed to the top turnbuckle, extended both arms, but Bear was suddenly getting to his feet behind him. Bear quickly grabbed Machina around the waist on the turnbuckle, and with a last ditch effort executed a back suplex from the top rope that brought the masked man crashing to the ground. Flashbulbs popped as Mike Bear stood once more, sweating and bleeding, and made a cutting gesture at his throat that it was time to finish things. Picking Machina up in a fireman's carry, Mike Bear with great exhaustion hit the whiplash, dropping Machina to the center of the ring with authority. Bear make the cover and hooked the leg, and Machina kicked out just a fraction of a second too late. The bell rang, and Mike Bear released the cover and raised his arms in the air in victory. His moment was short lived however, as Sid, Rudo and the Eclipse hit the ring and began to beat down the exhausted Mike Bear. The crowd began to loudly boo the actions of the Lone Gunmen, but then came alive as Hellrazor and Fear, the fWo Tag Team Champions, ran to the ring wielding steel chairs. In a couple short moments the ring was cleared as Damnation helped Mike Bear to his feet. The Lone Gunmen stood on the outside, with Sid and the Eclipse gesturing to their waists and pointing up at the fWo Tag Team titles. As the Underground stood victorious inside the ring, Sunday Slaughter went to a long overdue commercial break. Result: Bear by pinfall Lack Of Remorse When Slaughter returned from commercials we were backstage, and a rusty car drove up to one of the wrestler's entrances. The engine died with a cough and a sputter, but under the tinted windows it was impossible to tell who was inside. The door creaked open, and a leather clad boot stepped out and make contact with the pavement. The leg was favored, still weak from an earlier injury and the owner limped slightly as he walked forward and slammed the door behind him. Neo looked around himself with a sneer on his face, and then back at the car. "Get out you stupid slut... the ride is over." The passenger door opened, and a young but not amazingly attractive woman exited. She smoked a cigarette and hacked black smoke into the night air. She looked at Neo with a slightly hurt but also aroused expression on her face, and started to walk over to join him. Neo looked over at her as she came and held up a hand. "Get the hell away from me... did I say anything about you and I being some kind of item? You dumb slut... get the hell away from here. Go on." Now the woman's expression showed more clearly the hurt and she began to protest, but was cut off by a shout from behind her. Rottdogg ran up to meet her, still wearing a neck brace securely fastened around his neck. "Hey! Hey Marlene! Hey... where the hell have you been? I've been looking all over for you... you weren't in the hotel when I came back from the gym this afternoon and..." Rottdogg stopped as he saw Neo. He looked at his wife and then back at Neo again. He started to speak, then choked slightly. His mouth fell open. His wife stammered and started to explain. "Listen honey... it's not what it looks like... I just... I mean to say..." Neo lit a cigarette and turned to glare at Rottdogg. "What she means to say is that I've just violated your wife in every possible way you can imagine, and now as I look at your sorry ass I think a few ways you couldn't imagine. I used this cheap whore you call a wife and have now brought back the sloppy remains to you so your pathetic pile of crap self can deal with the remains. That just about cover it, doll?" Rottdogg's wife Marlene burst into sudden jerking tears and she ran sobbing into the arena. Rottdogg watched her go, and then turned back to Neo, his face red and enraged. Rottdogg's fists clenched in and out, and spittle began to fly from his mouth as he growled mindless obscenities in Neo's direction. Neo took this all in and laughed, and continued to laugh as Rottdogg charged him and knocked him to the ground with a hard punch. "That's it! That's it you useless pile of garbage... that's anger, now let's see if you can hold onto what you've got for yourself." Neo punched his fist upward, catching Rottdogg in the neck. The neck brace shattered sending bits of plastic stabbing into Neo's fist and Rottdog's neck and face. Rottdogg screamed a gasping scream and vomited blood onto the pavement as he clutched at his neck. He fell backward still gasping for air as Neo climbed to his feet and wiped a streak of blood from his chin. Rottdogg watched him with bulging eyes, mouthing the word "why" with silent gasps of breath. Neo smirked. "Why you? Because you were stupid enough to think you had something on me. Fact is I could really give a damn about you. You're a nobody in this federation... a nothing useless hack that has never and will never have anything resembling success here. You're a waste of skin in a promotion full of crap... and you just had the bad luck of getting in my way. Little boy, you're a speed bump. You think what I did to you was bad? Just wait and see. Xavier? Erik Kelly? Crusader? The Strangler? Those people are going to wish they were pathetic crappy you when I'm done." Neo gave Rottdogg another kick to the ribs and turned to walk away. As he neared the arena entrance he fished into his pocket and pulled out a set of keys. He tossed them to John Willis the fWo security chief and pointed back over his shoulder. "Park my car and wipe that stain off the road back there. I've got people to talk to." Mitch Wilson vs. Wretch Slaughter heads to the ring for a double-non-title second round Next Level tournament match, pitting Mitch Wilson against Wretch. "Paint It Black" begins to play, and the fWo International Champion Big Wretchy The Ratings Saver, accompanied by The Abusah, who is carrying a set of signs, appears on the entrance ramp to a huge pop from the crowd. Wretch heads down the entrance ramp to the ring and Abusah stands at ringside, yelling incoherently. "Calling All Stations" starts up and the cheers turn to boos as fWo Internet Champion Mitch Wilson heads out to the ring, wearing a scowl on his face. As he nears ringside, TMFAOYM holds up a sign which reads "MICH IZ UNKARIZMATIK!", before yelling at Wilson to "REED MI SINE!". Wilson obliges and his eyes bulge out. Wilson charges The Abusah and lifts him up by the neck, but he is interrupted by Wretch who baseball slides to the outside of the ring, driving his feet onto the side of the head of Wilson in the process. Wilson stumbles out, and Wretch hooks his head from behind, going for the Depression. Wilson heads to the corner and mounts the second rope, before leaping off and driving an elbow into the throat of Wretch. Wilson covers again and Wretch again escapes at two. Wilson covers, but Wretch continues to show great resilience by kicking out once more at two. Wilson begins to get upset, and brings Wretch up to his feet and violently shoves him into the corner. Wilson rips apart Wretch's tank-top and chops him hard again, the anger evident in each hard shot. Wilson begins to sacrifice quality for quantity, striking Wretch with rapid-fire no-windup chops. Wretch replies in kind before slapping Wilson on the chest with a smack. Wilson chops Wretch again, but Wretch grabs him, turns him around, and strikes with a pair of chops of his own. Wilson moves away however, and as Wretch stumbles backwards out of the corner, Wilson hooks him from behind and drives him into the mat with a german suplex. Wretch quickly runs at the side ropes and leaps backwards onto them, before leaping off with a guillotine-style legdrop. Wretch covers and hooks a leg, but Wilson is not finished yet and kicks out at two and three quarters. Wretch brings Wilson up and drops him with a Wretchinoku driver. Wretch signals that he's about to end it and begins to climb to the top. He reaches the top, measures Wilson, and leaps off with a 450 splash. Unfortunately for Wretch, Wilson moves and Wretch splats to the mat. Wilson makes his way to his feet and shakes his head, clearing the cobwebs, before bringing Wretch up and hooking his head to set up the Headliner. He sneers at the crowd, but suddenly his attention is diverted as TMFAOYM at ringside holds up a sign reading "BIOTCH WIT MIOTCH". Wilson frowns, wondering what the hell that means, and drops Wretch in favor of yelling at TMFAOYM, asking him what the hell he's doing. The Abusah responds with a heartfelt "EYE DON'T NO!", causing Wilson to shake his head in confusion at how one person can be so stupid. Behind him, Wretch is thinking the same thing as he hooks Wilson from behind and drops him with a bridging belly-to-back suplex. Three seconds later, Wretch has advanced into the semi-finals and Mitch Wilson is in a state of shock. Wilson spots TMFAOYM at ringside and charges out after him, chasing him off through the crowd before retrieving his belt and walking to the back, stunned. Inside the ring, Wretch retrieves his own belt and strikes a pose in the ring to the cheers of the crowd. Meanwhile... Result: Wretch by pinfall Challenger As Slaughter headed backstage, we saw the challenger for tonight's fWo World Title Match in his dressing room. Scott Slugger prepared for the match by doing some last-minute warm-ups, including some push-ups, a few jumping jacks, and stretching out. He didn't know the camera was present but likely didn't care, his mindset focused on one thing tonight, and that being his upcoming match. He finished his warm-ups before grabbing for a bottle of water from his gym bag and taking a swig of it, before spraying a little water on his hands, and then wiping it on his face to cool off. It was the first time you had ever tasted what it was like to be the fWo World Champion. It was your first reign. What I want to know is, if tonight goes as you plan what will separate it from victory number one?" Slugger contemplated the question before answering. "Number one was sweet, there's no doubt about that. You can never replace the feeling of the first one... never. But tonight will come just as close. Tonight, is the night, that the WGA gets his opportunity to lay claim to number five. And just as number four it will be unprecedented. Nobody else will be able to claim they did it, only one person will, and you're looking at him. And in just a few short minutes, the WGA will walk down that ramp and into that ring, with the crowd's reaction louder than any reaction from a crowd has ever been. A reaction to the Rolling Stones in concert wouldn't come close. A reaction to Eron The Relentless being thrown off the face of the earth - as much as we all like the possibility - would not compare. A reaction to Ice & Mitch Wilson killing each other with knives and guns in the middle of broad daylight in Times Square in New York wouldn't be as loud as the one the World's Greatest Athlete is about to receive when he walks out there. But let me tell you something that will. Tonight, when the WGA gives The Boston Strangler that ride on the Curveball Express, and he lays claim to the fWo World Title for a fifth time, the reaction will positively, without a shadow of a doubt, 'cause you to go deaf. And that is why number five will be close to number one, Geoff." Slugger gave the camera man a stern look, before exiting his dressing room, presumably heading towards the entrance to the stage... Champion In the other side of the Five Seasons Center, The Boston Strangler stood looking into a mirror. His fWo World Title was draped over his massive shoulder, and the glare of the gold shined off the mirror, producing an image of beauty. The Boston Strangler (c) vs. Scott Slugger Fireworks exploded as the sound of AC/DC's "Back In Black" began pulsating inside the Five Seasons Center in Cedar Rapids, Iowa. Six years ago this venue was the location for a gigantic match featuring Hulk Hogan & Ric Flair, but on this night, that match paled in comparison. Yes, tonight, both World Champion and challenger are in the prime of their careers, while Hogan and Flair were becoming shells of their former selves back then. As the stream of fireworks continued to shoot into the atmosphere of the building, the World's Greatest Athlete walked out onto the stage, which prompted the crowd to react the only way they knew how, and that was to cheer as loud as they possibly could. Slugger acknowledged the crowd with his trademark smirk and a small wave, showing his token of appreciation for their support. Slugger used the ropes to propel himself over the top rope into the ring, before he stood in the center of the ring. He procured a bat from inside his leather jacket and made a mock swing, and at the completion of his swings, fireworks sprinkled from the rafters above, and more display of pyrotechnics was in full force from behind Slugger. Perhaps it was perfect considering that the Fourth of July was in a mere forty eight hours. Certainly, Scott Slugger wanted to celebrate the nation's birthday in style with the fWo World Title around his waist. As the pomp and circumstance died down, the lights dimmed, and the eerie tune of "O' Fortuna" resounded off the arena walls. The ovation of cheers could not be described with words. The reaction was mind boggling. Moments after the cheers began, The Boston Strangler walked out onto the stage, with the fWo World Title draped over his shoulder. The fans applauded their knight in shining armor as he walked to the ring methodically, already signalling that the mind games had begun. The Strangler walked up the ring steps into the ring, before holding his fWo World Title above his head. Slugger eyed it as if it was the most important thing to him on the planet, and watched it as the referee retrieved it from the champion. The referee handed it to an assistant outside the ring, before the bell finally rang. Champion and challenger began to feel each other out so to speak, and circled each other, all the while exchanging pleasantries. Strangler reached for Slugger' arm quickly, and looked to have had the Massacre locked in, but Slugger escaped and slid out onto the floor. Strangler followed him out as Slugger backed away. The native of Boston, Massachusetts charged at the the man who was drafted #1 by the Seattle Mariners in 1993, and Slugger ducked and backdropped him on the floor. Strangler got up seconds later, and this time he was met with a flying headscissors takeover out on the floor. Slugger then brought Strangler up and whipped him into the ring backfirst, and then grabbed him by the hair and rolled him underneath the bottom rope into the ring. The crowd watched eagerly as Slugger hopped up onto the ring apron and then walked over to the turnbuckles. He then climbed up and signalled that he was going to go for the flying lariat, aka the 'Grand Slam'. He waited as the World Champion reached his feet, and then flew off the top ropes. Strangler however ducked, and instead Slugger slammed arm first into the mat. Strangler saw this as an opportunity to take over the match, and he did. He methodically stomped away on that injured right arm of the World's Greatest Athlete, before bringing Slugger up and slapping on an armbar. Strangler continued to work the arm over, obviously trying to set him up for the feared Boston Massacre. After a few moments of keeping him in the armbar, Strangler winded up and prepared to send him to the ropes with an Irish Whip, but then turned it into a short-arm clothesline. Strangler then went for a pinfall attempt, but Slugger kicked out at two. Continuing with the aforementioned game plan of wearing Slugger down and then going for the kill, Strangler brought Slugger to his feet and grabbed his arm, before taking him over near the ropes. He chopped Slugger across the chest while holding him by the arm, and then climbing out on the apron. He then draped him by the arm over the top rope, before dropping down to the floor, 'causing the armpit area of Slugger to rake across the rope, before propelling him back towards the center of the ring. He fell back onto the mat, before rolling over onto his knees... attempting to get up. Strangler hurried back into the ring and quickly rolled Slugger into a pin with a half-nelson cradle, but Slugger kicked out at two and a half. Strangler stomped at the arm some more, before he brought the former baseball phenom up and sent him into the turnbuckles hard. Slugger staggered out, and Strangler grabbed him by the hair and bulldogged him into the center of the ring. Strangler then covered the former-four time fWo World Champion, but Slugger kicked out at two. Strangler covered him again, but this time hooking the leg, but Slugger had the resliency to kick out again. Strangler decided now was the time to go full force in attempt to win the match, he sent Slugger into the ropes, Slugger returned and Strangler kicked him in the stomach, before hooking him for the gutwrench powerbomb. Slugger blocked it by reaching for the ankle, then reached up and hit nailed him with a forearm. This staggered Strangler briefly, and Slugger found himself free enough to try and recover in the match. Slugger charged to the ropes and bounced off, he came back for a flying bodypress, but Strangler caught him and launched him into the air with a fallaway slam. Slugger slammed into the mat, and Strangler followed it by bringing Slugger up to a vertical base and going for the gutwrench powerbomb again. This time he hit it, and went for another cover. The crowd gasped in awe as Slugger kicked out. The fans cheered this as they were obviously on his side, but holding no ill will towards the champion. Strangler grabbed Slugger and brought him up to his feet, before draping him onto his back in a fireman's carry. He fell back hitting the Samoan Drop, of which Slugger got his foot on the ropes at two and a quarter. Strangler continued, this time whipping Slugger into the turnbuckles. Strangler then charged in shoulder first, but Slugger fell out of the corner at the last minute. Strangler slammed into the post shoulder first, and then staggered out bent over. Slugger grabbed him and hooked him for the triple powerbomb combo. He hit the first one, and then another, but on the third time Strangler backdropped out of it. Slugger held on in an attempt on a sunset flip, but Strangler went to his knees and hooked him for the pin. One, two. However, Slugger had enough to propel Strangler off of him. Strangler went into the ropes, Slugger sprung to his feet, and as Strangler returned Slugger took him down with the drop-toe-hold. Slugger then got up to his feet and waited for Strangler to get up. Strangler did, and Slugger bounced off the ropes. Strangler ducked the lariat, and as Slugger bounced off the ropes and returned, Strangler took him down with a drop-toe-hold of his own. Slugger smacked the mat hard, and Strangler saw his opportunity. He quickly sprung to his feet, and went for the Boston Massacre. Slugger felt him reach for it, and quickly slid out onto the ropes to the floor. Slugger then took a moment to catch his breath, but Strangler wasn't about to rest. He bounced off the ropes and charged onto the floor with a nice looking pescado. Slugger moved out of the way however, and Strangler hit the impending pavement with a thud. Slugger quickly sent him back into the ring, and then went back to the top rope for the 'Grand Slam'. Strangler got to his feet, and Slugger hit it. It appeared to be over, and Slugger went to cover. However, two men hit the ring. Bad Attitude. Ice & Mitch Wilson, the reigning fWo Internet Champion. Ice distracted the referee, and Mitch Wilson entered the ring and began stomping that injured arm of the WGA. He then brought Slugger up and hooked him, hitting the 'Headliner'. He exited the ring, and went unnoticed by the referee as he made his way to the back through the crowd. Ice explained that he just wanted to tell the referee he was doing a fine job in officiating the match. The referee shrugged and Ice walked backstage to an enormous mount of boos, jeers and catcalls from the crowd. Ice looked amongst the sea of fans and saw nothing but middle fingers pointed in his direction, but he paid no attention. Instead, he smirked, and headed backstage. Back in the ring, Strangler slowly rolled over and laid his arm onto the WGA. Two and a half seconds later, Slugger shouldered out. The arena responded with a collective sigh of relief. Strangler got up and looked around the arena at the fans, who were now cheering wildly. The world champion sent the challenger into the corner with an Irish Whip, but Slugger reversed it. Strangler reversed the reversal, and Slugger smacked the turnbuckles. Strangler again charged in shoulder first, and again like last time, Slugger moved out of the way, this time on instinct. Strangler hit the post, and Slugger grabbed him by the head, and moved towards the corner. It was clearly obvious that Slugger was going for the Curveball. Strangler got up and slowly moved towards him, obviously in exhaustation. Primetime kicked him in the stomach, and secured him for the Primetime Bomb. The duo escaped through the crowd, barely, although the fans were about to kill the two men. Star relieved the referee of his grasp, before heading backstage to a chorus of boos. Champion and challenger remained out of it in the ring, feeling the affects from the 'Courage Required' and the Primetime Bomb that happened just moments ago. The referee began a ten count. One. Two. The referee was this close to reaching ten, when Strangler reached out with his arm and draped it over the challenger. The referee began to count. One. Two. Somehow, Slugger shouldered out of it. Frustrated, but knowing that could not be the end of the match, Strangler re-thought the match, and prepared to go back to work on the arm. He did just that with a devastating Fujiwara Armbar. Slugger writhed in agony, trying to escape from the hold. He reached for the ropes continuously, but kept falling short.
