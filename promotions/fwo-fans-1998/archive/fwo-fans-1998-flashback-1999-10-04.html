FlashBack, 4 October 1999

4 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The scene opened on the fWo studio, where Chris Finn sat smiling at the camera. "Hello, my name is Chris Finn and welcome to fWo FlashBack on Monday Night. While the other promotions are showing their latest episodes in action adventure dramas we offer you a look at some real action... action that happened in the last week in the fWo. Tonight's episode will feature some action from the big fWo Pay Per View in September, Fallout. We will also show you some action from Sunday Slaughter, which our directors dubbed fWo: Year Two. There will be some other comments from some of our highly trained wrestlers and technical staff, and answer some questions about the fWo. Remember, you can always call the fWo to ask your questions at [detail withheld], or eMail questions at our web site, fWowrestling.com. Just send any eMail to [email removed]. But now... welcome to the inaugural episode of fWo FlashBack!" Chris Finn swiveled in his chair to a bay of video screens as the lights dimmed in the room... Flying Frenchie vs. Black Panther After going through thirty other fWo stars, the finals of the Wrestler of the Year tournament ended with a match between Primetime and the Black Panther. Both men had already fought once in the night, and were weary. The match began with a simple lock up that the Frenchie took the advantage of. The Panther struck back with blows to the back and head. The Panther struck hard with a drop of the Frenchie into the metal guard rail that stunned the French superstar. An early cover failed to get the Frenchie put away, and the Panther continued to stay on the attack. The Frenchie battled back by attacking the knee, and a quick succession of near falls followed. The Frenchie attacked with suplexes and more near falls, taking advantage of the weakened knee. The Panther turned the tables when he tossed the Frenchie into an announcing table. The Frenchie fought back, and landed a splash... but the Panther thinking quickly was able to turn the tide, reverse the rollup, and walk out of the match the winner. But the night wasn't over for the Panther, as we will discover later in the night... Result: Black Panther by Pinfall A public service announcement The words "Learn with the fWo" filled the screen, and then faded onto a common suburban neighborhood. Some children had stumbled across a trash can that had been overturned, and were poking at the contents. One poke with a stick caused a bottle of medication to roll out, and on young boy picked it up. The label read "Viagra", and the pills inside appeared to be small blue triangles. Shrugging, the boy opened the bottle and swallowed down several of the pills. A strange expression came across his face, and he was forced to loosen his belt. He boy began to run around in circles, when suddenly the Mad Trucker appeared on the scene. "Now, now son... you see what's done happened here? You've swallowed some pills you found in the trash... and not just any pills... but sexual pills. Those pills are for olden's having trouble getting it up...like Bob Dole, Ric Flair, and James Armstrong. You can't take pills out of the trash and stuff it into your yapper boy!" The young man fidgeted uncomfortably, but smiled up at the Mad Trucker. "Thanks Mad Trucker, now I know!" The Mad Trucker patted the young boy on the head. "And knowing is half the match.... TOOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" Black filled the screen, along with the words: "A public service announcement from the fWo". Ice & Scott Slugger vs. Nikki & Speed Demon Sunday Slaughter featured two matches from the fWo Tag Team Tournament. Fans remember that the fWo Tag Team title was held up after a ladder match between Doomsday and the Hollywood Blondes, and this sixteen team tournament was declared to settle the Tag Title issue once and for all. The first match was between Ice and Scott Slugger and Speed Demon and Nikki. But fans saw earlier in the night that Nikki had abandoned Speed Demon after telling him that the pregnancy was a scam. As loud music and other noise was heard, Speed made his way out crying. Speed climbed into the ring and continued to sob and pout, before slumping down in the middle of the ring and crying some more. The infamous music of Scott Slugger played and out came he and Ice. Ice charged down into the ring and began to pummel Speed relentlessly, while Slugger took his time coming down. Seconds later, as seen via the Slaughterscreen, The Hollywood Blondes looked on from a luxury box. Ice continued to beat on Speed with ease, tossing him around the ring like a redheaded stepchild and beating him unmercilessly. Ice then hit his finisher, the 'Icebreaker' from the ropes. He pinned Speed, but at the count of two he pulled him up and laughed at him in his face. He then pointed at Slugger and asked Slugger if he wanted to finish things off. Slugger said "Sure... why not?", and Ice tagged him in. Slugger came in without removing his baseball jersey. He hit the 'Curveball' on Speed and prepared to pin him. He first pinned him with his thumb, but decided that was too easy. He then removed a strand of his own hair and gently let it rest above Speed' chest, but decided that was too easy. He then bit off one of his fingernails and went for a pin again, but decided that was even easier than before. Finally, he removed a button from his jersey and used it to finish off Speed, as Ice continued to laugh his proverbial ass off. James Armstrong giggled like a school girl and continued to give praise to Slugger. But this was not the last tag tournament match we would see on Slaughter... Result: Ice & Scott Slugger by Pinfall The fWo welcomes Havok One of the things many of the fans are talking about is the debut of Havok, TCW star and former champion into the fWo. Havok made his presence felt at the end of fWo: Fallout when he challenged Black Panther's Wrestler of the Year win. Havok called himself the true millennium man, and ruined Black Panther's moment in the spotlight. It would not end there however, as Havok made his presence felt with the winner of the 1999 Meltdown contest Primetime, and the current fWo World Heavyweight champion Scott Slugger. Because of his actions many of the fWo stars are rumored to be looking to take Havok down, and next week at Sunday Slaughter one will get his chance. Havok faces Chris Universal at Slaughter, but rumor has it that he will be on hand at Warfare to lay down "his law". But while Havok is a new fWo star in the making, an established member of the roster left the active list after this epic match with Primetime... Primetime (c) vs. Wolf Fang On Sunday Slaughter, weeks after making the challenge, Wolf Fang received his long overdue title shot at Primetime. Wolf Fang made his way to the ring first, his first appearance in an fWo ring in some time. Primetime made his way out with the Cruiserweight Title around his waist, and the Boston Strangler in two. The referee signaled for the bell and the match began. The match started out slowly with neither man gaining a clear cut advantage, before Primetime used a low-blow to take over, before following it up with a cradle piledriver. Primetime went for a cover, but only got a two count. Primetime followed things up with a series of short kicks to the back of the head, letting each repetitive blow take it's toll. Primetime went for another cover, and Wolf Fang got a shoulder up before three. Primetime went for an aerial attack next, flying off with a missile dropkick. He then brought Wolf Fang up and hit a fisherman's suplex, and Wolf Fang again narrowly escaped defeat. Primetime threw Wolf Fang over the top rope onto the floor, before diving out after Wolf Fang with a pescado. Wolf Fang moved out of the way and Primetime hit the mat. Wolf Fang whipped Primetime back first into the guardrail, before bringing PT back into the ring. He scooped slammed him, before going up top to follow off with an elbow drop. Wolf Fang took way too long climbing up on the way up, as if he seemed rather uninterested with completing his next move. PT followed him out onto the ring apron, and after several blows back and forth PT positioned Wolf Fang for a Primetime Bomb off the top rope. Soon after a pinfall was made, and Primetime retained the Cruiserweight Title. Just moments after that Wolf Fang announced his decision to leave the active roster to his partner Primetime, which would effect the tag tournament match later in the night... as the Hollywood Blondes advanced after beating Jackhammer in a handicap match. Result: Primetime by Pinfall Who is the dark man? Many in the fWo are wondering who the mysterious man in control of Fallen and Dina Sol... now called Poison. While few have answers, a big part of the mystery was revealed on Sunday Slaughter. It all began at the start of the show, and was caught on a security camera inside the arena. Hidden in shadows and seated on a throne of stone the man looked out over the dingy room. The thin woman barred her teeth and him, and clutched her hand in a fist. "More than a year S..." The large man stopped Dina Sol's voice with a swat of his hand, backhanding her into the wall. She landed near a feral looking man who wore a chain around his neck that latched him to the cold wall. Fallen watched Dina Sol's head crack into the wall and slide down to the ground, already unconscious. Fallen looked up into the eyes of the man who had attacked her, and his expression was one of dead stillness. The two locked eyes, and for a moment fire appeared in the eyes of John Skyler. It passed as he let his head droop down, Fallen once more. The dark man stood, letting his robes fall about his legs. His face was cloaked in shadows and dark green eyes pierced the dark. "Everything changes now. The time for fun and games are over... I've gotten stuck here, a bump in the road on the path to greater glory. Now... now there are imposters that roam the streets, and my plans are going to succeed. Others might deny it, others might ignore it, but this is the way it must be. There are five that must be dealt with... five that can stop us..." The man in shadows looked down at Fallen and the unconscious woman by his side that was once his wife. "One year has passed. One more must not. Find them... destroy them all." The scene faded after this and Slaughter began... but the mystery of who has been tormenting Fallen and now Dina Sol is slightly clearer... Scotty Cool (c) vs. Chris Kidman Jr. In this week's Classic fWo, we took a look at an Internet title match between Scotty Cool and Chris Kidman Jr. Both men have changed much over the last year, with Scotty Cool taking on the mantle of Death, and Chris Kidman taking on the mantle of Wretch. Scotty Cool has led the new faction of Dark Riders, and Chris Kidman joined the Underground. The match originally aired on July First, 1998 and was for the Internet title, which Scotty Cool held at the time. The match was hard fought, with the momentum swinging back and forth through the match. Scotty Cool attacked with power moves, and Chris Kidman Jr. used his high flying attacks to keep the big man off his feet. Chris Kidman hit a flying dropkick and made a quick cover, but Rage appeared and attacked Kidman with a steel chair to his head. Scotty Cool lay a hand on the chest of Kidman, still dazed after Kidman's attack. Scotty Cool won the match, and after he had left the ring with his title Jim Dudley appeared on the entrance ramp to announce that Rage had just become the newest member of the Empire. This was stunning news to Kidman, who had a history with Dudley... but in those days had allied himself with Scott Slugger and the Punisher Squad. Result: Scotty Cool by Pinfall Epilogue The scene opened on Chris Finn in the fWo studios. "I hope you enjoyed this first episode of fWo FlashBack... join us again next Monday when we will recap Warfare, Slaughter, feature a classic match from last year in the fWo, and have an exclusive interview with none other than the fWo World Heavyweight champion Scott Slugger, who has been a wanted man in the fWo of late. He will give his feelings on the Black Panther, Primetime, Cactus Jon and Havok. Also remember to watch Warfare and Slaughter this week, when we will have some huge matches and further developments in the fWo Tag Team Tournament. Also, watch Warfare for an announcement from James Armstrong regarding... the TCW? It's going to be a wild week... see you there!" The scene faded to black as highlights of the week, set to music by Korn played... and the first episode of fWo FlashBack came to an end.
