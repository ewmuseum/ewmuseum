Wednesday Warfare, 23 February 2000

23 February 2000

I'll show that coffee drinking tubby what it means to be a real wrestler who drinks real fluids. Rudo rolled his eyes. "Oh come on Machina, lots of people drink coffee, it's not like..." His cel phone rang again, and Rudo answered it with a sigh. "100.7 FM Always playing the hits. Who? Oh hey, Penny." Rudo put a hand over the phone mouthpiece. "It's Penny." Machina nodded, and reached for the phone. Rudo gave it to him, and listened for a moment. "Yeah, I love you too. But you've got to make sure I'm on the line before you say that hon... I mean, you wouldn't want to embarrass Rudo am I right?" Rudo coughed and looked in another direction. The limo was slowing down now, pausing to wait as the gate was opened so it could enter the talent entrance to the arena. "Well okay sugar... I'll see you tonight. Yeah, outside the arena will work fine. Okay... bye!" Machina closed the phone and beamed at Rudo. "She's great isn't she?" Rudo smiled a dopey smile. "Oh yeah she is... but listen, about the coffee. Don't you think that..." The phone rang again, and Rudo sighed. "100.7 FM Always playing the hits... what?" Rudo began to jump up and down in his chair. "We won? You're serious? 100.7 FM's caller contest?" Rudo's eyes went wide. "A question to win the prize? Okay... ask away. Capital of Utah? Why that's easy! It's Provo! What? Yes! Yes! We won! We won! God bless Provo, Utah! So... what did we win? A cruise? We won a cruise? To the Bahamas? Me and ten of my guests? This is great! We were planning a bachelor party and... what? You're going to provide us with strippers as well? Oh man! Yeah, I'll come by and pick up the forms... thanks! 100.7 FM is the greatest! Bye!" Rudo closed the phone and turned back to Machina shouting, "We won!" Machina and Rudo both did a strange sitting dance in the limo as it pulled up to the arena doors. Inside fireworks sounded, as Wednesday Warfare was taking to the air... Underground & Hellrazor vs. Millennium Men & Scott Collins Wednesday Warfare took to the air as the music of the Underground plays and Mike Bear and Wretch walk out onto the entrance ramp, holding the tag team titles high. After a moment of this, their music stops. Bear and Wretch toss the belts onto their shoulders, and point at the entryway... before "From This Day" starts playing and out walks Hellrazor, holding Lamont high, to a huge pop from the crowd. Bear, Wretch, and Hellrazor then confidently make their way to the ring, rolling in simultaneously before Wretch and Bear hand their belts to the ref. They prepare for the match as "Killed The Cat" starts to play, and out walk the Millennium Men, the Black Panther and Havok. They too stand on the entrance ramp, and after a moment, "Burning" starts playing and the Millennium Men are joined by none other than Scott Collins. Bear's expression in the ring turns briefly to shock, before he recovers and yells at them to "bring it on". Collins and the Millennium Men do so, charging the ring and trading fists with the Underground. The bell rings, and the referee manages to seperate the two teams, leaving Collins and Bear to start it off. Collins has the advantage, and whips Bear into the ropes, catching him with a stiff lariat. Collins brings Bear up, double underhooks him, and takes him over with a suplex. Collins covers Bear, but Bear kicks out at one. He brings Bear up again, and locks him in a double-arm trap, before taking him up and over with a version of the belly-to-belly suplex. Collins tags in Havok, who charges in for an elbowdrop, but is caught in a DTH when Bear rolls onto his side and hooks the ankle. Both men are quickly up, and Bear ducks a Havok lariat, before catching him halfway around and taking him down with a back suplex. Bear brings Havok up again, and hits a series of hard forearms to the jaw, backing him into the corner. Bear grabs the ropes for leverage, and begins repeatedly driving his shoulder into the stomach of Havok. He takes a moment to unleash some trash talk before one, and Havok takes the opportunity to mount the second rope and leap over Bear, taking him down with a sunset flip. The ref counts, but Bear escapes at two. Bear is quicker up of the two, and unleashes a superkick in the direction of Havok that nearly fractures his jaw. Havok crumples to the mat, but Bear is not done, and lifts him up again. He begins a whip into the ropes, then pulls back into a short whip, lifting Havok onto his shoulders and planting him into the mat with a death valley driver. Bear covers Havok, but Havok kicks out at two. Bear brings Havok up, and holds him in position while he tags in partner Hellrazor. Hellrazor comes in with a stiff punch to the kidneys, before twisting Havok's arm into an armbar, then using that to whip Havok into the ropes. Havok comes off with an attempt at a spinning headscissors, but Hellrazor catches him halfway through and holds him in an inverted piledriver position. Hellrazor then drops down into an inverted tombstone, slamming Havok's head into the mat. Hellrazor covers, but Black Panther dives in to make the save at the last moment. Undaunted, Hellrazor brings Havok up as the ref ushers BP back into his corner. He attempts a kick, but Havok catches his leg. Hellrazor attempts to turn this into an enzuiguri, but Havok ducks and Hellrazor hits the mat face-first. Both men are down, and now it's a race to the tag. Hellrazor quickly tags in Wretch, who dives for the leg of Havok... but Havok squirms free and dives into his corner for the tag to the Black Panther. Panther comes in and unloads on Wretch with rights, quickly whipping him into the ropes and powerslamming him. Wretch gets quickly up, but is hooked and taken over by the Panther in a towering vertical suplex. Panther holds on and brings Wretch up, snap suplexing him over. He holds on once more and brings Wretch up again, double underhooking him, lifting him up, and dropping him into a tiger driver with a pin. The ref's count reaches two before Mike Bear storms in to break it up, and he's met quickly by Scott Collins. Havok and Hellrazor join the fray as well, and the action is fast and furious. Somewhere in process of the brawl, Mike Bear prepares to hit a superkick on Scott Collins, but Collins ducks and the superkick strikes Wretch in the back of the head, causing him to stumble towards the Black Panther, who hooks his head and takes him over with a fishermans suplex. The ref tolls the three-count before Bear or Hellrazor can take notice of the pinfall, and the team of Scott Collins and the Millennium Men are declared victorious. The brawl inside the ring continues unabated as Warfare continues... Result: Millennium Men & Scott Collins by Pinfall New Members As the fans anxiously awaited the next match the lights suddenly dimmed, and Black Quicksilver came out onto the entrance ramp. He held Stump under one arm, and led a figure covered in a blanket down to the ring behind him. The person seemed to be having difficulty walking with the blanket covering their head and more importantly eyes, but Black Quicksilver led the form to the ring slowly and with care. Reaching the ring Black Quicksilver rolled inside, and then helped the covered form get into the ring. Once inside Black Quicksilver asked for a microphone, and was handed one reluctantly. He pointed to the crowd, and then to the covered form next to him. "For the last few weeks I've been looking to add a third member to the STUMP squad... and many of you have seen the results. Backstage it's just one loser after another, but finally I've come across the perfect partner to team up with. This man has wrestled at least twenty five matches in his career, and certainly one of those numbers in his win loss column is far greater than the other. Ladies and gentlemen... I give you the newest member of the STUMP squad and my new tag team partner... Silver Slowback!" Black Quicksilver yanked the cloth off the head of the figure beneath so quickly that the wrestler lost his balance and fell backward, promptly knocking himself out on the mat. Fans turned their heads sideways in an attempt to figure out who the prone form on the mat was. Looking closely, it appeared to be everyone's favorite Mike Heftel. The crowd sighed as Black Quicksilver worked to revive his partner, and Wednesday Warfare went to a commercial break. Dark Ninja vs. Rudo The groovy beat of "Body Movin'" heralds one member of the Canadian Connection, Dark Ninja; in singles action. He is predictably booed, yet shrugs it off and begins a light jog to the ring. Once there, Rudo emerges, "Dizz-Knee-Land" his key. He is met kindly by those in attendance, and soon faces Ninja inside the ring, looking to test his luck in the match to come. Looking to gain the advantage, he throws the first punch, yet much to his dismay, Ninja easily evades it. Dropping, he wraps about Rudo's leg with a drop-toehold that floors Rudo. Lifting him quickly, he whips him to the ropes, meeting him with a shoulder block that immediately has him on his back once more. Then heading off to the ropes, Ninja returns. Rudo is able to roll towards him, forcing Ninja to skip over him and head off the opposite ropes. Rudo hops to his feet and lunging at a rebounding Ninja, clotheslines him harshly across the chest. Rudo looks for a cover, yet only is able to squeeze out a one count. And then the tide begins to change, with Rudo faltering, missing badly on three consecutive punches before Ninja makes him pay for these mistakes, with in succession, a roundhouse kick clipping Rudo in the gut, a series of forearm smashes, and finally hooking his arms, and heaving him upwards with a double-underhook suplex and then dropping him to the canvas with authority. Hauling him to his feet once more, he lifts him with a Gutbuster drop, and upon bringing him down upon his knee, has Rudo clutching his chest in obvious distress. Ninja has the match all but won, and his running mate, Tom "the Kung Fu" Bomb runs down to ring-side, apparently to celebrate a certain victory. Yet he carries a kendo stick, and as Ninja "helps" Rudo upwards to put the finishing touches on this match, Tom looks to impress his "sensei" with an impromptu appearance. Ninja turns to see Tom and the stick and warns him to back away, yet Tom has his mind set and winds up with the kendo stick. He connects squarely in the forehead, however that forehead belonging to Ninja. Having misjudged his spacing, he has accidentally clocked Ninja. Rudo drops for a cover and earns the pin-fall victory. Tom only stands in shock, unsure of what went wrong. As Warfare cuts to break, we see an incensed Ninja literally "smacking" some sense into Tom's oh so thick skull. Flying Frenchie vs. Hiroshima Red lights flood the arena to announce the arrival of Hiroshima of the FTfWo. He lumbers towards the ring, glowering at the fans, announcers, and ring attendants. His lighting treatment ends when "Juke-Joint Jezebel" starts booming, bringing out the fWo World Champion in this non-title bout. The Flying Frenchie receives a mixed reaction from the Nashville crowd, as they clearly want to see Hiroshima pay for the crimes of his associates. Frenchie sneers at the fans anyway, shoving his belt in the face of any man stupid enough to want to slap hands with him. Frenchie drops the belt off with the time keeper, and climbs into the ring to face Hiroshima. The bell rings, and Hiroshima charges Frenchie, hoping to bring him down with a lariat. Frenchie ducks under Hiroshima's extended arm and applies a rear waistlock. He tries to bring Hiroshima down with a German suplex, but the larger blocks it, and forces Frenchie backwards into the corner. A smattering of elbows to Frenchie's head causes him to let go, and Hiroshima starts to attack Frenchie in the corner with a series of palm strikes. When he relents, Frenchie stumbles out of the corner and falls face-first to the mat. Hiroshima measures the champ before dropping a knee, which finds its mark. Hiroshima hooks the leg, but Frenchie kicks out at one. Hiroshima pulls Pierre up by his beret, before chopping him back down to the mat. Sensing that this may be his moment, he brings Frenchie back to his feet, then goes behind him and locks him in a full nelson, in preparation for the Oblivion dragon suplex. While he cinches in the full nelson, Primetime and Courage of The French Foundation make their way to ringside, to observe the action. Hiroshima hesitates a second too long after seeing them, and is caught by a backwards kick to the groin from The Flying Frenchie. Doubled over, Hiroshima quickly falls prey to a face buster. Frenchie then heads up top, and flies off with the Guillotine Legdrop to the back of Hiroshima's head. Instead of covering, Frenchie points towards his partner and Trapped opponent, Primetime, and brings Hiroshima back to his feet, positioning him for a powerbomb. He can't quite get Hiroshima up, and the move is countered into a backdrop by the savvy veteran. Frenchie quickly rolls back to his feet, and goes behind Hiroshima once more. Another rear waistlock is applied, but this time Frenchie hops onto Hiroshima's shoulders, and goes into a Victory Roll. Hiroshima's shoulder's stay down for the requisite three count, giving Frenchie the victory. Angered, Hiroshima looks ready to attack The Flying Frenchie, but is laid out from behind instead, when Primetime clocks him with the World Title. Frenchie asks for the belt back, but Primetime opts to strap it around his waist. His ire raised, Frenchie starts screaming at his teammate, and the two start to shove at each other until Courage breaks it up, and cooler heads prevail. Primetime hands the belt back to Frenchie, and the Foundation head back to their locker room, as Warfare cuts to commercial. She went back to smelling the flowers, obviously in a state of bliss at her new gift. He scratched his head and tried to remember if he had ordered any flowers for Violet... Ice vs. Mitch Wilson Warfare on NBC returned from a set of advertisements to Ice making his way out to a mixed reaction from the crowd. His patented theme song "Crazy Train" played resoundingly throughout the Municipal Auditorium, while Ice paced to the ring methodically. He climbed through the ropes and waited for his opponent of the night to make his way out. Soon thereafter, Mitch Wilson of the FTfWo came out wearing his Wilson Training Academy shirt and appeared to be focused on the task at hand. A big win over Ice would further validate his number one contender's position to the fWo Internet Title. Wilson climbed in and his eyes didn't leave Ice as the referee signalled for the ring bell. Wilson and Ice locked up in the center of the ring, before Ice gained the advantage into a side headlock. Wilson escaped and positioned himself into a waistlock, before Ice did a go-behind and grasped Wilson from behind with his arms that could be compared to the side of tree trunks. Wasting no time Ice quickly took Wilson to the canvas with a German Suplex, before standing up and staring cockily down at the quicker Wilson. Wilson shook off the cobwebs before getting to a vertical base, and the two men locked up once more. Again, Ice got the advantage this time with a hammerlock, and again Wilson countered with a go-behind into a waistlock. Ice countered into a waistlock of his own, but this time Wilson escaped with a mule kick that sent shockwaves throughout Ice' body. With Ice bent over, Wilson went on the attack with a normal DDT. Wilson brought Ice up and hit him with several chops before backing him into the corner, and nailing him with a couple of forearms. He whipped him into the opposite corner but Ice reversed it, and instead Wilson smashed into the turnbuckles. Ice charged in but Wilson used the ropes to elevate himself and this left Ice in a bad way as he was hit with both of Wilson' boots. Ice bent over and from behind Wilson came off the second rope with a bulldog. Wilson covered, but Ice got a shoulder up at two. Wilson continued, this time with a kneedrop, followed by a reverse chinlock to slow down the pace of the match. Moments passed by as Wilson worked Ice over, before the crowd rallied behind Ice and Ice began to get up while Wilson kept the hold intact. Ice attempted to break the hold with several elbows to the ribs, and finally did. Wilson released the hold and protected his stomach area, before Ice bounced off the ropes and charged at Wilson. Wilson however ducked the lariat attempt and spun Ice around before going for the kill. Three forearm smashes later and Wilson locked in the Inverted Russian Legsweep, before he released because Eron had appeared seemingly out of nowhere on the ring apron. Wilson approached Eron cautiously, and from behind Ice charged and used his shoulder to nail Wilson in the back. This propelled Wilson in the direction of Eron, and the two men collided as Eron went flying off the ring apron while Wilson bounced off the ropes and staggered back into the ring. Ice spun him around and hit an overhead belly to belly suplex, before going up to the top rope and diving off with a Flying Headbutt. He pinned Wilson, but at the count of two Wilson managed to kick out. Deciding he better go for the kill quickly, Ice hit the Icepick, chokeslamming Wilson into oblivion. Eron got up and laughed as Ice got the three count, in large part due to the interference on the part of the Internet Champion. Eron slid into the ring and began attacking Wilson, before bringing him up and throwing him over the top rope onto the floor. The two men fought on the outside, but Wilson gained the advantage. Out from the crowd came Perfect into the ring, and he spun Ice around and hit the Perfect Game, his patented finisher. Up on the stage, Wilson took Eron out with the inverted Russian legsweep, before heading backstage. Perfect basked in the glory having taken out his most hated rival, but his celebration didn't last for long as Collins appeared and a ruckus began. Ice soon recovered and join the battle, before a sea of a dozen or more security members came down to intervene, separating the three men as the crowd booed and chanted "let them go!" repeatedly. Warfare headed to another set of advertisements by the good folks at NBC. Result: Ice by Pinfall Food Troubles Backstage Kid Cool looked around himself, and smiled at what he saw. Catering. "Ah, but there's nothing better than plates full of free food..." He dove at the table, shoving big quantities of food into his mouth. He hadn't eaten in over a day, and needed to get his strength up in time for Trapped. The Cruiserweight match was going to be tough, and for some reason he was now having to face Ultra Violet in the match as well as Courage. Kid Cool wasn't sure how that came about, but he was sure that it was Cactus Jon's fault somehow. In reality, all of the problems of the world can be traced back to Cactus Jon. Kid Cool continued to scarf at the food, shoving doughnuts and sticks of cheese into his mouth. He was gorging himself too fast he knew, but if he didn't hurry then the food might get up and walk away like it was always doing in those California grapes commercials. Kid Cool wondered if the food continued to sing jazz tunes in the sewers and decided that they didn't. After the trip through the digestive system and out the other end they were likely too traumatized. He reached for a slab of ham and his eyes fell upon a small sign. "FOOD CATERED FOR COLLINS AND RIDERS" His eyes went wide. It occurred to Kid Cool that the food wasn't for him. He supposed he should have thought about that earlier, but it made sense that his food would be left inside Scott Collins' dressing room. Sure he had to sneak in through the ceiling tiles, but isn't that how catering usually worked? Kid Cool suddenly heard footsteps outside and froze. The handle to the door started to jiggle as whoever was on the other side was using their key to gain entry. Kid Cool looked around himself frantically. He quickly spit out the food he still had in his mouth, and tried to reform it into the shape of fruit and cheese sticks. He was just getting ready to hide under the table when the door opened, and Scott Collins, War, Stone and Plague walked into the room. Kid Cool smiled at them, and waved. A piece of celery fell from his chin to the floor. Collins looked at the others. Stone shrugged. Collins smiled. The door shut. A second later, passersby in the halls heard the screams of what sounded like an aardvark being run over with a lawnmower. Mad Trucker vs. Machina The WarfareScreen went to a live feed of a local Starbucks. The "Battle In A Coffee House" match was about to begin, and the Mad Trucker was already seated at the counter of the coffee shop, having himself his ninth cup of freshly brewed Starbucks coffee. The Trucker sighed with pleasure as he downed the coffee, marveling at the freshly roasted taste. The door to the coffee house opened, and Machina stood at the door with his arms behind his back, surveying the peaceful and yet very stylish scene before him. Machina smiled, and revealed what he had been holding behind his back. A cup of Lipton Tea. The patrons and the Trucker gasped as Machina raised an eyebrow, and then drank the tea down with one gulp. He then pointed to the woman wearing the stylish green apron behind the counter. "HA! That's right, Lipton tea... fresh, wholesome... and it doesn't cause heartburn or the jitters!" The Trucker jumped to his feet. "You... take... that... BACK!" He lunged at Machina as the match was officially underway. fWo officials locked the doors of the Starbucks, leaving both men trapped inside. Big right hands were thrown as the Trucker pounded the head of Machina, driving him backward into the stylish tables that had been set up throughout the restaurant. Machina struggled to get to his feet as the Mad Trucker spotted some copies of Doonsbury books that were for sale in Starbucks. He grabbed them and raised them over his head. Machina screamed, "No! Not that tired and unfunny cartoon strip!" The Mad Trucker flung the books at Machina and he yelped as he got out of the way in the nick of time. Back on his feet Machina ran to the other side of the shop looking for weapons. As he searched he yelled, "Coffee is just a conspiracy set forward by the Columbian cartel to try and hook the American populace by forcing them to drink a substance that leads to bad breath and shaky hands! With the US Army unable to aim a rocket correctly the invasion of the country will begin!" The Mad Trucker circled Machina and laughed at this claim. "Oh? What about your tea, nancy boy? Overpriced by the Asian economy, tea has long been a secret hippie source of hiding the wacky weed in with other ingredients and calling it an herbal supplement! It's just a facade for the pinko dictatorship that encourages marijuana usage!" Machina spat. "Well, DUH!" He spotted a row of expensive expresso machines and picked one up. "Hey Trucker... mocha this!" He tossed the equipment at the Trucker, striking the big man in the chest. The Trucker shrugged it off however, and advanced on Machina. "HA! Your fancy smancy coffee maker doesn't work on me... I'm not one of those foam drinkin' coffee nancys, I drink only the hard stuff... real coffee! The stuff you can find at truck stops that allow me to drive all night right through 'till mornin'!" Machina snapped his fingers. "Curses!" The Trucker grabbed Machina by the neck, and pulled him close. Machina struggled to get away, but the Trucker locked in a sleeper hold. "See, boy? If you drank a real drink you might be able to stay awake now... but your tea can't help you with this sleeper hold!" Machina struggled, but to no use. He started to fade, and the Trucker laughed. "Your tea can't save you know fancy pants!" Machina choked, and slowly he said, "True... but... one thing... you forgot... Trucker! You see... tea... satisfies your thirst... with only one cup... whereas your coffee... takes... more cups... and with more fluid..." The Trucker continued to lock in the hold, but seemed to be feeling uncomfortable. He shifted from foot to foot, and bit his bottom lip. Machina was nearly out, but continued speaking. "With... more fluid... and with... the way coffee... is harder to hold in... your bladder... you have to... go to the bathroom... more... often!" The Trucker was now showing serious signs of discomfort, and Machina summoned the last ounce of his strength to make running water noises with his mouth. The Trucker tried to hold on, but finally screamed and let go of the hold. He ran to the bathroom door, and found it locked. He pounded on it, and Machina cleared his throat behind him. The Trucker turned and saw what he feared most of all. Machina had the key to the bathroom. He smiled, and tossed it on the floor in front of him. The Trucker dove for it, and as his hands reached the key Machina quickly jumped on top of him and made a cover. The referee counted to three, and Machina leapt into the air the winner. Screaming he said, "You can take away our dignity... but you can't take away... OUR TEA!" Result: Machina by Pinfall Mending Time Ric Chronos wandered the halls of the arena, searching for signs of Fallen. The relative newcomer to the fWo was regarded with suspicion from many that he passed, although they gave him a wide berth. Many things about the superstar were unknown, and his association with Fallen only cast more suspicion over his intentions in the fWo. Ric Chronos ignored all of this, and continued his search. His path took him deeper into the arena, and soon he found himself opening the door to the cellar far beneath the main seating area. He spotted Fallen's bag propped up against a corner and considered it. He has joined the fWo for a variety of reasons, but when he had discovered that his old acquaintance John Skyler was among the employed superstars he found himself sidetracked into getting him out of his depression and back into the swing of things. Of course, John Skyler was in many ways right to feel depressed. After all, his fellow wrestlers hadn't done a thing to help him over the years, but that was all in the past. Ric Chronos would fix things... and he had all the time in the world to make it so. Movement came to the right, and Ric turned. "John." Fallen stood, still cloaked heavily in the shadows, and regarded Ric quietly. After a moment, Ric spoke again. "John, we've got a match. You and I against those two who helped hold you down. Guano Apes... a strange team. But we can beat them... you just have to trust me." Fallen was quiet for a moment, and then stepped forward from the shadows, and into the light. Matt Douglas vs. Oracle Oracle solemnly makes his way to the ring to a strong negative response. Upon sliding in Oracle climbs the turnbuckle and raises his arms, bathing himself in the boos of the fans. The former Dreamer hops off the corner and waits in anticipation for Matt Douglas' entrance. The fWo International Champion's theme music begins and Douglas jogs his way down to the ring, playing to the crowd as he goes. Douglas hands his belt off to the referee who signals for the bell. The two men stare at each other for a few seconds before locking up in the center of the ring. Oracle pushes the smaller Douglas to the ropes and whips him across the ring. Douglas returns and dodges a clothesline from Oracle with a crucifix rolled up into a quick pin. Oracle, stunned by the suddenness of Douglas' maneuver, barely manages a kickout. Douglas bounces back off the ropes and nails Oracle with a sharp elbow causing Oracle to stumble back. Douglas shoves the challenger into the corner and delivers a series of kicks to him. As Oracle gasps for air Douglas wraps his arm with Oracle's and hiptosses him out of the corner. Oracle rolls to his feet, but Douglas brings him right back down with an Asai moonsault off the top rope. Matt holds on for the pin but can only get a two count. After Oracle slips out once again, Douglas picks him up by his hair and pushes him back into the turnbuckle. Douglas measures him up and lands three repeat knife-edge chops that ignite red marks on Oracle's chest. Douglas attempts to whip Oracle across the ring, but Oracle reverses the throw and nails the champ with a short lariat. Douglas goes end over end before landing on the back of his neck. Oracle drops down for the quick pin, but Matt kicks out at one and a half. Oracle picks up Douglas and sends him back to the ropes, then brings him crashing down with a power slam that draws a two count.
