Introduction In the background music played from a portable stereo. Fans walked to the arena in pairs, some in larger groups. From the alley, a man watched with a silent, unknown expression. The fans didn't see him, but he saw them. He looked until he spotted one, a man with a black t-shirt. On it a single letter... 'f'. The F was circled in a rough line, and the man nodded with satisfaction. He whistled, and the man in the t-shirt looked over at him. Then he headed over, his eyes never wavering. Never darting from side to side. Never cautious. The man watched as he approached. Then he spotted it. Recognition. The fan stopped. "I thought..." "You thought nothing," said the man. In his hand, a smoke bomb. "Take this." The fan took it, and his face cracked the faintest smile before it returned to the stoic expression it was supposed to. "So it's true." "It's true," the man said. "Take it, and use it after the show. Make a show of your own." "Make a show of my own." That's right, thought the man. "Make a show of your own." Introducing: The New Eight "Ladies and gentlemen... ladies and gentlemen... settle, please, settle..." Ted Tallison looked at the fans seated around him, turning in a slow circle to take them all in. It wasn't what he had hoped for exactly... when he had the dreams about being back in the ring, but it was close enough. With the suit, which was far too expensive for his salary, the microphone which was his to command... Leadership. Life was wonderful. Or at least, it would be if people would respect the authority of the office. That particular bane was really Dudley's... if he hadn't fouled up the whole position so badly perhaps it would be considered more honorable in the minds of the fans. It was hard to come in after an attempted murderer on the run from the law... even if Tallison contained the skills needed to be both an effective and well-loved leader. It was really just baggage of the office, and he had to suffer through it. Sooner or later, people would realize that he was a much different person from Dudley and come to respect him as much as Silver Fox. Perhaps even more. Actually, likely even more... thought Tallison. After all, he wasn't a fruit. "One year ago... roughly one year ago...," but they still were not listening well. The crowds seemed more rowdy lately... harder to control. The families were starting to vanish, replaced by men in their late-teens, twenty something fans who were quick to scream, chant, and show real emotion. Tallison smiled, taking it as a sign of his mark on the company. Things were changing, and he was showing his impact by moving the company into this new direction. Besides, the older fans had more disposable income to take advantage of. He didn't recognize most of the shirts... but he smiled. A lot of the shirts were fWo ones. "One year ago I brought several new faces into the fWo. It was to start an injection of new talent... new types... new people into our roster. This year, the tradition continues with eight new wrestling superstars set to make an impact on this sport. These eight men, brought up from our developmental leagues, are here to become the fWo stars of tomorrow... but only four will pass your test. Tonight all eight of these new superstars will be introduced to you. Next week we will have four matches... and after the conclusion of next week's Monday Night Ruahh, you the fans will go to fWoWrestling.com, or one of our local internet sites or blogs, and vote on the four members you want to see remain in the fWo. These four men will be offered fWo contracts... while the other four will return to the developmental leagues to refine their skills." The crowd cheered at the announcement... bringing in new blood was always an easy pop." "Are you ready to meet them? Well then... let's get this show started!" Tallison gestured toward the FanTron, and as it flickered to life, the first of the new eight appeared...
