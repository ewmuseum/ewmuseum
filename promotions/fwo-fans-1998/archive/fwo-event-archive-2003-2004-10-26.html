fWo Events In 2003 the fWo begins the year struggling to balance the difficulties of a new and largely untested roster, escaping from the shadow of problems that plagued the fWo in 2002, and to make matters worse expelled from their home territory of the United States due to a series of events coming together at the close of the previous year. Working an aggressive road schedule, the fWo spent the year rebuilding to former glory and meeting unforeseen challenges from within and without. In no other year of the fWo's history were outside political factors as important or impactful.
