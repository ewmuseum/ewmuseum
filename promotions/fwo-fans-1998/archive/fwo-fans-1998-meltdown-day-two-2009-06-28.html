Coda A montage of moving images faded in and out on the FanTron as the lights dimmed. "It has been no secret that Mr. Braun was suffering from cancer for over a year, but his his progress in recent weeks was promising. While the majority of fans have been anxiously awaiting our tag team battle royal spectacular, Let’s take you to how this all started… Trust Ric Chronos and Ivy McGinnis walked through the bowels of the Key Arena with purpose. Ivy's steps were quick and measured, and the much taller Chronos was practically jogging to keep up with her. How she was moving so quickly, so evenly, in platform boots, was beyond him. "I hope everyone came," said Ric, breaking the silence. "They will," replied Ivy, "Either by loyalty or curiosity, everyone is going to be there." They passed few people on their journey; the others were busily preparing for the night's massive event. Most of the crew didn't know Ivy had come back, but she was looking to change that as quickly as possible. Arriving at their destination, Ric opened the door and gestured her to enter first. It was a small conference room, out of the way and rarely used. Ivy's gaze went from face to face; though confident, she was glad that everyone she'd asked to show up was here. Mike Bear. The Flying Frenchie. Alias. Karina Wolfenden. Impulse. K-Wolf and Alias were talking quietly among themselves, Pierre immediately eyed her and Ric when they walked in, and Impulse was near the back of the room, keeping an eye on everyone, though Ivy could read the look on his face: "These are all current and former World Champions, what am I doing here?" "Glad you guys came," said Ivy, as she sat on the small table against the wall, "and yes - before you ask, I'm back." Ric stood by the door, his arms folded across his chest. Alias raised his hand. "Really, Chris?" asked Ivy, and the room chuckled. "Keepin' with tradition," said Alias, "Why are we here?" "Good question," replied Ivy, "You're here because you're trusted. Either I've known you long enough and consistently enough to trust you myself," as she gestured to Alias and the Frenchie, "or people in this room trust you implicitly." She continued, "Plus, you guys have all had direct dealings with Killjoy - except you, Mark, but I know you've got Pierre's back." Mike Bear leaned forward, about to say something, but Pierre put a hand on his shoulder and shook his head. "Last night, Killjoy lost a match to Pierre, and by virtue, has to take off his mask. He hasn't yet, and if I know him like I know I do, he doesn't intend to." "Why?" "Because that's not his style, Knox," she said to Impulse. "So you know who he is?" asked K-Wolf. Ivy nodded slowly. "Then tell us," she continued. "She can't." Before Ivy could even attempt to reply to K-Wolf, everyone's eyes turned to the Flying Frenchie, who had answered Karina's question for her. "Zat's not how zis game is played," he continued, his eyes locked on their hosts at the front of the room, "Zis... agenda zat Killjoy has involves ze FWO, it seems, and therefore has certain rules zat need to be followed." "I think you get it," said Ivy, nodding, "What I'd like is for Killjoy to get unmasked tonight in the Meltdown match. That'll force him to act directly with this agenda he's got instead of these pointless mind games and allusions to everything else." "What makes you think that would change anything?" asked Impulse. "Trust me," replied Ivy, "And that's why I asked you guys to come here - because I trust you, and I'm hoping one of you - any one of you, can take the mask off." There it was: Ivy's request. The five wrestlers all looked from Ivy and Ric to each other; Bear started to speak in hushed tones into the Frenchie's ear. "I don't care who does it," continued Ivy, "If one of you does it, I'll make sure all of you get some kind of reward, and if you can't..." She exhaled. "Then you can't, and it's no problem. All I'd like you guys to do is try." Ivy leaned against the wall, and Ric stepped up. "Okay guys, we've got about an hour to go until the show... kick some ass." Alias and K-Wolf were the first to leave, in heated discussion the entire way. Impulse stopped for a moment in front of Ivy and gave her a hug. "This place missed you, Miss Ivy." "I missed you guys too," replied Ivy, as she smiled. Bear and Frenchie were the last two up, but the Frenchie gestured to his tag team partner to go on without him, as he stepped toward his former tag team partner. "What's up, Pierre?" asked Ivy. "McGinnis, I appreciate what you're asking of us," said Pierre, "but I cannot be a part of it." Ivy looked at Ric, then back at the Frenchie. "What? Why? I thought you'd be all over this." "It... is complicated," replied the Frenchie, "You spoke of trust as ze reason you asked us here zis afternoon, but trust is ze reason I cannot help you. I will not interfere with ze others and whatever zey do, but I cannot take part." "I'm sorry," said Ivy, "but I really don't understand." Pierre's gaze shifted from Ivy to Ric, then back to Ivy. "No, I guess not," he said, "like I said, it is complicated." "We can appreciate that," interjected Ric. "Once you're out there, though, it's your right to change your mind. You won the match - no one would begrudge you for making killjoy fufill the obligations of the match." He nodded at both of them and left the room. "I don't get it," said Ivy, "That's not the Pierre we know." "Maybe not the Frenchie that you know," replied Ric, "but Pierre is a bit deeper than most people see, and I know him better than anyone. Whatever's the problem, he'll work through it pretty quickly, I think." "Yeah," said Ivy, as she stared out the door, "I hope so." So Goes the Circle...
