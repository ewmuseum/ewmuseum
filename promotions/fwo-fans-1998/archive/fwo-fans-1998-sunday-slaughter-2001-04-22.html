Sunday Slaughter, 22 April 2001

22 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "Damn it!" swore Kodiak Vic Creed as he sped into the parking lot. This just wasn't his day. First, his flight was delayed due to weather. Then he almost wrecked the car trying to get through traffic. But at least he was here, finally. "Good," Vic said as he pulled around the side of the building. "Plenty of time yet to call out that goof Crucifix." He stepped out of the car and walked around to the trunk to retrieve his bag. "It's time for that big idiot to learn the Golden Rule: Don't Mess with the Main Man!" Had he not been so lost in his thoughts, KVC might have heard the footsteps approaching him from behind. He might have heard the swish of the 2x4 being swung. As KVC stuck his key in the trunk lock, the last sound he heard was the crack of the board snapping in half as it struck the back of his head. Crucifix dropped the piece of wood he was holding. He had stuck Creed with such force, that the other piece of the board had lodged itself in the rear window of the car. Stepping back, Crucifix watched Creed's unconscious body side down the trunk of the car and fall to the ground. "The Golden Rule?" A sadistic smile crossed Crucifix's face. "Silly goose, rules were made to be broken." He bent over and grabbed a fistful of Creed's bloody hair. "Broken like your head." Jackhammer/Rottdogg vs. Sphere/Vengence "War?" begins to play, and the crowd comes to its feet as Jackhammer makes his way to the ring. Jackhammer climbs onto the apron and steps into the ring, then awaits his partner. "Pressure Zone" hits, and the crowd gives a mixed reaction to Rottdogg, who comes to the ring without the company of the rest of NXS. He slides into the ring. "The Beast" begins to play, and the mammoth Vengence makes his way to the ring to loud boos, looking, like, really freaking scary and stuff. He stops outside of the ring and waits for his partner. "Back To School" hits, and the booing continues as Sphere makes his way to the ring. Sphere consults with Vengence, and a glare and growl from the big man convinces Sphere to let him start the match. Jackhammer begins for his team, and the two huge men tie up for a test of strength. Jackhammer, one of the few men in the fWo Vengence actually looks up on, begins to gain the advantage, but Vengence cuts him off with a boot to the gut. Vengence slams a forearm down on the back of the head of Jackhammer, knocking him down. Vengence drops a hard elbow on Jackhammer, then brings him back up to his feet. Vengence whips Jackhammer into the ropes, and charges into the opposite ropes. As he comes off, Vengence leaps and blasts Jackhammer with a leaping forearm shot to the head. Jackhammer goes down, and Vengence covers. The ref counts, but Jackhammer kicks out at two. Vengence brings Jackhammer up to his feet again and pastes him with a hard right hand, then whips him into the corner. Vengence backs up and goes for a spear, but Jackhammer dodges out of the corner. Vengence slams into the post, then stumbles back out of the corner. Jackhammer grabs him as he comes out, then lifts him up and drops him with a hanging vertical suplex. Jackhammer then tags out to Rottdogg, who charges into the ring, but gets hit with a drop toe hold by Vengence. He gets up to his feet, holding his nose, and turns around only to be grabbed around the throat by the massive right hand of Vengence. Vengence lifts Rottdogg up, then chokeslams him to the mat. Vengence walks over to his corner, but Sphere is taunting some fans. Vengence, annoyed, slaps him on the back, then pulls him backwards into the ring. Sphere turns to confront Vengence, but is rolled up from behind by Rottdogg. This gets two, but Sphere manages to kick out. As they get up, Sphere nails Rottdogg with a dropkick to the jaw. Rottdogg goes down, and Sphere brings him back up to his feet. Sphere headbutts Rottdogg, then backs up, and nails him with a superkick. Sphere covers, but Rottdogg kicks out at two. Sphere brings him back up again, and whips him into the ropes. Rottdogg rolls to the side of Vengence, right into his corner, where he tags in Jackhammer. Sphere begs off as Jackhammer hits the ring, but Jackhammer charges at him. Sphere takes advantage, grabbing Jackhammer by the tights and pulling him into the corner where Jackhammer hits his head on the turnbuckles. Sphere gets up, and is distracted by Vengence, who yells at him to tag him in. Sphere refuses, shoves Vengence petulantly, and brings Jackhammer up. Vengence, pissed, steps over the top rope into the ring and spins Sphere around. He then lifts him up, and drops him with the Eclipse. Vengence spits on Sphere, then leaves him for Jackhammer. Jackhammer, never one to not take advantage of a situation when it's presented to him, picks up Sphere and executes the HammerFall. He covers, and gets the pinfall. On the apron, Rottdogg turns to the crowd to celebrate, having though he played no real part in it won his first match in a good while. Vengence, still in the ring, sees him. For no real good reason, he blasts Rottdogg in the back of the head with the Stargazer, knocking him off the apron into the guardrail, which Rottdogg slams into chin-first. Rottdogg slumps, unconscious, and Vengence leaves the ring area. Inside the ring, Jackhammer celebrates, having scored a pinfall over Sphere going into their CyberSlam matchup... Result: Jackhammer/Rottdogg by pinfall Answers Fans of the fWo had more than likely never seen Aimee Horihito like this. They had seen her as the confident, self-assured wrestler; as the sly, conniving woman plotting to ruin the East Coast Laws for an as of yet unknown party or parties; but never as the angry, flustered woman she was now. She was standing in Chris Universal's office, clad in a leather jacket and leather pants, and an angry look. "Chris, when you brought me back, I had a few plans of my own, but this was not among them! I want to know, and I want to know right the hell now what happened last week?! I want answers!" Universal studied her, calmly. This was becoming a great skill of his keeping calm in the face of someone else's rage. "Aimee, I honestly can't tell you. I don't even know why you're asking me to be honest. Obviously you were working to ruin the Laws, for whatever reason, and obviously they found out about it. That's all there is to it. Life goes on after a diabolical scheme falls through lord knows I know that. You're just gonna have to live with it." "I don't believe this! They betray me last week, and all you can tell me is live with it?!" "Uh, Aimee, betray? If you didn't notice, you were trying to ruin them. You're not exactly an innocent bystander here. You play with fire, you get burned. You tried to ruin the Laws, you tried to screw the Laws, they caught you. My advice? Deal with it." Aimee stomped the floor, then turned and left in a huff. Universal leaned back in his chair. "Ah, it's good to know that the talent has a sense of morality." Sick And Tired "I'm getting sick and tired of this, guys," said Judge Steel. Markus Cairn and Mini-Rottdogg nodded. "I mean, he gives us all this crap about winning, then goes out and gets bitchslapped every week? Sure, he won this time, but he had nothing to do with it. As if that weren't enough, he gets knocked out after the match by the biggest jobber outside of himself and possibly the STUMP Squad in the fWo. It's ri-goddamn-diculous." At that moment, the door opened, and Rottdogg stepped in. His jaw was swollen, blood was running down his chin, and a close inspection of his mouth revealed that a front tooth or two was missing. In short, he was not a pretty picture. Worked up into a fury, Judge Steel turned on him. "You're pathetic, Kevin! You got punked out out there, again! You're lucky Jackhammer was your partner and Vengence was Sphere's, or you'd have lost, again! You made NXS look like crap, again! You are such an ass!" Rottdogg's eyes bulged. He couldn't believe this was happening. "What the hell are you trying to say, Steel? You haven't done better yourself, if you haven't noticed! Get it together, man! If you just focus and concentrate on winning, we can be NXS again the real NXS! The NXS that fooled, trapped, and bitchslapped Scott Slugger! SCOTT SLUGGER! You remember those days, Steel? Or do you even care? Do you care about anything other than cruising in the Civic and picking up chicks? No wait, that's a stupid question, I already know the answer. You DON'T, and I'm sick of it!" Judge Steel, angered by Rottdogg's explosion, exploded himself. "You wanna back those words up, pal?! How about at CyberSlam! You against all three of us! You got the balls?" "You better believe I do," growled Rottdogg. "Maybe I can beat a little sense into you, maybe not but you better believe I'm gonna beat the hell out of you." With that, he turned, slamming the door behind him on Steel, Cairn, and Mini-Rottdogg and possibly NXS, for good. Eddy Fate vs. Black Quicksilver "Slave To The Grind" begins to play, and the fans cheer as Black Quicksilver makes his way to the ring with STUMP. BQ reaches the ring and sets STUMP in the corner, the catapults himself into the ring. "You Can't Deny Fate" is heard, followed by "Beautiful People". The fans erupt with cheers as Eddy Fate begins to make his way to the ring, looking focused for his matchup with the five-time fWo Cruiserweight Champion. Fate slides into the ring and offers a hand to BQ, who takes it. They then begin to circle each other, and the referee calls for the bell. They lock up, and Fate gains the advantage with a side headlock. BQ pushes him off into the ropes. As Fate comes off, BQ leapfrogs him. Fate bounces off the opposite ropes, then leapfrogs BQ. Fate stops short behind Quicksilver, and as BQ turns, hooks him up and drops him to the mat with a snap suplex. Fate covers, but only gets one. Both men get back up to their feet, and Fate goes for a kick. BQ catches his foot, but Fate counters with an enzuiguri, nailing BQ. BQ drops, and Fate covers. He again gets a two-count. Fate brings BQ up and scoops him up, then slams him to the mat. Fate then runs into the ropes, leaps onto the second strand, and leaps off with an asai moonsault that nails BQ. He stays on for the cover, but again only gets two. Fate brings BQ back up to his feet and hooks him up for a suplex, but BQ falls and lands behind Eddy. BQ leaps onto Fate's shoulders, then drops down into a victory roll. Fate manages a kickout at two. Both men quickly get back up, and BQ armdrags Fate. Fate quickly gets back up, and catches BQ with a mexican armdrag. BQ kips up and takes Fate down with a japanese armdrag. Fate kips up, and both men leap into the air, going for a dropkick. They both, of course, miss and land on the mat, then quickly get up into a defensive position. The crowd applauds, and both men get up. They approach each other warily, then Fate catches BQ unsuspecting, takes him down with a double-leg-trip, and jackknifes forward into a pinfall. The ref counts two, then BQ rolls forward into a sunset flip position. This gets two, then Fate reaches forward and flips BQ into a sunset flip position of his own. A two-count, and BQ rolls forward. Another two-count, and Fate kicks out. Both men get up, going right back into a defensive position, and the crowd applauds again. Both men look at each other, each having clearly underestimated the other, then lock up again. This time BQ gains the advantage with a side headlock, but Fate counters out of it with a back suplex, then bridges up. BQ kicks out at two. Fate brings BQ up to his feet, but BQ rolls him up in a small package. Fate manages to kick out at two. As they both get back up, BQ nails Fate with a hard chop to the chest, backing him up. BQ sets to whip Fate into the ropes, but Fate reverses. As BQ comes off, he spins around Fate and drops him with a Cubic Zirconium Cutter. BQ covers, but Fate just manages to get a shoulder up at two. BQ waits for Fate to get up, then measures him and pastes him with a Super-Quick Silver-Kick. BQ covers again, but Fate again kicks out at two. BQ steps out onto the ring apron and waits for Fate to get up. When he does, BQ slingshots himself onto the top rope, then leaps off and wraps his legs around Fate's head for a slingshot hurricanrana. Fate holds on, however, and powerbombs BQ to the mat. He holds on, lifts BQ up again, and powerbombs him to the mat a second time. With BQ down, Fate heads to the top rope facing the crowd. He then flies off and nails BQ with a picture-perfect moonsault. Fate hooks a leg, but somehow BQ kicks out at two. Undaunted, Fate brings BQ up and goes behind him, setting him up for a german suplex. Fate drops BQ with the suplex, then brings him back up and takes him down with a second. Fate tries a third rolling german, but BQ flips and lands on his feet behind Fate. Fate turns around, and BQ goes for a kick. Fate catches his foot, spins him around, and hooks his head. He then drops him with the Twist of Fate, just like that. Three seconds later, Fate has secured a big victory going into CyberSlam. Fate steps up onto the top turnbuckle and extends his arms to the crowd as Slaughter goes to commercial... Result: Eddy Fate by pinfall Congratulations Slaughter came back from commercial to see Black Quicksilver (with STUMP) entering the STUMP Squad locker room, which was occupied by Mike Heftel. Heftel, who had been lounging around aimlessly, got up and welcomed BQ. "Hey, man! Congrats on that great loss you had." BQ nodded. "It was one of my better jobs, for sure. I can only hope to top it at CyberSlam." Heftel frowned. "No way, Sean. You know, and I know, that I'm gonna set new standards for jobbing at CyberSlam." "Oh yeah?" said BQ. "Yeah!" "Oh yeah!" "Yeah!" "Oh yea " "..." interrupted STUMP, saving us all from what likely would have been an extended session of Oh yeah-yeahs. "Yeah, you're right, STUMP, as usual," said BQ. "We should save it for the pay-per-view," added Heftel. "The pay-per-view where I'm going to job harder than you ever have in your life," added BQ nonchalantly. "Oh yeah?" said Heftel. "Yeah!" "Oh yeah?" "Yeah!" As the two continued to argue, Slaughter mercifully left the scene... Darkness vs. Ruben Ross Complete and total darkness. "Are you afraid of the dark?" "Enter Sandman." And with that, Darkness and Chris Finn emerge from the gloom. Darkness is a fearsome sight indeed, seeming to blend in and out of the shadows and black and back again. The fans boo loudly, but he doesn't seem to notice. He climbs up the ring steps, stops, and the lights slowly go back on. Darkness steps into the ring, with Finn at his customary spot at ringside. "Bulls On Parade" hits, and the fans pop like mad as Ruben Ross makes his way to the ring with the Internet Title around his waist. Ross unstraps the belt as he reaches the ring and hands it off to a ring attendant. He climbs into the ring and goes right after Darkness, charging after him. Darkness dodges, and Ross goes into the ropes. As Ross bounces off, Darkness takes him down with a powerslam. Darkness covers, but Ross kicks out at two. Darkness brings Ross up, but Ross fires back with a series of rapid-fire fists to the midsection and face. Darkness stumbles back, and Ross nails him with a clothesline, taking him down to the mat. Ross drops a quick leg, then covers. It only gets two. Ross brings Darkness back up and takes him down to the mat with a hard suplex, then nails him with an asai moonsault. He reaches forward and hooks a leg, but Darkness kicks out at the count of two. Ross brings Darkness up and whips him into the ropes, but Darkness reverses it. He makes the mistake of putting his head down, and Ross takes advantage by hooking him up, lifting him, and spiking him with a cradle piledriver. Ross brings Darkness right back up and backs him into a corner, then whips him across the ring into the opposite corner. Ross backs up into the corner, then charges across the ring and nails Darkness with a leaping leg lariat. Darkness stumbles out of the corner, and Ross lands on the ring apron. He immediately slingshots onto the top rope, then dropkicks Darkness in the back of the head, knocking him to the mat. Darkness gets back up, and Ross charges at him and hits a running lariat, knocking him over the top rope to the outside, where he lands on his feet. Darkness takes a second to confer with Chris Finn, but Ross quickly grows tired and charges toward the ropes. He then leaps from the ring with a running no-hands plancha. Unfortunately for Ross, Darkness moves, and yanks Finn out of the way with him. Ross splat-lands on the ground, and Darkness brings him back up and tosses him into the ring. Ross makes his way to his feet, but Darkness charges and nails him with a spear, knocking him to the mat. Looking to finish him off, Darkness brings Ross to his feet and wraps a hand around his throat. Darkness then lifts Ross up and drives him into the mat with a chokeslam. Darkness drops down and makes the cover, but to his shock, Ross manages to kick out at two. Darkness brings Ross up again and drags a thumb across his throat, then whips Ross into the ropes. As Ross comes off, Darkness wraps him up in the sleeper hold. Ross immediately lashes out with a back mule kick that nails Darkness and doubles him over. With Darkness doubled over, Ross hooks him up and drives him into the mat with a Ride The Lightning, then holds on for a pinfall. Darkness just manages to kick out at two. Ross brings Darkness up and goes behind him, then plants him into the mat with a Paint It Black. Ross again holds on for the pinfall, but Darkness manages to kick out. Still, Ross feels he's weakened him enough to go for the Plague of Darkness, and that's exactly what he does. Ross lifts Darkness up in the suplex position. As he does so, out of the corner of his eye he sees Erik Kelly emerge from the crowd and grab the Internet Title belt. Ross turns, and the momentary distraction allows Darkness to slip from his grasp, land behind him, and apply the sleeper. Ross begins to fade, and the last thing he sees is Erik Kelly exiting through the crowd, the Internet Title belt back in his possession and pointed at the ring and Ross. The ref raises Ross' arm three times, and it falls all three times. The ref declares Darkness the winner, and Darkness releases the limp body of Ross. Darkness then leaves the ring, Chris Finn behind him, looking completely ready for his battle with the Boston Strangler at CyberSlam... Result: Darkness by submission Lesson Vic awoke with a yell as the searing pain rose from the back of his head. It felt as if his entire skull was on fire. Crucifix stood over him, dusting his hands off. "Salt," he said. "Thought I'd spice up that cut on the back of your head." "You're about three seconds away from the ugliest beating of your life," spat Creed. He tried to get up but couldn't. He was being restrained. Turning his head, he saw he had been strapped to something, held by thick leather straps. A crucifix. He was strapped to a big wooden crucifix. "You sick bastard," said Creed as he struggled against his bindings. He looked around, trying to see where Crucifix had gone. Vic didn't recognize the room he was in, but judging by the pipes and the size of the room it was probably the arena basement. "Where are you, freak? You'd better be gone because when I get out of here..." Crucifix reappeared. He sat his full three hundred plus pounds on Vic's chest, his hands behind his back. "Such hostility. I've seen you do this very type of ambush on other people. Guess it's not so much fun when it's done to you." "You teaching me a lesson? Is that what this is?" Vic stared at Crucifix with murder in his eyes. "Oh you'll be learning something all right. It will be a lesson you won't soon forget." Crucifix brought his hands forward. "I have a present for you." In his hands were a hammer and two long nails. Vic's huge arms strained against the leather straps. "Look buddy, I don't know what you're planning on doing with those, but if you've got an ounce of brains in that head of yours you'd rethink it," said Vic through gritted teeth. "A champion's bravado." Crucifix nodded. "But a most undeserving champion. What do you really know about pain? I've seen you inflict it on others, but you really don't know what it's like to feel that kind of pain. Until now." Vic's body thrashed around, his legs kicking wildly. Crucifix stood up and walked over to Vic's left arm. "You sonofabitch!" Vic yelled. "I'll rip your heart out!" Crucifix held the nail to his lips. "Shhhh. Quiet now. I want your full attention on what you're about to feel. It won't be pleasant, but it will make you a better person. Until I take that belt from you at Cyberslam." "Somebody! Help!" yelled the helpless Hardcore champion as Crucifix placed the nail between the fingers of Vic's clenched fist. Creed could feel the sharp metal point touch the palm of his hand. The straps were tight; he couldn't pull away. He could only watch as Crucifix raised the hammer... then brought it down. Creed howled as his own blood sprayed up his arm from the hole punched in his hand. It felt as if he'd been struck by a lighting bold of pain. He couldn't see Crucifix. All he could see was the top portion of the nail sticking out of his hand. "Now you know pain," Crucifix said in a loud whisper. "But the lesson isn't over. Oh look! I still have one more nail!" The darkened room was flooded with light as the door was kicked in. Security flooded into the room, their flashlights shining on the fWo Hardcore champion. Many of them cringed at the sight of Creed's impaled hand. One nearly lost his lunch. "Someone get a doctor!" one of the security guards yelled. "Get him!" Vic weakly yelled out, his body drained of energy. "Get that bastard!" "Get who?" a security guard asked as he knelt down to undo the leather straps. "There's nobody in here but you." Unconsciousness soon followed. Havok/East Coast Laws vs. Black Panther/Ruin "Awake" begins to play, and the fans cheer as Havok makes his way to the ring. Havok slides in and waits for his partners. "Push Upstairs" begins, and the fans give a mixed reaction though one that's tending towards cheers to the East Coast Laws, the fWo World Tag Team Champions who gained a lot of fan sympathy last week after foiling Aimee Horihito's plot. The Laws hand their belts off, enter the ring, and join Havok in waiting for their opponents. "Du Hast" begins, and the fans boo loudly as the Black Panther makes his way to the ring. Panther steps into the ring and takes a spot in the corner opposite his opponents. "My Ruin" begins, and the fans give another mixed reaction this one tending towards boos for Ruin, who hit the ring and join Panther. Panther and Juno Law are elected to start for their teams, and Panther quickly shows off his size and power advantage by catching Juno in the middle of a cross-body block as the bell rings. Panther then hurls Juno over his shoulder with a fallaway slam. Panther brings Juno up and whips him into the ropes, then catches him with a big lariat as he comes off. Panther covers, but Juno kicks out at two. Panther brings Juno back up and whips him into the corner, then charges in with a lariat. Only problem is, Juno moves. Panther crashes sternum-first into the corner, and Juno blasts him with a dropkick, knocking him into the corner. He then whips the Panther across the ring. As Panther stumbles out of the opposite corner, Juno charges across and nails him with a flying forearm. Juno covers, but Panther kicks out at two. Juno tags out to Kobi, and the brothers lift the bigger Panther up and take him down with a double suplex. Kobi covers, but Panther kicks out at two with authority. Kobi brings Panther up, but Panther pastes him with a right, then takes him down with a double-underhook suplex. Unfortunately for Panther, this lands Kobi right in his corner, where he tags in Havok. Havok hits the ring red-hot, wanting to get a piece of Panther. He knocks Panther down repeatedly with a series of right hands, then whips him into the ropes. However, he misses the Panther's blind tag to Frank Snow. As Panther comes off, Havok leaps up and goes for a hurricanrana. Panther catches him, however, and falls back, dropping Havok on his face. At the same time, Snow leaps off the top rope with an elbowdrop that hits Havok squarely. Snow rolls him over and covers, but Havok kicks out at two. Snow brings Havok up, drops him with an inverted atomic drop, then runs him over with a monstrous lariat. Snow covers, but Havok again kicks out at two. Snow brings him back up again and kicks him in the gut, doubling him over. He then scoops Havok up in position and spikes him with an inverted piledriver, driving his head into the mat. Snow then gets up and tags out to Robert Jensen, who mounts the second rope and comes off with a kneedrop to the head. Jensen makes the cover, but Havok manages to kick out at two. Jensen brings Havok up and whips him into the corner, then follows him in with a bodysplash. Havok stumbles out, and Jensen hooks him. He then hits him with a DDT, a non-barbed-wire version of the Make You Bleed. Jensen covers, but the lack of barbed wire does him in as Havok kicks out at two. Jensen brings Havok up, but Havok lashes out with a low blow and rolls to his corner, where he tags in Juno Law. Juno slingshots himself into the ring with a dropkick, nailing Jensen. Jensen gets back up, and Juno hooks his head and drops him with a DDT of his own. Juno then climbs to the top, leaps off, and nails Jensen with the Just Fierce. Juno covers, but Panther breaks it up at two. This brings Havok and Kobi into the ring, as well as Frank Snow. The ref begins to lose control, and Havok and Panther's brawl spills into the ringside area. The ref turns his attention to these two, leaving a perfect opportunity for Damnation to hit the ring. They grab Juno and set him up, then drop him with a double powerbomb. As Jensen gets up, Damnation double-whips him into the ropes, then drops him with a double flapjack. Fortunately for Jensen, he happens to land right on top of Kobi, and when the ref turns back around (with Damnation having exited the ring and headed to the back), he sees Jensen "covering" Kobi. The ref drops down and makes the three-count, and Panther (who is still brawling in the crowd with Havok) and Ruin have stolen one from Havok and the Laws. Ruin celebrates as Slaughter heads backstage to the official Slaughter interview set, where Kristen Jacobs is waiting to interview the fWo's two top cruiserweights... Result: Black Panther/Ruin by pinfall Final Words Kristen Jacobs stood between Greg Davis and Eddy Fate. Fate, having already worked but having had the time to shower, was clad in street clothes. Davis, being close to working in the night's main event, was in his wrestling gear. The set itself was nothing complicated simply an fWo logo against a gray on blue-gray background. Still, it served as a good enough setting for interviews, and that's what it was doing tonight. "Eddy, Greg... you two will be meeting in less than a week at CyberSlam. CyberSlam is the biggest stage in the world, and it's my chance to shine on that stage. This is going to be, unquestionably, the biggest match of my career. But I'm ready for it. I mean, I've already held the Cruiserweight Title for ninety-one days longer than anyone, uh, EVER. You really think this punk is gonna stop me on day ninety-seven? No way, Krissy. At CyberSlam I'm going to do the world a favor, and denature Fate. Bet on it." "There you have it, the final words of the two competitors in the First Blood Cruiserweight Title match at CyberSlam the up-and-coming Eddy Fate, looking to prove himself, and the longest-reigning Cruiserweight Champion in fWo history, Greg Davis, looking to hold on to his title. We'll see who comes out on top... at CyberSlam IV." With that, fWo cameras headed back to the ring, though not before catching Davis and Fate staring each other down... Erik Kelly (c) vs. Wretch Slaughter heads down to the ring for its semi-main, a non-title matchup between the fWo Internet Champion and one of the potential CyberSlam main eventers. "Rockin' In The Free World" hits, and the crowd goes nuts as Wretch makes his way to the ring, looking focused for this big matchup. "POWERBOMB!" is then roared, and Erik Kelly makes his way to the ring, accompanied by Matt Loco, the fWo Internet Title belt around his waist for the first time in a good long while. Kelly unstraps the belt and hands it to Loco, then steps over the top rope and charges toward Wretch. Wretch ducks his clothesline as the bell rings, the unloads on him with a series of punches. Wretch backs Kelly into the ropes with his rights, then whips him across the ring. Kelly manages to reverse, and nails Wretch coming off with a big boot to the face. They try to throw him over the top rope, but it's no use. Kelly holds onto the top rope, then grabs Wretch and Strangler by the head and gives them a noggin-knocker. They both stumble off, and Wretch has the misfortune of being closest to Kelly. Kelly charges, and knocks him over the top rope with a big boot. This leaves it between two men with a history. The Boston Strangler and Erik Kelly. They lock up, and Kelly gains the advantage with a knee to the gut as Matt Loco shouts instructions at ringside. Kelly grabs Strangler by the throat and chokeslams him to the mat. He then brings Strangler up and points toward the ropes. He's about to end it. Kelly runs Strangler to the ropes, and moves to throw him over. At the last second, however, the Strangler shifts his weight, grabs ahold of Kelly with leverage, and throws him over the top rope. Kelly gets up on the outside, in disbelief, but inside the ring, the Boston Strangler is victorious. Strangler heads into this year's CyberSlam, one year after main eventing last year's, with a great deal of momentum for his matches with Darkness and the Battle Royal. As the Strangler, the embodiment of CyberSlam III, climbs up and stands tall on the top turnbuckle, Slaughter fades into the logo for fWo CyberSlam IV, and one fact becomes clear in the mind of fWo viewers.
