The crowd was screaming in horror, hoping someone would come out to make the heroic last minute save. But no one was coming. Nothing seemed to move but the chair. Which stopped just inches from Pamela's face. She opened her eyes and saw Vic smirking at her as he handed the chair to Matt Loco. "You're the one who has a problem with her," Creed said to Matt. "You take care of her." Suddenly, Pamela was shoved forward just as Matt swung the chair. This time, metal met flesh. Chair met head. Darkness met Pamela Dee. Creed nodded to Nick Brandish and the two men rolled out of the ring amidst a chorus of hostility. Matt dropped the chair and looked down at Pamela's unconscious form. "This is just the beginning bitch!" he yelled before following the two other men out of the ring. Medical technicians ran by the three men and quickly charged the ring to check on Pamela. But the three men didn't care. Message sent. Let the games continue. Slaughter faded to black.
