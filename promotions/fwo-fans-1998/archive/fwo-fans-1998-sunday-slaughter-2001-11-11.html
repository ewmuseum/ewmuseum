Sunday Slaughter, 11 November 2001

11 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Ric Chronos stepped in front of Tempest suddenly. "Tempest, honey, good to see you again." Tempest jumped, startled, then frowned as she stood in the hallway. "Hey, Ric. What's going on?" "Walk with me, talk with me," said Chronos. He attempted to put an arm around her shoulders, but Tempest shrugged it off. Chronos shrugged, then began to walk to his locker room. Tempest followed him warily. This couldn't be good. Chronos opened the door and walked in. Tempest followed. Chronos smiled. "You know, I've got a big match tonight with Ruben Ross. I've always found I wrestle better on a full stomach. So, well, let's cut to the chase here. Make me dinner." Tempest smiled, relieved. Oh, that was all he wanted. Dinner wait a minute, dinner?! The smile quickly became a frown. "You can't be serious. I'm not gonna make you dinner, Ric. Get real." The smile remained on Chronos' face, a sight that made Tempest wary. "On the contrary, babe. You will." "Hmm, let's see. No." "Now, now... you wouldn't want me to accidentally have a little slip of the tongue and, well, you know... now would you?" "No, I wouldn't..." "And besides, if I like it, I might just have to reward you. You get me?" Tempest didn't like the sound of his words, but she didn't have much of a choice. "Yeah, I get you." "Great, spectacular. Now I've gotta meet up with my boys, but when I get back I expect to have dinner on the table." "OK..." Tempest said reluctantly. Chronos smiled, nodded, and walked out the door. Tempest looked at the small refrigerator humming along in the corner of the room. "You have got to be kidding me..." Survivor Battle Royal Slaughter opens up inside the arena with thunderous fireworks and loud music. The fireworks and music die down as the crowd cheers. "Crawling In The Dark" by Hoobustank plays, and Jessica Jenkins, the only female in the Survivor Competition, came out to the ring. The Rider logo shines bright on the Slaughter Screen as she walks out, and then she enters the ring. She circles for the fans, a group of obvious redneck males whistle and shout "Take it off!" The crowd quiets down, and the battle royal begins. The lights dim. Suddenly a streak of lightning arches to the entrance way. As it hits sparks fly, and the crowd erupts. "Demon Cleaner" by Tool begins blasting throughout the arena much to the delight of the fans as Brand Frontier stomps onto the ramp way. He stands there for a second before placing his hands on his hips. Many flashes go off in the crowd as Frontier runs to the ring and slides in. Once in the ring he steps atop one of the turnbuckles and makes a cross symbol over his heart as he points to the sky. Brand's voice echoes through the arena as his music comes to an end... "All Hail The Paragon!" Brand enters and nods his head to his teammate Jenkins, who smiles at him. Apparently they have a game plan. Jenkins and Frontier circle the ring. The minute timer which signals out the first man slowly drifts towards ten, and when it reaches ten, the fans count down to zero. The siren goes off, and it signals member number three. "Kick Some Ass" by Stroke 9 plays over the PA system and Aren Archer, the panophobe, steps out in front of the Slaughter crowd. He nervously rubs the back of his neck, chews on one of his fingernails, and then takes off in a sprint toward the ring. Aren slides underneath the bottom rope into the ring and surveys the situation. Jenkins and Frontier look at him as he enters the ring, and Frontier grips his hands with the face of a rabid dog. They charge towards Aren, but Aren thinks quickly and slides underneath the ropes, and out of their grasps. Frontier begins to leap over the ropes, but Jenkins stops him, and shakes her finger, which told him not to worry about it and get eliminated. Aren looks confused with his wide eyes, and does the only thing a good panophobe can do...hide underneath the ring. The fans boo as Frontier and Jenkins walk around in the ring. They once again stare at the entrance and wait, and the fans once again count the final ten seconds down. "For Whom the Bell Tolls" by Metallica blasts around the arena PA system as the two Riders in the ring stares up the ramp way. The fans begin to boo as Jenkins and Frontier begin to wonder where Great Dragon is. Suddenly, a mist rolls onto the stage, and moves down the ramp way as the fans looks on in utter confusion. The mist rolls into the ring as they stare at it. Suddenly, the mist disperses, and Great Dragon stands in the ring, smiling at the confused wrestlers, wondering how exactly he did the mist illusion. Jenkins and Frontier shrug their shoulders, and the fighting begins. Dragon immediately sends a left a hand to the forehead of Jenkins, and then he and the much larger Frontier lock up. Frontier and Dragon push hard on each other, and then Frontier powers Dragon into the turnbuckle. Dragon pushes Frontier into the middle of the ring. Great Dragon gazes at Brand Frontier through squinted malicious eyes, and smiles at him. The two men circle each other for a second before Dragon unleashes a flurry of hard kicks and punches. Each is in turn summarily blocked by the massive Frontier. Brand steps back quickly, and as he does he opens himself up for attack and Great Dragon stiff kicks him in the jaw sending him spinning. As Brand stumbles a bit Great Dragon grabs him and plants his head into the mat with a forceful DDT. Spying a nearby turnbuckle Dragon quickly looks around. Jessica Jenkins is still regaining from the hard punch so he once again jumps to the top turnbuckle and leaps off daredevil like towards the motionless Brand Frontier. At the last minute Brand gets a knee up and crotches the masked man. A loud painful shout if heard from the crowd, and Dragon falls to the mat in pain. Frontier is helped to his feet by Jenkins, and then both teammates stomp away on the Gunman. The time of the minute clock elapses, and then the stage lights dim. "No Quarter" silently plays, and Xaxon comes out to the ring. The fans watch on as he slowly comes to the ring, not hurrying to help Dragon. Xaxon enters the ring, and he immediately grabs Jenkins by the hair, spins her around, and chokeslams her with huge velocity to the mat. The fans look on in shock of the move, and he picks Jenkins up once again, but before he could do anything further Frontier hammers him with an axe handle to the back. Frontier kicks Xaxon in the chest, and then bounces of the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. Xaxon hits the mat hard, but before Frontier can induce any more pain to Xaxon, Dragon grabs Frontier from behind, and quickly bodyslams him to the mat. Dragon pulls Xaxon to his feet, and they look to their side and see Jenkins charging straight towards them. They duck, and then spin around and grab her. They pick her up by the neck, and she receives another choke slam, this time a double. Dragon and Xaxon taunt her, and Frontier charges and spears both men to the mat. Frontier stands up, and the countdown siren sounds once again, signaling another wrestler is about to hit the ring. The arena's lights dim and what can only be described as an eighties-type, laser show begins piercing the darkness. A series of grinding noises is heard over the PA system, which is shortly followed by the theme for the old Dr. Who Television series blaring out in all its mono glory as Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman heads out of the back and scurries towards the ring. Dorkiman circles the ring, strokes his chin then begins calculating some variables on his calculator, while Frontier jaws out at the diminutive former university professor and beckons him to enter. PHD keeps circling the ring, punching numbers and taking notes, almost as if he's surveying the ring. Dragon stands up as Frontier has his back to him, and then Dragon motions to PHD. PHD nods, and then Dragon picks up Jenkins and tosses her to the ropes towards PHD. PHD jumps to the apron, pulls down the top ropes, and Jenkins flips over, being the first person eliminated. PHD turns and gives Arrows a bucktoothed grin, then produces a pair of her stolen underwear from his lab coat and flings it out of her. Angered by his PHD's lack of respect for his teammate, Frontier charges towards the 147 lb geek, but PHD ducks the big man's lariat attempt, allowing Dragon to grab Frontier from behind. PHD comes in through the ropes, and Dragon holds Frontier by the arms. Dorkiman proceeds to slap the massive Brand Frontier across the chest, once, twice then a third time, but the only damage he manages to inflict is to his own hand. Frontier laughs at PHD and begins to break his arms free from the grip of the much smaller Great Dragon. PHD, used to being laughed at from years and years of ridicule suffered at the hands of massive bullies like Frontier, shrugs off the laughter, then proceeds to sink his huge, buck teeth into the calf of the big man. Brand Frontier laughter morphs into a scream of pain, as PHD rends at his flesh for a couple of seconds, before rolling to safety. With PHD safely out of the way, Dragon then gives a belly to back suplex to Frontier, and Xaxon kicks him in the ribs, as the countdown siren once again goes off. To the pulsing beat of "Warrior's Call" by the Yunabi Drummers, Mufanga Kagos emerges from behind the curtain and rushes straight for the ring, tossing his tribal shield aside. Over the cheers of the legions of fWo fans, all that can be heard is the Wildman's "Guuuuuuuuuuuuuuuaaaaaargh!" yell as Mufanga gains the apron and leaps over the ropes. Mufanga Kagos charges right for Gregor Xaxon, as the giant German turns and welcomes the challenge. They begin trading punches, Xaxon's shots more effective than Mufanga's wild swings. Xaxon soon gets the upper hand, using picture perfect European uppercuts to drive the Wildman back toward the ropes, then proceeds to whip Kagos into the ropes and ducking for a backdrop. Unfortunately for the German, he telegraphs the move, so Mufanga stops short and raises a bare foot, kicking the German in the face. As Xaxon rears up, Mufanga quickly hooks him into a vertical brainbuster suplex, lifting him up and landing him on his head, hard on the mat. Frontier slowly gets to his feet, and PHD charges towards him, but he gets a huge boot to the face and falls to the mat like a rag doll. Frontier then turns to Dragon, and both men lock it up. Xaxon stands up, and then Mufanga charges at him. Xaxon bends over and flings Mufanga up and over the ropes, but Mufanga grabs the top rope and lands perfectly on the ring apron. He grabs Xaxon by the neck, and then Xaxon uses all the energy he can and flips Mufanga back into the ring. Meanwhile, PHD slowly gets to his feet, but Frontier turns around quickly and jumps and hits a scissor kick to his back. PHD falls back down to the mat face first, and Frontier goes back to pummeling Dragon who was wedged against the turnbuckle. As "Citizen Erased" plays throughout the arena, Martyr steps through the curtains. He looks at the fans with skepticism, if not outright apprehension. He pensively strides down the ramp, slapping hands with some fans on the sides. When he reaches the ring, he hops onto the apron and somersaults over the ropes and into the ring. Looking towards his opponents, Martyr stretches on the ropes. Immediately after Martyr's music dies down, Grendel comes from under the ring, and slides in. The timer had not finished, but Grendel wanted Martyr. Grendel pushes his way through the wrestlers, and then grabs Martyr. They punch each other back and forth, and then Grendel pushes Martyr into the ropes, and goes crazy with extremely fast punches to the head and the body. Martyr tries to block but he can't. Grendel then picks Martyr up with one hand tied around the neck, but PHD is pushed by Frontier from behind, and he falls into Grendel's legs, and both Grendel and Martyr crash to the mat. Xaxon walks around the ring, eyeing up his opponent. Mufanga Kagos gets in Xaxon's face and now both wrestlers are yelling at each other, nose to nose. Even with all the other people in the ring, thee two men seem to have singled each other out. Suddenly, Gregor Xaxon pushes The Wildman of Kagos hard in the chest, driving him towards the corner. Mufanga Kagos gets up and pushes his opponent back. They lock up. Mufanga gets the upper hand with a headlock, only to have it reversed by Xaxon with a headlock of his own. The Wildman muscles out and pushes the older wrestler to the ropes and launches himself with a spinning heel kick. Always with his wits about him, Xaxon sees the move coming and catches Kagos in midair, with Mufanga's leg trapped at an awkward angle up by his head. Xaxon then flips him back into a modified suplex, sending Mufanga Kagos crashing to the canvas. Frontier pulls a tired Dragon to the middle of the ring, and the whips him into the ropes. Dragon ducks a clothesline, and then spins Frontier around and hits him with a neck breaker. Both men remain motionless on the mat. Martyr gets to his feet, and takes a huge risk and climbs to the top rope. The fans are astonished at his decision, and then he jumps off and lands on both PHD and Grendel, sending the pair of Gunmen to the canvas. Martyr is quickly on his feet and once again begins climbing the ropes. He gets back to the top rope once again, and then jumps off, but its difficult to surprise the PHD twice, as the little man is prepared this time, rolling to the side, and catching Martyr with his dreaded Vulcan Nerve Pinch immediately upon his landing. Virtually immobilized, Martyr uses what little movement he can muster to try to break free, while PHD slowing inches Martyr towards the ropes. Grendel, not known for his patience gets to his feet, rips Martyr from PHD's hold, hauls him high in the air, then drops him out of the ring to the cement. The fans boo at Grendel, who beats his chest and screams. Grendel then turns around, and is once again met with Dorkiman, who is once again on the receiving end of a throw from Brand Frontier. PHD hits Grendel hard, causing Grendel to flip over the ropes and fall to the cement. Grendel gets to his feet and silently smiles as PHD looks back at him with an "It's not my fault" look. Grendel then proceeds to once again disappear under the ring. Back in the ring, Xaxon and Mufanga are trading punches, this time Mufanga getting the upper hand with a series of European uppercuts. He kicks the German swiftly in the gut, doubling him over. The Wildman grabs him in a front face lock and jumps, spinning and planting Xaxon with an insane Standing Tornado DDT! With Xaxon down, Mufanga ignores the chaos around him and climbs the turnbuckle... He raises his hands high signaling his finisher, the Wilddive! As he is about to leap, Xaxon jumps up from the canvas and climbs the turnbuckle! Trading short jabs high about the ring, Gregor Xaxon hooks Mufanga for a superplex and delivers, sending the Wildman down to the center of the ring with a bone-jarring impact, and narrowly missing the other wrestlers in the squared circle. The countdown siren once again goes off as "Long Hard Road Out Of Hell" by Marilyn Manson plays, and the Magi slowly creeps to the ring. Frontier and Dragon slowly get to their feet, and PHD gets up as well. He stares at the much larger Magi walking to the ring, and he taunts him to come in. The Magi enters the ring, and grabs PHD and throws him into the turnbuckle causing the little man to squeal in pain. The Magi charges in for to shoulder block PHD into the turnbuckle, but PHD ducks, causing Magi to crash into the corner. Magi grabs his shoulder and screams out in pain, bringing a grin to PHD's face. PHD climbs to the top rope, and begins to measure up Magi for some kind of high flying maneuver, only to have the Magi suddenly surge forward and shove the little man off the top turn buckle and down to the hard concrete floor of the ringside area, bringing a roar of approval from the crowd. The Magi looks to the outside and shakes his head at the now unmoving and eliminated PHD. The pain and exhaustion is etched in Mufanga Kagos' face as he never thought that this would be such a grueling match, and he honestly didn't think he would get this much of a fight from Xaxon, a fifty year old man. Mufanga is hanging on the ropes, using them to hold himself up. Across the ring, Xaxon charges with a clothesline, looking for a finish to this feud and to send Mufanga out to the concrete floor. At the last second, the Wildman ducks and Xaxon's own momentum nearly carries him out of the ring. Grabbing Gregor Xaxon from behind, and in one fluid motion, Kagos wraps him in a full nelson and slams his head forward down into the mat. The crowd goes wild at the sheer impact that floors the German. Again, Mufanga goes to the well, climbing the turnbuckle to the approval of the cheering crowd. Again he signals the Wilddive with outstretched hands. He gets ready, and Xaxon staggers to his feet again. Xaxon climbs the turnbuckle to trap Mufanga Kagos up there. Xaxon throws a jab into the Wilman's bruised midsection and hooks him for yet another superplex. He lifts, but Mufanga blocks it, both men tottering precariously on the top rope. Xaxon lifts again, but again Mufanga blocks the Superplex attempt. Then in a reversal, Mufanga Kagos lifts Gregor Xaxon, holding him high in a vertical suplex. The crowd holds it's collective breath, and camera flashes pop as the men seem to hang there for a second, before they drop backwards, Kagos suplerplexing Xaxon and himself clear out of the ring. The fans blow the roof off the stadium. With Kagos and Xaxon out of the match, there are now only three members in the ring. The Magi, Frontier, and Great Dragon. "Makin' Money" by Handsome Devil plays, and a third Rider, Matt Rivers comes to the ring to make this a three on one advantage against Great Dragon. The Magi and the winded Frontier stomp away on Great Dragon, and then Rivers slides underneath the ropes and adds to the beating. Rivers picks Dragon up, and then he whips him into the ropes. The Riders look at each other as if something was planned, and they as Dragon came running back, all three Riders jumped in the air and dropkicked him to the mat. The fans cheer loudly at the amazing triple dropkick, as Dragon lies helplessly on the mat. Frontier takes a breather as he leans against the ropes. Once again, the countdown siren sounds. The arena lights dim, and a red EKG-style line fills the Slaughter Screen as the Deftones, "Elite" blares through the PA system. Lance Knight walks out, seen for the first time in his new ring attire, which consists of a traditional amateur wrestling singlet, black, with a red EKG line on it not unlike the one that fills the SlaughterTron. As the line on the SlaughterTron begins moving, clips of Knight's matches in Japan flash on and off all across the screen. As Knight walks to the ring, he stretches his shoulders and looks at the amassed competitors in the ring, all of which are his Rider teammates and one Gunman. It is now a four on one advantage, and the fans look on at this silently. The four men tear away at Great Dragon with stomps and punches, and Frontier lifts him up and powerbombs him to the mat with aid from the other Riders. The fans boo at the huge disadvantage. They all stop the beating on the fallen Dragon and walk around the ring, taunting the crowd. But the timer runs out. There's only one man left to enter. Graphic Violence. "Down With The Sickness" by Disturbed blares over the PA system. A bald headed man wearing black shorts that read "IAV" on them steps out from behind the curtains. Many people do not know but the letters IAV stands for International Association of Violence, his first wrestling promotion. He stands there for a brief moment, passing off that trademark smirk. He looks at the arena, his first fWo exhibition match. But in his mind, he knew it wouldn't be his last. The fans booed him as he walked down the ramp. One fan holds up a "Lance Knight Rules" sign. GV takes notice of this and walks over to the man. He takes the sign from him and rips it into two. Graphic Violence then spits on the man and sticks up his middle finger while screaming "Don't make me beat you like I did Knight you overweight piece of shit." Graphic Violence the continues his way towards the ring. Rubbing his hands together with joy, GV jumps into the ring. Knight shakes his head at Graphic Violence's actions, and then all the riders charge towards GV. GV knocks them down like dominoes one by one with punches, and then he grabs Matt Rivers and literally launches him out of the ring. The Magi comes up to Violence with a running lariat, and GV ducks quickly. He kicks The Magi in the stomach and then sets him up and powerbombs him to the mat. The Magi then rolls under the ropes to the cement to get a moment of rest. GV curses both Rivers and The Magi, but then Knight comes up from behind and hits a lariat to the back of GV's head. GV falls to the mat, and then he and Frontier stomp away on GV. Dragon gets to his feet and comes to the scene. Great Dragon and Lance Knight push at each other as each tries to gain some ground. Neither giving an inch. Brand grabs Lance by the arm and jerks him away. Lance retaliates with a stiff punch to Brand's face that bloodies his nose. Frontier shrugs it off as he turns back to his original target, only to get smacked in the face once again. This time by the sole of Dragon's boot. With anger in his eyes Brand engulfs Dragon in his arms and simply throws him at the top rope. Great Dragon sails the four or five feet towards the ropes and bounces of the top one as he tumbles over. Brand turns his attention away for a second thinking Dragon has been eliminated. As he does he sees Graphic Violence stare to where Dragon went over. Brand looks back as he overhears GV. "Well, looks like we got ourselves another fucking pussy. Time to say bye!" snarls Graphic Violence as Great Dragon gulps. Dragon resigns himself to be eliminated finally as his enraged teammate storms at him. Suddenly Brand Frontier grabs Graphic Violence from behind, and spins him around hitting a perfect swinging neck breaker. Graphic Violence catapults up then down to the mat as Great Dragon takes the opportunity to spring to the top rope. "He's mine!" hisses Brand, wanting the honor of eliminating Great Dragon and gaining a measure of vengeance. He turns to face Dragon who launches a powerful top rope lariat, and takes the 'hero' down in one fell swoop. "No, ahondara, you are mine!" quipped Dragon, lifting up a somewhat weakened Frontier. Once again Dragon comes at Brand. The weary Irish warrior sees him coming and braces for the impact. It's too little too late as a spinning heel kick connects and it sends Frontier stumbling into the ropes. Great Dragon leaps at Brand but is halted in mid-air with a powerful spear in the stomach. Dragon bounces off the mat as Frontier picks him and to the delight of the crowd signals for his trademarked Thunder-strike heart punch. He hits the massive maneuver but before it solidly connects Dragon glances off some of the force with the flat of his arm. As he falls to the mat clutching his chest Brand pounces on him and after a few kicks to his head lifts him up and puts his head between his legs. He inches over to the turnbuckle and the crowd goes crazy. It's the Divine Right! Just as he is about to lift him up Dragon drops to his knee and forces Brand closer to the ropes. Brand still manages to clean jerk Great Dragon up and sends him out of the ring to the ground, never even touching the ropes. Enraged, Dragon jumps back up and in typical fashion catches Frontier off guard and hits him in the kidney with a stiff kick before pulling him over the top rope also. The outside referee distracted by the assault between Lance Knight and Graphic Violence didn't see Dragon's interference and concludes that both men are out. The two weary warriors stand and turn their gaze on one another. Great Dragon leaps at Brand and the two lock up once again. The heavy security surrounding the ringside area manages to separate the two. As the security guards hold down Dragon, Brand backs off. Brand smirks at the thought of Great Dragon being taken out of this competition. "An eye...for an eye." Mouths Brand as he points to Great Dragon. The fans began to boo, as Brand looks around, confused. As he turns around, however, he realizes why. Dragon managed to escape from the clutches of the security and had grabbed a chair. Great Dragon blasted Brand upside his head with the steel chair, and Brand drops to the ground like a sack of potatoes. Through clenched teeth Dragon's whispers are barley audible by the camera microphone. "Rule number one...don't mess with the master." He then turned and headed up the ramp-way, tossing the blood stained chair aside. GV and Lance Knight both get up to their feet, and then they realize they are the last three men along with The Magi left in the ring, though not for long. Suddenly, Aren Archer reappears from underneath the ring and gets to his feet. Apparently over his bout with Aphenphosmphobia, which is the fear of physical contact. Aren slides underneath the rope and looks at the men in front of him. Knight looks and sees the smaller Archer coming his way. Archer sees Knight stalking him. Knight, so intent on making his way to Archer, doesn't even see his own teammate, the Magi, being Irish whipped to the ropes, and the two Riders collide, with the impact of the running Magi sending Knight careening dangerously close to ropes. Archer then grabs The Magi, and launches him out of the ring. The fans cheer Archer, and the he does a crazy move. He climbs to the top rope and then does a suicide plancha, landing hard on the Magi. The fans erupted at his extraordinary height, but Archer had also eliminated himself. Leaving Graphic Violence and Lance Knight as the only two men left in the ring. Graphic Violence turns, only to see that the man who makes his blood boil, Lance Knight, has skinned the cat on the other side of the ring and made his way back in. GV smiles, ready to pick up where he left off on Wednesday night at the bar, but Knight, who dives at him with a tackle and takes him to the mat, surprises him. Before they even impact the mat, both men are firing punches at the other, and each doing their best to gain the upper hand on their opponent. It's clear that none of the bad blood has died down, as both men are putting their all into every punch. Finally, Knight blocks a left hand from GV, followed by a right hand, but the tables are quickly turned. Graphic Violence lifts his knees up and flips Knight to the mat, taking advantage of Knight's grip on GV's forearms. Knight releases his hold, allowing Graphic Violence to stand up, but as the big Gunman approaches, Knight fires a kick into the big man's face that sends him staggering backwards. Knight rolls to his stomach and then jumps to his feet, only to be leveled by a clothesline from Graphic Violence. Knight pops back up, catches Graphic Violence's second clothesline attempt, pulling the Lone Gunman into a head-and-arm suplex. Knight pulls GV back up, only to receive a low blow for his efforts. GV, now back in control, whips Knight to the ropes, following behind a second later for a match-ending clothesline. However, Knight turns as he hits the ropes and has enough time to wrap his arms around Graphic Violence's midsection, absorbing the impact of the clothesline and sending both men tumbling over the top rope together. However, Knight falls to the cement and GV grabs the ropes, preventing himself from being eliminated. The fans boo as GV has obviously won. But he has not won. GV asks for a microphone. He is handed one as Knight gets to his feet. Violence shouts between deep breaths, "This shit is not over Knight! Lets change the tables on this one. It's me and you. No more of this over the top rope crap. Neither one of us is walking out of here until one of us is put through a table!" Knight shrugs his shoulders and then slides underneath the ropes. GV throws the microphone away, and the two warriors resume their brawl. Lance Knight ducked a running lariat by the slimmest of margins, feeling the rush of the arm pass over his head. Turning quickly, Lance shot up a leg and caught GV in the chin with a superkick. Acting quickly, Knight pushed his weary body forward and caught GV around the waist. Locking him in, Knight flipped him up and over with an exploder suplex that rocked the ring. The fans exploded in the arena, and Knight screamed up into the rafters with triumph. With a final glance at Graphic Violence, Knight slid to the outside of the ring and tossed the ring apron aside with one arm. Knight slid out the table, pausing to glare at ring attendants who were uneasy about this change in the outcome of the match. Nobody stopped Knight however, and he lifted the table up with a grunt and shoved it into the ring. Following in after it, he stomped on GV's head for good measure. Knight pulled up on Graphic Violence, trying to bring him up into a standing position so he could finish him off with a suplex through the table. Knight dug his heels in and shoved upward, but Graphic Violence resisted. Violence locked a leg, and freed one of his arms. He popped Knight in the head with the side of one fist, breaking the hold. Knight stumbled backward, his equilibrium momentarily off balance. GV didn't waste the opportunity as he threw Knight into the turnbuckle with impact. Knight bounced off of it, stumbling slightly as he rebounded. GV picked him up in a quick military press, lifting him high above his head as the crowd booed louder than ever. Graphic Violence walked him toward the table, which was still laying flat against the mat. GV dropped Knight on top of it, and the impact of the table on canvas forced Knight to roll away in pain and clutching his chest. GV scoffed aloud and grabbed up the table. Extending the legs, he set it up in the center of the ring and pointed at Knight as he drew one finger across his throat. Graphic Violence reached for Knight, but Knight quickly hooked an arm around GV's leg. He toppled the big man with an inside cradle, and while pinfall was not the objective of the match the impact took the wind out of GV and gave Knight a much needed opening. Knight took to his feet as GV shook his head to clear the haze. Sweat poured down the foreheads of both men, both men who had already wrestled a tough match and were now having to do still more. Lance had quickly hooked an arm around the waist and neck of GV, and took him back down to the mat with authority with a strong overhead suplex. GV coughed a sick, deep cough as Knight kicked down on him, then picked him up and moved him toward the corner turnbuckle. GV was nearly limp as Knight moved the table closer to the corner, then signaled to the audience that he was going to finish it and send the Riders to a third victory. Knight picked GV up and placed him on the top turnbuckle, then climbing up to the first rope he hooked his arms around his waist. Knight was going to suplex GV up and through the table from the corner, and the fans in the arena took to their feet with excitement. Knight pulled, but GV was holding onto the ropes. A fire was burning in GV's eyes, and with a great scream of energy he held fast as Knight lost his grip on him. Seeing his opportunity, Graphic Violence shot out a hand and clamped it around Knight's neck, his other arm holding his back. With the last of his energy he shoved up and back as hard as he could. Knight tumbled back, struck the table, and shattered it in a rain of splinters and shards of metal as the table exploded beneath him. The bell rang. Graphic Violence collapsed down on the mat, sinking first to his knees and then to his side. The crowd booed louder than ever, pelting the ring with trash. Graphic Violence was dazed, and the sound was a dull roar in his ears as attendants helped him free from the ring. Lance Knight rolled to one side and shook his head. He glared at Graphic Violence between eyes that were slits of fury. Attendants were inside the ring, but Knight pushed them aside and stood shakily to his feet.
