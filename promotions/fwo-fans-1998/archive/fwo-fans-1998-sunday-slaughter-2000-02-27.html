fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 27 February 2000

27 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Fans sat anxiously in their seats, waiting for Sunday Slaughter to take to the air. Many fans were anxious to see the fallout of the events that went down the night before at fWo Trapped. Several fWo wrestlers were not at the event tonight, instead several hundred miles away to be present for the first day of ticket sales for CyberSlam 2000. Suddenly the lights dimmed, and Blind blared over the arena speakers. The crowd rose to their feet as the triad of the French Foundation appeared at the top of the entrance ramp. Courage and the Flying Frenchie without titles, but still looking confidant and proud of themselves, and behind them the new fWo World Heavyweight Champion... Primetime. Primetime held the fWo World title high above his head, and the three walked to the ring to the cheers of the crowd. Courage rolled into the ring and asked for a microphone as his partners came in behind him. Courage waited for the noise of the crowd to fall, and then pointed to Primetime. "Ladies and gentlemen... your new fWo World Heavyweight Champion!" The crowd cheered as Primetime held the title up once more, and the Frenchie was handed the microphone. "While it is true zat ze Frenchie is still ze superior wrestler, let it not be said zat ze Frenchie is Hoganing his way over ze wrestling world. Ze Frenchie gives everyone ze shot... but Primetime... zere will be a rematch." Primetime smiled and nodded, and he accepted the microphone from the hand of the Frenchie. "For you Frenchie... or for Courage... you got it. But as I stand here today your champion I can't help but think of all that the Frenchie Foundation has accomplished. For you see, between the three of us we have won more gold than anyone in the fWo ever has... twenty one championships between us! And nobody can even come close to..." But suddenly Primetime was interrupted by the music of Van Halen, and through smoke and lasers stood Scott Slugger, the man known lately as Perfect. "You three little goofs want to come in here and brag about how you've held lots of titles? You want to talk about how superior you are? Well in the fWo there is only one perfect, and as far as I'm concerned you three are just pieces of human garbage who are going to be completely titleless by the end of the night. Because you see, about a month ago I was guaranteed a little something called a World Title shot... to be used any damn time I want. And you know what I want? I want it... tonight." Primetime laughed, "You can go screw yourself 'Pansy', or whatever your name is... you get no title shot." Perfect smiled. "Then you will be stripped of the title... because that is the deal. My call, and I can do it any damn way I want. And I choose tonight." Perfect laughed and walked away, leaving the Foundation fuming inside the ring... Delivery... The delivery man whistled to himself as he walked down the hallway of the arena. He looked down at a clipboard and then up at the walls from time to time, obliviously trying to judge where he was heading to. He counted the doors to himself silently, and finally stopped at locker room number 42. He fixed his collar, and raised a fist to knock on the door. He hesitated. He was very cold all of a sudden, and shivered to himself. Strange. He knocked on the door, grunting quietly as he noticed that the door was freezing cold. There were footsteps from behind the door and the delivery man was struck with a feeling of primal dread. He suddenly began to think that it would be a fine idea if he simply dropped the package and ran in the opposite direction as quickly as possible. He was about to do just that when the door swung open. The delivery man gasped, and the color ran out of his face. Nightshade regarded him coldly, and then looked down at the package the delivery man held. Her eyes narrowed. "Candy." She said it not like a question, but like fact. The delivery man nodded and gulped, and handed her the box of candy. Nightshade looked at the label on top of the box and her eyes narrowed further. "Violet... it's for you. Candy." Violet bounded into the scene, grinning happily. She spotted the red box with white trim and her smile faltered. "That doesn't look like Clarence's wrapping... it's the wrong color and looks awful neat. Look Nightshade... there isn't even any duck tape used." Nightshade closed the door, but said nothing. Violet looked troubled, but then a glimmer of an idea came to her. "Say... this isn't you playing some kind of trickery on me is it?" Nightshade only glared harder at Violet... Briana vs. Brandi Brandi makes her way to the ring as the announcer proclaims this match for the fWo Women's Championship. Brandi plays to the crowd inside the ring as Briana makes her way down to the ring. Briana hands off the belt to the referee and awaits for the bell. As soon as it is rung the two women lock up. They jockey for position for a couple of seconds until Brandi slips Briana into a headlock. Brandi holds her there for a short period, tightening her grip, but Briana still manages to throw her off into the ropes. Brandi bounces off the ropes and comes back but Briana leapfrogs over her. On her way back Brandi walks right into an elbow from Briana, who then takes advantage of Brandi's stunned state by hitting her with the spinwheel kick. Brandi goes down, but manages to kickout just before three. Briana pulls Brandi to her feet and snap suplexes her before bouncing off the ropes. Briana drops an elbow on Brandi and covers again, but once again can't get the three count. Briana picks up Brandi and positions her on the top turnbuckle, but as she herself goes up Brandi elbows her in the mouth. Briana falls to the mat as Brandi stands atop the turnbuckles and jumps off. before she can hit, though, Briana gets a leg up. Brandi takes a foot to the face and falls to the canvas, allowing Briana to make yet another cover. Much like the others, though, Brandi sneaks out in the nick of time. Briana sends Brandi into the corner and attempts to follow up with a bodypress, but the challenger dives out of the way just in time. Briana staggers back into a rollup from Brandi, but kicks out at two. Brandi pulls Briana to her feet and backs her into the corner, then lets loose with a couple of chops. Brandi signals to the crowd, then hops to the top of the turnbuckles and attempts to hit a hurracanrana. But, just as Brandi reaches the peak of the move, Briana comes running out of the corner and delivers a running Lyger bomb. Briana holds on for the pin but Brandi grabs hold of the ropes. As Briana picks up Brandi again a single figure emerges from the crowd carrying a folding chair. The champion heaves up Brandi and lands a dangerous DDT, but before Briana can make the cover the woman with the chair slides into the ring and clocks her upside the head. Briana goes down in a heap, and the referee calls for the bell. The former Apocolyptica, Kristen, smirks and exits the ring as Briana gets the win via disqualification. Result: Brandi by DQ Reunited Slaughter returned from the preceding commercial break to a live, packed crowd who all appeared very enthusiastic about what had occurred so far, and what they were in store for later. As they did, the atmosphere blackened. Pyros burst before a catchy tune began to play, and a nifty video montage began playing on the SlaughterScreen. The catchy tune transformed into women making sounds as if they were experiencing something sexually stimulating. From the backstage area, a man wearing a pink outfit with a large "O" on his chest emerged. The outfit also entailed green gloves and silver boots, as well as a mask that covered only his eyes and nose, leaving his forehead and chin exposed. On the SlaughterScreen, the word "Orgazmo" could be seen, as the thousands upon thousands of fans wondered what the fuss was about. The arena lights came on and they could see the man more clearly, before he was joined by another man dressed in an equally ridiculous outfit. The SlaughterScreen now read "...and Choda boy!". The two men began walking to the ring as the fans groaned, and upon closer inspection one could acknowledge that it was Lance Star as "Orgazmo" and Mike Heftel as "Choda Boy". They entered the ring and Orgazmo began to speak, but could not be heard because of the loud amount of boos. Orgazmo threatened to shoot them with his Orgazmo Gun, but the fans didn't care what he wished to do. Some quote about turning it sideways and sticking it up somewhere would apply here. The jeers grew even louder as Star looked at the audience dumbfounded, not realizing why the crowd was reacting in such a negative way. Heftel & Star quickly discussed about what to do, before "Do you buy what the Sellout is selling?" echoed throughout the arena. The fans rose to their feet as the cocky, greedy & rich bastard himself stepped out onto the stage wearing a black silk button up shirt and black jeans. Universal produced a microphone as he reached the ring. Climbing in, he approached the two ridiculously attired wrestlers with caution. Speaking in a stern manner, he directed his attention to Heftel. "Mike Heftel, this is between Lance and I, and if you don't mind, I'd like you to leave. Fair enough?" Heftel nodded and shook hands with Star, before leaving. With Heftel out of the picture, Universal spoke towards Star. "Lance... what the hell is this? Is this your idea of a good gimmick? Is it? This is the most ridiculous gimmick I've ever seen. And I've seen a lot of bad ones, like the Gobbedlygooker, the Red Rooster, the Battler, Job Van Dam, Outback Jack, The Goon... but this one, in all of the twenty plus years that I've been following wrestling... this tops them all. If there were a list of all the bad gimmicks that have ever existed, this one right here would be at the very top of that list. Lance... what are you thinking? How could you embarrass not only me, but your family... your friends... YOURSELF?" Star was paying attention, but Universal didn't think he was paying close enough attention, and Universal grew angrier as he went on. "Don't you remember who it was that got you here in the fans Wrestling organization? Don't you remember that it was me, Lance? I paid for you to go to wrestling school, I got you hooked up with jobs in Japan, I got you the independent dates on the eastern seaboard. You wouldn't be here without me Lance. Your a very talented wrestler, likely the best pure wrestler this sport has ever seen, and you wouldn't be here without help from me? Without the financial support from me, without the emotional support from me, without the mental support from me, without the physical support from me... you would not be here. Don't you remember that, Lance?" Star looked down at the mat; his head nodding in an affirmative gesture. "I'm sure you can remember this past November 17th, when you were wrestling Scott Collins, and you ended up temporarily paralyzed. It was me who was there for you. It was me who paid for your medical expenses when the fWo couldn't. It was me who was there for you to pay for the rehabilitation you went through. It was me who was there giving you my never ending support, helping you make progress. Helping you walk on your own two feet. I was there for you, don't you remember? I was there, man. Every day. Every hour. Every minute. Every second. One way or another, I was there. And I didn't expect anything in return.. until now. All I want from you is one thing. One thing, that's all I ask. There's been something missing the past few months. The tag team division has totally sucked. Make no mistake about it, The Underground have been one of the best teams the fWo has ever had, but there's been something missing. And that one thing, is you and I. The... Hollywood... F'N... Blondes!" The crowd erupted at the sound of this, and Star seemed taken aback by the large reaction. Universal continued "What I am asking you... no, scratch that. What I'm TELLING YOU is... the fans want us to team again. The fans want to see us take over the tag team decision like I know and THEY know we're capable of. So, what do you say, Lance? How about it?" Lance nodded his head in the positive direction, before he and Universal embraced to a large reaction. Lance took the microphone from Universal, before saying... "One thing though, Chris. I do not like the name the Hollywood Blondes. Would it be alright if I came up with another?" Universal shrugged, and nodded... "That's fine by me. But since it's official, since the greatest tag team to EVER grace the fWo is reunited, let's throw out a challenge to the tag champs at Body Count. Put the straps up at Body Count against us, and see what it's like to WRESTLE a REAL tag team." The fans rejoiced in cheers as Star & Universal raised their arms high above, before exiting as the crowd prepared for the next match. Eron vs. Mitch Wilson Slaughter rolled on as we prepared for a rematch from the night before at Trapped between Mitch Wilson & Eron The Relentless. At Trapped, it was Eron who sneaked by with a victory, but undoubtedly, Mitch felt he would take tonight's match and the title away from Eron. With that said, "Calling All Stations" by Genesis began playing throughout the Kemper Arena. A red light showered the stage and Mitch Wilson came out to a resounding amount of boos and jeers from the audience. Wilson made his way to the ring, still apparently angry about what happened last night at Trapped. As his music faded, "Physical" by Nine Inch Nails started, and Eron The Relentless came out to an overwhelming amount of heat from the fans. Eron held his fWo Internet Title high above and bragged as he walked to the ring about winning last night, although the fans assured him with a double-finger gesture that he won by sheer luck. As he reached the ring a fan near ringside began calling him names which distracted him just enough to be nailed by two feet courtesy of Wilson, who baseball slid out onto the floor. The referee signaled for the bell, and the match was underway as Wilson took Eron to the floor once more with a snapmare takeover, before a sharp crisp kick to the back of the head. He followed up by rolling Eron into the ring, before leaping up onto the ring apron and then to the top ropes. Eron got up and Wilson took his chances by flying off with a high cross body, and hit it... however the momentum 'caused Eron to end up atop of Wilson in a pin. Wilson kicked out at two, before Eron quickly got up and took off his own trenchcoat and disposing it to the outside. Eron brought Wilson up by the hair and took him down with a quick scoop slam, before going out to the ring apron and propelling himself over into a legdrop. He followed up by bringing Wilson up and whipping him to the ropes, but Wilson reversed it. As Eron returned Wilson ducked for a backdrop, however Eron telegraphed it into a single-arm DDT. He followed with a lateral press, but Wilson kicked out at two. Eron brought Mitch up and whipped him into the turnbuckles. He charged in with an shoulder block, but Mitch sidestepped it and Eron slammed into the turnbuckles. Mitch rolled him up from behind but Eron kicked out. Eron got up and charged, and Wilson took him down with a spine buster. Another cover, and another kickout at two. Wilson brought him up and took him down with a fallaway slam, before running and diving and driving a knee into his chest. Wilson brought him up once more and whipped him into the turnbuckles, and as Eron came out from the corner Wilson took him down with a belly-to-belly suplex. Suddenly, Courage began to make his way down to ringside, and as Wilson had Eron covered Courage pulled the referee out onto the floor and shouted in his general direction. From the crowd emerged the new fWo World Champion Primetime, wielding some steel. He came into the ring and slammed the chair onto the back of Wilson' head before he could get up, and as Eron got up to thank Primetime he was greeted with a whack to the face as Primetime said "you're welcome" in his own way. Primetime tossed the chair to ringside before Courage let the referee go resume his duties. The referee did, and upon re-entering the ring he began asking Primetime what he was doing out there. Primetime merely explained that both Eron & Mitch sucked, and the referee should award the match and the title to Courage. The referee didn't buy it however, and seeing as Wilson nor Eron would recover anytime soon, he ruled the match a no-contest. This displeased Courage and Primetime, who responded with a Primetime Bomb followed by 'Courage Required', which totally and utterly rocked the referee's world. Primetime & Courage left the scene giddy about what they had accomplished. Result: No Contest The explanation The Black Panther's music starts up and he walks out to the boos of the fans. A giant smirk can be seen behind his mask as he enters the ring and grabs a microphone. "Ever since last night, everyone has been asking why I turned on Havok. All I've heard for the past 24 hours are questions asking my why. Well, you idiot fans obviously have a short memory. Let me take you back to last October. The event was Fallout '99, and in the Finals of the Wrestler of the Year Tournament, I was facing the Flying Frenchie. In one of the greatest matches of my career, I defeated the Frenchie that night. I became the Wrestler of the Year, and at the time, it was the greatest moment of my career. Then what happens? That bastard Havok decides to make his debut by ruining that moment. He cowardly attacks a guy who's already been in two long, grueling matches. A couple of weeks later, he actually has to nerve to ask me to team up. Was he stupid enough to actually think I would team up with him? The guy uses me to make his debut, and now he wants to team with me? I thought about it, and I decided to set the stupid bastard up. However, some things delayed that. First, Havok decides to go out, without my approval, and recruits Wolf Fang to join us. After a couple weeks, Wolf Fang decides he's bored and leaves us, there's a great team guy for you, huh? Anyway, then I won the Heavyweight Title, and literally saved the fWo. Being the Champion distracted me from my plan, however, after I lost the belt, it was back in full force. I fooled Havok, I fooled the fWo, and I fooled you fans!" The fans start to boo again. "What the hell is the matter with you people? Havok was the one who ruined my moment! I'm the savior of the fWo, remember? If it wasn't for me, there'd be no fWo for you all to enjoy! You're supposed to cheer me!" The booing grows louder. "What did Havok ever do? Win the Cruiserweight Title? Big deal, I don't consider a title that was held by Matthew Zhuk a very prestigious title. Pose in some girlie magazine? Let me just say this, the Cruiserweight title is the smallest in size, and when someone can totally cover himself with it, it's obvious he's not a real man like myself." Just then, Havok's music plays and the crowd erupts. Havok steps out onto the ramp with a microphone in hand. "So Panther, you think you're pretty tough? Why don't we have a match so you can prove it?" The crowd cheers at this challenge, as the Panther responds, "I don't think so Havok, I don't think you're good enough to wrestle me. After all, I was a former World Champion, and you already know my thoughts about the only title you've ever held. You're going to have to prove yourself. If you can face a mystery opponent provided by me at Body Count, I'll face you at Cyberslam. Since you're obviously not going to get past Body Count, this will leave me open for the battle royal at Cyberslam, and my chance to win the World Championship back." The crowd expresses their displeasure with the Panther, as Havok says, "You've got yourself a deal. By the way, what are you doing reading those magazines?" Hearing this, the Panther dives out of the ring and runs up the ramp. Havok runs down and they meet in the middle, trading a few punches before security breaks them up. Underground vs. Guano Apes The Guano Apes, Jackhammer and the Sphere formerly known as Surgeon, made their way to the ring to a positive reaction from the crowd. Jackhammer, bearing a goofy grin, approached the ring announcer and spoke a few words to him. The announcer shrugged and handed Jackhammer the microphone, who began to plug his CD. "Yes, that's right!" he continued, "I, Jackhammer, have released my very own CD, featuring hit single 'Kumbaya'!" The crowd slowly began to quiet down as Jackhammer continued. "And that's not all, on this very CD there are several unreleased tracks that are sure to be hits..." the crowd began to boo as Jackhammer drowned on, but these jeers became cheers all of a sudden. Jackhammer figured they had finally realized their error in music states, but in fact the Underground had come running down the aisle. Jackhammer turned around just in time to receive a clothesline from Mike Bear as the referee signaled for the bell. The title match was officially underway as Bear kicked Jackhammer to the outside and Wretch worked over Sphere in the corner with a series of punches and kicks. Jackhammer tried to regain his wits on the outside, but as he did Mike Bear elevated Wretch after he had bounced off the ropes and sent him flying over the top rope and onto the Guano Ape. The crowd popped, both in appreciation of the move and having noticed that Bear and Wretch have seemingly worked out their differences. Jackhammer and Wretch brawled on the outside while Bear sent Sphere into the ropes and brought him down with a backbreaker. Sphere tried to drag himself to the corner as Bear picked up Sphere and heaved him over his shoulder for the Whiplash. Before he could execute it though, a freed up Jackhammer rolled into the ring and pulled Sphere off of Bear's shoulders. Bear spun around in confusion, only to have Sphere thrown onto him. Sphere nailed Bear, who went flying back, and held on for the cover but the ref refused to count until the men got to their corners. Jackhammer turned to go to his corner but was met by Wretch flying off the top turnbuckle with a lariat. Jackhammer rolled to the outside as bear did the same, making the two legal men the pair of Wretch and Sphere. Sphere snuck up on Wretch and began to pound him in the corner before whipping him to the ropes. Wretch came back, ducked a clothesline, but was dropped on the return trip thanks to a shoulder block. Sphere dropped down for the quick pin, but Wretch slipped out at one. Sphere locked on a reverse chinlock in order to wear down his foe but it only took moments for Wretch to work up to his feet and push Sphere off into the ropes. Sphere came back, and Wretch monkey flipped him across the ring. Sphere scrambled to tag out to Jackhammer as Wretch slapped the hand of Mike Bear. Both of the big men came charging in. Jackhammer ducked a clothesline from Bear, spun around, but Bear bulldozed him with another clothesline. Jackhammer got to his feet, but bear was there waiting for him. Bear delivered a quick inverted atomic drop to stun Jackhammer, then came bouncing off the ropes with a running bulldog. Bear covered, but only got a two count. bear pulled Jackhammer back up, tagged out to Wretch, and sent him to the ropes. As Jackhammer came back he was lifted up by Bear, allowing Wretch to slingshot himself with deadly accuracy into Jackhammer with a neckbreaker drop. Wretch climbed to the top turnbuckle and jumped for a moonsault, but Jackhammer pulled both knees up in the nick of time. As Wretch gasped for air Jackhammer brought him up and back down with a scoop slam. Jackhammer tagged out to Sphere, who entered the ring and gave Wretch boot after boot. Sphere bounced off the ropes and drove a fist into the skull of Wretch, then covered, but only got a two count. Wretch pulled himself to his feet as his opponent bounced off the ropes and just barely dodged his attempt at a lariat. Sphere rebounded off the opposite ropes, but as he did so Bear gave him a forearm to the back. Sphere spun around and jawed with Bear until Wretch came up behind him and rolled the Ape up for a two count. Sphere scrambled to his feet, but as he did so Wretch dropkicked him in the face. Sphere popped up again, and this time Wretch nailed him with a jumping swinging DDT, driving his head into the mat. Sphere was out of it and Jackhammer knew this, so Jackhammer charged Wretch in the ring. Jackhammer grabbed Wretch and chucked him to the outside, prompting Bear to charge Jackhammer. The two men exchanged blows until Bear staggered the musician and Sphere grabbed Bear by the shoulder. Bear spun around, ducked a right hook, then gave Sphere the Whiplash. Sphere rolled out of the ring in pain as Bear ducked a clothesline from Jackhammer, turned him around, and executed another Whiplash. As a prone Jackhammer lay on the canvas Wretch slapped Bear on the back and hopped into the ring. Bear turned and demanded to know what Wretch was doing, and Wretch just starred at him before walking over to Jackhammer for the pin. But, before Wretch could cover, Jackhammer rolled him up in a small package. Bear ran to save but Sphere pulled him out of the ring. One, two, three, and the Guano Apes were the new tag team champions. As Jackhammer and Sphere celebrated their win and titles Wretch solemnly headed to the back while Bear looked after him... Result: Guano Apes by Pinfall Retirment Slaughter comes back from commercial to see Ice standing in the ring, holding a microphone. Instead of his usual angry or scowling countenance, Ice wears a serious look on his face. He pauses a moment to collect his thoughts, then begins speaking. "Last night, I was in a Triple Threat match with Scott Collins and Perfect. As you know, I was the first man eliminated. By Perfect. The man who brought me into the fWo. Since I've joined the fWo, I've done basically everything one man can do. I've held the World Tag Team Titles, the Internet Title, I've helped to turn back the Dark Riders multiple times, I've been a part of two stables that were great while they lasted... and I'm one of five men who have been fWo World Heavyweight Champion three times. One hundred and twenty-five men have competed in the fWo... and I'm one of the five who has accomplished that feat. I'm one of that elite four percent... but there is one thing I have never done since I've joined the fWo. That's defeat my former friend Perfect one-on-one. I've had two tries at it... April 1999, and at Twilight in December. Both times he pinned me one, two, three in the middle of the ring. And if he can do that... maybe he is better than me. *Maybe*. But... I want one last shot to prove that I've got what it takes to kick his ass once and for all. Scott, listen to me and listen to me real good. You and me. Wednesday Warfare. No disqualifications, no countouts. And if I can't beat you, I see no reason to embarrass myself further... if I lose, I'll retire." With this shocking challenge issued, Ice tosses the mic out of the ring and begins to walk silently to the back, knowing full well his career could be over in a matter of days... as Slaughter continues. Primetime vs. Perfect The fans stood to their feet as time had finally come for the main event of Slaughter. As announced earlier in the evening, Perfect reminded the fans of the title shots he and Dead gave each themselves last month and that is why he is facing Primetime here tonight. You'll also note that the last time Primetime & Perfect met it was for the fWo World Title and Primetime won; although some could claim that it was because Perfect was not at one hundred percent due to an injury occurred from the hands of The Boston Strangler. "Humans Being" started to blast throughout the soundsystem of the Kemper Arena, and an ominous red spotlight covered the arena. The only four time World Champion in the history of the fWo walked out onto the stage and was immediately greeted with a negative ovation from the fans. Perfect paced to the ring promptly and climbed into the ropes, upon where he raised his hands to an even louder amount of boos than what he received prior. The boos somewhat turned to cheers as "Blind" by Korn began to play, and the man who had been the fWo World Champion for not even a full twenty four hours came out. The fans didn't like either man but they hated Perfect more and showed it. As Primetime neared ringside Perfect circled the ring, and Primetime entered the ring. He immediately gave the title to the referee, who held it high above for everyone to see. Perfect saw his opportunity as Primetime walked into a corner and he attacked him from behind with a double axehandle, before slamming him head first into the top turnbuckle several times. He then turned him around in the corner and hit him with a multitude of shots, before bringing him near the ropes and whipping him. Despite the punishment that had been dealt, Primetime had the presence of mind to reverse the Irish Whip. Perfect returned and Primetime took to him with an elbow to the chest. Primetime brought him up and took him down with a hip toss, before bringing him up once more and kneeing him in the midsection. He then followed it up with a reverse neckbreaker, before going for the pin. Perfect kicked out at one and a half, and Primetime continued. He brought Perfect up by the hair and whipped him into the turnbuckles. He charged in for a splash, but Perfect moved and Primetime smacked the turnbuckles. From behind, Perfect rolled him up for a pin. A hook of the tights almost won the match for Perfect, but Primetime was able to narrowly kick out. Perfect continued by going out onto the floor and bringing a chair into the ring, and as he went to whack Primetime with it the referee grabbed it from behind. Perfect turned to confront the referee, and from behind a high knee leveled Perfect. Perfect however smacked into the referee, and the referee spilled out onto the floor. From behind Primetime took Perfect down with a belly-to-back suplex, before a cover. He finally realized the referee was out of it, and continued on the offensive. He went to the second rope and came off with an elbow , and again the referee was out of it. Finally, Primetime went to the floor and began helping the referee up, but this just gave the World's Greatest Athlete time to recover. Perfect slid out onto the floor with the chair, and whacked Primetime in the back with it. He then hit a vertical suplex on the outside, before rolling Primetime into the ring. He went for a cover, but the referee was nowhere to be found still. Perfect went out onto the floor and took the referee's limp body and rolled him underneath the bottom rope into the ring, before he went to the top rope and flew off with a frog splash. Another cover, but the referee was still out of it. Perfect brought Primetime up and hit the Perfect Game, before the referee finally came to. The referee counted. One. Two. Right before three, Courage arrived to save the day by pulling Perfect out onto the floor. He and Perfect began duking it out as the crowd cheered wildly, before Mitch Wilson arrived on the scene and he and Courage brawled up to the back. Perfect recovered before he went back into the ring, and covered Primetime once more. One. Two. Primetime kicked out. Perfect was undaunted, but believed the only reason Primetime was able to kick out of his finisher was because of Courage' interference, which was not seen by the referee. Perfect brought Primetime up once more, and looked to be going for one of his old finishers the 'Curveball', and he took Primetime near the corner. He hooked him, and went to the second rope. He flew off, but somehow Primetime had the strength to stop him and bring him down in an inverted atomic drop, which staggered Perfect slightly, before Primetime kicked him in the stomach and began going for the Primetime Bomb. Perfect backdropped out of it however, but Primetime held one for the sunset flip. Referee slid over to make the cover. One. Two. Perfect got a shoulder up at two and three quarters. Both men were out of it, and the referee was as well, even though he was beginning to bring himself up near a corner via help from the ropes. Primetime and Perfect got to their feet and charged at each other, and both men had the same idea as they went for a clothesline simultaneously. Both men hit, but this was not a good thing as they fell to the mat. The referee slowly but surely began a ten count. One. Two. Three. Four. Neither man had moved. Five. Six. Primetime finally got up to his knees, before pulling himself up at the count of nine. Primetime brought Perfect up by the hair and whipped him into the corner. Primetime came into the corner and Perfect went for a superkick. Primetime ducked however, and instead the left foot of Perfect caught the referee in the jaw again. Perfect turned around and Primetime kicked him in the stomach, before hitting the Primetime bomb. Primetime was out of it, so was Perfect, and finally Primetime slid over for the cover. The referee was out of it, but a second referee came down to the ring. He counted. One. Two. Somehow, someway, almost unbelievably, Perfect got a shoulder up right as the referee's hand was to hit the mat for the third and final time. The match continued. Primetime realized that the only way Perfect could have kicked out of his finisher was because the first referee was out of it, and a second referee had to charge to the ring to count the pin. Undaunted, Primetime went on anyways. He brought Perfect up and hit him with a bevy of chops, before whipping him to the ropes. Perfect countered with a reversal, and as Primetime returned Perfect went for a Samoan Drop, but Primetime fell behind Perfect. He spun him around and kicked him in the stomach, before going for a Death Valley Driver, which he hit. Cover. One. Two. Perfect was able to muster up enough energy to drape his foot over the bottom rope to break up the pin. Primetime, sensing he had to do something... special to get the pin, pondered where to go from here. He was exhausted, and was barely moving as the match had taken a lot out of him. He brought Perfect up and elbowed him in the stomach, before hooking him for another belly-to-back suplex. Instead he brought him up and placed him on the top turnbuckle, as the first referee began to move. It appeared he was going for the Primetime Bomb off the top rope, which won the match for him last night against Frenchie. However, instead, he went for a Russian Legsweep off the top rope, of which he also went for last night against the Frenchie. He hit it, and both men hit the mat with authority. They were out of it, and both men mustered up enough to simply drape an arm over one another. Both referee's began to count. One. Two. Three. The second referee went to the outside and retrieved the World Title. He brought it into the ring and raised Perfect's hand, but the first referee disagreed and took the World Title from him and gave it to Primetime, before raising his hand. Both men were still out of it and moving slowly on the mat, confused at what had happened. The referees continued to disagree with one another before Chris Finn & James Armstrong came out and conferred with both referees. Primetime and Perfect slowly made it to their feet, and the referees informed Finn & Armstrong what had happened and why each man thought they were correct in making the call as Sunday Slaughter went off the air... a dark cloud of confusion over the fWo.
