Sunday Slaughter, 30 July 2000

30 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction fWo Sunday Night Slaughter took to the air with an opening video. The video was not the regular opening of Sunday Night Slaughter, but instead was a video package of what went down at SummerSlaughter. The SummerSlaughter logo exploded onto the screen, and exploded off of it right into a black and white image of The Black Panther getting ready to suplex Fallen. Chronos comes up from behind and begins to german suplex Panther at the same time, for an unprecendented move. This black and white shot fades into color of Chronos holding his newly won fWo International Title. The image of Chronos holding his first (and surely not last) fWo singles title fades, before one word in white text on a black background appears on the screen. DETERMINATION The text slowly falls off the screen, before we spot Pamela Dee talking on her cell phone at ringside. The announcers talk about what she's up to, before fWo Camerawoman Kristen Jacobs makes a surprise appearance, and distracts Ice. Moments later we see a shot of Kelly "eradicating" Ice off the top rope with a powerbomb, before taking him out with a cinderblock. That image fades, and again a single word appears on the screen in the same manner as before. ERADICATION The text falls off the screen, before individual shots of the California Kings appear on the screen, and the former fWo World Tag Team Champions - Damnation - as well. We see more black and white shots of action from the fWo World Tag Title Match, before finally a shot of Damnation preparing for their finisher bursts onto the screen. War & Plague hit the ring and ruin all that, before we see Star planting Fear into the mat with his 'Shooting Star' finisher, and then Courage hitting the 'Courage Required' for the pinfall victory. More text resides on the screen. THE KINGS ARE CROWNED The text zooms off the screen, before an overhead shot of Mitch Wilson, Primetime, Wretch & Rain converging in the center of the ring is shown. This shot quickly moves to all four men going at it in certain sections of the ring. Shot after shot of is shown of high impact moves such as the 'Headliner', Primetime Bomb, Rainmaker & Depression are shown. Shots of Abusah & Scot Hartt getting involved. Shots of Moolah & Mae Young making a guest appearance. The number ten appears on the screen, before a shot of a groggy Wretch staggering is shown. The number nine comes up, before he walks right into a Headliner, which is briefly interrupted by the numbers eight, seven, & six are shown. A shot of Wilson moving Wretch into position for a pinfall attempt is shown as we hear the announcers in the background questioning if Wilson can make the cover in time. The referee's hand hitting the mat at 2.5 seconds is shown, then 1.5 seconds, and finally 0.5 seconds. As his hand hits the mat, an image of Wilson and Ice embracing in the ring appears on the screen, before more text appears. THE MAN OF THE HOUR This fades to black, before we find Slugger & Eron on top of a cage. We then spot Slugger being hiptossed off the cage, the video teases Slugger falling through the table, but quickly we find Slugger holding onto the side of the cage in an impressive fashion. Finally, he's knocked off. The war continues as Slugger & Eron uses parts of the cage to inflict mayhem & pain on one another, before finally we see Slugger hit the 'Curveball' in the ring, before using the inside top of the cage as monkey bars before hitting an impressive legdrop on Eron for the pinfall victory. Text bursts onto the screen as the image of Slugger having his arm raised fades to black. TO HELL AND BACK That text falls off the screen, and we see a close-up shot of Mike Bear walking down the ramp. We see a fast-paced shot of Strangler walking down the ramp as well, before the two men lock-up in the ring. Shots of The Strangler kicking out of the Whiplash, Bear escaping from the Massacre, Strangler kicking out of the 'Super Whiplash', Bear kicking out of the German Suplex, Bear kicking out of the 'Super Release German Suplex' are shown. Bear hits a death valley driver, but somehow Strangler kicks out of that. We see a still of Bear preparing to send Strangler into the corner, before the next image is of Bear holding his face with his back to the Strangler, and then finally we see the final ten seconds of the match as Bear can no longer endure that much pain and taps out. We see the two men congratulate each other and briefly embrace, before SummerSlaughter goes off the air. SUMMERSLAUGHTER: THE EPIC The video stops playing, and we head inside the arena for the beginning of the July 30th edition of Sunday Night Slaughter, live from Colorado Springs, Colorado. Major Announcements Fireworks explode. Sirens blare. And "Start Me Up" by Rolling Stones begins to play, signalling the entrance of the owner of the fans Wrestling organization, Chris Finn. Finn walks out onto the SlaughterStage dressed in a suit and tie, looking quite dapper for a night like tonight. Finn is greeted mainly by cheers, but some boos are scattered throughout the crowd as not everyone likes the guy. No matter. He enters the ring with jubilation, quite pleased about the events that transpired last night at the fWo pay-per-view - SummerSlaughter 2000. The announcers remind everyone at home that you can order the encore Monday night on pay-per-view. Finn asks for a microphone and is quickly given one, before Finn begins to address the sold-out crowd inside the World Arena in Colorado Springs, Colorado. "Thank you for joining us here tonight in what appears to be one of the most anticipated Sunday Night Slaughter's in recent memory. If you saw SummerSlaughter either in live or in person, we appreciate your support and hope you were treated to a fine night of fans Wrestling organization action. In any event, on August 17th we have a very special event forthcoming. As many of you well know, last December 30, one of our wrestlers - Joy - was tragically killed 'on the job'. The incident has been put behind us; but the pain will forever remain. Joy was a man who truly brought 'joy' to the workplace, and his presence is dearly missed. To honor this man on August 17 we are going to present a Memorial Show in his memory, and as many of you already knew, it will be the Ryan Moore Memorial Show live on Thursday, August 17, from the United Center in Chicago, Illinois. Late last night after SummerSlaughter ended, we immediately began planning for that event, and at this time I'd like to announce the main event for that special show. In the main event, for the fWo World Championship, The Boston Strangler will defend that strap against Scott Slugger. In order to prevent any interference from occuring like it did the last time the two men fought three weeks ago, any wrestler interfering in the match will face very, very strict punishment. However, there's much more to the main event than that. It will truly be a historical match, because if The Boston Strangler should retain he will beat the record eighty-nine day title reign that ironically Slugger set last year the very next day. However, should he lose, Scott Slugger will indeed set the pinnacle for any champion to look up to, with a record five fWo World Title reigns. More matches for the Memorial Show will be announced in the coming weeks, and I'm told our webmasters are busy getting a special area ready for the Show on fWowrestling.com. Now, I have a special announcement regarding our next big pay-per-view on August 26. As many longtime fans know Meltdown plays host to a special tag team battle royal. Days before the event, teams are chosen at random. Teams also choose from numbers one through twenty which order they will enter this match. Teams will enter every two minutes until the ring is full. At any time one individual from a team is eliminated, so is his partner. The only way to be eliminated is by being eliminated over the top rope and both feet touch the floor. The last team remaining will then fight in a cage match promptly after the match has occured, and whoever wins via pinfall, submission, or exiting the cage in any manner will be the official winner. The winner will then go onto receive a 'full sweep' of fWo Title shots over the span of one month. They will get shots at the Cruiserweight Title, the Hardcore Title, the International Title, they may choose their partner for a Tag Title Shot, and of course shots at the Internet & World Titles are also in order. In 1998, the fWo World Title was vacant and Ice ended up being the first Meltdown winner to achieve the glory of winning the fWo World Title. In 1999, Primetime received a full sweep of titles and walked away winning the Cruiserweight, Women's and fWo World Titles, however during different durations of time. Tonight, however, we're going to mix it up a bit by having a Meltdown 'Choose Your Fate' match between eight competitors. Now, those eight competitors may already be wrestling in other matches here tonight, but regardless they will still be competing in the Choose Your Fate match at the end of the night, which will be an elimination style match. The winner of this match-up will have two options regarding the Meltdown tag team battle royal. They can choose their partner, or they can choose to pick the twenty spot in the Meltdown match and their team will enter last, although their partner will be assigned randomly. The competitors of the Choose Your Fate match are as follows: The Boston Strangler, Scott Slugger, Mike Bear, Eron The Relentless, Mitch Wilson, Primetime, Wretch & Rain. Good luck to you gentlemen, and fans, I hope you enjoy what is about to transpire here tonight." Fireworks resounded as the theme for Finn played once again, before Slaughter went to a preview of Meltdown, followed by it's first commercial break of the evening. Ric Chronos (c) vs. Crusader Slaughter heads to the ring for an International Title match, and the fans are pumped. "Switchback" starts to play, and the fans come to their feet for the arrival of Crusader, a former fWo Cruiserweight Champion. Crusader catapults himself up and into the ring, before handing the belt to the referee who passes it along to the timekeeper. "Christian Says" then replaces Crusader's theme, and the cheers intensify as the new International Champion Ric Chronos appears on the entrance ramp, belt held high. Chronos then places the belt over his shoulder and heads down to the ring, before passing the belt off to the timekeeper and rolling into the ring. The ref signals for the bell and the match is on. Crusader starts it quickly by charging at Chronos, but Chronos counters with an armdrag. Crusader is quickly up however and leaps high before nailing Chronos with a superb dropkick that sends him back into the corner. Crusader charges into the corner with a leaping splash, before flipping Chronos out of the corner by busting out a rarely-used monkey flip. Crusader quickly runs into the ropes and drops a leg, but Chronos kicks out before even a one count can be reached. Chronos brings himself back up to his feet, and as he gets up, Crusader whips him into the ropes. Crusader tries for a second dropkick, but Chronos grabs the ropes to hold himself and Crusader crashes down to the mat. Chronos quickly hooks both legs and flips forward into a pinning position, but Crusader kicks out at two. Both men are quickly up, and this time Chronos gains the advantage, catching Crusader with a leaping forearm shot. Chronos immediately sets up Crusader and whips him into the ropes, before dropping him with a ring-shaking powerslam. Chronos drops down and hooks a leg, but Crusader kicks out at two. Chronos brings Crusader up and whips him hard into the corner, before charging in with a hard clothesline. He then measures Crusader and blasts him with a stinging chop, and Crusader fights back with a open palm slap to the chest. Chronos fires back with a chop, and Crusader hits a chop of his own, followed by a second, followed by a third to gain the advantage. He whips Chronos hard into the opposite corner, and runs off the ropes before catching Chronos stumbling out with a bulldog. Crusader immediately heads to the ring apron as Chronos pulls himself to his feet, and springboards himself onto the top rope. Chronos turns around, and Crusader leaps off with a Thesz press that finds its target. The ref drops down to count, but Chronos kicks out just before the count of three is tolled. Both men are again quickly back up and Crusader throws a kick at Chronos, who catches it. Crusader leaps up for an enzuiguri, but Chronos has this sequence well scouted and he ducks it. Crusader crashes down to the mat, and Chronos pulls him up and hooks his head in position for the Chronosphere. Crusader has that scouted himself and shoves Chronos into the ropes, and puts his head down. Chronos leapfrogs Crusader and quickly grabs him in a waistlock, before lifting him up and throwing him backwards with a german suplex. Chronos bridges up, and the ref drops down to make the count. Three seconds later, the ref signals for the bell. Chronos gestures for his belt, but Crusader does so as well, and a look at the replay shows that the shoulders of both men were down. The ref had no choice but to count both men down, and he declares the match a no contest. With the verdict rendered, fellow good sportsmen Chronos and Crusader shake on a contest, each looking as if they plan on a rematch in the near future. Both begin to seperately head to the back, but Chronos' journey is interrupted by the Black Panther, who gets in his way and begins to poke fun of Chronos for not being able to defeat someone as 'small' and 'weak' as Crusader. Chronos brushes it off for a while, but finally he starts to haul off when Crusader hooks his arm from behind, stopping the punch. Undaunted, the Panther throws a few comments Crusader's way. Crusader shrugs them off and turns as if to ignore Panther, but then whirls around and clocks the larger man on the jaw. Panther fires back and the two men begin to brawl on the entrance ramp. A group of officials led by Fallen are quickly out to break up the fight, and Fallen and Chronos, Crusader, and Black Panther each head backstage, keeping wary eyes on each other. Slaughter, meanwhile, rolls on... Result: No Contest, Chronos retains THERE JUDJEMEANT DAY IS KNOW Cameras took us backstage to find Wretch on a motorcycle with his lackey's in toe following behind him on tricycles, just as they did last night at SummerSlaughter. Tonight, however, they were without facepaint, and looked a little worse for wear. Abusah of course though was in good spirits. "EYE AM AN AMERRYCUN BADAZZ!" screamed the illiterate lackey of Scot Hartt. Wretch & Scott Hart simply rolled their eyes. Despite having lost last night in the epic Four-Way Iron Man Match, Wretch looked a little chipper. "So, what did those two old broads do to you guys, anyways?" Wretch inquired, obviously referring to their shenanigans that happened last night as they took part of the company of The Fabulous Moolah & Mae Young. Hartt rolled his eyes. "Dude, can we just not talk about it?" Wretch smiled. "Couldn't get it up, eh?" Hartt sighed. Wretch continued to pester his lackey in good nature. "Was she too much for you to handle?" Hartt again sighed. "Yeah, well at least I won't be forever known as the guy who jobbed to wood in the final second of the first ever four-way Iron Man Match." Wretch wasn't happy with that comment, but managed to turn that negative into a positive. "Dude, still, that's better than being known as the guy who had secks with an eighty year old woman, or however old she is." Hartt glared at Wretch, but Wretch ignored it. Turning his attention to Abusah, he found Abusah still spouting off the lyrics to Kid Rock' "American Badass". "WEN YA GUNNA LERN SUCKA... U JUS KANT FUCK WITH... TWISTID BROWN! YAH! BIOTCH!" Wretch parked his motorcycle, and his lackeys parked their tricycles. Wretch shrugged. "So, Abusah... what's it like not being a virgin?" Abusah shrugged. "A VERJIN? WAT IS THAT?" Hartt took him aside and whispered in his ear. "O! RITE! SECKS IS GOOD! Y DO U ASK?" Wretch laughed, before he walked away, his lackeys trying to catch up... The Package Finn reached his office, and inside a package was found. James Armstrong was already inside trying to find out what the contents were, but Finn stopped him. "Hey, isn't it a federal crime to open someone else's mail?" Armstrong scoffed. "Yeah, like you're going to prosecute me for opening a package addressed to you. You seemingly have no problem having your porn arrive at my house and letting me view that a few days extra, right?" Finn glared at his bald headed right hand man. "Hey! You know the only reason I do that is to pay you back for letting me let all my mags and videos show up at your house. You know my wife would kill me if she found out I was looking at porn again." Armstrong smiled. "Right, that's why you shouldn't care if I open this..." Armstrong opened the package and pulled out the Cruiserweight Title. "What? What the hell is this?" Finn shrugged. "Black Quicksilver said he'd mail it to me... he was going on some Road Trip to find Stump or something like that." Armstrong' raised eyebrows and looked at Finn in bewilderment. "Black Quicksilver? He's still around? I thought we released him like... a year ago. And why the hell did he mail you the Cruiserweight Title?" Finn explained to James that BQ was the champion and since he felt he had to find his companion Stump, he wouldn't be around to defend the title and thus sent it back to Finn before he went on his Road Trip. Armstrong shrugged. "Well, it's worthless if you ask me." Armstrong tossed it in the garbage can, and Finn stood up angrily and began to shout at Armstrong, but Armstrong ignored it. "Yadda yadda yadda. Wrestling is no place for gymnasts, and that's all cruiserweights are." We headed back to the ring for our second match of the night. Damnation vs. War & Plague Slaughter heads to the ring for a big tag team matchup. "Through The Never" by Metallica starts to play, and Chaos (War and Plague), accompanied by a camera-wielding Stone, head to the ring to a chorus of loud boos. Their music is replaced by "From This Day", and the boos are replaced by cheers as Damnation, fresh off losing the World Tag Team Titles, head to the ring, accompied by Lamont, who Hellrazor is carrying in his official Hardcore Feline Transport Carrier. Hellrazor sets the carrier down at ringside and Damnation rolls into the ring, and the referee calls for the bell. Fear and Plague start off, and they circle. Fear challenges the much smaller Plague to a test of strength, and Plague surprisingly accepts. Before Fear can gain the advantage, Plague kicks him hard in the gut. He then leaps onto Fear's shoulders, before spinning around and down into a victory roll. Fear escapes at one, and tries a lariat as he gets up, but Plague ducks it and hits Fear with a dropkick as he turns around. Fear doesn't go down, and instead is knocking into the corner. Plague charges in with a hardy boyz-style leaping leg lariat, and connects with his target. Fear stumbles out, and Plague bounces off the ropes, grabs his head, and leaps, going for a bulldog. Fear is too strong however, and uses his strength to reverse the momentum and drop Plague back in a back suplex. He quickly brings the smaller man back up and whips him into the ropes, before catching him coming off with a devastating powerslam. Fear makes a cover, but Plague kicks out at two. Fear brings him back up and goes behind him, before hooking a full nelson. He then lifts Plague high in the air and slams him to the mat in a sitting position, jarring his spine. Instead of covering, he heads over to Hellrazor and tags him in, and lifts up Plague in a side slam position. Hellrazor hops onto the second rope, and as Fear slams him down, Hellrazor leaps off with an elbowdrop. Hellrazor makes the cover, but War breaks up the count and saves the match for his team. The referee escorts him out of the ring, and Hellrazor whips Plague into the ropes. War takes the opportunity to make a blind tag, and as Hellrazor lifts Plague up in a gorilla press position, War takes him down with an Abusah-esque spear. He quickly gets on top of Hellrazor and begins pounding on him, and on the apron, Fear takes exception to this and hits the ring. Plague cuts him off, and the two teams quickly begin to brawl. The referee attempts to restore order, but it cannot be done and he has no choice but to call the match a double DQ. Stone meanwhile hits the ring and low blows Hellrazor, before lobbing her camera to Plague. Plague reaches inside and rips out the film, before choking Hellrazor with it. He meanwhile tosses the camera over his head where it is caught by War, who nails Fear with it. At ringside, Lamont hisses angrily, but cannot escape his carrier. Finally, a group of refs hit the ring and pull Chaos off Damnation. Chaos decides to allow them to do so, and laugh at the pummeled Damnation before heading to the back to more loud boos. Slaughter, meanwhile, continues... Result: Double Disqualification "D" Slaughter returned from commercials. The cameras panned down across the sea of fans, thousands of whom held sign supporting their favorite fWo wrestler. Or in some people's cases, hating their least favorite fWo wrestler. Signs like "Big Wretchy = Ratings" and "Rateings R' Us = Secks Krayzed Meanieacs" were seen, as well as signs like "can i join????" and "fwo sucks" were also seen. Unlike other organizations, the fWo let any sign in the arena, and sometimes that was a bad thing as seen by the "SID SNO IS GAY!!!!!" sign. Nevertheless, as the lights clicked on, the fans got ready for the next match, when suddenly, the lights went out. An eerie music began to play, before the appearance of fog masked the ring. The lights came back on and the same man that stood in the ring at the end of the Hell In A Cell match last night with Eron tied to a cross was visable. He was dressed in black from head to toe, and a mask covered his face. His tall, wiry frame was unidentifiable, especially because of the black masked that covered his face. The lights dimmed again, and a lone spotlight shone on the masked man. Suddenly, the fans turned their attention to the SlaughterScreen, which was black, save for one letter that appeared in smoky, white text. D. The lights flicker, before fading once again, and when they come back on, the ring is empty, devoid of any remains of the man in black. Slaughter went backstage once again. Rematch Another night, another city. Another door, same knock. "NOCK NOCK!" yelled Abusah, just as he had last week on Warfare. "Who's there?" answered Chris Finn, just as he had last week on Warfare. Finn got up to answer it, and before he could say a word, Team Ratings was in his office. Abusah & Hartt looked around, while Wretch casually glanced at a few things. Finn wanted to know why there were here, however, and Finn wanted to know why right now. "Wretch, what the hell do you guys want?" Wretch smiled. "You're always in a bad mood, I swear. We had one of our most... hey, is that the Cruiserweight Title?" Finn nodded. "You know, I held that belt once. I'd always wondered what happened to the thing..." Finn let out a groan. "James here just tried to throw it in the garbage." Wretch gave Armstrong an angry stare. "What? How dare you! That thing... that thing is worth a lot, man. I think that was the first singles title Big Wretchy ever held... or was it the Tag Title? I don't remember. Anyways, Finn... I was just wondering... you saw the end of that Iron Man Match last night, right? I was *SOOOOOO* close to just winning the damn thing... and then Mr. Uncharismatic had to go and put me through a table. What I was wondering if he and I could have a match on Warfare... a rematch. Now, yes, I know it's not a rematch because Rain & Primetime were involved, but... but... he pinned me, and I feel it was a fluke, and I feel your ratings will go up at least... ten percent this Wednesday if Mitch & I have a rematch. So, what do you say, eh?" Finn pondered it, before agreeing to it, just to get Team Ratings out of his office. "Yeah, sure, whatever. Leave me alone, I've got work to do... and you've got a match to prepare for." Wretch nodded with a big smile. "Yeah, but I've got a few days to prepare... which is totally sooooo cool." Finn threw a curveball in his plans. "Nope... you've got a match to wrestle tonight." Wretch gulped. "Tonight?" Finn explained. "Yep, it's a Meltdown 'Choose Your Fate' match. You against seven other guys... the winner will choose his partner, or he can choose to have his partner assigned at random, and instead be the final team to enter." Wretch' eyes lit up. "Really?" Finn nodded. "Yeah. It's a double-edged sword, really. You can have the best partner in the world and still lose. For instance, in 1998... Stevie Roberts had Austin Davis as his partner. They were the two most recent fWo World Champions at the time, and they still lost." Wretch scratched his head. "True." Finn continued. "That same year, Jim Dudley & David Spencer were the final team to enter. They didn't win." Wretch nodded. "I remember that." Finn again continued. "Or, to give you another example, last year the final entrants were a dream team of the Frenchie & Chris Universal. They didn't win, and Primetime slipped through the cracks with his most hated rival of all time - Wolf Fang - as his partner to win." Wretch nodded... "Yeah, well... my partners sucked... Total Eclipse in 1998... and that VL chick last year... I just couldn't win! But this year... hopefully, I'll get lucky. Hey, speaking of VL, whatever happened to her?" The owner of the fWo sighed, then shrugged. "Last I saw she was trying to make her way into the fWo, and she was supposed to be here tonight, but she's not." Wretch looked away. "Figures. Well, I'm outta here... thanks for the rematch on Slaughter, Finnster!" Wretch and his lackeys walked away, while Abusah continued to sing Kid Rock songs. "BAHWITDABAH DA DANGY DANG BING BING DING UP JUMP DA BOOGEY!" Mike Bear vs. Primetime Slaughter's cameras took to the ring once more for a huge match-up between Primetime & Mike Bear. Both men are coming off losses last night at SummerSlaughter, but if the saying about only being as good as your last match is true, then these men are certain to do well tonight. "Blind" by Korn explodes throughout the arena, pulsating off the World Arena walls. This immediately prompts the fans to boo as a result, as they know the man about to appear is someone they don't like. Primetime walks out onto the SlaughterStage, and looks amongst the sea of booing fans. He shrugs them off, before walking down the ramp and entering the ring. Fireworks explode for him, and he salutes the crowd with an angry glare. The fans shoot back with more jeers, and Primetime ignores them. He stretches out in the ring, as "Dragula" begins to play. The fans turn their attention to the SlaughterStage, and out walks the man who was *this close* to winning the fWo World Title last night. Mike Bear walked out, and held his injured arm close to him. Even though he's injured, his dedication to the fWo is what brought him to the next level, and he isn't about to give up now. He walked down and exchanged high fives with some of the fans, before entering the ring via underneath the bottom rope. As he did, Primetime immediately took to his shoulder, kicking and stomping away. He went at Bear like a pit bull, seeking to destroy him and work him over for a quick victory. The bell officially rung, and the match was on. Primetime continued to stomp away at Bear, before bringing him up and taking him down with an armdrag takedown. He locked in an armbar, and stayed with it for a few moments, continuing to work Bear over. The referee asked Bear if he wanted to give up, and Bear responded with a "fuck you!", obviously implying no. Primetime brought Bear up again, and sent him into the ropes. Bear came out staggering, and Primetime took him to the canvas with a head and arms suplex. Primetime went for a quick cover, which garnered just a two count. Primetime winded up and droped a knee into the weakened arm of Bear three consecutive times, before going for another cover. Again, Bear kicked out at two. Undaunted, Primetime brought him up and sent him to the ropes. Bear returned and PT took him down with an academic drop-toe-hold, before applying a move dubbed by "Lord" Steven Regal as the "Regal Stretch". Bear writhed in pain, but his never say die attitude showed through. After being in the hold for over a minute, Primetime got frustrated, and then brought Bear up and sent him into the corner. Primetime followed in, but Bear somehow managed to push himself up using the ropes, and PT was met with a high knee to the face. PT turned out towards the ring, and Bear bounced off the ropes before hitting a jumping bulldog with his uninjured arm. He then tended to his injured arm momentarily, before going for a cover. PT kicked out at two, and Bear went on the offensive. He brought Primetime up, sent him to the ropes, and as PT returned he was taken down with a high impact powerslam. Bear followed it up with a frogsplash attempt, which he hit. Bear went for a cover, but again Primetime kicked out. Last night's challenger in the main event then brought Primetime up, and went for a Death Valley Driver, the same move he nearly won the fWo World Title with last night. Primetime escaped however and fell behind Bear, before flawlessly executing a side Russian legsweep. He hit it, then backed up, and went to the top rope. He flew off with a legdrop, but Bear moved out of the way. Bear then covered him, but Primetime got a foot on the ropes at two. The match continued, picking up a higher pace as it went on. Bear brought up and shot Primetime into the ropes, he returned, and Bear struck with a superkick. This sent Primetime up and over the top rope onto the floor, and Bear waited for PT to get up on the outside. He then backed up and went for a baseball slide, but PT sidestepped it. PT then went for a clothesline, but Bear ducked it. PT then turned around and caught Bear' kick attempt, but then Bear countered with an enziguiri. Bear then sent Primetime up and over the guardrail, and followed him into the crowd. The two brawled throughout the thousands of Colorado natives, and at one point Primetime went for his Primetime Bomb in the crowd. Bear countered with a backdrop, and Primetime smacked the cement floor. Somehow, they ended fighting backstage, before making their way back onto the stage. Bear had Primetime in a headlock, but Primetime countered into a belly-to-back suplex. He then kicked Bear' weakened arm several times, before bringing him down near ringside, and rolling him into the ring. Primetime stopped to verbally berate a few fans, and as Bear got to his feet in the ring, Primetime came in from behind. He appeared to be going for the backdrop driver, but Bear countered with a mule kick. He then turned around, saw Primetime, and went for another DVD. This time he hit it, and went for a cover. Primetime kicked out at two, and that's when Bear knew he had to turn up the heat. He sent Primetime into the ropes, went for a backdrop, but Primetime went for the PT Bomb again.
