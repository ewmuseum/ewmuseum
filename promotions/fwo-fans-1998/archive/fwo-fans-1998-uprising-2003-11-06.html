Introduction The impact of the Wrestler of the Year tournament is still being felt... Arrival The camera panned the outside of the arena, awaiting the arrival of some key superstars. What the boys in the production truck didn't expect, however, was to see the cameraman roll over to one side, knocked out. The camera quickly turned to the bandaged, wobbly face of Jon Crisp. "Sean," he breathed, making mention of Black Quicksilver. "I'm coming inside. Be prepared." Crisp threw the camera down, which smashed. The scene immediately turned to Silver Fox, who sighed. "Oh, Christ...here we go..." And The Board Says... Behind the scenes at Uprising was, in one word, hectic. With the large fWo roster confined to such a limited space tensions always ran high, and Road Agents darted around on missions of peace or memo delivery. The walls did not escape the melee, and had become plastered with all kinds of notes and posters... The matches known about so far, referee allocations etc. Surrounded by the hastily scribbled literature was a sheet of white card, marked out with a table. On it were the names of 5 familiar fWo tag teams, all of who were vying for a shot at the WPO's prized belts... And who had thrown themselves into a round-robin tournament just to try and win a match for them. Emo Kids. LoD. The Villani Connection. Brotherly Love. Tim Walden & Jane Matheson. Beside the names of the Emo Kids and the Villani Connections, housed proudly in brackets, was 1-0... The winning record all teams wanted after their first match. 2 teams who hated one another, who'd fought one another many times, finally had something in common. Brotherly Love and the LoD also had something in common, albeit something less desirable. There was a pair of eyes observing this chart, and more precisely, glaring with disdain at Brotherly Love's record. "Bad starts aside," Elliot Harper seethed, "Tonight things turn around." Already decked out in his wrestling gear, you wouldn't think by looking at the elder Harper that he'd hardly clocked up 2 minutes of in-ring time since signing with the fWo. He wore the tights of a wrestler, had the toned physique of a superstar, the poise of a Champion... But the heart of a coward. Arms crossed, he was deep in thought, talking to himself in a slow, yet direct murmur. "We'd beaten the Emo Kids before, we should of beaten them again... We've beaten Walden and Matheson before, we WILL beat them again..." Then, his expression changed, and the pondering Elliot reverted back to the sneaky, conniving weasel he's better known as. "Or, the whole thing could simply be 'bypassed'?" The sides of his mouth rose, causing his cheeks to crease and form his trademark sinister smile. Then, after a quick glance at the clock, Elliot set off with purpose. In his haste, Harper was out of sight within seconds, only the sound of his boots on the tiled floor's echo being heard... Then, his voice. "Where's Silver Fox?" Elliot was heard asking someone with his usual lack of manners and respect. Where was Silver Fox.... And why did Elliot want him? Between Truth and Fiction "Hey, Yukio... a minute?" Yukio glanced back and saw Silver Fox leaning out of his makeshift office. She nodded and stepped inside; worrying slightly as she usually did when he shut the door. She fought back her paranoia; every event of late was one that needed to be questioned. Motives were never to be trusted and actions were almost always targeted... deliberate. Silver Fox sat down, not behind the folding table someone had set up for him as a desk, but on a chair and motioned for Yukio to do the same. She did, feeling her apprehension rise. "How is it to be back?" Yukio shrugged, not wanting to commit to anything in the event this was leading somewhere deliberate. Silver Fox glanced down at a paper he had withdrawn from his pocket and nodded at it. "It says here that you've completed the sessions we asked you to do. It looks like you made some real progress, did some good work. Are you glad now that you did it? That you went through with it? I know it wasn't completely your idea..." "No," Yukio snapped, slightly louder than she meant to. Composing herself, she nodded. "I suppose some good has come of it, yes. But I still don't understand..." Silver Fox signed and waved to cut her off. "It was something that had to be done. The Disney people wanted the therapy, and honestly in this instance I agreed with their assessment. I don't like forcing people into things, but I think this was likely good for you. This business... well, you know how it can be." Yukio nodded, feeling the flush on her face. She didn't like this topic. She hadn't been in favor of going to the mandatory sessions then, and even though she would admit now that they had been positive, the fact that she hadn't had a choice did not sit well with her. Perhaps sensing some of this, Silver Fox nodded and changed the topic. "Listen, I just wanted to touch base, see how things were going. I'm glad things are going well. I noticed you've been interacting with Temerario a lot lately... he's a good kid, I'm sure you can learn a lot from each other." Yukio's eyes flashed. "What's that supposed to mean? Look, it's an angle. It's money. If you think..." "No, no...," Silver Fox said, putting up his hands, "nothing like that. Look, I'm not trying to play matchmaker, I'm just saying." "Oh fuck what you're saying," Yukio said, standing up. "I thought you might understand how hard it is to be gay in this industry, but I guess it's all about pairing up the staff. Well thanks old man, but no thanks. I don't need your help to get laid!" Yukio slammed the door, leaving Silver Fox sitting surprised. He sighed and shook his head. Why had she gotten such a temper about it? Temerario was young, pretty innocent, and being paired up with someone might help him be happier about not taking risks. He was wrestling tonight in a hardcore match... and Silver Fox was worried about it. For Yukio, bonding with some members of the roster might help her feel like less of an outsider. Her anger had been so... so... Silver Fox sighed. If he didn't know better he would say that her assumption about what he had meant had strayed too close to the truth. If that was the case... well, romance was messy in wrestling. If Triple X and Crusader's recent examples were any indication. The last thing a woman coming off of therapy and a man questioning his own value in the industry needed was to throw a hopeless romance into the mix. But then, that was, as Silver Fox told himself, impossible. Revenge of the JOBBERS~! Rat and Dog, fWo's perennial jobbers. They may have been able to make it to the big show, but only because they seem to be the big show's bitch. They stood around the water cooler, discussing their plans for the evening. Flyer shoved his hands in a jazz hands style into the face of Rat as he said the word November, before starting his slow calm down. You animals should be proud.!" That's when the Animals grabbed Flyer by his head as he let out a "Woah! The belt!" before being RAMMED through the nearest wall, head first. Dry wall, but it still had to smart to say the least. Both men forced a man's skull through a wall. You don't see that everyday... especially not from jobbers. Rat and Dog walked away, satisfied for possibly the first time in their careers. The camera zoomed in on Flyer's bloody forehead, as his eyes rolled into the back of his head. "Where's Poochy go?" These were two men with a lot of history in the fWo, and in fact the fans remembered some of their more classic battles on fWo television from earlier that spring and summer in a very good way. But lo and behold, there you are on Ruahh bloodying Aimz in her return match. I guess you'll always just be an asshole deep down inside, won't you?" "You're surprisingly hard to find," snarled Elliot Harper, "For someone who doesn't have any purpose... Or at least not one I can see." He strode towards Sliver Fox, a man he had no respect for, and certainly no affection. The feelings were certainly mutual. Silver Fox reacted to the sound of Elliot's voice as one would to nails down a blackboard... Having formed a definite dislike for the Harper during the Survivor Competition, where he was unfortunate enough to be a liaison to Brotherly Love. He argued against the decision to let the Harpers in, but couldn't block it. Fox had seen too many good men, too many proud wrestlers enter to fWo to tolerate the snivelling weasel that was Elliot Harper. So, no, he wasn't happy to see him. "Maybe I'm hard to find," he retorted, his voice cold towards Elliot, "Because I didn't want you to find me?" Not even giving the respected fWo member's reply the time of day, Elliot simply blanked it and filled the doorway with his smaller-than-average frame. 'Chin up, chest out' was his pose, doing his best to look domineering, but Silver Fox knew him too well, and simply gestured for Harper to get it out of his system. "As I'M ready, I shall begin." Fox rolled his eyes, as if to say 'lucky me!'. "Now, as we all know, the World Peace Organisation have the fWo tag team titles... And good for them. Now, anyone with a booker's bone in their body would be throwing tag teams at them, making them defend every show... BUT some idiot..." Harper paused, very slightly, and smirked at Silver Fox. "...Decided to throw 5 teams into a round robin just to EARN a shot." "Well, maybe this person decided it was about time SOME people here earned something," he quipped back, his aged eyes still glimmering with the taunt, "Don't you?" Once more, Harper completely blanked Silver Foxes reply. "Now, probably by a memo mix-up, or admin mistake, the Emo Kids somehow got themselves a title shot tonight," Harper continued, while Fox's eyes narrowed at just the sound of Elliot mentioning the word's 'title' and 'shot'. "Which I'm assuming they'll lose, since I AM awake and we haven't stepped through the back of the wardrobe into Narnia... So I, Elliot Harper, on behalf of Brotherly Love DEMAND the next title shot!" "They got LUCKY!" was the best he could muster in reply, "So, after the WPO show them up as the flukes they are, I can go on to face the champions and show that Brotherly Love don't need to prove themselves worthy." The dramatic boast was quickly, and with much enjoyment, shot right down by Silver Fox. "No you can't, and yes you do." Wide eyed, Elliot could do nothing but stare back at the veteran. "Elliot Harper, I don't like you. "So, I'll tell you what I'm going to do," Fox decreed, seeing that Elliot was firmly on the back foot, "I'm going to close the door, whether you're in it's way or not... Then you're going to leave me in peace, preferably forever, and go earn a title shot in the round robin while I sit here and cheer on Walden & Matheson... Okay?" The door slammed shut as Elliot flinched back... His fists clenched and face red. They Came In Still looking decidedly grumpy following his "altercation" with Silver Fox, Elliot Harper burst through the curtain with a look of thunder about him. Behind Elliot, and looking a lot calmer, strode Thomas Harper... Ready to serve his brother as best he could, mainly correcting their 0-1 record. Into the ring they slid, having to fight through the round robin rather than find a backdoor route to a title shot. As soon as the brothers had climbed to their feet, Tim and Jane exchanged an all-knowing look, then charged them. In perfect sync, the couple sprung up from the mat into impressive vertical leaps and burst out with textbook Dropkicks. Tim's feet thrust into Thomas' chest, while Jane caught a shocked Elliot plush on the chin. The elder Harper staggered backwards and fell out through the ropes while Thomas stumbled a few paces in reverse, but managed to stay on his feet. As the bell rang for the match to kick-off officially, the fWo crowed popped at the sight of Elliot laying on the outside holding his mouth. Instinctively, Thomas charged Jane and Tim the moment they rose, trying for a double Clothesline, but the duo sprung into defensive action, seemingly knowing what the other would do. Matheson ducked Thomas' bulky arm with a forward roll, while Walden swung under the opposite limb and hooked in a Crucifix, pulling the younger Harper down but not forcing his shoulders onto the mat. Without even looking back to see what her partner was doing, Jane leapt onto the middle rope then sprung back with a Legdrop, landing straight across Thomas' throat which was being held a foot or so off the mat by Walden.
