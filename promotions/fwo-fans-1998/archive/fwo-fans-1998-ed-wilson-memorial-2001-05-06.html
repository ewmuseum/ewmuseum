A Tribute To Ed Wilson Tonight the fWo comes to you under unfortunate circumstances. The fWo was built on trying to be as real as possible. Unfortunately, this is one of those moments that we wish weren't real. This past Tuesday, in the early hours of the morning, a longtime member of the fans Wrestling organization passed away. Ed Wilson, handler of "The Lone Gunmen" characters (Machina, Rudo, Sid Snow, Total Eclipse, Billy James, Penny Pureheart, and others) passed away in Michigan. The circumstances of his death are still sketchy, but at this time it is known that poor lighting conditions were behind a car striking into Ed as he crossed a street while leaving the hospital where he worked at. As a result, Ed unfortunately suffered many injuries, and although he was rushed back into the hospital and attempt was made at saving his life, he passed away shortly thereafter. At the time of his passing, Ed owned and operated the Champions Wrestling League, another e-wrestling promotion. Although it only opened this past January 1st, the CWL had quite a large following and had many fans. Beyond that, it had a talented group of e-wrestling handlers and people behind the CWL that made the CWL what it was. Ed was the glue that held the CWL together. The fWo & the CWL were always on good terms and fortunately the fWo had the opportunity to work with the CWL on some occasions, as some fWo wrestlers appeared on the CWL' show "Thursday Night Titans", and likewise, CWL'ers appeared on the fWo show Sunday Night Slaughter. Ed was always behind the idea that people would get along better by working together, and I'm pleased to at least say that the fWo was able to work with the CWL before Ed's untimely passing. Tonight, instead of bringing you Slaughter, we will be bringing you some words from some of his friends here in the fWo, as well as some matches in tribute to what Ed brought to the fWo, CWL & e-wrestling itself. Please bear with us as this may come across as an awkward "show", but due to the circumstances, awkwardness should be expected. We will begin with a virtual ten second ringing of the bell, and then we ask that you give us ten seconds of silence. Please pay these respects by bowing your head during the entire duration. Ding.
