fWo — Card

Wednesday Warfare, 22 March 2000

22 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction BodyCount. The very first presentation of this epic event was mere hours away. With thirty fWo superstars heading to the ring, one will walk away with the greatest prize in the wrestling industry. The fWo World Heavyweight Championship. Presented live on Pay Per View, the fWo promises that by the time the night is over... nothing will be the same in the fWo ever again. Dead vs. Rudo The sounds of "Dizz-Knee-Land" by Dada fill the arena and the crowd cheers wildly for Rudo. The Lone Gunmen member makes his way down out into the arena, dressed to wrestle. He runs down to the ring, flailing his arms about and laughing the whole way. He slides under the bottom rope into the ring,and climbs onto each turnbuckle, clapping and cheering to entertain the crowd. Rudo's music dies down, and the loud sounds of Marilyn Manson's"Beautiful People" burst throughout the arena, getting a mixed reaction of almost all boos from the fans. Dead walks out wearing a black trenchcoat, with a cold stare in his eyes. He points to Rudo with one hand, and then makes his way down to the ring, unphased by the crowds negative reaction. He keeps his eyes fixed on Rudo, who stares back at him with a blank look on his face. In an instant, Dead drops his trenchcoat, slides into the ring, and goes right after Rudo. Rudo swings at Dead, but he just moves out of the way, and locks on an armbar. Dead starts to hit the twisted arm of Rudo, who screams in agony. Dead lets go, and Rudo drops to the ground, grabbing his elbow. Dead walks over, grabs Rudo by the hair, and pulls him up slowly. He drops an elbow right on the middle of Rudo's back, and Rudo stumbles towards the ropes. Dead whips him off the ropes, and nails a spinebuster. He follows up with a cover, but Rudo manages to kick out after the count of two. Dead pulls Rudo up, and whips him into the corner. He bounces off and stumbles into the middle of the ring where he is met by a DDT from Dead. Dead covers, but Rudo once again kicks out at two. Once again, Dead pulls Rudo up by the hair, and this time he nails a snap suplex, followed by a quick cover, but Rudo kicks out at two again. Dead seems very frustrated as he pulls Rudo up again. This time, he knees the Lone Gunman in the ribs, causing him to bend over, grabbing his ribs in pain. Dead picks Rudo up for a powerbomb, but Rudo slips behind him. He rolls him up, but Dead kicks out after one, and goes right back after Rudo. He punches at Rudo, but Rudo blocks it. He punches again, but this time Rudo can't block, and he falls to the ground like a pile of bricks. Dead starts to stomp on his ribs repeatedly, but pulls him up after a few seconds of stomping. Rudo swings at Dead's gut, but Dead holds him back. Dead nails Rudo with an uppercut to send him stumbling backwards, then levels him with a short-arm clothesline. Rudo slowly makes his way to the feet, and charges at the waiting Dead, but Dead scoops him up and gorilla presses Rudo high above his head. He walks around with Rudo hoisted high in the air, and laughs at the booing crowd. He then tosses Rudo right over the top rope down to the concrete floor. He goes out after Rudo, but is stopped by the referee. The referee checks on Rudo outside the ring, while Dead waits inside the ring, laughing. Suddenly, the crowds boos turn to cheers, as the Boston Strangler runs down through the crowd. He jumps over the guard-rail, and slides into the ring. Dead realizes that the crowd's reaction has changed, but before he can turn around, it is too late. The Strangler grabs Dead from behind and locks on a cobra clutch. Dead flails his arms around trying to escape the hold as the Strangler just laughs. Outside the ring, a recovering Rudo sees the Strangler, and starts to return to the ring, followed by the referee. The Strangler slips back out of the ring, unseen by the referee, as Rudo climbs the turnbuckles and nails a frog splash while the Strangler walks to the back. He covers Dead, scoring the three-count, and the victory. Rudo celebrates as an angered Dead storms to his feet, and walks to the backstage area methodically in search of the Boston Strangler. Result: Rudo by Pinfall One Ric Chronos and "Fast" Eddy Fate, dressed in street gear, are standing next to Fallen, decked out in his normal ring attire, with the addition of a small pair of sunglasses, in what appears to be a sizable parking lot. "Are you sure he's up to this?" Eddy whispers over to Ric. "I know you want him to relax before Bodycount and all, but he's in his ring gear, and if someone recognizes him, he could get mobbed by fans." "Hey, they have people here in costume all the time. They'll think he's one of the employees. Besides, he's about as ready as he's going to be," replies Chronos. "Come on, big guy, we've got a lot to do. This is my home town, and I just KNOW you'll enjoy what I've got planned. Take a look behind you," Fallen turns, and the camera now also picks up a large amusement park, coasters and rides ablaze. "This is Paramount's King's Island. It's one of the best parks in the world." Fallen regards Ric and Eddy with a skeptical look, as a Klingon walks past. "I'm not...sure.." mutters Fallen, but Ric grabs his friend's shoulder, and pushes him into the gate. "Three, please!" Ric passes money to an attendant, positively beaming with excitement. The trio emerge inside the cornucopia of vendors, games, and attractions. Ric steers the three of them towards a sign that says "The Beast". "I know some people who work here," Ric remarks, "and they're able to let us cut up in the line. You have GOT to ride this thing, it's the largest, fastest, and oldest wooden roller coaster in the United States. Trust me, it's a blast." Both Fallen and Eddy look skeptically at one another, as Ric talks to someone near the start of the line, and suddenly he turns back, waving his friends over. As they reach the start of the line, an attendant lets them in past the other customers, and the coaster slides in. After a few people disembark, the trio is let on to the coaster, and starts up with only the three inside. Ric pumps his hands in the air, a scream of excitement emanating from his throat, as the coaster leaves view. Mike Bear vs. Robert Jensen The crowd popped as the theme music of the Underground played, and out stepped former multi time tag team champion, Mike Bear. Bear played to the crowd a few moments before making his way into the ring. As he continued to get the crowd warmed up inside the ring, Robert Jensen made his entrance to a heavily mixed reaction. Jensen slid under the bottom rope and immediately rushed Bear, backing him up against the ropes with rights and lefts. Jensen then sent Bear to the ropes, and sent him back down to the canvas with a quick elbow to the face. As Bear got to his feet Jensen rebounded off the opposite ropes and tore into Mike with a clothesline that took both men over the top rope and to the outside floor. The two were on their feet soon, though, and traded lefts and rights until Jensen snuck in a rake to the eyes. After a few knocks into the guardrail and ring apron, Jensen delivered a vertical suplex to Mike Bear on the fWo entrance ramp, resonating throughout with a loud clang. Jensen took a moment to pause and play to the crowd, who let him know their dismay at seeing him dominating Mike Bear thus far, and grabbed a chair from the timekeppers table. Jensen laid into Bear with a crack just as he got to his feet. Mike refused to go down, though, and instead stumbled up the rampway. Jensen, enraged, charged Mike with the chair and swung, but before he could connect Bear had ducked out of the way. The chair thundered against the rampway, and as Jensen turned around he found himself introduced to one of Bear's boots. The Undergrounder pulled Jensen into a pair of legscissors and raised his arms to the crowd, but before he could execute his piledriver Jensen backdropped him onto the steel ramp. Jensen stompbed away at Bear some more as Mike scrambled up the ramp. When he found himself at the top of the entrance ramp and Jensen approached him, Bear grabbed his opponent by the front of his tights and sent him head first into the metal entrance way. Jensen stumbled backwards, and to the crowds delight Bear received a second wind and began to go wild on the former Bookerman. Unleashing lefts and rights, Bear whipped Jensen back behind the stage and into the backstage area of the arena. Bear introduced Jensen to a stack of wooden crates, a lighting fixture, and a big ol' concrete wall, among other debris. With Jensen stumbling about, Bear slammed im into yet another wall, and signalled for the Whiplash. Before he could apply the hold, though, Jensen snuck in a low blow to regain the advantage. Following a momentary breather, Jensen lifted up Bear and slammed him to the concrete floor, then went for the pin. Surprisingly, Bear kicked out at the count of two. Jensen pulled his foe back to his feet and, while Bear was regaining his senses, selected a chair from the various materials scattered around in this area of the arena. With wrestlers watching, Jensen swung and struck Bear across the face... or so that's how he would have wanted it to work out. In fact, Bear dodged the shot, kicked Jensen in the gut, and DDTed him onto the chair. Both men staggered to their feet, but it was Bear who took control and quickly executed the Whiplash. With Jensen incapacitated, Bear went for the pinfall... unfortunately, the Flying Frenchie hit the scene just then and assaulted Mike Bear with a steel chair across the back of the skull. Bear rolled off of Jensen and while holding his head got to his feet, but before he knew what hit him... again... another crack echoed, and Bear was laid out completely. While the Frenchie laughed, Robert Jensen rolled on top of Bear and made the cover. One, two, three. Result: Robert Jensen by Pinfall Machina vs. Mad Trucker Cameras returned to the arena where off to the site of the main ring setting a large pool had been placed. Within it were hundreds if not thousands of sausage links and patties. As the fans got ready for the "sausage pool" match workers using giant tongs spread around the meaty products. The rules were announced that whomever could score a pinfall within the meat first would be declared the winner. Machina came out onto the entrance ramp first and extended both arms in determination to win the latest battle between himself and the Trucker. With a loud TOOOOOOOOOOOOOT the Mad Trucker walked to the ring, licking his lips at the meaty sausage patties within the pool. Both men stepped gingerly into the meat, looking at the links and patties with some concern. Footing was hard inside the pool, and the two men walked toward each other slowly and carefully. Machina slipped first, coming down hard on the meat and gasping. The Trucker moved in, picking up big handfulls of meat and tossing them at the masked leader of the Lone Gunmen. Machina batted the meat aside and picked up a sasuage link and brandished it at the Trucker. Gasping, the Trucker took a step back and picked up one of the sausage patties. Machina thrust forward with the meat but was deflected by the Trucker's patty and then a hard punch to the face of the big man. The Trucker moved in for another attack, but suddenly Machina put a hand up and motioned for the Trucker to halt. Machina asked for a microphone and pointed to the Trucker. "Wait! Wait! Look at us! Look at what we've been reduced to... wrestling in meat! What are we... mindless male studs that are simply put on display for all these people around us? Well... come on! There has to be a better way to settle this than in meat..." The two stared at each other for a few moments, and then the Trucker slowly nodded. He raised his arms in the air and turned to the cheers of the crowd. Behind him Machina smiled an evil smile and clocked the Trucker from behind with the microphone. The Trucker fell face-first into the meat with a plop, and Machina quickly made the cover. The three count was made and Machina raised his arms in victory, while the cameras went to the back... Result: Machina by Pinfall Two "Fast" Eddy Fate sat across from Fallen and Ric Chronos in the restaurant, his ever-present leather jacket sitting in the seat next to him as he shook his head, covered in long wavy red hair. "You mean John's NEVER eaten Cincinnati chili?" Fallen shook his head. "Never." Eddy laughed and called over a waitress. "We'll need three large five ways over here, and a lot of Tabasco." Fallen look at Ric, who cringed at the sound of the order. "What's a 'five way'?" Ric smiled. "A horribly unhealthy mess of starch and carbohydrates." Eddy laughed and leaned closer to Fallen. "Don't listen to him, mate. In Cincinnati, chili is done very differently form normal chili. It doesn't have red beans, unless you ask for them, and they serve it over spaghetti." Fallen looked confused. "Spaghetti?" "Yeah! Chili, spaghetti and shredded cheese is the first part of it. That's a three way. Onions and red beans are extra, and if you get both, it's a five way." "So... a four way is a three way with onions or beans?" Eddy laughed. "Hey, you're catching on quick!" He munched on some oyster crackers as Fallen looked back at Ric. "Okay, so what's a 'coney'?" Ric turned in his seat slightly to see Fallen more clearly. "A coney is a hot dog with Cincinnati chili and cheese on it, with mustard and onions usually." Fallen nodded as the waitress brought back the orders. The oval plates were almost overflowing with noodles, dark chili and mounds of shredded cheese. She set down a red bottle of tobacco in front of Eddy and looked over at the group. "Is there anything else I can do for you?" "A small glass of water," Fallen muttered softly. The waitress leaned in a bit. "Please?" Fallen looked confused. "Thank you?" "What?" Eddy jumped in. "No no, John. Here, when someone asks 'Please?', it's like 'Huh?'." Fallen looked at him. "Huh?" The waitress looked at them. "What?" Ric reached over and grabbed her attention. "Glass of water, please." She walked away as Fallen shook his head. "It's like another world." Eddy shrugged his shoulders and started eating as Ric looked on and laughed. "Maybe it is, but it's home." Fallen looked out the window at the street, his eyes shining. "Home..." he whispered softly to himself. Courage vs. Hiroshima Warfare returned from commercial break to find Hiroshima already in the ring. Over the PA system came the music of Korn, and Courage makes his way to ringside, with the International Championship belt around his waste. He hits the apron and grabs a microphone. "It appears that I can't face anyone but idiots anymore. First that moron Douglas, and now this goon! I guess this is what happens when you have to save a belt that that jackass Eron made such a horrible disgrace! Now HeroSuba, I don't really know how you got a match for this belt, but I guess that since Douglas had it, anyone could get a shot! I guess I've got to show everyone why this belt is back on the waist of a certified SUPERSTAR, baby!" With that, he dropped the mic and the belt, and took after Hiroshima. Hiroshima locks up with Courage, and tosses him to the ground with ease. Courage takes a look around, and again grapples with Hiroshima. Again he meets with the mat, after Hiroshima tosses him to the ground. Courage decides to hit the outside for a second, to rethink his strategy. He climbes back onto the apron and climbs the top rope. Hiroshima bites on the draw-in by Courage, and is met with a missle dropkick from the champ. Courage wastes no time in pulling Hiroshima back up, and tosses him into the ropes. He backs into the opposite ropes and hits Hiroshima with a flying lariat. Courage picks up Hiroshima once again and throws him to a turnbuckle. Courage gets up some speed and lands a drop kick in the corner. Hiroshima stays up, so Courage sets him up on the second rope, and climbs to the top. He locks Hiroshima's head in a leg scissors, and plants him with a hurricanrana. Courage goes for a pin, but can only get a two-count. He picks Hiroshima up and hits him with a blow to the head. Hiroshima returns the blow, however, surprising Courage. Courage hits him again, and once again Hiroshima hits back. Courage slaps him in the chest, and receives a more devastating one in return. Courage, having enough of this, kicks Hiroshima in the stomach and plants him with a DDT. He picks Hiroshima up and suplexes him to the mat. He keeps the hold and repeats the process twice more. After the triple suplex, Courage senses that the match is at an end, and ascends to the top rope. He flies off the top and nails a 450 splash. He makes the cover on Hiroshima, but pulls him up at two. He wants the Courage Required. He runs toward the ropes, springboards off, and nails it. The one, two, and three is counted, and Courage retains his title. As he is celebrating his victory, however, he does not realize the bald-headed bastard Dead coming to the ring, chair in hand. Dead waits for Courage to turn around. He does, and Dead nails him with a chair. Courage stays up, but lands in Dead's hands, where Dead hits the Crucifixion. The two FTfWo members pound away on Courage, landing kicks and chair shots on the champion. Primetime and The Flying Frenchie run down to ringside and clear the ring. Hiroshima and Dead laugh at the French Foundation as they make their way up the ramp, taking in the flood of boos coming their way. Result: Courage by Pinfall Blindsided He carried two bags as he approached the yellow vehicle. The bags obviously contained luggage, as the madman from Boston was finished with his duties for Warfare tonight... and would be moving onto another venue for Body Count; where he had hoped to finally win the fWo World Title that he had so long desired. As he approached the cab, two figures emerged from the shadows, slamming him into the large masse of steel. Cold rain trickled from the night sky, and some light from the arena shone on the two men. Dead & Hiroshima continued to pummel The Strangler, before Dead pulled out a needle. Hiroshima grabbed Strangler by one arm and held it, before Dead quickly injected something into him. The Strangler tried to fight at first, but quickly he was subdued. Deprived of all his thoughts, he passed out in front of two-fourths of the FtfWo. Dead and Hiroshima quickly through the Strangler into the back of a black Ford Ranger nearby; before Dead occupied the drivers seat and Hiroshima occupied the passengers seat and they quickly sped away, with the driver of the yellow cab finally getting out of the cab to yell at the suspects... it was all for naught however, by the time the individual did it was too late... time was running out for the Strangler. Black Panther vs. Matt Douglas As Warfare comes back from a commercial promoting fWo Action Figures (Guaranteed to keep you coming back for more (because they break each other into little *$^# pieces), "Higher" by Creed begins to play, bringing the former International champion Matt Douglas down the ramp. This match is interesting because it pits two of the men who comprised the Millenium men against each other. As that point is being made by Chris Finn and James Armstrong (Armstrong's point actually was that the money he "found" the other day didn't come from Finn's wallet, and it definitely wasn't spent on strippers), "Killed the Cat" hits the PA system and the Black Panther makes his way to ringside, amid a chorus of boos for the former world champ. The Panther blows them off and hits the apron. He grabs a microphone. "If it weren't for me, you wouldn't BE here!," he yells at the crowd. "If it weren't for ME, you wouldn't have anyone to cheer for! If it weren't fo..." his tirade is interrupted by Douglas dropkicking him through the ropes. Panther starts to get up and recompose himself. However, he doesn't get much time to do so, as Douglas had him lined up for a missle dropkick off the top rope, which he nails. Douglas pulls the Panther back into the ring. As Douglas makes his way onto the apron, the Panther gets to his feet and grabs the head of Douglas and giving him a neckbreaker off the rope. Douglas falls back off the apron and to the concrete below. Panther goes to the outside and grabs Douglas, pulling him back inside. Once inside, the Panther begins to wear down the smaller Douglas with chops to the back and head. Panther throws Douglas into the ropes, and nails a clothesline. Panther scoops up Douglas and promptly hits a powerslam. The Black Panther goes for a pin, but cannot get the three count. Douglas kicks out at two, giving the Panther more reason to pick him up again and hit a swinging neckbreaker on him. Another pin, another two count. Panther scoops up Douglas once more and calls for The Pounce, a superplex. He locks Douglas in, and goes for it. However, Douglas blocks it. Panther goes for it again, and Douglas blocks it again. Instead, Douglas hits a suplex of his own, sending Panther to the mat. He picks up Panther and takes him to a turnbuckle. A couple of chops later, Douglas grabs Panther and starts to give him an Irish Whip. The Panther reverses it and tosses Douglas into the turnbuckle. However, the referee is in the way, and Douglas floors him in the corner. Douglas comes back to the middle of the ring, where Panther hooks him and hits the Pounce. He goes to make the count, but does not realize that the ref is out. He gets up and starts to taunt the crowd a little bit. As he is doing this, Havok runs down to the ring to take out the Panther. The Panther sees Havok running down, and gets ready to brawl. However, what both wrestlers do not see is Douglas getting up. Havok hops up on the apron, with a chair ready to swing. He gets in and starts to back the Panther up. He lines up the chair with Panther's head. As he starts his swing, Panther ducks. Behind him is Douglas. Havok, realizing this just a second too late, nails him with the chair. Havok is upset with the recent developments, but doesn't have a lot of time to reflect, as Panther tosses him out of the ring as the ref is getting up. Panther looks the leg, and the ref counts the three, declaring the Panther the winner. As Panther is celebrating, Havok climbs back into the ring and starts to brawl with him. Douglas struggles to his feet, head ringing from the chair shot, and starts to join the brawl. All three men continue to brawl as Warfare heads to a break. Result: Black Panther by Pinfall Burial He was gagged and bound in the back of the truck. Tape covered his mouth, while rope prevented him from struggling. Even if he tried, he couldn't. Whatever had been injected in him, had been taking over his body, and he was in a deep sleep. Dead & Hiroshima stopped the truck, and looking at the background all one could make out was a forest. Near the truck was a grave, that had obviously been dug earlier. A pile of dirt hovered over the empty space, as the two men exited the vehicle. Dead also removed a tripod with a camera from the truck, before setting it up and letting it capture the imagery that was to occur. Dead pushed the bed of the truck down, before he and Hiroshima pulled the Strangler from the truck. His body hit the ground with a thud. Slow rain trickled, but it wasn't too much of a nuisance. Even if it were, Dead & Hiroshima had a task at hand. They dragged him over to the grave, before dumping him in. Both men procured some nearby shovels, before they began placing all the wet soil, covering the Strangler' body. Little by little, they began to cover him. More and more, as time passed, the soil began to reach a point where it would be impossible for anyone to try and remove themselves from. He was buried, and there was nothing nobody could do to save him. Dead and Hiroshima stood over his mock grave, before Hiroshima walked over and removed the camera from it's secure position, and focused it on Dead, who stood with his back to the camera. Dead began shouting things that weren't quite audible, and to an extent it appeared as if he were urinating on the man's grave. It wasn't completely visable, and as Dead turned around he gave a cold stare to the camera, before speaking; "Tonight you have all witness the death of your savior, and with him out of the picture, Body Count will be easier than we could have ever hoped. And after one of us secures the fWo World Title in our grasp, we will move onto CyberSlam... and run our own damn pay-per-view... and ruin the celebration of the beginning of the third year of the fWo. Because if we have any choice, there won't be a third year, and on the night of April 29th, the fWo will come to an end. Trust us." The camera dropped, and a scene of static was all that remained. Scott Collins vs. Primetime "Burning" blares over the Cincinatti Gardens PA system, bringing out the leader of the Dark Riders, Scott Collins, the man who will be facing his very own tag team partner at Body Count, Primetime. Collins walks to the ring with a determined look on his face. The fans are on their feet, anticipating the arrival of Primetime, when "Blind" hits, bringing out the French Foundation member himself. Primetime sips from a bottle of Evian water as he stands on the entrance ramp beneath the SlaughterScreen. Reveling in the mixed reaction from the crowd, PT rips his French Foundation shirt off, and strides to the ring. He steps in and meets Collins face to face in the ring, locking in a staredown. Words are exchanged, and Collins pushes the man who he will team up with to face Perfect and Mitch Wilson in a mere four days at Body Count. Primetime turns away with a smirk on his face, and responds with a slap to the face. Collins now turns away, and he responds with a punch that sends Primetime back. Collins continues the assault with a barrage of punches, before whipping PT into the ropes. Primetime ducks a clothesline, and bounces back with a flying shoulder tackle. PT bounces up quickly, and hits Collins with a dropkick. Collins bounces up, and walks into a Japanese Armdrag from Primetime. Primetime locks in an armbar with Collins on the mat, but Collins quickly reaches a vertical base, and reverses it into an armbar of his own. Collins releases the armbar, but before PT can get away, he clenches on a waistlock. PT reverses the waistlock into one of his own, before taking Collins up and over with a release German suplex. Primetime jumps on Collins with a cover, but Collins kicks out at two. Primetime picks Collins up by the hair, and throws him into the corner. A few backhand chops follow, and Primetime clearly has the advantage in the early stages of the matchup. He goes for an irish whip to the opposite corner, but Collins reverses it and sends Primetime into the corner, hard. PT hits the turnbuckle corner, and immediately drops to the mat, holding his back in pain. Collins takes advantage of the sudden back injury to Primetime, by sending him into the ropes and catching him in a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. A cover by Collins is not enough to end the match, as PT kicks out at two. Collins stomps on the back of Primetime, before picking him up by the hair. He lifts him up, and connects with a big vertical suplex. Another cover by Collins, and another kickout by Primetime. This time, thinking quickly, Collins places PT on the top turnbuckle. Collins himself climbs to the second, and hooks PT for a superplex. He tries once to get him over, but can't. A second try is blocked with a punch to the gut by Primetime. PT then pushes Collins off of the turnbuckle, and stands up on the turnbuckle. PT comes off with a flying elbowdrop, and connects. PT covers, and Collins this time kicks out at two. Primetime thinks that the elbowdrop softened Collins enough for the kill, so he sets him up for the Primetime Bomb. However, PT's back is still too weak to lift Collins, and he can't get him up. PT tries again, but once again, he can't get Collins up. Collins then knocks PT down, and grabs a hold of his legs. He slingshots PT into the ropes, and Primetime flies over the ropes, and lands on the cold, hard floor. Collins steps over the ropes, and to the outside where he grabs a hold of PT's hair. He looks to whip PT into the guardrail, but PT reverses, and sends Collins into the rail. He rolls Collins back into the ring, and grabs a nearby chair. PT slides into the ring with chair in hand, waiting for Collins to get up. Collins reaches his feet, and PT swings the chair at him. Collins ducks, and turns around, dropkicking the chair which was still in PT's hands, into his face and out of the ring. Collins covers, but PT kicks out at two. Collins sends PT into the ropes, and looks for a clothesline, PT ducks, and nails a boot to the gut, setting Collins up for the Primetime Bomb. Collins though, backbody drops PT, and looks for a shortarm clothesline. PT ducks it, and nails another boot to the gut. He sets him up again, but Perfect and Mitch Wilson come running out! Perfect grabs the chair that was at ringside, and nails PT in the back with it. PT falls to the mat, and Wilson runs behind Collins, and plants him with a bulldog. The two FTfWo members continue their attack on their Body Count opponents as Wednesday Warfare goes off of the air... Result: No Contest
