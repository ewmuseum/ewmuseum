Introduction Previously... Karma RUAHH~! headed to the ring, as "The Hate In Me" blasted throughout the Sports Complex. Black Quicksilver, Aimz, and Halo appeared at the top of the ramp, heading down to the ring. Most were still in shock over the events that occured just three days ago at Unleashed: Highway. Why did Aimz help BQ? Perhaps they'd soon find out, as the trio was now in the squared circle, and BQ had a microphone. "Well...we're back in Florida." Not surprisingly, this got a huge reaction. "Why?" Also not surprisingly, this got a huge NEGATIVE reaction. But BQ knew what he was doing, while the smirk came across his face. "You see, that's the problem with you people. Some of you don't even know who I am, yet you'll cheer me when I mention your hometown. Just like you'll cheer her...", he said, pointing to Aimz, "...when she's feuding with valets who don't know how to wrestle, but when she's facing guys, you scream at the top of your lungs that she doesn't even belong in this business. And that brings me to what happened on Friday.", BQ said, before pausing for a little while. "Well, actually, it goes back further than this past Friday. It was about a month after the fWo resumed operations. I wasn't even on camera yet, but I showed up to each and every show to help out some of the new people. There was one of them in particular that I bonded with. And it was this woman right here.", he said, looking at Aimz. Naturally, there were a few catcalls from the crowd, apparently jumping to conclusions on this relationship. "But the fact that we were friends...this didn't sit too well with the people in charge. On one hand, you had Aimz and a group of her friends known as the Rat Pack. All the guys in this group had ripped bodies...nice hair...and were treated like superstars. According to what managment wanted YOU people to know, THOSE were the type of guys that Aimz hung out with. But on the other hand...you had me. A guy who wears a mask and is notorious for his sense of humor. So in other words, they figured the public viewed me as a loser, and didn't want them to know that the most popular female in the fWo was associated with a loser." It was all coming together now. "But like I've said in the past few months...I'm not a loser anymore. So, since Disney had a little trouble trying to screw ME over, they decided to have some..."fun"...with MY friend. They made her stop doing what she loved to do. They made her stop wrestling, and they threw her into a feud with a valet, hoping that ratings would increase and more people would buy tickets just to see them fight. And it was just one week ago, after Aimz had gotten her shirt torn off in the middle of this ring, she came to me. She came to me because she was sick and tired of being toyed with, much like I was four months ago." BQ paused and lowered the mic, looking around at the crowd. "The three of us ARE the fWo's Karma, and the people who have wronged us...sometimes they get what they want...sometimes they get what they need...but we've dealt with them for far too long, and now...they're going to get what they deserve." BQ dropped the mic, and the group of three disgruntled employees left the ring. No music. Just looks of confusion and worry from the crowd. They headed to the back, as RUAHH~! went elsewhere... Kodiak Vic Creed lowered himself into the chair behind him. His body ached in places he didn't even know existed. The past few weeks had been more physically demanding than he would have expected, but it wasn't altogether surprising. Nick Brandish had done everything in his power to destroy the Main Man. He'd come closer than anyone had suspected. After sitting for a few seconds, Vic shifted uncomfortably. He'd kept the seriousness of his neck injury on the kayfabe so he could return to the ring in weeks, days, rather than months or never at all. Vertebrae had been damaged, and the spinal cord was bruised. The burning sensation in his limbs came and went. Presently, the burning ate at his arms and legs. Being driven through a table, battered with a steel chain by a four hundred pound psychopath, and shot in the chest with a taser at close range had all further aggravated his injury. Mark Lively squared his shoulders as he sat behind his desk, misreading Creed's fidgeting for nervousness. "Vic, this is Simon Cowl. He's the attorney for Disney." Creed didn't think the lawyer was much to look at, but there was something in his eye that told the Main Man to stay on his toes with this guy. David Mori cleared his throat. "Of course you know David," Mark said offhandedly. Creed looked at the wrestling "expert" and wanted to tear out his spleen. Vic leaned back in his seat. "So? What's going on?"
