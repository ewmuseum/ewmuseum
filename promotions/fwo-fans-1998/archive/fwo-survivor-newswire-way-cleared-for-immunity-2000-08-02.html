Way Cleared for Immunity

2 August 2000

fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE WAY CLEARED FOR IMMUNITY August 2, 2000 With Round Six of the fWo Survivor contest now a memory, one thing is certain... the dynamic of the contest has changed. With the ripples of the alliance voting during round five still echoing in the minds of the wrestlers, tensions were high during a seven man gauntlet style match to determine immunity. Interference was non-existent for a change, but the past clearly impacted the present as Kodiak Vic Creed survived three wrestlers to win the immunity challenge. Many speculated that his rivalry with the now departed Red Dragon prevented Creed from winning earlier immunity challenges, and this seemed to be confirmed with today's event. Fans of Creed now speculate that the road is clear for future victories, but fWo Survivor coordinator Stevie Roberts was quick to point out that it might not be the case. "The problem is that the early rounds favored someone of Creed's size more. Every other event will be based on things like teamwork, which Creed has shown he has very little of, and a quick offense. Kodiak Vic Creed's skills lie in stamina and power, and while they will help him, he's going to find the road ahead very difficult to travel. Later events will favor people like Suicide or Arch Angel." Still, all bets are off when it comes to Survivor. Many speculated that the winners of the contest would be Mike Extreme, Juno Law, or the Red Dragon... all of whom are gone now. Vegas odds makers are rapidly shifting the standings, and currently have wrestlers Nick Allstar and Arch Angel as the odds-on favorites to win the event. The end of round six featured a brawl between all of the remaining fWo Survivor wrestlers, many of whom were venting frustrations after several days of tense events and voting periods. Stevie Roberts declared that such actions will not happen again, and work will be done to prevent outbursts like the one seen today. More news as it comes available.
