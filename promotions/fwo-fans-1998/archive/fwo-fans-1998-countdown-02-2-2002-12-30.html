Ross stared blankly at the scene, eyes narrow, slowly raising his hands in the air as the crowd booed him as loud as they could. The lights went out. Screams. In the distance, the bell rang. Obviously given Flair's condition, the lights needed to be on for the EMT's to do their work. The bell continued ringing for 30 seconds. When suddenly a familiar guitar riff started playing alongside it. This wasn't the work of the timekeeper--this was AC/DC. Ruben Ross recognized the theme standing on the ramp because he'd been in the Qualiseum all the time when Hoffman came on. Was this Ice? The lights came on. The fans erupted. Ross swirled around, waiting for the Big Blonde Machine. *SMACK* *RUAHH* He hadn't even been close. Sure, it'd been blonde, and with machine-like efficency had put him down with one giant shot to the head. But it'd come from her crutch. Welcome back, Ivy. Result: Erik Kelly, via pinfall, to retain the fWo World Title
