fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE THREE REMAIN August 9, 2000 Battered down to three men, the fWo Survivor contest now enters the final weekend of the contest with an extended break for the wrestlers. It is a time for reflection, for introspection, and for the three remaining men to contemplate their next move in the fWo Survivor contest. They survived nine brutal rounds... the next steps will be the most intense yet. fWo wrestler Suicide became the ninth man to be voted out of the contest. In a rare display of honor, the wrestlers who voted for Suicide claimed to regret doing it stating, "I like Suicide... I like everyone here. We are all friends here. Whatever happens next we all will be friends forever. Or at least... that's what I hope. God Suicide, I'm sorry." Suicide was well liked by the fans of the fWo Survivor contest, many of which enjoyed his antics and fresh interview style. Suicide was a favorite of fWo Survivor coordinator Stevie Roberts who told reporters, "I hope to see Suicide in the fWo someday... he's got a ton of talent, has a good personality, and deserves to get to the top... in whatever promotion that might be." Suicide performed well in the fWo Survivor contest, winning one of the immunity challenges and being nearly vote free during the majority of the contest. Now the waiting game begins. Ruben Ross, Kodiak Vic Creed, and Reno Davis will have a five day break from the contest, time they will no doubt spend polishing off their interview skills so they can go into the final leg of the contest with as much momentum as possible. On Monday one final three way match will determine the final immunity contest. Then the final voting phase will begin which will narrow the field down to two. From that point the ten wrestlers voted out of the Survivor contest will return, if only for one vote, to determine who the ultimate winner will be. Voting results were as follows: Suicide: 2 votes Ruben Ross: 1 vote Kodiak Vic Creed: 1 vote More news as it develops.
