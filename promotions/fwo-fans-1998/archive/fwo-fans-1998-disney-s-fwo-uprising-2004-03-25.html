Faithful Resolution Uprising cameras returned to the locker room of the fWo's oddest, yet most popular tag team Lost & Found. Chris Shepherd and Deacon sat silently in meditation like they normally did when they first arrived at the Milkhouse Arena. The past weeks certainly weighed down on Deacon and his manager as they struggled to keep the Internet Title afloat against Disney's wishes that the title be destroyed. Moreover, superstars in the federation such as Eli Flair, Donaven Winters, Codemaster and Fatal Attraction targeted Deacon for his beliefs, values, judgments and the gold title he strapped around his waist since the beginning of 2004. Yet, in the back of both men's minds laid a far more troubling issue, one that kept them awake at night in fearful confusion... ...and which just happened to walk into the locker room smoking a cigarette. As 'Cocky' Craig Miles walked into the room with his duffel bag, both Deacon and Shepherd stood up nervously. For the past few weeks, it seemed that Craig had been avoiding their presence...and out of the blue, he was now confronting them without trepidation. Miles let out a crooked smile before saying, "Hey guys..." "Hey Craig." Shepherd responded, while Deacon gazed at Miles with a compassionate look. Miles put out his cigarette immediately, he remembered the 'house' rules and turned around talking before Deacon or Chris Shepherd could barrage him with the hundreds of questions that had built up inside themselves the last two or three weeks. "Alright guys, I know you're not happy with me. I know why...I only hope you understand that what I'm doing is out of respect...out of protection..." "I don't think we need protection, Craig..." Shepherd responded quickly before Deacon could respond himself. Miles looked to Deacon and nodded his head disapprovingly. "You don't? Let me ask you something, Deac...let's say Eli Flair decides to take Shepherd on his offer let's say from out of nowhere, he blindsides you." Miles began to pace back and forth, looking up at the ceiling while talking, "Now, while you're trying to deal with that situation that freak Winters decides that with your back turned, the time is perfect to carve you up with a knife...you don't think you need protection? I admire that, Elijah. But...excuse my French, let's cut the sh*t already. Your values, your morals...they're a target for everyone that has something against life itself...and then that title is around your waist for freaks like Savage and Speck to try and use your morals and values as a stepping stone in their careers..." "We've told you from the start, Craig. We know the mountains we'll have to climb, but we won't take any shortcuts," Shepherd responded quickly once again. "Shortcuts? Is Winters bashing Deacon's head in with a chair a good example of this? How about Savage painting Amen on Deacon's forehead? Listen, Chris...I value your judgment. I admire what you and Deacon try to do night in and night out. But let's face it, these attacks will never stop because you're not teaching anyone the consequences for their actions. Even when Jacobs made your lives a living hell, it took me to not only figure out where the hell Faith came from, but how to get things back on even ground." "By trying to break his ribs?" Shepherd's question had a slightly snide overtone, which made Miles stop and look at him straight in the eye with no expression... "You think someone like Flair or Winters will stop where Jacobs did? I'm just trying to watch Deacon's back. I know you can't do what I do, but I'll be damned if these punks don't think twice about attacking him..." "Please, don't use t'at word. Damned is not what you are..." Miles inhaled deeply and exhaled overdramatically...Deacon had finally spoken, but he could say no more. "I've signed us up for a match, tonight." Deacon and Shepherd looked at each other in confusion as Miles eyed them both seriously. "Its against Fatal Attraction, if you want to prove to me the way you're climbing this mountain is the right path...then tonight's your shot. I'll play it cool, but I'll be dam I've decided NOT to kill you. Uprising continued on as "Not Gonna Get Us" brought out Joey Melton. Many fans were still perplexed as to why a 40 year-old man was making his entrance to a song performed by a pair of alleged 18 year-old lesbians, but Melton was all business as he entered the ring, even though he didn't know who his opponent was. After several seconds of standing in the ring, Melton, along with many others, looked up at the huge screen over the entranceway, as it came alive with the image of, what some would call, a god among men. SPHERE. "Hello, Joseph. Wait...that is your name, right? Joseph? I have no idea anymore. I mean, we just got guys coming and going around here these days...some of them are gone before I can even beat their ass! Just...dammit, someone give me a roster sheet." Joey, say hello to your opponent for tonight...Dallas Jones!" The Press Conference (Or: How Xias Spent His Spring Break) The fWoTron came to life in the fWo Arena with an image of a small, gray room packed with people sitting in chairs. Most of them had cameras and notepads and/or laptop computers, a sure sign that they were press. On the wooden stage above was a podium, and then behind that something covered up by a huge blue curtain. From the back stepped a young Japanese man in glasses, as the words "STAR Press Conference, Last Monday, Tokyo, Japan" appeared in the right-hand corner of the screen. The man cleared his throat before beginning to speak- a translator to the right of him quickly repeating whatever he said in English for the members of the non-Japanese media in attendance. "Welcome to the STAR press conference in the greatest city in the world, Tokyo, Japan! We are here today to announce the forming of a new force in professional wrestling- a group that will bring more pro-wrestling action to this nation than has ever been brought before. Pro Wrestling STAR is proud to announce that we will present our first-ever wrestling event, THE FIRST FIGHT 2004, right here in Tokyo at the historic Budokan Hall!" The crowd applauded this as the man nodded. "And I am also proud to give you the hottest gaijin in Japanese professional wrestling, the current CWA Tag Team Champion and superstar of the international entity that is the Fans Wrestling Organization.....Xias!" The crowd applauded again as Xias came out of a door to the right of the stage, waving to the crowd as he stepped onto it. He bowed to the crowd before moving over to the podium, his tag team belt over his shoulder. This time, a Japanese man to the left was providing translation of his English as he spoke. "Thank you!" Xias said. "I am proud to announce that I will be a part of Pro Wrestling STAR's first-ever tour, starting with THE FIRST FIGHT right here in Tokyo! It is with great gratitude that I thank the management of the best wrestling organization in the world, the fWo, for allowing me to work here in Japan once again, because this is without a doubt my favorite country in the entire world to wrestle in front of!" The crowd again applauded Xias for that, who nodded his head. "I love this place, and I'm really going to love wrestling in front of sixteen thousand of the best fans in the world at the Budokan Hall!" The man in the glasses stepped forward as Xias stepped aside to allow him to speak again. "Xias will not be the only superstar from the fWo on this great event- Pro Wrestling STAR is also very proud to welcome home Japanese native the Great Dragon!" "Great Dragon, a true legend of Japanese wrestling, will be teaming with one of Japanese wrestling's biggest rising stars, Ken'ichi Himura, to face two fellow legends in The Amazing Casoraki & Okiyo Supreme at FIRST BATTLE 2004! This is a true dream match for fans of professional wrestling around the world! But that's not the Main Event....in the Main Event, you will see what we are announcing as an 'American Tag Grudge Match'! Xias & Rave will take on the team of former IOW World Heavyweight Champion Kory Storm and his partner, who has been demanded by Storm to be kept a secret. "All I have to say is that no matter who Storm's partner is, with an American legend like Erik Rave as my tag team partner, they don't stand a chance in the Budokan! At THE FIRST FIGHT 2004, I plan on showing this new organization the fighting spirit that only an fWo superstar can provide! Thank you!" He turned and walked off the stage as the video faded from the fWoTron.....and was replaced by another one.
