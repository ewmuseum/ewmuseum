Divine Declaration The lightning streaked across the Fantron as this week's episode of Thursday night's fWo Uprising went live across the nation. The fans rose to their feet on cue and cheered loudly. The lightning effects on screen were soon replaced with the camera panning up the black boots, the black and yellow tights and on to a muscled chest as electricity arched to the entranceway and "Thor" by Therion blasted out of the house speakers. The audience's ovation grew louder as the sparks flew and the smoke billowed from under the grating. As the yellow lightning symbol scrolled by on the large screen, 'The Paragon' Brand Frontier appeared. Stretched across his massive chest was Vic Creed's own 'It's My World' t-shirt. A buzz shot through the crowd at Frontier's questionable attire, Brand strode to the ring, all but ignoring their quizzical look. They still cheered him however, whether it was the over active marketing department or the segment guy in editing, the man known as Brand Frontier was the hero of the Fan's Wrestling Organization, like it or not. Now in the ring and having retrieved a cordless microphone from the technician at ringside Brand waited, impatiently for the loud roar to subside. A full minute passed, the ovation continued. Frontier's nerves were on edge, more so than usual lately. Some even said he had a different edge to him then normal. None said it to his face. Finally, after pacing the ring, Brand brought the microphone to his lips. "We'll be here all night if you keep this up." The crowd grew louder. Frontier squinted. "I've got something to say... actually... I've got a lot to say so I'm going to say it, whether you stop or not. I appreciate this, I do, but I'm here for business tonight." Many in attendance raised an eyebrow over Frontier's blunt statement. Some wrote it off as the Paragon being obsessed with the feud with Vic Creed. Others thought back to Eron's statements on false heroes, if even briefly. As the noise level lowered Brand began, "First off I know everyone here is wondering why I'm sporting a rag such as this." Frontier motioned to the black and white shirt, plastered with Creed's 'catchphrase'. "You see, I was getting ready to come out here tonight and low and behold I passed one of those vendors out there. This shoddy piece of crap caught my eye. I stopped to look at it for a second, I wondered to myself why in the hell Vic Creed would get such fanfare when the guys in back hadn't issued the Hammer of God shirt yet." The fans cheered, for whatever reason. "Then I took a really good look at what the shirt said." Again Frontier pointed to the words plastered across the front. "It's My World." Pointing over his shoulder and turning so the stationary camera could see his massive back Frontier continued, "And You're Just Living In It." "See, I figure since the fWo hadn't gotten around to my shirt designs I'd go ahead and claim this one as my own... especially given the way I laid out the World Heavyweight Champion and former 'owner' of this shirt... with the Hammer of God." Expectedly and given the hatred earned by said World Champion the rabid fans cheered Frontier's actions. The camera pulled in close on Frontier's face and it showed on the Fantron, all the anger boiling under the surface very evident as he went on. "Creed thinks he's done with me. He thinks simply because the fWo needs to get as much out of him as they can, while they can, that I've been pushed aside. Well... maybe that's what he thought... until Monday night. Monday night I think I sent a good strong message to him..." Brand snarled, "He might wish he was done with me, but I'm not done with him. I want him in the ring, I want that strap around his waist and I want everything that makes Vic Creed who he is. Everything." Again the fans cheer. "Now, tonight I plan on taking a step back into 'real title contention'. I'm facing one of the few active Legends in the fWo right now in Jon Crisp." A round of cheers arise. "He's the next step in my march on the way to Wrestler of the Year. You see, I figure I'll comment on this because of that masked manipulator Spyder. He thinks because we don't all come bouncing out here spouting off about how we're going to be the next Wrestler of Year that we don't want it. That we can't taste the challenge, that our appetite is not whetted by the thought of claiming such a prestigious honor." "Spyder... you're wrong... esse... professionally I can't think of anything I'd rather be... esse, anything I'd rather have... esse, than the title of 2003 Wrestler of the Year. "Jon Crisp is a legend in his own right, always the bridesmaid so to speak, the main has gained that status without ever holding the World Heavyweight Championship, and to think I would look beyond him is a mistake. I'm thinking, always thinking, about my next opponent just as much as I'm thinking about one Vic Creed. Frontier almost snarls. The fan's cheer even given Frontier's tone and mood. "Your response reminds me," Frontier points to the audience. "It reminds me about something I read this afternoon. This afternoon I surfed over to the Eff Dubya Oh website and caught some ramblings, ramblings of a madman so to speak. I had the pleasure of reading the thoughts and proclamations of 'The Relentless One'." The audience, as expected jeered the mention of the name. "Eron called me a fake." More boos. "The bastard had the audacity to say that I was the biggest fake in the business. He couldn't be more wrong.
