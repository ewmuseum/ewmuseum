Introduction Time is like water, fluid and often changing. It seems like only yesterday, but it was six months ago when the fWo returned to action after a long hiatus. After a desperate inner-struggle against a shadowy board of directors, the fWo felt that they were finally on the right track and prepared themselves for normal life once more... or at least, as normal as it can get in a wrestling promotion. But just as things seemed to stabilize, the promotion was thrown into chaos none of them saw coming. Banished out of the United States after a stern new drug law was passed, the fWo has spent the last three months moving from country to country like modern-day nomads without a home. The journey has been harder on some than on others, as trials and tribulations have tested them all much harder and farther than any would have believed. Tonight one man, a desperate father unhinged from reality, will finally confront his long-lost son and the results of the meeting will finally bring a close to some issues... and ignite dangerous new ones...
