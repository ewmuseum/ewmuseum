Meltdown 1999, 27 August 1999

27 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Black Quicksilver vs. Stone In a rematch from their earlier contest, Stone took the early advantage with the help of her cohorts the Dark Riders. But as the match went on it was the Black Quicksilver and Stump that took the lead, dominating Stone at every turn. Just as Black Quicksilver was about to finish off the match from the top rope, the Dark Riders emerged dragging Black Quicksilver's ally Briana to the ring. Distracted, Black Quicksilver was taken advantage of by Stone, who executed a DDT and rolled him up for the pinfall victory. After the match the Dark Riders drug Briana into the ring and set to whip her with a chain, but Black Quicksilver valiantly offered to take her place, and was whipped ten times with the chain as a result. The Dark Riders left the ring as Briana tended to Black Quicksilver but was interupted by the Chameleon and Total Eclipse, who attacked Black Quicksilver while he was down. Ground Zero came to the ring to chase the team away, as the fWo went to the next match... Result: Stone by Pinfall Mike Bear (c) vs. Rain vs. Crucifix vs. Cactus Jack All four men came to the ring for the four-way Hardcore Title match, and it lived up to all expectations as the battle wandered out of the ring, into the crowd, and into the backstage area. Rain picked up the champion and tossed him through the glass window of a limosine in the backstage area. Mike Bear crawled through the glass, as the occupant of the limo exited to see what the commotion was. Crucifix worked on Cactus Jack, the two trading blows up into the second tier of the balcony. This would prove to be the key to the end of the match, as Rain drug Mike Bear back into the arena to finish him off. Crucifix suplexed Cactus Jack off the balcony of the arena and over the edge... where he fell upon Rain. Mike Bear, too exhausted to stand fell backward onto the fallen pile of bodies. Crucifix was helpless to stop the pinfall from the second level of the arena, and Mike Bear retained the Hardcore Title. Result: Mike Bear by Pinfall Black Panther (c) vs. Ice vs. US Marshal In what would prove to be a more physical match than the viewers expected, the Black Panther defended his title in a one-fall triple threat match. The Panther and the Marshal started out working in tandem against Ice, but soon the partnership dissolved and the two superstars began trading blows. Ice took advantage of the situation by sliding to the outside and resting as the two superstars traded suplexes, submission holds, and near pinfall attempts. The Panther finally trapped the US Marshal in a Texas Cloverleaf submission hold, but it was at that moment that Ice struck from behind with an underhook backward suplex. Ice drove the dazed and weary Panther into the mat face-first, and rolled him over for the quick pinfall victory. Ice walked from the ring with the Internet title held over his head as the Panther looked after the US Marshal inside the ring. Knowing that it was not over, the Panther looked after Ice with a bitter expression that spelled trouble down the road. Result: Ice by Pinfall Black Quicksilver & Lance Star, Darkness & Jackhammer, Crucifix & Melissa Smalls, Thumper & Twitch, Ultra Violet & Joy, US Marshal & D-Lo Brown, Black Panther & Mike Bear, Kid Cool & Death, Ice & Mad Trucker, Cactus Jon & Total Eclipse, Machina & Boston St The entrants of Meltdown came in this order: Black Quicksilver & Lance Star 1 Darkness & Jackhammer 2 Crucifix & Melissa Smalls 3 Thumper & Twitch 4 Ultra Violet & Joy 5 US Marshal & D-Lo Brown 6 Black Panther & Mike Bear 7 Kid Cool & Death 8 Ice & Mad Trucker 9 Cactus Jon & Total Eclipse 10 Machina & Boston Strangler 11 Briana & Fallen 12 Scott Slugger & Falcon 13 Prophet & Courage 14 Rain & Plague 15 Eron & Stone 16 Primetime & Wolf Fang 17 Chameleon & Oracle 18 Wretch & VL Tha Murdera 19 Flying Frenchie & Chris Universal 20 Black Quicksilver and Star started the match, but Black Quicksilver was obviously the weak link of the team after the beating he took earlier in the night. Darkness and Jackhammer manhandled the two men until Crucifix and Melissa Smalls came into the ring and went after Darkness and Jackhammer. It was more of the same when Trouble arrived in the ring and everyone went after the big men. Lance Star took advantage of the situation and pitched Thumper over the top rope. With an opening presented to him, Darkness grabbed Melissa Smalls and tossed her over the top rope, and then grabbed Black Quicksilver and tossed him out of the ring as well, leaving the team in the ring alone. Ultra Violet and Joy came to the ring, and quickly ran around the outside to avoid being grabbed by the big men. US Marshal and D-Lo came to the ring, and then Bear and Panther. The two teams worked with UV and Joy, and eliminated Darkness over the top rope with a combined team effort. The Marshal then attacked the Panther, before the lights went out in the arena and Death made his appearance. Death walked to the ring with the reluctant Kid Cool, but inside the ring Kid Cool tried to eliminate his own partner to impress Ultra Violet. This failed, and as Death knocked Kid Cool unconcious with a punch to the head, Ice and the Mad Trucker came to the ring. Black Panther met Ice as he stepped over the top rope with a superkick, knocking him to the outside nearly as soon as he stepped into the ring. Satisfied with this revenge the Panther attacked Cactus Jon as he made his way to the ring with the massive Total Eclipse. With a brawl breaking out in the ring between all teams, the Boston Srangler came out onto the ramp with the recently missing Machina. Machina stepped into the Strangler's hands, and executed a "fastball special" into the ring and onto D-Lo inside the ring. Working together the Strangler and Machina pitched out D-Lo, and then went to work on Death. Death countered with a double clothesline and attacked the weakened Mike Bear from behind, pitching him fromt the ring. Ultra Violet meanwhile revived Kid Cool, and instructed him on how to properly win a battle royal by jumping over the top rope. Kid Cool scratched his head in disbelief but gave it a shot, much to the dismay of Death, who attempted to stop him, but a moment too late. Briana came to the ring alone, but her partner Fallen soon followed, being led to the ring by Dina who had a leather strap around his neck. She pointed to Cactus Jon and released Fallen, who sprung into the ring after Jon, clawing, biting and tearing at the Canadian superstar. Cactus Jon fell over the top rope backwards with Fallen on top of him, both of them eliminated from the match. Fallen continued to tear like an animal at Jon as Scott Slugger and the Falcon came to the ring. Soon the battle was joined by Prophet and Courage, along with Rain and Plague. The eight men battled in the ring until Eron and Stone came to the ring. Eron scored a lucky suplex which eliminated Machina, and Courage kicked out Stone to bring the match back to six men. The uneasy team of Primetime and Wolf Fang came out next, and quickly went after seperate wrestlers. The Chameleon and Oracle came to the ring and Oracle helped Prophet, but the Chameleon came from behind to eliminate Courage. Angry at his partner being eliminated as well, Oracle delivered a kick to the Chameleon and went after the Falcon. Rain took advantage of the weakened Chameleon to toss him from the ring, and then went after Scott Slugger. Slugger ducked under and pitched him from the ring as Wretch and VL came out. Soon after Universal and the Frenchie finished the event participants off, and the eight men squared off in the ring. The Falcon attacked the Frenchie, but he ducked under and flipped the Falcon out. At the same time Scott Slugger sent out Wretch, and both teams were eliminated. Universal had Wolf Fang on the ropes, but Primetime attacked from behind with a kick and grabbed the arms of Wolf Fang as Universal toppled over. Primetime held onto Wolf Fang on the ring apron, and suplexed him back inside. Wolf Fang powered out of a pinfall attempt and locked on the Wolf Bite, but Primetime punched into the jaw of Wolf Fang to escape. Both men went for clotheslines, but it was Primetime that ducked under and hit a swinging DDT, and then covered Wolf Fang for the pinfall victory. Inside the ring Primetime held his arms high, signalling the end of Meltdown, and the winner for 1999. Result: Primetime
