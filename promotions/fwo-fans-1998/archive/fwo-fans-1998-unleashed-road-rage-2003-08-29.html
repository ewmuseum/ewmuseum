Introduction The summer burns to an end with the start of the Labor Day Weekend, and the fWo's big outdoor arena show. The Moab Fairgrounds are known for the Arches, a natural wonder second to none. It is here, in the shadow of this natural beauty that the fWo has come... the likelyhood of destruction to follow in their wake... Fighting against (with) Reason It had been an odd night. Instead of being his usual, friendly self, Kevin Masters was very focused tonight. Tonight wasn't a true wrestling match, it was more than that. It was going to be a personal battle. Kevin wasn't just fighting for himself. He wasn't just fighting a booked match to defend his championship. He was fighting to protect what he held dear. He was protecting his legacy. Protecting what he had earned. He was protecting the personal property that had been defiled for weeks. He was protecting what he cherished, the paints he now held in his hand. He was protecting the makeup that made him the hero he is. He glanced at the belt, and smiled. He then opened the first jar, and looked to the mirror. He ignored the dark shadows under his eyes, and the sick feeling in his stomach. It didn't matter that it felt off to fight this match. All that mattered was his honor. He dipped his finger in, and what he felt wasn't pride. It was pain. He felt pain shoot through his hand, as he looked at the makeup. A razor blade had cut his fingers, but as one part was sharp, another in his middle finger was rusted. It stuck now in his palm, which had been caught as Kevin tried to pull his hand away. And it bled. It was bleeding pretty heavily, and Kevin cursed. He knew who had done it, who the true culprit was. This just confirmed it, and it also confirmed his gut feeling. But on this night he would fight his gut. He would fight himself, because he had a greater enemy tonight. He had a rival, and he had something to prove. Something to protect. He wrapped his hand with tape and nodded to himself. He hurt, but was ready, and he had to do it. The paint was on, and his transformation complete. Now, he was the Dynamo. Carraba vs. Joey Villani The tension between the Emo Kids and the Villani Connection had been rising up over the past couple of weeks. With the breaking of Carraba's aviator sunglasses and the stealing of Atkins' aviators, the Villani Connection seemed unstoppable in their quest to rise to the top of the mountain. Tonight, the Emo Kids want revenge. And as the lights over the entrance began to flicker and Lacuna Coil's "No Need to Explain" began to play. Joey said, knowing he was going to get a loud pop of boos. "I didn't come out here for ya wops ta boo me! I came out here because I'm a rockstar!" The crowd seemed confused as to what Joey was talking about. It was obvious that he was impersonating Carraba but the crowd thought that last comment was just plain stupid. Which it was. But Joey reached into his tights and pulled out a pair of aviator sunglasses. To be more exact, Atkins' aviator sunglasses. But before Joey could say anymore, the quick beats of Saves the Day's "Banned From the Back Porch" hit the PA. The crowd erupted in a wave of cheers. The artist formally known as Chris Carter ran down the ramp as some of the ladies in the crowd yelled "We love you Carraba!" Before Carraba slid into the ring, he posed for the crowd. "You know, you don't have to let him treat you like this..." Despite the fact that they would be opponents soon, Jane Matheson wasn't going to shirk away from what she felt was right. From the moment they were aligned as part of Survivor III's Green Team, Jane had hated Elliot's treatment of his brother, Thomas. Everyone but Thomas himself knew he was being used, but was so blinded by the proverbial brotherly love that he couldn't see it. Or didn't want to see it. "Elliot told Thomas not to talk to you," was his innocent reply as soon as he heard Jane's words and saw her approach. He looked fearful in case Elliot would stumble across them and feel betrayed. "And do you have to do everything he asks?" Matheson enquired, wanting to see Elliot Harper get what was coming to him more than anyone else... And what better distributor of justice than the one who's suffered the most at his hands. Thomas nodded. "He looks after me," Thomas added, showing he felt safe around his manipulative sibling. Jane sighed, seeing that Thomas was blinded to his brother's negatives. Even though they'd been in opposite corners many times before, Thomas was always placid around Jane, who was always pleasant towards him... Trying to gain his trust. "And is yelling at you 'looking after' you?" she questioned, trying not to push too hard. "What about hitting you?" Thomas' head dropped and his fringe masked his face. "I deserved it," he mumbled softly, "I did bad." "No you didn't, Thomas," Jane shot back, direct and sincere, "You did everything you could... HE's the one who did wrong, not you." It didn't seem to work though, as Elliot had hammered guilt into Thomas so harshly that it would be hard to sooth. "Thomas did bad, didn't win for Elliot." Jane cautiously sat down beside the bulky Brotherly Love member, trying to look through his fringe and into his face... Only for Thomas to turn his head away. "Winning isn't everything, Thomas... There are more important things than making your brother famous." It looked as if Thomas was starting to come around, as if Jane had finally opened up a chink in his armour... And was about to exploit it, turn Thomas on Elliot so he'd have to do his own dirty work for once. But, it wasn't to be. "Thomas," came a shout from down the corridor, around the corner... Still distant. "Where are you?" it continued not sounding best pleased. Before the echoes had even subsided, Thomas got to his feet, leaving Jane sitting and growling at the interruption. Elliot's voice repeated itself from out of view, and Thomas knew he must obey. "I have to go," he solemnly stated before trundling off towards Elliot's voice, "You'd better go... He gets angry..." Jane sighed and leaned back against the wall, disappointed at once again not quite getting through to him.... But she wasn't done yet. "Thomas," Jane called to him, attracting the Harper brother's attention, "You don't have to do anything you don't want... Not for anyone." He didn't reply before he turned and walked off out of sight, but there was a brief look that said he understood... And maybe even might take heed of her words.
