Sunday Slaughter, 24 March 2002

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Tempest stood outside the door to Monte Burns' locker room and raised her hand to knock. Then she lowered it. Then she raised it again and lowered it. She shook her head and said, "Oh come on, just do it." She lifted her hand and knocked, taking a deep breath. "Yes?" asked the voice of Monte Burns from inside. "Monte? It's me, Tempest," she said, biting her lip. Silence hangs heavy in the air and Tempest tries again, "Um... is now not a good time?" Still nothing was heard from the other side, as Tempest stood before the door. Suddenly, the handle turned and the door swung wide open. Looking right into Tempest's eyes was Burns, with a mixture of pain and anger showing on his face. Tempest summoned a smile and said, "Hi... um, how are you? I mean uh..." She shook her head and said, "I just wanted to thank you for taking on Ice last week. I didn't know you would do something like that and... Well I noticed." Burns just stared into her eyes, not saying a word. He looked down at the floor, obviously thinking through what she said and what he wanted to say in response. A moment later, he lifted his head back up and gazed into her eyes. Then, he slammed the door. Tempest jumped and stared at the door quizzically. Her lips moved a moment and then she sighed and turned away. She turned back a moment and called, "Good luck against Winters Monte..." and more quietly, "You might need it." Slaughter went to its opening credit package and intro, and inside the arena, the fans roared their cheers... Donaven Winters (c) vs. Monte Burns Slaughter heads to the ring for a battle of old enemies, who are meeting once more. "Nobody's Real" plays, and Monte Burns makes his way to the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd. Burns stops by ringside and begins digging underneath the ring, looking for weapons, as his music dies down. "Piggy" plays, and the crowd boos as Donaven Winters makes his way to the ring, Hardcore Title around his waist. Halfway down the entrance ramp, Winters charges at Burns, who side-steps him and shoves him forward, causing him to slam into the side of the ring, knocking the belt loose. Burns immediately grabs Winters and begins hammering away at him with punches, knocking him back towards the guardrail. Burns grabs Winters and lifts him up, then drops him throat-first across the guardrail. Winters stumbles back, and Burns leaps onto the guardrail, then leaps off with a version of an asai spinning lariat, knocking Winters down to the floor. Burns makes a cover, but gets just two. Burns brings Winters up to his feet again and hits a snap suplex, driving Winters into the floor. He then brings Winters back up and double-underhooks his head, going for a double-arm DDT. Winters fights back, driving his fists into the midsection of Burns, causing Burns to loosen and finally lose his grip. Winters then heads over to the pile of weapons Burns has pulled out from under the ring and begins digging around in them, searching for an appropriate weapon to use on his opponent. He finally comes up with a small cassette tape. Winters stands up from the pile, and just in time, as Burns is approaching him. Winters swings punch-style with the cassette in his hand, shattering it and knocking Burns back down to the floor. Sharp-eyed viewers note that, ironically enough, the cassette is one made by Wham!. Winters then comes up with another weapon a good old-fashioned steel chair. Winters taps the chair on the ground, waiting for Burns to get up. Burns does, and Winters swings the chair at him. Burns ducks it and rolls behind Winters, who sees it and lets his follow-through go completely through, nailing Burns with a (slightly less impactful) chairshot, just on the backswing rather than the frontswing. Winters turns around and approaches Burns, then brings the chair down on the top of his head, knocking him down to the floor. With Burns down, Winters elects not to go for the cover, but rather heads back under the ring. This time, he emerges with a table. Burns begins to set the table up, preparing to execute who knows what kind of move on Winters and perhaps secure his first fWo Hardcore Title. Burns finally gets the table completely set up, then brings Winters up and throws him on top of it. Burns then climbs up himself, joining Winters on top of the table. Burns brings Winters up to his feet and hooks him about the head, preparing to drive him through the table. He then falls back, executing a russian legsweep, slamming the back of Winters' head into the table. To the great surprise of both Burns and the fans, the table fails to break, instead staying in one piece which may be just as well for Burns, considering the move executed and its impact. Burns rolls over onto Winters and makes the cover, and the ref counts the pinfall by slapping the top of the table. One. Two. Winters kicks out. Burns curses, sure that he had won it, then gets up and steps onto the apron. He then heads to the top rope, looking to put Winters away once and for all and bring the Hardcore Title home to add to his proverbial trophy case. Burns reaches the top rope and looks down, measuring Winters. He then leaps. The Establishment. Misses. Donaven Winters rolls off the table, and Monte Burns crashes through it at high velocity, shattering the table and landing hard on his back and neck on the floor. Table debris flies everywhere. Winters slowly drags himself to his feet and brings Burns up, knowing that this is his best opportunity yet. He brings Monte Burns to his feet and hooks him up, then executes the Reflection on the floor, throwing Burns hard to the ground. Winters crawls over and makes the cover. The ref makes the count. One. Two. Three. Donaven Winters secures a tough, hard-fought win, and retains his Hardcore Title once again. Winters, weary, grabs the belt and raises it high in the air in triumph as Slaughter heads backstage...
