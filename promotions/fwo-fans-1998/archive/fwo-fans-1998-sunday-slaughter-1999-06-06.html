Sunday Slaughter, 6 June 1999

6 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Rain fell in Colorado as the rental cars and limousines arrived in the backstage parking lot and wrestlers were sent in their separate directions by fWo security. The fWo had not run a show in Colorado Springs before, and tensions were high amidst the backstage personnel regarding running a show in this new environment. Suddenly a purple beetle drove into the parking lot, came to a stop by colliding with another car, and Ultra Violet the fWo World Champion rolled out. Pausing to shake himself, Ultra Violet collected his bag, title, and small crystal before getting clumsily to his feet. As he did this, Chris Universal walked by and sneered at Ultra Violet, pointing to his belt and telling him that tonight he leaves the arena with two titles. Ultra Violet looked confused by this, and asked if Universal was getting the Tag Team titles. Universal rolled his eyes and left Ultra Violet to ponder this lesson of math as other wrestlers came to the arena. One wrestler in particular was causing a commotion... Eron the Relentless. Eron was arguing with a backstage official who was telling him that since he didn't have a match tonight there was no reason why he should be in the arena. Eron pointed to Bookerman entering the arena through the backstage entrance, arguing that Bookerman wasn't even on the active roster anymore let alone the card. The official commented that he didn't know anything about a Bookerman, but that he would have to leave. As Eron turned to go, Oracle walked by and laughed at him before entering the arena. Finally the limousine of the Dark Riders arrived at the arena, and Stone holding her fWo Womens championship title, was followed by Death and Scott Taylor. The group stalked into the arena, knocking aside fWo security guards and wrestlers as they entered. Death grabbed Geoff Green and yelled at him, asking where Silver Fox was. Geoff responded that he didn't know, and the Dark Riders stalked away, leaving destruction in their path. Once they were safely out of eyesight, Geoff Green pulled at the side of his face, ripping off a latex Geoff Green mask and revealing Silver Fox beneath. Silver Fox laughed as the Chameleon joined his side. Pointing at the arriving French Foundation limousine, Silver Fox told the Chameleon that "he knew what to do". Inside, fireworks exploded as the show officially began... The big challenge Slaughter in-arena action opens up with "Baad Ass" Austin Davis storming down the shiny entrance ramp with a look of vengeance on his face and an intense glare from his eyes. Davis climbs into the ring and demands a microphone. Davis receives one and the Slaughter music stops as Davis begins... "The past week or so I've spent sitting around the house in Arizona contemplating on my future here in the fWo. Contemplating on what has happened to me here in the fWo, and pondering what *will* happen to me here in the fWo. I've came to a conclusion... I'm not ready to stop. I'm not ready to quit making life hell for Chris Universal. I'll be here for a long time to come... and that's a guarandamntee. Secondly, I have a challenge for Chris Universal. In just under... 12 days from now, there'll be an fWo house show. I'm issuing you a challenge. You pick a partner, I'll pick a partner, and we'll get it on in this ring just like we did the last time we had a tag team match in the fWo on opposite sides of the fence back in October. If you want to be a man and accept it, I'll be here the rest of the night. Any time you feel like coming out here and accepting, go right ahead. If you don't accept by the end of the night, I'll hunt you down and make sure will NOT walk out of this arena. You'll be carted out in either a wheelchair, an ambulance, or a hearse. Trust me". Davis was about to leave the ring when we heard "Do you buy what the Sellout is selling?" and out walked Chris Universal dressed in an Armani suit with the Internet Title draped over his shoulder and the beautiful Megan Dawn at his side. Universal had a microphone with him and Dawn held it up to his face, as he slid his sunglasses up past his forehead and looked at Davis with concern. Universal wondered if there was a catch involved, and Davis said no... but now that he thought about it, they could add some stipulations. Davis went on to say that if he pinned Universal in the tag team match, Universal would have to cough up Megan Dawn for thirty days. Then, he dropped the bombshell... if Universal pinned Davis in the match, Davis would retire from the fWo. Universal readily accepted while Megan Dawn looked at him shocked, as if she didn't have confidence in her man. Universal then said he would introduce his partner on Warfare this coming Wednesday. Davis said he didn't care when he did it, 'cause the fact of the matter was that on the 18th, Megan Dawn would be his bitch. With that, Slaughter went to a commercial break reeling from this huge announcement... Rudo vs. Wild Thing A shrieking howl comes from the backstage area; and out comes Wild Thing. He enters the ring and waits for the new leader of the Lone Gunmen, Rudo Insano. Rudo comes out, looking uncertain of what he's about to do, before climbing into the ring. Rudo looks at Wild Thing and scratches his head, before Wild Thing grows impatient and charges at Rudo, spearing him through the middle ropes and out onto the cement floor. Wild Thing then brings him back into the ring and pins him, as Rudo appears unconscious. Wild Thing then leaves the ring, and several referees run down and attempt to help Rudo up. Rudo quickly gets up and tells everyone to get away from him, then he notices something about his shoes... his face brightens, as it appears as if he has an idea. He then emphatically announces that he lost becaues he was not wearing his "lucky" shoes. He demands Wild Thing to get his freakish ass back in the ring so he can kick it with his lucky shoes, but Wild Thing simply shrugs it off and leaves. Rudo appears to be almost crying as Slaughter heads to a commercial break. Result: Wild Thing by Pinfall The return of Nixon? Outside the arena most of the fWo superstars had already arrived, and security was getting ready to lock the backstage entrance and turn their focus to the inside action. As John Willis took one last look around the backstage parking area, one last superstar came running into the building. Nixon carried with him a bag and baseball bat, and waved to John Willis as he ran by. Willis waved him in, and locked the doors, joking with another fWo security guard about how Eron was kept out earlier in the night. The cameras turned their attention from the security personnel to Nixon, who turned a corner and tossed his bag on the ground. He ripped off the Nixon mask, revealing Eron the Relentless beneath. Leaving the bag on the ground and keeping the steel bat, Eron muttered to himself that after dealing with Oracle he would be coming back for Willis... Prophet vs. Oracle Prophet came to the ring to a poor response from the crowd, but waved at them anyway, as if they had just delivered a standing ovation. Lights dimmed as Oracle made his way to the ring, the crowd cheering the stoic man. The crowd continued to cheer as the former tag team locked up, with Oracle taking Prophet down to the mat with a quick drop toe hold, and then working the arm with a sitting vertical stretch. Oracle slapped the head of Prophet as he continued to work in the hold, yelling and mocking his former partner. Prophet attempted to spin around and get off the defensive, but Oracle dropped repeated knees into the face of Prophet, keeping him grounded and helpless. Oracle finally spun backward and let Prophet get to his feet, and then took him down again with a running dropkick. As Oracle went for a cover, the crowd erupted as Eron appeared on the entrance ramp and ran down to ringside. Spotting him on the edge of the ring, Oracle released his cover and charged, but was met with a blow to the head by the bat Eron was carrying. Oracle stumbled backward, and Prophet wasted no time making a cover on the unconscious Oracle. His job done, Eron began to head back to the backstage area, but Prophet ran to catch up with him. Prophet began to thank Eron, who returned his thanks by attacking him with the bat, and pushing him off the entrance ramp. Security hit the scene and swarmed Eron, escorting him from the arena much to the anger of the fans, who booed Eron's ejection loudly. Result: Prophet by Pinfall Scott Slugger and Ice interviewed... Backstage Scott Slugger was in his dressing room with Ice, taping up his fists and getting ready for his Hardcore title defense later in the evening. Matt Loco was interviewing the pair, discussing their upcoming Tag Team title shot at Fight the Future I, the first House Show of the new series. Ice scoffed at the question, telling Loco that it would be child's play taking the titles from the poor French wannabes, and that he personally planned to lay down the biggest beating Mitch Wilson has ever seen. Scott Slugger then grabbed the microphone and told the crowd that on Friday he would carry two titles, and would have no trouble taking three, four, or however many he wanted. As Slugger was about to continue, the door to his locker room swung open, and Waru, along with two large unknown men faced down Slugger. Waru told him that he had no idea what it meant to be hardcore, and that if he wasn't gutless like all other Americans he would put his title and life on the line in a real hardcore match... Stone (c) vs. Apocolyptica Apocolyptica came to the ring with Melissa Smalls, and had her arms taped and looked ready to take the Womens title away from Stone. As Stone's name was announced the entrance ramp remained empty, and an angry Apocolyptica and Melissa Smalls began to walk up the entrance ramp in search of her missing opponent. Suddenly chairs and trash cans began to fly out from the backstage area, and the full compliment of Dark Riders emerged. Apocolyptica began to back up and return to the ring, but Death ran forward and grabbed her by the neck, and then chokeslammed her into the steel entrance ramp. Stone watches as Death mauled Apocolyptica on the ramp, and dispatched War and Plague after Smalls. With Smalls and Apocolyptica detained by the Dark Riders, Stone grabbed a microphone and informed the crowd that she had held the fWo womens title for some time now, and wasn't going to defend it against just any challenger. Stone demanded a womens battle royal at the Fight the Future house show, with the winner facing her in the ring for the title right after the match. Death then grabbed the microphone and declared that the night would be grinding to a halt until Silver Fox showed his cowardly face... and dared the fWo security team to try and get them out of the ring. Backstage cameras spotted Silver Fox watching these events unfold with a smile on his face, and spoke quietly into a cel-phone, "Now". Result: No Contest The plan unfolds Cameras backstage found the Chameleon wandering the halls, cellular phone in hand. Around his waist was the fWo International title, and as he rounded a corner he came face to face with the Flying Frenchie, who was screaming at an fWo official about his missing belt. The Chameleon asked the Frenchie if he was perhaps missing something, and then ran as the Frenchie rushed after him. Both men ran through the halls with the Frenchie chasing closely behind the Chameleon, who quickly darted toward the ring entrance, and ran out onto the entrance ramp. The Chameleon ran to the ring, and slid inside where the Dark Riders waited. The Chameleon offered the International title to Death, telling him that he got what Silver Fox and he wanted. Death looked confused, but the Frenchie who was enraged quickly slid inside the ring and shoved Death. The Frenchie quickly grabbed his title and yelled at Death, who responded by attacking with high fists to the face of the leader of the French Foundation. The rest of the Dark Riders joined the attack as Primetime and Mitch Wilson ran to the ring to help their leader. During the confusion, the Chameleon slid from the ring and walked back up the entrance ramp, laughing at the brawl he created inside the ring. Scott Slugger (c) vs. Crucifix The lights in the arena went dark as whispering was heard over the arena speakers, and Crucifix slowly made his way to the arena. He carried with him a crowbar and an axe, and sat in the center of the ring waiting for Scott Slugger to make his appearance. The house lights rose as Scott Slugger's music bared over the arena's speakers as the Hardcore Champion made his way to the ring, Hardcore title in hand, and a trash can full of weapons in the other. Slugger walked to the ring and began to dump the contents of the trash can into the ring, finally entering it himself with a shovel in hand. Crucifix charged Scott Slugger, but he ducked under the attack and swung the shovel, colliding and breaking it across the face of the challenger. Blood sprayed from the face of the mysterious Crucifix, and he rolled outside the ring to recover. Not wanting to give him the opportunity to rest, Scott Slugger grabbed a brick from the assortment of garbage in the ring and climbed to the top turnbuckle. Slugger dove to the outside of the ring with the brick raised, but Crucifix moved out of the way and pushed down on Slugger as he fell past, sending his head colliding with the metal ring barrier. Slugger crumpled to the floor as Crucifix walked around the ring looking for something to attack with, and finally settled on a circular saw. Starting it, Crucifix attempted to cut into the head of the champion, and Scott Slugger held him back with all the strength he could muster. Slugger was finally able to push Crucifix aside, and the saw met with the metal barrier and sprayed an arc of sparks over the crowd before breaking the saw blade off and sending it spinning off wildly into the audience. Dropping the useless saw, Crucifix attacked with a series of kicks to the face of Slugger, who was still dazed and bleeding profusely from the skull. Slugger tried to crawl away from the frantic attack, but Crucifix grabbed the garbage can and tossed it at Slugger, hitting him squarely on the back. Crucifix then grabbed a pair of numchuks that was in the pile of material brought to the ring, and smacked Slugger in the head. He then wrapped the chain around the neck of Slugger, and proceeded to choke him on the entrance ramp. Slugger began to lose consciousness, and crawled to the edge of the entrance ramp. In desperation, Slugger tossed himself off the ramp, sending himself and Crucifix plummeting down fifteen feet into the crowd. Crucifix hit his head on one of the chairs, and Slugger took the opportunity to grab another chair and put it over the head of Crucifix. Slugger then stomped down on the steel, crushing Crucifix's head between the arena floor and the steel back of the chair. Fans began to turn away in disgust, as Crucifix's head began to bleed badly, and Slugger continued to stomp the chair. Crucifix refused to submit, and refused to scream, and only gritted his teeth and continued to work to get free. Finally tired of stomping, Slugger slumped down on the body of Crucifix, using the chair as added weight, and got the pinfall victory. As Slugger stood and was handed his belt, fans gasped at the bloody face of the champion. Slugger raised the title in the air, but as he did so the lights dimmed, and a low rumble was heard over the arena. Fans looked up in confusion, and to their astonishment rain began to fall from the rafters. Tired and confused by what this meant, Scott Slugger limped from the arena. Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall Backstage happenings Cameras returned to the backstage area, where several events were unfolding at once. Geoff Green and fWo camera crews caught up with VL, who was carrying a steel baseball bat and a batch of barb wire. VL was stomping through the halls of the arena, and finally approached a single locker room, with "Stevie Roberts" written on the door. VL stood for a moment outside the locker room, and then kicked at the door, sending it flying open. From inside, Robert's voice was heard, yelling "what in the hell?" before the door to the locker room was slammed shut, and sounds of a scuffle were heard from inside... Meanwhile, on another end of the arena Primetime was standing guard outside the French Foundation dressing room. He was doing a poor job of it, because Nikki the Valley Girl was dancing for him, and massaging his neck. Nikki kissed him on the side of the face, and continued to rub the Tag Team champion as various members of the fWo roster walked by. One member in particular looked horrified at the scene, and rubbed his eyes as if he could not believe what he was seeing. Finally, Joy shook his head in disgust and walked away with a new found purpose... Finally, fWo cameras followed Chris Universal into his dressing room, as he was making final preparations before his big title match. Chris Universal walked with a swagger, but suddenly stopped cold as he looked inside his dressing room. All of his clothes and bags were marked with black paw prints, and as Universal looked over the scene in horror, his eyes settled on a note laid atop a bench. Picking it up, Chris Universal read: "Chris Universal, the Internet Champion.Megan Dawn by his side looking like one in a million.However, for Universal, all is not well.As his life has turned into a living hell.It looks like the Panther has struck.And Universal's dead out of luck.So Universal, ask yourself this.Do I know where my Title is?" Crumpling the note up in a fist, Universal angrily stomped off toward the ring... Ultra Violet (c) vs. Chris Universal "Do you buy what the $ellout is selling?" begins to play and out walks the fWo Internet Champion "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal. At his side is Megan Dawn, and with him as always, of course, the Internet Title. Universal pauses and takes a deep breathe, before walking down the ramp and into the ring. Universal climbs up onto the ring apron and holds his Internet Title with one hand high in the air, showing everyone that he *is* the champion. Universal then climbs into the ring and the green dollar $ign pyro lowers into place, before gold fireworks begin shooting out. Dawn stands behind him as Universal poses, before giving Dawn the Internet Title as she exits the ring. Suddenly, we hear "Rage!" blare all throughout the arena, and out comes our fWo World Champion Ultra-Violet. UV makes his way out with Violet, who carries the World Title for him. UV sprints down into the ring and trips, falling face first onto the ramp. He then stands up, scratches his head in wonderment of what happened, before hopping up onto the ring apron. Violet glares at Megan Dawn and Universal simply stands there smirking, before rolling his eyes. The ring announcer reminds everyone that the Internet Title is NOT on the line, so if Universal loses, he does not relinquish the title. UV looks rather sad about this, and tells Universal if he does lose, he'll give him a belt to make up for it. Universal turns rather angry and instructs the referee to ring the bell, who does. Universal and UV lock-up in the center of the ring, and UV quickly hip tosses him, which surprises everyone, even Ultra-Violet himself. UV then offers to help him up with one hand, and Universal quickly pulls him down to the mat into a small package. UV quickly kicks out at the count of 2 and a half. Universal and UV spring up back to their feet at the same time, and Universal charges at UV with a clothesline. UV ducks and as Universal bounces off the ropes, Violet trips him. Megan Dawn looks over at Violet and the look on her face appears to say "bitch". Dawn then gets up onto the ring apron and starts to undo her shirt... and as all this happens, Rudo Insano begins to make his way down to the ring. Rudo appears as if he's scouting the match, but appears to be more focused on Dawn's shirt coming off and her bra being revealed, as does Ultra-Violet and every other red-blooded American in the building. UV watches as Universal stands behind him... meanwhile, Violet sees "her man" staring at Megan Dawn, and decides to put an end to it. She runs over and as Dawn is about to take off her bra, she pulls her off the ring apron and the two exchange pleasantries. Universal rolls up UV for a pin, but Rudo distracts the referee. Universal sees this and walks over and pushes Rudo off the ring apron, Universal then turns around and hits the "Cha-Ching" on UV as UV was getting up. Universal goes for the pin, and at the count of two and a half, Rudo pulls the referee out and begins to argue with him. Meanwhile, Dawn & Violet are either engaging in an intellectual conversation (highly unlikely) or saying un-nice things about each other. As the referee and Rudo argue, Mankind suddenly comes running down to the ring! The crowd erupts as he climbs into the ring, and as Universal gets up and sees him, Mankind kicks him in the stomach and then nails the double-arm ddt on him. He then stands up and does the "bang-bang!" gesture, before pulling Mr. Socko out from his pants. The crowd goes into a frenzy, as he places the sock over his hand... he then waits for Universal to get up, and locks in socko on him... choking him out. Universal slowly goes unconscious from the effects of socko. Mankind then places UV, who is still out-cold from the "Cha-Ching" he receives a few minutes earlier, ontop of Universal. Mankind goes outside the ring, and tells the referee of the pinfall in the ring. Mankind then goes over and grabs Megan Dawn, turns her around, and kisses her for a moment. She slaps him, and he just smiles evily and leaves the area. Inside the ring, UV has pinned Universal and has retained his World Title, but is still unconscious. Dawn is wiping the kiss from Mankind off her mouth, as Violet climbs into the ring and jumps up and Dawn, cheering for her man who is still World Champion, as Slaughter goes off the air... Result: Ultra-Violet by pinfall
