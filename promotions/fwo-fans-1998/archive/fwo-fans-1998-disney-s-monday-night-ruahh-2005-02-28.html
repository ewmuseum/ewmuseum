Introduction In a night that few will forget, the fWo set the stage for Cyberslam in an explosive night that none could have predicted. In the World Heavyweight Championship match Eli Flair defeated the fWo legend the Flying Frenchie to once more take the fWo World Heavyweight Championship for his own. In the 30-man BodyCount main event it was Deacon who survived and came out the winner, barely managing to get past a former BodyCount champion... Kodiak Vic Creed. Now it is these two long-time friends... enemies... rivals... Eli Flair and Deacon have a showdown at Cyberslam for the fWo World Heavyweight Championship... Setting the stage Backstage heads nodded and fingers slid casually over their technical boards. Lights flickered on silver and wood consoles, lighting up as show time emerged. For the fans in their seats, it was the moment that the first fireworks exploded and then were drowned out in an onslaught of pumping, pulsing music. For the technicians of the fWo, the magic started just a few minutes earlier. With the microphone tests, the light cycles, and the final sweep of security... this was the start of the show for the men and women who were never seen. The fWo had been running shows for nearly eight years. In this time, countless promotions had come and gone. Some had blazed bright, some had flickered into the dark with barely more than a memory. The legion who followed this sport, worked in this industry, and even those who walked down the entrance ramp had their stories. Every one had come from somewhere else, had lived through the glory and the despair of the wrestling industry. Some, schooled in the finer arts of professional wrestling. The skill and talent of the ring, the rush and energy of executing the perfect move... of upholding tradition and working for the glory and betterment of an industry they lived, loved and most of all respected. Some came bathed in blood, forged through the underground pits where the art of wrestling was of no more use than the art of survival. Where bricks, steel and stone were as valuable as any suplex or hiptoss. Each viewed the other with the upmost content... but in the strange world of professional wrestling they lived in a dangerous coexistence. Each believed the other was inferior. Each was, of course, quite wrong. But in the world, ignorance and contempt were what made the world go around. Ignorance, contempt... and of course, ego. It had been over a year since Monday Night Ruahh~! had first taken to the air. It had been penned by the finest marketing men in the industry had to offer. Of course, many didn't understand the subtleties, and this was what John Smith enjoyed most. Subtlety was the spice of life... the gears to his great clockwork John Smith folded his hands on his lap, and watched the cameras that watched the technicians. It had been a broad, vast game, and it was slowly coming to fruition. Not the end, no, for the end would be years, perhaps decades from now... if the end ever came at all. It was certainly possible to go on forever. Jesus went on forever, even after he died and was reborn again. God was forever, and his hands had not been in the pie for thousands of years. Chaos had returned to the fans Wrestling organization.
