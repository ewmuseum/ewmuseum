Disney's Wednesday Warfare, 23 November 2005

23 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The 11/21 Warfare Report Show report from Dave Anderson. Just got back from the show, which I guess didn't air on television due to the holiday. I hate this time of year, and the stupid football game that plays. I don't care about the stupid Broncos and Cowboys this year or any year. Anyway, the fWo hasn't been in Salt Lake City since April 2002, even though this has always been a strong market for them. Well, big mistake in the booking! We had a good crowd, although the lack of presence at the area hurt, as well as the holiday. I'd estimate about 14,000, but some of the arena was roped off. Everyone hitting the road for turkey rather than watch wrestling I guess. I doubt the fWo will come to Utah again any time soon, although the crowd was actually quite lively. The show opened with old fWo wrestler Fyre (remember her?) and the national anthem, then she introduced fWo GM Ted Tallison, who got good heat while he ran down the wrestler of the year tournament. Tallison promised all of us a "big surprise" in three weeks. Well, a lot of good that did us in the arena. They then talked about tag action, which led us to: (1) Sphere/Doctor Curiosity defeated Tom Seena & StepJack. Even though they'll be opponents in the first round of the Wrestler of the Year tournament next week, Sphere and Curiosity still competed as a team for this match... almost. Sphere gave Curiosity a wedgie before the match and pointed this out to the ref. The official spent most of the early moments of the match reprimanding Curiosity and telling him to "fix himself". Sphere used the distraction to steal Seena's hat and chains. He used one of the chains to punch Seena in the head, while wearing Seena's hat (sideways, of course). When Curiosity was finally ready to go, he faced off with StepJack. Doc was somehow able to temporarily blind StepJack with his labcoat, but due to the length of the coat, couldn't find the right spot to uppercut StepJack, as it appeared he was attempting a low blow. Eventually, Sphere was able to fool the ref into thinking Seena was bringing a hubcap into the ring, and he entered the squared circle, giving StepJack a low blow himself, allowing Curiosity to get the winning pinfall. Sphere got on the microphone and promised his tag partner that getting a win wouldn't be that easy next week. On the FanTron, Michelle Masters was backstage and confronted Coral Avalon again about the shadiness of the Blue Rogues in light of their actions against C.T.U. last week. Coral apologized, but again pointed out the Codemaster was a close friend, much like Youngblood was one of Michelle's that Coral didn't like. Michelle got upset with Coral and stormed off in tears...but not before a Cruiserweight title match was signed for next week. (2) Spyder defeated some local wrestler. The crowd wasn't as much into Spyder as they were a few years back. I can't understand what the point is of his character now, because he seems to only show up for TV once every few months. You can't build any momentum that way. Anyway, Spyder ran down Utah and the Mormons, and said that we were stupid for marrying all these women rather than just having sex with them like he did. Whatever. Spyder gets ultimately chased away by Renshaw with a steel chair. For this, Renshaw gets a pop. On the FanTron, Mike Heftel stood near a backstage entrance, apparently locked out of the building. He repeatedly banged on the door, pleading with someone to open it. A security guard asked Heftel to move out of the way, and when he did, the security guard opened the door with no problem and entered the building. Heftel watched the door close, then started to bang on it again. He finally gave up and walked away, but not before blaming Mega Job. Backstage, Alex Creed, who was trying to avoid Cabbot Wilson, discovered an upset Michelle Masters. He offered his "Mad Mecca Comfort" (wacky Alex), but also reminded the redheaded Cruiserweight champion about her promise of a rematch. The two hugged and went their ways with that question still hanging about. (3) Craig Miles defeated the Midnight Cowboy. Crowd was really into Miles. Miles was on the defensive for the majority, with the crowd cheering him on. Midnight Cowboy then went on a tear wowing the crowd with his power arsenal, hitting the Spinebuster and Powerbomb. Late match, after several failed Miles comeback attempts, with all of us really into things, Cowboy goes for a big boot, but Miles ducks and catches his opponent with an Inverted Side Russian Legsweep and then hit his finisher 'South of Sanity' for the quick pin. In a short promo, "Untouchable" Carson Nash was hyped as coming soon. A short video package of Nash working out and being in the best shape of his life was shown. The crowd didn't seem to be very fond of Nash as they booed even into what I assume is a commerical break. In a backstage segment, Keith announced that he was the future Wrestler of the Year to the C.T.U. Brandon Youngblood breezily announced that not only was Keith NOT going to win the tournament, he wasn't going to get out of the first round since he needed a miracle to beat Youngblood last time. The Only Diamond in the Sport then proceeded to stare Zimmerman out of the locker room. The audio was a little rough here, and it was hard to hear. (4) Rana Venenosa defeated Ichabod Michaels with the figure four leglock. Venenosa was very over, and surprisingly, so was Michaels. He acknowledged the guy next to me with the "Frog is God" sign and thanked him during the entrance. Lots of heat throughout the night, and especially this match. Utah loves Venenosa. Michaels did some good microphone work and about midway through the match John Tohru came down to cheer him on. Venenosa countered, did the "ref, check the time"/low blow spot and won with the Bloody Nose Dive. Kodane commiserated on the way to the back. Intermission. Old fWo wrestler/valet/eye candy Yukio threw t-shirts into the crowd. When she walked by, she slapped everybody's hands, gave one guy an autograph and even hugged another guy. She came across as a genuinely nice person. (5) Keith Scott Zimmerman defeated Brandon Youngblood via pinfall to advance to the quarterfinals of the WOTY tournament. Youngblood and Zimmerman got into a massive chopfest early, turning both their chests red. Brandon began to get the upperhand and had Keith scurrying to the outside of the ring on a number of occassions. Keith turned it around by turning a coldCOCKED into a springboard leaping swinging DDT. KSZ worked over Brandon's neck. Some hope spots for Brandon but Keith always cheated or hit the neck to get his control back. At the end, Youngblood got in a spot where he dodged the leg lariat, but when he tried the spinebuster his neck wasn't in it and Keith obliterated him with the leg lariat after. After a cradle piledriver, Keith flew off and hit Brandon with Michelle Masters' own 'Chelle Shocked (no, seriously) and won the match. Post match it looked like Keith was going to crush Brandon's neck region (the esophawhatever) but Michelle did a run-in and brawled Keith out of the ring. Ted Tallison came out onto the stage and told us we were all in for a treat, because the fWo had commissioned some of the finest new atheletes from "down low". The crowd cheered thinking more luchadors, but I had that bad feeling and sure enough, midget wrestling. This led to: (6) Mini Strangler defeated Cactus Jr. Not as bad as it sounds, but pretty bad. Both of the midgets (or I guess juniors is the right term) were dressed like the old wrestlers. Short, pointless match but good for a few laughs. After the match the Strangler gestured to his waist for the World Heavyweight Championship. Can't see that happening any time soon. As he got ready to leave the ring, out came "Ultra Small", who were dressed up like Ultra Violet and Scotty Cool. They even had Scotty Cool eating M&M Minis. That got a huge laugh. Brief brawl, and they went to the back. (7) Ninja K defeated Gianni. Ninja K got a big pop, and Gianni was pretty over and played to the crowd quite well. Gianni dominated most of the match, and the way he played to the crowd made me think that he needs to get back into a title picture. Gianni hit a nice series of chain wrestling moves, but Ninja K managed to slide out of a series of suplex attempts and ultimately Ninja K won with his finisher. (8) The Blue Rogues defeated Silver & Gold, fWo World Tag Team Title Match. Right away the Rogues should get their clocks cleaned in the beginning, but slowly take control at the six minute mark, and try everything to regain control when Sonny Silver gets the "hot tag". Suddenly the other five members of the Rogues run to the ring, but Troy fends them off with a chair from ringside. During this, Silver tossed the Codemaster should be thrown to the outside. It's chaos on the outside of the ring, and this leads to a ref bump after Sonny hits Coral Avalon with the Corona (harsh roundhouse kick). Sonny has it won and the crowd is going nuts, but the ref is out. Then this black-haired girl came out from the crowd, jumped on the apron and begins bad mouthing Sonny. Troy meanwhile is still working with the other members of the Entorage. The girl distracts Sonny long enough for Coral to recover and catch Sonny with the Second Impact for the three count. Troy looked frustrated and pissed, and I wouldn't be surprised if there was a breakup angle in the works, which is a big problem because the fWo needs good teams, bad. World Champion High Flyer came the ring to talk and got a pretty good deal of heat. Apparently, Flyer wants *his* Cruiserweight title, and he doesn't care what Tallison thinks, big shock, so he's putting up his belt and we're gonna have some title on title goodness. RIGHT. NOW. So this our main event: (9) High Flyer defeated Michelle Masters. Both were very over. Flyer got some boos, while Masters got lots of cheers, but it wasn't terribly anti-Flyer, though. Decent match that was carried by High Flyer working skills. One bump Masters took looked pretty bad, but she got up and seemed okay. The referee got bumped and Flyer hit the Elevated Boston Crab. A second ref ran in and counted two. Flyer then knocked the second referee out and took a chair into the ring, but Masters countered and got two from the first ref. Eye gouge, and Flyer hit a nasty looking reverse DDT. Flyer goes for the chair again, gets caught by the referee, and just clocks him with the chair. Notes: All in all, an enjoyable show with a lively crowd, although there were a number of pretty nasty fights that broke out. The fWo badly needs better security. There was a sense that some guys were elsewhere with their thoughts, but that, and the late changes in the card was understandable. I hope it's not another 3 years before they come back!
