He then took him right back down with another dragon screw. With Alias down, Death braced his knee, then jumped up and came down, driving the point of his own knee into Alias's. He did it again, then a third time. Death then pulled Alias by the leg towards the middle of the ring, held onto the leg, and then turned Alias over, applying a half crab on the leg that he had been working over the course of the match. Alias grimaced in pain, but worked up the strength to start dragging Death towards the ropes, pulling him further towards the edges of the ring. Death held onto the half crab stubbornly, pulling on it as hard as he could. Alias put all he had into pushing himself backwards, towards the ropes, trying to get in range so he could grab one and force a break... finally, he reached out, as far as he could - Death broke the hold, and before Alias could react, grabbed him by both legs and yanked him towards the middle of the ring again. Instead of reapplying the hold, Death brought Alias up to his feet, then set him up for a whip into the corner. Alias crashed into the turnbuckles, and Death followed him in, catching him with a dropkick to the leg. Death quickly regained his footing and grabbed Alias' leg, then wrapped it around the ropes and pulled on it as hard as he could. The referee warned Death to break the hold, and at four, he did. Death let Alias go as he stepped back, and Alias limped out - right into Death's grip. Death set him up and whipped him across into the opposite turnbuckles. Alias slumped in the corner, and Death charged in and leapt, going for another big splash. Alias moved. Death slammed head-first into the steel ringpost, and behind him, Alias grabbed him in a waistlock. He then lifted Death up over his head and slammed him down to the mat with a german suplex. Alias bridged up, going for the pinfall. One. Two. Kickout. The crowd gasped at the near-fall, and both men got to their feet. Death kicked out, going for the leg, but Alias caught his foot. He then countered with a headbutt to the nose, followed by a knee to the midsection, and finished off with an elbow to the top of the head, completing the Click Click Boom. Death stood where he was for a moment, swayed, then fell face-first to the mat. With Death down, Alias immediately stepped out onto the ring apron and headed for the turnbuckles, fighting through the pains he felt in his leg to climb up to the top rope, knowing that if he hit this high-impact maneuver, it would almost surely be over, and that was worth a little pain. More than a little, in point of fact. Alias reached the top turnbuckle and perched, ready to fly - Death was there. He gave Alias a hard shot to the gut, then another, then swept Alias' legs out from under him, crotching him on the top. Death shook his head to clear the cobwebs and started to climb up the ropes to join Alias. He stood up in front of him and hooked him, going for a superplex - Alias caught him with a big right hand to the head, then another, dazing the leader of the Dark Riders. He then shoved him down. Death crashed to the mat below, and Alias stood up on the turnbuckles. He leapt. He flew. He connected. Glasgow Kiss. It connected squarely, and the crowd popped, thrilled by the possibility of seeing Death go down here. Alias bounced off of the five-time former fWo World Champion, then lay on his back for a moment, feeling the impact of the headbutt himself. He slowly crawled back over... and draped an arm over Death's chest. One. Two. Thr - Kickout. Death barely managed to get a shoulder up. The crowd released their breath in disappointment - it had been thatclose. Alias, however, was not fazed. He knew what to do when his opponent was on the ropes. He reached down and brought Death up, then hooked him by the head. It was time to end it. Alias lifted Death up to his feet and kicked him in the gut, doubling him over. He then positioned Death between his legs, setting him up for the A-bomb, the tilt-a-whirl piledriver - and Death dropped down to a knee and gave Alias a low blow, stopping the move cold and doubling Alias over in the process. Death stood up, then sent himself backwards into the ropes. Alias raised his head - Death flew toward him - double-underhooked his head - spun - Alias threw him off before he could hit the Death Trip. Death landed on his feet, and charged at Alias. Alias ducked the attempted lariat, then grabbed Death in a half nelson, setting up for the Pulped. Death struggled, however, then escaped his grip and double-leg tripped Alias to the mat. Death immediately grabbed the legs and stepped through, then turned Alias over, applying the Death Trap tightly. Alias shouted in pain and tried to pull himself to the ropes. He came closer... closer... closer... reached out a hand... And fell short. Death took a few short steps forward, dragging Alias with him, then leaned back even harder on the hold. The referee continued to check Alias, and finally Alias had no choice but to tap the mat in submission. The crowd booed, and the referee called for the bell. Death continued to hold on to the hold as the bell rung, and the referee grabbed him and attempted to pull him free. Death released the hold - just long enough to slam the ref with a forearm shot, knocking him down. Death then reapplied the hold, shouting to any camera that would listen that this was what would happen to Quicksilver at Legends. Mitch Wilson rolled into the ring and made his own attempt to pull Death off of Alias, but Death was having none of it, and angrily shouted at Wilson not to interfere. As Wilson stood, deciding what to do next, the crowd began to cheer wildly. Black Quicksilver was here. The World Champion had come through the crowd, and leapt up onto the apron. He threw the World Title into the ring, then springboarded onto the top rope and leapt off. Mitch Wilson turned - just in time to eat a missile dropkick from Quicksilver that sent him crashing into Death, breaking the hold. Wilson rolled out of the ring, angered but not wanting to get more mixed up in this than he had to, and as Death pulled himself to his feet, the World Champion unloaded on him with a SuperQuick-SilverKick, sending Death flying over the top rope to the outside. Quicksilver then checked on Alias, making sure he was OK, before retrieving the World Title and taking it with him to the ropes. Quicksilver leaned over the top rope and lifted the belt high in the air, shouting at Death, who shouted back at him. Ruahh cameras faded to black on the image of the World Heavyweight Champion, perched on the ropes, belt held high in the air and gleaming in the lights... Result: Death by submission
