Introduction It is nearing the end for the landmark fWo show Wednesday Warfare. For six years the show has been on and off the air... but one thing about Warfare has remained constant: it has provided the fans with some of the most explosive and surprising events ever to be seen on television. But the exile from the United States and natural decline in interest of professional wrestling has taken a toll on the industry, and Wednesday Warfare has fallen. Along with Sunday Slaughter, the fWo is now in the business of looking for two new weekly shows to take their place. But after a disasterous showing to the network executives on Sunday Slaughter thanks to the antics of several fWo roster members, one network has now pulled out completely of the wrestling business, leaving the fWo with fewer options... and much more to worry about. Meanwhile... Ruben Ross. Brand Frontier. Legends in the fWo who got their start in a humble contest known as fWo Survivor. Part training camp, part psychological torture, fWo Survivor has always been viewed by the wrestling industry as the go-to place for upcoming superstars. This year the competition is stronger than ever... with thousands of wrestlers looking to compete in a tag team challenge. Survivor III begins tonight.
