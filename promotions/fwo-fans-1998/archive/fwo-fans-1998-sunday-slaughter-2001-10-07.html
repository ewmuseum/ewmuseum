Sunday Slaughter, 7 October 2001

7 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "Big night tonight, huh?" Ric Chronos asked his 3vil compatriots as the trio took the final steps to their locker room. "Oh yeah," smirked Primetime. "After all, you've got that big title defense against... heh... Quicksilver." Chronos frowned, and Frenchie cut in. "What Ric means is zat we've got ze chance to reestablish our power... last week, we were embarrassed. Quicksilver beat you, and surely Universal is trying to screw with us by bringing back zat Neo jobber... perhaps he will last longer zan a couple weeks zis time... anyway, it is not acceptable. Non. Zis week... we show what happens when somebody embarrasses T'reevil." "Yeah, there's that," conceded Primetime. "Tonight," he said, and opened the door, "the fWo gets a bigger beating that the Mariners soon will from the Cleveland... Indians..." Primetime trailed off as he saw the inside of the locker room. Chronos stepped forward first. "What the hell is this?" On the coffee table in the middle of the dressing room stood an empty bottle of Guinness. The table itself was spray-painted with a strange, Asian symbol. "It's a bottle of Guinness. Hardly tasteful. Zat tastes like horse piss. Ze finest champagnes... now zose are adult beverages." Chronos shot Frenchie a look. "Oh. Right. You mean what ze 'hell is zis in a general sense. My bad." "I know that symbol..." started Primetime. "It means... fate." "Fate." Chronos said the word, forcing it through his lips like he hated the taste of it. "Fate. Fate. Fate! FATE!" Chronos suddenly and violently upended the table, sending the bottle shattering against the wall. He began rampaging through the room, and the cameraman quickly beat a hasty retreat. Shattering and shouts came from inside the locker room as the cameraman backed out and away. *crash!* "Oh, zat was expensive..." *boom!* "Oh, that's gonna leave a mark..." Slaughter headed to its intro package, leaving behind the scene of destruction... STUMP Squad vs. East Coast Laws Slaughter begins inside the arena as "War?" blasts over the PA system, bringing out STUMP Squad members Mike Heftel and Jackhammer. Both men reach the ring, and await their opponents for this evening. "Push Upstairs" begins to play, and out walk Juno and Kobi, the East Coast Laws, for their first match as a team in several weeks. The Law brothers reach the ring, and it looks like Kobi will start the match out against Jackhammer. Jackhammer reaches for Kobi, but the smaller man ducks under the STUMP Squad's resident giant and dropkicks him in the back of the leg, sending him to the mat. Jackhammer attempts to get up, but Kobi hammers away, slowing him down. Kobi pulls Jackhammer up and sends him into the ropes, but makes what some would call a fatal mistake, as he ducks when Jackhammer comes off the ropes, and is met with a foot to the face. Jackhammer lifts a dazed Kobi over his head in a gorilla press, and holds him there for a few seconds before dropping him to the mat. Kobi bounces off the mat, and stumbles around again, before Jackhammer takes him down with a rolling anklelock. "I'm DA MAN!", Jackhammer shouts, as he keeps the submission hold on Kobi. Everyone involved in the match seems confused at Jackhammer's comments--even Jackhammer himself--and Kobi grabs the bottom rope in the confusion, forcing Jackhammer to release the hold. Jackhammer waits for Kobi to get to his feet, and charges, but hits the bottom turnbuckle when Kobi executes the dreaded drop-toe-hold. Kobi tags in his brother Juno, and the duo plant Jackhammer with a double side suplex. Kobi goes out to the apron, while Juno makes a cover and gets a count of two. Juno brings Jackhammer up and goes for a Russian legsweep, but receives an elbow to the face. Jackhammer follows up that scientific move by hitting Juno with a big boot to the face, and covers Juno for a two-count. Jackhammer pulls Juno up by his hair, but gets a fist to the midsection and is downed with a DDT. Juno once again gets a two-count, before deciding to go for a submission move on the big man. He hooks Jackhammer's legs for a Boston Crab, but Jackhammer manages to kick him into the corner. Jackhammer charges Juno, and once again is unsuccessful at hitting a maneuver, as Juno gets both feet up and wraps them around Jackhammer's head, most likely in a hurricanrana attempt. However, Jackhammer counters it by turning it into a jumping powerbomb. Jackhammer falls backwards after performing the maneuver, and accidentally tags in Mike Heftel. Heftel jumps up and down on the apron, before he climbs to the top rope, not noticing that Juno has crawled over and made the tag to Kobi. Heftel leaps off, and bellyflops right onto the mat, as Kobi now climbs to the top. Once Heftel gets to his feet, Kobi leaps off and nails him with a spinning heel kick. Heftel totally oversells the move and ends up on his feet, but isn't there for long, as Kobi sends him to the outside with a superkick. Jackhammer rushes into the ring, grabs an unsuspecting Kobi by the neck with both hands, and heaves him across the squared circle. Juno takes advantage of the pre-occupied Jackhammer, leaping up to the top, and nailing him with a missle dropkick. Both Laws begin to double-team Jackhammer, until two men make their way through the crowd and get into the ring. Those two men are Waru and Barry Bladerth. Those two men both receive roundhouse kicks once they reach the ring. Juno and Kobi take a breather in one corner, as Heftel gets back in the ring, and stands in another corner with Jackhammer, Waru, and Bladerth. All four men are ready to attack the Laws...until six men run down the ramp and get in their way. Sphere... La Parka... Mr. T... Matt Douglas (?)... Destroyer... Famine. These six men are ready to defend the Law brothers against Heftel, Jackhammer, Waru, and Bladerth...or so it seems. All six men slowly turn to face the Laws. All ten men charge at the Laws. Juno backdrops Famine out of the ring, while Kobi does the same to Destroyer. Juno lays out Waru with a Manhattan Drop, while Kobi grabs Matt by the hair and tosses him over the top rope and onto the floor. Heftel runs over to a corner in an attempt to hide from the attack, as Sphere and La Parka are dispatched to the outside with dropkicks. Kobi hits a reverse DDT on Bladerth, and then helps his brother send Jackhammer over the top with a double-clothesline. Heftel rushes out of the corner, right at Juno, and is taken down with a simple shove. Both Juno and Kobi climb the turnbuckle in opposite corners of the ring, then Juno leaps off and nails the Just Fierce, while, at the same time, Kobi hits the Heat. Kobi covers Heftel and gets an easy three-count, giving the win to him and his brother. Heftel, Jackhammer, and everyone else on the outside of the ring regroup, as Slaughter goes elsewhere... Result: East Coast Laws by pinfall Gone "I understand, but really John, this isn't a good time." Chris Universal was looking at John Willis with dismay. Willis looked beat... dark circles underlined his eyes. With the eyes themselves there was an expression of haunted horror, from within the eyes John was far away, his mind still reeling from something he hoped was all a dream. He had something held in his hand that Universal suspected as a dog collar, and was horrified at the prospect of finding out that he was correct. Willis looked up at Chris Universal, and Universal knew at once that he was wasting his breath. A security guard needed some amount of inner confidence, and Willis was for the moment completely defeated. For other jobs it would be a near impossible obstacle to overcome, but for a security guard... Universal sighed, aware that John had never responded to what he said. "I understand. Fine John, I understand. Listen, I'm not going to accept a resignation... you take some time, with pay of course. You get yourself... well, heal yourself John. I know it didn't seem like it last week, but this is a family here and you're always welcome back. Understand John? You're always welcome back." John looked at Chris, his mouth opening and closing silently. Finally he just turned and walked out the door, not a word spoken since he handed Chris his request for a leave of absence. Chris was still holding it as he watched him go, hoping that this wasn't the last he had seen of him. It wasn't good... John was one of the few members of the auxiliary staff that had been under long term contract and wasn't in danger of vanishing with all of the other staff that had recently departed. John knew the people here, and by and large the roster respected him. But now... "Universal... Universal... what happened to my match?" Universal turned and saw Hellrazor coming up to him, Lamont slung in the crook of one arm and for a moment was confused by the question. Then his eyes focused as his mind turned from one bad situation to another. "I don't know... you know I don't have the book right now. Those three egomaniacs are controlling that right now Hellrazor. You're going to have to take it up with them... and they seem to be booking at the last minute and with whatever whims cross their mind." Hellrazor frowned and started to say something, but slowly became aware that Universal looked ashen, and he turned just in time to see Willis push through an exit door. Hellrazor frowned. "He's not leaving is he?" "I don't know." Universal sighed. "He's still under contract... but he's got some real problems after what that psychotic did last week." Hellrazor looked momentarily confused. "Psychotic?" "You don't know?" Universal blinked. "Neo... last week?" Hellrazor's eyes narrowed. "What did that hillbilly do?" He lightly patted Lamont's head, but his body language was tense and upset. "Neo... well, he kidnapped John and forced him into this bizarre... I can't exactly... well, you know John had a puppy..." Hellrazor nodded, but his eyes were even darker now. "Flint. Yes, what happened?" Universal was uncomfortable, aware that both Hellrazor and Lamont were staring at him. He gulped, then looked away. "He killed John's dog... snapped... well, but you get the idea. John's taking some time, can't say I blame him. I hope he comes back of course... and I'll leave the door open." Hellrazor was silent, the hand petting Lamont still. His eyes were narrowed into slits, and dark. Slowly he nodded, although the movement of his head was nearly indistinguishable. "I understand." Silently, Hellrazor turned and walked away, Universal watching him go with trepidation. Things were out of control, and this recruitment guy hadn't shown up yet. Touma his name was... and Universal was nervously aware that a lot of his hopes currently rested on someone he had never met. As these concerns overwhelmed him he quickly forgot about Hellrazor and his reaction to the news of Neo's actions. Touma Is Here! "Just a few months... a few months of working here and I'll finally be really to take those exams and get into the University. For it is then that I, Touma, rising star of the Eastern infusion of Western culture will achieve his natural destiny of getting into college and being successful! Up until now I, Touma, have been denied my natural destiny of getting into a first rate Tokyo college and dating college girls. Eight long years of failure on the exams, eight long years stuck in a prep school, but with this talent coordinator job I will finally get the skills I need to pass those exams! Finally I will be able to date college girls! This I swear!" Touma raised his fist into the air for nobody in particular. A maniac smile creased his face, determination blazing in his eyes. His backstage pass was slung off his belt, and it swung dramatically. "Ah!" Touma's face broke into a wide lecherous grin. "And with this position I may get a chance to date wonderful wrestling women! Nyah ha ha ha ha!" Passersby looked at the young Asian man with slight interest, blinking at his hunched stature and reddening face. He appeared to be in a world of his own, his mind racing with potential fantasies and ideas. Touma was once a young and slightly promising Tokyo student. Raised in the country outside of Tokyo, Touma left home with dreams of attending the top Japanese universities. Touma's skills were not at the same level of his dreams however, and a dismal performance on his entrance exams sent Touma to one of the many shameful prep schools. Touma wanted to be successful, but one factor or another continued to block his abilities to move ahead in his academic career. Eight years later Touma found himself no closer to his goal of getting into a top University than he was the first day he failed his entrance exams. Touma got into working as a talent coordinator during these long years of disappointment, and despite being a natural at this profession his dream of going to a good University eclipsed everything else. Now he was back in the states, hired just a few days ago to be the talent coordinator for the Fans Wrestling Organization. He approached the job with gusto, determined that this time it would lead to the experience he needed to finally pass those exams and bring honor to his name. But if along the way there was a chance to date a few girls... well... "Touma, you forgot your dinner!" Touma turned and spotted a young woman in large glasses coming over to him. She smiled happily, her eyes narrowed as she beamed at him. She carried a small box in her arms and was gesturing it toward Touma. "Urara, not now! I've got to meet with the president of this organization and that means that I've got to stay... focused." Down the hallway Tempest walked by them, and Touma's face reddened as his mouth broke into a wide smile. "Wrestling girls... wrestling girls... nyah ha ha ha..." Urara glared at Touma, folding up her glasses and placed them into her jacket pocket. This was hardly new behavior. Since coming to Tokyo she had become aware that Touma's roving eyes for women were impossible to prevent. Touma didn't understand why his dreams of getting into a top University were continually dashed, but if you had asked Urara she could have identified the problem in one. Women. Touma's priorities had always been skewed toward his lecherous behavior, which was more often than not unproductive and one-sided. Still... Urara was convinced that eventually Touma would come to realize that what he really needed from romance was right in front of him. She knew that her love for him would eventually be returned... but Touma needed a little bit of education first. She had stuck with him for these long eight years. Through failure after failure she had continued to stick by him, putting her own career on hold so she could stay with him no matter where he went. She was a smart girl, and knew that she could have already entered and graduated from the top University in Japan twice over by now... but sometimes you have to place your heart before your ambitions. Sighing, Urara placed the boxed dinner back in her bag and grasped Touma's hand. He was still looking after Tempest, and Urara jerked his arm hard to get his attention put back on her and the job at hand. "Touma, we've got to go meet the president. Now come on, Chris Universal is down the hall this way." With a last glance and a sigh, Touma relented and followed Urara down the hallway toward Chris Universal's office. Determination! Determination! Getting through this job would ensure a success on his exams... and if there were some wrestling girls that he could date along the way, well... all the better. With a smile, Touma went to meet the president, Urara as always closely behind him. The Alliance? Slaughter went backstage and found Black Quicksilver in Sphere's "office", admiring a framed photo of Kate Beckinsale. The door swung open, and in walked Sphere, who was furious after the beating he received earlier. He spotted BQ looking at the picture on the wall. "You miserable little toad!", shouted Sphere. "What ah you doing in my office?" "Didn't you read the opening sentence? I'm admiring this framed photo of Kate Beckinsale," answered BQ. "I can SEE that! What's I'm trying to say is that you should have been out there, helping us with our attempted takeover! After all, it was your idea to form the Alliance!" "Hey, I got better things to do tonight. Oh, and I got another match with Chronos, too. It's for the World Title this time." Sphere wasn't pleased with this response. "Oh, is that so? Well, as the commissioner of the fWo, I'm punishing you by announcing that your Wominternopean Jobberweight Title will not be on the line in that match tonight. That ought to damage what little pride you have left, sunshine!" BQ laughed it off. "Pride? Ha! I suck, remember?" "Oh, yes, go ahead and laugh you bloody tart. I just hope you're happy about what happened out there tonight! If you were smart, you'd stop masterminding these invasion attempts. Mr. Bladerth, a dear, close, personal fried of mine, suffered his fourth career-ending injury!" BQ tried to think of the perfect response. "Uh, well... live and learn... or something." Sphere continued. "And Waru... I do believe he was deemed incapacitated even before he set foot in the ring! And as for... ", but Sphere was cut off as Matt Douglas, also known as Wolf Fang during his days spent in the fWo, ran into the room, began to shout "RUAHH~!", and dove toward BQ, but instead crashed into a statue of Hugh Grant. Sphere picked up where he left off. "As I was just about to say, what is the bloody deal with this young gentleman? He wasn't part of the Alliance's plan... he just ran to the ring with us when we passed by him in the hallway! I haven't a clue as to what rock he climbed out from under!" BQ offered his opinion on the matter. "Yeah... he didn't even come close to hitting me there... and I didn't even move out of the way! It's like he's on the show, a one-time deal for those of you wondering, for the sole purpose of a cheap parody. Remember, according to internet reports, he was fired by myself and John Reese." Sphere chimed in. "Why, yes, I also remember hearing that you fired Mr. Reese shortly thereafter." "Hey, what's with you British guys? You know, with your little 'Why, yes' and 'I do believe' stuff?", BQ asked of Sphere. Sphere shot back with "Oh... oh, like you filthy Americans are any better! You know, with your 'Let me tell you something!' and 'I just want to say this' garbage! It makes me sick! Now, get out of my office, and just remember who's responsible for giving you that title shot tonight!" Slaughter went elsewhere before BQ could make an obligatory "kayfabe" joke. Monte Burns vs. Donaven Winters The sounds of Trent Reznor welcomed us back from the commercial break, as 'Somewhat Damaged' by Nine Inch Nails echoed throughout the arena. 'The Fallen Angel' Donaven Winters walked out onto the stage; his meager outlook on life emphasized by the expression on his face. Winters began walking to the squared circle, when a backstage worker made his way out from behind. Winters nearly plastered him for catching him offguard, but instead his attention was focused on what the worker had presented him with: A bouquet of dead flowers. Roses, to be specific. Donaven inspected the note closely, and his hands shook as he read the note. Seemingly, what was written turned him from a more somber mood... to more of an angered focus. Winters grabbed the note and stuffed it inside the bouquet of flowers, before tossing them aside. Someone was trying to screw with his mind... and with his mind as fragile as it was, that wasn't a good thing. Winters slid into the ring and tried to focus at what was at hand -- the second match in his best four-out-of-seven series with the fWo Cruiserweight Champion, 'Monte Burns'. Right on cue, 'Nobody's Real' by PM5K started up, and Burns was greeted with jeers and boos from the crowd. Burns grinned as he stammered out onto the stage. Standing with sheer overconfidence, Burns began walking down to the ring. He started jawing with a fan at ringside, which took his attention away from the match. This allowed Winters to plot a sneak attack, and attack he did. He leaped over the top rope with a somersault plancha, which took Burns out in a big way. From there, he grabbed the bouquet of flowers and waited for Burns to get up, before feeding them literally to Burns. Burns coughed them up as pure hatred for Burns continued to drive Winters. 'The Fallen Angel' rolled 'The Golden Child' into the ring, and the referee immediately signalled for the bell. Burns started to get up and Winters charged, spearing him into the mat. Winters went for a cover, but Burns kicked out at two. With the title still wrapped around Burns' waist, Winters grabbed it and snatched it from the champion. This match was not for the title, but was very important to the series the two men had agreed to. Winters already had an advantage, and was up one win to zero... and if Winters won again tonight, Burns might not have a chance of recovering. History proven that it was hard to recover from a two-nothing disadvantage in a best of seven series.. but it was not impossible. Winters had the title as Burns began to get up, and disregarding the fact it would disqualify him, Winters went to take Burns' head off with the Cruiserweight Title. As he swung, the referee stepped in and snatched the title away from the Detroit native. Winters scowled at the official, his expression showing nothing but anger for the zebra. This allowed Burns to get an advantage, and he snucked up on Winters with a low-blow from behind, before spinning him around and planting him into the canvas with a double-arm DDT. Burns went for a cover, but Winters kicked out at two. Bringing Winters up by his blackened hair, Burns swung him to the ropes with an Irish Whip. When Winters returned, Burns went for a clothesline... but ultimately, Donaven ducked that. When both men came face to face again this time Burns hit his intended target with a spinning heel kick. Burns waited for Winters to get back to a vertical base, and when he did he kicked him in the midsection, before attempting a reverse neckbreaker. Winters however countered out of it and hit a DDT, and now both men laid on the mat, resting from the series of exchanges. The referee slowly began counting to ten, but it didn't last long. Winters struggled to his feet, and began walking to the top rope. As he began climbing up them while facing the crowd, Burns began to get to his feet. Winters stopped as he stood on the top rope, looking at the fans with hatred & disdain. From behind, Burns staggered and hit the ropes, which crotched his opponent in the corner. Burns began climbing up the ropes from behind and softened Winters up in the back with several punches and forearms. He hooked him for a belly-to-back suplex, but Winters positioned himself so Burns fell to the mat with Winters atop him. Winters hooked the leg, but at two, Burns' free leg got to the bottom rope. Frustrated, Winters brought Burns up and sent him to the ropes... when Burns returned he caught Winters' foot; as Winters attempted a kick. Burns spun him around and when Winters completed the 360 turn, Burns went for a clothesline. Winters ducked it, and when Burns hit off the ropes he came back and was taken down by a spinning heel kick from Winters. Bringing Burns up to his feet, Winters began going for the Shattered Society, also known as the "Yamamoto Special '78", but to be technical, a reverse death valley driver. Instead, Burns escaped and then turned Winters around and took him out with a double-underhook suplex into a backbreaker. A cover garnered only a two count, and Burns decided to ascend to the top rope. Rather than go for the Establishment, instead he went for a top rope elbow, but Winters moved out of the way. Winters decided to take a high risk of his own, and went for a standing moonsault, but Burns got his knees up just in time. With Winters bent over, Monte scoopslammed him to the mat, before charging towards the ropes and leaping off with an Asai moonsault. Donaven returned the favor as he got his knees up in time, before Donaven hooked him and took him out with the spinning Kudoh driver, also known as 'The Waste'. Donaven covered Burns, but Monte kicked out at two. A frustrated Winters looked to take a high risk again, hoping this time it would pay off. He went to the top rope and descended with a shooting star press, but Monte moved out of the way, and Donaven crashed to the canvas. Both men struggled back to their feet with aid of the ropes, before Donaven charged at Burns. Burns ducked the attempted elbow, and from behind he leaped up onto Winters' shoulder and then took him down with a victory roll. For added leverage he held onto the nearby ropes, of which the official did not see. The referee counted to three, and the series was now tied at one win apiece. Looking to escape quickly, Burns retrieved the title and headed to the backstage area, while Donaven remained in the ring complaining to the referee. Feeling that the official was against him just like society was, Donaven kicked him in the stomach, before wiping him out with the Broken Dream. Winters stood over the referee, feeling some pride in what he had done... before he slid out onto the floor and headed to the back... looking for Monte. Meanwhile, it could be assumed that the referee Winters injured wouldn't be seen for awhile... while Slaughter cut to a commercial break. Result: Monte Burns by pinfall (Series Tied 1-1) Calling Out Slaughter returned from commercial to the sounds of "Christian Says" playing over the arena sound system, and nearly being drowned out by the thunderous boos of the crowd. Ric Chronos stepped out, flanked by his fellow members of 3vil, the Flying Frenchie and Primetime. The dastardly, evil, evil, eeeevil threesome made their way to the ring and stepped in, basking in the boos of the crowd. Chronos made one gesture, then another, then finally gestured for a microphone, which was tossed to him. "I'm gonna make this short and sweet. Eddy Fate, get your pansy little ass out here right the hell NOW!" Seconds passed, then "Disconnect" began to play. The fans cheered as Eddy Fate appeared on the entrance ramp with a microphone. "Ric, you old blighter! What in the bloody hell do you want?" "Oh, you know perfectly well what I want, Eddy. That little stunt you pulled... where do you get off pulling shit like that?" "Where do I get off pulling shit like that? Well, let's see... looks like Cedar Rapids, Iowa to me, how about you?" Chronos growled and fumed as the crowd chuckled. "Really funny, asshole. You know what? You disgust me. You're not even a man. You know you can't touch me, so you resort to stupid little pranks." "I can't touch you? I can't touch you?! Please. I would beat your bloody ass all over this arena, you wanker, and you know. In fact, if you've got the danglies, how about we dance tonight for the title?" All three faces of 3vil wore bemused looks at this suggestion. Chronos just laughed. "You? A title shot? Puh-leeze. You haven't done jack to get a title shot and you know it. But, I'll tell you what. I'm feeling generous. You defeat somebody of my choosing, and you'll get a shot... at Halloween Hell." "Fine by me. Who's your man?" "You know him very well... the Black Panther." "Let the bodies hit the... FLOOR! POWERBOMB!" "Hey, th-that's not Panther's music," stuttered Chronos, thrown off-guard. Erik Kelly stomped out to cheers, causing Fate to move to safer ground a bit closer to the ring. A microphone was in Kelly's hands. "Now hold on just a damned minute... as far as I knew, I was the number one contender." The arena went dark. Fog rolled in along the entrance ramp, and knowing who was coming out, Kelly took a few defensive steps back. The Boston Strangler appeared out of the fog like an apparition and spoke six simple words as the lights came back on. "We... are the number one contenders." "Hey, I respect what you've done, man, but Ric and his wankers have the book... if they say I can be the number one contender then that's the way it is, mate." "No way. I won Unstable, in case you missed it. You two would know, since neither of you even made it to the final match." "Perhaps a compromise can be reached. Let us not forget last week. Should Mister Fate win his match... it seems to us that we all have a legitimate claim. Perhaps... a four-way match?" Primetime took the mic. "Don't do it, Ric." Frenchie nodded. "Zey're baiting you." Chronos took back the microphone, looked down... then looked back up. "You got it." The crowd went nuts, and Chronos' 3vil teammates grimaced. Primetime took the microphone from Chronos. "Alright Ric... if that's the way you want it... then we'll back you. In fact, tell ya what. We'll soften them up tonight. Me and Frenchie against Strangler and Kelly." "I like zat plan." "So there you have it guys... Fate against Panther, Brian and Pierre against Strangler and Kelly, a four-way match if Fate... heh... wins. Good luck... you're gonna need it." Chronos threw the mic aside as "Christian Says" resumed, and Slaughter moved on... Arrival Boots out of car and Neo stepped out into the twilight. Sounds from inside proved that the show was already running, but this was of little importance to him. As he walked toward the arena entrance he noticed camera men and production staff moving out of his way to give him a wide berth as he passed. This was good, and it meant that his little example the week before had the impact he had intended. "Well I'll be a son of a bitch..." Neo stopped in his tracks as a smirk passed over his face. Since returning to the fWo he hadn't seen many familiar faces. Wrestlers were either actively avoiding him or just weren't around. The old days had wrestlers all over the hallways. Chatting, complaining... but the fWo he had returned to found more wrestlers keeping to themselves in locker rooms. It was a surprise then that the first person he ran into on this night that he knew was not only a wrestler, but one of the more pathetic examples of the previous cartoonish decade. "Roberts. You miserable sack of shit." Stevie looked up from a pile of papers and his eyes narrowed as they rested on Neo. "Oh. You. Hello." "What?" Neo smirked and let the bag he was carrying slide down his arm until it was resting on the floor. "Just hello? No, 'hello baby... shagarific'? What's the matter Roberts, the sappy Austin Powers rip off no longer happy that he can't go out and play with the Steve Ausin clone and the baseball man? All your little superpower wrestling friends retired and gone home?" Stevie let the Survivor applications drop and he pointed at Neo. "Listen here you sadistic freak... a gimmick is a way to live in this business. You can't...." Neo took a quick swipe at Stevie, knocking the papers out of his hand and scattering them on the floor. He shoved his shoulder forward, driving Stevie back into the wall behind him with a solid thud and moved his head in within inches of Roberts'. "A way to live? You pathetic piece of shit... Neo never needed a gimmick and Neo did just fine. You don't have the balls to get some dignity in this sport then that's your problem. But don't you ever dare to tell me how this business works, 'Pestilence'. You want to go around and play like you're some kind of Hollywood star so it can get you hard enough to drive home and plow some fat trailer trash cow keep it out of my face. I'm not going to have some washed up never was get in my way this time... you understand? Stevie's lip had curled over his teeth, but he remained silent and still. Neo hadn't seen any other wrestlers, but he had passed plenty of temporary staff. They seemed afraid of him, which was a good sign considering most of them hadn't come to the fWo until this week. Perhaps they had tuned into Slaughter last week to see what they were getting themselves into and discovered that some of the people in this organization didn't play with the kid gloves. Neo stopped for a moment, laughing to himself as he remembered the screams of John Willis. That pathetic piece of shit had been always sticking his nose in Neo's business when he was around the first time. Neo had meant to teach him a lesson, but had gotten tired of the politics and bullshit back then. Back then it was all about a few bitches whining to get ahead, their tired thread worn characters about as stupid as it came. Thankfully the worst offenders had gone, retired, or otherwise gotten the hell out of dodge. The fWo was all about opportunity now, and Neo would take that opportunity and... A cat was looking at him about twenty feet ahead. It was standing in front of an open door, the tail lightly swishing back and forth in the air as it regarded him. The cat appeared to be studying Neo carefully, and Neo felt his momentary good mood slipping away. If there was one thing he hated more than yapping, drooling little mutts it was stuck up ratty cats. Shit creatures, all of them. God had put them low on the earth so they were easily booted into a wall, back snapping and blood vomiting out of their hairy mouths as he cleared the miserable creatures out of his path. Neo advanced, as the cat stared at him. The cat wasn't moving, and Neo's lips curled into a grin that showed yellow teeth and bloody gums. The cat's tail continued swishing back and forth, and seemed no more alarmed that Neo was advancing on it than of discovering a plate of fish. Neo's foot swung back, the boot poised to strike as he came within distance of Lamont. Seconds before contact, Lamont's head turned to the open door to the right and blinked at Hellrazor. Neo followed Lamont's movement and his eyes went wide. The shovel swung down, and Hellrazor turned it so that the flat steel cracked into Neo's head with a bloody thud. Neo stumbled backward, stars suddenly flooding over his vision. He raised a hand to his head, but before he could scream his surprise Hellrazor swung the shovel in an arc that caught Neo in the face, driving his nose backward with an audible snap. Neo felt a gush of warm blood pour down his front, the coppery taste flooding his mouth. Neo fell backward, slamming down onto tiles. He was still too surprised to scream out in pain, and reaching out he felt his hands slide over blood soaked tile. How could so much come out of him so quickly? Before he could ponder this question Hellrazor was on top of him again, this time punting his boot forward into Neo's groin. Bright fresh pain exploded inside of him, and this time Neo did scream as he felt the ice cold agony wash over him. Hellrazor turned to Lamont, and the two of them regarded Neo silently. Then Hellrazor raised the shovel again and spoke to his feline companion. "Are we finished here?" Lamont didn't respond, but instead turned his gaze back on Neo. Neo had slipped into unconsciousness, the pain forcing his mind to shut down in a faint. Hellrazor recognized this, and turned back to Lamont. "He's out now, he wouldn't be able to feel anything else." Lamont looked up at Hellrazor, then sniffed at the air. Hellrazor nodded. "You're right... he won't feel it now." Hellrazor raised the shovel over his head. "But he sure as hell will when he wakes up. You don't screw with another man's pet, hillbilly." From a distance heads turned as they heard the wet thuds of shovel against skin, but everyone was smart enough to stay as far away as possible. Ric Chronos (c) vs. Black Quicksilver Slaughter heads to the ring for its main event, a possibly historical rematch. On one side, Ric Chronos. A dominating champion who has held onto the belt for over a month and counting. A three-time World Champion. A member of perhaps the most evil and devastating stable in the history of the fans Wrestling organization. A true legend in the making. On the other side, Black Quicksilver. A... well, he has a really cool mask. They collide... now. "Slave To The Grind" plays, and the fans pop big as Black Quicksilver makes his way to the ring with STUMP. BQ sets STUMP aside and hops up onto the apron and vaults into the ring. He does a few stretches as he waits for his opponent, then begins to joke around with some ringside fans who apparently actually think he has a shot at winning. "Christian Says" hits, and the cheers turn to thunderous boos as Ric Chronos makes his way to the ring. The fWo World Title belt is strapped around his waist, and notably absent are his 3vil buddies. Chronos climbs up the ringsteps and steps into the ring, then shoots a disdainful look at BQ. He then unstraps the World Title and hands it to the referee, who raises it high in the air to signify that it is on the line. Chronos smirks at that certainly it's only a formality considering who he's facing then turns to BQ as the bell rings. Chronos stretches out his hand for a shake. BQ looks at it thoughtfully, then turns to the fans, who urge him not to do it. Finally, BQ shrugs, turns back to Chronos, and kicks him really freaking hard in the head. Chronos is stunned and driven to the mat, and the fans go nuts. BQ makes the cover, but Chronos kicks out at one. He quickly gets to his feet and goes after BQ with a clothesline, but BQ leapfrogs him. Chronos comes off the ropes, and BQ goes for an inverted monkey flip. Chronos counters by grabbing BQ's legs and lifting him up into the air, apparently setting him up for a reverse powerbomb. BQ counters by rolling forward into a variation of a victory roll that scores a two-count before Chronos kicks out. Both men get back up again, and Chronos goes for another charging clothesline only it's a bluff. BQ takes the bait and leapfrogs, then eats a rock-solid forearm in the mouth as he lands. Chronos brings BQ up and whips him hard into the corner, then charges in himself with a hard knee to the gut. He backs up, then charges again with a clothesline. With BQ already woozy in the corner, Chronos takes a few steps back once more, measures BQ, and charges in. This time, BQ uses the ropes to prop himself up and roll Chronos up in a sunset flip. Chronos just manages to get a shoulder up before the three-count. As they get up, Chronos pounces on BQ with a version of a Thesz press and, clearly rattled and pissed off, begins absolutely wailing away at BQ with fists to the face. His rage finally satisfied, Chronos lets up and brings BQ up to his feet. The No-DQ stip coming to mind, he throws BQ through the second rope to the floor. As BQ gets up, Chronos nails him with a baseball slide, knocking him to the floor. Chronos rolls outside and brings BQ up, then whips him hard into the guardrail. BQ stumbles out, and Chronos whips him across into the opposite guardrail. BQ staggers out, and Chronos kicks him hard in the gut, doubling him over, then spikes him into the floor with a DDT. With BQ down and out, Chronos begins pulling aside one of the protective mats that cover the hard concrete floor. With it aside, he brings BQ up again and sets him between his legs. He then crosses his ankles, setting up a Figure-Four DDT. He goes for it, but to the amazement of all, BQ counters by backdropping Chronos onto the cold concrete. BQ then, with a burst of energy, leaps onto the guardrail and leaps off with an asai moonsault that nails Chronos squarely. However, he stays down for a few moments, clearly feeling the effects of his beating at the hands of Chronos. Finally, he shakes it off and brings Chronos up to his feet. BQ grabs a chair and approaches Chronos, then tosses it to him with a shout of "Here, hold this!" Chronos shakes his head. "No... you take it." Before BQ can kick it into his face, Chronos hurls the chair at BQ, and it strikes him in the head with considerable force. BQ spins in place a bit, then tumbles to the ground like a bowling ball. Chronos brings BQ up and takes him over to the Australian announce table, where Mick and Donk erupt with cries of protest. Chronos throws BQ onto the table, then climbs up to join him. He hooks BQ's head, perhaps looking to DDT him through the table, but BQ counters with a gutshot. He then hooks Chronos' head and drives him through the table with a Cubic Zirconium Cutter. The table shatters, sending debris and shrapnel everywhere. The wrestlers remain in the rubble for a good period of time, only slowly beginning to revive. BQ is up first, logically, and he helps Chronos to his feet. Chronos counters by nailing BQ below the belt to stop him cold. He then nails him below the belt again just to be cruel. He then nails him below the belt a third time, just to ensure that the fWo will never be cursed with BQ's offspring. Chronos straightens up and throws BQ into the ring, then follows him in. He then applies the Hourglass, a dragon sleeper, or as James Armstrong repeatedly has referred to it, a "sweaty armpit submission". BQ squirms to escape the hold, but it's no use. BQ hooks the leg, and the ref drops down to make the cover as the crowd goes nuts. One. Two. ... Primetime and The Flying Frenchie pull the referee from the ring. The Five Seasons Center explodes with boos at this blatant but legal cheating, but their boos quickly turn to cheers again as Black Quicksilver takes out both Primetime and Frenchie with a somersault plancha. The ref manages to dodge in time, and now both PT and Frenchie are out. BQ rolls back into the ring and makes the cover. One. Two. Kickout. The rest allows Chronos to get a shoulder up, and the crowd groans in frustration. Not yet done, BQ has a brainstorm. He retrieves STUMP from where it has sat on the apron all match. BQ crouches, STUMP in hand, waiting for Chronos. Chronos slowly gets to his feet... staggers... turns around to face BQ... And Quicksilver blasts him with STUMP. The crowd erupts and begins to chant. "BQ! BQ! BQ! BQ!" BQ makes the cover. Hooks the leg. The ref drops down to count. One. Two.
