Sunday Slaughter, 25 June 2000

25 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The fWo World Title. To date, only thirteen men have reached the mountaintop and can say they have held the title. The latest, The Boston Strangler, who won it after holding just one other title in his career, the fWo Internet Title. Thirteen men... and at SummerSlaughter, someone else will be determined to become number fourteen. To many, it is the pinnacle, the highest achievement you can possibly have in professional wrestling. To others, it's something within their grasp, yet alluding them every time they become one moment... one second... away from greatness. To some, it is something they hope to achieve someday in their career, yet quite frequently, that day seems all too far away for them to envision. Beginning tonight, that day will be closer than it ever has been for many, yet to others, it will be something within their grasp and unfortunately allude them when they are close. And to some, it's their only opportunity in the forseeable future to reach that very pinnacle. Tonight, sixteen men will try to overcome the impossible odds of receiving an fWo World Title shot. Sixteen men will walk into the tournament, and one will walk out on the next level. The path to the fWo World Title just a mere twenty four days away, as they receive their shot at the next big pay-per-view, SummerSlaughter. That night will be their night, their time, their opportunity to change the landscape of wrestling forever. Who would think that Mike Heftel or Hellrazor would get that opportunity? Who would have ever thought that so short in the tenure of the careers of Ric Chronos, Crusader, Erik Kelly or Neo that they would have that opportunity? And then you have to consider the others, who have been in the fWo in some form since day one... guys like Wretch Mike Heftel & Mitch Wilson, and to a certain degree the fWo Hardcore Champion Xavier... this is their chance. This is their time. This... is the next level. Mitch Wilson (c) vs. Mike Heftel The sounds of Scatterbrain's "Down with the Ship" allow the crowd to groove a little, and Mike Heftel makes his way down to ringside. He is actually met with a response that is admirable, and he returns the favor, saluting the crowd as he climbs into the ring. He warms up as "Calling All Stations" from Genesis hits the PA system, and the crowd boos the song as much as they boo the man who deems this a classic, Mitch Wilson. Wilson, the new Internet Champion, is making his way down toward the ring, as he looks at Heftel. "Mr. Main Event" laughs at his opponent, sensing that this won't be long. Wilson climbs into the ring, and stares Heftel down, as the bell rings, starting the match. Heftel and Wilson lock up, with Wilson turning it into an armbar. Heftel reverses it, and Wilson flips out of the reversal. Heftel follows that with a legsweep, sending Wilson to the mat. Heftel immediately locks Wilson into a headlock. Wilson starts to use his power to get up, and, when he gets to his feet, the Internet champ brings Heftel toward the ropes. Wilson whips Heftel across the ring...when Heftel turns into the ropes, he sees that Wilson is lowering his head for a backdrop. Heftel uses the almighty sunset flip, catching Wilson off-guard. At the count of two, Wilson kicks out. The two get up, and Wilson charges Heftel. Heftel gets out of Wilson's way, and throws him into a turnbuckle. Wilson runs face-first into the corner; he comes out woozy and backwards. Heftel sees this and runs toward the ropes. He uses his momentum to hit Wilson with a flying bulldog. Another cover, and the ref counts. At two-and-seven-eighths, Wilson kicks out. Mike Heftel just came within an eyelash of winning the Internet Championship...something that is still very much a possibility. Heftel picks up Wilson, and whips him toward the ropes. Wilson hits the ropes, and sees Heftel lowering HIS head a little too quickly; Wilson senses that Heftel did not learn from Wilson's mental lapse. Wilson grabs Heftel around the neck and hits a swinging neckbreaker. Wilson picks up Heftel and whips him into the ropes. Wilson catches Heftel upon his return and hits a powerslam. Now, Wilson has the upper hand. Wilson picks up Heftel again; this time he nails him with a gutwrench superplex. Wilson now senses the end. He picks up Heftel and sets him up for the Headliner. He locks him in, and hits the inverted russian legsweep. He covers Heftel, and the ref counts the three. Mitch Wilson moves on in the Next Level tournament, and he lets the crowd know it (as well as the "fact" that he's gonna take the whole thing). Meanwhile, Slaughter heads to a commercial break. Result: Mitch Wilson by pinfall to advance in the Next Level Tournament Conversations, Part One The sunglasses covered his eyes as he pulled his Dodge Viper into the Kohl Center in Madison Wisconsin. "What a foul place. Wisconsin? You've gotta be nuts to want to live here... especially in Madison. What a hell hole." The World's Greatest Athlete, Scott Slugger, stepped out of his vehicle and looked outside the portal of which he just entered through. Thousands of fans who couldn't buy a ticket to get into the ring shouted his name loudly. Slugger acknowledged them, before beginning to walk further into the the Kohl Center. As Slugger did this, he removed his sunglasses and slipped them inside his leather jacket pocket, before approaching the office of the owner of the fans Wrestling organization, Chris Finn. He knocked on the door several times, before Finn finally opens it. Finn says hello, but Slugger doesn't want any part of small talk. "You want to know why your ratings are dropping, Finn? It's because you've been allowing stuff like what happened last night constantly, and it's getting pretty damn old. I'm sick of the shenanigans, I'm sick and tired of being sick and tired of being screwed. You know, and I know, just like everyone else knows that if I hit the Grand Slam last night, you're looking at the five-time World Champion." Finn tries to work around it. "But..." Slugger doesn't care what Finn has to say. "No buts. I take it you've already decided that Ice will be the number one contender, Finn?" "Well... yeah... he did win at Warfare..." Slugger thinks this is a very bad thing, and thus gets ready to position himself. "And then last night he had the audacity to prevent myself or Strangler from coming out on top cleaning. That doesn't sit well with the WGA, Finn. That doesn't sit well with the money-making machine that you've built your company around. Thus, I have a suggestion. No, I have a demand. I demand that you give me a chance at redemption so to speak, and let me face Ice here tonight. Yes, I know we've faced each other a thousand times, and the last thing this world needs is another match that features Scott Slugger kicking Ice' ass all around the ring. But, it has to happen, Finn simply because the WGA has a plan. Last time, Ice put his career on the line if he couldn't defeat the WGA - he got lucky, but I put him on the shelf for three months. Now, tonight, I say we reverse roles. The WGA isn't going to go to the lengths of putting his career on the line because that's been overdone as of late. See for example, The Strangler, Dead, Scott Collins, Mick Foley, The Rock, so on and so forth - no, the WGA is going to be original. You put the WGA and Ice in the ring one on one tonight, and if somehow, someway, that nothing, that nobody, that pimple on the World's Greatest's Ass is able to defeat me, then I will never ask for a World Title shot again. But if the WGA beats him in the middle of that ring tonight, then it is I who will receive the fWo World Title shot, not him. Do we have a deal, Finn?" Finn contemplated the offer for a few minutes, before agreeing to the deal. Slugger forced Finn to shake hands with him to seal the deal, before telling him that Ice was about to take a ride on the Curveball Express. Slugger then left the office, while Finn told his assistant to make sure they had a live satelite feed from the hospital in Colorado were Neo was located up and ready for a live interview later on in the show. Ric Chronos vs. The Black Panther Sunday Slaughter and The Next Level tournament continued as "My Hero" by The Foo Fighters began to play. The fans rose to their feet in anticipation of The Black Panther. The tall skyscraper of a man filled with mystery and intrigue came out, and looking beyond the mask that covered his face you could ascertain that he was focused and determined to take his game back to the next level. Panther stepped over the top rope into the ring, and held his arms high above the air. The crowd reacted uncertainly, before his music stopped, and was replaced by "Christian Says", from Tones on Tail. The former fWo World Tag Team Champion Ric Chronos came out, looking somewhat dazed, appearing to have a hangover from last night's partying with his partner Fallen and Damnation. Nevertheless, he remained focused as he entered the ring. He did, with little emotion, but little did he know he was just prey. Panther charged at him and caught him offguard with a forearm, 'causing the referee to ring the bell. Panther then worked him over in the corner with forearms and punches, before bringing him down to a sitting position. The former fWo World Champion then worked him over with some knees to the neck, before bringing him up and snapmaring him to the mat. Panther then showed his agility for a big man with a sitting dropkick to the face. He quickly went for a cover, but Chronos kicked out at one and a half. He brought him up and whipped him to the turnbuckles, and a dazed Chronos then walked right into a knee to the midsection, followed by a swinging neckbreaker. Another cover, and another kickout. Panther took him into the corner with a side headlock, wore him down for a moment, and then shot out into the ring with a bulldog. He hooked the leg, but Chronos kicked out of the pinning predicament at two. Undaunted, the 1999 Wrestler of the Year brought Chronos up and attempted a hanging vertical suplex, but Chronos escaped and fell behind Panther. He grabbed him in a waistlock, but Panther then broke free with an elbow. He then charged and bounced off the ropes, returned for a lariat, which was ducked by the 'Man of the Hour'. Panther stopped, turned and was met with a knee, and then a single-arm DDT. Panther kicked out of the subsequent pinfall attempt by Chronos at two, but Chronos began to take over. He swung Panther into the ropes, and when Panther came back he was nailed with a flying forearm. This only staggered the six foot ten athlete, and Chronos followed it with a belly-to-belly suplex. Chronos then took a high risk maneuver that paid off, as he flew off with a flying elbowdrop. He attempted another pinfall, but Panther kicked out again. Chronos whipped Panther to the ropes, kick to the stomach, followed by a double-arm DDT. He then appeared ready to go for the Chronosphere. He brought Panther up, took him near the turnbuckles, went for it, but Panther blocked and set him back on the turnbuckles. Panther then went for his patented finisher, The Pounce, but Chronos fought off. Four or five punches later, and Panther stumbled back towards the ring, and crashed on the mat. Chronos then flew off with a frogsplash, hooked the leg, and got the cover to advance to the second round of the Next Level Tournament. Result: Ric Chronos by pinfall to advance to the 2nd Round of the Next L Wretch & Company Arrive More arrivals for Slaughter occured, as a trio of men entered the arena in style. Well, at least one of them did. Wearing unusual attire (in his case) consisting of nice slacks and a nice dress shirt, Wretch walked in beaming with confidence, fresh off his win over Lance Star last night at Critical Condition. He was accompanied by his lackey, Scot Hartt, and his lackey's lackey, The Mutha Fuckin' Abusah Of Yo Momma, or TMFAOYM for short. The three men finally came across the door of Chris Finn, and Wretch knocked slightly. Abusah was very impatient however, and began yelling incomprehendably. "LET US IN~! I WEEL ABOOS YO MOMMA IF U DUNT LET US IN!" Finally, the door opened. Chris Finn stood, his jar agape. "What the hell do you guys want?" Abusah was going to speak more, but Wretch cut him off. "Mr. Finn, your ratings have been dropping, correct? And you would like them to start rising again, right? Well, your solution is here. Forget Britney Spears, forget loading the shows with matches that look to be good on paper. You want your company to be saved? You want your ratings to come out from the garbage? You're looking at the solution: Big Wretchy, The Ratings... Saver." Finn rolled his eyes, then chuckled, before finally realizing that Wretch was serious. "You're... serious?" Wretch nodded. "Very, Finn. You see, you thought the solution was a big name celebrity. That didn't work. Then you thought loading the card on Warfare a few weeks back would work. It didn't work, but it didn't fail. The ratings stayed the same. Well, if the third time is the charm, Finn... this is the third time, and Big Wretchy is your charm. That's right. I'm just here to tell you that over the next few weeks... the ratings will go up, Big Wretchy will make the fWo economy rise out of the proverbial seller it's been in, and in the end you will be able to thank Big Wretchy... for saving... you ratings." Finn' mouth fell open, before Wretch and his lackey and his lackey's lackey walked away, with Abusah giving Finn a dirty look. Finn shrugged, before heading back into his dressing room. Erik Kelly vs. ??? Slaughter heads to the ring for a first round matchup in the Next Level tournament, which will feature Erik Kelly against a mystery opponent. A mighty cry of "POWERBOMB!" echoes through the arena as "Break Stuff" starts to play and Erik Kelly and Pamela Dee appear on the top of the entrance ramp. Kelly begins to make his way to the ring, but his music is suddenly cut off mid-lyric and the arena suddenly goes pitch-black. A crash of lightning and a crackle of thunder are heard, and the lights go back up ever so slightly. The thunder and lightning continue to echo through the arena as Kelly rolls into the ring, beginning to grow angry. Suddenly, a bright flash of lightning strikes right in the middle of the ring, followed by continued silence and darkness. And then... the lights go back up. Erik Kelly is spun around inside the ring and hammered on the face with a gigantic cinder block by none other than Rain. Rain brings the fallen Kelly up and positions him, before driving him into the mat with a Rainmaker. Rain brings the limp body of Kelly up again and repeats the Rainmaker, slamming Kelly hard into the mat. Rain brings Erik Kelly up a third time as Pamela Dee cringes at ringside, and again blasts him with a Rainmaker. The fans are in silent awe of the viciousness of the returning Rain, who places a foot upon the chest of Kelly. The intimidated referee makes the count, and Rain advances to the second round of the Next Level tournament. As the ref raises his hand, the lights again go dark and thunder rolls inside the arena. When the lights come back up, Rain is nowhere to be found and Pamela Dee is inside the ring, tending to Erik Kelly. Slaughter continues with the fans still shocked at the brutality of the returning Rain... Result: Rain by pinfall to advance to the 2nd Round of the Next Level To Conversations, Part Two Chris Finn sat in his office looking over a tall stack of papers. Finn wasn't happy at all tonight, probably because the main event didn't go off as planned last night at Critical Condition. Just as Finn couldn't become any less happy, one man who was certain to displease Finn entered the room... without even knocking. "Finn... just the pathetic loser I was looking for." Anger resided on Finn' face now. "What do you want, Eron?" Eron stood in front of the desk that Finn sat behind, looking down at the young owner of the fWo. "It's simple. Slugger gets his chance but I don't get mine? Ice didn't just screw Slugger or the fans last night, Finn. In case you didn't notice, he screwed me too. And so Slugger gets an opportunity to face Ice to determine who will get the title shot? That isn't fair, Finn... and you know it. So what I'm proposing is this... last week you defeated me on Slaughter, correct?" Finn smiled, perhaps the first time he had smiled since Friday, and nodded. "Well, I want a re-match with you... with a few stipulations. Number one, if I win... I'm promised a title shot as well, Finn. Number two... if I lose, I'll change my ways... and stop ruining your precious little shows and stop putting your major stars on the shelves with injuries. Look at it this way, if you don't give me the same chance, I'll sue." Finn looked at Eron in bewilderment and disgust. "On what grounds?" Eron smiled. "Discrimination." Finn considered it for a few moments, before accepting it. "Alright... Eron... you have a deal." Eron grinned evily, before telling Finn that not even the strongest breath mint would stop Eron from giving Finn a breath... of fresh death. Eron then stormed out of the office, before Finn asked a nearby assistant if they had that satelite feed for the Neo interview from the hospital ready. Primetime (c) vs. Big Wretchy, The Ratings Saver "Blind" echoed throughout the Kohl Center as the fans awaited on their edge of their seats for the arrival of the new fWo International Champion, Primetime. The member of the Future Foundation headed to the ring with the title around his waist, even though it didn't mean much to him, he was now one step closer to the 'grand slam', or to be more in-depth, the first man to hold every single championship the fWo has ever offered. Primetime was greeted with boos as he held the title above his head, the referee telling him the title would be on the line here tonight. Primetime handed over the belt as 'Paint it Black' by Gob signalled the entrance of Big Wretchy, The Ratings Saver. Wretch came out to a huge ovation of cheers, before telling them all that he would be the man to bring the fWo back to prominence. Wretch entered the ring and he and Primetime circled each other. The match began slowly, with both men hooking up in a collar and elbow tieup. Primetime went for a headlock, before Wretch whipped him into the ropes. Primetime returned and knocked Wretch down with a shoulder block. Primetime, waited for Wretch to get back up, before bouncing off the ropes and returning... he leapfrogs over Wretch, and returns and hops over Wretch, who had dropped down to the mat. Primetime returns once again, Wretch back to his feet, and Wretch attempts a hiptoss. Primetime counters into a hiptoss of his own. Wretch counters that, before Primetime counters that with another hiptoss... with both men close to the ropes, Wretch goes flying over the top rope onto the floor. Primetime gets a head of steam before baseball sliding out onto the floor, nailing Wretch with both feet, knocking Wretch back onto the commentators table. Primetime then goes out onto the ring apron and hits a asai moonsault on the floor. Primetime continues the attack, sending Wretch into the stairs with an Irish Whip. Wretch reverses it, however, and instead Primetime goes crashing into the stairs. Wretch recovers, and slams him head first into the mat. He then rolls him into the ring, and climbs in himself. Wretch whips Primetime into the ropes and takes him down with a back bodydrop. Both men spring to their feet and Wretch hits a standing dropkick. He then scoops up Primetime and hits a front forward slam. A cover, and a kickout. Wretch whips Primetime into the turnbuckles and follows up with a running elbow to the jaw, before scoop up Primetime and positioning a superplex. Instead, he goes for a hurracarana off the top rope, and hits it. Wretch whips Primetime into the ropes, but Primetime reverses, and Primetime goes for a backdrop. Wretch however, quickly runs up behind Primetime, grabs him by the hair, and hooks him in for the reverse ddt. He covers him, hooks the leg... but Primetime kicks out! Wretch decides another hurracarana from the top rope should do the trick, and positions him in place. He goes for it, but Primetime turns it into a powerbomb from the top rope. Primetime gets up, looks at the fans who are in awe of what just happened, and he pulls Wretch in place for the Primetime Bomb. Suddenly, Scot Hartt and Abusah hit the ring via the crowd. Hartt distracts the referee as Abusah climbs in. Primetime drops Wretch aside, before telling Abusah to bring it on. Abusah does, and charging and going for a spear. Abusah misses, instead slamming into the turnbuckles. Primetime points to his head, signifying he had the move well scouted. However, from behind comes Wretch, who hits the Depression (Reverse DDT). He then pins Primetime, and defeats him to advance to the second round, as well as win the International Title. Hartt enters the ring with the title, and hands it to Wretch, who celebrates by holding it high overhead. He celebrates briefly with his lackey and his lackey's lackey, before the trio of men exit. Meanwhile, in the ring, a disgruntled Primetime stands with his head slumped. Result: Wretch by pinfall to advance to the second round and win the INA Live interview with Neo... Cameras cut to the Saint Martien hospital in Alameda, Colorado for the promised interview with fWo wrestler Neo. Neo lay in the hospital bed, an IV stuck into his right arm and a scowl on his face. The interviewer was a young man named Sam Kirshner, and this was in fact his first interview for the fWo. Picked up on a working program through an independent federation in Memphis, he was excited about this big break and hoped it would lead to a full-time interviewer position with the fWo. Of course, he was hoping that his assignment would be with someone of greater importance... the Strangler, Cactus Jon... or perhaps even Eron. But a job was a job, and he was determined to shine. So that was how Sam found himself on the forth floor of the hospital twitching nervously before the live camera feed as Neo turned slowly toward him. "What." It wasn't a question it was a statement, and Neo looked rather pissed off. Sam began to suspect that he should have done more research into the person he was interviewing. "Um... yes. We are live from the Saint Martien Hospital where I, Sam Kirshner, am live... oh crap, I already said that didn't I? I mean... oh come on man... you're blowing it... you're blowing it... I mean, okay. I'm here with Neo, live, for the fWo. Neo, a few days ago the fans of the fWo were horrified to learn of your beating at the hands of... who?" Sam looked down at a piece of paper. "Is this right? It is? Okay... well, the beating you suffered at the hands of Rottdogg, Steel and Cairn... known some time ago as NXS... the resident, well... some might call them losers I suppose... anyway... you were found after the show by members of the fWo production crew who were preparing the ring for movement to the next venue, Critical Condition which occurred last night. Oh... and um... you can order the replay on Tuesday. Anyway, you suffered several serious medical conditions as a result of the beating... and many are wondering if you will be present at Wednesday Warfare for your part in the Next Level Tournament to face a man you know well... the man who defeated you for the Hardcore Championship... Xavier." Sam held the microphone down in front of Neo expectantly. Neo scowled up at him. Then he looked at the cameras. Neo sneered. Slowly, he reached over and took hold of the IV stuck into his right arm. He smiled, and yanked. The IV sprung free, and blood began to pour down his arm. Sam dropped the microphone and screeched, "Oh Christ!" Neo smiled wider. "Sorry... he ain't here." A moment later the camera feed cut out... leaving far many more questions than answers. Chris Finn vs. Eron The Relentless Slaughter heads to the ring for a rematch from the previous week, this time with a title shot being awarded to Eron should he win. "Start Me Up" starts up, and Christopher Randal Finn begins to make his way to the ring, decked out in an fWo logo shirt and jeans. Finn rolls into the ring and warily eyes the entranceway, before silently muttering a prayer that the next person to come down to the ring will be the Boston Strangler. "Physical" begins playing and Finn's hopes are dashed as Eron The Relentless appears on the entrance ramp, smiling evilly at Finn. Eron slowly stalks to the ring and climbs in, before beckoning Finn over. Finn stays back, and as the bell rings Eron charges at Finn, who has the smarts to duck. Eron smashes into the corner and Finn winds up, before taking Eron to Flair Country with a big chop. Unfortunately, Eron simply laughs it off and clocks Finn upside the head, knocking him down. Finn staggers back to his feet, and Eron takes him down with a hard clothesline. Eron brings Finn back up and whips him into the ropes, before taking him over and blasting him with a powerslam. Eron brings Finn back up and strikes him with a high roundhouse kick, knocking him into the ropes with such force that he bounces off of them and back at Eron. Eron leaps high into the air and clocks Finn with a spinning heel kick, taking him back down. Eron laughs cruelly at the downed Finn, before picking him up and throwing him over the top rope to the outside. Finn crash-lands hard, and Eron slides casually to the outside and picks him up. He then lifts Finn up in a gorilla press position and drops him unceremoniously neck-first onto the guardrail. Finn clutches at his throat as Eron pulls him back up and smashes his head onto the steel ringsteps, busting Finn open for the second time in two weeks. Eron rolls Finn in and climbs onto the apron himself, before heading up to the top rope. Eron leaps off and drives his elbow into the heart of Finn with such impact that Eron himself bounces off the ring. Eron makes the cover on Finn, but pulls Finn up at two when he decides he has a better idea. Eron goes behind Finn and hooks his arm, before wrapping a forearm around his throat. Seconds later, Eron has the Boston Massacre fully applied on Chris Finn, and Finn immediately signals to the ref that he gives, which gives Eron a number one contender spot. Eron refuses to release the hold however, and Finn has begun to slip into unconsciousness when 'O Fortuna' hits and the fans pop like mad for the fWo World Champion, the Boston Strangler. Eron releases the hold on Finn and looks to the entranceway, daring the madman from Beantown to hit the ring and duke it out. Eron is growing impatient when the Strangler suddenly leaps over the guardrail by the announce table and slides into the ring. Eron hears the sudden increase in cheers but, being a heel, fails to turn around. The Strangler wears an insane grin on his face as he casually walks up behind his enemy Eron and slaps on a Boston Massacre of his own. Eron screams in pain and shock and thrashes wildly, but cannot remove himself from the hold. A dozen or so security officers charge the ring and begin trying to pry the Strangler off of Eron, but even their advantage in numbers cannot do it. The cameras then cut backstage to see another backstage attack... Result: Eron The Relentless by pinfall to earn a shot at the World Title Crucified (II) The smell of ether reeked throughout the arena. It seemed as if the prone body had been lying in it's current position for hours, but it had been only fifteen minutes. Another cross... more nails... more rope... and more ether. Another attack... although it appeared as though little physical harm had been done. Last Wednesday it was Robert Jensen... this time it was Frank Snow. And although Frank Snow hadn't been on the shows in quite some time, he was still a part of the backstage crew... tonight he warmed up the crowd in a dark match that only the audience saw before Slaughter took to the air. And now, he stood tied to a cross, knocked out not by a punch or by other physical means, but by ether. And just as the attack on Jensen... nobody saw who committed the crime. Scott Slugger vs. Ice Slaughter headed down to the ring for the Main Event of the show, the fourth ever meeting between former friends Ice and Perfect. The fans awaited for the participants arrive, before "Bastard" began to play, and out walked all two hundred and eighty one pounds of the Big Blonde Machine, Ice. The arena is bathed in blue light, making an awesome visual for the six foot six mammoth of a man. He approached the ring and tore off his "Big Blonde Machine" t-shirt, and threw it into the ring. He then stepped in and with total confidence stood in his corner, awaiting for the man he felt he protected for far too long, the WGA. On cue, "Bastard" is replaced by "Back in Black", and out walks the four-time World Champion, Scott Slugger. Fireworks explode on the entrance ramp behind Slugger as he nears the ring, not taking his eyes off Ice for a split second. He climbs in and immediately he and Ice go at it tooth and nail. Punch by Ice. Punch by Slugger. Punch by Ice. Punch by Slugger. The process repeats itself until Ice gains the advantage with a punch to the neck, before pressing Slugger high above the ring. Flashbulbs pop off throughout the arena, before Ice drops Slugger on the top rope neck first. Feeling it's enough to finish the WGA off, he covers him, but Slugger kicks out at one and a half. Ice grabs Slugger by the hair and introduces him to the turnbuckle several times, before setting him the corner in a standing position. He unloads with a barrage of reverse knife edge chops, which echo throughout the arena. The sounds of Ice' hand smacking against the bare flesh of Slugger cause the crowd to pay homage to a wrestler who has wrestled far past his prime, and yell 'whoo' at the end of each resounding chop. The Freight Train whips Slugger into the turnbuckles, but Slugger reverses it. Ice reverses it again, and brings Slugger towards him for a short-arm clothesline. Ice brings Slugger up and sends him off into the ropes. Slugger returns and is taken down hard with a shoulderblock. Ice cowers over his former friend, grammacing as he looks down at the fallen four-time World Champion. Ice continues to dominate in the early goings of the match, bringing Slugger up and sending him into the ropes once more. Slugger returns and Ice nearly detaches his head from his body with a lariat from Parts Unknown. Ice attempts another pinfall, but it's still too early in the match to finish the World's Greatest Athlete off. Ice brings Slugger up and nails him with some forearms, before sending him to the turnbuckles. Ice charges in, preparing to assault Slugger by sending all two hundred and eighty one pounds full force into him. Slugger moves however, and Ice crashes into the turnbuckles hard. Slugger then turns Ice around and hits his first offensive move of the match, a Orange Crush. Slugger doesn't have quite enough energy to complete the cover, and instead both men are out on their feet early. The referee begins a ten count which gets cut off at three as Slugger rolls over and drapes an arm on Ice for a count of two. Slugger pulls himself up via the ropes and sends Ice into the ropes. Ice returns and ducks the superkick attempt, but when he turns back around after ducking it he's met with a high knee that sends him back into the ropes once more. He bounces off and walks out into a kick to the stomach, and then a powerbomb attempt by Ice. Ice blocks it, and then takes Slugger off his feet by knocking the insides of his legs. He goes to catapault Slugger into the corner, but Slugger quickly hooks him for an inside cradle. Ice kicks out at two, and the two men rush to their feet. Slugger quickly hits Ice with some forearms before sending him to the ropes with an Irish Whip. Ice returns and is taken down in a crucifix for a pinning combination. Ice struggles out at two, and Slugger is again undaunted in his attempt to win a shot at the World Title. Slugger brings Ice up and takes him down with a suplex, before floating over for a pin, and again, Ice kicks out. Slugger decides to use his old finisher, the same move he attempted last night to win the World Title at Critical Condition. He goes up to the top rope and and prepares to fly off, but in from the outside comes the new fWo Internet Champion, Mitch Wilson. Wilson steps into the way of Ice as he pushes him aside, and instead Slugger takes Wilson down with the Grand Slam (flying lariat). Slugger is pissed, but Wilson' interruption didn't stop the match. Instead, Slugger brings Wilson up and throws him over the top rope onto the floor much to the delight of the fans. "Mr. Main Event" crashes on the outside, while back in the ring Slugger brings Ice up to a vertical base. He knees him in the gut, before hitting an Elevated Reverse DDT, otherwise known as the 'Perfect Game'. Slugger then rolls over for a pin, but Ice gets his foot on the rope at two. Before the match can proceed any further, Wilson jumps up on the ring apron. Wilson appears ready to distract the referee, not only does he accomplish that, he gets the attention of Slugger. Slugger walks over cautiously, but the referee keeps him at bay, instead ordering Wilson away from ringside. Wilson laughs it off at first, but then obeys orders after the referee threatens to see to it that Wilson be stripped of the Internet Title. Wilson heads backstage as the fans taunt him, cheering the referee's decision. Meanwhile, Ice has recovered, and he grabs Slugger from behind and takes him down with a bulldog. He covers Slugger, but Slugger kicks out at two. Ice then decides to take a high risk maneuver of his own, and goes up top. He flies through the air, and all two hundred and eighty one pounds of him crash on the prone body of the WGA with a diving headbutt. Ice decides that should be enough to finish him off, and covers him. Slugger somehow kicks out at two, and the match continues. Ice brings Slugger up and signals for the Attitude Adjuster. He goes for the chokeslam, but Slugger breaks free with an elbow to the head. Slugger then grabs Ice and hooks him for the Curveball. He goes into the corner, but Ice blocks it, instead planting him in the ring on his feet. He reaches up and hits Slugger with a low-blow which goes unseen by the refereee. Ice hooks him for a powerbomb, but not just any powerbomb, a running powerbomb. As Ice is about to hit it, Slugger escapes and instead now stands feet first in the ring. A kick to the stomach is followed by a powerbomb, and another, and another one before Slugger took home the victory and bagged himself another fWo World Title shot. The crowd exploded in cheers, as it ensures them of seeing Slugger get another shot at becoming a five-time World Champion. Slugger celebrated as the show went off the air, heading into Warfare with two number one contenders to the fWo World Title. Result: Slugger by pinfall to earn a World Title shot.
