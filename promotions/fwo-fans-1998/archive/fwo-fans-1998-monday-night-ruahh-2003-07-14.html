Introduction The calm is always most silent just before the storm... Epidemic IV. fWo Monday Night Ruahh did not open with a token fireworks display. Instead a single word written out in plain white text sat ominously on the pitch black video wall. Disease. Slowly but surely the letters bled into the next word. Malaria. The word remained only for a few seconds before fading away to be replaced by a shot of a mosquito feeding on human blood before taking off momentarily and landing on a fresh human victim, plunging itself into its next meal. And then the deep voice that wouldn't have been out of place in a movie trailor spoke up. "Don't let the bed bugs bite... especially if they're mosquitoes. Malaria is a life threatening parasitic disease transmitted from person to person via the bite of a mosquito and is responsible for 1 million deaths annually. Typically malaria produces fever, headache and vomiting. Left untreated it can destroy the red blood cells and clog the capilliaries that carry blood to the brain and other vital organs. From this we learn that diseases can develop in any time or place under any circumstance leaving nothing immune. Not even the fans Wrestling organization. At Meltdown, a new disease develops and the epidemic begins." The footage faded to black and the fWo returned to regular programming. Temerario vs. Calm The fans rose to their feet as Monday Night Ruahh~! took to the air. The ring announcer, stationed in the center of the ring, already had Calm beside him. Calm, one of the wrestlers brought in last year to replace the fWo roster moved uneasily from foot to foot, anxious about the upcoming match. At one time called 'the real Alaskan Juggernaut', his career had certainly deviated strongly from Kodiak Vic Creed, the current fWo World Heavyweight Champion. As 'Man Without Fear' began to blare over the arena speakers, the lights dimmed and sparks exploded into the air. Leaping out from behind the curtains, the masked daredevil Temerario jogged to the ring, slapping hands with the fans as he came. Often silent and usually completely focused, Temerario slid beneath the ropes and came to his feet in front of his opponent. Temerario offered a hand, but Calm was either too nervous or proud to accept it. The bell ring, and at once Temerario began the match on the offense with a spinning heel kick. Calm staggered back, but came to his senses as Temerario rushed forward with a clothesline, and pulled off a quick reversal and then a plancha over the top rope. As Temerario rolled back into the ring, Calm took the advantage with several quick aerial moves and a huricarana, a newer move set for the wrestler based on his smaller size. But as he moved in for a reverse chin lock, Temerario surprised Calm with an arm drag turned into a rolling shove to force the former Alaskan Juggernaut to the outside. Running now, Temerario landed a springboard to the outside, followed by a running uppercut. The match continued to shift back and forth in momentum, with Temerario using more aerial moves, and Calm using a light brawling style. Calm took advantage of a missed tope to slow the pacing of the match down, grabbing Temerario by the neck and baring down. Breaking the hold on the referee's count of four, Calm trapped Temerario in a modified camel clutch to injure the lower back, then moved it into a modified bow and arrow submission hold. Temerario attempted to rally back, but Calm was able to successfully execute a tope con hila, and follow it up with another plancha to the outside. Exhausted from the quick pace, Temerario was unable to get any kind of offense going, and Calm continued to dominate the match with submission holds, a huricarana from the top rope, and finally a solid moonsault. Calm hooked the leg as the referee slid down for the count. 1... 2... But Temerario shoved an elbow up and got a knee to the face for his efforts. Laying still, Temerario watched as Calm went to the ropes for a second time. His arms out for the crowd, Calm went for a second moonsault which landed well shy of its target. Temerario used the miss to nail a quick missile drop kick, then picking the taller man up hit the Temerario Driver. Calm lay still, and Temerario fell on top of him for the count. 1... 2... 3! Temerario stood, his arm held up in the air by the referee as the bell was run. He flexed, pleased and went to the corner turnbuckle with his arms raised. He was on a roll... and nothing bad could happen to him now. Result: Temerario, by Pinfall Warnings Temerario, on the corner turnbuckle, didn't see Spyder emerge from the crowd. The crowd roared their warning, but too late as Spyder grabbed Temerario from behind and shoved him off. The masked wrestler cracked his head on the floor and lay still.
