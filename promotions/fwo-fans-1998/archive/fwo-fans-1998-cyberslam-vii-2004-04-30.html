Introduction It is the show seven years in the making. Cyberslam VII, where legends come to the ring to do battle on a national stage. Where anything and everything can happen, and for the fWo, where events will take place that will forever change the direction of the promotion... Prologue Ted Tallison rode up the elevator with his eyes closed. The sensation of slowly rising was strange, but not uncommon and like so many other things in his life ran over him like water. He would be flying to the arena later, to be a part of Cyberslam. It seemed like just yesterday that it was the four of them, his brother, Smooth... and Fox. It seemed like yesterday, and it seemed like a million years ago. Ted opened his eyes as the elevator doors slid open revealing bright white hallways. Hospitals, Ted thought, always looked the same no matter where you were. He had been all around the country as part of the fWo, and a visit to the hospital was all-too-common. They all looked like this, smelled like this... Ted hated it. But he needed to see Fox. It had been too long, months in fact, since he had seen him. The busy road, always traveling, slowly losing any position of credibility with the company with others had it so easy... He secured the vase of flowers under his arm and walked forward, ignoring the others who he passed, ignoring the doctors and the visitors, ignoring everything but the fifth door down the hallway. Watching his friend slowly fade away over the months was hard, and having not seen him since March... or was it February... meant that the slow decay was only going to be all the more striking. He wasn't looking forward to opening that door. A year ago he remembered sitting in a small room, talking and drinking as the fans trailed out of the arena, pondering how Creed would sell as a champion and wondering where Erik Kelly would end up. They talked about Spyder, now dead, and how he had quickly risen in popularity. They talked about everything... and nothing. They drank and enjoyed the twilight of the night, feeling glorious in the fact that their long overseas road trip had come to an end. Feeling... Tallison arrived at the door to Silver Fox's room and pushed it open. Fox had been good to him, a friend in a business that didn't believe in them. What happened was... Ted Tallison looked into the room, then back at the door. He had gotten the wrong room for sure but... "Excuse me," said Tallison, turning to a nurse that was walking down the hallway, "isn't this Silver Fox's room?" The nurse gave him a puzzled frown, the clearity came over her face. "Oh, you mean the wrestler? Well, yes it was... but it hasn't been his room for well over a month now." "A month..." Ted's face must have shown his worst fears confirmed, as the nurse quickly put an arm on his and smiled. "Oh no, no... he's fine. Or at least, I imagine he is... he checked out of the hospital over a month ago." "He...," Ted's head was reeling, and he found himself needing a drink, a stiff one, and damn the consequences, "he checked out?" "That's right. Complete recovery." She smiled at him, and walked away, off to her rounds, leaving Ted Tallison standing confused and lost. The bed was clean and empty, sheets crisp and the small bedside table bare. Or nearly bare... as Ted walked slowly into the room he saw a cut rose, dark green in color, sitting as if waiting for a visitor to come and discover that the man they had all depended on, the man who meant so much to all of them... ...had left them.
