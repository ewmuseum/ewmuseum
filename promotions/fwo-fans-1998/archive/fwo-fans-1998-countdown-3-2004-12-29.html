And the anger grew. The crew blocked the exit. The Deacon stood above them, his eyes staring down one and then another of them, each backing off slightly while maintaining their insults. The Deacon stepped forward. He felt the surge on his right side, a jab or punch from someone, shoving Deacon into Matt, his son falling to the concrete from the impact. And then madness hit the scene. Matt began to shout. The crew began to turn their insults to him. And the Deacon felt his blood boil. The inebriated guy shoved Matt who staggered to the concrete, steadying himself against a wall. And the Deacon's blood boiled over. This was not a wrestling match, nor could it be called a fight. It was violent, chaotic, and without merit or skill. At the end of it, six men lie on the ground. One child cried hysterically as he watched his father bleed on the concrete. And one giant, breathless and spent, leaned against a wall wishing that he'd turned the other cheek and got out of there.
