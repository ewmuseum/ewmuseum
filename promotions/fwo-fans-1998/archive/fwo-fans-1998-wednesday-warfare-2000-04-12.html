Wednesday Warfare, 12 April 2000

12 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction All of the wrestlers stood in the ring, many wearing formal attire. They looked around nervously, still not sure of why they had been told to come to the ring like this. As they watched, Scott Slugger was handed a microphone, and the former World Champion began to speak. "When I came to the fWo some twenty four months ago, I told everyone that I was the Chosen One. I was hand-picked to be the next fWo World Champion. Well I was right wasn't I? Scott Slugger did become a world champion, and he showed you all that you were wrong! Well somewhere along the way things got derailed - by a pack of good ol' boys from the Northwest who couldn't compete and they knew it. Well I've just got one thing to say - yeah, I just got one thing to say to that bunch of slapnuts sitting in their little offices in Redmond... what goes around, comes around. So without further ado, it is my honor and my privilege to introduce you to the man who once was Travis Beaven's best kept secret, who will once again change the game... the Power IS... Waz!" Dressed in a simple shirt and jeans, Waz came down the entrance ramp, microphone already in hand. Much of the crowd was unsure of who this was and looked around nervously. Waz swung into the ring and shook hands with several of the wrestlers, who looked pleased that he was there. Waz began to speak. "You know, after giving months of my life writing for the Fans Wrestling Organization, I stayed with the fWo with one thing in mind... and that was to beat that IWO pile of crap promotion at their own game! And you know what? Within a matter of weeks, the fWo was not only getting in the game, they were changing the game! And that's when the good ol' boy network kicked in - afraid of change, and more importantly afraid of their jobs - the political BS took place in the back to bring Waz down. And you stayin' at home know who you are 'cause you're watching me now. And then one day I'm told that there's gonna be a change in direction - a change that I knew sucked! And you know what? I wasn't the only one who knew... Dudley knew... Scott Hart knew... Silver Fox knew... Trouble knew... Shocker knew... and they left! They're gone! Darkness... he knew it, and they suspended his ass! Well you know what? That's all over now. It's done. And Waz is back in charge again. And I wanna turn around now and I wanna say something to everybody in this ring. It is over. The Northwest boys management is over. The inflated egos in the back, afraid to lose their spot - it is over. It is the dawning of a new day - it is your opportunity - seize that opportunity!" Suddenly, from the top of the entrance ramp came a single voice. "Are you done yet?" Jeremy came out onto the entrance ramp, dressed in a cheap black suit and both arms out like an airplane. Suddenly Rudo came awake with a jump and looked around himself. He was sitting in the back of a limo which appeared to be slowing down as it pulled into the America West area in Phoenix, Arizona. Rudo rubbed his eyes. What a rotten dream... Black Quicksilver vs. Mike Heftel Warfare's fireworks exploded through the arena as the show took to the air. Fans were on their feet cheering as the music of Twisted Sister played, bringing out Black Quicksilver. The fans greet him with cheers, something he enjoys as he slaps the hands of various fans on the way to ringside. He starts to focus his energy and intensity on the match that is about to take place. He awaits the music of Mike Heftel, but that never hits the speakers. Instead, "Martini Time" by Reverend Horton Heat hits the speakers, and a man with a little swerve in his walk, along with a glass of what seems to be alcohol. He is dressed as what can be best described as a lounge lizard. Fans groan as they see Mike Heftel returning to his old haunts as a revolving door gimmick wrestler. He comes to the ring with a mic in his hand. "On Sunday, I was the "Silver Slowback," he begins. "But TONIGHT, I am Marty Teeni!" The fans started to chant "Sco-ott Ha-all! Sco-ott Ha-all!" Heftel turns to the fans. "Who?" he asks, before remembers what he was going to say before the interruption. "Sean, I never meant to hit you over the head with Stump, and you gotta believe me on that one. Yet you insist on a match. I don't want to have to hurt you, buddy. But realize that you're not stepping in that ring against that wannabe Silver Slowblack. No no no, you're taking on the king of svelt, Marty Teeni. Now, if you'd just let me finish my drink first..." However, this is met with a plancha by BQ, which brings about the bell. Teeni's glass falls on the ground, and his drink, which turns out to be Sprite (as it fizzes on the mat) spills everywhere. BQ picks up Heftel..er...Teeni and takes off toward the ropes; upon his return, he lands a dropkick to the head of Teeni. As Teeni hits the mat, the quick and agile BQ runs toward the ropes again, hitting a springboard moonsault. He goes for a pin, but only gets a two. He picks Teeni up and elbows him in the head. This wakes Teeni up, who starts to shake his finger at BQ in a manner of some balding wrestler from another promotion. BQ takes another chop at Teeni, who continues to waive his finger, this time with his eyes wide. BQ, having enough of this, kicks Teeni to the gut, and lands an inverted powerslam. Black Quicksilver goes for another cover, but gets a two-count yet again. He decides to go to the top rope. While he is doing this, Marty Teeni, thinking that he will get the best of BQ, gets up. However, he forgets the other half of the process, and just stands there, holding a finger to his head and telling the fans that HE'S from LAS VEGAS! He's not from down here in...well, he forgets that. A second later, he forgets where he is, as BQ hits a missile dropkick. BQ picks up Teeni and takes him to the turnbuckle. He picks Teeni up, places him on the second turnbuckle, and proceeds to superplex him from the top rope. BQ goes for another cover. The ref counts...1, 2...no. Kickout at two. BQ, getting a little upset at this point, picks up Teeni and sends him to the ropes. BQ takes off for the set of ropes not being used by Teeni. At the point of collision, BQ nails Teeni with a drop kick to the side of the head. BQ senses victory, and heads for the top rope. He looks to hit his finisher, the Silver Splash. He flies off the rope, and promptly hits it. However, he doesn't go for the pin. Instead, he picks up Teeni, and sets him in the turnbuckle once again. This time, it's Teeni's back that is facing the mat. BQ climbs the ropes, holding on to Teeni. He punches Teeni, and pushes him off the ropes. He counts to one, and takes off. As Teeni hits the mat, BQ again hits the Silver Splash. He covers Teeni, and three seconds later, gets the victory. He begins to celebrate his match, but stops. He climbs out of the ring, takes a look at Teeni, and starts to walk backstage. He stops once again at the ramp to look out at Teeni, who is still down but moving a little bit. He thinks for a second, and then continues to the back, as Warfare hits a break. Result: Black Quicksilver by pinfall Training The screen shifted to a camera shot of a shabby looking wooden sign. It hung outside an undisclosed office in an unknown location. The word "THE DOJO" was written on it, spelled incorrectly as "THE DOEJOE". Inside found Tom the Kung Fu Bomb commenting to several students who looked up at him anxiously. "Now students, for the past 3 days, we have been working on what is the most important moment in you getting a WHITE BELT OF DEATH~! in Tom Kwan Do." Tom walked over to a raddy-old 80's boom box that was seated atop a pile of worn crates and pressed the play button. In unison, the whole group of teenagers began to sing along with the music coming from the radio. "Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, Heroes in a half shell, Turtle Power!" Several students pumped fists into the air at this last refrain, and Tom smiled contentedly. The scene pulled away as the camera faded to black. Wretch & Scott Hart vs. Courage & Lance Star "I'm With Stupid" by Static X echoed throughout the America West Arena, home to the National Basketball Association's Phoenix Suns. The fWo International Champion Wretch stepped out onto the stage, before Scott Hart stumbled out near his side. Scott Hart appeared to be excited to actually wrestle on a fWo tv show for the first time since last Summer when he annoyed the hell out of everyone by challenging them to matches for a pay-per-view that would be held the next day. Wretch and Hart entered the ring, and Wretch posed for all of his fans. The obsessed Frenchie fan was seated in the front row, and seemed annoyed by the Wretch fans, but continued to write notes as he watched Warfare unfold. "Reclaim My Place" by Korn echoed loudly throughout the eight year old arena, before Courage came out to an overwhelming negative reaction. He strolled down to ringside before exchanging greetings with the obsessed Frenchie fan, and rolled into the ring. He did not have a partner yet for the match, but Courage didn't mind. He wanted to take both Wretch & Scott Hart on by himself, but the fWo insisted he have a partner for tonight. Courage looked at the stage to see who would be his partner for tonight, and he was not pleasantly surprised as it was "The Future" Lance Star, who came out to a mild positive reaction from the crowd, but had no theme music as he walked to the ring. Star rolled into the ring and Courage scoffed at him, but Star held out his hand for a handshake anyways. Courage reluctantly accepted the kind gesture, but as Star returned to the corner, Courage wiped his hand on his wrestling tights, as if Star had some kind of disease or something. Courage and Scott Hart locked up in the center of the ring, with Courage getting the easy advantage with a side headlock. Courage then progressed into a snap mare takeover, before bouncing off the ropes and hitting a perfectly executed sitting dropkick, planting both of his boots into the back of Hart. Courage tagged out to Star, and they sent Hart to the ropes with an Irish Whip. Hart returned and Star kicked him in the stomach, before hitting a beautiful swinging neck breaker. Star followed it up with a knee drop into the forehead of Hart, and attempting a lateral press. Hart kicked out at two, and Star brought him up by the hair before a snap suplex. Star tagged out to Courage, who flew off the top ropes with a cross body block, taking Hart off his feet down to the mat. This resulted in a cover, but Wretch interrupted the count with a stiff kick to the back of Courage. Courage and Wretch came to blows, but this allowed Hart to come up from behind with a double axe handle to the back of Courage, and then a fall away slam. He tagged out to the newly crowned International Champion Wretch; who flew off the top rope with a senton bomb. He went for a cover, but Courage got his foot on the rope at the count of two, breaking up the pin. Wretch worked Courage over for the next several minutes, with a 'rocker dropper' like move, a fireman's carry takeover, and a death valley driver. He tagged out to Hart, who came in acting pompous and smug. He hurried and applied his finisher, "The Final" (Texas Clover Leaf) to Courage, who writhed in pain. Hart didn't have the move executed one hundred percent correctly, and this enabled Star to come in and pull Hart up, before taking him down with the Shooting Star. Star waited in his corner for Courage to come tag him, and Courage crawled over before he made the tag. Star came in and he took down Hart and Wretch with right hands and forearms, before whipping Hart into one corner, and flying in with a splash. As he staggered out though, Wretch grabbed him from behind and hit the Depression (reverse DDT), before covering him. The referee made the count, and Star kicked out a moment too late. Courage came into the ring and looked frustrated as Star apologized for the loss. Hart jumped up and down wildly before Wretch shot a glare in his direction, as they went backstage while Warfare went to a commercial break. Result: Wretch & Scott Hart by Pinfall Dress up The scene turned to a segment taped earlier in the day. Ultra Violet fidgeted as the tailor took measurements. Ultra Violet wasn't too sure about all this measuring business. He have given the tailor his measurements... K feet and MC2 inches high, %^ inches around the middle... well, to be fair he had gained some weight. Perhaps {+ inches around the middle. Something in that range anyway. "Stand STILL Mr. Ultra." Ultra Violet sighed. All this dressing up stuff was sure a pain. Why did he have to wear a tuxedo to his wedding anyway? Ultra Violet thought a big pair of Bermuda shorts and a tank top would work just fine. He looked over at Violet, who was reading through an issue of Modern Bride and giggling happily to herself. She felt him watching her and looked up smiling. Ultra Violet smiled back. That was the reason he was going through all of this. Violet. Soon she would be known as Mrs. Violet. Or, Ultra Violet thought, she might want to keep her name, in which case she would be known as Mrs. Violet-Violet. or perhaps the other way around. Mrs. Violet-Violet. Either way was fine with him, but Ultra Violet was sure that his mother would favor the more traditional taking of the last name. Violet-Violet... yeah, she wouldn't like that near as well as Violet-Violet. Or perhaps Violet Squared? Ultra Violet wasn't sure and got into his favorite thinking pose. The tailor grunted in disapproval. "Mr. Violet, we can't have you doing that here it's just not... Mr. Violet? Mr. Violet? My word. Miss? Miss yes... your... ahem... husband to be as it were has gotten distracted again." Ultra Violet had spotted something amazing, and his mouth hung open in wonder. It was a beautiful purple sequin tuxedo. It sparkled and glittered in the light. Now THIS was a reason to get married. It even seemed to have a light up heart that glowed on the back. Classy. Violet saw what he was looking at. "Oh Clarence no..." Scott Collins vs. Mitch Wilson "Blind" begins to play, and the fans erupt with boos at the unexpected arrival of Primetime, who makes his way to the announcer's table and puts on a headset to provide color commentary for the next match. "Burning" begins playing, and the crowd cheers as Scott Collins and Stone make their way to the ring, and both roll in simultaneously. Before the music of the fWo Internet Champion begins to play, Stone takes a microphone from the timekeeper and addresses Collins. "Scott - I've got a pretty good idea of what should happen right now." As Armstrong at ringside speculates on a possible exhibition of the perverted arts, Stone continues. "Since you've already had two shots at Mitch Wilson... and you've lost both times... why not give me your shot?" Collins seems reluctant, but Stone drops the mic and engages in a conversation with Collins, appearing to tell him that that wasn't a question. After a moment, Collins sighs and rolls from the ring, giving his shot to Stone. The arena lights fade to red and "Calling All Stations" begins playing, signaling the arrival of Internet Champion Mitch Wilson, who walks down to a chorus of boos. As Mitch reaches the ring, he hands the belt to the timekeeper, and sneers at Primetime, who returns the look. Wilson rolls into the ring and stares at Stone as the bell rings. They lock up, and Wilson gains the advantage with a headlock, which he turns into a headlock takeover. Stone counters with a leg-scissors of the head, and Wilson kips up and out of the hold. Stone is quickly up and attempts a lariat of Wilson, but Wilson ducks and Stone hits the ropes, before coming off and ducking a Wilson lariat. Coming off the opposite ropes, Stone leaps into the air and smashes Wilson as he turns to face her with a leaping forearm shot. Stone hooks a leg, but Wilson kicks out at two. Stone brings Wilson back up to his feet and attempts a whip into the ropes, but Wilson reverses it. Stone re-reverses the whip however, and short-whips Wilson right into a powerslam. Stone covers again, but Wilson escapes once more at two. Stone gets in the face of the referee for this, allowing Wilson to make his way to his feet. He charges at Stone from behind, but Stone anticipates him coming and ducks his clothesline attempt, and hooks him from behind and takes him down with a Russian leg sweep. The ref, who the clothesline just missed, takes a moment to regain his senses before counting the pinfall, and thus Wilson kicks out at two. Stone brings Wilson to his feet again and attempts a back suplex. Wilson however flips onto his feet and double-underhooks the arms of Stone from behind, before twisting around and dropping her on her face with the move known elsewhere as the impaler. Wilson quickly rolls Stone over and covers, but Stone kicks out at a count of two. Wilson brings Stone up again, and whips her into the ropes before catching her coming off and lifting her over his head. Wilson spins around a few times before slamming Stone down onto his knee with a tornado backbreaker. Wilson covers, but Stone manages to kick out of the move at two. Wilson, beginning to get frustrated at his less-than-successful match, brings Stone up again and hammers away with forearms, backing her into the corner. Wilson takes a few steps back, spins, and attempts a rolling elbow, but Stone moves. Wilson re-directs his arm in mid-move and he strikes only the soft turnbuckle pad. Wilson turns, pleased with his quick thinking, to see Stone charging in at him. He lifts her up over his head, but she lands on the apron. Wilson quickly spins around and plants a rolling elbow right into her face, knocking her off the apron. Primetime boos at this development, and Collins seems angry. Wilson attempts to head outside to attack Stone further, but stops on the apron when he sees a pissed-off Scott Collins staring up at him. Wilson shrugs and backs into the ring, and Collins helps Stone to her feet. Stone shrugs him off and rolls back in, and Wilson immediately begins stomping away on her as she enters. Stone is down, and Wilson brings her back up. Wilson attempts to chop her, but Stone is beginning to get pissed and absorbs the chop of Wilson, before grabbing him by the shoulders and tossing him into the corner. She begins hammering away on him with punches, before taking a few steps back and charging in with a clothesline. Stone then stands in front of Wilson and repeatedly strikes him with standing clotheslines until Wilson is slumped sitting in the corner. Stone takes a few steps back, then runs forward and kicks Wilson right in the face. Wilson slumps back, and Stone brings him to his feet. She attempts to whip him into the ropes, but Wilson reverses it. However, he makes the critical mistake of putting his head down, and Stone hooks him up and lifts him onto her shoulders for the Earthquake. From his perch, Wilson sees Primetime standing by the announce table, devoid of headset and holding Wilson's Internet Title high. Wilson is pissed, and hammers away at the head of Stone. He then rolls down her back into a sunset flip, and the referee counts, reaching three a split-second before Stone can kick out. Wilson immediately slides out of the ring and chases after Primetime, who has fled through the crowd. Inside the ring, Stone is mightily upset, and Scott Collins attempts to calm her down as Warfare rolls on... Result: Mitch Wilson by pinfall The misadventures of Kid Cool Kid Cool knocked and then paced nervously. In one hand he held a box of chocolates, and in the other a batch of roses. He sniffed. Something wasn't right. He smelled the chocolates. No, those seemed normal. He smelled the roses. Also normal. He sniffed the air again. It occurred to Kid Cool that he had forgotten to finish bathing earlier today, and he looked down. He was dressed in a towel and ducky slippers. Panic began to set in. How had he gotten from the hotel room to the arena wearing just this and not notice it? Behind the door he heard footsteps and began to look around himself frantically. If Nightshade saw him like this she was likely to lose all respect in him. The hallway was empty, and now the doorknob was starting to turn. Kid Cool yelped and then remembered a trick from the cartoons he watched as a child. Kid Cool stood sideways and sucked in his breath. Sideways he would be thin as paper and she would never see him. The door opened. Nightshade looked at him through flat angry eyes. Kid Cool was silent, trying not to breathe. She was just a foot to the right of him, but because he was turned sideways she wouldn't be seeing him right now. The problem was that Nightshade did seem to be seeing him, because she was swinging what looked to be a very heavy fist. Kid Cool crumpled to the ground as Nightshade picked up the flowers and began to beat him with it. Ric Chronos & Fallen vs. Damnation "Time" by Anthrax rocked the America West Arena as Ric Chronos & Fallen made their way out to a large positive reaction from the fans. Chronos hopped over the top rope into the ring, while Fallen rolled into the ring via underneath the ropes. Fallen slumped into a corner nearby, while Chronos awaited in the center of the ring for their opponents. "Sad But True" by Metallica began and echoed throughout the building, while Damnation walked out onto the stage. Hellrazor & Fear held their arms high above the crowd as they strolled to the ring, the crowd giving this new duo a big positive reaction to their entrance. Hellrazor & Fear entered, before the referee sent one man from both teams to their respective corners. Hellrazor & Chronos would start things off in the ring, while Fallen & Fear would man their corners. A collar and elbow tie-up took place in the center of the ring, with the quick Chronos garnering the advantage into a side armbar. He then twisted Hellrazor arm hard enough Hellrazor spilled to the mat, before bringing 'Razor up to his feet and taking him back down with a short-arm clothesline. Chronos then brought him up and took him down with a single-arm DDT, before going for a cover. Fear interrupted the count at two, however. Chronos continued on the offensive, throwing Hellrazor into the corner before charging in with a shoulder block. Hellrazor moved however, and Chronos smacked the outside pole. As Chronos staggered back towards the center of the ring, he was met with a fisherman's suplex by Hellrazor, of which Chronos kicked out of at two. Hellrazor tagged in Fear, and the two men sent Chronos into the ropes, and upon returning the man of the hour was met with a double-team shoulder block on the behalf of Hellrazor & Fear. Fear went on the attack with a double-armed chokeslam, using both of his large arms rather than one. A cover ensued, but at two Fallen interrupted the count with an elbow directly into the back of Fear. Fear held his back as he stood up, and Fallen then took him down with a chopblock from behind. Chronos rolled through and tagged out to Fallen, who came in and went to work on the larger Fear. First a piledriver which was easy for Fallen to execute due to a pulling of the tights, and then a inverted swinging neck breaker. Fallen tagged out to Chronos, who came in and caught Fear as Fallen sent him towards him in a belly-to-belly suplex, but Fear countered into a go-behind, before Chronos countered into a go-behind of his own, before taking Fear down with a nicely done belly-to-back suplex. Chronos then took Fear down with a regular DDT, before tagging out to Fallen, who dug deep into his arsenal, and finally going up to the top rope. He waited for Fear to return to a vertical base, before launching into a missile-dropkick, of which Fear moved out of. Fear then tagged into Hellrazor, who came in a house of fire. Hellrazor took both men down with a double-Japanese arm drag, before taking Fallen over the top rope onto the floor with a psychotic clothesline. Hellrazor then returned to the ring and took Chronos to the mat with a spear, before sizing him up and putting him on the canvas with a kick to the stomach, followed up by a gut wrench suplex. Hellrazor then went for the cover, but Fallen interrupted the count. Fear was there to meet Fallen however, and took him down with a lariat. Fear then helped assist Hellrazor with a face buster on Chronos, and three seconds later, Damnation had picked up their second victory in the tag team ranks. Result: Ultra Violet by Pinfall The return of the Lone Gunmen Lights flickered, and then pitched into darkness. The moment played out, and then suddenly a loud crash of explosives came and a blue spotlight shone on the top of the entrance ramp. There stood Machina, Rudo, Brandi, and the Total Eclipse. On the back of the Eclipse sat Sid, carrying a large flag with the trademark Lone Gunmen circle L painted on it. The five came to the ring amidst a mixture of cheers and boos, and were accompanied by "Semi-Charmed Life" rather than the regular Lone Gunmen music. Sliding into the ring one by one, the five Lone Gunmen walked around the ring, thrusting their arms into the air and encouraging the crowd response. Rudo had the stick and shouted, "Hello, Arizona!" The crowd cheered, and Rudo paced around the ring, his fWo Cruiserweight Championship title around his waist. "I know we've been gone for a few weeks, but let me just say that we are all glad to be back here tonight... and let me assure you that not another night will go by that the Lone Gunmen aren't in the arena to give you fans what you came to see! Hit the music!" Macarena began to play as Rudo and Sid handed the Total Eclipse a pair of sunglasses. As they settled on the big man's face he at once began to dance, much to the horror and delight of the viewing crowd. As the song ended the crowd clapped for the Lone Gunmen, and Rudo handed the microphone to the leader of the Gunmen, Machina. "Hi!" The crowd looked at Machina expectantly. "Um... hi." The crowd looked at each other. They waited for Machina to do something funny. "Um... nice night here." The crowd began to boo, and Rudo and Sid exchanged nervous glances. Machina continued, looking nervous. "Well, um... I guess it's nice you all came out tonight. There was a nice play going on over at the Phoenix box office... it's West Side Story. You all should go see it, it's good for your education." This got further boos, and Machina looked more nervous than ever. "I say... um... can you stop booing please? You see, I'm a bit afraid of speaking in front of people actually..." The crowd booed louder, and Machina looked frozen in fright. Rudo took the microphone from him with a confused look on his face. Rudo addressed the crowd. "Uh... yeah, okay right. Listen, I've got a Cruiserweight belt here, and dammit, I know, you know, and everyone knows that it's the Cruiserweight belt that gets all the action. So that's why I'm making a challenge right here tonight for next week's Warfare. We are going to call it a Cruiserweight Battle Royal! I'm going to invite twelve of the best Cruiserweights from the fWo, and the winner will face me at CyberSlam for this title... the best title in the industry. So... be ready! And stuff!" The show went to a commercial as Rudo turned back at Machina with a confused expression on his face. Perfect vs. Eron "Humans Being" starts to play as Warfare heads to the ring for its main event, and the lights dim to red as Perfect makes his appearance at the top of the entrance ramp. Perfect makes his way to the ring, looking to be all business for this matchup. He quickly rolls into the ring and gestures for the microphone, and the timekeeper tosses it in to him. "You know, there's a reason I joined FTfWo - because the fWo management couldn't help screwing up. They shoved Mitch into that ridiculous DNA angle, they hired Scott Hart, they let the Frenchie put the crowd to sleep in his quote-unquote main events... but there was one mistake they made that was worse than any of that. They hired Eron. Since then that rambling babbling freak has been a drag on this worthless promotion even more than Frenchie! Well... that all ends tonight. Eron, you call yourself the Perfect Drug? I call you the Perfect Piece of (Censored)! Get your worthless ass out here... I'm fixing that mistake." Perfect tosses the mic aside and "Physical" starts up, prompting Eron to rush the ring and charge in. He quickly begins to trade punches with Perfect, and Perfect gains the advantage. Perfect whips Eron into the ropes and catches him coming off with a dropkick. Eron goes down, and Perfect drops a leg before covering. Eron manages to kick out at two, and Perfect brings him up again. Perfect whips him hard into the corner, and charges in with a leaping bodysplash. Perfect quickly mounts the second rope and begins to pound away on Eron with rights, but Eron counters by throwing Perfect over his shoulders and out onto the apron. Perfect manages to land on his feet, and as Eron gloats, Perfect grabs him by the hair and throws him violently to the mat before slingshotting in with an elbowdrop. Instead of covering, Perfect brings Eron to his feet and whips him into the ropes. As Eron comes off, Perfect catches him and throws him high into the air with a flapjack. Eron smashes head-first into the mat, and his nose rams at a high speed into the canvas, busting it and causing blood to flow freely. Perfect, unperturbed, brings Eron up and tosses him unceremoniously through the ring ropes to the outside where he lands with a splat. Perfect waits for Eron to get up, and when he does, Perfect runs the ropes and comes off with a somersault plancha that nails Eron, taking him down. Perfect lands in a mounted position, and rains down stiff right hands onto the nose of Eron, causing him to gush further. Finally Perfect climbs off of Eron and brings him up, before whipping him into the ring post. Eron bounces off like a pinball, and lands slumped against the guardrail. Perfect charges at him and clotheslines him over, causing him to hit hard on the concrete. Perfect waits for Eron to get to his feet, before charging at the guardrail and leaping over it with a shoulder block, which is dodged at the last moment by Eron and takes out a fan, for which the fWo is of course not responsible. Eron quickly brings Perfect up and tosses him over the guardrail to the outside. Eron climbs back over and immediately grabs a chair. As Perfect climbs to his feet, Eron swings the chair and it clobbers Perfect on the head. The referee from inside the ring admonishes Eron, threatening to DQ him, but when Eron rolls in wielding the chair, the referee quickly backs down. On the outside Perfect has crawled to his feet and he climbs onto the guardrail. Eron quickly turns to him and swings, but Perfect drops from the guardrail and Eron's chair hits the ropes before bouncing back into his own face. Eron holds onto the chair as he staggers about the ring, and Perfect quickly mounts the apron again and leaps in with a springboard missile dropkick that blasts the chair into the bloody face of Eron, which more than likely does not help his bloody condition. Perfect covers Eron and hooks the leg, but Eron barely manages to escape at two. Perfect once again brings Eron to his feet, before hooking him up and taking him over with an exploder suplex. Perfect brings Eron up once more, before taking him down with a nearly vertical brain buster. Perfect covers again, but Eron kicks out at two. Perfect brings Eron up and attempts a whip into the ropes, but Eron reverses and Perfect goes in instead. Eron makes the mistake of putting his head down, and Perfect capitalizes with a beautiful rocker dropper, driving the mangled nose of Eron straight into the canvas once again. Perfect covers once more, but Eron shows great resiliency and kicks out at two. Perfect gets up and attempts a standing moonsault, but Eron moves and Perfect hits the mat hard. Eron immediately heads for the legs of Perfect, but Perfect kicks him off and into the ropes. Eron stops himself by grabbing the ropes, and waits for Perfect to get up. Perfect is quickly up, and ducks an Eron clothesline. Perfect kicks blindly backward, and Eron doubles over in pain as it is obvious that Perfect's inverted testicular kick has found its mark. Perfect turns around and leaps onto Eron, double-underhooking his head and taking him down with the Curveball. Eron's head spikes into the mat almost vertically, and he falls limply to the mat. Perfect heads for the legs of Eron and he wraps Eron up into his own finisher, intent on adding insult to injury. Perfect's face is to the ropes as he applies the move, and the ref is intent on checking to see if Eron will submit or not. Because this is as it is, the ref never sees Scott Collins rushing in from the crowd, carrying a lighter and flash paper. Collins leaps over the guardrail and charges toward Perfect, before lighting the paper and throwing it into the face of Perfect, where it explodes with a fireball, much as the fireball thrown months ago at Collins by Perfect did. Perfect falls backwards, releasing the hold, and Eron drapes a hand over him from the side. The referee counts one, two, three and once again Eron lives up to his promise of there being a screwjob. The very moment the ref's hand hits for the third time, Dead rushes in from the back, and he charges at Collins, nailing him with the fWo World Title and knocking him back into the crowd. Dead quickly rolls into the ring and sets the title belt on the mat, before bringing up Eron and dropping him with the Crucifixion on the belt. Perfect by this time is stirring, and despite the flesh of his forehead being colored a bright red from the flames of Collins, he struggles up to his feet. As he reaches his feet, Dead tosses him an object very familiar to him... the fWo World Title. Dead brings Eron to his feet, and Perfect waffles Eron with the belt, smearing it with blood. Dead holds onto Eron, before shoving him at Perfect. Perfect catches him, and takes him down with the Perfect Game directly onto the belt. Suddenly, the arena lights dim and the fans explode as "O Fortuna" begins. After about 15 seconds of darkness, the lights come back on and the fans are treated to the sight of Perfect laid out in the ring and Dead on the outside of the ring, clawing at the hangman's noose around his throat. Inside the ring the Boston Strangler pulls up on the ropes, and Dead is hung from the ropes. Dead desperately attempts to free himself, but cannot and soon passes out from the pressure. The Strangler unceremoniously dumps him outside of the ring, before picking up the fWo World Title and holding it high above his head. The fans cheer wildly, before the Strangler throws the belt back over the top rope where it lands on Dead. The Strangler leaves the ring, the only man standing in a bloody sea of carnage, and silently heads to the back as Warfare goes off the air... Result: Eron by Pinfall
