fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE NEXT VICTIM August 5, 2000 fWo Survivor narrows the roster from six wrestlers to five over the weekend, as the voting gets tighter and moods darken. This round the victim is Nick Allstar, a wrestler who had flirted with voting disaster several times in the past. Nick Allstar had been one of the sleeper wrestlers who worked his way into the top six. His interviews were consistent, his wrestling record solid, although usually just shy of gaining immunity. Nick Allstar was also heavily rumored to be a member of the recent Survivor alliance that crumbled during round six. Several fWo Survivor observers commented that Nick Allstar would be one of the first one out of the contest, but after a few rounds went by many changed their minds, picking Allstar to go the distance and win the contest. However Nick Allstar has recently been invisible to the contest, going over a week with no interviews and missing a pair of voting rounds. The strangest thing about this round were some of the comments made by wrestlers while voting. While it was clear to observers during round six that the alliance had crumbled and that wrestlers could no longer be trusted, several wrestlers made comments regarding how others were voting, as if there was some common alliances working. However, when looking at the range of voting wrestlers who were claimed to be voting one way usually voted another. Stevie Roberts commented on this odd phenomenon. "Lots of people are lying." said Roberts. "It seemed clear to us during the last round that some deception was going on within the Survivor contest, but it appears some of the wrestlers have not yet clued in to that fact. You can't trust anyone... that's obvious now. We think a few people are clinging to the concept of alliances... but the alliances simply aren't there anymore." When asked if he thought new alliances would form, Roberts laughed. "With only five people left in the contest it would be suicide for a wrestler to go to another wrestler and suggest an alliance. Everyone wants to win... period. If a wrestler gives away who he is voting for all it does is give away your strategy. Based on the last two weeks, a couple of wrestlers have smartened up to the concept of out strategizing the strategists. Of course, I could be wrong... but if I was in Survivor right now I'd be playing it close to the vest and just working on giving good interviews and wrestling well. It seems a couple of the people in the contest are trying to out-think the contest... and are suffering as a result." Results were as follows: Nick Allstar: 2 votes, 1 absent vote: 3 total votes Kodiak Vic Creed: 2 votes Ruben Ross : 1 vote More news as it comes available.
