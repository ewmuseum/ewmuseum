Sunday Slaughter, 11 June 2000

11 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction James Armstrong & Chris Finn strolled into the Nassau Veterans Memorial Coliseum with big, bright smiles on their faces. With a confident nature, Finn held the door open for Armstrong. Armstrong thanked him, and then Finn proceeded into the arena as well. Something was not right. Finn.. Armstrong... acting.. well, happy? Together? No, on the night of June 11, 2000 - something was not right at all. Crusader (c) vs. Mike Heftel The June 11 edition of fWo Sunday Night Slaughter took to the airwaves. The fans in attendance rose to their feet and cheered, while fireworks and pyrotechnics went off near the stage. Loud music hummed throughout the arena, and the atmosphere was electric from the get go. To start things off, Scatterbrain Result: Time Limit Draw Was it worth the ratings? The overjoyed duo of Armstrong & Finn entered their luxorious office, before sitting down. Finn reached for a bottle of the finest champagne, before pouring him and Armstrong a glass. Finn took it upon himself to make a toast. "Cheers.. to Britney Spears!" Armstrong followed suit and said the same, before they gulped the champagne down rather quickly. Finn then sat in his chair, looking around the room. "You know, James, I've always been somewhat hesitant about having to use celebrities on our shows to boost the ratings, but I knew... somehow, I knew Spears would come through with us." Armstrong concured. "Yeah. And it's a good thing we didn't hire those teen punks from N' STINK. Did you hear they charged one million to perform at a private barmitzvah last week? Ridiculous." Finn nodded. "Absolutely. But this Spears appearance did a lot for us. And we didn't even have to put the fWo World Title on her, either." Finn grinned, before Jeremiah Frankton entered the room, looking very unpleasant. "Uh... Chris... I've..." Armstrong tried to hurry him up. "Spit it out, Jer. What is it?" Frankton' mouth chattered with uneasyness. "I've... got... bad news." Finn & Armstrong frowned. Primetime & Lance Star vs. Scot Hartt & The Abusah The team of Scot Hartt and Abusah come out to Result: Primetime & Star I'm Hardcore. "Morons. Kelly. Xavier. Even Jensen. All of them, just morons. You're willing to hurt yourself over a silly title like this? Pathetic. What for? For who? Pride? There is no pride in wrestling. There is no loyalty, except loyalty to yourself. And even then, you guys aren't loyal to yourself. You put your body on the line... night in and night out for this? Not for respect. Not for recognition. But for a silly title. Well, not me. This title means about as much to me as toilet paper. And we all know where toilet paper ends up. You want to know the definition of hardcore? I'm the definition of hardcore. I'm hardcore because I travel all around the world, just like the rest of you do. But there's a difference. I have to put up with the idiots in every town in every city in every state... and sometimes every country. I put up with the young fans, asking for autographs. Don't they realize I might be busy? That I might actually have a life that I need to deal with? No, they persist, wanting my signature on a piece of paper. Why should I give it to them? I shouldn't have to. I'm a human too, you know. And that's why I'm hardcore. Because I deal with you idiots, and spend time wondering why the rest of you try to get close to us when you could be out doing better things with your time. But you're not. You're trying to come close to greatness. And that's why you are never going to be anything more than a nickel and dimer at the local convenience store living on paycheck to paycheck. But my counterparts are no better. Look at them. They're willing to risk life and limb. For what? For this?" Neo held up the fWo Hardcore Title he won this past Wednesday at Slaughter, before continuing. "Not anymore. It's mine. And it will remain mine because I refuse to lose to an idiot named Xavier, and a long-haired walking talking piece of monkey poo who calls himself Kelly all the while shouting "powerbomb" as if we're supposed to be scared of him. Kelly, I'm not scared of you. You're scared of me. You fear Neo, you fear that once we get into the ring again I will once again outsmart you and use your disadvantages to my advantage. And in a moment, I will. And I will expose you for the fraud that you are. You're no tougher than me or anyone else in the lockerroom. You think you are, but you're not. You're nothing, Kelly. Nothing. And that's not just my opinion, that's not just your opinion because I just told it to you, that's everyone's opinion." Neo laughed, before slinging the Hardcore Title over his shoulder, and leaving the area. Neo (c) vs. Erik Kelly vs. Xavier Slaughter returns to the wailings of Result: Xavier by pinfall Well, was it? We continue from where we last left off at the office of Armstrong & Finn. Their frowns were nearly etched in stone across their face. Frankton continued. "The ratings report you guys got on Thursday? Wrong. That 10.5 you thought we had scored? Wrong. Nowhere even close. Not on the level of say... the company that promises huge, gigantic, monstrous, business changing surprises... but, not good, either." Finn & Armstrong failed. Their experiment had failed. Their forray into the use of big name celebrities from other industries... failed. It didn't produce the results they had hoped for, and now it looked as if they were painted into a corner with nowhere to go, and nowhere to hide. It was now that they would face riddicule and criticism from the so-called experts. The Robert Rider's and the David Keltzers and the Wade Meller's would be all over their asses, saying they failed, and failed big time. Finn & Armstrong looked at each other. Panic came across their faces. Finn was very unhappy. "Alright. That's it. We've tried the celebrity route. Now we've got to try the "load a card with a lot of pay-per-view caliber matches" route. Let's book a Warfare for the ages. Let's not hold anything back. Let's make Warfare... a show to remember. Let's focus all of our energy into hyping that show, and if that fails... well, we've got one more thing we can try." Armstrong looked at him puzzled. "And what's that?" Finn looked at him sternly. "What else... blackjack and hookers!" Armstrong grinned, but Finn still had a look of panic written across his face. Courage (c) vs. Havok Slaughter returned to the ring for an fWo International Title Match. "When Worlds Collide" by Powerman 5000 begins to echo throughout the NVMC, as the fans stand to their feet and immediately acknowledge the presence of Havok, a clear favorite considering he's from nearby Long Island. Havok rolls into the ring and awaits for his opponent of the night, the fWo International Champion, Courage. Courage makes his way out to a thunderous ovation of jeers, all the while grinning cockily as he enters the ring. Havok turns his back for a split second, a perhaps fatal mistake, as Courage immediately takes advantage of it. He charges and slams Havok with the International Title, which knocks him to the mat. Courage then hands the title to the referee and pushes him out of the way, before unloading a flurry of offensive moves. He brings Havok up and whips him to the ropes, Havok returns and Courage takes him down with a drop-toe-hold. He then charges into the ropes and returns with a sudden elbow to the back of Havok. He then follows it up by bringing him up and dropping him with a brainbuster, before covering him. Somehow, Havok kicks out, but Courage continues with a confident attack. He brings him up and takes him down with a snapmare takeover, and follows that up by lining up Havok for a sitting dropkick. Right on target, and both boots hit the face of Havok with sudden impact. Havok clutches his face while Courage poses to the crowd, taunting them & telling them he is world's better than they and Havok are. Courage turns his attention to the match and confidently looks at Havok with a smile, before bouncing off the ropes and driving a knee into the chest of Havok. The four-time fWo International Champion brings Havok up, peppers him with an array of forearms, before taking him down once more with a slingshot suplex. He brings him up again, and nails him with a Michinoku Driver. He covers him with a finger, and at the count of two, Havok kicks out. Courage looks at the crowd now, signifying he's just toying with Havok. After walking over to Havok, he bends over, and Havok reaches up and brings him down into a small package. The referee moves into position to count. At two, Courage narrowly kicks out. Now perturbed that Havok actually had a snowball's chance in hell of winning, Courage decides to finish it, and finish it quickly. He then hooks him for a Implant DDT and hits it, before running and jumping off the middlerope, and completing his finisher, 'Courage Required'. He hooks the leg, and pins him, before noticing Crusader out on the stage. Courage grimmacing looks at Crusader as the referee hands him his title. Crusader looks at Courage with anger and fury, and Courage gulps. Crusader begins charging down the ramp, and flies into the ring with such velocity he accidentally knocks the referee over. He narrowly misses Courage, diving for his leg. Courage heads out of the ring and into the crowd, following suit. Back in the ring, a groggy Havok attempts to recover from the beating dished upon him by Courage, but as he turns around, he's met with a superkick from Primetime. Primetime then hooks him and slams him with a Primetime Bomb, before holding his fWo Internet Title high above the crowd. In from behind comes "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson, who hammers him in the back with a steel chair. Wilson responds to the boos from the crowd with a look of anger, before nailing 'The Headliner' on Primetime, and doing a mock pin on both Havok & Primetime, which is counted by Ice. The special guest referee of the main event at Critical Condition then mockingly raises the hand of Wilson, as Slaughter heads to a commercial break. Result: Courage by pinfall Meeting of the minds. Slaughter returns, this time backstage. In the office are the powers that be, the brains behind the product, the... yadda yadda whatever. Finn & Armstrong are mapping out a card for Warfare. "I say we force Pierre to comeback!" Finn disagrees. "James, he said he'd return when he wished, but now is not the time." No luck. Although, the return of the Flying Frenchie would likely pop a helluva rating. Finn proceeds. "Alright, so far for the main event we've got The Boston Strangler versus Eron for the World Title, we've got the three-way dance between the Future Foundation, we've got Scott Slugger against Mitch Wilson, Ric Chronos & Fallen vs. The Guano Apes, and... what else?" Armstrong shrugs. "How 'bout we put Ice up against The Black Panther... whoever wins, is owed a shot at the fWo International Title." Finn concurs. "Hey, there's a solid idea. Any other ideas?" Armstrong thinks for about thirty seconds. "Yep." "What?" "The return of The Flying Frenchie!" "James... we've... discussed that." "Oh, yeah. Right. Well, let's have the hardcore clan in a hardcore match for the hardcore title. Let's hope they all kill each other. Let's have an exploding death mine timebomb cage glass thumbtacks fire... whatever. Let's just hope one of them dies. That... WILL draw ratings, I guarantee you." "Okay. We'll have the hardcore match... but... without... the whole... dying thing. Xavier versus Neo versus Kelly versus Jensen. Nice. We're set." Armstrong nods, and finally, Slaughter goes to the final match of the evening. Scott Slugger vs. The Black Panther vs. Eron The Relentless Sunday Slaughter returns to the music of FooFighters, and the crowd rises from their chairs in anticipation of the Black Panther. When he emergers from the back, the crowd gives him their appreciation. He thanks them all on his way down the ring. He slides in and awaits one of his competitors in tonight's matchup. "Physical" by Nine Inch Nails begin to play, the crowd's cheers immediately turn to boos as Eron the Relentless makes his way down the ramp. He administers a very nice gesture to the crowd as he slides into the ring. The Black Panther charges him, but Eron hops out, and starts to wiggle his finger at TBP. As the Panther looks down at Eron, AC/DC hits the fWo airwaves, and Scott Slugger appears on the entrance ramp. The crowd rises to their feet once more, and cheers loudly for Slugger as he makes his way toward the ring. He and Eron have a staredown by the ring, and then he slides into the ring. He and the Panther exchange looks, each of which seem to have the telepathic message of beating the daylights out of Eron. Eron, meanwhile, slides into the ring. TBP and Slugger both charge him. Meanwhile, Eron, thinking quickly, pulls out a microphone from the back of his tights. "Hey, now hold on a minute...let's not be hasty. I'M not the one who said the Mariners suck, bat-boy. If you want to know who said that, look at the jackass next to you." Slugger is touchy about his M's, and turns to the Black Panther. The Black Panther looks at Slugger in complete denial. Eron holds the mic up to his lips again, and begins to speak. "And Panther...Slugger...ummm...Slugger...says...that, ummm...you...don't pounce so well?" Panther looks at Slugger, and his brow narrows. Nobody tells him he doesn't pounce well. Eron saw this change in his demeanor, and drove that point home. "Yeah, he said you suck. Your pouncing is just horrible. I mean, just the other day, he was telling Kid Cool that he's a MUCH better pouncer than you." That was all Panther needed to hear, and he tore at Slugger. A kick to the midsection, and Panther raises Slugger up for a backbreaker. He connects, and Slugger, still feeling the effects of their match four days ago, winces in pain. Panther, too, feels a bit of pain as he does this; he hasn't quite recovered from Warfare. TBP picks up Slugger and whips him into the ropes. Meanwhile, Eron grabs a camera and begins to take pictures of the action. The Black Panther hits a flying lariat onto Slugger, and goes for a cover. At the count of two, Eron kicks Panther in the back of the head. Panther looks at Eron, and begins to charge him. Eron grabs the mic again. "Hey, Slugger said that your mask makes you look like a GIRL. A damn sissy, even." Panther turns around, and charges Slugger, who has been struggling to get up. Slugger sees this coming, and falls to the mat, and hits the dreaded drop-toe hold. Panther looks at Slugger, and Slugger shrugs his shoulders. He lets Panther get up, and then they lock up. Slugger gets the upper hand with a headlock. Panther counters, and gets Slugger in a headlock of his own. Slugger lifts up Panther, and drives him to the mat with a back bodydrop. Both Panther and Slugger wince in pain. Slugger is the first to his feet after a few seconds of catching his breath, and he picks up the Panther. He feels that the Panther is still too banged up from Warfare, and wants to go for the Curveball. He locks the Panther in, and goes for it. Panther tries to block it, but to no avail. Slugger hits it, and goes for a pin. Again, at two-and-a-half, Eron kicks Slugger in the head. Slugger slumps to the mat, and then gets up and looks at Eron. Slugger takes after Eron, who in turn grabs the mic again. "I overheard Panther telling Kelly that Machina's Rod hits better than A-Rod. He also said something about John Olerud tossing Buhner's salad...whatever that means." Slugger won't have any of it. He continues to charge Eron. Eron, seeing this, hops over the ropes. Slugger stops and looks down at Eron. Eron wags his finger at Slugger, and keeps his attention long enough for Panther to get to his feet. Panther bounces off the ropes opposite Slugger and charges hard towards the WGA. The Panther flips over the ropes and catches Slugger's neck, driving down toward the top rope. He crashes Slugger's head into the rope, which slingshots him back onto the mat. Panther hits the mat on both feet. He climbs back into the ring and looks at Slugger. Time for the Killer Instinct. He picks up Slugger and hits the pumphandle slam. But Panther wants a little more. He wants the Pounce. He climbs to the top rope, and lines up Slugger. Eron gets into the ring, and picks up Slugger so Panther can hit it. Panther looks at Eron suspiciously, but continues anyway. He flies off the top, and nails it. However, his momentum carries him past Slugger and to the corner. He slips and falls. Eron now sees his chance. He grabs Slugger and covers him. The ref counts the three, and Eron gets the win, despite doing nothing in the match. Panther gets up and charges Eron once more, but Eron escapes untouched. Panther helps Slugger up to his feet, and they both receive a nice gesture from Eron. Panther & Slugger look at Eron with a look of sarcasm, but from behind Eron steps The Boston Strangler. Eron backs right into him, and appears startled. Eron exits into the crowd, giving The Strangler an unnice gesture as well. Strangler doesn't care, telling him that after this Wednesday he won't survive until Critical Condition. Eron gives him another gesture, this time pointing to his crotch as he then exits further into the crowd. Strangler pays no attention, until he notices in the ring from behind Slugger & Panther is Ice & Mitch Wilson, both wielding chairs. They slam into the backs of Slugger & Panther, before daring Strangler to enter the ring. Just as the fWo World Champion is about to, Slaughter concludes, heading into Warfare with one of the most loaded cards in the history of the fans Wrestling organization. Result: Eron by pinfall
