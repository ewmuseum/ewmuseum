Introduction With only a week to go before Legends, all eyes are on the Wrestler of the Year tournament. Within the next few days, the competition will be fiercer than ever as all eyes turn to the few who will struggle to pick up the honored mantle of Wrestler of the Year... and become the stuff of legend. Nintendo- Did we pay EGM off or is Mario Kart:DD! really the nex Kodiak Vic Creed came from the locker room area, and had already made his way into the ring. Standing before Frontier and Eron, Vic Creed booted Frontier hard into the face as the lock was broken. Lifting his challenger up in a gorilla press, KVC came down hard with the Power Driver, slamming Frontier down as the referee began to stir. Eron slowly moved up to his feet, seeing KVC brutalizing Frontier and smiled. This was why he was confident. This is why he was ready. But suddenly... Eron could hear the crowd once again cheering. Slowly turning around, once again feeling the pain from his lower back... Xias, his opponent at Legends, had come packing with a steel chair. Xias slid into the ring, as now the referee was definitely was conscious. Seeing the pandemonium in the ring, the referee signalled for the bell and a no contest, as the men continued to battle. Xias smashed the chair into Eron's face, as KVC powerbombed Frontier. Those two then started to battle, as Frontier and Eron both tried getting back up to join in the four man escapade. All four men tirelessly battled, as the match came to an unfinished decision. Once again, Eron and Brand could not resolve their differences. But now, Eron had Xias to worry about, due to his own decisions, and Brand Frontier was faced with the Alaskan Juggernaut once again. Shucks, You Get Stuck With Second Best? I'm a dreamer.... The crowd climbed to their feet as the soft J-pop beats of Megumi Hayashibara's "Kimi Sae Ireba" filled the arena once again, cheering for the arrival of Xias. He emerged from the back in more casual wear than his usual wrestling gear- a white shirt featuring characters from the anime Angelic Layer contrasted with one of those black-with-blue-flames Hawaiian shirts, worn over it with the middle open to show off the white shirt underneath. Dark blue khakis rounded out the casual look, a look the fans weren't exactly used to seeing from Xias. Despite the relaxed clothing, however, his demeanor was no more relaxed than usual, his normal stern, cold expression evident as he made his way down the rampway. He ignored the outstretched hands of the ringside fans as he made his way up the steps and into the ring, climbing through the ropes. He stopped a moment to look out at the sea of cheering fans, before reaching and grabbing a microphone. "I'm not here tonight to offer any witty blurbs or cocky promises." said Xias. "Everyone saw what went down on Ruahh~!!, and there's not much else left to say. I have very little left to say to Eron- well, very little that can't better be expressed by pounding my fists down into his face." The crowd, of course, roared its approval for this plan of action, but Xias just ignored them and waited for them to hush before continuing. "Before I get back to Eron, however, there is one thing I want to make clear to an old rival. Brand Frontier..." Again Xias had to pause due to the roar of the crowd- this time for the mention of The Paragon. "I didn't come out there last week to help you against Creed or Eron, Paragon, so don't get the wrong idea." Xias stated when the fans had quieted down. "I haven't forgotten our rivalry, and I doubt you have either. Someday in the future, we're going to have to get back into this ring and compete once again. Until then, just remember that I came out there to get my hands on Eron, not to help you. You probably realize this already, but I'm just making sure you and especially the rest of the world knows it." There was some faint booing from the crowd, which Xias also ignored. "Now then, with that out of the way, we turn back to Eron. As I already said, I have nothing left to say to you that can't better be expressed with some well-landed punches, hence why I'm out here right now. Eron, waiting another week-plus to get my hands on you again isn't something I wish to do, so I'm calling you out. If you have just a bit of honor or courage left in that broken body of yours, you'll respond to my req-" "Sellout" from Biohazard. The cavalry, it seemed, would be responding. cHEESE and egg NOG made a sudden appearance on the stage, each man dressed for action, unlike their counterpart in the ring before them. egg NOG wore his "sellout" t-shirt and wore it proudly, constantly pointing it out to fans that booed his arrival. cHEESE smirked as he watched his brother work the crowd, only to tap NOG on the shoulder and point to Xias in the ring. egg NOG looked to cHEESE and then to Xias in the ring, nodding. He reached behind his tights and pulled out a microphone of his own. "Blah, blah, blah." egg NOG began, moving his fingers up and down like a mouth, "You know Xias, you amaze me. Really. There you were, aimed to be one of those elite Rogues and you wussed out. Tucked tail and ran off to Manga-world. But that's ok, we rebounded, picked up a better player in Fly'. He knows the business and has an appreciation for it, unlike some people... who just so happen to be standing in the ring, today. We learned to get over you, shame you couldn't." The remark drew boos from the fans, not liking anything the Legion had to say. "Now as far as Eron goes... he can wait, Xias." Again, more boos, "You see, Xias, you've got another challenge awaiting you. Me. The Gee-Oh-Dub. Tag Team Wrestling's only Dynasty. We're calling you out, tag team style. You'll be given time to find a partner, of course, granted you still have a bridge left to cross. It's like this, Zee: You want Eron, you go through the Rogues." egg NOG snarled, his eyes narrowed and he stared a hole right through Xias. "So help me God of Wrestling, you're not clearing this hurdle." "Sellout" boomed once again, the Legion backing up the ramp slowly. cHEESE chuckling as he watched Xias stand calmly in the ring, he slid his thumb across his throat as he disappeared behind the curtain. Xias was all alone once again. Injured (Pride) Lance Knight knocked on Silver Fox's door a single time before pushing his way in. Fox looked up and nodded his head solemnly at Knight, who took the chair across the desk from Fox. The United States champion looked rather cross as he waited for his boss to reveal what he'd been called in for. Fox took a sip of his coffee, wishing it were something stronger, and eyed his secondary champion. "How are you feeling, champ?" The question was blunt, but it did have multiple meanings, and Fox was confident that Knight would pick up on it. "If you mean the knee, it's fine, and it has been since the Creed match," Knight snapped irritably, "But you knew that, yet you still forced me to slow down my schedule and take a week off." "So if you're asking how I'm feeling personally, then I guess you could say I've got a bit of pent-up frustration I'm ready to work off in the ring. Who do you have for me tonight someone for the US title, or can I get my hands on Kinkade again?" Silver Fox did his best to give Knight a stern look, but the look of desire, almost of physical need, on his face deflated Fox's attempts at running a hard line. When he spoke, his voice was nearly pleading with Knight to understand. "Listen, Lance, I know how bad you want to get a fair shot against Kinkade, but we've decided it's best to hold that off. So no matches against Kinkade for the time being." Knight looked defeated, if only for a moment, because his face brightened almost immediately. "You have to have the third round of the Wrestler of the Year done by a certain date to get the whole thing wrapped up by Legends, right?" Fox answered, unsure of where Knight's line of questioning was going. "Yeah, but I don't see what that has to do..." And then it became crystal-clear. "Then I do get Kinkade in a one-on-one, because we're slated to meet in the third round! You've got what, a week, to wrap up this round? If you won't give him to me tonight, I think I can wait that long." Knight stood up to leave, a triumphant smile on his face, and as he walked away, he paused a moment, looking over his shoulder at Silver Fox. "But if you can't deliver Kinkade to me tonight, at least gimme someone. I don't wanna lose my belt because of a lack of defenses." Meeting of minds. The Rogues continued their assault as the Alaskan Juggernaut slowly approached. They were not interested in what Creed wanted, they were doing their job, destroying myths. Creed took the gold strap that now was more a part of him than his own skin and slid it under the bottom rope. He then slowly climbed into the ring, very slowly, allowing the group of legend killers to finish what they started. That is exactly what they did. By the time Creed had called for a microphone Brand Frontier and Xias both lay in a bloody heap. Eron and the Legion of Dairy had moved to one side, ever cautious, biding their time, waiting for Vic to make the next move. "This right here is what I'm talking about..." Creed snarled. The audience, those not already doing so, jumped to their feet cheering. Cheering? Yes, cheering. Thanks to the United States Heavyweight Champion Lance Knight. Looking for a hero, someone, and anyone to help Frontier and Xias from these growing odds they reacted zealously. Lance Knight had accidentally walked by a monitor backstage and witnessed the carnage in the ring. With Creed arriving it could only get worse for his old Survivor mate. He had to make a stand. Creed never got the chance to finish his statement as Knight slid into the ring and met Creed toe to toe. The two champions traded wild swings. Each rocked the other back a few steps, both countering and coming back for more. Until Eron had had enough. He slammed into the back of Lance, grabbing him as he did and sent him sprawling to the mat with a belly to back suplex. cHEESE had climbed to the top rope and came sailing down, landing squarely on his face. Lance was down but not out and had managed to roll out of the way in time. Of the two fallen warriors Xias began to stir first as egg NOG went after Knight. Creed brushed himself off, as if wiping away the thought of Knight daring to touch him. He then walked slowly over to Frontier. Looking down at him with a sadistic grin. Xias was now to his feet and spied the situation Lance Knight was finding himself in. Eron, cHEESE and egg NOG had backed Knight into the corner and the Rogues had began to close in. The Undeniable One looked quickly to Frontier. Creed had taken the heel of his boot and dug it into the soft tissue at the base of Brand's neck. The act had shocked Frontier from his stupor and he strained against his arch-foe. Sizing up the situation Xias dove head first into the Rogues taking Eron down and through the ropes to the outside. Knight seized the moment and sprung from the corner sending egg NOG and cHEESE crashing to the mat via a double clothesline. The action seized the crowd and the cheered in unison. With everything happening so quickly it caught Creed by surprise as well. That moment of distraction was all Brand needed and with a mighty roar he grabbed Vic by the leg and pushed himself free, seething with anger. Creed stumbled back and was off balance just enough that Lance was able to send him tumbling over the top rope with a super kick to the side of his face. Frontier was now to his feet, standing side by side with Lance Knight. The Legion of Dairy saw their predicament and as Xias slid quickly back into the ring, they exited. Vic Creed snatched his championship from the apron, keeping anyone from touching HIS gold. Eron was back to his feet, a small trickle of blood drying under his nose. The Legion of Dairy stoically stood beside their leader. Brand Frontier, Lance Knight, and now Xias kept court in the ring. Knight and Frontier eyed Xias warily, just months ago these three men contested for the United States Championship in a heated feud, now they were shoulder to shoulder, seemingly standing against a tide of growing evil. Creed snarled, almost rabidly, he could see he wouldn't get to Frontier this night, he'd have to wait for Legends it seemed. Finally throwing his hands up in disgust, Vic waved off the 'heroes' as he backed up the ramp way. Eron, egg NOG and cHEESE too could see that the confrontation was pointless. With the World Champion nearing the top of the ramp the Rogues walked away as the fans cheered loudly. The adulation turned to hatred again, out of the blue, the fans knew something the heroes didn't. Xias was the first to feel the vibration of someone entering the ring behind them. He turned just in time to come face to face with an aluminum bat, wielded by none other than Kellen Kinkade. Xias slumped to the mat. Knight turned, as did Frontier. Both men however turned simply to give Kinkade a better target and one by one the Survivor II alumni crashed to the mat. Kellen Kinkade stood in the ring, seemingly victorious. Amidst the carnage Kellen smiled, good things come to those that wait. Good things indeed. Result: Kellen Kinkade? How about we call it a No Contest instead?
