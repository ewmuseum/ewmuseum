Sunday Slaughter, 11 March 2001

11 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Chris Universal looked over the documents on his desk, studying the results carefully. Seconds passed. MUD sneered at MUD, and MUD sneered back. At MUD's side, Toad joined in with a sneer of his own. Finally, Universal looked up. "You're right," he said. "Uh... which one of us?" asked a MUD. "He is." Universal pointed to the other MUD. "He is MUD. You," Universal said and pointed to the other MUD, "are not. As a matter of fact, I know exactly who you are." Universal paused. "You're Rottdog." MUD's eyes went wide. "What?!" "You heard me. Not only do your blood patterns not match those of the actual MUD, which we obtained from some of his past employers, but your patterns match exactly those of Rottdog. The fWo keeps records of all the random drug tests it performs, and your blood matches Rottdog's exactly." Rottdog shook his head. "That doesn't make sense, though. If I was Rottdog, wouldn't you have recognized me? We've been in the fWo at the same time before." Universal thought it over briefly, then shrugged. "Not really. I never paid attention to you, to be honest. Why would I have?" MUD chuckled. Rottdog thought it over briefly, and then his eyes went wide again. "You're right! It's all coming back now... after they released me from prison, I screwed around for a while before I finally got a job, as a janitor at some arena. Some federation... TW or something... they showed up, and I remember hearing about this guy named MUD." "Me," interjected MUD. "Right," continued Rottdog. "I must have become obsessed or something, because I cut my hair and started dressing just like him, and then I hired that midget you guys used to have to play Toad... and for whatever reason, I think I actually started thinking I was MUD!" "Fuckin' n-n-nutcase," mumbled Toad. "And then you hired me, and we all know what happened since." Rottdog's shoulders suddenly slumped. "I guess this means I'm fired, right? No way you'd want to keep me on the roster after this." Universal looked at Rottdog. "Actually, no. You're the Hardcore Champion, after all - you're under contract, and you're not fired." Rottdog beamed, and Universal gestured to MUD and Toad. "You can go." They did. Universal turned back to Rottdog. "As for you, you're free to go as well." "Thank you, Mister Universal. Thank you." Rottdog exited, leaving Universal behind, shaking his head at the whole situation. Rottdog whistled a happy tune, pleased with his sudden realization about his true identity. He turned a corner, and bumped into three unexpected faces. "Steel? Cairn?" Rottdog stared at the third person. "Mini-Rottdog? I thought you were dead." Judge Steel shook his head. "No, not really. We're glad to have you back, Kevin. Let me tell you how it all went down. Markus -" Steel pointed a thumb in the direction of Cairn, who grunted "- saw you at Body Count, and he recognized you. He found me and we tried to talk to you after the show, but you brushed us off. Remember?" Rottdog nodded. Mini-Rottdog jumped in. "I didn't, though. All you had connecting me to you was money, so it's not like I really gave a crap about you thinking you were MUD - not to mention the fact that I hadn't realized it. Anyway, I had always thought there was something familiar about you, but I wasn't sure what. I talked to Judge and Markus, and we all tried to talk to the real MUD, but he didn't return our calls. We were still trying to figure out how to get through to you when Carnage attacked me." Markus Cairn picked up the thread in his deep voice. "We visited him in the hospital the next day and hatched a plan. We'd fake his death. It'd make the news, and MUD would hear about it, and hopefully it'd get you back to being yourself again." Cairn grinned to Steel. "Good thing those doctors were so unscrupulous, huh?" Steel grinned back. "You know it!" They high-fived, and Cairn continued. "Carnage's second attack was just as unplanned as the first one, but it gave us a good platform for our idea. It served our needs perfectly, and here you are." Rottdog grinned. "You're damn straight, here I am. Hardcore Champion Rottdog... gotta love the sound of that. And, if I'm not mistaken, this means the most fearsome force in fWo history is back. N..." "X..." said Steel. "S!" shouted Cairn. "God we rule, don't we?" asked Mini-Rottdog. The foursome bashed forearms and walked off, as confident as they had ever been in their lives. Before they disappeared, Rottdogg slipped and fell. He then got up as if nothing had happened. NXS was truly back. Vengence vs. Carnage "Guilty Conscience" brings out the former Hardcore Champion, Carnage. Carnage is heavily bandaged from his recent stabbing at the hands of MUD... er, Rottdog... er, whoever. He crouches behind part of the entranceway set and awaits his opponent. "The Beast" hits and Vengence slowly begins to make his way down the entrance ramp. His brow lowers and, as he realizes that Carnage is not in the ring, the crazy Corteia pops out from behind the set, decking him in the back of the head with an elbow. Vengence goes rolling down the ramp and Carnage follows him down, all the while laying in stomps. Vengence climbs back to his feet and swings a right hand at Carnage but the smaller man ducks and pulls Vengence's legs forward, dropping him onto the ramp. Carnage begins to rain in wild lefts and rights until Vengence pushes him off, sending him tumbling back up the ramp. Both men make it back to their feet and Carnage charges at Vengence but Vengence sees him coming and takes him up and over with a huge back body drop. Carnage falls nearly seven feet before hitting the ramp hard and writhing in pain. Vengence brings him back up and leads him toward the ring. He rolls Carnage. Vengence grabs and folds a steel chair at ringside, tosses it in, then climbs up on the apron and steps over the top rope to enter the ring. Carnage makes it back to his feet but Vengence immediately whips him hard into the corner and follows him in with a vicious clothesline. The near seven footer whips Carnage into the opposite corner and, as he bounces out, grasps him by the neck, looking for a chokeslam onto the folded chair in the center of the ring. Carnage thinks fast, though, and kicks Vengence right in the little Vengies, doubling him over. Carnage quickly brings the monster down with an inverted DDT right onto the steel chair. Carnage drops down for a cover but Vengence manages a kick out at two and a half. Carnage drops a falling head butt onto the monster and then brings him up with a whip into the corner. Carnages moves back and takes off with a running! start, nailing a corner splash. Carnage follows up with a whip into the opposite corner and another splash. Vengence stumbles out of the corner and Carnage picks up the steel chair. He tosses the steel chair towards Vengence, who catches it. He attempts a spinning heel kick into the steel chair but Vengence manages to sidestep it and bring the chair crashing down on Carnage's skull. Carnage collapses to the mat and Vengence throttles him across the throat with the chair. Vengence drives the mat into Carnage's bandaged chest several times then sets the chair up in the center of the ring. He lifts the crazy Corteia to his feet and whips him into the ropes, lifting him up in a military press. He looks to drop Carnage onto the chair, putting him away, but Carnage manages to slide down out of the press and grab Vengence by the hair from behind, slamming his face across the seat of the steel chair. The chair dents and Vengence is busted open. Carnage lets out a gleeful "hAhaHAha!" at the sight of blood and slides the steel chair out of the ring. He mounts the top turnbuckle and flies off with an Asai moonsault, but Vengence gets his knees up. Both men are down and struggling to find their footing. Vengence is up first. As Carnage makes his way to his feet, the monster Vengence flies at him with a clothesline and both men go toppling over the top rope to ringside. Vengence is quickly to his feet. He lifts Carnage up and drops him throat first across the guardrail. Vengence follows up with a series of stomps before lifting Carnage up and bringing him up and right back down with a brainbuster right on the arena floor. Carnage is out but Vengence doesn't make the cover. Instead, he reaches under the ring and pulls out a table. He slides it into the ring and then rolls Carnage in. The monster lifts Carnage up and hooks him, driving him into the mat with a full nelson slam before setting the table up right in the center of the ring. Vengence sets Carnage up for a powerbomb through the table. Carnage, however, nails a low blow as Vengence places his head between his legs. Carnage ducks behind Vengence and brings him over with a snap German suplex--one half of the Maximum Carnage. Carnage eyes the table and pulls Vengence up, laying him across it. The crazy Corteia decides to take a great risk despite his serious injuries, much to the delight of the crowd. He slowly scales the top turnbuckle and, looking to complete the Maximum Carnage, flies off with an elbow drop. Vengence, however, rolls off of the table and Carnage's bandaged body goes crashing through it. Vengence rolls over to drape an arm across Carnage and the three count is tolled. Vengence casts a cold glare across the crowd as his name is announced as winner in this Hardcore match. The crowd begins to boo, and Vengence soaks it in... thinking that it's for him. It's not. Behind Vengence, a returning Kodiak Vic Creed slides into the ring. And he's not alone. In the hand of Creed is a chain - the very chain he was forced to wear during Hell Week. Creed gets up to his feet, lifts up the chain, and swings it. It connects with the back of Vengence's head with a sickening crack, and Vengence drops to the mat as if shot. The referee approaches Creed, but Creed swings the chain at him. It wraps around his leg, and Creed yanks on the chain. The referee falls to the mat, clutching his knee. Creed then frees the chain, brings up the semi-conscious Carnage, and wraps the chain around his neck. He then throws Carnage over the top rope, hanging him over the ropes. Carnage, quickly brought back to full consciousness, claws frantically at the chain around his neck. Creed simply pulls back harder, and Carnage quickly slips into unconsciousness. Creed then loosens the chain, and Carnage falls to the floor. Creed picks up the chain and swings it in an arc above his head, a scowl on his face, having successfully served notice that number one, he is back, and number two, he wants a piece of the Hardcore division. Slaughter, meanwhile, heads backstage... Result: Vengence by pinfall Big Surprise Coming "... and that is number 347 on my list of 500 reasons why ALF rules," said Mike Heftel. "Very... uh... interesting, Mike. Unfortunately, I've gotta go in a few minutes - I've got a match. Internet Title Tourney, against Black Panther. And you know I'm gonna win... after all, masked wrestlers suck!" said Black Quicksilver. Heftel blinked, twice. STUMP said nothing. Quicksilver and STUMP were walking alongside Heftel, as wrestlers sometimes have nothing better to do than walk aimlessly. The group continued walking for a few moments, then were roughly slammed into by Sphere, who had happened to have been walking on the opposite side of the hall and had decided to take it onto himself to "accidentally" "bump into" the STUMP Squad. "Well, if it isn't Heftel, Quicksilver, and STUMP... the SUCK Squad!" he said, and chuckled at his own joke. "Uh, Larry?" said Quicksilver. "That joke kinda sucked." "It did," agreed Heftel. "..." STUMP said. "Shut up!" yelled Sphere. "That's not the point. The point is that you suck, you bunch of pansies!" Quicksilver deftly countered the barb with one of his own. "At least I don't shop at Sears!" "Ooh, diss!" shouted Heftel, and attempted to high-five STUMP. STUMP didn't reciprocate. "You win this round, Quicksilver..." said Sphere angrily. "But at the pay-per-view, we'll see who's laughing after I unleash my big surprise! Bwa-ha-ha-ha! BWA-HA-HA-HA-HA-HA!" Sphere then shuffled off, still cackling maniacally under his breath. "What a wack-job," said Quicksilver. Heftel nodded. "..." said STUMP. Black Quicksilver vs. Black Panther Skid Row signals the entrance of one third of the STUMP Squad, Black Quicksilver. The fans cheer lightly but seem disappointed until BQ pauses on the entrance ramp and points back to the curtains where his fearless leader, STUMP, appears, being carried by Mike Heftel. The fans go crazy at the as STUMP and Heftel take their place at ringside but their mood changes when "Du Hast" pierces the arena. The Black Panther, Daniel Longshadow, makes his way down the ramp to a shower of boos and steps up onto the apron, casting a cold glare towards BQ before stepping over the top rope into the ring. The Panther simply stands in the middle of the ring awaiting an offensive move from BQ. BQ eyes the enormous Native American up and down hesitantly, gulping in anxiety and looking down to Stump for advice. He nods towards his leader and goes to lock up with the Panther. The Panther grins at this attempt, grabbing BQ by the arm and twisting it. BQ screams as the Panther lays two big clubbing forearms across his back, dropping him to his knees, before lifting the smaller man up by the arm for a short arm clothesline. BQ manages to duck the clothesline and slip behind the Panther. He quickly lands a dropkick to the back of the Panther's knee, staggering him, and a second high flipping dropkick right into his back, knocking him into the ropes. BQ slides under the Panther's legs and pulls him down to the mat. The referee slaps the mat twice before Panther kicks out and gets right back up to his feet, looking very angry. BQ climbs to the apron looking for an aerial move, but the Panther will have none of it. He grabs BQ by the head and drives a knee into his gut before bringing him back into the ring hard with a vertical suplex. The Panther is right back up, stomping on BQ. He brings BQ up and lifts him off his feet, dropping him hard across his knee with a front backbreaker. He leaves BQ there, stretching the STUMP Squad member's back across his knee with a painful submission. The ref checks for submission, but BQ refuses. The Panther brings BQ up out of the hold and hooks him before taking him over with a big double underhook suplex. He makes the cover but BQ kicks out at two, once again infuriating the big man. The Panther lifts up BQ with another vertical suplex, holding him up for over five seconds, and drops him down with force. He covers once again and once again BQ kicks out. The Panther roars in anger and signals for the end of the match. He brings BQ up and hooks him for the Killer Instinct, but as he lifts him up on his shoulder, the nimble BQ slides down his back. The Panther turns around and swings a right at BQ. BQ ducks and steps back, nailing a stiff Super-Quick Silver Kick into the Panther's chest. This only staggers the Panther, but BQ sees the opening and heads to the top rope. He flies off with a skytwister press, but the Panther regains his composure and snatches BQ out of the air in mid-flight. He skillfully sets BQ down, hooks BQ, lifts BQ, and drops BQ - Killer Instinct. The three count is academic from there, and the former Internet champion advances in the Internet Title Tournament courtesy of his devastating pumphandle slam finisher. The Panther casts a malicious glance towards Heftel and Stump at ringside as his hand is raised in victory. BQ rolls out of the ring and the STUMP Squad hightails it up the ramp as "Du Hast" plays and the Panther soaks in the boos. Result: Black Panther by pinfall The Roses In the official fWo weight room, Erik Kelly did a series of repetitions. The apparent weight on the bar would no doubt drop the jaw of any passersby. The sheer mass of the weights prevented one from determining exactly how much weight was on the bar, but it was quite clear that however much it was, it was truly astounding. Matt Loco was spotting, and calling out the numbers. "97! 98! 99! 100!" Loco helped Kelly set the bar back in position, and Kelly sat up and wiped the sweat from his brow. "Good session, Erik," said Loco. "Hellrazor doesn't stand a chance." Kelly took a moment to catch his breath, then smiled. "You got that right, Matt." Before he could continue, there was a knock at the door to the weight room. "I'll get that," said Loco. Kelly nodded. Loco walked to the door and opened it. Security chief John Willis stood in the doorway, a long thin box in his hands. He spoke without preamble. "Somebody left these on my desk," he said, and pointed to a tag on them. It read "Kelly". "I heard you guys would probably be in here, so I figured I'd bring them to you." Loco nodded sharply, growing impatient with the interruption. "Great, great," he said. "Hand it over." Willis did so, and spoke. "Say... you guys don't have any leads on the steel bar guy, do you?" "None." "Thanks anyway. See you la -" Loco shut the door in Willis' face and walked back towards Kelly. "What was it?" Kelly asked. Loco shrugged. He opened the box. Inside were three black roses. Kelly looked at them, then looked up at Loco. "OK," he said. "This is starting to get on my nerves." Boston Strangler vs. Sphere "Back to School" by the Deftones hits and Sphere walks out to a chorus of boos. He walked slowly, confidently down to the ring. The man who defeated Mike Heftel to have the chance to take on The Boston Strangler, stands still in the middle of the ring, thinking of ways he'll cheat himself a victory in this match. When O Fortuna hits the loudspeaker, fans can't restrain their cheers. The whole arena seems to erupt, as Boston Strangler makes his way to the ring, inching forward at a slow but steady pace. Sphere backs up, as Boston Strangler pulls himself to the apron. Holding on to the ropes, Strangler starts to get in. Imagine his surprise when Sphere runs full tilt at him, and sends him flying off the ramp and crashing down to the floor. A wide grin appears over Sphere's face, as he steps out of the ring and grabs one of his favorite things in life. A steel chair. Bashing it over Strangler's head once gives his grin new life. Twice makes it even wider. He doesn't get the chance to hit Stranger a third time, however, as a kick to his midsection sends him flying backwards, where he hits his head on the quarter-inch mat, which supposedly protects against impact on the concrete floor. It does nothing this time and Sphere's head feels like it hit the hard floor, which it basically did. Stranger manages to get back to his feet at the same time as Sphere, despite having been hit by a steel chair twice. Strangler walks behind Sphere and rolls him into the ring. Strangler than rolls himself into the ring. That turns out to be a mistake. Sphere, using every shortcut he can, starts kicking away at Strangler. Feeling he has the advantage, Sphere gets Strangler to his feet and sets him up for the superkick. Using his head, Strangler ducks the kick and catches Sphere's leg. Seeing the predicament he's now in, a fearful look hits Sphere's face. But, that's not all. Strangler's fist soon joins it there, after he throws Sphere's leg down hard. With Sphere on his back, Strangler attempts to make short work of him. Using Ric Flair's trademark move, the Figure-Four leglock, Strangler does his best to make Sphere tap out. It's not enough, though, and Sphere reaches the ropes and grips on with his middle finger. The referee notices that quickly and makes Strangler break the submission. Strangler goes after Sphere, who's still on the ground. Sphere chops him in the knee and knocks him off his feet. Sphere then gets back to his feet and starts attacking the knees of Strangler, trying to keep the technical artist off of his feet. Grabbing on to the ropes, to stop the assault against his knees, Strangler cringes in pain. Not only does Sphere not stop his attack, he seems to intensify it. The ref warns Sphere to play by the rules, but he'll have none of it. He wants to keep Strangler down and out for as long as it'll take him to win this match. The ref, seeing that his warnings were doing little good, steps between Sphere and the fallen Strangler. Sphere finally backs off and gives his opponent a little breathing room. Strangler hauls himself back up via the ropes, with a angry look on his face. When the ref finally gets out of his path, he takes Sphere down quickly, pounding away on his vicious opponent. Getting himself off of his wounded opponent, Strangler picks him up. Gutwrench powerbomb. Sphere's neck takes the brunt of the attack and he lies still. Strangler makes a cover, and the ref makes the card. A kickout at two...the ref's arm almost hit the mat for three, but Sphere kept himself in the match. Strangler's rage intensifies and he hits a German suplex. He goes for the cover again. Another kickout at two. Going back to work, Strangler hits a snap Suplex. He tries for the pin again. This time, Sphere landed a little too close to the ropes. He got his foot up at the count of two. Cursing himself, Strangler tries for another attack on his opponent. Sphere counters whatever thoughts Strangler might've had, with a low blow. Bending over in pain, Strangler nearly falls off his feet. But he manages to stay up. Sphere changes that quickly, however, this time hitting his patented superkick on Strangler. Sphere tries to knock the former Internet champion out of the running for the now vacated championship. The ref only gets to two, though. Strangler regains his footing and stands toe to toe with Sphere. They trade blows. Strangler gets the advantage and never loses it. Hitting a German suplex, he waits until Sphere gets back to his feet. Boston Massacre. Sphere can do nothing to counter the devastating move and screams in the arms of his offender. It's all over; the Boston Strangler has advanced in the Internet Title tournament. Result: Boston Strangler by submission Pissed Chris Universal leaned back in his leather chair and sighed. Chris Titan vs. Havok "Conflict" by Disturbed is heard crashing over the speakers and the fans react negatively when they see Chris Titan at the stage. Many of them curse and point fingers at him as he walks down the ramp, but the young star flips them off and enters the ring. The crowd begins to erupt into cheers as they hear "Awake" by Godsmack replace Disturbed. Havok stands tall at the entryway, holding his tag title in his right hand. Havok points at Titan - who is standing in the ring - and then sprints forward. A ringside attendant catches the tag belt that Havok throws to the side, and as Havok enters the ring, the bell sounds. Titan tries to start the match off with the upper hand, as he throws a wild clothesline. Havok sidesteps it and knees Titan in the gut, before picking him up and slamming him down to the mat. Havok drops an elbow across Titan's sternum and then brings him up. After a brief struggle, Havok manages to shoot Titan off to the ropes. Titan returns and ducks under a leapfrogging Havok. Titan turns around and is met with a swift kick to the gut, followed by a snap DDT. Havok hooks the leg, but Titan manages to kick out with a considerable amount of strength. Havok keeps the pressure on by bringing Titan back up and hitting him with a European uppercut. Titan stumbles back, and Havok laces in a hard chop that sends him straight into the corner. Seeing Titan vulnerable, Havok stomps at his stomach a few times, before mounting him and driving the fists down. The crowd counts along, but before the seventh punch could be laced in, Titan grabs Havok's tights and drops him face-first across the turnbuckle. Havok stumbles out of the corner, and Titan snaps him to the mat with a three-quarter-nelson neck-breaker. Titan reaches back for the cover, but the cover lacks a good lateral and as a result, Havok shoulders out. Havok gets to his knees and rubs his neck in pain. Titan takes a moment to trash talk in Havok's ear before kicking him right in the shoulder blade. Titan grabs Havok's arm and locks it tightly, sporadically taking a moment to drive his right hand into Havok's shoulder area. Havok manages to roll through the arm lock, kip up and then reverse it on Titan. Havok intensifies the move into a hammerlock, causing Titan to roar in pain. Titan reaches for the ropes, but finds himself too far. Taking the same arm that he reached for the ropes, Titan reels it back and elbows Havok a few times to the face. Titan comes off the ropes and tries swinging around with a DDT, but Havok drops him to the mat by both legs and then locks him into a sharpshooter. Titan hollers out in pain while grabbing at his hair as the referee asks him if he wants to give up. Titan responds negatively. He reaches out for the ropes, only being a few feet away. After another ten seconds of struggling, Titan finally manages to grasp the bottom rope, and Havok releases the hold. Havok waits on Titan to recover and as he does, he nails him with a spinning heel kick. Havok springs to the middle rope and tries for an Asai moonsault, but Titan gets his knees up to counterstrike. Havok stumbles about the ring doubled over, and falls into Titan's grasp. Titan picks him up in a reverse fireman's carry and tries for the Titanium, but Havok slips out and boots Titan in the gut. Havok locks him into a pump handle and calls out to the crowd, who responds in cheers. Havok gets Titan up halfway through the Final Impact, but Titan slides behind and pushes Havok. The referee gets in Havok's way and falls to the mat as a result of the collision. Titan swings a haymaker Havok's way, but Havok ducks under it and grabs Titan from behind. Havok lifts Titan in the air in a reverse suplex position, before crushing him on the mat with the Headache. Havok makes the cover, but no referee is present. Immediately, the East Coast Laws come out to capitalize on the situation. Juno slides in with a chair and tosses it over to his brother Kobi on the other end. Havok turns around to Kobi, but gets the chair thrown right into his face. Havok holds onto it, but when he turns around, Juno leaps up and thrusts his foot straight into the steel, causing it to smack Havok in the face. The Laws make their quick exit through the crowd, as the fans boo at the screw job. They begin to cheer however as Crusader comes from the back and slides into the ring. Titan gets up and his eyes widen as he sees his mortal enemy. Taking advantage of his shocked state of mind, Crusader kicks Titan in the gut and drops him to the mat with the Flipside. Havok manages to roll over for the pin as Crusader exits the ring. The referee returns to consciousness, and tolls the final three-count. Godsmack hits the speakers once again, as Havok's arm is raised in victory. Havok soaks in the cheers of the crowd, then follows in the path of Crusader to the back.
