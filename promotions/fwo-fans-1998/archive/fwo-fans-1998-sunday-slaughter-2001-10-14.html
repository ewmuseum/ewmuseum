Sunday Slaughter, 14 October 2001

Introduction "Hey... you can't come in here!" Neo turned, and Jim who ran the gate into the arena gasped. Nervously he ran his tongue over his front teeth, feeling the somewhat sharp metal braces there as he fretted. He had been with the fWo when Neo came in the first time unannounced... months ago that had been and Neo hadn't even been with the company. Now he certainly was, but orders were to keep him out. Without a medical release... and just looking at Neo's face made it clear there wasn't one of those... Neo couldn't wrestle. Given the events of the last two weeks, Jim could certainly understand Universal's decision to not want him around if he wasn't going to wrestle. Neo had turned, and Jim noted with mixed revulsion and horror that Neo's left eye had swollen so badly that the darkened purple skin oozed a yellowish white fluid that had smeared over the pupil. There were bandages -although no casts- and plenty of them, but the most gruesome thing Jim could see was that eye. It didn't look anywhere close to healthy. Jim wondered not for the first time what drove these men to show up in this kind of condition and want to fight. The pay was good... but there was a lot more to it than money if men were willing to show up beaten, broken and halfway to the grave just so they fight. Neo had turned away from Jim, apparently deciding to ignore his advice completely. Jim cleared his throat and tried again. "Sir? Sir... listen, you can't go in there. Orders from the president... you don't have medical clearance so you don't have permission to enter. Just... God, go home and fix that eye of yours!" Neo stopped, and Jim gulped. He turned, and Jim flinched again at the sight of Neo's face. He was running his tongue furiously over the braces on his front teeth in a nervous gesture that Jim wasn't even consciously aware of. Neo spoke, and it was his normal voice run through rough gravel. "I remember you. You're going to stop me from going in here?" Jim gulped. "I... I... yes. I mean, yes. That's my job and, oh no... come on..." Because Neo was advancing on him now, closing the distance between the two and Jim went from his nervous defiance to fear. Neo's face had broken into a smile, and Jim had an instant of disgust as he saw Neo's lips pull back from bloody gums and a missing tooth before his world exploded in bright crimson pain. Manipulations "Please... come on in." Ric Chronos' voice echoed through the locker room. Chronos was sitting down, and Primetime and Frenchie flanked him. Seconds passed, then three men entered the room. Erik Kelly. Boston Strangler. Eddy Fate. The threesome stood in front of Chronos, who gestured to them and smiled. "Good to see you guys." Kelly scowled, Fate frowned, and Strangler spoke. "We cannot say the same to you." "Yeah, I figured that would be the case, but I don't particularly give a damn. I called you three in here because I have something to tell you. You've all been booked tonight in the main event... against each other." "And what makes you think we'll fight each other?" asked Kelly. "We won't be manipulated," added Fate. "Oh, but you will be. You see, the winner gets a stipulation suited to them added to the main event at Halloween Hell. Paul... you're the master of the Boston Massacre. Nobody else in the match has a submission move they regularly use. Wouldn't it be in your favor if it were an I Quit match? And you, Erik. You have a physical advantage over all of us... you're the best brawler in the fed... wouldn't you love a cage match? Eddy. Eddy, Eddy, Eddy. You're the little fish, the odd man out, the 'which one of these does not belong'. You're at the biggest physical disadvantage, you don't have the big-match experience... wouldn't it help you if you could win any way you can? Say, in a No-DQ... No-Countout... Falls Count Anywhere match?" The three men were silent. "Yes, yes it would. And your reactions have told me all I need to know... I'll see you all later, huh?" The three men filed out of the room, their actions telling the story. Primetime smiled. "Great work, Ric." Frenchie nodded. "Yes, indeed... but I can't help but wonder if zis is such a good idea. Why give zem any kind of advantage at all?" Chronos just smiled. "I can still beat them, even with any of those stips. This weakens all of them too... it's an acceptable risk, my friend. And don't forget... we've got the book. We can do whatever the hell we want." "True, true... but what do you plan to do with that book? You've obviously got an idea..." "And I would bet zat idea involves us." "Yes, yes it does... all in good time, my friends. You'll see." Hellrazor (c) vs. Crucifix The sounds of Marilyn Manson's "The Beautiful People" blast over the arena's speakers, as former Hardcore champion Crucifix makes his way down the entranceway. Crucifix grabs a chair from the announcers' desk and throws it into the ring. As he slides in himself, the music is changed to Machinehead's "From This Day", and Hellrazor appears at the top of the ramp to a huge ovation, bringing with him Lamont's Hardcore Feline Carrying Case, a shovel and the Hardcore Championship belt. He sets his things down by the timekeeper's table before rushing to attack Crucifix. Hellrazor is met with a quick series of kicks as he slides in, but gets up and retaliates with a series of rights. He whips Crucifix into the ropes, and catches him with a stiff spinebuster. Hellrazor drops down for the early cover, and Crucifix is able to kick out after a one-count. Hellrazor grabs the chair and swings at the former Hardcore champion, but Crucifix ducks and delivers a palm thrust into Hellrazor's midsection. Hellrazor doubles over and Crucifix hits a scissors kick that sends Hellrazor down to the mat. Crucifix slides to the apron and slingshots himself off the top rope with a legdrop onto the back of Hellrazor's head, turns him over and covers. Hellrazor kicks out just after two, and Crucifix attempts to apply the Crucifix Crossface. Hellrazor elbows Crucifix in the face repeatedly before Crucifix can lock the hold in, and gets back up. He runs to the ropes and catches Crucifix with a lariat, and another just as Crucifix kips up. Hellrazor signals to the crowd for an elbow drop and heads to the corner. He climbs the turnbuckles, but takes too long and his caught by Crucifix with a springboard dropkick that sends him flying to the floor. As Hellrazor recovers, Crucifix dives over the ropes with a corkscrew plancha. Hellrazor manages to roll out of the way and Crucifix crashes onto the floor, narrowly missing the security barrier. He's lifted up by the Hardcore champion into a suplex position, and dropped headfirst with a brainbuster. With Crucifix down, Hellrazor heads to the timekeeper's table to get the shovel. Walking back to where Crucifix is lying, Hellrazor readies himself to deliver Crucifix the same punishment Neo received last Sunday. As Crucifix slowly gets up, Hellrazor looks slightly uneasy, continuously readjusting his grip on the shovel. Back up, Crucifix staggers around clutching his head, not quite sure what's going on. Hellrazor rears the shovel back, and swings down towards Crucifix's face. Still holding his temple, Crucifix looks up just in time to see the shovelhead coming at him... ...and Hellrazor stops the swing short. Hellrazor drops the shovel and looks down at Crucifix, who remains cringing, still expecting the impact. As Crucifix realizes he's been spared, he sees Hellrazor give him a quick nod of respect. However, as Crucifix returns the gesture, he's kicked in the stomach and taken over by a gutwrench suplex. Crucifix is quickly back to his feet, but is taken down again by a side Russian legsweep onto the arena floor. Hellrazor grabs the steel ringsteps, and brings them towards Crucifix. Crucifix is carried to the top of the steps, and Hellrazor sets him up for a piledriver. Hellrazor lifts him up, but Crucifix fights his way back down. Hellrazor tries again, and again Crucifix keeps himself from being lifted. Hellrazor tries a third time, and is backdropped off the steps to the arena floor by Crucifix. Crucifix flips off the steps with a shooting star press and makes the cover, but Hellrazor is able to kick out just before the three-count. Crucifix follows up with a heel kick spiked down into Hellrazor's midsection, and a standing senton splash. He covers again, and again Hellrazor powers out before the three. Realizing he's going to need another big move before he can regain the Hardcore championship, Crucifix gives Hellrazor another series of shots to the stomach before rolling back into the ring. He grabs the chair and ascends the turnbuckles, ready to unleash an aerial maneuver to the outside. Hellrazor is back on one knee just as Crucifix reaches the top, and leaps up to hit the ropes. Crucifix loses his balance and crotches himself in the corner, dropping his chair to the ground. Hellrazor sees his opportunity and climbs up as well, hooking Crucifix's head beneath his left arm. He hoists Crucifix up and falls backwards down to the arena floor, hitting a devastating No More Pain to the outside. Crucifix's head hits the ground with a wet thud, and he crumples on the safety mats. Hellrazor makes the academic cover, and retains his title over Crucifix. After taking a minute to regroup and grab Lamont, Hellrazor helps Crucifix back to his feet. The two begin walking back up the ramp as Slaughter rolls on. It was half question, half statement. Chris Universal frowned as the man doing the asking turned his head to one side and spat a giant wad of what looked disturbingly like meaty chew. The man looked like something right out of Deliverance. He wore a leather bushman hat, one side pinned up with a bone claw. A denim jacket was covering his shoulders, or at least doing a decent impression of it. The cloth was badly ripped and torn in areas, and Chris could see the greasy skin beneath. His pants were leather, but it was a dirty, smudged kind of leather and not as elegant as what you normally see. The man had a pudgy, mean little face and was currently looking at Chris out of one squinted eye. "You der Univarsal?" Chris blinked. "Yes... er... yes. And..." He looked down at the glob of chew on the floor and frowned. "And you are?" The man spat again, earning another grimace from Chris. He extended his hand and nodded. "Dis here Remy Biodeaux, Univarsal. Dat China, dat some kind creole lait dis Remy. Say da job for dis Remy. Say da job be dis lagniappe of all da jobs in dis parish. So... whet job be y'at?" Chris Universal blinked. "I... what?" He extended his hand and shook the hand of the stranger with confusion. He felt as though he had just been answered in another language, one that consisted of a lot of spitting and nonsense. Remy grinned, chewed, and spat. Chris began to wonder just how much chew he had in there. He nodded back behind him toward a collection of several crates. Universal looked over toward them for the first time and his brow wrinkled in confusion. "Are those..." Remy turned and nodded. "Gasoline. Dat sure be gasoline cher. Gasoline for da firerotechnics yeah?" He went into his pocket and pulled out a slip of paper and handed it to Universal. Still confused, Chris read it aloud. "Dear Mr. Universal, this man comes highly recommended as one of the best pyrotechnic masters in the industry. I've told him to purchase some equipment on the fWo account so he can get started. I think you will really like his work and I've gone ahead and issued him the standard two year contract. Touma." Chris Universal looked up from the paper. "Two... two years?" Remy spat with a smile. "Duex years. Where da firerotechnics go, eh?" "Pyro..." Chris Universal looked over at the gasoline again. "You mean this crates of gasoline?" Remy nodded. "And da matches." "Matches... you mean..." Chris Universal began to look alarmed. "You mean that your pyrotechnics display consists of gasoline and matches?" Remy laughed, "Na dere, na. You combine dem der hoss, you combine dem for da fire. Da fire..." Remy got a dreamy, distant expression on his Cajun face. Universal snapped his fingers in front of him and waved, but Remy seemed to be lost in his own little world. Universal sighed. "Great. I've got a pyromaniac hillbilly as my new pyrotechnics expert. I can't wait to see who shows up next." Donaven Winters vs. Monte Burns The boisterous crowd here in St. Louis begins to sound off with a mixture of boos and cheers as "Somewhat Damaged" by Nine Inch Nails sounds throughout the arena. "The Fallen Angel" Donaven Winters walks out from the back, possibly a bit surprised by the crowd's reaction. Nonetheless, Winters makes his way down the ramp, looking out at the crowd with his game face on. And not that "I'm ready to defeat Lord Zoob and rescue Princess Helena" kind of game face either. This was the "I'm ready to rip Burns limb from limb" game face. As Winters and his game face step into the ring, "Nobody's Real" by Powerman 5000 begins to play, bringing out the current fWo Cruiserweight Champion, "The Golden Child" Monte Burns. Unlike Winters, the crowd mercilessly boos Burns, pelting him with a mixture of cups, chips, and slander. Despite all of this, Burns slowly walks down the ramp, eyeing Winters inside of the ring. Both men knew that with their Best of Seven Series knotted at one win apiece, this match would provide one of them with the lead and much-needed momentum. As he hands the belt over to the official, Burns tries to get in an early shot by going for a clothesline on Winters. But Donaven is ready for this and ducks the clothesline, instead hitting Monte with a back heel kick. Winters follows that up by backing Burns into the corner with a series of knife-edge chops to the chest. The ref tries to restrain Donaven a bit, but Winters brushes him to the side. Winters then whips Monte in the far corner, with Monte's back crashing hard into the turnbuckles. Donaven tries to follow that up by rushing the corner, but Burns is able to get a boot up to stop the momentum. As Winters stumbles back a bit, Burns climbs up to the middle turnbuckle and leaps off with a bulldog on his opponent. He makes a quick cover, but the ref only makes it to two. Before Donaven can get back up, Burns stomps away with kicks to the back of his head. Finally, he lets Winters back to his feet, only to send him crashing back to the mat with a kick to the stomach and a DDT. Another cover only gets another two count for Monte. After yelling at the official for what he considered a slow count, Burns looked back to Winters, who was now on one knee, and slapped him hard across the face. Winters responded in kind with a right to Monte's jaw that sent Burns back a step. Burns tried to get in a punch of his own, but Winters blocked it as he got back to both feet and kicked Monte hard in the stomach. With Burns doubled over, Winters quickly lifted Monte up in the air and drove him down hard to the mat with a stalling brainbuster. Donaven made a cover, but Burns was able to make the kickout in time. Winters slowly pulled Monte to his feet and backed him into the ropes with a couple of kicks before whipping him across the ring. As Burns bounces off, Winters attempts to hit a flying lariat, but Monte manages to duck that and bounce off the opposite side. This time Winters is able to get his hands on Burns, dropping him hard across his knee with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Winters starts to make the cover, but realizes how close they are to the ropes and pulls Monte closer to the middle of the ring. He goes for the cover then, however Burns is able to counter that into a small package of his own. After a two count, Burns lunges at Donaven, only to have Winters put him down with a drop toe hold followed by a le magistral pin attempt. At two, Burns reverses it into a cover of his own, himself garnering a two count. As Donaven kicks out of that, the two men get to their feet a few steps away from each other, their eyes meeting as the crowd buzzes after the heartstopping nearfalls. Both men charge and drop each other back down with a double clothesline. The ref stands over both men... 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... 7... Burns gets to one knee... 8... Winters is pulling himself up by the ropes... 9... both men are now standing, with Winters up against the ropes and Burns in the center of the ring. Burns charges hard at Winters, but "The Fallen Angel" looks up just in time to back drop Burns over the top rope and crashing down to the outside. With Burns down, Winters slowly climbs to the top of the nearby corner, as the crowd begins to get to its feet in anticipation. Winters looks down at Burns for a few seconds, then leaps off with a Firebird splash that sends the crowd into a frenzy. Neither man seems to be moving as the referee begins to count... 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... 7... Winters grabs up to the apron... 8... Burns starts to pull himself up with the railing... 9... Winters slides in to break the count just as Burns rolls in as well. Winters desperately reaches over and covers Burns, but "The Golden Child" gets a shoulder up just before the three count. Donaven slams his hand to the mat in frustration, as he struggles to his feet and pulls Monte up by his golden hair as well. Donaven grabbed Burns and started to go for a sweeping uranage, but Burns elbowed his way out of the move and kicked Winters in the gut. Burns then looked for a double-arm DDT, but again Winters was there to counter, turning it into a northern lights suplex instead. Winters managed a two count with a bridge, before Monte kicked out and the two quickly got to their feet again. This time Monte swung for Donaven's head, but Winters ducked and hooked Burns up for another of his signature moves, the Waste, which had worked in their last match. But Burns felt it coming and slipped out of the hold before instantly dropping Winters to the mat with a side russian legsweep. After swiftly heading to the top turnbuckle, Burns leaped off...and connected with The Establishment on the fallen Winters. Three seconds later, Burns had the victory and a 2-1 series lead. As soon as his hand was raised in triumph, Monte rolled to the outside and slowly made his way up the ramp. Back in the ring, Winters got to his feet and glared hard at his enemy before exiting the ring as well. Result: Monte Burns by pinfall (Burns up 2-1) Behold The Power Of... With the thoughts of his loss to Monte Burns still fresh in his mind, Donaven Winters threw open the door to his locker room and walked inside. As he let out a deep sigh, Winters sat down on the bench, contemplating what had just occurred. A few moments later, Winters looked up and noticed a large tray of cheese on a table against the far well. Confused, Winters slowly walked over to the table and looked over the cheese before he saw it: Another note. Donaven reached out and grabbed the note, beginning to read. "Here's a little cheese, " whispered Donaven softly to himself as he read, "to go with all of the 'whining' you have been doing lately...Signed...." As he read the final few words, Winters crumpled the note up in his hand and looked around the room, clearly bothered once again. After a few moments of holding his feelings inside, Winters finalled lashed out, viciously knocking the tray of cheese off the table and to the floor below. Then, he walked back over to the bench, took his seat, and ran his hand through his dark hair. Vic Creed (c) vs. Tempest Slaughter returns for an all-important fans Wrestling organization Internet Title defense, as the champion 'Kodiak' Vic Creed was booked by his colleagues 3vil to take on Tempest. Tempest is introduced first by Matt Panzer, and she is greeted with a mixed reaction of cheers, boos and sympathy. The incidents over the past few weeks led fans to believe that Tempest had been enduring a struggle, and was torn in different directions. As her music died down, Tempest stood in the ring, prepared to do battle with a man who outweighed her, was taller, and had no problems inflicting pain upon his victim... be it male or female. The sounds of 'Walk' by Pantrea erupt throughout the Kiel Center, and the fans react with boos and jeers for the "Main Man". The Internet Title slumped over his shoulder, Creed begins to size Tempest up as he reaches the ring. The champion climbs into the ring, staring at the challenger with a smirk, as she asks for a microphone from ringside. Tempest waits for the Alaskan Juggernaut's music to fade, before she approaches Creed. Never one to let a lady talk first, Creed grabs the microphone. "Now, I know what you're thinking. You're thinking that... well 'I'm screwed'. I know, I know... you're in the ring with the most deadliest arms in the world. The purest three hundred and forty eight pounds this side of a negative piss test. The biggest dog of the yard. Well... have no fear. This is like an open pass for 3vil to come and wreak havoc, which is exactly what they do. 3vil rushes to the ring. Frenchie and Primetime slide in and begin trading blows with Erik Kelly. Meanwhile, the Strangler is stumbling about the ring, dazed, when he bumps into Eddy Fate, just getting up. Instinctively, Strangler wraps Fate up in the Massacre. As he does so, Ric Chronos hops onto the apron with a chair. Strangler sees him and releases Fate, then walks over. He dodges a chairshot from Chronos and tries a shoulder to the gut that nails Chronos, causing him to drop the chair into the ring. Chronos shakes it off and leaps over the still-bending Strangler, using Strangler's back to flip onto his feet. Chronos quickly grabs the chair. As Strangler turns around, Chronos blasts him with a chairshot, then clocks him with another. He positions the chair, then brings Strangler up and spikes him with a Figure-Four DDT on the chair. Chronos then wakes up the referee as Frenchie and Primetime gain the edge on Kelly and knock him through the ropes, then leave the ring. Chronos then hops from the ring to join his mates and see what develops. Eddy Fate, who missed Chronos' attack, sees his opportunity with Strangler down. Never one to look a gift horse in the mouth, crawls over. He drapes an arm over Strangler, and the referee makes the count. One. Two. Three. Erik Kelly leaps to break it up, but it's too late. Eddy Fate wins, and the main event of Halloween Hell is now No-DQ, No-Countout, Falls Count Anywhere. 3vil smiles collectively, plenty OK with this result. With the match over, Fate gets up and stands side-by-side with Kelly. The two grab the Strangler and help him to his feet as well. Though Strangler is bleeding from the forehead, he's still aware enough to see 3vil. The two trios begin to trade trash talk, shouting and pointing at each other in a threatening manner as Slaughter goes off the air...
