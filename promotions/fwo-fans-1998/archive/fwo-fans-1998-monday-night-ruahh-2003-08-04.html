Instead of covering, Knight went to him and turned him over. Knight then hooked Slugger by the legs and took him up and over with the Knightmare 2, an ocean cyclone suplex. In the crowd, one solitary, angry Canadian screamed, then buried his head in his hands. Inside the ring, away from the relatively obscure in-joke, Knight covered Slugger with alacrity and hooked the leg. One. Two. Kickout. Slugger managed to kick out, and the crowd cheered. Knight was undaunted, and wasted no time in bringing the WGA to his feet once more. He went behind Slugger and hooked him by the arms, preparing to execute a tiger suplex. He took Slugger up... and over, then held on for the pinning predicament. One. Two. Kickout. The crowd cheered again at Slugger's resiliency, and also at Knight's unflagging determination. Deciding to wear Slugger down a little more before going for another pinfall, Knight brought Slugger up and whipped him into the ropes. As Slugger came off, Knight hooked and, then spun and drove him to the mat with a spinebuster, planting Slugger - almost literally. Knight brought Slugger to his feet again and prepared to execute another devastating maneuver. He grabbed Slugger and wrapped him up in a funky position, preparing to execute the move known as the Knightmare 1. Knight paused, waiting to counter any attempt Slugger might make to escape, then saw there would be none. He set to execute the move - And found out the hard way that Slugger was just playing possum. It was a mule kick, that most simple and most devastating of maneuvers. Knight doubled over in pain, and staggered away from Slugger. Slugger stood up and shook his head, clearing the cobwebs. Lance Knight turned toward him again. Slugger leapt into the air. He double-underhooked the head of Knight. And drove him into the mat with the Curveball. The crowd exploded with cheers. Slugger covered, and the referee dropped down to make the count. One... Two... Th - Mitch Wilson yanked the referee from the ring. The crowd exploded with boos, and Ice cursed angrily, unable to believe that Wilson had been able to slip by him after all. The referee shouted at Wilson, who smugly smiled back at him. Ice made his way to the other side of the ring to physically remove Wilson. Slugger, who had seen the goings-on, rolled from the ring. He stepped between Wilson and the referee, completely blocking the view of the ref, and pulled back a fist to strike. Wilson heard footsteps. Instinctively, he ducked his head. Ice's right foot, moving at lightning velocity, crashed into the face of Scott Slugger, who was knocked backwards, continuing to keep the referee out of view. Wilson then grabbed Ice and, before Ice could react, drove him into the ground with the Headliner. Wilson then grabbed Slugger and rolled him back into the ring, where Lance Knight, who had made his way back up, looked on with disapproval. The referee slowly got to his feet and rolled in himself. Inside the ring, Lance Knight frowned mightily, not pleased at all at this turn of events. He had only seen the tail end of things, but he wanted a clean victory, nothing else. He sighed, then, without other options, turned to Slugger, who was getting to his feet, and grabbed him. He lifted Slugger up, and drove him to the mat with the Knightfall. Knight sighed again, and made the cover. One. Two. Three. Lance Knight's hand was raised in victory, and he took the US Title belt as it was handed to him. He took another look at Wilson, who was standing at ringside and grinning, then began to back up the ramp, glaring at Wilson all the while. Meanwhile, Wilson set about to finding himself a microphone... Result: Lance Knight by pinfall Revealed? Mitch Wilson found the elusive microphone and took a few steps back from the ring, ending up about halfway up the entrance ramp, a safe distance away from the recovering Slugger and Ice. He waited for the booing of the crowd to reach a level that he could at least be heard over, then raised the microphone. "Scott... Tony... earlier tonight, I told you that I was going to reveal my tag team partner tonight. I'm a man of my word. Ladies and gentlemen... the footage!" The FanTron went a darker black, and the fans heard a pair of voices speaking in apparent voice-over. "Did you see that movie... 'Night of the Living Dead'?" "Is that the one where all the bodies come to life and start eating all the people?" "Yeah. Did you know... that was based on a true story?" The screen was suddenly filled up by a the huge face of a butt-ass-fugly, gooey, dripping, decaying, slimy, zombie. "BRAINS!" it shouted. Mitch Wilson cackled on the entrance ramp. "That's right... my partner is AUSTIN DAVIS!" The crowd booed wildly at this announcement, having been duped by Wilson into believing that he actually would reveal his partner, and upset to the point that even these clips from an all-time classic movie could not pacify them. Inside the ring, Slugger and Ice scowled, highly unamused. "Look at Davis up there! He's never looked better! I bet he could eat... 384 brains!" Wilson laughed at his own joke. On the FanTron, a zombie descended onto a paramedic, eating his brains. "Send... more... paramedics!" the zombie then croaked into a communicator device. "Look at those moves! Austin Davis is back and better than ever! He looks - BAAD ASS! See, that's with two 'a's, because it's an acronym, y'know..." Inside the ring, Slugger and Ice had had enough, and they stepped towards the ropes to deal out some pain to Wilson - - and the lights went out. It was pitch-black, so dark that even the fans by the ring could not make out what was going on. There were sounds of a scuffle... a moment of silence... and the lights came back up. Scott Slugger and Ice lay unconscious in the middle of the ring. The fans booed deafeningly, and Mitch Wilson laughed his evil laugh. "Looks like I got ya again, guys! Oh man... are you two a couple of suckers or what? Any time... any place... we can get you whenever... we... want!" Wilson continued to laugh wildly, his deep, rolling laugh louder than even the deafening boos of the crowd...
