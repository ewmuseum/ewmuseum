The framework for this match had been laid after Legends, when Mitch Wilson cut a promo wearing what he called the Real Internet Title, saying that the belt won the previous night by "Superstar" Vince Jacobs was a fraud, with no connection to the incredibly prestigious history of the Internet Title. Jacobs, naturally, took exception. On the following show, a new problem arose - a Disney employee named James Sunderland, who had been sent to order Silver Fox to retire the Internet Title - both of them. Fox refused, and Disney acquiesed. While Fox made moves to strengthen the division, requesting the help of Deacon, Sunderland fumed, and plotted. Unbeknownst to Fox or the other participants in the division, Sunderland struck a deal with somebody in the fWo to sell out to him, in order to accomplish his goal of killing the Internet Title. Though Fox did not know Sunderland's specific plans, he knew that Sunderland was planning something, and that it would begin at Mob Rules. For protection, he convinced Scott Slugger to serve as a special referee for tonight's match, to keep order and to prevent Sunderland from accomplishing his goals. In return, Slugger would get a shot at whoever won the Internet Title Unification Match on the next Ruahh. It was clear that tonight, something would happen that would change the Internet Division. The only question was... when?
