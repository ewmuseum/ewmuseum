Introduction Somewhere high up in one of the Cow Palace's luxury boxes in San Francisco a celebration was taking place. Chris Universal waved the cart of steaks and wine the attendants were wheeling into the room and turned to smile at the occupants of the room. Megan Dawn lounged in one of the plush sofas, wearing a low cut dress and sultry smile. Her eyes glittered in the soft light and shifted slightly as Universal filled her wine glass with a rich Merlot. Ice leaned back in the couch, the fWo Heavyweight Championship slung over his shoulder and a bottle of Dom in one large fist. There was an air of celebration in the box suite despite the cage match that they knew was coming later in the night as the Dynasty enjoyed themselves in the glow of their victory on Sunday Slaughter. Scott Slugger came into the room, and was the only member of the group dressed in his wrestling gear. He tapped a folk against a bottle to get the attention of the room, and then held up a wine glass as he began to make his toast. "Gentlemen... some sense of justification has come tonight, as once again the most powerful stable in all of professional wrestling once again stands at the top with the championship once again ours. We all wish Lance Star was here to see this, but we are sure that he will be back very soon... and then it's time to add the Tag Team titles to our legacy. Last Sunday night was a strike in the right direction for us, and now it stands within our grasp to make tonight an even bigger milestone in the careers of the fWo." Universal nodded and exclaimed, "Cheers!" as Ice smiled wide and patted the fWo Championship over his shoulder. Scott Slugger nodded to him, and then continued with his speech. "Now what could be better than what went down on Sunday Night as Ice once again regained the fWo Heavyweight gold? Yes, kicking David Spencer's ass will be sweet for us... but I say we do something even better... even bigger. Yes, I'm talking about Ice giving me a title shot later on tonight, passing the torch onto Scott Slugger, and going into the Millennium as the four time fWo Champion of the World! It will be a celebration of Slugger! Now, who's with me?" Scott Slugger looked around the room, but his excitement was not reflected on the faces of the other wrestlers in the room. Ice and Chris Universal exchanged a glance, and an understanding passed between them. Ice cleared his throat and spoke first. "Scott... man, that sounds petty nice, but I think I'll just hold onto the belt for now. But that doesn't mean you shouldn't go for some other gold. Like... the Internet title maybe. Or maybe the Cruiserweight belt." Chris Universal nodded. "Yeah, Scott that's right... you can catch up with Slugger and hold all the titles or something. Wouldn't that be pretty cool?" Scott Slugger looked between Ice and Chris Universal with a dazed expression on his face. "No celebration of Slugger?" Ice and Universal shook their heads slowly, and Scott Slugger slumped, resigned. "Okay... yeah, that's okay. Maybe the Internet title, yeah. Uh... I'm going out for a walk... I'll catch up with you guys later." Scott Slugger walked out of the room and Ice and Universal watched him shut the door behind him. Ice turned to Universal. "Problem?" Universal shook his head in the negative. Finally, with Spencer nearly unconscious, Ice brings him up, and slaps a hand around his throat, causing Spencer to snap his head back and spit as all people about to be chokeslammed do. Ice then lifts the man known as Death up, and slams him hard to the mat, but again hurts his own knee. Ice slumps down in pain, but again simply strengthens his resolve and, face straining from the pain, gets back to his feet. He brings Spencer back up as well, and goes for a final chokeslam before leaving the cage. However, Spencer, always the crafty veteran, struck with a testicular kick, causing Ice to double over, and then went behind the big man and dropped him with a kneebreaker, blasting the injury of Ice. Spencer then collapses to his knees and begins to crawl to the door. Finally, he reaches it, and smiles evilly as he realizes he's about to become a three-time fWo World Champion. He reaches to the ropes to help pull himself out...but suddenly the door is violently slammed in his face, causing him to fall backwards, nearly unconscious. We now see that Scott Collins, with broken and bloody handcuffs hanging from his wrists, has slammed the door in the face of Spencer. Collins laughs evilly, and in the ring Ice has begun to stir. Ice sees his opponent down, and, resolving to win the match, valiantly makes it back to his feet with the help of the ropes. Instead of heading out the door like most people would do, Ice begins to climb the cage, enduring unimaginable pain in his knee. Finally, Ice reaches the top, and slashes his thumb across his throat. Then, instead of turning out and winning it, Ice turns and faces the ring, standing gingerly on top of the cage. There, in front of a sold-out Cow Palace in San Francisco, Ice flies into Warfare history by leaping off the cage with a literally Death-defying flying headbutt from the top of the structure. Unfortunately for him...Spencer moves. Ice crashes into the mat, and is completely out. David Spencer, realizing that if he tried the door again, Collins would slam it again in his face, begins also to climb out of the cage. He makes it to the top, and begins the slow descent to the bottom. However, below him lurks Scott Collins, who pulls a chain out of his trusty pants and wraps it around the foot of Spencer before he realizes what's going on. Spencer tries futilely to drop, then to kick at Collins, but Collins dodges and soon has tied both feet of Spencer to the cage. Collins hits him low, and Spencer unclenches his hands from the cage, in the process falling and landing upside down, banging his head on the cage on the way. He stretches to reach the floor with his hands, but cannot quite do it. Collins kicks him viciously in the head, and walks towards the ring. He opens the door and rolls in, and physically drags Ice from the ring, giving him the hard-fought victory over Spencer. Collins dumps Ice outside the cage and begins to walk back to Spencer. Ice has slowly begun to recover by now, and once he realizes that Collins helped him from the cage, follows him, limping. With Collins unsuspecting, Ice clobbers him from behind with a forearm, and shoots him the bird. Collins, focused on Spencer, briefly goes down, but ignores it in favor of getting up, grabbing a chair, and hammering Spencer about many body parts. Ice, meanwhile, gathers up his title and begins to walk to the back, only to be met by the Flying Frenchie, who, hiding in the crowd, has performed a springboard lariat using the guardrail as a springboard. He berates the downed Ice, causing Scott Slugger and Chris Universal to run to the ring as well and attack Frenchie. Slugger and Universal hammer TFF, who finally decides he's had enough, and rolls out and backs away, pointing at the Dynasty. Slugger and Universal attend to Ice, with Slugger gathering up his title for him. Meanwhile Frenchie, not looking behind him, walks right into a Boston Massacre from behind from the Boston Strangler, who had been lurking on the ramp. This is quickly broken up as Courage hammers the Strangler from behind with a chair. This is cheered on by Primetime, following Courage out. Courage, Frenchie, and Primetime, wearing a great deal of rib tape, begin to stomp down the Strangler. Warfare goes off the air on this chaotic scene, with Ice still the champion as the cameras fade out... Result: Ice
