Sunday Slaughter, 10 September 2000

10 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The 9.10.00 edition of fWo Sunday Night Slaughter exploded onto your television screen inside the arena, with the usual fireworks and screaming music echoing throughout the arena. The fWo continued it's trek through California, which ends at Unstable on September 23rd in Sacramento. The fans appeared ready as they had just gotten done wrestling some dark matches, which featured the likes of Robert Jensen & Frank Snow. The real action was about to begin, when "Snap" by Slipknot roared over the arena sound system. Not recognizing the theme, the fans didn't know whether to boo or cheer. Five seconds later, they did. The most wanted man on the fWo roster walked out with a duffle bag, and dropped it on the stage. Primetime raised his arms as the crowd booed him relentlessly. Primetime walked down the stage and rolled into the ring as some fireworks went off overhead, before he received a microphone from a ringside attendant. His theme slowly quieted, and Primetime slightly tapped the microphone nonchalantly, in order to see if it was in working condition. With that out of the way, Primetime immediately brought up why he was out there in front of all the fans from San Jose. "I'm not going to come out here and piss and moan, because I'm not like that. You see, I'm going to come out here to boast... and brag... about the wonderful things I've done over the Summer. It all began in June... when Havok was taking up space on the roster. Snap. He's now in a hospital. Then, July came... and Matt Douglas was taking up even more space on the roster than Havok. Snap. He was in a hospital, but he must've taken some viagra or some crap because he came back nearly a month ago. Not to worry... he and I cross paths again, next time his new home won't be a hospital, it'll be six feet under. And then... at the Ryan Moore Memorial Show... I took out the supposed "Man Of The Hour", Ric Chronos, and he hasn't returned since... and ten days after that, John Skyler was another casualty. Snap. Crackle. Pop. And for those of you who are intellectually challenged, I'm not talking about that damn cereal Rice Krispies. Moving right along, we proceed to last week, where I interrupted a number one contender's match between Rain and Eron The Talentless. The winner was supposed to be given a World Title shot this week against Scott Slugger, but Primetime put an abrupt end to that. Now, I don't see that as a bad thing, because Rain & Eron suck... and frankly, they were boring you all to death out here, ratings were dropping because everyone was changing the channels to watch something just as boring but at least it gave you something back, you know, 60 Minutes or whatever that crap is called. The way I see it, I did a favor for everyone, by putting those two pieces of garbage out to the curb... and the way I see it, I never got thanked. So, I'm calling James Armstrong out here, so he can thank me in person." Primetime impatiently paced in a circle around the ring, waiting for the acting owner of the fWo to arrive. Thirty seconds passed before Primetime again told him to get out there, and finally, "Pretty Fly For A White Guy" by the Offspring hit the speakers. The fans reacted with laughter, as Armstrong was surely anything but. Armstrong stolled down the stage with a confused look on his face; entering the ring with caution as Primetime waited for him to be standing face to face with him. Armstrong received his own microphone, before Primetime addressed him. "James, you... busy?" Armstrong scratched his head. "I am the acting owner of the company you work for, and you want to know if I'm busy?" Armstrong looked around the ring. "Brian, I'm always busy, and when I'm not busy, I'm gettin' busy with the blackjack and hookers... on second thought, screw the blackjack!" The fans laughed, but Primetime didn't find it amusing. "You find this funny? You find this funny? I don't find not being thanked for what I did last week humorous at all..." Armstrong stopped him. "You want to be thanked? You want to be given something in return?" Primetime nodded. Armstrong looked around in confusion, before offering PT... "Bun cake? Do you like bun cake? Because we have plenty backstage..." Primetime again didn't find it funny. He grabbed James by the collar, and pulled him close to him. "Let me tell you something, you bald headed little oaf! You want a laugh? You want to be funny? I'll put you in the [censored] hospital, your family will find that real funny... no, let me tell you what I want, James. I don't want any [censored] buncake. The only thing right now that would please me is a shot with the fWo World Champion, Scott Slugger, and I want it tonight. That... Armstrong, is what I want!" Primetime continued to hold Armstrong in his clutches, when Armstrong finally gave in. "Okay, you get it tonight... you get the match." Primetime smiled. "That's right, James. That's right." Primetime pushed Armstrong backwards, and Armstrong smacked the mat with a thud. Clutching his neck and back, Primetime laughed, before "Back In Black" by AC/DC hit the speakers. The fWo World Champion walked out onto the ramp, before charging down to ringside. Primetime met him out there, and the two exchanged punches. Armstrong got back to his feet slowly, before grabbing the microphone, as PT had the advantage and was ready to powerbomb Slugger on the cement floor, before Armstrong stopped him. "You know what, screw you, you're not facing Slugger tonight!" Primetime looked incensed as the crowd responded with cheers. Slugger backdropped out of the Primetime Bomb attempt, before retrieving his title and rolling into the ring where he joined Armstrong. Primetime got up and was about to enter, but Slugger swung his belt loosely in his direction. Primetime thought better of it, and instead grabbed the ring steps and tossed them flying into the ring, narrowly missing either the WGA or the acting owner. Security hit the ring and escorted Primetime backstage, who was very angry and resisted it as much as he could. Back in the ring, Slugger posed for the crowd, before Armstrong interrupted. "Scott... as much as I like you, it would not be fair if you didn't defend the fWo World Title like we had announced just moments ago, so you will still defend the title here tonight. Got it?" Slugger nodded, before lipreaders could ascertain that he asked Armstrong "against who?". Armstrong looked around in contemplation, before responding. "Austin Davis." Slugger' mouth fell open, before his look of shock turned to a smile. The fans booed this decision, but Slugger grabbed the microphone and eased their fears. "No, don't boo James. Boo Davis when he comes out here later tonight, because he and the WGA are going to step into this ring - and I'm going to clobber his bald headed ass just like the Mariners did to the Twins earlier today!" The fans cheered Slugger' words, as he held the World Title for everyone to see. Pictures were taken, flashbulbs went off throughout the arena, while Slaughter headed elsewhere... The arrival... A camera backstage of the San Jose Arena catches a limosine pulling up. Once the limo stops, the driver gets out and approaches the passenger door in the back. Before he can get there, the door flies open and out steps the 6'11", 336 lb. monster known as "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly. The driver barely has time to react when Kelly steps out, pushes the driver against the limo, and powerwalks into the arena. Erik strides right past him, sees a nearby table with refreshments, and decides to answer Green's question with a resounding overhanded double ax handle onto the table, smashing it in two. Green runs in a direction putting him farthest from Kelly. Kelly continues his march, and it appears he's heading for the ring.
