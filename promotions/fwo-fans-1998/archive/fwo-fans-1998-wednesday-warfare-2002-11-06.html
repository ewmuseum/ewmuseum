Wednesday Warfare, 6 November 2002

Introduction Previously... The fWo returned from a hiatus with an entirely new roster and a suspicious leadership crew in the form of the Board of Directors. Seizing control of the fWo through a majority held stock seizure, the Board of Directors was comprised of former fWo talent Jim Dudley, Cactus Jon, David Spencer, Stephen Roberts, Graphic Violence and an unknown sixth member. In a moment of poorly masked narcissism, Jim Dudley made the live Pay Per View re-debut of the fWo more about himself and reliving old glory than any serious attempt to revitalize the company. But his glory was short lived, as fans in attendance rejected the Board's vision of the new fWo in a violent display that marred the event and turned victory into destruction. In addition, the fWo wrestlers ousted as part of the board's power appeared at the event, anxious for vengeance. As the Pay Per View went off the air, all was chaos and nothing was known save the fact that the future of the fWo is still very much in jeopardy... Progress "You've got to be kidding me." Chris Universal was holding onto the door and looking with some distain at the two men standing on his doorstep. Universal was unshaven, and looked slightly annoyed at being woken so early in the day. He reached into his jacket pocket for a cigarette and didn't find one, then simply sighed and held the door open farther so his visitors could enter. The once Kid Cool stumbled into Universal's home, a laptop tucked under one arm and a dirty Star Trek hat perched on his head. Behind him the man carried a heavy stack of papers and nodded with thanks as Universal pointed him to a desk where he could set them down. Once done, he turned and extended a hand to Chris Universal. Universal looked at it, and simply shook his head. "Why now? You've been gone for years... and now suddenly this is a big deal for you? Do you realize how hard it was and what a mess you left us all in? I suppose this vacation of yours was so important that you had to screw the rest of us over? Look, we made it run and we did our best. But let's face it, the mess we are in now is largely because of the fat contracts you gave to some of those idiots. Dudley got the money to buy out the fWo's stock because of how much you overpaid him. Same with Jon, Dudley and Roberts. The other two... well, Christ... this is your fault." "Yeah, like, whatever." Kid Cool snorted. "You put the fWo into a slump, let's not forget that." Universal rounded on Kid Cool and grabbed him by the shirt. "I stuck it out while your pal here went off to who knows where, and you ran off to join the fed of the moment. And what happened? The CWL fired your ass because you were such a shitty worker. Comedy goes so far, but eventually people start to realize that all you can do is toss a leg drop here or there. Don't lecture me, kid." "Yeah, well, like, fine. And stuff." All the talk about wrestling being an honorable sport and all it really took to tear it all down was some greedy has-beens with money. I'm not getting involved... besides, I've got some other business going on that demands my... attention." The man sighed, and sat down. "We need your help with this Universal. Yes, this is a mess. Yes, this is largely my mess. I know what led to this, but I also know how to end it. You say you're done and I believe you... but do you really want it to end this way? Let it end under your own terms. Let them see what you're made of. Because if you stand here right now and tell me that you wouldn't like to see Dudley fall then you've become a shitty liar. Besides... it isn't as grim as you think. There are others working on this." Universal was silent for some time, then turned back. "Others?" The man shrugged. "Let's get to work. We've got a promotion to save." Universal looked down at Kid Cool and shook his head. "Fine. If it's about information, then fine. If I can help give that bastard something back... then fine. But as I said, I've got other business to attend to. If it's something simple..." "It's simple." The man smiled. "Let's get started." Dead The hostility of fWo fans still drilled their hatred through the arena even after Halloween Hell and its turn of events. The fans didn't care for these new upstarts; they wanted their favorites back. They were still rowdy, chanting slanderous phrases towards the production crew and announcers. Suddenly, they all stopped as something had caught their eyes. Donaven Winters. He mulled slightly at the top of his entrance ramp; as if he didn't want to be noticed. His crow black hair was matted and mangled, his face was covered with a thin layer of stubble. The fans had turned their attention from drunken insults to curiosity of the Fallen Angel. Their eyes were locked on him as he reluctantly made his way down the ramp, head and shoulders slumped towards the ground. One tattered boot in front of the other, he reached the ring. He climbed up onto the apron and ducked between the ropes, standing in the center of the ring. His distraught blue eyes looked out towards an empty sea of faces that all stared back towards him. Hesitantly he brought his arms out in his trademark crucifix position. With his head still down, he brought his arms up in front of his head and unclenched his fists. There he stood, alone in a full arena. Bare palms facing the world. They arose with a round of cheers as if he were signaling them, but what they were about to see would shock them. The cameras focused on his palms; they were stained in a ghastly crimson hue. Fans had quickly stopped cheering and were now in a profound sense of wonderment. Winters dropped his hands and produced a microphone out of his pocket. He then took a deep breath before facing the masses. "The... the other day I was released from a hospital in southern California," he stuttered nervously. "I don't know how I got there, I didn't remember who I was, but something felt surreal about the entire situation." He paused for a brief moment to inhale. "There was something inside of me that was very angry with the world. I knew there were certain people that had to be blamed. There is always someone to blame for everything. They released me from the hospital like it was jail, ten dollars in my pocket and nowhere to go. I got a taxi to take me to the nearest grocery store because I had refused to eat in the hospital." The fans were still clueless as to what Winters was getting at. "At the grocery store I saw a car.... the car was sitting idle, keys in it, doors unlocked. I couldn't resist. I stole that car. And something in my head told me to drive. I drove.... and a few hours later I ended up in Phoenix, Arizona. I remember being there almost two years ago. Just after Austin had his accident. The one where he had almost died and ended up eating out of a straw for six months." The fans cheered at the mention of Austin Davis and his near- death. They obviously still had a lot of unbridled hatred for the man. "Well, let me tell you a little story," Winters looked around nervously. "I was driving the vehicle that nearly killed him last year. I didn't mean to hurt him... it was just... he got in the way.... and when people get in the way... they eventually get hurt..." He trailed off, tears welled in the corners of his eyes. A twisted sympathetic look swept over his face. "The gnawing in my stomach told me to keep driving. It was almost like the little devil and angel on the cartoon character's shoulders. I was obeying that little devil. Anyway, I drove past that intersection and gas station. I even felt a hint of sorrow and remorse in my heart. I'm sorry I did that, Austin. I digress.... I kept driving and I found the house. This... this was a few days ago, mind you... and it was really quiet there. I saw an open window and I climbed in. I... I really shouldn't have. But I couldn't help myself, the burning in my stomach told me to keep going..." The fans had become completely silent, their eyes and ears encompassed and hanging on to Donaven's every word. "I was a little curious at first, so I went through the house, all of the belongings, everything. In the cellar I found some good strong rope, I also found a handkerchief in one of the dressers. Then in the kitchen... there was a really big carving knife that would do the trick nicely." He paused again, hesitating as his breathing became heavy and stagnated. Almost as if he were having an asthma attack. "Also, I found a sledgehammer... I didn't know what I would use it for at first.... but I knew it would be very handy. So I waited for a few hours.... and he came home. I had the hammer in my hands.... wielding it like it was the Sword in the Stone. I brought it up over my head and swung down, direct impact with his head. Knocked him out cold. Next thing I knew.... I had dragged a chair from the dining room and put it in the den with me. I lifted his heavy and unconscious body and seated it in the chair... nice and proper... like we were going to have tea. I took the rope and tied his hands behind the back of the chair.... tied his legs around the legs of the chair.... And then I took the handkerchief and tied it tightly around his mouth... gagging him." The crowd began to stir a little, they realized what was going to happen next with this story. "Then... I found some tape in a desk drawer.... I sat in front of him... waiting for him to wake up out of his comatose like sleep. You should have seen the little angel sleep... the large red welt on his shaven head was magnificent. Finally... he woke up... I took the duct tape.... taped his eyelids to his outer eye socket.... I wanted him to watch everything..." He became extremely hesitant; almost choking over himself to get the words out. "And.... and I did something I probably shouldn't have.... I.... I.... I...." He began to openly weep. He fell to his knees and buried his face into his hands. "I... killed..... him." Tears poured from his eyes. "I...." Every word struggled to come out through his bawling. "Killed...." Each person in the arena's mouth hung open with that choked word. "Austin...." Astonishment swept over the entire arena. "...Davis" And it hit them. The fans were in a complete stupor. They watched the distraught Fallen Angel shakily get to his feet and leave the ring, barely under his own power. The fWoTron went back to the still of his hands in the air, crimson palms to the world. A horrifying image as the fWo took it's first commercial break. Welcome back to Warfare The show came back from commercial, with the new fWo World Heavyweight Champion in the center of the ring. Jim Dudley. With the fWo World title over his shoulder and a microphone in one hand, Jim Dudley smiled out at the audience. "Welcome fans, welcome to the rebirth of the fWo! For all you fans watching at home, it's true... you can believe your eyes... this is Wednesday Warfare. And while...," and here Jim Dudley looked up the entrance ramp with dismay. "Well, I can't really speak toward what we just saw there... but rest assured that the fWo is about quality. It's about tradition, and yes, it is about this belt. This belt right here." Jim Dudley tapped the belt over his shoulder and beamed. The fans responded with angry boos, but Jim Dudley shrugged these off. "As you can see we have a brand new entrance screen there, and tonight in this very ring you will see the best the fWo has to offer... the new fWo that is... because the fWo is about entertaining you. The fans. And tonight, with the return of the premiere show the fWo has to offer, Wednesday Warfare, I promise you a very special title match." The fans cheered suddenly, and chants of "Kelly" and "Ross" began to break out around the arena. At these, Dudley frowned and glanced from side to side. Then the smile slid back onto his face, and Dudley beamed at the crowd. "Yes, it's true, the fWo Hardcore Championship will be on the line tonight!" The cheers turned to boos once more, and Jim Dudley ducked a flying cup aimed at his head more agility than he had shown just days before at Halloween Hell. Jim Dudley patted the fWo World Championship once more, and smiled. "But I know, I know... you want to know when this belt is going to be defended. Well fans, you simply need to tune in next week when I'll defend this title against a former champion. What champion? Well, you will have to tune in to find out... because Dudley here isn't going to spoil the surprise. But that isn't all. Next week we will have some exciting news about the fWo's brand new championship belt, the United States Heavyweight Champion. Yes, that's right, next week... next week..." The fans were booing louder, and Jim Dudley's smile faded once again. Looking concerned, Dudley gestured toward the top of the entrance ramp, and out walked the other members of the Board of Directors. Cactus Jon. David Spencer. Stephen Roberts. Graphic Violence. An impressive collection of wrestlers turned controlling force behind the fWo. They stood at the top of the ramp, and Spencer in particular looked with mistrust at the fans assembled in the arena. Perhaps remembering the disastrous end of the show just days earlier, they looked uneasy and restless. Satisfied with the rest of the board in place, Jim Dudley turned back to the crowd. "Tonight will be a special night, and have faith... this night will not be marred with the appearance of washed up, has been wrestlers who are attempting to make a name for themselves at the expense of you, the fans. We have taken special measures to ensure that tonight's event runs smoothly. Runs soundly. There will be no invasion tonight, there will be no disruptions tonight, and you will be entertained tonight. Is that clear?" The fans had begun booing so loudly that Dudley could barely be heard over the end of his speech, and finally gave up and signaled for his music to be played as he exited the ring. He walked up the entrance ramp, nodded to his partners, and pushed the curtains aside so they could vanish into the backstage area. As they walked, Dudley grabbed Jon from his shoulder to stop him. "Hey Jon, have you seen him anywhere?" Jon turned around with a confused look on his face. "Seen..." as he began to speak, whom Dudley was talking about popped in his head. "Oh, no, no I haven't. But it seems like he enjoys being fashionably late. He came to Halloween Hell after it was all over, wondering why in the hell we were all battered and bruised. I'm sure he'll pop up somewhere. I mean, he does have "business" to talk about, and you how he loves business." Dudley walked into the makeshift office and tossed the championship belt on a small desk that had been set up there. Dudley nodded to Jon with a sneer. "Yeah, I know. Thanks Jon." As Cactus Jon, former three time fWo Heavyweight Champion left the room Jim stood in his spot, scratching his chin, before also walking out the door. "The Paragon" Brand Frontier has arrived. And he walks right into one of the new owners of the fWo. Graphic Violence. Neither man has forgotten their association a year ago. Neither man has forgotten the confrontations that sparked many a brawl behind the scenes and off camera when their personalities did what only their two personalities together could do. Grate. Both men have endured a lifetime of war, both men have stood in the footfalls of utter defeat only to find their own inner strength to fight their way out. With that resolve and determination fueling such polar opposites it is no wonder they have never gotten along. Since the days when Graphic Violence berated a young fan and embarrassed the kid's father Brand Frontier and him have been at odds. Staring now at one another, near eye to eye the tension causes the fans to forget to jeer or cheer either warrior. Instead they sit on their hands with baited breath at the next movement by either man that will signal the beginning of physical confrontation. Instead they stare and circle each other. Once. Twice. Then, on the third revolution Brand simply backs away as Graphic Violence eyes go vacant and a scowl forms on his face. The fans watch on in shock as the show goes elsewhere. The fans were booing louder than ever, trash pouring into the ring as the Board of Directors slid outside of the ring, made their way quickly up the entrance ramp, and smiled at their success as the show went off the air. The fWo was theirs, and Wednesday Warfare had begun... in their image and under their control. Certainly there were kinks to work out, issues that had to be taken care of. Like any great project, there was still much work to be done. But the poison was flowing now, and what they had done could never be erased. All eyes from the crowd watched them disappear behind the curtains, and while all were angry one man in particular looked more grim than any of the others. Fans around him gave him plenty of space... he smelled like the street he had been living on. His shabby coat was dusty and threadbare, the result of living outside in the elements for too long. He was a large man, but months of hard living had turned the once athlete into something more worn, something darker. He was never at the top of any card, but his life was what he always hoped it would be. Now... now he was nothing. When a man has something and loses it suddenly, strange and desperate thoughts take root. Frank Snow watched the Board of Directors depart. The fWo would be in Los Angeles the following week... and so would he. He had his ticket, and he had his gun. This wasn't a game to ruin; this was his life. Next week it would be Dudley's... and anyone else who stood in his way.
