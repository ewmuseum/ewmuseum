Sunday Slaughter, 12 December 1999

12 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Somewhere in a quiet neighborhood near fWo headquarters in Redmond, Washington a limousine pulled up to an unassuming, quiet suburban home. The door swung open, and an attractive young medical assistant helped a man out of the car. The owner of the fWo stepped gingerly onto the concrete, testing his leg and finding it for the moment satisfactory. Travis shivered for a moment at the cold air, again wincing at his neck injury as he did so. His leg was heavily bandaged, and his neck still showed bruising from the attack by Spencer over a month ago. This aside, he made his way into the home, pausing to collect the newspaper that had been tossed onto the front porch. This took considerable effort, and Travis grimaced in pain as he reached for the paper. Cursing the Dark Riders as he bent, he was reminded of what got him into this situation. He had it for a moment, but as he lifted it the band snapped and the paper loosed from it's roll and fell open to the ground below. Cursing under his breath, Travis again reached for the paper but hesitated as his hand hovered over the top of it. Something on the page caught his eye, and his brow furrowed as he looked closer at the headline to the page. It read: "WRESTLING PROMOTION FOUND TO HAVE EXCESSIVE TAX VIOLATIONS". Beneath this headline was a photo of Primetime standing next to the acting commissioner of the fWo, James Armstrong. Travis' eyes were wide, and slowly narrowed as he got back to his feet. He looked around himself, noting the quiet neighborhood. The leaves had long fallen, and the bare trees gave the street a peaceful feeling. Travis took all that in, before clinching his hands in fists and yelling, "FUC... Chapter One: Getting Ready ...K?" Kid Cool looked in confusion, trying to gather what Violet was saying to him. Violet sighed, and spoke slower so Kid Cool could understand her better. Outside the backstage area the fireworks that heralded the beginning of Sunday Slaughter were exploding, giving the fans in attendance that the show was officially taking the air. "What I'm saying is this Kid... I got you booked for a big match tonight... because I really like watching you... move... in the ring. Flexing those... big... muscles and getting all sweaty..." Kid Cool smiled a smug grin, trying to flex his pectoral muscles but achieving little movement. Violet ignored his efforts and rubbed Kid Cool's back, looking around her shoulder to see if Ultra Violet was watching. He wasn't, and Violet at once stopped rubbing Kid Cool's back and looked frustrated. Kid Cool pointed to a section on his back mentioning, "Hey babe... this area right here could use some work... babe?" Violet was staring at Ultra Violet fixedly, chewing her lip and trying to think of what to do next. Ultra Violet was sitting next to NXS, watching a monitor of a competitor's pay-per-view. Ultra Violet scratched his head saying, "I don't get it... wrestle in water? That doesn't make any sense..." RottDogg nudged Ultra Violet and pointed at the monitor where several pert young women were splashing each other in the water. "Oh come on Clarence... it's COLD water... get it?" Ultra Violet thought about it, and finally came to some sort of conclusion. "Oh I get it... because they are going to get thirsty, right? Hey... that's not a bad idea, I'm always getting thirsty in the middle of my matches..." Ring attendants came to inform Kid Cool that it was time for his match, and Violet called out to Ultra Violet as she walked Kid Cool out of the room. "UV, watch us on the monitors okay? We're going out for our match and you wouldn't want to miss it." Ultra Violet heard this and nodded, changing the channel of the monitor back to the arena feed just as one woman on the competition's pay-per-view was struggling with her rather small bra. Sounds of anger and dismay came from the room as Violet escorted Kid Cool toward the ring... Stevie Roberts vs. Wolf Fang Stevie Roberts walked down the entrance ramp, still wearing a robe and doing a slight dance as he shuffled toward the ring. He slid under the ropes and asked for a microphone, going from cheerful to somber in the space of time it took for a ring attendant to fulfill his request. Roberts looked around himself, and nodded before starting to speak. "You're looking at one of the originals in the fWo, baby... a man that's been around since day one! I've worn my masks from time to time... and there was even a day that I once shagged my way into wearing that fWo World Heavyweight Championship that everyone wants so badly. Lately I've been hanging and shagging with the hardcore legend herself... VL. We made quite a tag team, but now it looks like VL is on the fast track to becoming the fWo Hardcore Champion at Twilight... and that leaves me wondering, 'What about Stevie?' Well, tonight I'll find out. Because if I can't beat that little pack rat misfit Wolf Fang, I don't deserve to be in this ring, baby! That's why I'm asking right now, I'm putting on the line right now, my career in an 'I QUIT' match. Come on Wolf Fang... bring it, baby... YEAH!" Wolf Fang's music played, and with the Black Panther and Havok behind him the wrestler walked to the ring, pointing up at Stevie as he went. Wolf Fang nodded to the referee to accept the challenge that Stevie Roberts set forth, and slid into the ring. Stevie at once attacked with kicks to the head of Wolf Fang, trying to keep the Millennium Man off his feet and down on the mat. Stevie let Wolf Fang climb to his feet, but as he righted himself Stevie ran to the ropes and rebounded off with a diving drop kick into the leg and knee of Wolf Fang. The knee blew backward, and Wolf Fang stumbled down to the mat and into the corner. Wolf Fang held the knee as the referee asked him if he should call the match. Wolf Fang refused to give up as Stevie taunted the fallen wrestler. The referee warned Stevie about his offensive tactics, as the once leader of the fWo stable Doomsday went back to work on Wolf Fang. Stevie dominated the match by continuing to work on Wolf Fang's injured knee. Stevie grabbed the head and applied a neck lock, trying to cut off the oxygen of the former member of Ground Zero. Wolf Fang struggled to the ropes, finally grabbing them and forcing Stevie to break the hold. He did so, but hit the back of Wolf Fang's skull with his elbow for good measure. With Wolf Fang down on the mat Stevie went to the ropes and executed a diving splash, and went for a cover. The referee didn't count, and Stevie remembered that this was an 'I Quit' match. Stevie picked Wolf Fang up and signaled that he was going to execute a Michinoku driver, but Wolf Fang blocked the move and hit a jaw breaker. Both men went to opposite ends of the ring, trying to catch their breath and get ready for the next attack. Stevie ran to the attack first, but Wolf Fang ducked the running lariat attempt and grabbed Stevie from behind. He quickly executed a tiger suplex, and then a second, favoring the knee but keeping the attack on. Wolf Fang reached for a head lock, but Stevie Roberts punched backward and attempted a DDT. Wolf Fang held up, grabbing the waist of Stevie and bringing him to the mat for a ankle lock submission hold. Stevie reached around and over, grabbing the neck and bringing him over his body and down to the mat. Grabbing the leg and neck, Stevie attempted a modified abdominal stretch, but Wolf Fang went under the arm and swept Stevie Roberts up and over his body. Wolf Fang grabbed Stevie's legs, and in a split second had applied the Wolf Bite. Stevie Roberts tried to reach for the ropes and hold on, but Wolf Fang had the hold locked in hard in the center of the ring. Finally, standing the pain no longer, Stevie Roberts said 'I Quit' and Wolf Fang released the hold, raising his arms into the air in victory. Stevie looked up at him, and then around at the crowd. With a rueful smile on his face, Stevie nodded and helped raise Wolf Fang's arm in the air before walking out of the ring, and out of active competition. Courage yelled after him, "And stay out, tubby... you're finished in the fWo now... once and for all!" Jake picked himself up off the cold arena floor, wiping at his bloody nose and sniffing hard. He looked around himself, and a pouty, angry expression came over his face. "I'll fix him... I'll fix him good." He attempts a back kick, but Bear ducks again. Finally, he connects with a powerful roundhouse kick to the side of the head. Ninja picks up Bear and backs him into the ropes, before striking with a series of side kicks to the stomach. He then whips Bear across the ring, and catches him coming off with a spinning chop. He covers, but only gets a one count. Ninja lifts Bear, but Bear strikes back with an elbow to the gut, followed by another, staggering the Ninja. As they get up, Ninja tries a superkick, but Bear ducks and rolls to Wretch, who tags in and cleans house, dropkicking first the charging Tom The Bomb, following with a dropkick to the Ninja. Wretch gets up first and ducks a kick from Ninja before kicking him in the gut and taking him down with a sunset flip. This gets two before Tom breaks it with a sledge to the back of the head. This brings back in Bear, who jumps Tom, knocking him back into the corner. Meanwhile, on the outside, Jon has grabbed the chain-link section and charges into the ring, taking down Fear with it. Mike Bear charges him, but also gets a face-ful of mesh. Joy (c) vs. Hellrazor Joy skips to the ring first, holding the daisy-painted Hardcore Title high and dragging behind him a shopping cart filled with Pokemon action figures and giant lollipops, with Glee bouncing to the ring behind him. Hellrazor enters next, carrying only one weapon- Lamont the cat, in a pet carrier. Joy scales the guardrail and prepares to leap off, but Hellrazor catches him coming down with a big boot. Fallen entered the scene, and handed Death a slip of dirty paper. Death looked it over, reading it with evil satisfaction. "So... the card has been made. Little Scotty Collins thinks he's got us just where he wants us... well Scotty, the nightmare is just beginning... and you don't understand what you've let out of the box. Death began to laugh a deep, dark laugh, but Fallen did not join him. Spencer noticed, and bid Fallen to speak what was on his mind. "Death... this new plan of yours worries me... Poison... Dina... she's lost her mind. She's dangerous... but even insane she's not as big a threat as Demon. William... he's sick. He's going to really hurt someone... bad. You've got to pull back... or it's going to end badly. You know I'm right Spencer... let's pull back." Spencer looked at Fallen silently for a moment, as if considering his words. But then a large smile drew over his face. "You pathetic coward... we aren't stopping now... we're never stopping. Never. Now get out... go find your spine, boy... and don't come back until you do." Primetime (c) vs. Scott Slugger vs. Flying Frenchie vs. Ice The time finally arrived for the main event of the evening, and one of the most highly anticipated matches in recent memory. Despite having cheered their proverbial lungs out the entire evening, the fans stood to their feet as Matt Panzer began the introductions. First, Panzer introduced one of the challengers, former two-time fWo World Champion Ice. Ice came out as "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne echoed throughout the arena, and despite being one of the most hated wrestlers in the fWo, received some cheers from the crowd. Ice paid no attention and was clearly focused on becoming the fWo World Champion for a third time. Next came The Flying Frenchie, who was booed by everyone except some obsessed fan in the first row. Sitting next to the wacko fan was Chris Universal, who seemed annoyed at the fans' relentless cheering of TFF. Frenchie was just as - if not more focused than Ice - since it had been almost a year since he had been the fWo World Champion. Longtime fWo viewers noted that almost one year ago Ice pinned Slugger - not Frenchie - in a three-way dance to become the champion for a second time. Coming out next was the three-time fWo World Champion Scott Slugger, who received a mix reaction from the crowd while "Back in Black" played throughout the building. Slugger was not clearly as focused as his friend Ice and one of his most hated rivals Frenchie appeared to be, but looked confident enough to win the title for an unprecendent fourth time. Slugger slid into the ring and the three men waited for the reigning fWo World Champion Primetime to be announced. Frenchie was closest to the ramp and he looked up at the stage for his friend to make his way out, while Ice and Slugger shook hands and conversated amongst themselves. Slugger & Ice seemed to be plotting something, and their true intentions became known before Primetime could be announced. The two members of the Dynasty attacked The Frenchie from behind viciously, pounding on him relentlessy. Ice held Frenchie in his large arms giving Slugger the opportunity to assault the masked man from Toulouse, and as Slugger prepared to do just that the lights dimmed, and pyros exploded. "Bawitdaba" began to play loudly over the sound system, and Ice pushed Frenchie to the mat. Ice & Slugger looked at the stage with interest, waiting for Primetime to come out. The fans exploded as Primetime had become a favorite of theirs in recent weeks, although they still kinda hated the overconfident champion. Primetime stepped out onto the SlaughterStage with the gold shining brightly around his waist, and the fans erupted even louder than before. Primetime began to walk down as "Bawitdaba" continued to play, while Ice and Slugger salivated at the mouth for the opportunity to take the gold away from PT. Frenchie snuck up from behind and scooped Slugger over the top rope onto the floor before Ice turned around at charged at him. Frenchie hip tossed the larger man down to the mat, while Primetime and Slugger began to engage in a exchange of lefts and rights out on the floor. Ice got up to his feet and Frenchie charged him and took him down with a high cross body, resulting in a pinfall attempt. Ice kicked out at one and a half, and Frenchie quickly thought of what to do next. Slugger got the upper hand on Primetime and whipped him into the guardrail, before charging at him and taking him up and over the guard rail with a clothesline. Back in the ring, Frenchie bounced off the ropes and staggered Ice back into the turnbuckles with a dropkick, before following that up with a well-timed monkey flip. On the outside, PT & Slugger went at it tooth and nail, slamming each other face first into the floor, chairs, walls and other hard objects. They continued to fight up into the crowd, while Ice & Frenchie continued it on in the ring. Security tried to direct Slugger & PT back to the ring to no avail. Frenchie waited for Ice to get up from the monkey flip and then took him down with a spinning heel kick. Frenchie continued to dominate by going to the outside. He climbed to the top rope and waited for Ice to get up, before diving off with a flying bodypress. Ice however caught him in midair, and tossed him overhead with ease. Frenchie crashed to the mat hard after the fallaway slam, and Ice took a few moments to recover from the previous attack. Back in the crowd, Primetime and Slugger began to make their way back to ringside, as PT had Slugger in a side headlock, dragging him through the crowd. As they neared the guardrail, Slugger nailed PT in the ribs with an elbow to the side, and then followed it up with a back-to-back suplex onto the cement floor. The referee went to the outside to check on the two men, and the fans did not help any as they got in his way and posed for the television cameras. Meanwhile, Ice brought Frenchie up and whipped him into the ropes, and as Frenchie returned Ice nearly removed his head from his body with a lariat. Ice followed that up by press slamming Frenchie into the air; but the Frenchie escaped and fell behind Ice. He went for a waistlock, but Ice countered with a go-behind into a waistlock of his own. From there, Ice hooked his arms up around the Frenchie neck, and went for a full nelson suplex. The crafty Frenchie quickly countered with a mule kick, which made Ice bend over in excruciating pain. Frenchie followed it up with a face slam, also known as the "X-Factor" in another promotion. On the outside, a security guard helped Slugger to his feet, while the referee watched the ring with interest. Frenchie decided he was going to go for the kill and he went to the ring apron. He climbed to the top rope, and signalled for his Guillotine Legdrop. He leaped through the air and hit it, before covering Ice. The referee hurried back to the ring, with Slugger right behind him as Primetime struggled to his feet. The referee slid underneath the bottom rope and began to count, halfway in the ring and halfway out. One, two... and Slugger pulled the referee onto the floor, and the two began to argue. Frenchie stood up, confused as he had not seen what happened, and looked at the two men arguing on the outside. With everyone distracted, Universal snuck into the ring, and spun the Frenchie around. He took the Frenchie out with the ever notorious 'Cha-Ching!', and left the Frenchie for dead. After Primetime decked Slugger from behind with a double-axehandle to the back, Ice crawled over and went for the pin, and the referee slid underneath the bottom rope to count. Primetime mounted the top rope and flew off at the count of two and nailed Ice in the back with an elbow which broke up the pin just in time. Primetime then pinned Ice, and at the count of two Slugger pulled him off. Slugger pulled him to his feet and took him down with a small package, of which Primetime reversed. Frenchie and Ice stood to their feet, and while Frenchie appeared reluctant to break up the pin, Ice wasted no time and dove and nailed Primetime in the back with an elbow of his own. Ice brought Primetime to his feet by the hair and whipped him over the top rope onto the floor. He turned around and saw a very angry Frenchie, who charged and took him over the top rope onto the cement floor with a clothesline. Both men crashed onto the ground, and Slugger reached his feet back in the ring. He waited for Frenchie and Primetime to get up, before charging and diving over the top rope and taking both of his enemies down with a suicide plancha as Ice scattered out of the way. Primetime, Frenchie and Slugger crashed in a heap, and Ice saw this as an opportunity to win the match by pinning someone. Seeing as how to win the title the first two times he didn't defeat the champion, he decided to bring the champion Primetime up. He turned him upside down apparently going for a tombstone on the floor, but instead just draped Primetime over his shoulder and steadily walked up the nearest ringsteps onto the ring apron. He climbed up to the top rope, and it was obvious he was going for his patented "Icebreaker", technically known as a top rope powerslam. The two men flew through the air and the larger Ice hammered Primetime into the ring, completing the move. Ice' popped up off of Primetime and was exhausted, as Primetime spat up blood, apparently bleeding internally after sustaining such a hard blow. The referee entered the ring and since neither men were in position for a pinfall, he began a ten count while Frenchie and Slugger both struggled to their knees on the outside. Frenchie used the ring apron to help him to his knees, while Slugger got up on his own accord. At the count of six back in the ring, Ice draped an arm over the champion, while Primetime draped an arm of his own at the same time. Both men remained on their back, and appeared to be just reaching out in a desperate attempt to win. Frenchie reached the top rope on one side, while Slugger did the same on the other. As the referee reached two, both men leaped off in a simulatenous frogsplash. Slugger landed on Primetime, and Frenchie landed on Ice. The referee had no choice but to count both pinfalls. Since neither man wanted to match to end in a controversial double-pin screwjob finish, Slugger & Frenchie jumped to a vertical base, and charged at each other. Both men went down as a result of both men going for a clothesline which both former World Champions were successful at. All four men were down and out of it, and the referee began another ten count. Seven seconds into the count, one of the four men showed signs of life. Frenchie struggled in his first attempt, but got to his feet on the second try. He stumbled and looked at the crowd through his mask, but nobody knew exactly what the cagey veteran had up his sleeve. Frenchie had his choice as to who he could pin, and as he surveyed the scene he showed some concern for his best friend Primetime, who spat up more blood. The crimson ran down his chest as he clutched at his ribs, while Ice appeared to be unconscious. Frenchie decided to go after Slugger, who seemed to be weakened the least. Frenchie dragged him over near the corner, and signalled for the 450 splash. Ice rolled over off his back onto all fours, but was still spent from two big time moves in a row. Frenchie climbed up to the top rope and gazed around the arena, before signalling yet again for the 450. Frenchie flew through the air in a graceful manner, before hitting the coup de grace. Suddenly, a man who appeared hellbent on revenge arrived on the scene via way of the crowd. He climbed over the guardrail, and upon closer inspection he was revealed to be the insane Boston Strangler. Strangler carried a rope in one hand and reached the ring. The referee began to count the pinfall, and as he did Strangler pulled Primetime from the ring and held the rope up in the air for Frenchie to see. Ice got to his feet and watched the situation develop from afar as the Strangler waited for Frenchie to make his move. Frenchie saw this at the count of two and climbed to his feet. He walked towards the Strangler who continued to threaten Primetime, as he lassoed the rope around his neck. Frenchie picked up the pace and as he bent over the top rope to reach down for the Strangler, Ice crept up from behind and spin him around. A kick to the stomach and a running powerbomb later, the fWo had a new World Champion as Ice achieved victory for a third time; becoming the third man in the history of the promotion to win it three times. Strangler meanwhile tightened the rope as Primetime struggled to escape. Slugger remained out, and Ice celebrated as the referee handed him the title. Ice held the title high above the ring, before noticing his fallen friend on the mat. He walked over and knelt down beside him in an attempt to console him, while Courage made his way down the ramp. Courage slid into the ring and tried to help the Frenchie up, but it was to no avail. Courage decided to save his other friend, and got some momentum before leaping off the top rope with a springboard dropkick, nailing Strangler right in the jaw and sending him sprawling back onto the commentators table. Courage then checked on Primetime, but Strangler got the final laugh as he caught Courage off guard from behind with the infamous 'Boston Massacre'. The show went off the air in utter chaos as Universal joined his stablemates Ice & Slugger in the ring, while Strangler continued to work on Courage on the floor. Primetime remained in the grasp of the rope... gasping for one... last... breath... before we faded to black. Result: Ice by Pinfall
