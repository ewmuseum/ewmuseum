Wednesday Warfare, 19 April 2000

19 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Scenes from earlier in the day played, as various fWo superstars were welcomed to the Los Angeles area by excited fWo fans and supporters. The Flying Frenchie, Primetime and Courage were seen shaking hands and cutting the ribbon on a new building construction for the psychologically ill in the valley. The trio from the French Foundation was received warmly from fans who generally boo them inside the fWo environment, and the reception went off generally without a hitch, save one overzealous Frenchie fan jumping the barricade that had to be beaten back by Los Angeles' finest. On Hollywood and Vine new fWo wrestlers Erik Kelly and the Crusader signed autographs and plugged the upcoming fWo:The Music Volume Three which was scheduled to be released April 25 around the nation. Kelly and the Crusader were also hyping CyberSlam 3, an event which both of them would have a part in. At DisneyWorld fWo wrestler Scott "Perfect" Slugger pointed out what a worn out ride Space Mountain was, and asked onlookers if they thought a ride on the Slugger bat would prove more exciting and worth the money. Finally, Scott Collins appeared at the Los Angeles International airport where he had lost his luggage during the plane flight. Collins signed autographs as he punched airport officials in the head and screamed. As these clips faded, the event proper began as fireworks exploded from the rafters of the Staples Center as another edition of Wednesday Warfare took to the air. Courage & Future Frenchie vs. War & Plague As Warfare returns, "Through the Never" by Metallica plays, and out walks War, alongside his tag team partner, Plague. The Four Riders members continue to the ring, when "Reclaim my Place" hits. The arrogant superstar known only as Courage steps out onto the entrance ramp, with a smug look on his face. He continues down the ramp, stops, and looks back, when suddenly "Juke Joint Jezebel" hits, and the crowd rises, thinking that the Flying Frenchie is coming out. However, we see Lance Star dressed perfectly like TFF, and wearing a "Why must Ze Future Frenchie be so damn handsome?" T-Shirt. Courage holds his face in his hands, and eventually continues to the ring. Courage steps into the ring, and locks up with Plague. Plague takes Courage in an armbar, but Courage nails some elbows to the face to escape. Courage runs the ropes, Plague drops to the mat, and Courage jumps over. As Plague gets up, Courage jumps on the ropes, and hits him with a back elbow. Cover, but only two, as Courage goes back to the offensive playboard. Courage nails some stiff looking kicks to the torso, and backs him into a corner. Courage nails some chops to the chest, before going for an Irish whip to the other side. It's reversed, and Courage goes crashing in. However, Plague runs at him, and Courage gets a boot up before tagging in the Future Frenchie, Lance Star. Star goes to work with some chops, and eventually a snap suplex, with a bridge for a cover. However, War comes in, and with a boot to the gut of Star, breaks up the count. Plague scurries back to the corner, and tags in War. War uses the power advantage to lift Star up, and throw him into the corner, nailing him with lefts and rights. The big man sets Star on the top turnbuckle, before taking him over with a huge superplex. War covers, but Star kicks out at two. Frustrated, War decides to tag out to Plague. Plague comes in with an Irish whip. Star ducks a clothesline, and nails a perfect float over DDT. With Plague out, Star decides to climb the turnbuckles. Emulating the (real) Frenchie, Star goes for the 450 splash. However, he misses horribly, getting only about 1/2 of a rotation, and landing right on the mat, in front of Courage. Courage lets out a sigh, smacks Star on the back, making a "tag", runs against the ropes, and nails the still unconscious Plague with the Courage Required. He covers, and the Star/Courage tandem pick up their first win. Star gets up, and begins jumping up and down in the excitement of actually winning. "Mon Dieu! Mon Dieu! We won a match! Zut! We need to be a team once again, mon ami!" Courage just shrugged it off, although it appeared he was considering it since they won the match. Result: Courage & The Future Frenchie by pinfall Love is in the air Kid Cool cleared his throat and adjusted his tie. It came off in his hand and he grunted to himself as he clipped it back on. He stood outside the Warfare Girls locker room with the poem in his hand and began to psyche himself up. "Come on Kid Cool... get it together. Just think... what would Papa Smurf do in this situation? Yeah, look for Smurfberrys probably... but what if Papa Smurf was horny and wanted to get his groove on? What would he do then? He'd read her a poem is what he would do... or get the chick drunk and take her to the back seat of his Smurf mobile... but I don't have a Smurf mobile so it's poetry for me. Okay... let's do it." Kid Cool knocked on the door. There was some movement from behind it, and then a muffled scream. "Shut up or I'll shut you up," came from behind the door with an angry whisper. The scream turned into a whimper, and then fell silent. Footsteps neared the door, and then opened it. Nightshade glared out at Kid Cool. Kid Cool felt his heart melt, and he was momentarily lost. Then he remembered the poem, and held up the piece of paper. "Loves a Sensation, Caused by Temptation, A Man sticks his location, Into the girls destination, To increase the population, For the next generation, Do you understand my explanation, Or do you need a demonstration?" Kid Cool finished reading and beamed up at Nightshade. She was frowning. Kid Cool thought fast. "Please?" Nightshade smiled a thin smile. Kid Cool's heart leapt. VL vs. Sid Snow The song titled Result: VL by pinfall Backpeddling Neo dropped a burnt out husk of a cigarette to the ground and pulverized it with one boot. He coughed, and looked around himself with an expression of annoyance and distrust. In one corner of the room Kid Cool was slumped in a corner with his underwear pulled up over his head in an atomic weggie. He appeared to be unconscious. Neo turned to leave the shoddy backstage entrance room and ran face-first into Lance Star. Neo blinked. "Oh... oh hey Lance! Good to see you! Looking well... no signs of any crippling injuries I see..." Star looked Neo up and down, and then with a grunt shoved Neo up against a wall. "Listen here newbie... you want to run your mouth in interviews about Cactus Jon, that's fine. You want to insult Scott Collins, see if I care. Mike Bear? Again, who cares. But you keep me out of your little deluded fantasy games from now on... hear?" Neo stammered. "Of... of... of course! I would never want to upset you Mr. Star... Lance?" Lance frowned. "Mr. Star." Neo gulped. "Of course Mr. Star. Listen, I didn't want to say those things... it was just the fWo script writers, you understand? I mean... Chris Finn wrote all those things out for me you know. Even the parts about your mother which didn't make it on the air but will be released in the special Neo directors cut video due out in the summer in fine video retail stores everywhere." Star looked hard at Neo, and then released him. "Get out of my face... I've got to have a little conversation with Finn." Star stomped away, and Neo watched him go with a thin smile on his face. "What an idiot... that Jackhammer putz has more brains in his guitar than Lance Star has in his whole body. Of course, Jackhammer's guitar has more brains than Jackhammer himself so..." Neo was suddenly interrupted by a firm hand on his shoulder. He gulped again and turned to see the fWo Tag Team Champions the Guano Apes behind him. Jackhammer was frowning. Neo gulped again. "Why... hey guys!" Black Quicksilver, Primetime, Mike Heftel (in Silver Slowblack guise), Robert Jensen, Briana, Dark Ninja, Fallen, Matt Douglas, Kid Cool, and Scott Hart Warfare comes back from commercial to find that many of the participants in the Battle Royal for a Cruiserweight Title shot are already in the ring - Black Quicksilver, Mike Heftel (in Silver Slowblack guise), Robert Jensen, Briana, Dark Ninja, Fallen, Kid Cool, and Scott Hart in the ring. As they await the arrival of the final two men in the match, "Higher" starts to play and the crowd cheers as Matt Douglas is announced and makes his way to the ring. He rolls in, and the cheers turn to boos as "Blind" starts up and former Cruiserweight champ Primetime slowly makes his way out to the ring. Primetime waits outside of the ring for a moment, wary of entering the ring, then rolls in and the bell rings. Immediately the participants pair off. Dark Ninja goes to work on Kid Cool, attacking him with a series of kicks to the chest and head. Cool slumps back into the corner, and Ninja charges in with a boot that heads for the face of Cool. In a rare smart move, Cool ducks and Ninja crotches himself on the top turnbuckle. Cool goes to work on Ninja with chops as the Stump Squad trades blows with Primetime. Primetime strikes with a questionable kick to the lower abdominal area of Quicksilver and then takes down Heftel with a hard DDT. Scott Hart in the corner stands and laughs at the battling participants, and never sees Robert Jensen rush up behind him and rudely throw him from the ring. Briana makes her way over to the corner of Jensen and strikes him with a hard leaping forearm that nearly sends him over, but Jensen pulls himself back in and begins wailing away at the face of Briana with rights. Briana matches each one with one of her own as Matt Douglas and Fallen battle in the middle of the ring. Fallen attempts a vertical suplex, but Douglas slides down his back and takes him down with a back suplex. Douglas runs to the ropes, going for an asai moonsault, but Black Quicksilver runs up behind him and shoves him over the top rope as he stands on the second rope. Douglas shows his agility however by skinning the cat to re-enter the ring, and eliminating BQ who had moved on to a brawl with Kid Cool. Cool immediately focuses on Douglas, and the two trade ringing chops. Primetime in the corner is stomping away at Mike Heftel, and arrogantly pauses to taunt the crowd. Heftel reaches forward to pull Primetime into the corner where he slams into the turnbuckles. Heftel makes his way to his feet and attempts to hiptoss Primetime over the top, but Primetime reverses the move and sends Heftel over instead. Robert Jensen and Fallen brawl on the opposite side of the ring, and Fallen counters a back suplex from Jensen by flipping over onto his feet and taking Jensen down with the Lowfall. Fallen then scoops up Jensen and tosses him from the ring. Briana and the Dark Ninja are trading kicks in the corner, and Ninja gains the advantage. He brings Briana out from the corner and whips her into the ropes, but she reverses it. As he comes off, he strikes her with a massive roundhouse kick to the face. Briana staggers back, and Ninja charges at her with a running superkick, knocking her over the top rope. Unbeknownst to him, Tom The Bomb has come in from the crowd - wearing a "DOEJOE" gi - and he pulls down the top rope. The Ninja's momentum carries him into the ropes and the pulled strand sends him flying to the outside. Tom quickly escapes through the crowd and Ninja, steaming, follows him. It's down to four men - Matt Douglas, Primetime, Fallen, and Kid Cool. Primetime and Cool pair off (as do Fallen and Douglas), and Primetime attempts a whip into the ropes. Cool somehow manages to reverse it, but makes the lethal mistake of putting his head down. Primetime hooks him up and lifts him over his head, before charging towards the ropes and Primetime Bombing Kid Cool over the top rope to the floor where he lands with a sickening splat. Meanwhile in the corner Douglas and Fallen are trading chops, and Douglas gains the advantage. He lifts Fallen onto the turnbuckles, climbs onto the top rope, and hurricanranas Fallen off. Primetime immediately takes the advantage and picks up a vulture elimination of Fallen, throwing him over the top as Douglas gets back to his feet. Primetime charges at his long-time enemy Douglas, who ducks a lariat and dropkicks Primetime into the corner. He leaps in for a 10-punch count along, but Primetime throws him over his shoulders. Primetime walks off celebrating, and doesn't notice Douglas has landed on the apron. Douglas springboards onto the top rope, yells at Primetime who turns around, and leaps off with a dropkick that sends Primetime stumbling backwards. Douglas gets up and leaps onto the shoulders of Primetime, but Primetime catches him in Primetime Bomb position and takes him near the ropes. He attempts to Kid Cool him, but Douglas reverses the momentum and hurricanranas Primetime over the top rope, landing in a sitting position on the apron. He pulls himself back in and is declared the winner. Primetime stews on the outside as Douglas celebrates earning a Cruiserweight Title shot... Result: Matt Douglas Toastmaster Trouble "Okay... that was very good Carl! Who wants to come up and try next?" The room was dimly lit, and from the outside of the glass door a small sign read 'Toastmasters Meeting 4:30-7:00. Tomorrow night: Get Your Pet Fixed 5:00-8:20'. Machina raised his hand, and walked to the front of the room. "Hello, my name is Machina." In unison the rest of the room greeted him. "Hi, Machina!" Machina cleared his throat. It was hard standing up here in front of all these people. These eyes roamed over him like a bad comb over and Machina felt his cheeks grow warm. He looked down at his notes. The words were becoming blurry, and somewhere deep down Machina could feel his pulse racing and his breath coming in quick, harsh jabs. What was it that Rudo had told him? Picture them all naked. Yeah... that might work. Machina closed his eyes for a second, and then opened them. Before him was the same crowd, except they were now all unclothed. Machina grimaced. Still, he did seem more comfortable. He looked down at his notes, and that was when he realized that he was picturing himself naked as well. Machina yelped a quick scream of embarrassment and passed out. The rest of the room nodded to themselves. This was not uncommon at Toastmasters. "Think he pictured himself naked again?" A large man nodded to the woman in the crowd who posed the question. "Oh yeah." Boston Strangler vs. Eron "Physical" by Nine Inch Nails brings us back from commercial, and the cameras scroll to Eron walking down the entrance ramp. He is swallowed up by a flood of boos from the crowd, something that doesn't really affect him that much. His demeanor is that of focused yet cocky. He warms up in the ring as "O Fortuna" blares across the speakers. The boos turn to a standing ovation for The Boston Strangler, who walks down the ramp, completely focused on Eron. The Strangler slid in the ring, his first mental mistake in this match up. Eron stomps at the Strangler, before picking him up. The two start to brawl, as Eron backs the Strangler into a corner. He chops away at the Strangler's chest. Strangler seizes an opportunity after such a chop and delivers one of his own, only this is more vicious. The two trade chops again, with the Strangler winning again. The Strangler begins to pound away at Eron, and suddenly, the two are in mid-ring. A few chops more, and Eron is the one backed into a corner. The ref gets in between the two, and the Strangler backs off. Eron, seizing this opportunity, rakes the eyes of Strangler. He grabs the head of the Strangler and hits a swift suplex. Eron gets up and picks the Strangler up, and repeats the suplex. Eron gets up again, and this time runs toward the ropes. Upon his return, Eron leaps onto the Strangler, knocking the wind out of him. He covers him, leg unlocked. Count of two, kick out. Eron picks up Strangler and brings him to his feet. He starts to pound away at the back of the Strangler, softening it up for what would hopefully (for Eron) end in his finisher, the De-Rouger. He picks up the Strangler, and executes a back breaker. The Strangler winces, and Eron continues. He rolls over the Strangler and begins to stomp at his back. He lays in two elbows to drive home the affect. He picks up the Strangler and grabs him by the hair. He runs him toward the ropes and tosses him over. The Strangler lands safely...on his back. Eron is not satisfied with this. He follows him to the outside, and begins to remove the pads on the outside, revealing the cold, unforgiving concrete. He walks over to the Strangler, and picks up him. He kicks up to the gut, and sets him up for a powerbomb. He begins to execute it, but something is awry. The Stranger has blocked it. Eron tries again. Blocked. He tries a third time. This time, a move is executed. However, it's not the move Eron chose. The Strangler tosses Eron over his back. Not only does this move hurt the Strangler's weakened back some more, but Eron lands on his feet. Eron turns around, grabs the Strangler, and kicks him to the gut once again. This time, he does not fail, and the Strangler's back feels the concrete. Eron picks him up, and grabs hold of him. He picks him up, and drives him to the concrete with a suplex. He picks up the Strangler, and forces him to the apron. Eron hops onto the apron, and grabs the Strangler. He wants another powerbomb, this time from six feet in the air. Eron stops for a second, to let the boos of the crowd to sink in. He goes for the powerbomb. He doesn't connect. The Strangler has lifted him up and sends him crashing to the concrete below. Unfortunately, due to Strangler's weakened state, he too has fallen to the floor. The ref begins to count. At the count of eight, the Strangler begins to arise. He must use both arms to push himself up, but he achieves it. He picks up Eron, and tosses him into the ring. He slides in behind him, and goes for a cover, leg unlocked. Kick out at two-and-a-half. Both men are feeling the pain of a grueling match now. Eron, who is stirring, begins to get up. The Strangler tries to beat him to the punch, but fails. Eron stomps at the head of the Strangler, sending him back to the mat. He picks up the Strangler and sends him to the ropes. As the Strangler comes back, Eron has him in his sights for the superkick. He connects. Time for the De-Rouger. Eron locks the Strangler in for the Boston crab, or the De-Rouger. He has him in the middle of the ring. It becomes a war, between strength and dedication. The Strangler refuses to give. He maneuvers his hands so that he can lift his body off the ground. After about a minute of intense pain, he succeeds. He begins to inch his way toward the ropes. Eron senses this, and tries to move back to the center of the ring. Yet, he cannot. Dedication wins out, and Eron must break the hold. He complains to the ref, and then to the fans. This allows for the Strangler to muster up a little strength of his own. Eron turns around to find the Stranger standing face to face with him. Eron punches; Strangler blocks it, and returns one of his own. Eron punches again; same result. Eron punches once more; same result, although Eron falls to the mat this time. Strangler picks Eron up and locks him in for a German suplex. He connects. He picks him up, and lands another. He picks him up again; this time, he locks in a gut wrench powerbomb. He has set him up for his finisher, the Boston Massacre. Meanwhile, Eron has gathered his thoughts and has come up with an idea of his own. He leaves the ring. Eron clutches his side as he walks up the ramp. The ref begins to count; the Strangler protests, and then begins to leave the ring to charge after him. However, something stops him. Someone from the stands has climbed out and grabs Eron. The bald head gives it away. Dead punches Eron in the head, and then kicks him in the gut. He grabs his head and keeps it between his legs. Dead picks him up, and hits the Crucifixion on Eron. He stands up and spreads his arms out; the Crucifixion pose. He turns around, and looks eye-to-eye with the Boston Strangler. The stare down is intense, but does not last long. Dead begins to point at the Strangler and points to his title. He looks down at the unconscious Eron, and spits at him. He begins to walk up the ramp as Warfare heads to a commercial. Result: Boston Strangler by Count Out Coming The water dripped from far away and made small echoes in the darkness. The black was deeper that soot and nothing could be seen in any direction. When the voice came from the black, it was whispered and more than a little frightened. The voice shook with a terror that knew no depths and spoke in short, gasping breaths. "He's... coming... on the last... day... of April... He's... coming... back..." Havok vs. Mike Bear vs. Wretch vs. Black Panther The time finally arrived for the main event of the evening, and one of the most highly anticipated matches in recent memory. Despite having cheered their proverbial lungs out the entire evening, the fans stood to their feet as Matt Panzer began the introductions. First, Panzer introduced one of the participants, the 1999 Wrestler of the Year and former World Champion The Black Panther. The Black Panther came out as "Killed The Cat" echoed throughout the Staples Center, and he received a very large abundance of boos. The Panther paid no attention and was clearly focused on walking away from this monumental match-up the victor. Next came Havok, who was cheered by nearly everyone in the home of the Los Angeles Lakers, Clippers & Kings. Havok stood in a corner directly across from his rival, staring at him with a look of hatred as he did. "I'm With Stupid" played loudly on the arena sound system, and out came the fWo International Champion, Wretch. Wretch was accompanied by Scott Hart, and the fans jeered both men as they walked down to ringside. Although Wretch had been getting booed since Body Count, it would be safe to mention he still had his own group of fans. Wretch slid into the ring and stared at his two competitors, one of which was a friend of his since they were children. Havok, who had been silent as of late, walked over to Wretch and began speaking about the change in attitude of Wretch lately. From behind came a charging Black Panther, who nailed Havok in the back, which propelled him into Wretch. Wretch held up his fist and Havok ran into it, before Wretch held his mouth agape and covered his face, not believing what he had done to his friend, in a moment that would make Linda McMahon blush. Panther seized his opportunity, and began hammering away on Havok with knee drops and elbow drops. He then whipped Havok into the ropes, and as he returned he took him out with a power slam. "Dragula" by Rob Zombie started up, and out came the perennial fan favorite of the match, Mike Bear. Bear charged to the ring, looking for one man. His former friend Wretch knew he was a targeted man, and began to run away. Bear chased him around the ring while Panther & Havok continued inside the ring, as Bear turned a corner he was met with a steel chair to the head by Scott Hart. The fans began booing loudly, and this gave Wretch the chance he needed to get an early advantage. Havok hip tossed the larger man down to the mat, while Wretch worked over Bear on the outside. Soon enough, however, Bear recovered and he and Wretch began to engage in a exchange of lefts and rights out on the floor. Panther got up to his feet and Havok charged him and took him down with a high cross body, resulting in a pinfall attempt. Panther kicked out at one and a half, and Havok quickly thought of what to do next. On the outside, Wretch got the upper hand on Bear and whipped him into the guardrail, before charging at him and taking him up and over the guardrail with a clothesline. Back in the ring, Havok bounced off the ropes and staggered the 1999 Wrestler of the Year back into the turnbuckles with a dropkick, before following that up with a well-timed monkey flip. On the outside, Wretch & Bear went at it tooth and nail, slamming each other face first into the floor, chairs, walls and other hard objects. They continued to fight up into the crowd, while Panther & Havok continued the action in the ring. Security tried to direct Bear & Wretch back to the ring to no avail. Havok waited for the Panther to get up from the monkey flip and then took him down with a spinning heel kick. Havok continued to dominate by going to the outside. He climbed to the top rope and waited for his larger nemesis to return to a vertical base, before diving off with a flying body press. Panther however caught him in midair, and took him to the mat with a backbreaker. Havok' momentum had been thwarted, and the Panther took a few moments to recover from the previous attack. Back in the crowd, Wretch and Bear began to make their way back to ringside, as Bear held Wretch in a side headlock and drug him through the crowd. As they neared the guardrail, Wretch nailed Bear in the ribs with an elbow to the side, and then followed it up with a belly-to-back suplex onto the cement floor. The referee went to the outside to check on the two men, and the fans did not help any as they got in his way and posed for the television cameras. Meanwhile, Panther brought Havok up and whipped him into the ropes, and as Havok returned the Black Panther nearly removed his head from his body with a lariat. Panther followed that up by pressing Havok into the air; but he escaped and fell behind The Black Panther. He went for a waistlock, but Panter countered with a go-behind into a waistlock of his own. From there, Panther hooked his arms up around his ex-friend's head and shoulders, and went for a full nelson suplex. Havok quickly countered with a mule kick, which made Panther bend over in excruciating pain. Frenchie followed it up with a reverse neck breaker... on the outside, a security guard helped Wretch to his feet, while the referee watched the ring with interest. Havok decided he was going to go for the kill and he went to the ring apron. He climbed to the top rope, and signaled for his Millennium-Sault. He leaped through the air and hit it, before covering Panther. The referee hurried back to the ring, with Wretch right behind him as Mike Bear struggled to his feet. The referee slid underneath the bottom rope and began to count, halfway in the ring and halfway out. One, two... and Wretch pulled the referee onto the floor, and the two began to argue. Havok stood up, confused as he had not seen what happened, and looked at the two men arguing on the outside. With everyone distracted, Bear snuck into the ring, and spun Havok around. He took the Havok out with the Whiplash, and went for a cover. The referee hurried back into the ring and Wretch leapt up to the top rope. As the referee reached two, the pinfall attempt was only interrupted as Wretch flew off with a leg drop to the back of Mike Bear' head, splitting up the pin. Wretch celebrated but as he turned around he was met with the large hand of the Black Panther, who appeared to be going for a choke slam. From behind however, Havok crawled to his knees and hit the Panther with a lowblow, and Wretch moved in for the kill. He grabbed Panther from behind and hit the Depression, aka a reverse DDT. Wretch covered the Panther and at two the count was broken up by Bear, who pulled Wretch off of him and positioned him for a Whiplash. Noting that nobody had ever escaped from the Whiplash, as Bear went for it, the agile Wretch countered into a beautiful spinning DDT. Wretch stood in the ring with his arms outstretched, but from behind came Havok, who rolled him up for a pin. At two, Wretch kicked out. Havok was picked up by the Panther however, and met with a choke slam in the center of the ring. Panther turned and he and Wretch looked at each other, both of their rivals out of it. Wretch quickly covered Bear, and Panther quickly covered Havok. The referee begrudgingly counted both pins, and since neither man wanted a screwy double-pin finish, they jumped to their feet at two and charged at each other... both men falling to the mat after they each went for a clothesline. With all four men on the mat and exhausted, Bear was the first to struggle to a vertical base. He climbed up and grabbed hold of the man closest to him, which was Havok. He whipped him to the corners and charged in with a side elbow, but Havok moved and instead Bear met the buckles. Havok spun Bear around and took him down with a single arm DDT, before focusing on the Panther. He brought him up to his feet and hooked him, while Wretch saw his opportunity to seize the day, and attempt to take Bear out. As Havok went to suplex Panther, Panther blocked it, and set Havok up on the top rope. He climbed up to the top rope, and it was obvious he was going for his patented "Pounce", technically known as a superplex. Wretch climbed a corner and prepared to do a move from there, but Bear stumbled to his feet, and fell backwards, thus making Wretch land crotch first on the ropes. Panther hit the Pounce hard, and both he and Havok crashed on the mat. Panther had enough to drape his arm over Havok, as Bear went to the ropes and prepared to hook Wretch for a Whiplash from the top rope... at the last moment he noticed Panther getting the pin on Havok in the ring, and as he set Wretch back in the corner, Hart reached in and grabbed his leg, keeping him in the corner. The referee counted to three, and Panther came out the victor in somewhat of an upset. As Bear yelled at Hart with anger, Wretch nailed him with a forearm, before pushing him back towards the ring. Bear crashed, and Wretch flew off with a senton bomb, before escaping with his fWo International Title intact. Wretch and Hart went backstage after getting the last laugh, while the three men in the ring remained out like a light. Warfare slowly faded to black, with announcers reminding the fans to tune into Slaughter this Sunday. Result: Black Panther by pinfall
