Wednesday Warfare, 18 December 2002

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Previously... Events accelerate toward the end of the year. With the fWo's December Pay Per View Twilight only days away, all eyes are on the promotion and what steps will be taken to ensure long standing success. For after Twilight comes Countdown... three shows that often decide the future of the fWo and create cataclysmic change that has nearly destroyed the fWo in the past. As Countdown looms ever closer, events look nearer than ever to destruction. Eron has identified Wretch as his betrayer, and vengeance threatens to be overwhelming. Borst has reverted to his dangerous ways of old, with the US Championship around his waist. New tag teams have emerged on the scene, with a huge match announced at Twilight to crown new Tag Team Champions. And, just days ago, missing fWo Champion Erik Kelly made a challenge for Twilight that only complicates matters further... Dudley Silver Fox rubbed his temples and looked at the other men sitting around the table. Kid Cool, Thumper, Twitch and Samuel Smooth looked back at him with varying levels of interest. Silver Fox coughed in his hand and began. "Okay. So, things aren't going so bad. We're due to start up house shows in late January, we're finalizing booking venues for our television through March... and all things considered we are in good shape. Most importantly, things aren't going so badly with the wrestlers booking themselves. With one notable exception... Dudley." Grumbling from around the room, and dark glances were cast at each other. "So, Erik Kelly wants his revenge against Dudley. Fine, we assumed it was just going to get blown off since Dudley is making money in several smaller promotions that have seen fit to hire him in a cheap attempt to get more publicity for themselves. Amazing how some people give lip service to it all just being a game, but then turn around and brag about signing a trouble case or attracting a few hundred extra fans to an event. But that is neither here nor there. Fact is, Dudley accepted and we're in a tough spot. Yes, he is going to get paid for the event, but let's be honest here... if Dudley wins then we've got a bigger problem on our hands..." Kid Cool snorted. "Wins? He's going to need a miracle to..." "He might just have one." Samuel Smooth looked around the room. "Think about it. If there is one thing Dudley does well, it's taking every pathetic malcontent and using them to his own advantage." "Yeah mate, you would know about that wouldn't you?" Thumper glared at Samuel Smooth, who fell silent. Silver Fox sighed. "Smooth is right. We don't know who Dudley has been dealing with, and all we do know is that there are plenty of people who would love to make the fWo show ass. Jealous, bitter children who are unable to get their own promotion running have elected to work against us taking every cheap shot they can rather than actually doing any work. So be aware that we may be in for trouble. We don't know who Dudley might convince to show up with him, but the match is signed and set for Twilight." "That's not the only thing you need worry about, bub." Twitch coughed. "There are a few people here who don't like Dudley and might take it upon themselves to... settle matters with him. Dudley gets hurt bad outside of the ring and you've got a nasty lawsuit on your hands. Now tell me... you think the crowd of people we have on our roster will be welcoming him in with open arms? He basically screwed over the old and the new to get where he is. Then you've got the fans and others who blame him for Starr's death. And face facts mates, he was responsible in his own way." Silence descended on the room. Dark glances again from the men at the table, then a knock at the door startled them out of their contemplation. Silver Fox answered it, and nodded to a young delivery man at the door. "Here's the list of talent that's already arrived... and one other thing... Jim Dudley's here too." Silver Fox's eyebrows raised. "Dudley's here? Tonight?" Silver Fox glanced around the room, a look of slight panic on his face. "Get out there. Calm people down. Don't let anything happen... we can... we will get through this without anymore trouble." Kid Cool grinned. "You hope." Silver Fox didn't reply, but the mood in the room spoke for him. It was going to be a long night... Push It "After you, m'dear." "Why thank you, good-sir-knight!" Eli Flair held the door for his very pregnant girlfriend Angel, as the two entered the familar arena in Charleston. The CSWA has been through here dozens of times; and the fWo Hardcore Champion knows the layout well. "Trip's been a little down on himself in the past few weeks, hope a match with me tonight'll get his fires goin' again," said Eli, walking rapidly toward the locker rooms. "It should," replied Angel, "I talked to Ivy earlier today and she told me he was looking forward to it." "Yeah? How's she doing?" "Bored. Very bored." Eli laughed. "She's gotta rehab that knee of hers. Speak from experience." As he opened the inner door, he came face to face with, and nearly crashed into, a man he didn't think he'd see again at a fWo event. Jim Dudley. "Flair," said Dudley. "Dudley," replied Eli. "You know where Fox is set up in here?" asked Dudley, avoiding eye contact. "Yeah," replied Eli, staring a hole through the top of his head, "Most likely he's three hallways over. Big room with double doors almost directly across from the hot zone. Can't miss it." Dudley didn't answer as Eli and Angel passed him and headed down the hall. Jim looked in their direction for a second and a half and headed in the direction Eli pointed. "Hey!" called Eli, finally. Dudley stopped and looked. Eli's back was still to him. "I might not always agree with or respect Kelly as it relates to that belt," said Eli, finally, "but I'm looking forward to him fuckin' you up at Twilight. You deserve it." Jim Dudley looked like he had a rebuttal for a moment - but it was lost to the night air as Eli and Angel continued down the hall. Angel, however, turned around once, for just a moment. She gave him the finger and stuck her pierced tongue out at him. New arrival "Tim... good to see you. Haven't seen you in... what? A year?" Tim shook Silver Fox's hand and smiled. "Almost two. Been down in the Orlando leagues under Twitch and Thumper. They aren't so bad, but they aren't you. Remember the great times we used to have down there? Damn... memories man, memories..." They walked together through the hallways in the backstage bowels of the arena with Fox nodding from time to time at people as they passed by. Finally, they reached a dressing room and Silver Fox gestured toward it with a smile. Tim nodded and smiled in return. "Thanks again for the personal tour. It's been my dream man, my dream to be here. Finally I'm in the fWo... God, I've never wanted anything more." Silver Fox smiled, but it was a pained one that didn't reach his eyes. "Wish your brother felt the same way. You know, I really thought I knew Joseph but now... now I just don't know what to think." "He's never had his heart in it, you know?" Tim put down his bag and shrugged. "I was the wrestler of the family, not Jospeh. He got into it because he had to. After you do time it isn't like people are knocking down your door to hire you. He came here because he had no other choice." "Yeah, I know all of that..." Silver Fox sighed. "But the last couple weeks... the match with Spyder, and then the match with me... he's a different person than the man I thought I knew. This whole accusing another wrestler of being on something just because he's Mexican... I just never imagined that Joseph held that kind of hatred in him... it never came up before." Tim looked troubled, but shook his head. "Yeah well, that's a surprise to me to. But who knows? He's had a hard life. Just remember, you've got the good brother here now... you know, the one that actually likes wrestling? So when do I debut?" Silver Fox smiled. "Soon enough. Pick out a ring name yet?" Tim shrugged. "I'll have one when you're ready for me. Thanks again... and don't worry about Joseph... he does what he does, and all things happen for a reason, man." "Yeah." Silver Fox sighed. "Don't I know it. Good luck, Tim. Here's hoping you don't need it." Fallen vs. Spyder vs. Calm The crowd stirred restlessly as Warfare returned from a commercial break and once more lights began to pulse overhead. Inside the ring stood Calm, introduced without his Kodiak Vic Creed-similar nickname, and for this he looked glad. He stood against the ropes, testing them against his back and sighing slightly as he flexed and bounced off of them in an attempt to limber him up. The lights dimmed further, and Fallen emerged from the top of the entrance ramp. He looked out at the crowd, his face still, and then beckoned to a figure behind the curtains. A woman stepped out who was not immediately recognizable, but after the pair walked to the ring it became more obvious to the older fans who this woman was. Dina Sol walked next to Fallen, and the two said nothing as they reached the ring and Fallen slid inside. He looked down at Dina on the outside and nodded, then dismissing Calm entirely he focused his attention toward the top of the ramp and the final opponent in this three way contest. Fireworks exploded, and Spyder walked out onto the entrance ramp. A microphone in his hand, he looked out at the crowd and smirked. "Holmes... you shut that music off right now and listen good to me. Since coming to the fWo I've beaten anyone I've gotten into the ring with. Remember that cacho... remember what I say. So now I am thinking to myself that it is time I was given a title match against someone worthy of my time, because this waist looks too lonely without some fine gold wrapped around it. So Great Dragon? You have this little title you're defending? Well holmes, you better get ready because I'm coming for you. You may not know when and you may not know where, but that Cruiserweight championship is mine esse... you just don't know it yet." The crowd booed in response, but Spyder ignored them and walked down to the ring, He looked sourly at Fallen as he came, nodding slowly to himself as he did. The match was announced as a Falls Count Anywhere contest, and as Spyder reached the ring the bell sounded and the match was offically underway. Fallen shot a glance back at Calm, his face dark and full of intent. Calm gulped and raised his hands, wanting no part of Fallen at the moment. The distraction was picked up on by Spyder, who slid quickly into the ring and aimed a hard roundhouse kick at Fallen's head. Fallen ducked at the last second and drove his body forward, using the momentum to drive Spyder back into the turnbuckle. The air rushed from the masked luchador at the impact, and Fallen grabbed his neck and delivered held chop after held chop onto his chest. The referee pulled Fallen back, and Spyder rushed forward with a running lariat. Fallen ducked quickly, grabbing Spyder's midsection and slamming him down with a kneeling press slam that shook the mat. Fallen went for a cover, but at that moment Calm attacked from behind with a double axehandle into Fallen's back. Fallen grunted and rolled off, and Calm kicked down at him repeatedly in an attempt to keep him off his feet. Spyder rose, grabbed Calm from behind the neck with a swinging neckbreaker and dropped him with a solid thud before turning his attention back to Fallen. Fallen was slowly getting to his feet, and Spyder wasted no time and springing forward, diving under his legs and wrapping himself around the ropes with Fallen in tow. The hanging tarantula submission hold was devistating, and Fallen screamed in agony as Spyder bore down oblivious to the referee's objections. Calm stood and began to kick at Fallen's chest, the trapped man helpless to defend himself. Grinning broadly at this turn of events, Calm leveled hard chops that darkened the skin where they laced across Fallen's chest. Spyder finally released the hold, and Fallen crumpled to the mat. Spyder re-entered the ring, and Calm raised his hands up in cooperation for a high five. Spyder kicked him in the face, picked him up by the waist and threw him from the ring before turning his attention back to Fallen. Spyder picked him up and dropped him with a quick suplex, then ran to the turnbuckle and climbed up to the top. He dove off with a frog splash that hit its mark, then signalled that he was going up for another. As Spyder climbed, Dina Sol quickly moved around to the corner and grabbed Spyder's boot to prevent him from going any higher. Spyder turned, annoyed at the disruption and kicked down at her, catching Dina in the face. She stumbled backward, and Spyder continued to climb until he reached the top ropes. Just as he was about to dive off, Dina rose and grabbed his boot, then tugged on it causing Spyder to lose his balance. Spyder tumbled, falling face first to the mat and crushing his skull against the mat and rebounding into the metal turnbuckle. The padding prevented serious injury, but Spyder screamed in pain and clutched a nose that was suddenly gushing blood beneath his mask. Fallen rose slowly, clutching his arm and glaring at Spyder. Before he could move in, Calm had entered the ring again and raised his arms in a test of strength. The crowd cheered for this, but Fallen ignored them and aimed a hard kick for Calm's groin instead. Calm collapsed, and Fallen advanced on Spyder who was slipping slowly to the outside of the ring. Fallen kicked him out and rolled under the ropes. On the outside, Fallen grabbed Spyder's head and rammed him into the steel of the steps leading into the ring, then hung him over the metal gate seperating the ringside area from the crowd. Spyder's neck was hung over the steel along with his arms, and Fallen walked back and climbed on top of the announcer's table. He dove with a flying axehandle, but Spyder slipped free at the last moment and Fallen collided with the steel, The barrier toppled, and Fallen lay amidst a ruin of chairs, gate and padding. Spyder got to his feet gingerly, hoisted Fallen up with a belly to back suplex, and brought him down on the concrete with authority. The two continued to brawl out into the crowd, Spyder with the upper hand now, and moved farther and farther away from the ring. Behind them the referee tried to keep his eye on the pinfall attempts and behind the referee came Calm, determined to inject himself into the match when the time was right. Fallen picked up a chair and brought it around just as Spyder leapt for him with a spear, and the chair was caught between them as Spyder's head crashed into the metal of the chair as the chair was driven into Fallen's midsection. Both men fell back into a small section of the crowd, and the fans nearby cheered with enthusiasm at the carnage. Calm moved into the scene at last, and fell on top of the body of the helpless Spyder for the cover. The referee counted once, twice, and then a large hand grabbed Calm and threw him off of the masked man. Oktober picked up Calm, turned him into a piledriver and drove him down into the concrete. Snarling at the referee, he punched Fallen aside as he made his way toward Spyder, who was retreating as he came forward. Security officals were flooding onto the scene, but the crowd didn't make their job easy as they moved to block them from reaching the wrestlers. Oktober picked Spyder up by the neck, then snapped him to the left and down onto the concrete. The effect was devistating as Spyder clutched his neck in agony and let out a piercing scream. The security had finally got to Oktober, who came with them willingly. There was not a smile on his face, but his eyes were locked on Spyder's, who was clutching his neck with pain and still screaming a high wail of agony. All was chaos, and Fallen, ignoring Spyder now moved in on Calm and hit his signature DDT amoung the rubble of chairs and fans. The referee counted to three, and Fallen's music played as he rolled off of Calm and spared a parting glance at Spyder, who was still screaming prone on the ground. Medical officals moved in to take care of Spyder as Oktober was herded toward the curtains. He passed Silver Fox who was out to look on Spyder's condition as he came, and the two glared at each other as they passed. It was Oktober who spoke first. "Consider this my statement of fulfilling my contract Dudley gave me... which you can't do anything about. If you're going to drag my brother into this hell, I'm going to make sure he's taken care of." Silver Fox snarled, "We will see about that. You keep attacking this man because you're a racist and I'll..." "You'll do nothing," Oktober screamed back. "You're a fool... I'm no racist, that man's an addict and you've got him on the God damn payroll. But screw you Fox... you're a glorified janitor here, remember? Don't start thinking you have any power because you don't! We run the show here, not you." "Get out of my sight," Silver Fox growled. Oktober looked at him, and unreadable expression on his face. Finally, the big man turned and vanished behind the curtains. Silver Fox turned and looked back at the carnage with dismay. Spyder was scheduled to wrestle at Twilight... but as he was loaded on a stretcher it was obvious that wasn't going to happen now. Up the ramp came Dina Sol and Fallen, and Silver Fox frowned still deeper. Why was she here, why now? Why with all of their history were these two working together... and when was Dina going to make good on her threat from years ago? The fans rumbled with anticipation for the next match, and Silver Fox turned his attention to more pressing matters... Eron began opening doors to the site, before finally finding an empty, small locker room. Pulling out of his duffle bag, Eron slapped a magnetic sign with his name on it to the door, before walking in and planning the night ahead of him.
