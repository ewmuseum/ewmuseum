Not What You Think The familiar scene of fireworks and the familiar sound of music & sirens go off throughout the arena, before the opening theme to fWo Sunday Night Slaughter quiets down. Without much prior notice, the most recent fWo World Women's Champion Briana walked out onto the stage carrying the Women's Championship. The fans were quiet, and didn't know how to react to this, as by all rights and purposes women were no longer active competitors in the fWo and hadn't been since May. Briana climbed into the ring and held the Women's Title above the ring to little or no reaction, before the cameras went backstage to find a frantic James Armstrong, pacing back and forth near the arena entrance. Who he was waiting for walked through the entrance on time, as the most wanted man in the fWo - Primetime - made his way in. As he did, Armstrong welcomed him in. "Hello, Brian! Glad to see you here tonight." Suspicious, Primetime grabbed him by the collar. "Tell me why you're glad, or else what I said last week will come true." Primetime set him down to explain himself. The acting owner did. "Well, I'm giving you a World Title shot, just like I said I would." With a smirk... "Thank God you've come to your senses. Slugger is a dead man." Eyebrow raised, Armstrong shook his head slightly. "What? No, you're not facing Slugger." Putting two and two together, Primetime assumed that he would be facing the winner of the Boston Strangler & Scott Slugger match. Again, Armstrong didn't see it that way. "No, Brian, you've got it all wrong. You're facing a World Champion alright, because the Women's Title is back... Briana is the champion, and SHE is in the ring right now... waiting for you." Furious, Primetime grabbed Armstrong by the collar again. "What?! You're screwing with me again, James? Is that it? No, don't answer that. Because if that bitch that is in that ring has her career ended tonight, it's going to be on your hands." Primetime pushed Armstrong to the ground, and headed towards the ring... Briana (c) vs. Primetime Back inside the actual arena, "Snap" by Slipknot began to play, and out walked a former fWo Women's Champion himself - Primetime. Primetime was greeted with boos as usual, but paid no attention to them. Instead, as he walked towards the ring, with little effort he popped his knuckles, and climbed in. The referee signalled for the bell, and the match was on. Primetime and Briana met in the center of the ring, and Primetime looked down upon her with anger. He began talking trash, perhaps telling her how she would find a spot at the local hospital emergency room shortly. Not knowing exactly what was said - it was obvious Primetime had crossed the line, and received a slap for his comment. Primetime smirked as the crowd exploded with cheers. Primetime dared to do her to do it again, and she winded up to. Before her hand could make contact, Primetime grabbed it, and stopped her. With evil written on his face, he pulled her right in position for a Primetime Bomb. With her head between his crotch, he smiled to the crowd, before completing the move. The crowd gasped as her back bounced off the mat with a thud, and Primetime looked down at what he had accomplished with a proud smile. Primetime brought Briana up again, and then hit his new finisher - the Snapshot, before just telling the referee to count the pin. He did, and we had a new fWo Women's Champion in Primetime. The referee retrieved the belt, and Primetime looked at it as if it were a joke. Tossing it to the mat, he stood in the corner with his arms raised, before looking back and seeing Briana struggle to her feet. Sneaking up from behind, he grabbed her by the waist, and everyone assumed what was coming. A backdrop driver. Instead, the crowd actually cheered for a man who never received cheers. "Mr. Main Event", Mitch Wilson. He entered the ring and Primetime released Briana from his grasp, before exiting the ring. He grabbed the Women's Title, and held it up, before talking trash to Wilson, who now waited in the ring. Referees rushed the ring to help Briana from the ring, while PT headed backstage. Wilson had his Cruiserweight Title with him, as his match was next... Crusader seems stunned that Wilson kicked out, but knows that it's gonna take more, so he lifts Wilson up for a Michonoku Driver. Wilson gets out and lands behind Crusader, and hooks him in a waistlock. Crusader counters with a go behind, but Wilson nails a mule kick. With Crusader keeled over, Wilson drops him with a dragon suplex. Wilson covers, and at 2 and a half, Crusader kicks out. Wilson brings Crusader up to his feet, and at this time 'The Ego' Greg Davis begins to make his way to ringside. He covers, and with three taps of the mat, the fWo has a new Cruiserweight Champion. The referee hands Crusader his newly won title, and as he holds it up for all to see, Greg Davis hits the ring, and attacks Crusader from behind. Crusader fights back, and eventually, the men begin brawling up the ramp. Security rushes out, and splits up the men, and Slaughter goes into a commercial break.
