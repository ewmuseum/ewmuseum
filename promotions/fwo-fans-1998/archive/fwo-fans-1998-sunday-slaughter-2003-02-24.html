Introduction Previously... Almost two months ago the Fans Wrestling Organization was expelled from the United States following a drug bust in their own locker room. Unbenownst to anyone at the time, fWo investor and strange businessman Mr. Tort had used his political connections to enforce existing sports drug laws, while at the same time supplying new fWo roster member Spyder with heroin. The result was deadly consequences for these actions, and the fWo was forbidden to run shows within the United States. Traveling first to Canada and then to South America, the fWo now continues their road trip into new territory: Africa. With a completely new playing field in front of them, last-minute travel arrangements have brought the wrestling promotion to the coastal pleasure town of Cape Town, South Africa. With a weary roster suffering from the pain of constant travel and severe jet lag, all eyes are on the not-so-simple task of surviving the night... Into Africa The bonfire blazed high, the intense heat sending glowing cinders wafting towards the star-filled night. The drums ebbed and flowed, but the hammering beat was always there, a steady rhythm of bellows, screeches, cries and screams. The drums whispered and sang. The penetrating depth of its bass filled Mufanga Kagos' head like a thick stormcloud of song, and he closed his heavy eyes. It must be the jetlag, thought Mufanga, as his head lolled to his chest. The Muzungu tribe lerched and danced around this fire, an orgy of twisting, bending bodies and limbs. They were mostly naked, even the women, except for the odd scant loincloth, and the intricate headdresses they all wore. They sang in a tongue unknown to Mufanga (weknowit... itsingsofdeath), but the melodies and voices were still almost hypnotic. Mufanga Kagos was the outsider here, invited by the Muzungu to take part in thier annual tribal rites. The fWo thought it was a good idea to get friendly with the South African government, during this first leg of their African Continental tour, and volunteered Mufanga, the "Wildman of Lagos", to take part in this visit to the sparce desert. He knew they couldn't tell he was actually from Detoit, and was as much an African native as he was the King of France. They had no idea that he sat so still and quiet because he didn't know the language or the customs. They thought he was being stoic, being proud and reserved. He felt so out of place (home.wearehome) that he felt nervous, shifting on his crosslegged seat in the sand. It must be the smoke from the fire, thought Mufanga. My head is starting to ache again. "Drink, you must drink." A dark moon-like face loomed down on his, shadowed by the violent fire behind him. The figure held out a cup, brimming with liquid. Mufanga Kagos looked around frantically, searching for the African guide that brought him out here. What was he supposed to do? Walk back to Capretown? Hell, Slaughter was due to start in a half hour... And what the hell was this stuff they wanted him to drink? The guide was gone now, he saw. Mufanga was on his own. He nodded somberly and took the roughly hewn cup from the tribesman. It felt like an animal hide in his hands, and probably was. He raised the cup to his face, and the smell hit his broad nostrils. Bitter, like vinegar, but that wasn't it. An oily smell. "Drink." The man said again, smiling a wide toothless grin. His headdress had dozens of tiny mouse skulls sewn into the design in a frantic patchwork. Behind him, the drumbeat was picking up pace, and the dancers followed, circling the fire in dizzying run. Mufanga drank at once, upending the cup over his open throat. It was like drinking fire. An intense heat flowed into his stomach and seemed to grip his heart as it did triple beats in his chest, as if keeping time with the African drummers. The cup fell to the sand, and Mufanga's head slumped to his chest as his eyes rolled back into his skull. "Come. It is time to dance." The toothless old man medicineman said, extending his hand. A thin line of drool escaped Mufanga's open mouth as he stood to his full height. The lights of Capetown glittered on the horizon as Mufanga begun a low growl, just loud enough over the drums so the old man could hear. The ancient tribesman backed away at once, his eyes looking like saucers in his dry, wrinkled face, just now seeing Mufanga Kagos for the first time. "It's... you." he said in his thick-tongued accent, his body paralyzed in fear as he stood face to face with the Beast. Yes.Itisme. And Mufanga Kagos danced. The crowd grimaced, realizing how much damage that must have done to Monte's bad back, and Voran rolled over into a cover, hooking the leg. ONE! TWO! THR-NO! This time, it was Monte who kicked out just before three. Voran grabbed Monte and turned him over, and placed him in a Bow and Arrow Lock. As Monte screamed out in pain, Voran digged his knees further into Monte's injured back, and screamed "TAP OUT YOU WASHED-UP FUCKER!". The camera panned around this scene of pure and unadulturated pain- Voran, blood flowing so fast from his forehead that it seemed like he should be passing out soon, and Monte, Voran's knees digging deep into his injured back and causing possiable permanent damage. And yet still, Monte refused to submit. After around three minutes more of mind-numbing pain, Voran finally had to release the hold, the rapid loss of blood imparing his vision. He took a moment to pull himself up using the ropes, to try and get back to full conciousness. Monte, meanwhile, managed to stand up also by using the ropes, despite his back being in extreme pain. He ran foward at Voran, catching him by surprise and clotheslining him over the top rope and to the outside, and went barrelling out with him. As voran got back up, Monte kicked him in the stomach and drilled him with a Double Underhook Backbreaker. He picked the exhausted Voran back up and slid him onto the Spansih announce table, then climbed onto the apron and up to the top turnbuckle. Monte pointed down at Voran, holding his back for a moment, and the crowd erupted. He knew what he had to do...... .......and he flew off with a Flying Elbow from the top turnbuckle and onto Voran's chest, both men crashing through the announce table. Voran and Monte layed on the outside in a heap of wreckage, neither man moving. After a few seconds, Monte made it back to his feet, and he slid into the ring, as the referee was up to the count of 7 at this point. As he reached 9 with no sign of movement from Voran, Monte grabbed the referee by the collar. "Stop the fucking count." Monte told him. "I don't want to beat him like this. I want to take his title, and take his pride, not win by count out." The referee apparently agreed, because he stopped counting. Monte slid to the outside and picked the bloody and beaten Voran up- he still hadn't moved since going through the table, and Monte figured that he had passed out due to a combination of the blow and the drastic blood loss. His blood loss had reached Austin-at-Wrestlemania 13 levels- his face was covered in a crimson mask thanks to the previously-stiched-up cuts that Monte had re-opened with the chair shot. Monte tossed Voran into the ring and covered him. ONE! TWO! THR-NO! Voran's leg was on the bottom rope, and the referee was forced to stop his count. The crowd booed loudly and Monte stood up and yelled something at the referee, but the referee told Monte there was nothing he could do. Monte angrily pulled the still-unconcious Voran into the center of the ring, and then pointed to the top rope. As the crowd cheered, Monte climbed to the top for the third time that night, and was about to try his finishing manuver for the second time. The Establishment. Monte flew off..... .......and he hit it. The crowd erupted as Monte covered Voran, and it was academic at this point as the referee slid to the side of Monte and Voran and made the count. ONE! TWO! THREE! ........NO. The crowd erupted in boos. Why? Because the referee had been pulled out of the ring. By Jason Winters. The former IOW owner. Oh, fuck. Jason Winters smiled as the referee angrily yelled at him, but only responded with a punch to the face, knocking the fWo referee down to the floor. Jason Winters slid into the ring with the chair Monte had left on the outside, and as Monte got back to his feet, cracked the chair over Monte's bad back. As Monte held his back in pain, Jason helped Voran back to his feet, and Voran gave a strange expression. The fans wondered if Voran was even involved in the planning of this screwjob, or if Jason had done this all alone. Maybe, just maybe, there was a shimmer of hope that the Voran Xias of old would return, kick the former owner in the stomach, and drill him with a Cradle Piledriver..... .....but then, Voran gave a sickening smirk to the crowd, and they knew. Oh, they knew. And they responded with one of the loudest negative reactions ever to be heard. Boos, jeers, "VORAN SUCKS!" chants, you name it, they screamed it. Voran just continued to smirk, as he wiped the blood out of his eyes and picked Monte up. Jason placed the chair into the center of the ring, and Voran kicked Monte in the stomach. He delivered the Cradle Piledriver to Monte on the chair, his neck crashing down into the metal. But that wasn't enough. Voran picked Monte up again. Another Cradle Piledriver onto the chair, and security had to hold the fans back from rushing into the ring at this point. Voran turned Monte over and covered him as Jason slid to the outside and tossed the referee back into the ring. Jason grabbed the referee's hand, and made the count. One. Two. Three. Was it an official decision or not? Didn't matter. Because as "Fight Song (Slipknot Remix)" by Marilyn Manson blared over the speakers, and Voran Xias and Jason Winters raised arms in the middle of the ring while Slaughter slowly faded out, the result of the match was as clear as it could be. Voran and Jason were the winners. And Monte and the fans.....they were the losers. Result: 'Undeniable' Voran Xias via Pinfall (?); Retains IOW World Heavy
