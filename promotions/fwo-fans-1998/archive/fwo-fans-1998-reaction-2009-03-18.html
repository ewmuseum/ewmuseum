The Start of a Long Evening Inside of the Key Arena Parking Garage there are pillars after pillars with cars in between them. A mixture of the finest cars and some rather rickety cars that your parents probably had when they started to have sex. You might be lost as to why there's a camera on the parking garage, but as the noise filled your ears it instantly became clear to you. RRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR Smoke filled the garage as the black Audi TT flew into the screen and slammed itself into the first parking spot it could possibly find. As the door flew open and the man stepped out of it, the tension inside of the parking garage alone rose a few degrees. He threw the door shut on his car and walked around to the trunk of the luxurious car before he tossed it open and dug around the contents. The camera zoomed in on the man and it didn't take a rocket scientist to see the boiling red hot eyes and figure out that the man behind those fiery things was the man they called Dusk. YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAH! The fans instantly rose to their feet inside of the arena, but frankly this moment isn't about them. His left hand wrapped around the top of the trunk and he slammed it shut before he turned his attention elsewhere. As he began to walk, anger in his step, and bruises plastered across his face and body, it was evident that there was something in his hands. As he came closer to the camera it was revealed to be a baseball bat. He walked with a purpose as he looked around before he kicked the door directly in front of him open and continued to walk through the backstage area. The backstage crew was already hard at work on getting the show started, but when word had come down that Dusk was arriving to the arena and he was filled with rage, they instantly knew what the show was going to start with. He stormed through the backstage area, his eyes glancing from side to side. Everyone knew who he was looking for though and they weren't going to get in his way. The moment it had been announced that Dusk and Deacon would be meeting in the evening's main event, they knew that the fireworks would be lit the moment the two men stepped inside of the building. That was obviously the truth as they looked at the baseball bat in his hands that he clearly intended to bash someone in the face with. One man though wasn't so lucky as he turned around and ran smack into the face of the Lost Soul. "Where is he?!" The man just stood there, eyes wide open, until Dusk pushed him out of the way and kept moving with one purpose in mind. "I want the bastard and I want him now! DEACON! Come out so I can kill you, you limey fuck!" Oh yeah, it was going to get ugly. Real ugly. He came up to a set of double doors and as he slammed his foot into the door, the parting of the seas happened, and standing at the far end of the hallway was the Mute Freak, or at least his back. Various wrestlers, managers, and a variety of backstage crew hugged against the wall as Dusk ran down the hallway and tugged on the shoulder of Deacon before he began to repeatedly slam his fist into the face of the 7 foot beast! The commotion in the backstage area was something else as people started yelling as Dusk and Deacon started to trade punches with one another. With the baseball bat on the ground, Dusk found himself firing shot after shot to the face of Deacon and ignored the punches that were flying back at him. They hurt, sure, but the pain he was doleing out on Deacon more than made up for it until he felt the hands tugging against his clothes and pulled him away from Big D! "Get the fuck off of me! Get off!" Dusk's voiced filled the entire hallway as nearly two dozen people seperated Dusk and Deacon away from each other. While Dusk's eyes looked filled with fire, Deacon looked a little dazed from the surprise attack on him. Dusk though had been crucified and was a man looking for revenge and blood for the weeks of attacks. "MOVE!" Dusk pushed some of the people off of him, but for each one he pushed it seemed like two more came to be in his way. Yet, they couldn't keep him back for long as he dropped his shoulder and pushed through the crowd until he leapt on top of Deacon, blasting fist after fist into the face of Deacon until blood started to stain his knuckles and Deacon was crouched on the ground, trying to protect himself as the various people were holding his arms while they tried to stop Dusk! "This is for Mary-Lynn!" Dusk slammed his fist again into the face of Deacon until a forearm wrapped around his neck and drug him away from Deacon once again, but Dusk quickly pushed the guy off of him before he decked the backstage man and dropped him onto the cold concrete! Dusk looked around, but was met with the large, meaty hand of Deacon who knocked him into the nearby wall and started nailing him with a variety of punches to the head, neck, and body! Dusk though refused to go down without a fight as the two started trading punches again, but it was clear that Dusk had awakened the beast as he started to lose the fight quickly. As Deacon went to slam another fist into Dusk's face again though, he was dragged off of Dusk and slammed into a nearby wall. The Lost Soul shrugged the blows off, feeling the impact of them, but knew that there was something bigger at hand. He pushed each man off of him, his rage nearly out of control at this point, and then picked up his baseball bat off the ground before he moved through the crowd again and slammed it against the jaw of Deac. Deacon dropped to one knee as Dusk looked at him in the eyes. "This is for David!" He then hit him again with the baseball bat and blood came flying off the swollen bottom lip of Big D. A few men tried to stop Dusk, but he quickly flashed his bat at them and they backed off. He rose his bat again as he looked down at Deacon. "This is for Tony!" The bat slammed against his face this time and left a welt against his right cheek. "This is for Jack, you fucking coward!" BAM! Deacon found himself on both knees from the three blows to his face from the baseball bat, and as Dusk went for another one, another swarm of people came against him before they pushed him against the wall again. Another fifteen people flew into the room and they seperated Dusk from Deacon again as Dusk threw the bat into the crowd as he stood on a chair and looked at the fallen Deacon. "I'm going to kill you! Do you understand me you fucking cunt?! I'm going to kill you and leave you for dead in that ring! You're dead!" They kept dragging Dusk out of the room as he pointed straight at Deacon, who was slowly rising to his feet again. Dusk had awakened the beast, but Dusk wasn't worried about that. He wanted the beast tonight. "I'm going to kill you!" He was going to slay the beast tonight. He happened to glance to the left – something didn’t seem quite right… THERE!!! The big blonde beauty was a DEAD giveaway. The mark he’d been looking for was walking like a sheep, head down in a book oblivious. Decker kind of regained his senses just as he saw the big blonde bitch slam the door behind her. The loud noise seemed to wake something in him, as if he’d been physically stuck in Vaseline for the last few seconds.
