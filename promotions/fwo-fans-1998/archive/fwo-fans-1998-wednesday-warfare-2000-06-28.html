Wednesday Warfare, 28 June 2000

28 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Seeing Is Believing "I don't believe this. Slaughter jumped up a point, but only because of two things. How the hell can this be?" said the timid owner of the fans Wrestling organization, Chris Finn. His right hand man James Armstrong stood there in wonderment. "What?" Finn showed him the proof. "Look at this. Ratings were up, but because of two quarter hour segments. The ones that showcased... I don't believe this... Wretch." The look on Armstrong' face was priceless. His jaw fell open, nearly touching the floor. Usually, this expression only happened to Armstrong when he was playing blackjack or hanging out with some broads, but for some reason, it happened now. "Liar. There's no way. No way this could be true, Finn." He scanned the paper more closely. "I don't.. believe this. Did I mention I don't believe this?" Finn nodded. "I don't believe it either." Just then, the door knocked. Finn asked who it was. "It's me, it's me, it's that Ratings Saver, Big Wretchy!" Finn sighed. He opened the door, looking at Wretch in a disturbed manner. "Yes, Wretch?" Wretch grinned. "That's Big Wretchy to you, pally! On to my question - am I booked tonight?" Finn looked back at Armstrong, who reached for the Warfare card, then handed it to him. Finn looked at it, before spouting off what was booked. "Let's see here... we've got four matches from the Next Level Tournament... that being Neo versus Xavier, Crusader versus Hellrazor, Lance Star versus Machina, and Courage versus Mike Bear... then in the main event... we've got The Boston Strangler & Scott Slugger versus Ice & Mitch Wilson versus Ric Chronos & Fallen versus... well, you and Scot." Wretch looked at Finn as if he didn't believe him. "What? I don't believe this!" Finn showed him the paper. Wretch looked at it hard. "What? I don't believe this!" Armstrong muttered something about nothing being believable anymore, before shrugging. Finn finally asked Wretch if he wanted anything else. "Well... I was wondering how we did on the ratings Sunday." Wretch grinned, before Finn showed him the proper document. "What? I don't beli... er... wait a minute... I told you, Finn! I told you that I would save your ratings! And to prove it further... not only am I going to be in the main event tonight, I - myself - Big Wretchy, The Ratings Saver and fWo International Champion am going to sit at ringside for each and every match and keep the ratings up!" Finn tried to reason with him. "Look, Wretch... Big Wretchy... whatever you're calling yourself... we don't really need that." Wretch looked at him in a shocked manner. "Don't really need that? Let me ask you something, Finn. Who's quarter hour ratings were the highest on Slaughter? Mine. Don't really need that? Bah!" Finn finally gave in. "Alright, you and your lackey and your lackey's lackey can have three seats at out ringside, but no 'causing trouble, you got that?" Wretch gave him the thumbs up, before exiting. "I bet... the ratings for this segment will be... through the roof!" Finn and Armstrong sighed, as Warfare began inside the arena. Neo vs. Xavier Fireworks resounded throughout the Bradley Center, thus signalling the start of fWo Wednesday Warfare, dated June 28, 2000. As they did, the Warfare theme echoed off the walls of the arena, before a trio of men stepped out onto the stage and greeted with a huge ovation of cheers. On the right was The Mutha Fuckin' Abusah Of Yo Momma, on the left was Scot Hartt, and in the middle was the fWo International Champion Big Wretchy, The Ratings Saver. As the three men walked down the ramp they found some seats at ringside before well... sitting in them, anxious to watch the action that was about to unfold. Lights dimmed, and the intense music of Godsmack pounded out over the arena speaker system. First only his shadow was visible, then Xavier walked slowly out onto the entrance ramp. Dressed in full leather and sneering out at the crowd from behind dark sunglasses, Xavier strode with complete confidence down the steel gantry toward the ring. He slapped no hands, signed no autographs, and spoke to none of the fans. Xavier slid into the ring and looked around at the arena with distain. He brushed his hands off and clinched then into fists. As his music slowly died away, he prepared himself. Clips were shown on the TravisTron of events leading up to this match, including the Hardcore Championship match where Neo lost the title to Xavier. A clip of Neo's interview on Slaughter was then shown, and as the footage died away Xavier snorted with laughter. Neo's name was called... but the entrance ramp remained empty. Xavier snorted with laughter again, and began to demand that the referee call the match and award him the victory so he would advance. An argument ensued between the two, and suddenly Stigmata crashed over the speakers. Xavier looked up the entrance ramp as Neo slowly made his way out from behind the curtain. He walked with a limp, and hunched over was a shell of his former self... but the snarl on his face was unmistakable. This was Neo, and Neo was coming to the ring for his match. Xavier threw up his hands in disgust and paced inside the ring as Neo make the long walk to ringside. Neo took his time walking up into the ring, pausing quickly to growl at a fan who was holding a Neo sign. Ringside attendants urged Neo to give up the match and go get medical attention, but Neo shrugged them off and climbed into the ring. Xavier and Neo came together in the center of the ring, staring hard at each other. Xavier began to curse at Neo, and Neo responded with foul language of his own. The two were soon screaming at each other, and then Xavier brought up his hands for a lock up. Neo however cocked his fist back and hammered a hard uppercut into Xavier's face. Xavier stumbled backward surprised, and the referee quickly yelled a warning to Neo and signaled the timekeeper to ring the bell. Neo either didn't hear him or didn't want to, because he advanced on the still stunned Xavier and hit another rabbit punch into the big man's face, this time opening up a gash on Xavier's mouth. Xavier looked down at his shirt, quickly growing dark from crimson blood. He began to yell an angry protest, but Neo was on him again, punching with closed fists at his opponents face and snarling a rabid, insane growl. Spittle flew in the air as Neo began to scream, and as the bell rang in the background to signal the end of the contest Neo continued to hammer away at Xavier. A referee grabbed Neo's back, and Neo whirled around and grabbed his shirt. Pulling the referee toward him, Neo punched down into the referee's face. As the referee crumpled, Neo headed back for Xavier who was still shocked by this turn of events. Security ran toward the ring as the crowd shouted in angry confusion. Flash bulbs burst into the arena sending bright halos spiraling around the ring. Eight security men slid inside the ring and clawed at Neo in an attempt to pull him free from his fight. Finally one of the men brought a billy club down hard into Neo's leg, and Neo fell backward screaming. Security began to pull Neo free from the ring, but now Xavier had regained his senses and charged into the fray, tossing security men aside as he tackled Neo down on the mat. Geoff Green was out now, and screaming for the cameras to go to commercial... go to commercial now. Somewhere in the arena word spread that Neo had been disqualified and Xavier would advance... but the attention was for a change firmly on the loser... and the seemingly insane actions of the man called Neo. Result: Xavier by disqualification A Friendship Is Forged The 6 foot 10 inch giant known as the Black Panther knocks firmly on Fallen's door. Fallen opens it and looks oddly at him as the Panther motions towards the room beyond. "May I speak with you, John?" Fallen nods once, and steps aside to let him in. The Panther sits in one of the two small chairs in the room, and looks steadily at Fallen, who looks away from him after a few moments. Finally, after an uncomfortable silence, the Panther speaks. "I have been watching the fWo closely since the actions of Death near the end of the millenium. I have seen a friendship bloom between you and Ric Chronos..." Fallen interrupts him. "We were friends a long time ago. I... I'm starting to remember what a real friend is now, after..." The Panther pauses, and then quietly whispers, "...after Joy's death?" Fallen jumps, as if an electric current arced through his body, his eyes firmly fixed to the floor. The Panther, noting the reaction, continues to speak. "I know it is hard on you, John. Joy was my friend as well. He was the reason I fought Death. He was the reason I sought to save the fWo from destruction. The World Title was merely a bauble compared to the reasons I did what I did." The Panther shifted slightly in his chair, for the first time showing discomfort, before continuing. "I miss him as well, but you have a new friend now, and he needs help." Fallen finally raised his eyes to look at him, wondering how much he really knew of the man behind the mask. "What do you mean?" The Panther, unused to showing empathy, tried awkwardly to lean forward and demonstrate his concern, but the mask hid his expressions of the attempt. "We've both lost friends before, but Ric... Ric is an alcoholic. I've spoken a little to his best friend, Eddy Fate, and he's told me that Ric has had problems before." Fallen's expression changed from one of depression to one of concern. "How so?" The Panther spread his hands as he continued. "His father passed away recently, and Ric almost lost himself to the bottle then. His acceptance into the fWo and his mission to bring you out of yourself are the only things that have kept him from going back. But he isn't strong enough to handle the pressure of being an fWo singles contender and helping you. You saw how focused he was Wednesday night. If he's concentrating on the Next Level tournament, if he's out drinking with Damnation, how can he be here to help you?" He stands up and walks over to Fallen, holding out his hand. "You need to be Ric's friend, John. He needs your help, because he can't help you anymore. But I was there when Joy died. I feel the same pain you do. Ric doesn't understand you like I do. I'm willing to help, to be your friend while you save Ric." Fallen looks at the hand. "Why would you do this for me?" The Panther stops for a moment, but keeps his hand out. "Because it is what Joy would have wanted." Fallen tries to think as the various emotions wash over him, and finally puts his hand in the Panther's. "Thank you. Thank you... my friend." The Panther smiles, squeezing the hand in a firm yet tender manner as Warfare resumed inside the arena for the next match-up. Crusader vs. Hellrazor Warfare switches gears to the sounds of "Switchback" by Biohazard. The music brings out fWo Cruiserweight Champion, Crusader, to a huge reaction from the capacity crowd. Crusader steps into the ring, and "From this Day" by Machinehead plays, bringing out one half of the fWo tag team champions, Hellrazor. Hellrazor steps into the ring, noticeably absent is Fear. The bell rings, and this first round Next Level tournament match is underway. Collar and elbow tieup, brought into a side headlock by 'Razor. Hellrazor shoots Crusader into the ropes. Crusader ducks a clothesline, and comes back with a lariat of his own. Crusader brings Hellrazor up, and looks for a death valley driver. However, Hellrazor slides out of it, and pushes Crusader towards the ropes. Hellrazor rolls him up, but can only manage a two count. Both men quickly up to their feet, and Crusader ducks a >clothesline, and then drops Hellrazor with a quick belly to back suplex. Crusader climbs on top with a quick cover, but Hellrazor manages a shoulder up at two. Crusader, the young superstar, brings Hellrazor back up to his feet. Crusader looks for a kick, but Hellrazor holds onto the leg. Crusader looks for an enziguri kick from this, but Hellrazor ducks it. Crusader lands on his feet, and Hellrazor then takes him over in a fisherman's suplex. He bridges off of it, and into a pinning combination. He gets a two count, but at that, Crusader is able to get a quick shoulder up. Hellrazor brings Crusader back to his feet. A quick shot to the mouth via a forearm by Hellrazor is enough to back Crusader into the corner. Hellrazor perches Crusader on the turnbuckle, and climbs after. He tries hooking him for a suplex, but Crusader won't budge. Crusader nails some forearms to the face of Hellrazor, before hooking him. He takes Razor over and down to the mat with a big neckbreaker. Feeling that the high impact move was enough to garner a win, Crusader jumps on top of Hellrazor with a cover. One, two, and no, somehow, Hellrazor was able to get a shoulder up, and stay alive in the match, and the next level tournament. Crusader, with an astonished look on his face, brings Hellrazor to his feet, and looks to plant him with a DVD. However, on the way down, Hellrazor is able to hook Crusader into a small package. One, two, and somehow, Crusader gets out of that surprise pinning attempt. Both men reach their feet at precisely the same time. Crusader with a lariat, ducked. Hellrazor with a boot to the gut, Crusader keels over, and Hellrazor looks for a facebuster. However, somehow, Crusader grabs Hellrazor by the legs, and drops him to the mat. Crusader jumps over Hellrazor, hooking both legs. He recordes the three count, and with the win, advances into the second round of the next level tournament. Result: Crusader by pinfall Starstruck The cameras went backstage to find Lance Star, who was standing near his stablemates in the Future Foundation - Courage & Primetime. Already dressed in his wrestling gear (which consisted of a black shirt with bold white lettering that ready "JUST DRINK IT", blue and black wrestling tights, and while they weren't part of his attire one could note he had some freshly grown sideburns), Lance Star began to speak in a serious manner which is a behavior we hadn't seen from him in some time. "Mackinuh... Machina.. macadamian nuts... it doesn't matter what name you were born with, it doesn't matter what your driver's license says, it doesn't matter what your mom said when she conceived your ugly ass and truth be known, it was simply "What the hell is this?"... the only thing you're going to be called tonight and from this point on forever is "loser". Because I am the future of professional wrestling, I am a winner, I am the man who will take the fWo into the Next Level, and if won't be me, you can bet it will be Courage. Because Courage equals rock and roll, Courage equals asses in the seats, and Courage most definately equals ratings. One of us will be the man who is handed the torch to take the fWo to the Next Level, and tonight I'm going to use that torch to light your proverbial ass on fire." Lance Star then winked at the camera, before camera's headed to the ring and the ring announcer prepared to introduce one-third of the Future Foundation, Lance Star. Lance Star vs. Machina The Next Level tournament continued as Lance Star made his way out onto the entrance ramp to the music of Rage Against the Machine. On either side of him stood the other members of the Future Foundation, Courage and Primetime. Star began to walk down to the ring, but then turned and nodded to his partners that they should return backstage. With a shrug Courage and Primetime complied, both anxious as to the result of this match and the match Courage would have with Mike Bear later in the night. Lance Star continued down to the ring and swung himself up and over the top rope of the ring. He stood, absorbing the surroundings and loosening himself up for the match to come. The lights dimmed somewhat, and the voice of a young girl played out, "He's here..." The lights dimmed further, and on the TravisTron the words "Judgment Day Is Now" flashed into view. A great motor could be heard, and then down the entrance ramp came Machina, riding inside a small shriners automobile complete with a fez atop his head. In the background Kid Rock declared him an American Badass, and Machina circled the ring several times in the small motorized automobile before stepping out and slowly climbing up the steel steps to the ring. As Machina climbed inside the ropes Lance Star rushed him, but too slowly as Machina was able to drop back out onto the floor. Machina attempted to climb in on another end of the ring, but once more Star prevented him from entering. The referee stepped in to push Star backward and allow Machina entry, but Machina looked as though he had already lost any confidence the crowd might have given him upon entry. As the bell rang Lance Star and Machina locked up, but it was Star who kicked at the leg while tying up the hands of the masked wrestler. With Machina now stumbling back, Star quickly applied a headlock and dug the palms of his hands into the neck for additional leverage. Machina moved toward the ropes and made it, causing the headlock to be broken. Star anticipated this however, and retaliated with a stiff blow to the back of the head with his forearm. Star backed up, allowing Machina to rebound off the ropes and stumble back toward the center of the ring. Timing his attack, Star rushed forward and hit a running face slam on Machina to the mat. Star let himself fall backward into the ropes, and accelerated off of them, jumped the prone body of Machina, ran up to the second rope, and landed a full body splash that hit perfectly. Star fell on Machina for the cover, but the masked wrestler was able to get a shoulder up in time for the two count. Star pushed himself backward and stood, beating Machina to the vertical base. He hammered Machina with a short burst of hard martial arts kicks, first to the body, then the head. Machina was reeling now, and Star brought him forcibly to his feet. Star grabbed an arm and attempted a whip into the ropes, but Machina at the last moment reversed it. Star went into the ropes, but the still groggy Machina was surprised as Star barreled forward, flattening him with a tackle. Machina fell backward, and Star wasted little time in locking in a knee bar. Machina struggled against the hold, trying with everything he had to move toward the ring ropes. As the referee asked for a submission, Machina reached and grabbed hold of the bottom ring rope, and Star was instructed to break the hold. He did, but followed up right away with a double underhook suplex that took Machina down to the mat once more. Machina rolled backward and up, coming groggily to his feet. Star went into the ropes once more and charged forward with a tackle, but Machina ducked to one side and spun around to add pressure to Star's momentum. With a shove up and over the top rope, Lance Star was sent crashing to the outside. Machina fell to his knees, taking the moment to catch his breath while the referee started the ten count. Outside the Lone Gunmen had made their way to ringside, and now stood around Lance Star looking at him with open dislike. Lance Star slowly got to his feet on the outside, glared at Machina's partners in the Lone Gunmen, and then moved back toward the ring. He entered slowly, and Machina met him as he slid under the ropes with a kick to the back of the head. Star stood, and Machina who was now starting to gain some momentum hit with a back heel kick that sent Star staggering back toward the ropes. Machina went for a second one, but Lance Star ducked under it, and came back with a lariat. Star was still slow following the tumble to the outside however, and Machina caught him first and lifted him up in a clumsy gorilla press. Machina dropped Star backward to the mat, and stumbled and fell himself into the padded turnbuckle. Machina stood against it breathing hard and waiting for Star to get to his feet. As Star did, Machina charged out and hit a forward spear in the center of the ring and drove both of them down to the mat once more. Machina went for a cover, but Lance Star got a shoulder up at two. Machina stood and began to argue with the referee, but it was Lance Star who took advantage of the situation by creeping up from behind the masked competitor. As Machina turned, Star kicked into his midsection and planted Machina to the mat with a float-over DDT. Star was slow in going for the cover, and as a result got only a one count. Wasting no time Star got back to his feet and grabbed at the head of Machina. Bringing him into the corner, Star propped him up and began to hammer hard into his head. After eight punches, Machina stumbled forward and fell face-first into the mat. Star on the other hand climbed, and from the top rope signaled once into the air and then dove with a split-legged moonsault onto the helpless opponent. He hit it hard, and again went for a cover. At the count of two Machina draped a foot over the bottom rope, breaking the count once more. Frustrated Star slammed his fist into the mat, then stood and in anger removed his elbow pad. He tossed it to the outside and pointed up into the air, signaling for the Shooting Star. He drug Machina into the center of the ring, but then Rudo got up onto the ring apron and began to point and yell at Lance Star. The referee moved over to get him back onto the arena floor, but as he was distracted Sid slid into the ring and behind Star. Sid grabbed the tired Star's shoulder and spun him around, and then tossed a bag of power into Star's eyes. Star stumbled backward as Sid made a quick escape, and a very groggy Machina hooked the leg and dropped Star backward to the mat in a pinfall position. The referee, finished with Rudo, noticed the cover and dropped to the mat to count. The hand fell three times and the bell rang, and Machina wearily slid to the outside to celebrate with his partners in the Gunmen. Their celebration was short lived however, as Courage and Primetime ran to the ring with steel chairs. The Gunmen made a quick retreat through the crowd as Courage and Primetime glared at the departing cowards. They joined Lance Star inside the ring and helped him to his feet. Star was disgusted and shouted at the referee who allowed the cheating to take place. The referee just shook his head that he didn't see any cheating, and Lance Star planted him with a fist to the face. Star again signaled at the crowd for the Shooting Star, and planted the referee into the mat with his trademark finisher. As Wednesday Warfare went to a commercial break, the Future Foundation walked back up the entrance ramp toward the locker room. Result: Machina by pinfall Crucified (III) The smell of ether again reeked throughout the area adjacent to this particular lockerroom, which was coincidentally given to Eron The Relentless, as noted by the placard on the door. Nevertheless, the cross that had been seen in previous attacks was present, yet nobody presided in it. Instead, the lockerroom was empty, as it appeared Eron escaped before the attack could occur. Thus far he was the only fortunate one. Robert Jensen & Frank Snow weren't so lucky. And if history should tell you something it's this - those who fail to realize it are doomed to repeat it. Someone was going to fall prone to another attack. Soon. Courage vs. Mike Bear Warfare returns to the sounds of "Surfacing"by Slipknot, which brings out Supreme Overlord Courage for his first round Next Level tournament match with Mike Bear. The former International champion stretches for a bit in the ring, until "Dragula" by Rob Zombie hits. This song brings out Mike Bear, and Bear wastes no time running to the ring, and begins to trade fists with his arch-rival, Courage. However, it's the Future Foundation superstar gaining the upper hand. Courage takes one big swing, and Bear, however, ducks it. Bear smoothly gets behind Courage, and drops him with a belly to back suplex. Bear with a quick cover, and Courage kicks out at two. Bear bringing courage back his feet, and sends him into the ropes. He catches him in a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker, and immediately jumps on with a cover. One, two, but this time, Courage once again kicks out. Back to a vertical base for both men again, and Bear sets Courage facing the crowd on the top turnbuckle. Bear climbs up to the second, and looks for a belly to back superplex. However, Courage hits a few elbows to the face of Bear, and Bear goes falling to the mat. Courage takes the opportunity, and flies off onto Bear with a big elbowdrop from the top. Courage covers, but Bear manages to get out at two. Courage brings him back to his feet, and hits a few forearms to the head, which back Bear into the ropes. Courage nails a Courageinoku Driver on Bear, and immediately covers. One, two, and a kickout there by Bear. Courage lifting Bear up to his feet, snap suplex, and a beauty. Cover. Bear kicks out again at two. Courage decides to stomp on Bear's head for a while, and then go to the top. Courage looks for a big splash, but Bear is able to roll out of the way, avoiding the blow. Bear up to his feet now, quick DDT! Cover, and Courage is able to get a shoulder up at two and a half. Bear brings Courage up, a few punches to the face, before whipping him into the ropes. Bear ducks down, Courage jumps over. Coming back, Bear leapfrogs. However, it's Courage on the third try who is able to connect with a swinging neckbreaker. Courage covers, and Bear gets a shoulder up at two. Courage brings Bear up, and the two begin trading fists in the middle of the ring. Bear ducks a shortarm clothesline by Courage, and nails a boot to the gut. He sets Courage up, and drops him with a forward piledriver, more commonly known as the Pedigree. He covers, and Courage once again kicks out at two. Bear then gets that manical look on his face, and sets himself, and Courage on the top rope. Bear comes off of the top with a regular piledriver, not a tombstone, and plants Courage's head into the mat. He covers, and once again Courage with a shoulder up at two. He punches Courage, and it sends Courage through the ropes, and onto the apron. Bear follows with him onto the apron, and looks to set him up for a powerbomb to the floor. However, Courage blocks, then hooks Bear, and nails the same russian legsweep to the floor as he did to Crusader at Critical Condition. With Bear on the floor, Courage takes some time to mock the crowd, before doing a suicide dive through the ropes, and connecting with Bear. He rolls Bear into the ring, and covers, but Bear is able to manage a shoulder at two. Courage gets an idea, and simply steps back onto the apron. He motions to Bear to come get him, and Bear seemingly obliges. Bear takes a swing, Courage ducks, and drops his throat across the ropes. Bear stumbles around blindly, and then walks right into a springboard dropkick by Courage. Courage jumps on top with a cover again, and once again, Bear kicks out! Courage lifting Bear, looking for a uranage. Bear with a few elbows to escape, and then he quickly drops Courage with a death valley driver. One, two, and three, and Bear takes the win into the second round of the next level tournament. As Bear celebrates inside of the ring, Courage grabs a chair from ringside, and nails Bear with it. Courage repeadately slams the chair onto Bear's fallen body, and it takes several referees to restrain Courage, who finally just grabbed the chair and slammed it to the mat. He then exited to a deafening ovation of boos as Courage headed backstage... Result: Bear by pinfall Shaking Up The Foundation Warfare followed Courage backstage to find Lance Star & Primetime awaiting his arrival. As he reahced backstage, Courage put his hands to his head, and looked at Star and Primetime before blurting out some obscentities, but not necessarily at them. Star & Primetime joined in the yelling. "Can you never win without cheating, Chris?" Primetime asked. He gave Primetime a cold stare, before Lance chimed in. "Yeah! Everytime you win, it's by cheating. How come?" Chris shook his head and looked at the floor as if he didn't want to hear it. Instead, he took their words and turned it against them. "Well, what about you two?" Lance looked at Courage suspiciously. "What about us, Chris?" Courage chuckled. "Well, you just lost to Machina, who hasn't won a legitimate match since... well, ever. And you lost to Wretch on Saturday. And then you, Brian. You... lose the Internet Title? To who? Mitch "I am as boring as a mime but not nearly as pretty" Wilson... and then you lose Sunday... to Wretch. You guys both lost to Wretch! How credible is that?" Lance brought up the fact that Courage lost to Wretch in April, but Courage shrugged it off. Primetime then looked at Lance. "Speaking of Wretch... will you be serious? The comedy act has grown old. We know you're a funny guy but come on... Saturday was ridiculous, Lance." Lance pondered things before coming up a solution. "Look... this isn't doing us any good to point out our shortcomings. Instead, let's take all these negatives and turn them into a positive. We are The Future Foundation, and afterall, we rock a helluva lot. So, let's re-establish ourselves. Brian, quit fooling around. You have more talent in your pinky than half the roster has in their entire bodies. Chris... look at the tag team division. It's weak. You and I get along so well we might as well form a full time team and take over the division. Who's going to stop us? The drunks? Time-boy and the manic depressive? The fat guy and the gay guy? Nobody could stop us, Chris. We'd own the divison. We'd be the kings of the division." Courage took what Lance said to heart, before shaking his head. "Yeah... and since we're both from California, we might as well be the California Kings..." Lance concurred. "That's a helluva idea, Chris. A hell of an idea." Primetime nodded. "You're right, Lance. We should stop the finger pointing at each other, and just take control of the damn place. Screw the tournament. We're already at our own level and nobody and I mean nobody is anywhere close to us." The three men nodded, before they put their fists together and as the veteran of the trio, he gave them some more encouragement. "Gentlemen... the future... is NOW." Chris and Lance agreed as Warfare went to it's final commercial break... The Boston Strangler & Scott Slugger vs. Wretch & Scot Hartt vs. Ric Chronos & Fallen vs. Bad Attitude (Ice & Mitch Wilson) Warfare heads to the ring for a big main event, a four-way tag team match. "Paint It Black" begins to play and the first participants in the match, Next Level Round Two qualifier Wretch and Scot Hartt, leave their seats at ringside to enter the ring. The loud reaction from the crowd is all it takes for anyone to acknowledge how popular they are. Wretch and Hartt enter the ring as "Christian Says" starts playing and the crowd begins cheering as Next Level Round Two qualifier Ric Chronos and Fallen enter, looking pumped up for this big match. They hit the ring and take an opposite corner from Wretch and Hartt. The cheering turns to boos as "Bad Attitude" hits and Ice and Next Level Round Two qualifier Mitch Wilson head to the ring, looking traditionally intense. They enter and exchange icy glares with the other participants in the match, before their attention is diverted to the entrance ramp as "Back In Black" begins and Scott Slugger appears to a big pop from the crowd. Slugger struts to the ring, before turning and gesturing to the back. With that, the lights turn black and "O Fortuna" hits to a gigantic pop from the assembled crowd. An engine revs, and the Boston Strangller appears, guiding his badass Harley down the entrance ramp as Scott Slugger charges into the ring and goes straight for Ice, who is more than happy to return the blows. The ref doesn't have time to explain that one man from each team will be in the ring at all times, but figures the hell with it, they'll figure it out. The bell rings and Scot Hartt and Ric Chronos pair off, Chronos hitting a series of big chops to the smaller Hartt. Chronos backs Hart into the corner then charges in with a clothesline, and Hartt stumbles out into a big DDT. Chronos covers, but Hartt kicks out at two. Chronos brings Hartt up and tags out to Fallen, who charges in at Hartt, who catches him off-guard with a surprise belly-to-belly. Hartt begins to go to work on Fallen as Ice lariats Scott Slugger over the top rope to the outside. Ice steps over the top rope and onto the apron, before leaping off and clobbering Slugger with a double axehandle. Ice brings Slugger up and whips him hard into the guardrail, where Slugger strikes with a crash and clatter of metal. Ice charges at Slugger, who back body drops him over the guardrail into the crowd, inadvertantly crushing a few fans. Slugger waits for Ice to get up, then leaps onto the guardrail and leaps off at Ice, using it as a springboard. Slugger nails Ice with a version of the Grand Slam, and basks in the fans' adulation as Ice recovers on the floor. Back in the ring, Scot Hartt still has control of Fallen, and whips him into the ropes. Hartt lowers his head, and Fallen responds by kicking him full-on on the jaw. Hartt jerks up, and Fallen kicks him hard in the gut to double him back over. Fallen double underhooks Hartt's arms and drives him into the mat with a ring-rocking tiger driver. Fallen covers Hartt and hooks a leg, but the pinfall is broken up by Wretch. Fallen brings Hartt up and whips him into the ropes, not noticing Hartt making a blind tag to the Boston Strangler, which is perfectly legal as the rules of the match state that anybody can tag in anybody else at any time. Fallen nails Hartt with a jumping lariat, and rolls through with the move. His momentum carries him to a standing position... right in the face of the Boston Strangler. Strangler hauls off and clocks Fallen, pulling back his ham-like right fist, winding up, and roundhousing the smaller man across the jaw. Strangler winds up and nails him again, knocking him into the ropes. Strangler whips Fallen across and catches him on the rebound with a big boot that nearly decapitates the former tag team champion. Strangler covers Fallen, but Wretch makes the save at two, using the logic that the match is being contested under first pinfall wins. Strangler brings Fallen up to his feet and throws him into the corner of Wretch, telling Wretch that if he wants some, come get some. Wretch shrugs and tags in. He charges at Strangler, but Strangler ducks a clothesline, and knocks him into the Bad Attitude corner with a big boot. Strangler begins unloading with punches and chops as Scott Slugger rolls Ice into the ring from the outside. Slugger heads up to the top rope to go for the Grand Slam, but Ice catches him in mid-move and turns it into a huge powerslam. Ice covers, but Slugger kicks out at two. Ice brings Slugger up, and doesn't see Slugger tag out to Scot Hartt. Ice whips Slugger into the ropes, but Slugger leapfrogs over Ice, who looks up to see Hartt flying at him with a big flying shoulder. Hartt goes behind Ice and somehow lifts him up and plants him into the mat with a back suplex. Having taken a beating all match, Ice musters up the strength to tag out to Ric Chronos after kicking out at two. Chronos hits the ring and charges at Hartt, who ducks a clothesline and back body drops Chronos to the apron. Chronos hooks his arm to hiptoss him to the outside, but Hartt latches onto Chronos mid-move and only Chronos tumbles to the ground as Hartt grabs the ropes and skins the cat back into the ring. Acorss the ring, Mitch Wilson slaps the Boston Strangler on the back and climbs in, looking to soften up his Next Level foe Wretch. The fresh Wilson hooks Wretch up, and plants him with the Superstar Suplex. Seeing his partner getting pounded, Hartt charges at Wilson, who sees him coming, ducks his lariat, and clamps on the Headliner. Wilson floats over into a cover automatically, and before anybody else in the match can react, Wilson has won the match for Bad Attitude. As soon as the bell rings, Eron charges down to the ring, weilding a chair. He clocks the unsuspecting Slugger on the back with from behind, and Slugger tumbles off the apron to the floor. The Strangler spots this and leaps down, only to eat a chairshot from Eron. Seeing this, Ric Chronos and Fallen quickly come to the aid of the groggy duo of fWo World Champion and top contender. Chronos ducks a chairshot from Eron, and Eron never sees Fallen leap up and dropkick the chair into his face, knocking him over the guardrail into the crowd. Fallen and Chronos help Slugger and Strangler back up, and the four eye the retreating Eron angrily. Suddenly, thunder rolls and the lights cut out, only to turn back on a second later. When they do, Rain is standing face-to-face by the ring with Ric Chronos. He snarls at Chronos that his date with the Storm is near. Chronos takes a swing, but the thunder/darkness combination repeats itself and when the lights reappear, Rain is nowhere to be found. High above the ring, the Black Panther looks down on the chaotic events with a high level of interest. Panther continues to stare down at the ring as Warfare goes off the air... Result: Bad Attitude by pinfall
