Sunday Slaughter, 15 October 2000

15 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Tonight, it began. The 2000 Wrestler of the Year tournament. In general, the second such tournament the fWo has held. Last year was the first, and featured at least one very notable first round upset: At the time, Scott Slugger was in the midst of his record setting eighty-nine day reign as fWo World Champion, and "The Future" Lance Star was essentially an unproven rookie and was riding the coattails of Chris Universal as part of a tag team. Slugger was more than confident, perhaps too confident, and it cost him. He lost. Star did the unthinkable, and pulled out the biggest win of his career. Although Star lost in the next round to the eventual winner of the tournament (The Black Panther), that win alone put him in the same category as Slugger and others as far as wrestling ability went. No matter what, however, it was an upset. A big upset. Tonight, the second annual Wrestler of the Year tournament would begin. Certainly, there would be some first round upsets... without question. Thirty two names were entered in the tournament itself, but already, at least three found themselves out of action. Newcomer Erik Rave, who earned a spot on the roster by faring well back in August at the Ryan Moore Memorial Show, was handed his walking papers a week ago due to lack of committment to the organization. The door was left open for a return, but as it were, his first round opponent would receive a bye to the second round. His first round opponent happened to be Wretch. Wolf Fang was also handed his walking papers a week or so ago, and tonight, Jackhammer found out that he would not face his former partner in "Ground Zero", instead, he also would receive a bye to the second round. And a favorite in the tournament, Ice, would also not be competing due to an injury suffered in a match against The Flying Frenchie last week on Slaughter. It was a re-aggrivation of the same injury suffered earlier this year, and it looked like Ice would be on the shelf for quite awhile. Thus, his opponent Carnage, would also advanced to the second round. Acting fWo owner James Armstrong finally returned to television after a one week absence, and informed everyone of the changes in the Wrestler of the Year tournament, before heading back to his office. He intended to settle there the rest of the night, and hoped not to be disturbed - the meetings he was obligated to attend that ended this past Wednesday burned him out considerably. He didn't look well, and hadn't slept in several days. He also did not have any of the stuff he absolutely craved during the past two weeks. Bun cake. Armstrong sat down behind his chair, and prepared to dig into a very big piece of his favorite food... when he paused, and realized, something was wrong. Before the cameras could capture what wasn't right, Slaughter headed to the ring for it's first match from the left portion of the Wrestler of the Year tournament brackets, which flashed onto the screen. Primetime vs. Plague Slaughter exploded inside the arena, as the fans prepared themselves for the Wrestler of the Year tournament. The fans focused on the ring before they began to boo as "Through The Never" started up. Plague begins to make his way to the ring, accompanied by Stone and a wary-looking War. Plague rolls into the ring, and his partners take their positions at ringside. The boos then intensify as "Snap" begins playing and Primetime makes his way to the ring, looking quite confident as usual. He quickly climbs the steps and steps into the ring, and the ref signals for the bell. They lock up, and Primetime gains the advantage with a knee to the gut. He quickly hooks Plague and takes him over with a snap suplex. Primetime drops a hard elbow, before bringing Plague back up. He whips him hard into a corner, before charging in with a lariat that nails Plague squarely. Plague stumbles out, and Primetime grabs him and gorilla presses him hard down to the mat. Plague quickly rolls out of the ring and begins talking to Stone, obviously looking for a counterstrategy. Primetime quickly grows tired of this and reaches out, grabbing Plague by the hair. He pulls him up onto the ring apron and then over the top into the ring, where he lands with a thud. Primetime quickly brings him back up, but Plague catches him off-guard with a low blow. Plague follows it up with a dropkick, knocking Primetime down. He then mounts the second turnbuckle, and flies off with a variation of a frog splash. Plague reaches forward and hooks a leg, but Primetime kicks out at two. Plague brings him back up, and takes him down with a hard reverse neckbreaker. He follows it up with a legdrop and another cover, garnering another two count. Plague brings Primetime back up once more and whips him hard into the corner. Primetime stumbles out, and Plague runs into the ropes and catches him, bulldogging him into the mat. Plague then slashes a thumb across his throat and heads to the outside, before beginning to climb up to the top rope. He reaches the top and measures Primetime. He then leaps. Primetime, at the last moment, raises his knees, and Plague slams into them, driving all the air out of him. Plague flops off of Primetime's knees and to the mat, and Primetime takes a moment to gather himself before shaking it off and bringing Plague back up. He sets him up in the middle of the ring and measures him, before running off the ropes. As he does so, Stone reaches out and grabs his foot, stopping him. He turns around and begins to jaw with her, and Plague has time to recover. He stalks up behind Primetime, before hooking him from behind for the Dark Driver. Primetime spins out of it and catches Plague with a shot to the kidneys, causing him to release him. Primetime then hooks Plague by the head and lifts him up. He then drops him across the top rope, before driving him into the mat with the Snapshot. Primetime reaches forward and hooks the leg, and three seconds later, advances in the Wrestler Of The Year tournament. Primetime raises his arms high in the air to taunt the crowd, before leaving the ring and leaving War and Stone to tend to their fallen comrade. Slaughter, meanwhile, rolls on... Result: Primetime by pinfall "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson vs. The Abusah Slaughter returned to the ring as "Connection" by Elastica rendered in a roar of cheers from the crowd, and their favorite incoherent lackey, Tha Mutha Fuckin Abusah of Yo Momma, excitedly walked to the ring. Abusah rolled into the ring and posed for the crowd, just when "Last Cup of Sorrow" by Faith No More took to the speakers, bringing out the fWo Heavyweight Champion, Mitch Wilson. The fans immediately booed the wiley ring technician, as Mitch put an evil grin on his face and entered the ring, handing his belt off to a ringside attendant. Mitch and Abusah met at center-ring, as the bell rang. Abusah went to lock up with Mitch, but Wilson had other intentions in mind, as he hit a stiff kick to the mid-section of Abusah, followed by a hard toss into the turnbuckles. Mitch followed in with a hard Lariot, and went to work on Abusah with resounding chops. Abusah chopped back at Wilson, but the fWo Heavyweight Champion answered with a kick to the groin, followed by a whip into the opposing corner. The velocity of the whip caused Abusah to stagger forward, in prime position for a Northern Lights suplex by "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson. Mitch held on for the cover, but Abusah managed to kick out at two. Wilson immediately followed up with his deadly Fujiwara armbar, showing great intensity on his face. Abusah roared in pain, but managed to reach out with his feet and get a hold of the bottom rope, causing Mitch to release his grasp. Mitch continued working the left arm of Abusah with various leg and knee drops. After softening him up, Mitch shot Abusah off to the ropes, and took him down with a leaping leg Lariot. Abusah went down, but managed to get back up, to see Mitch charging at him. Abusah took him over with a deep armdrag, and followed up with another one as Wilson got up. Mitch angrily charged Abusah once more, but was picked up and dropped to the mat with a back-crunching spinebuster by Abusah. Abusah roared out "I M WYNENG!" before covering the prone Mitch Wilson to render a two count. Abusah stomped away at Mitch, before lifting him up and slamming him right to the mat. Abusah made his way to the first rope, and flew off with an elbow drop that connected with Wilson. Abusah tried for another cover, but Wilson wouldn't be insulted, and kicked out at one. Abusah got Mitch back to his feet, and shot him off to the ropes. Abusah made the terrible mistake of putting his head down too early, and Mitch capitalized with a swinging neckbreaker, followed by an elbow drop from the middle rope. Wilson hooked the leg and made an authoritative cover, but Abusah kicked out at two and a half. As Abusah tried making his way to his feet, Mitch Wilson heartlessly kicked him in the ribs, and then in the head, before lifting him back up and hooking him up for the Superstar Suplex. Mitch hit it and held on for the cover, but was disappointed with a kickout by Abusah and two and three quarters. Mitch took Abusah up and set him on the top rope, following up as well. Looking to put the match away with his all-new, devastating finisher, Mitch tried hooking Abusah up in a brainbuster, but Abusah managed to push the champion off of him and down to the mat. Abusah yelled out "SOOPERFLIE LEEP!" and leapt off of the top rope with a splash, but fell short of his target. Mitch immediately took advantage with a kick to the gut, followed by a hard DDT to the mat. Mitch covered, but Abusah managed to kick out once more. TMFAOYM made his way up to his feet by the ropes, and Mitch charged, only to be backflipped to the outside. Abusah followed, and in a cheap imitation of Steve Blackman, yelled out, "O ITZ PAHRTEE TYME!" Abusah took the fight right to Mitch Wilson by slamming his face into the steel steps, followed up by lifting him up and ramming him spine-first into the steel ring post. Mitch held his back in pain, as Abusah rolled himself into the ring. Mitch hopped onto the apron, and shoulder-blocked Abusah through the ropes, and jumped over for a sunset flip. Abusah kicked out at two and three quarters, but Mitch followed straight up by hooking Abusah for the Headliner. Abusah elbowed his way out of it, and took Mitch over with a side Russian legsweep, floating over for the cover. Abusah fell short a split second of advancing in the Wrestler of the Year tournament, but continued his offense anyway by applying a full Nelson. Before Abusah could do any damage out of that move, Mitch hit a mule kick south of Abusah's border, and hit the Headliner out of nowhere to make the cover, and take the three-count over Abusah. "Last Cup of Sorrow" resurfaced as Mitch exited the ring with his title, but the action wasn't over, as Carnage entered the ring and began relentlessly attacking Abusah. Luckily, Wretch and Hartt came down to make the save on their teammate, as Carnage retreated back into the crowd. In one fluid motion, Wretch hooks Bear's head on the way down and drives him into the mat with the Depression. Wretch then rolls over onto Bear, and the referee makes the count. Three seconds later, Wretch has won the match. The referee raises his hand, and Bear pulls himself slowly to his feet before engaging in a staredown with Wretch. On the outside, Kelly and the Strangler begin to brawl into the crowd as Slaughter goes off the air... Result: Wretch by pinfall
