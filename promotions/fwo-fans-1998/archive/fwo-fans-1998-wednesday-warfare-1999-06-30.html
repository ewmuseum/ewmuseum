fWo — Card

Wednesday Warfare, 30 June 1999

30 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The cameras open on a small office, where Chris Finn and James Armstrong sat with the commissioner of the fWo. Travis sat hunched over the written card, rubbing his forehead and considering. Looking up, Travis nodded slowly and said, "We've got a good card here tonight... the Charleston crowd is going to really enjoy themselves. How are Crucifix and Rain?" Chris Finn shuffled some papers nervously, clearing his throat. "Well Mr. Beaven, Rain hasn't shown up to the arena yet, and Crucifix has been restrained to his locker room by security. He's not happy about the situation... this match tonight is liable to get violent..." Travis nodded, and picked up the telephone, ordering additional security around ringside. Finn continued, "Listen Travis... you're not going to want to hear this... but the fWo has some real problems right now. A couple of agitators have stirred things up and everyone is on edge. We both... James and I... warned you about Silver Fox... what he has about and why hiring him was a mistake. He's got the Frenchie and Cool... I mean Death... all riled up, and you know those two are some of your bigger draws. If they get angry and bolt... you stand to lose a lot of money. And there is also this Hardcore title. It's an insurance nightmare... you've got wrestlers getting injured and blaming the promotion... Sid Snow is pondering a lawsuit over what Slugger did to him in that damn department store. And then there is Heftel. His new angle... well, it's not good." Finn finished, and James Armstrong cut in. "Yeah, and what about Scott Slugger Bev? When does he get a title shot?" Travis looked up at this, and shook his head. "Yes, I know that Finn... and I'm sure Slugger will get a title shot very soon. I know all of it, but we got ourselves in this position and there is no sense complaining about it now. Now... is Austin Davis here yet?" Finn looked confused by this turn in the questioning, and looked over at Armstrong. "Yes, Mr. Beaven... he arrived a few minutes ago... but why is he even here? He wasn't scheduled for a match tonight, and in my opinion he should be suspended and his contract be looked at. I mean... he's a name, but he's trouble. Send him up North... or to Atlanta." Travis shook his head, "No... no. Austin Davis has some problems, but the fans like him, and you're right... he's a big name. Which is why I want him wrestling tonight. But later tonight he's going to have to answer to a few things... you can be damn sure of that." Finn looked startled and gasped, "Tonight? But against who?" Travis was about to answer, and suddenly the phone rang. Travis picked it up, and looked suddenly annoyed. Motioning to the door, Travis had Finn and Armstrong leave the office as he talked to the person on the other end of the line, telling whoever it was that "Reinstatement was unlikely... no matter how many titles he held..." Finn and Armstrong walked to the ring, uneasy about what was to come... Silver Arrival As the loud explosions of Warfare sounded and the opening theme played, the limo of Silver Fox and his compadres pulled up outside of the arena. Silver Fox stepped out, followed by the Chameleon, Total Eclipse, Black Quicksilver, Fyre, and a large masked man. Silver Fox smiled as he neared the backstage doors, and was stopped by Dick Venom. Dick Venom asked Silver Fox if based on the results of the three way match at Numbered Days the identity of the masked man would be revealed. Silver Fox only laughed, and pushed Dick Venom aside, addressing the camera directly. Silver Fox laughed, and asked if the Flying Frenchie enjoyed being made the fool... and if his little friend Death enjoyed submitting to his will. Silver Fox then told the viewers that in four days, while the rest of the country was celebrating their independence he would free Death from his burden of knowing the identity of the masked man... and show the world what it meant to be ruled mind... body... and soul... by silver. Silver Fox then told the others that he was going to the ring alone, for dealing with the so-called "real wrestler" lackey of the Frenchie was certainly no challenge. Silver Fox laughed, and walked forward... not seeing what was happening behind him. A large net fell on the group, and as the Silver Fox laughed and made his way to the ring sounds of a struggle was heard... Mitch Wilson vs. Silver Fox Warfare returns from the commercial timeout to find Matt Loco standing outside commissioner Travis Beaven' locker room attempting to get a word from the commissioner. Loco finally reaches Beaven who says; "I can promise you ... no, I can guarantee you that this will be a night you never forget. Austin Davis will get his tonight, I assure you of that..." With that, cameras return to live action and Silver Fox begins to make his way down to the ring. A silver spotlight shines on Silver Fox, as he walks down the ramp getting the red-carpet treatment once again. He slides underneath the bottom rope and into the ring, and the crowd lets him know how they feel about him by shouting various profanities at him. Silver Fox simply shrugs them off, as Mitch Wilson makes his way down to ringside. Wilson has made it known recently that he's going to teach that pompous ass Silver Fox how to wrestle. Wilson and Silver Fox circle each other in the ring, before locking up. Wilson quickly applies a headlock, but Silver Fox uses his momentum to whip him into the ropes. Wilson returns and Silver Fox takes him down with a drop toe hold... before following it up with a running knee drop after bouncing into the ropes. The crowd responds to Silver Fox bowing; proud of what he just accomplished. With Silver Fox bent over, Wilson comes back to his feet, and takes Silver Fox out with a version of the "rocker dropper". Wilson then slaps on a reverse chinlock and begins to systematically work over SF. Silver Fox tries to tell the ref that it's an illegal chokehold, but the referee isn't buying it. With that plan backfired, Silver Fox attempts to slide very slowly towards the ropes. Using every ounce of energy in his body, SF begins to slide more and more over towards the ropes... with a little help from Wilson, who appears to have a plan. As SF is about to touch the rope which would force Wilson to release the hold, Wilson smirks and releases the hold... making Silver Fox seem like a dumbass for wasting all that energy trying to reach the ropes. Silver Fox seems appalled at Wilson while laying on the ground, and slides outside the ring. He looks up at the ramp and begins motioning for help, but as he turns around he's met with a suicide plancha from Wilson... who flies over the top rope with amazing agility. Wilson now on the attack, vertically suplexes Silver Fox on the ramp. He then follows it up by throwing Silver Fox headfirst into the ring steps... Wilson continues, but notices The Chameleon and Black Quicksilver coming down to the ring. Wilson slides into the ring as BQ & Chameleon reach the ringside area, and begin helping up Silver Fox. Wilson "winks" at Chameleon, and then begins distracting the referee. Suddenly, BQ & Chameleon begin attacking Silver Fox... laying him out with a vicious piledriver on the ringsteps. They then roll him into the ring and Wilson covers Silver Fox, getting the easy pin. The referee raises Wilson' hand and the crowd is befuddled as to why Chameleon and Quicksilver attacked their new friend. They join Wilson in the ring and celebrate, before removing their masks revealing Frenchie & Primetime. The crowd explodes in an ovation, applauding the tomfoolery executed flawlessly by the French Foundation. As Wilson and his cohorts celebrate, down come the actual Chameleon & Black Quicksilver, and a pier 6 brawl erupts between the two groups... with Silver Fox still smarting from the piledriver on the outside. Cameras break away from the action to find Scott Slugger & Ice, walking down a hallway, plotting for Slugger' match with Waru... which is coming up next. Result: Mitch Wilson by Pinfall Davis has a match... The cameras found Austin Davis pacing outside his locker room, and he looked up sharply as officials approached. "What in the hell do you bastards want?" Looking nervous, one of the officials stuttered an answer, "W-w-well... Davis... you're booked in a match today..." Davis smirked and grabbed the official by the neck. "I don't think so jackass... I've looked at the damn card, and I'm not on it." The official handed Davis a clipboard, and he looked at it sharply. "What? What in the hell is this? Second match? Who in the hell is 'The Bludgeoner'"? The officials walked backward slowly, shrugging and gesturing toward the ring entrance before quickly running away. Davis tossed the clipboard against the wall, creating a hole in the plaster and walked toward the ring entrance cursing. Over the arena PA speakers, the sounds of a Viking call were heard... Mike Heftel, The Bludgeoner vs. Austin Davis As the lights dimmed, sounds of a Viking horn were heard. As the crowd looked around confused, men dressed in Viking costumes with great shields and horned helmets lined the entry ramp with torches. As the horn sounded again, a man in great Viking garb with a sword, helmet, and furs marched out to the ring. Inside, he raised his sword to the crowd and let out a mighty bellow. Fans looking closely noticed that this man, introduced as "The Bludgeoner" was once Shane Austin, or as he was even more commonly known, crazy Mike Heftel. Austin Davis' music began to play and the wrestler walked out on the entrance ramp looking angry and annoyed. He walked up to some of the "Vikings" on the ramp, and shoved them into the crowd before running to the ring. Once there he stood and stared at "The Bludgeoner", who raised his sword in a salute and gave a mighty Viking bellow. Davis snatched the sword away from him, and broke the flimsy metal over his knee. He then grabbed Heftel by the neck, and delivered a wicked DDT, which laid the man out. Davis tossed his shield on him, and went for a cover. Because of the heavy fur and shield, "The Bludgeoner" was unable to budge and was easily pinned. Austin spit on the wrestler and began to exit the ring, the commissioner suddenly appeared on the Warfare Screen. Travis looked angry, and told Davis and the crowd that he had made a fool out of him, the fWo, and a superstar just trying to get over a gimmick. Davis looked back at Heftel, who was still trying to budge under the weight, and laughed. Travis only frowned further, and pointed at Davis. "You've done it now Davis... before the cameras go off the air you are going to get yours..." Austin Davis only laughed again, and walked back up the entrance ramp not looking very frightened... Result: Austin Davis by Pinfall Scott Slugger (c) vs. Waru After weeks and weeks of Waru tormenting and playing mind games with Scott Slugger' mind, the time has finally come for the match to actually happen on Warfare. With Waru already in the ring, "Back in Black" plays and out comes the fWo Hardcore Champion Scott Slugger... accompanied by Ice. Slugger makes a grand entrance, carrying with him the baseball bat... and Ice is bringing down a shopping cart full of weapons, which includes a Nintendo 64, a Richard Simmons workout video, a empty bottle of "Sobe", a 12-inch tv, a cd tower, a mop, a broom... and a garbage can. Ice guards the shopping cart full of weapons on the outside, and Slugger makes his entrance in the ring. As Slugger poses, Waru charges at him... and Slugger goes to take his head off with a swing of the baseball bat. Waru ducks, and Slugger turns around to meet a spinning wheel kick from Waru which makes the bat go face-first into Slugger. Waru, attempting to finish it off quickly, goes for a cover. A kickout by the resilliant Slugger. Waru brings Slugger to his feet and chops him repeatedly... before whipping him into the turnbuckle. Waru gets a running start and does a handspring elbow into the chest of Slugger, and Slugger staggers out of the corner... Waru chopblocks him and begins working on the leg. Waru drops repeated elbows into the leg of Slugger. Another cover, and another kickout by Slugger. Waru hits a flying fistdrop. Another kickout by Slugger. Waru begins choking Slugger relentlessly, until Ice slides a chair into the corner... in full view of Waru. Waru begins walking over to grab it, and Ice hurry and uses his strength to pull as Waru had a good grip on the chair. Waru goes flying through the middle & bottom rope onto the floor, and it looks as if Ice has bought Slugger some time. Slugger gets to his feet, and shakes off the damage done to his leg by Waru. Slugger goes outside, and grabs piece of the guardrail. Waru gets to his feet, finally, and turns around and is introduced to the guard rail by Slugger... Waru is busted open already, and now Slugger is on the attack. Slugger throws Waru back into the ring. He hits a quick snap suplex, before telling Ice to toss him one of the weapons. Ice tosses him the Richard Simmons workout tape, and Slugger waits for Waru to get up, before slamming it into the forehead of him repeatedly. Slugger asks for another weapon... Ice tosses him the cd tower. Slugger repeats step one, slamming it into the forehead of Waru repeatedly. Slugger asks for another weapon. Ice tosses him a mop... and Slugger tries to forcefeed the end of the mop into the proverbial ass of Waru. The referee warns Slugger that while this may be a hardcore match, it's not *that* kind of "hardcore" match. Slugger agrees, and smacks the chest of Waru with the metal part of the mop. Slugger is then tossed the broom, and Slugger takes a batting stance... Waru slowly but surely climbs to his feet, staggers briefly, before turning around and ducking the swing of the broom. He lays Slugger out with a lowblow, before telling Ice to toss him the Nintendo 64. Ice, unthinkably tosses him the 64, before realizing what he just did. Waru nails Slugger with the Nintendo 64, and then goes for a pin... the referee counts one, no kickout by Slugger. The referee counts two, no kickout by Slugger. As the referee is about to count three, Ice pulls him leg first out of the ring and the two begin arguing. As the two argues, Waru climbs outside and lifts the shopping cart up into the ring. He then begins to make a slow climb up to the top rope... but first jabbers with some fans at ringside. This allows Slugger some time to act seriously hurt, while reaching for the shopping cart at the same time. As Slugger climbs up to his knees, Waru begins to fly off... but Slugger pulls the shopping cart into the way, and Waru smacks HARD into the now crumpled cart. Slugger then grabs Waru, and hits the "grand slam" on the shopping cart... further damaging it. Ice tells the referee that there is a cover going on in the ring, and the referee climbs back in, and counts the pinfall. Slugger gets the victory, but is obviously worn out from the match. Suddenly, Aimee & Hiroshima run down to ringside and begin attacking Ice, who fends them off easily... we go backstage where an arguement among Doomsday is taking place... as VL prepares for her match with Judge Steel later in the evening. Judge Steel walks into the picture, and begins yelling at VL... but Stevie Roberts steps in the way, separating any possible physical altercations. Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall Crucifix vs. Rain ... we resume arena action as the cage begins lowering for our much anticipated cage match. The arena darkened for the entrance of Crucifix, who came down to the ring looking very ominous. Crucifix grabbed a microphone at ringside, and shouted, "Scott Slugger...watch and learn...quote the Crucifix, forevermore!" Crucifix, who held a trash bin full of weapons, looked up at the stage and waited for Rain to come out, but Rain doesn't appear. His music isn't playing either, which leaves the massive Crucifix befuddled. Suddenly, the lights come back on and Rain comes flying off the top rope and clothesline Cruficix into the mat. Rain continues, first throwing Crucifix head first into the steel mesh, and then landing a vicious piledriver. He then starts to walk out of the cage, but Crucifix gets up in time and keeps him from getting out. Reaching into his trash bin, Crucifix removed a branding iron and jabbed Rain in the back, causing Rain to scream in pain. Crucifix then grabed him, turns him around, and grabed him by the throat... he goes for a chokeslam, but Rain negates it with a punch to the side of the head, and then a kick in the midsection area. With Crucifix bent over, Rain goes for a powerbomb, but Crucifix escapes by using a backdrop. Rain holds on, and it looks like a sunset flip pinfall attempt, but Crucifix falls onto Rain, knee first... pinning his shoulders with his knees. He then begins choking Rain out. Second by second passes with Rain gasping for air. Crucifix begins laughing maniacally, and he thinks he has the match won. He is about to walk through the cage door, but Rain gets up in time to stop him. He grabs Crucifix and turns him around, before whipping him into the turnbuckles. Crucifix reverses it, and Rain goes sternum first into the cage. As Rain staggers out, Crucifix grabs him and takes him off his feet with a sidewalk slam. Crucifix continues the onslaught, dropping knee drop after knee drop on Rain. He then lifts up the massive size of Rain, and bodyslams him into the canvas. Crucifix then steps onto the bottom rope, and then the middle rope, and then the top rope, before turning back around to face the ring... but Rain is back up to his feet because Crucifix took too much time climbing up to the ropes. Rain climbs up onto the middle ropes, rakes Crucifix in the eyes, and then superplexes him off the ropes and back into the ring. Both men, dazed & exhausted, are out cold in the middle of the ring. Rain reaches his feet first however, as Crucifix took the blunt of the blow. Rain quickly (by his standards) climbs up to the top rope and flies off with an elbow drop, but Crucifix moves out of the way. Rain is rolling around on the mat, after having injured his right arm. Crucifix, seizes the moment, and brings Rain to his feet. He goes for a powerbomb, and this time, he succeeds. He goes for another powerbomb, and succeeds again. He goes for a third one, and nails a third one. Finally, with the crowd showing their approval for the punishment dished out, he brings Rain to his feet... and chokeslams him into the mat. Rain is out, and Crucifix methodically walks over to the door... it appears as if he's going to make his exit through the cage and pick up the win, but suddenly, he decides to *climb* out of the cage on the opposite side. As he makes the slow climb up the ropes... and up the links of the cage, he gets on the outer side. He's about to climb down, when Rain makes his recovery, and sees Crucifix climbing down the cage. Rain slowly, but surely, walks over to the cage and begins to walk out of the cage through the doors... Rain makes it all the way out before Crucifix climbs down, and thus Rain is the winner. Crucifix, in shock, laughs things off as a bolt of "lightning" zaps the top of the cage and fireworks spray into the crowd. Rain, looks disturbed by Crucifix' actions, but pleased that he got the win. Gesturing up, Rain screamed into the air, and lightening struck down onto the cage, lighting the sides ablaze with fire. Crucifix quickly fell backward, as Rain laughed and gestured to the rafters once again, as great sheets of water fell, dampening the blaze. Both men looked at each other ominously, but Rain would go on to face Scott Slugger for the title at Fight the Future. Nevertheless, we go backstage where VL, Stevie & Judge Steel continue to argue about the upcoming match. Result: Rain VL vs. Judge Steel fWo cameras spot The Surgeon in the crowd measuring skulls and mumbling about brain transference, as Judge Steel makes his way out of the lockerroom for his important match with VL with the winner becoming a member of Doomsday. Samuel Smooth accompanies Judge Steel, while out comes VL, accompanied by the leader of Doomsday, Stevie Roberts. VL wastes no time in kicking things off, absolutely brutalizing Judge Steel from start to finish. However, Judge Steel gets the upper hand when VL misses a moonsault attempt. Judge Steel controls the match for several minutes before going for a pinfall. At the count of two, Stevie Roberts pulls the referee out of the ring and tells him "are you going to stand for this? The Surgeon is measuring skulls at ringside, baby, yeah!" The referee looks at Roberts as if Roberts has a glass-forehead and the referee can see right through him. Samuel Smooth comes over and begins arguing with Stevie, who tells him to go get Surgeon so they can have a group discussion. Smooth does what he is told, and it appears as if Surgeon is too pre-occupied to even hear a word Smooth is saying. The cameras come back to the ring where Roberts has laid out Judge Steel while VL distracted the referee. Roberts gets out of the ring just in time as VL comes back over and covers Steel, getting the victory and thus becoming a member of Doomsday. Smooth comes back to the ring and argues with Roberts and VL again, until Roberts & VL decide they've had enough and kick his ass. Smooth is an absolute mess in the ring, and if that isn't enough problems for him, Micky Maliboo comes out and reminds him that he still has to face him on Slaughter, with Prophet as the special guest referee. Result: VL by Pinfall Cactus Jon wants a match... Cactus Jon was seen backstage pacing outside the commissioner's office, angry and trying to get a match signed between himself and Ultra Violet for Fight the Future. Cursing, Cactus Jon finally kicked open the door and barged inside, slamming his hands down on the commissioner's desk. Cactus Jon began yelling, and it was some time before he was calmed down enough to talk coherently. Sighing, Travis told Jon that he would get his match signed on Sunday Slaughter... between himself and Ultra Violet. When Jon yelled that nothing would prevent Ultra Violet from simply not showing up, Travis told Jon that he would find a special guest referee for the match... and would send the referee with Ultra Violet to make sure he showed up at the arena. Nodding with approval, Cactus Jon left the room, bumping into Eron the Relentless as he went. Jon excused himself, but as he walked away Eron uncharistically gave him the finger, and grumbled something to himself as he walked away... Ground Zero (c) vs. Wrestling Conglomerate The music of the Wrestling Conglomerate played as Melissa Smalls and Apocolyptica walked out onto the entrance ramp for their tag title shot. Smalls held up several small posters of herself in various states of undress (all completely appropriate for the viewing audience) and tossed them into the crowd reminding them that Bikini Breakout officially kicked off on the Forth of July. Apocolyptica wore high leather boots, and looked grim in a dark outfit with a leather and silver choker. Inside the ring they waited for the champions, Ground Zero. Jackhammer walked down the entrance ramp as Wolf Fang strode quietly out of the crowd. Wolf Fang slid into the ring and attacked Apocolyptica at once, starting the match off with a bang. Wolf Fang tossed her into the ropes, and attempted a lariat, but Apocolyptica slid under his legs and brought her head up as she went, delivering a hard head butt to the groin. As Wolf Fang bent in pain, Apocolyptica grabbed him from behind and dropped him to the mat with a DDT. The cover only got a two count, and Wolf Fang managed to punch Apocolyptica in the face and tag in Jackhammer. Jackhammer entered and tossed Apocolyptica into her corner. She tagged in Smalls, and both women grabbed Jackhammer and delivered a double suplex. Still unable to get the two count, Smalls methodically attacked with kicks to the head of the big man, until he grabbed her leg and flipped her to the mat. Jackhammer delivered a leg drop, and punched the woman repeatedly in the head, before signaling for the powerbomb. He delivered it, and Apocolyptica entered the ring a moment too late as he got the three count. Wolf Fang and Jackhammer left the area as Eron strode to the ring, and told the two women to "hit the bricks", as he reached for a microphone... Result: Ground Zero by Pinfall Eron speaks his mind. Eron paced the ring, and then grabbed a microphone and shouted, "Where is my god damn music?" F**k Off by Kid Rock began to play over the arena PA system. Eron sat on the turnbuckle and addressed the crowd. "6 years ago, I was walking down the streets of Chatham Ontario Canada, my abrubt hometown, as I walked wondering what to do with myself. I walked down on Queen St. past the Library as I wondered to myself if I should end my life on the road and settle down. I told myself to keep my life on the road. So I went to Toronto to Sullys Gym. There, I was put up against Adam Copeland to graduate and I lost. The crowd there, for the match, cheered both of us. The crowd cheered me. I failed. And you people still cheered me when I left the Gym. I went to Smoky Mountain Wrestling where my name was Mr.Self Destruct, some nut and I teamed with Bob the Killer Clown, don't ask. We had a Loser Leaves town match against the Thrill Seakers Lance Storm and Chris Jerico, and we lost as I got pinned by Lance. Bob got booed, Thrillseakers were booed, and I was cheered when I slammed my finisher the Becomming, a Crucifix Powerbomb on jerico. The fans cheered, and I was kicked out of the Smoky mountain Wrestling. I then went to Japan where I had a feud with Black Pegasus, most know him now as Chris Benoit, and I lost almost each match with him. I beat him twice, and the fans in Japan liked Ara Milenko. Me. You fans cheer for any reason. When I was in the RWA as Ruiner you cheered when I was the bad guy, as Eron the Relentless in the FFW you still cheer for the once member of a false KKK group, in the TCW you cheered for me even though I held the World Title for a sweet week. And now, I'm in the fans Wrestling organization and still Eron the Relentless, and you cheer. I have finally earned my respect and you cheer. Why? No reason. Cause you come here and cheer for the guy who either gets beat up or has an attitude. Well, I dont really give a shit about the cheers, the fans, the merchandise, NOTHING! Its N-I-N assholes now, Now I'm Nothing! All I hear is 20,000 isconsiderate robots being sold out watching muscular men slamming each other. Nothing more. NOTHING is the keyword here. NOTHING ELSE MATTERS!" The crowd began to boo, as Eron smirked at them, and continued. "A certain Poem gets it almost right, with a little corrections. Here it is: We all need... a haven. A sanctuary. A place to hide. A place to escape. A place to dream and hope. A place... where the darkness won't be so frightening. So it is written. So it shall come to pass. But for some, the darkness is a relentless enemy. It cannot be eluded. And once the darkness senses your fear; once it senses your pain, it is never far behind. No-one can empathize with these people. No-one can understand their futility. No-one can forgive their hopelessness. No-one feel their pain. Quote King Nothing, nevermore." this was a poem from Scott Levy, I changed it for the better. I am that Darkness. I am the relentless enemy. And the people, are all you. You cheered for me cause you needed a warrior, someone to protect you, from your warrior. You thought, that if you cheered me, I would like you too, but it dont work that way. Na no, you can't hide true fear. true power. I am not a greater power, I am the greater powers controller. I am not the Reaper of Wayward souls, I am the rebelling Wayward soul. I am not the underdog, but the one looked upon to make the underdog look good, but I do not do my job." The crowd booed louder now, and began to toss things into the arena as Eron continued, "At Numbered Days, I fought a nemisis of mine, made by you people. The Oracle. A failure. Someone who could never make something of himself. I injured him by bodypressing him through a Table. It was for the better of him. It'll give him time to put his life together and see that the only reason he was the heel, was because he was the failure. His leg broke because of himself. His heart told him he was gonna lead himself to trouble. I heard what he was thinking. He was thinking that he shouldn't have put himself through this. As I watched, I thought to myself "What have I done? Why didn't I hammer his other leg during that fall?" As I watch you crowds of 20,000 people hip and hopping on the floor, and all that, you people have some effect on wrestlers. Your energy turns our adreneline into power. But, now to me, it turns into pain and twists me. Twists me like a Thorn around a twig, ripping pieces of flesh off, like my dreams. My nightmares, dont even think of going there. The places my nightmares could make a Bishop fall in fear. They make every hell spawn quiver in fear. And, as I still go on the road, theres only one thing I can still say:When will the fans learn? I've had 3 days to think about me showing my true thoughts, and I knew something had to be said. I had to be one, and not one with the fans. You fans have ruined my career, and I can't take it anymore. No more punishment, no more times being pushed back, no more cheers. It all must go away before I can take off. Before my real career starts. And..." Now the crowd began to chant "You'll never be anything! You'll never be anything!" Jumping off the turnbuckle, Eron began to scream. "I said SHUT UP! Now, as I was saying, my real Main Event status, starts NOW! I may have to take on Mid Carders here and there, because you people put me there. But when I am done, destroying every man who walks in this rings soul, and making them nothing but a bitter hunk of meat, I will, yes, I will be known throughout the world, as King Nothing. King, of what you can never take, but you always desire and have, Nothing. You fans are nothing, destiny is nothing, and Fame is nothing. But still, as a King, I still take. I am going straight for gold, and to be put on the fWo History. And how am I going to do it? Ha, you'll see. You'll see, I'll work harder than any man has, because I am no mere man anymore, I am Eron the Relentless, DarkEron, you can call me. Take a breath, of FRESH DEATH!" With that, Eron tossed the microphone into the crowd and rolled out of the ring, giving the fans the finger as he left. He passed the commissioner at the top of the entrance ramp, and the two exchanged angry words as Travis made his way to the ring... A word from the commissioner. Warfare returns from the "Summer Slaughter" promo and finds the owner and commissioner of the fWo already inside the ring. Travis shows signs of pain in his back from the actions of Davis this past Sunday on Slaughter, but appears to be okay. Oddly enough, Chris Universal is sitting ringside... it appears as if Universal goes out of his way to tell Travis something. Travis nods his head, before beginning to speak: "This past Sunday on Slaughter, Austin Davis physically, verbally & emotionally assaulted me. It felt as if I had fallen... off of a roof. He then proceeded to declare himself the new "owner" of the fWo, and proclaimed himself the World, Internet, International, Cruiserweight, Hardcore, Tag Team & Women's Champion simultaneously. Well, we saw our fWo wrestlers take a stand and show that they weren't going to stand for this new "boss" of theirs... because quite frankly, Austin Davis is not the boss. I am the boss. I never gave control over to Davis, I never signed any papers relinquishing my ownership of the fWo. Austin Davis is not the owner, and thus his decisions will not stand. The champions will remain champions. Davis, I'm sick of your crap... and if you lay your hands on me again, you'll be gone from the fWo FOREVER. You hear me, Davis? And another thing... I know you're here tonight; I know you have Chris Universal' Internet Title and despite my differences with Chris... he won that belt in this ring. So why don't you come out here, and give him the belt?" On cue, "trust me" blares over the arena speakers and suddenly, a garbage truck drives out underneath the TravisTron, and down close to the ring. As Davis' music continues to play, he steps out of the truck, and gives the double-middle finger to Travis & Universal. He then climbs on top of the garbage truck, with a microphone and the Internet Title, and begins speaking. Davis throws the Internet Title into the back of the truck, landing in a pile of rotting garbage. Davis says that Universal can go get it himself, because he's not a giving man unless it's giving someone a kick in the ass. Davis tells Travis that Travis may be sick of his crap, but he's sick of Travis' crap as well. Davis says that Travis has a conspiracy to kick Davis out of the fWo, and that if Davis would so much look at Travis the wrong way, Davis would be gone. He continues, saying that if Travis wants him out of the fWo... and Davis wants control of the fWo, they should have a match at Summer Slaughter. If Davis wins, he would be the new owner of the fWo. If Travis wins, Davis would retire from the fWo and Travis would get what he wanted. Travis says he's not a wrestler, but he'll have someone at Summer Slaughter to fight Davis... even thought it will absolutely KILL Travis for him to ask for that person's help; seeing as how he's been gone from the fWo for quite awhile now and he left with a burnt bridge in the fWo. Travis says he'll accept the stipulations, if Davis' opponent wins, Davis retires from the fWo. If Davis wins, Travis will sign over the company to Davis and Davis becomes the new owner of the fWo. Davis smirks, before closing Warfare off by saying "I'm not ready to retire, and at Summer Slaughter, you'll be looking at the LEGAL new owner of the fWo... TRUST ME!" With that, Warfare comes to a close reeling from this huge development...
