fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 13 February 2000

13 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Backstage Machina stood at the arena entrance, smiling and shaking hands with wrestlers as they entered. He was decked out in full Coke garb, and handed bottles of the liquid to wrestlers as they entered. Jackhammer and Sphere accepted their bottles of Coke from the leader of the Lone Gunmen and continued on into the arena where a booking sheet had been posted. They scanned it quickly, and Sphere's face fell. "What? Debut match with Chronos... hell no. Oh hell no!" Jackhammer attempted to calm his partner down. "Listen, we go talk to the bookers... I'm sure everything will work out just fine. It's a typo or something I'm sure." Jackhammer and Sphere didn't notice that Armstrong had approached from behind, and Sphere gave a small yelp when he turned and saw him. "Problem gentlemen?" Sphere hiked a thumb back at the booking sheet. "Yeah... what gives? I specifically said that I wasn't going to face that loon... neither of us are." Armstrong grinned a thin smile. "Listen. Ric Chronos made his debut in the ring with Jackhammer and Fallen. It only makes sense that we roll out a Chronos debut match with someone associated with the situation... and that's you, Sphere." Sphere started to protest, but James Armstrong held up a hand. "Can I continue? Good. Now you can refuse to go to the ring. I'm sure Mike Heftel is walking around here somewhere looking for a match and would be more than happy to take your place. But you do this for us and I'll do something for you. How does a Tag Title rematch sound to you? Next Sunday... you against the Underground for the titles." Sphere didn't like it, but considered with Jackhammer. They exchanged quiet words, and then Sphere turned back to Armstrong. "We don't want any of that shit that went down last time. Tag titles on the line... in a cage." Armstrong shrugged. "Fine. Now go suit up." Inside the arena, fireworks exploded, signaling that the show was about to begin... Dark Ninja & Tom "the Kung Fu" Bomb vs. Black Quicksilver & Waru The Canadian Connection's theme music blares over the arena PA system and out step Tom "the Kung Fu" Bomb and his sensei, the Dark Ninja. The two men make their way to the ring with the Ninja giving Tom advice along the way. Tom nods in agreement as they step through the ropes and, soon, Black Quicksilver's theme comes on. BQ and Stump lead Waru down to the ring, all the while talking strategy with him. Black Quicksilver and Dark Ninja acting as starters. The two lock up and BQ takes control early with an armdrag followed up by a dropkick. The Ninja quickly slides to the outside to take a breather and exchange a few more words with his trainee, Tom. After a few moments Ninja slides back into the ring and approaches BQ. Quicksilver goes for a lockup but Ninja catches him with a martial arts side kick, knocking BQ off balance. With his foe staggered the Dark Ninja stays on the attack with a series of left and right kicks and jabs. BQ is knocked senseless and falls into the corner where Ninja finishes off his onslaught with a spinning superkick. As BQ tries to crawl to make the tag the Ninja points to Quicksilver and then to Tom the Bomb. Tom nods his head yes, and Dark Ninja makes the tag. Tom rushes in and attempts to jump on top of Quicksilver, but BQ dives to Waru and makes the tag just in time. Tom flops onto the ground as Waru charges him. Waru scoops him up and drops him with a slam, causing Tom to arch his back in pain. The Dark Ninja decides to cut his loses now and rushes Waru with a flying crescent kick. Waru continues to get hammered while, back in his home corner, BQ seems to be having a conversation with Stump. BQ solemnly nods his head and turns to Waru, who is attempting to make the tag. BQ winds up with Stump and wallops his partner, then leaves the ring for the locker rooms. As Waru falls to the mat, busted open, Ninja drags Tom the Bomb back into the ring. Tom is elated to see Waru incapacitated, and begins to warm up for his big moment. For about a minute Tom readies himself with jumping jacks, push ups, and sit ups. Finally, Waru slowly gets to his feet. Tom sees his chance and charges in a chop that just glances off the chest of Waru, but it's enough to get the job done. Waru goes to the canvas like a ton of bricks, and Tom makes the cover. One, two, three, and it's all over. While Ninja kicks Waru to the outside Tom grabs a microphone and says, "Look at that! Did you see it?! Wahoo! I'm a karate master! Flying Frenchie, you're next!". The Dark Ninja pulls Tom to the side to talk about modesty as Slaughter goes to a commercial. Result: Dark Ninja & Tom "the Kung Fu" Bomb by Pinfall In the hands of the champion Backstage the Flying Frenchie and James Armstrong watched Tom's comments with a grin on his face. "Can you believe zis moron? Seriously... can zu imagine ze buy rate for a card zat headlines ze Frenchie against zat Canadian fool? Well, if ze Frenchie was in ze ring I am sure it would do okay... but still..." James Armstrong nodded next to the Frenchie. "You can't blame a guy for wanting to get to the top of the card, though. Actually... you would be surprised... Tom's segments have been really bringing up the quarter hours. People like that goofy stuff... I mean... how else can you explain Ultra Violet?" The Frenchie scoffed. "Please... do not talk to ze Frenchie about zat purple haired moron. Ze Frenchie wants nothi'g to do wit' zat fool. But speaking of ze buy rates... you have still not said who ze Frenchie will be facing at No Escape t'is month." James Armstrong corrected the champion, "No... not No Escape, Trapped. Trapped, remember?" The Frenchie waved his hand in a sign of frustration. "Non, I care not. But again I ask Armstrong... don't make ze Frenchie ask a third time." Armstrong smiled. "It's good actually... the booking committee went over it and decided that if you manage to hold onto the belt into the Pay Per View..." The Frenchie broke in. "Non... zere is non question zere. Ze Frenchie will have ze belt. Now continue." Armstrong cleared his throat. "Well, assuming you go into Trapped the champion, the booking committee has determined that you will pick your opponent... providing of course that you must pick a worthy challenger. No Sid Snow defense." The Frenchie smiled broadly. "Armstrong... zat is ze greatest news I could ever have he'rd. In return I promise you zat you will get ze greatest match ze fWo has ever seen. I will face... non. Why tell you when I can tell ze whole world?" The Frenchie walked out of the room, leaving James to ponder exactly what the Frenchie had in mind... Ric Chronos vs. Sphere "Time" by Anthrax begins to play and out walks Ric Chronos to the ring for his fWo debut, getting a good reaction from the crowd. The music of the Guano Apes begins to play, and out walks Sphere. As Sphere enters the ring, silently Fallen steps out onto the entrance ramp, watching with interest. Chronos starts quick, hammering on Sphere with a series of punches. Chronos whips Sphere into the ropes, and catches him with a traditional spinebuster. Chronos then brings him up, before lifting him onto his shoulders, and then tossing him off into a flapjack. Chronos quickly goes to the head and applies a dragon sleeper, working on the neck of Sphere. Sphere begins to power out with a series of elbows, and Chronos switches to a straight headlock to compensate. Sphere backs him up against the ropes, and attempts to whip him off, but Chronos uses a reversal to turn it into a short-whip DDT. Chronos covers, but only gets two. Chronos brings Sphere up again and whips him into the ropes, but Sphere reverses and sends Chronos in instead. Sphere leapfrogs Chronos as he comes off, but Chronos stops behind Sphere and quickly latches on and drops him with a reverse DDT. Chronos again covers, but gets two. Chronos brings Sphere up again, and sets him in a powerbomb position, looking for his Figure Four DDT. Chronos attempts to hook him, but Sphere has the move well-scouted and takes Chronos down with a double-leg takedown. He holds on to the move, and uses momentum to slingshot Chronos into the corner, where he smashes against the turnbuckles. Chronos staggers out, but ducks a clothesline from Sphere. Chronos acts quickly, hooking Sphere in a reverse neckbreaker position, before turning it into a tornado version of the move, dubbed ironically the Chronosphere. Chronos brings Sphere up again, and this time locks him in the Figure Four DDT position. He hooks the legs, and drives Sphere's weakened neck down into the mat. Chronos quickly rolls over and covers Sphere, and the three-count is tolled. At this, the watching Fallen simply turns around and walks to the back as Jackhammer rushes past him towards the ring. Jackhammer takes Chronos by surprise, clotheslining him up and over the top rope, but Chronos manages to land on his feet, and continues staring down Jackhammer as he backs to the back and Slaughter continues... Result: Ric Chronos by pinfall Threats Slaughter returned from the commercial break to a backstage argument between Chris Universal and Dead. Other wrestlers, officials and members of security held the two men back. Dead said he wanted a match with Universal "as soon as possible", and Universal refused, saying he had already beaten Dead twice and there was nothing left for him to prove. Dead smirked, before suggesting that the two men meet at Trapped, with Dead putting his career on the line figuring that would be enough to bait his longtime rival into a match. Universal again refused, saying he had better things to do, like listen to old Sheryl Crow albums. This made Dead furious, but Universal interrupted, saying "Wait, cool your jets, baldy. You give me a match with your goons tonight, and put their careers on the line tonight in a gauntlet match, and you have yourself a deal... you'll get your ass kicking at Trapped." Dead pondered this for a moment with a twinkle in his eye... then confidently said "Alright, alright. But you're not facing Hiroshima or Mitch. You're facing Heaven, Living, and Hell. Just remember, you couldn't defeat them at Invasion!, so don't expect to do the same tonight. There's going to be a little stipulation though at Trapped if my career is going to be on the line. We're not going to have any normal type of match. We're going to have a no-holds barred, falls count anywhere in St. Louis brawl. Weapons will be legal, bones will be broken, and blood will be shed. Your bones will be broken, your blood will be shed... and there's nothing in this world that will keep me from accomplishing this." Universal said "yeah yeah, whatever baldy." and walked off, which angered Dead even more... Hellrazor (c) vs. Frank Snow Frank Snow makes his way out first to a negative reaction from the crowd. Snow starts to walk up the steps, but thinks better of it and reaches under the ring. Snow begins to set up four tables, one on each side of the ring, as Hellrazor's music begins. The Hardcore Champion comes running out holding Lamont in the air to a big pop from the fans. Hellrazor tosses the cat to the ring attendant, who is immediately clawed, and charges Frank Snow as he is setting up the last table. Hellrazor rams into the table with an elbow, causing Frank Snow to stumble backwards and fall to the arena floor with the table on top of him. Hellrazor steps onto the table and walks to where one could assume Snow's head is and begins to jump up and down. After a couple of hops Hellrazor throws the table off of Snow and lifts him up by his hair. Snow struggles, though, and shoves the champion stomach first into the barricade. Snow picks up a chair and dents it over the head of Hellrazor who had had the wind knocked out of him by the previous maneuver. Hellrazor stays on his feet, though, and is the victim of another chairshot. Hellrazor is staggered this time, allowing Snow to pull him onto the nearest table. Snow climbs up onto the ring apron and leaps off, dropping an elbow through the table. And only the table, because Hellrazor rolled off in the nick of time. Hellrazor covers but only manages a two count. The champion pulls the challenger back to his feet and rolls him back into the ring. Hellrazor reenters as well, but not before sliding one of the unbroken tables in. Hellrazor sets it up in the corner and executes a vertical suplex on Frank Snow, plunging him into the table upside down. Hellrazor jumps back to his feet and begins stomping on Snow, unaware that Robert Jensen and the Dame are running down the aisle. Hellrazor pulls Snow onto the ring apron and sets him up for a powerbomb through the only remaining table, but as he does so Jensen slides into the ring and clocks him in the back of the head with a chair. Hellrazor is stunned, and Frank Snow takes advantage by backdropping him through the table. Snow then hops off the apron and covers the champion, picking up the three count and the win. As Snow is handed the title, though, the Dame taps him on the shoulder. Snow turns and is gouged in the eyes by Dame. As the new champion stumbles around blindly Jensen jumps off the apron and brings the chair down on Snow, knocking him out. Robert Jensen and the Dame then make their way back up the entrance ramp and to the back. Result: Frank Snow by Pinfall Chris Universal vs. Heaven, Hell, Living Slaughter resumed with Chris Universal pacing around the ring, awaiting his first opponent in the gauntlet match. First out was Living, who charged the ring as "Burn" by Nine Inch Nails played throughout the arena. The red strobe light flickered as Living approached the ring. He dashed towards the wealthy Universal, who responded with an attempt at a lariat. Living ducked it and bounced off the ropes on the other side, returning to take Universal down with a quick elbow to the jaw. He then dragged Universal by the head over to the ropes and placing him neck first over the second rope. After running and bouncing off the ropes on the opposite side, he leaped and Universal moved and Living ended up flying through the ropes onto the floor. Universal quickly regrouped and went onto the floor. The referee tried to stop him but he quickly grabbed a chair and waited for Living to get up, before slamming it up against his back from behind. He then rolled Living into the ring, and whipped him to the ropes. Living returned and Universal took him down to the mat quickly with a powerslam, followed by a thunderous elbow to the chest. He brought Living up and nailed him with an elbow to the head, before a shot to the stomach, followed up by a fisherman's suplex. Living shouldered up at two, before Universal brought to his feet and slammed him to the mat. As he did, Heaven began making her way down to ringside. She rolled underneath the bottom rope into the ring, and Universal got wide eyed and the two exchanged glares in a proverbial staredown. Living stood up and stood by Heaven, and the two charged Universal. Universal countered with a kick to the stomach on Living, before he reached up and grabbed both of them and took them down easily with a Cha-Ching. He then covered both of them simultaneously, eliminating them both rather handedly. Before he could get up he was met with a double axehandle into the back from behind by the massive man known as Hell, who was attempting to stop the pin but got there too late. Living and Heaven ripped off their FTfWo shirts, before heading up into the crowd with dazed looks on each of their faces. Back in the ring, Universal and Hell brawled back and forth, and after several exchanges in offense, Hell went for his finisher, the "Highway To Hell"... a fireman's carry into a diamond cutter. He went for it, but Universal escaped and landed in front of Hell, before taking him out with the Cha-Ching. Three seconds later, and the careers of Living, Heaven & Hell were over. Universal looked down at Hell with a smirk on his face, before Dead charged the ring. Universal escaped, but Hell did not. Dead reached down and grabbed his servant and brought him up, before kicking him in the stomach, and taking him out with the Crucifixion. The impact of the move was heard throughout the arena, while Universal left the ringside area. Dead then looked down at Hell, unsympathetic for what he had done. Dead left the ring to an enormous amount of boos from the crowd. Hell did not move for several moments, and one of the commentators got up to check him out in the ring as Slaughter moved to a commercial break. While the fans watching at home were watching advertisements for products most normal people would never think of buying, in the arena the fans were watching a staff of medical personnel from backstage rush out to the ring with a stretcher and a neck brace. They checked him over for several minutes, before helping him to the stretcher and strapping him in. Hell was taken backstage, where medical officials evaluated him some more, before sending him off to the nearest hospital elsewhere in New Orleans. Result: Chris Universal by Pinfall Backstage attack As The Boston Strangler was told by Finn & Armstrong they didn't have anything planned for him tonight and was told he could fly to the next city or go home, a figure emerged from the shadows and blindsided the Strangler. He slammed him head first into a table, before throwing him into some boxes nearby. Finn & Armstrong called for security as Rain continued the vicious assault, tossing Strangler up onto a nearby buffet table before following him up on it as well. He hooked him for a powerbomb off the buffet table through another table, which contained various types of food and candy for the wrestlers backstage. The sound echoed throughout the arena as the events were shown on the SlaughterScreen out near the ring, prompting the fans to boo as Rain continued to assault their hero. Rain found a large mug of coffee before slamming it into Strangler' head repeatedly as security finally made their way over to pull the large man off. Vehemently, he pushed the officials away before walking off in another direction. Finn & Armstrong chased him down to no avail, as the security team helped the Strangler to his feet. A trickle of blood came out from his forehead, but it appeared to annoy the Strangler more than 'cause him pain. Strangler walked off in search of Rain, looking for revenge. Underground (c) vs. French Foundation Fans in the New Orleans Arena stood to their feet in anticipation of the next match-up. From the backstage area loomed the challengers for tonight's contest and two-thirds of the French Foundation. Primetime & Courage walked out onto the stage, and were promptly greeted by jeers from the crowd. Courage wore his prized fWo Cruiserweight Title around his waist, and Primetime signaled that he would soon have some gold around his own waist. The two made their way into the ring as "Bawitdaba" stopped playing inside the arena, and as quickly as it faded away "Dragula" began, signaling the imminent arrival of the fWo World Tag Team Champions. Mike Bear & Wretch made their way out to an ovation of cheers from the fans, and Mike Bear & Wretch acknowledged them in turn. Oddly, though, Wretch was carrying both fWo World Tag Team Titles. Bear & Wretch reached the ring and all four men went to their respective corners, before the referee signaled for the bell. Wretch & Courage start things off. The two men circle each other before locking up in a collar and elbow tie-up. Courage gains the advantage with a side headlock, before pulling the hair of Wretch and taking him down to the mat. Wretch complained about a hair pull, but Courage denied having any involvement with it. Courage brought Wretch up while maintaining the side headlock, before tagging out to Primetime. The two men whipped Wretch to the ropes and as Wretch returned Courage took him down with the dreaded drop toe hold right into a perfectly timed DDT by Primetime. Primetime went for a cover, but Bear came in and kicked at the back of Primetime, breaking up the pin at two. Primetime argued with the referee about the illegal interference, as Wretch slowly got up and argued with Bear on the ring apron about "saving" him from the pin. From behind, Primetime struck with a double-axehandle, which sent Wretch flying into Bear, and knocking him off the ring apron to the floor. Primetime then took Wretch and hit a quick snap suplex. Primetime went for another cover, but Wretch managed to kick out. Primetime brought Wretch up and nailed him with an elbow to the ribs, before following that up with a sidewalk slam. The former leader of the Primetime Players brought Wretch up and tagged out to Courage, and Courage took control. A nicely done snap mare by Courage, followed up with a beautifully executed sitting dropkick. Bear pulled himself up on the outside via aid of the guardrail. Courage went for a cover, but Wretch kicked out at two and a half. Courage brought Wretch to a vertical base, before taking him down with another snapmare, and a solid kick to the face. Courage tagged out to Primetime and Primetime came in and the two men hit a double-team snapmare, followed by a double-sitting dropkick, and followed by two solid kicks to the face. Primetime went for another pinfall attempt, but Wretch shouldered out. Primetime decided to go for the kill, and he tagged out to Courage. Courage began going to the top rope as Bear climbed up onto the ring apron... Primetime went for the Primetime Bomb, but somehow Wretch back dropped out of it. Bear shook the top rope, and Courage found himself in a bad way, crotched. Wretch struggled up to his feet and walked over to where Courage was located. He climbed up on the second rope before he leaped and took Courage down with a nicely done hurracarana. Wretch then crawled on the mat as the fans cheered him on. Primetime was instructed back to his corner, and Wretch finally made the tag. Bear came in a house of fire (rather than cheeks of fire), and he nailed Courage with a couple of hard right hands before whipping him to the ropes and taking him down with two reverse knife edge chops to the chest. Primetime came in and Bear took him down with a back drop, and moments later both men got to their feet and charged, prompting Bear to take both men down with a simultaneous DDT. Bear followed it by tagging out to Wretch and telling him to climb up to the top rope, as Bear prepared to take Primetime down with the Whiplash. Bear hit it, but Courage got to his feet and fell back into the ropes, crutching Wretch on the top rope. Courage then waited for Bear to get up before charging at him and taking him down with a spinning heel kick. With Bear out of the way Courage went to the second rope and hooked Wretch for a suplex, before turning it into a fisherman's suplex. Both men hit the mat, and the referee went for the count. Three seconds later, the match was over. The referee gathered the fWo World Tag Team Titles and gave one to Courage and one to Wretch. With the fans unsure of what to think, they sat and watched as the referee ruled that both teams were victors since both men's shoulders ended up pinned to the mat. However, since only one team can be champions, he ruled Primetime & Courage the NEW fWo World Tag Team Champions. Since the finish was controversial he decided that the Underground would receive a rematch this coming Wednesday on Warfare, with the titles on the line. Primetime & Courage seemed disturbed by this, as did the Underground, and all four men thought about kicking the referee's ass... while Slaughter went to a commercial break. Result: French Foundation by Pinfall The decision When Sunday Slaughter returned from commercial break the new fWo World Tag Team Champions were still in the ring, and seemed to be waiting for something. The lights dimmed, and the fWo World Heavyweight champion the Flying Frenchie came out onto the arena to a mixture of thunderous applause and boos. The fans seemed mixed on their opinion of the champion as he walked to the ring, but the Frenchie still held the championship title up high as he walked to the ring. He swung himself inside, and raised both fists into the air for the crowd. He was passed a microphone, and after laying the biggest title in the game on the mat before him, he addressed the crowd. "Here! Let us all give a great round of ze applause to ze new fWo Tag Team Champions of ze world!" The Frenchie put down the microphone as the crowd again gave a mixed response to the news. The Frenchie began to speak again. "Fear not putain... I am confidant zat you will defeat ze losers on Wednesday. But despite zis greatest of news, zis is not ze reason I am out here tonight. Non, I am here because ze bookers in ze back have done ze right thing and placed ze awesome responsibility of selecting which fWo superstar will make it up to ze next level and face ze Frenchie for zis World Championship." The Frenchie paused, letting the moment play out. The fans began to chant "Strangler! Strangler! Strangler!" and the Frenchie smiled a wan smile. "Non. Not ze Strangler. Ze Strangler is as we say in France... mid card talent at best. Non, what I have decided was not easy... for ze opponent tonight perhaps has ze greatest chance of unseating me, ze Frenchie, as champion. But what would ze Frenchie be if not a man of honor? A man of courage? A man who steps up to ze goal and kicks ze ball into ze net when asked to?" The Frenchie ignored the boos coming from the crowd and turned to face his partners in the French Foundation. "Primetime... months ago you and I were to face in ze ring, one on one like ze champions zat we are. Foolish men ruined zis match for ze fans... but ze Frenchie wants... non... demands to make zis injustice right. So at Trapped... Primetime... I would be honored if you accepted the challenge and ze title match." The crowd fell silent, stunned by this development as the Frenchie extended a hand. Then they began to cheer, and Primetime grasped the Frenchie's hand and shook it. Then all three members of the French Foundation raised their arms in the air as Slaughter went to a commercial break. Scott Collins vs. Ice "Crazy Train" hits the speakers and is followed by the entrance of Ice, focused as always, generating a positive crowd response. As he reaches the ring, "Burning" begins signaling the entrance of Scotty Collins. He too is met with polite applause, yet looking to get the match quickly underway, charges the ring, and swiftly is under the bottom rope and up on two feet. A series of stiff punches neutralizes Ice, and a clothesline puts him on his back. Looking to take control from the start, Collins lifts Ice and whips him into the ropes and drops him once more with a shoulder-block. Collins diverts his attention to regain his breath, but is caught by surprise as Ice lunges at the back of his knee and brings him down with a chop block. Stomping the head and neck of Collins, he lifts him and cranks on a headlock. Releasing him from the hold, Ice begins to thrash Collins, throwing all of his weight behind a series of vicious punches. Collins is able to defuse these with a flurry of his own. The two exchange punches, brawling into the corner before locking up, so close they can feel the other's breath hot on their face. Still locked up, Ice shoves Collins into the ropes, and suddenly has Collins and himself flipping dangerously over them. They come crashing down to the floor, arms still entangled with each other. As the referee begins the slow count to ten, both men rise to their feet. Ignoring the referee's count, the two continue to scrap it out, using only their heads as shields against the oncoming knuckles, and willpower to continue on. Continuing to match the other blow for blow, they make their way to the entrance ramp, the referee since now having decided it best to let the two simply fight it out. Then Perfect emerges and looks to prolong the hell he has put both Ice and Collins through. The referee now signals to the time keeper to call for the bell, thus ending this one in a no contest. Neither Ice and Collins acknowledge this as they turn their attention towards Perfect, and soon all three men are swinging wildly. To make matters worse, Eron materializes on the entrance ramp, Internet title around his left arm. Watching from afar on the stage, he simply laughs at the men before him, still no definite contender standing in his way. Result: No contest Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Black Panther "Killed The Cat" plays, and the Black Panther makes his way to the ring, looking more intense than usual and not slapping hands with the fans. He awaits the entrance of the fWo World Champion the Flying Frenchie, who holds the belt high in the air as "Juke Joint Jezebel" plays. Frenchie arrogantly makes his way to the ring, before somersaulting in. He hands the belt to the ref, the bell rings, and it's underway. They lock up, and Panther shoves Frenchie to the ground. Panther charges at TFF, but Frenchie quickly regains the advantage with a drop toe hold that sends Panther face-first into the turnbuckles. As Panther stumbles out, Frenchie wraps him up in a small package. Panther kicks out at one, and receives a dropkick from Frenchie as he gets up, knocking him down. He gets up quickly as Frenchie leaps onto the ropes, and leaps back with an asai forearm smash. Frenchie mounts the second rope and jumps off with a senton bomb, knowing from experience that high-flying is the best way to combat the Panther. Frenchie hooks the leg, but Panther kicks out at two. Frenchie brings Panther up again, and lets fly with a superkick. He quickly rolls him up with a front flip, but Panther kicks out at two. As Panther gets up, Frenchie leaps over him and pulls him over with a sunset flip, again getting two. Frenchie gets up first, and jumps onto the second rope as Panther gets up, before leaping off and catching him getting up with a bulldog. Frenchie wraps the Panther up in a la magistral, but Panther kicks out once more at two. Frenchie quickly decides to head for the very top rope, but Panther stumbles up and against the ropes, crotching TFF. Panther sees an opportunity to hit his Pounce, and quickly scales to the top. Frenchie is somewhat recovered though, and always being one to take a shortcut if it presents itself, executes a testicular kick on the Panther. The ref fails to see it due to the close proximity to each other of both men. Frenchie sees an opportunity to use a version of a move that helped him win at Invasion, the diamond dust. Frenchie quickly does a front flip into an ace crusher, and Panther is down. Frenchie covers... and Panther miraculously kicks out. Frenchie now is looking set him up for the Guillotine Legdrop, and looks to put him in position with a fishermans buster. Panther instead hits a knee that may or may not have hit Frenchie low, and Frenchie staggers away. Panther bounces off the ropes, rears back, and nearly decapitates Frenchie with a monstrous clothesline. Sensing an opportunity, Panther brings Frenchie up and hooks him in a fishermans suplex. Frenchie manages to kick out at the last moment, however. Panther isn't done with him, and brings him up to hit a swinging neckbreaker, which earns a two-count, and a double-underhook suplex, which gets the same. Now Panther is a bit upset, and shoves Frenchie into the corner, before hammering him with a series of stiff rights, not letting up until Frenchie is slumped and sitting in the corner. Panther takes a moment to walk out and gloat and flex for the fans, and Frenchie pulls himself up with the help of the ropes and charges at him. Panther turns at the last moment and lifts Frenchie into a gorilla press, before dumping him unceremoniously onto the outside by the entrance ramp. The referee takes a moment to chastise Panther for this, and as he does the blond, goateed Mitch Wilson comes flying out of the crowd with a slingshot legdrop onto Frenchie with the help of the guardrail. Mitch brings Frenchie up, and hooks him up in the setup for the Inverted Russian Legsweep. He takes a moment to trash-talk TFF, and Frenchie elbows out. He strikes at Mitch, and the ref turns away from Panther to see both men brawling to the back. He makes the ten-count, and awards the match to the Panther via countout. Panther celebrates in the ring as Slaughter goes off the air... Result: Black Panther by Count Out
