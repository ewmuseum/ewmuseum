The six competitors in the match were still brawling, just as they had been at the beginning of the match. The referee continued to shout at all the participants to clean it up and get back to one-on-one in the ring, but none of them gave much of a damn for that particular idea. Finally, the ref threw up his hands and called to the timekeeper, who rang the bell. In the corner, Brand Frontier wound up and clocked Winters with a big left, knocking him over the top rope to the outside. Black Quicksilver, meanwhile, caught Primetime with a SuperQuick-SilverKick that sent him flying outside as well. Death stood up and groggily leaned against the ropes - a bad place to be. Black Quicksilver and Brand Frontier linked hands, and the two charged at Death, catching him with a double clothesline and sending him over the top rope to the outside. The crowd cheered, and the three fan favorites stood in the ring, only now realizing that that sound they had heard was the bell. They looked around in puzzlement as Matt Panzer got up to announce the result. "Ladies and gentlemen... due to the failure of the participants to obey the referee's directions to return the match to a one-on-one state... the match has been declared a double disqualification!" The crowd booed at the result, and Chris Finn and James Armstrong immediately began to discuss how this finish would piss off the smart marks on the internet no end, so at least something positive had come from it. All three Dark Riders gathered on the ramp while their opponents regrouped in the ring. Troy had grabbed a microphone. "Hey... " Troy stepped up on the bottom rope and leaned over. "Tony Danza." She pointed at Primetime. "You may be thinkin that you got off easy this time around, but guess what sunshine... I'M the boss, b(FCC)h. And next week, you and I are going to dance one more time... for my belt... because some things just shouldn't go unfinished, and I won't take 'no' for an answer." Primetime looked at Winters, then Death. He turned to Troy and shook his head "yes". Troy nodded as a grin crept across her face. "Just acquiesce one small request before you go, hm?" Now she pointed at Death and Winters. "Leave Angela and Mona in the back next week. I don't want their dresses to get splattered with your blood." Troy hopped down off the rope and saw BQ hold his hand out, so she tossed him the mic. "While we're on the subject of making announcements regarding title matches...I've made my decision as to who gets a World Title shot at Meltdown. Some people here wanna see Brand Frontier get the shot... ", he said, pointing to the man who was a tag partner of his on this night. "But some want me and Death to go at it again... just so I can get ANOTHER win over him. But... neither of those matches will take place at Meltdown." People in the crowd whispered amongst themselves. What was BQ talking about? "That's because, at Meltdown... I'm defending the World Title against Death AND Brand Frontier... in a TRIANGLE match. And not just ANY triangle match... it will be an ELIMINATION match!" BQ threw down the microphone as the crowd began to cheer, and RUAHH~! went off the air as the Dark Riders remained on the ramp, looking into the ring at BQ, Brand, and Troy... Result: Double Disqualification
