Sunday Slaughter, 31 October 1999

31 October 1999

Introduction "Why are you sitting out here, Ultra Violet?" The young boy was obviously a fan. Dressed in fWo clothes from head to toe, the youngster couldn't have been older than twelve or thirteen. Over one shoulder was slung a blanket, and the boy's eyes were wide as he looked in awe of the man that must have been his hero. The young boy wore an Ultra Violet t-shirt and chewed his lip cautiously. Ultra Violet smiled at the young lad and patted him on his head. "Hi kiddo! Hey... why are you at the fWo show tonight? It's Halloween and you should be out getting some nutritious candy! I hear at the big soccer game across town they are having trick or treat night!" The boy chewed his thumb and shook his head. Mike Heftel leaned over the passenger chair, bumping Glee as he went. Joy put his foot down slightly harder on the peddle, looking with grimly serious as the needle pushed past fifty and neared fifty five. "I'm not sure mother is going to like me driving the van going this fast...," Joy mumbled. In the back of the van Briana and Kid Cool played cards, with Briana obviously doing much better in the game. Kid Cool scratched his head, "Zounds... I just can't seem to win at cards! What do you think Muffin?" The dog yapped in response, and Cool looked over the new cards that Briana just dealt him with a groan. Glee looked around at the night sky with a strange expression on his face "Golly, it sure is a blustery night out tonight. The wind is blowing, the leaves are all over the road... I hope nothing creepy happens!" The van pulled into the backstage area, and Heftel spotted Eron and Ultra Violet standing in the middle of the parking lot. "Huh, they must be waiting for the Great Orange Man. Golly... look at Eron's face! He's as white as a sheet!" Joy scratched his chin as he looked at Eron. "Gee wilikers... he does look shook up! Come on gang... let's go see what's the matter!" The five piled out of the van with Muffin and ran over to Eron and Ultra Violet. Ultra Violet looked confused, and scratched his head. Eron pointed back at the arena shouting, "I'm telling you... a Cowboy Ghost!" Joy gulped. "A Cowboy Ghost? But where?" Eron pointed at the arena more insistently. Briana stepped up with both hands on her hips. "There aren't any such things as ghosts... but it looks like there is one thing we've found here. A mystery! Come on gang... let's go!" Muffin barked, and Kid Cool gulped hard. "Zounds..." Courage (c) vs. Eron Eron walked to the ring, looking over one shoulder and up into the rafters for signs of the apparition that he had seen earlier. Eron started to slide into the ring, but then stopped and looked under the ring apron with some concern. It appeared empty, and Eron climbed into the ring still looking for signs of the Cowboy Ghost. Courage came out onto the ramp, his arms spread wide and the fWo International title strapped around his waist. Jake stood behind him, and the two walked to the ring. From time to time Courage held out the title to the crowd, but when they reached for it he jerked it back and laughed. Courage walked around the ring, mocking Eron from the outside. Courage slid into the ring and the bell rang, but then Courage slipped outside again and started to walk to the back, yelling that he was fine taking the count out loss. Eron raced after him, grabbing him by the head and delivering an underhook DDT. With Courage stunned Eron hauled him back to the ring, taking a moment or two to pound his head into the steel guard rails as he went. Eron spit on Courage and sent him back inside the ring with a roll before climbing in himself. Eron went for a legdrop but Courage rolled free. Courage was angry now, and pounded on the face of Eron with both fists, finally grabbing his head and repeatedly pounding his skull into the canvas of the mat. He wasn Black Quicksilver vs. Hellrazor Hellrazor was in the ring, waiting for his first match. He shivered a little bit, as if a sudden chill had come over him. Black Quicksilver came to the ring, waving Stump in the air and getting a strong reaction from the crowd. Black Quicksilver rolled inside the ring and waved Stump in the air, strutting and raising his arms to the fans. Hellrazor attacked from behind, firing tight rabbit punches into the back of the head. Black Quicksilver dropped stump and fell to the mat, where Hellrazor applied a leg scissors submission hold, trying to take Black Quicksilver out of the match before it had even begun. Black Quicksilver reached for the ropes, but Hellrazor used one arm to swat at Black Quicksilver and kept him grounded in the middle of the ring. Black Quicksilver slowly started to rally back anyway, shaking his fists and climbing first to one knee and then one leg. Black Quicksilver drove an elbow into the stomach of Hellrazor and freed himself. He ran to the ropes, but suddenly the lights in the arena went out. A green glowing form floated down from the rafters, and fans watched in horror as what appeared to be a Cowboy Ghost floated over the ring. The ghost fired his six shooters into the air and let out a whooping cackle. Speaking with an errie echoing voice the Cowboy Ghost moaned, Result: No Contest Chapter Seven: The Chase Kid Cool and Joy walked down the hallway of the arena, the lights getting dimmer as the two ventured deeper into the building. Muffin trotted near them as Kid Cool complained. "Aw man, why are we down here in this spooky arena looking for... *gulp* ghosts..." Joy was in the lead, but looked nearly as spooked as Kid Cool. "Just keep a smile on your face buddy... if we just keep our cool... Kid Cool... heh heh... then we won't..." Joy trailed away as his eyes landed on a catering table set up outside a locker room. It was piled high with cakes and candy, and both Joy and Kid Cool broke out into wide grins. The two ran to the table and at once began to stuff their faces with the goodies. "This stuff is great!," remarked Joy. "You bet!," said Kid Cool. "I don't like candy," said a third voice. Joy and Kid Cool turned around and found themselves face to face with the Cowboy Ghost. "Yoinks! Like... let's get outta here!" The two ran in place for a few seconds, and then bolted down the hallway with muffin next to them. Behind them the Cowboy Ghost chased them, moaning and howling. Kid Cool and Joy ducked into one room, and shut the door. The Cowboy Ghost opened it up and followed them in. Kid Cool and Joy exited from another door, ran a short way down the hallway, and opened another door and went in. Likewise, the Cowboy Ghost followed. Kid Cool and Joy exited another door at the same time the Cowboy Ghost exited a door, and the two came face to face. Kid Cool and Joy screamed and ran down another hallway, with the ghost in hot pursuit... Chapter Eight: Sleepytime Ultra Violet tried to stay awake, but it was hard. He had been up late the night before, and the quiet peaceful nature of the parking lot made it hard to keep from falling asleep. He wanted to stay awake, because he wanted to see the Great Orange Man Primetime (c) vs. Lance Star Primetime came to the ring, looking nervous and unhappy about the situation. He was flanked by the Flying Frenchie and the Strangler. He got into the ring, grabbed a microphone, and yelled, "Okay Slugger... who is it gonna be?" Scott Slugger walked out onto the entrance ramp, Chris Universal, Lance Star, and Ice stood behind him. Slugger raised a microphone to his mouth, and spoke slowly. "Primetime... Primetime... Primetime. Well, we all drew straws... Star... go kick his ass. And you know what Primetime? I think the rest of us will just... stick around to make sure nothing funny goes on." Lance Star walked to the ring and slid inside, facing Primetime with a smug expression on his face. The two locked up, with Primetime taking the quick advantage and twisting Star into an armbar. Star broke free using the ropes, and then spun and delivered hard kicks, which Primetime blocked. Primetime followed up with a punch to the jaw that sent Star backward and earned a warning from the referee. Primetime attempted a side standing kick, but Lance Star caught Primetime's leg and held it before applying a sukui-nage, which is a judo move much like a powerslam, and then attempted a juji-gatame. Primetime blocked this, and kicked into the face of Star, opening up a large cut in his forehead that had been bandaged. Both men stood to break the ten count, and faced each other once more in the ring, both men breathing hard. Primetime grabbed Star's arm and kicked upward, one kick hitting Star's face and doing further damage to the exposed wound. Star broke free of Primetime's grip, and fell down to the mat, rolling toward Primetime as he went. Star grabbed Primetime and threw him to the mat with hip toss and attempted to put him in a triangle submission hold. Primetime saw it coming and reversed the submission attempted, moving over Star and switching the hold into an Achilles tendon hold. Star struggled to get to the ropes to break the hold and managed to do so, clinging to the ropes for support and attempting to stand on his damaged leg. Primetime on his feet tried to take Star down, but Star blocked by holding onto the ropes and kicked up at the midsection to knock the air from Primetime and buy him some time to recover. Primetime was hit with the first kick but shrugged it off, executing a belly-to-back suplex, then got behind Lance Star to apply a modification submission hold that was a mixture of a half-nelson and sleeper hold. Star again struggled to get to the ropes and was finally able to do so before losing consciousness. Primetime now turned to the knees of Star, striking with quick, calculated kicks to the knee cap. Star dodged one kick and executed a leg sweep. With Primetime dazed, Lance Star grabbed at the head of Primetime and slammed it down to the mat from belly-to-belly position. With Primetime on the mat Lance Star drove his knee into the head of Primetime and applied a mixture of a triangle hold and leg lock in the center of the ring. Having seen enough, the Flying Frenchie got onto the ring ramp, trying to get inside the ring and break the hold. The referee restrained him, but in doing so took his attention away from the match at hand. Scott Slugger slid a pair of brass knuckles into Lance Star, who grabbed them and quickly used them... cracking the jaw and sending Primetime down hard to the mat. Chris Universal and Ice grabbed the Frenchie and tossed him from the ring apron, as the Boston Strangler moved around to join in the conflict. Meanwhile inside the ring the damage had been done... as the referee counted to three and put an end to Primetime's Cruiserweight title reign. fWo security mobbed the scene, trying to maintain order... Result: Lance Star by Pinfall Chapter Nine: The Capture Kid Cool and Joy ran down the halls, with Muffin the dog chasing behind them. Behind Muffin was the Cowboy Ghost, floating down the halls after them. Joy and Kid Cool turned a corner and collided with Briana, Glee and Mike Heftel. The five wrestlers all became a tangle of bodies on the floor, and then the Cowboy Ghost turned the corner and was upon them. The five cowered as Heftel stammered, "A r-r-real live ghost! Oh no!" The ghost bore down on them, but suddenly stopped. Muffin had hold of the back of the ghost with his teeth and was growling. The ghost tried to shoo him away, but by then Briana and Heftel were on their feet. "Hey wait a minute! How can the Muffin grab hold of a ghost?" Briana, Heftel, Glee and Joy all surrounded the ghost, who attempted to run away. Heftel held the ghost, and grabbed at the Cowboy Ghost's mask. "Let's see who the Cowboy Ghost REALLY is..." Heftel ripped at the Cowboy Ghost's mask, and Briana gasped as it was revealed that the Cowboy Ghost was... "The Chameleon?" Briana looked amazed as the Chameleon stood unmasked before the small group. "Wait a minute!," said Glee. "The Chameleon would never tell the crowd to take their food with them as they left, he would want them to drop their food so the Total Eclipse could eat it. This can't be the Chameleon... which means that the person under the mask can be none other than..." Glee ripped off the Chameleon's mask, revealing... "Barry Bladerth!" It was Barry Bladerth, who glowered at the five wrestlers before him. John Willis walked into the area with Austin the Giant and other members of the fWo security team, and handcuffed Barry Bladerth, still clad in the Cowboy Ghost costume. John Willis scratched his head. "I don't get it... why would Barry Bladerth want to ruin an fWo show?" Mike Heftel stepped into the area. "That's easy sir. You've forgotten that there is a big soccer game going on across town. It makes sense that he would want to drive people away from the fWo and over to the sport that he really likes. His big scheme was to take money and fans away from the sport that never gave him anything, and over to soccer, where he would be a big star once again. Isn't that right Barry?" Barry glowered at Heftel. "That's right... and I would have gotten away with it too... if it wasn't for you meddling kids!" Security hauled him away kicking and screaming, as the gang complimented each other on a job well done. Suddenly Joy yelped, as the head of the Cowboy Ghost seemed to float on the ground toward them. "Zoinks! It's the real Cowboy Ghost!," screamed Kid Cool, and he started to run away. "No it's not!," said Briana, as she lifted the Cowboy Ghost mask off Muffin, who barked happily. In unison the whole gang said, "Muffin...!," and then they broke into laughter, patting each other on the back. Epilogue "Wake up Mr. Ulpra Violet! Wake up!," The little boy shook Ultra Violet in the parking lot, and the purple haired wrestler came awake with a short, wiping a long string of drool from his face. Ultra Violet looked around, and then punched the ground in disappointment. "Aw man... fell asleep again! I'll never see the Great Orange Man..." The little boy held Ultra Violet's hand and pointed him in the opposite direction, "But wait Ulpra Violet... look over there!" Ultra Violet looked and there, illuminated by the moonlight, lay a single bottle of vitamins. Ultra Violet picked them up, a look of awe on his face. "The Great Orange Man... he really WAS here!" The little boy smiled at Ultra Violet, and the purple haired wrestler walked him back in the direction of the arena. "Well little boy, it's the end of the show, and you know what that means. It means that you get to go home, and I've got to go get changed up and hit the road so I can go to the next show. But can you tell me what we all learned tonight?" The little boy thought for a moment, and then snapped his fingers. "I know! It's that laying in the arena parking lot late at night is good for your health because you get vitamins!" Ultra Violet ruffled the little boy's hair. "That's right... and that goes for all you at home too! Now remember, until next time this has been..." But then Ultra Violet ran into the heavy metal door and passed out in a heap on the floor. The boy shrugged, and walked inside. The show ended then, as the fWo wished one and all a happy Halloween night.
