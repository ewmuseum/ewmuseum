Sunday Slaughter, 1 July 2001

1 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Fireworks went off in East Rutherford's Continental Airlines Arena, and the fans went crazy as Sunday Night Slaughter hit the airwaves. However, their massive cheers turned to boos as "My Hero" hit the speakers, bringing out The Cult Icon, Jon Crisp. The fans, a bit puzzled as to why he has come out without La Parka or Mr. T, booed him even more because of it. He smirked as he walks down towards the ring, and held his arms in the air once he got inside the squared circle. He called for a microphone, received one, and looked at the crowd. Then, he began to speak. "East Rutherford... wow, does this place remind me of Paris." The fans cheered the Icon's comparison to a wonderfully historic city. "I mean, if this weren't the only place dirtier than Paris, I wouldn't know the difference." The fans' cheers immediately turned to boos, and Crisp smirked, before continuing. "It's a shame that a place in the United States needs a "Body Odor" factor in their weather reports for when it gets too hot. I mean, a simple shower and deodorant stick is all you need to stay clean. "But I digress... and since I brought up Paris, I should inform you of why I'm out here, giving this show the highest ratings for an opening segment that anyone has ever seen. I have not one... not two, no... but THREE surprises for all of you. However, these aren't just ANY surprises. Only TRUE fans will appreciate what I, the Cult Icon, have done for you, the Common Idiot. "The first of these three surprises is... well, he needs no introduction. In fact, I'm sure that you, being the TRUE fan of legends and icons that you are, will know just who this is by his music. Hit it!" The fans began to get to their feet, and within a few seconds, music began to play. Juke Joint Jezebel. He had come back. The place exploded. And Gary Coleman walked out onto the entrance ramp. The crowd grew silent. Coleman walked down to the ring, waving to the crowd, who just sat in stunned silence. That silence quickly turned into boos, which surprised Crisp. He welcomed Coleman into the ring, and bent down to shake his hand. Crisp turned towards the crowd with a puzzled look on his face, as the continued booing rang throughout the building. "See, this is why I said only TRUE fans would appreciate this. Everyone knows what Gary Coleman ONLY walks to wrestling rings to the tune of "Juke Joint Jezebel". But no matter. Gary, they don't seem to like you, and that's a shame. You might be a rather pint-sized...hell, TEASPOON-sized icon, especially when compared to me... but that's ok. You go find a dixie cup and have a seat, and sit back for our next guest. "Now this man DEFINITELY needs no introduction. He's a legend in his own right, and someone who is on the rise on the baseball field, but could easily hold his own in the wrestling ring. I think EVERYONE should realize who this man is just by his music." The fans began chanting "SLUGGER! SLUGGER!" in anticipation, as Crisp did a pretty bad job of foreshadowing. Then, "Back in Black" hit, and the roof was blown off in appreciation of Scott Slugger. Only, it wasn't Scott Slugger. Rather, it was John Rocker, reliever for the Cleveland Indians, and New York/New Jersey Public Enemy #1. Rocker threw his left arm up in the air and he ran down towards the ring. Crisp, once again surprised by the boos Rocker received, pleaded with the crowd. "I thought you New York-type idiots LOVED this guy! Come on! "Alright, so you may ask yourself, 'Why did the world's greatest man, Jon Crisp, bring these excellent icons and inspirations out here? Why did Crisp bestow such a pleasure on me? I'm from New Jersey. I don't deserve the finer things in life.' Well, you don't. But, I recognize that you third-world types need some sort of luxury once in awhile, and as a result... And then, Crisp was cut off by "Down With The Ship". He looked up at the entrance ramp as the crowd went crazy, and saw Mike Heftel. "What in the hell do YOU want?" Crisp snarled. "Did you finally figure out that this is your home?" "This isn't my home, although it is quite cozy," replied Heftel, getting some cheers as a result. "You have disgraced some of the greatest names in this industry, do you realize that? How could you bring out Gary Coleman to The Flying Frenchie's music? Or John Rocker to the music of the great Scott Slugger?" "Who are those guys?" Crisp replied. "You don't know who they are?" Heftel asked. "Look," Crisp replied, "I don't have time for old farts from WCW annals. You're wasting my time... umm... what is your name? Tricky Dick? Waru? Oh, wait, I know...Samuel Smooth. Sorry Sammy... you look like such a nerd up close." "Oh, THAT does it! You may call me a nerd, or not know my name. But you DARE scar the name of Samuel Smooth in my presence? "This means war." Heftel left the entrance ramp, while Crisp just looked in that general area. "Moron," he said to himself as he left the ring. Fans began to pelt Gary Coleman and John Rocker with various objects, as Slaughter moved towards its first commercial break.
