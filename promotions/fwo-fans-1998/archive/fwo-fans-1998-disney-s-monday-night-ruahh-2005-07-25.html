Introduction My body eventually goes numb, And my heart has stopped beating. I'm afraid of who or what I might become. My life has no meaning. And with the taste of Him on my lips, I say goodbye to world with the last of my breath. -Slow Dying "Do You Believe In Logic?" And backstage here at the fans Wrestling organization, it was massive chaos. Why? The Disney icons were here, of course. Mickey Mouse, Donald, Goofy, and Sora were all scattered out, surrounded by various wrestlers (we won't name any names), kids, staff, cameramen, et al. Michelle Masters, kinda in the middle of it all, was just smiling like the Chesire Girl. She was standing next to Goofy and Donald, who were posing for a picture with an unnamed green haired wrestler. 'Chelle turned around...and looked up. At 5'7" and 126, the Fiery Thunder Girl was used to being the smaller person in a matching against the average wrestler. But at 6'9", 299, this man was like a tower overlooking a potted plant. She smiled sweetly and waved up at Logic. "Hi! I'm Michelle." It took David Walter Smith a few moments to process all of this. Disney characters? Check. Complete insanity? Check. Redhead staring up at him? Check, and check. The sheer ludicrousness of the situation was enough to distract him from his contest tonight with Jeff Garvin. It caught him completely off guard to the point where he extended a hand down to the Fiery Thunder Girl. "I'm David," he said, voice betraying signs of surprise. "They call me Logic around here. Sometimes, I think I'm lucky to still be employed." Michelle giggled. "You're big. And you look kinda evil. I don't think you'll have a problem keeping a job in wrestling, Dave." She paused. "You're not a lawyer, are you? Cause I know this other Dave, and he's a lawyer, and we think he likes to nail Sonny Silver, and..." The redheaded cutie trailed off as she saw the horrified look on Logic's face. "Please, never bring up the concept of Sonny Silver engaging in intercourse. There are few things that keep me going in my life right now, considering I can't win a match to save mine." The Immense Intellectual ticked off the points on his fingers as he spoke. "Number one, Discovery Channel documentaries. I especially enjoy ones featuring the platypus. Number two, all varieties of puzzles. Keeping my mind in top shape is a priority. And number three, Sonny Silver will never be able to engage in sexual intercourse. Please don't say such things again." "Sorry." Michelle said. "If it makes you feel any better, I don't like him at all. He's a big jerk. And he beat up my husband for no reason." She smiled her sweetest smile at Dave. "At least you seem nice. How'd you get the name Logic?" He blushed despite himself. "I hate him too. He made me cry." Silence for several seconds, while the world realizes that YES, he admitted that of his own free will. "As for the name...I went to college when I was twelve. I'm an agnostic, who believes in the scientific method and that all things can be reasoned out. Logic seemed sort of appropriate." "So you're like a geek in a cute guy's body?" Michelle said, applying a little logic of her own. "One might say that. Opinions on my appearance vary, however." He fought to keep the blush from his face. The whole sordid situation with Lindsay Troy had made him wary. The fact that Michelle was married made things quite easier, however. He could choose to focus on the things they had in common. Like hating Sonny Silver. "So, Michelle," he began, cocking his head to one side. "what is all this?" "I'm in a commercial for this video game called Kingdom Hearts II. It has Mickey and Donald and Goofy in the game. We're making the announcement tonight in the ring. It's a bit silly, but I am so excited." The Fiery Thunder Girl said with a big excited grin. She jumped up and down a bit. "I feel like I'm going to Disneyland, only better. It's so awesome." She frowned. "Wait. You don't hate Disney and now have to hate me, too, do you?" Rather than yell at her, he seemed genuinely happy for her. "Congratulations!" Logic smiled at her. Suddenly, his smile deepened as he looked at the Disney characters behind her. "You know something," he managed, trying to fight off a giggle fit. "I have an idea..." Michelle giggled herself. "Oh? Do tell, Mr. Super Smart Guy?" "We have Disney characters. Probably somewhere there are more costumes. We both dislike Sonny Silver. Therefore, I pose a question to you. How shall we annoy Sonny Silver with what we have at our disposal?" "Well, we do have a spare costume. But it's a Pluto costume and I'm too small to fit into it." Michelle grinned up at Logic. "You, though...." "I could fit," Logic said, nodding. "But we'd need something for you..." As Logic started to don the Pluto costume and the two mused over a costume for Michelle....Michelle's eyes lit up. "I know. I've got my costume for tonight." She ducked into a locker room and emerged in a moment... in full Yuna (FFX-2 era) gear. *wolf whistle* She had barely appeared when.... "WHAT IN THE UNHOLY NAME OF COMMODORE CHARISMA IS THIS HAPPY HORSESHIT?????????!" And with his usual good timing, Sonny Silver, the fWo's Silver Lining and one half of the tag team champions, tore through the hallway in a foaming rage. "Ah, the buttfugly retarded love child of Lita and Christy "I Have No Talent, Only Silicone" Hemme. Dressed up like Lara Croft. I should have known." Sonny said with a sigh of disgust. "And what the fuck is this? The child you and Rob had?" Sonny said, glaring at Logic, who was now in the Pluto costume. "Pluto" simply bounded forward, wrapping his arms around Silver as if for a photo-op. Sonny glared at the Disney character in utter disgust, and attempted to extricate himself. He must have been shocked to find that he could not. "Awww, it looks like Pluto likes you, Sonny!" Michelle said, giggling and playfully whipping Sonny in the side of the head with her braided hair. The fWo's evil half of the tag team champions just started screaming. "If you don't call this retarded slobbering thing off me, when I face you tonight I'm gonna rip off your long fake hair and use it to strangle your fucking husband to death! I'M COMMODORE CHARISMA, FUCKBITCH!" And Sonny went absolutely coronary-ial when Michelle pulled out a digital camera...and took a picture of Sonny hugging Pluto. Pluto seemed quite content to keep the former tSC champion in his "loving" embrace. After a while, he spoke, but in a voice obviously disguised to prevent Silver from figuring out who the hell he was. "We should sing, Michelle." Michelle clapped. "That's a great idea, Pluto." And without further ado.... "Do you beeeeeeeelieve in magic.....in a young girl's heart?" Sonny pierced the air. "NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! GET YOUR PSYCHO DOG OFF ME AND CEASE THIS NOISE POLLUTION AT ONCE!" "Just a spoonful of sugar, helps the medicine go doowwwwnn, the medicine gooo dooowwn, the medicine goo dowwn, just a spoonful of sugar helps the medicine go down, in the most delightful way!" "I'D RATHER DIE OF SARS OF THE COCK AND BE CONDEMNED TO A FIERY TORMENTED HELL THAT CONTAINS NOTHING BUT CLONES OF YOU, YOU UGLY, FOREHEAD IS A SEVEN HEAD, FAKE WIFE STRIPPER WANNABE BITCHFIST!" Sonny retorted. "And for each second I'm being held hostage by this steroid-enhanced Disney PCP dream, I shall KILL you." Finally, said steroid-enhanced Disney PCP dream took off his foam head. "YOU MOTHERF--MMMPPHHHHH!!" And placed it promptly over Sonny Silver's head. "Awww," Logic gushed, turning to Michelle. "Isn't he just PRECIOUS?" Michelle took a picture of Pluto-Sonny. "He is adorable. And these pictures will make many happy memories." "IF YOU DON'T DESTROY THOSE PICTURES, YOUR BLOODY CORPSES WON'T BE RECOGNIZABLE, FUCKERS!" "Hello, Sonny," Logic said, glaring daggers at the tag team champion. "I do love when you make empty threats. Touch one hair on my or Michelle's head, and you'll be wearing the Pluto head PERMANENTLY!" "I'm gonna KILL you, "Logic", (quote fingers are evil!) Sonny spat at the ground as he struggled and managed to get himself free of the Pluto head. "Then I'm gonna revive your dead fucking carcass and make you cry AGAIN! Just because I CAN." Sonny retorted as he finally managed to scramble free. "And you are SO. FUCKING. DEAD. tonight." The Menacing Marauder said, pointing his finger at Michelle.... right before he ran the other way. As fast as he humanly could. "You haven't seen the last of me, "Logic!" He called out over his shoulder as he vanished into elsewhere. "Well played, Michelle," Logic said, ignoring the fleeing Sonny. "Have fun tonight, and congratulations on your commercial." Once again, Logic extended his hand for a handshake. Apparently, he wasn't THAT familiar with Michelle Masters and her usual choice of greetings. Michelle looked at the hand. And then she took it. AND then...she jumped up (hey, she was short) using the hand for leverage and just barely managed to catch Logic around the neck for a hug. "You were awesome!" She said, hugging/hanging around the neck of Logic. "We'll have to torment him again sometime. I'll make sure you get a copy of the pictures." She noticed Logic's expression. "Oh, sorry!" 'Chelle let the hug go, looking a little embarassed. Thoughtfully, the Thinking Man's Grappler rubbed the back of his neck. "Don't worry about it, Michelle. Just go beat him up for me. I'll see you later, okay?" "Bye, sweetie. Take care." And 'Chelle turned back to the insanity she had left behind. She started herding the Disney characters towards the stage. "We're on, soon, guys."
