Wednesday Warfare, 25 August 1999

25 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Somewhere backstage in the arena Nikki waited. Dressed in a tight leather skirt and tight white sports bra, she leaned against the wall running a small ice cube over her chest letting the melted water run between her heaving breasts. The atmosphere was thick with sex and wanting, and Nikki inhaled a deep breath as the door to the room slid open. With a loose open shirt covering only part of his chest, the Speed Demon entered the room and looked deeply into the eyes of Nikki, taking her all in and letting his gaze crawl all about her sensual body. He drew nearer, until only a thin distance remained between them. Circling his arm around his waist to draw her body up against his, the Speed Demon huskily exclaimed, "Give me some sugar baby." Nikki slid her hands under her top and removed it with a quick gesture as the blue censor bars appeared on the screen to avoid costing the owner of the fWo a large FCC fine. The pair moved to the floor of the room, their bodies pressed tightly against each other as the two experienced the act of physical affection for what appeared to be the first time. Nikki stroked the hair of the Speed Demon as he clumsily worked at her skirt, gasping in raspy tones. As the two stroked and caressed each other and drew nearer to each other the fireworks of Wednesday Warfare sounded from outside, barely masking the moans of ecstasy from Nikki. "Oh... Speed... I've missed Primetime since he's been gone, but finally... finally I think I'm ready to move on... Primetime was acting so strange, and I'm glad he's back to normal now... but..." Nikki's comments were suddenly halted as the Speed Demon sat bolt upright and looked her in the face with alarm. "What? Who did you say is back to normal?" Nikki rubbed her hands on his exposed chest and cooed into his ear. "Primetime... why silly? What's wrong?" The Speed Demon stood up, the signs of his passion fading like a swim in a cold river. The Speed Demon looked up at the air, sighing softly with a nervous expression on his face. "Oh crap." Flying Frenchie vs. D-Lo Brown As the music of Warfare died down, fans were treated to the man just discussed, Primetime. He walked to the ring along with his tag team partner Mitch Wilson and the man who would be wrestling that night, the Flying Frenchie. The trio walked to the ring and slid inside, standing for a moment to bask in the reception of the crowd. The reception in question was largely boos, but it did not seem to faze the French stable, who commented to each other on how fit they all looked, and posed for the crowd despite the garbage that was being hefted into the ring. The Flying Frenchie warmed up inside the ring, waiting for his rather unique challenge in D-Lo Brown. The part-time WWF wrestler came to the ring with his usual musical entrance, and slapped the hands of anxious fans as he went. Inside the ring he stood on the top turnbuckle and shook his head at the crowd, hopping backward and attacking the Flying Frenchie at once with a series of roundhouse punches. D-Lo tossed the Frenchie into the ropes, but Mitch and Primetime pulled him out of the ring by the legs as he bounced off their side of the ring. Angry, D-Lo attempted a plancha from the center of the ring, but the French Foundation met him with a swinging chair to the head, batting the star against the ring apron like a baseball player hitting a grand slam. As Primetime and Mitch kicked at D-Lo outside of the ring, the Frenchie rolled back inside and distracted the referee by pointing out how his laces were at different lengths. Eventually D-Lo was rolled back into the ring, where the dazed star was helpless before the Frenchie's attack. Standing over the athlete the Frenchie pointed down at the fallen star and then pointed to various parts of his body, trying to get the audience to encourage which part of him he would use to batter D-Lo. Eventually he settled on his knee, and raised his leg up, patting the knee for encouragement, and then dropping it into the skull of D-Lo. The Frenchie then put a pinky on the chest of D-Lo for the pin, but after the two count the Frenchie changed his mind and covered D-Lo with the other pinky. Again, as the referee counted to two, the Frenchie changed his mind once more and untied a boot. Removing it he used a toe to cover D-Lo, but D-Lo had awakened by the moment, and grabbed the bare foot of the Frenchie and bit it. As the Frenchie yelped in pain, D-Lo grabbed the smaller wrestler and hit his "low down" finishing move, covering the leg and getting the three count. D-Lo slid out of the ring as the rest of the Foundation slid in, making a quick getaway up the entrance ramp as the trio steamed inside the ring. As Warfare went to a commercial, cameras cut to the backstage area where Scott Slugger had arrived at the arena, his duffel bag slung over his shoulder and a walk with some purpose as he made his way to his locker room... Result: D-Lo Brown by Pinfall By the gleam in his teeth Warfare came back from commercial to find Kid Cool standing before the camera. Around his neck hung a large sign reading "PICK ME UV", and Kid Cool's expression was one of begging hopefulness as he began to speak. "Man, life is like a box of chocolates. It's all creamy but if you leave it under the sun you get this brown gooey mess that looks a little like baby poo. I'm going to be in my first fWo Pay Per View, and it's a really bitchin' one. Meltdown kicks some big old cowboy tail and I'm going to be wiggling my oh-so-over behind to the place that each and every man in the world would like to be. The only problem... well... it's my partner. You see, I'd rather wear Sally Jesse Raphael's panties like a ski mask... with her inside them... than wrestle with the no-good, no-talent bum Death. Scotty Crap if you ask me. You know, I'd wouldn't get into the ring with Scotty Crap of the Dork Riders if the ring was filled with Budweiser with a bunch of drowning hookers dressed in pasties and g-strings. Except... You know who is going to be there. The wrestling god. The man who defines what wrestling is. The technical god of all that is wrestling and athleticism prowess. I'm talking about the man. The myth. The legend. Ultra Violet. Ultra Violet, I need your help if I'm ever going to realize my dream to carry the fWo World Heavyweight Title. So I'm asking you... no... I'm getting on my knees... I'm begging... I... oh man, this floor is dirty... I want you to mentor me. Mold me. Make me into a wrestling master like yourself. Teach me the armbar... and... um... the other armbar. The one that goes sorta backwards. The... um... backwards armbar. I need you Ultra Violet... and I think you need me. The young spunky kid who has stars in his eyes and a gleam in his teeth. The young man who is like a ripe flower in need of fertilizer. Ultra Violet, be my fertilizer." With that, the camera went back to the ring before the metaphors could get any more insulting... Hollywood Blondes vs. Ground Zero ... we return from the gracious sponsors 30-second plugs of their fine products. We find the former three time, three time, three time fWo World Tag Team Champions Ground Zero coming to the ring to a nice ovation from the crowd. Ground Zero sits in the ring and waits for The Hollywood Blondes to make their way out. "F-ck Off" begins to play and out walks the controversial duo. The crowd gives them a large negative reaction, while some fans begin heading for the concession stands because they feel the Blondes are "boring". The Blondes slide into the ring and the match immediately starts up. Blondes attack Jackhammer and Wolf Fang before Wolf Fang throws Lance Star into the turnbuckles, while Jackhammer deals with Universal. Wolf Fang clotheslines Star over the top rope onto the floor, Jackhammer then presses Universal high above the air. He then drops Universal outside of the ring onto Lance Star, and both men hit the ground hard. Jackhammer and Wolf Fang wait a minute until they get up... as they're getting back to their feet, Wolf Fang goes running and bounces off the other side of the ring ropes... he returns and Jackhammer gorilla presses him high above the air, and he drops him on both Universal & Star, and all three men crash into the floor. Jackhammer, alone now inside the ring, waits as Star and Universal get up. Star and Universal are staggering on their feet, and now Jackhammer looks down at the duo, and yells "ARRRRRRRRRRRGH!" before grabbing the top rope, and launching himself over with a pescado. He takes both men down with ease and nearly kills them in the process. Jackhammer finally gets up off them, and slides Star into the ring. He goes for a cover, but a kickout at two. He whips Star into the ropes as Universal and Wolf Fang return to their respective corners. Star returns and Jackhammer grabs him by the throat. He then tosses him into the turnbuckles in the corner where Wolf Fang is located. Jackhammer then grabs Star and hiptosses him into the middle of the ring... Star goes flying through the air and crashes on the mat. Star slides over into the corner and he tags in Universal. Universal comes in and then thinks twice about going after Jackhammer. Instead, he says he wants Wolf Fang. Jackhammer asks the crowd if they want to see Wolf Fang kick The Sellout' ass in front of a hundred thousand people. They all respond with a resounding yes. He tags in Wolf Fang. Wolf Fang comes in and dazzles the audience with several technical maneuvers before he whips Universal to the ropes. Universal reverses it, and Wolf Fang returns after bouncing off the ropes. Universal ducks for a backbody drop, but Wolf Fang goes for a sunset flip. Unable to get Universal down that way, he reaches up and pulls Universal down by the tights, thus revealing Universal' bare naked ass to the audience. Universal is embarrassed as he's taken down for the sunset flip, and subsequent pinfall. Three seconds later, and Universal and Star are losers. Universal slides out of the ring furious and embarrassed at the same time. Star tries to calm him down but Universal won't. His emotions are getting the best of him, and as Wolf Fang laughs at Universal, Jackhammer actually breaks a smile in the middle of the ring, as Warfare heads for a preview of Meltdown. Result: Ground Zero by Pinfall Who has the courage? The crowd sit with patience, waiting for the next event to occur. "Reclaim my Place" rips from the speakers as Jake pushes aside the curtains, and begins the long walk down the arena isle. Courage appears from the curtain seconds later, wearing a metallic short-sleeved shirt, and his typical black leather tights. Courage bows his head, and raises his arms as the pyrotechnics rocket off behind him into a smoke formation of the word Courage. Courage turns to see the result of the fireworks, and nods in approval though muttering something about how it'll all improve once he wins Meltdown. Courage shouts his typical phrase of "C'mon Baby!" in attempt to psyche himself up, while finishing the stroll down the isle. Jake climbs on the apron, and holds the ropes open for his employer, West steps through and again raises his arms with pride, to a negative reaction. In typical fashion, Courage points to the crowd, then to his lips, following by gesturing towards his ass. This forces an even stronger negative response. Chris West turns to Panzer, demanding the microphone. Courage breathes in the atmosphere before beginning his speech. "You know with Meltdown around the corner, and the fact that Meltdown requires forming a unit with a partner... though thinking about it, with my moronic partner, Prophet, I may have to tie him to my back, to stop this talentless imbecile from getting us both eliminated... Anyway this TEAM event, has put me in the mood for a bit of tag-team wrestling, before I move on to dominate the singles division. And for all those idiots viewers out there, tag-team wrestling does require a tag-team partner... and who else to be my partner, than Mr. McDonald's, Jake!" Jake takes a step forward and raises his arms, almost in the exact style of Courage. The gray singlett, holding the huge bulk of Jake's weight, shakes in an ugly fashion to the crowds disgust. Courage smacks Jake across the back of the head, signaling that, that was enough and that the sight of him bouncing up and down was making him sick. "So with myself, the talent of the team, and Jake the.. err.. back-up force. I find us to be unstoppable team, basically because it contains... none other than... ME!" As usual this provokes the crowd into a booing response. "Don't mark me wrong, I am definitely not falling into the scene of tag-wrestling, because with idiots like Universal and Prophet as the competition.. frankly, I wouldn't waste my time..." Courage pauses, and waits for the crowd to silence. "So a suitable opponent for this unstoppable team is required, and after a quick look up and down the roster I came to a halt at a particular man... I came to a stop at this man, not because he's a hero, not because he's a 4 time cruiserweight champion, not because he's a fan-favorite, I came to a halt at this man simply because he's so disgustingly ugly, that he needs a mask to walk around in public! Who am I talking about? No its not the masked retard, that being Black Panther.. its Mr. Hideous, Black Quicksilver." Courage pulls the microphone away from his mouth, and looks at Jake as the two laugh. "Now we didn't ask for a handicap match, we asked for a tag-match.. and that we will get, so Quicksilver, you can bring that thing.. that head.. that STUMP, and he... she... it will be your partner for this contest.." Courage grins a 'Stevie Roberts-Shagadelic' sort of grin, before returning the microphone to his mouth. "So Quicksilver, if you have the testosterone, if you have the guts, if you have the COURAGE.. then you'll answer that challenge and you'll meet the man they call Courage, and Burger Kings number one customer, Jake, in the ring.. for a tag match, and we, or should I say I, will totally, utterly and completely rock your world.. C'MON BABY!" Courage throws the microphone to the ground, bows his head and raises his arms as the pyrotechnics send Warfare to a commercial... The Infallible Barry McGee vs. Ice The crowd sat fidgeting, wondering what would come next. Their shock was apparent when a familiar tune began to blare over the arena speakers. As "Born In The USA" played, a rugged handsome man strolled out onto the entrance ramp, his bright orange headband and bright orange sleeveless shirt adorned his upper body. As the man cupped an ear to the crowd to hear their reaction, fans nodded to each other than Mike Heftel had sunk to new lows. Announced as "The Infallible Barry McGee", he stood in the center of the ring and with a bit of help from ring announcer Matt Panzer tore his shirt from his well-oiled chest. Grabbing the microphone from Panzer, "Barry McGee" shouted for the crowd to "Say your prayers, take your vitamins, and splash on some Old Spice, because anything else would be uncivilized!". He continued to flex as Ice's music was played, and the large superstar made his way to the ring for his match, shaking his head in disgust at the display going on in the center of the ring. The two locked up in the center of the ring, or rather, attempted to as Ice slid off of McGee's body with all the oil that had been smeared onto his chest, back and arms. McGee wagged a finger at Ice, and ran to the ropes. He was moving a bit to slow however, and Ice had no trouble stepping to one side and letting McGee sail past him. Ice struck with a kick to the back of the head, and McGee went down to the mat. McGee leapt right back up to his feet however, and Ice looked more annoyed than ever. Working quickly, the big man executed a quick standing suplex, bridging it into a corkscrew bodydrop on the war down to the mat. Despite landing on his neck, McGee got to his feet once more and wagged his finger at the big man to show that nothing he was doing was hurting him. Ice responded with a chop to the chest, which McGee shrugged off again. This continued for some time, with Ice going through his standard moveset and McGee no-selling everything. Ice finally had enough, and simply punched McGee in the mouth with a closed fist. Surprised at this sudden stiff turn of events, McGee stumbled backward, putting his hands up for mercy and looking suddenly more like a rich man's gimmick than of an old, washed up has-been. Ice didn't stop with his attack however, and picked McGee up and hoisted him up above his head. Ice let him dangle there for a moment, and then simply dropped him from eight feet above the canvas. McGee landed on his neck, and cried out in pain. Ice kicked him in the head, driving the side of his boot into the helpless man's skull with solid, sickening thuds. Surprisingly, the referee did not call for the beating to stop, and it was some time later than Ice finally put a boot on McGee's chest for the pinfall victory. Walking from the ring shaking his head, Ice was heard to exclaim that this Friday he would be the two time winner of Meltdown, even if he had to drag his partner to victory... Result: Ice by Pinfall Things become clearer Somewhere in the dark, where creatures of such despair and sorrow hide their faces from the light of day, sat Fallen. He slumped against a cold brick wall, his face resting on the damp mortal and turning a pale shade of blue. His lips moved slightly, just enough to make the viewer realize that he was not dead, but simply asleep. Beneath thin eyelids his eyes flickered rapidly and a low moaning was heard escaping from his lips and echoing in the hallway. Sounds of footsteps were heard, and Dina Sol walked near to the sleeping man, accompanied by a man shadowed just enough by the dark to remain a mystery. The man asked, "Have we done it? Is he in?" and something about his voice was familiar... just enough to send a chill down the spine of anyone listening in. Dina flashed a bright smile, the whites of her teeth contrasting with the deep red of her lips. "Oh yes, he's in. Fallen will be the last man entered in Meltdown... a surprise to all paired with that little bitch Briana. But no matter. We've created a man that knows not of fear... of pain... of anything but of what we tell him. He is our dog to control... he has no name... he is only Fallen." Dina smiled at this, pleased with what she had created. The man spoke again, this time with some concern. "And Machina? What of his role in all of this?" Dina bristled, and looked back defiantly. "He is of no concern. The fool got lucky is all... capturing you and I on film and threatening to expose our secret. But that's been taken care of... he is of no consequence now... he's a neutered pup, and after Fallen gets through with Jon he will end Machina's pathetic career... forever." The laughter that followed would threaten to send a normal man into insanity, so it was with some relief that the cameras returned to the ring and the next match... Darkness vs. Mad Trucker The arena was pitched into black as Darkness walked to the ring, silent, menacing, and giving everyone in the arena the impression that this was a man who could really cause one bloody hell of a lot of trouble if his mind was set on it. He entered the ring over the top rope, and waited for his old enemy to make his way down to the ring. The Mad Trucker obliged, and delivered a loud "TOOOOOOOOOT" to the audience who cheered him on. Having been not himself for the past few weeks, the fans were pumped to see the old Trucker they knew and loved back in action, and as the big man climbed over the top rope himself, the arena was abuzz with excitement. The two men locked up at once, both looking for some kind of advantage but neither man giving any quarter in the test of strength in the ring. Amazingly, the Mad Trucker started to get the edge, pushing back Darkness and nearly bringing him to his knees before the capacity crowd. Darkness took the low road however, kicking at the groin of the Mad Trucker and bringing him to his knees. An uppercut followed up the cheap shot, and then it was Darkness locking his arm around the neck of the Trucker and snapping him back over his shoulder to the mat. Darkness kicked at the Trucker from behind, and then clamped on a sleeper hold. The referee checked the arm of the Trucker as the fans waited with bated breath for it to rise or fall. The arm went down once... and then a second time. The arena fell into a hush as the fans waited to see if the Trucker could escape the deadly hold. The arm began to fall for a third and final time... but then it shot up into the air, and the Trucker got onto one foot... and then another. The Trucker drove an elbow into the chest of Darkness and ran to the ropes, bowling the dark warrior down with a thrust of his shoulder. The Trucker hit a running legdrop, and Darkness went down, crushed under the massive weight of the Trucker's leg coming down on his skull. The Trucker was ready to finish things off when he spied a man at the top of the entrance ramp... the man responsible for his recent malaise. The Surgeon stood there, nervous but still evaluating the Mad Trucker for his insane research. The Trucker turned to confront him, and the momentary distraction proved all Darkness needed. From behind came a mighty DDT, and a quick cover. Darkness emerged from the ring the champion as the Trucker shook off the cobwebs inside the ring. The Surgeon took in all of this, but failed to notice the Flying Frenchie coming from behind. The Frenchie attacked the Surgeon without mercy, and was soon joined by the Mad Trucker. The two opposite men were bound by their dislike of the man who had done them so much harm and beat him down at the top of the ramp. fWo officials hit the scene to break up the brawl as the Frenchie and the Trucker tossed out a challenge for the Surgeon to take his punishment once and for all... in a steel cage on Warfare in one week. Result: Darkness by Pinfall Some would call it an ordinary night Geoff Green walked by, lugging his cameras behind him. It had been a busy night so far, and the main event was still to come. He left the cameras by the restroom, and wandered inside to relieve himself. In the hallway Eron the Relentless walked by, looking for the Dark Rider Stone. Unable to find her, Eron grumbled under his breath about how he was going to win Meltdown no matter what that bitch did to stand in his way. This threat went largely unnoticed, although a couple of other fWo superstars entered the area on their way to somewhere else. Judge Steel passed through, looking from side to side as he went. Somewhere behind him came Samuel Smooth, who was arguing with the Surgeon. "Man, I ain't gettin' into no cage with no honky mo fo... You on yous own, hos." Samuel Smooth dismissed the Surgeon, who was looking somewhat pale at the predicament he was finding himself in. Geoff Green walked into the room, looking around for something. Spotting the cameras near the restroom, Geoff picked them up and left the area, scolding himself for being late to get to ringside for the main event. Los Latin walked into the room, and with a sneer took in the shabby American decorations and presence. He looked at his upcoming schedule, and scratched off named and wrote new ones in that he felt were better befitting an athlete such as himself. Cactus Jon chatted with the Black Panther, who had his Internet title slung over his shoulder. His business completed, Geoff Green exited the bathroom and looked around with some confusion as he noticed his cameras were no longer there. Scratching his head he asked the other people in the room if they had seen his equipment. Few responded, and none in the affirmative. The human drama continued to unfold in the tired way that all dramas do near the end of the night, with nearly nobody paying much attention to what was transpiring. One man did however... and as the fWo wrestlers and personnel went about their business Rain looked grim. For things were happening that did not bode well at all... Scott Slugger (c) vs. Stevie Roberts One half of the fWo World Tag Team Champions Stevie Roberts, accompanied by VL, makes his way out. He says "Tonight, I'm going to become a two-time, two-time, two time fWo World Champion... and later tonight, I'm going to shag baby. Not once, not two times, two times, two times, and certainly more than three, yeah baby~!" as the crowd boos him. Roberts climbs into the ring and holds the ropes open for VL, before VL goes outside and waits in the corner near the commentators tables. Out comes the reigning fWo World Champion, Scott Slugger. Slugger, with the fWo World Title draped over his shoulder, uses his baseball bat to point down at the ring. As he walks down, he says into the camera "Tonight, I'm going to prove that Stevie Roberts is a one-hit wonder... and after the match, VL can shag with my bat because she's not woman enough for the World's Greatest Athlete." Slugger drops his bat and climbs into the ring. Roberts, surprisingly, gets the advantage in the early going but the momentum switch backs to Slugger. After a hammerlock, Slugger whips Stevie into the ropes but Roberts reverses. As Slugger hits the ropes, VL trips him, and then pulls him outside the ring and brutalizes him on the outside, including hitting him in the stomach with Slugger' bat. The momentum easily shifts to Roberts as VL puts Slugger back in the ring, with all of the action outside happening unbeknownst to the referee. The end of the match comes when Roberts hits a Steviekick on Slugger. He goes for a cover, but Slugger kicks out. Roberts then brings Slugger up, and thrusts him into the turnbuckles. He walks in, and grabs Slugger by the head, and then hitting the swinging double-arm DDT (curveball)! He goes for a cover, but Slugger kicks out. Roberts, frustrated on what to do, throws Slugger over the top rope and tells VL to hit him with the bat again. Roberts distracts the referee, as VL goes to hit Slugger with the bat. Before she can, Slugger nails her with a low blow, and then uses the stairs to hit the swinging double-arm DDT on the floor. He then climbs up onto the ring apron, and up to the top ropes. As Roberts moves the referee out of the way, Slugger flies off with a perfectly executed missile dropkick. Not finished with Roberts yet, Slugger yells out "Let the World's Greatest Athlete show you how it's done!" He then throws Roberts into the turnbuckles, and then hits the curveball on him. Three seconds later, the match is over and Slugger has retained his fWo World Title. Suddenly, three mysterious masked men hit the ring and attack Slugger, and knock him unconscious using his bat which they found on the outside. They then pull out a black can of spray paint, and spray painted "S" on his back. The three unknown men remain in the ring embarrassing Slugger, as Warfare comes to a close, with everyone hyped up and excited for Meltdown in just under two days. Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall
