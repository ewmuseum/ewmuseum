Introduction It was a location far away from the hustle and noise of the arena. While wrestlers taped their wrists and put on their tights in preparation for the first Sunday Slaughter of the new year, one man sat on the damp earth and looked quietly into the heavy fog. The snow had fallen in patches on the earth, and it was cold enough in the gloom that the man's breath showed thick on the winter air. Twilight in the sky now, and the falling sun cast long shadows of gravestones across the grass and snow. The city of Boulder was lighting up in the distance, the twinkling of building and house lights giving the city a peaceful, hopeful appearance. Fallen sat, ignoring the cold of the snow and the earth seeping through his jeans where he sat. He glanced with an expression that suggested deep introspection at the gravestones before him. One in particular was now difficult to read with the incoming darkness of the night, but he could still make out the name that was etched in the stone. Even in pitch blackness he would know that name. Ryan Moore's gravestone stood as it always had stood for the last two years. Cold stone with a simple name, birth and death date, and inscription. 'Here lies Ryan Moore, beloved by friends and cherished by family' Ryan did not know if this was the gravestone he would have chosen for himself or not, but felt that the inscription did some amount of justice to the man who was really buried there. John Skyler was beloved by friends... even in the sometimes harsh environment of the fWo he was generally well liked by those around him. Ryan did not know about family; John's parents had died when he was still a teenager and he was an only child. He had no children, and that left only one person who could really be considered family... and she was hardly that in the end. "Will you change it then? Change that headstone so reads true?" Fallen turned and took in Dina Sol. She was dressed much warmer than him, obviously used to the colder weather. A long, thick black coat hung over her shoulders, and her long hair was pinned neatly back behind her head. She looked cold and beautiful, and Ryan understood how John could have fallen for her. She was looking past him now, past him to the gravestone that bore his own name but contained the body of her former husband. Ryan glanced at it as well, sighing so his breath could be seen. "Yes... but it is only words on stone. This is not enough to honor the man, I think we both know that." She looked at him, and in her eyes he could not tell if her expression was heartbroken or harsh. "Honor him? That business killed him. To honor him would be to burn it to the ground and make sure that more like him... like Kenny for that matter... don't fall to it." Ryan shook his head. "Violence begetting violence? No, that won't be my path... I have returned to it, and there is a better way. A cleaner way... we both know that. First Ric Chronos and those that stand with him... but that task is for me and has little to do with John. Then the world championship. John deserved to carry it, to be honored by it. By the end of this year I will have it... or die trying." Ryan looked back at the tombstone, feeling a chill rise up his spine at his own words in reference to the scene of death around him. Dina was looking at the stone, the same mixed expression worn on her face. "You want my help." It was a question, but one that both of them already knew the answer to. Fallen nodded. "I need your help... but I won't do it your way." Dina looked at him, her eyes locking with his. "We'll see. We will see." Thousands of miles away lights were flashing on, fireworks were launching from rafters, and thousands of people were standing to their feet in excitement. The scene was one of jubilation and high energy. Fallen wondered about that... wondered how long that would last. The Family That Plays Together... Brand Frontier looked around.
