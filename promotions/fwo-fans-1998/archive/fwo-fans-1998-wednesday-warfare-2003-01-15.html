Introduction Previously... Finding themselves in Canada, the fWo struggles through booking an international venue week by week with an uncertain future. Questions of when the fWo will return to the United States continue to plague the tired managers of the promotion, and wrestlers have begun to protest their fluid future in more and more violent ways. Meanwhile, the effect of not having a strong US presence has slowly begun to effect the promotion. Merchandise sales have started to slip, and the first sign of television ratings weakness have begun to impact the promotion...
