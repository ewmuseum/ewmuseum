Wednesday Warfare, 15 March 2000

15 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction A long limousine pulled up to the backstage entrance of the Baltimore Arena, site of Wednesday Warfare. Fans cheered from behind protective barriers as the driver exited, and walked to the back of the limo to open the door. The Flying Frenchie stepped out, and regarded the Baltimore crowd with an expression of intense distain. He held in his hand the useless videotape, and tossed it to a ring attendant that arrived to take care of his bags. "Take zis putain... discard zit... or shove ze thi'g up ze Bear's backside if you please." The ring attendant looked cautiously down at the videotape containing Bear's challenge as Courage and Primetime stepped out of the limousine. Courage frowned around himself. "My god, Baltimore does smell..." Primetime nodded. "Always has. I hate coming to this place... it's almost as bad as Chicago. Of course, this place has the worst timekeepers in the world... always yelling at you to go into a four hour rest hold. Please! Baltimore is stuck back in the stone age..." The Frenchie ignored this as he walked up to the booking sheet that had been posted near the entrance of the arena. "Wretch tonight, eh? Well very well. Ze Frenchie will take out ze aggress'ion on ze little sewer rat." The trio walked into the arena as sounds from inside signaled the start of the show... Jackhammer vs. Chris Universal As Warfare comes back from a commercial break, the fans are greeted with the sound of System of a Down. This means only one thing, as the tag team champs make their way to ringside for a singles contest between one-half of the champs and one-half of the team they would face at Body Count. Before they could make their way down the ramp, Jachammer turns and tells Sphere that he can handle this by himself. As Jackhammer is playing to the crowd, the ever-so-famous "Do you buy what the Sellout is selling?" hits the speakers. The crowd erupts as Chris Universal comes down to ringside with Lance Star. Universal, seeing that Sphere was not with Jackhammer, decides that he too could do this on his own, and Star returns to the backstage area. The opening bell rings, and Jackhammer immediately goes after Universal. Universal, being the quicker of the two, escpaes Jackhammer's first lunge at him. When he goes after him a second time, Universal drops underneath Jackhammer and tripped him. Jackhammer makes a third lunge, and again is tripped by the $ellout. Jackhammer got up and heads to the ropes. As he does this, Universals bends down for a backdrop. Jackhammer saw his opportunity and hit a quick DDT. With Universal stunned and on the ground, Jackhammer hits him with an elbow. He picks Universal up and lifts him up over his head, demonstrating his massive strength. He held Universal up for a minute, and then dropped him to the mat, knocking the wind out of the $ellout. Jackhammer went for a cover, and got a two before Universal kicked out. Jackhammer picked up Universal and threw him into the turnbuckle. Universal nailed it and bounced out of it and right into Jackhammer's arms. He lifted him up as if he were a small child, and held him straight up in the air for an extended period of time, before hitting a devastating suplex on him. A second cover was met with a second two-count. Jackhammer, getting frustrated at what he thought was a slow count, picked up Universal once again and went for a chokeslam. However, as he went for it, something out of the corner of his eye caught his attention. He immediately dropped Universal to the ground and started to yell at security about something. It took a minute before security could finally find the "JACKHAMMER IS GOLDBERG" sign in the crowd. Jackhammer, fuming more and more with each passing second, demanded that security removed the sign. Arena security made their way out into the stands and recovered the sign. Once Jackhammer was satisfied that the sign was discarded, he turned around to go back on offense. However, this time he was met with a kick to the midsection by Universal. Universal then hit a double-arm DDT. Universal, sensing he had to act quickly to get victory, waited for Jackhammer to get up. As he did, Universal started to make a break for the ropes. He then jumped, hit the top rope, and nailed Jackhammer in an amazing modified Cha-Ching!, which was met with a massive reaction by the fans, who were taking pictures of the move. He covers Jackhammer and gets the three count. Sphere comes down to the ring to help a stunned and frustrated Jackhammer up and to the back. Meanwhile, Lance Star comes down to celebrate with Universal. Both teams stare at each other with intensity, as if a brawl is about to take place. However, before it can, John Willis, Austin the Giant, and fWo security comes down and gets in between the two teams, as Warfare heads to a commercial break. Result: Chris Universal by Pinfall Love interupted Kid Cool straightened his collar and coughed nervously. He stood outside the dressing room to the Warfare Girls, and inside could hear giggling and laughing. Bracing himself he knocked, and looked down at the flowers he held in one hand. The door opened, and Kid Cool looked nervously into the face of Nip. She looked back at him, blankly. They stood this way for several minutes until Kid Cool coughed again and attempted to look around her. Because of Nip's bust this was no easy task, but he still managed to spot E. Zee and Felacio. Kid Cool frowned and looked back at Nip. Her expression of curiosity hadn't changed and Kid Cool decided that he had better say something. "Er... is Nightshade in?" Nip's expression changed instantly. Color fell out of her face and she looked with stunned horror at Kid Cool. A moment later she swooned backward, and passed out. With Nip's body no longer obstructing his view, Kid Cool peered around inside the locker room for signs of the fifth Warfare Girl. She was nowhere to be found. Dejected, Kid Cool turned and started to walk away. He didn't see Eron until it was too late. He collided with Eron, dropping his flowers in his surprise. Eron's expression was one of anger, and before Kid Cool could do anything Eron had cocked his fist back and let fly with a deadly shot to the face. Kid Cool felt a tooth crack and dislodge from his mouth and he fell to one knee. Eron grabbed his back, and moving quickly rammed Kid Cool into the side of one wall, leaving a bloody stain from the collision of his mouth against concrete. Still not letting go, Eron spotted the commissioner's office and the plate glass window outside of it. Eron gritted his teeth together and ran Kid Cool into the window, sending him through it in a spray of glass and blood. Kid Cool lay motionless on the floor inside the commissioner's office as Eron started to walk away. He spotted the flowers that Kid Cool had dropped and picked them up, smiling now for the first time since his appearance. He walked past the wreckage of the window and tossed the flowers inside the office, letting them rest on the broken and bloody body of Kid Cool... Mitch Wilson vs. Rain We return from the commercial break to a standing room only crowd, as the Baltimore Arena is packed. Nothing for them to cheer about, however, as lightning begins to crackle before "Symphony of Destruction" begins to play. A man standing nearly seven feet three inches and weighing exactly three hundred and sixty pounds emerges from the backstage area, and immediately everyone recognizes it is the man known only as Rain. He reaches the ring quickly, before "Calling All Stations" begins and out walks the reigning fWo Internet Champion, Mr. Main Event Mitch Wilson. Wilson is greeted negatively by the audience, as a red spotlight bathes the stage in crimson. Wilson nears ringside, and the referee retrieves the Internet Title from him and holds it high overhead, before giving it to the timekeeper at ringside. He signals for the bell, and the match is underway. Rain & Wilson lock up in the center of the ring, with Rain garnering the advantage with a reverse chinlock. Rain then uses his size advantage to buckle down on Mitch from behind with his free elbow, before whipping him into the turnbuckles. He charges in with another side elbow, but Mitch moves out of the way, and chops him. This hardly fazes Rain, who grabs him with both hands tightly around his throat, before tossing him into the corner. Rain continues, literally using Wilson as a punching bag, with shots to the face and ribs. The former Internet Champion brings Wilson out from the corner and puts him between his legs, perhaps going for the Rainmaker early, but Wilson slips free and takes it to Rain with a cheapshot and proverbial low blow. Mitch follows it up with a swinging neckbreaker after bouncing off the near ropes. He goes for a cover, but Rain presses him off quickly at the count of one and a half. Mitch continues on the offensive, following it up with a lightning quick elbow drop, driving the point of the elbow deep into the chest of Rain. He then goes for a high risk maneuver, going up to the top rope and flying off with a flying body splash, but Rain moves out of the way. Mitch hits the canvas, and Rain goes on the attack. He brings Wilson up and whips him into the ropes quickly, and when Wilson returns Rain takes him down with a big boot. He then brings him up and whips him into the corner, Wilson hits the corner with an impending smack. Rain follows in with a strong clothesline, before grabbing Wilson by one arm and taking him down with a short arm clothesline. Rain follows with a pinfall attempt, but garners only a count of two. Rain continues, bringing Wilson up and whipping him to the ropes, before ducking for a backdrop. Wilson returns for a sunset flip, and as Rain is about to fall back, he counters by reaching down and grabbing Wilson up by the neck with both hands. He literally presses him high overhead, before slamming him to the mat with a variation of D-Lo Brown's "Sky High". He hooks the leg for a cover, but Wilson somehow unconceivably kicks out, despite the fact his head just hit the mat at twenty miles per hour. Rain decides it's time to finish Wilson off, seeing as how he's sprawled out on the mat in a bad way. Rain brings him up and brings him between his legs, before this time he hits the Rainmaker. Again, Wilson' head smacks off the mat, and Rain confidently walks around the ring for a moment, raising his arms in victory... obviously assuming the match is over. Rain salutes his God's of the Storm in kind, before walking over to Wilson and getting ready to make the cover. However, the premature celebration hurts him, and as he is bending over for the pin, Wilson reaches up and hooks him for a quick inside cradle for the pin. Three seconds later, and Wilson retains the Internet Title. Wilson slides out to the floor and the referee retrieves the title for him, before going out onto the floor and giving him his title and raising his hand. Rain looks to be perturbed in the ring at the referee, as it appears Rain thought it was a fast count. As Wilson boasts about his victory he walks backwards up the aisle, but this hurts him. From behind, Collins appears on the scene and attacks him with a double axehandle, sending Mitch sprawling down the ramp to the floor. Collins and Wilson continue to battle, before Mitch' friend appears on the scene to help Mitch out. The two men battle with Collins over the guardrail into the crowd, while the referee appears to have found himself in a bad position on the inside of the ring: Trapped in the clutches of the large hands of Rain, he is powerbombed to the mat, before a horde of officials and members of security make their way out to stop the madman's carnage... Result: Mitch Wilson by pinfall Coming Soon The screen is jet black and dark. The monotonous nothingness of silence flatly sits on our ears. There is a sudden hum in the background, which grows louder and louder. White digits begin to spill across the blackness, ones and zeroes, zeroes and ones, dancing across the screen with maddening speed. The hum reaches a high electronic pitch, then there is an airy, sighing sound, like wind passing through a crystal wind chime. The numbers freeze in place. The screen remains black as the sound of helicopter blades pump through the air. A single spotlight shines across the middle of the screen, bringing grey digital letters in its wake which sit between the numbers surrounding them. It. Can't. Be. Broken. March 2000 VL vs. Hellrazor vs. Robert Jensen "From This Day" by Machinehead begins blaring throughout the arena as the ring announcer introduces one of the participants for tonight's battle. However, we cut to a backstage shot of Hellrazor looking for a referee, and eventually finding one. Grabbing the referee by his collar he tells him that he's not going to wait to begin the battle in the ring, oh no, he's going to take the battle to them in the lockerroom. Hellrazor takes the referee with him in search of VL, and before they find her, Robert Jensen literally comes out of nowhere with a garbage can to the face, sending Hellrazor sprawling backwards into some nearby boxes. The referee declares the match underway, and Jensen continues the attack. He grabs Hellrazor by the hair and brings him up before nailing him with a plethora of forearms; and then taking him into the boxes with a Michinoku Driver onto the floor. Jensen goes for the cover, and at two, the reigning fWo Hardcore Champion arrives on the scene with a legdrop to the back of Jensen' head. VL then grabs Jensen by the hair and takes him into an adjacent area before whipping him backfirst into the back of an ambulance, but Jensen reverses it. VL smacks the cold steel of the heavy ambulance, before Jensen charges at her... VL sidesteps it and opens the door to the ambulance, and Jensen hits the door with an impending crash. VL charges and takes Jensen down with a spinning heel kick, before attempting a cover. At two, Hellrazor arrives with a beer bottle and slams into VL's back, thus opening a cut. A rush of crimson spills out onto her flesh, before Hellrazor grabs Jensen and fisherman suplexes him through a buffet table nearby. Hellrazor bridges it for the pin, and at two VL recovers to kick Hellrazor in the the testicular area, thus taking all of the testicular fortitude out of Hellrazor in one fell swoop. VL then brings Hellrazor up to his feet and takes him back down with a russian legsweep. As VL crawls back up to her feet with the blood continuing to trickle down her back, Jensen rushes towards her but VL takes him down with a drop-toe-hold into several panes of glass nearby. VL then spots a table, and sets it to the side of the ambulance, before grabbing Jensen and placing him on top of it. The seven time fWo Hardcore Champion then walks around to the front of the ambulance, before climbing up onto the hood. From there, she climbs up onto the top of the ambulance, before screaming out "Fuck you, muthafucka!" and flying off the ambulance with a reverse frogsplash, aka the Illegal Activity. VL crashes through Jensen through the table, but VL is in a bad way as well. Despite that, she crawls over for the cover, but before the referee can count Hellrazor flies off the ambulance with an elbowdrop, before pushing VL off of Jensen and covering him for the three count. Amidst all the carnage, we have a new Hardcore Champion, and his name is Hellrazor. All three participants however are worse for wear, and none of them look to be the true 'winner' after that brutal battle. Result: Hellrazor by Pinfall Ninjas, lies and videotape The scene opened on a video store in an undetermined area of the country. Patrons were walking back and forth, trying to decide when video to take home. One figure in the store towered over the others, as Tom the Bomb whistled quietly to himself as he looked intensely at the shelves. "Brian's Song? What in the hell is that? Uh... sir? Sir?" A video store employee blinked at the large man, and not without some hesitation walked over to the wrestler. "Er... yes?" Tom pointed to the video shelf in front of him with a look of disgust. "What is all of this? Steel Magnolias? The First Wives Club? Where are the good films?" The storekeep blinked. "Er... good movies sir?" Tom frowned. "Yeah... the Karate movies!" The store employee attempted to restrain laughter as he directed Tom over to a shelf in the corner of the store. Tom smiled, and pounded the employee on the back so hard he crashed into the wall. Tom didn't notice as he picked up all the videos on the shelf, including "Enter the Dragon", "Karate Kid 1,2 and 3", "The Next Karate Kid", "3 Ninjas", "3 Ninjas Kick back", "3 Ninjas still haven't hit puberty" "3 Ninjas Adventures in Canada", and of course a wide assortment of "Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles" tapes. Grinning gleefully he headed for the counter... Machina vs. Mad Trucker The lights dimmed, and Machina came out onto the entrance ramp dressed in a long robe. He beamed at the audience, and made his way down to the ring slowly, taking great care not to trip over the folds of cloth. The crowd had never seen a boxers vs. briefs match before, and were anxious to see what was going to develop. Machina entered the ring and with a sudden whoosh he removed his robe, and stood before the capacity crowd dressed only in a pair of dark blue silk boxer shorts. The crowd booed loudly, expressing their intense displeasure at this sight. Suddenly, a loud TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT was heard over the speakers, and all heads swiveled up to the top of the entrance ramp. There stood the Mad Trucker, wearing a long flannel robe. The crowd cheered loudly for a moment, and then seemed to realize what was about to come and began to chant "NO! NO! NO! NO!" The Mad Trucker made his way to the ring undaunted, and it was announced that the first wrestler to be stripped out of their chosen form of underwear would be declared the loser. As the crowd appeared to make a mad dash for the bathrooms or concession shops set up outside the arena proper, the Mad Trucker entered the ring. He let his robe fall, and there he stood, clad only in Fruit of the Loom boxer shorts which were somewhat obscured by the mighty gut of the Trucker. The two men glowered at each other, and then collided, coming together with a slap as they each attempted to rip off the other's clothing. The Trucker swung and struck Machina in the jaw, sending the masked wrestler reeling backward. As Machina attempted to clear the cobwebs from his head, the Trucker moved in and ceased Machina in a mighty bear hug. With the sweaty flesh and hair pressing into Machina's body, the smaller superstar started to fade. The referee, anxious to avoid any unconscious incidents like last time was quick to check the arm of Machina. It fell once... then twice. The referee was about to call for the bell as he hand dropped a third time, but then Machina's fist raised and he began puffing himself up. Machina punched and clapped at the head of the Trucker in an attempt to free himself, but it was no use. Suddenly overcome with an idea, Machina reached down and grasped the elastic band of the Trucker's underwear. The Trucker looked suddenly alarmed, and Machina grinned a devilish grin. With a mighty tug Machina pulled upward, giving the Mad Trucker a massive wedgie. The Trucker released the hold and staggered backward, clutching the little Truckers in pain. Machina waved to the remaining members of the audience and climbed to the top turnbuckle, measuring the Trucker. He flew off, but the Trucker had recovered enough to catch Machina in mid-air. Machina seemed not to mind, and again went for a wedgie attack. But the Trucker was one step ahead of the masked member of the Lone Gunmen, and quickly turned upside down and into a piledriver position. Instead of driving Machina into the mat, the Trucker gave off a loud TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT and grasped the elastic band of Machina's boxer shorts instead, taking his grip off of the superstar. The loose fit of the boxers caused Machina to slip down and out of them, falling naked to the mat below. The Mad Trucker raised his arms in victory and swung Machina's underwear around his head for the crowd, who looked on in horror at the scene that was before them. Machina cursed and scrambled for his robe, but it had long since been taken away by the ring attendants. Angry and shaking his fist at the Trucker, Machina collected as much of his dignity as he could muster and strode out of the arena, head held high and package swinging freely in the wind. John, BodyCount in now less than two weeks away. You can look at this as trouble... or you can look at this as an opportunity to teach every fWo superstar a lesson in what it means to betray one of your own. Matt Douglas vs. Mike Bear Warfare heads to the ring as "Dragula" starts playing, and Mike Bear heads to the ring alone for his International Title shot. The crowd gives him a good reaction as he rolls into the ring and awaits the arrival of INat Champ Matt Douglas. "Higher" starts to play, and Douglas comes out, belt strapped around his waist. He hops onto the apron, and slingshots over the top rope into the ring. Douglas unstraps the belt and hands it to the ref, who signals for the bell. Douglas and Bear circle and lock up, and Bear gains the advantage, shoving Douglas into the corner. Douglas uses his agility to leap onto the second rope, and leaps off at Bear with a sunset flip, but Bear charges in to avoid it. Douglas manages to land on his feet, and as Bear turns around, he leaps up and takes Bear over with a hurricanrana. The ref counts, but Bear quickly kicks out. Both men are immediately up, and Douglas leapfrogs a charging Bear, before dropkicking him in the back of the head to send him face-first into the turnbuckles. Bear stumbles out, and Douglas leaps onto his shoulders before taking him forward with a victory roll. Bear kicks out at two, and both men quickly get back to their feet. Douglas again attempts a hurricanrana, but this time Bear catches him and plants him to the mat with a ring-rocking powerbomb. Bear cradles the legs from above, but Douglas kicks out at the last moment. Bear quickly brings Douglas back up and sends him into the ropes, before nearly decapitating him with a stiff lariat. Bear drops onto Douglas with a senton, and reaches back to hook the leg. Douglas kicks out at two. Bear again brings Douglas up and attempts a kick which is caught. Bear quickly counters with an enzuiguri, taking the smaller man down. Bear again brings Douglas up, and measures him. He then launches his foot at the face of Douglas, but Douglas moves. Bear turns to find his face full of dropkick courtesy of Douglas, and rolls to the outside to regain his bearings. Douglas quickly builds momentum, then bounces off the ropes and flies over the top rope with a tope con hilo, taking Bear out and maintaining the offense. Douglas is quickly up and rolls Bear back in, before hopping onto the apron himself. Bear quickly regains his bearings and strikes outside with a series of forearms, gaining an advantage over Douglas. Bear then steps onto the apron himself, and lifts Douglas onto his shoulders, looking for a devastating death valley driver off the apron. Douglas struggles, knowing this move could seriously injure him, and manages to wiggle free into what Bear sees as a powerbomb position. Bear looks to leap off the apron, but Douglas fires back with a series of punches, fighting back with everything he has. Douglas finally gains the advantage, and spins over to the back of Bear, before using his momentum to propel himself off the apron with a bulldog onto Bear. Douglas immediately jumps up, pumped up, and after briefly stopping to pump up the crowd, rolls Bear into the ring. Douglas heads to the top rope, looking to finish off Bear with his Impact maneuver. Douglas leaps, but Bear somehow manages to muster the energy to move at the last moment. Bear struggles to his feet, still woozy from the high-impact bulldog performed by Douglas, and brings him to his feet. He lifts him onto his shoulders, and takes him down with the jaw-jarring Whiplash. Bear covers, but the ref suddenly notices that on the apron is French Foundationer Courage. The ref, being legally mentally incompetent as all referees are, quickly leaps to his feet and gets in the face of Courage. Bear sees this, and gets up, wondering what the hell is going on. He never sees the Flying Frenchie smash a large video camera over his head. Needless to say, Bear is out. Frenchie rolls Douglas onto Bear, kicks the camera out of the ring, and gestures to Courage who vacates the apron. The ref notes the pinfall, and counts one, two, three and Douglas retains his International Title. The Frenchie laughs as he hears this on the way back to the locker rooms, and Bear in the ring clutches his head as the ref hands the barely-conscious Douglas his belt. Black Panther vs. Boston Strangler Cameras head down to the ring for the Main Event of Warfare, and a buzz runs through the crowd as they await. "Killed The Cat" starts to play, and the crowd responds with loud boos to former World Heavyweight Champion the Black Panther. He shrugs this off, and rolls into the ring, surely looking arrogant under his mask. The lights suddenly dim and fade to pitch black, and a fog rolls in across the entrance ramp. The fans pop huge as the ominous tones of Orff's "O Fortuna" hit the loudspeakers, and the menacing visage of the Boston Strangler glares out from the entranceway, before striding purposefully to the ring. He quickly enters and stares across at the Black Panther, who looks a little less smug. The bell rings, and the referee signals the match underway. Panther immediately charges, but Strangler sidesteps him and Panther bounces off the ropes, heading back towards Strangler. Strangler ducks a clothesline from Panther, and Panther hits the ropes again. Strangler quickly charges at him and catches him coming off with an explosive spear that takes Panther down hard. Strangler hooks a leg, but Panther kicks out at one. Strangler brings Panther up again, and shoves him into the corner. Strangler quickly strikes with a series of forearms directly to the side of the head, and Panther stumbles out. Strangler grabs him by the head and attempts to hit his tornado bulldog early, but Panther throws him off at the last second. Panther takes a moment to shake off the effects of Strangler's attack before bringing him back up. Panther quickly whips Strangler into the ropes, and plants him with a rotated spinebuster. Panther hooks a leg, but Strangler escapes at two. Panther brings Strangler up again, and decides to go to work on the right arm as to prevent the Boston Massacre. Panther quickly locks Strangler into an armbar, and takes him down, quickly rotating into a cross armbreaker. Strangler is in intense pain, but refuses to submit. Panther decides to increase the pressure by rolling onto his stomach and pulling up on the arm. Strangler struggles in the hold, but barely manages to reach the ropes with his left arm. Panther is up first, and blocks Strangler's attempted windup punch with the left hand, and follows that up by grabbing Strangler's right arm with his and taking Strangler over with a one-armed armdrag. Strangler slowly gets up, clutching his arm, but still attempts to fight back with another left. Panther ducks and quickly goes behind Strangler, locking up his right arm. Panther then proceeds to place Strangler in his own move, the crossface chickenwing. He locks it on, but this has the opposite effect of what was intended on Strangler; it pisses him off. Strangler quickly fires back with left elbows to the head, and after the third Panther stumbles back and releases the hold. He quickly regains his wits and charges at Strangler with a clothesline, but Strangler catches his arm in mid-move and twists him into the Boston Massacre. Panther has nowhere to go, and flails his free arm in the air helplessly. After a few moments, the arm ceases flailing, and the ref takes it. He lifts it once, and it falls. He lifts it a second time, and it falls again. He lifts it a third time... and a chair strikes him over the head. Dead steps through the ropes, and strikes the Strangler on the back with the chair. Strangler releases the hold, but doesn't go down. The Panther stumbles forward as Strangler turns to face Dead, and Dead spits his mysterious black mist at Strangler. Strangler ducks however and the mist strikes the Black Panther in the face. Strangler quickly launches a foot at the face of Dead, and it drives the chair back into Dead's face, causing him to fall back through the ropes. Strangler turns back to Panther, but Panther strikes him with a low blow, as the mist had no effect on him due to his mask. Panther lifts Strangler up on the turnbuckle to hit the Pounce, and climbs up after him. Strangler fires back with a stiff left, and Panther stumbles away. Strangler turns to face the crowd, and reaches back to hook the head of Panther. He then spins around, driving Panther into the mat with a tornado bulldog. The referee is slowly beginning to come to his sense, and Strangler lifts Panther up from the ground before taking him over with a german suplex. Strangler holds on and brings Panther up, then proceeds to re-clamp the Boston Massacre on him. Panther has nowhere to go, and the woozy referee sees him tapping out and calls for the bell. Strangler releases the hold after a moment, and the referee raises his hand high in victory as Warfare goes off the air... Result: Boston Strangler by Submission
