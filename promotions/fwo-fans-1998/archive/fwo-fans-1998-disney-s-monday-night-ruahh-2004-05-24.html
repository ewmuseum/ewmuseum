Introduction In three weeks their world will change... forever. Technicality "What the HELL do you mean there's no more tag divison?!" Dorjai slammed his fist down on top of Gary Schroder's desk, sending paperweights toppling over and papers scattering. The resident hothead of the Revelations tag team glared menacingly over the desk and into the eyes of the new General Manager of the fWo. Who was conveniently on his knees quivering behind his desk. "L-l-look M-i-i-ister G-G-G..." "Look nothing, Schroder. Troy brought us in per her agreement with Murphy and we signed a contract to compete. So you've got thirty seconds to explain to me how Kenyon and I are supposed to fulfill our contractual obligations when you upped and fired the entire tag roster minus the two of us!" Gary gulped. If he had known he was going to deal with the temper of Dorjai Garai he would have "conveniently" gotten a flat tire on the way to the arena. "No one wants to see tag wrestling anymore, it's as simple as that," Schroder began, standing up from his crouch. "The crowds aren't hot for the matches, team merchandise is at an all-time low, and let's face it...teams with names like foods found in Whirlpool Refrigeradors and gimmicks like two Italians from the streets of Brooklyn aren't getting over. The two of you should be thanking your Boss or whatever the hell Lindsay Troy is for making sure that you two are still getting a paycheck." The last part of Schroder's statement sent Dorjai through the roof. He lunged and grabbed at Schroder's collar, his face contorted in anger. Schroder shrieked, but before Dorjai could do any more, Kenyon stepped forward and put his hand on Dorjai's arm. "Partner, just because there aren't any teams left doesn't mean that we're out of work." "What!?" Dorjai and Gary said in unison. Kenyon merely smirked at Schroder. "You see, Mister Schroder, since we can't be fired and since we can't compete as a team, there's nothing stopping Dorjai and I from entering the singles circuit. Is there?" Schroder gulped. He had a feeling this conversation was going to go more south. Dorjai let out a chuckle and dropped Gary into his high-backed leather chair. "Looks like the two of us are going to be even bigger pains in your ass than you previously thought, eh Gary? Oh, and don't worry. We'll represent Disney real well." Revelations gave Schroder one last collective smirk before exiting the office, leaving Gary Schroder nervously sweating at the outcome of the meeting. Jacob Winters vs. Shirley Savage (c) "All For You" by Our Lady Peace. Jacob Winters strolled through the thick curtains and onto the stage where he was greeted with a mixed reaction. His body was flexed for action, and he took his time walking to the ring. 'The Angel' as he was sometimes called, Winters gracefully entered the ring and went to his corner, where a propped up Kendo Stick was waiting for him. He grabbed the handle and swung it around, nodding with approval as he sat back down and waited for the champ. "Maps" by The Yeah Yeah Yeahs. The curtains swayed as Shirley Savage stepped onto the platform. The fans immediately rose and gave a collective endorsement by their cheers and applause. She would usually smile and absorb their positive energy, but once again, she descended the ramp with out a smile. Her demeanor was resolute because she was operating under special circumstances. She climbed on the ring apron and hopped over the ropes to the inside. She walked to her corner, where the infamous, rusty shovel waited. The bell quickly rang and both combatants went to work. Shirley rushed Winters and grappled with him, only to instantly break out and hit him with a spinning neckbreaker. The fans began to roar, perhaps immaturely, as Shirley taunted her opponent to get up. Jacob pounded the mat in frustration before regaining his footing. He plunged a right hand in the direction of her face, to which she promptly ducked. She took advantage of his surprise by slamming his midsection with a stiff knee, followed by a thunderous DDT! Savage had all the momentum on her side as she mounted on Winters' chest to pound his face with open fists. Her 'mounting' would have been welcomed at any other time by him, but Jacob was at the point of wondering if he would ever feel pleasure again. Her onslaught had arrested him. He felt hopeless. That was, until he sorted out the clutter inside himself to expose motivation. Winters reached up and boxed Shirley's ears, allowing him to scurry free from his attacker. Shirley crawled to her feet to continue the match, but Jacob would have none of it. Jacob clobbered Savage with a Spinning Heel Kick that sent her sprawling into a corner. Winters quickly climbed on the turnbuckles and struck Shirley's head a handful of times while she was pinned. He eventually relented his strikes, pulling her out to the middle of the ring to hit a Hurricanrana! The fans grew uneasy as the upper hand exchanged. At the tail end of his Hurricanrana, Winters hooked her leg for a pin. 1. The referee had barely pounded out a two count before Shirley broke free. She had more of a reason to win than Winters, and she let him know it. She rolled on her stomach and flailed a punch that miraculously landed on his neck. She popped up to her feet, and with what little energy she had planted a quick dropkick on the back of his head. The fans began to cheer as Savage relented. She stood up and marched over to a corner, where she took hold of the shovel and walked back over to her opponent. The fans sensed what would happen next and became excited to no end. However, her weapon attack would be more difficult to execute because in a limber move, Jacob Winters had jumped to his feet. He quickly spun his body on a dime and kicked the shovel from her hands, which made a loud clank as it hit the rings surface. He reached out, and by grabbing her long hair, threw her down to the ground next to her shovel. She didn't feel any ill effects, really, as a result of his move. It looked like Jacob Winters, however, was buying the move she was selling. He grabbed the shovel and propped it up on her stomach. He then walked over to the ring ropes where he bounced up and down to test their elasticity. He dismounted, took a few steps back, and looked as if he were going to attempt a lionsault. Shirley Savage had something else in mind. The fans saw that she wasn't hurt and rose to their feet as she grabbed the shovel and rose to her feet. Jacob Winters, oblivious to her recovery, sprinted to the ropes. He bounced on the ropes; before flipping backwards, Jacob Winters was struck in the back Savage's mighty swing of the shovel. The fans erupted as a look of excruciating pain emerged on his face. He tumbled over the ropes and to the outside to the outside, where he had the mobility of a rock. Since the match was NO DQ and had no count out, Shirley took the shovel and exited the ring, standing on the apron. She looked at her pained opponent below and saw a genuine opportunity to deal out some unnecessary pain...the same kind Winters had inflicted on her limousine driver. Cradling the shovel underneath her thighs, took a giant leap and landed a dangerous leg drop. Shirley's face lit up with pain as well; she didn't expect it to hurt her as much as it did him. She grabbed the back of her legs and cried out softly with agony. Winters still showed no signs of moving, especially after the impact of her devastating aerial move. Shirley Savage and Jacob Winters looked like piled up dead bodies in a morgue. Both pairs of eyes were clamped shut, their heaving lungs the only sign that they were still alive. The fans grew impatient, and began to chant the champion's name, hoping she could bring some life to the proceedings. "SHIRLEY!" "SHIRLEY!" "SHIRLEY!" Shirley reached up to the apron, mightily pulling herself to her feet. She grabbed Jacob Winters, guiding him to and sliding him back in the ring. She crawled back in the ring and lifted him to his feet. Sapped of her energy, she gave a less-than-enthusiastic Irish Whip to the corner. Winters looked down, and to his relief, saw the Kendo Stick standing right in front of him. The fans cheered, beckoning for Shirley to hit a running maneuver. She agreed to their requests, rushing towards her opponent. With lightning speed, Jacob Winters swiveled and swung the Kendo stick with every ounce of his remaining energy. The brittle wood connected with her forehead, splintering as a result of his thunderous Home Run swing. Shirley immediately collapsed backwards and the crowd was instantly silenced. Jacob Winters staggered forward and draped a tired arm on her chest. The referee slid to the floor and pounded the count, which echoed off the cement walls. 1. 2. 3. Jacob Winters smiled as he hoisted his newly-won Cruiserweight title high above his head. He backpedaled a few feet before exiting the ring and hurriedly making his way to the back. Unfortunately, Shirley Savage couldn't be angry or sad. She was out cold. Paladin brought Primetime up to his feet again and gave him a stiff elbow shot to the head, then another. He set Primetime up again, and once more sent him into the ropes. Primetime came off, and Paladin went for a big boot. Primetime rolled underneath it and came up behind Paladin. As Paladin turned, Primetime gave him a hard kick to the gut, then set him up for a piledriver. With a bit of struggle, Primetime lifted him up... then fell backwards, spiking Paladin into the mat. The crowd oohed, and Primetime rolled over, hooking the leg for the cover. One, two, and Brand Frontier broke it up. The referee started to escort Brand to his corner, and Primetime led Paladin to the Riders corner and draped him over the top rope, where Winters and Death took turns pulling down on his head, choking him. The ref turned back to the action, and Death let go of Paladin, letting him stumble back towards Primetime. Primetime grabbed Paladin by the head from behind, bending him backwards, then drove him down, sending the back of his head into Primetime's knee. Primetime reached out and tagged to Winters, then held him open for his partner. Winters hit the ring and drove his knee up into Paladin's head. Primetime let him go and went to the corner, and Paladin stumbled off. Winters came up behind him and took him down to the mat with a chop block to the back of the knee. Winters picked up the leg and then drove it knee-first into the mat, then again and again. He grabbed Paladin by the leg and drove him to the ropes, then draped Paladin's leg over the bottom rope. Winters then jumped up and brought his body weight down on Paladin's leg. Paladin grimaced in pain, and Winters set his leg, then jumped up again - and landed right on Paladin's upraised knee. It took its toll on the knee, but it took more of a toll on Winters' genitals, causing him to fall over, clutching himself. Paladin started to crawl towards his corner. In the corner, Brand Frontier reached out his hand for the hot tag... Paladin stretched out his hand... Winters grabbed Paladin by the foot and yanked backwards, pulling Paladin towards the middle of the ring as he swiped his hand down for what he thought would be a tag. Winters then rolled to the corner and tagged out to Primetime, who hit the ring and grabbed Paladin by the head, pulling him back up to his feet. Primetime set Paladin up, then whipped him across the ring and caught him with a dropkick to the knee, dropping Paladin down. Primetime stood up above Paladin and shouted down at him, then grabbed him by the legs and took him over, applying a boston crab. Primetime sat down deeply on the hold, continuing to work over the already damaged legs of the veteran. The ref asked Paladin if he wanted to quit, and the no he received could certainly be described as emphatic. Primetime continued to apply the hold, then freed one arm to gesture to his corner, turning it into a single-leg crab. In the corner, Donaven Winters stepped into the ring. Winters stepped up onto the second rope. The referee stepped in his way, trying to keep him out of it, then bailed out as Winters jumped off the ropes with a legdrop to the back of Paladin's head that connected squarely. Death looked on approvingly, grinning. On the opposite side of the ring, Brand Frontier stepped in and charged at Winters as he got up to his feet. Brand caught Winters with a clothesline, taking him up and over the top rope to the outside to cheers from the crowd. Brand then hit the ropes and blasted Primetime with a running roundhouse kick, breaking the boston crab very effectively. Death's grin became a grimace, and he appeared to consider stepping in - then stepped back as the referee got in Brand's face, ordering him back to his corner. Brand looked at him furiously, but listened to both the ref's words and BQ's shouts from the corner, returning to his side of the ring and stepping out. Paladin again began to crawl across the ring, looking to make the tag and finally get out of the match, having taken a pounding. He pulled himself towards his corner... closer... closer... And once more, a hand closed around his foot. Paladin mule-kicked Primetime in the face with his free foot, causing PT to stumble backwards. Paladin gave a mighty push forward - And tagged out to Brand Frontier. The fans erupted as the paragon of the fWo hit the ring, ready to kick ass and take names. As Primetime got up, he found himself run over by a Mack truck of a lariat. Donaven Winters stepped through the ropes into the ring and launched himself at Brand with a spinning heel kick - Brand caught him in mid-air and hurled him down to the mat with a variation on a pumphandle slam. Primetime got up, and ran at Brand - Brand caught him, spun him, drove him down with a tilt-a-whirl face-first slam, planting Primetime into the mat. Donaven Winters pulled himself up to his feet, ready to continue the fight. He swung at Brand - Brand caught his arm, twisted, then lashed out with a heart kick, dropping Winters to the mat. Winters rolled back to his corner, and Brand picked up Primetime again. He set him up in a suplex position, then lifted him up into the air. He held Primetime up... then drove him down on his head with a brainbuster, spiking him into the canvas. Brand stood up and pounded a fist against his chest, signaling to the crowd that it was time to stop Primetime's heart - if he could be said to have one. He brought Primetime up and grabbed him by the arm, but Primetime reached out and gouged an eye, causing Brand to release the hold. PT hit the ropes, and came off going for a lariat - but once more Brand caught his arm, and this time held on, holding Primetime open for the Hammer of God. Suddenly, Brand let Primetime drop to the ground. Death had stepped through the ropes for the first time in the match. The referee had signaled - Death had blind tagged in as Primetime hit the ropes. The crowd held their breath as Death stood in his corner, grinning at Brand, who looked back at him with a face that said he was ready for anything. The two men stepped forward, and the crowd began to cheer as they got closer... closer... then stopped, face-to-face. Brand looked slightly down at Death, who, despite his small size disadvantage, held his gaze. Death then began to talk, and Brand took it in without responding - then responded. With his left fist. The crowd erupted as Death was knocked backwards. Brand approached Death, and Death lashed out with a right hand, cold-cocking Brand in his tracks. Death caught him with another shot, then a third, and this time it was Brand's turn to stumble back. He walked into the ropes, and Death set him up and whipped him across into the opposite ropes. As Brand came off, Death spun, going for a rolling elbowsmash - Brand ducked underneath it. Death turned, and Brand reached out both hands and caught Death by the throat, then lifted him up. He then drove him down and forward with a double-arm chokeslam, drawing a huge pop from the crowd. Brand made the cover and hooked the leg - and Death quickly got a shoulder up at two. Brand reached down and grabbed Death by the head, then went to bring him up - Death caught Brand with a low blow, doubling him over. Quick as lightning, Death leapt into the air and drove down with his leg across Brand's head, taking him into the mat with a rocker dropper. Death brought Brand up and shrugged him up onto his shoulders, holding him there in a firemans carry position - then spun his opponent down to the mat with a faceslam. With Brand down on the mat, Death headed to the corner and stepped out onto the apron, then began the climb up to the top rope. He reached the top turnbuckle and stood up, preparing to hit the Death From Above - Brand was up. He swept Death's legs out from under him, crotching him on the top rope. Brand began to climb up to the top, then hooked Death's head as the two men stood on the turnbuckle. Brand then fell backwards, taking both men to the mat with a spectacular superplex. The crowd popped for the big move, and both men lay on the mat, having taken a heavy impact. Death, still the fresher of the two, rose first, sitting up, and a moment later, Brand started to pull himself up to his feet as well. Both men got up, and Brand caught Death with a stinging left, then another. Death staggered backwards, and Brand charged at him - Death ducked underneath and lifted Brand up, then drove him down throat-first across the top rope with a stun gun. Brand lay on the mat, and Death ran into the ropes, then leapt onto the second strand. He then came off with an asai moonsault, connecting squarely with the midsection of the man who would be the special referee at Unleashed. Death made the cover and hooked the leg - one, two, kickout. Death brought Brand up to his feet again, and went behind him and hooked his head, preparing to hit the Death Wish. He set to lift Brand up - and Brand spun in his grip, going from facing the ceiling to facing the mat. He then lifted Death up and took him down with a release northern lights suplex to cheers from the crowd. Death lay on the mat, and Brand began to crawl towards his corner. He got closer... closer... Death stood up... Brand tagged out to Black Quicksilver. The fWo World Heavyweight Champion. BQ hit the ring and blasted Death with a SuperQuick-SilverKick before he could do anything. Death hit the mat, and Donaven Winters and Primetime hit the ring. Winters and Primetime charged at BQ, who leapfrogged both of them. The two turned, and BQ grabbed both of them by the head and drove them down to the mat with a double Cubic Zirconium Cutter to a huge pop from the crowd. BQ stood up, then turned just in time to see Death charging at him. BQ dropped down and took Death down with a DTH~! - sending him face-first into the crotch of Donaven Winters. Death rolled away, and Winters held himself in intense pain. Death pulled himself up to his feet, and BQ hit the ropes. He jumped up onto the top, then flew backwards, moonsaulting and trying to hook Death's head on the way down for a reverse DDT. Death stepped forward out of his reach, and BQ landed on his feet. BQ charged at Death as he turned, then leapt up onto his shoulders, going for a rana. Death caught him, then held him in the air and drove him down with a powerbomb. Death held on, brought BQ back up again, and hit another powerbomb. Still holding on, Death lifted BQ up, then slammed him down with one... more... powerbomb. Death let go and staggered backwards into the ropes, and BQ lay on the mat, dazed. Death stood against the ropes, then ran a thumb across his throat. It was time to end it. BQ began to pull himself up to his feet - Death leaned back into the ropes, then ran at him. He leapt up, double-underhooked BQ's head and spun - BQ threw him off. Death landed on his feet. BQ lashed out, going for another SuperQuick-SilverKick, but Death caught his foot. He held BQ there for a moment, then spun him. As BQ spun back towards him, Death went for a clothesline - but BQ, using his momentum, jumped up onto his back, spun, then brought Death down with a sitout facebuster. Temporary Refund Adjustment. The crowd erupted, and with Death down, BQ vaulted onto the apron and quickly climbed up to the top rope. Silver Splash time. BQ reached the top and stood - Death was back up. Death, still woozy, launched himself into the ropes, causing BQ to lose his balance and fall onto the turnbuckles, crotching himself. BQ then fell to the mat. Death quickly went to the corner and set himself up on the second turnbuckle. As BQ pulled himself to his feet, Death reached out and grabbed him by the head, then applied a double-underhook. He then jumped off, spun, and drove BQ into the mat. Death Trip. The crowd hushed, and Death floated over into a cover, hooking the leg deeply. One. Brand Frontier and Paladin hit the ring. Two. Donaven Winters and Primetime hit the ring. They leapt over the pinfall and caught both men with flying forearms. Three. The crowd exploded with boos as ref's hand hit the mat for the third time, giving the Dark Riders the victory, and perhaps more important, giving Death a pinfall victory over the fWo World Heavyweight Champion going into their World Title match at Unleashed. Death stood and raised his arms in triumph. Across the ring, Donaven Winters and Primetime were brawling with Brand Frontier and Paladin. Death took this in and rolled to the outside of the ring, then quickly retrieved a chair. He rolled back in. Brand and Paladin were gaining the advantage on Winters and Primetime - then Death struck, slamming the chair down onto the back of Brand Frontier's head, then swinging it baseball-bat style into the back of Paladin's leg, causing him to fall to the mat and clutch the injured leg. Death stood over Paladin and laughed down at him - then heard something behind him. He turned. Black Quicksilver superkicked the chair into his face. The crowd went nuts, but their joy was short-lived, as Donaven Winters and Primetime fell on BQ, who tried valiantly to fight them off but was no match for two men at once. Winters and Primetime pummeled BQ to the mat, and a few feet away, Death dragged himself back up and grabbed the chair. He set it down in position and gestured for Primetime and Winters to give him BQ. They tossed the World Champion at him, and in one fluid motion, Death drove BQ down head-first into the chair with a second Death Trip. With Black Quicksilver and Brand Frontier down and out, Death then gestured to Paladin. Winters and Primetime grabbed Paladin by the legs, then dragged him towards the center of the ring. Death picked up the chair, then brought it down on Paladin's legs again and again, hammering them as the crowd booed. Finally tiring of this, Death picked up Paladin's right leg, then placed it into the crook of the chair. He grinned an evil grin down at Paladin, then headed out to the apron to begin the climb to the top rope. As the crowd threw drinks into the ring, Death reached the top rope. Security begin to sprint down to the ring, but as quickly as they slid in, they were ejected by Winters and Primetime. Death stood on the top rope... And leapt. Death flew through the air and drove all 248 pounds of his body down onto the chair with a flying splash, connecting squarely. The crowd gasped. A loud crack! like the sound of a starter's pistol echoed through the arena - but it was almost immediately drowned out by the screan that escaped the throat of Paladin, growing louder and louder as it unspooled from his mouth, devolving into an animal howl, then fading into silence. Death, Donaven Winters, and Primetime stood side-by-side in the ring, with Black Quicksilver, Brand Frontier, and Paladin fallen before them, and Paladin's very career possibly in jeopardy. The Dark Riders finally allowed officials and technicians to hit the ring, and they flooded in, attending to Paladin, as the Dark Riders stood watching it all. Death looked down first at Paladin, and laughed. He then looked at Brand Frontier, then at Black Quicksilver, and gestured across his waist... At Unleashed, if he had his way, Death would become the fWo World Heavyweight Champion for the sixth time. And the Dark Riders would reign triumphant. Ruahh cameras faded to black on this scene of devastation... Result: Dark Riders by pinfall
