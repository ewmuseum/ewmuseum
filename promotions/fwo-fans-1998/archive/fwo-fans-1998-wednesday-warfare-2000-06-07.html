The Arrival Of Britney Spears The opening montage of Wednesday Warfare kicked things off, before the fWo went live for their presentation of the June 7th edition of Warfare, which would forever be known as "the show that Britney Spears appeared on". Outside of the Broome County Veteran's Memorial in Binghamton, New York, a long white stretch limosuine with a hot tub in the back pulled up. The vehicle stopped, before the driver got out and walked to the back. He opened the door, and outstepped four very large bodyguards, that appeared to be fairly young. Their tight white t-shirts could hardly contain their muscular bodies, as they crowded along the luxorious stretch limo. Outstepped a beautiful young female, easily recognizeable anywhere in the world. Britney Spears. She looked around as reporters tried to bum rush the vehicle, but the bodyguards kept them at bay. Near the arena stood two men, one of whom already had a trail of drool running down his chin. The other was the owner of a very successful wrestling organization, and even though their ratings had been slumping the last few weeks, he was generally perceived to be in a good mood most of the time. Chris Finn & James Armstrong stood and waited for one of the most successful performs in music history to take the long walk over to where they were located. Britney motioned to the limosuine driver that she was ready, and the four bodyguards surrounded here. The six of them began to walk over to Finn & Armstrong, who eagerly awaited them. Finally, they reached the goofy looking duo, and Finn extended his hand to welcome everyone. The bodyguards stood there and acted as if he didn't exist, instead looking for people who would pose an actual threat to the well-being of Spears. Finn shook hands with her, and after she shook the hand of Armstrong, she looked perturbed about something, and one could notice her hand ever so slightly rub up against her outfit, as if to wipe something off. The driver headed back to the limosuine, while the bodyguards and Ms. Spears walked with Finn & Armstrong into the arena, where Warfare prepared to go live inside. The Black Panther connects, sending Slugger and the ladder down toward the mat. The ladder hits, followed by Slugger's crumpled body. The Panther rolls Slugger off the ladder, and then sits it upright. The Panther starts to climb. One rung. Two rungs. Three rungs, and then a fourth. Then, it's the WGA. Slugger pushes the ladder together, and then sends it flying toward the mat, with the Panther's back facing the ground. Panther hits the ground, followed by the loud crash of the ladder. Slugger hobbles toward the fallen Panther, and sets the ladder across TBP's neck. Slugger then makes his way toward the turnbuckle. He climbs to the top, and signals to the crowd. Here comes a flying legdrop. The lone Frenchie fan in the stands climbs to his feet; he, too, will cheer for Slugger on this night. Slugger begins to fly into the air, as graceful as a bird. And then, in one sudden swoop....pain. To both men. Slugger connects with the leg drop, but he misses the angle he wanted, and takes most of his body onto the ladder. The Panther narrowly missed severe injury by moving the ladder off his throat. However, he might have had a rib or two broken by Slugger's leap. The two men again lay on the ground, unaware of what is going around among them. A minute goes by, and Slugger is the first to arrive on his feet. He raises his gimpy leg so he can move the ladder and set it upright. Slugger is again ready to climb the ladder. As he begins, the Panther begins to stir. One rung; Panther looking up. Two rungs; Panther on one knee. Three rungs; Panther on his feet. Fourth rung out of five; Panther climbing the other side. Fifth rung; the two stare eye-to-eye. Panther and Slugger look each other square in the eyes. Slugger, unsure of what to do thanks to his weakened condition, goes for the head of Panther. Panther ducks it and grabs Slugger. The Panther has Slugger in a position where Slugger can not fight. The Panther also has him in a position for his finisher. The Pounce is coming. Panther lifts him up, and the ladder starts to give way. Panther flies off the ladder, and connects with the superplex. The ladder falls to the ground, next to the wrestlers. The Panther, with maybe a third wind, begins to get his thoughts together. He begins to set the ladder up, although in slow motion. He sets it up, and begins his journey to the bat. At the third rung, he feels another being climbing the ladder. He looks down, and sees a pair of boots. He looks up, and sees a pair of eyes. They belong to Scott Slugger. Slugger knees Panther in the gut, and locks him in for The Curveball. Slugger looks toward the ground, and makes Panther climb one more rung. Then, he takes off, and lands it. The two men who have fought so bravely in this matchup now appear to be exhausted to the point where they can no longer continue. Yet, both men do. Slugger struggles to get the ladder up, and he begins to climb the ladder. The Panther takes advantage of this delay, and rests for a minute. He, too, begins to climb. There will be no more interruptions now...whoever gets to the top first will win. Except, for one small detail. The ladder has been pushed over. Both men come crashing down to the mat. Slugger, clutching his ribs, looks up and sees the rope lowering. Eron has made his presence known, and he is awaiting the bat. Slugger tries to get up, but to no avail. His body has been punished to the point of shutdown. The same with Panther. This is Eron's moment, when nobody can stop him. Eron grabs the bat, and looks down at Slugger and Panther with a narrowing brow and a dark tone in his eyes. Then, salvation. The Boston Stranger. The Strangler comes in from the crowd and slides into the ring. Still a little bandaged and injured from Eron's attack recently, the Stranger comes in with one thing on his mind: destroy. He grabs Eron and locks in the Boston Massacre. The fans are going nuts, for they are seeing their champion lay waste to the man they hate the most. Security and medical personnel rush to ringside to break up this melee, and the announcers wonder who lowered the rope, as the cameras fade to black, and thus ending the show. Result: No Contest
