Sunday Slaughter, 6 August 2000

6 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The opening montage of Sunday Night Slaughter fades into a live shot of the Casper Events Center in Casper, Wyoming. The building appears to be jampacked to full capacity, before we see a shot of the SlaughterStage. With the thousands of fans in attendance, fireworks shoot off from strategically placed locations on the stage. Music hits, and it's "Dragula", by Rob Zombie. The crowd' reaction turns from cheers into louder cheers, and there is quite a difference between the two, rest assured. The music can only mean one thing... devil worshipping is running rampant in Wyoming. Scratch that. It can mean only one thing... the appearance of Mike Bear. Bear stepped out onto the SlaughterStage, and stared at the sea of fans. As they rose to their feet and cheered him some more, Bear wasted no time and immediately began to walk down to ringside. He exchanged some handshakes and high fives with some fans, before entering the once empty ring. Bear immediately requested - and was given - a microphone. Without hesitation, his music faded, and Bear began to speak. "You know, this past Wednesday I and the World's Greatest Athlete... a certain fWo hall of famer... had a match. While the match could've came off better, everyone on this earth who saw it knows I had the match won. I had the legend himself... pinned in the middle of the ring. And this loud-mouthed, obnoxious, arrogant punk who calls himself Primetime... enters the ring and blows everything. That didn't please me at all... and it was ruined by Primetime. I had him beat... I had Scott Slugger... four-time fWo World Champion... beat." Suddenly, "Back in Black" begins to play. The audience again has something to cheer for... and this time it's perennial favorite Scott Slugger. The World's Greatest Athlete himself. Certain hall of famer. fWo legend. Icon. Holy Ruler of the World. That may be taking it a little too far... but nonetheless, the WGA appears on the ramp. He walks out to a mammoth sized amount of cheers from the crowd, and joins Mike Bear in the ring. Slugger immediately removes his $500 pair of Ray Ban sunglasses and demands his own microphone. He is promptly give one. Slugger waits for the cheers and music to die down, before approaching Bear from about five feet away from him in the ring. "Mike... Bear. The WGA pretty much agrees with everything you said. He agrees that the WGA is a certain hall of famer. He agrees that the match could've come off better. He agrees that the WGA is a legend. He agrees that Primetime is a loud-mouthed, obnoxious, arrogant punk who doesn't know when to stop talking. But the only thing that the WGA doesn't agree with... is that you... had me beat." The fans cheer at the words Slugger had spoken, but the cheers faded as Slugger continued. "You mean to tell me that your finishing move is going to pin Scott Slugger? I've kicked out of the legenday 450, I've kicked out of the legendary Bad Ass Bottom Line, I've escaped defeat at the hands of the Boston Massacre, I've survived the De-Roguer, I've been through it all... and you mean to tell me that a Whiplash is going to beat the man... the myth... the legend himself... four-time fWo World Champion Scott... Slugger?" Bear tries to talk, but is interrupted. "I kicked out of it once. I kicked out of it twice. And without question, without a shadow of a doubt, before Primetime could've stuck his nose where it didn't belong, I would've kicked out of it a third time." Bear is outraged by this and by being previously interrupted, and he tosses the microphone aside. Bear and Slugger come nose to nose in a ring, and it looks as if a war is about to be declared, when suddenly, the unforgiving image of that brash, arrogant, obnoxious, loud mouthed jerk Primetime appears on the screen. "Ladies... ladies..." Bear & Slugger turn their attention to him. Both have incensed looks on their face; but this doesn't stop Primetime from speaking. "If you want to have a match with me, you know where to find me. Out here, later tonight. Three-way dance. I'll be here, waiting, for both of you, with one thing in mind. Snap." Primetime was obviously implying that a place in the hospital next to Havok & Matt Douglas - whom he previously injured over a month ago with the dangerous move known as the backdrop driver - could be arranged for Slugger & Bear. Primetime laughs, before Slugger speaks up. "Primetime, you can't snap crackle and pop your way out of this... eat all the cereal you want to, it doesn't matter... because later tonight, you're going to take a ride on the Curveball Express!" Bear glared at Slugger, while referees charged the ring while Primetime continued to laugh on the SlaughterScreen. Before Bear & Slugger could come to blows, the masse of referees prevented it, and made sure each man exit the ring accordingly to the backstage area. Slaughter goes to it's first commercial break of the night. Crusader vs. "The Legend" Jon Spratt Slaughter returns from it's first commercial break of the night, with the fans anticipating the first match of the night. "Bombtrack" bellows inside the arena, and out walks "The Legend" Jon Spratt for his second match of his comeback 'tour'. It is a non-title match with recently crowned fWo International Champion Crusader, who won the title just days ago by virtue of being Ric Chronos in what was a sure-fire crowd pleaser. Jon is welcomed by the fans who don't react to him favorously with boos or cheers, instead he gets a mixture of both. He doesn't care about the fans reaction however, and takes it in stride as he enters the ring. As Jon stretches his shoulders and cracks his neck in the corner, his opponent makes his way out onto the entrance ramp to the sounds of Biohazard's "Switchback". Crusader has arrived, and the fans cheer in appreciation of the young talented International Champion. He gives a wave to the crowd as he holds his title belt up high for all to see, and turns his attention to the man on the other side of the ring. With a grim nod, he hands the belt to one of the valets at ringside and steps out of the corner to meet the Legend in the center of the ring. A clean collar and elbow tieup results in a quick hammerlock for the speedier Crusader. Jon feints a back elbow before twisting out of the move with a flourish, and pulls the youngster into an Irish whip into the corner, following it up with a clothesline. After the impact snaps Crusader's head back, Jon grabs his arm a second time, and whips him into the opposite corner, charging in with a second clothesline. This time however, Crusader isn't there. The fast thinking wrestler had rolled himself over the turnbuckle and onto the outside apron. Jon's charge met up with nothing but unyielding steel as his arm hit the ringpost full force. Following this minor miscue, Jon stumbles away clutching at his arm a bit and trying to get the circulation going. Meanwhile, his opponent has prepared himself for an attack of his own. Jon turns just in time to see Crusader flying at him with a springboard plancha. A pinfall attempt, and Jon kicks out at two. Both men rise to their feet as Jon takes a moment to remind Crusdaer who he is dealing with by slapping him in the face. Angered by this move, Crusder charges Jon, who catches him with a quick kick, followed by a swinging neckbreaker that earns him a two count of his own. Jon stands up, before dropping a quick knee across the throat of the young superstar, leaving him gasping for air. Sensing an advantage, Jon picks Crusader up, and executes a series of 3 snap suplexes, before bringing him up for a fourth time, and hitting a nasty looking DDT. A cover, and a hook of the leg, and this one is in the books. But not quite. Crusader kicks out at two and a half, much to the surprise of Jon. With a scowl, he drags Crusader up by his hair, and lifts him high preparing to hit the Legend Bomb and end this thing once and for all. At that moment, Crusader catches him by surprise with a quick hurricanrana that sends the Canadian Legend over onto his back. Sensing an opportunity, Crusader runs for the opposite ropes, and flies back with an Asai moonsault. Cover, and a two count for Crusader. This one isn't over yet. Both men pull themselves to their feet, and Crusader goes for a kick to the midsection which is caught by Jon who attempts to send him over with a Dragon Screw. As Jon pulls on the leg, Crusader retaliates with an enziguri that staggers the big man. With Jon slightly dazed, Crusader locks him in and runs up the ropes delivering a tornado DDT that leaves the former three time World's Champion flat on his back. With Victory in his grasp, Crusader climbs the ropes and signals that it's time for Jon to go to school. Gravity 101. The impact of the move is incredible, and would have probably secured a 3 count for Crusader, had Jon not moved out of the way at the last second. By playing possum just long enough, the wily veteran of the fWo had lured the younger and less experienced wrestler into his trap, letting him hit nothing but canvas. Crusader tried to pull himself to his feet, and regain his wind, but in a brief instant Jon was on him and locking in the Torontula. Crusader groaned in pain and shook off the referee as he pulled himself towards the ropes. He inched closer and closer as Jon bore down even more on the hold while Crusader attempted to drag him along. Just when it appeared that Crusader might have a chance at the ropes, Jon released the hold long enough to drag him back to the center of the ring and clamp it on a second time. The ref asked once. The ref asked twice. Finally, Crusader tapped out from the pain of the move, and Jon released his grip. He stood proudly in the center of the ring as the ref raised his hand in victory, and then reached down to help Crusader to his feet. The look on the young International Champion's face was all disappointment at the outcome of the match, but as the two wrestler's eyes met, they shared a look and a nod of respect before heading their seperate ways. He's also sporting primarily yellow, and has on a brown wig with a horrible looking brown moustache, and small goatee. He calls himself the "Mouth of the North" Scoty Hartt, and carries with him a megaphone of which serves no really purpose other than to magnify the sound of his annoying, whiney voice everywhere he goes. As the trio walk further inside the arena, The Wretchster stops and makes sure his bandana is in place, before talking to his cronies. "Alright, Disciple... the goal here tonight is to soften up Mitch Wilson as much as possible, brah. And Scoty... if you can, distract Ice wil Disciple softens up Mitch for me, dude." The laughter of which is even more annoying than Scoty' actual voice, but that's besides the point. The Wretchster has been hanging and banging in California... or some other hellhole where your skin turns orange seemingly overnight, dude... he's been taking his vitamins..." "The Wretchster has been saying his prayers... and he's been pumping iron in the gym, brah. And Mitch Wilson won't know what hit him... when Wretch-A-Mania runs willlllllld all over his ass, brah... and tonight Mitch Wilson is going to fear the spear from Disciple brah, and then he's going to face the wrath of my near eight inch pythons, dude... AND WHATCHA GONNA DO WHEN WRETCH-A-MANIA RUNS WILD... ON YOU! HUH, BRAH, WHATCHA GONNA DO?!" Wretchster salivates some more, before Scoty shrugs, and Disciple nods... because that's what they're good for. We leave this chaotic stage backstage to the ring for our next match-up. Hellrazor vs. Plague Paranoid' by Black Sabbath returns us to Sunday Slaughter, as the crowd has mixed feelings of the entrance of Plague. As he makes his way to the entrance ramp, his tag team partner War, and their friend Stone, accompany him down to ringside. Not being seen much lately since the retirement of Scott Collins, Stone appears to be a little more relaxed than noticed in the past as it appears she is now focused on her business career once more. As they get closer to ringside, War & Plague touch fists as Plague jumps into the ring and await the arrival of his opponent. And he doesn't wait long, as 'From This Day' by Machinehead blares over the PA, signaling the arrival of fan favorite Hellrazor. The crowd pops louder as Fear walks down to ringside with a Heineken, and 'razor has a large feline transport in one hand. It is apparent this transport houses the brains of the Damnation outfit, Lamont...Hardcore Feline Extraordinaire! The 6'6 Hardcore legend walks confidently towards ringside, placing Lamont's transport nearby the Austrian announcer's table. One of the announcer's attempts to pat Lamont, only to get clawed fiercely. It appears Lamont is grumpy about something...perhaps 'razor has him on a diet. Not having much time to attempt to find this out, the match begins with the High flying Plague trying to distance himself from the larger Hellrazor. They lockup, with 'razor taking the advantage and irish whipping Plague to the ropes. Upon return, 'razor drops down as Plague jumps over, bounces off the other rope, and goes flying as 'razor executes an armdrag takedown. Plague gets back up to have 'razor immediately dropkick him, and knock Plague out of the ring, and against the barricade. 'razor decides not to follow, as War and Stone get nearby. Instead, he goes over to Fear, points towards the crowd to distract Fear, and grabs his Heineken to have a drink. The crowd laughs at this, as Fear starts to complain. However 'razor ignores him, reaches over when Plague gets close enough, and glady suplexes him back into the ring. 'razor goes for a cover, only to get 2. 'razor picks Plague up, whips him hard into the turnbuckle, then bounces off the ropes and bulldogs Plague. Hellrazor IMMEDIATELY after bulldogging, goes for a pin, and almost gets the 3 count. 'razor picks up Plague, sets him up for a Gutwrench suplex, only to have it blocked, and Plague sneak a small package. It was broken just before the ref could drop a 2 count. 'razor, a little perturbed with this runs at Plague, who dodges and watches 'razor land hard in the turnbuckle. Plague jumps on the rope, grabs Hellrazor by the head, and spins around to land a nicely executed Tornado DDT. Instead of going for a cover, Plague decides to irish whip 'razor, only to have 'razor spring off the ropes with a flying body tackle. Hellrazor brings Plague up to the top rope for No More Pain, but Plague from out of nowhere suddenly counters into a Northern Lights Suplex off the top rope, goes for the immediate pin, and pulls of the 3-count. Fear, who was polishing off his beer, didn't notice what was going on until after the fact, starts to climb into the ring, before Stone sneaks up from behind and low blows Fear, dropping him to the canvas. War spins 'razor around, then grabs him by the legs, setting up his reverse-powerbomb finisher. Plague hops up onto the ropes, sitting and waiting. Slaughter heads backstage after the commercial to find a nice & tidy office... with two occupants inside. In a chair slumped behind a desk sits the owner of the fWo... while to the right of Chris Finn on a plush leather couch sits James Armstrong. The last three weeks have been hell... just hell... but it's going to be well worth it." James sat up on the couch, and took part in the conversation in the way only James Armstrong can. Armstrong raised his eyebrows. "Oh, really? When was the last time you slept?" Finn shrugged. "It was either two weeks before SummerSlaughter, or the week before... I can't remember." Armstrong looked around the office. "Ah... say, this is a really nice office we've got here in Casper... what the hell kind of name for a city is that? Casper?" Finn shrugged. "I don't know... at any rate, I'm just glad the negogiations went well, I can put that behind me, and focus on more preparations for the Memorial Show." Armstrong raised his eyebrows once again, as he didn't know what Finn was speaking of. "Negogiations? With whom?" Before Finn could answer, Ric Chronos barged in the door. "Hey, it's drunk boy!" shouted the rather impolite Armstrong. Chronos glared in his general direction, before shrugging it off and looking at Finn. "Finn... this battle between Panther and I has got to stop. I want a match with him. I don't care if it's tonight, I don't care if it's Wednesday, I don't care if it's at the Memorial Show." The comment about being a drunk might be partly true, but being a fool... Chronos is not. Chronos charged and beckoned Panther into the office wall. Finn quickly pressed a number on his cell phone, and called for security. Armstrong moved out of the way as the office went from nice & tidy to a scene reminisicent of a war zone. Panther & Chronos knocked things over, got some fists in, before finally security arrived led by John Willis. They worked quickly to split the melee up... before Armstrong looked at Finn and noted that he should hire a bodyguard. Finn sighed as the melee was taken out of his office... which wasn't looking at all how we first saw it.
