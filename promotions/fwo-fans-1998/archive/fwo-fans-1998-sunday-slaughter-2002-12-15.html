Introduction Previously... Two months ago a group of disgruntled past employees took control of the promotion through a ruthless seizure of the company's stock. However, through the efforts of the former owner and the Championship Wrestling League, order was restored to the Fans Wrestling Organization... but at a price. In addition to having a bloated roster filled with questionable personalities, the cost of regaining a controlling interest in the company forced alliances with mysterious partners. In addition, Eron and Graphic Violence have obtained places on the roster... and last week the man dubbed the most dangerous fighter in the world was able to survive in a battle suited to his brawling nature and take control of the newly formed United States Championship. But this is nothing when compaired to the dangers that lurk around the corner from unknown enemies... and with fWo Twilight a week away anything can happen... Control Snow fell silently on the town down the hill. The lights could be seen through the haze. "Look at them down there... walking around like they own the streets they walk on. Chins high as they prepare for the holidays. Never knowing that their time runs short... the night of their finale draws ever nearer... and I shall finally be rid of them. Ah yes, justice comes swiftly through the wind like a hawk." The room was silent following this pronoucement, the echo of the fire crackling all that could be heard in the giant room. "How are things progressing?" The man standing near the door coughed and looked at his clipboard. "Very good. The permits are nearly complete and I've spoken to our members in Congress. We should have more word on things just after the holidays. When Congress gets back in session all should work according to plan." "Excellent... excellent." The older man sitting behind the desk rubbed his hands slowly together. "They will rue the day when they crossed my business." Runner-Up The fans were beginning to get restless, eagerly awaiting action. A few puzzled heads slowly began to turn as the audience noticed a man standing at the top of the entrance ramp. They wondered where the music and lighting was, and, more importantly, who the man was. Some hushed whispers in the crowd whispered about fWo's Survivor II as the man got into the ring. He stood squarely in the center, looking out into the crowd with confident eyes. The clearing of his throat into the microphone that he held rang loudly over their almost complete silence. "Most of you know me. Some do not. My name is Martyr." The noise level rose somewhat at this utterance. The fans recognized the name from the last Survivor contest, and some of the more observant ones had read his remarks about the fWo in several interviews posted online. The vast majority, however, still didn't know what to expect. Eugene raised the microphone to his lips once again and spoke clearly, articulating each syllable. "Sorry about the lack of lighting and music. You see, it is my first night as a permanent member of the roster, and we couldn't get all that stuff ready." He let a smile creep onto his face. "It might be for the better, though. The pretty colors and loud noises might've scared or confused you people. After all, we are in Texas, right?" The chorus of jeers only made Marder's grin grow wider. Beaming, he began again. "That's what I'm all about, though. I don't need cheap parlor tricks. I don't need flames shooting out of all four turnbuckles to tell you that I can kick some ass, before setting said ass on fire. I can prove my skill by beating the piss out of any no-talent hack that makes his, or her, way out right now, but-" The fans began chanting for Brand Frontier, but Martyr quieted them by raising his voice. "BUT, there's a time and a place for that, and though the ring may be the place, the time is certainly not now." Donning his best southern accent, Martyr exclaimed, "There'll be some a-fightin' to come later, y'all!" The fans were starting to get fed up and Martyr saw bits of garbage flying towards him. Looking out into the crowd, still flashing an enormous smile, he raised a scolding finger and shook it. "Now, now. Behave, gentlemen and," Martyr looked disgusted for a moment, "Ladies? I can't really tell, you're all equally fat, and it has this deforming effect on your faces. Plus, with both sexes sporting genuine Texas waterfalls, gender becomes a hit-or-miss game out here. Anyhow, at the risk of tearing you away from the exciting and thrill-packed matches that the fWo has lined up for you this fine, fine, evening, I'm going to tell you a little story. Sit down and get comfy- this'll be just like kindergarten. All four years of it." Eugene barely ducked a beer bottle as it sailed passed his head. The fans were really agitated. "As those of you with any wits at all may have gathered, I was in Survivor II. I made it pretty damn far. In fact, I was the runner-up. At least, that's the results that were presented. I know that even you people, oblivious as you may be, were surprised by the final verdict. I was more charismatic, more talented, more educated; I had everything Brand could muster up, and then some. So why didn't I win, you ask? Think about it." Marder looked around for several seconds, and then quickly stopped, as if he had made some terrible error. "Sorry, my mistake. I forgot about the whole Texas thing again. Hope you didn't give yourselves an aneurysm or anything. Let me do the thinking for you, then. I didn't win because the fWo wasn't interested in a winner who was this talented. After all, do you see Maven taking on HHH? Hell no. Sure, Ross was champ, but that was a dismal failure. Look at him now, beating up valets to get his jollies. And Brand- well, let's simply say he's not here just for the wrestling. At least, not the clothed kind." The comment regarding Frontier and Aimz touched a nerve with nearly everyone, but they were already riled up. Barely able to contain his glee, Martyr continued his tale. "So when I heard that the fWo was opening again, this time in the not-so-able hands of Jim Dudley, I jumped at the opportunity. Knowing Dudley would only want to sink the boat, I pleaded my case to him, and he was more than happy to let me aboard. I was sick with myself then, I really was. Acting ignorant and unaware. But the ends preempted the means, and here I am. I had to make my debut timely, of course. Or else I'd end up going down, too. Half the guys that wrestled under Dudley's regime, you don't even know their names anymore. I will not end up a footnote in fWo's history. I will not be a paragraph." "No," said Martyr, after a moment's contemplation, "My name will be the title. And you're entering a new chapter. So you'd better listen." The audience was so preoccupied with pelting Martyr, they didn't notice his change in disposition from bemused to stern. "My first order of business will be to clear my name, to solidify myself as the true winner of Survivor II. It's an issue of pride. I will not have people saying that I got a free ticket in with the rest of the fresh meat. I demand a recount. This time, with the real votes, and not the ones fabricated by this company to screw me over." Eugene stood for a moment to let the words sink in. By now, the fans had quieted a bit, hearing his change in tone. "Very well. This a warning, a threat, consider it what you will. I don't care who you are or what your standing is- that doesn't factor into my formula. It's a simple equation, and I only have three variables: my anger, your ass, and how hard I'm gonna kick it. From what I've seen of the roster in my brief tenure, they're going to have to work very hard to keep their collective asses from getting handed to them, courtesy of Martyr." Martyr gazed into the crowd for a few moments before speaking slowly and deliberately. "My story will be that of the fWo. This is my legacy, this is my time, and when all is said and done, my name will forever be etched into the mind of anyone who hears it. Don't seek salvation." When The Magi completed his turn around, Dragon jumped into the air, and took The Magi down with a Tornado Kick. The Magi fell to the mat, and Dragon covered, hooking the leg. At two and a half, however, Dragon released the cover as the fans stared at the two with confusion. Dragon rolled over, and whispered to The Magi: "What's happened, Jason?" Horito took a whiff of Jason's breath, and smelled Whiskey or some other cheap liquor on his tongue. Horito shook his head. "Why, Jason? Why are you drunk?" Horito asked, as Jason mumbled: "I-I-I wiiilllll beeee f-f-f-i-" trailed off Jason, as Horito responded, coldly: "Alright, time to end the match..." Dragon got up, and lifted The Magi up, as he began panting heavily, and was losing sight. Dragon measured up The Magi, and quickly swung his leg in a buzzsaw kick, connecting with The Magi's head. The Magi stood there for a second, before he collapsed to the mat, his eyes rolling into his head. Dragon shook his head, and covered loosely, as the referee quickly counted the pinfall, and 'Reclaim my Place' cued back up on the PA system. Dragon accepted the Cruiserweight title, and showed it off as most of the crowd cheered, though some disappointed fans booed at Dragon, but he shrugged it off. Horito kneeled over to Jason, and whispered to him calmly: "I hope you sober up soon, Jason. Because if you disrespect me, the fans and the ring again, you will be hoping that I hit you as stiffly as I did tonight..." Dragon got up, and rolled out of the ring, heading up the rampway, Cruiserweight championship slung over his right shoulder, and paused at the top for a few seconds, so that some fans with cameras could take photos, before he headed backstage. Meanwhile, two EMTs made their way to the ring to check out The Magi as Sunday Slaughter came to an end. Result: Great Dragon via Pinfall; Retains World Cruiserweight title
