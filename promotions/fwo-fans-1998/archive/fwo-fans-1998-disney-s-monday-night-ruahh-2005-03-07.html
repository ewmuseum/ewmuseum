Introduction Previously... With Cyberslam edging ever closer, the World Championship match between Eli Flair and Deacon continues to build in intensity... but will Flair manage to hold the belt long enough to defend it at the greatest stage of them all? Anatomy Poison Ivy and 'Triple X' Sean Stevens entering an arena together - at the same time - without an insult, tears, or a huge blowout, was a sight to see. After a year- and- a- half apart, it was something most fWo observers thought they'd never see again, mostly because of how messy everything ended, but also because most wrestling relationships were never strong to begin with. Usually, someone's lonely... in need of companionship... and, because they're so cool with the females they work with daily, it's easy to mistake fondness for love. Not these two... it was love. The worst part was, when she looked into his eyes, there was nothing that said he was kidding, nothing that said he was overexagerating. Poison Ivy believed him. Halo vs. Calm RUAHH~! headed down to the ring, where Calm was already standing, waiting for the arrival of his opponent. "Reach For The Sky" hit, bringing out the "new look" Halo, -- he sported a bright yellow faux-mohawk and wore a silver jacket and silver tights -- along with Black Quicksilver. BQ stayed a few feet behind Halo, as his protege' stood at the top of the ramp, looking around at the crowd before sprinting down the ramp and sliding into the ring. He climbed up to the second rope and played to the crowd some more, before removing his jacket and tossing it to BQ. He then hopped off the turnbuckle and turned around, circling Calm as the bell rang to start the match. The two men engaged in a collar-and-elbow tieup, which ended when the much larger Calm shoved Halo into the corner. Calm showed Halo (and everyone else) the size of his muscles instead of staying on his opponent, giving Halo the chance to get out of the corner. They circled and locked up once again, Calm shoving Halo down to the mat this time. Halo bounced up right away and kept his focus on Calm, who still wouldn't follow up on the advantage he had. Instead, Calm slapped his shoulder, telling Halo to come get him. Halo shot off the ropes and went for a shoulderblock, but the "Real" Alaskan Juggernaut didn't even budge. He told Halo to try again, so the youngster went for it. He hit the ropes and went for another shoulderblock, but Calm stepped forward, hitting Halo with a shoulderblock of his own that knocked him to the mat. Once again, Halo was quick to jump to his feet, and he wasted no time in slapping Calm across the face. Calm obviously didn't appreciate this, and when Halo motioned for *him* to go for a shoulderblock, he was all too happy to do so. Calm attempted to run past Halo and hit the ropes behind him, but Halo casually stuck his foot out. *WHAM* That was the sound of Calm hitting the mat face-first after tripping over Halo's foot. Calm got to his feet, obviously angry at the fact that Halo had just embarassed him. He charged at the cruiserweight, only to have Halo duck down and crawl between his legs, then blast him with a dropkick once he turned around. Calm stumbled back a few steps and was hit with another dropkick that sent him into the ropes. He came forward off the ropes, only to be hit with a third dropkick, this one being enough to knock him to the mat. Halo immediately dove on for the cover, but Calm threw him off after a two-count. Both men were now on their feet, and Halo charged at his large foe, only to almost have his head taken off with a clothesline, twisting around and flipping in mid-air, crashing to the mat back-first. Calm had the advantage, picking Halo up and whipping him into the ropes. He ducked down, but Halo managed to leap over him with a sunset flip attempt. Calm stood his ground, not falling backward, and figured all it would take to cut Halo's momentum off was a right hand to the face. That right hand ended up hitting nothing but canvas. Halo had moved, and waited for Calm to turn around as the big man held his hand in pain. Once Calm turned toward him, Halo hooked him in a 3/4 facelock and tried to twist him around, where he would execute an inverted layout DDT. Calm broke free, though, and managed to pick Halo up in a gorilla press. He held him up there for too long, though, as Halo slipped out of his clutches and then slid down his back, rolling him up for a two-count. Halo got up seconds before Calm, giving himself the chance to hit the ropes. He charged at a still dazed Calm and took him down with a jumping leg lariat, then quickly sprung off the top rope tailbone-first, coming down on Calm with an Arabian press. Halo summoned the strength to drag Calm near the turnbuckles, then headed out to the apron, before climbing up to the top rope. The crowd got on their feet, knowing what was next. Road To Ruin. 3-count. Halo had his hand raised in victory, and soon BQ was in the ring to congratulate his "student". The two of them noticed Matt Panzer standing at ringside, offering BQ the microphone, but the former World Champion waved his hands and shook his head, before leaving the ring with Halo. The two backed up the ramp, BQ raising Halo's arm in the air, as RUAHH~! headed elsewhere... The between reaction of the fans were expected in this match, anytime facing Eli Flair the crowd probably wasn't going to be with you. Token climbed into the ring and stepped through the ropes, he climbed the nearest turnbuckle and posed as... "The music is your special friend Dance on fire as it intends Music is your only friend Until the end. Cancel my subscription to the Resurrection, Send my credentials to the House of Detention I got some friends inside." "When The Music's Over" By The Doors struck up as Eli Flair made his way out from behind the curtain to a roaring ovation from the crowd. Flair walked down to the ring, the former Asylum World Champion, the former CSWA World Champion, the two time fWo World Champion... Legend was an understatement. The bell rang as the two men both began pacing around the ring, glaring at each other. The crowd could tell by the feeling in the air that this would be one for the ages as the two instantly went in for a lock up. Flair being the bigger of the two was able to secure control by quickly going to a side headlock. Token knew what Flair was trying to do, it was a simple task, work on Token's head and neck for his reverse DDT. Token instantly shot backwards and sent Flair flying off of the ropes. Token ducked a lariat from Flair as he came back across the ring before driving a low drop kick square into Flair's knee. Flair was caught off guard as he was already put on the ground and clutching his knee at that. See though the knee wasn't Token's goal, getting the large man on the canvas was, and with Eli's legendary chin he knew that he'd have to begin pounding on it now to succeed later. Token dove over to try to mount Flair, but Flair managed to shoot a hand up and catch Token with a fist early into the mount attempt that sent Token rolling to the ropes, both of them got to their feet quickly as the stared across the ring at each other. "Back to square one eh Flair?" Token stated with a bit of a grin as Eli looked at him and nodded. The two once again went back to pacing as the crowd began chanting... "Eli!" "Eli!" Continuing the circling Token smiled at Flair "Hey Eli, they like ya, they really like ya," with that slip up Flair managed to catch Token's arm off guard and wrap it behind him in a hammer lock. Flair used his height to his advantage and began lifting Token off the ground a bit at a time by the wrist. Flair realized Token was confused and quickly went to hit his reverse DDT, Token managed to drop down to a sitting position, pulling his head out of Eli's grasp. From there he managed to take Flair down by strategically hitting Flair behind the knee doubling him over. Flair's face hit off the mat with a thud as Token was quickly over top of him going after Flair's head and locking in a side headlock from the sitting position, using his weight to try to keep the bigger Flair down. Flair managed to get to his hands and knees when Token managed to drive a few shots into Flair's forehead to slow his movement upwards. Flair shook off the blows and continued trying to rise to the occasion but Token literally while retaining the head lock leapt into the air and pushed Flair back down to the mat. The champion was not in a very forgiving place: In the clutches of Token Weed. Token began opening up with his right hand, pounding Flair across the skull. These blows weren't exactly hurting Flair, but they were sure putting a few cob webs in his skull. Flair went to get up again, making it to his hands and knees. Token instantly went to jump in the air, and flair managed to scoop him up into his arms like a new bride. Flair instantly got to his feet... He paused for a moment before spinning 180 degrees and dropping Token with a side slam. The canvas shook from the impact as The Eliminator reached down and hooked the far leg. 1... At one and a half Token kicked out, as Flair was quickly back to the attack as he brought Token up by his hair. Flair quickly put Token into a front face lock and went for a vertical suplex, but Token managed to block by wrapping his foot around Flair's leg. Flair threw a couple of punches into Token's ribs hoping that would let him pull Token up, but once again Token managed to block. Flair went to hit Token in the ribs again, and the former Asylum champion managed to pull his head out of the front face lock and drilled Flair in the stomach with the toe of his boot doubling the champion over. Token put Flair between his legs and went to bring him up for a piledriver, but Flair kept himself firmly planted to the ground and managed to bring Token up into the air and drop him with a back body drop. Flair reached down and grabbed his stomach as he went over to bring Token up again. Token wasn't exactly a big fan of this entire hair pulling thing as he shot a right hand into the mouth of the World Heavyweight Champion sending him stumbling. Token isn't exactly a guy to let another go stumbling away as he fired a left jab into Flair's mouth again. He threw another right hand into Flair's mouth that sent him hurtling back into the corner, Token smiled. Right where he wanted Flair. He sent Flair for an irish whip into the far corner, Flair bounced off the turnbuckles and came back at Token. Token exploded into the air going for a Pump Kick, but Flair managed to duck the move, as Token managed to land on his feet. Token spun around and was met with a huge right hand from Flair that sent the crowd into a frenzy. Token fired back with a right of his own, the two seemingly finding a common ground with brawling. Flair fired a left which Token managed to block as he sent an inside crescent kick to the mouth of Eli Flair. Flair stumbled back a bit of blood trickling from the left side of his mouth. Token took a step forward and planted The Eliminator with an outside crescent kick that managed to double Flair over. Token grinned as he leapt into the air and planted the back of the Champions head with an Axe kick that floored Flair. Token went for a quick pin, hooking one of Eli's massive legs... 1... 2... Flair kicked out hard as Token figured he would. Token instantly went back to work on Flair's head locking him in a head scissors. Making it a point to manage to bar Flair's arm across his stomach and hooking it into place with the other one. Flair began shouting in pain as he began struggling to find the ropes, once again he had let himself open to getting into the clutches of Token. Flair struggled, using his legs to shimmy (yes shimmy) himself over near the ropes, Token was quick to put the breaks on and quickly scurry with Flair back towards the middle of the ring. Token reared back hard on the modified arm bar/head scissors combo as Flair once again let out a scream of anguish. The fans managed to slowly get behind their anti-hero as once again he made his way closer to the ropes. "Eli!" "Eli!" "RUAHHHHHHHHH~!" The roar of the crowd caused Token to sigh, he knew Flair had managed to get a grip on the ropes with his other arm. Token being smart kept the hold locked on until the ref told him to let go, Token backed up for what seemed like a clean break... The guy who never lets up... Clean break? Please. Flair rolled himself outside of the ring to compose himself as Token slid out baseball style and managed to crack The Original Nobody right in his injured arm, Token continued on to the outside of the ring with Flair leaning up against the railing. Token went to continue his assault as Flair spun around and drilled him with a right hand jarring his arm. Causing Flair to wince in pain, Flair shot a left hand forward this time smacking Token again across the nose sending a trickle of blood flowing from Token's nose. 3... Was the count from the ref as Flair continued his offensive on the outside as he charged forward and sent a big boot across Token's face, slamming his head backwards onto the apron. 5... Flair was satisfied as he delivered a few sharp kicks to Token's mid-section and rolled into the ring. 7... Token was back up and managed to roll into the ring. Flair was instantly bringing the former Asylum champion to his feet. He front face locked Token and dropped backwards planting him with a hard DDT. Flair smiled as he made the cover, hooking the far leg and pushing down with his forearm across Token's chest. 1... 2... KICKOUT~! Flair seemed almost shocked as Weed was still on the ground in a daze, he brought Token to his feet and sent him whipping into the ropes, Flair went for another big boot that Token managed to counter this time with another hard drop kick into Flair's knee. Flair shot forwards landing on his face as both men were down... The fans began to chant once again. It started softly at first. "eli!" "Eli!" "ELi!" "ELI!" the crowd roared as both men got to their feet, this was time for once again another war. Token came forward and smacked Eli across the nose with a half haymaker shot, Eli stumbled backwards and then came back forcing Token to eat a hard left hand from him. Token bent down from the move and force fed the champion a throat punch that dropped Flair to his knees. Token smiled as he unloaded with low kicks smacking Flair in the head, Token then took a step back and planted Flair across the bridge of the nose with a stepping side kick that sent a steady trickle of blood down Flair's nostrils before toppling the champion over. Token was about to go for a cover when he saw red hair flying towards the ring from the crowd... Kinkade made his way down to ringside with a chair, hopping over the guard rail and sliding into the ring... right? Wrong, Kinkade plopped the chair down right between the guard rail and the ring. Kinkade began smiling as Token felt something grab him by his hair... BAM~! The collision on the mat was thunderous as Eli Flair dropped Token Weed with The Fallen One. Flair managed to reach over and drape his body across Weed for a pin. 1... 2... It's all elementary my dear Watson. .... 3~! Result: Eli Flair retains with The Fallen One for a pin
