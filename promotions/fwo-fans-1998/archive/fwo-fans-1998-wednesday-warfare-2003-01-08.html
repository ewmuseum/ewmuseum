Introduction Previously... On the final show of the year old grudges were finally settled, new rivalries were born, and the world watched in shock as the unthinkable took place. But due to political manuvering from old enemies of the fWo, the promotion has been caught in a legal trap that has forced the once great promotion from performing on their home soil. Banished from the United States, the fWo must go abroad. Reeling from the sudden shifts of venue, organizers struggle to set up alternate arenas for an unknown amount of time. Continued interest in the fWo is also unknown... without the ability to run shows in their home market, it is uncertain how long American television companies will continue to run their shows, or how long their home market will continue to care about a promotion they are no longer able to experience live... Once again And so they went onward. Light snow fell from a quickly darkening sky. The air was sharp, the chill cutting through the thin coats and jackets easily as the small group stood huddled together against the cold. There was no sound in the air, nor any movement beyond the occasional bird soaring lightly through the afternoon sky. The casket was lowered slowly into the ground and made a hollow thump against earth and stone. There was audible emotion from the crowd, simply the continued silence. The day was short against the looming darkness, and as the first shovel of dirt was pitched into the grave the crowd slowly began to disperse. "We've got a plane to catch." Silver Fox nodded and turned to face Stevie Roberts. His eyes were dark; all of their eyes were dark. They were a small collection of his friends and companions in the wrestling organization they called home. The older crowd... these days, always the older crowd came to these functions. Chameleon, Nodar, Primetime, Ultra Violet... others who were active in the fWo, and of course the staff. It was always the staff that had to come to these things. Roberts looked down at the grave and shook his head. "Feels strange, doing this again." "It does," Kid Cool agreed. "...but count me out of the reading of the will this time. Just my luck I'd find out I had a long lost sister. Bet it would be Nightshade." Silver Fox grinned, but it felt false on his face. "I just don't understand how this could have happened... how all of this happened. We were just getting to know him again. Now, we're on our own." Terry Nodar coughed. "What's going to happen to the fed?" Silver Fox shrugged. "We don't know... other than this eventuality was planned for. The promotion will keep running, and the way the bylaws are set up we aren't going to have some evil owner grabbing control of it. But this thing with Tort... this thing with pushing us out of the country..." "I'm sorry..." Terry slumped his shoulders. "I feel responsible... it's us he really wants to get back at. You're just the one he can get his hands on. But what will happen to the others?" Silver Fox shook his head. "They were after us. Sure, they could go after the Asylum... but somehow I doubt it. This was personal, and it served it's purpose." "It's not all lost," said Stephen Roberts. "You've got friends by your side. I'm here to help out... full time." Terry shook his head in agreement. "And I'll always do what I can. You know that." Silver Fox nodded, but his eyes were closed and all he could feel was the cold of the winter air. "It still feels like we're running." "That's because we are." Kid Cool walked over and dropped his flowers on the ground in front of the tombstone that bore the name of his father. The others walked to the series of cars that would take them to the airport and out of the country... for how long they didn't know. Kid Cool lingered behind, touching the headstone with fingertips that had gone numb with the chill. He sighed, and closed his eyes. "Goodbye old man... hope you're doing better than we are... wherever you may be." The Arrival As they walked through the doors of the General Motors Palace in Vancouver, fWo Hardcore Champion Eli Flair and his traveling companion for the evening, Lance Knight, couldn't help but feel as though they had arrived. In spite of the lingering pain from Countdown, in spite of being essentially exiled from their home country, both men had set out to do something upon returning to the fWo, and just hours before the year had ended, both had achieved their goals. They had traveled together almost exclusively since Countdown and had sat in together on the meeting earlier in the day, in which Silver Fox and Samuel Smooth tried to explain to a largely bewildered roster that they had absolutely no idea when they'd be able to return to the United States for tours. While such a realization was certainly a chaser of reality that cut into the drunken feeling of accomplishment that came from Countdown, both Flair and Knight were excited to be starting the New Year in the fWo on the right foot. In fact, as "Total Elimination" looked at his younger companion, he was relieved to see that Lance looked much less neurotic than he had in a little over two months, when they had first started traveling together again. "Saddle up, kid! 2003 gonna be the year that Lance Knight wrote?" Eli asked, but his mind wasn't on Knight so much as it was his pregnant wife, who was probably sitting in her tour bus right now, preparing for the night's gig. Lance could see that his friend was preoccupied, but he answered him anyway. "Yeah, EJ, I'm anxious to see what Fox and Smooth have in mind for me. They wanted me to come see them during the first dark match tonight, so hopefully I'll have some idea of what's going on then." They walked along in silence for a couple of paces, but then Knight turned and looked at the giant of a man, his body a mass of muscle and scars and tattoos. He was huge, of that there was no doubt, but Lance was equally sure that Elijah Flair's heart was every bit as big as his body. In the ring and on-camera, he could be downright monstrous, but if you were one of Eli's friends, you knew he'd go to the wall for you, and Lance had to admit, that was a great feeling. Motivated by this knowledge, Lance did his best to put Eli at ease. "Hey, Eli, she's gonna be fine. I can't even imagine what it'd be like, worrying about your kid being born on stage in a club somewhere, but then again, look at the parents you've gotta figure something interesting is gonna happen." Eli smiled, recognizing his friend's attempt at humor. In April, a chance encounter had led to these two men becoming friends, and there wasn't a time since that Eli hadn't believed that he made the right decision in dragging Lance to the card game that he was playing with Sean Stevens and Scott Wasner on that evening. Chuckling to himself at the remembrance of how awestruck Lance had been, and how Wasner, better known to fWo fans as Scott Slugger, had proceeded to drain Knight's wallet of the bonus he had collected from OSW for working a match for them earlier in the night. "The kid's definitely in for an interesting life...but that's one scenario I'm not looking forward to. But it's a better one than her being back in the states outta reach." And now Eli's smile was a little broader, as he thought about how happy he'd been with Angel, especially since their commitment ceremony, less than an hour before the year 2002 had ended. Momentarily distracted by his own thoughts, Flair failed to realize that Lance Knight was doing some heavy thinking of his own. And Knight's wasn't nearly as pleasant, as the memory of "Triple X" Sean Stevens' comments to him echoed through his head. Grabbing Flair's forearm, Knight stopped and looked up into his friend's face, the urgency and need plainly visible on his visage. "Ivy told me he was going to apologize, Eli," Knight started, his voice wavering slightly, "but all he did was shift the blame off onto me." Result: Jon Crisp & Black Quicksilver The Meeting Lance knew that he was a couple of minutes early, but he wanted to make a good impression for his first official meeting with the road crew. He had gone before them a little more than a week earlier to set up his matches with Triple X at Countdown, but this time, things were different. They had requested to see him, and Lance was fairly certain that it was in regards to the direction they'd like him to go in for the first part of the New Year. And while he was unsure exactly how much of a role the four men were playing in booking decisions, they were still his bosses, and he was more than willing to listen to whatever ideas they had for him. Knocking confidently on the door, Lance entered the makeshift office they had assembled in one of the arena's conference rooms. Inside sat the four members of the road crew: Silver Fox, Samuel Smooth, Twitch, and Thumper. Lance remained standing just inside the door out of respect for the four men and their achievements, but Fox quickly put an end to that, beckoning him towards one of the two empty chairs opposite Twitch and Thumper at the long conference table. With Fox to his left, Smooth to his right, and the men in charge of the tag division directly in front of him, Lance was sure that the meeting was set to begin, but Smooth cleared his throat and addressed the superstar, informing him otherwise. "We've asked Sean to join this meeting as well, so we'll begin as soon as he gets here." Too bad it won't have the emotion or the intensity that it had before, now that I know Sean does respect me." Moments later, another knock came at the door, and Sean Stevens was called into the room. Triple X was dressed to wrestle, his black leather pants gleaming under the fluorescent lights of the office, the blue accents on them nearly lost in the shine.
