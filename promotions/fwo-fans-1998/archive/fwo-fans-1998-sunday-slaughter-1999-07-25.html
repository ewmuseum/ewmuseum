Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Sunday Slaughter opened on Chris Finn, sitting deep within the fWo Towers in Redmond Washington. Around Finn were piles of tapes, film reels, and still photos hung on the wall. Chris Finn said, "Welcome, fWo fans. The fWo has been all around the world in the fifteen months that it has been in existance... putting on shows all around the world, and entertaining millions of fans. There are many wrestling promotions in the world, but only one fWo. It's something special that the workers give the fans in every show... that extra bit of effort, that showmanship, that special extra something that goes out to the people that are entertained by what we do. Tonight is a special night, as we show you a year's worth of Sunday Slaughter, and fWo memories. We hope that you enjoy these matches... and these comments from the wrestlers that entertained the world." With that, the lights dimmed, and the first match began. Dropping the useless saw, Crucifix attacked with a series of kicks to the face of Slugger, who was still dazed and bleeding profusely from the skull. Fans looked up in confusion, and to their astonishment rain began to fall from the rafters. Tired and confused by what this meant, Scott Slugger limped from the arena.
