fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE TRUST NO ONE August 7, 2000 Desperation was the theme of fWo Survivor Round Eight. Desperation because with only five men remaining in the contest and two weeks of proof that you can't trust anyone, wrestlers are more edgy and nervous than ever. Going into round eight and the latest immunity challenge, everyone wanted the prize that only one man could have. Today, that man was Kodiak Vic Creed. The battle royal event was perfectly suited for the large man, a fact that many wrestlers knew going into the last voting round. Several wrestlers were trying to preempt Creed getting an immunity chance by voting him out prior to the battle royal, but in the end fell short by one vote... and one vote was all it took for Nick Allstar to be eliminated, and the road cleared for Kodiak Vic Creed's win in round eight. "We knew that some wrestlers would have strengths that others didn't. Obviously big men are going to have an advantage in a battle royal." Stevie Roberts explained to a group of reporters following the event, "We structured the contest so that everyone would have at least one or two matches that they would do well in. Tag matches, fast paced matches, gauntlet matches... those all test different skills, like stamina, agility, or technical skill. While Creed won today, he's going to have a tough time with the remaining three events... because they are all suited toward other types of wrestlers." Now the voting begins once more, but now it's getting close to the end. The wrestlers will only vote three more times, and either Arch Angel, Suicide, Reno Davis, Ruben Ross or Kodiak Vic Creed will survive. One wrestler commented on his thoughts going into the voting. "A few days ago there was some... agreements. Yeah agreements, I'm not afraid to admit it. It felt safe then. But now I don't know whose side anyone is on... and so I could be going out tomorrow." Another wrestler shared similar feelings. "I think that there are only two people in this contest, friends and enemies. You think someone is one of those... then in a moments notice they are on the other side." More news as it comes available.
