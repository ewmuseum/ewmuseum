Silver Fox beamed and looked around the locker room with an expression of pride. Of happiness. There they all were, the wrestlers who knew him as their leader, their manager... arguably the most positive and favorable general manager they had ever known. It had been when he went to the hospital, when he was put into a coma through the actions of Ray Chavez that it had fallen apart. They were part of Disney then... but with Silver Fox back, surely no longer. Silver Fox patted Ted Tallison, the man who had most missed him, who had been lost without his guidance, on the back. "It's great to be back Ted... it's great to be back. Now, now and most importantly... I have some business with a Mr. Murphy. I'm going to get things right, get things the way they should be... and I think all of you know what that means. What it will mean for all of us, and for the future of this promotion." There were cheers in the room, and why shouldn't there be? They knew what the stipulations were after all... Silver Fox was named controller of the fWo by the old board. Something that Mr. Tort had done right after all of these years. His being in a coma left control over to the biggest investor... in this case, the Walt Disney Corporation. But now he was free from his coma, back in charge, back in action, and certainly the mechanations of Disney were about to come to an end. Silver Fox waved goodbye to them and headed down the hallway, still smiling. His meeting with Mr. Murphy was now, and it was time to get some paperwork signed and put to rest once and for all. As he watched him go, Ted Tallison frowned, ever so slightly, and ever so briefly. Silver Fox was dressed in a crisp, green suit that bothered Tallison in a way he didn't understand. It had to be his nerves telling him that something just wasn't right... it had to be. The green suit vanished into the darkness... and Tallison waited. Waited for resolution, waited for the hope he hoped for. Ted Tallison hoped his nervousness was just normal anxiety... and thought dark, green, thoughts. Start Getting Serious!
