Sunday Slaughter, 10 December 2000

10 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Sunday Night Slaughter began innocently enough. Fans waited inside the arena in anticipation of the final show before the last pay-per-view of the year. Outside the arena however, a rather cold Raleigh braced itself for the arrival of the Dark Riders. The Riders arrived at the arena, and as the camera crew caught up with the trio that comprised the Riders (War, Plague & Stone) - Stone was audibly heard saying that tonight was the night they took care of their "obstacle", and they were going to do so immediately. Time, afterall was money, and not to be wasted. The camera crew stopped and the camera only caught the backsides of Stone, War & Plague as they were now deep inside the arena. Meanwhile, the cameras looked elsewhere to find Chris Universal standing nearby looking quite anxious. Cameras tried to get a word with him, but he refused comment, only saying that he was waiting for his former valet and ex-girlfriend Megan Dawn to arrive. We then were quickly taken to the opening Slaughter montage... 'Black Plague' Ruben Ross (c) vs. 'The Ego' Greg Davis vs. Juno Law Slaughter kicked off the way it usually does, with fireworks screeching throughout the Raleigh Sports & Entertainment Arena. Fans cheered wildly, before their cheers turned to boos. Result: Law by pinfall Obstacle Clarisse walked down the halls of the facility, looking for her locker room. A nearby security guard guided her properly, and she thanked him before entering the locker room. Cody was nowhere to be found, and Clarisse presumed he must have been running a few minutes late. As she fixed her hair in front of the mirror, a knock was heard at the door. Crusader vs. Plague Plague continued beating away on Crusader in the backstage area, who took a hard shot to the head from the vase. The two finally reached the entrance, and appeared at the top of the ramp, as fans began cheering for the action in front of them. Plague continued to dominate, running forward with a headlock on Crusader down the ramp and hitting a hard bulldog. Plague picked up the former two-time Cruiserweight Champion, and dropped him sternum-first across the guardrail. The punishment was furthered with a whip into the steel steps by Plague. A referee finally came down to restore some order, and called for the bell, starting the unscheduled match. Plague brought Crusader up by the arm, and whipped him into the steel guardrail. Crusader arched his back in pain, as Plague charged in with a knee-lift to the gut. The ref ordered Plague to get the match back into the ring, but the Dark Rider did things his own way, picking up Crusader and ramming him spine-first into the ring post. Plague rolled Crusader back into the ring (finally) and sprung himself in with a springboard legdrop to the back of Crusader Result: Plague by pinfall Request Slaughter returned from the commercial break; where backstage, 'The Ego' Greg Davis was seen walking down a hallway holding his head. Obviously, he was still suffering the affects of his previous three-way dance with Juno Law & Ruben Ross. Nonetheless, he turned a corner, and knocked on the door of the acting owner of the fWo, James Armstrong. Nobody answered, so Davis took it upon himself to enter the room without being welcomed in. Armstrong was seen talking on the phone. He saw Davis, and normally, he wouldn't be mad, but it seemed as if this was a very important phone call. "I... look Vince, I'll call you back. Someone's here. Yeah. Uh huh. Tomorrrow. Alright. Bye bye now." Armstrong hung the phone, and looked at 'The Ego' increduously. "Greg, what in Ahmad Rashad's name do you want?" Davis smiled. "Look, boss... all I want is a shot at Law at Twilight for the Cruiserweight Title at Twilight. Is that so much to ask?" Armstrong sighed. "That's all you wanted?" Davis nodded. "That's the only reason you came in here and interrupted my very important phone call?" Davis again, nodded. "Dammit, I thought you wanted me to set you up with a game of blackjack... or hookers. Eh, consider it done." Greg smiled. "This is just too perfect. Thanks, James. I'll make sure you don't regret this decision..." Davis gave a corny thumbs up sign to Armstrong, who just sighed in disbelief as Davis exited the room. Armstrong then appeared to have a lightbulb go off in his head, and called Davis back into the room. "Yeah?" Armstrong then asked Davis if he saw Wretch or Bear later, to tell them that he wanted them both in his office at some point by the end of the night. Davis gave another corny thumbs up, and this time, left the room completely... Sphere vs. Vengence Iron Maiden allows for the entrance of Sphere, for this hardcore contender match. He walks down towards the ring with various objects, including a broom, a trash can, and a deck of cards...you know, your typical set of hardcore weapons. ELO hits the arena speakers, and Vengence hits the entrance ramp, driving a forklift, and hauling various objects to the ring. Among the objects on the forklift are several garbage cans, a weed wacker, a vacuum (the big ones, with the hoses), and a wooden rocking horse. Vengence stops just short of the ring, and begins to lift the stuff above the ropes. Sphere, standing near the ropes, wonders what he is doing. All of a sudden, Vengence drops the stuff on top of Sphere, who gets lost under the debris. Vengence points and laughs at Sphere, as he climbs into the ring. He looks down, and begins to sift through the rubble. And then, something odd happens. A spoon flies into his mouth, attached to an arm. Sphere is trying something he heard some guy from a northeast indy fed say: He's trying to gag Vengence with a spoon. Vengence tries to get away, but he cannot. Sphere has that mandible spoon hooked in rather snugly. Then, all of a sudden, Vengence falls over the ropes, and onto the forklift. Sphere, achieving what he wanted, quickly gets out of the ring, and hops into the forklift. However, Sphere, not quite sure how to drive this thing, runs into the ring. He then realizes how to backup; however, he goes too fast, and begins driving backwards into the crowd. Dozens of screaming fans are trying to take cover from this out-of-control fork lift. Sphere finally gets control of it, and begins to drive forward. However, not knowing how to brake, he drives onto the other side of the entrance ramp, and into the crowd once again. "Sorry!" can be heard from Sphere as fans begin screaming once again. Meanwhile, Vengence has gotten up, and, despite being a bit afraid for his well-being, he begins to climb towards Sphere. This causes Sphere to not know where he's going, which causes him to drive all over the place. Vengence gets directly in front of Sphere. "Move out of the way!," Sphere yells at him. "Stop driving, you lunatic!", replies Vengence, before decking him in the head. Sphere falls out of the forklift, and Vengence, once the forklift stops, hops down, and begins to brawl with him, right back to the ring. Vengence clubs Sphere with his fist, which, of course, has the key to the forklift in it. Sphere gets cut open a bit by the key over his forehead, and the fans begin to cheer, as they get to see blood. Vengence tosses him into the ring, and he climbs in after him. He nails him with a garbage can, and then goes for a cover. At two, Sphere kicks out. Vengence looks around, and grabs the rocking horse. He picks up Sphere, and places him in the horse. He pushes him a bit, as if he's rocking him...as Sphere is rocking, he gets a rather content look on his face. Then, out of nowhere, Vengence nails him with a broom. Sphere, looking rather pissed, calmly takes one foot off the horse. Then the second. Vengence, getting a bit tired of his antics, throws his arms down in disgust. It is right then Sphere, who never took his HANDS off the horse, turns and swings with such force into the body and head of Vengence that the horse destroys upon contact. Vengence hits the mat, obviously out. Sphere covers him, and the ref counts. However, Sphere, using the line of logic that he alone can't cover the mighty Vengence, decides that he needs some added weight on top of Vengence. So, to achieve this, he grabs one trash can, and places that on Vengence. Then he grabs a second, and places that on Vengence (all are bottom up). He then sits on top of the second one, and the ref makes the count. However, his patience and craftsmanship are all for naught, as Vengence kicks out, causing Sphere to fall off of the trash cans. Vengence gets up, and picks up the weed wacker. He proceeds to move upon Sphere when someone comes out of the crowd. Make that two people, from opposite ends, converging on the same spot. Carnage. Erik Kelly. Each with some form of weaponry...Carnage a chainsaw, Kelly a blow torch. They get into the ring area. The ref, seeing this, immediately calls for not only the bell, but security. Carnage revs up the saw, while Kelly ignites the flame, while security comes down and gets involved. Vengence still has the weed wacker, while Sphere has grabbed the horse head (and the key for another fork lift ride), and all four men glare at each other, while Slaughter moves on.... Result: No Contest Another Arrival Slaughter catches up backstage again with Chris Universal, and finally, it appears as if Megan Dawn has arrived. A long, white limosuine pulls up, and 'The Sellout' opens the door. Dawn steps out, and the two briefly embrace. "Been awhile since we hugged like this." It seems as if the two are deep in thoughts of memories past, but they quickly begin talking about other matters. "Lance is here. I just need you to talk to him. Find out where his head is out. Tell him that wrestling me won't solve anything. Tell him I don't want to wrestle him. Please, Megan." Megan nodded. "I said if there was anything I could do I'd do it, and now I'm going to." Universal thanked his old flame as the two walked into the arena, while Slaughter headed back to the ring. 'Mr. Main Event' Mitch Wilson (c) vs. Jackhammer Dust for Life hits the speakers, bringing out Jackhammer for this International Championship match. He comes out to cheers from the crowd, to which he replies in kind, and steps in the ring. Faith No More then hits, and Mitch Wilson, the fWo International Champion, makes his way out to a chorus of boos from the fans. He doesn't really care, as he strides his way down towards the ring. The new leader of the A.S.S. team slides in the ring, and charges Jackhammer. However, Jackhammer, appearing to have done his homework, senses that this would have happened. He lowers his body, and backdrops Wilson onto the mat. He lets his momentum continue into the ropes. He bounces off, and hits a splash onto the gut of Wilson, which immediately turns into a count. One. Two. Wilson kicks out. Jackhammer picks Wilson up; Wilson seemingly loses control of his head, and it inexplicably goes flying into the crotch of Jackhmmaer, causing him to bend over. Wilson gets up to one knee, and DDTs Jackhammer to the mat. A cover by Wilson brings a two, before Jackhammer can kick out. Wilson picks up Jackhammer, and whips him into the ropes. Jackhammer bounces off the ropes and collides with Wilson. However, Jackhammer stays on his feet, and it is Wilson who falls. Jackhammer takes off for the ropes again. Wilson looks like he will get up; however, he stays down on his stomach. This apparently is what Jackhammer wanted. He stops short of hopping over Wilson, and, instead, kneels down. He grabs the head of Wilson and thrusts it upwards. He then locks him in a camel clutch, something unaccustomed to out of this combination. The fans cheer in delight at the surprising move, and the ref asks Wilson if he will tap. He tells the ref what he can do with his tap, and doesn't give up. One minute goes by, and Wilson hasn't tapped. Jackhammer, knowing that this wouldn't go anywhere soon, gets to his feet, and lifts Wilson up while still having it locked in. However, as Wilson begins to get to his feet, he quickly sits down, causing Jackhammer to go flying with him. Wilson nails the jawbreaker on Jackhammer, causing him to fly back. Wilson quickly gets up and grabs the legs of Jackhammer, and puts him into a figure-four. Jackhammer lifts up in pain, but won't give. After roughly thirty seconds, Wilson suddenly lifts up with his hands, causing more pressure on Jackhammer, and, even more important, his shoulders to hit the mat. The ref counts; one....two....and then, Jackhammer busts out a shoulder. Not only that, but he gets momentum, and causes Wilson to fall down on his back, knocking the wind out of him. The ref begins to count his pinned shoulders. At two, Wilson suddenly realizes what's going on and gets a shoulder up. However, Jackhammer has already begun to reverse the submission hold. Wilson, not wanting this to happen, throws up an arm, a la some old guy with saggy breasts from Atlanta. Jackhammer, however, completes the turnaround, and Wilson is left feeling the pain. After ten seconds, he breaks the hold, and heads toward a corner. He gets his senses back, turns around, and is immediately met by a charging Jackhammer. Jackhammer nails him in the corner, and Wilson stumbles out. Jackhammer grabs him, and sets him up for The Owie. The fans rise to their feet. If he nails this, it's out. He makes sure he's set, and lifts him up. He takes flight....and, seemingly out of nowhere, he flips over. Wilson catches his head with his legs and nails a hurricanrana on Jackhammer. The fans, rather amazed-although-pissed, begin to boo loudly. Wilson quickly makes a cover on Jackhammer. One. Two. ThreNo. No dice for Wilson, as Jackhammer barely got a shoulder up. Wilson, rather pissed, signals for the Headliner. He sets Jackhammer up, and nails it, as Hammer is still a bit groggy. He makes the cover, and three seconds later, Mitch Wilson has retained the International Championship. Wilson, looking rather shaky at first, then confident as he still is champ, grabs the belt and holds it high in the air, as Slaughter rolls on.... Result: Wilson by pinfall Rematch? Backstage, cameras take us right into the eye of the storm, as Crusader & Plague are engaged in an arguement. Tons of security members struggle to hold the two men away from each other, while Stone stands idly by with a smile on her face. The two continue to talk trash about one another, with Plague mumbling something about "bITchES" once again, and Crusader calling Plague nothing more than Scott Collins "bag carrier". Stone seems unfazed by the comment as does Plague. Eventually the two seem to come to an agreement that a rematch will be set in stone for Twilight... Result: Damnation by pinfall Making Peace The fWo's only television show returned from a commercial break to find Megan Dawn already standing in the ring. It seems as if the crowd remembers her despite the fact she hasn't been on television in quite some time. We catch the trail end of a favorable reaction to her, before Dawn addresses the crowd. "I'm here tonight to do a favor" Dawn says, brushing her blondish/brown hair to hair to the side, revealing a chest full of ample cleveage, which is covered in spots by her somewhat revealing outfit. "A favor for Chris Universal. I don't know what the deal with Lance Star is. But Lance... you need to realize that Chris is your friend, and last Sunday, when he successfully prevented you from becoming permanently disabled at the hands of Primetime, he was doing you a favor. We both know and realize that you're a grown man and you can take care of yourself, but sometimes, everyone needs a little help from their friends. And Chris is your friend - not your enemy. Primetime - Primetime is the enemy. Not me, not Chris... and not these fans here in Raleigh, North Carolina!" Longtime fWo fans noted that Universal had a favorable working relationship with the wwf's Mick Foley, who was an acquaintance of Travis Beaven and worked for Beaven while he was the owner of the fWo - and obviously, Dawn herself had learned something from Foley as well: How to draw a cheap pop from the crowd. "Or anyone else, for that matter. Lance... would you please come out here, right now, so we can talk about this? Please? If you won't listen to Chris - at least... listen to me?" The crowd looked immediately to the stage as 'My Generation' by Limp Bizkit began to play, which brought out 'The Future' Lance Star. Star looked at the crowd who responded somewhat negatively to his presence... but Star ignored it, as evident by the smug expression on his face. Star reached the ring and climbed in apprehensively, as he was obviously unsure about Dawn. Dawn & Star were friends as well, perhaps because they shared a mutual friend in the case of Universal. But as we learned this previous week, it had been sometime since Star & Dawn had spoken. Star stood across from Dawn, as Dawn thanked him for coming down. Star seemed unfazed by what she said, and Dawn struggled to recover as to not be embarassed by Lance' lack of response. "Anyways... Lance, Chris is your friend. Chris is not trying to be your big brother - he never was. Friends help friends, that's just the way it is. Lance, Chris was just trying to help you, much like he did last year when you wear nearly paralyzed. Don't you remember that? Either way, Chris succeeded. You weren't seriously injured by Primetime last Sunday, were you? And you couldn't even thank him for that. But that's not the important issue - Lance, Chris does not want to fight you. Can't you see that? He wants to be your friend. He's sorry about not calling you, but can't you realize that his father was dying? Can't you realize that he was on his death bed, and Chris had to take care of his father?" Lance still seemed unfazed by what Megan Dawn was saying, and continued to have a lifeless expression on his face. Dawn continued. "Lance, please... talk to Chris, straight this matter out. You both need to talk this situation out, and apologize, and bury any hurt feelings between the two of you. Please. I care about you both." Dawn handed Lance the microphone, and without hesitation, Lance began talking. Coldly, he looked Dawn right in the eyes. "Are you done yet? Good. Because I'm tired of you rambling. Let me ask you something, Megan. Don't you realize that Chris Universal is, has been, and always will be about himself? When you wanted to spend time with him - what was he out doing? Wrestling. His work life engulfed him. But you were fortunate in that you could travel on the road with him, and even then, he didn't pay attention to you. He went to fancy restaraunts, he went to lavish hotels, he slept around with other women..." The crowd "oohed" at this, but it didn't seem as if they believed Lance. "He treated people like crap. And all the while they bought his sincerity, they bought that he was trying to help me, that he cared about me. If he cared about me, why did he not pick up that phone after he left the fWo back in March?" Dawn seemed ready to answer, but Lance interrupted her. "I'm not finished yet." The crowd again "oohed". "Now, something tells me, that just like he does, YOU need to stay out of my business. Am I clear here, Megan?" It appeared as if Dawn was taken aback by Lance' heartless attitude, but nodded slightly. "Good. Now if I may be serious for a moment - you look like a slut. How 'bout having that rich boy shell out some dough and arrange for you to have a makeover?" The crowd hissed and booed at this, as Star continued to stare at Dawn coldly. Dawn had enough of his attitude, and slapped him. Lance held his face, before grabbing a hold of Dawn' shirt. "You tell Chris that come next Saturday, I own him." Lance pushed Dawn backwards and she stumbled to the mat. The crowd booed again, and Lance just laughed off the comments, staring down at the fallen Dawn with a callous snarl. Slaughter cut to a commercial break, with the crowd continuing to show their hate for Star. 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly vs. 'Kodiak' Vic Creed "Walk" starts to play, and the fans immediately register their displeasure as the massive Kodiak Vic Creed steps onto the entrance ramp from backstage. His muscles rippling and his face grinning, Creed slowly strolls to the ring, basking in the boos. He steps into the ring and awaits his opponent. A mighty yell of "POWERBOMB!" is heard, "Break Stuff" hits, and the crowd goes nuts as the mammoth Eradicator, Erik Kelly makes his way to the ring. He is accompanied by Pamela Dee, who takes her spot at ringside as Kelly steps over the top rope and into the ring. He stares down Creed, who returns a glare of his own. The ref signals for the bell, and the two bulls immediately charge each other. Neither gives an inch. Kelly runs into the ropes and shoulderblocks Creed, who uses the momentum of the impact to propel himself backward into the ropes. He then comes off and blasts Kelly with a shoulderblock of his own, knocking Kelly into the ropes. Kelly bounces off the ropes and catches Creed off-guard with a huge boot to the head. Creed goes down, and Kelly mounts him and begins hammering away. He finishes his attack and brings Creed up. He then whips him into the ropes. As Creed comes off, Kelly grabs him, lifts him, and hurls him to the mat with a spinebuster variation. Kelly drops down and makes the cover, but it only gets two. He brings Creed back up and whips him into the corner, before following him in. Creed lifts a boot high and catches Kelly square on the jaw, causing him to stagger out. Creed then mounts the second turnbuckle and flies out of the corner with a bulldog, taking the larger man down. He covers, but only gets two. Creed brings Kelly back up and hooks him in a belly-to-belly suplex position. He tries to take him over, but cannot do it on the first try. On the second, he gets the mastodonic Kelly up and over his head with an overhead belly-to-belly suplex in an amazing feat of strength. Creed floats over into a cover and the ref makes the count. Kelly manages to get a shoulder up at two. Creed is undaunted and brings the larger man back up to his feet. He whips him into the ropes and catches him with a thunderous lariat. To Creed's shock, this only staggers the big man. Creed then runs into the ropes himself, leaps, and nails Kelly with a flying lariat that hits him square on the throat. Kelly is dropped, and Creed covers again. Again, it only gets two. Creed, all business, brings Kelly right back up and whips him into the corner. He follows him in with a clothesline, then begins choking Kelly with his boot. Creed finally releases it and Kelly stumbles out of the corner onto his knees. Creed takes the initiative and mounts Kelly in a camel clutch, wrapping his humongous forearms around the neck and throat area of Kelly and pulling back with tremendous force. Kelly is in major pain, and Creed smirks as he inflicts it. After a good twenty to thirty seconds in the hold, Kelly begins to slump. Creed still holds him up, but it's clear he's losing it. The ref takes an arm and lifts it. It drops. He lifts it again. It drops again. He lifts it a third time... and somehow, some way, Kelly's arm stays up. Kelly begins struggling, the veins on his neck popping out, and finally manages to lift Creed up and climb to his feat in an incredible show of raw, brute power. He takes a few stumbling steps towards the center of the ring, then runs backwards into a corner like a locomotive in reverse, smashing Creed between the turnbuckles and Kelly's own monstrous bulk. Creed slumps down to a sitting position, but Kelly, getting a second wind, brings him right back up. He whips Creed into the ropes.
