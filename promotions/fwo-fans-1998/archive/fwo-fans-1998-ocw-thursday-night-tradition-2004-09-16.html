OCW Thursday Night Tradition, 16 September 2004

Introduction Last week, Thursday Night Tradition began OCW's future as a promotion in the Pro Wrestling industry. Although there were some problems during the course of last week's show, the management hopes this week to rectify the problems that occured last week, although only time will tell how successful they will be. A not-so morale boosting meeting... Brian Cage shut his eyes, and took a deep breath. A good chunk of the roster sat in front of him, and he wasn't pleased. He had gone over the debut show tape, and had selected the roster members which, in his eyes, had embarassed the OCW during what should have been a glorious debut show. Brian intended to chew these poor souls on this. "Alright, do any of you have an idea why I've called you in today?" asked Brian, as the sacrificial lambs merely stared amongst themselves, unsure of what to make of this. Brian shook his head as he placed down his hands onto the table firmly. "I've brought all of you in today, because I watched over last week's debut show, and, to be blunt, I wasn't happy by the work put out by the lot of you." muttered Brian, his eyes stared into the audience of workers, unsure of what to make of that statement. "First off, the opening match, between Bantam and David. I like both of you guys..." started off Brian, as he trailed off, and the other present roster members stared at both men. Brian continued on, "However, that match was pretty damn sad. Busting out bulldogs, GFC, and so forth within a minute or so of the start?! Only a spot machine who has to rely on a few somewhat impressive moves, like a Jeff Hardy would need to bust out the big moves in the first moments. But where do you go from there, huh?" asked Brian, his face contorted into a small sneer as David attempted to open his mouth, but Brian quickly responded, apparently expecting the question to be rhetorical: "You can't. Basically, when you hit all your bombs in the first minute, you can't build up to anything, unless this is a match like Steve Austin versus The Rock from WrestleMania Seventeen, where you up to the stakes. Of course, that's normally saved for big blow off matches, so for the most part, your formula will not work within the confine of an average five to ten minute match. Even in the main or semi-main, it won't work. You don't need to work a five minute headlock or even work around a body part, but you do need to at least make the top tier moves significant. If you hit a German Suplex in the first minute, then it becomes a throwaway move rather than one which should be feared, like Vader's was. You understand?" asked Brian, as David and Bantam nodded their heads, as did the rest of the roster members present. Brian began to pace around the desk as he walked towards Rush. "I've already talked to you and the Cowboy, and given that your situation was similar to David and Bantam's, I see no reason in restating my point again. I was also going to talk about Dr. Curiosity's 'match', but I decided that we didn't have all night, we should just let that one go." stated Brian coldly as Rush nodded his head half-emphatically. Brian muttered to himself at the response, but nonetheless walked over to the last two men called in for the 'talk', ArchAngel and Stephen Hailowe. "Steve, Mike, I needed to talk to you about your match. Too be honest, I was expecting the match to go on a little longer than it did. Now, I didn't want you to slap on a boston crab for three or four minutes, but given that you were both given the semi-main, so I expected a longer match. In addition, given that the match had similar problems to David and Bantam's, I hope that both of you will learn from this meeting, and try to build up in future matches more. There are occasions where hitting a big spot at the start may be important, such as surprise attacking an opponent, causing them long-term damage to work over, etc., but even then, it can't be something like a Brainbuster or Exploder or what-have you. Maybe a Chokeslam or something which is 'easier' to hit in the context of the match." stated Brian to the two of them, and they merely nodded. "Alright, I guess that's all for now. I'm not angry at you guys, but I do want our product to differentiate from that of the fWo's. We can find plenty of shit there, and I don't want us to be ridiculed as being a clone of sorts. It's for both your benefit and my own that I decided to bring this up now, rather than when it is too late, and you'd be more hesitant to change." said Brian, as he tapped his hand against a table. "And, as you all know, if you need my help as a Road Agent of sorts, I'm always available to help. Now...get out there and show everyone what OCW is really made out of!" stated Brian, beaming at the men who moments ago he had grilled. All the men could do, of course, was stare at each other in confusion at Brian's almost split-personality level of behavior within the last few minutes. Ambition v. Ambition The OCW roster began to file out of the room. Dumbfounded looks resided on many of their faces. The last man to rise was David Walter Smith, better known as Logic. Drawing himself up to his full 6'9" height, he glared directly at Brian Cage. War, however, was no short man himself, and his eyes met Logic's. "We have issues to discuss," Logic said, gaze boring through the man once known as War. "Do we?" Cage asked, "I thought we just discussed them." "You might have," Logic said, a touch of anger creeping into his voice. "But I have not." Cage sighed, and braced himself. Of all of the Survivors, Logic was the smartest, the strongest, and unfortunately, the most headstrong. Those three things were not a combination that was in Cage's favor. "Go ahead then," Cage said. "Maybe you can explain to me why you saw fit to hit Bantam Diablo with that damned backdrop driver of yours." Logic looked at Cage as if the Survivor mastermind had three heads. To him, the answer was apparent. "Because, it helped me win the match. It's my finisher. People don't get up from it." "That's beside the point," Brian explained. "We can't have you dropping people on their heads every show. It loses impact." "Does it?" Logic asked, mirroring Cage's earlier sarcastic question. "I remember doing it every night during the Survivor IV competition, and you not having one problem with it." Logic was painting Brian into a corner. Cage knew the way Logic's mind worked. Based on pure logic, David Walter Smith was right. But there was more than winning matches at stake here, a fact that Cage attempted to explain. "There's more than winning on the line here. We're trying to establish OCW as an alternative to those other guys. Based on wrestling. Things like a backdrop driver have to mean something. What good does winning matches do if OCW folds because we don't succeed in establishing our niche?" David Walter Smith didn't even blink as he gave his response. "I accept an offer from another federation." Cage went pale. He had thought Logic, like all the others, was on board for OCW completely. Now, not only did he have opposition to his vision, but potentially belligerant opposition. "I don't have that option, damn you!" Cage screamed. "This is it for me! It's OCW, or nothing! Stop being so damn selfish!" "Listen to yourself," Logic said. "All you care about is yourself. Your dreams for OCW, your ambitions. You don't give a damn about me or the other Survivors. You haven't since you brought in Kinkade and Rush. For someone who hates the fWo, you sure are willing to take their castoffs--" "It's no business of yours what I do in this company!" Brian snapped, anger showing in his reddening face. "Look, I understand that you want a wrestling company. But I'm not going out there dressed like a damned clown or anything. I go out there, and I bust my ass like everyone else. But you can't take Proof Positive from me. It's the best move in my arsenal." It was a clear impasse. One man's will to win against another man's desire for success. Ambition pitted against ambition. "I'm going to ask you again, David," Cage said, voice quieting. "Please don't do the damn move anymore. Don't do it tonight." "Tonight?" Logic asked, "I have a match?" "Three-way with Black Spider IV and Brookes Davidson. I don't like three-ways, but we needed to get more people on the card." "Fine with me," Logic said. "I'm going to go prepare." Seconds later, he was out the door, leaving Brian Cage alone, shaking his head. "Please don't do the goddamned move..." But...why?! *sad* Brian Cage stood alone in the room, shaking his head, muttering something. Another door leading to the room opened. It was from the toilet. Brian Cage turned around annoyed, wondering who it was. Holy hell - it's the one and only Christian Elijah! The "Regular" smiled at War, whose face was starting to turn red. "Elijah, where the hell were you when I called for that meeting just now?" The question sorta wiped the smile off Elijah's face. He realized the seriousness of skipping the meeting. But... he did have a good excuse after all. "Bad stomach man." Brian Cage's glare didn't seem to take too kindly to Elijah's excuse. "Do you know," asked Cage, "why you got eliminated from Survivor from the first place?" The real reason was... meh, Elijah couldn't be fucked. But he wanted to know anyway. *shrugs* "The reason is because you spent too much time in the toilet rather than wrestling! Even the janitor spends less time in the toilet than you! So since you finally made it tonight, I'm not gonna have you hanging around the toilets. This company is built on wrestling and your job is a wrestler! You've got a match tonight my friend!" "But why????!" whined Elijah. "I'm not even on the card!" "Well, YOU ARE NOW! It's you and that good for nothing Midnight Cowboy! Now, get outta my face!" The "Regular" walked away meekly, not daring to anger his boss any further. Brian Cage smiled. God, it felt good to be back at his bullying best. "Potential Personified" Chris Moliano vs. Doctor Curiosity "Cocky" by Kid Rock. Chris Moliano appeared from behind the curtain and strode to the ring with a purpose and one solitary goal: to win a match for the first time ever. Dressed in red pants and boots and with his wrists heavily taped Moliano was ready for action. His walk lacked its usual distinctive swagger and he seemed less intent on strutting his stuff and more focused on the job he had to do inside the ring. This was not the first time had had faced Doctor Curiosity, the two men had met during the Survivor tournament with the Doc defeating him, but this time would be different. Completely different. "Hermann Loves Pauline" by the Super Furry Animals. The fans hate him, you hate him and so does just about everybody else, but the fact remains he got further in Survivor than you did. The ultimate fish out of water, fresh from the lab and heading to the ring...Doctor Curiosity. Along with his sidekick Eegor the Doc walked down the aisle long white lab coat and all. One could argue this was his first real match in OCW, last week he overcame the not so dreaded El Smashy in unconvincing fashion. Then one could also argue that having never won a match himself; Moliano was hardly a big step up. A negative fan reaction followed Curiosity into the ring as he made his way to the opposite corner from where Moliano was standing and prepared to showcase his wrestling skills to the world once again. Ding Ding! The two combatants stared at one another from opposite corners of the ring as the bell rung. Showtime. Curiosity stepped forward casually toward Moliano with a somewhat cocky smile on his face. He extended his hand as Moliano stood in his corner as if to offer a handshake, when suddenly he slapped Chris right in the side of the face before laughing out loud and returning to his corner to remove his trademark long white coat. Moliano was angry and seemed eager to impress, his determination to succeed showed as he began to step forward towards Curiosity who started to back away by circling the ring still smiling from the slap he had just delivered. Suddenly the Doc swung with a right hand but Moliano countered easily as he blocked the punch and then took Doctor Curiosity down hard with an impressive looking arm drag into an arm bar. Chris pulled back to apply additional pressure to the right arm of the Doc as he pulled him up to his feet and turned the arm bar into a hammerlock. Curiosity was in pain and tried to get out by swinging his left elbow back to try and strike Moliano in the head, but Chris once again had the move scouted. Chris was able to duck underneath the elbow before picking up Curiosity and sending him down hard onto his knee with an atomic drop. Curiosity yelled out but stayed on his feet as he walked away gingerly. He then spun round but again Moliano wag once more the first to react as he scooped up the Doctor and slammed him down to the mat once again with a perfect scoop slam. Moliano jumped up into the air as Curiosity arched over on the mat to feel his lower back as a shooting pain shot through his upper body. "Who's the Daddy?" Chris screamed playing to the crowd who roared a slight cheer of approval as Moliano began to get himself worked up by doing two or three standing star jumps to the delight of much of the crowd. As the Doctor got back to his feet Moliano changed from a star jump to a beautiful looking dropkick. It was safe to say that Doctor Curiosity had certainly not expected Moliano to come flying out of the blocks in this manner. As soon as his body hit the deck he began to roll out of the ring under the bottom rope in an attempt to break up the considerable momentum Moliano was building up in the early stages of the match. A wise veteran-like manoeuvre indeed. Doctor Curiosity regained his breath on the outside as Moliano really started playing to the crowd inside the ring to get them firmly behind him. His head was ringing but one thought ran prominently through Curiosity's head. What the hell was happening? He was the runner up of Survivor, and here he was being outclassed by a young rookie who was one of the first few men eliminated and was yet to muster a single win inside the squared circle. The Doc shook his head to clear it once and for all he had beaten Moliano before, and now he was going to do it all over again. Smack! Focusing too much on the thoughts in his head as he pulled himself back up to his feet with the aid of the ring apron, Curiosity had not seen the baseball slide coming. The sliding red boots of Moliano crashed into the side of his face and sent him sprawling into the barricade. Chris pointed to himself with pride as he gazed at the crowd cheering him on - Moliano was competing with a smile on his face, and the results were simply stunning. Chris strode confidently to the outside and began to fire away with some stiff right hands into the face of Curiosity who was down on his knees. One... Two... MOL-EEE-ARNO! Three... Referee Mark Glass began to exercise his ten count as Moliano unleashed a powerful haymaker which knocked the Doc down and perhaps even out. Four... MOL-EEE-ARNO! Five... Moliano slid inside the ring with Curiosity down as Glass continued his count... Six... Doctor Curiosity was barely moving on the outside and Moliano could sense that his first win was well on its way...as could the crowd who could barely believe the incredible display Moliano was putting on going to school on the Doc as they continued to chant his name. MOL-EEE-ARNO! Seven... "Here comes eight", Moliano spoke with a smile. Eight... With the Doc still down this was it. Eegor was going crazy (or should that be crazier) on the outside pulling out his hair and yelling at Curiosity to get up but to no avail. Moliano scaled the turnbuckle in victory and started gesturing to the crowd in celebration. The roar from the fans rung through his ears, all that was left now was for him to stand on that turnbuckle with the crowd loving it almost as much as he did. All that remained were the final two counts as Moliano listened hard to the voice of Mark Glass. His frustration at not winning was about to be lifted. MOL-EEE-ARNO! Nine... The count combined with the chants from the crowd was like therapy. He waited ready to erupt with a roar of triumph, cupping his ear to hear the final count. Ten. Ten? TEN? Thud. The sickening sound of Moliano's body being tossed from high up on the turnbuckle down to the mat brought a gasp from the crowd in unison. The Doc had been biding his time, utilising the referee's count to gain maximum amount of energy back. As soon as he heard the count of nine he jumped up like a flash and slid under the bottom rope. The sight of Moliano up high on the opposite turnbuckle with his back turned was an opportunity he could ill afford to miss as he threw his body down to the canvas. Now it was Moliano's turn to lay motionless. His mind still full of thoughts of winning and success, he had not even realised exactly what had happened as he lay flat out on the mat. Curiosity regained some more strength and shook his head to try and relieve the ringing in his ears from the stiff shots he had taken on the outside just moments earlier. The wind had been taken very much out of Moliano, and Curiosity knew that as of now, he was in complete control. And boy was Chris going to suffer. The Doctor walked slowly over to Moliano who was yet to make any significant movement with a look of intent on his face. It was time for some serious pain. Curiosity kicked gently at the head of Moliano, playing with his lifeless body whilst grinning sadistically. Finally he grabbed Moliano by the hair and pulled him to his feet, Curiosity stared into his eyes before reaching back and slapping him in the face once again, sending Moliano back down to the mat. The way his body landed after being tossed off the turnbuckle had left Moliano in considerable pain and with no real energy left in his body. He was breathless, without energy and worst of all at the complete mercy of the sinister Doctor Curiosity. Seeing Moliano in this condition brought joy to Doctor Curiosity as he again pulled Moliano up by his hair and this time set him up for his only legal finishing manoeuvre the Curious Yellow. There was, of course, only one thing to be done. Frank was gonna have to get his face seen. His name heard. Frank was gonna have to be the guy that roster saw and feared the most. They swayed back and forth like a ship encountering turbulence on he seas, each trying to find leverage but coming up empty by the other force. The fans began to grow restless to as the wrestlers began to go Greco-Roman style, doing what looked like some kind of off shoot of line dancing in each others arms. Both were frustrated. One of them did something about it. CRRAAAAACK!!! Midnight Cowboy's head jutted forward and planted itself on Elijah's forehead, connecting with a powerful headbutt, to an almost distant warning by the referee. Christian staggered backwards, as well as Cowboy, who didn't expect the blow to hurt him this much. He shook his head a little bit before putting a grapple on Elijah and doing something about it, This time executing a harsh T-Bone Suplex that pleased the sadistic crowd. Elijah rolled around in agony, his head and back aching after the dangerous suplex was hit. With his opponent lying face up on the canvas, Midnight Cowboy thought it best to move to his repetoire of submission moves now that he had transitioned himself back into control of the match. He decided on the One-legged Boston Crab to work over the back, which he initiated on Elijah. Christian whined and shouted in agony as The Midnight Cowboy positioned himself in a comfortable stance. The fans cheered as Midnight Cowboy smirked slightly, his grip tightening every now and then just to feel Elijah squirm beneath him. Cowboy failed to realize that there was a motive to all this squirming, and was surprised to feel the referee tap his arm. "Break your hold, son." Midnight Cowboy, thoroughly confused, turned to see that they had managed to slither across the canvas to a local ring rope, which Elijah conveniently grabbed. Cowboy followed the referees orders, breaking his hold and lifting Elijah to his feet. He whipped him to the ropes and intended on giving a clothesline on his returns, but his plans were foiled. Elijah ducked the clothesline attempt and continued to the ropes, running back and lunging with a HUGE forearms smash on his return, that send Midnight Cowboy sprawling awkwardly into the ring ropes. Elijah used Cowboy's position against the ropes to his advantage, managing to strangle him on the ropes. The fans began to boo loudly as Christian Elijah seemed to be gaining steam. He grabbed the winded Midnight Cowboy back to his feet and lifted him up before putting him down with a side slam, which knocked the wind out of his lungs. Elijah mouthed to the crowd while he strutted around to Midnight Cowboy's feet and applying his own submission. This time it was the Sharpshooter, and Christian Elijah beamed with ecstasy as Midnight Cowboy writhed on the canvas. "Give up?!" Elijah asked devilishly, a smile crawling on his face. His smile grew wider as he heard no answer. Midnight Cowboy bit his lower lip as his knee joints and lower back throbbed. His sweat covered face glanced up at the crowd, who's eyes were trained on him and who's mouths moved with motivational words. The "Cow-boy" chant began again, only this time their chants got so loud that the armadillos of Amarillo, Texas were likely shaking in their shells, or whatever the hell you call their protective backings. "Cow-Boy!" "Cow-Boy!" "Cow-Boy!" "Cow-Boy!" "Give up?" Elijah repeated. He smiled wider again, thinking Midnight Cowboy didn't answer again, but his ears could not catch Cowboy's muffled groan as he wrenched on the dangerous submission hold that Bret 'The Hitman' Hart had made famous: "No." Midnight Cowboy burst up in a rolling movement, causing Christian to break his hold and for him to distance himself to safety as Cowboy rolled away. He rose up and just as Elijah did, by sprinting forward, rammed his head and upper body into Christian Elijah's midsection. SPEEEEEAR! The crowd went insane as Cowboy collapsed along with Christian, the Spear being a last ditch attempt to take out Christian. The Cowboy recovered enough strength a few seconds later, and rolled over to Christian, weakly draping an arm over his chest. One. Two. Three. Keep the corks in those champagne bottles folks, because now isn't the time to celebrate. Midnight Cowboy's delayed pin caused the crowd to groan a collective groan. Midnight Cowboy kneeled and leaned back, emitting a deep sigh. He looked down at Elijah and saw that he was panting. Both of them were dog-tired. The match was reaching an end, but its outcome still hinged on who wanted it more. He lifted up Elijah and began to strike him, but Christian found a way to somehow return the blows. They went back and forth, as they did with the grapple earlier. The fans seemed to be just annoyed by this volleying exchange as they did earlier, and began to boo. This disappointed verbage stopped almost immediately as Elijah ducked a strike and caught him, digging his shoulder into Cowboy's armpit and letting cowboy's arm drape over his own shoulder. Everyone knew what was coming next. A trademark of the Rock and Booker T. The fans deflated almost completely as Cowboy's back crashed to the mat with a Modified Urange Slam. It was over. The undefeated streak for Midnight Cowboy was going to be over. One. Two. The crowd, groaning at Cowboy's downfall just seconds before, became electric as Midnight Cowboy broke free with a vengeance, throwing Elijah off of him and hurried to his feet, in obvious discomfort, yet only taunted Elijah to strike him and finish the job. Midnight Cowboy wasn't about to lose in his home state, and he was extremely bored with this tired two-step. Christian Elijah swung with a wide hook, but it surprisingly landed and Midnight Cowboy staggered back. The fans wondered if Midnight Cowboy forgot the fact that he ASKED to be hit, but they were quick to forgive as Midnight Cowboy struck back much harder than his opponent did. Elijah's fatigued body fell to its knees, and Midnight Cowboy grinned slightly: he had put himself in position for Cowboy's finisher, Voices after Midnight. He put Elijah's head in between his thighs and held his arms behind his back. Christian, however, wasn't ready to give up yet, and quickly rammed his head upwards, staggering Cowboy backwards. The fans exploding into almost deafening boos as Christian pointed at his head, clearly outsmarting the Midnight Cowboy. Quickly afterwards, Elijah snapped forward his full body weight, going for his signature Superkick... Bootlicker. To Christian's dismay, however, his leg was caught, and an ENRAGED Cowboy, obviously still in discomfort, spun Christian around ala Shawn Michaels at Wrestlemania XIV. Cowboy quickly connected with a half rolling elbow to Christian's face, and as Christian staggered back, Cowboy grabbed Christian's head, and, taking a page out of Toshiaki Kawada's book, step kicked Christian several times in the face, disorienting him. Cowboy quickly set up a standing scissors and hand tying position once more. Christian, slowly coming to, attempted to struggle a bit but came to grips that this match was out of his reach now. He hoisted Elijah in the air and brought him down in his infamous Double Underhook Spike Piledriver. The fans went berserk at the moves completion, a figurative death sentence for whoever faced Midnight Cowboy. Midnight Cowboy collapsed to his knees, stared at the crowd for a ever brief moment in joy, and covered Christian, his fingers making the numbers that the crowd shouted out. One. Two. Christian realized what was going on, but it was too late... Three. "Simple Kind of Man" erupted as the referee lifted the Midnight Cowboy's hand in victory. He fell back and braced himself on a ring rope, breathing heavily to catch his breath. In a show of good sportsmanship, he helped Christian Elijah to his feet to congratulate him for being a challenging opponent. Christian was less than enthused and broke free from Midnight Cowboy's touch, leaving the ring and charging through the tunnel to the back. Midnight Cowboy shrugged and weakly climbed a turnbuckle, absorbing the riotous cheers of his home state crowd, having overcomed the odds of a weakened mental state to become victorious. As Cowboy absorbed the cheers of the Cal Farley Coliseum with all of the strength he had left, the OCW logo emerged on the screen and Tradition two faded to a close, another show completed, this time on a higher note. Result: Midnight Cowboy via Pinfall
