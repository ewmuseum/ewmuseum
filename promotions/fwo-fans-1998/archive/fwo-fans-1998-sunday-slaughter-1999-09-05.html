Sunday Slaughter, 5 September 1999

5 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Backstage at Sunday Slaughter the talent were escorted to their locker rooms in preparation for that night's event. Geoff Green captured some of the entering wrestlers on film, getting footage together for the as yet unnamed new fWo recap and news profile show that would be debuting on September 13. Rain stood on the edge of all of this activity, looking at the brackets for the "Wrestler of the Year" tournament ending at fWo: Fallout. The tournament looked to be serious business, and Rain noted that the winner of the tournament would get an fWo Heavyweight title shot that must end in a fair match... only pinfalls or submissions. Grunting, he looked up, scanning the locker room for signs of the man he would meet in the first round, Crucifix. He saw Mike Bear wearing a black jacket, wandering around the locker room with a slight limp, but no signs of the would-be Hardcore champion who was scheduled to face him. Ultra Violet entered, and promptly ran into the wall face-first. He stumbled back from it and rubbed his head with a slight grin, and then wandered away in search of his locker room. Kid Cool saw this, and he mimicked Ultra Violet's actions, colliding with the wall head first. Kid Cool obviously didn't have the frontal lobe strength that Ultra Violet did, and he fell to the ground in a heap unconscious. Watching all of this, Rain shook his head in dismay, and looked around the room for signs of Crucifix. Mike Bear entered the area dressed in a hockey jersey, and held his arm gingerly from where it had been attacked on Warfare. Rain looked at this, and his face grew grim. On the other side of the room Geoff Green coaxed Mike Bear, who was already dressed in his ring attire, to a spot he had set up for interviews. On the other side of the room the Mike Bear wearing the hockey jersey wandered off in search of Wretch. Rain sunk back into the shadows of the room, deeply disturbed by the events that only he seemed to be noticing... D-Lo Brown vs. New Ninja Warfare started with the usual fanfare and explosions, and amidst all of the noise D-Lo Brown came to the ring and stood on the turnbuckle, waving his arms and head at the crowd. Suddenly, the lights dimmed and a blue light shone out into the crowd, hunting for a single man. The New Ninja came from nowhere, weaving his way through the crowd and rolling into the ring silently. D-Lo charged, but the Ninja ducked under his attack and hit a spinning dropkick from behind. The Ninja seemed more focused than before, and kept D-Lo from getting to his feet with attacks to the midsection and head. The New Ninja used quick submission-style holds, wrestling with a different and more deadly moveset for the usually more brawling focused wrestler. Climbing to the top rope, the Ninja executed a perfect moonsault from the turnbuckle, and covered D-Lo for the pin. Before the ref could count to three, D-Lo put a leg on the rope, breaking the count. The Ninja used a neck hold, trying to cut off the oxygen of the larger D-Lo. D-Lo Brown rallied, getting to his feet and shoving the Ninja into the ropes. D-Lo executed a lariat that took the Ninja off his feet, and followed it up with a series of hard punches. Brown went for a cover, but the Ninja kicked out at the last second. Brown picked up the Ninja for a snap suplex, and then went to the top rope for a moonsault. D-Lo dove off... but the Ninja managed to roll out of the way. With D-Lo stunned the Ninja hit a legdrop, and covered D-Lo for the three count. The Ninja walked from the ring and exited through the crowd, the first man to defeat D-Lo Brown in the fWo in a long time... Result: New Ninja by Pinfall An offer... Backstage cameras picked up shots of the Flying Frenchie, strolling confidently down the halls of the arena on some unknown mission. He stopped outside of a locker room, but his back obscured the name plate on the door. The locker room door opened and the Frenchie stood looking at the occupant inside silently for a moment. Unseen by the cameras, the man inside the locker room attempted to shut the door, but the Frenchie held the door open with his foot. The Frenchie began to speak, slowly at first and then faster. "Yes, zit's me. I know... zu and I zave been... not allies in ze past... but now I zave somezing to offer. My alliez... ze Primetime... ze is zin ze position to win several titles for ze Foundation. I want to buy... ze insurance policy zu make sure zis takes place." The hidden man inside the locker room attempted to shut the door once more, but the Frenchie again blocked the door closure with his feet. "In return... in return big man... zu get a title shot... of ze best title zat Primetime wins. Understand now?" Silence, but the Frenchie smiled, at it was obvious that the man who was hidden had nodded his head in acceptance. The Frenchie extended a hand, and shook the mystery man's hand to seal the deal. The Frenchie then walked away from the locker room, catching sight of Geoff Green and the cameras for the first time. The Frenchie frowned and threatened Geoff not to show the video to anyone... but before the video went off it caught sight of the man the Frenchie had been talking to... and the Boston Strangler was smiling. Los Latin vs. Silver Fox A silver spotlight brings out Silver Fox, still on crutches. He slowly makes it to the ring, discussing with the referee the unfairness of him having to wrestle. Los Latin comes out next, jawing with the fans on his way to the ring. He slides into the ring, and joins the conversation with the ref and Silver Fox. The three seem to come to some form of agreement, and Los Latin offers his hand to Silver Fox. Silver Fox shakes on the agreement, but Los Latin takes this opportunity to kick Fox Result: Los Latin by Pinfall Courage and Jake accept the challenge fWo newcomer Courage wandered into the area, flanked as always by his bodyguard Jake. He was approached by Matt Loco, who shoved a microphone in Courage's direction and began to interview the cocky young athlete. "Courage... Courage... in a very short time you've found yourself right in the center of the fWo, and my close personal friend, the challenge has been laid down by Black Quicksilver for a tag match... what do you say, do you accept the challenge for a match with Black Quicksilver tonight?" Courage looked annoyed, and shoved Matt Loco backward. "Listen Loco... I'm not your close personal friend, and I'll never be your close personal friend. Fact of the matter is, I am new to the fWo, and you know what? I am going to accept the challenge of Black Quicksilver and that freaky little piece of scalp that he carries around... but not for tonight, and not for Warfare. Courage is going to Hotter Than Hell, and he's going to wrestle in front of the big screens, and the big crowds, and he's going to push the buy rates through the roof. Jake and I will be there next Friday... and Black Quicksilver and that freaky little thing of his can show up and take the biggest ass kicking I can dish out." Courage gave Matt Loco one more look over, and shook his head in disgust, ending the interview. Ultra Violet vs. Kid Cool The music of Kid Cool played, and the scrappy youngster walked to the ring waving at the largely uninterested crowd. Kid Cool warmed up inside the ring, ready for his practice match against the man who had agreed to become his mentor. The man himself came to the ring to the blaring sounds of "Rage", with his attractive valet Violet by his side. Violet sat at the announcing table while Ultra Violet climbed into the ring and faced his opponent. Ultra Violet and Kid Cool locked up, and Ultra Violet showed Kid Cool how to properly trip and fall at this point in the match, allowing his head to fall dangerously close to the metal turnbuckle. Meanwhile Violet giggled with Chris Finn and James Armstrong, who told her their famous "Man from Nantucket" limerick. As Ultra Violet showed Kid Cool how to pander to the audience by waving goofily, Violet looked over the Tournament Brackets for the "Wrestler of the Year" contest. She pointed to one area of the "winnings" list with a questioning look in her eyes. Chris Finn explained that the winner of the tournament would receive an award, a trophy, a new car, a title shot and a ladies diamond ring. Violet scratched her head at this, as an idea began to form in her head. Inside the ring Ultra Violet instructed Kid Cool to pick him up and deliver a piledriver. Kid Cool picked Ultra Violet up clumsily, and put his head between his legs. Kid Cool delivered the piledriver, but sloppily as Ultra Violet's head never hit the canvas. Ultra Violet explained that he had done it "Undertaker" style, and now should try it Japan style. Kid Cool picked Ultra Violet up, but lost his grip and dropped Ultra Violet directly on his head. This seemed to be the ring thing, as Ultra Violet was knocked into unconsciousness. Kid Cool was about to pin Ultra Violet, and it was announced that this was his one hundredth match, and Kid Cool found he couldn't do it. He climbed up to the top of a turnbuckle and spun off, allowing himself to drop down directly on his head and knock himself out. As luck would have it, Ultra Violet's arm managed to fall upon Kid Cool's chest, and the count was made. Violet thanked Chris Finn and James Armstrong for the company, taking a last calculating look at the photo of the prize diamond ring before going to collect her unconscious associates... Result: Ultra Violet by Pinfall Wretch (c) vs. Primetime Wretch comes to the ring alone, carrying his fWo Cruiserweight Title in pride. He rolls into the ring and hands the title to the referee, as he defends his title here tonight. The ring announcer introduces his opponent. "... his opponent is from Buffalo, New York and weighs in at two hundred and twelve pounds. He is a former fWo World Tag Team Champion, and is the winner of MELTDOWN! Tonight, he begins his first match in his full sweep of title shots. This... is... Primetime." As Primetime walks out, his name appears in purple on the Slaughter-big-screen and Kid Rock screams out "My name is Kid..." as Bawitdaba continues to play. Primetime actually receives a nice reaction from the crowd, as a purple spotlight shines on Primetime. Primetime raises his arms in the air, before pointing up at the ramp and out comes The Flying Frenchie & Mitch Wilson. Frenchie walks out with a mic, and Primetime' music quiets down slowly. Frenchie begins speaking "10 mont's ago, I came to t'ree people. Mitch, Brian, you bot' took me up on my offer, and ze world of professional wrestling was changed forever. But ze t'ird man, he turned me down. No hard feelings. But while you two kept getting better zan ever, becoming dominant players in zis game of human chess, zis ot'er, his career has faltered. Now he needs ze opportunity zat I gave you two, ze opportunity zat ze fWo wouldn't provide for you two zen, and won't provide now. Primetime, you'll be making a lot of enemies in zese next few weeks, as you go on to challenge for every title in ze fWo, so zis is for you. Tonight, we welcome into our nest, wit' open arms.....Ze Boston Strangler!" The Boston Strangler walks out with a evil look on his face, before accompanying the French Foundation down to ringside. Primetime climbs into the ring, and the referee walks over and shows the Cruiserweight Title to Primetime, who puts it around his waist. He then gives it back to the referee and tells him "It fits perfectly, thanks." The referee, confused, hands the title to an anonymous ringside person, and has the timekeeper ring the bell. The match begins slowly, with both men hooking up in a collar and elbow tieup. Primetime went for a headlock, before Wretch whipped him into the ropes. Primetime returned and knocked Wretch down with a shoulder block. Primetime, waited for Wretch to get back up, before bouncing off the ropes and returning... he leapfrogs over Wretch, and returns and hops over Wretch, who had dropped down to the mat. Primetime returns once again, Wretch back to his feet, and Wretch attempts a hiptoss. Primetime counters into a hiptoss of his own. Wretch counters that, before Primetime counters that with another hiptoss... with both men close to the ropes, Wretch goes flying over the top rope onto the floor. Primetime gets a head of steam before baseball sliding out onto the floor, nailing Wretch with both feet, knocking Wretch back onto the commentators table. Primetime then goes out onto the ring apron and hits a asai moonsault on the floor. Primetime continues the attack, sending Wretch into the stairs with an Irish Whip. Wretch reverses it, however, and instead Primetime goes crashing into the stairs. Wretch recovers, and slams him head first into the mat. He then rolls him into the ring, and climbs in himself. Wretch whips Primetime into the ropes and takes him down with a back bodydrop. Both men spring to their feet and Wretch hits a standing dropkick. He then scoops up Primetime and hits a front forward slam. A cover, and a kickout. Wretch whips Primetime into the turnbuckles and follows up with a running elbow to the jaw, before scoop up Primetime and positioning a superplex. Instead, he goes for a hurracarana off the top rope, and hits it. Wretch whips Primetime into the ropes, but Primetime reverses, and Primetime goes for a backdrop. Wretch however, quickly runs up behind Primetime, grabs him by the hair, and hooks him in for the reverse ddt. He covers him, hooks the leg... but Primetime kicks out! Wretch decides another hurracarana from the top rope should do the trick, and positions him in place. He goes for it, but Primetime turns it into a powerbomb from the top rope. Primetime gets up, looks at the fans who are in awe of what just happened, and he pulls Wretch in place for the Primetime Bomb. He hits it, and makes the cover... hooks the leg, and we have a new fWo Cruiserweight Champion. Primetime celebrates, first standing on the second rope in the turnbuckles, and holding it high above his head. Then, he wrapped it around his waist, and began the walk up the ramp, joined by the Boston Strangler, Flying Frenchie & Mitch Wilson. He looked into the camera, and said "One down... seven to go." Slaughter went to a preview of the card for Hotter Than Hell on Friday, before proceeding to a commercial break. Result: Primetime by Pinfall Save a buck or two Warfare went to a commercial, and a beach was shown. On it several attractive people in skimpy outfits frolicked, splashing in the water and tossing beach balls to each other. Several women walked the beach, their tight thong bikinis showing ample portions of ass. On the beach a single life guard station stood as a silent sentinel for the beach, with muscular tanned men protecting visitors from the dangers of drowning or virginity. A pair of male life guards looked through binoculars, enjoying what they saw. One was a young man, obviously in his late teens. The other was Austin Davis. "Looks good you little bastard... looks real good." The young man seemed excited by this statement, but then pointed out a pair of attractive babes using the pay phones on the other end of the beach. "Wait! Look over there!" Austin Davis held up the binoculars and grimaced. "Wait a minute... it looks like that dumb bitch isn't using 1-800-COLLECT!" The younger boy grabbed a floater, and started to rush to the scene. "I'll save her!" Davis smirked at the boy. "No... this is a man's job, you piece of trash!" He shoved the boy over the edge of the life guard station and set off to "rescue" the young lady. Looking into the camera, Davis smiled and said, "You guys want to save some god damn money? Use 1-800-COLLECT... you'll save lots of your damn money... trust me." The 1-800-COLLECT logo came onto the screen, and the commercial ended, showing fans where Austin Davis wound up after his fWo stint. Doomsday (c) vs. Ground Zero The challengers came out first, shaking hands and mingling with the fans at ringside. They climbed into the ring and awaited the true fWo World Tag Team Champions, who came out next to a huge negative reaction from the crowd. Stevie Roberts & VL Tha Murdera posed on the stage, with Stevie holding one of the tag titles in front of his ... area. The camera focuses on Roberts, who smiles cockily, before saying "You know, Blondes... I don't care what you say. They're still fake.", before laughing and going down to the ring with VL. Roberts slides into the ring and immediately, the match is on as Wolf Fang takes down Roberts with a double-leg takedown, and then brawling with him onto the floor. VL thinks about attacking Jackhammer, but wisely decides against it. Jackhammer goes to walk to the corner, and VL attacks him from behind, before tossing him out onto the floor. She then goes to the outside and walks around to where Wolf Fang and Roberts are, and she pulls Wolf Fang off of him by the hair. She then slams him head first into the guardrail, which promptly busts him open. She throws him into the ring, before springing up onto the ring apron. She climbs up to the top rope and flies off with a clothesline. VL continues to dominate WF for the next little while, before tagging out to Roberts. Roberts comes in, and attempts to wrestle, but WF outwrestles him before tagging out to Jackhammer. Jackhammer uses power moves until suddenly, "F-ck Off" by Kid Rock begins to play and out walks The Hollywood Blondes. The Blondes come down to ringside carrying their "fake" tag team titles. Universal pulls VL off the ring apron and slams her with one of the titles. Wolf Fang spots Star, and runs off the ring apron at him. Star sidesteps him and Wolf Fang crashes into the guardrail. Star then slams him head first on the steps, before tossing him aside. Star climbs into the ring and slams Jackhammer from behind with the title. Universal climbs in and tosses a dazed Jackhammer into the corner and pummels him with the title. Star grabs Roberts, and nails him with the 'Shooting Star' urnage suplex. He then kicks him out onto the floor. Universal and Star then bring Jackahmmer into the center of the ring, and hit a double-team 'Shooting Star' on him. They kick him out of the ring, before grabbing a microphone. Universal begins speaking; "Finally... the REAL fWo World Tag Team Champions have come back to the garbage dump known only as Boston! The city where it's so cold, Stevie Roberts loses his mojo 'cause his little Steve shrinks up like an old man getting out of a pool. Doomsday, The Sellout thinks it's time to put up, or shut up. So, if you guys have got the ovariological fortitude, why don't you go two-on-two with The Hollywood Blondes, both of our tag team titles on the line, winner-takes-all in the first-ever tag team ladder match in fWo history?! Show up on Friday, bring your belts, we'll bring ours, we'll latch two of them to a cord which will hang from the ceiling... and The Sellout will climb rung... by rung.. by damn rung, reach up and grab the titles, make your asses famous in the process, and then everyone... and The Sellout means EVERYONE will realize that we are the best damn tag team there was, the best damn tag team there is, and the b-b-best damn tag team... there ever will be. And there's two things you can do about it: Not a damn thing, and not a damn thing. It's as simple as that." The Blondes threw down the microphone and spat at the teams as they left, before walking up the ramp into the backstage area as their music played. Result: No Contest
