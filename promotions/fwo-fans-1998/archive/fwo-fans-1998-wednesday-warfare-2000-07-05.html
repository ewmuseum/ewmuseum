Wednesday Warfare, 5 July 2000

5 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Punishment Wednesday Warfare began backstage as Chris Finn met with The Boston Strangler & Scott Slugger, who were airing their displeasures with what occured during their title match this past Sunday Night. "Yes, I know... I know something has to be done, and something will be done. I can't punish Rain because he's in the tournament, but a punishment is forthcoming, I assure you. As far as the others, I figure that Neo will learn his lesson when he's ERADICATED in one fell swoop, huh? Erik Kelly versus Neo right here tonight on Warfare. And there's more... Primetime thinks he's the future, well, he's going to be punished for injuring Havok & Douglas as well when tonight he takes on Ric Chronos & Fallen in a HANDICAP match. And then... Mitch Wilson has this chip on his shoulder... he likes to walk around thinking he's some tough guy... well, tonight he'll be in store for an asswhoopin' to hell and back from Damnation. And finally... Eron... well, let's see how he likes it when he has to deal with you two tonight in the main event!" The eyes of the World Champion and the World's Greatest Athlete lit up, before Strangler responded, just to make sure he heard correctly. "Us?" he asked, before Slugger chimed in with "And me too?". A half-smile came across the face of Strangler, before Finn nodded. "Yes... the both of you... against Eron. And I want you guys to ruin him... damnit, ruin him. Good luck, gentlemen... I'll see you guys later." As Finn turned to exit the area, Slugger stopped him with a brief tug on the shoulder. "Chris... have you came to a conclusion regarding the World Title situation?" Finn pondered the question asked, before turning and nodding. "Yes, and I'll make an announcement later. Fair enough?" Both Strangler & Slugger nodded, before Warfare headed inside the arena... Ric Chronos & Fallen vs. Primetime Wednesday Warfare blasts onto the air with fireworks popping throughout the arena. The Warfare announcers welcome everyone at home to another edition of Warfare, as the sounds of Korn's "Blind" pulsates inside the Allstate Arena. This song, which is a very good song, brings out Primetime for the handicap match signed by Chris Finn just seconds ago at the top of the show. Primetime, a man who's been injuring fWo superstars left and right, reluctantly steps into the ring, and awaits his opponents. Then, "Christian Says" by Tones on Tail, whoever the hell they are, starts up, and former fWo tag team champions Ric Chronos and Fallen come to the ring, and both immediately slide in. Fallen eats a hard right from PT, but Chronos is able to catch Primetime with a few punches that back the superstar into the corner. Chronos looks for an irish whip, and it's reversed by PT. Primetime looks to follow up with a big splash, and he runs at Chronos, but he is stopped midway via a huge superkick by Fallen. Fallen jumps on top with a cover, and PT gets out of it at two. Fallen brings the Future Foundationer up, and sends him into the ropes. PT ducks a back elbow of Fallen, but walks right into a sidewalk slam by Chronos. Chronos covers, and once again PT manages a shoulder up at two. Finally, the referee orders Chronos to the outside, for the time being, and Fallen continues his work on Primetime. Fallen whips PT into the ropes, and PT ducks a clothesline. PT stays behind, and hits Fallen with a spinebuster. He covers, but at two, Chronos enters the ring with a boot to the back of Primetime's head, which breaks up the count. Chronos stays inside of the ring, and lifts PT on his shoulders. He signals for Fallen to go to the top, and Fallen obliges. However, PT slips off of Chronos, and falls behind him. He plants the Man of the Hour with a german suplex with a bridge, but Fallen, who is on the top turnbuckle, comes off with a big flying elbow to break up the count, and the bridge. At this point, we see the Black Panther walk down to ringside, and take a close look at the match. Fallen sees this, and acknowledges Panther's presence at ringside. He goes back to work on Primetime, by bringing him over with a snap suplex. Chronos falls to the outside, and hears Panther's clapping. Chronos, who doesn't trust Panther as far as he could throw him, decides to get in his face, and goes to the outside. Meanwhile, Fallen plants PT with a reverse DDT, but instead of covering, he notices Panther and Chronos arguing on the outside. Fallen goes over to take a closer look, and then, Chronos shoves Panther. Panther takes exception to this, and shoves Chronos so hard, that he goes over the guardrail and into the crowd. Fallen's still looking at this, and from behind, Primetime, who has recovered, hooks Fallen for a backdrop driver. He's about to plant him, and injure another superstar, when Panther comes in to break up the hold, with a hard punch, because he's obviously concerned for Fallen's career. Primetime releases, and then he and Panther begin brawling. Panther gains the upper hand, when suddenly, Lance Star and Courage run down the ramp, and enter the ring. Courage goes to work on Fallen, and Star helps PT kick the crap out of the Panther. Courage nails Fallen with a chair, and Star is able to plant Panther with the Shooting Star. Then, PT pulls Panther into prime position, and Courage comes off with the Courage Required to the Panther. PT goes over to Fallen, spits on him, and then sets him up, and brings him down to the mat with the Primetime Bomb. The Foundation begin putting the boots to Fallen and the Panther, when suddenly, Machina, the Total Eclipse, with twinkie in hand, and Sid Snow run down the ramp, and enter the ring. The Foundation decide to take the high road, and they exit the ring, and walk up the entrance ramp, as Warfare cuts backstage. Result: Primetime by disqualification A Belated Birthday Gift For Erik Erik Kelly sat focused in his locker room. It was going to be just him tonight, as he had Pamela stay in Chicago to attend to some of her personal affairs. Tonight, it was going to be about Kelly the sadistic, Kelly the punisher, Kelly the mean motherfucker who showed Neo what pain was all about. Wearing his "ERADICATION IS UPON YOU" t-shirt, Kelly grabs the black baseball bat in one hand, and a cinder block in another hand. He walks out of his locker room, a look of intense ferocity about his face. His demeanor unnerves Sid Snow as he walks by, Sid taking a few steps out of Kelly's direction, just to be sure none of those objects get used by accident. As Kelly continues down a darker hallway, he comes to an intersection, and quickly stops, backing out to get out of view. Peeking around the corner, he looks to his left to find Neo there, back to Kelly, smoking a cigarette at the open rear entrance of the arena. He seemed totally oblivious to anything around him, and from the sounds of it was talking to himself and chuckling. Erik figured he was psyching himself up for what he intended to do to Kelly tonight...but Erik wasn't gonna give him that satisfaction. An evil smirk forms on Kelly's face. Next to Pamela and that outfit she wore last night, this would be one of the better birthday presents he could wish for. Erik walks down towards Neo, who appears unexpected of this advance. Before anything can be seen, the camera fades out, and a WHAAAAZUUUUUUUP! commercial comes on. "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly (c) vs. Neo Warfare returns to "Whatever" by Godsmack blaring over the PA system. The Allstate Arena comes alive with excitement, boos, and some cheers from the freaks of the place as they await the arrival of Neo.... Thirty seconds later, he still hasn't come out. Hmmm...he must be trying to psyche Erik Kelly out, the announcers are commenting. When suddenly the TravisTron comes alive with action. The scene is a large garbage dumpster. Two feet are sticking out from the top. A huge dent was noticeable on the side of the dumpster...about the size of someone's head. A cinderblock was also in view nearby the dumpster...split in half. Someone here said hello to block, no doubt about it. The camera backs away to see the tall figure of Erik Kelly, arms folded across his massive upper body, looking in the direction of the dumpster, a grin on his face while he shakes his head in the direction of his prey. It's a good guess that the individual who went head first in the dumpster was Neo, and that Neo never saw Kelly coming from the previous segment. As the crowd cheers its acknowledgement of Kelly's presence, Erik walks to the backside of the dumpster and retrives the baseball bat he had with him earlier. It too has a significant dent in it. Kelly proceeds to go into a rage and shout as he swings the bat and continues to hit the dumpster in many areas, causing an enormous amount of noise and vibration. Noticing that Neo's legs did not move, Erik grins, drops the bat, and goes to retrieve Neo. Neo is out cold, bleeding from the forehead from the blows he took. Erik slings him over his shoulder and proceeds to walk past the camera towards ringside. As he gets closer, the sound system blares the opening rifts of Limp Bizkit's "Break Stuff", followed with Kelly's addition of "POWERBOMB!" added to the song. As he walks through the curtain, his face is a picture of fierce intensity as the crowd cheers on the near 7 foot monster. Kelly still has Neo over his shoulder, and Neo doesn't look like he has a clue where he is. Kelly walks up the steps, over the top rope and drops Neo in the middle of the ring. Erik rips his shirt off from his back and throws it in the audience, where a happy 14 year old catches it. Looking absolutely pissed off at the world, Erik looks to the fans with his hands on his hips wondering what else he should do. A few fans are already giving him a good idea: Powerbomb. Erik smirks, gives the fans a thumbs up, walks over to Neo, and puts him between his legs. Milking the crowd, Kelly looks at all corners of the Allstate Arena, as he brings the house to its feet, as they chant in unison for him: POWEEEERBOOOOMMMB!". Kelly points up, lifts Neo up, holding him HIGH in the air, then Erik adjusts his hands to Neo's stomach, where he proceeds to execute what is noted as the nastiest "Eradication" Powerbomb on record, as Neo's body bounces off the mat. Erik adds insult to injury, dropping to one knee, and putting his pinky finger on Neo's chest. Kelly glares at the ref, who gets the message and drops to give the 3-count. Kelly's music blares again, as EMTs rush the ring to check on Neo. Kelly grabs a microphone. "I hope everyone was taking notice! Hardcore just went to the Next Level! This worthless piece of shit is no match for "The Eradicator", and he's just a scratch on the ass of this league. Keep your eyes open, fWo. Kelly's targeting you, and there ain't a daaaaaamn thing...." Thunder suddenly blares throughout the arena, halting Kelly's speech. Lightning flashes as well in the direction of the TravisTron, where a message flashes: "When It Rains...It Pours". Kelly slams the microphone down, causing feedback from the PA system. Erik powerwalks up the ramp, the look on his face unexplainable, but the message clear. His next intended target had to be Rain...Warfare goes to commercial, as the camera catches Neo opening his eyes wondering how he got where he is. Result: Kelly by pinfall Broken Warfare went backstage as Chris Finn & James Armstrong sat around their office talking about the drastic change in ratings recently. "I don't believe it, James... I can't honestly believe it. We lose Dead - ratings wise he helped, but backstage he was a pain in the ass - we lose Frenchie, we just lose Scott Collins, we lose Ultra-Violet, we lose all these superstars... and our ratings decline a little as expected. Then, "Big Wretchy, The Ratings Saver" comes along and ratings start to go... up?" Armstrong shrugged. "Fans these days... you know, you don't know what to expect from them anymore." Finn nodded. "Well, at least ratings are going up in all areas, and not just during the Wretch segments or we'd have a real egomaniac on our hands." Before Armstrong could respond to the proceeding comment, someone barged into the room. His face wore an expression of bitterness and hate. His red hair looked out of place, a rarity for this man. It looked as if he didn't catch a wink of sleep recently, and that only added to his bad mood. This is Eron. Eron looked at Finn with a glare, before unloading with a verbal barrage. "God damnit Finn! You think you can punish me for what happened Sunday? Well guess what? You can't. Because I still get my title shot, and I still get it tonight. That's what the coin toss was for, right? To see who got it first... well, Slugger won, he got his shot. Now, where's mine?" Finn scoffed, before standing up and pointing at Eron. "Look, Eron, you ruined your own chances of getting one tonight. In fact, you're not going to get one... instead, you'll receive an opportunity to get one." Eron nearly came over the desk of Finn and ripped his head off, but Armstrong stepped in front of him, telling him to cool down. "James, I'm going to rip his head off! Get the hell out of my way!" James finally calmed him down, somewhat, before Eron asked what Finn meant. "You'll find out later, Eron... run along, I've got work to do." This only angered Eron more... he grabbed a nearby vase with some flowers and water in it and threw it against the walls. Shards flew everywhere, and the water spilled onto Finn' desk. Eron stormed out of the office before Finn could do anything. Damnation vs. Mitch Wilson Warfare heads to the ring for the second of three handicap matches, each set up by Chris Finn to punish those who interfered in Slaughter's main event. The fans erupt as "From This Day" starts to play and the fWo World Tag Team champions Damnation appear on the entrance ramp. Fear and Hellrazor both make their way to the ring, missing Lamont who is probably drunk, sleeping, or both. As they enter the ring, "Calling All Stations" starts to play and Mitch Wilson heads to the ring cautiously, before rolling in and eyeing his opponents warily. The bell suddenly rings and Hellrazor charges at Wilson, who immediately slides to the outside but is followed by Hellrazor, who begins to back him slowly around the ring. Suddenly, from the inside of the ring, Fear grabs Wilson by the hair and, in an amazing show of strength (especially considering Wilson has short hair), lifts him from the outside of the ring over the top rope. The massive Fear immediately winds up and clocks Wilson on the side of the head with a huge right fist. He hooks up Wilson as Hellrazor slides back in and lifts him up, before dropping him with a delayed vertical suplex. Hellrazor brings Wilson up and takes his turn, whipping Wilson into the ropes and catching him with a powerslam. Hellrazor brings Wilson up and whips him into the ropes, and Fear sets up behind Hellrazor. Hellrazor back body drops Wilson high into the air, and Fear catches Wilson in mid-air in a powerbomb-type position, before planting him with a falling powerbomb. The crowd pops at this, and Hellrazor begins to work the crowd as Fear brings Wilson up, wanting to inflict more punishment. He gestures Hellrazor over and the two take down Wilson with a jarring double snap suplex. Hellrazor then runs into the ropes, and Fear lifts him up in a gorilla press before dropping him on Wilson. Hellrazor elects to inflict yet more punishment on the staggering Wilson, and whips him into the corner where he strikes the turnbuckles with heavy force, rattling the ring. Damnation backs into the corner and Hellrazor takes Fear by the arm, before whipping him into the corner. Hellrazor reverses the whip himself, adding to the momentum, and Fear goes flying into the corner with a leaping lariat. Fear vacates the premises as Hellrazor gets a running start from the opposite corner himself and charges in, before leaping up with a body splash. Mitch Wilson sees him coming however, and just manages to get out of the corner in time. Hellrazor crashes into the corner, and Wilson stumbles out... right into a double-arm choke lift from Fear. Wilson kicks wildly to escape however and one of his kicks strikes Fear south of the border, causing Fear to drop Wilson and double over. Wilson plants Fear with a DDT and takes a moment to recover from the beating he has taken. As he does, Hellrazor has recovered, and charges at him with a clothesline. Wilson just ducks and Hellrazor runs into the ropes, before getting caught coming off by Wilson with a kick to the stomach. Wilson quickly hooks Hellrazor and drops him with his patented Superstar Suplex. Not wanting to risk any more damage, Wilson brings Hellrazor up and hooks him for the Headliner. Before he can execute it however, a recovered Fear walks up behind him and hooks him in a full nelson before lifting him into the air. Fear holds Wilson high above the ground, before spinning and dropping down into a sit-down full nelson slam, compacting Wilson's spine. Fear gestures to Hellrazor and Hellrazor quickly heads outside and begins to climb to the top rope. Fear lifts Wilson into a gorilla press, and as Hellrazor reaches the top, tosses Wilson high in the air. Hellrazor leaps off with a sit-down faceslam, completing the Final Judgment. Hellrazor rolls Wilson over and makes the cover, and three seconds later Damnation is victorious. As soon as their arms are raised, the fans begin to boo as from the back is coming Ice, who appears to be wearing a pair of large chemical resistant gloves and holding in his hands a large, smoking block of dry ice. Damnation, facing the opposite way, doesn't see him coming and Hellrazor never sees Ice clock him from behind with the block. Fear turns around only to say hello to Ice's block, which Ice uses to blast him full-on on the face. Wilson begins to drag himself to his feet and takes the opportunity to spit on both members of Damnation, before the duo of Bad Attitude leaves the a decidedly negative reaction from the fans in attendance. Meanwhile... Result: Damnation by pinfall Rubber Right outside the Warfare entrance stood Big Wretchy The Ratings Saver, Scot Hartt & The Abusah. Wretch turned and looked at his lackey and his lackey's lackey with a devious grin. "Rain may be seven feet tall, and weigh... whatever he weighs, and he may possess the God's of the Storm, but the only thing that can stop me tonight is that lightning of his, baby. And not even that..." Wretch grinned with a sly as he glanced down at his boots made of rubber.. "... can stop Big Wretchy... The Ratings Saver tonight. Are you two ready?" Hartt nodded, while Abusah enthusiastically replied with a "HO HELL YAH!" before the trio entered the arena... Big Wretchy The Ratings Saver vs. Rain Result: Big Wretchy by pinfall to advance to the finals of the Next Leve Crucified (IV) Crumbs of twinkie lay interlaced between his fat rolls. He lay up against the wall with either arm tied up against this structure known as a cross. Immediately, one would have to think that whoever did this had help. But from who? The Total Eclipse didn't know, he was knocked out with ether before he could see who had done it. And mysteriously, like the times before, nobody else could have seen it. Machina entered the room and looked at Eclipse, before looking at Sid & Rudo. "Wait a minute... is something out of place here? Sid, did you tie Eclipse up again?" Sid blushed, before outright denying it. "Machina... you know I only have eyes for a certain baseball player." Machina thought about that for a moment, before nodding. "Right... well, one can only take that horrible smell of ether for so long. We better get help for Eclipse." Sid nodded, before Warfare headed back to the ring... Crusader vs. Mike Bear Warfare returns to the arena to the hypnotic sounds of "Switchback" by Biohazard. The fans erupt with an ovation of cheers as Crusader walks out onto the stage. Crusader acknowledges the crowd, before beginning to pull off his Gravity 101 shirt. He reaches the ring and tosses it into the crowd, before doing some warm-up exercises in the ring. "Dragula" begins to blare throughout the Allstate Arena, before one of their perennial favorites makes an appearance; the leader of the Underground, Mike Bear. Bear raises his arms in the air as he walks down the ramp, before charging into the ring and rolling in underneath the bottom rope. The referee signals for the bell, and Crusader grabs Bear by the arm as he enters and whips him to the ropes. Bear returns and he's met with a spinning heel kick. Crusader then brings Bear up and scoops him to the mat with a slam, before springing up to the top rope and flying off with a springboard bodypress. The moment of Crusader 'causes Bear to roll up on top of Crusader for the pin. Crusader narrowly escapes defeat at the very last split second by kicking out. Bear now in control whips Crusader to the ropes, and ducks for a backdrop. Crusader leapfrogs it, and turns and waits for Bear who takes him off his feet with a superkick. Bear with another cover, but Crusader kicks out at two. The leader of the Underground brings Crusader up to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckles. Crusader reverses it and Bear smacks into the buckles back first. Mike Bear narrowly dodges the charging Crusader who hits the turnbuckles sternum first. From behind Bear takes him down with a reverse neckbreaker. He then goes up to the top rope and flies off with an elbowdrop, before another cover, and Crusader again kicks out. The action continues as Bear brings Crusader up once more and nails him with three reverse knife edge chops, which resonates off the arena walls as his hands smack the bare flesh of the newcomer Crusader. Bear then whips him to the ropes, but Crusader reverses and goes for a short-arm clothesline. Bear ducks it, then brings Crusader back and elevates him up onto his back in a firearm's carry position. Bear is going to go for the Whiplash, but Crusader falls down behind him in a waistlock. He charges him into the ropes and goes to roll him up for a pin, but Bear holds as Crusader does a backward somersault on the mat to his feet. Bear charges with another superkick, this time Crusader ducks it and leaps off the second turnbuckle with a springboard Lou Thesz Press. He hits it, and the referee positions to count. One. Two. Thre - no. Bear somehow shoulders out of it, and the crowd gasps in awe. Crusader considers telling the referee it was a count slow, but thinks better, and continues on the offensive. He slams Bear head first into the turnbuckles a few times, before paying him back with a few chops across the chest. He then whips him into the ropes, and as Bear returns Crusader takes him down with a beautiful flying forearm. Crusader follows up with another cover, but again Bear kicks out. Crusader waits for him to get up, before whipping him into the turnbuckles, and charging in with a beautiful handspring elbow. This staggers Bear and as he walks out of the corner, he's met with a kick to the stomach, followed by an inverted swinging neckbreaker. Thinking it's over, Crusader prepares to finish things off by going to the top rope and flying off with the Gravity 101. Instead, a groggy Bear stands up and trips backwards into the ropes, thus crotching him on the ropes. Bear spots this as a perfect opportunity to finish things off. He hooks Crusader on his back fireman's carry style, before hitting the whiplash for the pinfall, and securing a CyberSlam2K rematch of Wretch vs. Mike Bear in the finals of the Next Level tournament. Bear celebrates his win with dignity and pride, and congratulates Crusader on a heck of an effort, before the two shake hands and exit to a nice pop from the crowd. Result: Mike Bear by pinfall to advance to the finals of the Next Level Announcement Warfare heads backstage for the final backstage segment of the night, as Chris Finn stands by with reporter Matt Loco. "I'm standing by with the owner and chairman of the fans Wrestling organization, and Chris, everyone is concerned right now about the World Title situation. What do you intend to do about the number one contender spots each held by Scott Slugger and Eron The Relentless, and furthermore, what about the winner of the Next Level tournament getting a shot at the gold as well?" Chris contemplated it for a moment before responding. "Matt, I've came to a decision that this Sunday at Slaughter in addition to the finals of the Next Level tournament we will see a match that will determine the true number one contender to the fWo World Title in a match between Scott Slugger and Eron. The winner will receive a World Title shot next week on Warfare, while the winner of the Next Level tournament will still receive their shot at the pay-per-view in San Antonio on the 29th, SummerSlaughter. That's all I have to say about it, Matt. Good day." Finn exited the area, as Warfare headed to the ring for the main event. The Boston Strangler & Scott Slugger vs. Eron The Relentless "Physical" By NIN, as it has done so many times before in recent weeks, brings fWo fans back from the last commerical of the evening. From the fans in attendance, it brings out much hatred, as well as many foreign objects. On this night, a blow-up doll with the likeness of Eron, a crowbar, and some raw hamburger meat are among the objects tossed at the most hated man in the fWo. Eron returns each of the favors with a smirk, followed by tossing something of his own...the bird. A trademark of his in recent weeks, Eron uses it so much that it was become habit for him (he flipped his own parents off when they came to see him before the show). He climbs in the ring and awaits one of his two opponents. AC/DC blares over the speakers, and Scott Slugger makes his way down toward ringside...or does he? He is nowhere to be found. Eron smirks. Maybe this will be a fair matchup, after all. O Fortuna begins to play, and the fans continue their appreciation from Slugger to the fWo champion, the Boston Strangler. He walks confidently toward the ring, and then gets on the apron. Eron looks at him menacing. As he does this, Slugger gets up out of the first row of the crowd. The fans begin to cheer louder as he slides into the ring...behind Eron. Slugger gets up and taps Eron on the shoulder. Eron turns around, and is met with a ki ck to the gut from Slugger. Strangler climbs into the ring, and watches as Slugger nails a double-arm DDT. No, this was not the curveball. However, it was devastating nontheless. They pick him up together and toss him into the ropes. As Eron turns around off the ropes, he sees Strangler and Slugger both ready to give him a flip. They lower their heads, and Eron times that just right. He grabs the neck of both Slugger and Strangler, before DDTing them both. Eron gets up quickly and goes toward the top rope. He takes aim on Strangler, and nails a knee to TBS' gut from the top. He then grabs Slugger, and suplexes him to the mat. He goes for a cover on Slugger. At two, he kicks out. Eron gets up to find Strangler getting his wind back. Eron walks over and grabs the hair of Strangler. He whips him back to the mat. Eron grabs the legs of Strangler. He's going to go for the De-Rouger. He locks Strangler in, and turns him around. He has him in it, and the ref goes to see if he's ready to give up. Strangler has been in this situation before. He will not give up. In fact, out of the corner of his eye, he can see Slugger getting up. Strangler starts to muster up all of his strength into his legs, and with one swift motion, he tosses Eron out of the move...and right into Slugger. Slugger catches Eron by the neck, as Eron came out of the De-Rouger bent over. Slugger grabs Eron with both arms, and hits the swinging double-arm DDT....the Curveball. Slugger picks up the Boston Strangler, and motions toward Eron. Slugger says something to Strangler, and Strangler smiles. He walks over toward Eron, and bends down. He locks his massive arms around Eron, and sets him in the Boston Massacre. Eron doesn't want to give, but soon reality checks in. He'll have another day, and he gives. Strangler and Slugger win, and begin to celebrate by stomping at Eron. Eron, being smart, rolls his way out of the ring to escape further damage. Eron limps his way up the ramp, turns around, and flips off everyone (but especially Slugger and Strangler) the inger...meanwhile, the announcers let it be known that fans should tune in to Slaughter for the finals of the Next Level tournament. And, with that, the July 5th edition of Wednesday Warfare is history. Result: Strangler & Slugger by pinfall
