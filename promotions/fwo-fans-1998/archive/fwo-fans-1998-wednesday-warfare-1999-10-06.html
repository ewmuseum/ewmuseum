Wednesday Warfare, 6 October 1999

6 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The sky was dark, and on the ground leaves littered the concrete. Fall was here, and the fWo was in town for another show. Wrestlers slowly made their way into the arena, bags slung over their shoulders and pay tickets in hand. Wretch and Mike Bear chatted together as they passed the Speed Demon, who was slumped against a wall. His face was unshaven and his eyes circled with dark gouges. His face was dirty and stained with tears, and in one hand he clutched a picture of Nikki and himself, taken in a photo mat during his road travels. Wrestlers saw the Speed Demon, but gave him a wide berth, not wanting to get involved with his problem. Some wrestlers pointed and laughed, mocking the Speed Demon where he sat. The Speed Demon began to sob quietly to himself as the cold air from outside chilled him where he sat. The room grew quiet as wrestlers went to their locker rooms, leaving the Speed Demon alone. The Speed Demon looked at the wall opposite him through tears, shaking slowly. A skittering noise came from behind him, and the Speed Demon slowly turned while wiping his face with one dirty hand. Fallen stood, looking down at the Speed Demon with some regret. "I'm sorry William. I know how it is... better than anyone else here I know." The Speed Demon sniffled and looked up at Fallen. Mumbling at first, the Speed Demon asked, "John... John it hurts... I loved her John... now it all just looks... dark." Fallen's face grew grim as he put a hand on the Speed Demon's shoulder. "I know it does William. But now you've got to run... and run far. You are in great danger, and things can get so much worse for you than you can ever imagine. Run William... your soul is in danger now. If he knew I was warning you my life would be in great danger as well... for all I know he is already aware... but my life is not worth what it once was." The Speed Demon looked at John with pain and confusion painted across his face. "Run... my soul? John... I don't understand... what..." Fallen picked the Speed Demon up by the neck and tossed him in the direction of the parking area. "Dammit William run! You don't understand what he's capable of... get out now and don't look back... don't ever look back!" The Speed Demon shuffled out in the direction of the parking area, looking over his shoulder with the same expression of confusion on his face. Fallen watched him go, and shook his head. To himself, he said, "It may already be too late... too late for all of us..." Inside the arena the rumble and crash of fireworks sounded, and Warfare took to the air. Fallen took one last look at the departing Speed Demon and vanished back into the arena depths... Head meets object The pounding could be heard all up and down the halls. Some fans could even hear it in the stands, and looked around curiously. As the cameras traced the hallways of the arena the sound grew louder, until the source was finally found. "You know, you're going to do some serious damage..." Black Quicksilver looked at Jackhammer with a frown and shook his head. The big man was pounding his head into the metal lockers repeatedly, and looked to be very upset. As Black Quicksilver spoke he stopped the assault to his frontal lobe and turned to the masked man. "He's gone Black Quicksilverly guy! Wolf Fang is gone and I didn't stop him! Now what will Jackhammer do?" Black Quicksilver tried to pat Jackhammer on the shoulder, but the big man went back to the head banging and Black Quicksilver backed up with exhaustion. "Look man, he had to find his own way... don't take it personally. The best thing you can do is get your head up and go forward. Stop... er... beating yourself up over it." Jackhammer turned to Black Quicksilver with a hopeful expression on his face. "Hey... will you be my partner? You don't need Stump when you have me... I'm better than Stump anyway, right?" Black Quicksilver looked down at Stump and quickly looked sideways and cleared his throat. "What's that Geoff? An interview? Well, okay... er... I've got to run big man, take care of yourself, okay?" Black Quicksilver beat a hasty retreat, leaving Jackhammer alone in the room. Jackhammer lowered his head in sadness and went back to banging his head against the metal locker, getting progressively harder with each blow. "Ugh... THUD!... Jackhammer doesn't feel... THUD!... so good... Jackhammer... THUD!... getting dizzy... going... THUD!... to fall... THUD!" With that last collision with the locker Jackhammer lost his balance and toppled to the floor in a large heap, completely unconscious... Courage makes his arrival. While Black Quicksilver was trying to calm Jackhammer down, in another part of the arena a limousine was rolling up to the backstage entrance. A very large, fat man crawled out of the passenger seat and went to open the door. fWo wrestler Courage exited the limousine wearing sunglasses and smirking as wrestling attendants took care of his bags and ring equipment. Courage surveyed the scene around him, nodding with approval as workers around him struggled to get everything prepared. Smirking at Jake, Courage began to walk into the arena, throwing the door wide in front of him. The door collided with Eron the Relentless who was standing just inside the entranceway. Eron was holding a cup of coffee and had the fWo International title slung over his shoulder. The coffee flew across the hallway, splattering some of Courage's bags and Jake. Jake howled with pain as the coffee scorched him, and Courage looked alarmed. "My god! Are you okay?" Courage walked past Jake to look at his luggage, yelling at some ring attendants to clean things off. After seeing that things were going to be okay, Courage turned to face Eron, who was fuming over being bumped by the door. "Hey carrot top... watch what you're doing or I'll drop your goofy ass so fast it will make your giant head spin! What in the hell are you doing getting in the way of Courage... don't you have some locker rooms to sweep up?" Eron started to reply but Courage was already walking away, gesturing at ring attendants to bring him equipment. "Hurry up with those bags... Jake, where in the hell are you? Get over here... I'm thirsty, find me something to drink... Dammit, what is that pounding sound?" Jake scrambled after Courage, limping slightly where the coffee burned him. Eron folded his arms and looked at the pair with a growing expression of hatred. "Thirsty are you? Well I'm fix that..." Partnerships dissolve, While Black Quicksilver was trying to calm Jackhammer down and Courage was making his arrival to the arena, Geoff Green stood backstage with Oracle, talking about the upcoming Tag Team Tournament. Geoff started to film, but then looked around himself in frustration. "Hey... is anyone else hearing this pounding noise? Somebody get on that... it's interfering with the whole shot... find out what it is and shoot it, okay?" Green turned back to Oracle, letting the camera drop to his side for the moment. "I don't know Oracle... this place is a real zoo. I miss the old days when I worked at CNN and used to be able to sleep with the interns, you know?" Oracle smiled and began to answer, but before he could Geoff was shoved aside by Prophet. Prophet continued forward, grabbing Oracle by the neck and tossing him into the wall. The side of Oracle's head broke open and blood began to stain his blond hair a dark crimson. Prophet picked Oracle up off the ground and choke slammed him down again, leaving him in a tangled lump on the floor. Prophet turned to Geoff Green and tore off his Dreamers t-shirt. With Green still laying on the ground Prophet began to scream, "The Blue is rising, Green! Let the whole fWo hear me... you will respect my power! All of you!" Prophet stormed out of the area, kicking a cameras out of the way as he went. Geoff yelled for the fWo medical staff to get on the scene was quickly as possible, and held his head with a weak hand. "My god... he's gone insane... quick, tend to Oracle... he's hurt bad... and... hey... that banging noise is gone..." In the distance the ring announcer could be heard introducing the first match of the night, a Tag Team Tournament match... Fallen & Poison vs. Trouble Fans in the arena turned their attention away from the TravisTron to the ring, where Trouble was already waiting. This was a match in the Tag Team Tournament to be concluded at Mob Rules, with only one of the two teams advancing forward. Twitch tried to get the crowd pumped up, as Thumper did some stretching exercises in the ring. Next out came Fallen & Poison, who were not received very well by the crowd. Poison appeared to be crying, and Fallen continued to look like he just saw a ghost. His pale face looked at the crowd, before he slid into the ring and sat on his knees. Poison climbed up onto the ring apron and continued to sob. Twitch wasted no time and charged at Fallen, but Fallen sprung up and speared Twitch into the mat. He pounded on Twitch relentlessly, and even came close to losing the match by disqualification when he didn't pay attention to the repeated warnings by the referee. Fallen finally let up and took Twitch to the outside. Fallen continued his path of violence by whipping Twitch into the guardrail, before hitting a brainbuster. Fallen took him back into the ring and made a pinfall attempt, but Twitch kicked out at two. Fallen brought Twitch up and piledrivered him in the middle of the ring. He uncharacteristically went out onto the top rope and flew off with a legdrop, but Twitch moved out of the way and tagged out to Thumper. Thumper came in and whipped Fallen to the ropes, before hitting a flying elbow to the face. Thumper continued with a shot to the solar plexis, before taking Fallen down with a thunderous snap suplex. He attempted a quick cover, but Fallen got his foot on the ropes. On the outside, Poison continued to cry. Thumper brought Fallen up and went for a spinebuster, but Fallen countered into a DDT at the last moment. Fallen then brought Thumper up and hit him with several kicks to the back, before a few forearms to the face. Fallen then whipped Thumper into the ropes, but Thumper reversed it and ducked for a backdrop. When Fallen returned he took Thumper out with a reverse DDT, before he locked in a nervehold on the neck of Thumper. Thumper tapped out due to the enormous amount of pain, and as he did, the mysterious masked man walked solidly to the ring. Poison continued to cry, before the powerful masked man climbed into the ring and brought Poison into the ring. He chokeslammed her repeatedly, until Poison finally stopped crying. Fallen looked on unfazed by what just happened, and he and Poison advanced to the second round of the tag team tournament. Speaking in harsh tones, the mysterious masked man pointed to the backstage area and said, "Come... the hunt is still on..." Result: Fallen and Posion by Submission Blame Canada! The crowd sat on the edges of their seats in preparation for their next match, and began to boo loudly when James Armstrong appeared on the entrance ramp instead. Armstrong looked around at the crowd and nodded to himself, confidant in his presence. Armstrong walked slowly to the ring with something folded under his arm and a microphone. Stepping into the ring he said, "Friends... countrymen... I come before you to speak of a great evil that has infected our country. No, no... not genital herpes, although I feel your pain, believe me. My fellow Americans... times have changed, our kids are kids are getting worse, they wont obey their parents and they just want to fart and curse! I ask you, should we blame the government? Should we blame society? Or I ask, should we blame the images on TV?" The crowd looked blankly at James Armstrong, who now unfolded what was under his arm, revealing a Canadian flag. "No, blame Canada! Blame Canada! With all their beady little eyes and flapping heads so full of lies, blame Canada!" James Armstrong attempted then to rip the Canadian flag in two, but found that it was sewn together too well and settled for tossing it aside as he continued. "The worst part of that country is not the fact that they screwed with the definition of ham and called it bacon... no! The worst part of that country is a little third-rate promotion I call Toronto's Crappy Wrestling! This TCW is an offense to all of us wrestling fans, and that's why I'm laying down a challenge to any of those wrestlers if they have the stones to accept it! We in the fWo, in America mind you, have this little event I like to call Mob Rules coming up in under a month. So Cactus "Spratty" Jon, if you've got the guts you will bring your Canadian ass and three losers of your picking to meet me and my team... Team USA! Jon... I'll see you there... if you've got the guts to show up!" James Armstrong attempted to start a "USA" chant but the crowd largely ignored him. Settling down and patting himself on the back, James Armstrong returned to the announcing table, challenge made. Chameleon & Total Eclipse vs. Doomsday We return to the ring, where VL tha Murdera and Stevie Roberts await their opponents in this first-round tag team tournament contest. Total Eclipse lumbers out from behind the curtain and waddles half-way down the entrance ramp before halting and pointing back to the entrance. The Chameleon emerges, dressed in a fat body suit. He joins Total Eclipse and the two enter the ring. The two teams stare down briefly, before VL and Chameleon head to the apron, starting the match with Total Eclipse and Roberts. Quick punches and kicks from Stevie to start, but the don't faze the large man. Stevie hits the big man with a clothesline off the ropes, but nothing comes of it. Stevie shouts "Shag it!" and promptly kicks Total Eclipse in his tender nether regions, taking him to his knee. Dropkick from Stevie knocks TE all the way down, and Roberts makes the tag. VL hits a kneedrop from the top turnbuckle, but Chameleon breaks the count at two. VL goes to toss Chameleon from the ring, but Total Eclipse clotheslines her from behind, knocking her to the floor. TE and Chameleon celebrate with Sumo-style belly slaps, bringing some jeers from the audience. Vanessa steps onto the apron, and Chameleon supelexes her back in. Total Eclipse follows up with a huge splash, but Roberts breaks the count. As the ref ushers Roberts out of the ring, Chameleon drops a leg on VL, and goes for the cover himself. The ref counts to two before remembering that Chameleon wasn't the legal man, and finally returns him to his corner. TE brings VL to her feet and whips her to a neutral corner, but VL avoids the following avalanche attempt by Total Eclipse, and tags in her lover. Roberts hits a bulldog off the ropes on the dazed big man, and follows up with a series of kicks to TE's abundant gut. Roberts heads up stairs, but Chameleon shakes the ropes and Stevie crotches himself on the top turnbuckle. Total Eclipse crawls over to his corner and makes the tag, bringing in Chameleon. Chameleon attempts a superplex on Roberts, but he can't pull it off in his fat-suit. Roberts swings at Chameleon to knock him back to the mat, and follows up with a fist drop. Roberts drags Chameleon to Doomsday's corner and tags in VL. VL runs to the ropes and is elevated by Stevie on her return, allowing her to hit a frog splash for the pin. Eclipse slowly enters the ring to collect his partner while Doomsday celebrates their victory, promising the crowd that they would regain their titles. Wednesday Warfare then went to commercial, while the viewers went... somewhere else... Result: Doomsday by Pinfall Deeper into a strange new world "Where am I?" Rain walked away from the glowing doorway into what appeared to be another hallway. It looked normal enough, but seemed off somehow. He passed a sign plastered to the wall that read "fWo Presents Halloween Hell LIVE on Pay-Per-View" and continued toward the sound of a chanting crowd. A midget ran by wearing some green tights over his head and a man chased after him with a t-shirt that read 'The Gecko'. "Come back here with my tights, Tiny Eclipse... dammit!" Rain continued forward as the sounds of the crowd grew louder and louder. He stopped just outside of the backstage exit and looked out at the fans in attendance. Several held up signs for 'Scotty Cold & Ice' and a few argued over who would win, Everybody's Friend Austin Davis or Rockin' Jam Dudley. Rain's expression turned from confusion to concern as he took this all in. From behind him came the sound of a familiar voice. "Hey Rainman... what's with the getup? You've got a match out there... and President Machina is in attendance with foreign dignitaries, so get out there!" Rain turned to see Commissioner Ultra Violet urging him on, and then walked to the ramp. Pimping music played, and Rain turned back to the Commissioner. "Yes! Yes, Rainman... that's you! Go on!" Rain started down the ramp as Commissioner Ultra Violet shook his head. "Man, I don't know what it is with wrestlers with low IQs... you've got to have brains to succeed in this business." Rain walked to the ring where Scott Slammer already stood awaiting him. Rain now was out in public, seeing the crowd first hand. Things were very wrong here, and as Rain climbed into the ring he knew some real bad things were afoot. One of the fans in attendance knew that something was wrong as well, and wondered how he would be able to get close enough to Rain to warn him... Black Quicksilver vs. Courage Back in the arena we know, loud music was pumped into the arena, signalling only one thing. The crowd jumped to their feet and began to scream as the first wrestler made his way out onto the ramp. Black Quicksilver is greeted by a roar from the crowd as he makes his way to the ring to face his nemesis as of late, Courage. Stump in hand, he basks in the love of the crowd, while the sound techs argue over whether they should be playing Prodigy or Twisted Sister. Eventually they give up all-together, and start Courage's music. Courage steps out of the entrance-way lethargicly, stumbling his way to the ring. After several attempts, he manages to climb the ring stairs and enter the squared circle, and the match is on. BQ whips Courage to the ropes and catches him with a hurricanrana upon his return, nearly getting the three before Courage kicked out. BQ brings Courage back to his feet and whips him into the corner, but Courage reverses it and follows him in with a weak clothesline. Courage follows up with some slow kicks in the corner before Black Quicksilver shoves him out of the corner. BQ hops to the top and connects with a flying-overhead neckbreaker on Courage. Confused by his opponents strategy in the match, BQ heads to the corner to consult with Stump, giving Courage the opportunity to attack him from behind. Courage hit a punch in the back of the head, and stumbled back to attempt another attack. Courage attempts a tornado DDT, which BQ turns into a side slam. Courage rolls out of the ring before the pinfall attempt, and walks around the ring, stalling. As the ref's count approaches ten, Courage enters the ring once more, and the two men tie-up. Courage applies a headlock, and BQ reverses it into a side suplex. Courage rolls back to his feet, and hooks BQ with a lazy inside cradle which is easily reversed by the smaller man for the pinfall. Black Quicksilver rolls off Courage and grabs Stump, holding him up high for the crowd. Jake entered the ring now, and he looked in at Courage who was obviously weary and confused. With Jake helping, Courage leaves the ring in a stupor, as Warfare heads to commercials. Fallen's tough, but Poison is the weak link." Wretch nodded thoughtfully. "I think we can take all of these teams no problem... but what next?" Bear looked at Geoff Green. Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Glee Inside the ring the ring announcer got ready for the next Triple Threat match when suddenly the Flying Frenchie's music played. The Frenchie came out onto the ramp, holding the fWo Womens Title above his head proudly. The Frenchie beamed at the audience and walked to the ring, stopping for a moment every now and then to hang his head in disgust of the fans that they were letting into the arenas these days. He climbed into the ring and asked for a microphone, and then addressed the crowd. "Zut! What a lot of ugly Americans we have here today! Let me just say zat Masseur Armstrong was correct that ze only place zat is worse zan zis is that Canada. What a stinking country! But zis is not why I am here tonight. I am here about zis!" The Frenchie held up the Womens Title once more. "Now, I know zat you are thinkings, 'What an amazing feat zis Frenchie has accomplished winning zis title in such a tough match' and zu would be correct. It was a tough match for me. Still, it would not be fair to keep zis title because I am so superior to all of the... how do I say... prostitutes zat ze fWo has working for zim. So I have decided to give zis title up to the most worthy competitor. Glee? Can you come to zis ring?" Glee's music began to play and Glee excitedly came to the ring, beaming wide and hopping up and down and clapping her hands. She reached for the title, but the Frenchie pulled it back. The Frenchie waggled a finger in front of her and said, "No, no my little woman, zis is not for you yet... you are merely the first woman to be competing in my 'Frenchie Womens Title Challenge'!" Glee looked doubtful at this, but put up her fists in preparation of a fight. The Frenchie only laughed and shook his head again. "No, no... zis is not ze wrestling match! It is ze Challenge! You will do what I say and if you do zis thing zat I ask well enough, you will get zis title. Are you ready?" Glee nodded, but with less energy and more suspicion. "Excellent! Your challenge will be... to wash the Frenchie's new car!" A very muddy Toyota Four Runner was driven down the ramp toward the ring as ring attendants brought out hoses and pails of water. Glee looked at all of this with more suspicion now, and turned back to the Frenchie, who had produced a large white shirt. "And you must wash zis car quickly... and wearing only zis long white shirt!" The Frenchie handed the oversized shirt to Glee while the crowd roared with approval. Glee looked more skeptical than ever, but took the shirt and went to change. A few moments later she reappeared, barefoot and wearing the long white shirt. The Frenchie pointed to the water and the car, and she got started. Glee scrubbed the car well, and the Frenchie even helped by spraying water at the car for her. This water splashed off and got Glee very wet as well, turning her white shirt transparent under the spray. Glee grumbled, but then remembered what Joy would say and turned her frown into a nervous smile. Finally Glee finished the job, standing back in her dripping and very see-through shirt to admire the job that she had done. The car was spotless and shining, and the crowd roared with approval for both her job and her appearance. The Frenchie inspected it carefully, and nodded with approval. "Yes, zis is very good!" Glee grinned and hopped up and down again, causing various parts of her to shake and jump and making the crowd roar with approval once again. The Frenchie smiled as the car was driven away and turned back to Glee. "However you failed to clean ze inside of ze pipes. Therefore I have no choice but to disqualify you from title contention." Glee's face fell as the Frenchie turned to leave. Watching him go, Glee stood dripping as the Frenchie waved goodbye to his fans and blew kisses before vanishing backstage. Death vs. Darkness vs. Boston Strangler The arena goes black as Darkness enters the ring for the next match-up, a three-way elimination contest. He stands in the corner as The Boston Strangler is introduced to a mixed-reaction. Darkness attacks as soon as the Strangler enters the ring, picking up their long-standing rivalry. The two beat on each other considerably before a "4" appears on the TravisTron, bringing out Death, who was strangely alone. Death enters the ring cautiously, while the other two men remain focused on each other. As Darkness starts to gain the edge, Death strikes from behind with an inverted DDT. The eyes of Death and the Strangler lock momentarily, and then they start stomping away on Darkness. Death picks up Darkness, and he and the Boston Strangler deliver a double-team powerbomb. Strangler starts choking away on Darkness, but Death kicks him square in the head to gain the advantage. Death unloads on the kneeling Strangler, punching and kicking and spitting away on him. He turns his attention back to Darkness and goes for the cover, but Darkness kicks out before the three. Both men back up, and Darkness is caught with a jawbreaker from Death. Death goes to apply his Deathlock, but is met with a boot to the face to send him to the floor. Darkness up now, and once again he heads to the Strangler, who has returned to his feet as well. Darkness jawing at the Strangler, and poking his finger into the Strangler's chest, but the Boston Strangler pays heed to none of it. Instead, he wraps his hands around Darkness's throat and lifts him, choking the life out of him. He tosses Darkness into the corner, and unleashes a flurry of knees and forearms. Darkness crumples in the corner, and the Strangler continues his assault, stomping away on his foe. Death sneaks up on BS and delivers a german suplex with a bridge, earning a two-count. Death brings Darkness back to his feet, and grabs both he and the Boston Strangler by the hair. His attempt at a double-headbutt is met with a spectacular double-team chokeslam, and both men attempt the cover. Amazingly, Death gets a shoulder up, but not by much. Strangler attempts the cover again, but Darkness breaks it with an elbow to his spine. Darkness now focuses on the Strangler's lower back, hitting a series of backbreakers and a spinebuster without once attempting the cover. He gets the Strangler in a Boston Crab, but Death breaks it with a hard lariat on Darkness. Death punches away on Darkness as he gets back to his feet, but Death's punches don't deter an enraged Darkness. Darkness whips Death into the ropes and catches him with a hotshot, leaving Death writhing on the mat, grasping his throat. Darkness drives his knee into Death's neck, weakening him further. Darkness stops attacking Death when the Strangler makes his presence known, hitting a Somoan Bulldog on Darkness. The Boston Strangler goes to cover Death, but thinks better of it, and brings Death to his feet instead. He lifts Death in the air with a military press, but Death squirms out of it and lands on his feet. Death kicks Strangler in the gut and lands the Death Penalty, with a La Magistral cover for a two-count before Darkness breaks up the pin. Death starts yelling at Darkness, and whips him to the turnbuckle. He catches Darkness with a belly-to-belly as he rebounds from the corner, and makes the cover for only two. Death checks on the Strangler, who appears to be stirring, before bringing Darkness back to his feet and deliving the Death Penalty to him. Death gets the pinfall, eliminating Darkness. As he returns to his feet, he quickly searches for the Strangler, who is nowhere to be seen. Death slides to the outside and checks under the apron, and is met with a face-full of shovel. Laid out, the Strangler rolls out from under the ring and tosses Death back in, before locking Collins into the Boston Massacre. Unconscious, Death's arm drops thrice, and the referee awards the match to the Boston Strangler. The Strangler saunters out of the ring while Death regains consciousness. When his senses fully return, Death decks the ref for his shoddy officiating, and screams out that he will get his vengeance upon the Strangler. Result: Boston Strangler over Death, Death over Darkness Universal toys with Speed The Speed Demon sat on the side of the road and watched the cars carrying workers zip by. Few gave him any notice at all, but one slowed and allowed for the tinted windows of the limousine to roll down. Chris Universal stuck his head out of the window and looked around. Addressing the forlorn Speed Demon, Chris Universal said, "Hey, what in the hell are you doing here? You look like a freakin' loser!" The Speed Demon raised his head, his tear-stained face looking into Chris Universal's. "Have you... sniff... seen Nikki?" Universal rolled his eyes from inside the limousine and started to shake his head in the negative, but then an crafty look crossed his face and he replied, "Oh sure... she's back at the arena... said she wanted to talk to you! I think she still loves you, man!" The Speed Demon's face lit up with hope. "Really? Oh boy!" The Speed Demon jumped to his feet and started to run back toward the arena. Universal watched him go and shook his head in disgust. Lance Star now looked out the window as well, watching the superstar sprint back to the arena. Universal laughed. "God, what loser..." The limousine window rolled up, and the limo pulled away. Behind it the Speed Demon continued to sprint as fast as he could back to the arena... Scott Slugger (c) vs. Black Panther Warfare finally reached the time for the main event, a match in which the fWo World Title would be on the line. The challenger made his way to the ring, still fresh off his win at Fallout where he became the wrestler of the year. Panther climbed into the ring and awaited the champion, who came out to his usual music and the usual chorus of boos. Slugger wore the prized fWo World Title around his waist, and smiled cockily at the fans. Slugger slid into the ring and stared at Panther, not taking his eyes off of him for even a second. With his eyes locked on Panther, he managed to hand the fWo World Title to the referee and continued to stare a hole in the bigger Panther. Panther signalled that it was his time to become the new champion, by motioning to his waist. Slugger then pointed at Panther and told him to kiss his ass. With that, the match began and the fans were solidly behind the masked Panther. Panther and Slugger locked up in a collar and elbow tie-up, before Panther gained the advantage. Panther took Slugger down onto the mat with a headlock takedown, before Slugger escaped with scissors headlock, using his legs to wrap around Panther' head. Both men sprung up to their feet, and Slugger charged at Panther. Panther nailed him with a forearm shot to the face, before going for a vertical suplex. Slugger escaped and fell behind Panther, and then went for a waistlock. Panther escaped behind Slugger and went for a waistlock of his own, but Slugger resorted to dirty tactics with a mulekick. Panther clutched himself in pain, and bent over in the corner. Slugger attempted to take advantage by quickly going for the 'Curveball' swinging DDT in the corner, but as he jumped up onto the second turnbuckle, Panther countered into a Northern Lights suplex and maintained a bridge. The referee counted the pin, but Slugger managed to bridge out of the pinfall himself. He twisted Panther around in a position to where it looked like he was going for a stunner, but instead went for a snapmare takeover. He then nailed Panther with a kick to the back, before bouncing off the ropes and taking Panther with a sitting dropkick. Slugger followed with a pinfall attempt, two count only. Slugger used his versatile mat skills by applying a reverse chinlock, and working Panther over. Panther fought it off by getting back to his feet. He managed to nail Slugger with several elbows to the stomach, before sending Slugger to the ropes. Slugger returned and Panther ducked for a backdrop. Slugger leapfrogged him and then rolled him up into a pin from behind, but Panther kicked out rather quickly. Both men hopped back up to a vertical base. Slugger charged and was hip tossed off his feet. Slugger hopped back up and was taken down with another hiptoss. This time, Panther ducked for a backdrop and Slugger decided to take advantage. He went for a cradle piledriver, but Panther quickly backdropped out of it. Slugger followed through and managed to go for a sunset flip. Two count only. Panther jumped up to his feet and charged, but Slugger took him down with a drop-toe-hold. He then slapped on a anklelock, and worked over the legs of the Panther. He then turned Panther over and went for a sharpshooter. Continuing to work on the legs, Slugger confidently smiled and told the Panther to give up before he snaps him in half. Panther refused to do so, and fought his way towards the ropes. Just as he was about to reach the ropes, after using all the energy he could to reach them, Slugger let go, breaking the hold. Slugger slapped the back of Panther' head repeatedly, before pulling Panther back towards the middle of the ring. He split the legs, before hauling off and nailing him in the crotch with a clinched fist. Slugger then pushed the referee out of his way and went out onto the ring apron and up to the top rope. It proved to be a fatal mistake as Panther staggered to his feet and into the ropes, which crotched Slugger on the turnbuckles. Panther noticed Slugger and took advantage by climbing up onto the second turnbuckle. It appeared he was going for his trademarked superplex, but instead he hooked Slugger and drove him into the mat with a belly-to-belly suplex off the top rope! Panther remained dazed on Slugger, but the inadvertent pinfall attempt only garnered a count of two as Slugger draped his foot on the bottom rope. Panther rolled off of Slugger and remained on his back on the mat. The referee began a ten count. At six, both men reached their feet and charged at each other for a double-clothesline, both men going down at the same time. The referee began another ten count. At seven, Panther made it to his feet first. He brought Slugger up and whipped him to the ropes. He went for a big boot, but Slugger ducked. Slugger grabbed Panther by the head and took him down with a reverse neckbreaker. Moments later, he hooked a leg for a pin. Two count only. Slugger went back up to the top rope, and prepared to dive off, while Panther attempted to get to his feet. Slugger dove off, but Panther caught his cross bodyblock attempt and turned it into a fallaway slam. Panther then waited for Slugger to get up, and went for a clothesline, but Slugger pulled the referee into the way and the referee ended up on the mat unconscious. Panther looked concerned for the referee and Slugger went onto the floor. While this was going on, the ominous Death walked out onto the stage and watched with a great deal of interest. Slugger slid a chair into the ring and with Panther' attention diverted elsewhere, he smacked him from behind. Slugger then propped the chair in a sitting position, before he grabbed Panther and went for his patented finisher. As he went for the Curveball and sprung up to the second ropes again, Panther this time used his strength to prop him up on the turnbuckles. He then nailed Slugger with a few shots to the jaw and head, before folding up the chair and placing it towards the middle of the ring. He then signaled for his finisher... and hooked Slugger for it. He hit it, and went for the cover. The fans counted to a hundred, as Panther had the match won. The referee was still out cold, however, so Panther attempted to wake the referee up. The referee finally showed signs of life as Panther turned around. As he did so, Slugger was back up to his knees and he nailed Panther with a lowblow. With the chair still in place, Slugger quickly went for the 'Curveball' a third time and this time, it was the charm. He pinned the out cold Panther, and the referee counted to two and a half as the Panther kicked out at the last second. Slugger was annoyed and reached for the chair to hit the Panther with it. The Panther blocked the chair and took it away from Slugger. The referee got on his feet and spotted the chair in the Panther's hands and called for the bell. The DQ victory was announed for the champion and Slugger retained the title yet again, and Death continued to look on from the stage, as the show Warfare came to a close... and Panther screwed out of the title for the second time in a short span. But it was not quite the end just yet... Result: Scott Slugger by DQ Epilogue It was hours later, and the fans had gone home. The production and ring attendants had packed away the ring for the night, driving off to their next location in Detroit. The arena was cold and dark, and no sounds could be heard... aside from the shuffled steps of the Speed Demon. He roamed the halls, looking less and less certain as he went. "Nikki?" There was no answer. The Speed Demon's head began to slump as he called again. "Nikki?" Again, only silence greeted him. The shadows in the arena were dark and overbearing, and the Speed Demon dropped to his knees, his head coming to a rest on the cold concrete. "Oh Nikki... why..." Things were silent for a moment, and then a cold dark voice drifted out of the darkness. "Why indeed William Bailey... why indeed." The Speed Demon jerked around with a gasp, looking up into the eyes of a large, dark man. His face was cloaked in shadows, but the Speed Demon recognized it. "My god... it's you..." The large man looked down at the Speed Demon, seeming to trap his gaze with his. "That's right William Bailey... it's me... and I offer you revenge against that harlot who broke your heart... and I offer you revenge against everyone who ever did you wrong. Everyone who laughed at you and everyone who made fun of you... all you have to do is take my hand, and everything changes for you." The large man reached out a firm hand, laced with scars and cloaked in shadow. The Speed Demon looked up and the moment spun out. A single tear ran down the cheek of William Bailey, once known as the Speed Demon. Moving slowly, he reached up and took the hand of the dark man. The air seemed to grow colder as the dark man helped him to his feet, and drew him into the shadows of the arena. "You made a wise choice William Bailey... but that is your name no longer. From this day forward you will be called only... Demon." The laughter of the dark man rang out through the halls of the cold arena, and was soon joined by the screams of the man once called William Bailey. All across the world, the night grew colder.
