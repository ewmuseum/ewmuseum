A glare that Smyth, Cage, and Glass all saw. Slowly but surely, Logic drew himself up to his full height. The fans looked at the scene oddly. The emotionless Logic certainly seemed to be very emotional indeed right now, and at the moment, that emotion was directed towards referee Mark Glass. One of Logic's massive hands shot out, grabbing Glass in an Iron Claw. A shocked crowd of six thousand began to jeer, as Logic and Glass began a conversation that no one could hear. Were it possible for them to hear it, however, it would have sounded something like...this. "Why did you bump into me? Why did you stop me from executing Proof Positive? Why, Mark? Why?" The response came, albeit in a gurgled tone. "Frank......Smyth....he threatened my job if you hit the move...please don't hurt me. PLEASE!!!" The last word, the exhortation of "PLEASE!!" was all that the fans could hear. Thus, you really couldn't blame them for their continued jeering when the emotionless Logic angrily spun OCW referee Mark Glass around, and locked his arms around Glass' slender waist. PROOF POSITIVE. The official bounced off the canvas neck first, before landing on his stomach, motionless. It was at that moment that the guilt began to fill David Walter Smith. He had shot the messenger. In his mind, however, he was already justifying the act. The referee had lacked honor. Mark Glass was a good official. Even if he had been fired by Smyth, he could have found employment elsewhere. Smyth....Cage...the two truly guilty men in this entire debacle. They had made sure that Logic wouldn't be able to use his best move. They knew what Proof Positive could do. Whether they were trying to keep the title on Kinkade, or simply prevent Logic from using the move, David wasn't sure. Maybe it was both. Attacking Smyth and Cage would be useless, he determined. The best way to hurt Smyth and Cage would be to attack the company. Attack what they were trying to build. To attack Outcast Championship Wrestling's franchise. To attack Kellen Kinkade. Logic turned from the downed body of Glass, and now began to gaze at Kellen Kinkade, blissfully unaware of the now very, very angry three-hundred pounder behind him. Just like that, six thousand jeers turned to six thousand cheers. Behind the curtain, Frank Smyth and Brian Cage simultaneously went white. Dawn Kinkade on the floor didn't look much better, as she attempted to signal to her brother what was going on. The exhausted Kinkade glanced over his shoulder, and found Logic standing. Well, fuck. Kinkade, however, wasn't helpless. He had a nice shiny golden belt which he could use in self-defense. Logic reached for Kinkade's shoulder, and the champion swung over his shoulder, trying to catch Logic in the face. Sadly for Kinkade, he missed. The belt flew across the ring, and Kellen needed a new plan, fast. He tried a wild swing, but Logic kneed him in the midsection. Quickly locking arms around his waist, Logic took Kinkade vertical, and promptly sat down. Piledriver. The United States champion clutched at his neck, feet kicking in pain as Logic crossed the ring, and raised the US Title to more cheers from the Kinkade-hating crowd. The belt was then lowered, but purposefully. Right in the center of the ring. Smyth was about to rush down to the ring, but Cage stopped him. It wouldn't look good for them to be on camera. They hadn't been yet in OCW. Smyth gritted his teeth, and closed his eyes. Logic yanked Kinkade off the ground, forcefully. In an act of desperation, Dawn Kinkade leaped to the apron, but the look Logic shot her forced her down to the floor once again. David Walter Smith had lost his mind. Spinning Kinkade's back to face him, he hooked a battered Jersey Devil easily, draping one arm over his neck. Logic paused to relish the moment. This was OCW's champion in his arms. One of the fWo's greatest stars, ever. The man that the Survivor leaders wanted to build OCW around, after leaving fWo behind. Logic spat upon the OCW ring. Nothing had really changed. OCW was about him. It was about Doctor Curiosity, Brookes Davidson, Shawn Arrows. Men who had put up with Brian, Stevie, and Frank throughout the entire contest. Not fWo rejects like Rush and Kellen Kinkade, and certainly not idiots like the Midnight Cowboy. With a mighty heave, David had Kinkade airborne. All of the hatred he felt at that moment for what Outcast Championship Wrestling was, for the Survivor leaders, and for everything that had happened over the past six months came out as he drove Kinkade neck-first to the canvas. PROOF POSITIVE. On the United States title. Kinkade's momentum took him over, even after crashing neck first to fifteen pounds of gold, so that he balanced precariously on his knees. Logic spoke to him, but the words were drowned out by the mad cheering from the New Mexican crowd. By the time he got up, Logic saw that a camera was focused squarely upon him. It was time for fun. Looking squarely at the lens, his face returned to its angered expression, voice bellowing out to the heavens as he extended a hand, and then rapped his fist into his chest. "THIS IS ONLY..." Once again, Logic pounded his fist to his chest. "THE!" Both arms shot up, triumphantly. "BEGINNING!" With that, Logic saw the camera light click off. Judging his work for the evening to be done, he exited the ring, and began to walk down the aisle. Smyth and Cage dashed down the aisle, each man shooting the Pensive Punisher a glare that could have killed. This situation wasn't over, Logic realized. Fank and Brian would probably come up with more ways to cripple Logic's chances of winning. But for now, he was content to savor the scene in the ring. Mark Glass and Kellen Kinkade, both down, with Dawn Kinkade, Frank, and Brian all kneeling around the fallen champion. The only one he felt badly for was Dawn. She truly WAS an innocent bystander. As Logic disappeared behind the curtain, the question on the lips of the fans in the arena was simple. "Is Kellen Kinkade's reign over?" Somewhere in the back, a very excited Doctor Curiosity gave an exultant "JAH!"
