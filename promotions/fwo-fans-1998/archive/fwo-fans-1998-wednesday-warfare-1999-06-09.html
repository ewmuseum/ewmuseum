fWo — Card

Wednesday Warfare, 9 June 1999

9 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Quiet music played in the background as black and white footage was shown of the fWo one year ago. Cactus Jon walked to the ring in slow motion, holding the World Heavyweight title high above his head. Walking down the aisle to meet him was the masked Death, along with War, Famine, and Pestilence, who was drug along behind the massive Death against his will. Chris Finn and Scott Taylor interviewed superstars as they came down the entrance ramp, and the Total Eclipse ate the contents of a vendor cart. Austin Davis was shown piledriving Cactus Jon, and Markus Cairn and Hiroshima battled inside the ring. As the footage slowly came to an end, the sounds and lights of Wednesday Warfare came alive in Phoenix, Arizona as Wednesday Warfare officially took the air. Several fWo announcers were on hand, dressed in suits, to welcome the audience to the show. Chris Finn and James Armstrong took the play-by-play duties for the evening, while Matt Loco and Dick Venom were dispatched backstage to get comments and reflections on the evening from several fWo wrestlers. Scott Taylor was not in sight, until Geoff Green and the fWo camera crews discovered that he had been dispatched to the backstage area of the arena to assist with the loading and unloading of baggage. Scott Taylor seemed displeased with this job, and loudly complained about his situation. Chris Finn reminded viewers of the matches and events that occurred on year ago on the premiere edition of Wednesday Warfare, and noted that tonight would be a special tribute to that original show. Chris Finn announced that nearly everyone present at the original show would be on tonight's show in some capacity, either through interviews or matches. He then took the viewers to Matt Loco, who was backstage with former fWo wrestler Jim Dudley, who was on hand as one of the special guests invited to the show. Dudley was asked about the state of the fWo, and he responded that it seemed the more things change the more they stay the same... as Cactus Jon was in the World Title scene with a request for a World Title match against current champion Ultra Violet, and that the Dark Riders despite having some new faces were still causing trouble for everyone. Dudley said that the only big thing that had changed was that he wasn't wrestling in the promotion any more, and that was a big disappointment to all of the Dudley fans around the world. Loco asked Dudley if that would be changing at any point in the future, and Dudley only smiled and told the reporter that one never can tell what the future might bring. As the interview was winding to a close Austin Davis walked in front of the cameras, shoving Matt Loco out of the way as he went. Davis looked at Dudley and sneered, telling him that he should watch the beating he was about to give to the she-male and thank God he had hung the boots up so he wouldn't get the same kind of ass whipping. Dudley only glared back, as Austin Davis left the area on his way to the ring... Austin Davis vs. Sid Snow Pink strobe lights began to flash as small feathers began to fall from the rafters. Dancing his way out onto the entrance ramp was Sid Snow, who waved to the crowd and blew them kisses as he sauntered to the ring. Sid jumped into the squared circle, but much to the dismay of the fWo officials at ringside he demanded a microphone. Sid waited for the booing crowd to settle, and then breathed into the microphone. Sid ran his hands up his sides, and gasped, before slowly addressing the crowd with, "this is for all the women that want to be me... and the men that come to see me!" This gathered more boos as Sid Snow gyrated his hips for the crowd, and then told them to settle down once more. Sid Snow said, "Many of you out there are already aware of the fact that I'm a hard man... but are you aware that I'm hardCORE as well? Well Scott Slugger... I've seen what you can do with that big bat of yours, and now I'm challenging you to a hardcore match with the hardest man in the fWo... Sid Snow! But not just any hardcore match... I'm suggesting a department store hardcore match in two weeks at Fight the Future 2. Are you man enough to accept the challenge of Sid?" Sid began to gyrate for the crowd again, until the loud music of Austin Davis crashed over the arena loudspeakers. Austin Davis walked out onto the entrance ramp and sneered at the audience, before walking to the ring. Davis gave the announcing table the finger as he passed, much to the dismay of James Armstrong, who was waving politely. Davis climbed into the ring, and simply stared at Sid Snow, who was flexing and posing in the center of the ring. As Austin Davis looked on, Sid Snow grabbed the microphone again, and asked the crowd if they wanted to see a pose down between the two wrestlers. The crowd responded to the negative, but Sid ignored this and flexed again for the crowd, showing off his arms, abs, and butt. This gathered more boos, but Sid ignored them and pointed to Davis, telling him to "show what he's got". Davis slowly paced the ring, and finally took a position near Sid. Davis looked ready to pose, and then shrugged and kicked Sid in the lower abdominal region. Sid reeled backward and Davis caught his arm and tossed him into the ropes, taking him down to the mat with a hard lariat. Davis stomped on the head of Snow, who attempted to roll free from the attack. Davis picked Sid up and readied a back bodydrop, but Sid ducked under and ran to the ropes, coming off with a punch to the face. It didn't seem to effect Davis much, and he invited Snow to attempt the punch again. Sid took another weak shot at his face, but Davis again seemed not to be effected. Frustrated, Sid slapped Davis, which generated a quick response from Austin Davis, who grabbed Sid by the neck and slammed him down to the mat. Davis waited as Sid got to his feet, and then delivered a quick reverse DDT. As Davis got ready to cover Sid, Megan Dawn came out onto the entrance ramp to watch the match. Davis saw Dawn, and sneered at her as he asked where her "sugar daddy" was. Megan only shrugged, and slowly walked to the ring and toward Davis, causing the ref to leave the ring in an attempt to get Dawn to return to the backstage area. Davis watched Megan closely for signs of a trick, and failed to see an fWo camera man remove his hat and equipment and climb into the ring. Still holding the large camera the camera man, who was obviously Chris Universal, struck Davis in the back of the head with the camera. The damage done Universal made a quick escape, but not before draping Sid's arm over the chest of Austin Davis. Seeing that Universal's plan was completed, Dawn turned to leave the area, and the ref climbed back into the ring. At first confused by this turn of events, the ref counted to three and awarded the match to Sid. As the loud music of Sid played, Austin Davis roused and looked around for evidence of what had happened. The ref explained that Sid had won the match, and an angry Austin Davis grabbed him and tossed him out of the ring. The ref bounced and crumpled on the steel entrance ramp, as Davis turned his attention to Sid, who was just starting to come to. Davis planted a hard kick in the groin of Sid, commenting that "you won't be using that for awhile". Davis then went after the ref on the outside of the ring, chasing him to the backstage area and cursing the entire way. Result: Sid Snow by Pinfall Silver Fox and crew comment on Warfare Cameras turned to a small room backstage, where Dick Venom was with Silver Fox, Fyre, the Total Eclipse and the Chameleon. Dick Venom as attempting to get their comments on the anniversary of Warfare, and get some comments on what their future plans were in the fWo. The Silver Fox only laughed, and told Dick Venom that he would have to wait and see, for the game was just beginning and that it was important not to give any the plot just yet. The Silver Fox waved around to the individuals in the room, and told Venom that he only saw the surface, and that there were other chess pieces not yet on the board... like the student and the king. Venom attempted to interview Fyre, but as she began to speak Silver Fox cut her off, and ordered her not to speak to anyone and "know her place". She humbly submitted, and Dick Venom turned to the Chameleon and the Total Eclipse. The later was stuffing his face with chicken wings, and the Chameleon addressed the crowd. Dick Venom asked what what the Chameleon was up to, and he responded by saying, "What are we trying to do Dick? The same things we are trying to do every night... try to take over the world." The Total Eclipse added "narf" to the conversation, and Dick Venom prepared to end the interview... but before he could the door to the dressing room opened, "Twitch" walked into the room. Twitch smiled at Dick, and told him that he was pleased to be at Wednesday Warfare for it's anniversary edition, and commented that it was even better this year than when he was there the year before. "Twitch" then asked Dick if he wanted to know who he really was, and Dick nodded "yes" quickly. "Twitch" began to pull at the latex mask he wore, while Chameleon covered the camera lens... Flying Frenchie (c) vs. Briana When the cameras returned the ring Briana was already there, wearing a new pair of black and silver tights, as well as shoot fighting boots, a headband, and tape on her wrists. Briana stood on the turnbuckle and pointed at her waist, signaling that she would win the International Title that night. The music of the French Foundation played, and the Flying Frenchie came to the ring carrying a briefcase and accompanied by Mitch Wilson and Primetime, the fWo Tag Team champions. Primetime was accompanied by Nikki the Valley girl, who hung on the tag champion, giggling and rubbing his back all of the way to the ring. Primetime looked slightly tired, but very happy with himself and his current position in life. The Frenchie got into the ring and opened the briefcase, withdrawing the fWo International title and holding it up to the crowd. Behind him Mitch Wilson and Primetime posed with the Tag Titles, as the Frenchie shouted at the cameras that the French Foundation was the most powerful organization in the entire fWo, and held more gold than any other stable. He then told the crowd that not only would the gap between he and Scotty "Loser" Death widen, but that he would walk in AND out of the ring the fWo International champion. The bell finally rang, and the Frenchie wasted no time rolling out of the ring as Briana attempted to charge him. Pointing to his head, the Frenchie leisurely walked around the ring, mocking Briana from the outside. As he walked several fans jeered the French superstar, and his attention turned to them as he began to make fun of the audience. Briana used the distraction to climb to the top turnbuckle and launched off with a diving plancha into the Frenchie from behind. The Frenchie was driven face first into the steel barrier, and happy fans dumped bottles of beer on the International champion. Briana grabbed the head of the Frenchie and drug him to the ring apron, rolling him inside and preparing to follow herself. As she climbed inside Mitch Wilson grabbed her leg, preventing her from entering the ring and giving the Frenchie precious time to recover. Briana kicked backward and freed herself, climbing into the ring and going for a cover. She could only get a one count, as the Frenchie had recovered and was able to easily kick out. The two locked up, and the Frenchie grounded Briana with a quick drop toe hold. Holding her upper torso down, the Frenchie repeatedly dropped a knee into her head. He then grabbed her legs, and brought her over in an inverted bow and arrow pinfall attempt. The hold was not stable, and Briana was able to work her way free before the three count. As the Frenchie released the hold and backed up, Mitch Wilson handed him a pair of brass knuckles. The Frenchie palmed them and swung at Briana, but she ducked under and delivered a hard kick to her stomach of the Frenchie. The brass knuckles were dislodged from the hand of the Frenchie, and Briana grabbed it and delivered a hard uppercut to the Frenchie. The Frenchie fell backward unconscious, and Briana made the cover. The ref counted to three, and awarded the match to Briana. As she held the title up for the crowd, Mitch Wilson entered the ring and pointed out the brass knuckles to the ref. Seeing them, the ref yanked the title from Briana's grasp, and reversed his decision in the Frenchie's favor. He lay the title over the unconscious body of the Frenchie as Briana protested, and finally grabbed a chair and decked Mitch Wilson from behind. Primetime and Nikki drug the body of Mitch and the Frenchie out of the ring as Briana threatened the referee, and the cameras left the ring for the backstage area... Result: Flying Frenchie by DQ Mad Trucker says watch the Mad Trucker Out behind the arena a large semi-truck was parked in the fWo parking lot, where the Mad Trucker was giving some pre-match advise to the US Marshal, who was scheduled to debut inside the ring later in the night. The Trucker was going over some basic crowd techniques, including shouting TOOOOOOOOOOT versus cupping a hand to the mouth and calling TOOOOOOOOOT. The Trucker explained that each have their own meanings. Cookie and Alice arrived on the scene, and the Mad Trucker thanked them once more for rescuing him from Darkness. The Trucker wished the US Marshal luck, and sent him into the arena with a final TOOOOOOOOOT. The Trucker then turned to the camera and told the fans that this Friday he was going to step into a ring without ropes, but with barb wire instead. He reminded the kids watching at home that bouncing off barb wire was not something they should try at home, and that they should leave the hardcore matches to the professionals. At that moment fWo wrestler Joy walked by, muttering to himself and shaking his head. The Trucker slapped him on the back and told him that he looked upset, and Joy turned to the camera. Joy told the cameras that he had been watching the disgusting display of personal displays of affection between Primetime and Nikki, and reminded the children watching at home that pre-marital sexual relations had no place in the lives of happy Americans, or even Canadians. Joy told the Trucker that the two would never be happy with this sort of behavior, and that he was going to make it his mission to show the two the error of their ways, and introduce them to a new life of buttoned-up sweaters and chaperones on sanctioned dating activities like bowling or going to the library. The Trucker seemed confused by this, but delivered a good TOOOOOOOT to Joy for good measure in an attempt to cheer him up. As Joy made his way into the arena, the Trucker turned to the camera once more, and reminded the viewers to tune in on Friday for his big match against that evil Darkness. Cactus Jon vs. Brawler The cameras came back to the ring where the Brawler stood. Scenes were shown of one year ago when Cactus Jon, the fWo World Champion at the time, defeated the Brawler with little trouble. The Brawler addressed the cameras and informed the crowd that history would not repeat itself again. He flexed for the audience, who responded with bored booing, as Cactus Jon's music began to play. Jon, who has recently demanded a world title shot from Ultra Violet, came to the ring quickly carrying a Canadian flag and saluting the crowd. Jon stepped into the ring and quickly ducked to the side as the Brawler attempted a spear. Jon punched downward into the back of the Brawler as he passed, and picked the Brawler up from the side and snap suplexed him over the back and down to the mat. Cactus Jon hung back and waited for the Brawler to get to his feet, and then charged with a running drop kick. Cactus Jon attempted a cover, but was only able to get a two count. Cactus Jon got to his feet, noting that the Brawler did appear to be in better conditioning, and grabbed the wrestler in a side headlock. Jon worked the headlock in tighter, trying to cut off the oxygen of the wrestler. The Brawler began to fade, and finally dropped down to the canvas helpless. The ref checked the am of the Brawler, but Cactus Jon stopped him, mentioning that he didn't want the match to end that way. Jon then climbed to the top rope, and dove backward with a moonsault that found it's target. Cactus Jon covered the Brawler, and easily got the three count. Jon again climbed to the top rope, and was handed the Canadian flag. Jon waved it for the crowd, and told them that in just a few short weeks he would gain the fWo World Title, and return it to Canada where it rightly belonged. This drew a mixed reaction from the Arizona crowd, and Jon left to go backstage. Meanwhile, the ref checked on the Brawler, who coughed and pointed to the departing Cactus Jon. The Brawler told the ref that Cactus Jon looked strange to him, and his neck look chaffed, as if he was wearing a collar... or a mask. Result: Cactus Jon by Pinfall Eron talks with the commissioner, and a new face Backstage Eron the Relentless was found talking to the Commissioner of the fWo, Mr. Beaven. Travis was discussing with Eron some of the complaints he had about his treatment in the fWo so far. Travis said that he was looking at some of Eron's evidence he had gathered so far, and determined that there was not significant proof that he was being discriminated against by fWo staff members. Eron, angry with this response, pointed out that he could not wrestle a match without biased referees holding him back from victory. Travis told Eron that he would rectify the problem by assigning a special guest ref to upcoming matches, specifically the match with Oracle at Numbered Days. Travis told Eron that he would find the perfect person for the job, and sent Eron from the office. Travis picked up the phone and waited for a moment, before asking for Prophet. Meanwhile, cameras followed Eron out of the room where he walked down the halls, gathering dirty looks from other wrestlers and personnel as he went. As Eron passed a backstage exit door it opened slightly, and a head poked inside, looking carefully up and down the halls. A thin smile crept onto the face of the unknown wrestler, and he crept into the arena. In the light, it was obvious that the unknown person was in fact Mike Bear, and he crept into the building, and into the commissioner's office. Bear let the door close behind him, leaving the audience wondering what business the TCW wrestler had in the fWo... US Marhsal vs. War The lights dimmed, and the crowd hushed as War of the Dark Riders made his way slowly to the ring with the rest of the Dark Riders behind him. Stone, Plague, Scott Taylor and the massive Death walked behind him, each scanning the crowd for signs of the French Foundation or Silver Fox or one of his lackeys. Inside the ring War flexed and screamed to the crowd for the Marshal to come to the ring and take a beating, who responded with boos and jeers at the heel. As the group awaited the arrival of the US Marshal, the Warfare screen came to life and the Silver Fox came into view. The Silver Fox laughed at the Dark Riders and the crowd and asked if Plague was feeling well. Plague rubbed his arms, which looked red and still bruised from the beating Death gave him earlier. The Silver Fox then asked Death if he really trusted everyone around him, specifically Stone. Death turned slowly and looked at Stone, who shrugged and looked equally confused by Silver Fox's statement. The Silver Fox then turned the camera so the side of the room came into view on the Warfare screen, and Stone, or someone who looked like Stone, was bound and gagged on a chair. Death saw this, and turned back quickly to the Stone that was in the ring with him, grabbing her by the neck and bringing her close. Stone choked out that it was all a trap, and Death looked back up at the Warfare screen, and then once again at Stone. Angry, Death ordered that Plague and Taylor follow him, and the four left War in the ring as they marched up the entrance ramp to the backstage area. Death, not taking any chances, kept Stone held fast by the neck as they walked. With this drama unfolded War again yelled for the Marshal, who this time obliged him by coming out onto the entrance ramp and waved his hat in the air. The US Marshal was met with cheers, and he slapped the hands of fans as he walked to the ring, a smile on his face and a finger pointing warningly at War. Inside the ring the US Marshal went over the rules of the match with the ref, and was attacked from behind by War while he was distracted. War attacked on the US Marshal from behind, punching away with hard swinging fists. The US Marshal got to the ropes, and struggled to get to his feet. War went back into the ropes and rebounded off, but the Marshal ducked under his running clothesline and flipped him up and over, with War landing on his back. The Marshal again signaled to the crowd, and dropped and elbow from a standing position. With War grounded, the Marshal dropped a leg and then applied a Texas cloverleaf submission hold. War was too close to the ropes though, and easily extended one of his big arms to grab hold. The US Marshal quickly released the hold, not wanting to cheat in any way to get a victory. War slid to the outside and paced the ring, and the Marshal quickly slid to the outside after him. War caught the following Marshal with an elbow in the jaw, and then rammed his head repeatedly into the steel steps. The Marshal was dazed, and War rolled him back into the ring. War attempted a cover, but the Marshal kicked out easily on one. Frustrated, War attempted to set the Marshal up for a piledriver, but the Marshal kicked and stomped at the feet of War to get out of the attempted move, and delivered a quick shot to the jaw. War backed up out of the way of the Marshal, but this only opened him up for an attack, and the Marshal wasted no time applying a hanging suplex. Calling it the Hangman's Noose, it was enough to put War away, and the Marshal easily got the three count. He retrieved his hat and waved it to the crowd from the second rope of the turnbuckle, much to the delight of the fans. Meanwhile backstage cameras caught up with the other Dark Riders, who were hot in pursuit of Silver Fox... Result: US Marshal by Pinfall The hunt Camera crews caught up with the Dark Riders, who were quickly stomping through the hallways in search of Silver Fox. Death kicked open doorways and stormed into locker rooms, disrupting and angering various fWo wrestlers who threatened the Riders for their trespass. Finally rounding a corner in the bowels of the arena the Dark Riders found the Chameleon waiting, and fell upon him, holding him down and delivering hard punches to his face. Finally, with the Chameleon restrained, Death ordered he tell him where Silver Fox was. The Chameleon stammered out the location of one of the suites in the arena, and with the Chameleon bound the Dark Riders drug him to the location he specified. Outside the suite the Dark Riders used the Chameleon to bludgeon down the door, finding not Silver Fox inside but Primetime, Mitch Wilson, and Nikki the Valley Girl. The trio looked surprised at the appearance of the Dark Riders, who looked equally surprised at the French Foundation's presence. Laughing, the Chameleon ripped off his mask, revealing the Flying Frenchie beneath. Angry at this trickery, the Dark Riders stormed the room and joined the Foundation members in battle, forgetting for the moment that the imposter Stone was nowhere in sight, and ignoring the Frenchie who was dressed in the Chameleon's costume. The later slowly slipped away, watching the battle rage onward with a thin smile. He was nearly knocked over by another Frenchie, who ran to the suite angry and confused to find the Dark Riders laying siege to his sanctuary. The Frenchie in the Chameleon's costume smiled at this new arrival, and pulled yet another mask from his face, revealing the Chameleon once again beneath. He slipped away quietly, headed for the mysterious lair of Silver Fox. There the Silver Fox waited, along with the Total Eclipse, Fyre... who was dressed in Stone's outfit, and a mysterious man in the shadows. They watched a small television set with a direct feed to the French Foundation suite, where even now the battle still raged. Ultra Violet (c) vs. Black Quicksilver As Matt Panzer prepared to announced the main event there was a sudden commotion at the top of the entrance ramp. fWo security attempted to restrain someone, but she broke free and the crowd gasped as Briana marched to the ring. Briana got into the ring, grabbed a microphone and stood stoically inside the ring for a short time before starting to speak. Briana addressed the Flying Frenchie, who she said that she knew was busy at the moment fighting silly battles among foolish men. Briana told the crowd that she knew, and they knew, that she was robbed that night... and that the crowd knew she was robbed that night. Briana told the crowd that right now she should be the fWo International champion... a title that got honor from the Black Panther, and lost it from the Flying Frenchie. Briana informed the crowd that she had been backstage talking to the commissioner, and that he had agreed to a rematch between the two in one week, at the first Wednesday Warfare of the new season. Briana then said that because of the stupidity of the referee she had been screwed once tonight, and that it wasn't going to happen again, because she had gotten a special guest referee for next week, and that he is no stranger to the fWo. Briana then pointed up to the top of the entrance ramp, where a familiar masked man stood. The Texas Kid walked to the ring, wearing a referee shirt. Grabbing the microphone from Briana, he told the crowd that the Kid was back, and the fWo had better get ready. Finally done with this business Briana and the Texas Kid left the ring, allowing Matt Panzer to continue with the introductions. He introduced the Black Quicksilver first, who came to the ring wearing the WWF European title and carrying Stump. Black Quicksilver climbed into the ring and wildly waved Stump over his head, which generated a wild response from the crowd. The Black Quicksilver slumped down in the corner of the ring, and awaited Ultra Violet's entrance. Purple strobe lights shone down on the audience, as they got to their feet to welcome the fWo World Heavyweight Champion. Ultra Violet stumbled onto the entrance ramp, confused about how he could be at the first episode of Wednesday Warfare when he was at the show a year ago. Ultra Violet wore the World title around his waist, which tonight was not only upside down, but backwards as well. As a result, Violet followed closely behind, absorbed with her reflection in the title just over Ultra Violet's ass. This made for an awkward situation when Ultra Violet came to a quick stop on the ramp to pose for a picture with a fan. As Violet rubbed her nose with a painful expression on her face Ultra Violet resumed his trip to the ring, finally jumping inside with a giant "WHOOO-YEAH!" Ultra Violet paced around the ring sniffing at the air, finally casting a wary look at Stump and questioning Black Quicksilver as to why he carried someone's scalp to the ring. Black Quicksilver responded with a quick shot to the face, and grabbed both arms of Ultra Violet. Black Quicksilver then shouted that he did this move first, and proceeded to multiple pummel Ultra Violet in the skull with his head with the champion's arms trapped. This did little to stun Ultra Violet, who shrugged off the shots to his skull with a laugh. Ultra Violet broke free and began running around the ring, trying to find just the right point to attack. Black Quicksilver continued to turn around so he could keep facing Ultra Violet, the result of which is that both men became so dizzy that they fell down. As the ref laid on the ten count, Ultra Violet attempted to stand but fell over again. Black Quicksilver on the other hand crawled to his corner of the ring, and attempted to tag in Stump. He did so, and tossed Stump in Ultra Violet's general direction. It happened to land on Ultra Violet's chest, and the ref began to make the three count. The ref got to two before Ultra Violet realized what had landed on top of him, and screeched in horror. Jumping up and running around the ring wildly Ultra Violet screamed that he had part of Watermellu on him, and finally grabbed it and slung it out into the ground. Alarmed at this development, Black Quicksilver ran after it, momentarily forgetting that he was in the ring and proceeded in only running head-first into the ring ropes, which sent him snapping backward and down to the canvas. Ultra Violet meanwhile was breathing quickly, trying to recover from his earlier horror. As the Black Quicksilver hit the mat in front of him, Ultra Violet quickly went for a cover. He got a two count, but Black Quicksilver managed to kick free. Black Quicksilver then kicked Ultra Violet in the head, and ran to the top turnbuckle. Looking out at the crowd for signs of Stump, Black Quicksilver again underestimated the thick-headed nature of Ultra Violet's head. Shrugging off the effects of the kick, Ultra Violet came from behind and suplexed Black Quicksilver down to the mat. Grabbing the leg this time, Ultra Violet managed to get the three count. Ultra Violet then helped Black Quicksilver up to his feet, and asked for the microphone. He addressed the crowd and asked them to please check their personal items for signs of Wateru's head. A single hand raised in the crowd, and Rudo of the Lone Gunmen stood holding the Stump. Rudo yelled to the ring that he had Stump, and he wouldn't give it back until Ultra Violet agreed to face him in the ring on Friday. Ultra Violet, confused at this turn of events, told Rudo that he was already facing the other ninja. Cameras quickly went backstage, where a jubilant New Ninja jumped up and down in the excitement of his upcoming title shot before being told that Ultra Violet wasn't talking about him. Saddened, the Ninja slumped before the cameras returned to the ringside area. Ultra Violet and Rudo were arguing about the Stump, with Rudo telling Ultra Violet that he would not give him back the Stump until he wrestled him, and Ultra Violet was arguing that he would only face him if he DIDN'T give him back the Stump. Black Quicksilver, annoyed with Rudo, told him and the crowd that he didn't have the World title and his hostage taking of the Stump was stupid since Ultra Violet didn't want it anyway. The three continued to debate, with their argument growing more heated by the moment. With it showing no signs of abating any time soon, Geoff Green and the fWo camera crews took the one-year anniversary edition of Wednesday Warfare thankfully off the air. Result: Ultra Violet by Pinfall
