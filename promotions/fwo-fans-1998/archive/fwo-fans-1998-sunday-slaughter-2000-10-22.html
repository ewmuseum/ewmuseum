Sunday Slaughter, 22 October 2000

22 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pre-Slaughter Meeting fWo Sunday Night Slaughter opened up with a shot of a large office, presumably backstage inside the confines of the Providence Civic Center. A large table sat in the middle of the room, and several personalities sat in chairs throughout the location. Acting owner James Armstrong sat at the end of the table, while to the left four men sat. None of them were recognizeable, but they all looked like a bunch of attorney's. Armstrong hummed quietly as he ate some of his favorite treat that he loved so dearly. Before long, the door swung open and entered four other men. fWo Play By Play Announcer Jeremiah Frankton, fWo Color Commentator Stevie Roberts, fWo Columnist Dick Venom, and fWo Camera Crew President Geoff Green. Armstrong looked to be all business now as his colleagues joined him, and he moved the bun cake off his table, and addressed everyone. "Thank you all for coming. I'm sure you're all wondering what this is about. Quite frankly, so am I. Rather than keep you all in the dark, I thought it'd be best to tell you all what has gone as of late, and what the meetings I attended a few weeks ago were about. Apparently, a certain female individual claims that she has some incriminating photos of myself & Chris Finn participating in some sexual activities with some hookers. This apparently took place in Vegas, from my understanding. However, I don't remember doing anything like this when we were in Vegas in August. I and the men seating across from you attended these meetings with the attorneys representing the female individual. Basically, we were told this individual will settle out of court for one thing, and one thing only: Legal documentation freely passing the fWo on over to her." Frankton and friends looked at each other and sighed in shock, and Armstrong nodded. "Yeah. To be blunt, this bitch ain't going to get that. This is blackmail, and I won't stand for blackmail." Frankton posed a question. "And if you don't pass the fWo over to her?" Armstrong pondered the question, before answering it quickly. "Well, if we don't, she's going to inform Finn's wife of this, and I know he won't be pleased. I haven't spoken to him in a few weeks, and I don't think he even has been following the fWo as of late. So basically, I've decided that we're going to call this woman's bluff. And if she does contact Finn' wife - who cares, it's not going to hurt me, right?" Frankton and the rest rolled their eyes, before Armstrong tried to turn things into a positive. "I mean, how many photos can she possibly have of I & Finn screwing hookers, you know? This incident didn't happen anyways. She said it happened in Vegas, and the last time we were there, I was in the hospital all Saturday night from the car crash. So there!" The lawyers rolled their eyes as well, and Armstrong put his fist in the air. "So come on guys, let's band together, and go out there and give the best Slaughter we possibly can, okay?" Yeah, like that was going to happen. Ruben Ross vs. Black Quicksilver Sunday Slaughter begins inside the arena, as the sounds of "We're Not Gonna Take It" bring out the fearless leader of the Stump Squad. Black Quicksilver steps out onto the entrance ramp, along with what I guess you could call his manager, Stump. BQ continues until "Firestarter" by Prodigy starts up. The Black Plague himself, Ruben Ross steps up to a delightful response from the crowd. Ruben continues to the ring, steps in, and the bell rings. The competitors circle each other, and then lock up. Ruben takes BQ in a side headlock, and then shoots him off into the ropes. Ruben leapfrogs BQ coming back, and as he comes back, cacthes him with a swift dropkick that takes BQ off of his feet. Ruben continues the attack by bringing BQ to his feet, and bringing him down with a snap suplex. Ruben heads to the top, looking for a 450 splash, bu BQ moves out of the way. BQ brings Ruben to his feet, and nails a quick DDT. BQ hits the ropes, and then executes a springboard moonsault, which connects with the Survivor. BQ covers, but at two, Ruben gets a shoulder up. BQ brings Ruben up to his feet, and looks to hit another DDT, but the Black Plague counters into a northern lights suplex, complete with bridge. One, two, and at that point, BQ uses his arm to sweep Ruben's legs from out under him, breaking up the bridge, thus, successfully kicking out. BQ and Ruben get to their feet at the same time, and Ruben looks for a short arm clothesline. BQ ducks, and takes Ruben down with a belly to back suplex, before heading to the top rope. BQ files off with an elbow drop, connecting with Ross. He covers, and at two, Ruben kicks out. BQ lifts Ruben up and goes for a michinoku driver, but on the way down, Ruben beautifully turns it into a small package. At two there, however, it's BQ kicking out. Both men to their feet, and Ruben slides between BQ's legs. He gets up behind him, and drops him with a quick reverse DDT. Ruben covers, and once again, BQ kicks out. Ruben brings BQ up for a vertical suplex, but BQ gets out of it. He turns Ruben around, and drops him with a Cubic Zirconium Cutter. He gets up, and for some odd reason shouts "BANG!", before covering. One, two, and Ruben gets a foot on the ropes. BQ slams him to the mat, and heads to the top. However, Ruben has already had too much time to recover, and he crotches BQ. Ruben then takes advantage of the situation at hand, and climbs up after him. A superplex later, and Ruben covers BQ. However, Ruben can't quite pin BQ, as he kicks out at two. Ruben quickly brings Quicksilver to his feet. He looks for a Death Valley Driver, but BQ rolls out of it, and lands on his feet. He drops Ruben with an impaler DDT, and then covers. At two, however, Ruben kicks out, so BQ heads to the top. He looks for a guillotine legdrop, but it misses. Ruben gets behind BQ, and nails a reverse Fire Thunder Driver. Ruben then signals to the crowd, and he lifts BQ into position for the Plague of Darkness. However, when the vertical suplex lift comes, BQ gets out. Ruben turns around, ducks a BQ clothesline attempt, spins him around, lifts him up, and then executes the Plague of Darkness. Ruben covers, and the three count is academic. Ross wins the hard fought match, and as the referee raises his hand, Juno Law comes from the crowd with a chair. He nails Ruben in the back with it, sending him crashing to the mat. He hovers over Ruben, until BQ gets up, and spins him around. Juno ducks a BQ clothesline, and then nails a spinning heel kick to BQ, knocking him out. He prepares to do some damage with the chair to BQ, until the Red Dragon runs out, running Juno from the scene. Slaughter fades to commercials with Juno walking up the ramp, with a grin on his face. Result: Ross by pinfall Mike Bear vs. Greg Davis Result: Bear by pinfall Fore ... and the fWo Cruiserweight Champion Crusader made his way out onto the stage with a golf club in hand. As Davis became aware of the music, he pushed Clarisse off of him, and rose to his feet. His jaw dropped, and Crusader hit the ring. Cru swung, but Davis ducked. Cru swung again, and Davis again, ducked. Realizing he was fortunate, he started to flee the ring, but Clarisse was in his way. Clarisse slapped him across the face, and as he spun around, Crusader hit him with a stiff shot to the ribs, before hitting a double-arm DDT. He then went up to the top rope, and stared out into the sea of fans, before diving off and hitting the G101. Crusader then reached for the golf club, and as Davis laid on the mat, he went and stood near Davis' head. He then handed the golf club to Clarisse, and then split Davis by the legs wide open. Crusader then looked out at the crowd, who knew what was coming. Clarisse winded up, and swung downwards hard. Davis yelped in pain, and the fans cheered loudly. Crusader then asked for the golf club, and for extra measures, this time, he did the honors. Davis again yelped in pain, and then Crusader figured that he had enough, and pushed Davis aside. Crusader then walked over and hugged Clarisse, before the two exited the ring to an ovation of cheers, leaving Greg in the ring, helpless... and hurt. Jon Spratt (c) vs. Hellrazor Back from the commercial break where Result: Hellrazor by pinfall Ready? Backstage, Sunday Slaughter rolled on as Ric Chronos waited near the wrestler entrance talking to his friend, The Black Panther. Sphere did some light jogging outside the entrance a few feet away, before he headed inside the arena. Chronos looked at Panther and the two nodded at each other, before Chronos turned to head inside the arena. Panther put his hand on his shoulder and stopped him, before asking Chronos if he was ready. Ready for the strenuous task of wrestling just two months after suffering one of the worst injuries a wrestler could ever endure. Ready for the fans. Ready to be in the spotlight again. Ready to take the risk of having it happen again; and end up being on the shelf... again. Possibly longer. But there was no talking Chronos out of this, he had made up his mind. With a firm nod, emphatically Chronos responded with a "Yes.", before heading out into the arena... Ric Chronos vs. Sphere Iron Maiden hits the speakers, and Sphere heads down towards ringside, appearing to have a rather serious tone on this matchup. He slides into the ring and loosens up as Tones on Tail hits the arena. The fans hit their feet and cheer for Ric Chronos, ready for his first match since he was injured, and ready for his first-round Wrestler of the Year Tournament match. He salutes the fans, and makes his way down toward the ring. He climbs into the ring, and raises his arms in the air, as a "Chronos" chant begins in the arena. The bell rings, and the two lockup. Sphere gets an armbar, which is reversed by Chronos. Sphere reverses, and brings Chronos into the ropes. He whips him across the ring. Chronos hits the ropes, and bounces off to find Sphere with his head down. Chronos nails Sphere with a kick to the head, swinging it upward. Chronos then follows this with a clothesline, and then a cover. The ref counts, but only gets a two. Chronos picks up Sphere, and whips him into a corner. This is followed by a splash by Chronos, which brings a huge pop from the crowd. Sphere stumbles out of the corner, and right into Chronos grasp. Chronos locks him in, and nails a belly-to-belly suplex. Another cover gets another two. Chronos picks up Sphere, and locks him in for a vertical suplex. Chronos picks him up, and holds him up for roughly 15-20 seconds, before dropping him down into a DDT. A cover by Chronos gets two and a half, before Sphere just does get a shoulder up. Chronos looks up at the ref, who informs him of the break of the count. Chronos picks up Sphere, and lifts him up in a vertical suplex position again. This time, he sets him up on the top rope. Chronos climbs up, and keeps him locked in for a superplex. He lifts him in, somehow twists his body, and drops Sphere face-first for an inverted superplex. Chronos falls onto his stomach, knocking the wind out of him a bit. He winces, and takes a few seconds before he covers Sphere. One. Two. Two and a half. Kickout a blink later. Chronos is a bit surprised, yet undaunted. He goes to lift Sphere up again; yet, Sphere not only stays down, he brings Chronos down with him, into a small package. At two, Chronos gets out of it. Sphere gets up, but it's well-known that he is banged up from the inverted suplex. He charges Chronos as he gets up. However, Chronos lifts Sphere up with a back-bodydrop. Sphere lands on his feet; meanwhile, Chronos backups up a step, and locks Sphere into a neckbreaker. He nails it, and signals for the end. Chronos locks Sphere in for a powerbomb. He locks the right ankle behind the left knee. Chronos drops back, and nails the Chronosphere. He covers, and gets the three, and the fans go crazy. Ric Chronos, successful in his return, climbs onto the ropes and signals to the fans, as he moves on in the tournament, and Slaughter heads to a commercial break. Result: Chronos by pinfall Elephant Poo? Slaughter returned from the commercial break to find James Armstrong in his office alone, studying tapes of classic Lucha wrestling. Actually, we all knew that couldn't be true - Armstrong HATES Lucha wrestling. Instead, rather, he was watching porn, and looked to be enjoying what was on the television screen before him. The door opened, and entered Frank Snow. Who? Oh, right. The guy who claims he's really hardcore. One of them, anyways. Snow began to rant and rave about the Stump Squad, before demanding any type of match with the Stump Squad at Halloween Hell. Armstrong, however, has other thoughts. "Frank, I'm going to be frank, here. I can't do that, because you couldn't draw flies if you were covered in elephant poop. Got it? Now get out of my office." Armstrong went back to watching his porn, when Snow snapped. He grabbed a video tape box and threw it, before threatening Armstrong. He turned to leave once he realized Armstrong wasn't paying attention, and as he opened the door, a brick came flying and nailed him square in the forehead. The camera panned outside the office to see where the brick came from, and there stood the STUMP Squad with shocked expressions on each of their faces... even Stump's. BQ turned to Heftel and yelled with a "you shouldn't have done that" tone of voice. "I told you we could open the door without throwing bricks at it!" Armstrong groaned, and welcomed the STUMP Squad into his office. BQ said that he doesn't know what Dragon was doing earlier when he saved him from the attack by Law, but BQ assumed that Dragon was going to attack him. Thus, BQ wanted a match with him at Halloween Hell. Armstrong considered it, while a team of EMT's came over to check on Snow, and stretchered him out of the hallway. Finally, Armstrong told BQ no, considering that BQ & Dragon fought three times in September. However, he will put BQ in a match... on a team with Ruben Ross against Juno Law & The Red Dragon. BQ thanked him, but Armstrong just waved him off. "You're welcome, The." BQ turned around with a puzzeled expression, and asked him why he called him "The". Armstrong, as goofy as ever, responded with "Isn't your first name The? I always thought your name was The Blackquicksilver." BQ sighed and shrugged off the comment, before exiting with Heftel & Stump. As they reached the end of a hallway, BQ groaned. "The? The? What kind of idiot would name their kid "The?"" Heftel, always the opportunist, chimed in with his two pennies. "The same type of idiot that would name their kid Black?" Erik Kelly vs. Vengence The sounds of ELO run rampant in the arena, and out walks the big man Vengence. Boos rain down on him in a stinging pour. He climbs into the ring as "No Leaf Clover" hits, and the fans' boos turns to a line of cheers, and a hustling Erik Kelly makes his way to ringside. He slides into the ring, gets up, and sees Vengence charging him for a clothesline. Kelly ducks it and turns around. Vengence follows suit, and Kelly is waiting. He decks him with a closed right fist, which knocks Vengence back into the ropes. Kelly then gives a big boot to Vengence, which sends him over the ropes. Kelly turns around and the crowd cheers. However, Vengence, who kept himself afloat on the ropes, climbs back to the apron. He amazingly begins to climb to the top rope. Kelly turns around as Vengence has set himself. Vengence takes off from the top with a clothesline, and nails Kelly with it. A cover by Vengence garners a two count, before Kelly can break out of it. Vengence gets up, and pulls Kelly up with him. However, Kelly nails Vengence in the gut with another closed fist as he gets up. Vengence bends down, giving Kelly some time. Kelly locks Vengence in for a powerbomb. He lifts him up, and tosses him over the ropes, onto the Spanish Announcers table. It's a shame that the Spanish Announcers' table gets picked on so much, but that's the way it goes. The table crushes under the weight of Vengence, and the fans begin an "fWo!" chant throughout the arena. Kelly decides to try to inflict some incredible harm to the body of Vengence, as he steps over the ropes, and eyes up Vengence. He begins to run a few steps, and then takes off with a Foley-esque elbow onto Vengence, totally destroying whatever had been left of the Spanish Announcers table. The ref begins to count both men down. At five, Kelly begins to stir. He picks up Vengence, and goes to whip him into the steel steps. However, Vengence musters up all the energy he can, and reverses it, tossing Kelly into the steps. He slumps over, and Vengence hobbles over towards him. He picks him up, and tosses him into the ring. Vengence climbs in after him, and picks him up. He whips Kelly into the ropes, and follows up with the StarGazer. Kelly slumps over and Vengence calls for the end. He calls for the Eclipse. He picks up Kelly, and tries to lift him up. However, Kelly uses his weight to keep him down. Vengence tries again, but cannot do it. Out of seemingly nowhere, Kelly breaks the hold, whips around, and kicks Vengence in the gut. He then grabs the head of Vengence, and sets him up for the Eradication. "POWERBOMB!" is yelled, and the fans unite in a "KELLY! KELLY!" chant. However, before Kelly can pick Vengence up, Vengence uses his power to get out of it, and move his head to the side of Kelly. He lifts up Kelly, and begins to flip him. At the point that Kelly's head is next to his opponent, Vengence grabs the head of Kelly, and nails a modified Eclipse. He quickly goes for a cover. One. Two. Thre....foot on the ropes. Vengence can't believe it. He picks up Kelly, and decides to go for the real Eclipse again. He locks him in, and picks him up. This time, Kelly uses his momentum to fight the hold, and hits the mat. Vengence turns around again, and sees Kelly's boot headed for his sternum. Vengence bounces back; he won't fall for that again. Instead, Vengence takes off for the ropes. As he bounces off, he feels Kelly's entire leg in his gut. He slumps forward. Kelly uses this not to hit the Eradication, but a sitdown DDT. Kelly quickly covers Vengence, and the ref counts. One. Two. Three. No, Vengence' massive foot was on the ropes. What goes around comes around. Kelly sighs to himself, and picks up Vengence. Kelly whips him into the ropes. As Vengence comes back, Kelly gets ready to go for the Eradication one more time. This time, as he goes for the boot to the midsection, Vengence blocks it. He catches the boot, and spins Kelly around. Kelly turns around to find Vengence trying one of his own. Kelly catches it, and headbutts Vengence. Vengence falls backwards, and Kelly kicks him in the gut. Vengence falls forward, and Kelly locks him for the Eradication one final time. "POWERBOMB!" is heard through the arena. This time, the move goes off without a hitch. Kelly flattens him, and it's academic from here. He covers. The ref counts. One. Two. Three. Kelly wins. He sits up and celebrates for a short time. He celebrates for a short time only because he isn't given a celebratory time frame before another man makes a visit. The Boston Strangler. The Strangler stalks to the ring and slides in. He kicks Kelly in the head, causing him to fall frontwards back onto the mat. The Stranger wastes no time, locking Kelly into the Boston Massacre. He holds it in as ref after ref and security personnel after security personnel hustle down, trying to break the hold. They continue their unsuccessful attempts to do so as Slaughter heads to a break... Result: Kelly by pinfall Primetime vs. The Black Panther Slaughter heads to the ring for its main event, a big Lumberjack Match pitting two fierce rivals against each other. "Last Resort" starts to play, and Primetime's Future Foundation partners the California Kings, Lance Star and Courage, begin to make their way to the ring to a considerably disapproving reaction. "Eye of The Tiger" starts up, and Greg Davis, minus Clarisse for the moment but still recieving considerable boos, makes his way to the ring and joins the Kings. Davis was also noticeable walking slowly, as his crotch was still clearly damaged from the golf clubs shots he sustained earlier in the evening. The boos intensify as "O Fortuna" begins and the Boston Strangler makes his way to the ring, a maniacal look on his face - as is typical. He joins the others, who warily avoid the nutcase. "Paint It Black" then starts, the boos turn to cheers as Wretch begins to make his way to the ring. He looks reluctant to be a part of Primetime's group, but nevertheless takes a spot at ringside, though away from the both the Strangler and the trio of the Kings and Davis, who are chatting amicably. "Christian Says" then starts up, and the cheers continue as Ric Chronos makes his way to ringside, taking an opposite side from Primetime's group. "Switchback" then hits, and Crusader makes his way to the ring to more cheers, joining Chronos. This is followed by the playing of "Break Stuff", signalling the cheer-laden entrance of the mammoth Erik Kelly. This is proceeded by "Dragula", and the entrance of Undergrounders Mike Bear (accompanied by Brittany) and the Flying Frenchie, who make their way to the ring together. "Snap" then starts playing, and the fans shower the ring with boos as Primetime makes his way to the ring, looking arrogant as ever. He rolls into the ring and awaits his opponent. "My Hero" begins, and the cheers start up as the Black Panther begins to make his way to the ring, and if ever one could detect a facial expression under a mask, it's now - he's pissed. Panther gets about halfway to the ring, then says screw it and charges. He quickly springs up to his feet, cat-like, and begins engaging in fisticuffs with Primetime as the bell rings. Panther's anger gains him the edge, and he grabs Primetime and whips him hard into the ropes. Primetime comes off, and Panther grabs him and plants him into the mat with a ring-rocking powerslam. Instead of covering, he mounts Primetime and begins wailing away with rights and lefts. After doing this for a good long period of time, he finally gets off and raises his fists to the crowd. He then brings up Primetime and hurls him out to the face lumberjacks. They immediately pounce on him, raining fists and feet, the attack dominated by Chronos. Primetime quickly slides back into the ring, only to meet a kick to the face from Panther. He brings Primetime back up and shoves him into the corner, before charging in with a lariat. It connects, and Primetime slumps from the corner, stumbling out into the center of the ring, before being grabbed by Panther. Panther double underhooks him, then takes him over, slamming him to the mat with a suplex. Panther then goes for the first cover of the match, and garners a two. He brings Primetime back up, and takes him over with a snap suplex. He quickly heads into the corner and leaps onto the second turnbuckle. He then leaps off, dropping a leg across the throat of Primetime. Panther then covers and hooks the leg, but Primetime kicks out at two. Undeterred, Panther pulls him back up. Catching Panther temporarily offguard, if only for a split second, Primetime gives Panther a hard forearm shot that may have been to the gut or perhaps to the area just south of the gut. In any case, it's good enough to double Panther over. Primetime grabs Panther by the mask, runs him to the ropes, and hurls him over the top to the outside before collapsing back into the nearest corner to recover and enjoy the view. The heels immediately begin to open up on Panther, though Wretch abstains from it, watching briefly, before physically pulling off a few members of the group. As they begin to argue, Panther takes the opportunity to roll back into the ring, but the damage has been done. Primetime exits the corner, then brings Panther back up and takes him down with a snap suplex. He immediately brings Panther up and dropkicks him square in the mask, knocking him to the mat. Primetime quickly heads to the outside and climbs onto the top rope, before measuring Panther. He then flies off with the Ratings Drop splash, nailing it squarely. Primetime arrogantly makes a cover, forgoing the hook of the leg, and as a result, Panther kicks out at two. Primetime is upset, but decides to take his anger out on Panther. He brings him up and slices him with a hard knife-edge chop, before kicking him in the gut and running into the ropes. Primetime leaps high into the air and drives Panther to the mat with a rocker dropper, before covering and opting to hook the leg this time. It gets a one... two... and a last-second kickout from the larger Panther. Primetime gets up and slashes a thumb across his throat, signalling for the Snapshot. He brings him up and hooks him, but at that moment, Mitch Wilson, belt around his shoulder, strolls onto the stage, causing a hail of boos from the fans. Primetime hears these, drops Panther, and turns, before seeing his enemy. Mitch smirks sardonically and offers him a wave in mock greeting. Primetime is enraged and leans over the ropes, pointing at Mitch and yelling a series of quite blue words at Mitch, most of which begin with motherf and end with ucker. As Primetime yells at Mitch, lumberjacks from both sides begin heading towards the entryway, as, no matter what their affiliation, it is their duty to prevent interference. Frenchie, also, is no big fan of Mitch's anyway. As the lumberjacks threaten Mitch, who stays at the top of the ramp, they begin to realize who they are standing near and almost immediately a brawl breaks out, gaining the attention of the referee. Crusader goes after Greg Davis, Kelly charges the Strangler, and Chronos, the Underground, and Wretch (temporarily coexisting with Bear) converge on the California Kings. Primetime never sees Panther coming up from behind him and hooking for the Killer Instinct. He lifts Primetime up, but somehow Primetime slides down his back. As Panther turns, Primetime grabs him, lifts him up, slingshots him, and Snapshots him. On the outside Chronos sees this and leaves the fray, grabbing a conveniently located chair on the way. Chronos hits the ring, charges at Primetime, and swings the chair. One problem. It connects with the skull of the just-getting-up Black Panther, knocking him back down again, for good. As Chronos checks over his fallen friend, Primetime blasts him with a superkick, knocking the chair back into his face and him over the top rope to the outside. Primetime then brings Panther up and plants him to the ground. With a Headliner, drawing the ire of a watching Wilson. Primetime then heads to the top, waves to Wilson much as Wilson earlier did to him, then yells at Frenchie, causing Frenchie to turn and see him fly off the top with a breathtaking 450 splash that connects squarely with its target. Frenchie curses (in French, of course), and Primetime casually taps the ref on the shoulder and goes over to make a casual cover. One. Two. Three. Primetime is the winner. The second the bell rings, all the lumberjacks spill into the ring. Wilson rushes the ring, carefully unstraps his belt and sets it aside, and then continues rushing into the ring. All hell breaks loose with the Cali Kings brawling with Chronos and Panther, Damnation and Eddie rushing the ring to help Chronos and Panther, Crusader and Davis trading fists, Bear and Wretch going at it, and fWo World Champion Mitch Wilson, Primetime, and The Flying Frenchie advancing towards each other with murder in their eyes. Slaughter goes off the air on this clusterfrickerrific scene... with Halloween Hell a mere six days away, and one of the biggest matches in the fWo's near three year history between Wilson, Frenchie & Primetime set to take place. Result: Primetime by pinfall
