Wednesday Warfare, 12 February 2003

12 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Previously... The road to CyberSlam is filled with twists and turns. Erik Kelly continues to chase the fWo World Heavyweight Champion Spyder and his stable the Triple Threat. Now, having beaten Fallen and Neo he stands at the cusp of getting the rematch he so desperately desires. But the Triple Threat still has tricks up their sleve, and are vying for the fWo World Tag Team Championships as well, taking on Triality in their bid to control the organization. In addition, number one contender to the World Heavyweight Championship Kodiak Vic Creed has an unexpected challenge in the form of Eron. With so many explosive elements, anything can happen. With tensions and frustrations mounting, the fWo finds itself in Brazil and early arrivers to the arena have been shocked at the massive crowd that has formed to watch Wednesday Warfare. But is the crowd here to watch wrestling, or has something terrible gone awry that will cause further harm to the promotion... Welcome to the show "Excuse... well, pardon me..." Silver Fox edged toward the building trying not to bump into what appeared to be a massive amount of excited, anxious fans. His eyes were wide... getting attendence in this world tour of theirs had been tough, but unless he was massively mistaken this appeared to be a sell out. Standing room only. Hell, they might actually have to turn people away from the building with this kind of response. Had he known this was the kind of crowd he could draw in Brazil... well who needed the US anyway? Spotting Samuel Smooth standing a small distance away from the building he waved and headed over toward his fellow road agent and manager. But Smooth didn't have the kind of excited expression that Fox would have expected... in fact, Smooth looked more dismayed that anything else. Perhaps he was just peeved because the crowd wasn't there for him. It would follow suit with Smooth's character... the jealousy ran deep. "Hey, would you look at this crowd? This is the best turnout we've had since... well, since this whole thing started up! Can you believe this?" Smooth looked at Silver Fox with a wan smile on his face. "Yeah, it's great. But..." "But what?" Silver Fox shook his head in disapointment. "Listen, you're not a star anymore. But you provide a valuable, valuable contribution! Don't you see that? It isn't all about what goes on in the ring." Smooth let out a harsh laugh and shook his head. "Man, stupid cracker never gets anything. Man, it IS all about what goes on in the ring. Tonight anyway... here, look at this." Samuel Smooth handed Silver Fox a local newspaper. Looking confused and slightly annoyed, Silver Fox glanced at the full page ad. "So? It's an advertisement for soccer. That sport's big down here, Smooth... and this would be one of the first games of the year. So?" "So?" Smooth pointed to the ad. "Look at the date." Silver Fox did. It was for February 12th... today. "So there's a big soccer game going on today. Frankly, that makes this crowd even more impressive. Hey, good for us I say." Smooth shook his head. "Look at the arena it lists. This arena. Parque Santiago stadium." "What?" Silver Fox's face went an instant angry red. "But those bastards promised us this arena! This is... fuck, what are we supposed to do now? We're on the air in thirty minutes! What arena are we supposed to go to now?" Samuel Smooth laughed his harsh laugh, "Look at the ad closely fool, this is our event. See where it lists the fWo? Only problem is that this event is advertised as a soccer match... not a wrestling show. These fans are here to see us... but they think they are here to see soccer. Dig fool?" Silver Fox looked around at the crowd as realization began to sink in. The crowd around him was excited, noisy, and anxious. Fans were waving soccer banners and carried soccer balls and gear. He looked back at Samuel Smooth with mounting alarm. "These people aren't going to be happy when they see they've just paid to see a wrestling show are they?" Samuel Smooth shook his head slowly. Silver Fox let out a long sigh, and felt the onset of a massive headache coming on. Contract signing Warfare faded in on Matt Panzer, the announcer, who cleared his throat impressively and looked out at the crowd. They looked angry, and when the opening Warfare theme ran he noticed that several loud soccer fans had gotten up to leave. "Ladies and gentlemen... I introduce at this time representing the fWo World Heavyweight Champion and the men who will challenge the fWo Tag Team Champions this Sunday at Slaughter, Neo... Fallen and Spyder... the Triple Threat!" The three men came out onto the entrance ramp followed closely behind by the silent and coniving manager Dina Sol. The crowd booed heavily, but if it was a result of the team or the fact that they were not coming out onto the ramp to play soccer... it was hard to say. The heel stable walked toward the ring, ignoring the fans and their reaction as the spotlight stayed locked on the four of them. Spyder rolled into the ring, followed closely behind by Fallen and Neo. Outside, Dina Sol paced the ring and looked anxiously up toward the top of the entrance ramp. Matt Panzer, who had stayed in the ring during this introduction, was joined by two referees and a man in a suit bearing a small wooden stand. The man set the wooden stand up in the center of the ring, then retreated to one side and like the referees and Dina Sol, looked up the entrance ramp with some aprehention on his face. "And, introducting next..." But Spyder grabbed the microphone out of Matt Panzer's hand. "Esse... esse, give the people in the ring the proper respect! You are looking at the past, present and future of the fWo! The one time leader of the promotion and fWo World Heavyweight Champion, the lord of the dance, the sexy beast... my name for any of you gringo idiots who do not know it is Spyder! I am your king! Hail, hail to the king!" Spyder stretched his arms wide and grinned to the crowd, but the response was still negative and Spyder's grin soured as he continued. "On either side of me are the men that this Sunday at Slaughter will take the fWo World Tag Team Championship belts from this Triality team... the two pretenders to the title that will fall before the combined might of the triple threat! Mommy, stop me... it just hurts so bad to be this good!" Fallen and Neo raised their arms to the crowd but before Spyder could continue the lights dimmed, and though the majority of the crowd didn't know that "Let The Bodies Hit The Floor" could signal the arrival of only one man, the Triple Threat at once turned their full attention to the man who pushed his way through the curtains. Erik Kelly. Once the champion, this Sunday night the challenger. Behind him strode Victor Covington and Carson Covington. The current fWo Tag Team Champions. The three of them would step into the ring against the Triple Threat later that night in a huge six man tag team contest... but for now it was all about the signing. The three men strode down the ramp, and their gazes were locked on the Triple Threat inside the ring. Behind Erik Kelly and Triality came Silver Fox, Samuel Smooth, Twitch and Thumper. Behind these four came ten armed security guards... part-time police for the Brazil offical security team, tonight here to do nothing more than maintain order. A bigger task than they knew. At ringside Erik Kelly glanced up at Spyder with hatred on his face, then swung himself up and into the ring. Behind him Triality entered the ring, and soon the three stood toe to toe with the Triple Threat. In a matter of weeks enemies had been formed that would last a lifetime. Kelly grabbed the microphone from Spyder and shouted into it, letting his feeling be known to all present. "Masked man... bug... this Sunday I'm going to squash you like I did at BodyCount, except this time your little surprise buddies here aren't going to be able to save you. This Sunday I will defeat you, and I will go on to become the three time fWo World Heavyweight Champion!" Kelly paused to let the audence react, but the reaction was muted at best as angry fans still looked for some sign of the soccer match they had been promised. Non-plussed, Kelly continued. "Only two men have ever held the title more than three times, and that's Scott Slugger and Ric Chronos. It's going to stay that way for a while too, because after I beat you this belt isn't moving from my waist. Not for you, not for Kodiak Vic Creed, not for Eron, not for anyone! But little man, if you want we can just scrap this whole six man tag match tonight, and I'll take you on right here, and right now. Just say the word!" Spyder leaned in with a sneer on his face and showed little fear in staring down the challenger to his title. "Yeah esse? Fine by me... let's go... right here, right now!" But before anything further could happen Silver Fox stepped between them wearing a stern and concerned expression on his face. He too held a microphone, and was quick to stop the escalating problems. "Hold on a minute... as much as I'm sure these fans would love to see that match...," and here Silver Fox looked around doubtfully at the crowd was seemed to be getting smaller by the minute, "it will have to wait until this Sunday at Slaughter. Slaughter will be a night of champions, and the two biggest titles in our business will be on the line. Your World Heavyweight Championship Spyder, and your Tag Team Championship belts, Triality. Right now we are here to do nothing more than sign these contracts. You want to work out that aggression? You will have your chance... later tonight when Erik Kelly and Triality takes on the Triple Threat. No holds barred. Now, let's sign these papers." Spyder and Erik Kelly didn't look away from one another, and as one by one Fallen, Neo, Carson Covington and Victor Covington signed their contract they remained with their eyes locked and faces set. Then, finally, Spyder looked down and picked up the pen to sign the contract and make it offical. He turned back to Erik Kelly and held out the pen. As Kelly took it, Spyder slapped him in the side of the face. The response was immediate. Erik Kelly sprung forward and grasped Spyder's neck. Neo and Fallen fell upon Carson and Victor and the four began trading punches. Desperately security, Twitch, Thumper, Samuel Smooth and Silver Fox tried to pull the six men apart... but it was little use. Finally the Triple Threat was driven from the ring, and Erik Kelly, breathing hard and furious... his face red with rage... grabbed the contract. He shouted out to Spyder, who was already heading back up the entrance ramp. "Fine... tonight I'll rip you and your little friends apart... Sunday I'll take what's mine!" Erik Kelly raised his arms into the air as Carson and Victor Covington both climbed turnbuckles and followed suit. On the entrance ramp the Triple Threat exchanged glances, then vanished behind the curtains as Wednesday Warfare faded into a commercial. Own Goal. "Ken, John... I don't think I need to stress how important you guys are going to be tonight, when these fans come to realize that they aren't going to get a soccer match, we could be looking at a full scale riot... so please, keep things tight." Silver Fox sighed, despite his absence the last show had gone relatively smooth, it seemed all too convenient that upon his return, disaster had struck. As Ken Armstrong and John Willis turned and paced away discussing security procedures, Fox shook his head and turned to Touma, who stood before him clutching a clipboard. "And now you, Touma... I have a special job for you, as public relations... it shouldn't be a problem for you to call up the publishers of this paper and find out who placed that damned ad, because when I find out who it was... they'll be relocating from whatever sunny beach they're sitting on to a nice cold court room." Fox scowled as Touma took out a pen and prepared to leave... only to be interrupted by the sound of clapping. Silver Fox spun around to find Kellen Kinkade, leaning against the corridor wall with an arrogant grin plastered across his face. "I have to commend the setup here Sherlock, sheer genius... first you deploy the goons and now we have Dr. Watson here making some phonecalls, brilliant... I can only wait with baited breath for what you do next, the Scooby Gang should be along any second." Kinkade said in an abrasive manor, as Fox bit his lip. "Let me guess... you have something to do with this." Fox sneered through clenched teeth. "Ah... and I would have gotten away with it, were it not for those pesky kids." Kinkade chuckled "Of course I do dipshit." Fox grew red with fury. "Then I have one question for you Kinkade, and that is why... why do this to us?" Kinkade shook his head. "The question you really want to ask Fox, is not why... but why not? It only seems right that after two weeks of playing the chaos card on me, I should return the favour... no transportation, a no show from you and shambolic wrestling conditions... you've seriously hindered my little game with Scott Slugger, and as result his capitulation will arrive later rather than sooner, which is not how it was meant to be." Kinkade answered, stepping forth to stare Fox in the eye. Fox was not impressed. "Very impressive, and not once do you mention the fact that you are blatantly dodging Scott, you have been since Bodycount and everyone knows it, what's the matter Kellen? Are you afraid that he's going to come along and squash you? Make that little stunt that you pulled at Bodycount seem like a distant memory, face it Kinkade... if he beats you, you drop right back to square one." Fox wore a smug expression, but he was soon glaring again as Kinkade stepped forth and prodded him in the chest. "A word to the wise... there's an old saying that my Father used to bust out now and then... don't sow what you don't want to reap, right now you're sowing the right seeds for a punch in the face." Kinkade snarled, his glass eye shining under the corridor lights. "And a word to those who would do well to be a damn sight wiser, my Father used to bust out an old saying too... don't bite the hand that feeds you Kinkade, now get the hell out of this arena and get out of my sight, you're suspended." Fox folded his arms defiantly. Kinkade smiled, defiantly. "I like that saying Fox, but it's not good as another old favourite of mine... don't let your mouth write checks that your ass can't cash, if you'd care to take a glance at my contract... you'll see that the only one man that can suspend me, and he's the man that brought me here." Fox narrowed his eyes. "And he would be?" He questioned. "Fuck you... you can wait." Kinkade replied abruptly, Fox rolled his eyes... he'd set himself up for that one. "But I will take up your offer to get out of the arena, after all... by the looks of things, in about five minutes the shit is going to hit the fan and a couple of thousand fans are going to be yearning for a taste of your blood... so I'll leave you here to deal Fox, have a good night!" Kinkade chortled, turning and walking away. Fox watched on in dismay, as Kinkade turned back one last time. "Oh... and a little advice, if you do decide to throw some soccer related crap out to please the angry mob, I might suggest you have Scott Slugger fill in for a great soccer player in Ronaldo, after all... they have a lot in common, they're both up there as two of the greatest athletes in the world. And there both there as the ugliest motherfuckers in it too, you think I'm dodging Ragnarok? I'll be around on Sunday, Fox, then we'll see who's dodging who... now hurry on out there, there's a bunch of fans waiting to see you score." Kinkade winked, turning away and leaving as Silver Fox contemplated his revenge. Martyr vs. Aimz As the show came back from commercial, Aimz was already in the ring, waiting for her opponent. The crowd was still applauding her, partly because of her looks, but mainly because of the heart and ability she displayed routinely. The look in her eyes was telling of her nerves. She knew that she would face one of her biggest challenges yet in the fWo- a fight with the vicious and cunning fWo Hardcore Champion, Martyr. As "I'm A Monster" began to play, the fans turned their cheers to jeers, with even more fervor. In his short tenure with fWo, he had already become a hated man, an image that Martyr did not outrightly seem to mind. In fact, as he stood on the ramp, peering out into the diminishing crowd, one could swear that he was actually enjoying it all. And the grin that soon appeared on his face negated all other thoughts. As he walked down the ramp, two more men appeared from behind the curtains, and were given the same treatment from the audience. Scott Slugger and Mitch Wilson weren't fazed, either. The two thirds of Ragnarok didn't follow Martyr, however, and simply stood at the top of the ramp, observing what was to unfold. Martyr drew nearer and nearer to the ring, one hand removing the championship belt that he had fought so hard to earn from his shoulder, and throwing it next to the ring steps. Martyr then began a slight sprint to the ring and jumped on to the apron, soaring over the ropes and into the ring with a springboard lariat that hit its mark, prompting Aimz to hit the mat and the timekeeper to call for the bell. Immediately, Martyr was up and stomping away at Aimz's knees, searching for a weakness. Amy clutched her knee, writhing in pain, and Martyr took the opportunity to lift her by the hair and send her to the ropes. As she came back, Martyr gave a kick to the gut that caused her to reel backwards, both hands around her stomach, showing sheer agony. Martyr brought her back down to the mat with a solid swinging neckbreaker, then tried to get an early pinfall. The referee barely had to drop to the mat, however, as Aimz had kicked out within seconds. Her fighting spirit evidently once again coursed through her veins as she hopped to her feet and sent several punches Martyr's way, backing him up into a corner. There, Martyr got several more kicks to the gut before Amy climbed up to the second rope and gave her opponent six punches to the face before locking his head between her legs for a frankensteiner. Martyr, however, had other plans. He quickly ran forward and brought Aimz down hard with a sit-down powerbomb, her head nearly missing the opposite turnbuckle. Martyr remained in that position, and went for another pin, this one bringing slightly more favorable results, earning him a two-count. Frustrated, Martyr stood again and dropped to the mat, applying an inverted STF to the suppine young combatant. After a few of Aimz' cries of torture escaped her throat, Martyr released the hold and stood up again, this time going to the top rope. Once there, Martyr sat, perched in wait, while Aimz got her bearings. Amy looked up just in time to see the cross-body block coming her way, and reacted accordingly, pushing Martyr into the corner with a hard spear that caught him in mid-air. Martyr was shaken, but fought back quickly, giving Amy a few kicks to her side. Aimz caught the third one in her hands, but Martyr spun around with an attempted enzuigiri. Aimz, however, telegraphed the maneuver, and ducked, releasing Martyr's foot. Martyr spun around, disoriented, and this gave Amy the perfect opportunity to lock up from behind, for a German suplex. Martyr was just as quick, alternating positions with Aimz by going behind her. He then pushed her forward, sending her running into the corner, and following in. Aimz regained control fairly quickly, though, and hopped onto the top rope, then back off, catching Martyr with a tornado DDT that planted his head firmly into the mat. The fans erupted as Amy made the cover, urging the ref to drop to the canvas, as well. One... Two... No! A stern kick-out nanoseconds before the third slap occured cost Amy the victory. Aimz was relentless, bringing Martyr to a standing position, then throwing him to the ropes. Martyr nearly ran into a hip toss from Amy, but instead slid under her arms, coming up behind her and hitting a textbook Russian leg sweep that brought her down hard. Without wasting a moment, Martyr grabbed his adversary's leg and applied the Atrophy, waiting for Amy to tap. To his surprise, however, the hold was broken within seconds after Aimz's hand reached the ropes. Martyr's stern expression revealed just how fed up he was. He got up in an instand and began stomping on Amy's stomach, still hurting from the gunshot she'd received less than a month prior. Genuine pain shone through Amy's eyes, and it took all of her effort to roll out of the ring and to the outside. Appreciating his own work in the ring, Martyr nodded to his cronies, commending their dutiful secutity posts, and looking for approbation, then turned just in time to see his hardcore belt flying at him from the top rope. Martyr caught it, utterly confused, but didn't have even a moment to contemplate the consequences of his actions as the belt collided with his face, aided by a missle dropkick from Amy. Again, the fans cheered Aimz as the blood poured from Martyr's nose. Amy quickly covered as the referee, looking very unsure, counted the pinfall and reluctantly raised Aimz's hand in victory. On the mat, Martyr stared up at the lights, still holding his belt, glad that the match was non-title. Taking several moments to catch his breath, he began to wonder what had happened, and he met Amy's cold eyes. While Slugger and Wilson went inside the curtains, Martyr shook his head, mouth agape. The front right of the one car collided with the front left of the other car with great impact. Both men get out of their cars, one man started yelling before he even got out. "Didn't you see my fuckin' blinker on asshole? This spot was mine" said the baled headed man as he stood out of his car. When he turned around it was clear to see it was Steven Shadows. Eli Flair threw his hands up in the air. "Just can't get rid of you can I Shadows. Everywhere I go there you are. I hope you know you're paying for the damages to my car." Shadows pointed at Flair and began to yell so loud spit was flying out of his mouth. "Pay for your damages!? You're fuckin' crazy Flair. "Is that so Flair? Well, it seems to me we're at a standstill and there is only one way I can think of settling this" he said with a smirk on his face. Flair grinned right back at Shadows. "Only one way I can think of too...buddy." The two men started walking around the car to meet each other. But just then arena security pulled up to the accident. Two security guards got out of the patrol car and approached Shadows and Flair. "Seems you two had a little fender bender huh? Well why don't you guys just move the vehicles into a parking spot and allow us to clean up the glass so no one gets hurt. I assume you've exchanged each others insurance information and everything?" "Yes sir" said Shadows. "You betcha" said Flair. "But hey Flair...I'll meet you at this very spot in about an hour so we can exchange that other information." Flair smiled as he got into his car. "Oh I'm looking forward to it Shadows. I'll see you then." Flair and Shadows got into their respective vehicles and both sped away as fWo Wednesday Warfare continued. Introducing the Cheat Codes. The Fuse Bros., whom weren't seen for a good few weeks on television walked down the ramp to their theme song, as the Mario Bros.' music blasted over the PA. Tyler held a rather cocky grin on his face, while Conor carried a sheet of paper. They both slid into the ring, as Tyler took a microphone and Conor went back to the outside, looking under the apron for something. "Hello Mushroom Kingdom!" But the reply were not cheers. Though the people didn't boo either, it was apparent that the last two times the Fuse Bros. saw action, cheating facts had won them their battles. "We finally figured it out!" Tyler stopped, waiting for the Mushroom Kingdom to gasp, but, again, they didn't. Instead, they just questioned what Tyler was talking about, as everyone turned to one another to chat. "We finally understood why, over the last two Matches, you people booed us!" Ty walked over to the corner, where Conor had found what he was looking for under the apron, pulling it out. A tape dispenser. Ty then went on. "Yes, that's right. We realized we weren't being true to all of you. So Conor and myself took a trip to the library two weeks ago, and typed out that!" Ty pointed to the sheet of paper, in which Conor held above his head for all to see. "Mushroom Kingdom, the reason why we have not been around in this game for the past week, is because we've been working on that. Yes sir, my Brother and I have made official fWo Cheat Codes!" Conor started to paste the sheet of paper against the side of the ring post, smiling all the while as he did. "Yep. Now you cannot boo us anymore! We looked on the INTERNET for these puppies... modified them... And now we can cheat fairly!" Cheating fairly... that was new. "Paste it up Conor. Plug it into the system!" And as Conor did this, Tyler laughed, setting down the microphone. Then another theme song hit, one which brought out the Tallywhackers. Video game: on. Cheat codes: attached. All systems: go. Davy would've kicked out by now, still fresh in the battle, but what the Referee didn't see, was Tyler's feet on the second rope. Two. An enraged Cagle entered the ring, only to be double dropkicked by the Fuse Bros., who then rolled out of the ring, and detached the "Cheat Codes" from the ring post. "Unplug it! UNPLUG IT!" Cried Conor, as his Brother finally pulled the Cheat Codes off of the post, as the tape left some stuck to the sides of it... and hastily, as Dingleberry and Cagle recovered, they ran up the ramp to get away, all the while Conor screaming to the Mushroom Kingdom that booed him. "THEY WERE CHEAT CODES DAMMIT!! THEY WERE CHEAT CODES!! WE FOUND THEM ON THE INTERNET!! THIS. IS. LEGAL!" Result: Fuse Bros. (Tyler pins Dingleberry). Bash in the Back Lot "I told'ja, kid - don't worry about it." Twitch rolled his eyes. "I am worrying about it, kid, that's my job." Eli Flair sat on the bench, taping up his knuckles. In front of him, Twitch had been trying - unsuccessfully - to talk him out of this parking lot confrontation with Steven Shadows for nearly ten minutes. Poison Ivy sat off to the side, silent. She agreed with Twitch, but understood Eli's need for closure in this. "I don't see the problem," continued Eli, "I told you - this is going down outside the building. This is going down later tonight. The fWo will be completely blame - free in the entire situation." Twitch looked toward Ivy for support - for a Psycho Bitch she's unusually logical. "Ivy?" She shook her head and put her hands up. "I'm outta this one, Twitch... I can only talking him outta things he doesn't wanna do." As Eli stood up, Twitch grabbed his forearm - not in a threatening way, but more of a 'Stop and listen' type. "I'm not kidding, Eli. You think about something.... they don't have a complicated, easily abused legal system here. You get arrested again and you're toast. Now, you got a helluva negotiator there," gestured toward Ivy, "but from a business standpoint.... arrested twice in less than a month will force us to take action." Eli seemed to consider this for a moment. "Like I said before, Twitch... don't worry." He left the room, Twitch and Ivy both speechless. "Ivy," said the agent finally, "I've got an idea." "Yeah?" "Gonna need some help getting it organized but I think I know how to keep this from ending badly. Can I count on your help?" Ivy turned around and put her foot up on the bench, brushing a tiny, tiny smear off the toe of her perfectly- shined boots. "Well, my friend.... will see what I can do." Twitch smiled. For some reason, "I'll Try" from Poison Ivy seemed to constantly translate into better results than most people's assurance of their best. Tonight, he hoped the streak continued. A Warrior's Honor "Hey, Mufanga Kagos! Good to see you again." Silver Fox said as they passed in the hallway. "Good to see you too, man," Kagos said, "Hey, if you are booking the card tonight, maybe I could..." Mufanga trailed off as Silver Fox continued down the hall, greeting other wrestlers as they checked into locker rooms. "Hey, I just want to wrestle..." Mufanga whispered to Fox's back, but mostly to himself as the bustle of backstage life surrounded him. English and Spanish dialects mixed together to create a steady hum of white noise in the background. He was back in the industry, but he was low-card again, an unsignificant jobber with no hope but to serve the rest of his time as dark match fodder to upcoming fWo hopefuls. He was away for a long time, hurt badly... the doctors said he'd never wrestle again, but here he is, in Salvador of all places, ready to prove to the fWo that he was better than ever. Unfortunately, Mufanga's "better than ever" was still the fWo's "not good enough", not even for a rapidly depleting crowd of angry Brazilian soccer fans. Hell, the fans were leaving in droves anyway, why rush them straight out the door with a Kagos match, right? "Ah, Christ..." Mufanga sighed. Mufanga Kagos' head was beginning to pound again, a steady ache that sank in behind his eyes and seemed to throb in time with his quickening heartbeat... another headache was coming on.
