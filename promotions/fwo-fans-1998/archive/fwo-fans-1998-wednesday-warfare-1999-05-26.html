Wednesday Warfare, 26 May 1999

26 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Fans were welcomed to Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania as the fWo appeared live for Wednesday Warfare, on the eve of the big May Pay-Per-View, Extreme Measures '99, More Extreme Measures. Shots were shown of the arena, and the surrounding area, as fans stormed inside for a night of live wrestling action. At the announcing table was James Armstrong and Dark Rider associate Scott Taylor. As the pair went over the matches for the evening, it was noted that high above the ring was suspended a cage that would be used in the upcoming cage match between Oracle and Darkness. Cameras went backstage where a brief glimpse of Eron the Relentless was seen, and the announcers speculated what he was at the event for, since he was not scheduled for a match. Suddenly, crates were tossed aside as two men rushed into the backstage area and headed for the ring. Weilding baseball bats, Trouble made their presence known as they entered the main arena area to a mixed reaction from the crowd... Lone Gunmen vs. Ground Zero vs. Trouble vs. Chameleon & Total Eclipse Trouble stormed to the ring, with Twitch and Thumper laughing and shoving fans out of their way. Inside the ring Thumper produced a large bottle of beer, and doused himself with it. Calling the other three teams "girls" Trouble urged them to come and take a beating. The Chameleon came out riding on the back of the Total Eclipse, and seemed to be struggling to get down. The Eclipse held fast, and the two of them climbed into the ring this way, with the Eclipse bellowing out to the crowd for food. Happy fans began to toss bits of food in the direction of the ring, pelting Trouble and the unlikly pair. Ground Zero came to the ring quickly, and from different directions through the crowd. Jackhammer climbed into the ring and began to hammer away on Twitch, who staggered back from the assualt. Wolf Fang grabbed Thumper by the leg, and grounded him as he applied a quick figure four. The crowd then erupted as the Lone Gunmen hit the stage, surprisingly to a large ovation. This seemed to confuse Rudo, who began to shout "no, we are the *heels*! The *heels* here!" but Machina grabbed him by the ear and drug him to the ring. The two joined in on the fray, which erupted inside and outside of the ring. The Total Eclipse and the Chameleon were the only ones not joining in on the action, as the Total Eclipse began to devour the vendor cart at ringside. Inside the ring Twitch was jumping up and down on Rudo, who had been flattened on the mat by a clothesline from Jackhammer. The big man of Ground Zero had his hands full with Machina, who was running around him in circles until he was caught by Thumper, who grabbed Machina and suplexed him to the mat. Wolf Fang meanwhile had climbed to the top turnbuckle, and leveled Thumper with a flying dropkick before being grabbed by Rudo and dropped to the mat with a drop toe hold. Outside the ring, the Total Eclipse continued to eat as the Chameleon struggled to get free. During this chaos Aimee Horihito arrived, and pulled up a chair to ringside and watched the action with interest. Jackhammer and Wolf Fang grabbed Machina and executed a double suplex to the mat, and Wolf Fang climbed on for the pin. The Chameleon, seeing this, finally kicked the Total Eclipse in the stomach and dove into the ring to break the count. Rudo then kicked the Chameleon, and rolled him up for the pinfall. Rudo raised Machina's arm in congradulations, but Machina only laughed and ripped off his mask to reveal the Chameleon beneath. Confused, Rudo pushed aside the Chameleon's mask, revealing Machina with a taped mouth. During this confusion, the ref counted out the Total Eclipse, who was roaming for more food. The Chameleon seemed not to care, as he continued to laugh at Rudo and Machina... but quickly stopped laughing as Aimee climbed into the ring and delivered a hard shot to the groin of the Chameleon before blowing a kiss to Rudo and escaping through the crowd. With just two teams in the ring, the math settled into more of a traditional bout, with Twitch and Wolf Fang echanging quick holds and counter holds. Twitch finally was able to execute a sleeper hold, but before it could be firmly applied Wolf Fang fell to his knees, turning the hold into a jawbreaker. Twitch was rolled up, but Thumper came in to break the hold. Jackhammer was tagged in, and he powerslammed Twitch to the delight of the crowd. As Jackhammer went for the cover, Thumper reached into the ring and decked Jackhammer over the head with a wrench. Twitch rolled over the stunned Jackhammer and covered him for the pin. Wolf Fang ran into the ring to check on his partner, and then tossed the fallen wrench at Twitch and Trouble as the left the ring side area. As Wolf Fang checked on his partner, the French Foundation came onto the Warfare Screen and began to mock Ground Zero, with Primetime making fun of Jackhammer's head injury and reminded them that this Friday they would keep their Tag Team titles... and take Ground Zero's dignity. Wolf Fang only stared on stoicly as he tended to Jackhammer... Result: Trouble by Pinfall Wedding preperations Backstage Fallen paced the fWo halls, attended by Joy and Chris Finn. The two tried to calm Fallen, who was nervous and edgy. Chris Finn passed out cigars, and the Speed Demon, Billy James, and the Showstopper arrived to help Fallen celebrate. Fallen continued to babble to the others, commenting to the others that he was nervous, and didn't know how was going to sleep for the next few days before Friday and the big wedding. Chris Finn patted Fallen on the back, and told him not to worry, as tonight would be a special party just for him. With that, music started up, and out walked Valley Girl, dressed in a skimpy gown. The bump and grind music continued, as Valley Girl began to slowly strip for the group of cheering men. As Fallen turned red with embarrassment, the camera focused on Valley Girl, who grinned evilly, and licked her lips before the camera went dark... Stevie Roberts vs. VL VL's music played, and she came to the ring with her customary barrel of weapons. She stood on the turnbuckle, and motioned to her waist to indicate that she wanted a Hardcore title shot quickly. Stevie Roberts' music played, but the leader of Doomsday failed to come out on the entrance ramp. As VL waited inside the ring, the Warfare screen lit into action, and showed Stevie Roberts, wearing a very small, tight pair of swimming trunks. Roberts walked to a hot tub, and smirked at the camera. Roberts informed the crowd and VL that it payed to be a real fWo star... you got a hot tub in your dressing room, wine, and all the ammenities he wanted. Roberts then looked around the dressing room, and noted that there was one thing missing... and that was a hot looking babe to shag. Roberts then moved closer to the camera and told the crowd that this is where VL came in. Roberts told VL that she knew where he was, and if she wanted to "wrestle" it was fine with him... because he had a long ring rope she could "bounce" off of. VL turned red, and quickly grabbed a shovel and ran for the backstage area, as Roberts' laughter filled the arena and the Warfare screen went black... Result: No Contest Oracle vs. Darkness Oracle's music began to play as he pushed his way through the crowd rather than make his arrival from the entrance ramp. Oracle got into the ring, and slumped onto his knees as he stared at the entrance ramp and waited for Darkness to arrive. The lights in the arena went out, and the cage slowly lowered from the rafters as Darkness and the Mad Trucker stalked to the ring. Darkness crawled into the ring, but was met with kicks to the head by Oracle, who pushed Darkness from the ring and shoved his head to the arena floor with one hard boot. Oracle held Darkness fast, urging the cage to fall faster and crush the wrestler's head beneath the steel. The Mad Trucker injected himself into the fray, ramming a crowbar into the back of Oracle and rolling him back into the ring. The Trucker then rolled Darkness into the ring as the cage finally settled on the floor. Darkness and Oracle circled each other, both men already hurting from the agony suffered earlier. Oracle charged Darkness, who stepped aside and sent Oracle into the side of the cage, and then rammed an elbow against Oracle on the side of the cage. Darkness picked Oracle up by the hair and stared into his face, before breaking out into maniacal laughter and slamming him up and down into the mat. Leaving the bloody Oracle laying on the mat, Darkness walked to the cage door. Pulling himself to his elbows, Oracle managed to grab Darkness by the leg, and sent him crashing down face-first. Oracle then scrambled up and went for the cage door himself, signalling the ring attendants to open the door. As Oracle stepped through the door, Eron the Relentless rolled out from under the ring apron and slammed the steel door into the face of Oracle. Oracle feel backward, as Eron pulled a pair of handcuffs from his pocket and cuffed the dazed Oracle to the side of the steel cage. Laughing, Eron made his way back to the backstage area as Darkness crawled out of the cage. Declaired the winner, Darkness and the Mad Trucker raised their arms for the booing crowd. Suddenly, Alice and Cookie arrived on the entrance ramp. Alice pointed down at Darkness and the Trucker and yelled at them and the crowd that they had found the real Trucker. This confused and angered Darkness and the Trucker, who started up after Alice and Cookie on the entrance ramp. Alice and Cookie held their ground, and Alice held up a single silver hupcap to the crowd. This seemed to give the Trucker and Darkness pause, as fWo security arrived to keep the two groups apart... Result: Darkness The *other* party fWo cameras picked up a group of women heading into a Chippendales club in downtown Pittsburg. As they entered, their laughter could be heard, as they discussed their plans for tonight's bachelorette party. The guest of honor Fathom was there, along with Wynd, Fyre, Melissa Smalls, and Briana, as the group of party goers picked out a table on the side of the club. The six women chatted together, as the music and beat in the club kept a constant tempo. Sounds of the party continued for hours, as man after man danced passed them on the stage. Finally the party showed signs of breaking up, as the women went their seperate ways. Fathom walked slowly down the street as she attempted to hail a cab, but a small black BMW pulled up next to her before she could signal for the taxi. Confused and annoyed, Fathom bent over to see who the driver was, and then smiled. "Oh, it's you. I didn't expect to see *you*..." she said, as she climbed into the car and leaned over to give the driver a kiss before it drove off into the night... Ultra Violet vs. Scott Slugger Scott Slugger came to the ring to a large ovation, and the Hardcore title draped over one shoulder. Slugger asked for the microphone, and began to tell the crowd that he didn't give a goddamn if Ultra Violet walked out on both feet or got his ass kicked into his skull, as long as he took the fWo World title from that bastard Scotty Cool, Death. Slugger then told the crowd that tonight was about toughening up Ultra Violet, and it was time for him to come to the ring and take his medicine. Ultra Violet staggered out onto the entrance ramp with a giant goofy smile, and waved to the crowd who erupted in cheers and chants of his name. Shocked at this reaction, Ultra Violet looked around at who the crowd was cheering for, and then remembered Violet. Ultra Violet motioned to the backstage area, and Violet came out, carrying a bucket of purple paint. Ultra Violet proceeded to dip his hand in the pant and leave purple hand prints on fans in attendance. Ultra Violet walked around the ring and finally took notice of the Slugger inside the ring. Beaming, Ultra Violet leaped onto the entrance ramp, but became tangled up in the ring ropes. Ultra Violet's smile grew into a frown, and then panic as Scott Slugger shook his head in disgust. Slugger then picked up the microphone, and told Slugger that he was there to toughen him up, and toughen him up he would do. Slugger told Ultra Violet that he had to watch out for headbutts, like this one. Slugger then delivered a headbutt to Ultra Violet to show him exactly what he meant. Slugger then went through several other moves on the helpless Ultra Violet, and finally paced the ring, looking at the fruits of his labor. Slugger raised Ultra Violet's arm three times, and watched it drop three times with little effort. Upset with this, Slugger told the crowd, "Screw it... I'm going to even things up another way." before he slid out of the ring and made his way backstage, leaving Violet to attempt to figure out how to free Ultra Violet, a task she seemed to be having trouble with... Result: Scott Slugger by Submission Lucky and unlucky men Cameras picked up on giggling and squealing, as two long slender legs could be seen trashing about on a bed in a locker room backstage. Sounds of pleasure could be heard in the halls, and as people passed the dressing room of Nikki the Valley Girl they shook their heads at the sound of a very familiar scene. Inside the locker room Nikki cooed with pleasure and said, "Oh you... you're incredible... that was such a great bachelor party tonight... and she is soooo lucky... but tonight you're all mine..." The sheets rustled a little bit, and Billy James poked his head up to look at Nikki with a giant goofy smile. "I'll all yours for as long as you want me baby... but you're right, she is lucky. I don't think John Skyler took more than a glance at you, even though you were the stripper at his own party!" Nikki settled back into the bed and sighed a long sigh of content. "Oh Billy... those two are going to be very happy aren't they? As long as Fallen doesn't get hurt in that lumberjack match tonight..." Billy James began to pull on his pants as he answered, "Oh, I wouldn't worry about it. At least Dina is out of harm's way... she's all the way across town. But she's not going to see John until the two meet up in Baltimore tomorrow night. Hope all goes well with her party, but Melissa told me that they were just taking her to a club and breaking up the party early." Nikki tied a robe around her, and walked to the door of the locker room. Nikki turned to Billy and said, "Well, it was nice getting to... know... you better, but I've got to... er... meet... with the executive committee. It was a good party, those two are going to be a very, very happy couple..." Death (c) vs. Cactus Jon The lumberjacks slowly marched to the ring, and they included the Dark Riders War, Plague, Stone, Twitch, Thumper, Ice, Austin Davis, Chris Universal, and Ultra Violet. Scott Taylor noted that Scott Slugger was not present, and declaired that he must be too frightened to show his face at ringside. Hearing this, James Armstrong's face grew red, and he decked Scott Taylor, laying him out with one punch. Dusting off his hands, James Armstrong seemed pleased with himself, and wondered aloud just where Scott Slugger was. The lumberjacks at ringside were obviously uncomfortable with each other, and tensions were hot as Cactus Jon slowly made his way to the ring. Jon stood in the ring, and signalled to the crowd that tonight he would be taking the title home. The lights dimmed as Scott Collins, leader of the Dark Riders came out onto the entrance ramp and smirked at the man in the ring and the men at ringside. The smirk did not last long however, as Scott Slugger attacked from behind with a baseball bat, cracking it over the head of the fWo World Champion. At this, chaos broke out at ringside, as the lumberjacks began attacking each other and Scott Slugger gloated at what he accomplished. Inside the ring Cactus Jon looked confused as to why Scott Slugger ruined his match, and began to shout at Slugger as the cameras went off the air... Result: No Contest
