FlashBack, 1 November 1999

1 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The scene opened on Chris Finn, sitting alone in the fWo arena before a long back of fWo monitors. Scenes from last week's Warfare, Mob Rules, and Slaughter all played on the monitors as Chris Finn began to speak. "Hello fans and welcome to this week's edition of fWo FlashBack. We didn't join you last week as a result of the actions that took place on Wednesday Warfare. It's been a very tumultuous week in the fWo, faces we thought were gone forever returned. In addition, the 1999 fWo Mob Rules event took place live on Pay Per View, and if you missed this epic event it's not to late to order the replay this Tuesday night. Obviously it was a night to remember as Scott Slugger the fWo World Heavyweight Champion becomes the Mob Rules winner, adding another notch to his belt. Folks, Scott Slugger has held the fWo World Heavyweight Championship longer than any other wrestler in the history of the fWo... and you've got to know he's feeling pretty cocky about it. We also saw fWo Tag Team champions crowned... Mike Bear and Wretch became the fWo World Tag Team champions after a long and grueling tournament. Well, we are going to hear from Bear and Wretch on Warfare and I'm told that a challenge is going to be issued from the two men. Folks, we've got a huge FlashBack in store for you... but now let's take you to Classic fWo, and this match comes to you from BattleBowl '98... the last edition of the big Pay Per View event that is coming up in just a few short weeks." Cameras faded off as FlashBack got under way... Ultra Violet, Flying Frenchie, Jackhammer, Cactus Jon, Wolf Fang, Brawler, Fathom, War, Hunter, Black Quicksilver, Ice, Speed Demon, Apocolyptica, Mitch Wilson, Markus Cairn, Rudo, Showstopper, Primetime, Shocker, Scotty Cool, New Ninja, Boston Strangler, As the fWo security attempted to restore order to the area, Chris Finn along with the commissioner Travis went over the rules for the over-the-top timed Battle Royal. Chris Finn introduced once again the wrestler who drew number one, Ultra Violet. Ultra Violet came to the ring excitedly, and quickly scrambled to get into the ring. Once inside the ring, Ultra Violet looked about him and started jumping up and down, declairing himself the winner. Ultra Violet excitedly screamed "I'm the only one in the ring, therefore I've won!" Ultra Violet then took the Battle Bowl trophy, and began to leave the ringside area. Officals explained to Ultra Violet once again how the Battle Bowl rules worked, and a confused and hurt looking Ultra Violet returned to the ring. Next came the Flying Frenchie, who had drawn number two. The Frenchie at once went after Ultra Violet, and for two minutes the match closely resembled their cruiserweight title match of old. With both wrestlers using quick aerial moves, neither gained the advantage. Jackhammer came out next, and quickly chokeslamed both the Frenchie and Ultra Violet. As Jackhammer went for a double clothesline, the Frenchie and Ultra Violet went for a double suplex, and pitched the member of Ground Zero to the ground. Ultra Violet and the Frenchie resumed their fight, as Billy James came out to the ring. As James climbed into the ring, Machina appeared on the screen and simply pointed to the dressing room. Dropping his head, Billy James elimiated himself by jumping over the top rope and returned to the backstage area. Cactus Jon came out next, and the Frenchie's attacks centered on the Hardcore champion as Ultra Violet found a problem with his boot, and sat down to correct it. Wolf Fang then entered the ring, and began stomping at the head of Ultra Violet, who looked confused as to why his shoe tying had been distrubed. The Brawler was number seven, but as quickly as he entered the ring, a quick chop by Cactus Jon and a missle drop kick by the Frenchie sent him outside once more. Meanwhile, Wolf Fang had Ultra Violet on the top rope and began bouncing him up and down on it in an attempt to dislodge the tag champion. Wolf Fang was distracted however, when Fathom came to the ring, wearing only her light blue shimmering bikini. Wolf Fang reached into his tights and pulled out a twenty dollar bill, and then chased Fathom around the ring shouting "take it off!" A surprised Fathom ran into the ropes, and sprung backwards into Ultra Violet, knocking him from the ring. War came to the ring next, dressed in his old costume from when he was a member of the Dark Riders. Seeing this, Cactus Jon grew enraged, and quickly clotheslined him from the ring, and right on top of the Hunter, who had come to the ring as number ten. As the Hunter dislodged himself from War, Apocolyptica came out of the crowd and hit the Hunter with a sledgehammer, who was knocked out and unable to get into the ring. Next came the Black Quicksilver, who dove into the ring and began attacking everyone with the Stump, nailing the Flying Frenchie last, and sending him from the ring. As the Black Quicksilver looked on at the Frenchie, who yelled at him from the outside, Wolf Fang collided into him after a toss into the ropes by Fathom, and was sent from the ring as well. On the outside, the Black Quicksilver and the Frenchie traded blows. With only three wrestlers in the ring, Wolf Fang, Fathom, and Cactus Jon, Wolf Fang and Jon traded blows, as Fathom snuck up behind the Hardcore champion and kicked him in the groin. She then attacked Wolf Fang in the same fashon, until Ice made his way to the ring as number twelve. Ice picked Fathom up in a chokehold, and tossed her from the ring and into the second row of the crowd. Ice and Cactus Jon traded hard blows, with Ice finally picking up Jon in a backbreaker hold. The Speed Demon then came to the ring and admonished Ice for hurting a fellow wrestler. Wolf Fang came from behind and decked the Speed Demon with a blow to the head. The Speed Demon picked himself up and grew insane with rage, launching himself into Wolf Fang and then Ice, the later of which he knocked from the ring as Jon toppled into the ropes and managed to climb back in. The Speed Demon then leapt from the ring and began attacking fans at ringside before being drug away by security and Ice, who pummled the enraged racer for revenge. Apocolypitca entered the ring and attacked Wolf Fang, and the two traded hard chops as Cactus Jon recovered. Mitch Wilson came to the ring at the fifteen man mark, and put the weakened Cactus Jon into a submission hold in an attempt to wear the man down before tossing him from the ring. While these four wrestlers went at it, Markus Cairn came to the ring and joined Apocolyptica in attacking Wolf Fang. Wolf Fang rallyed back, and drove Apocolyptica into the ropes, but she was saved by Cairn, who gave Wolf Fang a quick series of suplexes into the ring. Rudo Insano came to the ring and sat in the corner watching the action and not doing anything. As the ShowStopper entered the ring, he used the distraction of Cairn to deliver a quick DDT and push him up against the ropes. Scotty Cool then entered the ring, and at once picked up Rudo and pitched him over the top rope. Meanwhile, Cairn and the Shocker traded blows inside the ring, but Primetime quietly entered at number twenty and crept up behind the two, pitching both from the ring with a quick scoop. Primetime then went after Cactus Jon, and he and Mitch Wilson attempted to send the hardcore champion out of the ring. Cactus Jon held onto the ropes, and yanked at Mitch Wilson's tights, sending the man to the floor. Cactus Jon then turned on Primetime, and began delivering quick blows to the face of Primetime. The Shocker then entered the ring, and at once leapt at Primetime. The Shocker proceeded to choke out Primetime in the corner of the ring, as Apocolyptica again traded blows with Wolf Fang, and Cactus Jon went after Scotty Cool. As Cactus Jon had Scotty Cool in the air, the New Ninja arrived in the ring and delivered a drop kick to the champion, sending he and Scotty Cool to the ring floor. As the crowd looked on stunned by two heavy favorites being eliminated by the Ninja, Apocolyptica ducked a lariat attempt and knocked Wolf Fang from the ring. Meanwhile, the Boston Strangler came to the ring as number twenty three, and at once began trading blows with the freshest man in the ring, the New Ninja. The New Ninja fought back, but the Strangler quickly locked on the crossface chicken wing in the center of the ring, and began to damage the back of the Ninja. Apocolyptica then arrived on the scene and began kicking at the face of the Strangler, forcing him to break the hold. Primetime meanwhile had rallyed back, and tossed his former partner the Shocker from the ring. Machina then came to the ring, but paced around on the outside rather than fighting anyone. Primetime went after Apocolyptica, who was tired having been in the ring longer than anyone else. Primetime sent Apocolyptica into the ropes, but she ducked under and hit a flying body press that sent Primetime stumbling to the outside. The Strangler then caught Apocolyptica with a heavy right hand, and tossed her from the ring as well. Oracle then came to the ring with the Prophet, but the refs informed the Prophet that he could not enter the ring. As Oracle and the Prophet argued the point, Darkness came to ringside and began attacking both men, and all three brawled off into the crowd. After being gone from the ringside area for over a minute, the refs declaired the Oracle out of the match. Scott Slugger came to the ring as number twenty six, and attacked the Strangler, as the New Ninja went after Machina by dragging him hair-first into the ring. The New Ninja proceeded to maul Machina as Stone came to the ring. Stone was stopped before she could get into the ring by a delivery man who presented her with a large batch of flowers. Stone entered the ring and swung the flowers at the head of the Ninja from behind and delivered a DDT, then went after Machina herself. The Ninja recovered and again brawled with Stone as Machina hid in the corner of the ring to avoid being seen and attacked. During this the ref called for Darkness to come to the ring, but on the monitors it was shown that Darkness, Oracle and the Prophet had brawled off into the backstage area, and were attacking each other in the fWo offical's parking lot. Scott Slugger and the Boston Strangler continued to trade blows as Sid Snow came to the ring, and demanded an fWo Women's Title Shot at the next House Show or he was going to sue the sexist pigs of the fWo booking commitee. Seeing this the New Ninja and Stone teamed up to pick him up and hurl him from the ring before going back to their brawl. Wynd came to the ring as the last wrestler in the Battle Royal at number 30. Wynd and Stone began to beat down the Ninja, but the Ninja ducked under a double clothesline attempt and pitched Wynd from the ring, who had become distracted by a vedor selling t-shirts at ringside. Wynd stood on the outside of the ring looking at a poster of Ultra Violet and chewing her thumb, totally engrossed in the visage of the tag team champion. Inside the ring Stone caught the New Ninja in a jawbreaker, and delivered a move before drop kicking him to the outside. Stone then went for Machina, who again hid from behind the Strangler and Scott Slugger. Scott Slugger was punched by Stone as she tried to grab Machina, and Scott Slugger flipped her to the outside, but got caught in the top rope in the process. The Strangler then tried to get Scott Slugger over the top rope himself, but lost his footing and fell over the Slugger, grabbing onto the member of the Punisher Squad in an attempt to save himself. The two broke free of the rope, and Machina was left alone in the ring. A surprised Machina then looked up into the fWo big screen where it announced himself the winner. Machina took all this in and began to pat himself on the back, telling everyone in the arena that he had single handedly beaten every fWo superstar in the ring, and forever forward would be known as "King Machina." Result: Machina Interview with Machina Back in the fWo studio Chris Finn sat with Machina, both of them looking up at the monitors at the conclusion of the match. Chris Finn turned to Machina. "Well... do you have some words for us after the conclusion of that match?" Machina turned back and shrugged. "Not really... but this would be a pretty lame interview if I just sat here and picked my ass, right? You know Finn, it's funny... that match was a real turning point for me and my career. Obviously I overcame great odds to defeat like... eighty or ninety wrestlers in that match, all by myself. It was a great night, but not the greatest night. You know why? I was on top of the world back then, and at the top of my game. Who knows where I would have gone next in the fWo... I had my title shot and was going to take on Austin Davis for the fWo World Heavyweight belt. Of course, I had to blow it all... run for president. And why? All for the 'Powers That Be'. Those two writers... you know who I'm talking about... the 'Powers That Be' booked me in a big program that involved me not wrestling for months, doing jobs everywhere... it was a real black mark on the life of Machina. And why? I mean, sure I had the reputation of being the most sexually... endowed... wrestler in the..." Chris Finn cut him off and quickly tried to take the show to a commercial. Machina kept talking, but his microphone had been cut. Angry, he grabbed Finn's microphone and started shouting into it. "I shouldn't be punished because I'm a great wrestler! You tell the writers that I'm not jobbing anymore! I'm not following the scripts anymore! I'm not..." Machina squinted and looked hard off-camera. "Um... line?" From off camera a voice said, "I'm not going to take it anymore." Machina nodded and resumed, "I'm not going to take it anymore!" Machina dropped the microphone and stood still, scratching his butt. The director yelled "cut" and a few minutes later the show went to commercial. Aimee Horihito vs. Nikki Aimee Horihito came to the ring in a special presentation of one of the dark matches from Sunday Slaughter. Horihito waited in the ring for Nikki the Valley Girl, the former fWo Womens Champion stalked to the ring. Fans were reminded that the "Frenchie Mandatory Invitational Womens Championship Contest" was currently ongoing, and that Fyre would get the opportunity to go for the title on Warfare. The two girls locked up, but then Nikki asked for a break because she had chipped a nail. Aimee ignored her crying and shoved her to the mat, and leaped atop her. She grabbed Nikki's head and began to repeatedly slam it into the mat. Nikki stood and stumbled around the ring as if in a daze. She dropped to her knees in front of the referee and began to fiddle with his belt. The referee started to call for the bell, but then simply stood still with a smile on his face. Aimee grabbed the back of Nikki's head and tossed her to the other side of the ring, and then grabbed her by the midsection for a quick suplex. The referee started to count, but then slid to the outside of the ring and called for the DQ on Aimee for no apparent reason. He quickly ran to the back as Aimee yelled at the ref and Nikki gloated. Aimee picked Nikki up and tossed her out of the ring, knocking her head against the steel barrier. Scowling, Aimee stalked to the back. Result: Nikki by DQ fWo Rankings When the cameras returned to the studio Matt Loco was sitting next to the rankings for the week. "Hello fans! I'm Matt Loco, and I've got the fWo rankings for the week. Remember, the way this chart works is that every wrestler who won a match moves up three notches on the chart. A loss moves the wrestler down three notches. If the wrestler wasn't on the chart, they start at number 20. If the wrestler didn't wrestle, they move down one notch. At the end the numbers are adjusted to make a twenty count again. Remember, we had four shows to go over, but matches from Mob Rules do not count for this ranking. With that in mind, this is what we have for you now." Loco held up the chart, which read: 1. Scott Slugger (c) 2. Mike Bear (c) 3. Black Panther 4. Flying Frenchie 5. Death (Collins) (c) 6. Machina 7. Mad Trucker (c) 8. Boston Strangler 9. Primetime 10. Chris Universal 11. Eron (c) 12. Cactus Jon 13. Wretch (c) 14. Jackhammer 15. Courage 16. Lance Star (c) 17. Ultra Violet 18. Crucifix 19. Micky Maliboo 20. Rain over: Ice, Black Quicksilver, Plague, Havok, Los Latin Matt Loco looked back at the cameras. "Join us next week when I'll go over the rankings again. Thanks and good night!" The cameras faded off into another segment... Stevie Roberts Interview The cameras returned to Ken Armstrong in the fWo Studio, sitting down with Stevie Roberts. Ken: Hello Stevie... and thanks for joining us tonight. Stevie: Yeah, glad to be here I guess. Your producer didn't get me a lot of choice in the matter. Ken: Let's start with this Stevie... how's the leg? Stevie: I had an MRI on the leg last week and it's in better shape than we thought. Obviously when that little punk... Plague I guess his name is, but I thought he was my friend... did a real number on me, but Stevie's momma grew her boy tough. I'll be back in the fWo in time for Battle Bowl, and you can bet your ass I'm going to be going after that little bitch. Ken: How did it feel to be betrayed in that way? Stevie: How did it feel? How do you think? It sucks, because I thought I had a friend back again. But you know what Ken? I've learned a big lesson... there are no friends in wrestling. This business is full of a bunch of cheats and liars and it's as disgusting as it gets. Ken: What about VL, your partner? Stevie: Obviously I hope that things stay there. I care for VL, but I'm a bit worried that what happened with Johnny... well... Plague... is gonna happen to her. I really like VL, I've given up other women for VL and that's a pretty big statement if you know me. I think that the two of us got really screwed by Commissioner Travis... he's a dick and I'm glad he got punked out because now maybe VL and I can get a fair shake. Ken: How did it feel to see Spencer return to active service in the fWo? Stevie: Well you know... David Spencer was a really important person to the foundation of the fWo and a lot of people overlook that. He was a heel, and that's fine because every promotion needs a heel. Obviously I didn't like him inside the ring, but professionally I can't say much bad about guy. He helped to redefine what it meant to be a heel when in the other promotions you had movie stars and some supposed undertaker. As Death he brought real hatred to the fWo. The fans hated him, the other fWo wrestlers hated him... you've got to respect that kind of power in the industry. So I'm not happy in that I'll have to face him again, but as a character I'll be interested to see where he goes inside the promotion. Ken: There were some rumors about a year ago that you had a problem with the pills... Stevie: That's really not something I want to discuss. Ken: You were a world champion... Stevie: Over a year ago, yeah. Ken: Do you see yourself back there again? Stevie: Well I hope so. I mean, come on... I was a champion once, a Tag Team Champion... I should be back up at that level again. I better be. Everyone laughed when I got my shot, but I helped carry the promotion when the ratings were starting to build. When I headlined a show the buy rates were big, so it would be big business if I was up there again. I mean, something else too... can you imagine a main event with David Spencer, that phony calling himself Death and me? Triple threat match? Yeah, I think that would pretty much kick ass. Who wouldn't want to see that? Ken: Who are some of the people you keep in contact with in the fWo? Stevie: Well obviously VL. Other than that I've always got along well with the Chameleon and Total Eclipse... I like both of those guys and have a good friendship with each of them. You know, I still talk to Rage, a wrestler that used to be around a long time ago. You know I think that Jim Dudley who was a friend of his got a bum rap too. I don't hear from Dudley because the two of us never really spoke, but I always got the impression that he got a bum deal. Ken: Any other words for the fans of the fWo? Stevie: Just to thank them for cheering for me, and I'll be back. Count on it. Ken: Thanks for joining us, Stevie. The cameras faded out as the interview came to a close. Finish The scene returned to fWo studios, where Chris Finn was wrapping up the show. "Folks, that's the end for today's show. Don't forget to watch Warfare this week, where we've got some major matches on tap. Also we will find out who the new Commissioner will be of the fWo. This is going to be a major surprise to a lot of people, and we hope you tune in to find out exactly who it will be. Folks, we've got BattleBowl coming up in a few short weeks, but join us in just one week as we release the first few names for the BattleBowl battle royal, recap the fWo rankings, present another Classic fWo match, run an interview with fWo wrestler Scott Collins... AKA Death, and present a new feature to FlashBack, an interactive feature where you the fans will get to select your 'Match of the Week'. Thanks for joining us... for the fWo I'm Chris Finn... good night!" The show came to a close, the scene fading out...
