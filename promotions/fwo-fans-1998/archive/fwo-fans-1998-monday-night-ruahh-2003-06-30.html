Introduction The teams have been announced. Fourty wrestlers: twenty teams. Meltdown and the chance of a lifetime. The road to Meltdown is offically underway, and heaven help them all. Planning "What's next on the list?" Samuel Smooth, complete in sparkling green hat and jacket raised an eyebrow. "Looks like just the conference call. You on that, Fox?" Silver Fox nodded, glancing around the table where Smooth, Twitch, Tallison and Buckly... Kid Cool... all sat. They were reviewing the open issues list for the promotion and attempting to plot out what came next. They had run down promotional appearances, interpromotional negotiations, and the recent influx of new talent looking for a spot on the roster. Twitch and Tallison had given a report on the Survivor III contest, which was running smoothly thus far. "Yes, the conference call with the investors is tomorrow. The board will be on the phone, and while none of you have to present I believe that it would be best if you were on the call. It's scheduled to go around forty five minutes, shouldn't run over. We're talking about early buys, rating trends, the state of offline and POP revenue and similar business items." "Sounds like a bore," said Twitch, yawning deeply. "All part of the new business," said Silver Fox, who down deep agreed completely with Twitch's assessment. "But it's necessary. Important to keep everyone in the loop." "Any talk about the contracts and how the returns are on those?" Silver Fox nodded. "I'm sure the issue will come up. We spend heavily on Collins, and there will be a report on how well that is going, as well as our other big contract staff... Creed, Kinkade, Flair." "I'm sure it will be thrilling." Tallison grumbled, "and any chance of going over my proposal for my name returned to me?" "I don't see that as being on the primary issues for the board and investors to look at Ted, sorry. But I'm sure we will get some resolution soon." "Stupid Disney blokes... one of them recommended I change my name to Nemo to help promote that bloody stupid fish movie." The group laughed, and Silver Fox leaned back in his chair, enjoying the moment. It almost seemed normal today, mind you needed to squint your eyes slightly so you couldn't see the ABC crew running about with clipboards, and Smooth was looking more garish than Silver Fox would have ever believed possible... but such is life. As they say... on with the show. Temerario's mission "I don't know Scotty Bowman." Temerario taped up his wrists, a grim smile on his face. Twitch nodded and shrugged. "That's fine, mate. That's what this whole event is about... working with other people and doing things you haven't done before." Temerario shrugged, biting off the end of the tape and turning to look at Twitch. The scarred face under the mask made Twitch shudder slightly, and he hoped that the young cruiserweight hadn't seen it. "I'll meet with him. The two of us, in the main event of Meltdown... going for the gold. That's it, is it? Well, fine by me. The more, the merrier. But I've been in the fWo for months now, been used as a jobber or as a stunt double to put wrestlers over. No longer, Twitch. I'm making a run now, and I'm going to show people exactly what I'm made of. Scotty Bowman can be along for the ride... makes no difference to me." Temerario turned, walked away from Twitch, and headed to the ring. Twitch shrugged, sighing slightly to himself. He didn't understand kids in the business today... didn't any of them realize that there were years... many years... that came after it was all over? Outside the crowd roared as Temerario charged down the entrance ramp, and Twitch answered his own question. How could one be safe when the crowd was there to urge them on? Temerario vs. James Renshaw Temerario shoved past the curtains as 'Man Without Fear' wailed out of the arena speakers. Inside the ring already was former fWo Hardcore Champion James Renshaw, who looked slightly nervous to be performing in front of such a large crowd. Temerario walked to the ring, arms raised and face stoic as he made his way down the entrance ramp. He slid into the ring, came to his feet with a quick kip up, and paced around the referee and Renshaw. The bell rang, and Temerario flexed quickly and offered a hand to shake. Renshaw took it, and both men backed away from each other as the match began in earnest. Temerario ducked out of the way of a running clothesline, but was shoved backward by Renshaw as he passed. Renshaw attacked from behind with a flurry of quick punches, but Temerario shrugged this off and retaliated with a spinning heel kick that sent Renshaw tumbling to the outside. Posing for a second in the ring, Temerario ran forward and dove with a plancha to the outside, catching Renshaw as he got to his feet. The two continued a fast-paced brawl around the ring, then rolled inside and exchanged blows inside as well. Temerario blocked a punch from Renshaw, then ran forward with a charging clothesline that sent both men toppling once more to the outside. James Renshaw was up first and caught Temerario, driving him groin-first into the steel corner, smashing Temerario's head against the steel. Temerario fell to his feet as a surprised and happy Renshaw climbed into the ring quickly and waited as the referee laid on the count. But at the count of eight, Temerario was able to get back to his feet and roll inside. With Temerario dazed from the post, neither man was able to gain the clear advantage. Both wrestlers continued with a fast-paced move set, ending with Temerario catching a solid tornado DDT from the second rope onto the still very green James Renshaw. Temerario continued the attack in the corner with quick ju-jitsu kicks, but gaining his bearings, James Renshaw countered and traps Temerario in a sleeper hold in the center of the ring. Struggling with all of his might, Temerario was finally able to escape, and moving quickly attempted a sleeper hold of his own, but with the sheer upper body strength of James Renshaw the former Hardcore champion was able to turn the hold into a powerful slam into the corner. James Renshaw attempted a belly to back suplex, but Temerario kicked him back and attempted a cross body which sailed over the ducking Renshaw. Renshaw attacked back with quick punches and kicks, and feeling daring landed a spinning heel kick sending Temerario down to the mat. Pointing to his head for the crowd, James Renshaw locked the masked Temerario in the figure four in the center of the ring. Temerario attempted to reach the ropes, then tried to reverse the hold, but both were for naught. The referee asked if Temerario wanted to quit, but Temerario shook his head furiously and went for the ropes once again. James Renshaw broke the hold, and drug Temerario back to the center of the ring and attempted to apply the figure four once again, but Temerario kicked him away and landed a quick snap suplex from nowhere. Temerario and Renshaw both fought to get on their feet and as they did so traded quick blows, ending when Temerario landed another spinning heel kick that cracked into Renshaw's head. Renshaw, struggling and dazed went to the corner and attempted a quick pinfall, but Temerario rolled through it as the referee dove into position. 1... 2... But Renshaw powered up and out of it, and attempted a huricarana, but Temerario easily countered into a snap powerbomb. Temerario made another cover, and this time Renshaw looked completely out of it. 1... 2... 3. The bell rang and Temerario stood, fist in the air and looking at the crowd with satisfaction. This was in then, the first step in the road to a championship. He nodded, smiling slightly under the mask as Monday Night Ruahh~! escaped to a commercial break.
