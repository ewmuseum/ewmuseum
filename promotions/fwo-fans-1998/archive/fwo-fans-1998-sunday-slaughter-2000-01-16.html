Sunday Slaughter, 16 January 2000

16 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Geoff Green passed Mike Heftel a cup of coffee and sat down next to the often changing wrestler. Today Mike Heftel was simply dressed in a warm blazer and jeans, and watched the sky grow darker as the night came upon them. Inside the arena it was chaos, but outside it was relatively quiet and peaceful. Wrestlers had been arriving at the arena for the last few hours, and were pleasantly surprised by the fact that the booking sheets had been posted early in the day. Austin Davis wasn't booking the Slaughter show, and that was a good thing as far as Mike Heftel was concerned. Geoff fiddled with a light meter and noted how the technology had changed over the last two years. VL pulled up to the arena on a motorcycle and walked inside. Heftel and Green noted this, and nodded to each other. VL had a great deal of respect in the backstage of the fWo, largely because over time she had proved that she not only could work with the men in the promotion, but work on their level. Now Rudo was arriving, and Geoff Green noticed that he had arrived alone. Machina was likely with his girlfriend Penny out protesting wrestling, and there was some kind of gay pride parade across town that Sid was off taking pictures of. Rudo walked toward the entrance of the arena but was approached by Scott Collins before he got inside. Rudo looked alarmed, but Collins was saying something to him and Rudo seemed to calm down. The pair walked into the arena with Collins looking as though he was persuading Rudo of something. Mike Heftel thought silently if perhaps the Lone Gunmen was the place for him. Certainly being in a group could have advantages. Before he could think about the topic any further, two vehicles pulled into the backstage loading area at once. The limousine pulled in and Chris Universal stepped out. He looked over at the second vehicle and stood steady. The taxicab door creaked open, and the red hair that appeared gave away the occupant. The Boston Strangler stood in the cold and regarded Chris Universal silently. Universal broke the silence first. "Let's do this." The Strangler held his gaze a moment longer, and then a nod came that was so small it was nearly invisible. They walked into the arena together. Geoff looked over at Mike. "Going to be a wild night." Mike shrugged. "What would a boring night be anyway? Two hours of Wilson matches?" Inside, there were loud fireworks. Havok (c) vs. Courage The television camera pans towards the entrance ramp zooming in, and soon thereafter Courage appears with a confident swagger and an extra hop in his step as he prepares for his final match in the United States before the fWo kicks off its two week tour of western Europe. He stands atop the ramp and makes a belt gesture around his waist letting the fans in attendance and watching from home know where he believes the cruiserweight strap belongs. In one hand he loosely holds a microphone and he holds up the other hand to signal for quiet. The jeering only grows in strength and an irritated Courage soon interjects, "You know they wouldn't treat a superstar like this in France." The hooting only grows uglier as a 'U!S!A!' chant is started and spreads like wildfire. He begins down towards the ring continuing to speak his mind; "I just heard that the results of a new study found that Philadelphia has the fattest people in the nation." He looks around and continues as he reaches ringside. "And from the looks of it, that study was pretty damn accurate." The mass of Philadelphians begin a self-insulting cheer of pride just as "Anthem for the Year 2000" by Silverchair begins and the cheers reach a new level. The cruiserweight champion Havok comes into view with the cruiser title clinging to his waist and a stack of glossy Playgirl magazines with himself on the cover in his arms. A large smile from ear to ear on his face, he hands out copies of them to his feminine fans as he passes them on his way to the ring. With one copy left, he slides into the ring and politely offers it to an impatient Courage. Courage does not take a liking to this gesture and pounces on Havok swinging wildly and connecting, driving a surprised Havok into one corner. The referee jumps in-between the two grapplers, squeezing them apart and allowing Havok a chance to recover and remove the title belt that still remains strapped about his waist. He tosses it aside and once the referee moves out of his direct path, it is Havok's turn to attack unexpectedly. He lunges forward but is introduced to the mat as Courage uses Havok's momentum against him with a drop toe-hold maneuver. Courage leaps upon Havok and turns, grabbing his legs, and looking to slap on a devastating elevated Boston Crab. Havok escapes a possible early defeat, shifting pressure towards Courage, and causing him to roll forward and himself roll on top of a stunned Courage, in turn pinning him to the mat. Courage tries desperately to free himself and does so, shoving Havok up and off of him as the referee reaches a 2 count. Courage rises and charges at Havok who comes back at him, but Courage connects first with a kick to Havok's kidney. He slings one of Havok's arms over his head and looks for a snap suplex, but Havok holds strong and instead removes his head from under Courage and looks to hit him with a suplex of his own. He lifts him, and keeping an elevated Courage parallel to the mat, swings around and brings Courage crashing downward face-first into the mat with Courage ricocheting hard off of it and recoiling away from Havok clutching his ribs and nose. Havok allows Courage time to stand and then charges poised to hit him with a clothesline. Courage ducks it and turns, grasping Havok about the waist. He looks for a German suplex type maneuver, but Havok once lifted and sent flipping over Courage's shoulder is able to land on both feet. Changing gears quickly he comes back at Courage, grabbing his head and jerking him forward so that his head is squeezed between Havok's legs. He executes, lifting Courage and quickly spinning towards the center of the ring, and whipping Courage to the floor with a commanding Powerbomb that once more has Courage bouncing off of the mat. Both men take a minute to regain their breath, each retreating to opposite corners. Circling one another, Courage rushes, but Havok leapfrogs over him. Courage's momentum allows him to leap into the ropes and he springs off with a springboard moonsault. Havok turns and somersaults towards the rope. Courage lands on both feet just as Havok turns. It is now Courage's turn to leapfrog over an oncoming Havok. Almost as if in instant replay, Havok duplicates Courage's springboard moonsault, and as before Courage somersaults forward as Havok soars overhead. He finds his feet but Courage is at him almost immediately with a drop-kick that strikes Havok across the face and floors him. Courage drags Havok into the center of the ring, thus eliminating Havok's chances of reaching the ropes to escape what is to follow. He successfully locks on a Boston Crab, and then slowly, inch by painful inch, elevates it and increases the pressure as Havok cringes in pain. A cheer arises as Wretch, both Havok and Courage's opponent to be at Invasion!, dashes towards both men. Wretch dives into the ring and Courage, with the Boston Crab still in full glory, cannot react in time and receives a boot to the face that flings him backwards. Wretch yells at him to rise, and a rare side of Wretch comes out. Courage stands and Wretch leaps, knifing through the air with a spinning heel-kick that wallops Courage's chin and sends him tumbling through the ropes and down to the arena floor. Wretch now turns his attention to a semi-recovered Havok. Angered at Wretch's interference, though he might have been just moments from losing his title, Havok walks up face-to-face with him. An up-close argument begins and shoves follow. Havok's Millennium Man teammate Black Panther hastily makes his way out to separate the two, and he is followed by Wretch's Underground partner Mike Bear as the two are held back from attacking the other. The referee calls for the bell, and the match is ruled a no contest. Slaughter cuts to commercial as the war between Wretch, Havok and Courage reaches new heights. Result: No Contest The road to championships "So... you're not... um... upset?" Rudo still looked suspicious of the whole situation as he eyed Scott Collins out of the corner of his eye. Collins seemed oddly... happy. Rudo took a stab at the reason. "So... figure out what Stone's real name is?" Collins shook his head. "Nope. But I'll figure it out. In fact, I've got Matt Loco digging up her entry application into the fWo. She had to sign with some name after all. But that's for later on. Rudo, when you beat me at Warfare I realized that you had the potential to be one of the greats in the fWo. Remember now, I've wrestled everyone. Two years... never missed a big show. I've seen them all, and wrestled them all. Hell, I've BEAT them all. Anyway, with a little work you can get to the top of the card. Wrestling with the big boys. Imagine for a moment Rudo... your name... headlining CyberSlam. Holding the fWo World Title. I mean, Tag Team champion is great... but..." Rudo's eyes had glazed over and he was smiling fondly into the air. "Champion... yeah..." Collins now slapped Rudo on the back. "You've just got to get a little tougher. Just a little more... focused. And I can help you with that. What do you say? I've got about an hour before my match... so come with me. I'll teach you how to be tougher. Stronger. A champion. What do you say?" Rudo's eyes were still glazed over and he mumbled, "Champion... Champion..." Scott Collins laughed. "I'll take that as a yes... pal." Flying Frenchie vs. Matt Douglas The familiar sounds of KDFM blasted over the arena speaker system, and soon the entire crowd were spouting boos in unison. After a few moments the Flying Frenchie emerged onto the entrance ramp, smirking at the thought of this being his last match in America before the fWo makes its way to France for their January Invasion! pay per view. Frenchie slid into the ring and began to taunt the fans as he waited for his opponent, the man formerly known as Wolf Fang. "Higher" sounded, and Matt Douglas swiftly made his way to the ring as the fans cheered. Douglas slid into the ring but before he could even to his feet the Frenchie was on top of him, stomping away. As the bell rang the Frenchie scooped up Douglas and slammed him to the mat, followed up by a legdrop across the throat. TFF went for a quick cover, but Matt got the shoulder up at two. Frenchie picked up Douglas and sent him to the ropes before charging after him with a clothesline, but Douglas ducked the Frenchie's arm on his way back. As TFF bounced off the opposite ropes and made his way back, Douglas sprung onto the middle and rope crashed into his opponent with an Asai moonsault bodyblock. Douglas rolled through the move and hooked the leg, but could only manage a one count before slipping off. Douglas quickly hopped to his feet and grabbed TFF by the head, and then threw him through the ropes and to the outside. The Frenchie got to his feet just in time to see Matt Douglas coming straight for him and immediately dove to the side. Douglas, however, saw this and was able to catch himself in time by grabbing hold of the top rope. As the Frenchie got to his feet, smiling in confidence thinking he had outsmarted his foe, Douglas pulled back and let himself flying into TFF with a slingshot bodyblock. Both men got to their feet at the same time, but it was Douglas who got off the first move. Grabbing the head of Frenchie, the former Millennium Man executed a face crusher right onto arena floor. While the Flying Frenchie rolled on the floor in pain Douglas slid into the ring and climbed to the top rope, waiting in anticipation. TFF slowly got to his feet, and Matt leapt at him with a flying double axe handle, but Frenchie dodged him, allowing Douglas to hotshot himself across the guardrail. TFF took a few seconds to regain his senses before posing for the crowd, who were booing him mercilessly. The Flying Frenchie then grabbed Douglas by the head and rolled him into the ring, where he lazily laid on him for the cover. One... two... two and one half... Douglas kicked out! TFF pounded on the mat in frustration before positioning Douglas in the corner with a snap suplex. TFF then scaled the top turnbuckle and signaled for the Guillotine Legdrop. But, as he did, the Black Panther made his way down the entrance ramp. TFF paused and looked up at the fWo World Champion, who stood there, quietly fixated on the Frenchie. TFF pointed to Panther and then himself, but as he did Matt Douglas, who had gotten to his feet while the Frenchie was distracted, slammed himself against the top rope, crotching TFF on the top turnbuckle. As the Frenchie sat there in stunned pain, Douglas climbed up Frenchie's turnbuckle and delivered a series of shots to the head. Then, raising his arm to the crowd, Douglas jumped up and took TFF back down to the mat with a top rope frankensteiner. Douglas reached back and hooked the leg of the Frenchie and got the three count. As the bell rang, Douglas celebrated his victory over the former world champion, while the Frenchie simply sat there in disgust, looking at the Panther. Chuckling to himself, the current champion turned and made his way back to the locker rooms. Result: Matt Douglas by Pinfall Off to France, land of opportunity Kid Cool stumbled over to the table where Chris Finn was sitting and sniffled. He appeared to have a large black eye, and was dirty. Not just wrestler dirty either, but hobo dirty. Chris Finn had the sudden realization that Kid Cool had been sleeping on the streets in the last few days and wondered why he hadn't stayed in the hotel that the fWo reserved for him. Kid Cool began to speak, but it was little more than high pitched squealing and Finn winced at the sound. Kid Cool seemed to understand that he wasn't making any sense, and started over. "I want to go to France... not on Wednesday, but now." Chris Finn blinked. Understanding came over his face. "You know Buckley... the stories about the French whores are largely exaggerated." Kid Cool waved this aside. "I don't care about... all the stories?" Chris Finn nodded slowly. Kid Cool frowned and continued. "Well, that's fine. I want to go over early to train... France is where all the greatest wrestlers come from, and I'm hoping to learn at the Flying Frenchie's dojo." Chris Finn groaned. "You know Kid, I don't think they call it a dojo in Franc... aw, screw it. Yeah sure. Go to France early." Kid Cool grinned and shouted, "yippee!" He scrambled out of the room, and left Chris Finn to himself. Finn looked over the pile of paperwork and groaned again. "Oh, for the days when I could just stay at the announcers table with some porn. Well, better get..." His words trailed off as he noticed the new arrival at the door. "Oh, crap. Hello, James. VL (c) vs. Cactus Jon Cactus Jon stepped out onto the entrance ramp with a determined look on his face. As "Bombtrack" played and the crowd booed, the former world champion made his way to the ring to prepare for this bout over the Hardcore Championship. Next, "Shook Ones, Part II" played over the speaker system, and VL mad her way down to the ring, wielding a baseball bat wrapped in barbed wire. The site of this object did not faze Cactus Jon, who stepped through the ropes and to the floor. VL charged at Jon with the bat held high over her head. As she brought the weapon down, though, Jon tackled VL and straddled her. As VL to reac hher dropped foreign object, Jon produced a pair of shiny brass knuckles. Sliding them onto his hand, he began to repeatedly strike VL in the face. The cult VL fans had littered the area surrounding Jon with trash, prompting the challenger to stop his assault and taunt the crowd. As he did so, though, VL brought up her knee, jamming it in the crotch of the Canadian legend. Jon fell backwards and off of VL, who then grabbed the brass knuckles and, with an evil smile, put them on herself. As Jon staggered to his feet, still reeling from the low blow, VL stepped into him with a monstrous right hand. Jon's forehead practically exploded in blood as he fell back against the ring apron. VL then took up her barbed wire wrapped ball bat and stuck Jon across the back with it. Jon doubled over in pain and tried to make his retreat by crawling. VL simply laughed before smacking Jon in the side of the head with the bat. As Jon lay motionless, VL made the cover. One... two... thr... kickout! Somehow, Jon was still in the fight. VL brought her foe up to his feet and whipped him into the ringpost with a loud THUD. Jon staggered backwards and right into VL who nailed him in the midsection with the end of the bat. Jon dropped to one knee, grimacing in pain, as VL hopped onto the ring apron. Holding the bat high in the air, VL began running down the apron as Jon slowly got to his feet. The Canadian turned just in time to see the sight of VL sailing through the air, and was met squarely in the face by a shot from the barbed wire bat. Jon's face was now bleeding insanely, but he managed to stay on his feet. VL then chucked the bat into the ring and began searching under the ring apron. A few moments later she pulled out a wooden table and proceeded to set it up. Meanwhile, Cactus Jon slowly rolled into the ring and grabbed the bat. After finishing her business with the table VL climbed onto the apron where she was greeted by Jon, who clocked her in the face with her own weapon. The force of the blow sent VL flying off the apron and onto the table, but the table remained intact. Jon stepped onto the apron and covered VL who was still lying on the table. One, two, three, and the fWo had a new Hardcore Champion! That is, Cactus Jon thought they did until a second referee ran out from the back. While Jon celebrated with his brand new belt and VL recovered from the bat shot, the two refs squabbled over whether or not the pin was legitimate. After some elegation, they agreed that since VL was on the table at the time that the pinfall was not valid. The bell rang and, despite Jon's arguments, the match was restarted. VL still seemed out of it after being struck in the face by the baseball bat and slowly moved for Jon. But, Jon caught VL with a boot to the gut and set her up for the Canadian Crusher. As Jon lifted, though, Hellrazor emerged from the crowd. Just as Jon finished the move and made the cover on an unconscious VL, 'Razor stepped through the ropes and whacked Jon across the back of the head with the barbed bat. Jon rolled off of VL, holding his head in pain. Hellrazor picked up the prone Jon and placed him on the top turnbuckle, then drove his head through the table outside the ring his DDT off the top rope, known as No More Pain. Hellrazor then rolled back into the ring and drug VL to the outside, where he forced her to cover Cactus Jon. Reluctantly, the ref made the three count, and VL would still be champion when she went to Invasion! to meet Hellrazor in the mother of all death matches, the skyscraper match. Result: VL by Pinfall Toughness "This is where people go to be tough? Looks... well, looks like a place Sid Snow would go." Rudo looked skeptically at the biker bar. Motorcycles were lined up in front of it in a long row, gleaming in the moon light. Scott Collins took in the scene with a slight smile on his face. "Oh you bet... this is where I came to train when I was just getting started in wrestling. Lots of toughness in there. Why, they have this obstacle course inside that will knock your socks off. War comes here all the time. Why, that's his motorcycle right over there." Scott Collins pointed to a red and black Harley that leaned at the end of the line of bikes. "That's his pride and joy, and tonight he wanted you to ride it. So go on... get on 'er." Rudo looked wary and slightly frightened. "I've never ridden a motorcycle before..." Scott Collins laughed, but his eyes looked narrow and full of malice. Rudo didn't notice this as he looked at the bike. The thing was, he wanted to ride it. The motorcycle called to him in a way he had not felt before. Rudo walked over to it, and Collins stepped backward into the cab they had taken to the biker bar. Rudo climbed onto the bike, but predictably it toppled over sideways, striking the bike next to it. The chain reaction that followed was seen by Collins as the cab pulled out of the bar parking lot and onto the street. Collins laughed as the bar emptied out of angry leather-clad patrons who advanced upon Rudo. "Dumb ass... driver, back to the area." The driver turned and nodded to Collins. "But your friend?" Collins waved the driver on. "Keep going. He's just about to learn about what it means to piss off the leader of the Riders." When the cab was a block away Collins still could hear the screams, and he laughed. Chris Universal & Boston Strangler vs. Living, Hell & Heaven Slaughter continued as "Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson echoed throughout the arena. The "FTfWo" logo flashed onto the SlaughterScreen, before the trio of Heaven, Living and Hell entered the arena. They stood on the stage in unison and basked in the boos of the crowd, as Living acted very psychotic. Living charged down to the ring leaving Heaven & Hell behind him, before rolling into the ring, and continuing to roll until he fell out of the ring on the other side. He then got back up and screamed something about not wearing any diapers, before he came back into the ring and slumped in the corner. Heaven & Hell entered the ring, and they joined Living in the corner, although both remained in a standing position. They awaited their opponents calmly, and would not have to wait much long before "O' Fortuna" began to play. The lights dimmed before The Boston Strangler came out onto the stage. He stared into the depths of the crowd, looking at his fans with an expressionless look across his face. This didn't last for long, as he smiled at them with the "insane" yet loveable smile that he is known for, and should have patented. Strangler walked down the ramp methodically, and as neared the ring, Living jumped up from his slumped position in the corner, got a head of steam and charged up onto the top rope, before he dove off with a spinning heel kick. Strangler sidestepped him and Living smacked the pavement with such a profound thud it echoed throughout the arena. Strangler seemed amused by Living, but as he went for him Hell came out onto the floor from behind and spun him around. He kicked him in the stomach and then swung him towards the guardrail. Strangler reversed it, and instead Hell smacked the steel. Heaven leapt from behind the Strangler onto his back, and began to claw away at his eyes... which was a bad move on her part, as the Strangler started to fall back and attempted to keep his balance at the same time.. but couldn't, and instead Strangler stumbled back near a set of ring steps, before he fell backwards. Heaven took the blunt of the blow, and appeared done for as her back hit the steps. Strangler got up and looked down at her somewhat apologetic, before he smirked. He turned around and was met with a chair to the head by Hell, before Hell and Living threw him into the ring. Hell & Living stomped away at the Strangler, before "Do you buy what The Sellout is selling?" was heard. The crowd erupted as Universal charged down to the ring with Megan Dawn behind. Universal slid underneath the bottom rope and took Living down with a lariat. He spun Hell around and hit him with a bevy of lefts and rights, before kicking him in the stomach and taking him down with a DDT. Strangler pulled himself to his feet with the help of the ropes, while Universal went for a quick cover on Hell. At the count of two, Living stomped him from behind, but just as quickly as he broke up the pin, Strangler turned him around and nailed him with a shot to the tender nether regions, before taking him down with a hellacious gutwrench powerbomb. Strangler got to his feet as did Universal, and they both did a quick 'throat slash' gesture with their thumbs. On the outside, Heaven went to begin to climb back into the ring, but Megan Dawn pulled her from the ring apron and the two engaged in a cat-fight, of which neither won... but the male fans in the audience were pleased by. Back in the ring, Universal quickly grabbed Hell and went for the 'Boston Massacre', while Strangler brought Living up and hit the 'Cha-Ching' at the same time. Strangler pinned Living while Hell tapped out at the same time, and Universal and Strangler were both victorious. Dawn joined Universal in the ring, before he looked Strangler and stuck out his fist. Strangler copied this, before both men touched fists as a sign of being united. Their three opponents left the ring, before Universal & Dawn made their exit. Strangler motioned that he needed a microphone, and was given one. The crowd silenced for a moment to hear the Strangler out as he began to speak in a stern tone. "Austin Davis...Dead... whatever you want to call yourself these days... we have a little message for you. You've come back to the fWo, at the request of Chris Finn, and managed to trick him into giving you control of the federation. Because of your treachery, and his total incompetence, the entire roster is being made to suffer. There are some of us who have vowed not to take this sort of treatment, and we're prepared to do something about it. But it has proven to be somewhat difficult.. for even in his weakest moment, Chris Finn somehow managed to give you an ironclad contract. Now, before you and your little horde of fools break your arms patting yourselves on the back, we'd like to put a challenge on the table, should you choose to accept it. You haven't been cleared to wrestle until the 23rd of January, but we would like the opportunity to be the one to welcome you back, with a match on Slaughter. But your glorious return, it deserves more than just some silly little match.. it deserves a match of great importance.. don't you agree? So what we propose is this: If we manage to defeat you, you will give up your power in the fWo. It's as simple as that. You step down as commissioner, and return to active duty as a wrestler. That's all. Of course, a man like yourself, you're probably thinking "Well, what's in it for me?" We'll tell you what's in it for you Davis... if you somehow manage to defeat us... we'll retire from active competition in the fWo - Forever. That is our contribution... our career. We await your answer... though we suspect a chance like this would be too good for you to pass up. In fact, we're counting on it." Death & Scott Collins vs. Ice & Perfect "Paranoid" plays and out to a large amount of boos walks David Spencer, aka Death. He shoots the crowd the bird as a large "4R" appears on the SlaughterScreen, and out walks Scott Collins to a mixed reaction, but a large one nonetheless. As he rolls into the ring, Spencer rolls out, and Collins flexes at him. "Crazy Train" starts playing, and out walks Ice, to cheers. He slowly makes his way to the ring and rolls in, eyeing Collins warily. "Humans Being" by Van Halen starts up, and out walks Perfect to his new music and a shower of boos. He soaks it in, before rolling into the ring himself. Ice and Perfect head to their corner, as Collins walks to his and gestures to Spencer to get in. Spencer climbs slowly up onto his corner, but is suddenly grabbed and tossed in by Collins. Collins begins stomping away, and Ice joins in the fun. Perfect shrugs and joins as well. Collins brings Spencer up as Perfect mounts the top rope. Collins whips him into the ropes and straight to Ice, who lifts him up and chokeslams him. Perfect then leaps off the top rope with a somersault legdrop onto Spencer. Spencer is out, but Perfect brings him up again. He whips him across the ring to Ice, who gorilla presses him up, and into a Death Penalty from Collins. Collins kicks the unconscious body of Spencer out of the ring, but is attacked mid-kick by Perfect, who nails him from behind with a low blow. However, he doesn't see Ice coming from behind him, and Ice locks in a full nelson, and turns it into a slam. He nearly covers, before realizing that it would be pointless, as it is still technically a tag match. With that in mind, he gets up, only to be met by fists of fury by Collins, who backs him into the corner. Collins then DDTs him out, and covers. Perfect, staggering up, sees this and attempts to break the count with an elbow. Collins sees it coming and moves, causing Perfect to hit Ice. Perfect is fine with this, and gets up immediately to trade blows with Collins. He gains the advantage, and leaps up into a hurricanrana. He hits it and rolls into a pin, but Collins kicks out at two. Perfect brings him up as Ice rests in the corner, and sends him into the ropes. He catches him coming off with a knee, then a Russian legsweep. He drops a quick leg and covers, but Collins kicks out at two. Perfect brings Collins up, and attempts a kick. Perfect brings Collins up again, but he can attack, both men are clobbered by a double clothesline from Ice. Perfect is up first, but Ice charges at him and clotheslines him over the top. He then heads over to Collins and brings him up, before charging at him with a big boot, knocking Collins over the top as well. Showing amazing agility for a man his size, Ice leaps onto the guardrail himself and dives forward at Collins, smashing him with a forearm. He takes a moment to recover, before tossing Collins over the guardrail and climbing back over himself. He grabs Collins by the tights, and throws him on top of the announce table.
