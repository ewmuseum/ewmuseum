fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Thursday, 02.22.01 "When in doubt, beat on some French guys!" -Mitch Wilson fWo Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) Q: I really want to join the fWo... when is it ever going to be accepting new members? A: The fWo looks to begin looking over applications beginning in November or December of 2000. This does not necessarily mean that a horde of new members will be brought in, but this is the time we anticipate that new members will be able to walk through the doors of the fWo. That said, the way to get into the fWo is through the Waiting List, which closed as scheduled on November 3rd. People who sent in applications within the allotted time period will be looked over, anyone who sent in anything after the deadline will not. The fWo will review each application thoroughly, and contact the people we wish to bring in. Next year the fWo will likely go another route of bringing new members in, so try then if you did not get placed on the waiting list on time. Q: How do you make your character images? A: The character images are made using Poser, 3DStudio, and Photoshop. You can find Poser for sale on the web at http://www.metacreations.com, 3DStudio can be located at http://www.autodesk.com/ and Photoshop is located at http://www.adobe.com/. These are the tools the fWo uses, but they are not all required to produce 3D images. Poser can be used alone, but the results you get are sub-par at best. The fWo creates the mesh (the 3D makeup) of the character in Poser, but we don't use Poser to render (draw) the image. Instead the mesh is imported into 3DStudio and rendered from that program, which gives us much better results in terms of color, sharpness, detail, and motion. 3DStudio is a very expensive tool, and usually requires a high-end system to run. Alternate 3D applications that will work are SoftImage, Lightwave, and Ray Dream Studio. No matter what you use in the end, your system needs to have at least 32MB of RAM, a 200+ speed processor and a large hard drive to do much high-end graphical work. Q: I bought Poser, but I can't get it to work... any advise? A: The biggest problem that people run into when using Poser is that they run it on a system that is too old or too slow to give them great results. Unless you are using a system with a 400+ speed processor, don't bother trying to do much animation... the system and Poser's poor rendering engine will usually crash. Poser is designed to do very low-end 3D work, which is why the rendering engine is so poor. Even buying and using a cheap 3D application like Ray Dream Studio will give you infinitly better results. If you've never worked in 3D before, Poser can be daunting to learn. Luckily, there are several great tutorials online that can teach you the program using screen captures and RealVideo lessons. Q: How do you create your logos and non-3D stuff? A: We use Adobe Photoshop, which is the undisputed king of graphic tools in the computer world. It's also the king of expensive tools, as Photoshop usually runs around $750 dollars for the full version. Photoshop is a very high-end tool, and it's main purpose is to create offline (printed) material at high resolutions. Using it for the web allows you a great deal of freedom, but can be considered overkill if all you do is art for your eWrestling promotion. Consider using Paint Shop Pro, which can be located at http://www.jasc.com/psp.html which can be downloaded in a demo version for free on many sites on the internet. You can also use Microsoft's PhotoStudio, which gives several wizards for creating banners and art. Q: Can you help me out with my web site, or design Poser art for my efed? A:Sadly, no. It takes quite a lot of time during the month to make sure the fWo site stays active and is updated with fresh new content, as well as other day-to-day responsibilities that the ownership entails. However, we do recommend checking out the very designer that created this site and his other work at kayfabe.com. They will more than likely be able to help you out more than we would, although it may be some time before they are actually able to assist you in some form. Q: My eWrestling character looks like one of the characters on your site. Can I use the art? A: No, we create our images for our players, who then have rights to the character. We understand that you might want the images for yourself, but it's simply not fair to our players or our viewers if we allow this to go on. Theft of artwork from our site (or other eWrestling sites) will land you on the Blacklist page, so don't do it... as it will look bad for you and the other players in the promotion you're in.
