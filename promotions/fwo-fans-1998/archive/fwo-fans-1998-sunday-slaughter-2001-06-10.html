Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Slaughter opened on the dark confines of what could be considered the catacombs of The Scope. One of the fWo's newest additions, Donaven Winters was slumped down in a corner of a small room. It's not completely dark, the outlines of a table and a few chairs are visible. Winters' head was down, hair hanging in front of his as he moved his finger around in a figure eight type pattern on the floor. He tipped his head upwards as he saw someone walk past the door, it was a crew member. He shrugged it off, seemingly paranoid that someone might interrupt his thoughts from the darkness. "Mike Heftel," he started talking aloud to himself. "Now, Jackhammer. The pieces of the puzzle are starting to fall into place. An interlocking jigsaw that will be put together over time. Every defeat, every victory, every action I make will put that puzzle together. Heftel was just the stepping stone, he is the epitome of what is wrong with today's world. He doesn't strive for success, he loves to lose. It's incomprehensible that he does that to himself. Maybe he loses for the fans? Does anybody really care about the twenty thousand people that pack themselves into an fWo arena week after week? I don't. I always look out at their blank faces, they cheer madly for a man they hated three weeks ago. Their minds altered easily by the simplest of actions inside of the ring." He stopped for a moment. He looked back up towards the wall across the room from him before he put his head back down. "All it takes is one word," he said. "One word can turn a nation of people against you. They're fickle, they have no true allegiance. They also rely on others. Mike Heftel is one of these people. You all love him for losing, he's never going to amount to anything in life and the same goes for Jackhammer. You cheer for him, all he does is lose. He has been against and with Heftel and BQ over the years. Always changing. Proving that you cannot trust anybody in this world. Which is why I go at it alone. And this is why Jackhammer will fall this evening. He is a future prototype of Society's disease. And he's soaking it up. Jackhammer, if you're listening to me right now, you are just another one of Society's wastes. Your Descent is coming sooner than you expect. Prepare for the worst." Donaven stopped moving his finger on the floor, he looked up and back down at his hand as a dark smile crossed his face, hidden by his hair and the darkness. Silently, inside of his mind and cold heart, he began to laugh. The pieces were coming and they were falling directly where he wanted them to. Regardless, you should be proud of that match, because as soon as you were pinned, justice was done. The new reign of Ric Chronos as the fWo World Champion began at the very moment that you found yourself too weak to overcome me. Notice that I say NEW reign, and not SECOND, because as we all know, I was never beaten for this title to begin with." Chronos strides back and forth, slowly, his arrogance clear. "And while we're speaking of justice, it seems that a travesty of justice was allowed to occur at Redemption. See, a certain man by the name of the Boston Strangler " The crowd erupts at the mention of the Strangler's name, and Chronos has to pause, his disgust with the crowd becoming more and more evident. "As I was saying, the Strangler got a fluke victory over Erik Kelly, in order to become the number one contender to MY title. Well, I don't allow such things to go by without notice. Strangler, you may have won the right to face me at Damned, but I can guarantee you that you won't make it to the pay per view. Now that I have the only person I can trust backing me," Ric jerks a thumb back over his shoulder towards Manson, "I think it's time I started clearing the playing field a little bit. Strangler, you had better grow eyes in the back of your head, otherwise, you'll never see what we're going to do to you." Chronos hands the microphone off to Adam, who glares at the entrance ramp briefly. "I'm a man of few words, so I'll make this short and sweet. Insane Wonder, GET YOUR ASS OUT HERE." Adam and Chronos glare towards the entrance ramp, and a few moments later, "Sugar" by System Of A Down brings out the Cajun, microphone in hand. He cautiously approaches the ring, and Chronos and Manson back off a little to allow the smaller man to climb in. "Hey, Adam, Ric." Wonder visibly gulps after saying their names. "I'm here, just like you asked. Did you call me out to tell me that Tuna gets a title shot?" Manson and Chronos share a glance of exasperation before Manson stalks over to the cruiserweight. "No, you idiot. We're calling you out here because we're giving you the opportunity of a lifetime," growls Manson. "You've worked in the past with both Ric and I, and we want to give you this one opportunity to join up with us." Wonder looks back at the behemoth, obviously under quite a bit of duress. "I.. um... uh... I'm not too sure. I mean, we did team well before, but I've got a good thing going with a shot at the Cruiserweight title, and I don't want to ruin that. Can I have a little time to decide?" queries Wonder. Manson tosses the microphone to Chronos, and in one fluid motion, snaps a claw hold onto the Cajun. "Wrong answer, punk," screams Chronos. "If you're not with me, you're part of the conspiracy against me. And we have to show everyone what happens to conspirators..." Manson lifts up the Insane Wonder, dropping him in chokeslam fashion to complete the Exhale The Vile. Knocked out, Manson lifts Wonder's prone body up, as Chronos continues to berate him. "You see, the conspiracy had a something they used against me, and I've decided that it's time for me to turn the tables, and use their own weapons against them. So, I hope everyone in the back watches this, and learns the same valuable lesson that Wonder is about to be taught. This is the price for going up against Ric Chronos, the 'Man Of The Hour'!" Ric drops the microphone, and steps behind the still dazed Wonder. Locking arms, Chronos twists, and Wonder ends up in a position very familiar to fWo fans. Ric Chronos has Insane Wonder in position for the Backdrop Driver. Before he can hit it, though, the ring is assaulted as The Boston Strangler, Eddy Fate, and Greg Davis rush in to prevent potential injury to the Wonder. Manson begins brawling with Fate and Davis, but realizes he's outnumbered, and ducks out of the ring. Strangler takes a wild swing at Chronos, who ducks, dropping the Cajun and bailing from the ring. Slaughter, meanwhile, heads to its final commercial of the night... Underground vs. Reckless Abandon Main event time on Slaughter, and the fans are more than ready. "Du Hast" starts up first, bringing out the Black Panther. "Engel" is next, bringing out his tag partner, fan favorite Mike Bear. "POWERBOMB!" rattles the eardrums of those in attendance, followed by "Break Stuff". Out from the back walks Erik Kelly, Vengence, and Matt Loco. Bear and Kelly take their place on the apron, as Panther and Vengence are set to start things off. The bell rings, and Vengence stalks across the mat towards the Panther. Panther springboards off of the nearest ropes and nails a missile dropkick on the big man, sending him reeling. Vengence is right back up on his feet and takes Panther's head off (figuratively, of course) with a stiff clothesline. Vengence rushes over and takes a swing at Bear, but Bear manages to duck. Bear tries to get back at him, but the referee cuts him off and yells at him to get back in his corner. Reckless Abandon takes advantage of the situation, as Kelly comes in and starts to stomp down on the former Wrestler of the Year as well. Bear finally steps back onto the apron, giving the referee a chance to get back to the match at hand. Panther manages to crawl over and tag in Bear, and Bear comes in a house of fire. He takes out Vengence with a mean DDT, but the referee gets back in his face. Apparently, the referee saw Kelly leaving the ring and went over to tell him to stay out until he was tagged, and he didn't see Panther make the tag. Bear is furious, but nonetheless Panther comes back in. Vengence stands, groggy from that DDT, and is met by a superkick from Panther. Vengence falls back into his corner, where Kelly makes the blind tag and comes in. Panther gets a few punches in, then rushes over to tag in Bear. The ref sees it this time, and together Bear and Panther double-dropkick the Eradicator. Kelly doesn't stay down long, but the Underground mates send the Eradication Inc. leader into the ropes, taking him over with a double hip toss. Bear makes the pin, but the Boston native manages to get a shoulder up at two and a half. Bear picks him back up and sends him into the ropes once again, but Kelly comes back with a shoulderblock. Bear is up quickly but runs into a big boot from Kelly. Kelly tags out to his partner Vengence, and they both stomp down on the former World Champion some more. Panther climbs the turnbuckle, and nails a Corkscrew Plancha onto Erik Kelly, effectively taking him out of the picture. Kelly rolls out of the ring, and Panther follows suit. Panther sends Kelly flying into the ringsteps, splitting them apart. Panther stays after him, smashing his head into the protecting railing and then the announcers' table. Panther whips him into the steel ring post, knocking the big man almost cold. Meanwhile, in the ring, Bear and Vengence are going at it. Vengence sends Bear into the ropes, but telegraphs the back body drop. Bear steps to the side and takes Vengence down with a Rocker Dropper, and covers. One, two, but not three as the other half of Reckless Abandon manages to get a shoulder up. Bear yells at the ref and slaps his hands together three times, but the ref puts up two fingers. Bear turns around, and finds a large hand around his throat. Bear manages to get a knee up into Vengence's midsection, breaking free. He picks Vengence up on his shoulders, and drops him hard with the Whiplash. The referee's hand lands on the mat once, twice, thrice, making the Underground victorious. Bear celebrates, not seeing as Kelly smacks Panther over the head with a chair on the outside. Kelly rolls in behind the former champ, and taps him on the shoulder. Bear turns around to get a kick to the gut, and his head between Kelly's legs. "POWERBOMB!" cries Kelly, and he plants Bear firmly into the canvas with the Eradication. The rest of the Underground appears at the entrance and starts to rush down. Ruin follows behind them, followed by Fear's little clique. Once again tonight, all three stables are in the ring and brawling. A seemingly endless line of zebras run down the ramp, sliding in and trying to keep these competitors apart, but their efforts are fruitless. The copyright notice and fWo logo come up in the corner of the screen as the three groups continue their Pier 6-er. Result: Underground by pinfall Jumped But then they faded out, as Slaughter headed backstage. The door to the locker room of the Boston Strangler was busted open, knocked off its hinges, and screams of pain were coming from inside the room. The camera took in the chaotic state of the room we saw earlier in the night... things wee overturned, crushed, broken. It looked as if a tornado had gone through the room. Another scream of pain was heard, followed by a pair of vicious, hyena laughs. Steam began to float into the room from the bathroom/shower area, and the cameraman turned towards the open door to that area. And looked in. Ric Chronos and Adam Manson stood, laughing. In front of them, handcuffed to the nozzle of the locker-room-style open shower in front of them, was the Boston Strangler. From the steam and the screams, it was obvious that the water was scalding hot. The cameraman dropped the camera and ran from the room, hollering for security. Meanwhile, the camera, laying on its side on the floor, captured Ric Chronos and Adam Manson beating the scalded Strangler with lead pipes as Slaughter went off the air...
