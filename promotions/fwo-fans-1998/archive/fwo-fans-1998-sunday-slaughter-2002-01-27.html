Again, For The First Time As soon as the man saw the place, he understood two things: first, that he has seen it before, in dreams so wonderful that his conscious mind would not let him remember them; second that it was a place of glory, blood and betrayal. He was standing on the far corner of Einhorn Avenue and Main Street, fifty yards from a billboard that declared "fWo Slaughter Live!" in twenty-foot screaming blue neon letters. He was here. The man walked up to the gates, now barren and empty this early before the show. A gum-chewing cutie with a bright red streak in her blonde hair sat in the box office listening to Papa Roach on her Discman and reading one of the tabloid magazines of which his mother was so fond. To the girl's right, in the arena's remaining display case, there was a poster showing Brand Frontier. The man knew he should get going -it was almost two o'clock and his meeting was at two-fifteen- but he paused a moment anyway, staring at the poster behind the highly polished glass. Frontier was standing with his hands on his hips, his chest thrust out, a stance that said "Here I am. Look at me and be afraid." His teeth were glowing white, a carnivore's grin on his confident smug? features. His eyes were a pale, faded blue. A movie star's eyes. "You voted me out," The man said to the man in the poster, the man who was but wasn't Brand Frontier. "You voted me out, but I made it anyway. What happens now, Brand?" "Hey, fella," the blonde ticket-seller called, making the stranger start. "You gonna come in, or stand there talking to yourself?" "I'm coming," the man said. "I've talked enough to this one." He got moving again, walking into the arena. Entrance "Be careful tonight, Eli," warned Poison Ivy as they entered the arena, "You and Trip might be in this hardcore match, you and Trip might be the two most competitive people I've ever met, but you're also both FRIENDS now... and there's no reason for you to kill each other." "Yes, Ivy," replied Eli, "But Trip and I are both adults... and we both have a job to do. I think we can handle it." Eli opens the arena door for her, and they enter. They're taken aback for just a moment at the space the backstage area has - this is their first trip to a German stadium in several years. "Listen," said Ivy, squeezing his arm, "I've gotta find Universal before the show starts." Ivy turns and walks down a corridor... one of many. Eli turns in another direction, following the general flow of traffic toward the lockerrooms. He opens a door--- --- and comes face to face with Donaven Winters. They stare at each other for a moment, before Eli Flair offers his hand. "Good match last week, Winters," he said, leaving his hand up. After a long moment, Winters shakes it. Eli nods at him, and walks past. "Maybe next time," continued Eli, "you'll be able to beat me on your own." The door closes as Winters turns around with fire in his eyes. He takes a step toward the door, but stops before he enters. "He's fuckin' dead," said Winters to himself. Zero stood against the interviewing rail that adorned the area set aside for promos. He looked across at the camera in front of him, then lowered his head back down to try to gather his thoughts. What would he say? What would make the impact that he so needed to have inside of Sean Stevens' head? He closed his eyes and traced his memories back to the years before. "Thirty seconds," said a technician. He looked over to Zero. Zero's eyes rose up to meet the technician's. He nodded his head. The thoughts were slowly coming into his head. On the drive to the arena, he had tossed a few ideas around before slowly discarding one or another for some particular reason. Feeling the moment that everything he had to say was about to have to be verbally presented intensed the moment more so, but it pushed his concentration level to a gentle calmness. His eyes opened fully to see the hand of the video director slowly count down. Five. Zero watched with a mute face. Four. His lips began to draw back. Three. His eyes began to narrow. Two. He pulled his stance upright. One. "Stevens," he said, low in tone yet articulated to present a clear representation of his inner feelings. He was doing a live backstage interview. It was different than his normal methods of communication inside of the wrestling world. "How has it been? Nothing quite like a shot from the past to help stir up the present. The problem is, you do not seem to be very open to this ideology. I question why. You even went as far as to have me banned from the ringside area last week. I traveled all the way to London, just to sit in the back for the whole night." His eyes opened ever so slightly as he altered his tone of voice. "You don't want me to come out? That was fine by me. I didn't even bother to make so much as an issue of it. You want to play things your way, I'll let you assume you have the reigns. You're Sean Stevens, 'Triple XXX.' You're the 'Blue Eyed Badass.' You can even go so far as to call yourself. 'Planet Earth's Champion.'" Zero arched an eyebrow at the last mentioning. "Yes, I remember you from HEW. I remember those days, those years. Your rival buddy always seemed to end up being Eli. In fact, I believe you're facing him tonight. Will it be more of the same? Will you be counting the ceiling tiles while Eli adds yet another win over you?" Zero paused as the camera closed in on just his upper torso. "During those days, you had Eli and I had Mason. It would stay that way for me for quite some time. In fact, the stretch in CWL consisted of mainly Havoc in my life. But then, there at the end, there was someone else. There was someone new, yet it was a familiar face." The camera completed the zoom in on Zero, from his neck up. "That person was you, Sean." He paused for a moment as his eyes narrowed back into their nearly closed glare. He took a long breath, paused, then slowly exhaled. "I remember those moments. There you were, someone who I barely knew but yet was able to see strength in. You didn't buckle under everything that was being thrown at you, but yet, you took everything to a greater level than was intended. You and I were about to have our moment. I saw the determination in you. I told you that. I helped you with a supporting word. And then you walked out. Your sense of determination turned into pride. You developed a self-concept that I can see the results of now in the fWo." He tilted his head sideways. "And I'm here to fix a problem that I somehow feel responsible for." The corner of his mouth emitted a small upward curvature. His eyes opened ever so slightly. "No Sean, you were not the reason why I came back to wrestling. But since I am here, there are loose ends that do need to be corrected. I'm starting at the most focal... and vocal... of those loose ends. Hello Sean Stevens. Don't worry about me coming down to interfere in your matches. I'm not going to run down the aisle just to throw a punch. That's not how this game works. You think you're in control. Live by that idea. Breathe it as if it were the truth, but you could die by that idea if you hold it too close to your heart." His eyes narrowed back up again and he straightened his mouth. "I'll see you around." The camera faded out on that statement. Zero kept the facial expression until the camera director acknowledged that it was complete. The director turned to Zero and told him, "Good work." He then diverted his attention back to the schedule that they had to maintain. Zero was just one aspect of a long list of priorities for the night. Zero nodded his head to the director, but did not verbally respond. Instead, he grabbed his bag from beside his ankle and began walking back down the hall of the locker rooms. His job from the booking sheets was done. In his head, he debated his choice of words. But the end result, as he knew, was all the same. It was rough around the edges, but at the heart, that was the Zero that had always remained neatly stowed away inside of Lint Douglas' body. Triple X vs. Eli Flair "Renegade" by Jay-Z and Eminem begins, its telltale opening beats signalling the arrival of "Triple X" Sean Stevens to the arena proper. Many of the German fans are booing, but a good number are giving him the respect he's worked for. After all, the cream rises to the top and it's just a matter of time, right? But that was the past. This is the present. Tonight, Triple X and Eli Flair are here to test each other's limits, just one more time. The bell rings, and the two men circle each other. The referee reminds them that this is a Hardcore match - no countouts, no DQs, Both men nod, and they lock up. Eli is bigger and stronger, and immediately walks Triple X backwards into the ropes. The referee is about to start a five count, but with no DQ, he can only stand back and count a pinfall. Eli Flair grabs Trip by the throat and throws him back toward the middle of the ring. Following through, he attempts to stomp on Trip's midsection, but Triple X is able to catch his foot, spin it, and land a well- placed elbow to the back of the knee, putting Eli down to the mat. Triple X held onto Eli's ankle and began to turn it - an anklelock submission hold of some sort, but Eli rolled with it and kicked back, breaking the hold and sending Triple X back a step or two. He rolled backwards and got to his knees, nipping up just as Trip got back to him, and they nodded their approval to each other, to the applause of the crowd. The two men shook hands, and circled again. Eli was once again able to get the better of Trip, pushing him backwards into the corner. Triple X was able to see this, however, and once he was close enough, he braced himself against Eli's stature and kicked his feet up to the middle turnbuckle, giving him the leverage. The buzz in the crowd had begun to grow, however, wondering what was on Triple X's mind... and they didn't have to wait long to find out.
