Survivor IV: Episode Six, 20 May 2004

20 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Following the strange matches put forth by the Disney corporation, the fWo Survivor wrestlers are determined to make up for it with a pure, take-no-prisoners wrestling show. A show that will prove to the world exactly what they are all about... and showcase the very best in professional wrestling... Will You Will, Or Will You Won't? The past few weeks had been trying for the Gold Team. They'd overcome the loss of two of their team members and put together a big win last week. Spirits were high coming into this week, despite losing Shawn Arrows at the end of the show. This week would be an easy one to win, right? As far as they were concerned, this was the first challenge that was based soley on wrestling talent, and they were certain it was one of the places they outshined the guys on Silver's side of the fence. Therein actually lied the problem. All of the Gold Team believed that they would be selected to wrestle in the one and only match on the card. Each one had plans for the match they would put together. They had not gone about the decision of who would be in the match by design. Brian Cage's design. He'd requested that they save the decision for airtime. Apparently, he knew that all of them wanted into the match, and he expected some good television. Enough with the setup, eh? Let's join our regularly scheduled program. The boys had arrived in their locker room, and things were already getting heated. Guess who was the main cause of the aggravation? "What the FUCK?! You four sons of bitches are keeping ME out of the match?!" Yep, you guessed it. Brookes Davidson. Rana stepped forward. He'd established himself for some time as the leader of this group both emotionally and physically, and now was his time to be the leader he was born to be, "Brookes, look at the facts, man. You're injured. We all know that you could put yourself through the match because that's the kind of guy you are. We respect that... but look at it from the perspective of the rest of the team... we need to put together the best match poss---" An insertion from Davidson, "Best possible match... right... which masically means you and Brock frolick around the ring like the boyfriends you are, and the rest of us stand around and cheer wildly as Brock BOLDLY takes on his fear of the ring. Well, fuck that. I'm not watching Rey Misterio, and fucking Potsy Webber do the tango!" Brock Tyler's eyes fell to the floor slowly as Brookes began yet another verbal onslaught of him and the rest of the team. Sometimes Brock, and really the rest of the team, wished that Brookes wasn't as good as he said. Otherwise, they'd have voted him off long ago. From the other end of the room, Benjamin Davis cleared his throat. With disdain in his heart, Brookes shot an icey glare at Benjamin. Everyone turned to see what Davis had to say. Everyone except his protege, Taylor Rogers. Rogers was looking away towards nothingness, wishing that his manager would sit down before this turned into ANOTHER altercation between the old man and the angriest person on earth, it seemed. "Honestly, I've grown rather tired of this man shouting foul words, and trying to bully everyone around with his abrasive attitude. What we need to do is have a formal vote, and be done with it. Now, there are six of us in the room---" Davis started, but was unable to finish. "Fuck that. You ain't voting for shit old man." "Now, come on... he's as much a part of---" came the meek voice from the corner of the room belonging to Stephen Hailowe. "Hey, Bill Gates, when I want your input I'll point to you and tell you to speak. Until then, shut the FUCK up. Thanks." Brookes shot back. Apparently, he wasn't going to let anyone speak tonight. The room fell back to an awkward silence. Rana, for the life of him, couldn't understand Brookes Davidson. Then again Brookes couldn't understand Rana Venenosa. The whole team was so different, so opinionated... so not-teamish. Something had to be done to restore order to this meeting. Once again, Rana had to be the glue that held it all together. "It's really simple, Brookes. We're not called "Team Rubicon"... you are NOT the end all be all of this team. We're putting to a vote who should be in the match. Brock?" Rana had regained order, and put a little shame on Brookes Davidson at the same time. Brock Tyler was never one to speak up, or be the first to do anything, so when his name was called, he looked warily in Brookes' direction. Davidson was clenching both his teeth and his fists, angry with himself for letting this meeting get away from him. Tyler smiled half-heartedly, "I'd like to see Rana and Rogers, personally." A soft chortle came from the the eldest of the men in the room as Benjamis Davis said, "I vote the same." Brookes turned his attention back to Davis. Benjamin caught his evil glare from across the room, and felt the heat from it. His hands went up in a protective gesture, "I mean Taylor votes the same." Whether or not that was true, we'll never know as Taylor Rogers seems to not be able to speak for himself tonight. The attention turned to Stephen Hailowe, who wanted to be in the match just as much as anyone, but he knew better than to rock the boat at a time like this. "Sounds good to me." Rana smiled. This waas exactly what he wanted. No Brookes. No Hailowe. No Tyler. No chance of ruining the match with a horribly blown spot. No chance of being injured by the INCREDIBLY stiff shots of Brookes Davidson. Of course, no one knew he'd wanted this all along. He didn't need to vote, and neither did Brookes. "Fucking sheep, I swear to God..." Brookes mumbled as he walked out of the room without so much as a handshake or wave goodbye. The other members of the team sat or stood looking at each other with worried eyes. "You don't think he's going to do something tonight, do you?" Brock Tyler asked his now good friend, Rana Venenosa. Rana just watched after Brookes leaving the room, and shook his head slowly. Who knew what Brookes would do? "I have no clue." A Dose of Advil Logic had been greeted as he entered the arena an hour earlier by an fWo official, who handed him a piece of paper. On that paper were five words, five words that made Logic's heart do flip-flops. "Tonight: Reno Davis versus Logic." Reno Davis had at least five times the credentials that Logic did. The Unwanted Angel was an accomplished submission grappler, and a capable high flyer. But that wasn't all by a longshot. Davis had been the Asylum United Kingdom Champion. He had won gold in the most lawless environment in the world today. He had been a Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling Tag Team Champion. He could compete with others as a team. It was perhaps that last bit of knowledge which infuriated Logic most. He knew Reno could be a team player. He certainly was when it came to drinking. As of late, however, it seemed as if Reno's only partner was the bottle that he carried at his side. When Logic had first entered the contest, he regarded Reno with respect. Now, he regarded him as something of a joke. A joke that Logic wanted to reach its punch line. That was why he now stood at the door to the fWo Survivor locker room. It was still an hour or two before the show began, and he knew full well that only one other man could possibly be inside this early. Reno Davis. The Unwanted Angel sat in a folding chair. He too held a piece of paper in his hand. Reno's reaction to the news, however, seemed to be less focused on nerves and thought, and more focused on imbibing. Drastic action needed to be taken. Logic walked over to Reno, and calmly snatched the Jack Daniels out of Davis' hands. Turning the bottle upside down, he emptied the remaining liquor onto the floor, tossed the flask in the air, and punched it across the room with a flourish. Reno began to mutter, and Logic immediately knew what the redheaded man was saying. "Alcohol abuse, I know, I know. But Reno, let's face it, the only alcohol abuser in this room is YOU." Davis knew Logic was right, deep down. But admitting that he had a problem was a different thing entirely. He stood, prepared to charge Logic, but the big man shook his head. "Shit, Reno...why the hell are you here, anyway?" The plain language caught Reno off guard. It was far different than the usual flowery speech of the Pensive Punisher, and it seemed to demand a response. "You know, man," Reno began, "I really don't know anymore." Logic, on the other hand, thought he knew perfectly well. "It's Amy Roche, isn't it Reno? That's why you're here...you're here to find her again. You could give a damn about this contest! You could give a damn about me, or Windsor, or Bantam, or Curiosity! Reno, at the end of this show, one of the guys on our team is going to be gone, and let's face it, there's at least a good chance that it WON'T be you. It's going to be someone else on this team who DOES give a damn! Someone who's dream is going to end tonight!" The expression on Reno's face began to sober, but Logic wasn't done. "And you know why, Reno? You want to know WHY we probably haven't voted your ass out tonight? Because, we know that if you could sober up, and stop with this drunken BULLSHIT, you're a great asset to this team. Tonight, Reno Davis, is your final chance to prove that. Tonight, Reno, you and I are going to step into the ring. Team Silver's only match tonight! You know what that means? This show has two matches. Two of Team Gold's best, and us. We're representing Team Silver. If you're sober...you can give me more than a run for my money. If you're not, Reno, even you while drunk knows that I'll take you apart." Reno sighed, and sat down. None of Logic's words seemed to have had an effect, after all. The frustration on Logic's face was evident. He was wasting his rare emotions on a drunken moron who didn't understand the magnitude of the opportunity that was before him. If reason wouldn't work, then perhaps it was time to see if rage would. "Reno, fuck Amy." That did it. The Unwanted Angel sprang towards Logic, screaming obscenities, and pouring punches into Logic's chest. The big man weathered a dozen blows, and then placed both hands on Reno's shoulders, pushing him back, before rubbing his now bright red skin. "That's it, Davis! If you don't want to sober up for yourself, for your future in the contest...then just do it for today! Do it because you hate me for saying that! Do it because you want to beat the hell out of me! I don't care! Just bring the Reno Davis that beat Kodiak Vic Creed and Ruben Ross to that ring! Because tonight, Reno, you and I are going to WAR!" Logic ducked out of the room and closed the door split seconds before Reno could reach him again. The Thinking-Man's Grappler was pleased to note a quickening of his heartbeat and breathing. He was ready for a battle tonight against a top competitor. And if Reno Davis could get his act together... Logic smiled at the thought. Sobering Reality Meanwhile, Reno Davis was left alone in the Team Silver locker room. He surveyed the scene carefully. His eyes were immediately drawn to the puddle of Jack on the floor, and the now broken bottle that had landed on the other end of the room, but, for once, he was able to tear his eyes from them. Instead, he now looked at the lockers in the room. Eight of them, seven still bearing the names of Team Silver competitors, men that he had disparaged mentally on his very first day of the contest... Kenji Lee, getting his ass kicked in a few moments. Bantam Diablo, jerkass in a mask. Steve Windsor, Big stereotypical dumb guy...probably, who cares? Doctor Curiosity, creepy guy with a creepy sidekick. Logic, Stupid haircut. Nick Santiago, He's in Survivor? And Christian Elijah was in the bathroom, Reno remembered. Most of the sentiments he still agreed with. Kenji Lee HAD gotten his ass kicked. Bantam WAS a jerkass in a mask, he knew virtually nothing of Windsor, Curiosity was creepy, and Santiago, well... But Logic now, as opposed to being a guy with a stupid haircut, was a guy with a stupid haircut who had pissed him off. Pissed him off by being right. He was here for Amy, but that seemed to be a less and less possible goal as time went by. Tonight, before him was a man who was undefeated in the contest. A man who had beaten a luchador in Bantam Diablo, a shoot fighter in Brookes Davidson, and a cheater par excellence in Doctor Curiosity. It was time, then, to see how well the big man fared against a tough son of a bitch who knew lots of submissions and many ways to rain death from above. Logic had gotten in Reno's head. He knew it. And more importantly, he knew what Logic was trying. If the big man could get in Davis' head, he could force Reno to act without reason. Force the Unwanted Angel to make mistakes. Against Logic, one error could be deadly. Davis smiled, despite this. He knew Logic's plan. And now that he knew it, he could combat it. Reno heard Logic walking outside. He was somewhat surprised the big man hadn't left, but decided now was as good a time as any to burst the big man's bubble. He opened the door, and smiled at Logic, his buzz from earlier drinking starting to fade. "You'd love for me to come out there tonight angry, wouldn't you?" Logic sighed, and nodded. Davis had found him out. He remembered back to the second show of the Survivor contest, and his thoughts after walking out on a disgustingly drunk Davis. For the pen truly was mightier than the sword. Could Logic defeat Reno Davis in the ring? Maybe, maybe not. But could he beat Reno mentally, intellectually, logically? The answer was an emphatic yes. Any other answer would be simply illogical. He was far less confident of that now. If Reno truly could get his act together, he wouldn't be as able to beat Reno mentally. And then, the maybe that he had thought of would become even more in doubt. "I'm not going to play THAT game with you. You're the technical wrestler, the guy who focuses on what happens in the ring. That game, Logic, I am more than capable of playing." Reno's words snapped Logic out of his thoughts. Despite the fact that his task for the night had just increased in difficulty tenfold, he couldn't help but smile. He loved nothing more than a match with an equal, and a sober Reno Davis was certainly that. He reached out, patted Reno on the shoulder good-naturedly, and headed off down the hall, calling out back to Reno. "See you in the ring, Reno..." Odd How Things Change It had only been a week. One measly week since the lot which played host the show designed to showcase the Survivor contest in all of its glory had buzzed with team solidarity. The reason? A much needed and hard earned victory for the Gold Team, who had already sent three of their own home due to what seemed to be turning into a permanent stay on the loser's side of the fence. Now, it was time for another challenge and the ties between the team's members were severed once more. Each arrived to the lot alone and kept the same company, or lack thereof, while preparing for the challenge, which would set one of them against the other in what was supposed to be an epic match-up while the remaining three would simply be given a standing place ringside to watch the action, in their own individual ways. Brock Tyler, a man who many considered to be the underdog in a contest full of people who definitely weren't, sat in the catering section mulling over his thoughts, most of them involving the formation of plans that would make those who doubted him and his abilities take notice. For so long he had been just a fan. Sure, he had been content with this, but the point of the contest wasn't to kiss the ground that his teammates walked on while feeding on the scraps they left behind. He had proved his worth and now wanted to prove that he could hold his own against any of them. In the other corner of the room, seated at a small table that hugged the walls tightly, was Matthew Wakeman, known to the masses as Rana Venerosa. He stared down at the healthy meal that sat before him, getting colder by the second. His mother had always told him to eat a balanced and nutritious dinner, no matter the circumstance. And it was good bits of advice like those that had elevated him to the top of the Gold Team's proverbial food chain. If any of them deserved to wrestle in the big match, it was him. One would think he'd know that. Yet, as he pushed his seat away from the table and walked out of the room, completely ignoring Brock Tyler, his only real friend in the contest, it was clear that he wasn't as easily convinced as one would think. Below them, in the lot's cold, dingy basement, the eerily unnatural glow of a computer screen barely allowed Stephen Hailowe to be seen. Judging from his strange choice of location and sanctuary, this was what he wanted. However, he couldn't fight the other feelings of want that were brewing within him. Sure, he had kept a constant goal of remaining under the radar throughout the contest, but was it really that necessary, especially if stepping out for long enough to wrestle a match could mean wonders for his career, both in and out of the ring? Logging onto Google, he ran a search for the word "priorities". If anything could help him make up his mind, it would be the internet. Meanwhile, in a bathroom on the complete other side of the lot, Brookes Davidson had already made up his mind. He wanted to wrestle. Not only did he want to, but he had to. There was no one else that was suitable enough. Staring into the mirror, his boyish good looks stood out in comparison to many of the weathered faces Fans Wrestling Organization fans had grown accustomed to. You wouldn't expect to find such conceit within them, would you? Of course you wouldn't. And that was what Brookes had been going for all along. He wanted you to take a glance at him and immediately want to get behind him. Then he wanted to rip the person you really should've been supporting's head off so that you despised him. Why? Simple. Because he was the best and he could, even when injured. He winked at himself. Yeah, he was just that good. If only others on the team, Taylor Rogers for example, had the same kind of confidence. Then again, what did the man dubbed 'the Terrible' need confidence for? His manager, Benjamin Davis, brought enough of it to their outfit already. No. All Taylor needed were his ears. The ears that allowed him to hear his manager's demands and carry them out. He had no motive for the evening. Truthfully, he would have much rather been at home with a good book or some popcorn and a movie. Davis, on the other hand, had already begun preparing a speech telling the rest of the Gold Team just why Taylor Rogers should be apart of the big match, should he need it when the time came. He began his usual routine, sliding his yellow elbow pads onto his arms. They fit snugly. Yet, for some reason, they just didn't fit. And as all of this went on, the lot slowly began to fill with people, people who seemed to be doubling in amount with every show that Cage and Smyth put on. The ring sat, menacing, in the middle of this sea, white lights shining heavily over top of it. Only two of them would feel their heat. The others would simply be left out. And to think, it had only been a week. The Big Man Is Frustrated The Golden Bear was seated at a table chowing down on some food in the backstage area of the arena when he heard the news. Tonight's show would feature only two matches, both matches would be singles matches. He had to find out who would be in the Team Silver match. He was hoping it would be him so he could show people what he was really made of. He needed this chance to prove to people that he belonged here and that he was no big goof that didn't talk to anyone and was a hack. The Golden Bear pushed his chair back and stood up. It was off to the locker room to find out who was in the match for his team. He really wanted it to be a match against Logic, the other big man on the team. He wanted to show everyone that he could take down the biggest and baddest anyone had to offer. Why not start with the other big man on the team? He would soon find out if he would get the chance as he arrived at the locker room door. Golden Bear entered the room and walked over to the bulletin board where the matches were posted for the night. He looked at the paper for a few seconds and turned around with a disgusted look on his face. Half of his wish came true, the match did involve Logic. However he wasn't the opponent for the big man, it was the drunk, Reno Davis. "God damn it, they pick some alcoholic reject to fight Logic? What is this crap?" The Golden Bear mumbled to himself as he looked around the empty locker room. He was disappointed to say the least. "Out of everyone, why the hell would Reno be picked? The man can't stay sober long enough to even compete in a decent match, let alone put on a classic match to win us a competition. Whoever is in charge of the booking around here needs to be slapped." The Golden Bear murmured to himself once again. He didn't really care if he was talking to himself or not, he was pissed and he felt like expressing his anger. If someone walked in and heard him talking, great, if not then oh well. Just then another Silver Team member did walk in, it was Bantam Diablo. "Hey Diablo, what the hell is going on with this bullshit of a match tonight? Logic versus Reno Davis? Who the hell set that match up?" The Golden Bear demanded to know. Bantam Diablo claimed to be the team leader so he was hoping he would have some answers. "The bastard gave me his word he'd show up sober. That's the only reason he's in the match." Bantam Diablo said quickly, walking back out of the locker room. Obviously not wanting to get into it with The Golden Bear. "What the hell? Reno Davis shouldn't be in this match. Logic is undefeated and they put a pansy like Reno Davis up against him and expect him to win? I could take down Logic without breaking a sweat and instead they send an alcoholic. Shows how much brains this team actually has." The Golden Bear said to himself once again. "One thing is for sure, if Reno Davis messes this up he's dead." The Golden Bear said as he walked out of the locker room heading back to the dining room to finish his meal. The Golden Bear wasn't happy about the match decision but at this point there wasn't much he could do. If Reno Davis was the man to face Logic tonight then that's the way it was. He wasn't going to jeopardize Reno's shot at a win just because he wanted to face Logic instead. He would play it fair as a lumberjack and do his job, but he wouldn't show favorites either. Reno Davis was going to have to prove himself tonight that was for sure. Two Sides to Every Story: Side One It had been mere moments since the Gold Team had decided on who they'd be sending into the ring later that evening and already you couldn't walk through the lot that the Survivors inhabited without hearing whispers, each coming from a different mouth and each telling a different story, all of them wrong. None of them knew what had really happened. None of them knew of the intense desire to compete that pulsed through the wrestler's bodies. None of them knew just how hard it was to let that pulsing cease in favor of watching two of their teammates progress forward while they'd simply be left behind. They were just spectators; stupid marks who only had ideas of characters and angles, rather than feelings and the real people who felt them. Yet, as Taylor Rogers shuffled toward the bathroom that doubled as his dressing room on show days such as this, the expression his pale face paraded through the hall made it seem like he didn't really know what happened either. All he knew was that for whatever reason he had been selected to go against Matthew Wakeman, or Rana Venenosa as the crowd knew him, in a match that Cage and Smyth had demanded be the match to end all matches. So many emotions ran through him at that point. He had grown up wanting to do nothing besides wrestle. Now he just wanted to go home. It wasn't fun anymore, the politics and shady business dealings that his superiors seemed to make constantly had caused it to be that way. Aside from that, regardless of whether or not he wanted to wrestle, he certainly didn't want to wrestle over those on his own team, who he had grown quite accustomed to over the weeks prior. He knew how bad they wanted it. Most importantly, he knew how bad he didn't want it. He couldn't just leave though. It wasn't in his character. And that's all he really was, wasn't he? A character? Made to entertain the fat slobs who come and sit ringside just to boo him so that their confidence might be boosted. A man with a mask pressed so tightly to his face that it almost completely made everyone forget just what the face beneath it looked like. Even he had started to forget. He reached the bathroom's door and placed his hand on the knob. It was either turn or turn back. Twisting, he made his decision... Confidence The scene had shifted backstage where Nick Santiago threw the snack table on the ground and the crash alone caused alot of commotion as stage hands ran off in different ways. "What the hell is this bullshit about Reno Davis representing half of our side against the Gold Team?" Nick shouted angrily as Pablo looked on with a smile. Pablo let out a small laugh before answering back. "Dont worry, I'm sure Reno can put on a decent show with Logic carrying his ass for team silver to gain an easy win." Nick calmed down a little and then looked up at Pablo and released a devilish smile. "If not I'll make sure he tastes some of me again. He got away lucky last time and it defintely wont happen again. If I have to I'll pull him outside the ring and knock him around a few times. "I've given the spotlight to the silver team these last few weeks and this is how they show me respect?" Nick said scuffing his feet against the ground. Pablo looked back at him then walked a little ways down the hallway and turned around. "Dont forget about the swerve to come soon." He said. Nick looked back and walked up towards him. "Trust me, I'm not forgetting." Nick said while turning the corner and then stopping abruptly as did Pablo. Nick peered up into Pablo's eyes and then spoke. "You know Pobs, the longer this contest goes the more confident I get." Nick said. "Good, confidence is a key element to success. As Papa once told me Nick, If you dont believe in yourself then no one will." Pablo replied. Nick's head shook in a compliance for a moment before Nick responded. "Papa was a smart man." Pablo looked down at Nick and it was obvious the very mentioning of Papa had shook a nerve in Nick. It'd been years since Papa's death and Nick had mostly just blocked it way not wanting to deal with the situation and truly grieve. "That he was Nick, that he was." Those were the words that beckoned from the mouth of Pablo Santiago as the two walked off in silence but yet with more confidence than ever before. Bump in the Road Benjamin Davis whistled a happy tune to himself as he walked toward the lot's makeshift gorilla position, the showdown between his client, Taylor 'the Terrible' Rogers, and Rana Venenosa drawing nearer with every step. The extra spring in his step showed his excitement. Obviously, he hadn't expected Taylor to be picked for the match. Now, with his managerial skills at ringside, he was going to make sure that he walked out the victor. "Why so giddy?" Came a venomous voice from behind. Davis needed not even turn around. He knew just who it was. And because of this, he kept walking. "Sorry Brookes." He said, yawning in order to mock the already irritated "Rubicon". "I don't have time for you at the moment. Can I take a raincheck...?" A stiff kick came zooming towards his head, knocking him into the wall that stood, unmoving, to his left and interrupting whatever retort he had planned within his now aching head. "Well look at that!" Brookes chuckled. "Looks like you just made time, doesn't it?" Grabbing him by the arm, Brookes began dragging him back towards the doorway he had just exited from. He was unconscious. "What? No response from the peanut gallery? I would've expected more out of you Benjamin." His mouth stayed shut as his body skidded across the hallway's concrete floor. It remained that way even as Brookes paused to open the door and then toss him inside of the room from which he came. "My apologies Benjamin." Brookes said unsympathetically. "If I can't beat Matthew, nobody will." Smiling widely, Brookes walked away from the scene of a crime that he had just committed. In mere minutes, the match would be beginning. Benjamin would surely miss it. And nobody would ever know why. Branching out It was turning out to be a bad day for Doctor Curiosity. Not only had Eegor forgotten to pretend to be deformed, but he had also failed to make any further breakthroughs in the design of his Curious Boots, which when finished would be certain to give him an edge in the ring. He thought nothing else could go wrong, until he saw the matches announced for the night. "Reno Davis against Logic? Vhat ze hell is going on? Ve're only allowed von match and zose buffoons are put in it?" The Doctor turned around, scowling furiously and throwing an evil glance over at Eegor. The Doc felt that he was beginning to be left out of the Team Silver decision making process. Maybe trying to conquer his compatriots one by one was the wrong tactic; after all, it was taking Logic an awful long time to come to his senses and agree to be Doctor Curiosity's new assistant. The door opened, and through it strode Bantam Diablo. Doctor Curiosity smiled, feeling that this was now the time to make his insidious mark upon another member of the team. "Vhat is going on, Diablo?" The expression on Bantam Diablo's face was unreadable. It probably had something to do with the big freaking mask that covered his features. "Hello Doctor. You have seen the card for the night?" "Jah, and it does not do our team justice! Vhy am I not in ze match? I only lost to zat meat-head Logic because of ridiculous circumstances. Zere should be room on ze card for ze more talented members of our team, like me... and maybe your good self." Doctor Curiosity left the bait out there, trying to snare the interest, and possibly the goodwill, of his masked team-mate. Diablo had his own agenda though. "Reno Davis, he is a danger, no?" The Doctor scratched his chin. This was not the reply he expected. Nodding his head, he continued to listen to the masked man. "He is a drunkard, and that's why he should not be team leader! Yet, he has promised to give up drinking for tonight, so he can wrestle at his peak." "Vell, I vill give up drinking too if it vill get me a match instead of me sitting back here like an expensive lemon! I demand..." Bantam Diablo raised a hand, and it actually shut the German up. As surprised as anyone in the room, BD continued. "Ah, but surely your clever, clever mind sees that Reno Davis is just setting himself up as a target. He and logic are in the spotlight!" "JAH! I vant zat spotlight!" Bantam Diablo paused, unsure why Curiosity didn't seem to understand. Maybe it was the language barrier? "No, Doctor. If Reno Davis wins, he does so against all the odds, and he takes the spotlight. Not just that though, he becomes a marked man. Everyone will be wary of him, and he will have a target on his chest." "A tar..." Curiosity started to reply before it finally sunk in. This could be a way for him to rise up in Team Silver. With the spotlight on Reno Davis or Logic, he would be able to become the power behind the throne. After all, he had Logic in his back pocket, didn't he? "If Reno Davis loses, we can all vote him out as a drunken loser. You see, doctor, this is a chance to become the puppet master, to control from the shadows, where it is safer. We'll see if Reno stays off the booze, and act accordingly." "Jah, I see vhat you are getting at. Of course, I did all along. I just had to test you." "Then we understand one another. Good day, Doctor Curiosity." The masked wrestler left the room, and left Curiosity's mind ablaze with potential. The chance of Reno Davis not drinking was close to zero.
