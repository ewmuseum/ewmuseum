...and now back to your regularly scheduled broadcast... Times are bleak for the fans Wrestling organization, a global institution one year into a historic and now tragic return to the industry of professional wrestling. Greeted with fanfare, innumerable height and a lucrative television contract, all the glitter and gloss has faded in the face of unconscionable violence and highly publicized controversies. Wrestling in front of a crowd of terrorists, Krow and Steven Shadows used cerrated knives in the ring as an angle, drug and alcohol addictions were exposed on live television…these were only a few of the reasons for TBS’ decision to take the fWo out of their timeslot following their baseball playoffs coverage. The effects of the recession had hit TBS and the fWo itself, forcing roster and live show budget cuts. As the Countdown PPV’s draw on this night, William Beaven and the fWo have pinned their hopes, dreams and most importantly, Beaven’s promise to return the fWo to prominence on the select few wrestlers still willing to bleed, break bones and fight for the fWo. What’s left will not be important on this three-night PPV, what will be is the aftermath. If the fWo doesn’t provide three nights that the industry and it’s fans laud with praise and excitement, then perhaps, four years removed from the infamous Countdown Riot…the fWo will go gently into the good night this time. Along with a quick kick towards the groin, just for good measure… except it was blocked. Ric’s left hand had darted down, intercepting Frenchie’s right leg. This didn’t last long, Frenchie shooting Ric into the ropes to lead into an exchange of hiptosses and leg sweeps, neither man gaining and outright advantage before coming to a standstill again.
