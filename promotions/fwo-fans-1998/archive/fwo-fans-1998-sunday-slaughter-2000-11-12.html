Sunday Slaughter, 12 November 2000

12 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Arrivals As of last Sunday, eight men remained. With the Flying Frenchie rendered unable to compete with his knee injury, Primetime has secured himself a bye to the semi-finals. And as of tonight, only six men remain. Wretch. Ric Chronos. Mitch Wilson. Erik Kelly. Mike Bear. Crusader. A camera crew caught up with each of those individuals as they entered the ON Center in Syracuse New York throughout the day. The first arrival was the self-proclaimed "true fWo World Champion - Mr. Main Event" Mitch WIlson. As the first 'Grand Slammer' walked down the arena entrance, the camera zoomed in for a close-up... presuambly for a comment on tonight's match with Erik Kelly. Wilson smirked, before offering these words: "Tonight, Kelly, I'm going to prove that I'm too damn good for your own damn good... and there ain't a damn thing you can do about it." The image of the smirking Wilson faded to a shot of "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly and his lovely valet, Pamela Dee. The beauty and the beast had their own things to say about Kelly' opponent of the night. "In a few hours... I'm going to do something for the fWo that should've been done long ago, Wilson. Just as I did to your friend Ice. I'm going to unleash three hundred and thirty pounds of pure unadulterated eradication on your ass... and unfortunately for you, that's too damn bad for your own damn good." Kelly menacingly glared at the camera, while Dee winked at the camera. That highlight cut to a shot of Team Rateings, and it's fearless leader, Big Wretchy. The self-proclaimed Ratings Saver had been unusually silent as of late, but no matter - tonight, he had something to say. "Ric Chronos, you can claim to be the 'Man Of The Hour', but the fact is, I've been in an hour long marathon match this year, and you haven't. The fact is, you can claim to be the Man Of The Hour, you can claim to be the Woman Of The Hour, you can claim to be the Retard Of The Hour, but tonight... your nothing but a pawn in Wretchy' game to become Wrestler of the Year... oh, and if nobody is down with that, I've got two words for ya - YOUR MOM~!" Wretch and his lackeys shut the locker room door behind them, while Slaughter cut to a shot of the aforementioned 'Man Of The Hour'. Ric Chronos entered the arena with a duffel bag in hand and a all-business like expression on his face. Chronos stopped and stared at the camera, offering these words of advice for Big Wretchy. "Wretch, I'm quite impressed with what you've achieved over the last six months, but the past is the past... and what is waiting for me in the future is a shot at that bastard Primetime next Saturday if - WHEN I get past you tonight. You keep your lackeys out of the ring, because you can be sure I'll have a big black cat watching my back. Time's up, Wretch." With that we went to our final comments from the other two remaining superstars, Mike Bear & Crusader, who just so happened to be entering the arena at the same time. Bear & Crusader stared at each other intently. Bear with his girlfriend Brittany at his side, and Crusader with his beautiful fianc?e Clarisse at his. The females tried to pull them away from each other, but both Bear & Crusader had something to say. Bear smirked as he walked up to the man who defined gravity. "Crusader... I respect you and all, but tonight is not going to be unlike this past July when I defeated you in the Next Level tournament. You will fall victim to the Whiplash, because I'm the only wrestler undefeated in tournament action this year thus far." Crusader had something to say about that. "You may have defeated me then, yes... but tonight, it's a whole different story. That was five months ago. We've both come a long way since then. You may be able to do a little gravity defying of your own... but tonight, the G101 will be dropped, and school will be in session. I'm your teacher... and in that ring, you're going to be in MY classroom." Bear chuckled, before saying "We'll see." And with that, tonight we would know who the final four wrestlers of the WOTY tournament would be. Primetime was one of them. But who else would join him in the semi-finals this coming Saturday at Fallout? Slaughter was about to begin, and we were about to find out. "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson vs. "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly The November 12th edition of Sunday Night Slaughter begins in the ring for it's first match of the night, and it was a matchup in the Wrestler Of The Year tournament, one that pitted two of the fWo's top stars against each other. The fans inside of the ON Center braced itself as fireworks kicked off the night's action, before everyone heard a mighty yell of POWERBOMB is heard, followed by "Break Stuff" hitting. The crowd comes to their feet for the mammoth Erik Kelly, accompanied as always by the lovely Pamela Dee. Kelly steps up and over the top rope into the ring and waits for his opponent. "Last Cup Of Sorrow" begins, and the crowd erupts in boos as fWo International Champion Mitch Wilson starts to make his way to the ring, looking focused yet arrogant as usual. He steps into the ring and unstraps his belt, before handing it to the ref who hands it off to the timekeeper. Wilson then turns to Kelly and stares him down. The ref signals for the bell, and Wilson runs into the ropes and charges at Kelly. He hits him with a shoulderblock, but Kelly isn't fazed. Wilson tries it again, but again Kelly no-sells it. Finally, Wilson tries it a third time. Kelly grabs him, lifts him into the air, and hurls him to the mat with a spinebuster. Kelly grabs Wilson and brings him up, before hurling him into the corner. He charges in with a lariat that finds its mark squarely, and Wilson stumbles out. Kelly grabs him, spins him, and plants him with a tilt-a-whirl side slam, before making a cover. The ref makes the count, but Wilson kicks out at two. Kelly brings Wilson back up and lifts him up, before tossing him to the mat with a fallaway slam. Kelly walks over to Wilson and drops a tree trunk-like leg, before covering again. Again he gets two. Kelly brings Wilson back up to his feet once more and runs into the ropes, before nailing Wilson with a hellacious clothesline that sends Mr. Main Event for a flip. Kelly brings Wilson up again and wraps a hand around his throat, setting up for the chokeslam that precedes the Eradication. Wilson knows this, and lashes out with a desperation kick to the genitals. Kelly releases his grip and drops down onto all fours, and Wilson wraps him up and rolls into a la magistral cradle. Kelly kicks out at two. Wilson takes a moment to shake off the punishment he's recieved, then brings Kelly back up. He double-underhooks the arms, then spikes Kelly with an inverted double-underhook faceslam. Wilson knows this won't be enough to put Kelly away, and brings him back up. He whips him hard into the corner, before stalking in and unloading on Kelly with monstrous, stinging chops. Mitch then begins stomping and kicking away at Kelly, driving him down into a seated position. Mitch backs up across the ring, measures Kelly, then charges across the ring. He drops down three-quarters of the way across into a baseball slide, and both of Wilson's feet slide directly into Kelly's crotch. Pamela Dee shields her eyes at ringside, and as he gets up, Wilson smirks towards her. He then turns back to Kelly, and brings him up. He hooks Kelly by the head and grabs a leg. Wilson tries to lift Kelly up, but he can't do it. He tries again - no dice. Wilson finally musters up all his strength and lifts Kelly up off the ground before spiking him with a picture-perfect Superstar Suplex. Wilson makes the cover and hooks the leg. To his amazement, Kelly not only kicks out but powers out, throwing Wilson a few feet, at two. This angers Wilson, who violently brings Kelly back up. He takes Kelly down with a snap suplex, and holds on. He then brings Kelly up again, before taking him down with a regular suplex. He brings Kelly up a third time, and completes the trifecta with a gutwrench suplex, driving Kelly to the mat. Wilson signals that it's time to end it, and brings Kelly back up to his feet before lacing the legs and hooking the head for the Headliner. He tries it, but in an amazing show of power, Kelly refuses to budge. Before Wilson can react, Kelly's hand shoots out and grabs Wilson by the neck. Wilson releases Kelly, and Kelly lifts him high into the air and slams him into the mat with a chokeslam. Kelly brings Wilson back up, and shouts the dreaded word to the crowd - POWERBOMB!. He places Wilson between his legs for the Eradication. On the entrance ramp, Lance Star begins sprinting towards the ring. Inside the ring, Kelly lifts Wilson up and Eradicates him. Kelly goes to cover Wilson, but Star, reaching the ring, grabs his attention by continuing to sprint - after Pamela Dee. Kelly steps to the outside of the ring and begins pursuing Star. They circle the ring twice, before Kelly's charge is suddenly halted - a recovered Wilson has rolled to the outside of the ring, laid in wait, and DTHed Kelly head-first into the steel steps. Star stops his pursuit of Pamela to help Wilson roll the massive Kelly into the ring. Wilson follows him in, then brings Kelly up and takes him to the mat with the Headliner. Wilson makes the cover, and three seconds later advances to the final four. Wilson rolls to the outside and joins Lance Star, who blows a sarcastic kiss at Pamela Dee as the duo heads to the back. Dee enters the ring to check on Kelly, who makes his way to his feet, righteously pissed. Wisely, the ref vacates the ring before damage can be done. Kelly continues to rage inside the ring as Slaughter went to a commercial break. Result: Wilson by pinfall Pre-Slaughter Fallout Booking Meeting, Part One Slaughter returned with more pre-taped action from earlier in the day. Backstage in a meeting room, the acting owner of the fans Wrestling organization, stood with a pointer stick and a goofy look on his face. Before him sat Jeremiah Frankton, Stevie Roberts & Dick Venom, who were well known members of the 'booking committee' within the ranks of the fWo. Armstrong didn't hear it though... and instead continued. "That's right... Wretch & Mike Bear against Damnation - for the fWo World Tag Team Titles. What an idea! I'm a genius... what do you guys think of it?" Frankton rolled his eyes, Roberts sighed, and Venom again muttered something about being a better booker. Armstrong didn't care about how they felt, and instead looked to get him some bun cake, before continuing with the meeting. Wretch vs. Ric Chronos Sunday Slaughter returned from an ad break to the sounds of "Christian Says" by Tones on Tail. Ric Chronos, a man who has overcome alcoholism, and a severely injured neck, steps out on the entrance ramp to a large reaction from the capacity crowd, which is even upped to a higher level when "Paint it Black" by Gob starts up, and Wretch steps out. He does a Hogan-esque pose, before cupping his ears, causing the crowd to pop. Wretch runs to the ring, slides in, and the bell rings. He stands up, and Chronos looks for a clothesline. Wretch ducks and nails a few fists to the face of Chronos, before sending him into the ropes. Chronos comes back and walks into a sidewalk slam by Wretch. Wretch drops a quick legdrop onto Chronos, and then heads to the top rope. He looks for an elbow drop, but Chronos moves out of the way. Chronos brings Wretch to his feet and nails him with a quick samoan drop. Chronos goes for the first cover of the match but Wretch kicks out at two. Chronos brings Wretch up and sends him into the ropes. A tilt a whirl backbreaker attempt from Chronos is turned into a huracanrana by Wretch. Wretch goes for another one as both men get up, but Chronos turns it into a sit down powerbomb. He holds on for a cover, but Wretch again kicks out at two. Chronos lifts him up into a DDT attempt, but Wretch turns it into a northern lights suplex with a bridge. At two Chronos gets out of the bridge, and pin attempt, but Wretch maintains the advantage with a few boots to the head of Chronos. He brings him up and nails a Wretchinoku Driver for two. Wretch begins to get annoyed with the fact that he can't put Chronos away, and then he complains to the ref. A mistake by Wretch, as Chronos nails him with his own finisher, the Depression, from behind. He covers, but at two, Wretch has the sense to get his foot on the bottom rope. Chronos, frustrated, slams his fist on the mat, before going back at Wretch with some hard punches to the throat area. Chronos brings Wretch up and perches him on the middle rope. Chronos follows after by climbing to the top. He hooks him for a full nelson bomb from the top, but Wretch blocks it. Chronos keeps struggiling to execute the move, but Wretch turns it into a top rope diamond cutter. The crowd gasps, knowing Chronos' past with neck injuries, and Wretch covers. One. Two. Chronos is okay, and he kicks out. Wretch brings Chronos up, slams him in the middle of the ring. He looks for an asai moonsault, but Chronos gets the knees up, and Wretch drives himself into Chronos. Chronos brings him up, and nails an implant DDT. A cover by Ric, but Wretch kicks out. At this point, Mike Bear makes his way to ringside, to lend some moral support for Wretch. Chronos brings Wretch up, and looks for a vertical suplex. Wretch from behind grabs a waistlock, and runs him to the ropes. He turns it into a pinfall attempt, but at two, Chronos shoots Wretch into the ropes. Wretch bounces back, ducks a clothesline. Wretch hits the other side and nails a flying lariat. He covers quickly, but just as quickly, Chronos kicks out. Wretch looks to whip Chronos into the ropes, and thinking quickly, and nonchalantly, Bear reaches out to trip Chronos on the whip. The only problem is, Chronos reverses the whip, and Bear ends up tripping Wretch. Wretch falls, and begins to question what Bear did. Bear looks apologetic, but in the meantime, Chronos nails the Chronosphere on Wretch. A three count later, and Ric Chronos advances on to the final four, and Fallout. Bear gets into the ring, and tries to help Wretch up. Wretch shoves him back, and Bear takes offense to this, shoving him back. A horde of referees follow, and break the melee up, as Slaughter cuts to break. Result: Chronos by pinfall Pre-Slaughter Fallout Booking Meeting, Part Two Slaughter rolled on backstage, as the pre-taped booking meeting continued. "Er, how'd that get in there?" Mike Bear w/ Brittany vs. Crusader Slaughter returned from commercial once more to the sounds of "Switchback" by Biohazard. Bear covers, but at two Crusader gets a shoulder up. Bear brings Crusader to his feet, and nails a sidewalk slam for another two count. Perplexed what to do with him, he sends him into the ropes, and looks to back body drop him out of the ring. Crusader lands on the ring apron, and when Bear turns around to go to the outside, he is met by a huge springboard dropkick from Crusader. Crusader hits the mat, and Bear jumps on for a cover. One, two, but Crusader has the tenacity to kickout. Bear brings Crusader up onto his shoulders, and looks for the whiplash. Crusader falls out of it, and grabs Bear from behind. He hooks him and drops him to the mat with a big bulldog. A cover by Crusader, and a kickout by Mike Bear. Crusader slams Bear to the mat and looks to go to the top. However, he takes his time getting there, and Bear recovers to crotch him. Bear then follows after him and nails a huge superplex. Bear covers, thinking he has it, but the ever tenacious Crusader still is able to kick out of the move. Bear brings Crusader up to his feet and nails some stiff forearm shots to the head, and sends him into the ropes. Crusader ducks a clothesline and stays behind. Bear turns around, and Crusader nails him with a boot to the gut followed by a twist of fate. Crusader jumps on for a cover, but Bear manages a shoulder up at two and a half. Crusader goes for a Michonoku Driver, but Bear turns it into a small package. One, two, Crusader gets out, and gets a quick pinning position for himself. One, two, Bear bridges out of it, and both men get to their feet. Crusader charges with a clothesline, and Bear ducks it. Bear stays behind and nails a beautiful full nelson bomb. Cover by Bear, and Crusader kicks out. Bear brings him up, and goes for a whip. Thinking nonchalantly(much as Bear did earlier) Wretch goes to trip Crusader, but Crusader reversed it. Bear goes down, and then quickly up, as he turns to Wretch and asks him what the hell happened. Before Wretch could respond, however, Crusader comes from behind and nails a belly to back suplex. He then points to the heavens and climbs up. Gravity 101 connects, and the three count is academic. Crusader takes the win, and advances. Carnage (c) vs. "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly Metallica hits the arena in full blast, and the fans cheers drowned out the noise, as Erik Kelly makes his way to ringside, alongside Pamela Dee...and, yes, not one, but TWO cinderblocks. As they near the edge, Carnage clears the cobwebs out of his head, and sees his predicament. He sees no other choice but to take Kelly with him. As a result, he grabs hold of the arms of the mighty man; Kelly, a bit surprised by this turn of events, cannot shake loose. They fly off the edge together, and head for an inevitable accident at the bottom. They fly head-over-heels onto the floor, the tricycle flying a few feet away from them. The ref looks at both of them, and has no choice. He begins to count them out. Meanwhile, medics get ready to assist both of them. However, Kelly, who didn't take as much of a blow as expected, gets to his feet at five. He wobbles toward Carnage (not like a Total Eclipse wobble...more like a drunken stupor wobble.). He picks him up, and tosses him toward the ring. Kelly continues to do so until they reach the ring (the ref has stopped counting.) Kelly tosses Carnage into the ring, and sees the table, all ready for an Eradication and a new Hardcore title reign. Kelly eyes up the table when an image hits the SlaughterScreen; Vengence has decided to make his presence known, even if it's only on the screen. Kelly looks at this and climbs to the second rope, and begins to motion for Vengence to come out here. Vengence just laughs at Kelly; Kelly, unable to take any more of this, decides to go after him. However, as he tries to climb down, he feels himself being picked up. Obviously, he forgot about the match he was in. Carnage, shaking the previous pain inflicted on him, nails him with a snap German suplex onto the table. The fans go crazy at this, and Carnage climbs out of the ring. He grabs the garbage can, and slides back into the ring, hustling as he does so. He puts it on top of Kelly, and climbs to the top rope on the other side. He signals for the Maximum Carnage, and takes off, nailing the elbow onto of the garbage can, which still sits on top of Kelly. He covers Kelly, and gets the three. Carnage celebrates rather slowly, as he is shaken up. He grabs his belt and takes off up the entrance ramp, leaving Kelly to ponder what he shall do to Vengence for this. Carnage stopped to look back at Kelly one last time, before raising his arms in victory. But when he turned around to face the arena entrance, he was speared by The Motha Fuckin' Abusah Of Yo Momma. Abusah stood over Carnage and bragged about what he just did, before Hartt joined him as well, and challenged him to a handicap Hardcore Title Match at the Fallout pay-per-view... Result: Carnage by pinfall Pre-Slaughter Fallout Booking Meeting, Epilogue Slaughter continued on with it's final segment of the night, and the final part of the "Pre-Slaughter Fallout Booking Meetings". Armstrong was the only one left in the room, as it seemed as though nobody was in the mood for bun cake at the moment, and left in a hurry. Armstrong shrugged. "That just means more for me... those fools don't know what they're missing, I'll tell you that right now." Armstrong chomped down a piece of bun cake, and washed it down with some cheap champagne. Looking around the room, he reached for the phone, presuambly to make an important phone call. Quickly, he dialed some numbers. It rang several times, before someone must've answered. "Yes, this is James Armstrong... I was just wondering about the status of Pierre. Yes, Frenchie. Uh huh. Yeah. Well, can he at least walk? I understand he's too hurt to actually wrestle, but can he at least walk without much difficulty? He CAN? But he's too hurt to actually compete, you say? Huh. Okay, well, thanks anyways." Armstrong hung up the phone, before walking over to a desk, and finding a 'post-it' note. There, he wrote a note, and posted it to a favorite porn magazine of his. After that, he left the room... but the camera man remained long enough to find out what the note said. "Call Pieree - find out if he would like to referee Primetime/Mitch on Saturday." And with that, Slaughter headed to the ring, for the main event. Primetime vs. "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson Slaughter heads to the ring for its main event, a huge four-way match based on the winners of the night's WOTY tournament matches. "Switchback" starts up, and the fans cheer as Crusader begins to make his way to the ring. He slingshots himself over the top rope and into the ring. His music is replaced by "Last Cup Of Sorrow", and the cheers are replaced by boos as fWo International Champion Mitch Wilson makes his way to the ring. He unstraps the belt and hands it to the timekeeper, before climbing up the steps into the ring. He takes a corner opposite Crusader. "Christian Says" then begins, and the fans cheer once more as Ric Chronos makes his way to the ring. He steps into the ring and measures up his opponents, before taking a third corner. "Snap" starts, and the boos are thunderous as the match's final participant, fWo World Champion Primetime, slowly makes his way to the ring, basking in the boos. He reaches the ring and steps in, before tossing the belt to the timekeeper on the outside. He then turns his attention to Mitch Wilson and charges at him, fists-a-flyin', and the ref calls for the bell. Ric Chronos and Crusader advance on each other as well. Primetime and Wilson trade fists, and Primetime finally gains the advantage. He whips Wilson across the ring and plants him with a powerslam as he comes off the ropes. Primetime follows that up with a quick, hard elbowdrop square to the throat. He brings Wilson back up and shoves him into the corner, before blasting Wilson with a series of lariats. Primetime finally stops, and Wilson stumbles out of the corner onto all fours. Primetime winds up and kicks him square in the kidney, causing him to slump to the mat. Primetime makes the cover, but Wilson kicks out at two. Across the ring, Ric Chronos whips Crusader into the ropes, but Crusader leapfrogs up and over Chronos, landing behind him. He reaches back and grabs Chronos by the neck, before dropping down with a reverse neckbreaker. He then runs into the ropes, leaps onto the second strand, and leaps off with an asa"h"i moonsault that finds its target. He covers, but Chronos lifts a shoulder after two. Crusader brings him back up and attempts another whip, but in the chaos, Chronos slams into the back of Primetime. Primetime quickly spins and decks Chronos, knocking him to the mat. Chronos looks up, and sees his own personal demon - Primetime. Re-energized, he reaches out and grabs Primetime by the foot, tripping him to the mat, before leaping onto him, mounting him, and wailing away with punches to the head, finally opening up Primetime's forehead. Shrugging, Crusader turns away from the mess to Wilson, who is slumped in the corner. He walks over, but Wilson grabs Crusader by the tights and pulls him into the corner, where Crusader's head slams into the top turnbuckle. With Crusader prone above him, Wilson punches up, nailing Crusader with a low blow. Crusader stumbles out, and Wilson pulls himself to his feet and follows him. He spins Crusader around, hooks him, and hiptosses him over into a Mitch-inoku Driver. He reaches forward and hooks a leg, but Crusader kicks out at two. Wilson pulls Crusader up and begins to go to work on him with punches, as across the ring Primetime finally bucks Chronos off of him and makes his way to his feet. Chronos charges at him, but Primetime ducks and instinctively back body drops Chronos from the ring to the outside, where he lands with a splat. Primetime then steps out onto the ring apron, before leaping off with an elbowdrop that hits Chronos squarely. Back in the ring, Mitch has Crusader set up on the top rope, and climbs up, setting for a gutwrench superplex. He hooks Crusader, but Crusader fights back and finally shoves Wilson to the outside, where he takes out both Primetime and Chronos. Crusader shakes off the cobwebs as the others pull themselves to their feet, before stepping up onto the top rope and measuring them. He then hurls himself at his opponents with a plancha from the top, taking everybody out. Everybody is down for a few moments, then finally they begin to seperate themselves from each other. As it turns out, Crusader is paired off with Primetime while Wilson brings Chronos to his feet and throws him into the ring before following him in. On the outside, Primetime catches Crusader offguard with a rake of the eyes, before lifting him up high in a suplex. Primetime then dumps Crusader over the guardrail into the crowd. With Crusader disposed of, he then begins to dig under the ring, before pulling out a table and setting it up. Inside the ring, Ric Chronos has whipped Mitch Wilson into the ropes, but has made the mistake of lowering his head. Wilson quickly stops, hooks him up, and takes him down with the Headliner. Wilson makes the cover, but Primetime rolls in from the outside and pulls him off at two. He then brings Wilson up and attempts a whip into the ropes, but Wilson reverses it. Primetime comes off and Wilson repeats Chronos' mistake of putting his head down. Primetime hooks him up, lifts him, and charges towards the ropes. In an incredible move, he throws Wilson over the top rope to the outside with a Primetime Bomb. Wilson goes crashing through the table, scattering debris everywhere. Primetime then begins shouting at the downed Wilson, missing a recovered Crusader scurrying to the ring apron, where he goes up top. He then measures Chronos and flies off, hitting a beautiful Gravity 101 that pops flashbulbs across the arena. As he gets up however, Primetime calmly strolls over, grabs him by the hair, and hurls him over the top rope. He then brings Chronos up, slingshots him, and Snapshots him. Primetime makes the cover, and three seconds later, wins the match. As Primetime has his arm raised, Lance Star, carrying a towel, sprints in from the crowd, slides into the ring, spins Primetime around, and plants him to the mat with the Shooting Star. He then drapes the towel, which bears the logo of the Wilson Training Academy, over Primetime. He spits on the World Champion and goes off to help his friend Wilson up as Slaughter cameras focus on the bleeding, towel-draped Primetime, lying in the middle of the ring... Result: Primetime by pinfall
