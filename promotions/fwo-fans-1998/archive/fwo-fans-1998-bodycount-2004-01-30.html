Introduction It's the most important event of the year thus far... BodyCount... where the winner will go on to wrestle in the Main Event in Cyberslam. It is where careers are made... and broken. The Show Goes On Ted Tallison glanced around the parking lot and a worried crease came over his brow. There were too many trucks for one thing... the fWo traveled (back when they did travel, he reminded himself) light and quick to slim expenses, but there were easily three times the amount of trucks and people than usual. If Disney was trying to run them efficiently, he couldn't understand the waste in the transport. There was also the security... it seemed to be at least three times as tight as usual. This perhaps, was a good sign. Since the events of Countdown the previous year he had been quietly lobbying for added security. This business breeds insanity, and the extra expense in people wouldn't hurt. He hadn't known John Willis well, but remembered that one of his never-ending battles was to get more security. Well, it hadn't been in time to save him, but perhaps someone was finally listening. "Ted, what is all of this?" Chris Finn waved to Tallison, and Tallison nodded back with a vague expression on his face. "No idea, really... I didn't know they were doing all of this... I..." But Tallison spotted a trio of gentlemen that might be able to explain it all. Thomas Murphy, who rarely seemed to show his face around the fWo anymore, his seemingly always-present attorney Simon and Carl Michelle... the cheerful marketing manager that Tallison, and much of the locker room, had come to loathe. "Ah, Mr. Tallison," said Murphy, smiling a smile that never reached his eyes, "good to see you. And you are...?" Murphy was nodding toward Chris Finn, who extended his hand and introduced himself. Murphy nodded and turned to Michelle. "He should be pleased... you've got a new announcing partner tonight, or rather, an old one. Mr. Armstrong?" Murphy signaled to someone behind them and Tallison and Finn turned to see a very familiar face indeed... James Armstrong, one of the original announcers in the fWo and certainly not one of the favorite veterans. James Armstrong approached, smiling, and clapped Finn on the back. "Just like old times, eh you son of a bitch? Come on, we've got an hour or so... let's hit the local strip joints and drive by the Jr. High School, eh?" Chris Finn sighed, a man defeated, and allowed James Armstrong to lead him away. Tallison watched them go, and turned back to the three Disney executives with a questioning look on his face. "What's with all the equipment? There's more stuff here than we ever bring to shows..." Michelle nodded wide a bright, wide smile. "Well Mr. Tallison, glad you asked. See, we at the Walt Disney Corporation are determined to make tonight's event a success. As such, we have decided to give the event the proper attention it deserves and thus increase interest and buy rates. See, we have amazing confidence in our brand's ability to succeed and be popular... and all of this added equipment is toward that end." Tallison nodded, smiling back. Perhaps these people were not as bad as he feared after all... perhaps it was just a growing experience... the factors of getting to know new partners under rough circumstances. Suddenly, there was a roar of excited screams, most of them sounding like young girls, from behind him. Tallison turned, and saw a young blonde woman beaming and waving to what was undoubtedly a crowd of fans. "Who is that?" Tallison asked, pointing. "That," said Carl Michelle, smiling wider, "is one of our most successful brands. Ms. Hillary Duff, who will be performing tonight... and we expect that it should gather a very strong audience." Tallison looked over his shoulder at the young pop singer, and then back at Michelle. "Wait a minute... people pay to see this because it's a wrestling event. One of the biggest of the year, actually... it sells on it's own." "Hmmm," said Michelle, in a noncommittal fashion and shrugged. "If you say so, but we want to make sure the event is successful... and that's why we have arranged this concert, which will be the highlight and prime spot of the show. Now... if you will excuse us?" Tallison watched them go, shoulders slumped and mind reeling. Nothing made sense anyone... nothing at all. The End of the Beginning "Where were you?"
