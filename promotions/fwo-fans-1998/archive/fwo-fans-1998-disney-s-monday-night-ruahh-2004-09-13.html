Introduction In two weeks the fWo returns to their classic Wrestler of the Year series... and this year the stakes are higher than ever. Twenty four of the world's greatest wrestlers will compete, with members of the European based CWL in attendence to watch the challenge. But as the tournament is ready to begin, the Walt Disney Corporation has begun to focus on other matters... and other annoyances from within their own promotion... Mission Impossible: The Assignment As RUAHH~! came into focus the words "Recorded Two Hours Earlier," on the screen...an unlikely person sat in the S5S locker room waiting, contemplating, strategizing something most likely, supernova. That man, 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs grabbed his gym bag and fumbled through it looking for tape to apply to his wrists. He grabbed the black tape and started to roll it around his wrist; the sound of tape stretching quite possibly the only thing heard in the arena. As Vince continued with the tape, the door swung open to the locker room and in walked Rana Venenosa and 'Cocky' Craig Miles. Both smoking cigarettes, as well as adorned with sunglasses and leather S5S jackets. Miles' eyebrow arched as he strutted in, since usually he and Rana were the first two men to show up in the locker room before RUAHH~!. Miles took a long drag off the Newport, both men taking a seat and inspecting their comrade, while wondering why Vince made it a point to show up so early. "What's going on Vince?" Miles said with an easy flair, a far cry from nearly 5 months ago when both men attempted over several months to injure each other; for very different reasons over the same man the former Internet Champion, Deacon. "Just thought I'd get an early start tonight. Still a little ticked at what happened last week." Vince replied, a loud sound emanating from another wrap. "Don't worry about Flair, Alias, or Troy." Miles said with a puff on his cigarette, "In the end, those three are more likely to see the truths we fight for are actually truths they abandoned for personal pride." Vince just nodded. Another wrap. "Listen, while you're here Vince...I think you should hear something that's been simmerin' in my head for some time. Its Soldier related." Miles spoke as a waft of smoke crept out from his lips. "Shouldn't we wait for the other members before we discuss anything." Rana said while attempting a smoke ring, which looked more like a pretty cloud. "No, its alright. I like to save these discussions for the more cerebral minds in the group. It's very important, but not something too hard to dumb down for the loons. Things have been quiet, lately. Dudley hasn't called me in since I burned his contract offer on air. Basically, I suspect that Disney and Dudley have something up their sleeve as it pertains to our group. It'd be a mistake to wait for it." Miles continued with a smoke ring exhalation. Vince looked up, his cold eyes now focused on Miles finding the same page. Rana just nodded, "And to think they said they liked me." Another cloud escaped his mouth, Rana frustrated at its non-circular pattern. "They'll like you as long as they think they own you, kid. To tell the truth, I'm not sure what it is...call it a hunch, actually. What we need to do is get prepared for anything and everything. I don't think they've got what it takes to put a real dent in the long-term plans, but I'd like to stay a step ahead for right now, consider them as smart as ourselves..." Miles stated as he blew a smoke ring towards Rana, just to annoy him a little. It worked, oh did it work. Jacobs stood to his feet. "We need to get proof of what Dudley and Disney have planned for us. I have an idea. Let's place spies over the arena so we can find out what the hell is going on." Vince said "Great idea...But who could we get to spy on Disney?" Rana asked, while crossing his legs and flicking his ashes off to the side. "We got some Soldiers trained in...surveillance work Academy style." Miles said with a wink as he dropped the ashes from his Newport to the floor. "I know I can get some idiot in the back to spy on Disney for us. Just takes some persuasion from The Superstar." Jacobs said with a smirk. A MILESwide grin formed on the man known as War, he stood up himself, placing his hand on Vince's shoulder. "Take care of it my man." Vince nodded, toothing an evil grin and grabbed his wallet from his bag holding it up with a wink. As he headed out of the door, Scotty and Big Bobby Bowman walked into the locker room. "Where ya headed to Vince?" Scotty said "I got to take care of something for Craig. I'll be back." Vince replied while not missing a step. Bobby and Scotty walked into the locker room and saw Miles and Rana standing in the middle of the room. Craig dropped his cigarette on the ground stamping it out with his barefoot as he looked at Scotty. "Hey Scotty, bring the MIA truck around. We've got people to watch." Miles said as the camera faded out. WELCOME TO RUAHH~! No mouse ear logo. No fWo tagline. No pyro. No fireworks. This is Monday Night Ruahh~! and filling your screen is none other than the man that's flipped this world upside down, all around from town to town. He's brought his soldiers, he's brought his war... And if we know anything by now, Craig Miles wasn't going to beat around the bush. "It seems some of you have a problem with us..." A waft of smoke crept up Miles' grizzled face, his large rimmed silver sunglasses covered his snaky eyes, but the crooked smirk holding the Newport suggested an aura of being one step ahead of you at all times. "We can come out here and pussyfoot around, making claims or making judgments..." The camera faded back from Miles finally, along with him stood the members of HEAT. That's Nick Brandish, Rana Venenosa and Lance Knight to those that skim everything. "You don't like us?" Inhale...exhale... "Do something about it." The Arco Arena started booing, making their live presence felt. The show had started off on an awkward foot, the leather jacketed HEAT standing under a single spotlight in a darkened atmosphere. "Brand Frontier..." The crowd roared as Miles nodded his head, after being in the business ten years you know what to expect... "...last week you challenged me. The fact is, you need to learn there's a bigger picture in all of this. You may not want to be a leader, you may despise the line I'm about to cast...but the fact is either you can walk out into the fire tonight...or you can let some other four get their chance to take on THIS four tonight. You want to see heads roll? Then...let's fuckin' bowl, bitches." Miles dropped the microphone with a loud, echoing thud. No Music. No Fanfare. There was no need to set up this war, anymore... ...it was here... ...now was the time to fight...and the members of HEAT four Soldiers of the Fifth Seal awaited who'd be brave enough to stop them tonight. You know what happens next. The bad guy talks about bravery, Hell, he even called out the guy's name, what'd you expect? Out stomps the mighty hero, the paragon of the fWo, the antithesis of the entirety of Death's Riders: Brand Frontier. Microphone of his own in hand Frontier strides to the edge of the ramp way amidst the cheers and adulation of the packed house. His thunderous voice echoes out over the crowd, not stopping for the ovation to die away before continuing, "The only four bitches I see are a madman, a monster, a pretender and never-was that's standing in that ring." The fans erupted of course, Brand too knew the response the paying public would spew forth, it was Pavlovian. Miles took a drag off his cigarette, nodding over the raucous cheers followed by "BRAND! BRAND!" chants. He picked up the microphone and smiled, "We've got one, who else wants to party?" "If you think this is going to be a party son, you'r sorely mistaken." Brand smirked. "As for who else wants a piece of your little circle-jerk squad... how about these guys?" Frontier motions behind him with a nod of his head as the silver and black curtain billows out. Again, the fans that dared to sit shoot back to their feet and join the rest of the arena in the uproarious ovation as out from the back come: Chelsea Vice. Lindsay Troy. And, Alias! The three burgeoning heroes take their spot next to the Survivor II Champion, shoulder to shoulder, building a wall of defiance against the Soldier's of the Fifth Seal. Their chins raised high, Alias stood closest to Brand on his left side, arms crossed. Chelsea and Troy cut an impressive pose on the right of Frontier. "How does this work for four that are brave enough to stand 'gainst you tonight?!?" Miles looked things over, nodding. He flicked his cigarette out of the ring and leaned over the ropes, inspecting his opponents with a light hum...stroking his chin and insinuating that he was very impressed with the outcome. He turned around to the rest of the members of HEAT, "Alright, I apparently need a monster, who's that gonna be?" Nobody raised their hand, since nobody wanted it eaten by Nick Brandish. "I guess I'll take the pretender since I had to pretend to Deacon a lot...and well, Lance never was stupid enough to marry a chick that's banged something a grimy, disgusting and odorously putrid as Kellen Kinkade...and well, guess Rana gets to play the madman..." "Whoo!" and a pump of the fist came from the Poisonous Frog. "But the thing is...we're up a very diverse group tonight. A prick, a chick with a dick, a cum-drainage trick...and someone obsessed with 12 inch penises." Nick Brandish clapped, "They're gonna love me!" Miles nodded and turned around, "I don't think I forgot anything, Brand...but how about we all make our way out in say, ohhhhh, 90 minutes and find out?" Miles lowered the mic, his body language asking in a Seinfeldian tone "What is the deal with this?" "I respect your attempts at humor Miles, it's also ironic in how your stall tactics only serve to increase Disney's ratings by placing all of us in the main event, but speaking on the behalf of these guys... you're on... you self important prick." The words were harsher than normal for the hero, but the point was clear, he wasn't exactly playing the stereotype he's always accused of playing. Nevertheless, 'Thor' cued up as Miles bowed in honor of his guests leaving, Ruahh~! fading somewhere else. Follow The Sign RUAHH~! headed to the locker room of current fWo World Heavyweight Champion Black Quicksilver. He was joined at this time by Aimz, and they were both getting ready for their tag match against Fatal Attraction later in the night. "So...why do you think we're teaming tonight? "Probably because Dudley wants to see me get my ass kicked but doesn't want me to defend the title.", BQ answered, wrapping tape around his wrist and hand. "I know that doesn't sound like it makes sense, but trust me. I know Dudley. He knows defending the title is something I take pride in, no matter who it's against. Not in the typically male light, either...like, really damn weird.'' she groaned, stretching her arms and neck in preparation, ''Any idea what's up with that? But if he tries to shake your hand...don't do it." "I get you there", Aimz laughed. "But hey, you showed him who not to fuck with, right? I mean, h--whatthehell?!" Aimz was cut off by the sudden appearance of someone. Someone who definitely hadn't been invited into this locker room. BQ literally found himself starting Death in the face. Neither man said or did anything at first, until... *SMACK* BQ's head went to the side. He quickly looked back up at Death and held out his right arm, stopping Aimz from moving another step. "Now you know what to do.", Death told BQ. And with that, Death turned and walked away. Aimz had no idea what just happened, but BQ did. And yes, he knew *exactly* what to do.
