Introduction The fWo travels overseas to begin a multi-week Asia and Canadian tour... but little does the roster or staff know that the final days of the year may prove to be the final days of their lives... Debris "Vic Creed." Venom dripped from the lip of Brand Frontier. The Fan's Wrestling Organization had traveled to the grand Tokyo Dome for Uprising and the jam-packed arena buzzed with excitement as the fWo made a rare appearance overseas. The man that stood in the ring, street clothes and jacket still in place, had brought Uprising to the air sans the normal pyrotechnics and carnival like atmosphere it had done so with for a year now. The crowd waited for the Paragon to continue, confused at what they had just witnessed. For, you see, before the show came to the airwaves the 'mighty hero' had stormed into the ring. The Hammer of God struck down Halo. Righteous Thunder rained down on Dog, and Frontier delivered his Divine Right onto the hapless Rat. A local worker managed to skitter from the ring just in time, missing the wrath of this creature of vengeance. "Vic Creed... tonight... if I have to sacrifice myself to do it, you will pay for your crimes. I... swear... to... God." The feed was cut, whether it was by design or timing from the network doesn't matter. Brand Frontier had laid down a promise to all that was Holy. Honoring A Fallen Colleague Uprising returned from a commercial break, and it the show was already underway with a confusing yet hot start. The commentators introduced the show to the fans viewing at home back inside the United States before Chris Finn entered the the squared circle. The fans gave him a nice welcome, even though they were still in shock from what occured just moments earlier. Fans continued to settle into their seats, and the fWo prepared to officially kick things off from the Land of The Rising Sun. Finn had a microphone in hand already; and as things quieted down, he began to speak. "Welcome to Uprising... this is something I'd rather not be doing, but unfortunately... a year ago tonight, Lance Starr passed away. Lance Starr was a former fWo Cruiserweight Champion and a former fWo World Tag Team Champion on several occasions here in the fWo, but before he came to the fWo he wrestled extensively here in Japan. It is tonight, on the year anniversary of his passing, that we honor him with a ten bell salute. If I can ask you all to bow your heads in a moment of silence, we will begin the ten bell salute." The fans inside the Tokyo Dome bowed their heads, and the timekeeper began to ring the bell as Finn exited the ring. An image of Lance Starr appeared on the screen; and the bell began to toll. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. Seven. Eight. Nine. And then one man decided to interrupt everything. Austin Davis. Red and blue lights pulsed throughout the arena, and the fans arose to their feet, wondering what was going on. Davis appeared in the ring, and his theme music rang out throughout the Tokyo Dome as fans looked on in stunned silence. Davis simply smiled, but the smile spoke volumes. Interrupting a ten bell salute honoring a deceased wrestler was sacrilege, and Davis had just committed an unholy sin... and did so without remorse. The fans began booing, as realization had sat in. Davis grabbed a microphone, and and his music faded, he began to speak. "Death is funny. One minute you're living, breathing, eating, sleeping... and wrestling, just like Lance Starr. The next... it's all taken away from you. Cody... do you want that to happen to you? Because the next time you and I are in this ring... you're going to be just like Lance." Davis paused before stretching out his arms in a crucifix pose. "Dead." Without warning, Crusader hit the ring a house of fire, and everyone in attendance reacted with a collective cheer that set the roof of the dome spiraling towards the Pacific Ocean. Crusader and Davis exchanged punches before Crusader got the upperhand, and took Davis down with a DDT. Slightly bandaged and still bruised from the attack by Davis on Monday night, Crusader fought through the pain - literally - before going to the top rope... but the crafty Davis staggered to his feet and caught Crusader up on the turnbuckles. Davis and Crusader continued to exchange punches, before Davis caught him with a thumb to the eyes. The two bitter enemies remained embroiled in brutal conflict, as Davis superplexed his rival off the top rope into the ring. Fans reacted with boos, but those quickly turned to cheers. Davis stood out in a crucifix pose once again, but Crusader got to his feet, charged and speared the black trenchcoat off of Davis' body - literally. Crusader now had the upperhand, and as he mounted Davis and pinned his shoulders to the mat with his knees, he unleashed a flurry of punches. Davis couldn't defend himself - with his hands at least. On impulse, Davis defended himself the only way he could - and unleashed black mist, catching Crusader offguard as the mist temporarily blinded the fan favorite. Davis climbed to his feet, and caught Crusader with a quick DDT, laying out the man who, at this specific moment, was Davis' flavor of the month. Davis made it a point to ruin the lives of others and Crusader was his current target. Davis climbed down on his knees and told Crusader that it would soon be over... ... before Chris Universal appeared on the stage and stormed down to ringside. The reaction that Crusader received was loud, but on this night, the recation that Universal received was louder. He was Lance Starr' best friend, and many would never forget that. Universal and Starr teamed up on a show in Japan back in the late 1990's sometime... and fans remembered the duo as two great wrestlers who were friends in and out of the ring. Universal was also set to be Crusader' tag team partner in an upcoming match against Davis and Chris Titan. Beyond that, the history between Universal and Davis is well documented. Universal hit the ring and he and Davis exchanged pleasantries. Those small barbs at one another became a conversation of insults and putdowns. The cameras captured the moment, as Universal and Davis verbally ripped each other a new asshole. Crusader came to, and as he climbed to his feet, Universal and Davis appeared ready to lock horns. This would be their first physical encounter in quite some time, and the fans were ever-so-ready to witness the re-excitement of a feud that was one of the cornerstones of the history of the promotion. Crusader staggered to his feet finally, and then... ... Chris Universal made a deal with the devil. Universal blindsided Crusader, kicking him in the stomach and in one fell swoop laid out Crusader with his patented running neckbreaker, more infamously known as the "CHA CHING~!" The fans stared on in disbelief, reacting with boos immediately as Universal had done the unthinkable and aligned himself with a man many considered to be Universal' mortal enemy. Universal climbed to his feet and he and Davis stared at one another, and shared a smile. More boos came from the crowd, and it was virtually guaranteed that it would be the loudest ovation of jeers on this night. Davis handed the microphone over to Universal, and while basking in the boos, he began to speak. "Cody... Cody... Cody. What were you thinking? A year and a half ago... I wanted to bring you back to the fWo to inject some new life, some new blood into the promotion. And it was set to happen, but for whatever reasons, you backed out. And no, I'm not getting revenge over something so trivial, but if you want motivation, there's part of it. Want more motivation? Tonight, a year ago, my best friend died. Scratch that. He WAS my best friend. Because as someone once taught me, you keep your friends close, but your enemies closer. And I think you can see that's my new motto. You see Cody, you're a nobody, just like Starr was. So much potential... but you won't ever live up to it because you're pretty much dead. Just like Austin said a few minutes ago, you're DEAD. And it's funny... because now you and Lance are forever linked." The fans booed but Universal spoke over the boos. "See, when you came to the fWo, you were hyped beyond belief as the new big man on campus. And you got off to a hot start, no question about that. But it was Lance, LANCE, LANCE STARR OF ALL FUCKING PEOPLE WHO HANDED YOU YOUR FIRST LOSS! That is pathetic, Cody. Pathetic." The boos grew louder. Universal was no doubt cutting an interview that was inspired from somewhere, something... or someone. And that someone stood next to him with a grin from ear to ear. "Just like Lance. You're dead, Cody... dead. You may as well give up now... because I know more than anyone when Davis has his mind set on something, it happens. I'm one of the few who were fortunate to keep coming back... but I had to buy a company and fucking fire his ass to get him out of my hair for a year. You? You have no such options... because you're a never was. Some people are wannabe's, some people are has been's, you're a never was, and you never will be... just like Lance. My so called best friend..." Universal spat at the mat as security began to hit the ring. "Oh yeah, here comes the calvary." Davis drove a knee into the back of Crusader' head; as Crusader was attempting to get to his feet. "What are you going to do, huh? Arrest me for speaking the truth?" Security grabbed the microphone as Universal laughed maniacally, a side that many had never seen from him before. Security and officials ushered the two from the ring, as the fWo medical staff hit the ring to tend to Crusader. Uprising abruptly went elsewhere, as the show had unquestionably started out with a proverbial bang. Xias <3 Japan The Tokyo Dome. Japan. It was quite the place to hold a show, indeed- a huge arena in the middle of a city which probably has quite its share of differing opinions. Some people just find it weird when the subway talks to them or an oversized stuffed animal leaps out from a small building to plug a new videogame. But others "get it", and they love it. Xias Xias smiled at his reaction as he made his way onto the stage. Rather than power-walking, he just stopped there as if to take it all in, smiled again, then continued down the rampway, walking slowly. He stopped in the middle of the rampway to look down at his red symbol flashing there, then put his head down to look at it. After a moment, he threw his arms up into the air, an explosion of red pyro going off behind him and just smiled when the crowd cheered louder.. Xias continued down the rampway, still smirking all the way as he climbed up the steps and entered the ring through the ropes, calling for a microphone. "You guys rock." The parts of the crowd that could speak English cheered. "I mean, seriously. I'm gonna have to forget the whole 'turning back to the darkside' thing for at least a couple shows, because this is my favorite place in the world. How can I be all angry and angsty when I'm surrounded by everything that I love? Anime. Videogames. Japanese wrestling. And, oh yeah, that wonderful music of yours. I'm glad to see you guys love Megumi as much as I do. Not only is she the best musician ever, but also the best voice-actress. I mean, who the hell else could have pulled off Rei from Evangelion? Answer: NO ONE." Realizing that he was starting to ramble and that obviously not everyone in the audience were anime fans, Xias cleared his throat and then continued. "But on the subject of Japanese wrestling....really, there's a concept you guys have that I can't help but agree with. It's called fighting spirit. Tonight, I'm going to show all of you the fighting spirit I have burning inside me when I take on someone you fans should know well. He's a gaijin named....." "....Crusader." The crowd cheered, recognizing the name. After all, Crusader had spent some time in Japan the last time he left the fWo. "Now, I know a bit earlier you all saw Crusader get attacked by Austin Davis, and that's unfortunate....but I know that Crusader will still be able to enter that match tonight. You know why? Because like me, he's got fighting spirit. Something most of the fWo roster lacks. Like Aimz. You know her? Little Miss Slut Slut? No? Well, that's okay, she sucks anyway. In fact, all of her little Rat Pack group just really blows, know what I mean?" Xias realized he was starting to lose them again as he was met with silence....but, he knew just how to get them back into it. "Koko de, Minna no hou ga, hidoi amerikajin no fuan yori, shienshimashita. Watakushi wa, fWo o yamete, NJPW o hairimasu. Minna ga, suki desu ka? The crowd loudly cheered Xias' little Japanese speech, and he smiled, noting that they were the anti-French when it came to foreigners speaking their language- not to mention the growing feelings of anti-Americanism, which his little speech played off of. He gave the fans a little nod and a wink, having just said something that would have pissed the fWo front office off had they been able to understand it. "We'll see what the future holds." said Xias. "But in the meantime, I'm gonna wrap this up by saying that I'll see you all later tonight, and I promise I'll re-define just what fighting spirit means." With that, "Kimi Sae Ireba" started up again, leaving Xias to take his leave. He smiled as he slid out of the ring and walked around the ringside area, slapping hands with his newfound fans. A happy Xias is truly a thing to behold. Cheats Never Prosper... Stuck in the cramped confines of his locker room, which was more akin to a broom cupboard, he was still seething about the match, the loss, and the man who set it up. Silver Fox. Tonight, there isn't a rule I won't break to ensure we win." Even during his speech, Elliot's body language was awkward, as the minuscule room was almost giving him claustrophobia. "I bet he had a say in space allocation too," he whispered sharply under his breath. "We gonna beat 'em tonight," Thomas chipped in with his usual drawl, "Aren't we?" The deep brown eyes of Elliot answered the question well before his head began to nod and his lips parted. "Oh, we're not letting Savage and his post-op wife get anything over on us," he assured, "You just do what you do best, and leave the thinking to me... I have a feeling that I can get RIGHT under Jeremy's skin." His usual menacing look was amplified, as if every thought in his head was devoted to plotting rule breaks and infringements. HEAR THAT, SILVER FOX?!?!?" Thomas pushed his body back against the wall, shocked, as Elliot thrust up from his seat and bellowed out the sound travelling through the thin walls. "BE SURE TO WATCH, OLD MAN... AS IT'S ALLLLLLL GOING TO BE FOR YOU!" His voice echoed, his mouth smirked, his plan was set.
