Sunday Slaughter, 11 April 1999

11 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction The fWo hailed from Grand Rapids, Mich. for a live presentation of Sunday Slaugher. With only 11 days remaining until the biggest Pay-Per-View of the year, many of the fWo superstars were on hand to film promotional spots, and be interviewed by local radio stations about their upcoming matches. Promised for that night were the first fifteen participants in the battle royal, dubbed the "Unlucky 15". Chris Finn and James Armstrong were on hand for the show. Austin Davis interview fWo Internet Champion "Baad Ass" Austin Davis; still nursing some injuries sustained in the empty arena match against Cactus Jack from this past Friday, came to the ring with the Internet Title on his shoulder and a bag made out of black cloth. Davis tossed the bag into the ring with reckless abandon, and climbed into the ring. The crowd booed the heinous, bald-headed Davis, who didn't seem to be affected whatsoever by the loud and angry audience. Davis demanded a microphone from fWo personnel surrounding the ring, and was given one in quick fashion. Davis told the crowd and national television audience looking on that he had several important things to say, so they better listen up or he'd kick each and everyone of their asses accordingly before the night was over. Davis said that the match with Cactus Jack took a helluva lot out of him, and it would be stupid of anyone to think that he would be able to wrestle at full strength two days later in a match against Scott Slugger for the fWo World Title. Davis said it was a long hard road back to the mountaintop of the fWo, but he wasn't sure if all that trouble was worth it. Davis continued by saying that he didn't think the fWo Internet Title was worth it, and if Travis Beaven wanted it, he could come out and get it from him. The crowd looked on shocked, not knowing yet what Davis had planned. Davis said that before Beaven should come out, he should bring HIS fWo World Title out to him because that's the reason he was there and he wasn't going to leave the damn ring until he had HIS fWo World Title in his possession. He said he'll make a fair trade, the Internet Title for the World Title. Beaven started to make his way out; but had no World Title in hand. As Beaven came into the ring, Davis said "You're about to make the biggest mistake of your pathetic life if you take ONE step further". Beaven threatened Davis with legal action, so Davis finally allowed Beaven to come in. As Beaven did, Davis took his fWo Internet Title and nailed Beaven right in the side of the head with it. Beaven collapsed in a heap, with the Internet Title falling nearby him. As head of fWo Security John Willis made his way into the ring, Davis went after him and the two began to scuffle. Meanwhile, a swat-team with mace and nightsticks charged into the ring, with Chris Universal shortly behind. The swat team helped handcuff and shackle the Internet Champion; as Universal was caught on camera stealing the fWo Internet Title and the bag that Davis brought to the ring, before exiting out of the arena through the crowd. As this happened, the commissioner staggered to his feet with help from Willis, still groggy from the attack. It appeared that Davis did not see what happened, and as the swat team brought Davis to his feet, Beaven said that he was okay and he told Davis that he would let him go if he promised to not lay a hand on him (Beaven) in the next two weeks before CyberSlam, or he would be stripped of the fWo Internet Title, suspended from the fWo for one year, and his World Title match with Slugger tonight would be awarded to Chris Universal. Davis, thought it over, before shaking his head yes, and muttering "trust me" towards the commissioner. Realizing that Davis knew he would be stupid to go back on his word, Beaven ordered the swat team to take Davis back to his lockerroom, where he would then be released from security. As the swat team proceeded with Davis up the shiny entrance ramp, Scott Slugger appeared on the TravisTron and taunted Davis, an obvious attempt to provoke him to attack him outside of the ring. Davis shrugged it off and continued up the ramp without struggle... Black Panther (c) vs. Joy Joy entered to the ring escorted by two great big Barney dinosaurs, and of course the Speed Demon. Joy attempted to sing to the crowd, but the crowd wanted no part of it and started up a loud chorus of boos for the ever-loving hated superstar. Joy eventually gave up as somebody through a huge beach ball into the ring and knocked down one of the Barney dinosaurs. As Joy attended to one of the fallen Barney dinosaurs, the other dinosaur removed his costume and it became known that it was the Black Panther. Panther quickly disposed of the Speed Demon; before attacking Joy from behind. As the match progressed, Joy fought back but Panther maintained a hefty advantage over the over-happy Joy; hitting an arsenal of moves including a standing moonsault, a gut-wrench suplex, a tornado DDT, an flying fistdrop. Panther took one of the large heads of the Barney dinosaurs and planted it over Joy's head, before putting Joy on the top turnbuckle and hitting his patented superplex. Panther went for the cover, but Speed Demon jumped up onto the ring apron and distracted the referee. Panther lunged and knocked the Demon off the apron onto the floor, and ducked an attempted punch by Joy who still had the Barney dinosaur head intact. After ducking the attempted punch, Panther nailed Joy with a lowblow and finished him off with a face-slam for the victory. Panther quickly grabbed his International Title from the timekeeper, before exiting up the ramp. As he got to the top of the stage, Panther spoke into the camera saying that he would defeat Chris Universal again this Friday at the final Road to Cyberslam house show. Result: Black Panther by Pinfall Ultra Violet vs. Mitch Wilson As the sounds of "Rage" filled the arena Ultra Violet walked to the ring accompanied as always by Violet, but more unusual by Nightshade, who lurked behind. Ultra Violet happily dipped his hand into a bucket of purple paint, and "autographed" several fans on the head, chest, arms, and on signs. Violet trailed along behind looking carefree and whistfull. Nightshade frowned at the crowd and sat at the edge of the ring apron smoking a cigarette. Mitch Wilson came to the ring with Primetime and the Flying Frenchie, and all three quickly entered the ring and began stomping down Ultra Violet. The ref finally cleared the ring of the Frenchie and Primetime, who stalked the ringside area lending support to Wilson inside the ring. Wilson tossed Ultra Violet into the ropes and landed a quick clothesline. With Ultra Violet on the mat, Wilson locked the leg on the mat and applied a sitting armbar. Ultra Violet squirmed to get free, pushing on Wilson's face with one hand. It happened to be the purple painted hand, and Wilson swatted Ultra Violet's arm away in disgust. Wilson let Ultra Violet struggle to his feet, and then took him down with a european uppercut. Ultra Violet bounced up, and Wilson went to the ropes and rebounded with a flying tackle. Ultra Violet used the momentum to carry himself over into a cover, but only got a two count after the Frenchie reached in and pulled Ultra Violet off Wilson. Ultra Violet turned to give the Frenchie a scolding, and Wilson was able to land a French legwhip from behind which took Ultra Violet down. Wilson slapped a modified elbow to knee back submission hold on Ultra Violet and locked his hands together. Trying to give himself some momentum, Ultra Violet slapped the mat which the ref mistakenly took for a submission, and awarded the match to Mitch Wilson. After the bell was rung the Frenchie and Primetime entered the ring to beat down Ultra Violet, until Scotty Cool ran in to clear the ring. Scotty Cool helped his old partner to his feet, and the two walked to the backstage area. Result: Mitch Wilson by Submission Black Quicksilver & Stump Backstage, Black Quicksilver argued with Stump. Black Quicksilver seemed to be defending himself against an accusation by Stump that his record was far worse than the Stump's was. Black Quicksilver explained that he had wrestled far tougher atheletes than Stump, but Stump seemed unimpressed and continued to sit still on the bench. Black Quicksilver told Stump that it didn't matter anyway, because he was booked to be in a title match on Friday. Black Quicksilver listened for some time, and then laughed, telling Stump that there was no way he could get an Internet Title shot. Black Quicksilver continued to say "can not!" repeatedly until he huffed and looked away from Stump, sulking. Cactus Jon vs. Fallen A ladder was placed outside the ring, and hung high from the rafters an fWo flag was suspended. In order to win the match a wrestler would have to carry the ladder into the ring, set it up, climb and obtain the flag. Fallen came to the ring first, looking sullen and despondant. Fallen walked under the ladder, then entered the ring and slumped down in the corner. Cactus Jon came out to a surprising pop from the audence given his recent temperment inside the ring, and made his way slowly to ringside. Jon seemed surprised himself at the reaction he was getting, and when he reached the ladder he looked up at it. Jon grabbed the ladder and tossed it into the ring, where it landed on it's side with a loud thud. As Jon entered the ring Fallen sprang to life with a baseball slide into the ladder, causing it to crash into the face of Jon. Jon fell back outside holding his now bleeding head, and Fallen ran to the ropes, rebounded, and sailed over the other side in a flying plancha. Jon saw him coming however, and stepped aside while shoving Fallen down in a face slam into the mat. Jon then climbed into the ring and set up the ladder. As Jon began to climb Fallen reentered the ring and grabbed hold of his leg, trying to keep Jon from climbing. Jon kicked down at Fallen, finally succeeding in kicking him away. Undetered, Fallen rushed the ladder with a running shoulderblock, and tipped it over, sending Jon crashing neck-first into the ropes. Fallen surveyed the damage, and drug Jon into the center of the ring. Setting the ladder on the leg of Jon, Fallen began stomping on the ladder, causing additional damage to the leg and taking away Jon's ability to climb. Jon worked himself around until he had turned over, and shoved the ladder up into the face of Fallen, knocking him backward into the corner. Jon then picked up the ladder and charged with it, ramming Fallen several times in the chest area until he slumped down, seemingly unconcious. Cactus Jon set the ladder up once more, and began to climb. As he did, he noticed that Fallen had begun to cough blood uncontrollably, and appeared to be going into shock. Jon looked up at the fWo flag, and heard the crowd chanting for him to grab it, but instead climbed down and left the ring. Fallen continued to cough and shake, and Jon quickly returned with fWo medical personel. With Fallen attended to, Jon climbed the ladder and obtained the flag, winning the match. Fallen meanwhile was placed on a stretcher, and wheeled from the ringside area. Several fWo medical staff and strangely fWo star Fathom were on hand to help Fallen into an ambulance. Inside the ring, Cactus Jon looked on with some concern. Result: Cactus Jon Dark Riders Interview Deep inside the area the Dark Riders War, Plague, Scott Taylor, David Spencer, and the new, large Death stood facing a battered and bloody Stevie Roberts. Roberts looked to have been badly tortured, and Plague grinned and laughed manaicly as Roberts winced in pain and fear. Death closely at Stevie, and told War to release him. War looked confused at this order, and Death raised a single fist in his direction. War quickly complied, cutting Stevie's bonds with a knife, and letting Stevie drop like a heap to the dirty floor. War kicked Stevie, telling him to get moving. Stevie, unable to stand, crawled away as War looked back at Death in confusion. Death said nothing, but Scott Taylor said, "We traded the Frenchman for Roberts... now we let Roberts bring us a bigger prize... on Friday..." Smiling with understanding War watched as Roberts slowly crawled away... Scotty Cool vs. Primetime Scotty Cool came to the ring beaming at the crowd and slapping hands as he went. Scotty Cool jumped into the ring and climbed the turnbuckle, gesturing to his waist and signalling that he would obtain the fWo World Championship title once again. Primetime came to the ring alone, but the Flying Frenchie and Mitch Wilson could be spotted at the top of the entrance ramp, just behind the curtains. Primetime and Scotty Cool locked up, and Scotty Cool quickly overpowered the smaller man. Primetime was tossed into the corner, but deftly avoided Scotty Cool's charge and Scotty Cool crashed into the turnbuckle shoulder first. Primetime went back into the ropes and charged Scotty Cool, executing a perfect spinning French dropkick on the dazed Cool. Primetime then hooked the arm of Scotty Cool, and tossed Cool to the mat with a standing backdrop. Covering Cool, Primetime was only able to get a two count before Cool tossed him off. Primetime went to the ropes to attempt another dropkick, but Cool ducked it, and followed his defensive move with a running lariat. Scotty Cool from nowhere executed a Scotty Dropper, and covered Primetime. Before the count could be made the Flying Frenchie and Mitch Wilson ran to the ring and attacked Scotty Cool, earning him the DQ victory. All three members of the French Foundation beat down Scotty Cool and kicked the large man, until Ultra Violet ran to the ring to break things up. Ultra Violet and Scotty Cool stood facing the French Foundation in a standoff, until referees were able to seperate the two groups. Result: Scotty Cool by DQ CyberSlam's Unlucky 15 Chris Finn entered the ring with a sheet of paper, and announced that the first fifteen members of the CyberSlam annual battle royal were choosen, and were about to be announced. It was reminded that the winner of the Battle Royal would receive a automatic title shot at the fWo World Champion, whomever that may be, at Extreme Measures in May. Chris Finn then read from the list, announcing the first fifteen participants. They were: 1. Mad Trucker 2. Apocolyptica 3. War 4. Prophet 5. Tom the Bomb 6. Fathom 7. Joy 8. Darkness 9. Oracle 10. Sid Snow 11. Melissa Smalls 12. Waru 13. Shocker 14. Jackhammer 15. Ice The next fifteen are scheduled to be announced at this week's Wednesday Warfare. Scott Slugger (c) vs. Austin Davis The moment had finally arrived for perhaps the biggest match in Sunday Slaughter history; and the crowd sensed they were about to witness something special. "Trust me" echoed throughout the arena over the public address system, as Austin Davis - appearing without his fWo Internet Title - made his way out onto the ramp with a look of disdain for everyone in the arena, and quite possibly the entire planet. Davis snarled before walking down to the ring quickly, climbing inside and taking in the boos from the crowd. Davis took his vest off, threw it into the crowd, looked up at the technical crew up in the bleachers and made a motion for them to cut his music. He then demanded a microphone and said "Scott Slugger, you're three seconds away from losing MY fWo World Title"... Davis then paused for a moment and a giant smirk appeared over his face, before he spoke again. "If you're not down with that, well, I only have two words for you... " as he was about to finish, a group of fans yelled out "suck it!", which caught Davis offguard. Davis then said "No, you stupid pieces of trash, not that. Trust me!" before smirking once again. Davis then said "NOW, you can suck it!" and tossed the microphone outside the ring. He then motioned quickly for Slugger to come out, and finally Slugger' music blasted throughout the arena, as the fWo World Champion made his way out to a standing ovation from the onlooking crowd. Slugger brought with him his bat, and as paused for a moment to pose for the crowd while standing on the stage, Davis climbed outside the ring and charged full speed ahead at Slugger. Slugger anticipated an attack and prepared to take Davis' bald head off his body with a swing, but Davis ducked. Davis turned around and ducked another swing, before he dropped to one knee and nailed Slugger with a lowblow. Slugger dropped the bat and Davis grabbed it with one hand, grabbed Slugger with the other, and began a quick walk down the ramp towards the ring. Davis dropped the bat near ringside, rolled Slugger into the ring, and climbed in afterwards. As he rolled Slugger into the ring, the World Title came off of the fallen champion. Davis snatched the belt away, looked at it, and then took it with one hand, and grabbed Slugger by the back of the head in the other. Davis then took the title and put it in the face of Slugger and said "you can kiss the World Title goodbye, and after you're done doing that you can kiss my ass!" before slamming the World Title into the face of the champion. Davis then took the World Title, gave it to the referee, and ordered him to ring the damn bell. The referee handed the belt to the timekeeper, and shortly thereafter the bell was rung. Davis brought Slugger to his feet and whipped him to the ropes, and when Slugger returned he grabbed him by his hair and threw him over the top rope out onto the floor. Davis followed him outside the ring and grabbed one of the lengthy electrical cords from near ringside, and began choking Slugger. Davis then grabbed Slugger and slammed his face into the guardrail, which busted open the forehead of the World champion. The referee warned Davis not to do it again or he would be disqualified. Davis responded by telling the referee not to piss him off again or he would never breathe again. Davis then grabbed Slugger and rolled him back into the ring; and it appeared he was going to continue the attack, before a fan caught his attention with a huge banner depicting one ugly looking logo. Even with a closer look at it, you could not tell what the hell it was supposed to be. Nevertheless, Davis took the banner and rubbed his backside against it, spat on it, ripped it up, stomped on it and gave it back to the young fan, who began crying. Davis laughed and when he turned around he was greeted by a flying Slugger, who catapulted himself over the top rope onto Davis for a plancha. A roar from the crowd echoed all throughout the building, as a bloody Slugger smirked and went on the attack. Slugger whipped Davis back first into the guardrail, and as Davis staggered back out from it, Slugger hit him with a vicious drop toe hold into the ring steps, busting open the Internet Champion. Slugger continued the attack and rolled him into the ring, before climbing up onto the ring apron and back into the ring. Slugger brought Davis to his feet, and nailed him with a hard right hand. A groggy Davis mustered enough strength to return the favor, and the two traded punches before Slugger took a page out of Davis' booked and nailed him with a lowblow. Slugger followed it up with a pinfall attempt, but only received a count of two. Undaunted, Slugger grabbed the fallen Davis and began to wear him down with a headlock, which cut off the flow of blood to Davis' head. The referee noticed that the headlock was illegal, and was choking Davis in the process. The referee warned Slugger and gave him a five-count, and finally Slugger released the hold at the count of four. Slugger then bounced off the ropes and nailed Davis with a kneedrop to the forehead, again capitalizing on the part of the body that has been effected the most. Slugger then dropped another knee, before making a half-assed attempt at a pinfall, which Davis quickly kicked out of. Slugger then got cocky and told the referee it was a slow-count, and as he argued, Davis recovered and rolled up Slugger from behind with a handful of tights as well. Slugger barely kicked out at the last second, and now Davis argued with the referee. As he turned around, Slugger hit a belly-to-belly suplex on Davis and went for another cover. Davis kicked out, and Slugger brought him to his feet, before slapping on a side headlock. Davis quickly escaped and whipped Slugger to the ropes, Slugger reversed, and as he did he quickly caught Davis in a hammerlock. Davis appeared to not be able to escape the hold, but he reached up with his free arm/hand, and grabbed Slugger by the head area, and took Slugger out with a stunner. Davis appeared to have the match won, but too much had been taken out of him and he was unable to roll over to make a pinfall attempt. The referee began a ten-count, and at the count of eight both men made it to their feet. Davis and Slugger began exchanging blows again, Slugger gained the advantage, bounced himself off the ropes and came back for a flying elbow, but Davis pulled the referee in front of him, and the referee was met with a hard elbow to the side of the head. Taking his mind off Davis, Slugger checked on the referee. As he did this, Davis recovered and grabbed Slugger. He whipped him to the ropes, and Slugger ducked his clothesline. Davis turned around for a backdrop as Slugger bounced off the ropes, and Slugger caught him with a modified version of the "Baad Ass Bottom Line" DDT. The crowd erupted and Slugger went for the pin, but down came Cactus Jon. Jon made his way into the ring, he and Slugger began to trade blows. The two fought back and forth; and this gave Davis an opportunity to heal. Scotty Cool began to make his way down to the ring, and he joined the fray by helping out Slugger. He spinned Ice around and dished out some punches, and he and Slugger traded punches back and forth with Ice. Davis saw this and as Cool turned around, he caught him with a DDD. Jon and Slugger continued to trade blows as Scott Taylor and War began to make their way down to the ring. War, Taylor & Davis began kicking and stomping the fallen Scotty, as Jon& Slugger fought outside the ring. Slugger gained the advantage and threw Jon into the ringpost, before coming back into the ring and having it out with the massive War. Slugger got rid of War out onto the floor, and took Taylor out with one punch as well. Davis turned and looked up at the entrance ramp, where Chris Universal was seen walking out with the Internet Title wrapped around his waist. As Universal walked out, a mysterious purple masked man made his way out from behind and cracked a steel chair over Universal's back. He then bent over and began dishing out some punches to the fallen Universal. Davis appeared to be shocked by what was going on, and as he looked on Slugger took the opportunity to roll Davis up from behind. The referee was still out, but Scotty Cool began to count. One, two, three... an angry Slugger then told Cool to wake up the referee, but he would not do such a thing. Cool simply shrugged and began to walk off, and an angrier Slugger walked over to Cool and pushed him from behind. Cool turned around and nailed Slugger with a punch, that staggered him. Davis already back to his feet turned Slugger around, and nailed him with a Davis Death Drop. Davis then woke up the referee as Cool walked up the entrance ramp, while Universal and the purple masked man fought to the dressing room. Cool turned around and saw Davis waking up the referee, before Davis grabbed Slugger and pointed at Cool, shouting "Here's a late birthday present, Scotty", placing Slugger on top of himself, and demanding the referee to count. The referee counted slowly, but finally reached three as Cool looked on shocked. War & Taylor came back into the ring and pulled Slugger off Davis, before the three of them began stomping Slugger once again. Finally, Cool charged back down to ringside and cleared the ring. Davis grabbed the World Title from the timekeeper and had War wrap it around Davis' waist. Jon rolled back into the ring disgusted by what had happened, as Cool checked on the fallen Slugger. Slaughter ended with confusion as Davis, War & Taylor began to exit the ringside area... Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall
