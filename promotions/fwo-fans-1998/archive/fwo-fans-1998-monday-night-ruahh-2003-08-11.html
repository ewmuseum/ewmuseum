Introduction The fWo enters paradise... Maui, Hawaii. But it is not all sun and sand, but biter rivalries that have been pushed past the boiling point. For tonight the fWo is bringing out nearly all of the championship belts in a winner take all event that could shape the promotion for months or years to come... Hail to the Chief Silver Fox looked at his watch for the fifth time in so many minutes. The show was about to start and he hadn't seen the man he was waiting for yet. As usual, he was taking his sweet time in arriving. "He'll probably show up late because he knows I want to see him," Fox sighed. Fox caught the movement out of the corner of his eye. He turned in time to see two men walking towards him at a brisk pace. From the pressed suit, shined shoes, "high and tight" haircuts, and sunglasses equipped with what appeared to be ear mic's, Fox thought he was being approached by the Secret Service. What was Dubya doing in Hawaii? "Excuse me, but are you supposed to be back here?" Fox was a little taken aback by the agent's question. "Uh, yes I am. I'm with the fWo." The second man stood behind the first, scanning the hallway for movement. As he turned, Fox caught a glimpse of what appeared to be the butt of a gun hanging from a chest holster. The first man studied Fox carefully, waiting several seconds before speaking again. "Could I see some identification please?" "Of course." Fox reached for his wallet in his back pocket. Both men straightened up. "Slowly," said the first while the second's hand moved into his jacket. Fox pulled out his wallet and handed his identification to the agent. He looked it over, examining it, looking for any signs of forgery before handing it back to Fox. The first spoke into his cufflink. "All clear." The two agents motioned for Silver Fox to step back. Fox did as they instructed, his curiosity piqued. Two more agents walked through the back door and motioned someone forward. Silver Fox was almost disappointed when Kodiak Vic Creed walked into the building with his assistant. "Vic!" Fox called out. The four agents quickly surrounded Creed. "What is all of this?" "I've been getting death threats." Creed pushed the men aside and walked up to Fox. The agents followed closely behind the Main Man. "Nothing but love for the most important man in Professional Wrestling." "From who?" "I don't know and I don't care. The monkey suit brigade wasn't my idea." Vic shot his assistant a dirty look. "I was going to send them packing, but then I realized that there were too many nutjobs right here in the fWo. So just like when the Main Man bangs a skank in the back of his limo, a little protection can't hurt." Creed took the World Title off his shoulder. "So who did you have in mind for me tonight? Which lamb are you sending to the Juggernaut for slaughter?" Fox shook his head. "I hate to break it to you Vic, but you're not wrestling tonight. Not without medical clearance." Vic draped the World Title over his shoulder and pulled his sunglasses off. "Say again?" Creed stared into Silver Fox's eyes, and Fox could see how very not-well the Champ was taking the news. "I want you to go to the doctor." Fox handed Creed a small business card. "This doctor. If he says you're cleared to wrestle, then you can be back in the ring by Uprising." "I'm not seeing any damn doctor." "Yes you are. Check your contract. If you don't have medical clearance, you're not wrestling. If you're not wrestling, you're not defending your belt." Fox leveled a Creed-ish stare back at the Main Man. "And you know what happens if you can't defend the title." Creed was fuming. "Try taking the belt from me. I'm daring you. I'm begging you." Fox decided it wasn't worth the potential grief to continue talking to KVC, so he turned to Creed's assistant. "Show him his contract. The contract he signed. Get him checked and he can wrestle." Taking his leave from the circus that was the fWo World Champion, Silver Fox disappeared down the hallway. He hoped that his headaches for the evening were ending, but somehow he just wasn't convinced. Pardon the Interruption... The fans sat in their - Maui Memorial Stadium - seats anxiously awaiting the beginning of the show. Random chants could be heard off and on, echoing over everyone else's conversation in certain parts of the arena. Besides the group of drunken college students in the seats nearest the aisle to the ring, it was safe to say this had the potential to be a fairly harmless event, which had to make the security guards on duty feel good. Suddenly, the fan- tron lit up. It was sudden, out of the blue, no warning whatsoever. Most of the fans weren't prepared for who they'd see, and instinctively booed his appearance. They didn't give him a chance to speak, because ... well ... a lot of them didn't want to hear what he had to say. His problems didn't stem from wrestling, or a storyline ... he had betrayed them ... betrayed humanity, the kids, and they'd let him know it. It was the fWo's Internet Champion, Triple X - neck up. ...and, he'd sit there, silently ... until the arena calmed down, until the scattered booing would stop ... until they'd atleast hear him out. To his surprise, the booing slowly started turning into cheers. The section of fans who had booed him so passionately only seconds earlier was being drowned out by actual fans of the troubled superstar. Now was as good a time as any. "We as performers often like to think that we're untouchable," he began. "...always on top of our game. That nobody could break through our defenses. ...that's a lie. I'm human, every performer in the back is human, we make mistakes just like the next man, only we get criticized a little harder because of our position in life. ...because we have a little more money, a little more access to material things." He scratched his head. "Well ... I can tell you from experience that I'm not perfect. ...not close. I get frustrated when I'm not able to do something, and sometimes I let it carry over to my personal life. Let's see ... I can sometimes be shy. Irresponsible when I want to be. I've been in plenty of fights, some I started, some I didn't. I've been so drunk at times ... that I could barely stand." The fans cheered. "Why am I telling you this? It's easy ... I'm telling you everything that I am, to lead to telling you everything that I'm not. I'm not mean spirited, I'm not stuck up, and I don't think I'm better than any of the performers on the fWo roster in terms of social class. I would never purposely hurt anybody ... I'd never pick on someone defenseless. I don't prey on the weak, and I don't ... hit ... women." He paused, thinking his next sentence over. Letting the fans ... his fans digest everything he just gave them. "I am a performer." "I enjoy performing for you fans, and it is because of you that I'm able to do some of the things that I do. I don't take my job for granted, I don't think I'm too good to get out there and bust my ass so that my opponent will look as good as he possibly can. I'm not racist, I'm not sexist, and I'm not a rapist. "I'm a performer. ...an athlete. So is the rest of the people behind that curtain waiting to come out here and perform for you. We love this business, we love you fans, and it is our hope that in return, you love us right back." "Thank you for your time ... thank you for your patience, and enjoy tonight's show." He turned away, almost as if he was done, before looking directly into the camera one last time. "Oh, and if it needs to be said, if you need to hear it from me, personally. I'm innocent..." He swallowed hard, fighting back the tears. "...I'm innocent." The fan-tron faded to black. The rest of the show was next, but the fans - Sean "Triple X" Stevens' fans - showed their appreciation and loyalty, by cheering.
