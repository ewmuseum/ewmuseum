Introduction Following Unleashed: Road Rage, the fWo travels to an unlikely venue: Shiprock, New Mexico. It is an area steeped in tradition, rich in culture and deep within the Navajo reservation. Fans here are rabid for wrestling action, usually shunned by the national promotions. But it is not the venue that is the complete story... in three days the fWo will release the tournament brackets for the Wrestler of the Year tournament, to conclude at fWo Legends in October.
