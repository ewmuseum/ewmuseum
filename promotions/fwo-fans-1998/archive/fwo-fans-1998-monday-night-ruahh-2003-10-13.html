Monday Night Ruahh~!, 13 October 2003

13 October 2003

Introduction Tonight the Wrestler of the Year tournament continues... Divine Retribution It's Monday Night. Time for Ruahh~! Brought to you by the good folks in the fWo. Let's get the party started on the right foot, shall we? What better way than to start with the man standing at the top of the Mountain, smiling as he pissed on everything down below. Animals do that to mark their territory. He did it just because he could. Bombs exploded on the Fantron, the roaring of the wanton destruction replaced by the opening chords of "Walk" by Pantera. THEJuggernaut UNcaring UNbeatable UNstoppable Kodiak Vic Creed stepped through the curtains looking awfully smug. He looked like a very large child who'd just gotten away with something he shouldn't have. With a child, it might be that he'd stolen five dollars from his mother's purse or swiped the last cookie from the cookie jar. With Creed, things were on a much larger scale. He might have just had sex with his assistant's girlfriend. He could have just sliced someone's brake line out in the parking lot. Or maybe he was smirking about something he was planning on doing, but hadn't done yet. There were no lines that couldn't be crossed when you dealt with someone who didn't have a conscience. The fans always gave Creed a negative reaction. Many of them only knew about his misdeeds in the ring. Some of the smarks heard rumors about his actions outside the ring. Creed was an equal-opportunist that way: he was a dangerous, callous asshole in any medium. Cross him, and he'd make your life Hell. Kodiak Vic Creed - Destroyer of Worlds. In fact, that's close to what his T-shirt displayed as he stepped through the ropes. He flexed for the camera as he slung the fWo World Title over his shoulder, stretching the fabric of his "It's My World" T-shirt over his mammoth chest. Vic waited for the jeering to subside before starting to speak. His first few words could only be heard by the people in the front row. After trying again, Creed angrily tapped the mic that the Timekeeper hand handed him. The fans started laughing as Creed stormed towards the ropes and snatched a working mic from a ringside assistant. "That's funny huh?" Creed snapped, before throwing the defective mic at the back of the ringside assistant's head. It smacked against the back of her skull and bounced a good foot and a half into the air. The young woman stumbled forward and collapsed beside the Timekeeper's table. The man stood up and gave Creed a shocked look as he checked on the young woman. "Make sure the mic works next time, Assmonkey," Creed barked as he stepped back toward the center of the ring. "But that shouldn't surprise me," Creed said as he finally addressed the crowd. "There's not much that the fWo can actually get right. Just look at my T-shirt. The fWo is still selling shirts off their website that nobody even wants to see anymore. You know, merchandise for people who either couldn't draw heat if they were on fire, or for people who couldn't handle the heat and ran back to the kitchen. But the most dominant World Champion? No, he doesn't even get a mention. Talk about throwing money away. They're wasting marketing dollars." "It's also pretty obvious that they're trying to break me as well. Look at the people they've been throwing at me lately - Superstar Vince Jacobs, the U.S. Champ Lance Knight, the Hardcore Champ Jack Lundin. Guys that, in any other promotion, would pick up a Heavyweight Title quicker than a sailor picks up crotch critters on shore leave." Kodiak Vic Creed lifted the belt off his shoulder and held it up with one arm. "But I've faced the best, and I've still got the only World Title that means anything in the business. The fWo want the belt off me because I have a tendency to make life difficult for people. If they can't take a real challenge, a real fight, then they should either go after another title or another line of work." Meanwhile, the fans were having trouble nailing down a decent chant. Many of them were busy keeping an "Asshole" chant going strong, while some toyed with the idea of chanting for the "Paragon". Creed decided to make the choice for them. "Since you sheep need someone to think for you, you'd be better off keeping the Asshole chant going. Chanting for Frontier is like pissing in the wind it might give you something to do, but you just end up looking stupid in the end." Decision go with Frontier. Vic lost the smug look. "When will you people get it through your collective heads? I'm done with him. I've beaten everyone worth facing. I've dominated like no one in the history of the fWo. There isn't anyone left to face." The fans wouldn't stop. "Enough with Frontier!" But the fans hadn't had enough. Frontier clearly hadn't had enough; his desire reflected in his actions from the past few weeks. He'd be robbed time and time again of both the World Title and, more importantly, revenge. Few people knew the toll their encounters had taken on Creed's body; the wonders of modern medicine had seen to that. It didn't take a genius to see, judging from the more creative ways Creed had escaped his encounters with Brand Frontier, that the Main Man wasn't anxious to confront the Paragon anytime soon. No man, no matter how dominant he appeared, was unbeatable. With each passing day, it looked like Brand Frontier had Kodiak Vic Creed's number. So engrossed in tormenting the World Champion, the fans didn't see the lone man running through the crowd. They barely noticed him hopping over the barricade. But when Brand Frontier slid into the ring, everyone focused on him like a laser beam. Especially Kodiak Vic Creed. Both men were in street clothes. Frontier charged, as did Creed. The two titans collided in the center of the ring, shoulder-to-shoulder, chest-to-chest. Frontier, being the smaller of the two yet not a small man by any means, staggered backward but stayed on his feet. Creed quickly followed up with a charging lariat that spun Brand in the air. Vic was clearly irate as he slid out of the ring. He grabbed the Timekeeper by the throat and shoved him to the floor while yanking the chair out from under him. Creed folded it up and slid back into the ring. He was sick of it all the fans, the cheers, and especially Brand Frontier. Maybe beating the mighty Paragon into a red paste would finally shut everyone up. Creed turned and raised the chair over his head... There's a saying that says 'Hell hath no fury like a woman scorned.' That may very well be, but the fury of Brand Frontier, driven by having his personal life eviscerated by Kodiak Vic Creed, is something pretty impressive to behold. Frontier, on his back a second before, kipped up to his feet like a cruiserweight. Creed caught the sudden move and decided to keep his current train of thought, that being to spread Brand Frontier's brains all over the mat. That was the wrong choice. In a split second, Frontier planted his feet and drove his fist like a hydraulic piston into Creed's solar plexus, compressing the chest cavity and, more importantly, his heart. The same severely scarred heart that almost killed him when it went into severe arrest over a year ago. The same heart that almost took Sarah Creid's father away from her on his hospital bed, yet ended up taking him away from her when he recovered. Hammer of God. Creed, for the second time in a week, collapsed to the mat. He lay there motionless, save for the occasional twitching of his leg. Brand Frontier looked down at the helpless Kodiak. It would be so easy, so easy... The fans cheering snapped Frontier out of his daze. He walked over to the corner and retrieved the one thing that meant anything to Kodiak Vic Creed. Standing over the fWo World Champion, Brand Frontier held up the World Title before tossing it on Creed's chest. "That was just a taste of things to come Vic," Brand said as he walked away from the fallen Champ. "Thor" cued up as Brand Frontier hopped out of the ring. A referee came down to the ring to check on Creed's condition, but Brand already knew what the future held for the Main Man. Pain. Lots of pain. Between one and another Temerario nursed a sore arm, gritting his teeth against what appeared to be growing pain. Perhaps he had come back from the injury too quickly. Perhaps... but it seemed wrong somehow. Or perhaps... but no, the pain was nothing. Transitory, nothing more. The crowd was there, cheering... it was what he wanted and what he needed. Loss or victory, it meant little in the long run. He began to peel his mask off of his sweaty head when he heard his locker room door bang, as if someone had stumbled into it. Frowning, he stood to see if there was a problem in the hallway when it opened with a jarring thud and a young woman stumbled through it. Temerario had a slight moment to register that it was Yukio, a former member of the fWo roster who had been working dark matches for the developmental fed in recent weeks before another, larger and far less friendly face appeared in the doorway. "What's the matter, huh? Do I intimidate ya?" Neo was grinning a wide, sneering smile that was filled with malice and intent. He glanced up and saw Temerario and snarled. "This don't concern ya boy... get on out of here and leave us be." Neo grabbed at Yukio's arm, and she kicked him hard in the shin. He spat and swore, then swiftly delivered a hard backhand that cracked her head to one side and caused her to cry in sudden, sharp pain. "Girl, don't make this none more difficult than..." But Temerario had heard more than enough. Jumping up from the bench where he had sat he grabbed Neo's outstretched arm and spun him around. Neo cocked a fist back, but Temerario was too quick, even after the match, and ducked under. He brought up a boot that connected directly in Neo's stomach and grabbed his head as Neo's body jerked downward. Running forward with a clumsy bulldog, Temerario smashed Neo's skull into the hard, concrete wall and let go. Neo stumbled back, unconscious and bleeding heavily from the forehead. Temerario turned to Yukio and nodded. He didn't know what to say... didn't know what exactly Neo had been about to do, but knew that he had done the right thing. In a violent sport there were still rules, and still sins against God. Yukio looked up at him, then across at Neo. She spat at him and got to her feet, feeling gingerly at her neck which was growing an ugly red welt from where she had been slapped. Temerario looked at it, frowning. "You will need to..." "Shut up," Yukio said, cutting him off and glaring at him. "I don't need your help. Understand? Don't start thinking you're some kind of male hero because you're not. Not to me anyway, maybe to one of those silly girls... Aimz or Dawn or Ivy or..." "Then leave." Temerario's voice had gone cold and his jaw was set. "Sorry I helped." He didn't mean it of course, but now, in the middle of this ugly drama he did not want to be reminded of her... not when... But Yukio merely glared at him and slowly stomped out of the door, sparing one last ugly look at Neo. Temerario watched her go, and sighed. He didn't understand women, least of all these Americanized ones. He had not expected gratitude... but certainly he had not expected hostility either. Sighing, he walked back to the bench and began to pick up his clothes. If he hurried he could leave for the next arena early, and praying for safe travel, he began to pack his bag. Scott Slugger vs. Joey Villani Ruahh~! headed to the ring for a second-round Wrestler of the Year Tournament matchup, one that it would be fair to say absolutely nobody saw coming. "No Need To Explain" played, and Joey Villani, fresh off a shocking upset over Mitch Wilson, headed to the ring. The crowd booed, but Villani shook it off and rolled into the ring. He stretched a little and waited for his second straight legendary opponent. W. G. A. "Back In Black" hit, and the crowd exploded in cheers as Scott Slugger stepped out from backstage. Slugger did slap hands with the fans, but otherwise wasted no time in making his way to the ring. He stopped at ringside for a microphone, then rolled into the ring and climbed up onto the ring ropes, leaning out towards the fans. Fireworks exploded from the turnbuckles, causing Villani, standing too close to one, to jump. Slugger repeated the pose and the fireworks on the opposite ropes, then, as the music died down, stood a few feet away from Villani, who watched him cautiously. "Kid..." Slugger said. "I'll be honest. I don't know a whole hell of a lot about you, but I do know this - you defeated Mitch Wilson. One, two, three. You didn't just defeat him, you embarrassed him. In my book, that makes you A-OK. And if anybody does something like that to Mitch Wilson... I want to shake their hand." Slugger reached out a hand to Villani. Villani looked at it cautiously for a moment, then extended his hand and took Slugger's. Slugger shook for a second, smiling - then yanked Villani towards him and gave him a hard kick in the gut, knocking him to the mat. "First lesson of singles wrestling, kid - never forget that Ric Flair stopped being the dirtiest player in the game years ago." The fans cheered, because hey, they like guys with attitude, right? Plus all that built-up respect stuff Slugger has. Anyway, Slugger tossed the microphone aside and brought Villani back up to his feet. He whipped him into the corner, then followed him in with a quick charging back elbow. Slugger then took Villani out of the corner and brought him down to the mat with a quick, crisp snap suplex. Slugger got up and ran into the ropes, then dropped a leg. He dropped down and made the cover, but Villani quickly kicked out. Slugger brought Villani up again and whipped him into the ropes, then ran off the ropes himself. Slugger leapt high and clocked Villani with a jumping forearm to the face. He brought Villani back up again and knocked him backwards into the ropes with a series of forearms to the face. Slugger grabbed Villani and set to whip him across, but Villani managed to reverse the move, sending Slugger across the ring instead. As Slugger came off, Villani leapt up and hit him with a sharp dropkick that clocked him in the face, knocking Slugger down. Villani quickly bounced off the ropes, then dropped onto Slugger with a senton splash. He reached back and hooked the leg, but Slugger kicked out at one. Villani brought Slugger to his feet again and whipped him hard into the corner. Slugger slammed into the turnbuckles, and VIllani ran across the ring and hit Slugger with a leaping splash. Slugger staggered out of the corner, and Villani ran into the ropes, grabbed him by the head, and drove him into the mat with a bulldog. With Slugger down, Villani chose not to cover, but instead hopped quickly onto the second rope. He quickly measured Slugger, then stepped up onto the top rope and waited for Slugger to get up. Slugger staggered up to his feet... and Villani flew from the top rope. He caught Slugger flush in the face with a missile dropkick, driving his feet into the nose of the World's Greatest Athlete. Slugger dropped to the ground, and Villani landed smoothly next to him. He quickly rolled onto Slugger and hooked the leg, thinking that he might be on the verge of pulling off his second huge upset in as many weeks. One, two - Slugger kicked out. Villani pounded the mat in anger, then lifted Slugger up to his feet again. He whipped Slugger into the ropes, and as Slugger came off, Villani set to take him up and over with a belly-to-belly suplex. Slugger blocked it with a leg, then drove his knee into the gut of Villani, knocking him backward. Slugger charged at Villani, who ducked and backdropped Slugger up and over his head. Slugger, however, landed on his feet. Villani turned, and Slugger unleashed a superkick aimed at Villani's head. Villani saw it coming and ducked, then let loose with a kick of his own, a spinning back kick that cracked Slugger in the face and dropped him to the mat. Once again, Villani signaled that it was time to go up top, and headed to the top strand facing the crowd. He took a quick look over his shoulder, confirming Slugger was still there, then leapt off the top rope with a moonsault. Villani flew... And Slugger rolled. Villani splatted into the mat with the impact of his miss, and his momentum carried him up onto his feet, staggering backwards into the ring ropes. Slugger quickly got to his feet, and as Villani came off the ropes towards him, he caught him with a hard kick, doubling him over. Slugger then hooked Villani and lifted him up, then drove him into the mat with a powerbomb. Slugger held on to the move, lifted Villani up again, and executed it a second time. Slugger continued to hold his grip on Villani, lifted him up one more time... and hit a third powerbomb. With Villani down, Slugger signaled to the crowd that it was time to end it, giving the old "round 'em up" signal. Slugger waited, and Villani slowly got to his feet. Villani turned to face him... and Slugger sprinted at him. He leapt into the air, double-underhooked Villani's head, and spun. He then drove Villani into the mat. Curveball. Villani was down, the crowd was cheering, and Slugger made the academic cover. One, two, three. The ref raised Slugger's hand, and the fans continued to cheer as the WGA made his way into the third round of the Wrestler of the Year tournament. Slugger raised an arm to the crowd in acknowledgement, then picked up the microphone from where it lay in a corner of the ring. "Alright... I've never been one to talk just to hear my own voice - OK, actually that's exactly what I've been, so scratch that. But tonight, I'm gonna get right to the point. Mitch Wilson. Ice. I'm tired of playing games with you two punks. How long have we been doing this no, and the most we've done is tag team matches? Uh-uh. Not anymore. At Legends... Halloween night... we're hitting the ring. Myself. Whichever one of you two punks has the guts. So come on out here and tell the WGA who's it gonna be, alright?" After a moment, "Crushed" played, and Bad Attitude (sans intro) stepped out from backstage in street clothes, looking displeased in general. Shocking, huh? Mitch Wilson carried a microphone in his hand and looked in the ring at Slugger with a smirk. "So, one of us is gonna face you at Legends, huh? Hmm. Let me see. Um, gee... no. It's not that we don't want to fight you, it's just that we think this would piss you off more." Slugger nodded as if he had expected that. "OK. Your choice." Wilson blinked. "Say what? No tantrum, no threats? Just 'your choice'?" "Sure, sure. You want to say it now, you can say it now. That's cool. Of course, by saying your choice, I'm just using a figure of speech, because you really don't have a choice. You see, before tonight is over, a match will have been made between myself and one of you for Legends," Slugger said matter-of-factly. Ice took the microphone and laughed. "OK there, Scott. What are you gonna do, zap us with your special World's Greatest Athlete mind control powers? I believe I have two words that sum this up - what. Ever." Ice tossed the microphone aside and headed backstage, and Wilson following him, throwing a middle finger over his shoulder at Slugger as he went. Inside the ring, Slugger leaned forward on the ropes facing the entrance, as serene as could be, as Ruahh~! moved on... Result: Scott Slugger by pinfall The New Brand of Hero "I quit." Plain and fucking simple." Kevin Masters turned to walk out of the office. "Are you stupid, son? Are you that much of a fucking child? Didn't your father raise you better than this?" But he LIVED to give you this. To give you his legacy. And you shit it away. Look at you... He never would have let something like this, someone like Lundin stop him. Never." "YOU DIDN'T KNOW HIM!" You can try to escape, but you'll never come back again. And when that problem eats away at you more, you'll never be back here to solve it. I guarantee that. Or, you can listen to me, and be a man." Silver Fox waited, looking at Kevin Masters. Staring at his fist as it shook. The rage the young man held within was obvious, for now it was painted across his face as he turned back. I'm not going to be the next Brand Frontier. I'm not a fucking hero anymore." Kevin said. A sly smile appeared on Fox's stern face, "You think I give a damn? You be what you have to be Kevin. But you be it HERE. Now get out of my office." With that Kevin gave Fox one last glare, and turned away, storming out of the room. Fox flopped back down into his chair and exhaled deeply. Things really were changing, and not for the better. Steve-O vs. Crusader As Ruahh came back from commercial, Steve-O, one half of the fWo tag team Pretty Fly, was in the ring, bouncing back against the ropes and awaiting his second-round challenge in the Wrestler of the Year tournament. This mega-tournament, spanning months and involving literally every wrestler the fWo had to offer had been picking up steam in recent weeks, all building to the finals at fWo Legends at the end of the October. For the winner of the tournament, the biggest glory and honor the promotion has... arguably a greater honor than the fWo World Heavyweight Championship. For when a wrestler is declared the Wrestler of the Year, it is an honor that lasts for the entire year and no one can take that award away. As Steve-O's music died down, all eyes went to the top of the ramp and the man who had defeated Triple X in the first round. Long time ring vet Crusader walked out onto the ramp, blue and gold sparks falling all around him. He smiled at the reaction from the crowd and waved, saluting the fans that were chanting his name. As he walked to the ring scenes from the near-epic battle between Triple X and Crusader were shown, a match that several believed had been one of the best contests to date. Tonight, Crusader would be switching gears to tackle a decidedly tag-based wrestler. For the winner, the mountain of the Flying Frenchie, an fWo legend, would be next. The bell rang as Crusader slid into the ring, and at once both men locked up. The crowd popped at their immediate start to action, as Steve-O applied a quick armbar. Crusader reversed it, laying on an armbar of his own. The two fell into the ropes and the referee broke it, but at once Crusader took it to the mat with a drop toe hold and slapped on the armbar again. Steve-O moved to the ropes, then rolled over and locked an armbar of his own on. Neither man with the advantage, they broke and returned to their feet. Crusader nodded in respect, and Steve-O extended a hand... only to slap Crusader across the face to the gasps of the crowd. Steve-O went for an irish whip, but it was reversed as Crusader tossed Steve-O into the corner and began lacing hard chops across his chest. Staying on the offensive, Crusader landed a quick hiptoss, armdrag and then a Japanese armdrag in quick succession. Steve-O grabbed for a headlock, not finished and tapping into his amatuer skills with a shouldertackle. Crusader tried to reverse into a legsweep but was dropped hard by an elbow. A big clothesline sent Crusader to the outside, quickly followed by Steve-O, and the two began to exchange hard rights and lefts, brawling on the outside. Crusader tried a shoulder block, but Steve-O shifted away and he banged it on the ring post. Crusader was quickly tossed back in the ring, with Steve-O working over his midsection in the corner. Setting Crusader up, a huge T-Bone gutbuster by Steve-O brought Crusader to the mat. Steve-O worked quickly, trying to use the element of surprise with his old-school attack to work Crusader into a pinning position. He leveled a big knee to the midsection and continued the abdominal assault. A european uppercut by Steve-O, but Crusader was showing signs of life and came back with a stiff forearm and a swinging neckbreaker. Signaling to the fans, Crusader headed to the top, taking far too long... and an executed moonsault hit the mat as Steve-O rolled free. On the mat the member of Pretty Fly applied a seated abdominal stretch, which he transitioned into a crucifix roll. 1... 2... But Crusader shot an arm up, determined to stay in the match despite the surprising onslaught from Steve-O. Steve-O picked up Crusader and hit a quick bodyslam, then went to the top rope. But he was cut off by Crusader, who hooked the tights and hit a huge top-rope flatliner that spiked Steve-O into the mat. Both men were down, but it was Steve-O up first, shaking. They began to exchange forearms, and Crusader dug out a Sky High from nowhere! The crowd got to their feet, screaming as the referee dove for the pinfall count... 1... 2... But Steve-O kicked out at the last possible second. Crusader drove a knee into Steve-O's midsection, then launched into an all-out fury of shoulder blocks and drilled Steve-O down with a sitting piledriver. Once more Crusader climbed to the top rope, signaled to the crowd, and flew with a picture-perfect moonsault. It landed on target, and Crusader hooked the leg. 1... 2... 3! It was over... Crusader had advanced into the third round. As the crowd screamed around them, Crusader waved, saluted, and then glanced up the entrance ramp and narrowed his eyes into slits. There was the Flying Frenchie, looking on with a smirk on his face and clapping quietly. They stared each other down, knowing that in a short matter of time they would collide... with only one being able to continue in the most important tournament of the year. The fWo cameras returned to a buzzing Birmingham crowd awaiting more fWo sealed-of-approval Sports Entertainment. The cameras cut back to the entrance as Miles walked through sporting a poncho-style Eli Flair shirt (to a few cheers), blue jeans, a dangling Newport, yellow Oakleys, no shoes...and one MILESwide smirk. Boos, cheers, beer floaters and "We're Not Worthy" bowing sessions were given to the tag team specialist and 'Professional' Wrestler making his first live fWo television appearance. Nodding in approval, Miles brought the microphone up to his lips, as the crowd quieted... "Being a 'Professional,' I have a certain joy in doing my Internet research before I reach my next pit stop to the Wrestling Hall of Fame. And I know I haven't been here too long, but I gotta say that there's a lot of guys in the back that have a certain penchant for calling themselves, or even thinking that they're God. He knew he caught the crowd off-guard with the subject matter he was taking on. However, someone in the third row was REAL pissed noticing their beer got soiled with menthol. Meanwhile, back in the ring with his free hand, Miles started to count... "So, we've got a man from Beverly Hills that thinks he's YOUR GOD. The SUPERSTAR himself, Vince Jacobs." Miles' eyes rolled under his sunglasses, as he stuck out his thumb to signify numero uno. "We've got a man 'also known' as God himself trying to turn a couple of lovable stoners into something they're not. Bad people." The index finger, thus we have two. "Honestly, who wants to see that? Sphere - mental note...Stoners are like dogs, anything you say to them they forget in 5 minutes. Trust me, midgets are the way to build a franchise. But hey - at least those two have the knowledge to not throw it in our faces EVERY night. But get this!" Miles' gaze returned to the camera, his mood - incredulous. "What's even worse is that he's named after a dairy product known mostly for causing constipation!" Boos quickly turned to laughs and cheers, Miles grinning from his own joke as well...
