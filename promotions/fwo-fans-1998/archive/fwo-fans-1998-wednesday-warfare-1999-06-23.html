Wednesday Warfare, 23 June 1999

23 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction With Numbered Days just a few days away and fresh in the minds of the the competitors that will be competiting at the event, a grim atmosphere decended on the arena as wrestlers arrived stoic and ready for battle. The security guards gave Eron the Relentless no problems this night, as he was allowed into the arena easily, although the dirty looks that followed his arrival signalled that all was still not right with the superstar. Other wrestlers, like the TCW newcomer Mike Bear, came to the arena alone leaving visitors to speculate what the true nature of the new wrestler was. Micky Maliboo was seen signing contracts with the commissioner, who called over Scott Hart to inform him that his match was signed for Sunday. The Commissioner then turned to Mike Heftel, telling him that his new gimmick would be unveiled on Sunday. Chris Finn spoke with Geoff Green about some upcoming interview segments, and several wrestlers crowded around Mick Foley to hear him tell tales of what it was like to compete in the death matches in Japan. Few made noise as they milled around the arena, Scott Slugger and Ice chatted quietly in a corner, holding their respective titles. The two walked down a hallway on their way to their dressing rooms, when a low rumble was heard. Looking around them, they began to notice small drips of water running along the floor. Ignoring the noise at first, soon the rumbling grew louder, and as the pair turned a corner they were met with a gigantic wall of water. A title wave swept the pair off their feet, crashing them into the hard concrete wall of the arena... leaving the pair dazed and confused. As security arrived to help the two to their feet, Warfare's music began to play and the sound of the crowd overwhelmed all... Mike Bear vs. Chris Kidman Jr. Bear and Kidman started off the match with a lock-up, just feeling things out in the beginning of the match. Bear over powered Kidman into a hammerlock; but Kidman used his agility to escape out of it with a snapmare. He then bounced off the ropes and did a half-somersault into a necksnap, going to work on Bear' neck. Pinfall attempt by Kidman, and a kickout at one and a half by Bear. Kidman continued, this time throwing Bear out onto the floor... he then went up to the top rope, and waited as Bear struggled to get to his feet. Kidman flew off with a flying bodypress, but Bear managed to catch him with both arms, and then drove him into his knee with a shoulder breaker. Winded, Bear caught his breath; and then rolled Kidman into the ring. Bear grabbed a chair and threw it into the ring, and then slid underneath the bottom rope and climbed in himself. Bear then grabbed the chair off the mat and waited for Kidman to get up. However, the sneaky referee stood behind Bear, waiting for him to lift it up higher to hit Kidman. As Bear did this, the referee grabbed the chair away. Kidman was still smarting from the blow received, and was on all fours as if he was getting up. Bear turned around and yelled at the referee, who pushed Bear backwards, and he tripped back over Kidman. However, Bear sprung back up to his feet, and as Kidman finally reached up, he turned around and ducked Bear' superkick attempt just in time... unfortunately, the kick caught the face of the referee, who fell to the mat instantly. Bear seemed apologetic for what happened, but he then turned around and was lifted up by Kidman who hit him with a Michinoku Driver. Kidman then pulled him into the corner, and went up to the top rope... Bear however, got up rather quickly, but was hit with a missle dropkick by Kidman. Kidman went for a cover, and got one as a referee came running down to ringside to make the count. Bear kicked out at two, and Kidman seemed befuddled as what left to do. He signalled for the shooting star press, and went up to the top rope to execute. Kidman flew off and somehow, someway, Bear rolled out of the way and Kidman hit into the mat with a sickening thud. Bear then got up to his feet, and pulled Kidman up. He executed a near perfect death valley driver, and then covered Kidman in the center of the ring. As Bear celebrated up in the ring, Falcon came down to ring with something in one of his hands behind him. Falcon warned Bear that he had something that Bear wanted, and meanwhile we went backstage to see a limosuine arriving. The limosuine pulled up and several armed guards got out of it, and Chris Universal stepped out dressed in a leisure suit, and made his way into the building. Result: Mike Bear by Pinfall The return of Fathom The crowd cheered the arrival of Dina Sol, known to fWo fans more commonly as the retired wrestler Fathom. Dina walked out onto the entrance ramp, and was interviewed by James Armstrong, who first asked her how she enjoyed married life. Dina announced that she was enjoying herself, and only wished that John Skyler would have been able to make it to the show tonight, but that he was home with a serious illness, and she didn't know if he would even be able to make it to Numbered Days. Dina then announced that she would be present at Numbered Days to be the guest ring announcer, and that she would be taking over an announcing position on Sunday Slaughter. She then announced that while she was coming back to work, she was never, ever, ever going to wrestle again... and that everyone could trust her on that. When asked how she could be so certain, Dina announced that she was pregnant, and that John and her were going to have a baby. While the crowd absorbed this information, James Armstrong remembered something, and reached into his bag for a large envelope, which he handed to Dina. James told Dina that he couldn't deliver the envelope like he promised he would many weeks ago, and Dina snatched in angrily from his hands. Abruptly ending the interview, Dina stomped backstage leaving James to wonder exactly what he had said wrong... Darkness vs. Thumper Warfare returned from a commercial break to find Thumper, accompanied by Twitch, awaiting his opponent of the evening, which of course, is Darkness. Darkness made his way out as "Whispering Souls" played. The ominous presence of Darkness was felt immediately as he climbed into the ring and grabbed Twitch, and threw him over the top rope out onto the floor. He then turned around, and kicked Thumper in the stomach, and continued as the bell rang. Darkness choked Thumper repeatedly in the corner, giving no mercy to Thumper. He then grabbed him and slapped him hard, repeatedly, over and over again... taking his frustration out on one-half of the team they call Trouble. Darkness then threw Thumper out through the middle ropes onto the floor, and followed it up with a suicide plancha... and both men crashed into the ground. Darkness onslaught continued as he threw Thumper over the guardrail, and followed him out into the crowd. As they reached a portion where there was a fivefoot dropoff from one section to the "fWo Live Internet Broadcast" section below that, where Matt Loco & Scott Taylor were reporting. Darkness gorilla pressed Thumper high into the air, and dropped him... Taylor & Loco quickly got out of the way, and Thumper went chest first onto a table and knocked several lap-tops off. Twitch soon appeared on camera, and Darkness grabbed him by the head, brought him up onto the table, and hit a piledriver right through the Internet broadcast position. Darkness then took Thumper back down through the crowd, and threw him back over the guardrail. He then unhooked a piece of the guardrail, and literally ran at Thumper with a clothesline using the guardrail. Finally, he brought the action back into the ring and pinned Thumper relatively easily, as Twitch was left back up near the Internet Broadcast area, out cold. As Darkness quietly celebrated in the ring, and suddenly, the Mad Trucker wheeled out on stage using a wheelchair. He then challenged Darkness to a match at Numbered Days. With everyone all thinking the same thing (that Trucker could not possibly wrestle in two days with a broken leg), Warfare went to a preview of Numbered Days... Result: Darkness by Pinfall Losing It... "Do you buy what the $ellout is $elling?" blares over the soundsystem as "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal makes his way out underneath the TravisTron with a microphone in hand. Universal wastes no time in climbing into the ring, and as the camera focuses in on him, it becomes apparent that Universal is wearing a bandage on his forehead, covering up the damage that Mick Foley did this past Sunday. Universal doesn't even acknowledge the dollar $ign pyrotechnic that lowers into place and begins shooting off the gold & green fireworks. Universal motions for his music to be stopped, and then begins speaking: "Over the past few weeks, I've been losing it. Losing what, you may ask? My grip on things here in the fWo. First, I was cheated out of my fWo World Title match against Ultra-Violet a couple of weeks ago. Then, I lost the Internet Title to the Black Panther. I failed to acquire Mr. Socko last week on Warfare. Austin Davis, my mortal enemy, acquired the services of Megan Dawn for thirty days. And finally, just this past Sunday... I lost a lot of blood at the hands of Mick Foley. Losing things within my grasp is not a feeling that the $ellout is accustomed to. What I am accustomed to is waking up each morning with breakfast already made. I'm accustomed to having people walk up to me; and I say... "I'm Chris Universal... WHO THE HELL ARE YOU?". I'm accustomed to getting what I want, exactly how I want it. And the past few weeks, I've had neither of those feelings. Until now. You see, this Friday... I can and will turn it back around, so I'm back on top of the fWo. This Friday, I meet Mick Foley in a First-Blood match. And the winner of our match... which will be me, will go on to face the Black Panther. I've never been able to beat the Black Panther, but I plan on changing that on Friday. I plan on re-acquiring MY Internet Title at Numbered Days. And finally... the biggest acquisition of my career will come on Friday. The wrestling world will be shocked, and the revolution WILL be televised on pay-per-view. Buy the ppv, see Mick & BP do the j-o-b, and watch ME become the most powerful man in the history of the wrestling... if you BUY what THE SELLOUT is SELLING!" With that, Universal exited to a chorus of boos... Universal did not seem to care, as he smirked and brushed off the fans on his way back into the lockerroom. Joy vs. Waru Joy came out (with the Happiness Title intact around his waist) on the ramp and offered anyone to come take a lick off of his big lollipop. He then pulled out a giant lollipop from backstage and smiled proudly on stage, almost stroking it and licking his very own lollipop. The crowd booed Joy, who urged them to get up, be happy, and come lick his lollipop (which is a giant size smiley face on a stick). Suddenly, Waru comes out from behind and grabs the lollipop from Joy, and then smacks him repeatedly. Waru then takes Joy and throws him off the stage threw a table, before going down there and demanding the ref count the pinfall there. The referee reluctantly obliges, and then Waru goes back up on stage and begins stomping the huge lollipop into pieces. Joy slowly crawled back towards the ramp, and finally made it to his feet. Waru charged at him and he took the Happiness Title off of himself and swung at Waru, cracking him upside the head. He then turned towards the ring and held his Happiness Title high above his head, feeling proud of what he had done... as he turned back towards the TravisTron, he was attacked by Primetime & Nikki... who said they would "fornicate all night long and there was a damn thing" Joy could do about it. As they walked to the back, Nikki saw the lollipop, looked at Primetime, and bit her lower lip as if she was telling him something. Waru meanwhile made his way backstage, and noticed a strange dripping coming from the celing. As Waru quickly looked around him, the sound of thunder was heard and the lights went out in his area of the hall. Sounds of a beating were heard, with Waru crying in pain... Result: Waru by Pinfall All plans have a flaw... Backstage a limosine pulled up, and Silver Fox, along with the Chameleon, Total Eclipse, and Fyre stepped out of it. Dick Venom quickly ran up to the four, and asked them about their plans tonight. Silver Fox only laughed, and told Venom that he wouldn't be so foolish as to tell their plans on national television when the Dark Riders and French Foundation were likely watching backstage. Venom then asked where the two mysterious men were tonight, the large masked man and the smaller masked man. Silver Fox only laughed, and then leaned closer to the camera, whispering what he said as if to make it confidental. Silver Fox said, "Just remember Primetime... Mitch... Stone... Plague... and War... your leaders betrayed others to get where they are today. Ask yourselves... aren't Death and the Flying Frenchie a large and a small man after all? This Friday you will see the formation of the greatest stable the world has ever known... count on it. And for all those marks on the internet that say I'm getting a 'push' too fast... well, you're jealous, and I know who you are..." Silver Fox laughed deeply, and lead Fyre, the Chameleon, and bringing up the rear the Total Eclipse into the arena. The Total Eclipse began to get left behind because he could not walk as fast as the others, and as he struggled to keep up a pair of hands suddenly grabbed him from behind and thrust him down to the hard floor. The Black Quicksilver, weilding Head, battered the Total Eclipse until the man could no longer stand, and proceeded to drop heavy camera equipment on his leg, causing the big man to scream out in pain. As the Chameleon and the others ran to save their partner, Black Quicksilver grabbed Stump and quickly ran away... Stevie Roberts & VL vs. Rudo & Sid Snow VL and Stevie Roberts came to the ring, with the rest of Doomsday trailing along behind. Judge Steel and Samuel Smooth looked disgruntled with their current place in the team, and paced around the outside of the ring as Stevie and VL made out. The Surgeon on the other hand looked stragely alive, and began to measure the heads of fans in the audence, mumbling something about "cloning Stevie". Rudo and Sid Snow came out onto the entrance ramp, with Rudo calling out to the crowd in the way Machina used to. The crowd booed him, thinking that Machina's bit was stale when he wasn't fired, and blows now. Rudo flipped off the fans, and then began smacking Sid, who was tired and unable to defend himself after the match with Scott Slugger last Friday. Sid limped, favoring the leg that had been put into the chipper, and held his head where the Piranha had bitten him. Angry and disgusted, Rudo tossed the beaten Sid into the ring and stormed off to the backstage area, where he was met by Billy James and the Shocker, insisting Rudo give them some answers about the future of the Lone Gunmen. Meanwhile, Stevie Roberts covered the helpless Sid Snow for the pinfall victory. Roberts and VL celebrated inside the ring, until Judge Steel entered the ring and shoved VL aside, telling Stevie that they didn't need her. Roberts shouted back as VL was held back by Smooth and the Surgeon. Finally calming down, Roberts told Steel that since he felt this way he could wrestle VL next week on Warfare... and that the loser was out of Doomsday forever. This seemed to satisfy Steel, who reluctantly walked backstage with the rest of Doomsday. Result: Stevie Roberts & VL Broken forth walls The cameras switch to an empty room with a single chair in the center of it. The moment holds, and then footsteps are heard. In walks the Speed Demon, wearing jeans and a t-shirt. He sits in the chair and looks into the camera. He clears his throat, and said, "Hi. Oh, well, my name is William Bailey. Some of you may know me as the Speed Demon. That is, if you've been looking close. You see kids, I'm what they call a JOBBER. That means, when one of the mid-carder guys wants to win a match instead of doing the big J.O.B. (that means lose) to the big winners in the promotion... you know, guys like Scotty Cool, Black Panther or the Flying Frenchie. You see, wrestlers usually have a number in their contracts that is like... the number of wins they have to have. So people like me are brought in to make sure they get that number of wins and don't like... sue the owner or something. They could likely make a hell of a lot of money too, 'cause he's got all that computer industry money. Anyway, if you do know me then you know that I haven't had a win in like... well, a long ass time. So I'm here tonight to ask for someone to please put me over just once. You see, I've had some problems with my wife lately because she says I'm a big loser. If I could just get one win, I'm sure she would start respecting me again. Now, I think I've exposed the business enough for tonight, but there is just one more thing I've got to say. You know, there are people that write these matches we wrestle in called bookers. As many people backstage know, it pays to kiss the ass of the bookers. How best to do that? With magical green clovers and purple horseshoes. You see, our bookers are small little elves who live in the happy forests of Narnia. Many think that they are evil elves, but really they are... hey! What's going on? Where are the cameras going? I'm not done, dammit!" As the camera backed out of the room, more complaining was heard... Ultra Violet (c) vs. New Ninja The New Ninja came to the ring to a good reception, having been absent from the fWo for some time. The Ninja got into the ring and climbed up onto the top turnbuckle, pointing to his waist and pointing to various cheering fans in the crowd. Ultra Violet's music began to play, and he wandered out into the entrance ramp looking confused. This got large pop. Ultra Violet realized where he was, and asked for a hard hat and some gloves. Violet and Malcom then came out onto the ramp, and told Ultra Violet where he really was. The title was firmly around his waist, fastened with what appeared to be duck tape. As Ultra Violet stumbled down to the ring, the Ninja grew tired and ran out to grab him. The Ninja grabbed UV by the hair and drug him to the ring, where he leapt onto the turnbuckle and delivered a high jumping legdrop on the standing UV. Ultra Violet fell to the mat, and the Ninja grabbed the leg and snapped over. Ultra Violet was helpless as the Ninja stood him up and attacked with several head butts. This had little effect on UV, and he recovered enough to mount an offense. Ultra Violet whipped the Ninja into the ropes, and hit a spinning heel kick. He then climbed to the top rope and prepared a frog splash, but he missed it and fell to mat with a dull thud. The Ninja, sensing victory, tossed Ultra Violet to the mat with a suplex. Malcom, sensing victory, quickly ran backstage. Violet, sensing a nearby flower cart, began to "Oooo" with some "Ahhhh". As the Ninja made a cover, Malcom emerged with Cactus Jon, who quickly ran to the ring and pulled the Ninja off. The ref, seeing this interference since it was directly in front of him, called for the DQ. Jon then helped up Ultra Violet, telling him that since he saved his title he should get that title shot now. Ultra Violet nodded, and as the cameras went off the air told Jon that he would see to signing the match right away... assuming he remembered... Result: New Ninja by DQ
