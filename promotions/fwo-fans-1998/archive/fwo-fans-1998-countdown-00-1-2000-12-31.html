Countdown '00: 1, 31 December 2000

31 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "Life it seems, will fade away Drifting further every day Getting lost within myself Nothing matters no one else I have lost the will to live Simply nothing more to give There is nothing more for me Need the end to set me free Things are not what they used to be Missing one inside of me Deathly lost, this can't be real Cannot stand this hell I feel Emptiness is filing me To the point of agony Growing darkness taking dawn I was me, but now He's gone No one but me can save myself, but it to late Now I can't think, think why I should even try Yesterday seems as though it never existed Death Greets me warm, now I will just say good-bye" -"Fade To Black", by Metallica Introduction History is a funny thing. It means different things to different people and often it is as fluid as rain, written and rewritten on the whims of mankind. Facts, or things once believed to be solid and constant change over time, and the very meaning of the facts can change until they no longer resemble their origins. Often the facts themselves are forgotten in the heat of the moment, and once thought essential details are lost forever in the excitement of the moment. On the outside to all of us, James Armstrong portrayed himself differently. To us, he pretended that he wasn't happy, and was already regretting what he was about to do with the fans Wrestling organization. But inside, he was very excited and had become caught up in the moment. This likely 'caused him to lose sight of the fact there was one small detail that would prevent him from selling the fWo tonight. He just didn't know it yet. Neither did we. But we were about to find out; just as Armstrong was. The Beginning Countdown To Oblivion 1 began near ringside of the American Airlines Arena in Miami, Florida. Ironically, just one year ago tonight, the America Airlines Arena held it's very first event - a National Basketball Association game between the Miami Heat & Orlando Magic. Fans sat impatiently, waiting for the show to start. Matt Panzer began walking down the ramp with Jeremiah Frankton. Frankton immediately took his seat at ringside, and put on his headset, in preparation to call tonight's action. Panzer took his spot in the ring, and began counting down inside the arena, telling everyone that the show would start in ten seconds. Ten. Night. Eight. Seven. Six. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. Everyone looked to the stage, and noticed something different about the stage. There was a portion of the stage to the right, with another CountdownTron. On it, a clock was ticking down to what some people felt was the true millennium. Tonight was New Year's Eve afterall. The numbers read one hour and fifty nine minutes, and everyone realized that in two hours, it would officially be 2001. Fireworks shot off from strategically placed positions inside the American Airlines Arena, before the arena lights dimmed, and "Pretty Fly For A White Guy" by the Offspring began to play. Finally, the show began. State Of The fWo Address The acting owner of the fans Wrestling organization James Armstrong walked out onto the stage with the fWo World Championship draped over his left shoulder, and a microphone in his right hand. Armstrong was greeted by boos and jeers from the crowd. Armstrong had gotten used to it, and this point, he didn't care. He didn't care about anyone but himself. He was going to sell the fWo, and didn't care what would happen to it afterwards. Armstrong ignored the fans, and entered the ring via the steel steps. Once inside the ring, he signalled to the fWo technical crew to cut his music, as he didn't want to waste any time with what he was about to announce. It should be pointed out that there were several chairs set up inside the ring, as well as a table. He spoke quietly and cautiously at first. "Thank you, thank you." The fans jeered slightly upon hearing this, but quieted down so Armstrong could continue, this time in more of a matter-of-factlike approach. "As of this moment, I am still the acting owner of the fWo, so I am fully responsible for the following announcements. Before I begin here, I have been instructed by my lawyers not to comment on the status of Mike Bear or Wretch... they are no longer gainfully employed by the fWo, and that's all I can say about that. Second of all, as many of you learned on fWoWrestling.com, Ric Chronos was injured last night once again at the hands of Primetime. Essentially, he has re-aggravated the same injury to his neck that he suffered this past August, and the last time he was out he was out for nearly three months. Unfortunately, doctors told me this morning that the re-aggravation of the original injury could set him out for over four months. That said, he will not be able to compete in the fWo given his current condition, and since he cannot compete, he cannot defend this fWo World Title." Obviously, the fans did not like the first two things Armstrong had to say, and it showed by their influx of jeers. "Given that, as of this moment, Ric Chronos is no longer the fWo World Champion." The fans again showed their disapproval. Armstrong stared into a sea of fans standing with middle fingers pointed in his direction; and endured more boos yet again. "Now, if I could, I'd like to introduce you all to the fine group of people I will be selling the fWo to tonight." Armstrong turned to the Countdown Stage, and expected to see the people he had been in heavy negogiations with since Christmas last Monday. Instead, he heard something he didn't expect. "Start Me Up", by The Rolling Stones. And then, he saw someone he didn't expect to see. The Return The true owner of the fans Wrestling organization. Chris Finn. Finn walked out onto the stage, and Armstrong sat in the ring with his mouth dropped. Unlike the boos we were hearing earlier tonight, we were now hearing cheers. A lot of them. Armstrong placed the fWo World Title on the table behind him, before gulping at the sight of Finn. Finn had not been seen since August 23rd, 2000 - the final Wednesday Warfare, when The Boston Strangler snapped, and put him in the Boston Massacre with little provocation. Finn walked down the ramp slowly at first, before picking up the pace, and entering the ring. Armstrong backed away from him slowly as he came face to face with Finn. "Just who the hell do you think you are, James?" Armstrong stumbled for an answer, but Finn didn't give him enough time. "I don't want to hear what lame excuses you have. Do you realize how stupid you are? You've been wasting MONTHS of time negogiating with people about selling the fWo. And why is that, James? Do you have any idea? It's because you don't OWN the fWo. You are acting in my place - that meant you could make certain decisions, but selling the fWo was definately not one of them. And your lawyers must be the cheapest ones around, because they would've told you that from the very beginning. You had NO authority to sell the fWo. You know, I didn't watch the fWo for months. I didn't watch wrestling for months. I didn't hear about this sport, or this promotion, until two weeks ago. When a certain individual came to me and told me what you were up to. I checked out the proper paperwork, and sure enough, I and this individual came to the conclusion after talking with our lawyers, and other authorities on the subject, that you had no authority to sell the fWo. None at all. But you had no idea, do you? This individual wanted to tell you, but I told him to wait, because I wanted to do it myself. You better go back there and tell that group of investors that there is no deal... why? Because James, you are no longer the acting owner of the fWo. Your priveleges... are hereby officially revoked. Simply put, James, you're fired." The fans ovated. Armstrong no longer had any power, but perhaps it was too late. Had Finn not come here sooner, perhaps Wretch, Mike Bear & even Ric Chronos would still be... here. But they weren't. Finn continued. "But hold on a moment, James. You may want to hear this. I decided to pick tonight because I watched Countdown To Oblivion 3, and I watched Countdown To Oblivion 2, and I saw the decisions you made that led to the departures of Wretch & Mike Bear. And late last night, I took the first flight out of Idaho to meet with that certain individual that informed me of what you were trying to do here in Miami. And I decided to show up and ruin your intentions. Didn't I do just that, James?" Armstrong nodded slowly, with a look of shame on his face. Finn smiled, before continuing once again. "Nevertheless, I met with that individual today. And James, you were right, the fWo was going to be sold today. In fact, it already was. Just not to the people you think. I'm pleased to announce that as of 2pm this afternoon local Florida time, I sold the fans Wrestling organization." The fans didn't know what to think. Since they trusted Finn to a certain degree, they allowed him to make to continue before passing judgment on his decision. "I'm pleased to introduce to you all the new owner of the fans Wrestling organization..." The lights dimmed. A familiar phrase echoed throughout the arena, before loud music played. That familiar phrase was... "If you buy... what The Sellout... is selling." The New Owner "Mr. $ellout" Chris Universal walked out onto the stage to a huge ovation of cheers from his hometown fans in Miami, Florida. Wearing a very expensive suit, Universal strolled down the stage with a pleased look on his face, before he entered the ring and shook the hand of Chris Finn. Armstrong sat in disbelief, as he certainly did not expect this to happen. Finn continued to talk as Universal stood near him. "Yes, that's right. Universal was the one who told me what Armstrong was trying to do. Two weeks ago, Universal had a meeting with the Armstrong, and learned some things that were both funny and disturbing at the same time. You see, James was trying to unload the fWo so he could do what he dreamed of doing all along - building his own casino in Las Vegas with blackjack and of course, hookers. Selling the fWo would do just that. Armstrong of course, being the blithering idiot that he is, told Universal this directly. For some reason, James thought that he could trust Universal with this information since Universal offered to work for free. Universal offered to work for free because he didn't need the money, he had *plenty* of it. And I do mean plenty. His father unfortunately passed away this past September, and left Universal a large sum of inheritance money. Universal thought about purchasing the National Basketball Association's Miami Heat, but that wasn't an option, unfortunately. Instead, Universal and I hammered out a deal this morning, and signed the proper paperwork at 2pm this afternoon." The fans cheered upon hearing this, and Armstrong looked very dejected and embarassed in the ring. Armstrong decided he had largely enough, and began to walk away, but Finn had one more thing left to tell him. "James, don't leave yet." Armstrong turned slowly back to face Finn, who slowly walked towards him. "Here, Chris. Hold this." Finn gave the microphone to Chris Universal, before taking off his suit jacket, and setting it on the table. He then looked at Armstrong, and smiled. Armstrong thought that Finn was going to shake his hand, but Armstrong got something else he certainly did not expect. A hard right hand to the jaw. Armstrong fell to the mat in pain. He held his jaw, as Universal gave Finn back his suit jacket. Finn put it on, before looking down at Armstrong, and chuckling. He then asked for the microphone. "I've been waiting to do that for two years... it's about damn time." He then looked at Universal, shook his hand, and then acknowledged the crowd and exited the ring to a huge ovation. Armstrong helped himself up with help from the ropes, and looked at the crowd. Smilling. Laughing. Making fun of him. Just as he had done to them for the past two years. Armstrong sighed, and prepared to leave. But Universal wasn't done with him yet. An Offer "James, don't leave just yet." Armstrong quickly protected his face, prompting fans to laugh again. Armstrong frowned as he looked at Universal, who was slightly chuckling. "James, Finn fired you, but I have the power to re-hire you. And I'd like to have you commentate with Frankton... you know what you're doing there, at least. So, how about it? Go join Jeremiah... and finish tonight's show?" The fans booed quietly, but Armstrong nodded, and joined Frankton at ringside, but not without tripping first, which brought more laughs from the crowd. "Thanks, James. With that out of the way, I have some important announcements to make regarding several things here tonight, so please, bare with me." Important Announcements "First of all, tonight, Austin Davis was scheduled to take on the fWo World Champion. But, we obviously do not have an fWo World Champion. Ric Chronos has unfortunately been hurt, and like James said earlier, he is unable to defend the title. Now, obviously, the simple solution would be to award the title to Austin Davis. But, something happened earlier today. I actually already made my first decision several hours ago. I fired Austin Davis, and released him from his contract. He is no longer employed by the fWo." Fans in the crowd was pleased by this decision; and showed it by cheering wildly. Universal continued. "Now, the title is vacant for the second time this year. Ironically, the last time that it was vacant, Davis ended up being the next champion - tonight, however, that won't be the case. After the deal was completed, I called up Mike Bear & Wretch, and asked them if they would like to be interested in working for the fWo again under new contracts. They were both very interested. That said, I signed both to new deals this afternoon, and they are live in the building tonight, and they will be a part of a first here tonight. Tonight, the fWo World Title will hang high above the ring in the first ever four-way ladder match." Fans seemed pleased by this decision as well, knowing that Mike Bear & Wretch were back in the saddle once again. "Yes, I thought that would be something you all liked. However, the other two participants may not be someone you like. I have decided since Mitch Wilson was never actually pinned for the fWo World Title, he will also be in the match. The other reason he is in this match is because the fWo International Title will be included in this match - since the titles mean essentially the same thing. The other participant... and like it or not he is still a major player here, but it will be Primetime. However, I have placed Primetime on probation... and if he attempts to seriously injure someone again, I'm not going to sit on my ass and do nothing about it. I guarantee you that, Brian. At any rate, the only way to win this match will be to climb a ladder... and reach both the fWo World & International Titles to become the undisputed fWo World Champion!" More cheers from the crowd were heard as Universal continued. "In other action, a battle royal will be held tonight to determine the number one contender to the fWo Internet Title, and they will face the champion at some point in the near future. To save time, I will only name a few of the participants of that match. 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly, 'The Future' Lance Star, 'Black Plague' Ruben Ross, Crusader and at least ten others will be involved in that match. Also, Crusader will meet Plague tonight in a steel cage match, and the fWo World Tag Team Titles will be on the line in a match between Damnation and 'Kodiak' Vic Creed & 'The Future' Lance Star. And my final announcement... as you know, the fWo has been based in Redmond, Washington since it's establishment back in 1998. Well, as of April, the fWo will have a new headquarters right here in Miami, Florida! And for those of you that aren't aware, the fWo's biggest pay-per-view of the year CyberSlam 4 will be held from this arena in April of 2001!" Fans popped loudly at these statements, before Universal thanked everyone for their support, and exited the ring. With that, Countdown To Oblivion 1 went to it's first commercial break of the night... and fans inside the arena noticed by the special clock that there was an hour and thirty minutes left until 2001. Black Quicksilver vs. STUMP's Kidnapper Countdown To Oblivion 1 returned from the commercial break, to find STUMP'S Kidnapper already in the ring. He wore the same attire he wore at Countdown 3 and 2, and this time, he had STUMP at ringside with him. STUMP sat on a chair at ringside, and looked very anxious to be back in the good graces of his buddies in the STUMP Squad. However, that would not be possible without the help of one man. Black Quicksilver. "Slave To The Grind" by Skid Row began, bringing out the essential leader of the STUMP Squad, Black Quicksilver. Quicksilver raised his arms in the air, before pointing at STUMP'S Kidnapper in the ring, and telling him that he was going down quicker than you could say "1-2-3". BQ slid into the ring, and STUMP'S kidnapper immediately attacked. The two men battled back and forth, before STUMP'S kidnapper went up top, and flew off with a legdrop. BQ moved however, and went on a roll of moves. He hit several of his signature moves, including the Cubic Zirconium Cutter, before finishing things off with the Silver Splash for the pin. STUMP'S kidnapper quickly slid out onto the floor after losing and grabbed STUMP. Red Dragon & Mike Heftel quickly ran down to the ring and joined BQ; and looked outside the ring cautiously at the kidnapper. The STUMP Squad told the kidnapper to hand over STUMP, but he shook his head no, before finally walking up to the stage. Finally, he got on the microphone, and addressed the STUMP Squad. "It was ME... Black Quicksilver.... it was ME... ALLLLLLLLL ALONG!" The kidnapper took off his mask, and then his shirt. Underneath his original shirt, he wore a shirt that said "Chris Titan is here." Everyone assumed his name was Chris Titan, and a few fans remembered him from Tornado Wrestling - but still didn't act surprised. Pissed that nobody knows who he is, Titan began jumping up and down throwing a fit on stage. Finally, he pointed at STUMP, then pointed off the stage. Titan then grabbed STUMP by the... well, a body part. And threw STUMP off the stage. Almost instantaneously, the fans began booing and jeering Titan. Titan seemed proud of his accomplishment, and showed it by winking at the STUMP Squad. Heftel went berserk in the ring, to the point of screaming out "Oh my God, you killed STUMP! You bastard!" Heftel tried to charge at Titan, but BQ & Dragon held him back. Titan laughed as he headed backstage, while the fans continued to boo. Shortly thereafter, fWo medics rushed to the scene, to help STUMP off the ground via a stretcher... Claire joined the STUMP Squad, and everyone wondered if STUMP would be okay or not. Result: BQ by pinfall Remember Me? Backstage, Juno Law walked away from the caterer's table with a fresh cup of coffee in hand. From the corner of his eye, he caught the image of Scot Hartt & The Abusah celebrating with Wretch, who earlier today signed a new contract. "What does your contract say?" Wretch looked at it. "Sweet! What does yours say?" Hartt looked at his. "Dude!" Both men then looked at Abusah, who still apparently did not have a contract, but acted like he did. "WAT DOEZ MYNE SAY? SWEAT? DOOD? WAT? TELL ME!" Hartt & Wretch laughed, before pushing Abusah away. Abusah frowned, and then saw Law walking. "HAY! UR DA GY EYE SPEERED! A LONG TYME AGOH!" Law walked up to Hartt' lackey, and asked him if he wanted a medal or something. Abusah remarked that he already had a medal before - he was the Hardcore Champion. Law smirked. "Great job. You were the champion of a division for people that can't wrestle... or who are mentally retarded. Oops, your mom never told you, did she?" Abusah shrugs as Hartt walks over. "Why are you picking on Abusah? He has back-up you know." Law shrugged. "Who? Wretch? I'm so scared. Please." Law attempts to walk away, but Abusah gets in his way. "HOAP U FEAL BETER AFTER DAT DOCK MEST UP UR MYND!" Law just stood their with his fists clinched, as Abusah & Hartt stood in confusion. Suddenly, Law took them down with a lariat, before grabbing a nearby broom and using it in ways that would likely hurt someone. Law then spit on both men, before walking away. Wretch came over to check on them, while Abusah was confused. "WAZ IT SUMTING EYE SED?" Wretch shrugged and helped his lackeys up, while Countdown went elsewhere... Damnation (c) vs. 'Kodiak' Vic Creed & The Future "From This Day" by Machinehead started up, bringing out the fWo World Tag Team Champions. Hellrazor & Fear stepped onto the stage beneath CountdownTron. Fans noticed the specially constructed Countdown Clock/Stage now read one hour and seventeen minutes until the start of what many felt was the true millennium. Damnation walked down to the ring with Lamont in a special carrier, and as they reached ringside, the ovation of cheers grew louder. Raising their arms in the air, the fans continued to applaud the two Underground representatives. Damnation removed their belts and gave them to the referee, who gave them to someone at ringside. "Walk" by Pantera replaced the music of Machinehead, and on cue, out came two of America's Superior Specimens, the reigning fWo Cruiserweight Champion Lance Star, and the 'Kodiak' Vic Creed. Star & Creed joined the champions in the ring, and decided Star would begin the match first. It was the first time Creed was teaming with someone inside an fWo ring. Star & Creed did well in their first outing, and in fact executed several double-team maneuvers during the match, catching the champions offguard. Eventually, however, it was the team who had teamed longer that pulled out the win in the end, as Damnation hit their finishing move (Judgment Day) on Star for the victory. Star complained of a quick three count, but the referee insisted that his decision was final. Creed decided that the referee should die, and prepared to assist him with it, but Star talked him out of it, saying that Creed didn't need to get in hot water with the new owner. Fortunately for the referee, Creed agreed... Result: Damnation by pinfall Strange Visitor, Part Three The cameras took us backstage for another visit to the little boys room. 'The Eradicator' Erik Kelly was once again preparing for the match he had - last night, he lost the fWo International Title back to 'Mr. Main Event' Mitch Wilson, the same person he had defeated the belt originally fifteen days ago. Tonight, he was scheduled to be part of the big battle royal, with the winner recieving a shot at the fWo Internet Title. If you've been following along, a strange visitor talks to Kelly, but nobody (not even Kelly) knows who this person is. You see, they hide behind the bathroom stall, and talk to Kelly, refusing to reveal their true identity. Last night, they told Kelly to dump Pamela Dee... and Kelly didn't listen. Ironically, Dee in a way was responsible for the way Kelly lost the title back to Wilson. Had she not been at ringside, 'Kodiak' Vic Creed likely would've never made his way down to ringside, which in the process distracted Kelly, and 'caused him to lose. Kelly had realized this last night, but didn't know what to do about it. The guy was right, but he didn't know how to break it to Dee. But tonight, perhaps the visitor, if he were around, that is, would tell Kelly what to do. Kelly splashed water on his face, before looking hard into the mirror before him. In the process, he saw in the mirror what was behind him. Seven open doors to stalls, one closed. Thinking that it could be the visitor, he asked with some curiosity. "Are you... is... is that you?" His deep voice growled. He got a response. "Yes, Erik, it's me. I hate to say I told you so, but I told you so. She cost you the title. You realize, that, don't you?" Kelly nodded with reluctance. In a way he was wrong... but in another way, he was completely right. "Yes... I know..." The unknown visitor chuckled. "And now I bet you're wondering how to keep her away from the ring tonight. I heard Universal' announcement. A shot at the fWo Internet Title. Not quite the World, but it's a step in the right direction." Kelly again nodded. "Yes, I've been asking myself how Pam will understand me not wanting her at ringside. I don't want to lie to her, but I don't know how to do it without hurting her in the process, either." The sly visitor already had a plan. "Tell her that it's an over the top rope battle royal. Bodies will be flying everywhere - BECAUSE OF YOU." His voice got noticeably louder at the end of that sentence, before he quieted down again. "With her at ringside, she could get injured. You're only concerned about her safety." Kelly nodded again, slowly. "That will work." The man had another suggestion. "After you win tonight - and believe me, you WILL win, take her out to dinner. There's plenty of nice restaraunts in South Beach, and you guys can party the night away. You know, celebrate." Suddenly, the bathroom stall door opened, and Kelly turned to see if he could see who it was. "Here. On me." Kelly walked over and saw a fist full of cash sticking out. There had to be at least ten thousand dollars in that wad. Kelly grabbed it reluctantly, and thanked the man. "Thanks. I will find a way to repay you." The man refused. "You can repay me by winning that match." The door shut, and nobody - even Kelly - saw who it was. Kelly exited the room, and cameras followed him out. Dee was there waiting for him. "You ready, Erik?" Kelly shook his head yes. "Yes, but I want you to stay back here tonight - it's a battle royal, and I don't want you getting hurt. A lot of bodies are going to be flying from that ring tonight. A lot of them." Dee looked suspicious, but then nodded. "You're right. I'll watch your match from back here. Good luck!" Kelly and Dee hugged. "Thanks. I've got to meet with Universal real quick... need to ask him something." Kelly and Dee said goodbye, as Kelly left the dressing room area... Crusader vs. Plague Countdown To Oblivion 1 returned to the ring, to find it enclosed inside the confines of a steel cage. "Paranoid" by Black Sabbath brought out the Dark Riders trio, comprised of Stone, War & Plague. The arena dimmed, and they walked down the ramp almost as if they were on a mission. Plague entered the cage, while Stone & War remained outside the ring. The lights came back on, and "Switchback" by Biohazard echoed throughout the arena. The popular Crusader came out and charged down to the ring. Noticeably absent from his side was Clarisse, who was not present tonight due to visiting some friends on the West Coast. Crusader glared at Stone, before entering the cage. He and Plague went at it at once, and the referee signalled for the bell. The match went back and forth, and several times either man could have won. However, in the end, it was Crusader doing the impossible. Doing the unthinkable. Doing the undo-able. Plague kicked out of a Flipside and a Gravity 101. Crusader was confused. One of those two moves usually meant the end of the match. But not this time. Instead, Crusader found himself on a new level. A G101 from the top of the cage. Hitting it in Superfly Jimmy Snuka like fashion, Crusader covered Plague for the victory. Finally, the feud was over. Crusader raised his arms as he stood on the second rope in a corner, still sore from what he had just endured, while the cage began to rise to the ceiling. War & Plague grabbed Stone from the ring, and walked backstage. Stone didn't look too pleased. They bumped into Lance Star who was walking out, and a hurt Plague still found strength to call Lance a "biTcH". Lance ignored it as he entered the ring with a microphone, and began to get Crusader' attention. Result: Crusader by pinfall Bragging Rights "Well, well, well... if it isn't the man who thinks he defies gravity. "You think you're so special. You come into the fWo, and think you're a big deal. For nearly two months, you WERE a big deal - but it was ME that ended it. MUD' face turned to an expression of sheer joy and happyness. "I've... w-w-what? Toad, guess what..." MUD turned to look for Toad, but Toad wasn't there. "The Eradicator" Erik Kelly, "Kodiak" Vic Creed, "The Future" Lance Star, Jackhammer, Hellrazor, Fear, "Black Plague" Ruben Ross, "The Ego" Greg Davis, Sphere, Vengence, Black Quicksilver, Mike Heftel, Red Dragon, Juno Law, Crusader, War, Scot Hartt, The A Countdown To Oblivion 1 returned to the ring, where fans inside the America Airlines Arena waited for the next match. Three minutes passed before Vengence eliminated Sphere, and then himself in the process. Vengence then chokeslammed Sphere over the guardrail into a sea of fans, and left the area quickly with a look of pleasure on his face. Medics rushed down to ringside to help Sphere, and this distracted Dragon, Heftel & Black Quicksilver, who were still in shock and concern about their friend STUMP. This allowed Vic Creed to sneak up from behind and dump all three members of the STUMP Squad out. At the same time on the other side, Erik Kelly eliminated Greg Davis & War, before tossing out Jackhammer as well. Creed turned around and flexed his muscles to brag, but then he saw Kelly. Kelly & Creed immediately began going at it tooth and nail. Lance Star eliminated Hellrazor, before locking up with Crusader. Ross joined them, while Juno Law went after The Abusah & Scot Hartt. Finally, everyone saw that the obvious favorites were going at it, and decided to get the big men out. In the process, Hartt & Abusah dumped out Juno Law. Pissed, Law plotted revenge as he walked backstage. Creed finally was eliminated, but Kelly held on, and then eliminated Hartt & Abusah. Finally, it was down to Erik Kelly, Ruben Ross, Lance Star & Crusader. Kelly saw Ross, Star & Crusader exchanging punches in a corner, and eliminated Crusader, while Ross got rid of Star. Kelly had some difficulty with Ross, but Star helped eliminate the fWo Survivor winner, and Kelly was deemed the winner by the referees. Kelly walked backstage, and knew that the person he had been talking to knew something... perhaps too much. Countdown To Oblivion 1 went backstage for perhaps the final time of the evening... Result: Erik Kelly I? The story of The Boston Strangler was one that was confusing. For two years, everyone knew the man. And now, we all felt as if we hardly knew him. But that was okay. He didn't know even know himself sometimes. Especially as of late. All he knew was pain. Giving it and receiving it. He didn't like receiving it but giving it was special, almost like the Holidays. And as the Holidays came to a close, as the year came to a close, he felt like he was not being given the opportunity to give pain as much as he used to be. And that's when he decided it would change. Chris Universal was going over some paperwork when he heard a knock on the door. Since it wasn't an unusual occurance, he got up and answered it. Oddly enough it was the same man he was competing against just three weeks on December 10th; in a three-way dance with the Flying Frenchie. It was The Boston Strangler. The Strangler had a duffle bag slumped over his shoulder and his eyes looked as if he hadn't slept for several days. He struggled in and looked at Universal, but he couldn't really speak. Universal asked him what he wanted, but the response he got wasn't... well, assuring. Strangler looked around in a daze... and then just spoke. "A... match." That wasn't assuring either, and Universal wanted to know with who. Strangler motioned that it didn't matter who it was with... he just wanted a match. Universal nodded as if he was fine with that. "Sure... I'll book you for next Sunday. We'll be in Tokyo. Your tickets for the flight will arrive by Tuesday. Be there." Strangler nodded, and turned to leave. He looked back at Universal and acted as if he had something to say. "I... never... mind." Strangler turned and walked away, and Universal wondered if his ears were deceiving him. Universal mumbled something that was barely audible. "I... what the hell?" On that note, we were quickly taken to the ring for the main event. A four-way ladder match for the fWo World Title... Primetime vs. Wretch vs. 'Mr. Main Event' Mitch Wilson vs. Mike Bear ... which in the process would unify the fWo International Title with it; since the titles meant essentially the same thing. Cameras quickly gave us a special look at the Countdown clock with the special stage underneath it. Twenty two minutes remained until the start of 2001 and the end of 2000. The fans inside the American Airlines Arena prepared themselves for what was a first - a four-way ladder match. The only way to win was to grab both the fWo World & International Titles, which were hanging above the ring right now. Four men would enter, one would walk out. Champion. The first man out was greeted by boos from the crowd. The final fWo International Champion will go down as "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson. Wilson came down to ringside and entered the ring, and then stood underneath the two titles, staring at them, as they hung directly overhead. The next participant was welcomed with cheers. Wretch, the same man who was screwed out of the fWo World Title last night and essentially quit the fWo because of that reason, was back. Signed to a new deal by Chris Universal, he walked onto the stage and headed towards the ring. Wilson took his eyes off the prizes to look at Wretch, and their eyes didn't leave each other for a good thirty seconds. Wretch finally joined Wilson in the ring, as several referees came down to keep the two men from wrestling before the other two joined them. The third participant walked out onto the stage as "Snap" by Slipknot pulsated throughout the arena. It was Primetime. Right now, there wasn't an active wrestler on the planet more hated than he. He was nearly responsible for the exodus of four superstars in a matter of two weeks. While the Flying Frenchie was no longer with us, and Ric Chronos was going to be out for some time due to a re-aggravation of the same injury Primetime inflicted him with this past Summer, Mike Bear & Wretch nearly quit because of the actions of this man. Then you have him injuring people left and right... ending the career of the Flying Frenchie... and being just a dick in general, and that was the perfect ingredients for one of the most hated men on the planet. The fourth and final participant was the man who was screwed out of the fWo World Title Friday night at Countdown 3. Mike Bear. An ominous whistling sound was heard, before the arena dimmed. "Engel" by Rammstein boomed throughout the American Airlines Arena. Bear walked out onto the stage dressed with an Underground t-shirt and blue jeans, while wearing Wolverine boots. Brittany was at his side, but she would not stay. This match was about four men, and four men only. Brittany wished Bear good luck, and the two said goodbye. The Philadelphian then looked towards ringside, and saw Primetime. The referee's couldn't contain Wretch & Mitch, who unloaded on each other with punches, chops and forearms. On the outside, Bear targeted Primetime. He had a bullseye painted on Primetime' head, and prepared to pull the trigger. The referee signalled for the bell. It was on. Wretch grabbed Wilson and took him to the outside, throwing him between the top and middle ropes. Bear took Primetime and sent him flying hard into a set of steel steps. A few ladders were set up against the guardrails, so the wrestlers could begin using them when they wished. Bear grabbed one and set it on the edge of a ring apron, before turning and seeing Primetime. Primetime got up to his feet and Bear charged him and took him down with a flying forearm, which sent Primetime reeling up against a guardrail. Meanwhile, on the other side, Wilson sent Wretch into the crowd, and they began going at it over there. Bear then clothesline Primetime up and over the guardrail into the crowd, while Wretch slammed Mitch Wilson head first into a row of seats. Bear continued to get the upper hand on Primetime and whipped him into the guardrail, before charging at him. This time, Primetime ducked and backdropped Bear over the guardrail, back onto the side where the fans were not seating. Primetime then climbed over and waited for Bear to get up. PT charged, but Bear took him down with a drop-toe-hold, right into the steel steps once again. Bear then grabbed Primetime and slung him back into the crowd, thinking that would keep him at bay. Bear caught his breath for a moment, raising his arms and saluting the crowd, who erupted into a chant of "welcome back!" Meanwhile, Mitch Wilson returned back into the ring after disposing of Wretch with a reverse Russian Legsweep near one of the announcers tables. Bear turned back towards the ring, and before he knew it, he had a face full of steel, thanks to a baseball slide via Wilson. The ladder didn't slide off the apron however, instead it just knocked Bear silly. Bear stumbled backwards, and soon found himself back in the crowd, reluctantly. With Wretch, Primetime & Bear all out of it on the floor, Wilson found himself in perfect position to become the champion. Wilson grabbed the ladder, and set it up in the ring. He then boasted to the crowd that it was over, that he was going to claim the titles right now. Wilson began climbing rung by rung up the ladder, but one thing he probably didn't count on was someone stopping him. Primetime re-entered the ring and began climbing up the other side of the ladder. Wretch also got up, and saw the action going on in the ring. He began looking underneath the ring for something, but he wasn't even sure exactly what. Soon, he found a table, and slid it into the ring. Wretch sat the table up to the side of the ladder Wilson & Primetime were climbing up, before Wretch climbed up ontop of the ladder. He didn't measure up quite to Wilson & Primetime, but still had a plan. Wretch began pushing the ladder, and he tipped it over. It landed slumped up against the ropes, while Primetime and Wilson were in unenviable positions. Both men happened to land crotch first on the top rope. Bear finally returned, and came in. He measured Primetime and caught him with a lightning quick superkick, before doing the same to Wilson. Primetime & Wilson both fell outside the ring, where they remained for a few moments. As Wretch stood on the table in the ring, he looked up at the titles, and reached, but was a few feet short from making it. He didn't realize this, but he would soon have company up there. Bear joined him, and they exchanged forearms, before Bear scooped Wretch up onto his back and appeared to be going for the Whiplash. However, Wretch escaped quickly and fell behind him, then hooked him for the reverse DDT. The Depression. Both former tag team partners went crashing through the table. The fans sat in awe of what had just occured, but the unbelievable hadn't been seen yet. Wretch decided that he wasn't through with Bear. Perhaps at this very moment, Wretch realized that this was the closest he had been to the fWo World Title since losing it two months ago, just a week after winning it. He knew all the stops had to be pulled. He grabbed the ladder that laid slumped against the ring ropes, and positioned it in a corner. Then, he moved the pieces of wood next to Bear out from the ring. Wretch then picked Bear up, and scoop slammed him in the ring. What followed next was expected. Meanwhile, on the outside, Mitch Wilson & Primetime resumed their rivalary. Wilson slammed Primetime to the floor, before retrieving a steel chair from near the technicians and timekeepers and such that sat at ringside. Primetime groggily stood, and came face to face with a steel chair. Back in the ring, Wretch took his bandana off and threw it into the crowd. He waved his arms back and forth, and did the running routine. Last night he was interrupted. Tonight, he wasn't. The Ratings Saver Legdrop. Bear was now more than dazed and confused, but Wretch wasn't done. He had to take a high risk. He looked around. The top rope wasn't good enough. He was going to the top... to the very top. He began climbing up the ladder he positioned in a corner, and soon found himself ten feet in the air, staring into an arena of fans who were at a fever pitch. Cheering wildly as Wilson & Primetime went at it, they also acknowledged that Wretch was about to put his body, his livelyhood all on the line. Wretch paused and looked at the Countdown clock, which now read eight minutes and thirty seven seconds until 2001. While Wretch was pre-occupied, he didn't realize that he had a visitor behind him. It was Wilson, who had just finished off Primetime with another shot. Wilson quickly joined Wretch on the top rope, and grabbed him with a waistlock. Wretch tried to fight it off with elbows, but Wilson kept his grasp. He stood on the rung below the top, which is wear Wretch stood. Wilson quickly positioned him, hooked him, and executed an Inverted Russian Legsweep from the top of the ladder. The Headliner. A boom echoed throughout the arena, as the weight of the two men hit the ring with an unforseen impact. Jeremiah Frankton exclaimed that they were dead, as it was an essential freefall to the center of the ring. Fortunately for Mike Bear, he was not in the way, in fact he was now standing on his feet. Bear' mouth fell open, his jaw nearly touching the mat. Like the fans, he could not believe what he just saw. Nobody could. But it happened. Bear learned something from the past three days. Anything could happen in the fWo. As Wretch & Wilson rolled slowly on the mat, their bodies in shock from the collision with the squared circle, Bear contemplated his next move. But this gave the chance for Primetime to sneak up from behind. Primetime spun Bear around, kicked him in the stomach, and hooked him for a vertical suplex. Instead, he slingshotted him off the top rope into a Michinoku Driver. The Snapshot. Primetime reached his feet after hitting the move, realizing there were no pinfalls in this match. He raised his arms and looked out at the fans, before looking down and spitting on Bear. Primetime was doing anything to make the fans boo him. He thrived on the boos. Booing him was like cheering him - it made him work harder, and be every bit as evil he was capable of. Primetime looked at Bear, and with a snarl, spit again. He then moved Bear to the floor, then realized that Wretch & Wilson had exited the ring to catch their barrings. This gave Primetime the perfect opportunity to win the match. He brought the ladder out to the center of the ring, and began climbing. Slowly at first, but then he picked up the pace. But Bear was not out yet. He grabbed a ladder and slid it into the ring. He then set it up next to Primetime's ladder, and began climbing. At the same time, Wilson & Wretch began to recover as well, and soon thereafter, found themselves climbing up as well. Primetime & Bear duked it out, while Wretch & Mitch did up in the air. Fans looked at the clock again. Four minutes remained until 2001. At this moment, after what they had seen, there was no question they would never forget this New Year's Eve. But they still hadn't seen everything. Bear reached over towards PT, and grabbed him, before positioning him so he wasn't looking towards the titles anymore. With his arms hooked around Primetime' head, Bear pushed himself off his ladder, and hooked him for part of his finisher - the Ace Crusher. The Whiplash. Both men hit the mat with great force, and since Wretch was on the same ladder as Bear, he quickly had to position himself so he was on Mitch' ladder. He reached up and grabbed Mitch by the head from behind, looked to the mat, and fell quickly. The Depression, again. All four men were down and out in the ring. Each man's finisher had been hit. We'd seen the Depression (twice), we'd seen the Headliner, we'd seen part of the Whiplash, and we'd seen the Snapshot. You could sense that each man had very little gas left in their tank. Moments passed and two ladders remained standing, while four men were down and out. Fans cheered in attempt to get someone to their feet. The first man up was Mike Bear, but so was Mitch Wilson. Bear walked towards Wilson, and Wilson grabbed him by the hair and sent him flying over the top rope onto the cement floor. Wilson then turned and saw Wretch charging at him, and he quickly backdropped him over. He then turned and yelled some profanities at them, telling them to get the fuck out of "his ring". Wilson then turned... and was taken out of the ring by Primetime with a clothesline. Both men went tumbling up and over the top rope to the floor. The former leader of the Future Foundation got up about twenty seconds later, and saw Wretch getting up as well. He grabbed him and sent him flying over the guardrail into the fans. Primetime turned and saw a dazed Bear, who was standing. Primetime charged, but Bear bent over for a backdrop. Primetime stopped, grabbed him, and hooked him for what appeared to be a piledriver. But it wasn't a piledriver. It was a Primetime Bomb. With Bear out of it on the cement floor, Primetime felt that the match was almost his. The clock now read that we were forty five seconds until the start of what some felt was the true millennium. Primetime grabbed the same chair that Wilson nailed him with earlier, and as Wilson got up, Primetime returned the favor. A chair shot to the back, and then a whack to the leg area. Wilson fell to the floor, and likely wouldn't get up for the rest of the match. Primetime tossed the chair to the side, and rolled into the ring. The fans booed, as Primetime walked around the ring, bragging that the match was his. Thirty seconds left until 12am Eastern Time. Primetime took one of the ladders, and folded it up, before placing it on the mat, and pushing it to the floor. He then positioned the other ladder underneath the titles perfectly. With excitement, he began climbing the ladder. Fifteen seconds remained. Suddenly, the lights dimmed, although you could still see inside the arena. Strobe lights flickered near the special stage that had been constructed. Primetime wondered what the hell was going on, and unknowingly let it distract him from reaching the titles that were well within grasp. Primetime looked around the arena as fans counted down. Ten. Nine. Eight. Seven. Six. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. 2001 had finally arrived, and it arrived with a bang - literally. Suddenly, the special clock that had been built began to self-destruct in a show of amazing pyrotechnics. Fireworks shot off all over the place as the lights were offically off now. The excitement inside the arena was at an all time high. Fans cheered as the New Year had begun - and for some, the official beginning of a new millennium. And what better way to kick that off with the return of a man who first entered the fWo claiming to be the 'Man Of The Millennium'? The same man that Primetime injured this past June 24th at Critical Condition. The same man that had entered the fWo at the end of Fallout 2000. With the clock officially in millions of pieces, a figure emerged wearing a black trenchcoat, black sunglasses, black jeans... all black. The return of Havok. The lights came back on, and Primetime sat in shock in the ring. He didn't know, but Mike Bear had recovered, and was in the ring. Primetime looked down and saw Bear, who charged and pushed the ladder. The ladder tipped, and Primetime fell to outside the ring. He hit the floor hard. A sickening thud echoed throughout the arena. The fans gasped as Primetime instantly lost consciousness. Bear re-positioned the ladder to his liking, and began climbing. Wretch & Mitch Wilson were slowly coming back into the ring, but it was too late. With great jubilation, Bear reached with both hands, and grabbed both titles. The referees signalled for the bell. Mike Bear had done it. A five month journey that began back in July when he won the Next Level tournament was finally over. Mike Bear was champion. And not just any champion, he was the reigning fWo World Champion. Undisputed. He held the fWo World & International Titles to his chest, as sweat poured down his face. Exhausted, Bear quickly climbed back down the ladder, and rested in a corner, while Brittany ran down to ringside, and joined her man in the ring. The Countdown To Oblivion was finally over. We saw the exits of Bear & Wretch, and the returns of Bear & Wretch. We saw the debut of Chris Titan. We learned that MUD was finally signed to an fWo contract. We saw the return of Chris Finn. We saw the ownership of the fWo change hands, and not because of a match - Chris Finn sold the fWo to Chris Universal. We unfortunately also saw Ric Chronos suffer another injury, and we also saw the return of fWo superstar Havok, who had finally recovered from the injuries Primetime inflicted on him back in June. And right now, we saw two people who loved each other dearly in the ring, enjoying the success one of them had achieved. We also saw fireworks shooting throughout the arena, and confetti & balloons slowly falling from the ceiling. And we also saw something else - text on the CountdownTron flashed over and over again, as the fWo wished it's fans... Happy New Year. Result: Mike Bear becomes the undisputed fWo World Champion
