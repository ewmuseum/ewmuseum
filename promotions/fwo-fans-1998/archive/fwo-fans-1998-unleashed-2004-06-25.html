Introduction Previously... In and Out and All Around Gary Schroeder lifted his head briefly from his desk, he stared at his office door and then looked slowly to the watch that hung to his wrist. Looking blankly at the watch Schroeder attempted to make out which 'hand' was which and what the time was, struggling with focus Schroeder was startled when the door to his office pushed open and as the person entered Gary jumped slightly in his seat. He heard the voice of a woman, "I've made men do things on first impression's sir but certainly not jumping in fear." Schroeder smiled, still looking at his watch, and as he saw the lady approach his desk he spoke, "Well I was just..." "Dozing off?" The woman replied quickly. Gary looked to her now, his eyes at first met hers, he could tell they were wild, some form of fire waiting to burst from the retinas and claim his body to their deep dark pit of hell. Without even realizing it his eyes slowly shimmied down her body, as he looked closely at her chest and then zoomed in on her legs. "Am I up to par Mr. Schroeder?" The woman said in a sarcastic tone, "I sure hope you don't size up your wrestlers the same way, soon enough you would be a dead man." Schroeder's eyes jumped back to hers and a smile then formed on his face, he was still in a haze and quite tired, "I'm sorry I didn't catch your name, you are..?" "Sundae," She responded quickly again, "Sara Sundae." "Sundae?" Schroeder stated in a confused manner. "Yes, like the ice cream. Look Mr. Schroeder I hate foreplay and I'm not about to tip toe around why I'm here, because you and I have just become new friends." "We have?" Schroeder stated even more confused. "Yes Mr. Schroeder, you and I will be working colleagues, I'll help you out and you help me out." Sara stated in a set tone as she sat down and opened her brief case. "Look lady," Schroeder laughed, "If this is some kind of way to get into KVC's locker room you'll have to try another method." Sara laughed as well and then grew suddenly stern, "Sit down Mr. Schroeder." And as Schroeder took his seat in front of her she began to unpack random objects from her briefcase, "I have been sent by your boss Mr. Murphy, he's a good friend of mine and wanted moe to come on the road with you and the group. To observe, be a silent watcher so to speak. I won't get in your way, I'll make some suggestions and you can choose to accept my constructive criticism or not..." "Now you just wait a second," Schroeder started to speak up. I'll make my rounds at first, getting to know some of the gentlemen on staff, perhaps hear what they have to say." Sara stopped again, "Although I'm sure they will have wonderful things to say about this establishment. Now Mr. Schroeder, Gary, you don't mind if I call you Gary do you? Of course you don't Gary. Sara shot him a wink that should have been accompanied with some kind of small flash to demonstrate the actually intensity of it. However the wink was a warm one and Schroeder smiled at the sight of it, "Sure," he mumbled and she left his office quicker than she came in. Outside Sara rested her back against the wall and breathed out a heavy breath, "Whew," she said softly, relieved that she was able to get in and to get out. If only they knew how scared she was, how she couldn't disappoint Mr. Murphy. Matters were not important now, Waylon called, she has to go see him. The fans greeted her with boos as she walked down the ramp and climbed into the ring. Death was up, he was almost on top of Brand, and then he leapt, grabbed Brand's head, double-underhooked, spun, and drove down. Death Trip. The crowd gasped, and Death lay by Brand Frontier, knowing that if this were a regular match, he would be a cover away from victory. It wasn't, and he wasn't, so he sat up and turned to look at Brand's head. No blood. The crowd cheered once again at the sight, and Death cursed violently and stood up, then slid out of the ring under the bottom rope. He shoved Matt Panzer aside and grabbed the chair that he sat on, snapping it flat as he raised it. Death rolled back into the ring, where Brand Frontier was slowly pulling himself to his feet. Brand got up to a knee... to one foot... staggered... and then he was up. Death raised the chair as far above and behind himself as he could. Brand Frontier turned... Death threw the chair forward. Brand Frontier dropped down onto his back, throwing himself out of the chair's path. Instead, the steel slammed into the head of the official, who had been unfortunately positioned behind Brand. The ref fell to the mat as if he had been shot, and ironically enough, became the first participant in the match to bleed. Death had moved forward as he threw the chairshot, and Brand was in position to grab his legs from behind. He did, sending Death crashing down to the mat. Brand stood up, then bent over and hooked Death by the upper legs, lifting him up. He then turned... and slammed Death down. Face-first. Into the top turnbuckle. The crowd erupted at the modified version of the feared Divine Right, and Brand fell backwards after completing the move, then brought himself back up to his feet. As he did, Death slowly rolled over from his stomach to his back. A line of bright red blood flowed from his forehead down his face. The crowd erupted at the sight - Brand Frontier had busted Death open, and was the winner of the match - or would have been if the official had been conscious to see it. Brand quickly turned to the official and attempted to shake him into wakefulness, but the referee was long gone. Brand continued his vain attempts, and suddenly he heard it - the cheers of the crowd had become violent boos. Brand turned and launched his foot upward. It connected with a solid CLANG into a steel chair, which was then driven back into the face of Primetime. Primetime fell, but right behind him was Donaven Winters, and Winters drove his forearm into Brand's face, knocking him down over the official. Winters immediately began to lay the boots to Brand, then helped Primetime up to his feet. In the corner, Death sat up, shook his head, and wiped the blood out of his eyes. He saw Winters and Primetime there and grinned (evilly, of course). He stood up and gestured to Winters and Primetime, who nodded and brought Brand up to his feet. Winters and Primetime lifted Brand up, holding him in a double flapjack position, and Death quickly positioned a chair. He then headed into the corner and leapt up onto the second turnbuckle, then stepped up onto the top. Winters and Primetime still held Brand in the air, with his head facing in Death's direction. Death leapt. Winters and Primetime drove backwards and down. On his way down, Death grabbed Brand's head and spiked it into the chair with a DDT. The crowd booed wildly, and Death rolled Brand over eagerly. Sure enough, blood flowed freely down Frontier's face. Death wiped his face as best he could, then spoke to Primetime, and Winters rolled from the ring. Primetime reached into his tights and emerged with a small package (more unfortunately phrased words were never written). He leaned down and broke the package open underneath the nose of the referee. The ref stirred, blinking, and as Primetime rolled from the ring, the ref stood up slowly, hanging onto the ropes for balance. He saw the scene before him. Brand Frontier was down and bloody. Death stood over him, apparently uninjured... but before the official could call for the bell, he saw the blood stained on Death's arm, and Death's wound betrayed him, as blood began to flow down his face once again. The official paused only a moment, then called for the bell. It was duly retrieved and rung, and as Death and the other two Dark Riders watched impatiently, he conferred with Matt Panzer. Finally, Panzer nodded and raised his microphone. "Ladies and gentlemen... The referee has informed me... That as both Brand Frontier and Death were bleeding as he saw them, this match will be declared a DRAW!" The crowd began to boo loudly at the decision, and inside the ring, Death's eyes went wide in disbelief. He had succeeded in one way - preventing a loss - but had been unable to fool the ref into awarding him the win. At ringside, Chris Finn noted unhappily to the viewing audience at home that there was no instant replay in professional wrestling, and James Armstrong echoed those sentiments gleefully. Inside the ring, Death's face went from disbelief to anger, and as the official turned, Death ran towards him, leapt, then drove him to the mat with the Death Trip. He turned towards Winters and Primetime, noted that Frontier was still down, then, with a muttered obscenity, decided against doing any more damage to the fallen paragon... for now. The Dark Riders departed to the back in a huff. As Brand Frontier began to stir in the ring, Finn and Armstrong began to speculate as to what this meant for the main event at Meltdown, with Finn holding out for an official decision while Armstrong campaigned openly for Death to receive the shot. Unleashed, meanwhile, went off the air, chaos filling the air as Black Quicksilver did not know who he would face on Pay Per View next month... Result: Draw
