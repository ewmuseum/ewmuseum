Wednesday Warfare, 12 May 1999

12 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction It was a dark and stormy night. The fWo came from New Orleans, Louisiana for Wednesday Warfare amist rain, thunder, and lightning. Sensing that something bad was going to go down, the fWo stars filed slowly into the arena, cautious and nervous... Aimee Horihito vs. Apocolyptica New fWo star Aimee Horihito came to the ring amist a loud rush of cheers from the audence. It was announced that Aimee would be facing Stone for the fWo world title on Friday, and as Horihito warmed up in the ring, Stone arrived on the second balcony to watch the match with interest. Apocolyptica came to the ring slowly and quietly, and stepped into the ring facing Aimee. After the bell rang Aimee bowed to show respect, and Apocolyptica kicked her in the midsection and delivered a hard DDT. With Aimee stunned, Apocolyptica quickly covered her for the pinfall victory, much to the stunned crowd's surprise. High in the rafters, Stone laughed with amusement, as Apocolyptica quietly rolled out of the ring and headed for the backstage area. Result: Apocolyptica by Pinfall Trouble vs. Lone Gunmen New fWo Tag Team Trouble came to the ring, spraying beer at the fans and tossing around a female blow-up doll. Trouble proceeded to introduce the crowd to their blow-up doll, named "War". Before their ring "play" could border into the sick and surreal, the music of the Lone Gunmen played, and Machina and Rudo came out onto the entrance ramp. Machina then proceeded to introduce the crowd to "Mr. Ass" Sid Snow. Before Machina could explain in what way Sid's ass was "bad" they were attacked from behind from the Chameleon and Total Eclipse, who loaded the two into a large garbage dumpster and rolled it off the entrance ramp. Over the crash the Chameleon and Total Eclipse laughed at the two, until Machina stood up inside the dumpster, and laughed, explaining that all the garbage and Rudo cushioned his fall. Responding to this, the Total Eclipse jumped off the entrance ramp, crashing down on the two below. Somewhere during the ensuing chaos, Twitch and Thumper wandered over to where Rudo was laying unconcious and pinned him for the victory. Suddenly, War and Plague arrived on the scene, and battered Twitch and Thumper with crowbars. As the Dark Riders stood over the chaos, the lights dimmed into s silver hue, and silver roses began to fall from the rafters. War seemed confused, but Plague grew somber and looked up with a mix of horror and realization... Result: Trouble by Pinfall Wild Thing vs. Cairn Wild Thing came to the ring surrounded by wolves, and howled up at the rafters. Markus Cairn arrived to little fanfare, but looked determined in the upcoming match. However, he was quickly pounced upon when he stepped into the ring, as Wild Thing battered him with quick chops and scratches on the chest. Markus Cairn flung the Wild Thing backward, up and over the top rope, and through a table on the outside. Cairn followed him to the outside, but were attacked by the wolves that had come to the ring with Wild Thing. This gave Wild Thing a chance to get back into the ring, and he climbed to the top rope. He executed a flying plancha, bringing the hands across the face, drawing deep ribbons of blood. Wild Thing then brought Cairn back into the ring, locking him in a figure four, and digging the metal claw into the calf of Cairn. Unable to grab the ropes, Cairn was forced to submit, and walk away from the fWo. As Wild Thing left the ringside area, Wolf Fang arrived from nowhere, and delivered a hard chair shot to the back of Wild Thing's head... Result: Wild Thing by Submission Black Quicksilver (c) vs. Plague Plague walked to the ring alone, still shaken by the incedent earlier in the evening. As he walked he kicked silver roses out of his path and rolled into the ring quietly, without the usual manaical laughter. Black Quicksilver walked to the ring with Stump, which seemed to be wrapped in a plastic bag. Quicksilver scolded Stump as he walked, then rolled into the ring, where he was at once attacked by Plague. Plague seemed more focused than usual, and kept Quicksilver from getting to his feet with attacks to the midsection and head. Plague used quick submission-style holds, wrestling with a different and more deadly moveset for the usually insane wrestler. Plague then executed a perfect moonsault from the turnbuckle, and covered Quicksilver for the pin. Before the ref could count to three, Chris Kidman jumped into the ring and stomped on Plague, breaking the three count and earning him the DQ victory. Al Snow and Joy then hit the ring, and all four began brawling as Plague slunk from the ring. As officals seperated the four, Black Quicksilver made his way to the backstage area, carrying Stump and the Cruiserweight title with him. Before he could get backstage, a masked man attacked the Black Quicksilver, screaming that he "wanted what was his". As officals drug the masked man off BQ, fans recognized him as a larger than usual... Blue Blazer? Result: Plague by DQ Death (c) vs. Darkness Darkness came to the ring with the Mad Trucker, who used the Hardcore Title to smack young fans in the audence who reached out to slap hands with the wrestlers. Darkness stood silently in the ring, and awaited Death. The Dark Riders escorted Death to the ring, and he stepped inside the squared circle and at once locked up with Darkness. The two began trading hard chops to the chest, until Death attacked with a low blow to the groin. With Darkness on the mat, Death attempted the move formerly known as the Scotty Dropper. Darkness was able to counter it though... and struck upward with an uppercut. Darkness then executed a powerbomb on Death, and folded his arms over for the pinfall. At that moment the Dark Riders entered the ring, along with the Mad Trucker, and a brawl broke out inside the ring. As the cameras went off the air, officals attempted to seperate the wrestlers from the wild brawl in the ring... Result: No Contest
