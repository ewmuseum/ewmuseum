Introduction The long black limo pulled up in front of The Pyramid, and seconds later, an esteemed personage indeed stepped out. Chris Universal himself. The owner of the fWo straightened his suit, checked his tie, and strode off towards the arena. He was looking forward to what would hopefully be an uneventful night. The fWo had been very eventful as of late, and nothing appealed more to Universal at this moment than a night of sitting back in his office with a good cup of hot, black coffee and the Oscars on the television. He was rooting for "Gladiator", personally. Damn fine movie. Damn fine, indee "Hey, Universal!" Universal snapped out of his reverie. Standing in front of him were two men who probably were the exact opposite of happy. Mike Bear. Wretch. Bear had spoken, and now Wretch was speaking too. "We want to know, since I " Bear snorted, but Wretch continued. " was screwed out of the World Title last night, what exactly is going to happen to us as it regards to CyberSlam?" Universal thought it over a moment. He had outlined this recently, he knew, and he mentally filled in the blanks based on the results of Dead Or Alive. "Well," he said, "both of you will face each other. The winner of that match will face Mitch Wilson who will face a mystery opponent earlier on to make it fair in the CyberSlam main event." "So Mitch Wilson is the only person guaranteed a spot in the main?" asked Bear with a look of disgust on his face. "Exactly," said Universal. "Man... that blows. Guess you'll have to rely on me to pull the buyrates, huh?" asked Bear. Wretch shook his head. "He means rely on me, of course." "No... I meant me." "Right, you meant me." "Me!' "Me!" "ME!" "Your mom!" Universal sighed and left the two to their bickering. All he wanted was to get to his office, but if this was any sign, tonight was not going to be fun. STUMP Squad vs. NXS Beck hits the speakers, and the group known as NXS... Steel, Cairn, and Rottdogg, make their way down to ringside. Mini-Rottdogg accompanies the group as well. They don't quite get the reaction they were looking for however...well, they don't get ANY reaction. They look around, and then climb into the ring as Twisted Sister hits the speakers, bringing out the STUMP squad. The fans erupt for them, as Black Quicksilver, Mike Heftel, and Jackhammer, along with Stump, make their way down towards ringside. They climb into the ring, save for Heftel, who gets stuck in the ropes and bounces back towards the floor. NXS sees this and immediately charges the preoccupied members of the SS. However, BQ sees this out of the corner of his mask, and he lowers his body, lifting Judge Steel over the ropes and on to the ground next to Heftel. Jackhammer disposes of the other two himself, and then lifts up BQ. As Steel is getting up, Jackhammer tosses BQ at Steel. BQ nails him with a beautiful splash, and the crowd goes nuts. BQ helps Heftel up, and rolls him into the ring. Then, he takes Rottdogg, and tosses him into the ring. Heftel looks at Rottdogg and looks a bit nervous. This is the kind of guy who could go nuts, Heftel thinks to himself. However, he decides to go for some offense, in the form of an elbow. He hits the elbow, and gets a little more confidence...enough to try a boot to the gut. He connects on this, and now he's brimming with confidence. As a result of this confidence, Heftel signals to the top. He rolls his hands over one another, as to signal a 450 splash. BQ sees this and shakes his head. However, in a show of support, BQ slaps Heftel on the shoulder while Heftel climbs the ropes. Heftel throws his arms up to the crowd once he hits the top rope. Then, as this isn't customary for him to be this high off the ground, he gets a bit dizzy. This dizziness causes him to pass out. Rottdogg sees this and laughs at Heftel. However, BQ quickly hops on the top rope and nails a missile dropkick on Rottdogg. Cairn and Steel yell about BQ not being the legal man; however, the ref overrules him, since BQ actually tagged in when he hit Heftel on the shoulder. BQ covers Rottdogg; however, at two and a half, both Cairn and Steel break up the count. Jackhammer comes in, and a two-on-two brawl begins. Cairn tries to nail BQ with a clothesline; however, BQ ducks, and nails him with the CZCutter. Meanwhile, Jackhammer lifts up Steel, and plants him with a powerbomb. BQ covers Cairn, but only gets two and a half before he kicks out. Cairn gets up, and is met by a kick to the gut from Jackhammer. Jackhammer lifts him up, and nails The Owie, which gets a massive reaction from the crowd. Then, BQ moves towards Heftel, and picks him up. He signals to the top for both men, and Heftel looks a bit confused. Then, BQ explains something to him, and Heftel's face lights up. He begins to climb up top, while Jackhammer positions Steel on his shoulders, facing Heftel. While Heftel gets ready, BQ climbs to the top as well. Heftel appears to be a bit wobbly; however, he closes his eyes and takes off. With his eyes still closed, he holds out his arm, and clotheslines Steel. Jackhammer uses this momentum to take Steel down with more force. As soon as Steel hits the canvas, BQ takes off with the Silver Splash. He nails it, and the crowd erupts. BQ covers Steel, and gets the three. Heftel, amazed that he flew off the top, wants to do it again. However, BQ and Jackhammer persuade him against it, and the trio begin to celebrate in the ring. As this is taking place, Sphere makes his way down to the ring with barbed wire on his arm and a chair in his hands. He slides in the ring and clobbers BQ, then Jackhammer. Heftel turns around, and meets the steel chair with his face. He falls to the ground in a heap. Sphere grabs the barbed wire and takes it off of his arm. He then proceeds to attempt to make Jackhammer eat the wire. Meanwhile, a sea of black and white striped shirts make their way down towards ringside, with intentions of stopping Sphere. As they slide into the ring and prevent Sphere from permanently altering Jackhammer's diet, Slaughter continues onward. After hitting him with a martial arts combo, Juno nails Havok with a roundhouse right. Havok is hurled backwards and nearly over the turnbuckle with the surprising force of Juno's right hand. Placing Havok on the turnbuckle, Juno attempts to follow his offensive assault with even more offense. Juno tries to DDT Havok off the top rope, but it doesn't quite work out that way. Havok pushes Juno and he lands with a loud thud on the hard mat below. Juno struggles to get to his feet-which is exactly where Havok wants him. A missile dropkick puts Juno back on the ground. Juno again gets to his feet where he's met with The Headache. Juno definitely got a headache after the move, but didn't stay down for the three count. After nailing Juno with an evenflow DDT, Havok makes his way up to the turnbuckle. He goes for the Millennium-Sault, but Juno rolls out of the way. Juno follows it up, by rolling Havok up. The ref gets halfway through to, before Havok kicks out. Both men get to their feet at the same time. They trade a couple of punches, before Havok shocks Juno by kicking him in the midsection. Havok then hits the Final Impact. All Kobi Law can do is watch in horror as his brother is pinned. While "Awake" plays Kobi rushes to his brother's aid, and Havok's arm is raised in victory.
