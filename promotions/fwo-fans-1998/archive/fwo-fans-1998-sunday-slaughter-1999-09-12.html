fWo — Card

Sunday Slaughter, 12 September 1999

12 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction It has been called the first honest sport, despite being a mixture of drama and athletic ability. Inside the squared circle, men compete in the oldest contest known to man... wrestling. It is within the confines of three ropes and four turnbuckles that legends are made... and memories bourn. Today, twelve men step up to the challenge in what is the greatest tournament of the year. One man alone will carry the title of Wrestler Of The Year, more men shall comfort themselves with the knowledge that defeat did not dispel the honor they showed inside the ring. Today, the man from a promotion called FFW named Oracle will step into the ring to attempt to prove himself an equally strong singles wrestler as he faces a man from the promotion called TCW by the name of Mike Bear. Bear is two days away from a defeat suffered at the hands of the now Hardcore champion Crucifix, and he is certainly looking to prove something. Today, the returned madman the Boston Strangler continues the slow road to rebuilding his legend as he faces a man that stunned the world and crushed the hearts of millions when he adopted the guise of Death. The Boston Strangler was the first man to defeat Scotty Cool to obtain the fWo Internet title, now over a year later both men want to show who the better man truly is. Today, the mighty mammoth known as War puts strength and respect on the line when he faces the fWo Internet champion Ice. Both big men have grown used to overpowering their opponents through brute strength and force... neither man knows what to expect in this contest of physical equals. In a battle of such ruthless opponents, the question is posed if either man can survive. Today, the most lethal woman in the fWo and Dark Rider member Stone is out to prove that a woman can become wrestler of the year... and the man in her path is the former fWo World Heavyweight champion Ultra Violet, whose simple ways have won him the hearts of millions of fans. In a classic battle of good against evil, only one can emerge victorious. Today, the man who is out to prove that he is the greatest wrestler in the world, Courage, will take on the former tag team champion VL. VL is a classic ring veteran, being an active member in the fWo since it's inception and capturing more titles than any other woman in the promotion. Courage has much to prove, and he hopes to start proving it today. Today, two deadly enemies face once more in a battle that must be confined by the ring. Rain and Crucifix will face one another in a battle that could end the career of one man, or perhaps send them both into early retirement. In this contest everything is at stake... and there can be only one winner. Today these twelve wrestlers will compete for the ultimate prize... and only six will move on to the next round. For the one year anniversary of Sunday Slaughter, this shall be a night fans will not soon forget. This is the Wrestler of the Year tournament. This is Sunday Slaughter. This is the fWo. Mike Bear vs. Oracle Oracle came to the ring to the strange blue light that reminded everyone of his earlier days in the promotion when he was a successful singles wrestler. Oracle nodded to the crowd, and rested against the ring ropes in anticipation of Mike Bear's arrival. Bear came onto the ramp, obviously injured with added taping and braces on his arms and legs, but made his way to the ring in a steady and determined manner. Inside the ring Oracle and Mike Bear nodded to one another, each giving the other a sign of respect for stepping into the ring for this contest. The two locked up, with Oracle taking the quick advantage and twisting Mike Bear into an armbar. Bear broke free using the ropes, and then spun and delivered hard kicks, which Oracle blocked. Oracle followed up with a punch to the jaw that sent Bear backward and earned a warning from the referee. Oracle attempted a side standing kick, but Mike Bear caught Oracle's leg and held it before applying a sukui-nage, which is a judo move much like a powerslam, and then attempted a juji-gatame. Oracle blocked this, and kicked into the face of Bear, opening up a large cut in his forehead that had been bandaged. Both men stood to break the ten count, and faced each other once more in the ring, both men breathing hard. Oracle grabbed Bear's arm and kicked upward, one kick hitting Bear's face and doing further damage to the exposed wound. Bear broke free of Oracle's grip, and fell down to the mat, rolling toward Oracle as he went. Bear grabbed Oracle and threw him to the mat with hip toss and attempted to put him in a triangle submission hold. Oracle saw it coming and reversed the submission attempted, moving over Bear and switching the hold into an Achilles tendon hold. Bear struggled to get to the ropes to break the hold and managed to do so, clinging to the ropes for support and attempting to stand on his damaged leg. Oracle on his feet tried to take Bear down, but Bear blocked by holding onto the ropes and kicked up at the midsection to knock the air from Oracle and buy him some time to recover. Oracle was hit with the first kick but shrugged it off, executing a belly-to-back suplex, then got behind Mike Bear to apply a modification submission hold that was a mixture of a half-nelson and sleeper hold. Bear again struggled to get to the ropes and was finally able to do so before losing consciousness. Oracle now turned to the knees of Bear, striking with quick, calculated kicks to the knee cap. Bear dodged one kick and executed a leg sweep. With Oracle dazed, Mike Bear grabbed at the head of Oracle and slammed it down to the mat from belly-to-belly position. With Oracle on the mat Mike Bear drove his knee into the head of Oracle and applied a mixture of a triangle hold and leg lock in the center of the ring. Oracle attempted to break free, but finally lost consciousness in the center of the ring, and the match was awarded to Mike Bear. Bear saluted the crowd, and limped to the back, obviously in great pain. Oracle was helped from the ring as well, anxious to have a rematch with Mike Bear at a later date. Result: Mike Bear by Referee's Decision Stone vs. Ultra Violet Stone came to the ring to the vast boos of the audience, and gave them all the finger as she went. Stepping inside the ring, she crotched down and motioned for Ultra Violet to come to the ring and take a beating. Ultra Violet's music played, and along with the lovely Violet he walked to the ring with a large, goofy grin on his face. Ultra Violet waved to the fans happily, but was stopped by Violet as they neared the ring. Violet whispered something in his ear and Ultra Violet seemed to not understand. Violet sighed and more loudly told Ultra Violet that he needed to win if they were going to get that diamond ring that the winner of the tournament received. Ultra Violet seemed not to understand why he would need a diamond ring, but nodded in agreement and mentioned that if they won a diamond ring he could probably give it to Sid Snow since he would enjoy that kind of thing. Violet seemed to not like this idea, but wished Ultra Violet luck as he climbed into the ring. At once Stone began to hit Ultra Violet with a series of palm blows to the head. Ultra Violet seemed unaffected by this as Stone walked around the ring mocking Ultra Violet and the crowd. Stone then grabbed Violet by the hair and began to shake her, which seemed to get an angry reaction from Ultra Violet. Running from behind, Ultra Violet took revenge with forearm smash to the back of the head that sent Stone up and over the top rope to the outside. Ultra Violet ran to the other end of the ring and came off the ropes with a perfectly executed tope to the outside and onto Stone. On the outside, Ultra Violet positioned a chair and jumped onto it, leaping at Stone with a missile drop kick. Ultra Violet rolled Stone back into the ring and executed a German suplex in the center of the ring, and followed it up with a reverse DDT. Ultra Violet covered for the three count, but was only able to get two. Climbing to the top turnbuckle, Ultra Violet attempted a diving headbutt but missed as Stone rolled smartly out of the way. Stone punched down on the prone Ultra Violet, and then propped him up in one of the corners. She promptly dropkicked his knee and then executed a Dragon screw and turned the aftermath into a knee cross hold. Stone continued to work on his leg and knee, hoping to eliminate the flying tactics of Ultra Violet. Stone picked UV up and powerbombed Ultra Violet to the outside and climbed up to the top turnbuckle to execute a rolling senton from the top turnbuckle to the floor and onto Ultra Violet. Back in the ring, Stone hit a brainbuster on Ultra Violet and went up to the top but like Ultra Violet before her missed the diving headbutt. On his feet now, Ultra Violet ran to the ropes but Stone welcomed him with another big palm-blow to the chest. Stone did a running Stonebomb, made a cover, but only got a two count. Stone placed Ultra Violet on the top rope, performed a brainbuster from there and went for a cover. Ultra Violet kicked out at two once more. Frustrated, Stone placed Ultra Violet on the top rope again, and attempted to perform a frankensteiner but Ultra Violet blocked it. Ultra Violet quickly executed a swinging DDT from the top turnbuckle sending Stone down to the mat. Ultra Violet signaled to the crowd, and hit a reverse brainbuster on Stone. Violet urged Ultra Violet to hit one more, and Ultra Violet scooped up Stone for a second reverse brainbuster. Ultra Violet covered Stone, and got the three count. Violet was ecstatic, jumping up and down and hugging Ultra Violet, who appeared surprised by this reaction. Result: Ultra Violet by Pinfall Boston Strangler vs. Death Death came to the ring, looking grim and yelled at the crowd to shut up as he entered. The crowd responded with jeers, and Death began punching random fans in the front row until they silenced in fear. Death asked where their hero the Strangler was, and mocked him inside the ring. The Strangler walked out onto the entrance ramp with a huge maniacal smile, and waved to the back for some ring attendants to bring out what appeared to be a giant crate. Still grinning, the Strangler had the fWo attendants remove the crate, revealing a giant candy statue of Death. The Strangler and the fans laughed, pointing up at Death who fumed inside of the ring and began to scream obscenities. The Strangler then took one of the crow bars that the ring attendants used to remove the crate packaging and swung at the statue, breaking it into pieces and sending shards of chocolate into the crowd. Death's face grew red with rage as the Strangler took his time walking to the ring, leaving the crow bar behind with the demolished candy statue. The Strangler entered the ring and Death attacked him at once with swinging punches that sent the Strangler back into the corner. Death punched into the face of the Strangler, who fended the blows off with his forearms and kicked the big man backward. The two exchanged quick mat moves then, slowing down the pace of the match staying strongly focused at the task at hand. The Strangler attempted a crossface chicken wing submission hold, but Death powered out and attacked with a chop to the chest. Both men exchanged chops until Death was able to hit a drop toe hold and quickly put the Boston Strangler in scorpion deathlock. Death started to work on Strangler's knee, trying to ground the larger man. Strangler escaped to the ropes and stood, and then delivered a series of kicks to the head of the leader of the Dark Riders. Death reeled, and the Strangler put Death in triangle hold, which looked effective on Death's elbow. Death screamed in agony, and delivered a punch to the jaw to break the submission attempt. Strangler chopped and kicked back, aiming for the arms and upper torso of Death, who punched and chopped into the neck to cut off the Boston Strangler's air supply. Death began to turn the tide into his favor, but when Death ran to the ropes the Strangler hit a roundhouse kick on Death's head on his way back. Strangler the DDT'd Death, and made a loose cover which earned him a two count. On his feet, Death attempted a Northern-lights bomb, but he could not lift Strangler into the air. The Strangler kicked to free himself, and Death hit a low blow to the groin to put the odds back in his favor. Death then switched his hold into a powerbomb, which was successful but on the following cover only got him a two count. Death ran to the ropes again for a lariat and Strangler dropkicked him from behind. Strangler grabbed for Death and picked him up into the air with a great deal of effort and attempted a brainbuster, but Death twisted his body in the air and pressed the Boston Strangler. Death dropped him and as the Strangler rose Death quickly hit two successive lariats. The Boston Strangler attacked back with a chop in the arm, but Death continued to hit more lariats in rapid succession. After six blows, he finally knocked Strangler down to the mat. Moving quickly, Death hit a Northern-lights bomb, and covered with a La Magistral. The Strangler kicked out a moment too late, giving the victory to Death. Death slowly stood and started to bad mouth the Strangler inside the ring, but the Strangler would have none of it and drove him from the ring with a large clothesline. The Strangler held up his arms, and got the cheers from the crowd as he continued to look on with intensity at the leader of the Dark Riders on the outside of the ring. Result: Death by Pinfall VL vs. Courage VL walked to the ring with Stevie Roberts at her side, and then two complained loudly about the decision made at Hotter Than Hell III to hold the fWo World Tag Team Titles up for a tournament to begin in the next week. VL and Stevie walked over to the announcer table and berated Chris Finn and James Armstrong, asking the pair where their titles were. Unhappy with the answer that James stuttered out, VL kicked him in the face, sending him over the guard rail and out into the crowd. VL pointed to Chris Finn and threatened to bitch slap him if he didn't produce some good answers by Warfare. The music of Courage played, and the relatively new fWo wrestler and his bodyguard Jake walked to the ring, oblivious to the fans and focusing completely on VL and Stevie. Courage argued that Stevie should have to return to the dressing room for this contest, and the ref agreed, sending a protesting Stevie to the back. Jake did remain at ringside, and the two wrestlers were quick to lock up. Courage had the strength advantage, but VL used her ring knowledge to slide down and under his legs, bring his arms that she was still holding onto up into the groin of Courage. VL then jumped into the air and hit a jumping bomb, knocking Courage to the mat. VL then walked over to shout at Jake, pointing to the backstage area and demanding that he get the hell out of the area. Courage meanwhile came up from behind and hooked the leg of VL, rolling her into a small package. This got only a two count, as VL powered out easily. Courage held onto the leg of VL however, and drove his elbow down into the knee joint. VL screamed in pain, and Courage was warned by the referee that another such attack would result in a quick disqualification. Courage shrugged this off and went to the top turnbuckle to attempt a dangerous high flying move. Courage jumped and missed in an attempted twisting somersault drop, but VL could not stand due to the damage to her knee. Courage kicked at her head and went back up to the top turnbuckle, but missed the a second time. Nursing his head where he collided with the mat, Courage began to grow frustrated, and attacked with slaps and kicks to wear VL down. Courage attempted a pinfall and hooked the leg, but again could only get a two count. Courage swore that the third time was the charm, and he went back to the top rope, but missed the somersault drop again and this time VL stood up weakly after rolling out of the way. VL performed a short clothesline, and began to shake off the effects to her leg. Yelling now, VL picked up Courage and brought him down to the mat for a powerbomb, and then executed two more sequential powerbombs. She made the cover, but Courage kicked out with only a millisecond to spare. Standing now, VL went to the turnbuckle and climbed to the second rope to perform a superbomb. Too slow in climbing with the bad leg, VL was caught up to by Courage, who turned her attempted superbomb into a huracan rana. With VL on the mat Courage climbed up to the top rope and executed a perfect moonsault that found it's target, but still could only get a two count on the pinfall attempt. Yelling at the ref, Courage threatened to strike him, but then thought better of it and turned back to VL. She was waiting for him and scooped Courage up and hit a death valley driver. Not going for the cover, VL picked Courage up and executed a series of quick suplexes, ending in a cross arm suplex. She got a quick two count on the cover, but Courage laid a foot on the ropes to break the cover. VL stood and went for a leg drop, but Courage rolled free. VL stomped on the head of Courage then, and went to the turnbuckle. Taking too much time due to the wounded knee, Courage stood and ran at VL, hitting her in the weakened knee with a superkick on her ascent. VL fell down to the mat and Courage went to the top rope, but this time was successful in the twisting somersault drop, and quickly hooked the leg for the pinfall victory. Courage quickly slid out of the ring, and from a safe distance mocked VL with his bodyguard Jake. VL watched this from the ring, silently vowing revenge. Result: Courage by Pinfall Ice vs. War War came to the ring, looking serious but unusually calm and collected. At his side was Plague who sniveled quietly and drooled as he trotted to the ring. Inside War flexed his muscles, awaiting his opponent. Ice did not disappoint, and he walked quickly to the ring with the Internet title slung over his shoulder. Ice grabbed a microphone from the announcer desk and addressed the crowd, holding up the Internet title to them in defiance of his boos. Ice yelled out, "Mad Trucker... you want a piece of this you miserable hick? You meet me in the ring at Hotter Than Hell Four... I'll teach you what it means to get your ass handed to you... down your throat!" War had heard enough, and attacked from behind with a jumping knee. War rolled Ice back into the ring and took Ice's back, but Ice hit a back elbow and ran to the ropes. War went into the ropes as well, and both big men criss-crossed inside the ring until Ice hit a hip attack as they passed. Ice tossed War into the ropes to attempted to hit the hip attack again, but he missed the landing and came down on his leg. Ice struggled to get up, and the referee looked in at Ice's leg to see if he had suffered any serious damage. War came in from behind and tossed the referee out of the way and attacked the leg with kicks into the shin. The referee warned War not to pull some actions again or he would be automatically disqualified, and War simply looked at the referee and growled. The referee backed off, and War went back to attacking the leg of Ice, applying a leglock on the Internet champion. Pulling Ice to his feet, War executed a perfect Teioh lock and turned it into an STF, but Ice escaped to the rope and forced War to break the hold. War punched down on Ice grounding him once more, and went up to the top rope. Before War could launch himself off the top turnbuckle Ice pushed the referee into the ropes, toppling War and sending him crotch first onto the turnbuckle. With War still straddling the turnbuckle, Ice did German suplex from the top rope, sending War down to the mat hard. War kicked out at two when Ice attempted a cover, and raked the eyes of Ice, earning another warning. Ice meanwhile stumbled backward, and War caught him with a European uppercut. War then picked Ice up and hit a powerbomb and followed it up with a diving hip attack, and then powerbombed Ice again. Ice was unaffected by this attack and came back at War with a hard lariat which sent War to the mat. Ice prepared a piledriver, but War lashed out with a closed fist into the injured knee of Ice, toppling the big man. War followed up but was taken down with a sitting spear by Ice, who then attempted to place War in a Texas Cloverleaf. War grabbed the rope to break the hold, and then kicked low when Ice released the hold. War hit a Yakuza kick, but Ice grabbed the legs and rolled War up for a pin. War broke free on the two count, and attacked the weakened knee of Ice again, this time behind the referee's back. War ground in on the knee, but Ice stood and suplexed War over. Ice covered War, but War managed to use his momentum to his advantage, continuing to roll Ice over and cover him in a small package while applying pressure to the weakened knee. Ice kicked out a moment too late, and War silently left the ring, leaving Ice to tend to his wounded knee. Result: War by Pinfall Rain vs. Crucifix Thunder rumbled in the rafters of the arena, and a lone man walked slowly to the ring, wearing a long full trench coat. Rain looked grim, and when offered the microphone by Chris Finn only looked at the announcer slowly and shook his head. Rain waited inside the ring, sitting silently in the corner of the ring and waiting for his long-time rival. Crucifix came out onto the entrance ramp with the Hardcore title strapped around his waist, and pointed down at Rain inside the ring. Lightening flashed and struck the corner posts, sending a spray of sparks into the ring... but Rain did not move. Crucifix began to walk to the ring when Mike Bear ran out from the backstage area and attacked Crucifix from behind. fWo security officials hit the scene and separated Mike Bear from Crucifix, pushing him to the backstage area. Bear pointed back at Crucifix, telling him that if he won tonight he would slaughter him next week. With the path cleared, Crucifix walked to the ring, looking over his back from time to time for signs of the re-emergence of Mike Bear. The second Crucifix stepped into the ring Rain ejected himself from the corner of the ring into Crucifix in a running spear. Rain dominated the match in the early going, keeping Crucifix completely off-guard with hard chops to the chest and face, and then attacking with a trio of powerbombs. Rain ignored the cover and continued to punch Crucifix the corner, moving from open fists to closed fists, and opening up the forehead of Crucifix. Crucifix's pale face became crimson red, and the referee stood between the two men in an attempt to settle the pace down. Rain grabbed the referee and slung him away, and went back on the attack. Rain attempted a fisherman's buster, but Crucifix blocked it and switched it into his own brainbuster. Crucifix took a few moments to walk around the ring and get his second wind. Rain began to rise, and Crucifix quickly climbed to the top turnbuckle. Crucifix dove off from the top rope with a diving headbutt and landed square into the chest of Rain. The referee began to make a count, but Crucifix picked Rain's head up after the one count. Crucifix stomped down on the head of Rain, and climbed to the top rope once more. But Crucifix missed his diving headbutt attempt as Rain quickly sat up. Rain picked Crucifix up and delivered another powerbomb and then attempted another, but Crucifix blocked it and hit a somersault koppo kick that caught Rain in the chest. Wiping his face free of blood Crucifix began to scream and lightning again struck the turnbuckles, spraying a new shower of sparks in on the two men. Crucifix hit a palm blow on Rain, but Rain attacked back with a deathvalley bomb that came out of nowhere, and then followed it with another three sequential powerbombs. Rain looked around at the crowd and nodded, and covered Crucifix. He only got a two count, as Crucifix powered out strong, tossing Rain off his chest with a hard throw. Rain punched down the head of Crucifix and attempted another cover, but again only got a two count. Rain stood, and Crucifix grabbed the legs and rolled Rain over, putting him into a small package. Rain reversed the cover, and rolled up Crucifix, but Crucifix powered out and both men went to opposite ends of the ring. Both men stared at each other... and a long moment went by. Then both men charged, and Rain went for a running lariat. Crucifix ducked under and hit a Crucifixbomb on the return. Crucifix made a cover, and Rain kicked out with just a hair away from the three count. Crucifix picked Rain up and placed him on the top turnbuckle, and executed a fisherman's buster off the top rope. Crucifix then climbed to the top rope and attempted a splash, but as he flew from the top turnbuckle Rain sprung up from the ground, hitting him in mid-air with a spear. Rain grabbed the legs of Crucifix and bent him over in a cover and got the three count and the victory. Rain raised his arm in victory, looked down at the bleeding Crucifix, and walked silently from the ring. Crucifix looked at the departing wrestler, and grinning through a haze of blood raised his arms to the air once more, sending the third and final burst of lightening from the rafters as the show went off the air.... Result: Rain by Pinfall
