Sunday Slaughter, 7 November 1999

7 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "This is the dumbest thing I think I've ever seen. Dumbest." Chris Finn sat on a folding chair and pouted as Commissioner James Armstrong arranged his notes and addressed the short crowd. "Ladies and gentlemen... many of you know me as James Armstrong... but from this day forward I will be addressed as Mr. Armstrong. I have been selected as the commissioner of the fWo, and as a result there will be some significant changes in the promotion that we all know and love. Mr. Green... the lights?" Geoff Green lowered the lights as the slide projector came on. James Armstrong pointed to the first slide. "Okay, as you can see here we see Chris Finn and myself at the announcing table. This is how it has been for years, but ultimately does not increase buy rates. That's why I'm going to implement... slide please... bottles of Vodka. The crowd will wonder exactly when the announcers will stop making sense and pass out in a drunken stupor, and thus keep tuned in. Next slide please. Okay, here we see midcard female wrestlers like Glee. While this kind of wrestling has been popular in the past, I'm certain that ratings will go up if rather than wrestling in a ring the female wrestlers fight in a shower... or tub of creamed corn. Next slide please. Okay, here we see a sample script. It's nice and all... but with a few added explicitives it can be much better. For example, rather than saying "I want the title shot" a wrestler can say "Give me the f**king title shot you f**king f**k." These are sure to increase ratings. Oh... and no more rest holds, arm locks, or submission moves. All matches should be under three minutes and people who don't speak English will no longer get microphone time. Also, Scott Slugger will from this day on be addressed as Commander Slugger, King of Wrestlers. Any questions?" The small crowd looked at James Armstrong with stunned expressions on their faces as Chris Finn held his head in his arms. "Dumbest ever..." Death (c) vs. VL Warfare began as VL walked to the ring with Stevie Roberts at her side, and then two waited inside the ring for her Hardcore Title shot. The music of Death played, and he walked to the ring flanked by Stone, War and Plague, oblivious to the fans and focusing completely on VL and Stevie. Death tossed his Hardcore belt at the referee, and the two wrestlers were quick to lock up. Death had the strength advantage, but VL used her ring knowledge to slide down and under his legs, bring his arms that she was still holding onto up into the groin of Death. VL then jumped into the air and hit a jumping bomb, knocking Death to the mat. VL celebrated, looking at the fans with her arms above her head. Death meanwhile came up from behind and hooked the leg of VL, rolling her into a small package. This got only a two count, as VL powered out easily. Death held onto the leg of VL however, and drove his elbow down into the knee joint. VL screamed in pain, and punched into the face of the champion. Death shrugged this off and went to the top turnbuckle to attempt a dangerous high flying move. Death jumped and missed in an attempted twisting somersault drop, but VL could not stand due to the damage to her knee. Death kicked at her head and went back up to the top turnbuckle, but missed the a second time. Nursing his head where he collided with the mat, Death began to grow frustrated, and attacked with slaps and kicks to wear VL down. Death attempted a pinfall and hooked the leg, but again could only get a two count. Death swore that the third time was the charm, and he went back to the top rope, but missed the somersault drop again and this time VL stood up weakly after rolling out of the way. VL performed a short clothesline, and began to shake off the effects to her leg. Yelling now, VL picked up Death and brought him down to the mat for a powerbomb, and then executed two more sequential powerbombs. She made the cover, but Death kicked out with only a millisecond to spare. Standing now, VL went to the turnbuckle and climbed to the second rope to perform a superbomb. Too slow in climbing with the bad leg, VL was caught up to by Death, who turned her attempted superbomb into a huracan rana. With VL on the mat Death climbed up to the top rope and executed a perfect moonsault that found it's target, but still could only get a two count on the pinfall attempt. Yelling at the ref, Death threatened to strike him, but then thought better of it and turned back to VL. She was waiting for him and scooped Death up and hit a death valley driver. Not going for the cover, VL picked Death up and executed a series of quick suplexes, ending in a cross arm suplex. She got a quick two count on the cover, but Death laid a foot on the ropes to break the cover. VL stood and went for a leg drop, but Death rolled free. VL stomped on the head of Death then, and went to the turnbuckle. Taking too much time due to the wounded knee, Death stood and ran at VL, hitting her in the weakened knee with a superkick on her ascent. VL fell down to the mat and Death went to the top rope, but this time was successful in the twisting somersault drop, and quickly hooked the leg for the pinfall victory. Death slid from the ring as an fWo official approached the champion and pointed backstage. Whatever he had to say, it alarmed Death and the Dark Riders, and the four ran to the back... Result: Death by Pinfall Backstage trouble Security officials quickly moved to hold back the Strangler, who punched wildly in an attempted to get free. It was Primetime that he was aiming for, and he swung out at the fWo World Champion. "We are ready... ready to finish what we started!" The Strangler lunged again at Primetime, and the champion ducked out of the way. "You're insane Strangler... and I don't see any reason why you should ever get your title shot!" The Strangler lunged again at Primetime, and this time the champion fell to the ground. The two scuffled as security tried in vain to separate the two. The Strangler struck out and punched Primetime in the face, bloodying his noise. Primetime spat, as security pushed the two apart once again. Primetime was enraged, and shouted at the Strangler as they were drug apart. "You want your match? You got it you lunatic... bring it on Wednesday, and you won't leave the arena alive!" Primetime was drug away by the security officials, past a rather fat ex-bodyguard. Jake watched as Primetime was escorted away, and idea seeming to pass over his face... Fyre vs. Micky Maliboo Fyre came to the ring to the vast boos of the audience, and gave them all the finger as she went. Stepping inside the ring, she crotched down and motioned for Micky Maliboo to come to the ring and take a beating. Micky Maliboo's music played, and along with the lovely Vixon he walked to the ring. Micky Maliboo entered the ring and at once Fyre began to hit Micky Maliboo with a series of palm blows to the head. Micky Maliboo seemed unaffected by this as Fyre walked around the ring mocking Micky Maliboo and the crowd. Fyre then grabbed Vixon by the hair and began to shake her, which seemed to get an angry reaction from Micky Maliboo. Running from behind, Micky Maliboo took revenge with forearm smash to the back of the head that sent Fyre up and over the top rope to the outside. Micky Maliboo ran to the other end of the ring and came off the ropes with a perfectly executed tope to the outside and onto Fyre. On the outside, Micky Maliboo positioned a chair and jumped onto it, leaping at Fyre with a missile drop kick. Micky Maliboo rolled Fyre back into the ring and executed a German suplex in the center of the ring, and followed it up with a reverse DDT. Micky Maliboo covered for the three count, but was only able to get two. Climbing to the top turnbuckle, Micky Maliboo attempted a diving headbutt but missed as Fyre rolled smartly out of the way. Fyre punched down on the prone Micky Maliboo, and then propped him up in one of the corners. She promptly dropkicked his knee and then executed a Dragon screw and turned the aftermath into a knee cross hold. Fyre continued to work on his leg and knee, hoping to eliminate the flying tactics of Micky Maliboo. Fyre picked UV up and powerbombed Micky Maliboo to the outside and climbed up to the top turnbuckle to execute a rolling senton from the top turnbuckle to the floor and onto Micky Maliboo. Back in the ring, Fyre hit a brainbuster on Micky Maliboo and went up to the top but like Micky Maliboo before her missed the diving headbutt. On his feet now, Micky Maliboo ran to the ropes but Fyre welcomed him with another big palm-blow to the chest. Fyre did a running Fyrebomb, made a cover, but only got a two count. Fyre placed Micky Maliboo on the top rope, performed a brainbuster from there and went for a cover. Micky Maliboo kicked out at two once more. Frustrated, Fyre placed Micky Maliboo on the top rope again, and attempted to perform a frankensteiner but Micky Maliboo blocked it. Micky Maliboo quickly executed a swinging DDT from the top turnbuckle sending Fyre down to the mat. Micky Maliboo signaled to the crowd, and hit a reverse brainbuster on Fyre. Vixon urged Micky Maliboo to hit one more, and Micky Maliboo scooped up Fyre for a second reverse brainbuster. Micky Maliboo covered Fyre, and got the three count. Maliboo raised his arms in the air, as Oracle watched from the entrance ramp with a bemused expression on his face... Result: Micky Maliboo by Pinfall Orders... Somewhere deep in the arena David Spencer bade Fallen come to him. "Take the girl... go to the surface... and destroy all those that dare tarnish our reputation." Fallen grinned a sick and confused grin. "Is it time to take out that fool Collins?" Spencer smiled, and shook his head. "No... not yet. Let Collins twist in the wind with his foolish slaves... for now let the others do our bidding... my sources tell me that the imposter has his own problems for now. Go forth and build our reputation Fallen. Take the girl, and make them fear us..." Fallen smiled and backed out of the room, as Spencer laughed a sick and hollow laugh... Fallen w/ Poison vs. Darkness Fallen came to the ring, looking serious but unusually calm and collected. At his side was Poison who looked nervous and cried quietly as she trotted to the ring. Inside Fallen flexed his muscles, awaiting his opponent. The lights came to darkness, as the massive Darkness strode to the ring. Darkness got inside the ring and looked out at the crowd, grinning with a dark smile. Fallen attacked from behind with a jumping knee causing him to fall from the ring. Fallen rolled Darkness back into the ring and took Darkness's back, but Darkness hit a back elbow and ran to the ropes. Fallen went into the ropes as well, and both big men criss-crossed inside the ring until Darkness hit a hip attack as they passed. Darkness tossed Fallen into the ropes to attempted to hit the hip attack again, but he missed the landing and came down on his leg. Darkness struggled to get up, and the referee looked in at Darkness's leg to see if he had suffered any serious damage. Fallen came in from behind and tossed the referee out of the way and attacked the leg with kicks into the shin. The referee warned Fallen not to pull some actions again or he would be automatically disqualified, and Fallen simply looked at the referee and growled. The referee backed off, and Fallen went back to attacking the leg of Darkness, applying a leglock on the Internet champion. Pulling Darkness to his feet, Fallen executed a perfect Teioh lock and turned it into an STF, but Darkness escaped to the rope and forced Fallen to break the hold. Fallen punched down on Darkness grounding him once more, and went up to the top rope. Before Fallen could launch himself off the top turnbuckle Darkness pushed the referee into the ropes, toppling Fallen and sending him crotch first onto the turnbuckle. With Fallen still straddling the turnbuckle, Darkness did German suplex from the top rope, sending Fallen down to the mat hard. Fallen kicked out at two when Darkness attempted a cover, and raked the eyes of Darkness, earning another Warning. Darkness meanwhile stumbled backward, and Fallen caught him with a European uppercut. Fallen then picked Darkness up and hit a powerbomb and followed it up with a diving hip attack, and then powerbombed Darkness again. Darkness was unaffected by this attack and came back at Fallen with a hard lariat which sent Fallen to the mat. Darkness prepared a piledriver, but Fallen lashed out with a closed fist into the injured knee of Darkness, toppling the big man. Fallen followed up but was taken down with a sitting spear by Darkness, who then attempted to place Fallen in a Texas Cloverleaf. Fallen grabbed the rope to break the hold, and then kicked low when Darkness released the hold. Fallen hit a Yakuza kick, but Darkness grabbed the legs and rolled Fallen up for a pin. Fallen broke free on the two count, and attacked the weakened knee of Darkness again, this time behind the referee's back. Fallen ground in on the knee, but Darkness stood and suplexed Fallen over. Darkness covered Fallen, but Fallen managed to use his momentum to his advantage, continuing to roll Darkness over and cover him in a small package while applying pressure to the weakened knee. Darkness kicked out a moment too late, and Fallen silently left the ring, leaving Darkness to tend to his wounded knee. Result: Fallen by Pinfall The Hunt Stone lay on the workout bench backstage, pumping iron and grunting as she lifted the heavy weights above her head. She never heard the noise behind her that signaled a new arrival... or arrivals. As she started to lift the weights up again strong hands shoved the metal bar down, pressing the steel into her neck and choking her. Stone looked up wide eyed at Chris Universal, Lance Star, Scott Slugger and Ice. The Dynasty. Slugger smiled a Cheshire grin and raised his bat, bringing it down hard on the skull of Stone and knocking her swiftly into unconsciousness. Laughing the four left the locker room as they came, in search of their next victim... Ultra Cool 2000 vs. NXS Already in the ring were NXS, taunting the crowd and pointing to their obviously stuffed groins. Ultra Cool 2000 came to the ring, led by the lovely Violet. Ultra Violet grinned happily at the crowd as Kid Cool looked more intense, staring down the men in the ring with grim determination. In was Ultra Violet and RottDogg who started the match but Steel was soon tagged in. RottDogg held Ultra Violet as Steel repeatedly chopped Ultra Violet, sending the purple haired warrior to the ropes. Coming off them Ultra Violet hit a jumping forearm smash and tagged in Kid Cool. Kid Cool worked the arm of Steel, trapping him in their corner as he tagged in Ultra Violet once more. Ultra Violet's spinkick hit Steel, and then Kid Cool dropped his knee into the jaw. Steel stumbled back and made a hot tag to RottDogg, who came back with a punch to the jaw of Ultra Violet. Ultra Violet tagged in Kid Cool, who slid under the legs of the charging RottDogg and made a diving punch into his testicles. All offense, Kid Cool chokeslamed RottDogg, and did a diving headbutt on him. Kid Cool went for a cover but Steel made the save. Both of NXS were inside the ring as the ref warned Ultra Violet on the outside. NXS double teamed Kid Cool, getting him up for a double powerbomb. RottDogg rolled Kid Cool up but the count was only two. Tagged in, Steel powerbombed Kid Cool, but when he went for the cover Ultra Violet made the save with springboard dropkick. Then he hit springboard spinkick on Steel. Ultra Violet suplexed Steel, went for a cover, and RottDogg made the save. Steel aimed a suplex at Ultra Violet, but Kid Cool hit a flying clothesline to interfere. The ref tried to maintain some control but all four men were inside the ring and attacking quickly. Ultra Violet and Kid Cool attacked with a double-team face crusher, but the move damaged Kid Cool's head and he rolled to the outside. Steel hit a shoulder tackle on a distracted Ultra Violet, and then lifted Ultra Violet on his shoulder as RottDogg executed an airborne DDT. Steel applied a cover but Kid Cool made the save. Steel and Kid Cool started to brawl outside, with Kid Cool smashing a chair over Steel's head. RottDogg went to the top and aimed a springboard dropkick but was dropkicked by Ultra Violet in midair. Kid Cool came back in the ring, and powerbombed RottDogg. With RottDogg helpless in the ring, Kid Cool used a piledriver to finish him off and get the three count. Ultra Cool 2000 celebrated inside the ring, arms raised high. Result: Ultra Cool 2000 by Pinfall More Canadians "TCW huh? Well I don't know..." James Armstrong looked skeptical as he looked the large wrestler standing before him up and down. "That's Canada territory... and I don't know about bringing in more Canadians. I just don't know Mr... er... what was your name again?" The wrestler rubbed his goatee and smiled. "The Foreign Phantom, Mr. Armstrong." Armstrong dismissed this comment as if it was meaningless. "All I'm saying is that I think we've had enough Canadian wrestlers in the fWo already..." The Phantom shrugged, and started to walk away. "That is too bad... for I was hoping to wrestle the great Scott Slugger... or the Flying Frenchie. Obviously I wouldn't be able to beat those stars, but just to get in the ring with them..." The Phantom started to leave the room but James Armstrong stopped him. "Whoa! Hold up there, I like your attitude young man... consider yourself officially instated into the fWo right now. Go down the hall and sign your contract... I'll take care of the rest." The Phantom watched Armstrong walk away with a bemused smile on his face. "Predictable idiot." Black Quicksilver vs. Joy Joy walked to the ring handing out lollypops and Kool-Aid. Joy grinned as he got into the ring, announcing to the crowd that he had an open contract in the back for a best of three Happy Matches and that he was looking forward to seeing who would accept his challenge. Black Quicksilver walked to the ring with Stump, excited about his Cruiserweight Title Match on Warfare. Inside the ring both kicked hard in each others' faces, landing hard standing kicks. Black Quicksilver missed a kick and Joy executed a spinning heel kick that caught Black Quicksilver hard in the head. With his opponent reeling, Joy suplexed Black Quicksilver belly-to-belly and then belly-to-back. Black Quicksilver seemed fired up by this, and attacked back with a chop followed by a belly-to-back suplex. Climbing to the top rope, Black Quicksilver flew off, hitting a springboard dropkick. Black Quicksilver went for another, but Joy caught Black Quicksilver's kick and whipped him to the mat, and then worked on Black Quicksilver's knee. Joy did a Tigersuplex, and went for a cover... but Black Quicksilver reached the ropes breaking the count. Joy then placed Black Quicksilver on the top turnbuckle but Black Quicksilver jumped from there and Taiwan-pressed Joy. All offense now, Black Quicksilver landed a springboard wheel kick, and went for a cover. Joy kicked out at two, but barely. Black Quicksilver went for Dragon suplex, but Joy blocked it. Whimpering, Joy ran to the ropes, but Black Quicksilver hit a dropkick on his way back and threw him with a Dragon suplex. Rolling him up in an inside cradle, Black Quicksilver got the three count. Result: Black Quicksilver by Pinfall The attack continues... Backstage Plague stood alone outside the dressing room of the Dark Riders, simpering and keeping watch. He started to yell as the Dynasty walked toward him, but Lance Star clamped a hand over his mouth as the four wrestlers converged on him. Ice grabbed one leg as Chris Universal grabbed the other, and then jerked his legs apart causing Plague to howl from beneath the hand of Star. Scott Slugger raised his bat once more, and brought it down hard on the leg of Plague. The smaller wrestler struggled in pain and the desire to free himself, but Ice produced some rope and quickly bound him up. Gagging him, the four wrestlers kicked down the door of the Dark Rider dressing room and went inside all fury and intent... Rain (c) vs. Grendal The lights in the arena flickered as Rain walked out onto the entrance ramp holding high his newly won Internet Championship. Rain was silent, letting the thunder speak for him, as he was flanked on either side by strange alternate versions of Austin Davis and Ice. The trio walked to the ring, and when they got there Rain lay the Internet Championship title over the ropes. Reaching for a microphone, Rain addressed the crowd in a silent but deadly tone. "None of you can stop me. None of you will stop us. This is the reign of my army and I will defeat any who tries. Let the lesson of the Mad Trucker be learned by you all... I cannot be stopped. The belt is here, if someone thinks they can take it then let them try." The lights suddenly blew out, and Grendal stepped onto the entrance ramp, looking at Rain through the face paint of a madman. Grendal ran to the ring and speared Rain, taking the champion by surprise. Grendal pounded away on Rain's head, until the strange versions of Davis and Ice pulled him off. Holding Grendal for their master, Rain did a Judo-style powerslam and hit some high kicks. Grendal didn't care and freed himself with a massive punch to both of the men, and clotheslined Rain. Grendal pressed Rain to the corner, punching hard until Rain's head had opened up. Rain attacked with a kick to the shin, and then tried to blow out Grendal's knee. Grendal blocked it and hit some elbows, and then a sidewalk slam. Then he put Rain in necklock. Rain couldn't escape for a minute, and tried desperately to get to the ropes. He finally did, and Grendal broke the hold with a kick to the back of the head. Grendal powerbombed Rain. Rain lay on the mat dazed, and the referee put on the ten count. Rain stood up at six, wearily. Grendal ran to the ropes and aimed a clothesline, but Rain blocked it and put him into a cross arm scissors. Rain screamed as the lights again flickered and bore down on the hold, trying to break Grendal's arm. Then Rain switched into a version of triangle hold, choking the air out of Grendal. Grendal tried to stay in the match but finally darkness crossed over his eyes and he passed out, unconscious on the mat. Rain stepped over him, raising his title and screaming at the crowd through a haze of crimson blood. Screaming he said, "Nobody will ever stop me!" Result: Rain by Submission Old faces A strange promo began to air, filled with American flags and strange warbling music. A man was facing away from the camera, and spoke nearly to softly to hear. "It's been a long time, and I don't imagine most of you remember me. Well, the wrestling world has become a sick and twisted place and only one man can brave the darkness and lead our country back into the light. And if you don't like it... well..." The man suddenly turned around, and on his face was a rubber mask of Richard Milhouse Nixon. "My fellow Americans, kiss my ass! This former president is gonna smack some booty and grab some looty!" He began to laugh maniacally as the cameras faded out with the words. "He's coming back." Underground (c) vs. Mystery Team The fWo World Tag Team Champions came to the ring, holding their belts up for the crowd and flanked by the massive Fear. The Underground slid into the ring and waited to see what team from the outside would rise to the occasion and take their challenge. The lights dimmed, and red light began to flicker as the sound of "Iron Man" by Black Sabbath filled the arena. The Road Warriors from All Japan Pro Wrestling came out onto the entrance ramp, and the crowd looked at this arrival in shocked amazement. Bear and Wretch looked nervously at each other as the legendary tag team walked to the ring, shoulder pads and familiar garb worn. Inside the ring Hawk started the match with Mike Bear, and the two paced each other before locking up. Hawk won the test of strength, and pushed Bear to the mat with a hard thud. Hawk then delivered a legdrop on Bear, and climbed to the top rope. Hawk was going for a splash, but Wretch interfered with a jumping kick on the ring apron that sent Hawk down hard into the metal turnbuckle. Bear hit a big boot and aimed a chokeslam, but Animal entered the ring and interfered. Animal was kicked by Wretch, who joined the three men in the ring as the referee desperately tried to maintain order. Hawk hit a running lariat on Bear and went for a cover, but Wretch made the save. Wretch was tagged inside, and Hawk at once went on the offensive and chopped Wretch. Hawk went to the ropes for a running lariat on Wretch, but Fear punched at his back as he hit the ropes. Wretch then hit a jumping kick on Hawk. On the attack now, Wretch executed a belly-to-back suplex. Bear was tagged in and the Underground did a double-team powerbomb, and Bear went for the cover. At the two count Animal entered the ring and made the save. Bear aimed for a chokeslam, but Hawk powered out and whipped him into the Road Warrior corner. Animal punched at Bear's head as the referee was tied up with Wretch, who wanted to save his partner. Weakly now, Bear powered out and attempted a German suplex, but Hawk reversed it. Hawk covered but Bear powered out at two. Bear hit a DDT in the center of the ring, and both men crawled to make tags. Wretch and Animal came in, and Wretch ducked a clothesline by Animal and hit a body splash coming off the ropes. Animal was dazed, and with Bear keeping Hawk busy on the outside Wretch executed a perfect Ligerbomb inside the ring and rolled Animal up for the three count and the successful title defense. The Underground escaped the ring weary, but still Tag Team Champions. Result: Underground by Pinfall Warnings given Inside the Dark Rider dressing room War tried to break free but to no avail as Universal, Ice and Star held him fast. Scott Slugger swung and connected with his bat on the head of War, but the big man only screamed and struggled harder to get free. Slugger smiled and pressed the bat into the throat of the big man, slowly cutting off his oxygen. War coughed and gasped for air, as Slugger spoke a deadly warning. "Tell Scotty that when we get our hands on him, he's going to be closer to "death" than he's ever been in his entire life. Got it you big dumb retard?" War gasped, and his body finally betrayed him as he slumped to the floor, unconscious. Laughing, the Dynasty left the locker room as the cameras returned to the ring for the final time in the night... Eron (c) vs. Courage In what was billed as the final meeting between the two, Courage came to the ring in a return bout for the International Championship. Courage looked strangely alone without Jake present, and gave several fans the finger as he walked. Eron came out onto the top of the ramp and stood, holding the International title at arm's length and sneering at the crowd. The fans booed both men equally, and Eron slung the belt over his shoulder and marched to the ring. Once there the two began arguing, and it quickly turned to punches inside the ring. Eron took Courage's back. Courage hit a back elbow, but now behind the challenger Eron dropkicked Courage, and he went outside. Eron climbed out of the ring and stood on the apron, waiting for Courage to stand. He dove off with a flying forearm from the apron, but Courage saw him coming and dropkicked him in the air. Both men went down on the floor. Courage stood up first, hung Eron's neck over the steel guardrail, and hit a legdrop. Courage dragged Eron up on the apron, hung Eron's neck over the second rope, hit a quick pair of legdrops trying to batter the champion into unconsciousness. At the urging of the ref Courage drug Eron fully into the ring and attacked with a pair of back suplexes, and then a cover which Eron kicked out of at two. Courage aimed a powerbomb, but Eron blocked and tried to use the momentum to switch it into huracanerana, but Courage blocked this and put him into the Boston Crab. Eron escaped to the ropes breathing hard. Rolling to the ring apron, Eron hit a forearm on Courage from the outside. Eron suplexed Courage over the ropes and down to the floor, and then hit a diving forearm to Courage on the floor. Both men lay still, and then slowly crawled back into the ring. Eron went up on the top rope, and again hit a diving forearm. Eron covered, but got a two count only. Eron aimed a Tigerdriver, Courage reversed it. Eron went for German suplex, but Courage blocked it. Eron raked the eyes and then quickly slapped on a Tigerdriver, driving the challenger to the mat. Climbing to the top rope, Eron hit a frog splash, and made a cover. Two counts again as Courage kicked out hard. Eron went up on the top turnbuckle again, and attempted a diving clothesline, but Courage hit a lariat from below which spun the champion around and slammed him to the mat hard. Both men fell down as the crowd, who had been booing both men now began to chant "fWo! fWo! fWo!" Both men stood up in opposite corners. Eron rushed and aimed a monkey flip, but Courage caught him and slammed his face to the turnbuckle. Courage applied a half nelson suplex, and then lifted Eron as if for a powerbomb but then turned back and dropped Eron head first into the turnbuckle. Firmly on the attack, Courage hit a DDT and then attacked with a full nelson suplex. Courage went for a cover, and Eron kicked out a hair away from three. Courage and Eron stood up slowly, and rushed each other. Eron's forearm and Courage's lariat hit both at the same time, and both went down. Courage was up first, and hit a spinning DDT which snapped Eron's head back as it struck the mat. Courage fell upon Eron and got a two count, but Eron suddenly twisted over and trapped Courage in an inverted hangman's cover. Eron placed both feet on the ring ropes, and the referee did not see him use this added leverage. The referee made the three count with Courage kicking out a split second too late, and Eron successfully retained the belt. High on the entrance ramp Jake watched his former boss argue with the referee, as Sunday Slaughter went off the air for another week. Result: Eron by Pinfall
