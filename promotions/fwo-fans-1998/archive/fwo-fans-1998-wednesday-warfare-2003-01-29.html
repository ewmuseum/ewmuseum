Introduction For five years the fWo has run strong, showcasing some of the very best the industry has to offer. Now, as BodyCount has come and gone, all eyes are focused on the road to CyberSlam and the trials and tribulations that will take place there. Year six for the fWo promises to be more troubling, more confusing, and more disturbing than ever before. At BodyCount, Kodiak Vic Creed went through 39 other wrestlers to become the number one contender at CyberSlam for the fWo World Heavyweight Championship. But his victory is not without controversy... In the main event at CyberSlam, Erik Kelly defended his fWo World Heavyweight Championship against the masked luchador Spyder. But Spyder did not come to the battle alone. Threatening a backup plan that suggested the return of one of the fWo's most infamous wrestlers Austin Davis, in reality Spyder had aligned himself with none other than Fallen and the once Plague, now Neo. Through their underhanded antics, Spyder now holds the highest prize in the industry. Finished with Canada, the fWo sets off on a desperate struggle to fill venues in areas they have never been before. The fWo finds itself in Mexico City on unfamiliar and dangerous ground... Your new World Champion Fireworks exploded as the fans, nearly seven thousand of them, rose to their feet, arms in the air. The fWo had never come to Mexico City before, and as Chris Finn and Steel looked around nervously from the announcers table, they couldn't help noticing that a majority of the signs that were waved celebrated the victory of their new fWo World Heavyweight Champion. As cameras moved into position, Geoff Green warned Kristen Jacobs to be on her guard... the equipment was rented and the security lax beyond belief. If they could escape this night without disaster... The roar of the crowd grew to an unbelievable level as the lights focused on the top of the stage and Rammstien blared over the arena speakers. The feedback from the music whined and popped, but it was drowned out by the ovation from the crowd surrounding the ring. At the top of the ramp, the fWo World Heavyweight Championship slung over one shoulder, stood the masked luchador Spyder. On one side, dressed in black leather tights and a chain mail shirt, stood Fallen, hair pulled neatly into a pony tail and a dark expression worn on his face. On the other side stood Neo, revealed as Plague during the closing moments of BodyCount, he looked smug, confident, and while not an exact replica of the original Neo, certainly a deadly shadow. Behind them strode Dina Sol, Fallen's one time partner, now seemingly the group manager. Together, the three walked to the ring, Spyder holding his hand high above his head in acknowledgement of the huge ovation the crowd was giving him. He was in Mexico City, his home, and to the crowd around him he had brought honor to the country by winning the industry's biggest prize. The fWo World Heavyweight Championship was shined and bore the name of the new champion on the gold nameplate.
