Introduction Previously... The plot to destroy the fWo has been foiled... but tragedy struck when and where people least expected. As the fWo sits at the dawn of rebirth, nothing will ever be the same again. In Memorial Fans in the arena stood slowly, looking up as the lights in the arena dimmed. Unlike tragedies past, there were no wrestlers on the entrance ramp, no showing of men in tights and costumes. Instead a simple image of Lance Starr was highlighted on the giant screen over the entrance ramp. Lance Starr as he was in the fWo, a champion... a wrestler... and an entertainer. Lance Starr, a man who walked down the entrance ramp countless times for the fWo, and after his career as a wrestler ended, a leader in the CWL. Lance Starr, who was regarded by many as a quiet, but effective member of the promotion. A man who loved wrestling in the ring, a man who loved the thrill of competition... but also a man who rose to the occasion when he was given a far different task. On the screen, Lance Starr. Fans in the arena turned to look at his image. They had heard from the newsletters and the internet... nearly all of them had been at home watching their televisions when Frank Snow stepped into the ring with the gun. They saw the crazed look in his eyes, the fear, and the desperation. Then the picture went black, and those that could sought news anywhere they could get it. They didn't want to believe it, and there were some in the audience who felt that this was just another booking trick... that Lance Starr would emerge from behind the curtains and challenge Dudley for the title. That his death and his entire injury was nothing more than a ploy to grab ratings. But on the screen, Lance Starr. The bell rang, and if there were those who believed it was all a con, the sat silent and still and didn't say so. Perhaps it was just respect after all, for by and large these people were here to see the destruction of a promotion... to see the train wreck. But if that was the case, they respected the moment for what it was. How many wrestlers had been buried this year? Had been honored in black shrouds? What happened to this sport that they loved? Lance Starr's image slowly faded from the screen as it went to black. They would remember him, of course. He was a wrestler... a superstar... a man that they respected. He was Lance Starr, and he was gone. One final task Stephen Roberts leaned against the wall and waited. November was a strange month... while the holiday buzz words were all about being thankful, Roberts found it a time of quiet reflection. The month was gray, winter moving in and the days getting shorter and shorter as the leaves fell and all died. Rebirth of another year was still months away, and Roberts found his mood getting more and more grim as November marched onward. Last week Lance Starr had been shot... it was an accident after a fashion; it was Dudley that Frank Snow was after. Dudley who had masterminded this entire scheme to get revenge. Ah, and what a strange thing that was after all. Revenge? Against a wrestling promotion? Against an entertainment industry? What were they all playing at if it wasn't entertainment? How could it have really gone this far? Gotten this out of control? Who lived their lives for this hobby so much that they were willing to lie, to cheat, to steal... and to kill... What was it all if not a game? Stephen Roberts leaned against the wall and waited for Beaven to arrive. Beaven... the old owner back again after all this time. When he had gotten involved with Dudley and the others he had done it because he wanted to put himself in the position of undoing it all if he had to. Someone had to look out for the boys, and he wasn't going to be able to do that sitting on the sidelines and hoping it all worked out for the best. No, he was going to do it by getting on the inside and then working to take it down. Dudley, Jon, Spencer... they were egomaniacs and Roberts was pretty confident that he would be able to undermine their efforts quick enough. Graphic Violence was another story... why exactly he was involved was something only he seemed to know. Then there was the other and of the five, everyone had better be wary of... "Roberts?" Stephen turned, and nodded grimly. Travis extended a hand, shook it, and then Terry Nodar patted him warmly on the back. Roberts didn't return his smile. "It's done then? This whole affair is over with?" "It is." Travis nodded. "The paperwork has been signed. The funds are being transferred, and as of midnight tonight the majority of the shares fall back into my hands. With your percentage, and the others..." Stephen Roberts narrowed his eyes. "Others?" "Well, one of the six of you released your shares last week into the public market. I have no idea whose those were, but it's damn odd. Then Graphic Violence agreed to sell his shares in return for the ability to try out with the company. We have no real reason to keep him on mind you, and a try out is just a try out, and if he doesn't make it..." Travis shrugged, but Terry and Stephen were looking uneasy. "You lied to him?" Nodar's face was in a frown. "So this is what we worked to change the leadership over to? So the fWo can still be mired in deception and politics?" Stephen nodded. "He doesn't deserve it, but you made a deal with him. It needs to be honored." "It will," Travis replied, "it's just that... well, there is something you don't know. I'm not planning on..." Roberts cut him off. "Listen, that can wait. There are more important things tonight. First, Dudley still has the title, and isn't going to just walk out of here. Do you really want to have the police take him out, hold the title up again? The fans out there are apathetic to the fWo. The people who came to the show tonight came to the show because they wanted to see a disaster, not a wrestling program. Okay, so you've regained control of the fWo. But unless you start treating this like a wrestling program, like the promotion that these people used to love, you're not going to keep anything but an empty arena." Travis sighed. "Dudley has the title. We could take it off him and give it to Kelly... that would be the right thing to do... but Terry here has proposed another idea... a more fitting idea. Let Dudley have his last walk down the entrance ramp. In the end, everyone will get what they deserve. And second?" "Second..." Roberts started to say, but at that moment, Jim Dudley's music sounded from within the arena, and all attention was focused there. Travis turned to Terry and nodded toward the curtains. "Showtime." Roberts watched them go, a strange feeling inside of him. Terry had offered him a spot with the CWL, and he was planning to take it. But for now... he wish he could have told them the important part. Who the sixth member of the Board was, and why they were in more trouble than they realized... Last stand Jim Dudley tossed the curtains back with one hand and strode out onto the entrance ramp, the fWo World Heavyweight title draped over one shoulder. He looked out at the fans, out at the people who once cheered him and a sneer fixed on his face. God, how he hated these idiots. God, how he hated everything about them. At his side walked Cactus Jon and David Spencer. The three of them walked down the entrance ramp together, three of the first champions in this promotion who had turned its back on them. They sweat and bleed for the fWo, and now these idiot fans chanted for the Boston Strangler. For Lance Knight. A fucking memorial for Lance Starr? What about his memorial after this promotion had tried to kill him? Kill all of them? But he was the god damn World Heavyweight Champion. He was the man... now and forever. Let them walk down the ramp and take the title away from him. It didn't matter. Fact was, he never lost. Fact was, the history books will always put an asterisk by his name as the man who left the fWo undefeated. So they push him out... what they had done in the last several months would ensure that this promotion would never reach the popularity it once had. Promotions like Action... like the Asylum... like 21Wrestling... like IOW... these promotions had taken the lead after Jim Dudley had delivered the corpse of the once giant in the world of professional wrestling into their hands. How many of them would welcome him in? How many of them would make him their champion? He suspected they all would. After all, he was the most powerful man in wrestling. He was the one who had done what thousands had dreamed of doing. He had killed the fWo. Jim Dudley stepped into the ring and held the championship belt over his head. The fans may have been booing, but Dudley didn't hear them. So fine... he wasn't in charge anymore. But he was leaving the champion, and the thought brought a smile to his face. Cactus Jon and David Spencer looked at the crowd around them with concern. Last week a crazy man... a man they had driven to insanity had entered the ring and shot Lance Star... killed him, but it could have very easily been one of them. It was close, and they were nervous. Eyes darted around at the crowd, wondering how many of them had murder in their hearts. "This title... this championship belt... this is the greatest prize in the game. No matter what you hear anywhere else, this is the fWo World Heavyweight Championship. People who hold this title are legends... and no other promotion can claim with honesty what everyone knows. He who holds the fWo World Heavyweight Championship... rules... the... world." He waited, and the response from the crowd was much what he expected. They shouted, the screamed... but he knew that they knew. He knew that no matter what they told themselves when they went home that night... they had seen the champion tonight. They had seen him. He smiled, but the smile sunk from his face when he looked up at the top of the entrance ramp. Travis Beaven stood there, along with Terry Nodar. So Beaven was in charge again. Well, fine. They could take his title from him... but it would be just that. Taking, and everyone would know it was tainted. The smile returned to his face and Dudley extended both of his arms. "Well? Come on then... do what you have to do. Strip the champion of the title... these fans are really excited to see that. Aren't you?" The fans booed, and Dudley's smile extended still wider. But as he waited and neither Travis nor Terry made any attempt to come to the ring his smile faded once again. Terry was holding a clipboard in one hand, and now handed it to Travis. "Congratulations Jim, you've held that title several times... more than most in the fWo. For that, you should be commended. It's an honor to hold that belt, and it's something that we all take very seriously. You, me, the fans... we all respect that belt, and it's for that reason that you're not going to be stripped of the belt. No, that title is won and lost in the ring. That's the way it should be, and that's the way it will be." "Oh, is that so?" Jim Dudley's smile broadened. "Well then Beaven, you've played into my hands. Because you see, I'm not going to just wrestle anyone. No, I know how your mind works. I know what you would like to do. Throw me in a handicap match against Ross, Kelly and the Stranger maybe? Handcuff me and make me wrestle Brand Frontier in a strap match? Or is it a cage match against your buddy Fallen and some of his friends? No Beaven, when I ran this promotion I made sure to include a little clause that title matches are determined by me. My stipulations, and only my stipulations. And guess what? The stipulation I choose is one on one... and with an opponent that isn't part of an active wrestling roster. Any roster. And that goes for your little CWL too, Rudo. Jesus Christ, you always were a pathetic little jobber." Terry's expression didn't change, and was he mistaken or did he see a slight smile on Beaven's face? Travis shrugged. "I don't agree with how you've chosen to defend your belt, but it is your choice to make Dudley. So fine... nobody on an active roster. One on one. Is that all?" Dudley smirked. "That's all, you bastard... that's all. So go find yourself a has-been, some washed up nobody who doesn't belong in the ring and bring him down here. Bring me some glory hound who wants another run at the title so I can prove once again who is the man... who is the king... who is the ruler of the world." Terry snorted and leaned over to whisper to Travis. "Has been? Christ, why don't we just get him a mirror to wrestle?" Travis smiled. "It's just what you thought it would be. So get his opponent prepped, and I hope you're right about Dudley's ego getting the better of him."
