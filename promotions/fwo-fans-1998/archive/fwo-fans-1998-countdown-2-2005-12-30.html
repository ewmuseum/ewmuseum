A fan reached out, and Flyer slapped his hand away, then shoved Miles forward, toppling the guard rail and sending them both into the crowd. Fans scattered... running for their lives. As Flyer got to his feet, a fan ran forward and began to pelt Miles with kicks to the chest. Growling, Flyer shoved the fan out of the way, ripping his shirt off as he spun backward. Holding the black shirt in one fist, he shoved it down onto Miles, wrapping it tightly around his neck. Miles gagged, straining... and then noticed. The shirt had a white circle... and a white 'f'. He blinked, and then he began to scream. High Flyer didn't let go. Miles slowly watched... ...his entire world go black. He felt nothing more. The bell rang. Flyer's fist went into the air. In his dreams... Miles saw the darkness coming. And was helpless to stop it. Result: High Flyer by Pinfall
