The FanTron clicks on and a standard 'office' scene comes to view. Fake plants stand beside a recently hung picture of the fWo 'Legends in the Making' poster, a desk placed in the corner of the makeshift office with various desk accessories is vacant. The buzz in the crowd grows and then a shadow is seen off camera, pacing it seems. Finally the cause of the shadow steps in front of the camera. Seemingly out of place in the role he's taken. Silver Fox, dressed for the event in a nice gray suit and blue tie, looks to the camera as cheers wash over the arena. Nodding his acceptance of the accolades Fox clears his throat and if anyone would take the time to notice it would seem the age lines on his face have deepened. Was this job finally taking it's toll on him? How exactly does the man known as Silver Fox deal with the mounting pressures he has put on himself for the love of this sport, for the love of this federation. Time would tell. All business though, now that he's stepped in front of the camera Silver finally addresses everyone. "Ladies and gentlemen, I'm sure you have already been welcomed by Chris Finn and the rest of the broadcast team but I would also like to say thanks. Welcome to fWo's Legends pay-per-view. "For the past few weeks Kellen Kinkade," Fox pauses as the audience jeer loudly, "Vic Creed," the hatred grows even louder, "Lance Knight," cheers for once, "and Brand Frontier," The crowd pops big time, "have decided that their personal vendettas were more important than the fact they were on the shows to compete, to wrestle. The diehard fans in attendance and surely those watching around the world have a surge of electricity wash over them as Fox continues. "So on that note, and after a lot of deliberation, I have made some changes to the matches of these four men. Matter of fact, I guess you can say I've canceled said matches..." "Kellen Kinkade versus Lance Knight for the Unites States Championship will not happen tonight." More negative reactions. "Brand Frontier versus Vic Creed for THE World Championship is also... canceled." The crowd nearly revolts. Fox waves his hands downwards in a calming motion, trying to finish off his little speech as quickly as possible, to get out from in front of the cameras once again. "What will happen however is the first ever title versus title contest of this fashion. That's right. Kellen Kinkade versus Brand Frontier versus Vic Creed versus Lance Knight... in a Sixty Minute, Iron Man, Championship versus Championship match-up!" The rabid fWo fans in attendance quite simply erupt. These four men, all having faced each other multiple times over this tumultuous past year would now be stuck in the same ring with one another, with two of the hottest prizes ever on the line. "Now, to get the details out of the way and get on with the show," Fox continues hurriedly. "when the match has finally reached the 45 minute mark there will be a bell rang. The wrestler with the most pin falls or submissions at the point will be crowned the United States Champion. The match will then be restarted but the pin fall/ submission count will be held over. At the 60 minute mark the wrestler with the most wins over all will then be declared the undisputed fWo World Heavyweight Champion!" Fox nods as the audience stomps their feet and cheer their agreement. Silver Fox had just made fWo, nay, wrestling history and the Legends audience couldn't be happier. As the FanTron fades away with Fox looking on it finally began to sink in. Exactly who would survive this brutal affair, forget hardcore, forget cage matches or dog collar matches, this was four men who in various forms or another had been engaged in hotly contested confrontations over the past year thrown together to fight over possibly the two richest prizes in wrestling history.
