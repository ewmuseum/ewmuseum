Sunday Slaughter, 4 June 2000

4 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rate me baby one more time. "Britney Spears, Finn?" came out of the mouth of Finn' right hand man and bastard in general, James Armstrong. Finn shrugged. "What... with our sagging ratings as of late... I figured we could use any publicity we could get, James. So, I placed a few phone calls, did a few favors, and here we are. Besides, I thought you liked her." Armstrong stammered. "Yeah, I do... but not her music. Don't get me wrong, I'd love to 'hit' her one more time, if you read what the James is writin' - but damn... like Slugger said, Finn. She has two talents... the left one and the right one." Finn grinned, before standing up. "Exactly. And I intend to use the left one and the right one to draw as many viewers as possible. But I'm concerned with tonight's show. We're going up against Game Seven of the National Basketball Association's Western Conference Finals between the LA Lakers and the Portland TrailBlazers. We're also going against Sunday Night Heat, and we're also running up against some other... shows... or something. 60 Minutes or some crap like that. Anyways, I'm confident with what we have planned for the next several shows - starting with tonight - that we will see a big spike in the ratings." That said, the opening montage for Sunday Night Slaughter began. Matt Douglas (c) vs. Crusader fWo Sunday Night Slaughter took to the airwaves in style with the challenger for tonight's Cruiserweight Title Match coming to the ring. "Switchback" by Biohazard echoed throughout the Pepsi Arena in Albany, New York. Crusader came to the ring, and exchanged "high fives" with the fans, as he clearly was the favorite. Inside of the ring, the two men meet face to face, and as a sign of respect to one another, they shake hands. Then, they lock up, and Douglas immediately slaps on an armbar. Crusader is able to flip out of it and turn Douglas over with an armdrag. Crusader bounces against the ropes, and runs at Douglas. He attempts a huracanrana, but Douglas catches him. Douglas flips Crusader in mid-air, and brings him down to the mat, face-first. Douglas climbs on with a cover, but the newcomer gets a shoulder up at 2. Douglas punches Crusader, knocking him into the turnbuckles. With Crusader stunned, Douglas steps to the outside. Douglas climbs the turnbuckles, and sits himself on the stunned Crusader's shoulders. He turns it into a huracanrana attempt, but Crusader holds him up there, and takes him down to the mat with a running power bomb. Instead of covering, Crusader grabs Douglas by the hair, and throws him to the outside. Crusader climbs the turnbuckles, and comes off with a corkscrew plancha. It connects, and both he and Douglas go flying into the barrier at ringside. Crusader ends up on top, and pounds Douglas with a barrage of punches. Crusader picks him up, and looks to whip him into the other guardrail, but Douglas reverses it. Douglas sends Crusader into the guardrail, and then runs at him. Crusader backbody drops Douglas over the rail, but Douglas lands upright. Douglas balances himself on the guardrail. Douglas jumps on Crusader's back, and takes him over with a huracanrana. Douglas rolls Crusader back into the ring, and climbs the turnbuckles. Douglas comes off with a perfectly executed guillotine leg drop. However, instead of covering, Douglas climbs the turnbuckles once again. Douglas comes off with a sentonbomb, but Crusader is able to get out of the way at the last second. Douglas pops up, and walks into a sky-high. Crusader runs against the ropes, and then hits Douglas with an Asai Moonsault. Finally, Crusader points to the skies, before climbing the turnbuckles. With Douglas in a vulnerable position, Crusader flies off with the Gravity 101, but misses. Crusader hits the mat full force, and this buys Douglas some time to recover. Douglas goes to the top rope, and prepares to fly off with "The Impact", and he does. Crusader catches him in midair however right into a sitdown powerbomb. He then brings him up and hits a tornado DDT, before going for the cover. Three seconds later, and Crusader wins his first title in his young tenure thus far in the fans Wrestling organization. The referee awards him the title, and Crusader holds it high overhead for everyone to see. He then straps it around his waist, and helps Matt Douglas to his feet, before the two embrace on a hard fought match. Slaughter proceeds to a commercial break, with one major thing having occured already... Result: Crusader by pinfall Eavesdropping... Slaughter proceeded to go backstage and find some interesting things going on in the back. Courage walked down a long stretch of hallway, past several doors, several of which were open or slightly open. One of the doors slightly opened happened to be the office of Finn & Armstrong. As Courage got closer to the door, he could hear Finn & Armstrong talking. With them unaware that Courage is listening in, they continued their conversation. "Crusader' merchandise is selling, he's drawing ratings, he's even got his own section of Crusader fans in every arena we attend. He's been a brillant pick-up, James... in fact he's the best cruiserweight newcomer since... Courage. And in my own opinion, he's better than Courage... and c'mon now, Courage is pretty popular." Armstrong scoffed, but agreed tentatively, saying that Crusader should hand Courage' testicles to him in a jar after kicking his ass someday. Finn balked. "You know, Crusader may be the man to take this promition into the future..." Armstrong disagreed. "Ever hear of Pierre Delacroix? Yes? Call him. Bring him back. That's why our ratings are dropping... there's no Frenchie on our shows." Finn shrugged. "Whatever." Suddenly, Courage barges into the office. "What do you mean Crusader may be the man to take this promotion into the future? Hello?! I'm Courage, baby... and along with Primetime and Lance Star, we're the Future Foundation. Say it with me. FUTURE FOUNDATION. Not French Foundation, because let's face it, without Courage & Primetime... the French Foundation absolutely sucked! I'm ratings, baby! I equal rock and roll. Crusader wouldn't know rock and roll if it bit him on the ass, baby. In fact... I could beat that idiot in under thirty seconds." Courage seemed somewhat reluctant about saying this, and upon completing that sentence, Finn chuckled loudly. "Courage, you've got yourself a deal.. if you can beat Crusader in under 30 seconds.. then you'll officially be the greatest talent we've ever employed. However, if you lose Courage, then you'll be wrestling the next ten houseshows against talent such as The Abusah, Scott Hart and the loveable, Mike Heftel. Got it?" Courage nodded, but not before reminding Finn that sooner or later Crusader would get what was coming to him... but until then... Britney Spears was going to get what was coming to her. "Finn baby, you've got yourself a deal. Crusader is going to find out why Courage equals rock and roll, baby. And sooner or later, he is going to get what is coming to him. But in the meantime... Britney Spears... good lord, she can't keep her hands off me, you know... but who can, baby? Anyways... she's going to get what's coming to her right now." Finn disagreed. "Oh, no she isn't, Courage. Since you're in your wrestling gear right now, I figure you can use a warm-up to prepare for that match with Crusader. Go find Primetime, tell him to get ready... because he's going to be wrestling too." Courage stormed out of the office angry, in search of Primetime... Cactus Jon vs. Dark Ninja Slaughter rolled on with the return of Cactus Jon, who was greeted with a nice "welcome back" chant from the fans. Jon rolled into the ring and looked to be in the best physical condition he had been in in sometime, and awaited his opponent of the evening. He soon found out who his first opponent would be in his return to the fWo, and the man was no stranger to Jon. It was the Dark Ninja, and fWo fans would note that there was a bit of history between the two. The Ninja entered the ring and he and Jon wasted no time in locking up. Ninja got the early advantage with a side headlock, before Jon backed him into a corner. The referee requested a clean break, and one occured. The two locked up again, and this time Jon took the advantage with a side headlock. Ninja backed Jon into a corner, and the referee requested a clean break. Jon released Ninja, but Ninja replied with a chop to the chest, and another, before whipping Jon into the opposite corner. Jon smacked the turnbuckles, and Ninja charged in with an elbow. Jon grabbed him by the arm however and worked his way into an armbar, but Ninja escaped, bounced off the ropes and returned for a crucifix. He hit it, and the referee went into position to count. Jon kicked out easily at one and a half, and the two rushed back up to a vertical base. Jon sent Ninja into the ropes and as Ninja returned he was taken down with a lariat. Jon then brought him up and swung him into the ropes again. Ninja returned and Jon hit a powerslam. He went for another cover, but Ninja kicked out. Jon brought Ninja up once again and took him to the mat with a snapmare takeover, before backing up and kicking him in the back of the head. Jon brought him up again and whipped him into the turnbuckles, before charging in. As he did he was met with a jumping kick to the chest, which sent Jon to the mat. Ninja noticed he did some real damage to the chest of Jon, so he attacked the area in question. Ninja brought him up and pummeled him with some forearms to the chest, before sending him into the turnbuckles with impending authority. Jon crashed into the corner sternum first, and as he staggered out Ninja turned him around and hooked him for a belly-to-belly suplex. Ninja then brought him up and judo chopped him into the chest area several times, before sending him into the ropes and as Jon returned he was met with 'The Final Wrath', a jumping thrust kick to the jaw. This sent Jon into unconsciousness, and Ninja quickly covered him for the pin. Ninja came away with an upset victory, as it was nearly a foregone conclusion that there was no way Jon would lose this match. Ninja celebrated as he went backstage, while several referees came out to the ring to attend to Jon... Result: Ninja by pinfall Challenges... Slaughter continued backstage where Scott Slugger entered the arena in style. His Dodge Viper pulled outside the Pepsi Arena, and he stopped the car, before exiting the vehicle. He began to walk inside the arena, and was greeted by Jeremiah Frankton, who began interviewing the World's Greatest Athlete, while the camera man focused in for a close-up. "I'm here with Scott Slugger, and Scott, I have one question. You are not scheduled to be here tonight, you're not scheduled to wrestle, yet you're already in your wrestling gear. Why?" Slugger looked at Frankton as if he had a brain that didn't function properly. "Jer, I am here for one reason. I'm sick of the Dudes With Attitude trying to get under my skin, they're trying to piss me off to the point where I start making irrational and illogical decisions. Well, I'm not going to do that, but what I am going to do is make a challenge here tonight. The World's Greatest Athlete is always up for a good challenge, and being a four-time fWo World Champion, this is going to be one heckuva challenge, even I will have to admit that. I'm challenging the Two Dudes With Attitudes to a handicap match right here tonight. I want to kick both of their asses at the same time. And for anyone to think that the WGA can't do it is simply fooling themselves. For anyone to say that Scott Slugger can't do it... well, all I have to do is point to my track record, because it speaks for itself. I've done nearly everything there is to do in the fWo... and on that alone I can kick their asses any day of the week, today just happens to be Sunday... and Scott Slugger never takes a day off. Mitch, Ice... your asses belong to me in a short awhile, and I promise you... you won't feel pleasure. Because I'm going to unleash all the fury and frustration out on you, and when I'm done with you two you'll know what it's like to have a bad attitude." Slugger pushed Frankton aside as he made his way further into the arena... meanwhile... Primetime (fWo Internet Champion) vs. Courage (fWo International Champion) vs. Lance Star Sunday Night Slaughter returned to in arena action as "Last Resort" by Papa Roach began to play, and out walked the trio who claimed to be the future of the fWo and professional wrestling. Primetime wore the fWo Internet Title over his right shoulder, while Courage wore his fWo International Title over his left shoulder. Lance Star followed the two men with no titles on his shoulders, but he didn't care. He was focused... on the task at hand, and it appeared that tonight he would be taking on Courage & Primetime, his two closest friends. The trio entered the ring and posed for the fans, and most of the response The Future Foundation received were boos, although there were scattered cheers throughout the crowd. The Future Foundation ignored the boos, and Primetime & Courage handed their titles to the referee, who signalled that both titles were on the line tonight in this match. This infuriated Primetime & Courage, who questioned the referee's insanity and wondered if he wanted to live. Lance however pulled them aside and calmed them down, and the two came up with a plan. Primetime instructed the referee to ring the bell, before the three young athletes circled each other, preparing to lock-up. Just as they were about to make some form of contact, they stopped. Courage walked over to Lance and pushed him with a single finger, and Lance fell hard to the mat. Courage then pinned him with a single pinky finger, before the referee awarded the match to Courage. Since Courage did not pin Primetime, Primetime retained the fWo Internet Title. Courage & Primetime helped Lance Star up, before the three men gave the fans - who were really looking forward to the sight of the Future Foundation exploding right before our very eyes - collective middle fingers. The fans began to boo once again, and the Foundation once again ignored the negative response from the crowd. As Slaughter went to another commercial break, the Future Foundation continued to pose... Result: Courage by pinfall Looking For A Fight. Erik Kelly roamed around backstage looking for Neo. "NEO?!" shouted the monster of a man, who was in search of a man who in his opinion spoke too much. He screamed his name again, and again, before finally, Neo appeared. Neo responded. "Yes?" Kelly stormed over in his direction, and swung a backhand. Fortunately for Neo, he ducked out of the way, and his head remained intact on his neck. Neo tried to calm him down, but Kelly wouldn't have any of it. Kelly grabbed him by the collar. "You swiney bastard... I'm going to rip your head off and..." Suddenly, James Armstrong appeared on the scene. "Calm down Kelly, or fines may have to be levied." Kelly let go of Neo, not wanting anything to do with fines this early in his career in the fWo. Armstrong continued. "Now, what appears to be the problem?" Armstrong asked this question to either Neo or Kelly, and didn't mind who responded. Neo spoke first. "I don't know, James. Here I am minding my own business backstage, when I here this guy screaming my name. I haven't been around for weeks, Kelly. What's your problem?" Kelly' face turned crimson red. He clinched his fists, before raising his voice. "My problem is you. I heard what you said on Warfare, and what you said in your most recent promo. Do you have a death wish, boy?" asked the fWo Hardcore Champion. Neo again downplayed it, acting as if he had no idea of what Kelly was talking about. "Erik... with all due respect, I don't have a clue of what you're speaking about. I've been gone for about a month, I just returned to night to get back into action." Kelly grumbled. Armstrong stepped in between both men. "Look... Neo says he hasn't been around in weeks, and I haven't seen him, Erik. You claim he's been talking trash about you... I haven't seen anything from Neo lately. No disrespect intended, but..." Kelly looked at Armstrong with disdain and disgust. He pushed Neo away and headed for Armstrong. This gave Neo his chance. From behind, Neo nailed him with a weak double axehandle. Kelly acted as if a mosquito had just bitten him... acting as if the shot had no affect. He turned around, and Neo was gone. Kelly screamed out "POWERBOMB!" before heading in the direction of Armstrong, but as he did, several wrestlers intervened, making sure the big man didn't get himself fined... or suspended. Acceptance. Ice & Mitch Wilson sat in their lockeroom looking at a monitor, and watching the Slugger challenge that aired earlier. As Slugger continued to talk trash, Ice & Wilson turned the television off. Ice looked at the camera. "Sluggsy, we know what you were going to say because you're so bland and boring... that we decided to turn the television off and just guess what you had said. You go first, Mitch." A rare grin came over the face of Mitch, who looked at Ice. "You know, Ice... I bet he did a lot of trash talking, stammered out some catchphrases, and talked about how sick he was of us." Ice grinned as well. "And I bet he went on and on about how great he is, and how we sick, and he probably gave us a silly nickname as well." Mitch concurred. "And I'm sure he probably made a challenge... and being the big dumbass that he is he probably challenged both of us to a handicap match... and you know what, we're in an asskicking type of mood tonight, so we'll kindly accept." As Chris Finn walked by he managed to hear this comment, before he interjected. "Not so fast. I'm the owner of the fWo and with the possibility of The Boston Strangler missing some action for awhile as a result of his injury last week on Warfare, I can't afford to have you two hooligans injure Slugger as well, since he's an integral part of not only the main event at our next pay-per-view, but our company as a whole. That said, he'll have a partner at his side tonight, whether he likes it or not or whether you like it or not. Good luck with your match." Finn walked away, while Ice & Mitch shrugged it off. "Partner or not... tonight, Scotty... you're going to feel pain." Ice and Wilson walked away, while Slaughter continued backstage. Injury Report. Finn walked back to his office, where he was given some papers by Matt Loco. Finn looked at the report for several moments, before scratching his head. "Boy, Eron sure did do a number on The Strangler, eh?" Loco nodded. Finn shrugged. "Well... this report says he's going to be out of action for another week. I had a big match planned for Warfare that involved him... and now that can't happen. This report says he can't wrestle... but it doesn't say he can't appear. Find him. Go visit him, Loco. Make sure he's at Warfare." Loco nodded, before rushing out of the office. Finn sat down and turned his attention to a nearby television, which showed the Los Angeles Lakers celebrating. "God damn Lakers. Well, here's to Indiana stomping their asses to hell and back in the Finals." Finn then pounded his desk, before the last person Finn wanted to see entered the office. Eron The Relentless. Eron entered without knocking, and nearly scared Finn right out of his chair. Finn looked at Eron with disgust, and Eron returned the look, before grinning. "What do you want?" asked the grumpy Finn. Eron replied. "One thing. Let me be Slugger' partner. Please. I beg of you, and you know how much I hate beggars." Finn seemed hesitant. "No... I've already decided who will be Slugger' partner. You... well, I do have something you can do... go get your wrestling gear on... you're up next." Eron refused. "No one tells me what to do, Finn... nobody." Finn gave him a look of authority. "You want to remain in that main event at Critical Condition, Eron? Hell, I should pull you out of it for trying to injure the fWo World Champion and our top star. You'll either be out there for that match, or you'll be out of the main event. It's as simple as that." Eron looked at Finn hatefully, before kicking over a water cooler as he walked out. Finn screamed, but it was too late... Eron was gone. Slaughter went to a set of commercials... Erik Kelly (c) vs. Eron The Relentless Slaughter resumed as "Physical" by Nine Inch Nails began to play, and out from the backstage area emerged currently the most hated man in the fWo, Eron The Relentless. The fans loudly jeered a man who comprised one third of the fWo World Title Match set to take place in twenty days. Eron brushed off their reaction as he climbed into the ring, before walking to a nearby corner and slumping. "No Leaf Clover" by the bastards who sued Napster begins to play, and out walks the fWo Hardcore Champion, Erik Kelly. Kelly stormed to the ring in an obvious bad mood, and as he entered Eron just added to it. Eron bent over and appeared ready to show Kelly a full moon, but Kelly quickly charged and big booted him right in the ass, thus sending him flying shoulder first into the ring post. Eron staggered out and Kelly chokeslammed him once, and then again, before preparing for The Eradication. As he did, Neo appeared at ringside wielding a metal pipe. Neo slid into the ring and then yelled at Kelly, who turned around. Neo charged with the pipe, but Kelly grabbed him by the neck and chokeslammed him as well. Kelly then prepared to take the liberty of kicking Neo' ass, but from behind came Eron with the pipe. He slammed it into the back of Kelly, who crashed forward. Neo then stood up and it appeared it was time for a double team. Neo grabbed Kelly up by the hair and whipped him in the direction of Eron, but Kelly reversed the Irish Whip. Eron accidentlly nailed Neo with the metal pipe, and then appeared to begin apologizing for his mistake. Before he could, Kelly grabbed him and hooked him for The Eradication... but it wasn't a regular powerbomb, he brought him near the ropes and dropped him onto the floor. Eron hit the floor hard, and then Kelly climbed over the top rope and retrieved the injured & prone body of Eron. He then rolled him into the ring and pinned him, before turning his attention to Neo. Bam. Just as he was getting up, he was met with the metal pipe yet again, this time to the head. Neo then gave Eron a look of disgust, before exiting to an enormous amount of boos, while Slaughter went backstage one last time this evening... Result: Kelly by pinfall Getting Away Tired of being on camera again this evening, Finn directed Jeremiah Frankton to go find the Future Foundation and pass them along a message. Frankton wrote down the message before exiting the office, and making a long journey to find The Future Foundation. He looked in their lockerroom, but had no luck. He looked outside for their custom Future Foundation limosuine, but had no luck there, either. Frankton passed the word along to the guys in the back he was looking for the Foundation, and if anyone saw them to tell them that Finn needed to speak to them. As Frankton got back to the office of Finn he was now winded, and told Finn that he couldn't find the Foundation anywhere. Finn shrugged, and then finally muttered something about having to call Britney Spears. Frankton exited the office, but the camera man remained. Finn asked the party on the other end of the phone to speak with a "Ms. Spears", before noticing the camera man was still present. Finn directed the camera man to leave, and the camera man obliged... before we headed back to the ring for our main event of the evening. Scott Slugger & ??? vs. Bad Attitude "Bad Attitude" by Deep Purple roared throughout the arena, and out onto the stage stepped "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson and "The Big Blonde Machine" known as Ice. The two men were welcomed into Albany, New York with a big New Yorker's welcome, as they were greeted with boos, jeers, catcalls and other unnice gestures. Ice & Wilson didn't care, because this was home. Two men with bad attitudes, and not caring what the rest of the world had to say. Ice & Wilson entered the ring, and their music was replaced by "Back In Black" by AC/DC. The fans erupted in cheers, because much to their liking the World's Greatest Athlete was about to make an appearance. Pyrotechnics exploded on the stage, while the former four-time fWo World Champion made his grandoise entrance. He posed on the ramp as the ovation of cheers went from loud to earsplitting, and Slugger walked to the ring alone without a partner, contrary to what Finn had said earlier. Slugger stood at ringside while his music faded, and he stared at Ice & Wilson hatefully before "My Hero" by Foo Fighters began to play. Out came The Black Panther, and it appeared the Panther would be the tag team partner of Scott Slugger tonight because of what he did on Warfare last week. Slugger awaited him at ringside, and finally the Panther reached the ringside area. Slugger took it upon himself to start things off, while Wilson did for his team as well. Panther & Ice took to their respective corners, the referee signaled for the bell, and this match was underway. Slugger & Wilson locked up in the center of the ring with a collar and elbow tie-up, before Wilson gained the quick advantage with a side headlock. Rather than keep the move for long he positioned into a hammerlock, and applied pressure. Since Slugger was in position, Wilson decided to quickly go for his finisher 'The Headliner', but Slugger escaped. Slugger and Wilson locked up again, this time Slugger taking the advantage into an armbar. He then worked over the arm of Wilson for several moments, before sending him to the ropes. Wilson returned and Slugger took him down with a Japanese Armdrag, before following it up with a dropkick. Slugger tagged out to Panther, and the duo sent Wilson into the turnbuckles. Wilson staggered out and Panther took him down with a lariat, before bringing him up and hitting a hanging vertical suplex. He then brought him up again and nailed a backbreaker, before going for a cover. Ice interrupted the count at two, and Panther tagged out to Slugger. Slugger came in and worked over Wilson some more, first hitting a belly-to-back suplex, before a very fast snap suplex. Slugger went for a cover, but Wilson was able to kick out at two. Slugger sent Wilson into the ropes before ducking for a backdrop, but Wilson came back and kicked him in the chest, which sent Slugger sprawling backwards. Wilson then charged and took him down with a flying forearm, and tagging out to Ice, who came in looking to stomp a mudhole in the ass of Slugger and walk it dry. He first brought Slugger up and pounded him with forearms before shooting him into the ropes. Slugger returned and he was taken down hard with a spinebuster. Ice went for a cover, but Slugger kicked out at two. Ice brought Slugger up and sent him into the ropes, before charging in with force and speed and slamming into Slugger with a clothesline. He then brings Slugger out from the corner and body slams him to the mat, before bringing him up once more. He tags out to Wilson, and Wilson goes to the top rope. Ice hits a Northern Lights Suplex, before positiong Slugger closely enough for Wilson to execute an aerial move. Wilson flies off with a frogsplash, before hooking the leg. Panther comes in and interrupts the count. Wilson brings up Slugger and attempts another 'headliner', but Slugger counters and pushes Wilson into his corner, which knocks Ice off the ring apron. Wilson staggers out and Slugger grabs him, before hooking him and hitting the Curveball. Slugger is dazed, and is now crawling towards his corner, trying to tag out to the Panther. Panther is reaching, trying to make the tag, when all of a sudden, the camera captures an arm reaching up and grabbing Panther by the leg, before pulling him off the ring apron. Slugger doesn't see this as he was in a crawling position with his head facing the mat, as he was trying to get closer to the corner. He looks up and sees Panther gone, and a moment later he's in the air as a result of Ice grabbing him by the neck for a chokeslam, aka the Icepick. Slugger is sent into the mat with such a velocity that it appears Ice just broke his back. Ice then rolls Wilson atop Slugger, before the referee counts the pin, and Ice & Wilson are victorious. Ice & Wilson exit the ring, and as the camera focuses on the two men it finds Eron, escaping from ringside. Ice and Wilson glare at Panther as he pulls himself up via aide of the ring apon, while Slaughter goes off the air, with the announcers reminding everyone at home to tune into Warfare for the huge appearance of pop sensation Britney Spears. Result: Ice & Mitch Wilson by pinfall
