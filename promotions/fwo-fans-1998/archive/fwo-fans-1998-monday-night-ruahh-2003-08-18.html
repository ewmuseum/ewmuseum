The crowd lulled into a slow chorus of boos as the lethal Koji Tamura walked stealthily to the ring. Silent as always he directed his eyes to the floor and kept himself focused. Tonight it was about the belt. Tonight he had a chance at fWo gold. Suddenly it became difficult for Koji to concentrate. As he lifted his head his eyes were hit with a cloud of aqua glitter that dropped from the ceiling. "Around the World" resonated off the walls as the Bowmaniacs in the crowd shouts their praise to the fWo Cruiserweight champion, Scotty J. Bowman. Bowman cupped his hand to his ear as another famous blonde once did and listened to the support of his fans. It was nice to hear such a positive reaction after such a hard night on Thursday. Bowman had lost to a man he held little respect for, and his title reign was now in doubt. He entered the ring as an unspoken underdog, which is not how a champion should feel before an upcoming battle, a battle that was scheduled without words three days ago. As Bowman passed by a young female fan in the front row she waved her hands in the air and fainted. Bowman shrugged his shoulders and smiled. He took another two steps before two frantic teenage male fans caught a close-up glimpse of their hero and fainted as well. Bowman was a little creeped out and quickly slid into the ring. Scotty J. snapped his fingers and "Big" Bobby, his 7'2'' manager who had slipped down to the ring unnoticed beneath all the glamour tossed his cousin a microphone. "Koji, I know you and I got off on the wrong foot." The calm and determined martial artist stood solid and emotionless. Bowman continued. "I think it's because I don't understand your culture But I watched 'Mulan' last night, and I think I see it now. Inside that body of a strong Japanese martial artist, you're just a small Chinese girl, fighting for freedom, or liberty, or something... Ok, I didn't really watch Mulan, I watched 'Weekend at Bernies,' but I think the message is the same, right?" Koji shook his head. It's best not to read too much into Bowman's speeches. "So anyway, now that we've come to an understanding, how about you and I call this whole little spat off? Huh? I'll even throw in this NEW 'Bowmaniac #1' t-shirt, straight off the line! It retails for $19.95! You can't beat that value!" Tamura took the shirt from Bowman's outstretched hand and tore it in two shreds without so much as glancing anywhere but SJB's eyes. Bowman, outraged, dove at Tamura with a forearm, but the martial artist blocked the maneuver and drove a stiff side kick into Bowman's side. Bowman hunched over and Tamura drove the base of his palm into the Cruiserweight champion's jaw. Tamura followed up with a knee lift and took Bowman over his shoulder. Tamura dropped Bowman on his back with a powerful spinebuster. Tamura dropped down and applied a Victor Cross armlock which sent Bowman howling. Bowman kicked his legs and inched towards the bottom rope but Tamura applied more pressure. From the outside "Big" Bobby discretely took hold of his cousin's ankle and dragged his foot over to the side, allowing SJB to casually place it over the rope. The referee called for the hold to be released and Tamura complied. Bowman struggled to one knee as Tamura stood up. Tamura leaned over for a strike but Bowman dove under his hands and drove a shoulder into Koji's shin, bringing the man down on his back. Bowman raised Tamura's leg in the air and dropped an elbow on his knee. Bowman continued the offense by swinging around and applying a sleeper hold to Koji as he sat up. Bowman held his arms tight as Koji swatted his arms about, trying to beat the Cruiserweight champion off. OF HIS NECK. BEAT HIM OFF OF HIS NECK. Get your head out of the gutter. Sheesh. As the fight was wrung out of Tamura, the referee held his arm into the air and dropped it to the ground. A second time. A third time -- but instead of falling limp to the floor, Koji's arm shot up, and he drove his fingers directly into Bowman's eye. Bowman released the hold at once, falling backwards and clutching his hand to his eye. Koji took a moment to regain his breath and positioned himself in fighting stance. SJB wiped the water out of his eye just in time to watch Koji's hand fly into his face with a back mounted punch. Then Tamura's foot was raised for a spin kick, a step kick, another, and another. For the moment Tamura used Bowman as his own personal punching (and kicking) bag, hitting a barrage of strikes. Finally, Bowman was hit with a Cyber Kick Rush that put him out on the mat. Tamura leaned in for a cover. 1... 2... Kickout. Bowman held his shoulder up and tried to escape the clutches of Tamura but the rigorous Japanese fighter smelled blood and continued the onslaught. Koji stepped to the side and drove his foot hard into Bowman with a back brain axe-kick that dropped the champ once more. Tamura eased down to one knee and began to strike down upon SJB's sternum with rapid-fire punches. Bowman fell lifeless again and was hit with a cover. 1... 2... No. Bowman lifted his shoulder to the air at the last second. Koji was so close to the belt he could already feel it around his waist. He lifted Bowman up by his blonde hair and lifted the top of his foot up for a kick into Bowman's neck. Tamura then drove his shoulder into Bowman's midsection and pushed Bowman against the ropes. Tamura lifted his leg for a thrust kick but Bowman avoided it and hit a dropkick into Koji's opposite knee. Koji fell to the mat and Bowman dropped an elbow. SJB listened to the crowd roar in anticipation of his comeback and bounced off the adjacent ropes before hitting a senton over Koji's chest. Bowman popped up and repeated the move over again and again. He ran so fast and hit the moves so quickly that he winded himself. Bowman struggled to pop off the mat after the final senton and Koji Tamura stood on his feet. Tamura lined Bowman up for a hard kick to the back of his head but was taken by surprise as Bowman slapped a small package on him for a quick pin! 1... 2... 3! No! Tamura, annoyed by the sneaky maneuver kicked out before the referee's hand hit the mat for a third time. Bowman and Tamura reached their feet simultaneously. The two locked up quickly, allowing Tamura to drive his elbow into Bowman. Bowman came back however with a knife edged chop that caused Tamura to backstep. SJB got himself into the kicking business as well with a spinning heel kick placed directly into Tamura's nose. Bowman pounced with a boot to Tamura's midsection and took hold of Koji's arms, before dropping him quickly with a double underhook powerbomb. The crowd roared as Bowman made another cover. 1... 2... Kickout by Tamura. Bowman slapped the mat in frustration. Bowman held Tamura up and drove a hard left hand into his face. Tamura dropped back and blocked the next punch before it reached him. Koji held Bowman by the wrist and pulled him into him while lifting his knee into the Cruiserweight champ's forehead. Bowman fell to one knee. Tamura swung again for a spinning heel kick but Bowman ducked under it. Tamura shot out his arm for a backhand but Bowman was ready and caught it. Bowman pulled Koji in and hoisted him up on his shoulders. Flashbulbs lit. Bowman spun Tamura and dropped him down on his neck. "The Bowmanator." Not taking any chances Bowman made a quick cover. 1... 2... 3. No. The crowd - in shock - watched as Koji Tamura raised his shoulder. Bowman bounced off the mat and held his hands in the air, seemingly triumphant. The referee quickly informed the champ that he had not yet successfully retained. Tamura was stirring, and finally raised himself to his knees. He was poised and ready for one final strike. He got to his feet and eyed his opponent. As he shot his boot into his jaw. Bowman was ready for Tamura with a perfectly timed 'Scotty J. Kick!' The cover. 1... 2... 3! The match was over. Tamura, lying motionless on the mat, had wasted his first opportunity at the belt. He began to wonder if he held himself back. The match was his and he failed to capitalize. There would be other nights, he thought, he decided, he hoped. "Around the World" played for a second time as the party began. Bowman, blowing a kiss to his female fans took hold of his belt and exhausted, raised his arms to the air. It was a hard fought win, and it almost wiped the memory of Thursday's failure away. Almost. Result: Scotty J.
