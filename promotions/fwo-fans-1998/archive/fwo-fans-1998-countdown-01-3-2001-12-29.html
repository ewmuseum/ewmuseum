California had been unseasonably cold, but cold weather in California is relative. As wrestlers drove to the arena, they took note of many fans dressed in shorts cheering them as they arrived. The Arco Arena in Sacramento was a historical arena for the fWo; for it had held a pay-per-view or two and some major historical things had occured there. But none more historical than tonight, the beginning of the third Countdown To Oblivion series. The past few months had been dark; with evil standing triumphantly over good. Little did anyone know that it was about to get worse. There was a light shining at the end of the tunnel but not very brightly. Still, some hope is better than no hope at all... but after tonight perhaps that hope would be all but gone. Impetus A week removed from one of the most violent nights in fWo history, a night where blood was shed, bodies more than broken and spirits shattered forever... the fans Wrestling organization zeroed in on Sacramento, California. Twilight had changed everything but nobody knew what Countdown would bring. Outside the arena came Lance Star hobbling on crutches, his leg having not fully healed from the injury sustained two weeks ago at Slaughter. Star slammed his car door shut before walking forward towards Arco; he still wasn't used to the injury but it was far better than being temporarily paralyzed like he had been in late 1999. Fans outside the arena greeted Star as the light turned to dark, the sun setting off to the west making way for the dark night. From behind came a madman rolling a wheelchair, all the while sporting a cleanly shaven head. His goatee hairs brustled in the wind. He violently shoved Lance Star into the wheelchair, before telling Star not to move or he would be hurt, and hurt bad. Little did Star know that he would suffer more pain tonight, regardless of the threat. The man pushed him into the arena, telling Star to remain silent as they neared the arena entrance. Several backstage workers raised their eyebrows, but didn't notice anything out of the ordinary. They should have. Austin Davis pushed Star out onto the stage as 'Down With The Sickness' by Disturbed began to play throughout the Arco Arena. Fans instantly rose out of their chairs, booing and yelling profanities at his appearance. Davis sneered at the fans as he proceeded towards the ring, before grabbing Star and pushing him into the squared circle. Star remained on the mat as Davis asked for a microphone. Davis walked back over, pausing to stare at the sea of people he did not care one iota for, before placing his boot hard into the neck of Lance Star and holding him down. His music faded, however, the boos did not. Davis quickly asked for a chair, and a ringside attendant tossed him to one in the ring. Davis ordered Star to the center of the ring and face the stage while remaining lying down facefirst. Once he did that Star placed the chair in a sitting position, one of the bars across Star' neck to keep him down. Davis took himself a seat, and several seconds passed before Davis finally began speaking above the crowd, emphasizing his first words to silence the audience. "What happened seven days ago at Twilight was just the beginning. What happened seven days ago was just a little taste out of what you can expect from Austin Davis. I sat in a hospital for months with machines making sounds; tubes plugged into me, doctors feeding pain medication into my body, shots given to me, and it allowed me to do some reflecting. And during this period of reflection I realized that although I was on my death bed... although I was very close to dying..." Davis paused as the fans briefly cheered at this statement, showing their obvious distaste for this man. "... that I had unfinished business in the fWo. So that one thought has been driving me to get back here... and now that I am, rest assured, business will be taken care of." Davis sneered as the fans reacted with jeers; before the former owner of the fWo continued. "And that brings us to here, that brings us to now. Chris Universal... I know you're back there, I saw you earlier this evening, and I know that you're going to come out here when I tell you to. Get your ass out here." Thirty seconds passed before Chris Universal, the owner of the fans Wrestling organization walked out onto the stage. Universal had complete power now, thanks in part to Davis' help at Twilight, but clearly Davis had ulterior motives. "First of all..." Davis began to speak, but Universal cut him off. "You listen here. You lay a hand on Star I promise you you'll never hear the end of it." Davis laughed. "Oh, is that so? Don't you remember that contract you signed me to? The only way you can fire me is if I were to kill someone in this ring or out of it, and I'm not going to kill Lance, but if I don't get what I want, I promise you leg injury or not... he'll never walk AGAIN." Davis' voice boomed throughout the arena, showers of jeers following while Universal looked on. "What I can't understand is how I paid for your medical bills... and you repay me by screwing Ruben Ross at Twilight? I always thought you were sick and twisted... and it doesn't surprise me that you did such a thing... but I can't believe how cold blooded you are." Davis considered that statement before looking down at the mat, scratching the side of his head slightly as a smile of irony crossed his face. "Let me explain to you something, Chris. Yeah, you paid for my medical bills and I appreciate that, but I already did you a favor. With me coming back to the fWo I figure... who would be better as my boss. Three washed up guys or you, someone I know and someone I can manipulate... that's right, I said manipulate into whatever I want. So it was very simple, the only choice was to help you out. But that's where the favors ended, Chris. Let me relate an old proverb back to you Chris. Maybe this will help you see things a little clear. Obviously I have a taste for snakes and I've been compared to snakes in the past. It goes something like this. An old woman was walking along a path and came across a poisonous snake. The snake appeared to be beaten... and wounded. She took the snake home and over many months nurtured it back to health. One day, when the woman was feeding the snake, it turned around and bit her, repeatedly. As the woman lay dying, she asked the snake why it would do such a thing after she had taken care of it... so the snake, with a sadistic smile replied and said... "Lady, you knew I was a snake when you helped me." Davis paused and let realization set in. The crowd fell silent, Universal bowed his head in shame. Davis smiled, and spoke loudly. "And that's exactly what happened here. I don't like you, I have never liked you, and I never will like you. You helped me because you needed a favor; I gave you that favor. But I didn't do it for you, I did it for me.. because I am Austin Davis, and I just spent four months in a hospital bed, with tubes and machines hooked up to my god damn body, sitting there second after second, minute after minute, hour after hour and day after day feeling helpless and unable to do a god damn thing about it. And the minute I get released from the hospital, I became the most sought after free agent in wrestling history. I appeared on three pay-per-views - including yours - in the span of twenty four hours, because all of you wanted to sign me to the largest deal in wrestling history. That's right. You can have comfort telling your Ruben Ross', your Wretch', and all the other pieces of crap on the roster that I'm now the most highest paid wrestler in the promotion. I'm sure they'll enjoy that. But back to my point. In the end it was you, who came crawling to me, wanting to sign me and you outbid two other promotions for me. And don't think for a second that I signed it because I wanted to come back here and help the fWo. I wanted to come back here and hurt people, end careers, maybe win a few titles and make some damn good money in the process. That leads us to tonight. You give me a World Title shot... or I give your friend Lance here a broken neck. And believe me, this time he won't walk again, let alone wrestle." Universal considered the alternative briefly. It was a no brainer; obviously he didn't wish to see Lance Star severely injured once again. The broken leg he had suffered was bad enough, and nobody will ever forget his unfortunate mishap in November of 1999 in a match against Scott Collins, leaving him temporarily paralyzed. With a stern tone, Universal granted him his wishes. "Alright, Davis, you'll get your World Title shot. Let him go... he can't even defend himself!" Davis chuckled. "Jump." Universal raised his eyebrows in confusion. "What?" Davis chuckled again. "No, no Chris. You see, the correct reply would have been 'How high?'. "No. Any individual with a sense of compassion would've made that choice. But you know what?" Universal walked towards the ring... feeling that he could get into the ring and get a piece of Davis before Davis tried to do something to Star. "You won't get your title shot." Universal neared the ring slowly, until Davis got off the chair; shoving it aside. Davis drove his knee deep in between the shoulderblades of the former Future Foundation member. Davis reached at his head with his right hand, holding the microphone with his left hand up to his mouth. A snarl came over the face of the three-time World Champion. "You move one more inch, and his neck will snap. You give me that title shot. I promise you. He will never walk again if you make one more move towards this ring." Universal realized that Davis was clearly serious about his threats, and Universal decided not to take any chances. Davis again gave him one more threat. "You give me that fWo World Title shot, or I will break his fucking neck." Davis dropped the microphone and put his hands around Star' head, Star reached for the ropes, trying to escape, but Davis drove an elbow into the top of his head. "Okay, you get the title shot. You have my word. I promise you, here, live on national television... my word. You will get an fWo World Title shot." Davis smiled. That was all he wanted. But he wasn't done. He looked down at the helpless Star, and laughed. Positioning his hands around the neck of Star in such a fashion that would surely do some damage, Davis twisted. Snap. Davis had seemingly broken Star' neck. Universal looked on in horror as Star' entire body fell limp, his head hitting the canvas as the crowd fell silent. Davis stood to his feet and stretched out his arms, telling Universal that he was the 'master of puppets'. Universal quickly rushed the ring and grabbed the chair, swinging at Davis and narrowly missing as Davis escaped to the outside. The silence turned to perhaps what could be considered the largest reaction of hatred a man had ever received inside an fans Wrestling organization ring. As Davis walked backwards up the ramp Universal checked on Star, before grabbing the microphone, incensed. "You bastard! You will pay for this Davis! You won't get that title shot tonight, no fucking way... not after what you just did!" Davis looked quite disturbed by this. "No, instead, tonight you're going to face someone you have a deep history with, someone that hates you just as much as you hate him. You're going to face... THE BOSTON STRANGLER!" This brought a large reaction of cheers from the crowd, before Universal added one more thing. "And I will be the referee." Universal dropped the microphone as Davis snarled, before turning and disappearing into the back. The owner of the fWo returned his attention to his seemingly unconscious friend. Medics rushed the ring while Jeremiah Frankton & James Armstrong sat in silence, Countdown To Oblivion 3 fading to a commercial quickly from this scene of chaos...
