Sunday Slaughter, 30 January 2000

30 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Machina stepped out of the jetway and into the Miami airport. He sniffed at the air and turned to Rudo. "I see that the parade for Machina's homecoming isn't here." Rudo shrugged. "Well, that's hardly a surprise... I mean, first off it's not like you're the Frenchie or something. Second, the United States isn't your homeland. But we've got more important things than your ego to worry about right now." Machina frowned. "If there's anything more important than my ego around, I want it caught and shot now." Rudo sighed and reached into his bag for the nine millimeter. Suddenly Machina yelped with happiness and ran toward Penny, who was standing near the gate. Rudo considered, and then put the gun back inside the bag and walked over to where the two stood. Machina was hugging Penny, and tossing her up and down while bouncing with joy. Penny finally got him to put her down, and looked suspiciously at Rudo. "Have we met?" Rudo nodded, and extended a hand. "Yeah, I'm Rudo. I'm a wrestler." Penny gasped and glared at Machina. Rudo slapped himself on the forehead and mumbled, "Oh right, the crazy girl who hates wrestling." He extended his hand again and said, "I mean I'm wrestling... with some tough issues in my life, like how a sport like wrestling which is so barbaric and completely inappropriate can be marketed to people of such a young age." Penny beamed and shook Rudo's hand. Smiling, Rudo continued. "The social ramifications alone are staggering when you stop to consider them. Children learn that the way to solve problems is through violence rather than reasonable understanding and love." Penny was nodding now, and smiling at Rudo. Machina noticed this and smacked Rudo on the head. Whispering to Penny Machina said, "It's for his own good you see... he's got a brain problem." Penny nodded and pointed down the jetway toward the exit. "Well very nice to meet you Mr. Rudo. Now come on you, I've got a car that will take us to the arena where this so-called 'Fans' wrestling organization is holding a show tonight. You would not believe what they are doing now... some sweaty male wrestler is actually getting married to his big-chested mentally abused girlfriend. I mean, I can't believe that in this day and age things like women's rights would be so completely ignored in this obviously male dominated industry. I mean, 'Violet'. What kind of a name is that? It's like half of his name, so it's like she's treated as if she's half of a person." Machina choked at the mention of Violet's name and had to be pounded on the back by Rudo. Machina looked at Penny wide-eyed. "Violet is getting married to Kid Cool?" Penny shook her head, and Machina breathed a sigh of relief. "No, and let me tell you that using a name like 'Kid Cool' just spits in the face of what that James Armstrong said about catering to a more adult audience. No, she's getting married to some 'Ultra Violet' guy. That's the whole thing with her just having half his name, I... Machina?" But Machina had fainted in the middle of the airport terminal at the mention of his old enemy's name. As Rudo and Penny struggled to get him back to his senses, the fireworks of Sunday Slaughter began to explode inside the arena as the show took to the air. Canadian Connection vs. Guano Apes As The Canadian Connection is introduced, the crowd turns their attention to the entrance ramp where Tom the Bomb and Dark Ninja emerge onto an fWo scene for the first time in more than one month. Ever since losing the number one contendership for the tag team titles to The Underground on a December edition of Warfare, they have yet to set foot in an fWo ring. The two reach the ring, stretching and strategizing as "War?", by System of a Down hits the speakers for the first time ever. The Guano Apes, Jackhammer and Sphere, step out onto the stage. They make their way to the ring, moments away from their debut in tag team action as a tandem. Both of them as well had not seen action for some time; not since Twilight. As they walked to the ring the lights in the arena suddenly dimmed, and on the giant Slaughter Screen an image of a stopwatch was seen, counting down the time in harsh contrast to the high-energy atmosphere of the crowd. It faded away as the Apes got to the ring, both of them unsure of what this development meant. Ninja steps out first for the Connection, Jackhammer and Sphere settling on the latter. Both men step forward and once the match is underway, lock up in the center of the ring. Sphere is able to find a solid footing on the mat and simply motor forward, Ninja unable to keep himself from being backed up into the corner. Ninja escapes this tight spot with a lighting quick kick that finds its way just below Sphere's waist. The referee's vision is obstructed by Sphere, and Ninja thus is able to get away with a potentially illegal kick. As Sphere bends forward, groaning as he reaches for his midsection, Ninja leaps out of the corner, and in succession, sends two kicks glancing off of Sphere's cranium. Sphere lurches upwards, clutching his head only to have Ninja deliver a devastatingly accurate kick that hits with solid force right to his exposed stomach. Now clutching both his bruised head and his weakened gut, Sphere is defenseless and Ninja neatly sweeps him off his feet with a leg sweep. Ninja drops for a cover, yet Sphere is able to turn a shoulder at two, despite feeling a bit nauseous. Ninja lugs Sphere over into the Connection corner, stomping his chest numerous times before tagging in Tom the Bomb. Bomb jumps in and continues stamping on Sphere's chest. Lifting him, he sends him into the ropes and on the way back meets him with a diving clothesline that sends him hard to the mat once more. Looking for a quick pin, Tom covers, yet Sphere is able to kick out at two. Tom heaves him up by his arm and then he himself goes off the ropes. On the way back he leaps into a cross body block, however Sphere is able to catch him and hold his ground. Stumbling in tiny circles with unsteady steps, Sphere takes a few quick shuffles backwards trying to balance, right into the Connection corner. An alert Bomb is able to reach for Ninja who makes the blind tag. Ducking the rope, he sizes up the an unaware Sphere, and dropkicks him right to the center of his back. This sends Sphere pitching forwards. He falls to them at, sandwiching Tom in the process, and at the same time covering him. The referee drops and gets to one before Ninja realizes his mistake and leaps on Sphere with a knee to the back to break up the pin. Being the legal man in he looks to slap on an Armbar submission hold, yet Sphere is able to wrap up his arm and hip-toss him to the mat. He then lifts him and chucks him into the turnbuckle. Grabbing his head he begins to repeatedly pound it into the turnbuckle, a throbbing pain beginning to form in Ninja's head. Sphere lets him stumble backwards while he tags in a hungry Jackhammer. All seven feet of Jackhammer step over the top rope and then run full speed at Ninja who has the unfortunate fate of being in the way of a Jackhammer clothesline that socks him clean across the face and turns the lights out momentarily as he thuds off the mat. Jackhammer then targets Tom the Bomb who challenges him, yet ends up with a large dent in his forehead as Jackhammer head-butts him to the mat with authority. Turning his attention back to the Ninja he brings him to his feet and slapping a hand around his throat, he supports him with a hand to his back before easily lifting him upwards looking for a Chokeslam. As he is about to cement Ninja to the canvas, Tom the Bomb, still on the ground, is able to scamper up behind him and deliver a windmill low blow. Jackhammer feels a numbing pain grip his extremities and he falls, Ninja able to escape the hold and regain his poise. The referee motions for Tom to stand on the apron which he does so as Ninja begins working on a temporarily ineffective Jackhammer. Finally, seizing hold of the opportunity, he goes off the ropes, and on the way back, twirls into a Spinwheel kick, clocking Jackhammer harshly across the mouth, his jaw snapping, and sending him tumbling backwards. Ninja drags Jackhammer away from Sphere and drops for the pin; however Tom the Bomb begins to bounce up and down on the ropes, reaching in and pleading for a tag. Ninja falls back on his heels, and shaking his head, finally obliges, half-heartedly slapping Tom's outstretched hand. Bomb jumps in and lifting Jackhammer to his feet, backs off a few paces. Giving Ninja a confident thumbs up, he leaps with a jumping Superkick, looking to dislodge each and every one of Jackhammer's teeth. He soars forward Result: Guano Apes by Pinfall Do Not Disturb A small group had formed backstage and looked at the door reading "David Spencer" with dismay. David Spencer had arrived at the arena early and was in a foul mood. The fact that catering was late with the food didn't help matters either, and when Mike Heftel approached David Spencer and asked for directions to the loo the big man promptly threw him at a wall and stalked off. Now it was showtime and Slaughter had already took to the air. John Willis turned to Austin and hitched a thumb at the closed door. "Are we sure he's in there? I can't hear anything." Austin nodded. "He's in there alright... a few minutes ago we could hear him singing that Meow Mix song to himself. It is a catchy tune, but I think he's signing in anger." Willis and Austin looked at each other again and coughed nervously. Matt Loco wandered into the area and beamed at the two security guards standing out side Spencer's door. "Hey there! I say, is that Spencer's locker room tonight? He's on the match list with an open challenge... so who is he fighting?" Willis pointed to the door. "We don't know... he's in there by himself and we're a bit worried about his... er... state of self." The three men looked at the door again. From inside Spencer's voice singing "Always Coca Cola" could barely be heard. Matt Loco suddenly collected himself and put a hand on the door knob. "Right then. Somebody has to go in there, so I'm going inside to have a look." Matt started to turn the door knob and then looked back and glanced around at Willis and Austin. "Is nobody going to say, 'No, you can't possibly... let me go instead.'?" Willis and Austin shook their heads. Matt Loco slumped. "Oh well." He went inside and closed the door behind him. The singing stopped, and for a moment there was silence. From behind the door came Matt's voice in a loudly confidential whisper as if introducing the subject of nipple-piercing in a nunnery. "Um... hello David Spencer... it's me Matt. Everything quite cricket in here?" There was no response, and Willis and Austin leaned in toward the door so they might hear better. Suddenly a great crash was heard from the other side of the door and the wall seemed to push outward as something heavy collided with it. Then near silence again, although John Willis could swear that he heard tiny whimpering inside the room. A few minutes passed, and then the door creaked slowly open. Matt Loco crawled out bleeding and in an obvious state of disarray. He looked up at the two security guards with a thin smile. "Right. So he's not interested in coming out right now, and I think he might be a bit upset about the house still." Matt Loco fainted then, thinking strangely enough about ladybugs. Fear vs. Double S Fear enters first, confident enough to take this match without his Underground colleagues. Fear waits in the ring for Sid, looking generally menacing. Suddenly, the lights go out, before a multi-colored strobe light begins flashing around the arena. Loud stripping music plays as Sid Snow suddenly appears on the entrance ramp, holding a bottle of water. He takes a drink from the bottle, and swallows rather than spits. Sid then makes his way to the ring, before rolling and grabbing a microphone. "My name is Double S, Sid Snow, and I AM THE *DAME*! And Fear, baby, I'll have you know that-" Sid is cut off by a monstrous forearm from Fear, knocking him down to the mat. Fear drags Sid up to his feet, and whips him into the ropes, but Sid rolls to the side and behind Fear. Fear quickly turns around, but is locked in a bearhug by Sid, who applies extreme pressure on the hold. Fear gives a yell of rage, before raining down punches on the head of Double S. Sid releases the hold, and Fear charges at him, using a kick to the head to knock the Dame over the top rope. Fear rolls to the outside as well, and grabs Sid before whipping him hard into the steel ring steps. Double S hits hard, but Fear is immediately on him again, picking him up and whipping him into the steel ringpost. Sid crumples to the floor, and Fear heads to the announcers table, picking up a chair. Fear waits for Sid to get up, and when he does, smashes the chair over the head of Double S, leaving a massive dent in the steel. From inside the ring the referee warns Fear that this is not a hardcore match, but Fear appears not to be listening as he rolls Sid back into the ring. Fear lifts the smaller man up, before going behind into a full nelson and lifting up Double S, before planting him with a sitdown full nelson slam. Sid is out, but Fear isn't done yet. Fear brings Sid up again, and grabs him by the throat with both hands. He then lifts up Sid, and spikes him with a two-handed chokeslam. Fear still isn't done, and brings Sid up, before lifting him into a Gorilla press. He holds Sid up, before dropping him into a stomachbreaker in the combination move dubbed Thantophobia. Fear brings Double S up once again, and repeats the Thantophobia finisher. The referee begins to get in Fear's face as he brings Sid up, warning him once again against excessive violence. Fear simply ignores the much smaller man and lifts Sid into a third Gorilla press. He holds him in position, before running to the ropes and throwing Sid like a javelin through the French announcer's table. The referee rings the bell immediately and awards the match to the unconscious Double S. Fear simply walks off, unperturbed, as Slaughter rolls on. Result: Double S by DQ Being nice Backstage James Armstrong walked with a newspaper folded under one arm. He whistled a tune as he went, and tipped his hat at people that he passed in the narrow hallway. He stopped outside Chris Finn's office and rapped the door with one fist. From inside Finn's voice urged him to enter, and James Armstrong discovered Finn sitting behind a makeshift desk that was burdened with stacks of papers and forms. Undaunted, he dropped the paper on his desk, causing several small paper avalanches to break out at the desk. Finn groaned, and looked up at Armstrong with mild annoyance. Armstrong beamed. "I brought your paper, Mr. Finn... and you will be happy to note that not only did I pay for it, I used real American money. No slugs or Canadian coins here. I also took your car in to be washed... and oh yeah, because of that here is your mirror." Armstrong fished into a pocket and pulled out a small side door mirror. He dropped it on Finn's desk with a thud. Finn looked at it, and noticed that it appeared to still be dripping. He looked up at Armstrong and searched for words to begin. "Why?" Armstrong's smile faltered slightly. "Why sir?" Finn looked at Armstrong and decided that it was going to be a long day. "Okay Armstrong, I give. Why are you being so... well, I'm unsure of the right word to describe it. Nice?" Armstrong sat in a chair across from Chris Finn and smiled. "You told me I needed to be a better person if I was to get my power... er... my job back. So here I am. Being nice." The two looked at each other for a long moment. Armstrong started to fidget. He looked at Finn and had trouble reading him, and so blundered out with, "Hey, you want I should go get us some hookers?" Before Finn could answer his door was swung open and Scott Collins walked into the office. He looked at Armstrong, rolled his eyes, and then put all of his attention on Chris Finn. "Listen here spanky, you're the match writer right now so you're going to write me a little match." Finn shook his head. "No, no... we've got too many matches already, the writers are tired man... let them sleep!" Collins grabbed Finn by the neck and shook him to make his point a little more clear. "I want baseball boy in a match with War. No, with Plague. No wait..." Collins scratched his chin, suddenly struck with an idea. "Say... how about baseball boy against both War AND Plague... yeah! That's the ticket... set that up for me spanky!" Collins walked out the way he came in leaving Finn and Armstrong in the room alone. They looked at each other. Finn cleared his throat. "So you're going to be nice, huh." Wretch vs. Oracle vs. Courage vs. Matt Douglas The Underground's music blared, signaling the arrival of Wretch in this four corners elimination contest. Wretch slid into the ring just as Creed came over the loudspeakers and Matt Douglas made his way to the ring. Next up was Oracle who slowly walked down to the ring. Finally, the cruiserweight champion Courage made his entrance. Each man took up a position at one of the ring corners, and soon the match was underway. Matt Douglas and Wretch started out. The two lobbied for position for a moment until they both locked up. Wretch pushed Douglas, but Douglas pushed back and soon had Wretch into his own corner. Douglas broke clean, but as he did Courage came down the apron and began to choke out Wretch with the tag rope. Wretch, however, dropped himself down and hotshotted Courage across the top rope. Courage was sent sprawling to the floor as Wretch celebrated. Unfortunately, Wretch celebrated into a running spinning leg lariat from Douglas. Courage slid back into the ring and charged at Douglas, clotheslining him to the outside. Courage then hopped onto the top rope and shot himself off with the Courage Required, nailing Wretch. One... two... three, and Courage had made the first elimination. Wretch rolled to the outside and walked to the back, obviously upset, as Oracle charged Courage. Oracle pounded on Courage in the corner, then took a step back before running in for a body press. But, before Oracle could connect, Courage dove out of the way. Oracle bounced off the turnbuckles and right into a k-driller from Courage, who signaled for the Courage Required. As Courage bounced off the top rope, though, Matt Douglas grabbed the ropes and knocked Courage off balance. Courage fell onto the mat as Oracle made the tag out, and Douglas ran into the ring full of fire. Douglas peppered Courage with fists as he got to his feet, but Courage battled back with a jawbreaker on top of his head. Matt Douglas stumbled back and almost right into Oracle who was in the midst of rushing Courage. Oracle began to lay into Courage, and together with Douglas they sent the cruiserweight champion to the ropes. Courage came back and ducked a double clothesline, and on his way back off the other side Courage grabbed both Oracle and Douglas' heads and bashed them together. Oracle stumbled for the ropes as Douglas went to the mat. Courage covered Douglas, but Oracle nailed Courage at the count of one. Oracle pulled Courage to his feet and executed an inverted atomic drop that stunned him. Oracle then signaled to Matt Douglas, and as Oracle grabbed Courage in a bear hug Matt Douglas bounced off the ropes and hit the Foundation member with a running neck breaker. Douglas quickly covered Courage and got the three count, but as he got to his feet and raised his hands in victory he was rolled up from behind by Oracle, who got the three count and the win. Douglas was furious, though, and before Oracle could even have his hand raised he was being shoved by Matt. Oracle wouldn't take it lying down, though, and shoved back. The two looked ready to go at it when fWo security hit the ring and kept them separated. Result: Oracle by Pinfall Telling it like it is As Slaughter returned from a commercial break the lights in the arena suddenly dimmed slightly, and Japanese music began to play. The crowd stood to see who was coming out onto the ramp, and then sat down disappointed when Waru made his way out from behind the curtains and walked to the ring. He carried with him a microphone, and showed no signs that he was going to wrestle. He slid into the ring and took in the crowd before starting to speak. "Hey... hey... I know several of you have been asking yourselves, 'Where has Waru been?' Well, not that any of you hardcore freaks out there cared enough to send me a card or maybe even a box of chocolates or something, but I've been at home nursing an ankle injury that I sustained while wrestling in one of those so-called 'Hardcore' matches that is really nothing more than an excuse to cover up two bad wrestlers by having them work on each other with pipes and other non-wrestling materials. Is that what you want? You... the fans? I had a chance to see some of you coming into the arena earlier with your signs. Signs like "Fire rules" and "Hellrazor fears Sammy V" and "Chris Brown 3:16" and "Crusader Is Here". Well it's because of you that we are subjected to these boring, stupid matches that go nowhere and showcase none of the wrestling skill that this sport was built on. I mean SPORTS entertainment, not sports ENTERTAINMENT. I want WRESTLING, and I'm sure that a lot of you do to. Remember in the old days when we would be treated to a Bruno Sammartino headlock that would go on for over and hour and keep us on the edges of our seats the whole time? Every day thousands of you come to the arenas we showcase our talents in, but instead of getting a solid wrestling match you get garbage wrestling. Well people, have no fear! Waru is here!" The crowd booed this, but Waru seemed not to notice or care. "Right now there is a title being held by Hellrazor. Now here is a guy who has been more concerned with his damn cat than with WRESTLING. Well Hellrazor, I'm going to fight your kind of match, a Hardcore match, and I'm going to win. And when I do I'm going to retire the belt and make sure that none of us have to suffer through these non-wrestling garbage matches ever again! So if you're man enough, then I'll be waiting for you next Sunday on Slaughter. Let's see if you've got the spirit of a wrestler... or if you're garbage at heart too." With that, Waru slid out of the ring and headed back up the ramp, being careful to avoid the garbage that was tossed at him from fans on the outside. French Foundation vs. Millennium Men The team of the Flying Frenchie and Primetime - representing the French Foundation - made their way to the ring first to a thunderous chorus of boos. Frenchie slid into the ring with a smirk on his face as he held the fWo World Championship belt high in the air while Primetime played to him. As the two continued their pre-match party the Millennium Men theme blared over the arena, prompting the Foundation to cut out the foolery. Black Panther stepped out first, and the savior of the fWo less than a month ago was received by the crowd in what had to be the loudest reaction of the night thus far. Havok appeared out from the entrance next, and together the two soaked in the cheers. Then, at a methodical pace, each of the former champions walked to the ring. The French Foundation yelled a few jeers at the two, especially Frenchie at Panther, before the latter and his teammate were even able to enter the ring. Havok attempted to climb into the ring, but Primetime dove at him with a boot which Havok was able to avoid at the last second. Panther tried to climb through the ropes, but once again the FF was there to prevent him entrance. The crowd wanted to get the match underway, and they let the Foundation know this by pumping out even more boos. Primetime and the Frenchie looked as though they couldn't care less, though, and continued to mock their foes from within the ring. Panther pulled Havok over and the two conversed for a few moments before Panther started towards the timekeepers table. A puzzled look appeared on the faces of TFF, but all became clear once Black Panther pushed the ring attendant away and picked up the fWo World Title. The Frenchie's face took on an expression of both horror and shock, and the Panther was visibly smiling beneath his mask. Together with Havok, the two Millennium Men began to walk back up the aisle. Primetime looked at the Frenchie. The Frenchie looked at Primetime. Without saying anything both FF members dove out of the ring and attacked the Millennium Men from behind with rights and lefts. Panther stumbled and dropped the belt as Primetime wailed away at Havok. The Frenchie ceased his attack on the former champion and grabbed the title belt, allowing the Panther to recover and bulldoze him from behind. Primetime attempted to whip Havok into the barricades but Havok reversed the effort and sent Primetime into the railing. While Primetime lay slumped over the guardrail the Foundation leader wasn't managing much better. Black Panther lifted the Frenchie up and dropped him throat first across the cades. TFF rolled on the ground in pain, clutching his neck, as each Millennium Man began to lay into him with stomps and kicks. Primetime attempted to come to the rescue but Havok ducked his clothesline and back dropped him over and into the fans. Much to the delight of the fans Havok decided to follow Primetime into the crowd, and together the two began to brawl through the fans. Meanwhile, Panther grabbed Frenchie by the beret and began to pick him up when TFF snuck in a low blow. Panther released the world champion and stumbled about in pain while TFF got to his feet and, once again, picked up his world title. Black Panther slowly turned to face TFF but found himself the recipient of a blow to the face from the gold belt. Panther went down like a brick, but the Frenchie added insult to injury by laying on top of the Panther and counting the three himself. The Frenchie lifted his belt over his head and started to celebrate his "victory", but as he did the Panther spun Frenchie around and grabbed him around the throat with his hand. The Frenchie dropped the belt in shock as Panther literally threw the champ into the guardrail. Back in the crowd Primetime slammed Havok's face against the same railing and dumped him over and onto the entrance way. The Panther was busy with the Frenchie, though, whom he rolled back into the ring. The bell rang, and the match had finally officially begun. The Frenchie stumbled to his feet quickly but upon seeing the approaching Panther TFF backed into the corner, begging not to be hit. The Panther ignored his request though and began to lay into TFF with rights and lefts. Then Panther climbed onto the second turnbuckle and, to the count of the crowd, delivered ten shots to the forehead in quick succession. The Frenchie hobbled out of the corner, but Panther tossed him back in before signaling for the Pounce. Panther lifted Frenchie to the top rope, but as he himself climbed up Primetime slid into the ring and yanked him off. Panther fell to the mat and rolled to his feet, but once he was up he was right back down again thanks to a flying dropkick from the Frenchie. Havok rolled into the ring as Frenchie attempted a quick cover, but Panther kicked out at two. Havok attacked the Frenchie with an axehandle smash, and soon all four competitors were going at it. The Black Panther whipped Primetime into the ropes where Havok back dropped him to the outside before turning his attention to the Frenchie. TFF kicked Havok in the gut, doubling him over, and then supericked Panther right in the jaw. On the outside Primetime grabbed himself a chair and slid back into the ring. As the ref checked on Panther, Primetime lifted the chair above his head and brought down a crushing blow onto the skull of Havok. The smaller Millennium Man fell to the mat as Primetime ditched the chair and made a cover. The ref, finished checking with a barely moving Panther, counted one, two, two and one half... kick out from Havok! The crowd exploded in cheers, but not just because of Havok... Mike Bear, Wretch, and Fear had just appeared on the entrance ramp. The four men in the match, though, hardly noticed the Underground and continued their war. Panther rolled to the outside with Frenchie still attacking him while Primetime set Havok up for the Primetime Bomb. Primetime lifted, but Havok countered with a frankensteiner and got a two count. Each man scrambled to his feet, and Primetime caught his opponent in a spine buster slam. The ref counted again, but only got to two before Havok fought his way out. Primetime seemed frustrated as he grabbed Havok by the head and ran towards the ropes, but Havok put on the brakes and sent Primetime back to the outside. Meanwhile Frenchie had disposed of Panther on the outside and climbed back into the ring, scaling the turnbuckles to the top rope. The Frenchie leapt for a flying hurracanrana but as he wrapped his legs around the head of Havok and flipped back the former cruiserweight champion dropped to his knees, rolling up the current fWo world champion. Frenchie couldn't fight out of the predicament, and Havok scored himself the one two three to pick up the win. As Panther slid into the ring to celebrate with his partner the Underground nodded and headed to the back. Result: Millennium Men by Pinfall An American Idiot in America Kid Cool searched the list of workers that night for his name and found no mention of himself. He frowned, and looked at the paper more closely. Still no sign of his name. He grunted and searched into a pocket, fishing out a pair of thin-rimmed spectacles. He placed them on his face and leaned forward to look at the booking sheet. After many minutes he found his name. Kid Cool: Clean up catering. Kid Cool frowned and put the glasses back into his pocket. As he did so his hand brushed over a Sharpie marker and then closed around it. Looking around himself to see if anyone was watching him, Kid Cool leaned forward with the marker and scribbled out the name "Chris Universal". Working quickly now, he wrote his own name over it, and smiling to himself capped the marker and placed it back into his pocket. He started to walk away, but a voice stopped him in his tracks. "What did I tell you about trying to add your name to the booking list Buckly? You're not going to wrestle in the main event you know... do you think we are so stupid that we just have only one copy of the booking sheet? And do you really think that nobody would find it odd that you're suddenly in the main event? C'mon Kid... you're just wasting our time here." Kid Cool turned and looked sourly at Chris Finn. "It's not fair! When do I get to be in the main event, huh? How come I'm always the jobber who is stuck on the low card?" Chris Finn sighed and pulled down the booking sheet Kid Cool had marked on and replaced it with a new one. "Because Kid, you ARE a jobber. And you ARE a low card talent. Even with all we've done to try and get you over... letting you get the rub from the Frenchie, UV... hell, we even put you in a match with the Slugger for the World Title. You're not over Kid Cool, and you never will be. Your name even stinks... 'Kid Cool'? You're neither Kid nor Cool, Buckly." Kid Cool now stepped forward and jabbed a finger into Chris Finn's chest. "You take that back. I am so amazingly cool you could keep a side of meat in me for a month. Why, I am so hip I have difficulty seeing over my pelvis." Chris Finn brushed the hand away and strolled down the hallway. From over his shoulder he said, "Yeah, whatever Buckly. Just do a good job cleaning up the catering tonight. I'm not paying you to just sit around and bitch... we've got several people on the roster that are filling that job already." Kid Cool watched him go while clenching and unclenching his fists into tight little balls. He chewed at his lip and mumbled, "You wouldn't be saying that if my dad was here." Then Kid Cool burst into tears, and walked dejectedly out of the room. Eron (c) vs. Ice "Crazy Train" echoing throughout the arena signals the arrival of Ice. He is met by a gracious applause, slowly establishing a fan base among the fWo faithful. Intent on claiming hold of the Internet title, he makes his way to the ring, sliding in and rising, almost daring his opponent to test him. Eron follows, "Physical" laying the ground for his entrance. He emerges, arrogantly and deliberately flaunting his gold.
