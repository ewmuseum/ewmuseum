fWo Survivor NEWSWIRE COMPLETE CHAOS July 31, 2000 While the idea may have seemed good on paper, officials are now in agreement that Round Five of the fWo Survivor show was a bad idea. The eight man tag team tornado match was supposed to encourage quick teamwork and strategy... and perhaps both items were accomplished a bit too well. Tensions that had been mounting between the wrestlers were brought to a head as partners failed to trust one another and still more bad feelings were sown. The hostility that had erupted over recent days between the Red Dragon and Kodiak Vic Creed only intensified, as officials are now contemplating some kind of match between the two to settle their grudge once and for all. More bad feelings seemed to come out between Arch Angel and Reno Davis, and the conclusion of the event between Ruben Ross and Suicide will undoubtedly lead to Suicide wanting some revenge at some point down the road. With the high amount of angry feelings within the fWo Survivor competitors, it makes the next round of voting all the more dangerous. Alliances could be dissolving already and eight men still remain inside the contest. "Seven after tomorrow." said fWo Survivor coordinator Stevie Roberts. "We will be nearly half complete by 2pm tomorrow... and frankly I'm not sure what the landscape is going to look like by this time next week. We were all surprised by some of the eliminations so far... all bets are off. All bets are off." In related stories, wrestler T-Bone has signed a deal with shoe maker Rebok to star in a series of commercials detailing the life of a professional wrestler. Juno Law has continued to actively compete within Tornado Wrestling, Mike Extreme has surfaced in several promotions, and rumor has it that Ronnie Randall will be making an announcement about his career shortly. More news as it comes available.
