Meltdown 2009 Announcement Fans in the arena turned to the big screen, where the flickering lights meant only one thing: an FWO announcement was about to take place. Last week the epic drawing for Meltdown selected twenty teams at random from forty of the FWO’s biggest superstars. Established tag teams were separated; men who had never stepped foot in a ring together will now work together for the biggest opportunity the FWO has to offer. The FWO World Heavyweight Championship The FWO Tag Team Championship The FWO Cruiserweight Championship The FWO Internet Championship The FWO Hardcore Championship Five championship belts, a chance for all titles all offered up to the winner at a time of their choosing. To be clear, of their choosing: anytime, anywhere, after any match or at any event. If the Hardcore Champion Krow is in the ring, having just survived an epic hour match against the FWO’s toughest competitors, is spent, broken and tired… that title could be up for grabs once more at Krow’s weakest moment. The prize is more valuable and more treasured than any prize in the industry: the teams are important. Vital. Planning and preparation is key for victory; teams working together to a point, knowing if they survive to the end they will immediately betray one another in the final stretch to claim the prize as their own. Since the announcement, twenty teams have started their training and strategy. Every minute matters. Every second is important. But this week two teams must start all over again. It is the Meltdown Shuffle, and two teams have been randomly drawn from the first ten teams. Starting now, starting tonight, two teams will be shuffled. Next week, two final teams will trade partners. In two weeks, the two mystery members will be revealed. Then… the battle will commence. Two teams will be randomly shuffled… tonight.
