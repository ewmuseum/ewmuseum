Introduction State of the union: The fWo has run like a boat on still waters of late, the championship title of Deacon has been challenged from several sources, the Internet and Hardcore champions have faced a steady onslaught of new and old faces. The tag team champions, while controversial by the back office, are slowly gaining their stride. In a few weeks an fWo milestone, Meltdown, will return to the air with the glowing promise of a championship title shot at every title the fWo has to offer. If Graphic Violence had been so inclined, he was sure he could track Creed down. After all, he was trained in Urban Tactics And Recon (UTAR). Why waste the energy and resources in tracking down a man who was living on borrowed time? Which was why Graphic Violence walked the hallways of an arena rather than an airport. Amidst the chaos of the fWo, he felt comfortable.
