fWo — Card

Wednesday Warfare, 9 February 2000

9 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Outside the arena fans who had arrived late quickly scrambled to get inside before the opening fireworks. They passed the small gathering of protestors with little concern. The protesters looked cold, unhappy, and generally unpleased with spending another Wednesday night outside a wrestling event. Among them were Machina and Penny, and as Machina raised the sign up and down to get attention from passersby he winced. His arm was giving him trouble since his match on Slaughter with the Frenchie, and he was trying to hide the fact that he was sore from Penny, who looked at him curiously. Penny grumbled aloud. "I can't believe all these people are going to see some stupid wrestling show. Can you believe this Ed? I mean, there are museums, concerts... nice concerts mind you, none of that Sheryl Crow filth..." Machina nodded, "Yeah, it's a real... OW!" His arm had given him a major seizure again, and he dropped the sign. Penny showed signs of concern, but Machina pulled away. He struggled to think of something that would cover the fact that the left side of his body was covered in bruises from the match. "I've just... got a tack in my shirt. Yeah, just a tack. It's digging into my arm you see, and there is blood everywhere." Penny looked at his clean, white shirt with confusion. Machina thought quickly. "I mean blood on the inside! Yeah, that's it... er... I'm just going to run into that arena and get something cold to put on it so all the blood freezes or something. No need to be suspicious... I'll just... hey! Here's Rudo! Rudo, keep Penny company will you? I'll be right back." With that Machina fled into the arena in search of an ice pack or something cold to put on his arm. Rudo walked up to Penny and picked up Machina's sign. His headache was better, and he was feeling not so bad. He needed to get back on track with his plan involving Scott Collins... but just lately it was getting harder to remember what that plan was. He decided he'd spring into action on Sunday. He smiled at Penny, and she returned the grin with a warm expression of bliss. Penny cuddled up next to Rudo, and the two continued their picketing. Inside the arena, the fireworks were beginning to sound, signaling the start of Wednesday Warfare... Mike Bear vs. Havok Mike Bear emerges first for a rare singles appearance, focusing on one of two match-ups he will appear in on Warfare. A solemn look across his face, he wears his half of the tag team championship about his waist, though it is not on the line. His followers greeting him with a loud ovation, he undoes the title and adjusting his arm-pads and tightening his boot laces, prepares for his opponent. Havok follows suit, "Nitro" signaling his entrance. He too is met kindly by the Georgia Dome crowd. Acknowledging this, he raises a hand to thank them before sliding into the ring to test his luck against Bear. Havok dabbles with some quick jabs before Bear interjects and grabbing one of Havok's arms, slings him into one corner. Bear follows in and as Havok meets the turnbuckle, he greets him with an unpleasant knee to the groin. He repeats this once more using the opposite corner, effectively bringing Havok to his knees. After driving repeated fists into Havok's head, he applies a headlock takedown. After a Legdrop and a stream of stomps, Bear has Havok on his feet. He sends him into the ropes. Looking to meet Havok with a back body drop, Bear is taken aback when Havok dodges to the side and wraps himself around Bear with a Crucifix. Unable to bring the big man to the ground, a resourceful Havok manages to slide off of Bear and ends up in a position facing him. Leaping into action, he strikes with a kick to the gut and then lifting both of Bear's arms, brings him crashing deep into the mat with a double arm DDT. Havok rolls over a shaken Bear yet can only muster a two count. Havok lets out a groan, having figured the match won, yet he brings Bear to his feet and looks to wrap this one up. Whipping a less than impressive Bear into the ropes, Havok throws himself off the adjacent ropes. The two shoot off their respective ropes and as they meet in the middle, Havok is able to slap on a hold, and extends it into a flying bulldog that has Bear coming up close and personal with the canvas. Well in control, Havok efficiently hops to the top turnbuckle and shortly thereafter takes to the air. All hope Mike Bear held in turning the tables and capturing a victory are extinguished as Havok connects with a graceful Millenium-sault. However looks do deceive, as the maneuver is equally as devastating, leaving Bear unable to react when Havok makes the cover and in due time rings up another victory. Cashing in on a golden opportunity to hone his skills, Havok makes his exit as Bear looks to calculate what went wrong in this his latest outing. Result: Havok by Pinfall Eron's challenge Suddenly, from the TravisTron, Erons face was shown, as the fWo Internet Title rested on his shoulder, and he smiled at the camera. The crowd booed loud as Eron just grinned from his negative crowd responce. "I said I was gonna make an announcement, and here I am. You see, I made a mistake before with that old scrap called the International Title. And that is that no one ever really wanted to face me... for you see, they were too afraid. So, this time I'm gonna force some people. You see, I've made an arrangement with the heads of the fWo to bring you a title contest on Sunday Slaughter. In that very area, there will be a Number One Contender match for the Internet Title. Whoever wins will fight me at fWo Trapped. I've already chosen the participants, and they will know who they are at the show. So, for the people I chose... well, break a leg you sad midcarders because it'll be your time to...wait, I forgot, I'm still gonna be the champion after Trapped, so how would they shine?" Eron laughed at his own joke as the fans in the arena grew louder with their displeasure. "Anyways, whoever I'm gonna fight, take a breath..." Eron paused, and the crowd began to chant along with his signature phrase. "OF FRESH DEATH!" Eron grinned as the screen faded to black, and the fans continued to boo. Wretch vs. Kid Cool vs. Plague The music of the 4 Riders came over the arena speaker system, meaning that the cruiserweight contender match was about to begin. Plague walked out alone and slid into the ring, getting a small reaction from the crowd. Next, the entrance music to the rightful heir to the fWo, Buckly Beaven himself - Kid Cool - came down to the ring to a huge mixed reaction, mostly comprised of detractors and the occasional cult fan. Cool slid into the ring and soaked in the reaction, realizing that this is the most heat he has ever had inside a wrestling ring. The Underground music played next, and out came the fan favorite for this bout, Wretch. Wretch ran down to the ring alone and slid in. As he did Plague immediately went on the attack with lefts and rights to Wretch's head. Kid Cool tried to assist Plague, but the Rider wanted none of it and shoved him to the mat. Cool pounded on the mat in frustration as Plague picked up Wretch and dumped him to the outside. Plague climbed through the ropes and dropped to the floor, intent on following his prey. Wretch was the first on the attack, though, and began to deliver his own series of fists. With Plague staggering, Wretch grabbed his foe and sent him into the railing with a crash. Meanwhile, Kid Cool slid to the outside and picked up a steel folding chair. Cool ran at Plague and tried to drive it into his skull, but Plague moved and Kid only made contact with the barricade. Wretch didn't miss with a kick to the gut, however. Wretch grabbed Plague by the head and slammed him into the ring apron a couple of times before sliding into the ring. Cool followed them both in and this time went after Wretch, but Wretch simply shoved Kid Cool through the ropes and back to the outside. As Kid Cool continued his hissy fit on the outside, Wretch whipped Plague into the ropes. Plague came back, but was met with a dropkick from the tag team champion. Wretch went for the quick cover, but Plague kicked out at one and a half. Wretch pulled Plague back to his feet and lifted him for a slam, but Plague slipped out the back, bumping into Kid Cool. Cool bounced back and hit the turnbuckles as Wretch took advantage of a distracted Plague and rolled him up. One... two... three. The ref orders Plague out of the ring, and Plague simply stares at him in disbelief. Kid Cool hops to his feet and begins to mock Plague, taking credit for the elimination. Plague slides to the outside as Wretch floors Kid Cool with a right hand. Wretch continues to beat on Cool until Plague, having retrieved a chair from outside the ring, slides back in and clocks Wretch in the back of the head. Wretch goes down in a heap, but before Cool can so much as smile he's met in the face with a chairshot of his own. Plague drops the chair and exits the ring, leaving both men out cold in the ring. The ref begins to lay down the mandatory ten count, but at the count of six Kid Cool stretches his arm out and covers Wretch. One... two... three! The bell rings, and Kid Cool has become the top contender to the fWo Cruiserweight Championship. Result: Kid Cool by Pinfall A deal... The backstage doors ripped open and Rain stormed into the room ignoring everyone around him, looking around in a fit of rage. He walked menacingly to the arena entrance, some tried to stop him but realized there was just no use. Rain walked through the curtain and down to the ring, fans were unsure of this unusual spectacle as Rain is not known to make unscheduled appearances. Rain reached the ring and demanded a microphone, and there was nobody around that was going to say otherwise. "There is a great disturbance here in the fWo, and needless to say I will take care of this problem ." Rain spoke slowly but with a harsh, serious tone. "A few weeks ago... I was foolish and bragged about my power, and did not use it to my benefit, and as a result, I lost the Internet title to a man who knows one move will not win him anything... ever again. Especially against the Storm." Rain then began to almost growl as he spoke the next words. "Chris Finn..... fWo.... you may try to keep me away from the ring, but the Storm will find his way, it is the way things must be." Rain then stood against the ropes staring at the entranceway saying his next words. "I am not leaving this ring until a sacrifice is made, for it is time, the God's need fulfillment, and I, the Storm will fulfill. fWo send your best.. I am in the mood to destroy." As Rain finished those words, a figure materialized from underneath the giant Warfare screen looming overhead. It was apparent quickly as to who it was. The unmistakable bald head of Dead paced towards the ring. He looked at Rain with a unsure expression on his face, before slowly entering the ring with caution. He approached Rain and covered the microphone with one hand, talking to the former Internet Champion with concern. Dead spoke to Rain further; although none of the conversation was audible. Dead and Rain seemed to come some sort of agreement, before Rain tossed the microphone to the mat. The two men began walking backstage, while everyone wondered what exactly the two superstars had to say to each other. Double S vs. Frank Snow vs. Robert Jensen vs. War Warfare returns from commercial, all four participants having since made their entrance. The scene serves evidence of the match having all ready begun. Robert Jensen lies in quite the precarious position, dazed and confused while trying to smother the pain in his nose, courtesy of War popping him with a haymaker punch. He lies on a table as well, and right as we return from break, we see War about to drop through Jensen from the ring apron. He is interrupted by Frank Snow who stands inside the ring. Grabbing War's head, he lunges at him, and sinking his teeth deep into the back of his head, proceeds to clamp on in what can only be described as a barbarous and cannibalistic act. War howls in pain, reaching to mend his wound, yet stumbling forward, trips off of the apron and falls onto Jensen, in turn putting him through the table with a loud crackling of decimated wood. Sid Snow who is outside in the ring aisle as well, now comes into view, sliding to cover up Jensen. The referee slips out of the ring and registers the three count, officially eliminating Jensen, and leaving both Frank and Sid Snow, and War left vying for a shot at Hellrazor's Hardcore championship belt. Snow leaps to his feet, hopping up and down, his face radiating his inner delight. This momentary feeling of joy is short-lived however, as Frank Snow, coming off of the opposite ropes, darts under the rope with a baseball slide that catches Sid from the side and throws him off-balance. The latter Snow now slides out into the aisle and begins to slowly reduce Sid to his knees. Meanwhile, War has shaken off the effects of earlier, and now comes up behind an oblivious Frank Snow. Snow swings at Sid yet misses badly, the two changing positions with Sid ending up now facing War. Or at least his chest. Sid slowly looks up into the eyes of War, fear suddenly gripping him. He begins babbling aimlessly, yet Frank Snow sees opportunity, snatches it, and capitalizes, dropping to a knee and in one motion, executing a low-blow that connects on both Sid Snow and War, and has both grasping for breath and overcome by a numbing pain, their knees weakening noticeably. Snow only comes harder, spinning his equal only in name and quickly DDT'ing him hard into the cement floor. Then leaping back up, he grabs War and does the same, drilling War's cranium into the unforgiving floor below. Snow remains atop, and the referee drops and carefully slaps out a three count, so as not to damage his hand. Now Snow lifts Sid Snow to his feet, victory on the horizon. He begins to use Snow's head as a punching bag. He lets up for just one second, and in that margin of time, Sid Snow is able to sneak in a quick yet surprisingly efficient kick to the gut. Miraculously gaining an edge, Sid hesitates before realizing what to do. He grabs hold of the head of Frank Snow, and follows through with a series of knees. Reaching for a chair, he folds it, and seemingly not sure how to exactly use it, struggles to gain control of it. When he does, he looks to bring it down upon Frank Snow before him, yet Frank instinctively puts a hand to cover his face, and the chair glances off his hand and right back into Sid's face. The steel claps his face loudly, and he falls to the ground. Frank Snow holds is hand gingerly yet is able to fall on top of the wrestler before him. At the count of three, Frank Snow has claimed victory and in the process won the right for a shot at Hellrazor and the fWo Hardcore title strap. Result: Frank Snow by Pinfall Looking for ice Machina wandered backstage looking for ice. His arm was hurting him badly, and he needed to get something cold on it to relax the pain. Luckily Rudo was outside with Penny, so at least he didn't have to worry about anyone hitting on her or anything. After all, Rudo was married... in addition to being his best friend and somebody who would never get between Machina and his property. Machina stopped for a moment. "Property." He said the word to nobody in particular, but a goofy grin passed over his face as he said it. Yes, he was really falling for this girl. He needed to ask her to marry him, but he hadn't quite figured out how to pop the question yet. He couldn't do it in front of all the other protesters... half of them were lesbians and the others he was sure secretly wanted him. Machina spotted ICE written on one of the locker room doors and breathed a sigh of relief. Finally. He pushed open the door, and saw Ice sitting on one of the locker room benches knitting a sweater. Ice looked up and saw Machina. "Dammit! I'm going to kill you..." Ice started to get up as Machina quickly turned and fled down a hallway. He turned first one corner and then another, and finally turned his head backward to see if Ice was following him. He never saw the Coke machine until he ran into it face-first, knocking himself out cold and sending cans of Coke shooting out from the machine. They began to pile up on top of him, and soon covered most of his body as he drifted deeper into unconsciousness. Everything was quiet for a moment, and then footsteps were heard. Two men in business suits walked by, stopping at the pile of Coke cans covering the unconscious Machina. They looked at him. They looked at each other. "Are you thinking what I'm thinking?" The other man nodded excitedly. "I think I am thinking what you think I'm thinking!" They both shouted at the same time. "Our sponsor!" Matt Douglas (c) vs. Hiroshima vs. Oracle The arena is bathed in red light, as the first competitor, Hiroshima, makes his way down to the ring for this one-fall three-way match for the International Championship of the Fans Wrestling Organization. Hiroshima waits on the outside, as Oracle emerges from behind the curtain. With his hands, Oracle pantomimes a belt around his waist, confident that he'll come out of this match victorious. Creed starts playing, signaling the arrival of the fWo International Champion, but before he even steps out onto the entrance ramp, Hiroshima jumps Oracle from behind in the ring. Hiroshima whips Oracle into the corner and follows with a high knee. Hiroshima then starts choking Oracle with his boot, as Matt Douglas finally makes his way down to the ring. Grabbing Hiroshima by the arm, he spins him around and catches him with a quick jawbreaker. He kicks Hiroshima out of the ring, then turns his attention to Oracle, just as The Dreamer bursts out of the corner with a lariat. Douglas quickly kips back up to his feet, this time being caught by a drop kick from Oracle. Oracle then picks up the young International Champion and heaves him out of the ring, where he's caught by Hiroshima. Hiro rams Matt Douglas back first into the ring post twice, then rolls him back into the ring. As he enters, Hiroshima and Oracle lock eyes, apparently forming a short alliance. They pick up Douglas, and bring him crashing to the mat with a double-team powerbomb. Judging that his isn't enough, Hiroshima brings Douglas into piledriver position, while Oracle mounts the turnbuckles. The duo hit the nasty spiked piledriver on the Champion, and Oracle quickly goes for the cover. Hiroshima puts a stop to that instantly, putting the boots to Oracle. He pulls Oracle off, and starts to apply a standing figure-four, but it's countered by a hair pull from Oracle. Oracle keeps a hand full of hair as he returns to his feet, then drops Hiroshima face first to the mat. The cover gets two, so Oracle changes plans, and turns to look for Matt Douglas. He sees him a second too late, as Douglas has launched himself over the top rope and onto Oracle with a hurican rana. He hooks the legs for two, but a boot to the face from Hiroshima breaks the count. Both Oracle and Douglas get back to their feet, and all three men start brawling with various punches and kicks to each other. Surprisingly, Douglas gets the best of the exchange, despite being outweighed by 50 lbs. by both Oracle and Hiroshima. A double inverted Russian Legsweep brings both of his opponents to the mat, and Douglas quickly heads upstairs. He steadies himself up top, then comes flying off with The Impact on Hiroshima. He turns on the mat and covers for two before Oracle shoves him off of Hiroshima, and covers Hiro for two himself. Douglas stops that by applying an armbar on Oracle, which he quickly counters into a roll-up for two. Douglas gets back to his feet, only to be planted to the mat by a spinning DDT from Oracle. Oracle briefly considers going for the cover before deciding against it, and locking the champion into the Oracle's Dream in the dead center of the ring. The ref lifts Matt's arm once. It drops. He lifts it a second time. It drops. He lifts the arm a third time, and it's at that moment that Hiroshima headbutts Oracle in the back of the head, breaking the hold. Oracle spins around and smashes Hiro in the face with a roundhouse right, which is returned in kind. Douglas starts to recover in the corner while the two men brawl in the middle of the ring. Hiroshima ducks a final shot from Oracle and goes behind, hooking a full nelson, which he quickly turns into an Oblivion Dragon Suplex. He doesn't get the chance to attempt to cover, however, as Matt Douglas kicks him out of the ring again, then goes for the cover himself. He hooks the leg tighly, and the resulting lateral press gets him three. Matt Douglas rolls off of Oracle and out of the ring, right in front of Hiroshima. Hiroshima delivers a stinging short-arm clothesline, then rolls the International Champ back into the ring. He stands over him menacingly, while Oracle himself heads to the outside. Hiroshima positions Douglas for Oblivion, but that idea stops short when Oracle re-enters the ring with a chair and clocks Hiro with it. Oracle then starts whacking away at Douglas, and starts to alternate between beating the two men. He stops when he sees Dead head towards the ring, and he warns the leader of the FtfWo not to enter the ring. Undaunted, Dead does just that, then points at his head, daring Oracle to swing at him. Oracle looks ready to comply, raising the chair above his head, and gets caught with a kick to the gut. Dead nails the Crucifix in short order, then grabs Hiroshima, and aids him out of the ring, as Warfare heads to commercial... Result: Matt Douglas by Pinfall Relaxing The WarfareScreen went to footage shot earlier in the day. The scene showed a training session back at the Ninja's kickboxing gym somewhere in the wilds of Canada. The Ninja looked tired, and slightly frustrated. "Okay Tom..." Tom seemed to be ignoring him as he pawed through a magazine he was holding. "Hey Ninja, this is pretty cool... it's an interview with Havok and... hey wait a minute... where are Havok's pants? What kind of magazine is this?" The Ninja lost his patience. "Tom-San!" Tom turned, dropping the magazine in disgust. The Ninja nodded that he had gotten Tom's attention, and began. "Okay....Tom-San, today we're going to be working on meditating. The key to it is to let go of all your muscles. You need to be in a total state of relaxation. Ready?" Tom nodded firmly. "Yes-sir, sensai!" The Ninja nodded in return. Things were going better now. "Alright, watch me." The Ninja sat down and began to get into the meditating position. In a moment he was there, and with his eyes closed he said, "Now you try it." Tom's brow furrowed, and he concentrated on his position. After a short struggle, he did it. The Ninje smiled as the scene faded out. Briana (c) vs. VL A dumpster filled with weapons was at ringside and fWo Women's Champion Briana was in the ring as the sounds of Mobb Deep's "Shook Ones, Part II" filled the arena in Atlanta. VL's entrance video played, but the hardcore legend in the making did not appear. After a minute of waiting, the WarfareScreen turned to snow, before the fans in the arena were greeted to VL, who was sitting in her hotel room in an easy chair, with crutches to her side. She addressed Briana with the following, "Briana, you little muthafuckin' bitch. You think you're real smart, signing for a match against someone who can't even walk, let alone wrestle. So I pulled one over on you, muthafucka. I ain't even show up to the arena. However, muthafucka, you ain't goin' home unbruised. I got a muthafuckin' replacement for myself. This person hates yo little muthafuckin' Britney Spears ass more than I do. This person feels that you have ruined their life. This person wants to save the fWo from your evil, Brimuthafuckinana. So without anymore bullshitting, hit the muthafuckin' music." At this point, the arena lights went out and the screen went dark. Then, a blast of pyro exploded on the stage and appearing in the aisle way was a figure with its blond hair in a ponytail and its back turned to the audience, wearing a silver shirt and black leather pants. As the WarfareScreen filled with the sights of New York City at night and the audience was commanded to "break the wall down." The fans exploded at the sight of this figure and the surrounding hoopla. However, the fans did not expect the three letters that would appear next on the WarfareScreen. "Q B K" The figure turned around with a microphone in its hand and revealed itself to be the former Apocolyptica, Kristen Wallace, who had returned to her original blond locks and appeared much different from how she looked when she was last seen in the fWo. She was wearing a silver bra that match her open shirt and appeared shorter than usual, as the fans booed loudly, upset by the way that they had been misled. She also appeared to be lightly tanned and had either lost a lot of muscle tone or put on a little weight in the midsection, understandable considering that she hadn't been competing actively in over eight months. She waited a while for the boos to die down before speaking. When she finally did speak, she said, "Were you expecting somebody else?" She took a breath before starting up once more, "For those of you who don't suffer from ADD, you might remember me as that cartoon, Apocolyptica. Well, Apocolyptica is dead. My real name is Kristen Wallace. You might also remember that this past July, I was crucified, humiliated, and left for dead in a parking lot during a fWo show. The months that followed were an absolutely horrible experience, one that I would not wish upon anyone. Anyone, that is, except for you, Briana. I know it was you who attacked me, Briana. I know it was you who harassed me nearly every day and night. I know that it was you who ruined my life. I won't let you ruin anybody else's life. I'm going to get rid of the disease before it spreads any further. This isn't about your title, Briana. This isn't about wrestling. This is life. No more black hair and pale skin. No more lifts in my boots. If I have to beat you bald, naked, and on fire, I will. There will be no turning back." As Kristen dropped the microphone, the fans in attendance and Briana herself were confused by the proceedings and Briana mouthed "What are you talking about?" as Kristen motioned for Briana to come out and face her on the ramp. Briana hesitated, but then ran across the ring and knocked Kristen down with an amazing plancha. The two women rolled around on the ramp, but Kristen gained the upper hand with a vicious slap to the face and then got up, pulling Briana by the hair and setting her up for a powerbomb, which she executed on the ramp. This knocked the Women's Champion out cold, but the blond beast wasn't done. She grabbed Briana by the hair and flipped her into the dumpster at ringside before slamming the dumpster's lid shut. She then pushed the dumpster up the ramp as Briana attacked the kick the lid open and screamed for help. Once the dumpster was on the stage, Kristen used all of her power to push the dumpster off the stage while Briana was still inside, to the shock of the crowd. fWo officials came out to pull Kristen to the back, while medical personnel opened the dumpster and attended to an unconscious Briana, as Warfare went to a commercial. Result: No Contest More relaxing The WarfareScreen went to footage shot earlier in the day, with the subtitle "2 minutes later" shown. The scene showed a training session back at the Ninja's kickboxing gym somewhere in the wilds of Canada. The Ninja looked pleased, and watched Tom in his meditating position. Finally, the Ninja clapped his hands together. "Good work, Tom! We're really getting somewhere!" There was no response. The Ninja looked closely at Tom. "Good job, Tom, you're really deep in there! Alright, let's go, we've got a plane to catch." Tom didn't move. From somewhere deep within him came a low snore. The Ninja's eyes grew wide. "Tom...? Tom, let's go. Tom-San?" Tom still didn't move, and the Ninja hung his head in frustration. "Ooooh brother." The scene faded out once more. Underground (c) vs. Riders A large 4R appears on the WarfareScreen as "Burning" begins to play and out to the ring walk the combination of Stone and Scott Collins, looking generally menacing and imposing as always. Collins, still wearing a small bandage on his head, takes the time to shout a few not-so-kind words into the camera directed at Perfect. Both members of the Four Riders roll simultaneously into the ring, awaiting the arrival of the fWo World Tag Team Champions. The music of the Underground starts and the fans cheer as Mike Bear and Wretch, wearing the fWo World Tag Titles, make their way down to the ring. Both quickly enter the ring and take off the titles. Wretch and Stone start off as the bell rings, and Wretch quickly gains the advantage with a hiptoss. He drops a corkscrew elbow before Stone can get up. Stone quickly gets back up, and ducks a charging lariat from Wretch, who bounces off the ropes towards Stone, who grabs him and takes him for a spin with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. She quickly covers, but Wretch kicks out at two. Stone brings Wretch up, and sends him hard into the turnbuckles where she opens up on him with a series of kicks. She backs up a few steps, before charging in with a splash that nails directly on its target. Wretch staggers out of the corner, and Stone grabs him and holds him in position after tagging in Scott Collins, who strikes with a hard punch to the jaw. Collins sends Wretch into the ropes, and slams him with a stiff spinebuster coming off. Collins quickly drops a leg and covers again, but only gets two. Working well with her, Collins tags in Stone, who drops a leg of her own before bringing Wretch up. She whips him into the ropes, but Wretch leapfrogs over her and hits Mike Bear on the blind tag. Stone turns too late to see this, and this time leapfrogs Wretch. As she turns around again, Wretch comes off the ropes and blasts her in the face with a back elbow, knocking her back to Mike Bear, who lifts her up in position for a backdrop driver. However, Stone flips over and lands on her feet, before hooking Bear up for a reverse suplex. Bear manages to reverse this before he can be taken over, powering the smaller Stone onto his shoulder, before slamming her down hard in a powerslam. Instead of covering, he brings her up once again, before nailing a picture-perfect superkick. Stone hits the mat like a sack of bricks, and Bear goes for the cover. He gets two before she kicks out. Bear brings her up again, and whips her into the ropes, catching her coming off and planting her with a side suplex. As she gets up, Bear leaps up into the air and nails a beautiful dropkick, a surprising show of agility. Bear covers, but again gets two. Bear brings Stone up, and hooks her up for a suplex. He holds her in position...and finally brings her down. He makes the tag to Wretch, who quickly scales to the top rope, before flying off with a flying splash. It nails directly on target, and Wretch hooks a leg. The move gets two before Collins rushes the ring to break the count. As the ref clears the ring of Collins, Wretch launches himself at Stone from a standing position, cleanly hitting a somersault senton. He brings her up quickly, whipping her into the ropes. Wretch bounces off the ropes himself, and clocks Stone with a leaping forearm. Wretch brings her up again, and dropkicks her hard in the back of the right knee. Having decided on a body part to work, Wretch pulls Stone to her feet, and takes her over with a dragon screw leg whip. Holding on, he drags her to her feet for another, taking her over again. Wretch quickly places Stone's leg over the bottom rope before exiting to the apron, and slingshotting himself into the ring with a legdrop directly onto it. Wretch brings Stone to her feet again, before tagging out to Bear, who slingshots himself in with a chop block to the back of the leg on Stone. Bear brings her up, and hits a hard kneebreaker. He brings her up, and whips her into the ropes. She leapfrogs over an attempted front chop block, but lands on her leg too hard and stumbles for a moment, allowing Bear to kick her hard in the back of the leg, taking her down. Bear, near the Riders' corner, tosses off a comment to Collins before going back to work on the knee. He drags Stone out to the middle of the ring, before dropping a knee on her knee. He then locks on a painful figure-four leglock, looking for the submission. Stone is in intense pain, but reaches deep down inside herself to roll the move over...and does so...only to have Bear quickly re-roll over back into the hold. Stone is in deep trouble, as her boyfriend Collins shouts encouragement from the corner. Stone gathers all her energy, and lunges for the ropes, making it. The ref counts to five for Bear to release the hold, and he does so at four. Stone struggles up to her feet with the help of the ropes, but is immediately thrown in a front flip by a front chop block from Bear...sending her flipping right into her corner. Bear realizes his mistake a second too late to stop Stone from tagging in Collins, who charges into the ring, clobbering Bear with a superkick. Wretch charges in, only to be hooked up and taken over in a head and arm suplex. Bear struggles to his feet, and Collins goes behind him, taking him down with a release german suplex. Wretch stumbles up, but Collins slams him hard to the mat. The ref is lost in all the chaos, and does not see when Bear catches Collins turning around with a low blow. With Collins doubled over, Bear shows great strength by lifting him onto his shoulders, and heading towards a corner. Wretch sees this and sees an opportunity to hit a double-team finisher, quickly shaking it off and climbing to the top rope. Bear turns to face the ring, and Wretch hooks the head of Collins from behind. He then leaps off and takes down Collins with an elevated swinging Depression, a sight to behold. Wretch makes the cover, but the ref informs him that he is not the legal man. Bear turns to play to the crowd, and the referee notifies Bear that he indeed is the legal man. Behind him, Stone has struggled up and produces a pair of brass knuckles, which she uses to clock Wretch with as he gets up, hitting a glancing blow but good enough to knock him down. Meanwhile Bear, annoyed, turns back towards the ring to make the cover himself, and as he does so is clocked by a leaping brass knuckle-enhanced punch from Stone. Stone again crumples to the mat as a result of her hurt knee, but Bear is down and out. Stone, still on the mat, quickly hides the brass knucks and drags Collins over to Bear, placing his arm over the body of Bear. The ref counts one...two...three, and new fWo World Tag Team Champions are crowned. The referee procures the belts, and hands one each to Collins and Stone, who help each other up to celebrate with the belts. As they do so, a second referee sprints to the ring, and begins to inform the original ref of what went down. When he hears this, he grabs the belts from the grasps of Collins and Stone and throws them into the corner. Collins and Stone are outraged, while opposite them Wretch has revived and is reviving his partner Mike Bear. Bear slowly gets to his feet as the bell re-rings, which is missed by Collins and Stone in their arguing. Bear and Wretch quickly storm up behind the Riders. Wretch kicks Stone in the back of the right leg before quickly executing the Depression on her. Bear spins Collins around, kicks him in the gut, and lifts him into a firemans carry, before dropping him in the Whiplash. Bear rolls onto Collins and the ref counts to three, and the Underground retains their titles. The ref hands them the belts and they walk to the back triumphant as Collins and Stone begin to recover in the ring, expectedly pissed as Warfare continues... Waru looked at Black Quicksilver's hand as if it was a wild animal of some kind. Finally he shook it, and nothing foul happened to him. Waru began to relax as Black Quicksilver started talking again. French Foundation vs. Dead, Perfect & Mystery Partner The arena shook with emotion as "Juke Joint Jezebel" began to play and out walked the French Foundation, comprised of the fWo World Champion Flying Frenchie, the fWo Cruiserweight Champion Courage, and former fWo World Champion Primetime. The trio walked to the ring with plenty of confidence, while the fans around ringside showed just how much they loathed the French Foundation. The Foundation shared the same detestation with the fans, glaring at them in such a manner one could see the Foundation didn't care about these American peasants. The Foundation entered the ring and the Frenchie held his treasured piece of tin high above the air for the world to see, with an expression of pride gleaming on his face. Courage lifted his Cruiserweight Title into the air, while Primetime pointed at the two titles and claimed the Foundation would soon have a third. The Foundation were interrupted by the loud music of Marilyn Manson, more specifically the cult hit song "Beautiful People". The arena darkened as a red spotlight highlighted the stage, while dozens of strobe lights with various colors flickered throughout the arena. Fog eminated from the stage as two figures from the backstage area emerged. The clean shaven head of Dead and the well maintained hair of Perfect were obvious from the get go. Dead was expressionless as he walked towards the ring, while Perfect looked at Frenchie with a "you're pathetic" look chiseled upon his face. As Perfect & Dead neared ringside, Primetime & Courage slingshotted themselves over the top rope, taking both men down with a simultaneous crossbody. The two men then lifted Dead & Perfect up, before Primetime and Courage then moved out of the way as Frenchie leapt over the top rope with a suicide plancha. Frenchie accurately took down both men, before rolling Dead into the ring. Primetime rolled Perfect into the ring as well, while Courage stood guard at ringside, anxiously anticipating the "third man" the FTfWo had promised. Courage glanced back every so often as Primetime & Frenchie fought off Dead & Perfect. Frenchie whipped Dead into the turnbuckles as Perfect and Primetime went out to their respective corners. Frenchie wasted no time in going for the kill early, as he attempted a cartwheel handspring elbow into Dead, but Dead caught him with a waistlock, before he brought him out to the center of the ring, and hit a release suplex. Dead sprung to his feet, and a few moments later Frenchie returned to a vertical position as well. Dead charged for a lariat but Frenchie sidestepped it and went into a waistlock of his own, before maneuvering into a victory roll. Dead shouldered out at two, and Frenchie went on the offensive attack. Whipping Dead into the ropes, the World Champion dug into his bag of tricks and and caught the bald meanie off guard with his patented eye gouge, before taking him down with a quick snap suplex. Frenchie then quickly tagged out to Primetime, who came in and went on a flurry of offensive moves. A double-team DDT with the assitance of Frenchie took Dead out, before Primetime followed it up with a quick somersault legdrop. He covered Dead, but Dead kicked out at two. Primetime decided to go for the kill early, and began setting up Dead for the Primetime Bomb. With Dead positioned, he went for it, but Perfect came in and clothesline Primetime to the mat. Dead struggled up as Perfect went back to the corner, before Dead eventually began to make his way over on his own two feet, preparing to tag in his partner. Primetime interrupted his chance with a double axehandle from behind, before he snapmared Dead into the center of the ring, before kicking him in the back. Courage continued to stand guard at ringside, before Frenchie invited Courage up on the ring apron to join in the ass kicking festivities. Primetime tagged out to Courage, and he and Courage whipped Dead into the ropes, before they went for a double super kick, taking Dead out in style. Courage followed it up with a side headlock, before he smoothly transitioned into a running bulldog. He tagged out to Primetime once more, before they whipped Dead to the ropes. Perfect made a blind tag to Dead, unbeknownst to Primetime & Courage. As Dead returned he ducked the double team clothesline attempted and then took both men out with a lariat, before rolling to the outside to rest. Perfect came in and Frenchie did as well, and the two men collided. Both men exchanged a flurry of punches while Dead gathered a chair at ringside. Frenchie gained the advantage on Perfect with a thumb to the eye, before he whipped him to the ropes... Perfect reversed and as Frenchie bounced off the ropes, Dead slammed the metal into his back, and Frenchie staggered right into a kick to the stomach by Perfect, before he followed it up a swinging neckbreaker. Perfect went for a cover, and at the count of two he was pulled off of by Primetime. Primetime dragged him out to the floor, and a brawl ensued there. As the referee went to the outside to break up the brawl, Dead went into the ring and as Courage approached him Dead nailed him square in the head with a chair shot. Dead waited for Frenchie to get up, before swinging, but Frenchie ducked, and then pulled a new trick out of the bag with a spinning heel kick, which sent the chair into Dead. Perfect hit his finisher on Primetime on the outside, before sending him over the guardrail into the sea of fans at ringside. Frenchie covered Dead as the referee went back into the ring, and as he got to two Perfect pulled the referee onto the floor. Perfect and the referee argued on the outside, and Perfect reminded him he was the legal man. Courage came out on to the floor, and he charged at the World's Greatest Athlete. As he did, Perfect caught him off guard with a drop-toe-hold into the ring steps. Perfect then sent Courage into the sea of fans as well after executing his finisher for a second time tonight, before he joined Dead in the ring. Back in the ring Dead lifted Frenchie up in the center of the ring and hit the Crucifixion. Frenchie crumpled in a heap in the ring, and things didn't look good for the World Champion. Primetime and Courage struggled with the masse of screaming fans and members of security who have no clue whatsoever what it's like to have rows of fans standing in front of you blocking your view, thus making the well earned money you spent on tickets to be a total waste. Back in the ring, Perfect came in and laid out the Frenchie with a third rendition of his "Perfect Game", further rendering him unconcscious. The two men debated on who should cover the fallen champ. Just as Primetime & Courage were about to make their way back through the crowd into the squared circle, the other members of the FTfWo (Heaven, Living, Hell and now Hiroshima) appeared amongst the dozens of fans at ringside, brawling with the two men to the back. This left Frenchie alone in the ring. Suddenly, on the stage, a familiar face began to make his presence felt. As he walked closer to the ring, the crowd pondered why he was there. He appeared somewhat different than the last time we saw him last nearly four months ago because of the leather jacket he sported and new goatee he didn't have before, but the confident stroll gave little doubt to who he was. He entered the ring as Dead & Perfect stepped back warily, not sure of what the man was going to do. Afterall, he had been friends with the man inside the ring laid out on the mat for over a year. The man looked down at his former friend with a sympathetic look on his face, before reaching down and helping him up to his feet. Pierre looked at his old friend with confusion on his face, before the man hooked him and took him down to the mat with an inverted Russian legsweep. He then covered him for the pinfall, thus ending the match. Mitch Wilson then stood up and casually took off the leather jacket, revealing a "FTfWo" t-shirt. Wilson looked at Dead & Perfect who smiled at him with expressions of brillance, truly happy about what Wilson had done. Perfect walked over and greated Wilson, shaking his hand. Dead walked over and followed suit, before Dead & Perfect stood on either side of Wilson, raising his arms into the air, and welcoming him into the FTfWo. The crowd showed their displeasure for what they had just witnessed, jeering relentlessly and throwing sacks of popcorn, empty soda pop bottles and other numerous items into the squared circle. Wilson, Perfect & Dead looked out into the crowd with evil on their faces, while a team of medical personnel began to storm the ring, accompanied with a stretcher. The team entered the ring cautiously, dragging Frenchie out to the stretcher. They quickly applied oxygen to him, as he had sustained an abnormal amount of damage. The team strapped him onto the stretcher tightly, before preparing to take him backstage. They reached the backstage area, as more trash was thrown into the ring at the FTfWo. They loaded the fWo World Champion into a nearby ambulance, and all of this was being shown on the giant Warfare screen in the arena. Dead procured a microphone as the image of Frenchie remained on the screen, and Dead spoke. "Look at your World Champion... we destroyed him. Is this the best you've got, fWo? Is it? Well, your best isn't good enough. If this is the best you have... well, you guys are fucked, aren't you?" Dead laughed with a sinister grin as we faded to black, with the final image of Frenchie being loaded into an ambulance being etched into our memories forever...
