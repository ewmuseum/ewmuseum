Wednesday Warfare, 16 February 2000

16 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction "Hello... Baton Rouge!" Machina's arms were spread wide as he metaphorically kissed the sky. This was the life worth living, a man who was about to hear the words he had been waiting for his entire life. Or at least as long as he had known Penny. Penny... ah, Penny. His friends thought that he was crazy for getting involved with someone who so obviously disliked... no... hated wrestling, but Penny was the apple of his eye. Assuming he could fit an apple in there that is. But whatever, he stood outside the arena beneath flashing signs that informed visitors that this show, Wednesday Warfare, was sold out. Filled with fans... people who would be entertained tonight by the comic styling of Machina and his Coke sponsorship. He was getting paid some big bucks for this too... nearly twenty grand. All he had to do was get into the ring, explain the glory of Coca Cola, and the check was as good as his. Nothing could stop him now. Above his head thunder rumbled, and Machina looked up. Looked like it was going to rain tonight, and Machina hoped it was the good rain and not the creepy Rain rain that came out of that long haired hippie that was on the roster. Before he could ponder this any further, a car pulled up and Machina spotted the blond hair that belonged to the head he had fallen deeply in love with. Penny was here, and thoughts of hippies and rain passed from his mind. Machina waved, "Penny! Penny! Over here!" Penny spotted him and ran toward him, her hair blowing in the wind, her eyes half closed and her head arched back. "Oh Machina... Machina..." The two embraced in the parking lot, and behind Machina Rudo and Sid exchanged a smile. Except Rudo's smile wasn't as strong as Sid's, and he looked nervous... edgy somehow. Machina saw none of this however, and Sid was dabbing at his eyes with a hanky. Machina looked into Penny's eyes with a question, and Penny nodded. "Yes Machina... yes. I will marry you." Machina leapt in the air, fist pumping. He yelped, "Whoo yeah!" and then clamped his mouth shut again, embarrassed at uttering the words of his old enemy. He ran around in circles, excited and overjoyed. Sid clapped him on the back, proud for his friend. Rudo walked over to shake Penny's hand, and she pulled him closer so she could whisper in his ear. "...but I'm only doing it so I can stay close to you... lover." Rudo darted a glace sideways to see if Machina had overheard, and saw that he had not. Behind them the arena loomed huge, casting a shadow over the parking lot and the future of the Lone Gunmen. The sounds of explosions could even now be heard from within, as the show took to the stage... Eron lays out reality Warfare began with the usual explosions of fireworks, and inside the ring stood Cairn and the Showstopper. The Showstopper pointed to his shirt which read, "SHWSTEPPER SECKS MACENE". The two were about to start wrestling with the lights dimmed and Physical by Nine Inch Nails began to blare over the arena speakers. The fWo Internet Champion walked out onto the ramp and posed for a moment with the title dangling from one clenched fist. A look of satisfaction and smugness was written across his face, and he strode down to the ring with the confidence of a veteran. Getting into the ring, he looked at the two wrestlers that were ready for their match and laughed. Asking for a microphone, he addressed them and the audience. "Take a walk you two... in case you didn't notice the show has begun, and the fWo isn't going to allow you two losers to kill the ratings with your dark match quality wrestling. Now go on... beat it... your champion has spoken!" Cairn and the Showstopper exchanged an angry glace, but rather than press the issue decided to head backstage and leave Eron to his own devices. Eron held up the title once more, ignoring the boos that were coming from the crowd. "This is my belt... and you will respect your champion!" The crown booed louder, and Eron pushed both hands against his ears in defiance. Finally, he laid the belt on the mat and leaned back against the turnbuckle. "You see this? This is the Internet Championship. Now, I was planning on defending this at fWo Trapped in two weeks... but the wrestlers that I hand picked for the job... the best wrestlers in the fWo I might add... couldn't get the job done. You two little wet ends can go back to being curtain jerkers and leave the wrestling to the stars... to Eron!" The crowd booed louder still, and Eron had to scream to be heard over them. "And you little punk bitch Wilson? You're not even in Stinky's league. But mark my words... I understand how many Eron fans there are out there, and that's why I've arranged for a special match for you all tonight. I know, I know... I told you all I wasn't going to show up tonight, and if it wasn't for a man who is known the world over giving me a call to wrestle, I would have stayed home rather than come to the hell hole of America that is Baton Rouge. But I lined up a special match with one of the brightest stars in the world of professional wrestling... and how could I say no to that? Who else but Eron would bring you the most dangerous man in all of wrestling? That's right... I'll survive this match... if he lets me! So come on down suplex machine... and take a breath... of FRESH DEATH!" As the arena lights dimmed to bright orange, all eyes turned to the entrance ramp where smoke began to pour out of the gate, and loud rock music began to scream over the speakers... Eron (c) vs. The Inhuman Suplex Machine The crowd began to roar as Matt Panzer announced the arrival of the Inhuman Suplex Machine. A small form could be seen amidst the smoke, and then he stepped into the air. A robot appeared to be making it's way down the entrance ramp. The Inhuman Suplex Machine walked jerkily to the ring, shiny metal pants and flashing lights mounted on it's boots. A metal helmet adorned the head, and the fans groaned as they realized that Mike Heftel had been booked for another match. Eron laughed as the robot entered the ring, and charged it with a quick lariat. The Inhuman Suplex Machine fell back against the ropes, but then launched itself at Eron and grabbed him around the waist. The Inhuman Suplex Machine picked Eron up and slammed him to the mat with a reverse suplex, and then backed off to let Eron get back to his feet. Eron did, and looked angry. He yelled, "Dammit Heftel... stop kidding around and fall down when I hit you!" The Inhuman Suplex Machine made no response, and when Eron attempted a chop it grabbed his arm and turned him around locking the leg and then whipping him back with a snap suplex. Eron was slower to get up this time, and he rubbed his neck and cursed. Eron extended a hand, but before The Inhuman Suplex Machine could grab hold he slid around behind him and applied a neck lock. Unable to get it cinched in because of the metal parts of the costume, The Inhuman Suplex Machine quickly reversed the hold and executed another reverse suplex. Eron pounded the mat with both hands, and rolled to the outside. Inside the ring the robot began to chant "Survive... survive... survive..." as it walked to the outside after Eron. Eron was chased around the ring by the robot, and finally slid back inside. As The Inhuman Suplex Machine followed in after him, Eron kicked downward. The Inhuman Suplex Machine was unable to get to it's feet, and Eron repeatedly kicked at the upper torso and head of the robot. Finally Eron dropped an elbow, but screamed in pain as the metal costume caused more damage to him than Heftel. Eron rolled away holding his arm, and The Inhuman Suplex Machine was quick to grab him and suplex him repeatedly onto the mat. The Inhuman Suplex Machine went for a cover, but Eron got the foot on the ropes at two and a half and again rolled to the outside. Eron pointed up at the ring and yelled that he wasn't getting back inside, and the referee argued that he needed to get back in before he was counted out. Eron just shrugged and started to walk away, but at that moment Mitch Wilson emerged from the crowd with a steel chair, and leveled it on the head of the champion. Seeing this, the referee called for the bell to end the match. As the referee and The Inhuman Suplex Machine looked on, Mitch Wilson continued to hit Eron with the chair. Security hit the scene and pulled Mitch off the champion, but the damage was done. As medical personnel came out to look at Eron, The Inhuman Suplex Machine attempted to get out of the ring. Too heavy for very agile movements, the costume became caught in the ropes and more members of the fWo backstage crew had to come to the ring to dislodge him. As they did so, Warfare went to a commercial break. Result: Eron by DQ Toughman Wednesday Rudo walked backstage, looking for Scott Collins. Elsewhere in the arena Machina was celebrating with Penny and getting ready for his big exhibition later on. Rudo was happy for Machina, but he was also worried. Things were getting out of control, and he wasn't even sure how they had started to go wrong. He knew that he had been friendly with Penny... but now it seemed like she wanted him. Rudo hadn't done anything he shouldn't have... but he had still lied to his wife Brandi when she asked him who he had been talking to on the phone. This was trouble... big trouble. "Hey Rudo, looking for me?" Scott Collins was in front of him, and in his deep thought he had nearly walked right into him. Thoughts of Penny and Machina were dismissed for the moment, something Rudo was finding easier and easier to do for some reason. "Yeah Scott! Boy, it's good to see you. Listen, I was hoping you could help toughen me up a little more... you know... give me some pointers like you've been doing? I think it's really helping... I can now lift more weight than I could just a month ago!" Collins smiled. "How much weight could you lift a month ago, Rudo?" Rudo started to answer, and then stopped. For some reason he was having trouble remembering. Collins could see that Rudo couldn't remember, and smiled wider. "It's not important little Canadian boy, it's not important... but listen, I've got just the thing for you. You see, I've been trying to find out the real name of Stone..." Rudo looked confused. "Who?" Collins smiled still wider. "Stone. The woman that hangs around with me?" Rudo smiled, recognition finally coming. "Oh yeah... yeah... Stone. Wasn't she like Stevie's girlfriend?" Collins shook his head. "No, you're thinking of VL. Now listen Rudo, I want you to sneak into Stone's dressing room and look in her bag for things that might have her name on it. She's in the shower right now, so you've got to be extra tough and sneaky to get it. You got it?" Rudo looked doubtful. "Just her name? That doesn't sound very hard..." Collins smiled. "Oh, it's not... it's a real easy one. Now go to it!" Collins pointed to Stone's dressing room, where the sound of running water could be heard coming from within. Rudo shrugged and went for the door, turning the handle easily in his hand. He entered, and Collins smiled wider. From behind him War and Plague appeared from around a corner. "He's going to get killed, Scotty." Collins turned. "It's Scott. Now come on... that time boy has challenged both of you to a match. We've got to find him before that and teach him some respect..." Frank Snow vs. Double S "I Know You Want Me" starts playing as strobe lights fill the arena and The Dame, Double S walks daintily to the ring. "Whatever" begins to play and out to the ring, holding the fWo Hardcore Title high, walks Frank Snow, wheeling with him a excessively large cart. He stops outside the ring, and begins to unload, throwing chairs in the ring as Double S dodges. Snow then pulls a table out from the cart and begins to set it up. Impatiently, Double S runs into the ropes, before leaping out towards Snow with a plancha. Snow sees him coming and shoves the cart in front of him, and it catches the flying Double S neatly. Snow then topples the cart, sending Double S into the ring apron, and finishes off the table. He turns his attention to Double S, hopping onto the apron before bringing Double S with him. Snow sets Double S up in a powerbomb position and lifts him up. He then runs on the apron, before leaping off with a Liger Bomb through the table. He holds it and the ref counts, but the count is broken up at two by Robert Jensen with a wrench, who clocks Snow. Jensen stomps on Snow a couple times, stomps on Double S twice for the hell of it, and rolls Double S onto Snow. The ref again begins to count, but it is broken up as VL comes rushing down the rampway wielding a chair. She bashes Jensen on her way down, then smashes Double S in the back, breaking up the count. Jensen is up, and quickly turns VL around before dropkicking the chair into her face. She goes down, and Jensen takes the mounted position and begins raining down punches. Meanwhile, Frank Snow has somewhat recovered and begins to get up. Double S has as well, and Frank "helps" him to his feet. He kicks him, doubling him over, and spikes him with a piledriver. Snow then grabs the chair formerly possessed by VL and rolls into the ring before heading up to the top rope. He positions the chair for the Snowfall, but out of nowhere Hellrazor appears behind him, wielding Lamont. Lamont reaches out and scratches the hell out of the bare back of Snow, shocking him and sending him flying out to the outside in a somersault. Fortunately for him, Snow lands right on Double S, unintentionally hitting a flying somersault senton. The referee counts and reaches three before the pin can be broken up, and Snow retains his Hardcore Title as Hellrazor joins Jensen and VL to make it a 3-way brawl as Warfare continues... Result: Frank Snow by Pinfall Words of wisdom The Flying Frenchie sat in his locker room with Courage and Primetime. On a bench next to them lay a bounty of titles... the World Heavyweight Championship, two Tag Team titles, and the Cruiserweight Championship. "Zut. We've got half of ze belts in our posse'ion. Primetime grinned. "More than half actually. With our win on Slaughter I'm now a four time tag team champion... that got to be like a record or something. But Frenchie... I still can't believe that you picked me to face you at Trapped. What if I beat you?" The Frenchie scoffed. "Nonsen'e... zat is impossible, even for you Primetime. But regar'less... if Primetime were to win zen ze the Foundation would still have half of ze belts... which is ze way zat it should be. We shall go to Paris and Jasmin will meet us and zere will be a party for all." The Frenchie's smile turned then to a scowl. "But now it is time for ze revenge. Mitch Wilson... I gave him ze chance to be ze star and how does zit repay to me? By joining ze filth of ze Earth and backstabbing ze Foundation. Well tonight Mitch... tonight I am going to rip out ze spleen and spit down ze nostril. Mitch Wilson will not escape ze wrath of ze Frenchie." There was a knock at the door, and Courage stepped up to answer it. Ultra Violet had knocked at the door, and seemed confused when Courage answered. He looked at the number on the door, and down at the number that had been written in crayon on his hand. He looked in as if expecting the Frenchie to turn into Violet. "Is this room Qty C?" The Frenchie growled and tossed one of the Tag Titles at him. Ultra Violet ducked, and it sailed over his head into the opposite wall of the hallway. The Frenchie picked up the other belt and Ultra Violet pointed at it. "Hey, you've got Q of those! Where did you find the Qnd one?" The Tag Title sailed through the air at Ultra Violet's head but Courage snatched it before it could strike him. Courage turned back to the Frenchie. "Come on, Pierre... you know tossing a title at a moron is bad luck... we've got to defend these against Vommit and Snuggles later tonight..." Courage slammed the door, and Ultra Violet was sent spiraling backward in the hallway. He rubbed his noggin, and it reminded him of food. "That was rude... someone should teach that meanie a lesson," Ultra Violet said as he wandered away in search of his dressing room. French Foundation (c) vs. Underground Warfare steamrolled on with the next match for the fWo World Tag Team Titles. Coming out first were the most recent champions The Underground, comprised of Wretch & Mike Bear. Wretch & Bear greeted the fans with high fives as they walked down the shiny entrance ramp, before entering the squared circle. Once inside, they became all business, focused on regaining their fWo World Tag Team Championship gold. "Bawitdaba" began to play and that signaled the imminent presence of Primetime & Courage, who came out moments later holding the fWo World Tag Team Titles high overhead. Courage had his prized Cruiserweight Title wrapped securely around his waist as they entered the ring for this monumental rematch. The referee held both belts for the crowd to see, and moments later the match was underway. Wretch & Courage started things off as they did on Slaughter, and this time they began with a classic "test of strength". Courage challenged Wretch to one, and finally Wretch gave in and went for it after some urging from the crowd. The two men locked up as Courage gained the early advantage, taking Wretch to his knees. But Wretch gained some momentum after the crowd cheered him on. Wretch capitalized; forcing Courage down to the mat in agony. Rather than continue the "test of strength", Wretch grasped just one of Courage' hands before climbing up to the top rope... he walked the top rope to the middle before flying off into a hurracanrana. He hooked the legs, but Courage kicked out at two. Wretch continued with a crisp Irish Whip... which sent the Cruiserweight Champion back first into the corner. As Courage staggers out Wretch hooked him for a Northern Lights Suplex with a bridge. Wretch was a half count away from winning, but Primetime interrupted which prompted the crowd to shower him with boos. Wretch ignored Primetime's interruption and proceeded, pulling Courage up by the hair and taking him down with a snapmare takeover, followed by a front facelock. Wretch then brought Courage up to a vertical base and hit a nice running bulldog, before following it up with an elbow drop. Wretch walked over towards Bear and seemed ready to tag out, before he decided to cover Courage. The Cruiserweight Champion kicked out at two, thwarting Wretch's attempt at victory. Wretch pulled Courage up and gave him a stiff forearm to the face, followed by a chop to the chest. He then dutifully whipped Courage sternum first into the corner and as Courage staggered out backwards Wretch rolled him up for another pin, and Courage was able to kickout once more. Both men sprung to their feet and Wretch nailed Courage with a shot to the tender nether regions as Primetime complained to the referee about a fast count. Wretch followed it up with a piledriver, before pointing to the top rope. Bear seemed a bit disturbed that Wretch hadn't tagged out yet, but didn't mind since Wretch was on a roll and appeared to be going for the kill. Wretch climbed to the top rope and flew off with a senton bomb, and fortunately for Courage he moved out of the way just in time. Wretch hit the mat hard, and Courage crawled over and tagged in the man who will meet The Flying Frenchie at Trapped on February 26. There would be no escaping for Wretch as Primetime quickly took over. A kneedrop here, a kick to the chest there, and Primetime was in control. Primetime whipped Wretch into the ropes and duck for a backdrop, but Wretch leapfrogged it and bounced off the ropes on the other side. As he turned around Primetime caught a jumping Wretch and turned it into a 180 degree spinebuster, sending Wretch into the mat with authority. A hush fell over the crowd as Wretch truly hit the mat hard, and Primetime went for a cover. Bear came in and kicked Primetime in the back, breaking up the pin. Primetime appeared angry but continued the match nonetheless. He whipped Wretch to the ropes near Bear and Bear saw his chance. He hit a blind tag, and as Wretch returned Primetime took him down with a powerslam. Primetime hooked Wretch's leg for the pin, but the referee couldn't count it since Wretch was no longer the legal man. Bear pulled Primetime off by the hair and kicked him in the stomach, before taking him out with a sudden Michinoku Driver. The ref went to the mat for the pin, but at the count of two Courage interrupted with a sitting dropkick as Wretch went out to his corner. Courage & Primetime brought Bear to his feet and whipped him to the ropes before ducking for a back body drop, but instead Bear took them out with a double reverse neckbreaker. Courage slid out onto the floor holding his back, but he was too close to Wretch and Wretch took a risk by running and jumping off the ring apron into a hurrancanrana on the floor. Wretch jumped back onto the ring apron as the crowd exploded in cheers. Bear brought Primetime to his feet and staggered him with some stiff forearm shots, before whipping him to the ropes. Primetime reversed it however, and as Bear neared the ropes Wretch tagged in blindly. Bear returned and Primetime went for a kick, but Bear caught his foot. Primetime went for an enziguiri... but Bear ducked and Primetime hit the mat. Bear pulled him up by his hair before turning him around and taking him out with the Whiplash. Mike Bear quickly had to move out of the way though as his partner flew off with a senton bomb, this time hitting for good. Wretch hooked the leg and since he was now the legal man, the referee counted the pin, and three seconds after that the fWo World Tag Team Titles changed hands for a second time in 72 hours. Everyone wondered if they would change again this Sunday at Slaughter since The Underground would be facing the Guano Apes. Nevertheless, one of those two teams would meet the team of Havok & The Black Panther at Trapped. The Underground celebrated in the ring, while Havok & Panther came out onto the stage to stare at the champions, telling them that one way or another they would be the new champs come February 26. Result: Underground by Pinfall Mission Possible Rudo looked at the steam coming from the bathroom with no small amount of trepidation. Stone was in there, and seemed to be singing something. It sounded like "Uptown Girl" but he wasn't sure. Rudo looked around the room for her bag, and finally spotted it laying against a box of flammable newspapers and in front of some cans of gasoline. It was dark in the room, and Rudo looked for the light. Not spotting it, he reached into his pocket for a lighter, and lit it. He walked over to the bag, his eyes never leaving the bathroom door. The song had changed to "Rump Shaker" and Rudo shivered slightly. He unzipped the bag and looked inside. The first thing he spotted was a leather whip. Cringing slightly he pushed it aside and dug through the bag's contents. Several wrestling outfits, what appeared to be a spare set of jeans... and this was odd. Rudo pulled out what appeared to be a collar and leash. Funny, he didn't remember Stone having a dog. He continued to dig, and gasped as his hands landed on something smooth and banana shaped. He pulled out the cucumber and looked at it. Vegetables. Interesting. Deeper still he found several photos, some tic tacs, a piece of paper with some numbers written on it, and finally her wallet. Jackpot. Rudo opened the wallet and looked inside, hunting for the driver's license. He found more photos, some money, and then finally what he was looking for. A drivers license obtained in the town of Springfield. He couldn't make out the state of the license, but what he was really interested in was the name. He read it aloud, "Stone." From behind him came an answer. "Yes?" Rudo's eyes went wide and he slowly turned around. Behind him was Stone, dripping wet and wrapped in a towel. She wasn't smiling. Rudo gulped. "Oh shit." Fans stirred nervously in their seats as they waited for the next match to begin. A loud crash came from backstage, but was then followed by silence. Fans in the arena shrugged. Such noises were commonplace in the fWo. Matt Douglas (c) vs. Havok The crowd waited in anticipation as Matt Panzer rattled off Havok's vitals, including the fact that tonight he would challenge Matt Douglas for the fWo International Championship. As Panzer finished Havok's music started up, and soon the former cruiserweight champion and Millennium Man made his way down to the ring, looking pumped to face the man who was once his stablemate, though under a different name. Havok hopped over the top rope and scaled the turnbuckles, playing to the crowd, when Creed's "Higher" began. Matt Douglas stepped out onto the entrance ramp carrying the international title belt and began quickly jogged down to the ring. Douglas slid in and exchanged a few words with Havok before going to his own corner to hand the belt off to a ring attendant. The bell rang, and the match was on. Havok studied the champion and before locking up with him, but soon found himself pushed to the corner. Douglas eased up off in a clean break, and the two once again locked up in the center of the ring. Havok wrenched back Matt's arm behind his back, but Douglas reversed it into a hammerlock of his own. Havok attempted to reverse it again but Douglas held on. Having no other choice, Havok grabbed Douglas by the head, leapt into the air, and snap marred him over. Douglas rolled back to his feet almost as soon as he hit the mat, but Havok was waiting for him with a dropkick. Douglas smacked the mat hard, but once again propelled himself back to his feet almost instantly. Havok nailed him with a stiff forearm, backed him to the ropes, and whipped Douglas across. Douglas rebounded and came back, but Havok chucked him straight up in the air with a back body toss. Douglas hit the canvas face first, but Havok allowed the champ no reprieve. As Douglas crept to his knees Havok laid into him with several fists before throwing him to the outside. Douglas slowly recovered, giving Havok more than enough time to nail him with a running tope. Both men careened into the ringside barricades, and several moments passed by until Havok made it to his feet. The challenger picked up the fallen champion and rolled him back into the ring. Douglas stumbled around for a bit, trying to get to his feet, but was sent back to the mat after Havok launched himself onto his opponent with a springboard clothesline. Havok covered but only managed a two count before Douglas kicked out. Havok picked up Douglas and slammed him down, then signaled that he was going up top. The former cruiserweight champion climbed to the top turnbuckle and jumped, but Douglas kipped up out of the way in the nick of time. The crowd popped for this display of playing possum, and continued to cheer as Douglas sent Havok to the ropes and brought him down with a hurracanrana. The ref hit the mat counting, but only reached two until a slightly stunned Havok kicked out. Douglas was relentless, however, and stayed on top of Havok with boots to the chest. Havok backed himself into a corner, though, and the referee broke the two up. Matt Douglas wasn't about to let his momentum fade, however, and as soon as Havok was up Douglas charged him and pressed him into the turnbuckles. Havok collapsed back, and Douglas hopped to the top turnbuckle. Havok began to stumble out of the corner, and as soon as he did Douglas leapt and bulldogged the challenger to the mat. Douglas turned him over and covered, but Havok kicked out at two and a half. Matt brought Havok to his feet and whipped him to the corner, but Havok reversed and sent Douglas there instead. But, as Havok charged in after him, Douglas propped himself up on the top turnbuckle and leap frogged back and over his opponent. Havok nailed the corner head first and teetered a bit before turning right into Douglas, who planted Havok with a quick dangerous DDT. Havok flattened out and rolled to his back while Douglas climbed to the top rope. Then, to the roar of the crowd, Matt came flying off with the Impact. One, two, three, and Matt Douglas remains the fWo International Champion. Result: Matt Douglas by Pinfall Just another day in paradise War, Plague, and Scott Collins walked silently through the halls of the arena, obviously angry from the expressions on their faces. They kicked open doors, pushed ring attendants out of the way, and cursed anyone near to them. It was clear that they were hunting for someone, and after Ric Chronos' interview it was pretty obvious who. Scott Collins grimaced with anger as door after door revealed nothing to them. They rounded another corner in the arena, and suddenly Collins was face to face with Ultra Violet. The two looked at each other silently, and doubtless their thoughts returned to what was the closest of friendships and most biter of rivalries. Collins moved first, and then Ultra Violet continued on his way in the other direction. Ultra Violet walked on, looking for Violet as he attempted to remember his dressing room. He tripped over something, and went sprawling into the arena wall. Rubbing his head, he looked back at what he had tripped over. Kid Cool lay there, and appeared to have been sleeping on the arena floor until Ultra Violet knocked him over. Kid Cool frowned. "Oh, it's you. What do you want?" Ultra Violet smiled. "Violet. I can't find her anywhere." Kid Cool sulked. "She really liked me you know... it wasn't just for fooling." Ultra Violet smiled a wan smile. "Now Buckly Beaven, I know I'm not the brightest cookie in the lighthouse, but I'm pretty sure the pretty lady wasn't that fond of you." Kid Cool stuck out his bottom lip and pouted. Ultra Violet looked at him for a few minutes more, and then lost interest and wandered away. Kid Cool watched him go. "I'm better than him. The magic 8-ball said so. I've got a Cruiserweight Title match that I earned... I'll bet I could get Clarence in that match too. That would show him who the smarter wrestler was..." The clicking of heels came, and Kid Cool looked around. Nikki stood there, hands on hips and eying him carefully. "Who are you?" Kid Cool gulped. "I'm... Buckly." Nikki sighed. "I haven't slept with you yet, have I?" Kid Cool shook his head, confused. Nikki hiked a thumb toward an empty locker room. "Okay then, let's go." Kid Cool gulped. Rain vs. Chris Universal ... we returned from advertisements to find the man known only as Rain heading to the ring for his first match since Invasion! back on January 22 in France. His giant stature climbed over the top rope with ease, before he raised his arms before the crowd as they booed in kind. His attacks on the Strangler from Sunday Slaughter were played on the Warfare screen, prompting the audience to jeer even louder. Their boos soon turned to cheers as the arena system blasted "Do you buy what The Sellout is selling?" and out came Chris Universal. Universal seemed focused at the task at hand and charged to the ring, and the match started at once. Rain began with kicks to the back, and Universal struggled to his feet and the two men began going at it with a flurry of lefts and rights. Universal got the advantage and painted Rain into a corner and hit him with continuous rights to the jaw. Universal changed plans of attack, now bevying Rain with a bunch of forearms to the face. He whipped Rain into the other corner and followed him in, but after Rain hit the turnbuckles he countered with a jaw to the cheek of the former two-time, two-time Internet Champion. Rain then took a shot to Universal's midsection with a knee, before taking him down with a hip toss. He brought him back up and whipped him to the ropes, before going for a sleeperhold. He locked it in and the referee raised Universal's hand once, then for a second time. Both times his hand dropped, but on the third his hand maintained a vertical position, before he countered with a back suplex, sending Rain to the mat. Both men struggled to get to their feet, and Universal did first. He kicked Rain on the mat repeatedly, before bringing him up and nailing him with an elbow to the stomach. He sent him into the ropes and as Rain returned Universal went for a crucifix. Rain kicked out at one and a half, and the two men rushed to their feet. Rain took the advantage with a thumb to the eye, before whipping him to the ropes and taking him out with a big boot to the face. Rain brought Universal up once more and went for a hanging vertical suplex, but somehow Universal escaped and fell behind Rain. Universal rolled Rain up for the pin from behind, but Rain kicked out. Back on their feet, Rain charged and took Universal down with a stiff lariat. Rain followed with a slap to the chest as he pulled him up to his feet, before a nice sidewalk slam. Rain went for the hanging vertical suplex once more, and again he took too long as Universal fell behind him and spun him around, hitting the 'Cha-Ching'. The impact staggered Rain backwards and he went up and over to the top rope onto the floor, and Universal took that moment to rest. Suddenly, the Boston Strangler began to make his way down, and Universal distracted the referee, blabbing about stocks and bonds. Rain got to his feet but from behind Strangler took him down with the Boston Massacre, leaving it locked in for more than a minute as Universal continued to distract the referee. Finally, the Boston Strangler left to a huge ovation from the crowd as Rain was clearly out of it on the outside. Universal discontinued the distraction and the referee came to see Rain still out on the floor, and he began a ten count. Moments later Universal had won the match, and he rose his hands to a huge ovation. He walked to the outside and spat on Rain on the floor, before leaving as Warfare went on to more commercial breaks, which hopefully will not include those damn Coca-Cola commercials with that damn girl giggling. Result: Chris Universal by Count Out Cola Clash 2000 After a barrage of Coca Cola ads which included a nearly naked pre-teen girl sniffing the bubbles off a can of Coke, the lights in the arena went blue and the trademarked L in a circle wrote itself on the WarfareScreen. Machina appeared at the top of the entrance ramp, a bag filled with bottles of Coke in each hand and a Coca Cola hat on his masked head. He walked to the ring, opening the bottles up and spraying them into the crowd much to the dismay of owners of expensive electronic equipment. Once there he swung himself inside, saving one of the bottles of Coke for himself. He pulled a microphone from his tights, and smiling he looked around at the crowd. "Hello Baton Rouge!" The crowd responded with a mix of boos and cheers, and Machina sniffed the microphone suspiciously. Shrugging he continued. "You know, many of you many know me as a world class athlete and always tough competitor in and out of the ring. Sure, wrestling is my first love, but I kick some serious ass at Sorry." At the mention of the board game the crowd began to chant "ECW! ECW! ECW!" and Machina waited for them to settle themselves. When they did, he continued. "But what you might not know is that I couldn't have done any of it without the glorious taste of Coca Cola. It's always the right one, inside and outside of the ring. Available at the concession stand, it's always just the right thing to quench your thirst and make friends. Because Coca Cola isn't just a drink, it's a state of mind. Come on everyone... sing with me!" Machina pointed up to the Warfare Screen, where the spinning Coke logo had been replaced with song lyrics. With roughly half of the crowd following along, Machina sang, "I'd like to buy the world a Coke..." But before he could get any farther the Warfare Screen started to bend forward... and then it shattered outward. Cloth tore and metal crunched as an 18-wheeler emerged at the top of the entrance ramp. There was a moment of silence, and then... "TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" The door opened, and to the roar of the crowd the Mad Trucker exited the vehicle and pumped a fist into the air. With a large hose in one hand he let out another loud toot, and then pointed to Machina. "Listen here and shut your yapper! You keep talking about Coke... well I've got just one thing for you! Taste the Joy of Cola!" The Mad Trucker pointed the hose at the ring and Machina and turned a switch. Gallons of Pepsi suddenly surged into the ring, knocking Machina off his feet and coating the ring with the dark cola liquid. Machina tried to get to his feet and the Pepsi was aimed at him again, coating the Coca Cola spokesman with the contents of the truck... gallons and gallons of Pepsi. The Trucker finally dropped the hose and climbed atop his truck, once more pumping his fists into the air for the crowd. The crowd began to chant "Trucker! Trucker! Trucker!" And to the delight of the crowd another "TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOT!" was ripped forth as Warfare went to commercial. Flying Frenchie vs. Mitch Wilson With the main event now nearly upon them, the crowd is buzzing with anticipation. Despite the slight break where an ad for Trapped was shown while the ring crew cleaned up the mess of Pepsi that was sprayed around the ring, the crowd cheered and buzzed excitedly. The cheers turned to a zealous booing of Mitch Wilson when he emerges to "Calling All Stations". He cares not, indifferent to their hostility, having knowingly alienated himself from them upon his joining the FTfWo. Wilson becomes impatient in the ring after a short delay, yet just then, "Juke Joint Jezebel" breaks over the sound system and the Flying Frenchie becomes all too visible for those in attendance. However, the fWo World champion is met with a mixed reaction, possibly out of respect or many choosing the lesser of two evils in this match-up. No sooner has his music begun, when Frenchie is in the ring opposite Wilson. Undoing his title, which is not on the line, Frenchie meets Wilson, chest-to-chest, in the center of the ring. Words do not do justice to the emotion felt by both men, yet the Frenchie is first on the attack, taking Wilson by surprise with a go-behind, then slapping on a headlock before twisting it into a side-headlock takedown that has Wilson at his mercy on the mat. Frenchie continues, unloading with a flurry of quick punches before rising and falling into the ropes behind him, and dropping onto Wilson, introducing him to his elbow. Frenchie snatches one of Wilson's arms and applies a step-over Armbar, looking to hyperextend the arm. He then steps back over yet retains his hold on the arm and lifts Wilson, using a simple Armbar. Whipping Wilson towards him, he floors him with a short arm clothesline. Unrelenting, Frenchie has Wilson up and sends him to the ropes. He meets him on the way back with a hip-toss. Wilson only pops back up to his feet, yet Frenchie simply grabs him and hip-tosses him onto his back once more. Frenchie drops for a cover, yet at one and a half, Wilson manages to turn his shoulder. Frenchie now helps him to his feet before using an Irish whip to send his opponent into the corner. Frenchie follows in yet Wilson is able to explode out of the corner with a clothesline that upends the champion. He then drops and looks to apply an Armbar submission hold early. He is never able to fully lock on the hold, yet Frenchie feels the effects. Struggling to reach the ropes, he is able to find them, yet Wilson refuses to slacken the hold. He at last does so after repeated warnings from the referee, however by this time much damage has been done. Wilson begins to shake off any signs of ring rust, hooking a now lifted Frenchie and following through with a well-executed snap suplex. Not about to let up, he callously brings Frenchie upwards before pitching him into the ropes. Using a flapjack, Wilson has Frenchie in a very sticky situation, taking to the air before careening violently downwards and smacking the canvas. Mounting a fallen Frenchie, Wilson grabs each of his arms and has Frenchie wincing in unbearable pain with a standing Surfboard. Then letting up, he wraps his arms about Frenchie's waist in a waist-lock, and putting all of his weight in the opposite direction, falls backwards. In turn he lifts Frenchie with a wheelbarrow suplex that drops him on his back. Wilson hurries to smother Frenchie with a cover, and it appears he has wrapped this one up. Yet Frenchie reaches deep down inside to gather just enough strength to kick out at the last moment and stave off defeat. Wilson is a bit taken aback, and even more so when Frenchie from his prostrated position lashes out with a kick that strikes him across the face. Wilson is brought involuntarily to his feet by this hit. Frenchie rises as well and taking a step towards Wilson, leaves his feet and connects with a dropkick that sends Wilson tumbling backwards into the corner. He smacks into the turnbuckle and staggers back out where Frenchie in one fluid motion locks him up, heaves him skywards, and brings him to the canvas with a Brainbuster that sends a jarring pain throughout Wilson who crumples to the mat. Frenchie having gained a second wind now heads towards the turnbuckle, hopping neatly onto it and letting all know he is just about ready to whip the 450 Splash out of his arsenal of moves however raining down upon his parade is none other than Dead. He emerges to a host of fervent taunts and jeers, yet unperturbed continues towards ring-side. He circles the ring, successfully distracting the referee who blows his top and accusingly commands Dead not to involve himself. Dead doesn't, yet Perfect then sprints out, steel chair in hand. The referee is focused solely on Dead, as is Frenchie, and neither realize the presence of Perfect. He climbs the apron, behind Frenchie who remains on the top turnbuckle. Winding up, Perfect Delivers a stinging chair-shot to the back, sending Frenchie pitching dangerously off of the turnbuckle, hurtling headlong into the mat with a devastating impact. The brutal nature of the chair shot coupled with Frenchie landing squarely on his head is enough to leave the champion incapacitated. Perfect drops to the ring aisle inconspicuously, and once the referee turns from Dead, he sees only Frenchie and Wilson, neither stirring. He begins to count, and finally upon reaching six, we see Wilson begin to show signs of movement and at last regain his composure. To his delight, the Frenchie is sprawled before him, and he has only to latch onto his arm and apply a Fujiwara Armbar. Frenchie is unable to react to anything at this moment, much less such an overwhelming submission hold. When he does not react, the referee has no choice but to award the match to Mitch Wilson by submission. Dead and Perfect enter the ring and quickly help Wilson to the back, leaving a broken Frenchie in the ring and Warfare to come to a close... Result: Mitch Wilson by Submission
