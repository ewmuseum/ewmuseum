Starting things off hot FWO opening theme rolled and then cut to the parking lot area. A black stretch limo pulled up to the arena. The driver of the limo hopped out, running to the back to open the car door. Out stepped ‘Superstar’ Vince Jacobs, no sunglasses, no one thousand dollar custom Armani suit. He was wearing a pair of blue jeans, a “Pro Wrestling’s Phenomenon” t-shirt and a black leather jacket. He had a bandage over his forehead from the cut he received at Cyberslam, a cut that required twelve stitches, something Vince was not happy about. As the watching crowd's jeers echoed through the arena, Jacobs slammed the door behind him and pushed the driver out of his way. The former FWO Heavyweight Champion made his way into the arena through the parking lot double doors. He was definitely on a mission as he passed a few workers in the backstage area. Jacobs saw a few of the newer FWO contingent standing around the back. He turned his nose up at them and kept on walking. Vince made his way down the hall toward the entrance ramp. There was no time to cue up his music but Vince didn’t care as he walked up the steps and headed to the stage. He walked from behind the curtain and strolled down the ramp. It took about a half-second for the crowd's jeers at the sight of SVJ to turn to raucous, full-bellied, hatred. The malevolent former champion made his way down to the ring for his second ReAction since his return from the three year hiatus. He looked at a few fans in the front row wearing a Deacon “I Believe” t-shirt and had some choice words for them. Jacobs rolled into the ring and asked for a microphone. He paced around the ring before putting the microphone toward his lips. “Tonight was supposed to be a celebration.” Jacobs paused. “Tonight was supposed to be an inauguration if you will. A time to usher in the rebirth of the FWO in the right way. YOUR GOD should be standing in this ring right now with the FWO World Heavyweight Championship around this gorgeous waist.” Jacobs stopped as he soaked in the jeers from the fans. “Every single one of you peons saw that I had your ‘hero’ about ready to give up. He was on the verge of losing what he holds so dear to him, the World Title. His precious if you will.” Jacobs continued. “Every one of you ingrates didn’t appreciate what I did to Jack over the past month. You sit here and make him out to be some sort of hero for the people. Some sort of Robin Hood, but Jack is a liar and a thief. He has all of you bamboozled and he will show his true colors very soon.” Jacobs took his jacket off as the jeers were starting to get deafening after Vince’s last comment. “What… you people don’t want to hear the truth? Jack affected three people’s lives three years ago with his greed. And I thought it was time to give him back a dose of his own medicine. You peons may not agree or like what I have done but SVJ doesn’t answer to anyone.” Jacobs paced around the ring again. “Now at Cyberslam, the big mute freak was incapacitated which let me take on Jack one on one. Jack now knows he can’t beat me one on one. He knows that it is a matter of time that I will regain the championship that was stolen from me.” Jacobs walked over to the ropes looking at the Fantron. “I got a proposition for you Jack. How about we settle this tonight? I am not in my wrestling gear tonight but I can still beat you. Let’s see if you have the balls to come out here tonight and face me one on one for the FWO World Title.” Jacobs looked on at the stage and waited to hear from the FWO Champion. The fans erupted in anticipation of seeing a Flyer and Jacobs go at it tonight. “You know Jack it doesn’t surprise me that you wouldn’t come out here tonight. I think Mary-Lynn has more balls then you anyway. Matter of fact, send her down instead or maybe that fruitcake Tony Davis, since every one of your friends has a spine and you apparently don’t.” Jacobs said as he continued to try and bait Flyer out to the ring. "Vince Jacobs," the telltale Egyptian accent broke through Jacobs' tirade, garnering an immediate cheer from the crowd. "When Jesus say he who not sinned, cast first stone." The curtain parted again. Several security guys came through first, followed quickly by the 7 foot giant known as the Deacon. The security ordered him to leave. Deacon didn't listen. He's a bear of a man; they do that sometimes. "I t'ink he talking about what you doing." "Oh hell no." Jacobs said. "I don't have time for you tonight." Deacon paced along the top of the ramp way rubbing his neck. "You had time to make world believe I put people on crosses. You had time to put Jack and I at more odds t'an we would been anyway. You had time to take me out of MY Cyberslam Main Event. You had time to try end my career!" Jacobs looked incredulous. "I didn't take you out of any main event. I would've loved to kick you around that ring same as I did to Jack. And I don't know why you're bothering to accuse me of trying to end your career at Cyberslam. I had nothing to do with you faking that injury, but I've told you for over 6 years that I wanted to end your career!" The security guys started pushing at Deacon as he began to power walk to the ring area. "T'en why not try tonight?" The crowd's cheers were deafening. "Works for me," SVJ said, tossing the microphone to the side. "It match t'en." The Deacon shoved passed security. Next stop – Deacon vs. Superstar Vince Jacobs. Yeah, that's what we call a 'hot' opener. Superstar Vince Jacobs vs. The Deacon Deacon staggered toward the ring. Jacobs waved him in, anxious to get his hands on the mute freak. Deac went to roll under the bottom rope. Wrong move! Jacobs pounded his boot on Deacon's face. Or at least where Deacon's face had been. Deacon had faked the roll into the ring. Before Vince could comprehend what'd happened, Deacon grabbed that foot and dragged him under the ropes to the floor. The big man started to pound away on the smaller Jacobs as security watch on, a tall skinny one actually cheering as these two superstars tore into each other. The Deacon grabbed Jacobs by his long flowing locks and rammed him face first into the steel ring post. To say Deac was pissed was an understatement, and everyone knows you really have to do a lot to get Deacon pissed at you. Jacobs had done that, and then some. The seven-foot monster walked around the ring to the timekeeper’s table. With a well-placed glare, he "urged" the timekeeper out of his way and took the vacated chair. After folding the chair, he locked on Vince Jacobs, the so-called "Reason There is a Show" now leaving the show, crawling up the ramp like a teenager in a Friday the 13th movie. Jacobs tried to gather himself, but he was not expecting this. He wanted Flyer and the FWO Heavyweight Title, not some enraged ogre, and one with a steel chair in hand stalking his prey. Deacon threw the chair down on the ramp, the metal on metal clang echoing, and he power-walked to Jacobs, now scampering like a babe. Deac stomped Jacobs in the back of the head. Jacobs stopped crawling. The mute freak grabbed Jacobs and pulled him back toward the ring, tossing Jacobs back in. With a slow turn, Deacon got a huge pop from the crowd as he went back to the ramp, grabbed the steel chair, and discus-tossed the chair into the ring. Jacobs staggered to one knee and fell down again, struggling, pulling up on the ropes to get to a defensive position. Deacon climbed over the top rope, grabbed his chair, and wound up for a home run swing. The crowd was into this match that hadn't even been made an official match yet. This was a fight plain and simple. Nothing more, nothing less. Vince made it to his feet; he turned around and was met with a vicious chair shot. WHAACKK!! That sent the Superstar back to the mat in a heap. Jacobs’ stitches opened, his blood painting the ring. The crowd cheered as Deacon stood over Jacobs with the chair in hand. An FWO referee finally made it into the ring and signaled for the bell to be rung to start the match. Fitting time for the ref to come down after Vince was hit by a steel chair. Deacon dropped the chair and then down for the cover on Vince. ONE… TWO… KICKOUT BY JACOBS!! Jacobs was not done just yet, even though he was bleeding profusely. Deacon rolled off Vince as the Superstar staggered back into the corner. Vince wiped some of the blood from his eyes so he could see. He pulled himself up into the corner as Deacon raced in and nailed Vince with a big clothesline. The crowd's cheers grew, everyone still standing on their feet as this was definitely a great way to start ReAction. Deacon nailed Vince with a big right hand in the corner. Vince tried to cover up. Deacon pounded his chest once and then unleashed a flurry of rights in the corner until Vince fell to the mat once again, stopping as the referee made it to the count of 4. Deacon's adrenaline waning, he rubbed his injured neck and stretched it, grimacing from the pain. He grabbed Jacobs by the hair standing SVJ to his feet. The mute freak whipped Jacobs into the opposite corner. Big D raced in after Jacobs but this time the Superstar was quick enough to move out of the way, Deacon hitting the corner sternum first. SVJ turned Deac around and nailed the seven footer with a big right hand. Jacobs quickly pulled Deacon from the corner and drove him down to the mat with a double underhook DDT. Instead of Vince going for a cover, he mounted Deacon and started drilling the big man with some vicious rights to the face. The referee yelled at Vince to stop but SVJ was like a bull that saw red and Big D was the color red. Well, it could have been all the blood that Vince was spilling that he saw instead. But nevertheless, Vince was continuing his barrage on Big D, the referee warning SVJ. If the ref was going to waste his breath, he might was well waste it on something really illegal. SVJ wrapped his fingers around Deacon's throat and started to squeeze. This, the ref wouldn't allow. He stepped in and physically pulled Jacobs off Big D. The referee checked on Deacon, or at least tried to, Vince was not waiting as he went back into the corner like a raging beast. SVJ pulled Deacon out of the corner and drove him down to the mat with a quick neckbreaker. He knew the neck was injured and he went right after it like a shark that smelled blood. The Ratings Grabber climbed to the top rope. He leapt off the top and landed on Big D nailing him with the Shining Star. The guillotine leg drop from the top found its mark and created more damage for Deacon’s neck. Jacobs picked the big man up with some effort. He pushed Deacon back toward the ropes. Jacobs whipped the seven footer into the ropes but Deac reversed it, sending Vince into the ropes. Jacobs bounced off the ropes as Deacon put up his size infinity big boot. Vince did a baseball slide under Deacon’s foot, quickly getting to his feet and throwing out his Superstar Kick. Deacon caught Jacobs’ foot. Vince jumped in the air and drilled Deacon with an Enziguri. Deacon fell to the mat once more. Jacobs went for a cover. ONE… TWO… THR--- NO!! DEACON KICKED OUT!! Deacon barely got his right shoulder up off the mat. SVJ rolled off Deacon and stood to his feet. Jacobs grabbed Deac and moved him between the top and middle ropes. Vince grabbed Deacon’s head and wrenched backwards putting more pressure on that injured neck. The referee stepped in to admonish Vince again but Jacobs continued to apply more pressure as Deacon screamed out in pain. The ref warned Jacobs to break the hold once again and Jacobs continued with the pressure. The referee started his five count on Jacobs to break the hold. ONE… TWO… THREE… FOUR… Jacobs finally broke the hold before the five count. The fans however were not happy and they let Jacobs know how they felt with a loud chorus of boos. Jacobs looked around the arena through his crimson mask and just soaked the jeers in. Vince went back over to Deacon bending down to pick him up. Deac snatched Vince's throat. Those jeers immediately turned to cheers! Deacon got to his feet, his big hand wrapped around Vince’s neck. With a scream and an unbelievable feat of strength, Deac hoisted SVJ in the air with a gorilla press. Vince hit the mat with a thunderous thud as Jacobs writhed on the mat in pain. Deacon leaned against the ropes to gather himself. He held his neck trying to keep it from tightening up on him, rubbing it and stretching. When he turned, the seven footer grabbed Vince by the head and set him up with Vince’s head between his legs. Big D hoisted SVJ up in the air but Vince started to nail Deacon in the head and the side of the neck with big rights. Deacon held on to Vince, shaking his head and screaming as he finally drove Vince to the mat with a big powerbomb. Added impact on Vince’s back and spine. Both men lay on the mat exhausted. The crowd pounded and screamed as Deacon finally gathered up enough energy to make it over to Vince and drape his arm across Vince’s chest. The referee quickly dropped down for the pinfall attempt. ONE… TWO… THRE--- NOOOO!! Vince Jacobs kicked out once more. The crowd gasped as everyone in the arena thought it was over. These two men were not one hundred percent going into the match and after this match, they would be even worse. Two major players putting it all on the line because of the disdain they both held for each other. The two men slowly made it to their feet. The crowd tried to pump Deacon up. It must've worked, he was to his feet first as Jacobs pulled himself up with the help of the ropes. SVJ was weak. He might've lost several pints of blood throughout all of this. His face and hair were now colored red. However, that did not stop SVJ as he struggled to get to his feet. Deacon threw the first shot, which hit Jacobs flush in the jaw. However, Jacobs was not going to go down as easily as he threw a right hand of his own at Deacon. The two men continued to throw back and forth shots at each other. Deacon got the upper hand of course since he is the bigger of the two men, sending Jacobs back to the ring ropes. Deacon stepped back then lunged forward, taking Vince out of the ring to the floor with a big clothesline. Jacobs landed on the floor, his back hitting the guardrail as the fans at ringside pointed at the fallen superstar. Jacobs struggled to his knees as he started to crawl around the ring. The mute freak gathered himself enough to roll out to the floor looking to finish Jacobs off. Vince went under the ring apron looking for something as Deacon came around the side of the ring stalking Jacobs. Vince quickly stood up and threw white powder in Deacon’s face. The crowd erupted in jeers that drowned out the entire arena. The referee saw what happened and quickly called the match. Deacon was blinded by the powder as Vince walked over to the timekeeper, sitting in a newly confiscated chair. It wasn't his night. SVJ shoved him out of the chair. Jacobs folded the chair up and drilled Deacon with a massive shot to the head. The referee tried to plead with Vince to put the chair down but Vince looked to be a changed man. He had a blank stare as he looked at the chair and then looked at the fallen Deacon. Jacobs surveyed the dent in the steel chair as he threw the chair under the bottom ropes into the ring. The fans ringside gave it to Jacobs as he just turned and smiled before struggling to pick up the three hundred pounds of dead weight on the floor. Vince got Deacon in the ring and shoved him under the bottom ropes. SVJ slowly rolled into the ring as the referee pleaded with Vince to stop. Jacobs grabbed the referee by his shirt and drove him down to the mat with a quick Star Struck. Vince continued to revel in the jeers before his attention was back on Deacon. The look in Jacobs’ eyes knew he had bad intentions in mind. SVJ took the steel chair and placed it around Deacon’s neck. Vince looked out into the crowd and pointed to the fans ringside that held their "Faith is the Evidence" or "I Believe" signs high. Deacon lay motionless on the mat. SVJ mounted the top rope, a bloody smirk and wild look in his eye telling all of his intention. From the top rope and with his arms outstretched, ten security guys slid into the ring to stop what was clearly on his mind. SVJ climbed back down and backed out of the ring area, the security guys making a phalanx line to back him away from the scene. Jacobs lifted his arms again, garnering the loudest jeers of the night. The look in Jacobs’ eyes told the story. The self-proclaimed Pro Wrestling’s Phenomenon had snapped. Result: Deacon via DQ It's been a bad night... The fWo really needed to hire more security. As Jacobs backed down the rampway away from the Deacon, Malik Roland came through the crowd and hopped the barricade (how come fans never do this?). Malik slid into the ring. The jeers for Jacobs slowed, the crowd not certain what was happening and why Malik Roland had entered the ring, now hovering over Deacon, the Mute Freak still lying on the mat with a chair wrapped around his neck. Malik mouthed something to Deacon and then slung himself into the ropes, rebounded, and stomped onto the chair around Deacon's neck. The crowd's confusion turned back to jeers, this time for the Mammoth newcomer to the fWo. Why had he done this? It didn't make sense. (And we all know how important something being logical is to a wrestling fan) Malik rolled back out of the ring, a sinister sneer on his face, and hefted the metal ringsteps, tossing it into the ring with a resounding KLANG. He got it up, and then deadlifted over his head as he walked to where Deacon lay motionless with the chair still trapping his neck... The crowd's cheers came a second too late to warn Roland. He could feel the stairs go backwards. He let them go and spun as first a right hand, and then a boot to his stomach sent Malik jumping through the ropes to the outside. Sean "Triple X" Stevens stood in the ring, protecting the Deacon as a gargoyle to a gothic cathedral, daring the Mammoth to come back in. Malik backed away, mouthing as he made his way up the metal ramp, leaving Trip to clean up his mess that was the Mute Freak, and former world champion, Deacon. He dodged the small band of protestors on his way into Key Arena, pretty skillfully for being distracted by his phone. Too distracted to notice someone lurking in the shadows to his left. The person silently stepped into frame as Flyer finished typing a text message. Before Flyer had the chance to further react, the person wrapped their arms around Flyer's neck from behind. "My iphone! You di-" It took less than a second, but it was a medley of slight panic, calculating and speculating as to what demon lay before him that ran through High Flyer's brain as he struggled against the attacker, dropping his iPhone on the ground in the process. The wooden object EXPLODED upon impact, and fans roared with their first fecal chant of the evening, “HOLY SHIT!” they screamed in excitement as GV and Krow lay prone amongst the ruble which once was a cheaply made wooden table. The impact of over 400 pounds of man sent pieces of lumber into the audience and scattered across the ringside area. GV pushed away some of the table and attempted the cover on Krow. 1…2… NO! Krow narrowly kicked out! GV angrily rose to his feet and rested against the ring apron for a few moments, sending the necessary stomp into Krow to keep him grounded. GV, exhausted from the bout, re-entered the ring and brought in a steel chair with him. A look of vicious intent was written across his face, as Krow struggled to his feet. GV set the chair up in a seated position near the ropes. The 280 pounder, the 6’8” tall behemoth of a man suddenly bounced off the ropes, jumped off the steel chair, off the top rope and… Fans: OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOHHHHHH!!! DOUBLE JUMP ROPE FLIP PLANCHA ONTO KROW!!! The uncharacteristic maneuver sent the entire Key Arena in “HOLY SHIT!” chants across the board. Ring announcers sold the maneuver as one of the most amazing feats they had ever seen. Shadows crashed his entire 280 pound frame into the much smaller Krow, crushing him between himself and the floor below. Shadows teased the pin fall, but decided against, closing in on Krow’s face and whispering devilishly… “Get the fuck up…I’m not done with you yet.” Krow remained motionless, despite GV’s verbal efforts at reinvigoration. Thus, GV pried the lifeless Satanic God off the floor and lobbed him into the ring. There, Krow laid like a rag doll, unmoving and staring blankly before him. GV grabbed Krow by his wrist and pulled him to his feet, only to deliver a SICK Short-Arm Clothesline. Chants of “THIS IS AWESOME!” rang across the Key Arena as both men laid motionless for quite some time. The official rushed to both men to check on them, but was shocked to find their lifeless bodies still responding. The official counted the customary ten count…but at Seven, Krow began to stir. GV quickly grabbed the top rope and prevented the Backdrop Driver. GV furiously elbowed Krow in the back of the head to release the grip. Krow stammered away, GV locked him up and SLAMMED him with a Snap Gargoyle Suplex!!! Krow slammed directly on his head and was left dazed in a seated position. GV got up with a slam of his fists and pulled Krow up to his feet, positioned behind him, locked him in a Dragon Sleeper and… OLD SCHOOL UNSUITABLE FOR CHILDREN!!!!! The crowd MARKED out heavily for the old school version of Shadows’ finisher – a Lifting Reverse DDT! This one as a Spiral Version of the maneuver. Krow slammed violently on the back of his head and GV covered! ONE!!! Krow kicked faintly… TWO!!!! … … KICKOUT!!!!! He pulled Krow’s lifeless body onto it and began to ascend the top rope. The crowd rose to their feet as GV uncharacteristically headed to the top rope…DOVE OFF… CRAAAAAAAAAAAASSSSHHHH Fans: OOOOOOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHH!!!! KROW MOVED!!! His eyes had turned a deep red as he looked through the parts of his long hair across at his foe. Krow’s teeth gritted angrily as he pulled himself to his feet, his eyes never leaving Shadows as he rose to one knee. Krow pulled off his right elbow pad and hurled it outside the ring, SCREAMED into the night, shot off the ropes…. WHACK!!! SLIDING ELBOW TO THE FACE OF SHADOWS!!! Krow wasted no time, a second wind reinvigorated his injured body and he pried Shadows off the ground, and LIFTED HIM UP IN A DELAYED VERTICAL SUPLEX WHEN… Fans: OOOOOOOOOOHHHH!!! BRAINBUSTAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHH!!!!!!! Krow DRILLED GV to the ground with a Delayed Brainbuster! In a state of shock, GV fired a shoulder up to an excited audience who began chanting, “THIS IS AWESOME!” over and over again with the accompanying claps. Krow sat up in shock, flicking his hair from his face and staring angrily at the officials with three fingers up in defiance.
