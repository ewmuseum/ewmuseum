Introduction Previously... Two nights remain for the fWo in 2004... and historically Countdown has been the most brutal night of the year for the promotion. This year, all eyes are on Cyberslam 2005... but what will it mean from the roster as 2004 comes to a close? The Year Ahead Thomas Murphy looked down at the documents in front of him and blinked. Attendence, up. Promotional sales, up. Advertising revenue, up. Hell, even the action figures were doing better. Sure, it was the holiday season, but the growth was far outpacing what the Incredibles toy line was doing. All in all... the picture could be summed up with three words. Total revenue up. Thomas Murphy smiled, and the expression felt strange on his face. Good lord, he was going to be promoted. For that matter, this boon to his media arm of the company could only mean that he would be next in line to succeed Eisner. All from a fluke... a little promotion he never wanted but appeared, however strangely, to have captured the hearts of the American people like nothing those other idiots in R&D and box office productions could do. He supposed a lot of the credit had to go to Dudley. After all, things had grown more sharply since he was appointed. Still... best not to let the fame get to Dudley's head. Also, it would be far more to his advantage if this little victory was held very tightly to his own chest. Politics at Disney were, summed up neatly, a complete bitch. Of course, this changed everything. If there was money to be gained here there was far more to mine. He had been more or less hands off up to this point, and perhaps it had shown in the overall direction of the company. Wrestlers wandering aimless, individuals attempting to gain control of titles and storylines if for no other reason to provide clarity... hell, there was even some little critic out there who decried the whole medium as another form of reality TV. Well, thought Murphy, this form of Reality TV is selling like a mother fucker. Internet critics be dammed. All the same, it was time to take a more active interest. Market some of these men like never before. Make them the Beattles for God's sake... he could see lunchboxes, teen concerts and... good lord, what if some of them could actually sing? There were world tours to consider... hell, China owed him one after he didn't go after stronger terifs with all the piracy they were doing to his media. Perhaps a tour could be arranged and the government could pack the areas full with willing, or perhaps even unwilling, idiot civilans. Murphy burst out laughing in his plush office and lit a cigar.
