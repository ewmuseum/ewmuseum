HTH House Show, 13 August 1999

13 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Cameras opened backstage of the Louisiana Sports Complex. Several fWo officials including the commissioner stood together nervously and looked out into the stormy night. The sound of an approaching vehicle came through the storm and the commissioner looked to his left, where chief of security Mike Willis stood. The commissioner nodded, and Willis held up a pair of handcuffs, which gleamed in the low light. A limousine pulled up to the backstage area, and the fWo officials all tensed, awaiting the occupant with nervous anticipation. In the distance, the thunder ripped through the sky and the storm grew even more intense. The driver exited the limousine, and held in his hands a heavy straightjacket... French Foundation vs. Chameleon & Total Eclipse Fans gave Mitch Wilson and Primetime a poor reception as they walked to the ring, much to the surprise of the French Foundation, who tried to slap hands with the fans and give them balloons. The Chameleon and Total Eclipse got a similar reaction, boos from the crowd as the Chameleon rode out on the Eclipse's back with a dangling hot dog in front of him to urge him on. Inside the ring the Chameleon had a tough time getting around the skilled techniques of Mitch Wilson and the power of Primetime, and tagged in the Eclipse to soften the French team up. The Eclipse attacked with heavy swinging fists, and then a wrench that he was hiding in the folds of his stomach. Primetime managed to grab the wrench from the meaty hand of the Eclipse, and while the referee wasn't looking he tossed it to the outside of the ring so nobody would get hurt with it. Mitch, sensing that someone could trip on the wrench, went to the arena floor to put it under the ring. However as he did so the Eclipse to the opportunity to chokeslam the distracted Primetime, and make the pinfall without Wilson breaking the count. The Chameleon and Eclipse left the ring the victors, bad mouthing the fWo tag team division and pointing to their waists. Inside the ring Primetime and Wilson looked unhappy, but knew that they had wrestled the match fairly. As they got ready to leave the ringside area, Wolf Fang came out of the crowd and asked Primetime what in the hell was wrong with him. Primetime greeted Wolf Fang cheerfully, and Wolf Fang responded that he had better be back to normal come Meltdown... Result: Chameleon & Total Eclipse by Pinfall US Marshal vs. Ice The US Marshal came to the ring to a great cheer from the crowd, who waved replica hats at their favorite fWo lawman. The Mashal geared himself in the ring for Ice,who ran out from the backstage area with malice on his mind. Ice took it to the Marshal at once with great punches in the corner, finally beating the Marshal from the ring and taking a moment to flex inside the ring for the crowd. Outside the Marshal grabbed the legs of Ice and drug him from the ring, taking the advantage on the outside. The Marshal appeared to have the upperhand on the outside, but when the referee warned the Marshal and told him to bring it back in the ring he relucantly complied. This distraction was enough for Ice to turn the tables, and he executed a perfect standing suplex on the Marshal. Ice then readied a chokeslam, but the Marshal powered out and after a blow to the head caught Ice in a piledriver. The Marshal covered him, but could only get the two count. From there Ice went on a maniacal attack, driving the Marshal's head into the exposed steel of a turnbuckle and hammering away with shots to the solarplexus and groin. Ice then executed a chokeslam and went for a cover, but he could only get a two count. The battle left the ring and went to the outside, where both wrestlers were counted out. Ice and the US Marshal didn't hear the decision, but continued to battle up the ramp and into the backstage area. With the double count-out ruling, it was announced that the Internet Title match at Meltdown would be a triple threat match between the champion the Black Panther, Ice and the US Marshal. Result: Double Count Out Wretch (c) vs. Ultra Violet Ultra Violet came to the ring with a bucket of paint, and began to handprint autograph books and t-shirts. One woman offered her chest to UV to handprint, but Violet steered him away from the young girl quickly. The Cruiserweight champion Wretch came to the ring, and held the title up high for the crowd, who had mixed reactions to the tattooed and pierced young man. The match started with Wretch and Ultra Violet locking up in the center of the ring, and both men trying daring high-risk moves right from the start. Ultra Violet went to the top rope and hit a flying legdrop, but Wretch was able to kick out before the three count. At that point in the match, Kid Cool came to ringside with a large sign that read "Mentor me UV" on it. He stood at ringside and shouted encouragement to the purple wrestler, which seemed to confuse the challenger. Ultra Violet pointed to himself with a questioning gaze, and Kid Cool waved excitedly and shook his sign. Wretch came up from behind the distracted wrestler and hit a DDT, knocking him out cold in the center of the ring. As Violet scratched her head at the sign Kid Cool carried, Wretch went to the top rope and hit a 450 splash to finish Ultra Violet off. As Wretch left with the title Kid Cool and Violet went in to support UV, who still seemed confused by the entire situation. Result: Wretch by Pinfall Flying Trucker vs. Mad Frenchie The Mad Frenchie made his way to the ring, sneering at the fans in his large denim shirt and big frame. Climbing over the top rope, the Mad Frenchie flexed his muscles and prepared for a fight, eying the turnbuckle with a mixture of fear and longing. The Flying Trucker walked out and delivered a loud "Le TOOOOOOOT!" to the fans, and seemed for a moment to remember some key bit of information about himself... but then shook his head and slapped the hands of the fans on the way to the ring. Once at ringisde the Flying Trucker grabbed the steel steps and attempted to toss them into the ring, but they caught on the top rope and swung back, smacking him in the head. The Flying Trucker went down, and shook his head. Looking down at himself with Trucker boots on he exclaimed, "Zut! What is this American filth..." before the Mad Frenchie grabbed him by the head and tossed him into the ring. The Flying Trucker attempted to defend himself under the evil assault of the Mad Frenchie, who battered the head and upper body with stiff shots to the head. As this was going on Chris Universal made his way to ringside to scout the actions of his upcoming partner at Meltdown with a concerned look on his face. He urged the Flying Trucker to get up and fight, and more importantly stop screwing around and remember who he was. The Flying Trucker just looked confused, and the Mad Frenchie used the distraction to execute a pumphandle slam to the mat. The Mad Frenchie then started to climb to the top rope, but shook his head and seemed for a moment to be close to remembering something just outside of his grasp. He climbed down from the turnbuckle and waved to the fans, and then covered the Flying Trucker for the pinfall victory. He then rubbed his head in confusion and walked from the ring as Chris Universal grunted in disgust. Result: Mad Frenchie by Pinfall Doomsday vs. Chaos The lights faded to black as Chaos made their way to the ring to take on the fWo Tag Team Champions, Doomsday. War shouted that they could "make it on their own", and Plague looked strangely focused as he mumbled "Stevie... Stevie..." over and over again. Stevie did show then, along with his partner and co-champion VL. The two posed at the top of the entrance ramp, and then shared a deep and lengthy kiss which seemed to include plenty of tongue. VL started off the match against Plague, who paced frantically and motioned for Stevie to get into the ring. Stevie ignored his request, and VL used his distraction to attack from behind with a running dropkick. Plague made it over to tag in War, and War used his power to toss VL up into the air like a rag doll. She tagged in Stevie, who delivered a shot to the groin and then hit a swinging neckbreaker. Plague screamed at War to tag him in, but Stevie kept War in his corner and allowed VL to choke him while he taunted Plague from the ring. VL came in and brutalized War, tagging frequently with Stevie to keep a fresh man in the ring. War began to scream then, and lashed out with an uppercut that caught Stevie in the jaw, knocking out one of his teeth. As Stevie reeled backward War made the tag, and Plague rushed into the ring to attack Stevie. Roberts rolled out of the ring however, and he grabbed his title and VL and then both left the ringside area as Plague screamed at them from the ring. Stevie just laughed as he and VL pointed to their titles and waved at Plague, taunting him as they took their titles and left the arena champions. Result: Chaos by Count Out Mike Bear (c) vs. Rain Deep thunder rolled through the arena and sudden blasts of rain began to pour from the rafters. The lights darkened, and suddenly great bolts of lightning struck the entrance ramp and sprayed sparks over the crowd. Rain stood at the top of the ramp, covered in sparks and looking determined as he strode to the ring. Mike Bear came out on the ramp next, and carried a steel baseball bat with him as he walked. As he neared ringside he was met by a deliveryman, who handed him a quickly wrapped package. Opening it, Bear discovered his Hardcore Title, and held it up for the crowd, who cheered wildly. At that moment Rain dove from the ring over the top rope and struck Mike Bear on the outside, sending him to the mat and into the steel barriers. Rain picked up the title and held it up for the crowd, who responded with another roar of approval. Rain then struck Bear in the head with the title, opening up the face of Bear and causing blood to flow freely from Bear's head. The two brawled around ringside and then back into the ring, both men attacking with every weapon they could find and even ripping apart the ring and using the pieces to batter each other. Rain grabbed a chain from Bear's hands, and wrapped it around his neck to choke out the champion. Bear started to fade, and the crowd roared as Cactus Jack walked to the ring to examine things closer. Rain warned Jack to stay on the outside as the ref checked Bear for consciousness. As Bear's hand was about to fall for the third time, Jack reached into the ring and grabbed the legs of Rain and pulled him from the ring. Rain shoved Jack back into the metal rail as Bear choked inside the ring, attempting to come back to life after the choke hold. Cactus Jack meanwhile attacked the already tired Rain, delivering a DDT and rolling him into the ring where Mike Bear collapsed on top of him. The ref counted to three and then Jack entered the ring and continued to pummel Rain who struggled to get to his feet. Suddenly from out of the crowd came Crucifix, who slid into the ring and went right after Cactus Jack. Bear and Jack rolled from the ring on opposite sides leaving Rain and Crucifix inside the ring. The two enemies stared at each other silently, and left the ring themselves on separate sides. Result: Mike Bear by Pinfall Death & Mystery Partner vs. Darkness & Eron the Relentless Death came to the ring first, amidst dark blue light and sinister music. At his side was Stone, and fans assumed that she would be his mystery partner. They were surprised when Death sent Stone backstage, and remained in the ring by himself as the music of Eron began to play. Eron walked out onto the ramp and started yelling at Death, pointing his finger at him and walking steadily to the ring. Eron slid inside and got in Death's face, but then smiled and stepped back, extending a hand to the leader of the Dark Riders. Laughing manically, Death shook his hand and raised Eron's hand for the crowd. The crowd stood shocked as it became obvious that Eron was Death's mystery partner, and the music of Darkness began to play. Darkness walked out to the top of the entrance ramp, and stood looking grimly down at the ring and his supposed partner, Eron. Darkness frowned, and then raised his hands up into the air, bringing them down quickly and pitching the arena into complete darkness. Suddenly the lights began to quickly flicker and the laugher of Darkness rang out over the crowd. Fireworks exploded at the top of the entrance ramp and O Fortuna blared out over the arena speakers. The crowd erupted as the Boston Strangler walked out onto the entrance ramp dressed in his ring attire and a familiar insane grin. He held a straightjacket in one hand and tossed it into the crowd as he and Darkness both walked to the ring side by side. The Strangler started the match against Eron the Relentless, who was pushed into the ring by Death. Eron turned to shout at Death when the Strangler attacked from behind, delivering a flying drop kick to the back of Eron's head. The Strangler tossed him into Death, and Eron frantically tagged him and dropped from the ring. Death looked uneasy about getting in the ring, but finally entered and raked the eyes of the Strangler. Darkness extended a hand for the tag, and the Stranger tagged him in with a lunatic grin on his face. Darkness and Death grappled, with Darkness putting Death in an armbar and Death grabbing the neck of Darkness and hitting an inverted jawbreaker. Darkness and Death continued to trade punches as Eron entered and attacked Darkness as well as the ref attempted to get him out of the ring. The Strangler stepped into the ring and grabbed Eron from behind, trapping him in the crippler crossface and causing him to tap out as the Strangler picked up his first victory since returning to the fWo. The battle wasn Result: Darkness and the Boston Strangler by Submission Scott Slugger (c) vs. Black Panther The fWo fans braced themselves for the main event as the Black Panther walked to the ring with the fWo Internet title in tow. The Internet title would not be on the line tonight however, as the Black Panther awaited the champion. Scott Slugger came out onto the entrance ramp as was hailed by a line of fireworks as he walked. He held the fWo World Heavyweight Championship title over his head and smiled a cocky smile to the crowd in attendance, holding the title near to a fan and then pulling it back at the last second. Slugger rolled into the ring and laughed at the Panther before going back to posing for the booing crowd. The Panther grabbed him from behind and delivered a quick suplex that sent Slugger spiraling to the ground and then rolling out of the ring. Slugger paced the outside of the ring trying to get his bearings, but the Panther quickly swung to the outside to take the fight to the floor. The Black Panther attacked with hard chops that laced across the chest of the champion, and rolled him back into the ring. The Panther followed close behind, but Slugger had time to get his second wind and beat down on the Panther as he rolled inside. Slugger sent the Panther into the ropes and hit a cross-body splash and rolled him up for a cover. It wasn't enough however, and the Panther kicked out with authority. Both men traded holds and counter holds, with the Panther finally hitting a Fisherman's Suplex that took Slugger off his feet. The Panther waited until he stood, and hit the Pounce on Scott Slugger and went for a cover. The Panther got two before Scott Slugger put a foot on the ropes to break the count. The Panther rolled off, and waited for the Slugger to stand so he count hit the Pounce once more. Scott Slugger managed to duck under and reverse the hold however, quickly kicking the Panther from behind and landing a Swinging Double Arm DDT. Slugger covered the Panther and hooked the leg, and the Panther kicked out one half second too late, allowing Scott Slugger to get the pinfall victory. Slugger rolled out of the ring holding his head, tired and obviously spent from the tough match with the Panther. As he walked up the entrance ramp he failed to notice the trio of mysterious men in shadows watching him with grim expressions on their faces. As the show came to a close Chris Finn reminded the viewers not to miss Meltdown, where one man would win a full "round robin" title shot opportunity... Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall
