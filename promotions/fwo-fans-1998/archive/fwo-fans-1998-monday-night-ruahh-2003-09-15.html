Introduction The show rolls on... Prerequisites. The tidal wave of jeers began right as first glimpes of the WPO was seen behind the curtain and KMFDM's "Juke Joint Jezebel" was heard. Trademark grin in place, The Flying Frenchie marched down the ramp in a joyous mood, his partner Ric Chronos not far behind. When the two reached the ring, they took nearly a half minute to seemingly smile at every individual fan who was sending vehement rancor their way, then the Frenchie began to speak, retrieving a microphone from his back pocket. "I will 'ave to ask you to quiet down tonight, because Ric and I 'ave some very serious business to attend to. It seems that two idiots who do not quite know what they are getting t'emselves into had a few words for us last week." The fans, not quite sure what TFF was alluding to, didn't have much time to think before Ric Chronos took his turn to speak. "You see, Pierre and I designed an ingenious ranking system and employed it in the fWo tag team division so that you, the adoring fans, won't have to watch us take on The Animals for our very distinguished titles. Last Uprising however, we were challenged without precedent by Phoenix- a team comprised of some guy nobody cares about and some masked guy nobody cares about." The crowd gave mild applause at the mention of Phoenix, as Martyr and Rahl had been impressing audiences as of late. "Ric, I just don't understand. It's all based on wins and losses, and simple enough for even a Texan to undestand. I might be getting a bit carried away, but you get the idea. Now the question is as such- what kind of a putain does it take to-" Fans roared as AFI's "Days of the Phoenix" played and Martyr appeared with Rahl at the top of the stage, microphone in hand. Frenchie smirked, staying quiet, and the music died down quickly as Martyr began to speak. "Now, hold on, Pierre. The way I see it, since we were the second-to-last team in the 10-team match, losing only to you, we should be next in line. Seems fair to me. How about you, Rahl?" The masked man nodded slowly in assent, but Chronos only stood, slowly shaking his head. "You don't get it, do you? It's all in the numbers. The way we counted is that you guys need two more victories in a row to be at the top. If you want your title shot so bad, you can go ahead an try. But I don't see what all the work would be for- you'd fail miserably in any case." Martyr cut the booing that followed this statement short with a wave of his hand. "You've got it. Two matches. Starting tonight with the Animals." In the ring, Pierre and Ric began to laugh hysterically. "Oh, mercy," Ric cried. "That'll be one to watch." As AFI's heavy riffs began to play again, Martyr and Rahl went back through the curtains, Chronos and his partner slowly exiting the ring and following a safe distance behind. Driven Towards Destiny It had been a rough journey so far for Kevin Masters, but he had made it. Yet it seemed that every time he took a step into the future, a ghost from the past was there to meet him. Whether it was Jade, Locke, or in this case Jack Lundin, each time Kevin was forced to look into the eyes of someone he thought he had left behind. They all knew how to push his buttons in one way or another, but Lundin... he seemed different. He didn't just want to push Kevin's buttons. He wanted to destroy him. It was this that Kevin was really afraid of. He hadn't minded so much when it was just Remnant. Some masked man who seemed crazed in general. Yet when that mask came off, and he saw those eyes, the haunting eyes of Lundin, the ones that peered straight into his soul... He felt his stomach drop. "Listen man, I thought I could stop him." Kyle Dillon's words shook Kevin out of his daydream. "I told you I could handle it." Kevin said, pushing the images of the eyes from his mind. "But you don't know him like I do. He isn't really after you, it's something else. It used to be me... I thought me coming here would solve all of this. " Dillon honestly cared about his friend, but Masters found no comfort in his words. "Kyle, it obviously didn't work. He wants me. He probably wants my belt." Kevin said, gesturing to the strap on the chair. Kyle quickly rejected his claim, "Shutup, you and I both know it's not a piece of gold he's after." "But if not that, then what? I didn't cross him in REALM. I had enough to worry about with Locke. One enemy was clearly enough for me, so why would he do this? What the FUCK would make him bother with me?" Kevin quickly became driven by frustration. He pushed Dillon backwards, causing Kyle to breath in deep. Kevin stared at his friend, and Kyle just shook his head. "Stop. This is what he wants. He wants you to get worked up. To turn on me." Kyle said, trying to stay calm after being pushed halfway across the room. "Damnit! He'll never get me... But I'll give him something." Kevin said, strapping his second boot and streaking makeup on his face. Kyle watched as Kevin walked towards the door, preparing for his WOTY match. He was full of fury, and falling right into Lundin's trap. Yet all Kyle Dillon could do was shake his head as his friend opened the door, and turned back towards him. "I'll give him THE DYNAMO." Eron tried pulling body up, and just he was standing, Crusader launched himself off the top rope, missile dropkicking Eron back down to the canvas as Crusader rolled back up to his feet, throwing his arms in the air as the crowd cheered loud. As for Crusader, once again, the mysterious Tron text lead to his distraction, and eventual downfall.
