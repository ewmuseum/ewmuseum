Disney's Monday Night Ruahh~!, 10 January 2005

10 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Previously... With Countdown behind them, the fWo marches into the new year and the enevitable road to Cyberslam. In the final shows of the year the Flying Frenchie, with the unwilling assistance of the masked luchador Spyder, bested Eli Flair for the fWo World Heavyweight Championship... but it is a victory that Eli Flair is not planning on taking lightly. But on this first show of the year the eyes of the promotion are on more than the fWo World Championship... for with a new commitment from the Walt Disney Corporation, the fWo now has additional funding beyond anything they had experienced before. What is more, what were professional atheletes are now being viewed as celebreties. For the roster of the fWo, they find that fame and fortune is now right before them... The Start Of It All "I've been going to fWo events since 1999 with my son, and it's sort of... well... you know... it's like a family tradition for the two of us. The wife and daughter get to go to see the Peking Arobats when they come to town, but this is our thing... our guy thing. Favorite moment? Well, I was in the arena when Ultra Violet won the World Heavyweight Championship from Scott Collins... that was when he was the leader of the Dark Riders... and what a moment that was. We've been to every fWo show that has come to town, and it has been a long time, but we are excited to be here tonight. Deacon is the guy I'm hoping to see tonight... if he gets into the ring and wrestles I think my son and I will be able to go home... well, pretty happy. You know? But really it's just being here... seeing the show... up close like this is really is pretty amazing..." THE FWO PRESENTS "We are live outside of the Target Center in downtown Minneapolis, where for the first time in a long time, professional wrestling has come back to Minneapolis. This event, titled Monday Night Roo Hah is a showcase of the Fans Wrestling Organization, a long-running wrestling promotion that is perhaps best known for a series of drug scandals in late 2002, as well as the disgruntled shooting of several employees. But today all of that is behind them, and as you can see, this hometown crowd is excited about their favorite superstars returning to Minneapolis. This crowd doesn't care about the crowd... they are just hoping to get a glimpse of their favorite superstars as they arrive at the arena... and... and yes, here they come!" SEASON EIGHT: 2005 "The Flying Frenchie... I know he isn't, like, the most popular in this country because of the whole Iraq thing and all, but he is just, I'm saying, just the cutest wrestler there is and if I get to see him up close I will just, well, I came here tonight hoping to see the Flying Frenchie, and I'm going to get him to sign... well, anything he wants to... he is just so cute, I mean, he is really just the hottest wrestler there is, hottest guy there is really, and I just can't... this is so exciting... the Flying Frenchie, he is like, oh my god... oh my god... OH MY GOD! It's HIM! IT'S HIM! IT'S HIM!" MONDAY NIGHT RUAHH~! The limousines came through the fog, headlights cutting a swath through the low mist that hung over the concrete. But these lights were nothing compaired to the sunburst of flashbulbs that exploded on either side of the arena entrance driveway. Inside of the limousines heads craned to the windows, mouths open and eyes wide at the rush of the crowd. "We're sold out tonight I hear," said Brand Frontier, his arm in a sling and his head heavily bandaged, "and with this crowd I believe it." Milo Samus nodded, smiling wide. "They've been here for over an hour... over an hour Frontier... and they're here for us." The limousines pulled to a slow stop, and suddenly the noise level rose to a roar that could be heard several miles away. Warm air created puffs of steam from screaming fans, who were held back from the backstage entrance by a shaky chain link fence... and several dozen nervous security guards. The doors opened and the noise seemed to triple... boots stepped out of cars and looked for the first time at what the marketing and promotion of the fWo by the Walt Disney Corporation really meant. They were several deep, shoulder to shoulder, wearing fWo shirts, waving cameras, signs, and screaming. Alex Creed, his head wrapped pointed up to a ledge where several fans had climbed. "Jesus, will you look at that? She's got a wedding dress on..." Kodiak Vic Creed nodded, wincing slightly at the pain in his back and squinted up at the woman in the gown. She was waving a large sign saying 'Marry Me Miles'. Vic Creed shook his head. "Sick. We've got a bunch of sick freaks as fans. This wouldn't have happened when I was champion." They came out of their cars and looked with wonder at the fans that were assembled, shaking the fence and screaming for the wrestlers to come closer. Every second flashbulbs flashed, suddenly heightened as Rat of the Animals climbed up onto the hood of one of the limousines, turned, and dropped his pants. "Ha, ha! Take this fans! Full moon over Minneapolis!" Jon Gianni grabbed him by the ankle and shoved, pushing him off the car. "Get off of there you fucking idiot... you're making us all look like chumps. And don't you even think about it." For Dog, Rat's tag team partner had moved closer, fists raised, but begged off as Gianni gave him a withering glare. Vince Jacobs, bag slung over his shoulder, shot an angry glare over at Milo and shoved his way past several of the security guards to get into the arena, ignoring the fans and Milo, who had run to the fence and began signing autographs for the fans. Beaming he smiled for photos and waved as the crowd screamed in appreciation. Looking out from under his mask, Rana Venenosa watched as Spyder moved to the fence as well. The luchador pulled out a Sharpie and began signing the breasts of several female fans, chuckling to himself as he gave out hotel numbers to several of the more attractive girls. "Si muchacha... and bring your sister as well..." Rana Venenosa shook his head, disgusted, and headed to the arena, the fWo Internet Championship slung over one shoulder. Eli Flair and Ivy walked toward the arena as well, Flair's eyes dark and hiding his true feelings as he looked at the fans assembled to greet them. Ivy's look was incredulous... but quietly pleased. It had been such a long road to this point... it seemed nice to her that finally there was some appreciation and mass appeal after such hard work. Pulling out a digital camera she snapped photos of several of the fans, and waved goodbye before vanishing into the arena. High Flyer exited one of the limousines with XXXStasy, Flyer's expression was one of surprise, his hands trembling slightly as he saw the crowd. He flashed a glance at Rana, who even now was pushing the doors to the arena open, and his jaw tightened. XXXStasy, who had been gone from the fWo for some time, smiled in surprise and waved to the fans, then pulled off his t-shirt and tossed it into the crowd. It was jumped on by anxious fans who shoved one another aside to get at the souvenier. They continued to exit from the limousines, and each had a different reaction to the massive crowd. When Black Quicksilver and Halo saw them, the later blushed a deep red, while Black Quicksilver smiled and nodded to Aimz. "Shame Silver Fox isn't here to see this, huh?" Aimz shrugged, "Who said he isn't? For all we know he's in the arena already." One limousine door opened and for a second the fans were confused... but then they recognized the newly signed Token Weed. He winked at one of the girls in the crowd, then waved and headed for the door. News reporters scribbled furiously. "When did he get signed?" "Late last year... rumor has it some other wrestlers got signed as well." "I've heard about Silver... who else?" "Stewart... but who knows if it's true?" "Zimmerman? Can't be..." Jeremy and Shirley Savage walked toward the ring, the fWo World Tag Team Championships over both of their shoulders. They walked quietly, hopeful that the huge crowd outside was only the start, and that 2005 would bring them an active, strong, competitive tag team roster to face. Sphere and Samhain exited another limousine together, the fWo X-47 Title... the former Hardcore and Cruiserweight belt... draped over Sphere's shoulder. Sphere tossed back his hair and rolled his eyes. "I've seen bigger." Samhain shrugged, and they headed to the door of the arena. John Renshaw exited a limousine waving an American flag, followed by Paladin, Calm and several stagehands. Jon Crisp came, waving to the crowd and pumping his fists into the air. Then the noise grew, and the heads of wrestlers and camera crew alike turned. "Hmph," said Crisp, "always the showoff..." The Flying Frenchie had also climbed on top of one of the limousines, and as if on cue his entrance music was pumped through the outside speakers. He thrust the fWo World Heavyweight Championship over his head, clutching the shining gold belt in both fists and raising his head to the sky. The crowd screamed, some cheers and many boos... but the excitement was in the air. Everyone could hear it, and from the noise the crowd was making several could no doubt here it in nearby towns. The Frenchie beamed, his white teeth gleaming in the bright January sun. "Zis," said the Frenchie, patting the fWo World Heavyweight Championship, "zis is more like it." The head that wears the crown? Not so heavy. Fireworks exploded, cascading blue, white, and red sparks over the entrance ramp, accompanied by the opening sounds of KMFDM. The first RUAHH~! of the new year was on the air, and it was opening with an appearance from the FWO World Heavyweight Champion, The Flying Frenchie, Pierre Delacroix. Initially, the crowd popped, as they were wont to do, seeing the Champion, but their applause turned to derision and boos as Pierre strutted down the ramp and toward the ring. Whereas he would routinely engage the fans with various insults and mockery, tonight, he simply held at his arms and spun around, displaying his title for all the world to see. Slowly, he ascended the ring steps, grabbing the top rope once he got to the apron, and slingshot himself into the ring. The cameras cut to a crowd shot as Frenchie took a microphone from a ringside attendant, and focused on several signs, some that even had correctly spelled words, many of which called for the Original Nobody to reclaim what he had lost at Countdown 2. The cameras came back to the champion, who was beaming. It was kind of his thing. "Everyone, quiet down now. It's time for someone important to speak." About the only ones surprised by the crowd's vehement booing were newborns. "For eleven days, I have held zis title. I took it from Eli Flair. I defended it against Ric Chronos. And now, at ze gentle age of t'irty-two, I am a four time Champion of ze World. Zat being ze case, I have decided zat considering ze recent...let's call it volatility...of ze World Title situation, I am giving myself ze night off." What? The crowd booing when they're told they'll see no World Title match tonight? Shock of all shocks! "Oui, it pains me to not defend zis belt as much as it pains you not to see me defeat any one of your heroes. I do remind you, however, zat ze last four champions had a combined t'ree successful defenses. Still, zat is not ze reason I have come out here tonight. Zere are two men in ze back I want to address, starting wit' ze lesser of ze two. "Spyder, you incompetent, I want you to know zat as much as I did not need your assistance, I wouldn't dare to dismiss how you helped me take zis title. Mon Dieu, can you do not'ing right. What was ze t'ought process? "Me hate Frenchie. He bad. Spyder give him shiny title." Zis is what I get when I'm on your bad side? It makes one wonder what would happen if we were allies, but zat simply could never happen. You, ma soeur, are beneat' me. I t'ought I had dispatched you at Twilight, yet you pop up again, only to be humiliated once more. Let me tell you zis, Spyder. So long as I hold zis belt, I will defend it against any competitor whom I see fit to...and I shall never see you fit to challenge for it. You are contemptible, petit bug man, and zis title shall not be debased by your very proximity." Pierre smiled smugly, and then focused his attention on someone else. "Which brings us to Eli Flair." The crowd erupted at the name of the former champion. "Eli, zis is ze point where I would normally remind you zat I am a villain, a cad, a cur. I have not looked into it, but you more than likely had a return match clause written into ze contract I had to sign to get zat match at Countdown 2. You know as well as I do, however, zat just because you have ze right to a match, doesn't mean you'll get a fair one. I could walk out of ze match, giving you a count out victory, but retaining ze title. I could ask friends to run out to ze ring and earn a disqualification. It is easier to keep ze title, mon ami, zan it is to win it. "But I shall not do any of zese t'ings, Flair. You gave me ze opportunity to win zis belt, not because you had to, not because I'd won zat right, but simply because I asked for it. Zis one time, Eli, I will return ze favor. You name ze time and ze place, and you'll have your match. You had better make ze most of it." With that, Monday Night RUAHH~! went to its first commercial break. The fWo Presents: Countdown One's Muay Thai Street Fight! Once the show comes back live from commercial Chris Finn, much like he did the other night at Countdown 1, stands at ringside from the announcer's position. With a microphone in hand, a light blue spotlight shows down on him from the darkened ceiling, drawing attention as it does. "Ladies and Gentlemen, at Countdown 1, as I'm sure all of you are fully aware of by now, we had some... technical difficulties from an unusual source. High Flyer, for reasons all his own, interrupted the transmission worldwide for the final show in the Countdown series. That, in and of itself, was a problematic event. While the in house audience still witnessed the spectacle the event was meant to be, no one else across the globe enjoyed the events of that night." The heated and jampacked crowd jeers in agreement to Mr. Finn's statement of the obvious. "Another unfortunate side effect of High Flyer's actions was the inability for anyone to witness the conflict between "Cocky" Craig Miles and the mighty hero, Brand Frontier!" Chris' voice was drowned out momentarily as the audience erupted in cheers at the mention of Frontier's name. Holding his hand up to quiet them, he continued, "As I had announced to the fans in attendance that night in Oklahoma City we will see that... fight... in it's entirety... tonight in Minneapolis!!!" The cheap pop worked as the crowd cheered, as much for the chance to see the epic conflict as hear their city's name. "Not only are we seeing it tonight... we're seeing it NOW!" With much energy and exaggeration Chris points dramatically to the FanTron as the screen flickers from the static fWo logo background to... "Cocky" Craig Miles vs. Brand Frontier The final conflicts in life are hoped to be epic struggles in the desire for added meaning to ones existence. Often though we are let down with the actuality of said conflicts, we are left wanting more, desiring that spark, that gold at the end of the rainbow. More often than not though, we go out not with a bang, but with a whimper. Then, in the times of war we hope that we survive, but not with scars that run too deep. We look for the easy way out, the path of least resistance, praying to our gods that we live to see another day. This event straddles the line of both truths. Craig Miles, set about on a crusade he believes heart and soul in, looks at this event as a crucible of sorts. The testing of a hero if you will. Brand Frontier looks to the confrontation as another in this, his never ending battle. This conflict, whether either man believes it or not, will end... now. The scene is a dark one, taking place late on the evening of December 31, 2004... the final night of fWo's epic Countdown series. The scene is cast in black and white, displaying the reluctance the organization has in broadcasting the event at it's basest level. The match, as Frontier recommended, was not to be sanctioned by the governing bodies of the Fan's Wrestling Organization and therefore is being held at the top of the Metro Parking Garage, on Robert S Kerr Avenue number 321 to be exact. The location of this immortal combat was secreted away, only a choice few individuals being privy to the location until moments before the broadcast. Atop the parking garage the camera crew pans around the bright lights of Oklahoma City, peering over the edge of the structure as if giving clue to the impending doom for the two warriors. In the middle of the vacant level, a large barrel is spotlighted, with a large metal bowl sitting atop it. To the side of the barrel is another, smaller container gleaming brightly under the harsh light. A referee, out of uniform, possibly having volunteered for this assignment, walked out from the darkness as the crew came closer to the containers. In the large bowl sat tar, not the type you think of when it's mentioned around the fWo either. No, this was real tar, not the artificial substance currently pumping through Vic Creed's veins. This was ink black, much like the way Craig Miles portrayed outwardly his heart. In the smaller container, catching the light, was broken glass, shards of destruction and pain awaiting use. The Muay Thai Street Fight was ready to begin. From one side of the garage level appeared the General of the Fifth Seal, the Cocky One, The Professional and King of the Blindside Chairshot. The former fWo Internet Champion, Craig Miles was ready. Dressed in a black Vennenosa shirt and heavy jeans Craig sauntered to the center light, the bright ember of his menthol dangling at the tip, ready to drop. From the other side of the rooftop appeared the anointed hero of the fWo, the former United States and World Heavyweight Champion. The 2004 Wrestler of the Year, Brand Frontier, too was dressed for the occasion, his black jeans taped down at the legs with electric tape. From elbow to knuckles, his arms were too, but with white surgical tape instead. The two men stood across from the other, over the tar, seconds away from impending doom. The "official" of the contest instructed both men on the events that would transpire. Firstly, they would each make sure their arms were taped to their satisfaction, both already had. Secondly they would dip and roll their forearms in the tar before finally laying it against the shards of glass. The contents would then be removed from sight and the men would engage in battle, the only way to win would be to not respond in ten seconds after being laid to the ground. Both men breathed heavily, realizing the level they had allowed their war to take. Craig Miles went first, unusually silent overall, having already tossed his Newport to the pavement before mashing it out with his boot. He rolled his arms carefully in the tar, allowing it to enveloped the white tape as it clotted against it. Making sure his hands were not affected, allowing for better grappling, Miles then gingerly laid his arm in the broken glass before turning and placing it against the other side. When he was done Miles' arms, appropriately, looked like weapons of singular destruction. The official looked to Brand Frontier. Frontier watched intently as Miles acquainted himself with his new weapons, moving them around, getting used to the feel, smirking. The referee finally asked for the hero to do the same, anxious in his own right to get this carnage underway. Brand Frontier shook his head no. The camera zoomed in quickly as Miles questioned him aloud, "What the fuck you mean NO... no what? Don't tell me you're chickening out Brandon..." Frontier interrupted quickly, "I'm not chickening out Craig... I'm just not going to stoop to your level. You might be willing to do this, and that's exactly something I wanted to prove... if not to just myself, then to everyone watching." "My level? You're the one that suggested..." Realization set in. "Ah... you think you're proving how much better you are than me because I'm willing to take the risk, I'm willing to do whatever it takes for my dream, for my cause. You're trying to turn that back on me. I never thought you were dumb Frontier, just misguided, but you've certainly proved yourself not... and if this is the fight you want to have then I'm more than willing to make you like this." Miles spat out, shaking his head. "The real thing I'm proving Miles, is that I don't need nothing more than I already am to beat you... so if you're ready to do this," Brand smoothed down the tape on his arms, emphasizing his decision to forego the coating of tar and glass. ", then let's do this..." Eight or nine workers ran in at the referee's reluctant signal, carrying away the tar and glass rapidly, leaving the two face to face. The referee announced the start of the contest as he pulled away, also being enveloped by the blackness of the night. The two men fired away simultaneously at each other, fists connected hard, snapping the heads of the other in the opposite direction. And just like that, it had started... Brand retaliated the fastest, landing another heavy right hand to the side of Miles' face. Another and another connected as the General staggered backwards. A fourth right hand landed Craig flat on his back, against the hard concrete. Frontier, consumed with the moment, swore he could hear the roar of the crowd as he lurched toward his foe. Miles countered with a stiff kick up, connecting with Brand's stomach as he had lunged forward. Craig kicked again and Frontier stepped back, just long enough for the Cocky One to get to his feet and lunge, tackle-like, and forearms first in to the gut of the hero. The shards of glass and sticky tar rubbed against Frontier stomach, gashing it at the same time as sealing it. Shiny liquid could be seen coating the thicker, darker, tar that was now smeared across the hero's stomach. Brand grabbed at it, pulling away a hand also covered in the sticky mix. Before Craig could get another strike in Frontier fired out finally with a left hand that splayed Miles to the pavement once again. Brand followed up quickly this time, not letting the madman have time to react. He lay in heavy boots against Craig's chest, stomping as if Miles were a brush fire and he was the only thing that stood between it and an inferno. In reality, was that not the case? After his anger had had a chance to subside, Frontier reached down to the prone form. Miles had defensively curled into a ball, covering himself, cutting himself at the same time. Brand grabbed a clump of matted hair, and pulled Miles to his feet. Craig lashed out, with a smirk firmly on his face, as he grabbed around the hero in a bear hug, squeezing for everything he was worth. Brand yelled out as much in pain as in anger. Blood trickled down his back. Miles then pulled Frontier up in the air from the bear hug position, slamming him down to the concrete surface behind him in one quick motion. Just like that, and that is the effect of this type of combat, Craig Miles had the upper hand. Seconds in to the contest and both men were covered in tar and blood. Blood spilled from the side of Miles' cheek from when he covered himself from Brand's stomping attack and now Frontier himself bled heavily from three gashes on his side. Brand lay on the ground for a few seconds before finally kipping up into a standing, battle ready stance, still hearing the cheers of the non-existent crowd of fans. Miles lunged forward this time, trying to stay in control, but Brand stepped to the side, hammering against the jaw of the General with a thunderous right hand. Craig nearly crumpled to the ground, having grabbed onto Frontier's waistband being the only thing stopping him. He threw his arm back wildly, striking out with the shards of glass, hoping to make contact in any way. Miles' wish was granted too, as Frontier instinctively caught the flailing appendage. Craig reacted quickly, pulling the arm away, slicing Brand's palm as he did. Frontier pulled back as the hint of pain creeped in, a bit of it splattering against the lens of one of the mobile cameras. With the same hand Frontier fired out, smashing the closed fist against Miles nose, erupting it in a blood bath of it's own. It was possibly, probably, broken. Craig went down to the concrete surface again, three times already. Brand lunged even quicker this time and found himself astraddle his nemesis, pounding away with wild haymaker like swings. In a total of five minutes, neither man was recognizable. Craig Miles face was nothing more than a crimson mask, while Frontier looked like a rabid animal had attacked him in the wilderness. Miles, out of desperation, swung his right arm up and out, slicing it's way across Brand's cheek. This caused the hero to roll from his attack position quickly, to his feet, as he backed away a bit. He tried to get his head about him as the shock of the attack sunk in. That was all the respite Craig needed. He lashed out with jabs and gouges of his own, startling the dazed hero. Miles, executing probably the only real wrestling move of the night reached around Frontier, grabbing his head and bending him back, planted the back of Frontier's skull against the concrete with an inverted DDT. Brand Frontier lay motionless. The official appeared out of the darkness quickly, conducting the count. With each exhale another number was fired out, but when he reached seven Frontier's leg moved and his head shook. In one swift motion the paragon of the fWo was on his feet, standing taller than his six foot seven inch frame gave him credit for. Almost Hulk Hogan-ish, Frontier pointed to Miles, waiving his finger to the negative. The former World Champion was not going down that easily, even though blood continued to pour freely. Brand dove at the startled villain, grabbing him by the throat before lifting him high in the air, smacking Miles against the concrete with a choke slam. He actually bounced, leaving a bloody splatter behind as he rolled to his stomach. Frontier lifted him up again, righteous vengeance powering his every move now. Brand reached down and grabbed his adversary by the crotch and chest before hoisting him high in the air. Looking to the camera Frontier nodded slightly before stepping out from underneath the Cocky One, letting him flatten against the hard surface once more. This time it was Craig Miles that did not move. The Mighty Hero staggered a bit, light-headed from the assault and loss of blood. He stepped backward, reaching for something to stabilize himself. Being as how they had not moved more then five feet from where the carnage started he found nothing to hold on to, so, he fell backwards hard to his backside. The referee had, by this time, came to the count of seven on Craig Miles himself. In a spirited rally of his own, Miles too came to his feet, even though he was more unsteady than Frontier had presented earlier. He stumbled over to Frontier, lifting the hero up with a handful of red hair. Miles brought a thunderous fist and forearm down on Brand's left shoulder, over and over again. Blood spurted from newly open wounds, as Frontier's face twisted in pain. The pain worked the opposite of what Miles had intended though as he looked down in the face of Brand Frontier and saw a creature of rage looking back. Brand fired out with another shocking left hand to the side of Miles face. Craig fell backwards himself as Brand landed on top of him, pinning him to the ground with a forearm across the throat, his knees held Miles tar covered arms to the ground. "Look at what you've become Miles. Look at yourself." Brand pummeled Miles face over and over. "You wanted to make me a righteous hero, one that deserved the cheers he received. You wanted to test me, to mold me. All you've done is realize what the Deaths, and the Winters and the Giannis have already seen. I fight, not because someone tells me to, I fight because I am supposed to. You didn't create me Miles, I have survived what you wanted to do, and have not changed. You. Have. Lost." With each of those last three words Frontier punctuated them with repeated jabs to the forehead. Brand finally rolled from Miles, the massacre having a quick and deadly effect on both men. Craig Miles face had swollen, his right eye already bruised, his lip engorged possibly keeping the Cocky One from responding. Frontier began to stir again, having used almost all of his energy to "teach" Craig Miles that final lesson he finally caught his breath. Everything he looked at though split in two. Miles too began to move, rolling to his stomach before pushing himself up to a kneeling position. He spit to the ground a dark glob, the black and white presentation of the match taking away some of the edge of the gore. He breathed heavily before rising to his feet, swaggering back and forth a bit as he did. Brand too had come to a standing position, both men now mere feet from the other, staring. That's when the cobra struck. The Cocky One launched himself again, possibly with all his will, at Brand Frontier. Arms folding, pushing on with his head, Miles speared Frontier down to the concrete. Craig rolled over him, raking his arms across his chest as he did, multiple scrapes and gashes still oozing Frontier's life fluid. Miles came up again, smirking slightly, no one really knowing what was going on in the head of the still quiet madman. He dove again as Frontier stood, but the hero moved to the side and Craig stumbled forward, expecting to the hit the irresistible force that was the paragon. He caught himself and quickly spun around ready to attack the hero again, this wasn't over. Or was it? Hammer. Of. God. "Cocky" Craig Miles' chest exploded in pain. There was a small crack that Miles heard in his ears as his face contorted in pain and his heart skipped a beat. "Fuck." He exhaled rapidly before dropping to the ground like a two hundred and forty pound bag of rocks. Frontier stumbled forward with the impact himself, falling on top of Miles as he did. The two lay motionless as the referee once again came in to view, a count of two already on his lips. Three. Four. Five. Brand opened his eyes and looked to the stars. Six. Craig didn't move. Seven. Frontier blinked, the stars having disappeared behind heavy lids. Eight. Stand up damn it. Miles moved his arms, feeling finally returning to them. Nine. Frontier rolled to his chest, lifting a knee under him and pushing up with all his might he came to a hands and knees position... Ten. If there had been an audience they would have rained down a chorus of jeers like none heard before. After a brief, monstrous and bloody fifteen minutes neither man responded to the ten count. Sanctioned or not, both had agreed to the stipulations of the contest. Both had failed to rise. Frontier now found himself standing, leaning against the referee and one of the workers for support, he accepted a gray towel and held it against his stomach. It was soaked in seconds. Finally emergency medical technicians came in as the realization washed over Brand Frontier what had happened. After months of build-up, after the hype and the sneak attacks and mind games Frontier had proven that Miles was nothing more than a mad man, someone not to be deified, but pitied for his lost soul. Winner of the contest or not, Brand had proven what he set out to prove all along, thereby winning his own personal battle. Craig Miles however, and in his own mind, had won himself. He had made Frontier fight him, not for family or for a belt, but for the federation itself. He had made Frontier the hero he never professed to be. With those thoughts swirling in his dizzy, oxygen deprived head, Craig Miles never saw it coming. "It" being Kodiak Vic Creed. Brand caught the arrival of the Main Man out from the corner of his eye. He turned as quick as could be expected, readying himself for he arch-nemesis attack, only to have Creed push past him and flatten Miles back to the ground in one swift motion. Vic's circulatory system threatened to explode from the strain it was under. Veins popped out all over, twisting Creed's visage into that of a monster Frontier knew all too well. This time however the Kodiak had a different victim in mind, one that had came after his own family, after Alex. Creed lifted Miles up with one hand, pushing a few of the workers and E.M.T.'s away with a swat. The secret location must not have been so secret after all, all things considered.
