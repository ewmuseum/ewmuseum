Introduction Previously... After the launch of Sunday Slaughter, the fWo finds itself on the road to regaining much of what it once was. But new problems have begun to surface. The fWo finds itself for the first time in years without a focused management; instead the owner choosing to run the promotion by proxy and give more freedom to the workers themselves. While in some cases this is embraced as a new era of professional wrestling, in other corners wrestlers and managers are already plotting on ways to circumvent the power structure for their own, selfish benefit. With the fWo's second Pay Per View Twilight just a few weeks away, how will the talent structure use the event to further their own careers? Most importantly... what groundwork will be laid for the end of the year run of shows Countdown... and who will emerge as the leader of this new generation of wrestlers?
