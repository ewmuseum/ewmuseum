Unleashed, 17 June 2002

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Chris Universal evaluates the bumps and bruises that he didn't have eight days ago. A match with Vic Creed (see Unleashed last Monday) and a couple of "surprise" appearances in matches on the house show circuit will do that to you. Bruises appear on his arms, legs, his back... all over his body. And he can only think to himself that it will be worth it in the end; or so he hopes. Being the owner of the fans Wrestling organization is an unenviable task, but Universal manages to handle it well and has since December of 2000. Universal sighs and looks over the papers on his desk. Before he can even read a paragraph, the door to his office nearly goes flying off its hinges; as if a gust of wind suddenly burst through the Conseco Fieldhouse. Vic Creed. The Main Man. The Alaskan Juggernaut. The longest reigning fWo Internet Champion in history. Universal jumps to his feet, caught offguard by the presence of a man who has ended careers and shattered dreams just for the hell of it. Before Universal can even muster up a word, Creed already has a bunch for him. "I'm not waiting until SummerSlaughter. I want Kelly. Tonight. Tell Ross & Frontier they have the night off, or they can fight each other for all I care. It should be Kelly & I in that cage tonight. You know it, I know it, and most importantly, your paper champion knows it." Universal thinks over the situation for a minute, before chuckling. "You know, Vic... you're right." The audience gasps slightly, and Creed is even shocked by Universal' response. Until... "No, no, you're completely wrong. First of all, you've done NOTHING to earn a match with Kelly tonight. Second of all, Ross is owed a rematch... and tonight, he'll be getting it in that very ring inside a fifteen foot high steel cage and let me go on record saying, that the only way to win is by pinfall or submission! Third of all, if you want to earn a match with Kelly... you're going to have to do it in the ring, not by coming into my office and demanding this, that and the other. That's why tonight it's going to be you and Brand Frontier, in the ring, and the winner will face the fWo World Champion at SummerSlaughter!" Creed' shirtless body begins to turn red with fury and rage, as if several blood vessels are about to burst. All Creed can muster up is a word, no... a sound, that can only be described as... "AUGHARGHHHHH!" Creed walks forward towards Universal and lifts the table in front of him up slightly before turning it over, sending papers, a phone and other items flying off the desk. Universal rightfully begins fearing for his life, and security quickly enters the room. "Get him out of my office. Now." Creed begins mouthing off a flurry of profanities that are censored, while security does their best to escort The Main Man out of the room. "You've got a match with Frontier and I suggest you worry about that, Vic." Universal gives Creed a stern look as Creed continues to yell. Meanwhile, a flustered Universal stands in his makeshift office, and begins cleaning up the mess Creed left behind. A promo for the ages. "Well, you know somethin', I'm just sitting back here, and I'm looking at this sheet, and I realize that I have to fight Eli Flair. Eli Flair! Ha! His name makes me laugh! He will be no match for the unmatched talent and power that only Beef the Slightly Annoyed can POSSIBLY bring to an fWo event! Why..." Beef starts, but can't find the right words to express how much Eli Flair is really going to kill him tonight. "OH MY GOD, I'M GOING TO DIE." Beef finally says, bursting into tears and crying into the shoulder of El Janito. "Why, WHY DID I GET ELI FLAIR?! WHY?!?" Janito pats Beef on the back and tries to reassure him, in the only way that a Hispanic superhero who acts British can possibly reassure anyone. "There there, my `ol chap. It seems to me that this bloody little toerag is just a wanker who relies on hardcore weapons, an intimidating persona, a woman who could beat both of us up at the same time with one hand tied behind her back, and a bloody tendency to attract Greek letters." Janito says. He then pauses before he realizes Beef's position. "Oh my, you really *are* screwed." Beef looks down at Janito. "Oh, *you're* a lot of help." He proceeds to wander away, leaving Janito to blink and respond. "Hey, I will have you know that my advice saved legions of ferrets from being... violated by those bloody rednecks! Yeah!" The Final Frontier At ringside, the standing room only crowd looks to the entrance ramp as Unleashed explodes, live on the air. Fireworks set off from strategically placed locations. The crowd roars, cheering wildly in anticipation of who is going to walk out first. They're about to find out. The crowd is at a fever pitch, when the lights go out suddenly and a bolt of lightning streaks to the ramp-way. Only one makes use of that entrance and once the bolt hits its like the fWo fans are energized by the impact as sparks fly and smoke billows out of the grating. A blue under-light fades in at the entranceway casting a shadow on the massive frame that fills it. "Last Man Standing" by BioHazard hits as Brand Frontier stomps onto the ramp way to the delight of the rabid fans. Microphone in hand he breaks with the norm and keeps his position at the top of the stage. He paces and points into the crowd as if signaling for the faithful to scream even louder. The fWo marketing department has done their job as Brand is as over as can be. With a knowing grin he raises the microphone to speak as the music fades. "All hail..." he's interrupted of course as the crowd once again loudly pops for The Paragon. "You know what tonight is right?" he stops for a second as if expecting the fans to answer. They do in their own way as, like Pavlov's dogs, they scream back in a predetermined response. "Tonight is the night we have all been waiting for. Tonight is the night that I sure as heaven have been waiting for. Two years to be exact." Knowing what he is referring to the majority in attendance voice their approval. They came for this match. They came for this clash of the titans. This wasn't a generational war, or a show of sportsmanship. This wasn't about someone following a script or being a team player. This was life, as real and as raw as it could get. This was real for the man the fans called Brand Frontier. "Boston Strangler, defeated." The fans pop loud for his mention of the former fWo legend. "Sabian Lords, defeated." Less name recognition follows that name but a chorus of jeers ring out nonetheless. "Hunter Blackwell, defeated." More boos but mainly because if Brand Frontier is talking about him he must be a bad guy. "Ric Chronos, defeated." The fans erupt at the mention of another fWo Superstar. They know that Brand and Chronos have been colliding head on for the past month and most were witness to the collision last week on the inaugural Unleashed when Frontier put Chronos away to advance in the Internet Championship Tournament. With that seemingly behind him the fans know he can now set his sights on the one true target. Kodiak Vic Creed. "Now we move to what I came here for. Everyone has seen the video packages and heard the internet rumors now it can be finished. Once and for all. Tonight in that very ring for the first time in two years Brand Frontier and Vic Creed will collide!" Just then the crowd erupts in jeers, signaling to Frontier the atmosphere has changed. Turning suddenly he barely manages to duck a massive clothesline by the monstrous juggernaut. The show is less than ten minutes old and Vic Creed is already making his presence felt. Creed recovers quickly and the two fighters clash on the rampway. Heavy lefts and rights are thrown. No light is visible when these punches connect. This isn't about sports entertainment. For the second time in just under fifteen minutes, security is once again in the position of trying to contain the monster. They prove to be pretty much ineffective as Creed throws Frontier into the metal archway. Frontier grabs his back as a heavy boot lands on Brand's head. The week old tissue above Brands eye reopens and a trickle of blood is seen. Enraged Frontier catapults himself at the Alaskan behemoth and the two tumble halfway down the ramp. Suddenly the fans erupt in cheers again as Monte Burns along with Eli Flair sprint from the backstage area. They struggle with the two entangled, enraged grapplers. Finally Eli manages to leverage Creed away and Burns, along with a few guards backs Frontier down the ramp. With a massive grunt Creed pushes past Eli quickly and surprisingly given Flair's size. Frontier sees the charge and meets him head on. Jon Crisp and a street clothed Lance Knight pop the crowd as they too enter the ruckus. Each man picking a combatant and helping the other two restrain them. Eli and Crisp hold tight to a heavily struggling Vic Creed while Lance and Burns push back Brand Frontier. The two men push against their restrainers as Unleashed breaks unfortunately to commercials. fWo Hardcore Champion Eli Flair vs. Beef "Let the dollies hit the floor... Let the dollies hit the floor... Let the dollies hit the floor... Let the dollies hit the... ... ... FLOOOOOOOOOOOOOOR!" AAAARRRRRRMMMMMMMDDDDDDRRRRRRRAAAAAAAGGGGGGGGG!!!!!! Beef entered the arena with both arms in the air, a look of absolute death on his face. He was flanked by Sir Janito Garza of the Kingdom of Beefville on one side, and Steve the Rambling Communist on the other. Sir Janito gave a hearty British wave to the people, while Steve alternated between threatening the crowd with OddJob the stepladder, and pretending to reach out to smack some hands, pulling away at the last minute. The trio entered the ring and Beef gave off a huge yell, raising his fist in the air and doing his best to scowl at the people. That is... until he heard the first few chords of "Phucking Phreak" by Velvet Acid Christ. As the strobe began and the red spotlight shone onto Eli Flair and Poison Ivy from the entranceway beneath the TravisTron, Beef noticeably took a step behind Sir Janito. The two members of Team Extreme received one of the loudest ovations of the night, taking their deliberate walk toward the ring. Steve the Rambling Communist hopped up onto OddJob and motioned for Eli to slide into the ring and face his man Beef. Immediately, Beef grabbed Steve and pulled him back, as if to ask him what he's trying to do. "You Fucking Freak! Shut your fucking mouth!" said the Brad Pitt sample looping in Eli's entrance theme as he climbed to the top turnbuckle and held his belt up for all to see. Janito and Steve continued to try and mentally build Beef up for the match, and when the lights came back on and Eli hopped down to the mat, he looked the part. "Cause we're all dead... cause you're all dead..." looped the music, as Eli dropped his sunglasses on the mat and looked down into Beef's eyes. His opponent's eyes grew large, but he didn't take a backstep. As the music died down, and Eli backed up to take off his trenchcoat and glasses, and give his belt to the timekeeper, Beef remained frozen in position. The referee rang the bell, and Eli began to circle Beef, but he remained frozen in position. Finally, Eli walked up and stared at him again, to no response. He tapped Beef on the forehead, and the Slightly Annoyed one crumpled into a heap on the canvas. Eli, for his part, looked completely confused. Shrugging his shoulders at Ivy, he picked Beef up and backed him into the corner. As soon as their eyes met, Beef scurried to the top turnbuckle, asking the official to back Flair off and let him get away from the ropes. As Beef returned to the center of the ring, Eli put his hands up, offering him the first shot. Beef looked around, and began to shadow- box with the King of Extreme. As punch after punch came within half an inch of his face, Eli looked to the side, at Poison Ivy. Ivy shrugged. Seems to be a lot of that going on. Eli couldn't take it anymore - he swung with a sharp clothesline. Beef caught sight of it, however, and was able to dodge. "ARMDRAG~!" Beef grabbed Eli's arm and forced him down with the Armdrag of Death. Eli was close enough to the ropes to grab the bottom one, putting a five- count on Beef's wrenching. However, Steve was right there, standing on his tiptoes to knock Eli's hand from the ropes, and Beef dragged him back to the middle of the ring. Ivy saw this, and got right in Steve's face on the outside. Steve shoved her, so Ivy shoved him back, knocking him down from the top step of OddJob. Back inside the ring, Eli was on his feet, and had backed Beef into the corner. A well- placed elbow to the temple broke the armbar, and another sent Beef reeling. Eli hooked him from behind and dropped him with a full nelson slam, and Beef rolled to the outside. Sir Janito met him there and, joining Steve, the three began a strategy session. As the crowd counted along with the official, Beef put up his hands asking for "Just another second." Eli called Poison Ivy over to him at this and these two began talking as well. Mega Job broke, and Beef returned to the ring. Eli locked up with Beef and quickly twisted his arms around his back. Outside the ring, Steve and Sir Janito looked as if they were surrounding Ivy - trying to corner her, as the case may be. She looked toward Steve, toward Janito, and took a step toward Janito. He backed up a step - but it was enough for Steve to grab Odd Job and slide into the ring. Janito ran at Ivy but she got a hand in his face, and he began to swing wildly as if he could not see her/ reach her. Ivy looked confused. Steve climbed to the top of OddJob and tapped Eli on the back. He turned around to see the midget standing there, yelling at him, one word at a time. Beef took the opportunity to hook Eli from behind, himself - and take him down with a full nelson slam. Momentarily stunned, Eli was unable to dodge Steve the Rambling Communist splashing him from the top of OddJob. Meanwhile, Beef dropped down to one knee, staring intensely into his open- palmed hand. RICE-A-ROONI! Having performed the Most Electrifying Move in Three Counties, with Steve still sitting on Eli Flair's chest, Beef backed up, tore off his kneepad and threw it into the crowd. Eli shoved Steve off his chest as Beef waved his arms to the crowd and bounced off the ropes, hopping four times afterward, vaulting over Eli a moment before he pulled himself to his feet, staring at Steve. His attention leaves Steve, as he turns his head the other way to see Beef... doing the Macarena?!? The official, meanwhile, looks as if he's given up. His attention is momentarily turned toward Ivy holding Sir Janito back on the outside, and just before Eli took a swing at Beef, Steve the Rambling Communist kicked his knee from behind, getting his attention. Eli turned, just in time to eat a fist to the groin and as he doubled over, OddJob crashed into his head. As Beef finished the Macarena and turned around, holding his leg over his opponent, he seemed momentarily stunned at the fact that his opponent was... DOWN. A look of pure confidence crept over his face as he moonwalked backwards, soaked in the chorus of boos at his stalling, and took three fateful steps forward, seemingly surprised and dismayed with each one that Eli is still down. EPIC BEEF DROP! The cover, ONE... TWO... Kickout by Flair! Poison Ivy couldn't take much more of this. She slid into the ring, kicking Sir Janito in the face as he tried to stop her, and she grabbed Steve by the hair and tossed him to the outside, garnering a massive pop. Beef, for his part, seemed lost at the failure of the Epic Beef Drop. He grabbed Eli by the hair and attempted to pull him up, but one of the King of Extreme's massive hands wrapped around his throat. He didn't send him into the corner, he didn't chokeslam him. Eli simply VAULTED his young opponent over the top rope to the floor, and quickly followed, sending Beef back into the ring. The look on his face seemed to speak of a man who was not going to play any more games. Chokeslam. Fallen One. Three- Count. Eli grabbed his belt and left the ring, exasperated with the entire situation. El Janito slid in, helping Beef to his feet. Beef kind of just staggered around before he looked at Janito. "Man, what the hell was the license plate number of the eighteen wheeler that just hit me?" "TBE031." Janito responded. "Oh. Right." Beef said. He took a step forward. Then he collapsed. The Flying Frenchie vs. 'The Golden Child' Monte Burns All the Hoosiers in attendance immediately got to their feet in anticipation as Matt Panzer announced that the next match would be another match in the first round of the Internet Title Tournament. Boos rang out in full force as "Juke Joint Jezebelle" by KMFDM played throughout the arena, and the Flying Frenchie walked out from the back. The Frenchie slowly made his way to the ring, looking completely disgusted by the fans in attendance. As he entered the ring, Frenchie stood on the apron and beamed to the crowd...who responded with middle fingers and other assorted inappropriate gestures. Frenchie's music ended and was replaced by Powerman 5000's "Nobody's Real," triggering a huge cheer from the crowd. Making his way out from the back was "The Golden Child" Monte Burns, who seemed ready to take on the challenge of facing one of the fWo's legends. Obviously still upset about the beating from Frenchie last week on Unleashed, Burns sprinted to the ring and slid inside, as the official called for the bell. Burns immediately jumped to his feet and dodged a clothesline attempt from Frenchie then followed up with a flying forearm off the ropes to knock Frenchie to the mat. The crowd was going crazy, as Burns rained down rights and lefts on Frenchie, who appeared shocked by Monte's opening flurry. Burns finally pulled Frenchie up to his feet and whipped him into the ropes. Frenchie ducked a clothesline attempt by Burns, but "The Golden Child" was ready for Frenchie to bounce back off the ropes and caught him with a spinning heel kick to the jaw. Burns made the early cover, but Frenchie was up before the official could even reach two. Both men got to their feet, and Frenchie tried to catch Burns off guard with a superkick. But Burns was ready for the move and caught Frenchie's foot and shoving him in the chest to knock him back down. Burns immediately went to work, dropping an elbow down across Frenchie's knee. Burns repeated the move two more times, each time causing Frenchie to reach for his knee in pain. Still holding Frenchie's leg, Burns started to go for a figure four, but Frenchie stunned him with a thumb to the eye and followed it up with a DDT to take the advantage. After shaking off the pain in his knee, Frenchie looked down at Burns with pure distain and kicked him repeatedly in the head. Then, knowing where Burns was really hurting, Frenchie dropped a series of four elbows across the middle of Burns' back. As "The Golden Child" clutched his back in pain, Frenchie continued the assault by dropping his right knee into Monte's lower back three times, each time sending more and more pain up and down Monte's back. Frenchie then grabbed Burns by the wrist and pulled him closer to the middle of the ring. After landing a couple more kicks to the back, Frenchie walked over to the far corner and climbed to the top turnbuckle. A moment later, he leaped off from the top and landed an elbow once again across the back of Burns. Frenchie turned over and made the cover, but Burns found enough strength to kick out at two. After uttering something in French, the Frenchie picked Burns up by the hair and whipped him hard into the ropes. Frenchie caught Burns with a kick to the midsection and started to lift him up for a suplex, but Burns managed to block the move. Burns took the opportunity then and started for a suplex of his own, but he the pain in his back was too much, and he had to drop Frenchie back down. That was the opening Frenchie needed, as the fWo legend reached down and grabbed Monte's leg and executed a perfect fisherman's suplex with a bridge for the pin attempt... One... Two... And Burns just managed to escape before the official could reach three. Frenchie started to argue that it was a three count, but even he realized that he needed to keep his focus in order to advance to the next round. Once again, Frenchie pulled Burns up by the hair and talked trash to the youngster, backing him into the corner with hard knife edge chops. Frenchie grabbed Burns by the wrist and whipped him across to the other corner, following close behind for the attack. However, Burns realized what was coming and used the ropes in the corner to leap into the air and land behind Frenchie. Just as Frenchie turned around, Burns landed a boot to the gut and dropped Frenchie with a double underhook backbreaker. The crowd roared with approval as Burns ran forward to the near side of the ring and nailed a springboard asai moonsault off the ropes. Burns reached down and hooked the leg as the referee dropped to count... One... Two... The crowd sighed in frustration as Frenchie kicked out right before the official's hand was to strike the mat for the third time. Now Burns was the one who couldn't believe the result, asking the official about the count to no avail. As both men got to their feet, Burns stayed on the attack with a couple of kicks to Frenchie's left knee, hoping to injure the knee further after working on it earlier. The crowd called for him to finish off the match, making it obvious who their choice was in the match. Frenchie had other plans, though, as he elbowed out of a side russian legsweep attempt by Burns. Burns tried to regain the advantage with a clothesline, but Frenchie ducked that and blasted Burns in the jaw with a vicious superkick that sent the crowd into a loud chorus of boos. Frenchie ignored the crowd and looked for victory, hurrying over and climbing to the top turnbuckle. After a glare to the crowd, Frenchie leaped off with a beautiful 450 splash that connected squarely with the fallen Burns. Frenchie reached down and hooked both legs this time as the official counted... One... Two... The referee was only a couple of inches away from reaching three when Burns used his remaining strength to shoot his right shoulder up from the mat. Frenchie was outraged, vehemently portesting that it had been a three count. As Frenchie continued his argument with the referee, Burns made it back to his feet and snuck up behind Frenchie and rolled him up for a pinfall attempt... One... Two... The crowd once again cursed as Frenchie kicked out before the referee could reach a three count. Burns looked shocked as well, but both men quickly got to their feet, realizing that either man could take control and move on to the second round. Frenchie attacked first, attempting a back heel kick. Burns ducked the move and went behind Frenchie to set up for a german suplex. Now it was Frenchie who countered, slamming an elbow into the side of Monte's head to get out of the hold. Frenchie grabbed Burns in a side headlock and ran forward looking for a bulldog. Unfortunately for Frenchie, Burns was able to get his head together and shove Frenchie forward into the corner. As Frenchie staggered backward, Burns grabbed him by the shoulder and spun him around. A boot to the midsection was followed by a double-arm DDT, and Burns was ready to put the match away. With Frenchie laid out in the center of the ring, Burns headed to the far corner and started to climb to the top. However, Monte's back was acting up again, and his climb to the top took way too long, allowing Frenchie the opportunity to bounce into the ropes and crotch Burns on the top turnbuckle. As the fans showed their disapproval with boos and obscenities, Frenchie hovered near the ropes for a moment to catch his breath. He slowly walked over to the corner and landed a couple of rights to Monte's head before starting to climb up as well. Frenchie stopped at the middle turnbuckle and smiled devilishly at the crowd, eliciting even more boos. Once on top, Frenchie started to lift Burns up with him for the superplex. Determined to fight back, Burns swung and connected with a right to the gut. Frenchie retaliated with a shot of his own, but Burns made his next two shots count, landing hard shots to Frenchie's left knee. As Frenchie reached down in pain, Burns took his chance and shoved Frenchie to the mat. Before Frenchie could get back up, Burns stood up on the top turnbuckle and leaped off with the Establishment...and connected! He crawled back over to Frenchie and draped an arm across his chest as the official dropped to count... One... Two... Three! With that win, Monte Burns had gained a pinfall victory over the great Flying Frenchie and advanced to the next round of the tournament, inching one step closer to becoming the new fWo Internet Champion. As the referee raised Monte's arm in victory, Burns looked out at the crowd and smiled, glad to have them on his side for a change.
