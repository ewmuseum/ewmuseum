Sunday Slaughter, 1 October 2000

1 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction If tonight's Slaughter could be summed up in one word, it would be irony. Three men. Seven titles. The Flying Frenchie had won the Women's, Cruiserweight, Hardcore, International, Internet & World Titles in his storied near three year career with the fans Wrestling organization. The only championship(s) to elude him were the fWo World Tag Team Titles. It's ironic that tonight he would be teaming with a man he had vehemently feuded with earlier in the year against, and now was a friend of. Mike Bear. It's also ironic that last year when Frenchie had the opportunity to win the Tag Team Titles, his attempts were thwarted because of Mike Bear & Wretch. It's very ironic that Mike Bear & Wretch were The Underground.. and now Frenchie was a member of the same Underground. And it would be remiss if anyone failed to mention that the tag team champions that Frenchie & Bear would be teaming up against tonight were also current members of the Underground. Damnation. Mitch Wilson had won the Women's, Cruiserweight, Hardcore, Tag Team International & Internet Titles in his tenure with the fWo; despite taking about four months off from late last year until earlier this year. Since he returned, he had won the Cruiserweight, International, Internet & Women's. At present time he is the current Women's Champion, although in return for his fWo World Title shot, tonight he would lay down to the goofy acting owner of the fWo, James Armstrong and relinquish the title to him. Ironic, considering that a year ago if Armstrong was the acting owner of the fWo, all Wilson would've had to do was ask for a shot. But he betrayed The Flying Frenchie & Primetime last October. Tonight, he faces the fWo World Champion; whom he had feuded with and had some great matches with since mid-July. Wretch. Primetime on the other hand had won the Women's, Cruiserweight, Tag Team, International, Internet & World Titles during his stay with the fWo. Unlike Wilson and Frenchie who had both taken time off in the past year, Primetime hasn't taken time off since he came to the fWo back in the Summer of 1998, and it didn't look like he would any time in the near future. Tonight, in his bid to become the first Grand Slam winner, he would be taking on the Hardcore Champion. The Abusah. It seemed as if Primetime had the easier task than the other two men (Frenchie, Wilson) going after the elusive Grand Slam honor. The Flying Frenchie & Mike Bear were afterall facing two time fWo World Tag Team Champions, and Wilson was facing the man who just seven days ago defeated Scott Slugger. The irony of it all is that these three men were once friends and once comprised the most successful stable in fWo history. The original "French Foundation". Now the most bitter of enemies, they found themselves fighting on one night for one thing. The Grand Slam. Celebration The first Sunday Slaughter of October 2000 hit the airwaves, and inside the arena the fans came to life as fireworks exploded on the stage. After the celebration of fireworks stopped, confetti & balloons began to fall from the ceiling, and a marching band emerged from the backstage area. They gather around the ring, and continue to play their music of choice, when an eruption goes off, and a huge paper mache 'Wretch-Head' style float comes out onto the stage. The fans cheer wildly, as they notice Wretch standing atop the float in the middle, with his lackey Scot Hartt to the left, and the fWo Hardcore Champion The Abusah to the right. The trio known as 'Team Ratings' wave to the crowd, while Wretch holds his fWo World Title for everyone and their mother to see. The fans continue to cheer the Champion, before the float comes to a stop, and the three men hop into the ring. Wretch lands perfectly on his feet, while his lackey & his lackey's lackey fall to the mat, before getting up nonchalantly, and trying too hard to not look embarassed. Wretch retrieves a microphone from some nobody at ringside, before addressing the crowd. "You know something, dudes... we shocked the world!" The thousands of Wretch-A-Maniacs again cheer, pumping their fists in the air. Before Wretch could continue, however, "Last Cup Of Sorrow" by Faith No More starts up, resulting in the cheers turning into a loud ovation of boos. "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson comes out onto the stage, before walking slowly down the ramp, and looking sarcastically at the float. He side steps it before climbing into the ring, and then looking at Wretch with a smirk. "You know, Wretch... all this pomp and circumstance isn't going to save you from the eventual loss your going to receive tonight. It may delay it, but it will not stop the inevitable. And the inevitable is me pinning your shoulders to the mat for the umpteenth time in the last three months and becoming the fWo World Champion and first Grand Slam winner in the fWo's history." Wretch puts his hand up, stopping the overconfident Wilson from speaking further, and interjects with some valid points of his own. "Mitch... I worked three... long... years to win this title? You hear me?! You think you had it bad as "The Dude"?! I hoped and prayed every night I could of been at the same level as "The Dude". You complain that Primetime screwed you over? The list of people who screwed me over starts from the very first god damn day I was here... and Mitch, if you want to come in and try and take that away from me... you know what?" Wretch turned so his face was hidden from Wilson, before turning back to him with one of those small little noisemakers in his mouth. He blew it; and it made a loud sound and a little thing popped out from it. It barely grazed Wilson' nose, while Wretch then joined Hartt & Abusah for a laugh. Wretch turned back to face Wilson, who didn't look amused. Instead, he charged at Wretch with a clothesline, but Wretch ducked. Wilson turned back around, and found himself on the receiving end of a speer from The Abusah. Planted into the mat, Wilson groggily tried to get up, but Wretch stood over him and put him back in his place. He then looked at the crowd, who knew what was coming. His bandana found itself in the crowd, and his arms waved back and forth. A charge to the ropes here, a leap there, and then Wilson once again met the most ridiculous move in sports entertainment today. The Ratings Saver Legdrop. Place goes nuts. The audience roared with enthusiasm, while Wretch stood over Wilson with an overjoyed smile on his face. Wilson shook it off and rolled to the outside, before Slaughter went to a commercial break... with everyone wondering at the end of the night who the Grand Slam Winner would be. Instead, Wilson blocks the punch, and Venom goes to thank him, before Wilson lays him out with a shot, and then kicking him from the ring. Referees come down to help the lowly Internet Reporter from the ring, while in a display of sheer irony, Armstrong thanks Wilson, and Wilson nods. Wilson then lays down, and the three count is all but academic. James Armstrong is the new fWo Women's Champion, and fortunately, he was rumored to want to retire as champion. Armstrong held his newfound title above the ring, before exiting the ring, showing his title off to the fans while he headed backstage.... meanwhile, WIlson warmed up in the ring, before "Paint It Black" hit... and the fans stood on their feet in preparation for the main event. Result: Armstrong by pinfall Wretch (c) vs. "Mr. Main Event" Mitch Wilson The fWo World Heavyweight Champion Wretch, making the first defense of his title reign walks out onto the stage, the fans roaring with applause. Wretch, looking pumped, quickly steps into the ring and hands his belt to the ref, who holds it high before passing it off to the timekeeper. The ref then signals for the bell, and the match is on. The two long-time enemies circle each other warily, neither man wanting to make a mistake. Finally, they lunge forward and lock up. Wilson gains the advantage with a hammerlock, but Wretch reverses into one of his own. Wilson counters by reaching back and sweeping Wretch's leg before trying an elbowdrop. Wretch rolls away, but Wilson is quickly up. Wretch seizes the opportunity however by leaping high in the air with a dropkick, knocking Wilson down. Wilson gets back up again, and Wretch grabs him and whips him into the ropes, before planting him with an inverted atomic drop. Wretch follows it up with a charging clothesline, before leaping onto the ropes and leaping onto Wilson with an asai moonsault that connects. Wretch hooks a leg, but Wilson kicks out at two. Wretch brings him up again and runs into the ropes, before taking him down with a spinning heel kick. Wretch makes another cover, but Wilson once again kicks out at two. Wretch attempts to bring Wilson up again, but Wilson counters with a hard shot to the gut that may or may not have been low. Wretch is doubled over, and Wilson quickly hooks his arms before spiking him with an inverted double underhook faceslam. Wilson quickly rolls Wretch over and covers, but only gets two. Undaunted, he brings Wretch up and whips him hard into the corner before following him in with a hard lariat to the upper chest and neck. Wilson then violently rips open Wretch's tanktop and begins laying in a series of stinging chops, raising bright red marks across the chest of Wretch and in the process angering him. Wretch grabs Wilson and spins him into the corner, before slapping Wilson across the chest. Wilson fires back with a chop, and Wretch with another open-handed slap. Wilson regains the advantage with a hard knee to the gut, stunning Wretch. Wilson quickly grabs Wretch, hiptosses him over, and drops him with the Mitch-inoku Driver, looking to put him away early. Wilson reaches forward and hooks a leg, but Wretch kicks out at two. Wilson brings Wretch up and wraps him up into a hammerlock, before slamming him down onto the hammerlocked right arm. Wilson brings the arm of the downed Wretch out away from his body, before leaping high into the air and driving a kneecap into the elbow, causing Wretch to howl in pain. Wilson quickly drops down and applies a cross armbreaker, but Wretch locks his arms together, attempting as best he can to prevent Wilson from applying the hold. Wilson attempts to seperate the hands of Wretch, but has no success. Finally, he rolls over, catching Wretch offguard and causing Wretch to seperate his hands, allowing Wilson to maintain a painful submission hold, pulling the arm of Wretch up and attempting to seperate it froms its socket. Wretch struggles to get out of the hold, and finally makes the ropes after being in it for a good 15 seconds. Wilson releases the hold, before bringing Wretch up again. He hooks Wretch's head and legs before taking him over with a devastating Superstar Suplex. Instead of covering, Wilson floats over and locks on a triangle hold, wrapping his legs around the head and right arm of Wretch while pulling up on said arm. Wretch squirms in the hold, looking for a way out, before rolling over onto his stomach and lifting Wilson up into a powerbomb position. Wilson shifts his weight to counter and rolls into a cross armbreaker, before rolling Wretch over and lightning-quick locking on the Luminary Lock, the devastating fujiwara armbar. Wretch is in intense pain, and tries hard for the ropes. He can't pull Mitch over with him, however, and Wilson simply locks the hold in harder. The fans come to life, chanting Wretch's name to implore him to escape the hold. He desperately tries to escape, before his eyes suddenly go wide. He begins slapping the mat, before pushing up off the mat in an incredible display of adrenaline-fueled strength. Wilson is caught off guard and releases the hold, and Wretch suddenly gets to his feet and begins shaking his head and pointing at Wilson. Wilson tries a right hand, but Wretch blocks it. Wretch blocks a left and then another right, before blasting Wilson with three rights to the forehead. Wretch whips Wilson into the ropes, before catching him coming off with a big boot. Wretch then stands over the head of Wilson, and pulls off his bandana before tossing it out to the wildly cheering crowd. He crosses his arms once, twice, then runs into the ropes. He hops over Mitch. Pauses. Lifts his leg. Drops the leg. Wilson rolls away. The crowd is shocked into silence, and Wretch bounces off the mat with the impact, going back to a standing position. Wilson quickly gets up, and Wretch staggers into his arms. Headliner. Cover. One. Two. Kickout. The crowd goes nuts as their hero escapes what they throught was certain tragedy. Wilson is less happy about it, and brings Wretch to his feet. He whips Wretch into the ropes, but makes the critical mistake of putting his head down. Wretch stops short of him, hooks him, lifts him, and goes for the Wretchinoku Driver. Mitch rolls through into a small package. One. Two. Wretch reverses. One. Two. Kickout. Both men are quickly up, and Wretch ducks Wilson's attempt at a lariat, before leaping high and taking him over with a standing hurricanrana. Wilson is down, and Wretch sees his chance. He heads to the top. Measures Wilson. Flies off. Hits the 450 splash. Hooks a leg. One. Two. Wilson kicks out. The fans are disappointed, thinking Wretch had him, but Wretch isn't quite done yet. He brings Wilson up and goes behind him. Reaches forward. Hooks the head. Wretch has Wilson in position for the Depression, and is ready to drop him on his head in a move that would surely dim his lights enough for Wretch to score the victory. All around the arena, flashbulbs pop. Wretch tenses, tightening his hold on Wilson's head. And then it happens. Wilson spins and rolls, freeing his head from the grip of Wretch, and grabs Wretch's arm. He then twists it, sending a spasm of pain through the arm of Wretch and throughout his body, disrupting his concentration enough to enable Wilson to grab him, lift him up, and crotch him on the top rope. Wilson quickly scampers up the ropes and grabs Wretch. Everyone in attendance thinks he's going for the gutwrench superplex, a move that scored him a pin over Wretch in one of their meetings. Instead, he hooks Wretch by the head. Holds him high above his head. Falls back. Super brainbuster. Wretch's head hits the mat at a sick angle, and the crowd falls into a hush. Wilson simply smiles evilly, flops onto Wretch, and hooks his leg. The ref drops down and makes the count. One. Two. Three. Mitch Wilson is the fWo World Heavyweight Champion. Wilson rolls off of Wretch, and grabs the belt from the ref, before holding it high above his head to a shower of boos. He then wraps it around his waist and strikes a pose, drawing more boos from the crowd. The boos suddenly turn to cheers as the Flying Frenchie hits the ring and attacks Wilson, wailing on him. He is quickly joined by Primetime, who begins attacking the fWo World Champion as well. From the back, Ice charges the ring, a chain wrapped around his fist. He quickly hits the ring, spins Primetime around, and blasts him. Frenchie turns and the two begin to brawl. They are quickly joined by Primetime and Wilson. More people decide to join the party, as Frenchie's Underground-mates Mike Bear and Damnation charge the ring to help their ally. Frenchie and Primetime begin to trade shots, and the Underground splits off with Damnation going after Bad Attitude and Bear joining Frenchie in the fight against Primetime. From the back, the California Kings rush the ring to help Primetime, and meanwhile, Wretch has recovered and begins clocking random people for the hell of it. From the back, his lackies Scot Hartt and The Abusah sprint to the ring, with Abusah chanting "PHYTE! PHYTE! PHYTE!" all the way. They hit the ring and join the fray. It's complete carnage, as the Underground, the Future Foundation, Team Rateings, and Bad Attitude go at it like wild dogs as Slaughter goes off the air... Result: Wilson by pinfall to win the fWo World Title and become the firs
