HTH House Show, 6 August 1999

6 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Fans arriving at the arena early were able to meet some of their favorite fWo superstars, who signed autographs and posed for photos outside of the arena. The "Hotter than Hell" road tour kicked off with a bang, with several media outlets from FOX and MTV on hand to film segments with several fWo superstars. Wrestlers like Wretch, Death, Eron, and Mike Bear were interviewed for an upcoming special on MTV to promote the fWo and music, and FOX interviewed Scott Slugger and the Black Panther about the life of an fWo wrestler on the road. In short the fWo fans were treated to a special look at their favorite wrestlers, who showed up to make the event a special one for the lucky participants. Black Quicksilver vs. Stone In the special attraction match of the night, Black Quicksilver took on recently de-throned Women's champion Briana. Briana was accompanied to the ring by War and Plague, and obviously in a foul mood after losing the Women's title, she took no time in using cheap shots on Black Quicksilver. Gouging the eyes and kneeing the groin, Stone quickly got the upper hand on Black Quicksilver, and delivered suplex after suplex to the masked star, demolishing him inside the ring and sending a message to the fWo locker room. Stone then caught sight of Stump, and grabbed it, tossing it up the entrance ramp in her fury. As she turned back to Black Quicksilver, he caught her with an inside cradle and scored a surprise pinfall. Angry, Stone beat down Black Quicksilver inside the ring, getting assistance from War and Plague. The three hammered on Black Quicksilver, with Stone obtaining a chain and using it to whip the masked star brutally until Ground Zero and Briana arrived on the scene to make the save. Black Quicksilver was reunited with Stump as he was helped to the backstage area. Result: Black Quicksilver by Pinfall Nikki the Valley Girl (c) vs. Briana Briana remained in the ring for her next match, and Nikki quickly came to the ring for her first title defense. Obviously inexperianced as a wrestler, Nikki was demolished by Briana, who attacked her with ease, setting her into pinfall attempt after pinfall attempt. Nikki seemed to function best on her back, and was able to get out of every pinfall attempt in the nick of time. Briana caught Nikki with a swinging neckbreaker and took Nikki up for a brutal powerbomb, something fans had not seen Briana do in nearly a year. But as Briana went for a cover, the Speed Demon slid out from under the mat and jerked her from the ring, earning her the DQ victory. Angry at the Speed Demon, Briana threated some aggressive actions, but the Speed Demon told her it was nothing personal, and that he was just trying to keep "his girl" happy. Briana seemed disgusted with this, and walked quickly away as the Speed Demon went into the ring to celebrate, but Nikki was upset with the results of the match, and asked for a rematch from Briana because she wanted to be taken seriously for the title, and not pull a "Sable" as champion. Result: Briana by DQ Death (c) vs. Darkness The arena went dark for Darkness' entrance, but no wrestlers were seen. A commotion backstage prompted cameras to go to see what was the matter, and they found Death and Darkness already locked in battle in the backstage area. Death collided with some metal lockers, demolishing them and spraying their contents all over the floor. Darkness picked up Death and speared him into a dressing room door, destroying it in a million shards of wood and glass. It was the dressing room of Eron, who looked angry at this intrusion, and delivered a superkick to the face of Darkness. Eron continued his attack on Darkness until Death shoved him aside and delivered an uppercut into the face of the dark superstar. The two continuted their war through the backstage area, finally prompting the referee to declare the match a no contest. Result: No Contest Wolf Fang (c) vs. Wretch Wretch came to the ring with the Falcon and Mike Bear, who were seated with the announcers for the next title match. Wolf Fang walked to the ring with the title in hand, and loyal companion Jackhammer by his side. Wolf Fang and Wretch wrestled a close match with neither man getting the upper hand as time went on. Wretch attempted to turn the match into an ariel contest, but Wolf Fang worked to keep things on the mat with quick shots to the legs of the challenger. Wolf Fang then turned it up a notch by delivering a wicked elbow to the back of the knee of Wretch, causing him to scream out in pain and earning a warning from the referee. This put Wretch in an easy position for the Wolf Bite however, and Wolf Fang quickly slapped it on him. As Wretch tried to hold on, two figures came out from the crowd and attacked Jackhammer. They were Chris Universal and Lance Star, and Wolf Fang quickly released the hold on Wretch to go out to save his partner. This left him open for attack, and Wretch took no time in delivering a backward DDT and scoring a surprise pinfall. Wolf Fang joined Jackhammer as the pair battled the Hollywood Blondes on the outside, causing fWo security to run to ringside in an attempt to maintain order. Result: Wretch by Pinfall Crucifix (c) vs. Mike Bear vs. Rain Mike Bear walked from the announcing table to the ring, but before he could climb in Crucifix slid out from under the ring and attacked Bear with a brutal swing from a crowbar. Bear's head opened up from behind, and he fell to the floor dazed and laying in a pool of his own blood. This did little to stop Crucifix, who signalled for the bell to be rung as he grabbed a glass bottle at ringside and shattered it, using the edge to carve into the skull of Bear. Bear then drove his fist up into the bottle Crucifix was weilding, sending shards of glass into the face and skin of the champion. The two worked their way to the ring, when suddenly the lights went dark and thunder was heard overheard. Rain decented from the rafters on a cable, the storm swirling around him, and at once went after Bear. Crucifix and Rain seemed to work together for a moment, both attacking Bear and sending him from the ring. Rain and Crucifix then turned their attention to each other, both men delivering hard chops in the center of the ring. Crucifix's pale face was stained red with blood, and when Rain executed a DDT in the center of the ring he lay still from loss of blood. Rain covered the champion, but Bear emerged from the outside with a piece of barb wire and whipped the back of Rain's head with it. Bear then tied Rain to the ring ropes with the barb wire and lay over Crucifix, getting the pinfall victory. His victory was short lived however, as Cactus Jack ran down from the entrance ramp and slid into the ring, nailing the new champion with a lead pipe. He then walked over to Rain, but the wrestler had freed himself and the two began to brawl outside the ring. Meanwhile Crucifix had stood, and motioning to the rafters a large cross lowerd, and Bear was tied to it and raised high. Crucifix raised his arms and lightning struck from above to hit all four ringposts, before he grabbed the Hardcore title and made his escape with it. Result: Mike Bear by Pinfall Doomsday (c) vs. Hollywood Blondes The Hollywood Blondes walked to the ring look confidant, and motioned to their waists that they would be the next fWo Tag Team champions. Doomsday took the battle right too them, with VL and Stevie running to the ring to attack the Blondes on the outside. After some four way action inside the ring and out the outside, the match settled in with VL and Universal. VL was quick on the attack, taking the steam out of Universal with a flurry of punches to the midsection. No stranger to sneaky attacks, Stevie raked the eyes of VL and tagged in Star for a little double team action. Helpless on the outside, Stevie watched his partner get it from both of the Blondes, until a desperate VL delivered a hard heatbutt into the groin of Star and made the tag to Stevie. Stevie cleaned house inside the ring, and as the Blondes attempted to regroup on the outside Ground Zero arrived on the scene and caught Universal in the giant grasp of Jackhammer. Jackhammer rammed Universal into the ring post, and then tossed him into the ring where he was easily pinned by VL. Roberts then yelled at Ground Zero that he didn't need their help, and the two quickly climbed into the ring to go at it with the champions. Star and Universal joined in as well, and soon a massive brawl had broken out inside the ring. Suddenly, a giant net dropped from the rafters, trapping all three teams inside of it. On the top of the entrance ramp the Chameleon and Total Eclipse stood laughing at this sight, obviously amused by the helpless teams inside the ring. Result: Doomsday by Pinfall Black Panther (c) vs. US Marshal The US Marshal, who had not been seen in some time in the fWo got a rousing ovation from the crowd as he entered, slapping hands with the fans and stopping to sign autographs. The Black Panther entered in much the same fashion, and once inside the ring both wrestlers shook hands and wished each other well in a rare display of sportsmanship. The two locked up, with the veteran Black Panther winning the tie up and taking the big man to the mat. The Marshal overpowered the Panther's attempt at a hold, and delivered a hard lariat to send the smaller man into the mat. The two wrestled this way with neither man gaining the advantage but both getting frequent near-falls. The Panther attempted a tope from the top rope, but was caught by the Marshal who quickly executed a crucifix powerbomb on the trapped wrestler. The Marshal made a cover, but the Panther rolled up the Marshal in a small package, getting the three count and taking advantage of the surprised wrestler. For a moment the Marshal appeared upset, but then the two shook hands, and raised each other's arms to the grand applause of the crowd. Suddenly, Ice appeared on the entrance ramp, telling both wrestlers how cute they looked, and that he was considering coming down to kick both of their asses on principle. The Panther and Marshal invited him down, but officals blocked this and warned Ice to go back to his dressing room. Smirking at the Panther and the Marshal, Ice told them to "watch their backs". Result: Black Panther by Pinfall Cactus Jon (c) vs. Scott Slugger As the music played for the main event, Scott Slugger walked to the ring, ignoring the fans who booed him and climbing the top ring post and surveying the scene around him. Cactus Jon walked through the backstage area on his way to the ring, and as he turned a corner he ran directly into Dina Sol. Jon looked with a mixture of surprise and apology at the woman he had run into, and then continued on his way. From behind him Dina's gaze was far less friendly, and she whispered "It will be Jon, then" before going on her way. Jon walked to the ring to the applause of the fans, finally climbing into the ring and facing down Slugger. Jon looked silently at Slugger, who appeared at first to back down under his gaze... but then slapped Jon across the face. Jon responded by grabbing Slugger by the neck and chokeslamming the challenger. Jon went for the cover, but could only get a one-count. Slugger rolled to the outside to regroup, but Jon was after him, driving his head into the steel steps and opening his forehead up. This seemed to enrage the challenger, who began screaming at Jon on the outside and tossed chairs at the champion. Jon ignored these rants and blocked a punch, and then whipped him into the metal barrier, before climbing back into the ring and then up the turnbuckle to the top rope. Jon prepared to dive to the outside, but Slugger rammed the turnbuckle with his shoulder, causing Jon to topple off. In a sickening drop, Jon twisted as he fell, catching his foot in the ropes and slamming head-first into the edge of the ring. Jon's foot dislodged, and he collapsed to the floor with his temple bleeding and nearly unconscious. fWo medical personnel ran to ringside to look after Jon, who appeared to be badly injured after the drop. Scott Slugger shook his head to clear the cobwebs, and attacked the medical team with a vicious series of punches. Threatening others who had come to aid Jon, Slugger rolled the helpless champion inside the ring and grabbed the referee by the shirt, pulling him down to the mat and yelling at him to count. The referee did, likely against his better judgment but intimidated by Scott Slugger, and awarded the match and the title to Scott Slugger. Slugger grabbed the title away from the referee who presented it to him, and tossed him from the ring. Slugger then shoved the title in Jon's face, screaming at the now obviously unconscious Canadian superstar. fWo officials entered the ring to pull the enraged Slugger off of Jon, but Slugger batted them aside and cleared the ring once more. While Slugger was distracted with this medical teams pulled Jon from the ring and quickly got him to safety. Slugger watched them go, and then looked down at his newly won title with satisfaction. He climbed the top turnbuckle, and held it up to the crowd with a smug, cocky look on his face. Yelling that it was his, and that it would never be anyone else's ever again, the cameras went off the air, ending the first house show in the Hotter Than Hell series with Slugger staring hard into the camera and the title raised high... Result: Scott Slugger by Pinfall
