People Caught In Love Like Whirlwinds

1 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stupid lock. Why did the stupid lock always have to do this? It was a long day, and she was tired. Three classes. MUBA meeting. And on top of it all she wasn't there. He didn't know why he expected her to be, but it was always the hope despite the fact she was in school, that she'd somehow just magically appear and hug him, kiss him, make him feel great, take some of the attention away from her back which was absolutely commiting homicide on her. Three good-sized books, a couple notebooks, more or less 372 binders, to say nothing of the face paint or the System Of A Down CD or the sketchbook or the fan mail that was piling up or the radio interview he had tomorrow or the possibility of the contract extension or the guy from Rolling Stone following him around and as he walked to the bathroom he saw it there in vivid colors brighter than the sun the blue and gold she'd put on, trying desperately to rally the team and rooting alongside the pep club at Maples. They'd lost by ten. Arizona was a damn good team, but they'd let it get away from them like they expected it would all be taken away from them, the secret would be revealed that he was a geeky nobody who taped "Iron Chef" religiously and cried during "Down To You" and he was really just a goofy-looking fucking cruiserweight who was going to be stripped of the title Sunday in lieu of BQ and he was going to have to go back to San Diego and that roach-ridden apartment of his youth back where it all began--he dunked his face in the water and most of the paint came off. She breathed again. If she wasn't off tomorrow, she might've had an aneurysm. She got the spot underneath her nose, and undid her hair, new red streaks dotting the formerly brunette landscape. So tired, she thought, so tired and we're still in the opening month of getting back together. He still didn't tell her about his lost Christmas and especially what happened in Vegas and the guilt was slapping him about the head again. "23 and on top of the world," laughed the Rolling Stone guy; "23 and on top of the world," he laughed back but he was laughing long after Ruben stopped. It was damn near 2 and he had to wonder if he would be on AIM so they could chat. He hadn't sent her an e-mail in a couple of days, and while she was trying to be less possessive and not make him the absolute focus of her life sometimes the littlest shit would happen somewhere and a fire consumed her heart. Her eyes lit up like a Christmas tree and she'd soon be lost in some daydreaming revelry of the time they split a 12-box of Krispy Kremes, a 12-pack of Corona, than sat back and watched "Ferris Bueller's Day Off" in an increasingly semi-diabetic coma, she in his jersey and he in her sweater, falling asleep on each other and he couldn't help but chuckle. He choked back audibly. Couldn't cry. He started crying now and she knew she wouldn't be able to stop. She had to have it in here somewhere, beneath the pile of dirty shirts and panties next to the Palm Pilot? No, next to the Discman maybe the Game Boy Yeah, there it was She looked like an angel. She was curled up in his lap, Sabres jersey on back when she was blonde. Faint makeup on her eyes and the lamp's dim light made her look like an angel, small smile on her face hand draped over her waist, cuddling her close. The other arm, looking broken, hanging limply at his side. His head was tilted back. You could've shoved a volleyball down his throat his mouth was so wide open He killed the lights and put a sleep timer on the T.V. before curling up in his jersey, reaching for the C.D. to put in that he made her the soundtrack to the dreams that kept him going and kept her sane His eyes began to flutter She put the picture on the pillow The drums came in And Black Thought began laying it down Tomorrow was another day to survive. Soon, he thought. Soon. If you were worried 'bout where I been or who I saw or What club I went to with my homies Baby, don't worry, you know that you got me... --The Roots & Erykah Badu, "You Got Me"
