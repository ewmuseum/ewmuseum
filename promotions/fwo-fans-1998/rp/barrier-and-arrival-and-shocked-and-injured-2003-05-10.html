Barrier and Arrival and Shocked and Injured

10 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was quiet. A little too quiet. The lights illuminated the hotel lobby as if it were some sort of rock concert, while a receptionist busily flipped through paper works. Seems like she's come under fire recently with noise complaints due to the events transpiring in her hotel. "Damn wrestlers," She muttered under her breath. Doesn't bold well for Flyer, who was next in line. He walked up to the counter, handed her a small pass and she smiled at him. It wasn't the same sort of smile Kate gave him everytime they locked eyes. This one was empty. "Hi! How may I help you!" She seemed quite enthused for a receiptionist. Must be what they taught her in training school. "Yeah, I'm looking for Samuel Smooth's hotel room." Flyer said, as Kate placed the bag down at her feet. She wrapped her arm around Flyer's waist, and Flyer gave her a quick glance before returning his gaze to the attractive woman before him. She flipped through papers, pulling her glasses further up her nose before shifting her black hair entirely behind her. Now, being able to see, she checked through the hotel registry. "I'm sorry, there's no one listed here by that name." Flyer sighed. He hated when people used code names to keep their identity secret. "Well, is there someone listed, maybe with some sort of animal name as their check in identity?" The receptionist paused for the moment, flickering through the papers. "Well, here's two guys named Rat and Dog." She raised her eyebrows. "Now who would want to be called that?" :"Probably someone who's deeply disturbed." Flyer said, grabbing a pen off the desk and twirling it in his hands as if it were a baton. "What about a fox? Some sort of fox like creature? Maybe Foxie Brown?" "Fly, I doubt there's a Foxie Brown staying at the hotel." Kate played with his bandages, picking at them as if they were a scab. And really, they were. Eventually he would shed them, and he would be completely healed, but until then, you couldn't help but play with the wrapping paper. "Oh! Here it is!" The receptionist said, raising her head from her paper work and giving the couple before her a smile. The same vacant empty smile they had received from before. "Foxie Brown, here he is. I'll give him a call, maybe you can go right up." "Mucho apprecianto."
