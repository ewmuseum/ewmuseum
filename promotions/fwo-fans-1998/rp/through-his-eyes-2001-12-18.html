Through His Eyes

18 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the day at the office completed, Monte Burns stood in the middle of his bedroom. His body still ached a bit from Slaughter two days ago, where he had competed in a tag match and a Hell in a Cell match in the same evening. A lot had been going through Monte's mind lately, ranging from business at Platinum Gold, problems with his family, and the ongoing struggle with Donaven Winters. Everything had been building to a head over the last couple of months, and Burns knew that he could have some trouble handling all of it at once. There was no question he could handle one of those problems, very likely two of them as well. But with all three pulling on him at once, Monte knew that times would be rough in the forseeable future. Just as Burns was taking off his shirt, he heard a knock at the door. "Yes?" he said. "Mr. Burns," began his butler from the other side of the door, "Miss Davidson is waiting for you downstairs." "Tell her I'll be down after I shower," said Burns. "Yes, sir," said the butler. Burns heard the footsteps of his butler walk away and pulled off his shirt. As he walked into the large bathroom, Monte began to think about Monica and Donaven. He knew the two of them had been best friends for a long time before she attacked him last year. From the first time he considered bringing her into this, Monte wondered what had happened between the two of them. Monica never said anything about it, and he wasn't about to try and get that out of Donaven. Even many of the people Monte had used to get information on Winters had no idea. This had troubled Burns a bit at first, but Monica had proven herself to be a valuable asset since then. In many ways, she had helped contribute to the destruction of Donaven Winters, and all of that would culminate at Twilight in the Last Man Standing Match. After removing the rest of his clothing, Burns stepped inside the shower and turned on the water. The warm, soothing water felt great running down his sore body. He noticed a cut on his left arm, something he hadn't noticed before. The injury had probably occurred while fighting near the steel mesh of the Hell in a Cell. As Burns lathered himself up with soap, he began thinking back to what had happened in that match. "I had the match won. I had the Boston Strangler pinned in the middle of the ring," thought Burns. "Then, Winters ruined it all. I could have gotten a shot at Ric Chronos and the fWo World Championship. I stood my ground against the best the fWo had to offer. Creed. Panther. Douglas. Winters. Strangler. Quicksilver...okay, SOME of the best the fWo had to offer. Even 'Black Plague' Ruben Ross found out that Monte Burns was a star." Unfortunately for Burns, the match had ended with him getting pinned by Ross. That, however, was not going to stop Burns. He had went into a match that no one expected him to even compete in. Since he arrived back in April, Monte had been telling everyone how good he was, how he was better than most of what the fWo had to offer. This past Sunday night went a long to proving that he was correct. After finishing his shower, Burns turns off the water and grabs a towel hanging overhead. Monte dries himself off and wraps a second towel around his waist. He takes a quick glance into a large mirror before opening a door and walking into an ajoining bar. Seated at the bar is Monica Davidson. Monte smiles as Monica turns to see him. "Hey, Monica, good to see you," he said cheerfully. "Yeah, good to see you too, Monte," she replied.
