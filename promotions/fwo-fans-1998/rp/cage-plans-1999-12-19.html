Cage Plans

19 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::The scene opens up to Wretch behind the driver's seat of his new black mustang, tapping on the steering wheel. He looks impatiently to the left of him, and as the camera zooms out we notice the car is parked about a half a foot away from a small exit that leads out to a small alley. Wretch looks at the watch, and then back at the door nodding. The door opens, almost on cue, as Geoff Green exits backfirst. We hear him whisper under his breath 'I think I lost him... thank god.' and turn around, falling over the car door, and landing face first into the passenger seat. Wretch laughs, as we see a young man, about 16, come out the door, wearing Wretch's wrestling gear and a blonde wig. Wretch pulls a 20 out of his pocket and gives it to the young man, thanks him, and speeds off with Green still lying facedown in the passenger seat.:: Wretch: C'mon Geoff... get up. ::Green tries to bring himself into the upright position, but finds it nearly impossible at the speed there going.:: Green: I... need... you... to... slow... down.... ::Wretch nods, and slams down on the breaks as Green goes flying forward, landing underneath the glove compartment.:: Wretch: You good? Green: Ughhhhh... ::Wretch pulls Geoff's arm up, positioning him in the seat.:: Wretch: Alright... you're good. ::Wretch looks over at Green, who seems to be covered in the filth that was all over the bottom of the car.:: Wretch: ANYWAYS... I have a cage match. Green: Uh... huh... Wretch: You want to know some 'Wretch Trivia'?! Green: Nuh... uh... Wretch: Tough crap! Did you know, that the only other time I was in a cage match, I defeated five peoples... and got the chance to fight Stevie Roberts?! Green: Nuh... Wretch: See?! It's credientials like those that make me the man! ::Wretch nods to himself, doing an RVD-pose. Green looks over at the steering wheel, and tries to grab for it, but ends up falling face forward onto the wheel. Wretch laughs at him, shoves him back into his seat, and takes the wheel again.:: Wretch: Anyway, the thing is those Canadians are all 'We're bad' and stuff, and they like to hit people with things. Green: Yuh... Wretch: So I devised a plan... if they can hit us with things... how about I hit them with things, you know... like chairs and stuff... Green: Uh... Wretch: But then I remembered... You're going to be in a cage, and cause the ref's a bitch, he's all like 'No weapons in the cage'.... Green: Uh... huh... Wretch: SO, there was only one thing I can do then... Green: Use... the... cage? Wretch: Use the cage, for what? Green: Luh.... Wretch: No, cage is for *climbing*... But I'm betting if I can hide, you know, like a frying pan or something, in my pants... I could really whack the hell out of them! Green: Righ'... Wretch: Dude, are you okay? ::Wretch looks at Green again, who seems to be half-unconcious. Wretch gets a look of worry on his face, and then remembers something.:: Wretch: Hey... you remember that episode of Hey Dude... and Mr. Ernst went all stupid cause they dropped paint cans on his head, so then they hit him with something and he went all un-stupid? ::Green looks over to Wretch, frightened.:: Green: No... please... no. ::Wretch grins, and then slams on the brakes again. Green goes flying back forward, falling to the ground. Wretch nods and laughs.:: Wretch: I better be getting a raise for making you all better again. ::Greens moans, and falls back to the ground as the screen fades to black.::
