The Dungeons of Hyrule: Chapter I

5 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
September 2nd, 2003; Morning The interior of the first floor of the Dungeons of Hyrule was a large, brightly lit room. The first floor actually consisted of a first floor and a first-and-a-half floor, as there was a balcony-like thing going on around the room where more things were stocked. On the first-and-a-half floor was a small staircase that would take a person to the second floor, which was the living area that "the Codemaster" Allen Brown made his real residence. The newer, more current-y stuff was placed on the first floor. That was where all of the Xbox, Gamecube, Playstation 2, Game Boy, and if this were present day, even N-Gage stuff was placed. Sure, nobody loved N-Gage, but business was business. The balcony area was where the older stuff, the NES, the Super NES, the Genesis, the N64, the old PSX stuff... everything was. Also, controllers and zapper guns were placed in plastic wrap in a bin near the counter. The area behind the counter itself was huge, because that's where all of the games were really kept. After all, most used game stores(like Gamestop) only display the boxes and let the consumers bring them to the counter. Also, things like old game consoles were also placed back there, in a seperate storage room whose door happened to be there. The counter was made of mostly glass, like a jeweler store's counter. Under that glass were various accessories that went for various consoles, like the Game Boy link cables or the carrying cases. Stuff like that. The place had just opened, so there was nobody inside except two men. One was a short black man with a doo-rag on his head. He kinda had a passing resemblence to J. August Richards from Angel, except shorter. The other guy was at least six feet tall, but he was rather thin. He was white, with rather short brown hair, and he liked to wear sweater vests. The two men were just hanging about, trying to get the place in order. "You really think he's gonna show up, Stick?" the black man asked of his tall friend, who was nicknamed "Stick" because of his slender build. His real name was Martin, but he was never actually called this. The other reason why he was called Stick was because the black man was named Stone Brown, who was Stick's best friend. Hence, we get a nifty pun as well as an accurate description. "He's your cousin." Stick replied. "Yeah, I know, but Allen's got the whole wrestling gig, y'know?" Stone answered, "He's gotta be busy." "Well, maybe that wrestling 'gig' ain't working out too well. I mean, he's got that Resident Evil zombie for a partner." Stick said, referring, of course, to Ken War. "Okay, first of all, Resident Evil zombies don't respawn like that guy does," Stone said, leaning back against the counter and looking at Stick seriously, "Secondly, that guy doesn't nearly scare me as much as that creepy little worm that comes to the ring with Allen." "You mean Mike Heftel?" Stick asked. "Yeah, that's the guy. God, that guy gives me the willies." Stone replied. The two nodded in agreement. Nobody had yet to come in, but it was early, and someone would come in soon. It was, after all, a rather popular establishment. So, Stone turned away from the entrance and looked at Stick, "I dunno, man. Guy's been acting pretty wonky ever since he moved to the eff Dub. Told me he's got all of these crazy dreams." "No kidding?" Stick asked. "Yeah. Man should really give up goin' out and gettin' drunk." Stone said, "What got him to take leave from fWo was when he apparently dreamed about the Fire Temple." Stick blinked and looked at Stone like HE was very drunk, "The Fire Temple? From Ocarina?" "From Ocarina." Stone repeated, non-chalantly. "That's crazy." Stick said with a shake of his head. "And before that, he dreamed that he was Mega Man, and then he told me that he dreamed he was on a broadway musical with freakin' Eli Flair and Mega Job." Stone said. There was an awkward, terrible pause. "He REALLY needs to get off the booze." Stick said, with a shake of his head. A customer came in, which took Stone's attention. But before Stone went about his business, he took another glance at Stick and finished their conversation from there. "Tell me about it."
