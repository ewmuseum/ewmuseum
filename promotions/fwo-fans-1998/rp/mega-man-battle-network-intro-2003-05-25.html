Mega Man Battle Network: Intro

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The setting. A tool which we writers use to set up the context and enviroment where our stories would be set. The background, if you will, which discusses the time and the place of the story. Without a setting, you're writing in front of a blank empty lifeless room that's trapped in neithertime... like the 70s, handicapped by the lack of an enviroment, only having one's self to interact with. Either that, or you're Aaron Dillon. So, let us set a time and a place, and therefore the setting of our story. The time is that it's some time in the dusk hours, because of the faint amounts of sunlight that could be seen in the room. The place? A delightful, respectful room with multiple bookshelves with books in them, a fireplace, a large painting of a distinguished gentlemen above the fireplace, and a large leather chair. Now, we note this leather chair because of the guy in the purple robes and the Burger King Crown, who was giving the cheesiest smiley this side of Mayor McCheese from Cheeserton, New Cheese. Eddie Scott Poser. "Oh, hello!" Poser said, "Isn't it wonderful to just simply get lost in a story? It sure is. Except for the part where you get lost, especially if you're reading something scary. Then it's all not fun. Like, this one time, I was reading Chicken Soup For The Soul, and I was so scared that I wet myself!" "Eddie, the book is supposed to be an enlightening look on life. It's not scary." Cameraman Mark said, off-screen. "IT'S SCARY!" Poser said, acting so defensively that he actually stood up, hunched over, onto the leather chair and made hissing noises at Cameraman Mark. After five minutes of this, which involved Cameraman Mark offering Poser some cat food, then trying to chase him off the chair with a stick, then trying to tempt Poser with a bottle of milk, and trying to sic a sick chihuahua on him, Poser eventually calmed down after Cameraman Mark threatened to call animal control. "I'm the King of Poland!" Poser protested, "I don't deserve animal control!" "You're not the King of Poland. You just put that crown on your head one day and said to yourself, 'Hey, look, Marky Mark! I'm the King of Poland!', and you made me play along," Cameraman Mark said, "Just read the freaking cue cards already." Poser didn't even look at the camera any more. Instead, he was glancing off toward the side, and his eyes twitched back and forth as if he was reading. "Um... long word, the, long word... HEY~! This stuff makes my brain smoke!" Poser said, as smoke came out from his ears. "Eddie, most of those words have like four letters each." "That's too much!" Cameraman Mark audibly groaned and just simply stood in front of the camera, "Look, the fWo paid us a lot of money to talk about how the Codemaster had a big battle ahead of him in the form of Dr. Wily sending robot masters after him, and you're ruining it by being a moron." "I'm not a moron. I'm an idiot. Eron's a moron, and you shouldn't associate me with him." Poser said with a sad frown. Mark understood the point, "You're right. I'm sorry." Mark sighed and left camerashot, "Let's just start the stupid story already." Fade.
