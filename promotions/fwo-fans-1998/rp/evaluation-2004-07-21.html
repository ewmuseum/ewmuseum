Evaluation

21 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Every time I close my eyes I see what I've lost. I see all their faces. They seem more real to me than the guy sitting across the room from me wrapped in a white doctor's smock. "David, how are you feeling today?" I'm not restrained. No straightjacket or straps for me. The good doctor thinks I'm jacked up on tranquilizers or some other kind of medication. Chemical bindings, designed to keep me docile. He's not afraid to be sitting in the same room as me. He has no idea that if I wanted to be violent, the last thing he'd ever see is my hands on his head, followed by the view of the wall directly behind him. The crack of his neck breaking would be muffled, but I'm sure it'd be all he would hear. It's funny how you can sometimes tell who the straight shooters are. This guy, he's okay. The guy standing outside my room, he's a crook. I can see the fear in his eyes as he stares at me through the observation glass. He's probably the one who's been feeding me placebos and pocketing the actual medication. Nice little racket he's got going on. When the patients start freaking out because they're not medicated, the doctor just ups the dosage on paper. More product for him. More profit. That's one of the reasons I won't play his game. The only one who profits from my pain is me. "David?" He's wondering why I'm not responding. I can see the doctor outside the room watching me. He's waiting for me to lash out. He and I are the only two people who know what he's doing, which is why he's smart enough to be on the outside looking in. At least he thinks he's smart. If I wanted to put my hands on him, a door wouldn't save him. He underestimates me. His mistake. I decide to answer. "Yeah." Just enough tired in my voice to make him think I'm under control. "Let's talk about what happened in the Church." "Okay. I cut myself." Not exactly a shaving nick though. "Why did you cut yourself?" I still wonder that myself. Wait, no I don't. I know why, but what's done is done. I give the abridged version. "I wanted to hurt myself." "Do you still want to hurt yourself?" No. Yes. More like yes, but there's other people I want to hurt more. Answers like that won't get me out of here though. "No." Good thing I'm supposed to be drugged or he might have picked up just how little I care. "I see from your evaluations that you've been behaving." I nod. Yeah, I've been good so far. But that's because he doesn't see what goes on in here when the lights go out. Everyone is locked up in their own private Hell with no way to escape. And sometimes, sometimes the Devil comes to make a special visit. The next time he comes to visit me it'll be the last one he ever makes. I see him write something on his clipboard. "Let's see about reducing your medication. If you continue to behave and keep seeing the other doctors, maybe we can get you out of here." Not if I get myself out first.
