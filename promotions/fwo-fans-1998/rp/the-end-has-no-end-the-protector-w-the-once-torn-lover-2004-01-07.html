The End Has No End (The Protector w/ The Once Torn Lover)

7 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Hi this is Lindsay Brooke reporting live from a murder scene. It appears that just about twenty minutes ago there was a murder-suicide in the downtown area," Lindsay Brooke attempted to move past people who stood around the outside of the police lines. She was a reporter and a good one at that. No matter what she was going to get past these people and hopefully get a good shot of the scene for the news. As she moved closer she flashed a snazzy smile for the camera and continued to speak, "There has been nothing but mayhem in the downtown area lately and it is believed that the man responsible for the murder and suicide is a local politician. But as you can see the area is being carefully guarded and it almost impossible even to breathe. Excuse me!" Lindsay said as she shoved past two men who stood in her way, as she got closer to the police line she attempted to move past an officer. "Ma'am, if you could please just step to the side-" Lindsay cut him off, "I will not, the people need to know what is happening and I am here to bring them their answers," she smiled to herself knowing that the quote she just stated would probably be used in the upcoming year's add for the news. Suddenly a hand came out quickly and smacked the camera out of the camera man's hand and it fell hard to the ground smashing as it hit. Lindsay immediately howled in anger, "How could you!?" She screamed. The man that had hit the camera to the ground looked at her with a hard expression, "Now you listen to me lady, with respect I do not want any of this taped! There is a man in there who is dead, and how dare you try to paste his face on the news. You will listen to what the officer has told you and you will move to the side. I don't give a shit who you are and what you want. You wait just like all the others, plus this crime scene is still very fresh and we are trying to work here. Without people getting on our nerves, so be a princess and get out of the way." Lindsay Brooke had never been talked to by a man like that and the fact that she had gave her a thrill.
