Steer Clear

15 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"No, not in there!" So much for ever bringing kids on the road again. Amy had decided to bring her 'adopted' son to keep her company during the trip to Calgary for Wednesday Warfare...unfortunately, he wasn't as meek and silent as she'd hoped. She was kicking herself for not recalling her childhood and the wanderlust being raised in suburban Nova Scotia, even though any city in the province wasn't exactly any more diverse than her hometown. The winter had struck an untimely boredom into Stephen, though... Four year old boys and boredom mix to equal doom, in case Amy hadn't already figured that one out. She'd taken him to Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers, run through what seemed to be every single mall in Calgary and patted the horses of a dozen mounted police but she still wasn't getting a break, his temporary fixation became knocking on the door of every hotel room he walked past. "Hey.......I've got an idea! It's ten o'clock...let's go to bed!" The redhead tried her best to sound excited, though knowing full-well that her sleeping pattern would be shot way off by the early 'bed time'. Again, she wasn't given a break. "No! I wanna meet wresslas!" Amy sighed and prayed their earlier visit to Eli, Ivy and Sean would have been enough for him. Both of the men seemed to fear the energetic young boy like a plague, but Ivy seemed to have magical properties; She'd not only kept a conversation going with Stephen, but managed to entertain him the entire time. "But you already met Eli Flair, Poison Ivy and Triple X!" Once again, she brightened her tone to feign excitment. "Isn't that enough?" "No! I wanna meet Brand Frontier! He's da MIGHTY HERO!" Of all the people he could seek... "Uhhh...well...I don't think Brand's here right now......and I'm sure he's really busy..." "He's never too busy! He can do anything! He has little boys like me, did you know that?" And the barrage of questions continued..."Do you ever see him? He's da BEST! I bet you really like him too huh mommy?" No matter how much patience Amy could muster, she quickly grew weary of the subject upon the last of the questions her son bombarded her with. "Uhh...yeah, he's really nice....and stuff...." She craned her neck and searched the halls, hoping Ivy or Brandon himself weren't in the vicinity; That could raise a few too many questions. Finally, she'd spotted Stephen's eyes drooping a bit and took the opportunity while she could. "Hey, how about I take you to bed?" "But I don' wa--" "Well if you do go to bed I'll...uhhh....hmm...you can meet all the wrestlers you want tomorrow at the Saddledome!" "REALLY?!" "Really!" She clapped, smiling a tired smile for his sake. "Otay!" Well, at least the first phase of the torture was over for the night...now she'd have to make good on her promise at Warfare. It had already been set for her to face Sean, one of the few people she trusted enough to go 'easy' on her for fear of her son spending the rest of the night with a swollen, bloodied 'mommy'... One good sign at least, but how would she explain why he wasn't allowed to see his hero? On second thought, how could she avoid them bumping into him? Stephen would instantly start up conversation, Amy knew that...but facing Brandon Roche again was an awkward situation she wasn't exactly looking forward to.
