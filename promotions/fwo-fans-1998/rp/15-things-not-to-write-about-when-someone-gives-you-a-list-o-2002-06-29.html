15 things not to write about when someone gives you a list of things to write about

29 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"You know you want some of this." Jon Crisp couldn't believe what he was seeing. Here he was, at Chili's, simply because there was nowhere else to eat in Moores Hill, Indiana. But, at this particular Chili's, things were not at all what they appear to be. For instance, sitting in front of him was a chocolate cake. "Come on, eat me, damn you! Who cares if I'm fattening? I have lava that's made of frickin CHOCOLATE!" Crisp looked around. He wasn't sure what to do. So, he did what he thought was best. "...Excuse me?" "Look, Goldilocks," the chocolate cake decided to pick out Jon's sun-bleached hair, "you know what want me. I know what you want to do to me. I don't even cost that much. And, I'll leave your system early. I've been known to just crap out pretty quick. Well, not me specifically. I have friends who told me about friends they had who crapped out quick. But, really, when you think about it, how would they know? I mean, how do they get in touch with some poop? Because that's what I'll become. You know what?" "Erm...what?" "Don't eat me. I'm not ready to become poop yet." "Fine by me." "I'm sorry sir, what was that?" Crisp looked up, and found the oddest-looking waiter he had ever seen. His curly hair seemed to have no starting nor stopping point. His eyes bugged out to the point that it seemed as though his brain were trying to exit his skull via the eye sockets. "Hi, I'm Kevin, and I'm your waiter. I was on call, and I just got here. Ye-yeah!" "Are...you ok?" "Yes, of course," he said through with an excited, rushed tone. "I just got here. Did you know it's 51 hours to go from the East coast to the West coast?" "Um, yeah, I did...thanks th..." "Yeah, so I was in California, and they called me in. I rushed back here. But they called me in six days ago. Manager said he didn't need me then, but I had a strike on my record. What can I start you off with, maybe an appetizer? How bout the Bloomin Onion?" Crisp just stared at him, unsure of what to do next. The waiter decided to jump ahead in the reaction line. "YOU HEAR ME!," he yelled, his head faced towards the kitchen. "I'M OFFERING HIM A BLOOMIN ONION! WAIT TILL I GET MY PERMIT, SO I CAN BLOOMIN BLOW YOUR HEADS OFF!" Kevin turned back at Crisp and smiled. "So, do you need a few minutes?" "Er...yes...whatever you want me to do..." "Just so you know, sir, if you don't order now, I won't return for 45 minutes." "Ok. How about some chips and salsa then?" "Chips and salsa sound great. I'll be right back with those." "In 45 minutes." "What's that?" "You said," Crisp replied, "in 45 minutes, you'd return." "Ah, yes...well, you ordered something. So I'll return with that, and then won't answer your requests for extra salsa." "Oh." "Be right back, sir." Kevin went screaming towards the back. Meanwhile, Crisp turned back to the cake, who hadn't been cleared off his table yet. The cake was silent. Then, he spoke up again. "Hey, you got a Playstation 2?" "What?" Crisp looked around, not sure what to do. Nobody was looking at him, so he decided to continue the conversation, if you can call it that. "Yeah, I do." "What games you got?" "NCAA Football 2002..." "You got any of the mature games?" "I have a few." "Are they suitable for children, in your opinion? I have a child, a Werther's original...yes, I know we're lucky, he's so sweet and creamy...anyway, he wants this game...Half-Life...and I just don't know if it's suitable for him." "Er...you have a child?" "Do you see a problem with chocolate wanting to procreate?" "Just a small one." "How do you think we got here in the first place?" "A bean?" Crisp wasn't sure how to answer it. This was all very strange to him. "A bean? What the hell do you humans know? This fork knows more than you do!" Then, the fork spoke up. "It's true, I'm quite smart." "See?" Crisp wiped his eyes. Was he tired? "Hey, do you know what electronic Endust is?" Crisp turned around in his seat. A man with what could be a large, puffy afro was speaking to him. Really, it was just a bad haircut. "No...no I don't. Hey, did you hea..." "Neither do I. I'm John Comey." "I'm Jon Gi..." "I know who you are." "Oh, then you watch wrestling." "No. I'm your handler." "My what?" "I'm your handler. I control you." "You're crazy. Nobody controls me." "Oh? Hold on." This fellow took out a pen and paper, and scribbled something down. Then, Crisp's arm went numb, a stick of bubblegum appeared sticking out of his ear, and two bobblehead dolls were making out in his booth. "See?" Comey showed Crisp the piece of paper. On the piece of paper was the recap of things that happened. At this point, Crisp had had enough. He got up, and left. The last thing he heard in the restaurant was Comey, talking the chocolate cake. "Man, Jeremy really gave me a shitty list to work with. How's your life, cake?" "Not so bad. I'm trying to make it with that shake over there." ** Crisp walked back to his hotel, blasting the latest Eminem CD in his discman. Damn, hotels really do suck, he thought to himself as he entered his room. He didn't have many reasons as to why they sucked, per se. He just held the opinion that they did. Crisp got onto his laptop, and logged onto the internet. His AOL Instant Messenger quickly started up, and he came across one of the more tireless workers in the business...the guy known as Tomer. TomerStaysOnTooMuch: Logged on for 11 days, 4 hours, 34 minutes Crisp wrote to him one message. "Damn, son, get off the blasted internet. Go look at some sunlight, or some nudie girls. Do something." TomerStaysOnTooMuch (Auto-Away Message): Making COOKIES and 34 websites. Back in six minutes. Crisp went to another site, where the "smarks" talked up the business. Ewplanet.com was the name of the site. Why the E was there, he didn't know. He didn't know what it meant, nor did he care. At the site, numerous people were talking about their last post, and how much the site sucked. Who the hell are these people? Crisp thought. Do they think their leaving actually means something?
