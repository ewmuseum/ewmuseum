Lost in America

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a long and lonesome walk to the hotel. Davy's scrotum was still in excruciating pain as he peeked into the lobby. Children giggled and pointed at the foreigner, whose hands where massaging his testis. The elevator doors slid open, releasing Davy from inside. The Australian native made his way to room number 513. He took the plastic key card from out of his back pocket, and opened the door to his room. Davy instantly fell onto his bed face first. His body was in complete agony after the long day he had just endured. Dingleberry had experienced something different that day. He experienced life outside Australia, and it wasn't what he expected. He was a lost in America, confused and befuddled. People where uptight, the weather was awkward, and the American accents where just plain stupid. Not to mention his tallywhacker was still throbbing in pain. Davy hated America with a passion. After a long warm shower, Dingleberry's body had been eased, but his mind hadn't. The Crocodile hunter had come to a decision. He was going to return to Australia, where he belonged. After packing his dirtied khaki outfit back into his suitcase, Davy was ready for bed. He slipped open the covers, and rest his head upon the pillow. It was then that he noticed a blinking red light from across the room. It was coming from his telephone. Although unenthusiastic about getting up out of bed, Davy's curiosity had gotten the better of him. He stumbled over to the telephone, and pressed the blinking button. Message one, Friday, 8:47 pm. "Hey Davy, it's Craig. I'm just calling to confirm your acceptance into the Fans Wrestling Organization. Tomorrow morning at 10:00 am, there will be a limo waiting outside the hotel for you. You will be driven to the Sowunmi Travels Airport, where you will be flown to Charlotte, North Carolina for contract negotiations. Congratulations, and welcome to the family." Davy stood still with an uneasy look on his face. It seems he'd gotten himself into quite the pickle of a situation. Well tomorrow he would be flying. The question is to where.
