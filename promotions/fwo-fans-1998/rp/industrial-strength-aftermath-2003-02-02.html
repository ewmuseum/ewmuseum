Industrial Strength Aftermath

2 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Forty - five minutes on stage was all it took for Angel to convince herself that she still had it. Forty - five minutes gave her back all the confidence she thought she had lost over her pregnancy. Decked out in a corset, flowing black dress, and knee- high leather boots... she had her audience wrapped around her little finger. And by the time she left the stage, she'd made the night's headliners, Choke, into believers. Her boys, the muscle behind Valerian's Garden, already believed. As did her guest for the evening, Amy Campbell. And they didn't need forty- five minutes to do so. It's past two now, and the band has chipped in five bucks a head to rent a motel room for the express purpose of taking a SHOWER. The boys are sacked out in the bus already, a combination of being their own roadies and Angel guilt- tripping them into carrying her gear as well (She's a MOM now!) has wiped 'em out. Fortunately, the next three days are there to simply drive back to the States. Amy barely noticed the water turning off in the bathroom, as she was half watching television and half- babysitting/making faces at Mariella. The baby's got quite a smile. "Amy," called Angel from the steamy bathroom, "I'm really not hungry... if you still are, feel free to attack the rest of the noodles." She leaned out, wrapped in a towel. "How's my sweetie doin' there?" "Laughing at me, actually." She chuckled, still watching MJ's toothless grin - and to think they said babies that young rarely smiled. "Congrats...you gave birth to a normal member of society." "Yeah," replied Angel dryly, "This girl's got my genes and Eli Flair's genes. I doubt there'll be anything normal about her." She kissed the baby's cheek, sitting down on the bed opposite Amy, pulling clothes out of an overnight bag. "Have a good time tonight, Amy?" "Surrounded by friends and music, how could I NOT have a good time?" Amy smiled. "Tonight really got at me...old friends, good music...almost felt like I'm back to who I was before I got all creepy and depressed." "I remember depressed... wasn't much fun. It's just... you've gotta just hang in there and look for a way outta it." Angel shimmied her way into an oversized "MINISTRY" T-shirt and a pair of flannel boxers before tossing her towel on the floor. "I don't know if I would've made it without my boys. Music literally saved my life a few times." Amy flashed a crooken grin and nodded almost to herself. "Yeah...music keeps me going. Wrestling's just kinda a fun job, y'know? It's good stress-relief...but nothing for me beats the relief in music, a piercing or new tattoo. And I know that sounds kinda funny, coming from someone phobic of thin needles...but I've got no problem with getting pierced, the adrenaline mixed with pain is something just too good. I wish someone'd tell the kids out there slicing themselves up just to get an eyebrow ring or something." "Well where the hell were you ten years ago?" asked Angel sarcastically, as she ambiently ran her right hand over her patchwork- scar of a left wrist and forearm. "But it looks like you're running outta room to modify. I dunno, always thought about getting my eyebrow pierced but I never seemed to get there." She laughed. "How'd that look? I'm a mom now... I don't think I could imagine my mother with body piercings or ink." She seemed to think about something for a second. "What made you think about the wrestling business, to begin with? How come you're not playing in a band if you love music so much?" Angel added a pair of thermal socks to her nighttime wardrobe. "And please tell me to shut up if it's personal." "Well, around the time I got into this...I'd become 'mommy' to someone. A lot of shit was happening and I figured I could sit around and cut myself, or get my ass beat down as a release...the second alternative didn't seem quite so fucked up, so I took that route." She sighed. "Like you said...I've kinda run out of skin to work on, I need this permanent 'punishment' to keep my head on my shoulders." Angel would have replied, but Amy cut back in to finish the thought. "Sorta keeps me out of trouble...going so many places, doing so much...and constantly being sore, bloodied and worn out makes you think a hell of a lot less about your problems." "Plus...well...it's kinda fun to kick someone in the stomach a few times when you're pissed at the world." "Try a mic stand to some asshole's groin sometime, it's almost cathartic." Angel backed up and leaned against the pillows and headboard of her bed, yawning heartily. "This must be - I hate to use the word boring - but compared to what you guys must do on a wrestling tour, this has gotta be toned down to the nines, hmmm?" "Let's see..." Amy tucked both arms into her roomie Metallica t-shirt for warmth, rocking back and forth while she sarcastically pretended to ponder with a smile. "Well...I blast a bunch of music in my room, then sit around and talk to the four or five real friends I have in the company, then blast some more music, ice bruises and sleep.....................oh yeah, wrestling's a riot alright." "But it was wrestling that introduced you to Brandon, right?" She looked at her leg, which contained the only decoration on her entire body - the "Valerian's Garden" logo superimposed over the Chinese symbol for 'Angel'. "I think you're going to be good for each other. You'll teach him how to have fun and he'll help you be a bit more serious. And who knows? I certainly didn't see myself where I am now, two years ago." "The difference between us kind of scares me...I mean, he had a near eight-year marriage and I've barely been dating for eight years. It's definitely a learning experience but as long as I'm with him, nothing else matters...y'know? No matter how opposite we are...there's something about him..." She caught herself smiling a little too much, her mismatched eyes brighter than usual. "Err..yeah...but just don't go insinuating marriage quite yet." Amy winked to Angel, "Might scare the poor guy off, can you imagine spending every single day with someone like me?" "You sound like me." Angel's voice was suddenly quiet. "But besides that, isn't he already spending every single day with you? I mean, except for the days you're hanging out with the band... you guys are on the road together all the time. I've only done the travel part of a wrestling show... but it seems to me if Ivy and Sean can go from one extreme to the other in three years, you and Brandon, who actually like each other from the start, shouldn't have issues with something as pedantic as age." "It's not age...it's...well...I want to say maturity, but I think we're both equally 'mature' in our own right." Amy furrowed her brow, bobbing her head as she tried to work through what she was trying to say. "It's...like......mental age? I'm not sure...it's not like it'll ever stand in our way, it's just a nuisance. He's more settled down, while I'm kinda...out there." "There's nothing wrong with that," assured Angel, "You do what works for you and you'll be fine. Personally, I really do think you're good for each other." She smiled as she laid Mariella down in her travel- crib right next to her bed. "But as far as a serious relationship goes... it might've been a lifetime and six years since I was twenty but if you ever need a few days off, we've always got room on the bus for ya." "And on that note, my friend... it's time to pass out. Don't worry about the television, I'll sleep right through it." "Meh," Amy reached forward to the remote and flicked the TV off, shrugging. "I don't really watch it anyway. If crazies start calling my cell phone at five in the morning, just promise them alcohol and they'll go away...and if you wanna call them by name, it's Ryan, Renee and Wayne." She laughed again, though softly, "'Night, you...and MJ, of course." "G'Nite, Auntie Amy," replied Angel in a high- pitched 'baby' voice as the lights went out. In no time at all.... everyone slept.
