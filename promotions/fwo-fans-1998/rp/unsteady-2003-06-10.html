Unsteady.

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brandon paced. It had been nearly forty-five minutes since Uprising went off the air and while the road crew hurriedly disassembled the new set, Brandon made no effort to leave. A few minutes ago he saw a few of the Survivor III contestants walking out towards the rental cars, he was the last of the on-air talent left in the building. Or so he had assumed. Earlier in the night and shortly after his match with the Great Dragon Brandon and Amy had fought. After getting dressed he had went looking for her, the car was where they left it and upon returning to the locker room he could see that she hadn't snuck back in to get her bags. He simply didn't know where she was. Ivy and Eli had already left and now that he had waited so long he assumed she had left with them. Everything was getting messed up now. Things with Alex were at their lowest point in weeks and with his mother's failing health the family life was strained. Ana was by more often and with the road schedule picking up he and Amy seemed to never have any time for one another. The rides between cities and the like were fun but nowhere near a replacement for the 'quality time' they had been lacking. Now, all of this, Kinkade had made some off color comments and whether they were true or not Brandon had taken them to heart and confronted Amy over them. Now she was gone. Again, or rather, that's what he thought up until he turned, slinging his backpack over his shoulder as he hefted her duffel bag in his right hand. There stood Amy Campbell, his earthborn angel. Whether apologetic, hurt, stemming from some fervent anger or all three, the look in her wolf eyes was filled with some kind of sadness that managed to grip him. The redhead just stood for a second, watching him as he watched her. When she finally spoke, it was obvious enough that she was laboring to keep her tone aggressive even though the words didn't quite come out that way. "You have the keys." It would've been left at that, but she couldn't help but reason further. Still, Amy avoided the glaring argument. "And I...well...I was getting nervous being alone around here. Are you leaving now?" "Yeah. I figured you road back to the hotel with Ivy. I waited just in case." Brandon tossed the aforementioned keys in Amy's direction. "You drive?" "I hate driving. You know that." She grumbled, dropping the keys to the floor and kicking them his way. Eventually, it was apparent that there was no way Amy could hold back from bringing up the obvious. Somehow, her question came out incredibly casual...entirely too non-chalant. "Still think I'm fucking Kinkade?" "Never did... and to use your terminology I, however, think you're fucking oversensitive." The harshness in Brandon's word stung his own ears. Picking up the keys he finally looked back to Amy. "We can discuss this while we're moving, we've done the public display thing once tonight." The confused and unintentionally rabid mighty paragon walked up to Amy. Looking at her with sad and tired eyes he hoped she would just follow him, just to get the hell out of this place at least. And she did just that, but with a livid fire in her eyes. Aimz was far from pleased, and she made that known as they she walked behind Brandon. "Since when is it oversensitive to get offended when someone basically says you could be a repeat of their sluttish, uncaring bitch of an ex-wife?" Sighing and resigning himself to the fact the conversation wouldn't simply wait until they were in the confines of the small car from Hertz Brandon cocked his head to the side as he responded. "Well actually Amy when all I did was ask a question in the confines of the events that happened in front of my own eyes and then to have you shoot back so harshly seems to define oversensitive to me." Brandon didn't let up now that he had opened up either, not caring now that a few of the looser lips attached to the stage hands were within earshot. "I know what I heard Kinkade say, all I did was ask for you to clarify, maybe put me at ease. All it would have taken was for you to address specifically what he said and that would have been the end of that." The towering man turned suddenly and jostled the lock on the trunk of the car as he continued, much to the surprise of the shocked and caught off guard Amy Campbell. "I never said you a repeat of anyone, I said that with my history I hoped that you would understand where I was coming from, wanting to know what he meant. It was really that simple..." "Then that would mean with my history, I have every right to 'wonder' if you're really Alex' gay lover...see how ridiculous that sounds? By even considering that I might be fooling around with Kinkade, you were damn near comparing me to someone who didn't even love you enough to keep her knees together." Amy'd practically snapped, "By your logic, you're claiming you wouldn't be offended if I accused you of still being with that dumb bitch because she constantly comes around me talking and acting like you wish you still had her." With a last scathing, lingering glare, she ducked into the passenger side of the car...effectively leaving Brandon 'out in the cold' to think her words over until he finished filling the trunk. She was good at this fighting thing, and Brandon could tell it. Slamming the trunk possibly a little harder than intended he finally slid in to the little car, his knees knocking against the dashboard. "By my logic I asked you a question you could simply answer or could fly off the handle about. You chose the latter for whatever reason. I didn't accuse you of a damn thing. All I asked was that you explain the bastard's comments to me, then you go off like I had asked you how big his penis was." As Brandon continued the tires squealed, signaling the frustration in the resident 'hero'. "If you want to imply that Alex and I are having a lover's spat that's fine, whether or not you wish to ask me if something said in passing had any significance or outright accuse me of bending him over is another thing. And that's all I did. You're right though about one thing. Once you flew off the handle I shouldn't have followed suit. The backstabbing comment was over the top but you caught me off guard with the way you reacted." "So let's say I do start believing the shit Ana says about you two, you're saying you wouldn't get defensive if I came out and acted like you might be hiding something from me?" Amy raised an eyebrow, though her glare didn't cease. "That's bullshit, Brandon." "I. Asked. You. A. Simple. Question." Brandon punctuated every word, growing weary of the altercation and the lack of intelligence it seemed to contain. "How in the hell did I act like you were hiding something from me? You heard exactly what Kinkade said, I asked for you to make a statement on what he said. You blew up like a rookie. That's the part that's bullshit. Out of the blue and me having never asked you anything about your past you act like I gave you the third degree. If you need to go back to the stadium and watch the tape I'm sure they'll let us. You can then show me where I 'accused' you of anything." Brandon sighed heavily again. The smaller of the redheads began again, trying to calculate her words. "It's how you acte---actually, fuck it." She raised a tattooed arm to rub her temple, leaning as far away from Brandon's side of the car as she could. "Talking to you's about as easy as getting time together that you won't waste with work." "Talking to me is easier than getting time with me where I don't 'waste' it on work... all you have to do is talk, not argue and blow up over nothing." Brandon leaned over a bit, counteracting Amy's attempt at distancing herself. He stroked her hair with a heavy hand. Seeing the hotel ahead he let his eyes weigh as heavily as his heart for a second, before blinking and shaking his head, bringing himself back to a more or less state of alertness. Still, she turned back to gaze distantly out the window, propping her chin on a fist. "Well what else is there to talk about? We don't do anything! Every week it's the same thing: Finish the show, go back and sleep, go home, watch the kids, repeat process. Everything's so routine...that kind of crap shouldn't be going on until people've been married ten years. The only people who you bother hanging around are guys like Deacon, and that just leaves me to follow Ivy around all damn day." Her eyes hadn't wandered away from the window, even as they pulled into the hotel parking lot. The moments it take to retrieve their bags from the trunk are silent ones. Neither makes an attempt to speak until the heavy door of their room closes behind them. Once the lock shuts though Brandon takes the opportunity to come behind Amy and put his large arms around her. "Deac's a little stiff, I know that, but we're working on something for the business. It's just been a bit time consuming." "You tell me what you want to do and we'll do it. Anything. The world on a string and all that sweetie." Brandon turns his feisty better half around; reluctantly she complies until they are once again staring in each other eyes. "Is that where all of this is coming from though? Us not spending time together and how it seems we've been overtaken by sameness? It happens dear, sometimes a rut is a good thing so we can get things done... we just have to remember why we're doing this stuff and that we're doing it all because we love each other." "I just...arrrgh...." Amy lowered her eyes, coming close to burying her head against his chest. "It drives me nuts...every time I think we might get a second together, either Ana walks in and makes me wanna kill her or Deacon strikes up a conversation or...ugh...I don't know, it's just frustrating. And I don't want to have to ask to do stuff, you'd be surprised how much I hate seeming commanding..." "Well, as much as I would like to be psychic, I'm not. If you don't ask to do something I won't know you want to do it. You already know how lost in work I get. I just need you to smack me around every once and awhile to snap me out of it... and not to over react when I get worried about stuff. We're supposed to be a team." Brandon pulled Aimz to him, closer this time then either probably felt comfortable with after everything that was said but he couldn't help himself. She responded in kind, leaning against her Mighty Hero with a rather mighty sigh. "I'm...well...sorry. I've just been frustrated 'n stuff, and Kinkade's driving me nuts. It's just cabin fever, maybe..spent too long injured." Momentarily, she raised her head to look him in the eye with a half-forced, apologetic smirk. "I don't often blow up like that...think you can find it in that heart to forgive me?" "Only if you forgive me first." With that the two melted in to the other's arms. The questions remained though. Exactly what was the past between Kinkade and Amy? What was happening with Ana being back so strongly in Brandon's life with his mother so sick? How would this fledgling couple deal with these harsh realities that seemed poised to destroy them? [This has been a Brand Frontier/ Aimz collaboration.]
