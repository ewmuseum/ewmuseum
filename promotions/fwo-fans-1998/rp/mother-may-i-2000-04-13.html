Mother may I?

13 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Mother may I? April 13, 2000 **Cameras pick up Ultra-Violet, son of Floyd, son of Elmerah they pick up Ultra-Violet. UV stands in his father's house, pouting at the red haired man talking to him. The red haired man, of course, is UV's father and mentor, Infra-Red.** I want you to go in there and tell your mother that you're getting married. This is really something that she should know. You've been putting this off for weeks. What's the problem? **UV looks sheepishly at his father.** Well I don't know. I'm just not sure mom will approve of Violet. Violet doesn't even watch "Days of our Lives!" **Infra-Red claps his son on the back with the meaty hand of an ex-wrestler.** Don't you worry about that son. I married Tina because she's totally dependent on me and defines herself entirely on my approval or rejection of her. I like having that kind of power over a woman. Plus she's really great in bed! She does this thing with her legs and tongue amazing. **Infra-Red gives his son a proud smile. He then considers what he just said. His eyes grow slightly wide and he sputters out a quick correction.** I uh mean I um married her because she's a kind and understanding sort that deals well with all people in a caring and sympathetic way. I'm sure she'll approve of your marriage. No go on and tell her. She's not watching TV right now, so she should be all ears. **During his father's diatribe, Ultra-Violet's face turns to shock and near hysteria. He looks horrified at the words his father has spoken, unsure that his ears did not deceive him. A dry mouth croaks out a broken voice, trying to confirm the harsh words of the aging grappler.** Pops, did you say that that **UV swallows hard.** MOM IS NOT WATCHING TV?!?! What's wrong with her? Is she sick? Why didn't you tell me sooner!?! **Infra-Red chuckles softly.** No, she's quite all right, I assure you. I've actually managed to convince her that she should take up reading. I believe she's perusing some fine literature right now. Go on boy, talk to your mother; don't make me slap the taste out of your mouth. **UV nods glumly.** Yes sir. **Ultra-Violet walks into the living room where his mother, Tina, seems to be a permanent fixture. Bottle blond hair sports a tie-dye handkerchief wrapped around it this fine afternoon. It's done in a stylish red and purple to pay homage to the two wrestling men in her family. An imitation fur boa is draped about Tina, complimenting the Wal-Mart stretch pants and spandex top that grip tightly to the middle aged woman's only slightly deteriorating form. Her bare feet show off a rainbow of colors; each toenail has been painstakingly painted in its own unique gloss. Conspicuous by their absence, the off-brand "Lee Press-on Nails" that typically adorn UV's mother are nowhere to be seen. They've apparently been removed to allow for easier page turning. The pages being turned, are of a peculiar sort. They are a good deal larger than those of a book, but a shade smaller than a typical newspaper. The top notch camera crew gets a peak at the periodical's cover. The leading headline declares, "ALIEN BABY SON OF PROWRESTLER!" As UV approaches, Tina looks up and puts down her paper.** They found Big Foot! He's been living under the waves at Loch Ness. Of course the Men in Black got to the poor creature and carted him off in the flying saucer they made at the Bermuda Triangle. **Ultra-Violet nods slowly, soaking up the information. He interrupts his mother.** Who saw all this go down? Were there any witnesses? Only Jesus and Elvis, but I've come to count on them for reliable news no matter what the circumstances. Anyway, they're going to keep Big Foot at Area 51 until the devil tries to invade America again. Then they're going to breed him with a pair of midget, Siamese twins to create the perfect soldier **UV cuts in.** I suppose they'll clone him and outfit the new army with the ray guns they found on the Yeti last year? **Tina smiles.** There're no reports on that yet, but I was thinking the same thing. The Aboniable-Sasquachinator is just the thing to put evil in its place. I'm just hoping they don't try and wake Godzilla. We all remember what happened the last time. **Mother and son share an understanding look. Tina then turns thoughtful.** It also says that that Machina fellow you know died and has been replaced with a woman. **Ultra-Violet snorts in disdain.** Mother you really shouldn't believe everything that you read. Actually Mom, I have something to tell you. I'm getting married, at Cyberslam! **Tina beams with joy and pride at her son's announcement. She gives his a teary-eyed hug and then pulls him back to cast an appraising eye at him.** My little Clarence is getting married. Tell me all about it! **UV shifts a bit.** Well. You see, there's this nice lady that I know. As I understand it, some time ago she tried to make me jealous by being all girly on Kid Cool. Now I didn't realize what was going on for a while, but when I did, by-jeepers-golly I was steamed. I put that Kid Cool meanie in his place and got the nice lady back in my corner. I was so glad to have her back that I asked her to marry me and stuff. That all seemed good. But then **Tina interrupts.** but then an outrageous, masked, cruiser-weight foreigner kissed your girl. A brave young fellow interceded himself in the situation and tried to keep the anguish of this dirty little secret from you. It worked for a pay per view or so, but you found out the truth. You got mad and challenged the wrestler to a match at the biggest event of the year. He conceded, but only if you agreed to a hair verses mask stipulation. In the end, you might end up getting married with no hair on your head! **UV blinks.** Yeah mom, that's right. How did you know? **Tina sighs dreamily.** How romantic. That's the exact same way your father and I got hitched! **Ultra-Violet slowly processes this batch of information. The gears turn and then** So did Pops win? **Tine lowers her voice.** Now it's not my place to say, but why would a grown man have foot long hair if he were not recovering from some past trauma? **Ultra-Violet thinks on this and frowns. With a glum voice he mutters** Thanks for the pep talk mom. Anytime sweetie! **Cameras fade to purple.**
