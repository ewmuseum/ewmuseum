Eye For An Eye [10] Visited Upon

28 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In some cultures marriages are arranged. It wasn't a custom practiced in the United States but regardless my father had taken it upon himself to arrange my marriage. It started as a date with destiny. And ended as a marriage to fate. August 27th 1993. "I expected you to be here, which is why I came to find you before you came to find me." Vince Kinkade said as he approached a brooding John Hanson who sat amongst the crowd at WWC's weekly event just seven days after being publicly humiliated. "I don't think you have anything to say that I want to hear." Hanson growled in reply. "Actually you're wrong, I came here to apologize for the behaviour of my son Kellen, you see he's just made the transition over to being a heel and he was told to come out here and abuse some of the fans... I didn't realize that he'd pick you out and I'm sorry for it. But that's the business you know? It can be brutal at times." Hanson brushed his hand over the eye patch covering the injury sustained at the hands of Vince "Don't speak to me about brutal... and you can keep your apology, I want to hear one from the boy right away because I know you don't want me to come seeking one out." "As you wish." Vince said as he turned away from Hanson "I'll go backstage and find him right now." As Vince Kinkade pushed and shoved through the crowd his arrogance became further evident... such remarks as "Which family member gets the rub next, asshole?" and "Going backstage to abuse your power are you?" were completely ignored. "Kellen!" Vince said with delight as he made his way through the curtains to the back "Excellent work last week son, the crowd were really making some noise." "Thanks, so what are we doing this week?" An eager Kellen asked. "Much of the same, I've spoken to the guy we used next week and he's happy to come back and make it an ongoing thing. Perhaps we'll have him managing someone to take you on down the line but for now insulting him is perfect... get out there and do your stuff!" "Wow, really?" Kinkade said "I'm surprised actually... the guy looked pretty pissed when he was leaving last week." "Exactly, he's a professional that knows how to do his job... now go on, you don't want to miss your prompt." Kellen nodded and made his way toward the curtain parting backstage and entranceway... finally making his way through the curtain as "Outshined" by Soundgarden started to play. The booing was far more convincing this week and Kinkade had to keep a proud smile to himself in order not to break character. "Seven days and here we are again!" Kinkade roared as he grabbed a microphone and made his way into the ring "I look around and I see a lot of familiar faces, can't say that any of them are pretty but it's nice to see that you all turned out again. Even my cyclops buddy in the front row." All eyes were once again upon John Hanson who'd already stood to his feet with a furious scowl upon his face, extending a middle finger in Kinkade's direction. "Easy there fella, you'll take somebody's eye out with that!" Kinkade laughed as those at ringside all chuckled to themselves. Hanson continued to mouth furious comments toward Kinkade, before waving him down to the ringside area "Oh, you want me to come down there do you?" Kinkade cried as he wiped a joyous tear from his eye. "Looks like you and I don't see eye to eye on this matter." He said as he clambered through the ropes "Surprised that you showed up after last weeks mocking, then again... when you only have one eye you probably need to see things twice so that they soak in." Kinkade dropped down to the arena floor and started to make his way toward Hanson... putting a hand over his right eye as he did "Lets do this! I'll even cover my eye so that it's a fair f-" He never finished his sentence, John Hanson reached into his pocket and pulled a handful of something out of it... hurling it forth into Kinkade's face. The WWC champion immediately stumbled backwards and fell to the arena so as to sell the shot, which he presumed his father had arranged as part of the segment. But as he felt the substance that'd hit him in his exposed left eye start to burn, he knew something was terribly wrong. "Twenty years I've waited for that you little piece of crap, tell your father what goes around comes around!" Kinkade heard John Hanson scream as he staggered back up and tried to make his way to the back, the burning pain was becoming extremely intense. "Fuck... help, it hurts!" Kinkade screamed as he burst through the curtains into the backstage area and collapsed to the ground. "Jesus Christ!" Vince Kinkade roared "Get security out there to take care of that crazy son of a bitch... Kellen, let me see your eye!" "I can't!" Kinkade screamed "I can't open it, whatever he threw at me is burning... help me!" Vince desperately searched around before laying his hands upon a bucket of water; he didn't hear the medical staff scream "No... wait!" as he threw it into Kellen's face to wash away the substance. As the cool water hit Kellen in the face he should've been instantly relieved, instead the pain grew more intense than he could ever have imagined. And that's when he passed out.
