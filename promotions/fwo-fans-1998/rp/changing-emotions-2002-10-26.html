Changing Emotions.

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brand Frontier pushed past the curtain as he avoided the camera crew and the various news reporters that were allowed backstage during an fWo event. The screams and adulation of the fans were fresh in his ears, yet as excited as he was feeling the predominant emotion running through him was one of anger. Earlier in the night Brand Frontier basically lost his son. There was no other way to put it, no one else to blame. Not Ana, his wife or the stage hand that pulled him away from his family. Not Chris Universal for calling for him, nor even the Boston Strangler. It was his fault. He let himself be swept up in the event, the spectacle that is this business. As soon as the boss called Frontier ran, tail wagging to see what was wanted of him. What hoop he was wished to jump through and what deed he needed to perform in gratitude of his place in the federation. Maybe that was a little harsh on the situation but at the moment it seemed right. That is what lost his son, his own self-indulgence... and he was angry about it. Ana did the best she could given the situation. It was a hectic moment and one Brandon should not have abandoned her during. He didn't do his job. Just like he didn't do his job four years ago when Ana disappeared. Pregnant with Sean at the time Brandon lost his family in a heartbeat. For a month he was lost as he toiled in his own misery searching for his beloved. Once again that happened tonight. In a heartbeat everything changed. On the heels of being told he must face one of the dastardliest men in the organization all Brandon could think of was his family and what he might lose. It was all about perspective. He was back to that again. Wasn't what he was doing for his family? Wasn't everything he has put himself through and made his family suffer for for the better cause? If not, then what was it for? As he made his way down the hall to the dressing area his mood switched from anger to doubt. Maybe this quest wasn't about doing the right thing; maybe it was just a way for him to feed a massive ego? Maybe he was just a product of the very things he fought against? Or maybe he was doing as he always did, over analyzing things. Making the harsher and more complex than they needed to be. Maybe he was processing these feelings in the wrong direction. He had done that a lot as of late. He used to be so sure of himself. He used to stride forth and never give a thought about his actions. Brandon knew that his every move was calculated for the cause. It did of course justify the means. Now as he walked into the dressing area and saw the last stragglers from the fWo event grabbing their gear his emotions flipped again. Doubt replaced by resolution. This is what he was here for. This night he did what no one thought he could do. He stood toe to toe with one of the vilest individuals in the federation and won. The victory awarded from the shoulders on the mat was less than the victory he felt in himself. It was a win he needed not to further a cause or to take a step up an unseen ladder. No. This win was about proving something to his self. He needed to know that he could meet the likes of these cretins nose to nose. It was also a win that would send a message to the likes of the Boston Strangler. Anger sidled up beside resolution at this point. He looked over to the bench where Ana had made a makeshift bed for Sean. The last hour had taken its effect on him and he was fast asleep. He wanted so much to see his Dad beat up the bad guys but he just couldn't keep those heavy lids pried open a minute longer. Earlier as Brandon, Ana, and a host of others watched from the monitor in the dressing room the carnage in the ring took place Sean had already begun to fade. As Ruben Ross and Scott Slugger are taken down by the combined might of the Boston Strangler, Austin Davis and the monstrous Vic Creed Sean floated off to sleep. He awaited help to arrive in another form but none came. Looking quickly to Sean Brandon glanced to Ana as he paced. Their eyes locked for what seemed like forever. By the fire in his eyes Ana knew the question and as she laid her hand on their now sleeping son she slowly nodded. Brandon needed to be who he was and she knew it. He sprinted out of the room without a word. Now as he returned, his son looked more comfortable. Safe almost. That is what angered him the most, the false sense of safeness that he lived under. Earlier when he saw the Boston Strangler sitting close to his son Brandon's world stopped. What would have happened had he not arrived in time? Would the Strangler have hurt his son because Brand Frontier interfered in his business the week before? Austin Davis routinely brought bags of glass to the ring and nothing was done about it. People were allowed to traipse around with sledgehammers and nail guns. Hardcore matches had conditioned the fans to lust after blood and violence.
