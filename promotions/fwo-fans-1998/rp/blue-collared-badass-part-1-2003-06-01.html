Blue- Collared Badass (part 1)

1 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Blue- Collared Badass(pt. 1): Monday, June 2, 2003 Detroit, Michigan 6:00am His alarm had sounded twenty minutes ago, but he wasn't quite awake. He laid there, on his twin sized bed allowing his body to catch up to his mind. The night's sleep had done him good, the weekend off did him better... he got up from his bed and glanced over at the mess he had made the night before - Chip crumbs, and juice drippings all over his dresser where he kept his clothing items. ...he'd clean it up later. He walked into the bathroom, and immediately turned the sink water on to wash his face. He removed as much sleep from his eyes as possible, before searching in the medicine cabinet for something he couldn't find at the moment... ahh there it is... he thought as he opened the jar of 'Noxzema' and began rubbing it all over his face. His pours had immediately opened up, his face felt amazing, if he wasn't wide awake before, he most certainly was now. He had to be to work by six forty- five, and he was already running late. ...there was no time for a shower, but the more accustomed he became to the life of an average Joe, the more tricks he learned. Like washing up a certain way to make sure your pits didn't stink, no matter how hard you may have sweat that day. After washing up in steaming hot water, he began to wipe the fog from his bathroom mirror, and in doing so, he did something he hadn't done for a long time now... he looked at himself. Not at the blue eyes, that had become his trademark, he actually looked at himself. He had trimmed his hair significantly shorter than it was when he was last seen in the public's eye, just below his ears, he could barely even get a pony- tail, but he could. All the acne, the blemishes that were beginning the form had began to clear, his psychiatrist told him that they were stress related but he didn't really believe him. How did he get here? ...and, better yet... how long would he stay? Those were questions he wasn't ready to answer. Besides, if he left, were would he go? To the CSWA? ...that was an option. Chad Merritt wasn't the most trustworthy individual on the planet, but he did elevate Sean in ways no other promoter before him did. The fWo? Please... the more he got to know the fWo, the less he liked it. Growing up in an unselfish household taught him to be a certain way, and that was something he carried over into his career. When you bend over backwards for people, and you feel a certain way about them... if it's not reciprocated... it screws with your mind to the point where you're given two options, to either walk, or explode. ...he would never find another Lucky, no matter how hard he tried, and that realization drove him off the deep end. Atleast he had the 'Rat Pack', right? Wrong. Brand Frontier was a very nice man, who he had hoped to get to know better, but the more he found out about him, the less he wanted to know. There are various types of people in this world, one is a hero, and Sean wasn't in that category. He loved Poison Ivy and Eli Flair like no other, Ivy was his girl, Flair, his brother... but, neither could replace Luck, nor did they try. They knew enough about me to know that trying too hard would make matters worse. He'd often say. AC? Honestly... he had no feelings when it came to her. He didn't dislike her... but, he didn't like her. In fact, he never even thought about the situation to determine whether he did or not. Once she stepped out of his life, he moved on and didn't look back. ...it was better that way, much better. Lance Knight? Lance was cool, but let's be real... Lance asked Sean to do him a favor, that Stevens gladly did. He jobbed to Lance on more than one occasion, even going so far as to say, I Respect You in a match, and if that wasn't enough... he even tagged with him, helping him gain enough credibility to enter into the United States title division. ...then left him with nothing to do as a result. He wasn't mad, but the fact remained the same, he wouldn't have done that. His second alarm sounded, it was six fifteen, time to get his clothes and go to work. I'll call Ivy from there Stevens mumbled, as he put on his blue, prison- esque jumpsuit and walked out the door. Damn... he didn't even get to say goodbye to aunt Janice. I'll call her, too.
