Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 7 - Part One

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You ever reach a stage where the movement is so fast and all your concentration is focused solely on one object that everything around is silent. Almost as if life has been halted just for your sake. That all existence as you know it ceases to move, to breathe, to be alive. Almost as if you and the object are all that exists, and in one moment. You two own the world. Angel Dalton was close on Steve Douglas' heels, as he chased the fleeing Douglas down through the dark alleyway. Douglas was quick on his feet, as he seemed to flow with ease as he jumped up a silver fence and seemed to float over the top, and as soon as he hit the ground he was half way down the next part of the alley. Angel could keep up, he grabbed a hold of that fence and was over it faster than Douglas was. His feet hit the ground and Angel was running as hard as he could. He had to get him. Angel could see a road coming up and Douglas heading right for it. Angel held out his gun and let off one more shot, the bullet smacked hard into a garbage can that Douglas happened to be running past. 6 shots left. Douglas made it to the road, only to notice that all traffic had stopped. No cars moved and no persons standing on the side of the road moved. Douglas laughed to himself before jumping up onto the hood of a car and beginning to make his way across the road by jumping car to car. Angel reached the street and watched Douglas jump to the final car, Angel jumped from the hood of the first car and then stumbled once he hit the second one. Getting composure Angel's eyes caught the driver of the second car, Angel stared at him for a few moments noticing that the driver did not move at all. Angel was up to his feet and jumping from hood to hood till he finally made it to the next alleyway and as Angel jumped from the final car, the street became alive again and before Angel's feet touched the pavement the sound of traffic and people were heard echoing from behind. Angel turned to look back at the alive street as he continued to run. *SMUCK* That was the sound of a lead pipe being smashed into Angel's head. *THUMP* That was the sound of Angel hitting the ground.
