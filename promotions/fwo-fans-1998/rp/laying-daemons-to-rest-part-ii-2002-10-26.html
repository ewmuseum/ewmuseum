Laying Daemons To Rest. Part II

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sweat smell behind the curtain was heavy. With all of the other E.W.A. Superstars entering and exiting through there it was expected. For lack of a better description it was the smell of combat. Brandon stood there waiting for his cue to enter the ring. He paced nervously. This was the night. The night he finally got his love back. This was the night that he might pay the ultimate price for his family, and he wouldn't have it any other way. "Princes of the Universe" by Queen blasted through the arena and a stagehand signaled to Brandon. He swept the sweat from his forehead and stepped to the curtain. When the pyrotechnics hit Brand Frontier pushed passed the cloth and strode out onto the entranceway. The fan's in attendance at Madison Square Garden erupted. They always gave Brand an extra boost of adrenaline but honestly on this night it was not needed. It was almost distracting. He had a goal this night, one that was more important than any title or accolade they could give to him. This time he entered the ring to win back his wife. Brandon Roche looked down the long ramp-way and into the ring. There stood his one obstacle in the safe return of his beloved Ana. "The Kodiak" Vic Creed. This monster of a man was ominous indeed and it seemed from the expression on his face that he was going to enjoy this beating. Brand had been in a war with Creed's cohorts, The Brotherhood. They scoffed at the idea of a rookie coming in and standing in their way of total federation domination, but that is exactly what Brand Frontier, Jon Hawkman and Chris Reid did. And this was the price they had paid. Now though it was all about to end, maybe for real. As Brand stomped to the ring Vic tossed off his Brotherhood t-shirt and flexed his muscles. He let out a monstrous roar as he pounded his fist into his open hand. A wicked smile creeps across his face as Frontier steps through the ropes. Forgoing any of his normal in ring acts Brand just pauses for a second while he looks to Creed. He seems to mutter something under his breath as he makes a cross symbol over his chest. Creed steps up to Brand as the come nose to nose in the middle of the ring. Stillness fills the arena as the fan wait to see what happens next. Long seconds pass as the two warriors simply stare at each other. Brand might be taller than Creed but he is twice as wide, both imposing figures taking up much of the ring. Suddenly Brand steps back away from The Kodiak. As he does, Creed cocks a hand back in readiness of Brand's first attack. The crowd begins to cheer at the thought of The Paragon nailing Creed but they are shocked instead when Frontier simply falls to his knees in front of the monster. Brand reaches his arms out to his sides as if begging Vic to begin his onslaught. Vic simply stand there for a few seconds taunting Brand and laughing at him. The humor in the situation quickly fades though as an evil smirk glides onto The Kodiak's face. The impact of the first kick to Brand's head sends him falling backwards. Creed moves in and begins stomping on the hapless Frontier. Trying to soften the blows but not fighting back proves hard for Frontier has he blocks a few of the heavy boots by habit. Resisting the urge to throw his attacker off of him and battle anew he just lays there. The crowd reacts as expected and has begun to rain boos down on the ring. They are unpleased with what they are being forced to watch to say the least. When Creed backs off for a second to savor his handiwork Brand begins to rise. Instead of getting to his feet though he simply returns to his kneeling position and with exaggerated movements puts his hands behind his back. Creed pauses for a second, himself almost in shock at Brand Frontier not fighting back. He probably figured Brand would only be able to take so much of it before boiling over like usual. Seizing the moment Vic stomps over to Brand and lays a heavy fist right to the face of the Paragon. Frontier's head again snaps back as blood gushes from the corner of his mouth. Creed grabs a handful of his victim's hair and pulls him to his feet. Brand complies and as he stands he is caught in a vice like hold that pushes all the air from his body. Creed wrenches in with the bear hug and drives his forehead into Frontier's face as he holds him so close. A cut opens along the top of Brand's eye now and his face begins to swell. The crowd itself has grown uneasy with this mugging. They are not used to seeing someone get handled this way without fighting back. It is almost obscene. Tiring of this form of torture Creed tightens the bear hug and suddenly springs Brand over his head with a belly to back suplex. Frontier crashes to the mat and holds his back as Creed now hovers over him and begins another assault filled with heavy stomps. The announcers at ringside are going crazy as they try to explain the situation to the rabid fans watching at home. Two medics have now stationed themselves at ringside just incase this slaughter is anywhere near done. Taking a break from the awful beating Creed stares out into the crowd and soaks in the response from the masses. He raises his arms in the air and the fans begin to cheer wildly. Being a veteran of the mat war Vic swings around quickly with his fist up and ready for an attack by Brand Frontier. Instead he is surprised to see that Frontier has risen from the mat and stood a few feet behind him. He just stands there, barely able to keep his balance as he simply puts his hands behind his back. Creed laughs sadistically. Creed cocks his massive arm back and set his feet apart as he hammers Frontier in the head with another devastating punch. Brand's nose shatters as blood spurts out from the newly open wound. Frontier's face now a crimson mask it is hard to recognize the hero.
