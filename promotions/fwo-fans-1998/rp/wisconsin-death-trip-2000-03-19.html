Wisconsin Death Trip

19 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Wisconsin Death Trip March 19, 2000 [TV-HC] (VL is driving in her black jeep on a highway. With one hand on the steering wheel and the other hand on a cup of coffee, the stereo blasts Mobb Deep's "Shook Ones, Part II", her theme music. She is wearing a red Avirex leather jacket and a pair of baggy jeans, as well as boots.) So here the fuck I am in Wisfuckinconsin. Land of muthafuckin stank-ass cheese and muthafuckin tasteless beer. I got a big match coming up in six days. Big muthafuckin match. There's gonna be muthafuckin explosives, barbed-wire, wooden boards, you name, it'll be there. And there's gonna be pain. Oh shit, there's gonna be pain. Pain for me, and a shitload of pain for Hellrazor. You see, Tommy, you may have my title, but you don't have my soul. I've held that title seven muthafuckin times, and I wiped my ass with it every time I held that piece of tin. A title spray painted red. Where's the gold, where's the muthafuckin platinum? Where's the real muthafuckin titles? They say I'm not good enough to hold those titles. I say I'm too fuckin good for this shit. I've kicked Tommy's ass enough times to deserve to move on up like the Jeffersons. But them muthafuckas try to keep a sista down forever. And so on March 25, I'ma set Tommy on fire. I'ma wrap Tommy in barbed-wire. I'ma make Tommy bleed out of every vein the muthafucka got. But I'm not gonna do it for a title I don't give a fuck about. I'm not gonna do it cause I like beating his brains in. I'ma do it cause I got shit to prove. Cause some muthafuckaz don't think I'm for real. Cause while you might be able to get a bitch on her knees, after she's down for a while, she gonna bite ya dick off. I don't give a fuck if Saturday is my last hardcore match. I don't give a fuck if Saturday is my last match. If I need to paralyze myself or even kill myself, I'ma get respect. (The jeep pulls up to a tollbooth, as VL plops in some change and heads into the state of Illinois, on her way to Indiana.) [To be continued...]
