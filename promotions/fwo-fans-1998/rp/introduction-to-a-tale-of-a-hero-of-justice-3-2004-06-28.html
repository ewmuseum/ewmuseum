Introduction to a tale of a Hero of Justice. (3)

28 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 6, 1988 100 Degrees, bloody hot out Waylon watched from behind the bleachers after he received his diploma, he hated being in front of people, hated their sneers. I mean, the only reason he was still hanging around was to talk to Sara. She was almost up there, almost ready to walk in front of the ongoing smiles. Waylon didn't get a single smile, except for one from Sara. "Sara Sundae" Waylon immediately turned his attention to the crack of space in between two bleacher seats. He puts his eyes as close to that crack as he could to watch her walk toward stage, how she moved, with such elegance and at the same time confidence. She was the most beautiful girl that he had ever seen. Toady he had to tell her, today was the day he would profess his love to her. Waylon eyed her as she descended the stairs and smiled for one final picture for her parents. Time came to a halt when her eyes caught Waylon's, a blurry haze fell over and Waylon's surroundings grew darker, his eyes stuck on her eyes and Waylon began to feel dizzy. Waylon fell forward and his head knocked against the bleacher and he quickly recovered by grabbing on of the bleachers with his right hand. For a moment he felt sick, he was scared. Waylon moved to the left, constantly keeping his gaze fixated through the crack in the bleachers and as Sara moved, he followed from a distance. Finally coming to the end of the bleacher seats he moved his body around the stairs into the open, after spotting Sara with some classmates he quickly ducked back. "Damn it all," Waylon muttered to himself, "Damn it all." He repeated, trying to gain the courage to go at it again, he turned his body to the open again and then stared at her again, "Okay," he began, "Gotta do this." With a fierce determination Waylon was onward! Seeing his destiny before him he had to strike at this moment, this was his check mate, this was his triumphant moment! Today Sara would be his, as he would tell her that he loved her, and she would respond to him the same words. "Wait..." Waylon stopped himself abruptly, frozen and staring at Sara. Suddenly Waylon felt sick again, he had doubts. Meanwhile Sara takes notice... "Hey Sara, is Perceval staring at you or what? That guy is such a creep," Kirsten who had been standing with Sara had been staring at Waylon the whole time since he had popped out of behind the bleacher. She turned to Sara now, "That guy has been drooling over you for years, it's so pathetic. I mean what a loser, no one likes him anyway, he's just annoying." Sara smiled, "Sorta, but I like him, he always been my friend. But he is kind of a loser," Sara didn't really want to say it but it was true, no offense to Waylon but he was a loser. He was cute, and nice, but Sara knew that he wasn't cool. She liked him because she saw past it, but at the same time saw him in the same light as others did. Sara looked over at Waylon to see him frozen and looking at her, she smiled and then raised one eyebrow. Sara was startled by an most unwelcome interruption. "That kids a bitch!" Greg had heard the whole conversation and was staring directly at Waylon. He moved past Sara and toward Waylon. Waylon looked at Greg who was now coming at him, "Damn it all." Greg had gotten closer by now and began to talk to Waylon, "So Perceval, decided to come out into the sun? I thought this weather turned freaks like you into ashes." Waylon having no good comeback merely began his plea, "Why do you always have to come down on me Greg, I never got in your face. Why do you get in mine?" A circle had now formed and the mob had come together, random shouts came from the audience like, "Fuck him up Greg," "Punch the faggot" and Waylon had begun to back up. "You want to know why I hate you," Greg began as he stepped closer to Waylon, "I hate you because people feel so sorry for freaks like you. They let you come to our schools, they let you be around us, when the truth is we hate you. We hate you Perceval, because your useless. No one likes you, no one ever will. I hate you because it's instinct. So get OUTTA here!" Greg pushed Waylon hard and he hit the ground fast, no sooner than hitting the ground Greg followed the push with a swift kick to Waylon's face. Immediately Sara jumped in front of Greg, "You get away from him Greg, you asshole! You've done enough!" Greg looked at Sara, his eyes full of fire and his chest breathing heavy, "What's it matter anyway Sara, I'm doing us a favor." Greg proceeded with spitting at Sara and then exiting through the small crowd. The crowd died off and Sara was left looking down at Waylon. "I'm sorry you had to see that Sara," Waylon began, "But I really got to tell you something, I've been meaning to all my life." Waylong paused for a moment to look up at Sara, he studied her for a moment, she was facing the crowd, "Sara?" "Huh?" She turned back to him, at once her face turned to frustration, "Oh Waylon, why do you always have to do this? Why do you have to embarrass yourself, you have such potential you know." She leaned down and looked at him, "I don't think you're a loser Waylon, you're my best friend. You have to get out of here, go put your mind to use. Stop trying to play with the big boys Waylon, you can't cut it. Look, school's over and I'm out of here. I can't look after you anymore and I wasn't doing a good job." Sara stopped and raised herself, she turned back to the crowd, "Being in the spotlight is not your show Waylon, but it's mine. You've been great and we'll keep in touch, but we were meant to be friends...forever. I wish you the best Waylon, but I think being in your life right now is just bringing you hardship, you need to find something to channel all of that energy into. Happy Graduation Waylon." Waylon was in shock, "Oh... Sara." Sara cut him off, "I can't talk about it right now Waylon, there is an after party, I'm invited. Have fun tonight..., " Her eyes look to him one more time, a soft touch and then quickly she reacted with, "Gotta go." And like that she was off and Waylon watched her walk away, "But Sara," He continued, "I was suppose to tell you I loved you...you were suppose to love me." Waylon laid back down on the grass relaxing his body, "You will love me." He wouldn't see her face again for sixteen years.
