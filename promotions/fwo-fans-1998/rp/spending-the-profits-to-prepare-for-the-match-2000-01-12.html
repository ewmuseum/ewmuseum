Spending the profits to prepare for the match...

12 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Spending the profits to prepare for the match... January 12, 2000 [The scene- a local "Toys 'R' Us" in Hartford, Connecticut. Havok is shown standing in the front of the store, with oen hand in his pocket and another pushing his shopping cart. He notices the cameraman, looks over, and speaks to him.] Havok: Hey! I'm glad you're here. I'm going to do a little shopping to prepare for my match against Wretch and Courage. Of course, I will be going to Sea World for a trip tomorrow. Anyway, it wouldn't look good if the Millennium Man was unprepared, so I'm gonna stock up for my match at Invasion. I have some money from selling thos eexcellent magazines. [He pulls his hand out of his pocker, revealing a giant pile of cash in his hand.] Havok: Actually, I have enough money to buy that whale I wanted. But I'm not going to Sea World until tomorrow. Anyway, let's check out the fWo aisle, where I can get some good training equipment. [He walks over to an aisle full of fWo action figures. He looks through them all, talking to himself.] Havok: Courage figure, Wretch figure... where's Havok's figure? I see about ten million Hellrazor figures, but can't find any Havoks or Lamonts! Dude, they have to be here somewhere. [He searches more, finding none.] Havok: Damn, I'll have to come back later. [He looks over to the end of the next aisle, and sees a box for a board game. He runs over, pulling the cart behind him, grabs the box, and, staring at it, turns to the camera and speaks.] Havok: Mystery date! This brings back memories. I mean, personally, I prefer Monopoly, and Scrabble, because I get money in Monopoly and can spell out funny words in scrabble, but this is cool too. It was Chris Kidman, Jr.'s favorite game when we were kids! Oh, Wretch would love to play this. [He tosses it into the basket. The camera pans down to read the box. It says "Mystery Date" and has a picture of four girls playing it on the cover.] Havok: What else would I need for a match against Courage and Wretch? YES! The movie! [He runs to the children's movie section and picks up a copy of "The Wizard of Oz." He holds it up to the camera, and talks about it.] Havok: In this movie, the lion had no courage, right? So, if I watch the movie, I can watch hat the lion does. Then, I'll know everything that I should *not* expect. If Courage has courage like he said, and the lion doesn't, then Courage will do everything the lion doesn't do. Then, by process of elimination, I can know everything Courage will do during our match! I am a genius! I've got to buy these and get ready for the match! Then I need to pack to go to Sea World tomorrow. [He rushes to the front of the store, and before hearing the price, he throws a pile of cash onto the counter and runs out with his stuff. The camera fades out as the cameraman wonders if the Millennium Men has lost his marbles.]
