Prelude to Oblivion 2

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bronx, NY December 30th, 2002 6:48 PM Just like old times. The scent of home was in the air. Eli Flair missed the Bronx with a passion that can only be experienced after leaving a place of birth and returning after life- altering changes have been made. "Yo, Flair! Kick some ass tonight!" Eli laughed at the statement - he recognized the people walking to the main entrance of the arena as some of the boys Terry Cooper trains in his gym just three blocks away. Hi, it's April. Say something. "Something. Hey April, it's Eli. I'm down the street at the show, was hoping you'd be home. Haven't seen ya since I moved to Cali and was hoping you'd come by and meet Angel," said Eli into his borrowed cell phone, "And I... kinda wanted you to be around tomorrow night for this... thing I've got planned. It would really mean a lot to Angel and I if you'd go." ... "Alright well... you know who to call when you get this. Love ya." He clicked the phone off and looked around - if he was cold, standing in the streets of the Bronx in only wrestling tights and a "Stump: It's not just a stump, it's an Attitude" T-Shirt - he didn't show it. "Full circle," he said to nobody in particular, "Full circle from this town... who'da thunk it?" "I would," came an unexpected reply from behind. He spun around, face to face with a young man of about twenty- five. Sloppy brown hair covered his head and a grin the size of Manhatten had appeared on his face. Johnny Fizzbin. One time professional wrestler in the CSWA as a favor for Eli Flair and Poison Ivy, now a greasemonkey in his own auto body shop and faithful assistant trainer at COOPER'S GYM. "Back in the day you always had more than the other guys," continued Fizzbin, "You could tell when you were in the ring... this kid's going places." They embraced in a quick hug - two men who have been through death and life together. Two deaths that have affected them both deeply ironically brought them closer together. The first was Eli's best friend, Johnny's older brother Paul Fizzbin. He chose a life of petty crime, and was shot by police trying to open an ATM. The second was Eli's wife, Poison Ivy's big sister, Alicia. "Seen April around?" asked Eli, knowing Fizzbin was the focal point for the entire 'hometown crew,' "I was trying to get in touch with her while I'm actually in the area... would love for y'all to meet Angel." Fizzbin shook his head. "Nope, she's been MIA for about a month now." "Damn. You stickin' around?" "Wish I could, but Coop needs my help in the gym tomorrow. Catchin' the show and headin' out." Eli smiled. "Another Mid Week Madness show?" "'S there any other kind? Listen man, take it easy." They shook hands and Fizzbin headed toward the official entrance. Eli watched him go, immersed in thoughts of the past. Coop's getting older. April's mysteriously vanished. Fizzbin doesn't have time to hang out and grab a beer. If this is what it means to "get older, mature, and move on?" You can have it. Oblivion is waiting.
