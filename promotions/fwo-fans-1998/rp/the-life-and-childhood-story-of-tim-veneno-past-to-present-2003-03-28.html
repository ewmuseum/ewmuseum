The life and childhood Story of Tim Veneno: Past to Present.

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a newly founded wrestling promotion as Reed Young headed the helm of the promotion. Tim wasn't so sure if it would be the best idea to go into Action seeing how Reed had a bad track record in the past. But, Tim decided to give it a try, as it couldn't hurt him. Tim sent in his resume to the offices (his house) of Reed Young. Weeks went by before Tim heard back from Reed, as Reed sent him back a letter, it wasn't the nicest letter, but it still said "Welcome to Action! Wrestling" at the end of it. Tim's stay in Action Wrestling wasn't the greatest as he finished off there with a not so impressive record of 1 win and 9 losses. Tim had felt that the staff at Action didn't like Tim much and because of that they embarrassed the Rahl character. That was not true though as his stay in the next promotion would just show that Tim still needed to work on his character before he would make it anywhere. Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling was Tim's next stop in the world of wrestling. During Tim's short lived stay in PIW it would prove to Tim that it wasn't the Action staff that hated Tim, it was the fans that hated Tim. The fans barely even cared about Tim and his Areumna'derin Rahl character. Tim figured this out when he was arriving to the arena where he would fight in his last match in PIW, a fan wondered up to Tim and asked for his autograph. Tim was shocked and surprised that he had a fan, but whatever Tim thought to himself. He grabbed the kid's piece of paper and the sharpie and released a smile to the kid. Tim looked at the piece of paper, as it was a picture, a picture of PIW superstar Sylo. Tim didn't think anything of it as maybe he thought that the kid was going to have all PIW superstars sign it. So Tim signed the photograph and handed it back to the kid. The kid looked down at the autograph and then back at Rahl. "Areumna'derin Rahl?" The kid questioned Tim. "Who's that?" The words spilled out at Tim, each letter murdering Tim's soul. Tim couldn't believe it; the kid thought he was someone else in PIW. Tim went off to wrestle in his last match ever in PIW of course losing it. Veneno backed his bags and left the PIW arena a loser. Little did Tim know that his luck was about to change.
