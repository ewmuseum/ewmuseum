Burlesque Bachelorette

8 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Crank your volume and mosh in your chairs, bitches. This is for our fun. "Ames...no, NO. Damnit, woman - you worry too much! Okay. Alright. Yes. I'm hanging up now." Beep! Renee tossed her cell against the couch. That was the fifth call today to make sure that her best friend's bachelorette party would be cleancut and not involve any 'major partying'. "Man," smiled the blonde as she turned to the rest of the room that consisted of Aimz' second oldest sister and a classically tall, dark and handsome childhood friend of theirs. "She believes we're gonna plan one of those cute little black tie things, but why would she call upon us for this if that's what she really wanted?" Of course, wanting that sort of shindig and then leaving the planning up to her two sisters and best friend wasn't exactly the brightest plan of action on Amy's part so, in their minds, it was her own fault if she didn't like the results. They all laughed aloud. "Ryan," Renee pointed, "You've got music lined up, yes?" "Guaranteed. None of that new age opera shit she's trying now, either." "Paula - drinks?" The brunette - not surprisingly, as her sister, close enough in looks to be Amy's twin - gave a thumbs-up gesture. "Open bar." "Excellent...quite excellent." Only they knew what was to come for the nervous bride... Later that night... Beer two "This isn't at all what I wanted you to do." Amy, drink in hand, leaned against the bar and wagged a finger at her best friend. She feared tonight getting her into too much trouble, because that's what usually followed closely behind alcohol with the little redhead and her friends...so she'd asked - nay, begged Renee to keep things tame. Of course, in favor of 'old times' her request had been ignored and Aimz found herself being dragged out to the VIP lounge of a club they'd gone to religiously since before they legally could. Then again, what's the worst that could happen? Famous last words, as Brandon once said...but very little she could do would rival his big party with everything from transvestites to handcuffs. When Renee saw the little redhead crack a smile and shake her head, she recognized the train of thought. "After the night he had, there's no way you'll get in shit for anything we do. 'Sides, I'm not gonna let you touch anything that normally gives you a hangover." "Yeah," mumbled Amy, "And don't let me get too far gone. Really not feeling like fucking something up tonight." Beer four... "Make sure I can get home by midnight, 'kay?" Five and a shot. "I'll be fine." ...or two. "Y'know the guy I'm marrying? Yeah, he's fun." ...or three. "If I didn't know him, I'd probably be marryin' your cat." ...or four. "Seriously. I mean, for a cat...that's a damn sexy cat." When's too much? "...So well-groomed 'n...well...got those big green ol' eyes. Cute cat." Post-burlesque show... "Y'know, guys..." Amy started, putting arms around Renee on one side, her longtime friend Ryan on the other. "That's real nice of y'all. Girl strrrippers...can't get 'nta any trouble with girls..." Friends from throughout Amy Campbell's life surrounded the table, and she'd had some slurred form of conversation with each one over the past hour. Usually, that consisted of "Didn't I live with you once?" or "Yeah, you're that guy from that band...The Bobs 'r somethin'.", but she remained somewhat (however barely) coherent. Alexander Keith's beer had started the evening, while a barrage of other rums, whiskey and vodka had quickly turned it to progress while most of the guests enjoyed a hired Suicide Girls burlesque show where most of the women in the 'audience', Amy included, were too far into a drunken stupor to really care that they were watching members of the same sex - they all enjoyed it just the same. "Except those times..." Jenna Gartner, a random friend of many years popped in with a chuckle. "Y'told Brand about that yet?" Amy's smile was dopey - it matched the lazy haze in her eyes. "Nuhh...was drunk. No point, he'd o..only like i'too much anyway. 'N I only ever went neear girls when we were'll drunk before..." With that laughably poor response she went back to using her pierced tongue to toy with her lip ring, crosseyed in her attempts to watch it. Her maid of honour looked around at the bottles on and surrounding the pushed-together tables. The majority had been empited by the bride herself... She'd keep away from jail...hopefully. But juging by the conversation compliments of those empty bottles, Aimz would have no secrets and one hell of a stomach ache the next morning. Aimz was being sent off into marriage with rum and her favorite hardcore anthems... Sound "typical" of a punkish 21 year old? Maybe, sure. But do you see any transvestites? Huh? HUH?! That's right, Mister Smarty Pants. [This has been a rushed Katie/Katie collaboration. Thanks to Avenged Sevenfold for the song I've broken so many bones throwing down to.]
