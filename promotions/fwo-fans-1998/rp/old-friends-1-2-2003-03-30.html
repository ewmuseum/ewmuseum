Old Friends (1/2)

30 March 2003

Wednesday, March 26, 2003 Portland, OR 7:38 PM Scott Slugger opened the door of Tony's Place and stepped in. He wasn't very comfortable - he could see immediately that it wasn't his kind of crowd, and besides, his suit itched - but he stepped forward anyway, joining a small line. About a minute later he reached the maitre d', who seemed to be the apothesis of all maitre d's. Thin, slick-backed hair, a thin, somehow snooty mustache. Probably French. It figured. If there was one thing that plagued his life, that he could never escape, it was Frenchmen. The maitre d' looked down at him and asked, "Name please?" "Wasner, but I don't have a reservation." "Well, I'm sorry, but we only require that our patrons make reservations ahead of time - " "I'm here to see the owner, actually. Tony?" The maitre d' laughed, a hoh-hoh-hoh kind of laugh that confirmed Slugger's worst suspicions. "You are not serious, right? Please, just leave us and... I don't know, go down to the Burgerville up the road or something." "First, I like Burgerville, and second, I am serious. Tell him Scott's here." The maitre d' cocked an eye at him. Slugger pulled out his wallet and peeled off a hundred. He placed it carefully in the man's shirt pocket. "OK?" The maitre d', still clearly annoyed, sighed and walked to the back. Slugger stood there, absently shifting on his feet. A minute later, the maitre d' returned with a man in tow. The man was huge - he was clearly the biggest man in the restaurant, and by far at that. Close-cropped blond hair stood out in the mood lighting. A Brooks Brothers suit covered the rippling muscles. A hand rubbed the blond goatee. The maitre d' looked from the man to Slugger, Slugger to the man, and the man to Slugger again. He shook his head, then turned to help the next people in line, who had been waiting patiently. Slugger extended a hand. "Tony." The man looked hard at Slugger for a moment, then took the hand and pumped it twice. Slugger winced inwardly. He still shook like he used to. Ice gestured for Slugger to follow him.
