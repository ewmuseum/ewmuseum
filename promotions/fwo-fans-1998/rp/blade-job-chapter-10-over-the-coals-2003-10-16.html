Blade Job - Chapter 10 -Over the Coals

16 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Detective Cole Somers saw Ben Jacobson roll his eyes in exasperation as his partner yet again asked the same question he had asked ten times before. "Where were you the night of-" "I told you were I was... I TOLD you I got a call from Kevin that night, but my answering machine took it. He left a message, and I never returned it. That's all. I haven't seen the guy in five years!" "Yeah, you said that," Somers cut in gruffly. "But we're askin' you where you were that night. Why didn't you answer your phone that night, Ben? Where were you?" "I...," Ben stopped short, suddenly trying to recall that night two weeks ago. "I... don't remember." "Y'See Ben," Doyle cut in from his place across the deserted IPW locker room. They had been through this routine thousands of times, him and Somers. He knew his cues like a classically trained actor. "Y'see, that's an answer that we like. Saying you don't remember the details sends a big red flag up in my mind... in my partner's mind too. Makes us think that you might be hiding something." "That's bullshit," Ben exclaimed as he busied himself by stripping the worn wrestling tape from his wrists. This was fucking surreal, man. Was he really in a wrestling ring only a half hour ago and now he's getting asked about murder? "I barely knew Kevin MacKay. I tagged with him a few times years ago, and that's it. I don't know why he was calling me, and I don't know why I didn't answer the phone." "Well, here's the thing-" Somers started. "Wait a second," Ben said as he pulled baggy grey sweats over his trademark leopard-print tights. He was going to shower, but the hell with that. All he wanted to do right now is get out of here and away from these prying, cops "That happened on a Monday night, didn't it? Monday is Ruahh!" "What the fuck is a Ruahh?" "The fWo Wrestling show, on Monday nights." Ben stated emphatically as he grabbed his gym bag and stood up. "I was at the arena, as I always am on Monday nights. That's why I didn't answer the phone! That's why you can stop these fucking questions right now. If I'm not under arrest, I'm leaving." "Fine, Ben," said Somers as he chewed on the cap of his standard-issue ballpoint pen. "But don't leave town. We aren't through with you... not even close." "Whatever." Without saying anything further, Ben walked out. "He's hiding something, man." Doyle said from his perch on the locker room bench. "I feel it in my bones." "He's a nut." Somers agreed. "A freakin' whackjob. I don't know whether to arrest him or commit him. But here's the thing. If his alibi checks out, if he was at that fWo rasslin' show on the night of the murder, we're back at square one." Doyle stood up, sending off twin firecrackers as his knees popped with the strain. Sometimes he wondered why he did this... wondered why he trailed these freaks around this cesspool of a city. Five years till pension. He thought to himself, not for the first time that day. "Well, let's go talk to the fWo, then."
