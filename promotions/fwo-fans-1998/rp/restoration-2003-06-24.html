Restoration

24 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Padrona's absence from the fWo as of late proves only one thing. He is willing to leave it behind and come back. After his glorious and respectable victory at Cyberslam against Deacon, one of the worlds most celebrated athletes, Padrona seemed to lose faze with the world, losing and winning minimum matches after that Pay-Per-View match. Something got to Padrona after that ladder match, and maybe it was the show of respect from Deacon, or the fact that Padrona won on a stage such as Cyberslam, but beyond that, something had changed the mind of the man known to the world as the Thai-Fighter. For years he grew up wishing to be the very best Thailand ever had to offer, and now where is he? On the bench, wondering to himself if he has enough to come back. The question could be asked millions of times. "Why did you leave Thailand" Simply put, it wasn't his choice. Either he moved to America in search of new challenges or retire in his prime. But even the challenges from such as Griff Truxell, Eli Flair and Deacon were not enough to pull Padrona to the area he wanted to be in. Something was missing that was keeping him back. The challenges were there, the wrestlers were waiting, but there was only one thing holding the Thai-Fighter back. Honor There was no honor in sitting out, and there was no honor in being an average Joe working for pay and going home for the night. People will always say that professional wrestlers live two lives. Their character and their self-image. I don't think those people have met Padrona. Someone forgot to tell them, this is all about his own self-image. There wasn't a difference between Padrona in the ring and Padrona out of the ring. Unlike other wrestlers who go home at night, Padrona would stay focused. Maybe he still doesn't know that this all isn't real, or maybe he just doesn't care. But as far as the Disney officials are concerned he better learn fast before he hurts someone. Being respected by your peers is one thing. Being respected by your fans is another. But how many men and women can honestly say, "I respect myself!" Padrona can't. Not anymore, not after leaving Thailand and walking out on the fWo. He owed them something. A new Padrona, a new face, a new style. Maybe people will soon know why strives for perfection and lives for honor. Maybe soon people will realize that he aims to be respected and aims to be seen as a great athlete and not a performer. fWo...Ruahh~!...Uprising...Unleashed...WRESTLING...ENTERTAINMENT Time draws near, and as the second tick away and the hands on the clock slowly creep forward, always know one thing. Muay Thai will be restored
