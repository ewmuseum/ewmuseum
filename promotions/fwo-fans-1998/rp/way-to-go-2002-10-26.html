Way to go.

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The phone receiver seemed to weigh a ton. Brandon sat back on the stiff hotel mattress and looked around the room. This was the fifth time in as many minutes that he had attempted to call home. Every time he lifted the phone up to place the call he could hear Ana and her reaction to the events on the last few fWo Slaughters. He wanted to let them know he was Ok but at the same time really didn't want to hear her go on and on about what happened. That was bad of him he knew. She was only trying to watch out for her husband. There were plenty of people in the world that would trade places with him in a heartbeat to have someone care for them that way. Right now he might actually take them up on that offer too. A shake of the head clears those thoughts though and once more he lifts the receiver up to his ear. A few numbers are tapped on the gray box and the dial tone changes to a series of beeps. It would only be a few seconds now, and with that he sighed a heavy sigh. A click was heard on the other end and Brandon thought for a second that the call had been disconnected. He was almost relieved. Then he heard a small breath on the other end. He knew who it was instinctively. "Jarod?" "Hi, Da-Da... you go bye bye?" "Hey buddy!" Wow, it was little things like that that made him realize all he was giving up for this sport, all he was giving up for this life. Little words and phrases would set him off and he would miss his family like mad. "Hi, Da-Da... Seah bed...he bed." "What did you say Jarod? Sean's asleep? Is he taking a nap baby? Where's Mommy?" "Seah bed... Mom-ma bed." Brandon laughed to himself for a second. Wow, how time flies. He berated himself for missing so much of his son's young life. Look at all he had lost, and look at how little he had gained. Look at how much he had to lose. Suddenly the voice on the other end changed and it shook Brandon. His smile faded though he didn't realize it. "Brandon?" "Yeah babe. Where's Sean, Jarod said he was in bed?" "If you were here instead of on the road again you would know that he has been sick for three days now. He had a high temperature and fell asleep in your chair." Here it goes. He knew it wouldn't be long before she was on him about everything, about all of the decisions he had been making lately. "Well... you could have simply called and told me you know? I've been busy...but, I guess you already knew that... didn't you?" Here it comes. "Oh you mean busy risking your life for some stupid code of honor? Or do you mean busy being some psychos pin cushion? Or do you mean playing lapdog to all those fans and the other wrestlers? Which one, which one occupied your time so much YOU couldn't have called and checked on us without us having to be the ones to give you little updates?" "Calm down Ana... I'm sorry. I get in late most nights and I was afraid to catch you in bed, that's all. You know the story babe. I've been doing this for five years... you know what it takes when I am on tour. I can't always get back home. They have the signings and the appearances and..." "...and Sean waited for you to call him after the show Sunday like you always do and you were no where to be found." "Didn't you see what happened Sunday Ana!? I went to the hospital after the show. I feel like a cross stitch I have so much thread all over the place. I didn't get out of there till almost one in the mor..." "So the next day your fingers was too sore to call then huh?" "No they..." "Don't even bother answering Brandon. Don't waste your breath. It's always something. A store appearance or some Make a Wish Foundation event or something for the Frontier Foundation... always something. I guess you aren't coming home this weekend either?" "I'm scheduled to appear in some capacity on the show Ana. You know I have a set number of days I have to be on television. If not then I breach the contract." "Would that be so bad? The only reason you wanted into the fWo in the first place was to get your hands on Creed. I tried and tried to get you to stop this stupid vendetta and you never would. Now what? Creed is six feet under, not far enough for my tastes but still, that's where he's at and you still can't let all this go. Just let it go." Where did this sudden change in heart come from? Ana was his greatest supporter but suddenly she was more the lead detractor. Nothing he did was right, not even for the family. He didn't even understand why he cared anymore, and that hurt him a lot. God he missed Sean and Jarod. "I'm just wrestling Ana, some nut decided to jump me. It wasn't my fault. I wasn't on some holy mission or telling someone to eat their wheaties and drink their orange juice or anything. I was minding my own business when he decided to come after me! How is that my fault?" "Now you're playing victim? You know how this business is Brandon, someone is always trying to put themselves over and for some reason you seem to have a big bullseye on your back..." "...actually... it's a cross..." "Oh, funny. Now you want to make jokes about this?" Great. Like he needed her to be madder at him. He just had to go and be sarcastic. He may be a good wrestler but sometimes he says the stupidest things. "You can't even make it home for Easter and you expect your flippant remarks to just make this all better. Well, it can't. This is Jarod's first real Easter. You missed it all and for what? So you can parade around in your spandex and play superhero!" "Lower your voice Ana, the kids don't need to hear how upset you are..." "What? You don't want them to hear me yelling at you? Why, are you afraid they might understand that their Daddy chose wrestling over them when he Did have a choice? Fine. Hold on a sec' let me make sure they don't hear how selfish Daddy is..." Brandon sits there for a second fully expecting to hear the bedroom door slam close in the background. ::Click:: Click? "Ana?" Silence. "ANA!?" Dial tone. Way to go hero...
