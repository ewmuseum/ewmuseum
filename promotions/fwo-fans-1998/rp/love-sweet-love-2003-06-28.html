Love Sweet Love

28 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Have you ever wondered if it was possible for you to find the perfect some one? To glance across a crowded room, lock eyes, and fall in love? I've always wondered if this phenomena "Love at First Sight" was ever true. I had been told so many negative things about it, but when I glanced across the room and my eyes met her icy blue eyes. I knew, she was the girl for me. She was beautiful, yes which is usually the norm for a party like this, but there was something else about her, it was almost like she felt the same way. She could see so deep into my soul that it was almost repulsive; she could see my want I knew. I approached her; I didn't know how this was going to go. -------------------Brandon's Thoughts----------------- It was a nice party in a suburb of Denver, it wasn't the usual place for Brandon to hang out but he'd been told by a few of his ring crew buddies about this man who always threw parties in Denver, they said all the ritzy people show up, and then it just turns into a good old party. Well the ritzy people were showing up, and it wasn't looking very partyish. Brandon had begun his approach towards a brunette with stunning curves; she was gorgeous in every form of the word. She had these ice blue eyes, eyes that could entrance a man, and they had. She had just finished a dance with a man who was possibly double her age. Brandon didn't care who he was, he had to have her. Brandon took the final drink of his coke and rum and placed it on a table as he continued his approach. Finally he stopped and tapped her on the shoulder. "Miss, can I ask you for the next dance?" Brandon asked full of confidence, the women looked at him harshly for a moment until Brandon kissed her hand causing her to crack a warm smile. "I'd be thrilled," she stated wrapping her arms around Brandon. The two began to chitchat. "So, what's your name?" Brandon asked, striking the same smile he flashed to his fans every Monday and Thursday on television. "My name?" She asked before continuing "Darlene Rowe, I'm in town on vacation," she looked around the room and pointed to a man in a long tailed white tuxedo. "Mr. Walters, do you know him?" she asked as Rush shook his head in an obvious no. "Oh well he invited me to this splendid party," she paused twirling her hair around her finger for a moment. "What's your name?" she asked, as Brandon smiled. "Brandon Thompson," He stated as Darlene looked at him and smiled, "And where are you from" Darlene asked. "L.A.," Brandon said grinning, he was the California boy through and through. "Why are you in Denver?" she asked, Brandon looked at her, upper class people usually think lowly of wrestling. "I wrestle with the Fans Wrestling Organization." Brandon stated looking at her a bit sheepishly. "Really? I'm a huge fan! Now I recognize you, I didn't know Rush could clean up so well." Darlene stated giving a sarcastic giggle. Brandon smiled the same smile; he looked down at her and gave her a light kiss on the cheek as the dance ended. Brandon had found a girl, one that had enough money not to be after his own. One that could possibly love him. The way things would go now, all depends on the way everything else crumbles. Could a long distance relationship last? People say they never do. Maybe, just maybe this one could.
