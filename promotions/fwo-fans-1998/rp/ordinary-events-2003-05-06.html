Ordinary events

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Spyder walked over to the small refrigerated cooler that he kept with him at all times. A Coleman, it did the job with style and ability, melding form and function together in order to provide an all-inclusive solution that was also pleasing to the eye. He sat down. The bed creaked softly under him. He scratched the side of his head. It was itchy, and the leather of the mask was starting to form a small rash. Grunting, he flipped open the computer he carried with him at all times and hit the power switch. It didn't come on right away, and Spyder sighed as he plugged it into the wall to recharge. He waited. The processor was a few years old. He needed to buy a new one... but when would he find time? Seems like there was always something going on. Never enough time to take care of the little things. The heater came on in the hotel room. It was already plenty hot, so Spyder got to his feet, walked to the wall, and located the temperature knob. He turned it. The heater went off. He walked back down and observed that the computer was still loading up. Windows ME. It was slow and he needed to upgrade, but needed a new computer first. He opened the cooler. The ice inside had melted somewhat, and when he pulled out the carton of milk it had some water pooled around the base. The drips fell on his leg. He opened it and drank, then spat the milk out just as quickly. Grimacing, he turned the carton and discovered that the expiration date was labeled as April 25th. The milk had gone bad. He tossed the carton into the garbage and then thought better of it. Spoiled milk would stink up the whole room. He opened the hotel room window and tossed the carton out. He heard it thud and splash a few moments later, followed by a scream. Spyder ignored this and went back to the computer. He logged onto a wrestling message board and signed himself onto AIM using a screen name that would allow people to think he was actually a fourteen year old girl. Nothing was more fun than trolling the net geeks into thinking they were getting in on with a young girl instead of a sexy man among men, wrestler among wrestlers. He also logged onto the Bank Of America website and selected for several bills to be paid using Online Bill Pay. He glanced at the wrestling message board. He clicked on a subject line that read "Day to Day Problems", then thought better of wasting his time and went to search for porn. Not finding any free porn after about twenty minutes of looking, he gave up and picked up the remote for the hotel TV. Nothing happened. Sighing, he put the remote down and figured that the batteries must be dead. A few hours later he fell asleep while the TV blared in the background. Conan O Brian. A rerun.
