Indiana Speck and the Temple of Fire: Flight of the Fire Keese

31 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi. Welcome back to the Codemaster and his crazy misadventures with his mishappen allies with their crazy quirks. I know you were excited when his last session of this random run of idiocy was suddenly replaced by sessions that were far more useful than the Codemaster ever would be, but now would be the time to cry. We have returned to the Fire Temple, within Death Mountain, and boy, did nothing happen between the time when we last left the Codemaster, the other Codebreakers, and Link, or what? Frankly, I'm amazed you're even looking at this. Don't you have an Aimz session to attend to? Or maybe Brand Frontier? Why the HELL are you still reading this? Sigh. I guess you're still here. That means I have to go through the tired process of explaining what this place is like. Well, after one drops down the LONG ladder that goes like three stories down, or, in the case of Ken War, an additional five feet thanks to the crater you just left from simply falling down that shaft and landing as hard and as violently as possible in the effort to die, you'd be in the lobby. The lobby of this place was a cave-like room with a large staircase that went up to a platform. There were four different passages in this place, two of them off of doors next to the sides of the staircase, and two more on the top of the staircase, to the left and the right from the stairs. There were two giant torches at the top of the stairs with bats randomly flying into the torches and setting themselves on fire before trying to fly into hapless pray and set them on fire. One such flaming bat tried to fly into the Codemaster, but he suddenly turned to it. "Hey. You. You come anywhere near me and I'm gonna have to get medieval on your ass." the Codemaster said. The flaming bat suddenly stopped and considered its options. It could deal with the Codemaster, who had his Tome of Eternal Darkness book open and was preparing to cast one of its more horrible spells. Possibly Magick Attack, which would utterly obliterated the tiny bat with a single zap. Or maybe Bind, so that the bat would go around attacking other bats. "But that's my job." Link said, before he splattered the bat into tiny little bat chunks with his sword. The other fire bats, seeing this, quickly retreated somewhere else in order to not get splattered into tiny little bat chunks like their friend. "You are an especially violent little man." the Codemaster said to the shorter Link, who quietly cleaned the bat chunks from his sword with a cloth. A very, very bloody cloth. Once Link put it away, he simply shrugged at Codemaster and they pressed on. They climbed up the stairs and exited to the right room. We're still in the lobby, and one fire bat turned to the other. "They killed Lenny!" it said. "What? Not Lenny! He was a good natured Fire Keese! Wasn't he at the Annual Fire Keese Charity Olympics?" the other fire bat asked, stunned at the prospect of Lenny being dead. "This is an outrage! Even the Gorons didn't do this to we Fire Keese! We must destroy those things and possibly dance on their corpses!" the first fire bat said. "We don't dance, Murray. We just kind of flutter about." "Shut up. Get the rest of the society together, we're gonna teach these bastards a lesson." the first fire bat said, flying away. The second one shrugged with its firey wings and followed the first one. Back to the Codemaster, who was in a chamber with a bridge over troubled lava, and lots of platforms floating in the lava. He had definitely heard the voices but couldn't make out what they said, "Hey, did you brothers hear something?" "No." Link said, as he shoved his sword through one of the firey skulls that fly out from the cracks, killing it. He proceeded to pull the sword out and kick the flaming skull off the bridge where it fell into the lava with a splash. "neu!!11" Ken War answered. "I only heard something about how we killed a Fire Keese named Lenny and now they're going to sabotage our sacred quest to retrieve the Megaton Hammer." Mike Heftel said. "Oh." There was a pause. "Wait, what?" And that was when the entire room was suddenly filling up with Fire Keese, who were no doubt pissed off at the fact that their mate, Lenny, was splattered into tiny little bat chunks by Link. The Codemaster looked at them, before he glared at Link, "See, I knew you were gonna get us in trouble." With that, they ran away, with the Fire Keese all chasing after them. They jumped over a break in the bridge(well, Ken War didn't), and entered the next room, which was thankfully Fire Keese free. They all looked at the next room with horror. There was a giant, tall cage tower that they were meant to climb, so that they could push a large stone block over a hole that shot out fire, which would shoot the block upwards into the next room. Mike Heftel whistled, "I already dislike this place." "Shut up." the Codemaster said. There was a knock on the door. "hay!!11 r u gune lte mi en??/" Mike Heftel let him in. He quickly shut the door before the Fire Keese could get in, squishing one in the process. A voice on the other said suddenly screamed in horror. "OH MY GOD! THEY KILLED KENNY!" "You bastards!" the others chimed in. Codemaster sighed, "Man, has that joke been beaten into the ground or what?" And then a sandbag fell on Ken War, killing him again. "...Aw, hell."
