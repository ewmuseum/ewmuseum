Superman...

6 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Over the past month, the Soldiers of the Fifth Seal did quite a number on Milo’s right knee, and the effects of the injury were slowly catching up on him. On camera, Milo looked as if he had a supernatural force fueling, giving him immunity to the injury… but behind the scenes, a very human side of Milo was beginning to surface. December 5, 2004. 11:37PM “You REALLY need to slow down Milo…” Max said, observing from the other side of the shadow filled hotel room. Milo only replied with a grunt, and began to grit his teeth as he struggled to plant his foot on the floor. His inflamed knee periscoped his shorts, exposing what appeared to be some discoloration. After a moment of struggle, Milo slumped back onto the bed with a sigh. “Milo… you should probably take Monday night off.” Max said with sincerity, truly worried about the career of his best friend. Milo laid his head onto the mattress, and closed his eyes. “I can’t Max… Twilight is coming up.” Max stood up, violently slamming the chair he had be seated on against a neighboring table. “Are you insane?! You’re throwing your career away Milo! Fuck Twilight…” Milo quickly interjected. “Fuck Twilight? It’s one of the biggest Pay-Per Views of the year Max.” “Yeah, and at this rate it could be your last. Look at your knee Milo… it’s swelled out from the other night. You can’t keep taking shots to your knee… you’re not superman you know.” Silence momentarily filled the room. “Okay… you’r right Max. No action for me Monday night.” Milo replied, his eyes remaining shut. Max cracked a smile. “Good man Milo. You can rent some movies or something while I’m at the show.” Milo’s eyes opened abruptly, and he quickly sat up. “You’ve gotta be fuckin’ joking… Look Max, I said I wouldn’t wrestle, I didn’t say a damn thing about sitting here in this crappy hotel room during the card. I’m going to be at Ruahh~!.” Milo’s manager looked on in disgust, truly annoyed by the stubborn nature of his friend. “Why though?” “Max, I know it’s dangerous but if I stay here… it’s like I let the S5S win. Sore knee or not, I can hold my own… I’ll be fine.” Max released the air from his lungs in a loud sigh. “Okay, well… atleast put some ice on you’re knee. You look like you’re part ‘Grimace’”. Milo would be at Ruahh~!, but was he making a big mistake? Only time would tell…
