Redemption.

30 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sound of water dripping was audible from another room. The view of the camera shifted from a wall to Chris Universal sitting on a chair with a towel covering the lower portion of his body; with his upper body in a slumped position and his hands covering his head. His long hair covered his face making it unable to view what type of expression was on his face, but his sigh was conclusive evidence the expression wasn't a good one. He removed his head from his hands and looked at the camera in disbelief. Not knowing what to think or what to say, or how to feel or what to do, he struggled to put together some words in the form of a sentence and began to speak in a quiet, "worried" tone. "I... can't believe it. Let me... let me start from the beginning. Wednesday night. Twenty four hours ago. The moment everything began to change... for the worse. The man who built this promotion from behind the scenes returned to "reclaim" what was his. To restore order to the promotion that was headed towards hell at light speed. At what cost? At what price? All along I've told you all that everyone has a price, and ANYONE can be bought. Some people believed me, some people ignored me. But both of those types of people are now realizing that I was not blowing smoke out of my ass, I was telling you the truth. The truth hurts. We all want the truth in the end, but I'm not sure if any of us can handle it. The truth is, Travis Beaven "sold out". Not for money like most of us do; including me. Not for respect. For personal gain. Nobody stepped up to the plate to avenge what happened to you, Travis? Are you fucking blind? We all did. We all want Spencer gone from this promotion... at any cost. All of us. And you aligned yourself with him... for what? You thought you could control him by yourself... history has proven that it is not a individual effort, it's a team effort. You could've came to us... but instead... you... made a deal with the devil. We all thought we knew you. You were always easygoing... always wanting to help out... always wanting to make this promotion succeed, just like the rest of us. But you changed... and just like everything else has in the last twenty-four hours, you changed for the worse. The truth hurts. Pain, at times, can be closer to the heart than the things we cherish most. The end result is that only the strongest will survive. Tomorrow night, we will find out who will survive, and who won't. Tonight... Ryan Moore, didn't. Tonight... Ryan Moore, lost his life as a result of an accident that could happen anywhere. Everyone wanted to blame "ratings" as the reason for Owen Hart's death... but really, how logical is that? The wwf wasn't desperate for ratings, and I don't think they'd have one of their workers repel down from the ceiling from a cord for a ratings point. That's another story altogether. Accidents do happen. Tonight... an accident happened, just like it happened a month ago to Lance Star. Only this time... it was an accident that one can never recover from. This accident is permanent. Tonight... a man who loved bringing happyness to the fWo, a man who had no unmoral fiber in his body, a man who cared about everyone... died. While Ryan and I were never close behind the scenes, I would never wish something like this about him. He was a good guy, and well liked. He was there for everyone. Just like Travis. Ryan never changed. Travis did. For the worse. It's something he'll have to live with the rest of his life. Can he be forgiven? Certainly. Will his mistake be forgotten? No... just like Ryan's death is permanent, so is Travis' mistake. We will never forget it. Ever. God bless you, Ryan." Universal head slumped back into his hands once again. He stood up and walked out of the room, and moments later returned wearing some boxer shorts. The dripping of the water was no longer audible as Universal sat back down. This time he looked into the camera with confidence as several tears streamed down his right cheek. "Tomorrow night... the dynasty finally falls apart just as some had predicted. As corny as this may sound, I dedicate the match to him. None of us tomorrow night will be thinking about the match, however. I won't, at least. I'll be thinking about Ryan... and how much life he brought to the fWo. How much "joy" he brought into this organization. I suspect we all will. But Travis... you'll be thinking about the choices you've made, and what you can do to redeem yourself. What you can do to make us forgive you. Tonight was a bad night in more ways than one. It didn't turn out the way you wanted it to, did it, Travis?" Universal stood up and stared into the camera with a hint of vengeance, before finishing. "Do the right thing, Travis. Make the right choice. Redeem yourself. For us. For Ryan." Universal walked out of the room as we faded to black.
