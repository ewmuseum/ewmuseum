The One In South California - Part Six

7 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pages 85 to 101 of The One In South California. *** *** *** *** *** *** INT. HONDA CIVIC Dean, again, drives while smoking, Cassidy in the passenger's seat. CASSIDY You remember Christian Brady? DEAN No. CASSIDY Yeah, you do. I dated him when I was like 15? DEAN I didn't pay attention to who you were dating when you were fifteen. CASSIDY Yeah, I know. You were busy fucking my sister at that point. DEAN Roxy? CASSIDY No, I was dating Jamal Goldman then. This was when you were with Katy. DEAN You dated a guy named Jamal? CASSIDY Yeah. He wasn't black, though. DEAN I don't give a shit what color he was, that's fucking funny. CASSIDY That his name was Jamal? DEAN That you dated a guy named Jamal. Little Irish Cassidy Brennan, student of the esteemed Brentwood School. How'd your dad feel when he found out you were going to the movies with a guy named Jamal Goldman? There's a 50/50 chance: he's either a Jew or black. CASSIDY Shut up. I only went to Brentwood for two years, asshole. Seventh and eighth grade. Ugh, I'm not Brentwood, I'm a Uni girl. So in art, she put paint all over my chair and told everyone I had my period. DEAN Yeah? CASSIDY So when I DID have my period, I put my tampon in her lunch. DEAN You pulled it out in front of her and put it in her food? CASSIDY No, no, no. She had bagged lunch. We were in the bathroom at the same time and my tampon happened to dance its way into the bag with her tuna sandwich. DEAN Fairly appropriate. Seriously, that's maybe the most disgusting thing I think I've ever heard in my life. Let's get back to the friendship between your dad and Jamal before I pass out. CASSIDY Anyway, after everything Penny and Rosie put my dad through, I could have gotten gangbanged by a tribe of African Jews and he wouldn't have even noticed. DEAN Yup. Could've. CASSIDY Anyway, back to the point. Christian. You broke his nose at the Beverly Center a few years ago. He went to Fairfax. DEAN That doesn't really narrow it down much, dollface. CASSIDY Well, whatever. I saw him the other day. He's in some shitty band that got signed and did some ridiculous tour and stuff. Huge junkie. Huge. His whole band's fucked up. DEAN Okay. I'm gonna be over here driving. When you figure out what the point of this story is, just give me a little tap. CASSIDY No, no. I'm saying he just got a boatload of cash. He's got new money, he's got a bad addiction, and he's scared as fuck of you. So we have a big-time client for whatever we heist today. Holy shit, this is a heist, isn't it? DEAN Y'know, Cass, if I could take all of those weekly moments of brilliance and gather them together, I would put them in a little bottle and feed a little bit to you each morning. CASSIDY What do you mean? DEAN I mean, if you spent your time thinking about constructive things instead of thinking about getting fucked all the time, we could do whatever we wanted. CASSIDY But we do do whatever we want. DEAN That's two logical thoughts consecutively. I can smell the nymphomania coming. CASSIDY Fuck you, Dean. You can't mention me getting fucked and expect some excitement not to happen. That's fixing the fight. DEAN Good. Put it toward the search and seizure. Dean parks the car at the station and pops the door open, getting out and sitting on the hood of the car to wait for the other two to arrive. FADE TO: EXT. SIERRA MADRE VILLA STATION Nuñez steps off the train and Lowell follows. LOWELL No, really, can I have the good backpack for a little while? I'm badass too. I've done time. Without a word, Nuñez looks at him, drops his backpack off his shoulders, and hands it to Lowell, who eagerly tries to grab it from him, only to immediately nearly drop it from the weight. LOWELL (CONT'D) Bustin' caps, muhfucka. Bustin' caps. NUÑEZ Oh, just gimme 'at shit. Nuñez grabs the bag back from him and throws it on, walking into the parking garage, with Lowell trying to catch up. DEAN Well holy shit. CASSIDY What? What, what's going on? Dean hops off the hood of the car and slowly walks toward the two of them. DEAN You didn't fucking tell me about him. You didn't fucking tell me he was coming along with you. (Looking at Nuñez) You have got to be goddamn kidding me. Jason Nuñez. NUÑEZ My nigga! The two embrace for a second. DEAN What are the odds? NUÑEZ This is some shit, man. This is some shit. LOWELL Heyyy, cool, we're all homies, we're all down. What's up Dean-o?! Long time no see, my man! DEAN Yeah? If I'd'a known Nuñez was in on this, I would've been down from beat one. They walk over to the car and Cassidy gets up to hug Lowell and then, after looking at him, steps back. CASSIDY What the fuck do you have all over you? LOWELL I'M SORRY THERE WAS NO DRY CLEANER FOR MY DUMPSTER-BOUGHT CLOTHES! FADE TO: INT. HONDA CIVIC Dean and Cassidy sit in the front, Nuñez and Lowell in back. DEAN Alright Low, where are we going? LOWELL Joey Dynamo's house. 34 Davidson Court. It's past an In-N-Out. And a gas station or something. DEAN Joey Dynamo? Wanna explain this one? LOWELL Yeah, you know, the pornstar. DEAN No, I don't know. Jay, you know? NUÑEZ No fuckin' idea, kid. LOWELL Oh man, you should see him pound these chicks. BAMbamBAMbamBAM BAM BAM! He's got this tattoo of a flaming black skull on his leg and this huge uncut dick. CASSIDY Ewwwwwww! Uncut? What the fuck? People don't get circumsized these days? Where is he from, like, the jungles of Africa? (To Nuñez) Y'know, no offense or anything. DEAN Only Lowell could make this one "Ew" a penis. CASSIDY Dick. DEAN Exactly. LOWELL It's like 20 inches, too. It's fucking crazy, he probably rolls it up when he walks around just to show he can. DEAN To show whom? NUÑEZ You've got a serious fixation on my man's dick, nigga. Your ass is stayin' in the car for sure now. LOWELL You guys have really never seen an uncut one, though? CASSIDY I'm pretty sure it would send my spinner wheel straight to "dyke" if I did. LOWELL Oh, c'mon... It's not that weird, right guys? Like... Right guys? NUÑEZ I'unno, man. I seen a couple niggas uncut back in the football locker rooms at the high school, man. 'At's some pretty unholy shit, Low. DEAN Anyway, the plan: scout the building. If there's multiple people, then Nuñez at the front door, Cass and I take sides or back. If he's alone, Cassidy runs interference, we hop in from there. Questions? LOWELL Yeah, what do I do? DEAN Shut the fuck up. LOWELL C'mon, seriously, dude, what do I do during all of this? NUÑEZ He just TOLD you, man. DEAN You stay in the car. Collect the cargo. LOWELL Shit. I'm driving the getaway! DEAN I wouldn't let you drive a golf cart. LOWELL Oh what the fuck? Come on! Who orchestrated this whole thing?! My brother and me. DEAN "My brother and I." LOWELL Fuck you, man, seriously. I set this whole thing up and you assholes are telling me I can't play the game? Fuck you. No, fuck all of you. With a 20 inch uncut dick. DEAN Arright, I'll pull over and you can do this job yourself, motherfucker. LOWELL God dammit, guys. Do you know how excited I was? DO YOU HAVE ANY IDEA? Come on, guys! This isn't phhhhhhhttt-- Nuñez violently slams his hand across Lowell's face, knocking his glasses off and holding his mouth shut. NUÑEZ The Kid up there knows what the fuck he's doin'. He's the Coach K of this stuff. If you even breathe another whiny breath, I'll make sure it's your last. DEAN And this is why I do jobs with Nuñez. CUT TO: I/E. HONDA CIVIC-FRONT/BETWEEN 32 & 34 DAVIDSON CT-DUSK All 4 of them pull on black rubber gloves. Nuñez ties a black bandanna around his face, rooting through his bag and pulling out both of his guns, affixing the suppressor to the pistol. NUÑEZ Dee, I got a sub and a Kimber. What's the set-up? DEAN Doesn't look like anyone's home. (Thinks for a moment) Cassidy's got her little 9. You can have my Baby Eagle, keep your Kimber. I'll take the sub. Cassidy dumps out the contents of the two bags and pulls out a form-fitting blue zip-up hoodie, which she puts on. While Nuñez and Dean trade guns, she hands Dean a black zip-up hoodie of his own and leans over, tugging at his hair, tying it into small pigtails. Dean is far too busy looking over the gun to notice or care. DEAN (CONT'D) Everyone clear on positions? A nod from everyone in the car. DEAN (CONT'D) Let's go have a little fun, kids. NUÑEZ One last trip around the block. 'Stime it's f'the money, my nigga. The doors open one after another, and each carefully exits the car. They disperse in the background as Cassidy slowly, confidently struts up the driveway. A sly smirk comes across her face. Cassidy reaches the front door and begins jogging in place, ringing the doorbell. JOEY soon opens the door just enough to peek his head through. JOEY Yeah? CASSIDY (Still jogging in place) Hi! My name's Elaine. I just moved into the neighborhood. JOEY Uh, good to meet you. I'm kind of busy right now. CASSIDY Oh. Are you busy? Do you have company? JOEY I'm just a little busy. I'm sure I'll see you around the neighborhood and we can talk then. CASSIDY Oh, you're right, I was just going for a jog and saw something over in your yard, I'll talk to you later about it. Okay, good meeting you! JOEY Wait, what? Where was it? CASSIDY I don't want to make a bad first impression by bothering, I'm sure it's nothing. JOEY (Opening the door a bit more) I'd like you to show me, actually. CASSIDY Okay. Well, there was something in one of your bushes that you should probably take care of before it's too late. Cassidy begins to step back as Joey steps through the door, immediately getting WHACKED across the face by Dean, hidden in the bush the assault rifle, swinging it like a baseball bat, holding the stock. CASSIDY (CONT'D) (Shrugging and looking to the dropped body) Too late. (To Dean) Way to knock that one out of the ballpark, tiger. DEAN I'm tellin' you, I could've been the next Willie Mays. Dean looks around back of the house. DEAN (CONT'D) (Louder, short of shouting) Jay, we're in. Nuñez runs around the front of the house and comes in the front door, picking up the unconscious body, throwing him onto the floor of the foyer. Suddenly, Lowell appears. LOWELL Yup! That's him! DEAN Get back to the fucking car. LOWELL In a second. CASSIDY Ew! I forgot about that. I kind of wanna see it. DEAN Somebody's gotta take care of this guy with the silencer. LOWELL Wait, we've gotta do this. DEAN Lemme ask you a question, Lowell. Let's say you walk into a bathroom to piss and the seat's down. Do you lift the seat and then piss, or do you just take a seat to get the job done? Nuñez hands the gun to Cassidy. NUÑEZ Do it up, babygirl. CASSIDY (Unbuckling his pants) Hold on. She takes the gun from Nuñez and, using both her free hand and the one using the gun, reaches down and tugs his pants down, eyes clenched, almost in fear. She steps away and she and Lowell look down. Lowell's jaw nearly drops. Cassidy, meanwhile, shrieks and accidentally pulls the trigger of the gun, shooting a round directly down. NUÑEZ Holy sheeit. Lowell is nearly bowled over with laughter, while Cassidy looks down in horror. DEAN What the fuck did you do? NUÑEZ Your girl just blew that shit clean off, son. DEAN You're never going to recover from the guilt of having shot a penis, are you? Cassidy still looks down in shock. DEAN (CONT'D) Alright, if he wasn't already dead, he's gonna bleed out now. Lowell, back to the fucking car. Low-pro. Everyone else, search is on. Nuñez and Dean wander through the spacious, well-decorated house, turning over couch cushions and slashing the couch open. Dean kicks a hole in the massive TV and examines the contents. Cassidy snaps out of her little daze and searches Dynamo's pockets, taking his money and cellphone, and then runs UPSTAIRS where she enters the master bedroom, slashing the mattress and looking under the bed, finding nothing. She makes her way to the closet, which is locked. Backing up, she runs and kicks the door as hard as she can, which merely cracks the outside. She kicks it repeatedly to no avail, before backing up again and SHOOTING two holes in the center. She rears back and KICKS the holed section with all of her might and finally breaks through, to expose the sound of a puppy barking. She looks in and sees a nervous miniature boxer puppy shaking and barking at her. CASSIDY Awwwwww! C'mere boy! I'm not gonna hurt you, I'm gonna love you! The dog continues to shake and bark as Cassidy reaches in and wraps her arms around him, pulling him through the hole and sitting, placing the puppy on her lap. CASSIDY (CONT'D) You're my baby now. My little boy! Did that man have you locked up in there? I made sure he can't dick you around anymore. Aw, a little black boxer all locked up. I'm gonna name you Mike Tyson. You're gonna grow up tough, just like him, too. You're my lil boy, aren't ya! Cassidy then stands up and begins to examine the hole in the door, reaching through it and unlocking the door. The closet contains MASSIVE amounts of drugs; kilos upon kilos of cocaine, boxes of pharmaceutical ketamine, Vicodin, oxycodone, and various other prescription drugs. Cassidy's jaw nearly drops as she runs to the stairs. CASSIDY (CONT'D) BACKPACKS! UP HERE NOW GUYS! The other two come running and almost jump up the stairs, turning the corner into the bedroom and stopping in their tracks when they see the closet, Mike Tyson barking at them all the while. DEAN Wow. He got that shit put away fast. NUÑEZ God. Damn. Dean steps in and starts loading it into his backpack, filling it and trading it with Nuñez for an empty pack, which he proceeds to fill. Once each bag is filled, the three of them make their way downstairs and out the door, loading the trunk of the car. Cassidy begins to head toward the house once again. DEAN Cassidy, we're good. Let's go. Now. CASSIDY I'm not leaving without Tyson. DEAN What the fuck does that mean? NUÑEZ I'unno, man, but there were a couple corners in there we didn't search. If she's goin' back in, we might as well give it another look over. Dean lights a victory cigarette, strutting in behind the other two. Cassidy walks UPSTAIRS and grabs the small puppy, stamping back down the stairs with him in her arms. An engine is heard REVVING near the house, causing Dean and Nuñez to look through the back windows in investigation. DEAN Oh, look. We have guests. NUÑEZ FUCK! I left one of the guns in the car. Fuck. DEAN Arright, everyone just take cover. Cassidy, put that dog in the bathroom. CASSIDY I'm not gonna leave him! DEAN (Showing true anger for the first time) Put the fucking dog in the goddamn bathroom! She runs into the bathroom with the dog and closes the door. DEAN (CONT'D) Leave your gun out here! She opens the door just long enough to slide her pistol out and locks the door behind her. Dean crouches behind a chair; Nuñez next to the couch. They exchange a momentary glance before the door is POUNDED upon. After a few moments with no answer, the door is KICKED wide open and FIVE THUGS, armed with subs and AKS, enter the house, fanning out to cover the living room and make sure the coast is clear. Eyes dart from the tattered remains of the furniture, fingers finding triggers. Dean issues Nuñez a nod and holds up three fingers, which quickly fall to two, then one, then-- Dean stands, as does Nuñez, instantly opening fire. The first bullet to escape the barrel of Dean's assault rifle SPLATTERS the head of the thug closest to the door, sending him tumbling through it and down the small set of concrete steps. The thugs return fire, along with a few shots from Nuñez, SPRAYING the room with bullets. Dean and Nuñez quickly take cover. Pieces of fabric, wood and stuffing go FLYING in all directions. Nuñez pokes his gun up over top the couch and fires off two rounds. Dean uses all of his lower body weight to SHOVE a recliner into them, sending it crashing through the glass French doors as they dodge it. Aiming his rifle around the couch with just his hand, he fires a few rounds toward the thugs. The silhouette of one of the thugs against the wall. Blood flecks the surface of it and said thug falls against it, hit in the chest but not fatally so. He raises his AK, holding it with one hand, and squeezes the trigger, spraying more rounds into the couch. Dean turns, his back to the couch, and sees a marble coffee table. He flips it over and takes cover behind it, using it as a shield, curling his gun around the side and FIRING. Nuñez, meanwhile, is picking his shots, firing off single rounds at a time, being conservative so as to not run out of bullets. The thugs hastily fire their AKs until they run out of ammo, their panic and the recoil of the weapons preventing any from hitting the intended target, forcing them to retreat around the left side, into the kitchen. THUG #1 (Whispering) Who the fuck are these guys? Meanwhile, Dean looks over to Nuñez with a sly smirk. DEAN I'm gonna get 'em outside. NUÑEZ Aw fuck. Don't do anything stupid, nigga. DEAN (Sliding the submachine gun across the floor) Gimme the pistols. Nuñez slides his guns across the floor and Dean picks them up. NUÑEZ Only a round left in one. A couple in the other. Seeing the thugs run into the kitchen, Dean gets up and runs out the open glass to the backyard. OUTSIDE Out of the corner of his eye, he sees a skinny, balding man in the driver's seat of the black van the men arrived in, nervously shaking and looking frantically through the glove compartment for some sort of weapon, seemingly oblivious to Dean. Dean TAPS against the window on the passenger's side. The man quickly looks up and immediately gets BLASTED through the glass by a casual Dean Matthews. He walks around to the driver's side and pulls the door open, pulling the body out and letting it limply fall onto the ground. He hops in, turns the car on, and REVS the engine, clamping down hard on the brake and flooring the gas, sending the wheels whirring and getting a large screeching sound against the patio. The men in the kitchen immediately notice and one runs out toward the door of the kitchen, only to get 2 rounds in the chest, blood splattering all over the white cabinets. The other two men see this and run out the back room toward the stairs. Dean hops out of the car and climbs up onto the hood, struggling until he finally reaches a windowsill on the side of the house. He jumps, attempting to pull himself up, and hangs for a few moments before his hands can't support his weight. He falls and CRASHES into a structure overhanging the garden, left in a heap face-down in the dirt.
