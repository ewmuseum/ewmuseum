The journey was about to begin... but the question was, were they ready? Tyler and Conor were greeted at the large oak doors (well, not really. They were just basic glass doors. It was the entrance to the Arena... but to the Fuse Bros., they were large oak doors). Anyway, they entered. A Staff Member greeted the both of them, as they took off their ugly looking brown and green caps, and tipped them in the way of the doorkeep. The Fuse Bros. were very polite... hell, you had to be. You were in a brand new game, that wasn't even finished loading yet. Pft, the loading just started! The Fuse Bros. were meant to soak this all in for the first couple of weeks until they could warm up... and then take a charge. So, moving along, The Fuse Bros. walked the hallway of the Arena. They had never been in one before... and seeing as fWo Wednesday Warfare was being hosted in an Arena as nice as this one, dammit, that made it all for the better. Conor had to stop for a second, half-way down the hall. He felt his leg twitch... he didn't think he could walk another foot. "Just... go on without me." He said to Tyler, who rubbed his head confused. "Just go on... go!" Tyler snapped his heels down, as he swung his head across in a 360. "What, and let the henchmen already kill you? No no, this game is still loading, moron! C'mon. Tonight, everything is gonna be fine. The game should take a week or two to load - it's a slow system. I promise." Conor's odd green eyes flickered in the lighting, going right along with his matching green hat, and green overall suit. "Really?" Ty walked over to Conor, patting him on the back. "Power." He stuck out his right hand for another prop. Conor returned it with his reply. "Power."
