A Rainy Day in Washington - Body Count pregame.

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestlers are creatures of habit. Most of them do the exact same thing in the locker room, night after night. Some play cards. Some listen to music. Some - usually the ones who are new to the promotion - just keep a low profile, doing whatever, as long as they stay out of everyone's way. Eli Flair likes to be the first one at the arena for a pay- per- view. If management says "Be at the arena by 1 PM," he does one better and is there by 11 AM. He likes to get there early so he knows where everything is. Eli gets his own drinks. Eli gets his own food. Eli locates janitors and similar people quickly in case he needs them to get him a table or a ladder or something during a match. Today, however - just about everyone was at the arena early. There's a feeling in the air that something is going to happen tonight. Since this is Eli Flair's first Body Count, he doesn't know if it's because of the uniqueness of the event in the fWo year or if it's something else. But he doesn't care - it's something he can get used to. "Eli, can you come here for a minute?" The security guard has entered the lockerroom at a very inopportune time. Eli Flair is sitting at a small folding table, playing poker with Scott Slugger, Tempest, and his guest, Seattle hometown boy and CSWA World Champion Evan Aho. "Can you give me a second?" asked Eli, showing his cards to the guard. "Oh.... sorry about that," replied the guard, "Finish the hand." Eli turns back around, but the damage has been done, as Slugger quickly folds. Tempest follows, with Aho close behind. Eli sighs, laughing to himself, as he gets up and follows the guard. Immediately, his three opponents grab at his hand to see what it was he had. Outside, the security guard motions for Eli to walk with him. Eli's just about ready for the matches tonight, even though they don't start for another few hours. He's showered and put on his tights - topped with an old- school CSWA "GUNS CONTROL" T-shirt. "We had an incident with a fan trying to get backstage, but she insisted that she knew you," he said, "and Universal advised us to simply ask you if it was true." "I doubt it is," replied Eli, "The only person I know from Seattle is in the lockerroom playing cards with us." "Alright," replied the guard, "then we'll deal with her." Eli nods, but stops just as suddenly. "Let's go - my curiosity is piqued. Where is she?" "Just inside the door near the entrance," he responded, "We figured it'd have been a little too cruel to make her stand in the rain, she looked like she was already sick." "Yeah?" "She was pretty pale, at least." Eli takes in this information nonchalantly, it seems.... but in reality he began to walk faster. His mind was focused on one thing and one thing only.... more specifically, one person only. And he didn't really know, but he hoped it was her. It was. "Eli!" She broke away from the guards who were standing in between her and the backstage entrance, and jumped into his arms. "Thank the gods, these guys were really giving me a hard time about being here!" He hugged her tightly, and gently put her back down on the floor. "What are you doing here, Angel?" he asked, "I thought you were recording the album." Eli gestured to the guards that she was okay - that she was with him. "We're done recording," replied Angel, following him back to the lockerrooms, "but TJ and Mickey are rerecording some of the guitar parts and they want try and 'tweak' it a little bit. But, that's nothing I need to be there for, so when Ivy asked me to keep an eye on you this weekend I said I would. Then when she comes back and I go back to the studio, they'll have fought over everything and compromised on everything I'll have something to actually hear." "Ivy should've called me and told me you were coming," said Eli, "and I'd have picked you up." "I asked her not to," replied Angel, "I wanted to surprise you." "Well," said Eli, "you certainly did that." They stop walking, and he hugs her again, picking her up off her feet. "It's really good to see you." "It's good to see you too," she smiled, "I missed you." They turn their faces toward each other and kiss - a deep, passionate kiss that's been a month in the making. After a minute or two he puts her down and begins to lead her toward the lockerrooms. "C'mon," he said, excitedly, "I want you to meet the boys. And Tempest, of course." She laughs at his enthusiasm, at least a little in mocking. But there's a twinkle in her eye that matches his, one that's been missing from both for a month or longer. Eli Flair felt something special in the air this morning. That something special, he's sure.... is currently holding his hand.
