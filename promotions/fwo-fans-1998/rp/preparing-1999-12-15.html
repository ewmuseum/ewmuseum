Preparing.

15 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Inside the French Foundation locker room, the Flying Frenchie finishes adjusting his knee- and elbowpads. The Frenchie picks up his goggles from the vanity, and slips them over his head, tightening them in the back. Finally, he dons his beret, placing it snugly on the crown of his head. He turns to face the camera, a stern look upon his face.) Strangler, on Sunday, you made ze single biggest mistake of your life. You took it upon yourself to attack ze Foundation, bot' physically and mentally. You hung Primetime, and cost him his belt. You put ze Massacre on Courage. But your highest crime, Paul is zis: You kept me from winning back ze World Title. T'anks to you, zat strap is gone from ze Foundation. T'anks to you, it's once again around ze waist of ze personality-impaired Ice. Now, I'm sure zat you've a funny feeling in ze interviews of your stomach right about now. Perhaps you t'ink it's remorse, zat you feel bad about what you've done. Perhaps you believe it to be regret, knowing zat zose actions will make you ze Foundation's enemy for life, when you could have just as easily chosen to stand wit' us not so long ago. Monsieur Strangler, I hold zat it's somet'ing else. I hold zat it's fear. Tonight, on Warfare, you step into ze ring wit' me, Ze Flying Frenchie, Pierre Delacroix, in a no-dq match. We all know what happened ze last time we faced. I beat you, pinned you in ze middle of ze ring, to retain ze Internet Championship. So tonight, when you come face to face wit' me, you're squaring off wit' a man zat you know to be better zan you, more capable zan you. But more so, you're facing a man who's not very pleased wit' you. I know you, Strangler. I know what you can do, and I'll be ze first to admit zat you can be impressive at times. But you are not French, mon ami, which immediately puts you at a disadvantage. I will come to zat ring, prepared to beat you until my fists are raw and bleeding. Tonight, no matter what it takes, I will see to it zat you will be on your back, counting ze cracks in ze ceiling, while I pick up anot'er win over you. You know zis to be true, Paul. But don't let zat intimidate you. Show up. I'll be looking forward to it. Just like last time, Strangler, Je Gagnerai. (The Frenchie stares hard into the camera, as the scene fades to black.)
