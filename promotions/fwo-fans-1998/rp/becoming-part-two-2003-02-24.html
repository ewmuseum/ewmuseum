Becoming- Part Two

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The rain never lets up. It keeps pounding down, despite your best wishes for it to stop. You look up into the rainclouds, and all you see is darkness. Perhaps, though, perhaps you can see something more. Perhaps, you can see into the Heavens. Only when it's dark. Jessica shook off the dark thoughts, stopping a bit to wonder where they were coming from. She was 13. Abused by her father. Ignored by her mother. And she still had to go to church with them, while maintaining the image of the perfect Christian family. "God will punish the unholy." her father had said. "This family isn't unholy. We go to church every Sunday, so get your lazy little butt out of bed and into your church clothes." With a hesitant sigh, Jessica looked down at the stone tiled ground as she stood under her mother's umbrella outside the church, awaiting the opening of the doors. Finally, a clergyman opened the doors to the church, and the line slowly began to make its way out of the pounding rain and into the church. Why does my body shake everytime I enter this place? Jessica wondered as she entered the church. Perhaps, perhaps God doesn't like me. That's not what she was taught in Sunday School, but yet, Jessica couldn't remove her thoughts from this dark place. Her body shook, perhaps from the sudden change in tempature, perhaps not, as she entered the place of worship. She took off her coat, hanging it next to a few others, then stopped. Looking around, she realized there was only one other person in this small cubicle, darkness hidding it from the rest of the lobby. Her father, teeth gnashing and finger pointed at her. He slapped her across the face as he spoke through gritted teeth. "You'd better behave, do you hear me?" he said in an angry whisper. Jessica could only lower her head and nod as her angry father turned and walked back into the main lobby of the church. She looked down at her feet, at the tiled ground. It opened up. It swallowed her in darkness, covering her with everything unholy and inpure. She opened her eyes. She was swallowed into Hell. Her mind suddenly drifed to one of those old Puritan tales about the angry God that dangled the sinner over the pit of Hell. And she reazlied now, God had let go of her. She looked around- and she was in her house. Her father lunged at her..... I am becoming something that is unholy and inpure.
