Somewhere I Belong - One

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everyone wants to tell me what I am. I'm an impressionable little girl but also a goddess, I'm a sinner and a saint at the same time, I'm the perfect being and I'm scum, I'm relatively moral and a whore all at once, I'm linkened to winter, but then again I'm summer... Get it? Who and what a person is...it's all a matter of perception and opinion. If you asked...let's say...Ivy about me, you'd probably get something along the lines of 'Good kid', 'She's great' or, in a particularly drunken moment 'So about my uncle Mary...', but that's beside the point. Now, let's say you ask Martyr about me; 'Fuckin' bitch' would probably be the first thing from his lips, closely followed by something along the lines of 'Me have big balls, me big strong man!'. All a matter of opinion... Speaking of Ivy back there...I'll pull out a quote she'd have me live by: "Opinions are like assholes, the internet is full of both." I just happen to ignore a few opinions when it suits me. Especially when my parents have formed one on Brandon Roche... "He's.....nice." Mom would fidget with anything near her as usual, this time it was the tablecloth in the dining room of a house I was thankful to be rid of when I turned 18. The first blatant veer from saying what was really on her mind always came right before the same time-honored line every female got upon introducing a boyfriend to her parents. I sort of wish I'd been given some magical power to see into the future so I could follow the standard advice. "Just make sure you don't get hurt." Hey, at least she was trying not to piss me off. My dad had never been quite so kind, he was way too much like me for my tastes...he didn't take shit, I mean stubborn as HELL and he'd say whatever was on his mind whether you were the Queen of England or some homeless guy who just so happened to be sitting there when he got an idea. Did I mention he was stubborn? "I hope you don't expect me to like him." That was fine, I'd gotten used to hearing it everytime a male associated with me in any way had met him. "But you should avoid 'im...I think he's just out for young blood. You say he's been married?" "Yeah..for a few years." It raised my ire, but I delivered an obedient answer in a tone that'd clue him into my mood. I don't know what I was hoping for in that, though...it's not like he cared whether or not I was ready to fly into a rage, because if I did he'd just make sure to match it. You know, the usual 'Anything you can do, I can do better' game, even if he couldn't...the guy had control issues. "He's not someone you want to deal with." He's not? Coulda' fooled me. "I'd hate to say this..." Really? You're looking awfully chipper for someone who regrets trying to crush their daughter's will. Too bad you know me too well, eh? You're gonna 'Hate to say' a lot of things in the future. "The guy's bad news for ya. You're just going to be hurt...after being married, you think he wants some kind of serious relationship? Of course not! He just wants something pretty and young to occupy his time because he got bored of his wife." All a matter of opinion...bad opinions don't mean shit........don't take it to heart, you DON'T want to punch your own father in the teeth. If I could stay cool, I could tell him why he's wrong, maybe even without SAYING he's wrong and thus avoiding World War III. "He's only twenty six." "Doesn't matter." You fucking asshole. "But...I'm only six years younger. He and Ana broke up because...well...I think they stopped being able to find a reason why they said 'I love you' every day." "I bet he finds plenty of reason to tell you when you're in bed, huh?" Oh yeah...now HERE'S the conversation I've just been itching to have with my dad ever since I left home! Sarcasm aside, I was searching for pointy objects to stick in my ears. Thank you so much, Captain Tact. "We haven't...ehm..." "You're kidding me." I wish I was...but it's not like I'd be telling him that. Surprise surprise, dad...you might be wrong about something! "I'm not..." ...unfortunately. Even my mom looked shocked. Did they really think that little of me? I can picture them in the grocery store with their wallets open: "This is Colleen, the oldest...she's got a rich husband and the lovliest little Persian kitten. Here's Paula, her boyfriend is soon asking her to marry him, and he's got a great job...think dental plan! Oh yeah...and this one's Amy. She's some pretty, huh? Yeah, she could've been a model...but instead she sleeps with anything that acts committed, mostly professional wrestlers." "...Oh, well...." By the time he'd gotten over the initial 'shock', my mom had excused herself to call her sister. This is just what I needed now. Again, sarcasm aside, I hadn't had a single civil conversation with my dad in a few years...at least none that ended civil, they all began more or less tame until our egos collided. "Did I ever tell you how ridiculous you were to get yourself shot over a guy?" Notice how he never said anything close to "Jeez, I'm sorry sweetie...I guess I was wrong in bashing what could possibly be the greatest thing that's ever happened to you." and just ran along right into telling me how stupid I am? Welcome to my childhood, ladies and gentlemen. I think he took my silence as a "Yes", just didn't give a shit either way. "Well it was damned stupid...your life isn't worth some guy's." "Oh, so now I'm worth something?" "...What?" What could he be thinking? Try "Holy shit, she actually contested me!"...as if it didn't happen every single time he got pigheaded and too far into my business. "Of course you're worth something, you always are! It's just that I've been telling y--" "Trying to tell me what to do." "No!" "Want me to quote you word-for-word telling me that I shouldn't be with who I am just because you don't like him after knowing him for all of the ten minutes you two spoke?" "Don't speak to me like that! You've got no right to." "And you've got a right to tell me that I shouldn't be with someone? You don't know Brandon, not even a bit...who are you to form an opinion? He was a good husband to his wife, a GREAT father to Sean and Jarod and most of all he actually treats me right, regardless of what mood either of us are in." Look at big bad Forrester Campbell now...mouth gaping, almost amazed I was actually talking like this. It was expected that I'd bite back after a few minutes...but apparently I normally didn't talk quite like this of anyone I was defending. "I'm sure you've wondered why the hell I ever chose to put myself between him and a bullet, right? Maybe if you spent our visit paying attention to us as opposed to any negative possibilities you could pick out, you'd realize why." Okay...so maybe I didn't normally talk like that about someone else. I didn't normally feel like this about someone else, though. "You..." He looked almost like he was a second from finally breaking and admitting he was wrong. "...you really like this guy, huh?" Well, close enough to satisfy me. "I really like Brandon." And maybe, just maybe I'd finally get him to learn a boyfriend's NAME! "Hrmm...well, I guess I could pretend...but I still say he's just out for a couple months of fun." ...And people wonder why I'm always so relieved to be on the road, as far away from Nova Scotia as I can get?
