Nightmares, Revisited

31 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The soft music of a music box floated over Tempest and she smiled up at a blond man in his late thirties, with eyes as blue as deep water and a warm smile. As soft as the music box was, she couldn't hear his voice, but she understood nonetheless as he handed her a small clown doll with a shiny white porcelain face. It's clothes were a bright multicolored patchwork, with festive filigree and a lace collar. "Thank you daddy!" She squealed with delight and hugged him. He seemed so much taller than her, a giant, a rock she could always lean on. She held his hand as they watched the clowns roll and tumble, flip and fly in the small arena. They seemed so grand, so weightless and beautiful, like butterflies in human form. Maybe someday she would be one. *BANG!* Fireworks went off and she jumped as a clown flew from a canon and several others leaped in synchronous form. She clapped her hands, smiling, beaming. All the bright lights shined in her eyes, so much hope, so much promise, almost blinding. *BANG!* Tempest screamed but no sound came from her mouth. Her hand was caught in the iron grip... the death grip of her father. She screamed and cried, but no one stopped , no one would help. Her face was covered in blood and he lay there still and silent and she could feel herself slipping off the edge of a great chasm. She clutched the clown doll tightly; now it was her only friend and her only family. *BANG!* Tempest's body hit the walls of the pit and they shattered from the impact. Faces loomed and leered at the edge of the circle, lost in a haze a foul smoke. It was blessedly quiet at least. The silent faces swirled in her vision and she struggled up. She could barely catch a breath before she was knocked back again, her opponent looming over her. As the light caught in just the right way, she could finally tell that it was Eddy Fate. *BANG!* Tempest hit the steel of the cage and her vision blossomed red. The sea of faces outside the cage were almost indistinguishable except for the expressions of horror that seemed universal. She clawed her way up the cage to a standing position and through the haze of her vision she saw a pale figure advancing on her. A Ghost perhaps? And suddenly Tempest froze as a smoking white Arrow flew toward her. She saw her father's face frozen in the shock of his final agony. The piercing pain in her chest... "Daddy!" Her scream echoing, choked with blood... And as the smoke dissipated, the Ghost Arrow dissolved into the smoke from a gun, just beneath the smiling face of Eddy Fate. "Daddy!" Tempest screamed, awakening covered in sweat. Tears rolled down her cheeks as she remembered once more that he was gone, taken from her forever. She flailed briefly in the bed and found the small battered clown doll and hugged it close to her. Her tears fell on it's hard face and rolled off like the memory of those long departed.
