Haunting Past (pt 2)

24 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I can't help but remember that day. Believe me, it's a day I'd rather forget. It was a couple years ago. Sarah had been bugging me to take her to the carnival. I had a couple days off, so I gave in. I figured it'd be a good father-daughter time. You know, the kind of wholesome family crap they show on TV. I should have known better. Nothing's wholesome when you're KVC. We walked down the midway, passing all the carnival barkers. They shouted out to me, daring me to try knock down all the bottles with one throw, or toss the ring onto the neck of a bottle. I wasn't buying into their deal, but Sarah was. There's something about a giant stuffed bear that messes with people's minds. She pulled on my sleeve and begged me to win one for her. Hey, she's my little girl. Nothing too good for Daddy's Little Girl. So I decided I'm going to win her a nice, big prize. I started looking around for a contest suited to my unique skills. Sure enough, I find the old carnival favorite: the Ring the Bell contest. You know, the one where you take a big hammer and smack the plunger, trying to send the gadget up the pole to ring the bell at the top. The Monkey Boy with the hammer saw me coming. "Hey big guy. Looking to win a prize for the little lady?" I nodded. "Damn rights. I'm planning on taking that big pink bear off your hands." "I like your confidence, and I'd hate for you to disappoint your little girl." He stuck his hand out, and I handed him a couple bills. "You get three swings. If you ring the bell, the pink bear is yours." The kid was maybe a quarter of my size. He spat in his hands, grabbed the hammer, and slammed it down on the plunger. The ringer shot up the post and rang the bell. He smiled and handed me the hammer. "See? Nothing to it. You ring the bell and I'll go get that bear for you." Sarah was glowing.
