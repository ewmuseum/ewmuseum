Phone Calls

4 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Hello?" "Hi... how are you?" *stretch/yawn* "Not bad... tired." Angel covered her mouth, all the way back in Kansas City... she'd forgotten about the time difference. Instinctively, she'd dialed Eli's cell number after her show was over, but while it's 11:30 PM where she is, it's about 8 AM for him and he's definitely not awake yet. "I'm sorry, dear," whispered Angel, "want me to call you tomorrow?" "It is tomorrow, Angel," reminded Eli, sounding a bit more awake, "And no. Right now's great. How was the show?" "Insane. The kids loved the new songs." "I heard about some'a those new songs... anything you want to tell me?" Angel didn't answer immediately - she took a deep breath first. "Well..." "Quite a departure from the poetry you scripted on Corpus Noctem, y'know. You don't make such a drastic departure like that new one - Tempest told me about it - without somethin' going on." "...." "Talk to me, Angel... remember, I know where you live." She laughed, but it was forced. "I... talked to someone a few days ago." "Who?" "My mother." "What? How?" "We're through Nebraska in a few weeks and I... I didn't want MJ to wonder who her family was." "What happened?" "She was nice enough, considering we haven't spoken in eight years. Blames me for breaking up the family, thinks I drove Uncle Mark to suicide for being a bitch to him and shit... Dad wouldn't even speak to me." Silence for a moment. "You're tired," said Eli, finally, "Go get some sleep." "... The nightmares are coming back ..." Fuck. "You're no good to the band or to our daughter when you're exhausted," replied Eli, "Go to sleep. I'll call you tonight. And if you can't sleep, don't do anything crazy.... just write. Ok?" "Okay," replied Angel, "I love you." "You too, sweetie," said Eli, "Kiss MJ for me." They both hung up, but lingered on the phone for a few more seconds. Separations are tough... but they're a part of life. But it doesn't mean either one has to like it.
