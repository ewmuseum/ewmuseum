A Beautiful Mind - Discovery

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Twilight had come and gone. Steven Shadows made his way back home so that he can rest before Countdown was to begin on Sunday. His whole demeanor was changing, and he knew why. Instead of hugging and kissing his wife when he got home, he merely just dropped his bags and then locked himself in his bed room. His wife was worried and she had every right to be. *** I can't believe what you've turned me into Steven rubbed his head in frustration. "What the hell are you talking about?" You have totally ruined me. You've turned me into a giant pussy. "I haven't turned you into anything." Oh, but that is where you are wrong. I was the one who got you where you are today. I am Graphic Violence, not you. You're Steven Shadows, the pussy that needed me all his life to survive. And you still need me now. Steven paced back in forth in his room while waving his finger from side to side. "No, you're wrong. Go away; I don't want to hear this." I was the one that took you to the top. I was the one that the promotions wanted. I was the one who made you a world wide fuckin' superstar. And now look it, you've ruined my career! "NO! It's my career. Not yours" said Steven as his pacing began to pick up. It is my god damned career that you've fucked up. You wouldn't be where you are today without me. But now you've ruined all of my credibility. You sunk like the titanic during you Old School stint where you decided to get married to that cunt and try to take medication to drown me. Steven was pacing really fast in his room. Both hands squeezing his head while his fingernails dug into his skull. So now you've got me wrestling women. You've fucked my career by attempting to block me out for the past year. But I'm not going to allow you to do this to me anymore. "But...I've taken my medication. You can't be in my head right now. JUST GO AWAY!" Heh, medication!? There is no medication out there that can keep me suppressed. I just sat back and watched you tank my career, and now I'm back to claim it. It'll be a lot easier if you just allow me to take control once again. Steven slammed his face in his bed and began to scream at the top of his lungs. "No! NEVER! I'm not going to allow you to control me again!" Have it your way. I'll make your life hell until you do. Talk to you later Steven *** Her mouth dropped wide open as she put her hand over it. Her breathing was heavy, he eyes showed fear. She was standing right outside his door with her ear pressed up against it and had heard everything. She had heard him talking...to himself. She ran down the hallway but tripped. She turned back in fear that the door might open, that he might have heard her listening in. But the door didn't open. She went down stairs and into the kitchen where she looked for the phone book. Looking for a number to someone that might be able to help before it was too late. Or was it?
