Gefallener Engel

17 May 2003

The knife's tip dug into his snow white skin and pulled. Blood slowly came to the top and followed the trail the knife was making. As he pulled the knife from the top layer of skin, the blood still followed a straight line down the underside of his forearm. As it got the edge of his palm, it flowed into the small cracks and creases. Forget spit. This was the real swimming pool of the future. Aim to depress. The knife was raised again, dulled by his blood. He brought it to his left arm and began to carve. Once again blood began to trickle down his biceps, dripping onto the egg-white bed sheets. The outline of his art was an upside-down spade. Although bleeding, it resembled something of a heart with a dagger piercing into its top. Glancing in the mirror he saw the reverse image of himself and his work of art. His eyes appeared content. In the mirror behind him was the silhouette of a female's body sleeping on her side. Her right arm was above the sheets and was slightly red. On her biceps was the same carving that he had just made on his own left arm. He turned his body away from the mirror and dropped the knife into the small waste basket beside the bed. His right hand went to the night stand and grabbed a tissue. He pressed it to his arm momentarily and brought it back. It left a perfect copy of his spade. The phone rang quickly and he answered it. Before letting the person on the other end say anything, he spoke and hung up. "The person you are trying to reach is no longer here." He pulled the phone out of it's jack and switched off the lamp. In the dark he moved to the female's head and whispered into her ear. "And we said... until we die..."
