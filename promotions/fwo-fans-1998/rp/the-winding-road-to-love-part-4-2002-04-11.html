The Winding Road to Love (Part 4)

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fresh off defeating Sphere to cement his shot against Tempest at Cyberslam, Monte Burns reached the backstage area once again, pulling his long hair back into a ponytail as he stepped through the curtain. Burns gave a wave to the producers near the "gorilla" position, hoping they had understood why he had made the unexpected appearance before. At the time, he had more on his mind and didn't really care what they thought. Now, though, as he thought back on it, he understood how trying it would be to deal with that kind of attitude from wrestlers on a weekly basis. Hell, he should know better than anyone else having run his own promotion before. Unfortunately, Burns didn't have time to spend apologizing to the producers or even catching up with anyone else backstage. Unlike most Slaughters, Burns needed to get on his jet and get back to New York as soon as possible. Platinum Gold, Inc. was holding an early board meeting on Monday, and the members would be none too pleased if the CEO of the company missed the meeting due to a wrestling commitment. As Burns made his way down the hallway, however, he was unable to escape one person who was on his heels. "Monte, Monte, wait!" It was the voice of Kristen Jacobs, one of the fWo's roving backstage reporters. Burns had always been someone who despised being interviewed backstage, often thinking it was a waste of his talent as well as the interviewer's. Interviews went so much better when they were done in front of a live crowd. Even Kristen's excellent figure and fiery red hair couldn't make these interviews appealing. Monte stopped, rolled his eyes, and turned around determined to make this short. Strangely, when Burns turned around to look at Jacobs, he didn't see a microphone in her hand. There was no cameraman following her with a video camera. In fact, Jacobs was dressed in her street clothes, looking like she was about to leave the building herself. "What is it, Kristen?" asked Burns a bit confused. "Well..." Jacobs began before stopping for a moment. It seemed like she was debating whether to finish. In the end, she did. "Well, did you really mean all that you said out there about Tempest tonight? Are you being completely honest here?" Letting out a deep sigh, Burns looked down at the floor for a few moments before lifting his head back up and locking eyes with Jacobs. He had spent everyday since Body Count answering this same question, and even though he understood why it was asked, it was still become more annoying by the day. "Beginning with what I said after out match at Body Count," said Monte with a hint of anger, "I have done nothing but tell the truth about my feelings for Tempest. I've said that I care about her. I've said that I feel she's a wonderful person. I've said that I want to spend some time with her and get to know her better. I don't know what else I can say, Kristen." By the end of his response, Monte's tone had gone from one of anger to one of anguish. The insinuations by Jacobs and others around the country that he was somehow faking his feelings for Tempest had taken their toll on him. He no longer talked with the fervor that he had before, even when talking about Tempest in interviews. It seemed that this conversation was no different. "I understand, Monte," said Jacobs with a bit of a soothing tone. "But with all that Tempest has been through, I'm sure you understand that I just don't want her to get hurt again. She's been a good friend to me, and I hate seeing her down." "Listen to me, Kristen," Monte began, obviously wanting to make one final comment before heading off. "I have no intention at all of hurting Tempest. Will things work out between us? Obviously, I can't say for sure. The way it stands right now, I don't know if we can cross what's between us. But I assure you, I have done and I will do nothing to intentionally cause her anymore pain and suffering. You have my word on that." With that final comment, a smile came across Kristen's face, and Monte even managed to form one of his own. Not long ago, the "word" of Monte Burns would have meant just as much as a bag of jelly beans. Something, however, was telling Kristen Jacobs that he could be trusted. Now the problem was convincing Tempest to do so.
