Sinking In

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The hotel room the fWo provided for the wrestler's to use before Ruahh~! provided a bit of relaxing time. But then again, that just gives the wrestler's time to think, and thinking was never Flyer's friend. Arriving earlier in the day, Flyer had taken out the n64 that he had purchased with Allen Speck at a local pawn shop in Philadelphia and hooked it up to his television inside his room. Mario Tennis had quickly become his favorite as it was the only game he owned for the system itself as of yet. He sighed; tossing the controller to the floor after Waluigi had just kicked his ass in a game of intense singles action. He turned to his laptop, which was hooked up to the wall through the hotel's electricity, a luxury one doesn't miss unless he's traveling on the road for two hundred plus days a year. There wasn't much he could do without the internet, so he booted up an old episode of Aqua Teen Hunger Force. He turned the lights off in his room, shut the blinds as if he were about to commit some sort of crime. But the only crime he could think of was the time he was spending away from his family. He huddled underneath the covers before placing the laptop on his lap. The theme song to Aqua Teen Hunger Force played in the background as Flyer fixated himself on the laptop's monitor, and let out yet another sigh. The phone rang, just when the episode was actually going to begin. Flyer flailed in his bed, nearly dropping the laptop off of the edge while he rolled over to the nearest table, where his cell laid. He had a smile on his face and his eyebrows were raised as he flipped the cell phone open to answer. "Kate?" "No Fly, it's me; you're assistant, Eddie Scott Poser." And with that, all the air Flyer had in him left. "Heard you're kinda secluding yourself, so I'm gonna come up to play air hockey with you." "I don't have an air hockey table Eddie." "What? Are you saying I almost walked next store for nothing?" "Goodnight Eddie." "Wait! What about Rua-" Flyer cut him off, closing his cell phone and placing it back onto his nightstand. He shook his head a couple of times and looked over to the clock. Eleven thirty. Odd he was feeling tired this early. Or maybe he was just bored. See, he's already lived the lifestyle most professional wrestlers are famous for. Drinking with the guys is out, since he's never been a big drinker to begin with. As is trying to pick up some sort of wrestler whore that wants a one night stand with a B-Level celebrity. He has a wife and a child he loves at home and he doesn't want to do anything that would risk them losing him. But in a sense, he already has. And that revelation was slowly sinking in. Sure, Kate may be fine with Flyer going off to live his dream life. But this wasn't his dream life. It was his dream job, yes, and he was in the relationship that he always wanted, but they didn't combine to make a life. It makes two, and it doesn't work like in the movies, where one life stops while the other goes on. Kate and Allocca were at home, living their lives without him. The wrestling world has a cruel desire to play with emotions. It made Flyer feel completely fulfilled and yet alone at the same time. The detachment Flyer felt while sitting in that hotel bed, he shut his laptop completely off and placed it next to his cell phone on the nightstand. Rolling over, he hugged the pillow and tried to imagine it was her. But no matter how hard he squeezed, he knew the truth.
