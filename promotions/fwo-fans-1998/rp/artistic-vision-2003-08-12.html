Artistic Vision

12 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I don't really need to tell you my attitude has changed now do I? It doesn't take a rocket scientist to figure out that I am a changed man on the inside, not as much so on the outside. I still sport the mask, and ask for respect. But for giving my respect. No more of that nonsense and mindless bothering that toys with me day after day. All I have to worry about now is what I desire in life. But what is all this I hear about me, Padrona being unique. Call me egotistic, but when wasn't I unique? It took you long enough to notice now didn't it. All it took was a change in attitude, a few beatings and a few choice words. Charming isn't it. So now this is what you see left. Me, Padrona, Vilas Lavagna, the Thai-Fighter, I really couldn't care what you called me. Just remember my name is all I am asking. You forgot me before, and I will be damned if I let you forget me again. History has a way of repeating it's self and this is one history, one fate that I do control. But don't take my word for it. Let my actions speak for them self, and let my words speak my mind. Let's look and grasp the moment for a second. I am here, fighting for success, my own desires, and my own goals. So why are you here? Do you actually care about what is happening in my life, or is this just part of your every day routine? Or maybe you feel sorry for me? I am the one who should be feeling sorry, if that was even possible. I got five men, all targeting me for their own petty reasons. Such a poor group I work with. Is it really hard to see who will be leading soon enough? Not me of course, I am not a leader. I don't set the standard for others and hell I'm not even going to say I am the standard. I am just going to say I am me. The Thai-fighter and nothing more. Where you expecting something else? Maybe a cheap pop? No, not my style, although now a days it's hard to tell what my style is. I am just going to leave you with this. Follow closely, because starting now. You miss a little you miss a lot. Is that how I am described? / Is it how I am seen? To each other own opinion, but my own thoughts do not agree I am but one person One soul One mind I live alone in this society Feeding off my deepest rage, expressing in ways no person could possible understand Is that why I am seen as different? Is it why I am even feared? Aren't we all just alone; all but one mind acting in are best interests Is violence really so hard to see The good it does The reviving sense it brings to your soul The crushing blow it brings to your foe I had behind a shroud of white A cloud, with two lights visible Watch...listen...react as you may As time continues to click forward we will soon become one Soon you will begin to feel what I only have began to show The gift of terror, the parade of gloom's army Listen...To the words I do not speak Watch...The actions I don't make Feel...The blood I do not spill Fear...The mask that covers the face Of what... You may possibly never know
