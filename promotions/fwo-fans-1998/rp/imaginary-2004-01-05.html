Imaginary

5 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You're too important for anyone You've played the role of all you've longed to be. But I...I know who you really are You're the one who cries when you're alone. So your life starts out fine; You're a pretty little girl, don't mind going to Sunday school and wearing that little red velvet dress with the white lace trim mom sets out for every Christmas concert, church play...anywhere you need to look good. You despise barettes, but you know what? That's fine - you can handle them because as long as you're the one getting the smiles and stares for being such a little cutie, life's a charm. And you're growing up. Fifth grade? Simple. Sixth? A breeze! Junior High? Road block. This great freedom you're suddenly getting might be a little confusing. The school system wasn't made to encourage free thought, but to gather and recite information shoved down your throat to 'prepare' you for the world outside; One where, instead of six hours a day of taking orders for free, you get paid to bend over on hand and knee for some haughty adult. Due to that design - simple function - the sudden release (Because you can take it, right?) of nap times, colouring books and playground recess can be tough. Junior high school mashes you in from 7th to 9th grade. So you're getting kids from ages 13 to around 16...big differences, no? Little Miss Velvetdressandbiblestudy might get a sudden slap of reality or, God forbid, choices. Being that age and raised how she would have been, she's not gonna have ANY clue what the fuck to do. Then there's puberty. Nice little thing, that...not so comfortable now? Don't get too afraid, this'll just be a discussion of hormones. More specifically - suddenly noticing Mr. Popular who, in this case, is a little wannabe punk rock boy. Your sister's listening to Metallica, that cute 'older man' (16 really counts when you're 12, doesn't it?) digs NOFX and for whatever reason, has taken an interest in your fine little face and utterly laughable innocence - What's a princess to do? You've got two choices now: Bend Over - Be what your parents always wanted and please those around you. Experience - Learn, grow and quit hiding from the real world you'll inevitably have to face or get anally fucked (See: Bend Over) by. There's an issue. Man, would your folks ever be pissed if their baby girl walked to the kitchen table late - she didn't hear mommy calling her for supper because her Black Flag was too loud. And there's no real reasoning behind it at first. Your hoop earrings turn into a barbell in your belly button...then your upper-ear...then your eyebrow, next you're stretching every new hole you find. Why? You've got a boyfriend now! You're listening to heavy metal, punk, all of that 'different' stuff whether or not you even enjoy or understand it. He's doing it, your NEW friends are doing it...it's what's right. Your 'independence' may only be a shift from relying on your parents to relying on your new crowd, but it's still a change. Something different. Something that found you caught at the exact right moment at the exact right time. So congrats, Miss Teen. Your boyfriend's got you pregnant, and you've been everything but disowned by your family. You're drinking, you're experimenting with anything his 'loving' hand holds out, you're miscarrying and you're still rejected by your parents. You're royally fucked, but you don't care because you can avoid mirrors and thought. Avoid the fact that, for the next few years, every guy will cheat or lie, every drug or drop of rum will lead to nothing but illness, every class you skip because you'd rather be doing what your popular friends are...well, it's all just going to keep going and still, you're only screwing yourself over. And still...you don't see it. Yeah, you have your occasional 'revelation' that lasts all of a month. You've got the guidance counsellor telling you that maybe college isn't best for the little rebel - maybe you should get by on that attractive little face and attitude like you have been and try modelling...just stay away from any system because you'll only be damaging it, yourself or both. Yeah, that's fine. A few men later, you've found yourself dabbling in fighting for a living. Modelling agencies don't seem to like tattoos or violent tendencies as much as you do, but this seems to be a quick buck. Get involved with a pro wrestler, become one...and that's exactly how it went. "The next thing I knew," She smiled pleasantly, apparently not phased in the slightest, "I was working with fWo...still getting drunk and popping pills for a hobby. And the one I met...well...Superman. God, I hated him." The same pleasant smile, this time with a laugh. "That's alright to say now, yeah? Good...because I really, REALLY couldn't stand him. We first met when we were set to tag together - y'know, new kid teams with a name to help sell her. Some of the usual crazy, 'shots fired!' kinda shit was going on that night...so I was headed home early. So was he, apparently. Again, it was the usual; The nice, goody two-shoes helps the damsel in distress when she's walking in the rain. Well, as I said, I couldn't stand him. He was everything I'd have liked to have thought I was entirely against. He had a wife, kids, a sparkling clean image and a pretty fat paycheck. I had a halfway abusive 'boyfriend' and a habit, and I don't even want to imagine what my public image was like. I even called him Grandpa for the longest time, just because he was so...I dunno...mothering? Anyway. Even when he grew on me, when I saw how happy he was with his choices and started making some of my own - like to cut out the retarded chemical bullshit, the drinking and all of that...well, he was out of the question. Wife and kids, remember?" "But you know me..." She laughed, "Determined. I knew his wife was what I'd consider to be a lying tramp, and didn't really give a shit whether or not she knew I was attracted to him. I don't even know what it was at first but we bonded so quickly, so smoothly and at one point, that big sappy 'instinctual kiss' happened. Of course, the wife was not pleased. I knew we'd both fucked up big time, and we ended up not speaking for something like a month. I started getting these weird notes telling me to keep away, which I assumed were from a jealous wife and mother of two...but not exactly. They'd broken up, which I wasn't even aware of when I took the bullet from his stalker to save his own life. I didn't care in that second, when I saw her pull a weapon out...when her finger tensed, I jumped because I just didn't give a good God damn what happened to me. Not so long as he was still breathing. It wasn't so I could have him. It was probably the most, possibly only entirely selfless thing I've ever done. He'd changed me from someone who didn't even care if she hurt herself into a girl, young woman, whatever you want to call it, who cared about someone else finally. Someone honest, someone who wouldn't betray her. Even if I couldn't have him in MY life, I knew he had to live...because he's the world's only completely honest, loving human being." Aimz sighed, shuffled in the booth and continued speaking to her sister across the table. "Brandon Lelan Roche. Forget the other guys I thought I was so taken with. Forget everybody who could use me, sit by and watch me destroy myself...everybody who could say "I love you" just as easily as he does, only have it be an absolutely glaring lie. He's the reason I'm not dead under a bridge somewhere, the reason I wake up every morning and most importantly, the reason I am who I am sitting right here in front of you, right now. And I don't care what anyone else says. Through the shitstorms I've brought upon myself, he helped me find myself. I'm still not the little Princess I was and I never intend to be...but I'm not caught up anymore. I'm myself. I don't have to veil with an image to protect my feelings because everybody knows by now that it just doesn't work. There'll always be things and people to hurt me in the world, but I've learned now how to stay true and stop hurting myself. Instead of being so caught up WITH myself and protecting who I thought I was, I've got time to progress...and I have. I'm getting married next month, mom and dad don't hate me, I'm successful and, opposite of what most would think, instead of those things MAKING me happy...it was me becoming happy that had me find them. I've got one person to thank for it - I once saved him physically, but he saved me entirely. That's my story and I'm sticking to it." Amy Campbell, say hello to yourself. [No, it's not very good. It's hardly even relative, but it's what came out and I needed it.]
