Reinforcements

11 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm a constant paradox. Rei always hated it when she let herself think too deeply, for too long. The things her twisted brain would come up with would haunt her for hours on end, and she wouldn't be able to let go of them without the aide of the mask. On this day, her thoughts were on the paradoxal nature of herself. Back when she had believed herself to be just one of many, she had always considered herself the "good" personality of the three. Kali Saturos was a vengeful psychopath and Jessica was an obsessive, close-minded bigot. But she was just a slightly off-balanced, but otherwise normal girl standing in the background making sarcastic comments. Then she found the truth. The truth was, she was never a good person. There really wasn't any Kali Saturos or Jessica- they were just other names for facets of her own personality. There was only Rei. That meant the vengeful psychopath was not Kali, but Rei. The obsessive, close-minded bigot was not Jessica, but Rei as well. But doesn't that mean the nice, slightly off-balanced girl was me too? Rei didn't have the answers. Neither did the mask. Was the nice girl just a little act Rei put on so she could feel distanced from the vile acts she committed as her alter-egos? Or was there really a nice side to Rei too, waiting to be released once again? The answer is irrelevant. Kali's faint voice echoed through her head. Rei didn't answer- there was no point in answering if Kali was just another part of her, right? I'm not a part of you. I AM you. As am I. We are not Kali Saturos and Jessica. We are Rei. We are Eternity. The mask found its way onto her face again, and suddenly everything was clear. It didn't matter whether or not there was a nice girl inside of her- the nice girl never did anything for her. The nice girl would never be a major part of her again. No, she was bad to the core. Her destiny was to be strong and uncaring. Her fate was to be a vengeful warrior. And she had her sights set on one man. Xias. How can I possibly stand up to him again after what he did to me? Eternity thought to herself. Suddenly, it was clear. She needed the one person she ever met who was stronger physically- while still weaker mentally- than Xias. She needed someone that she could use to completely and utterly destroy her enemy. She needed reinforcements. She needed a resurrection. And she would get one. We are Eternity. And we will eliminate Xias.....with some help from the Purifier.
