-January 8th: 12:20 pm Minneapolis, Minnesota- Life on the road for the Fans Wrestling Organization was sure to be trying for Sonny Silver. Hell, it always was for anybody in general. Anybody that's followed him knows how long he's been doing this. As his character, he reminded people constantly that he was in this business for sixteen years, getting screwed over, et al. Shortly after the fall of The Squared Circle, Sonny had been picked up by the Fans Wrestling Organization and had been given a nice two-year contract with a six-figure income, being paid well in lieu of the fWo's soaring popularity with Disney at the helm. He was already in Minneapolis, just a couple days away from his first showing, new story and direction for his character all mapped out. Hopefully, it'd take. He'd spent enough time at Le Meridien, a famous five-star hotel provided to the fWo stars prior to the first showing of the New Year. Now he wanted to simply take in the sights of the place as best he could. It was always an adventure he had. Tradition for himself, if you will. Get to know the surroundings. It wasn't like he hadn't been to Minneapolis before, but this time under the wing of Disney and the fWo, it was a spectacle. One he felt he had earned. Now on a cold, bustling afternoon, he had gone for a walk. Looking on at each of the passers-by. Some probably knew who he was. Others didn't. His face had been shrouded in a black hoodie and his body covered in warm clothing, including some black boots and navy blue jeans, so it wasn't a hard thing to see. Nevertheless, it was a good day to relax and that's what he'd been doing the past hour. After he had bought a Java Mint from a Starbucks around the corner, a long walk to the Chain of Lakes was all he wanted to do. (Damn fuckers are everywhere, aren't they?) "TAXI!" Okay, so he was feeling lazy today. Plus, it was probably far from where he was. Sonny waited patiently while shivering in the cold, hoping the caffeine would kick in soon. He then decided a big gulp of his piping hot drink would cool him down. "OW!" Okay, so it burned his tongue a wee bit. Finally, a taxi courtesy of Burnsville Plus pulled to the curb. He saw that another passenger had already inhabited the back seat. They had been shrouded in a black trenchcoat, red snow cap, and red scarf. "Meh," he muttered under his breath. "At least if he's got a gun, I'll leave a handsome corpse." And nodding as if that was that, he hopped into the taxi, told the driver his destination, and that was that.
