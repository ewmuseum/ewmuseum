Thai-Fighter - Prologue

7 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"What do you see now," Shygen asked, peering down at Padrona who was sitting on an overcast hill that look down upon the river below and shadowed a gorge some twenty feet underneath his own position. Shygen walked up and placed her hand on Padrona shoulder and listened as the wind whistled through the gorge and up towards the hill. "My future," replied Padrona, opening his eyes and gazing at the light blue sky, and small puffs of white clouds that dotted the sky. His body sat on a patch of grass, not much bigger then his body, but enough to provide comfort from the rocky terrain. He to took a second and smelled the fresh air and the soaked in the beaming sunlight of the morning Thailand sun that shined down from the heavens above. Padrona stood up and walked closer to the edge of the hill, which turned into a steep cliff that would send any man plummeting to their death. The green foliage of the tress below added with the sent of clean air, relax the much tense body of the young Thai man, who would constantly work himself into an unstable state of mind. His quest for greatest and the respect of honor's would soon leave him battered and bruised, but through his mind, it was a small price to pay. "I see what will become of me if I change my state of mind," Padrona added, turning around to Face Shygen as she crept closer to his position. "I do not wish to make mistakes, but something tells me I will be in grave danger when I arrive at my final destination." Shygen took a small package of herbs from her side pack and placed a few leaves under the arms of Padrona to heal his sickness. "You will be relaxed soon enough. The pain should subside very shortly and you may continue on with your training," Shygen muttered, placing the final herbs under Padrona's wrist guards. Padrona turned back around and gazed at the gorge below. Shygen calmly walked forward and picked his chin up with her hand, pointing his head towards the sunlight and the river. In the far distance was Bangkok, the capital. The symbol of this free nation and the symbol he was fighting for. "Thailand will live through you Padrona," Shygen said with confidence, pointing out the view from atop the cliff. "You will bring respect to these people and you will share it with them. Show them success in it's full form," she added with a much louder tone, building his confidence level up, and pushing him to continue working. Padrona looked deep into the heart of the outcast city in the distance and slowly dropped his eyes back to the gorge below. Shygen lifted his head again, but was quickly snapped away. Padrona turned her around and he peered into her eyes, adding a shock to the already scared mind of Shygen. "Everything here is my home," Padrona yelled back at Shygen, getting a jolt of resentment form her body, but she was pulled back in close. Padrona closed in to her ear and whispered, "I will bring honor to my people. I will show perfection." Shygen smiled, and Padrona let his grip go. He took one last look at the city in the distants before yelling out, "Perfection to my people," getting a echo form the gorge below. Padrona turned around and looked back at Shygen with her arms crossed. He gracefully walked past her and started down the cliff. Shygen drew closer to the gorge and looked down herself. "Every living thing, Every living life," she whispered to her self. Shygen joined Padrona down the mountain and carefully begun their quest for a greater value then gold or silver. A value of honor...and respect...
