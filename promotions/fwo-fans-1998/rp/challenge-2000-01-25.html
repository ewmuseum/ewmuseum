fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Challenge January 25, 2000 Sunday. Post-Slaughter. Hiroshima is seated on a bare wooden bench. The locker room itslef is a mess, as several of the other lower card talents with whom he had shared this space had left without bothering to pick up after themselves, their minds too concerned about grand plans for their advancement up the ladder. Hiroshima, as usual, wears a bitter face. He has long since given up hope of being at the top, more worried about drowning at the bottom. His head turns almost imperceptibly, and his eyes lock with the camera. "Tonight, I returned to the ring, after a month long absence. The fWo has neither seen nor heard anything from me since I fell to the Strangler. And it moved along just fine without me. "I do not delude myself, as to my standing in this organization. But I do expect a certain amount of respect from them, nonetheless. "Oracle, you come out, and you complain that the fWo is ignoring you, not giving you the chance you deserve, simply because you've been the man in every other promotion that you've called home. It should be said, however, that you were part of the power structure of several of those federations. You point towards earlier tonight, where you were the first one to be sent out of the battle royal, as a sign of the lack of respect they have for you. I would call your attention to this, young sir: I wasn't even shown leaving the ring. "It's true. I've watched the tape, and the camera crews neglected to show how I was tossed. They felt that it was a foregone conclusion, that I would not last long, so my exit wasn't deemed worthy for broadcast." Slowly, Hiroshima turns his head side to side, audibly cracking his aged neck. "Oracle, you seem to think that you're being held down because of your age. I'm 20 years your senior. I say you're held down because you're not worth being pulled up. I know, Oracle. I've seen it hundreds of times before. There's desperation in your eyes. You can see your career crashing, your worth to other promotions plummeting. So you say, 'I must make my move, and I most do so now.' "I've seen it before. I don't need a crystal ball to see how your story ends. But I can help you. You think going out and challenging the pup will bring you back to glory? It will not. Nothing can save you now. But I will help you. I offer you a challenge, and not an open-ended one, as you gave the child. Next week, when the fWo returns to the States, that is when we will meet. On Slaughter, I will show you what you have to look forward to, and it is not a beautiful sight. After I am through with you, and you are lying on the mat, broken, spent, and forevermore useless, you will look up at me. In turn, I shall cast my gaze downward, looking at you, while you cough up blood, and I shall offer you my hand. "I will be the first person, Oracle, to welcome you to oblivion." Hiroshima's lips seal as he turns away from the camera. He cracks his knuckles, as the promo cuts to an ad for Warfare.
