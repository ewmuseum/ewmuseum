Worst. Interview. Ever.

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Claire: Why are we doing this again? Jackhammer: I am guessing it is because we have not had a run-in with them in the past few weeks. Claire: So we're just gonna jump La Parka when he comes out of the bathroom? Heftel: AW, HELL YEAH! HE'S GOIN' DOWWWN! As Heftel, Jackhammer, and Claire wait outside a bathroom at a gas station, Black Quicksilver stands near his car. He's distracting Evil-Lyn, with some help from Stump, of course. BQ: So, uh, yeah. I wear a mask. Mr. T is over at his car, filling it up with gas. Mr. T: T WILL NEVA LISTEN TO YA RULES, NO! T WILL NEVA DO WHAT SET IN STONE, NO! KNOW WHAT T WANTS AND I'LL GET IT FROM YOU, T DO WHAT HE LIKE AND HE LIKE WHAT HE DO! Sphere comes out of the gas station's food mart, carrying a Baby Ruth. He sees the members of the STUMP Squad standing near the bathroom, and let out his war cry. Sphere: SURRRRRRRRRGE! Sphere throws his Baby Ruth at Heftel, and it hits him in the face. La Parka finally comes out of the bathroom, but is immediately shoved back in and given a "swirly" by Jackhammer. Claire kicks Sphere in the nuts, but is then tackled to the ground by Evil-Lyn. Mr. T: NOW IT'S TA-HM TO PARTA AND WE'LL PARTA HARD! BQ watches the brawl between the two groups, as Mr. T continues singing, even though he's done filling up. A fan walks up to Mr. T and surprises him. Fan: Hey, Mr. T! Can I have an autograph? Mr. T: DAMN, SUCKA! YOU ALMOST GAVE T A HEART ATTACK! DEFIBULATA, FOO! Fan: Uh, I'm not that smart. Can I have an autograph? Mr. T: HERE YA GO! Mr. T gives the fan a 1-800-COLLECT t-shirt. Mr. T: SQUASH THAT BUG! By now, Claire had rolled on top of Evil-Lyn and was repeatedly slamming her head into the ground, but Evil-Lyn managed to reach up and rake Claire in the eyes. She rolled back on top and sat on Claire, laughing maniacally as she grabbed a nearby funnel and shoved it into Claire's mouth. She reached for a can of gasoline, but opted to go for a bottle of Caffeine Free Diet Pepsi instead. She poured the soda down the funnel, but Claire knocked the funnel out of the way and spit the soda at Evil-Lyn. Evil-Lyn: AAGH! MY EYES! BLAST YOU, TEELA! Jackhammer steps out of the bathroom and walks over to BQ, as does Mr. T. Jackhammer glares at Mr. T as the fan rushes over. Mr. T: YO CLEAN, T DIDN'T DO NUTIN'! TAKE HIM INSTEAD! Mr. T throws the fan in front of him, as Heftel, Sphere, and La Parka all limp over. Heftel is eating Sphere's Baby Ruth, which looks like this: Sphere wasn't too happy, and let Heftel know by raising one finger in his direction, which looked like this: The fan looks at Mr. T, then at the STUMP Squad, then back at Mr. T. Fan: Hey Mr. T, did you know that there are NO stables in the fWo right now? The fan laughs for no reason inparticular as he looks at the STUMP Squad again. BQ: AHCK-UM? There's the STUMP Squad, you know. Fan: Huh? What's this little stumpy thing? Who's in it? For once, Jackhammer makes himself useful. In a related story, the fan's head met Mr. T's windshield. Meanwhile, Claire and Evil-Lyn had been taking turns slamming each other into the side of a dumpster. Evil-Lyn slammed Claire into the dumpster back first and released her from her grasp. She backed up and charged, but Claire ducked and did what can be considered an actual wrestling move, backdropping Evil-Lyn into the dumpster. She dusted herself off and joined the rest of her group. Sphere: You MISERABLE little toe rag! I highly doubt that Earl T's insurance covers fanboys getting thrown through his windshield! Jackhammer: Since when did you recover your British accent? Heftel: And how come La Parka doesn't talk or smoke the dope anymore? Stump: ... Mr. T: 1986? NAW, IT BE 2002, SUCKA! Sphere: Well, you see, it's like...we're morons, so we just plum forgot about not acting like ourselves. BQ, who had been shaking his head in disgust for the past few minutes, finally speaks up, as La Parka goes over to drag Evil-Lyn out of the dumpster. BQ: Jesus Christ! What do we have to do around here to get people to recognize us? Jackhammer: We could die. Heftel: Yeah. All the rage. BQ: Nah, maybe we could do something that many would consider the Worst Booking Decision Ever. Heftel: I thought me winning the Cruiserweight Title was the Worst Booking Decision Ever. Jackhammer: I thought trying to get people to care about me was the Worst Booking Decision Ever. Sphere's group had begun to get into Mr. T's car, when Mr. T stopped Sphere from getting in the passenger side. Mr. T: HEY, SUCKA! HOW 'BOUT YOU DRIVE? Sphere: That's mighty considerate of you, Earl T. Mr. T gives Sphere the keys and gets in the passenger side, while Sphere walks around and gets in the driver's seat. Sphere starts the engine and begins to drive away, but has trouble steering. His voice can be heard by the STUMP Squad from inside the car. Sphere: Oh, now THIS is just absolutely SMASHING! How am I supposed to see where I'm going with a bloody CORPSE looking at me through a frickin' big bloody hole in the windshield? Nevertheless, the car continues down the road, as Heftel and Jackhammer climb into the backseat of BQ's car. Jackhammer: If anyone makes a joke here, I *will* kill them. BQ catches a glimpse of the battered Claire, and sets Stump on the hood of the car. BQ: Hey, you alright? Claire: Yeah, just a little Caffeine Free Diet Pepsi poured into my mouth, little beat up. BQ: Beat up? You were just in a fight and I missed it? AWW, CRAP! Claire: It's alright. Maybe I'll give you a DDT later or something. BQ: OhmyGodohmyGodohmyGod... Claire smiles at BQ and gives him a hug. Stump: ... BQ: No! I will NOT have her give me a flying headscissors! Claire: Huh? BQ: Nothing. Now keep hugging me.
