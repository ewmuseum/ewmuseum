Sins Of The Father - Prologue

18 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"This bottle, of Stephen's Awakens ancient feelings Like, father, dear father, The son is drowning in the flood" - Weezer, "Say It Ain't So" Ric looked at the small, silver flask, scrutinizing it with a harsh gaze. The initials "MC" were still emblazoned on it, their lettering the only indication of its sole owner. Ric absently tossed the flask aside, knowing exactly what it held. This caused dust to kick up from the adjacent floorboards, and he continued to root through the piles of memorabilia and papers that belonged to his late father, Mike Chronos. Digging deep into a chest, he discovered a leather-bound journal, yellowed and aged with the passage of time. A small lock proved to be of little resistance, and Ric was soon leafing through the pages of his father's memories. Ric knew exactly what he was looking for - his conversations with his Hong Kong connections had given him the clues he needed to follow up his investigation here. Huang Chin had known Mike Chronos to some extent, and Huang also knew of Fate and Tempest - there was a connection there, and Ric was determined to find it. He had learned some minor details, and in so doing, had uncovered Tempest's shady past - but there was more to it, so much more. And Ric, much like his father, held the type of stubborn determination that could not be swayed until his goals had been met, his objectives satisfied. He had always blamed that personality trait on the Greek side of his heritage. And with that, Ric immersed himself into his father's memories, hoping to find that last, vital piece to what had been puzzling him.
