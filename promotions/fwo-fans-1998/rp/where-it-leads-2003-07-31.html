Where it leads...

31 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
During those most desperate moments... The wind cut across the top of skyscrapers and sped through the darkest alleys and corridors. Amongst a patchwork of the finest suits and dresses, an anomaly existed. Trent walked the noon sidewalks of Chicago during the lunch rush. Glass statues spilled their contents out, rushing over concrete like a majestic and gaudy river. His eyes shifted around nervously for a few moments, skin pale and clamy to the touch. Everyone passed ignored the vision of deprivation and poverty. The leather trench coat was smooth to touch as Trent bumped into him. "Sorry," he echoed in a rasp whisper. The man bore through him with a horrific glare, then continued on his way. The same quality leather flipped through his fingers as he sifted through the wallet. He quickly removed the contents and tossed the item to the ground. No one even questioned him, for he was just an insignificance--an inconvenience. A few steps down, Trent turned into a dark ally. A couple trashbags worked as a couch for a moment. The memories of past glory flashed with each blink as if the tragic tale were etched on the back of his eyelids. Imagination melded into reality, and Trent built an image of him throwing Allyra to Eugene Marder's arms. The car drove off and he walked back into a sporting arena. Trent rolled his head back and leaned up against the brick wall. His hand gently pushed his stained blonde locks out of his face and bit his lower lip. Tears began to well up, threatening like the blackest clouds hanging overhead. It was then that those big white letters hit like a truck's headlights. When he woke up, he was in the police station, filing a missing person's report for Allyra. During those most desperate moments, we crack.
