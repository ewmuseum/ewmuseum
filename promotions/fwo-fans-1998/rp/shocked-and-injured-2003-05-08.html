Shocked and Injured

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Flyer hadn't even finished unpacking yet when he received the call. Hell, he didn't even start unpacking, but he was already set to head back on the road. Guess that Smackdown game with his son would have to wait. Because when the fWo calls, you drop everything. Everything. This was his break, his shot at glory. The one he's been waiting for for years. He was called on March 31st, 2003, a day before he was set to have his candlelight dinner to commemorate the two year wedding anniversary to his wife, Kate Young-Harmen. Flyer had chosen April 1st as the wedding date, primarily because he thought it'd be an interesting symbolic gesture to their marriage. He also had this idea of shouting out April Fools during the ceremony, but he realized that he probably wouldn't have left the place alive. See, Flyer's always been a jokester, a trickster if you will. His backstage reputation as a prankster has been well documented. In fact, that was his inspiration for his Snow gimmick. A light, care free genuinely kind person trying to prank people into buying snow. Hell, he used to love the gimmick so much that he'd even do it when the camera's weren't rolling, selling snow cones to the wrestlers in the back with unique flavors that would make Harry Potter's jelly beans all taste like prime rib. But this was no joke. Flyer, on his anniversary was set to meet with Samuel Smooth in London, England, and to make the trip; he'd have to leave immediately. Kate wound up calling a babysitter so she could tag along. It was her anniversary too, and the couple wanted to spend the next couple of days together. Flyer explained to Smooth that Kate was going to be his translator. When Smooth said that they were in England, Flyer made up some story about not understanding the dialect, their nuances, and their crazy rhyming curse words. Smooth was skeptical, and that's when Flyer fessed up that it was his wedding anniversary, and he wanted to spend time with his wife as well. With that, the couple was on their way to merry old England. They flew coach, as a bargaining chip to get Kate to come along with him. The whining babies and smoking passengers didn't bother them, mainly because they were together, and all their dreams would come true. "England!" Kate cried out, disrupting a few passengers around her, including a man who was smoking a cigarette next to them. He sighed, blowing the smoke towards the window of the airborne plane. "I haven't been here in three years!" "Oh yeah, you were with me when IWO came overseas." Flyer smiled, wrapping his arm around his wife. "If only you could be my valet again..." he trailed off, looking towards the big screen in front of him that was playing some sort of Chevy Chase movie. He shuddered, and looked down to his feet. "Allocca's too young to come on the road with us, you know that." She rested her head on his shoulders. "Besides, you remember what the wrestling world is like. Is that any place for a two year old son?" Flyer let out a sigh, realizing she was right. "I just want everything, you know?" She nodded. "I want the professional wrestling career; I want to travel around the world. I'm not done seeing everything, and I want to do it on some company's dollar." He smiled. "Not to mention that I love this business, even though it is dreary and disgusting at times." He sighed. "I just want you to be there with me, enjoying everything. I just..." Flyer looked into Kate's eyes, placed his index finger under her chin to lift her head, and smiled. "I just want to be with you. Always." She just smiled. There was nothing she could say in response to that, and she rested her head once again on his shoulders. Flyer reached around her, wincing in pain before pulling his wife in closer. He'd fight through the pain to be closer to her. "We are upcoming to our destination, London England. Please lift your tray tables to their upright position and extinguish all cigarettes." Flyer smiled. "Showtime."
