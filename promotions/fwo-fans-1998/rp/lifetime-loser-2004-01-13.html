Lifetime Loser

13 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A life of solitude isn't easy. As early as he could remember, David Cross was alone. His mother didn't hide the fact that she had adopted him to collect a little more on her welfare check. How she managed to adopt at all was a mystery to him, but when you're six years old you don't think of such things. All David knew was that his real mother didn't want him, so she gave him up to his adopted mother who also didn't want him. Now his adopted mother was dead. Growing up, David wanted to fit in with the other kids at school. His adopted brother always looked out for him, but David's classmates always seemed to find something to make fun of him over
