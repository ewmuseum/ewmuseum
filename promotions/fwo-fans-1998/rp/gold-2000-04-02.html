fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Gold. April 02, 2000 :::The Pepsi Center was a crazed in the hours before Slaughter. Let's face it, though, things always get a little bit crazy when the fWo comes to town. Most people think only of the sightings of major superstars at local bars and diners, but the most feverish of action takes place in the arena. There, dozens of people who'll never get any recognition bust their asses to make sure that everything is ready and in place when the show begins. It's a hectic job, to be sure, and one that does not welcome interrupions. Sadly, things never seem to go off without a hitch. Case in point. Eugene the prop master is entrusted with the responsiblity of having all the weapons and gimmicks doled out at the beginning of the show. On days when VL is wrestling, it's a particularly difficult job. When he looked at the booking sheet for tonight, he was quite pleased to not see her name listed. It promised to be an easy show, until he was accosted by one of the challengers for the Tag Team Titles tonight, one Mike Heftel.::: C'mon Eugene, you've gotta let me see them. I absolutely do not! I have been given a sacred duty, and it does not include entertaining midcarders. Now if you would please get out of my way, I have a job to do. :::Eugene attempts to walk away, but is stopped when Heftel grabs his wrist.::: I say, do you always go around grabbing men?!? Eugene, this is important. Finn told me that you have them. Is it true? And I told you, that the championship belts are in the custody of the champions. That's how things work in the fWo. Right, I know about that. Like, Courage has the International Title with him, and Mitch keeps the Internet Title between shows. But what about when the champ is a little bit loopy? You have it then, right? When the champ can't be trusted not to lose it? It's the new "Chadwick rule". :::Eugene lowers his head, his chin touching his chest.::: Okay, that's true. And the current champs, the Guano Apes, they're not quite the shaprest knives in the drawer, now are they? Those who live in glass houses, Heftel.... HEY! I'm crazy, not stupid. You have them, right? Yes. Can I see them? :::His resolve broken, a dejected Eugene grabs a black metal case from his cart o' props, and sits it on a tall trunk. Using his left hand to shield his actions from Heftel, he rolls the combination locks on the case into position The lid pops open, and inside rest the fWo Tag Team Titles.::: My God, they're beautiful. Are you happy now? Because I really must get about my business. :::Heftel stares at the titles, in awe, not responding to the prop master at all. Well, not until Eugene stomps on his foot, at least.::: OW! As I was saying, Mr. Heftel, are you finished? Yea...yeah. Thanks, Gene. Eugene. Right, Eugene. Say, I don't suppose you know if Sean and I are gonna leave with the belts tonight? It's really not my place to say. Good day. :::Eugene storms off with his cart, leaving Mike to stand alone, his mouth agape.::: I'm going to be a champion.
