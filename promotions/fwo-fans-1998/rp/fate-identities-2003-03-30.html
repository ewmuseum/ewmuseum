Fate: Identities

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Desperation. Survival. Necessity. Friendship. Kevin Masters had become familiar with those words in the past year. It had been difficult, a year of change. Going from actively wrestling, to not. From being in the ring, to being the man behind the desk. He had done everything in the past year. Finished a wrestling career; started a new career. Died, returned, and then restarted a wrestling career. It had been one hell of an adventure so far. He smiled to himself as he turned on the car. The engine roared to life and the dashboard lit up, reminding him of the low fuel level. He hadn't needed much gas recently, driving from the small motel to the training center for the OCW. He generally would car pool with another wrestler in Orlando to get to the shows. It was all pretty easy on his car as a whole. He was happy to have the new Aviator, a gift from his friend Jonothan Enders, but it sure could use up the gas. His mind began to travel again, going throughout the past month, but the ding of the seat belt alarm brought him back to reality. He clicked the belt in place, put the SUV into reverse, and pulled away from the motel. He began to drive towards the airport, looking around for a cheap gas station on the way. Pulling into an Amoco, he stopped the car at the pump and stepped out. With a swipe of his credit card, the pump started working, and the gas began to flow freely. A man approached from the car beside him, grabbing the wiper to wash off his windshield. He paused, staring at Kevin, and then smiled a big smile. "Hey man... Aren't you... Aren't you that wrestler?" the man asked. "Yes, yes I am." Kevin responded, smiling back and extending his hand. The man grabbed it and shook it, "Man that's some bullshit. You aren't seven foot tall!" "What?" Kevin said, a confused look now painted across his face. "Yeah, on that fWo website, they got you listed as seven foot." The man replied, sure of himself. "I don't think..." Kevin stammered, but the man cut him off quickly. "Man, I gotta tell my friends that Deacon isn't really seven foot tall. Shit... They probably lie about the whole roster." The man said, and began to walk back towards his car. "Wait... I'm not Deacon." Kevin yelled at him. "Oh... You aren't? You just said you were. Which one is it?" the man said. "No, I'm not Deacon. I'm the Dynamo." Kevin replied. "Yeah... sure you are... crazy sonofabitch." the man mumbled, walking back to his car and scrubbing at the windshield. Kevin sighed to himself as the gas pump clicked. He pulled it out of the tank, and put it back on the switch. Taking a last look at the man, he shook his head and got back into his car.
