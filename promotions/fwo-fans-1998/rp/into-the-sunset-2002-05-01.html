Into The Sunset...

1 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday, April 28, 2002 Outside the MGM Grand Arena Las Vegas, NV 8:16 PM PST The blue Mercedes turbo diesel - a 300-series left over from the mid-80s - wasn't a Dodge Viper, but Scott Wasner supposed it would do. The show was over (it was broadcast live to the east coast and on tape delay on the west, so they had to start at 6:00 local), so Wasner wore his street clothes, an old "70 is a bad year" t-shirt, shorts, Nikes, and a Mariners 2001 division champions cap. He looked back at the MGM Grand and thought about how he had got here. It had been a hell of a ride, hadn't it? "Yeah. It has," he spoke aloud. The exit door from the arena opened and Wasner saw Mitch Wilson step outside. Wilson had one hand in the pocket of his sweat pants and the other in the pocket of his windbreaker. He used the latter to fish out a pair of keys, then looked up and saw Wasner. "Hey, Waz. How's it going?" Wasner chuckled and muttered under his breath. "He called me Waz. How's THAT for meta?" "What was that?" "Oh, nothing. It's going good. How's things with you, Mitch?" "Well, they're... different, that's for sure. Universal just told me he wants me off-camera. They want Creed to stand on his own, no 'advisor', and I can understand that. I wasn't technically under contract, so I guess I can do whatever I want now. I think I'm gonna start a fed of my own. A little indy in Denver." Wilson walked over to his Lamborghini and leaned against the driver's side door. Wasner nodded. "Good luck with that." "Thanks." The door opened again, and both men looked towards it. This time it was Tony Barrett, better known to fWo fans as Ice. Oddly enough, he still had on his parachute pants and shiny jacket, and appeared to be attempting to bust a move. Wasner and Wilson shared a chuckle at the big man's antics. Wasner yelled towards him. "Hey, left foot! You can cut the kayfabe, the show's over. Been so for a good 15 minutes." Ice looked at him quizzically. "Kayfabe? Foo, whatchu be rappin' bout?" Wasner and Wilson looked at each other. It was Wilson who spoke. "Oh my God... it's not a gimmick. He really did lose his memory when that fish whacked him in the head, and he really does think he's Vanilla Ice." Wasner thought for a moment, then held up a hand in inspiration. "Excuse me for a moment..." He opened the Benz's trunk and dug through, then came out with a large box. He opened the box and pulled out... "Is that a trout?" asked Wilson. "Yep, sure is. A frozen trout." "Um... why do you have a frozen trout?" "Why not?" Wilson scratched his chin. "Hmm. I have no real answer for that." Wasner turned to Barrett. "Come over here." Barrett did so. Wasner slapped Ice around a bit with a large trout, knocking him back against the arena. Barrett held his hand as he leaned against the wall. "Man... what the hell was that?!" "That, my friend... was me saving you from a lifetime of embarrassment." "Oh." Then: "OH, holy shit, you're right. I thought I was... sweet merciful crap. That's it. I quit. Nobody needs a profession where he can end up thinking he's Vanilla Ice." Barrett stormed over to his Hummer and hopped into the driver's seat, slamming the door behind him. Wilson yelled after him. "What about Universal?!" Ice rolled down his window as he started the car with a growling rumble. "Tell him to piss up a rope for all I care, I am so outta here!" He put the Hummer in gear and it roared out of the parking lot with a blast of exhaust. "Man knows how to make an exit," said Wasner. Wilson nodded. "Speaking of..." "Of course. Good talking to you. I'll see ya when I see ya." "Likewise." Wilson stepped into the Lamborghini and peeled out of the parking lot. "Well... I guess that just leaves us, Geoff." Behind the camera, Geoff Green nodded. "So where are you going, Scott? What're you gonna do?" "Well, I know a guy up in Seattle who can get me a job at KOMO as an on-air personality. Might even get to be involved in some of the Mariners games when KOMO gets the contract next year. But before that... I think I'm gonna go find Steph. A friend of a friend watched CyberSlam on pay-per-view and said he saw her in the crowd... and you know what, I think I might have too. So maybe she's in California. And if I do find her, I'm gonna talk to her, and maybe she'll even take me back. I wouldn't if I were her, but a guy can hope, right?" "Yeah. A guy can hope." "Yeah. And I guess that's all you need, right?" Green nodded silently. "Well... so this is goodbye. I am gonna miss it some. But I'll be fine, and so will they. I know they can't hear me, but I do want right now to thank almost everybody I've met and worked with in my time here... I couldn't have done it all without you. And Geoff, here comes the fitting capper, and I mean every word of it... Until next time, it has been your pleasure." Without another word, Wasner got into the Mercedes. He started it up and pulled out of the parking lot, driving off to the west. The sun was setting, all purple and orange and red, and it was beautiful.
