Step 1

11 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"So I made the cut, what do you know esse!" An excited Avo Chavez let out a sound that sounded like a bunch of random words that complied the sentence. Mask Mark #1 turned to Mask Mark #2. "Avo this is the beginning of a new era for Mexicans like us." Said Mask Mark #1. Mask Mark #2 just stood there and nodded his head. Its still not known where Mask Mark #2 came into play he just kind of showed up one day. He didn't deserve an introduction. "It's great to know that I am going to be working along side some of the greatest wrestlers of all time!" Mask Mark #1 looked at Avo Chavez in a weird confused face. "You know people like, Jordan X Shabazz and Richard Andrew Murray." Mask Mark #1 looked at Mask Mark #2 as they both looked confused. "Or Desmond Kinkiad and Simon Krask." Mask Mark #1 looked at Mask Mark #2 again as his eyes lit up. "AH Kellen Kinkiad will be joining us in this adventure I take it?" Asked Mask Mark #1. Mask Mark #2 never spoke even when he was spoken too. Avo and Mask Mark #1 didn't even know if Mask Mark #2 could even hear or speak. "Or how about Joey Villani and Frankie Villani, you know them right?" Avo looked at Mask Mark #1. Mask Mark #1 looked back at Avo and scratched his mask that he was wearing. "AH! I got it. Millie Vanilli." Mask Mark #1 looked at Avo Chavez and they nodded to each other. PEVLIC THRUST! "You can't go wrong with stealing a line from our good ol buddies Pretty Fly." Avo yelled out. Mask Mark #1 and Mask Mark #2 both agreed as they nodded their heads. Avo then grabbed his head. "What's the matter?" Asked Mask Mark #1. "You know what I was thinking?" Asked Avo Chavez. "Wha?" "How did I get into Survivor III yet I don't have a tag team name?" Avo and Mask Mark #1 thought for a second, before Mask Mark #2 snapped his fingers. Avo and Mask Mark #1 looked at #2 and agreed. "That's perfect! The Mexican Militia!"
