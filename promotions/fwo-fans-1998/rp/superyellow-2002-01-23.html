Superyellow

23 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Light rapes me. One hundred and one steps up the sidewalk. "Fine, fine... I don't mind at all... don't say anything about it... honestly! Gosh to betsy..." Twenty feet from the truck, eight hundred pounds unloaded. Bright red paint, rental cost thirty dollars per hundred kilometers. "How many more? Well, no of course I don't mind. Sure is swell to be out here in this weather and all." Storage boxes, one foot by one foot square. Wood crates, bound by metal staples and cross nails. Sanded for your protection and mine. Jesus Christ it was just one big fucking job after another wasn't it all these pathetic measly slobs walking one direction and then the other with their greasy go nowhere lives and their sick shitty faces pale measly... "Last one I think, sure is a shame in a way though that it has to end. Physical work isn't so shabby, really builds the foundation for a good night." Dirt on the ground, pebbles on the dirt. Trival things, focus and concentrate. "Out for a drink? Well shucks and peanuts I guess I'd just love to. Love to." Focus and... focus and... Once upon a time there was a baby tuocows and it walked, oh lord it walked on a burning pile of coals. The steam from the ground bubbled and burned the hooves until they were akin to hamburger and gristle and my god my god my god this is the worst pain of it all the brain burning and bubbling like beef like stinking rotting beef that burns and smokes like coals and I scream oh god I scream inside by I wear this happy smile like a mask and oh my God why have you pissed upon my face and drank upon my blood and remember than they will pay like never you can imagine how they will pay my mind my mind my mind is a dark cloud and their are things there dark things things that flap and fly and eat oh yes they eat like the feast of souls and my harvest will come true one innocent life spilled on the ground and one... "What? Oh... sorry... I guess I was just inside myself... you know guys, I don't think I can make it out for drinks after all... not feeling well... just a bit froggy and perhaps I'd better just head on back to the hotel and lie down. You know how it is... yeah... working out in the sun setting up the equipment. Sure is good to work though, and golly if you guys aren't just the best people. I'll see you tomorrow and maybe we can do it then, won't we? You say hello to the boys at the bar now, never really was one for drinks anyway and that will do just fine you know? Just fine. If you see Urara you tell her I'll see her tomorrow, but fellows I'd better just go lay down and try and get this shaken before it takes hold, you know?" Ten blocks to the hotel. Smile plastered on my face. Nine blocks from the hotel and I shake the hand of a nice man in the street. Eight blocks from the hotel and I'm sweating. Seven blocks from the hotel and my hand is shaking. Six blocks from the hotel and I wave at a baby. Five blocks from the hotel and my teeth are grinding and I can taste blood in my mouth. Four blocks from the hotel and I'm crying and there are tears tears tears running down my face. Three blocks from the hotel and the noises are pounding into my skull. Two blocks from the hotel and I can't raise my feet and I drag them I drag them I drag them along the ground. One block from the hotel and I close my eyes and embrace the darkness. I sleep outside tonight. I sleep in the street like a bum and I dig into the ground with my hands. Is this what madness is well then bring it on baby... the explosion of my pain will be your orgasm into the fire pit of hell. Revenge is an ugly beast and what I want I will take. The darkness is all around me. All around and I can smile and bite into my lip to make the blood run. Six hours before dawn. Seven hours before I smile. Eight hours before I laugh. How many hours... how many hours... how many hours until I destroy?
