Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 4 - Part One

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Douglas let lose of the handle and his rather large black suit case landed on the bed and rustled the sheets. It looked as though this would be just another day in and day out. He would come, people would point, he would smile and children would cry. Oh how he hated it all. Hated every person he walked past and how they stared at his face, they all knew he was monster now, they all knew every time they looked at him. The truth hurts. "Shut up," Steve said softly, not really demanding his request. Why? Then you would be alone, you hate to be alone. "I know I hate you," Steve said once again in a soft whim perish tone. Oh Steve, why do you lie? "I don't lie," Steve began and turn to walk across the room, heading toward the bathroom door of his room. He outstretched his hand to grab the handle but hesitated. Always willing to turn and run away when you find it to hard. You get all sappy inside and then you begin to cry, like the baby you are. So angry at everyone else, when you caused your own pain. "Damn you!" Steve opened the door and now looked into the mirror, behind him stood a man who haunted him day and night, for every waking moment and every moment in his dreams he looked into the eyes of Kenny Rock. Kenny smiled at him and began to speak once again. Damn me Steve? You damned yourself, stop fighting it all the time. Accept what you are. Steve began to look at his own reflection in the mirror, and he felt Kenny's hand rub against the skin of his cheek. Steve's tension relaxed as the hand rub his scarred skin and he rested his eyes for a moment as he listened to Kenny's soft tone. You don't hate me Steve, you love me. You love what you are to do, and you love what you will always be remembered for, a great man who cleansed the world of it's hateful creatures. A man who showed the rest of the viewers who it really was that sick, that were freaks, that were the ugly. Steve began to close his eyes and continue to listen to Kenny as he slowly slid his hand down his stomach and lower into his pants. He smiled in delight. *CRA-BAM* The door to his hotel room smashed open and in walked Angel Dalton, Dalton looked at Douglas who turned in shock toward him, Dalton looked down at Douglas' hand and then back at Steve, "Bad time?"
