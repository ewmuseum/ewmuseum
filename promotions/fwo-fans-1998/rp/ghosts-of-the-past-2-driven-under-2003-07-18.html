Ghosts Of The Past [2] - Driven Under

18 July 2003

September 23rd, 1999 An unusually perfectly fitting grey Tech Nine hoodie, an unusually unfitting pair of UFO 'rave' pants topped off by bright, bright red Element sneakers and enough metal under skin to make a Star Wars makeup artist sick... ...And a goal to make headway in life using only her fists. Not the usual image of a freshly 18 year old girl. Then again, most people with a brain were choosing to be different. This came naturally to Amy Campbell, though... She had two barbells in her right eyebrow, a stud below her lip and a dress sense that was screaming either from a dumpster or skate shop. She was either the usual brand of 'punk rock poser' that you could pick up a handfull of on any sidewalk, or she was genuine. This girl had one ice blue eye, while the other was so dark brown it rivaled black and together they answered the riddle: The look in her eyes told her story - She was genuine because instead of saying "I am rebellion. LOOK AT ME, I'm acting out!!!", they pleaded for help. Nervous of crowds and apparently alone, the redhead stuck close to buildings - though even the height intimidated her - in her trek into a seedier region of New York City. She mumbled to herself about not belonging...even the toughest upbringing in her little Canadian oceanside city hadn't prepared her for going off on her own to New York in search of a career. On these streets, it seemed like taking pride in your appearance alone was even screaming 'Rape me!'...just about everything was terrifying to the comparitively little girl. She knew nobody and refused to make eye contact regardless of how tough she might have been physically... And she was stumbling head-first into a fight why?! On her way to the little gym that just might contain her future, Amy considered for the millionth time just packing up and heading back to Nova Scotia. It seemed easier there...and much more honest. As far as her parents were concerned, she was here for art school on a scholarship with her friends...which actually was true, except for the fact that Amy had never really grasped the concept of 'attendance', so instead of sitting in a classroom for hours a day she'd taken to living in a two room residence with three other people and...well...fighting. Why fighting? She'd done enough of it in high school...plus it was stress relief, easy enough money. Wrestling was what she'd hoped to end up in - Same level of stress relief, much less chance of your IQ dropping six levels and...well...money - Plus enough time to keep up her art and guitar work, which couldn't otherwise be done if you had a cracked skull. Sadly, she hadn't done too well career-wise in this shithole thus far. Seven wallets and two months into her stay in the city and already she needed an out...this would hopefully be it. Every few seconds, she sped up with the feeling that someone who just happened to be walking behind her was stalking after her...always, it ended up being pure coincidence. Mouthing curses to herself, she hit a dead run across a street and then alleyway - it was getting dark, and dark in combination with a small town girl fresh out of high school wasn't the safest scenario - but she finally made it... Right back into the arms of danger, of course. But at least here there was a measure of security..........along the lines of very large men who looked more like ex-cons than trained guards. This was definitely the farthest cry from a Nova Scotian suburb, and Amy Campbell was the farthest cry from a New York 'street rat' - rough and tumble as she may have been. Nonetheless, she was due to meet the one person who'd make her feel at least slightly comfortable in this self-induced mess... But for the time-being, he was knocking a few teeth out. Continue...
