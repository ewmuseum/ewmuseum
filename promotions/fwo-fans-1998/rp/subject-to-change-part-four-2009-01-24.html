Subject To Change : Part Four

24 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As the base of his throbbing skull was driven into the canvas, all of Billy Kilcullen’s 168 pounds were channelled through his twiggy neck. The baying mob of a crowd, just a few seconds ago demanding the rookie’s in-ring execution, fell deathly silent at the sight of his neck becoming the crumbling foundations of a limp body’s collapse. The sudden vacuum left in the wake of the crowd’s rampant cheering sucked the adrenaline from Karina Wolfenden, taking her body’s heat with it to leave the K-Wolf with a sudden, icy chill of regret. “Oh shit,” panicked the official under his breath, knowing just five seconds ago every camera flash would have caught him revelling in Karina’s eye-watering assault. “Shitshitshitshitshitshit.” Karina tried to convince herself that the kid was just selling the move. That the crowd had been caught hook, line and sinker. That the referee was playing his part with pantomime exaggeration. She tried, and she failed. Ignoring the blood dripping from his lips and the bruises beginning to warp his forehead, Billy Kilcullen’s fear-drenched, watering eyes were all that Karina could look at. “BILLY!“ thunderously shattered the guilty hush. At ringside, a wild-eyed Frank Kilcullen straddled the barricade, a security guard struggling to restrain each arm. “GET THE FUCK OFFA ME! THAT’S MAH BOY! GET OFFA ME!“ Trapped within his still body, with the bellowing of his father flooding his ears, all Billy could do was to keep looking right into Karina. Her face bleached of all colour, the K-Wolf shuffled back to the ropes. Repulsion and guilt waged war for her expression as Frank overpowered security and scrambled up through the ropes. “WHAT DID YOU DO TAH HIM?!“ wailed Frank, his bulky, bruiser’s body now defined by the fearful eyes set within a raging face. “WHAT DID YOU DO TAH MY SON?!” Experiencing the rapid come-down from her violent high, the K-Wolf could only look blankly back as the roaring bear that was Frank Kilcullen whimpered over his youngest son, unable to heave him up into the safety and comfort of his arms for fear of doing more damage. Shrinking under the judging glares of the crowd and transfixed by the sight of Billy Kilcullen’s proud father squeezing his limp hand, Karina Wolfenden couldn’t bring herself to say a word, her shock-frozen face resisting the release of any emotion. She didn’t know what to say. She didn’t know how to feel. She knew only one thing. Skulking out under the bottom rope, retreating within herself for shelter from all that was swirling around her, Karina Wolfenden knew she couldn’t ever get back in that ring… or any other.
