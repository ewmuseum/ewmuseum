Eye For An Eye [9] Sins Of The Father

28 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At least a shooting star gets its chance to shine. I wasn't a shooting star, granted they fade away in a flash but they still had that moment in time where they flashed brightly across the night sky. I was a stone that was sinking fast, never anything more than a dull piece of rock. Thrown in at the deep end and left to sink to the very bottom. Tied to a promotion that I would drown in the process. August 7th 1993. "Just shut the hell up, all of you." Vince Kinkade bellowed at the top of his voice "I can't hear a damned thing you're saying when you all open fire at once like that." He stood at the front of a room that was completely filled with wrestlers, all of them crackling and roaring before him like a blazing fire of discontent. Deep down he'd always known the day would come where people would try to take action against his will to see Kellen Kinkade succeed. "Right... the next person to talk out of line is fired." Vince said as the room calmed. "Fuck it then, I'll be in ECW if anyone wants to join me." Landslide... a seasoned veteran spoke out as he got to his feet enraged and stormed out of the room. "Good." Vince spoke back folding his arms "Anyone else want to take their ball and go home? Because if you want out I'll give you your release right now." "Oui." Pierre LeSmith spoke up from the back "I would rather work for Steve King, ze money is good in UKWF." "Steve King? So be it French boy... but if I know King you'll be getting powerbombed through eight sheets of glass by the end of the decade." Pierre got to his feet along with his midget accomplice Pepe, as the two exited through the door the visible signs of frustration began to grow on Vince Kinkade's face. "Two down... any more to go?" The next man to stand up had Vince swallowing hard in shock. Larry Dupri was one of the best workers that WWC had and at the very top of the backstage leader pecking order. "There won't be anymore walkouts, Vince." Larry replied as Vince sighed with relief. "Good to hear Larry, you keep these guys under control... I don't want to fire anyone else today." The WWC owner continued, cracking a smile that quickly faded from his face as the stern expression remained upon that of Dupri. "I don't want you to fire anyone else either Vince, but we have a few terms that we need to discuss with you and if all doesn't work out then you might just be facing a walkout." Vince Kinkade swallowed hard again. "Simply put Vince, we're taking a stand... it's bad enough that you've put the most prestigious belt in this promotion around the waist of your boy long before he's ready. But to bury is underneath him like you have it the straw that's gonna break the camels ba-" "Cut the bullshit Larry." Vince cut in furiously "Just what are you saying?" "What we're saying Vince, is that none of us are doing any more jobs for your boy... we're not gonna lay down for him or you anymore. Now you can fire us all for refusing if you like but while you might be stubborn I know you aren't an idiot, attendances are bad enough on the back of you giving Kellen the belt without considering how badly they'd plummet if you lost any more of us... ...one man does not a promotion make." A few moments of morbid silence filled the room. "Fine." Vince scoffed. "Alright... so when is Kellen going to drop the belt?" Vince raised his head from the lowered position of dismay he'd been hanging it in to look Larry in the eye. "He isn't." August 8th 1993. "So how'd it go?" Kellen Kinkade's voice filled one of the vast rooms in his father's multi million dollar mansion in Short Hills. Vince had just made his way through the door to the sight of Kinkade slumped in a chair with Soundgarden's "Jesus Christ Pose" blazing through the expensive stereo system located in the room. "Turn that bullshit off!" Vince shouted "And why aren't you training?" Kellen hit a button and the music cut out before he looked at his father and sighed. "That bad huh?" Vince could've told Kellen the truth about the meeting that'd taken place but as usual he opted for a perversion of the truth "The meeting went fine... I'm just not happy that you're slouching around in here listening to sacrilege when you could be on the weights or the treadmill." "Oh." Kellen replied with widened eyes "So the guys are alright with me picking up the title? Cool... I guess you were right afterall." "I'm always right." Vince shot back. "So who's my next angle with?" Kellen said with more intrigue in his questioning than ever before. A long pause followed. "Dad?" "We've changed the plans a little Kel, you aren't going to be working with anyone for the first couple of months." Vince replied. "What? But I mean... I'm the champion, surely-" "I've decided that we're going to turn you heel, I think I made a mistake in trying to turn you into a hero in a time where heroes aren't the good guys anymore. It's a risk but I think if we have you insult some members of the crowd here and there we might have it go full circle and get some better interaction and reaction from the fans." Kellen was quiet for a moment. "Alright then." He said to his father as Vince made his way toward the door "I trust your decision." August 20th 1993. Vince Kinkade paced through his backstage halls nervously, tonight he turned his son heel but deep down he wasn't sure if he was the right man to be scripting it. He'd never played the bad in wrestling in a career that spanned over thirty years and as result it didn't come as second nature. He'd looked through the curtains and across the crowd all night, trying to pick out someone that looked different from the rest so that Kellen could single them out with his taunting and get over easily. But so far he hadn't come up with a thing. Ironic that as he turned to make his way back toward the stands for another look the answer was staring him right in the face. "Been a while, Vincent." Vince looked right into the remaining eye of a man who he hadn't seen for almost twenty years. John Hanson stood before him with a patch over the eye that Vince Kinkade had accidentally gouged out. "John, it has indeed... almost twenty years but you look as good as you ever did. How's life been?" Both men wore false smiles, Vince masked his guilt and John masked his rage. "It's been a little rough, kind of hard to get a job when you're visually impaired... thought I'd come back to this old haunt and see if you might have any work for an old head who knows the business." Vince smiled a wry smile to himself. "Not at the moment." Vince replied, unable to resist the desire to get in one last shot in "But I'll keep an eye out for you." Vince didn't notice Hanson's fists clench so tight that his palms began to bleed from the pressure his finger nails were exerting, the man who he'd been responsible for disabling turned and walked away but as he did the past few seconds worth of exchange produced an idea on Vince's head. "Wait!" Vince cried as Hanson turned back "Maybe I do have something for you to do, people around here will definitely remember you as one of the best up and comers of all time. How about I give you tickets to the next couple of events... front row seats and we'll have certain wrestlers pay tribute to you?" Hanson's eyes lit up "Sure... that'd be great Vince, I don't know how to thank you." Making his way toward Vince, Hanson extended a hand. Vince looked down at it and saw the blood dripping from the tiny cuts Hanson had made. "Not a handshake." Vince said patting him on the shoulder "Now go and find your seat... show starts soon." "Thanks again!" Hanson said as he paced down the corridor and disappeared. "Who was that guy?" A voice said from behind Vince, startling him for a second as he turned to see his son Kellen clutching the WWC championship in hand. "Oh... that guy? He's one of the props for the segment your about to do... remember how we talked about you turning heel and insulting some crowd members, he's going to be in front row... now by all means pick out some other people but don't overblow it because we don't want a lawsuit. When you're ready to really tear into someone pick him out, he's in on it." Kellen smiled "Great, are we still going with the music that I suggested?" "Yes... should make a nice change to what you were using before, now go on... get out there and do your stuff!" Vince delivered a reassuring couple of pats to Kellen's back and his son went on his way, feeling good about his role as champion for the first time. He had a new direction and his father finally seemed to be listening to his ideas again. Kellen made his way down the corridors and reached the curtains... waiting for a few seconds for his new theme music to queue up. "Outshined" by Soundgarden. He stepped out onto the air and over exuberantly thrust his title belt into the air, he wasn't getting much of a reaction from those in attendance but he remained confident that once he'd given them a taste of the new Kinkade, he'd get a reaction bigger and better than ever before. Kinkade grabbed a microphone and rolled under the ropes, pausing for a few seconds even though the crowd weren't really making any substantial noise. "Alright, simmer down people... the champion has something to say." Kinkade began as the crowd murmured with confusion given that they weren't drowning him out in the first place "I bet you've all been wondering just where I've been?" A silent crowd didn't give Kinkade much to work with. "I knew you had, so to fill you in... I took a few weeks out to really make the most of my championship benefits. Limo rides, champagne and plenty of women, it's good to be the champ people... ...that is until you have to come back to a shit hole like New Jersey." The roof in the small venue literally blew off, furious fans venting their frustration toward the hometown champion with a chorus of abuse. Kinkade dodged several items from the food stall that'd been hurled in his direction and moved on. "Just the sort of reception I expected from a bunch of nickel and dime Jersey folk, you know I did some thinking while I was away... I don't really belong here amongst you people anymore. I mean I never really did in the first place; I feel like a victim of circumstance as I sit here and look across the crowd tonight... a handsome man in ugly town. We've got some fat chick wearing a tent as a dress over there. Some bald dude who's toupee would've been more convincing if he'd come here tonight with a dog on top of his head. And check this freak out in front row!" Kinkade added, pointing out a figure standing at the very front of the crowd. John Hanson. "So what's up my man... I'm guessing you're lost because the pirate night fancy dress party is a couple of buildings down from here." The champion remarked as the intense booing suddenly turned to robust laughter. Suddenly everyone was turning to face the man wearing an eye patch in front row "That's right people... get a good look at squints down here, keep an eye on him in fact because I'm pretty sure he's got an eye on you!" The laughter continued to echo through the arena as Kinkade clutched his stomach and unleashed a cackle deep from the pit of it. Meanwhile on the outside Hanson twitched nervously... his fingernails once again cutting into the palms of his hands inside a furiously clenched fist. "You gonna let us know what the score is with the patch? Or is a talking parrot going to land on your shoulder to reveal all, captain?" Kinkade added, but Hanson had already turned and started to make his way out of the arena "Looks like he's out of here, maybe someone caught his eye!" Kinkade made his way toward the ropes and started to step through them "That's all for now, good night and god damn every one of you!" he roared as "Outshined" by Soundgarden hit again and he made his way to the back... ...oblivious to the hole he'd dug himself into.
