In the Garden Of Good and Evil / The Morning (Mid)

31 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Click Here to read the previous section *** "You will sit, and you will listen, do you hear me Steven?" Greg Douglas, the father of a man who would grow up to be one of the most famous faces stood hovering over his ten year old son. Lately a fire had grown within the boy, he had begun to change, there was a darker side to him, "I'm sick and tired of this shit you're pulling Steve, both your mother and I have had enough and we are putting a stop to it now." "Fuck you!" Steve shouted at the top of his lungs and turned to walk away. Greg reached out and grabbed Steve hard by his arm causing him to moan in pain and they both knew his arm would bruised later. But Greg didn't care, his son had to be disciplined, he jerked Steve around so that he was facing him, "You will listen Steve! And don't you ever disrespect me!" Steve snorted and brought the slimy snot into his mouth, he formed it into a ball and spat it into his father's face, it slapped hard against Greg's cheek. But what revolted Greg the most was, the feeling of it on his hand as he wiped it off. Greg did not think in his next moment, his hand whipped back and came hard across Steve's face. Steve was sent hard to the ground. Greg face turned from anger to horror, however, then back to anger, "Is that what you wanted Steve? To be treated like another man? Well I can do that, if you want." Steve looked up at him and smiled, he liked the pain, "Fuck you, I hope you die." Greg would continue to beat Steve for another twenty minutes. He was merely conditioning him to be a man. To discipline him. Like all children should be. *** Steve opened his eyes, he had temporarily fell asleep on the reclining chair in his room. He had never remembered what he dreamt actually happening. And he wondered, was it real? The time spoke of it being 10 a.m., the Morning was not yet over, and he had a session to attend with his therapist. This should be interesting. "Indeed." Are you planning on being honest? "Of course not." Good, because we don't want to be locked up again. Don't do that to us Steve. "Are you kidding me? I'd never do such a thing, the doc will think I'm Jesus by the end of the week." Such a smart boy you are Steve.
