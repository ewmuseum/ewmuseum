In Between Time(pt. 3)

4 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
- In Between Time pt. 3 - Lucky inhaled deeply as he cut his eyes in the direction of the luxurious apartment building just before him. He couldn't help but wonder why this was so... hard. He had been here several times ... even lived here for awhile. He approached the apartment building, slowly opening it's entrance door, and beginning his journey, three stories in heaven's direction. He paused for a moment ... and, reached inside his gym bag for something. Cologne. Sean Stevens was Lucky's best friend in the entire world. So much so, that he lifted his spirit's when things were going good, but also told him things he didn't want to hear when they were going bad. Smoking ... atleast when it came to Luck, puffing the cancer stick ... was bad. No, very bad. He sprayed himself with 'Animale' twice. One ... to rid himself of the stench that had followed him inside the building as a result of another one of his intimate sessions with a cigarette. Two ... so Sean wouldn't get in his face, for his intimate session with another cigarette, after he promised he'd stop. He made his way to the top ... took a moment to catch his breath ... then, knocked. "Who is it?" "Sean, it's me ... Shannon." ------ An hour later. The night went smoothly. The two friends caught up ... shared a couple of laughs ... and, reminisced all in one hour's worth of conversation, and a plate or two of spaghetti, that Ivy - Sean's girlfriend - made him a couple of days earlier(that he never quite finished, because it's all she knows how to cook ... which makes it, pretty much, ALL she cooks). Lucky stared down at his plate for a moment ... then, smirked, as his eyes met Sean's. "I honestly never thought it'd work when I first found out you and Ivy got together. I'm jealous. What's your secret?" Sean scratched his head. "No secret ... we just ... do it. Have you ever been tired of just ... talking about something? Back when we were in XWL, HEW, and TCW ... I had fun doing my thing ... but, after a while it just got..." "Tiring?" Lucky interrupted. "I was gonna say 'old' ... but, tiring works. What about you? I can't wait to dislike your chick, like you did mine ... you think she'd let me shove a glass bottle in her leg?!?" Lucky paused for a moment ... and, Sean didn't rush the conversation. He decided to let his friend tell his story in the manner in which he wanted. "Nobody ... really worth mentioning, I don't think. Me? ...I'm still living the life we lived way back when. Fucked up part is ... I'm not even a wrestler ... OR, a bodyguard for that matter!" Steven shot his arms in the air, submissively. "To each his own. Just because it got tiring to me, doesn't necessarily have to mean the same for yo--" "But, that's the thing ... the shit does get tiring. You think I like living like this? This hotel this night ... that hotel another. I wanna get my life together, Sean. And, I mean it this time. And ... that's ... sorta why I came here. To Orlando, I mean." Triple X's eyebrows arched, as he listened to his closest friend. Luck rubbed his chin as he continued. "The reason I'm here ... in good shape ... is because I've been here for the past two months. Hitting the weight room ... sparring. Getting my wind back." He looked Stevens directly in the eye. Sean's opinion about what was coming out of his mouth next, was very important to him. "I wanna wrestle. And, not just be wasted potential. When you come into a city ... you get first class treatment ... dinner at the finest restaurants. Shit, the CSWA even did a story on your life! The ... C-S-W-A, Sean! You're making moves ... and, no ... I don't want to get in your way, or slow you down ... but, I want to be there with you. And, I wanna make a wave or two myself, while I'm at it." Stevens paused, and took it all in. "...you sure? It's tough, Luck. I won't lie. Way harder than when we were kids. The business has evolved. In a MAJOR way." Lucky interrupted. "Look how small I've gotten. I'm dedicated, man. And, I'm bigger than most ... stronger than the average guy. I think I can do something good for wrestling. And, besides ... I was asking your opinion. I wasn't asking you." A moment of silence. Sean broke the tension. "I respect that. Do what you gotta do, to get what you need to get. But, don't take it lightly. And, don't get in over your head. That's all I'm saying." Lucky sighed. "If i didn't think I could do it ... I wouldn't. You've gotta know that, Sean. Trust me. I'll be alright." "I believe you." ------ The very next morning. Shannon Davis left his best friends apartment a new man. He went in ... his protege'. But, he honestly felt like the toughest part of becoming a wrestler was over with. If Sean 'Triple X' Stevens believed in him ... he certainly could make it. Because Sean had seen it all, done it all, and faced some of the toughest sons- of- bitches in the industry. It was a cool morning ... but, Lucky paid it no mind. He couldn't have been happier, as he stood there waiting for his cab to arrive. He would go to XWL studios today ... train for about six hours ... grab a bite to eat, then return to the apartment, where Sean told him he could stay until he found a place nearby. Then, in about three weeks or so ... he'd give Edward Brown a call. Let him get a look at him. Have a possible meeting or two. Because Ed worked with Luck in the past, he wouldn't have to fill out all the paperwork, or worry about a try out. It was a 'yes' or 'no' situation ... but, something told him ... the answer would be a resounding 'yes'. Rumor had it ... Ed got off on signing 'big men'. Life couldn't get any better... "Hey stanger..." Davis turned his attention in the direction of the voice, as his heart almost leaped out of his chest. It was her - the hooker he had slept with a couple of weeks ago. The woman he had threw out of his room in frustration. The woman he had been thinking about ever since. "Don't shoot," Lucky pleaded, worried that the beautiful woman standing before him remembered how rude he was. "Look, lady ... Before you curse me out, I want to apologize. My head was fucked up that night. Really fucked up." She smiled. "I know. You told me remember? Even cried!" "No I didn't!" "Yeah, you did!" Davis began turning red ... as he extended his hand. The lady accepted. "I don't think we introduced ourselves properly last time. I'm Shannon. As in Shannon Dohery - the crazy chick off of Beverly Hills 90210. And, you are?" "...Michelle. Like the little girl off of 'Full House'. Except ... I have a much more interesting life story. Atleast I think. "I'd be inclined to agree." Suddenly, his cab pulled up. "Where were you headed, Michelle?" She responded quickly. "Uptown ... to the grocery store. Gotta get lunch for my son for school next week." "Well, if you let me join you ... I have a cab here, that we can share." "Nevermind. I have a car..." She pulled out a set of keys and pressed the alarm button on the silver, '2001 Toyota Avalon' in the parking lot. "It's not much ... but, it gets me around." She smiled. "I bet it does ... I bet it does. Well ... my friend lives in that building over there." He pointed toward Sean's building. "I'll be staying with him for a while, when I'm not on the road. I'd like to have dinner with you sometime, my treat." "I'll think about it..." she shot back quickly. "How will I know what your answer?" She grinned, slighly... "Trust me ... you will." She began her walk toward her car, as Lucky looked on. "I think I'm in love."
