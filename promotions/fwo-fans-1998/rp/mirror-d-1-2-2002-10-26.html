Mirror'd, 1.2

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brandon rubs back the short red-brown hair atop his head. He winces as his hand passes over the fresh bump on the back of his head. He then passes his fingers lightly over his forehead. The two-inch gash above his eye was still swollen from the few stitches it took to close it. When it happened it looked worse than it was. The sweat made it pour over his face like a faucet but in reality he had been hurt much worse mentally. Ric Chronos had proved to be a stiff competitor and Brandon chided himself for continuously looking past him. Whenever he did he paid for it in the ring. Chronos was amazing in the ring and as much as Brandon pushed, Chronos pushed back just as hard. But when he compared Ric to his real target he was hard pressed to keep his focus on the matters at hand. Chronos had decided to be a roadblock on his way to Creed. If he wanted to be such a fool and put himself between two forces on a collision course, then so be it. He couldn't respect someone like Chronos though, the way Chronos conducted himself prevented that. Brandon stepped onto the cold tile floor and flicked on the heat lamp embedded into the drop ceiling. He looked closely into the lighted mirror at the new stitches and then to the cut at the corner of his mouth. He then looked down at the small indentations in his abdomen and on his arm. The fact that he was tanning from the summer sun just helped highlight the four year old gunshot wounds. Standing there he began to take a survey of all the visible markings his life has given him. They were nothing though, really. He was young and just starting in this business. He wouldn't trade any of it if he could; he slept well knowing he was doing what he should. "Look at me world!" Brandon boasted to himself. "See that no matter what I can't be broken. It doesn't matter if I go down in the history of the fWo, just so long as I never stand down!" Brandon nodded with his thoughts. He smiled as if looking into the eyes of his family. He knew that he had reasons, reasons never to simply walk away. He would show them that. He had done a damn good job of it so far. Chronos, Creed, and even the Boston Strangler had to realize now that he was strong enough to get the job done, both in and out of the ring. They had to look at him as the young lion that was ready to challenge the established foundation and rock it to its core. This wasn't about respect; it wasn't about what Brand Frontier wanted. It was about what was right. He was the Irresistible Force... he would not be stopped. He came into the fWo with but one goal. It wasn't what Ric Chronos thought it was, either. It had nothing to do with the World Title. Not just yet, that is. He didn't care if people doubted his cause or his reasons; this wasn't a popularity contest, no matter what Chronos thought.
