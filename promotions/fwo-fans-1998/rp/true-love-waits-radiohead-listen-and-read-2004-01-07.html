True Love Waits - Radiohead, Listen and Read

7 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This story is done, I have finish the series, and it has left many people questioning where exactly I was really going with it. Well at first I had no idea how I was going to end it nor did I know which direction. After much thought and realizing how much I had tied in religion I came to the conclusion that I had to base the story on the fact that... 1 - Steve Douglas was the host body of Lucifer, who haunted him in an image of Kenny Rock And 2- Angel Dalton was the reborn messiah and the second coming of Jesus Christ himself. ------------- So we had good vrs evil. Now I wanted to make sure that I made Douglas a product of society, that the reason he had been driven to hate humans so much is the fact that we had rejected him. That we had judged him and thrown him away once the fire we saw in him had withered. And if you notice, it happens to people all the time, someone becomes popular and then their friends turn on them once the persons spark has faded. However it was really important for me to also establish the fact that Steve Douglas had done to other what had been done to him (like ruining Kenny Rock's career, or framing Prod for rape). So in turn it was bitter karma that had actually caught up to Douglas and instead of accepting responsibilities for his own actions and mistakes he turned on everyone else and blamed them for his misery. This made a prime character for the devil himself to cut a deal with. So I made it that after Kenny shot himself in front of Steve this pretty much sent Steve on such a low level of his inner psyche that he was able to speak to Lucifer however only in the form of Kenny Rock. Of course the devil wants to crush God and what better way to do it than to convince a mortal to blow up a whole bunch of them and frame terrorist, cause a world war and then ka-boom Lucifer was laughing in victory. ------------ Angel Dalton however came out of Dallas and when I was made to write for him I based him on the fact that he was torn. He hated the world he grew up in and had only been given tragedy the whole way through, so why help a world that sucked? However as he went along he would realize how important life was and all that jazz and how he was the only one that could save it. Now that meant that all obstacles like people believing him that he had been sent from God was completely unrealistic and that would be a constant weight on him. Angel had to save everyone that in turn thought he was the ultimate fool and crazy. Unfortunately Dallas only wrote a few segments/RP's along the way as he was out of constant contact with me so I wrote AD the best that I could, overall I feel comfortable with what I did. ----------------- Now as for the content I wrote in the story itself. Here's the thing, I realized long ago that if I was going to do the whole Good vrs Evil/Heaven vrs Hell/ End of The World sha-bang that I would need to go all out. So I figured Douglas being the ultimate evil he had to be able to gain some form of power, to make him stronger than just the average human. That's where the cannibalism came in and the gory details of him eating his enemies. I wanted him to really come across as the sickest thing you'd ever see. In order to match that power that Douglas had I made it so that Noah Reily chosen mission was to die again and join his soul with Dalton so that Dalton could match the strength of Douglas. That's what happened there, and I'm a huge fan of shock value so that's why I had Reily die so "easily". As for the altering of time (during the chase scene in my series) where I had the whole world stop except for Dalton/ Douglas I made that kind of left up to you on what to believe there but my feeling was that it was actually the "higher above" who had stopped time as if you notice Dalton was quite surprised when this occurred. And to be honest who are you to say that this is not possible? It could be. Now as for the ending, I didn't want to do the cheesy death where the wrestler dies in some kind of tough guy ending. I wanted to make it so that Douglas was really just a pathetic example of a person who in the end learned nothing and grew into nothing that was greater. I wanted him to die as horrible as he was and I knew I had to kill Dalton to do it. After all he is Jesus Christ, so therefore in order to save us he has to die for us. Right? (wink) So in order to accept life, he had to accept death. Now, the whole them not existing has puzzled a lot of you and also bothered a lot of you. Well the way I look at it is this...of course my writing is going to be there and of course there are matches that have been written with my characters.
