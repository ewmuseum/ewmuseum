The Untold Story(Part 10 - Final Post)

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"When it rains it pours." Sean Stevens ran up his apartment stairs as fast as he could, with visions of stopping a fire dancing in his head - that AND saving what's left of his relationship with Ivy McGinnis. As Sean entered the smoke- filled apartment, he forgot one important piece... the woman standing in his living room, covered by nothing more than a trench coat. Johns - "Don't worry... I turned the oven off." Stevens shot her an annoyed glance. Stevens - "You think you could've did it BEFORE we smoked the building out?" Johns - "I'm sorry, Sean... but, I was in the bathroom. I could hardly smell anything until I came out here. All the noise you and Ivy were making... I was sure somebody would call the police... I had to borrow a shirt and some jeans from your closet. I'll return it." Stevens - "Keep it. No offense, Linda... but you have been nothing but TROUBLE. Because of you my apartment is smoking - as a result... I might get evicted. And, I may have lost the love of my life. The gentlemen in me thinks I should let you stay the night... y'know... 'til the rain stops." Sean entered his kitchen, and picked up his cordless off the hook. "But, the desperate boyfriend in me says you gotta go. I'll call you a cab to your hotel." Johns - "But... bu--" Stevens - "Goodbye, Linda." Sean turned his back to her... and, as a result... she left the apartment, and began her wait in the lobby. Sean dialed ten digits on his phone... but, it wasn't the cab company... it was Ivy's cell phone number. She didn't answer. But, he knew better. All he wanted was her voicemail... to leave her a message. To let her know how he felt. beeeeeeeeeeeeeep "Ivy... this is Sean. I know you probably don't want to hear from me. That's fine. I can accept that. And, if you don't want to be with me anymore... as a result of something I did... I can accept that, too. But, what I can't and won't accept is you leaving me because of Linda. "Ivy, Listen... you are the best thing to happen to me, since... Lucky." he smiled. "Okay... you're even BETTER than Lucky. If I lost you because of something I didn't do... I'd be destroyed. I'm not asking you to take me back... I'm asking you to think. Think about everything that went down... how it went down... and, if you can honestly say you think I cheated on you... then, you can go. I'll never pursue you again. "But, Ivy... I know you know better than that. You KNOW me. And, know that I'd NEVER do anything like that to you. I'm sorry for everything that's gone down... Please forgive me, and please honor me by being my wife. "I love You." He hung up. ****** The Next Morning. It had stopped raining completely... and, the sun was shining. Sean sat at the window nearest his balcony thinking. Hoping his phone would ring. Hoping Ivy still wanted to be with him. Hoping things could be like they were. Sean had done everything he could to see to it that it was... ... now... It was up to Ivy Lillian McGinnis.
