The Winding Road To Love (Part 5)

17 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With a soft breeze whipping through the cool night air, a long black limo pulls up in front of a nice restaurant, the name being hidden slightly by the people passing by on the sidewalk. The driver opens his door, walks around to the side near the sidewalk, and slowly opens the passenger door. A few long moments pass, as the driver starts to look around nervously. Normally, when he walked around and opened the passenger door, the passenger or passengers stepped out of the limo. This time no one was stepping out. Wanting to make sure everything was alright, the driver leaned down and peered into the backseat. "What the fuck are you doing?" yelled a voice from the backseat. As the driver jumped back startled, a pair of men in suits emerged from the limo. With his blonde hair pulled back, Monte Burns turned to look at his friend Lance Fortune and just laughed. "Can you believe this guy?" he said, gesturing to the driver. As Fortune chuckled, Burns turned his attention back to the driver. "You are a limo driver. You drive the limo. Nowhere in your god damn contract does it say anything about looking into the backseat when passengers are there. Get some manners or else you'll be out of a job." "Yes, Mr. Burns," said the driver, while looking down at the sidewalk. Shaking his head, Burns turns towards the restaurant and walks to the door, where Fortune stands waiting for him. The two men walk inside the restaurant and stand waiting to be seated. As they wait for one of the hosts to come by, Fortune looks over at Burns and shrugs his shoulders. "What was the big deal back there?" he asked his friend. "I mean, I know that's a pet peeve and all, but why the big blowup on the limo driver?" "He was being a jackass, Lance!" Burns said with a bit of annoyance. "Someone needed to teach him some manners." "Monte, come on," Fortune said rolling his eyes. "That was a little over-the-top for just a little mistake. I know there's got to be something else on your mind." "Well, the guy honestly did piss me off," Burns said with a sigh, "but I've been thinking a lot about this whole Tempest thing lately. Even when I went out there on Sunday, I wasn't really sure I wanted to do it. I mean, I honestly care about her, but sometimes I'm not sure if it would be worth it. I think..." Just as Burns was about to say something else, the door to the restaurant opened...and in walked Tempest. Immediately, the look of concern that had been on Monte's face disappeared and was replaced by a big smile. Fortune saw the smile on Monte's face and turned to look as well, seeing what had drawn Monte's gaze. "Well, speak of the little fox..." said Burns with a cocky sneer. "She's quite the catch, Monte," chimed in Fortune as he looked Tempest up and down. "She's not caught yet, Lance," said Burns with a glance. "Not just yet..."
