Brief Interlude Before Warfare.

9 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Brief Interlude Before Warfare. February 09, 2000 :::The scene opens with a side shot of the parking lot at the Georgia Dome in Atlanta, Georgia. From the lesser-than-average number of cars, such as a particularly ugly old Buick, visible in the lot, it's obvious that this is the talent parking lot. The sound of a large, powerful engine can be heard in the background, and is soon identified as that of a blue and white 1999 Dodge Viper, trademark car of none other than Perfect. It cruises into the parking lot, before finding a spot right next to the building. The engine is shut down, and after a moment, Perfect emerges from the driver's side door, in a shirt reading "Trade Griffey Now!", an FTfWo leather jacket, and a pair of sweat pants. He carries a large leather duffel bag in one arm. As he begins walking towards the arena, a ringing can be heard from inside the bag. Unperturbed, Perfect takes a few steps back to the car, sets the bag on the hood, and removes the cell phone from inside.::: Yeah? Excuse me - you said it's who? Really? Well, what do you want? You heard our invitation, I see... well, I gotta grant you it makes a lot of sense, you wanting this opportunity. I'm assuming you're in town, then... Oh, that's no big deal if you're a little late. We can handle those idiots two-on-three anyway. Anyway, good to have you aboard. You know the fWo arrangements and all. And on a personal note, I've always respected your talent. Good, good, great. I'll tell the D-Man to tell Hell he has the night off... and I guess I'll see you at the show. Oh, wait, wait. Before you go, what about that whole thing with the time limit? Good point. We are FTfWo after all... screw their rules. Yeah, yeah. This is great. Again, good to have you aboard. They'll never know what hit 'em. Alright. See you at the show. :::Perfect flips the cell phone off with a click.::: Oh, this is rich... :::He tosses the phone back into the duffel bag, zips it up, tosses it over his shoulder, and continues on his way into the arena as we fade to black.:::
