Whisper

20 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday July 16th, 2003 "Sorry about that..." Amy returned to a table that had her staring across to Brandon Roche. July 16th...birthday celebrations, since the next day was in fact her 21st birthday...but also an Uprising, so it was best to enjoy time without any possible bruises or bloody patches. So far in the day, not much had been done - And that was entirely fine, Amy'd been physically "Under par" lately as far as feeling healthy went, so the rest was welcome. What wasn't, however, was the need to excuse herself in the middle of a meal to get sick about three times already this week. "Fish never really agreed with me...I guess I've got a little stomach bug or something." She mumbled, sitting back down. "You look great, though...at least one of us is enjoying it." "Well, I think you still look amazing, even with the bloodshot eyes." Brandon smiled, he was a slick operator and he knew it. The look on Amy's face spoke volumes though on his perceived 'slickness'. The slight smile turned in to a toothy grin as he continued to wrestle with his half eaten flounder. Between mouthfuls he continued, "Well, what can you expect though sweetheart? Not to turn this into a sermon Deacon would be proud of but you need to take better care of yourself. If you keep getting sick like that you'll end up dehydrated and falling on your face in the ring." Realizing that he was doing exactly what he had said he didn't want to do Brandon simply looked at Amy Campbell as she had picked up her fork and was teasing the dead fish on the edge of her plate. "But anyway, now that you've taken care of business again... what say we try our hand with a small piece of cake? Then we can move on to the presents!" "NO." Realizing her reaction was a bit too strong, Amy repaced herself. "Err...sorry, no. No cake for me...I can hardly look at food right now, but presents sure do sound nice." She winked. Engulfing the last bit of filet from his plate Brandon spoke with a slightly full mouth, "Then presents it is." Signaling to the waitress she immediately ducked behind the curtain as if on cue. Moments later she returned with two discreetly wrapped boxes. With the bows crinkling under her touch she laid them on the edge of the table and retrieved the nearly full plate from in front of the birthday girl, who watched her walk back with a chuckle. "Jeez, Brandon...you didn't overlook a single thing." Brandon only smiled and nodded towards the gifts...and, despite their surroundings, Amy quickly forgot her manners. Wrapping paper flew and the first box came open with a red face and a jolt back shut - Not to mention an eyebrow raised at the Mighty Hero, who leaned back with a cocky smile, wink and no real comment. The second, however, found the redhead nearly jumping OVER the table to wrap arms around Brandon's bulky neck and shoulders. "Summer Sanitarium in Norfolk?!" She smiled with a gaping mouth and eyes just as wide. "I'll be hard pressed to make up for that one, BL." "Well, start saving your money," He winked, "You've only got until October." The Paragon quickly, gently let her sit back down and unwrap the third - A rather pricey, somewhat "un-Aimz" but still appropriately dainty ruby and gold necklace. Her thoughts first went to his bank account, but quickly sprinted back towards keeping herself as poised and "not girly" as possible. "Holy shit...will I ever need to start saving." The smile began to make her face sore, but violently refused to diminish. "You're...well...whoa..." Amy laid the necklace on the table, still open and to the side as she lovingly looked in to the gray-blue eyes of her lover. He too returned her stare, remarking to himself how the fire dancing in the rubies paled in comparison to flames smoldering in his heart for her. Things had taken so many twists and turns that he never really and truly knew where he was, but the one thing that he had grown to count on was that Amy Campbell was his center. He had also started thinking of the very real possibility of spending the rest of his life with her, the necklace itself almost a test to see her reaction over something such as it being presented to her on behalf of Brandon Roche. It had gone well he reasoned as he reached out and grasped her hand. She twinged a bit and glanced out the corner of her eye, catching herself being uneasy again at a show of affection between the two now that all eyes were on them from her display. "But I can think of a few things that can be done for repayment long before October that don't require saving a penny." He said, glancing at the first box that had brought red to the raver's face. Swiftly collecting her breath, Amy steadied a shaking hand that she soon raised alongside her brow. "That was almost the first time you'd have made me cry in public." She began, still trying to supress tears and steady her voice - The wink that followed helped. "But this could very well be the millionth time you've tempted me to slap you in public." "But..." The redhead smiled, eyebrow still raised. "That was a pretty expensive looking necklace...shall we?" Was her finish, glancing to the coat check. Nearly stumbling over himself, Brandon's napkin fell to the floor out of his lap as he quickly pushed his chair away from the table, grabbing Amy's hand and the presents in one swoop they were three steps away before anyone even noticed. "Yes, we shall." As they paid the check and made off to the car, Amy glanced to the smallest box storing the little necklace and then back up to her mighty hero. "Y'know, half of the time I don't even think I deserve you. I mean, like someday....maybe, y'know...uhh...so what I'm thinking is..well...I'm not exactly the most practical choice for mother of the year of cover of 'Modern Bride', and you're still sticking by me despite everything." Brandon watched her with an amused, quizzical expression before even thinking of starting the car, so Amy took it as a cue to finish her rather scattered, uncharted point. "Just that...like...well, thanks 'n stuff. And sorry I'm not the best with my emotions." The last apology, in her mind, made up for a happy mist quickly building back up in her mismatched eyes. "Amy... I look at you every day and don't see what I've done to deserve someone like you. You're porcelain to my clay. I just wish you would realize how great you really are, I never said I was looking for perfection though, I was given you though and I think that is as close as anyone will ever get." Tears welling in his eyes as he spoke, Brandon's voice broke at the end. He stroked back her hair and found himself staring all over again. With a quick kiss, stroking his hair back herself, Amy leaned back. "Let's just get somewhere alone together, or the people in the parking lot are gonna get one hell of a show, and I don't exactly feel like sharing your tears with anybody else." Brandon grinned almost sheepishly and nodded, setting the car off on its path back to the hotel. This was the first decent birthday in a while. Albeit, there was a day left to it - but life was finally coming together. They'd both moved past Kellen Kinkade's mind games, and Amy'd somehow managed to divert any rage toward's Brandon's ex-wife for nearly a month. Things seemed calm and routine, but in an interesting way... ...She wasn't settling down, was she? [This has been an Aimz/ Brand Frontier collaboration.]
