Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 4 - Part Two

18 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FLASHBACK to August 18th, 2002 Richard Hughes sat in his office, behind his desk and his eyes stared hard at the screen of his computer. The lights were off, as Richard found it more soothing when the only light of the room was that which blared off of his screen. It was ten o'clock and everyone had left hours ago, but Richard was adamant on finding out what that symbol was that they had found on the sheet. The phone rang. Richard jumped slightly. He moved slowly and by the fourth ring he picked up the receiver and held the phone close to his ear. "Hello" Richard spoke to the phone with a sense of exhaustion in his tone. An excited voice hit Richard hard and it hurt his ear, "Hey Rich!" Richard held the phone away an inch away from his ear and the voice continue, "Hey Rich it's Thomas." Richard sighed, "Hey Thomas." Thomas still excited replied quickly, "Look Rich, I found out what that symbol is. It's the symbol for a goat's head." Richard leaned forward in his seat, "What?" Thomas continued, "Yeah, bring up the definition for what a goat's head symbolizes." Richard typed quickly on his keyboard and waiting for the web page to load. As Richard waited, Thomas rambled on, "Look Rich, if you follow the shape of it you'll notice it resembles the shape of a pentagram." "What?" Richard said suddenly, "What are you trying to say?" The web page began to load and Richard began to read what the definition was, Thomas continued to speak quickly into his ear, "Rich, it's the symbol for Lucifer...Satan... you know, Mr. Fallen Angel, I think were dealing with some kind of cult killing or sacrifice here because it's something crazy if a symbol like that is left hanging around." Richard nodded, "Thanks Thomas, I'll see ya tomorrow." There was a pause, "Be careful Rich." "Yep," Richard said lightly, "Have a good night." Richard Hughes then hung up the phone and began to read the rest of the web page on his screen. It was when his phone rang again, that Richard looked at the receiver. He waited a few more moments before finally picking the phone back up. "Hello?" Richard said into the receiver, however he could hear nothing. Richard listened closely and still no sound came, until almost a weak sound of someone crying. "Hello? Who is this?" The sound of crying grew louder, and suddenly he heard a man say"Please don't," and immediately he hung up. However, what truly disturbed Richard was that, the sound of the man crying out... was his own voice. It would be a few moments of sudden fear before Richard would jump again, the phone was ringing once again. Richard moved even slower and picked up the phone and bringing the receiver up to his ear even slower than the previous times. Richard waited for a few moments, trying to reach inside of himself, to gather strength just to say "Hello". However it was the voice of Thomas that broke the silence, "Hey Rich." Never before had Richard felt such a sense of relief, "Oh hey Thomas." Thomas spoke quickly, "Look I just wanted to remind you about tomorrow, we have lunch with the Chief." Richard nodded again, "Yeah I know. See ya." Hughes hung up the phone and let lose a long deep breath, Hughes then proceeded to give way to a small laugh in which he felt he truly deserved, maybe staying late at the office was a bad idea. Not getting any rest had taken it's toll on his body, he was hallucinating phone calls. "Laugh all you wish Detective Hughes." Richard was immediately up and out of his chair, staring at a man who stood in the shadows at the edge of his doorway. "Who are you?" Richard demanded. The man moved in closer, the faint light washed against his face to reveal what once was a man. Now a monster, with hardly any flesh on his face looked hard at him, Richard saw the muscles in the man's open cheeks twitch, almost so that he was smiling, "Please Detective," the monster spoke, "Have a seat in your chair. I'd hate for you to feel uncomfortable." Richard Hughes remained standing and the monster grew angry, "SIT DOWN!" The monster demanded and stepped forward. Hughes abided by his orders and slowly took a seat. The monster seemed to smile once again and his body relaxed, "Thank you Detective."
