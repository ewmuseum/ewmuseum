fWo Home Video presents Death

29 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**Thanks for purchasing this fWo Home Video product!** **The first few bars of "Rage" by Savatage play, only to be replace mid-line by "Burning" by Manowar... the opening credits play.** **Suddenly we cut to a studio, complete with two chairs and a tv set. Seated is Scott Collins, the second man to bear the name Death in the fWo. He's dressed in a white suit, with sunglasses, and appears to be fielding questions from one of the fWo Home Video staffers.** fWo Home Video: Mr. Collins, thanks for agreeing to do this tape. Death: Yeah. Lemme get this straight, you're going to show clips of my matches, and I'm gonna talk about 'em? fWo Home Video: Essentially correct, Mr. Collins. Let's start with your early career. You wrestled at the first fWo event, in one of the more heavily hyped matches of the night. Death: Yeah, I remember. Me and Clarence against Dudley and Briana. Then we got turned on by Falcon and Ripper. Whole thing was no contest. I'm still pissed I didn't get the fued with Dudley after Cyberslam. fWo Home Video: Really? You seemed perfectly happy to wrestle at a lower level on the cards... Death: Yeah, sure, and you're happy you have this (censored by fWo Home Video) job, too. fWo Home Video: We don't have footage of that match, of course, it didn't even really start. Death: Yeah. What's next? fWo Home Video: Later that year you wrestled Austin Davis for the Internet Championship... Death: Had to beg the (censored by fWo Home Video) commisioner to get that match. **A clip of the match is shown, with Scotty Cool celebrating with his then-friend Ultra-Violet post-match.** Death: I was getting to be the most popular figure in the fWo around that time. fWo Home Video: Uh, I don't know about that. Despite a long title run, you had a lot of easy defenses against fluff opponents. You only started to get popular after you lost that belt and started to team with Ultra-Violet in Ultra-Cool. A team, I must say, that many have called the greatest of all time... Death: Whatever. Clarence had zero to do with my popularity. I mean, I was so popular back then, I could change the color of my tights and it would pop a buyrate. fWo Home Video: Many have suggested that you... only got your chance to show off your skill in-ring BECAUSE you had a popular comedy wrestler like UV on your side. Death: What are you saying? You saying I rode UV's popularity to the top? fWo Home Video: (Pause) Let's move on. You lost the belt to the Strangler, then won a series of matches with UV at your side. **A fast-cut clip of Ultra-Cool matches, many ending with the shoulder-perch 450 splash the duo used to finish their opponents.** fWo Home Video: You were involved in some hilarious comedy segments involving a love quadrangle around Valentine's day that year... Death: It was a 'psuedo-shoot' thing. Backstage, Stone and I were getting along pretty well. At the time, I was a face, she was a heel. The suits said it would be funny if we got together onscreen. Goofy face and bitchy heel. fWo Home Video: We all know where that relationship went... Death: Stone's the greatest influence of my life. **A clip is shown of the Death unmasking himself and revealing Scotty Cool at Cyberslam '99... then the next night, the match between the new Death and Stone, ending in Stone turning on her students and joining the Four Dark Riders.** Death: It's funny. At the time, she was wearing a shirt that said '4' on it, but really, she was the first in our group. Besides maybe that jackass Taylor. fWo Home Video: Jackass? I take it his recent attempt at betrayal was not looked upon favorably? Death: You bet your ass it wasn't looked upon favorably, Spanky. fWo Home Video: Please, Mr. Collins, keep it civil. Death: Whatever. fWo Home Video: You lost your World Title to Ultra-Violet, after a brief reign. That match, documented with snapshots on your webpage, is considered by many to be your best ever. Death: It was a good match. I hit a lot of good moves. fWo Home Video: Of course, the rumor is that UV carried you through it. Death: (Pause) You might want to rethink that. fWo Home Video: Let's go to the clip! **The match in question is played in it's entirety, but certain spots stick out... UV kicks out of the Death Penalty, sitting straight up and shouting 'C!' just before the three count. Scotty fails to kick out of UV's Purple Piledriver, however.** fWo Home Video: Since then you've languished in fueds with Silver Fox and David Spencer, with your foes always one step ahead of you... Death: (quietly) I also fueded with Slugger... fWo Home Video: ...who was always one step ahead of you. **Death says nothing.** fWo Home Video: Your latest fued with Spencer... Death: (suddenly) I was screwed, you know that? fWo Home Video: What? Death: Spencer screwed me, the promotion screwed me. It's a big conspiracy against me, brother. Everyone's jealous at my popularity. fWo Home Video: Mr. Collins... you're one of the least liked men in the sport. Death: You know what I mean. Armstrong told me before that match, swore up and down, that it was taken care of. fWo Home Video: What? Death: Spencer was supposed to lay down. The lights were supposed to go out, and he was supposed to get ambushed in the meantime. Did any of that happen? No. I was screwed. fWo Home Video: Spencer's riders ran in on you, and you lost the match as a result. Let's see the clip. **A clip is shown of the recent Twilight pay per view. David Spencer is brutally applying three piledrivers to Scotty Cool....** Death: This (censored by fWo Home Video) promotion is kicking me to the wayside. They're tearing apart my image. I'm supposed to be this big bad guy, but they make sure to put me across as this whining egotist. fWo Home Video: Mr. Collins, that was part of your gimmick as a face... you were a narcissist. I remember some of your catch phrases, "I impress myself!" and, "It's all about me, baby!" Death: I deserve better treatment than this. I made alot of people's careers! Where the (censored by fWo Home Video) do you think Machina's career would be if I hadn't worked all those comedy matches with him and Rudo and UV? Where the (censored by fWo Home Video) do you think UV's (censored by fWo Home Video) career would be if I hadn't won all those (censored by fWo Home Video) matches for us? How many people have I put over at the top of the card? Dudley, Slugger, Frenchie... the list is long, Spanky! When's it my time? (censored by fWo Home Video)ing SPENCER comes back to town with the (censored by fWo Home Video)ing gimmick he (censored by fWo Home Video)ing GAVE me, and I wind up putting him over, after being TOLD I was gonna (censored by fWo Home Video)ing win the match? What the (censored by fWo Home Video) is that? fWo Home Video: Uh... Mr. Collins, please calm down... Death: You want calm, do 'ya, Spanky? fWo Home Video: Uh... yes, please... Death: I'll give you calm, (censored by fWo Home Video). **Death stands, and calmly grabs the tv that he was watching his match clips on. With calm strength, and calm grace, he hurls the tv at the wall, where it shatters.** Death: How's that for calm, Slimjim? You can take this video and shove it up your (censored by fWo Home Video). fWo Home Video: (outraged) SIR! What do you think you're doing? Death: I'm turnin' it around, you hear me? the fWo needs me. I'm the glue that holds the whole damn thing together. Without me, this place would be NOTHING. Mark my words... by Cyberslam, there's gonna be some changes around here....
