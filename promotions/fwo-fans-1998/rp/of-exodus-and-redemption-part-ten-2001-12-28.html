Of exodus and redemption (part ten)

28 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The gun fired and David Spencer screamed. The smoke drifted from the barrel, and Fallen pocketed it. He winced at the pain of the hot metal against his leg, and turned away from Spencer. "Jesus Christ... why? Why did you do that? You bastard... my leg... you've shot my leg..." Fallen turned and saw David Spencer clutching the wound on his leg. Blood poured through his fingers as he struggled to patch the wound as best he could. It was having little effect... and Fallen smiled at the sight of Spencer on the floor, tears pouring down his face and in complete agony. Fallen turned again and noticed a small framed box hanging off the main entrance hallway. Inside it was a dark black mask, plain. Beneath the frame was a small gold nameplate bearing one word and a date. "Death: 1997-1999" Fallen looked at the mask for a moment, surprised at how a simple piece of black cloth could mean so much. This was a symbol of so many conflicting things that Fallen was caught by the sheer awe of it all. The history... looping and looping back around like so many infinity symbols turned end on end. "We all wear masks in our own way... don't we David?" "You shot me you son of a bitch... you shot me!" David screamed at him, cursing and howling in pain between words. "You're going to jail... do you know that? You're going to prison and by God you're never going to get out... you sick freak... I'm calling the police right now..." Fallen sighed and punched his fist through the frame and withdrew the mask inside. He turned and regarded Spencer with something of pity and distaste. "No, you're not going to do that. I've been in your shed... I stopped there before I came to the front door. I saw what was in those crates... and I saw what it is that you've come to. Your wrestling school must not be doing very well at all for you to get involved in the drug trade. That must have been near two hundred kilos of cocaine you have out there... your wrestling school just a front for your pathetic attempt to skim a little more money off the blood of the innocent. You're a miserable man David Spencer... and you won't be calling the police. But with that wound to your leg, you won't be wrestling any more either. Your leg has been shattered... bone blown to bits. Even if they do get it so you can walk again you're never going to run or jump... let alone wrestle ever again. You're no hero... not anymore. And you never will be." David Spencer screamed then, and as Fallen walked down the hallway to the front door his wailing and cursing escorted him every step of the way. Finally as he had turned the door handle and was about to step outside he heard one last question drift down the hallway toward him. "Who... who are you?" Ryan Moore closed his eyes and the voice inside of him was silent. "I'm Fallen. And now David Spencer, so are you."
