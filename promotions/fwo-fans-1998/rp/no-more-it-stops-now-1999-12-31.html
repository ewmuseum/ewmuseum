No more, it stops now

31 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on a locker room in the back of the United Center. Mike Bear is watching the end of the second Countdown to Oblivion. The camera had just caught Joy in the ring, telling the Dark Riders to be more happy) Mike (chuckling): Yeah Joy! You tell him! We have the upper hand now! (However, Mike appears more worried as Joy runs up the ramp to confront Joy) Mike (concerned): Joy, man, you better watch yourself. Don't, oh no, he put the sticker on him. (As Mike watches the following events unfold, he becomes increasingly more concerned about Joy's well being, but when Joy lands and Fallen screams, all Mike can do is stand there, his mouth agape. A tear rolls down his cheek) Mike (in a weak voice): Ryan. He killed him. (Tears start to well up in Mike's eyes as he shuts them and grows angry. When he finally reopens them, several tears have already rolled down) Mike (crying and angry at the same time): Why Ryan? The man who didn't have a real enemy anywhere. The man who lived his life only to give others the happiness he felt everyday. Huh David? Why did you kill him? Does that somehow make you feel big? To take a someone so bright and full of life and to snap your fingers and have it taken away by one of your zombies!? HUH SPENCER?! HOW DO YOU SLEEP AT NIGHT?! Spencer, it ends right now. Nothing else matters. Not that title, nothing. You will be stopped. You will be brought to justice. You will not get away with this. Spencer, prepare for your end. (The camera fades out as Mike drops to his knees, crying, and asking why it had to be Ryan. The camera fades to a still of Ryan Moore, Joy, and then to black)
