Disillusionment: Book Three - Chapter II

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, let me say something, just in case you are as stupid as the Disciples. I am not guilty of what I have been charged of on national television. I don't go to the authorities, because THE FUCKING PRICKS WHO RAPED AND KILLED STEPHANIE ARE THE AUTHORITIES, JACK ASS! Let me walk you through this step by step, so you can understand. Officers that were working for the Disciples, who are the authority...not the police, the police are bullshit, the Disciples are the authority...raped and killed Stephanie because they knew...or thought, that I love her. They did it so that I would try to get revenge, which would make me very easy to capture. Once they realized that I was not going to fall into their trap, the Disciples said that I was the one who killed Stephanie, even though nothing could be further from the truth. But this step was successful, because now every Joe in the world is out to get me and their three million dollars. Is that so hard to understand? Yes, I ran, but what was I to do? I wasn't going to turn myself into the Disciples. The Disciples have no concept of life in prison. If I went to them and told them that they were wrong, and it was really their officers who raped and killed Stephanie, they would capture me, and they would torture me, and they would kill me. I am guilty of no crime committed against a regular civilian, however, I am a criminal...at least by the Disciples' standards. I have done everything in my power to make their lives a living hell, and they have done the same in return. But I have not, nor would I ever, committed rape and murder. I did consider suicide, though. Surely you remember, when I was staring down the barrel of my ten-millimeter. I couldn't do it, though. I had to keep going. To keep going for Burkenstock. To keep going for Stephanie. The Disciples have already taken their lives, and I want to prevent them from taking more. Ignorance is bliss. In your reality, the Disciples are a fictitious group that was conjured up in the crazy mind of a punk wrestler who thinks he's better than he is. In my reality, though, I know better. I've wondered if I'm crazy, and I think I might be, but the Disciples are not a product of my insanity. You live happy, knowing that he'll wake up tomorrow and the paper will be filled with the latest exploits in Washington, because you think that Washington even matters. You'll read about someone like me, someone who has angered the Disciples, but has been accused of other crimes, and you'll think that they got what they deserved, even though they were just working to free him. WHY DO YOU PEOPLE HATE US SO MUCH?!? All we want to do is destroy the organization that has you so brainwashed you don't even know they exist.
