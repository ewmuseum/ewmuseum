Introduction to a tale of a Hero of Justice.

28 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a story that is just like every other story. It's all about one thing, one simple thing, and how that one simple thing can cause such and effect that it is a tale that you can tell over and over again and still it will stay true to it's meaning. A story that compels you to hang on every word just as though you could envision yourself inside of it. This story is all about a girl... And the love of a boy... ------------------ Vancouver, Canada August 17th, 1979 96 degrees, Beautiful Sunny Day Cue in: "Staralfur" by Sigur Ros The sweat poured down Waylon's face, it was such a hot day on his ninth birthday and all that he could think about was drinking as much juice out of that juice bowl that his Mom had made before she could catch him doing it. Sure it was his birthday but she always wanted to make sure that the other kids had as much as they wanted, after all she wanted to try and make the other parents approve of her. No one liked her though, or Waylon for that matter, or his father, or his sister. Everyone hated the Percevals, they were the joke of the community, the natural born losers, the nobodies, the wannabes, you get my drift. The only reason why any parents had come today was to save face, get some cake and then take off. They knew their kids hated Waylon, and most of them didn't care when they took Waylon's presents back. It wasn't that the Perceval family was ugly, or broke, or un-hygienic, they were fine physically. However after a few moments of conversation you would grow uneasy, you knew you didn't like them but you couldn't figure out why, in fact, you didn't care. They were annoying, they were useless, so why even try to be there friend? This was the way it would work for pretty much all of the Perceval's relationships, with themselves and with other people. However Waylon Perceval was different, he was in love, and in love with the prettiest girl he had ever seen, she had light blonde hair and ruby red lips. Her name is Sara Sundae, and she stands five feet away from Waylon, licking vanilla ice cream from a cone that she holds in her hand. Waylon stares at her in amazement, watching her tongue become coated with the ice cream only to have it washed away with one single swallow. A smile forms on his face as he watches her, since the first day that he had saw her he had become fascinated with every aspect of her, both physically and mentally. Knowing that he would see her would always give him a feeling in his stomach, similar to the one that he would feel on Christmas morning before he would get to see his presents. Feeling a strong moment of strength Waylon felt an urge to approach her, he began almost a strong march towards her and as she turned to face him his march melted momentarily into a slow somber. Bracing himself for rejection he awaited the reaction that he was used to, and that normally was the person's back turned to him and for his words to fall on deaf ears. Sara however smiled at him. No one had ever smiled at Waylon like she just did, not even his mother. Waylon edged up to her and she held out her cone as he got closer, "Want some?" She asked curiously feeling sympathetic for him, she had always liked Waylon, not because he was attractive but only because he held a strong heart and she had not sensed that from any other kid she had come across. Waylon did not respond at first, only to look down at the cone in shear amazement, as he was being offered something that he had never had to ask for. "Well?" Sara asked impatiently, "You want some ice cream?" Waylon looked to her eyes now and studied them for a moment, thinking briefly about what he would do if she shoved it in his face.
