The Untold Story(part one of...)

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For the first time in a long time... the negative vibe that the rain gave, didn't have a negative effect, or cause a negative reaction. Sean 'Triple X' Stevens sat near his window, inside his Orlando, Florida apartment... smiling. Not that same arrogant smile he's flashed time and again, on camera, to strike fear into the hearts of his opponent... it wasn't even the phony smile he'd give in a situation where he was REALLY afraid. No... this smile... was genuine. From the heart. Warm and fuzzy. And, as the gentle set of hands - belonging to the attractive woman standing behind him - touched his shoulders... it was suddenly clear why. Poison Ivy - "You ready to eat?" Sean turned around and faced the woman known to the professional wrestling world as Poison Ivy... but, standing there... in her too big, 100% cotton 'Blue- eyed Badass' t-shirt - in support of Sean - pajama pants, and footies, she was simply... Ivy Lillian McGinnis - his girlfriend. Stevens - "In a minute, Ivy." Sean returned his focus to the soothing sounds of the rain, as Ivy positioned herself in front of him, embracing him around his neck. Sean returned the warm hug, by wrapping his arms around her waist, and resting his head on her breasts. Poison Ivy - "Something the matter?" Sean released his grip, and Ivy retaliated. They both made eye contact, as Sean shot her a half smile. They had been through so much - Ivy and Trip. Two years ago... they flirted with the idea of getting together, but Sean's fear of doing anything to get her, and Ivy's fear of being anything else but 'Mrs. Hornet' kept them apart. It caused bitter feelings between the two for several months, until one day eight months ago, under the CWL banner, they talked... and, decided to bury the hatchet... and, be friends. Deep down... Sean didn't want that... neither did Ivy. But, in fear of the other person not feeling the same way... they convinced themselves that THIS was the way it had to be. Sean moved on... to Linda Johns... Victoria Jacobs... and, various other nameless people he met in the hotel lobby of whatever city he was in. To his credit... he did love one of them dearly - Linda... but, Ivy was his match. She was the one he wanted. And, the moment Linda Johns left his life, and allowed him to see that... he began to believe it even more. Ivy also moved on. ...returning to the side of Hornet and a few other people in the wrestling business. But, no matter who she was with... how much fun they were having... or what they were doing... it always led back to Sean. Sad thing... Eli Flair knew it... her late mother knew it... even telling her on her deathbed to find Sean and be happy for once in her life... she just chose not to know it. That is... until one cold night... at an fWo show. Ivy and Sean ran into each other backstage, and shared a cab, as they were staying at the same hotel. That night... they talked... looked at old photographs, some even of the two of them beating the living crap out of each other, in one of Triple X and Eli Flair's many historic battles. And, whether it was by accident... coincedence... fate. ...they kissed that night. And, despite a few bumps in the road... they've been together since. They've been through so much together. Ivy felt there was nothing they couldn't conquer. So if there was something wrong with Sean... she wanted to know. She smiled at him as well... placing her hand on his shoulder. Stevens - "Funny thing I just realized, Ivy." Ivy's eyebrows shot to the top of her forehead in interest of what Sean was about to say. "For the first time in a long, long time... nothing's wrong. I was just sitting here... thinking, y'know? I'm happier now than I've ever been in my life, I believe. I'm back in the ring, where I belong. Back in SCW, where I belong. And, honestly... I really think it's only a matter of time before I'm World Champ there, too." Poison Ivy - "If somebody would have told me - how you're acting now - is how the famous 'Blue- Eyed Badass' acted when he was in the confines of his own home... I'd have swore they were liars. Ivy paused... before giggling to herself. "C'mon, Sean... let's get something to eat. You got somebody coming over, right?" Stevens - "Yeah... I'm supposed to cut a promo for this tournament I'm in. But, it's no rush. You cooked?!?" Sean chuckled, as somebody knocked on the door. Ivy went to open it. Poison Ivy - "...alright... that's enough. ...smartass! I ordered chinese earlier... I'm warming it up now. "Who is it?" There was no answer as Ivy opened the door. Nobody was there... however, on the 'Welcome' mat... was a package sent via 'Priority mail'. Ivy gave it the one over... then turned to Sean. "I think it's for you, Sean..." Stevens - "Oh yeah? From who?" Poison Ivy - "...nobody I know. Open it up... let's see." Ivy handed Stevens the package, as Sean casually glanced down looking at the address in the left corner. He almost choked... and, his face immediately began to turn red. Ivy, noticing this, grew concerned. Poison Ivy - "What is it, Sean? C'mon... open it..." Suddenly, the sounds of wood being knocked on, stole the moment, again. Sean immediately jumped up to answer it... and, was relieved to see the partially wet, white guy, in the 'fWo camera crew' jacket, carrying a camera by his side. Sean immediately let him in... shooting Ivy a funny look, as if to say: "I'm sorry." He directed the camera man to where the promo would be cut... and, waited for his cue.
