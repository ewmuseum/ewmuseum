Darkness: Needle In The Hay

3 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Darkness: Needle In The Hay (a tribute to pop culture ES:RIP) We all saw what happened at Countdown, but we all are wondering what happened in the darkness. I promise that will come with time, but now I give you the part that scares me. What came after. Not weeks after, but right after. I give it to you not as a memory, but as a live event. I write it through his eyes, as he sees everything in the present. I'm sorry I can't be more specific, but I cannot attach myself to this one. It is hard to even write. I don't know if I like that movie anymore, and it scares me that he was so afraid (yet so resigned to it). cold.dark.night. I roam the streets of the city. New York is huge. It's bright at night, but there are dark alleys. I find one. Human urine and feces line the ground. It doesn't scare me. It does scare me. No, you are just a part of me I no longer appreciate. Nothing scares me. Not now. I've been scared enough tonight. Staring down the barrel of that gun and seeing my father's eyes as he looked at meandmouthedthosewordstomeashepulledthetrigger. No. Stop. Don't pity yourself. Stop. No. Put down the razor blade. My conscience screams at me. I tell it to go away. It never listens. No one ever listens. not to me, not to what i have to say. nobody listens. not smooth or fox. none of them. they never listen. they don't hear me. they don't see me. i am invisible. "please. stop. i can see you. i can see you in the reflection of that blade. please stop. please don't." i wish they would say those things to me. You really must stop this. You must do something else. There are other options. do you ever have these thoughts that race through your head? the conscience that defies the life. they tell you to do one thing or to do things at all. they tell me to do a lot of things. tonight i don't listen. tonight i take this razor blade. i found it in the bathroom with the remnants of the white powder on it. someone cut lines, but everyone turns heads. it's accepted in silence. who cares about a line right? do what you must to get by. no more getting by. a clean slice. two. three. that's all it takes. i drip onto the street. i lean against a wall. i don't care if the ground is clean beneath me. I see the world. I see it as it fades away. Fades to black. I think of Wes Anderson and Owen Wilson. I think of my favorite scene in my favorite movie. I think of all the blood. No, don't do it. It was just a movie... This is your life. Your life. Stop. i wonder if someone with nerdy glasses and a striped shirt and a dirty hat will save me. i doubt it. I think of Elliot Smith and his song and wonder if he died singing it. I don't cry. I don't speak. I am thinking of you now. Your face. Your kiss. I will see you soon... You are the last face in my mind. iloveyouandialwayswillpleaseihaveneverforgottenyoudonna. Goodbye world, I tried to stop me. Goodbye world, I had to do it. goodbyeworldiwillnotmissyou.
