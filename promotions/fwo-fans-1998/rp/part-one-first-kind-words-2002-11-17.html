Part One: First Kind Words

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 4, 1995 Creensboro, North Carolina "I'm serious, Flair - you don't have it." As the majority of the locker room held them apart, Eli "The Eliminator" Flair and "Hot" Johnny Tropic somehow managed to stay nose- to- nose. Poison Ivy, fresh off her first CSWA PAy- Per- View event, stood between them. "Fuck you, Tropic." Johnny Tropic just interfered in a match between the Eliminator and former CSWA World Chmapion Degadeth, costing Eli the match. While Eli was aware of the interference, the plan was that he would kick out of the pin attempt and go on to win the match, setting up victory in the Winning Streak tournament in two months and a long run with the CSWA's IntraContinental championship. "No man, fuck you. If you can't take the shit, you don't belong here," taunted Tropic, "And from the look of it, you certainly DON'T belong here." Unfortunately, Tropic tattooed Flair so hard with a steel chair that he knocked him completely unconscious. Thus all plans were shot to hell. "Do you even GET how this business works?" asked Ivy, "You're not supposed to kill each other out there, are you? Especially when you're making your first appearance." "Oh, what's this?" asked Tropic, "The blonde groupie can talk?" Ivy's hand moved quickly, as she slapped Tropic across the face. "Don't call me a groupie you little shit." The two men finally stopped struggling against their restrainers - and retreated to separate parts of the lockerroom. Tropic joined up with his friend Scotty Michaels and his buddies, as Eli and Poison Ivy sat down alone. "How's your head?" asked Ivy. "Hurts like hell," replied Eli quickly, as he held a towel to the bloody slit in his forehead, "Don't worry too much about it." "Are you fucking kidding me?" The twenty- year old Ivy McGinnis had evidently not learned patience in college, "You carry Degadeth to his best match in years and can't even enjoy the rub because some MORON doesn't know how to throw a chair? Bullshit, I'm not worried about it, I'm fuckin' PISSED about it." A hand suddenly rested on Ivy's shoulder. She looked up, her hands curled into fists - only to hold herself back. The former two- time Unified Champion, HORNET, stood there with his hand on Ivy's shoulder. "For what it's worth, Eli, I think you had a great match out there. And I think you've got a pretty damn good future in front of ya." Eli considered this for a moment. "Thanks, Champ, 'preciate it." Hornet nodded in his direction and walked away. "That's a class act," observed Eli, "He's got five matches to wrestle tonight, including the main event against GUNS - and he had time to tell a pair of nobodies like us that he likes what we're doing?" "Yeah," agreed Ivy, "The Adlers and Windhams of the world could take a cue from that." Eli and Ivy continued to speak on what they'd like to do over the next few shows as he peeled away his elbowpads, kneepads, and various other extraneous gear. Largely ignored by the rest of the locker room, they both knew there was plenty of time to make it to the top. All they needed was the chance.
