Well, this night was done. They had walked into the Arena, taken it all in (or at least tried to), and had a good time watching the show. It was still so surreal to the two of them... there they were, in the world's biggest castle. *They*, the Fuse Bros. Tyler turned to Conor, as Conor continued to watch the broadcast of Wednesday Warfare. "I lot of odd henchmen in this castle." He stated. "That one in that mask... he looks like some sort of ninja!!" "True." Tyler nodded in approval. "He has some leather strap with him too. We can only assume he is tough... and maybe a Level Four Boss." "Then there's that GV or whatever they call him. He's pretty scary." Conor's face became worried, as he took off his lime green "C" cap and rubbed his messy brown hair this time. "Are you sure we can take these guys?" Lunging for the remote control, Tyler turned the television off. "I'm sure. But enough of this for now... let's go get some fuel. We might as well put this game on the shelf until Sunday." The Fuse Bros. rose, and began to head out of the room. Conor turned a final time to Tyler. "Why? What are we doing Sunday?" "Playing this game, Conor... playing this game."
