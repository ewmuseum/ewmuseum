Eradication.

1 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Eradication. April 01, 2000 Denver. The Pepsi Arena. Midnight Saturday. "fWo Sunday Slaughter: April 2 SOLD OUT!!!!!" flashes across a large electronic sign, before being replaced with reminders of other, more mundane shows. Beneath the digital billboard stands Akira Hashimoto, Hiroshima. Looking about as pleased as always, he stands tall, his lips firmly tight. For a few moments, he merely breathes, before beginning to talk. "Erik Kelly. You are new around here, so I felt that perhaps I should give you a few pointers. Isn't that what the Strangler would want from me?" Hiroshima chortles. "In only your second match in the fWo, you have a chance to earn a shot at the second most prestigious title they have, the Internet belt. All you have to do, son, is beat me. Don't get carried away with yourself, however. It shall not prove to be an easy task. "We in the FtfWo have worked long and hard to capture the highest titles in the fWo. Obviously, they'd quite prefer that we not be in posession of them. Given the hurdles that we have had to jump through to get to them, we are not about to let some neophyte take them from us. "Child, you spoke of taking a stand, of siding with the fWo in taking us down. This does not sit well with us. Dead asked me himself to make an example of you tomorrow, to prevent you from ever stepping into a ring again, much less meeting Mitch for the Internet belt. I am not so insistent on hurting you, Kelly. Beating you, yes, but not hurting you. "I leave you with this advice. Those who take a stand against us, don't find themselves standing for long. Ice can confirm that one for you" Hiroshima pivots and walks off into the night, as the scene changes to an ad for CS III.
