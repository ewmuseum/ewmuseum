What You Don't Know...

17 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The halogen lamp flickered overhead, causing the shadows to tremble around Kodiak Vic Creed's hospital bed. With a blink, his eyes moved from the ceiling to the window. Outside, the moon winked a tired eye through the cloudy sky. The city kept moving, glowing, surging forward. But the moon just rested its tired head amongst the stars and watched the bugs beneath it scurry across the planet's surface. "Lazy ass," Vic muttered through dry lips as they spread into a smile. The medication pumping through his veins weighed him down, pressing him into the mattress. Vic rolled his head to the side as the door opened. Dakota stepped into the room with the desk nurse chasing after her. "You can't go in there with that!" the round-faced orderly shouted sternly. "Yeah yeah," muttered Datoka as she quickly closed the door behind him. She pulled out a pack of matches and lit the cigarette pressed between her lips. "You've got some bitchy nurses, hun." "Guess that's why I like them." Vic swallowed and felt it burn all the way down his throat. "They remind me so much of you." Dakota approached her husband's bed and admired all the tubes and hardware attached to his muscular frame. She ran her ruby-painted fingernails through his hair. "So was it worth it? Having that match with Nick?" "I wasn't going to back down from him." Vic closed his eyes and let his head sink into his pillow. He wasn't sure how much time had passed before he opened his eyes again. Dakota was still standing beside his bed, watching him. The cigarette was gone. The room was brighter
