Girls' Night Out - "The Morning After The Night Before"

12 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Shit," he thought aloud, "what happened last night?" His head thumped, his eyes were half-lidded and heavy. Strands of bleached dry straw-like hair covered his cheeks, sticking in the Tijuana humidity. He could feel the perspiration on his skin as he wrestled the thin sheet off of his waif, dehydrated form. The taste of stale beer and cigarettes a familiar reminder of why he doesn't "do" this, not often at least. He pulled himself upright, and felt the wave of nausea through his frame, shaking him somewhat. Familiar, yet troubling. Didn't he quit the decadent life, to distance himself from this guilty remorse that he always felt the morning after the night before? That horrible, guilty, feeling. It was always that much worse when there were patches of the evening completely missing, wasn't it? He jumped out of his skin, as a body laying next to him shifted. How had he managed to completely miss this hot babe in his bed? She was perhaps taller than him, judging by her legs hanging off the end of the greasy Motel's single bed. She was thin, in shape, and had no ass to speak of. That isn't to say that Jade Argent was disappointed. She was naked, face-down, hair strewn across the place. He couldn't be disappointed, not yet. Silently he prayed for a pretty face, full lips, and a cracking set of tits. 'Michelle', he thought to himself. Then he remembered, Meltdown. 'fWo's on the road again, obviously.' he recalled. 'We came down to San Diego to make preparations for the show, thats where I overheard Michelle and that moron husband of hers talking about a night out in Tijuana. All the girls were going.' He had a crush on Mary-Lynn Mayweather, thats why his ears had pricked up. That's why he had used his exceptional detective skills to figure out who was organising this get-together and where it was going to be. "A Mexican shindig." He had said it aloud in the locker room, before realising that he wasn't supposed to be eavesdropping. Nobody paid him any attention. Nobody ever paid him any attention. He was over it though, didn't care. He didn't need their second rate approval anyway. He remembered that he had tried and failed to coerce Xander Scott onto this excursion with him. Xander couldn't legally drink anyway, and last time Argent plied him with booze he nearly got the both of them fired. Ivy McGinnis' wrath was not something to be tampered with, he had learnt. So he vowed to go it alone. Of course, he couldn't just show up unannounced and expect to be accepted into the girls party, all 'coincidental'. They were smarter than that. 'I'll coincidentally be hanging out at the same bar as them.' he remembered the thoughts running through his mind as if it were present, 'Perhaps make eyes at Mary-Lynn across the bar, seem surprised to see her there. Wave her over. Work my magic. Maybe even buy her a drink to say sorry for the spit in the eye.' His plan. In all of its flawless glory, even if he may say so himself. (He did.) His desperate attempts to recount the night before were once again interrupted by the girl in his bed, who shifted noisily onto her side. Her back facing the Australian, he could reach over to cop a glimpse at her credentials. He wasn't disappointed. He'd had better, but he'd had alot worse, and as the saying goes: 'You've gotta take the rough with the smooth.' He was in a crutch, though. How in God's name had it got to this? He wasn't an irresponsible decadent bohemian, not anymore. So, confusedly, he continued his seemingly fruitless task of recalling the night before in step-by-step fashion. His mental check-list so far had him with a perfect plan to seduce his current crush, and a place and time that the girls would be meeting for this supposed gathering. He recalled going into the bar, the correct bar, and sitting at a stool by himself. He didn't speak any Spanish, and the staff clearly didn't like the androgynous look of this outsider and so were making no efforts to make life easy for him. The last thing he remembers is managing, at long last, to get hold of a Corona, on the house no less. And then things seem to get sketchy in the ol' headbox for him. He's distracted from his musings by what he can only describe as rodent-like scratchings coming from somewhere within the yellowed, dank, cheap wino hotel within which he had found himself just moments before when he fluttered into consciousness. Lifting his feet from the stained, worn burgundy carpet, lest some rabid Mexican vermin bites his toe off and gives him the swine flu, he starts to drink in his surroundings a little more. Just where in the hell is he? 'Certainly times aren't that hard economically that this is the place which the Beav' decided to hole us up in?' he wondered. The scratchings seemed to get louder now as they got closer, and then the toilet in the bathroom flushed. He hopped to his feet, scared, hoping that he hadn't gotten himself into one of THOSE situations again. You know the one, where you wake up in bed with a woman and her husband walks out of the bathroom having no idea that you've just banged his chick and passed out... the one that usually ends up with a bloody nose or worse. A silent prayer, the second one of the morning. Anyone would think he was going to start wearing a cross, or something. To his delight, however, he is confronted by a very tall, leggy, thin, tanned blonde who was completely naked from head to foot. He considers that she resembles a pretty Anna Kournikova, cute in the face and a smoking body. Small, pert tits. Washboard abs, well conditioned. And well trimmed, which is important. Of course. He beams at her, though deep down he knows it must be too good to be true and is half expecting her to slap him, or break into a tirade. "Good morning Mr. Argent." she whispers difficultly, in broken English. "Would you like some K?" she waved a zip-lock bag clasped tightly between long slender digits. "No." he struggled. He didn't usually know the word 'no', but on this occasion he was more interested in getting to the bottom of this situation entirely. He had two tall blond girls in his motel room, he had no recollection of ever meeting them or how it had got to this - waking up at 4 in the morning with the sun beating down through the open, nicotine-coated windows, post-coital and hungover. Feeling like a rat, smelling like an alcoholic. "What happened, last night?" he finally struggled, as he slumped heavily back down onto the bed. "Oh, you don't remember?" she teased him, as she slithered snake-like toward him. A seductive purr in her meandering, she draped an arm over him. "Last night was something special," she began, "last night was the first day of the rest of your life." It almost sounded scary, harrowing. "Tell me, you're a big shot in the wrestling, aren't you?" she poised for his reply. His brow began to bead with sweat, a test? "Sure. I'm the best at what I do, without question." he barely hesitated. The blonde's eyes lit up as she beamed a bright smile at him and almost playfully jumped on him, sending him back onto the squeaky coiled mattress with the other blonde. "My sister and I, we are going to take you to new heights..." "Sister?" Argent quizzed. The blonde nodded down to the girl in his bed, passed out. "Oh. You're sisters?!" She nodded again. 'MOTHER. FUCKING. SCOOOOOOOOOOORE!!!' There were cheerleaders in his head, all of them Megan Fox, chanting "Oh Jade Argent, you're the man. You can fuck anything, yes you can!'... As they continued with the pom-poms and the chanting and the amazing flexible dancing, he was distracted and pulled away from his day-dream by Blondie, again. "We are Rhyme and Reason." both girls said in unison as the previously sleeping twin had sat up, leaving Argent sandwiched and entranced, fascinated and wondering what else they would do in unison. "But... what happened last night?!" he protested. "As they say in Meh-Hee-Kow, sweety... Mañana, mañana." Frustrated, he conceded. Who knows, there might be time for something special, yet.
