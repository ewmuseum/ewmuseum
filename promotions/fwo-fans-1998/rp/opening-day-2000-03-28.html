fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Opening Day. March 28, 2000 The Tokyo skyline is clearly visible against the setting sun - the orange hues of the sky provide a beautiful background for the highrises of the capital of Japan. We slowly pan into the interior of one of these buildings - a hotel, as it were. As we head inside, a familiar form is glimpsed - none other than Perfect. What is he doing in Japan? Good question. As we wait to find out, he flips through a series of channels, coming across a series of Japanese programs until he finally runs into - Puro... resu? That almost looks like our good old fashioned American Sports Entertainment. But where are the angles, the promos, the 20-minute headlocks?! Wait... scratch that last one. Onscreen, Vader and Kenta Kobashi tangle. Hey... I know that guy! He's a big fat piece of (censored), or at least that's what he called himself once. Who's that other guy? Ah, who cares. He's probably some nothing, some nobody. Probably those wacky guys at that one Arena website like him, though. What a bunch of goofs. There comes a knock at the door. What the hell? With my luck, it's probably some fWo cameragoof who wants an interview. He chuckles at his own wit. Yeah, like they'd follow me all the way to Japan. Reaching the door, he opens it to reveal - Dammit. Dammit dammit dammit. Finelli, don't you have a life or something? Flash Finelli steps in, camera in hand. Actually, no. Ah. Thought so. Let me guess, you want my thoughts on something or other, right? Yep. How the hell did you know I'm in Japan, anyway? Well, everybody knows you like baseball. Right... And everybody who follows baseball knows that Opening Day is tonight, in Japan, so where else would you be but here? My God... that's actually... logical. Well, it all starts with logic. Right, right. Well, let me get ready to cut a promo here... Pause. OK. Flip that sucker on. Finelli does so, and we switch to the view from his camera. Perfect stands against the window, and the beautiful sunset. Well, Frankie, since all the recent events of my life have in some way revolved around Scott Collins, I don't see who else I would address. Collins, you know me. I know you. We've been at each other's throats since the very beginning. I'm sure you remember one of our first encounters - February Nineteenth, Nineteen Ninety-Nine. The day when I - what was it? Oh yeah. Took your World Title. And of course, we'll ignore the fact that if I hadn't been out there to distract that roid monkey Ice a month before, you never even would've taken the belt in the first place. Remember that, Collins? He had your ass right where he wanted you - and when Ice has you where he wants you, that's a sad, sad statement about just how much you suck. So Collins, in a way, I can take credit for making your very career, bicep boy. Without me to hand you the World Title on a silver platter, you would still be fighting Austin The Giant every week. And despite the fact that it is more than a bit embarrassing to be the one who has to take the blame for inflicting Scott Collins on the wrestling world, nonetheless that's a burden I have to bear. But how, Scotty, have you been repaying me for making you a star? You horned in on my spotlight after I beat the ever-loving crap out of the Legendary Evil Meanie David Spencer and single-handedly drove his ass back to Britain. You took my spotlight again after I kicked the hell out of Ice. And you took my spotlight AGAIN at Body Count when myself and Mitch carried you two worthless sacks of suck to a **** 1/2 MOTYC. It would've been *****, but hell, even we can only do so much to make you two look like actual workers. Collins, one year ago, you did that for the first time. I was in the midst of my FIRST record-setting World Title reign, and I was cruising along to CyberSlam, easily dispatching all my so-called competitors with the flick of a finger, when I'm informed that my opponent in the main of the biggest show of the year is you. "Great", I said, looking forward to what essentially amounted to a night off. So the big show comes around, you're supposed to be crippled, and I'm standing in the middle of the ring, when the lights go out. "Big deal", I think to myself, knowing that fWo management is routinely late in their payments. When suddenly some masked monkey and his two pansy-ass goons come strolling down the aisle. In comes the masked man, and it's you. You, for the first of many times, stealing my rightfully deserved and earned place in the spotlight! You couldn't even beat me without resorting to cheating. And as we all know, real men get it done themselves. Collins, let me make this perfectly clear to you. I am THE ratings draw around here. I pop the buyrates, I raise the numbers, I bring in the people! I'm the man who held the World Title for 45% of 1999. I am THE ONE MAN who should be out there every night, with the spotlight shining bright on me. But without fail, you come in and decide to rain on my parade. No more, Collins. No more. It's a month on the dot to CyberSlam, Scotty. You and me, pal. Once and for all. If you've got the little Scotties to accept - and I know you do, since I've seen you selling all those groin shots like a mother - then haul your ass over to Seattle where those kings of rock known as the Mariners play, and when the dust has settled once and for all and I've pulled out your eyes and stuffed them down your pants so you could watch me kicking the crap out of you, you will never take a piece of my spotlight... again. Now, Flash, if you'll excuse me, I've the Cubs and Mets to watch. See ya! Perfect quickly gathers up a few things and heads towards the door. But... but... Scott! Man! I don't have any plane fare home! Perfect stops at the door. Well that was pretty stupid of you, wasn't it? He exits, shutting the door behind him. Oh man... I really need to talk to Finn about a raise. I wonder what the dollars-to-yen conversion rate is... Fade to black.
