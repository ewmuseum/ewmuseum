A tale of a Hero of Justice (Final - For Now)

28 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Waylon pulled up realizing that it was a rest stop. Luckily only one car was parked outside the stop and the man managed to make it to the men's bathroom without being noticed. He walked inside the bathroom and saw two feet under the doorway of a stall, the sound of whistling was comforting as he reached the mirror. . Turning briefly to the side to stare at the stall in which the other man was seated, the man in the stall flushed his toilet. The stall opened and before the man stepping out of the stall could catch a glimpse of his attacker, Waylon cracked him a hard hook in his jaw and knocked him bck, the man gripped his mouth and tried to shout out. Waylon pushed the man and he fell back, slipped and smacked his head against the toilet seat. "I'm so sorry," Waylon said softly as he lifted the man up and placed him back on the toilet seat, he rested the unconscious man's head back and then began to feel the pockets of his pants. Finally feeling the keys, Waylon pulled the car keys from his pocket and quickly exited the bathroom. The car took a few moments to start, however once it did he began to head to the border. Waylon figured it would be about an hour before the man regained conscious and then about an hour to walk to the next stop. Waylon had by now pulled the car over and taped his picture ove the picture that was on the man's ID. Throwing the car around on the ground and making it nice and dirty Waylon was satisfied that it would pass as his own identification. It would be a half an hour before Waylon would reach the border. He pulled up to the booth and looked out his driver side window with a large grin, "Hello." It was a woman, about thirty five, brown hair, pimple scars and a mean glare, "ID please." Waylon handed her his information and he grew a slight nervous, trying to recover and act casual Waylon made some conversation as the lady read his ID, "So how's your day?" She looked at him for a moment and then stared back at the ID, and then back at Waylon, "Looks pretty dirty there sir, kind of hard ID to read, do you have anything else?" Waylon sifted through his pockets and retrieved the wallet, he handed it to her and she opened it coming. Looking through it she verified that the other pieces held the same name and then her eyes caught a glimpse of the container that held the serum. "What's that?" "Propane?" "Propane?" "I like to barbeque." "Really?" "Yes." "Really?" "Uh-huh." "Looks like something else." Waylon looked over at the container and then back at her, "Oh you may not recognize it, it's a special kind of tank, holds more propane, but same size as all the others." "How does it hold a larger amount if it's the same size?" The lady questioned. "Well," Waylon piped up, "It's one of those new age ones, with more space however, it looks like the old ones and weighs less." "Oh really?" "Yes." "Really?" "Uh-huh." "Still looks like something else." Waylon looked back at it again, "I think I see what you mean," then he looked to her with a softer confused expression, "But it's not something else." She handed the wallet back to him and then got off of her seat and began to exit out of her booth. A tremendous fear came over Waylon as he watched her walk around the car and then look at the tank more closely. "You know.." She began as sweat began to form at Waylon's brow, "My husband and I do a lot of BBQ'ing, and I was thinking about getting him something bigger, but if this is the same size with more room...that would be better." Waylon grew relaxed, "Sure would." "Where can I get one of these?" She looked at him hard again. Waylon smiled, "Canada, only in Canada. Try a propane shop, I got mine in...in...Quebec." "Oh." She stated in a disappointed tone, "Quebec. Well have a great day." "Bye now," He wanted to waive but just merely drove on, Waylon was exhausted and he ditched the car once again and took the bus out of town. He headed as far south as he could before finally stopped in Sacramento, hitting up a motel with a bed in it.
