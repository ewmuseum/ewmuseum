The Ross Report

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For the first time I heard boos last week. I won't lie, it does have me in some emotional distress. Enough to the point where I won't be showing up for Warfare tonight. I am not yet booked in a match for the World Title, and now would be the perfect opportunity to take it. First Erika, then Jim Dudley, now Machina. Talk about sliding scales. What really irks me is that I'VE been the one on the side of scornful e-mails and random booing in the streets from people I once considered fans. People I busted my ass for night in and night out when 3vil was in charge, when Erik Kelly was doing the same 3 moves a night. The question of "Why?" has come up on considerable occasions. Here's your answer. I want you to know about Eli Flair & Poison Ivy. I want you to know about how Flair had a World Title handed to him and decided instead to leave the company. How he affiliated himself with 3 of his worst enemies in order to try to take down the CEO and watch as it fizzled out so severely to the point where attacking a valet was a watermark. I want you to know about the Eli Flair that under the auspices of an "I Quit" match, damn near killed another man in the ring and broke two of his fingers, hospitalizing him for a good part of the year. I want you to know about Eli Flair getting the opportunity to win every fWo title within the span of a couple months, and despite winning the least important of them, has chosen to focus on his loss to me. I want you to know that his "man of action" talk has consisted of mostly talk and beating up a woman the old directors beat up. I should also add that up until the events of last Wednesday, he was one of my only two friends backstage, and 24 hours previous to the event I had footed the bill for a trip down to Puerto Nuevo where I let him convince me into coming back and addressing the fans in my hometown, if nowhere else. Now let me tell you about Poison Ivy. A smart woman who didn't even want to be in this business and only dragged along because her brother-in-law was suicidal. She's the reason he won his only World Title to date so far, one well-placed cane shot upside the head of an over-the-hill fifth-rate Sting impersonator. Sometimes we agreed, sometimes we didn't. Usually she had something smart to say. That ended Wednesday. I stood in the ring explaining my position--as THEY had wanted me to. People who've seen me for the last six years, my biggest fans in the world, and then my friends come out. Obviously, I was expecting support, but when you're the best fWo World Champion of all-time, there's envy from all sides. I've wanted to do this since I was 10. I've been working on doing it since I was 16. I haven't taken a self-imposed break yet until now. I worked my way up the ladder and earned a position of superiority. I tried to explain all of this. And then Poison Ivy comes out and accuses me of being a male prisoner and a bitch. If she'd actually done her homework instead of talking first and getting facts later, she would've realized everything I said was true. I am cleaning up the Asylum, and I have helped to get rid of the seedier elements. We had a common enemy, and she chose to ignore it in order to deride me in front of my hometown. My hometown! (Not to mention the fact she wrote another column at the Asylum server in which she pretty much agreed with me 100%. POT. KETTLE. BLAAAAAAAAAACK.) And then Eli Flair jumps in. And of course he doesn't defend his friend...he sticks by Ivy. Talking about his legacy--what legacy is that here? That faceless midcarder Winters damn near killing him? The HARDCORE Title? How many PPV's has he headlined? How many World Titles of note has he won without Ivy cane-shotting the guy? How long has he dealt with the pressures of being a media magnet and figurehead of a prestigious company when he can't even deal with the pressures that got him into the ring? These were my last two friends in the business saying this to me. In my hometown. They'd set me up from the beginning. This was never about me addressing the fans and letting them know the motivations behind my actions, this was about them getting some rub from a main-eventer. It pained me to do what I did, but after a couple minutes it became very necessary. A man who simply wants what's his, what he's OWED. Two people only out for themselves. And suddenly I'VE become the bad guy in this. Fine.
