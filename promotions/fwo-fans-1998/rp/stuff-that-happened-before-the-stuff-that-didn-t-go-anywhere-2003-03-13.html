Stuff that happened before the stuff that didn't go anywhere else.

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brandon pulled Amy in to her hotel room by her arm. In his other hand were the remnants of an Otep t-shirt. Amy, herself resisting Brandon's urgings, was the former owner of said shirt. Now clad in black bra and jeans curse words flew from her mouth. Something or someone had lit a fire underneath the sometimes-spastic redhead and seemingly Brandon had to play big brother and retrieve her from harms way. Once inside the room Brandon kicked the door closed as Amy finally pulled away. Turning angrily she struck out with a stiff punch to his shoulder before firing another series of less than humble language towards the mighty hero. "What in the hell were you thinking out there?" Brandon's normally calm exterior had been replaced by a furrowed brow and a slightly agitated growl. "You're trying to get us shot aren't you, and for what? Sometimes you just have to keep your head down and not rock the boat!" Amy recoiled after landing the stiff punch, maybe as habit from her usual experiences; she half expected retaliation for her actions. Not finding one coming though she steeled her resolve and defended her actions. "That's a nice thought coming from Mr. Hero. Keep your head down and don't rock the boat. I guess that's what you're doing with the fWo eh?" "Look, I'm just saying there's a time and place for everything and popping that grease ball in the face and starting that riot was probably not the best thing in the world for us right now. We just need to pray that no one recognizes you, or me..." "Hey, I don't care what language he was speaking, that guy was talking trash...he got what he deserved and I was hoping they WOULD shoot me, it'd be hilarious to see those military bitches get beheaded or whatever they do around here for hitting a peaceful Canadian..." She winced at her own obviously stupid wording, "Well, a Canadian. And according to you, 'Now isn't the time nor the place' for anything so don't even bother shooting me that bullshit. You would've hidden your face and crawled away, I do things differently." "I didn't say hide your face and crawl away but the punching was bad enough. It's... well, you didn't need to throw that rock... or even start that fire, small as it was." Flustered Brandon just shook his head as he gathered his thoughts. Finally realizing that he still held the tatters of a newly acquired band shirt Brandon turned again to his girlfriend. Amy had simply sat on the edge of the bed with her knees touching and her elbows propping up a heavy head, not really caring that Victoria's Secret was in plain view. Rifling through her duffle the Paragon came across his Survivor t-shirt and tossed it to her. "And the tearing of the clothes... that poor man, that one was just passing by..." Slipping the miles-too-large shirt around her neck, Amy turned up her nose and quickly flung it back and Brandon. "Eew, I don't want your sweaty gym shirts." She pouted and sighed heavily, shaking a little from the adrenaline of the scene she'd made outside. "And it was all a statement! These people live in fear of EVERYTHING...I figured I'd toss a little anarchy in, y'kno----okay, so I was just really pissed off." Finally, she 'd admitted defeat. The redhead examined her right hand and a small trickle of blood running down the back of it. "Ow...think I sliced my knuckle when buddy tackled me into that window..." Amy winced and then smiled, "Ah well...it was worth it, that was actually kinda fun! How cool would it be if they came and tried to arrest me or something? I'm gonna wake up in the middle of the night staring down the barrel of a machine gun." "Hmmm... You might be right." Brandon smirked a little as he picked his shirt from the grungy carpet. "That was clean by the way. If it smells it's from that stuff you work out in, I put it in your bag the other day..." Peeling off his own shirt as he defended his laundry habits Brandon stepped over to the bed and tossed his own shirt on top of the remains of Amy's. A knock on the door startled them both as Brandon put a finger to his lips and motioned for Amy to go to the bathroom. Slipping to the door as quietly as the old floors would let him he clicked the lock down and pulled open the door halfway. "It was her wasn't it?" The familiar voice brought comfort to the mighty nervous hero. It seemed that news does indeed travel fast and Ivy McGinnis had come to confirm that the trouble-making flame haired 'American' from the recently quelled uprising was none other than the Rat Pack's own. "Yeah..." With a raise of his eyebrow Brandon's feelings on the whole situation was made readily apparent to the manager of champions. A smirk of her own followed by a glance down the hallway reinforced the actual severity of the situation though, bringing them both back to center. "Just keep her in here for the night. Sean and I will be down in a few hours with some food and things." A nod of the head, a quick look again down the hall and Ivy was gone. Brandon closed the door quickly making sure that the feeble lock was in place. Amy had only semi-hidden; she popped up from sitting on the floor behind the bed and furrowed her brow. "Let them come for me, I said I don't care. He started it even... even if I did throw the first punch..." Even with the knowledge that her best attempt at an innocent look wouldn't fool anybody, she tried. Looking at Amy using her best and most often defensive mechanism Brandon simply shrugged off the lecture he felt the urge to give. He realized that he didn't need to 'change' Amy, nor try to reign in her free spirit, that was one of the things that pulled on his heart the most. Instead he simply sighed the sigh of the defeated and slid on to the edge of the bed as Amy joined him. "Well, it seems we've got some time to ourselves whether we've been avoiding it or not..." "You think I've been avoiding spending time with you?" Amy laughed aloud, quickly kissing him on the jaw reassuringly. "Nooo no no...thanks for the confidence," Even when she was being nice, sarcasm shone through. "But considering I've spent the last few days trying to think up an intricate rape plot so Ivy and Sean'll quit making fun of me, this is a welcome break." "And if you think you've been the only one getting the rib from the guys then your sadly mistaken. Sean and I did a dark match last week and right in the middle of a spot he asked if I had given you the 'hammer of god' yet..." Grinning uncontrollably and shaking his head Brandon blushed. "It was all I could do to finish the move. It looked horrible." The redhead grinned just the same, "Sean deserves to be castrated in his sleep." Quickly, she re-thought the statement with half a chuckle. "But then...Ivy might take a knife to me in mine..." "Heh... see, now you're doing nothing but putting mental images in my head. Not a good thing." Half reclining now Brandon opened up his arms with a casual invite. After another glance to the door Amy accepted and lay close to the mighty boy scout. Hugging her tight to him for a second a random thought crossed his mind. Of course he made it spoken. "So is this... I don't know a polite way to say it... are we moving too slow for you? Ya'know I'm just, I just figured we'd try to get to know each other first... before..." Realizing the stammering that had accompanied said random thought Brandon decided it best to try a different tactic. "See, Ana was... well, she's been the only person I've been with. I know you don't really know that much about how we met and all but we met back in grade school. We were together ever since... so I'm not exactly Mr. Worldly..." Amy smiled, biting her lip. It really did strike her as sweet, like almost everything he said seemed to be. "You're way too nice...it kind of scares me. I might be the most impatient person you've ever met most of the time, but I've seriously got all the time in the world for you. Even though I figure you know everything there is to know about me now, take your time. It's like if I asked you to marry me or something nuts like that...I sure as hell wouldn't expect an answer or even acknowledgment right away." "Besides...it doesn't seem like there's any timer on when you're gonna cheat on me or something's gonna go wrong anymore, so I've got no reason to hurry." She grinned and pinged his chest - which was a stinging, but kind gesture coming from Amy - playfully. "Well there's plenty of reason to hurry..." Stilting his eyebrows in a mock 'come hither' fashion Brandon smiled his big cheesy smile. "But there's just more reasons to wait. Like the old saying goes... Abstinence makes the heart grow fonder..." "I could kill you for that one!" Amy quickly pounced and tackled him down. She positioned herself to sit on his stomach victoriously, and chuckled as she stuck her tongue out at him. "Jeez, you're slow with everything...I would've expected Hero Boy to catch me first, now what would happen if I was Borst, huh?" She winked, mocking his constant morals and lessons lovingly. "Well honestly if Borst jumped me like this I think he got the wrong type of mail order bride don't ya think?" Using Amy's slight giggle as a distraction Brandon reversed the "deathgrip" which ended with them both on the floor. Unfortunately or fortunately depending on how you look at it Brandon once again ended up on the bottom. "Plus... if he made me feel like I'm feeling now I'd have to rethink some of my life decisions..." "Don't tempt me when I'm feeling relatively innocent." Amy smirked, flicking his bottom lip with sharp fingernails. "You're too easy, you've gotta fight back or it's no fun humiliating you!" Grabbing Amy by her small waist Brandon pushes up with his left arm before planting a foot down for leverage and hoisting himself in to a standing position. Amy, still attached to Brandon's stomach pushes against him with little effect before finding herself pinned to the bed with the mighty hero on top of her. "Better?" For a minute she was shocked at all the sudden movement, mouth agape but after a second she smiled with a raised eyebrow and a bit of a laugh, "A little precarious of you, dontcha' think?" Amy half-heartedly tried to shove him off, but ended up considerably less than successful, both of them chuckling... In the midst of their playful encounter the events of the day had been left behind. Instead Amy and Brandon found themselves lost in each other. The rest of the evening would play out much the same. Small talk ranging from favorite candy to weird places for sex to eventually a more serious debate on the events that surrounded them would come and go. It seemed that in the midst of taking things slow the two had reached somewhere neither had really been in awhile. Comfortable. Safe. In love. [This has been an Aimz/ Brand Frontier collaboration.]
