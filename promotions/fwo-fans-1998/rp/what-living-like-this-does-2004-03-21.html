What Living Like This Does...

21 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The night of Saturday, March 20th, 2004 Jynx me something crazy Thinking if it's three then I'm as smooth as the skin rolls across the small of your back It's too bad it's not my style If you need me I'm out and on the parkway, patient and waiting for headlights, dressed in a fashion that's fitting to the inconsistencys of my moods It's cold out here in the park. I should be on a plane to Orlando to prepare for RUAHH~!. What am I still doing here? It's times like these where silence means everything And no one is to know about this It's times like these, where silence means everything And no one is to know about this It's so quiet out here. There's nobody around me. There's nothing here to protect me anymore. It's this damned company, they're not providing like my uncle was provided. But whatever I have gettin myself into maybe has been slicing inches from my waist It's my fist vs. the bottle (and thank god you weren't there...) And that's how bad could this hurt or against I won't feel a thing (and thank god you weren't there...) I tell you all about it It's just not working out (...to watch me hit the bottle) not working out Who cares? I'll take one more sip. This night in the park, this quietness, this was all worth it for me. It's a campaign of distraction and revisionist history, oh It's a shame I don't think that they'll notice (it's a shame, I don't think that they'll notice) It's a shame I doubt they even care (it's a shame I doubt they even care) No one is to know about this I've never felt better. This is why we were taught so much better than this This is what living like this does And with that, Dallas Jones passed out. Too many drinks for "The Future" caused him to fall asleep on an empty parkbench in Trenton, New Jersey. Where'd he get the money to buy this? He spent the money in which he was supposed to fly to Orlando for RUAHH~! on Monday Night. Dallas, we have a problem.
