Falling Down the Totem Pole

1 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Why do I always have to rely on the fWo website to find out if I have a match? Chris Universal's such a never-will-be bottom-feeding asshole. Great, it's down. That shows a company full of potential. Jesus, I might as well pack up and head to the WWF, join the nW... ...oh wait, here it is. Ok, where the hell is this thing? This format sucks. What the hell is that little picture of a roid-freak at the bottom? Is that supposed to be Creed? My God, what a goof. What moron made that picture? Or is that Davis? I hope I see that no-talent, ratings-sucking jackass next week. I don't care if he is "Baad Azz", I would pummel him until he learned to spell. Ok, here it is...Slaughter preview...I can't believe I have to act like a common fan. This is ridiculous. Boston Strangler is fighting Wretch?! What in the hell is this? That's ratings? Watching a man who wants to feud over a damn HAMBURGER wrestle a man who had his fifteen minutes of fame extended to nineteen? What a shit card this is. At least I'm not on it. Or wait...I am. Against...who? What in the name of Lion King is this? Jon Crisp vs. Mufanga Kagos. What is a Mufanga Kagos? Is that a disease? Maybe a car? Why am I not fighting Wretch? Someone's going to pay for this. Oh, someone's going to definitely pay for this. It will start with this rookie. I'm sick and tired of fighting these rookies! So this Sunday, we'll be one less on the roster. Kagos, prepare for your worst nightmare. You're about to become 0 and 1. Kagos, your biggest humiliation in your career will be your last. At least it will be in front of the largest audience to watch the show. Because I'm ratings. I'm a crowd-pleaser. And, after Sunday, you'll become part of the crowd. Your career will be over. And mine will begin to take off.
