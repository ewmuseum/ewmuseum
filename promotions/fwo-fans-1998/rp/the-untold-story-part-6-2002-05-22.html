The Untold Story(Part 6)

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sarasota, Florida. Superior Championship Wrestling Presents: Showtime. It was strange. The last time Sean attended an Superior Championship Wrestling(SCW) event... he was an active competitor. Top tier. Regarded by many of it's athletes as the greatest World Champion of all time. A legend. That was last time. Sean was on a mission. To find Linda Johns. To iron out his past... and, form his future the RIGHT way, with Ivy by his side. It hadn't been long since he last competed there, so he was still fresh in everyone's minds. Everybody knew who he was. Even the newcomers who hadn't seen him in the flesh, heard the stories. Normally, Superior Championship Wrestling's security is tight as freshly banged nails... but, Sean was cool with the security team... and, because they were in Florida... Sean's hometown. They simply presumed he was there as some sort of a ratings boost. No questions asked. No explanations necessary. It was quite surprising... but, Sean didn't dwell too hard. He knew EXACTLY where she was. SCW's owner, Edward Brown had re- hired Johns as his personal assistant again, after patching things up and forgiving her for choosing Stevens over her job. THAT's what drove Sean away. The lucrative contract the fWo offered him had nothing to do with it, he'd tell you. Sean continued down the long corridor, before stopping at a door, marked: Edward Brown. Not even pausing to knock... he rudely opened it... causing Brown - who was sitting on his plush leather sofa, to almost choke on the carrot sticks he had been consuming at the rate of a nascar driver. Sean didn't sit... he just stood there... staring. And, Ed... hardly afraid of Sean... stood as well. Eye- to- eye... man- to- man. Brown - "Give me the name of the security guard that allowed you through MY doors... and, I'll personally see to it that he NEVER works again." Stevens - "Trust me, Ed... if I were a cold hearted, careless, son- of- a- bitch like you... I'd do it. ...but, I'll leave THAT up to you. I'm lookin' for Linda." Brown - "Well, I'm afraid you're out of luck, Sean. Because I take pride in being who I am... a cold, careless, son- of- a- bitch... and, I could care less. I'm not telling you anything." Stevens - "Cut the crap, Ed. I know you don't like me... and, you know I down like you. I'm not out here to be your friend. I'm not out here to ask you for any favors. And, as far as I'm concerned... you're the second coming to Satan. And, I'm not interested in making any deals with the devil. But, I know you'll do anything for this promotion. And, I also know that, right now, you're in MY hometown, with fans out there who will jump that guardrail, and rip that ring to shreds at my command. What can I say... I'm loved here." Brown - "Are you threatening me?" Brown had a small walkie-talkie attached to the right side of his hip. He slowly began to reach for it, in hopes of Sean not seeing it. He did. Sean snatched the device and, threw it full strength at the white cinderblock wall, that made up the foundation of the building they were in, smashing it into pieces. Stevens - "I guess you could say that." Brown hissed hard. Sucking his teeth, silently cursing himself for being in the postion he was in. He simply hated being outsmarted, and for the time being, it appeared... he was outsmarted. Brown - "... in the lobby. Getting me a cup of coffee." Stevens turned his back to Brown, heading for the dressing room exit. "But, I swear to God, Sean. You ruin my show... I'll make you regret EVER being born!!" Slam. Stevens was gone.
