Sweet Child O' Mine - Prologue

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SWEET CHILD O’ MINE Prologue – Classic Timing January 22, 2003 5:58 PM Toronto, Ontario, Canada “Angel, is it just me or do you take twice as long in the bathroom as ever before?” No sooner did TJ step away from the door than Angel opened it and stepped out. “I’m eating for two, sleeping for two, I’m as big as two people… why shouldn’t I be allowed to put on makeup for two?” An off- handed suggestion that Valerian’s Garden take a working vacation of Canada so their lead singer could be with her husband (Shhh!) when she had their baby turned out even better than they could’ve hoped for. The clubs have loved ‘em, their CD has sold out, and they’ve even found an Internet bulletin board populated by Canadian fans tracking their route masked against the fWo’s schedule, letting the band know where they should play and what they thought of the show before and bla, bla, bla ad infinitum. Angel might’ve suffered two cases of false labor while touring but it hasn’t dampened any of their spirits, and it hasn’t caused any problem with their performances. Quite the contrary, it’s been a fun ride. “Because there’s six people on this bus, and even as two… we can pick your pregnant ass up?” She playfully punched TJ in the arm, although their definitions differ as he held his arm in pain. A quick look in her compact and she added two black lines away from her eyes with mascara. “You havin’ a good time, Teej?” “Oh hell yeah,” replied her bassist, “Tunes?” “Tool?” “It’s always Tool. Bowie?” “KISS?” “Lords?” “Lords… Farstucker?” “Lust?” “VooDoo?” “Evil?” “Perfect.” TJ turned on the bus’ sound system and forwarded tracks to “Out Comes the Evil” by Lords of Acid. He retreated to the back end of the bus to search through some of his “working clothes” – he has a reputation for being a glam rocker and doesn’t intend to disappoint. Angel danced ambiently around the bus, in as much room as she could with the couches and electronics set up. “You know, Teej,” she said as she danced, “Think we could get Praga to mix some of the new album?” “Probably not,” replied TJ, “He’s been pretty busy since he left Lords. You’d do better trying to shake hands with Maynard again and actually remembering your name this time.” “Ha-ha,” she said, sarcastically, “You’re so goddamn witty. It just so happens Troy knows someone who knows someone who thinks Maynard might be a fan of the band.” “Oh great,” continued TJ, “So your dreams are coming true by rumor now?” She put down he compact and looked at her best friend, not with wit in her eye but with a serious look. “You just wait, Teej… we’re gonna be there. Soon as this baby is born and I’m back in the saddle again, we’re taking over the world. Hell, we’ve conquered North America now, right?” “Definitely,” replied TJ, “If you’re back.” Angel stopped, mid- powder, and looked back toward TJ. “Why wouldn’t I be back?” “Think about it, Angel,” replied TJ, no trace of malice in his voice, “EJ’s on the road right now… and you won’t be able to go back on the road for at least a few weeks. My prediction is that you’ll fall completely in love with this kid and we’ll all go back to basement jam sessions in the span of a few months.” “Oh c’mon,” replied Angel, “I’m gonna need to take a few days or weeks or whatever off, but the band’s not finished. And at the worst, this kid can go on tour with momma or poppa for a few years, just like Ozzy’s kids.” “Great,” said TJ, “So you’ll have another Jack or Kelly Osbourne runnin’ around.” TJ headed past Angel toward the front of the bus and looked out – Nine and Mickey were smoking just outside the door. “Hey, we havin’ a show tonight or what, guys?” “Fuck off, glitterboy,” replied Mickey with a smirk. “Ahhh, what a kidder,” said TJ to Angel, ignoring the two men outside except to give Mickey the finger as only a friend can, “Gonna play some more of the new stuff tonight, Angel?” He looked in her direction after he didn’t get an answer… and saw her standing next to the couch, an intense look on her face. “You okay?” No answer again. As TJ took a step toward her, she sank to her knees, holding her stomach. “Hey—“ He put a hand on her shoulder and she looked up at him, pain etched across her face. In that moment… he knew. “The baby?” “Ohhh…. HOLY fuck,” said Angel, as she somehow was able to stand back up, “Yeah… I think this one’s for real.” TJ looked around, confused. “Shit, shit shit shit shit shit! Where’s Troy?” “Troy’s havin’ a drink in at the bar,” replied Mickey as he stepped back onto the bus, “Why?” “I’m… fuck…. Going into labor,” Angel’s words were a struggle to get out, leaning against the side of the bus, “FUCK!” TJ and Mickey both flinched, as Angel punched the bus window so hard her hand went right through it. “TROY!” called both men. “Whadd’reya doin’ ta my bus?” asked Troy, finally climbing inside, “You gonna get inside or what?” His eyes locked on Angel, holding her stomach with one hand and staring in shock at her other – covered with rings, blood, and broken glass. “Go get Nine,” said Mickey to TJ, “and Sex… and let ‘em know what’s up. We’ve gotta get her to the hospital.” “And page Eli,” added Angel. “Yes,” agreed Mickey, “Page the man… he should be at the arena.” Troy was already behind the wheel, turning the key. “Alright, kiddo… next stop, the birthing room.” Angel didn’t answer… she just glared at him. The unspoken statement was loud and clear. Fuckin’ move, man!
