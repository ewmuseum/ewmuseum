Mother of the Year

13 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Let's see those tits!" When David Cross walked into the Gentleman's Club, it was like returning to high school years after graduating. It was somewhere he felt familiar, without any real emotional connection to it. He'd spent years as a mop boy in a peepshow, so he used to seeing gyrating naked women trying to milk a few bucks from crude men drunk on overpriced liquor. Less mess in here though. Not so much jerking off. David walked through the neon darkness, past the waterfall of smoke that spilled off the stage. The resident bouncer watched the show with a detached interest, yet didn't seem startled when Crucifix tapped him on the shoulder. "I'm here to see Dakota," he yelled over the blaring music. The bouncer never took his eyes off the girl spinning around the stripper pole like a spoke on a bicycle tire. She was obviously no headliner. Her shoulder-length black hair was a bad dye job that didn't conceal her blonde roots. The makeup was just as piss-poor as her hair coloring. The poor girl couldn't even afford to get a decent costume. Not even that memory made him feel as dirty as he felt now, seeing this poor girl trying to make a few bucks simply by taking off her clothes. She probably didn't have a driver's license yet. David tugged on the Neanderthal's tie. "Uh, Lurch? I rang." Lurch's hand engulfed Cross's head. "That's it smartass. You're outta here." "Bup I'n hurr ta fee Dakofa." "Yeah, you and everybody else." Lurch released David's head and grabbed his upper arm instead. "Let's go." Despite his protests, David was dragged to the back of the club and thrown out the back door. The bouncer unbuttoned the cuffs of his shirt and rolled his sleeves up before stepping outside himself. The two guys shooting pool nearby smiled at each other as they imagined the beating Lurch was dispensing. Seconds later, the door opened quickly and David stepped back in. The two men by the pool table just watched him in shock as he passed the stage and snuck behind the curtain while School Girl tried to work the banana clip out of her hair, all sexy-like. Once upon a time, when the club was more of a dance club rather than a "dance" club, there were two sets of bathrooms. Now that the club's owner had taken the establishment in a new direction, the Ladies Room was now the Ladies Dressing Room. Women never came into the club so using the bathroom was never an issue. David pushed the door open about a foot and started to slip in, checking his six to make sure nobody was coming up behind him. He should have been watching his front, as he almost slipped right into the ample cleavage of tonight's headliner, Lindsay Lovelace. "Hey! You can't be back here!" she screeched. "Dahling, eets okay." Cross snapped his fingers and swiveled his head. "I am Dakota's Hair Stylist." Lindsay rolled her eyes. "Figures. Diva." David let Lindsay pass by and closed the door behind him. The row of sinks and mirrors were still there, but to make room all but one toilet had been removed. Presently, the only woman still in the room was a redhead wrapped in a housecoat, snorting lines off the edge of one of the sinks with a rolled up dollar bill. "Classy," David said under his breath. He cleared his throat to get her attention. She looked up and pinched her nose as she plopped herself down in a chair. "Dakota?" he asked. "That's me honey." Dakota wiped her nose with the back of her hand, and then pinched his nostrils again. "You're that guy who had some kind of important news for me, right?" She blinked rapidly and pulled a pack of cigarettes out of her pocket. "Someone die and leave me money?" she asked as she lit one. "Funny, but it's the exact opposite." David rubbed the back of his neck, trying to find the right words. "Look, I don't know the best way to say this so I'm just going to come out and say it." He took a deep breath. "Your daughter, Sarah, is alive." "Yeah, I know. And?" David's jaw hung slack for a moment. "That's... not exactly the reaction I was expecting." "Well why would I be surprised?" Dakota held her cig between her fingers and picked up a small, opaque bottle. "You want a taste?" she asked as she tapped the powdered contents on the counter top. David shook his head. "Nick had me working at one of his clubs in Bangkok. I saw Sarah when I was there." She chuckled once and picked up her dollar roll. "Better her than me." In fear of being knocked over with a feather, David placed a hand on the counter and watched Dakota snort up her nose candy again like a proverbial vacuum cleaner. "How... how long did you know?" "I dunno." Snort. "Six months maybe?" Snooooort. David bit his bottom lip to try and shake the feeling of dread and nausea he suddenly felt. "What was she doing in Bangkok?" "She's staying with someone." Dakota smiled and stared at him with dull, bloodshot eyes. "A friend of Nick's. She'll be making more money than me soon. Does a Mommy proud." "And... Vic doesn't know?" Dakota laughed. "Why the fuck would I tell that bastard? Let him suffer. It's more fun seeing him miserable." The angry knot in David's stomach got the best of him, and he slapped the roll out of her hand, knocking her cigarette ash into her lap as well. "What kind of Mother are you? Allowing your thirteen year old daughter to be some kind of Bangkok cumdumpster for a bunch of horny Thai men?" "Don't forget the tourists." Dakota brushed away the ash and picked up her dollar bill. "They're big tippers." Lurch shoved the door open, followed by Lindsay Lovelace. She hadn't even raised her arm to point at Crucifix before Lurch punched Cross in the back of the neck. His bloody nostrils flared as he bearhugged David and lifted him off the floor. There wasn't any point in fighting back. David had done what he had set out to do. Now he felt worse than he had before. Dakota stood up as David was dragged out of the room. "Hey!" she yelled. "If you see my ex-husband, tell that asshole Thanks for the crabs!"
