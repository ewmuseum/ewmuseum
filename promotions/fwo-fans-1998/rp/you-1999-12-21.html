You?

21 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The black limousine makes a rapid stop, in front of the quality hotel, Courage is currently occupying. Courage walks through the doors, while two young boys scurry in order to take his luggage. "Hurry up you idiots!" he shouts, and the 'servants' of this hotel complete the job. Once again, he ignores their gesture for a tip, and enters the limousine. Geoff Green awaits, and immediately begins filming.) You? You think you equal rock 'n roll? Havok, the only thing you'll ever equal is an fWo jobber. Let's not forget Havok, you were pinned by Heftel. Heftel? ..and you think you're rock 'n roll. Primetime is rock 'n roll! Why? He's a former World Champion, he's a French Foundation member, to put it simply.. he rocks. Courage is rock 'n roll! Why? Because I'm Courage, the greatest. The skill, the talent, the looks all formed into one. Havok, you're not rock 'n roll, you're not great, and you're not original, because that Millennium man gimmick, it's been done more than Strangler's. Havok, you don't know the first thing about rock 'n roll, and until you start worshipping Courage, you'll never get close to rock 'n roll. (Courage flicks a small button, located on the arm rest of the limousine. A television extracts from the roof, and footage of Havok's recent interview begins.) [Havok points to himself again.] Havok: *This* is Rock n' Roll. Havok: *This* is the real Millennium Man. Havok: *This* is greatness. *PAUSE - Havok's face is paused on the screen* (Courage laughs arrogantly, before gesturing towards the screen.) *That* is a Y2Moron *That* is an idiot pinned by Heftel. *That* is a moron who was defeated by Courage, even after Courage had brought down two TCW jobbers. *That* is the reason the fWo sucks. (Turns to face the camera once again.) ...and Havok, as for your little ladder match with Primetime. Do you think Primetime has time to waste with an idiot like you? Primetime is a former cruiserweight champion, he doesn't need that crappy belt back. Primetime is a World title contender. You're an 'idiot of the year' contender. Though if you really want to get your scrawny physique beat from pillar to post, then the PPV after Twilight, there will be a ladder match. Courage vs Havok. Who ever your idiot friend, named Ken is, will get his wish. He'll also get to see Courage rock your world, baby! ...because *only* The Foundation equals rock 'n roll! Not you, you suck. (Scene fades to black.)
