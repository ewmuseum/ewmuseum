In the age of super-boredom, hype and mediocrity...

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If you could go anywhere, where would you end up? Who would you take with you?... The corny voice on a small-budget travel agency commercial faded away in Amy's mind, and she was suddenly aware of all the people around her... Surrounded by them. All familliar faces, but the redhead felt nothing as she looked them over... She was sitting in the center of a friend's birthday party...watching TV. Music blasted around her, and she just drowned it out with volume on the set. People were smiling and getting nothing in response: she didn't even spare a frown. Even her clothing was dreary; all black, without a hint of flesh showing...though you couldn't blame her, even inside it felt like the North Pole. Very few people in a small corps of friends understood Amy Campbell and even when they did, her change of mood in the great adventure of maturing she'd undertaken in the past ten or twelve months was still pure mystery. If anybody knew her, though, it was the girl who Aimz found slumping down beside her with a smile while she giggled, "Jeez, Ames...who died?" "What?" Amy was suddenly torn from the tiny universe that had been contained in the blank stare she'd projected ahead. "Oh...hey, Renee..." The young woman couldn't help but smile even wider and shake her head, laughing again as she muttered "Same old Amy." 'Yeah, same old me...' She thought to herself. 'You know I wasn't like this a year ago...don't you? Just keep pretending like it's all cool.' But only a small, forced grin parted her lips. But then, Renee spoke up again after a long search into her long-time best friend's eyes. It seemed like she was the only person Amy would have a conversation with anymore...but why? "Look, what's wrong? You've been really...distant...lately." "Lately? I've been 'displeased' with a lot of things for a while now." Silence for another moment, then her friend's heart sank. "That's depression, isn't it?" Amy's reply was curt and simple as ever. "Yeah. So?" "Maybe you should see a-" She got cut off by the redhead, "A what? A doctor? Yeah right...and what're they gonna say other than 'I can't help that you can't wrestle worth shit suddenly and I have no idea why you're a fucking loser'.?" "No, but I'm assuming this is one of those times where I tell you not to be so hard on yourself? You finished off that drinking issue that would've become a serious problem and that accomplishment in itself is something eighty percent of people wouldn't have the will for, but at the same time you're growing way far out from everybody except for, like, me and.....that guy..." "Darcy?" She shook her head. "No, even he notices you getting distant, and that's sure as hell a statement if you're pushing Darc', of all people, away. But I'm talking about that other guy...Brandon, or something." A sudden color came to Campbell's cheeks when she realised that her growing friendship, though she denied it, with the Mighty Hero hadn't gone un-noticed. "What're you trying to tell me, exactly?" Renee had hit a 'soft spot', so to say. Aimz wasn't up to start pouring out everything she thought about everyone, especially Brandon Roche but her best friend was outstanding in her persistance... No matter, she wouldn't give an inch and Renee knew that. "Ah, nothing...it's nothing, just nevermind." She managed a weak smile and brushed aside the oncoming conversation that Amy'd all but taken a bat to. "Well why don't you at least just hang out with some people. I mean, you know them...but they're starting to kinda wonder if you're not getting some kind of ego..." Aimz' answer was just as concise as they'd been all night, no matter who'd been talking. "Do you think that?" "Of course not! They just don't know you li--" "Meh," The redhead sank back against the sofa, pretending not to care as she always did. "Fuck the rest of them...they can come talk to me, I might not be the best conversationalist anymore, but maybe I'll teach them not to assume the negative before they can actually see for themselves, hmm?" "Alright." Was the only reply she got. It was her 'little sister' of sorts turning the tables and becoming the one suddenly so silent and unmoving. Renee did move after a moment to stand up...however, she made another sudden change and turned around, smiling after a moment of thought. "Hey...Amy?" "Mmhmm?" "Y'know all about what you just said?" She tried to look a little 'stupid', wide-eyed at the redhead. Amy just mumbled half-heartedly, having almost forgotten her words from before. "Yup..." Another smile. This time it was twice as thoughtful, and threw in a verbal slap. "Practice what you preach." ...maybe I'll teach them not to assume the negative before they can actually see for themselves...
