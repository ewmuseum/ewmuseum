Indiana Speck and the Temple of Fire: Boulders: 1, Physics: 0.

1 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Over the fence, through the door, to the wonderful Wizard of Oz we go. Okay, so it didn't rhyme. And okay, so we're not going to see the Wizard of Oz so much as we're going to see a giant room with tall pillars and boulders that somehow manage to roll around the room as if they're possessed. And they seemed to be possessed into SQUISHING the Codebreakers. So, after like five minutes of running around, the Codemaster stopped in his tracks and held his hand out, causing all of the boulders that were chasing them to suddenly stop in their tracks. "HOLD IT!" Codemaster shouted. "What the hell are you doing!?" Link shouted, as he and Mike Heftel cleared out to safer ground, leaving Ken War and the Codemaster alone to face the boulders. "I refuse to sit by and watch this blatant disregard for the fundamental laws of physics any more! You boulders should not have even chased us for as long as you have! This is just a flat plane, which has more than its fair share of friction! You boulders would have lost all of your momentum and come to a stop no more than four and a half minutes ago! NOT TO MENTION that it is physically impossible for a boulder on a FLAT PLANE to simply stop and go off in another direction! Not to mention that every time you run into one of us, we simply bounce off, you stop rolling, pause, then START rolling again! But your spherical nature would NORMALLY cause us to simply be crushed under your weight! What the hell do you idiots have to say for yourselves!?" the Codemaster ranted. One of the boulders turned to another, did what humans would probably refer to as shrug, and proceeded to roll over and crush Ken War to death. The Codemaster looked at this, before he sighed. "Well met." He ran. I mean, what was he going to do against boulders that felt like defying the laws of friction, acceleration, and momentum? Well, first he tried hitting it with his Keyblade, but then it broke in two. "Lousy Kingdom Hearts weaponry. No wonder Sora sold it to me." he grumbled, before he tossed away the broken pieces of the stupid weapon and dashed through the next door along with Heftel and Link to find themselves in the next room. It was a large, circular room, and the Codemaster and his allies had found themselves on narrow strips of land that just happen to A) not have any guardrails to prevent you from falling off and B) are about three stories above the previous room. The path ran to a door, and also branched off halfway to a gated passageway that was apparently linked to an eye. "Well, genius, I think you have to shoot the eye, now." Codemaster said to Link. "What?! But I need my arrows for shooting creatures that piss me off." Link objected, before he pulled out a small notebook and pointed at the section that said 'things that piss Link off'. The Codemaster glared at it, and then at Link, "But... but but but... all it says here is 'everything'!" Link pulled out his sword, "Yeah, that's right, bitch. So, you keep givin' me orders and I'll personally slit your throat and every last throat of your family members, INCLUDING Shirley and Mexican Wayne!" "Who the hell are those?" "No idea, but the point is that I am not a elven brother to fuck with. Got it?" Link said, sternly. "Fine." With that, the Codebreakers carefully walked across the narrow walkway, although Ken War DID fall off, which alerted a certain group of beings that were still rather pissed off at our heroes. "HEY! That was one of those guys!" Murray, the leader of the pissed Fire Keese, just as Ken War fell into the lava and died again. "Let's kill them!" And with that, the Fire Keese rushed upwards and rapidly approached our illustrious heroes. And, well, Mike Heftel was shockingly the first to notice it. "Hey, guys, do you see that big, all-encompassing light that's heading toward us that sound like thousands upon thousands screeching bats that are all wanting to kill us for murdering some guy named Lenny?" Mike Heftel asked. The Codemaster sharply turned to Link, "Man, do you EVER go anywhere without pissing people the HELL off?" Link responded by calmly dropping a bomb on the Fire Keese, before he left the room toward where they were headed. Heftel and Codemaster shrugged and followed Link into the next room. "Uh oh." was all Murray could say before he and the other Fire Keese were consumed in a massive explosion that caused the entire Fire Temple to shake. Of course, have you ever heard the term "out of the frying pan and into the fire"? Well, replace "frying pan" with "cages over scorching hot magma" and replace "fire" with "a big wall of flames that just now appeared by our heroes". Heftel wet himself, "Aw, mittens." The Codemaster spotted a door on a high ledge and pointed at it. Link nodded, but also proceeded to shoot some Fire Keese out of the sky with his bow and arrows, then slice a flaming skull in two, and ended up reaching the ledge just inches before the wall of flame could reach him, and in double the time it took for Codemaster, Heftel, and Ken War(who SOMEHOW managed to instantly climb up three stories and reassemble himself out of molten lava) to reach it. "Having fun?" Codemaster asked Link. "Fuck you." Link responded. And then they entered the next room, filled with even more firey death than you can shake Ken War's left femur at! ........But hey, we'll talk about the next room later, won't we?
