Unexpected twists

2 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Squalor followed William Magnum Decker around like stink on shit but the fat bastard was too dense to notice. Decker sat in his cramped rented room. The lights shining through the small dirty window promised excitement and entertainment but this, too, went unnoticed. The mostly-full bottle in his hand and the television were all the entertainment Decker wanted tonight. Perhaps ‘entertainment’ was too strong a word; what he really wanted was to drink himself into oblivion so he could forget the name ‘Fairview Reed’ for just one night…just one fucking night… He tipped the bottle and took a long, deep drink of the clear liquid. He winced as the bottle came down and he opened his mouth to try to dissipate the alcohol’s sting. He hated vodka, especially cheap vodka, but the dingy convenience store across the street wasn’t the kind of place that stocked high-end hooch and Boone’s Farm just wasn’t going to cut it tonight. The ghostly glow of the television did its best to illuminate the tiny room but the deep dim shadows were not about to concede defeat, and that was probably for the best – the room was littered with a cacophony of stained crumpled clothes and fast food wrappers. Again, a sad little scene and again, a perfect vignette of the life of William Magnum Decker. Television in the wee hours of the morning is a wretched and pitiful prospect, especially without the dubious benefits of cable. It didn’t really matter to Decker – his beady eyes weren’t registering the images anyway. He was far more concerned about racing to the bottom of the bottle and hitting what passed for sleep. He was almost there so of course that’s when his luck soured once again and the local station he was tuned to ran their sports report. Even through his liquor-induced haze the words ‘Fans Wrestling Organization’ assaulted his ears like an ice pick. The bottle slipped from his fingers, his eyes cleared briefly and he sat up with a mumbled curse. The announcer’s words were slipping by too quickly for him to focus on but that was irrelevant; even here in the filthiest little corner of Seattle in the darkest part of the night he couldn’t escape this case, this one fucking case – the case that proving to be his one single failure as a private dick. Fucking Fans Wrestling Organization… Fucking Fairview Fucking Reed… His arm was cocked back before he knew what he was doing, ready to hurl the bottle at the TV but his hand was empty and the bottle was spilling its contents on the threadbare carpet. It took him a few precious moments to realize what was happening before he reached down and picked the bottle back up, and it went right up to his lips. Maybe it was the copious amount of vodka he’d consumed, maybe it was the six months of fruitless searching, or maybe he was just tired – whatever the reason William Magnum Decker’s legendary temper fizzled before it really got started. And there it was; Decker didn’t even have the energy or interest about this case to get angry anymore. He was spent emotionally. He didn’t care about Fairview Reed, he didn’t care about the exorbitant amount of money he was promised, he didn’t care about his impeccable record. He just wanted to go home… His phone rang. Decker chose not to answer. At this time of night it could only be a wrong number. He took another long swig and scratched his balls. A smile found its way to his face – he felt good for the first time in a very long time. Quitting this case was the right decision, the best decision he’d made since agreeing to take the case in the first place. His phone rang again. He sighed through his smile. Not even a wrong number could bring him down; he was going home. He grabbed his phone from the nightstand and answered it. “Lo?” “I take it you have nothing to report.” The bottle dropped from his fingers again. That voice… “I…um…listen; I-“ “Quiet. I understand. I have monitored your progress and am not displeased. This assignment was a test and you have proven to be adequate for the task.” That voice – the gruff, deep sound like someone playing a bass violin with a chainsaw. It was HIM. The man who’d sent him on this search in the first place. “Listen, I-“ “QUIET! Fairview Reed is no longer your concern. Concentrate on the physician.” “Wha…who…” “Burlington Werner is your focus now. Start with the Germans, they are the key.” Click. William Magnum Decker’s world turned upside down. Thirty seconds ago he was ready to fold his tent and head home. Thirty seconds ago he was confident and sure of his decision. Thirty seconds ago he was smiling… One phone call took all that away from him. “Fuck…” Fuck indeed.
