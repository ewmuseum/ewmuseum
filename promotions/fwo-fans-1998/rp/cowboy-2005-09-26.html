Cowboy

26 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 14th would be a day that Olivre Braley would always remember. It wasn't just a day that people everywhere feel the sweet sting of Cupid's arrow, but a day that held a great deal of personal importance to him. On this day in 1998, he won the National rodeo championship for the third consecutive year. This wasn't all, however. This was the day that changed Olivre Braley's life. This was the day he became...The Midnight Cowboy. Coming off the heels of a rodeo win earlier in the evening, Olivre found himself celebrating with friends at a local bar. Because he was an upright man and mostly abstained from alcohol, it wasn't surprising that he ducked out early. A friend offered to call a cab, but Braley decided he would walk back home. And why not? The streets were clear, it was a gorgeous evening, and the sky overhead was dotted with lustrous stars. The beauty was halted abruptly as a rustling in the brush nearby caught his ear. He frowned briefly but ultimately thought nothing of it and continued on his way. On the other hand, the noises were becoming more apparent, and soon a voice accompanied these awkward sounds. "Help!" The verbal S.O.S. caused Midnight Cowboy to spring into his action, tearing his way through the disproportionate tree limbs that stood in his path. He arrived at a scene that would forever chill him. A man had forced himself upon a helpless girl, who pinned beneath this monster who had lust on his mind. The woman's lips drew blood; little streams trickling down her mouth, the droplets falling off her chin into the small puddle on the earth. Braley would not allow the man to follow through with his perverse plan, and immediately grabbed him. He pulled him to his feet, but the man seemed to be struggling with embarrassment as his pants that were around his ankles. Braley lunged forward and knocked the shocked man to the ground with a furious haymaker. The man's naked rear-end fell upon the soft earth, but not for long. Olivre Braley lifted the man up, and with all his might and knowledge of fighting, painted a portrait of destruction with the man's blood. The female victim sprawled backwards, in awe of this hero that had come to her rescue. Minutes passed and the beating finally reached a conclusion. The man, a mere pulp consisting of flesh and broken bones, laid motionless on a random mess of leaves. The woman rushed to her feet and opened her arms as if to embrace Olivre, but he put up a palm to stop her. She stood back and shot a puzzled look at this rugged man with a cowboy hat, dimly lit by the moonlight. She leaned forward, ready to hang on whatever he might say, if anything. The Midnight Cowboy back-peddled a few feet, tipping his hat slowly and politely panting, "Ma'am." With that solitary word, Olivre Braley strode off into the night, leaving behind a rescued damsel who just HAD to tell someone about this whether it be the newspapers, major news channels or by the growing internet... To her delight and Olivre's regret, the story showed up on all three. And what was a good, selfless deed had turned into nothing more than a mockery, a butt of jokes that unfortunately retained their humor for quite a while...
