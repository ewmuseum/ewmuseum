A journey of a thousand steps, Part 7

4 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mike Bear storms down the nearly empty hallway. Any art or furniture was removed long ago once the knowledge of who would be a frequent guest on this particular floor got out. There are no trash cans between or anywhere near the office from which he emerged and the elevators where he's headed. Faced with a possible strike from the janitorial staff and no way to evict the tenant hosting him, the building company compromised, making sure that no matter what state he was in, that his exit would cause as little damage as possible. CRUNCH Supposing that he didn't leave dents in the drywall from punching the wall. Oh well. They'll need the plaster and paint again. There was always so much about walking out of here that infuriated him. When there is nothing to break, nothing to direct your anger towards, how can the anger be released? You can't slam an elevator door, and contrary to apparent popular belief, hitting the down button to call the elevator more than once will *not* cause it to come faster no matter how you do it. He walks to the elevators quickly and hits the button. His heavy breathing and the snarl that contorts his face are all he has to prevent him him from kicking through the elevator door and jumping down the empty shaft. That would at least get him where he needed to go faster, he thinks. He wonders to himself what he truly means by that, but that thought is short lived as the elevator arrives after what feels like an eternity, but was actually no more than 20 seconds. He rarely notices it, but time often seems to slow to a crawl when he becomes as enraged as he is now. To wait for anything is torture, things must happen immediately, and with that mindset comes a heightened awareness of his surroundings, and thus a heightened awareness of how long each passing second is. The elevator ride is comparatively short, this office building a relatively recent construction with fast elevators installed. However, his ride isn't over at the lobby. The doors open to reveal an attractive blond with her hair tied back in her early 30s. Wearing a gray Lycra long sleeved shirt with a loose black vest over top, loose fitting black pants similar to those in a martial arts gi, and black fingerless gloves, she appears ready for a fight. But then, in a fight, both people are required to fight back. This is most like a beating, like always. Brittany lunges at Mike with a right cross to the chin. Mike staggers a little, but doesn't go down. However, that leaves him open to a straight left to the solar plexus, doubling him over. Brittany quickly moves behind her husband and applies an extremely painful hold to Mike's collarbone, causing him to yell out in pain and to go down to one knee. Brittany smiles and waves to the lobby with her free hand. “Sorry again about this. We'll be going back up.” With her free hand, she pushes the button to return to Dr. Talbert's floor. The doors close once again, and Mike knows it's time for another talk. His wife's voice drips with sarcasm, one of the reasons he fell in love with her originally, now just a painful reminder of how far he's fallen. “Well here we are again, honey. You've stormed out of your friend's office like a child, and I've got to drag you back, kicking and screaming. I would love to go one month, hell, one *week* without having to walk into that lobby before your session is supposed to be over.” Mike considers arguing the point. This doesn't happen *every* week. The thought is brief, though, as he returns to thinking of the extreme pain being inflicted by the hold his wife has on him. “Let me guess, you're upset because of his friend being there to help you too for some reason I can't fathom. God forbid that someone else wants to help you too. But no, better to act like a child here we are...” The elevator doors open to the same hallway as before. Brittany notices the dent in the wall and shakes her head. She moves her free hand in front of the elevator door to prevent it from closing. “You just can't walk out of here without wrecking *something*, can you? Well, what are you waiting for?” Mike looks up at Brittany with a tangled web of emotions on his face, most prominently pain, sorrow, and anger. He says nothing. “Oh don't even. I'm not letting go. Get up. NOW.” Mike lowers his head once again. In agony, he gets up ever so slowly, but has to stay hunched over due to the hold still being applied. Brittany pulls him along back down the hall he had just walked in a rage. This time the pace was much slower. “Right, better to act like a child than to make an effort to try something new that might cause scenes like this to be memories instead of regular occurrences. I wish I was smart enough to know why you can't see past your own stubbornness to see how much this kills me. You think I like making this part of my day?” Tears had begun to fall. Even in his current predicament, without being able to see her face, Mike could tell from the slight warble in her voice he had made her cry again. He could always tell. No emotion ever did justice to how it made him feel. Shame was always a good start. “Apparently you do, because otherwise you'd be making an effort to try and make this better and I could wait for you at home. Well I'll tell you what's going to happen now.” Brittany stops in front of Dr. Talbert's door. She leads Mike around to face her and releases the hold. Mike's face immediately shows a wave of relief and he quickly bring up his hand to covers the area. The relief is short-lived, as Brittany grabs a hold of his sweatshirt and bring pulls him into within 6 inches from her face. Mike has no choice to look at her eyes now, a mixture of anger and sadness, coupled with the tears staining her cheeks hurt just as bad as the collarbone hold. “You are going to walk back into that office, apologize to Dr. Talbert, greet and apologize to the new doctor who is going to help you, and you are going to do what they say, or... or...” Brittany seems visibly shaken, but steels herself for what she was about to say next. “Or someday I may not be here to pick up the pieces for you.” Before Mike can react to that bombshell, Brittany opens the door and practically throws him through it. Swiftly bring the door to a close again, her resolve breaks almost immediately. She falls to her knees and sobs softly, careful to not make too much noise so that she could be heard on the other side of the thick oak doors. She loathed herself for saying such a thing. May not be here? An empty threat if there ever was one. But what could she do? You can't just bang your head against the wall continuously and then complain your head hurts. She needed to try *something* to motivate him. Pain hadn't worked. Unconditional love hadn't worked. If fear worked, on that day she would apologize. For now, though, she'd have to keep being the strong one, and that meant stop crying and walk through that door to speak with her friend Rachel. She dries her tears on her vest and takes a few moments to compose herself. She removed the tie in her hair and let it touch her shoulders again. She hated having it tied back, but it always got in the way when... no, no, don't think about it. Just smile. One of us has to. She calmly walks through the door and waves back when Rachel sees her walk in. “Brittany! Hi! You look lovely today.” “Rachel, please, I'm sweating, my hair's a mess, and I'm dressed for a kung fu movie.” “Well I'm just being honest. I'd never look as good as you do after all that.” Brittany smiles a little more genuinely. Leave it to her to cheer her up again. Good thing those walls around the office were soundproofed. She had her doubts that there were any circumstances where Rachel would lower her natural volume. Not in an office, at least. Brittany takes a chair from against the wall and brings it up to Rachel's desk to sit in. “Thanks Rach. You look nice as always. So, when am I going to get to meet this new boyfriend of yours?”
