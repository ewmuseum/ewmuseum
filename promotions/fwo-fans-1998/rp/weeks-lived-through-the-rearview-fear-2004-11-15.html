Weeks Lived Through The Rearview: Fear

15 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 7th, 2004. *Tick* *Tick* *Tick* With every second that passed, the fear that dwelled within Milo's head grew. He'd never been this scared in his life. He'd been chased by a deranged psycho stalker, fought the best the Asylum had to offer, and went toe to toe with Asylum owner Joe Campbell. Milo Samus was no stranger to struggle... but anything prior to this paled in comparison. It was a long battle, and what made it worse was the fact that he could do nothing to end it. This was a battle of a different sort. This enemy had no face... and knew no mercy. Worst of all, it was stealing the one person Milo had... his best friend... his lover. "Why Jill? Why couldn't it be me laying in that hospital bed?" A question he'd asked himself a thousand times before. If only there was some way to remove the cancer which was consuming Jill's body... If only Milo could be medication for her, and flush the poison from her body... but all he could do is watch as the cancer ravaged her. *Tick* *Tick* *Tick* Milo tried to ignore the second hand on the waiting room clock, as it continued on its slow, redundant journey. The soft buzzing of neon lights above him was the soundtrack to, what had to be, the worst day of his entire life. In front of him sat a small table, which was littered with a library of assorted uninteresting and out-dated magazines. Why someone would want to read "The Outlaw Biker Tattoo Revue", as they waited to visit a terminally ill loved one, was a total mystery to him... but with few options, he read on. "Mr. Samus?" a nurse spoke softly, trying her best not to startle him. “You can see her now.” “Thank you.” Milo replied, tossing the magazine back onto the unorganized heap. He then stood, and followed close behind the nurse as she led him down a well-lit hallway. The nurse stopped, and turned toward Milo. “This is the room.” Milo nodded to her, and made his way through the lavender door casing. His knees felt weak as he made his way toward the hospital bed. In front of him, lay Jill Adams… It seemed as if she had an angelic glow about her, and her beauty somehow managed to withstand the turmoil the cancerous minions had been putting her body through. Milo stood by her bedside, gently gripping her hand. As hard as this was for him, he could only imagine how hard this must be for her. “Milo?” Jill gently spoke, her eyes remaining shut. “Is that you?” Her voice fell upon his ears like the soft sound of a harp. She was heavily medicated, and it had been days since he’d last heard his voice. “Yes, I’m right here with you Jill.” Milo said, gently caressing her hand. “How are you feeling?” “I don’t know how much longer I can hold on Milo…” replied Jill, her eyes remaining shut. Hearing her say this hit him like a dagger to the chest, his eyes began to well-up with tears, and he struggled to prevent himself from breaking down. His heart was in his throat, and this felt like a nightmare… one in which he just couldn’t escape. “Don’t say that Jill…. Don’t give up yet. You’ve come too far to give up. Please don’t leave me….” Milo pleaded, his body trembling. “We can beat this Jill…”. “I want you to know Milo… that I love you… and that I’m sorry.” Her voice was fading… slowing down, with every uttered word. “Sorry? For what?” “I… I’m sorry Milo.” She replied, continuing to fade away. “Sorry for trying – to make you choose between me and your dreams.” Milo broke into tears… It was impossible to hold back. “Jill… you are my dreams. I started to think dreams didn’t come true, but then I met you. You’re a dream come true… I would give up anything in this world for you… and I wish I had of told this more often… but I love you Jill.” Although her eyes were closed, she too had tears running down her face. “Milo – you made me the happiest woman on earth – and I thank you.” And with that, her eyes momentarily opened, as she squeezed Milo’s hand for the last time. Her grip became slack… “Jill… Jill!?” Milo said, burying his face in her chest, hugging her tightly. “Please come back Jill!” No matter how bad this hurt… And no matter how bad he may have wanted to be with her… She was gone.
