Getaway - The Arrival

22 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The foursome's trip had been long, but still much better than last week's sojourn to, and especially from, Afghanistan. From Seattle to New York, and then to Paris had been a near criminal length of time in flight, made worse with the knowledge that they'd be making the same trip in reverse in just a few days. It was made better for all of them that it had all been first-class, and for three of them that it had been on Pierre's dime. Passing through customs had been the breeze that Frenchie had promised, as he was every bit the celebrity in his homeland that he'd boasted about. Throw in a short trip by rented limousine to this small estate just outside of Paris, and the trip was already looking well worth it. Frenchie exited the limousine first, and then assisted the lovely Brittany Bear out, who was in turn followed by her husband, Mike. The last to exit was the Wrought Iron Kid himself, Brawn. The Flying Frenchie looked at his two old friends and his new one, a ten thousand megawatt beam on his face. "It's exactly what we need. A few days of down time, and no rowdy locals. Ze only bad t'ing about spending four days in France is not spending ze ot'er t'ree hundred and sixty-one here." Mike Bear, jetlagged, grunted what might have been an approval while his wife rubbed the back of his neck. "Yeah," was the only thing Brawn could reply, as he stood silent, absorbing his surroundings. "Outside of my hometown, Seattle and Paris are the only places I've ever visited." Brawn hesitated, and it was clear he had something else to say, but didn't. He looked at Frenchie and smiled. The weeks of questioning and second guessing went away with this hospitable gesture. He dug his thumbs into the straps of his backpack, and the foursome began scaling the front steps. Brawn looked over his shoulder. "Lots of space to roam around in," he remarked. "Kinda like home." A crisp breeze brushed across his face and the four of them stopped, eyes closed, and enjoyed the feeling. "I bet the morning runs will feel great." "Tut tut tut," Pierre interjected. "You've been in Paris for less zan an hour and already you talk of running." Pierre slapped Brawn on the shoulder, and ushered his friends inside. "I did not want to get us too bogged down wit' an itinerary, but I did have a few t'oughts. It's been a very long trip, and I suggest we relax here for ze day. Mike, I've got a few old haunts I wanted to introduce you to, if Brit doesn't mind me monopolizing your time." Turning towards Brittany, he continued. "I know you're not much of a 'shopping' woman, but I still have a few suggestions if you'd like to give it a try. And Brawn, mon ami, have I got somet'ing for you." Brawn stopped and turned back towards Frenchie, not quite knowing if he should be excited. "I've convinced Jasmin to work wit' you tomorrow, all day. So you might make your morning run a little light." Brawn blinked back at the offer. "Hold on. You're showing Bear around France, she's shopping in a city known for it, and I flew half way around the world for training?" Pierre took his student’s comment in stride. "Zose two don't have any idea what zey're missing. Jasmin taught me everyt'ing I know. I don't expect you to pick it all up in one day, of course, but if you're serious about succeeding in our sport, zis is an opportunity zat only I and Kid Cool have been able to take, at least among members of ze FWO. I trust you'll be a quicker study zan he was." The pupil looked at the ground and sighed, dropping his bag at his feet. He noticed on a counter a ceramic poodle, which held four pairs of similar keys. "Are these house keys?" Brawn said, picking up a pair. Pierre nodded. He put them in his pocket. "Jasmine gets me tomorrow, but I'd like to check out Paris for myself tomorrow night. Is that okay?" "Commence l'aventure!" Pierre replied, uncorking a bottle of wine.
