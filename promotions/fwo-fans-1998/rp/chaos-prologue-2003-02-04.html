Chaos- Prologue

4 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
People don't realize the value of their own lives anymore. It's all a bunch of psycho-babble bullshit, or pop another pill for whatever you need. People don't realize what was sacrificed to create the world we live in. Yeah, I'm not saying I'm a saint either. Don't look at me like that. I'm nothing more than some asshole who was selected. That's right, selected. I was chosen. As The Second. The Prophet. The First, also known as The Destroyer, has yet to arrive. And The Third and The Foruth, also known as The Killer and The Leader, have yet to realize their purpose. To bring out the Chaos in them will be a struggle- I can forsee that already. I'm not just a Prophet, you see. I'm also a Seer. One who can see the truth, even when it is shrouded within the dark clouds of lies. What allows me to see through the mist? Chaos. It is with His power that I see the truth. It is Him who has showed me what will happen next. You see, as The Prophet, it is my mission to deliver the absolute truth to the masses. To be able to convey the truth to all of you, I must first be able to see it. Chaos realizes this, and grants me the power of sight. So allow me to give all of you a little bit of advice. Stop fighting over which religion is right. The answer is simple. None of them are. This "God" of yours never existed. He didn't create the Earth in seven days- please. You actually think a diety needs seven days to create the world? Chaos created the world, and he did so in an instant. A blink of the eye. Nothing there one moment, the Earth there the next. You can stop searching now. Chaos is your creator. Chaos is your "God". So why must Chaos employ the help of four of its own creations? Allow me to tell you that story. Want to know how we all came to be? Listen closely to the words I speak. Because when Chaos rises again....you'll want to be ready. Be ready to serve.
