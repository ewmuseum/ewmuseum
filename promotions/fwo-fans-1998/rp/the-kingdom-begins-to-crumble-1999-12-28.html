The Kingdom begins to crumble.

28 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene begins in a vaguely familar place, a place that has not been seen in quite some time. To avid viewers they would know that the dreary warehouse in which Geoff Green now nervously stands is also a familiar spot for fWo Internet champion Rain to work out. Geoff Green cautiously enters the dimly lit warehouse with his camera dangling from his hand. He looks around galncing before the well used pieces of equipment and the dusty boxing ring in the center. Geoff quietly proceeds to the corner of the warehouse avoiding all eye contact with large dangerous looking men and other sorts of escaped convicts. Geoff finnaly makes it over to the side of the warehouse where Rain usually is, but he notices no one there. He looks around side to side before he turns and attempts to leave. Rain's massive hand grabs Geoff tight on the shoulder as Geoff nearly jumps out of his socks, as he turns around and isn't relieved to see Rain.] Rain: Leaving so soon ? Geoff Green: Well... ummm... I thought you weren't here, so I was going to leave. Rain: That's alright Geoff, the fWo couldnt find me if I left them a trail of bread crumbs, it's getting annoying to have to give notice when I want to speak my word to my public. Geoff Green: Well.. yeah, I mean I know where you were and .. Rain: Turn the camera on Geoff. Geoff Green: -Righto. [Geoff unpacks his things and gets ready as the massive Rain walks to a nearby bench and picks up the shiny fWo internet title. He admires it for a minute before sliding it over his shoulder and standing before the camera.] Rain: The Millenium draws near, the mark of the exsistence of the human race since the saviour came two thousand years before. Though a higher power celebrates as well, but the god's of the storm will celebrate my good fourtune. The God's have entrusted me with a great power in this time of human confusion and my time to capitalize is coming nearer. Tommorow marks the beginning of the Millenium countdown, today marks a new record, as no single person has held this prestigous title as long as I. Geoff Green: Question.... what happened at Warfare, I mean that was insane ! Rain: Warfare... a triumphant night and only a sample of chaos yet to come. The storm showed his power in great numbers and many a foe shook in their shoes. The Black Panther did give me a run for my title though in the end I came out above and beyond. I will admit the match was won in a less then pleaseable fashion but nonetheless, won. Panther, you have skills but not the strength. The night was capped off with the mad scene at the end, my wrestlers showed my strength, and I will not stop there. I will stop at nothing until the fWo has crumbled at my feet. My Army will prevail, I will lead them, and I will prevail. Rain: As I continue my trek upwards through the fWo, I have reached a detour. Primetime. A man who has lost his titles, and his dignity. A man who lost at Twilight and is in sorrow need of redemption. You believe that my title is your way of strengthening yourself ? As I make my way through the fWo ranks ? I fit must be so, I will destroy you with the clench of my fist. This title shall never leave my hands, only more titles added to it. A desperate man Primetime is the easiest to defeat. Remember this fWo...... when it RAINS it Pours. [The scene fades to black]
