Christmas...even crazier than I am

19 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene fades in on a street in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It's rather busy, just like any other big city during the day. However, given the date, there is a great deal more congestion. Parents scramble everywhere in an attempt to get their children the latest toys and over-hyped paraphernalia. Somehow in the middle of this, Mike Bear is taking a leisurely walk down the street. He's wearing a white shirt and a black fWo jacket over it, a baseball cap with "Underground" embroidered on the front, a pair of blue jeans and hiking boots. His hands are jammed in his pockets given the brisk Canadian weather, but he doesn't appear to be that cold otherwise. He starts even as people run between him and the camera) I've never taken a stance against a certain country in all my wrestling days. Well, maybe France...anyway. I've never taken a side in the US vs. Canada battle that's gone on for years. I've never had a real problem with Canada. I made my name up here. And that hasn't changed. What has changed is my respect for their supposed "Hardcore Legend" Cactus Jon. Jon, let's take a trip down memory lane. Way back when I was working for you in TCW, I didn't get the push I thought I deserved, and began to believe it was your fault for not giving me the oppurtunity. I believed you were screwing me. That's what drove me over the edge. But you ended up helping me after everything was said and done. You helped open up my love for pain, which helped me win the fWo Hardcore Title. You were the one who got me promoted in the first place. However, last Sunday on Slaughter, it was a different story. Jon, this time you really did try to screw me. Wretch and I deserved a return title match against Ultra Cool 2000 because of the circumstances surrounding our loss. Then you came along and got your two little piece of crap Canadian partners in a number one contenders match against us, despite the fact that they hadn't wrestled a single match. Then during the match, you pulled the freakin ref out of the ring when we had the match won! And then you freakin spit on me after the match! You...spit on me. I should be spitting on you and your promotion. I carried TCW on my back with the New Breed when no one else could. And you have the nerve to disrespect me? Well Jon, tonight we meet in a strap match. We've never met in a match before, you've only been my employer. On Slaughter I will show you why I was the one who carried your promotion. I'll show you how far I've come since the day I left TCW. Ah, here we are. (Mike comes to a stop in front of a large toy store and proceeds inside. In here, every man and woman for themselves. Fist fights break out over who gets the last toy on the self, and the lines to check out stretch around the entire store and out onto the street. Mike pushes his way through the crazed crowd and heads for what's left of the most infamous toy this season: Pokemon. You'd never know it was the section for the world-wide mania if it wasn't for the large Pikachu cardboard cutout, and that's almost destroyed. Mike turns around as a few parents manage to scrape up some of the last remaining toys, the less popular/damaged ones) Jon, this reminds me of your pitiful little tag team. Maybe once they had something inside them, but even that just plain sucked. Even though they're complete and utter garbage, they're with you so they get airtime. And finally, in a short while, they'll be sitting in the closet behind the Destroyer and Barry Bladerth where they belong and should have stayed. I still can't understand how they got put into a number one contenders match for the Tag Team Titles. On Wednesday, in the main event, the first, and last one you two will ever be in, you will be destroyed. Wretch and I will kill you, and we'll make sure you never come back to challenge us again. Jon, Tom, Ninja, by Wednesday, you all will undoubtedly fear...the...Underground. (The camera fades out as people run through the store carying armfuls of toys while other walk out dejected. We cut to a promo for Twilight '99, the last pay per view of the Millennium)
