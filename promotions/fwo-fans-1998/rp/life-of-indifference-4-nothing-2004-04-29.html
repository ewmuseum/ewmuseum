Life Of Indifference [4] Nothing

29 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life is a series of transitions. The transition from being nothing to a living, breathing entity. The transition from crawling to walking. The transition from child to adult. But these are things we know and see, physical changes for all to behold. But what about the ones we can't see? The transition from somebody to nobody. The transition from sane to insane. The transition from being innocent... ...to being a murderer. July 13th 1995. It was the splash of ice-cold water hitting his face that awoke Kellen Kinkade, he went to wipe his face but it became quickly apparent that both his hands had been tied firmly to a chair bolted firmly to the floor in the middle of a large white room. Attempts to kick out also revealed that his legs too were firmly secured, after a few moments of struggling he managed to blink until the water wiped slowly from his good eye. "I'm gonna do some translating for the boss, but most of this conversation is going to be in the universal language of pain so you should be good for the most part." One of the Yakuza's spoke in perfect English as he stood before Kellen clutching in his grasp a cutthroat razor. "You'd better fucking kill me, because I'm good at remembering fac-" Kinkade didn't finish as he suddenly felt the blade swipe across his face just a few inches below his only working eye. "I'm sorry, when I get angry I occasionally will mix up speaking and slashing. You are encouraged to speak when spoken to only by me via the boss... any other outbursts will result in further cutting of your face. Do you understand?" The Yakuza wiped the blade of his razor across his sleeve. "Crystal clear." Kinkade growled as blood slowly started to trickle down his cheek. The Yakuza turned to his boss for a few moments and the two conversed in Japanese before he turned back to Kinkade "We understand that you were informed of your situation in the fight you took part in earlier tonight, we also understand that you know exactly what attempting to stray from the path you'd been asked to walk would result in and we also understand that you cost us a large sum of money." Another few seconds of conversing lead to the next words. "Understand that at this current time we're still coming to a decision on whether or not we'll just torture you to a horrific level or spare you and lop off all of your limbs and incinerate them. But before we do we're going to bring your management in here and see if he can change the situation a little." The Yakuza looked to the door and clicked his fingers, two other gang members quickly burst in dragging Matsumoto with them before pushing him to the floor, he too had been bloodied and beaten. "Please... I tell you everything I kno-" Matsumoto didn't finish as the razor toting Yakuza kicked him in the ribs. "Shut up old man, I already told you about the rules of conversation... must we go over them again." The Yakuza took a step towards Matsumoto who screamed and clutched what appeared to be a stump that was formerly his pinky finger. Another conversation between the Yakuza in his boss brought about a couple of sinister laughs. "Well gentlemen, I have spoken with the boss and he has determined that an eye for an eye will suffice. Considering that both have you have tried to fuck with us tonight... we in turn shall fuck with you, call it a test of your loyalties to one another if you will." The Yakuza closed his razor "Matsumoto... either you can join your friend here in being tortured and both of you will live to see another day. Or you can walk away without being harmed, but we will kill your prize fighter to make sure you remember that we are not to be fooled with... your choice?" "WALK!" Matsumoto screamed "I walk now..." Matsumoto struggled to his feet and started to hobble away as Kinkade roared "You piece of shit, you can't leave me!" to little reaction, what did prompt a reaction however was the hand of the Yakuza on his shoulder. "There is one other small matter, throughout our questioning you insisted that you knew nothing of this fighter and his plan to steal from us. If this is indeed the case then he has caused you much trouble and we are sure that you will take no issue in implementing the first few pieces of torture upon him." The Yakuza kicked towards Matsumoto a leather bag, which Kinkade's trainer picked up and wasted no time in marching toward his former apprentice with. "The boss feels that to start proceedings, you should probably urinate on this individual just as he urinated on your trust." The Yakuza urged Matsumoto on as he started to untie the string holding up his pants. "It will be pleasure." Matsumoto grinned. "Piss on me and I'll kill you motherfucker, if I have to sew back on whatever these pieces of shit cut off I'll find you and when I do you're dead." Kinkade snarled more out of desperation than anything, as Matsumoto let flow. "Can't sew back on arms when dead, American." Matsumoto chuckled as he relieved himself "What you want me do next?" "The pliers should prove to be quite useful on his teeth." The Yakuza pointed out as Matsumoto reached into the bag and produced them. "No... you fuck... n-" Kinkade's words were stifled out as Matsumoto reached into his mouth with the pliers and started wrenching away, after an intense struggle he finally wedged his foot into Kade's ribs and ripped away... dropping the pliers and a couple of teeth to the floor. "Aaaaaaaaaarhhhhh." Kade screamed as blood flowed down from his mouth onto his chest in a jet like fashion "I'll kill you... I'll kill you!" "Now what you want?" Matsumoto turned to the Yakuza who simply barked back "Blowtorch." Matsumoto produced the blowtorch and after fumbling about and lighting it... approached Kade and worked him over for several minutes with it despite his screams and pleas. "That will be all." The Yakuza cut in as Matsumoto pulled the blowtorch away from Kinkade's arm revealing a hideous burn "We will finish the job from here." Matsumoto nodded and quickly made his exit, looking back just in time to see the Yakuza open up his razor blade and approach the struggling Kinkade. The cuts and screams were left behind as he closed the door. July 14th 1995. Matsumoto flicked through a few files and folders and sighed with frustration, finding a new top fighter was proving more difficult than he had anticipated. He hadn't slept all night in his search for a new moneymaker and rubbed his eyes for a second, seeing something briefly that couldn't be real. He rubbed them again, and again. "It's not a dream." Kellen Kinkade snarled, as he edged out of the darkness in the corner of the room "I'm not a ghost either." "You dead!" Matsumoto roared "They kill you!" "Not quite dead, although I look and feel like it." Matsumoto grimaced, aside from the missing couple of teeth and burns. Kinkade was also sporting a series or razorblade cuts and heavy bruises. Matsumoto tried to move out of his seat but Kinkade quickly rushed forward and pinned him down "You smell that fucker? That's the smell of your urine." "Let me go, I scream!" Matsumoto pleaded as Kinkade gripped him by the throat. "I didn't feel like washing it off after they let me go, told them you had a lot of money hidden away and that I'd bring it to them along with your head as long as they let me live. They said they'd give me a week, which is more than enough time than I need to take your money and get out of the country. But I guess the question running around your mind is probably still why I haven't taken a shower." Kinkade snarled furiously as Matsumoto struggled for air. "It's because the smell of your piss all over me... makes it so much easier to do THIS." Kellen placed his hand over Matsumoto's mouth and brought the knife back and forth, in and out of his body... ...until he struggled no more. July 16th 1995. "Can I get you anything sir, a drink perhaps? The airhostess was polite in her questioning, and tried not to stare too long at the man sitting before her. It wasn't easy to look past the cuts and burns all over his arms or indeed the couple of missing teeth in his mouth when he smiled. "I'll be just fine thankyou very much, although if you'd be a honey and let me use my cell phone for just a couple of seconds... I'd be really grateful." Kinkade placed his hand on her wrist and slowly caressed it with his thumb. "Alright... but just a couple of seconds or I'll get in trouble." The hostess said, turning and walking away with a smirk upon her face. After punching in a few numbers and waited for an answer, Kinkade finally spoke. "Hanson?" Kellen asked "This is Kellen Kinkade, before you hang up just hear me out." A few seconds of delay passed before Kade smiled to himself. "A long time ago my father took your eye, as direct result of his actions you took mine. We're nothing but victims at his expense and I think it's time that we did something about it... ...I think it's time we did some taking of our own." Kinkade hung up the cell phone and rested his head back, dreaming of things that no man should dream.
