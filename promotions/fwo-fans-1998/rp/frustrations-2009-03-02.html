Frustrations

2 March 2009

William Magnum Decker was pissed. Seattle sucked. And he was starting to think he was wasting his time trying to find Fairview Reed. If it weren’t for the ridiculous amount of money he’d been offered and the open-ended deadline for completion he would have cut his losses long ago… And thinking about THAT pissed him off even more. Right now he could be at home, comfortable in his familiar squalor, taking on cheaper payoffs, sure, but jobs he could get done far easier. A few shakedowns, a few infidelities, a few missing people. His professional career was rich on the misfortunes and misery of others. Right now he should be sitting in his own shitty apartment in front of his own shitty television with a bottle of his own shitty liquor, goddamn it! He raised one arm halfheartedly and waved the bartender over. Fairview Reed was proving to be a ghost. If he hadn’t happened to have been watching television on THAT particular night in THAT particular bar and seen that wrestling show he still wouldn’t believe that there WAS such a person as Fairview Reed…but if it was on TV it must true. And so he’d WASTED the last month chasing this bastard on nothing more than a hint of a clue. The fiasco in Afghanistan, the ensuing protests – none of that meant anything to him. He’d been skulking around the Key Arena for weeks and was frankly beginning to hate the sight of it. Security was marginal at best and he’d been everywhere and seen everything that he needed to EXCEPT this Fairview Reed guy… The bartender poured him a drink. Before he could escape Decker gestured for him to stay and then expertly downed his fresh libation. The bartender sighed and poured another shot; he paused for a moment with a vaguely sarcastic questioning look. Decker waved him away. What a fucking poor excuse for a liquor jockey, not knowing his clientel’s drinking habits… He glared at the TV, the same TV that had given him his only glimpse of his prey. That was going to have to change this week… …and NOTHING was going to stand in his way.
