I Think I'm Paranoid. Two.

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I've got a secret I cannot say A modern movement to give it away You got something that I understand Hold it in tightly, call on command Leap of faith--do you doubt? Cut you in, I just cut you out... Angelina Barker walked past the gym, and headed down to the parking garage. She picked up a Daily Aztec at the kiosk on the second floor before heading off to her Acura. Her second day back at school, and it was already losing the charm and good feelings she'd sort of built up for the place over her winter vacation... "Don't try any funny shit." Her back stiffened immediately. Very slowly, she angled her head and spit out her gum on the concrete. "Let me amend that," the voice spoke from behind her. "Don't move unless I tell you to move." She froze. "Open the car." She did it. He moved right past her, then grabbed her arm. "Get in." She sat down in the driver's seat, looking straight ahead. "Drive." "Where?" "I care. Just do it." She quickly shifted into drive. The car went squealing out of the lot, and quickly reached westbound interstate 8. It was only here she could sigh out loud. "Jesus Christ..." She was interrupted as the assailant began putting his lips to hers, slipping her tongue. That was it. She started smacking him around, weaving in and out of traffic as it whistled by the Q. "JESUS FUCKING CHRIST, RUBEN, YOU SCARED THE SHIT OUT OF ME!" Ruben Ross merely began laughing his ass off, snickering like the Mad Hatter as he scratched his goatee. "I could've sworn we were talking about this a couple of weeks ago." "Well, we were, but I didn't think you were just going to pop up and psuedo-kidnap me. You know that's been going on around campus, I was piss scared there for a second." She was shaking slightly. Ruben didn't notice, he was looking west towards the exits. "Aren't you supposed to be working?" Angelina asked him. "Monday." "When you leaving?" Ross looked at her incredulously and checked his watch--Thursday. "Monday morning, allowing me to recover from my hangover on the flight." He fiddled with her radio knob. She smacked his hand away. "HEY!" "Driver's rules." "Oh, holy geez, Ang, you're gonna put some Avril Lavigne or some shit." He yawned and threw a mint in his month. "Then I'm going to have to kill myself. Of course, you'd enjoy that, you'd get both the Super Bowl tickets." "You got tickets?" Angelina slowly got on the on-ramp for northbound I-5, wading through the excess downtown/airport traffic during the big week. "I thought we'd have to sneak in." "Nah, I finally caught up to Warren and snagged a couple. Field level, 15th row, at the 40. Not too shabby." Ruben smiled, thinking about how he was going to watch the Bucs eviserate Al-Queda in black and silver Sunday in his hometown. "You know what else he said?" Angelina was looking for the Mission Bay exit, and slightly worried if she was going to see her dorm again tonight. Her roomate seemed off, down--they hadn't gotten to talk. "Hm?" "He thinks I should jump too." "Warren Sapp thinks you should join the WWE?" "Yes. Everybody does! Keller, Meltzer, the people online...the fWo's killing off my good name in order to stupidly get Erika over by squashing low carders on PPVs. I worked every show for the people who didn't even want to push me to the top to begin with..." Angelina was half-listening. There was the exit, around the loop, out west towards the beach. "...yeah, so I'm going in Monday and I find out things haven't changed, I'm gonna quit. I'll have to wait 30 days before I can make the leap." "To WWE?" "Yeah, there's a clause in the contract. Hell, I can wait that out standing on my head, this is 23 days I haven't wrestled. And even I can't piss away millions in a month. Then, off to Smack!Down and the best show in the world. They're thinking of putting me against Rey or Edge to start off with." "I like Edge, he's cute. Got a nice set of teeth." In spite of himself, Ruben almost chuckled as they drove down Garnet. Sometimes he forgot the beautiful girl next to him was 18. Probably because he was deathly worried one of his best friends in the world would find out he was banging his barely-legal sister. "We're here." Whatever you do Don't tell anyone Whatever you do Don't tell anyone...
