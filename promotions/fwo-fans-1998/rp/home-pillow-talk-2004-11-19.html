"Mmmmm," sighed Angel as she squeezed Eli's ribs tightly, "I'm about ready to fall asleep now." "Probably a good idea that'cha don't," replied Eli, as he ran his hands through her hair, "since at some point tonight, we're going back to being parents." They lie in the apartment's king-sized bed, underneath a fleece blanket. Angel had her head rested on Eli's chest, her arms wrapped around his ribs, while Eli had one arm around her waist and the other hand massaged her hair and scalp. Ten months, packed into two frenzied hours, had found them in this place, completely content to lie in each other's arms. "So, you gonna tell me?" asked Eli, out of nowhere. "Tell you what?" "About this," he continued, as his hand found the circular scar on her side, "and all the other war wounds I found tonight?" He could feel her tense up, so he continued the scalp massage and squeezed her. "I've been there, Angel... the road can be tough. But as someone who has been there, I'd like to help if I can." Angel moved slightly under his grasp, and she turned toward his ribs and kissed him at the top of his six-pack. "But you didn't have a baby girl's welfare in the back of your mind when you were there." Eli's face flushed red, guiltily. "Touche." "It's okay," replied Angel, as she got up a bit and crawled her way to her husband's face to kiss him. Other than the scar on her side, her bare back was creamy, pale, and flawless to the eye and touch, "It was hard... but we survived." "Tell me about it?" The two bodies melded into one again as Angel lay down again, her head level with his, and her right leg draped over his body underneath the blankets. "Our level of respect was less than zero. It was like people bought tickets to our shows just to fuck with us. Most of the kids were cool, but every now n'then... trouble." "Yeah?" "This guy stabbed me with a little bitty penknife because...
