Scar

27 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Two Months Earlier… Steven warned me that this was going to be a lot of work. I silently curse to myself as I realize that the overfed brother of mine was right. I told him that I was going to start anew, debut with a new look and everything. Fix my image. For six months, the fWo has been in business in my home and shit’s tough. The students aren’t rolling in like they once were and PRIME and I had parted ways last year, sticking strictly to a “per appearance” basis. The cash flow was running dry and if I had any hope of going back into their good graces, I’d need to make myself appear willing to play along. Once I shared my plan with Steve, the response was laughter. That I’m an old dog set in my old ways. Fucker. So now here I stood, face to face looking at the razor in my hand. This… this was weird. I’d trimmed my beard before, no big deal. But when the first patch of hair left my skin and I saw a portion of my bare face for the first time, I almost jumped. I didn’t want to be the love child of Bruiser Brody and Mike Knox, but sheesh, I have to stoop to this?! Anyhow, my meeting with William Beaven was tomorrow and if I had any hope of finding a spot amidst an evolving roster of growing and talented athletes, then I’d need to go above and beyond to show these assholes that I was somebody different from who they hired years ago. Even though I was completely justified in picking apart those sorry fucks one by one so long ago. Each roster member brings something good to the table or they wouldn’t be employed by the fWo. It would be an uphill battle to get just the tiniest step forward so that I stay ahead of the other participants. OW. I nicked myself with the razor and within moments, the white shaving cream became a sickening pink. Trying to subside the searing pain, I think to myself everything that’d happened in the past three years since fWo had closed down. Growth, depression, love, a child, championships, tables, stairs, ladders, broken arms, failed attempts to lead, fighting for power, maintaining power, strangleholds, betrayal, lovelifehalloffamedivorcehateandnothing… It all runs at once and before I know it, the face staring back at me is covered in soft welts and scars, a throbbing cut on the side of my face. My eyes cast downward and the water running in the sink, turned the clear water in a gruesome red. I just try to ignore the pain as I just about finish with the last of the stray hairs. My mind drifts toward all the bodies I’ve hurt and have been stained by my very presence on this Earth. Michelle Masters, Keith Scott Zimmerman, Alias, Karina Wolfenden, Max Danger, Dusk, High Flyer, Vince Jacobs, Lindsay Troy, Tchu, Killean Sirrajin, Wade Elliott, Danny Ferguson, Shawn Stewart, Jeremy and Shirley Savage, Coral Avalon, Annabelle Summers... and many more after that. Some names you’ve never heard of, some you have. It’s been one of the curses I’ve lived with going down this road… I don’t forget. I just let the cut drip lifelessly, refusing to blot it. I know full well that signing on the dotted line with the fWo and making my presence known again is essentially signing a death sentence. Pfft. What’s one more scar, anyhow?
