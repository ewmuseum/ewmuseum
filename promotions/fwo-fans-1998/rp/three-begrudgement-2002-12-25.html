Three: Begrudgement

25 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Envy is the desire for others' traits, status, abilities, or situation." Ric played with the rapidly cooling General Tso's chicken on his plate. Prodding it with a chopstick, he looked around the restaurant, peering at the bleak surroundings. Dallas on Christmas Day wasn't exactly the cheeriest of locales to begin with, and the fact that the only restaurant open was partially filled with other lone diners only heightened the almost depressing mood. Finally shoving the food aside, Ric decided to go back up to the buffet line. The beauty of a Chinese buffet was always that if you didn't like something, you could go try something else. However, something else Ric didn't like came through the doors a moment later, following in the wake of a small family. He looked over at the tall young woman, whose hair was a golden contrast to the dark auburn of Ric's, and he knew that he had been found by the last person he wanted to see. "Lira." His voice was almost flat enough to conceal the sneer. He didn't bother trying to sit back down, or to try and pretend he didn't see her - he knew the only reason she would be here was because she had purposefully tracked him down. She strode over to Ric, her pert lips twisting into a half-smile. "You know it doesn't have to be this way," she curtly declared to him. "Even with all the bad blood between you two, I talked to Eddy, and he wants you over. The flight back to Cincinnati is only two hours - we could be there before 3PM our time." Ric brushed past her, and picked up a plate, hoping she would take the hint. She refused to be dismissed so easily, and picked up a plate to follow. "Listen - if I had wanted to spend Christmas with you, or Eddy, or anyone, I would have called. By all rights, I should be in Honolulu by now, but all Hawaii flights were cancelled, leaving me stuck here until Friday. Otherwise, we wouldn't be having this conversation right now, and I'd be working on my tan." Ric absently shoveled some more food on his plate, hoping that the discussion was truly over. "You hate sesame chicken," she said, and when Ric looked down at his plate, he knew she was right. Damn her for knowing him so well, and damn him for making such a stupid mistake. Ric gave in to the pressure, and since he couldn't dissuade her, he thought he'd just ride it out. "All right, you've made your point. I'll hear you out, but I'll be damned if I'm flying back to Cincinnati today." She smiled, and for just a moment, Ric forgot how much he resented her for leaving him. Sitting back down at the table, Ric waited patiently as Lira ordered a drink. "So, what made you decide to fly all the way in to Houston. I thought you and - what's is name?"
