January 15th, 2004 - Room 214

12 February 2004

January 15, 2004. New York Presbyterian Hospital Room 214. Jasmin Desjardin sat next to Pierre's bed, watching his chest rise and fall. Ric and Lyra, had left only half an hour earlier, and most of that time since she'd been sitting there in silence, watching her lover sleep. In her lap was a stack of papers, which she thumbed through, and at places marked with little yellow tabs, signed her name. When she finished with that, she put the papers away in her bag, and started speaking in French. [[I'd wanted to have those down two weeks ago, but you had to get yourself shot.]] Jasmin chuckled lightly, and stroked his face. Still sleeping, he instinctively nuzzled against her hand, bringing a warm glow to Jasmin's face. [[You were right. I had a decision to make. I choose you. Now heal up, because I'm going to need you for quite some time.]] Jasmin leaned over and kissed Pierre's forehead, then gathered her bag, and walked out the door, heading toward's the floor's family room. As soon as she turned down the hallway, Pierre had another visitor. Thomas Desjardin entered the room, holding a flower filled vase, and walked over to Pierre's bedside. He sat the vase down at a nearby table, and spoke quietly. [[She doesn't know what she wants. She doesn't realize that the choice isn't hers to make.]] Thomas grabbed a pillow from the side of the bed. [[It's mine.]] Thomas placed the pillow over Pierre's face. [[Mine.]]
