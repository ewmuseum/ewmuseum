Wish You Were Here (Part 5 of 5)

7 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I can change the world With my own two hands Make a better place With my own two hands Make a kinder place With my own two hands With my own With my own two hands I can make peace on earth With my own two hands I can clean up the earth With my own two hands I can reach out to you With my own two hands With my own With my own two hands I'm gonna make it a brighter place *** Date: Monday 4th, August Year: Present KA-THUMP! What you just heard was the sound of Levite Prod's head hitting the mat floor very hard. His head rocked so hard against the ground Prod was temporarily unable to see anything but the color blue. Slowly trying to raise his head to see his surroundings Prod let his head fall back down again to touch the mat floor once more. Looking up into what was a blurry haze of the referee, Prod desperately opened and closed his eyes trying to regain any form of solid sight. The referee leaned down while counting out, as he got closer he spoke into Prod's ear, "Get up Levi, your still not done... the execs won't be happy with you if you stop now." Prod grabbed a hold of the referee's shoulder, "I don't think I can see, I don't think I can get up." The referee said nothing, instead turned back and looked up at the opponent who was facing Prod in the dark match on Monday Night Ruahh! The opponent caught eyes with the ref and nodded, he stood over Prod and grabbed Prod's hair ripping him off the mat floor. Prod yelled out in pain as the opponent then proceeded to lift Prod into the air and then Gorilla Press Slam him into the mat once again, Prod felt the man on top of him and heard the ref quickly count to three. Prod could hear cheering in the background, even though it came from hardly any audience members. Most of them were taking a piss, or getting some form of food or beverage from the concession. Prod had amounted to a nobody in his time so far at the fWo. This was fine, he was at least getting paid and so far had been able to fight any other past demons. Five minutes had passed and Prod's sight had adjusted mostly back to normal, with the help of a crewman Prod made it back stage and grabbed a free bottle of water to suck back quickly, as Prod finished his gulp and leaned back against the arena wall he sighed. Looking across the room he noticed as reporters rushed to try and get a word in with the other wrestlers that walked by. The important ones, the stars, the heroes, the hated. Even to be hated as a celebrity would be nice, but not as a rapist. "Be careful what you wish for Levite." Prod turned to look into the eyes of Noah Reily, a man who was known as -I- to few. Prod had met Reily years ago, Reily had come to him stating that he would be important in helping save the world. As far as Prod was concerned, Reily was insane. "Look Reily, I have no time for your blabbering, I have no idea how you made it into the fWo, or why you seem to be following me. But, and I mean to phrase this in the nicest manner... fuck off," Prod placed the water bottle down and turned to walk away when he was stopped by Noah Reily's following sentence. "You are going to die." Reily spoke sternly. Prod turned to face Noah, "Now your threatening me?" Reily shook his head, "No Levite, I am merely stating a fact. If you continue to ignore me, you will die. The time is coming soon, he is coming back and if you do not help me. You will die, at his hands." Prod pointed his finger at Reily, "Listen to me you Jesus Freak, you stay away from me. If I catch you coming near me again, I will kill you." Prod began to walk away once again. Reily took a step forward, "Listen to me Prod, I need your help, I need you to-" Prod cut him off once again, "Save it! I'm out," Prod continued to walk away, "Be smart Reily, and stay away." Noah stood watching Prod walk away, shaking his head Noah muttered under his breath, "Time is running out, I'll have to shake things up."
