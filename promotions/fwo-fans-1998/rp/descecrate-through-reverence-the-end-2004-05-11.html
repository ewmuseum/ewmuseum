Descecrate Through Reverence: The End

11 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
She slipped into the room wearing a Slayer hoodie, deliberately torn jeans and bruises no amount of makeup could hide. In hindsight, it could've been sold as a great soap opera plotline: Happy loser couple living their lives as usual until a crazed former-friend of the boyfriend comes back for revenge - ties him up, kills his favorite friend and rapes his girl not two feet away from the chair he's bonded to. Perfectly cheap melodrama that shouldn't have been real. Unfortunately, it was and for those who play the cards as they're dealt, it still wasn't any easier to deal with. Two hours later, it was only harder. "It's healthier for us both. You know me well enough to know I'm not just looking out for myself. I won't let one stone kill two birds." Finally, "I just need to go, Reno. I can't give a breakup speech. I can't breakup...I just have to go. We can't do this anymore. Neither of us will survive another second in a hospital." She didn't stay another second. That early April afternoon, Amy Roche didn't finish reading the list of Survivors. She said a silent prayer for Amy Campbell. [FAST FORWARD to "Walk This Way"]
