The Xenosaga: V

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Street Fighter: The Movie. I know what you, the reader, would be thinking right now, and I can some it up for you in just eleven words, "Don't use that kind of language on the fWo website, motherfucker". However, this was the kind of torture that the Tallywhackers had cooked up for the Codemaster, Allen Speck. As we return to the scene of the torture, the Tallywhackers had seemingly come back from doing something OTHER than watch their evil torture in action. "So, when the ol' deeeehvil of a loyon came back, Oy ambushed 'im from behoynd weeth nothin' but a rock and a handmade net made out of voynes and scotch tape! The ol' bugga was a royt bit diff'cult to captcha, but Oy was a wee bit too woyly for 'em. And then..." Dingleberry said, right in the middle of his story to Cagle, before he noticed an odd sight. The Codemaster had apparently gone for the popcorn while they were out, and while he had popcorn everywhere under the sun on his body, most of it sticking to the chocolate syrup that was still stuck on his body. But most noticably, he still held the popcorn bucket on his head, trying to block out the movie in front of him. To your average viewer who had not seen just WHAT the Tallywhackers had done to the Codemaster, you would think that the poor guy was out of his mind. Actually, that wasn't too far from the truth. "...Ah'd say he's done gawn awf and lost it, Dingerburry." Cagle noted. Dingleberry nodded in agreement. Finally, Dingleberry wandered over to the Codemaster and pulled the bucket(and therefore, his hands) off of his head. The Codemaster tried desperately to put the bucket back on his head to avoid the horrible, horrible power that was Street Fighter: The Movie. Finally, Cagle brightly turned it off, and the Codemaster stopped struggling. It took the Codemaster a full twenty seconds(between heavy, tortured breaths) before he spoke. "You people are sick, depraved, sadists who deserve to be locked away in the deepest padded room on the Earth and have things thrown at them for the rest of their natural lives." the Codemaster said. "Hey...Oy'm not sick!" Dingleberry protested. "Yeah, Dingerburry ain't got no fever." Cagle added. "I wasn't talkin' `bout that kinda sick, idiots." the Codemaster said. "Well, we got a special treat fuh you, Codemastuh! Cagle and Oy weent t' the reental place, and we peeked up this noice little flick that you'll love! Eet's called, 'Mortal Kombat: Annihilation'." Dingleberry said with a threatening glare. Despite being black, the Codemaster almost went white upon the very utterance of those three words. It took him a full thirty seconds before he spoke his next three exasperated words. "You... wouldn't... DARE!"
