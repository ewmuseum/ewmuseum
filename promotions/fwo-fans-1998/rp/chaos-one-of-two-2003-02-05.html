Chaos- One of Two

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I know what you're thinking. If Chaos is so fucking powerful, why does He need the help of a bunch of humans? Well, allow me to tell you the story. Of how the Earth was created, and how the being that created it lost His divine power. From His own perspective. It was another council meeting. As usual, they weren't actually going to address any of the actual problems. Just sweep things under the rug, as they always do. But where are my manners? Allow me to introduce myself to the reader. I have many names, but I usually prefer the name of "Chaos". However, I have also been called many other names. I'll give you some of them for your own information. The Babylonians referred to me as Lucifer, and worshipped me as the true creator. They were paritally right. The Christians refer to me as Satan, a being that represents everything evil in this world. They had things a bit reversed. The Greeks and Romans each called me the God of the Underworld, under the names Hades and Pluto respectivley. They were right as well, but wrong at the same time. Confused? You should be. Allow me to explain: Before the Earth was created, another world existed. This world would be constructed quite similar to Greek and Roman mythology- a great deal of them somehow "remembered" what life here was like. Not one-hundred percent accuratley, of course- they got the names wrong, for one, and there are plenty of little mistakes here and there. But the basic construct of the world was correct. I am the God of the Underworld, and there are many Gods. We are lead by Azirin, the Ruler of the Gods and one of the biggest morons to have ever existed. As you can probably guess, I'm not a huge fan of Azirin's. Anyway, it was my goal to get back at Azirin, whose father created this world we live in. I found a way to create my own world, one that would exceed anything this world could ever imagine. So, I created the Earth, and the most intelligent beings? Humans. Why are they the most intelligent? They're mini-Gods, basically. Created in the image of God-hood. So yes, Christianity as indeed correct there. Just as I predicted, MY planet, the Earth, exceeded anything this planet could imagine. Azirin, being egotistical, created the sentinet beings of his wrold vastly inferior to him. The humans were easily more intelligent and adaptable than Azirin's little beings, and the Earth soon surpassed his planet. Obviously, he didn't like this. With the help of the rest of the Gods, he claimed that I had violated the rules and guidelines set forth by the council of the Gods. I was placed in confinement and stripped of my powers, leaving the Earth unprotected. Every bad thing that happens isn't the result of the being Christians condemn as "Satan"- that would be me. The being they believe to be "God", Azirin, decides who dies and who lives. He viciously takes family members away from others. With no remorse, he brings down natural disasters on an unsuspecting populace. Filled with jealousy for my creation, he slowly destroys it with each moment that I am confined- and a great deal of humans worship him for it! The being that kills their brothers, mothers, fathers, and sisters without thinking twice is the one worshipped. So you see, when my Prophet proclaimed that your "God" never existed, he wasn't lying. You believe your "God" to be kind and loving- and yet, at the same time, you also believe he decides when it is someone's time to die. That is ridicolous. Your "God" does not exist- only the cruel, unforgiving diety known as Azarin. Well, I had enough of Azirin destroying my creation. It was time for me to take it back.....once and for all. To be continued....
