Faith & Destruction

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life was normal in my hotel room. My angle with Shadows had went off well, and the fWo was really starting to get into the Deacon character. I was wrestling twice a week at least, and from all reports, doing a solid bit of ring work. And then none of that mattered. I heard a knock at the door. I put my book down, My Utmost for His Highest, and opened the door to see Chris. His eyes were red and he didn't make eye contact, looking down. He glanced up, still not looking at me but at something to my right, and tried to speak. His voice wouldn't come to him. "What t'e matter?" I asked, my heart already racing for fear of why he was acting so unusual. He didn't look at me, and tried to speak again. Nothing came. "Is everyt'ing ok?" I asked again. I knew it wasn't, but what could cause him to look so pale. "The show," he slowly began. "Uh... a ... their was... Oh God, Deac... a bomb." I stood there, my mind unable to connect the seemingly separate dots of his phrases. And then the tears came to his eyes and they connected. The fears came true. "I...," I tried to say something, but my mind wouldn't stop spinning or my hand shaking. "I was going to go to the arena, see if maybe I could do something." I wanted to say that I would go with him, but I merely stood there, still trying to understand what was happening. Chris looked away from me and headed down the hallway. I stepped out of my room and closed the door. Chris turned around and waited for me to catch up. When I did, we both walked side by side up the hallway, the silence speaking volumes of the situation. I wish I could say that I prayed at this moment, but I didn't, or at least not in the traditional sense. I couldn't grasp words at this moment, so I only moved forward to see what I could do.
