"Where are you ever going to learn?" Part 3

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Previous Parts: 1, 2 Still Tuesday 29th April 2003 "You reckon any of those fat fucks hitting each other want a job?" Borst said, turning to the barmaid, busy cleaning the glasses with her spit. "Cos I tell you, I've not been so entertained for years. Just watching them kick the crap into each other, especially that huge bald guy there. Legend." "Eh?" she finally replied, having not listened to a word he said, being too engrossed in the fight ensuing on the far side of the bar. "I was just saying that... fucking hell." Borst's train of thought was interrupted by someone stumbling past and spilling beer all over his lap. And it ain't nice when you're wearing shorts. Sticky legs. Borst turned quickly and swung his fist violently, smacking the culprit in the face. "Fucking so and so..." he muttered, turning back to the bar. "I know the shaved head can look kind of manly..." the barmaid said, looking over the bar and the laid out culprit. "But she still looks like a woman pal. You do realise that don't ya?" "What difference does it make?" Borst replied, taking a mouthful of his beer. "She's not dead or anything." Another of Borst's many interesting theories. It's actually alright to hit women, even knock them out. Just so long as they're not dead. "Yeah, but you will be." The barmaid said, nodding in the direction of a huge guy standing behind him. "That was my woman. Did you know that?" the curly head monster asked, slapping Borst on the back to get his attention. "That was..." Borst suddenly turned around and smacked the guy square in the jaw, knocking him to the floor. "For fuck sake." He said, getting up and crouching over the guy. "If you're gonna do something, do something. Don't spend all fucking day yapping about it." One of the guy's friends didn't take kindly to seeing his pal and woman knocked out, and of course, we all know Borst is super tough and he kicked the crap into this guy. I'm hardly going to be putting Borst over if I say he got his head kicked in by everyone in the bar. A few more people followed in the being knocked out by Borst game, as the two separate brawls merged into one... Eventually, it kind of fizzled out. You know, super tough Borst was kicking everyone's arse. Something along those lines. So, everyone's bored, fed up and hurting. Borst is standing around about five laid out guys, and gal, and one of these beefy guys comes up to him shaking his head in disbelief. "Fuck, you're good." He told Borst. Borst nodded. "Well, it's hard to be modest at times like these so I won't even try...you were all shite. I'm hard. You're not." Borst stopped and thought for a moment. "But saying that, as I was thinking out loud before being so rudely interrupted by this impromptu brawl, you're a hell of a lot harder than those fucking wimpy fWQuo wrestlers, and you're about ten times more entertaining." "So guys, in return for forgiving me for beating you all up, I'll give you guys jobs. You get paid for fighting. You'll have a blast." Borst began to walk around the bar, nodding at the brutes still standing. "You, you, you, and you, not you you fat cunt, you though, and you... I like your style." Borst looked down at the floor. "And that guy, he took about ten bottles round his head to finally be knocked out. I want this guy." Borst turned to the barmaid. "Hey, can we get this guy some help? I wouldn't mind him being conscious so that I can tell I'll pay him ten bucks a week to get beat up." "The doctor will be round soon." The barmaid replied. "That was quick..." he said. "Get off..." Borst muttered, kicking a guy in the head that was trying to pull himself to his feet using Borst's leg. He looked up again and said to the barmaid. "You wasted no time calling him, woman." "No, I meant he's already here." The barmaid replied, getting a cloth out and wiping some blood from the bar. Borst looked over to the corner of the bar. "Oh... you mean that guy unconscious with the untouched beer and empty bottle of pills beside him. I see what you mean when you said he'll be round soon." "No, he's the guy you just kicked in the head..." she replied. "Right then." Borst said clapping his hands together. "While we wait for doc to come round, lets sort this all out. Seeing as you're all shit scared of me now..." Ahem. "I assume you're all in?" "Scared of you?" one of the guys grunted. "I ain't scared of no-one. But if you's paying us to fight like this, then I'm in." Several others echoed that statement, grunting or nodding, and secretly cowering at Borst's sheer presence. "Great. This plan is full proof. I can't think of a better way to use all that money I got from the fWQuo than to use it against them." Borst said, grinning. "Any questions people?" he asked. "Yeah." Grunted a beefy baby faced long haired sailor. "Why do you have a beard?" Borst looked the guy up and down and took a step back. "Because it's better than having a boyfriend. Any other questions people? No? Right then. Mark this date people... the BWF is born!" Borst fact of the day: Borst is actually a fictional character created by Steve King.
