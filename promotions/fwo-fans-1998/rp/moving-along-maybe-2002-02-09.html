Moving Along...Maybe

9 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As rain poured down outside of the Platinum Gold, Inc. headquarters, Monte Burns and Lance Fortune exited a private elevator and walked forward into the lobby. Brushing past the receptionist, Burns and Fortune reach the glass doors and stop suddenly. Burns looks from side to side at the street outside, then turns around and walks back to the receptionist desk with an annoyed look on his face. The receptionist looks up from her paperwork, sees the look on Monte's face, and forces a worried smile. "Y-yes, Mr. Burns?" she asks nervously. "Where is my limo?" Burns demands as Fortune looks on. "I was told he would be here. What's going on?" "Um....well..." stutters the receptionist, "The d-driver called a few minutes ago. I guess he's running late." For a few moments, Burns stood there silently, shaking his head at the complete ineptness of those around him. Fortune, knowing how upset his friend could get, chuckled a bit to himself as he watched for the limo to arrive outside. Finally realizing that the receptionist had nothing to do with the situation, Burns walked back over to where Fortune was standing near the door and sighed. "I am so sick of dealing with these imbeciles, Lance," said Burns while scratching his head. "I have to deal with enough morons in the fWo, let alone having my employees suffer from this disease also." "Maybe you need to start looking elsewhere for help," said Lance with a shrug. "Obviously, this crap in the fWo is pissing you off, and these employees will just make it worse." "I guess I could fire a couple of people next week," remarked Monte. "It's not like there's any holidays coming up or anything. When I fired those people before Thanksgiving, I never thought I'd hear the end of it." As the two friends shared a laugh over Monte's last comment, Fortune noticed a long black limo rounding a corner across the street. The limo pulled towards Platinum Gold, Inc. and the two men began to walk out the doors. Instead of stopping in front of the building, however, the limo continued on down the street. Burns and Fortune looked at each other flabbergasted. After letting out a deep breath to at least relieve some of his anger, Burns walked back over to the receptionist desk. This time the young woman wasn't able to muster a smile. In fact, as she started to speak, Monte quickly cut her off. "I want you to get on that god damn phone right now and call that limo service," Burns demanded. "That asshole was supposed to be hear five minutes ago, and I will not be dicked around by this prick. I want that limo here now!" Burns punctuated his last remark by slamming his fist down on the desk. A security guard down the hall heard the noise and stood up, but he quickly sat back down when he released it was the CEO. Re-adjusting his tie, Burns walks back over to Fortune, who looks a bit annoyed as well. "Our reservation is for 5 o'clock. If that limo doesn't get here soon, we may have to take a taxi," said Fortune reluctantly. "Yeah right. I'll be damned if I'm going to ride in a taxi here," claimed Monte with a chuckle. "You won't be seeing 'The Golden Child' riding in a taxi in New York." "True. I must have been out of my mind to suggest that," said Fortune with a roll of the eyes. "So, how are things going in the fWo? I haven't heard you talk much about it lately." "Things are going alright I guess. I won the Cruiserweight Title a couple weeks back from Fate," Burns said while examining the road. "I'm not really sure where I'm headed right now. Since I have already proven myself to be better than all of the cruiserweight competition, the office is probably going to send two or three of them at me at once. Can't say that I blame them. Having a champion who can beat all the challengers doesn't sell many tickets." Fortune nodded his head in agreement, as Burns prepared to walk back over to the receptionist's desk. Just as he started to walk away, Fortune reached out and grabbed his arm, pointing to the limo that had just pulled up outside. After shooting a glance at the receptionist, the two men walk through the doors. The limo driver hurries out of the car and rushes over with an umbrella. No words are exchanged at first, as Fortune and Burns walk out to the limo under the umbrella. As the driver opens the passenger door and Fortune steps inside, Monte finally glares at him and speaks. "Could you use your phone and connect me to your boss?" he asked calmly. The driver looked confused for a moment, then replied. "That's no problem, sir," he said with a confused smile. "May I ask what it's about?" As the driver finished his question, Burns stepped inside the limo and sat down next to Fortune on the plush seating. With the door still open, Monte looked up at the driver and gave him a sly grin. "It's about your termination," Burns said matter-of-factly. The driver looked down at Burns with a mixture of confusion and sadness. Monte simply slammed the door shut and waited for the limo to start moving.
