When champions collide... or sit on a hospital bed together...

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brandon looked through the vertical blinds. The city was teaming with life and the lights and faint noises through the thick glass made him feel all the more like a caged animal. Hurt as he was adrenaline pumped through his body, working it's magic more than the medication that slowly dripped in to his veins. He was five hours removed from one of the toughest challenges he had ever faced and he certainly felt every second of the after affects. His ribs, always a problem since his earliest days in professional wrestling, were bruised severely. The cut above his eye required over a dozen stitches and bandages dotted his body covering the smaller abrasions. His left arm and upper back were the worst though; first-degree burns covered his forearm and upper shoulders. The doctors told him there might be slight scarring on his arm but his back would be fine, if he did as he was told and stayed out of the ring until it healed. Did they really know whom they were talking to? Either way there was no way he was getting any sleep tonight... this morning... whatever. After the grueling battle with Borst ended Brandon found himself backstage amidst a flock of well-wishers and friends. Twenty minutes later during the Creed/ Kelly bout he collapsed, mostly from exhaustion but the injuries didn't help matters. Upon waking up in the Emergency room Brandon discovered that Amy Campbell had been brought in as well. She too managed to work through her injuries for an hour or so before needing to be wheeled away. How good of a boyfriend was he that didn't even realize she was gone? Nor was she present for the backstage celebration upon his return to the locker room as the new United States Champion, yet he didn't notice. Instead he celebrated and began to watch that bastard Creed cheat his way to another victory. He mentally kicked himself as he paced through the room. A phone call to Sean and Jarod, much to late than it should have been allayed their worries, the knew their father would be alright, he just had to be, but it was great to hear his voice telling them so. Ana was terrific with the call, even though she would have to be up in four hours to head to work. She knew the bond those three shared and couldn't stand in the way if she wanted to. The kids were super happy that Brand Frontier was now the U.S. Champion. They rambled on and on about how their mom didn't let them watch the end of the match, which Brandon was happy to hear.
