The Art Of Self Destruction, Phase IV (Ringfinger)

11 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The arenas had always seemed so desolate after the events. Emptiness was abound in the air and darkness was abound as only a few janitors remained, and each of them shut off the lights as they left for the night, their jobs complete. He was still in his locker room, facing the far wall, head dropped down and sitting in the same steel folding chair that he had been all night, save for his match and backstage altercation with Zero. A bitter feeling of shame rose up over him as he just sat motionless. Zero needed him for that one night. For ten minutes of his night, and he couldn't help. He wanted to fight back, to help Zero win the match, but his will was shattered and the shards weren't dull enough to put up any resistance. Systematically, his entire body was giving in and shutting down to any commands he gave it. From his heart, to his own brain, nerves, muscles, neurons, atoms, nothing obeyed him any longer. No matter how hard he fought, it was an uphill struggle, one he was surely going to lose. Help me. Help me. Help me. What was left of his own material and self-processed thoughts were screaming for help. Those cries weren't going to be answered, they would soon realize the cold truth. Just as soon as the new, antagonistic truth enveloped the old sense of good. Differentiating an angel on one shoulder and the daemon on the other would be nearly impossible. An impossibility that was going to be scoured up and down until the real truth, which was coveted and soon to be sought after, was once again found. * * * What were you doing out there tonight? Why the hell didn't you fight back? Zero was your partner, someone you could trust, someone you had to trust and you let him down! You told me not to fight back. Lies! You were supposed to put one hundred percent of yourself into that! Your blood was supposed to flow as you fought. A true adrenal rush. The night was supposed to be yours. Instead you stood there blankly and you caused the loss. Because I was doing what I was told. Don't you fucking lie to me! You know what I wanted, when I wanted it and how I wanted it. You didn't listen to a word I said. It went straight through your skin, it never sunk into that dense skull of yours. All of this is your fault. I... I'm sorry. * * * I... I hope he can't hear me, or read me right now. It seems like whatever I do, however hard I try... it's not sufficient. If I were twice the man I am now... It... it would still only be half of what it wanted from me. I wish I could fight this. I can't help but feel that I'm just playing a mindless game of follow the leader. And that I'm a pawn on its chess game of life. I just want an exit... A way out. I feel so pathetic, useless, worthless, I can't completely describe this feeling I have. I feel like... like I have no feelings any more. I wish I could explain it. Empty... empty like the bottles I used to drink from. Empty like my heart has always been. A bottomless abyss, a giant void without feeling. * * * Next week, things are going to be different for us. Much different. You've got to keep up with what goes on. Understand me? Yes. Look down at your left arm. The inside of your forearm, to be exact. What do you see. A tattoo. What's the tattoo look like? It's a bar code... What are the numbers imprinted underneath the bar code? Zero-zero-six-eight-zero-one-five-zero-one... Do you know what that means? No... It means that I control you.
