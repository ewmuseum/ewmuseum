The Restless

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Exel looked down at the ground beneath him. Green and full of life. He looked up at his beautiful wife, the one he had tried so desperatley hard to forget. To pretend that she had never existed. "Forgive me." he said silently. She did not look back. She had no ability to do so, and even if she did, she could no longer bare to look into his eyes. His eyes that were once vibrant and full of life, just like the ground they now stood on, but were now cold and.... Dead. Just as she was about to be. "First, your actions are reprehensible." .One was jarred out of his remincising by the words of .Two. "What was that?" he asked her. She just sighed. "It is almost time for the release, almost time for the fight, and yet here you are thinking about your past life." .Two said with a slight sigh. "Why is it that everytime I think I've made a step forward with you, it always ends up being a step back?" Maybe because of statements like that. .One thought silently. "I apologize, .Two." .One said, bowing his head slightly. "It will not happen again." "It had better not." .Two said. "I am going to prepare for the release with Rei. Do not disappoint me tommorow night, Destroyer, or I will send you back." .One only nodded as .Two walked away, leaving him sitting there, on the stone in the forest, to contemplate her words. Send me back? How could I be sent back at this point? I've gone too far, done too much, to be sent back to what I use to be. The Purifier. How fitting. But if he was looking for sympathy, he found none in the cold, dark, and dead forest. He looked down at the ground below him. Its never green here. he thought. It's never green anywhere anymore. Color has lost its meaning. Why is that? Why can't I seen the greens, the reds, the blues? Why can I only see the blacks and the greys? There was no answer, only the low hum of the wind blowing past him. He stood up suddenly, looking around him, and he realized that everything he was, everything he had done, meant nothing now. He would be judged. And nothing he did previously would matter. So restless, am I. I wish for an end to it all. Maybe I will get lucky, and find one.
