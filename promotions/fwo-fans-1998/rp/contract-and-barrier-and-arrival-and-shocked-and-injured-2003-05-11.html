Contract and Barrier and Arrival and Shocked and Injured

11 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Knock. Knock. "Come on in." It was the voice. THE voice of the fWo's talent arrangement, Silver Fox. Flyer followed suit, twisting the knob and entering the room. Kate headed in herself, the bag in her hands finally weighing her down after all this time. She trudged along like a little trooper, as Flyer walked into the spacious hotel room. Seemed like the fWo liked to pamper those in power. Samuel Smooth walked out of the kitchen area, a towel over his head. He was putting on his shirt as he entered, which gained weird glances from the approaching couple. Silver Fox came out of the room as well now, placing on his pants as he walked. Flyer and Kate were definitely perturbed. Samuel Smooth was the first to extend his hand to Flyer, who was hesitant to take it. "What the hell were you guys doing back there?" Flyer asked, pointing over to the now vacant room. Smooth kept his hand there, and Flyer reluctantly shook it. "We were just playing a rousing game of strip go fish." Flyer coughed. Kate blinked. Neither moved much after that. "Sorry for the state of affairs." Silver Fox said, also extending his hand. Flyer shook it reluctantly. "We were just playing... oh, just don't bother. I couldn't give you an explaination even if I wanted to. Anyway, I'm Silver Fox, I believe we all met in Africa." "That doesn't really explain the Strip Go Fish." Flyer chuckled to himself. "I mean, me and the misses played strip poker once, but unless you've got fifteen women wrestling fans in there, I'll be a bit distant during the next five minutes of our conversation." Silver Fox let out a sly smile, before offering the couple the love seat. They took it rather quickly, as Smooth picked up the bag Kate had been carrying and placed it next to them. They each took a seat on the opposite side, as if they were in the midst of an interrogation. "So, what's with the bags, if you don't mind me asking," Smooth said, dropping it quietly on the hotel floor. "Usually it's the gentlemanly thing for a man to do the heavy lifting." "Well, besides the fact that Kate can handle herself..." Flyer trailed off once more, and lifted his shirt, exposing the wrapping around his midsection. Fox and Smooth took this in for a moment before Flyer continued. "It's only the one, and it's a precaution." Flyer lowered his head and shook it from side to side. "There was this ladder stunt at the recent aWc pay per view. It wasn't either man's fault." Flyer sighed. "These things just happen. I just wish I could move better than I am right now" Smooth picked his leg up and crossed it. Fox glanced over to his partner, and nodded slightly. "It's a shame, because if things worked out tonight, we wanted to throw you right into a program with Great Dragon, stemming from the stellar match you two worked in Africa." Fox trailed off. "We even wanted there to be a Cyberslam match between you two." "You know, kickstart the evening with a bang." Smooth rubbed his chin, and there was a moment of awkward silence. "I'll do it!" Fox sighed. "I knew you were a hard worker, battling through injuries, but we can't take the risk right now with you. One wrong bump and I'm sure that rib could puncture a lung." Fox trailed off. "I know how hard it is to want to work with an injury but be unable to." Flyer's head sunk. His dream, his entire hope had been squandered by a simple rib injury. He rested his head in his hands, as Kate wrapped her arms around him. "Oh, don't worry Jack," Smooth uncrossed his legs, and leaned forward. "We still have plans for you. At least, if you're willing to sign on the dotted line." Flyer looked up, and there was a twinkle in his eye. He was six years old again, and it was Christmas morning. "You can start when you're healed up..." Fox trailed off, looking over toward his partner in talent relations. They knew something that they weren't telling the 27 year old superstar, but what it was, Flyer couldn't pin point it. He wouldn't know it till weeks later when the fWo returned to American for Cyberslam VI. He wouldn't know that the fWo was in a fight for it's survival and that it might not have lasted until his debut. Even if he did, he wouldn't have cared. This was the happiest day of his life. The greatest job in the world, and the greatest woman by his side. Could life be any better?
