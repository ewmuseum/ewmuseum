Love Letters

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC Note: No this is not from the same mystery man as the previous one. This is totally a different one, this one is a bigger surprise. The scene opened up to a mystery dark room. The room was black and all that could be seen was a stool and a little hand held video camera sitting on the bench. Enter masked man #1 Masked Man #1: AVO! AVO CHAVEZ! Get in here! Enter Avo Chavez (Who's supposed to be masked man #2) Avo: Gah! Esse you weren't supposed to say my name! Masked Man #1: Why not Avo? Avo: Because now its not a surprise to the general public who I am in this interview. Masked Man #1: Oh don't worry I haven't hit the record button yet. Avo Chavez and the masked man looked at the camera on the stool and noticed a flashing red light. Avo: What's the red light mean? Masked Man #1: Erm I don't know Both men shrugged and they continued on with life. Masked Man #1: Ok Avo sit down in the chair where the camera is and I will record your interview where you tell everyone why they suck and you're the best. Got it? Avo: So you want me to sit down on the camera? Masked Man #1: No- no sit down when I take the camera off the chair. Avo nodded his head as he agreed to what the masked man was saying. The masked man picked up the camera and Avo sat down in the chair. Avo: Hello ladies my name is Nathaniel Chavez, I am 5'6 pushing 5'7 in a half and I am a slim, built 156 pounds of pure steel. I have been single now for 22 years now and I am still a virgin. I have my way with the ladies and I normally have to beat them off with an ugly stick because they are so beautiful I feel bad for turning them down. I am a party animal who loves to get laid but I wouldn't know what it feels like to get laid because well I haven't gotten laid yet. To think about it actually a girl has never even acknowledged me before. Oh well. Masked Man #1: Um if I could interrupt Avo for a second. This isn't a love connection thingy this is wrestling! You know your job? Avo: No, I don't wrestle I FIGHT! Masked Man #1: You don't fight anymore in the Asylum Joe Campbell fired us. Avo: Ah yes Jose Campbell, well I bet you someone will like this tape. So send it in with the others. The Masked Man threw the tape in a stack of other tapes labeled with various sex names.
