Unleashed

7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Flair." The word was spoken slowly, forcefully, with the promise of something much more disgusting and vile to come, like a bile-filled belch that directly precedes a violent spew of vomit. He'd just been sitting there, minding his own business and concentrating on the cheap bottle of rotgut he clutched desperately when his concentration and the silence of the alley were both broken by this one word. The timbre of the voice had automatically sent a wave of fear down his back, radiating outward until a tremor had traveled through his entire body and made his hand twitch, nearly dropping his precious booze. He swore he was alone in this alley; one of the reasons he chose this particular site had been the location and the lack of other street people. But there was absolutely no mistaking that voice, the deep basso rumble that reverberated through the alleyway and resonated in the depths of his ears and the base of his skull. The voice was bubbling over with malice and frustration, bordering on rage. His head darted around wildly, trying to discern the origin and location of the voice, but the alley was small enough that after a quick search he was certain that he WAS alone.
