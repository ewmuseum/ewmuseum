The Phan Hiroshi Ultimate Karate dojo was closed for the holiday, only allowing high-ranking students inside to train. Of those students, a select few were given the key to allow them 24-7 access. Master Phan and Master Hiroshi didn't have much faith in the students they had, given the handicap of them not being Japanese. Yet despite being inferior (as the instructors saw it), there were a couple of students who had some elements of potential. At 1 a.m., one of those students was having the elements kicked out of him. A short Asian man stood on the edge of the sparring mat, his black gi wrinkled showing signs of a struggle. "Get up!" demanded Master Hiroshi, his scarred forehead beading with sweat. His student had pushed him hard, and that was something he didn't abide. There was a line between teacher and student, one that he was adamant about protecting. David Cross stared up at the ceiling. "Yes Master Hiroshi. Just as soon as the room stops spinning." "Yosh!" Hiroshi stomped down on Cross' stomach. David gagged and rolled onto his side, before receiving another kick. "Get up now!" Cross tried to sit up. "But the room! It just wouldn't be safe for me to try stand in a moving room like this. Maybe if I vomit it might help." Hiroshi stepped back and gave David a look of disgust. "What is your problem! Why you come here to waste my time like this! If you want train, we train! If you want to come here just to get beat up, go elsewhere!" David knew he was right. He'd been feeling sorry for himself, big time. He'd never let self-pity get to him before. Even when his face was burned and disfigured, he tried to stay strong. Now he was letting himself go. He looked up at Master Hiroshi's displeased face. "And I thought Dusty Rhodes had a hideous forehead," Cross commented as he stared at the mass of scar tissue above Master Hiroshi's eyebrows. "That just looks nasty." Master Hiroshi slapped Cross over the head. "What does my forehead have to do with a dirt road? You need to learn manners again! Woman bad influence on you!" "How did you know there was a woman?" Hiroshi pointed at his nose. "I can smell her stink on you. Women dirty your body!" "Ah, sweet sweet dirty." David raised an eyebrow. "Wait a sec. You mean you've never, you know... played Hide the Hiroshi Hoagie?" Hiroshi looked indignant. "I am Master of P.H.U.K.! I have no need for such things!" "Yeah," Cross nodded his head knowingly. "I polish the soldier now and then too. But this girl was special." "If she's so special, where is she?" David stood up. "I don't know. She slept with another man. She thought she was helping me, but I guess she only helped herself." "You need to focus!" Master Hiroshi pointed to a tattered leather mat in the corner of the dojo. "On the square, now!" David untied the belt on his gi and took off the jacket, gently placing it on a nearby bench. Master Hiroshi made sense, somewhat. Ever since he'd seen that tape, David Cross had lost his focus. He'd lost sight of who he was and what he wanted. He was spiraling out of control, and control was needed. Master Hiroshi picked up a bamboo staff. "Assume position!" he demanded.
