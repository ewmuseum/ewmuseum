Crutch

14 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Voice-over: The following advertisement is paid for by 3vil. Cue evil laugher. The screen fills with black and red boots. Yellow. Black kneecaps. More yellow. Crutches. More yellow. And while Ric Chronos stands there, he...there's something off... Chronos: Hi, everyone. I thought that since Twilight was coming up, I should show everyone how we got here, and how I became the best World Champion this side of David Arquette. I think I'm very interesting due to the fact that nothing has ever been my fault, ever. Even back in school, I was an ace at history. But the teachers only cared about the answers in their books and not the answers I gave. I never forgot that. My daddy was a wrestler and since I can't think of anything all by my lonesome, I decided to do it, too. The problem was, I knew pretty early on I was only decent at it and nowhere as good as my father. It made me hit the bottle pretty hard. But what I didn't have in talent or charisma I made up for with trying really hard, and then the fWo hired me to opening up shows and such. Now I'm champion and I'm going to drive this company into the ground. But, as usual, I always have friends. I realized that while I wasn't any good, if I had something to lean on or to help me out, it'd make me look better. People would start to think I was good because I hung out with good people! I heard somebody call it the Waltman Theory once, whoever he is. So I always try to have somebody or something with me: Fallen, the Black Panther, Primetime, Frenchie, Jim Beam, Jack Daniels...just whatever I need to get through. But at Twilight, everything's going to be ruined. That meanie Ruben Ross won't stop coming at me. And not in the fun Primetime sort of way. You see, he's mad because he was the champion of the computers for a really long time. But then he went for a move for the first time and missed, so I hit him with one of my three moves and won it from him. He's been a big meanie ever since. He beats me in the Wrestler of the Year tournament, and I think he cheated, because I hit all three of my moves and he still won clean in the center of the ring. Then I teamed him with BQ because Pierre said BQ wasn't any good, but he hit me with the same move and won again. And then last week I pinned him, and all it took was two chair shots and my move on a chair, and then I showed him the title because the belt wasn't on the line but he just kept spitting at me and Brian and Pierre and saying bad words and saying he was going to hurt us something awful. Pierre said I shouldn't worry about it though. dreamy sigh Pierre...... Uh, anyway, that brings us to Twilight. He says he's going to win in his own hometown. He's quicker and younger and usually sober, and a whole bunch of times I've seen him win and stuff without chairs or people helping him or ANYTHING! I don't know how he does it. I think he's a cyborg. Or a wookie. And when I lose the title and Brian & Pierre lose theirs, we won't have any more power. And I don't care. They're meanies too. They want my belt. Everyone does. They're all against me. Stupid Jim and Jack and Brian...well, not Brian. He's so nice. He showed me that his name was on top of mine on the new Superstars section and we laughed and laughed... But since I can't compete with him, I'm going to find him this Sunday. I'm going to walk up to him all by myself and everything and in a clear, calm voice say "Please don't hurt me." Chronos smiles. Then a choked-up sob follows. He begins to tear at his face (!) and it begins to come off...yeah, that seems about right, considering... "Chronos" back in "normal voice": And NOBODY steals my thunder, bitch. You better make Sunday count. The replica fWo World Title falls to the ground. Voice-over: The previous advertisement was paid for by 3vil. Cue evil laugher.
