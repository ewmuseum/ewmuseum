Because of me ?

31 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Because of me ? December 31, 1999 [The scene opens peering through the broken down door to Rain's dressing room. courtesy of the fWo security. Rain is seated on a bench in the middle of the room with his wrestling attire still on. The recently retained Internet Title sits undisturbed on the bench beside Rain. Rain's hands are on his face, and his hair is flowing over. It's hours after Warfare to Oblivion two has ended and the Internet Champion has no pride for his recent win of the night. He looks up, sunglasses are gone, and a look of guilt or confusion rests in his eyes.] Rain: What is happening ? What is happening ? Is this my fault ? Is the man known as Joy deceased because of me ? Is a new born reign of terror entering the fWo, because of me ? Does a nation weep because of me ? Is a terror not yet known to the fWo coming, because of me ? Yes. Rain: Why have the God's forsaken me ? My night of victory was supposed to come last night. Instead my night of shame became announced. Even if I had won the World Title, would things have changed from the way they are now ? The God's swore to protect me, I SERVED THEM. The Storm still resides within me, and I know this is not the fault of the God's as they could not have forseen this. If my lust for power was not so great, none of this would have happened. Most people are in danger during this time, I have the Power of the Storm to protect me. The crystal, is gone. Spencer was not smart. But even the most moronic of individuals could figure out that when the Crystal was crushed, more happened than what it seems. Machina is figuring out. The fWo is under attack, and I do not know what to do. Am I to blame ? [The scene fades to Black.]
