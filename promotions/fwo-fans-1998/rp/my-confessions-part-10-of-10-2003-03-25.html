My Confessions - Part 10 of 10

25 March 2003

MY CONFESSIONS Part Ten - Catharsis "What was up with that set tonight," asked TJ, handing Angel his beer bottle, "you were all over the damn place and didn’t answer anyone’s signals.” “Don’t ask,” replied Angel, taking a long drink as she held a towel to her torn- up forearm, “the only thing keeping me from breaking down is the fact that I’m in that post – performance numb phase.” Angel walked off the stage after her performance – slash – nervous breakdown and headed outside to look for any sign of her parents’ being there. She held the smallest amount of hope that they would’ve suddenly shown up at the last minute to see her play just like in the movies. They didn’t. Angel is sweating, bleeding, and drinking. But she’s oddly at peace with herself. “What next?” asked TJ. “We leave,” replied Angel, “Tonight. I don’t want to come back here ever again. I want to collect my baby, get on the bus, and go back home. I need to sleep in my own bed tonight. And I want Elijah there with me.” “That’s, huh,” said TJ, “He’s halfway around the world right now.” “I just need to talk to him is all.” “Call him?” “He’s working right now.” TJ drained his beer and grabbed Angel’s arm, looking over the bloody cuts, “Well, you’re gonna live. We got all the glass out.” She shook her head – for a moment TJ thought she would start to cry again but she held back. “They’re gonna pay, Teej… they’re gonna pay.” “Who?” “Who do you think?” She stood up, pacing defiantly, “Every time I sing. Every album we do. Every publicity shit we do… I’m gonna dedicate it all to my parents for never believing in me. For never supporting me. For lighting that fire under my ass and making me say ‘Okay – things are getting harder and harder now… but I’m gonna make it. I’m gonna make it for the sole reason that I can shove it up their ass.’ They’re gonna be so sick of seein’ my face, Teej… they’re gonna have to live with the fact that the biggest fuckin’ band in the world succeeded despite their apathy… not because of their support.” As she kicked the door with adrenaline, TJ raised his drink in the air. “Yesssah! And we start with tonight! This was Night One of the Great Valerian’s Garden Comeback!” She laughed. “How can we have a comeback? We haven’t really made it yet.” “Patience, grasshopper,” replied TJ, “I think you were onto something with a return to California. We go home, we lay these songs down on tape. And we make ’Under the Surface’ the biggest release of the year.” Angel smiled, running her hand over the scars forming on her forearm. “Scarred.” “Hmm?” “Scarred,” she repeated, “This entire trip has been the basis for the words I wrote… why not let this be the culmination? ’Scarred’ by Valerian’s Garden.” He thought about it. “I like it.” Nine suddenly stuck his head out the back door. “Otep’s done, Angel… and she wants to officially meet you. So does a bunch’a people… someone in there says he’s boys with Eli.” “Shall we?” asked TJ, “The public wants to hang with the biggest band in the world.” “One second, Nine,” said Angel, before she turned her attention back to her best friend, “We’ll be there someday, Teej…” She embraced him, kissing him on the cheek. “Thanks for stickin’ around.” “Well,” he replied, “Didn’t have much to do these past ten years.” He held the door for her as she scooted inside. “Besides, Angel… we’re your family. Can’t do much without a family.” “Fuck my family.” “A family,” he corrected, “is the people who love you no matter what… the people who don’t need an explanation because they already know what you’re about. And you’ve got that on this bus… you’ve always had that in some fashion.” Despite the weight of the past two days, Angel smiled. “I know.” March the twenty- third, two- thousand- three… was the night Valerian’s Garden truly staked their claim on the world of music. The message would spread quickly… … as good music is wont to do. And when it comes down to how well a singer feels what she’s singing… nobody does it better than the girl simply known as Angel. ~A New Beginning~
