Kids

8 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cut my tongue out, I've been caught out - like a giant juggernaut. Cooper's Gym, Bronx, NY December 8, 2003 4:22 PM local time "What's the big news?" asked Luis Gonzales, as he walked through the door to the gym's locker room area, "I've gotta be at work by five." Happy hours, golden showers, on a cruise to freak you out. The six foot six, two hundred and thirty pound man crossed the room toward where Rosalyn Callasantos sat, looking at him with that innocent glare that's melted most of their highschool. In the bathroom, the sound of someone throwing up echoed into the lockers themselves. We could fly a helicopter, nothing left to talk about "That sounds like Randy," observed Luis. "It is," replied Cally. Entertain you, celebrate you, I'll be back to frame you-- Rosalyn Callasantos pulled her legs up, resting her knees on her chin. Her jeans pulled up just slightly, giving Lou a glimpse of her shins and ankles - covered in black & blue marks. Nobody worked harder at the gym than she did, and the tragedy was that nobody seemed to be making less progress. When I grow up, I'll be stable. When I grow up I'll turn the tables She tried as hard as she could, but the smallish brunette had trouble running spots, trouble climbing the ropes, and trouble with her wind. But she was always there, trying her best. You had to respect that. Trying hard to fit among you, floating out to wonderland "What's his problem?" asked Luis, "Did he - gasp - get a B-PLUS in something?" Cally laughed along with him, but she shook her head. "Miss Ivy was in over the weekend. She got the gentlemen who run the fWo to have a dark match at their Countdown to Oblivion shows this year with some of the boys here." "Don't tell me--" She smiled. "Randall Knox was the first name on her list." Unprotected, God, I'm pregnant, damn the consequences-- "Hah!" said Luis, as he leaned into the bathroom, "Y'okay in there, Randy?" *COUGH* "I'm okay," came the haggard reply, "Just a little nervous." Luis turned back towards Cally. "He's got nothing to be nervous about, I wish he'd realize it. Can't you talk to him?" Cally stood up and cracked her elbows. "He won't listen to me, he never has." Her gaze lingered into the bathroom. Luis recognized it - she's had a ridiculously strong crush on Knox since she first laid eyes on him. His obliviousness and her shyness kept them apart, but it seemed everyone knew she was interested except Knox himself. "Knox? Calm down, son," said Luis, "It's just twenty thousand people--" Retching sounds. Cally winced. "--but it's not on TV, and half the people there've probably seen you wrestle here in the gym at least once." When I grow up, I'll be stable. When I grow up I'll turn the tables... Silence. "Can you guys give me a minute?" asked Knox. "Sure," replied Cally, gesturing with her head towards the door. She and Luis headed out, walking slowly toward the ring. Cally did a quick two-step dance as they reached it, showing that a few bruises and achy body parts weren't enough to dampen her spirits. "What'chu think of that guy that came by last time Ivy was here full time? Miles, was it?" "He seemed nice enough," replied Cally, hopping up onto the ring apron, "definitely someone who's been around the business." Luis laughed. "You're sixteen, lil' girl, how's you know what someone around the business is like?" Blood and blisters, on my fingers, chaos rules when we're apart "You just have to be observant," said Cally, "Mr. Flair and Miss Ivy taught everyone in this building everything that we needed to know about the wrestling business--" "--except how to wrestle," said Luis, sticking out his tongue. Cally laughed, and mock - slapped him. "Asshole." "You'll miss me when I'm gone." "Try me." Watch my temper, I'll go mental, I'll try to be gentle-- "Do you think he's ready?" asked Luis. "I think he's as ready as he'll ever be," replied Cally, "and that's pretty darn tough. Fizzy believed in him, Mr. Coop believed in him. Miss Ivy believes in him... and he's good. He's really good." For over a year now, Randall Knox has been training and wrestling in Coop's Gym. He seemed to have an affinity for the sport of professional wrestling, just as completely as he had the knack for the sports-entertainment side of it. Johnny Fizzbin and Eli Flair were just about as good as you could get when it came to wrestlers giving trainees in-ring experience, and it wasn't long before Knox had learned all they had to teach. "But if I ever find out that you're slackin' off in school," Ivy had said to him, "I'm going to kick your fucking ass from here to Australia." He never forgot. "Hey," said Knox, as he emerged from the locker rooms - ever present skateboard in hand, "think you can give her a ride home on your way to work?" Knox had cleaned himself up a bit - there were no spots on his black tanktop or his jeans, nothing in his blonde hair, pushed behind his ears. "It's cool," replied Cally, "I'll walk with you. You feeling better?" When I grow up, I'll be stable. When I grow up I'll turn the tables... "Yeah, I am." ...When I grow up... "Cool. Luis, you'd better get to work." ...When you grow up... "Arite," said Luis, "Listen, Randy... you sure you're alright?" ...When we grow up we'll turn the tables... "Yeah, I'm fine." Don't take offense, don't make amends, rip it all to shreds and let it go. "Aight, killa, I'm out." Luis smacked his knuckles against Knox's, and gave Cally a reassuring squeeze on the knee, "By the way, did Ivy have any idea who's gonna be your opponent?" How you like me best, you let me know. Knox laughed. "'The Machine' Luis Gonzales," said Knox. Suddenly, it seemed as if the color had drained from his face. "Are you... kidding me?" "Nope." "Need to puke?" asked Cally, amused. Luis regained his composure quickly, however. "Not at all, lil' girl." An unsteady step toward the door. "But I think," he said, "I need to call out tonight." Rip it all to shreds and let go Knox and Cally watched him leave the gym, muttering to himself the whole way. She looked at him and smiled. "Ready to go?" "Just about," replied Knox, as he put on a backwards baseball cap and dropped the board to the gym floor, "think Momma Callasantos is up for feeding a stray?" How you like me best? You let me know... "Sure," replied Cally with a smile, "My mom loves you." She took his arm as the two walked out of the gym... twenty-three days removed, from a date with destiny.
