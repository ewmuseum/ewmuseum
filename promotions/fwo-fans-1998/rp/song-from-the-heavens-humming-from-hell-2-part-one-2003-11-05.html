Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 2 - Part One

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Hello and welcome to the Seacoast Suites, my name is Henry how may I assist you?" The kid had bleached blonde hair, I mean I know that he may be dressed in some fancy little suit and tightened his tie tight around his neck but he didn't fool me. He wasn't nice, he hated his job, probably was thinking about how ugly and hard I look. How he is forcing a smile because if he doesn't hold onto his control he might look away and or nervous might make me the "guest" know what he really thought of me. I hate him already, and all I want is to slowly tear into his eyes and peel them from his skull. I wanted to rip his pathetic heart from his body and chew on his flesh for fresh breath. "Hi my name is Steve Douglas," I spoke in a respectful tone, "I'm here as a part of the fWo. This is my assistant Spencer Sullivan who also has a room here," I could smell his temperature raise as his presence quickly grew agitated. He was uncomfortable and I could smell his disgust. "Excellent, Mr. Fox wanted me to tell you that he has reserved one of the top floor suites for you, it is on the tenth floor, room 1020, you have a great view of the city and you are in direct view of the pool." He spoke in a fake tone and then turned his attention to Spencer after he handed me the key to my room, "Now you sir have been placed in 1022, which is across the hall from Mr. Douglas' room." Spencer took the key from him and said nothing; Spencer was a man of few words. He was sick, but he liked it, he liked hurting , I let him taste and then he engulfed it yearning for more.
