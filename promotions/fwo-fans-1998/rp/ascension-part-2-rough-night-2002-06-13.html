Ascension, Part 2 (Rough Night)

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 11th, 2002 Lance Knight walked into the dressing room after going through a plate-glass window through a table. Tonight, he was sharing the locker room with Eli Flair, "Poison" Ivy McGinnis, and one of his opponents for the night, "Triple X" Sean Stevens. Eli and Sean were very good friends, and Eli and Lance had really bonded over the course of the house show tour, ever since Flair had taken Lance to his first card game backstage at the final Slaughter. However, Sean and Lance really hadn't warmed up to one another, whether it was because of their unique relationship with Poison Ivy (she was becoming a mentor to Lance, and she was Sean's girlfriend), or simply because the two men were enough alike that their personalities just naturally conflicted. At any rate, Lance was hoping to get out of the locker room before Sean made his way back, but he knew that plan was a bust when he saw Eli and Ivy sitting in the room, discussing their plans for the match Eli would be wrestling later in the evening. They turned their attention to Lance, who was still breathing heavily and bleeding from a score of minor cuts on his chest. Eli smiled, favoring Lance with a slap on the back as the smaller man walked past the bench on which he sat. "Rough night?" the hardcore champion inquired, his gaze following his friend, whose shoulders were slumped as he trudged towards his locker. "He's not standing in a pool of his own blood," chimed in Ivy, doing her best to lighten the mood, "so how hard could it have been?" Much to Ivy's relief, Lance brightened at this. "Yeah, at least when I went through a table against you, Eli, it was because you put me through it. Tonight, I just made an ass out of myself and splashed an empty table and pane of glass where Grendel was a couple of seconds earlier." Both Eli and Ivy looked on with concern, but it was Ivy that spoke first. "Through a pane of glass and a table? That's your biggest one to date, isn't it?" "Yeah, and it was exactly like we planned out before the match. But I had no idea it'd hurt this damned bad," Lance replied, looking down at his singlet, which was cut and bloody. This set him off thinking about his conversation with Chris Universal the night before at MSG. Eli immediately noticed Lance's change in demeanor, and he too did his best to cheer Lance up. "What's wrong, Atlas? You look like you've got the weight of the world on your shoulders." Lance didn't immediately answer Eli's question, but instead sat down on the bench nearest his locker and steepled his hands in front of his face. Having spent quite a bit of time with this young man over the past month, Eli and Ivy both knew that Lance was thinking about something, and rather than interrupt his train of thought, they went back to their conversation. This continued for nearly five minutes, until an exultant Sean Stevens returned to the locker room. He shared a handshake and congratulatory words with Eli and a deep hug and kiss with Ivy, but then something caught his attention. Without a word, he hitched a thumb in Lance's direction, as if to question what was up with him. In response, Ivy solemnly shook her head, wordlessly warning Sean to just let the youngster be. Sean then proceeded to hit the showers, and Ivy and Eli returned to their conversation. As the sound of running water came and went, Lance Knight eventually made his way to his feet. He walked past Eli and Ivy, who gazed at him quizzically, seeming to pay them no mind, just as he paid no mind to the blood that was still slowly oozing down his chest. As he reached the door of the locker room, Eli finally spoke. "Hey, kid, where you going?" "I've gotta talk to Chris, Eli. You've just given me the most amazing idea."
