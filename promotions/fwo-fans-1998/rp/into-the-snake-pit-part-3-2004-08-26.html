Into the Snake Pit - Part 3

26 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The rain drummed harder now. Nick sat on the small wooden stool, the only piece of furniture in the room, and stared out the small basement window. He could see water splashing up off the ground, could see the blurred image of people hurriedly walking by. Nick wondered what the people he'd left down here thought about before his father got to them. Footsteps approached the door. Nick didn't bother to get up. He just watched the rain fall and squinted slightly as the door opened and the light switch was flipped. A single lightbulb hung overhead and shone down on the big man, who kept his back to the door. He didn't have to see the procession that entered the room. He'd seen it all before: two men armed with semi-automatics, one man known only as the Doctor armed with a large medical bag, a young boy dressed in a catholic schoolboy outfit, and finally the man known across the Pacific Rim as Steel Serpent. "It's been a long time Nikolas," his father said as he approached his son. Nick stood up and turned around. He looked down at the small, frail-looking old man and knew he was anything but. There was no point in trying to read his father's face. It'd be simpler to read the expression of a stone. At least Nick came by it honestly. "Why am I here?" The Serpent began to slowly circle his son. "I hear you're becoming a big star in America. Number one superstar wrestler! Why do you waste your time with that fake garbage?" "It's what I do. Why am I here?" "You are here because you were asked, by me, to do a job on behalf of an associate of mine." Nick heard the footsteps stop directly behind him. He watched the two armed men standing on either side of the door. You did not do the job. You killed my associate instead." The Vishanti job. That fucking Creed, always a thorn in his side. The Doctor was off to the side, assembling a small folding table he'd brought in with him. Nick looked over at the small Thai boy and guessed him to be around twelve years old. "Take your shirt off Nick." The Serpent addressed the young boy next. "Take your brother's jacket and shirt, then go and get the bowl." Brother. Another bastard child of the Serpent's Brides. Nick wondered if he'd been taught the ugly reality of Life yet. He took off his jacket, pulled off his t-shirt, and handed both to the boy who quickly departed from the room. The Doctor reached into his medical bag and pulled out a short length of woven leather rope, gauze, and a salve of some kind. "You are mine," the Serpent told the Monster. "I made you. The tattoos on your body are markings representing your status within the family. You are the first one to wear the markings on your face. A most distinguished honor. How do you show your gratitude? By bringing disgrace to my house!" When the boy returned with what looked like a cooking wok filled with burning coals, Nick realized what was about to happen. There were only two guards. The Doctor wouldn't try and stop him. The boy couldn't stop him. That only left the Steel Serpent. Nick sat down on the stool. His father circled again, walking around to the front of the big man. "Your body markings will be covered by the blackest tattoos we can make, to reflect the darkness you've brought to your family. As for your facial markings..." The boy set the metallic bowl down on the table. Steel Serpent reached in, swatting aside a few hot coals before withdrawing a long, flat blade. "Your facial markings must be removed." The Doctor walked up to Nick and began swabbing the tattoos on his face before handing him the strand of bundled leather. "Bite into this," he said as if he was reading medication instruction to a patient. "Try not to move." The club patrons above him danced, sang, drank, and enjoyed their lives. Nick Brandish watched his father, who stared back at him with a predator's eyes, move closer to his face with a glowing hot blade. "When you leave here, you start over. You prove your worth all over again. Fight to earn my blessing." He could have run. He could have easily overpowered everyone in the room. Instead, Nick Brandish bit down on the leather rope and screamed as strips of his face were seared off. His eyes rolled into his head and nearly lost consciousness. Blood from his gums trickled into his mouth and dribbled down his throat. Through it all, the Monster filed away all that agony as a debt to be paid to Kodiak Vic Creed. Creed would pay. Creed would suffer.
