His Perspective

11 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOTE: This all takes place two weeks ago, directly after Triple X vs. Eli Flair. That should explain everything leading up to date. ------ Slaughter had ended. And, like so many shows in the past... Whether it was TCW: Confrontation, SCW: Showtime, or HEW: Zero Tolerance... the outcome almost always was the same - this time being no different. Eli Flair won... Triple X lost. Face it Sean... you're just not good enough. "Dammit, I had that match won," Stevens attempted to convince himself as the steam from the hot shower began to rise. It had been nearly an hour since he had wrestled in one of the night's most entertaining match-ups, against his long time rival, now, good friend, "Total Elimination" Eli Flair. And, while it was true... they no longer hated each other's guts... no longer wanted to see the other carried out on a stretcher with a one way ticket to somebody's hospital... and, honestly, genuinely cared about each other's well being as friends... there was still something intense about getting the opportunity to wrestle one another, yet again. Had the match won? Please... you had what Eli allowed you to have. It's called mind games, Sean. Like every other time you two have wrestled... Eli had you right where he wanted you. So what you hit your lame excuse for a finisher... he'd have kicked out. He'd have kept going. ...he always kicks out and keeps going. "Whatever," Sean mumbled. There were no hard feelings, however. Sean and Eli were friends in the truest sense. They left it all in the ring, and even walked up the aisle together, helping each other through the curtain - oh, yeah... Ivy was there, too. Not only can't you beat Flair... you're stupid. You think Ivy actually might want something to do with you? That's a laugh! She's simply using you, until Hornet comes back calling. That's where her heart is, you know? Hornet. Not you... him. She loves Hornet... she could never love an arrogant asshole like you. You're just not good enough. "Not true. I'm not listening to you." Sean and Ivy had made arrangements to meet up sometime after the show - arrangements Triple X had definitely intended to keep. He was nervous. This... had been a long, long time coming - he and Ivy talking, hanging out, ironing out their past. But, never did Triple X ever think it could be as nerve-racking as it was. You know why you're nervous, right? Because deep down... on the inside... you know. ...you know that Ivy doesn't care about you, half as much as you care about her. She's Hornet's property for life. If anything... he's just letting you rent, until he's ready to come home. You're a loser, Sean. You can't beat Flair... can't beat Hornet... can't win Ivy McGinnis. You're a choke artist in every sense of the word. You're pathetic. Sean turned off the shower. "...fuck you..." He wasn't gonna let his insecurities or inner most fears win this battle. Right then, and there he had realized he can overcome anything he truly decided to put his mind to. Sean liked Ivy... hell, who was he kidding... he loved her. And, while he'd love to have her in his life as a love interest... if she wasn't interested - while it would definitely hurt - he just wanted her in his life. In any way she'd have him.
