The Xenosaga: The Deleted Scenes - Take 55 1/2

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Cagle! Bring een the vid-yo tapes!" "CUT!" Beef the slightly-annoyed yelped, slightly-annoyed. "Video tapes? Who the hell thinks video tapes are torture." "Well, with all do respect Director Beef, the Slightly Annoyed God of Everythin' and everyone," Cagle began. Beef of course had been forcing James to call him this for quite a while. "Ah reckon Street Fighter: The Movie is about as torturin' as you can--" *SLAP!* "Do not say that!" Beef screamed. "Say what? Street Fighter: The Movie?" *SLAP!* "Do not say that!" Janito replied, "Oh sodding hell, say what?" "You know what!" "Street Fighter: The Movie?" *SLAP!* "Do not say that!" Steve stepped forward, and gave a threatening glare. "SILENCE." And all went silent. That is, until John Rocker burst onto the scene with a 95 mph fast-ball, a wicked slider and movement that made you go ":O". "Gawd damn New Yorkers are the worst damn fans in the world. New York don't no shit!" Odd-Job, being a New Yorker (not really, but for the sake of beating the shit out of John Rocker because it's fun, we'll say he is), did not take kindly to those words. He didn't like them. NOT_AT_ALL! NOT_AT_ALL!! "...." Thusly, Odd-Job proceeded to hurracanrana that sumbitch. John Rocker must've flew ten feet and slammed into Allen. Allen Speck was on his cell phone, complaining about the chornic case of diarrhea Dingleberry had during the filming of that take. The cell phone was dropped, broken, and then stepped on by 2,000 raging canadians named Celine Dion. Allen frowned, "Crap, there goes THAT running joke." "I wish Susan was here. He'd know what to do." Rocker said, as he brushed himself off. "Susan?" Asked Beef, cracking a smile. "Yes." "THE_Susan?" "...Yes." The entire room, excluding John Rocker burst into laughter that sounded a lot like the following: "BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!!!!" John Rocker frowned, "That's bullshit." "That's SO what Susan would say!" "BWAAAAAAAAAAAAAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!" "....." Odd-job joked. "BWAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHA!" Suddenly, Richard Simmons bursted through the door. He was directly punched in the nose by Gene Simmons, former KISS front-runner and lover of Richard Simmons. However, when he read the dirtsheets and got the facts on Gene Simmons relationship with Pat Sajack, well fuck me Freddy, things just haven't been the same. Great Dragon appeared shortly after, amidst the on-going jokes of Carson Covington being green and Janito secretly lusting for the jumpin' jack maniac Simmons (which one, you ask? Take a guess, foo'). Dingleberry did a back-flip. "Now that's what I call some serious showboating!" Great Dragon said. The room busted out into laughter once more: "BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!!!!" Dingleberry ran up and humped Great Dragon's leg. "WHAT THE HELL!?!" "BAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!" "IS EVERYTHING I SAY FUNNY TO YOU IDIOTS!?!?!" "BWAAAAAAAAAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!" The scene faded, while Steve gave everyone a threatening glare. "SILENCE." The room grew silent.
