Two Messages

22 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lately I've been carrying around a handgun to protect myself. It's a good, reliable Glock. I've never tested it out, but I know it's there when I need it. I know a lot of wrestlers who've had to tote these pistols around, but I never thought I'd become one of them. In fact, I don't like carrying it around. It reminds me too much of what happened to Lance Starr. In my years in the fWo I'd never seen someone get shot on a show until one year ago. Lance Starr was my friend. And I'll tell you first hand that it's hard to lose friends. We rode up and down the roads across North America and we even traveled together in Japan. I didn't think I'd forge lifelong friendships in the fWo, but Lance was a genuine friend. He never really made it big - I don't know if he ever would have - but he worked damn hard on shows without getting recognized for it. And now, he's gone. Universal and Davis have a lot of audacity. Davis already cost me my family. He pissed all over the Lance Starr tribute. Universal took a shit all over the respect I had for him. These two men have taken over my life. Crippling them is all I can think about. I don't even need a partner for Unleashed. I'll go to Singapore and take them both out. Universal told me that I'm just like Starr: dead. That's where he's wrong. I'm alive and well. He can't piss all over me without me doing anything about it. And if he wants to make me dead, then he'd better guess again, because I'll make sure to kill him first. People tend to think that fucking with my life will bring them to the top. For the desperate has-beens that Universal and Davis are, that's their meal ticket. What they don't realize is that when people mess with my personal life, they get hurt. Ask Chris Titan if he remembers falling head-first through the fWo online broadcasting station at Cyberslam. He won't remember, and that's my point. I used to be intimidated by all these experienced veterans. That is, until I realized that I was at the same level as them and I could beat any one of them on any given night. But for me this isn't about winning titles or getting my pretty face on a magazine cover. I can win any title on any given night. I can beat any man on any given night. I don't let that shit worry me anymore. What worries me now is that I've got a score to settle with Davis and Universal. I hope they got the message when I whipped them both around with a cane. If they didn't, here is the message: You are my bitches. You pissed me off, and therefore I will hurt you until you scream for mercy. I will cripple you and there's nothing you will do about it. The phone rings. "Hi," I say. "It has been a while. You're downstairs in the hotel lobby? That sounds great. I'll be down there in a couple of minutes. I look forward to what you have to say." Looks like I have a tag team partner for Unleashed. And it looks like I have another message for Universal and Davis: You're really, really fucked.
