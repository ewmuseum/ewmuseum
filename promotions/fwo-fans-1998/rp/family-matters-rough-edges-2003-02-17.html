Family Matters: Rough Edges

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life had a way of turning back on itself more often that not. It seemed that there was never a straight answer to anything or a straight road out of a problem area. When things seemed their darkest though the man known as Brand Frontier knew that he had something to fall back on, a support system to get him through the dark times. His family. That is, until recently. Because of actions taken by dark men and reaction to darker times Brandon Roche found his support system fractured at a time he needed it the most. When one relies on such a system to get them through life what happens when the system itself is what has caused the current and darkest of problems? It's a time of rebuilding, a time to realign oneself and set new boundaries, new support. A new life. One built on the ashes of the old, stronger than before but different. Now was the time to bring the pieces together to make sure they fit, to make sure there were no fault lines to begin the process which had so recently grown out of control to destroy the system the first time. Brandon and Amy Campbell had grown close over the last few months. Dire situations conspired to draw them together. During such a time their friendship was forged under fire. It is strong and resolute. A friendship born of hard times normally lasts the longest, and if the previous five months had shown anything it had shown that they would always be there for one another. The friendship came at a time when Brandon's family had collapsed under the strain of the aforementioned dark times. Now he tries to fit Amy in to the hole that was left behind. The edges are jagged and the opening still fresh. The red haired sprite is not an exact fit, and maybe that is the best thing of all. Different than others he has known, she brings something to the table no one has before, an energy that he cannot describe Brandon is drawn to it like a moth to flame.
