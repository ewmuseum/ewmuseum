Explosions and Mumbling

22 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just to prove The Underground doesn’t hang around in a bar all day, every day, The Flying Frenchie, Mike Bear, and his wife and manager Brittany Chambers have decided to take in a bit of theater. … OK, fine, they’re not watching a play or a performance, but they’re in a theater. That counts right? … Fine, they’re at a Seattle movie theater watching a daytime showing of Fast and Furious. But they’re not really paying attention. “Pete, why the hell did we come to this movie?” Some less than others. Frenchie is mostly watching the film, Mike has barely glanced at the screen, looking mostly in the direction of his tag team partner. Brittany is browsing the FWO website using her iPhone between games of Super Monkey Ball. "I watched ze first t'ree movies, I'm due a satisfactory finish." (Cars drive by quickly) “Whatever. So seriously, you’re gonna be a dad? That I did not see coming. Must’ve been a heckuva trip…” “Well…” “Wait a second, you only went home that one time where we came with you and Brawn. We were sleeping in the next room!” “Well, Mike, you know zey say about ze French.” “Well I…” Sensing another Mike-induced “incident” about to happen, Brittany chimed in. “Speaking of announcements, I’ve got one of my own.” (Cars explode) “Oh God, don’t tell me you’re pregnant too.” Brittany looked up from her phone and gave Mike a puzzled look. “What would be the problem with that?” To the untrained eye it was a puzzled look, at least. This was clearly a look that says “If you answer incorrectly, you will be physically incapable of ever having children.” Mike was familiar. He brought his hand up to stroke his goatee, thinking really hard to come up with the right answer for a good 20 seconds. Finally, sheepishly, he said, “Nothing… honey?” Brittany glanced over to Frenchie and subtlely shook her head. Frenchie just shrugged slightly. “No, Mike, I’m not pregnant. That would be especially inappropriate in light of my actual announcement. I’m planning on asking Ivy to book me in the last qualifier for that Silver Pyramid match on the 29th.” That drew stunned looks from both Mike and Frenchie. (Cars explode quickly) “Brit, really? I mean, you’ve been away from the ring for a long time. Are you sure you…” Brittany rolled her eyes and interrupted her husband. “Michael, seriously, we just had a tag team match together in February that we won, if you forgot. It’s a qualifier on top of that. If I can’t beat whoever they throw in there, that’ll be a clear enough sign I’m not ready. Most of all, I don’t want to look back on this opportunity when I’m retired from this business and say that all I ever did in the FWO was be your arm candy.” “Brit, I... Sorry. I didn’t mean it like that. I just don’t want to see you get injured by getting in over your head.” Finally, the 3 people in the back had enough. "Could you shut up!? We're trying to watch a movie!" Bear stood up from his seat and turned around with the fire usually reserved for KSZ. "Oh come on, you can't actually be following the plot! Now I’m trying to have a conversation here!" Frenchie craned his head back dismissively. "I hate to break it to you, but ze good guys win. I'm sorry, I just spoiled every action flick since 1945." Mike finished giving the three teenagers the Death Stare and sat back down. One was pretty sure that the scary man 5 rows in front of them was Mike Bear of the FWO, but dared not speak another word. Brittany continued her conversation as if nothing had happened. “Over my head, huh? You better watch out or I’ll have you double booked on reAction in your tag match and against me in the qualifier.” Mike chuckled a little. “I don’t think I meet the cruiserweight limit. Besides, I’m a former FWO World Heavyweight Champion.” “And I’m the one who single-handedly dragged you back to your therapy sessions more times than I can count. What’s your point?” Brittany stared a hole right through Mike, who did his best not display any emotion. Determining that “the look” would not end, he turned back to his tag team partner. (Long pause in the action where they trim the budget down by having Vin Diesel mumble) “So Pete, that reminds me, we need to discuss all the tag team stuff. We don’t even have a tag team name. All the best teams have team names.” “Mike, ze current World Team Team Champions do not have a team name.” “I said the best teams.” “Well I am still partial to Grizzly Beret.” “There is no way I’m letting you name us Grizzly Beret. What does that even mean? Is it a furry beret? Seems a little girly.” “You realize I wear a beret in ze ring.” “I’m talking about the furry part! Listen, I came up another name. You ready? Rude, Shrewd, and Tattooed. What do you think?” “It would seem a more fitting name for ze trios we have so many of now.” “No no, stay with me. The Tattooed part because, well obviously.” “Even t’ough I have none.” Barely paying attention to Frenchie, Mike continues. “The Shrewd part because we’re both experienced ring veterans.” Frenchie smiled that trademark Frenchie smile. “Of course.” “And the Rude part because, well, you’re French.” The smile disappeared and Frenchie raised a finger in objection, but thought for a minute. “You know, I’d be offended, but you may be on to somet’ing zere.” (Exploding cars drive by quickly) “I know, right?” “Still, it is too long. A good name should be concise.” “Yeah, like World Peace Organization? Nothing says concise like a 5 syllable word.” “Oh whatever. Why can we not just be Ze Underground?” “Because Chris and I were The Underground, and then we had Fear and Tom join and it got confusing, and new members means higher merchandise sales. So despite the fact that there’s only two of us…” “AHEM. Three of us.” Brittany had not broken her stare. Mike cringed slightly, trying to imagine how to never look at his wife again to avoid the Death Stare. He swallowed tentatively. “My mistake, three of us, and since there’s another wrestler in the group, we need a new name for our tag team.” “Well zis is a tough one. How did we do t'ings like zis in ze old days?” “We made Eddie do it.” “Zat is what is missing, a good lackey. Ze Underground had Eddie. Ze Foundation had Mitch. We will have to work on zat.” (Driving explosions win a quick race against exploding mumbles) Still feeling the Stare on the back of his head, Mike turned his attention, but not his eyes, back to Brittany. “So… honey. Would you (hard swallow) like me to help you practice for your upcoming match?” “Oh, I had something like that in mind.” Her flat tone confirmed that Mike knew exactly what he was in for. He found himself wishing for unexpected parenthood. The Flying Frenchie just smiled knowingly as more explosions, cars, and mumbling filled the screen.
