Leaping Before Learning to Walk: The Current State of Affairs

6 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jon Crisp brushed his tattered hair out of his face. He looked into a mirror at the Motel 6 in Bumblefuck Egypt, his stay for the night. He had been sleeping in beds for the last two weeks, after going for quite some time without. He didn't know just how long it had been, only that it was too long. Crisp gazed into the mirror, and saw the last who-knows-how-long wearing on his skin. Wrinkles took the place of fair skin. A 26-year-old man appeared to be 43, and aging years by the minute. He sighed, and slumped back onto his bed. The bed was covered by a 1979-model grandmother spread: floral patterns one could have sworn they saw as curtains on a popular disco game show. He didn't care; as long as it kept him warm, they could have been made out of sheep shit, and resembled just as much. He didn't remember much while he was off on his drunken stupor. In fact, three things came to mind. One, Jennifer didn't enjoy him going off, and they were on the outs. He hadn't spoken to her in almost two months. He tried calling twice, both collect. Once she wasn't there. The second time, someone else picked up. A man's voice. The call wasn't accepted. Second, he remembered that someone took all of his money. He still had some money in the bank, but it was obvious that whoever Jennifer was seeing took liberal use of their bank account; it was quite obvious that getting a joint bank account was a bad idea, although he didn't think that he would be the reason for it. Lastly, he remembered that he had scores to settle. He always kept that in mind. In fact, it's what drove him to his stupor of haziness and drunkiness. Crisp cursed Chris Universal's name as other thoughts caused the mental pleasure of Crisp bashing Universal's face in off the mind landscape. Crisp grabbed a copy of the paper he cut Wretch with two weeks ago. Plantiff: Jon Crisp Defendants: Chris Kidman, Jr., fans Wrestling organization, Mr. T, La. K. Parka. Crisp looked at the last two names. Perhaps he did that out of spite. But those idiots had it coming to them. They pushed him over the edge. They cost him the biggest win of his fWo career. "T's gonna lose his chains over that", Crisp said aloud, smiling. "That should lead to some hilarity." His smile turned dark, wretched. He wasn't comedy anymore. No longer. It held him down. He was serious about his career now. His priorities were in order. Short term: Deal with Wretch. Take his money. Get back on my feet. Long term: Erase Universal. Crisp began to let his mind drift some more, generally towards the many promiscuous women he found in New Orleans. Eventually, it drifted back to Jennifer, asking him back. Finally, Crisp had some peace. At least for now.
