Master-MIND, Phase I

14 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everything was dark and cold. He wouldn't be able to see his own hand three inches from his face. His body shivered and straightened up against the cement wall he had been propped against. He could feel rope around his wrists behind his back. Rope wrapped around his ankles; he couldn't move at all. Every so often he thought he could hear something moving across the room; but there was no real way of knowing what was happening. No matter how hard his eyes strained for a view of something other than the color black, it wouldn't come. His body ached every time it moved even an inch. It was like pins and needles jabbing into his already worn body. His mind began to wander, wondering where he was; what was happening to him; what would happen to him. Even the thought of being dead crossed his mind. Thinking that he was in his grave; but still having the sensation of being alive. His thoughts were soon broken as he thought he heard something familiar. It hurts, doesn't it? His body froze. He thought they were gone and dead after what had happened between he and Eli. Suddenly he became very frightened. The thought of everything from February until CyberSlam reoccurring and having to go through it all again. It was too much to handle in one thought. The voices you hear, constantly pulling your attention and focus in so many different directions that you can't concentrate or think coherently. The voices of your peers, telling you what you should and shouldn't be doing with your life, pushing you to do what they consider is the right thing. The voices of authority, descending upon you with their self-righteousness and demanding that you follow their rules and their way of thinking in order to control and monitor you. Everything that was being said couldn't be truer. He knew it was true, but he couldn't help but listen to what it was telling him again. It wasn't telling him what he should or shouldn't do. It was telling him what had happened, what was wrong and what could have been avoided through everything. He let his eyes close as the voice continued. Voices, all telling you what to do. It hurts, doesn't it? "It hurts terribly," he whispered to the darkness.. And now me, a new voice to enter into your confused and catastrophic little world. "What do you want?" he asked cautiously. What do I want? I want you to do whatever it is you want to do, Fallen One. If you want to sit in a darkened corner and stare at the blank wall until humanity finally destroys itself, that is what I want as well. If you want to curl up on the cold cement floor with your arms wrapped securely around your knees and your infantile sobs echoing into the uncaring darkness, then that is what I want. For the first time in a long time a semi-smile crossed his face in the darkness. Someone or something was finally going to listen to what he wanted and what he had to say. I want you to deal with the pain that THEY have forced upon you. Take as much or as little time and energy and thought as you need; they cannot hurt you here. I will make sure of that. Finally, he was saved.
