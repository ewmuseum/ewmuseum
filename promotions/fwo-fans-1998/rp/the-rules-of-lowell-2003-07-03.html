The Rules of Lowell

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please Note... The following contains clippings from Lowell Dot Com's online handbook of 'Follow The Czar' guidelines. If you are content with the way you are currently living your life- change, 'cause no matter what you think, his way is better. That is all. Ah, Summer... 'Women-in-tight-binkis' Summer. Is there anything, including saving the life of another human being, that compares with watching a hot twenty-year-old stroll around in a thong? Lowell's BMW came to a screeching halt at the end of his driveway. He adjusted his rear-view mirror. A group of ten or so ladies were walking past his Summer house, down to the beach. ...In the Dot Com's own words, read easily from the ear-to-ear, delighted smirk on his face: I think not. He leapt over the door, locked it and made his way up the walk-way to his front door. Rule One - Never let your guard down. Click. Clunk. Clank. Shunk. The four best pad locks money can buy. His front door swung open and he stepped inside. Rule Two - Talk to yourself whenever possible, especially when others are watching. It'll keep them guessing at your sanity- whether you have it or not. People don't want to screw with a nutcase. "Home sweet Summer home... Complete with telescope conveniently aimed towards the beach." Peeking into the first door on his left, a small office, he found his only Lowell.com employee, Darren, frantically typing at his computer. No doubt doing site work. He smiled and entered the room. "Dar-DEVIL!" He screamed in Darren's ear, causing him to slam both hands down onto the keys. Illegal Operation. Reboot In Process. The computer shut off. Darren's jaw dropped, tears built up in his eyes... "I-I h-have been w-working on that f-f-for three w-weeks." Lowell was silent for a minute, then banged his fist down on the monitor. It turned back on. "Look, I fixed it." He smiled proudly, then dropped an Alf key-chain down into Darren's lap. Rule Three - Keep your friends close, and your lackies closer. If possible, an actual leash might do fine. However, Darren, with the tiny shards of self respect he still had left, refused to wear it outside the house. Lowell has been holding a secret grudge ever since. "And there's a lil' somethin' somethin' for you, my friend." Keeping Darren happy, that's the KEY(chain) to a good working relationship. As the Czar of Cashflow left to do other 'important' things, Darren held the small furry creature up. Then sighed and flung it out the open window. Rule Four - Well... You'll just have to watch fWo Uprising to find out. Text-Life, SUCKAZ.
