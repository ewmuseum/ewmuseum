THE DARK SIDE OF THE MOON Part 5 - Money "Money, get back I'm alright, Jack, keep your hands off my stack. Money, it's a hit don't give me that do goody good bullshit. I'm in the hi-fidelity first class travelling set and I think I need a Lear jet." "How did we get here? How the hell...." Poison Ivy's words trail off as she passes the Netherlander Theater. She's been walking for almost an hour, trying to piece together the exact moment that her family began to fall apart. She smiles briefly at a group of people that she recognizes from the diner - but that lifts her spirit for merely that moment. Her family has been shattered by death, and it's only getting worse. Ivy and Brian used to be close. When Alicia first met Eli, they hit it off like nobody else in the world, and slowly began to spend inordinate amounts of time together. Brian and Ivy, the remaining McGinnis children, became close almost out of necessity - everyone in the neighborhood was Eli's friend, hence Alicia's friend, hence not very prone to becoming friends with the little brother and little sister. Not at first, anyway. Life went on, Brian and Kim got married and had the twins. Ivy started college. Alicia and Eli got married. They had their own lives, but in the midst of the chaos that was the Flair family, and the Eliminators on the street, the McGinnis house was always the warm, calm center of the universe. Everyone knew, even those in opposing gangs, that the McGinnis house and 'Center Field' were open to anyone who would leave rivalries and hatreds outside. Alicia died. The family pulled together to keep Eli going and each other going. Poppa McGinnis died. The family, Eli included, held fast to each other. Ivy seemed to feel this loss more than the others, and they supported her how she needed to be supported. Now Momma McGinnis is on the brink, and the remaining members of the family seem to be at each other's throats. Ivy wipes her eyes - there's tears welling up in the corners. When did she and Brian drift so far apart? Her thoughts are suddenly broken by the sound of her phone ringing - not the incoming call ring, but the voicemail ring. She dials her password and listens.... immediately hanging up and redialing.... and waiting.... "Hey, it's Ivy. I just got your message, you're in the city right now?" "...." "Great! Listen, can we meet for coffee or something? I really need to talk to someone who would understand." "...." ".... Yeah. Fifth Avenue? Perfect. I'll walk it - that should give you time to finish up there. Right.... right. Bye." Ivy puts the phone away and begins to walk again - this time with a purpose. The sun is suddenly obscured by clouds. A wind begins to blow. Ivy doesn't notice.
