Monday (Part 2 of 2)

6 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday, February 4, 2002 12:18 PM Eastern "Well, this is the end of the line. I don't think they'll let me any farther - you know how it is with the security now," Scott Wasner said. The fountain continued its dance behind them. They were in Tampa International Airport, as Stephanie was preparing to head back to Seattle. She had a small carry-on bag over her shoulder and was sitting on the edge of the fountain display. A small band-aid was on her cheek. Wasner was sitting next to her. "Yeah, I know. It's a hassle, but what can you do." They sat in silence for a few moments. "Listen, before I go, I want to talk to you about something." 'Sure, go ahead." "This wrestling thing... I'm not sure about it. You're dealing with psychos like Creed all the time, for one. For two, I'm not blind - I noticed the way some of the other guys were acting. You've got women, booze, drugs... all basically right in your face. I know you've had problems before, and that kind of temptation..." "What, you don't trust me?" "No, it's not that... well, I don't know. I mean, everybody's only human. You make mistakes. Everybody does. I'm not saying if you did any of that I'd be OK with it, but you gotta be honest, it's not like it's impossible. Look at it from my perspective." "Yeah. I guess you're right." She smiled. "Of course I am." Then her face became serious once again. "But that's not everything. This being on the road all the time... I'm sure you're used to it, but me... I don't know. I don't know if anybody can maintain any kind of a real relationship like this. This is... well, I don't know what it is. But it's not good." Wasner bowed his head in thought. "Yeah. I know. But..." "But what? But what, Scott?" She was beginning to become angry. "Dammit, I don't think you understood me. Or maybe you did and I didn't put it right. What I'm saying is I can't keep a relationship like this. This is not gonna work, Scott. Yeah, back in September we needed each other. Both of us did. But if this is what it's all going to amount to, I don't know if we do anymore. Do you have any idea how I feel every night, by myself in your huge house while you're out there, God knows where doing God knows what?!" "Yeah!" Wasner practically shouted. He took a second to calm down. "Yeah, as a matter of fact I do. Yeah, I've been on the road, like you said, but that doesn't mean I'm used to it. Every single night I'm lying in some hotel bed in the midddle of Shithouse Falls, Arizona, trying to stop thinking of you for just a second so I can get a little fucking sleep. Every single day I'm just trying to get through. Every single show, I'm spending my time backstage with a bunch of people I barely know and can't relate to at all instead of being with you. Yeah, I think I know how you feel. This is killing me. Killing me! But I have to believe it'll work out. I have to keep the faith." He took a deep breath and spoke very plainly. "Can you?" She sat silent for a moment. "Yeah. Yeah, I can." "OK. That's all we need." They shared a kiss. "I better get going. I don't want to miss my flight." "Alright. We're swinging by the Northwest, starting in a couple weeks at Body Count... I'll see you again then. And I'll call whenever I can." "OK." "I love you." "I love you." She left. ----- 2:35 PM Eastern "Scott, I scheduled our meeting to start five minutes ago." "You know me, Chris - always fashionably late." "Right." Chris Universal's face showed that he was in no mood for jokes. "Have a seat." They were in Universal's hotel room. Universal was sitting on a chair of his own, and Wasner pulled one out to face the owner of the fWo. "Chris, listen - " "Shut up, Scott." He did. Universal continued. "You had a legitimate, shoot fight with Vic Creed backstage last night. True or false." "True." "OK. I'm gonna make this short and to the point, because I have things to do. That is not in any way, shape or form acceptable. You've been around here for a long time, Scott. You're a huge name. We have a lot of young workers on this roster, and I'll be quite frank - you're a big man in the locker room. They respect you - some of them even look up to you, as hard as that is to believe right now. You're supposed to be setting an example, not pulling shit like that." "But Vic - " "Shut up. I don't particularly give a damn what caused it. That's between you, Vic, and Stephanie. Maybe it was justified, maybe it's wasn't, again, I don't give a damn. What I give a damn about is what caused it. I can't have people in my company doing anything like that, especially someone with the name you have and the push you're getting. Don't forget, Scott - that you're even here right now is a big favor. You fucked up once, Scott, but I let you back in. Consider this strike two. You're familiar with baseball, Scott. You know what comes next." "Yes, sir." "Good answer. Now, your weekly paycheck for Sunday's show is being put towards paying the fine I'm imposing on you - $25,000. That won't cover it all, of course - there'll be a little less than $1000 taken from your next paycheck as well to cover it." "Yes, sir." "Now get out." Wasner nodded and walked from the room, shutting the door behind him. In the hallway, he put his arm against the wall and let his head drop onto it. He took a deep breath, then walked down the hallway and out of sight.
