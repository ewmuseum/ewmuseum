the end justifies the means, Part Ten

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November 22, 2001: "Jon, glad I could reach you..." Chris Universal was on the phone. The two had conversed a few times in the past few months. Jon had used the time to vacation with his soon-to-be wife, Jennifer. This was not on television, of course. Television had been showing the drunken stupor of Jon Crisp, the downfall of his career. This was not the case. He had been getting in the best shape of his life, and gearing up for the biggest run of his career. "Hi Chris, what's going on?" "A big program...that's what." "Oh? Lay it on me." And then Universal told Crisp what he wanted to hear. Fuck The fWo. He would get to be a part of it. Austin Davis was coming back, for a longer run. He was being paired with The Boston Strangler, for a heel run geared at playing off the hatred that Davis and Universal have for each other. Universal and Davis both wanted a few rookies for the stable, and Universal had suggested Monte Burns and a returning Jon Crisp. Davis agreed, and now Crisp was getting wind of it. "We can build it up quickly, with you running a short angle with a returning Wretch next month." Crisp's ears perked up again. Did he hear Wretch? Former champion and instant ratings Wretch? "Chris, I don't know what to say..." "Be at Fallout. We'll go over things with Chris. Austin will be there too, and you can talk to him." This was too good to be true. A second run of the FTfWo...an angle with Wretch...this was all too good to be true. Too bad it was.
