XXXstasy looked around his dressing room once more hoping he wasn't forgetting anything. Gym Bag? ...check. Car keys? ...check. Clothes, Plane ticket, Hotel key? ...check. Good to go, he thought as he slung his gym bag over his shoulder, shut off the lights and went on his way. Sean got through the arena, past the guards, through the doors, almost inside his car when he heard his name, and the sounds of someone running in his direction. Stanley Grimes. Stevens was very displeased. "Stan, please... Okay? It's been a long night, I just got finished wrestling in the main event, I'm sore, tired, cranky... please leave me alone. I don't want to talk business, I don't want you telling me how prepared I should be for Frenchie... I know he's a bad dude... but, I am, too. I just want to go home for a couple of days, relax... and prepare myself mentally." "Sean..." "Yes..." "You dropped your Driver's Lecense... I was just returning it." Dumbfounded, "Oh... umm... thanks." "And, I know we aren't friends, in this business you don't make a lot of friends, and there's always somebody trying to get something out of you. That's not me, Sean. I'm here to help you... to help you become that World Champion you desire to be, to see your career blossum in the manner in which it already should've. I'm on your side, Sean... I'm one of the good guys, and if you'd just give me the opportunity, I'll gladly prove it." Stevens thought it all over, before making the most serious face he could muster, "You're not gonna give up are you?" "Depends on what 'no' will get me. ...if no'll get me in that car, so we can discuss wrestling, emphatically no... if it's gonna get me punched in the face... I'll scream it from the door... waaaaaaay over there," he pointed in the direction of the entrance about 20ft in the opposite direction. Sean couldn't hold it any longer, he sighed, then laughed. "Come on, Stan... might as well get this over with." "Oh, thank you! Thank you so much!!" "Stan, your gonna have to can the excitement, cool?" "Oh, yes... very cool, sir." "Good, now get in..." Trip pushed the automatic lock button on his key ring, as he and Stanley both simultaneously enter the vehicle. "So, Stan... be honest... it's been awhile, how'd I do this week?" "That depends on what honesty'll get me... if I'm going to get punched in the face, I can get out of this car right no--" "--honesty Stan." "Honestly? You weren't half bad..." They drove off into the night, leaving the chilly air, and North Dakota behind them.
