Blade Job - Chapter 8 - Dan Bayer

9 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dan Bayer picked Mufanga Kagos up like a ragdoll, benchpressing the smaller man over his head without even a grunt of effort. Kagos thrashed in Bayer's grip, wrenching at the big man's fingers while being hoisted over seven feet in the air. "I see how it is." said Kagos conversationally, as he reared back and kneed the man in the mouth will all his might. The mans head snapped sideways like a speedbag at a boxing gym as his knees buckled and he fell to the stained canvas, Kagos landing on him. Kagos held on for the pin as a referee with eyes as big as dinner plates started the count. Bayer kicked out at two, tossing Kagos off him in disgust. "You'll pay now, boy-ah," Bayer said in a growl, and then turned his head and spat a bloody tooth onto the canvas. He got to his feet quickly, surprisingly nimble for such a large man. "I'm not your fucking boy." Kagos roared, and charged across the ring. Mufanga leapt at full speed and hit Bayer with a leg lariat, hitting the big redneck square in the barrel chest with the heel of his barefoot. That knocked the five hundred pounder back a few steps, long enough for Kagos to get to his feet and hit another dropkick, this time sending the fat bastard into the ropes. As the behemoth trundled his way off the ropes, Mufanga leapt up, quick as a cat, and grabbed the man's keg-sized head in a rana. He twisted for the headscissors and- "Your mistake, BOY!" Bayer screamed in a southern drawl as he slammed Mufanga down hard with a powerbomb. Again, the breath whooshed out of Kagos as his ribs compacted into the mat. Mufanga started to get up, and found he couldn't. He looked down at his body in a sort of disgusted, frightened awe. It seemed his body had had a mutiny, and decided this was the time to go on strike. Kagos willed his body to move as Bayer circled his prone body. He fucking commanded it to get up and go, soldier. This wasn't the time for loafing, young man! Up and at 'em! Ten-HUT His body was AWOL, it seemed, because Kagos wasn't moving an inch, and Bayer was climbing the corner, standing on the second rope with his giant ass resting on the turnbuckle. Those ropes will never hold him... Mufanga Kagos thought in a daze as Dan "Grizzly" Bayer jumped, five hundred pounds of bastard hillbilly plummeting down at him. Mercifully, Kagos was knocked unconscious by the blow, and didn't have to hear the crowd booing him as the ref counted to three.
