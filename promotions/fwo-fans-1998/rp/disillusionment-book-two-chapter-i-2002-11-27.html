Disillusionment: Book Two - Chapter I

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When you live your life like I have, you get more accustomed to fighting. When your very survival depends on your ability to defend yourself with your bare hands, as mine has, you tend to get very, very good at it. Anyway, I told you that if you stuck with me, maybe I'd be willing to tell you a little more about the Disciples of Confusion. Well, I'm willing as I'll ever be, so today I'm going to give you a little history lesson. The Disciples were formed in the early 1930s. No one knows the exact dates, because the Disciples have never kept any written records...it makes it much more difficult to incriminate them. Anyway, if you happen to notice that this coincides with another rather large even in history, you're smarter than you look. That's right, at the same time the Disciples were forming in Germany, Hitler was coming to power. It doesn't take a genius to see that the two might somehow be linked, and I assure you, it is not a coincidence. Hitler was the Olaf Heersh of his day. The fascist leader was the very first Disciple Imortalius. Since then, the status of Imoralius, or leader of the Disciples of Confusion, has only changed hands three times. You see, Confusion is like a religion to these guys. History tells us that Hitler killed six million Jewish people because he thought they caused the economic strife in Europe and he wanted to create an Aryan world. History is wrong, though. Yes, Hitler did kill the Jews...just not for those reasons. As I said, Confusion is their religion, and the Disciples wanted the whole world to follow it. Orthodox Jews, along with Catholics, tend to be the most stubbornly attached to their religion, and Hitler decided that it would be easier to kill the Jews than to convert them. In a way, though, the Germans actually won World War II. No, they didn't convert everyone in the world to Confusionism, but they did get their allies into all the most powerful positions in the world. By 1950, the entire world, except China and regions of the Middle East and Africa was under Dicsiplic control. That's how it has remained to this day. Not much has changed, except the leaders. Not all the Imoralius have been German. No, Hitler himself was Austrian, remember? In fact, despite being set in Germany, the Disciplic order has only had one German Imoralius, and that is Heersh. After Hitler died, Stalin took over, and after that, we don't really no what happened for the next twenty years. Those of us who oppose the Disciples are trying to learn more about their history everyday, but, as I said, they don't keep written records, so it's hard to keep track of the fuckers. All we know for sure about them now is that they like to kill and they do it often. I've heard estimates from some of my friends who are now long passed that said the world population would be fifty percent greater if the Disciples had never come in to power. That's, like, three billion people whose lives have been ended or prevented from starting by the Disciples. That's a pretty destructive force if you ask me. What I just said is probably very confusing to you, and it should be. It's gotta be a bitch to find out that everything you've ever been told is a blatant lie. You don't have to believe me. I keep saying it, but it's probably better if you don't believe me, as you will live if you don't. Anyway, back to me. I still run. For some reason, I feel like my time is running out...like I have to do something fast. I wish Burkenstock never would have told me that he thought I was the chosen one. That's a lot of pressure to be under, ya know? To be told that you are being relied upon to save the entire world from the dangerous clutches of the most deadly organization that has ever existed. I feel like I have to do something soon, but how do you take down the guy who's holding all the aces in his hand when all you've got is a pair of threes. Damn. I'm scared. You may think it's funny, a grown man, tough guy like myself admitting his fear, but YOU HAVEN'T SEEN WHAT THEY CAN DO. YOU HAVEN'T FELT THE CLUBS THEY CARRY ACROSS YOUR SKULL. YOU HAVEN'T SEEN YOUR FRIENDS KILLED OR BRAINWASHED. You aren't on an international database that tells over Disciplic officer to shoot you on site! I AM! DAMMIT, SOMEONE HELP ME!
