12 April 2003

6:45 p.m: April 2nd, 2003 Somewhere between London and Greenwood, England. Brandon sat in the little yellow rental car looking through the dirty window at the neon sign. It had been nearly three years since he had ventured to a place like this and he honestly figured he had finished with this business. The urge came out of nowhere though one afternoon when Amy and him lounged around waiting for the roster meeting before Warfare. Conversation had waned and everyone sat around bemoaning the fate of the company and the tough touring schedule they were forced to endure. Sensing the weight of the company's fate leaned heavily on everyone's shoulders Amy had tried to randomly change subjects as she is want to do. Tattoos were the subject of the moment and as various superstars lamented their ideas and failed designs an old fire rose in Brandon's stomach. A few weeks had passed and had Brandon tried to get the idea out of his head. With Amy urging him on though and speaking of her own desires he had finally gave in. Now they sat outside of a random parlor making sure they knew, or rather he knew, what he was getting in to. An idea had been formed but had not been made concrete. Brandon looked from the half-lit sign back to Amy. "So, you're sure about this huh...?" "I dunno," Amy grinned in return to his somewhat nervous expression. "I heard tattoos hurt...I dunno if I could handle that." Glancing at her neck, the place he liked to kiss the most, Brandon cocked an eyebrow to her sarcasm. "I just don't want to do something I'll regret. It took me a long time to get the cross on my back. Almost three years of hunting before I finally sat down and designed one myself..." "Hey...I've got a severe phobia of thin needles; you think your stomach's churning right now? C'mon, do something on a whim for once." She squeezed Brandon's hand, smiling with bright eyes. "It'll be fun! No pain, no pleasure...y'know, all that good jazz. Everything I've had done holds some significance to me, but none took three years to design." "Well it's not like it washes off with soap and water ya'know? We'll have to sit down and take inventory of your artwork soon enough. Half the fun will be in finding out what they represent. The other half of course would be me watching you point out all the ones normally hidden by clothes." With a wink Brandon went on. "But I think I've got an idea. I've wanted to do something to honor my Dad... something along those lines. What about you?" Amy smiled even wider at the chance to divulge the idea. "Lightning, waaaaaaaaay low on my hip... just a little bolt. I've got one side that's basically blank, might as well fill the void with a cute little logo. Kinda like yours... and well, I guess you could say it sort of honors someone who's pretty high up on my books too. So congrats, Brandon Lelan Roche...you've got a permanent place, right by my groin." The blood rushed to Brandon's head. Blushing, he let out a laugh calming himself in the process. "That's perfect! I couldn't think of a place I'd rather be... so let's do this!" Brandon glanced out the window to his right before looking back to Amy. Making sure no one was in the way of his car door he slung it open and motioned for her to do the same. He wanted to use the sudden burst of energy her none-too-subtle comment had stirred in him to make the move. If he didn't he feared he'd back out. Amy stood beside him, staring with eyebrows raised in an amused expression. She took a quick step over and tugged on Brandon's hair, sliding a hand into one of his for a second, "Hey, don't look so scared...they smell fear." A small fit of giggles interrupted her a moment, but the unusually energetic redhead continued. "Oh come on...let's put on our big boy face. I mean, I'm here, think I'd let you make a mistake? And if you need a hand or any extremity to squeeze while you're in there, I'm the woman." She winked. "Now c'mon...I've checked this place over and stuff, there's talent and cleanliness, what more could you ask for?" "Sedation?" Half serious Brandon shot back with the first thing that popped in his head. He wasn't nervous in the least about the needles that was Amy's fear, his hinged more on the right approach to this memorial. As they approached the door he spied a few nice pieces of flash, the Celtic designs caught his eye of course. An idea began to form. "You can be sedated when we get back, I told Ivy to get plenty of alcohol in case I came out of this with my back covered in ink. The appointments are already made, so we just basically walk in and say what we want. Guy in here's not one for talking and...well...tattoo guys scare me more than the prospect of another tattoo seems to scare you, so it's good that way. And don't be weird, you're so embarrassing sometimes..." She nudged him, hoping to point out the obvious bad joke before Brandon could take offense. "Okay, what about this then..." Brandon's question continued as he pushed the latch on the squeaky door and stepped in. The warm air caught him in the face and he tilted his head back unexpectedly. "... how about my Dad's name in Old English somewhere? Or something along those lines?" "Old English? You're Irish. Think Gaelic...Celtic! That'd be it..." "Well I was just thinking like script or something. Hey... hmmm..." You could almost smell the smoke coming from Brandon's ears as a thought began to form. "What about Ogham writing. The line script used on funeral pires and whatnot...?" The other patrons of the parlor eyed the two inquisitively as if they knew who they were but couldn't believe it. Aimz smiled, "That's different. Do it...and without being all hesitant and thoughtful and Brandon-ish." "You first. I'll grab a piece of paper from the dude and work out some ideas... between watching you that is..." A large, overly tattooed man looked to Amy, then a chair at the back of the small room they'd been lead to. After a few minutes, Brandon was furiously doodling on his own paper, pointing another man nervously to different 'prototypes' in between glancing at his girlfriend, who had pants lowered and a gun already at her hip, her head tilted back and eyes shut tightly. She really did hate needles. Seeing that her nervousness wasn't another shot at toying with him Brandon reached his hand out to her.
