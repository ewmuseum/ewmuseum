New Year's Revelations: Two

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, life was doing cartwheels around her head again.... Brandon Roche was the first person to kiss Amy Campbell in 2003, and she'd kissed him back on instinct...only to be drawn far away, as if God had saved both from the awkward moments destined to ensue. Both had been lost in a crowd of half-drunk party-goers, and Amy was the first to find her way out - only to sit in the farthest, darkest and emptiest corner of the room at a two-seat table she occupied alone. Alone, without realizing that someone had been watching her the entire time... But it certainly wasn't Brandon. "Be right back," said Poison Ivy as she struggled to her feet. She kissed Sean Stevens on the forehead and headed toward the bar. More toward the wall of the bar, over in the corner. Toward Amy Campbell, sitting alone with a wash of guilt on her face. "What's a nice gal like you doin' over here alone?" asked Ivy, shifting her balance, "Where'd Brand go?" Amy'd tried to pretend she hadn't noticed Ivy, it just wasn't a time she needed someone - especially someone so positive - around her, but it was obvious enough she'd noticed her, so the redhead muttered a reply to the other woman's question without making eye contact. "Probably far away by now." "Uhmmm...no, he was talking to Erik and Pamela when I last saw him," replied Ivy, a hint of confusion in her voice, "But that's not here or there. You're what, twenty? Twenty one? Why are you sitting alone on new year's eve?" Ivy looked back at the table she had come from - populated by Eli Flair, Angel, TJ, Lance Knight, Sean, and several rotating faces. "Why don't you just join us?" "First thing," Amy mumbled again. She kept her tone low, but not shy and far too firm to be meek. "I don't know half of them." Her thoughts wandered back to what had happened at the stroke of midnight, and suddenly the urge to mash her own skull into the bar rose. "And I'm not fit to be around people right now anyway." "FIRST THING," replied Ivy, "is that you work with these people and in this business it can only help to know what they're all about. Second thing is, how many of them do you think I knew a year and some change ago? Want me to count 'em off? Three. Ruben, Eli and Sean." Ivy leaned in, practically forcing eye contact on Amy. "And I don't give a flying fish whether or not you're fit to be around people. I saw what happend between you two and from a girl who's been in similar situations before... the LAST thing you need is to isolate yourself." As Amy geared up for her usual style of a counter statement, she gestured to Ivy's table. "I want to think...and that means alone. I'm sure you're not the only one who saw what happened, and I'm also sure you're not the only one who's going to get after me about it by the time the night's over." She quickly came to question Ivy's motives, though. Obviously the older woman had no ill intents, but it seemed this was all part of Amy's stone-faced act to not allow anyone to care so she wouldn't get hurt - the very same act that came crashing down with the ball in Times Square that night. "Similar situations or not, why are you even concerned about it? It's good to isolate yourself sometimes, you know." The "Psycho Bitch" demeanor quickly faded away from Ivy's face. "Look," she said, "Regardless of what went down here tonight, or what might go down in the future, it's obvious Brand cares about you. Every time we talked while I was rehabbing, it was so flippin' obvious. And..." She stopped for a second, pretending she had something in her eye. "You might or might not be aware, Sean and I have had some rough patches since the whole thing started... and there was nobody at the time I could talk to about it. And I just..." " ...I just thought how unfair it would be to do the same thing to someone else... just because we don't know each other." Did this woman just imply Amy and Brandon would be together? Campbell tried not to consider the possibility, though she'd been secretly toying with the thought for a while. "I appreciate the thought," She tried to manage a weak smile - or at least a pleasant half-smirk - for Ivy's sake. "But there's no way in hell he and I will ever be together. For starters, he's got a wife...remember?" "Nobody said you two would be together," reasoned Ivy, suddenly understanding where this conversation had come from and where it was going, "and it only takes a halfway decent human being to say 'Hey - cut the crap and enjoy the party'." She gestured to the table again. "Let me buy you a drink. If you don't want to talk about it, those nutballs'll help take your mind off things. What have you got to lose other than an ulcer at twenty- two?" 'One night out of my head won't send me to alcoholism,' Amy reasoned herself, that time. When she spoke to Ivy again, it was with a slight smile. "You know what? Okay...I guess it couldn't hurt anything, but here's hoping they know what not to talk about or someone'll leave with a broken nose." "GOOD," remarked Ivy as she turned toward the bar. "Coke, no ice for the minor here." She turned back toward Amy and Amy's somewhat surprised look. "Eli and I are something like family... I know what'cha drink." "They didn't ID me before...but thanks for saving me." She chuckled a bit, "I was about to give in...and when there's one drink in me, I don't think a train could stop me from taking another...and another until I find myself waking up under a table with one hell of an embarassing story - and that's the last side I want to show with people like Eli around." "Don't get nervous," replied Ivy, laughing, "if you can't have fun in this business then it's murder. And if you can't relax with THESE people...." "...then I should join a roving troupe of clowns?" "Exactly!" She laughed again and gestured for the redhead to stand. When Ivy continued, she smiled knowingly at the lithe young woman's obviously forced grin. "C'mon, kiddo - let's have some fun."
