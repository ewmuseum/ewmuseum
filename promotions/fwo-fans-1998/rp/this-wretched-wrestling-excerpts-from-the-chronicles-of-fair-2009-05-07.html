This Wretched Wrestling... (...excerpts from The Chronicles of Fairview Reed)

7 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Still is my back extremely uncomfortable from the various abuses I have sustained via an unforgiving steel chair recently, and again just a very few short days later as some burly plebian discharged his misplaced rage and inherent inferiority upon my unoffending self! I simply do not understand the morals or motivations of this motley crew of rogues and rapscallions with which I am forced to interact! And so my lower lumbar region is both sore and tender but Burlington Werner has proclaimed me fit and hearty (AGAIN!) as I had no doubt he would… I have this unshakable, spontaneous insight that Mrs. McPhineas’ shall interrupt me from this session with a heartfelt yet unnecessary query as to whether or not I feel fit to contribute yet AGAIN to this ever increasing SHAM of an existence!!! I do know that Mrs. McPhineas indeed has proven herself time and again as to our course of action, but this ‘wrestling’ organization is proving itself to be more a weekly nuisance than any progress to our ULTIMATE goal – the discovery and exploitation of The 9 Books!!! I know that Mrs. McPhineas has our best interests at heart, and I do concede that My Assistant has allowed me to count my Spartan appearances in the canvas arena as part of my weekly physical calisthenics, no matter that my only objective is to succumb as quickly and believably as possible so that I may continue my research! I still fail to wholeheartedly embrace this ‘Sports Entertainment’ excuse for our asylum in these United States, and feel almost…despoiled, besmirched… by my actions within the wiry ropes, for ultimately my goal has always been to seek and search and secure a way back into that other realm… And although I may appear as to be oblivious to my surroundings I do retain a modicum of peripheral information ingestion. I know that Mrs. McPhineas and My Assistant are keeping some sort of …dare I say Important…information from me and I feel in my soul that it has to do with our quest. I KNOW that they both care for me deeply as do I for them, but I am also feeling that something momentous is nigh – that both all of our lives as well as the future of this fighting organization into which we’ve cast our lot shall be tested and changed dramatically. I see …pain, incredible intense pain. I see a player revealed, and another tested and respected. I see chaos and pandemonium and irrationality and hate and unrest. … I see a journey – a trek for us all… Another journey. Always I knew that our current haven was temporary at best, but in retrospect I am surprised how quickly settled into routine and a state of ease and comfort… So engrossed in discovering the secret way into the Realm have I been that I have been admittedly lax in both guardianship and awareness…and so SOON after our narrow encounters at home!!! Surely I have not forgotten the mithril blades of The Hunting Party, and yet I have let myself be LULLED into a sense of security. I blame myself entirely – my dear companions attend their individual duties with the utmost perfection and execution – to the point that I believe I have allowed myself to go ‘soft’ – I believe that is the terminology. I do not feel prepared. I do not feel ready. I miss the armor, both physical and arcane, of Hurtheart Manor, to say nothing of the boundless knowledge there left behind. ‘Manor Mercer Island’ (as we’ve jokingly dubbed it!) has proven a worthy stronghold, and it rends my resolve painfully to know that we shall be on the roadways once again…and once again we shall be fleeing for our freedom and mere steps ahead of ‘so-called’ JUSTICE… And THERE is Mrs. McPhineas as I knew she would be. - Oh wretched day! Mrs. McPhineas has informed me that my very dear and good friend Tony Davis’ very career is hanging upon the razor’s edge! Oh that I could assist him in any way that I could! Mr. Davis is the very epitome of the ideals I had hoped for this wrestling nonsense and yes the only person I have yet encountered that could comprehend (let alone appreciate) our story entire… Oh Wretched Misery! Oh Cursed Fate!!! … Mrs. McPhineas also states that I shall appear for ‘exploitation and participation’ once again this week… Again – I see no gain or advantage in this participation, but Mrs. McPhineas assures me that our collective menagerie is ‘right where we need to be’… If coming from anyone else on this dubious plane I would instantly dismiss it as mere reassuring drivel; from the mouth of Mrs. McPhineas I acknowledge it as gospel. My close, secretive, beloved circle of associates are right where we need to be, and I shall endeavor to be gladdened and guided by THAT knowledge...
