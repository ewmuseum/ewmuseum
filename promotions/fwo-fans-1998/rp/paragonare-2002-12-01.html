Paragonare

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday November 22nd, 2002 8:23AM Hotel in San Diego, CA Another dizzying sequence of events was about to pounce right back into Amy Campbell's lap... The morning had a slow start, it wasn't even 8am when she'd stumbled out of bed with her usual 'grace' and found a note from Darcy, goading her to meet him in the hotel's dining room for an early breakfast. Though she wasn't feeling like dressing and going all the way downstairs, or even interested in talking that early on in the day, she had no intention of leaving her boyfriend hanging, if not just to avoid another round of nagging. This was her start to the day... This was Brand Frontier's... Brandon Roche really didn't like confrontation. A professional wrestler not liking an act of aggression, as much of an oxymoron as that sounds it was true. Without seeking them out he had lived through so many trials and tribulations that when it was time to seek out answers it was with more than a smidgen of dread. He knew that the questions he had were really none of his business. He knew that he looked to endanger the budding relationship with what he was about to do, but after reading what that hack had written he needed to know if what was there in black and white was true. He had stood there on the mat for five solid, long minutes. His hand poised to rap against the weathered and repainted door. With a turn of the handle though it was a moot point as the object of his questions flung open the door and once again ran right into him. He'd intercepted Amy's trek, which had promised food. That was mistake number one, in her eyes...getting between an underweight girl and breakfast. She hadn't really said much to him for almost a week, though, so Amy made her best early-morning attempt at looking perky and pleased to see the Mighty Hero. "Wha----whoa....uhh...hey?" "...You know, we really have to stop running in to each other like this. People will start to talk." Brandon had smiled when he said it, but found Amy in no mood for a joke. "Heh..." She huffed a bit of a chuckle, but her face didn't indicate even a trace of amusement. "Yeah...crazy, isn't it? Not like they'll wonder why you're showing up on my doormat at all hours of the day or anything. Really, there's this thing called tact..." "Ouch. Retract the claws there kitten." The smirk on his face doesn't go a long way in easing the early morning tension in the air. "I just figured I'd try to catch you early on. I can't say that I'm not pissed at what's going on but I can deal. So..." Amy was known to be spontaneous, but her mood just wasn't going to drastically budge. She raised her eyebrows and looked at Brandon expectantly with folded arms. "So...?" "So... I... that is... I read something a few days ago. Something you left in the car last week after the show..." The slight flush of color, mainly from agitation, quickly faded from her face. "...What would I have left in your car?" "It was a computer printout. A bit smudged but, I read it. I saw it was from a wrestling site." If Brandon wasn't nervous before, he was now. Why was he even talking to her about this? Curiosity was one thing but this was actually stupidity. For some reason though, he continued. "It was an article, from that reporter that said we were an item. She wrote some stuff about you... I don't know... I just wanted to talk about it." "Yeah? Well I don't. It's bullshit." "It's bull that she wrote those lies about you or bull that she showed everyone the truth?" Good God Man. What did you just say? Wincing even as the words slipped through his dry lips Brandon realized he had just slid head first in to a reaming of royal proportions. "It's as truthful as if I were to say I gave a flying fuck for your opinion, or if I said your wrestling career might have actually meant something. Don't you have a wife or something? Concern yourself with what people say about her; don't even waste your time bothering with my story. According to Sandra Peake and anybody who bought her lie, which means you, I'm the epitome of lost causes." Brandon filed the bad timed comment of Frontier being the Champion of Lost Causes away for another day. This would not be a time for such banter. He had surely over stepped his boundaries, and if there was a truly relationship of some sorts forming then he had certainly strained it. He looked around the hotel balcony as his stinging pride took it's beating. "Hey, let's take a deep breath... look, why don't we step back inside so we don't end up in the headlines again, okay?" "Fine." It was time to continue her stand-offish, defensive act. Amy was short and concise with her words, hoping Brandon would notice her hurry and maybe abandon the thought of moving back inside. "And what, exactly, do you aim to discuss?" "Nothing. Everything. I don't know, I just thought we could talk about what was in the article. I didn't mean to say... look, I didn't mean that I believed any of it. Let's just take a second to talk." With that Brandon motioned past her into her two-night residence. He nodded in the same direction with his head in the hopes of corralling her, for lack of a better word, out of the public eye. Amy reluctantly followed, most likely realizing that even if she wanted to control every situation, she was in no position to drag the Paragon back out into the hallway. "Fine." Brand picked up the chair in front of the desk and twirled it around. As he straddled it he looked over at his... friend. He had certainly botched this up, he sought only to know her better but as usual he had to try and fix everything. "Look, okay... I just read that thing and some of the things it said in there was a bit startling. I know you don't know me and I don't know you but I thought we were going to try this friend thing out. I had questions and instead of sitting on them I believe in asking them..." "Then get around to asking them instead of delaying my breakfast. I already told you none of that shit was true." She cast Brandon a fierce look that appeared to dare him to defy her. "Besides...what should you care what people say about me? You're 'Brand Frontier'...you've got better things to deal with, like saving little kids or balancing your bankbook..." "I care about my friends. I thought that's what we are, were... whatever. She said you had a little kid; it just kind of surprised me is all. I didn't come here to save the world and I don't care what you decide to do to yourself as far as her other accusations. I mean... I do care, but you make your own choices." Brandon began to double back on himself. He knew she wouldn't be the warmest person to talk to about this but now he doubted himself more than ever over this decision. He stood now and came closer to her. "Look, I figured this was bothering you. You were carrying around a crumpled up copy of her dirt in your pocket. I just thought..." The interruption was swift. "I didn't have a 'little kid', my FORMER best friend did. What I have is a promise I'm not about to break." The look in her eye and shakiness in her hands made it obvious enough that Amy was trying to cover some other emotion with her usual shield of anger. Feigned rage had become like a safety net, and it usually managed to make people back away...but not the man they call Brand Frontier, and that had the little redhead feeling as if she was backed into a corner. Now she actually had to think it through and explain, instead of running away from the topic like she'd done with everyone else. "It's my job to care for Stephan...err..." She didn't like showing any degree of emotional involvement. Brandon didn't need to know her weaknesses, in Aimz' opinion...nobody she might end up having to defend herself against did. "...The kid, but I already know I don't do a good job of it, so don't even bother trying to get me there." "Well... the kid... deserves nothing but the best. Whether it's yours by blood or not if you made a promise over it then you need to honor it." Brandon let a bit of his fatherly side slide into him at that point. Not meaning to sound condescending but doing so anyway. "I know it's hard. Sean came along when neither Ana nor I was ready. I screwed up a lot. Mistakes can be made it's just trying to live past them that matters. You made a promise to your friend, that's a good..." His head cocks to the side a bit as if something finally caught up to him. "You said 'former' friend..." "Car accidents aren't pleasant things, especially when someone's carrying a child. I'm keeping a promise to the dead." She snapped back at him and turned away, a fire in her eyes as bright as her hair. "Amy..." Brandon had noticed the path of her feet, she'd seemed to finally have given in and decided on a strategic retreat. It was then that the Paragon made a more daring move, reaching out and placing a hand on her shoulder that plead with the redhead to stay a moment more. But he wasn't dealing with anybody rational, or even somewhat resembling level-headed and, for his efforts, the Mighty Hero got forcefully pushed back a few paces when Amy turned around, putting every ounce of her weight behind the shove. She figured he'd have taken a hint, or at least sought to end things on a note she was more accustomed to, using fists...but no, Brandon appeared to stay entirely calm... He had stepped back twice already, and she was a fiery spirit, her training was evident as she moved the two hundred and sixty odd pound man. Another push was coming; he could see it in her eyes. He had poured salt on a gaping wound and like a trapped and scared animal she was intent on earning her freedom. Brandon put his hands up in defense as she lunged again. This time though he was prepared and managed to snare a wrist. In keeping his balance he used her weight as a counter and pulled her close even as he righted his footing. Now mere inches apart Brandon looked down in to Amy's eyes. They were shockingly quiet as they looked into each other. Amy stared right back...her breathing had steadied and the flicker of anger in her eye had melted away, it seemed. Without a word, she swallowed hard and made a first attempt to pull away, however weakly, as if she didn't entirely want to at that point, otherwise she'd have used the same energy she'd exacted on Brandon only seconds before. For another few moments, she remained placid before finally harnessing a small burst of energy, snatching her arm out of the grasp of the Mighty Hero and back against her chest protectively. Still silent, Amy's eyes lingered on Brandon momentarily, just before she turned away from the Paragon. Her mind was awry, it seemed, with thoughts, the faded spark of anger and every other emotion that was stirred and swirling around within the redhead who always tried not to show her methodical thought. But her absent-mindedness had blazed through when her foot caught on the backpack she'd had laying on the floor near the bay window. All the laws of physics kicked in then, along with the domino effect as she shot her arms out, ready to topple any instant. What those laws of physics hadn't taken into account, however, was the presence of Brand Frontier, there to lunge forward and catch Amy by the waist before she could tumble. Brandon stifled back a smile in an attempt to keep the conversation on the level it deserved. He had heard the rumors of Amy Campbell's out of ring grace but this was the first time he had experienced it first hand. Laughing now would not be a good thing, especially holding the temperamental girl in such a close proximity to himself. Instead he began to put her to her feet when a flash of light caught him in the corner of his eye. Amy too was caught by this sudden burst of illumination and she looked back, up over her head. Instead of setting herself back up she held her position as the realization of what just happened occurred to both of them. It had been the same thing they'd seen night after night for years: not anything natural, or even industrial lighting...it was the familiar blink of a camera. Coming from where, they couldn't be sure...both had been distracted enough not to label an exact spot, but both were studying the row of buildings beyond the windowpane. It suddenly occurred to the two how last week's pictures of them came to be, and possibly who the 'photographer' across the street, as the case may be, was. The knowledge came as a relief, but at the same time a curse...both knew full-well that even if the person taking the pictures was still watching, which they undoubtedly were, the snake wouldn't hesitate a moment to plaster images of the odd situation all over the press, regardless of Aimz and Brandon discovering the camera. Amy couldn't help but smirk a little, though, still glancing out the window from the corner of her eye. "Hey, Brandon?" "Hmm?" The Paragon was still distracted by staring out through the glass, not quite as amused by the discovery as Amy had been. "You can finish picking me up now."
