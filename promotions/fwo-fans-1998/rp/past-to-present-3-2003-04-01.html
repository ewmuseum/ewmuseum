1 April 2003

The month was now July as Tim was still staying his friend Skylar Chryst, till he could find a place of his own. It was mid afternoon as the birds were singing so gaily, Skylar was off at work as Tim was on couch searching through the classifieds for a job. Tim ran his finger through the many adds that completed the section of the paper, when the phone rang. Tim set his paper down on the coffee table as he leaned over to pick up the phone. While reaching over to pick the phone up he accidentally knocked it over, pushing the phone off its table that it was on the phone slammed onto the floor with force. "Shit." Tim got himself out of the couch and walked over to where the phone was laying on the ground ringing. Tim approached the phone and kneeled down to pick up the phone, he placed his left hand on the phone and then something caught his eye. It was a piece of paper that Tim noticed, it had a street address, and then it said underneath it "Orlando Development League." Tim picked up the piece of paper and decided to go check things out. Tim grabbed his jacket and walked out the front door forgetting to answer and pick up the phone. The answer machine picked up. "Hello you've reached Skylar Chryst I am currently not home right now so please leave me your name and your message and I'll get back to you as soon as possible thanks!" The answer machine then proceeded onto beeping 3 times in a row. "Tim? Tim you there? Tim pick up the phone its me Skylar...well you must be busy or gone, just wanted to check in with ya see how the job hunting was going. Well I guess I'll talk to you later then." Skylar hung up the phone as Tim was already out the door. Tim waited outside for the local bus to stop. After 20 minutes of waiting a bus finally came, Tim got onto the bus and asked the driver if the bus made a stop to where he wanted to go. The bus driver nodded his head yes, Tim paid the driver his money and grabbed a seat towards the back of the bus. The busy was pretty empty, near the middle of the bus there were 2 kids and a mother sitting directly across from them, in the front there was an old lady and near the back there was a black guy listening to some music with his headphones. Tim noticed the two kids right away as they were making a lot of noise. "You always get to be him, I want to be him this time." The kids argued back and forth before the mother got involved in the argument. "Shhhh sit down and be quite there's other people on the bus and they don't want to listen to you two hoodlums fight over some nonsense." The kids sat back down in their sits but before they stayed quiet they had to get the last shot in. "You maybe able to silence me, but you will never be able to silence THE BOSTON STRANGLER!" The kid held his fist mighty high in the air before being punched in the shoulder by the other kid. "You're not the Boston Strangler, I am the Boston Strangler, and you're Scott Slugger." "I don't want to be Scott Slugger, I was Scott Slugger last time, it's your turn to be Scott Slugger." "No way am I being Scott Slugger, uh huh." "Fine I'll be Erik Kelly I'll just kick your ass." The mother immediately turned his head towards her son who just said ass and slapped him across the face. "I don't want to hear you say that word EVER again! You understand me? If I catch you saying that word one more time I will wash your mouth out with soap so fast you won't care who the hell is Scott Strangler or Boston Slugger or whatever there names are! You hear me?" The kid let out some tears before sliding down in his seat. "But Erik Kelly says it." "I DON'T CARE WHO SAYS IT ITS NOT APPRIOATE!"
