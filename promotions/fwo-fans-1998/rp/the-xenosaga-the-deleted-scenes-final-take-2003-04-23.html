The Xenosaga: The Deleted Scenes: Final Take

23 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE FOLLOWING ARE DELETED SCENES FROM THE XENOSAGA SERIES. THE REASON WHY THEY'RE DELETED IS BECAUSE MEGA JOB IS INVOLVED AND GOD FORBID THAT MEGA JOB BE INVOLVED IN THE REAL THING. ALSO, IT FEATURES A MUSICAL WITH CARSON COVINGTON, POISON IVY, AND ELI FLAIR. THAT, ALONE, IS TORTURE. "I_AM_NEWJACK~! AND_THAT'S_HOW_WE_GANGBANGIN_UP_IN_HERE!" "Ahem," Dingleberry proceeded. "Well alroyt Mistuh Jack, Oy'd loyk to thank you." "BEE YI BEE YI! FUCK THA PO-LICE!" "Ameeyin to that!" Cagle said in response to New Jack's terrible microphone skills. Then they shared a laugh, as Codemaster almost regained conciousness. "Royt...leet's geet 'im to Slaughter~!" New Jack had left, and the take had finished. Proudly, Beef stood up and proudly shout out his next word, "CUT!". There was a loud applause from everyone who happened to be there: Speck(who just now got up), Dingleberry, Cagle, Beef, Janito, Steve... and everyone who bothered to make a cameo in this stupid series. Then, Billy Joel came in with a quiet, piano solo just as the applause died down. Allen Speck heard it and walked toward him, before grabbing a microphone from the nearby stand. And then he started to... ...sing? "Now, the final take is done... And I know it's been a lot of fun. But now, the curtains have to close, So now you can stop taking your NoDoze." A man playing the saxophone, and that man happened to also be Steve, began to come in, as Speck went into the next verse of his song. "I'm sure you've had some excitement, But this series really needs some refinement, `Cause it was written by a team of monkeys! Scene cut to a trio of monkeys at a writing board, making "ooh ooh, ahh ahh!" noises. One has a collar that says "MIKE RENNER" on it, another has a collar that says "ALEX CLARK" on it, and the third one has a collar that says "RICH MURRAY" on it. They're flinging poop at one another. As the scene cut back to Allen Speck, he walked out to his right, passing Beef as he went. Beef smiled and waved, and started singing, himself, in that speak/singing kind of way that they do when they have a break from the music. The last word of everything Beef says has a riff of piano and drums. "Hey! I was walking down to Beefville, On a sunday afternoon, I was reading that Scott Powell article, And I believed he was a loon. I mean, who'd be crazy enough to like Voran Xias? A man whose last name can't be rhymed with anything, At least not in any civilized language? I bet he didn't even get invited to Spring Fling. Know what I'm saying?" Beef suddenly ducked down, and behind him, El Janito, John Rocker, and Ahmed Johnson sang in response. "No, we don't!" Janito and Ahmed Johnson picked up John Rocker and with Rocker's arms outstretched, they flew him away while making airplane noises. As they disappeared from view, we see the man that was behind them, and that man was, of course, Allen Speck, still holding his microphone. "Now your intelligence is fading, Not like you had much in the first place, `Cause you clicked the link to read this, Now your brain is lost in space." Allen Speck suddenly tore off his dirty, chocolate syrup, popcorn, and lego covered robe, exposing a very nice-looking three piece suit complete with a bowtie. How he managed to wear that without any of us knowing was beyond any of us. "Let's hear it for Aaron Dillon, The man who won't ever be happy, He'll sit there and be unhappy, While the rest of us call him "Slappy". Speck tossed the microphone over his shoulder. As he did so, the music became more upbeat. The cameraman followed the microphone as it went, before it was caught by New Jack. "ROCK, NIGGA!" Then New Jack tossed the microphone straight up and smashed it with his guitar(playing it before he struck it), and sending it flying skyward. The camera followed it up, up, and up, as the microphone smashed through the celing and kept going. It didn't come back down just yet, but it was a moot point, as the camera panned down to see Great Dragon. Great Dragon pulled out an electric guitar. Out of thin air. God knows how THAT happened, but then the Dragon, man, he blasted out guitar riffs as if he were Stevie Ray Vaughn. Nobody asked exactly where Dragon learned how to play guitar, they were too busy moshing. The camera panned to the right to see Oddjob. Playing drums. And he was rocking it "hardkorr!!!11" like John Bonham on "When The Levee Breaks", man. Lord knows HOW he did it since, you know, he's a FREAKING STEPLADDER, but a lot of this sequence has been... well... off the wall. After Oddjob's amazing drum solo, the camera pulled back and to the left, not to see John F. Kennedy being shot in the head, but to see James Cagle playing a mean `ol harmonica, and blazing some major league sounds out of it. This continued for just a bit, just under a minute, before Cagle finished, and the camera panned past James Cagle. Davy Dingleberry. With a fiddle. Playing "The Devil Went Down To Georgia" with the assistance of Oddjob on the drums, and Cagle singing the lyrics. "Devil went down to Jawja, he was lookin' fuh a soul to steayil. He was in a bahnd, cuz he was way behand and he was willin' to make a deayil! And ol' Devil jumped up awn a hickory stump and saiyed, "Boy lemme tell ye what! Ah guess you didn't know it, but Ah'm a feedle player too! And if you'd cerre to take a derre, Ah'll make a bet with you! Now ye play a purdy good feedle boy, but give the devil his due. I'll bet a fiddle of gold against yo' soul, 'cause Ah think Ah'm better than ye!" And then the music... stopped. Everyone looked up. The microphone was heading back to Earth. The drums and guitars started to build for a climax. People looked up in anticipation for what was to happen. El Janito. Beef. Adam Nowell, while he was eating an apple and not particularly caring. Hans Krueger. Eli Flair. James Cagle. Billy Joel, while he was slamming his fingers into the piano along with the climax music. Finally, a single hand shot up and caught the microphone. The camera panned down and saw short blond hair, green eyes, and a frown. He wore a "TOUT" shirt, lord knows why he did since nobody knew what the fuck "TOUT" meant. But the point is clear on who this man was. Carson Covington. And now he was singing, as all music stopped, except for Billy Joel on the piano and Great Dragon playing a slow tune on his guitar. "It's not that easy being green, Having to spend each day like the color of the leaves, When I think it could be, Nicer being red, or yellow, or gold, Or something much more colorful than that." Carson Covington slowly walked around Billy Joel's piano. "It's not easy being green, It's just that you blend in with so many ordinary things. And people tend to pass you over, `Cause you're standin' out like flashy sparkles in the water Or stars in the sky." Now came in Beef with the electric triangle! Yes, the electric triangle. He struck the triangle a couple of times as Carson continued his song, unknowing to someone approaching him with mean intentions on his mind. "But green's the color of spring! And green can be cool, and friendly-like! And green can be big, like a-- HORK! And that someone, Hans Krueger, grabbed Carson by his throat, took the microphone, and shoved him away. He turned to Great Dragon(in a moment of pure weirdness for the writer of this, that's for sure) before he spoke. "You there... start playing ze Rammstein song." Krueger said, before Dragon shrugged and started to play to guitar chords for Du Hast. Oddjob joined him on the drums. Beef continued to play the electric triangle, until a big cane poked out from the left, moved past him so that the hook would grab him as it pulled back, then it did so, pulling Beef off to the left. "Du." And that was as far as he got in the lyrics of "Du Hast", because Great Dragon stopped playing, and Billy Joel started to slowly play piano music again. Krueger was utterly confused, until Allen Speck walked next to him and slyly took the microphone from Krueger's hand, and began to sing again. "We're winding down, But maybe that's `cause the show has freezed. We hoped you enjoyed your stay, Maybe you'll stick around for our River of Dreams." Allen tossed the microphone toward Billy Joel, who caught it and set it on a microphone stand on his piano. Joel hesitated, as Adam Nowell slowly brought in the bongos. He sighed, not really wanting to be here, before he started to play the introduction to this writer's favorite Billy Joel song. "In the middle of the night, I've been walking in my sleep, From the mountains of faith, To the river so deep, I must've been lookin' for something, Something sacred I lost, But the river is wide, And it's too hard to cross." Everyone in the room, except the people playing music(Nowell and Joel), and the all-too-serious people(Flair, Ivy, Covington, and Nowell) started to do the Snoopy Dance. Hey, it had to happen at SOME point in this freaking musical, so it might as well have been here. "And even though I know the river is wide, I walk down every evening and I stand on the shore, And try to cross to the opposite side, So I can finally find out what I've been looking for." Anyway, Joel would've continued doing the singing, but Steve leapt up and grabbed the microphone, before he "sang" the next chorus. Which is to say that he only said one word from every line of the song and was horribly offkey with the rest of the song. "NIGHT. SLEEP. FEAR. DEEP. SOMETHING. SOUL. LOSE. STOLE." Somewhere between "FEAR." and "DEEP.", the music stopped, but Steve pressed on until Eli Flair picked up the three foot, nine inch Steve, grabbed the microphone, and dropped him. Then, from there, he handed the microphone over to Allen Speck, who sang the next bit. "I don't know why I go walking at night, But now I'm tired and I don't want to walk anymore, I hope it doesn't take the rest of my life, Until I find what it is that I've been looking for." In the background, Poison Ivy and Eli Flair had become the backup singers, even if it was horribly out-of-character of them, as they sang "In the middle of the night, I go walking in my sleep" together at the correct times. Allen Speck handed the microphone off to Davy Dingleberry, who continued to sing, though his Australian was obvious here. "In the middle of tha noyt, Oy go walkin' in me sleep, Through tha jungle of doubt, To a river so deep, Oy know Oy'm searchin' for somethin', Something so undefoyned, That it can only be seen, By the eyes of the bloynd, In the middle of tha noyt." Davy Dingleberry shrugged his shoulders, and put the microphone back on Billy Joel's microphone stand, before he Snoopy Danced his way over to where everyone else had Snoopy Danced to. Suddenly, everyone stopped. The music stopped. Everything. Stopped. Then, Evil Smokey leapt up on top of the piano, put Billy Joel in his mouth, then screamed out. "RIVERDANCE!" Evil Smokey stood in front of everyone else in the room, as they all started to do the Dance Which Features The Use Of No Hands Whatsoever. * . * Allen Speck woke up in a cold sweat. He had NEVER had a dream THAT fucked up before. I mean, a broadway musical? With BILLY JOEL in it? Oh, come on. Nervously, Allen glanced over and prodded the person that was beside him in bed, "Wake up, wake up... I had this really horrible dream. I... I was the star of a musical... and Billy Joel played pianos, and I sang, and we all did the Riverdance. And... and... Eli Flair and Poison Ivy were there, and..." Suddenly, the woman in bed beside him sleepilly sat up. For some reason, Suzanne Pleshette woke up next to Allen. "Go back to bed, Bob." "Yeah, but, but... the dream, and..." Allen stopped cold, and glared at Suzanne Pleshette for a long, agonizing moment, "Did you just call me 'Bob'?" * . * Allen Speck woke up in a cold sweat. He looked around him for a long, agonizing moment to discover that nobody was around. He nervously sighed before he spoke again. "I hate those kinds of dreams." Allen tried to roll out of bed, only to discover that his lower body was that of a fish's tail. "Aw, damn it." * . * Allen Speck woke up in a cold sweat. Thankfully, this was the real thing, this time. He glanced at the table to find several empty bottles of vodka, before he spoke his next couple of words, "Right. That's the last time I go drinking with Adam Nowell. Ever. Again." Fade. :: THIS HAS BEEN A MIKE RENNER / ALEX CLARK / PETE RUSSO JOINT. POT POWER! ::
