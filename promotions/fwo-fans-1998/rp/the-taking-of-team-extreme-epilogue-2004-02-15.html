The Taking of Team Extreme - Epilogue

15 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Taking of Team Extreme Being What Happened, between Then and Now Epilogue: Now. So why did I do it? Why does anyone do anything? To make a buck? To right a wrong? Because they believe in the cause? I don't know what goes on inside my head, and I don't know what kind of problems my actions have caused, but I know one thing for sure. This is not the way. The current trend of badass wannabe heroes... is not the way. I hope I've shown that so far, I hope I've given the heroes a wake-up call. Complacency breeds unpleasant experience. You're looking at the unpleasant experience. It's not complicated. I have a higher purpose. If I can cause enough damage, it'll make the heroes care. If I can hurt enough people, it'll make the heroes try to stop me. To bring a hero into the world, to cause a hero to fight for the people... that's worth anything. To know that there are people in the world to stand up for my daughter... it's one thing to be her father and protect her, but quite another to say "Look at my life and do as I did." That's worth the cost... just a few mutilated bodies and my own atrophied soul. And to give the world back its heroes, may be enough to grant my redemption. I'm doing this to save you all, but only you can save me. I've come back to this place to destroy, but only you can rebuild. It all comes down to choice. And you can always go over your choices when you look back on them. And I know, with every fiber of my being, if I had to go back and do it all over again, I'd do the exact same thing. What does that make me? Think... before you answer. Because while I'd prefer no innocent bystanders caught in the mix... ...if you stand in my way, you're guilty.
