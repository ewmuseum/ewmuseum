Three: Courtesy

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Kindness is the fundamental nature or quality of treating others with respect and reverence." "Sean?" Brandon knew that his eldest son heard him. Standing at the bottom of the steps he could hear Sean's footfalls on the carpet in his room. No response came though. Brandon wondered if Sean really thought not answering him would make him go away? Slightly amused the man known as Brand Frontier took the steps up two at a time tossing his son's coat on the banister as he went. "Sean Christian, didn't you hear me?" A sterner tone should have elicited some sort of response from the boy, but none came. Brandon finally peered in to his sons room more than a little aggravated at the time it was taking for Sean to get ready. Sean Roche was in his own world though. A gift from Santa Claus this year was placed squarely over the young Roche's ears with the volume too loud to say the least. "Superstar" Sean, as he was known in the old G.W.O. was listening to a little pop music by one of the cookie cutter performers on the scene totally oblivious to his father. He truly kept rhythm like his father, it was sad. His voice, as he was singing along, was worse. Brandon realized that it was a good thing Sean was as big as he was for his age. If he sang like that in public he was surely going to need to be able to protect himself. As the patriarch of the Roche family laughed to his self he completely pushed the door open and his son saw the movement and turned his head, never missing, or rather never hitting a beat. A wide smile arched across Sean's face as he saw his father. The gap in his front teeth indicating that Brandon would one day soon be paying for a mouth full of metal could easily be seen by such a smile. It also served to sooth Brandon's aggravated state even more as he signaled for Sean to remove the headphones. "Hey, Daddy..." Sean still moved to the too loud music from the tiny speakers dangling around his neck. "Why you got your jacket on? I thought Jarod was taking a nap and we was going to play Neverwinter Nights?" "Slight change of plans buckaroo. Chris called from work, they need someone down at the shelter for the evening to help with things." Sean's wide smile immediately turned upside down as the Earth Shattering News was delivered. How could his Dad just up and desert him like that? This was Christmas after all... he should be spending it with his family! "But Dad, you promised... this just... Sucks!" The headphones came from around his neck quick enough and they were tossed to the ground to add emphasis to his agitated state. Brandon didn't like to see his father's temper in Sean like that. He was always quick to respond when Sean or Jarod showed such a selfish side to themselves. "Look Sean, I don't have time for a temper tantrum. You're old enough to understand things; you don't need to act like this all of a sudden. What happened to the smile you had for me when I walked in? If that is that only good for when things are going your way, that doesn't seem fair to me..." Brandon's words were true and straight enough that Sean knew what he meant without having to be preached to. "I've got your coat in the hall..." Brandon stepped to Sean, stroking back his birty blond mane. "I thought that you and I could go together while Mommy and Jarod are asleep. That way we get to spend time with each other and still get to help Chris, Lawrence, Bobby and the rest. Remember what today is all about buddy, Ok? It's cool that you got all of those presents but we need to think about others. It's not just about Christmas Ok? You should do that all the time. I can't make it any more simple." "Ok Daddy... I'm sorry. I just got So many cool things I won't have time to play with them all..." Brandon interrupted knowing what Sean was feeling but also knowing that this was the perfect chance to teach his seven year old about respect and doing the right thing. "Look Sean, think about it this way... all of those people we get to help tonight might not have had a chance to get a lot of things for Christmas. Whether they don't have an address for Santa to find them or if they just decide they don't want the help offered to them it's not a reason for us to look down on them." Sean was looking to the floor at this point. Brandon's words were ringing true. He was a good kid but was just distracted by all the goodies he got. He knew the right thing to do after all. "I know Daddy, and I know I'll have all the time in the world to play with my new stuff. I... I just got excited. Can I still go now? Can I? You're right... I want to go see Unca' Chris and everybody." The smile had returned to Sean's face as he began to think about how great it was to have a Dad like Brand Frontier! The smile was contagious as Brandon also thought about how lucky he was to have such a gift as his two sons. No matter what was happening with Ana there would always be the two little miracles she helped him make. His smile began to hide a bittersweet reflection as he thought about his estranged wife and all of the hardships they had come upon in the last few months. It had been a tough battle to this point in keeping everything together and Christmas had been fun for the kids but was a strain on the two elders of the family as they tried to put on happy faces for their children during such a special event as this most grand of Birthday Celebrations. Brandon was jerked back to reality though as Sean repeated his ignored statement. "I've got my boots on Daddy, I'll get my coat and we can go... Ok?" "Sure thing Sean, let me leave a note for Mommy and I'll be right down." Sean galloped down the stairs as Brandon flinched at the noise. It would take a miracle for that to not have awakened Ana and Jarod. Thankfully as the mighty Paragon looked in on his slumbering family he noticed that the festivities of the day had tired them both out to the point of not noticing. "Daaaaddy... I'm ready. Let's go help out." And with that it was simple. Do the right thing, simply do it and don't worry about the little things. They always take care of themselves. Brandon reached for the keys in his pocket as he quietly came down the steps. Pulling Sean's hood over his head as they went Brandon looked back as the door closed and the lock engaged. ::beep:: ...Brand Fron...Brandon Roche... this is Michael Sweet. I'm the assistant editor for the Wrestling Observer. After your phone call today we attempted to speak to Sandra Peake on some of the concerns you voiced. If you could please call us back as soon as possible it would be greatly appreciated. Thank you..." ::beep:: Four: Decourus
