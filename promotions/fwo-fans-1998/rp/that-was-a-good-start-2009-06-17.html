That Was A Good Start

17 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[6/10/09, after ReAction] Max Danger hadn’t expected to be in the main event tonight, so he was in a serious rush to gather his things, get dressed, and get his butt on an airplane and to Montreal. Leigh Landers was trailing behind him, being her usual happy-happy self. “It looked like you were thinking too much.” There wasn’t a question asked, but she could tell that her charge wanted feedback on the night’s events; specifically his first promo in front of the FWO faithful. Danger didn’t answer, instead gave two slight nods of the head. This indicated to Leigh that she should continue. “You didn’t really say a whole lot while you were out there. It’s like you were too inside you’re own head. No big deal, as maybe you were nervous...” Max gave her one of those looks that told that that was obviously not the reason. ‘Cause, hey, Max Danger has wrestled and spoke and made a full of himself in front of thousands of people before. He wasn’t nervous. “...or not. Either way, it’s all good. Sometimes it’s good to be inside your own head a lot. I do all the time!” No truer words had ever been spoken. It wasn’t uncommon to find Landers standing in the middle of a hallway, staring ahead only slightly askewed, in a whole other world, with absolutely no realization of the going-ons around her. “But it was a good start. Next time you’ll really get the fans on your side and wow ‘em!” The Marvelous One wasn’t as certain as Leigh was, but she’s always been the overly optimistic type. Danger sighed as he hurried through Key Arena, heading for the parking lot and his rental car. “I’m still losing though It was hard for Danger to stomach, to be honest. He came to FWO on this night with a prize in his bag, something that showed he was on top of his game. But that was on the other side of the continent, in another country. A place he was heading to the airport for, in fact. Here, in Seattle, with the Fans Wrestling Organization... things just weren’t coming as easily as they were there. He’s been consistent in the ring, but just not getting the results he desired. “It’ll all come together. Meltdown is going to be a big show for you.” Danger looked at Leigh quizzically. “Um, I’m pretty sure I’m not booked for Meltdown, Leigh.” Now it was the Happy Bunny Girl’s turn to look at Max strangely. “Of course you are silly! You’re in the Meltdown match!” Max stared at her. “You know what I’m talking about, right?” Danger shook his head. “Seriously?” He nodded. “It’s a big tag team battle royale. You’re teaming with Mike Bear.” Max lifted his eye brows (‘cause he couldn’t lift just one). “You’re joking, right? You don’t know who Mike Bear is either?” Again, the Amazing One shook his head. “Sigh.” And Leigh sat down, indian-style, on the floor, bending over with her head and hands in her lap. After several long seconds of silence, Leigh spoke again. “Here is what you need to do. You need to start using that FWO application on your iPhone there and watch all the shows. You have plenty of time on airplanes to do that.” Danger continued walking, having now reached the parking lot and spying his car waiting for him. “I’ll get you a match next week and I’m confident you can pick up your first victory. But do that for me, okay? Catch up on all the shows, understand what’s going on around here. And PLEASE find Mike Bear and talk some strategy, m’kay?” Max unlocked his car and looked back at Leigh. She was smiling at him, ‘cause, well, that’s what she did. He couldn’t help but to smile back at her just a little bit. With a nod, he said, “OK” and jumped into the car. Leigh did a little hop and clapped her hands together giddily.
