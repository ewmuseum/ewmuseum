This time he's gone too far....

30 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Havok is shown in his locker room just minutes after Countdown has gone off the air. A tragedy has just occured. A man's life has just been taken. Loud screams and running feet can be heard outside the door, but Havok stays still. He is sitting with his face buried in his hands, and shaking his head back and forth. He looks up at the camera, and a singles tear runs down his cheek.] An innocent man's life was just taken. Why? He was just trying to spread a little happiness. He was standing up for us, and trying to bring a little love to the fWo. Yet, David Spencer decides to pull a stunt like that, and toss him over the ramp. He died, and it really makes me wonder. Is it worth it? No. David Spencer has gone too far. Ryan Moore, Joy, was a wonderful person, and a good friend of mine. And I, for one, am not going to sit here on my ass, and not do anything about it. As for Joy, Ryan, you'll be greatly missed. Rest in Peace, my friend. [He gets up and walks over to the door. He turns around and stares into the camera, with a cold look on his face.] As for David Spencer, I'll see you in Hell. [He storms out of the room, slamming the door behind him. The camera fades to black.]
