CORPUS DELECTI Part 2 - Echoes "There it is." For a moment, Eli Flair thought he had been ripped off.... but he wasn't. Beer number 23 had simply rolled underneath the bed. Utilizing all of the coordination he can muster, he gets down on his knees and grabs the bottle. A puddle of water, remnants of his sojourn onto the ledge in the pouring rain, has gathered on the floor beneath him. "I can't believe you're so weak. What did I ever waste my time with you for?" That voice.... Eli raises his head slowly to see blonde curls, a self- righteous look, and an evil glare. Emily. The first girl he was truly serious about since Alicia.... who put up with more of his baggage than anyone else to date, who he hurt deeply with his inability via error or omission to truly open up to her.... .... Who hurt him more deeply than anyone else since the person responsible for taking Alicia from him. "Do the girl a favor, Eli.... Don't call her back. Don't go after her. Don't try and win her over, because your love is like the deadliest poison.... it destroys everyone it touches." "Em--" started Eli, unable to finish. "Don't even tell me you can deny it," replied the Emily- voice, "You're an infectious disease." Eli staggered to his feet, about to reply, but nerves, emotions, and beer have overtaken him, and he stumbles backwards, hitting the wall hard. "Eli's love didn't destroy me," said another voice, "It kept me going for a long, LONG time." "Alicia?" asked Eli, confused as his wife's image enters his mind's eye as well. "It's okay, Eli," replied the Alicia- voice, "She's no more able to hurt you than I am." Eli shuts his eyes, but he can't block out the images.... "You can't keep saving him," said the Emily-voice, "Sooner or later he's going to have to learn to deal with what he is and what he does to other people." "I'm not saving him from anything that he deserves," replied the Alicia- voice, "He's suffered enough. He's gone through enough. He and Angel belong together and I think they deserve to be happy." His deceased wife, so to speak, just said that he deserves to be happy, even if it means being with another woman. Eli continues to try to pull himself to his feet. "You're a whiny, weak bitch," said the Emily-voice, "You'd deprive the whole world of its right to have a satisfying life for this loser. He's a thirty year old boy without the capacity to emote on an adult level." "STOP!" screamed Eli, "Get outta my head, both of you!" It works. Kind of. Because, at the moment Eli Flair told the voices to leave his head, they stopped speaking. But twenty- three beers on an empty stomach aren't exactly the best thing for staying lucid. Or awake. Eli Flair is lucky he took two steps forward, because he just pitched headfirst onto the bed, instead of onto the floor.
