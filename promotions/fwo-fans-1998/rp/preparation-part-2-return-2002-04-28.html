Preparation, Part 2 (Return)

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lance Knight found himself walking down the hallway to his locker room, his "History" shoot having just wrapped up and now mere hours before what would be the biggest moment of his still-fledgling career. He still couldn't believe that he was here, in the Arrowhead Pond, ready to compete in his second-ever fWo PPV match, at the biggest event of the fWo's calendar year. It was still somewhat of a surprise to him that he had made it this far already in his lifetime. He knew first-hand from a lot of the guys that he had worked with in the Indies that some wrestlers spent their entire lives chasing the fans Wrestling organization, and that a good many of them would never achieve their goals. It was times like this that he still found himself in awe of the profession that he had chosen to call his own, and it was at moments like this that he realized, more than anything, that... You can't cut it? Shocked, Lance paused in mid-stride, causing a backstage worker with an arm full of papers to nearly collide with him. Muttering, the technician moved around the frozen performer, his half-audible expletives falling on deaf ears. "I do not need this right now," Lance said to himself. Oh, but you do, Lance. If it weren't for me, you'd probably go out there tonight thinking that you had a shot at winning this match. The past few months, since he had been accepted into the fWo, had been a wonderful experience for Lance, in spite of losing his appendix and being poisoned by Grendel. The sensation that he was currently experiencing, that nagging voice in the back of his mind, had left him relatively alone, and his career had more or less flourished. Now, though, he felt as if he were back at square one. Oh, don't act like you didn't miss me, Lance. I keep you grounded, I keep you from thinking too far ahead. The way I look at it, if I hadn't come back tonight, you'd be going out there to get slaughtered. I'm preventing you a serious injury. "Don't patronize me," Lance hissed, drawing a look from the same technician who had nearly slammed into him.
