Coming Of AGe

6 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
August 4th, 2004. Not to be confused with October. It was definitely August. Tired. Exhausted. But absolutely freaked out and frightened, riding on a surge of adrenaline and imagination, Matthew Wakeman brought his bags to the counter. "I'm Matthew Wakeman, I preordered my tickets. Here's my ID." The blonde woman behind the desk for South West Airlines, clickity clacked at her keyboard. She squinted at her screen, and checked Matthew's ID twice. "Mr. Wakeman ..." Groaning, Matt was afraid something like this would happen. All he wanted to do was to get home and tell him mom all about the contest, and Meltdown, and punching Black Quicksilver in the face. "Is something wrong with my ticket?" asked Matt. "Yes. Well, no. Under your name here, you have two departure tickets. One for St. Louis, and one for ..." "... Orlando..." Matthew stared at her blankly for a few seconds, and then said, "Could you hold on a second? I need to call my mom." === "You! You there, are you Wakeman!?" said the man holding a cardboard sign that said WAKEMAN on it. Matthew nodded, as the man advanced on him, "My name is Hamid and you are LATE. I was told to be here a half hour ago, and I am charging you for that half hour! And I will be paid!" Matthew blinked at the aggressive Middle Eastern man, and just nodded, "Okay. I'm sorry I kept you waiting. There was some confusion with the plane and-" The man waved his hand in the air and said, "I do not want to hear it. Let's get in my car. I do not like airports. Everyone stares at me like I'm a criminal. I am a citizen of this country and I have the papers to prove it. But people are frightened of me, when I would be their friend. I have to be friends. I am the best Taxi Driver in this city, and everyone loves me." Matthew followed the man to the Taxi, putting his bags in the trunk. When Hamid settled into the driver's seat, his persona changed violently and he smiled warmly to Matthew, "So you are a big famous wrestler? You don't look so big." "I'm not," Matthew replied. "Which?" "Huh?" "Which are you not? You are not big famous wrestler or you are not so big?" "Both." "Ahhh, I see. You're not a wrestler," Hamid said with a little disappointment. He spun around to look directly at Matthew, "Are you SURE you are Wakeman?" "Yes, I'm sure," Matthew quickly shouted, "I am a wrestler. But I'm not big or famous." Hamid relaxed, turning back around to watch the road, "Hmmph. Maybe you are more famous than you think then? Yes. That must be it. But I wouldn't have guessed you to be a wrestler. You're very young." Matthew nodded, "I'm nineteen years old." "Nineteen, you say? I have a nephew who is nineteen. He's studying at University to become a Physicist. Very smart boy. My brother and I are very proud of him." Matthew stared out the window for a moment, and then slapped his head, "Holy fucking shit, it's my birthday!" Hamid stared at Matt a moment then laughed out loud, he burst into song, "Happy birthday to you, happy birthday to you! Happy birthday, dear Wakeman-the-Famous-Wrestler, happy birthday to you!" Matt found himself laughing along with Hamid, who said, "The singing comes at no extra charge, my friend. Now that you are twenty, and you remembered that you were born today, what are you going to do? Too young to drink still, but that's no reason to stop you from having a good time. Ahh here we are. Milkhouse arena, your fare comes to forty seven dollars, but since it is your birthday, I will knock off the time I spent waiting at the airport off the fare, you will give me fifty dollars, and I will take the rest as tip, sound good?" Fifty dollars poorer, Matthew stood outside the Milkhouse Arena, holding his bags as the Taxi sped away. === Left with not knowing what to do next, Matthew approached the box office. Near the announcement board was a note posted. HAVE YOU FAITH? FAITH IS THE EVIDENCE 08-04-04 ROOM # 555 P.S. Happy Birthday, Matt! After claiming the note from the announcement board, Matthew took a deep breath. It was now or never, right? === One step in front of the other. That's all it took. Descending down the steps into room #555, Matthew took a look around him, gasping in surprise at all the faces he'd never expected to see staring back at him. The gloom was oppressive, but the voice that rang out compelled him forward so that he could stand with the others. "It takes a different kind of faith to be here, gentleman. By now, in some way you should understand. I am not who I pretended to be, nor do I fear those I have fooled." The man sitting lit up a cigarette, which brought a smile to Matthew's face. "The fans Wrestling organization no longer exists as we have known it. Today, the Disney Corporation believes that Silver Fox has sold his rights to their company, but on very good sources, I disagree. And I'll even take it a step further and flat out say that in the long run, Meltdown will be nothing more than 3 hours of our lives wasted away watching failed artists, pointless history and disgruntled hacks combining to flop horribly at creating something meaningful for the future of this industry." The man exhaled a halo of smoke as Matthew nervously watched the eyes, staring upon himself. "Mistakes piled up, people tossed aside. You stand right here, right now because of this. I can look in your eyes and see in your soul that what's transpired this year is a crime against OUR traditions and lineage." The man, once again inhaled and exhaled - letting what he has to say, sink in to the eyes of his audience. Matthew felt the words truly possessed weight. "We are not the New School..." Inhale... "We are not the Old School..." Exhale... "No, its deeper than that for us. We are the forgotten. We are the castaways. We're not seen as those fit to do what we want, yet we've been subjected to the fates of having to suffer through other people's failures. I bring us here together, tonight, because I possess a vision that will right this wrong. Remember, a wise man once said, 'It's only after you've lost everything that you're free to do anything.'" Matthew's mouth formed a crooked smile, in recognition. "Nobody likes you..." Inhale... "Nobody wants you..." Exhale... "And most likely, most everyone hates you." Another inhale, this one longer. The words once again soaking in, Matthew could see there wasn't much disagreement in regards to the accusations coldly being stated. "I don't give a fuck. All of them from Disney to the turncoats that leave for OCW can go fuck themselves. They don't know us, they don't respect us and they don't know the consequences of betraying us." Matthew's smile grew, the cigarette was flicked aside - but the fire in this man's eye looked renewed. "In this room, while I see the potential to become something extraordinary... I also see apathy that has caused much recent failure. I see men who are devilishly cunning, and men so strong, they break torsos in half for a living. Yet, you are lost. You chose to let the others decide your fate. OCW, Fox, Green suited rejects, Disney, whoever - fuck them. They've never given us our purpose, a direction nor a path. Help? They don't give fucking help. They tell us to believe that we'll get what we deserve. But I don't see that coming, I've only watched promises go unfulfilled. Fucking Christ, look around this room...we are the unwanted. And, if you don't see this truth- you're dumber than those still waiting for it all to turn around and get better on its own." "Brian Cage...fuck him." "Disney...fuck them." "War is what they all deserve. Tonight, I declare that we stand up and fight..." Matthew's eyes widened as the speaker rose to his feet without a struggle... "...and give them the hell they deserve..." The silence was deafening as each man contemplated the decision required to follow this man, but it was broken... "If you do not wish to join me, you have sixty seconds to turn around and never look back, or may God have mercy on your soul." ... No one moved. === Granite City, MO The final weary steps brought him to the front door of the apartment he shared with his mom. He opened the door. She was asleep on the couch, her head tilted to one side. Chardonnay filled two wine glasses on the table, joined by a small birthday cake. Finally, seeing her after such a long time, Matthew started to cry. The one source of support his life had ever known, he'd missed her so much. Delores Wakeman's bright blue eyes opened, to finally see her son. As Matthew dumped his bags on the floor, she started to got up off the couch, and walked the last few steps to his embrace. She'd remembered him being shorter for some strange reason, though he was only gone for a few months. "I'm back, mom," said Matt, still holding her tightly. "I know. I saw you on TV. You've won." Matthew sniffled loudly, and broke the embrace to wipe his eyes, "I did it. And I know what I'm going to do. We're never going to be poor again. I've found someone in there that I can trust. Everything is going to be okay from now on." "I'm just happy to have you back, Matt." They sat down on the couch and Matthew cut his cake neatly in half and served some to his mother and himself, he laughed a bit, and said, "We sure showed him, huh?" Delores Wakeman rolled her eyes, "Oh, get off that. He doesn't matter anymore, not really." "But just the same...," said Matt with a knowing smirk. Laughing, she replied, "Just the same, you sure fucking showed him. There, I said it, happy now?" Leaning back into the couch, Matthew stared up at the ceiling of his own apartment, and loved it for not being the ceiling of the hotel room. "Yes mom, I'm very happy." Everything in the world in that moment was absolutely perfect. "Matthew," said Delores, "have you been smoking?!" Well, not everything. But close the fuck enough, right?
