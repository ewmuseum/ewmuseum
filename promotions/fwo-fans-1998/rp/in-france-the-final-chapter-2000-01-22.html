In France: The Final Chapter

22 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization In France: The Final Chapter January 22, 2000 :::The scene opens with Matt Douglas in his lockeroom. He appears calm, and there is no sign of nervousness::: Geoff: So Matt, have you learned anything about France? Matt: Oui, j'ai Geoff. Geoff: What did you say? Matt: I said, "Yes I have Geoff." Geoff: Oh, ok. So how did you learn to speak French? Matt: I didn't, I just know a couple of phrases. Geoff: Ok. So, any final words about the match tonight? Matt: Not really Geoff. I am just going to go out there, and show Primetime that I am not Wolf Fang anymore. So after I defeat him, he will recognize that he was more caught up in making fun of me, than in preparing for the match against me. ::: Matt continues to lace up his boots::: Geoff: That is all that you have to say? Matt: Well Geoff, there isn't much to say about a match like this. All that I can do is go out there and do my best to defeat Primetime. But if I lose, I won't hang my head, for I have already defeated two of the great stars here in the fWo. Which are, the Flying Frenchie and Eron the Relentless. ::: Matt proceeds to lace up his final boot, as he stands up::: Matt: Well Geoff, I am going to go get something to drink, so I believe we are done here. ::: The scene fades to black as Matt and Geoff walk down the hallway discussing all of the great sites that they saw here in France:::
