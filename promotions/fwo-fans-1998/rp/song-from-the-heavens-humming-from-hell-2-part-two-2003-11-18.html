Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 2 - Part Two

18 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FLASHBACK to August 17th, 2002 -THUMP- The sound of a bloody Steve Douglas smacking against the door of his hotel room as he now lay on the ground. Blood poured from his bandaged leg in which was currently occupied with a bullet from a gun held by Angel Dalton. He groaned in pain as he slid his hand near the wound cringing with the touch of his skin against his burning leg, "Fuck," the only word he managed to stammer out as he fought the extreme pain and his racing mind. The door opened and two hands helped drag him inside the room, Douglas looked up at the helping figure and then relaxed with relief, "Sean, help me on the couch." -CRACK- The back of Steve's head smacked hard against the wall after the impact of a steel boot cracked hard against his mouth. Blood shot out from his mouth as he yelled in pain immediately gripping his mouth; a few teeth remained scattered on the floor. Sean paced slowly back and forth looking down at the pathetic Steve Douglas, "You tried to control me, every action I wanted to add to the plan you voided, everything I tried, you tried to stop. I'm fucking tired of it Steve and I'm fucking tired of you." Steve shook his head, still grimacing, he forced his tongue to co-operate, "Listen, everything is fucked right now, Angel's gone, we got to forget the original plan, we can still work this-" -CRACK- The sound of the boot driving hard down into Steve's leg at his open wound, Sean pressed his foot hard into the cut grinding it with his heel, "ENOUGH! Enough of the bullshit Steve! I should have never believed you, you are after all pathetic, you never made it in the real world so why I figured you would keep true was beyond me, besides I have bigger fish to fry." Steve grew fierce through his bloody exterior and lifted his right index finger, pointing it up at Token Weed, "You touch me with that boot again, mark my words, I'll come for you." -CRACK- This time Sean responded with the boot to Steve's right side of his face, Steve slumped down lower to the ground, almost unconscious. Token Weed leaned down to look Steve in the eye, "Look at you Steve, look at where you are right now, bleeding, broken, and at my feet. How pathetic, how truly pathetic, the funny thing is you were the one who taught me that one rule. Only the strong survive. It's looking like right now, you're the weak one." Steve spat his blood at Token having the gob land half on his chin and his face, Token smiled and wiped it off, "You remember what gets me off? I just so happen, to be in the 'mood'." Token flipped his blade out. Steve looked at the glimmer coming off the silver blade, "What are you going to do? Kill me?" Token shook his head, "No, who said anything about that? I just wanna get off." Weird thing is, Douglas didn't even scream. His conscious came back to life before he could manage to peel his eyes open, using all the strength that he had left he slowly attempted to raise his head from the ground. As he did he felt the some of the remaining flesh and skin on his face rip and peel away as it was stuck to the ground. He yelled out in pain and his head dropped back against the ground hard. He calmed himself and tried to breathe slowly. "Fuck," was all he could manage to stammer out. Conserving his energy once again he lifted his head and wrenched on his arms to move his large body to a sitting stance. Taking a few moments to take everything in he focused mainly on his vision and how blurred it was. Barely focused on an object beside him he reached out with his hand to fell the soft blanket that rested on his hotel bed. This is what he would use to stand up, as he came to a full stance he yelled out once again and gripped his leg with his right hand. Until now he had forgotten that Angel Dalton had shot him in the leg. Taking it one step at a time he hardly made it to the opposite wall in which he now leaned against taking the strain away from his leg. Sliding his hand up the wall he flicked on the bathroom light and moved his hand over his eyes quickly, the light was blinding. He flung himself into the bathroom and grabbed a hold of the bathroom counter; his arms shook violently as he held himself up. He raised his head slowly and looked in the mirror. What he saw would change him forever, for through the hanging pieces of flesh and skin that dangled from his face... Steve Douglas could not recognize himself. "So, what do you want to do now?" Steve Douglas stared coldly into the reflection of himself, trying to find any similarities to what he once looked like. He spoke to a dark figure he could barely see the reflection of standing behind him, "I don't know, you always seem to know what to do." The figure's voice was familiar but when he spoke Steve's lips moved, "Well, look at you Steve, look at what you've become, all those scars on the inside have finally shown themselves. I guess you're the real Steve Douglas now." Steve attempted to touch what was his cheek but cringed instead, "Real? I'm real." The figure seemed to nod, "You are real Steve, now your as ugly on the outside as you were on the inside." Steve shook his head, "No... my face... but my face." The figure seemed to shred a smile through the blur, it was comforting, "Time to start over." Steve reached over and grabbed the shaving razor blade on the counter and held it in the air staring at it momentarily, "Time to start over." The screams could be heard all throughout the hallway, however no one came. After a few moments, Steve dropped the razor into the sink and pieces of flesh still hung to it's blades. Steve went to touch his face but pulled away, "What have I done?" The dark figure smile seemed to shine through the shadows,"It is not what you have done, it is what you must do from here on in." Douglas looked at figure and then turned to confront it, "Who...or what are you?" The dark figure smile seemed to grow to float in the air, before finally it's deep voice boomed a response, "I am all that is desire, I am all that is disgust, I am want everyone wants and what everyone hates. If you want, I can be something that makes you comfortable." The dark figure began to take form. Much to Douglas' response it was a man that haunted his dreams as he looked at the figure mold into Kenny Rock, "Kenny," Douglas mustered out. Kenny stepped forward, "Yes, you may refer to me as him if that makes you relax. But listen carefully Steven, you have been chosen to help aid me in our bitter revenge. As you are an outcast, so am I. I was thrown down by my peers as well and I know the perfect plan to gain back our respect. We must cleanse this world of it's filth." Douglas' fist clenched, "How?" Kenny placed his hand on Douglas' shoulder, "I can tell you how to gain the strength of twenty men, I can show you the way to pure power. But you must promise yourself to me, and all you ever wanted can be yours. Do you promise yourself to me." Douglas placed his hand on Kenny's shoulder, "I promise myself to you." Kenny wore a wicked grin, "Wonderful. Let us begin preparation, there is a certain man we must visit. His name is Detective Richard Hughes." "Evil is... a mortal entity and not a created one, an eternal entity and not a perishable entity: it existed before the world; it constituted the monstrous, the execrable being who was also to fashion such a hideous world. It will hence exist after the creatures which people this world. " - Marquis De Sade - "L'Histoire de Juliette, ou les prosperites du Vice"
