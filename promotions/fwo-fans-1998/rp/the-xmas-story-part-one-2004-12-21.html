The Xmas Story : Part One

21 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With everything under way for the holidays, and the freedom to return home hanging over the horizon, Matthew made his move as stealthily as possible, creeping along the parking garage. "Craig?" The Cocky One, turned to regard Matthew, his Newport hanging out of the side of his mouth. By the tone of voice he knew something was up. Warily, he said, "Yesssss?" "Would you and Nixon want to come spend Christmas with my mom and me?" Ashing onto the concrete floor, Craig thought it over for the briefest of moments. "Sure, Matt, we'd be delighted." The smile blossomed on Matt's face, finding his cell phone, he called home. "Mom?" "Hey sweetie, how are you?" "Is it okay that I have some guests stay with us for the holidays?" He could hear his mom laugh a little on the other end of the phone. She said, "I guess that means that you'll be coming home then?" "Well yeah, of course." "Oh that's good. I'm just relieved. I thought you were going to stay with new friends or something." "No no, I want to come home. I've got some people that I want you to meet." "Does this mean you're introducing me to someone special in your life, Matt?" Cringing, Matt replied, "Well actually ... yeah." Matt slammed the palm of his hand into his forehead, what was he thinking? "Really, Matthew? You're not teasing me, are you? You've met someone?" Feeling it was too late to correct the error, Matthew continued to uphold the lie, "No, I'm not teasing you." Miles was staring at Matthew, biting down on his knuckle to keep from bursting into laughter. Matt just looked at him with a hopeless expression on his face. "It's somebody really special to me. I'm just about to go talk to ... her." "Oh," replied Deloris Wakeman, her own confusion showing in her voice, "well in that case, I won't keep you then. I look forward to meeting everyone, Matt. Oh wait, is that Craig Miles person you've been talking about coming?" "Yeah he is, Mom, and his wife too." "Ahhhhhh. Okay then, love you, kiddo." "Love you too, mom." With the `end' button pressed on the phone, Miles finally let loose with the laughter, "Did I correctly hear you say, `her', Matthew? Is there something you're not telling me?" Matthew Wakeman, Survivor IV champion and Internet Champion looked around like a lost little boy in a department store, "I'll let you know as soon as I figure out what the fuck I'm gonna do." Craig watched as Matthew took off running at a full sprint, and burst into laughter as Matt dropped the Internet Title, and had to turn around to retrieve it. = Ivy was packing up her things, feeling good about the successful night. With much experience in the business, she heard the rapid footfalls of someone running down the hallway long before the door to her office burst open. She brandished the kendo stick like a lightsaber as she turned. "Oh thank God," said Matt, pleased at discovering Ivy was still here. "Can... I help you?" asked Ivy, a shine of polite inquiry over her guarded stance. "I uhm, do you, uhm, have any plans for the holiday?" She cocked and eyebrow at him, and very slowly said, "Why?" "Well, I was wondering if you wouldn't mind coming to my house to spend Christmas with my mom and me." To say that she was a little surprised was an understatement. Since she didn't reply right away, Matt continued speaking. "Craig and Nixon are coming. And I wanted to see if you wanted to come too. And ..." Since Matthew seemed to get a little choked up, Ivy prompted him, "And... is there more?" "And pretend to be my girlfriend." Silence. Ivy blinked at Matt. Matt said, "Well, it's my mom, and I kind of made a mistake and led her to believe that I, you know, had someone and was bringing them home. Only I don't, not really anyway, and I didn't want to disappoint her and ... stuff. So uhm, yeah." Ivy blinked again. And then she smiled, "Sure Matt, sounds like fun." = With alarm, the plan seemed to be working too well, with everyone doing a better job of keeping Matthew's cover than he did. Ivy, Miles, and Nixon warmed right up to Delores, who delighted in talking with them, hearing their stories. Matthew watched her, and it almost broke him seeing how happy she was. She talked about picking out her house, how she didn't like the feel of developments, and found this old thing near downtown Granite City, smaller than typical, but unique, and slowly she was decorating it how she always wanted to decorate where they lived, pictures of her and Matt everywhere. She told them about the two thirteen year olds who had their "lawn mowing business" growing, and how she looked forward to seeing them again since they'd expanded to shoveling the driveways and sidewalks. How her neighbors had invited her to spend xmas with them, and how she'd proudly told them, "No, my son is coming home for Christmas." Miles talked about things Matthew had never heard before, about him starting out in the business and how scared he was. He also embarrassed Matt, talking about how proud he was of him, how he'd come along. Over wine, Nixon and Delores joked about being a "wrestling widow" when the business was running, but how happy they were when "their boys" came home. Ivy talked about Matt. It seemed like she was making things up off the top of her head, but the scary part is that they were all accurate perceptions. This is what he learned about himself. He was really talented. He was such a sensitive boy. He's had a rough start, but wasn't jaded. He had such a big heart. He would do the business a world of good. Everyone was happy to have met him, and got to know him, and count him as a friend. It almost made him upset to think that they might be just saying that. Dinner rolled around. Amid more food than the five of them could possibly eat, they talked, and it was here that a few people's fun loving nature kind of took things for a turn. It started out with Craig, which was no surprise. "I tell ya, Miss Wakeman, your son is a modern day Casanova...and the energizer bunny to boot." Matt looked at Craig across the table, but Craig was holding eye contact with his mom who said, "Please, call me Delores." "Excuse me. Of course, Delores. But the way Matt's been carrying on, he's been making us old men really miss the energy and vitality of our youth." "Oh, you're hardly and old man, Craig," was her reply. "Well, maybe not, but I'm feeling it when I see this kid do what he does all night, wake up in the morning at the crack of dawn, pounding on my hotel room door wondering when we're going to get out of bed for breakfast." This brought a little giggle out of Nixon. Ivy took hold of Matt's hand and gave it a squeeze. Looking at her for help, Matt discovered to his horror that there was a delighted and mischievous sparkle in her eye. Miles wasn't done, "I swear to god, he's spent half his salary in the back of Rite Aid." Delores laughed, and turning a smile toward Matt, "Well, I'm pleased to hear that you're being safe, dear." Nixon piped up, "Craig told me that at one point, Matt brought home the complete collection of Marvin Gaye, and how from that point Matt seemed to operate without sleep without missing a step." "I'd kill to have that kind of energy again," said Craig. "You're telling me!" said Nixon, causing a burst of laughter at the table from everyone not named Matt, who to be truthful didn't quite catch what she meant as fast as everyone else did. "Actually," said Nixon, "Craig's been extra sweet to me since Matt's been traveling with him. I can only imagine it's your son's influence, Delores." Matt stared down at his plate, deciding that if he continued to eat, perhaps he wouldn't have to answer any questions for himself. "And there has been something I've been wondering, Ivy" asked Nixon, "how did you manage to catch Matt? Craig's had many a sordid tales of how often he'd have to answer Matt's phone for him, to avoid some girls who grew a little obsessive." "Well," said Ivy. Matt looked on in horror as he could nearly see the gears turning in her mind. "I didn't catch him. Matty... caught me. I was in my hotel room--" Eyes on Ivy. "Back me up, Craig... how long have I had an open door policy for anyone on the tour who wanted to talk?" Nixon laughed, though she seemed to stifle Craig's coughing. "Anyways," continued Ivy, "if I may finish my story..." = The hotel room door opened, incense and classical music filled the air. "Who's there? Oh... it's you. Hi." The fictional Matthew wakeman smiled a killer smile, and said, "Hi." With the graces of a cat, Matthew lowered himself onto the other bed in Ivy's room and leaned back sharply. "What's on your mind, Mr. Wakeman?" "Please, call me Matt." = Laughter broke out the table. "He said it like that?" asked Nixon incredulously. "Oh, I couldn't make this stuff up," replied Ivy. "Wakeman, Matthew ... Wakeman," said Craig in his best James Bond, causing more laughter to break out. Ivy turned to Matt, "C'mon, Matty... I can't do it right. Say it like you did that night." Matt shook his head, "Oh, no, I couldn't," which was both meant that he couldn't do it in front of his mother, or in reality. Delores said, "Do go on, Ivy." = "Okay, Matt... what can I do for you t'nite?" Fictional Matthew gave a suave sort of nod, "To be perfectly frank, Miss McGinnis-" "Ivy, please." "Very well, Ivy, I wanted to ask you out." = Delores giggled, "Well, that was forward." "What did you say?" asked Nixon. Ivy shrugged, "I couldn't say anything... I was really taken aback. Speechless, if you will." Miles coughed heavily. "I... Was... Speechless," continued Ivy. = Fictional Matt continued, "I've been noticing you for a while, and I got to thinking that you might be feeling lonely every now and then. Would you like to join me for a drink?" = "Matthew," inquired Delores incredulously, "you're only twenty." Smirking, Miles said, "Matt's a law unto himself when he's determined. He doesn't get refused much of anything." "That's about it," said Ivy, quite proud of her fabrication, "He really won me over." "Really?" said Nixon. "Wellllll...... he does hit all the right spots when it counts." There was a moment of silence as Matt spat a mouthful of chardonnay onto the table. Then the table burst into laughter that could've woken the dead. Delores was wiping tears from her eyes by the time the table settled down. With his face beet red, Matt quickly mumbled, "Excuse me, nature calls." The giggles commenced as Matt left the room. Delores said, "Oh dear, what are you doing to my poor son? I thought he'd drop dead with that last one." "Well, if Matt's in the bathroom, I hope he doesn't have any magazine's stashed there, or he could be a while." Delores laughed, and said, "Craig, I'm the one who packed everything up for the move from our old apartment. I know precisely what kind of magazines Matt looks at." Quiet. Continuing, Delores said, "It's a tricky thing about secrets. Even if you know, and I'll admit to thinking myself quite clever for having known for a long time now, you must respect someone's desire to keep them for as long as they need to. All in all though, I'm thrilled to have you all here. It truly makes me happy to see that my son has made such great friends."
