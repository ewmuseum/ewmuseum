Four: Decourus

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Chastity comes from showing restraint and simplicity in design or expression. It is purity in conduct and in intention." Brandon looked in the room. When he did he was prepared for the worst. Not being a family member he was given little information to go on. All he knew was that Amy had resisted doctor's orders and found herself home for the holidays. A few days later though and their fears were founded as she took a nasty spill on the linoleum in the kitchen and was once again in a hospital. At least she was near home, near her family. He knew he wanted to see her too. A few hours later and after a brief but expected argument with his estranged wife Brandon found himself standing outside of her room. He was caught a little off guard as he peered in Amy had just tossed a phone on the edge of the bed and hoisted her knapsack over her shoulder. She was fully dressed and seemed to be ready to leave. Pushing the door open slowly Brandon breathed in deeply, gathering himself. This was nothing more than a friendly visit he kept telling himself and would have believed it to, if not for the rate of his heart in his massive chest. "Wh...whooaaaaaaa-hohooo...........why are you here?" A smile spread across Brandon's face. Mixed with Amy's startled reaction he couldn't tell if it was out of happiness to see his Amy... his friend Amy, or out of relief that she seemed like her old self again. "I... needed to see you." Needed? What in the hell was that? Needed? Flinching at his choice of words Brandon stuttered unsuccessfully to try and cover for his slip of tongue. "I... wanted... I wanted to see how you were doing. No one would tell me anything so I figured I would take matters in to my own hands." Another smile would clinch the deal and his fumble in words would be forgotten. Amy was viciously holding back a smile of her own. She'd always used the standoffish act as defense, but now it was even more than that. Over the past few weeks she'd spent with Brandon, she'd learned that showing any sort of feeling usually ended in undue hassle, so the best plan of attack was to simply avoid it all together. "...and you think I need Mister Clark Kent flying out to take care of me, do you?" "I didn't say you needed anything. I said I nee... wanted to see you. Um... to see how you were doing." A blush formed on Brandon's face. How many more slips could he have in one sentence? A knot had formed in his stomach and as he spoke he closed the distance between them to a rather personal two feet. Amy now tilted her head back slightly to look Brandon in the face. A wince of pain arched across her brow as a jolt hit her neck, she waved it off though as she locked eyes with the mighty hero. "So... how are you... feeling?" The proverbial tension in the air thickened with their newfound proximity. "Fine." Campbell had snapped rather matter-of-factly, doing a rather successful job of remaining composed with a stone demeanor. "Just fine." Her words, as usual, differed from her feelings. In truth, she felt as if she could easily pass out at that very moment, and for some reason it seemed like the spinning sensation in her stomach and head weren't entirely induced by the concussion, though she'd never admit that to herself, let alone even tell Brandon she was anything less than in perfect condition whether he'd believe it or not. She didn't want what she saw as emotional charity...or, at least, refused to admit that she really did need it. Sensing that Amy's words were counter to her feelings Brandon leaned over and broke the barrier she had erected around herself. Her small frame was engulfed in his massive arms. Her face buried in the folds of his shirt she was forced to inhale his woodsy cologne. Brandon held the unexpected form of affection probably longer than he should, but the smell of her hair distracted him. As Amy began to muster what little strength she had left in her, Brandon squeezed tighter and she caved. She was dizzy and incredibly nauseous anyway, and at least he was keeping her steady. After all, a hug wasn't so bad...it wouldn't cause too much ruckus, right? Her stomach didn't quite agree with that, though; the contact and a different sort of tension were making it do proverbial cartwheels. Amy's arms finally wrapped around Brandon's elbows half way, returning his sign of affection. He finally pulled back after bringing himself back to where he was, and the situation he had put himself in. Still holding her by the shoulders though he absently pushed a stray strand of hair from her cheek. "I was so worried about you. I... I know I shouldn't have just showed up here like this. You have a life going on here and it was beyond me to just barge in to it. I just had to make sure that the fWo's new female Superstar was going to be ok..." He had half convinced himself he meant that as well. That is, until he looked back in to her beautiful mismatched eyes. Yet again, the 'Mighty Hero' had swept in and saved Amy's mood. For some reason she couldn't ever stay stone-faced around him, no matter how hard she tried. But it was then that she saw him staring into her and, seeing herself that he saw something more, she was forced to turn away for his sake. "It's...yeah, it's okay to be worried. You deserve a kick in the ass for spending the cash to come see a crazy fuck like me, though...you know damned well I'd be around." When Amy began to turn away Brandon did something he didn't think he would do. He held on. Turning back and saying those words to him were probably meant to shock him back to reality but they didn't. Instead he looked down again at his recent tag-team partner before finally breathing deeply and taking a slow and deliberate step back. "I'm a big time fWo Superstar remember? I've got cash to burn..." The joke elicited only a slight smile from either of them as they sized up the situation. Brandon knew where his emotions were going now. After all of this there could be no denying it. But deny it he would, after all, he was married. A wife, two children, a family he had built and fought for. "Seriously though, the Foundation paid for it. It's a business trip after all." A quick wink seemed to lighten the mood a bit. That is before Brandon stuck his foot in his mouth again. "So where's your ride at?" Amy couldn't help but sigh and lower her head, obviously upset even though she tried to mask it with smartass remarks. "My 'ride's being a jerk-off and not answering his cell phone." Unfortunately, true emotion blurted through once again. Within a month, at this rate, Brandon would know her better than she knew herself...if he didn't already. "It's nothing new...after all, he is a boyfriend of mine and, by his standards, I'm little more than an obligation that returns the favor." "Well then let me take you home. No obligations here, all you really have to promise me is that you'll smile half the time we are in the car and the debt will be paid." Then, Brandon smiled. It seemed that smiling in the company of this fiery redhead was almost obligatory. For all of the troubles they had been in during their short friendship they had managed to actually enjoy each other's company. A level of comfort had been reached by both of them. Even Amy felt it though it pained her to admit such a thing. Brandon stepped back and took the knapsack from Amy. She reluctantly complied and with a hearty yank on the stainless steel door handle they walked on. Content with their company. Five: Temperence [This has been an Aimz/ Brand Frontier collaboration]
