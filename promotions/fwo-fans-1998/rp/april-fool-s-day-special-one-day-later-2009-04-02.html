Fade in on... something is very wrong here. Oh no. This could be trouble. It appears that there will be a broadcast from the Key Arena tonight, but not by who you think. In a poorly lit storage room somewhere stands Mike Bear with a confident smirk on his face. He's dressed in his ring gear and a plum colored smoking jacket, and he appears to be puffing on a plastic yellow bubble pipe, tiny soap bubbles emerging. Next to him are several cinder blocks set up as a makeshift fireplace, and he has apparently started a fire with scrap paper and a few unsold Keith Scott Zimmerman t-shirts (good to know he doesn't hold a grudge). There are dozens of books haphazardly stacked all over the place, but luckily not near the fire. Especially lucky, since they were apparently checked out of a local library if the visible spines are to be believed. And just to drive the point, he has hung a sheet on the wall with the words “UNDERGROUND THEATER” written in spray paint. What have we done to deserve this punishment.
