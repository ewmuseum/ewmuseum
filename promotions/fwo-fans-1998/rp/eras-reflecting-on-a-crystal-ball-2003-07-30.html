Eras: Reflecting on a Crystal Ball

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Soundtrack: "You Know How I Do" by Taking Back Sunday Series: Eras (4/4) Kevin Masters was exhausted. He had remained in the fWo at Samuel Smooth's behest, and then for the past few weeks he had been bogged down with a knee surgery, which they covered by calling it a "suspension." He was glad he did what he did, and that he had stayed, because things looked better than ever for the upcoming months. Yet now, at this very instant, his body was wrought with more injury. It had been a tremendous weekend. First there was fWo Meltdown, his first fWo Pay-Per-View event. He had been booked in the opening match against Jade, in which the feud between the two came to a major head. It had been a flop. That's not to say he didn't wrestle well, but the match itself wasn't great. Going back and watching the tapes Kevin felt like he was back in REALM again, wrestling too green, too new for the fed. Then there was the hWo's Gruesome Gamez. Conversely he had been booked in the main event there, defending his championship belt. That match went better in his mind, for some reason there just was so much less pressure. It was hard to say, but he felt more confident there. More of a leader and less... new. In the end it really didn't matter. He got to wrestle twice in one night, and he had pulled it off. Now, even three days later, he still was exhausted. He missed RUAHH~!, well more specifically he wasn't booked, but he still wasn't there. Instead he was nursing his wounds, his broken nose, and sprained wrist. Things that hurt, but weren't devastating. He'd be back in action as soon as this Thursday, and he was ready for it. He knew he needed to improve, but Meltdown... Meltdown was just a start. What was to come was where it would get good. He could feel it. He was finally starting to fit into the fWo, and he was finally beginning to like it. It would only be a matter of time before, before his name would be the headlining name in the promotion. But he couldn't think about that now. He couldn't think about much of anything really. Too much had gone through his head in the past three days as it was, he didn't need anything more. He didn't want anything more. All he wanted was what he was going to get: a good night's sleep and some peace and quiet. He was ready for it, and maybe, maybe he even deserved it. Indeed, Kevin Masters was exhausted.
