Before the next bout...

22 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Before the next bout... January 22, 2000 ((The scene goes to in the backstage after the second match of Invasion 2000, right beside a dressing room door, with "fWo International Champion:Eron" put on the door. Matt Loco is standing in front of the door, holding his ear, as hes ready for the camera to prepare)) Matt Loco:Hello everybody Im Matt Loco, and im here now, standing in front of the most recent 2 belt holder in the fWo Eron the Relentless, who just beaten Rain for the Internet Title, while holding his own belt in the process, become a first time Internet Champion. Im here awaiting him to come to his dressing room so I can get a word, and until he comes, heres some replay on what happened. ((They show clips of what just happened in the match. The two starring each other down, Eron, attempting a Superkick but Rain grabbing the leg, Rains failed pinfall attempts, then Eron suprising Rain with a final Superkick, pinning Rain and winning the match. But before the clip finished, it went back to Loco)) Matt Loco:Wait here comes Eron. ((Eron walks over to the dressing room, sweaty as his hair is drenched down, sweaty all over, with both belts in his hand, ignoring everyone, including Loco, and heading straight to his dressing room, almost closing the door on Loco. Loco runs in as he sees Eron hold both belts in his hands, as they hang down, as he begins to laugh)) Matt Loco:Hello Eron congradulations on you're Int... Eron the Relentless:Shut up Loco and let me hold a moment to myself as i bass in my own glory! Matt Loco:Well, I must say, that was a pretty big comeback. No-one ever thought that you would pull it off. Eron the Relentless:Well Matt, thats why I like to keep my fan number to ZERO. Once you have a crowd cheering for you, you get cocky, and you feel that they deserve something. Thast what Rain did. He could have put me away, I know that, he knows that, but he wanted the crowd of stupid, unintellegent fools to have a "Rainmaker" before he tried to take my International Title. Well, it was a foolish mistake Rain will regret for the rest of his life. A mistake he cant take back, no matter what he says. Wheres your gods now when you need it huh Rain? Well, they're laughing at you. I never needed to make you give up. One kick is all I needed. One kick, which wasnt strong enough to break you jaw so I didnt have to hear you moan and bitch about revenge, but oh well it stunned you for the time I needed. 3 seconds. 3 long seconds actually. Matt Loco:Well, Eron, what do you think of the Internet Title? Before you said it was nothing but trash. Eron the Relentless:From seeing the list of fools who held this title before, and how badly made it looked from afar, it looked pretty crumby. But seeing it close, drenched in my own sweat and blood, it looks like a masterpiece. I represent the main device of communication all around the world. I represent every person who has even heard a strange noise when going to a computer. I represent the future...of everyone. And I must say, I represent it DAMN well! Matt Loco:Well, I must congradulate you on your winning of the titles... Eron the Relentless:I am not finished Bitchboy. Rain, you think that you still frighten me with your puny challenge? Everyone knows I have been up to any challenge, and if I have to defend these titles once again to your oversized excuse for an fWo Wrestler, than so be it. But now, I, Eron the Relentless, will go out, and party my ass off, with the only fans I care about, the cute 17 year old groupies. Thank you. Oh yeah, and fWo, if you feel I dont deserve the two most precious metals this side of the universe, you can take a breath...OF FRESH DEATH! ((Eron then puts on the Internet Title, as he looks at it, smiling. He then straps on the International Title, having the front of it show in the back, as he taps both and smiles, as he grabs his coat)) Matt Loco:And back to you Stevie and Chris... ((It then goes to the Hellrazor/VL Scaffold Match...))
