Unleashed

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Madison Square Garden. June 10, 2002 fWo Presents: Unleashed the aftermath. Almost every wrestlers' dream - other than to make it big, become the world champion of a successful promotion, and to be recognized as one of the top men in this business - is to compete in New York City, at Madison Square Garden. That is... if you're serious about overseeing the perfection of your craft, of course. Some of the most incredible workers this industry has ever known has competed and wrestled in some of the greatest matches of all time in this building. It's truly an honor... ... so you can imagine how disappoited XXXstasy was when he found out he wouldn't be competing on this night, can't you? No you can't. Sean "Triple X" Stevens has been in this business for almost eight years. He's wrestled in the Garden before... and, he was sure he'd get the opportunity to wreslte there again. Instead of being disappointed - like half the guys who weren't used during the show - he was excited. Excited because Chris Universal gave him the "thumbs up" to run wild. Ecstatic because next week, he gets the opportunity of a lifetime - a shot at one of the most recognized championships in the game; the fWo Internet Title. But, that wasn't all he was feeling. Even though excitement was the main ingredient that made up Sean's emotions... he was a little down. Unleashed had come and gone, and Sean, alongside his girlfriend Poison Ivy had just took Eli Flair to his old apartment to rest and heal some of the wounds caused by two cowards who both had their shot at beating him one- on- one, at one point or another, but couldn't get the job done. ...but, did so tonight, in a collaborative effort, that also required weeks and weeks of scheming. Sean Stevens sat on the steps, directly in front of the buildings entrance. Ivy had gone home... as well as the rest of the fWo roster. Leaving Sean to himself, with nothing but time to think. As the cameraman, silently pointed his state of the art, recording utensil at Stevens... he faced it. And, began. Stevens - "I want to give a shout-out to my friend who's probably asleep in his apartment... I'm sure you know him... his name is Eli Flair. And, I want to send a message to two people who KNOW who they are, without me even having to speak their names." Stevens was dressed in a thin grey sweatshirt, in an effort to keep his arms warm, as the night's temperature decreased rapidly from about 90 to 60 degrees. Yet, had on a pair of "Nike" gym shorts in an effort to keep his legs from sweating. His hair was still braided back into a ponytail, as seen earlier in the evening on Unleashed. "Eli Flair is a proud, proud man. He doesn't ask me to share his beliefs, or be involved in his beefs, simply because he's a man's man... and, can stand on his feet. So I don't expect him to ask me for any assistance in disposing of you two jackasses. And, out respect of his wishes... if things don't get TOO bad... I won't interfere. I'll mind my own business, and let the "King of Extreme" attend to his." He paused for a moment. "But, if he asks... I can and will promise you this. The games you played out there tonight in front of his hometown crowd... they'll cease. Because I'm not the one. And, contrary to what you may or may not believe... NOBODY fears you. No matter who you become, how many times you change your name, die, come back to life, or how big and ugly of a FREAK you are... it doesn't make a difference, because all the mind games stop when we're in the center of the ring, eye- to- eye, man- to- man. And, I've seen you two in action... you're mediocre at best. It would be no problem at all to see to it that you never compete again. "But, like I said... until Eli asks me... it's none of my business. But, what IS my business... is the Internet Title Tournament. And, my rumored opponent for next week. "Lance Knight... we haven't known each other long, but we've competed against one another enough times to know what to expect. As I've said in the past... I know you're good. But, that's as far as the kissing up goes. Because as good as you are... you're equally bad. "I see all the mistakes you make, Lance. And, sure... they don't seem as noticeable against an idiot like Grendel, or some other dime a dozen, so called competitor... but, that's not me. I'm faster than you, Lance... Stronger than you. SMARTER than you. What are you gonna do, when your best isn't good enough? What conclusions is your mind gonna jump to when you give it your VERY best shot, and I keep getting up? Are you gonna be able to dig down within the depths of your soul, finding strength that you never knew you had? Or are you gonna quit?" Triple X smirked. "Well, I got news for you, Lance... no matter what route you take. Whether it's the digging... or the quitting... you still lose. I know you've heard the stories, Knight... so let me be the one to tell you... they're true. I'm EVERY bit as good as they say I am. I've done it all... seen it all... and, conquered it. "What makes you think you're any different? Because you're not. Lance, everything you do... I'll be two steps ahead. You swing that punch... I'll duck it, and counter with a punch of my own. You try and kick me... I'll sweep your fucking legs right from under you and finish you off. They don't call me the 'Greatest Wrestler on the Planet' because it sounds cool. Unlike most of the people here... my nickname fits. "NOBODY can beat me. And, don't mistake that for being cocky. Call it confidence. And, if the rumors ARE true... next monday... I'm gonna confidently beat the living hell out of you in the middle of the squared circle... "... Why? Because I'm the 'Blue- Eyed Badass', I'm THAT damned good, and *I* can." Sean turned his attention away from the camera towards the night's sky. It was a beautiful night... maybe Poison Ivy would like to go for a walk in Central Park or something... ... ... Nevermind.
