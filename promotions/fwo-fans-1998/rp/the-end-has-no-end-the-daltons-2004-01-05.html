The End Has No End (The Daltons)

August 12th, 1973 - 10:43am "You're sure he's not going to be home?", he asked. Her eyes shimmered in the darkness. "Why, who can you mean, Mr. Telephone Man? Not my handsome husband. He's in Seattle" He put her down on the bed, her legs dangling over the side. "Turn on the light", she said. "I want to see what you're doing" He turned on the bedside light and looked down at her. The apron she was wearing, all she was wearing, was pulled to one side. "Take that thing off" "You take it off", she said with a smile. "You can figure out the knots" He bent to do it. She always made him feel like a dry mouthed kid stepping up to the plate for the first time, his hands shaking everytime he got near her. "No, on your knees", she said. "Get on your knees for me" He dropped to one knees and crawled to her, reaching for the apron ties. She put one high heeled foot on each shoulder. He began to kiss the inside of her thigh, the flesh firm and warm under his lips. "That's right, Corey, that's just right, keep going up, keep--" "Well, this is cute, ain't it?" Myra Dalton screamed. Corey King looked up, blinking and confused. Robert Dalton stood in the doorway, a shotgun hanging loose in his arms. Corey felt a warm gush as his bladder let go. "So, it's true. I guess I owe that bastard Mickey Carmichael a case of Budwiesser. Goddamnitt" Myra found her voice first. "Robert, listen. It isn't what you think. He broke in. He was like a crazy man. He--" "Shut up, cunt". He was smiling. It was a gentle smile. He was wearing the same steel-grey suit he was wearing when she kissed him good-bye two hours earlier. "Listen", Corey said weakly. "Please don't kill me. Not even if I deserve it. You don't wanna go to jail for this. Beat me up, I got that coming, but--" "Get up off your knees, son", Robert Dalton said, still smiling his gentle smile. "Your fly's unzipped" "Listen, Mr. Dalton" "Please, call me Robert. I mean, we're practically best buddies. I've been getting your sloppy seconds, isn't that right?" "Robert, this isn't what you think. He raped me" Robert looked at her, and his smile was gentle and benign. "If you say one more word I'm gonna jam this up inside you and let you have some special airmail" Myra began to moan. Her face had gone to colour of unflavoured yogurt. "Mr. Dalton...Robert..." "Corey, that your name?" Corey nodded stupidly. "Alright, Corey. Why don't we step into the living room. There's something we need to discuss". He smiled at Corey, showing that he meant him no harm, then flicked his eyes to Myra. "You stay right there, puss, or you ain't never gonna find out how 'Days Of Our Lives' comes out. C'mon Corey" They stepped into the livingroom, and Corey began to blubber. He tried not to, but couldn't help himself. "Turn around and face me", Robert said. Corey turned. "Now, you know what you been doin'?" Robert asked. His smile was gone, and his face was very grave. Corey didn't answer. It was a stupid question. But, he did continue to blubber. "You've been sleepin' with another man's wife" Corey nodded. "You know what happens to guys if they get caught?" Corey nodded. "Grab the barrel of this gun, Corey. Careful. It's got a five pound pull, and I've got about three on it now. So pretend....oh, pretend that you're grabbing onto my wife's tit" Corey reached out one shaking hand and put it around the barrel of the gun. The metal was cold against his flush palm. "Put it in your mouth, Corey. Both barrels. Yes, that's right. Easy....that's okay. Yes, your mouth's big enough. Just slip it in. You know all about slipping it in, don't you Corey?" Corey's jaws were open as wide as they could go. His terrified stomach was trying to retch. The steel was oily on his teeth. "Close your eyes, Corey" Corey only stared at him, his eyes burning with tears. "Close those baby blue eyes, Corey" Corey closed them. Robert pulled both triggers. The hammers fell on empty chambers with a double click-click. Corey fell to the floor in a dead faint. Robert looked down on his for a moment, smiling gently. He then turned the shotgun so the butt end was in the air. He turned to the bedroom. "Ready or not, Myra, here I come" Myra Dalton began to scream. ************************************* August 12th, 1973 - 2:08pm "Mr. and Mrs. Dalton, this is the hardest part of my job" Myra Dalton lay in the hospital bed, her left arm in a sling. Already tears began to push their way through her swollen black eyes. Her husband Robert sat in a chair beside the bed, his grey suit dotted with red stains. The doctor, Dr. Ridley, stood over them, his sad eyes darting from one to the other. In his right hand he carried Myra's chart. "It would seem that, during your fall, your uterus ruptured, tearing the uteral lining" Myra tried to choke her sobs back, but instead released a wet cry that brought goosebumps to the doctor's skin. "What does that mean, doctor?", Robert asked, trying to take his wife's hand. She pulled it away before he could take it, covering her face and crying into her hands. "It means, Mr. Dalton, that your wife may never be able to bear children" Myra's sobs became louder behind her hands. Dr. Ridley turned his gaze to the window, fighting his own tears. "I'm terribly sorry", he said finally. The room was thick with silence, broken only by Myra's rhythmic sobs. After moments that felt like centuries, Dr. Ridley spoke. "Mr. Dalton", he said. "Could I speak with you in the hallway?" "Of course", Robert responded. He put his hand on his wife's knee as he stood, but she quickly pulled away. "Mr. Dalton", Dr. Ridley started once they were in the hallway. "Everything was so hectic when your wife arrived, I never really got the whole story of what happened to her" "She fell down our staircase", Robert responded quickly. "I was in the kitchen when I heard her scream. When I found her, I thought she was dead". Dr. Ridley could hear that Robert was fighting back tears of his own. "I carried her out to the car, and I didn't know if she was breathing or not. All I could think was 'Dear God, please don't take her from me'. The drive here was the hardest thing I've ever done" Tears were quietly streaming down Robert's face. "If you'll excuse me, doctor", Robert finally said. "I'm going to be with my wife" "Of course", Dr. Ridley said. Michael Ridley watched as Robert Dalton entered his wife's hospital room. Before Robert closed the door, Dr. Ridley noticed something that stayed with him long after Robert and Myra Dalton left the hospital. Myra Dalton couldn't look her husband in the eye.
