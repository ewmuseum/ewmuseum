Seven: Munificent

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Liberality is the cornerstone of showing or acting out of compassion for another, to be marked by generosity or openhandedness." Sean and Jarod looked up to their father, both literally and figuratively. He was a mountain of a man in his ideals as well as physical stature. For so long he had been the cornerstone of the Roche family that thinking he ever questioned himself on his actions or feelings seemed inappropriate. Sean was the oldest of the Roche boys and at the age of eight had seen far more turmoil in his life than one would think. Because of his father's profession and the resulting celebrity Sean has had to cope with life in the public eye. When he was four he was kidnapped and held for ransom. Through it all and accompanied by another victim of the wrestling world young Bobby Knight, Sean survived. He was his father's son after all. Jarod was now three and was not able to understand the strife his family had lived through. All he knew and understood was that when he needed his Daddy all he had to do was call out and he was there. In his mind that was simply the way things worked. He couldn't fathom a time when his father would not come when asked. He didn't know how fragile his family was right now. How close they were to not being a family any longer. Right now all they knew is that they had a whole day with their father. A whole day. It didn't happen often with Brandon's work schedule so when it did happen the patriarch of the family made sure it was a special time. This day for instance they had made their way to a local animal shelter and were volunteering their time with the animals. Brandon tried not to let his thoughts linger back to his strained relationship with Ana, nor to the loss he had suffered at the hands of Ric Chronos the night before. This was his time with his children; his two special and absolute joys in his life. The only thing his mind kept wandering to though, the one thing that caused him the most trouble in focusing on Sean and Jarod was none other than Amy Campbell. He had denied his feelings for so long, trying to make things work it was as if he was living a lie. Last night he held her in his arms and he finally felt content.
