Finality, The Third Part Of

11 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Powerful bolts of lightning crashed high above the darkened sky as Rei approached the temple. Trees were surrounding the brown stone building, a long staircase leading up to a single entrance. She looked around but it didn't do much good, as it was far too dark to see anything other than her immediate surroundings. With the moon in the cycle of New Moon, it provided no help to her as she attempted to take in her surroundings. This could quickly turn unpleasant. Rei thought. Especially if She shows up now. Rei's thoughts brought her back to the rest of her conversation with Jessica, after Jessica's startling statement about her attacker... "What the hell do you mean, God?" Rei questioned Jessica. "The Christian deity actually exists? Jessica smiled. "There's no other explanation, sister." Jessica said, pausing when she spat up blood. "Is that what we are, sisters? I never really knew what we should call each other. Co-existing beings under a single shroud of darkness?" "So back to what you were saying, Jessica." Rei said, stopping Jessica's rambling. "It is God." Jessica said. "That's the only explanation, since He is the only deity that exists. But I question why He would take the form of a female..." "A female?" Rei questioned her. And suddenly, it was all clear. "Cara." Rei said. "It was Cara Dyconin. The Dark Goddess, the former .Two. Isn't that right, Jessica?" "I suppose that's the form God has chosen to take." Jessica said with a weak smile. "That's the only explanation for what .Two did to me. It's confusing why He would choose to smite His most loyal of followers, but I suppose I am not one to question God's wi-" Jessica ended up cutting herself off, however, with a cough of blood. Rei stood up and turned around to leave, but Jessica called out to her, stopping her for a moment. "Wait!" Jessica said. "Cara....She did mention something to me. The Temple of Eternity. You have to go there, Rei." Rei nodded, and left the room, leaving Jessica to spit up more blood and suffer further. Rei shook her head, trying to get herself re-focused. "If the Christian deity exists, he sure isn't Cara Dyconin." Rei muttered to herself. "I have to find a way to stop her, and to find out where Kali is now. It's all up to me." With a brief pause, Rei began the long trek up the stairs- to re-navigate her destiny. even though we were so close together twilight's colors have now changed Existence is Eternity
