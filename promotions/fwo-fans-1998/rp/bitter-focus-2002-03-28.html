Bitter Focus

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The flickering of candles in the dark. A ring of fire. In the center sits the Hardcore Luchador, Crucifix. His lean body glistens with sweat, scars marking his chest and back. In his brief tenure in the fWo, Crucifix has been pushed and punished. He was thrown to the wolves, dragged through the Hardcore Division. Eventually, he earned a shot at the fWo Hardcore Title, which he won. And held for one night. But with the fall of the Hardcore Division, Crucifix found himself being set to the side. His matches became fewer and fewer. With all he had done for the fWo, he was being swept under the carpet. That simply would not do. Now, the Hardcore Luchador is back. Through fabric covered eyes, he looks out into the darkness.] Crucifix: Brandon. You are a most confused soul. You seem to think that my goal is to build a reputation at your expense. At your expense? What have you accomplished that would make your defeat that savory to me? I'm a former Hardcore Champion. I've tasted championship glory. No, my goals for you are much simpler. I'm using you to send a message. When I first came to the fWo, I was little more than a joke. Comedy relief. I was battered, bloodied, and beaten by some of the most dangerous men in professional wrestling. For all those beatings, for being run through the meat grinder, what did I hope to gain? All I wanted was respect. I simply wanted to be treated as an equal. Even after winning the Hardcore Title, I hoped that all my blood and suffering would pay off. The result? Half the roster didn't even know my name. The other half snickered when they said it. They laughed at my pain. They laughed at my suffering. Brandon, I'm having the last laugh now. You will bleed. You will suffer. And in the end, you will be broken, sacrificed on the Alter of Agony. With your blood on my hands, I will send out a message to everyone in the fWo, from Chris Universal right down the line: I am Crucifix. Remember the name, because your pain... is my pleasure. [Emerging from the darkness steps a young man. Crucifix turns his head slightly, barely acknowledging his arrival. The young man, David Cross, rubs his temples as he stands behind the Hardcore Luchador.] Cross: You're a sick man, Cruci. [Behind the mask, Crucifix smiles a sick, chilling smile.] Crucifix: You have no idea.
