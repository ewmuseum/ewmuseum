Bust it, that nigga Slick Rick said I waited long time sweatin it I got jerked but now I'm gettin it Hazy like asthma bizarre disaster Stress almost held me down from bein a master The faster, preacher, poet, a teacher "It's been so long" like Monifah There he was, fully covered in his multi-shaded jogging attire, Ric Tatum never lost the pace. It's been roughly 2 hours since he started but his body didn't feel that way. The hood covered his sweat induced hair, moving front to back following his step and head bobbing movements. He was ready, today was his day, only a handful of hours separated him from his destiny. Later on tonight he would face Stormwindz in the match that would for good put him ahead and onto the next level. He knew he was the best and didn't care about any of the doubters. Ric weaved with such smoothness between two discussing business men, one practically yelling on his cell phone while trying to keep up with his walking partner's conversation. The headphones were locked onto his ears, they were the pre-game pep talk a football star needed to go out and perform. The words of wisdom a young man seeked from elderly men, the bible parables from a preacher's mouth to a person in distress. His life was at stake tonight, this would be the moment he would move on. Unlike many others who had tried and failed, Ric Tatum was going to achieve the next step in his quest to the top. Believe that, I'm needed, in rappin, I breathe this Some pick up a microphone and can't even achieve this Oscar award winning your shit I'm bored with it Stop copy-catting son (why?) cause your dog did it Get your own lingo, make up your own jingle Ten years later you be bitin my single Though it wouldn't be a song others would steal, Ric thought to himself, when he retires others will study his style, his prowess and legacy. It was a given, some are followers, some are leaders and only you can decide your fate. The chorus penetrated his ears and flowed into the cerebrum I say it's my world, and I won't stop and if you stand in my way you bound to get dropped Say it's my world, and I won't stop and if you stand in my way you bound to get dropped The meaning of those words were that much more important to him. It's his world and no, he won't stop for anything. He was going to achieve what he wanted and nobody would mess with that. Some wish that I was gone cause they know I'ma win In a minute I 'll be makin six G's times ten I'm the computer, printin out data take notes then analyze the information and press send He thinks back to the countless hours of training, watching videos, studying styles and moves. Ric Tatum wasn't like any other athlete, wrestling will be, is and was his life. There was no mistaking it, everyone around him labeled him a joke, nothing more than a 15-minute of fame character. Nobody believed him when he said he would become a superstar, what fools. After tonight everyone would come back on their words. Architect, rap technician, man listen It's no doubt you gonna pump this in your system My skills ill, and all of that above Confidence, I'm not worried bout a street buzz He cornered a building coming face to face with an elderly woman with mere seconds to react and avoid contact. Swiftly he cross-stepped to the left to safety and open sidewalk, another tough spot became simple work. He was out to impress and silence the sceptics. Top choice, this here nigga got poise Ain't a man alive who could stop my noise I snatch a star from the sky, spark up your lye with it You stand astounded wonderin how I did it Now you're star struck, like I truck gold Treatin me like a nigga havin a million records sold A quick glance to his watch indicated that it was now 9 o'clock. About 12 hours separated him from the moment. He was out to conquer tonight, to impress and most of all to distinguish himself. O.C. do demolition, nil competition Like robot Kong on a destroy mission A matter of life and death you try to fight for breath I snatch your heart from a slice through your chest Who the best, from New York to Bogota Who got face thinkin they scare me with a scar, shit What I be bringin is a terrible sight A performance never again performed on the mic Tatum didn't care who or what it was. Tonight someone was going to be proven wrong and tonight, something would come that should have come earlier. Ric crossed the traffic-plagued street, not realizing any of this. To him, it was another obstacle and it was going to be beaten. I say it's my world, and I won't stop And if you stand in my way, you bound to get dropped... Lyrics: "My World" by O.C.
