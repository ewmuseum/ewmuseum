......And Yet I Wonder, Will You Be Right?

20 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have lost what is left of my sanity. I have died over and over again. I have lost everything but the light. Let me embrace the dark. "I don't think I agree with its perspective." Kali muttered as she looked around the darkened room. "Always gloom and doom, never anything more." "Our life leads us to such a viewpoint." Rei pointed out. "You should always try and find the brighter side of things." Jessica butted in. "That's what God wants you to do." "Someone to serve." Rei said, nodding her head. "You both always need someone to serve." Perhaps Rei is right. Perhaps I need someone to tell me what to do, in order to feel self-worth. Perhaps I'm not so different from Jessica after all. And yet, I disobeyed that which gives me the feeling of self-worth, and I am still here. Perhaps Rei and I aren't all that different after all. Maybe all I need.....maybe all I need is him. "Dirty thoughts!" Jessica screamed out. "Kali is having DIRTY THOUGHTS!" Kali rolled her eyes, as did Rei, which only angered Jessica more. "SEX BEFORE MARRIAGE IS WRONG!" Jessica screamed out. "I don't have the patience to deal with you right now." Kali said quickly. She snapped her fingers, and with that, Jessica was gone. "Perhaps I should leave as well." Rei said. "I am only in the way here." Rei turned to leave, but stopped when Kali spoke. "You held me back for so long, Rei." Kali said quietly. "Now, I understand why." Rei gave a knowing look before disappering as well. Are you right? Am I right? In the end, are ANY of us right? I don't think so. I think, in the end, we are all wrong. We all look out towards the harbor, and we all see nothing but the ocean. No boats. No people. Nothing. We only see what is there, not what is coming, nor do we see what was once there. I cannot claim to be right, but in the end, perhaps I like being wrong. As Kali looked over to where Rei and Jessica once stood, she reached a realization. For the first time in her meaningless existance, an existance controlled by anger and hate, she had realized what life was truely about. In the end, will you be right? Will you be right?
