Mega Man Battle Network: Sushi Man

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Codemaster had entered Sushi Man's level with the idea in his mind that this level would be a fucked up water level where he jumped higher than normal, had to deal with spikes hanging above his head as he jumped over spikes on the floor, and had to deal with his fair share of giant frogs that spawned little frogs. Instead, he ended up on the set of Iron Chef. The Codemaster blinked, "Um, what in the hell?" Suddenly, KAGA appeared before the Codemaster, and spoke to him. Unfortunately, the Codemaster didn't know what Kaga was saying, since he couldn't see the subtitles and Kaga spoke in Japanese. He didn't know that he was welcoming the Codemaster into Kitchen Stadium, and he didn't know that he was welcoming him onto Iron Chef. "You're lucky you're not talkin' about my mom, or I'd be knocking your ass to the ground." the Codemaster said. Then Kaga said something else and waved his arms in the direction of Sushi Man. "Hi." Sushi Man said, waving his spatula-holding hand. "Oh, God." "See, not all of us believe in violence. I, for example, believe in beating my opponents in an Iron Chef competition where the loser dies." Sushi Man said. "That is SO hypocritical." the Codemaster said. "Well, I'm not the one who kills you." Sushi Man claimed. "Yes, but you'd be directly responsible for it!" Codemaster protested. "Aw, you're no fun." Kaga glared at both of them to shut up, and explained the glorious rules behind the competition of Iron Chef. It was a battle between two respectable masters of the culinary arts, who use the power of a special ingredient, chosen by the members of Kitchen Stadium, to craft several dishes of powerful taste and art, to be judged upon by the guests at Kitchen Stadium. When the powers of the tastes had been thoroughly considered, the judges mark down their opinions and Kaga announces the winner via a suspenseful moment. When Kaga waved his hands toward the special ingredient. Eel. "Oh, HELL no." the Codemaster said, before he pointed the Dish Shooter directly at Sushi Man's head. "Mommy." BANG. CRASH. Okay, so, while the Dish Shooter didn't shoot bullets, but rather, glass dishes. And when it hit Sushi Man, it buried itself in his face, and sent him flying backwards so hard that he landed on his face and shattered the dish into pieces. Kaga looked at the camera and said something. The subtitles said, "Holy shit!" Kaga left. The Codemaster shrugged, looked up, saw the words "GET EQUIPPED WITH IRON SPATULA", picked up said spatula, and just simply left Kitchen Stadium.
