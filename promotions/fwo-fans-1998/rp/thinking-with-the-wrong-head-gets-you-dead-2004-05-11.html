Thinking With the Wrong Head Gets You Dead

11 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lenny double-checked the locks on his door before heading to the kitchen. As the crossed the threshold of his tiny apartment, his hand jerked to the gun tucked into the waistband of his pants. He was sure he'd heard something in the hallway. Paranoia got the best of him and he slowly walked back to the door, his palm resting on the cool, moulded-steel grip of the .44 like a gunfighter waiting to draw. Cautiously, he pressed his eye to the spy hole. The hallway was empty. "Goddamn," Lenny whispered as he wiped the sweat off the back of his neck. "I almost wish he'd just show up already." Part of him wanted that. But the sane part, the part that wanted to live to see another day, hoped that he would never come. That was wishful thinking. He always came. And when he left, someone was always dead. Always. Lenny started toward the kitchen again. Had she been worth it? He knew she was married; you could have bought the entire apartment building with the wedding ring she wore on her slender finger. But the club was hopping, the music was thumping, and the liquor was flowing. If he'd been less horny and much less drunk, Lenny would probably have blown her off. But she was drop-dead gorgeous, the kind of woman who'd never give a guy like him the time of day. She was also quite persistent, and he was horny. Drunk and horny. Now his dick was going to get him killed. In Lenny's neighbourhood, the part of town you didn't go to unless your only alternative was death or prison, word always got around quickly. When One-Eyed Jack told him that the Big Man downtown found out who slept with his wife, Lenny felt like someone had punched him in the stomach and kicked him in the balls at the same time. He knew the Big Man would want him killed. He was funny like that. His wife was a Grade A tramp who earned her nickname "Black Widow". That bitch was poison. Lenny wasn't the first guy to screw her, and he wasn't the first guy she'd screwed back. Word on the street kept getting worse and worse. This time, the Big Man wanted an example made. He didn't go to his usual hitters. He spent the big bucks, the kind of money you can only get by being the Big Man. He called in Joe Fixit. Lenny threw up for two hours, dry-heaving the entire last hour. Joe Fixit wasn't his real name of course, but it was certainly applicable. In the comics, Joe Fixit was like the evil-Hulk: muscle for hire. Outside the comic world, Joe lived up to his namesake. Rumour had it that Fixit was a monster through and through. Tattoos covered his body from head to toe. Physically he was gigantic. His suits were all custom-made and expensive as Hell. Joe once tore off a man's hand after he cut the guy off and the man gave him the finger. What he did with the severed appendage afterward varied from story to story, but it always ended up with the one-handed idiot ingesting his own limb through one of his orifices. He'd overturned vehicles with his bare hands. Dislocated arms and legs by yanking them out of their sockets. He was a one-man wrecking crew. Savage, powerful, remorseless and he never gave up. Joe Fixit was the Terminator of the underworld, and now Joe was coming after poor Lenny. Lenny opened the refrigerator. Still empty. He was going to be forced to leave the apartment soon or he'd starve to death. Nobody would bring him food for fear of being caught in the crossfire. Going outside made him a huge target, and he could defend himself much better if he stayed inside. There were guns, knives, and locked doors, plenty of things Lenny could use to take down Fixit. Lenny picked up the bottle of pills from the counter and popped a couple into his mouth. He needed them to stay awake if he wanted a fighting chance against Joe. But being awake for four days straight was making him hallucinate. He was seeing huge shadows moving across the room, voices whispering in the dark. "Four days." Lenny propped himself against the wall. "Maybe there really is no Joe Fixit. People probably end up killing themselves waiting for him to show up." He laughed weakly and rubbed the butt of his gun. "That's not a bad idea." His heart jumped into his throat as Lenny heard the creak in the hallway. He definitely heard something that time. Lenny yanked the gun out and pointed it at the door. Was it Fixit? Was it his neighbour? Did it really matter? Another creak, this time right in front of his door. Wood splinters exploded from the two bullet holes Lenny put in the door. Seconds later, a loud thud against the wall across the hallway. "I got him," Lenny said in amazement. He rushed the door, gun outstretched. "I got you motherfucker! I got -" The doorframe gave way like wet paper as the door blew into the room. It slammed into Lenny, knocking the gun to the side. The door still blocked his view and Lenny pushed it aside. Mistake. A fist nearly the size of his head hit him squarely in the face. The crunch was much louder inside his head. Lenny hit the floor and tried to focus on the truck that had just run him over. As his eyes came into focus, the Devil stared down at him with emotionless eyes. He'd never seen a man that big, ever. Lenny noticed him adjusting the leather gloves on his hand. "Sap gloves," he thought to himself. "Powdered lead in the knuckles. So that's -" The gun. Joe Fixit was standing in front of him and Lenny still had his gun. He raised it and fired once into Fixit's chest. The big man staggered back, but didn't drop. Instead, he frowned and kicked the gun out of a stunned Lenny's grip. He stepped down on Lenny's right wrist and began stomping on Lenny's hand with the left. The fingers split and popped open like ripe plumbs under the incredible weight and force of Joe's foot. Lenny screamed himself hoarse and finally Joe lifted his foot off the wrist. If there had been any food in Lenny's stomach, the sight of the bloody ribbons flopping around at the end of what used to be his hand would have made him throw up. So lost in his agony, Lenny didn't even hear Joe mumble something about "needing a new Kevlar vest after this one." The glint of steel out of the corner of his eye got Lenny's attention as he tried to sit up. Joe had a pair of pliers in his hand. He looked into the Monster's cold eyes. "I hear you've got a problem keeping your dick under control," he said in an unnerving voice that sounded like he'd gargled with razor blades. He kicked Lenny in the chest, knocking him back down to the floor. Joe knelt down across his stomach and tore Lenny's pants open. Lenny begged him not to do it. He pleaded for mercy. He cried out for help. But as Joe adjusted his grip on the pliers, Lenny realized in horror it was over. The tortured, agonizing screams Lenny made filled the entire building, prompting more than one door to be locked. Across the street, One-Eyed Jack shook his head and disappeared into the alley. "Live by the dick, die by the dick," he muttered as he stepped into the darkness.
