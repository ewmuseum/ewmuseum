Seven Point One

4 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He had been pacing back and forth through his hotel room for hours. The note that he had found in his locker room earlier in the evening lay open on the bed. It was haunting his mind, he had tried to sleep, but in the darkness his eyes remained open staring at the ceiling. Eventually they'd wander their way towards the note that was in his bag. After trying to repeatedly shake it out of his mind he gave in, read it over and over again. It was only a few lines, and unsigned, but he knew exactly who it was from. And it was obviously bothering him in a different way. In the past few weeks, nearly a month he had been very unproductive outside of the entire wrestling domain. It wasn't just being lazy, but there was no desire to accomplish anything. Everything seemed to be going slowly back downhill. Not that it had ever started to go uphill, but it had reached a ‘happy' median and he was as pleased as he could be. But ever since the hotel room incident, he hadn't really talked to anyone, Jon had gone away from him and he was alone again. The note from earlier in the night had struck something deep inside of him. Nothing makes sense anymore. He thought to himself. After over a year of doing anything possible to avoid and ignore me... leaving me a note in my locker room. I.. I just don't understand it. What's going to happen? ... didn't even want to talk to me when I tried. Pretended I didn't exist. Maybe I don't. Maybe this is all a dream I'm wading through. There's no real point to any of it, really. Wandering forever, searching for something that I'll never find. I wish I just had someone here to talk to. I'm so cold, so alone... I can't take this anymore. First one eye, followed by the other opened. They winced at the brightness of the lights that were on. The room looked like a white box, no windows, no doors, just a white box. Walking around, he noticed that the ‘room' seemed to be limitless in length. A buzzing noise filled his ears and he turned around. Monte Burns stood across from him. It was too surreal for him. Burns stood across from him, smiling that greedy, insidious smile that he always wore on his face. He went to open his mouth to yell at Monte, but nothing came out. Silence. He just stared straight ahead towards the beaming Monte Burns. But another figure appeared. Black, nearly impossible to tell who or what the other figure was. Just an undefined outline of a person, standing beside Burns. The insincere grin on Burns' face grew larger and larger. Begging to be attacked. And that's what he did. He ran, charging towards Burns. He dove forward, but fell to the ground in a heap. He picked himself up and looked around. Again, he was alone in the white box. Monte Burns had been nothing more than a mirage or hologram, created by his mind. Where did it go? He asked himself, still in thought. It was right there. I dove... it was gone. Why doesn't anything make sense anymore? I'm so lost. Jon... where did you go? Why does everyone leave me? I don't understand. I just want someone to help me. I'm failing again.. failing and falling... help me. His eyes had failed him again. He woke up from inside of the hotel room. The note was still sitting on the dresser beside him. And everything before that had been a dream. He hadn't paced back and forth, but the note had been bothering him. In time, he believed that he and the world would find out what the note really meant. But for now, his tired eyes closed once more, as once again Sunday fell into the hands of Monday.
