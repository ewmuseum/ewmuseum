Finality, The Second Part Of

9 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everything was falling apart faster than Rei knew how to put things back together. Jessica was beaten, bloodied, and in no shape to continue on. Kali Saturos was locked away somewhere by a homicidal cult leader, thanks in part to Rei and Jessica. Rei was the only one left, and it scared her. She had always been here, on the inside, always in the background making sarcastic remarks while Jessica and Kali lived out their lives. Rei had no idea how to live- and as she began to think more and more, she had no idea why she even existed. She had no idea why any of them even existed. What were they? Rei was intelligent enough to realize that most beings had only one personality inside of them, not three. Were they insane? More and more signs pointed to the fact that yes, they might just be one insane little girl who has convinced herself she is in fact three different people. It didn't take much for Rei to realize that Jessica and Kali Saturos are very much insane. Kali is a psychopathic killer who more than likely killed their parents and was used by a cult leader and then thrown away when she was no longer any use to her. She was born out of the beatings they used to go through by their father when they still existed as one. Their father would rain down on them with powerful punches, calling them unclean and sinful. Rei knew that Kali represented their hatred for their father, and the violence they had to go through by his hands. Jessica was born out of the paradox they grew up with- a loving Christian family to the outside, and an abusive hell on the inside. She grew up with conflicting morals that made her into what she became- a slightly violent, warped version of a Christian fundamentalist. She had very clear goals when she entered the Fans Wrestling Organization in place of Kali Saturos, but she did not foresee interference from a true Christian who took offense to her words. She became engaged in a bitter rivalry with him until someone- or something- took her out of the picture. So now Rei was questioning herself, and her own existence. Is she insane? She did not believe herself to be insane, but does an insane person ever believe themself to be insane? Why was she created? What emotions did Rei take from the single being she was once a part of? Rei had many questions as she approached the temple on that fateful night. And all of them would be answered. It was the night of finality, both for Rei and for the being she once was a small, but vocal, part of. if that ugly state is how you're going to live then just shut your eyes under the winter sky Existence is Eternity
