Dearly Beloved.

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I had successfully robbed Lance Knight and Poison Ivy, and it sucked. …Lance Knight of the opportunity to be a champion in the fWo, and Ivy of the opportunity to add another championship under her list of managerial successes. I didn’t really understand what I had done, until the bell rang, and I watched Trialty walking up the ramp with the same tag belts I was so sure Lance and I would win. Sean Stevens stood in the rain, talking to no one in particular. But, if you told him that… he’d have swung a punch in your direction, because in all actuality… there was someone there… in spirit. The thunder roared loudly… but he didn’t flinch. He allowed the heavy drops to pounce on top of his head, unfazed… he needed no umbrella… the rain was cold, but refreshing… his ears swelled of redness due to the freezing Detroit temperatures, but they didn’t even hurt. He wasn’t concerned if someone heard him… or thought he was crazy for standing there like he did. He missed his best friend’s funeral, because he was a coward. To afraid to say ‘goodbye’. To paranoid to face reality. And, because of it… everyone he loved suffered. He stepped closer to the tombstone, and continued speaking. You always used to tell me how fucking stubborn I was. Funny thing, though… I was too stubborn to listen. Maybe I shouldn’t have had Ed Brown let you go… maybe you’d still be here. Yeah, you would, in fact… know it in my knower. I wouldn’t have allowed you to go to New York to visit a fucking physician when I have one of the best one’s money can buy. If I had just minded my own fucking business, you’d have never left the apartment, and everything would be just like it should’ve been. Like it used to be. He wiped a tear from his cheek Ivy misses you like crazy, too. She says Eli does, but we never really talked about it, all that much. I can’t, for the life of me, understand what that shit’s about, because you were just as much apart of his life, as I was. But, whatever… I’m not looking for Eli Flair’s acceptance, y’know? Never did, either. He paused in thought. But, you know what irks me about the whole situation, Luck? He’s running around here playing mediator for the rest of the Rat Pack – I’ll explain them later – being father figures to people like Lance Knight, helping him through his little issues, and here I am, supposed to be his friend… and, he doesn’t even check to see if I’m okay. Fucked up, ain’t it? I don’t hold any resentment, really… I just think it’s kind of crazy for him to expect me to be there for him when he has issues, but doesn’t do the same for me. I guess the world revolves around Eli Flair in his head. …or maybe I’m being too hard. Maybe he thinks Ivy doesn’t need any outside help in dealing with me. I don’t know, but we haven’t talked… so how would I? I just wonder how he would've felt if I told him I didn't give a fuck that he was getting politicked out of the fWo title. Oh, and he and Angel had a kid. Married, too. That kid is gonna be spoiled as hell. He’s got two uncles, Brandon, and Lance, and one ‘special’ uncle that she loves the best – take a wild guess as to who that is. She’s got Amy, Ivy, and all the members of Valerian’s Garden. Not to mention two pretty cool parents, with no fucking idea what they’re doing. It’s a funny situation, but you know… that could also be where Eli’s head is at. Again… I don’t know. Sean sat there for a bit, just watching. Like he wanted to say something, but couldn’t get it out. Luck… I’m sorry for skipping your funeral, man. You dying, was like… me dying. I tried to get through it, y’know… to make the pain go away. I tried to turn to work… to cut everyone off… and, I thought not seeing you in a casket would only help more. I fucked up, and I wish I could go back, because I’ve realized – after talking to Brand the other day – that, the pain probably will *never* go away, and that’s something I’ve gotta accept. I have to learn how to deal with it, and that’s something I think I can do, even if soaking my body in it, was easier than fighting. But, I got to thinking on the plane ride out here, me living life the way I’ve lived it the past month doesn’t represent you. And, that’s what I want to do, Luck. Represent who you were… who we were. And, what we stood for. If you were wondering, the name “Outlawz” is dead. The Outlawz was Triple X and Lucky and nobody else. And, also… on your birthday, I put this on my life, man… I will never miss a birthday. And, your mom… I’ll always visit… I’ll always make myself available to your family, because your family is my family. And, I’ll make sure my kids know about their uncle Shannon – yeah, Ivy and I talked about having a kid the other day. Maybe it was because we were in the moment, after seeing MJ, I don’t know. I’ll keep you posted. But, I’m about to leave now. Thunder, Lightning, Loud Noise. But, I want you to know that I love you and I miss you, man… and, life won’t be the same without you. As Sean turned his back to the tombstone, he heard something… Lightning bolts had struck and you could see them in the air, but it looked like one, spelled out… something. I Love you, he mumbled to himself, attempting to read it. Nah, can’t be. Could it? But, no matter what… Sean knew. And, that’s what counted the most.
