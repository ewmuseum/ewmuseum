Beach Fan(the introduction)

6 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
San Diego, California Sean had only been in San Diego for nearly two days, and already he was sick of it. Not that it wasn't beautiful - it was. Not that the weather wasn't absolutely, positively splendid - because, that too was marvelous in his eyes. It was just that, it was all a little ... too beautiful. Normally, in a place like this, on a day like this, he'd be following his - then - girlfriend, Poison Ivy around. ...she'd be working as usual, and he'd be spending his day trying to get her to stop. Here he was, twenty- eight, single, in one of the most beautiful cities in the world, with some of the most beautiful people, walking around on beaches, sitting underneath palm trees, surfing ... and, he was feeling sorry for himself. Life certainly could be much worse, and while, he really did miss Poison Ivy, he came to the sudden realization that he had to move on. She probably already did, he thought. ...before pausing to ease the nauseating feeling at the mere thought of her being with somebody else. It wasn't that he wanted her back, he didn't. They broke up for a reason, and if they rushed back together just because they were feeling a little lonely, there was a very good chance that they'd be in the position of breaking up again very soon. ...or atleast that's what he told himself daily. You know what? To hell with this... He mumbled as he grabbed a towel from his hotel bathroom and headed for the beach directly outside, to the left of the hotel he was staying in. The sand was incredible ... it was pearl white, and unlike the sand in Orlando, that could sometimes be very unforgiving, it soothed his toes, with a warmth that he hadn't experienced in a very long time. He decided that the very least he could do was enjoy himself, so he sat the towel down, pulled out a pair of shades from his pocket, and put them on. He took his wife beater off, in order to tan, while he laid back in an effort to rest. "Hey!" He heard a voice call out. Sean didn't sit up, there were a lot of people on the beach, that person couldn't have been talking to him. "Aren't you, Triple X? ...The wrestler?!" The enthusiastic young female voice came closer and closer. "That'd be me," he said, eyes still closed. "What can I help you with?" He sat up, opening his eyes, immediately noticing the knockout standing before him which made him suddenly think of the Beach Boys' song "California Girls" for some very ... strange ... reason. He extended his hand. "I'm so sorry to interrupt you, I know rest is something you don't get too much of, but I'm one of - if not your biggest fan - and, I love you!" She was shaking. "That's very flattering, I didn't catch your name--" "It's Carla ... Carla Lee." "Call me Sean, Carla ... and, it's a pleasure to meet you. I enjoy meeting fans up close as long as it's not anything too crazy, y'know?" He laughed. "What is your definition of crazy? Like ... a fairly attractive fan - who's not a groupie, and won't promise you sex, that's followed your career since the XWL asking you out on a date?" Stevens swallowed hard, he wasn't expecting that. "I'm flattered, Carla ... really I am ... but, I'm in a relation--" He paused mid-sentence. ...before shaking his head unbelievably. "Wow ... being together with someone for two years will do that to you. I won't lie to you, Carla ... I just got out of a relationship with somebody, who I'm still madly in love with--" "--Poison Ivy, I know," she chuckled. "And, I'm not trying to take you away from her, or change your feelings for her. I've just dreamed about this day, and I feel like if I let this opportunity pass, I'll regret it. How many times does a girl meet up with the famous guy she's had a crush on for seven years?" Sean smiled. "You live around here?" "I'm a senior at San Diego State, my apartment is right down the street." "Give me your address, I'll pick you up in my rental, after I'm done tonight ... you'll be watching the show, right?" "I never miss an fWo show ... of course I'll be watching!" "Well, I'll do you one better ... if you want, I can get you a couple of tickets, so you and your friends can watch from the front row." She was on the verge of hypeventilating. "Would you?!? That is so nice of you. And, we'll still go on the date?" He nodded. "You got it." "Okay, let me go and grab a pen, so we can know where to meet each other. Thank you so, so much." This time, she extended her hand, and as he excepted it, she pulled him in for a hug. Nothing sexual, just sweet, he thought. Sean hadn't been out in months, ever since stepping away from the Rat Pack, and breaking up with Ivy, things began to look bleak in that area of his life. Sure he had no plans of being with this girl ... he still had it bad for Poison Ivy, and it would take months to get over her. But, atleast he'd be able to have fun... that's what he needed in his life most... To be able to enjoy it.
