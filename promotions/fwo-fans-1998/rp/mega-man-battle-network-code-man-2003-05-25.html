Mega Man Battle Network: Code Man

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Codemaster. Words don't really describe the guy, because let's face it: It's better to describe him in sentences! For instance, "Boy, the Codemaster is one cool cat." would describe him pretty well. Also, "Geewillie, Willis, that there Codemaster gets himself into some CRA-AZAY antics!" would be another good description. Now, what would NOT describe him would be the sentence "Codemaster is the stupidest, most RE_TAR_DED dildo of corn in tha history of America.". Then again, that's what we get for asking James Cagle something while we were quite drunk. So, anyway, we're apparently in a large lab for some reason. This lab had all of the latest technologies, and even some technologies that some idiot happened to pull out of a time machine some time ago. Several half-finished robot designs were locked inside watery tubes and were floating about like victims of "America's Funniest Dismemberings". Now, why brother like the Codemaster would be in a place like this, or why a show like "American's Funniest Dismemberings" never made it on the air are two completely different dobermans. The point is that the Codemaster was here. Anyway, for some reason, the Codemaster was with some big fat jolly white guy with a big jolly beard. He was the type of guy who'd be mistaken for Santa Claus in a heartbeat. He wore a big white lab suit, because... you know, he was fat. He needed a big suit. Anyway, he was Dr. Light, and he was rather frantic. "Code Man! It's terrible! Dr. Wily has stolen all of my new robots and is using them for direly evil things!" The Codemaster was confused, "Code Man? You just called me Code Man. Why did you call me Code Man?" "It doesn't matter! Dr. Wily's taking over! I mean, he even captured Mega Man! We need your help!" Dr. Light pleaded. "Why me? I'm not even a robot, suckah. I'm just a brother with a big-ass Taurus 44 Mag with a six-point-five inch barrel and fully adjustable sides." the Codemaster said, holding up said big-ass gun. "Where the hell did you get that?" Dr. Light asked. "I dunno." "Well, it doesn't matter. I guess I'm going to have to send you off to fight all eight robot masters. Now, how I'm going to do this is rather complicated, and it'll involve hundreds and hundreds of evil robots all aiming to kill you many times over, jumping over dangerous chasms, and avoiding large lines of spike traps that can kill you even if you happen to stub your toe on them." Dr. Light said. There was an awkward, terrible silence. "You're kidding, right?" "No, I'm not." Dr. Light said. "Well, don't I get anything COOL? I mean, you're sending me against robots here. I mean, I got mean, evil, spiteful robots who want nothing more than to wear my head as a hat on my case now. What's a brother to do in that case?" the Codemaster inquired. "Oh, I've got just the thing!" Dr. Light said, before he walked offcamera. "Okay, good, `cause, you know, I was gettin' worried for a sec--" Dr. Light came back with a mechanical red dog. "Meet Rush. He'll aid you." The Codemaster blinked. He looked down at Rush. Then he looked back up at Dr. Light. Then he looked down at Rush. Then back at Dr. Light, before he spoke again, "You're a crackpot, Doc." "No, seriously! He turns into a jet board that you can ride." "Why can't he turn into a giant dog cannon so I can blast things with great proficency? I mean, what actual things does this guy do aside from turn into a jetboard? Can't I just, like, walk?" There was an awkward pause. "...Just go shoot stuff, Code Man."
