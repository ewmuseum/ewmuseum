The new millennium- my time.

1 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization The new millennium- my time. January 1, 2000 [The scene- Havok's giant hotel room. Garbage, confetti, and other things are all over the floor and furniture. All of his party guests have gone home, but Havok's still there. He's wearing his wrestling tights, a white tee-shirt, and has the Cruiserweight Title belt in his hands. He is grinning and nodding, and seems very happy.] Last night, the fWo was saved by my partner in the Millennium Men, Black Panther. Thanks, Panther. But he wasn't alone, we all united together to defeat David Spencer, and we succeeded. But now, with this uniting stuff over, I'm going to turn my focus to the two men who want *my* Cruiserweight Title... Courage and Wretch. First, Wretch. Chris Kidman is a great friend of mine, and two nights ago, Wretch and I went to a double count-out. We agreed that this wasn't over. And I will be glad to give Wretch a rematch anytime that he wants. Now, onto Courage. I don't like this guy at all. I mean, he comes out here, and says he's gonna take my belt. I don't think so. Listen Courage, you set the date, ladder match at Invasion. It's pretty safe to say you've made your own bed, and now you're gonna lay in it, because at Courage, I'm gonna kick your ass all over that arena. But, let's make it a little more interesting, huh? Wretch, why don't you join us? Let's have a three-way ladder match for the Cruiserweight Title at Invasion, and we'll find out who the best Cruiserweight in the fWo is. I'll see you guys at Invasion. And you'll be just the first victims this year, because it's 2000. It's the new millennium. It's the time of Havok, and nothing can stop me this year. [He walks up, and over towards the door. He turns around towards the camera.] Oh, and Panther, buddy, I'll be expecting a title shot.... [He chuckles, then walks off as the camera cuts to a promo for Warfare.]
