Untouchable // Part.3 // See No Evil

8 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The bitter December wind blew in his face, stinging his eyes and chilling him to the core. He zipped his jacket to his chin. He walked the darken streets without giving thought to where he was going or why. His focus was on what had taken place last Sunday. He had fought and bled and almost died to get his shot at Kenny Rock, and in the blink of an eye, he had given it away. For, as much as he wanted to put Kenny in his place once and for all, there was one who wanted it ten times more. Steve Douglas. And, after all the pain and torture A.D. had put the man through, didn't he owe him something? A.D. just hoped that, after this, Steve may finally be able to fully forgive him. ************************** He hadn't heard anything until he rounded the corner of the building. As he did, he was greated by the sound of men and woman and chaos all standing in the hospital parking lot, talking, smoking, and letting the pre-Christmas cold seep into their bones. Police cars were scattered amongst the doctors and nurses and patients, bathing them in flashing red and blue light. "Steve has to be involved in this somehow" The thought flew from his head as quickly as it came. Quickly, he was running through the crowds, searching beyond the stiff, smoking bodies for any signs of his friend. That's when he saw him. It hit him like a punch in the stomach. At first, he was caught off guard, but, after a moments realization, he wasn't surprised at all. Kenny Rock. He was leaning against a police car, with his back to A.D. He was talking to a police officer. Slowly, A.D. approached, careful not to give the cop the wrong idea. Finally, he reached Kenny and put his hand on his shoulder. A.D. could feel the nerves and anger and the need for answers welling up inside of him. He had prepared himself for everything. Everything, except for what he got. Kenny was crying. Once he layed eyes on A.D., Kenny broke down again, throwing his arms around him and sobbing into his neck. The cop smiled a solumn smile at Kenny. "Thank you, Mr. Rock. I think I've got all I need." And with that, he turned and walked away. "Oh, Angel", Kenny sobbed. "I'm so glad you're alive. I'm worried about Steve. I can't believe he would have done something like this. I just can't...." Kenny turned, and watched the cop walk away. Once he rounded the corner, and left his view, Kenny turned back to A.D. "He's fucked, you know" Kenny said with a smile. "And there's not a damn thing you, or anyone else, can do about it". He looked A.D. in the eye, and, for the first time in weeks, A.D. was afraid of him. "That's typical of you, Angel", Kenny said as he walked away. "You've got the worst timing of anyone I've ever met". Kenny continued to walk away, without turning back. A.D. let him walk. He knew that, right now, he had more important matters to deal with.
