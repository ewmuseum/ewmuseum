Beach Fan(the warrant)

7 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
San Diego, California August 6, 2003 12pm ----- "Did you hear about our latest complaint filed?" Officer Ryan Adelman had been an officer on the force for nearly ten years, and was considered one of the few good cops left on the San Diego Police Department's 'Special Forces Unit'. He was offered a desk job, and even got a couple of detective opportunities that he passed up, in order to remain on the streets in uniform. There was something about his job that he loved ... and, he was determined not to leave until he was good and ready. "Yeah ... a young woman, twenty-two. Says she accompanied a guy to a bar on a casual date. Come to find out, this sleezebag beats her, then forces himself on her," the young cop sitting in Ryan's chair, sipping from a cup of coffee replied. The San Diego department loved Ryan's work ethic. Many drug dealer's were serving long sentences because of his dedication and willingness to see a case to it's successful end. If only we could train everybody to be as good an officer as Adelman, Police Chief Arnold L. Ramsey would always say out loud, to nobody in particular, before deciding to make Ryan the force's top trainer. In all actuality, it was like killing two birds with ... one stone. By having Adelman train, he'd stay off the streets and out of the cases that would have him in harm's way - a lot of the well known drug dealers knew Ryan's face, and there was a rumored hit out on him - and he would be able to teach some of these younger, hot shots what it takes to be a real officer of the law. It's brilliant, he thought ... and, within two weeks, Adelman was not only off the streets, he was forced to tend to a bunch of hot-headed brats. "Did you do your homework on the guy we're arresting?" "No ... not yet. ...should I?" "It helps," Adelman responded, rolling his eyes. "Because if you did, you'd understand the importance of what we're about to do ... about what you're about to experience. This has the potential to be big, and the department chose you to make the arrest with me! ...you're not even a little excited?" The young officer got out of Adelman's seat in a hurry, making sure to stand up straight, rearranging his face to show atleast modest excitement. "Ahh ... yeah, of course I am! It's just that, after the successful stint at the academy, and how fast they put me on the streets ... it wasn't a matter of how many years it'd take me to get the big busts ... no disrespect, but to me, it was a matter of how many days." Ryan sighed, shaking his head, as he placed his arm around the rookie officer's head, placing him in a headlock. "You've got a lot to learn, kiddo. That whole arrogant thing you got going on? ...It'll get you killed. No drug dealer is gonna take your police academy scores into consideration when he's pulling that trigger. You've gotta do your homework, case your surroundings ... you've gotta be one step ahead of the bad guys, or they'll be one step ahead of you. Remember that, kid ... I won't always be here to have your back." "Okay, okay ... give me the paper work on this guy." "No time. ...we're going to get him tonight." "Tonight? Where ... when?" Adelman smiled, "Oh yeah, that's right ... you don't know, do you? That guy who raped that poor young girl ... he's a wrestler." The rookie swallowed hard, "Like ... the one's on television?" "Yep ... I don't watch that bullshit, but from what I understand, he's one of the big boys, too." "This should be fun..." Adelman was quick to respond, "Indeed. ...get your gear ready, meet me in the lobby in an hour, we need to get a couple of plane tickets, and a warrant for the bastard's arrest." The shit has officially hit the fan.
