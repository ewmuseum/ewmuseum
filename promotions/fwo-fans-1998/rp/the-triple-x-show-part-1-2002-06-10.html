The Triple X Show(part 1)

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"It's funny..." Sean "Triple X" Stevens sat in front of a plain black backdrop, clad in his "workout" gear, consisting of grey sweat shorts, a sleeveless, white t- shirt, wrestling boots, and taped fists. He was staring directly into the camera, eyes trained. His intensity was off the page... his focus... made you the viewer a little uneasy. ...but, you watched anyway... "It's funny how all of these fWo superstars are coming out here proclaiming their dominance... running on and on about what they're gonna do to whom. Quite frankly, I could care less what Brand Frontier wants to do to Ric Chronos... or how Vic Creed plans on proving to the world how bad he SUPPOSEDLY is, by defeating Eric Kelly. And, to be completely honest... "... I don't even give a crap about Eli Flair's matchup with Grendel." His hair was considerably longer than it was when last saw on television. Then... it was closer to shoulder length... now... it was hanging just below it. From his training session, only moments earlier... it was still soaked with sweat... he removed it from the front of his face, and ran his fingers through it, as he continued on. "What I'm concerned about is the Internet Title tournament, that everybody apparently forgot *I* was in. Otherwise, why would Ruben Ross be considered a favorite? Because of what he's done here? Please. "Ross, has done what he's done in the fWo because he was prepared for worse, and he didn't get it. Who prepared him? ME. I was the guy making his punk a## famous in another promotion that shall remain nameless. I was the one who took him to levels he had never been, and hasn't been since. Ruben Ross... you think you're a favorite? Heh... I think you're a walking XXXstasy statistic. And, if by chance we meet in this tournament... I'd me MORE than happy to prove it." Sean sat there for a moment... gathering his words... thinking about everything he just said. Before looking up... with a confident smirk. "And, it doesn't end with you Ruben. I'll beat anybody and everybody, just to prove to each and every fan in attendance that I truly am the best. I'll beat Ric Chronos... "I'll prove to Chronos that just like Primetime... Frenchie... and, the other fWo champions that thought their accomplishments meant anything to me other than a credible victory... that he's wrong. I've seen it all... done it all... beat them all. From Eli Flair to Zero the Hero himself. "Unleashed is here, guys... the time for talking is up. I'm happy I get another opportunity to show you guys just how inferior you are. So while everybody is talking about how they're so Unleashed now... how they'll do this, do that... win this, win that. Be reborn here or there... "... I'd just like to welcome everyone to The Triple X Show. But, you might not like what you see." The camera faded out to an fWo Unleashed commercial. Then to black.
