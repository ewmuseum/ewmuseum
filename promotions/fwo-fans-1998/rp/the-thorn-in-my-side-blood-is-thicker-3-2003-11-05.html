The Thorn In My Side (Blood Is Thicker #3)

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Originally posted under the title "Something From Nothing" during fWo's Survivor 3.) "What're you gonna do now, suck your thumb? Or call for Mommy Elliot?" The digs kept coming, each one being accompanied by hoots of laughter from the other wrestlers. Thomas was the only one not finding it funny, as a tear slowly descended his right cheek... More ammunition for the bitter Greg Hoffman. "Baby wanna cry?" mocked Greg as he put on a dumbed-down voice, "Stupid fuckin' retard." Everyone has a breaking point, and Thomas had passed his. Encircled by the hooting audience, Hoffman took great delight in performing for them at Thomas' expense. Threatened and upset, Thomas reacted in the only way he could. "Hey, wha-" Hoffman didn't even have time to question the charge of Thomas, as his satisfied grin quickly dissipated into a contorted expression of shock. He couldn't move, his feet rooted, as the giant hands of Thomas Harper clamped around his neck... This grip was vice-like. "Get this fuckin' retard off of me!" Greg wheezed as he tried to peel back Thomas' rigid fingers. Connected by the neck, Thomas pushed the wrestler back against the wall where with seemingly no effort exerted, Hoffman was lifted a good two feet off the ground. "I'm sorry... I'm sorry..." Greg croaked, his airway constricted. The men who just a few seconds ago were laughing at the methodical mocking of the Harper brother, now tried to pull him away from Hoffman, which wasn't an easy task. "You're mean to my brother, and you're mean to Thomas!" Two men were on each arm, desperately trying to pull them away from Hoffman's throat. As Greg tried to breathe, there was a whistling sound as the small amounts his lungs were getting passed through the constricted airway. "Buddy, he's sorry!" said one of the men, trying to appease the enraged Thomas Harper. "Look, he'll apologise!" "He didn't mean it, man... It was a joke!" Their words had less effect than their physical attempts, as 5 of them were then pulling away at Thomas' large frame. "They're right... I was just..." Hoffman appeased in a breathless croak, "...Joking..." Then, Elliot returned. "What the..." exclaimed Elliot as he ran to see what the commotion was, only to be faced with the sight of his own brother choking the life out of his #1 talent. "Thomas! Let him go! Now!" Elliot demanded, trying to get through to his larger, younger brother. "But he was..." appealed Thomas, tears welling up in his eyes, "...He was mean!" "Let him go!" roared Elliot once more, his harsh words devoid of any love or fondness. But before Thomas' resolve could falter, he was finally pulled off, toppling backwards and taking three of the men with him. Hoffman dropped down to the ground and grabbed his throat. Heaving air into his lungs to get the purple pigment from his face, he spoke as soon as he was able to. "You're fuckin' finished, Harper..."
