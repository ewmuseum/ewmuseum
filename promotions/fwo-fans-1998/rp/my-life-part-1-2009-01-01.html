My Life - Part 1

1 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The year is 2007. Christmas Eve. 1937 hours. It has been rough since the fWo closed its doors. Thanks to Neo and the arson fantasy he wanted ensure came to fruition, finding a job in wrestling has become a very difficult task. Not only did the fWo close its doors, but the entire staff and its performers were forever tarnished with the reputation of “oh, you are coming from *that* promotion”. It has been awhile since I ventured out into the public and tonight being Christmas Eve…well, I could sure use a drink. I pass by McSorley’s which in NYC is one of the oldest bars still around. I think they said it opened its doors in 1862 and only allowed men to converse there until 1970. McSorley’s, my kind of Irish bar. “How you doin’ son?” the barkeep says to me. “Names Clinton, Clinton Coldren. Pleased to meet you.” I don’t understand why some people feel the need to do the stupid James Bond introduction. “Fantastic Clinton. Why don’t you get me a jack and coke, double, with little ice.” He makes my drink and hands it to me. “Wanna run a tab?” “Yeah, why not. I think I’ll be here for most of this shitty night anyways.” After thinking about it, he might as well give me the bottle. I can tell the barkeep has something on his mind as he continues to dry off glasses he just cleaned. He has that look on his face that he’s seen me before. Here it comes… “I’ve seen you before haven’t I?” I sip my drink. “This is my first time in this joint pal. Likely you haven’t.” “No no, I’ve seen you before.” He persists. “Yeah, that’s it…” he says with a smile. “Graphic Violence from the fWo! Man, I used to be a big fan…” “Used to huh?”. I don’t really want to entertain this idiot’s conversation but then again he’s pouring my drinks. So I might as well. “Well, I mean you were awesome. Hardcore champion, fWo wrestler of the year and then BAM!” Bam? Christ… “The fWo was no longer around. I was a big fan of you son. It’s a shame what happened to that promotion. Why haven’t you turned up anywhere else? I thought you would have been a major free agent to pick up.” Ah. The million dollar question everyone wants to know. I explain to him that what happened with the fWo, the riot and the promotion crumbling to the ground, caused a massive ripple effect. Not only were the owners screwed from any future transactions, but the wrestlers were tainted as well. Everywhere I went to do get a job, the door was closed immediately on my face. I was one of the biggest superstars in the wrestling business at that time; and the door was being closed on *my* face. “That’s rough son. Here, next drinks on me.” “Thanks Clinton, I appreciate that.” I’m about to do something I normally don’t and blame it on the alcohol. Open up. Why not right? After all it is the holidays. “I’ve been trying to get back into the business for the past year, but no one will take me due to the fWo mishap. I’ve lost the desire to be in the ring and found solace in this bottle of jack daniels you poured me. The only company to come knock on my door has been the Army again, which I am contemplating re-joining at this point.” “Sounds like you need a purpose. The fire is not burning inside that body of yours huh?” He was right. “Yeah, that is basically right. The Army gave me purpose and got my blood flowing. I never felt so alive than when I was being shot and maneuvering my team from cover to cover. Ah, those were good times.” I down my drink now and whip my chin. “I want to feel alive again.” I look down at my glass and then to the right where I see a bum passed out from drinking too much in a booth. “I don’t want to be like that guy a year from now. They way my life is going, I’m going to be like that jackass and that is not what I want.” What the fuck is wrong with me? Why the hell am I telling this barkeep Clinton Coldren my life story? Snap out of it Steve, you god damn pussy! “Alive huh?” the old man says. “I’ve got something going on that will make you feel the way you desire without the risk of death. The down side, it pays nothing.” He smiles and ask me “So what do you want Mr. Shadows? To feel alive, or get paid tons of money to pretend?” At first I wanted to rip his head off. Immediate anger came over me when he said I was a person of pretend. Who the fuck did this guy think he was? I was a combat veteran. Wrestling was a means of making money that worked out very well for me but this asshole had some balls. I got up out of my seat with every intention to punch this asshole right in his jaw…but then I looked to my right again. There he sat, with long hair draped over his face and wearing a long trench coat. A bum in a bar, drowning his sorrows from a glass of alcohol. Something I was doing right now. As I thought about it, the barkeeps offer intrigued me. I told him “I need a change, otherwise I’m going to end up like him. Face down in a bottle and passed out.” He smiled. “Well then Mr. Shadows, how about we get that blood pumping again and see if you can really live up to that name the wrestling industry gave you. Graphic Violence.” He put the glass down his was cleaning and said “follow me if you want to find out what I’m talkin’ about.” He opened a door that apparently led to a basement. I followed as the old man definitely caught my interest. What I saw before me was out of a movie. I smirked and gave a light chuckle. “You’ve got to be kidding me Clinton.” He looked back at me, no longer the nice barkeep that worked upstairs, and with a straight face I haven’t seen in a long time he said… “Does it look like I’m fucking kidding to you?”
