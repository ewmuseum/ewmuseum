Tim walked away holding his head in shame. No one even knew who he was, how would he ever make it in the business of wrestling? That night, Tim was last seen exiting the arena shedding a tear, what was left for Tim? He had no more pride, he had no respect from anyone, he had no money, and no job. It was a nice cool day late July of 2002. Tim hadn't been out of the wrestling business the long yet, but had almost already forgotten about wrestling all together. Tim had moved in with an old friend of his who he met at the ring when he was training to become a professional wrestler. "So Tim, you think you'll ever be back in the ring again?" His friend questioned Tim, Tim looked up from his position on the chair to reply. "Do I need to answer that Skylar? I really don't think wrestling was my thing." Skylar handed Tim a coke to sip on. "Eh I guess we'll never know." Tim shot back at Skylar before he finished his answer. "Do you think you'll ever go back?" Skylar put down his drink as Tim could feel the mood drastically change. "We don't need to go into that." Tim took a sip of his coke before setting it down on a coffee table. "You're right, I'm sorry for bringing it up." Skylar grabbed a spot on the couch and turned on the television and started to surf through the channels on cable. Skylar came across some news as it showed some protestors on the TV. "Leave it here" Tim demanded, and Skylar respectably listened to Tim's demands. "You know Tim, I hate protestors, and they're a waste of space on this earth." Skylar took another sip of his coke and reclined his feet back on the couch. Tim looked over at Skylar and grabbed his coke off of the coffee table. "I totally agree."
