fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Arrival. December 24, 1999 :::The scene opens up on a bright, sunny Chicago day. Birds are chirping, people are smiling, the whole bit. Suddenly, the birds scatter and a large black hulk blots out the sun, as we pull back and see that in fact, the large hulk is the Humvee of Ice, shot from road level. The Humvee continues on its way, and as we pull back even more, we can see that this is the parking lot of the United Center, site of both CyberSlam II and Twilight. The large black vehicle pulls to a stop in front of the building, and the camera approaches as the driver's side door swings open, and out steps Ice, in leather jacket and jeans with a duffel bag. It suddenly becomes clear that the camera operator is Geoff Green, as he asks-::: Geoff Green: Ice! Ice! A word! Please? Ice: Green, get the hell away from me. :::He's serious, and focused- we can see it in his eyes, as can Geoff, who backs off a few generous steps. Still, being an intrepid fWo reporter slash cameraman, he continues as Ice begins to walk into the building.::: Geoff: Are you sure? Ice: Yes. Geoff: Do you have any thoughts at all on your matches with Scott Slugger, and, if you get past him, the Flying Frenchie? Ice: Yeah, just one. They can both rest assured that it will NOT be a happy holiday season for either of them once I get my hands on them. That goes double for Slugger. :::Ice stops at the door of the arena, turning towards Geoff and the camera.::: And Scott? This ain't the end of it. Mark my words, I will get a piece of your ass in the very near future, no matter who your boiler room boy is. Now, Geoff, the time for talking is over. It's time to let my fists- :::Ice pounds one of said into a palm.::: - do the talking. :::Ice continues into the arena, slamming the door behind him.::: Geoff: There you have it, folks, Ice as intense and furious as ever. And folks, I would NOT want to be in Scott Slugger OR the Flying Frenchie's shoes, tonight. :::The screen slowly begins to fade to this white text on black background- ICE... SCOTT SLUGGER... FLYING FRENCHIE... THREE MEN...ONE NIGHT...ONE HELL OF AN END TO THE YEAR! TWILIGHT '99, ONLY ON PAY-PER-VIEW:::
