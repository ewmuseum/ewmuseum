Testing the Ropes

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Gund Arena. Joey was tightening the turnbuckle. The rest of the crew had stepped out for coffee, but Joey opted to stay behind. He was new on the ring crew and wanted to make sure that he did a good job. How would it look if Tempest climbed to the top rope, only to have the turnbuckle give way? As he finished tightening the bracket, Joey had the feeling that he was being watched. He looked to either side, seeing only rows upon rows of empty seats. What a rush it must be, Joey thought to himself, to wrestle in front of all those people. "It's nothing special," a voice said from behind him. Joey spun around, startled. Standing in the ring with him was the number one contender for the World Title, Kodiak Vic Creed. "Mister Creed!" he said. "I didn't hear you come into the ring." "Yeah, I know." Creed's face was emotionless. "Quiet like the dead." Joey suddenly felt very uncomfortable. "So, uh, what brings you here so early?" "Getting a feel for the ring." Vic took two quick steps back, bouncing hard into the ropes. He sprung forward, approaching Joey at a rapid pace. Too scared to move, he watched the big Alaskan stop just short of him. "Ropes feel good." "Th-thanks," Joey stuttered. Creed pulled his sunglasses down, staring hard at the young man. "Do I make you nervous?" Joey weighted his answer. "You just seem like you're in a bad mood. You've been pretty quiet since the house shows started." "Chris Universal hired you, right?" asked Creed. "Well let me tell you something about him. He's a two-faced bastard. He told me I'd have more time to spend with my daughter if I wrestled exclusively for the fWo. Now I'm spending more time away than I was before, all because the fWo feels the need to pander to the sheep." "Wow," Joey said." That sucks. But at least you're getting World Title shots." "That's probably the only thing keeping me from unscrewing Universal's head from his shoulders," grumbled Creed as he slapped the turnbuckle. "The fact that I get to beat up Erik Kelly on a regular basis. I've been the number one threat to the World Title since I joined the fWo. Now it's time to pick up my belt, at the expense of Kelly's health and well being." Joey thought for a moment. "You get to beat up Brand Frontier as well. That should be a bonus for you." Vic stopped his examination of the ring and turned to the young man. "What do you know about it? You have no idea what it's like for me to get my hands on Brand Frontier. We've dance from federation to federation, and it's a dance that's never ended. Until now. It's going to end between us in the fWo. I'm going to deal with Kelly... then I'm going to end Brand Frontier." The mere mention of the fWo Survivor seemed to enrage the Alaskan Juggernaut. Joey began to wish he'd left with the others. Creed was looking at him oddly, like he'd offended the former Hardcore and Internet Champion. "I... I'm sorry," Joey said, unsure why he was apologizing but doing it anyway. Creed slipped his sunglasses back on. "Yeah..." He stepped through the ropes, wandered down the stairs, and slowly walked up the aisle. Joey quickly cleaned up his tools and slipped them under the ring. "That guy is one World Champion weirdo," he said as he hustled away from ringside. "This is definitely the last time I work by myself."
