Be Not Afraid

4 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cabbot Wilson could almost feel eyes on him as he waited by the airport's luggage carousel. His client, Kodiak Vic Creed, had just arrived on the last Air Alaska flight of the evening. Now it was just a matter of gathering up the big man and getting him back to the hotel without incident. "You should have stayed gone," Wilson muttered to himself. "You should never have come back." People waited anxiously for family and friends to come down the escalator, their hearts beating faster once they saw that excited wave from the person they'd been waiting for. Cabbot looked more like he was waiting for a corpse to arrive. Part of him believed that he was. He'd been lost in his own thoughts and missed Creed coming down the stairs. "Hey Cabbot," he said with a smile as he approached his agent. Vic looked at Cabbot's long face. "What's wrong with you? You look like someone pissed on your portfolio." "It's Shadows. You got my email, right?" Vic walked up to the luggage belt and waited for his suitcase to appear. "Yeah, I got it. He's pissed off because I tore up his boyfriend's picture. Boo Hyphen Hoo." "Jesus Vic." Cabbot lowered his voice. "He wants to kill you." "Yeah, him and about a hundred other people. Watch for my bag." "Vic, I think he's really serious." "So am I."
