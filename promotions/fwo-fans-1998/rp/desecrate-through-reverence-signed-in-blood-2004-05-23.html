Desecrate Through Reverence: Signed in Blood

23 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
They called a few minutes ago to schedule another photo shoot, I looked too happy last time. Disney has Claire to be the perky one, I'm supposed to know the part I play and say nothing more. If only they knew. I'll wear studded bracelets and give the lens a polished middle finger and hey, they might even hand me a skateboard. Anything to keep up appearances. I'd like to take a picture right now and float it over to the fWo fan forum. They'd still call me cute, even through the black eyeliner tears. Is it my personality? Of course not. They don't know what that even IS. I don't even win matches, but I'm both adored and absolutely loathed thanks in no small part to two pert and ample reasons...both currently residing on my breastbone chest. Is that it, or is that the excuse? Maybe some see what I am. They complain. Those who hate me will find anything... I'm inconsistent because I have a seemingly perfect life, but I'm angsty on TV. Hell, I'm on TV...that in itself is enough for them to despise. They don't even know what's really here to hate. I wish they'd loathe what I do...I might feel justified. But now what? I thought I was untouchable. I've never been one to put up a front, but I could always just smile in people's faces and excuse it. Not with my past walking into fWo. People didn't have to remember me. I was nothing before. I'm nothing all over again...but I'll still be plastered on the insides of their eyelids. Everything was easier before this place. I could walk outside without being recognized or told by some little kid that I'm a lot uglier than I look on TV or the site, just because I went out without feeling like splashing on some makeup. I wasn't a marred accessory to a super-hero. I was miserable and drugged and hated everything just because I could. But I was small-time invisible. This is the first time during the near two years here that I've thought about this, and it's made me realize that I liked it that way. I don't want to have to face people with everything I am...I want them to see the personification of a perfectly imperfect image - the girl with the tattoos, bad attitude and differently colored eyes. That's all I've even wanted my own husband to think. I don't like it going deeper than that. People don't deserve my problems, and I don't deserve to complain to any of them. That's the purpose of this pad of paper, I guess. It's kind of funny. I'm laying in bed awaiting my execution - that's when Brandon gets home. He knows that I'm flawed enough already, but this will shatter me. Bye bye, Amy Roche...say hello to Amy Campbell again, and face her this time. I've just laid here wating an HBO movie (Gia) and crying...and writing this load of crap. I don't know what's wrong with me. I bring trouble unto myself, and every time it's like something inside of me screams "Told ya so!", but the very next day I start all the wrong patterns all over again. I never see it coming. I never bother trying. I've been trying to plan an escape. I don't want to, but I do. If I just disappeared, I could live happily as someone else again. I won't leave. I'm not going to run from a life that got a wrench in the works...not all over again. I've got to learn to be stronger than that. I have to deal. I can't stay this pathetic. Pathetic drama queen. You're the cause of your own fucking problems. Stare in that mirror and try to say that it's not exactly what you're trying so hard to ignore. You'll never love yourself, Amy...but you'll tolerate who you are. If you don't face your everything, you shouldn't even bother living. As if you even want to. I tried running again. I can't believe I considered it. [BACK to "Leech"] or [FAST FORWARD to "One Last Time"]
