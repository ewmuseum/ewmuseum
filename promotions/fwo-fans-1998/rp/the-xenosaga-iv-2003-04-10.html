The Xenosaga: IV

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Four words that should never be put in the same phrase together: Street Fighter: The Movie. "Son of a bitch!" Allen screamed in phantom pains. Oh, and believe you me, the actual pains would come in the future. This was getting out of hand. He could handle flying around as a 3D polygon. Hell, he could handle just about anything, but not this shit...not "Street Fighter: The Movie?" Yeah, not that. "What the flying fuck guys? Seriously...what're you trying to do here." "Well," Dingleberry replied. "We're troyin' t' turn ye brain een t' mush. Theen, shortly after, we'll pawn you, ye foysty leetle bugga, for a reasonable profit." Just as Allen Speck's look turned to complete, unadultured horror... "Hehe, just joshin' ye.... Oy reckon oy gotta go to the loo." Codemaster's facial expression turned from: X8O , to: X8O( almost instantly. "Yeah, and I've had to go there for about six hours now, y'ass!" Both Tallywhackers turned around, awestruck. "SIX HOURS!?!" Baffling! Lightning-striking! So ultimately, unfathomly strange yet amusing, Cagle chuckled a bit. Only a bit. Not a lot, just... you know, a bit. Dingleberry said shaken up, "Me, that's a moyty strong blattuh y' got there!" Cagle said just as, "Vereh, vereh maghty." "Well WOY deedn't you say so!?!" Dingleberry asked reasonably confused yet oddly intrigued. Speck would've slapped his forehead right then in there, but he knew of the sticky chocolate that resided on his forehead. OH, HE CERTAINLY KNEW OF THE RESIDING CHOCOLATE! THAT'S FOR DAMN SURE! "Well it's not like you would've unshackled me, or anything." Speck said, hoping....praying... "True as that raght there seentence may be, you steeyil should let us know these things." Cagle said, feeling slightly betrayed. "Hey, you'ant some pawpcowern?" Codemaster almost believed they were starting to be reasonable, "Uhhh.....sure." ***FIVE MINUTES LATER*** "Son of a bitch!" Allen screamed. "Oy! Oy just had that...whaddya call eet, Deja-vu!" Dingleberry screamed like a little school girl being dominated by her english teacher. He did it again, "UUUUUNH!" "...." Codemaster could only muster enough pride not to cry under these conditions...the most horrid movie ever put in theaters...well, excluding 'Legally Blonde', was about to start....and his hands were taped to a pack of popcorn cleverly, to where he could only lift the popcorn to his mouth and dump it all over him. Cagle started to press play, "Raght.....well, heyer goes nuttin Ah reckon....y'know....sump'n er other....Sheeyit, sump'n like that theyer....y'know...--" "FOR GOD SAKES MAN, START THE DAMN MOVIE!" Allen screamed, deciding listening to Cagle ramble for an hour was quite fathomably worse than watching the single worst movie he'd ever seen, excluding 'Legally Blonde'. Movie: On. "Son of a bitch!" "Oy...Oy just had that....whaddya call eet, Deja-vu!" Cagle replied, "Hey...Ah feeyil lahk Ah've beeyin in this sitchyation before."
