Somewhere I Belong - Bad Seed (Three)

19 April 2003

[Note: This is a continuation "Subplot-wise" of an earlier two RPs] Somewhere I Belong - One Somewhere I Belong - Two Wednesday April 9th, 2003 "Amy..." Nothing could wake Aimz when her system crashed. For a few days, she'd had a flu - and regretfully missed Warfare because of it. Bruised ribs and a partially broken jaw weren't helping her sleep any better, either, which was why caffeine pills had her awake during the day and NyQuil was getting her rest when she really needed it. There were some demons that even the strongest medication couldn't soothe, though...that's where Renee and a little piece of paper in her hand came in. Still in a coat and long pants to bundle up from slightly less than seasonal temperatures outside, the stout blonde was kicking at the side of Amy's bed - having invited herself in already. "Amy!" SMACK! Okay, maybe not the best way to wake her - especially with the news Renee held in hand. Unfortunately neither of them seemed to have time to waste, so that method was best...until the redhead kicked back in a drowsy fit. "Fuck you, that hurt!" She really *had* crashed. Upon rolling out of bed, Amy was still in regular clothes - black bondage pants with straps going every which way, topped only by a Valerian's Garden t-shirt whose back featured the logo for their newest album - Scarred. When their eyes met, Renee looked at her best friend's attempt to seem entirely angered at being woken so violently and laughed. "Sick?" "Very." Before Renee could so much as consider another word, Amy repeated the mantra she'd been chanting for two weeks: "I fucking hate England." An appropriate ode to the climate that had caused this horrible disease of sniffling and breathing far too loudly. Nonetheless, Renee laughed again. "Sick and hostile. This is why I love you." "Well right now I hate me. My body, more specifically." Aimz groaned, "It let me get sick somewhere I hate, and miss the show in Ireland. I love Ireland. I hate England..." "We've established that." Another laugh followed - Amy wasn't looking quite so amused. But regardless Renee hauled her out of the room, making sure to stuff the paper she'd held back into her pocket so the redhead didn't notice initially. "So...how's stuff?" "Shitty. I'm supposed to wrestle a guy at CyberSlam - that's like the pay-per-view. He's now injured." "Ouch." "What's more than 'Ouch' is that I'm going to make him wrestle. If he can walk, he'll do it. I'm not letting this slide." "Nazi." "I'd make Hitler proud." Renee nodded with a grin, watching Amy root through a pile of CDs - choosing the morning's soundtrack. "So aside from dealing out capital punishment...what's happening?" "Nothing. Without being hurt and sick, everything's pretty good." That only made it harder. The blonde needed to make this easier somehow. "Well...uhh...stuff's been going nicely with Brandon, I see." She shifted nervously. How could she say this? "Think you guys might move in together soon?" "Nah. He needs to get his living arragements or whatever all straightened out after the divorce, then we probably will...it's not exactly any huge step, since it's basically what we're doing already." "Right." Quite suddenly, Amy realized her best friend's tone. Something wasn't right..."Why'd you ask, anyway?" "Uhhh...well....y'see..." Something definitely wasn't right. Speechlessness wasn't a trait either of the girls possessed. "I see........?" "You see...well...umm........this!" Almost instantly, the yellow sheet of paper was in front of Amy's nose. Eviction Notice. "Eviction Notice?!" "That's what it says." Mumbled Renee, gingerly. "It was taped to your door, so I grabbed it while I was coming in." Amy's fists were balling. It was time to scramble for positives. "Y'know, you can always come live with me for a fe--" "You live with your mom, Renee." "Well what abou...no..." She thought for a moment, "Hmm...well........" "You don't know, do you?" It would've been the first time a problem hadn't been solved or spoken through by Renee...Amy was definitely nervous. She didn't so much mind losing the appartment - after more than a year with it, she still wasn't entirely moved in because of how much time was spent on the road...it was who she'd have to put up with for a month or so leading up to either finding her own place or being cleared to stay with Brandon that worried her. The fact that her best friend was silent for once when faced with a question worried her more, so Amy reiterated... "Renee? You've got no idea?" A sigh filled the loft. "I do. I just don't want to be the one to say it..." "Say it." Amy sneered to herself, remembering why she had to deal with this in the first place. "And be quick...I've gotta pay a visit to my land lord." The blonde knew two words she could suggest that would incite some sort of rage from Amy and she really didn't want to say it, but somehow knew Amy was thinking the same thing - just not admitting the possibility to herself. "Your parents." "I'd rather die in a fucking cardboard box than go back there to live even for a day." "Well you can't live in a box, now can you? It's your only option." Amy went to speak, then hesitated, then tried again...and finally succeeded. "It means they win, though." Her fists balled again, "They've said since I was thirteen that I'd never go anywhere 'The way that I am', and that sooner or later I'd always go crawling back for some sort of help. They'll love it...because it'll satisfy their egos. They'll think I can't live without them when everything I've been doing since I've been sixteen is to prove that I can." "I know, sweetie...but it's your only choice." "Fuck!" A fist flew...straight into a window. Seems like she realized the limit of her options. [Continue...]
