Adjust: Your Flight Plan

4 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Allen Speck never really cared for flying, but what else was he going to do to get to Sevilla, Spain? Swim? His flight mainly consisted of lots of sleeping, followed up with half hour of sheer panic when the captain indicating that there was a slight possibility of turbulance was followed by heavy shaking, and Allen, who hadn't been in an airplane in seven years, was just a touch nervous. He almost kissed the ground when the plane landed, but he thought better of it when he realized that he was in Spain. "Wow, Spain," Leigh said with a smile on her face. She had elected to come along with Allen, because Lord knows that Allen would just be completely lost in Europe without SOMEONE along with him, "I haven't been here in a while." "And I haven't been here, period." Allen pointed out. The two walked around the airport until they hit the baggage claim, whereupon they collected their luggage. After walking out of the airport, Allen searched his pockets for something. At first, he pulled out his Game Boy Advance(lord knows why he brought that since his arm was still in a sling), but after depositing it back in his pocket, he found his cellular phone. Using his only free hand, Allen dialed a number with a bit of difficulty. He would've let Leigh dial it in, but there's no way that he would have let a woman do simple things on his behalf. With the number dialed, Allen put the phone to his ear and spoke. "Hello?" he asked. "Yeah, it's Allen. I'm in Sevilla." There was a pause, before a look of shock came over the face of Allen Speck. He blinked, took a glance at Leigh, then blinked again. "What?!" Speck called out, giving a look of surprise to Leigh Landers as he did so, "You're kidding. When did that happen?" Allen groaned, before he put his hand on the bottom of the phone and turned to Leigh Landers, "They're in FRANCE." "What?" Leigh asked, surprised and somewhat confused. "France! The fWo! It went to Paris this morning." Allen said, before he went back to the phone, "Samuel, how long are you going to be there? You told me you'd be here today, and you're in France now, and I'm especially not fond of being led around, okay? So, you'll be there on Wednesday, right?" Speck smiled slightly at this. "Okay, good. See you on Tuesday, then." Speck said, before he hung up. He then performed an about-face and headed back into the airport. Leigh, confused, also followed him. "Allen, what're you doing?" Leigh asked. "Bookin' a flight to Paris. You got a problem with this?" Allen responded. "Er, well, no. Not particularly." Leigh said, as she walked alongside Allen Speck, "It's just that we've come so far, can't we take a break or somethin'?" "You're the one who talked me into calling Smooth in the first place. So, why don't we just get this over with, okay?" Allen said, as he approached the ticket receptionist. She was Spanish, which was, of course, natural since this was Spain. Still, Allen figured that holding up two fingers and saying "Paris" was good enough. "Hello, I need two tickets to Paris." A few moments and a slight language barrier problem later, and Leigh Landers was forced to do the talking.
