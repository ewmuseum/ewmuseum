Faith in the storm

12 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It had been a week since Deacon left the hurricane behind, but it had been a week where another storm seemed to stay with him. He had been nearly arrested for impersonating an American, no doubt due to his language difficulties. He had missed 2 flights, 1 show, and hadn't been near anyone he knew in over a week. To finally arrive in an arena to hold an fwo event, that was all he wanted to see now. Finally, he could see the silver lining. Salvador, Brazil would be host to Warfare. He entered the arena from the back, happy to be able to wrestle. He was able to watch Padrona on Slaughter, but even though a retribution was in order for the storyline, he couldn't give it. All his calls to fwo headquarters were met with, "You're WHERE?" All his answers usually were cities that he couldn't pronounce. He wasn't sure what he would do tonight, but he only knew that he was going to do something, and that was enough. He hadn't planned on any of this upon entering the fWo, but he did plan on adjusting to the problems and overcoming them. Tonight would be better. After the week he had just experienced, what could go wrong? And beyond the silver lining, just over the mountain ranges, a storm brewed.
