The Xenosaga: VIII

11 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sheep. Allen Speck, aka the Codemaster, aka the Blackest Man in the fWo, aka Your mother's secret lover, aka Barney, at one point in his life, would think "Why yes, sheep are rather nifty." However, now that two Tallywhackers have been chasing him around, and he's been hiding in a field of sheep for quite a while, he thinks "Sheep stink. They're so smelly and annoying. With their 'bAaAaAaAaAh's and such. Personally, I'd like to go tip a sheep over right now.' Thus, he did. However, the next chain of actions and reactions would create a great disturbance in this particular field of love-making and peace. See: Black-Rider, the biggest, baddest, motherf00kin' ram in the motherf00kin' field was all, "Yo homey, don't be all tippin' mah bitches and shiznit, y'heard meh?" Which more or less came out as 'Hmph' in the human language. Codemaster not realizing this threat, found it rather fun, tipping sheep that is, and did it again. This caused Black-Rider, aka the one motherf00kin' sheep you do NOT wanna mess with bitch, to be all "Hey nizzle, what da hizle did i tizle you mang? You want some of this shizzle?" "Hmph!" So all of Black-Rider's crew got together with Li'l Shawdy's crew, the rival gang of that field. They decided, they were gonna show this sheep-tippin' sumbitch what it's like to be... 'Rammed!' *Dr. Evil Pinky* Meanwhile, on the otherside of the field, sheep after poorless sheep were being tipped in a most unorderly fashion. A fashion in which was so unorderly, one may say its... 'Disorderly' *Dr. Evil Pinky* The Tallywhackers, saw their former captive tipping sheep. And they didn't like it. Not one bit. Not one damn bit! So Cagle was all, "Aww sheeyit, he done gawna get his ayass kicked bah mah big ollllll' boot!" And Dingleberry was all, "Croinky! Thees lollygagga's gone mad. MAD I SAY~! MAAAAAAAAAAAAD!" So, what happens when the Tallywhackers begin chasing the Codemaster around the field that Black-Rider rides? Well, for one thing, the rival crews had already began their assault by taking rather large stinkies in random places, hoping they'd step in it. Then they charged in a phalanx position, all trojan like. Once the 'Whackers and the Master of Codes saw this, for a moment they forgot their troubles and ran. "FUUUUUUUUUUUUUUCK~!" The three screamed, in unison. "HMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMPH~!" The rams hmphed, in unison. "FUUUUUUUUUUUUUUCK~!" The three screamed, in unison. "HMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMPH~!" The rams hmphed, in unison. This pattern continued until three o'clock in the morning, which was four hours from the time in which they were all "FUUUUUUUUUCK~!"'ing and "HMMMMMMMMMPH~!"'ing. Because after all, you can't "FUUUUUUUUUCK~!" and "HMMMMMMMMMPH~!" all day. So they jumped the fence and stopped for a break. Shortly after the mandatory five minutes, a super-pissed Codemaster gave them both stiffly-worked kicks to the ballbags, and both fell. And he ran, in a most triumphant manner.
