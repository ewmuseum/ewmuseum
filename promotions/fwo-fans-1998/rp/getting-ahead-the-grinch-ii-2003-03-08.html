Getting Ahead: The Grinch. II

8 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
in which little Bobby Smyth is disappointed. Being a fan of a team was never too hard... the only hard thing was when no one else saw your point of view. When you could care for something... but no one else would. The feeling of knowing that there was some good... even if everyone saw only the bad. Bobby Smyth, was in this position for at least a good month now. Since he was eight, he had loved the fWo. It provided little Bobby with countless memories... laughing, crying, marking... the fWo was his playground, and on no account did it rain out on him. Then, when the fWo had left television, only to return to TV in the fall, Bobby fell even more in love with the programming. To actually have something you never thought was going to leave you, leave... only to see it come back once again brought you into it ten fold. And on top of all of this, twelve year old Bobby Smyth had found a new team, a new hope... a new chance to laugh, cry, and mark. They were Tyler and Conor, the Fuse Bros.. And they were his favorite team. But then something happened. After numerous shows of struggling to win some battles (even though it's been heavily noted they could have won if done fairly), the Fuse Bros. went against the majority... changing their views and actions upon the fWo; turning into a team, that really, deep down inside bothered Bobby Smyth. And after his rich parents had flown him out for his birthday, to see the fWo in Kouba, Algeria, in one of the smallest Stadiums to date, Bobby was really disappointed in the antics the Fuse Bros.' had pulled. The show was now over, and basically everyone had gone home, but since there wasn't much going on outside the Arena, Bobby and his Mother, Carol, had decided to wait outside, to see if Bobby could grab an autograph or two... considering there couldn't be much done by way of security. Bobby had waited a while, getting a few autographs from some of the superstars... but deep down inside, he was holding out for Tyler and Conor... so that maybe this inadequate night could have a little bit of a put-up. But what Bobby Smyth didn't know, was that right behind the exit door, were the Fuse's, as Conor pushed the handle back, and stepped out outside to face the boy.
