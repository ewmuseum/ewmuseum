1 February 2003

BodyCount, post-Tag Title match I walked into the locker room, not knowing and not really caring if Ivy and Sean were behind me. I was angry, and there was nothing that either of them could tell me that would make things alright. It made me realize even more how important Eli had been at holding things together for us these past few weeks, as, without him here, I knew I'd be on my own if a confrontation occurred. Of course, there was no telling if Eli would've sided with me even if he had been here. After all, I'd basically thrown my partner to the wolves, and wasn't that exactly what Sean had originally thought I would do? "Y'know, I can't say you surprised me out there, Lance. ...In the back of my mind, I knew, though." He had started talking before he made it through the locker room door, and it got increasingly louder as it closed behind him. "But, get this through your thick skull, punk... you didn't pull the wool over my eyes... you pulled the wool over Ivy and Eli. Your friends. And, honestly... if it wasn't for your friends, I'd beat the shit out of your ass right now for leaving me out to dry like you did, bitch." "I left you out to dry? This is the third time since we started teaming with each other that you've only thought of yourself, even if it's only the first time we've lost as a result." "What? You let me get pinned, remember? The mature thing to do was to come and say something to me before we went out there and got our asses beat in front of the entire world." "You're also not the one who tagged in your partner when you were clearly in need of a break..." Sean tried to interrupt me, but I just continued talking, as I had too much on my mind to simply be silent. "For the past month, I've traveled with you, ate with you, worked out with you, and things have gotten better between us, outside of the ring, even as they've deteriorated out there." "Going into this, we were supposed to work together, even if we didn't like one another, so we could get a shot at the gold, and as I stood there on the apron, with the gold on the line, the only thing I could think about was that you were killing yourself in the ring." "Wait...you're trying to tell me you didn't break up the count to protect me? First... don't flatter yourself... I don't want or need you pretending to help me, so you'll look good in front of Eli and Ivy. I'm a grown ass man, last time I checked, and, I've been in this goddamned business for eight years, I don't need anybody telling me what I can and can't do. Let alone you." I could make out the disbelief in Sean's voice clearly, but I continued nonetheless. "That's exactly what I'm saying, Sean. I mean, you're going back out there in what? Thirty? Thirty-five minutes? To wrestle in the BodyCount match? You keep this kind of shit up, you're going to kill yourself." "I'll tell you this one time and one time only... with hopes of us never having this discussion again. I'll judge whether or not my body can handle what I do. " Sean began, and his tone made it clear that he wasn't happy in the least. But a voice from behind him silenced him, as Ivy spoke. "Sean, he's right. I can understand you wanting to bury yourself in your work, but when you border on getting yourself hurt, I actually think Lance made the right decision." As I looked across the room at my tag team partner, the man who had started to become my friend, I could see that he clearly wasn't in agreement with us, but the fact that Ivy had backed me up had at least forced him to think about how he'd been acting for the better part of the last month. He passed a hand over his hair, which was starting to pull out of the ponytail that had held it for the match, and the look of reflection on his face told me that maybe Sean was at least considering what I had said. At the risk of further angering him, I continued. "Listen, Sean, you don't have to agree with me, or with what I did, and I'm sorry if you think that I cost us the tag titles, but I did what I did out of concern. I'd do the same if my partner was Brandon or EJ, and it wasn't an attempt to leave you high and dry." I was relieved to see Sean sit down absently on a bench, and it was clear that he was indeed deep in thought. As I moved to my bench to prepare for the BodyCount match, I only hoped that he could find the balance in his life that he so desperately needed.
