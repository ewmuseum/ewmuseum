CORPUS DELECTI Part 4 - There and Back Again "Sit down, you're safe for the moment." "Yes, dear." Sitting in the mental facsimile of his old apartment, with the mental projection of his wife next to him, Eli Flair nearly forgets he's dreaming. "Are you happy?" asked Alicia. "Happy? I suppose," replied Eli, "Everyone has their ups and downs, right? I do okay most of the time." "That's not what I asked you," insisted Alicia, "Are. You. Happy. Yes or no." "No." "Why not?" "Because the Faceless, Emily, everyone.... they're all right. Everyone I love is dead." He looks at her with a half- serious smirk on his face. "Or deeply resentful." Alicia tosses her auburn hair over her shoulder and puts her arm around him. "Isn't my sister taking care of you? I thought she was supposed to make sure you didn't do this sort of thing to yourself." "Ivy's kept my brain from melting for seven years now, Ali," said Eli, "but she's got her own problems nowadays. Besides, it shouldn't be her responsibility to keep me from going crazy. If I can't do it myself then what the shit am I doing here?" "Where is Angel these days?" Eli sank back into the loveseat. "Recording an album." "Sounds like fun." "It's hell. I think I'm losing her." "You'll only lose her if you think you're losing her. So stop thinking it." Eli stands up, looking at her. "It's just strange talking to you about this." "Why?" asked Alicia, "You're very well aware of the fact that you're asleep, talking to a facet of your own personality with my face and voice." "I'm intellectually aware," replied Eli, "but do you really think that makes it any easier?" Alicia stands up and wraps her arms around Eli's waist. "I know it doesn't, but you have to try. I wanted you to be happy is all." Eli doesn't say anything.... but even in dreams, he can shed a tear. Alicia continued, "You know how much your happiness meant to the McGinnis family, right? You know perfectly well that every single member of that family is absolutely crazy about Angel." She let go, taking a step back. "But now, Eli.... you need to stop dreaming the same dream every night. Let it go." "I.... don't know if I can, Ali," replied Eli, "It's been so hard." Alicia smiled. "I know it has. And it'll be harder still before it's all over.... but I promise you, my love, you will be happy again soon." As Eli Flair watches the mental projection of his wife take a step away from him, he turns around. Everything suddenly begins to go dark, as if someone was turning out the lights. In his mind, this entire incident has, so far, taken place from 11:50 PM to 1:50 AM. For the first time in seven years.... Eli Flair's night of sleeping will be devoid of nightmares.
