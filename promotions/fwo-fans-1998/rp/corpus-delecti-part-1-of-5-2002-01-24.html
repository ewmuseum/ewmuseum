Corpus Delecti - part 1 of 5

CORPUS DELECTI Part 1 - Regrets, I've Had a Few "Twenty- one bottles of beer on the wall, twenty- one bottles of beer...." Eli Flair has a usually- unchanged ritual when he's in a city he doesn't know anybody in. He gets drunk. "If one of those bottles should happen to fall...." **CRASH** The bottles aren't falling. They're being thrown against the wall. Eli Flair checked into his hotel in London, changed into a pair of baggy jeans and a jWo T-Shirt, went out to a corner store, bought a 24-case of cheap beer, and returned. Twenty- one are gone, three remain. Eli's hair is matted with sweat. He's obviously in some kind of alcoholic shock. Outside, a raging thunderstorm has all but shut the city down for the night. Lightning flashes in the sky, illuminating the midnight hour like nothing else could. Eli stumbles to his feet, using the wall for support. He staggers over to the window, grabbing Number 22 off the bed before getting there. He looks out, noting for the first time that there's a small 'porch' outside, a deck just big enough for one person to stand comfortably. He pulls the sliding glass door open and looks outside, stepping back from the downpour of rain for a moment.... but just a moment. Cracking the beer, he takes a long swig of it, soaked to the skin in an instant. "MOTHERFUCKER--!" The bottle, half empty, will hit the pavement below in approximately three seconds. Half due to drunkenness, half due to his own feeling of hopelessness and despair, he sinks to his knees, resting his forehead on the railing. His hair immediately plasters to the rusted rail, as it is just as soaked as the rest of his body. His footwear - VANS - are going to have to be drying out for several days before they are suitable to wear again. He stands, as if the weight of the world is pressing down on him. "What? What are you bitchin' about now?" He turns around suddenly.... and in reality, there's nobody there. In his mind, however.... he sees the image of a voice from his past standing there. "April...." said Eli, "What.... How?" "How did I get here? I didn't. I'm inside your head, like everything else." Eli puts his hand to his head, trying to make heads or tails out of this statement. In reality, he has a tendancy to hallucinate when he's incredibly drunk. Every emotion in his head becomes characterized by a familiar face.... telling him things he already knows but can't accept, telling him things that he knows but hasn't realized, whatever you please. April is a fashion designer by day, and a writer by night. She lives in New York City, the apartment below Eli Flair's former residence. He took quite a fancy to her when he first met her, but they were destined to simply be friends. He sees her as she was the last time he saw her in person - puffy red furry coat, "Starfucker" baby tee, and large, loud silver glittered sunglasses. Her hair is black with purple roots. "You're a piece'a work, Eli, you know that? Beautiful girl, she says she loves you, and you let her walk out of your life like that? What the hell is the matter with you?" "It just wasn't meant to be," replied Eli, "We came from two different worlds." "Bullshit. Eli punches the wall. Hard. "Mind over matter, dude. How is it that a six foot nine monster of a man can tear his way through anyone with a length of barbed wire, and drop a one hundred and ten pound woman through a goddamn table, can't tell a girl how he feels about her?" "I did tell her," said Eli, "and she told me it didn't matter." "You told her that you loved her and you wanted her to drop her dreams and follow yours. Then you told her that you loved her and that you wanted to drop your dreams to follow hers. And you're surprised that she balked?" A beeping sound. Eli looks toward his bags, remembering the cell phone that he's still not used to. Pulling it out, he looks at the display to see Poison Ivy's number imprinted on it. He glances back toward April.... to see a blank space on the wall, as if she was never there. "Hello?" "Eli, it's Ivy. Listen, I just got in and there's a message on my phone from Sean.... have you seen him since the airport?" "Nope." "Okay.... you alright?" "Fine." "Sober up, Eli.... we've got a busy day tomorrow." Sober up.... interesting thought. "I've got a busy day tomorrow," said Eli to nobody, as he grabbed Number 24 from the case.
