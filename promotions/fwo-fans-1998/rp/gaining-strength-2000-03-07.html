Gaining Strength

7 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Gaining Strength March 07, 2000 ::The scene begins as we see the night sky, but the night sky is interrupted with the crackling of Lightning, then a boom of thunder. The clouds are odd to say the least, the clouds swirl in a grey blackness. To describe it best you may see soemthing like it in the Ghostbusters movies. The scene slowly scrolls down from this site as we see the face of Rain, goatee is trimmed to perfection, he is donning his sunglasses and a large black trenchcoat. His lips are sealed shut and his face is unmoving. The camera pulls away and we see that Rain is standing, staring at this dark circle in the sky. We see that Rain is standing at the edge of a large cliff, the terrain appears to be american, and below the large cliff we see a huge city. Littered with lights and sounds. One of Rain's hands are outstretched and opened towards the sky, suddenly he bows his head, kneels to one knee and speaks:: Rain: ......Mefortate, Stratheit, Markum, Defilia, Stildante ecrobum cor brekor........... ::The words are unknown to us and though we do not know what they mean there is a strange signifigance surrounding these powerful words. Rain is showing a side of him we have not before seen as we see into his faith, something barely touched on in the past.:: Rain: ...Deruinafe...Rteel... Covertannga.... ::As Rain speaks in this foreign tongue the devistating clouds above circle mysteriously, the lightning lights the sky more often, and the thunder grows louder. Suddenly Rain's face can be seen as only severe concentration, until the wind grows strong and a large bolt of lightning strikes down near Rain, as this happens Rain's head goes flies back wuickly and his concentration appears to be lost as the skies above slowly go back to a clear night sky. Rain lets out a scream of curse before standing back off and resuming his face of stone:: Rain: So close.... but if I cannot have it now then it must mean it is too early for me to have it. I misjudged, but not by much, I am close. I can feel that I am growing more powerful everyday, and I proved to myself and everyone that this was not just an illusion when I reigned blows of destruction on Slaughter. ::Rain's face turns sour as he thinks for a moment before speaking again:: Rain: Perhaps I should not speak of the devine power of the storm until I am able to display it more spectacularly. Slaughter. I showed great strength by defeating the man named Dead once again, and defeating the protector of the fWo, the Boston Strangler for the first time. Two of the fWo's hardest fighting brawlers could not destroy me and I proved my worth and strength, proved that I would be the one emerging victoriously at Body Count. Dead, you used my powers for your own benifit, manipulated my weakness, a weakness that I have no longer, and you are punished for it. Dead, you simply will be crushed under my feet as I overcome those who were holding to the ground, you have been feeling the weight on your shoulders, but soon the shoe will fall, and you will have no feeling whatsoever. Strangler. A man of many depths, depths that represent the wrong goals, and I am one of few who have seen this, and have tried to destroy him. My attempts have been close but not close enough, luck will not be with you always, and once luck runs out the sheer power and grace of the Storm will overtake you and whisk you to your most feared place. I have kept in the front of my mind that there will be 30 men in a cage fighting for one supreme goal, and I will be one of those men, I will be one of them men who emrge alive, I will be the only man who emerges victorious, few spots have already been given out, and Im sure that I will be given a spot unthankful of the fWo's graces. I will still fight with the power of the Storm at my side, within me, and I will destroy everyman who stands in my way of the prestigous title. I am powerful, I can win now, but soon I will be stronger and the fWo will bow to the Storm. When it RAINS.... it POURS.
