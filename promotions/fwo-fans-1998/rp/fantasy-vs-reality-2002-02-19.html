Fantasy vs Reality

19 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Vic rolled the towel tight, biting down on it to brace for the near-overwhelming pain that was coming. A cold sweat started to break, beads of perspiration trickling down his forehead and neck. He gripped the edge of the sink, knuckles whitening and veins running like blue cables up his forearm. This wasn't going to be pleasant, but it had to be done. All this just to take a piss. As the stream of urine splashed into the toilet, Creed exhaled hard through his nose. He wouldn't cry out, refused to. Still, some things were out of his control. Thankfully, the towel muffled his yell. He held firm, washed with relief when it was over. Creed spit the towel out. "If I find that bitch, I'll wring her neck," he sighed. He bent forward to flush and paused momentarily. Blood in the bowl. Not a good sign. "Damn, now what was her name?" Vic splashed cold water on his face and looked at himself in the mirror. He was flushed, bloated from the mass quantities of steroids in his system. He was pushing four hundred pounds now, and it was starting to take its toll. His joints ached every morning, not used to the stress placed on them in such a short time. But all in all, it was worth it. When Creed hit the gym, people stood in awe at the monstrous quantity of weight he'd lift. Sure, he'd get the occasional nosebleed from the strain, but it was minor compared to the gains. Kodiak Vic Creed was the longest reigning Internet Champion in history. Wasn't that what it was all about? Being recognized as being the Best? Vic was now pushing himself to the breaking point. He had to. Not only did he have the Body Count match to win, but he had to deal with Scott Slugger. Maybe things had gotten a little out of control with Scott. Maybe Vic had gone too far with what happened to Stephanie. But Vic wasn't concerned with what the boys in the back thought of him. He wasn't a locker room politician. He didn't play games. Creed was an angry man, and had a lot of frustration to work out. Sometimes people got hurt in their dealings with Vic. If Scott wanted to push the issue, Vic was more than happy to push back. There was a thin line between fantasy and reality. In fantasy, Scott Slugger was the World's Greatest Athlete who'd be facing Kodiak Vic Creed on Saturday. In reality, Scott Wasner had to get in the ring with Vic Creid. In fantasies, the guy with the white hat always got the girl and saved the day. They were heroes, saving innocent people from the horrible villains. In reality, good people got hurt by big bad men. Rarely were there heroes around to save them. More often than not, any heroes that were around just got hurt as well. Reality was often painful, physically as well as emotionally. Vic patted his face with the towel. If this were simply fantasy, the might Scott Slugger would smite the evil Kodiak Vic Creed at Body Count. The fans would cheer him as he raised the Internet Title over his head. But Scott Wasner? A snowball in Hell had more of a chance than he did. Scott was days away from a four hundred-pound reality check, Creed thought to himself as he walked out of the bathroom. Painful didn't begin to describe what was coming.
