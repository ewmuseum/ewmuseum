Respect (9 - Pregame 3)

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Once more, with feeling..." I couldn't remember who had said that famous quote, but as I muttered it to myself in the locker room after getting dressed for my match, it just felt somehow right in light of what would surely transpire. Though our previous encounters had been far from clinical, emotionless affairs, there was no way that tonight's match would fail to eclipse the fury and fervor that had accompanied the first three. At Twilight, at Countdown 3, and at Countdown 2, I had been struggling to earn Sean Stevens' respect. However, tonight, failure was not an option. If he wouldn't give his respect unto me willingly, I'd have no choice but to take it from him. I knew there was a good chance that one or both of us wouldn't leave this match with our consciousness or our bodies intact, but that was one of the risks I was willing to take. Never before had anyone done so much to make me feel so insignificant. The physical abuse I could take, but to take everything I've accomplished in my young life, and especially in my fledgling career, well...that was crossing the line. And Sean Stevens would have to pay. It didn't matter to me that he was Eli's best friend, or Ivy's boyfriend, or that those two had welcomed me into their consort with open arms. No, tonight's match was about one thing, and one thing only: RESPECT. One word, seven letters, but right now, it meant more to me than anything else ever had, more even than earning my dad's acceptance for my line of work, and far more than any other accomplishment that I had long toiled for in the prior 23 years of my life. This match was the alpha and the omega for me
