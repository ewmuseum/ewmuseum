Someone had to go this far...

4 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He felt a cold sweat. The dream. The dream he hasn't had for years. He woke up in a cold sweat. The emptiness. The emptiness that no longer exists. "Angel is home," said Eli, trying to convince himself, "Angel is home getting ready for the tour. She's home." He's trying to convince himself. Eight years... it's been eight years since he felt like this. Alone. Isolated. Unloved, unmemorable, unable to make his mark on the world. "No... no!" he continued, "I have made a mark." Has he? The Original Nobody... he's a dime a dozen. He's anyone out there. He's a few lucky breaks ahead of working in a gas station. He only met the woman who completes him in every way because of those lucky breaks. He only has a shot at Kodiak Vic Creed because enough nobodies in the crowd cheered for him. "Stop it... STOP IT." They aren't going to stop. They keep at it. They won't let you sleep. They won't let you love. They won't let you live. "I need a drink," said Eli, to nobody, "I need to get up and call Ivy and get her to meet me in the bar... I need to be around someone right now." No, you have nobody to be around. You have nothing. You are nothing. You will never feel true love. "Fuck you!" he shouted, "I know what true love feels like. I feel it right now!" She doesn't belong to you. "She's not property, I never said she was anyone's but her own." She belongs to the entire world. One Angel cannot be chained to a devil like you. "We're together because we want to be." Because you want to be. Because she has to be. "You're wrong." You're delusional. You didn't think it would last, do you? "Huh?" Everything good has come while you were floundering in the midcard. "That doesn't mean anything." You were alone at your last World Title, you'll be alone at your next. "No, I won't." Things are looking up in your career. Things will inevitably take a downturn in love. "No. Angel's different." You're not. A leopard cannot change its spots, and a nobody cannot become somebody. Even an original. "Shut up." There's nothing. "Shut up." There's nothing quite so lonely. "Shut... up." There's nothing quite so lonely as an empty room. And Eli Flair woke up with a start. The voice was gone. The room was empty. And the King of Extreme was lonely.
