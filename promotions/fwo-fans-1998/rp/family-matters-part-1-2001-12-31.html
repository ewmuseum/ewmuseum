Family Matters (Part 1)

31 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As the long black limo weaved through the streets on its way to the airport, Monte Burns ran his hand back through his hair. His pale face stared aimlessly out of the window. Obviously, Burns was thinking more about what had happened earlier in the night. The call had been unexpected and not necessarily wanted. Burns had been preparing for a match when the cell phone rang. With the Countdown to Oblivion series always playing an important part in the state of the fWo, Burns was focused on making a strong impression in the minds of the fWo staff, fans, and his fellow wrestlers. Instead, he had gotten a phone call. Monte was annoyed at first, but the words coming from Lance Fortune on the other hand changed all that. "Monte, it's Lance," he said. "Your parents called me to get in touch with you. Nathan's been in an accident." The words hit Monte all over. While he had been at odds with his family, and Nathan in particular, for a very long time, Burns still felt the pain deep inside him of a loved one in trouble. "When? How?" Monte asked hurriedly. "Earlier tonight at a show in Hartford. He took a pretty stiff chair shot and tried to go up top shortly after and lost his balance," said Fortune softly. "He fell to the floor head first. No padding." Burns took a few moments to allow what had been said to sink in to him before he had left the locker room. The time after that was a blur, with only bits and pieces of what happened still in his mind. He remembered asking Chris Universal for time off, being attacked backstage by Douglas, and their so-called match right afterwards. Monte was not focused at all on beating Douglas or wrestling him. All Burns had been concerned with was leaving the building and heading to see his brother. That was clear after Monte hurriedly left the ringside area following the pinfall. Somehow, he had managed to exit the arena and get into a limo, which was on its way to the airport at this moment. Sitting in the back of the limo alone, Monte sighed and looked out at the streets of San Francisco. After a few moments, Burns reached inside his pocket to retrieve his cell phone. Finding nothing, Burns remembered that Douglas had broken it during their backstage brawl. Shaking his head, Monte began to think about his brother Nathan and how the accident could have occurred. Why did Nathan decide to go up top right after a chairshot? Why didn't the place have any padding around the ring? Why was he even wrestling in a show during the holidays? These questions and others raced through Monte's head, trying to reason out exactly what could have happened and why. Unfortunately for him, the answers were much more difficult to come by than the questions. To be continued...
