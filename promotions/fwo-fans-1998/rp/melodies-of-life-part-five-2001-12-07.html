Melodies of Life - Part Five

7 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Some people are born great, some people assume greatness, and some people have greatness thrust upon them." Dr. Lovejoy thought he was a little off- center, but a nice guy nonetheless. The wrestling was an abnormal job, no doubt, but people don't change all that much, right? Right? "And some people try to get their greatness by fucking with the wroooooong people." MELODIES OF LIFE PART 5: Distortion DRAMATIS PERSONAE: Eli Flair Dr. Lovejoy Exposition "It's funny, someone told me that the fWo was the big- time. That the fWo was where the great ones went to be noticed. So I'm here. Apparently I attract a pretty damn good deal of attention to myself, because I've cut a total of two - count 'em - two promos in the ring, and they've both been interrupted." Eli looks at the camera, his hair still hanging in his face. With his arms crossed over his knees, sitting cross- legged on a stool, he looks like the epitome of evil, or a drugged- up mental patient. Either way, he's got the look. "I don't know what it is about me. Is it my good looks? My deboniare charm? My heart- shaped tattoo? My dozens of other tattoos? First it's the Flying Frenchie, now it's Jon Crisp. Sure.... it's like going from the Hilton to the Holiday Inn.... but it'll do. Frenchie's done it all in the fWo, and Crisp has.... not. But it'll do." "This might be a pretty good form of therapy.... he gets all his aggression out onto tape instead of on himself or those around him." "Irregardless, Crisp.... one of these days I'm going to go to the ring and say my piece from start to finish and nobody will interrupt me. Until that time, you've got my undivided attention." Eli suddenly shoots out a hand and grabs the camera lens, pulling it in VERY close.... his face is all that can be seen." "Aren't you lucky?" He gets off the stool and walks out of the camera view to the elevators. As he gets on, Dr. Lovejoy can swear she hears his voice, faintly in the distance. "Was it good for you?"
