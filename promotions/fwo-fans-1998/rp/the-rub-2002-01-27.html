The rub

27 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Neo sat alone in the room watching the television. With the lights off, the reflections from the screen cast unusual colored shadows on the white wall of the hotel. What was not cast upon the wall fell and danced upon Neo's face. His hands worked in his lap, tying furiously with dull, red ribbon. On the television four women discussed female issues in politics. Neo found the topic increasingly dull but forced his eyes to remain on the screen. From the corner of one a crimson tear streaked down his face and dripped onto his shirt collar. If Neo noticed this he gave no sign of it. Hours later he would absently dab at the dried blood with a washcloth before meeting members of the fWo ring crew downstairs so they could travel to the arena together. He reached for a note card and heard a ripping noise from his lap. Looking down for the first time in over an hour he noticed that the wrapping paper of the small gift he was preparing had torn. The small box of chocolates was visible from the tear and Neo cursed before tossing the chocolates against the side of the hotel wall with a sudden angry movement. A dark splatter painted the white wall and Neo made a mental note somewhere deep within himself to correct this. He sat still. In his mind he saw a bright brown field in late fall. A fire swept over the field, burning the wheat and long grass in a sudden consuming blaze. Ash was left behind, coating the ground in dark grey. The smoke from the fire turned the sky black, and somewhere among the ruin there came a scream of sorrow... pain... or rage. Neo walked slowly to a chest of drawers on the other side of the room. He reached into a bag there and pulled free a small box of chocolates. On the ground several paces away a cockroach had found the discarded box, along with three others that had been tossed there previously. The television droned on. Women commented on the most romantic things their husbands had ever done for them. "Sometimes a hug is the best way of saying I love you," the woman said. Neo held the box in his hands, his eyes again closed as he gave way to some dark inner voice. In one hand, the box turned and turned around. In the other Neo reached into his pants and began to pull. A still, blank expression was worn on his face that showed clear like the moon. "Sometimes a hug is the best way of saying I love you," Neo said. "Sometimes a hug is the best way of saying I love you." Sweat drops appeared at Neo's hairline, and after a few moments it pooled enough to drip down his face. The sweat mixed with the blood, creating a sticky, dark fluid that made his face shine. His eyes were closed tight, and his breath came in short, rushed puffs. "Sometimes a hug is the best way of saying I love you. Sometimes a hug is the best way of saying I love you. Sometimes a hug..." Neo's words cut off as he released into his hand. He opened the box with his other hand and pulled out some of the chocolates inside. He smeared them, smiling as he thought of the cooking show he had seen a few days ago. Basting they had called it. Basting. He was basting them. Frosting on chocolate. The television had moved onto a different show about a woman lawyer. How much time had passed? Shadows that were on one side of the room had moved far across the wall. Neo noticed the cold in the room for the first time. Being more careful, Neo wrapped up the box of chocolates and tied the red ribbon with great care before setting the gift to his side. He reached for a small stack of cards on the bed next to him and selected one from the top. Carefully because his hand was shaking, Neo wrote a small note on the card. "Hugs, Neo." He smiled, and it was a terrible smile of intent. His eyes closed and his mouth worked slowly, the muscles relaxing in his jaw. He opened his eyes, and looked at himself in the mirror. The smile that greeted him was disarming and friendly. He smiled wider, and blinked his eyes. "Some say that a kind word is reward enough... I say that a kind word is a reward, but so is a friendly handshake or a hug. Being good to others is being good to ourselves. My mother was a great influence in my life, and I'm not ashamed to admit it. Be yourself and the world will be your guide." Neo blinked, and noticed the crimson smear on the side of his face. He went to the washroom absently, whistling a small tune he had heard on the television. A few minutes later he would leave the room with the small gift in his hand and a smile on his face. Tonight was the show, and Neo didn't want to be late.
