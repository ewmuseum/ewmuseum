Seeing the Way.

13 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Seeing the Way. February 13, 2000 ::Drip. Drip. Drip. The sound of a single bead of water dropping from the pipes in the musty bioler room of the New Orleans arena. Drip. Drip. Drip. The room is large and dark, with cobwebs hanging from private corner's that no man dare stick his hand. It is late and the room is void of life, except for one man. Drip. Drip. Thud. The sound stops as we see Rain sitting on the cement floor against a large grey machine, he has placed his finger under one of the leaks, catching the drops of water. He wears a look of stone, showing no emotion whatsoever, his facial hair is neatly trimmed and his hair is neatly put back. He dons a large black trench coat and is still in his wrestling attire. Rain stares off into space, in a place that people cannot see, or maybe, a place they do not want to see.:: Drip. Drip. Drip. Rain: Some believe that the rain is good. Some play in the rain, some use it for their own special purposes. Sometimes people enjoy the rain. Sometimes people hate the rain. They do anything they can to avoid, to keep it away from themselves,they run from it or they try to be covered from it. Drip. Drip. Drip. Rain: No one likes the storm. It destroys everything and anything, people try to predict the next move of a storm in order to stay away from it. The storm always catches up to those people at one time or another, you can run, but you cant hide from the Storm, the storm picks and chooses which it chooses to destroy, and has no feelings of anguish. Void of all emotion. Drip. Drip. Drip. Rain: I am the storm of the fWo. Drip. Drip. Drip. Rain: The fWo has been holding me down, doing their best to keep me away, I tried to figure a way to fight, a way to be the best, but the fWo will have none of it. I didnt know how I would get it done. Chris Finn is the center of this, but I cannot attack him for he is barely even a man, and does not know what he is doing. One man made me realize why the fWo had been holding me down, and now I know. The death of Ryan Moore, the death of Travis Beaven, the man called Speed Demon went insane, the fWo believes that I did all this. One man has made me realize this, and that man was..... Dead. A man I have never thought to be capable of comprehendable thoughts has told me how the fWo is not going to let me rise, the fWo hates me and in return... I hate the fWo. There is one man who represents the fWo, he is their bodyguard, it's "saviour". That man is the center of the World's hatred towards me. Drip. Drip. Drip. Rain: Boston Strangler. You are the one who is leading the world against me. I know this, Dead has told me. You felt only a fraction of the rage I have towards you, and I will not rest, I will not sleep a second until I have removed you from the face of the earth, when I do, everything will be good again. You have conspired against me since the beginning and now it is your turn, I will make sure you cannot walk, cannot speak, cannot hear..... cannot breath. Tonight I did what I could, but it was not enough, The God's of the Storm have agreed with me, you are the one who will fall for the prejudice against me. Tonght was nothing compared to what I have in store for you. Drip. Drip. Drip. Rain: This Wednesday I face Chris Universal, a man I have never faced in the ring, and a man who is yet to meet the menacing power of the storm. Universal is Dead's biggest enemy, and I cannot understand why as Dead is a smart man who can achieve his goals. You are part of those who are against me, you will fall, and remember.... when it Rains.. .. .. ... it Pours.
