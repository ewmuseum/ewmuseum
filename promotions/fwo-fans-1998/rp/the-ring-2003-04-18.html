The Ring

18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's quiet in the Garden at 3am Sunday morning. Everything was already set up for tomorrow nights pay-per-view. Seats crowded the floor of the arena, the big stage was set up with a huge screen above it, and the ring - THE ring - sat in the middle of it all, a single light shining down on it from the ceiling. Wippit walked slowly down the aisles from the main level concourse, hands absently running along the seats as he approached the floor. he stepped easily over the hockey boards and prgressed through the floor seating towards the retaining barriers. He stopped there for a moment, and looked at what has been a battleground, and what will become a battleground again. Hopping over the barrier, he walked over to the ring steps and stepped up to the ring apron. An electric feel seemed to tingle trhough his body on contact, giving him a slight shiver. "This is where it all happens," he whispered to himself, not wanting to disturb the shadows much. He slipped between the top and middle rope, and stood erect, looking out over the empty arena, imagining the wave of sound that would pour over a wrestler when within the confines of the 'squared circle', sound which seemed to caress and envelop a person, whether it was the cheers for a beloved babyface, of the boos for a hated heel. In either case, it was appreciation from the fans, and heels loved the boos as much as faces loved the cheers. Wippit clombed to the middle turnbuckle of the nearest corner and held his arms out wide, basking in the cheers that he could hear in his head. Wrestling was what he loved, and being able to wrestle at Cybrslam was a dream come true. A dream he would relish and cherish the rest of his life. A small lyric came to mind, and he whispered it into the arena. "Feels so good to be alive..." Wip stepped down, gazing one last time around the holiest of wrestling locations, the Cyberslam ring, and then reverently stepped through the ropes again. He walked up the steel ramp to the backstage entrance, but stopped one last time at the curtain, and looked back to the ring. "Monday..." He parted the curtain and stepped through, returning Madison Square Gardens to the silence of the shadows.
