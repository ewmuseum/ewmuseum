The Xenosaga: The Deleted Scenes - Take 16

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE FOLLOWING ARE DELETED SCENES FROM THE XENOSAGA SERIES. THE REASON WHY THEY'RE DELETED IS BECAUSE MEGA JOB IS INVOLVED AND GOD FORBID THAT MEGA JOB BE INVOLVED IN THE REAL THING. "CUT!" Legos had fallen, and Allen Speck was buried up to his shins in legos. As soon as "CUT!" was heard, Allen Speck, once again, whipped out his cellular phone and dialed a number, "Leigh, you said nothing about legoes falling on me." He started to walk away, as Beef approached the Tallywhackers. "Legoes." he said, simply, glaring at Cagle, then at Dingleberry. "Well, Ah thought them were some good idears. It was either that or we brung it Ahmed Jawnson to axe kick him, and I don't reckon nobody wants that." Cagle pointed out. "Okay, legoes it is, then." Beef said, not wanting to deal with Ahmed Johnson. Ever. Somewhere in the room that Mega Job was using the film the Xenosaga, a big, fat, bald, horrible atrocity of a wrestler shed a single tear and ran out of the room, crying home to mommy. "Man, now I've got legoes stuck on my robe." Speck complained as he brushed past the crying Ahmed Johnson. Speck did a double take upon seeing him, and cupped his hand against his cell phone and turned to Mega Job and the Tallywhackers, "Hey, did any of you guys notice that Ahmed Johnson was here?" "No." Beef said. "No." Janito said. "NEGATIVE." Steve said. "Nope!" Dingleberry said. "What was the qwesturn?" Cagle asked. Speck shrugged and resumed his conversation on the phone, "What? No no no, a simple trip through a freakin' laundromat ain't gonna fix my robes. We'd need a car wash or somethin'." He wandered away. Beef shrugged and turned to someone else, "Do you think it's ethical to have an Atari Jaguar in here? I think we should burn it as soon as we're done with it." "Why are you talking to me?" the man, a guy from the fWEo by the name of Adam Nowell, asked, "I came in here because I wanted to get away from that idiot, Sarah the Jobber Slayer, for a couple of minutes, and now I have an entire ciyoke of idiots to deal with. .....What the hell is a 'ciyoke', anyway?" Beef, Janito, Steve, Dingleberry, and Cagle all huddled up and talked it over between themselves. After a minute(about three-fourths of it consisting of a conversation about how gay Janito was), they all turned to Adam Nowell. Beef was the one who spoke. "We know not of this 'ciyoke' you speak of." Nowell shrugged. Then he walked away. "ANGEL!" a woman shouted off screen, and Nowell immediately re-entered the room, shut the door behind him, and then pressed his back against the door while locking it. He then sighed. "Why me? WHY?" Nowell whined. Steve pointed and laughed at him, "HAHA." "Shut up, you," Nowell shot back, "What the hell are you idiots doing, anyway? Especially with those... cliche guys?" Cagle and Dingleberry merely shrugged. "We're filming an epic tale of kidnapping, torture, and Street Fighter: The Movie!" Beef said, excitedly, "I expect an Oscar." "I expect for you to get a Golden Razzie." Nowell said. "We need a sodding producer! Would you like to be the producer of our delightful little film?" Janito asked. Nowell thought about it, then he suddenly unlocked the door, swung it open, and shouted out, "SARAH! SARAH! WHERE ARE YOU!?" Mega Job and the Tallywhackers stared at this in shock. Then Janito turned to James Cagle, "Right then, you're the bloody producer." "Woo!" Cagle shouted, raising his arms into the air and doing the Snoopy Dance.
