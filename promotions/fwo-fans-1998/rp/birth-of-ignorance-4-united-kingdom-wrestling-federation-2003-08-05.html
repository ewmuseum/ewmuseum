Birth Of Ignorance [4] United Kingdom Wrestling Federation

5 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Trapped in the land of the free. Where to begin with the irony, opportunities everywhere if I could only accept that I would never be treated like a normal human being, if I could swallow my misanthropy and accept my designated role as a circus freak. Maybe things would be different at the other side of the pond. March 3rd 1994. Kellen Kinkade stood shivering at a location that seemed a far cry from his father's stately mansion, The CWL headquarters or the lush Titan Towers. The rain had been pissing down upon him from the second he stepped out of the plane onto British soil and didn't show any sign of stopping. He looked at the address then at the building before him and back to the address he'd noted down a final time to make sure that the heavy rain hadn't made the ink run. There had to be a mistake because in front of him stood not a mighty building but a rather dingy looking block of flats. Making his way into the building, Kellen was quick to press the button that would bring about the elevator. Jamming it several times with his thumb he watched as the numbers lit up. 6. 5. 20. 1. Sighing to himself he watched as the doors slid open to reveal a young man urinating in the corner "Sorry squire, I'll be finished in a minute." "Don't worry about it, I'll take the stairs." Kinkade swiftly replied as the man nodded his head and continued to piss in the elevator. It was typical that the offices would be on the twentieth floor, Kellen had hoped that perhaps there was some kind of miracle up there and the shabby exterior of the building was just to throw off anyone who wasn't serious about being involved with the UKWF. Kinkade reached the top floor and paced along the corridor a little until coming across an individual who was slumped against the wall with a syringe in his hand. "Excuse me there, have you got any apples?" The individual slurred whilst drooling down onto the floor. "Err, no." Kade was quick to answer, trying not to look the man in the eye as he passed beyond him. "Of course you haven't." The man said before laughing insanely to himself "You're not the tree!" Briskly continuing down the hall, Kinkade finally reached his destination... a door painted in the red, white and blue colours of the Union Jack. UKWF scrawled across it in black as Kellen gave the door several hard wraps. "Hold on a minute." A voice came from the other side. SMASH. "Come in officer, I'm sure you'll find that the-" The owner of UKWF stopped what he was saying as he opened the door "Oh, eh up... come in." Steve King scratched his head and belched, before turning back into the room. Kinkade was almost taken back at the fact that a wrestling promotion owner had come to the door wearing a heavily stained shirt and a pair of yfronts. "You could've mentioned that you weren't the fuzz, I've smashed my fucking telly and pushed it down the laundry chute now." King said with a discontented frown "So anyway, you must be that bloke from yankland." "Yes... I'm Kellen Kinkade." Kinkade said cautiously as he stepped into the room "And you're that guy." "Who was pissing in the lift... sorry about that, the shitter is broke and everytime I flush it the overflow leaks into the bedroom of the old guy downstairs. Wouldn't be so bad but I have to sit next to him on the bus in the morning and ain't too fond of smelling my own shit." Steve said with another sigh before sitting at his kitchen table to eat some cereal. "Right... well I came about a possible contract with UKWF but I can come back if now isn't convenient." Kinkade said, sitting down on a chair before observing the pile of books propping it up at one side. "Whnownabeeconvenunt?" Steve answered with a mouthful of cereal. "Sorry?" Kinkade asked as King swallowed down what he was eating. "I said why wouldn't now be convenient, cloth ears." King shook his head and went back to eating. "Well I was just wondering if you'd watched the tapes I sent you, and what you thought of my possibly signing with the company... I heard that you'd taken on Pierre LeSmith a while back and I've worked with him before." Kellen spoke before watching Steve scowl furiously. "Don't mention that French cunt in this house again." King uttered before spitting furiously on the floor "Yeah I saw the tapes, bit swanky though... had to take them down to Dave's. You yanks with your advanced technology." "VHS was no good?" Kinkade raised an eyebrow. "Betamax mate, betamax." Kinkade looked over at the video player that looked like it'd come from the stone age before mustering an "I'll keep it in mind... so you liked what you saw?" "Yeah." Steve picked up the bowl and drank the milk that was left in the bottom "Fairly impressive." "So we can sort out the terms and contracts and I can debut?" Kellen asked somewhat excitedly. "Yeah." King remained somewhat disinterested "It should be hilarious." There was a moment of silence. "Hilarious?" Kinkade thought maybe King was drunk and had used the wrong wording "What do you mean by that?" "You know... ahohoho, teeheehee, bahaha." Steve elaborated "It should be quality entertainment, we English love to laugh at the less fortunate... spazzy people, nutters, freaks like you." Kinkade stood up from his seat and angrily kicked away the books that were holding it up. "Oi, that took ages to even out." The UKWF owner shouted angrily "So yeah... as soon as you're ready we'll get you in the ring, you'll have people in stitches. Maybe you can take out your eye and throw it at people for a finisher, or roll it across the canvas so that they trip over it, we can do a few promo packages where you go around pubs sneezing and dropping it in peoples pints." Kellen felt the same bile rising as he had when he'd spoken to McMahon. "You know what's more hilarious? A pathetic limey pissing in an elevator and speaking like he's got a dick in his mouth. I don't want to work for a bum like you anyway so you can get your shits and giggles from the lunatic down the hall who thinks you're an apple tree." Storming furiously out of Steve's flat, Kinkade slowly realized that he'd spent some of his last dollars on a wasted trip to a foreign country. Back in the room Steve sat speechless at what'd just transpired before finally shaking his head. "Cock."
