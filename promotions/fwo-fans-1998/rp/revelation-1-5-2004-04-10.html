Revelation 1:5

10 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have always been a man of very few words. My counterpart generally does the talking for us both, but when I am asked to comment I do so as clearly and succinctly as possible. I do not believe in "beating around the bush"--if there's a point to be made, then its admittance and understanding should not be veiled. Now that I have gotten the formalities out of the way, I shall begin. My partner and I are here for one purpose, and one purpose alone: to protect Lindsay Troy, no matter what the circumstances, no matter what the obstacles, no matter what. Some might call us vigilantes. Some might call what we're doing a mission. They may be right on both counts. We've come back because we were asked to look out for her and to thwart any potential dangers that might see harm befall her. The person who asked this of us is the person who cannot be with her at this time. He has disappeared from the public eye for well over one year. And he did so at a Calling. He did so because there's some unspeakable force that keeps him from him Queen. He did so...out of love. Lindsay does not know our ties to the one that she calls King. She does not know that we were sent to A1E to ensure that she was healthy and well. She does not know that we have been in contact with her Estranged. She cannot know. Not now. And not by our utterance. She is in good hands in A1E. We know this because He knows this. Everywhere else she may roam is where the danger lies. There are forces at work that cannot possibly be comprehended by those on the outside. If Lindsay falls, We fall; and when He Lifts us up, he will seek to bring forth a mighty reckoning that no man and woman could possibly withstand. Heed my Revelation. Heed my Warning. Those who harbor ill-will and those who seek destruction will fail...because we cannot.
