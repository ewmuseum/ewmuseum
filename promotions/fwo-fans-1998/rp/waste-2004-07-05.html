Waste

5 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The ear-splitting screams was keeping the hospital up all night. Being stuck in a hospital bed with her neck strangled by a brace, Nerva was in an absolute rage. The nurses had given up going into her room. It wasn't worth it anymore. Nerva just kept on screaming. This was a type of rage she had never expressed before. Sure, there were times when she broke an arm, twisted an ankle, or dislocated a shoulder - but all of these injuries could be dealt with. An injury had never limited her as a competitor. When there was a fight, Nerva was always there regardless of her body's condition. But this injury wouldn't let her suck up the pain and fight. When Amy Campbell dumped her on her neck, it changed everything. Now she couldn't even sit up on her own. For once in her life, she was dependent on others for the simplest things in her life. She was sucking food through a straw. This was a type of humiliation Nerva could not swallow. It was like being raped. The future was unsure. Would she walk again? Would she fight again? Most important: would she ever get revenge on Aimz? These thoughts ran through her mind as she continue to shriek in rage. She didn't want to be here in the hospital. She didn't want to be like this. She didn't want her whole life of blood, sweat, and tears to add up to a moment like this. Eventually her vocal cords gave in as she was unable to even make a sound. She closed her eyes, and soon after tears began rolling out of the corners of her eyes. She went to sleep with a final thought in mind: I am Nerva's wasted life.
