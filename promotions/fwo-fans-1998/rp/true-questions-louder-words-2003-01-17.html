True Questions...Louder Words

17 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I could only gaze out my window, peering down at the traffic that rushed by, wondering to my self, and pondering the same question over in my mind, till the risk of answering seemed logical. "Was it worth it," I asked, banging my forehead against the thin glass that vibrated in the wall joints. The cold surface provided some relief but it didn't take away the pain of my emotions. My mind was still racing and the very fact of being here; surrounded by those I looked to for support, drove a stake of fear through my heart. I could only slump my body into my sofa and hold my hands over the pain that grew on my forehead. My eyes burned a whole through the floorboards, and the time of day seemed to move by at a quick pace. My mind wish for her to join me, but this was neither the time nor the place for her at this time. "They are all respected highly, aren't they," I asked, waiting for the gentle kiss of the night to answer my call. I could only sink deeper into the sofa and wait for a true answer to come. But who was I kidding, there was no easy answer, but just years of painful thoughts and ideas. Those ideas were over and soon I could finally do what I could have only dreamed about for years. I finally made it, and no one, not even my family could take that away from me. I was on the fast track to fame... NO... Immortality... I achieved something; many only get the chance to dream of. I became something much more then a simple man, but I have become a symbol of dreams that do come true. I show my fear to the world, but in a sense, only a small few can really see what I mean. I did not want handouts, but only what was rightfully mine. I looked down again at the busy street that was my reality. I work for perfection... And that very perfection could be my greatest regret... But my respect must start somewhere; my mind must follow a path. I have a goal and sure enough it will be followed. The cold pain of glass, ripped up my spin and into my cheeks. My breath appeared on the window like a graceful storm brewing in the distant clouds. I returned to my sofa, crashing my small frame into its comfortable pillows. There was so much more to think. Would I be respected and honored for the things I have done, or simply tossed aside. This was not a question I could answer. There was only one person that could answer my thoughts and enter my mind. He would decide my fate...
