Oh...a gime! (Part 2)

24 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Oh...a gime! (Part 2) January 24, 2000 ::The scene opens to Sphere and Jackhammer, the Guano Apes, still marvelling at the various workout equipment. While they stand there and survey the area, two men run by in back of them, snapping each other with towels:: Sphere: Well, what should we try first? Jackhammer: How about those? ::Jackhammer points to a rack of dumbells, so the two walk over to it:: Sphere: What are we supposed to do with them? Jackhammer: I believe you stand in back of the rack, while I sit down in front of it. When I give you the signal, you start shaking the rack, while I try to blindly grab as many dumbells as I can. Sphere: Gotcha. ::Sphere walks in back of the rack, and sets his hands on each side of it. Jackhammer sits down in front of it, cracks his knuckles, and flips Sphere the bird, signaling him to start shaking the rack. As suspected, one by one, each of the dumbells fall on Jackhammer's head. Sphere doesn't notice that all of the dumbells have fallen off the rack, and continues to shake it, causing more than a few on-lookers to believe he is having numerous body spasms. Jackhammer, uninjured by this incident gets up and stops Sphere from shaking the rack, informing him of what has happened:: Jackhammer: I've lost a step. I didn't grab one. Sphere: Well I thought that was quite exhilirating! Jackhammer: Exhilirating? Sphere: I mean...fun. Jackhammer: I see. What now? Sphere: How about that thing they call a "treadmill"? ::Sphere points to a treadmill that is already occupied by a young man around the age of 17. The two walk over to him, only to encounter a few vulgar words coming out of the teen's mouth. The teen turns his head and smirks at a young woman on the excercise bike near him right before Jackhammer quickly pushes the "accelerate" button. The teen loses control, spouts out one more vulgar term, and goes crashing into the wall behind him. The treadmill is still accelerating as Sphere decides to give it a try. Sphere sets one foot on, but goes flying into the teen, who is pretty much stuck in the wall. Jackhammer now decides to give it a try, but he suffers the same fate. Sphere sees him coming, so he moves out of the way, and Jackhammer goes flying into the teen, who is pretty much just a decoration now:: Sphere: That was even better than the dumbells. Jackhammer: Yes it was. Sphere: Hmm, why don't we go in there? ::Sphere points to a room with a label reading "Children's Jungle Gym":: Jackhammer: If it's made for children, then certainly we will be able to handle it. Sphere: Of course. ::Both walk into the room, and as soon as the door shuts behind them, screams are heard, as the camera fades to black, with a graphic reading "Too predictable. Uh, we mean...to be continued"::
