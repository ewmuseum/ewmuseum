previous parts: see-downfall archive. "I don't expect you to be too excited. You haven't said a word throughout this entire process," Daniel Moore said. The backstage was quiet considering the events that had transpired. Billy Lovemuscle had won the final WWR Open Invitational event, Downfall coming in a close second. There was more important news, though. Downfall had been announced the overall winner. A few men stepped into the locker room, still dressed in their black tights and simple clothing. Their hair remained drenched in sweat. These are the men Downfall had just defeated, and these are the men he had trained with over the past three months. They came bearing bottles of wine and congratulations, but Downfall would have none of it. He stepped out of the room, feeling unworthy of such happiness or praise. All of this was just so he could assure himself a spot doing what he felt he had to do. The trainer, Daniel Moore, stepped out of the locker room and tapped Downfall on the shoulder. Reluctantly, Trent turned around and stared blankly. "You go back into that room and celebrate. Do you think you're better than us just because you won this thing? You'll see just how hard real wrestling is when you get into it." People always mistook his silence and apathy toward their speech as some sort of ego. There was nothing Downfall could do but take it in stride and continue on. Fans emptied the arena in seconds, waiting the exit of their favorite stars. The crowd erupted as Downfall paced through the clearing. Amidst the screaming voices and reaching hands, he sighed outwardly into the chilled late winter night. The cloud faded as he continued down. Guardrails rattled as the fans pressured. Trent couldn't pretend to understand their motives. He was nothing special. Just a silent man that out-lucked a bunch of has-beens or rookies
