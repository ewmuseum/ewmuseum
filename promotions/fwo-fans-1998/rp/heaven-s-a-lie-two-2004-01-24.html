Heaven's A Lie - Two

24 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The couch made napping difficult. Her disorder didn't help...neither did the sudden company. He must've heard her shuffling about on the couch because Jarod Roche bolted into the room like the lightning on his daddy's tights and, judging by the sudden disappointment when he saw it was Amy laying there, he'd been looking for his father. Then again, he always expressed a degree of dismay in her presence... No less, and despite her fear of not ever being accepted by the boy so close to the love of her life, Campbell tried her hardest to win him over every time, with the same smile and bright eyes as usual. "Hey, you." "Hi, where's Dada? He was 'sposed to be here..." The words just made it even more evident, his disappointment. Things had calmed a bit however, it had been quite awhile since he had taken to sabotaging random objects around the house to 'take her out'. A flat smile came across his face as he climbed to the edge of the sofa's arm. "Watch dis." And before she could react Jarod sailed from the side landing a perfect elbow drop to her ribs. A move so perfect it would make Hulk Hogan jealous...well, most kids futile attempts at wrestling would make him jealous, but still. The pain was real though as his pointy elbow made contact. Jarod screaming "Elbow Drop." still hung in the air as Amy grasped her side. Biting her lip and exhaling sharply, the redhead very swiftly sat up - out of a vulnerable position - wincing as she moved. He hadn't thrown or hit her with any blunt objects lately, no...but even toddlers can inflict physical damage beyond their years. "How 'bout we try a new approach?" She sighed, sitting him down on the floor in front of her. "Here's the deal, sweetie: You don't hurt me for a bit, and you'll...uhhh...I'll...hmm. Okay, we're just putting up a new 'No moves on Amy' rule." Jarod stared. Amy pondered whether or not the stories of children murdering were really only urban legends. He continued staring. She continued wondering whether or not looks really could kill. Finally, she broke eye contact. "Your dad's sleeping in the other room, his head was achey and stuff...so it's just you and I." Another attempted smile. She could only pray Jarod eventually bought into the kindness. Himself realizing that looks couldn't kill, or hurt much, Jarod sighed. "We can't westle?" He pouted a bit. The secret weapon, the lip, extended. "It's you and me what?" "I'm a little sore," Amy tried grinning, noticing that they both had the same pouty 'secret weapon' helped a little, "So no wrestling for now. Maybe later, though...and it's just you and I here for now. We've got a little time to talk, so whatcha' wanna talk about?" "Army men." A two word response that struck a little fear in the red raver. "Toys?" As much as she wanted to be on the little boy's side, there were more pressing issues the two needed to discuss if she was ever going to win him over. So the topic took a turn..."Well, how about we figure out what you're gonna wear to the wedding?" Accenting that last word probably wouldn't help. A cocked eyebrow too reminiscent of his father to be funny showed that Jarod wasn't too pleased with the topic change. Why'd she ask if she didn't want to talk about his G.I.Joe collection? Who wouldn't want to talk about his G.I.Joe collection? Another sigh, "How 'bout camouflage?" "How about something like what your daddy's gonna wear?" She winked, "You don't want camouflage...you Roche boys are warriors, but that's big and scary. You don't wanna be big and scary for your dad's big day, do ya?" "How about a helmet then?" A big grinned followed. "Or a cute little suit?" "A penguin suit? How 'bout a mask wit' it?" Amy grimaced, "You could always wear a dress like me." "Why would you wear a dress, you not like a girl or nut'in?" "I am too a girl! I just don't talk about girly things because you'd run away." She winked, "And I think you should wear a tuxedo suit, like your dad and everybody else. Sean's going to!" "What's a traiter? You got somethin' in your eye?" "I...uhhh......what? There's nothing in my eye. And a traiter's a bad person," Like your mommy, she thought...but didn't dare say it. "Do you know what a wedding is?" Persistantly, she was poking around to see if there was any disdain regarding his father marrying again...Amy could only hope he understood. "You keep blinkin'. Weddin's are when a guy and girl get dressed up, and give each ot'er rings. Den they fight." Jarod locked eyes again. "Why you're eyes two dif'rent colors? Is one broken?" "Well, your dad and I aren't gonna fight. I want you to know that, okay?" Then, to his question..."...No. Neither of them are broken. They're colored just like yours, only one is different than the other...no real reason, it just is. Has been since I was born. It's like why's your hair blonde? Just is." "No, my hair's blonde 'cause of pigment. Is that the same den? You do too fight I hear'd ya before." Jarod's lips screwed sideways. He fully expected things to be the way they were with his Mom and Dad. "Sweetie...we argue, but I'm not your mom." That needed to be rephrased before he took it the wrong way, "...And that doesn't necessarily mean I'm better than her," eventhoughiam "...It just means I'm a different type of person. Your dad and I get along great, and we can work through any problems we have. Your dad and your mom just couldn't...that's not their fault, it's just the way things were meant to be. With he and I, they're meant to be different." "Well, I don' like fightin' no matter what. A suit huh? What color? No tie right?" Jarod's eyes lit up, like he could flick them on with a switch. They seemed to grow in size as well. Large round portals, burning into Amy's soul. "...We'll discuss the tie." She said. Another subject change was in order as a question tore a hole in her. "Jarod, I'm gonna ask you something and you've gotta answer with the truth. What do you think of me, and what do you think of me being with your dad?" "Being wit' Dada? Like when you two are in the bedroom and Sea' an' me can hear you fightin' and stuff? I like you. You're not Mama. Just dif'rent." Jarod shrugged his shoulders. "Then how come you act like you don't really like me much?" "Just teasing." Jarod said, squinting his eyes a bit as he did. Amy was floored. All of that torture, all of her own worry and he'd just been teasing?! "You...y'mean you...like me?" The ploy worked. "Sure." An innocent smile followed. Quite suddenly, the redhead felt a chill. "Jarod...you're not planning my death or anything, are ya?" "No Amy, I said I teasing." Jarod's smile was a bit more sincere this time. He had been practicing after all. "I jus' don' know you dat well. It makes me mad sometimes that you are here and Mama ain't." She let that hang in the air. This discussion was a pointless roundabout they tried having every few weeks. The only way to figure the youngster out was through his father - his larger twin.
