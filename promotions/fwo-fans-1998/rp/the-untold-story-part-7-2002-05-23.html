The Untold Story(Part 7)

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Linda Johns was a VERY attractive woman. Standing at nearly 5ft, 8 inches tall - without heels - she came across as head strong, determined, and about business. When she was in the office, she was a complete professional. More often than not, dressed in expensive, Versace business suits, matching shoes, and a leather briefcase. How she conducted herself inside the work world, made it virtually impossible to see her as anything other... ... that is... until you actually SAW her outside the work world. Linda Johns was an All American girly- girl in the truest sense. She cried during sad movies... Watched sports with her 'man' - or by herself - depending upon her relationship status. And, had a great sense of humor. During the weekend, she traded in the business suits for one of her all time favorite outfits - sweatpants, and a comfortable, fitted t- shirt. That's what drew her to Sean "Triple X" Stevens. But, on this day... she was "in character". Linda the fun loving, girl next door was gone. In her place... Linda Johns, personal assistant to one of coldest, shrewd businessmen on the planet - Edward Brown. Stevens - "We need to talk..." Sean swallowed hard, as Linda turned her attention from the coffee pot to him. A look of surprise came across her face. She had been waiting for this moment for months. And, finally... it had come. Sean Stevens... the man she truly believed she loved... the guy that she felt was destined for her... was standing right across the room. Johns - "Sean? That you?!?" she smiled. "When did you grow your hair back?" She took about five steps in Sean's direction. "It looks SO good!" Linda extended her arms. As if she expected something. A warm embrace... a kiss... Sean didn't know. And, even though his heart began to beat so fast it felt like it'd jump out of his chest at any moment... he could honestly say... without any doubt what- so- ever... Linda Johns wasn't the person for him. Beautiful? Yes. Intelligent? No doubt. But, there was certain things about her that just wasn't right. Like her hair... why was it the same thing day in and day out, night in and night out? As long as Sean could remember it'd been in one of two styles... either hanging down her back... or in a pony- tail, and THAT was usually the style she wore it when she was in the comfort of her own home. She needed to mix it up, he thought. Maybe some braids... maybe dabble with a different color, or two... maybe have the tips one color, and the hair and roots another similar color. And, what was up with the business suit? Sure it was professional... but, why not be comfortable for once. Maybe a skirt... a cool shirt... and, perhaps... some fishnet stockings? Stevens - "No... no hugs, Linda. I don't think that's such a good idea." Linda paused for a moment. Her joyous facial expression turned frown. Johns - "What's going on? Why? Aren't we? ... Aren't you here to see m--" Stevens - "I'm here to tell you that I did LOVE you, Linda. I know when we were together, you had your insecurities about where my loyalty was... and, I'm here to tell you that it WAS with you." Johns - "Then, what's the problem, Sean? Do you even know how EXCITED I was to here we were coming to Florida? This is a day I've dreamed about, ever since I last saw you. I know we went through... I had my problems... you... you were stuck on Ivy McGinnis..." Sean's face grew blank at the mere mention of Ivy's name. "... that's what this is all about, isn't it? Her. What's going on?" Stevens - "Linda you left me... and, I know how this looks... trust me, I do. But, it's not like that. Ivy and I got together AFTER you and I broke up. I swear." Johns - "Sure, Sean. Tell me anything to make it better. I QUIT my job for you! I gave up my LIFE for you! And, this is how you repay me?" Sean's tone was low... Stevens - "You also left me. I'm sorry, Linda..." Johns - "Sorry?!? You're sorry? You've gotta be kidding me! So what now? You choosing her?" Stevens - "Linda... I chose her." A tear formed in the corner of Linda's eye. Instinct alone told Sean to wipe it... and, embrace her. Hug her tight and let her know it'll all be okay. But, he realized right then and there... that wasn't his job anymore. And, if he had anything to do with it... it'd never be again. Johns - "I can't believe this... so... you can look me in my eyes and honestly say... you don't love me?" Stevens - "I'll always have love FOR you, Linda. You were a great friend... but, I'm In Love with someone else. I'm sorry." Sean turned his back to Linda that day... for good, he hoped. And even though the entire episode was sad... on the inside... he rejoiced. Because the future was clear... and, nothing else stood in the way of he and his destiny. Ivy Lillian McGinnis. ****** LAX airport. Hollywood, California. Poison Ivy boarded her flight with peace of mind. Maybe Sean was right. Maybe she had jumped to conclusions. Ivy did in fact LOVE Sean. And, she refused to let another man she loved exit her life because of... what he labeled... a misunderstanding. As the flight attendant ushered Ivy to her seat... she slowly began to drift off. Sean had no idea Ivy was coming to see him. But, she knew how excited he'd be when he realized she did. She could just picture the look on his face. Ivy couldn't wait to see it.
