Five: Debauchery

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Gluttony is an inordinate desire to consume more than that which one requires." Damn him. Damn her. Damn the other two hims. Damn them all. Ric was stewing at the Dallas Hyatt, and as if by instinct, he had left his hotel room around 5 in the morning. After all, he couldn't sleep. Luckily, the bar had been open, and sidling up to it, he watched the vacuous entertainment of ESPN2. "Can I get you something?" Ric looked back at the bartender, and murmured "vanilla Coke" before turning back to the TV. "Right. One rum and Coke coming up." The bartender quickly poured the drink, and set it down in front of Chronos. "That's $2.50." "I get free refills on this?" Chronos asked, not truly paying attention. The bartender snorted. "Yeah, right. We're not a free store, buddy." Chronos glared at the bartender, and fumbled for his wallet, eventually producing a $50. "Here. Run me a tab. I don't like being bothered, so just make sure it gets filled." The bartender slapped the $50 into the register, and regarded the massive stranger with the type of contempt usually reserved for only the worst derelicts. Chronos swallowed a large gulp, and noticed that there was a bit more of a kick to beverage than he expected, but that the taste was not unlike the vanilla Coke he had ordered. The stupid bartender had probably given him a Diet, but Ric just wasn't in the mood to argue. *****10 drinks later***** Chronos stared at the glass, which was about half full. He'd spent too much time learning about himself as an alcoholic not to notice the effects the drinks had been having on him... It was getting harder to concentrate, and the sumo wrestling on ESPN2 was now not only entertaining, but downright hilarious. But he no longer cared. He pulled another $50 out of his wallet, and joined it with two $20's. "Keep'em coming," he growled. *****8 more drinks later***** "T'hell with 'er. I thought she loved me, but the bitsch was juss using me. Prolly some shtupid prank planned by that Frontier punk. Or John. Prolly mad cause I wasn't even at hish funeral. John'sh a bashtard too, when it comes to shtuff like that. But it's not John, no, John's dead, it's Fallen. Ryan. Ryan's Fallen, and I hate him for it." "Sorry, buddy, I have no idea what you're talking about." the barkeep sardonically replied. "T'fuff? Scourse ya do. I'm Chronosh, 'n I'm an fW... fW... uh, fWo - yeah, that's it. fWo Champeen." The bartender rolled his eyes. It was way too early in the morning to be dealing with this. "Sure, buddy, champion of the world. Whatever." *****and 9 more drinks after that***** "Whaddaya meen, 'cut off'?" The bartender, although not amongst the brightest of creatures, was still able to easily identify the threat level of the drunk before him. This was going to be pretty, and the guy *was* huge. Maybe he legitimately was a champion somewhere, but the bartender couldn't let this guy go any further. "It means, sir, that I will not continue to serve you alcohol, because you are drunk. What else could it mean? Now, sit down or head back to your room - but don't even try to cause a scene." The bartender hoped that the word would be stern enough, and that the bear of a man would be appeased - but he couldn't help the sarcasm that filtered through to the comments. "MORE!" screamed Chronos. "I WANT MORE! NOW!" Uh-oh. This was definitely a turn for the worse. Time to hit the security button... ...but not quickly enough. With a speed that the bartender didn't think could be possessed by the drunk, Chronos had grabbed the bartender by the collar, and pinned him back against the far wall. The stench of Chronos' breath nearly caused the bartender to gag, as he flinched, preparing for the worst. "RIC!" a shrill voice pierced the air, and the bartender visibly relaxed as Chronos released his hold. "Who?" Chronos tried to focus first one eye, then the next, but neither was seeming to gather a more complete vision of his surroundings than the other. And then, in a shot, clarity came to him. He was plastered out of his mind, and in front of him stood the only woman he had ever given a damn about. Her cold hand was on his arm, restraining, pleading. But it was her eyes that had shocked him back to sobriety - the pitied, begging expression, and most of all... the hurt. The tears were flooding Ric's eyes now, and he experienced the repentance that only the inebriated can express. Ric buried his head in her arms, sobbing. "Shorry.... I'm so shorry..."
