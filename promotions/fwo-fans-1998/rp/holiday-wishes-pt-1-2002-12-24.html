Date: Monday, Dec. 16 Location: Hotel Room Julie opened her medical bag and pulled out a small flashlight. Gingerly she took David Cross by the chin and shone the light into his eyes, checking his pupil's reaction. "So is it usually like this?" she asked. "Is what usually like what?" David asked her back. "Do you usually get beaten up that bad?" She turned off the flashlight and slipped it back into her bag. "Take off your shirt," she said as she stood up and walked toward the ice bucket. "Now we're talking!" David gingerly pulled his shirt up and over his head, cringing with the effort. "But be gentle with me. I'm very fragile right now." Julie returned with a handful of ice cubes wrapped in a towel. "Good God David! You're a mess!" She eased the cold towel against his bruised flesh. He jumped slightly, and regretted doing so. "I don't want you wrestling that monster again. He could have seriously hurt you." The air whistled as David sucked in a breath, moving the ice pack to another sore spot. "Hey, I'm tougher than I look. They're just bruises. Nothing's broken, sprained, cracked, or twisted. It's all good." "It's not all good!" Julie stood up and folded her arms in front of her. "I knew you were a wrestler. I knew you took bumps and such. But this guy beat the Hell out of you! Next time he could do more substantial damage to you!" "So Vic was a little stiff. It's nothing anybody's surprised by when we wrestle him. He doesn't like to hold back, so nobody else does either when they fight him." David set the ice pack down on the bed. He reassured Julie as he stood up. "I'm fine. Really." She didn't look very reassured, so David kissed her on the forehead. "It's okay." He kissed her cheek, her neck. Her arms slowly began to unfold, slipping to her side. "I'm okay." He kissed her lips. She kissed him back. Julie's hands moved from her side to his, pulling him close. She saw David wince. "Did I hurt you?" David smiled. "No. All this kissing is giving me guy cramps." Julie threw her head back and laughed, allowing David to kiss her neck again. "I'm sorry. I just worry about you. I... I love you." David froze, paralyzed by an onrush of emotions. Julie looked into his face and saw the stupified expression he had. Immediately she regretted revealing her feeling for him, thinking she'd made a horrible mistake. "Oh God... I'm sorry... I shouldn't have..." Forcing himself to speak, David stopped her. "Don't apologize. Y-you're the first girl who's ever said that to me." Julie was hesitant. "Is that a good thing?" David's smile returned. "It's not good. It's great. I've loved you since the first moment I saw you working in the hospital." They began kissing more passionately, lowering themselves onto the bed. She straddled his hips and began unfastening his pants, then stopped. "Have you... you know... ever done it before?" "Who me? Oh sure. I've done it plenty of times," Cross said. "I'm...I'm just usually alone when I do it." This time Julie smiled. "Don't worry about it. You're fragile right now. I'll be gentle." Cross pulled her down to him. "Not too gentle I hope."
