Return to the Wall 1/2

21 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"What the hell is this?" You'd think the answer would be obvious by now. Eli Flair is standing in the middle of a very large open field with no end in sight... Dressed in a pair of boxers and a plain black T-shirt with the sleeves cut off, he's rapidly walking toward something he just asked nobody in particular to identify. But he's just fooling himself. It's eight feet tall or so... It's made of white stones.... It's got a single space to look through to the other side... THE WALL. Eli doesn't know whether to laugh or to cry. "You gonna make up your mind, or are you gonna stand there in your skivvies and stare?" Eli spins around at the sound of a familiar voice, and he comes face to face, quite literally, with himself. The Eliminator of 1994, at the prime of his life, with a career filled with promise, with Alicia by his side, with the PYBA United States Title that seemed like such a boon at the time. Eli Flair notices a white stone in his hand that looks like it could easily fit the space. "Make up my mind about what?" he asked, "And where the hell am I?" "You're inside the Wall again," replied The Eliminator, "Did you really think the psychobabble from the shrink would really help? You're fucked up, you know it, and the only difference now is that you've got authoritative proof. But we've got a problem right now... And you need to take care of it." The Eliminator walks up to the wall, holding up his white stone. Yeah... It definitely fits the space. "Y'see," began the Eliminator, "those of us inside your head are getting a little pissed off. On the one hand, your feelings of isolation got us started on rebuilding the Wall that you and that April girl knocked down a while back, but on the other, this new girl Angela has kept you from shutting things down completely. It's quite annoying." "So...." Replied Eli, "you want me to make a choice. Choose life or choose isolation. One or the other. No middle ground." "It'd be nice for all concerned." Several other forms, all without definite features, have begun to circle the two, moving in on Eli Flair very slowly. "That's easy," said Eli, "I choose life. I chose life a long time ago." The forms stop. "Did you?" Silence. "How can you say you've chosen life, when those around you don't know a thing about it? How can you say you've chosen life when you're currently lying next to a woman you're afraid to open up to?" "What?" "We'll be in touch, Eli." The Faceless moves his hand very quickly to slap him--- ---and Eli Flair wakes up with a start. He looks around.... He's in his own bed in Los Angeles. To his left is an open window, with a cool breeze flowing into the room. To his right, Angela Davison is sound asleep. Goddamn, he says to himself, she's beautiful. Eli lays back down, but he can't concentrate. He can't think. He can't fall asleep. It doesn't take long before he's on the telephone. "Ivy?" ... "Yeah, I know it's late. Yes, I know you're three hours later than me. But I really needed to talk to someone..." It's going to be a long night.
