Buzzed: Side B

9 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Leigh Landers stirred awake from her somewhat uncomfrotable sleeping position where she was seated against the wall. The musical tone that wanted so desperately to be the sound of a phone ringing had buzzed in Allen Speck's neglected leather jacket that hung against the arm of one of the room's chairs. She mumbled incoherently to herself and sleepilly opened her eyes. Allen didn't budge from his bed, still zonked out from the alcohol intake, and the phone wasn't gonna stop ringing until one of them answered it. So, Leigh managed to idly guide her right hand into the pocket of the jacket and pull out the offending device, before she flicked it open and answered it with a yawn, before her light English accent said "Hello" to whoever it was that she was speaking to. "I'm sorry, do I have the wrong number? I'm looking for Mr. Allen Speck." the voice on the other end, belonging to a serious-sounding man, who sounded somewhat aged, but weathered by the situations dealt upon he and the company that he helped to lead. "Right number, wrong timing," Leigh explained, trying to wake up somewhat, "I'm his friend, Leigh." "Leigh, huh? Where's Allen, then?" "Sleeping. If I woke him up now, he'd be awfully cranky," Leigh explained with a frown on her face, "So, if you've got anything to say, Mister..." she continued, before she paused, as she didn't know who was even calling. "Silver Fox." "Mr. Silver Fox. What I'm trying to say is that, as Allen's best friend and travel companion, you can trust me with whatever information you have regarding him, and that I can relay it to him as soon as he wakes up and gets over his hangover." Leigh said. "Hangover?" "It's a Ciyoke thing that Allen dragged me to so I could drive him home." Leigh explained. "I don't want any incidents with the roster, Miss Landers. We have enough of those as it is, especially with our television deals going belly-up." Silver Fox said, causing Leigh to bolt up from her seated position. Not wishing to wake up Allen from his alcohol-induced slumber, Leigh moved into the bathroom and shut the door. "Mr. Fox, I assure you that Allen's not going to cause any trouble. He has me to keep an eye on him, after all." Leigh explained. "And who are you, again?" "Leigh Morris Landers. I've been travelling with Allen since he got signed, since..." Leigh said, before she giggled, "Well, let's just say that I'm his sole beacon of sanity within the fWo, and call it even. I just take care of him, that's all." "I see." Fox said, before there was a pause, "Am I correct in assuming that you're a wrestler, yourself?" "Yes, yes, I am!" Leigh said, excitedly, "How'd you guess?" "Just a hunch." Fox said, "Anyway, I was calling to remind Mr. Speck that we've reviewed his request to change his gimmick, and we simply can't do it. We like him where he is, and while we have plans for him to at least get some in-ring action some time in the future, we can't do much of anything until he's been a manager for most of the year." "But his injury will be healed in three months! Just in time for your next pay-per-view! Can't you reconsider?" Leigh pleaded. "I'm sorry." Fox said, before he had to say his goodbyes and hung up the phone. Leigh sighed to herself as she placed the phone next to the sink. She wondered to herself exactly why she was still helping Allen along, even as her own career hadn't exactly taken off since coming back from her injury. Thinking about the injury made her stare at the scar on her arm again, before she left the confines of the bathroom, and finally went to bed. On a bed. When she woke up again, another three hours later, she would tell Allen about the phone call. He would not take it too well.
