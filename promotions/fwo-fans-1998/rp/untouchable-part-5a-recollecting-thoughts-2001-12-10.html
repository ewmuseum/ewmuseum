Untouchable // Part.5a // Recollecting Thoughts

10 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I always win my battles. While Steve Douglas is somewhere in upstate New York, getting his brains bashed in by some hillbilly hulk, Angel Dalton is probably still trying to recover from our chance encounter yesterday. Yes, I believe I'm still on top and it's going to stay that way because... I'm untouchable. Kenny Rock's neck pinched together as he felt his esophagus tighten, still a little strained by the strangled grip his arch-nemesis Steven Douglas had given him yesternight. Kenny stared into his reflection again, as it has become his custom, and traced his fingers across his chest, against his self-inflicted scar, noticing that it was completely healed. Kenny frowned and reached for his sledgehammer and cocked it back, ready to swing. But he was interrupted. Interrupted by the clamor of a woman's voice that deafened him. Her voice was coarse and raspy, blending in with the static of the intercom she spoke in. Kenny clutched Almighty harder and swung, sending shards of mirrored glass flying in every direction. Kenny's countenance is painted crimson as he chuckles to himself. "Steven Douglas has no idea what pAIN is. He doesn't know it like I do." Kenny's eyes shifted around constantly, struggling to find something to focus on. His mind began to wander to his encounter with Psychiatrist Dr. Roberts earlier in the day.
