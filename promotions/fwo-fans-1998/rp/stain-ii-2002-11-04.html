Stain II

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rahl slammed the phone down quickly after hearing the caller's voice. "How'd he get this number? How does he know where I am? Does he know where I am? Is he falling me again?" Rahl shot up from the position that he was lying in on his bed; it was safe to say that Rahl was paranoid. His head was scattering all over the room, looking left, and looking right, looking up, looking down. His eyes were blood shot red and were twitching every now and then. His forehead broke out in a cold sweat He looked down at his arms, the same arms that reminded him of the bad times, all scarred up from years and years of drug abuse. Then he spotted a tattoo on his right arm it ran down from his elbow to his wrist on the inside of his arm. As it said "You can not kill me." Rahl whispered those words into the thin air in his room, followed by..."I will not die." Areumna'derin got up from his bed and walked over to the bathroom. He turned the left knob as cold water splattered out. Rahl cupped his hands together and formed a temporary small bowl and splashed water over his face trying to snap himself out of his daze that he had gone into. Rahl was slowly able to regain control of himself, as he wasn't as paranoid anymore. His eyes had died down a little bit, and they stopped twitching then...the phone rang again. Rahl jumped from the sound of the phone rang. It had to have been hours since the phone rang. Rahl dashed over to his bed and slide a crossed it in a state of panic, he reached over onto his nightstand and turned a clock to face him, it was only 3:37 am. It had only been 5 minutes....
