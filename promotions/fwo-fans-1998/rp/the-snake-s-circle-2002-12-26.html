The Snake's Circle.

26 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was minutes before fWo's Twilight would see the air... and The Fuse Bros. anxiously sat in their locker rooms, hands in their faces, motioning back and forth with a nervous twitch as they watched the Pay-Per-View channel. No one said a word... they hadn't spoken to each other for at least two hours. They just sat there and swung their upper bodies back and forth with their heads in their hands, as they peeked through the cracks of their fingers, hoping that their first big match, and world one, level one, would bring them a hell of a lot of success. Their hats were not on. For they laid on the table in front of the sofa, as the giant big screen's glow of blue played over the wood... Then it happened. The opening kicked in, as the "Warning" screen appeared, forcing Tyler Fuse to stand up from his seat and watch intensely at the about-to-begin broadcast. Conor, on the other hand, hid himself even deeper into his hands... as he fell back to where Tyler had sat, and curled up into a ball. It was a weird sight to see... but then again in their first big Match, with absolutely everything riding on the line, anyone could be this nervous... and maybe even more. The Fuse Bros. didn't expect this to come out of world one, level one... they didn't expect to be put into an fWo Tag Team Championship contest... and a chance to already become the best. They didn't expect that at all... for what they did expect, though, was a few easy henchmen, the odd loser Boss... and then to just move on from there. Triality... Mercenaries... The Freak Show... This was not the case. And it scared them so far like nothing else had. After the "Warning" introduction, the prelude to the Pay-Per-View began... and Tyler grabbed his brown hat, putting it on his head as he looked down at his Brother, still quivering in a ball below. He tapped him on the shoulder as Conor slowly looked up. And handing his younger Brother his hat, Tyler offered his hand to help Conor up. Once to his feet
