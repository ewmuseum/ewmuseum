Eras: Present Decisions

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Soundtrack: "A Sunday" by Jimmy Eat World Series: Eras (3/4) It was a Sunday. For many on the fWo Roster, it was a very different kind of day, because they weren't going to a show that afternoon, or preparing for a match that night. For Kevin Masters however, it was very much a normal Sunday. The man known as the Dynamo sat in his apartment, alone as usual, reflecting on his life. Why was Kevin Masters different from all the others on the expansive roster that was the fWo? Because he didn't wrestle much on Sundays. He didn't do much of anything on Sundays. Or Wednesdays. Or any other day of the week. Instead he spent most of his time working out, and keeping fit for the ring. Waiting for his shot, his opportunity, to shine in the spotlight. Waiting... and waiting... and waiting... He had been scheduled to work a program with Padrona, but that had fallen through. Just like all the other programs he had been scheduled to work in the fWo. At one point he had hoped that his friend, Kyle Dillon, was going to return to the fed. That wasn't going to happen either. Kyle was content doing his indy work in Japan, and the fWo wasn't about to call him when they didn't need him. He had been sent a message earlier via eMail from Samuel Smooth. He had been expecting a plane reservation for either the following night's RUAH~!, or Thursday's Uprising. Instead he had received something totally different. It was an ultimatum. Kevin, It has come to our attention that your status within the fWo is not what we had hoped for initially. We feel that you could use more development as a wrestler, and we may have called you up prematurely from your stay in OCW. At this time we feel your time could be better spent training, and working on your overall skills. However, there is another alternative, but it requires more risk on your part. The alternative being that you work a program to demonstrate your proficiency to us. This would mean working with another of the fWo's Uprising superstars, and being prominently displayed on our new Thursday night show. It would also mean that if we feel you do not perform at the standards we desire, your contract will not be renewed past Meltdown, or the date of July 28, 2003. One of our scouts, or representatives will be calling you on Sunday, between the hours of 3PM and 5PM EST to request your decision in your future with the organization. Please note neither option guarantees a full contract or any concrete future with the organization. Sincerely, Samuel Smooth Kevin now sat with his apartment phone in his left hand, his cellular in his right. He stared at the clock. 3:11 PM CST. They were due to call. Just then the cell phone vibrated in his left hand, Kevin looked at it, and stared at the Caller ID display. It read "Smooth, Samuel." He gritted his teeth, then opened the flip phone and held it to his ear. "Hello?" he spoke into the phone reluctantly. "Kevin? It's Sam Smooth." Smooth replied, sounding more serious than in any of the past meetings Kevin had with him. "Yes Mr. Smooth, I was expecting your call." Kevin replied, sounding nervous. "Good good, well have you come to a decision?" Smooth asked. Kevin took a deep breath, holding the receiver away from him, then held the phone back up to his face. "Yes... Yes I have."
