Trees

13 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Trees. Huge, gigantic, towering trees. Everywhere the massive trunks rose upward toward the dense green canopy overhead. A plethora of species as far as her vision extended; delicate white-skinned birch, gloriously noble maple, elegantly twisting hickory … and standing stolidly among them were the oak. How to describe these magnificent titans??? She knew she’d never seen the likes of these trees before – not in the Tomies Woods, not in Beaufort-by-the-Sea, and not around Lake Washington. No, these trees were something different entirely. She knew that she was dreaming but these great organic giants, these Kings of the Forest, were somehow more than real, as if all the trees in the waking world were mere hints of what they could aspire to be… She felt awe and insignificant standing amongst these behemoths. She walked and wandered and wound her way through the impressive trunks. It was so nice here with the sun filtering down through the leaves and birdsong as her only companion. Peace and contentment filled her soul and she knew that she was safe here – no day-to-day business arrangements, no managing a house full of eccentric characters, no fear of being chased by fearful shadowy figures. She would stay here forever if she could… Soft footsteps on fallen leaves caused her to turn around. And there, sitting patiently as if it’d been waiting for her forever was the most beautiful creature she had ever seen. From the white tip of its tail to the black pointed nose this fox was vibrant and gorgeous and surreal – like the trees around them it was somehow more real than reality. It looked up at her with knowing black eyes and her own eyes grew wide and round. “Seamus?” The fox smiled and raised its red head toward the sky… Somewhere in the distance a wolf howled. Siobahn McPhineas was yanked forcefully from her dream. She shivered once and shook her head… There’d be no more sleep for her tonight.
