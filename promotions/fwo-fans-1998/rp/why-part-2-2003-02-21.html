Why? Part 2

21 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sleepy? Fuck me, Borst still looks it. Lying there in bed. Alone. A huge double bed in a small hotel room, all alone. Borst eyes slowly opened and upon looking at the calendar across the room, he smiled. Thursday the 21st of February, 2002. Borst yanked back the covers and sat up in bed, stretching out, still smiling. "Thank fuck for that." He said to himself, as he took a moment to jostle about down under before thinking about getting out of bed. "What a fucking dream. Mental." A moment of confusion came when Borst had leant on his pillow when moving about, and seen a few ten American pounds puff out of the pillow in question. "What the..." he muttered to himself as he began to empty the contents of the pillow onto the bed. It was absolutely jammed packed full of money. His eyes grew wide as he gazed at all the money covering the bed. "Where the hell did that come from? I thought I was skint..." A quick look around the hotel room and he had successfully deduced that it probably set him back a few bob to stay in an establishment like this. What's more, he couldn't even understand why he was in a hotel room. Borst looked over at the bed side table and saw an empty bottle of Jack Daniels gracefully lying on the top, surrounded by an ashtray full of cigarette butts, with traces of white powder covering things, and several beer cans scattered around to complete the display. "Fucking... I must have had a mad one last night." Borst said to himself, before patting himself on the back for such an impresses solitary display. "Right." He said, clapping his hands together. "I've no clue where I am, why I'm here or where I got all this money, but I think I'm gonna fuck off out of here asap." With that, Borst swivelled around and plonked his feet down at the side of the bed. His feet didn't hit the floor though, but something hit him hard on the leg. "Watch it!" a woman screamed. Borst looked down to see Yalenchka lying on the floor wrapped in a sheet, using a pile of cash as a pillow. "No way." Borst said. "No fucking way." He continued, as he scrambled out of bed and charged about the room, throwing things all over the place. Moments later, he found the United States Title, lying under a pile of clothes. "No no no!" he cried, starting to put 2 and 2 together, and doing quite well to add things up. Sure, it's February the 21st, but it's not Thursday, and it ain't 2002. Borst ripped the calendar down and tore it up into several pieces, throwing the bits all over the room. "What the fuck? Where'd this thing come from? Is this some kind of joke?!" Yalenchka began to get up, dressed in just her underwear to show off her figure to her less than attentive husband. "What's the matter darling?" she purred as she approached him to run her hand down his chest. Borst just froze and stared at her. "Darling?" she purred in her thick Russian accent. "It's real." Borst muttered to himself. "The first time I sleep in months..." Borst turned around and began to bang his head hard on the wall. His efforts only interrupted by the phone ringing. Yalenchka did the honours, picking up the phone and taking the message. Moments later, she turned to Borst and smiled before relaying the message.
