... on your knees.

6 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization ... on your knees. January 6, 2000 The coldness of the shack is eerily apparent from the first second of eye contact. The shack appears to have been empty for several months, but the snakes on the ground have already made themselves comfortable. A fresh lightbulb hangs, roughly ten to twelve feet from the ground, replacing the old one that began to flicker the last time we saw these familiar surroundings. Pieces of wood are scattered throughout the inside, as they've fallen off the walls since the last time we were here. The lone wooden chair remains in the center of the room, occupied. The light glistens off his fresh shaven head while an outline of his figure glows in the shadows. The concrete floor is worn and dirty, as the snakes continue to slither about. Their hissing sounds take longtime viewers back in time. Back to the place where one man vented his frustration with promises of violence, torture, and unadulterated evil on every single human walking the face of the planet. Back to... hell. "Welcome back." Davis said with a sneer. "Did you miss me?" Finelli and Green looked around the room at the snakes, and began to have second thoughts about coming here. Before either had a chance to respond, Davis continued. "Where were we? Ah, yes. July twenty-third, nineteen hundred and ninety nine. I remember that night like it was just seconds ago. That incompetent fool Machina had his moment... only because I let him. Nothing happens in the fWo... nothing... unless I allow it. The fWo is my puppet, and I am it's master. It's my playtoy... it's... dare I say... my baby. It will only grow, live and succeed if I want it to..." Davis stared off for a moment, before continuing. "... and I don't. The fWo is currently crawling... it has not yet begun to walk... and it won't. Ever. I will cut it off at the knees." Davis paused, and Finelli decided to interject. Loco had not yet entered the room, and it was probably a wise move on his part. "Austin..." One word came out of Finelli's, and Davis interrupted him. "Before we go any further... do not call me by that name any longer if you wish to remain living. Austin Davis is dead. I... am Dead. You can call me Dead. Proceed." "Sorry... Dead... but what... why do you wish to see the fWo fail? The fWo made you rich..." "Correction, I made the fWo rich. I took it to levels it had never dreamed of. When Chris Universal attempted to buy the fWo last June... Collins and his pantywastes were going to hang him. Who was there to save him? Me. How did Beaven thank me? He tried to downplay the situation. He said thanks, but I know he didn't mean it. He would've rather been hung by a rope than thank me. But in a desperate attempt to keep me out of his hair, he "praised" me and moved on like nothing had happened. That wasn't good enough for me. I wanted more. I didn't want a simple thank you note and a pat on the ass, I wanted... control. I wanted power. Beaven saw differently... and we all know what happened. I let Machina have his day in the sun, I let him have his fifteen minutes of fame... and he wasted it by bringing those other incompetent fools back to the fWo. I could eliminate him with a snap of my fingers... but that wouldn't be any fun, now, would it?" "I guess not..." Finelli replied, and Dead continued. "No, I want to have fun... and one by one... from the bottom up... I will eliminate the fWo from existance." "We... we just overcome terrible circumstances just last week... why... why... are you here?" "Why? Why are you wasting my time with such irrelevant questions? No matter. I shall answer your questions, no matter how pathetic or ridiculous they are. Why are we here? Simple. To complete a task that nobody else has been able to do. David Spencer attempted to do so last week. He failed. Why? Because David Spencer doesn't have the determination nor the desire to complete such a task. David Spencer is nothing. He fears me. The last time we saw each other over a year and a half ago, I dropped him on his head. Up until that point he had his way with the fWo. That night he lost whatever power, whatever edge he had over the fWo. I cut his throat, and watched him bleed. He hasn't been the same since. What was once a sinister, evil bastard has become nothing more than a glorified psycho from the looney bin. He seeked out and chose minions to help him with his task, but who he chose are very weak individuals, just like the fWo. William Bailey... Dina Sol... John Skyler... very week individuals. Any of my three servants would not even thinking about crying or become insane after killing someone, accident or not. Spencer has yet to learn that ridding the earth of the fWo is not a laughing matter, but it's not the most difficult task imagineable. Hiring minions to complete your work is a complete joke. Next... we have Collins." Finelli looked at Dead puzzled by where this was going, and Dead continued. "Ah, yes... the boy who's afraid of a little candy. He tried to deceive you all into thinking he was the epitome of evil, the paramount of pain... but he, like Spencer, is nothing. Up until recently, they both called themselves Death... but neither of them possess the sheer evil required to rule the fWo with an iron fist. Like Spencer, he chose weak slaves to complete his conquest. Stone... Plague... War... need I go on? Collins is supposed to be their leader, but he has a characteristic that prevents him from undoing Spencer as the true leader of the Dark Riders: emotion. He has shown signs of affection towards Stone, and even the slightest sign of affection towards another human being automatically eliminates any chance he ever had of being one pure evil individual. Collins became "Death" at CyberSlam last year, but not even a month later did he fail in his conquest and he lost the World Title to his former best friend... Ultra-Violet. He once had the potential to succeed and get rid of the fWo completely, but that night, all that potential went down the drain. Since then, Collins has done nothing important except fail. He's had his fair share of titles, but he never did capture that World Title again, did he? No, he's failed a lot since then. He will probably deny that our meeting over two months again ever happened, but I gave him a simple task and he failed in that aspect, too." "What do you mean?" Finelli asked. "A week after I began my... vacation, so to speak, a young man by the name of Lance Star entered the fWo. He showed great potential to set new heights in wrestling and accomplish more than any one person in the business ever could. He had it all. The wrestling ability, the charisma, the talent, the looks... and I saw it in him before anyone else. I knew... that he could be stopped. Legally, I could not return to the fWo and do it myself. I contacted Collins, and made him an offer he just couldn't refuse. I told him I would return someday, and when I did, I would make his life a living hell once again... unless he could complete a task. So I promised Collins complete immunity from my path of rage when I returned to the fWo if he could accomplish the mission given to him. Needless to say, he only did half the job... and Lance Star returned to the fWo tonight in a wheelchair to watch his best friend compete for the fWo World Title. The doctors say he'll wrestle again. I say he won't." "But..." Finelli interjected, but was quickly interrupted. "But NOTHING. Why do you think he was trying to get out of his contract last week? Why do you think he was trying to leave the fWo to head for Atlanta? He knew I was coming, and he wanted no part of it." "You... you... you... in other words, had Collins botch the move on purpose in order to..." "Prevent this so-called "future" from happening? In a word, yes. And Collins failed. Our deal is off because candy boy couldn't get the job done." "You're... sick... that's... I can't believe..." "Believe it. You call me sick, but I call myself an opportunist. Surely, you can't fault a man for looking out for himself. Collins... how can you expect to defeat me... when you are merely a man, and I am immortal?" Dead sneered, and Finelli looked at him in shock. "You don't hire a boy to do a man's job. I made an error in judgment and figured even a simpleton like Collins could complete such a simple task, but I was wrong. It won't happen again. Collins... Spencer... they both align themselves with such weak fools to help them in their quests, but not I. I've assembled an army of soldiers, and each of us are focused on one goal: making the fWo suffer, and then killing it as if it were nothing. We will not become distracted. We may get sidetracked here or there, but nothing will stop our mission from becoming a reality. Nothing. No one. When we decide it will happen, and it will, the fWo will die. We will kill it. Unlike Spencer or Collins, we will not fail. It is... as simple as that. Trust me." "What... you're nuts... the fWo is stronger than you... and your damned army..." Finelli could barely muster up enough to say those words, and Dead laughed. "You are wrong. Dead wrong. The fWo is a collection of weak minded individuals that can be taken down easily. But I want to have some fun while I'm taking it down, so I'm going to take it down slowly. Methodically. We will not be stopped. One... by one... every single soul that wished to see me gone from the fWo will be... obliterated." Finelli spoke one last time... "What does Slugger have to do with this?" "Scott will reveal his reasons and intentions when he so desires, understood?" Finelli nodded, as Dead stood up from his throne, and his army entered the shack. They slid to the ground and while on their knees they looked up at him in admiration, as the snakes slithered about on the floor. Dead opened his mouth one last time, and uttered three words. "Fuck the fWo." The lightbulb begins to flicker, for already; time has begun to run out for the fWo...
