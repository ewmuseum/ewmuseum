Treadmill.

15 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Treadmill. February 15, 2000 The banner proclaims this to be "Wilson Training Academy", "New Orleans, LA". It's completely deserted, long after closing time, save for the fact that across from the trio of rings, the sound of a treadmill can be heard. We pan up from the legs on the machine, constantly running... running... running against the unforgiving machine, to a slightly sweat-soaked t-shirt proclaiming "FTfWo", finally to the equally unforgiving face of Mitch Wilson. He wastes no movement as he speaks, doesn't even look at the camera, but rather continues with his workout. Well, well, well. Look what we have here, everybody. Myself versus none other than an old acquaintance of mine, one Pierre "Flying Frenchie" Delacroix, tomorrow night, no title on the line. Pierre, you know me. You know how I prepare for any match, against anybody... whether they're one of the biggest stars in the fWo or they're a green rookie fresh outta some no-name indy. Pierre, you know how seriously I take my career, and you know that I find it patently unacceptable to lose in any match, let alone one this big. Rest assured that I will be on your ass from the beginning of tomorrow's match until the second I drive your head through the mat with my inverted russian legsweep. Why am I so confident that that will take place? Pierre, like I said - you know how I train for matches against people I've never even seen before in my life. Just think about what I've been doing for a match against a man I know inside and out? And really, that's what it boils down to, isn't it? I know your every move, Pierre. I know every single move you'd think of using in any given situation, and I know the counters to them. Hell, I know the counters to the counters to the counters for them! Really, man. We teamed together for 10 months. It's a fact of life that anybody would know at least some of your tendencies after that amount of time, and I'm not just anybody. I am the hardest-working, smartest, most wrestling-educated man the fWo has ever seen... and I've got your number. No two ways about it, my friend. Well, so to speak anyway. Mitch reaches forward and pulls a water bottle from a cupholder on the control panel of the treadmill, takes a quick swig, and puts it back. And speaking of my match tomorrow... it happens to be a non-title match. You may not know this, fWo, but I haven't had a World Title shot in my entire career. Typical disrespect for my talents - and you wonder why I joined FTfWo! It's all about respect, and since almost all of the illiterate morons the fWo employs don't respect pure talent, it takes a title to get anybody to notice you around here. And though I may not get a shot at the World Title tomorrow night, you can rest assured that tomorrow I will set into motion the wheels of a plan that will culminate in me procuring an fWo Championship... or to put it in terms the other talent would understand, me get belt soon! "Duh, what belt?", you ask? Well, I'm afraid you'll simply have to wait and find out about that one. Mitch stops speaking for a moment, and adjusts the machine. It begins to speed ever faster, endlessly moving forward yet going nowhere. Mitch speeds up in time with the machine. I suppose that leaves one last thing to discuss. Primetime, what you are doing is a phenomenon commonly known as "whistling in the dark". It comes from the practice of nervously whistling to reassure oneself during a situation of extreme crisis... and that is exactly what you are in, my friend. The fact remains, Brian, that you and your little friend, the man who couldn't equal my talent in his wildest dreams, have done little to nothing to prove yourself as a challenge to me. You talk about winning this title and that title, this event and that event, all your past glories, and yet you still fail to provide any compelling reason why I should believe you to be unstoppable or anything of that matter. Your ramblings only prove to me that every once in a while, even a blind squirrel finds a nut. You bring up the fact that you and Courage are the tag team champions, as if that is supposed to impress me. Look at who you beat, Brian. You beat a mentally retarded freak and a tatooed abnormality that should never have even come near the belts in the first place. Hardly stiff competition, man. Yet you run your mouth about it anyway, and in response I say - so what? I give nary a damn about the titles you have held, the buyrates and ratings you claim to have popped, or anything of that matter. All that matters to me is that I know you even better than the Frenchie - for months we were one in the ring. They never said "Mitch Wilson" or "Primetime", they said "Mitch Wilson and Primetime". I learned from that experience, friend - I learned your strengths and your weaknesses, as I did those of our opponents. You were busy soaking up the endorsements and the glory, and that was your choice. The only problem is, it's finally come back to bite you in the ass. And believe me when I say... we will meet, and this will be settled once and for all. Soon. With that, Mitch turns his attention away from the camera and resumes paying full attention to his preparing, leaving the camera to slowly pan back on the image of that one, driven man, before finally fading to black.
