Venturing through an Unstable mind (Part 3)

17 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Venturing through an Unstable mind (Part 3) April 17, 2000 ::The scene switched instantly and as Rain looked down on himself he saw outfitters clothing on himself as well. He was in the middle of picking up his gear and as he looked around he recognized the spot, he was in the middle of a South American jungle, a jungle that he and Steve wanted to do a little adventuring into but half of their team never made it out.:: Steve: Those clouds are looking pretty nasty huh ? Ive never seen anything like that before ? Are you sure that compass doesn't work, were're in the middle of nowhere. Hey the wind's really picking up. Rain: Steve, come back here ! Where are you going ? :: Steve began trekking through the bush at sprinting speed as winds picked up and the trees began to rustle, soon enough the dark clouds above began to rage, crackling thunder came down, and booms of thunder reigned upon Rain, who desperately searched for his good friend. Suddenly it became night and Rain couldn't see anything at all except for the blasts of lightning, he felt hungry, thirsty, and he had no energy. He tried to yell for Steve as he felt his eyes roll into the back of his head and everything was black. Rain began to open his eyes and he saw a native man dressed in a red robe dabbing Rain's forehead with what appeared to be a towel. Rain quickly rose out of the bed and looked around. He was in a stone room, very small, and the bed he laid on could be compared to the stone wall around him. He realized the place he was in as it was very familiar to him. This is where he had spent his nights in the Temple of the Storm after he had lost consciousness in the jungle and woke up in the warm place, Steve's fate was not the same though. Rain knew a native language and began to speak it to the native man.:: Rain: Where's Steve ? Did he make it ? I didn't mean to lose him, I cant believe I left him, he's here right ? Where am I ? Native Man: Im am sorry, I know not of this Steve, and you lay in the place of the Storm, a sacred place known by very few, you have been blessed as you were guided by the storm here, and we will teach you to use your chosen strength. Rain: Steve ? Chosen one ? This isn't making sense. Native Man: It will. ::Rain decided this was too much for him and laid his head back onto the pillow, but as he closed his eyes, they were re-opened, and he was once again standing in the dark room. The people from his past sitting in the desks around him emotionless, except for Steve who sat back with a foot on his chair chewing on a toothpick.:: Steve: Where the Hell were you on that one dipshit ? You got found by some kooks, but me ? Do you know where I am ? Ever see all the snakes and insects in the jungle ? Im sure you have. You know what happened to me, and why ? Because OF YOU ! Rain: That is untrue ! :: Steve was no longer relaxing his feet on the desk, he was standing now, hunched over the desk barking his words.:: Steve: You want me to ditch you in the middle of the jungle to get eaten alive ? I dont think so ! :: Rain turned away from Steve, and looked in the other direction, into the eternal blackness. The darkness was interrupted though as another spotlight appeared and the man sitting there was cloaked and his face could not be seen, around his finger he spun a crystal around. A crystal that Rain remembered very well, along with the other members of the fWo. Rain walked over and grabbed the crystal, as he did the scene changed and Rain was now into the famous alter world where nothing is the same. Rain stood in the backstage area of an fWo event, everything was wrong.::
