Girl Talk? (1/2)

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The past twelve hours had seemed to be a blur as fast as...well...a gunshot. Only in retrospect, though...while that time was passing, Amy couldn't have been any more bored. Brandon had tried desperately to help her pass the time, but he was who he was and could only do as much as he knew...the extent of which was trying to pull her into some semblance of conversation regarding any number of topics.... Other than music or anything she was really interested in, of course. Both had been too nervous to say anything about the topic they were mutually curious over, and eventually Amy'd found herself drifting off into a nap compliments of morphine. She'd been too drugged to resist the nurses re-inserting the IV and, with a pro wrestler phobic of needles, they seemed even happier - if possible - about that fact than Amy did when the door opened once to what she thought was her newfound...whatever he might be. "Bra---IIII...vy?" She was greeted by a face she hadn't exactly expected, but probably should have. Poison Ivy looked into the room, surprised that Aimz even recognized her. She was out of her working gear - dressed for the weather in a red fake fur coat, knit cap, and faded black jeans. "How you feelin' in there, kiddo?" "Feeling drugged..." The redhead muttered, not quite as groggy and numb as she'd have liked to be. With the blankets pulled down, the comfortable "hospital gear" were putting the stars on her hands, flames on her forearms and bloodied bandage on her stomach on full-display, not to mention the small celtic cross inked onto her upper-hip. Most people would look at the 'battle wounds' and tattoos as signals of how 'hardcore' this girl was...but anybody who knew her knew much better. "...but my guesswork says if it wasn't for morphine, I could be feeling a whole lot worse." Ivy pulled her hat off, letting her blonde hair fall around her shoulders. "You're telling me," she agreed, "When I had my knee worked on they couldn't give me enough painkillers." She surveyed the room ambiently before sitting down on the 'stool on wheels that was next to the bed. "So how bad is it? You gonna be able to get outta here and go visit Angel up on three soon?" "Is she hurt?" Amy paused for a second, the medication had slowed her thought process marginally and it took her a second to piece things together. "Brandon just left...said something about seeing her, but he'd wo..." She was trying to keep grammar in check. "...woken?..me up to tell me...so I sorta forgot to ask why." Ivy laughed for a moment, before she saw just how out of it Amy was. "No, kiddo... well... kind of." Ivy stopped talking, took a moment to organize her own thoughts, and tried again. "Remember that phone call Eli got before the show started last night? Angel had a baby girl. She's in maternity." "And," continued Ivy, "Brandon's been here with you since you came in, so he hadn't met her yet either." "Well that bites...he should've taken me." She pouted, "I love kids when they're too young to talk or throw up on you." "With a bandage like that?" asked Ivy, incredulously, "If you could make it to the door without falling over I'd be shocked and amazed. But I wouldn't worry too much about it... Angel's just as bored as you without painkillers so I wouldn't be surprised if she came down to visit." "Oh blah, I'll be fine...allthough, we should have a competition...who's bitchier after the drugs wear off: The chick who took a bullet or the one who had a kid?" Amy chuckled on instinct, but the instant it hit her belly she pounded her head back against the pillow. "Okay....serious oww. Jesus, who'da thought I'd be on my back this long thanks to Brandon..." "Give it time," said Ivy with a wink, "I'm sure he'll do a bit better in, umm, different surroundings. But that begs the question... has something finally happened? Perspiring minds wanna know!" And to think making people blush was usually Amy's job... A little taken aback and pink in the face, the little redhead tried to dodge as best she could with a "Maybe...but for future reference, there has GOT to be an easier way to say 'I love you'....preferably one that doesn't involve taking a bullet to the gut." She continued, totally ignoring her use of that rotten "L" word. "But...yeah. After all of this, we don't want it to be too...public, y'know?" "Uh- oh, UH-OH!" exclaimed Ivy, also choosing to discreetly ignore the 'L' word, "Why do I have the feeling this is gonna be one of those tragic romances between star- crossed lovers?" She put her hand on Aimz' wrist just below the IV's entry point and said, with as much sarcastic seriousness as she could muster, "Have we changed our name yet, to Juliet?" Momentarily, mismatched eyes had nearly popped out of Amy's head, but she smiled with wonder at the same time. "I could smack you for that!" She laughed a bit, despite the pain. "But that would take effort. And...'star-crossed lovers'? I'm Canadian, care to enlighten me on the lingo?" "Heh, don't worry about it, kiddo," replied Ivy, "But seriously... anything the rest of the pack can do to help?" "For now? A bottle of STRONG rum and a lifetime supply of Mr. Noodles. For 'Juliet'? A handbook on how to keep life away from the public." "Sweetie, if you figure out how to keep your personal life away from the public you'd best share. But if it's rum and Mr. Noodles you crave... give me about an hour." Ivy got up and kissed Amy on the forehead before putting her hat back on, "As for right now... tell me one thing..." "Is he worth it?" The question made even the redhead reflect for a moment. Hoping to make actions speak louder than words, Amy ran a hand over her bandaged abdomen, gesturing all around the room with a grin despite the searing pain nipping at her. "I'll put it this way - you think I'd be in this mess for anyone else?"
