Endurance? BAH!

18 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Why me?" The words escaped through heaves of air as Ichabod Michaels lay on the dirt covered concrete. The hot air of Monterey California was permeated with humidity. John Tohru, who was running around the corner stopped and hoisted Ichabod up to a standing position. "Ichy if you want to get better, you need endurance." John took off running again as Ichabod slowly puled himself away from the wall and began to run. Ichabod turned the corner to see John leaned against the wall. John took a swig from his water bottle and then handed it to Ichabod, who in turn gulped down the remainder of the fluid inside. "John, why do I need endurance?" John cough lightly and stretched his legs. "Well, for the same reason I do. Guys like Vic, Brand, and Kinkade they don't necessarily 'need' speed. They have power and they have size. But guys like us, we don't have those advantages. I mean hell Ichy no offense meant but you're the smallest person on the roster. Even Aimz has you by an inch." John stopped talking as he reached into the cooler the two had brought with them and retrieved another water bottle. "Call it what you will endurance, stamina, whatever but even you have to admit. As long as your finisher remains 'Not in the Face'. You can't back me up in the ring and I need that sometimes. Ok, a lot of times." John cracked the seal of the bottle and sipped out a small amount of it. "Ok, I understand that part now but John. Why are we running around a school?" John looked up from his bottle at Ichabod. "Well, honestly I just figured it worked in highschool. Why not now? By the way, where's N?" Ichabod looked around a bit puzzled himself. "No clue haven't seen him, err heard from him all day." John Tohru pushed away from the wall and lifted up the cooler. "What now Johnny?" John turned his head towards Ichabod. "Well, next stop is at my cousin Ben's chinese restaurant. If your going to build any muscle you need to gain some weight first. Which means, eat till you can't anymore and make cousin Ben comp the bill." John smiled as Ichabod was quick to grin. "Besides, any self respecting half japanese man wouldn't own a Chinese Restaurant." John laughed out the words as the two headed for his car. Just because I don't speak doesn't mean I'm not here. Maybe these two will be real wrestlers someday.. I just highly doubt it. N
