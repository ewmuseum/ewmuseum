fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Vengeance. February 26, 2000 A huge, hulking figure sits on a locker room bench, motionless as he sits among the forest of stainless steel lockers and the spartan, antiseptic walls of the locker room. A moment later, there is a movement - a finger depresses a button on what appears to be a remote control. We pan forward to a television, sitting on a shelf. The VCR on top of it appears to be rewinding, and we cannot see what is on the tape. After a moment, the tape begins to play, and it becomes clear that it is showing Ice vs. Scott Slugger from the Road To CyberSlam Tour in April 1999. Ice grabs Slugger by the throat, going for the kill, but Slugger kicks him in the knee, causing him to stumble in the opposite direction. Slugger hits him with a chopblock, and Ice topples over backwards, landing on his shoulders. Slugger holds on to the knee, and uses an awkward-looking bridge to keep Ice down. One. Two. Three. That was the first time we ever met, Scott. You were the World Champion, and you pinned me, clean, one two three in the middle of the ring. In terms you might understand, that was strike one for me. The tape now fast-forwards, and plays again. Ice vs. Slugger from Twilight. Ice again wraps his mammoth right hand around the throat of Slugger. Slugger counters by pulling Ice into a small package, literally out of nowhere. One. Two. Three. The second time we met. I was the World Champion, and you pinned me, clean, one two three in the middle of the ring. Strike two. Tomorrow is my last pitch, Scott. Collins is a nonentity to me. I couldn't care less about him one way or the other. It's me and you, Scott. You've got me down 0-2, and I have no doubts that you're preparing some kind of screwball or spitter, looking for me to go down swinging. Should that happen, Scott, on Slaughter I will do something that redefines the term "drastic". But let's not focus on that. What we need to focus on is this. You. And Me. Tomorrow, Scott, I guarantee that I will put my hand around your puny little throat, and I will chokeslam you straight into the very bowels of Hell. And it's been a long time coming, hasn't it? For all the times you've betrayed me, for all the times you've shoved me out of the spotlight for the sake of your own gigantic ego, for all the times you took credit for my success... it's gonna happen. Tomorrow, your life is in my hands, Scott. Will I take compassion on you? Don't bet on it, little man. What I will do is this... I shall strike down upon your ass with great anger and furious vengeance, and I shall break your (CENSORED) NECK! I'm gonna watch you (CENSORED) bleed... And it's gonna feel good. The camera pans from the screen of the television, which has long since turned to static, and slowly back to the face of Ice. The look on said face is one of pure, unadulterated hatred, and the look in the eyes is beyond scary. We focus in on the cold, hard eyes of Ice, before fading to black.
