Prelude to Oblivion 3

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Opening bell's in a half hour, Eli," said Brand Frontier as he laced up his boots, "Don't forget, Smooth wanted to have a quick meeting with us all before we hit the air." "Yeah, thanks kid," replied Flair, not really committing. Brand looked him over - it can't be nerves. Flair had a good two and a half years experience in pressure matches on him. "Angel coming to tonight's show?" "Yeah," replied Eli - as his brain caught up to him, "I'm alright, man... just miss my partner in crime." Brand laughed. "We all miss Ivy," he said, "but she'll be back as soon as she can walk, yes?" "Yep," said Eli, "Jeez, listen to me.... I'm missing someone who's gonna be back at any time and bitching about it to you... and you've got some stuff on your mind y'self, huh?" As Eli leaned back, Brand looked away. "Don't worry about it," he answered quickly, "Let me - let us get through the next three days and we'll see where the chips land." Brand opened the door and started to walk out, suddenly turning his head. "Let Angel know that if she wants to come on back here and you're not around, there's plenty of people to vouch for her - plenty who want to see her." "Gotcha," replied Eli as he pointed an outstretched fist toward the Paragon. Brand smacked his own fist against it before he left. Alone at last. Eli stood up, going over his match in his mind. Already he'd put in one of the longest days of the entire company. He arrived at the arena early to have a brief workout with some of the newer wrestlers still getting a feel for an fWo ring. He took one for the beleagured road agents and went over Sean Stevens and Lance Knight's match with them - since nobody else in the company knew how Triple X worked quite like Eli Flair, as well as the matches the three men (and Grendel) worked on the house show circuit both with the fWo earlier in the year and for a few other promotions following its demise. He single- handedly saved the day when, through his knowledge of the town, he ordered food for tonight and tomorrow night - enough to feed an army or a wrestling promotion - free of charge. But now... as the moment approaches... there's nothing to do but hope he doesn't screw it up. Funny to hear from this man... but in a make- or- break situation, all you ever worry about is screwing up. He looked around, lost for a moment, and began to wrap his hands and wrists in black athletic tape. He's physically ready for the match as far as his gear is concerned, but something's missing. The clock told him he had five minutes to get to the meeting. Eli bent over while still seated, his knuckles pressed together, his chin on his hands. Prayer? Never. Memory. His best friend, Ivy McGinnis, who would typically be here to tell him to stop stressing over his job that night. Who would be telling him so to cover the fact that she is just as nervous. His girlfriend Angel, inching ever closer to her delivery date. The smile and the laugh that he sorely misses when on the road, the eyes that hypnotized him. And a moment in time that he was fortunate enough to be witness to.... five years ago. A grizzled veteran that Eli Flair idolized retired from the ring with the words "I've loved every minute of my career. I have no regrets.... but it's time to go home." Eli Flair has loved every minute of his wrestling career. Twelve time World Heavyweight Champion. One of the men who revolutionized the art of "getting over" in the past decade. One of the most respected athletes in the history of this business. Regrets? I've had a few... but not in this business. "Not yet," whispered Eli to himself, "It's not time to go home yet." He stood up and left the room - left his doubts - behind. He had a job to do... one that many did better and many did worse. But nobody did it quite like Eli Flair. Welcome to Oblivion.
