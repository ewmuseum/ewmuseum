Six: Assiduity

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Diligence is characterized by steady, earnest, and energetic effort." The alarm buzzed dully in the background. The trip home from Nova Scotia was uneventful but Brandon couldn't rest comfortably. His head was filled with so many thoughts; his body ached still even after almost a week since his last match. Ric had done a job on him that was an understatement. Coming home at three in the morning he didn't expect a welcoming committee, but what was there surprise him. Or rather what wasn't there. Ana had taken Sean and Jarod to stay at her mother's house. She knew she would be angry but he didn't expect this. Lately Ana had been so distant. He had tried over and over again to make things better. It just seemed like there was always something for her to be angry at him about. Be it his new schedule with the fWo or his working with the Foundation again. Anything could set her off. The months the fWo were on hiatus things seemed to improve. It was cyclical in nature almost. The best times of them together were when Brandon wasn't wrestling. Especially when Brandon wasn't in the same federation as Vic Creid. As that name popped in his head he absently rubbed the scar on his chin. A stiff chair shot from the Kodiak almost six years ago had placed that marker on him. Much the reason for his continued use of the goatee. Slowly the mighty paragon pulled himself up to a sitting position in bed. Leaning against the headboard he let his mind wander back to those days. Vic Creid and he were asked to work a few matches together. In the resulting aftermath Brandon would never think of a worse time in his life. Brandon didn't care much for Vic and his business practices; they were diametrically opposed on pretty much everything. The promoters wanted to hype the real life distaste the two had for each other so a story was crafted where Vic and his cohorts took Ana, who at the time was known as the Earthborn Angel in the E.W.A., away from Brand Frontier. Brandon's nose wrinkled and his brow dipped even thinking about those horrid months. The story was a hit amongst the fans even though things between Creid, Ana and himself crashed and burned behind the scenes. It seemed that even though Creid and himself didn't click the same couldn't have been said about Ana and the self styled Alaskan Juggernaut. That is where it all started to go wrong. A confession and three months later Brandon and Ana split for the first time. Accidents happen and even though he was touted as The Paragon Brandon Roche knew everyone made mistakes. Forgiveness was given and with all his heart he wanted to move on. The hatred was there though. He could never stand Victor Creid again. Brand Frontier and Kodiak Vic Creed in the same ring normally spelled disaster. Much the reason he and Creid have only wrestled two times in the five years since. That is, before The Paragon won the Survivor II contest and made his way in to the fWo. Here it was a different story. Brandon wanted to wrestle Creid. If in some way beating on him in the ring would change everything that happened all of those years ago. Like it would fix the cracks started in the foundation of his marriage. His head throbbed; the late night and the alarm were working their magic. Lost in thought for the last few minutes the little black box still buzzed. Brandon rolled out of the bed, muscles stiff and ribs aching as the small alarm clock was finally silenced. He knew he couldn't lie in bed all day. He had to work the stiffness out, he had to be ready for the series of shows come the new year. Tomorrow was Countdown 3 and not knowing exactly what to expect he figured Ric Chronos and Brand Frontier would probably throw down. As Brandon slipped his old gray workout shorts on he thought about how Ana always angered easily when he would start wrestling again. It had always been worse the times Creid and himself were in the same federation, both times during their stint in the E.W.A. and even that time in-between. What was it about being so close to the Main Man set her off. Maybe there were still feelings there? Maybe there was something more he didn't know? Shaking the thoughts out of his head Brandon was sure not to allow himself to sink in to a vat of pity this day. He had too much to do and a short time to do it. The way the booking has been going he didn't know from one minute to the next what type of match he would be in. Something could be set in stone and then changed at the last minute with little notice. It was hard to keep up but he would manage. He was Brand Frontier after all. Laughing at his little inside joke to himself Brand trekked downstairs to get a few miles in. He would have to call Ana and the kids when he got back and try to iron things out. He knew though in the back of his mind that things were not good, things were probably over between the two of them and they were both just hanging on to memories. Maybe that is the reason his eye had wandered to the fiery redhead in the fWo? He didn't know, he just knew that it was things that he had to deal with whether he liked to or not. The crisp morning air bit in to his lungs as he stepped outside. A new morning greeted him, maybe even a new beginning... Seven: Munificent
