The Business - mise en scene

15 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bright lights shine down from the ceiling, numerous beams as they're all focused on a small twenty by twenty foot area. Everything else around him is dark, but inside the light, he's become a focus. A jovial smile on his face hides his sinister intentions, but his baby blue eyes make you forget all about that. His green hair however, is lowered in front of him, shielding his face from view. Long wild green hair, shadowing everything underneath it. A surfer necklace around his broad and strong neck tangle with the shoulder length hair, fraying it, one strand at a time. His posture is sturdy, his body chisled, and his hand extended as if greeting someone. Fingers shaped in a rock on position, he's not head banging, but he is right there in spirit alone. Standing in the corner of the light, he may be the center of the attention, but he's not hogging it all. He's hiding, forcing himself into the corner of the room as if he were the dunce from kindergarten. His pants have the word Lunatic written down the side, and are the same shade of green as his hair. In carpenter style, he could hold hammers and tools, but chooses not to, for whatever reason. He cracked his head from side to side, the people watching on edge. See, he's a professional wrestler, and the light is focused on the wrestling ring. He's inside, playing to the crowd. Some of them are cheering, most of them are booing, but he's getting a reaction. It's what he does. And the fans hang on every word. He is their movie for the next thirty minutes. Although it's a lot more like Saturday Night Live. And he wouldn't have it any other way.
