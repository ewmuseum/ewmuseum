Injured

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is part of an aWc series I wasn't able to complete, and has relevance to the fWo signing Flyer. I simply expanded it. This is part one. "Hi everybody!" A door swung open, and standing there was none other than former aWc heavyweight champion, High Flyer. He had a bag lying on the ground that he slowly kicked inside the house. He clutched at his sides as he moved due to breaking a rib at an aWc pay per view event not even a week before this. He was being quite careful not to disrupt the healing process. A young child came racing down the stairs, skipping every other step and then diving from the fifth one down to the bottom hardwood floor. He ran up even further, and wrapped his arms around Flyer's legs. His hair was scraggly and he couldn't have been any older than two. He was wearing the same shirt as Flyer, which covered his entire body, body and legs. "Hi Dr. Dad!" Flyer smiled, and winced before kneeling on the floor. He hugged the small child and shook his head. A door slammed in the distance, and a voice called out. "Fly?" It was Kate Young-Harmen, Flyer's wife. She walked into the room wearing a white turtleneck sweatshirt and long black jeans. Seems like the Bethlehem Area had hit a cold spell. Her hair was a bit disheveled, but otherwise, she wasn't worse for the wear. "Fly," she smiled, before turning a bit cold. "I still don't think Allocca should be watching the Simpsons at age two." "Oh come on," Fly said, returning to his feet and wincing in pain once again. He walked slowly over to Kate and gave her a hug. He whispered in her ear from this position that "He probably won't remember this in six years anyway." "That's not the point Fly." Flyer took his head off Kate's shoulder and they looked into each other's eyes. She flashed her eyelashes as Flyer simply let out a half smile. He kissed her on the forehead, placing his hands on the sides of Kate's head for support. "I know," Flyer said, before dropping down even further and kissing her on the lips. "It's just good to be home." "Ewwwww!" Allocca said, as Flyer and Kate both broke their embrace up in mid-stride, turning their attention to their two-year-old son. They're cheeks rested on one another, as Flyer let out a sigh of happiness. "What did daddy say about doing that around grown ups?" "That the monster'd come an get me." Flyer let out a smile, and then raised his arms up to the air as if they were claws. Allocca's face lit up in pseudo horror as he ran over to the living room area. He let out a small joyous scream as Flyer lightly jogged over. "Get over here!" Flyer called out, smiling throughout every step. Allocca however, was trying to avoid his father's clutches. He stood behind a couch, checking each edge of it to see which would be the best escape route. Instead, Allocca climbed over the top, and landed right into the arms of Flyer. "Don't climb on the furniture!" Kate yelled. Flyer caught Allocca in his dive, but immediately fell to his knees and rested his boy on the couch. Allocca landed with an oomph, as Flyer clutched his right side in pain. Allocca's eyes narrowed in confusion. "What wrong daddy?" Allocca sat up, and tilted his head to the side in sympathy. Kate ran over to her husband's side, and helped him to a sitting position on the couch. "I didn't hurt you did I?" Allocca lowered his head and tears started to well up in his eyes. "Mommy always says not to play with the monster." Flyer turned to his son, and placed an arm around his shoulder. "No, no big guy. Not at all." Flyer lifted his aWc t-shirt, and there the bandages lay. "See? I was already hurt. It wasn't your fault in the slightest. It was mine." "You're fault?" Allocca asked. "I thought bad things only happen to bad people?" Flyer smiled. "No, they happen to stupid high risk people too. Like your father." "So, being stupid is like being bad?" Allocca began to almost tear up again. "You're going to go to hell!" Allocca clutched Flyer's arm, and dug into it with his non-existent fingernails. "Don't go to the devil. He's all red and firey, and those horns aren't fashion accessable." Flyer turned his attention to Kate. "Have you been watching Trading Places with him?!?" Kate twisted her face into a guilty expression, as Flyer still couldn't stop smiling. He'd been on the road for the past six weeks, and now, he was going to be home. And he was going to enjoy every minute of it. "Hey Allocca, I gotta talk to your mother," Flyer said, nudging his son off the couch. "Go play with your playstation. I'll take you on in Smackdown." "YAY!" Allocca yelled before running back up the stairs from where he came. Flyer kept on the couch, as Kate lifted Flyer's shirt up to check out his injury. "It's nothing Kate. In fact, if it's anything, it's a blessing." Flyer said, cupping Kate's chin the palm of his hand. "Can I just tell you how sexy you look when you dress like Season 4 Buffy?" Kate narrowed her eyes, but then just lowered her head into her husband's lap. They just stayed there, motionless. You could hear a pin drop. So when Flyer's cell phone rang, it was quite jaring. Kate lifted her head up from Flyer's lap, as he reached into his jean pockets for the phone. He flipped it open, "Hi, I'm Jack Harmen's phone, but you probably already knew that before you..." Flyer stopped in mid sentence. "Oh, I thought you were Joey or Tony. Those guys are so gullible." Flyer listened to the other end, and after a few moments, his jaw dropped. "What? What is it?" Kate waved her hand in front of her awestruck husband's face. "Fly?" No response. "Jack?" "Thank you."
