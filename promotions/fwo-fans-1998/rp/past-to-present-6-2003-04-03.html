After the last words fell out of Silver Fox's mouth he hung the phone up, destroying one persons life, while saying another's. Tim had a huge smile across his face for he was now going to the big leagues, but he then remembered Skylar, he looked over at Skylar as Skylar finally opened his mouth. "You're not going to take it, are you?" Tim felt bad because he had no other choice, but to take it. "I have to take it, I need the money. You can come with me, we could run the tag ranks." Tim said hoping Skylar would agree and everything would be peachy, but that would only happen in a perfect world, a perfect world something Tim is far from. "What about us both hating the fWo and everything it stood for? What happened to that? You're just going to leave me just like, because someone thinks that YOU might be able to wrestle a couple shows. You know what's going to happen to the fWo, they're going to bring in a bunch of no named rookies for awhile and gain there popularity back and then when they get there fans back they will bring back the Scott Sluggers, and the Boston Stranglers, and the Erik Kelly's, then what are you going to do? You're going to be back at the same position you were just in." Tim had no reply to come back with. But he didn't think Skylar even wanted a reply because he didn't even give him a chance to reply before he opened his mouth again. "You know what? Fuck that, take that fucking job, but you can't stay here anymore. Get your shit and hit the road, don't talk to me ever again, don't think about me, don't think about my name, forget everything...EVERYTHING!" Tim was thrown a garbage bag full of his valuables. Skylar then pointed to the door, as he was so mad steam could have been coming out from his nose and his ears. Tim slowly turned around and walked out the door. Skylar followed him only to close the door and scream at the top of his lungs.
