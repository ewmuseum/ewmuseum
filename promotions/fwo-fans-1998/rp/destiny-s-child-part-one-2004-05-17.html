Destiny's Child - Part One

17 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There was a slight skip in his step as David Cross approached Michelle's apartment door. It had been there ever since Cyberslam when he told her how much he still cared for her. She was still working through some personal issues, which was understandable after what Crusader had put her through. But David was floating on the wings of Love, and Life was much better than it had ever been before. Two quick knocks on her door, and David checked his watch. Traffic was still a little heavy but they would still be able to make their dinner reservation in time. He'd even planned the route earlier in the day just to be sure they'd be on time. The florist had assured him that the flowers would arrive in time for dessert. David smiled to himself. Tonight was going to be an amazing night. The door opened quickly and David was startled to find himself face-to-face with... not Michelle. "Uh, hi," he said to Master Hiroshi, his former instructor and Michelle's father. Master Hiroshi stared at Cross with eyes narrowed. For a moment, David thought that Master Hiroshi was going to attack. As skilled as he was, David was quite sure that it would probably be a very one-sided battle, culminating in Cross being wiped up with a large mop. Hiroshi didn't attack though. He hesitantly stepped aside and let David in. This is just all sorts of strange, Cross thought to himself as he stepped into the hallway. He slipped his shoes off and walked into the living room with Master Hiroshi hovering behind him like a lethal shadow. Michelle was sitting on the couch, facing the television. It was off. "I was watching this earlier," David said as he motioned to the black screen. "The butler did it." Michelle didn't say anything. She didn't move. David sat down beside her. This was all wrong. Very wrong. "Michelle? Are you okay?" The bloodshot eyes and tears on her cheeks told him that no, she was far from okay. Throwing caution to the wind, David snapped at her father. "What did you do to her you bastard," he asked in a tone that was dangerously close to threatening. He placed a hand on her knee. "I won't let him hurt you," he told Michelle. "You know that." "I haven't caused her pain," Master Hiroshi said as he stood in front of them. "This is your doing." "My doing?" David stood up. "MY doing? I love Michelle! I love her more than I've ever cared about anything or anyone in my life." "With your life of misery and solitude, that's not saying much." Hiroshi disregarded David immediately. Cross came face-to-face with Master Hiroshi for the second time in a few minutes, this time deliberately. Few men had stood this close to the elderly Asian with bad intentions and lived to regret it. David didn't care. If he was going to be torn apart, he was going to make sure his executioner knew what the score was. "I love her," Cross repeated. Master Hiroshi tilted his head toward his daughter. "And how does she feel?" David looked down at Michelle. "Tell him," he said gently. "Don't let him intimidate you." Michelle didn't look up at either David or her father. She stared ahead at the television as the tears continued to flow. Master Hiroshi spoke for her. "You brought her here from Japan, filling her with silly ideas of Life and Love, something neither of you know anything about. She was humiliated and brought much disgrace to her family. She is going back to Japan where she belongs. Where she has always belonged. The Temple will be her home now." "She's a big girl," David snapped at Hiroshi. "She can talk for herself. We love each other." "She does not love you," Hiroshi stated. "You parade your feeling for her in front of the entire World on your silly television show. What else could she do? She disgraced herself. She disgraced her family. You brought her here and she was corrupted. Her shame is deep and your love means nothing to her." David ignored him. He knelt down on the carpet beside Michelle's legs. "C'mon Michelle, tell him. Tell him that you love me. I know you're going through a tough time but we can get through it together." Michelle's voice was hoarse. "I never said I loved you. Fate was always against us." Screw Fate. David stood up and jammed a finger into Master Hiroshi's chest. "You did this." Hiroshi's almond eyes stared into David's damaged crimson eye. "She never loved you. You're a mongrel dog. Who could ever love you? Who would ever want you?" Word for word, all of David Cross's lifetime insecurities were brought to the surface. He was a loser. He'd always been a loser. He'd lost friends. He'd lost family. Even his face couldn't remain intact, always being scarred in some manner or fashion. It was like his destiny was one of solitude and pain. With Michelle, David thought things had turned around. Now they were slipping away. Cross still had his finger in Hiroshi's robed chest. With amazing speed, Master Hiroshi brought his calloused hand up and slapped David across the face. A normal man would have been rendered unconscious from the blow, a blow from a hand that had shattered numerous cinder blocks in the past. Had Cross wanted to, he could have blocked the slap or at least evaded it. He took it. Master Hiroshi watched David stumble backward from the blow. "Get out of her dog. Michelle is leaving within the hour. You will never see her again. And if I see you again, you'll get more of the same." Michelle's lips trembled as David Cross walked down the hallway in silence. She didn't even notice that he left his shoes behind as he left her apartment. Outside her window, dark clouds opened up and shed their own tears of rain upon the city. Her heart broke as the apartment door closed. Master Hiroshi looked down at his daughter and placed his hand on the top of her head. "This is for the best," he said in what was as close to a reassuring tone as he could muster. "You have a great future ahead of you. Your Destiny lies on a much better path. He has no future. He is nothing."
