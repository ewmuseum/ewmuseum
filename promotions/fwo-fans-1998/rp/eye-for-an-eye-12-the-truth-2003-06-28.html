Eye For An Eye [12] The Truth

28 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've spent the vast majority of my life thinking one thing then being told something completely different at a later date. My mother always told me that my father loved us all but he was still the guy that used to cheat on her and beat us within an inch of our lives. I was always lead to believe that my future was in professional football only to find out at the age of eighteen that I had no future there and wrestling was my only option. And now I was under the impression that I was blinded when a far more horrific truth lay just around the corner. August 31st 1993. "Do you want a grape?" Dawn Kinkade chirped as she plucked another piece of fruit from the stalk and tossed it into her mouth. "No." Kellen replied bluntly. "How 'bout some orange juice?" Dawn mumbled as she continued to eat the fruit that'd been brought in for Kellen. "No." Kellen grumbled again. "Maybe I can pick you up a better attitude somewhere?" She uttered. "Listen Dawn, if you want to do me a favour you can tell me just why the hell everyone is being so evasive when they speak to me... if I wanted to talk about grapes I'd have gone to a vineyard. As it stands I'm sitting here and nobody is telling me whether or not I'm going to be able to see when I take this damned bandage off." Kellen spoke back furiously. "I'm gonna go get Mom." Dawn said meekly before trying to get up. "NO!" Kellen cried as he leant forth and snatched her wrist "Look... if you aren't going to tell me then she isn't either. Since you're so keen to do something for me why don't you go get me a glass of water, the jug next to my bed is empty." "Sure." Dawn got to her feet and snatched the jug from beside the bed, quickly making her way through the doors at the end of the room. The second she'd exited Kinkade put in motion a plan he'd been waiting to execute for days, if nobody was going to tell him whether or not he'd be able to see again he'd find out for himself. Grimacing he ripped the intravenous drip from the veins in his arm and dragged himself out of the bed, stepping down onto the freezing cold floor. As he made his way out of the room he could see other burn victims around him, he quickly began to realize that he could've had a lot worse than potentially being blinded. There were people so badly burnt they were hardly recognizable as human beings. Kellen continued to stagger through the ward... the painkillers messing with his coordination he staggered into the doors and threw out his hands, leaving a splatter of blood on them as he did. He looked back for a second and couldn't help but notice that there wasn't a single mirror in sight. Out onto the corridor Kinkade continued to stumble, he needed to get into better light so that he could take off the bandages and find out whether or not there was still any sight in his left eye. Finally he found a suitable spot, the lights on the corridor were sufficient enough for him to see his own reflection in a nearby glass window. Slowly but surely he unwrapped the bandages... he remembered hearing once about too much exposure to sunlight after a bad eye injury could do more damage than good but he'd been kept so far from the truth he didn't care anymore, as the final unwrap took place he dropped the bandages to the floor and looked at the truth before him. The truth was a gaping void where his left eye once was, he stood silently... unable to take in what he could see before him. It was at that very moment a child that'd been walking down the corridor with her parent came across him and looked him in the face, before screaming. "Mommy! Mommy! There's a monster!" The child cried as she ran away down the corridor, leaving Kinkade to look back to his reflection once again and agree with her. Kellen fell to the ground and cried, his heart filled with sadness for what was the last time... ...for soon vengeful desire would engulf it.
