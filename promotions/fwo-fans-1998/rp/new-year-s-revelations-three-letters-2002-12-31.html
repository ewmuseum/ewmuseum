New Year's Revelations: Three - Letters

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Linkin Park's "Runaway" was blaring through the room, and Amy Campbell was so far detached from herself that her victory at the final Countdown wouldn't bring a smile to her normally lovely features - which now looked weary and sleepier than usual. Graffiti decorations Under a sky of dust A constant wave of tension On top of broken trust Two relationships had been killed off in one swift blow earlier that night. When Brandon had kissed her, she was instantly aware that her so-called 'relationship' with Darcy Markson was null and void...but soon reality swept her back to her feet with the reminder that the Paragon was a married man - one she couldn't have. She'd been relieved when the crowd had knocked them apart afterwords; it saved her the daunting task of walking away, but it didn't mean she wasn't left to think of the consequences Brandon himself was facing. Then again, his actions had always spoken volumes more than his words, and they'd always shown Amy not to give into whims, to control whatever she felt because that's what would be best in the end. The lessons that you taught me I learn were never true Now I find myself in question Guilty by association Amy knew full-well Ana wouldn't be pleased in the least, and she wished she could somehow get a hold of Brandon again - just for a moment - so she could convince him to tell his wife she was the one who kissed him, even though it wasn't the case. But Brandon was Mr. Hero, he'd never sacrifice someone else's reputation to save himself, especially if he cared for the person like he did Amy. She just wanted life to be clear for once. It seemed like her relationship with Darcy was intended to die out for Brandon, but now it didn't fit - the Mighty Hero had a wife, after all. The little redhead just wanted to get away from herself for as long as she could, because this was nothing more than yet another in a series of examples meant to teach her a lesson she THOUGHT she'd already learned. The lesson was not to show emotion, to act cold as ice and that way people wouldn't grow fond of you, thus not permitting you to grow fond of them. But once again, that plan had been foiled and instead of kicking herself for it, she'd begun a vicious cycle once again: drinking the problems away. I wanna run away Never say goodbye I wanna know the truth Instead of wondering why Relationships had always been decieving, men always deceptive and it almost, in a way seemed to Aimz that she'd just been fooled again - this time by what would've been the strongest act of them all. She refused to believe that, though...because Brandon was so genuine, he'd never had an intent to hurt anyone or complicate things in the least. What had happened had happened and there was no mending it, just running away...that was the easiest way out. I wanna know the answers No more lies I wanna shut the door And open up my mind But fate had thrown another foul ball. When Amy noticed a slip of paper on the desk near the blank TV screen her eyes had been absently staring through, she'd eased her way off of the hard mattress and stepped over to retrieve it - praying it wouldn't be some sort of bill for whatever unintentional damage she'd done to her room. She stared blankly at it, with a gaping jaw after picking the small sheet up. It wasn't a bill - no such luck. Amy ran one hand through her hair, glanced over each shoulder and dropped the slip...just before hitting a dead-run out the door. The song still played, and the letter remained... --------------------------- "Amy Campbell, Everyone knows how you feel about my love. Stay away from him. He doesn't deserve to have his life ruined by a tramp like you. You've seen what I can do to you, I know you have. Don't be a fool, little girl." --------------------------- Paper bags and angry voices Under a sky of dust Another wave of tension Has more than filled me up All my talk of taking action These words were never true Now I find myself in question Guilty by association...
