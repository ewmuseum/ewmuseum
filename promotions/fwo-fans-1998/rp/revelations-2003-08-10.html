Revelations

10 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Today is my niece's birthday. She just turned thirteen. She's extremely mature for being that age. The last time I saw her, she had makeup on and was about to go on a date. Isn't that kind of young? I don't know, maybe I'm just getting up in years. I still look good though, hopefully. Anyhow, my sister is having a birthday party for her in a couple of hours. I was originally going to cancel going down there since I have tons of things to get down today. My sister phoned me at the last minute and told me that she had already said I'd be there. That's my sis for you, always trying to ruin set plans. Now I'm faced with the dilemma of getting a present. I've never liked receiving presents all that much, but I do enjoy buying them. I figured that up until an hour ago where it just hit me that I have no clue what little girls want. Is she even still considered a little girl? After much deliberation, I decided to go with about seventy-five Barbie toys and accessories. I'm not even sure if she still plays with Barbie. I could always ask, but then it'd look like I have no clue about my own niece. I'm starting to wonder if I actually do. Has it truly been that long? No, it couldn't have been. I see her when I have time. What has happened to me? I don't know what to blame all of this on. I'm guessing I only have myself to blame, but can you hate a guy for trying to make something of his life? My heart has always been with wrestling, yet here I stand a wrestler/actor. Did I sell out on my dreams or have they just changed with time? I guess I can say that I've changed with time as well. I find myself neglecting the things that once made me happy. Do I still deserve to be called 'The Equalizer'? When I look into the mirror, I no longer see what I once was and I'm unable to determine if that is a good thing or a bad thing. It could be stress. I have quite the history with stress-related issues. I could be overworking myself. I don't quite understand if it's the fame or the commitment that I'm fascinated by. I'm not quite myself in the ring anymore either. I'm so good with these types of ordeals yet I'm finding myself without an answer this time. I think I'm having a nervous breakdown or something. I'm still the same person, or am I. Enough of this shit! Let's just get the damn present and get through with this...
