12/29/2001 3:00PM One bag down, and one to go... The Strangler picked up the suitcase, and carried it over by the door before dropping it with a dull thud. The bellhop would be there soon, and he wanted to be sure everything was ready for him. What was it Darryl had said when he helped bring the bags up yesterday? Something about traveling light for a wrestler... Well, yes. He did travel light. He packed only the necessary items, and didn't waste space on most of the comforts from home. Most of the other guys had everything in their baggage. There were CD collections, laptops, handheld video games... and other electronic distractions. He didn't really interest himself with much of that nonsense anyway. The few luxuries he afforded himself were still locked inside of that safe, along with his money and travel documents. While he had the thought in his head, he reached into the closet, unlocking the safe and quietly pulling the contents from inside of it. The cash and documents he slipped into a pocket inside of his bag, along with a small box that held mostly pictures and trinkets representing his past... but the long yellow envelope... he paused as he looked at it. The Strangler slowly opened the flap, and pulled out a packet of paper before he began flipping through it. It was his fWo contract. After signing the extension upon his return to the fWo from an extended leave, he made sure that it was ironclad. Guaranteeing of title shots and other little amenities meant nothing to him in this. He could earn his title shots as he always had, by beating someone for it. What he did concern himself with was the part about termination. A small smile crept over his face as he mouthed the words that were written, and signed for by Travis Beaven when this contract was tendered. First, it stated that the contract would be honored through any changes in ownership (something he had insisted on, given the turmoil the fWo had been going through and all of the struggles for control of the company). But more importantly, it stated that all terminations would need to be agreed upon by both parties. Both parties. The clause had been inserted to protect his interests as well as Beaven's at the time. The then owner didn't want another wrestler who was being pushed towards the main event to take off as soon as someone offered him more money elsewhere. And the Strangler didn't want to be flat out released for any reason under the sun. So that was it. Like it or not, they were married to each other... The Strangler slipped the contract back into the envelope, and tucked it safely away in his bag. Some people carried mace for security... or a cell phone for emergencies... or a rabbit's foot for good luck. He carried his contract, because while it was all of those things combined... it was also the one thing in the world that the fWo could not take away from him. But what worried him, was that his deal was only a year away from being up. In essence, he knew that if he didn't break out of his slump soon, this Countdown could very well be his last. But he also knew that starting tonight, he controlled his own destiny. With a final zip of his bag, he was ready to go.
