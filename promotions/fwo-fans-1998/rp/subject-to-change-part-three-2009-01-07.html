Subject To Change : Part Three

7 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The ring rope shuddered in Billy Kilcullen’s white-knuckle grip, and the five feet up from the safety of the apron began to feel an insurmountable summit for his cemented shoes. Barely a spare second before caution would have engulfed him, Billy heaved back on the cable and burst free of his nervous, self-inflicted gravity. “You suck,” was amongst the mildest of heckles hurled his way. To such an altitude did Billy think he’d soared that the casing of the rope felt slick with ice. Toes within oversized boots clawed for grip, but the trembling misfired nerves. Before his legs could buckle underneath him, Kilcullen ejected, flinging himself off… only for the plastic coating of the rope, glistening with sweat, to deny him even the merest offering of traction. A gangly, flightless bird, Billy crash-landed at the feet of Karina Wolfenden, the canvas scrubbing the blotchy skin from his chin. He did not find her in a merciful mood. Three minutes in, Karina Wolfenden had felt all her pre-match buzz dissipate with a limp fizzle. It was like the worst sex she’d ever had, only not over nearly as quickly. As the awkward, stuttering, mistimed flailing and flapping of limbs delivered Billy Kilcullen to her feet, Kari’s temper bubbled over. “You fucked up!” they rejoiced in chanting. Billy’s bulging eyes somehow found room to swell further in their sockets as Wolfenden flung herself around into a fierce technicolor cyclone. A foot violently lashed out, cracking against his raw, bleeding chin. Out of the 2,191 fans packed into the building, 2,190 erupted in bloodlust. They were the crowd at the coliseum stabbing a thumb down to seal the fate of the defeated Irish gladiator. “Fuck ‘im up, K-Wolf, fuck ‘im up…” was their judgement. Gorging herself on the fans’ plentiful energy, the K-Wolf ensured that there would be a bountiful feast of it on offer. For two minutes, Billy Kilcullen was her plaything. Leather-bound knuckles whipped across his face, pummelling kicks ravaged his unimposing body, and the soles of her feet threatened to stomp his skeleton thought his milky skin. Such was the frenzy to which she had whipped the crowd, the thunderous ringside roar of Frank Kilcullen was smothered no sooner than it left his scarred lips. Billy’s trembling fingers grasped at Karina’s trouser leg, whiplash effect anchoring his head bowed with wide, pleading eyes met only by the eyes threaded by her sneaker laces. Had Kari known Billy Kilcullen had only two months of training under his loose-fitting belt, that he was prematurely thrown to the K-Wolf by his own impatient father, the baying crowd’s rapturous demand would have been ignored, and Billy’s indignity would have ended there and then. “C-4! C-4! C-4!” shook the very foundations of the building. She didn’t know.
