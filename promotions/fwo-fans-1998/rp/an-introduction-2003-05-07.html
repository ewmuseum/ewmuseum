An Introduction

7 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One hour. Two hours. Three? Nah...couldn't have been that long, could it? The sun hadn't even come up yet. Night's cool breath still tickled her bare shoulders. Heavy foot-falls of booted feet clomped on the sidewalk. She wore those boots wherever she went. The soles were practically nonexistant on the Doc Martens by this point. It was a good thing tonight was no different. Jade had no idea how long she had been walking. These kinds of things would often happen to her. Walking through the streets of her home town of Philadelphia would occupy many a night. She had come to know these streets so well that her auto-pilot would switch on, allowing her mind to wander. That wasn't always such a good thing, though. A bottle of Jack swung from her hand at her side. Slosh...slosh... Her hand, complete with swollen, bruised knuckles, shot down to clutch her medicine. The sweet burn caressed her tongue like some familiar, intimate lover. A tiny smile pulled the corner of her busted lip. The world around her was all ready a touch hazy and Jade was all the more happy to add on to that feeling. Satiated, she returned her hand to her side. That hand was throbbing badly... Ahhh yes, that. A faint trickle of blood broke free from the skin, drawing Jade's attention enough to make her stop walking. The dreadlocked woman smirked, raising the bloodied, beaten fist to eye level after having switched the precious liquor to the other hand. It brought her train of thought to an immediate and complete hault. What a night it had been. To think it had almost been erased from her memory... The sounds of her fists pounding into him were like packing meat. Over and over again they had driven into the flesh, tenderizing it to some internal, bloody pulp. Jade snarled, a raw desire coursing through her veins in the form of adrenaline. As his muscles contracted in protest, she pushed harder. The grip on his neck tightened and the blows grew harder. Jade resigned to her own world. "Rip his fuckin' heart out, Jade!" "That bitch is some kind of monster!" All around the small pit they yelled and barked like a pack of angry dogs. Each had invested countless hundreds and even thousands of dollars on the woman they had heard so much about. Bookies loved her. They could always count on making a pretty penny every Friday night. She had released the choking grip on her opponent's neck by throwing him against the concrete wall of the deep pit. The impact had been absorbed mostly by his forehead. It cracked open like an egg, blood pouring out like spoiled yolk. No time could be wasted, not in her mind...not in her world. Jade was back on him as if it were her primary mission in life to take him out. Hard kicks jolted his ribs over and over. They would most certainely reach a breaking point soon. Jade would only be gratified when she heard that beautiful dull snap. It came a couple of moments later. The man screamed out in agony as one of his ribs cracked clean in half. Through the crimson mask he wore, those screams sounded more like gurgles. Jade hovered over him, her verdant eyes cold and unforgiving. Their gazes met and he squirmed like a baby cow on the chopping block. "What are you waiting for?!" "Yeah! Snap his neck!" "No mercy!" Jade couldn't hear any of them. There was no sound in the place that she was in, only a steady, droning hum. It was that thing inside that made all of this possible. The constant drone was the very thing that had been guiding her through everything for as long as she could remember. Because of it, she was able to make all of those bookies who were now screaming at her, very rich and happy(?) men. At that moment, it had been telling her that to end this man's life would not be worth it. There were more important things to spend her time on. She had all ready formed a tight fist with which to aid in delivering the assumed will of that drone...but she relenquished it. When she took a step back, the man beneath her trembled with relief. He would not be the next in a long string of "unfortunates." He would go on to tell the tale of how he was lucky enough to walk away from his encounter. On the other hand, Jade would barely remember this evening. She would be sure to forget it completely by the following week. Jade never cared enough to remember them. A fight was a fight. She had seen plenty. This match had been over within a matter of minutes and consequently had been nothing worth remembering. She walked away from the pit that night without having broken a sweat. The money that had been placed in her hand would all be spent that night on a case of whiskey... ...and that was one of the bottles clutched in her hand. Jade shook her head and continued walking along the deserted street. Soon enough, she would reach her destination. While reflecting back momentarily on what had happened that night, she had remembered that she was going to meet her trainer and only friend, Bill Randolph. Some business about this new federation...or something. Jade had a very hard time remembering things. Jack Daniels didn't make it any better. ....and so was the story of her life... ....and here our journey begins... Welcome inside.
