A Beautiful Mind

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He was in his midnight blue Lexus sports utility vehicle driving home to New York. He was flipping through the FM radio stations, nothing good was playing. He decided to turn to the AM radio station WFAN 660 since he was crossing the Brooklyn Bridge and back in New York. "This time we are going to fine Mr. Shadows. It was our last show before our upcoming PPV Twilight, and we were looking forward for him progressing his storyline with Aimz. He failed to show, which put us the staff in an uncomfortable position to try and quickly find something to put in his time slot. If this type of conduct happens again, we are prepared to either suspend Mr. Shadows or permanently terminate his contract with the fWo." He rubbed his bald head with a confused expression on his face. How did he miss the show? He took a look at his surroundings and gave a brief smile. It was good to be home, back in New York. He was on his way to see his wife who he had not seen in over a month because of being on the road traveling with the fWo. He finally arrived home, and pulled up into his newly acquired 3.5 million dollar home he bought in Brookville Long Island. He got out of his car and opened the trunk to get out his duffel bag. When he closed the trunk and looked up, there she stood with a hug smile on her face in the doorway. He walked up to her and gave her a long hug, followed by a kiss. "Oh Steven! I'm so happy to see you. It feels like it's been forever" said his wife Lisa as she hugged him tightly. "Only a month, but yeah...definitely felt like it was forever. C'mon, get inside. It's freezing out here." He dropped his bags next to the door and hung his leather coat up on the rack nearby. "I have some lunch already made if you're hungry." Steven smiled as he held his stomach. "That would be great. I haven't eaten since Virginia." They headed to the kitchen. "So how has everything been here since I've been away? I hope you weren't too lonely." "Nah, not too lonely. I had the dogs to keep me company. I hung out with Michele a lot, did my Christmas shopping the other day and just been working a lot. So I've been kept pretty busy." "Good. I realize that I said I wasn't going to wrestle anymore after I left Old School for you. But you know, it's the fWo and I cou-.." She cut him off and put her finger on his lips. "Don't worry about it. The fWo has been a dream of yours ever since you could remember. The opportunity was there and you took it. I'm happy for you and you don't need to worry about me growing uneasy with not seeing you all the time." He let out a sigh of relief. "And this is why I married you." "Shut up!" she said as she playfully hit him on the arm. They made their way into the kitchen where she had already prepared a turkey sandwich with a glass of orange juice for him. "Although Steven" she said hesitantly "I heard what happened this past Wednesday with your no show. They say that if something like that happens again they might fire you. A new storyline you're going into?" Again he rubbed his bald head with confusion. "I wish I could say it was, but it's not. I didn't get to Warfare." "Why not? You can't afford to get fired. The insurance on this house is enormous alone and god knows that I don't make enough money to support us and to be quite honest this might be your last shot at superstardom." "Yeah, I know" he said. "I don't know what happened. I went to DiMaggio's to get some pasta and then left for the arena. Next thing I know I'm waking up in my hotel room." She looked at him with a great concern. "You blacked out? How did that happen?" "I have no idea." She looked down at the ground, not wanting to look in his eyes while she asked her next question. "Well, uh...have you been taking your medication Steven?" He sighed. "Pills pills and more fuckin' pills. Yes I've been pumping my system with so much crap. I hate it." "I know you do baby, but you have too. You know that." Don't lie to her "Steven?" she said as she looked into his eyes. "Steven? Are you listening to me?" He shook his head. "Yeah. I...uh...thought I heard something. Listen, I'm gonna lie down for awhile. I think I've been driving too much and I just need to get some rest." He stood up and gave her a kiss. "Are you sure you're alright?" she asked. "Yeah, I'm fine...just tired. Wake me up later, maybe we'll go out and do something." He walked away and up the stairs. "Alright sweetie" she yelled. She bit her bottom lip, knowing that something was wrong. She looked over at the duffel bag that was sitting on the floor, then up the stairs, then back to the bag.
