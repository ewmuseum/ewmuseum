Sweet Child O' Mine - Epilogue

SWEET CHILD O’ MINE Epilogue – Mariella Jade “She’s beautiful,” said Eli sitting next to his wife’s bed as she held their daughter in her arms, “she takes after her mother.” Angel was crying. She held Eli’s hand while holding onto their daughter. “Look what we created… just look at her… this is incredible.” Eli smiled. “Hello, mom.” She smiled back, tears still flowing, “Hello, dad.” They kissed, but she pulled away, a nervous kind of look on her face. “Listen,” she started, “about what I said during the labor… about not wanting to stop singing—“ “I know,” replied Eli, “we’ll figure something out. However we do it, she’s going to have two full- time parents… but you’re not going to stop singing. I’d sooner stop wrestling.” “No,” interrupted Angel, shaking her head, “you can’t stop wrestling. I know how much you love it… how much it means to you.” She leaned back, looking into her daughter’s eyes, “How can this perfect little person cause so much tension? How selfish am I, talking about my career when this perfect little person needs me?” Eli kissed them both before standing up and pacing in a circle. “Angel… you’ve got a few days in here to think about it anyway. But consider this… you’re just starting to heat up. You’ve got four guys who’ve probably been discussing post- Garden plans all night. And you’ve got a half- finished record sitting in the studio back in California. You don’t need to give up anything… you’ve got so many people around you who will help you keep everything the way you’ve dreamed.” She kissed her daughter again. The baby slept. “How long’s that gonna last?” “Listen,” continued Eli, “I’ll take a part time schedule, I’ll be with you on every off- day, you know you’re always welcome on the fWo tour.” He sat down next to her once more. “It’ll be hard for the next year and a half… but I’m willing to make it work. You and I have been through so much already it seems only fair to share some of the adventures with our daughter.” “What did you mean,” asked Angel, “by ’the next year and a half?’ What happens in a year and a half?” He smiled at her and kissed her hand. “How many kids can say they know the ins and outs of the wrestling business and the music business? You talk education, there’s precious little about life you can’t get from that.” “Well,” replied Angel, “She’s not gonna be a wrestler. And she’s not gonna be a singer. This one’s gonna be President. Or Queen. Or empress of the fucking world.” “Or maybe,” interrupted Eli, “we can work on sleeping through the night?” They both laughed, as Eli kissed her full on the lips. Neither of them noticed the door opening off to the side. *Ahem* “Ivy!” Poison Ivy stood there, eyes locked on the baby. “Eli, before you hit the couch here, or the hotel room, or whatever… take a stop downstairs. Brandon, Alex, and Sean are down there waiting for some news on Aimz.” “Aimz?” asked Eli, looking back and forth, “What happened to her?” “It’s a long story,” replied Ivy, “but it’s better to hear it from the people who were there. But in the meantime… while we wait for the verdict on her, there’s about twenty people in this hospital who are dying to meet that bundle of joy. Up for some visitors?” Eli looked back at Angel, who looked at their daughter. She was sound asleep. Angel looked back and nodded, a smile on her face. “Bring ‘em in,” replied Eli. Ivy left the room to get the rest of the family, as Eli sat down next to his girls once again. “Mariella Jade Flair,” he whispered, “say hello to the family.” ~The Beginning~
