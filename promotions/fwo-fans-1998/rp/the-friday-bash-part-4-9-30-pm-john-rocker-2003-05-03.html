The Friday Bash - Part 4 - 9:30 PM - John Rocker

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At nine o'clock in the evening during the Friday Bash, John Rocker had arrived. He wasn't invited, he wasn't wanted, and quite frankly, he bugged the crap out of a lot of people. And right now, thirty minutes after he had arrived, he had found himself wanting to spike the punch. However, someone was there to stop him, because he had the same intentions in mind. Sanchis Su Idolo. Now, Sanchis had arrived earlier in the party, himself, and just like John Rocker, he was neither invited or wanted in the party. However, the Mexican in the Breatholizer: Blow Here T-shirt had arrived, and he had been flexing his Sanchis muscle all around every single broad in the party. Finally, at around nine-thirty, Sanchis had flexed his Sanchis muscle all the way into an argument with John Rocker over who would spike the punch. Why Sanchis and John Rocker wanted to spike the punch when there was alcohol freely available was anyone's guess. "Okay, you fuckin' chink, listen up. I'm from Atlanta, and in Atlanta, the guys who make the ninety-five mile an hour fastballs will fuckin' spike the punch." Rocker told him. "`Ehhhh, de Sanchis, `e can t'row a MEAN curveball in `es own right, esse! De Sanchis would have been in de Mexican Bas'ball League, esse, had dey not found de sleepin' powdure de Sanchis used to... accidently... chu know... spray in de ugly faces of de opposition!" Sanchis said, shifting his eyes from left to right to make sure that nobody heard him say that. "What the FUCK did you just say to me? You're just like all of those fuckin' New Yorkers, you think you can just walk all over me. Well, I ain't fuckin' standing for it!" Rocker ranted. "What are chu TALKIN' `bout, esse? De Sanchis `es from Meh-hee-co City, holmes!" "That's a suburb of New York!" "IN WHAT `VERSE, ESSE?!" Sanchis shot back, "De Sanchis, all `e wants to do es spike de punch, holmes! Des way, de hembras'll be all over de Sanchis like dey SHOULD be, holmes!" "HA! That's just the type of thing I'd EXPECT from a chink like you!" Rocker shouted. Now, this horribly racist and downright offensive argument might have continued, had the Pillsbury Doughboy not run across the table, spill some of his... ahem, "Pillsbury Juice", into the bowl of punch, and quickly scurried away. Rocker and Sanchis frowned. Then Sanchis simply ignored John Rocker and went about the normal business that was hitting on chicks. In particular, he approached a certain frizzly-haired woman in a Hawaiian shirt. "`Eeeey, bebe, what's chore sign?" he asked her. "Sanchis. Please leave me alone." the woman, Leigh Landers, said with a sigh, as this was the fourth time tonight that he's tried to hit on her. With that said, she wandered away. Sanchis did, too, because there happened to be a pretty-looking black haired thing that he could always hit on, and so he went for her, leaving John Rocker alone and angry(moreso than usual). That's when he pulled out the mushroom. John Rocker did not exactly eat the mushroom that he confiscated from the Fuse Bros. on Sunday Slaughter. He had been distracted with insulting the hell out of some passing New Yorkers and unconsciously stuffing it in his pocket. So, he was bewildered when he saw it in his pocket. Then, a moment that John Rocker would live to regret for quite some time occured. He ate it. * . * Thirty Minutes Later... John Rocker had just seen a machine gun-wielding hippo with a bandana and a German accent, and he wasn't liking it too much. In fact, when it wouldn't even respond to the fact that he didn't like hippos, especially ones from New York, that's when he figured that RUNNING would be a good idea. I mean, what're YOU going to do when you have a machine gun-wielding hippo with a bandana and a German accent screaming "HAIL HITLER!" chases after you? Hang around? Check real estate? PSSH. HELL NO! You're fucking GONE, man! And that's exactly what John Rocker was, as he ran across a world that closely resembled the Emerald City from Wizard of Oz if it had a bastard offspring with Wonderland, right after it just went through a three-way with the Restaurant At The End Of The Universe and Kurdistan. The house that John Rocker last remembered being in had been replaced by what could only be described as a snow globe. Most of the really good-looking chicks had turned into cattle, and most of the really bad-looking chicks had turned into Cher, so they looked worse. Outside the snow globe, violent explosions that could only be described as the ending of the universe could be seen. The inside of the globe had a really big cube within it that stood up on one of its points and did a little dance, which would be completely impossible given the fact that it was a giant cube standing on one point and could only theoretically just spin around. Not to mention the fact that disco lights came out of nowhere and that Frank Morgan, despite being dead for many years, was holding the snow glove and, despite being blasted constantly by the end of the universe, kept telling John Rocker to come back tommorow. So, don't be surprised if this world wobbles a little bit, that would be the fact that this world was actually being invaded by giant sperm at this very moment and Frank Morgan didn't like that too much. "FUCK!" Rocker shouted abruptly, drawing the attention of two of the sperm. The two sperm approached Rocker and spoke. "Oy thoynk SOMEONE has had a leeeetle too much to droynk!" the Dinglesperm said. Caglesperm turned to Dinglesperm questioningly, "You's a think so, Dingerburry?" John Rocker didn't like either of the two sperms, because they sounded too much like foreigners to him, and the last thing this fucked up crystalline, checkboarded, exploding-things-all-outside world needed was two foreign sperm. So he punched one of them and ran away. John Rocker ran up a staircase made out of ceramic kittens, and through a hallway made out of limburger cheese. At the very end of the limburger hallway were two peanut butter and jelly sandwiches wielding javelins, and Adam Nowell. Too weirded out from the situation and already screaming, Rocker hung a quick left and saw an inviting portal. The reason why it was inviting was, of course, the sign above it that said "DO NOT ENTER". He entered it. Then he plunged downward before landing with a violent splash in something resembling a lifetime supply of Pepsi Blue, but was actually just Nate and Steve-O's swimming pool.
