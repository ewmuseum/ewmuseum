12/29/2001 11:00AM "What the hell are we doing here?" the Strangler thought to himself as he stood just outside of the doors leading in to Sacred Heart Hospital. After all, he swore he didn't want to be involved in any of what was happening here. He had bigger concerns and better worries right now. But inside of him still lingered the need to be 100% sure. So it was, that he made his way inside, and up to room 516. Darryl was watching television while he lay on the bed, laughing at the classic episode of Bugs Bunny that was showing. He didn't even seem to notice that the Strangler had made his way into the room, and it was only after a cough and a wave that he turned his head in that direction. "You? What are you doing here?" he asked the Strangler in a confused tone. "Look if it's about the police questioning you, I'm sorry... I had to be honest with them of who I saw at the bar that I remembered. I hope it didn't cause any trouble..." "Trouble?" the Strangler interrupted him. "You want to talk about trouble? We know your story Darryl. We talked to Joseph at breakfast today. He told us about the trip to Cabo, the bust, the undercover work... everything. And that includes your current deal with Jack Cozens." "Oh..." "In fact... we paid Jack a visit earlier today, at his gym." "You didn't say anything...or do anything, did you?" Darryl's face suddenly went slightly pale. "I mean, he's pretty much a small time guy... but even he can cause enough problems for someone like me." "No. We didn't say anything to him, and we certainly didn't do anything either... do you know why?" "Why?" "Because," the Strangler continued "You got what was coming to you. While we don't support his actions, we're also not inclined to interfere with them either. That's for the police to do... ...and you probably know he has a couple of them on his side." "Then there's something you ought to know..." Darryl began, and reached for a sip of water from the plastic cup on his tray table. "It's a good thing you didn't do anything." "Why's that?" "Because ...it wasn't one of Cozens' guys who did this to me."
