The Great Escape

24 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lowell lay in bed, the soft, cotton sheets clinched to his neck. He’d just woken up from a nightmare; he dreamt he was downstairs eating breakfast with the pretty lady and her longhaired hippy friends, when all of a sudden… the pretty lady ripped off her face to reveal the face of… the Man! Dragged from the house, thrown in the back of an SUV, beaten over the head with a computer mouse (whip-action), then carted off to a secret lair (yes, a lair), where he would then be dissected, piece by piece, organ by organ, flake of skin by flake of skin, by a doctor whose credentials were questionable at best. Hyperventilating, Lowell’s eyes darted around the room frantically. He heard laughter coming from downstairs. Sinister laughter? Perhaps. He couldn’t yet tell. Lowell grabbed the TV remote from the nightstand and hit the Power button. The screen lit up, with a man in a suit so white Lowell figured it must have been made out of cocaine. The man raised his arms in the air, his face blood red, and cried out, “The EVIL ONES are COMING FOR YOU, LOWELL!” Okay, so he didn’t actually mention Lowell by name, but he might as well have. Lowell pulled the covers up over his head and began to devise a plan to get out of the house and away from the crazies downstairs. ‘This is what I’m gonna do… First, I’m gonna rig some sort of pulley device, lower myself down to the main level from the window – since my arms are too weak and sickly to hold my own weight – and then I break for the nearest gas station. Once there, I bribe the clerk with the hundski I stole off that fancy guy at the grocery store, and he gives me his Camry… and I haul ass back to wherever it is I’m from. …Where the hell am I from?’ “Breakfast!” Oh. Dear. Lowell. “Just a second!” replied Lowell. ‘Okay, how do I make a pulley? And what’s a pulley? Sounds like some sorta modified handjob, the kind you get when you’re trapsing around a small village and the indigenous women are all running up to you like ‘Misstaaaa, I suck youuuuu!’ And you’re all like ‘Nuh-uh. Get your mouth germs away from me.’ But then you notice you’ve got an erection, so you agree to let them give ya tug in exchange for some bent pennies and ball ‘o lint.’ Several minutes later, a “pulley” was created. Lowell slowly, and carefully, lowered himself out the window, bed sheets and comforter tied together, wrapped around the ceiling fan, and anchored to the door. Using a broomstick he found in the closet, he reached for the switch that turned on the ceiling fan, as he stood perched on the windowsill. Unfortunately, the broomstick was some six feet too short. And the sheets and comforter were poorly tired together. That is to say they weren’t at all. “OH SHIT!” ~~ Lowell sat upright in bed, his body covered in cold sweat. He felt below him – bed sheets, not grass. He heaved a sigh of relief, before laying back down in bed. “Breakfast!” His eyes shoot open. “…What the fuck’s a pulley?”
