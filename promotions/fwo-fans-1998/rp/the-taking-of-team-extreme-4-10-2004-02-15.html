The Taking of Team Extreme Chapter Four St. Louis, Missouri November 28th, 2003 9:41 PM local time "I think it's a good angle," insisted TJ, "The hunt continues." fWo Unleashed: The Mob Rules had just ended, and despite the overwhelmingly strong showing everyone made, only five of the people in this room over the age of two were happy with the result of the main event. "He's slippin' and he doesn't even realize it," insisted Eli Flair, "Brand's time was now and he lost. What the fuck is Fox thinkin'?" Angel re-tied the terrycloth bathrobe and put a reassuring hand on Eli's shoulder. "It's not just Fox that's got to worry about all this stuff - there was a lot that went into it, the least'a which were Brandon and Vic themselves. You know this." Eli looked as if he was about to snap, but calmed himself. "You're right," he agreed, "but it doesn't change the fundamental flaw of the hero not winning the big one. Maybe I'm oversteppin' myself but when Creed beat me he established himself as one'ta remember. This was gratuitous and I can't believe Fox let it go." Sex shook Eli's hand and gave Angel a kiss on the cheek. "Maybe Fox didn't have a say in the matter? In either case, I'm about to crash. G'nite all." "Night, Sex." "Goodnite, Stanley." He chuckled out the door at Angel's continued insistence on calling him by his full name. "It's true," agreed TJ, "You're not in the room anymore, E... you don't know what goes on." Angel shot her best friend the 'shut up' look, and for once, he listened. "G'nite, all." The room gradually emptied, until it was just Angel and Eli, sitting on the couch, watching their daughter sleep in the playpen. "He's right, y'know." "About?" "I'm not in the room anymore. I don't know what's going on. I just know that something's wrong." "What's wrong?" "Everything. Almost everything. The heroes just ain't gettin' it done. I'll admit, I'm surprised D's been puttin' up with Miles this long, but Cockring's been pretty good. And this thing with Brand..." He tensed, but quickly relaxed. "Never mind." Eventually, they fell asleep.
