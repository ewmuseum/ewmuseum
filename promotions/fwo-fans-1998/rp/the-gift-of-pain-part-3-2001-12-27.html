The Gift of Pain - Part 3

27 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The snow drifted from the evening sky, blanketing the small suburban neighborhood. Cars crept along the icy streets, leaving trails, which disappeared into various garages. A slight wind helped to move the snow, small drifts poking up on the sidewalks and making the evening considerably cooler than it already was. Not a good night to be out, unless you had to. The black limo, which navigated the streets of suburbia, stood out amongst the modest homes. Dark against the light snow, the sleek black car prowled slowly down the block. Most of the house lights were off; few people were awake at this hour. If they had been awake, heads would be poking out of windows, faces on the glass. It wasn't a very inconspicuous vehicle, given its surroundings. Either the owner didn't know that, or didn't care. This owner didn't care. Darryl Vishanti, his eyes bloodshot, stared out through the tinted window. The two men who sat with him were preoccupied with the weapons in their hands. An Uzi 9mm sat in each of their laps, each weapon being checked over with care by its owner. Kevlar Bulletproof vests were handed out. They looked as if they were going to face an army, not a ten-year-old girl. "Sir," one of the men said. "Are you sure you want to be here? We could have retrieve the girl and brought her to you." Vishanti continued to look out the window. His voice seemed to come from far away. "Vic Creed caved in my brother's skull in his own home. Creed's kid deserves the same consideration." "What about the other occupants in the house?" "His sister and brother-in-law," said Vishanti. "The brother-in-law can be killed. The sister is crippled and half-blind. You can have your fun with her before you kill her; it doesn't matter to me. But Creed values his daughter the most. She's the one that I want." One of the men appeared disturbed at the thought.
