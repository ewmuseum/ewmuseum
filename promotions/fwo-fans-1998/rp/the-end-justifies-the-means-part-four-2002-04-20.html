the end justifies the means, Part Four

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 23, 2002: "We just want to run this to get you over, kind of the testing period." Chris Universal sat at his "desk". Rather, it was a prop that the fWo carried with them to all events. However, for all intensive purposes, Universal's set was put together first, so that it could act as a de-facto office. Jon Crisp sat in his "office", listening to news he didn't want to hear. "Kevin is a fine wrestler, and should be able to put you over with no problem. And we believe that T and Parka will be able to coexist with Mike and Lawrence. Remember, this is only for a short time." The Burns angle had not gone well in the ratings, much to the surprise of Chris Universal. However, the reason for the angle stagnating had surprised him. It wasn't Monte Burns who had been dragging the angle down. Rather, he had been outstanding. This was expected by Universal, given Burns' credentials. However, Crisp's ability to get a crowd against him was unparalleled in every company he was in. Here, he was getting no reaction. It was as if nobody knew who he was, which was not a big surprise; he had headlined in Canada, after all. The U.S. fans didn't watch TCW, and didn't know the history behind it. Crisp knew that he was the reason behind the blowoff happening so soon. But, he saw an angle with Jackhammer as something of a punishment. He wasn't a comedy wrestler. He loved a good laugh, and thought that the STUMP squad was funny. If they weren't, they would haven't the ratings they were getting. But it wasn't for him. He wanted to move on into a more serious role. But Universal had other ideas. "We can get you over by using T and Parka interacting with them. The comedy, coupled with your serious wrestling, will in fact get you over. How do you think Mike Bear got over?" Universal asked him. "By being built up in solid angles, and having a solid stable behind him?" Crisp replied. "Well, true," Universal said. "But, this will work. Believe me." Crisp sat down, and pondered his options: 1. Say no, and have a black mark. 2. Say yes, be a team player, and maybe look bad in front of the fans. "Sure, I'll do it," Crisp said cheerily. "Anything I can do to help the company." "Great," Universal said. "We'll make sure to make it up to you in due time." Crisp left the office, his pride slightly hurt. However, spirits were high. He had an angle. He would be a regular. Meanwhile, Universal took note of Crisp's attitude, and he knew of something right off: "He can't say no."
