The Xenosaga: II

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Crocodile' sighed, "Alroyt Cagle....he's played the following vid-yo games: Club Race, Super Ghouls and Ghosts, JAWS, Boogerman, Pitfoyter, Foyt for ya Loyf, virtually every toytle for Sega CD.... what's next on the leest Redneck?" "I likes the purdy art on this 'un raght heeyer," Cagle said, holding up the cartridge for the NES game, 'Bible Adventures'. Codemaster, wide eyed and irritated screamed out, "OH_HELL_NO! If you make me play that, I sware to God I'll kill myself!" Well, he did have a right to be a bit pissed about this whole situation. I mean, his hands are duct taped to a NES controller, he's been holding a piss for four hours, and his face is sticky from all the chocolate syrup still stuck to it. However, Cagle would have none of that back-sass, as he sprayed him with a bottle of Windex. "Hey! Hey, hey, hey you little sheeyit, I'll have none of that back-sass!" Codemaster had to escpape, and fast. While the Tallywhackers... "Those damned Tallywhackers..." Codemaster groaned. were considering their options on what game to torture him with next, Codemaster was dilligently working at the duct tape with his teeth. However, it didn't seem to be working... infact, it like so didn't work. If it could have not worked any more than what it did right then, it'd be fired...for, well, not working. Allen was one pissed-puppy, but he knew they were rather destracted when Dingleberry said "Cagle, what did Oy say about touchin' me theh?" However, he was too busy to notice the perverted innuendo. He wouldn't have laughed anyway.... Soon, the duct tape proved to be too much for Allen. However, he decided to give it one more chance and simply pull his hands out. Luckily, his feet weren't tied up. Most likely because the Tallywhackers... "Those damned Tallywhackers..." Codemaster groaned. were too stupid. So he tried to push his hands out of those duct tape cuffs by using extra leverage with his feet. "C'mon baby....c'mon baby....just...a....little....HARDER!" ... That was loud, and Allen knew it. He looked up, and the Tallywhackers were staring right at him. "Ummm....what?" "Oh, aint nuttin' really. What're ye doin' there?" "Uhhh...writin' a letter?" "....Roooooooooooooyt. Carry on then." Phew. Close one. He almost had his hands free. Just a little bit more pressure, and he'd be free. So, he bundled up all of his energy. He breathed in deeply. He channelled all of his emotions. And in one big ball of emotional, energetic breath he pushed and yelled the following: "I_WILL_PULL_AND_UNBIND_MYSELF_FOR_I_AM_CODEMASTER_DAMMIT!!!" He was then, free. And Codemaster ran directly toward the front door. "...Well that sumbeeyitch." Cagle looked on, with interest. "Told ye he'd troy it Cagle. Hehehe, watch thees." Codemaster valiantly opened the door, and valiantly stepped out... And a valiant pile of legos fell ontop of his valiant form. There must've been ten valiant buckets of legos dumped on his head. And valiantly, this valiant Master of Codes did fall. "HER HER, HER HER HER! YER RAGHT DINGERBURRY! HER HER HER, THAT SHEEYIT THERE WAS FUNNY!" And thus the Tallywhackers... "Those damned Tallywhackers..." Codemaster groaned. were victorious once more.
