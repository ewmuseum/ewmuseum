2000: *My* year.

18 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization 2000: *My* year. December 18, 1999 The scene focuses on a birthday cake. The birthday cake is white, with gold and green frosting. There are twenty five candles, and all of them are lit with the exception of one. In the background, a fire rages on in the fireplace. A chair sits in front of the table where the cake is set, and Chris Universal walks into the picture. He reaches into his leather jacket pocket and pulls out a lighter. He lights the candle that was previously unlit, before looking into the camera and beginning to speak. "As I stand before you on my twenty fifth birthday, I cannot sit here and lie to you and say this year has been a good year. While I did achieve a great amount of success in the early part of the year, the last few months have been hell. Especially the last month. My best friend is paralyzed, I haven't held a title in months, the "powers that be" refuse to book me on pay-per-views in matches that actually MEAN something... yeah, life's been just one great joyride. When was the last time I was really on a pay-per-view in a heavily hyped match?" Universal sighed as he stared off camera into space. "July, the last time I faced the Panther at Summer Slaughter for the fWo Internet Title. A title that was around my waist for nearly three months, if you forget the two weeks that it wasn't. Wrestling is no place for people with long memories, anyways. It was also the last time I wrestled with a title on the line. Sure, Lance and I wrestled in the fWo World Tag Team Title Tournament; but we all saw how we were - and I hate to use this overused cliche - screwed. It's been like a snowball rolling down the hill since July; and has gotten worse." Universal shook his head in disgust as he continued. "No, nineteen ninety-nine has not been a good year at all. And that is why I had the fWo put together that video package the other day. To remind the fans that I'm still here. To remind the fans what it's all about: the fWo World Title. Hell, to remind ME what it's all about. It's not about the money for me at this point. I have more money in my wallet than most people have in their bank accounts. It's not about the moolah, it's not about the green, it's not about the cash. It's about the gold. The biggest prize in the game." Universal stared into the camera with a glare in his eyes. "Well, it's about god damn time that I face the music, act like a man, and stand up for myself. No more crap. No more "going through the motions" so to speak in my matches. No more mailing it in. I'm going to bust my ass to get a shot at the fWo World Title. And whether I get it on one of the final shows of the year, or whether I get it on one of the first shows of the year, I'm going to take my opportunity and make the most of it. And no matter who's the champion... whether it be Ice, whether it be the Frenchie... whether it be Primetime or the Strangler, whoever it is, when it becomes MY time... when it becomes MY opportunity to succeed... there is NO ONE on this planet that will keep me from achieving my dream and my goal of becoming the World Heavyweight Champion of the promotion that I work for, that I wrestle for... and from now on, that I bleed and sweat for. On that night, no longer will I be "Mr. $ellout", I will be a champion that everyone can respect... I will be... The Franchise. No, nineteen ninety nine has not been my year. But today, on my birthday, I guarantee you... the year two thousand will be." Universal motioned back towards the cake and blew out the candles... perhaps making a final wish as he did. . .
