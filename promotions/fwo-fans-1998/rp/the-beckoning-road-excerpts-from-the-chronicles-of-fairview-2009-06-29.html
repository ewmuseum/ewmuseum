The Beckoning Road (...excerpts from The Chronicles of Fairview Reed)

29 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mercer Island Manor is a flurry of activity as we prepare (yet again) for life on the road. What an abominable bother! How am I to concentrate with this dreadful noise and continued interruptions? More than once have I had to threaten Mrs. McPhineas with some awful, imagined doom lest she remove my treasured books from my immediate grasp! In all honesty I do realize that she is merely doing what she must to necessitate our impending travels but also do I know that my research shall suffer greatly for it; I have never found reading in a moving vehicle to be conducive to any worthwhile pursuit of true knowledge, and I have learned (to my utter and utmost discomfort) that I can follow the thread of a written idea for only so long before gastric distress assaults me and my temples feel as though being pierced by Mursi war spears… I know that I have no real say in this matter; Mrs. McPhineas has committed us to this course of action and follow through we must. And so our entourage shall board our metal conveyances and once again lead a nomadic lifestyle. The only shred of solace I can perceive is the true onset of our quest for The 9 Books! Mrs. McPhineas has one essential task before we embark upon our journeys and she has assured me that this is the week that she and The Assistant shall seek out the woman here in the Pacific Northwest with the storied and fearful reputation as the Witch of Tiger Mountain! Normally I would not even entertain the notion that such a visit would provide any concrete results – I have disproved so very many Mountebanks and Charlatans in my time on this plane of existence that the very idea of finding one with the true calling and gift seems remote in the extreme. Yet I have divined that this woman is indeed what others claim her to be, that she does possess some small talent in the Arcane Arts! I have no doubt that she is the lynchpin to our quest, that she will provide that one miniscule piece of knowledge that will send us spinning headlong towards our ultimate goal! Oh but it is difficult to remain patient and composed in the face of such promise! It is my heartfelt belief that this shall be the TRUE beginning of our journey – I can nearly feel the rough leather binding of the first of The 9 Books upon my palm. I can almost see the timeless, timely text in my mind’s eye. How long has it been since mortal eyes have gazed upon those mystic symbols? How many millennium have passed since this tome of wonder was held in the hands of one who had the knowledge, talent, and desire to be able to truly utilize its potential? OH, I nearly SWOON with giddiness! I can FEEL this in my heart of hearts, the nearness, the closeness of such wonder! This (and, I believe, this alone) has ignited in me an eagerness for the road, to know that one portion of our seemingly endless search is very near its conclusion and that our quest can TRULY get underway… It occurs to me that I would be remiss, in the course of this day’s ramblings, to fail to mention recent, peripheral events that needs have a profound effect on our chosen course of action. Mrs. McPhineas has her part to play, as our de facto leader and indeed the glue that holds our motley assortment together, and as our resident Sensitive. The Assistant is the solid foundation, the ROCK that is steadfast in the storm. Mr. Yip, despite his hermetic disposition and diminutive stature, is our strategist – his pen mightier than any sword, his mental prowess more fearsome than a pack of polar wolves. Mr. Werner, being a first-class physician and skilled surgeon, acts as our shadow guardian and gray knight, ready to hurl himself into any fray. And then there is me, your humble narrator… I sometimes feel as though I am merely a bit player in this grand drama; Mrs. McPhineas would heartily disagree, and has endeavoured to instill upon me in very recent days past that my role has been altered profoundly. Apparently no longer is it enough to merely participate in the falderal of this wrestling spectacle with which we’ve attached ourselves – it has been decreed that I must now prove myself in this field of pseudo combat and emerge victorious from these contests! How very droll! My continued presence is no longer good enough for the cretins in charge?!?! Very well then; I have observed these so-called ‘athletes’ long enough to be certain that sustained victories shall be no great feat, and I have the confidence in my talents and abilities to believe I can continue to make these physical encounters as brief and concise as possible – my research and our quest MUST take precedence o’er all else.
