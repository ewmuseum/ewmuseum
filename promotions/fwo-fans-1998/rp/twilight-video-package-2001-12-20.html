Twilight Video Package

20 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN: Jeremiah Frankton in front of a Twilight promotional poster in mid speech... JF: And of course, the main event, for the fWo World Title cue appropriate chyron , can Ruben Ross make the dream come true in his own backyard and pin Ric Chronos? Despite Chronos' attack on him Sunday night that caused the 2001 Wrestler of the Year to suffer a mild concussion and a severe thigh bruise, Ross has sent word to fWo officials that he'll be there at Twilight. Their paths have crossed before, but at Twilight, everything's on the line. fWo logo throws to...the Offspring's "Defy You". As the opening notes play, we see snapshots from SummerSlaughter of Ross flying off with the first Black Sabbath and missing, then Chronos hitting the Chronosphere to win the title, then 3vil revealing themselves to close the show. Chronos shows the WotY brackets, followed by Ross hitting the Panther with the Blackout, a pair of shots of him hitting Chronos with the Plague of Darkness, followed by pinning Primetime, and the Sabbath on Creed to win it all... You may push me around but you cannot win You may throw me down but I'll rise again As the first two lines play, we see 3vil looking out towards the rampway and Ross just standing there glaring at Chronos, a glare that is returned... The more you say, the more I defy you So get out of my face whoa yeah Video plays of Ross & Chronos talking crap against each other and then deciding to use each other's faces to check their respective ring sizes. You cannot stop us You cannot bring us down Never give up We go on and on PT & Frenchie combine to hit the Time Bomb/Guillotine legdrop combination, with Chronos holding the belt inches from Ross' face, both men exchanging profanities... You'll never break us Never bring us down We are alive Shots of Ross hitting Frenchie with the Blackout, playing to a cheering fanbase, Ross clearing house on 3vil and his 3 Hate Me jersey. When the wind blows I lean into the wind When my anger grows I'll use it to win Chronos glaring at an arena's fans at the top of the ramp, shots of him hitting the Chronosphere and the figure four DDT give way to him raising up the fWo World Heavyweight Title four times, exultant smile on his face... The more you say The more I defy you So get out of my way whoa yeah Ross eating the Chronosphere and kicking out, Ross eating the F4DDT and kicking out, before planting Ric with a Spin Cycle... All my will All my strength Rip it out Start again Slow-mo footage of Primetime's backdrop driver on Chronos at last year's Countdown 2 fades to Ross getting Eradicated off the stage at In Misery, fades to footage of both of them lying in unconscious crumpled heaps... You cannot stop us You cannot bring us down Never give up We go on and on You'll never break us Never bring us down We are alive Shots of Ross hitting high-flying maneuvers are intersped with Chronos laying him out with a sledgehammer last Sunday, only for Ross to come back and hit him in the face, only for Chronos to chair him viciously--something that has happened a lot in the past couple weeks. As we hit the bridge, guitar riffs fade to the background as we see & hear some of the verbal jousting between them: Chronos calling Ross a "never was" and a "crybaby", Ross dressing up like Chronos to call Chronos a moron and a codependent, amongst other things suitable for PG-13 ratings... All my will All my strength Rip it out Start again All my will All my strength Rip it out Start again (Can you leave it all behind?) Shots of the similarities between the men arise now, as we see Ross with the Underground and Chronos with 3vil, both men hoisting the Internet Title, both men smirking out at an audience, both men dropping opponents with brainbusters and trying to make them tap out to the dragon sleeper... All my will All my strength (Can you leave it all behind?) Rip it out ('Cause you can't go back) Start again (You can't go back) (Can you leave it all behind?) All my will (Can you leave it all behind?) All my strength ('Cause you can't go back, can't go back) Rip it out (Can't go back, can't go back, whoaaaaa) Start again Greatest Ross hits play now, as in rapid succession: official announcement of winning Survivor, Jeremiah doing the voice-over of him becoming the first black Cruiserweight Champion, longest reigning Internet Champion, and the 2001 Wrestler of the Year... Rewind to last year, with Chronos hoisting the Tag Team titles and being announced as the 2000 Wrestler of the Year, the huge pop as he won the World Title for the first time the last December 17th, him winning a battle royal to get a World Title shot at Cyberslam IV... ------------------------ The music stops. Jeremiah: And these two men are on an epic collision course for Twilight!!! Let's rock! ------------------------ You cannot stop us You cannot bring us down Never give up We go on and on 3vil running wild on the federation since their inception, laying out everyone who dares oppose them, including Fallen, Universal, Kelly, and the Strangler. Shot of them laughing and gloating, holding up their three titles... You'll never break us Never bring us down We are alive We are alive Whoa no Ross getting the Snapshot and rebounding with a Sit The Fuck Down, and a shot taken from the bottom of the ramp of fans cheering as he poses at the top of the ramp setting off his pyro... As the final notes of the song play, we see a night shot of the San Diego Sports Arena with the marquee proudly brandishing the words fWo's TWILIGHT...DECEMBER 22ND...SOLD OUT...GO ROSS.... Above it, the fWo World Title has been stretched out to look as if the roof of the arena has been replaced by it, with the faces of Ross & Chronos looming over the building like you see in all the posters. Copyright fWoE2001 notice. Fade to black... Or fade to BLACK PLAGUE?
