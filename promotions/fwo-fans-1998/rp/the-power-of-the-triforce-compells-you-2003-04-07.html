The power of the Triforce compells you.

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So, anyway, we should, by now, be quite introduced with Allen Speck and his "alter ego", the Codemaster. We should also be introduced with the fact that Allen Speck and the Fuse Bros. have probably gotten acquainted with one another. Little did anyone know that there was a major communication gap between Allen Speck and the Fuses. The Fuses lived their gimmick. Allen Speck did not. Hence why we were here, in the Fuse Bros. hotel room in England, at around two o'clock in the afternoon. Allen Speck stood in front of the Fuse Bros., and in the middle of the three of them was a table with something large and pointy covered with a towel. Tyler Fuse wore a brown shirt and hat, while Conor Fuse wore a green shirt and hat. Which was kind of normal for them. Allen Speck, on the other hand, wore a black fWo shirt and blue jeans, and his arm still required the sling. "Why aren't you wearing your robes?!" Tyler Fuse asked. "Because I traditionally don't want to look like an idiot for every waking moment of my life, rather than just four hours out of the week." Allen fumed. "Huh?" Conor Fuse asked. Allen slapped his head and gave up on this one, "I, uh, am having the... uh... robes... dry-cleaned... yeah. Don't Bowser gotta clean his shell every time he fights Mario?" Tyler and Conor looked at each other and silently debated this. Finally, Tyler sheepishly nodded his head in the direction of Allen Speck. Then there was your traditional awkward pause that kinda occurs when you have a conversation of this sort, before Allen broke it. "So, why'd y'all call me? Some of us have this time zone thing we gotta get used to." Allen said, refering to the fact that he'd been in Europe for the last two weeks now and still hadn't gotten used to the time zones. "Well..." Conor said, before he looked at his brother for guidance and found none, so he just simply looked at Speck again, "We kinda did a little research, and since you claim to be the 'blackest brother in the Hi, I Rule Kingdom' and all, we found out some stuff about this 'Hi, I Rule' place! And we figure that if we wanna make our Codemaster even MORE powerful, we should find him this 'Triforce' thingie!" So, Tyler Fuse ripped the towel off of the thing that it covered, which was resting on the table, revealing what appeared to be a trio of triangles connected at points, in such a way that a fourth triangle was formed out of the empty space inbetween the three triangles. It was made of wood and painted with an obviously cheap gold paint. Under all accounts, it looked like the Triforce from the Zelda games. "Oh my holiest God." Allen said, shaking his head in embarassment at this. "C'mon! With this, you can REALLY power up!" Tyler Fuse said with a wide smile on his face. "I, I think... I think I'm gonna be sick!" Allen said, before he held his mouth with one of his hands and quickly rushed off to the bathroom. Tyler looked at Conor. Conor looked at Tyler. "I think he likes it!" Sounds of gagging and dry-heaving could be heard in the background.
