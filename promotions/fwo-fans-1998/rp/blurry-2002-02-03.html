Blurry

3 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scott Wasner flipped aimlessly through the channels in his hotel room. He was tired, but sleep wasn't coming. The way he saw it, he had three choices. One, get drunk. Bad choice. He wouldn't go to sleep that way, and his flight was early in the morning. Two, try and catch a German cockroach and compare it, size-wise, to American cockroaches. Also a bad idea, not to mention disgusting, though surely scientifically fascinating. So, he came to his third choice - try and find something worth watching on German television. He was still in Germany - though a good majority of the rest of the boys had taken the first flight to Florida, Wasner had stuck around Germany. He had, after all, been born at a hospital near Rammstein AFB, his family had history tracing back to the country, and he had never really had a chance to visit the country since leaving as a very young child. There had always been something - not enough money, not enough time, what-have-you. The fWo had toured Europe multiple times, but had never actually stopped over in Germany before, and Wasner intended to make the most of it. At the moment though, the country itself was the furthest thing from his mind. His mind itself was well across the Atlantic and back in Seattle, back at his house - back with Steph. I haven't seen her since... God, since the 30th. I haven't seen her all year. Yeah, we've talked, but damn if the phone isn't a poor substitute. Earlier that day they had talked - it hadn't been the easiest thing to manage, what with the time difference, but they had finally managed to find a time that would work for both of them. And talking to her... man, it's like water to a guy in the desert. Yeah, that sounds like bullshit, but it's so true. I've been basically as alone as it gets for over a month... the rest of the boys, I don't know what to make of them. I don't know half of them from Adam, and the ones I do know I'd rather not be around in the first place. Jesus. What have I gotten myself into anyway? Wasner shut off the television and settled back into the bed. The bed was hard as a rock - a killer on his back, which had already been killed more than enough, thank you. He was in pain. He was exhausted. More than that, he was tired. Weary. Was it the right decision to come back? I'm in physical pain, I'm alone all the time, two straight weeks now I haven't been able to function for days after a show without getting painkillers from some slimeball backstage dealer, and I'm probably alienating about the only person who means a damn thing to me anymore. And for what? Money? The cheers of the fans? With my name and my abilities, I can get money plenty of places without all the shit that comes with this. I could be acting, I could be coaching baseball, I could be doing damn near anything. And the cheers of the fans. What the hell does that mean anyway? Faceless cash cows out in the audience, ready to throw you aside for the next hot fad as soon as the marketing machine tells them to. Dammit. NO. No, no, no. Yeah, there are problems. There are lots of problems, but there always are. I did make the right decision. I've just had... a bad few weeks is all. Tomorrow, I'm back in America... and back with Steph. Finally. They had been planning this since that day in early December when Wasner had been faxed a copy of the fWo's schedule. The return to America. Stephanie would fly in to Tampa to meet him, and they'd spend... well, at least two days together. That Sunday, and she had gotten Monday off... after that though, she had to go back. The road is a bitch. You get something good going, and it gets yanked away. No. Two days, Scott... two days. Make the most of them. You heard her on the phone... you know that this is as hard on her as it is on you. Maybe harder. Cut this pity crap and get some sleep... I've got two days ahead of me to make the most of. Two days I've been waiting for for a month. Besides, what's that me and her always say? Our little phrase. Keep the faith. Just a little longer. Sleep came to Scott Wasner, and he was at peace.
