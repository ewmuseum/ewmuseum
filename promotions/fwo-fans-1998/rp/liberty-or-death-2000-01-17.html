Liberty or death.

17 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Liberty or death. January 17, 2000 Slaughter had concluded the night before elsewhere in the city. Although most of the workers were already on their way to France for Warfare on Wednesday, he had stayed behind in Philadelphia. In the backdrop, was the pavillion over the Liberty Bell. He wore a black trenchcoat, black pants and a black sweater. The sunlight gleamed off the top of his shaved head, while his ominous stature cast a shadow over the ground below him. As he stared into the camera deviously, words came out of his mouth in a calm yet stern manner. "I stand before you today in Philadelphia... the so-called City of Brotherly Love. Behind me, is the infamous Liberty Bell. An important landmark in this supposedly "great nation" of ours. The Pennsylvania Assembly had it ordered in 1751, to commemorate the 50-year anniversary of William Penn's 1701 Charter of Privileges. It was later recast several time over the years after having cracked; and was rung to signify important events when Philadelphia was the capital of our nation. It supposedly has not been rung since 1840. If you look up the word liberty in your copy of the dictionary, it defines the word as "the quality or state of being free", and also "the power to do as one pleases" or "freedom from physical restraint". And this so-called hero of yours, fWo... this "icon" that has achieved superstar status within your organization despite having only held one title.. well, his "speech" yesterday reminded me of the word liberty. And that is why I stand you here today in front of the Liberty Bell." Dead turned towards the important piece of architecture behind him, before turning back into the direction of the camera. "The power to do as one pleases is quite a pleasant thing; something the fWo has not been able to do since I made my return at the beginning of the new millennium. Sure, Scott and I let them book their own show last night of which they took advantage of by trying to obliterate the World's Greatest Athlete, but Finn's plan backfired. Scott is still standing... not even two of your "best" can eliminate him. Your whole company, for that matter, couldn't either. Not a company he built almost singlehandedly. For he made a wise choice when he realized just how much he was being disrespected by the promotion he more or less built and kept together in 1999, and now you all are suffering from it as a result. He and I have you all in our grasp... with each of you gasping for breath... praying for a glimmer... a hope of liberty. Freedom from physical restraint. Freedom, period. And none of you had the courage to step up to the plate and take a stand for your company until last night. It came to me as no surprise that it was The Boston Strangler. For he has defended your company time and time again, although it can be said that perhaps he doesn't realize he's just being used to get rid of your main concern... us. He's a tool, and nothing more. Some have said he is the master of psychological warfare. Well, to the people that have said this, I say "go to hell" because you're looking at the supreme player of mind games. Nobody can get inside someone's head quite like I can, and I have. Ask Jim Dudley. Ask the beloved owner of this company. Ask Chris Universal. Ask any of them. All of them. Each and everyone of them will tell you the same thing. I fucked with their minds and none of them have recovered. Each one of them have been left second-guessing their decisions & regretting the choice they made when they chose to become involved in a war with me." Dead looked into the camera with a sly grin, and said proudly... "Oh yeah, I fucked them." He stared off at the snow that had befallen the ground just days earlier, before continuing. "If I may give you some advice, Paul... aligning yourself with a fool like Universal will not help you. The old saying "keep your friends close, but your enemies closer" does indeed apply here. But back to your much needed heroics of last night. You do not realize the choice you made last night was the biggest mistake or your life, one you will live to regret. Maybe. What is it with you anyways? You go after the original and up until recently more evil of the two "Death"'s... then you went after Collins... and now me. Obsession, perhaps? Likely. After watching you from afar and more recently... upon closer inspection... it is obvious you have a weakness. You might say... a fear. Paul... your speech reminded me of something else... something Patrick Henry wrote of over two hundred and twenty five years ago... perhaps you may have heard of it? You see... Henry is responsible for the quote "give me liberty or give me death". And that again reminds me of what you spoke of last night... your quest for liberty..." Dead stared into the depths of the lens with a powerful glare, not blinking, not breathing... just staring into the camera with a cold look. "... will be denied. Next Sunday... you search for liberty... but you will find none here. I won't give you your freedom. I won't give you liberty. I will give you but one thing... Your death." Dead stared into the sky, while the Liberty Bell rung for the first time in over one hundred and sixty years. Dead walked out from underneath the pavillion in the direction of the glistening white snow, while the crack in the bell began to open even more. A slow fade to black ended it all.
