Second Heartbeat: From Here

10 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The large suitcase, already full with the kids clothes for the week sat open. Brandon eyed it cautiously, there had to be a way to fit another few outfits and items in there. They were already up to seven bags, and he hadn't even gotten to his things yet. As he tried another technique, Amy walked out of the bathroom with a bag of toiletries. Somehow she had managed to finish her packing in half an hour, leaving him to worry with the kids things, he assured her it wouldn't be a problem. She smiled as he finally leaned on the top of the baggage, trying with a curved thumb to hit the lock, securing the bulging items away safely. He could have sworn he heard a 'click'. Or not. The lid flipped open and in a frantic dive both Brandon and the suitcase tumbled from the bed. The neatly and tightly packed clothes exploded from the case, covering the floor, and an upside down Paragon in the process. "Damn it." That was the third time it had happened. The flustered Irishman sighed, looking up to Amy for comfort, finding her leaned against the wall instead. She held her sides they hurt so much. That never stopped being funny. As she wiped away a tear induced by the laughter, Amy paused. "Does widdle Brandon need help with the big, bad suitcase?" "I think Jarod could do it," she finished mockingly, "Want me to go get 'im?" Brandon pulled the stack of underwear off his head as he looked back at his new wife. "Keep it up... we'll see how you fit in here." Then, startling the little redhead, Brandon kipped to his feet and lunged at her. She squealed from the surprise but found herself going head over heels once again, straight to the mattress. The tickling that ensued brought Amy to the brink of hysteria before he finally stopped and rolled to the side. "You realize we don't have that much time left right? Jon will be here with the van in about an hour. Are the boys dressed and ready to go? I know Sean finished packing toys... that's those two brown suitcases over there." Brandon motioned with his free hand, throwing a thumb 'over his shoulder'. "Stop worrying," smiled Amy, "They're all dressed, clean and lined up for inspection if necessary." The little redhead continued, poking Brandon in the stomach, "But you, buddy; You've gotta relax. It's not a week to stress about anything. We're not doing this just to give you one extra thing to organize. "You're gonna chill out," she winked, "Or I'll just have to find myself a new Irishman." "Hmpf..." Brandon looked out of the corner of his eye, "You know you couldn't get along without me. So get up and help me cram this stuff in the bag, I'll grab the other bag and get my stuff." As he stood and pulled Amy to her feet, but not trying to sound too anal he remembered some arrangements that had gone through, "Oh... Troy called back finally, Bimbo will pick us up from the airport. Lawrence and Bobby won't be back from their trip until the day after we get there, and Troy has a photo shoot in Paris. It seems that we'll be alone at the Knight Mansion for an entire day..." Amy began half-folding the clothing strewn about the room and stuffing it into the suitcase, but quickly gave up folding and took more to balling shirts and little pants up. Much faster, she smiled as Brandon looked on halfway in horror. Her smile only grew wider - watching him go insane over wrinkled t-shirts for the first day there was guaranteed entertainment. "You do realize that between the boys and I, you're basically taking a stampede of bulls through a china shop going to that mansion, right?" "Oh, and you'd better not expect me to keep from giggling when...err...'Bimbo'," she snickered, "Picks us up." "Er..." Picturing Amy tripping over her oversized boots and knocking over that orange and red monstrosity of a Ming vase in the front entranceway, Brandon cringed. "Well... maybe we can stay in a hotel... or something." With another smile, he too finally resigned himself to Amy's packing technique, "And you better not laugh at Bimbo. It's phonetic... sort of... Bobby, when he was real little couldn't pronounce his real name, that's what came from it, and it stuck. He's Lawrence's personal assistant and herbal doctor. He can seriously work magic." This whole trip was going to be magic. This would be their first real trip together as a family. A whole week for them to get fed up with each and to work it all out again. She'd spoken to Troy Knight on the phone on a few occasions and saw him for the wedding, but she hadn't had a chance to really get to know one of Brand Frontier's strongest allies and friends. Lawrence was as much a mentor for Brandon as his trainer Tobo, or even his father ever was. Bobby was a close friend of Sean and Jarods, they had had many adventures that Sean took great pleasure in telling over... and over again. "Oh, so like most of my friends!" Brandon's eyebrow raised as Amy's head lowered. "Err...well...different 'herbs', I guess." She still wasn't sure how well the Knight family would take to her. Amy was entirely aware that she could be 'a lot to digest' at times, and it wasn't clear yet whether Lawrence Knight would have any of that. "So...exactly how proper are these people?" "They are the cookie cutter English proper family you'd expect... tea, crumpets, and all that. 'pip-pip, jolly good day to you sir', any sterotype you can think of it's probably based on Lawrence. And Amy... for Heaven's sake chill out with the worry, you're telling me to ease up and have fun... you need to take your own advice. Troy is gay... and Lawrence doesn't judge him for that. I really doubt he'll have a problem with the Red Raver herself." "...And if he does..." With that left open-ended, Amy clicked the suitcase shut non-chalantly and laughed in Brandon's general direction.
