My Confessions: Past, Present, Future

27 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A sleepy- voiced Angel answered the phone. "Hello?" "Hiya. What time's it there?" "It's... fuck... it's like three in the morning. Where are you?" "Damn," apologized Eli, "I keep forgettin' - we're in the middle of the continent just like you, my usual sense of time difference is thoroughly skewed." "It's okay," said Angel, more awake as she stretched out in her bunk, "How'd the show go?" "Best show'a the year so far... I wasn't on it. Shadows was off doing promo work for Cyberslam so Twitch jus' really told me I could chill in the back and actually take a breather." "Isn't that bad though?" "Oh... no. Not a'tall. According to the boys in charge I've been going above and beyond on a nightly basis... the least they could do is give me a night off." "That's good. Any word on Fox?" Eli's mood was easily read - he didn't want to talk about the still- missing Silver Fox. "Nothing on him," replied Eli, "Oh! How'd the show go? Did you talk to your parents?" More silence. "The show went okay." Eli got the hint. "Oh!" exclaimed Angel suddenly, "One of your friends were here - he said he wrestled you once in the fWo in, I guess it's called a preliminary match? Dark match? Something like that." "Yeah? Cool! How'd he know you'd be there? I thought the show wasn't really advertised outside the locals and inside the fWo lockers." "Well he must've heard about it in the lockerroom then. Damn, y'know this'd be a lot easier if I could remember his name. Who's been in and out real quick lately? Like for one show or another?" Eli was confused. "Nobody, really... with the extended overseas tour we've really cut down on tryouts and one- shots. Only people who've been around sporadically have been..." He knows something. "Angel." "Hmmm?" "Did he have a British accent?" "No... why?" "What did he look like?" "Like a wrestler, I dunno. He was black, he looked like he was built, though not really big, y'know? Like a lighter wrestler - what're they called?" "Cruiserweights." "Yeah - cruiserweights. But he wasn't a cruiser - I could tell. He's probably a what'sit- brawler or martial artist." Holy.... "How could you tell?" "Just the way he carried himself - you're not the only one who notices things. He seemed to know you pretty well." ...Living... "King, that's it! Exxa King was his name." ....Fuck. "Angel, where are you right now?" "I don't really know, somewhere outside Vegas? We were gonna take half a day or something and do a little gambling before we head back home and hit the studio... got a lotta stuff to lay down." "No." "What?" "You get to Vegas and you wait for me, Angel. I'll be there in a day or so." "Why?" "Why? Trust me." "I do trust you... but you're starting to scare me, Eli... what's the matter?" "Just trust me, Angel. And don't go anywhere without MJ or the boys." "Eli. I'm not some weak- willed weepy woman. Something's going on, and I want you to tell me what." "That man you met..." "Yeah?" "That was Villam Ender." "WHAT?" "I'm leaving right now, Angel... please wait for me. I love you." "I love you too. Eli?" "Hmmm?" "Please hurry." Now it's a race
