A Day at the Races

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Don't worry, we'll have plenty of time to get to Buffalo for the show," Said Monte Burns as he patted Tempest's hand. She stopped fidgeting and smiled as she said, "Ok, ok... Actually this sounds like fun, I just get nervous about the show." Monte nodded and handed her a glass of champagne as they sat comfortably in the back of a limousine, motoring through the city. Tempest swirled her glass and looked out the window thoughtfully. "It's neat thought that they're talking about maybe having a Triple Crown horse again. Ya know I was two when the last one won." "Yeah, I was only a year or two older," Monte said with a chuckle. Burns took a sip from his glass of champagne and looked over at Tempest sitting next to him. Instead of wearing her normal clothing, Burns was pleasantly surprised to see Tempest wearing something much different than he and others were used to seeing her in. She wore a simple red silk dress, short enough to show off her sleek, athletic legs, with delicate spaghetti straps over her shoulders. Monte reached up and ran a hand through her hair and smiled. "You look wonderful tonight," he said as she turned and looked into his eyes. She smiled and winked as she said, "Well thanks. I try to clean up well. Besides, it's nice to show that I have a clue how to dress for something other than a rave. This is gonna be fun though." She hopped a little on the seat in excitement. "It should be," Burns said as he took another sip from the champagne. "I'm just wondering who I should put my money on for the race." Tempest snickered and said, "Geez, hadn't thought of that. I know I want War Emblem to win. It'd be great to see a Triple Crown winner. And he's really pretty!" She stopped and frowned a moment, then said, "I mean he's really good looking in horsy terms, strong, ready to go, ya know?" "Yeah," Monte said with a sarcastic nod of the head, "I know exactly what you mean." Tempest laughed and playfully elbowed him in the side. Burns chuckled and took another drink as he watched the streets, buildings, people, and everything else pass by as the limo drove through the city. A moment later, he turned back to Tempest and took another sip from his glass. "So you think I should go with War Emblem?" She shrugged and said, "I would. But that's just me being really hopeful." "You're a lot more than hopeful, Tempest," Monte said as he leaned over and softly kissed her neck. Tempest grinned and said, "Well someone has to be!" They laughed and she scooted closer as the buildings passed by and they neared Belmont park. ~~~ As the majority of the fans filed out to their cars to leave Belmont Park after missing out on history, Monte and Tempest waited in one of the private boxes with a pair of security guards. After checking his watch, Burns slowly walked over to the large window overlooking the track and looked down at the finish line where the biggest longshot in Belmont history had dashed War Emblem's shot at the Triple Crown - and Monte's hope of making some money. "I bet the Prince will be disappointed," Monte said with a sigh. "He was really looking forward to this." Tempest nodded and walked over to join Monte. "I know. I usually like to see an underdog win. But sometimes it would be nice to see a new legend be born." "Definitely," said Burns with a smile. "Especially when the legend could have won me some money." Tempest snickered and said, "But wasn't everyone else betting on War Emblem?" "Yes, they were," he said as he put his arm around her. "However, not everyone was laying down six figures." Tempest gaped at him. "You wha....? Well, never let it be said you do things halfway." "I guess I just enjoy betting on things," Monte said with a shrug. "It makes things much more interesting if there's money riding on the outcome. Besides, the odds were only 7-5, so I had to put up a lot to make anything. It's not like I'll miss that kind of money either." Tempest shook her head with a sigh and said, "That's going to take some getting used to. You know what happened when I finally earned enough money that I actually had six figures in the bank?" "What?" he asked. She chuckled and said, "That's when I hired Miyuke. I couldn't even look at my account balance, my brain just froze." Monte smiled and laughed along with Tempest, as the two of them stared out at the empty track below. Seconds later, there was a knock at the door, and one of the security guards opened it. As the door swung open, Bob Baffert walked through the door followed by some other officials. The silver-haired trainer of War Emblem walked over to the couple and smiled. "Monte," he said extending his hand to shake Monte's, "it's great to see you. I'm glad you could make it out today." "Thanks for the tickets, Bob," Burns said with a nod. "Sorry that you weren't able to come away with the win." As Baffert nodded his head, Monte motioned towards Tempest and smiled once more. "I'd like you to meet Tempest." Tempest smiled, her eyes a little wide with surprise. She extended her hand and said, "Hi Mr. Baffert. Monte never ceases to amaze me with who he knows." She chuckled nervously and said, "I was sorry to see War Emblem lose. He's a great horse though. Is he ok?" "Yes, he should be fine," Baffert said scratching his head. "That rough start was just too difficult to overcome. When he made his run about halfway through, I felt that he might pull it out, but it looked like he used up too much there." Tempest nodded somberly and quietly asked, "Could I uh... Could we see him?" "Of course you can," Baffert said as he flashed a smile over to Burns. "Just follow me." Tempest grinned as they followed Baffert through the hallways of Belmont Park and she said, "Well, at least he's not hurt. And he can still have a good career. I mean it's not like winning the first two races is anything to sneeze at." "Yeah," Monte cut in with a shrug, "but the winnings from this race along with everything that would come from being a Triple Crown winner would have been much better." Tempest snickered and patted Baffert's arm as they entered the stables and said, "Oh I know. But still, he's a great horse. I mean that's what you have to think about right? Not today's disappointment." Baffert looked at her and nodded a little. "You're right. It's hard to after the third try, but... you're right, he is a great horse." With that they stopped at a stall where War Emblem raised his dark head and sniffed in their direction. "At least he doesn't look too disappointed," said Burns as he grabbed a small bag of mints from a stool nearby and handed them to Tempest. "With his attitude, I wouldn't be surprised if he lost on purpose," said Baffert with a chuckle. Seeing Tempest with the mints, he reached up and petted the horse as he looked over to her. "Just be careful with your hands. I wouldn't want to get blamed for you losing one." Tempest chuckled and carefully put a mint into the palm of her hand, offering it to the horse with her fingers held back out of the way. She giggled as he snuffled and nibbled at the candy. "What do you mean? He's not stubborn is he?" She looked at Baffert with a facetious grin. "Not stubborn," Baffert said as he grinned. "Just snappy and usually hungry." Tempest smiled and offered another mint to War Emblem as she scratched under his forelock. "Aww... He's had a hard day. I don't think he'd bite me. Do you?" She turned and looked at Monte with a smile as she leaned toward the horse who made a small contented whinny. "I don't know," Burns said with a shrug. "It looks like you may hold the same kind of spell over horses that you hold over men." "Who me?" she said innocently. She looked from Monte to Baffert and back and then giggled as War Emblem nibbled at her hair gently. Monte laughed and said, "Oh no, not you." Baffert watched and laughed. He shook his head and said, "I should have had her come and see him before the race." They all laughed and the defeated horse seemed not to mind as Tempest offered him another mint and whispered, "Don't worry. Everybody stumbles sometime."
