Merry Christmas!

7 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bing Crosby's "White Christmas" plays as a black boot taps along to the holiday melody. Beside it, a green boot is also keeping time. Well, I wonder who the hell this is. But as we pan up, we see the expected man--Ruben Ross--wearing a Santa suit. He's next to a decorative tree, smiling away, even though we can see the sun fall on him and people behind him skating on an ice rink. Ho, ho, ho, everybody! Merrrrrrrrrrrrrrry Christmas! He drops the Santa voice. You know, it's truly the most wonderful time of the year. I remember all my great Christmas memories, as everyone else does. Pulling on my favorite pair of shorts and going down to the beach...snocone fights...riding my skateboard down a hill... Some may say it lacks the panache of a true winter, but of course those people are freezing their asses off come Yuletide when I'm lounging in a freezing 58 degrees. BRRRRRRRRRR. The first few years in Pittsburgh, I got snow. I liked snow outside of having to wear two times my weight in clothes, it was fun. But then I moved out here. And I found something better. Namely, sunlight after Labor Day. It's just like my fWo career. 16 months, 5 belts. And I am YOUR 2001 Wrestler of the Year. There was always something I liked, but I found something else better. I slapped Plague out of the fed...but then I got my first taste of cruiserweight gold. And that was so good, I had to do it again. Then I moved on to the Internet Title and pretty much owned that for six months. I lost to Ric Chronos, when I debuted the Sabbath. And you're saying, "Hey, Ruben, you lost the match, you've got a stupid grin on your face, does Chris know you're cutting a promo with this much eggnog in you?" I smile, friends, because the one time Chronos was able to pin me without any help is because of me. HE. CAN'T. BEAT. ME. At least not without Siegfried and/or Roy backing him up. No, wait, there was the time in the tournament....no, I pinned him...well, there was Slaugher last week...no, I pinned him there.... Huh! So, by my count Ricardo, you've got two more Slaughters and 15 more days with my belt. About 15 minutes away is a little place called the Sports Arena. It's the home of Twilight. It's quite literally my house. You'll walk in a two-inched three-move wonder, and I'll walk out of my joint the first-ever black fWo Heavyweight Champion of the World. And unless I somehow die between now and then, that's pretty set in stone. That's what the stupid grin was about, is all I'm saying. I'm going to go skate now. I'll see you Sunday. And I'll take my Christmas present a few days after that. Ross heads off to the skate rental as we hear Steve Miller's voice intone "Time keeps on slippin'/slippin'/slippin'...into the futuuurreeee..."
