The Taking of Team Extreme - 7/10

The Taking of Team Extreme Chapter Seven January 1st, 2004 Los Angeles, CA 1:22 PM local time "Always the one that's going to let her down... maybe you'll get what'cha want this time around..." **THWACK** Eli listened at the door to his bedroom, and recognized the song. **THWACK** "The trick is to keep breathing..." **THWACK** When Angel was pounding on the heavy bag and listening to 'The Trick is to Keep Breathing,' she was extremely bothered by something. Eli pushed the door open and peeked inside - Angel was dressed in a pair of his workout shorts and her black sleeveless tanktop, her hair ponytailed. She was facing the wall to the side of him - he could tell her eyes were red and puffy. Either she was still crying, or she'd stopped within the past twenty minutes. He silently walked up to the side and held the bag - finally in her line of sight. **THWACK** If he didn't know her, he wouldn't have believed she could hit that hard. "Wanna talk about it?" **THWACK** "There's nothing to talk about." "Sure there is... talk to me, Angel. What're you feeling?" **THWACK** "I'm angry," she said, "and I'm scared." **THWACK** "What's scaring you? What happened was a tragedy... but you heard what Ivy said on the phone, they got him." **THWACK** "FUCK!" Angel had hit the bag on an odd angle, and held her forearm/wrist in pain. "What are the odds," she said, her voice quivering, "that you'd've chosen this past fall to take an open ended break? What're the odds that Ivy would've got the kids booked on this particular card? If they'd have wrestled the night before, they'd've gone out the night before, and she would've been there for the fire, and she might've--" She swing her fist again, but Eli caught it and embraced her. "I've got no answers for you," he whispered in her ear, as she sobbed into his shoulder, "other than we lost some good people... and even more were physically or emotionally damaged." Sobs. "So close to losing the most important person in my life," she said, "I don't know if I can take it..." "You weren't close to losing me," he assured, "I'm here with you... and I'm not goin' anywhere anytime soon." Angel continued to cry into his shoulder, but her shaking began to slow. "Temerario, Twitch and Smooth were good men... and Spyder... he was one'a the boys." Eli laughed nervously, trying to get Angel to follow. "But this is where everyone's nerves are tested. This is where you'll see what D can do, what Brand can do as champion... this is where they all have to take hold and keep things going." Backed up a bit, Angel looked at him. "I'm serious," he said, "The way that promotion was run, everyone worked, everyone played. And when some of the leaders fall, those that remain owe it to themselves - to each other - to the people we lost - to pick up the slack." That thought seemed to pick up her spirits a bit. "In any case," he continued, "Ted and Ivy'll rally the troops, right?" "Right." "Because if they don't pick up the slack... the fWo is currently at its lowest point. And it'd be real easy for someone to slide in and seize the reins," was the thought Eli didn't share. He couldn't stand the thought of shattering her fragile confidence in those who remained.
