Rebirth

30 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**The scene: the back of a limo. The participants: Four Dark Riders... in their street clothes.** Scott Collins, aka Death: This is utter garbage. The contract is that ironclad? Stone: It sure is. Why the hell did you sign it? Scott: It was alot of money, Stone. Alot. I didn't buy that mansion off the prop cash Universal gave me... or the Z3. You like the Z3, right? Brian Cage, aka War: I sure do, that car is sweet. Scott: It sure is. Hell, the fWo's made me rich, you know? I thought I could just hire a lawyer and get the hell out of that contract. Get us all out. John Malcon, aka Plague: (calmly) How do you know they'd have treated us this well down south? Scott: Are you kidding? A draw like us? They had us penciled in to fight those guys... you know, the n-whatever. The one with Nash, and Hart... Stone: Faces? Scott: Tweeners. Brian: Tweeners, fine by me. As long as I can still kick ass. John: Bri, we've been doin anything but kick ass. That's the reason we were jumping ship with Scott, after all. Stone: Well, whatever. It's all bullshit now. We're stuck in the fWo. Scott: What can we do? Stone: Well, there is good news... Brian: Good news? How can being stuck in a contract with that asshole Beaven be good news? Stone: It's simple... the contract is so ironclad, he can't fire us. **Stone pauses to let this sink in, a wicked grin on her face...** Scott: Holy... John: Oh, wow... **Brian Cage slams his fist into his hand.** Brian: Perfect! So we can do anything we want! Stone: Within reason. Beaven can put us in matches, and if we don't show and put in a money-drawing effort, he can fine us. Scott: Shit. Stone: But, Scott, he can't do a damned thing to us if we go after him on our own time.... **The camera cuts to a scene of the limo moving down the city streets... a large '4' on the hood....**
