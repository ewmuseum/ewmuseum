Best Days: Brandon Thompson

28 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a simple enough idea, after last night Brandon needed a drink to still calm his nerves. The woman he had met, Darlene Rowe was amazing. In more ways than one. So he walked into the bar quickly ordering up a Coors light and plopped down at the bar. He could hear a familiar voice behind him, talking. "There is a next right?" Brandon heard the voice say, he spun around in his chair and looked over to the table. Jack Harmen aka High Flyer, was finishing his story and had spotted Brandon. Jack and Kevin Masters both got up and walked over towards Brandon each ordering a drink from the bar. "Wanna come over and do some talkin?" Kevin said lookin at Brandon, "man you had a real rough night didn't you?" Kevin stated as Brandon nodded. "You wouldn't believe me if I told ya," Brandon stated as Kevin grinned at him, "the stories these guys are tellin, that are the truth, I'd believe it if ya told me you got picked up by aliens last night." Kevin said sipping at his drink. "Why don't ya walk on over?" Kevin said as Brandon shrugged his shoulders, he'd worked in this place for 8 months and hardly knew a soul besides Amy, Kevin, and Alex from previous work. "I dunno..." Brandon stated as Jack jumped into the conversation. "We do need a next," Brandon sighed and nodded ordering another drink from the bar and walked over pulling a chair up to the crowded table. He looked around, man was this a crew of guys or what. Pierre, Ric, Luke, Alex, Kevin, lil Avo, a good portion of the fWo roster was sitting at a table drinking beer, this was awkward to say the least. "You my friend, are next," Pierre said with a smile, as Brandon looked over at them all "Next for what?" Brandon asked the group, Pierre looked over grinning. "Ze next to go o'ter the best day of your life." Pierre said smiling, Brandon looked around and nodded. "Aight guys, gimme a moment." Brandon took a deep breath, and then inhaled what was left of his first beer of the night. "Have you guys ever had something incredibly bad happen, but motivated you to do things that were incredibly good?" Brandon asked the group, many nodded their heads.
