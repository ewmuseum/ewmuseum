Words.

20 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
London is a city of many historical monuments - that much any grade-school child can tell you. It is a city that attracts tourists by the multitudes, a city with so much history you can drown in it if you aren't careful. Most of those who visit the city spend their fair share of time surveying the sights and sounds of both old and new London, immersing themselves in perhaps the world's most famous city. Not Scott Slugger. It is not that Slugger does not appreciate what London has to offer - that is not it by a long shot. Slugger, however, has been here many times in his life, both on tours with the fWo and on various personal visits. It is a city that he knows well, right down to the best workout facilities. It is inside one of these, a particular Gold's Gym just south of downtown London, that fWo cameras find Scott Slugger. The rest of the gym, perhaps owing to its strong selectivity in clientele, is sparsely populated at best.Slugger is in a tanktop and sweat pants, and is working out on a weight bench. The top of the tanktop is drenched in sweat, and Slugger's hair is wet and mussed. His face shows signs of strain - exertion - as he presses the bar, but it is clear that this is not something that is causing excrutiating pain, despite the relatively high amount of weights on the bar. Slugger presses the bar again and notices the camera. "You know, I can't believe how much you guys follow us around. Don't you ever have anything better to do?" The camera shakes a little, an obvious indication of "no". Slugger, still pumping, rolls his eyes. "So, I suppose you want a few words about my match tonight, huh?" The camera shakes again. This time, "yes". Slugger nods, continues pumping. "Fine. You want words, I got plenty. Tonight, it's me and Brand Frontier against the Boston Strangler and Austin Davis. fWo World Tag Team Titles on the line. I'll start with my partner, Brand Frontier. Brand, I don't really know you. You seem like a good enough guy, and I can dig that. Plus, I know you wanna take a chunk out of Davis and Strangler, and that makes you a-plus in my book. As far as I'm concerned, you work with me, I work with you, and it'll be a pleasure kicking a little ass and winning some titles. And plus, you can tell your kids that you won your first fWo title with Scott Slugger, which is always a good thing. Man, who to talk to next. I've got a lot of things to say to both of these guys, but the WGA knows that film is expensive and Chris Universal's cheap - trust me, I was with him in the Dynasty, dude never took the check - so I'll keep it short. Somewhat, anyway. Strangler, Paul, whoever. I've been waiting to get my hands on you for a long time, pal. If it weren't for you and what you did to my arm after I pinned your pansy ass in the middle of the ring for my fifth World Championship, that World Title probably never would've left Camp Slugger in the first place. That's right, I hold you personally responsible, pal - and I've been waiting almost a year and a half to get my hands on your psychotic ass. You listen to me and you listen to me good. I don't give a shit about your poor pathetic emotional problems. I don't give a shit about how you've supposedly been wronged by the fWo. I've got a lot to pay you back for, you son of a bitch, and tonight I'm going to take out all four hundred and eighty-three days of frustration on you. BABVA. Now Austin Davis, you nothing, you nobody. Last time I saw you in the ring, I personally knocked you on your ass, knocked you out of the World Title, knocked you right out of the fWo. Only fitting, considering that if it wasn't for me you never would've gotten anywhere in the FTfWo. I was the FTfWo, and you know it - I did all the work while you sat back and killed snakes, or whatever the hell lame supposed-to-be-intimidating crap you were doing that week. And yeah, it was a mistake. Those were the worst four months of my life, but that doesn't change the fact that if it weren't for me, your little wannabe bunch of losers wouldn't have gotten off the ground, let alone ever did what they ended up doing. And oh yeah, I bet you're real pissed off. Ooh, Austin Davis is *soo* angry, he's pissed, he's evil, he's blah blah blah. BFD. You mean nothing to me, Davis, not anymore. I've put you in my past, the distant past. When you end up crossing my path - like tonight - you still mean nothing to me. You're the ant crawling across my kitchen floor. Hell, you're not even that. You wish you could be that. And when it all comes down it, that's you in a nutshell, isn't it? You stir up your shit and you piss some people off, but when it's all said and done you're the Oakland Raiders of the fWo. Maybe you win a little, maybe you get in some people's heads - but everybody knows that in the end you're a paper tiger. Without somebody like me backing me up, you're a joke. Every six months you come back and you're all, 'ooh, ahh, fear me, I'm evil, I'm gonna kill the fWo, I'm gonna end your career, I'm gonna do this, I'm gonna do that, I'm gonna be the same little pussy I've always been', and then after another few months somebody dispatches you and you stay away for another six months. So what? I once knew a guy who had a boil on his ass. It acted the exact same way. You're the boil on the ass of the fWo, and sooner or later you're gonna get lanced, just like always. Tonight's the first step. Of course, I'll have to leave the rest of the steps to somebody else because I'll be too busy beating the ever-loving crap out of Vic Creed. Hi Vic - I haven't forgotten about you. You're still my bitch and all that. Be seeing you!" Slugger pauses, setting the bar back on its rest. "There, there's some words. Happy now?" The camera shakes "yes". "Great. Then go, I've got a workout to, well, continue. Go on, off with you. Git. Shoo." The cameraman does so, and Scott Slugger returns to his preparations.
