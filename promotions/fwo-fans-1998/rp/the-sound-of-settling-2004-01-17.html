The Sound of Settling

17 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's been a while since I've had the chance to sit down and hammer out a journal entry, but now seems as good of a time as any. My last journal entry was dated in the Survivor III period, which seems like ages ago. I used to write in a journal to cement the past into my memory, but I find myself writing today to combat boredom. In general, marriages are roller coaster rides. My marriage is a speedtrain rocketing towards a cliff. Jeremy and I have had this unique relationship that is never boring (to say the least!). Well, it's starting to get that way. I always expressed my distaste when our emotions raged. I wanted us to change. Now, I'm not so sure. We are far more subdued and settled these days. We have put ourselves behind shields. I have adopted a tendency of being distant around him, and Jeremy's drinking habits have increased. However, he is an adult male; he will be better off if he would handle his demons on his own... I just don't know where we went wrong. Woody Allen once said that "Relationships are like a shark. It always has to keep moving forward or it dies, and I believe what we have on our hands here is a dead shark." I wouldn't consider our marriage a dead shark just yet. It just seems like I can't find satisfaction, whether we're arguing or completely happy. If I had a choice between our old relationship and that of the current, I would want an equal concoction of both. Am I asking for too much in that case? Who knows. As I'm writing this, I notice that Jeremy is sitting in the floor, trying his hardest to annoy me. He is doing everything that would spur an argument; anything that would bring excitement and action to our experiences. He is picking at his toenails, and purposely crunching as loudly as possible on some carrot sticks. He knows this will unnerve me, but I can't help but embrace it with a smile. A glimmer of our past flickers on. While pondering the past, I should mention Dexter Pratt. The date has been set for Monday night. I will have the opportunity to catch up with an old male acquaintance. This really pisses Jeremy off, and I'm guessing that's why he drinks so heavily now. Dexter really was great, no, he was perfect. Why did I stray from him? Did his perfection bother me? Was I feeling inadequate? Even today, I can't produce a recollection or excuse if my life depended on it.
