A bottle smashed hard against Levite Prod's head and he dropped to one knee as a blue pick up truck sailed past. Inside the truck were some of the local townsfolk. Levite was back home in Rock Springs, this followed his being fired for raping a girl while serving as Douglas' bodyguard. He was framed of course, the girl who was under the influence of alcohol and drugs could not confirm if it was Steve Douglas and or Prod who had raped her that night and Douglas had managed to back his case up. With a hefty salary Douglas paid for the best lawyer, and paid the court as well... under the table. Prod had no foot to stand on, a young man from the country without a clue on how the legal system worked. Prod returned to his hometown as a rapist and a failure. Prod stood feeling the back of his head and the blood that matted his hair. He was a block away from home, he knew that his father and mother waited for his arrival. However, the glares and sneers from others weighed him down. His strength that he once had left him, he was weak. Finally he stood at the edge of the road that led up to his farm. Staring at the dirt road he contemplated his next step. Was he to go back home, to look into his parents eyes and see their disapointment in him. He knew he didn't rape the girl, but they didn't. No one believed him. He was a failure, an outsider. All because of Steve Douglas. Levite Prod turned and headed back down the road, away from his former home.
