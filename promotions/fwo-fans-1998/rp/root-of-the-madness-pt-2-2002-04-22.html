Root of the Madness pt 2

22 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
David Cross opened his eyes and stared up at the ceiling. The same nightmare, night in and night out. He'd tried to shake it, but guilt was a difficult cross to bear, no pun intended. The insurance policy Tyler had taken out named David as the beneficiary, obviously done when he and Tyler were on better terms. Cross tossed the blanket off his sweating body and turned on the lamp beside his bed. He sat up, touching his cheek in thought. As per Tyler's drunken wish, David had gone for extensive plastic surgery. David's dream had come true; he no longer looked like a freak. Not on the outside, anyway. But inside, he felt far from normal. Every time he saw his reflection, it felt like shards of glass were being twisted into his stomach. Two people had to die so he could look normal. As for feeling normal... the jury was still out. Wearing only his boxers, Cross walked out of his hotel room and down the hallway. His knee had started throbbing, injured earlier in the week as he trained with Crucifix. A bucket of ice would do the trick. But as he walked down the hallway, he realized two things immediately: he didn't have the ice bucket, and he didn't have the swipe card for his door lock. Cross: Sweet merciful crap. He walked back to his room, knocking on the door across from it. Crucifix wouldn't be sleeping, David thought to himself. The sicko never slept. Lucky bastard. Cross: C'mon pokey, open up. I'm catching cold out here. The door was unlocked, and opened slightly. A small man, about Cross' size but many years older, scratched his stubbly chin with a scarred hand. Cross: Did I wake you, sunshine? The man quickly pulled on the mask of the Hardcore Luchadore. Crucifix: Damn it man. People could see me. Get in here! David stepped into the room, Crucifix quickly closing the door behind him. Crucifix: You never know who's skulking around in the hallways. Cross: Don't worry. There's nobody out there except me and my boxers. Crucifix: Are those bunnies on your briefs? Cross: They're evil bunnies. Crucifix: Yeah, the bloody carrot in the paw is a dead giveaway. Cross: That's not a carrot, that's a knife. Now let's drop it. David looked for a clean place to sit down. The bed was covered with clothes, the table and chairs filled with assorted fast food containers. He nudged a beer case, the empty beer bottles clanked together. Cross: Hey, you're supposed to be a Hardcore Luchadore, not Hardboiled Lump-a-dough. Crucifix: I'm watching my calories. You know, it's a good thing you came by. I think we need to re-think our strategy going into Cyberslam. That loss to Burns took a bit of the momentum away, so I was thinking maybe I should - Cross: Wait. Stop. When I first thought of this idea, I figured I was helping you the way... the way an old friend helped me. I'd give you the same chance that he gave me. But now I think that I did it just to shake some of this guilt I've got. Crucifix: Hey, I don't care why you did it. I just know that I'm doing it, and doing it well. Heck, I'm going to Cyberslam! Cross: That's what I wanted to talk to you about. I think that I need to take it from here. Crucifix: I don't think I follow. Cross: I meant that I'll be going on... as Crucifix. Crucifix: Whoa woah woah! Hold on there! I don't know what you've been smoking, but I think you should give it back
