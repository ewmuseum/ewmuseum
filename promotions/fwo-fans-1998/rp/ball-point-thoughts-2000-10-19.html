Ball Point Thoughts

19 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He sat in a computer chair, staring out his apartment window. Below the city of Detroit was moving along slowly as it usually did in the middle of the night. A few people or cars were seen passing through the dim streetlights and back into the darkness. The happenings from the last month had been wearing thin on him, he wasn't sleeping or eating very much. His body felt old and weathered, like it would collapse into a heap at any second. His eyes were heavy from the lack of sleep, his hair was unkempt, and had a greasy look and feel to it. One could tell what a wreck he was from the frown that was basically fixated onto his face. In his hands was a small composition notebook and a pen. The notebook was open to the middle of it, the page he was on was blank. He moved the pen around the paper a bit, making words with air. The notebook was propped up onto his knees, his feet were on the windowsill. He moved his left hand onto his chin, deep in thought as his other hand began to write. Journal, 10.17.01, 1:46A.M It's been a while since I last wrote about anything that's happened in my life, but that's because nothing eventful has really happened. Though in the past month or so things have begun to change drastically and I don't think it's a very good thing. Lately I've been getting notes, and "gifts" backstage at Slaughter every Sunday Night. I know who it is, but I can't let anyone else know. I'm so fucking ashamed of myself. I can't even look at myself straight in the mirror. Monte Burns is tearing me down, bit by bit, and he's not going to stop until there's nothing left. At first I thought this would just be about the title he wore. And how much I hate him because of his old money values. He's lucky to be where he is right now, he never had to earn one penny. While I'm here scrounging for a living, most of my paycheck going back into medical bills and travel expenses. I'm letting everything get to me... and he's found the weakest part of my life. He's destructing me, because of this. I just want everything to stop... I want to be at peace with myself... I want to be left alone... d.w. After reading over what he had written, he tossed the pen onto the crude desk next to him. A sigh escaped from his mouth as he stood up from the desk. Again he watched out the window, trash floated by, fifteen stories below him, he knew that he had nothing left to live for. But he had no reason to die. He was in a world of limbo where black roses and expensive cheeses were a symbol of the future. And more specifically, a symbol of his ultimate undoing.
