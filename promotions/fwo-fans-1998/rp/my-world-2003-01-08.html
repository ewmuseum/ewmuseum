My World

8 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I sit alone in the dark, letting the shadows cover me like a blanket. For a moment, I allow myself to sense everything around me: the cool wooden floor beneath me, a light breeze blowing over my bare legs. There's a faint scent of rust in the air, probably from the old iron pipes running overhead like a den of metallic snakes. The stone wall behind me radiates an aura of chilled air, almost pressing against the heat my own body generates. I have no idea how long I've been here. A day is an hour here; an hour a day. When I'm down here, I never seem to feel hunger or thirst. My body takes care of it's own needs like a self-sufficient machine. This room is my solitude, and I stay here as long as I need to. An hour, two hours, a day or a week... none of it matters to me here. It's simple to focus where there's no outside distractions to take your mind off business. In the darkness is honesty. In the darkness is truth. Here, you can't be something you're not. Falsities are stripped away in the cold, in the dark. You are peeled back like the layers of an onion, getting closer and closer to the truth. That's why I want to come here. That's why I have to come here. To get in touch with the truth within. The truth isn't always pretty. It's not always pleasant. Sometimes, the truth can be downright horrible. Sitting here for as long as I have, I've been able to tap into the inner recesses of my being. My mind's eye has looked into my icy crypt of a soul, prying the gates open and unleashing the demons that lay within. My life has been a paradox: chaotically calm, peacefully violent. It's hard to remember what my life was like before. It doesn't even seem like my life when I look back at it. It was like someone else went through the motions while I stood outside myself and watched. Whoever that man was, once upon a time, is long gone. Sometimes I think that wherever he is, he's better off as a memory. He'd never survive in my world. My world is suffering. My world is pain. My world is death. But then I step through the doorway into the light, into the other world. Things are simple there. Pleasure and pain aren't synonymous. There is light, warmth, and beauty there. It's a place I like to visit on occasion, but I know I don't belong there. Not anymore. My world is suffering. My world is pain. My world is death. My base surface emotions are like dead skin; unappealing but easily brushed away. It's sometimes necessary to let other think that I care, I feel. My world is a harsh landscape of desolation and destruction. A cold, bleak expanse with no beginning and no end. It just is. There's no happiness or sadness. No anger, resentment, or envy. To an outsider, it seems like place of apathy. They can think what they want. They can't possibly wrap their minds around the truth of it. If they tried, they'd end up poisoning their own view of reality. Right and wrong would become twisted and horribly contorted. They'd never survive there because they're just a tourist in the realm. Me? I was born into it. I exist. I've been seen as a savior by some, a monster by others. If the man from my old life could see me now, he'd probably be horrified. He'd run back into the light, screaming in terror at the road I've traveled. I turn my mind's eye back out into the world... their world. It's time to go back. Business has to be attended to. But even as I stand up and prepare to walk into the light, I know that the darkness won't be far behind me. Inside me. Waiting.
