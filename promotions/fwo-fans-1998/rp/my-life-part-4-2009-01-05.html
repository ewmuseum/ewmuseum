My Life - Part 4

5 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From the moment the door shut behind me, the roars of a blood-thirsty army filled my ears. With every step I took deeper into the dark and damp urban hell, the growls and howls of demonic spectators demanding violence grew louder and with more ferocity. The hallway was dark and bleak, with an aroma of blood and sweat. The walls of the staircase were barren of human care, full of cracks and crumbling cement. I kicked past a few neglected and empty beer bottles as I descended further into the fiery depths of this homemade metropolitan Serengeti. Down here, there were no human beings, just animals. Human beings could control emotions. Animals either never had the ability or chose to neglect it. Confined in the earth’s crust, in this basement of brutality, was raw human animalistic emotion. It was human at its purest; the way God intended us to be. Thoughts of remorse, control and pity were cast aside for the favorable thoughts of anger, desire, and pain. It was Eden. I reached the bottom of the steps and scanned the environment. A sea of ravenous animals barked insanely within their manmade fight circle, throwing fists and money into the air and exchanging hand gestures and kind pleasantries such as, “Fuck yeah!” and “Tear his fucking head off!” As I observed the spineless spectators, who cheer for violence but dare not to get involved, a sudden gust of wind flew past my side. I turned slightly towards its source, and saw a bloodied and bludgeoned body get thrown into a nearby concrete pillar. The force was so extreme that bits of concrete cracked off and rolled to my feet. I slowly pulled brought a piece up to my face and observed it for a moment. It was rock hard; the force broke the rock hard element. Although the large tattooed brawler was soon to follow after his prey, he didn’t need to. I already knew that only he could deliver such a force. Amongst a sea of plebeians, the king of this underground fighting league sent deafening blow after deafening blow into his already brutalized opponent. Even while his prey remained motionless, sharp elbows came careening into his face. Each time a strike was retracted another quickly followed; and small pool of blood that rested at his prey’s head grew larger and larger until small rivers ran to the foot of my own boot. I dabbed my finger on the small trickle of blood and held it to my nostril. I closed my eyes for a moment to fully capture the scent of raw, remorseless pain. I deafened my ears the best I could and closed my mouth to avoid tasting the saltiness of cowardice that surrounded me; I wanted nothing to distract me from this moment of reunion. It was a scent I hadn’t smelled for years, but it was a scent that never became foreign to me. I recognized it immediately and embraced its warmth once again. I yearned for more than just its smell; I wanted the rest of my senses to engorge in this reunion. I opened my mouth slightly and began to bring the blood-soaked finger tip towards my lips, but the reunion was stopped by the sound of a life-threatening *crack*. The cracking sound echoed amongst the silent faced spectators. I peered over their shoulders to see the tattooed warrior pulling back on his prey’s arm, so much so that his elbow joint pointed in the opposite direction. While others stood in fright, mouths agape, I found myself grinning in excitement. Clinton Coldren cursed and screamed for medical attention, all the while scorning this mysterious bulky figure. I stared at the victor for a moment and observed his every feature, but the one feature that I found most compelling was his eyes. Though full of fire and rage, they were somewhat cold; numb to the sad emotions God bestowed on us all. He, unlike all the others, had no remorse for his actions. He, unlike all the others, had no compassion for his enemies. He, unlike all others, had only one mission in this hellacious pit of viciousness: Self Gratification. He wanted to live, and the only way for him to do that, was to bring others to the brink of death. My smile grew wider at my recent epiphany. In a way, he was just as sick as me. “No one can beat me, and when I am here doing this, I am a killer,” he shouted triumphantly into the crowd of fear-stricken observers. “You are all bringing me fuckin’ weak people, who cannot even sustain a punch to the face. Who the fuck do you think I am?” His words stung any of those who cowardly lowered their heads. “Bring me a worthy man; a man who will push me to my limit, a man who will push me to the brink of death.” I smiled wickedly. That feeling was returning. My blood began to race with adrenaline again. My palms started to sweat as I clenched my fists eagerly. “This is hand to hand combat, not some rinky-dink backyard school fight,” he shouted as he stuck his chest out proudly and circled in his place. “Until you do that…my one hundredth fight, for all of you pieces of trash, is on hold.” "That's right, keep your silence; it'll do your health wonders." He looks out into the sea of silent faces, his arms crossed over his chest. He curls a lip of disgust at the sea of lowered faces, and after waiting a few more seconds, spits angrily towards them all. As the spectators all cower in fear, I cannot help but feel a slight tickle in my throat. I find myself shielding my amusement by lowering my own head, as short chuckles begin breaking free. The ‘king’ turns his back to his plebeians and snatches his coat off the ground. As I hear his foot steps growing farther from the arena, my amusement can no longer be hidden. The gutless viewers simultaneously turn their heads as laughter escapes me. The excitement within my body mixed with the idiotic threats of an overly-masculine meathead draws hilarity within myself. I cannot help my laughter and even begin to arrogantly applaud the man for his act. He snaps around quickly with a scowl on his face. His eyes beam angrily towards the crowd and he shouts. "Cute,” he says arrogantly, searching for the culprit as he tosses his jacket aside, “Why don't you bring that school girl laughter up here?! And I'll see if I can help you become a man. I have plenty of juice left in the tank." His eyes scourer the audience briefly, but it is I who reveal myself to the champion. "You're super-masculine act is quite amusing,” I say with a wicked smile on my face. “It's like watching a brainless animal inside of a cage." I peer up through my long locks of hair and witness as the audience split in half, as if giving me an entrance way towards the bald-headed beast that stares a hole through me. I chuckle amusingly for a moment at their acceptance of a new challenger and lean arrogantly against the wall. I could see that every movement I made was angering the man further. So I decided to have a little more fun and proceeded to anger the heartless beast further by adding a bit of my own arrogance. "Hey!” I shouted towards the wide-eyed audience. “Does anybody have a banana I can feed this dumb ape?" Our eyes meet for a moment, across the hall of silent faces. I could tell that my arrogant smile sent jolts of rage through his body, by the way portions of his shoulders, chest and arms tensed. The veins of his neck even protruded a little, but he quickly composed himself. He recognized the mental game of chess I tried to play, and decided to kill my Bishop. "You've got to be fuckin' kiddin' me. You?” he asked arrogantly with a bit of swagger in his step. “You want to test your luck? Are you sure you're not seeing double right now? I don't have time to beat up the resident bar drunk. I'll do you a favor pal.” The mindless oaf reaches into his pocket and draws a wad of bills from it. He waves it playfully in front of him and proceeds to lob the stack at the base of my manmade entrance ramp. “Next round is on me,” he says with a sour grin. He played a good game of chess, but I was the champion at it. I slowly pushed off the wall and made slow and calculated steps towards the battlefield. Through the corners of my eyes, I could see that my entrance ramp was being closed off by the sea of onlookers. Though they all remained silent, their body language was universal; they wanted to see this battle of gods and they were silently struggling for a front row seat. The silence was remarkable; I could hear every single sound within the confines of hell. I could hear the groans of his last victim. I could hear the deep breathes of each astonished crowd member. I could hear the little rat squeaking fearfully in the corner of this tension-filled moment. And I could hear the stretching of this champion’s wicked grin as he cracked his knuckles emphatically. “I’m not here for money,” I said as I stopped at the base of the entrance ramp and planted both feet on the fallen bills, ripping a few in the process. “I’m here to fight.” I tore off my trench coat and launched it into the nervous audience. They quickly scattered in fear for a moment, but one of them neatly placed my coat on a nearby chair…how kind. I turned my attention back over to my new opponent and could see the sense of astonishment on his face. While it was only brief, I could see that my appearance surprised him. I could also hear the mumbles and whispers of a flabbergasted crowd. I admit that my body, upon first glimpse, is quite shocking; riddled in muscle and scars, battle wounds from years and years of physical torture. I broke the silence quickly, as I grew impatient of this war of words. "So unless you're getting cold feet, I suggest we stop with this nonsensical battle of words, and let our fists do the talking. What do you say?" He smirked at me and nodded. "So, you figured out how to lift some weights. Good for you. But you know what? I couldn't agree more. Talking is for losers, which is all you've managed to do so far,” he leaned back and readied himself into a fighting position. His fists clutched before his face and his knees slightly bent. He spoke roughly through his raw knuckles with a sense of eagerness, “Someone say ding.” I clenched my fists tightly while I stood perfectly still. My eyes grew wide with exhilaration and my grin stretched across my face. As the crowd tightly coiled into a circle around us, I signaled for its start with a single word. “Ding.”
