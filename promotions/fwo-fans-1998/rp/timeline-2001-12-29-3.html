12/28/2001 11:00PM Where could he be? Two draft beers and a plate of nachos had gone by (or about a half-hour if you were keeping standard time), and still no sign of him. The Strangler was confident that he would arrive, the question was when. He didn't even want to be here in the first place, much less have to spend the entire evening waiting... After a brief walk around the bar, checking to make sure he hadn't missed him coming in, the Strangler returned to his seat and signaled the waitress for another drink. He then glanced at the clock behind the bar, still somewhat visible through the choking haze of cigarette smoke. It said 11:15. At that moment, a loud noise from the adjoining room caught his attention. The Strangler stepped over into the doorway, and saw a band performing on the stage. "The Deadbeat Dads" read the name on the banner... yet the lead singer looks somewhat familiar. Of course. It was Joseph, the kid who had brought him his food back at the hotel. The Strangler recalled him saying something about having a show tonight. What a coincidence that he'd end up at the same place. Glancing around the room, he caught sight of Darryl standing near the stage, just off to the right. Another coincidence? Probably. Darryl caught sight of the Strangler, and waved him over, tapping a friend next to him to point out the star in their midst. Maybe it wouldn't be a good idea to head that way... he might bring attention to himself, which was the last thing he was looking for right now. A little bit of anonymity was hard to come by nowadays, and he didn't want to waste what little he had. Then again, when was the last time anyone had bothered to extend a friendly gesture towards him? Certainly not any of the other wrestlers, who had treated him with as little respect as they could, and even tried to take advantage of his situation. Sure, they seemed nice enough to people on TV, and people cheered for them... but some of them were real scumbags under it all. Nonetheless, a little company might help pass the time until the person he was meeting decided to arrive... What the heck. The Strangler decided to take a chance and start up a conversation. The kid had seemed like a decent guy. But just as he was about to head over towards where Darryl and his friends were gathered, he felt a much heavier hand on his shoulder, tapping slowly. "Hello, Paul. Sorry I'm late. Business, you know..." It was him. From across the room, Darryl stopped in mid-conversation, noting that the Strangler had turned to speak to a person in a long coat and dark hat. The two men turned back towards the main bar area, and headed out of sight. But there was something about the stranger that left Darryl a bit uncomfortable. Something familiar and dangerous...
