Chomping at the Bit

22 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wippit sat at a seaside cafe in Key West, an un-touched beer sitting on the table as he looked out over the water. The sun was just setting, painting the sky with reds and oranges, and he watched the fiery orb sink into the water as he contemplated his career in the fWo. Or lack thereof. Oh sure, he had his moments, the top of the list being the Tag Team champ with Ray Chavez, but every smark out there said he didn't deserve it, and perhaps that was true. But beyond that fact, what did he have? Unfinished storylines as workers got injured or quit. Ideas which seemed good at the time, but fell through after even the most casual scrutiny. Other ideas which just didn't happen. "Man, I feel like Spike Dudley." he mused to himself. And it fit. Here he was, in what a lot of people called the best fed on the planet (and a lot of others attacked because of that opinion), and he was going nowhere, doing nothing. His return? J.O.B. time. Just the 'little guy' to kick around, make the other guy in the ring look big, and then the three count. He wanted more. So much more. But backstage was hectic. This whole Disney thing, which seemed to hurt as much as help. Cyberslam coming up, so of course everyone was trying to give it their all. And all these whispers and rumors going around... the deaths, the conspiracies. And the self-styled 'Clown Prince of the Ring' felt left out, overlooked... just wrap him in a red shirt and throw him into a episode of Star Trek. The sacrificial lamb sent to the Slaughter... even though there was no Slaughter anymore.... He needed to do something. Anything.
