Erik & Pamela (The Contest)

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*It was a humid day in Brighton, Massachusetts. The date was July 19, 1993. A young man who was unusually tall for his age pulled up in front of a building in a worn down gold Buick Skylark. There was more rust on the car than there was paint. He got out of his vehicle and stretched. He looked to be in his very early 20s, appeared to be near seven feet tall, and seemed like he was about 280 lbs. He wore short blond hair and had steelish blue colored eyes. His clean shaven face showed hardened features, as he scratched at his chin for a moment. He looked up at a sign on the building. It read "WAAF/107.3". He smiled and walked toward the building. The building seemed rather sterile, nothing too out of the ordinary. He saw a sign that pointed up a flight of stairs, and proceeded to follow it. When he got to the top, he entered a small foyer and approached a desk where a young woman sat behind a desk.* "Hi, can I help you?" spoke the young lady. "Hi, my name Erik Kelly. I was the eighth caller earlier today on the Hillman Morning Show, and won front row tickets to go see the WWF tonight." *The woman nods, running a hand through her brown hair, and picked up the phone. She dialed the production representative, and then escorted Erik over to a room just down the hall. Erik entered into the room, shook hands with a man of slight build and in his late 20s. He handed Erik a group of papers for various waivers. Since he was in the front row, if he got injured, he couldn't sue the radio station. He also had to fill out a form stating that he would not be eligible to win for another 3 months. Paperwork was so stupid. The production representative handed him the tickets. 2 first row seats to the Boston Garden. This was gonna be the best thing since Paula Abdul, he thought. Now all he needed to do was make sure his buddy Brian was able to make it. He had to get back to Northeastern as soon as possible and find him before he made plans with the guys to hit the frat houses for keg stands. Erik walked down the steps, pleased with how the day was going. It had been a rough week for him already. Mary, the girl he'd been dating for 4 months now, dumped him because she needed to be with others before she got serious with anyone. Fuck her, he decided. Stupid bitch didn't realize what she was letting go. He was going to do something extraordinarily big in his life. He didn't have the slightest idea what just yet...but once he knew, he was going to be the best at it. It's the only way he knew. The perfectionist's disease. Either way, it was her loss. Good riddance. Erik got back into his car, looking to head over to Northeastern in time to find his friend and have a night out on the town, courtesy of the tickets he won.*
