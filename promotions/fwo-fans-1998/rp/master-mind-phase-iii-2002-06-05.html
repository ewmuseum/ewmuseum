Master-MIND, Phase III

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The same dark room. The time for choosing is NOW, Fallen One. The one who hunts you draws ever closer, intent on drawing you back into your former life of agony and pain. I have sworn to keep you safe here - and I put my very life in jeopardy day after day, night after night - but I fear I am growing weak. While your adversary is nearing his ultimate goal. "He will not stop me..." The time for choosing is NOW, Fallen One. I am sworn to protect you; I gave you my word that I would keep you from harm. But now your enemy approaches, drawing ever nearer and I am near powerless to stop him. "Maybe he can stop you. But he will not stop me." Fallen One, hear me. I know this is difficult for you - that you do not wish to hear these words, but cast your memories back unto your former self. Remember your past life, the pain and anguish and agony that were your only true companions. Free yourself of your preconceived prejudice and think of pain in a completely different way. "I am almost free." Take all your broken dreams, your shattered hopes, the tattered memories of your pitiful previous life, and embrace them. Grasp them to you, deep in the depths of your tortured being, and let the pain wash over your soul. Open your mind and heart and soul to the pain of your past and accept it. Let the anguish and agony fill you, lifting you up beyond your mortal limitations and giving you new strength, new purpose, new life. The flames of your failures will cleanse the impurities of your essence, and grant you clarity. Let the pain flow through your veins and skin and muscles, let the agony fester and grow every fiber of your body. "I will learn to love them..." Pain is the only truth in life, my friend. Even my fragile body may succumb to fate's cruel joke, but pain last forever. Open your arms wide; stop running from you pain, and open wide your arms to it. Pain will sustain you always, and never leave your side. Take your Pain and make it your own. Embrace it, acknowledge it, accept it, and use it against your enemies. See the truth of my message, and choose your path. "The path is the end..." Despite my best efforts, I can only hide you for so long. "I know," he whispered softly.
