The Untold Story(Part 8)

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Orlando, Florida. Sean's apartment. Rain. The day couldn't have ended any more fitting. In his career, Sean had been through it all. Wars in the ring that had him pysically burnt out. Injuries that kept him out of the ring for months that had mentally tore him to pieces. But, nothing... nothing... could have properly prepared him for what he faced earlier today. Sean closed the freezer, placing the opened hotdog package back on the inside... but, not before taking four of them out, for him to eat. He placed them on a pan, and put them in the oven, turning the nob to 375 degrees. Sure it was a little high for a hotdog... but, he was hungry. Sue him. From there, he walked lazily to his sofa, leaped on the longest chair, and began flicking channels. It had been a long, exhausting day. And, the fact that he hadn't been sitting in front of the television for more than three minutes before he began to doze off told the tale. Bang. Bang. Bang. Am I dreaming, he thought. But, soon after the banging continued. And, continued. Until two things became abundantly clear... One, it wasn't a dream. Two, whoEVER it was... wasn't leaving until somebody answered the door. Which is what Sean did. Maybe if he was thinking... and, looked through his peep hole he could've prevented what happened next... but, he was tired. Hardly thinking of any psycho killer knocking on his door eleven o'clock at night. What ended up being there... probably was worse. Stevens - "... Linda? What are you doing here?" Sean was shocked. Maybe that's why he backpedaled allowing her in. Linda had tears in her eyes. Similar to the ones she had earlier. Sean knew instantly... that this would be a LOT harder than he imagined. "You can't be here, Linda. What if--" Johns - "Sean I need you. I need more than I've ever needed anybody in my life. I know you're confused... I am, too. But, I know you didn't mean what you said earlier. I know you still love me, and I know YOU know you still love me." Stevens - "Linda... look... I'm sorry. I really am. But, I don--" Linda was dressed in a long khaki-colored trench coat. But, underneath... she was bare. Sean looked... because... he's a man. And, men look. But, his heart... wasn't there. Sure it was beating... but, it wasn't there. His alarm system was off and sounding. He took a couple of steps back... but, his eyes gave Linda the wrong impression. As she began to move in. ****** The plane ride wasn't half as bad as Ivy had expected. For he most part... she slept the majority of the way. The other time was spent figuring what she'd say to Sean, and how they'd fix their relationship. Ivy giggled to herself. What fixing? She thought. She trusted Sean. And, if Sean told her everything was cool, and that Linda Johns was nobody... she believed him. She sure would want him to trust her if Hornet's name ever came up. As tough as THAT would be - if not TOUGHER than their current situation simply because their relationship was FAR more public. Ivy signalled for a cab. Sure she could've called Sean. But, it was raining... and, it was starting to pour. What kind of girlfriend would she be to bring him out of his warm bed into the freezing rain. "A good one," she mumbled to no one in particular. A bright yellow cab pulled over. Ivy immediately hopped in, pulling down her umbrella. Cab Driver - "Where to, ma'am?" Poison Ivy - "Highlander Court. Off of Murkirk Road." Cab Driver - "Oh that's an easy one. Right around the corner in Zone A. That'll be five bucks..." Ivy digs into her pocketbook and pulls out a five dollar bill. She hands it to the cab driver and sits back... staring out into the rain. What a beautiful site. ****** Stevens - "What are you doing? We can't do this..." Johns - "Yeah, Sean... we can. We have the apartment to ourselves... Ivy doesn't have to know. And, after tonight... if you can still say you don't Love Me... I'll leave... and, never come into your life again. How's that?" Stevens - "No! I'm not doing this. All my life I've made the wrong choice. Now... I've finally got something I want... something I've ALWAYS wanted... and, you're trying to destroy that." Johns moved in even closer. "Linda if you loved me like you say... you wouldn't want to do that to me." Linda paused for a moment. Pandering her next move... thinking over the exchange she and Sean just had. She was still teary- eyed - albeit, a little more determined - but, nevertheless she turned to face him anyway. Johns - "Sean, I DO love you. You gotta believe me... I wouldn't have gone through all that I'm going through if I didn't. You don't think this is embarrassing? It is. But, I want to show you. What you're turning your back on. Please, Sean... just give me a chance..." Johns extended her hand... touching Stevens ever-so gently behind his neck. "Just give me a chance." She leaned in. And, her lips touched his. But, it wasn't passionate... atleast not from Sean's perspective. In fact... it was just... wrong. And, he immediately let her know it by pulling back. That gesture alone did two things... it made Sean see her in a different light. As somebody he couldn't even be friends with. It snapped Linda out of it. She began crying furiously... but, even then... Sean was reluctant to hold her. Or give her any comfort. Because he knew that even though the kiss was nothing to him... if somebody saw it... they'd get the wrong idea, and that was the last thing he needed. Sean sat there blankly as Linda balled like a baby. Something he too felt like doing as he looked up at his apartment door. ****** Cab Driver - "We're here, ma'am." Ivy couldn't believe how fast the driver got her to her destination. She hardly had time to think... let alone enjoy the silence of the trip. But, because he was friendly... and, got her there safe... and, quick... she tipped him an extra five bucks. Cab Driver - "Why thank ya, ma'am!" Poison Ivy - "You're more than welcome." Ivy opened her umbrella and walked into the building... Sean was on the second floor so there was no need for an elevator. Ivy walked up the steps, and into the hall. Strange thing, though... The closer she got to Sean's apartment... the more his door looked open. Her initial reaction was panic. Maybe something had gone wrong... or somebody was trying to rob him. Ivy had a lot of spunk. Something you HAD to have to be in her profession. She had decided within herself, that if someone WAS in her boyfriend's apartment... she wasn't gonna turn her back on him. And, she didn't. She walked to the apartment door with purpose. Until she was right up on it. She heard voices. Who could it be, she wondered. Ivy quietly crept up to the door, peaking her head in the door. Something she almost wished she didn't do. She gasped as the unthinkable happened.
