Finality, The Ending Of

14 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The long trek up the stone steps was not easy, nor was it enjoyable, but nonetheless, Rei had successfully completed it. As she placed her right foot down onto the top of the landing and wiped some sweat off of her brow, she took a deep inhaling of the warm night air. "That was quite the climb." Rei muttered to herself as she placed her other foot on top of the landing, standing straight up. She turned around and looked down at the distance she had climbed to reach the temple, and smiled a bit, seemingly happy with herself. She turned back towards the entrance to the temple, and bit down on her lip, suddenly a bit more nervous. "A place doesn't get the name 'Temple of Eternity' just by being a really neat tourist spot." Rei said to no one in particular. "Oh well. Can't hurt just to see what's inside." After a brief moment of hesitation, Rei walked towards the entrance. It was seemingly just a stone roof and walls built on top of a large plateau- so below her was nothing but stone. She walked into the door-sized opening, surrounded on each side by torches, in the left wall, and into the temple. Inside, she found nothing more than a single room, with inscriptions on the walls and a single, old chest in the center. "Not what I was expecting." Rei muttered. Rei walked towards the old chest in the center of the room, wondering if she'd find inside of it what she expected to find inside. She walked with easy, slow, and soft steps- she wasn't entirely sure why, but she had the feeling that she was being watched. When she finally reached the center and the chest, she leaned over to open it. "What, no traps?" Rei asked with a laugh. "Damned Tomb Raider." She inspected the chest to realize that it was unlocked, and with all of her strength, began to lift it up. After a few moments, she finally opened it up, and inside of it was.....a white mask. Rei gasped in shock. "It's still here!" she exclaimed as she took it out of the chest and wiped the dust from it. "The Mask of Eternity." Rei stood up and walked out of the room, holding the mask in her right hand, then used the light of the torches to inspect the mask. It was plain white, yes, except for two symbols on each side of the forehead. On the left was a small black arrowhead. On the right, an even smaller black heart. "It's the real deal." Rei said, nodding her head. With nothing else left to do, Rei placed the mask on. It covered her eyes, nose, and forehead, but left her mouth exposed. Rei smiled. Now, she had a way to fight Cara. She would not fight her as Rei. She would fight her.....as Eternity. The Finality was complete. at least under this bright moonshine let us sleep silently Existence is Eternity
