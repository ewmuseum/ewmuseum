A Road Paved In Broken Glass: Part Six

3 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The feelings that I had for her had to be buried deep within. I was lost without her, with only the hope of living my dreams to keep me going. Was it a mistake? Did I lose the one girl I truly loved for a stupid gamble? I'd really begun to wonder." February 4th, 2001. "Sorry, we aren't accepting applications at the moment." We don't want you… "We don't feel you would blend well with the rest of our athletes." We don't need you… "Unfortunately you don't have the ring experience we’re looking for." We don't know you… "Our apologies, we found a man for the job earlier this morning." We don't like you… Milo had heard it all, and no matter what he seemed to do, he could never get that big break he was looking for. He had been to almost every famous promotion, and every rising promotion he knew of, never to get further then the receptionist desk. "How do you get experience, if you won't let me wrestle?" Milo said, rolling his eyes at the receptionist. "One second" the receptionist said, snapping her chewing gum loudly. "I'll check the database for that question." Milo couldn't take it anymore; he had been sitting in a waiting room for over four hours, and he was only getting answers from a computer database. "Where is the owner at?" he asked in a very angry tone. "I want to speak with him at once." "He is a very busy man you know. Next Please!" "I'm not done yet! I want to talk to the owner right now!” Demanded Milo, slamming his fist upon the desk. "Would you like to speak to security?" The receptionist began pressing a red button that hung beneath her desk rapidly. "No!" Milo yelled. "Yes?" questioned the receptionist as Milo was escorted out of the building by two rather large security guards. Milo sat on the curb in front of the building, feeling empty, and lost. Salt-water began welling in his eyes, leaving a small wet mark on the concrete beneath him. "What the fuck is this for?" No matter how hard he tried to find the answers, he could never do it. Although discouraged, he would sit and watch fate play itself out. Good things come to those who wait." Thought Milo, and to his dismay that is what he found himself doing… yet again.
