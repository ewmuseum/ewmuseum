Reunion

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another night of fWo action has just concluded. The December 18th edition of Wednesday Warfare is officially in the books. The hours of the night have slowly progressed, with many competitors finding their ways home. Backstage in Charleston, The Magi made his way towards his locker room from the ringside area. He had been struggling, not only with his careless balance but also with the hallways themselves. Everything looked the same to him, and with his impaired sense of direction he could not seem to find his room. He held his right hand against the wall to steady himself and closely looked at each door for anything familiar. As he neared the end of another hall a door opened and out walked fellow Survivor II alum Martyr. The Magi squinted his eyes and looked closely. "Heeeeeeeey!" he said with a smile. "Man, it's you. How've ya been there buddy?" Martyr paused a moment and looked at The Magi. He was noticeably under the influence of something and barely seemed to know where he was. "If it isn't the one and only Magi. Nice to see you again." "Wow," The Magi remarked, "What have you been up to since, you know, Brand beat you?" Martyr smiled and shrugged off The Magi's unintentional insincerity. "I've been keeping myself busy, you know, working, training, looking after myself." Martyr paused. "And... you?" The Magi laughed, sending him off balance. Martyr helped him stand up straight and set him against the wall where the Magi leaned for the remainder of the conversation. "Man I've been around, you know, just doin' what I do." The magi laughed a bit under his breath. "Do you remember, that time when we were the Riders, and... wait, we were the Riders right? Yeah, that's right I think... and we were making fun of the other guys? Like we dressed up as them and stuff, and you were PHD? And you pretended to be like, that nerd dude? Remember? That was so funny." Martyr stared at The Magi for a moment before laughing along with him. "Oh yeah, those were good times. That was a fun event." "Yeah, good stuff. It would have been cool to win right? Like, it sucks that you lost." "Yes, well you know what they say, it was just a honor to be in it at all. And look, we're both here now, aren't we?" The two began to smile and laugh. "It's just a shame you're a drunk now. Can't be too good for the ring work I'll bet? That's a shame." The jovial conversation fell to an awkward halt with Martyrs comment. The Magi took a few moments to process his thoughts. His smile disappeared. "What did you say to me?" he asked. "I don't appreciate your disrespect, you know, man?" The Magi stepped up into Martyr's face. "If you got something to say to me, you better say it. I already scheduled one ass-beating tonight, you want me to make room for another?" Martyr stepped up in The Magi's face to combat his intimidation. He wiped the smell of cheap booze out of the air and put his hand on The Magi's shoulder. "You know what? I do have something to say to you Magi old pal... ...Good luck against Borst... It smells like you're going to need it." Martyr offered a wry smile and walked away, while The Magi, stumbling with Martyr's hand removed from his shoulder, watched him walk down the hall with a festering anger. Martyr made his way out of the building as The Magi continued his search.
