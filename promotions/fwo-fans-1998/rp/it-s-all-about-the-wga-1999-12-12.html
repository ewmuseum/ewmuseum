It's all about the WGA.

12 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:::We open up with an outside shot of an arena in Victoria, BC, Canada. After a moment, a long white limousine pulls up in front of the arena. It is exited by Scott Slugger and an fWo cameraman, and now we switch to the cameraman's POV (through camera, of course). Slugger walks towards the arena enough for the camera to get a great shot of him standing against the background of it, and looks at the cameraman.::: Make sure you get this right, cameraboy, because this is an important location in the very history of mankind. In fact, perhaps the most important location. Let the world know that this (very crappy, I might add) arena- :::Slugger gestures back to it.::: - will soon be the location where the World's Greatest Athlete becomes the first and only four-time fWo World Champion. So whenever you, Mr. Cameraguy, and whenever you, the faithful WGA fan watching this on television, walk by this crappy, worn-down, falling-apart arena, make sure that you stop and take a moment to stop in reverence for the place where *The Man* made history. I'm a bit cocky, you say? Damn straight. And I have reason to be. You see, despite what some internet idiots may think in their pathetic polls, the WGA is the best damn wrestler any of you will ever have the privilege to see in your life. Just reminding you of that. And hell, look at the group of pack rats- now where have I heard that phrase before?- that I'm facing. First, the current pretender to the World's Greatest Throne. Primetime. Son, you may call yourself Primetime, but that fact of the matter is that you are not ready for the prime time and you are not ready for the spotlight, fluke title victory aside. You've proven this by single-handedly attempting fWo ratings-cide, drawing house show crowds the size that WCW drew in 1991, and oh yeah, sucking. Face the facts, my man. You're not, and you never will be, worthy to wear my crown. Deal with it. :::Slugger takes a few steps back towards the limo, and begins to lean against it as he talks.::: Next, we have the Flying Frenchie. fWo, get ready for a rant the likes of which you've never seen, because I am sick and tired of the damn Flying Frenchie. This pathetic, skinny, noodle-armed sonuvabitch waltzes around the WGA's promotion like he owns the place, which couldn't be further from the truth. Every once in a while, he gives some whiny, nasal, holier-than-thou interview where he pretends like he has some type of talent at all- and that disgusts me. The Flying Frenchie doesn't have a damn shred of talent in his body, interview, ring work, whatever, and any educated fan of the fWo knows that. Everyone knows that *I*- I repeat, *I*!, am the measure of talent the rest of the fWo compares itself to, and can't nobody measure up to me. Especially not the Flying Frenchie, the ultimate in limp-wristed, insufferable, annoying nancy boys. This Sunday, I *take out* the Frenchie. Nobody, but freaking nobody, even *acts* ike they are the best this promotion has to offer when I'm around! Nobody. :::Slugger takes a moment to calm down, and begins to climb up onto the roof of the limo as he continues.::: Whew. That felt good. And the only opponent that leaves is Ice. Ice and I have been around the block in every sense of the word- we've been tag team champs together, we've fought for the World Title together and against each other, and we've fought for respect together, and against each other. This Sunday, Tony, you said it best- it's business, not personal. The World Title- what that nothing, that nobody, Primetime holds, is *the* *damn* *reason* I am in the fWo. It's all about the big gold, buddy, and I'm confident that when I walk out as World Champion, we'll still walk out as friends. But when we're in the ring on Sunday- watch your back. :::Slugger now begins to climb to the top of limo, and smirks arrogantly as he stands upon it.::: Because tonight, come hell, come high water, come armageddon itself, nothing- and I mean NOTHING- will stop me from coming out on top, and walking out of this pathetic, stinking, Candadian arena as *the* one, and, only World Champion. And that is a promise. Scratch that. *That*...is a guarantee. And hey, we all know how much those mean in wrestling, right? :::Slugger smirks again, before hopping off the top of the limo onto the pavement. He re-enters the limo, which drives off- leaving our intrepid cameraman behind, running behind the limo and yelling at it to slow down.:::
