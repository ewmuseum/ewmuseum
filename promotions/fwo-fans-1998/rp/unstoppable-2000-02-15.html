Unstoppable.

15 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Unstoppable. February 15, 2000 Sunday Slaughter was a good night for the French Foundation, most notably, Primetime and Courage. Primetime picked up one half of the tag titles, and the Frenchie granted him a shot at the World Title, while Courage added the tag title to his Cruiserweight Title. In a local gym, the fWo cameras caught up with the aforementioned tag champions, Courage, and Primetime. Primetime: What's that again, Mitch? Who carried who again, Mitch? Well, well, well. It sure looks like I found a suitable tag team partner. Someone who I don't have to carry on my back. Someone who actually does *something* in the ring, unlike you, Mitch. That man is Courage, and on Slaughter, it looks like I proved that *I* carried *you* on *my* back, Wilson. While you're busy hanging with the likes of Hiroshima, a true superstar if I've ever seen one, I became a 4-time, 4-time, 4-time tag team champion, and I believe that I am the only person to ever do so, right Chris? Courage: Of course, a record that won't be broken. Though unlike the last three times, this reign will be different. Mitch, you're an idiot, Courage has made the Foundation perfect, see for a long time the Foundation was a stable to be reckoned with.. though there was a weak link, a link that was so weak.. that the Foundation didn't rock. The Foundation didn't rock because of you Mitch, despite the good looks and skill of the Frenchie and Primetime, you degraded the Foundation enough to cause it to some-what suck. So after you left the fWo, and the ratings increased, Courage! baby, Courage! joined the Foundation.. and the Foundation finally equalled buy rates! ..rear ends in the seats.. and rock 'n roll, baby, yeah! See Brian it is a pleasure for Courage to hold the tag-titles with someone of your skill and calibre. Underground, you have no charisma, you suck. Mike Bear, I've never wrestled you, Why? Because you don't deserve to be in the same one mile radius as Courage. Wretch, I've constantly defeated you and this Wednesday will be no different, see Courage is the cruiserweight champion and Primetime is the former world champion, and together we're unstoppable. We rock, and we'll prove it Wednesday. Primetime: Guess what? That isn't all. Something a little more major happened. You see, the last time that Primetime and the Flying Frenchie were supposed to go one on one, it was savagely ruined. However, in the main event at Trapped 2000, the first main event that will actually garner an fWo buyrate over .5 will take place. Primetime. The Flying Frenchie. It doesn't get better than that. Well, maybe if Courage was involved, hey Chris? Courage: A triple-threat match would rock the arena, but as much as Courage appreciates the offer, Courage must live up to his duties as the greatest cruiserweight champion of all time, and one-half of the most rockin' tag-team champions that have ever graced the fWo. Don't mark me wrong, Courage would love a shot at whoever comes out the winner, though for now Courage is happy with his two titles. Primetime: Oh, and you Underground idiots, don't get your hopes up on regaining the Tag Titles on Warfare. Why? Because we equal ratings. We equal rock and roll. We equal the champions. Courage: C'mon, baby! The scene fades to black.
