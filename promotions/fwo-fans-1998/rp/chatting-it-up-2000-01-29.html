Chatting it up.

29 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Chatting it up. January 29, 2000 :::Geoff Green's de facto office, the fWo Van, is parked outside a 7-Eleven. Inside the van, Green reclines in the driver's seat, his arms tucked under his head. His breathing is a little unsteady, thanks to the broken nose, but his mood seems to be fine. The passenger door swings open, and Mike Heftel steps inside, a Slurpee in each hand.:::: Here you go, Geoff. :::Green reaches over and grabs one of the Slurpees from Mike.::: This is foolish, man. It's the middle of January, and you manage to talk me into Slurpees. How do you do it? I think it's pity, man. :::Geoff seems to think that one over, and he accepts it.::: So, you were telling me you have this great idea for a gimmick. Yeah, yeah, got it from Penthouse. Hey, hold up there, skippy! I thought I was talking to a Mr. Heftel, not Finn. Shaddup. It's Penthouse, not Midget Monthly. Besides, I just picked it up this once. Had some extra cash on me, and I haven't actually seen one in years. It's about what I expected, just a bit more hardcore, you know. Penetration and everything. But the stories are still great, if a bit unlikely. And there's a great article on the Mafia in it. I thought you already did a gangster gimmick... Yeah, Tommy Gunn. Havok keeps bringing up how he beat me as Tommy. Yeah, big whup. You tell him how he's the only guy you've ever pinned, Mike? Every chance I get. :::Both men chuckle, before Heftle continues his story.::: Anyway, there's this pictorial in there, girl/guy. Some sort of fantasy storyline. Great photography, good looking chick, and a guy that would make Havok and his weinerschnitzel blush. But there's this pic in there where the chick is pissing on the guy, and that's where I get this idea. :::Green nearly spits out his Slurpee, managing to only let a little slip down his chin before he wipes it off.::: Sick! You're letting Wretch get to you, Geoff. Yeah, one of the occupational hazards here. But that's twisted. Oh, I agree. But I see it in a faily mainstream porno mag, so I start to think, maybe that's something that a lot of people find erotic. Finally I get an idea. I could be The Urinator. What? Yeah, I could come out and piss on the fans. Hell, the industry's been doing it for years, why not just make it a bit more literal? Can you imagine the heat I'd get from that? I mean, most guys get booed because they're assholes, or they're trying to beat up the hero. But this would be unprecedented. How could you not boo somebody who might very well piss all over you? I don't know what our legal team would have to say about that. Hey, there's already the disclaimers on the back of the tickets. I ran it by them ealier today, hypothetically, natch, and they didn't really see a problem. I wouldn't take their word, Mike. We've got the same guys that represented Microsoft. :::Heftel shrugs.::: Either way, could you even show that sort of thing on broadcast television? Well not on Warfare, but on Fox, sure. It seems just a little....too out there, Mike. I'm not sure Finn would go for it right now. Maybe. Maybe not. But here's the upside: Since some people find that sort of thing to be erotic, my character would draw them into wrestling, increasing our fan base. I'd get over, and we'd make more money. It's win/win, Geoff. Wouldn't that threaten your heel status, though? Yeah, but eveyone's got to turn eventually. I make a better face anyway. I've got that "hapless but lovable" look. I don't know, Mike. Honestly, I don't like it. Isn't there something else you could do? I'm trying. I really am. But I haven't come up with anything lately, and they've been keeping me off TV, so what else am I going to do? I wouldn't be having this problem if Beaven had just run the Twilight match we asked for. You mean, that Cruiserweight 3-way with Havok, Universal and yourself, right? Right. Havok wanted to drop the belt to me. Chris was cool with it too. We had the whole scenario worked out. It would've beeen great. Instead they run some Millennium Man 3-way. You've got to admit that was a great match, Mike. Sure it was. I'm not denying that. But I'm getting old, and I don't want to become the next Oracle or Hiroshima. I need something, and I'm running out of ideas. How 'bout teaming up with somebody? I've thought about it. I've tried contacting Ed, thinking I'd make a good fit with his Gunmen, but I never hear back from him. Maybe there's something just a little less risque than The Urinator. Why not go back to one of your previous roles? MOTYC amused a lot of people, Mike. You think anybody would mind? Nah. You might want to revamp whichever one you use, try to flesh it out a bit, but I don't think it will be that big of a deal. Well, when you put it that way, digging up old gimmicks works for me. Good. Ah shit, I've got to get ready for an interview in half an hour. Want me to drop you off at the hotel? Fine by me. :::The engine revs up, as we cut to a commercial for Wednesday Warfare, only on NBC.::::
