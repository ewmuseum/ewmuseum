With a Splash...

8 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday night. It was quite possibly the last one Shirley would experience as a free woman. She sat at the end of the dock, right next to a beat-up old radio that cackled the words to "Big Yellow Taxi" by Counting Crows. "Don't it always seem to go That you don't know what you got 'til it's gone ..." She tightly embraced one of the poles that stood vertically at the side, and emitted a hot breath of smoke into the evening air. The vibrant orange sun was only beginning to fall in a picturesque sky. There wasn't a ripple in sight on the lake's surface as she then dipped her own bare toes into the crisp, cold water. This came to the delight of small fishes, who hesitantly fluttered to the surface and nibbled on her toes as if they were oversized kernels of corn. Survivor III had come and gone, with Shirley finding herself more happy with it's absence than anything else. Wrestling as a tag-team had sent the Savage marriage into even further disrepair than when they had began, and unfortunately, Shirley was entangled in another tough situation. Once a dream, Shirley found herself facing a career in the world's most reputable professional wrestling organization. *** She knew how fWo changed people; Jeremy was a prime example. Entering the tournament as a jerk, Jeremy Savage went to great lengths to claim 'complete ass hole' status as Fatal Attraction made their unfortunate exit. As the general wrestling term went, Jeremy was a 'backstage plague;' a rude, selfish, power-hungry brat that not only tried to take attention away from the other teams, but his own wife (and tag team partner). Perhaps that's why fWo wanted her and not him? Shirley shrugged as she tossed around the idea. Aside from the stress and pressures of wrestling, Jeremy was starting to come around as a person. His goodness was becoming apparent in tiny spurts, and Shirley was satisfied that he was at least making the attempt. Jeremy also happened to buy their beautiful cabin after that elimination. Shirley estimated the purchase was just an impulse buy; a reasonable method for coping after the unexpected drop-off from Survivor action. She didn't really mind. Shirley hoped Jeremy would 'cope' more often, in a recurring personal joke. With contracts signed, Shirley had more reason to worry than ever. The idea of being dragged along in a package deal would not fly with Jeremy at all; she just knew it. The possibility of a Jekyll and Hyde-type transformation was always there, but the ill effects would only intensify with that revelation. Perhaps she was too involved with the future to deal with the present, as was her habit. She figured that it was what probably led to Fatal Attraction's demise in the competition. Jeremy and Shirley thought they were always one step ahead, only to find out they were countless paces behind in the end. *** The sky became darker, and Shirley's eyes grew heavy as her body shivered into a relaxed state. Her head slowly bent and she peered into the water, where fish continued to observe her toes with careful precision. She twirled her toes slowly in a circular pattern as she gazed across the lake, although she couldn't see any further than twenty feet. The tranquility of her surroundings, coupled with the chilly water on her feet played a factor as she became subdued. Her eyes collapsed shut. They were closed for no more than a few moments before a loud thud erupted on the dock. Shirley became startled, but thought nothing of it as she shut her eyes again. The fish had stopped nibbling on her toes; a good cause for her to open her eyes once more. The fish had scurried off, as if they could sense some type of foreshadowing in the algae-afflicted water. The dock slowly swayed as someone approached her from behind. Reaching out with a sinister smirk plastered on his face, Jeremy tightly clasped Shirley's tan shoulder. An acute sense of surprise overcame her body, and her involuntary body spasm sent her fully-clothed body into the water with a tremendous splash. Her arms flailed wildly as she swam to the surface and choked for breath. She looked up and saw the silhouette of her husband, half-cloaked with the stale rays of a flood light. From what she could see (and hear), Jeremy was laughing hysterically. He wasted no time before diving in after her. "Did I scare you?" Jeremy asked rhetorically in his bass voice. Shirley shoved him firmly, but she couldn't help but produce a smirk. "No," was her response, which only heightened the comedic value of the moment. As Jeremy continued his howling, Shirley slowly turned around and reached for the ladder at the end of the dock. "Where are you going?" Jeremy asked innocently, curling his lips in a saddened frown. "Inside." "Ok." Jeremy said, surrendering already. "I just thought it was a good night for a swim," he chortled, but not as hysterically as before. Shirley turned around and saw Jeremy glaring at her warmly. "I was thinking...ya know, if you wanted, we could talk." "Who are you and what have you done to my husband?" Shirley asked playfully, reaching back and wrapping her arms around the dock's pole. She couldn't believe he was in the mood for conversation. Even with his good behavior, an uneasy feeling remained in her stomach. Jeremy's massive legs sloshed in the water as he approached her. "fWo?" she enquired; probably the most logical topic on both of their minds at the time. Jeremy shook his head in the negative. "Honey, we can talk about whatever you want." Jeremy said in a hushed tone, as he leaned over and kissed her tenderly on the mouth. Shirley couldn't help but emit a sentimental snort in mid-kiss. Her eyes quickly flooded with tears, which glimmered from the reflection of the moon. She always hoped for these sporadic and unprovoked displays of affection from him, but she had no idea how to react when she finally got her wish. She cried and sobbed, melting in the muscular arms of her husband. The two shared a tight embrace. Equally shared, both parties felt more together in that solitary moment than they had in the entire duration of their rocky marriage. "I don't know where I'd be without you, Shir'," Jeremy said softly, his voice beginning to crack with overwhelming emotion, "I just love you so, so much..." "How about we go inside to talk?" Shirley suggested with a resounding sniffle. Jeremy's face grew puzzled. "Why?" "Because I think we'll flood the lake if we keep this up," Shirley blubbered. Jeremy just laughed, and finally nodded in agreement.
