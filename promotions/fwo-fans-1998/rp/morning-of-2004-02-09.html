Morning Of

9 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday February 7th, 2004 Wedding time Nova Scotia, Canada Amy cringed as they poured into the house. Amid her dogs there were what seemed like an endless number of relatives - mostly female - here to get her ready. Her sisters, a few of her cousins, aunts, grandmother, mom...all so caught up that they hadn't noticed the 'blushing bride' - still clad in torn jeans, a studded belt and black tanktop - slip away into the frozen backyard. She cleared a spot of ice away from the clothesline stoop and perched on the bare wood, cell phone in hand. The most nervous night of her life had been spent away from Brandon. She'd gotten a solid hour of sleep maybe, and was up at 7am with jitters and a mother appearing twenty times as nervous flitting about. When she'd seen him at the kitchen table earlier in the morning (Prior to being shoo'ed away from the girly processions by Amy's grandmother), her father had seemed to have finally given in and decided that maybe this was the right thing. Whether he'd seen the glowing joy behind the nervousness in his daughter's expression that morning or realized that, over this past year, the only thing they'd fought about was her relationship was uncertain, but he'd really calmed and grown into liking the idea of Amy finally settling down. Looking back at it, he really couldn't see anything but good this had done...honestly, he'd always just been afraid of the man on the other side of things ending the relationship because of his sketchy family past and dealing the final blow to his little girl's heart. Brandon wasn't answering his phone. She'd felt sick all morning, be it nerves or the thirteen doubles at the bachelorette party. This was not helping. Was he getting cold feet? What was going on? She was halfway tempted to walk to the hotel to make sure things were okay, if it weren't for...well, time. After the second voicemail was sent, she had time to sneak back inside relatively unnoticed and put a coat on before stepping back out before her phone rang. Once again, the women inside were too busy fixing their own dresses and dreaming of Amy's 'makeover' for the day to really notice the lack of the girl herself. She grinned momentarily at her own corny nature when the wedding march MIDI rang out from her phone - that tipped her off to the caller. "BL?" Despite her nerves and not yet being sure where her husband-to-be currently was, Amy smiled - albeit biting her nail at the same time. "Where're you? Why didn't you answer? Is everything o--" Interruption, interruption much more welcome than static. "Amy... breath dear, breathe. I got the voicemail, I just overslept a bit so I'm trying to make up time. What's wrong? Did one of the caterers not show or something? Are you okay?" Amy lowered her head and sighed with relief. Despite being tired as all hell, she was still grinning. "I'm alright. Just a crowd around, everybody's going nuts with eyeshadow and hairspray and girliness and...gah. Just not my thing. I'm still avoiding getting into the dress..." "Not having second thoughts are you...?" Brandon chided. "No way would I have second thoughts, only about that dress...well, walking into a room of people in it. Not big on this formality stuff...and the craziness. I dunno. This is the most nervous day of my life, I needed to hear you. I missed you this morning...and last night...and," she paused, running a hand through her hair, "The kids are ready, right? Everything's in order? Your guys are okay?" Brand smirked, he wished Amy could have seen it. "Wait... I thought you had Sean and Jaord?!?" Amy screwed her mouth up halfway, but still smiled despite always finding Brandon's fallback to humor frustrating to a degree. "I'm serious. Does that mean things are okay?" "You'll be my wife in less than two hours, everything in the world is right for once." The sincerity was there, the feelings were there. Everything in Brandon's voice showed his absolute belief in what he had just said. "And tonight's all set? Away from everything, right?" Brandon took a second to respond, making sure the babysitters had been set and all the plans taken care of, "Yes ma'am. We have the next three days to ourselves. No worries." "Good." said Amy, "We'll need 'em...now let's go get married! I love you." "I worship you." Perfect timing, too. Amy'd been caught, and closed her phone quickly as her grandmother dragged her back inside... Nothing would go wrong today, she smiled. Nothing could go wrong today.
