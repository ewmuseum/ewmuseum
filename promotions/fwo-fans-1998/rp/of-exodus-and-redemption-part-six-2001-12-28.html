Of exodus and redemption (part six)

28 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
John Skyler withdrew the gun and pointed it at David Spencer. Spencer didn't seem surprised to see it. "I knew I would be seeing you sooner or later. A bit later than I suspected actually... but you were never the motivated type, were you John?" Spencer gestured John Skyler into his house and the two of them walked slowly inside. John held the gun on Spencer the entire time, his face impassive. David Spencer closed the door behind them and regarded John with momentary surprise, then a crafty expression crossed his face and he padded slowly down the hallway with John behind him. "You see, I had been following your progress. For a while there I thought you just might make it... but then that whole feud with Douglas... ah John, I thought I had taught you better than that. I really did." John held the gun constant. He listened to Spencer speak, but there was a loud ringing in his ears. For a moment John's resolve and his grip on the gun loosened, and Spencer leapt for it. But John was a step faster, and had brought the gun back up and dug it into Spencer's chest before he could do anything to break free from his newfound captivity. David Spencer laughed, and turned away from John once more. He picked up a bottle of wine and carefully poured himself a glass. "So this is what it's all about John? You've decided to come and kill me for my sins. Well, it isn't me you should be after. I didn't toss your little friend over the side of that ramp, John. You need to talk to William about that... except I hear he's dead, isn't he?" John blinked, and his mouth fell open slightly. Spencer's grin widened. "Oh... you didn't know. Well John, I'm afraid it is quite true. Quite true indeed." "How." It was a question in itself, and John held the gun more steady than ever. The ringing in his ears was growing louder... so loud that he could barely hear himself think, let alone hear what Spencer was saying. "Killed himself. He was depressed John... oh, so deeply depressed. I think... you understand that? Well, William was never the brightest bulb in the box... we both know that. But it seems he couldn't live with what he had done, accident or no and one day he climbed into a bath of warm water and opened his wrists up. Did you know it took the police almost fifteen days to find him? The people in the apartment below him complained that water was dripping down from their ceiling... can you imagine? To die in a little slum... nobody even caring that you're dead... well..." Spencer trailed off and glanced at John. John Skyler's hand was shaking badly. Spencer suspected that if he rushed him now he could get the gun out of his hand before he was shot, but better not to take the risk. There was some strange things going on here... one look at this man's face had told Spencer volumes that nobody else in the past two years had picked up on. There were too many unknowns to play this one quickly... Spencer would have to wait it out and look for an opening that was a sure thing. If it happened soon he wouldn't even do much more than take his gun away and send him packing. Spencer hated to get the police involved... not when his school was so close to opening and couldn't afford the bad publicity that would come from a stalker and a shootout. Spencer poured a second glass of wine and offered it to John. John didn't move to take it, and Spencer shrugged and placed the glass on a nearby table. "Your loss, John... or should I be calling you John? It occurs to me that perhaps you would rather be called something more... apt." "Fallen." John Skyler steadied his hand. The gun was pointed dead center at Spencer's chest and it held solid and true. "Call me Fallen." Spencer smiled, but the smile did not reach his eyes. "As you wish, Fallen... as you wish."
