The Letter.

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brandon, I have been thinking about things for so long now that it seems to be the only thing I think about. I know you know what I am talking about. It's hard to believe that it's been 18 years since we first met. We have been together for so long I really don't have a good memory of what it was like before. I know we were just kids but ever since we met we have been together. It seems like forever since I have been just "me". That may sound weird to you or maybe it doesn't and that is why you like to be on the road so much. I don't know. I know I have been feeling lost lately and I don't like it. It used to be that you and I were two halves of a whole, even before we were really "together". From friends to lovers to family it seems like our road has always went in the same direction. Until now that is. Now I see you going down a path I do not wish to take. That may sound selfish and if it does I am sorry for that, I have tried to change but it has not happened. I have been feeling like this pretty much ever since that bastard Creed kidnapped me. You were so hell bent on revenge that everything else took a back seat. It seemed like that was the only thing that mattered and your anger was always so close to the top. I was never afraid of you really, but there were times that I was close. You let someone like that monster fill you with hate. It clouded your judgment and kept you from seeing that you didn't need to fight for us, you just had to be there. That would have been enough. Revenge didn't help anything. It has hurt us more than he ever could. I have tried to be there for you and your self imposed quest. Through Troy Knight, Blackheart, Hagar, Lords and the rest I tried to be your support. I thought that you would notice and realize your family was always there no matter what. I was hoping that you would wake up. I shouldn't have hoped, I should have done something about it. Now, when I look in my heart I see that I waited too long. It's just not there. I don't fault your for your beliefs or the way in which you live them, I just wish we meant as much to you as they do. You keep saying that it is because of us that you do what you do, and I just can't get that to sit well with me. I would rather you be around all the time instead of trying to walk in your father's footprints. Those things don't matter to me. You always mattered to me. You and the kids was always the most important part. I've been thinking about this for a long time Brandon, I do love you. I just don't think that love is enough. I think that we are so far apart from each other there is no going back. I didn't want this, who does. I think for now though we should be apart and try to see who we are as individuals. I'm going home for a while. Sean and Jarod will be with me of course. Please come and see them, you and I might not be who we thought we were but you three are something special together. I will call when I get settled Brandon. Good luck dear. Please forgive me. Truly, Ana
