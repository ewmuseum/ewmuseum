Things Fall Apart: Prelude

16 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It all makes me sick, really. This place, how it works. How these words are supposed to change our lives, the fact that right now you're looking for an answer from this very page. Well, here it is: You have no life. If you think you know what's going to happen, I suggest you open your eyes, look around and realize one thing. Whether or not you like me, or hate me, you wanted this. I may be paralyzed, I may be crazy, but I am honest. So remember, the most difficult thing to swallow is one's pride, the hardest words to hear are the truth. With that we begin, my friends... From Aimz to Xias, the fans WRESTLING organization has a roster that's 5% genius, 5% think they're genius, but NOT. Then, round out the other 90% with apathetic, coat-tail riders, or the people that should be just taken out back and shot for insipid love stories about trust and tenderness... I wouldn't even grace their faces with the vomit of mine that they induce. Its not their fault, I don't blame most of them. Usually, I get upset with the audience that looks at the fucking eye candy and get brainwashed. Then, COMMEND this shit for happening in the first place. The fWo is a JOKE, an organization of untalented hacks that wouldn't know creativity without it first slapping them in the face. Look at it right now, a dreary, mindless, get your shit done on Monday sweathouse of sensitive, overdramatic characters... ...who SHOULD be wrestlers. This is your mecca? Is Poser your Jesus? No, no, I shouldn't go there. Then you'll make some idiotic relation to Deacon and I liked him. At least, he attempted to think every once in awhile. So, why do this now? Aren't you in some body cast, Craig? Well, I guess I could say the mind can do some magikal things. Or maybe I should say that love conquers all? Fuck that. That's the shit that makes this place stink. Maybe the nurse is cleaning my piss pan, right now and I'm thinking anyone pumping out Roche/Campbell Productions should drink it. I'll just say that even if I never can stand again, I'm still committed to bringing about the one thing this place needs. AN END. This place had it so easy, now it's gonna learn the price of opening Pandora's Box. Fuck July 30th. That's just cutting dead weight. Its the 40 year flood they should be fearing. And I'll tell you right now there's one big motherfucking black army of clouds coming with it.
