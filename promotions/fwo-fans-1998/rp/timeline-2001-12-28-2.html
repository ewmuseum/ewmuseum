12/28/2001 7:00PM He couldn't see his face. The Boston Strangler frowned, and went to work again... polishing and buffing his right boot. In what could only be described as somewhat obsessive behavior, he had always followed the regimen of keeping his wrestling boots as well shined as possible. While many of his fellow wrestlers spent all of their time on their tans, or their hair, or spraying some of that sickeningly scented oil on themselves, he spent his time on the parts of his appearance that mattered most to him. He had once heard a saying about how if you want to know what type of person someone is, to look at his or her shoes. Somehow, it had made sense to him... for behind the tans and white-toothed smiles, the oiled skin and salon-styled hair, none of those things showed true preparedness for the ring. Overlooking one's ring attire displayed sloppiness and, in his mind, a lack of respect. What you brought to the ring was what would get you through the match. Of course, actually winning a match lately had been an entirely different story. However, regardless of the outcome, in many cases one's best ally outside of knowledge and training was a good pair of wrestling boots. So it was, that much like the gunfighters in the old west would clean their pistols before a fight, or the ancient Samurai would hone their blades before each great battle, he polished and maintained his boots now just as he did before each and every match. That is, until he heard the knock at the door. "That was quick", he thought to himself as he got up to answer the knocking. Upon opening the door, he was greeted by a smiling young man pushing a small cart in front of him. Judging by the contents, his meal had arrived, and his stomach growled in response to the good news. "Delivery for room 424", the young man grinned. "Chicken breast dinner, side of mashed potatoes and mixed vegetables, one bottle of ginger ale... and a little extra something the chef whipped up for dessert that he wanted you to have... it's a Boston Creme Pie!" Did everyone know who he was? Of all of the amenities the hotel offered... being discreet didn't seem to be one of them. But the young man seemed only too happy to help out as he put the covered plate on the small tray table, and poured the ginger ale into a glass filled with ice. "Thank you..." the Strangler replied, glancing down to read the nametag of the waiter. "Joseph. But we're really not in the mood for any dessert." Well... OK..." Joseph shrugged. "But Luigi isn't going to be too happy..." "Luigi will survive, we're sure" the Strangler chuckled back. "But if it will save you an earful, just leave the pie in the mini-fridge. After all, if Luigi is upset, our next meal might be our last." "You just might be taking you chances with this one!" Joseph snickered. "Luigi is a good guy, with a lot of experience under his belt... but he tends to make things a little too spicy. Well for my tastes at least." The two men shared a chuckle together as Joseph walked across the room, pie in hand, and towards the small refrigerator located in the corner. Something about this kid, he was humorous and brash... almost likeable. He looked to be around Darryl's age, maybe a year or two older... but where their personalities seemed to differ, there was that same friendliness to them. "Nice boots" Joseph said, nodding towards where the Strangler had left his gear. "You know, if you don't want to waste your time shining those, we've got a guy here in the hotel who gives the best shoeshine in town. Walk outside on a sunny day after he's done with you and you could blind somebody, I swear." "Thanks for the offer" the Strangler replied, "but we don't mind doing the work. It's almost relaxing, actually." "That's cool, that's cool" Joseph nodded along. "Just thought I'd toss the info out to you. Anyway, I just need you to sign here, and we'll charge the order to the room. Except for the pie of course." The Strangler took the pen and receipt, and after checking the total, signed his name at the bottom. It was funny how he had to pause before signing for something, as half of the time it was using his real name and the other half was using his wrestling name for autographs. It was difficult at times not to confuse the two. "Thanks, Mr. Adams" Joseph smiled before shaking his hand. "If there's anything more you need, just call down to the kitchen. My shift is about over, so I won't be there, but we've got a full staff working tonight. I've got to get moving... my band goes on in a couple of hours and I still need to get home and get my gear together. Besides, you're probably looking to eat and relax I'll bet." "That's most likely the case" the Strangler nodded, his stomach again growling in objection at being made to wait for dinner. "But you have a good evening all the same." "Yeah, you too man" Joseph said with grin and a wave, before turning and pushing the cart out of the room. "See ya around." "Nice kid" the Strangler thought to himself as he stepped over to the table to lift the cover off of the plate. The scent of the food hit his nostrils, and though Joseph had been right about the amount of spices used, his stomach growled even louder this time for appeasement. At that moment, the Strangler paused, looking between the meal in front of him, and the polishing job he was in the middle of before the food arrived. Against the protests of his stomach, he put the lid back down on the plate, and stepped back over to the bed to continue his work. Hopefully, his food would still be warm when he was done.
