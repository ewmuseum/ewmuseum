Mind Games

24 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
""Master of Puppets I'm pulling your strings twisting your mind and smashing your dreams Blinded by me, you can't see a thing Just call my name, cause I'll hear you scream Master Master Just call my name, cause I'll hear you scream Master Master" -"Master Of Puppets" by Metallica Even he couldn't believe he pulled it off. He sat in darkness in a moment of reflection. It was the holiday season, a time he didn't particularly like. People rushing everywhere, buying things for their loved ones, singing songs. It just wasn't him. The only loved one he had was himself. And he had already given himself the best Christmas present anyone had ever given him. Twilight was twenty four hours ago and in one night he returned to the fWo, shocking the world and pulling off the inconceivable. When he learned that Chris Universal was paying his medical bills he decided he owed him at least a favor. Anything more than that was going overboard. So he accepted the contract and signed it, the ink drying no more than an hour before the match. When Universal told him that he wanted to screw Ric Chronos out of the title and laid out the plan he was elated to help out. Elated because in the end he knew he would pull the wool over Universal' eyes like he had done before and screw up his plans. He did not understand why Universal bought that he would help him out but the tag title match was the first step to making him believe him. Universal had made some stupid decisions as of late such as bringing Neo back or agreeing to the booking power match in the first place. It's not that Universal didn't have faith in Ross defeating Chronos because he did. It was personal between he and Chronos. He wanted the title off of him and thought that Davis would help him out. In the end; Davis got everything he wanted. A contract... an outlet for him to take out a year's worth of frustration out on people. And an opportunity to screw Universal again. It wasn't anything personal between he and Ross, either. By screwing Ross he screwed Universal. He was by no means fond of Chronos but he couldn't see following through with Universal' proposal. And that's why he did it. Months of plotting came to a conclusion. A violent conclusion. The glass he used represented the glass he punched in the hospital. That was the moment that woke him up. Using the glass relieved him of several things. The moments he felt sorry for himself, the moments he felt that life would never be the same again. Never again would Austin Davis feel sorry for himself or anyone else again. Months spent in the hospital didn't change him for the better, they changed him for the worse. If the world thought that Davis was sick, vile, evil and twisted before... they hadn't seen anything yet. Countdown was approaching and he had an iron clad contract. Universal couldn't fire him, either. And he couldn't help but think that Chronos owed him a title shot. He knew Chronos wouldn't give him one and he knew that Universal wouldn't book him in one. Either way he had a plan to get him one. What he had plotted to do next was going to be something very controversial. The master of puppets had many strings left to pull. The only question was who's? "They say a picture is worth a thousand words. At Countdown... my actions will speak louder and more violently than words ever could." His face exited the darkness and evil smiled. "Don't say I didn't... warn you." His face disappeared from sight. The sounds of glass being smashed followed.
