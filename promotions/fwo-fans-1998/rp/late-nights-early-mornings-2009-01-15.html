Late nights, early mornings

15 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Daddy! What ya doing?!” “Sleeping buddy, give me a couple of minutes, kay?” Sean 'Triple X' Stevens yawned, glancing over his shoulder at his alarm clock – 7am – this had to be some sort of cruel joke from God. “I'm hungry, daddy! I wanna eat-eat!” Trip opened his eyes, rolling over to his left. He'd never tell him, in fear of breaking his delicate little heart, but secretly, Sean hoped that this was just a dream, that he was still sound asleep, and that when he rolled over, baby Shannon wouldn't be there. But, he was, smiling, in all of his youthful exuberance. “Where's momma bear? Ask mommy. I'm sure she bought you your favorite raspberry toaster strudels, ” Trip responded, hoping, no, silently praying that Poison Ivy was up, taking care of business in the next room, as usual. Shannon didn't budge. “Mommy said I should ask you, because you had all the yummy food!” “Oh she did, did she?” Trip asked, as he sat up. “Where is she now?” “On the couch! Her and uncle Eli stayed up really late after we got home from the big party last night,” the young boy answered. Even though he should've known it before, it was now more apparent than ever that there was no other alternative. Sean 'Triple X' Stevens' peaceful slumber had been broken, and if Shannon Joseph Stevens had any say in the matter, he would never get it back. “Okay ... okay. I can do this,” Trip mumbled to himself, as he stood on his feet, a little hung over, a little woozy, very wobbly. But, like the ring general that he was, he somehow found the strength to persevere. “Why are you up anyway,” Sean said, glancing in his son's direction, as he – in a classic “awww” moment – took his father by the hand, and helped guide him down the stairs, in the direction of the kitchen. “The big machine changed colors and said vroom!” Shannon began simulating a moving vehicle with his arm. “The big machine?” said Sean, confused. “Yep, the big machine beside mommy's c'puter! It made me not so sleepy anymore.” Trip snapped his fingers, “The fax machine.” “Yep, the tax machine!” Trip laughed at his sons innocent mistake. He was as cute as a button, even when he was wrong. Once downstairs, they crept past the living room, and just where Shannon said they'd be, were the two culprits responsible for Sean's lack of sleep – Poison Ivy was flat on her back, Sean's platinum selling, 100% cotton, “King of the Cage,” girlie tee, and faded blue jeans. Out cold, she never made it around to taking off her left shoe, the right one was on the floor next to the couch. Eli was stretched out on the love seat, looking about as uncomfortable as you'd expect a six foot nine man on a love seat to look. Deep, deep, deep in the back of Sean's mind, he envisioned himself tossing a pail of water on them, so they'd know how he felt, but he didn't, and he wouldn't. As annoyed with them as he was, he loved them, and was proud to sacrifice his own personal happiness so that they could get an extra hour of sleep. “What do you want, kiddo? One toaster strudel enough?” “No, king daddy! Two toaster strudels!” Shannon made a muscle. “I'm a big boy!” Trip sighed, “What am I going to do with you, young man?” he said, glancing over at the fax machine. Strange he thought, the cover letter was addressed to “Sean Steven”, Trip was for certain that whatever it was, was for Ivy. Trip walked over to the machine and picked up the three page document, with a note attached.. “Daddy! Daddy! The microwave's beeping!” “One second, little Lucky,” Sean responded, opening the microwave door, and placing the strudel's on a paper plate. “Here, run up to your room, daddy'll be there shortly with a nice glass of milk, okay?” “Mmkay!” he said, as he scurried up the stairs to his room, where the loud thud confirmed that he leaped in his bed. “Dear Sean,” Triple X read out loud. “As much as it pains me to know that you won't be competing in the FWO on a full time basis, as I said to you last night, I respect you too much to drag you through the mud. Here is a revised version of your contract, where it is specified that you will only be wrestling for us in a part time capacity. As I said to you in our conversation, if you ever change your mind, my door is always open. Please sign it and either fax it or bring it with you next week. Warm regards, William Buckley.” Trip walked over to the refrigerator and poured his son the glass of milk he promised him. “You do know that Toaster Strudel's belong in the toaster, right?” said Poison Ivy as she tossed and turned, trying to find a comfortable position on the couch. Trip waved her off, “Don't talk to me, woman. Just know that you owe me big time for this one.” "If you can be on point until noon," replied Ivy, "then I owe you a thousand." “If I can keep that little bundle of energy on quiet 'til noon, then you owe me another baby. You down for round two?” Trip smirked to no response, as Ivy pretended to fall back asleep. “That's what I thought.” "If I can keep both my eyes open," replied Ivy, as she pressed her palms into her eyes, "and we can skip the actual being - knocked - up again part, then you've got a deal." “So, what you're saying is, I'll see you at noon?” Trip responded, shaking his head in disbelief. Ivy met her fiance with a loving gaze. “Love ya, Papa Bear.” “Love you too, Momma Bear.” As Sean Stevens made his way up the stairs of the Seattle, Washington condo that he's sharing with Poison Ivy, his brother-in-law Eli Flair, and a couple of younger wrestlers in need of a place to crash, he came to a startling revelation, that he initially refused to accept. “Daaaaddddddyy! My throat's dry!” Shannon Joseph called out from his room. That today was going to be one of many very long days.
