Harem - 6 - Back To Base

4 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
And once again, she finds her way back into the heart of one of the places she hates most... "Thanks for the visit," said Ivy as she reclined in her hospital bed, "Since Eli headed back to Los Angeles there's really nobody around anymore." Amy Campbell stood by the window, a smile on her face. This room was much nicer than the intensive care ward that Ivy had spent two weeks in. She was off the respirator, she was off the pills. All she had left was her IV and that was barely anything. Aimz, on the other hand, looked distracted by something. She was attentive to Ivy, and kept her up to speed on all the happenings in the fWo, but two things were definitely apparent. One, she hated hospitals, they made her uncomfortable. Two... Ivy has the distinct feeling there's something on Amy's mind. "I'll stay a couple of days if you'd like. I...uhh...can't really wrestle for a little bit, so I've got the time." Three...she'd mentioned no injury. "Didn't I tell you not to push it?" asked Ivy, as she suddenly tried to sit up, "You're gonna end up here next to me. What'd Winters do now?" "All Winters did was get beaten with one hell of a fight. This is the fault of someone else, and it's not exactly hurting...yet." Amy was fidgeting. She always had trouble staying still, but this was a nervousness above and beyond the norm even for her. "You're confusing me to no end, girl," said Ivy, as she took Amy's hands with her non- needled left hand, "What's going on?" The Psycho Bitch had dropped about ten pounds of muscle while lying in this hospital bed. She wasn't sleeping because she wasn't used to it. The sedatives they'd given her until recently just made her tired for hours. But her eyes still held their fire, they still caught every detail. "Amy," she continued, "tell me!" The redhead sighed, pouting. "Telling you ruins the fun of the guessing game! This leave of absence..." She grinned, "...is Brandon's fault. Since he's not exactly a known abuser, does THAT give any hints?" Slowly, it dawned on Ivy. "Oh my..." Amy blushed. "You're..." Amy grinned. Ivy pulled her close and hugged her friend tightly. "That's awesome! Is Brandon excited?" More blushing. "Brandon. Yeah, Brandon...see.....uhhh..." Aimz backed away from McGinnis slightly, sitting back down in the wheeled chair just out of arms' reach of Ivy's bed - Prepared to catch anything her friend would throw in an expected reaction to the next little revelation... "See, he...well.....okay, Brandon doesn't know yet. And, for that matter, won't for a few months." Ivy's eyes widened as she tried to sit up slightly in surprise. "It's not that I think he'd flip," continued Amy, "It's just that I...well...think he'd flip. He's got too much on his plate now - You know how much Creid's fucked with his personal life in the past, to tell him messing with Vic isn't just endangering our relationship but a baby too would be too much. I figure if I can push for the wedding to be within the next couple of months, barring the possibility of me gaining a lot of weight within a small amount of time, I can tell him after that's over with. Y'know, when he feels strongest about us." Ivy looked around, like she was going to throw something at her. But after a moment she stopped and exhaled sharply. "That makes sense... when Eli found out Angel was pregnant he botched a spot in a match and ended up with a concussion and nearly slipped into a coma. Of course, Angel was off the side of the road in a ditch after a bus accident, but as a rule she wasn't taking bumps." Her eyes gave off a wave of caution and concern. "I won't spoil it for you, and I do think you should tell him when the moment is right... just be careful. My false alarm back in the spring was the most nerve wracking days of my life." "I know when he does find out, he'll understand and be happy. But you know Brandon, that happiness would have plenty of worry...I just don't want to drop it on him at a time where the worry will outweigh the happiness because of the situation. Keeping Vic away from his personal life, preparing for main event matches and planning a wedding is enough for his shoulders to carry right now." She leaned back a bit, relaxed now that she wasn't holding a secret from everyone. The people who mattered would know...that is, except for Brandon. "My family's going to freak, and not in the good way. I want to get telling the people closest to me out of the way first, so I'm gonna see if I can visit Angel this week...she'll at least be able to offer some advice." Amy's face was contorted with worry when the sudden reminder of caution flashed through her head, though... "And I will be careful. I'm just not sure how to get so much time off without having to tell Fox directly...because I don't know if he'd be able to keep it private or not, and I don't want to risk this news going too far in the next little while." "Don't be afraid to ask for help," said Ivy, "and don't be shy about telling Brandon the same thing. Creid's prone to going after Brandon's personal life... keep yourself surrounded by friends. Alex, Fox, Eli's gonna be back on RUAHH~!!... take a few days off and hang with me or with Angel if you feel like there's some heat on." She laughed. "Basically don't let foolish pride get in the way of doing what's right for you and that kid. It's unfair, I know... but you've gotta think about more than your own actions now." "Don't worry, mom." Amy winked, "I refuse to even take a punch in this state." "Hey, I'm not the one gonna be called m---" The moment was abruptly interrupted by the patter of footsteps in the hall. "I'm sorry," said the nurse, "but visiting hours are over." Ivy glared at her. "When I want you, I'll call you. Otherwise, leave me alone." "Now, Ms. McGinnis," chided the nurse, "We don't want the orderly to come in and give you a sedative, do we?" She smiled at Aimz. "Five minutes, okay?" "Bitch," muttered Ivy under her breath, "Always something like 'Turn off that computer' or 'Take your medication' or 'You can't have a cell phone in here!' It's a real drag." "Bah!" Amy laughed, "Don't use that tone with me. Don't make me fetch your sedatives, young lady!" "Besides," She continued, "It's good to have rules sometimes. I'm gonna have to give up McDonald's for the next six months, so someone should be in my boat! Gah...I still can't believe I'm three months along already and didn't even know." ...Another rant was well in the works. "Well, I knew...but I didn't know. Like, not for sure...I always write everything off as accidental." "Yeah... I hear ya," Ivy's tone turned soothing and reassuring, "Listen... you should go before the wicked witch of the west comes back to kick my ass." She reached into her bag and produced a massive keyring. "If you're going back tomorrow, at least get a good night's sleep at my place. You remember how to get there, right?" "Yep." Amy smiled, letting the keys slip into her palm. "And don't worry, I'll leave most of your personal belongings alone." With a gentle hug, she was gone and laughing all the way as she saw the nurse heading back towards Ivy's room, inevitably to further torment the cooped up Psycho Bitch. Amy almost wished to be confined to bed herself. There were so many things to plan now, so many people to speak to and decisions to make...it was an awful lot to swallow, and she'd have much rather let it all float away with a pill or drink but Amy Campbell - the girl of NOW - knew better. She was young, but a year with these people - discovering her real family - had changed her from a rather reckless girl who'd sooner drink a problem away than deal with it into a responsible adult who faced her problems and situations, dealing with all of the shit life tossed at her as it came: No sooner, no later - Without any chemical help. Shedding demons of her past had lead to exactly what she'd dreaded then - Engagement, pregnancy...commitment. But now, in the year 2003, Aimz wouldn't have it any other way. She was happy... Finally, honestly happy with life. Maybe not calm, but settled... Content.
