Sweet

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempest sighed as she looked over the array of sweets in the window of a store. "Chocolate Town, USA," she said wistfully. "Good thing we only did the one show here. I could put on enough pounds to make it clear out of the cruiserweight division! But I think I deserve a little pick me up after last night." She chuckled and looked around mischievously before sneaking into the shop. Behind the counter a youngish clerk snickered at the furtive motions of yet another woman in search of illicit sweets. It's a good job being every woman's other man. But this was not your average bored housewife whose only form of excitement was culinary. Tempest sidled up to the counter with an expectant grin and said, "Hi! I'll have a..." She stopped and tapped her finger against her lips a moment and said, "Well gee, talk about tough decisions." She giggled and the clerk tried to supress both the look of recognition and the red tinge rising slowly up his neck to his face. "H... Hi," he stammered and quickly cleared his throat. It's much easier to boo someone from the far off bleachers than to see them up close. Tempest failed to notice and giggled as she said, "Well how about just a cup of hot chocolate. I hear those are amazing with fresh syrup and all." She smiled and seated herself on one of the old fashioned stools, her long skirt swishing delicately to the side, and propped her chin on her hands. Around her right eye, the skin seemed a bit darker, a hint of the rough night in the ring the night before that makeup couldn't quite conceal. The clerk turned and gave her the tall mug of hot chocolate with it's head of whipped creme and powdered chocolate and smiled. He took a deep breath and said, "Um, You... you're Tempest right?" She nodded and took a sip, her eyelids fluttering with delight. The clerk smiled and said, "I uh... I saw the show last night. You really took a beating though. Does it... does it hurt?" Tempest licked the whipped cream from her lips and looked thoughtful for a moment as she said, "Yes and no. It only hurts the next morning, sorta like a hangover. At the time, it all seems brilliant, even the losses." She chuckled and said, "So would you like an autograph or are you one of those terribly displeased people who doesn't love me anymore because of Monte?" She pouted, batting her big blue eyes at the clerk, a nearly irresistible expression. Having gone quite sappy, he smiled and stammered, "Well yes... I mean no... I mean... I'd love to have your autograph." He looked around, suddenly at a loss for what she might sign. He fumbled around under the counter and said, "So what's the deal with you two? Like, you really like him now?" Tempest laughed out loud and said, "Of course I really like him! Why is it that everyone seems to surprised at that?" She contemplated this suiiping from her mug as the clerk reappeared with a soft tissue candy wrapper in a soft pink. "Will this work?" he asked hopefully. "It's perfect," she said with a grin. She reached over and signed it carefully, certain not to rip it and smiled at her name nestled among the Hershey emblems and little candy pictures. "That's perfect," she said, "because it' my life these days. Everything keeps getting sweeter." She winked at the puzzled clerk and sipped at her cocoa happily.
