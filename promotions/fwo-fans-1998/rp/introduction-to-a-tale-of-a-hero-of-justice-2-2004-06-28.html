September 25th, 1986 55 Degrees, Overcast and windy "Can anyone please answer question six for me?" Mr. Jacobs stood in front of his class, most of their eyes kept swayed from his direction. Either his students were staring at the wall or outside the window, a few even looked up at the posters on the wall. He could count three eyes that looked his way and only one hand shot up in response to his question. Mr. Jacobs took a moment before acknowledging the person who had responded, "Anyone else?" He asked almost wishing as though another student was paying attention. The hand that had originally shot up still stayed. Mr. Jacobs then placed one foot on his seat and motioned toward Waylon, "Yes Waylon." Mr. Jacobs said. Waylon put his hand down and responded, "The answer is 528." Waylon smirked and then looked to his right, over three desks was Sara, she smiled back at him. They had been friends ever since his birthday, even though his Mother had forbid it, they had continued to talk. "Thank you Mr. Perceval," Mr. Jacobs said solemnly, "Now I would like for once for another student to answer a question rather than Waylon, and if no one has that ability then I would assume that I would need to double your homework? Now who has the answer for number seven?" The class remained silent, not a single reply and Waylon's hand shot up again. A few moments would pass before Mr. Jacobs would grow angry, "All right, it looks as though the workload has doubled." "Mr. Jacobs?" Mr. Jacobs looked to Sara Sundae, she held her hand up and almost a huge sigh of relief echoed throughout the room, "I believe the answer to number seven is..." Sara looked quickly over to Waylon who mouthed the answer to her, "496, the answer is 496." "Well," Mr. Jacobs began, "It would appear that if you've done your homework, either that or Mr. Perceval did just mouth you the answer. Now because of Mr. Perceval's kindness I will be doubling the workload anyway." *WHOOMP* Waylon's head rocked forward from a blow from behind, he looked down to notice a baseball on the ground. What he heard was a vicious response from Greg Rombner who was seated a few desks behind him, "Good job Waylon," Greg stated out loud. "And you've done an excellent job Mr. Rombner, of getting detention. Leave the class now." Mr. Jacobs was quick to retort. Waylon sat still and rubbed the back of his head, he didn't care about the doubled homework, school was easy, everything was easy, except for being liked. Waylon looked to Sara and noticed that she had been laughing. Waylon looked down at his desk and just as he did the bell would ring to notify him that class was over. Waylon stood from his desk and gathered his books, as he walked out of the classroom he went to turn the corner and.... *WHOOMP* Waylon smacked hard against the ground and looked up at Greg who stood over top of him with his fist still clenched, "You little faggot," Greg kicked at Waylon's leg, "Why do you have to fuck everything up all the time faggot? Why can't you just keep your mouth shut and stop making us look all stupid." Waylon began to get to his knees, "The only one that looks stupid is you." "What!?" Greg screamed as he kicked Waylon in the ribs and then followed with a harder kick to Waylon's jaw and then one cut his lip and blood splattered on the ground and dripped from Waylon's mouth. "STOP IT!" Sara ran into the mix and got in front of Greg, "Leave him alone Greg." Greg looked her up and down, and pausing for a moment on her cleavage, "You with this guy Sara?" A group had now gathered, Sara looked down at Waylon and then back at Greg, "No. I just don't like seeing anyone get hurt." Greg laughed, "No one cares about him Sara, so what does it matter?" Greg now moved Sara slightly out of the way so that he could make eye contact with Waylon, "Hey you fuck, you just watch your back." By now Waylon was standing and he rubbed his hand across his mouth, "Go fuck yourself Greg," and Waylon let lose a thick gob of split/blood that landed hard onto Greg's cheek. Greg was so alarmed that he immediately touched the gob on his cheek and then jerked his hand and watched the gob hit the ground, "You son of a," but before Greg could finish and then throw another fist Mr. Jacobs had already grabbed him by the arm and twisted it behind his back. "Mr. Rombner," Mr. Jacobs spoke softly, "You can come with me to that date at detention. We're going to learn all sorts of fun things today." As Mr. Jacobs passed Waylon with Greg still being held, he looked to Waylon's blood filled mouth and then shook his head, "If you are hurt Mr. Pereceval, go to the nurse, nonetheless, Mr. Rombner will be writing you an apology letter in detention today." "Thank you Mr. Jacobs," Waylon said softly. Mr. Jacobs said nothing as he took Greg away. As a few minutes went by the group had dispersed and all that was left was Sara and Waylon. Waylon looked to her and went to smile but held back because of the condition of his mouth. Seeing this Sara could only respond in a harsh manner, "When are you going to toughen up Waylon?"
