Life After Death

3 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So there I was back to my old ways, sniffing various drugs into my body, piercing my skin with random needles, god knows where they've been. I have no idea what state I am in, or what the day is, or even the hour. I don't even remember the name of this fucking hotel I am staying at. I am back to my old ways, the ways of Areumna'derin Rahl. Tim Veneno is now dead to me. You would think I would be happy now that I have a steady job, but I don't know if it was worth losing my best friend in the whole world. *** I have been talking to myself for hours, days, and weeks now. Randomly picking up the phone hearing a voice on the other line, not knowing that I wasn't talking to anyone till the operator would pick up on the other line and yell at me to hang the phone up. Every minute that went by seemed like it was another hour, days were longer to me, longer and slower everything came to me as a blur. I could hardly remember anything if I tried to, but when I didn't try everything came back to me. In the form of images, images would flash violently in my mind startling me, almost making me fall off my feet and onto the ground. *** I often found myself asking the question, was fWo even worth this? But, I knew I couldn't give up, after all the hard work I put in, I would be stupid to throw it all away, but I guess I am stupid seeing how my body is full of drugs right now. I can't even remember the last time I took a shower, I need to shower. *** I often find myself being very random, thinking of things and just doing them without thinking twice about what I am doing. I also find myself having multiple repeating thoughts, over and over again. The same thing, forgetting that I just did that very same activity just 5 minutes ago, I can't remember when the last time it was I took a shower, I need to shower. *** The shower felt very relaxing and I feel fresh ready for the new day. All of the sudden a loud irritating beep went off. I searched around my room searching for that god-awful noise, I finally found it; it was my alarm clock. The item that was supposed to wake my ass up, I was supposed to be in bed by now. I turned the alarm clock off and walked over to the blinds. *** It had to have been hours by now, I need sleep my eyelids are dragging over my eyes as my eyes are really heavy. I walked over to my bed and turned on my alarm clock noticing the time... 6:02 a.m.
