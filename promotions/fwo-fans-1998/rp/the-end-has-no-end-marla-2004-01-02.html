The End Has No End (Marla)

2 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 24th, 2003 "Mommy?" "Yes, hun?" "How does Santa know if I've been good or not?" "He just does", she said with a smile. She finished tucking her son in, then kissed the top of his head. "Get to sleep", she said. "Santa won't come if he knows you're still awake" "Ok", he said, and buried his head in his pillow. She rose and turned off her son's light, before closing the door. "Good night, Roland" "Good night, mommy" It was a long time before she fell asleep that night. She lay in the dark, listening to the soft snoring of her husband. Her mind was full of thought for the future. Her husband, Alex, had just been offered a full time position at the docks maintaining ships. This promotion would open financial doors for her family that she thought they would never see. They would finally have enough for a downpayment on a house, with some money left to fix Alex's truck. Perhaps now they could even afford to have another child, something she had been wanting for years. She smiled in the dark, beside the father of her children. It was not until the small hours of the morning that she finally fell asleep. And when she did, she had beautiful dreams.
