The Birthday Massacre Part Two – Promise Me Thursday, August 13, 2009 12:02 AM Promise me to pass the time Dance with me on plastic tears Kiss me, we won't feel alone Till morning when we disappear Ivy McGinnis has spent the past day and a half, for the most part, at her bar, TC’s. She was coming up on the age of thirty five, and, based on the past six years, had made a conscious decision to avoid anyone she knew for the next few days. To that end, she had planned to hide in the bar that she was part owner of for the next few days and avoid anyone involved with either the music business or wrestling business. She never expected the love of her life to show up. Triple X, Sean Stevens, had not been seen in the FWO for several weeks as he had been fulfilling his obligations as the World Champion of Empire Pro Wrestling. “Be honest, did I really surprise you?” asked Stevens, rubbing his fingers through his finance's hair. “Eli tipped you off, didn't he?” “I had no idea you were anywhere near the city,” replied Ivy, “Though I probably shouldn’t’ be surprised. How are you?” Sean grimaced, “I dunno, Mama Bear. I think I've officially crossed that threshold. Ivy's brow furrowed, as the meaning behind Sean's comment went completely over her head. Noticing and half expecting her to NOT understand what he meant, Trip continued. “I've crossed over into that place in my career as a wrestler where the bumps and bruises don't heal as fast as they used to, and there's never EVER really a day where I'm not working injured. But, all things considered, I'm still a World Champion.,” he proudly boasted, before a sudden sadness changed his mood entirely. “Can't wait to get back to the FWO, though, I can't lie. Do you miss us being around each other 24/7? I figured we could use the time apart, but I'll be honest, Ivy ... it's been hard as hell being away from you and Shannon. There was no way I could miss your special day, even if it hasn't been so special the last couple of years,” Sean laughed nervously. Before answering, Ivy picked up her drink. She looked at Sean, and back into the fermented collection of hops and barley. “You have no idea how hard it’s been, doing this without you, Sean.” He started to answer, but Cally had intercepted his comments with his usual drink – a beer and a shot of whiskey – before he could do so. Figures, she was with Ivy on this. “Is that a serious question, Sean?” asked Ivy, “Forget about the fact that I’m booking on the fly in the Beav’s stead because he has no idea what he’s doing, but to take both Eli and you away from me at the same time? I’ve missed you more than anyone who’s ever existed could possibly imagine. And I know you’ve been busy with the Empire… but it doesn’t make it all better, y’know?” “Touche' my dear,” Sean began, winking at Cally in a show of appreciation for the drink. Sean downed the shot without hesitation, causing Ivy to wince. “I see your point, and I think I have a solution.” “Unless it involves seeing you every day for the first time in what seems like forever, keep working on it,” replied Ivy. FW.com 2007 Wrestler of the Year, the 2008 3rd Place Wrestler of the Year, two time EPW World Heavyweight Champion,” he continued. “I think I proved what I needed to prove when I came back. Not too many people can doubt my legacy anymore, and now is as good a time as any to put all of my energy in effort in the hardest task I'll ever have conquer. It'll require all of my time, charisma, and intelligence to do so ... and, I think with the right timing, I just may be able to succeed at it.” At the bar, for the first time since she walked in the door, Cally saw a smile on Miss Ivy’s face. “You’re not messing with me, are you?” asked Ivy, “I might be doing less now than I was three months ago but I can still feel myself getting burnt far faster than I should be, and if you were there… that would be perfect.” She leaned in and took both of Trip’s hands in hers. “Are you going to be able to come with me next week, or will you be heading to the show yourself? Or am I crackin’ the bubbly too soon?” Trip's warm smile eased her insecurities, “Course not, McGinnis. I used to be good at this. The lonely nights, the hotel rooms, the road in general. It's not the same anymore, and the only thing I know for sure, is that I spend more time thinking about you than anything else. “Of course, you TOTALLY missed my attempt at setting a wedding date,” he smiled. “Figures. I don't want you doing something you don't want to do.” The ring still glittered on Ivy’s finger. “You’ll forgive me, of course?” she asked, “We’ve been dancing around that subject for what, two years now? I’m so used to everything revolving around this business, and with you and Eli doing your own things, my mind has been totally switched off from anyone actually looking to take care of me.” Ivy took a deep breath. “But, if it was up to me and we had all the paperwork filled out I’d marry you tomorrow, Sean.” "The blue-eyed badass beamed, smiling ear-to-ear, “You mean that?” He asked. “Then, let's do it. I'll finish up my last couple of dates in Empire, then I'll make my full time return here with you,” he said. “I think you and Shannon both need a happy ending, and I'm committed to seeing to it that you get it.” She smiled again. “Didn’t Eli teach you anything, Sean? There are no endings with us.” They leaned into each other, as Cally quietly told the stragglers at the bar that it was time for last call. The money they’d make tonight wasn’t as important as giving the two some time to themselves.
