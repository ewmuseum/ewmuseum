Void

29 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He stumbles through the park, in the dead of the night, cold rain pelting him. Alcohol coursing through his veins, hampering his movements. Everything before his was just a blur. Swaying back and forth, wind, rain and vodka pushing him left and right. The rain masking the tears coursing down his cheeks. He slips on the grass, and crashes into the lush green carpet. FLASH He walked in the front door, home early from a road trip, and stopped dead in his tracks in the foyer. In the living room, he saw his wife look up at him from where she lay on the couch... as well as the man she was laying with. FLASH He pushes himself up to his hands and knees, his hands clenching reflexively into fists, pulling out two handfuls of lawn and earth. He gets to his knees when a big gust of wind suddenly whips about his body, blowing him backwards to fall to the ground again. FLASH In a shocked silence, he simply turned around and walked out. His wife ran after him, pleading with him, don't leave, please don't leave, but he ignored her, got back into his Pathfinder, and drove... drove... he didn't know where or why, just drove into the night. FLASH He opens his eyes, water from the sky abouve cascading onto him. He opens mouth, half-hoping he would drown, and not really caring one way or another. His fists raise up, and then slam down into the ground as his eyes squeeze shut, a shudder rippling through his body. FLASH He did return later, his wife sitting on the couch hoping he'd come back, the other man nowhere to be seen. Numbly he sat down, and she spoke to him, her words seeming to fall on deaf ears, but he heard them all. they were burned into his soul - seeing other men, being fulfilled by them while he was away. Could take the stress with him anymore. Had to leave. Better this way. For him and their son. FLASH The scream seems to be ripped from hell itself, carnal rage and frustration erupting in one huge pulse from a man who invited death upon himself. He screams until there is no air left in his lungs, and still his body rages against life, a silent scream challenging life itself. Face frozen in more pain and anguish than most could bear to witness. FLASH He kissed his 3 year old son and hugged him close, promising him they'd visit often. Held him as long as he could, but had to give him up. He handed him back to his ex-wife, the woman whom he stilled loved with all his heart, but who couldn't bear the burden of that love anymore. She promised continuing friendship, and they would both have the common link of the child they'd created together. With just a small caress down his cheek, she leaves, the only two things in the world he'd ever gave a damn about leaving his life. FLASH He draws in a deep breath with a body-wracking shudder, and begins to cry. it's been said that real mon don't cry, but he doesn't care, the sobs coming from a heart which had been ripped open. He lays there, he doesn't know how long, grief pooring from his body, until finally, mercifully, he passes out. And there the man lies, cold and alone in the pooring rain. Dead inside. Hollow. Empty. Void.
