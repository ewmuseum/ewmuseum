My Life - Part 5

6 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pain is a double edged sword; it has a funny way of letting you know that you’re still alive, while still making you wish for death. I would’ve chuckled amusingly at my own esoteric joke, if it didn’t hurt to move my jaw. I allowed my senses to remain numb for a few moments, taking solace in the darkness and silence that overcame my body. The only sensation I could recognize was that of the cold concrete floor below me; his heavy handed strike must’ve sent me to the ground. Nevertheless, the cool sensation felt nice against the bumps and bruises that no doubt riddled my body. I lay comfortably for a few moments; my desired slumber being uninterrupted by any variables. Unfortunately, all good things must come to an end. Suddenly, my silent Eden was disrupted by the sudden click of my jaw. A shot of extreme pain shot through my face and sent an alarm to my body, reawakening the stings and aches that were previously masked by adrenaline…and a possible concussion. The concrete was no longer a soothing cool; it was stained with the sticky heat that emanated from my open wounds. The pain in my jaw reminded me that my heart was still beating, throbbing in unison with my heartbeat. It felt as if a sharpened knife cut across the side of my face and dug as deeply as possible. I tried to lift my hand to my jaw and help click it back into place, but my limbs were not responding to my commands. I even tried to grit my teeth, but a painful click reminded me of my injuries. Not the best time to be ‘tough’, I thought to myself. Vision began to return and lights danced above me. Dark shadows of demons raised their fists emphatically into the air and shouted viciously at me. I couldn’t make out their details; all I heard was a maddening ring echoing in my ears and blurry outlines of moving objects. I rested a few more moments and mentally ordered my arms to move. After a few moments of demands, my limbs began to respond. Like a general, I sent messages to my limbs with great ferocity. ’Get up, now! This is not a time to rest.’ Slowly, but surely, I brought my hands to my face and cupped the edges of my jaw. I ran my dry fingers across the borders of it and recognized how off-center it was. That man’s brick-like knuckles sent loud vibrations of physical hell through every section of flesh he struck. I could still feel the initial sting of each strike. Speaking of sting, I knew that what I was about to do would not be a pleasant endeavor. I took a deep breath to ready myself, clutching my jaw tightly, but as I did a mouth-full of blood was sucked down into my lungs. I coughed violently causing blood to spurt from my mouth like a water fountain. I could feel the spray of crimson speckles on my face as they fell from the sky. My jaw ached even more with the sudden movement and I let out a groan of anger and pain. I would not let pain defeat me. *Crack* “Oooh!” the spectators collectively groaned as I cracked my jaw back into place. Perhaps it was the sound of my bones being forcefully put back into their god-given structure, but I could finally insinuate the sounds of the barking demons; they were the chants of the spectators. I turned my head towards them and could see that some were being tended to by others. I was quickly reminded of the casualties of this battle. The man who clutched his left eye and screamed bloody agony was the very same man who got a bit too close when that behemoth sent his skull careening into my own, knocking my head into one of the spectators’ faces. No wonder the back of my head hurt, I knew I didn’t allow that ape to strike me there. Another man was holding onto his mouth with one hand and trying to collect his teeth with the other. I’d like to say that man’s injuries were an accident, but that’d be a lie. I struck him intentionally for supporting my opponent. Don’t shout against the beast, when the beast is within arm’s length of you. After resting for a few more moments, I began to hear the chants of the crowd to get up. They barked for more violence and demanded more bloodshed. I didn’t force my body to rise for their sake. I simply refused to lose to a brainless oaf. Albeit, an oaf who can break bones I once thought to have been calcified to the point of imperviousness. I suppose those years of torturous matches hadn’t made me my body any less human than my mind thought it was. Despite my human limitations, I forced myself to my feet – another endeavor that took more than a few attempts to achieve. As I sat upright and gingerly shook my head to force my vision and coordination to return, I noticed that the ape hadn’t risen. I took slight amusement in knowing that I was not the only one sent to the floor from such a deafening impact, and even more so that I was the one to rise from it. I slowly pulled my left leg towards me and used it as a cane to push off of. As my knee smacked into the concrete floor, I heard the cheers of the crowd grow louder. The ape was beginning to rise. I stared angrily at him and wiped the blood and sweat from my eyes, this man was not going to lose that easily. His masculine pride was not going to allow him to be defeated without being killed. “So be it,” I muttered to myself as I pushed off of my leg. “I’ll just have to kill him than.” I pushed hard against my leg, but through all the violent shakes that radiated from my attempts, I remained on one knee. My struggles were fruitless and I began to grow frustrated. I started making up excuses for my weakness, blaming my old age and lack of competitive action for my inability to rise from such torture. However, I quickly came to grasp that the fault of my folly was not stemming from my own flaws, but from this man’s dominance. Despite my aging years, I knew that my body was well trained for pain – but it had never gone through pain such as this before. My body had taken knives, barbed wire, 20-foot drops; and it all paled in comparison to this moment of physical horror. He was beginning to rise, so much so that he struggled to one knee. I stared harshly towards him and noticed that his eyes were dancing freely from a stinging head injury. I saw my opportunity to dominate, I could see he was on his last few breaths of consciousness, and thus I forced my body to rise to a vertical base. Behind me, a wave of blood-thirsty cheers roared in excitement as my opponent stared at me exhaustingly and lowered his head for a moment. I tried to stand perfectly still, as a dominant figure, but struggled to keep from falling, wobbling to and fro. My legs ached and my head felt woozy. My eyes began shaking violently and as I fixed my eyes on my opponent, I saw three bald-headed warriors all staring at me and struggling to get to their feet. I closed my eyes and shook my head, but there were still three angry beasts standing before me, all of them simultaneously shouting at the ground and getting to their feet. “I'd ask if you wanted to continue,” said the three beasts as blood poured from their mouths, “But judging by that popping sound your jaw just made, I think it's going to be difficult for you to answer." Their simultaneous voices rang in my ear. I could feel the warmth of my blood trickling down my lips and falling from my chin. I began questioning the source of my wound, whether it was external or internal. I spat the crimson away and felt my jaw aching with every attempt to speak. I opened my mouth wide and felt a wave of agony tingle across the borders of my jaw and head. The maddening hum began to return in my ears, but I refused to show weakness towards this man. While every word may have sent jolts of stinging pain coursing through my brain, I forced myself to answer him promptly. "If you're still breathing," I said through heavy breaths, fighting back the nauseating temptation to vomit. "I'm still fighting." I could no longer fight the temptation. I quickly fell to my knees before his reply and let loose a river of red vomit. I planted my hands firmly on the concrete, struggling to stay awake as I stared down at my own crimson insides splattered on the ground. Again, a wave of red blood poured from my mouth violently and created a warm puddle before my eyes. My head grew heavier and my eyes struggled to stay open. With all four limbs working against gravity, it was proving to become more and more difficult to remain conscious. The struggle became worse once the ringing in my ears grew louder. My senses began to wane and my body began to shake angrily. I shouted towards my limbs to remain strong, but each of them slowly began to mutiny. Before I could bark another order, another river of blood angrily spilled from my insides. "What have you done?" she screamed at me. "Lived...” was all I could say before submitting to death.
