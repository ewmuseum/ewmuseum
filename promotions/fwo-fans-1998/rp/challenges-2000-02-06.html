fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Challenges. February 06, 2000 :::The scene opens with a closeup of what appears to be the red letters "FTfWo" on a patch of leather, before it slowly pans back to show that they are an insignia on the leather jacket being worn by Perfect, who also sports a pair of sweat pants and a t-shirt under the open jacket which appears to read "Trade Griffey Now!". As the camera continues to pan back, it reveals that he is standing in the entry plaza at Atlanta's Turner Field, home of the evil and generally annoying Atlanta Braves. In the background against the bright blue sky we can see a huge closeup of the ball Hank Aaron hit for his 715th homer. Perfect takes a moment to take a seat on a nearby bench before turning to the camera.::: Hey, everybody! It's been a little over a week and a half since I last blessed you all with an interview - did all you fWo fans miss me? :::Perfect smirks.::: Actually, that's a pretty pointless question considering that I built the fWo, and if it weren't for me, there wouldn't be any fWo for you to be fans of. But you already knew that, so let's move on. You see, an old anything-but-friend is again trying to get that main event rub from moi. That, of course, is the leader of the other group trying to kill the fWo - but instead of blatantly, they're trying to do so via dragging down our ratings to the point WCW laughs at us just by their mere presence on the show - the group being the French Foundation, and the leader being the Freaking Frenchie. Frenchie, let me make something clear. I don't like you. I don't respect you. I think your matches make Lex Luger's look good and I think your interviews make Sid's look coherent. But I've always thought that under all that general crappiness you at least had a little intelligence. But obviously you proved me wrong on Warfare when you went after my partner, and through that went after me. Frenchie, you never learn do you? Christ, pay a little attention here. You think you're so incredibly hot because you beat me after I had already carried that big goof Ice to the match of his life and after I carried YOU to the match of your life? That don't mean a thing to me, punk. And even though you managed to pull off the incredible feat of losing your title without even having a match because you were too stupid to renew your own damn visa, you still managed to somehow become a three-time champion a few weeks later in a match where you carried by the Black Panther, for Christ's sake! In any case, congratulations, because you've held it now for a couple weeks, and your average title reign is an incredible 7 days! But take heart, you little frog, because you've only got another 99 days until you tie my average reign. You can do it, just try! Well, scratch that. You won't be able to do it, for two reasons. Number one, because the D-Man is gonna beat your ass and take your title on Slaughter, and number two, because if he doesn't cripple you Sunday, he and I'll cripple you and both your little asskissers on Warfare...assuming you got the écrous, which I doubt, to accept my challenge. This Wednesday, Frenchie...myself, Dead, and a partner of our choice versus you, "Not Ready For" Primetime, and "Insert Chris Jericho joke here" Courage. Just say the word, TFF, and you career will be over... :::Perfect snaps his fingers.::: Like that. And speaking of champions, something has come to my mind. I haven't held a title since December twenty-four, nineteen ninety-nine. Of course, that's because I haven't gotten a title shot since then, but that's not the point. And of course, because since the beginning of the fWo, a big gold belt has more or less been a part of my entrance attire, I figure, why, that's just wrong, you know? And seeing as the D-Man is gonna take the big gold that I've already held four times, the way I look at it, why not go after the next biggest belt, and one I haven't held before? After all, I've got nothing to prove regarding the World Title, what with my holding the records for most AND longest reigns. Plus, since that gives me a chance to take a title off the least credible champion the fWo has ever seen - not counting the Frenchie, of course - Eron The Talentless, well so much the better. Listen up, little man. I'd tell you to mark my words, but that's of no use since you probably can't read or write. I've got tomorrow off and Wednesday booked, so that leaves Sunday, February the twelfth, as the date I strip your worthless ass of your title. And I'm not asking you like Frenchie- no, no. I'm telling you. You will defend the Internet Title against me on the February 12 Slaughter, and I will take the belt. No two ways about it. :::Perfect gets up off the bench, and begins walking back towards Turner Field.::: Now...the big story around the fWo any more is that Kid Cool is a), a Beaven, and b), the new owner of the fWo. And of course, since I, the World's Greatest Athlete, the Big Time, am the man who built this company anyway, what would this whole wacky situation be without my comments on it? Well, let me tell you this. You see this patch on the jacket here? :::Perfect points to the "FTfWo" emblazoned on the jacket.::: You know perfectly well what it stands for, and myself and the D-Man mean every word of it. All three words of it, in fact. And quite frankly, it doesn't matter if the owner of the fWo is the departed Mr. Beaven, the red-haired freak, or hell, the guy who owns this stadium right here- :::Perfect gestures up to the large letters reading "Turner Field".::: - We're still going to do exactly what we say we will. It's that simple. In fact...it's Perfectly simple. :::Perfect turns away from the camera and begins walking away, before suddenly stopping cold and turning back to the camera once more.::: Oh, and hey, Collins. You remember how I said I'd "bring the heat"? Well, I wasn't kidding, was I? :::Perfect laughs, amused, before giving another arrogant smirk. The smirk still etched on his face, he turns away from the camera again and walks off as we fade to black.:::
