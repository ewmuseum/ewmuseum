Live On Larry King Live.

22 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Live On Larry King Live. March 22, 2000 As head of the fans Wrestling organization camera crew, Geoff Green runs and oversees the operation and facilitation of each and every camera angle you see on every Slaughter (Sundays on FOX!) and Warfare (Wednesdays on NBC!). Also among his duties are overseeing the accomodation of the large camera crew. Doing this on the financial resources allocated by the fWo isn't always easy, but Geoff manages. Today, for example, the camera crew shares two large, side-by-side rooms in a local Cincy motel. As we survey the surroundings of one of the rooms (it's in fairly good condition, but not overly so), in through the front door walks Geoff Green himself, carrying a pair of pizza boxes. A cheer goes up from the assembled cameraworkers (six were previously lounging in various places around the room, having nothing better to do), and Green sets the pizzas down on the coffee table, facing the television. The lids are quickly thrown back (one pepperoni and mushrooms, the other a combination of some strange and possibly edible meats), and slices are snatched. As chewing commences, one of the Anonymous Cameramen so often employed by fWo stars in interviews pipes up. AC: You know, we really don't have anything to do around here... is there anything on TV? Green: Well, there's God, The Devil, and Bob on NBC... The cameramen reply with various comments praising the highly funny show. Green: But then again, after only two airings, the ratings for NBCs God, the Devil and Bob are heading straight to... well, lets just say its not doing so well. A total of 17 stations nationwide, with an audience accounting for 4.23% of television households, are refusing to air the show. The premiere drew 14.42 million viewers, but the second episode drew only 6.36 million. The New York Daily News reports that viewers flooded NBCs Web site after the premiere with "overwhelmingly negative" comments. Many people complained about the shows depiction of God as a "beer-drinking hippie." Rebecca Marks, NBCs vice president of entertainment publicity, said last Friday that it was "the networks belief that God, the Devil and Bob was a funny, entertaining show and NBC hoped the audience would embrace it." The Anonymous Cameraman heard earlier speaks up again. AC: But... it's... funny. Doesn't anybody appreciate funny television anymore? Geoff: No. Besides, The Almighty L. Brent Bozell has decided that despite there being little religiously or morally offensive on the show, We Shall Not Watch It. And of course, we all know how intelligent he is... Murmurs of assent. AC: Hey... didn't Venom mention something about Slugger being on Larry King Live a few weeks ago? Green: Whatever happened to Venom, anyway? AC shrugs. Green: And besides, he said that Perfect would be on it almost a month ago. AC: Well, according to this TV Guide here, he's on it, and it's starting right now. Green: Wow... an internet reporter being wrong. I'll be damned. That's almost like somebody saying wrestling is fake or something. More murmurs of assent. Green: Well, what're we waiting for? He grabs the remote and sits down on the couch, before pressing a switch. The old, scary face of Larry King appears on the TV screen, and the cameramen, scattered throughout the room with pizza, bend in and stare mindlessly at the screen. Through the magic of television or something along those lines, our screen is suddenly filled with nothing but the TV, and thus the face of Larry King. Scary thought, huh? King: Hello, everybody. Welcome again to Larry King Live. I'm your host, Larry King. Tonight we have with us one of the biggest pro wrestlers out there. He's here to discuss the impact of the sport on America's impressionable and quite-clearly-too-stupid-to-think-for-themselves children. Ladies and gentlemen, live via satellite, from Tampa, Florida, Hulk Hogan! After a moment of hesitation, into the studio walks 'Perfect' Scott Slugger, wearing an FTfWo logo tee and jeans. He sits down next to Larry, who stares off into the distance, speaking to who he believes is Hulk Hogan via satellite but is actually a crew member. The crew member gestures to Larry and points to Perfect. Larry turns, and appears surprised to see Perfect there. King: Oh. Hello. And who are you, young man? Perfect: I'm Scott Slugger... Perfect... your guest? King: Huh? If you say so, son. Well, since we don't appear to have the Hulkster here with us so we can endlessly pimp the next WCW show, what do you do for a living? Perfect: Uh, Larry, I'm a pro wrestler. King: Really! Wow, that's great. You know, it's an incredible coincidence, but we've got one of the greatest wrestlers with us tonight live via satellite. Perfect is unimpressed, and somewhat bewildered by the actions of this senile old man. Perfect: Woooooow. King: Indeed. Ladies and gentlemen, live from Arizona, the Warrior! We cut to a view of Warrior, coming in from his gym in Arizona, wearing a ripped t-shirt and jeans sporting a great variety of holes. His face is indeed painted, and his non-painted eyes are wild and dangerous as he looks towards the camera. Warrior: Versimilicitude of the Warrior Way will gilloffily attire with the eggs and cheese! Back to Larry King. Perfect appears frightened next to him. King: Indeed, Warrior. Tell us, if you would, your feelings on using proper english? Back to Warrior. Warrior: Foke! Foke English! FOKE YOU ALL! FOKE FOKE FOKE FOKE - Warrior is suddenly cut off as a group of men in medical outfits rush the scene and begin stunning him with large tasers. He gets in one last sentence before being knocked unconscious. Warrior: SEND MONEY! Back to King. With the always-frightening Warrior now gone, Perfect's face has changed into one of growing annoyance. King: Wow. Mr. Slooger, I'm sure you can't help but agree with the Warrior. Perfect: Did anyone ever tell you you're an idiot? King: Yes, many times. So, how did you get your start in the business? Perfect appears less annoyed at finally being asked a normal question. Perfect: Well, Old Dude, it all started when I was drafted by the Seattle - King: Pilots, yes. Great team. Perfect is nonplussed. Perfect: The Pilots left Seattle 31 years ago... they're the Brewers now. King: So, what were your thoughts on the recent World Championship Wrestling pay-per-view? Perfect: Well, I thought it sucked harder than a vacuum cleaner at a convention of Monica Lewinsky imitators. Somewhere offstage, a rimshot is heard. King: There you have it, a ringing endorsement of Ted Turner's own WCW, which can be seen Mondays on TNT and Wednesdays on TBS! Perfect: But... I said it sucked. Worse than your show. Hey, pull the hearing aids out of your ass and stick 'em in your ears for once, would ya? A thought occurs to Perfect that they would likely need to be washed in between those steps, but he shrugs it off. King: I agree, it was the greatest pay-per-view ever, because Hulk Hogan was in it! What are your feelings on Hulk? Perfect: Evil Incarnate. Him or Eric Bischoff, and since nobody knows what Bischoff's doing anymore... King: Actually, he's my top consultant - he gives me suggestions on what to do on my program. Perfect: Well, that explains a lot. Hey, are we ever gonna get to the subject of this whole show? King: It has a subject? Perfect is now annoyed. Greatly. Perfect: For the love of... Listen to me, you four-eyed, gray-haired, senile old freak! If you don't shut the hell up with your demented ramblings that make Bob Backlund sound like the World's Most Intelligent Man, I'm gonna curl up this World's Greatest Right Fist here, wind it back, and blast your ass so hard I'll knock you back to the time when Hulk Hogan was actually as popular as you say he is! King: Hulk Hogan, you say? What a guy he is. Perfect: Never mind... well, before you actually bring out the Orange Goblin, I'd like to get in a few words in.. Collins, Primetime, watch your backs. You've only got four days until it's me and Mitch against you two nothings, you two nobodies, and rest assured when I tell you that you're gonna find out first-hand exactly what we meant when we said we'd cripple you two... unless you do it to each other first. King: Words of wisdom from Roddy Piper there, and we've got to go to a commercial break. When we come back, I will pull down Hulk Hogan's trunks and kiss his ass live on the air! Before we go to commercial, Perfect chimes in with his thoughts to a nearby camera. Perfect: Screw this. I've got somewhere to be, anyway. He pauses a moment. Perfect: Note to self... hurt my agent. Badly. As we go to break, sounds of a vicious beating are heard. The high school dropout security guards stand by and fail to help King, too engrossed in their task of booking WCW... And off to commercials. Meanwhile, back at the hotel... Anonymous Cameraman: Man... this sucks. Green: You ain't kidding. Green reaches for the remote, and presses the on/off button. The TV blips off... and so do we, no doubt praying for the safe return of Perfect from Larry King Hell.
