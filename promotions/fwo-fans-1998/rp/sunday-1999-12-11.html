Sunday.

11 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Sunday. December 11, 1999 :::We open up with a shot of Ice, wearing street clothes which nicely accent his ungodly muscles, sitting on a semi-plush chair in what, due to the fact that it's filled with weights, is likely a weight room. In front of him we see a television/vcr set, and the vcr appears to have a tape in it. Ice faces the camera.::: Ice: Hey, Foundation. You know, a few days ago I was going through the old fWo film libraries and I found this tape that I think you'll find real interesting. Take a look and judge for yourself. :::Ice grabs a nearby remote and presses play. We cut to the TV screen.::: :::From the banners hanging in the arena and the logo on the ring, we can see that this is a tape of Holiday Hell II. First we see a shot of the Flying Frenchie entering the ring, wearing the World Title around his waist. We fast-forward (w/cool ff effects) to late in the match. Frenchie is dazed, and Ice grabs him. He positions the Frenchie, and spikes him with a big piledriver. Frenchie is out, and Ice makes the cover. 1, 2, 3, and a new World Champion is crowned. We see a shot of Ice holding the belt high before the tape is stopped and we cut back to Ice.::: You see, that's what happened the *last* time I was in the ring with French Foundation member and the World Title was on the line. I walked out of that match with the gold held high, and even though I lost it the next week, I still held it four days longer than you had, Frenchie. :::Ice smirks, almost Slugger style.::: Not to mention the fact, Frenchie, that you've only held it once. I've already held it twice, and you can be sure as hell I'm not finished yet. By the time Sunday is over, I will be a three-time World Champion. And that's not just an opinion- that is a cold hard fact. Can you say that, Frenchie? Will you ever be able to say that? Frankly- No. And quite frankly, I don't give a damn whether it's the Frenchie I pin, whether it's Primetime, or even if push comes to shove, if it's Scott. Business is business, and the World Title is the World Title. No offense, man. But- just keep this in mind, Foundation. You *know*- let me repeat that, you *KNOW* that one way or another, one of The Dynasty is gonna be walking out of Slaughter as the World Champion. I mean, really. On your side, you've got Primetime, the most undeserving champion in fWo history, an absolute pathetic walking joke that's not even funny; and on the other side, you have the Flying Frenchie. The man who says that he deserves the World Title, but the last time he won it, he only held it for 3 days. 3 DAYS! How freakin' pathetic can you get? Let me answer that. It doesn't *get* any more pathetic than the French Foundation. Frenchie, Primetime, Y2C or whatever part of the Jericho gimmick Courage is ripping off this week, they..all..suck. There's not way to deny it, so don't even try. All three of you know that you are pathetic, you are worthless, and worst of all, you ARE WEAK. You see, I think that's what you don't get. You don't understand your weakness, and you don't understand that the Crazy Train is the epitome of strength, in all forms of the word. Mental... :::Ice taps his head.::: and physical. :::Ice gets up out of the chair and heads towards the weights, before selecting one particularly heavy-looking bench press. He lifts it almost nonchalantly, holding it up above his head as he talks to the camera.::: I'll leave you with this advice, boys. Be prepared, and be ready. Because once you're on the tracks, there's no turning back...when the Iceman cometh for your World Title... and the Iceman cometh for you. :::Ice holds the heavy weight up for another moment or two, before finally taking it and tossing it half-way across the room, where it crashes into the TV, which explodes with a sizzle. Off this image, we slowly fade to black.:::
