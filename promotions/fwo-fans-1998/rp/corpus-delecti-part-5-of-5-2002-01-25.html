CORPUS DELECTI Part 5 - Ten Thousand Voices A bright morning sun shone through the window, hitting the King of Extreme square in the face. He slowly, tentatively, opened his eyes, getting a good look through sober eyes at his room. It's not nearly as bad as he thought - empty beer bottles all over the place and a dent in the wall, but far less damage than his typical all- night benders. His first thought is that he must've passed out mighty early to have done so little to his room. His second thought.... is that he should be hung over right now. He should be, but he's not. But he IS still soaking wet. He peels himself off the bed and begins to strip out of his wet clothes, all the while looking outside at the London sky. Eli heads for the bathroom to get a towel, turning on the radio as he goes. I tried so hard, and got so far, but in the end, it doesn't even matter.... Eli smiles. Fitting song. He walks back into the room proper and grabs a plain black T-shirt from his bag, noticing for the first time that his message light is on. YOU-HAVE-ONE-NEW-MESSAGE "Hey, Eli. It's Ivy. Are you there? Wake up, you lazy bitch! .... Okay, well, it's 7:30 and I'm gonna grab a taxi over to the arena. Call me when you get this. Later." Eli hangs up the phone. "Wake up, you lazy bitch," he repeated.... and immediately begins laughing. He pulls out his cell phone and sees that it's off - no wonder Ivy had to leave a message. Easily remedied. "Ivy." "Yes, you are. I know you're Ivy." "Eli! Good afternoon, kid. Glad to see you're still alive. You coming to work today, or is this a holiday nobody told me about?" "I'll be there, Ivy - just give me a few to shower." "Solid. I'll hold down the fort until then. Oh, and Eli?" "Yep?" "If you see Sean.... tell him I said hi." "Solid." Eli hangs up the phone, staring at it for a few moments. Sean Stevens. "Triple X" Sean Stevens - one of his greatest opponents, who has quickly become one of his closest friends. His opponent this Sunday, and unbeknownst to Sean.... Ivy is quite smitten. Eli gets up again and heads for the window, looking out onto the London morning. It's gonna be a good day.
