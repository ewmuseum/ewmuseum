Who knows where thoughts come from? They just appear...

19 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ivy McGinnis was never a morning person, nor was she an evening person. She barely slept, was always surly, and never seemed to have time to just stop and relax. All that changed two years ago when she and an overly-energetic wrestler/fighter named Tempest first compared skills in the realm of computer hacking. She's still not a morning person, but she's actually up in the mornings now. She sat on the entrance to the bus, staring down the still-quiet city streets toward the Australian sunrise. Moments like these, she never really got in the wrestling business. Or all too infrequently. But, life must continue. "Hey!" she called inside the bus, "There's a show in 'Zee tomorrow, and it'd be nice for the band to be there!" "No...shouting...too...early..." came the groggy reply from the inside. A moment passes, and Lindsay Troy stumbles into view looking...well, she's had better mornings. Her perfectly tressed curls are all askew and her eyes are struggling to stay open. She's feeling a combination of still-drunk/hungover, and she yawns loudly as she stretches. "Jack Daniels is no longer my friend, Ive..." Ivy smiled. "Jack Daniels is nobody's friend, m'dear... he's a cruel man who likes to see pretty girls and boys puking. A sneaker flew towards the front of the bus. "Mornin' TJ," she laughed, then turned back towards Troy, "He always responds to someone waking him up by tossin' something useful. Like a shoe. Be glad he didn't hit anyone this time. Anyways... if you're hungry Angel and Mick are comin' back with some foodstuffs - bagels and whatnot. Did we have fun?" "Yeah. Angel and I had a bit of a heart-to-heart last night. Felt good to get some things off my chest and out in the open." Troy sits down on the floor of the bus and cracks her neck. "It may not be any of my business, so feel free to shut me up, but how come you bolted?" "She's a good girl." Ivy turned around, but quickly peeked back toward Troy... and saw the stall tactics wouldn't work. "You guys, talking about your significants 'n stuff... how you want kids someday, how she's got the most perfect Little Angel ever... and all I could do was think about Sean." Lindsay arched an eyebrow. "Sean Stevens," explained Ivy, "Triple X. The end-all, be-all love of my life and I'm here in Australia with a band I believe in and a girl that's more my sister than anyone but my sister ever was... and all I want to do is be there with him. And I can't because it's... complicated... but it just hurt to hear you guys talk about it.... y'know?" Troy nods her head. "I know, Ivy. More than you think I do." She scoots over closer to Ivy and looks out toward the horizon. The brilliant colors are now becoming more intense, and Troy sighs wistfully. "Marcus isn't right for me, Ivy. I don't see myself spending the rest of my life with him because I know there's only one guy for me. But he's gone. I don't know where he is or what he's doing. All I have left to hold onto is a promise that he made and my wish that he'll keep it." "That's what gets me from one day to the next...knowing that Matthias promised that he'll come back for me, no matter what. He saved me from myself when I thought no one could raise me up from my own personal hell. I'm so damn stubborn and independent that I had those notions indoctrinated into my brain and I said fuck everyone else. Jaime died, and I couldn't bring him back. He was all I needed and I didn't need another damn soul for the rest of my life. But the moment I walked into that USCW talent meeting with a belt slung over my shoulder, and the moment I locked eyes with Matthias as he stood in a corner away from everyone else, I knew. I saw. His eyes told me everything." A tear trickled down from the corner of Troy's eye, but she didn't notice nor care. She was going to get through this, no matter how much it hurt. "I need him, Ivy. Just like you need Sean. Just like Angel needs Eli. I wake up every morning and I question my existence. Then I turn around and the answer is right between my shoulders. "I do what I do for myself, and I do what I do for the fans, and I do what I do for him. Everything I've ever said or done in my life has been for a purpose, for something that I've believed in. No one can tell me what causes are the right ones or the wrong ones as long as I believe in someone or something." "Amen, sistah," said Ivy, tactfully ignoring Lindsay's tears (She'd want it that way), "You don't know if it's a right or a wrong choice until y'look back on 'em, and I know now that any choice I made that's put me here and Sean in Orlando was the wrong choice." "I need to be back there, Lindz... not just with Sean but to set everything right. The fWo, with Merritt, Randalls, Miles... everything." "In the world of hot-headed men, the voice of reason is always a woman. The Greeks and Romans knew what they were doing by worshipping the female goddess of Wisdom. Ivy, if it weren't for us this world would be so much worse off than it already is." Lindsay paused for a moment, then smirked. "I hear Disney is doin' a real bang-up job running the fWo. Or should I say ruining?" she snorted. "Why is it that all these corporate mega-giants think they have a friggin clue how to run a business that they should never be a part of in the first place?" With a shake of her head, she continued. "I remember when Radicomm helped the X Network run the XWF, and the reason that fed was so successful is because Xandor Cross, Xandor Alvarez, and Myself were in charge of things. Alaina tells me about all the corporate bullshit that goes down there, and our execs are getting pissed off. Hell, they're talking about pulling out of our deal with Disney and selling our stock because they're so disgusted." "The problem with Disney isn't their money... the problem is their insistence on hands-on micromanaging." Ivy rolled her eyes, "Y'know, for all my schooling I should've been in some kinda boardroom... and I'd be tearin' these fuckers new ones." She sat down next to her on the bus step and rested her head on Lindsay's arm, mock-exhausted. "I just need a way back in... I need some kinda loophole to slip me back under the radar and get to work. But I don't even know what kinda legalese they're using to keep me out of the arena... it really REALLY sucks." "It's probably something really insipid, I'd be willing to bet. That's what it is most of the time. And hey, if all else fails, send someone in to do your dirty work for you. I find that's always a good stalling tactic until you break down the system and get it to do what you want it to do the way you want things done." An impish gleam crossed into Ivy's eyes. "How much time do you have for a little work on the side... maybe more if things go well?" "Like my uncle always says," Lindsay said with a chuckle, "there are twenty-four perfectly usable hours in every day." Ivy laughed. "Well, new friend... Ivy's got a favor to ask." "You come to me with a request on the day of my daughter's wedding, knowing that I could not refuse." Both women laugh. "Exactly," replied Ivy, on the same level, "Damn the Man!" A wicked grin crosses Troy's face. "Save the fWo..."
