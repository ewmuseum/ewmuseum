Ice is back...

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a cloudy Saturday morning in Seattle. There was a chill in the air, and most of the patrons of the Pike Place Market were bundled up in heavy clothes - hooded sweatshirts, heavy coats, the like. Not Ice. The three-time World Champion stood in front of Pike Place Fish, the famed fish market and home to fish tossing. He looked much the same as he had the last time he had been seen on fWo TV before his recent return. His bright blond hair was buzz cut close to the scalp, and a blond goatee covered his chin. His muscular arms bulged against the tight "Path of Rage" t-shirt he was wearing. Sweat pants and shades completed the ensemble. A predatory grin stretched across his face. "That's right, baby. Ice is back." Ice removed the sunglasses and leaned his face close to the camera. "Now, I know it's been a while... and hell, some of you may not even remember me, as hard as that is to believe. For those of you who that applies to, let me tell you whose eyes you're looking into right now. These are the eyes of a 3-time fWo World Heavyweight Champion. A former fWo World Tag Team Champion. A former fWo Hardcore Champion. A former fWo Internet Champion. The winner of the first ever Meltdown. The man who all but single-handedly took down the original Dark Riders. I was kicking ass in this promotion when three-quarters of the current roster were just dreaming of the day they'd be let in. You'll never see a tougher, badder, meaner sonuvabitch than me, the Big Blond Machine. Just ask the men I've crippled. Just ask the men who've stepped out of the ring with me feeling like they've been run over by a Mack truck. Ask anybody in this damn fed that's had the sheer misfortune to step into the ring with me. When it comes to the fWo, just like the other guy on TV with blond hair and a bad attitude, I am the Big Bad. And as of this moment, the fWo is on notice. Anybody who's stupid enough to step in my way is gonna get a faceful of this..." Ice raised a clenched fist at the camera. "... and an assful of this." Ice lifted his boot into the camera view. "And that list starts with Scott Slugger." Ice paused a moment and took a deep breath, calming the anger the very mention of that name brought up in him. "For those of you who don't know the history between me and Scott, go find it out for yourself, because I don't have the hours it'd take to tell it all. Suffice it to say he hates my guts, and I hate his. And you know what? Since I've been back, I've wrestled in two matches... and gotten two pinfalls. Let's see, who were they over? I can't seem to recall... oh yes. Scott Slugger. And Scott Slugger. And Sluggsy, if you've got the balls - and I expect you don't - you'll accept my challenge. You and me. Slaughter. For the Internet Title. Just bring your ass, kid, because I'll be kicking it all around the Tacoma Dome. And once I'm done with Baseball Boy, the Path of Rage is focused on the rest of the fWo. Nobody is safe now that the Big Bad is back in town. You step in my way... I'm gonna gut you like, well..." Ice gestured to the fish market. One of the men working there procured a large salmon - a good 45-pound fish. In the best Pike Place tradition, he hurled it at Ice. Ice lifted up his hands to catch it, but something went wrong. The salmon, traveling at a high velocity, went through Ice's hands and struck him in the face with a fleshy, meaty thud. Ice fell unceremoniously to the floor of the Market. Cameraman Geoff Green left his (still set up) camera behind and sprinted to Ice's side. He shook the large man, trying to bring him back to consciousness. "Ice! Ice, you OK? Hey, Ice!" ----- Blackness. Wha... ? What... what happened? Last thing I remember... something about a fish... wait a minute... that's all I remember? OK, open my eyes. There's a guy kneeling beside me. He's saying something. He's calling me... Ice? Ice... what kind of name is that for a grown man? I mean, Ice? What the hell? Wait a minute! That must be it! He's calling me Ice - I must be... I must be... Hey. Cool. ----- "Ice, you alright, man?" Ice lifted himself off the ground and pulled himself to his feet. "Yeah, I'm fine. It's all good... yo."
