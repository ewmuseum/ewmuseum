Reuniting old friends

12 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Standing outside of ABC Television Studios is none other than veteran FWO cameraman Geoff Green. In the past three years he had done a plethora of camera work for productions of all kinds. From Hollywood to right there in New York City, he had seen big stars and not so big stars through his camera lens while working mostly on television shows. His knack for cinematography had gotten him jobs via word of mouth and through people seeing his impressively shot work on other programs. But that was kids stuff. Actors are wimps. Try keeping the camera steady when two 300 lb psychopaths are trying to destroy each other 4 feet away while keeping everything in frame and not getting any bones broken from the flailing tree trunks that pass for their biceps. That's why when they asked him to come back and be the head cameraman for the FWO again he barely resisted the urge to say “When do I start?” After all, he deserved to be paid for the great work that he did, but that didn't make him any less enthusiastic. He needed a challenge. Sure it was less money than usual, but that'd go up when this place got back on its feet, and he was sure that would happen soon. Of course, if one of their big stars wound up in jail for assault and battery, that could all go down in flames rather quickly. It is precisely this reason that has Geoff standing outside ABC's Manhattan building. The plan had been to interview his old friends in their hotel room after Brittany's first televised appearance in 7 years. Also, to get back the considerable amount of money Mike *still* owed to him. He was not going to forget, no matter how many times Mike dodged it or how long it took, but... but he was getting sidetracked. Right now he has to find Mike and Brittany and find out what happened. All he knows is from the call he got on his cell phone saying that Mike had burst onto the show and was being arrested. He assumes that is the reason for the squad car parked about 15 feet from the entrance the the building with the lights still on. Geoff knows to just stay alert and wait for them to be brought out of the building. A few more minutes of waiting and he sees Mike being led out of the building in handcuffs by two uniformed officers, followed closely by his wife. He couldn't hear through the glass that paneled the lobby of the building, but it's obvious Mike was ranting about something. Geoff holds the shot on Mike as they emerge through the door, and for the first time he hears what Mike is saying. “THERE ARE LIZARDS IN MY BRAIN GET THEM OUT GET THEM OUT I AM THE VOICE OF MY OWN GOD IT'S NOT ILLEGAL IF YOU DON'T GET CAUGHT” Mike continues to rave in mostly gibberish as the officer on his right jerks him forward. “Oh you got caught alright, let's keep it moving.” Geoff calls out to Brittany who had emerged from the building shortly after Mike and the officers had. “Brittany! Brittany! It's Geoff. What happened?” Brittany turns to Geoff with a worried look on her face. The officers load Mike into the back of the squad car and get in themselves. “Geoff, oh it's good to see you. Listen I gotta go.” “Brit, what happened in there? He wasn't even supposed to be on the show, was he? Why was he here?” Brittany appears to be frustrated and frazzled. The officers turn the siren on and pull away, causing Brittany to whip around and watch it speed away. She hails a taxi as she rattles off a hasty response to Geoff. “He was here for moral support. I can't really” Luckily for Brittany a taxi pulls up almost immediately. It causes her to end her thought mid sentence and jump into the cab. She tells the driver where she's going and the taxi pulls away. Brittany rolls down the window and yells at Geoff as the car drives down the street. “Do me a favor and get in touch with Dr. Talbert for me!” Geoff barely hears “for me”, much less has a chance to respond, but that doesn't stop him. “I don't know his number! Or what to tell him...” Geoff Green was at a loss on how to handle this situation. Ask him to manage 4 camera man shooting a battle royale, no problem. He did the only thing he could think of. He switched the camera off and called his new boss. “It's Geoff. He's been arrested. No, I don't, you'll have to talk to her. Yeah. Probably want to issue a press release. Just saying. Keep the fans informed so they know everything's ok. Yeah, I hope too.”
