My Modus Operandi - Part 4

5 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Yeah! You see that!? I got him down!” You shouldn’t be so excited kid; only reason why I’m on one knee right now is because you broke the rules and kicked me in the balls. “Get on him Jimmy ya dumb douche! Jeez, thus fuckin’ guy. I swear, Jimbo wouldn’t know when to wipe his own ass if I didn’t tell him.” You should definitely listen to him ‘Jimbo’ because in about three seconds when you turn around I’m going to uppercut that god damn smile right off your face. Aaaaand, there it is. Now go pick up your teeth. This is my ninety ninth fight at Clinton’s little fight club slash underground mixed martial arts gambling ring, and there has been no man to come close to beating me. Every fight has been more violent. Every fight I am finding myself with a blurry line of right and wrong. Perhaps it’s the chants of these drunken morons down here watching me fight wanting to see blood, wanting to hear bones crack. Maybe that is just my excuse so that I can see it; so that I can hear it. My mind has shifted from having a somewhat friendly bout for some cash into seeing my opponent as if he were the Taliban. It is killed or be killed as if I was back in Iraq. Trying to get myself out of that mind set has proven to be difficult at times. If Clinton wasn’t here watching over my fights, a few men surely would be dead by my hands. I can feel my eyes widen as I see this bum on the floor holding his face. I lift him up to his feet and throw him backwards into a concrete pillar. I can tell his mind is still fuzzy from my uppercut to his jaw. I give him a few good body shots to his ribs. I don’t want to knock him out; I want to punish this man and enjoy this inevitable win. I get in the clinch with him, turn my body and basically give him a belly to belly suplex to ground. His body goes limp and I get into a full mount position. I throw my elbows down into his face trying my hardest to put his head through the floor. Again, and again, and again. I think I just broke his nose, or maybe it was his orbital bone. I’m not entirely sure and nor do I care. I hear the roar of these blood thirsty drunks which fuels my rage even more. This guy, my so called opponent is not moving. I look up at the people. I cannot hear them and they are moving in slow motion. They’re apparently pleased with what I have done by the indication on their faces. I slap him in the face to see if he’s still awake and he makes a slight groan. Perfect. I can still play. I grab a hold of his arm and swing my legs over his chest and I pull. “TAP!” I yell. But he doesn’t. I hear the faint sounds of someone yelling which sounds to me like they’re from the Peanuts cartoon. I yell again… “Tap mother fucker or I will break your arm!” he doesn’t so I continue to crank back. That elbow is lookin’ like it’s going to pop. Stupid idiot, all he needs to do is tap. Then the sound is heard. POP. It echoes through the basement like a beautiful note in my ears. Someone is grabbing me from behind; who would fuckin’ dare… It’s Clinton apparently trying to say something to me but I can’t hear him. I let go of my opponent, stand up and look down upon him. My mind is now slowly starting to come back into focus and I can clearly hear a very upset Clinton. “Steve! He’s out man, he’s fuckin’ out!! Jesus Christ. Someone call an ambulance!” Clinton rolls the poor schmuck on his side and he spits out several teeth. “It’s ok kid, its ova.” Clinton looks up at me, visibly disgusted with what I just did. “Steve, you need to stop when I call it over. What the fucks your problem?” I smile. I can’t help it. I’m proud of my work. “Clinton, let’s face it. These people are not here to make bets and put some extra cash in their pocket. These people are here to see me, see blood and hear the beautiful sounds of bones breaking.” I look around the room and then claim my dominance. “No one can beat me, and when I am here doing this, I am a killer. You are all bringing me fuckin’ weak people who cannot even sustain a punch to the face. Who the fuck do you think I am?” I blow my nose on my downed opponent. Fuck him. He’s weak. “Bring me a worthy man. A man who will push me to my limit. A man who will push me to the brink of death. This is hand to hand combat, not some rinky dink back yard school fight.” I turn my back to them all, getting ready to call it a night. “Until you do that…my one hundredth fight for all of you pieces of trash is on hold.” As I expected there was totally silence; you could hear a pin drop at this moment. Then I heard it. The disrespectful sound of someone’s half a laugh. “I dare you to laugh in my face whoever that was”. I waited for the person to come forward, but as expected he was a coward. “That’s right, keep your silence. It’ll do your health wonders”. I take two steps towards the stairs, and the silence is broken again. I hear a lone wicked laughter. The chuckling is eerie, full of uneasy insanity. I tell the laugher “Cute. What don't you bring that school girl laughter up here and I'll see if I can help you become a man. I have plenty of juice left in the tank." "You're super-masculine act is quite amusing, it's like watching a brainless animal inside of a cage," smiles wickedly and shouts to the spectators. "Hey! Does anybody have a banana I can feed the dumb ape?" Finally, the laughing coward speaks. I can't believe my eyes as to who actually has the balls to crack a joke at my expense. "You've got to be fuckin' kiddin' me. You? You want to test your luck. Are you sure you're not seeing double right now? I don't have time to beat up the resident bar drunk. I'll do you a favor pal" I take out some cash and throw it on the floor. He's a bar bum; he'll take it. "Next round is on me." The crowd parts to let this man through. His walk is calm and docile as his long black trench coat drags across the blood-soaked pavement. "I'm not here for money," he says sternly to me as he continues his walk towards me. "I'm here..." He stops, making sure to plant to firm feet onto the money he just dropped and finishes his thought "to fight.” “So unless you're getting cold feet, I suggest we stop with this nonsensical battle of words, and let out fists do the talking for us. What do you say?" He removes his coat showing that he actually put some time, likely jail time, into that body of his. His scars tell a story, one that I can read like a book as I have my own. "So you figured out how to life some weights. Good for you." I grab my hand and crack my knuckles in my fist. "But you know what? I couldn't agree more. Talking is for losers, which is all you've managed to do so far.” This is going to be fun. “Someone say...ding". The resident beefed up bar bum before me smiles. “Ding”.
