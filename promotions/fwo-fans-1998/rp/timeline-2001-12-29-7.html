12/29/2001 3:00AM With a soft click, the Strangler unlocked the door to his hotel room and hung his coat near the heater in order to aid it in drying out. He noted that though he had walked for miles in the rain, he still carried the stench of tobacco smoke with him. With a sigh of resignation, he undid the laces on his boots, put them by the heater as well, and plodded towards the bathroom. A shower would do him good. Nothing helped melt away the chill of a cold rain like a nice hot shower. Plus, it might help him to relax a bit, something he hadn't been able to do for a few hours now...or better yet, weeks. As he peeled his clothing from his body, the Strangler wondered how smokers could stand the smell they carried with them. If it was this bad when one didn't smoke...it had to be worse if they did. Hopefully the laundry detergent would get the smell out of his shirt... He turned the water on, and once it reached the desired heat, he stepped into the shower. It felt good, warm and comforting, enveloping him in a watery embrace... so different from the rain. After a vigorous lather and rinse, he decided to stay in the shower a little longer, to let the hot water soothe and relax him. The Strangler hung his head, letting the hot water wash away the smoke and grime of the outside world... but the shame stayed with him. He had sunk so low, to have Universal replace him at Twilight with Austin Davis. Of all people... the one man who had given Universal more trouble in his career than anyone. Universal was a fool to choose him, and an even bigger fool to trust him. And now the Strangler knew he had to... ...do absolutely nothing about it. It was a topic brought up during his conversation at the bar, and even by Universal himself. Why did he always have to be the knight in shining armor? Why was he the one person who would put everything on the line for a company that would gladly trade him in for a younger, and more importantly, cheaper wrestler with the same abilities? Who was all of this sacrifice for exactly? He had little that he called family, fewer friends, and a job that took up far more of his life than he ever thought it would. And his thanks? Being brushed aside as soon as he wasn't useful to the current plans of the promotion. Universal used people... for money and nothing more. "Screw him" he thought. "That bastard insulted us by choosing Lance Star... and then added more insult by trusting the man who almost single-handedly destroyed this promotion. To hell with him, and his ego. It's his problem. He created it by bringing Davis back... and now he can deal with it." His conscience protested, considering the others who could be hurt by this latest development. The other wrestlers...the staff... the fans... what of them? He didn't want to help Universal very much right now... but with so many others in need... what could he do?
