The Book.

15 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization The Book. March 15, 2000 ::The scene begins in a place unknown to us, unknown to most wrestlers. A very old looking book shop, the books on the shelf are coated with a thick layer of dust, and cobwebs are a common site. The lighting is extremely bad, and the neighbourhood on the outside looks no better. The small room would make anyone closterphobic, and the room looks deserted. The door booms open and slams against the wall with huge crack, the man that walks in, is a Godly looking man, Rain. Sunglasses, a dark trenchcoat, and his long flowing hair easing down his back make him look more menacing. The large man slowly walks into the room taking in his surroundings, obviously not surprised by what he is seeing. Rain walks up to the counter and rings the ancient bell. There is no response so he hits the bell again, this time with more force, nearly crushing it. Then Rain hits the bell again taking a huge chunk out of the counter, this time their is a reaction, slowly but surly an old man creeps out of the back room, the man is more ancient then the bookstore itself, He hobbles out on a cane, hunched over and walking slow, he reaches the counter, squinting at the huge man.:: Old Man: If it is money you want, you came to the wrong place, I have no more than a dime. Rain: I am not here for money, I am here for something more ultimately important, and I know that you have it in your possesion. Old Man: I, Deter ? Have something in my possession ? Speak. Rain: I have been led here through no mistake or error, I happen to know you have a book, an original book, only one alive. A book that was stolen many, many years ago away from the temple of the Rabooli people. Foolish men in search for their own fame and glory stole this book from the temple, of course the men themselves perished soon after, but the book was never returned to the temple. It made it's way here. There are many different volumes to the book, and missing even one destroys knowledge. Now give me the book. Deter: I know of no such book. All that I have here are what you see, nothing mythically special. You may even find some Archie Comics on the bottom row. :: Rain's head turns and he obviously tenses up as his eyebrows furrow. His large hand immediately reaches over the counter and wraps around the old man's neck. Rain puts no pressure on the man, but continues.:: Rain: Do not patronize me, there is a reason I have been sent here, that book is the most important of the series and I must read it, the information is key, now if you follow the teachings of that book, I know that you will hand the book over to me now, if you do not you may steal the fate of the explorers. Deter: I am sorry, the book is at a great sake to me. Of course I follow the ways of the storm, and I know who you are, as there are very few of us, there aren't many I dont know, but you are the most famous of all, spreading the message to the world, and you may be the most powerful. I am sorry Rain, but I will fetch the book now. ::Deter slowly hobbles over to the far corner of the store where he slowly taps his cane on the floor, finally finding a hollow board, he bends over and lifts it up, slowly removing the book, the book is bound in leather skin, and is quite thick, a gold tassle sticks out of the top. The book itself looks to be in quite good condition considering it is centuries old. The man carries it with the greatest care, before laying it into Rain's outstretched hands.:: Deter: Take care of it and be on with your mission. Rain: Thank you Deter, the God's look down on you with a touch of goodness. Continue your good work. As I will continue with mine as the most important time of my mission approaches, Body Count. I will enter as one man, and leave the king, and offer a great present to the storm, the time is approaching and at Body Count, I will be the champion. Tonight though, I face a man named Mitch Wilson, for the title that is rightfully mine, this man is on the side of those who are against me, and will perish for his beliefs, you know what I speak is true. Deter: I do, it is your destiny. :: Rain gives the man a nod and slowly walks out the door and into the darkness.
