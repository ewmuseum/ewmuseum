The Deal

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The last time Kali Saturos, also known as .Three, was seen on fWo television, she was teaming up with her hated enemy .One to face the team of Deacon and Padrona on the 03/09/03 edition of Sunday Night Slaughter. Since then, both have disappered without anything resembling a trace. Where have they been? What have they been up to? That was unknown....even to those who are closest to Kali. And we were about to meet one of the many looking for her. Rei wandered through the constant abyss, wondering aloud why she was here. What she had done to result in....this. "Always picking up after Kali." Rei muttered. "That seems to be all I'm good for." She threw back her long blue hair, and looked around at her surrondings. They were strange....almost as if she had been here before, but she could not put her finger on where exactly "here" was. It appeared to be a swamp of some form. Myst rose from the ground underneath the light of the full moon, and made Rei even more nervous. She had no idea why she was here, nor did she know what her purpose was here, and that served to make her only more nervous. And then, she saw it. A single shape taking form in the darkness....a human-shaped form. Something that seemed to send chills down her spine while causing her to stare up in awe, all at once. Then, she saw the symbol on its chest. A red half-sun. "It is you." she said. "It is....Chaos." It nodded. "You are here because I have brought you here, Rei." It said. "You are here, because it is the will of Chaos." "I am not your servant!" Rei yelled. "I am not your willing slave, like Kali is. I am a free will, and I don't have to listen to you! And for that matter, where is Kali?" To her horror, It only laughed. "Foolish girl. Kali Saturos is not my slave. .One is my slave, and she opposes him under the pretext of serving me. I believed you to be smarter than that." Rei nodded. That would explain quite a bit. she thought. She rose her head again to speak to It. "Why bring me here and tell me this?" Rei asked It. "Because Kali Saturos is no longer under my control. I need your assitance to prevent her from killing my servant .One.....just as she killed your parents." Silence. And then.... "I will help you." "Good. I knew that you would make the right decision. I will be in contact soon." And just as soon as It appeared before her, It disappered, leaving nothing but the moon and the myst behind. Rei looked up at the full moon and sighed. It was not going to be easy, the next few months. But perhaps....perhaps it was time to take down Kail Saturos. Once and for all.
