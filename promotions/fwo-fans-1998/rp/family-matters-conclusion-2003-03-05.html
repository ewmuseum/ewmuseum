Family Matters - Conclusion

5 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chicago. The Windy City? More like the rainy one. Erik Kelly had been back in Chicago for a few hours, and the rain just kept coming and coming. The usual customs rituals were conducted with the typical delays you got used to in the life of a professional traveller. Erik Kelly knew it would take some time. What also didn't help was that because of the weather, his rental car wasn't available. It seemed like no matter how hard he tried, he found it tougher and tougher to get to Pamela Dee. ***************** An hour after getting his rental car and sitting in a parking lot of traffic on the highway, Erik reached Chicago Hope Medical. Instinctively, Erik decided to look around the outside. No sign of Creed. But better make sure. Erik walked around the building once, loitering and leaving some individuals suspicious as a tall, scary looking individual was hanging out at the hospital, soaked and purposeful in his walk. When he felt convinced Creed was not in the vicinity, Erik walked in to the hospital entrance, only to be met by a stern nurse scolding him for not using an umbrella, and because of his condition, he would not be able to go any further. After convincing the nurse to locate Pamela, who for all the time she had spent there was now known on a first name basis to almost everyone, the nurse showed Erik to the waiting room. ************************ About 15 minutes had passed when Pamela came rushing down the hallway into the waiting area. A still soaked Kelly wrapped his arms around his beautiful wife, who seemed more in control of her emotions than last time he held her. Pamela didn't care Erik was all wet. She just missed him so, and hated that they were apart so much these days. "I love you!" she said. He just held her tightly. When he finally let her go, and they sat down, Erik explained why he rushed here. "I know I wasn't going to see you until this weekend, but something happened that made me come here now." "What?", she asked. Erik felt like now wasn't the time to discuss this. She'd been through so much, and he trusted his wife. He had to believe it was just a ploy on Creed's part. "Let's talk about it later. Has there been any progress on your father?" Pamela shook her head. "None. In fact, the doctor's are doing a final look into the catscan taken today. I think they're going to tell me between the damage there, that it won't look promising. So I'm just waiting." That wait didn't take long. Barely finishing her comments, Dr. Sandra Baines entered the waiting room to greet the two. Sandra had been working with Robert Nava, Pamela's father, for over 10 years. She had gotten to know Erik & Pamela fairly well the last couple of weeks. As she approached, Erik could read her face. It wasn't good news she had. It took awhile for even Dr. Baines to get this out, because what hadn't been known to Pamela was that Dr. Baines and her father were starting to have a romantical interest in each other. She had hidden this from just about everyone, because she had only been divorced from her husband for 6 months, and felt divulging her relationships wasn't appropriate since this was a patient. When she finally was able to speak, only two words registered in Pamela's ears. "I'm sorry..." Her heart sunk. All the hope and prayers she made unanswered. Her father wasn't going to make it, and Dr. Baines gave Pamela a decision to make. Shut off life support, or let him live in a permanent coma for the rest of his natural days. Either way, Erik & Pamela stood there holding each other for hours as they had to make some decisions soon.
