Disillusionment: Book One - Chapter III

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
People piss me off. Why? Well, that's a question for which I have no simple answer. Yes, I know. It is a very misanthropic point of view, but if you knew all that I knew, you'd hate people as well. People are too ignorant, intolerant, reckless, and dangerous for their own good. I guess it's the ignorance that pisses me off the most. The fact that no one can look beyond what they've experienced and understand that there might be more out there and the fact that everyone mocks and hates what they don't understand drives me crazy. I have thought of killing before. For those very reasons no less...I was just sick of the ignorance. Let me straighten something out. It's not the government that killed Burkenstock. It's not the government that I run from daily. It's not the government that has the power to start wars, control trade, and determine interest rates. No, that's what they want you to believe. If you want to believe it though, go ahead. In fact, I encourage you to believe it. God knows you don't want to live the life I'm living, even if you could. You couldn't, though. You'd end up like Burkenstock. It's unfortunate that not everyone can hide like I can, but believe me, I don't feel lucky. This brings me to the subject of reality. I've said it before, and I'll say it again...perception is reality, not the other way around. I never said reality is perception. In Webster's words, "reality is the state or quality of being existant." He's exactly right. Just like good old Webster's said, if one does not exist, neither does their reality, because their perception is nonexistent. Reality is a product of one's mind. Alex's reality is simple, and small, and we all know why. Man, I could go for a cigarette right now. You are not the first who has said that I was insane. Every single person who's listened to what I have to say has said the exact same thing. I couldn't be the sane one in their minds, because my sanity scares the shit out of them. People want to believe I'm insane because if I am, everything I said can be overlooked due to the fact that I'm a raving lunatic. You fear my sanity, because it's my insanity that makes you comfortable. There, I said it...yeah, maybe I am a little insane. So what?!? Are you trying to tell me there isn't a little insanity in all of us? Don't try to lie to me. I see the twisted glimmer in all of your eyes when pain is brought down on someone you hate. Maybe that's why people loathe me so, they never get to see me suffer, because I always escape. No, I still don't think I'm the Chosen One...I just thrive on my own survival. Let me clear something else up, though, nothing I see...nothing...is imagined. The bullet that took down Burkenstock as I watched from the factory window surely wasn't imaginary. The officer working for the Disciples of Confusion damn sure wasn't imaginary. The rifle that struck Burkenstock in the back of the head and left him crumpled in a convulsing heap wasn't imaginary. And THE FUCKING WHITE VAN THAT CARRIED HIS SEEMINGLY LIFELESS BODY OFF INTO THE UNKNOWN WAS NOT OF MY IMAGINATION! How do I know this to be true? I read once when I was a boy that if one is able to question their sanity that it is indeed their very sanity that enables them to do it. I question my sanity everyday, because I want to believe that all this time I've been wrong. But everyday I see them...the Disciples. I see them walking the streets, eating their red meat...they look just like ordinary people. You wouldn't be able to pick one out of a line-up. I see them, and I see their destructive power, and it scares the shit out of me. I cry myself to sleep some nights, just thinking of what they do. Hey, I'm a modern guy. I can cry when I'm pissed off. It doesn't mean that me and my 10 millimeter can't take care of you...or me and my right fist, for that matter. The Disciples want you to believe that what I say is bullshit. They've trained you to believe it's bullshit. They've brainwashed you with their media and you buy what they're selling. Someday, maybe you'll understand...and that day, it will probably be your last.
