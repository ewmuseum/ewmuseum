12.15.02

14 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1:07am Earlier this evening I stood outside on a street corner in the rain. It was already cold with winter slowly moving it's way into the area, and the rain added a bit of a sting to the near- freezing temperature. This particular day had been extremely boring. Nothing to worry about with the fWo or anything wrestling related. Just Scott Wasner trying to get into my head. If only he knew why I was doing all of this to him... I was contacted by a lawyer of Wasner's ex-girlfriend, saying that I would be charged with stalking and attempted rape. I don't remember even laying a single finger on her... let alone thinking of using force to fuck her. Although she is very beautiful... regardless of what I've said on television. She must taste exquisitely. Wasner doesn't know how lucky he is. Back to the original point. While standing on the corner, I watched people. They all seemed busy, they all had something to do. Yet none of them had faces. Somehow they walked with determination, weaving in and out of one another, never touching the person beside them. It's amazing the way they work. Until one older fellow walked up to me and began some small talk. We spoke about the weather, current events, local sports. Normal conversation, though I didn't care for it, or really respond. Kept nodding my head in agreement with him. As he kept going on, I felt myself getting sick and tiresome of his meddle. The words all meshed together and my stomach began to flip in my body. I knew it was coming and I couldn't stop. This poor old man stood there talking to me and I threw up. I vomited all over him and I began to laugh. I laughed so hard at what I had done that it began to hurt. I doubled over in pain, still laughing. The old man stood in front of me in disbelief, covered in my projectile. His mouth hung open, disgusted and angry he walked away. I stood in the rain and laughed. Their triviality makes me sick.
