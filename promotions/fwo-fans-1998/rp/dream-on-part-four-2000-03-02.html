fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Dream on (part four) March 02, 2000 Many miles away from where Machina was dreaming about Aliens, Penny Pureheart tucked herself into her warm bed. The quilted covers were scented of flowers, and the entire presentation was very cozy looking, completed by the large overstuffed pillows that adorned the head of the bed. These pillows seemed to have no real purpose at all, but were piled up anyway. Had Machina been friendly enough with Penny to get into her bedroom he would have thought the pillows strange, since they served no real purpose. On this night the pillows were mostly scattered to either side of the bed, with Penny's face peeking out from the center. Her eyes were open. Penny was having trouble sleeping. Truthfully, she had been having trouble sleeping for over a week now. Since James Armstrong gave her the wallet of her husband to be in fact. He's a wrestler. The fact was out there like a bloated sore. Machina was a wrestler. While he was pretending to picket the vile sport with her he was the entire time lacing up the boots and taking part in the disgusting sexist practice itself. Penny sighed and looked at her dark ceiling. She wondered how many holes were in the ceiling tiles. She considered counting in an attempt to free up her mind, but no use. She supposed she should have to call off the wedding, although that would just play into Machina's hands. He had lied to her, and now he had to be punished. But how? Penny wasn't sure. At least there is still Rudo... Rudo indeed. The only reason she was marrying Machina in the first place was so that she could be with her true love, Rudo. Rudo had never lied to her. Rudo wasn't one of these vile abusers of morals like Machina was. She was certain of it. Rudo was pure. If he wasn't... well, she might just snap and then there was no telling what she might do. A shame, really. Machina was cute in his own way. So was Rudo. She had hoped that she might have been able to get something together with the three of them... but no. Not now. She looked over at the clock. 2:28 AM She tried to close her eyes and get some sleep, but it simply wasn't coming. Suddenly, the phone rang. She considered not answering it, but it could be Gretchen with details of the next rally. She reached for the receiver, picked it up, dropped it, fished it up again, and put it to her ear. Hello? Penny listened to the response. Her eyes narrowed and she almost hung up the phone, but then something was said that caught her entire attention. She smiled.
