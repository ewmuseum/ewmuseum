A Deep Conversation

30 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This must have been a form of false advertising. It just sat laid there- it did not walk, talk, and it did not volunteer to go out for a beer run. The rumors were a complete farce, for it did nothing. Nate was disappointed to say the least, staring down at his new Ponymaster Super Light-Heavyweight Championship of the world that was spread out for inspection on their tiled kitchen floor. Occasionally he would move a bit closer and nudge it, thinking it was sleeping, but still it did not move. "This bites," he exclaimed, frowning as he once again let the shoddy looking championship belt collapse to the floor. No 'on' switch, either... so how in the heck do you go about getting the thing to 'do' something. This wasn't his first time as a title holder. In fact, he was lucky enough to win the 21w Tag Team Championships at Titania last year. But he was going through a phase then. A certain phase that he doesn't exactly remember a lot of the time. He likes to refer to that as the 'PCP' era of his life. Damn that sly Token Weed for giving it to him for his Birthday. How could one man resist a walk on the 'wild' side? Boy, if only he could remember what he saw while his mind took that journey through the bowels of a middle-aged man. The wonders he could have seen. An hour passed and still, as Steve-O walked into the kitchen to throw a bag of popcorn in the microwave, he found his brother gazing down at the belt. He was lying on his stomach- a hand wedged under his chin to prop his head up, completely unaware someone was watching him. "Uh," began Steve-O, confused and angry at the same time. He was not informed of any new drug they were experimenting with and from the dull look on his brother's face- it seemed like he was missing out on something big. "...Brother? Dude?" A hand shot up to silence him. "Do not speak. The 'Nate' is in a state of mental enlightenment. The 'Nate' can see through this materialist flooring, down DOWN through the earth's core and into CHINA!" Nate's eyes widened, like he had just been given an important, yet shocking piece of information. "The Chinese Overlord is displeased with you. WHY ARE YOU NOT IN THE SALT MINES, BOY! GIVE US YOUR SPERM- Here. In this jar." He then held up... nothing. His hand was tightly clenching air. He shook it and demanded Steve-O take it. Steve-O half-squinting, slowly took the 'cup' from his brother. "...Okay. But don't you think that might be a little messy, bro?" Then, sunlight cut through the blinds, slowly crawling up his face, until it got to his eyes. Nate instantly came back down to earth, blinking several times and giving his eyes a rubbing. "...Uh, bro? What are ya' doing? DUDE! PUT THAT THING AWAY!" ZZZzzziiiippp. "You okay man? One minute you want me to spank it into this imaginary jar, the next you're yelling at me? I can take a creep out better than anyone, but I still have a massive hang over from last night, so do you think maybe we could keep it to a whisper?" "Sorry, it's just this Ponymaster-thing. It doesn't do anything." Nate said, then whispered to it that he'd like some Pop Tarts for dinner. Yet, it still did not move, and now he had just made himself angrier. He wanted some damn Pop Tarts. The jelly is fantastic. His favorites are the bags he finds at the back of the shelf- the 'discount' brand. Sure they were a little stale but he saved fifteen cents. Nate spat at the belt and crossed his arms, frowning. "See?" "Dude, you expect an inanimate object to cook you supper?" "...Yes?" "It doesn't do those things. I should know, I created it. Believe me, I did everything I could, trying to give the holder of it the power to see through womens' clothing- but it was not to be..." "Ah, I getcha'... couldn't get it to work, huh?" "No," he replied, "but I realized something. Sometimes, things work a little too well. Like, I was walking through this isle at the Super Store, ogling all these fine ladies as they bent over to pick up some Captain Crunch, when BAM! This fat chick walks buy and I'm blinded! It was like staring into a solar eclipse! I was K.I.A." "Oh man, fat chicks are so raunchy!" "Yeah, they are." Silence. "...Actually, I like big women. I feel like I'm Santa gettin' busy with Ms. Clause!" Sigh. "Yeah, those are the only ones we can pick up, dude. What with our small penises." Nate laughed, "Maaaaan, we are so not real men." "We're lucky we can even get the fatties', they're misfortune is our blessing, dude. Though, I don't think I've actually successfully laid one yet..." "Me neither, I always mistake 'it' for this roll of fat... boy is that embarrassing." "Yeaaaaaah, but whatcha' gonna' do? Besides, American women are getting fatter by the day, so there's plenty in supply." "True, but what if... like, when all the babes are no longer babes, and we just have all these tubbos waddling around, wouldn't that mean they would be the majority? Then everyone would be going after the fat girls and ONCE AGAIN poor us would be left with no one." Steve-O took a moment to take this all in. "BooURNS."
