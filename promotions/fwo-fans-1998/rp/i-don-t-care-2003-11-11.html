I Don't Care.

11 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He has a wife and a kid. I don't care. He has a life that everyone else wants. I want to ruin it. Because wrestlers like Codyr, they have everything. The fans, the success, the nice house with the beautiful wife and the kid who will grow up to become a genius someday and probably solve all of our world's fucking problems. I don't give a fuck. I've got my own problems. I always have. If I ever start worrying about someone else's problems, I need to go buy a gun, load it with bullets, and keep shooting until there's no more bullets left or I'm dead... whichever comes first. Not caring was what got me this far. I care about me and only me. Showing signs of compassion is a weakness. It allows people to look inside your mind, look inside your soul, and figure you out in an instant. Nobody will ever figure me out. If I can't figure me out, they sure as hell won't. Compassion is for fools. In this business you either eat, or you are eaten alive. You either bury others, or you get buried. I've got a graveyard. Some of the bodies keep coming back. Sooner or later, they will stay buried. Just like Chris Universal's dear friend, Lance Starr. Did that offend you? I don't care. I will do whatever it takes to bury others and make sure they stay buried. One by one... until the graveyard is a virtual cesspool of rotting flesh and decayed corpses that don't belong in our existence. One way or the other I will continue to get to Cody, to his wife, to his kid. He will suffer because they are suffering. I'm the one who doesn't care. I'm the one who doesn't give a fuck. We all have skeletons in our closet. All of us. And one day, they will come back when we least expect it and haunt us to our grave. But I will be there to bury it, and to bury you. Because I don't care. Even I have a skeleton in my closet... but it will stay there. If it ever comes out... I won't give a fuck. Austin Davis turned the radio up louder. He couldn't hear the song, he could only hear his thoughts. "All the pigs are all lined up, I give you all that you want. Take the skin and peel it back... now doesn't that make you feel better?" He'd just as soon kill someone without flinching rather than let them know that even he... sings.
