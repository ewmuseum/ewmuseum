Eulogy

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He had a lot to say. He had a lot of nothing to say. We'll miss him. He had a lot to say. He had a lot of nothing to say. We'll miss him. We'll miss him. We're gonna miss him. We're gonna miss him. Omega stood still over an unconscious Eli Flair as the crowd's jeering grew louder with every passing second. His arms were outstretched and his head was facing the rafters; white and silver strobes flashing over his face as ‘Rock is Dead' and Marilyn Manson's voice thundered through the historied Madison Square Garden. That was nearly three hours ago. Eli Flair had been helped out of the arena during a commercial break by Sean Stevens and Poison Ivy. Omega and Grendel left the arena and headed to their separate locker rooms knowing that they had done the job right for the night. So long. We wish you well. You told us how you weren't afraid to die. Well then, so long. Don't cry. Or feel too down. Not all martyrs see divinity. But at least you tried. Now he stood alone on an empty street corner in the heart of New York City. A black trench coat covered his body as he stood on the curb, not really waiting for anyone or anything in particular. His hands shifted down into his pockets as his body shivered while the wind blew across the street. His dark eyes darted up and down the empty street; but they saw nothing. Just like he was blind. Standing above the crowd, He had a voice that was strong and loud. We'll miss him. Ranting and pointing his finger, At everyone but his heart. We'll miss him. We'll miss him. We're gonna miss him. We're gonna miss him. Clenching his jaw, he jolted his head back towards the direction of Madison Square Garden. That was where it had all begun again. But it didn't sit right in his stomach. It felt as if he were dying inside again. Slowly and very painfully. But there were no medicines to cure what was happening. No way to recall What it was that you had said to me, Like I care at all. So loud. You sure could yell. You took a stand on every little thing. And so loud. He used to be passive. He never knew what he wanted. But now he felt different. There was a power that he could feel in his hands that he never had before. Something he always wanted from the people around him. Now he felt like that he could command respect among his peers. That they would be so fearful of him, that they would have to respect him. When he had something to say, he was going to say it loudly. Everyone was going to hear him now. Standing above the crowd, He had a voice that was so strong and loud and I, Swallowed his facade cause I'm so Eager to identify with someone above the ground, Someone who seemed to feel the same, Someone who prepared to lead the way, with Someone who die for me. But never before had he felt so alive. Grendel had rekindled something in his body that hadn't been active in a long time. His body ached for the drive to push on again. The purpose of living was there again. But it wasn't to live to be alive. It was to live to create chaos and destroy everything around him. Will you? Will you now? Would you die for me? Don't you fucking lie. Don't you step out of line. Don't you fucking lie. In the back of Omega's mind he knew that a sacrifice had been made so he could exist. A grave sacrifice that on the outside nobody would be able to recognize right away. Only Grendel and himself knew what had happened and what took place from May 11th to now, June 10th. And they would be the only two to ever know what happened. Omega continually told himself that he wasn't going to die. But slowly and surely his mind had been twisted in such a way that he did die. And he had been convinced that it was the right thing to do. He had to die. He had to come out of his hardened shell and take new form to become what he used to be. You've claimed all this time that you would die for me. Why then are you so surprised to hear your own eulogy? You had a lot to say. You had a lot of nothing to say. He moved to the middle of the street and looked up to the suspended street light that shone down on him. He then looked down at his shadow that was cast across the street and went into the darkness across the sidewalk. He slowly brought his arms out into the crucifix position, the very same that he had at Unleashed as he stood over his arch-rival, Eli Flair. Come down. Get off your fucking cross. We need the fucking space to nail the next fool martyr. As the rain began to fall, he brought his arms back down. He looked up at the sky, watching the water droplets fall around and on him. The rain seemed to revive him as he moved across the street and over to the sidewalk. In front of him was the hotel where the other competitors were staying. He smiled as he watched the lighted windows go out one by one. The superstars were all going to sleep for the evening while one was still awake, lurking in the shadows. "It has begun," he hoarsely whispered to himself. "Tonight was the beginning of something big. An era of Pain for the fans Wrestling organization. An era that nobody in the history of wrestling will ever forgot." A small smile broadened over Omega's face as he reached the front doors of the hotel. To ascend you must die. You must be crucified. For your sins and your lies. Goodbye... From May 11th to June 10th, he said goodbye to his past life. He gave his farewells to everything before May 11th. Nothing would ever be remembered. Grendel had brainwashed him into a drone to do his bidding. At the premier of Unleashed, in front of Eli Flair, Omega gave Donaven Winters' eulogy. Nobody knew this, except maybe subconsciously. As he stood over Eli Flair in the crucifix position, it was Donaven Winters hanging there. Nailed to an invisible cross. An invisible crown of thorns perched upon his head. Donaven Winter was dead. And with that crucifixion... Omega was born.
