Tis the Season of Giving

23 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up, before Wednesday Warfare, right after one of the dark matches have taken place. When Bawitdaba hits, the fans rise, thinking that Warfare as started, because a star like Primetime is coming out. When he steps in to view, he's wearing a French Foundation T-Shirt, and is carrying a red bag. In the hand that's not carrying the bag, is a microphone.) Sit down, and shut up! (The fans boo) Can't you people have any courtesy? I'm taping this damn segment to air on fWo programming. (The booing becomes unrelentless) First of all, I'd like to wish each and every one of you a very happy holiday. The gift of Primetime, the gift of greatness, is my gift....to you. (PT steps off of the entrance ramp, and starts throwing Primetime action figures into the crowd.) Have a damn action figure of a real wrestler. Now, this Friday, well, by the time this interview airs, tomorrow, I face the Boston Strangler. Foundation barred from ringside. Blah blah blah yadda yadda yadda. I should've known that Paul wasn't too smart. He's from Boston, the hell hole of the world, the place that brings us bums, hobos, idiots, and downright, worthless people. He's an idiot. By causing me to lose my title to Slugger's big blonde boyfriend, Ice, he screwed himself out of an fWo world title shot. Too bad, Paul, you had your chance. Now, the fat man, signed the match for it to be Foundation barred from ringside. No offense to Chris, and Pierre, but, I don't need them to help me win. However, they have front row ringside seats to watch their friend, and their partner face, and defeat the Boston Strangler. It's gonna be Christmas Eve, and I'm gonna give Paul his present early. I hope you like the Primetime Bomb that I'm gonna give you, Paul. I really do. It's gonna be a shame that on your Christmas Morning, you won't be with your kids, watching them open the 25 cent pack of gum you got them for Christmas, and you won't be there when they call you a worthless piece of crap, because Daddy's gonna be in a hospital bed. However, Paul, maybe I'll stop over and spread some holiday cheer.....especially to your wife. Merry Christmas. (Scene fades to black with Primetime having a million dollar grin on his face.)
