Of Failure and Revelation

2 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a long, lonely flight from Legends in Miami back to his primary home in San Francisco. Xias was alone in heading back to California- most of the fWo wrestlers were simply going to head over to Connecticut and spend a couple days there, rather than flying back and forth. Xias, however, couldn't do that, and didn't mind the trip, considering he had no idea whether he would have any desire to show up for Ruahh on Monday. He had lost to Eron. Again. That was all there was to it. Yeah, Eron needed to cheat to win, but Xias told himself in his head that this was going to be it- that he was going to beat Eron at Legends because he knew that he was the better man. And after that he was going to get the hell out of this shitty country and head back to Japan with Reika. But now, even though he still knew in his mind that he was better than Eron, he had no idea why he had lost. If he was really so much better, Xias rationalized, than he should have no trouble fighting off Eron's band of merry men, right? Right? He had no answer to give himself, other than silence. He gave out a heavy sigh as he leaned back in his chair, closing his eyes, continuing to try and work things out in his mind. He opened them when the battle theme from Final Fantasy 8 started coming out of his pocket in bloops and blips, meaning that his cell phone was ringing. He sat up, taking the small Sony phone out of his pocket. Seeing it was Reika, he pressed the answer button. He'd just be happy to hear her voice. It would cheer him up. "Hello, Reika." he said into the phone in a warm tone. "Matthew...." she started on the other end. "I saw the match." Xias sighed. "You lost." she told him. "I did." "But you saw what happened! Eron needed every dirty trick in the book to beat you! You and the fans both know that you're better, you don't have to stick around this business any longer!" "It's not that simple, Reika." "Why can't it be?" she pleaded with him. He didn't reply. "Why can't it be, Matthew?!?" she yelled out again. But still, he had no answer to give her. A long, uneasy pause lingered between them. "I'll be in touch." he finally told her. "I'm worried about you, Matthew." she replied. "I really am." And with that, the other end of the phone was silent. Xias pressed the end button and slid his phone back into the pocket of his jeans. With another deep, heavy sigh, he tried to fall asleep.... Eron was smirking, victorious again in the center of the ring. He raised his arms as high as they could go, as Xias just watched below. "You can't beat me." Eron taunted him. "You tell yourself you're better than me, but you're just trying to make yourself feel better. You know it, and I know it." Xias had no reply. "The truth is....you were better than me at one point." Eron admitted, lowering his arms. "There was a time when you could have beaten me, a time when you wouldn't have held back. Return to that time....and you'll win the next time we meet in this ring." Eron gave out his hand to help Xias up. Xias looked at it....and he took it. "Until then." Xias awoke suddenly, the images of his dream still filling his mind. He was panting, in shock, as sweat dripped down his face. "He speaks the truth." Xias realized. "I know now how I can beat Eron." With his problems solved, Xias only smirked as he lounged back into his chair. That old feeling was returning....
