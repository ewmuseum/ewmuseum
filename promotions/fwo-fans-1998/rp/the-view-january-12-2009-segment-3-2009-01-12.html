The View, January 12, 2009, Segment 3

12 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The View returns from commercial break with the show's theme music and a sweeping crane shot of the enthusiastic audience applauding. They've enjoyed the segments filmed so far, and why wouldn't they? They're all fans of the show. The camera switches to a wide shot of the interview couch, a familiar site for regular viewer. All the regular co-hosts are here today. Whoopi Goldberg and Barbara Walters are to the left as usual, Joy Behar, Sherri Shepherd and Elisabeth Hasselbeck are to the right. The camera zooms in on Barbara Walters as the music and applause dies down. Barbara: This is a first for The View. Brittany Chambers-Baer is the manager and wife of professional wrestler Mike Bear, who performs for the Fans Wrestling Organization, or FWO. The FWO airs its first show in three years this Wednesday on TBS at 9PM. We have a clip from their last show, take a look: The show cuts abruptly to chaos. It is the end of Countdown 1, December 31, 2005. A mass of humanity is tearing the Nassau Coliseum to pieces. Banners are falling from the ceiling and being set ablaze, suspended light rigs are sent crashing to the ring and the arena floor. We can barely see two men dragging a man with horrific burns up the entrance way towards an assemblage of wrestlers and police officers in riot gear. The camera gets knocked to the floor with a crunch. And The View returns to the studio. Dead silence. The camera is showing a wide shot of the five women again, though slightly off-center. The mouths of the five women hang agape, clearly not prepared for witnessing such an event. They sit motionless for several moments. Barbara stirs first, holding a conversation via eye contact with an unidentified person behind the camera. Joy is next, whose eyes widen momentarily, then glances nervously back and forth. Typically the woman who introduces the clip will also introduce the guest, but Barbara seems otherwise occupied. The camera zooms in a little tighter on the right side of the couch as Joy speaks up. Joy: Ple... Please welcome... Brittany Chambers-BAer... She tries to add some excitement to her voice towards the end, but can't muster the energy. Four of the co-hosts rise from their seats to nervously applaud Brittany, Barbara is still furiously trying to figure out what happened from whoever she's staring at. Only a smattering of applause is audible from the audience The camera swings much more quickly than usual over to the stage entrance on the right. Brittany pokes her head out slightly, looking around for a few moments before emerging from the entrance slowly and carefully. She walks to the couch and shakes the hands of the four standing co-hosts before stopping in front of Barbara and looks around nervously once again. Whoopi kicks Barbara's foot which brings her out of her death stare. She shakes her head slightly to clear the cobwebs and rises quickly to politely greet her guest. Brittany takes her seat between Barbara and Joy and the other sit after. A few seconds of awkward silence is broken by Whoopi. Whoopi: Alright, I've gotta ask, what the heck was that? Brittany: Yeah, um, I didn't know they were going to show that. Mike and I weren't there for show. Um, let's see, what was that? Well, that's sort of the reason there wasn't a show in three years. More awkward silence. Joy: Well, ok, but what happened? Does that usually happen at these shows? Brittany: Uhhh... You know, I'm not really sure what happened. Mike and I hadn't worked for the company in four and a half years at that point. All we knew was that it was a terrible tragedy. Many more people died than usual on that show. Whoopi: Wait wait wait. How many people usually die on these shows? Brittany: Oh... uhhhhhhhh... Brittany locks up. All her training to combat her crippling stage fright and fear of cameras falls away. All she can do is stare forward in terror. The tension in the room is palpable. Luckily for Brittany's sake, a world-renowned veteran interviewer is seated directly to her right. Barbara senses the nervousness in the her and puts a hand on her knee. Barbara: Let's move on. Just like that, Brittany comes out of it. She blinks several times and looks over towards Barbara, picking her hand up off her knee and squeezing with both of hers and mouths the words “Thank you.” Barbara nods politely and disengages he hand from Brittany's grasp. Barbara: So Brittany, you said you and your husband had not worked for the FWO in four and a half years, what have you been doing since? Brittany: First let me say it's an honor to meet you, Ms Walters, you're one of my heroes. Barbara: Thank you, dear. Barbara is fine. Brittany smiles wide. Brittany: Ok, (giddy snicker), Barbara. Brittany tries to compose herself as best she can. Brittany: Actually, we haven't worked for the FWO in seven and a half years, since the summer of 2001. Since then it has been mostly a period of recovery. Joy: Oh, did he suffer an injury? Brittany: No, no, Mike has had a history of mental illness. About ten years ago he was diagnosed with persecutory type delusional disorder, what most people would call paranoia. Five years later he hadn't shown much progress, and after a reevaluation he was diagnosed with borderline personality disorder. Sherri: Oh! So did he have an extra personality? Was one a woman? Because I've heard that sometimes people can have personalities that are the opposite sex. Everyone glares at Sherri in disbelief. Brittany: No, that's why it's called *borderline*. My husband never had multiple personalities, he just had some of the conditions that accompany such a case. I personally don't understand all of it, I leave that to his therapist, Dr. Jacob Talbert. Brittany smiles and mouths “Sorry” to the camera. Elisabeth: So does he still have it? Brittany: Well Dr. Talbert believes he's ready to work on a trial basis. His condition was aggravated by the stress of being on the road all the time. The FWO toured the US and even the world, and Mike wrestled a full schedule every week for 2 years. We're hoping with the treatment he's been undergoing the past few years has gotten him to the point where he won't be a danger to himself and those around him. Barbara: And your bosses know about this? Brittany: Know about it? The last time he was in the FWO he drilled a hole in the head of his former tag team part... You know, I probably shouldn't have said that. Once again, the co-hosts are speechless. Whoopi, Barbara, and Elisabeth are simply staring at Brittany. Joy has covered her mouth with her hand in shock. Sherri, on the other hand, seems lost in thought. It is she who breaks the silence. Sherri: So I hear there's a movie coming out about a guy who's supposed to be *THE* wrestler. What was his name? Randy Robertson! Do you or Mike know him? The stares shift sharply to Brittany's left, the expressions are still shocked, and now Brittany has joined them. Sherri looks at them all in confusion. Sherri: What? Oh, you know who my favorite wrestler is? David Arquette! He was so funny in that movie. Brittany has put her head in her hands listening to Sherri talk. Barbara shakes everyone out of their dumbfounded gazes. Barbara: Moving along... Elisabeth: Brittany, I had a question. A lot of people say professional wrestling teaches violence to kids, and quite a few children have been killed attempting moves that your husband and others perform on television. What do you have to say to that? Brittany: Well both Mike and myself were trained how to wrestle by experienced professionals. We are just trying to put on an entertaining show, and we can't be held responsible... Elisabeth starts getting angry and interrupts Brittany. Elisabeth: Children are dying because you people promote violence! Elisabeth continues her rant as we hear some commotion offstage. Brittany and the other co-hosts' eyes widen, looking off to their left. Sherri covers her head. Elisabeth: And you perform dangerous moves that shouldn't be attemp... And that was all she got out before Mike Bear ran onstage with a steel chair and cracked Elisabeth Hasselbeck over the head with it. He bends over and starts pointing and yelling at her. Mike: You want to promote violence you crazy *BEEP*? THAT'S how you promote violence! My dog told me to do it! Mike waves his steel weapon in the air triumphantly. The other co-hosts have since run for their lives. Brittany has jumped behind the couch. Security arrives and tackles Mike to the ground. ***We are experiencing technical difficulties***
