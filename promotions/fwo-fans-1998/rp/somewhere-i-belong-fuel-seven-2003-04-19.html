Somewhere I Belong - Fuel (Seven)

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another rude awakening... This one, however, was welcome: The man on the other end shared none of Amy's DNA. "Hey - this ain't no rest home!" Mickey poked Amy gently on the forehead, "Gonna move some crap to earn your keep, girly?" Amy opened her eyes with a glare already built in, but seeing Mickey standing there with an unlit cigarette in his mouth and a shit- eatin' grin on his face, she could tell he wasn't serious. She woke up as usual with a comment on the tip of her tongue. Unfortunately, at the same time the retort was trying to pry through, the event's and rage of the past two days hit...thank God they were quickly stifled. There was nothing to worry about, these people had no power over her and wouldn't twist it if they did. They were a trusted few, so Amy's mouth opened quickly in a smile accompanied by her usual brand of sarcasm. "Oh yeah, like you guys pick anything up..." She extended an arm out to bottles, clothes and practice cables that served as a second carpet and laughed, "And I'm like...black sash in Kung-Fu 'n shit...so you'd have a hard time gettin' me up and off the bus, but I'd like to see the attempt." "Don't blame me," replied Mickey, "Livin' with three women is tough." A well- worn black Converse low- top suddenly flew out from the bunk area of the bus and hit Mickey on the side of the head. "Mornin', TJ." "Go get me some coffee, bitch," came the bassist's reply. "He's such a kidder," replied Mickey, "Goin' out for a smoke, actually. You partake?" "Never have. Something about breathing just has me addicted." Amy winked. She wasn't the sort to lecture on the topic - their bodies, not hers. With a grin came another little jab and a thought, "Hey...'s Angel awake yet, or am I the only one with the honor of seeing 8am in rock 'n roll hell?" "I think she's still asleep. And good for you - I should really quit but I doubt it'll happen... shit, Angel had to get pregnant to stop smoking and the only other one in this band who can pull that one off is Teej." He headed for the door, ignoring the repeated plea of "Coffee, bitch!" from TJ. Amy herself headed to the bunks, reading over name labels while mumbling quietly to TJ. "Y'know, coffee every morning causes cancer...or something. Then again, everything causes cancer. Except waking a heavy metal leader..." She was getting no real response but a sleepy chuckle while pulling back her hair and slipping on a pair of black framed glasses to make sure she wasn't about to poke Nine in the eye. Nope, the label definitely read 'Angel and...angel.'... And the curtain definitely flew open easily, but what startled Angel more was the hand violently shaking her shoulder. "Waaaaakey wakey!" Very swiftly, a fist connected lightly with Amy's shoulder as *Angel* made her own little note to her intruder and the other occupant of the bus - a fact that TJ was already well aquainted with. "...doesn't cause cancer, no. Just death." "Next time I'll have my rings on," mumbled Angel, "so you'd better think twice. And then once again. How'd you sleep?" She checked on MJ's pillow- blocked area, she was still asleep. "Better than I would have anywhere else without BL. Mickey played his role in having me up at the ass crack of dawn. What about you?" She was still rubbing the sleep from her eyes behind frames - somehow managing to look Ivy-esque. Amy paused a moment at that thought, she really DID hang around Miss McGinnis too often. "Thanks for letting me stay here, though. Slept better than I would on some hard hotel mattress..." "Or in a bus station, I take it," Angel rolled out of the bunk and hit the floor. She straightened out her oversized, stretched- out 'Ministry' T-shirt and cracked her neck, "Don't mind Mickey... he's been on a creative spree that usually doesn't let him sleep. Oh! Before you head back to the show, you've gotta give a listen to the rough cuts for the album... when all the songs are actually written the battle is ninety percent won." "I've been dying to hear. The only time I hear Eli ramble is when it's about you and how good everything is with the band." Amy took a seat against the small table/booth setup near the bunks, still in the same clothes she'd worn the night before. She'd change when she showered, any sooner was just extra time she'd need to spend on laundry. "So when do we get MJ up to watch her be all cute, cooing and yawning and spitting up on all of your favorite shirts?" "I dunno," replied Angel, pulling on a pair of baggy army pants, "She's training for her life as a slacker, sleeping about ninety percent of the day. And forgive me, darling, but how the hell can you see anything that goes on with those coke bottles in the way?" She winked at Amy, smiling, effectively dealing a preemptive deathblow to any of Amy's glasses- fueled rage. "She's gotta be up in like an hour for breakfast, unless she needs changing between now and then if you really wanna stick around. Soon as Troy is up we'll swing by your place so you can grab a change of clothes." "That works. If I've got rampant musicians behind me, the land lord won't refuse. And I'll have you know..." She pouted her defense, "They're...like...'emo glasses' or something. Apparently people wear fake versions to look cool, so HA! Unfortunately, I can't really read without them...so fuck you." Amy had a wink and a smile of her own to follow that up. "Bitch!" countered Angel, without a hint of malice in her voice, "So when's the fWo hitting some downtime so I can have my man back?" "I've heard some...stuff. But I don't really know..." The redhead cringed when working a few kinks out of her jaw, "The only real way to get time off right now is to get injured." "Great," replied Angel, "When's he ever slowed down when he's hurt?" Angel looked lost in thought for just a moment as a goofy grin spread across her face, She looked away as Amy tapped her on the arm. "Shaddup, I'm not gonna get all sentimental and shit." "Dooooo iiiiiiiittttt! C'mon, I'm sure TJ doesn't mind." She looked like she was about to crack, but shook it off. "Okay, enough'a that. Where's there around here to get some breeakfast?" "McDonald's, McDonald's...oh, and I hear there's this awesome place called Mickey D's..." "Great," sighed Angel, "Some of us aren't twenty anymore, Aimz... that stuff'll go right to my hips. Please tell me I didn't just say that." "Sorry...what was that, Miss 'I have a baby and weigh the same as I did nine months before within a week'? Something about gaining weight? You and I don't even have hips for it to go TO. Some of us are twenty, and those of us who are will eat at McDonald's every day until we start having kids." Angel shrugged. "I call it stress, a metabolism like a raging inferno, and being up all night with Mariella because I was terrified of making a mistake... but when she's asleep, there was little to do but work out. So take it as y'will... it's not my fault I'm naturally predisposed to being skinny. If it's the crap they sling at Mickey D's, so be it... just don't expect me to go for the Shit McMuffin." "Oh, just order a veggie burger or something stuck-up like that, you rockstar you." Amy winked - praying that her impending doom wouldn't be too painful. "Well, I am the Gothic Diva, according to Metal Edge... hey... waitasec..." Angel grabbed a hair tie from the floor and pulled her long black mane back into a ponytail as she looked at Aimz with an arched eyebrow, "Speaking of divas, aren't you gonna be playing the Garden before the Garden is?" "Ah, yeah. Martyr..." The look on the redhead's face oozed a hatred, not a hatred fueled by cruelty - but played pity. It took a second before her thoughts strung together in a sentence that didn't involve expressing her intent through words like 'Smashy smashy', but Amy managed to articulate the follow up. "That little bastard Eugene's trying to make me look like not only a crap wrestler, but some kind of skank. Try to explain this - he comes at me backstage with barrage after barrage of personal attacks verbally, got me suspended for punching him in hopes of shutting him up, and then the little fuck comes to me with love letters and pleas for dates?! I laughed in his face..." She caught her balling fists, laying them out on her thighs in an attempt to regain composure. "...and now he's running around acting like I'd lead him on. I'm not sure when beating the shit out of someone became some kind of sexual confrontation, but obviously I must've missed that staff meeting." Angel nodded, "Yeah, it's like when we opened for Anger Management and Chas spends an hour beforehand hitting on me, the entire performance talking near Troy about how we're gonna make 'em look bad, then the half hour before they even went on, doin' it all again. Men are easily intimidated animals, Aimz... do yourself a favor and detach the leech from your neck." "I've been trying to for weeks. He's fucking weird, though. He turned 'Boy meets girl, boy tries to kick shit out of girl, boy asks girl out regardless of her boyfriend and his own past behavior then gets obviously rejected' into 'Boy meets girl, girl wraps boy around her finger and leads him on, boy's soul is shattered when she leaves him'." Another moment's pause and a sip of rather stale coffee later, Amy was back to it. "He's probably the worst drama queen I've ever had the curse of meeting. And now he's running around acting all hurt and shit and I know he's just trying to fish for excuses in case I beat him at CyberSlam. Drives me fucking INSANE...he's so fucking political, can't do anything simply - he has to involve his entire life. He turned us having to work together in something resembling a feud into what, at first, was a matter of hating one another as people...then somehow that twisted around and he 'fell in love'. I'm telling you, some people's lives and jobs just aren't interesting enough so guys like Eugene fill the void with a bunch of melodramatic bullshit." Another pause and Amy'd caught herself, unfortunately at the rant's end. "I just wanna wrestle, damnit...no backstage crap." She mumbled, massaging the back of her own neck. "C'mon," said Angel, laughing and helping her friend up, "Let's collect my daughter and get some munchies... then I think we could both use a shower. To say nothing of the smokers outside." She headed for the bunks where MJ was still asleep, as Aimz pulled her shoes on and stepped toward the door. "Oh, Amy... can ya... see the door?" "Ha..ha...ha. I hate you, by the way." Within a split second, a sandal zinged by Angel's knee and Amy was out the door, leaving the 'Gothic Diva' to look back in her direction as a smile spread across her face... "Something tells me she's in a marginally better mood." [Continue...]
