The Friday Bash - Part 7 - 10:00 PM - The Codemaster

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Codemaster didn't really belong in an enviroment such as this one. I mean, come on, he's the CODEMASTER. He's the King of Codes! The Game Genie! The Blackest Brother in the Hyrule Kingdom! He inputs the codes that makes the peoples fall down! He doesn't belong in this sort of party enviroment. So, of course, the Codemaster was hanging out with two of the various ladies here, telling him some of his wild, yet absurdly fictional stories. "So, anyway, I was just huntin' along in this dungeon thing, and this here wiseman, he was all like 'Dodongo Dislikes Smoke' and whatnot. And I was like, 'Suckah, you're tripping', because I didn't know what in the hell a 'Dodongo' was, or why it disliked smoke so much. And let's face it, the only brother in the Hyrule Kingdom who probably could understand that wiseman was the negro in the green tunic." the Codemaster said to his genuinely uninterested female companions. "Um, that's great." one of them, a blonde, said. "Oh, yeah. Then there was this time that I ran into a bookcase and picked up this thing called a Book of Mudora, but then I sold it on the black market for a comb and some pomade. My hair needed the treatment at the time, you know." the Codemaster continued. "Riiiight." the other, a redhead, said. She nodded to the blonde, and the two ladies that had been listening to the Codemaster silently stood up and walked away from him. The man who has been called the Codemaster, Allen Speck, sighed in relief having gotten rid of the two women who were bugging him, possibly thinking about the size of his... ahh... "Master Sword", rather than his personality. As Speck sat back and relax on the chair that he sat in, a certain woman with frizzly, black hair sat down next to him. Her thick-framed glasses glinted off of the light in the place, she wore a goofy-looking blue Hawaiian shirt, a white tanktop underneath, and black pants. She was Allen's best friend, Leigh Landers. "You know, Allen, the whole goal of you being at this party is to, you know, getcha away from the whole 'I'm the Codemaster' thing." Leigh pointed out with a slight grin on her face. "At a party with the Fuse Bros., the Tallywhackers, Pretty Fly, and John Rocker. I'm sorry, which party are you talkin' about again?" Speck asked Landers. "Well, this one!" Leigh said. "That was a rhetorical question." Allen responded. "Well, it needed to be answered." "Those types of questions ain't the kind you answer." There was an awkward pause. "Why'd I let you talk me into coming here, anyway?" Allen Speck asked to Leigh Landers, "It's not like I ain't a social butterfly or whatever, but these guys're just killing my heat, if you know what I mean." Leigh smiled innocently at Allen, "Not really. The fans enjoyed the way you clocked the Tallywhackers at Cyberslam." "Armstrong put 'Triforce' in front of everything I did!" Speck complained. "Must you be so negative?" Leigh asked. "Must you be so positive?" Allen shot back. "Well, hey, you're the one who was talking about Zelda before I even stopped to bother you. I don't think you should take it out on me." Leigh said. Allen sighed. Somewhere in the bustle of the party, he thought he could hear Nate having a conversation with a large patch of thin air. He found this strange, and therefore went back to his conversation. "True, but I was trying to get them away from me. Not that I ain't one for a little action, or whatever..." Allen said, before he realized that he was just digging a bigger hole for himself than usual and quickly cut that train of thought off before it could reach its tunnel, "I guess I just ain't interested in action tonight. I'd rather go get smashed or somethin'." Leigh rolled her eyes. "Right. And I suppose you're going to expect me to drive you back to the hotel while you and Adam Nowell stumble around the complicated verses of 'How Dry I Am', right?" Leigh Landers asked with a smile. "Oh, GOD. Nowell's HERE?" Speck said in surprise, snapping his head in the direction of his frizzly-haired best friend. Leigh nodded and pointed in a direction. "He's over there somewhere, trying to forget that he came here." she said, "He's with some loud, lecherous Mexican who tried to hit on me... and every other woman that's even approached him." Allen Speck sighed, before he stood up from his seat. He turned his head toward the direction that Leigh had pointed at, before he looked down at his best friend, "Okay, if I come out of there drunk enough to sing Billy Joel, you're driving." Leigh smiled and nodded, "Got it." With that, Allen Speck left the room, leaving Leigh by herself. After a few moments' pause, Leigh quickly hopped up to her feet and joined Allen.
