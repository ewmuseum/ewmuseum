Oh...a gime! (Part 3)

29 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Oh...a gime! (Part 3) January 29, 2000 ::The scene opens with numerous children screaming, and running out of the "Children's Gym". The cameraman rushes into the room, and finds Jackhammer attempting to swing across the monkey bars. The camera pans over and catches Sphere getting caught in the "twirly slide":: Sphere: This is harder then I thought! Jackhammer: My, these monkey bars are low. Why, when I was a wee tyke... ::A loud "creek" is heard, Jackhammer looks up, and the monkey bars come crashing down to the ground, followed by the "twirly slide" that is currently occupied by Sphere. The two start to have a conversation underneath the rubble that is barely audible. Two muscular men enter the room, toss the rubble out of the way, and drag the Guano Apes out of the room. They lead them outside and start shouting at them:: Muscle #1: What do you think you're doing? Jackhammer: Getting thrown out into the cold by you and your partner there. Muscle #2: Are you implying that we're gay? Jackhammer: Who said anything about being gay? I called you his partner. See Sphere? Muscle #1: You mean him? ::He points to Sphere, who is chasing a squirrel, while telling it to "come here":: Jackhammer: Yes, him. He is my partner. ::The muscular men try to hold in their laughter:: Jackhammer: Do you find something comical about my appearance? Muscle #2: Fag! Jackhammer: No thank you, I do not smoke. Muscle #1: Are you getting smart with us? Jackhammer: Well, I am a member of Mensa. Muscle #1: A wise guy, 'ay? Jackhammer: Sointainly. Sphere: Nuk nuk. Muscle #2: That's it, we've had enough of your crap! Jackhammer: You wouldn't hit a man with glasses, would you? Muscle #2: Why? Jackhammer: Just a moment. ::Jackhammer reaches into this back pocket searching for something, but can't seem to find it. He turns to Sphere, who is wearing a pair of glasses:: Jackhammer: Ah, bullhickey. ::Just as Muscle #2 takes a swing at Jackhammer, a cell phone rings. Jackhammer leans a bit to the left to retrieve his phone from his pocket, causing the Muscle to trip over Jackhammer's right foot. The meathead's forehead meets an acorn on the parking lot ground, and he's out cold. Muscle #1 takes a look at events that just unfolded with astonishment as Jackhammer is speaking to someone on his phone:: Jackhammer: Miami, you say? Yes, we'll be there by tomorrow! ::Jackhammer turns to Sphere, who has grabbed Muscle #1 and proclaimed it's "time for noogies":: Jackhammer: Hey! We got a match tomorrow! In Miami! Sphere: Miami? Jackhammer: Yeah, how'd you know? Sphere: Let's go! ::Sphere releases the muscle and the two wrestlers skip to their car, ticketed now for sure, singing "A hoop da hop a do be dop Mi..am..i". The screen fades to black shortly after the camera catches the one muscular man running away screaming while the aforementioned squirrel is trying to retrieve it's acorn from under the unconscious man's head::
