Daddy Day Care

14 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Dadddyyyy!” Triple X had left the building. When he was at home – well, not technically home, but amongst his family – and, the little guy came calling, he was simply Sean Stevens, father. “Did you do it, did you do it?” Stevens rushed into the bathroom, eyes bulging in excitement. “I did it, daddy! I did it!” Shannon Joseph clapped his hands as he jumped off of the toilet. “Let daddy examine.” Cautiously, Sean approached the toilet, extending his neck to look inside. “Hmmm, the water's pretty clear, buddy ... are you sure you used the potty or are you pulling daddy's leg?” Sean and Ivy's son poked out his bottom lip, melting his father's heart. “I promise.” “Good enough for me!” Sean said, rushing his son, lifting him in the air. “I'm so proud of you, wait 'til I tell your mother!” “YEAH!” Shannon raised his arms in the air. “I did it! Tell mommy! Tell mommy!” “Hold your horses, buddy. What did daddy tell you comes next after you use the bathroom?” “You said I should wash my hands,” Shannon replied, as he walked toward his stool, directly in front of the bathroom sink, as his father turned on the cold water for him. Stevens went into he and Ivy's bedroom and grabbed his cell phone, pressing the number one, for Poison Ivy on speed dial. “Momma Bear, guess what?” “......” “No, I'm not on my way to the arena just yet, but don't worry about that, this is even better! Shannon used the potty!” “......” “I know, me too! He's growing up right before our eyes, sweetie, and the words are just flowing now, I'm telling you, I was almost in tears.” Just then, Shannon entered the room. “Can I talk to mommy, King daddy?” “I know you're busy, Ivy, but he wants to speak to you.” Sean hands his son the phone, “Hello, Queen mommy. Guess what?!” “......” “Nope! I did it all by myself! I'm a big boy now! Tell MJ mkay!?!” “......” “Okay, I love you too! See you later!” Shannon hung the phone up just like most little kids do, without handing it back to the other adult in the room for a proper goodbye. Glancing down at his little pride and joy, Sean couldn't help but smile. He was getting soft. “Alright buddy, let's get ready to get you something to wear, so daddy can go to work on time before mommy spanks him!” Obviously not aware that his father was joking, Shannon frowned. “Daddy's get into trouble, too?” “Big trouble,” Stevens responded. “C'mon, let's get you a bath.” “Aw man!” The blue-eyed badass laughed as he turned on the bath water, and his son, as if on cue, ran in the opposite direction. It was going to be a very long day.
