Mojo Risin' Chapter Two: Riders On the Storm "No." It's been like this for hours. "No fuckin' way." The hot sun barely beaten back by the windflow with the top down, the radio playing at a decent volume-- "I said no. Seriously. Tell 'em to fuck off." --and the Psycho Bitch, ranting on the phone. Next to her, Eli Flair glared at the phone from behind his sunglasses. "Brian," continued Ivy, "listen to me very closely, little bro. Yeah, but I'm more mature, so shaddup. Smythe and Cage do not get any of the kids to wrestle for OCW." Silence. Eli knew what was going on on the other end. Brian Cage and Frank Smythe had certainly not made a friend with Ivy when they pulled off their OCW coup at Meltdown. And they'd contacted Ivy's brother Brian to see if any of the kids who regularly trained at Coop's Gym were either ready or willing to wrestle a few dates for the renegade OCW promotion. Cage had called Brian instead of Ivy because he knew he had a chance with Brian. Ivy was one to hold a grudge. And she never forgave them for not believing in the principles Silver Fox had instilled in the rest of the fWo organization about loyalty and family. To her way of thinking, they saw the family was in trouble and up and ran. With Fox still missing for whatever reason, she considered herself responsible for holding the family together, even if the more unruly and unpleasant members of said family needed to discuss how overbearing and annoying she was. As long as they could agree on something. Eli could just smirk. Cage and Smythe should've known. "Brian," continued Ivy, "Seriously, kid. Don't even think about lettin' Knox go there. They want to get a hold'a my kids, Cage's gotta talk to me. I mean really talk to me." "Ivy," said Eli, "Couldn't you've done this before we left Kingman?" Ivy ignored him. "I ain't gonna say it again, Brian." "Ivy," repeated Eli, "Can you please turn the phone off? Between your hot air and the blazing sun, I don't think I can take much more'a this." Ivy finally glared at him. "I'll talk to you later, Brian. Remember... Orlando gets nothing from us. Nothing." She clicked off the phone with a sharp exhale, but continued to hold it in her hands. "Sorry," said Ivy without a hint of remorse in her voice, "but someone's gotta look after the kids." "The kids are seventeen to twenty two," reminded Eli, "and Brian's doing a good job. And for that matter, your feud with those two aside, they've never done wrong by you. Or me." Ivy shook her head. Her blonde hair would have been blowing around wildly if not for the tight ponytail holding it all back. "We were committed to the fWo. And besides, they started it with that Survivor stunt." "Want some cheese to go with that whine?" "Asshole." "I'm serious, Ivy. Fox was down and out, Ted was drinking his life away, and you were completely overwhelmed. They did exactly what you were doing, trying to give a chance to some kids who have the tools to make it. The Mouse is running the show, don't'cha think some'a the people that built this company would have a chip on their shoulder about it?
