Respect (6 - Pregame 2)

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Hey, good luck tonight, kid...you're gonna need it. When Stevens is done with you, you're gonna be a smear on the mat." As I made my way through the security checkpoints, even the overly opinionated security guard couldn't bother me. I merely nodded my head at him and smiled. No, with what I had planned for tonight, there was no way I was going to drop another one to Sean Stevens. I had gone back to the hotel last night, filled the tub up with warm water, and sat reflecting on last night's match and what I had to do differently in this one to pick up the win. In the Twilight match, I'd focused too much on wrestling, and it had left Triple X with enough left to counter Knightfall and win the match. At Countdown 3, I'd tried to be a power wrestler and just pummel him into submission, but that hadn't been successful either. With the nature of this match and the tendencies I had observed in Stevens, I knew I'd need a fusion of both to make this my match to shine. The major disadvantage I had going into this match was that I had no idea just how good of a submission wrestler Sean was. Eli had made mention to me yesterday about not underestimating him in this match, and I think Eli would most definitely be the man to trust in that regard. After all, he'd wrestled Sean more times than I'd even seen him wrestle, and that's not counting all the times he'd watched Sean in CSWA, CWL, and who knows how many other feds. The major advantage I possessed was my size and the lack of injuries I've sustained in my career. My arms, legs, and torso were thicker than Sean's, which meant that it'd be a lot harder for him to manipulate them into the painful positions, and the fact that I'd never had a major injury other than the broken wrist in college should make it harder for him to cause enough pain that I'd actually submit. Of course, I wasn't ruling out the possibility, but I was going to do everything in my power to make sure that it didn't happen. I also had an ace up my sleeve, something to even the odds psychologically. There I was, just sitting in the tub, and the realization had smacked in the face with the force of Stevens' fists earlier in the night. Knowing that I'd had no luck to this point in keeping Trips out of my head, I knew that I'd have to do something early to get inside his. Frustrate him, get him to make a mistake, and that'd be my golden opportunity. I knew he wasn't going to be a pushover, but I was confident that tonight would be my night. I'd have to bust my ass out there, but that was a foregone conclusion coming into the match, wasn't it? The most important thing would be getting to Sean, all while not letting him get to me. He could stretch me until I was unable to walk, but I'd be damned if I'd let him into my head again, and I'd be further damned if I let him win another one from me. No, tonight was definitely going to be my night, and Sean Stevens would just have to deal with that fact.
