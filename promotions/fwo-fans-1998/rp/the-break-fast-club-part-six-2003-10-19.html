The Break Fast Club (part six)

19 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Steve-O, Evil-Lyn, Nate, and Funpants are sitting, in that order, on the railing. Nate: Funpants? Funpants: What? Nate: Are you gonna write your paper? Funpants: No. Nate: Well it'd be a waste for all of us to write a paper. That's why I say we roll them up and GET wasted! Steve-O: Totally! Funpants: You should really come up with something else to say. It gets REAL annoying after a while. Okay, after the first time. Nate: Dude, ain't you the smartest? Funpants: You have to ASK? Nate: Dude, I trust you. I mean...your name is Senor! How can you not trust a dude named Senor? Steve-O: Totally! Funpants realizes he can't hit Steve-O with number twelve, so he simply picks up a nearby stapler and throws it at his head. Funpants: Alright, I'll do it. Funpants looks at Evil-Lyn and winks at her. Evil-Lyn recoils in disgust, and then Nate looks at her. Evil-Lyn: Uh...what? Nate: Come on. Nate jumps off the railing, grabs Evil-Lyn, and pulls her off the railing. Evil-Lyn: Where are we going? Nate and Evil-Lyn are in another part of the library, and Nate is trying to put eye shadow on Evil-Lyn. Evil-Lyn: What are you doing? Nate: Is it SAFE? Evil-Lyn: You're gonna stick that RIGHT in my eye! Nate: Is it SAFE? Evil-Lyn: No! It isn't! And I'm already WEARING eye shadow! Nate: You are? I thought you just, like, naturally had black stuff around your eyes. Nate tries to apply eye shadow to Evil-Lyn one more time. Evil-Lyn: Squeak. Cut back to Funpants and Steve-O. Funpants is thinking about what to write, and Steve-O is just...being Steve-O. Funpants reaches over and punches Steve-O in the mouth just because. Number twelve. Back to Evil-Lyn and Nate, who is holding his crotch. Evil-Lyn: Sorry. I don't care if that was your way of being nice to me. Back to Funpants and Steve-O. Funpants is writing, and Steve-O is drawing a bunch of circles on one of the tables, possibly attempting to hypnotize himself. Cut to the closet that BQ is sitting in. Nate opens the door. BQ: You lost? Nate: Uh...yeah. Nate closes the closet door and leaves. Cut back to the library, where Evil-Lyn is walking toward Funpants and Steve-O. Steve-O: Wow, like, you look all hot. Funpants looks up from the paper and at Evil-Lyn. He then turns to Steve-O. Funpants: Moron. She doesn't look any different. Steve-O: I know. Dude, she's the only chick here. I need something to keep my dingy afloat. Cut to some random room, where Nate has spotted the rat again, this time playing a keyboard. Nate: Bogus. Cut to the door of the closet that BQ's in. His voice can be heard through the door. BQ: I wonder if Claire's are as nice as Evil-Lyn's. Back to the library, where Funpants has finished the paper. Steve-O is talking to Evil-Lyn. Steve-O: So what happened to you? Evil-Lyn: Excuse me? Steve-O: How'd they get so big? Evil-Lyn: That's not something you should ask a woman. Steve-O: Hey, I'm looking at your face, right? Doesn't that count? Evil-Lyn doesn't bother to tell him that he's looking at her chest. Evil-Lyn: I guess. A little. Cut to the hallway, where all five of them are walking. They're met by Evil Smokey, sweeping up. Evil Smokey: Goodbye, brother Funpants. Funpants: Bite me. Evil Smokey raises an eyebrow. Funpants: Or, no...don't. Funpants picks up the pace and starts walking faster. BQ: See you tomorrow. Evil Smokey: Most definitely, young Quicksilver. Cut to the parking lot, where Funpants wastes no time in hopping into his car and driving off. Steve-O leans toward Evil-Lyn, waiting for a kiss. Evil-Lyn: Eh, what the hell. Evil-Lyn turns Steve-O's head to the side and gives him a quick smooch on the cheek. Steve-O: BOIIIIIING. As Evil-Lyn walks to her car, Nate attempts to try the same thing with BQ. BQ shrugs, backs up, and hits Nate with the SuperQuick-SilverKick, then walks away. Evil-Lyn pulls out of the parking lot, leaving only Steve-O and a semi-conscious Nate. They don't have cars, so they'll probably be here for awhile. Cut back to the library, where Sphere picks up Funpants' essay and begins to read it. Funpants voice is heard while the essay is being read. Funpants: Dear Dickbrain...we refuse to accept the fact that we had to sacrifice a whole Friday in detention for whatever it was we did wrong...and we sure as hell think you're crazy to make an essay telling you who we think we are. You see us as you want to see us. In the simplest terms, in the most convenient definitions. Because that's the kind of guy you are. You're possibly the biggest jackoff in the history of mankind. Cut to the football field, where BQ is walking toward..."us"...as Funpants' monologue continues. Funpants: And you were right. Each of us is nothing but an asshole... Steve-O: ...and a stoner... Evil-Lyn: ...and a psycho-bitch whose sexual preferences continue to be a mystery... Nate: ...and...uh...another stoner... BQ: ...and a jobber. Funpants: Does that answer your question? If it doesn't, then screw you, and I hope you die a miserable death. Insincerely yours, the group of rejects that couldn't think of a clever name to call themselves, since we all don't like each other anyway. Well, I don't like them, but...whatever. Cut to BQ walking across the football field as he thrusts his fist into the air in a silent cheer and freezes there. "Won't you come see about me I'll be alone dancing, you know it, baby Tell me your troubles and doubts Giving me everything inside and out And love's strange: so real in the dark Think of the tender things That we were working on Slow change may pull us apart When will I get any of your heart, baby? Don't you forget about me Don't, don't, don't, don't Don't you forget about me Will you stand above me Look my way, never love me Rain keeps falling Rain keeps falling Down, down, down Will you recognize me Call my name or walk on by Rain keeps falling Rain keeps falling Down, down, down, down Don't you try and pretend It's my beginning We'll win in the end I won't harm you Or touch your defenses Vanity, insecurity Don't you forget about me I'll be alone dancing, you know it, baby Going to take you apart I'll build us back together at heart, baby Don't you forget about me Don't, don't, don't, don't Don't you forget about me As you walk on by Will you call my name As you walk on by Will you call my name When you walk away Don't you forget about me Don't, don't, don't, don't Don't you forget about me Don't you forget about me Don't, don't, don't, don't Don't you forget about me Oh, will you walk away Will you walk on by Come on, call my name Will you call my name..."
