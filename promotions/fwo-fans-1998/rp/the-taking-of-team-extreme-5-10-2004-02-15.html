The Taking of Team Extreme Chapter Five December 13th, 2003 Salt Lake City, Utah 2:21 PM local time "You what?" "We kissed." Angel lay back in her bunk, one foot dangling outside the privacy curtain, as she listened to Ivy stutter over the phone. It was quite an unusual happening - the unflappable Ivy McGinnis at a loss for words. "So, s'more details, girl," insisted Angel, "You know how it is on the road - you live in a vacuum." She rubbed her daughter's head gently as she listened to Ivy take a long sip of what was most definitely day-old coffeesludge. "We were at this sushi place, drinking sake and just BSing about whatever, and it just... happened." "So what, then? Are you two--" "No! Angel, jeez," interrupted Ivy, "That'd be like hooking up with Eli... just too incestuous for words." Angel laughed. "No offense, of course." "Of course." "So what do I do then?" asked Ivy, "I don't know how to talk to Alex. I don't know how to relate to him anymore after this... I really fucked up." "Bull." "Seriously." "Has he said anything?" "Nope. He doesn't want to talk to me." "Do you want to talk to him?" "Of course. I'm just afraid--" "Isn't it possible he's just as afraid?" "Maybe." "Screw maybe. Think about it. You're one of his best friends, you're the ex of another one of his best friends. Feelings flared, something happened... it's not the end of the world. I guarantee, you go talk to him or you'll regret it." "I already regret it." Angel thought about it for a moment, and dove straight into Ivy's brain. "What do you regret? Was it a bad kiss?" "No, not at all." "Did he force it?" "Nope." "Did you force it?" "I don't think so." "Were you wishing it was Trip?" Ivy sighed. "I... felt like I was cheating on him, but we're not together. And I was glad it was Alex, but it also felt like we were cheating our friendship." "So tell him that." "If it was that easy, Angel..." "It is." "Maybe. How's my boy?" "Eli's surviving... not really happy with what's goin' on with the business but he's pretending like he believes Fox and company have a plan." "Fox is a bit occupied." "What's up?" "Meeting with the old man next week. I don't know what it's about but I've had some feelers, think there's gonna be some kind'a change in policy or even in ownership. Disney's backed off lately which either means our relationship with them is about t'come to a very abrupt end--" "--or they're pulling back in order to regroup to take more direct control," finished Angel. She sensed Ivy smiling through the phone, she'd taught the Gothic Diva a good deal about the way businesses work on this level. "In either case," continued Ivy, "Don't worry about it... even if someone shut the place down, we'd probably just up-and-move elsewhere. What company in the world wouldn't like to gut this place for all the talent they can get?" "True." "But anyways, I know you guys've got a show tonight... go get ready. We'll talk when you're home again." "Talk to him, Ivy," insisted Angel once again, "Trust me on that." Ivy smiled. "I'll let'cha know."
