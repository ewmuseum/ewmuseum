Always Coca Cola, dammit!

16 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Always Coca Cola, dammit! February 16, 2000 Backstage Machina looked at the Coca Cola representative in disgust. A mess? A disaster? Did you see the same show I did? Double S handed Machina a towel and rubbed the giant red welt on his head. Machina dabbed at the Coke that had been sprayed all over him, and groaned as the towel stuck to his costume. Joy of cola my blue ass... have you seen such a sticky bunch of goo in your life? The Coke representative started to walk away, and Machina thought fast. He grabbed the shoulder of the man, his hand sticking to the wool suit he was wearing. Machina smiled. The man wasn't going anywhere now, by god. Now that he had his attention, it was time to sell. Machina fixed his face with the trademark Machina charm. The representative saw his face move, and looked strangely at him. Are you choking on something? Machina stepped back, his hand still stuck to the representative's jacket. No... this is my serious face. The representative scratched his head. You look like you're choking on something. Machina was indignant. Well I'm not. Now listen, maybe things out there could have gone better... I mean, the whole getting sprayed with Pepsi thing maybe wasn't the best image for a Coke sponsorship... but did you notice that I was gagging on it? That's a good message isn't it? Gagging on Pepsi? Everyone will want to drink Coke if Pepsi makes you gag... well, maybe the anorexics will want the gagging cola... but other than that... of course, the Trucker certainly doesn't look like he's thrown up or even missed a meal any time in the last... The representative stopped him with a raised hand. Stop you babbling fool. Listen, the Coca Cola company wanted to get into wrestling, and wrestling we have gotten into. But we can't afford to have these shenanigans associated with our product, or with our company. So good day sir. Machina thought quickly. eTour brings you sites that match your interests so you can search without typing! This was no good. Machina thought faster. A flag match! No... I mean a cola match! The Coca Cola representative stopped trying to pry Machina's hand off his jacket and listened. Machina saw that he had an edge and pushed for the advantage. This is what we will do... we take a bottle of Coke and a bottle of Pepsi to the ring. We suspend each up above a turnbuckle on opposite ends of the ring. Then the one who captures the bottle wins... I'll be sure to capture the Coke bottle... because the Trucker is too fat and there is no way he can climb! Think about it... a victory over your arch-rival in front of millions of fans! The Coca Cola representative considered for a moment, and then smiled. He ripped Machina's hand off his jacket and started to walk away. He was halfway down the hall before he turned around and nodded. Very well, Machina. Do it. But if you lose... you will never see another check from us again. Now good day sir, and... The representative looked at Sid with a certain level of confusion. ...and you. The representative walked away as Machina turned to Sid. Okay... we've got to win that match. Where is Rudo... we could use his help for this. Sid pointed at a nearby wall. Rudo's backside could be seen, as could his legs and feet. The rest of him was hidden from view by a large hole in the wall. Machina put his hands on his hips. Huh. That shouldn't be. Machina wandered away in search of Penny, as Double S wandered away in search of Hellrazor...
