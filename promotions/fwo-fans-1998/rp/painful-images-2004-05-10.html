Painful Images

10 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CyberSlam VII was over for me and Cody. As I looked down at Cody with EMTs surrounding him, I also realized that professional wrestling may have been over for him. I walked down the stairs from the stage and pushed through the mob of concerned officials and wrestlers until I got to Cody. He was on the floor, convulsing and coughing up blood like he was a human water fountain. It reminded me of a dark time in my life. That one night when my blood spilled across the floor and the bones in my body were crushed. I felt all the pain come back again as I knelt down next to him. I took Cody's hand. My head bowed down in shame. I shouldn't have let this happen. My job was to protect him. I shouldn't have gone to the back when he told me to. "Cody," I said, "it's going to be all right. You're gonna be fine." He tried to talk to me, but all that came out were coughs of blood. Ringside fans were crying at the sight of this. Even though he was a supposed 'bad guy' to these fans, they still sympathized with him. Back when I was in this same situation, the fans were cheering. They were howling. They told me to bleed to death. And I still hear those screams, even now. They pierce my ears. Cody's fall off the FanTron was an act of bravery. He did it to prove to the fWo management that he had the balls to do anything that'll pop a cheer. Prior to this fall, management was doubtful about his ability to take chances in the ring. Well, now he's potentially paralyzed. How's that for risky, you stupid fucks? After sitting in the waiting area for two hours, the doctors came out and told me that Cody needs to rest for the night and that I should go home. I sighed and decided to call it a night. I went to bed feeling uneasy. Although I beat Aimz that night, I knew that it was far from over. From my experience as a cage fighter, I came to learn that one match doesn't mean shit. As long as the person you hate is still standing, you're still not safe. Aimz came to haunt me in my dreams. Every pleasant dream - sipping a Corona on a beach in Mexico, driving a Ferrari down an open road in Italy - was interrupted by Amy's hands coming around my neck and strangling me. My body twisted and turned. I woke up twice from sleep apnea, coughing and hacking for air. At 3AM I stopped going back to sleep. I put on a robe and went outside of the pool house. I sat down and dipped my feet in the pool, letting my head sink forward into my hands. I thought living with Cody would be simple. All I had to do was protect him and collect my cash. But now it's not that simple. Amy kept pushing the right buttons and I ended up signing a contract with the fWo. Now I'm a wrestler, whether I want to be or not. Every time I see Aimz, her image brings back memories I could do without. She reminds me so much of that dreadful place. I don't ever want to go back there, but battling back and forth with Aimz is like being there all over again. My tears start to drip into the pool. I cry a lot, because I'm a woman who has to put a tough face on everyday. I don't like thinking about the past, but it's hard not to. The past shapes who we are today, and regardless of what we do, it'll always be a part of us.
