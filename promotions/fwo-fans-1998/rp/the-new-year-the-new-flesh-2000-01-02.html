The New Year, The New Flesh

2 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization The New Year, The New Flesh January 2, 2000 ((The scene goes to whats looking to be at Torontos CN Tower, as its around the morning as the sun is just coming up in Downtown Toronto. Not many people are inside, as the place is going to be getting ready for a busy day. But the camera spots one person buying a ticket on opening hours, as hes wearing Black Cargo Pants, and a Grey Bubblecoat. The face is definatly Eron the Relentless. The camera follows him all the way into the elevator, as it looks as if Eron doesnt notice. He turns around and does, as he looks a bit relieved, but also angry)) Eron the Relentless:Oh well look who survived the new Year. Hey Geoff. Geoff Green:Umm Hi. Eron the Relentless:So Look at this view of Toronto. Going up this elevator? Aint it great? I would just love to see you floating in the air, skin ripping right off of you due to the strong winds this high. That would be so great to watch...anyways, what did you do in the New Year Bitch Boy? Geoff Green:Bitch boy? Eron the Relentless:Its my new nickname for you okay? You are bitch boy now. You are my full time interviewer, no more of the other fruitcakes doing YOUR job. So now, bitch boy, what did you do on New Years? Geoff Green: ::sighs:: I was with my wife. We had a great time ringing in the New Year watching CNN. Eron the Relentless:Hahaha, no really what did you do? With your type of job I cant see anyway you getting a wife. Oh well, Wanna know what I did? Geoff Green:Well, actually n... Eron the Relentless:COURSE YA DO BITCH BOY! Well, I invited a couple old friends of mine from high School, most specifically 7 girls and 2 guys, amazing how 4 of those girls now work for Vivid Dolls. Anyways, we spent the new Year drinking some Vodka, getting drunk, then congradulating me on how Im STILL International Champion. Geoff Green:Oh really. Eron the Relentless:Oh really? Alright Geoff... ((Eron unzips his coat, as he has the fWo International Title still around his waist.)) Eron the Relentless:You idiot, did I lose it to Oracle? NNnnnno. I kicked his old feeble ass from one side of the ring, to the next, under the ring, over the ring, sailing the 7 seas. I showed him WHY I deserved the International Title, why he deserves to still be unknown in the Roster, and why I am the most respected figure, in the entire Entertainment Industry. Period. Geoff Green:You barely beat Oracle. Eron the Relentless:Geoff, it was an act. I had to AT LEAST make him look good, ya know, put him over as net nerds say. So I put on a play, where he was feeling reality. I took the ladder, shoved it down his throat, and then climbed up it, grabbed my property, and never let the criminal steal it. Its that simple bitch boy. Geoff Green:STOP CALLING ME... ((The elevator made a ping sound, as they were at the top floor now. Eron got out and walked over to one of the balconys as he looked out the window. Geoff followed, looking pretty angry holding the camera, while hissing)) Eron the Relentless:Oh come on Bitch boy, lighten up. At least Im not calling you George Guano. Unless, you really do, want to be called George Guano. Geoff Green:No, call me Geoff... Eron the Relentless:So when are you gonna ask me about Countdown? Geoff Green:What? oh grr...What was Countdown Like to you? Eron the Relentless:Interesting. Geoff Green:Interesting? Thats it? Eron the Relentless:Yeah, end of interview. No Bitch Boy, I was pausing. You interrupted me. Now the next time you do that, Im gonna grab your nappy hair, pick you up, throw you into a small metal cage, and mail you to Somalia where you can make many Churches proud by feeding the poor! Got that? Good. Geoff Green:Yes... Eron the Relentless:Okay, Countdown, I was watching in the back usually, I only came out during the celebration with Black Panther. I was watching all that was happening, on the last day with Joys funeral and all, there were some mixed emotions. I dont know why Black Panther was chosen, I had to put actually caring in the belt he first held, and now, Im the International Champion, and hes World Champion. My how things change for the worse. Geoff Green:The worse? Eron the Relentless:I'd rather keep the INAT belt, now World. Who cares if you're World Champion? Geoff Green:EVERYONE DOES! Its the pinnacle of manys careers! Primetime,Jim Dudley,Scott Slugger, many wrestlers, would tear their heart out for just one more reign! Eron the Relentless: ::yawns:: well, it proves Wizards First Rule. Geoff Green:Whats that? Eron the Relentless:People are stupid. Its also Erons First Rule. You know whats my second? Geoff Green:No. Eron the Relentless:Its the man who always does my interviews is always named Bitch Boy as a compliment. Geoff Green:WHAT!? A COMPLIMENT!? Eron the Relentless:YEAH! Do you know how many teenagers watching this interview right now curled up in front of their television eating Eggos would LOVE to be called bitch boy by me? Geoff Green:None. Eron the Relentless:Geoff, go f(BEEP!) yourself. Now, whats my next match for the new year. Geoff Green: ::sighs again:: we dont know yet. Eron the Relentless:Stop sighing Bitch boy! Geoff Green:Thats it, Mr."Im going to end the Millenium since Im International Champion" if you call me bitch boy ONE MORE TIME I mean it im going to drop this camera and NOT do one more interview for you. Got it? Eron the Relentless:You DO NOT remind ANYONE of Bad angles GOT IT BI...GEOFF? Geoff Green:Are we to an agreement? Eron the Relentless:Yes. I guess. Geoff Green:Fine. I have never been so Insulted in my life. Eron the Relentless:Thats what your wife told me when you said "I do" correct? Alright, just let the camera roll Geoff. fWo, its a new year, no not a New Millenium, thats not til NEXT year, I challenge anyone. Absolutly anyone. If anyone believes their half the man I am, you will come to the ring, on a House Show, on Slaughter, on Warfare, hell, even Flashback, and accept it. I will take on absolutly anyone, from War, to Showstopper, to Briana. Actually, I'd prefer Briana, but anyways, just someone accept, you may get to sniff a real Title! And whoever accepts, take a breath...of FRESH DEATH! ((Geoff spins fast around and begins to leave, as you can faintly hear Eron begin to cough, but he says "bitch boy" while doing it as the camera fades to black))
