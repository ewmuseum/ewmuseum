The Untold Story(Part 4)

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sean, this is Eli. I was hoping I could catch you... but, obviously you're away. Hit me back as soon as you get this message. It's urgent. I just got a call from Ivy. She's on her way out here. She didn't give me any details, and she was too stubborn to listen to anything. She sounded upset, kid. I don't know what went on between you guys... but, I figured whatever it is... it HAS to be involving you, because your relationship is about the only thing Ivy's sensitive about, these days. Do yourself a favor, Sean. Get up here. Later, kid. ****** It had been about two hours since Sean had left Reagan National. And, in that time... he took the opportunity to think. He listened to the message left on his cell phone by his good friend, Eli Flair, almost one hundred times... and, pinched himself nearly one thousand times, hoping, praying, that this all was a dream. How, in a matter of two days, could your life go from blistfully happy, to ruined? Sean wondered. At first... he simply chalked it up to Karma. But, that certainly wasn't the case... because, compared to who he used to be... and, some of the things he used to do... Sean was an angel. Three- hundred- sixty degrees the opposite. And, for his earlier sins... as a young, cold, careless, party animal, with a boatload of money... he had already paid dearly, in terms of his career progression. The only difference between Sean Steven today, and Sean Stevens yesterday, was that the Sean Stevens yesterday didn't think. As far as talent? Sadly... he is no better now than he was then. And, that in itself is more of a testiment to how natural and gifted a performer he was upon entering this business. Stevens - "Excuse me... how much longer am I gonna have to wait? I've been here for nearly an hour... I called and scheduled for my rental EARLY yesterday." Stevens rolled his eyes, as the man behind the cashier desk began punching some numbers on his machine. Stevens pulled out his cell phone again, and attempted to dial Poison Ivy's cell phone. No answer. Cashier - "Sir... we don't have the vehicle you rented. The couple who rented it three days ago are late." Sean hissed... giving the cashier a cold stare, that made him uncomfortable. Cashier - "...but, if you'd like... We can provide you with another vehicle." He glanced down at the screen for a moment. "A Lincoln Towncar. Very spaciou--" Stevens - "-- I know what it is..." Cashier - "...and, because of the misunderstanding... the first two days... are free of charge." Stevens - "I'll take it. When can I get it... I have somewhere I need to be, is all." Cashier - "We have one clean and ready now." Stevens - "Thanks." ****** Eli Flair and Poison Ivy were sitting on his sofa, silently, inside his apartment when they heard a knock at the door. It was rushed... hard... frantic, even. Flair immediately knew who it was... Ivy had no clue. She was too frustrated to care. Too hurt to and concerned about her own problems to even PRETEND she cared. Flair got up and opened the door... ...as Sean Stevens charged in, walking full speed, directly past him... and, toward Ivy, who was just now starting to realize what was going on. Stevens - "Ivy... you've gotta liste--" Slap. Sean didn't retaliate, though. Didn't even DREAM of it. Not only because Poison Ivy was a woman... but, because she was HIS woman... and, he... was a changed man. Ivy swung again. And, again. And, Sean... he just stood there... taking it... each blow, harder than the last. Red marks slowly appearing on his face and neck. Finally... Ivy stopped. She was tired. And, Sean embraced her. She tried to wiggle her way free. But, to no avail. And, for the first time, ever... Ivy was being held against her will... by a much stronger man... and, Eli Flair didn't budge. He knew Sean would never hurt her. And, he also knew... that for him to fly all the miles he flew, at the frantic pace, in which he did... ... It HAD to be love. Poison Ivy - "G- Get off of me, Sean." The tears were really rolling now. As Ivy tried to wiggle free again. "Sean... get off of me." She said again. But, Stevens just stood there... at a loss for words. He knew in order to make this right, he HAD to say something to Ivy... but, he didn't know what to say. So he just stood there... holding her... and, eventually... she gave in. ****** The next morning. Stevens - "Ivy... we need to talk..." Stevens gently woke Ivy from her sleep, rubbing her shoulder. The night before had been one of the longest nights of his life. He could hardly sleep in fear of the next day. TODAY. Ivy - as tough as she is - fell asleep from exhaustion, after spending most of the night crying. How could this be happening to her again? She would often say, feeling sorry for herself. She was right, though. Poison Ivy had been through so much crap in the relationship department, that she spent an awful lot of her time, working and alone. She TRULY believed Sean loved her... he had too... all the crap that they went through just to GET together was proof enough. Somewhere in the back of her mind... she KNOWS he still does. And, always will. But, for the most part... her mind is elsewhere. Focusing on the letter she obviously wasn't supposed to read. "Can you just sit there for a moment? Give me a chance to explain, Ivy... please." She nodded. Go on. "Look... I'm sorry about all of this. I'm just as new to this as YOU are, and I apologize for not being any good at it." Poison Ivy - "I'm NOT gonna feel sorry for you, Sean. I'd appreciate it if you were straight with me. Don't list my qualities... or traits... in some attempt to butter me up. I just want to know two things... who is she? And, why wasn't I told anything about her?" Sean swallowed hard. He wasn't trying to make Ivy feel sorry for him, nor was he trying to butter her up... but, her defenses were up full force. And, Stevens realized he had to take what he could get. No matter how little she gave. Stevens - "Linda. ...her name's Linda, Ivy. She's from the SCW. And, the reason why you haven't heard anything, Ivy... is because there ISN'T anything." Poison Ivy - "That's not what the letter I read said. And, it's not what you TRULY believe. Otherwise, you wouldn't have went to the measures you went to dispose of it." Stevens - "So what are you saying, Ivy? That I don't love you? That I have feelings for somebody else? If that's the case... please TELL me why in the hell I'm out here, when I could be out cutting promo's or even WRESTLING, making money? Tell me why all my free time is spent with YOU, either in person or on the phone?" Sean turned her face toward his... Ivy allowed it. Meaning, some of what he was saying was registering. "Look at me, Ivy. We've been through a LOT. I LOVED you LONG before Linda was in the picture, and the ONLY reason she was even in the picture was because you wouldn't give me the time of day. Remember? CWL. Telling me to f#ck- off politely?" Poison Ivy - "Don't put this on me, Sean. And, don't try and put this whole situation on my back. I didn't know what I wanted. I was confused. YOU knew that!" Stevens - "Yeah, I knew... And, I had to move on... how was I supposed to know you'd choose me over Hornet? I was just trying to prevent myself from being hurt." Poison Ivy - "I just have one question, Sean... where is this going? With her, I mean? Are you gonna write her back? Go and see her? What? And, how am I to know you won't re-discover your past feelings for her, and leave me." Sean embraced Ivy. And, she let him. Stevens - "Because, I've NEVER felt how I feel with you. I've never loved ANYONE how I love you. All I'm asking is you believe me." Sean released the embrace... and, looked Ivy directly into the eyes... a tear fell. But, Sean was there to wipe it away. He leaned in... and, kissed Ivy. At first gently... but, both were emotional... and, it soon became aggressive... passionate... a real 'I love you' kiss. And, that's how they'd spend the night. In each others arms. Tomorrow was tomorrow... and, it was true... they did both feel there relationship was at a vulnerable state. But, for now... they were living for the moment. Together.
