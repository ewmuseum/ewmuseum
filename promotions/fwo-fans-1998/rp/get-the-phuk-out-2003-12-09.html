Beads of sweat ran up David's cheeks, past his eyes, and dripped off his forehead to the floor below. Gravity gone wrong? Sweat dripping up? Nah, David Cross was just upside down. In the midst of handstand pushups, to be exact. He'd been at it for the past ten minutes, a fact that came as a shock to him. "What kind of herbs did you put in that tea?" David asked as he continued to lower himself to the floor. Michelle Hiroshi's green eyes sparkled mischievously. "I can't tell you that," she said with a sly grin. She leaned back on the hotel bed and clicked the remote. "It's an ancient family secret. If I told you that, I'd have to kill you." "Doesn't really matter, does it? Your father's going to kill me anyhow, once he finds out that you're with me." David dropped into a forward roll and sprung to his feet. He toweled the sweat off his chest and walked into the bathroom. It was like looking at a stranger in the mirror. He almost didn't recognize the body he saw. Whatever was in that tea was doing wonders for his stamina and physique. In two weeks, he'd put on a good five pounds of muscle. Given the meals Michelle was making for him, David wasn't surprised he was gaining weight. The fact that it was muscle, though, was a pleasant shock. He couldn't help but flex. "All natural. How many guys in the fWo can make that claim?" Click. Click. Click. David could hear Michelle cycling through the channels like a pro. "Don't worry about my father," Michelle called out to him. "I can't see him starting to care about me after all this time." David poked his head out of the bathroom. "He does care. Really. It's going to cost me my testicles, he cares so much." Michelle blew him a kiss. "That's too bad. I've grown to like your testicles." She turned back to the television. "And let your hair grow out, for crying out loud. I know people who would kill to have a head of hair like yours." Life is good David thought to himself as he splashed water on his face. My career is picking up, and I'm traveling with a beautiful woman that I l- He looked at his face in the mirror. I really do love her. That's... wow. A single knock at the door caught David's attention. He walked out of the bathroom and looked through the spy hole. An angry-looking Asian man stood in the hallway. David threw the door open. "Wang! Hey Michelle, Wang is here!" Tzu Ni stared at Cross with a look of disgust. Michelle walked up behind David and looked over his shoulder, hugging him around the stomach from behind. "Tzu Ni, what are you doing here?" she said with a smile. The angry man looked much less angry as he addressed the woman in front of him. He bowed deeply. "It is good to see you are well. I am here on Clan business." David looked confused as Tzu Ni walked past him and entered the room. "Clan? What clan?" Tzu Ni reached into the ceremonial robe he wore and pulled out a scroll. "The Clan of the Golden Phoenix. The clan you have desecrated with your presence." He handed the scroll to Cross. "Open." David unrolled the parchment. "It's written in Japanese. Michelle, can you translate? My chicken scratch is a little rusty." Michelle stood beside him and skimmed over the scroll.
