Erik & Pamela (The Offer)

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a long trip home from Egypt. And the fWo Champion had a bad case of insomnia. He looked outside the dark window of the jet, looking at the flashing lights indicating their presence in air. They had just gotten about halfway to the U.S. Erik needed answers. Why was Vic Creed in Chicago? Was something really going on? It had been about 2 1/2 weeks since he last saw Pamela. Could something be... "That's stupid..." Kelly thought. He just knew Pamela could never stand Creed. There was an air about him that women loathed. God's gift to himself. Still, until he got there, his mind would wander. Wander back to a decade ago, where it all began... *Erik Kelly and his friend, Brian Andrews, stood outside of the Boston Garden. It was such a humid evening in mid July of 1993. They just had the time of their life watching a WWF house show from the front row thanks to some tickets won on WEEI. What a 2 week late birthday present for the tall Northeastern undergrad. He unfortunately turned 20, which meant he was now too old to be a teen, and not old enough to be an adult. Erik's heart was racing. He looked at his watch. It was just after 11:30 PM. Roughly 40 minutes after the show finished. No sign of this Pete he was asked to look for. He still couldn't get over the fact that The Undertaker had asked him to meet him afterwards. Erik had no clue if this was part of the tickets he won, so he couldn't ask anyone about this. Brian was beginning to think this was a joke. That someone was playing a game with them. He wanted to get back to campus and get into the fraternity houses before it got too late to join the fun. He stretched his arms over his head, stifling a yawn, refusing to let sleep take over what could be a great night of partying.
