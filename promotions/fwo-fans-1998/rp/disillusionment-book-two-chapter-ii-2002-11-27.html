Disillusionment: Book Two - Chapter II

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sorry about the other night. I kinda lost it. I don't do that kind of thing most of the time. Usually I am Mr. Level-headed, but eventually this kind of shit gets to you. The running, the living on thirty dollars a week, the sleeping in factory basements, and the constant having to fight for your very existence...it has a way of wearing you down...of eating away at you...of stealing your will from the depths of your very soul. It hurts. It hurts a lot. It's not like a......like a physical pain. It's not even really an emotional pain either. I am not allowed the convenience of emotions. Emotions hold you down. The pain...I can't really describe where you feel. I think Freud called it the Id. That's where I hurt...my...Id. For those of you who are unfamiliar with Freud's work, the Id is like subconsciousness. It's kinda like how you feel when you first wake up in the morning, when you're not even sure of your existence. When I wake up, I feel the pain, but I don't know what is hurting. It's just always there. I feel it when I eat. I feel it when I piss. I feel it all the time, but I can never put a finger on where or why I hurt. Anyway, back to emotions. Like I said, I can't allow myself to feel them. When you're in a situation like the one I'm in, emotions are like one hundred pound weights strapped around your neck. Emotions, especially unpleasant ones, can affect you physically as well. They can wear you down, and I need to make sure I'm as fit as possible. The minute I start devoting my entire life to staying free is the moment I get caught. I dunno. I guess I've been doing a lot of thinking lately...too much really. Thought is another thing I don't really have the convenience of, but I can't help it. I go through my days without over-thinking my choices...living merely to live, but those few minutes before I go to sleep, knowing that I'll have to wake up in only three hours to continue running, that's when I can't keep myself from thinking. I wonder why I do this...why I do this, the running, the hiding, the fighting, the starving...it all seems so......pointless. At first I thought I did it for people like you. I had some superhero complex that I wanted to save the world from the Disciples. I wanted to allow innocent people like yourself, who weren't even aware that they were being oppressed, to understand and take hold of something better. As time has passed, I've become much more realistic. I don't do it for you anymore, because you are the same as the people who laugh at me and tell me that I am full of shit. You are the same as the people who refuse to believe me because you are too ignorant to look at the proof of what I say that you encounter everyday. I don't do it for you. I don't do it for myself either. It would be so much easier to just let myself get caught and killed. To give in to what would be the end of my suffering. Even if they tortured me when I was captured, that would be better than the hell that I endure everyday. Sometimes, in those last few minutes before I sleep, I wish that I could, like, have my memory erased. That the slate could be cleared and I could go back to living the life of a simple, ignorant human being, but then I remember why I continue...Burkenstock. Burkenstock was like a father to me, and he let me follow him around and he taught me for ten years before they killed him. He had survived longer than anyone else in the know that I've met...other than me, that is. Actually, I don't know if they killed him. Maybe they just put that slug in his leg, brought him to some facility, brainwashed him to forget, and set him free. If that were the case, I'd be happy for him, but I don't think that that is the case. Either way, though, Burkenstock, as I knew him, is dead. Revenge is powerful. Of all emotions that I no longer have the convenience of, revenge is the most powerful by far. The Bohemians talked about love, beauty and truth being the most powerful, but I've known love, beauty and truth, and none of them can hold a candle to revenge. People don't kill for love. People don't kill for beauty. Sure, people sometimes kill for truth, but they are the rare heroic martyrs, but millions
