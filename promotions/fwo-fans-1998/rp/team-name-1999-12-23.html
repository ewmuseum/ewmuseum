Team name?

23 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::The scene opens in a locker room inhabited by Black Quicksilver, Jackhammer, and their new teammate, Sphere. Jackhammer and Sphere are involved in some sort of conversation, while BQ, with Stump at his side is watching his match against Briana from the Road To Cyberslam IV show:: Jackhammer: But, we have to think of something, my dear friend. Sphere: Dear? We've been teaming for a week. As a matter of fact, we haven't even technically teamed yet! Jackhammer: Ah, but that will change tomorrow night, when, together, we team, along with Black Quicksilver and Briana, to meet the dastardly Silver Fox and his crew. Sphere: He's old. Jackhammer: Yes he is. Sphere: So *what* are we going to do after tomorrow? We're gonna form a tag team? The two of us? Jackhammer: You see, due to our entrance this past Sunday on Slaughter, we're already considered a tag team. We just don't have a clever name yet. Sphere: I see. Hmmmm.....how about....Fried Death Ship? Jackhammer: No, no. We're the good guys. Only the bad guys can have the word "death" in their name. Sphere: Okay. How about....Bludgeoned Pigeon Brains? Jackhammer: The younger kids wouldn't like that name. Sphere: Are you kidding? The kids eat that kind of stuff up! Jackhammer: This isn't the WWF, jabroni. Sphere: Roody-poo. Jackhammer: A name! Sphere: Oh, yeah....Da Baldies? ::Jackhammer and Sphere glance at each other, then both men burst out laughing:: BQ: Hey, guys, keep it down! ::Jackhammer goes over to where BQ is sitting, still giggling a little, while Sphere continues to slap his leg at his own joke:: Jackhammer: It seems you are immersed in this matchup. Could it be the fact that you won your fourth Cruiserweight Title in this bout? BQ: Cruiserweight Title? Oh yeah, I remember that. Nowadays, I'd likely have climb the ladder of success to get a shot at that title. Jackhammer: So why are you watching this? BQ: You know, just...watching. No special reason. Jackhammer: Alright, I'll leave you alone. ::Sphere finally stops laughing, and gets an idea:: Sphere: Hey, Blackjack! BQ: Black Quicksilver. Sphere: Yeah, that too. Got any suggestions for our team name? ::By this time, Jackhammer has gone back to his original seat next to Sphere. BQ continues to watch the match on TV, and nonchalantly spurts out a team name:: BQ: Bat Crap Monkeys. ::It looks iike Jackhammer and Sphere have gotten an idea, because it seems that a light bulb has flickered on over their heads. The camera pans over to Chris Finn, who is in the doorway of the locker room:: Finn: Yup, just a small lighting problem. ::Finn leaves the room as Sphere and Jackhammer both scratch their heads:: Sphere: Mmmpph. Hungry? Jackhammer: Me very hungry. Sphere: Ooh yadda da zappa. Jackhammer: A-ha. Yummy, yummy. ::Sphere and Jackhammer leave the room as BQ continues to watch the tape of his match, and the screen fades to black::
