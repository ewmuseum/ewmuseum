Wish You Were Here (Part 1 of 5)

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
so, so you think you can tell heaven from hell, blue skies from pain can you tell a green field from a cold steel rail? a smile from a veil? do you think you can tell? and did they get you to trade your heroes for ghosts? hot ashes for trees? hot air for a cool breeze? cold comfort for change? and did you exchange a walk on part in the war for a lead role in a cage? how I wish, how I wish you were here we're just two lost souls swimming in a fish bowl, year after year running over the same old ground what have you found? the same old fears wish you were here... *** Date: April 20th, 1994 Location: Boston, MA Following CWL's PPV UNFORGIVEN "One day Levi, your gonna be up there with me. Your gonna be waiting behind the curtain for your name to be called and then we'll both be stars." Levite Prod smiled, creating a quick vision in his mind. "And we'll strut down that LINE and I'll look up at those opponents and yell, 'Get ready for an asskickin!' That's when we'll charge the ring, our fists a flyin'!" Steve Douglas let go a loud stomach rumbling laugh which was accompanied by the laugh of his bodyguard Levite Prod. "Just gimme a beer Steve, tonight's show was great and all, but I'm fucking tired...I mean I swear the chicks out there are stronger than the fucking guys...I mean seriously Steve, do you stuff those tights? Cause those girls just go crazy when your coming off the ring." Steve raised one eyebrow mockingly at Levite, "Levi buddy, this is one hundred percent all natural... you forget I'm Canadian." Steve tossed a bottle of Miller Genuine Draft at Levite who caught it and then popped the cap off by using the table he sat on. Levite Prod had it all, he was young, he was rich, he was popular. This was all thanks to being the bodyguard to the CWL's Heavyweight Champion Steve Douglas. As far as he was concerned he could never thank Steve enough for finding him and giving him a job. Levite had never experienced a life like this, he was used to waking up at the crack of dawn and feeding the animals before heading out to work in the field all day. However now, Levite's life consisted of long nights involving women, booze, drugs, music, and lots of flashy lights. Being with Steve Douglas, you walked on air. Steve tossed his belt down on the bench in the center of the lockerroom. He looked toward Levite, "Look Levi, I'm gonna have a shower, then we're going to celebrate another wonderful win for ME. The town is ours tonight Levi." Levite took a rather large swig of his beer, the cold liquid raced down his throat and into his stomach. Slowly a warm embrace seemed to glide along his face as he grinned back at Steve, "It always is Steve." Steve turned back just before he disappeared around the corner to say, "Remember that." Levite laughed at the comment in his head, "Remember that," Levite spoke lightly and with sarcasm. No one would ever touch them, no one would take their place. They were on top of the world.
