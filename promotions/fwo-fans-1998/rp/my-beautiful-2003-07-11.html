My Beautiful

11 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wake up every morning to the sound of the radio screaming "Goddamnit," said Ivy as the alarm rang on the unfamiliar nighttable, "what's the point? Shows were preempted this week... would anyone really complain if I took the day off?" 6:30, 7:30, 8 o'clock and I got to leave She knew the answer before she even asked the question. Poison Ivy hasn't had a day off in five years, not counting recovering from knee surgery last winter. She's not allowed to. Walk out on the street with nothing on except the shame of last night Especially when she's punishing herself for imagined transgressions. And a number in my hand of a man I don't really need What happened last night? Ivy stretched her arms over her head, as her brain rebelled against the hangover. She had a vague recollection of herself and Sean being out having a quiet dinner together... a vague recollection of someone calling her name... Mike Luty. Fucking hell, where did he come from, why did he recognize her, and why did it have to be last night? The first time she and Sean had been able to have a nice night out in so> damn long... Where do I fit in, Where's my place here? Sure, he was polite. Sure, he was a familiar face from the old neighborhood. Sure, he was nothing like his brother Alex, whom she's pretty sure is still in prison. What do I need to say, what do you want to hear? But he also stayed just long enough to disclose to Sean that he was her former boyfriend. A dozen or so years removed, true - but things that happened during those years that Sean really didn't need to hear. She's respected the fact that Sean doesn't talk about his previous relationships, and it kills her that one of hers - Hornet - constantly slaps him in the face, so it's something she never brings up. Where do I fit in if she's in your bed Ivy crawled out of bed and saw for the first time, the note on her computer. "Out running. Back by ten - SS" She turned toward the bed - he was, indeed, gone. Where is my beautiful? Ivy grabbed a shirt off the floor and pulled it on. It was one of Sean's - as it was massive on her small frame. The two of them had had a fight during and after dinner, and even though neither of them kicked the other out of the room, she spent about four hours in a drunken stupor ranting to Angel over the computer. Late night, long day, doesn't make much difference to me anyway Four hours that she should've spent telling and showing Sean just how much he means to her. Four hours she'll never get back again. And four hours of leisure time that could've been used to make things right with Sean again. You called, I answered, but baby I'm only half awake Ivy opened her bag and pulled out an athletic bra and spandex shorts, along with her Bag O' Essentials for hotel bathrooms - maybe a bath would clear her head a bit. Why does it need clearing? Why is this fight - not their first and certainly not their last - causing so much stress for her? Why can't she give him some time to cool down, give herself time to cool down, and just move past it? "Hush little one with you here everything's going to be okay" Because she can't. Not this time. Is all you say but what if things don't go that way? Because she's tired. She needs a vacation. It's over, we both know, we're both tired of playing the parts Poison Ivy has been hanging out for far too long... the program Eli Flair has been running with Vic Creed has given her even more exposure in front of the fans as far as what she says and does in the ring. And the sheer extent of the planning she's done has given her more insider respect than she ever thought possible. To say nothing of The News, and how the Ivy League has regained its form as the most talked- about column on the Internet. I said goodbye, you said oh well nothing's really lost Eli Flair is able to go home every now and then and be EJ, husband to his wife and father to his daughter. Aimz and Brand Frontier are able to go home and be Amy Campbell, Brandon Roche. Poison Ivy has not been able to be Ivy Lillian McGinnis, private citizen, for longer than she can remember. And it's not fair. Call every now and than just to hear your voice talk to me The bathroom began to fill with steam as Ivy flicked through the radio until she came across NPR, and stripped down to her sore, never-well-rested skin and bones. Her hair was a mess. Who cares? It'd do her good to take just one day out of the year to not spray it, mousse it, braid it, cornrow it, dreadlock it, or even ponytail it. You still say everything's alright but it's not and that's something you're too blind to see Things have never been so swell, Eli? Good for you. Things have never been more of a mess for the Poison known as Ivy. The scalding hot water felt good, however... and it treated her as any good friend would... it left her alone. Where do I fit in, Where's my place here? Was that her phone? Yeah. She ignored it. Fuck you, this is her time. As pathetic as it might be to retreat to the bathtub for something as basic to living as quiet time to think. What do I need to say, what do you want to hear? She's not happy. She's always working, she's always playing a role for her fans, and she never gets to do anything for herself or her own life. God only knows when the last time she slept in either her bed or Sean's bed was. Where do I fit in if she's in your bed The joke around the wrestling business is that Poison Ivy had ice water in her veins, she was so single- minded and driven to succeed and be noticed. The ice water in her veins is currently leaking out her eyes. Thankful for the privacy, for the fact that Sean won't be back for a little while, for the radio doing a semi- passable job of drowning out the sobs, Poison Ivy cried quietly to herself, overwhelmed by how successful her professional life is and how empty her personal life has become. Leave her alone... she's given us so much of herself, the least we can do is give her her dignity. Where is my beautiful?
