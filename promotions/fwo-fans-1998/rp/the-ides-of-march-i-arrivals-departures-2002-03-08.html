The Ides Of March I: Arrivals/Departures

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He ran his hands over his face again and wondered just exactly what the hell it was he was doing here. Wasn't he happy? Weren't things good? Outside of the train wreck that was his back and neck, wasn't he having a good life? And then, just because he was too damn happy and paranoid, he'd hopped a plane on a whim and headed for San Francisco. Well, not technically San Fran, but that's what the airport ticket said. From there, it was a short trip to Berkeley. He was standing outside, barely insulted from the rain. It was nearing five o'clock. His bags at his feet, he zipped up his Niners jacket and scowled at Telegraph Avenue. She was so close to spring break she could taste it. Another week and a half. Of course, salving the senioritis a little bit was the 94 she'd gotten on the anatomy exam. And who was the homeless-looking fuck outside her building? As she got closer, she suddenly realized she knew the homeless-looking fuck. It was HER homeless-looking fuck. "Uh...hey?" He ran up to her and hugged her as hard as he possibly could. "Can't...breathe...pain..." "Oh, my God, sorry." He let her go and set her down. "Can we talk? Say, now?"
