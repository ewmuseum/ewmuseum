Born of Snake and Beast - II

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the days following the match with Jake "The Snake" Roberts, it was given out quite simply that a terrible accident has happened, which was, of course, the truth. Sympathy from all sides was unending, and poured into Mufanga's hospital room like a river of guilt. The RWO sent word that Jake Roberts had been fined and reprimanded (whatever that meant), and they were all deeply sadded by this "incident". John Rider, Mufanga Kagos' manager, was beside himself, taking all blame over what happened in the ring and apologizing over and over... even though Mufanga could not hear him through the daze of post-op anesthetics. All Rider would speak of was the match-turned-shoot with Jake Roberts... a match that never would have happened if he knew that Roberts was a disillusioned old man with a hate for anything youthful and strong. A wrestler that was past his prime and had a will to destroy anything that he sensed may upstage him. Have you ever had one of those nagging puzzles that you just can't get out of your head? Like the meaning behind a particularly obscure bit of lyrics, you just have to know what it means. Mufanga Kagos has one of those and it's named John Rider. John Rider is a leisure-suit wearing, gold-watch flaunting, chest-hair showing bag of guts. He is overly loud and overbearing, he sports giant sweat stains under the arms of his polyester shirts, even in the winter, and looks like he should be directing porn films rather than managing wrestlers. It is probably these attributes that makes him such a good wrestling manager. Now sometimes Rider is the type of person who's emotions can knock you over from across the room. Take for instance when Tempest decided to back out of her planned program with Mufanga Kagos over the Cruiserweight Title and left him struggling at the bottom of a growing roster. John Rider never gave up, even as Mufanga despaired. Even at the worst moment, when fWo brass offered Mufanga an announcing job on Slaughter, a move that usually said "We don't want you on TV, but we dont want to give you to the competition either, so rot here for awhile, willya?"... even then, after Mufanga told them where and at what angle to stuff that idea, Rider never despaired. He simply said "Let's go on the road. Back to basics, and all that shit." That was John Rider for you... sure he was slimy, and sure, he would steal you blind if you turned your back even for a second... but he was smart enough to know that he had a meal-ticket here in Mufanga Kagos. The world was ready for a young, black, honest wrestler, and Rider wanted to be at the station when that gravy train came home. It was this desire, not only for his own personal wealth, but for the good of his charge, Mufanga Kagos, that kept Rider at the young wrestler's bedside even as the doctors told him that Kagos may never wrestle again.
