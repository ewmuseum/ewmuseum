Disillusionment: Book Seven - Chapter III

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The minutes until I shall fulfill my destiny slip through the cracks like the sand of an hourglass...one grain at a time. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. For ten years I have struggled for every single waking second. As I look forward, I see not tomorrow. Tomorrow is as distant as the eastern sunrise...it seems so close, but always just out of reach. I don't live for tomorrow. Tomorrow has never been my goal. For ten years I've lived my life, never expecting to see a tomorrow, and yet, thousands of tomorrows have come and gone, and all.........all are remembered. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. I must force my life to live for each second, not allowing myself to look to the next. For I know that the force that drives my body and soul is weakening. I can feel it in my blood. My bones ache and tremor as I stand, looking out over the horizon that could be my future, but only if I have my way. I turn, looking behind me, at where I have been. I am not disappointed, nor am I satisfied; I merely......am. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. An afterlife? I don't know of one, as I have never died. What waits for me when the sands run out is there. I cannot change that now. The sins of my life will not enable me to reap the benefits of heaven, but perpetual purgatory is a thought to terrible to bear. Even still, the minutes pass by. They pass to the time. Only five of them left. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. With their expiration, Olaf Heersh, the propellant of my pain and insanity will land in a helicopter at that pad, and then...it will end. How? I do not know. The story has not yet been completed. Completion will come soon enough, and some will revel in it. Those that are not victorious will be dead, and will not need to hang their heads in the defeat that fate has brought them. The loss will be an easy one to withstand...painless. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. Three minutes. As I reach for my pistol I feel the sweat of a lifetime, accumulated over thirty years, through the good and the bad. I must maintain my grip. I must do what I have set out to do. And that means placing a leaden slug in the skull of a man who I've never seen face to face in my life. Another sin for which I will pay, but, nonetheless, a sin I need to commit. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. Two minutes and I look back over my shoulder once again. Looking back at the few joys I have experienced as my hourglass ran. My teenage girlfriend, covering my naked body with her own as we lay by the fire. Loving each other for no benefit other than the love. Burkenstock: the only man who believed in me, even when I did not believe in myself. The man who told me I was the "Chosen One." Those words are my greatest curse and my highest victory. Stephanie: the woman I never touched before she died in my arms. I can now admit that I loved her, which the tear that ran down my face as I stared into her lifeless eyes can confirm. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. No. No. Don't think of the good times, you idiot. Think of the hatred...the pain...the fatigue...the running...the bleeding. Think of the Disciples and their terrible exploits against the human race. Think of those who have died to allow you go get to this point, and how the Disciples ravenously consumed their being. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. It's time. As I run over the hill I can see the helicopter landing. The blade seems to spin in slow motion, as I can follow it's movement. The blade pops as it chops the air, and I see Heersh descending from his vehicle. I life my weapon as I continue my suicide charge. His guards, dressed in black and wearing ear pieces see me running down the bluff. The reach for their holsters, but it is too late. My index finger flexes and... Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. It seems to happen in slow motion as I actually watch the round leave my weapon and travel through the air, connecting with its target and ripping a hole through the brain of Heersh as his head explodes into a cloud of crimson. I slow my charge and drop my weapons as the return fire comes. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. My chest desires to separate from my body as the first shot blows it back and I watch the blood pour from my being. Another connects. Another. Another and Another. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. I fall to my knees and my torso follows to the ground. The burning lead that has infested my body prevents me from lifting my hands to protect my face as it falls to the concrete, opening yet another wound above my eye. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. I breathe in, but nothing comes. I gasp for air and shift my eyes up to see several guards run towards me and then decide to tend to their leader. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. My sands have run...my time is done. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. But, it can't be. Surely I have failed. The disappointment veils my pain as I close my eyes...one...final...time. Tick-Tock. Tick-Tock. It was all for naught......I............die. Tick-Tock. Tick-......
