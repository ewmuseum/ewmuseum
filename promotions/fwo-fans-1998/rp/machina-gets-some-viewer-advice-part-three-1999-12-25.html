Machina gets some viewer advice (part three)

25 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Machina gets some viewer advice (part three) December 25, 1999 ::The scene opened on Machina, once again sitting on an oversized comphy couch in front of a fire. He sat next to Rudo, and the two looked mindlessly at the camera. Rudo scratched his head and looked at Machina.:: Say Ed, do you like apples? ::Machina shrugged and then nodded.:: Yes Rudo, I do like apples. ::Rudo stood, and farted on Machina. The two laughed, and Rudo exclaimed...:: So how do you like them apples? ::The two continued to laugh, and then Machina noticed that the cameras were on. He coughed, and Rudo wandered away.:: Oh hello! Well folks, I've gotten a lot of mail about what I should do to get laid... I mean... share relations with that pretty piece of tail... I mean... ass. Well, shall we get started? What's that? ::Machina looked confused as the off-camera director motioned something to him that he didn't understand. Suddenly a sign flew across the room and struck Machina in the head.:: Ow! Dammit, what did you... oh yeah... the sign... ::Machina lifted the sign, now bent, which read...:: HELP SAV MACHINEA'S LOVE LIF. WRITE HIM LETTER WITH IDEAZ ::Machina left it up for a couple of seconds, and then tossed it behind him.:: Huh... I've got the weirdest feeling of deja vu. Anyway, our first letter comes from an ER-RON. Huh. Sounds like that guy from Planet Grayskull. I wonder if Teela knows that this guy is running around. Anyway, he writes: -------------------------------------------------------------- Dear Machina What is your problem you stupid South Korean? You're never gonna get her, shes one of those "Im too good for even people on TV" uhhhh people. Find some other chick, like a groupie. Use my advice, its the Runner Up Prize ya get for losing to me at Battlebowl 99. Buh bye Eron the Relentless -------------------------------------------------------------- Well, thanks for your input ER-RON. And don't you lose that sword of lions or whatever. Then that crazy old wizard and his cat will kick your ass. Okay! Our next letter is from a Jason Metcalfe and he writes: -------------------------------------------------------------- Hey Machina. I think you should just go up to this women and say..."Yo. I'm a man. Your a women. Risght. Well if you answerd yes to both of those questions. Me and you were ment for each other. Oh and you must be really tired, beacuse you've been running through my mind all damm day. " You see. You got to be smooth when talking to the ladies. Peace. Jason Metcalfe -------------------------------------------------------------- Damn Jason, that's pretty smooth. I'm not sure if I have enough moxy to pull that one off but it's always worth a try. Let's see if it works with Sid. Hey Sid, come over here. ::Sid wandered into the scene, sipping an oversized cup of Mocha Delight.:: Yeah Machina? ::Machina cleared his throat and read from the letter.:: You must be really tired, beacuse you've been running through my mind all damm day. ::Sid fluttered his eyes and clasped his hands together.:: Oh Machina! I... I always dared hope, but never did I think that this day would come... Oh glorious day... ::Sid moved toward Machina, but the masked leader of the Lone Gunmen was ignoring him as he dug for another letter. Heart broken, Sid ran from the scene crying. Machina pulled out another letter and cleared his throat.:: Okay! This letter is from a Stanly Toole and he appears to have a prediction for the future. He writes: -------------------------------------------------------------- In the year 2000, the phrase "looking out for number one" will take on a horrific new meaning as the world is overrun by giant, weak-bladdered dogs. Thanks, Stan -------------------------------------------------------------- Interesting. I'll watch out for that. Our next letter comes from a Mr. Amazins and he writes: -------------------------------------------------------------- Machina.. What is the one thing that separates you from Ms. Pureheart? --besides looks, wit, intelligence, and a future-- .. Time's up. Wrestling! She despises it, what it is about, and well.. everything and everyone involved with it. YOU are involved with it. You are a wrestler. She will not accept or even notice you if you are in or anywhere near and involved with the sport of wrestling. So with that said, it is time for a new attitude. Anti-wrestling. Just for a bit. Turn against the sport. Get her attention. Make her realize who you are, and then, well then it is all up to you to turn on your charm and work your, er..mojo. Good luck! -------------------------------------------------------------- That's some good advice. As you may already know, I've put into action plan "BRUCE SPRINGSTEEN". I also sent away for literature from her public action group, "Rasslin' Yucky Dumb Ewok Ruins". Hopefully that comes in soon. Okay! Our next letter is from a Jeremiah Frankton: -------------------------------------------------------------- Machina, What the hell are you thinking? This woman is not a reporter... she's a SPY! She's trying to bring down the fWo, not help it. But that's okay. When a man loves a woman, he can see past her flaws and accept her for who she is. It's abundantly clear that you love her, and you cannot see her flaws. So, even though she's trying to destroy the company of which both of us are employed by, here's what you do. Muster up the Chris West (Courage) to talk to her. Then, when you see the tape recorder aka the solar powered stapler of hers... you grab that thing, turn that sumbitch sideways, and stick it straight up her candy ass! And tell her that no matter how much you love her, you will not let her destroy the fWo and let your good friend Jeremiah Frankton become unemployed as a result of her doings! Got it? Do this or your push is off. No more TV time for you, pally. I'm the promoter, got it? GOT IT? Er... $5 bucks says you can't get it "up" on the honeymoon. If there IS a honeymoon. God, I can't imagine what your kids are going to look like... this is just... just sad. Jeremiah Frankton -------------------------------------------------------------- Interesting. I wonder if it really is because of spray cans that the ozone layer has a hole in it, or if it's a result of Sheryl Crow albums. Could go either way, but thanks Jeremiah, you've give us all something to think about. Huh. Our next letter comes from a Mr. Chadwick: -------------------------------------------------------------- Dear Mr. Ninja, In the course of daily events we all, at times, must make decisions. It has come to my attention that you desire the affections of this curious, almost allegorically named, "Penny Pureheart." In my own estimable opinion, such a regal figure can only be garnered with a proper investment in interest and attention. To wit, I'd propose an economic commitment to this extraordinary individual; present the young lady with a gift. As a long-time connoisseur of womanly wiles, I stand in a unique position to offer enlightened advice regarding this particular venue. I've found, during my many years spent searching the hearts of such exquisite creatures, that one offering stands well above all else. There is indeed a perfect gift that - as they say - "keeps giving." Such a gift is perfect in both spirit and form, embracing all that it is to woman. What is this item, you surely ask? I'll impart my wisdom on you, and trust you to put it to sound use: explosives. Blasting caps and nitroglycerine compounds will win the heart of any woman worthy of the name. A still better option is the gentle sloping curve of a simple hand grenade. Furthermore, when choosing a grenade's color, I'd advise looking into pastels. An aqua or lavender explosive is far more likely to impress than the traditional flat green. I wish you the very best of luck good sir, and hope you quickly obtain the object of your desire. Respectfully yours, Mr. Clarence Chadwick -------------------------------------------------------------- Wow. Fans, this is exactly what I was looking for. Solid advice from someone who is obviously an expert in his field. This guy has got to have slept with #... or maybe even C women. What a man, what a legend. Well kiddos, that's all the time we have for today. Join us next time when we will have Dr. Howards from the San Diago Zoo and Star Wars star Billy Dee Williams... or maybe it will just be me and some coffee. Either way, you're sure to enjoy it. Or... else. :;The camera faded out as Machina looked directly into it, eyes narrowed and teeth gritted.::
