Only Time Will Tell

4 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Night was the loneliest of all times for the man that carried the moniker of The Equalizer. Work had become too stressful lately and this man now found himself alone in the penthouse suite of a Hilton Hotel. This was a time for him to gather all his thoughts and figure out just where his place in this world truly was. A quick glance at the clock hung above the bed revealed the time to be ten o'clock. It was ironic that the night had only begun for Mr Ray Chavez. It had been so long since he was back on the road. It had obviously not paid off that he didn't take the precious time to get his routine back on track. With the entire burden of his past, he sat there starring at the full moon that shown itself from beyond the stars. He then reached over, without looking, and brought the glass of Jack Daniel's to his mouth. It tasted rather pleasant, even for a man who didn't drink on a regular basis. Oh no, Ray Chavez was the type of man who took his diet extremely serious. This night of personal indulgent wasn't for mere pleasure though. This particular night was one that Ray wanted to put behind him. It was this night, five years ago, that he lost his family. It had become so familiar after the last four years that Ray didn't fight what was to happen. The night flowed like a romantic poem in one continuous motion. Nobody was in any position to stop the events that would transpire. Ray continued swallowing down big gulps of the liquor. He had high hopes that he would pass out quickly and the night would be over like the ones that came before. The drink wasn't doing the trick. How could he lie to himself? Had it ever really done the trick? Ray stood up from the cozy leather chair and glanced back at the moon, which was now hidden beneath the shadows. He looked down at the glass in his now trembling hand and quickly put it back on the coffee table beside the chair. He staggered over to the bed where his suitcase lay open. The contents inside showed that everything was tossed together as quickly as possible. Then again, he never really was good at packing. He reached deep into the pit of belongings. His fingers moved quickly into the bottom of the case beneath the mess. It was as if they were connected to what lay at the bottom. There was no need to look, only the need to feel. He then withdrew his hand from the suitcase and took a brief moment to quickly glance at the item that rest in his hand. Thousands of memories instantaneously darted through his head like the speed of light itself. The sharp pain it made caused him to close his eyes and clench his teeth together. Something was wrong with him. Things had never been right with him since... my, had it been that long? He then placed the picture on the mantle beneath the mirror. He looked into the picture without a blink. How could she do that? How in the hell could it happen after all this time? Everything could be wrong in his life, yet when he looked into the picture... everything made absolute sense. "Hi sweetie..." there was a hidden pain to his words that even he could not understand. He then put his right hand against the wall to prevent himself from falling over. "This day always hurts. I go on with my life like you would have wanted it and I always know that this day will come. I try my hardest to pretend it won't, maybe hoping that it'll pass by without me knowing. Who am I to kid you though? How could I forget? I don't understand till this day why things had to be the way they are. How..." he quickly rubbed at his face before a single tear could slip by. "We were good people. I was always so sure about myself. I always knew what I wanted to do and where I wanted to go. Now, it's all a blur to me. I blame myself every day for what happened. Countless hours of therapy all led to failed attempts at trying to convince myself that there was nothing I could have done to stop the event that transpired," he chuckled to himself at that last statement. "Listen to how I'm talking. As though it wasn't my damn family. As though I didn't love both of you with every beat of my aching heart. Nothing will ever change anything. I love you both... so... so much. Look at me now. In the same damn place where it all started. I'm still a wrestler and you're still gone. Only time will tell...," the uncontrollable emotions were too much for even him to hide this time. He burst into tears that came from his soul as he began rubbing the picture. He then looked up into the mirror at what he had become; "only time... will tell."
