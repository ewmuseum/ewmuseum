Why? Part 5: Stir Stir Stir That Pot

21 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"A hero?" Borst said to himself as he wandered down the corridors of the arena. "Me? A hero? What the fuck?" Cast your mind back Pete. What happened when the bomb blew? Come on... you've gotta remember something heroic. "Nope..." Ding. Light bulb. "Fuck yeah. I remember now. I went back to the arena when it was falling to bits..." he said to himself. You like talking to yourself Pete? "And I went back to get my... yeah. I went back, pulled all those bricks and rubble away, and pulled those two... adorable children out. I expect those little kiddies and their parents will be wanting to shower me with praise... I'll be on the news, a huge hero worldwide. Fantastic." Borst continued to wander along the corridors, knocking bins over along the way, picking bits off the wall, and generally making a mess. At the end of one corridor, Borst encountered one of his worst nightmares... Stairs. He hates stairs. Just so much wasted effort... climbing... climbing... climbing. So monotonous and tiring. So instead, Borst stood at the bottom of the flight of stairs to ponder on what to do. After a few moments of pondering, our hero and US Ambassador in these times of need, looked up the flight of stairs, seeing the glistening cigarette machine on the floor above. Borst clapped his hands together and smiled. "That's right. I'm a hero. I think this calls for a celebratory fag." Bump. Borst took a step back and made a kind of, ugh face. "I know I said fag, but I meant the cigarette type." Aimz, who had just come down the stairs and bumped into the day dreaming Borst, stood and looked at him in a confused manner. So Borst set out to make his point clearer for her. "It's just sick you know, especially among the ranks of the fWQuo. Gay wrestlers. I mean, fuck, you and that guy Martyr now..." Borst shook his head. "Fuck off are either of you putting me in a waist lock. Us hero types shouldn't have to put up with that sort of thing." Aimz glared at Borst, staring a hole through his head. "The dick can wait, I'd rather put my foot i---" Amy did a double-take for a second, "Wait...MARTYR and I!?" Borst took a quick look around and leant towards Aimz. "Yeah, you two blokes. Disgusts me. Two grown men, gay men in a wrestling ring, lusting after each other." Borst took a step back and looked Aimz up and down and shook his head. "Why?" "Give up on the man thing, I know it's a ploy to have me flash you." She practically growled but quickly thought back to the topic at hand. "And if you think I even find Martyr remotely attractive, you'll find my foot up your ass A LOT sooner than you're hoping for." "Hmm..." Borst said, reaching out towards Aimz chest. "You could stand to lose a few pound, you're getting..." Borst took a quick squeeze of what he had imagined to be 'man-tits' only to get a hard whack on the arm from Aimz. "Oh..." Borst mumbled. "You're... hmm. Sorry about that. I was only going by what Martyr said. He told me you were an item, lusting after each other, sharing razors... and also led me to believe that you were a, well... man. But obviously, you're a woman, woman." "I SWEAR TO FUCKING GOD, if I wasn't already knee deep in shit with this company for being overly violent backstage...you sure as hell wouldn't be walking away from me. Well, you're not anyway...why in God's name are you here? Getting your jollies off of fondling me, or just being an asshole?" The redhead started well enough, but hadn't quite finished. "And that dumb cunt's telling people we're together?! It's all I can do not to castrate the sorry bastard whenever I see him!" Borst stood and pondered for a moment, rubbing his beard as he stared at Aimz. "Look." he said. "I was just going by what Martyr said, and hey, if you can't trust a gay man, who can you trust?" Aimz just glared at Borst. "OK." he said. "To be honest, I was hoping to get my jollies off of fondling you, AND to be an asshole. But to be honest, those molehills really didn't do much for me. I mean, I never really believed Martyr when he said you were a man, but... this has been a disaster. I'm really sorry young lady. I was misinformed. I'm sure you're lovely." Borst winked at Aimz and patted her on the head. "You seriously need to be out of my sight within the next sixty seconds." Aimz said. "If not, I wouldn't be too hopeful of keeping those grape-sized wank tanks you claim to have." "Hey hey hey hey hey, calm down woman. Don't shoot the messenger. I'm sure there's just been some huge hilarious mix up..." Borst glanced at the stairs, contemplated using them for the sake of a cigarette, but decided against it. The moment had passed. Instead, Borst turned around and began to walk off. He stopped when he got a few metres away from Aimz and turned back, squinting slightly as he stared at her.] "Are you sure you're not a man?" "Are you sure you don't want your dick in your own throat? If not, by all means, keep talking." Borst just sniggered, gave Aimz a quick two finger salute before wandering off, whistling, soundly satisfied with the potential trouble he may have managed to cause. And of course, he's a hero. Apparently. Why? Your guess is as good as mine at this point.
