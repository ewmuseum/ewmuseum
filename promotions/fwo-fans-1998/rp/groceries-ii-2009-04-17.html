Groceries II

17 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Assistant was intensely focused on something and Siobahn McPhineas knew to stop, wait, and focus alongside the huge blonde woman. The grocery store wasn’t crowded, per se, but there was enough bustle and activity that a drop-dead gorgeous redhead and a towering goddess standing frozen in the produce section did warrant a few odd glances and the occasional stare. The women didn’t know and/or didn’t care how the situation looked – they were well versed at being the center of attention in …unique… situations. The Assistant turned her head ever so slightly as if some internal tuner was honing in on whatever it was that piqued her interest in the first place. Siobahn had quit marveling long ago about The Assistant’s preternatural hearing and the irony of someone with such hearing being mute… There. Her bright, blue eyes narrowed just a bit and The Assistant used a quick hand gesture. Siobahn listened intently, trying to discern just what it was she was supposed to be listening to. “Ok, so Saturday night me and Jenny and Tom Miluke and Ray and DJ and Skinny Pete were all sitting around at Tom’s house ‘cause his mom and dad were out, right? And we didn’t have anything going on and Jenny says something about Tiger Mountain and DJ says we should go to the Witch’s house and we were all like no way! But of course the boys had to act all tough and like they’re not scared of shit, right? So anyways we all decide we’re going and all get into Tom’s truck and head to Tiger Mountain. So we drive up there all windy and stuff until the road turns to gravel and then we park. Tom and DJ are in front with flashlights, me and Jenny were in the middle, and Ray and Skinny Pete were in back with more flashlights. So we’re walking and Jenny and I are totally checking out Tom’s hot ass and whispering about it and we finally get to the witch lady’s driveway and we’re all standing around and Jenny says ‘so now what?’, right? And I swear to God right then she’s just there! She’s old and sick and gross and got white hair all sticking out and shit and we’re all about pissing our pants and ALL THE FLASHLIGHTS GO OFF!!! But it’s like the moon’s her own fucking spotlight and we can still see her. And then she’s coming at us but it’s like she’s freaking FLOATING or something – she’s walking on gravel but she’s not making a fucking sound! And we’re all just about freaking shitting ourselves, right, and Ray grabs my hand and Jenny’s hand and pulls us back behind him and the Witch is getting closer and closer, coming right at us … AND SHE HAD HER FUCKING EYES CLOSED!!! And all of us are scared shitless and just standing there and she starts whispering ‘She’s coming. She’s coming.’ Over and over and that’s when we lost it. We all just start RUNNING back to the truck and Jenny starts crying and I swear to God those assholes were all in the truck and the truck was running by the time Jenny and I got there… And we hauled ass and got the fuck out of there…” The Assistant broke her pose and looked down at Siobahn with one eyebrow arched. Siobahn didn’t need to ask her what that particular gesture was for – there was no doubt as to why they happened to be at this place at this time to overhear this story… A Witch? Here?
