The Games Begin

29 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Austin Davis sat in a rental car outside the arena in silence. The vehicle itself was virtually devoid of any noise. Davis breathing made not even a whisper, all the noise was on the outside... or on the inside of Davis' head. He had spent the vast majority of the afternoon and early evening hours contemplating very important matters. In a few moments, Countdown would begin... and while the fWo had staged two Countdown series in the past; Davis hadn't appeared publically at either of them. Davis was determined for that to change. Not only would he appear at Countdown tonight, he was hellbent on making an impact... in his own way. Since returning to the fWo at Twilight just seven days ago he had already made a splash. The fWo was only now feeling the impact of the splash; waves being sent throughout the organization just as chills had been sent up the spine of many when Davis returned. The Arco Arena towered to the south of him. Davis remembered that the last time he wrestled for the fWo prior to Twilight, it was at this arena in September of 2000. He didn't wrestle much and he was anything but victorious. But he considered the moment to be historical. He had spent months calculating the events of that night, as in May of that year he had lost everything he worked so hard to attain. The ownership of the fans Wrestling organization. The fWo World Title itself. He had it all and was the man who called the shots. And in one night... it was taken from him by two people. Scott Slugger. The Boston Strangler. In Davis' mind, Scott played more part in his downfall than Paul. Paul was good enough to do it on his own, and Davis knew that. It was the exact reason he named Scott as the referee; which proved to be a fatal mistake. Scott was known for wanting the spotlight... perhaps more than anyone in the fWo's history. Inside the ring he was a skilled craftsman, backing up every word he promised outside of the ring. Playing second fiddle to Davis didn't sit well with Scott. It looked good on paper at first when Davis pitched it to him. Scott had almost singlehandedly built the promotion on his ring work and the way he was marketed. A lengthy World Title reign also did him some good, and when the Dark Riders attempted to take over the promotion and the suits instead looked elsewhere like the Black Panther or the Boston Strangler for help... well, Scott was understandably insulted. And Davis saw that and knew it would be a good move to take advantage of it. Realization set in months later that Davis was only taking advantage of him. While Scott had been doing most of the dirty work, Davis was getting most of the glory. It was Davis who became the first wrestler to ever win Body Count, and the vacated fWo World Title in the process. It was Davis who faced the Boston Strangler in the main event of CyberSlam 2000. It was Davis who became the owner of the organization. Scott decided this had to change. When Davis accepted the match to face Boston Strangler a month later after CyberSlam and named Scott the referee, Scott had his opportunity. If he helped the Strangler win, surely all would be forgotten... and his act of honor would surely outshine Strangler' accomplishment of dethroning Davis and winning the World Title. Davis disappeared from the scene altogether; but it was for his own good. Had he stuck around Chris Finn & James Armstrong likely would have booked him in embarassing situations... and Davis wanted no part of that. He stayed in the shadows, lurking and plotting. Months of calculation finally became a reality in the latter part of the Summer of 2000. Davis discovered a way to get back at Slugger by using the Boston Strangler, and set the wheels in motion. He knew that if the Boston Strangler were to lose the World Title, that would be his moment to strike. The Strangler 'snapped'... putting Slugger in his clutches and inflicting damage with the feared and respected Boston Massacre. That wasn't enough. Davis returned and played mind games with the Strangler, eventually causing him to snap even further. When he again took it out on Slugger with the Boston Massacre, months spent planning was complete; the task accomplished. Slugger was not seen again in the fWo for a year until Halloween Hell. He extracted some small measure of revenge... but for what Scott had done to him it paled in comparison. He had everything, Scott took it from him in one night and a downward spiral soon followed. Fired from the fWo, removed from the spotlight, a car accident... ... Scott was surely to blame. A small measure of revenge wasn't enough, no, but since Scott was nowhere to be found... Davis had other necessary things to take care of. The Arco Arena was a few feet away. In a few moments, Countdown To Oblivion 3 was about to begin. Fans had began filing into the arena hours prior. The arena was packed full and surely inside, fans had their signs and such ready. Davis grabbed a bag out of the passenger seat, before removing the key from the ignition and opening the door. He shut it behind him, never looking back. Davis began a slow methodical pace towards the arena, almost as if he was waiting for someone to arrive. Moments passed before someone appeared, someone Davis had a keen interest in. His new plan was about to begin. Would anyone survive?
