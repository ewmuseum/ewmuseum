Snow Job

12 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene is familiar. Holiday Hell II. Ice is lying on the mat, while Frenchie holds Slugger from behind. Cactus Jon swings a chair at Scott Slugger, but hits Frenchie when Slugger ducks. Ice gets to his feet and piledrives Slugger, pinning him to win the fWo World Title. That scene dissolves to Frenchie, sitting in a locker room.) Ice, I don't know who made zat fraudulent video zat you showed ze ot'er night, but it's a lie, and you know it. Eleven mont's ago, you beat Scott Slugger, your "good friend", for *MY* fWo World Title. And zat was only after I had defended my Internet title in a match zat saw me pin ze Boston Strangler, and after I had been hit in ze head wit' a chair from ze Canuck. You lost ze belt to ze Candyman. You tell me which is more embarrassing. But zere's some more video zat I'd like you to see. (From Pain I, The Flying Frenchie and Ice are squaring off, with James Armstrong as the special referee. Just when it looks like Frenchie might be in trouble, Armstrong hands off a pair of brass knuckles to the French Superstar. Ice goes down after a left handed blow to his temple, and Frenchie gets the pin. The scene dissolves again, and this time Frenchie is beaming.) And Slugger? You go around t'inking zat you're ze greatest in zis promotion? Delude yourself if you want to, but we all know ze trut'. If Pierre Delacroix left ze fWo, it would crumble in a matter of minutes. While you play your good guy/bad guy games, I have, and always will be, ze guy zat zose morons pay zeir money to see beat. Ze best part is zat zey very rarely get zeir money's wort'. Ze only ot'er man to worry about is Primetime. Unlike ze ot'er two chumps, Primetime is a talented at'lete who poses a very serious t'reat. He is ze rightful fWo World Champion right now. Unfortunately, zat's a postion I want badly. But Primetime, I'm already signed to face you at Twilight, in what is sure to be an epic contest. And while I'm not going to promise zat I won't try to win ze belt tonight, I give you zis. Unlike zose ot'er two schmucks, zat say business is business, you mean more to me zan zat belt. I've got you covered, Brian. If zose two talentless bastards try to pin you, I'm zere. And if zey try to take you out entirely, zey're dead. No ifs ands or buts about it. Ze Foundation has lasted zis long because we are ze perfect unit. Ze Dynasty will go ze way of ze Punisher Squad, mark my words. (TFF glances at his watch.) A little over two hours now. Primetime, may ze best man win. You ot'er two? Stay out of our way. (Frenchie stares intently into the camera as the scene fades to black.)
