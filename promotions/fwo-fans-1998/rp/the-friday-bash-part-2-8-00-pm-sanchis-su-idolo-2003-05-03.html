The Friday Bash - Part 2 - 8:00 PM - Sanchis Su Idolo

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At eight o'clock in the evening, Sanchis Su Idolo walked into the Friday Bash, armed with his finest apparel(an old "Breatholizer: Blow Here" t-shirt that he stole from Steve-O, a pair of tattered jeans, and some old sandals that he found in a trash can), and ready to hit it off with some foxy ladies. That is to say that he'll use some horrible pickup lines in his thick Mexican accent and generally be the most obnoxious human being in the party. He spotted the first female he saw, some frizzly-haired woman in a Hawaiian shirt, and proceeded to give him the "Sanchis charm". "Man, de Sanchis es soooooo tired... chu wanna sleep with de Sanchis?" he asked her. "Go away, Sanchis." the woman said with a sigh, as she knew who Sanchis "Su Idolo" Guilherme was and how rude he can be. She immediately left, leaving Sanchis behind. Sanchis looked slightly upset, but he'd be even more upset when he spun around and saw Steve-O standing behind him. "Hey. You're wearing my shirt." Steve-O said. Sanchis looked down at his Breatholizer shirt, looked at Steve-O, looked at his shirt, then looked at Steve-O again before he spoke his next few words, "De Sanchis, `e doesn't know what chore talkin' about." "No, seriously! Are you my twin or something?" Steve-O asked. "WHAAAAT? CHU? DE SANCHIS' BROTHER? De Sanchis feels det chu are... how chu say... trippin'." Sanchis responded. "Trippin'? I think De' Sanchis has DE STEVE-O'S shirt on too tight, it seems it's cutting off the oxygen to your brain!" Steve-O responded, standing all high and mighty. "CHA! Whatchu talkin' `bout, Willis? De Sanchis, `e `as been de RIGHTFUL owner of de T-shirt for maaaannnyyy nights of de dir-tee dance! Chu've never been DET far wit' de hembras, `ave chu?" Sanchis responded, becoming more obnoxious than ever by including the old Gary Coleman catchphrase into the mix. Steve-O smirked, "I dunno, ASK YOUR MOM!" That did it. Sanchis brought out the red-blooded, hot-headed, Mexican temper that he was... not really known for, since he was more known for cowardice and self-absorbtion, but I guess the point is that Sanchis shoved Steve-O back. Seeing that his 'shirt' was wrinkled, Steve-O became angry... VERY angry. So angry that he might, oh... I don't know... SHOOOOOVE SANCHIS BACK ~! Now, Steve-O, being just a wee bit less wussy than Sanchis was, not to mention having pushed Sanchis back in a way that would have him trip over a running poodle, sent Sanchis spiralling into the lap of that same frizzly-haired, Hawaiian shirt-clad woman that he had hit on just three minutes ago. "`EY! DE PROMISED LAND! DE SANCHIS `AS REACHED DE PROMISED LAND AT LAST! Now, bebe, we `AVE to make out!" Sanchis shouted, excitedly. The woman took just one second and one look at Sanchis before she forcefully shoved Sanchis forward, which sent him right into Steve-O once again. Steve-O was ready to pounce of the thief when something pretty caught his eye. "SHY-KNEE!" His eyes lit up and he staggered towards a shot glass that was right beside a lamp, forgetting all about Sanchis. Sanchis stood all high and mighty and proudly smiled, "CHA! DET'S RIGHT, HOLMES! DE SANCHIS `ES DE MAN OF DES HOUSE, HOLMES! WHAT CHU GOT? NOTHIN', DET'S WHAT CHU GOT, BEBE! DE SANCHIS DEMANDS DET ALL O' CHU GOOD LOOKIN' HEMBRAS COME AND GIVE DE SANCHIS SOME OF DE HOT ACT-- ooh!" Sanchis stopped in mid-statement, because he just spotted the frizzly-haired woman and again tried to hit on her, thinking that she's probably changed her mind about Sanchis for warding away the drunken stoner, "`EY! Chu wanna let de Sanchis floss `es teeth wit' chore pubic `air?" The woman proceeded to "accidently" trip Sanchis and send him to the floor, before calmly walking away as if nothing had happened. Sanchis looked up, still flat on his back on the floor, "`Ey! Was it somethin' de Sanchis said?"
