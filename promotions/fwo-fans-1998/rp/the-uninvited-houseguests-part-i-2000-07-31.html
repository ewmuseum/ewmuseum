The Uninvited Houseguests, Part I

31 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 30th, 2001 Time: 6:14AM Eastern Standard Time Location: An Apartment Complex, Detroit, MI The door to Donaven Winters' apartment swung wide open. Donaven put one step inside of his living room and dropped the suitcases that were in each hand. They landed with a dull thud and he kicked them further into the apartment. After closing the door behind him, he took off his long coat and threw it over the back of a ragged recliner. After letting out a loud sigh he walked towards the telephone and answering machine. The red dot blinked over and over again. He depressed the button and the first message began to play. "Hey Donaven, it's Monica. It's been over a year since I last talked to you and I wanted you to know that I was co–" Cut off. Directly over that a second message began to play. Donaven glared at the machine for cutting off the message from Monica. "Dark Boy, it's Crisp. What the hell was your problem out there last night? I'd like to say I'm sorry for those little comments I made about you, but I can't. I can't apologize for something that's true. Unfortunately true. You do realize that Universal is going to make us get together every week, right? Well, you do now. Just don't fuck around with me anymore. Got that?" The message ended, leaving Donaven even angrier. Partly because his former best friend was cut off by an imbecile who was followed by people just as moronic. He scowled at the answering machine before picking it up from the end table. In one quick motion the machine had ended against the wall, twelve feet away from him. It hit the floor and small pieces flew into the air for a moment. When it settled, Donaven walked over to the machine and stomped down onto it, crushing it under his foot. "Goddam it, Crisp," Winters yelled. "All you do is screw things up. You couldn't handle a little tap to the shoulder as a tag-in and you go berserk on me. And, you somehow get my phone number and call my house, erasing a very important message. You're just a waste of space, festering like the rest. I look forward to the day when your skull is filled with worm holes, maggots and the stench of death." Winters threw himself down on the recliner, almost flipping it over in the process. "You're treading on thin ice," he stated. "You've started out the exact same way Davis did. Universal wants to force us together, than so be it. I can handle working with your kind. I'll put up with it, I'll deal with it. Last night the match was ours until you decided to do what you did. We may be partners, but I want your blood on my hands, Jon. I want to bathe myself in your blood. I need to cleanse myself of your filth. It's the only way it will work. Our day of battle will come, Crisp. Until then, we're stuck together and we might as well deal with it." Outside of Donaven's apartment three men stood in front of the doorway. It was 6AM and these men had business to take care off. Each of them had a suitcase with them. The man in front of the other three, an African American knocked loudly on the door. Donaven was not going to be happy with this little surprise. "HEY FOO," the familiar voice of Mr. T rang out. "OPEN UP ‘DIS HERE DOOR!" Inside of the apartment, Donaven sat, trying to ignore T's yelling. "Hey, T," another familiar voice spoke. "Do we get to eat all of his food?" "SPHERE," T yelled again. "WE BE EATING ANYTHING WE WANT IN DIS SUCKA'S HOUSE!" "Hooray!" Sphere exclaimed. *Shimmy* "I AGREE," T screamed. "WE NEED TO CHEER THIS MAN UP!" T. banged loudly in the door again. This time they could hear the footsteps approaching. The first of the chain latches unlocked, then the second. The door slowly opened. Donaven peered out from around the small crack in the door. He knew who they were, but he didn't want to be dealing with them this early in the morning. "What the hell do you three want," Winters asked. "WE HAVE SPECIAL ORDERS FROM MR. CRISP, FOO," T. yelled into Donaven's ears. "Great," Donaven responded sarcastically. "What are the orders from that belligerent dolt?" *Shimmy* A blank look came over Donaven's face as La Parka moved his body around. "Uh," Sphere started. "Wow! You have pretty wallpaper!" Sphere walked into the living room and dropped his suitcase. He walked over to one of the walls and began to run his hand up and down the wall, making strange sounds as he felt the wallpaper. La Parka followed Sphere's lead, he walked in and dropped his suitcase next to Sphere's and went towards Donaven's radio system. He turned it on to some obscure Mexican station and began with the incessant shimmies. *Salsa-Shimmy* Winters could only shake his head as he waited for T. to tell him why they were there. "WELL FOO," T. started. "CRISP WANTS US TO STAY WITH YOU FOR A FEW DAYS! WE GONNA BE ROOMIES, SUCKA!" Mr. T slapped Donaven on the back while he laughed. Donaven stood at the doorway, his mouth was wide open, his eyes had begun to fill with rage. The door slammed shut and Donaven had no way of getting rid of the three roommates from hell. This was going to be one long week.
