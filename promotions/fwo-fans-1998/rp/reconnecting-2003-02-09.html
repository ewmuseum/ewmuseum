Reconnecting

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was just another evening. Cody watched a hockey game on ESPN while Clarisse was upstairs sewing a baby sweater. Matilda was sound asleep in her crib, not making a single noise. Suddenly the phone rang and woke the baby from her sleep. Clarisse dropped her needles and ran to Matilda while Cody rolled his eyes and picked up the cordless receiver. He clicked the talk button and held it to his ear. "Hello?" "Cody Williams please." The voice had a Japanese accent. Cody leaned forward. "Cody speaking. Who is this?" Without a pause the voice answered: "Tsumaguro Hyomon." "Tsumaguro Hyomon? Oh my god, Tokyo Grand Prix Wrestling! How have you been lately? How's the wrestling going on there?" "Incredible. Business is booming. Many of our wrestlers are coming along well. It's all going great. But I think we could really get our name on the map if we had the honor of your presence and the grace of your wrestling." Cody leaned back and smiled. "Come on, Hyomon, I'm not that good." "You are," said Hyomon. "In fact you're better than you've ever been before. Your fan base is bigger than it has ever been before. Cody Williams, you are a star now and the fans in Japan miss you dearly. I would appreciate you wrestling for me again." "When do you need me?" The calendar read February 9. Cody hoped it wouldn't be before Valentine's Day. "Whenever you're available. We're going to put the heavyweight title on you." His eyes widened. "Talk about getting my feet wet. Let me talk to my wife about this and then I'll try to make some travel arrangements." "No, don't make arrangements. I will make the arrangements for you and send you a first class ticket." "Okay great. Thanks for calling, Hyomon." "It was nice speaking to you again. Goodbye." "Bye." Cody put the phone on the coffee table and leaned back into the sofa. He felt like a slouch just sitting. "Tokyo, Japan. Hmm. Tokyo, Japan." An idea popped into his head. "Hey Clarisse," he yelled out, "wouldn't you say it's time we had a vacation?"
