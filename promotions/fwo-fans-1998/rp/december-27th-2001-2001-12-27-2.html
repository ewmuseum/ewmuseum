"I don't know what to think, Ivy. I'm hardly the needy, whiny, emotional girl, but I'm also getting kinda sick of being good enough to sleep with but not good enough to confide in." "Trust me, Angel.... I'm not even good enough to confide in when it comes to Eli." December 27th, 2001 Part 2 of 2: The Angel (real), The Angel (assumed), and The Psycho Bitch. Sitting in Ivy McGinnis' Queens apartment, the woman herself is glad to have a little time to catch her breath during the holidays. She's involved in several different aspects of the wrestling business and has not had the opportunity to accompany Eli Flair to the ring all too much.... but that will change. Ivy is dressed in a pair of black leather pants, and a black tank top. Sitting across from her at the kitchen table, warming her hands on a mug of coffee, is Eli Flair's girlfriend Angel, wearing black jeans, a babydoll T-shirt that simply says *FUCKER on it, and her hair in a ponytail. She's got that "pale, sexy, goth" thing going, as per usual. This time to visit has been good, but Eli has wandered off again. "I'm about ready to say fuck it, though, Ivy," said Angel, in complete deadpan, "At least with Mike I knew how little I meant to him as a lover. Eli plays two opposite parts, and I never know which one I'm getting." "Listen, kiddo," replied Ivy, "did he tell you what today is?" "Nope." "Seven years ago, my sister died." Speechless. "They never found her body," continued Ivy, "and Eli still blames himself for taking her on the road with him." "Holy.... Ivy, I never knew," Angel looked very contrite in her apology, "He never told me." "Is it any wonder?" asked Ivy, "I bet to Eli, you're just another in a mid- sized line of women he's loved or put his trust in, and it's just a matter of time before you turn around and put the knife in his heart." "Nuh- uh, you're just being melodramatic." "Am I? Eli is the type of man who doesn't think before he jumps. He dives into things headfirst, because he's always believed people are intrinsicly good. And, either through action, omission, or death, there is only one woman he's ever loved who has not betrayed that trust." "One woman? Me?" Ivy sips her coffee, shaking her head sadly. "Sorry, hon," she says, "but I think he's just waiting for the other shoe to drop with you. His friend April, who he never got together with. And honestly, I think it's on his mind that she never hurt him because she didn't let herself be put in that position." Angel sinks in her chair, looking down on the floor, frustrated. "I have a lot of feelings for him, Ivy," she begins, "but I don't have the strength to fight for him against the ghosts of his past." "You don't have to fight the ghosts of his past.... just be there for him." "I am there for him, Ivy.... but if he doesn't want me around, or at least doesn't ACT like he wants me around, I'm not one to overstay my welcome." "Please don't," said Ivy, "Whether Eli knows it or not, he needs you, and he loves you just as much as you love him. Give him some time." Angel's eyes betray her calm demeanor, and show just how conflicted she truly is. "Ivy," she finally said, "I don't know what all those other women were doing with him, but I'm sacrificing a lot to be here. I've got a band that's on the verge of a deal back in California, and I've got a sometimes- violent ex boyfriend, ex guitarist who wants back on the payroll. Hell, Mike wrote half the music that goes with my words anyways, he deserves to be there. But if we get to the dance, and we go on bigger tours than our current scrounging.... I'm going to go, with Eli's blessing or without." Ivy's eyes betray her calm demeanor now.... about to bitch Angel out for her selfish attitude toward her relationship with Eli.... but stops herself when she realizes the most important thing. Angel is one hundred percent right. She can't be expected to wait around while Eli figures out whether he can trust her with his insecurities or not. And she can't be expected to put her whole life - her band and her music - on hold for him. But.... such is life. "Where do I fit in? Where's my place here? Where is my beautiful?" -Lennon Murphy
