Coca Cola, girlfriends and the evil toothbrush

15 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Coca Cola, girlfriends and the evil toothbrush February 15, 2000 Machina looked into the camera and smiled. Okay Machina, we're ready for our final take. Machina straightened his back in the chair and put on his winning smile, the one that always got him the chicks. Machina? Uh, you've got something in your teeth... Machina wiped at his teeth and came away with a crumb. He frowned at it. Where did it come from? It wasn't breakfast... could it be yesterday's lunch? It could be, he didn't have a toothbrush. The other night he had awoken to find the toothbrush had somehow gotten out of his travel bag and was laying on the pillow next to him. Seeing all those bristles jutting out with the special gum pick just creeped him out. He had taken the toothbrush and buried it in a deserted lot behind the hotel. Still, he couldn't quite get the idea out of his head that the toothbrush was at this moment digging itself out of the dirt and crawling back to the hotel to hide in the closet. Machina? Machina shook his head to clear his thoughts. He was here to film another Coke commercial. The director and filming crew looked like they were starting to get impatient, and Machina attempted to be all business. The checks for this Coke sponsorship deal were pretty big, and Machina liked the money. He was hoping he could save up enough to buy one of those million dollar diamond bras that Victoria's Secret were offering. He didn't have a use for it, but it was just so strange and weird that he had to have it. A diamond bra. Weird, man. Okay Machina, we are ready to start filming, just pick up the Coke can and lick the top like we discussed. Okay... and... action! Machina started in, and his thoughts drifted away again. Drifted to Penny. He was sure lucky to have Penny, and at Warfare she was supposed to tell him her answer. Would she marry him? He was sure he did. Who could resist him? And if he had a diamond bra by then, hey... icing on the cake. Yeah, this Coke deal was pretty sweet. He was going to get paid to do a Coke spot at Warfare. Big bucks... and hey who doesn't like Coke? Certainly nobody. I mean, if someone did hate Coke then he was sure that the appearance at Warfare would be totally ruined. But for now... Do do do do do, always Coca Cola! Machina beamed as the filming ended. Cut! And... that's a wrap. Thanks Machina! Machina gave him the thumbs up sign. The director was a nice guy, and Machina was starting to suspect that he might be on the other side of the fence. He would have to remember to mention his name to Sid... speaking of which, what was Sid doing right now? Probably getting coffee with that wet end David he suspected.
