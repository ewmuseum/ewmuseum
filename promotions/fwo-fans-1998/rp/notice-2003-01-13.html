13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Terrible. That's how things were now in the FWO. Just terrible. Scott Slugger, while never quite the competitor he thought himself to be, getting carved to pieces and killing himself for the crowd's amusement, when his pedestrian act would have sufficed six months ago. Wretch being hauled out as some sort of champion of the Old Guard. And Eron, the proverbial red-headed step child of the FWO, having the audacity to tell some of the newer wrestlers that they're overrated. Yes, he really said that, without any trace of irony in his voice. Well, that may be good enough for them, fighting like children. Squabbling over whose member was the biggest. But some on the roster had not forgotten what this business was all about. Not rivalries involving pregnant exes. Not whom blacklisted whom. Not even the sense of competition, and pride in a job well done. No, like all businesses, this industry was about money. Cold hard cash. And to earn the green, first you've got to get the gold. As much as anyone else, Frenchie understood that. But any title wouldn't do. It had to be one with respect, and real competition. The Cruiserweight division had long been a mainstay for just that, and looked to be there again, but sadly time and a hectic traveling schedule had taken its toll on The Flying Frenchie, and he no longer qualified for said division. But Chronos had solved that. Right now, the Tag Team Division in the FWO was about as large as it had ever been, and filled with actual specialists, not just men haphazardly thrown together. Ric had done the research, Ric had gathered the tapes, and shown Frenchie what had been going on under his own nose. This was the path to glory. And come Wednesday, the FWO would see that Frenchie and his partner were just the men to march down it. Little did it matter that the rights to the number one contendership were being fought without them. In time, they would find themselves in a contest with the titles on the line, and they would take them. They were too talented not too, eventually. But their first step was Warfare, and Damnation. They would never know what hit them. The Tag Teams of the FWO were now on notice.
