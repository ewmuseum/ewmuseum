In Between Time(pt. 1)

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(In the bottom right hand corner of the screen, the UltraSHOWTIME logo appears beneath the 'LAST WEEK' graphic.) BP: It's time now for the first half of our double main event! DDD: Woo-hoo! (CUE UP: "Set it Off" - Shuvel blares as the curtain opens and Sean "Triple X" Stevens steps through in a white, wife- beater, with a blue "TX" logo on the front, black & white tights, with his trademark "X" in several areas around the pants legs, and rear... and, matching black boots. His hair was hanging freely as he made his way to the ring, but once he slid inside, he removed a rubberband from his wrist and tied his hair into a pony- tail, and awaited the announcer.) JOHN MULLINS: Ladies and Gentlemen... this next contest is likely to be a WAR. First... standing to my left... He weighs in at 247lbs, and stands nearly 6ft, 2inches tall... Hailing from Orlando, Florida... THE "BLUE-EYED BADASS"! ... SEAAAAN "TRRIIIPPPPPPLE X" STEEEEEEVENS!! BP: I've seen Sean Stevens in big time matches in the past... and, have had the privilege of seeing him, both focused and intense... but, his intensity on today is off the charts. Just in case you don't know... Last week at Messiah of the Mat, Stevens seemingly had Scott Johnson all but beat... DDD: It appeared that way, didn't it? Who says Scott wouldn't have kicked out of the Factor-X? Huh? You don't know... you're just assuming! BP: Like I was saying, as Sean was going in for the kill... Max Blackshire interfered, for what - at the time - looked like no reason at all, and cost Stevens a shot at the Superior Title. ("Cochise" by Audioslave blares through the PA system as a red-light bathes the entranceway. Stepping through the mist with a sadistic grin hiding behind his wet, blond, stringy hair ...is Max Blackshire.) JOHN MULLINS: And his opponent ... he hails from Liverpool, England ... he is ... MAX ... BLACKSHIRE!!! (Blackshire approaches the ring with the same, dented, blood-red painted steel chair that he had brought with him to UltraSHOWTIME to "make his statement". To mark his proverbial territory. Eyes locked with Stevens in the ring, Max sets the chair near the ringsteps and slyly enters the ring.) BP: The referee is standing in between these two individuals... trying desperately to hold them apart. Max Blackshire has started with the mind games already... That red chair he brought down to ringside, Danny? Look at the dent in it... it's the same steel chair that cost Stevens the match last week at the pay- per- view. Sean Stevens grabs Canvas Jones by the collar and escorts him to the corner... He turns around to face his opponent–- LEFT HOOK BY MAX THAT SENDS SEAN ON HIS BACK! (Ding, Ding, Ding.) DDD: Max plays by a whole different set of rules. I don't think Triple X has ever had an opponent like him. Stevens can wrestle... but, in order to beat Max... he's gonna have to fight! And, I don't think he can. BP: Triple X is back up... Max is stalking him while he uses the turnbuckle for support... he charges... CLOTHESLINE! Triple X bounces off the turnbuckle into Max's waiting arms... SPINEBUSTER! And, look... Max isn't going for the pin... he's lifting Sean to his feet, by his hair... OH MY GOD! MAX TOSSED STEVENS ACROSS THE RING, ALMOST... USING NOTHING BUT... HAIR! DDD: Max is ruthless... Just look at the war's he's had with his rotting brother, Cyrus... Locked inside those cages. That almost lethal RING of FIRE match ... Sean isn't ready. BP: ...did you just say... "rotting brother"? ... have some respect, Danny! Triple X is trying to make his way to his feet, as Max makes his way over to where he is... Max with another handful of hair... I think he's gonna try it one more... LOW BLOW BY TRIPLE X! Blackshire falls to the canvas holding his crotch for dear life... SPEAR! TRIPLE X IS ON TOP WAILING AWAY!! RIGHTS AND LEFTS! Canvas Jones begins with the five count... DDD: Triple X appears to have lost his composure. That's what Max thrives on. It's exactly what he wants, and Trip is playing right into his hands! BP: Triple X is up on his feet... He's waiting on Max... slowly, Blackshire gets up... STANDING DROPKICK! Blackshire gets tangled up in the ropes... and, the crowd is going wild! RIGHT BY TRIPLE X... LEFT! Triple X steps back... he's signaling for it... FACTOR–- NO! MAX KICKED STEVENS DIRECTLY IN THE GENITALS FULL FORCE! Triple X is on the mat, screaming at the top of his lungs in pain!! We might have to end this thing early, Danny! DDD: I... I... think he's crying!! What goes around comes around! Trip drilled Max with a low-blow, and now, Max returns the favor! BP: Max untangles himself... He raises Triple X to his feet... LUNGING DDT! He's down for the count... ONE, TWO, THRE— TRIPLE X GOT A SHOULDER UP! Max is up on his feet... he sends Sean to the ropes... he bounces off... SLINGSHOT! STEVENS' THROAT BOUNCED OFF THAT TOP ROPE! And, look at Max... he's standing over Triple X's body... smiling. He's enjoying this! DDD: Can't say he didn't warn us all! Triple X brought this on himself... HE talked the talk, like he was tough. Now he has to suffer the consequences. Maybe after Max humbles him... he'll be a little less egotistical. I hope so! BP: Don't get ahead of yourself, Danny! Max lifts Sean up again... GERMAN–- wait... Triple X hangs on, and counters with a victory roll... ONE, TW–- Blackshire kicks out... Stevens is still down and Max is already on his feet. He hooks him again... BELLY- TO- BACK AND I THINK TRIPLE X LANDED RIGHT ON HIS NECK! OH MY GOD! DDD: Oh my God is right! Stevens slid outside of the ring... I think he's gonna give it up. Get himself intentionally counted out! What a coward! BP: I don't think that's true. Sean is probably having a hard time figuring out where he's at... he could be hurt permanently if he injures his neck... I think he's trying to get himself together. DDD: Well... he won't have long. Max is right behind him! BP: Half of Triple X's body is underneath the ring... Max pulls him out... he's gonna... FIRE EXTINGUISHER!! TRIPLE X JUST SPRAYED THE EMERGENCY FIRE EXTINGUISHER IN MAX'S FACE! The referee's giving him a warning... but, it doesn't matter... the damage is already done... Max can hardly see... Triple X is still groggy... but, he manages to slide inside the ring, and climb the turnbuckle... BODY TACKLE! TRIPLE X CONNECTS! And, both men are down... The referee's beginning his count!! What action! DDD: I told you... Stevens is trying to get the both of them DQ'ED! Smart man... BP: Canvas Jones is on eight... Triple X barely makes it into the ring, and slides back out! He has Max up... he tosses him inside the ring... Max is trying to make it to his feet, and he does... barely... Triple X measures him... FACTOR-X! TRIPLE X JUST PLANTED MAX BLACKSHIRE WITH ONE OF THE HARDEST SUPERKICKS I'VE EVER SEEN! The referee's down... ONE..... TWO..... THRE–- NOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Max got a shoulder up! DDD: He can't beat, Max. He's a machine... an... evil machine. But, a machine nonetheless! BP: Stevens appears to be flustered... He's up on his feet... I think he's gonna try it again... SUPERK–- Max caught his leg... SHIN WRENCH! HE COULD'VE KNOCKED STEVENS' KNEE FROM IT'S SOCKET! And, the way Triple X is screaming in pain... I think he just may have done that! Max is moving in... STIFF KICK TO STEVENS' SORE LEG! Sean is trying desperately to crawl away... but, Max won't let him get but so far... DDD: No right leg... No Superkick. No Superkick... no finishing maneuver. No finishing maneuver... No win. No win... means a loss. I can see all of this and I'm not psychic! BP: Stevens is trying to use the ropes for leverage... Max is closing in, yet again... Stevens kicks with his good leg... Max catches it! ROUNDHOUSE WITH HIS BAD LEG! MAX GOES FLYING IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION! And... And... CANVAS JONES GOES DOWN WITH HIM! DDD: No referee... No rules. No rules means... Max can now do anything he wants in there. I see this match ending soon. BP: Max is back up to his feet... HAYMAKER BY STEVENS! Max retaliates with a Solid punch of his own. STEVENS WITH ANOTHER! MAX! STEVENS! MAX! THESE TWO MEN ARE STANDING TOE TO TOE, TRADING PUNCHES IN THE MIDDLE OF THE RING! Triple X nose is busted... and, he appears to have a cut under his right eye. Max seems a little better off... but, he still has a red welt on his cheek! DDD: Triple X can't take too much more of this, though... BP: Knee to the gut by Blackshire... He hooks Stevens' head... SPIKED PILEDRIVER! STEVENS IS OUT! THIS CROWD IS GOING NUTS... and, the referee... is... down! Max is up on his feet... he notices the referee's down... and, turns his attention to the outside... What is he doing? (Max's dark swivel to ringside and suddenly glint with delight. Reaching over the top rope, Max grabs ahold of something as Sean Stevens begins to slowly stir.) DDD: 'What's he doing?' Finishing things! Finishing Triple X! BP: Oh no! He has that steel chair he used on Triple X at the Pay Per View! I think he's gonna try and finish Sean off for good... Forget taking his time... Max is gonna try and do to Sean, what FATE prevented him from doing to his brother... and, I think it's sick. Triple X is... TRYING to make it to his feet. Max approaches him... SPINNING HEEL KICK! Triple X summoned some strength from... somewhere... and, nailed Max off guard with a heel kick, causing him to drop that chair! The referee's showing signs of life... as Triple X takes a step towards that chair... DDD: He shouldn't do this... I'm telling you now. If Sean takes a swing at Max with that chair... he'll take this whole thing to a new level. This is a bad career move... BP: Triple X picks up the chair... the crowd is egging him on... Thank God! Canvas Jones is up... and, he has a grip on the other end. He's warning Sean... and, Stevens appears to be listening... Wait! No! Sean used all of his weight and pushed his end of the chair, knocking the referee to the mat! He approaches Max... Cocks back... CHAIRSHOT BY STEVENS!!!!! DDD: Damn... that had to hurt... (Stevens adjusts his grip on the chair as his wide-eyes glare at Max, staggering backwards from the impact of steel on skull...) BP: But, Max... is... Up! He went down for a second... but, he's popped right back up... and, he's... He's TELLING SEAN TO HIT HIM AGAIN! Triple X cocks back again... ANOTHER SHOT DIRECTLY ON THE SKULL! And, Max goes down... BLEEDING PROFUSELY! Canvas Jones is calling for the bell... I think he's gonna award this match to Max Blackshi–-MAX IS UP AGAIN!!! OH MY GOD! THIS... IS... FRIGHTENING! What do you have to do to STOP this man?!!? DDD: Kill him. Drive a stake right through his heart. I'm convinced... Max isn't human. And, the more Sean swings at him... the more driven he is to END Triple X's career ... end his life! (The screen turns black for a moment... and, when it lights back up... all you see at the moment... is an announcer standing in the center of the ring.) MULLINS: Ladies and Gentlemen... the referee has stopped the match. The winner... By Disqualification... MAAAAXXX BLACCCCKSHIR— BP: OH MY GOD! MAX BLACKSHIRE JUST CHARGED A CHAIR-LESS TRIPLE X AND THEY'RE BOTH ON THE MAT SWINGING WILDLY! SECURITY IS FLOODING THE RING TRYING TO SEPARATE THESE TWO INDIVIDUALS TO NO AVAIL!! MAX IS STILL SWINGING WILDLY, AS TRIPLE X HAS A HANDFUL OF HIS HAIR, NAILING HIS DIRECTLY IN THE FACE WITH A COUPLE OF SHOTS HIMSELF! THIS THING HAS GOTTEN OUT OF CONTROL! DDD: I agree... We need more help out here. BP: More security has entered the fray... and, the two superstars are finally seperated.... Triple X is still trying to pry his way through the bodies toward Max... but, look at Max... he just calmly exits the ring, with a... Smile? Max Blackshire is smiling! This man is nuts! Let's go to a commercial while we clean this mess up!!
