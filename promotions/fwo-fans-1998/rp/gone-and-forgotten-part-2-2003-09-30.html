Gone and Forgotten, Part 2

30 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thursday, August 21st, 2003- 11:07 p.m. EST It was just minutes after the broadcast of that night's fWo Uprising had gone off the air- a show that featured many matches, living up to the usual quality expected of an fWo show. One match in particular stood out, however, and that was Voran Xias vs. Eron. For the fans, it had been a last chance for the constantly losing and rapidly declining Xias, who hadn't one a match since a while before Meltdown. He had promised to give it his all against his longtime idol Eron, and on that night that's exactly what he did. He put all of his heart and effort into that match, and despite his defeat at the hands of his opponent, he made an impression on the fans that night. One that said he was perhaps a man worthy of being their hero, rather than their villain. But none of the fans knew just why Xias had put so much effort into that match. None of them knew what had happened to Xias just days earlier- nor did they know what Xias was planning to do following that show's conclusion. The only man who knew that was Voran Xias. And as Xias approached the office of Silver Fox, wiping the few drops of sweat that still clinged onto his neck from the match nearly an hour ago with a white towel, his smile was nearly ear-to-ear. He knew what he was about to do, and he couldn't wait to do it. He opened the door to Silver Fox's office, seeing the old man packing up his things, ready to get going. Fox smiled at Xias. "What can I do for you, young man?" he asked him. Really, the young Voran had gotten a lot nicer, mellow, and easier-to-work-with in the past month or so, and it was shown by Fox's reaction. Two months earlier and he would have shouted at him to be left alone following the end of the show, but here his reaction was far more pleasant. That only made what Xias had planned even more delightful. "You can go fuck yourself, that's what." Fox gasped in shock. "What the hell is wrong with you!" "I quit. See you around, you old bastard." And with those words, he turned and left the office, dropping the sweat-filled towel behind him and onto the concrete floor. Silver Fox called after him, as baffled as one would be in the situation, but Xias didn't answer. He just threw his long black hair back over his shoulder, and briskly walked up the rampway that lead to the garage exit, and soon out of the arena. Silver Fox looked down at the towel. It would be the last of Voran Xias he would see for quite some time. Xias was nearly ecstatic as he entered his black BMW. Closing the door behind him, he leaned back into his chair, not even turning on the car, just pausing a moment to take all of it in. He leaned his chair back and smiled. "Rest in peace, Voran." He drove away from the arena, never once daring to look back.
