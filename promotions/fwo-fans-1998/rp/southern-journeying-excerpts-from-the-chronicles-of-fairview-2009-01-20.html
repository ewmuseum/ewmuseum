Southern Journeying (...excerpts from The Chronicles of Fairview Reed)

20 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My shaking hand cannot be helped – our entourage is currently ‘on the road’ as they say and though I am elated to be moving forward my penmanship suffers greatly for it… We’ve been on the road for several days, traveling through the Southern States of the Colonies, and Mrs. McPhineas chides me constantly for missing the passing scenery. I would opine that if you have seen one ‘Waffle House’ you have indeed seen them all. Besides, with the arrival of Mr. Yip and Mr. Werner and all of their precious priceless cargo I have been able to get back to my research for the first time in many, many months! I can state with all of my honesty and integrity I am convinced that my discovery of the legend of The 9 Books and the initial confrontation with The Hunting Party was no coincidence – The Otherlanders appeared BECAUSE I found the passage referencing The 9 Books! It is my belief that The Man In The Suit Made Of Grass is shaken, certainly, and perhaps a bit nervous as well, to send such formidable warriors against we mere mortals… Our trip across The Pond seems to have thwarted The Hunting Party, as we have had no more encounters since our arrival, as I daresay I expected. These last few weeks have been tremendously refreshing, and now I feel a certain sense of comfort being reunited with my books, one in particular. Deep in the pages of The Ravings of St. Barastius I was rewarded for my dedicated excavation of this dusty, forgotten lore. I know there is another way in… 9 Books 9 Steps 9 Clues In a Lande not yet found A Circle…around A Circle…around A Circle I know there is another way in… Barastius (circa 1340) is generally met with skepticism (at the least!) but I believe I have pored through it so thoroughly I can separate the wheat from the chaff. To wit - when I first found this particular passage I felt the presence of magic – an ancient magic (Aureate Magic???) pass through me and around me and I knew in that instant that St. Barastius had written those words for my eyes! And that night The Hunting Party came for us… Our monstrous transport has stopped, and Mrs. McPhineas has called for a brief respite from her position in the operator’s chair. I am certain that she will INSIST that I leave this Leviathan on wheels and walk around aimlessly for reasons I cannot fathom. … … And there she is. I shall return shortly.
