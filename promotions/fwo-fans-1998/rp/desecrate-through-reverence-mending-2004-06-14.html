Desecrate Through Reverence: Mending

14 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No matter how many fights and scuffles there were, she still loved watching every single move he made. Just finishing the reps, Brandon placed the heavy weight back on the retaining bar as the metal poles quivered underneath. Even now, a full hour of full tilt exertion, the man most called Brand Frontier was still filled with anger and frustration. It had been barely two weeks since someone he would call mentor was crippled at the hands of other so-called 'professional' wrestlers and nothing looked to be closer to resolution. On top of that Brandon Roche saw his personal life in a state of upheavel as well. Amy, the kids and himself had been in the midst of moving in to their new apartment in Orlando when everything started to get complicated again. Out of the woodwork came an ex-boyfriend of Amy's. He brought with him the need for the smaller redhead to 'confess' past sins and unveil the newest twist in their ongoing soap opera. Amy had a child all her own. A little girl to be precise, a child born in questionable circumstances. A child that for the life of him Brandon still couldn't figure out why he wasn't already told about. Now he looked over to the woman he called wife and couldn't help but smile. In the midst of everything unraveling the way it had it had somehow drawn them closer together. Her flame red hair was matted to the back of her neck. The thin gray sports bra and matching shorts were stained with sweat. She too seemed to have her own inner demons in need of taming. She too looked to have failed in that attempt this day. Brandon looking at her, though, had caused her to stop kicking the bag almost in mid-stride, providing a comical break in the tension as the 'red raver' nearly fell to the ground from the weight of the returning bag. "Shit," she gumbled, "Stupid bag." The tiny redhead looked over to her husband, who seemed to be holding back a small spree of laughter. "Yeah, yeah." smiled Amy, "Why the sudden staring? I could get tired of it and kick your ass like that bag did mine." And in response Brandon simply... kept staring. It lasted a few seconds longer until it had driven Amy to the special place of discomfort she often fought back when in front of the cameras and crowds attending the shows. Finally she caved, a thunderous "gaaaah" escaping her lips before she stormed away and through the closest doorway. Right in to the men's locker room. Brandon smiled wide, standing there, counting in his head. By 'five' the red haired and now red faced raver emerged, before attempting to storm off again. It never works a second time as effective as the first, however, and she had lost much of her steam. "Wait." Her husband called out finally. "Come here. I was just messing with ya, wondering how long it'd take you to 'fold'." Amy reluctantly, though playfully, stepped toward Brandon. "Well, if anything...after a glance around that locker room, you're a much bigger man in my eyes." she winked. "Is there somewhere we can sit and talk for a sec, though? I've been thinking more and more about the whole...ermm...Scerinn 'issue'." She wiped sweat from her brow, finding locks of her hair clinging soaked to her forehead. "Oh, and this fucking Floridian heat. I'm Canadian, damnit!" "Sure..." Brandon placed a hand on the small of her back to usher to a secluded spot. It only took a second however before he was gently removing said hand, covered in sweat, grimacing as he did. Aimz chuckled, "Back in Nova Scotia, I'd kill to have weather causing me to sweat this badly in early June." "Anyway," she continued, "I found Scerinn and...uhh...well, think I could fly out to get her after this week's show?" "So... um, did you name her?" "Nope, that was Reno's disaster." Amy laughed, "But really...do you think we could handle it? I mean, she's not so young that she can't be babysat..." "Yeah, of course. We can handle anything... right?" Amy looked up, not sure if he was being comical, sardonic or using a tongue-in-cheek manner to address the situation at hand. What she found was a look of sincerity. "I can go with if you'd like, or I can stay behind and get this disaster of an apartment together. With her coming to live with us and the like, Jarod won't exactly be thrilled to having to share a room with Stephen." When Amy winced, Brandon hugged her tight. "We'll make it work." Amy smiled at that response. "We always do, don't we?" [BACK to "The Voice Within"] or [FAST FORWARD to "Plotting the Course"]
