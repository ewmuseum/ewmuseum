Burnt Offerings

15 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Amy's skull had obviously been split open by some otherworldly force... Her stomach was churning, but somehow not as badly as was normal in her post-drinking 'rituals'... And nobody was there. Or so she assumed, from her position...having fallen with a pain-filled thud onto the mangy hotel carpeting moments before. "Nnnngh...I'm gonna die..." Cue Brandon Roche walking in to the room with what could only be described as vile smelling rotten garbage or, using someone elses olefactory senses other than Amy's, breakfast. A stack of wheat pancakes and scrambled eggs filled the plate, their odor permeating the air, invading the raver's nostrils violently. The cheery expression on the Paragon's face was total indication that he knew exactly what he was doing. Stepping next to his prone, floored girlfriend Brandon kneeled. Pulling a piece of lean bacon from the tray the brave soul waved it under her nose, her eyes twitched. "Good morning sunshine! Good morning sunshine! You haven't forgotten about the radio interview we have this morning have you? We need to get to the gym soon so we can hit the station before we roll out of here..." Mr. Frontier knew that in the state his lover was in he was doing nothing but cheating death, but for the life of him he couldn't help it. "How many times have I ever reminded you how not funny you are? Is this torture because I went to Beer Festival?" Amy growled, trying to roll halfway under the bed, at least away from Brandon. "Get food away from me...I'm not going anywhere this morning - Fox thinks I'm sick, just tell the rest that. I'm riding with Eli 'n Ivy later...I need my rest damnit!" "I'm... not trying to be funny. Funny is watching you turn twelve different shades of green. Priceless." Brandon smirked as he tossed the slightly scorched pig meat into his mouth. As it crunched in his mouth he continued, "Fox can think you're sick, and yes this is punishment for the festival. We've a commitment to keep. Get up and let's make like a cheap Ed Wood movie, get the dead walking and all..." "You keep the commitment. I have...ehhh...the stomach flu. There'll be no problem." Somehow, the redhead managed to ooze up onto the mattress, sheltering herself underneath a down-filled comforter. The muffled, inevitable threat came from underneath - "I'm seriously gonna kill you if you make me leave this room, Brandon - not kidding." Sliding up next to her on the bed and patting her on the head, maybe a tad harder than proper, the mighty hero continued to push his luck. "But dear, I'm not making you do anything, I'm just giving voice to your conscience. How guilty are you going to feel if you miss the interview knowing all those kids are going to be at the station waiting to see you?" After swatting his hand away, Amy groaned..."Don't care. And that's not funny...'Let's make an example of Amy's hangover for all the kids!'." She muttered, "I'm not moving...with God as my witness, I'm not moving - nothing on this planet could so much as budge me." The small bucket of ice Brandon had retrieved from the machine just minutes earlier told a different story. With the fate of his future resting squarely on those tiny wet cubes he raised his eyebrows and reached for the container. After considering the consequences for another split second Brandon grabbed the edge of the comforter and in one swift motion it was pulled away, the cold daggers thrown on Amy's back and the blanket replaced. One swift motion, one fateful decision. Hero as he was he knew when not to be somewhere and in the same movement Brandon launched himself to the far corner of the room, close to the bathroom door, close to safety. That was the end of that! Amy shot up from the bed, eyes lit with a fire that forced the Paragon to brace himself for the possibility of a lamp or night table flying at his head...but nothing came. She turned away, tied her hair back nonchalantly.............and jumped. Squarely onto the chest of Brandon Roche - with a glass of water from through the night in hand. "The ice in this kept it cold. And we know I never like to be the only one in a room freezing their ass off." She smirked, tipping the glass upside down on her Mighty Hero's face and jumping backwards to avoid the splash. With the water dripping from his face, Brandon looked at the red eyed raver and grinned. The tinge of anger in her voice was highlighted by a sense of whimsey. He knew he had finally crossed the line and the next moment would tell whether he and Deacon would be sharing a hotel room for the next few nights. "I'm going back to bed now." Amy began after nabbing a towel to press on her back. "And you're not going to mess with me, or I'll make sure you'll enjoy the luxuries of using floors and couches as beds for the rest of your life." "No. no. no. no. no... you're up. We'll get some coffee in you and some muffins and you'll be right as rain. It's not my fault or the stations fault that you all decided to tie one on. Bitch at me if you like but I was up as late as all of you, and here I am ready to go." Pulling on Amy's hand Brandon emphasized his insistance by pulling her to him and kissing her full on the lips. Hopefully that would awaken her more than the ice. "Smooth as you are..." Amy pulled back, diving again under the cover of the bed's blankets. "This is the one time I'm not moving. And I can't drink coffee anyway...so you're lost there." "Hmph." Looking down at the small, curled form under the covers the fWo's paragon nearly joined her. The temptation was there but he knew that he had promises to keep and even though she talked about them not spending time together it would be best for her to get her sleep. They had made plans for the party finally back at the Frontier Ranch. That date was closing in as well as their promise of an honest-to-god double date with Eli and Angel, business left behind. He conceeded and as she nestled in under the warm comforter, he smiled an uneasy smile. He wasn't the happiest that she continued to blow off her responsibilites like this but, for now, it was a battle best left unfought. ...Well, playfully fought at the very least.
