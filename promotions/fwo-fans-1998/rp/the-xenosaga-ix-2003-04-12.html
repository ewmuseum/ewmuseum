The Xenosaga: IX

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For about five seconds, that is. Then the triumph faded due to the fact that he realized just how much he had completely misunderstood what had happened for the last four hours. And, in fact, he openly questioned it. "Hey, what in the HELL was with the last four hours?" the Codemaster asked. Dingleberry grimaced, as he was still in pain from being kicked in his Australian National Pride and wasn't prepared for any actual straining tasks such as the most difficult one for him to endure: thinking. "Oy! What're you babbling about, you bloomin' tart?" Dingleberry asked. "Well, I mean, there was a perfectly good 'Ram Destroying Catapult' right over there, why didn't we use that?" the Codemaster said, pointing at a catapult that had a large sign next to it. RAM DESTROYING CATAPULT Throws large, heavy things at big, dumb rams. Child-proof cap equipped to ensure that the children can't accidently throw themselves or shoot themselves with this highly volatile weapon of ram-squashing destruction. Cagle scratched his head at it. "That there chiyeld-proof cap was confyousin'!" Cagle shouted. "Oy agree, tha bugger was too much even for me." Dingleberry agreed. The Codemaster threw his hands(well, okay, his good one since the other's dislocated and all) up into the air in frustration, "THIS is the team that managed to kidnap me and foil my previous escape attempt? I am suddenly very embarassed for myself. You know what? I am outta here." The Codemaster started to walk away, but the Tallywhackers were just a wee bit more wily than the Codemaster had them figured for, as they slowly stood up and started to stalk him from behind. When the Codemaster suddenly realized that they were following him, he spun around. "Oh, for the love of God..." the Codemaster whispered to himself, before he spun around and quickened his pace. As did the Tallywhackers. The Codemaster started to run. So did the Tallywhackers. The Codemaster narrowly avoided falling down a small cliff. The Tallywhackers did not. "Aw, BUGGER!" Dingleberry shouted before he and Cagle fell. The Codemaster smiled and looked down the cliff, before he ran off in the other direction. At the bottom of the ten foot cliff, Dingleberry turned to Cagle. "CROIKEY, this is all YOUR fault." Dingleberry said, before he took off his hat and struck Cagle in the head with it.
