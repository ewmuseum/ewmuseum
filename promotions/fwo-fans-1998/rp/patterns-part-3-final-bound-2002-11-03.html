Patterns (Part 3, Final): "Bound"

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thursday October 31st, 2002 5:03pm Home It had been well over an hour since her mother left, and even longer since Darcy stormed out, but Amy's mood was only getting better. She was just hanging up the reciever of a portable phone when the door opened a crack and the bright, though forlorn eyes of Markson himself peered through, surveying the "war zone" for any signs of his proverbial enemy. And, of course, there she was...sitting on the couch, facing the doorway expectantly and trying her hardest to appear as if she was still angry for pride's sake. The only word the redhead uttered was "Back?", and she was using the softest of tones, though it contained a steely edge. "Don't start, Amy." was his only reply. The ponytail was now gone, and it looked as if Darcy had a bit to drink himself in his absence. She was still trying to look as angry as possible, and simply snarled "Don't talk like I'm three years old." "I'm not. I'm talking like I didn't come back just so we could pick up right where we left off. I'm tired and I don't want to keep up the same old battle." "Meh," Amy gritted her teeth. Despite her perkiness, his comments actually began to annoy her. "You're the tired one, huh? You didn't just wait around all afternoon for me to come back, so I wouldn't complain." "That eager to insult me some more, huh?" "It's not an insult, it's the truth." She scorned, "And I wouldn't be so 'eager' if you hadn't bitten my head off for saying one damned word." "I didn't bite your head off," Darcy said, seeing his attempts to stay out of another fight were fading fast. "I just said, if you have more words to mince with me, let them wait." "Why let them wait when I can get it all out now? It's like last year...you walked out, so don't expect posies and roses when you come back." "I'm not asking for that...I'm just asking to defer your battle so I can get some sleep." Amy kept trying, but her demeanor began to slip back into a happier state. A slight grin slipped when she thought of the date. "No rest for the wicked. Besides, it's Halloween...little kids'll be around shouting soon." "In an appartment complex?" "You never went trick-or-treating much as a kid, did you?" She tried to laugh, but ended up grumbling "Maybe that's why you've always reminded me of Frosty the Snowman's evil twin." "Excuse me? Sucka, I was the king of East Bay T or T!" Markson smirked. "That is, until I discovered the joys of vandalism." "Let me guess, 'sucka'...the Booker T costume's always been a favorite? And since when did YOU vandalize things? You're clean as a Windex commercial." Aimz cast him a mock sneer from her spot on the couch, snickering at her own little joke. As usual, she was the only one laughing at it...but that didn't make it any less amusing in her eyes. Darcy rolled his eyes. "I'm a thrill-seeker, you know that. Those were the days before I could get my thrills by hearing thirty thousand people chant my name." Somehow, somewhere, the mood had improved markedly. "You're such a wash-up." She giggled, hoping the little rib wouldn't offend Darcy, though his temper seemed to be a thin tight rope at that point. He sighed, closing his eyes. "And you have no idea how hard it is to wake up and remember that, everyday." She looked over the expression on Darcy's face and let out a shakey sigh herself. That's when a rare event occured; Amy stood up and walked over to him, hugging smaller arms around his thick waist while saying two words that VERY rarely, if ever, emerged from her lips. "I'm...I'm sorry." "It's not your fault Amy....you didn't throw me from that cage." Darcy growled, remembering the bastards that did so during a match in a long-forgotten federation. "No..." She raised her eyebrows, wrinkling her forehead in a pleading expression. Mainly pleading for something to make sure her words come out properly. "...I'm sorry for everything....just...yeah, everything. I know I'm pretty lousy to have to put up with." "No, it's okay Amy, really..." Darcy replied, leaning his head against hers. "I know I don't make life as easy on you as I possibly can." "Yeah, but at least you have a reason to dislike how I am." "I dislike fighting with you....I don't dislike you or you or any one thing about you." She looked up at him again, "But the fighting comes from my shitty personality...you shouldn't have to put up with that." Markson put his hand under Amy's chin to hold her attention. "You do NOT have a shitty personality....it just clashes with mine. And what makes you think I'm so perfect? We'd never fight if I was." "...I dunno..." The little redhead sighed and bit her lip a little. "You just...well, you don't do stupid stuff. It's my department to royally fuck up, I guess...that's all." "Well, that's just your nature as spontaneous. Sure, it bites you in the ass every once in awhile, but that's what keeps your life exciting and unpredictable. If I could live that way, I would." "Who says you can't?" Her eyes were bright, as were her spirits at that point. "I mean, you managed to get drunk like a year and a half ago and you did something pretty spontaneous. Well, we both did...and look how that turned out!" "I didn't say I was completely incapable...it's just against the way that I typically act. That's why I'm having such a tough time dealing with being on the sidelines....the rock in my life is gone." "What about me?" It wasn't in Amy's nature to be entirely serious, so she mixed the question in with signature goofiness, flexing rather puny muscles with a comical expression. "Grrr! Rock hard body!" With another roll of his eyes, Darcy wrapped Amy up in a bearhug. "Yeah, rock hard my ass...." "Pfft! I can be big an' mean when I want to!" She furrowed her brow..."Just look at this face! I make evil look good! Muaha...ha...ha..........."...and ended up coughing, following that up by pouting. "Okay, so I lost it a little." "Yeah, maybe a little....but seriously. I can't just up and decide to have you replace all that I had with wrestling. If I do, it'll fail. Maybe you ultimately will, maybe you won't. Time and fate will decide, not me." "But...why can't you let me become that important? You seem so sad when you don't have something motivating you." Her pouting was a little more on the serious side this time. "...and it really bugs me to see you so bummed out half the time." "Amy, we've been dating for all of a month officially, tops. It's not a question of me fighting it. If I put every girl on that pedastal after a month, I'd be an absolute wreck of a man." Her forehead wrinkled once again, her eyes in a pleading push-and-pull with Darcy's. "It's been three months this year, a month last and isn't there some kind of feeling, or don't you realize it yet? I mean, isn't the fact that our feelings were still strong enough to give it another go after you left over thirteen months ago?" "Hey now...you're a terrific person, and a devil in the sack..." Darcy said with a wink. "Seriously though, I have no idea what I'd do without you. Wrestling consumed my every being, ruled my life, gave me a dream and everything I ever wanted. You're very, very important to me, but it's just not the same. But that doesn't mean I don't care about you immensely, so don't get that into your head, alright?" "Alright." The pout soon turned into a coy expression, topped off by an evil little grin and a wink. "But who said I don't rule your life? I'm the fighter around here...it's your natural obligation to be my bitch." Darcy laughed....a loud, boisterous fake one. "Woman, I may not be able to run around in a ring for half an hour anymore, but that doesn't mean I can't still whip your ass." "Oh come ON..." She giggled. "When could you whip my ass? And even if you could, that'll all change after I've been in fWo a little while..." "Uhhh...come again?" Markson was entirely lost, as he should've been. Amy'd never mentioned fWo before in recent memory, especially not with reference to her being in it. "After you left, mom came and verbally bitch-slapped me, had me see what a priss I've been. Right after that, I got a call..." Her smile was almost ear-to-ear at that point, she was over-joyed to finally be able to say the words... "fWo wants me." "W-what? fWo, as in 'we always hoped fWo scouts were in the stands at pay per views' fWo?" "Mmhmm!" She beamed. "They said they've had their eyes on me and heard rumors of what Jane intended to do with me in OSW so they wanted to scoop me up before anyone else did! It's a new breed or something...they want me to be part of it. I even got an invite to the pay-per-view, which means a direct flight in about an hour and a half'll get me there on time." "That's....awesome, Amy. Congratulations." Aimz would've hugged him tighter, but she didn't get the feeling that he was terribly ecstatic. She looked up again and studied his face, seeing right through the well-faked smile. "What's wrong?" "Nothing, nothing..." Darcy answered, half-knowing she wouldn't buy it. "I'm happy for you, really." "You're happy for me..." She was still staring, studying him. "...but you're not happy." "Well, you know what this means, don't you...I'm out of a job again." Suddenly, Amy's expression brightened again. "Oh, really? That's awfully funny...'cause I seem to recall telling that rep that if fWo signs me, I've got a manager they've gotta take or the deal's a definite no-go." "Are you nuts? What if they don't sign you?!?" "What if they don't? I'm not leaving you behind, EVER. If they don't want a chick with a guy manager or some dumb excuse like that, I won't die working at a McDonald's to make rent." "Amy, I'm flattered, really....but fWo wanted you, they didn't call for me....I hope to God they sign you anyway, but I wouldn't want that on my conscience, that you turned it down for me." "If they're gonna have me, they're gonna have you. I wouldn't BE here without you." He was wide-eyed, still just standing there. "How do you figure?" "Last year when things first sparked between you and I, I was in a bad way...but you...well yeah..." Her nervous habits were showing through verbally. It wasn't like her to voice 'true' emotion. "...and you kinda...well...saved me from doing some really crazy stuff. This time around, I was coming off of that bullshit with Reno, Corg and them...again, you came in and saved me from doing some really stupid shit to myself. I wouldn't be alive right now if it weren't for you, and I wouldn't have been motivated to spend this last year wrestling as hard as my body would allow if it weren't for you pushing me." "Wow...." Darcy shook his head a bit, having difficulty taking all of that in. He searched for a response, and finally found one after almost a full minute of silence. "That's why I don't want you to have worked so hard for nothing, to turn down a real, bonafide chance. I don't want you to be in debt to me." "Whatever I do is of my own will. You're the reason I've gone this far and if you can't be as happy as I am, I'll make a decision accordingly." Amy was still choosing her words nervously, finding her 'feelings' to be an uncharacteristic topic. "So...yeah.....I guess you know why I'm always on your back about how you feel about me now." Darcy embraced her tightly. Never had he met someone so full of passion and drive, never had he felt such a connection with someone, not since...."No, I'm not going there..." he thought to himself. "Wrestling career aside....Amy Campbell, you're amazing." With a smile ten miles wide and eyes that were growing misty, Aimz stood silently with him, wallowing in the pure emotion between them before she pulled away when the urge to say something she'd wanted to for a long time rose to a nearly unbearable level...the very last thing she needed was to scare him off, so she quickly chimed in before her heart took over her head. "Well now that you've got me all soft and squishy inside...we've got a flight to catch!" "Excellent....twenty bucks says I get all packed up before you do!" "Bah! We'll be home by this time tomorrow, so I'm already packed. I fear NO germ!!! MUAAAAAAAAAAHA---damnit, it hurts to laugh like that." "Then don't laugh like that...." Darcy said, with a half-smile. "I'll laugh how I want! Go start the car, woman." Campbell giggled and poked him in the side again...and again...and again.........and again, until she finally darted out the door, shouting "Last one to the car wears a bra all night!" He would've protested, but that was just Amy being Amy...albeit, a version of her he hadn't seen in a very long time, but still just goofy little Aimz. He'd come so close to telling Amy his exact feelings a few minutes before, but he was still clinging to a distant memory of a girl...someone who hurt him, and Darcy was too afraid to risk heartbreak happening again at that point. They were still together, and actually cheery this time. It was Halloween night, two full days away from OSW's weekly show, Slam and the REAL 'trouble' had yet to come to light...but even when it did, Amy was too happy with the track her life was then on to care.
