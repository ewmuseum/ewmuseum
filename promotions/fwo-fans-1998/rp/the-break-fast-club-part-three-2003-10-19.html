The Break Fast Club (part three)

19 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's later in the day, and Sphere is in the library, looking at everyone while they're sleeping. He's looking at Evil-Lyn the most. He ponders on whether or not he should go right over to her, but he's interrupted by Nate getting up from his position on the floor. Sphere: Um...okay! Wake up! Who has to take a dump? Or a leak, at least? Everyone raises their hands. It is now even later in the day, and they're back in the library. The time is 11:22. Steve-O's head is soaking wet, a result from using the urinal next to Funpants when he could have used a stall. As one can tell, Funpants *forced* him to use the stall. BQ is tearing pages out of a book and throwing them around. Steve-O: That's, like, intelligent and stuff. BQ: You bet. I know it's wrong to destroy literature, but....eh. BQ tears some more pages out and then tosses everything, book and pages, at Funpants. Funpants drops his Playboy and picks up the book. Funpants: You tore up Angela's Ashes? BQ: Why not? Nate: Dude! Angela? I know her! If you were reading about her ashes...does that mean she's dead? Or were her ashes, like, her hash? Cause if that's a book about her hash, then I'd be totally into it, cause, you know, I like that kind of stuff. Now, I know...I know...it's kind of a shock...makes you wanna FLIP OUT...but...yeah, man. Steve-O: Dude, snap out of it! Nate: Huh? What? No, man, my mom said I was, but then my dad told me to just blow her off. Steve-O: Whoa. Should be pretty wild. Nate: Yeah? Steve-O: Yeah? Nate: Dude, what are we talkin' about? Steve-O: You were the one who said something about a rave. Nate: I don't know, man...cause if I do what my mother tells me not to do, it's because because my father says it's okay. There's like this whole big monster deal, it's endless and it's a total drag. It's like any minute... divorce... BQ: Who do you like better? Nate: What? Who's talking? BQ: You like your old man better than your mom? Nate: They're both my parents. Steve-O: Man, I'm glad I don't got YOUR parents. BQ: Aren't you his brother? Steve-O: Totally. Evil-Lyn: Ha! Everyone turns around and looks at Evil-Lyn, who just blows her hair out of her eyes and grins at them. BQ: Steve-O! BQ gets up and attempts to slide across his table, but gets stuck, so he scoots off it and squats down next to Steve-O. BQ: You get along with your parents? Steve-O: Do I get a prize if I say "yes"? BQ: Sure. Steve-O: Alright...no. Steve-O thinks about what he just said. Steve-O: Wait...I mean...no. Steve-O thinks again. Steve-O: Um...yeah? BQ: You win a prize! Steve-O: Awesome! What do I win? BQ: Your sneaker! BQ holds up the sneaker he stole from Steve-O earlier. Steve-O: Righteous! BQ hands the sneaker to Steve-O. Steve-O: You know something, man...if we weren't in school right now, I'd be wasted. BQ: You *are* wasted. You're *always* wasted. Steve-O: Dude, come again? BQ points his middle finger at the floor. BQ: Can you hear this? Want me to turn it up? BQ reaches down and picks up a remote control, then holds down the volume button. The word VOLUME appears below him, and the number of lines below the word increases. BQ: Can you hear me now? Steve-O nods. BQ: Good. Funpants walks over and puts a hand on BQ's shoulder. Funpants: Yeah, just like I thought. Scrawny. Steve-O spits on Funpants' shoe, and for the second time today, Funpants punches Steve-O in the mouth. BQ: How 'bout you, Funpants? Do YOU get along with YOUR parents? Funpants: My name is SENOR FUNPANTS. What do YOU think? BQ: I think "Senor" is a weird name for a white guy. Steve-O: Dude...help me out...why does everyone always make fun of this Senor guy here? BQ: Because he wears hot pants. Funpants: I wear FUN pants! I told you! There's a difference! And why are you still talking? I thought I punched you in the mouth. Steve-O: Dude, I didn't say it stopped, like, hurting and all. Funpants: Did it stop yet? Steve-O: I think so. Funpants punches Steve-O in the mouth again, and BQ turns to Funpants. BQ: My condolences. Funpants looks at BQ in confusion as he walks away. Nate: Yo...guy with the black face...what's your name? BQ: What's yours? Nate: I forget. BQ: Here's a free one. It's Nate. I actually pay attention to the other workers during their segments and matches. Nate: Mucho gracias, Senorita. BQ: You're welcome. Nate: I'm not fat! Funpants: Quickjobber....I DARE you to go on this whole spiel about how he's gonna get fatter, and then bust on him for being a virgin. BQ: For how much? Funpants: Nothing. BQ: How much if I direct that spiel towards YOU? Funpants: An ass-kicking. BQ raises his chin and pulls at the collar of his t-shirt with his index finger. BQ: Ay-eee. BQ decides to just simply take out a switchblade and stab a chair. Of course, it's steel, so the blade doesn't penetrate it. Steve-O: DUDE! What did that chair ever do to you? Nate: Bro, let him. I hate chairs, man. BQ: See? I'm trying to *help* him. Suddenly, Evil Smokey The Bear comes into the room...carrying a mop. Evil Smokey: Ah, Funpants. How are you on this fine day? BQ: Whoa, Evil Smokey's your dad? Funpants: No. Shut up. He's not even the same species as me. BQ: I have my suspicions. Hey, Evil Smokey... Evil Smokey: What up? BQ: How does one become a janitor? Evil Smokey: You wanna be a janitor...young boy? BQ: No, I just wanna know how one becomes a janitor, because Steve-O here, if that IS his REAL name, is very interested in pursuing a career in the custodial arts. Evil Smokey: Oh, really? You kids think I'm just some giant bear with a mop? Maybe so, but following a broom around after shitheads like you for the past three hours, I've learned a couple of things. I look through your letters, I look through your lockers, I listen to your conversations...you don't know that, but I do. I am the eyes and ears of this institution, and I will SWALLOW YOUR SOUL. By the way, that clock's twenty minutes fast. Cut to Sphere's office, where the time is 12:30. Sphere gets up and goes to leave, but then comes back, grabs an 8x10 glossy photo of Nitro Girl Siren, and quickly runs into his private restroom. Cut back to the library, where BQ is whistling Eagle Eye Cherry's "Save Tonight". He gets through the whole song, so he looks around, shrugs, then begins to whistle Green Day's "Good Riddance". Finally, Sphere walks into the library. Sphere: Alright, girls, that's thirty minutes for lunch. Steve-O: Here? Sphere: Here. Steve-O: Where? Sphere: Here. Steve-O: But, dude...I think the cafeteria is like, more built for eating. Sphere: Well I don't care what you think, Samantha. BQ: Uh, Dick? Excuse me, I meant to call you "Dick". Will milk be made available to us? Steve-O: Yeah, man, we're thirsty. Like, thirsty to the EXTREME MAX~! Nate: I have a very low tolerance for reintegration. Steve-O: I've seen him reintegrate. It's SO gross. BQ: Relax, I'll get it. BQ stands up. Sphere: Ah, ah, ah! Grab some wood there, bub. BQ shrugs and turns around, looking right at Evil-Lyn. Sphere: May I ask what you're doing, Quicksilver? BQ: I'm preparing to grab some wood, sir. Sphere: Very good, Quicksilver. Hmm...you. Sphere points at Steve-O, and Steve-O gets up. Sphere: You come with me. And you! Sphere points to Evil-Lyn. Sphere: You come, too. Evil-Lyn is slow to get up. Sphere: Come on, on your feet, missy! Let's go! This is no rest home! And if it was, *I* would be resting *with* you. Evil-Lyn gets up and walks over to Sphere and Steve-O. BQ is still looking in the direction of Evil-Lyn's seat, which is currently vacant. Sphere: There's a soda machine in the teacher's lounge. We can push it over and steal some if we try hard enough. Let's go! Sphere, Evil-Lyn, and Steve-O leave the library. BQ: Wait a minute. Where'd she go? Nate doesn't answer because he's now watching the rat play a drum solo, and Funpants doesn't answer because...well, *he's* Funpants, and the person he'd have to acknowledge would be *BQ*. Meanwhile, Evil-Lyn and Steve-O are walking in the hall. Steve-O: So what's your poison? Evil-Lyn doesn't answer. Steve-O: What do you drink? Evil-Lyn still doesn't answer. Steve-O: Okay...forget I asked, cause, like, I probably will, too. Evil-Lyn: Liquids. Steve-O: Liquids? When do you drink liquids? Evil-Lyn: Whenever. Steve-O: Alot? Evil-Lyn: Tons. Steve-O: Is that why you're here today? Evil-Lyn: You know why I'm here. Steve-O: Uh....why are you here? Evil-Lyn: Why are YOU here? Steve-O: Well you see, I found this horse out in my yard, so I started squirting it with a water gun, but then I found out that it wasn't a water gun, but it was a Gatorade bottle! So, like, then the horse got all this energy, and it's sweat was blue, and it jumped over the fence and it was like, a HOUSE EN FUEGO~! It destroyed about four cop cars, which was pretty cool, since I don't like alot of the cops that are always around my house. But then they busted out their tazers and they zapped the horse and they cuffed me, and I was all "ATTICA! ATTICA!", and bam! Here I am. Evil-Lyn: Yeah? That's very interesting. Now why don't you tell me why you're really in here...even though you told me yesterday. Steve-O: I did? Evil-Lyn: You did. Did ANYONE watch our segment on Uprising? Cut back to the library, where BQ is still staring at Evil-Lyn's seat. After a while, Funpants points toward Nate. BQ: What? You want me to stare at Nate? Nate: Huh? Senor Dude! You packin' the fudge? Funpants: Is that one of your pothead terms? BQ: Get with the times, Funpants. He thinks you're a homosexual. Funpants: How many times do I have to tell you guys that I wear FUN pants! Fun pants are considered to be very macho from where I come from. BQ: You're from Harrisburg. Funpants: Excuse me for being from Harrisburg. Nate breaks into his trademark giggle. Nate: Dude, I think it SUCKS for a guy to be from Harrisburg. It's now later on in the day, and everyone has their lunches. BQ tosses a can of Diet Coke to Evil-Lyn, who somehow manages to catch it while filing her nails. He then watches Nate set up a sushi platter. BQ: What's that? Nate: Sushi. BQ: Sushi? Nate: Yeah, like, rice, raw fish and seaweed. Seaweed must DEFINITELY be raw. I tried eating it fresh once, you know, right out of the ocean? Turns out it belonged to a jellyfish. We got in a fight over it and everything. I think he won, cause next thing I know, this dude was pissin' on me. Must've been a stipulation I didn't hear about. Steve-O reaches into his bag and pulls out six sandwiches, three bags of potato chips, an apple, a banana, two bags of cookies, and a one-liter of Jolt Cola. He looks at the apple and banana in confusion, before tossing them behind him. Evil-Lyn opens her Diet Coke, and....it doesn't fizz over. She opens up her sandwich, removes the meat, and tosses it on a nearby sculpture. She breaks open some Pixie Stix and pours the sugar on the sandwich, then adds some pieces of Cap'n Crunch cereal to it. She puts the top piece of bread back on, then throws it at Funpants, hitting him right in the back of the head. Evil-Lyn: I've SO been wanting to do that for the longest time. Funpants simply ignores the sandwich that hit him in the back of the head, as BQ comes over and sits next to him, taking his bag of lunch. BQ: What are we having? Funpants: It's your standard, regular lunch. BQ pulls a Thermos out of the bag. BQ: Milk? Funpants: Vodka. BQ goes in again and pulls out a can of something. Funpants: That's a beer. BQ: I can read. Funpants: Then why'd you say "a can of something"? BQ: That wasn't me. BQ and Funpants both turn and look straight ahead. They remain like that for a little while, but then shrug and turn back toward each other. BQ: And a ham and cheese sandwich. With *yellow* cheese, nonetheless. Well, Funpants...this is a very nutritious lunch. Did your mom marry Mr. Rogers? Funpants: No. Mr. Funpants. BQ: Wow, you have the same last name as your dad? Funpants: You're a mongoloid. BQ stands up. BQ: Here's my impression of life at the Funpants house several years ago. BQ (loud voice): Son! BQ (kiddie voice): Yeah, dad? BQ (loud): What the hell did you do to your little sister? BQ (kiddie): I gave her an indian burn because she wouldn't let me wear her hot pants. BQ (loud): How'd you like for me to whip you with my belt? BQ (kiddie): Screw you, pops, I have to study for my physics club! BQ (loud): You wanna study physics? How bout you try to calculate the velocity of my belt hitting your BARE ASS? BQ (kiddie): Really? BQ (loud): Whoo! Look at it swell! Steve-O: Dude, what about your family? Like, did your dad used to hit Funpants, too? Funpants: My dad never hit me. Steve-O: Dude, I'm sorry, but with hair like that, it's hard to, like, configure that your dad didn't slap you for not gettin' it cut. Funpants: Yeah, look who's talking. And what about Quicksilver? What's with the whole "hair hanging out of the mask" thing? You think that's cool? Huh? You think it's...different? BQ: You wanna come over sometime? Funpants: What? BQ: I can tell you like this style. I can have you fitted for a mask similiar to mine, and you can look like this in about a week. Funpants is dumbfounded. BQ: You don't believe me? Steve-O: I believe you. BQ: Cool. Steve-O: Did I stutter? BQ: I don't think so. Steve-O: I think I did. BQ: I don't think you did. Steve-O: Well I do. BQ goes over to Steve-O and rolls up his right sleeve, revealing a circular-shaped burn. BQ: Do you believe this? Huh? It's about the size of a cigar. You see, this is what you get in my house when you spill paint in the garage. You slip on the paint and you fall, and you land on a candle that you lit because the light was broken! For no real reason, BQ turns around, runs toward a table, jumps on it, then climbs up the second floor balcony. Nate: Dude, you shouldn't have said that. Steve-O: Said what? Cut to Sphere's office, where he's hanging upside down from a lighting fixture, reading a Maxim and holding an open can of Red Bull. Naturally, he ends up spilling the Red Bull all over himself when he goes to take a sip, and then falls off the lighting fixture.
