Run Down

3 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was well after two in the morning before Kodiak Vic Creed got back to the hotel. Flanked by his assistant and his chiropractor, Creed walked from the parking lot, through the lobby, and into the elevator in silence. When a small group of fans tried to stop him for an autograph, Creed almost knocked them over. He didn't even slow down as he walked through the mob with long, powerful strides. With his eyes hidden behind his sunglasses, it was hard to tell if Vic even saw them at all. Once KVC and his entourage stepped into the elevator and the doors closed behind them, Vic leaned against the wall. "Son of a bitch," he muttered as he lifted his leg slightly. Mike looked down at Creed's raised leg. "The knee again?" "Again? Try still." Vic tried to bend it and flinched. His arm shot out and struck the elevator wall out of frustration, narrowly missing Mike's head as his assistant ducked out of the way. "What the Hell was Fox thinking, booking me in two matches like that?" "I don't think Silver Fox is too concerned about your well-being Champ." Mike looked over at his girlfriend. Anita tried to take a step away from Creed, but the big Alaskan put a heavy hand on her shoulder to steady himself. "Maybe you should ice your knee back at the room. You don't have to work out tomorrow." Vic clenched his jaw as he bent his knee. "It's all Frontier's fault. Damn leg feels like it's on fire every time I put weight on it. If I was capable of regret, it'd be gone now." Anita shifted uneasily under Creed's touch. She continued to stare forward at the elevator doors, glancing up occasionally at the digital display counting off floor after floor. Vic gave her shoulder a squeeze that Mike didn't see. "I won't need an adjustment tonight Annie." Mike noticed her cringe and thought it was because she hated the name "Annie". Creed got out on his floor and Anita stepped closer to Mike. "Rest that leg Boss," Mike called out as he slipped his hand around Anita's waist. Vic just kept walking without turning around. He slid his door card across the lock and swung the door open. After taking a few steps into his room, he let his bag drop out of his hand and he collapsed on his bed face-first. He lifted his head slightly, pulled off his sunglasses, and set them on the table beside his bed. With his face buried in his pillow, Vic let the liquid fire burning in his knee spread throughout his leg. Even his toes hurt when he tried to move them. He didn't even want to guess at how bad the damage was, but it didn't really matter in the long run. It's not like he was going to take time off to heal it. Vic didn't move when he heard the knock at his door. He grabbed the half-empty bottle of Crown Royal sitting on his bedside table and tossed it towards the door. The knocking stopped for a few seconds before starting up again. "Vic?" A woman's voice. "It's Melonie. I missed you after the show." "Not tonight Mel." "What's that?" Creed rolled off the bed in a huff and quickly hobbled over to the door. He yanked it open so quickly that the brunette's shoulder-length hair was pulled across her face by the vacuum. "I said not tonight. Now piss off." Mel stood in the doorway with a look on her face like she'd just been slapped. It didn't improve things when Vic slammed the door in her face. He stopped in the bathroom and yanked his shaving kit off the counter. "Gold-digging whores," Vic mumbled as he took his pants off and sat down on the edge of the bed. Setting the shaving kit down beside him, Creed reached in and pulled out a small glass vial. He held the milky substance up to the light and shook the small bottle. It was getting harder for Vic to get his hands on this stuff, what with all his contacts getting arrested and such. It's not like he was shooting heroine, the mix of anabolics and painkillers wasn't going to alter his reality at all. It was a necessity. He was a high-performance athlete who had to train, and didn't have time to "take it easy" when he felt sore. Vic pushed the syringe through the plastic seal and drew in some of the liquid. A hit of this and Vic would be ready to hit the gym in a few hours. He stood up and squeezed the plunger, pushing the air out with a trained hand. Satisfied, he poked the metal tip into his glute. Blood backwashed into the needle before Vic squeezed the liquid into his body. Creed slipped his underwear and pants back up. An almost cooling sensation spread down his leg while slowly warming the rest of his body. The pain was subsiding behind a tide of adrenaline. Vic tossed the needle into the trash and took his shaving kit back into the bathroom. He sucked in a deep breath and within minutes felt two hundred percent better. Vic walked over to the phone. "I hope I can still catch Mel before she gets too far away," he said as he picked up the receiver.
