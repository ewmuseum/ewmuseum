Mirror'd 2.2

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ric valued Ben Tomes opinion highly, so he had stopped by Custer High in response to Ben's voicemail without any reservations. Ben had helped guide Ric through a few of the early years, and the amateur wrestling standout turned coach was able to give Ric the pointers he needed to work out a few things that had been bugging him. The mutual respect from those younger days didn't make the conversation go any easier, though. Ben had chided Ric for a lack of focus - for concentrating so much on the objective past him that he ignored exactly what was going on around him. "Too many irons in the fire," the old man had said. Reluctantly, Ric did have to hand him one point - Ric has underestimated Frontier, even if only by a little bit. Other than that, Ric didn't really agree with what Ben had said. At least Ric knew that his grip on the reality of his profession was strong, and not as delusional as Ben seemed to imply. Regardless, Ric had agreed to stick around and assist in a little training of Custer's own wrestling team, but so far, it seemed like he had done little more than waste his time. Ric stared down at the 15 year old student, disapproval obvious in the way Ric glared. They'd been working on the arm drag - the arm drag, of all things - for the better part of an hour, and the student still didn't seem to get it. This was certainly not how he had originally planned on spending the hours leading up to Unleashed, and the snail's pace progress threatened to make him late - something Chronos abhorred. Making an excuse, Ric separated from the group, and headed for the now vacant locker rooms in order to clear his head and get some peace and quiet. Ric rested his head back, closing his eyes, wondering for the fifth time if his friendship to Ben was worth this hassle. He spent a few seconds calming himself - with all the new faces in the fWo's locker room, he hadn't had much opportunity to be alone, and perhaps he was letting that get to him. All these young, eager stars had come in to the fed recently, each one hoping to blaze a trail into superstardom - a trail that would lead most of them directly to the roadblock known as Ric Chronos. He wasn't about to let some up and coming baby in tights take his place, or supplant him in any way - particularly that Frontier whelp. A small shuffling sound pierced Ric's thoughts, and he snapped to attention. His reflexes were honed to a razor's edge, as he didn't want to fall prey to some potential trap. He'd been in the business long enough to know that you couldn't trust anyone not to be ready to jump you just for having the gall to beat their favorite wrestler a week ago on TV. But instead of some dire threat, Ric was faced with the same student he'd been working with for the past hour. Untensing, Ric prepared to dismiss the teen when the seemingly docile kid found the nerve to stammer out a few words. "Uh, Mr. uh, Chronos," he began. "Not Mr. Chronos. That's my father, and he's not going to be answering you anytime soon," Ric retorted. "Sorry." the kid replied in a small voice. He nervously shifted his weight from foot to foot, eyes downcast, as an uncomfortable silence descended. Ric sighed. "Yeah. Right. So are you going to stand there all day, or are you going to ask me whatever you wanted to ask me?" Ric would much rather have sent the kid packing than to engage him in conversation, but his debt of friendship to Ben wouldn't allow him that courtesy. "Well, uh, see, it's rare that someone can, uh, make it professionally when they're an amateur. But, you know Coach Tomes, and he said he had coached you a bit, so I was just wondering, uh, how I would get to be like you." Ric's eyes turned to steel. He knew he needed to break the hard truth to this kid, but he wasn't sure of the best way to do it. "What's your name?" Ric asked, ice dripping from his tone. "Danny, er, Dan. Dan Booth." Dan had seemed to only vaguely comprehend that something had made Ric a little edgier, and it struck Ric that the teen resembled a deer in the headlights of an approaching semi truck. "So, Dan, you want to be like me. I guess that means you'd like to wake up with a red-hot knife jabbing you in the back of the neck every day. Or maybe you want your knees to scream out each time you take a step, for your back to more closely resemble cole slaw than human anatomy, for your every waking moment to be a small slice of torment, all because you dared to breathe?" "Well, uh, no... I meant.." "I know what you meant. You want the fame, the money, the glory, that comes with being a star. Let me tell you - fame gets old when someone tries to take a picture of you every time you so much as think of going outside, the money is gone after the first major injury, and the glory... let's just say that the glory of being a champion is always overshadowed by the failure of defeat, or being forgotten. Trust me, kid, whatever it is you want - you sure as hell don't want to be like me." Dan stood there, mouth gaping wide, tears welling in his eyes. Ric started to turn, to put this entire mess behind him, when he finally noticed that he and Dan weren't alone. Fully half of the wrestling team stood a mere fifteen feet away, staring at the man who'd just humiliated one of their own. Ric cursed himself inwardly - he had meant to teach this Dan kid a lesson, to make Dan realize exactly what it was that he was wishing for. Instead, he had quite publicly embarrassed and humiliated the youngster in front of his peers. Dan hesitantly began to step away, but Ric placed his hand on the youth's shoulder, restraining him. "Ah, hell." Ric muttered. "Listen, Dan, I didn't mean it to be that harsh. I'm just a little jet lagged is all, and I have a lot to deal with right now." "Like Brand Frontier? Didn't he kick your ass a week or two ago?" Ric's eyes narrowed. He couldn't place exactly which one of the kids had made that remark, but he was certain it was made with the safety of anonymity, and as a direct response to how Ric had treated Dan. Fine, enough with the nice guy routine, Ric was going to... ...and just then, a thought crept into Ric's head. A good thought. He only received four comp tickets per show, as was the fWo's policy, but he could easily fork out the money to put the rest of these kids in seats tonight. What better way to get back at them, to prove his point, than to let them see it firsthand? "Perhaps he did... perhaps Frontier got a lucky count. But do you think he can do it again, against Black Quicksilver tonight? How about you all find out - as Dan's special guests at Unleashed." Dan's face lit up, as the excitement began to build in his teammates. Ric smiled himself, not at the thought of a good deed accomplished, but at the prospect of showing all of these young punks a thing or two, and all at the same time. Let them cheer for Brand - it would be just that much sweeter when he saw their faces deflate at what Ric was planning to do to Brand tonight. Maybe this was going to be worth it after all.
