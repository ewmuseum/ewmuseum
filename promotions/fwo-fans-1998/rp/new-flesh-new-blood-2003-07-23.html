New Flesh, New Blood

23 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aimz' 21st Birthday - July 17th, 2003 Amy Campbell's muffled screams couldn't be heard through the duct tape that was pasted across her face. The asphalt was slightly damp and very cold as her bare legs and stomach were dragged through the parking lot. Her hands were bound and were clenched by two unknown figures, cigarettes hung from both of their mouths as one pulled and the other kept a look out. The shorter of the two men lifted Amy up and threw her on top of a parked car. Blood had begun to trickle from beneath the cracked skin and run down both of her bare legs. Her eyes were wide as fear as the man smiled from behind the ski mask. "You're so beautiful when you're frightened," he whispered. He stared at her body, up and down taking in every inch. "So beautiful," he whispered again under his breath. For the surprisingly chilly July night, Amy had little on to "protect" her from anything that could happen. Her kidnappers were fully clothed in black, head to toe, hiding their true identities. Amy was trembling and her eyes were so wide that they were nearly white as she stared at her main attacker in disbelief as he continued to eye her like penny candy. The color in her skin was all but drained, as white as the pure driven snow. "The first angel," the attacker whispered into her ear, brushing back her red hair. "Don't think of this as pain... this is your heaven, my dear sweet angel. Pain is fleeting... but this, this will last forever." She tried to wriggle her hands free from the taught rope that were bound at her wrists. There was no give in her efforts. The man backed away momentarily and she got a combat boot clad foot to the side of her attacker's face. He snarled as the leather boot made contact with the ski mask. A hand snapped from behind his back and produced a small carving knife from a pocket. As he held it in the light, Amy's eyes became wider and wider as the knife glimmered in the light of a distant street lamp. "There's no need to try and escape," he smiled as his tongue ran across his lower lip. "I told you that I'm not going to hurt you. Your white skin, your wine red blood, all of this will mesh together and harden. Pain is fleeting. The scar will merely be a reminder to something you will never be able to let go. Everywhere you look I will be everything you see. I will always be on your mind. I'm going to be the parasite slowly eating away at your brain until it kills you." He bent down and kissed her forehead. "You will always remember me, Amy," he assured. "I'd let you add something to this, but I can't ruin the most perfect moment between us." The tip of his knife came down and pressed lightly at the grey duct tape covering Amy's mouth. She winced, terrified of blades since childhood, but never moved another muscle until the 'leader' backed up from her a bit, chuckling over the sudden reaction to the blade only to be nailed in the stomach by the comparatively little redhead's bound feet. He leaned over...he stood tall again, this time grabbing Amy around the throat... "A little more of your pain is still worth our cause." He growled. His sudden grip on her neck, among other things, sent the message straight to Amy...he wasn't your average person - He was strong enough to take a hard blow from her and bounce right back to hold her in place. "Don't be so confident that we won't be brute." "Don't worry...much." The second man added with a laugh, keeping his tone low enough so - if she'd have known it - Amy wouldn't have recognized the voice. Now she sensed him move as he sat at her feet, holding her legs down while her eyes remained locked on the knife that his comrade was lowering to her hip, exposed by tugging the shorts she'd worn slightly. He was going too slowly to stab her, but too quickly to just be threatening contact... And, indeed, he wasn't. The tape was torn fast from the hardcore champion's mouth, just in time to hear her cry out when the 'artist' began his work.
