Morning View of Clarity

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
8:10 a.m. April 3rd, 2003 Hotel The morning light as it was filtered through the hotel drapery shone in on the man the public called Brand Frontier. He had always been a stickler for his sleep regiment, one of the things he had attributed his fast recovery rate to. This time however a sliver of light made it's way in the room and as if from an old Tex Avery cartoon no matter how he turned, it reflected off of something...and right in to his eyes. After long moments and a couple of times padding over to the curtain in a comical display to make the room darker, Brandon finally gave up. He slumped back to the headboard and peered over to Amy Campbell. As flighty as she seemed normally you couldn't tell it now, she was dead to the world. Every once in awhile she would simply crash, her nervous energy getting the best of her as her body told her it was simply time to recharge. This was one of those times. A few minutes passed as Brandon rustled back and forth on the bed. Not exactly trying to wake Amy up he didn't move softly either. If she just happened to awaken he wouldn't mind either. Finally he had enough of the quiet though and 'accidentally' elbowed Amy in the shoulder, clearing his throat at the same time. "Ahem..." The first 'slip' was ignored as Amy barely even stirred, flinching her shoulder a little. A second nudge got a similar response, until... COUGH! Brandon had jolted up in the faked gesture, again accidentally nudging Amy, this time with a foot to her shin that had her flinch even harder. It didn't seem as if the Paragon was going to get any response at first but then a sleepy groan escaped as she exhaled, followed by a brown eye opening a notch, soon followed by its icy blue counterpart. "Hmm...whaa?" "Heeeey... you're awake too. I couldn't sleep for the life of me." "Well yeah, I sometimes wake up when I get kicked." Amy closed her eyes for another second, then cocked her head slightly, "Why can't you sleep?" "It's morning silly. The start of a brand new day... of course I can't sleep, by all accounts I should have been up and at'em two hours ago... I mean heck, it's already 8:15!" A sly grin slid across Brandon's face as he knew there would be hell to pay now. Grabbing Brandon's head and pushing it back down to the pillow, the much smaller of the two redheads attempted to assert herself. "Damnit, I only got to sleep like four hours ago. We've got nowhere to be, now nap or something...I need rest!" "You need breakfast! Let's hop in the shower and we can get going. We might even be able to get the early bird special!" Flipping Amy over on her back Brandon leaned down and planted a kiss firmly on her lips while they were in mid-protest. Grabbing the covers as he did he gave a mighty yank, sending them to the floor in a heap. Within a millisecond of the blankets leaving her, Amy'd already curled up into the fetal position, putting a pillow over her head but making sure to give Brandon a crack with it first. "I'm staying right here. You're being cruel." were her muffled cries. Seizing the moment and also an ankle Brandon planted a few knuckles at the base of her foot and rubbed upward, steeling himself from the coming onslaught. "C'mon girly girl, don't be a sissy... get up and let's have fun! I'm catching the plane this afternoon so we only have so much time..." "I don't wanna!" The pout was apparent, and she folded her arms when the pillow was pulled from her head. Soon enough Brandon's grin had half-convinced her, though..."Do I have to? Why dontcha' just go see Sean and Jarod later? Maybe even with m---well, they hate me...but still! I shouldn't have to get out of bed at eight in the morning...NOBODY should." And with that, her eyes shut tightly again as Amy rolled over and out of immediate tickling range. "... They don't hate you. You're just something new, rocking the boat as it were. Jarod freaked out when Power Rangers changed what time they came on. They're kids, creatures of habit. As you are proving yourself right now... you always get on to me about loosening up... practice what you preach plucky!" "Hey! How about this..." Pulling her legs down a bit Brandon straddled them as she attempted to shield her eyes from the light. "... we lay here for a bit and you give me a tour of that tattoos. There's enough of them that when your done we can get a bite of lunch..." "And I don't have to stand up, promise?" "Well... knowing us there'll be moving eventually... but no standing... this time..." Amy smirked, "Sounds like a deal. You find the tattoo, I'll explain it." "Well how about my favorite dragon right here? It's wrapped around your ankle... don't you know that you seal your soul to your body if one of these encircles an extremity and closes on the other side?" Brandon trailed his fingers around the edges of the tattoo in the hopes of making her squirm. And squirm she did. Insanely ticklish, Campbell tried to pull her leg away at first much to her Mighty Hero's delight. "Yeah...it...yeah. That was a couple of years ago when I found out I actually had a soul. Figured I'd keep it. I'm not sure how well it worked...but oh well." "Funny girl. What about the writing here then? I can make out a few of the letters but that's about it..." "Stephen's birthday." She grinned, but her eyes looked distant for a moment. "Same day his real mom died. And I know you're just trying to tickle me so stop." With a slight kick, Amy giggled. "That one's nice. Seems we're both sentimental fools... and here I thought you'd end up giving me a hard time about this one." Brandon's fingers trailed over the slighty raised and red area on his chest where his father's name now resided in everlasting ink. "Hmmmm... what... about... thisone!" The words shot out accented as they were by his lightning quick reflexes. He had caught her off guard enough to grab at her collarbone area and being to tickle like mad. Unable to help an outburt of laughter, Amy ended the tickling spree the only way she could - A hard bite down on Brandon's own neck. "No tickling, damnit!" When it looked like she had his attention, she continued. "Trinity, the triad...that's life, love and death. I had life, found love, now I can die happy." A wink followed, "I should get a smiley face inked into the center of it." and soon after, she'd clasped one of Brandon's wrists just *barely* in both of hers hands. "And no tickling this time!" "Well if you're going to be all serious on me now... what about the one's on your wrists? The stars as well? After these... I get to flip you over don't I... we're done on this side?" His eyebrow raised on its own. "The stars remind me of what my goal entering this 'business' was and the flames...kinda cheesy, Renee has them too - Sorta shows we've been burned by hellfire but still relent." Amy mumbled, then suddenly perked with a chuckle and mischievious glint in her eyes. "And you're not quite so lucky yet...I figured you'd've paid enough attention to notice I've got Japanese on my hip." "Oh yeah... here I was getting ahead of myself. Can't blame a guy for losing track when he's got this to look at..." His own cheesy smile kept it from being too sexist. "I should knock your teeth out for that..." Instead, Amy brought a knee up to quickly slam into Brandon's 'exteme, extreme lower abdomen'. "Ha! And that means 'Sister'...both of my sisters and I have it, and it just signifies that I actually DO get along with my siblings - even my brothers...just not my parents." "That just means you're the typical dysfunctional family is all." Laying to her side Brandon strokes back her hair. "Now what about this little ankh?" With the hint firmly in place Amy turned away from Brandon, exposing her back to him. He pulled away the red strands covering said ankh and outlined it with his fingers, eliciting a slight shiver from the little redhead. "That was my first. It's the same general idea as the triad...life, love, death and everything pure. But, the Egyptians used to believe that it was the key to eternal life after death...so here's hoping I've got good chances." She grinned a little, taking a second to close her eyes - not fully awake even yet. Letting his fingers trail down her back Brandon knew instinctively that she was trying to fall back to sleep. "...and what about THIS!" Leaving exactly where he grabbed to the imagination is probably best, but it did manage to snap Amy out of her penchant for a catnap. "Jesus! Way to make me have a fucking heart attack!" Amy instantly swatted back at him without much aim. After a second and a final connection with his ribs, she answered while gingerly trying to roll back to face Brandon, mainly so she could smack him with accuracy. "That's a 'modern' version of a symbol for an old Egyptian sun God, Ra. A lot of people believe that Ra and Yahweh - the Jewish and Christian 'One God' - were the same...it represents my belief I guess, and looks a lot cooler than 'GOD' on my back or something. Plus it's like the Godsmack symbol, and that's a damned fine band...so I just tell people I'm a superfan when I don't wanna explain the whole belief system thingy." "Heh... yeah I can see where that would lead to some interesting conversations given your penchent for choosing friends."A slight wink and a curled smile indicated the heavy teasing Brandon intended. "Seems to me you have a whole pantheon of beliefs covering that body of yours. I especially like the new one..." Amy finally managed to roll over, rubbing the slight sleep from her eyes. "Oh yeah? Something told me you would. Not quite as crazy or deep as the others, but what can ya do..." She smiled and glanced to the digital clock on the nightstand beside them. "Hey...it's not nearly lunch yet, you lied!" "Hmmm... yeah it looks like I did. But now you're awake and not nearly as hostile as before... seems my plan worked." "That'd be an incredibly clever plan you had........if I hadn't already seen it coming and planned tooooooo...dothis!" In a flash Amy was off of the bed to retrieve the cast aside linen, wrapping and curling herself up in a ball on the floor. After a second one of her eyes opened to a slit, checking to see if Brandon might've actually given up in the momentary silence. Instead she found that he had padded off to the small sink and had indeed returned with a full ice container of water. Her heart sunk as he cocked his head to the side and a positively Austin Davis-ish grin spread across his face. "Fine!" She furrowed her brow and stood up, half-pouting and half-pretending to be incredibly pissed off. An empty threat quickly followed, "But next time I'm getting my own room, and someone's getting locked out of it so I can get actual sleep." "You wouldn't be able to sleep... you'd miss me too much." Flicking wet fingers at the back of Amy's head Brandon knew he was playing with fire and water at the same time. "I only miss you if I don't know you'll come crawling back." Amy smiled with the usual gleam to her eyes, kicking Brandon's ankle softly when a thought came. "Hey you...what did you think of me when you offered me that ride back to my hotel when I was walking in the rain way back in October?" "Honestly..." Brandon hesitated. "You looked like a drowned rat. I figured I'd do my good deed for the day and give you a lift." "Hey - I wouldn't dig on looks, Mister Crew Cut...that was really hot stuff, I'll bet you got lots of chicks." She winked, trying to trip him to the floor. "Did you ever even consider we'd be where we are now?" "Ha! I figured if anything I'd have an harrassment suit waiting for me the next day. You know how the boys talk." Avoiding the foot at the back of his ankle easily Brandon pulled up close to his would be attacker. "Hell, we were jumping in each others beds before we even kissed on New Years if you ask them." The tinier redhead laughed aloud, still trying to get him to the floor without having to put in much effort as she sat down on it, back against the bed. "Yeah, I know. I was getting asked where you'd been at nights, and even had Sean telling me that I was gonna 'be a handful' before we even started together. But I guess they were seeing things we were just dismissing, maybe, and getting a little too mixed up in their facts. There's something I've wanted to know, though...are you happier, this very instance, than you were with Ana? Or just as happy? Less happy?" "Different happy. Don't ask me to put it in words sweety, I can't. I'm not as stressed about 'normal' things, if that helps you understand anything... I'm just more comfortable in my own skin now..." "Well no offense or anything, but your wife was kind of a....well...a bitch is the best way I can put it." Amy quickly tried to dodge whatever his reaction would be by getting back to her feet and hugging Brandon's arm. "But that's the past. So what're we gonna do now that you've worked so hard to get me up?" "A...actually... I'm feeling a little tired now. Maybe we could take another little cat nap before we do something..." "You hate living, don't you?!" Stifling a laugh and groan of frusteration, Amy shoved Brandon backwards as best she could. "Not now. I've got three wonderful reasons every morning to open my eyes for." It was cliche' but hopefully would catch Amy off-guard and with enough of an "awww" factor he could make his next move. "I...I'd hurt you, but that was just damn cute..." "So... we're in agreement. Back to bed for a bit... to... rest... and then off to lunch afterwards?" Knitting his fingers behind Amy's back Brandon pulled her to him suggestively as he spoke. Amy's grin was as wide as his. Finally there was a moment in life where she didn't have to think, didn't have anywhere to be and actually *could* lie in bed all morning with someone she loved. Among piss-poor so called 'feuds' and practical stalkers backstage, both of them could still smile. "...Sounds like a nice way to jumpstart the day." Next-> Trip. Guilt.
