The Road to Love, Head on Collision

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempest shook her head, tossing her coal black hair about her bare shoulders. She wore a deep blue top with tiny sparkling silver straps and pair of matched silver pants that shimmered as she stepped up to the head waiter with a slender Asian woman. The two laughed and chattered excitedly until the waiter asked them their names. "I'm Tempest!" she said with a grin. "I called in a reservation yesterday for two. Do you have anything by the window?" As the waiter checked, Tempest's friend and business manager Miyuki looked up and nudged Tempest. "You'll never guess who else is waiting." Tempest turned and saw Monte Burns, who smiled and took a step in her direction. "Oh no," She said as she rolled her eyes and turned the other way. Miyuki smirked and said, "Come on now. I thought we agreed that hostility wasn't needed at this point." "I lied to shut you up," Tempest whispered sharply as Burns came closer. "Hello there ladies," said Burns, as he stood in front of them with Fortune right behind him. "This is my friend Lance, and we were just about to grab a table for dinner." Tempest's eyelids fluttered as she said, "Well, you may be waiting awhile. I had to make a reservation a couple days ago." She looked from Monte to Lance and back and added, "And I only made it for two." Burns chuckled slightly, obviously humored by Tempest's remark and acknowledging her jab. He looked back at Lance, who was smiling as well, before looking back towards Tempest with a grin on his face. He nodded in Miyuki's direction before looking at Tempest once more. "That was great thinking on your part, Tempest," said Burns while nodding his head. "In a place like this, you always need to make sure and plan ahead for reservations." As Tempest looked over at him with a cocked eyebrow, Burns leaned over and whispered something into Lance's ear. Lance nodded and slowly walked away towards the dining area, leaving Monte there with the women. Tempest shifted her weight a little and said, "Well... some of us aren't used to places like this as a matter of course." She eyed him curiously and said, "But I don't suppose that's something you'd understand." "No, not really," replied Monte with an unintentional smirk. "I've become quite familiar with places such as this. This isn't one of the better ones, of course, but you take what you can get." Tempest stood up a little straighter as Miyuki put a hand on her arm and said calmly, "Well, it seems to be underrated if you ask me. So naturally I made a reservation for Tempest and myself. It's stock can only go up with the patronage of accomplished persons like Tempest." Burns nodded and shrugged his shoulders, deciding to hold off on saying anything else for the time being. He recognized Tempest's temperament and didn't want things to get out of hand. Instead, he caught sight of Lance motioning to him out of the corner of his eye in the dining area. "Well, ladies, shall we?" Burns said while motioning his hand toward the dining area. Tempest and Miyuki looked at each other and frowned a little. With a shrug they walked into the dining area and saw a table set for four... no sign of the separate arrangements Miyuki had set up for them. Miyuki's eyes widened just a bit as she turned to Tempest who was already facing the head waiter. "There must be some mistake," Tempest said. "We're not in the same party." "Oh, I took care of that," Burns said before the waiter could muster a word. Monte smiled at Tempest and motioned to her seat. "Lance and I are here together. You're here with your friend, who I must say is quite ravishing herself..." "Whoa!" Tempest said holding up her hand. "We didn't make reservations for four people, we made them for two." She turned to the waiter and said, "I mean if they had a reservation before us that's one thing, but I'm not..." Once again, before the waiter could say a word, Burns stepped in to cut him off. "Actually, Tempest," he said with a slight grin, "Lance and I didn't have a reservation. We just decided to stop off here, and once I saw you two, I had Lance tell the waiter to set us up for four. It was no bother." Tempest looked at him in complete silence, frozen, with her mouth slightly open. Miyuki put her hand over her eyes and then thinking quickly reached out to Tempest. But it was a moment too late. "Bother?! It was no bother? It looks to me like you somehow pushed your way past a reservation I made two days ago. What, you just walk into a place and all the normal rules go out the window?" Seeing the look in her eye and sensing that she was about to erupt, Burns hesitated for a moment. Despite all the games, he still didn't want to upset Tempest so much that he would ruin all of his chances with her. Unfortunately, the smugness won out in this round. "What you have to understand, Tempest," Burns said with a sigh, "is that I can get a reservation anywhere, anytime that I want. That's one of the perks that you get when you're 'The Golden Child' around here." Miyuki opened her mouth, but then shut it as soon as she spied the look in Tempest's eye. "Well that doesn't apply to me!" Tempest snapped, loudly enough to draw attention from the guests around her. The head waiter looked on very nervously as she growled, "I did make a reservation, and not to have dinner with you. So someone better find me a table of my own..." she wheeled on the waiter briefly and then turned back to Monte, "or you'd better go wait your damn turn!" Now, Burns was becoming less amused by the situation. He had already been humiliated in public once by Tempest back at Body Count, and now she was causing a scene at a restaurant with him. Instead of letting it go and just taking a seat next to Lance, Monte looked directly into Tempest's eyes. "How dare you!" he exclaimed with the smile long gone. "I offer to share a nice dinner along with my friend with you and your friend, and you treat me like this? That might be perfectly acceptable where YOU come from, but when you turn down a dinner invitation around here, that's being a little god damn rude!" Tempest stepped toward him and said, "Rude, where I come from is taking what isn't yours or thinking you're so important you can just do whatever you want and everyone will go along! I will not! This is supposed to be my table and I did not invite you to it!" Burns shook his head and sighed as he looked over at the table. "Well, I guess that's too bad," he said turning to the waiter. Monte made a motion towards the waiter and then took a seat next to Lance. The waiter then looked up at Tempest and smiled sheepishly. "I'm sorry about the confusion, Miss," he said nervously. "Your table will be available in just a moment." Tempest stepped in front of the waiter and "Excuse me! Last I checked, my reservation comes before him just showing up and expecting a seat! What the Hell is the matter with you?" She stopped and said, "Oh wait, I think I get it." She smiled and turned to Monte as she leaned over the table and said sweetly, "You just paid him off. That's how you do most anything isn't it?" "Most anything, yes," Burns said without moving his eyes off the menu. "However, there are some things that I would rather do on my own. But I've found that when I try to do something good, it only ends up backfiring in my face anyway. Of course, you already knew that." Tempest's smile didn't waver, but her tone dropped a notch as she said, "I have yet to see you try and do anything good. You'd try and buy me as easily as you did the waiter. But I'm glad you're accustomed to paying for things Monte. That way you won't be too far afield when you go off down the street to buy the affection you can't win from me." She stood up and started to walk away. "That's how you deal with everything isn't it, Tempest?" he stood up and called after her. "You just run off and play the struggling young woman routine!" he yelled, jerking his arm away from Lance's. "Go ahead, run away again, Tempest. It's tough to face your feelings, so you just keep running. Maybe one of these days you'll finally run into a wall and wake up!" Tempest wheeled around and marched back to the table, staring over it at Monte. Miyuki tugged at her arm, trying to get her out the door. "What would you know about struggle? You've never had a genuine struggle in your life! And if you wanted to convince me that you actually cared about me, maybe a little genuine respect would help! You know, something like not treating me like a second class citizen, just because you can buy everyone else around you!" She waved her arm to indicate the now terrified restaurant staff. "What do I know about struggle?!" Monte yelled, his voice rising to as loud as Lance could remember for a long time. "Just because I've had money, just because I grew up in a stable household, just because I lived in a nice house doesn't mean I don't know about struggle. I worked my ass off to become the best wrestler I could be. Hour after hour after hour working out, training, and staying prepared. I've been working towards becoming a wrestler since the day I could walk. And now that I am a wrestler, I also have to run a Fortune 500 company during the daytime! I have money, and I spend it. Like it or don't like it, but don't tell me that I don't know about struggle just because I came from a family with money and had two loving parents!" Tempest stalked over toward the table, standing just opposite him and yelled, "Oh excuse me! I forgot that hard work constituted breaking a sweat in your case! Maybe even having to open a door for yourself, or heaven forbid, wait in line at a restaurant. So let me give you a brief primer on struggle. It's about not getting whatever you want all the time!" With that she reached down and grabbed the edge of the table. Miyuki's eyes widened as she started to yell at Tempest to stop, but was too late. Tempest flipped the table up and over, sending it crashing into a glass partition. Patrons screamed as the sound carried and Tempest barked at Monte, "Have a great dinner... if you can scrape it off the floor that is!" Burns and Fortune leapt up from their chairs as they looked down at the debris on the floor. Monte started to go for Tempest, but Lance reached out and restrained him. After finally managing to break free, Burns grabbed Tempest by the arm. "Listen, you ungrateful bitch!" "What the Hell did you call me?!" Tempest snapped and yanked her arm free. "I don't owe you any gratitude! That's your problem Monte, you think everyone owes you or should feel graced to have your attention! Well I don't want it, cuz you're a jackass and no amount of money can make up for it!" "I'm a jackass, huh? Who was it that ran out this past week and saved your ass? Oh, right, that was me! Usually, when someone does something nice for them, they thank the person," he said, still fighting off Lance from behind. He noticed a couple of the waiters gathered near the entrance but paid them little attention. "You, on the other hand, bitch about it. You can't handle someone trying to help you out, because you're Tempest and you've got to do it all. That's your problem. You just can't stand it when someone else helps you out, when someone gives you some aid. This isn't Hong Kong anymore, Tempest, so why don't you start acting like it!" Tempest took a step back, her face contorted in anger as she wrenched free of Miyuki and grabbed the nearest breakable item, in this case a carafe of wine. She hurled it at Monte, narrowly missing him and then Lance Fortune's head. "I didn't need your help! And I don't need your pity! Or your charity! Or your oh-so-superior attitude that you have so much to offer me, when all you'd really do is one worse than Hong Kong! Being owned once was enough!" "I'm going to say this nice and slow so that you understand me," Monte said with a serious look in his eye. "You throw one more thing this way, and, woman or not, I'm going to come over there and beat some gratitude out of you!" Tempest grabbed a plate of pasta from a now deserted table and flung it at him with all her might. "Try it!" she screamed. "By all means come on over and see if you can live up your own press releases!" After ducking the flying plate of pasta, Burns ran forward before Lance could stop him. He dove straight for Tempest, and the two of them flew across a couple's table, sending even more plates and glasses crashing to the floor. Lance and Miyuki hurriedly tried to separate them, but they weren't able to do it. As Tempest and Monte tried to disentangle themselves from the tablecloth, four policemen finally arrived on the scene. The cops got Lance and Miyuki to stand aside, as they pushed the debris away and finally pulled Tempest and Monte to their feet, still kicking and lashing out at one another. With two officers apiece to keep them at bay, Tempest and Monte were both handcuffed. "You have the right to remain silent..." "I will not! Let me go you jerks! He started it!" Tempest squawked as she was shoved out the door and into a waiting patrol car. "What? Do you know who I am?!" Monte snarled as he was shoved into the same car. Miyuki and Lance Fortune stood in the center of the wreckage that used to be a fine restaurant and stared at the devastation around them. They looked at each other and then sighed, and started dialing their respective cell phones.
