12/28/2001 9:00PM He almost didn't answer the phone. While only a handful of people knew that he was staying there, the Strangler didn't put it past anyone to reveal his location and subject him to harassing phone calls. Other times, it was just the hotel staff calling to do a 5 minute poll of how he was enjoying his stay. It was funny how they always asked it on the first day... maybe before anything could go wrong. But this call... something told him he should take it. "Hello?" "Paul." The voice sounded almost smug, as though there was some great secret to be told. Of course, the Strangler recognized the caller right away, and his eyes narrowed a bit at the thought of the conversation. "Why are you calling us? Has something happened?" "No, nothing as of yet..." the caller said calmly. "If anything changes, I'll let you know of course... but you see, I'm in town right now and thought that we ought to meet up to discuss a few ideas I have in mind for you. There's a bar in town that's usually packed on Friday nights... a good place to discuss things without drawing any attention to yourself..." "Discuss ideas for us? Forget it. We already have our own plans." Well, that wasn't entirely true... but he'd rather be in control of his career than accept advice from someone else. "Besides, we don't feel much like being out amongst people... you know how much we hate crowds." "So much that you perform in front of them night in and night out? I think I understand something about you, Paul... maybe something you don't realize about yourself." "Really. And what would that be?" The Strangler shot back, slightly annoyed at the pretentiousness of the caller. "You like those crowds, because they are just close enough to make you feel cared about...but just distant enough that you can keep them that way... and not have to deal with feeling anything for them. Your life revolves around seeking approval... and you distance yourself at the same time from the masses who could one day reject you." "That's not true. We just don't like being in a crowded room, it's a natural concept with many people..." the Strangler answered. "Must there be a deep hidden meaning to everything?" "Sometimes, yes" The caller continued. "But meet me there in an hour... the bar name is O'Leary's Pub. I'll be there around 10." The Strangler began to protest, but heard the siren of a passing ambulance blaring through the phone. He paused, as he had heard the same exact siren passing by outside... "Well... we could be there. But why not just meet here?" the Strangler asked, as he moved towards the window, scanning the street. His eyes fixed on a pay phone at the corner, and the figure inside. "It's easy to get to and better than some old smoke-filled bar... besides we have a lot to do tomorrow, and don't much feel like going out..." "See you in an hour, Paul...better yet, I'll give a little extra time... 10:30 it is." the caller said, before hanging up the phone. The Strangler heard the dial tone in his ear as he watched the person in the phone booth exit, and cross the street to hail a cab. One phrase came to his mind, and passed through his lips in a whisper... accurately describing his feeling for the moment. "Son of a bitch."
