Just Dreaming ...

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams." -Eleanor Roosevelt Seth Ragin stood outside the Holiday Inn meeting room where the fWo road agent sat patiently. As time passed by the mumbling inside the room got louder. "Christ, can anything else possibly go wrong with this growing cancer!" The road agent was not too happy, not only was he not happy with the three but he was clearly unhappy with the state of the fWo as well. "Fucking board of directors, blah blah blah... we're great!" He just could not keep quiet, Seth did not really want to hear this so he made a muffled coughing sound hoping the road agent would hear him and calm down. It got quiet, really quiet. He looked at the clock adjacent to him and noticed that nearly 30 minutes passed. They had not bothered to even call to let them know that they were on their way. For all he knew, they were not coming, still. He opened up the door to the room as the road agent started another one of his tangents, "I swear, trying to fix a sinking ship sucks big donkey..." He froze as Seth sat down in front of him. "Can we talk about me and my fWo career until those two get here?" The road agent sat up and gained composure, "Sure, we'll talk about your fWo career..." "Well I would like to eventually work my way up to the United States" Seth began to talk but was rudely interrupted, "Your fWo career as a tag team contender, not a singles title contender. You do realize we brought you up as a tag team contender... nothing more and nothing less!" "Okay!" said Seth in reply. He really was at a loss of words, it seemed as though fWo was going to be hard to work with and advance in his career. He knew it was too early to judge and after what happened at Halloween Hell this past Thursday; anything at a moments notice could happen... his mom always told him to take things with stride. "So about Triality, where does management want us to be in the next few months?" The road agent shrugged, "I really shouldn't say until the other two are here!" Trying to talk to this guy was like trying to talk a brick wall. "No plans for a title run or at least title contendership... anything at all?" The road agent was reluctant to reply. "No. Nothing."
