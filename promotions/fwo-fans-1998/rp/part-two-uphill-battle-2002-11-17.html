Part Two: Uphill Battle

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 19, 1997 Chapel Hill, North Carolina "Medic!" Troy Windham dropped his newly- won CSWA Presidential Championship belt on the ground as he helped Poison Ivy bring the Eliminator to the medical area backstage. Eli was walking largely under his own power, but it was obvious he couldn't put any weight on that right knee. "Shit... Troy, don't worry about it. I'm okay." The Eliminator just lost the belt to Troy in a brutal match. The post- match festivities included Troy's stable, "Generation X=Press" putting Eli through one table while another was held on top of his body. The impact drove a piece of metal into the back of Eli's knee. "The hell you are," countered Ivy, "You need to see a doctor." "No," replied Eli, "No doctor." All the while Ivy and Troy attempted to guide Eli to the medical area, Eli pulled away from them. He got a step and a half away before his knee collapsed on itself and he fell to the floor. Immediately, a slew of CSWA wrestlers were over to give him a hand. "Get back!" he shouted at them, "I can make it!" "Bullshit," argued Ivy, "YOU. NEED. HELP." She stared into his eyes - and he knew he couldn't win this argument. "Okay," he agreed, "But only you and Troy help me up." The three continued to hobble toward the medics, interrupted only sporadically by a wrestler or staff member who told Eli and Troy that their match stole the show - that they both had a huge future in front of them. "I just want you to know I'm not going to accept the belt this way," said Troy, guilt still clouding his words, "If this is as serious an injury as their initial prognosis.... no fucking way." "Bullshit, Troy," retorted Eli, "This is your shot, kid. You've been working just as hard as me since I walked in the door - and they're finally taking notice." Eli shook his head, wincing in pain as he did so. "You take this belt and you be the King of the Slackers because it puts asses in the seats. Whether this is permanent or temporary, you take as much credit for putting me on the shelf as you can. You're one of the best heels in this sport, kid - don't forget it." As they reached the medical area and Eli sat down on one of the benches, Ivy sat down next to him while Troy lingered for a moment, the Presidential Title belt back in his hand, almost as an afterthought. "I'm serious, Troy." "Yeah, I know." "Go do your thing," continued Ivy, "You need to cut a promo on this while the emotions are still swirling in your mind." "You really think so?" "Definitely," agreed Eli, "Take all that shit in your head and turn it into King of the Slacker arrogance like only you can do. Show the media you're in charge. Show 'em that you're not just the King of the Slackers, that you're the King of All Media." Troy stared at them with a dubious look on his face - but he recognized the deadly seriousness of the situation, so he left with the belt - and the Boy Troy sneer etched on his face. "You think he'll be okay?" asked Ivy. "I think he'll be fine," replied Eli, "I'm just worried about my knee." "Yeah," said Ivy after a moment, "me too. But somehow I don't think this is the end."
