Training Daze - Week 4

23 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Luke Starkey and Alex Newsome sat at a round table with beers in front of them. It had become a familiar scene over the last several months. Just as familiar was the source of the beer they were drinking: Luke had somehow managed to be in the right place at the right time and somehow prevented a large man's girlfriend from being smacked by a drunken idiot inadvertently looking for trouble. The large man (after thoroughly kicking the drunken idiot's ass) was more than grateful to Luke, and once again Luke and Alex found themselves at a bar, drinking for free. They had been working in the gym all day, training for Luke's big wrestling debut mere days away, and after hitting the shower they hit the bar. The congenial conversation that had marked their early friendship wasn't going on. Each man seemed more than wrapped up in his own thoughts. Luke, despite his casual demeanor and air of nonchalance, was actually nervous and worried about the upcoming match at the fWo's Meltdown. He'd never wrestled before. He'd never been in front of thousands of people before. He'd never even worn tights before. Alex was also deeply entrenched in his own mind. The ongoing situation between Lance Knight, Brand Frontier, and Voran Xias had been escalating into something that promised to be ugly and definitive: one man was going to prove himself the superior wrestler over the other two and it was probably going to happen at Meltdown as well. Meltdown. Mere days from now, Luke and Alex's lives would be irrevocably changed, one way or another. The bored-looking waitress came dutifully by their table to collect their empty bottles and deposit fresh, full ones in front of them. Neither man seemed to notice the round firmness of her buttocks as she walked away
