Laying Daemons To Rest. Part I

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brandon pushed the tape into the VCR. As it slipped into the sleek black machine he glanced to the inscription. January 12th, 1998 He would remember that day for as long as he lived. For two months he had been haunted with the thoughts that he had lost his wife forever to a monster. He had searched everywhere for her and his epic quest had proved pointless. No information was found to her whereabouts. Following the instructions on the note that was stuck to his door that morning he had not gotten the police involved. He knew it was stupid but he was young and believed that was for the best. In those two months he deconstructed. His whole life fell apart. Ana was gone, three months pregnant with their first son and she was plucked so easily from his life. It was a mistake that he has made many times since. He kept promising himself to shield his family from these horrors but somehow something always happened. He thought back to when Sean disappeared with Troy's son Bobby. He could still see the bruised face of his best friend in his mind from mere weeks ago. All of that had happened because of him. He knew that if he had just walked away from all of this that none of that would have happened. They would have all been safe, but yet he still trudged along. Never stepping too far away from his ideals and this so called war he was waging. He also knew the elation he felt when he finally got Ana back. That was what this video was. The notorious ransom match. As the static filled the screen Brandon glanced around to see if Ana was nearby. He couldn't hear any movement in the adjoining rooms so he reached into the now empty videotape case and pulled out the very note that was left on his door that fateful morning. Dog-eared and worn it crinkled a bit as it unfolded. Looking over the letter like it were the first time he had seen it all of those old emotions ran through him again. Brandon Frontier. We have your wife. She is unharmed. If you follow our instructions she will be returned to you safely. In two weeks at the E.W.A Monday Night Madness show you will have to participate in a contest. There are special rules to this contest. We will pick your opponent and you will not be able to lay a finger on him. If you do you will never see your wife again. Your opponent that night will be The Brotherhood's own "Kodiak" Vic Creed. The following week Brand of course accepted those terms. Two weeks were a long time to wait and he knew the price he would pay for her return but it was a small price indeed. The monster known as Vic Creed had decimated his competition and being as green as he was Brandon knew that he would be hard pressed not to walk away seriously injured even if he was allowed to fight back. It was a small cost. The screen rolled a bit and the image flicked on. His memories flooded back of that night. The sights, the sounds, the smells. Everything. It was that night...
