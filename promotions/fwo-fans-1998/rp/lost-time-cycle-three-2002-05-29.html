Lost Time - Cycle Three

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Ivy?" "Sean--" stammered Ivy, "I--" The tension was overwhelming. Triple X had just returned to his apartment in Orlando for a fitful night of sleep before rejoining the wrestling promotion on the road... "What are you doing here?" asked Trip, "I mean, I wondered why you'd left the tour yesterday but I didn't think you'd be... HERE." Only, in an odd sense of deja vu, there was someone waiting for him in his apartment. This visitor, however, was somewhat more welcome. "You weren't home," explained Ivy, "and I wanted to cook you dinner. Remember, I owe you one." Trip can see where she's going with this. "Ivy... don't." "I stopped at the store," continued Ivy, putting some diced onions into a pot, checking the status of rapidly boiling water, "and picked up some spaghetti fixings." Ivy nervously continues stirring her pot of sauce - whatever might be in it, deliberately avoiding Trip's gaze. "Does this have anything to do with what happened last week?" asked Trip, already knowing the answer, "Because if it does, you don't have to do this." Ivy puts the spoon down and walks up to Trip, throwing her arms around him and hugging him tightly. "Yes," she replied, "I do." Trip looks into her eyes. He can't tell what her thoughts are, but he knows what name is on her brain. "If this is about Linda--" he began, but gets cut off instantly. "I'd like to do what we should've done from the start about Linda Johns," said Ivy, "and talk about it." Talk about it? That means she wants to talk. That's good news. "I'm willing to talk about it," replied Trip. Ivy, smiling, returns to his kitchen. "Sit down," she said, "and have a drink. Start from the beginning with her, and don't leave anything out." "You sure?" asked Trip. "Yes. I want to know, regardless of what it encompasses." "Okay," said Trip, taking a deep breath, "You want to hear the full story..." Triple X and Poison Ivy have had some problems in the past. Now, they're going to deal with their biggest current problem in a very direct way. They're going to talk about it.
