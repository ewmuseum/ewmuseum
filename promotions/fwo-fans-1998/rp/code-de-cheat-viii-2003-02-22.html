Code de Cheat: VIII.

22 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the tiniest print the Fuse Bros. were reducing their text to the sized font of about 2. And that was a small font, a very small font... but it was a price T and C were willing to pay. For they needed the entire thirty pages of cheat codes, and this was the only way to do it - on one sheet of paper. It was either that, or tape up every single page to each ring post... and there might not be enough room for all of that. Regardless, Conor had finally finished minimizing the words, and, by doing so, turned around to face his older Brother. "All done-" But something stopped him, as a giant hand placed itself against his shoulder. Conor snapped his head up, looking dead into the eyes of Eric Jeffries, the librarian. The mad librarian. "Sir." He started. "I'm going to have to ask you to leave..." Conor nodded. "I understand. Just let me hit print and we'll be out of here in a minute." Yet, Eric didn't seem that understanding. "You had your time." He spoke very dark and deep, as it intimidated Conor. "Now go." Again, Conor nodded. "Yes, just after I hit print..." He reached for the print button, only to realize Eric snatching the mouse from his hands... "You've stayed way past your time." He said. "There were good people turned away from this computer today... I think you've done enough." That's where Tyler stepped in, walking right in-between the two of them. "Hold it, big guy. If you just give us one more minute... we'll be gone. We're sorry we took forever, but, we need these cheat codes." Snarling, Eric looked down at Tyler's brown "T"-shirt, and then at Conor's green "C"-shirt. He raised an eyebrow. "What kind of people are you?" Taking his mouse back, Conor went to click print, but again it was snatched out of his hands. "I didn't say you could do that." He smiled evilly. "Now get out." Standing up from his chair, Conor joined Tyler by standing beside him. "Make us."
