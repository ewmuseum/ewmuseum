Just Dreaming .......

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams." -Eleanor Roosevelt Seth pushed the key card into the slot and quickly opened the door to their hotel room. All the lights were out to his surprise, he quickly moved through the room looking for his friends but to no avail. He saw the bathroom light on and glided right towards the door, without knocking he slung it open, no one was there. "Where the..." As he began to go off on a tangent, his cell phone rang loudly. He pulled it off his belt and opened it up. "Hello?" There was a bit of silence followed up by some static, he was about to end the call when a voice chimed in at the right time. "Seth, is that you?" Seth froze, "Ummmm yeah?" He leaned up against the wall; the phone call was obviously facing a little delay. "I'm sorry to deliver this news..." The first thing that went through his mind was that the fWo management decided to terminate his contract due to the missed meeting. His thoughts blurred out what the person was actually saying, he could not believe it though. "Pardon me but can you repeat that?" He waited patiently; just hoping it was not a contract termination. "Seth, there's been a serious accident. Tisha and Troy have been transported to San Jose Hospital!" Seth was astonished he could not believe it. His knees buckled as he remembered the car accident, the black Lincoln, and more importantly, the things he said and/or thought. His knees buckled causing him to fall to the ground. Gasping for air, his left hand clung to his chest. "Are" he tried to spit it out, "Are they alright?" He forgot about the delay and became impatient and worried. "ARE THEY ALRIGHT?" He shouted into the phone, "They... they are fine. Tisha only has a few bruises but Troy may not be so lucky, they are giving him an MRI right now." Seth took a deep breath as he stood up on his feet again, "I've got to go... I've got to get there and be with them both!" He hung up the phone and as quick as he entered the hotel he had left. As he ran down the hallway he could only think about what he had said and how he was going to apologize. ... Now only if this was a dream.
