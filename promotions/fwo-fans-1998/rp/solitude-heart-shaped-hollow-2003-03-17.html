Solitude - Heart-Shaped Hollow

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The world had definitely come crashing down. About eighteen hours before, Amy Campbell had arrived home from a direct flight she'd taken straight out of Tel Aviv. A few of her belongings were still piled under a mound of rubble that used to be a section of an arena, but some CDs and a few articles of clothing weren't all she was missing. "Just listen, Amy..." There was a voice almost booming from a speaker phone on a table near the foot of a couch that had held a tiny redhead for hours. It was definitely a friend, Sean "Triple X" Stevens...but the call wasn't at all welcome. Of course, everyone had worried; News of Brandon's accident got out and suddenly it was 'just awful' to the world that Amy had receeded into herself. They'd been talking - well, he'd been talking - for ten or so minutes by then, and Aimz had made sure he knew how unwelcome the intrusion into her sudden seclusion was. Regardless, Sean was droning out an official news report to her. "The incident also seems to have claimed the lives of two of the Fan's Wrestling Organizations most recognized Superstars.
