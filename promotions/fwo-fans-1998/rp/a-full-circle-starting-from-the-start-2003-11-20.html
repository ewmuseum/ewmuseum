A Full Circle, Starting from the Start.

20 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
September 1, 2003 After Ruahh~! The office of Silver Fox Fox sat behind his desk looking at the Carter brothers. Jim was still in his singlet, with a t-shirt on and a towel around his neck. Chris, who missed a flight home for this meeting, adorned jeans and a "Saves the Day" black t-shirt. The brothers looked worried; they didn't like the general vibe that they were getting from their supervisor. Silver Fox looked up from his notes at the two, visibly sweating young adults. "I see that you two are worried. Well...I understand why you all might be, but this is actually going to be a good thing for everyone." The arms that were quite bony at the time that were Chris were trembling. James was quietly panting. The two looked convinced that they were going to get fired. "The thing is, you all appeal to a certain group, a group that you identify well with, being the "Emo Kids." And that is super; you all have the individual look, which is even more. But for babyfaces, you all look a little, how do I say...wimpy" Tears started to roll down Chris' face. It was true that the younger Emo Kid had a sensitive heart and this was the case. "Christopher, no need to cry. The thing we are going to do, is you all will report to OCW on Wednesday. You all will polish yourselves to a skilled, fit team. Christopher, we are going to try to put some weight and muscle onto that frame, and James, we are going to get you a bit trim. You all will be down there, until further notice for the Wrestler of the Year tournament matches, in which you all will wrestle and we will evaluate your performances. Is what I am getting to clear?" The two nodded. "Yes sir," came from the nervous Jim. "Alright, see you all hopefully in a short while." The two got up and left the office. The road back to the fWo started here.
