Master-MIND, Phase II

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everything around him was still as black as the night. His hands and legs were no longer tied down, but the complete darkness of the room paralyzed him with fear. A fear so great that he dared not move from his position on the concrete floor. Through the darkness the soothing voice he had heard over two weeks ago went through his head again. Calming his tumultuous soul. Serenity is the lie they've been feeding you your entire life. Peace is the delusional dream of lazy, uninspired men. Silence and calm are the precursors to the inevitable explosion of mayhem and violence. You are safe here, Fallen One. Your mind is at ease, bereft of the outside interference and machinations of others. Free of the constant bombardment of unwanted advice and unasked-for suggestions. Safe from the confusions and struggles of this wretched mortal earth. A warm tingling feeling rushed over his body as he listened. For the first time in his life he felt protected from the unknown evil that had always lurked behind him in the shadows. Now that he was inside of those shadows, hidden from the outside world he felt safer than he ever had before. Safer than he was when he barricaded himself in his apartment, afraid that he would disappear if he opened the door. Safer than he was when he was still with his aunt and uncle at home. Just safe and secure. But for how long can you remain hidden from the world, and from your true self? I will not press you to return to the mindless drudgery of your former life. It matters not to me whether you wish to stay here, huddled in the soothing embrace of darkness for eternity. But what will that accomplish? The peace you currently enjoy is temporary; even now they seek you out, to return you to what you once were and the internal anguish of that futile, tortured existence. "I will come out again," he whispered to the darkness. The future is YOURS, Fallen One. Do you wish to hermit yourself in a fleeting sense of serenity, knowing that your former false friends are hunting you, wanting to force you into a pain-filled life that did not work? "They'll never find me," he said to himself. Or will you hunt them first... "I will find them before they find me. I will strike down upon them and unleash complete Hell. The future is mine."
