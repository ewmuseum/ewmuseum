Failure

17 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He picked her up and hooked her for a DDT, but she elbowed at his head repeatedly, forcing him to release the lock leading up to it. She kicked him in the stomach, hooked him, and nailed him with a picture-perfect Double-Arm DDT. She stomped on him once before climbing to the top rope, and prepared for her finisher- the Falling Star, a variation on the Shooting Star Press. She leaped off the top rope, going for the Falling Star.... .....but Downfall rolled out of the way at the last minute. The crowd cheered, getting into the match a bit, and as Kali woozily stood up, Downfall rolled her up with a School Boy from behind. ONE! TWO! THREE! The crowd cheered as Downfall quickly released the School Boy and slid out of the ring, and Kali sat-up in dis-belief. He circled aroudn the ring and backed up the rampway, eyes never leaving those of Kali Saturos. And she looked to him, the man who had beaten her with a roll-up in her first match-up.... ....and she smiled. Wednesday Warfare. I failed. "I shall look upon my enemy, and the dark place shall have his soul." Perhaps I was over-confident. After all, there was seemingly no greater force than what pushes me- what brings me to the edge and then pushes me off. Perhaps, perhaps I was not pushed. Perhaps I faltered, and perhaps I teetered on the edge of the ledge instead. Maybe. "You don't understand yet. But you will. You will." There is something about this person that pinned my shoulders to the mat. There is something that compells me, that draws me towards him. I am still unsure what that could be. Perhaps it is something I have yet to experience. I have been hidden from view. Hidden from the outside world by so long, thanks to Rei and her pills. There is so much that I have yet to experience. So mainly earthly, and un-earthly desires and feelings. Am I feeling one now? Hard to tell. "Are you going to tell me the truth, or are you going to tell me a lie? Because I would prefer the lie. Very much so." Yeah, perhaps I have done some things I'm not proud of. Maybe I got my shoulders pinned to the mat on Warfare. But maybe those of us who cannot relate to such things, maybe those of us who seemingly want to get out. Maybe it is my time to restrain them. And maybe, just maybe, this person who pinned my shoulders to the mat possesses the qualities I need to feel emotions. Maybe, for once in my pathetic life, I can become something greather than I am now. I failed. And you know what? I find myself caring less and less as each minute passes. Let it push me, let myself teeter on the edge. I will not fall. I will not fall. Fin.
