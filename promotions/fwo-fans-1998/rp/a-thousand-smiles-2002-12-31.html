A Thousand Smiles

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Amy's own thoughts made her sick. She was propped against the headboard of a hard hotel bed, blankly staring ahead at a pay-per-view movie - one of her favorites at that - on the TV screen at the bed's foot...and all she could think of was where 'her' Mighty Hero was? Well, she'd just seen him hours before in the ring against the tandom of James Cagle and Dingleberry, and watched his match with Chronos...but why wasn't he up here to talk to her yet? Then again, she reminded herself, Steven Shadows had gone considerably too far in the Aimz vs. Graphic Violence match a little over a week and a half before...it gave her every reason to be nervous, right? It had certainly given Brandon reason to stay with her nearly every waking moment since she got back on the road. The man had even gone to the hospital to see her, her! It sickened her that she didn't mind or try to protest her unofficial bodyguard, and even worse was the fact that she sat alone and thought about nothing other than his location when he wasn't there. 'Damnit, Amy. The man has a WIFE and you're wondering where he is, who he's with?' She'd try to put her thoughts to rest with that reasoning...but it never seemed to work. Why would she even bother thinking of him, anyway? She had a boyfriend who cared de---oh, wait---well.....she had a boyfriend, and that was enough to keep her from considering her makeshift caretaker too much, right? Why think this much when there was a perfectly good movie, and such nice relative silence before her? After a few more minutes, Amy laid back and tried to relax even though - after having seen it nearly twenty times already - 'The Fifth Element' didn't demand her concentration and after another few moments, she was halfway asleep. Until a knock came. She didn't shout to ask who was there - and didn't particularly want to know. Trying to ignore it, Amy shut her eyes for a second time...but it came again with a hint of urgency. It was a quiet, fast knock instead of a giant booming fist, so she ruled out the possibility of opening the door to come face-to-face, unprepared with 'Graphic Violence' and finally, groggily, stepped over to the door to open it with two fists readied. Brandon Roche stood there. A large smile plastered on his face, he had practiced the worried look on the way up the steps but whenever he thought about seeing Amy he couldn't help but grin. "Easy there killer... it's just me. I just got off the phone with the kids and I wanted to check on you to make sure you were set for the night." "Christ, Brandon! I didn't think you'd come this late..." For the first time in the longest time, Amy felt almost self-concious regarding her appearance - which was then rather shaggy - with her hair half-tied into a pony tail and the baggiest of clothes hanging low enough to show almost too much. "If I'd known you were coming, I wouldn't have bothered getting dressed." But she was Amy, so she never relayed that thought and instead pushed it aside with a smile and a lighthearted comment, though it caused her to go a little red after finishing for being overly flirtatious. "And miss you in this sexy outfit? I'm glad you didn't know I was coming." Brandon smirked, a blush planting itself on his cheeks. "So am I invited in for a few or are we going to stand here and wait for our picture to get taken again?" With a wink Brandon assumed the invite was there and stepped close to Amy to let himself in to her room. Being close to her he could smell the herball mixture of her shampoo, stopping momentarily to breath in the scent Brandon caught himself lingering and stepped by. She was a bit taken aback by Brandon's sudden personality - risque jokes and confidence were standing where a mild nervousness had once been. It confused her a little, but Amy'd always played that same game...though, she recalled, whenever she'd done it the intent was to mask and lighten other feelings. Nonetheless, she smiled up and the Paragon and allowed herself to sag back onto the bed, stretching her sore frame before sitting against the headboard once again. Brandon watched her, the smile still not wiped off of his face. For some reason, just then, she'd realized that this man was the only thing to make her smile as much as she had been lately for a long time. But she had to brush that knowledge away for a moment when curiosity at Brandon's unrelenting gaze took over. "Penny for your thoughts?" The little redhead swivelled so her shoulder rested against the oak board and she faced the Mighty Hero. "I was just thinking about the match tonight actually. You looked good out there... even having to deal with Cagle and that other guy. I don't think you're given enough credit by the boys in the back." Brandon sat down on the hard padded chair next to the small desk. Crossing his legs and leaning back he tried to make himself comfortable but it wasn't working. As he shifted in his seat he worried that he had let himself open up too much, that he had let his guard down and was too far gone. He knew he should have practiced the worried look more. The relentless smile had given him away. He knew that they shared a mutual attraction for one another but he also knew that he could never act on such a thing. He had his reasons she had hers, both very strong and valid. At that same time, Amy was reminding herself of the very same fact but she laughed it off and got back to the subject at hand, trying to keep Brandon as comfortable as possible - she didn't mind him expressing an opinion at all so she muttered a bit in reply, "Well according to them: when you've got tits, who needs respect?" "Well, I think it's high time you went out there and took some respect. That what this whole thing with Ric has been about as it is. I'm tired of everyone saying I'm the Lottery Champion, I earned my spot... just as much as you did. Maybe you need to go out there and take some respect instead of waiting for them to give it to you..." Brand caught himself. He didn't want this to turn in to a bitch session about work. One of the things he likes so much about being with... or rather hanging out with Amy was that work hardly ever entered the picture. It was like they both lived the business so it didn't consume everything about themselves when they were together. "I'll earn it in due time." Amy wasn't particularly concerned about 'the business' when she was around Brandon; there seemed to be so many other things they could openly discuss, so the subject took a quick turn. "But enough of that...what's up with you and Ana?" She bit her lip - maybe the redhead had gone too far, but she nee---wanted to know. Brandon stammered a bit. The quick change in conversation, especially to that topic shocked him. "I... she... that is, we are talking some matters through." Brandon felt awkward discussing this with anyone, especially someone he had 'those' feelings for. "There's been a lot of water under the bridge I guess you can say. We had some tough times in the past and I thought we were making progress. I... I just don't know..." She shivered almost violently for a moment when her eyes caught Brandon's. Even though she was inexplicably jealous of Ana 'Frontier', Amy wanted her to stay with Brandon because something within her couldn't stand the sharp glint of pain in his eyes when he talked about the relationship with his estranged wife. She regretted bringing it up, and tried to mend things - again without thinking - by hopping up and leaning over Brandon's chair to hug him around the neck. When she pulled back, it was to absently run a hand over his head as she said, "Hey......I'm sorry." in the softest tone the Paragon had ever heard her use. "It's ok kiddo. It's nothing you've done. Ana and I have been on this rocky road long before you and I... well... before we, before we met." As Amy had pulled back Brandon had caught her hand in his and now as they held hands he could feel the spark between them. "It's just the way it is ya'know? I'm trying my damndest and that's all I can do. But anyway, now that you've gotten me all depressed why don't you cheer me up with stories of your love life with that Darcy guy." Brandon was trying to turn the tables on this wonderful person, not out of meanness but more out of a defense mechanism. If he heard how great they were doing now maybe it would help put his feelings to rest. At the thought, Amy's expression changed to one of even deeper upset but when her attitude caught up to her, it was a head-on collision. "Well let's see...he does me great favors, like not calling to bother me, and from what I hear bringing other girls into MY appartment to stay with HIM while I'm on the road, so they mostly look after the cleaning I guess...it's pretty nice not coming home to a mess, dontcha' think? Hmm...what else.....oh YEAH! He recently made it clear that he's with me pretty much just to see someone living out his dream...oh, and because I'm one of the best trophy girls a guy could boast about, isn't that sweet?" She bit her lip when finished. The sarcasm had gone a bit too far, and brought her on the verge of tears that she held back with every ounce of her being, praying Brandon didn't sense exactly how deeply cut she was over everything. Another state of shock washed over Brandon as he realized things really were bad off for the couple. It took him aback and he wasn't as quick to respond as he would have liked. The rumors in the locker rooms were normally always that, rumors. This one though had turned out to be true and it sunk the heart of the Paragon. Instead of saying anything Brandon clung to Amy's trembling hand. It was obvious she was trying to hide the anger, the hurt that she felt over the situation. With a deep sigh he stood and using that same hand pulled her, however reluctant she was, in to a warm embrace. Words were at a loss but maybe he could help just by being there. Especially since it was him that had caused this episode. With a shakey sigh, the little redhead pulled back a little and looked up at Brandon with hazy two-toned eyes. For that brief moment, she forgot all about Steven Shadows, her physical state and most of all her 'hardcore' act, allowing herself to say something that the Mighty Hero had waited quite some time to confirm: "Thank you..." She swallowed hard and continued, "...thank you for opening me up - it feels good not to hide." Brandon just held on, instinctively knowing that nothing more needed to be said. Instead he stood there holding his newfound friend safe away from the real world, even if it was only for a brief time. [This has been an Aimz/ Brand Frontier collaboration]
