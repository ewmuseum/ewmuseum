After Warfare.

18 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization After Warfare. December 18, 1999 :::We open with a shot of an empty hallway, and we catch occasional glimpses of fWo workers, milling about an adjacent hallway. We hear the voice of Geoff Green.::: Green: Where is he, anyway? His match just ended...he should be back here by now. :::excited::: Wait until I tell him this- it'll be a huge scoop! The fWo might even double my salary for this one- Offscreen voice: Hey, Green- what exactly is twice nothing? :::Green turns toward the voice, and we see Scott Slugger and Ice making their way through the back, to the Dynasty locker room. Ice has an icepack taped around his knee and is holding the fWo World Title in his right hand.::: Green :::ignoring Slugger::: : Ice, great! You're just the guy I was looking for! Ice: Yeah? Green: You mind doing an interview for me? Ice: Well, actually yes. :::He begins to walk away, but Green scrambles in front of him.::: Green: Wait! Just a minute here, I've got big, big news for you. Trust me, you *want* to hear this. Ice: Start talking. :::Before Green can, Slugger interrupts, having an idea what Green is going to tell Ice.::: Slugger: Before you get started here, Tony, I'm gonna go ahead. I'll be in the locker room. Alright? Ice: Cool with me. :::Slugger quickly walks offscreen.::: Ice: Now, what is this big news you have to tell me? Green: Well, you remember how at the beginning of the show Scott was talking about wanting a shot at the World Title, right? Ice: Yeah... Green: Well, in the middle of the show, he went to Armstrong- Ice :::interrupting::: : *There's* a switch. Green: -and he told Armstrong about it. Ice: Get to the point, Geoff. Green: Well, Armstrong's signed a match for Twilight- you will defend against him and the winner fights the French-eee! :::Green has been grabbed around the throat and lifted violently into the air by the fWo World Champion, who has dropped his belt on the floor in the process.::: Ice: WHAT...did you just say? Green: You...gah...and Scott...ack...at Twilight... :::Ice nonchalantly drops Green on the floor and gathers up his belt, stalking towards the Dynasty locker room.::: Ice :::as he walks::: : What the hell would he do that for? I have no intentions of fighting my best friend, that's for sure... :::Ice arrives at the locker room door, and kicks it off the hinges, startling Chris Universal inside, who is reading the Wall Street Journal.::: Universal: Hey, hey, hold up big man..what's going on? Ice: Have you seen Scott around here? I got a few questions for him. Universal: I haven't, but I'll let you know if I do. Alright? Ice: Alright. :::Universal goes back to reading his paper as Ice stalks from the room, heading towards the back and the doors to the outside. From outside, we hear the sound of a powerful automobile engine starting up, and revving. Ice recognizes this as the sound of Slugger's Dodge Viper, and sprints towards the door, throwing it open as he goes outside to see the Viper peel away in a cloud of smoke. Ice throws his hands up in the air- he can't believe this.::: Ice: What the hell is happening...? :::Frustrated, he turns back towards the door, opening it and heading in as we begin to slowly fade to black.:::
