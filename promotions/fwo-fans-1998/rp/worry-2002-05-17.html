Worry

17 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Donaven's missing!?" Monica Davidson groggily yelled into the receiver of her telephone. The phone's ringing beside her bedside woke her up after the first couple of rings. Her arm stretched across the bed and lifted the receiver to her ears. On the other line was Lucille Winters, the aunt of Donaven Winters. She was sobbing and trying to speak at the same time. Monica's ears were having a hard time making out sentences through the crying, but she got the gist of what was being said. Monica looked over at the digital clock that sat beside the telephone. It's large red numbers displayed "7:38." She groaned to herself and sat up straight in her bed. She crossed her legs and threw the covers back over her as she intently listened to the startling information she was being told. "Kidnapped?" Monica let the question ring through to the other side of the conversation. She couldn't believe everything she was hearing. She had known that Donaven was in bad shape, but hadn't seen him since January. Which she knew was a mistake. It was a mistake to leave him by himself in the fWo. "Mrs. Winters," Monica started. "I'm so sorry to hear about this." She listened again, her mind racing wild about all of the possibilities. "Okay, Mrs. Winters. Yeah... keep me updated if you can. Thank you. Goodbye." Monica hung the phone up. The emotions that she had held in during the phone conversation suddenly came pouring at as she burst into tears just as Donaven's aunt had sounded over the phone. She laid back down in bed and buried her face in her pillow, sobbing as she did. She started to curse at herself for leaving him behind in the fWo; especially after they had promised each other that they'd stay together. She often wondered if Donaven thought about her; because she thought about him all the time. She hadn't seen CyberSlam, and didn't know that Donaven had tried to kill himself; that was until Ivy had told her a week later. "What a way to start a Saturday morning," she muttered to herself through the tears. As she thought, she realized that she thought about Donaven all the time. And now this; being kidnapped. It didn't make sense. She kept trying to answer questions that nobody had the answers to. All she knew was that she hadn't seen him since January. She missed her best friend. And most of all she worried that her best friend may be dead.
