Travelling Circus - Chapter 3

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The fWo is moving out. People are already packing up and rolling for Denver." Mufanga stepped into the tiny trailer with an air of ownership, closing the door behind him. "The only thing Universal is telling me is that we are doing house shows now, and I'm getting a week's worth of Cruiserweight Title shots against Tempest." Mufanga frowned, clearly struggling to recall the message, "Mostly I think he wanted to let me know that I'm still part of the team... just to clear the air, I guess... You know what the guys are saying, about the rookies losing the show for everyone." "Yes, how is Chris doing?" John picked the simplest thread to distract the wrestler. It steered away from the topic that he didn't wish to discuss just yet. "Fine," Mufanga said cheerily. A little too cheerily, forcing a smile to his face, straightening his shoulders. Removing his glasses, Rider merely held them dangling from his hand by one arm as he haphazardly skimmed the wrestling magazines spread out before him, waiting for the Wildman to continue. "Well, as fine as he could be I guess. Nobody is really talking... well, not to me, anyway. Everyone is pretty pissed off about losing the contract. You know what this means, John. Less people to watch the shows, less people to buy the merchandise, and less people who give a shit whether you live or die." Blinking in an effort to make sense of Mufanga's rambling, John took a moment before answering. "There will still be fans." "Fans? Bullshit. I'm back where I started. Just like I was back in the UWA, travelling the roads, and wrestling in Bingo halls again. I made it to the show, Rider, and I blew it. No, I didn't just blow it, I crashed the whole goddamn federation. Everyone in the fWo is looking at the rookies to blame... and..." "It wasn't the rookies, Mufanga. Ben? Ben... It wasn't the rookies, it was the Chronos stunt that made FOX shitcan the show." Mufanga indicated the nearby wrestling magazines with a wave of his hand. "Thats not what these guys are saying. Look at this sheet. The News. This writer, Damon Trask, is saying that the fWo shouldn't have hired me in the first place." Mufanga opened the magazine hastily, flipping through the pages. "Here it is... the fWo, in it's haste, brought in a group of talent that was not ready for TV, was not accustomed to wrestling for a television audience, and above all else, simply lacked heat. See, John? The wrestlers read these dirtsheets. Even the goddamn technical staff, most of the poor bastards fired last week, even they read this stuff. They are looking for people to blame, and they are gonna be looking for heads to roll." "Mufanga," Rider lowered his head, twisting his glasses in his hands. "Mufanga, nobody reads that filth. It's a well known fact that it's mostly lies anyway. Right?" "I guess."
