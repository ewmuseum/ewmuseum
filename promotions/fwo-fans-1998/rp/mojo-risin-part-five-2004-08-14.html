Mojo Risin' Chapter Five: Light My Fire "You've got her all wrong, Ivy," insisted Eli, as he rearranged their meager campfire for the fourth time, "Angel was always that fierce... she just didn't have any confidence in herself." Wood for the fire wasn't the problem - there was plenty of dried out trees all around. But with a half- dead butane lighter as the only source of flame, the two drifters realized they would have to conserve - they would probably only have one shot at getting a fire going. And they'd need to conserve for the second night of wandering... if it happened. "I dunno about that," replied Ivy, as she stretched out her sore legs, "she barely spoke the first time we met." "You were ten seconds out of the ring in full Psychobitch mode," reminded Eli, "That's enough to scare anyone. Besides, hey! The CSWA lockerroom is pretty overpowering when you don't know anyone. Trust me, Ivy... she hasn't found her convictions in the past three years, she's found faith in 'em." "Interesting," replied Ivy as she looked over the fire, "Not bad, not bad... how we gonna light it?" Eli held up his index finger as he reached into his bag, withdrawing the box he'd carried since Hollywood. He handed it to Ivy and stepped back. "It seems a waste, but it is flammable. And there'll be plenty left to enjoy." Ivy opened the box, to reveal a bottle of Remy Martin brand cognac. "This was Daddy's favorite kind," she whispered, "Thank you, Eli." "Happy thirtieth, kiddo," replied Eli, as he hugged her, "it's just a shame we've gotta use a bit to start the fire." She arched an eyebrow and took the lighter from Eli's other hand. In one deft move, she took a long drag from the bottle and lit the lighter. In the immortal words (mostly) of Craig Miles, COGNAC + FIRE = DRAGONFIRE~! and the dry wood crackled and popped as the pile caught. Ivy swallowed the remainder of the mouthful and passed it off. She leaned back and looked up at the cloudless sky, full of stars. "Jeez, it's beautiful out here," she mused, "If it wasn't for the whole 'stranded' thing, I'd say we should make a vacation outta it." "We'll have to contact Mikey and set up reservations," replied Eli, "or something like that." Ivy laughed. "Seriously, back to the story. Story time!" Eli took a drink of the cognac. "Where was I on it?" "Angel was never as timid as she seemed..." "Right," said Eli, as he took another drink and passed the bottle back, "Anyways, we talked for hours that first night, just wandering up and down the strip. I told her about the CSWA and the CWL and the fact that the fWo was interested and about you and your family and Alicia and just about everything under the sun
