Unlea- what? What do you mean we're a few days late?

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Job Cave. For those of the uninitiated, the Job Cave was a large warehouse in downtown Beefville: City of the Annoyed, that had been abandoned for several years. Previously, it had been a gang hideaway, before Mega Job moved in. They have done nothing to actually clean the place up, as they're usually too busy with whatever the hell a trio of bungling superheroes are doing. The place is run down, dirty, disgusting, and other "d" words. It was as if a tornado had gone through the entire bloody place and left the walls and celing intact. The only things that really signified that anyone lived here were the refridgerator, the computer, the TV, and the Jobmobile. Many beings of various origins come through the Job Cave. Pringles the Puppy was the Offical Mega Job mascot, though he didn't do much aside from bark and piss on Janito's tea set. He was excessively and needlessly cute, and thus, he always had a home in the Job Cave. The Pillsbury Doughboy is said to live in Mega Job's fridge, and basically, he just got lots and lots of head from the Pillsbury Hookers. He never has to actually leave the fridge, and those that disturbed his peaceful head generally get their asses kicked. Oddjob was the trusty stepladder of Steve, and basically, he's the Mega Job equivalent of Almighty the Sledgehammer or Payback the Shovel. Then you had Ken War. It wasn't known if Ken War actually lived in the Job Cave or just parts of him did. Ken War was apparently cursed with a constantly reassembling body, as he continues to die and then come back a few moments/minutes/months later. Seeing as though he died a lot, he never learned, and thus, he pretty much talked like an Abusah. He was also undeservedly an IWO Hall of Famer, proving that anyone can be in the Hall of Fame of the IWO. Now that you've been properly introduced to everyone, let the mayhem commence. "Isn't this like a sign of the upcoming apocalypse?" Beef asked, as he and his two partners sat around in the Job Cave. "What, do you mean the fact that we are all in the delightful little fWo thing?" Janito inquired. "I mean, it is well known that we are in a slump." "Yeah, and once we get our new design, we'll be better than ever!" There was a pause. "ALREADY." Steve the Rambling Communist said. "What? What do you mean we already have a new design? And did we ever get that deal from NBC from those Pee Eye Dubya wankers?" Janito asked. There was a pause. "Haven't we done these jokes before?" Beef asked. "They seem all familiar and stuff." "No, that was Ken War." CRASH! Right on cue. Ken War was just tossed through the entrance doors of the Job Cave, a large steel pipe driven into his chest. Some guy on a bicycle screamed "CAPITALISTS ARE THE MEAT OF THE FUTURE" and laughed maniacally as he rode away from the scene of what would have been the crime, had the victim not been Ken War. Ken War waited a moment and then rose to his feet before pulling the pipe out of his chest. He looked at it before he tossed it aside. "ey realy hat it wen ey hav a holyer than thou chset!11" he said. He then walked away. Beef turned to Janito. Janito turned to Beef. "Isn't it sad that Ken War does our jokes better than we do?" Beef asked. "Nonsense. Oddjob says more jokes than all of us combined. After all, he is the bloody moneymaker in this crew, as well as the future World champion of this company." Janito responded. "So you say." This conversation would've continued, had it not been rudely interrupted by a Yugoslavian mob. "THERE HE IS!!!" "Oh, great. You pissed them off again, didn't you?" Beef asked, turning to Janito. They all ran, allowing the mob to lynch Ken War. As they raced away from the Yugoslavians, and as the scene slowly faded to black, we could hear Janito's retort. "I thought they were too bloody stoned to hear me~!"
