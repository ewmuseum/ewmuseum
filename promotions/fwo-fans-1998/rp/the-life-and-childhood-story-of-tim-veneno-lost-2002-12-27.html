The life and childhood Story of Tim Veneno: Lost

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was the next day of school and Kristen's boyfriend didn't know how to react, for he had just heard the news that she had cheated on him...........with his best friend. "HOW COULD YOU?" Yelled Kristen's boyfriend. "Dude, it wasn't my fault she tackled me and started making out with me, calm down Ben" We now knew that Kristen's boyfriends name was Ben. "Bullshit, I should have known that you would do something to me like this...." Ben exclaimed. Ben soon remembered the note that Tim dropped off on his desk the day before. "Tim....Tim was in on it too wasn't he?" Ben started putting the puzzle together, or so he thought. "Tim? Who's Tim? His friend.... his ex-friend didn't know who Tim was "You know the freak that you and I use to make fun of." "Ohhhhh him. HAHAHAHAH Nah he wasn't in on it." He started to laugh hoping it would release some of the tension between Ben and him "He knew though, he told me to keep her with me last night and all I did was laugh in his face and....and...........and" Ben couldn't believe it, for once in Tim's life someone was feeling bad for what they did to him. Of course Tim didn't know it. "Dude don't worry he's a freak no one likes him anyways. Not even the teachers. Just let it pass and lets go shoot some hoops or something." Ben shook his head no at his friend "No.............NO................NO!!" The no's got louder and louder with each one. As Ben got in his friends face. "You fucked up and now someone has to pay!" With that said Ben walked off fast into the distance.
