Emerge.

2 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EMERGE (e-murj) intr.v. e·merged, e·merg·ing, e·merg·es -To rise from or as if from immersion. -To come forth from obscurity. -To become evident. -To come into existence. All fit Amy's latest accomplishment like a glove. It was a goal she'd had and finally reached and, though it may not have been significant or even seemed like an accomplishment to many others, the people around her recognized her struggle to wriggle out of depression. ''Someone's in a good mood!'' Renee Williams was trying desperately to mirror her best friend's winning smile, winning mainly because it was a victory to draw it out. They were sitting across from one another at a small garden table in a dimly lit restaraunt, nursing tiny glasses of ice water while they awaited some sort of service but sitting there, having a conversation for the first time in a week and a half, it didn't seem like they'd notice or care if the waitress ever came. ''Damn right I am.'' Amy was still beaming from ear to pierced ear. ''Why shouldn't I be?'' It was true. She'd been shot almost two weeks before, but it hadn't been a wound of any significance and within another week and a half to two weeks, it'd be healed up enough to resume perfectly normal activity with no pain. The few days she'd spent in hospital had been like spending a weekend in a monastary, having so much time to reflect and look ahead to the future. Sure, the 'monks' had tried to stick needles in her wrists every few seconds and apparently needed to know exactly how fast her heart was beating, but that made little difference; A few shouts and they'd go away, leaving her nothing better to do than talk to Brandon and sleep. ''I dunno...you tell me?'' And while she was reflecting, a new light had shone into the lives of her closest friends in the form of Mariella Jade Flair. Amy'd spent hours with the baby on the Valerian's Garden 'tour bus' when she was released from hospital and followed Angel around for a few gigs, relishing every minute looking at the toothless little grin of the child that never seemed to fade. She truly was a literal bundle of joy. ''I've got absolutely nothing not to be happy about.'' The redhead's smile dimmed a bit for the sake of the twitching muscles of her face, overworked for the first time in a while. ''Brandon, the Rat Pack...little MJ...there's NOTHING but positivity right now. I get to go back to work this coming week, everything's going so well for the first time in so damn long. I can't believe it.'' ''You forgetting your kid there, Amy?'' She laughed at Aimz' excitment over Angel and Eli's newborn. ''Nooooo no no...Stephen's with Angel and the boys.'' Renee laughed again and rolled her eyes, ''Wow, you're mom's gonna love that you left her grandson with a bunch of industrial metal crazies...one named Sex, might I add.'' ''Hey - they're my kind of people.'' Amy growled, making a half-hearted attempt to defend herself. ''They're your kind of people too, don't forget that.'' ''Yeah...but they're usually not the kind of people four year olds should really be influenced by.'' ''Better than sitting around watching a 'pop princess' on MuchMusic or MTV.'' ''True. At least Steph' won't be prancing around in belly-tops any time soon.'' ''We hope.'' She nearly howled with laughter, holding a hand over her stomach mainly to lessen the pain. ''Hey...ever notice that 'pop princess' has the initials 'B' and 'S'?'' ''Holy shit...it's like her parents saw her dismal fate a mile away!'' ''She was born blonde, EVERYONE saw it a mile away.'' Renee quickly stroked a long, natural lock of blonde hair. ''Watch it!'' By then, they'd both broken into fits of laughter that made them the little pub's center of attention, people of all sorts peering from behind pillars to glance at them with an array of different expressions - some amused and laughing as well, then some not quite so pleased by the appearance of the two pierced, tattooed 'loudmouth' girls. ''Kinda funny how we can get off into tangents like that...'' Renee chuckled again. ''But seriously...something just popped to my head when I mentioned your mom: Does she know about you-know-who...'' She eyed the other tables in close proximity to them, knowing Amy didn't exactly want her relationship with Brandon Roche to be public knowledge for a while. ''...yet?'' ''Oddly...yes. And she loves the idea. The ONLY thing she said was not to let our differences come between us.'' ''That's..............weird.'' And it was; Bev Campbell rarely ever said something good about her daughter's relationships or life, for that matter. She'd known the sort of things Amy's been into in the past, but may have been loosening up lately because her daughter was growing out of it all and surrounding herself with the 'right' people as opposed to uncaring scum. Maybe it was because Amy was 20 now...there were a lot of 'maybe's but either way, there was an incredibly grateful redhead when it came down to the disappearance of nagging. Amy's smile was once again unrelenting, ''Nah...I think she sees me happy now. The Rat Pack...they're good people. She even got to meet Angel yesterday and LOVED her, called MJ my adopted niece too...it was nuts. You know, she doesn't really think differently of people 'like us'...she sees personalities within. Guess I'm sorta growing up to appreciate how she's cool like that, she doesn't judge people based on appearances.'' ''But your dad's a harder nut to crack...I remember when I got an hour-long lecture after I got my eyebrow done.'' ''Yeah, but he's just starting to put up with it...plus, I think with me being how I am and my little brother kinda growing into it, he's stopped comparing us to the older siblings and kind of accepts that we're still good kids, just look like freaks.'' She giggled, ''Besides, now that I'm away from all of those problems....the addictions...y'know, he's lightening up. He's fine with everything I'm doing...even being on the road so much.'' Renee smiled back once again, saying ''Yeah, but he's the one getting worked up over your boyfriends...even Br---this recent guy.'' ''He's supposed to.'' She laughed, ''It means he's still my dad.'' ''You gushy little freak.'' She poked fun at her friend's tendancy to be 'daddy's little girl' through anything and everything. ''Barbie.'' ''Little Orphan Annie!'' ''Die!'' Amy swatted her, recalling the constant comparisons when she'd been a kid. When their laughter died down, there was a momentary silence where both girls seemed to have the same train of thought. ''We can bullshit like this again...'' Renee had started, leaving Amy to finish with. ''I know, it's great.'' ''You're back to being Amy, you know.'' The redhead thought for a moment while Renee continued, speaking from behind the same perpetual smile they'd both had. ''You're just...you again. No personal struggles...just goofy little Ames.'' Amy carefully considered her life as she'd come out of the slump, her mind particularly set on Brandon, Eli, Ivy, Angel, Sean, Alex and her new niece. ''You know...there's no better medication than the company you keep.'' She'd patted her longtime friend on the shoulder as the waitress came. They exchanged a knowing smile...even Renee wanted to get to know these people better. It really was funny to think of how doctors could perscribe any chemical to 'help', but the real fix always ended up being wonderful human beings... Wonderful human beings who rarely ever realize just how wonderful they are.
