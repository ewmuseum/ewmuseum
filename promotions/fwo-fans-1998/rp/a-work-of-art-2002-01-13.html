A Work of Art

13 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Louvre, home of many of the World's finest works of art, saw many visitors every day. People from various nations passed by the Pyramid standing by the entrance to the grand museum. From casual fans to ardent admirers, the Louvre had something to offer everyone who stepped through its doors. Even fWo Internet Champions. "Check out the abs on Venus," Kodiak Vic Creed said to fWo Road Agent Tom Hassan. "No arms, perky chest, but decent enough abs for a chick." Vic nodded approvingly. "I'd do her." "I'm assuming you're talking about the actual woman and not the statue," said Tom as he followed Creed into the next room. "I know you've been on the road for awhile, but I didn't know that statues got a rise out of the Main Man." Vic turned as a tour group passed by, a number of group members snapping pictures of the fWo goliath. "That's one, bunky," warned Creed. "One?" Tom asked as Vic approached another statue. "Is that some kind of warning?" "No, it's the number of concussions you're going to get as soon as you get to the parking lot." Vic smacked the road agent across the back of the head. "I let you come with me because I don't speak French. Don't make me beat you so bad you forget English." Tom sighed heavily as Creed turned his attention back to the statue. Herakles At Rest read the plaque beside the sculpture. Creed shook his head. "You've gotta be kidding me. First, they spelled his name wrong. It's Hercules, not Herakles." Tom briefly thought about correcting him, but opted against it. "This guy is supposed to be some kind of god, right? Some big tough guy, holding apples in his hand. And look at that physique!" Creed scoffed. He pulled off his T-shirt and stood beside the statue, flexing his muscular upper torso. "I make gods look like goobers." "Vic, put your shirt on!" Tom whispered loudly, his face reddening. "You're going to get us thrown out!" Ignoring Tom's pleas, Vic balled up the shirt and tossed it at Hassan. "Keep it. I look better than most of the artwork in here," said Creed with a smirk on his face. "It's like the fWo. I'm the Internet Champion, and I put everyone there to shame. Physically, nobody matches up with me. I'm a man without equal." Tom had apparently had enough of the blowhard champion. "You may be champ, but you're not the World's Greatest Athlete." "You're kidding, right?" asked an incredulous KVC. "You're mentioning me in the same breath as that has-been Slugger? The only thing that guy qualifies as anymore is the World's Greatest Washup." Two men, wearing security uniforms, entered the room and began to approach the two men. Tom nodded towards the exit, but either Creed didn't notice or didn't care. He made no motion to move, continuing his rant. "Once upon a time, Scotty might have been something in the fWo. But that was then. This is now. Look at what I've done since I got here. I've beat everyone worth beating, practically owned the Hardcore Title, and am on-track to retire MY Internet strap. It's too bad Mitch isn't here to see me become the mega-star that I am."
