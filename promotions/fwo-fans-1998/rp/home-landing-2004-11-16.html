Newark Airport Terminal C Tuesday, November 16, 2004 4:42 PM "Delayed again. Goddamnit." Eli Flair paced the large 'Arrivals/Departures' vidscreen at Newark Airport, his gaze fixed on the incoming flight from London. It was supposed to be landing at 12:04 PM eastern time, but delays had pushed it back, systematically, to five o'clock. The King of Extreme paced nervously, his steadily longer black hair pulled back into a ponytail, and he was dressed in a plain black long sleeved T-shirt and black leather pants. This was essentially as dressed up as he got. "Stop pacing, damnit. Makin' me nervous." Ivy McGinnis, on the other hand, was just the same as always. Three-quarter sleeved shirt, business-esque skirt, black stockings and combat boots. "What d'you expect?" asked Eli, "You do know what's about to happen, right? You do know who's on the plane, right? Ivy rolled her eyes and returned her attention to her magazine. “I think the entire world knows who’s on that plane, Eli,” she replied, “and if they didn’t I think it’s pretty safe to guess.” Eli continued to pace. “Can’t help it, kiddo… it’s been ten months now. I’ve never been away from someone I love this long, let alone my other half.” At last, he sat down in the seat next to her. “What happens if she’s grown past me? What happens if my little girl doesn’t know me? What if—“ To the delight of all around, Ivy clamped a hand over Eli’s mouth as he continued to rant about his girls and his worries. “Eli. You’ve talked to Angel probably five times a week or more, right?” He couldn’t answer, muzzled as he was, but he nodded his assent. “So you know what’s on her mind,” continued Ivy, “and you know that she’s been keeping you fresh in the baby’s mind, right?” Nod. “So why are you worried? Fuck you!” She suddenly pulled her hand away, as Eli had apparently bitten her palm, and Ivy punched him sharply in the chest. “Ass.” “All kidding aside, where do we even fit in?” asked Eli, “When she left on this tour I was ready to tear down the wrestling business, one ring at a time. She was excited about getting out of the country for a bit and showing MJ the world. But she’s stopped talking to me about the tour and her writing at all. It’s all about the sights and the books she’s reading and the other bands she’s seen. I feel like she doesn’t want me to know what the Garden shows’ve been like.” Eli looked deep into Ivy’s eyes. “You were on tour with’em for what, the Japan dates and the Australia dates, right?” “Yeah.” “What was the vibe? How was she?” Ivy shrugged, and looked as if she struggled with the words. “She was just… Angel, y’know? Played the show like it was her last night alive, wrote shit while Mariella slept, was a caring and loving mom. I swear, that woman has extra energy stored in another brain or something.” “So she’s… okay?” “As okay as Angel gets. You know her, dude. What’s up?” He started to answer, but stopped. “Nothing.” “Bullshit.” “What?” “Something’s up with her.” Ivy laughed quietly. “So you’ll ask her about it in a little while.” He smiled. “True.” ”Continental Airlines flight 3587 from Heathrow Airport, now arriving.” Eli stood up sharply, with Ivy following suit. They listened intently at the announcement as they both remembered the flight number they were looking for. “When’s the last time you were abroad?” asked Ivy. “Year and a half without breakin’ a sweat,” replied Eli, “Why?” “Because I don’t remember how long it takes to get through customs either.” They looked at each other, and then down the ramp that led into the forbidden zone of airport security. “I doubt you could pull your ’I’m Ivy Fucking McGinnis’ spiel with these guys, huh?” Ivy shook her head. “Probably… not. He laughed. “It’s all right. How’d they do, anyways?” “How d’you mean?” “Financially,” replied Eli, “The reviews I read and the limited conversations I had with Angel were all good… but you’re their manager. You know the truth in black ‘n white terms. How do their futures look?” “Does it really matter?” “No,” admitted Eli after a moment’s hesitation, “but I also know that she’s got the goods, and I’d really hate for her to have to spend even more years scraping the bottom with all the talent in the world. Seen too many come ‘n go too fast.” Eli turned back toward her. “And so’ve you.” Ivy nodded silently. The people all around them pushed their way through toward the baggage claim on the other side of customs. “Don’t worry ‘bout them,” she noted, “They’ve got the skills, they’ve got the drive, and they’ve got the brilliant manager to back ‘em up.” They both looked out toward the sea of people, trying to see a familiar face. “She’s comin’ down with TJ, right?” “Exactly right,” replied Eli, “The rest of the band is headed home, she and Teej are gonna stop off here and load out, find a place to play in the city. Open – ended break for most’a the holidays, I think she said.” “Who knows with her,” said Ivy. They both continued to look. “I don’t see her.” “Neither do—“ Eli suddenly stopped talking, as he felt something against his leg. He looked down and saw a very small child, tugging at his pants. It was… She had… “I missed you, Daddy!” Gulp. “Mariella?” The little girl smiled. Eli picked her up and hugged her tightly. “Daddy missed you too, sweet pea!” “Sweet pea ain’t the only one,” said a voice from behind him. Eli turned around— — as Ivy deftly picked the little girl from Eli’s arms. Angel stood there, her bag over her shoulder, and a pair of purple sunglasses over her deep blue eyes. She stared at Eli for a long moment, both of them unsure of what to do in this situation. For just a moment, at least. He scooped her up tightly and kissed her, squeezing so hard that the tears escaped the corners of her eyes. “How’s tricks, Ivy?” asked TJ, as he sidled up next to the Psycho Bitch. The rest of the people moving through customs had luckily given these people a wide berth. “Not bad,” replied Ivy, as she held the nearly – two year old MJ in her arms, “I think we need to give these two a moment.” “Yeah,” said TJ, “This is the first time in two days that Angel doesn’t have that nervous twitch in her leg. “Listen,” said Ivy into Eli’s ear, “We’re gonna go get the bags. I’m gonna bring MJ with me, give you and the little lady a moment alone.” She looked around at the people who continued to walk past the couple. “Such as it is.” TJ laughed quietly. “Want to see the conveyor belt, Miss MJ?” MJ giggled and nodded, as TJ took his surrogate niece from Ivy’s arms. Ivy laughed as well, and turned tail to follow the bass player down the hallway. “This is what I’ve missed,” whispered Eli into Angel’s ear, “My Angel…” He gently put her feet back down on the ground, and the two backed away slightly. Angel took both his hands and kissed them. “Honey,” she said through a slightly off voice, “I’m home.” A kiss on the lips. “I know,” replied Eli, as he put an arm around her and they headed toward the baggage claim, “we’re home.”
