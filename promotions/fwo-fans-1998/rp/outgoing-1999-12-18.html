Outgoing.

18 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Frenchie sits in the lobby of a posh hotel. It's late, and the room is near vacant. The Frenchie, in street clothes, keeps his chin down, using his beret to block the camera's view of his face. He has a cell phone pressed against his ear, and is speaking into it, his free hand very animated as he does so.) So it's a lock zen, James? Myself and Ice at Slaughter? Perfect....No no, I understand, ze title is not on ze line. I'll get my shot at Twilight, non? Besides, I want to show roid-boy a t'ing or two after Warfare. It's idiotic enough to dive off ze top of a cage in a World Title match, but ze sheer stupidity of zat pales in comparison to costing me a victory against ze Strangler. Christ, I didn't run around for ze last year telling everyone who'd listen zat I pinned him cleanly just to lose to him because zose Foundation wannabes are jealous.....no no, I wasn't talking about Slugger (Frenchie lowers his voice) not around you, anyway. Okay, merci beaucoup James. About Twilight, t'ough.......no, zat's fine, James. Ice, Slugger, it doesn't matter, really. You could toss Waru, or hell, even Universal in zat match, and it'd still sell, so long as I'm locked in ze main. I just got off ze phone wit' Brian, and he's, well, not concerned, but a little hurt over ze stipulations for his match.....No, of course he doesn't need our help, he's a damned capable wrestler, I wouldn't associate wit' him ot'erwise. It's just, you now, moral support.....I see, so zere's no way to drop ze "Foundation Banned From Ringside" stip....No, I don't t'ink ze Strangler would agree to it, and to be honest, I wouldn't look forward to asking him...Alright, so it's completely out of ze question, zen? What about getting ringside seats for Chris and I....I know you have zem, James, we always do, just for zis sort of situation. C'mon, no holding back ze comps, mon ami.....You what? Gave zem to ze reporter? What ze hell does she need ringside seats for? Oh...well, when you put it zat way....Could we talk her into assisting...no zat would never work, forget I asked....No, no problem, just trying to watch out for my boys, you know how it is. Yeah, you too James. I'll try to catch you on Sunday, zen. Okay, bye. (Frenchie hits power on the phone, ending the call, then turns it back on, hitting a speed dial combo.) Yeah, Brian? I hate to be ze bearer of bad news.... (The conversation continues in private, as the scene fades to black.)
