In the Garden Of Good and Evil / After Mourning (Beg.)

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The voice was a soft whisper...trailing through the thin air, slithering toward my ear. "Steeeve..." It coaxed me, made me curious. It wanted me to know it was there. Oh, and I knew all right, as the day it first came to me. I knew the voice would always be there. "Steve... are you sleeping?" I lay in silence hoping it will stop, hoping it will go away. But it knows, it knows that I lay awake. And it laughs. It laughs at me. "Stevey ol' boy, come out and play. C'mon Steve, wake up." "Go away," I mutter as if there is a slight chance that will work, however, it only excites it. "Ah, so the great man as risen from his deep slumber. Perhaps he will grace me with his presence and lend me his ear." I know the voice and I know what it wants, but I will not give in, not now, "Leave me Kenny." "You know I cannot do that Steve." "Why?" I ask as if I didn't already know the answer. "Because Steve, you are responsible. It's your fault that I'm here. You made me like this." "I MADE YOU INTO NOTHING!" "Exxxxxxxactly Stevarino! A big fat nothing, that's all I am now. Because you destroyed everything in which I was before." "I didn't pull the trigger Kenny." "No...I suppose your right Steve. Mr. Perfect Steve Douglas sat there helplessly while Kenny blew his fucking brains out!!! But go ahead Steve believe that you were the innocent one...believe it! Because we all know the truth." "The truth Kenny? Who are you kidding?" "No Steve, the question is, who are you kidding? Don't try to fool me you perverted little fuck! I know the thoughts that twirl in that twisted mind of yours. I'm in there now Steve, listening to all your demented fantasies, listening to your thoughts of death and torture. And I embrace them Steve, I embrace your mind, because Steve...you...you... truly are a piece of work. If only people knew what really went on behind that thick skull of yours." I close my eyes and pretend to sleep again, still listening to the voice talk. I would continue to listen second by second minute by minute hour by hour day by day month by month year by FUCKing year decade by FUCKIng decade century by FUCKINg century millienium by FUCKING...
