There's A Story to This Moral - Part 2

7 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"What was...that!?" A Mighty Hero had turned to a lost girl with a question after an awfully odd situation... In all honesty, she had absolutely no clue. Of course, she'd brushed the peck off as a simple, friendly 'Thank you'...but the boulder she felt she had in her throat said something much different, and much more confusing. Things had continued on normally, though. The pair began the usually physical training with one another, going over locks, holds, flips and anything under the rainbow...but the pauses between each move were laden with awkward looks, fidgeting, you name it. Neither were entirely comfortable anymore, and it almost seemed like Brandon was extra careful not to hurt her, shielding her fall with a part of himself each time he'd charge her. Now the mood was just a little different. It was a later in the afternoon, and things were picking up... Amy looked down to Brandon. Inches apart, time seemed to freeze as a trickle of perspiration ran down the side of her cheek and tumbled haphazardly to strike at Brandon's jaw. It mingled with his own and slid further down his chiseled chin as they looked into each other's eyes. Brandon's breath was heavy, in through the nose and out through the mouth. He tried to concentrate on his breathing to bring him back to center. He needed to get himself under control. Since earlier and the initial kiss on the neck, Brandon's thoughts had wandered. While they trained he had found himself a million miles away, trying to explain the feelings that were at the pit of his stomach. He had even had to cushion more than one blow, taking the brunt of it himself, as he was lost in himself. He had a wife for Christ's sake. What was he doing? He had a family that he had given everything to. What was he feeling? Now with things having gotten so far out of control he had taken another misstep in what seemed like an endless procession of them for the day. Amy lay lightly on top of him. He could feel her heavy erratic breathing against his cheek. With as over heated as he had become it actually felt good. As he looked into those big eyes Brandon found himself unable to speak. The ability had left him and as he swallowed it felt as if sandpaper scratched at his throat. He slowly licked his lips in a futile attempt to satiate his dry mouth. He tasted the salt on his lips; an after effect of the physicality Amy and he had been sharing in. The 'mighty paragon' absently reached up and stroked a stray hair from the corner of Amy's mouth. He watched on almost in shock as his body seemed to act on it's own. Amy moved slightly as if resting her head on his had and Brandon returned to his prone position, now almost afraid to move. 'Hold the hell on,' Aimz had put a stop to every thought crossing her mind, every little option or road the situation could lead down. She had to scold herself for once, and push hard against every instinct that tugged at her in doing so. 'He's got a wife and kid...you're not fucking that up for him.' The temptation was there, she'd finally realized...but it wasn't enough to break what each of them had 'outside' of one another. He had Ana, she had Darcy...this man was married, for Christ's sake. Quickly, Amy found a way to make the awkward position do a 180, a mischievous grin perking a corner of her lips while she chuckled, "Big, bad superhero boy taken down by a girl...let Sandra Peake print that." The scene now shifted back to full speed with Amy's comment. It was like a slap to the face and a bucket of ice water at the same time, exactly what Brandon needed. If he had followed through on anything where would he be? His consciousness now pulled back inside his body as the rest of the area came back in focus. Amy propped herself up on his stomach and sat upright. He had let her take him down hard and it seemed as if the feisty fiery sprite wasn't going to let him forget it. He grinned slyly back as she finished her upward movement and sprang to her feet. With an extended hand and a heavy heave he was vertical again as well. He still absently and lightly rubbed the back of her neck as they reached jointly for their towels. The arena had long since emptied. The stage had been set and Wednesday Warfare would come the next day. Their training had been eventful to say the least and as Brandon shook his head and wiped his face with his tan towel, Amy once again broke the silence by once again raising the tension. "So what's up with your wife?" Her tone was matter-of-fact. She wasn't 'kidding around' as usual and didn't even don the goofy little grin she'd shown Brandon a lot lately. He noted that Amy still didn't call her by name, instead referring to her in a less personal light by using a title. "Nothing, I guess. This business gets to her some times. Its been a real hassle lately as well. She was almost happy when the fWo went on hiatus. It let us be a... family again." Brandon felt uncomfortable as he spoke those words. Was that guilt he was feeling, or remorse for having those most forbidden thoughts and then speaking about his family? "She used to be excited about the business. Hell, she was even the 'Earthborn Angel' in the EWA, competed in a few mixed tag-team matches and the like as well." Brandon cocked his head to the side a bit. He seemed to brush an errant thought away as he continued... "Around the time of the whole Vic Creed fiasco everything changed though. Her love for the business, everything..." "He kidnapped her, right?" Amy had perched on a turnbuckle, cocking her head at Brandon with her next question. "What do you mean 'everything' changed?" He had opened himself up that line of questioning and even as those words escaped Amy's lips Brandon could feel himself wince. Six years later and the wounds were still raw. It was an unwritten rule that you didn't talk about what really went down, it just didn't happen. If that was the case then why did he find himself actually answering? "She... He, Ana... Ana made some bad judgment calls and it took us a long time to work through those. In the end it kind of soured her to the whole wrestling thing. It put a strain on our marriage and over some time we actually separated for a while." Brandon shrugged his shoulders absently as he leaned against the ropes. "She used to go on the road with me half the month. A few days Sean and Jarod would even tag along. It made this damn life we lead bearable. Now it's like we're like everyone else. I see her once a month for an extended time and the kids come on random days to spend time with me." He let his voice trail off at that point. A look of discontent formed on his face. Amy knew she was growing a little too nosy, but tried to ignore and not look too deeply into the fact in hopes that Brandon would do the same. She questioned him further while absently pulling her hair back into elastic that had been wrapped around her wrist, letting a few chin-length strands from the front fall forward. "You love her?" "Yes." Brandon's response didn't come as quickly as he expected. It was heartfelt though. The thought of his Anastasia brought a smile to his face. They had been through many a rough time but no matter what they had survived. That was the reason he had been so torn on the tumultuous situation he had found himself in lately. "Good..." His response brought Amy to turn around, facing him again...this time with a slight grin. "...good," She repeated, as if saying it to herself, but then looked up at Brandon again with a quick and deliberate statement. "Never, ever let someone you don't love come into your life." The Paragon's eyes suddenly regained their warmth, as did the slight smirk that spread across his face. He cast the redhead a look that almost said 'I see more in your words than you think', and made an according response that seemed to surprise Amy, whose eyes remained locked with his. "And once you've let them in your life never, ever let them go." Bite the hand that feeds you... The discovery continues. [This has been an Aimz/ Brand Frontier collaboration.]
