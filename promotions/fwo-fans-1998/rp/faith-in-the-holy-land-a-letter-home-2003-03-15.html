Faith in the Holy Land - a letter home

15 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chad, I'm a bit lonely and thought I'd take some time to write you a quick letter. It's been awhile since I wrestled in the CSWA, but I thought I'd at least try to let you know what's happening with me, outside of what you see on fWo television. The fWo is going well. My body has held out pretty well under the increased pace and I've not had many problems with my MS. I'm sure you know I'm working with Padrona, a cruiserweight. I've never been known to do a good job with that style, but I think I'm learning. He's pretty green, and that's made it harder, but I think we're starting to gel in the ring and out. This should run through CyberSlam and then we'll see what happens. I've heard some inklings of where I'll go next, but I'll leave that for you to watch - gotta have some surprises left, huh? As you know, we're touring the middle east right now. I should be able to write about how amazing it is to walk where Jesus walked, to listen to the bustle on the streets that Peter preached, to ... buy a splinter of the cross? I have been to the Holy Land many times in my life. I have been to Disneyland a few times also. Sometimes, I wonder if they're not all that separated. Sure, we need a space mountain or "it's a small world" ride to get the full affect, but in so many ways, it's identical. One panders to our desire to be entertained. The other panders to our desire to be closer to a God that many times seems so far away. But if God's in this land, He's certainly not in these trinkets that is sold to 'pilgrims'. In reality, I know God's in this land, as He's in every land... and I've visited quite a few of them since signing with the fWo. He is always working in lives, teaching us, reaching for us, loving us into an opportunity where we will come to fully understand what He did for us... in this land so many years ago. It's just... in this land, where so much of our biblical history happened, it is so far from the ideas that were taught. And that is true for Christianity or Judaism. I saw a young boy while I rambled around the city today. He was amazed by my size, and it's fairly commonplace to get mobbed when I go into the streets. I was amazed by the boys energy in spite of obviously not being well-fed. That's the toughest part of this, we've visited so many lands where so many people are struggling not to pay for their cable tv but for their very lives. And I know I have so much... and I know that there is nothing I can do... and I wonder where God is in all this mess? I know better, wiser men than myself has asked the same question, and I know the answer... it's just sometimes the questions scream a whole lot louder than the whispering answer. Well, I've rambled enough for one day. Thank Chris next time he calls you. He went over this and fixed all my grammatical mistakes... I knew I paid him for something! As always, you'll be in my prayers. Faith is the Evidence, Elijah
