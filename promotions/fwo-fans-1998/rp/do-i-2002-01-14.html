Do I?

14 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The much-recognized riffs of Joe Strummer can only mean the Clash. And these particular riffs can only mean "London Calling". A majestic shot of the most recognizable symbol of London, Big Ben. Tourists crowd around it, flashbulbs going off in numerous directions as they take shots of the enormous face of the clock. Ruben Ross wasn't one of them. Protected from the cold in the personalized Flames jersey he got from Christmas and adidas sweats, he was busy glaring at a laptop. Ross (muttering): That fuckhead's #1...what the gay is this?! WHAT THE GAY UNHOLY HELL IS THIS?! Is that thing taping me? Leno rule. Right. Gimme a five-count. I am suddenly in the mood for talking. Ross gets his five-count. You know, Mark Twain once said a lie can make it halfway around the world before the truth can even get it's pants on. Exhibit A: Ross shows the camera the current fWo splash page. Will Ross accept Creed's challenge? (mockingly) Will Ross accept Creed's challenge? Am I a little-dicked, pill-popping, "Oz"-reject, andro-drinking, Chewbacca-raping fuckhole? No. Am I a three-moved wonder who has to hide behind his two special friends just to beat BQ? No. So, you know what, it's on. See, I already took my biggest challenge out of the way from the start. Scott Slugger, and I pinned him cleaner than a nun's snatch. And comparing KFC to the WGA is no contest. Creed couldn't carry Slugger's jock with a forklift and five more bottles of Super Power 5000 or whatever he's on this hour. He gets a pin over me in which the legality of the situation's in question and I've already gotten clocked by Davis' new thing. So, he lays me out and THEN challenges me? Says a lot, don't it? I mean, I know it's not insecurity from not being the first Survivor. Or becoming a champion first. Or being the longest reigning Internet Champion ever. Or getting pinned and losing the 2001 Wrestler of the Year. Or wrestling the Scott Sluggers and defending the World Title I got to first while you claw and scrap just to barely beat BQ. Creed sees in me an image, an ideal, of what he'd like to be. Don't let his whole Road Warrior Hawk 2002 promos scare you--well, let them--but he wishes HE was the one with the adulation of millions, the big wins when it counts, the name in the record books. Instead, he's Roid Monkey. Oooh, look at me! I can rip off moves from Japan! Oooh, look at me attempt to be that thumb-pointing guy! I am big and strong and powerful and nothing will stop me and everyone should be afraid! Just don't put a pin by me, otherwise I'll deflate to my natural weight of 173 pounds. I'm putting up my gold. He's not putting up my old gold. It's fine by me. See, he got in some wins here & there through some cheating, luck & outside help--assisstants, if you will. Every time I've beaten Creed, it's been clean. 2001 Wrestler of the Year? Black Sabbath. The last time he got pinned for the title? Another Black Sabbath. Used the strap, whatever. But Sunday, I shut his sorry ass up for good. See, no matter how cleanly I win, he's always had an excuse. "Oh, he only won because of how I tossed him around." "Oh, he used a chair." "Oh, there were three other guys." "Oh, I got distracted by my mom getting double teamed for food stamps." But now? I don't care if I don't get both belts. Pinning him in the middle of the ring if I decide not to make him tap will shut his fat yap when it comes to me permanently. And then, when he's done lying down like the dog he is, maybe he can go back to his level and job to Heftel. 'Cause he's not on my level, and as far as I can see, it'll be a while before anybody else gets up here. Come Sunday, Victoria, there's two things you can do about that: nothing and like it, 'CAUSE I'M THE BLACK PLAGUE AND I'M OUTTA HERE LIKE I'M WORLD CHAMPION! We hear the final seconds of "London Calling"... I've never felt so much alive, alive, alive, alive...
