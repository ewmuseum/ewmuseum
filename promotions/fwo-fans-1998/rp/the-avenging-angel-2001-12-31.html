The Avenging Angel?

31 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When he was younger, Ruben Ross had nearly driven his mother insane during his terrible twos. Usually he could be pacified by the television or a Popsicle, but on those few times when he was uncontrollable he brought an almost possessed rage. Aquariums shattered. TVs were pushed over and hit with a bat. The punishment would fit the crime and he couldn't have cared less. He unleashed it. It was done. When he was younger, Ruben Ross was one of the smallest children in his grade. To compensate, he made up for it with jokes and usually above-average brains. This usally worked in his favor, and it worked all six grades except in fourth when a child had foolishly called him a nigger. Ruben merely took the comment in stride before landing a well-placed kick in the nuts and beating the child from one end of the playground to the other, culminating in a piledriver on a rock that knocked him out cold. He was suspended for two weeks. It was a walk. His parents were proud he'd stood up for himself but didn't exactly want to condone such crazy violence in the future. "Probably got it from that wrestling," they muttered under their breath. And when he came back, no one dared fuck with him. He'd lost pretty clean to the Strangler. Turnbuckle aside, Paul had taken a pretty basic move and captured the title with it. He knew he shouldn't have looked past him to Chronos, but he couldn't help it. And then Neo got in his face. Boy this. Massa that. And part of him remembered how he'd solved this problem before. His fist shot out by instinct and it was a grave disappointment when Neo's eyes crossed on the way to the concrete. Interrupting Davis during a promo wouldn't do him any good. He picked his spot, the way he'd planned all night. He waited for others and then made sure to make his count. Chronos was only champion by his hand. It was a reign that wouldn't see 2002. He bundled up in his Niners jacket and walked out to his rental car. He had a long drive to Los Angeles and wanted to do it while this much adrenaline was still in him. "It took a lot to piss him off," he remembered someone saying once--fifth grade teacher?-- "but once he got angry, Christ Almighty." Ross fired up the car with a quick start, chuckling at the memory, thinking of a very bright future to come.
