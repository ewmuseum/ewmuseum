The Freedom to Execute

8 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The pungent odor of urine, feces, sweat and blood filled his nose, The exhausting heat of a humid environment pressed against his crimson-gladden skin, The darkness that enveloped sent twitches of fear and uneasiness throughout his exhausted body, And the sense of helplessness tightened across his very id, as the ropes rubbed violently across his joints, Krow wondered aloud… ”Is this how death is supposed to feel?” And someone replied… “Sucks, don’t it?” questioned Shadows with a sadistic snicker. Suddenly, a jolt of electricity was sent through the Satanic God’s body. He tried to scream, his mouth agape, but nothing came forth. The pain left him in a momentary state of shock, his muscles tensed and he screamed in his mind, but his body was forced into a sporadic and forced stillness. And suddenly, just as quickly as the electricity was sent through his chest, it was removed, and seconds later the angry scream finally came. “You feel that, you son of a bitch?” Steven Shadows asked roughly as he tossed the jumper cables aside and slammed his hands on Krow’s forearms. “That isn’t the feeling of death. But it is the feeling of wishing for it. It’s the feeling of helplessness, the feeling that your life rests in another person’s hands. You should know this feeling well. It’s the same fucking feeling you so graciously gave to me.” Saliva dripped from Shadows’ insane grin, as he tilted his head and gripped tighter onto Krow’s dirt and blood infested forearms. He stared angrily into the blindfolded eyes of Krow, anxiously awaiting any sign of life…so that he could have the pleasure of briefly removing it. He tensed his grip over Krow, displaying the amount of power he had over his sworn adversary…but once again, that same sinister and clandestine grin returned to Krow’s face. As blood crept from the corners of his mouth, and warm sweat dripped down from his haggard face, the son of a bitch was still smiling. Shadows curled a lip of disgust and didn’t hesitate to sent a hammering fist across that fucking schmuck’s face. “Ain’t so funny now, is it?” Shadows angrily shouted. Unfortunately, though it grew bloodier, Krow’s grin it did not disappear. In fact, it grew wider with an accompanying chuckle. “Actually,” Krow said as he spat the remnants of his flesh onto the floor below, “It’s hilarious.” Shadows’ lips quivered with an angry frustration as the arrogant words of Krow stung through his ears. With every chuckle that expelled from his bloody lips came an animalistic desire to tear and shred the man’s face like a Serengeti Lion. He angrily shoved Krow, and the chair he was tied to, against the wall and snatched up a nearby pipe. Grunting like a wild boar, GV grabbed Krow across his throat and pressed his rough fingers into the two jutting nerves on his neck. Nerves began to explode across Krow’s face, and a dry cough expelled from his exasperated lungs. But despite the life-threatening situation, his grin remained clear. Shadows’ eyes widened in rage as he lifted the pipe over his head like a samurai warrior ready to behead his fallen foe. But as he dried fingers tightened across the cold steel, an explosion of debris fired from behind him. Before Shadows could turn to face the culprit of the interruption, he felt about 400 plus pounds of man throw him violently into the feces-infested ground. His face smacked directly into a mound of human waste, a pleasant mixture of “#1” and “#2”, and he could hear Krow’s amused chuckle. The culprits shouted and screamed for Shadows to “stop resisting” and to “stay the fuck down”. Of course, Shadows did not comply. With every bit of power in his body, the FWO’s resident psychopath began to attack his unknown assailants, but with each strike he connected with, another came careening into his face two-fold. It didn’t take long for the sting of each heavy strike to take it’s toll and leave Shadows into a state of unconsciousness. But as Shadows’ eyes grew weary and his head grew heavy, and as he felt cold metal handcuffs being latched onto his wrists, he peered up towards the amused chuckle of his long-haired adversary. He sat upon his wooden throne, his long black haired parted over his face, rested gently onto his ears. His eyes, no longer bound by the darkness of his blindfold, stared amusingly at the fallen Graphic Violence. And his hands, shockingly no longer bound, waved playfully in front of him. Shadows’ went into a state of shock; for weeks he had tortured this mother fucker with everything at his disposal. He shocked the man to the point of near death, rattled his skull with various ‘foreign objects’, and tore pieces of flesh from his body at will; and throughout all of his torturous endeavors. ”I was never helpless, Shadows,” he whispered slyly as he rubbed his reddened wrists and waved his fingers playfully at Shadows. ”You fucking mother fucker,” Shadows wanted to reply as he shot a stare of vengeance towards Krow. “I’ll fucking kill you. I swear to God, I’ll fucking kill you.” New York City Precinct April 8th, 2009 9:56 A.M. “Still want to kill me?” 21 Days. It had been 21 Days of physical and mental torture. 21 Days of nonstop agony and despair dictated by the very man he had bailed out of prison. 21 Days…of an excellent mind game. Krow smiled sinisterly as he stood before a seated and handcuffed Steven Shadows. Shadows painted a picture of fatigue; his eyes, though stinging with anger, hung low with rings of exhaustion and his face, though normally white, was painted with a dull paleness; the after effects of sleep deprivation. Shadows eyes froze onto Krow’s, and while the precinct was a buzz with the daily rituals and activities of official duties, to them it was as if the outside world had frozen still, leaving only their stare to remain. Krow looked deep within Shadows’ stare and smiled wickedly at his handiwork. For months, Krow had tortured Shadows in every imaginable way; he had taken every bit of what Shadows held close to his heart and tore it away from him. Before 2009, Shadows was revered as one of the saviors of the United States. Before 2009, Shadows was considered one of the most brutal and devastating of individuals. Before 2009, Shadows was somewhat sane. And when Shadows felt he had finally gained the upper hand…Krow took that satisfaction away from him too. In Krow’s mind, he was staring at a long-defeated man. He took his eyes off of Shadows and looked around the precinct of New York City observantly. ”I hope they didn’t treat you too poorly, Shadows,” he quipped. ”What the fuck are you doing here?” Shadows quickly interjected, his eyes never leaving those of his foes. “Bailing you out, that’s what,” Krow calmly replied, running his hand through his hair, parting it from his face and allowing his fresh bruises to shine in the office lights. ”Are you fucking crazy?” Shadows rhetorically questioned. “Do you realize what I’ll do to you once I’m out of here?” Krow sighed deeply and shook his head, clicking his teeth in disappointment. ”I wouldn’t speak so loudly, Shadows,” Krow said as he took a seat next to Shadows and looked out towards the officers finalizing the paper work for his release. He leaned sideways towards Shadows and whispered slyly into his ear. “You’re not free just yet. Those doors are pretty far away.” Shadows curled a lip as he felt Krow’s breath press onto his face. Krow could feel the tension building in Shadows, and the uncomfortable closeness between two sworn enemies thickened the intent of physical brutality. ”Fuck freedom,” Shadows sat as he spat angrily at the floor beside Krow’s feet. “I’d give it up in a fucking heart beat just to beat you within an inch of your life. I gave it up once before to help the ungrateful fucks of this country, what makes you think I wouldn’t give it up for my own self gratification?” And quite suddenly, as if he were waiting for the perfect moment to present his case, a thin stack of neatly stapled legal papers were placed upon the handcuffed forearms of Steven Shadows. He peered down at the papers sharply, but quickly peeked at the corner of his eyes to Krow. Krow did not make eye contact, instead he simply looked towards his hands and calmly played with the cuffs of his black autumn coat. ”Because wouldn’t you like more than just five seconds against me?” Krow asked with a sly grin across his face. ”What’s this?” Shadows said, motioning with his head. ”That?” Krow asked rhetorically as he fixed the collar of his shirt. “That is the opportunity you passed by. You see, because of your rambunctious actions against me…we happened to miss one of the biggest shows in the FWO’s history; a night that will live in infamy according to them; a night where the overrated battle in the overly-hyped squared circle with the idiotic drones cheering them on. Shadows…” Krow rested his head against the wall behind him. ”We missed Cyberslam.” ”Why should I fucking care?” Shadows sharply asked. ”You shouldn’t,” Krow retorted calmly. “I know I don’t. However, the possibility of beating you within an inch of your life in front of millions has crossed my mind as something aesthetically enjoyable. And I find myself somewhat saddened that I can’t accomplish that feat any longer.” Krow chuckled lowly. ”Then again, I wouldn’t trade our 21-Day experience for anything in the world. Especially not the last 10 minutes.” The man known as Graphic Violence sharply took his handcuffed hands and presented them to Krow in the form of two jutted middle fingers. Krow smiled passionately at the expression of distaste. ”Nevertheless, we can’t simply have our long-fueled war end in a wimper; now can we? After all, I can’t simply allow you to escape the just punishments you deserve” Krow stated as he presented a pen to Shadows. “That stack of papers before you is a contract. On that contract, is the chance you always wanted; the chance for our conclusion. The chance for you and I to meet one on one in the ring. Every base is covered in this contract. The FWO, yourself, and I are not held liable for any injuries or…,” Krow sighed amusingly, “chances of death that could occur from this bout. The match will have no rules other than the manner in which it ends – pin fall, submission or knock out. And included in this contract is the final chapter to my personal mission of taking everything you hold dear…” ”The FWO Hardcore Championship.” ”It’s your claim to fame in professional wrestling, isn’t it? The first “big” title you’ve secured in your entire career that etches your name in the record books. It’s the last thing you still hold close, and, coincidentally, the only thing I haven’t taken from you thus far.” A lull befell over the two men as Shadows stared blankly at the pen offered by Krow. ”If you sign that piece of paper, Shadows, you will be a free man. Free to maim, kill and destroy every inch of my life. Free to do so legally with the written consent of the Fans Wrestling Organization. Essentially, Shadows, you could walk away from murder without any persecution. I’ll drop all the charges, I’ll pay your bail. All you have to do is show up for our final meeting.” Shadows did not verbally respond, he simply snatched the pen from his hand in a rather rough gesture and sign on the dotted line quickly. He grabbed the papers and roughly tossed them to Krow, who caught them with ease. Krow looked over the papers and signed his own name on the dotted line. His smile went wide as he stuffed the contract into the inner-pockets of his coat and stood up from his seat. He walked forward a few steps, and spoke over his shoulder towards Shadows. ”You’re a free man, Shadows. Live these final days to the fullest.” And as Krow walked towards the front desk and spoke to some of the local authorities, no doubt being questioned for his generosity, Shadows stared hard at his cocky adversary and mumbled the very words he swore before. ”I’ll fucking kill you. I swear to God…I’ll fucking kill you.”
