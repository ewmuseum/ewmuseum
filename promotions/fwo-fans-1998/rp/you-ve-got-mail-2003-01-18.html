You've got mail

18 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Borst sat in a swanky hotel room on his king size bed, surrounded by a sea of mail. Some opened, some not. A cigarette hung from his mouth, the falling ash occasionally making small burns on the sheets. Borst didn't notice, and wouldn't have been too bothered either. He was engrossed in this mountain of mail from the past year. It had all been forwarded to his current employers, the fWQuo, from his residence back in Manchester after he had finally been tracked down. "Another debt collection thing..." Borst muttered to himself as he screwed up a letter and threw it out of the window. "Another dead wife one..." Out the window. "Will reading..." Out the window. "Who died anyway?" he asked himself as he put out his cigarette and rummaged through the pile of letters to find his packet of B&H. He lit up another cigarette and continued to look through some letters, throwing them out of the window promptly when he'd read the header. He stopped on one letter and the cigarette dropped from his mouth. "Ohhhh yeah." He said. "Zoe died." He does pay attention, really. Borst did know she was dead, he remembered hearing something about it a while ago, but he'd just forgotten. He just kind of got wrapped up in his own wheeling and dealing that it had gone in one ear and out the other. Besides, his estranged wife wasn't exactly his favourite person. She went all gay. "She was a nice girl, sometimes." Borst said to himself, sighing and shaking his head. "Anyway." Back to the mail. And as if by magic, the next one he opened happened to be a catalogue. Nothing like a bit of retail therapy to cheer you up. Note to reader. Smoke was coming from somewhere. Most likely the spot his cigarette landed. Lets not tell Borst, for comedy value. Borst flicked through the pages of the catalogue and suddenly stopped on a page, a big grin appearing on his face. "Fuck yes!" He yelled, before grabbing the telephone and dialling a number. "Hello, I'd like to place an order. Erm, it's... MOB/RSA 5365." ... "Yeah, on page 23. The one with blonde hair. Yalenchka." ... "Yeah. Mega. How long for delivery?" ... "1 to 3 days, that's fucking great. Thanks very much." Upon placing his order, Borst noticed the smoke, and the burning feeling on his arse. He jumped up, dropping the phone, and turned to see the sheets of his bed quickly gathering flames, with all his mail from the past year soon starting to burn too. Upon rescuing his cigarettes, he grabbed the phone once more and dialled down to reception. "Hi, this is Mr Borst in room 101. There seems to be a problem with the heating... I'm gonna need a new room." For effect, Borst put down the phone nicely, then proceed to yank it out of it's socket and throw it out of the window. Then, he left.
