Chasing The Dream - Part 1

16 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 5th 1999 - 2:33PM The soft sputter of the delivery car had come to a halt, as the key was turned putting the engine the rest. The door was flung open, and Milo went about his duties as a pizza boy. A few knocks on the large oak door, and the Pizza was delivered. Milo had received the $12.50 to cover the cost of the pizza, but as usual received nothing in form of a tip. In fact, in the 6 months of working as a pizza delivery boy, Milo had received only two tips. The first being one dollar and seventy-two cents, while the second was a simple tip to "Stay off the grass". "Cheap prick" Milo muttered, as he walked back towards his car, counting the money over again in hopes of a previous miscount. Milo sat in the car momentarily, falling into deep thought. This was quickly broken up as he noticed an entire family huddled by a picture window, watching his every move. Because it was an upper class Las Angeles suburb, the family was probably under suspicion of Milo 'casing their house'. "Better go eat your pizza, its gonna get cold." Milo thought to himself, as he turned the engine and began driving back into the city. Sure, there were other deliveries that were supposed to be made in this neighborhood, but Milo knew it was time to do something new with him-self. Things had grown so stagnant, that he sometimes felt as if he was living the same redundant life, as Phil (a.k.a. Bill Murray) in the movie "Groundhog Day". The time to do something new had come, but it was no surprise to Milo. He knew it was only a matter of time... As luck would have it, Milo had pawned most every possession, with the exception of some clothing, a knapsack, and a cordless razor, which previously belonged to his father. His reason for doing so was to pay a rent bill, which was already 42days delinquent. Deep within Milo's chest, there sat a dream... A dream to one day, wrestle among the professional ranks. This may be difficult, due to the lack of training on his behalf. But this gave Milo an idea...a great idea. He would pursue this, and seek the necessary training to make his dream a reality. But where could he get the skills required to make it to the big time? Canada. Alberta, Canada was the home of many prominent wrestling schools, and that is exactly where he was headed. Milo, as quietly as possible, returned the pizza delivery car by the curb of the shop. He threw the keys in the dash, knowing the owners of the car had multiple sets of keys. He then set off, taking the $12.50, along with one of the undelivered pizzas that sat atop the back seat. Walking and eating, 12 slices and 30 minutes had brought Milo to the "hike road". A road, which was notorious for having legions of hitchhikers, at most any given time. Milo stood, thumb extended as several cars passed him by. It was only a few short minutes before he saw the crimson glow of brake lights, and a large "hippie-like" van pulled to the shoulder of the road. Running towards the van, Milo had to prevent himself from yelling out in delight. Not only had he been picked up, but in the van, sat five very attractive young women. "Who cares if they're hippies..." Milo told himself, as he watched the side door slide open. "Where you headed?" One of the girls asked, her smile stealing the eyes of an awestruck Milo Samus. "Um... as far away from LA. As you can get me." "Well, we have a gig in Carson City, and you are welcome to join us there if you'd like." She continued. Her voice sounded more heavenly with every uttered word. "I'd love to!" Milo climbed into the van, and took a seat in the back. Upon entering the van, he was struck with an aroma, which he hadn't smelled since College, the aroma of marijuana. This didn't bother Milo too much, or at least not to enough to mention it. He was getting a free ride to Carson City, and would have tolerated most any smell.
