Inner Turmoil, Part IV (Forty-Six And Two) Epilogue

6 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"My shadow's Shedding skin and I've been picking Scabs again. I'm down Digging through My old muscles Looking for a clue." Life had been so simple at one time for him. Ever since the end of 1996, he had felt his life had gotten back on track. Time had moved quickly, though friends had never stayed the same, he always had someone to talk to. And when he had no one to talk to, he would always find someone that was willing to listen. In a stark contrast, things had changed drastically. The first ever friend he had made, Monica Davidson, was back in his life. Finally under good terms. The simplicity he once had, turned complex in a matter of moments on December 22nd, 2001. Twilight changed him, for the better and for the worse. Monica Davidson was the better, It was the worse. Wake up! He shot straight out of his bed into an upright position. Sweat dripped off of his body. His eyes moved around the dark hotel room, a small sliver of light came in around the drapes that covered the window. That was it and he wished he could make it go away. "I've been crawling on my belly Clearing out what could have been. I've been wallowing in my own confused And insecure delusions For a piece to cross me over Or a word to guide me in. I wanna feel the changes coming down I wanna know what I've been hiding in." It turned out that he was hiding within himself the entire time. The demon that he had swallowed whole and kept down for nearly eight years had reawakened inside of him. There was nothing he could do, but let the change consume him. Eating away what he was on December 21st and before. Once it starts, resistance is futile, no matter how hard one tries. He was trying hard, but he was failing. As much as he didn't want to give in, it would be a matter of time before he did. "My shadow. Change is coming through my shadow. My shadow's shedding skin I've been picking My scabs again. I've been crawling on my belly Clearing out what could've been. I've been wallowing in my own chaotic And insecure delusions. I wanna feel the change consume me,Feel the outside turning in. I wanna feel the metamorphosis and Cleansing I've endured within" He reached up and felt his face, the scabs from Countdown almost a week ago were still covering his forehead. His fingers nervously dug into them, ripping the scabs off, causing blood to come to the surface of his skin. His body slumped back down into the bed. Never had he felt this bad about anything before. Through what little light there was in the room, he could see his resting shadow going off of the wall. The shadow seemed different than it used to. Almost alive, with a life of it's own. He couldn't quite figure it out. What's the matter can't sleep? His heart told him that if he didn't respond, that it would eventually go away. "My shadow Change is coming. Now is my time. Listen to my muscle memory. Contemplate what I've been clinging to. Forty-six and two ahead of me." He was still clinging to the past, it was making him hang on to what had been. And the shadow had come, just two weeks ago. It had taken him nearly over. Though a fan, or anybody else wouldn't be able to notice, he did notice. The actions he took against Eli Flair were completely different from what he was used to. A 180-turn of what he had been doing through the past three years of his short career. Never had he wanted to make a man bleed like he wanted to see Eli do. He found it sickening at first, but then started to like it, now he craved it. "I choose to live and to Grow, take and give and to Move, learn and love and to Cry, kill and die and to Be paranoid and to Lie, hate and fear and to Do what it takes to move through. I choose to live and to Lie, kill and give and to Die, learn and love and to Do what it takes to step through." He realized that he was slowly losing control of himself and his shadow was taking over. The basic choices of life were still in his grasp, but for how long he didn't know. Change was imminent, there was nothing to stop it. Now was the time to learn to live with it. Now was the time to give up and let it completely take over. There was nothing he could, but obey, listen and work. If he followed with it, he would become stronger, more violent. People may begin to hate him, but it was a chance that he would have to take. "See my shadow changing, Stretching up and over me. Soften this old armor. Hoping I can clear the way By stepping through my shadow, Coming out the other side. Step into the shadow. Forty six and two are just ahead of me." Slowly, he got out of bed, he saw the light peering between the drapes. He looked over towards his own shadow that rest against the wall. Donaven Winters stepped into the shadow. The End. (Lyrical Credit: Tool)
