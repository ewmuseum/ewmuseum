Just Dreaming .

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams." -Eleanor Roosevel Inside a Holiday Inn outside of San Jose, California one of the fWo, road agents expect to have a meeting with all three fWo personalities in the Triality. The only person to show up for the meeting was Seth Ragin, fWo personality Carson Covington. He opened the door to the make shift meeting room near the lobby of the hotel. There sat the annoyed road agent that sipped a now cold cup of coffee and stared at the clock impatiently. "Where is Troy and Tisha?" Seth thought they would not show up so he was quite blunt with his response. "They aren't exactly happy with the current situation we've all been put in. They won't be here today!" Seth sat down after taking off his leather jacket and placing it on the back of his chair. He placed his hands on the table and began tapping his fingers against the mahogany wood in a musical hem. After a few minutes Seth decided he should break the silence, "What did I come out here for?" The road agent looked at his watch, "Well I wanted to talk to all three of you about the direction in which we think would suit the tag team Triality best but seeing as how Troy and Tisha are incapable of coming to a simple meeting... we may have to rethink all this." Seth's face got red, he was not going to allow his dream to be shattered because two other people were upset. "Give me a few minutes, I'll call them on my cell and get them out here in the next 30 minutes," the road agent nodded. "We need to talk about other things dealing with my singles career in the fWo." Seth quickly got up out of the chair and exited the small, dimly lit room. He rubbed his eyes as he pulled his cell phone off his belt. He dialed in the number to the hotel. "Room 336." "Troy Ikard." He waited as the hotel operator put him on hold. "Hello?" "Troy, you and Tisha have to get down here, this guy isn't to happy that you two no showed." You could hear Troy hushing Tisha down in the background. "Okay okay, so where exactly are you at?" Seth looked at his watch quickly, "I'm at the Holiday Inn on I-67 on the outside of San Jose. It's about 20 minutes away from where you are..." "You need to get here in the next 25 minutes or he's leaving... and we'll be leaving as well," there was a brief moment of silence before Seth gave the final blow, "To Orlando!"
