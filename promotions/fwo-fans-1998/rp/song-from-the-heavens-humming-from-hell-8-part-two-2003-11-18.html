Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 8 - Part Two

18 November 2003

The only difference between saints and sinners is that every saint has a past, while every sinner has a future." -Oscar Wilde "Most of the evils of life arise from man's being unable to sit still in a room." - Blaise Pascal *** The lamp threw long shadows on the off white walls. A.D. sat up right in bed, reading. He hadn't slept more than fifteen minutes since Fight Hell, and had begun to feel as if he would never sleep again. He had purchased the book earlier in the day from a thrift store on the corner. It was missing it's cover, and a few of it's pages, but he managed to keep up with the plot. It was about vampires coming to a small town in Maine and turning all of it's residents to vampires themselves. A soft russling in the closet drew his attention away from the book. Your time is almost at hand, Angel, came a voice from behind the closet door. "Leave me alone", A.D. said. You know I can't do that, Angel. We're all bound together forever. You, Me, Steve, Noah, Villam, Sean, Joe. We've been bound together from the dawn of time. Together, in this crazy mess. "What is going on?" Don't bother, Angel. You can't fight it. This has happened before, and it will happen again. And again and again and again. Long after the sun burns out. Long after every living being on this Earth has died off. It is a circle, Angel. And, the circle always closes. A.D. sat in silence, staring at the closet door. The closet door that was now open slightly. That feeling you've always had, Angel. You know which one I'm talking about. That feeling that something was missing. That you were waiting for something to happen. That you were destined for something more. You were right. By all accounts, you should not have even been born, but you were. And, even after you were born, you should not have pulled through, but you did. You did because It needs you, so It let you live. There was a time when I even doubted It's existance, but I've have learned a great many things since then. "Why are you telling me all this?" Because your time has almost come "How will I know went it's my time?" You'll know when you know Again, A.D. stared into the now almost completly open closet. "Is that it, then? Am I just supposed to wait for something to happen?" That's what everyone's doing, Angel There a rush of cold air as the shadow from the closet brushed A.D.'s face, and out the open window into the warm night air. As it touched his face, A.D. saw in his mind's eye the shadows true form. It had long, shaggy blond hair, bright eyes that glowed with dying blue embers, and it carried with it a sledge hammer. Remember Angel, in order to accept life, , the blue embers changed to a sudden blood red , You have to accept DEATH- *** Angel Dalton sat up in his seat, he was still on the plane to South Korea, it seemed he was dreaming. Uncomfortable, Dalton took a sip of the drink he had left at his seat, he looked out the window and tried to shake the image of Kenny from his mind.
