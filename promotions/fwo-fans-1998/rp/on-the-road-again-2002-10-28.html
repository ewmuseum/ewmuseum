On The Road Again.

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wow. Life on the road was sure different than it used to be. It had been a long time since Brand Frontier ran the road circuit. Life in the major league so to speak had made him soft. It was a testament to his ability to quickly adapt that kept him from waking every morning unable to move from the beatings at the hand of Ric Chronos. This morning was no different in that respect. Brandon had risen with the sun as is the norm and was just returning to the hotel after his morning jog when Lance and Ben were just getting up. They had a bit of a later night than he had so it was expected that they would be getting up a bit late. He on the other hand couldn't wait to get the day started. Today was different in one respect. Brandon was going to see his kids. The mere thought of seeing Sean and Jarod for the first time in two weeks brought an instant smile on his face. As some of the other superstars began to tax the water supply of the hotel simultaneously Brandon had already changed and packed. Time seemed to drag. He apologized to Lance and the gang for not going to breakfast as he headed out the door and hopped in the rental car. If he left now in eight or so hours he would be with his family again. With the fWo giving today off to the superstars Brandon had taken full advantage of the situation. If he drove straight through he would be in Memphis by the afternoon. That would allow he, Ana and the kids all night and most of the next day together. It would be a good Mother's Day for her. It seems that absence makes the heart grow fonder. With Brandon being forced on the road with the canceling of Monday Night Slaughter Ana realized just how much she missed him. Maybe the effects of the fWo's misfortune would be a good one for the Roche family after all. Brandon had thought that with his pay being restructured because of the television deals being cut there would be added stress to what was already a sticky situation. It didn't happen though. He was making more in merchandise sales, which was an added bonus that he didn't think about. With more fans coming to more shows across the nation they were eagerly scooping up the shirts and posters. That would help. Brandon had panicked as he normally does and overbooked himself on a few occasions just to make sure his bases were covered. He was afraid that Ana would panic about their financial situation or that she would be overly stressed about him being on the road for so long. They had talked the few weeks leading up to Slaughter going off the air and had made a reconciliation of sorts. With things in his family going so well he was shocked when things changed for the fWo so quickly. But that is the way it always is. Nothing is ever normal it seems. Right when things get comfortable they turn on their ear. The engine revved. Parking brake disengaged. Brandon Roche was on his way. There's nothing like an uneventful car ride... On The Road Again.
