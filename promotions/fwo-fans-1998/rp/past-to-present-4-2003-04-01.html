Tim finally arrived to the Orlando Development League, he swung open the doors as he saw a couple of trainers right away. By the looks of things they were in a middle of a session, Tim walked into the building and slowly closed the doors behind him trying to not interrupt things. Tim walked over to the wall where a row of chairs sat. He pulled a chair over and sat down in it and watched the trainers work on the amateur wrestlers. "COME ON! You want to make it in the business you gotta be able to at least take a little bump." Insult after insult shot out the mouths of the trainers. Tim had never seen this kind of in your face trainer before. He didn't know if he liked it or not but he knew it was something different. Something about it that pulled him closer and closer to wrestling. One of the trainers spotted Tim sitting down near the corner and walked over to where Tim was sitting. "Can I help you?" Tim grew nervous suddenly. "uh yeah I am looking to become a wrestler, I was just wondering what wrestling promotion do normally wrestlers get bumped on to from here?" The trainer looked Tim up and down. He thought to himself that Tim looked like he could make it to be a wrestler in the business. "Well it all depends, it depends on if you're good or not, how good you can wrestle, how good you can work the mic, but some guys if they're good enough, and I mean they have to be good they will get bumped up to the fWo. Would you like to enroll?" Tim thought about it for a second, he then remembered his conversation that him and Skylar had previously. "You know what? I would never join the fWo now that this is happening, it just goes to show you that all fWo employees are heartless bastards. Even the wrestlers they will never be happy with what they got. Ya know Tim?" Tim then thought back to his reply. "I couldn't agree more with you, I would never join the fWo...never." Tim looked at the ring and the wrestlers in the ring, and then back at the trainer. "Yeah I'll enroll"
