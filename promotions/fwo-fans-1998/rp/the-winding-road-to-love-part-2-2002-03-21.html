The Winding Road To Love (Part 2)

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The days had passed by, but the pain inside had not. It had been a couple of weeks since their last encounter, but Monte Burns was still thinking back to his tag match involving Tempest. He had a chance to prove how much he cared for her and he blew it because he was too concerned about winning. Monte could feel the battling emotions inside of him and was committed to making sure he could control those emotions. However, when he ran across Ice backstage this past Sunday night, those emotions got the better of him once more. Although he was able to defeat Ice later on that night, Monte knew that he would have to keep his emotions in check. There was no other way to handle the situation. Sitting at the dining room table, Monte looks down at the plate of food in front of him and sighs. Having been picking at his food for the past hour or so, Burns decides to finally stand up from the table. Leaving the plate sitting on the table, Monte exits the room to his left and enters the study. As he looks over at the large bookcase along the far wall, he notices a similar theme among the books. "Philosophy...Philosophy...Art of War..." Burns muses over the subjects of the books, realizing that none of them reference the subject that has been weighing on his mind the most. "All of these books about war, philosophy, and strategies can't help me at a time like this." As Burns sat down at his desk, he glares out of the window to the dark world outside. In all of his life, he had always managed to get what he wanted. He had all the toys as a child. He went to the finest private schools. He began wrestling training early with his father. He became the NEWA Heavyweight Champion. He even found a way to take his father's company right out from under Daniel's feet. Once he arrived in the fWo, Monte had set his sights on the Cruiserweight Championship, and he had won that three times. Now, however, he had set his sights on loving Tempest...and had been denied. Dealing with failure and rejection was not something Monte was accustomed to. When people turned him down in the business world, it usually resulted in Burns getting revenge somehow down the line. When people crossed him in wrestling, Monte made it a point to make them pay. With Tempest, though, he wasn't in a situation to make her pay. This was the woman he loved, the woman he cared for, and the woman he wanted to spend time with romantically. How could he get revenge on her? With all of that on his mind, Monte tried to decide what to do next. Curves were coming this crazy road, and Burns was not sure what the next step would be.
