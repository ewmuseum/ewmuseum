Arrival and Shocked and Injured

10 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
High Flyer took a deep breath in. "London." He exhaled, taking in the scenery around him. Being that he was inside of airport, it didn't take long. "It's about bloody time." Kate stood next to them, carrying a gym bag in her hand. They hadn't yet gone to baggage claim, so you can imagine how much they had for a two day trip. "So, why do you think they use the word bloody as an explitive?" Kate said with a frown. "Why didn't they use another word, like Salad." "It's about salad time? Come on, how stupid is that." Flyer said, turning to his wife who abruptly changed her frown into a puppy dog look of sympathy. She batter her eyelashes at Flyer, and he was melted. "And people called me the weird one in this relationship." "Hey, I didn't go around selling people snow." "Maybe that's what's wrong with the world, huh?" Flyer said, smiling. "You ever think about that?" He wrapped his arm around her shoulder, wincing a small bit due to his injury. She slipped off of him, gliding away as if Flyer were made of butter. "So, which gate do we go to pick up our bags?" She looked at her flight tickets, as Flyer let out a sigh. He must have struck a chord. "Sorry Kate, I was just being playful. You know, stress and me doesn't mix." Flyer placed his hands on her shoulders, and lightly began massaging them. She dropped her bag in response. "Don't worry about the bags, they're being sent to the hotel we're staying at." Flyer trailed off. "Seems like a bunch of fWoers are staying there this evening, something about some Eagles concert and their venue." "The Eagles?" "Yeah, don't ask me." He picked up the bag Kate had dropped, but winced in pain and dropped it almost immediately. "Hey, don't worry, I got it. You just rest. If fWo's signing you, you're gonna need to get better fast." Kate lightly touched Flyer's stomach. "I didn't know you broke so easily." Flyer sighed. "Listen, there's a cab waiting for us outside, it'll take us to Silver Foxie Brown and Sammy Smoothness, let's go." Flyer twisted his body away, as Kate stood there, narrowing her eyes. She had been married to Flyer for years, but he was still full of surprises. She shrugged her shoulders, taking in the gloomy England weather before lifting the bag and following suit.
