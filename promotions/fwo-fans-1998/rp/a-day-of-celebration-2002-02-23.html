A Day of Celebration?

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 23rd, 2002. Seattle, Washington. Safeco Field. Body Count. The fWo's first pay-per-view of the year had finally arrived, and the evening's card was star-studded to say the least. Six-time fWo Cruiserweight Champion Black Quicksilver vs. fWo legend the Flying Frenchie. fWo Survivor II winner Brand Frontier vs. three-time fWo World Champion the Boston Strangler. Zero, Triple X, Eli Flair, and Donaven Winters battling it out to settle personal differences and determine the fWo's next Hardcore Champion. Perhaps the greates fWo World Champion of all-time Scott Slugger vs. fWo Internet Champion "Kodiak" Vic Creed. Three-time fWo World Champion and resident madman Austin Davis vs. fWo World Champion Ruben Ross in his first PPV title defense. And, of course, the Body Count match to determine who will face the fWo World Champion at Cyberslam. However, as he steps out of his limo behind Safeco Field, the only thing on the mind of Monte Burns is his fWo Cruiserweight Championship match with Tempest. He had been waiting for this match for quite a while, mainly because of what happened the last time he had defended the title against Tempest. At that time, Burns was embroiled in his bitter feud with Donaven Winters, and Winters had interfered, causing Monte to lose the title to Tempest on Slaughter. Burns was determined to prove that what happened in their previous title match was a fluke, and that he was indeed the better competitor of the two. Tempest had posted some impressive victories over the past couple of months, most notably earning a couple of wins over her brother Eddy Fate. Still, Burns was confident that tonight he would once again prove that his claims were true - he was in fact the greatest Cruiserweight Champion in fWo history. "Would you hurry the hell up?" said Burns as he turned around. The limo driver seemed to be taking a little too long in getting Monte's gear out of the trunk, and Burns was not in the mood to wait outside because some idiot was wasting time "Listen, you lazy bastard," shouted Burns with more annoyance. "I don't have time to stand around while you figure out how to lift a god damn bag out of a trunk and carry it. Move it!" Finally, the driver pulled Monte's bag out of the trunk and walked up to the entrance where Burns was waiting. After letting out a deep sigh, Burns walked past the security guards and made his way into the backstage area. The driver lagged behind a bit, but Burns wasn't about to worry about him now. He made his way past some of the fWo officials, not bothering to say anything to them even though some greeted him. Burns had pissed off quite a few of the officials since his arrival in the fWo because of his cold attitude towards most of the crew. Most assumed that it was because Burns felt he was better than the rest of them, and while that was the case, it wasn't the only reason for his demeanor backstage. Burns simply wasn't used to having people work above him and not having complete control. As the CEO at Platinum Gold, Inc., Burns could say or do whatever he wanted, whenever he wanted, and wherever he wanted. In the fWo, he was just one of the many wrestlers, managers, officials, and other workers who combined to make the show as good as possible. As Burns finally reached his locker room door, he turned back to look down the hallway and saw the limo driver just rounding the corner with his bags. Burns simply shook his head, wondering if this was possibly a sign of things to come for the evening. When the rotund driver reached the doorway gasping for air, Burns just stared and rolled his eyes. "Just leave the bags right here," said Burns in disgust. "I wouldn't want a fat ass like you having a heart attack in my locker room. That would take away from my preparation for the match tonight." The driver dropped the bags right in front of the door and slowly nodded his head seemed to breathe out a "thank you" in between heaving. Burns could see the sweat streaking down the man's face and pooling on his forehead, and that made Monte absolutely sick. To see someone abuse their body with donuts, pizza, french fries, and other fatty foods that this man obviously loved was almost criminal to Monte. As the driver staggered back down the hallway, without a tip of course, Burns opened the door and stepped inside with his bags in hand. He dropped the bags over by a bench and sat down. The day not begun well, and Burns was thinking that maybe tonight was not going to be his night. Something out of the corner of his eye caused him to rethink that. Bottles of champagne. A smile came across Monte's face as he saw a couple of bottles of champagne sitting in ice on one of the tables in the room. Burns was always in the mood for a drink, especially a nice glass of champagne. And after tonight's title defense against Tempest, Burns felt that he would really be in the mood for a celebration.
