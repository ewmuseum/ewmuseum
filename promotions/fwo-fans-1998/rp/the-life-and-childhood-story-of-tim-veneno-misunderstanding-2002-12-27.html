The life and childhood Story of Tim Veneno: Misunderstanding

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was now Monday as the weekend was a long and dark one. Funeral services for Eric were held on that Sunday as the church was packed. Eric made an appearance but no one would talk to him. In one day Eric went from being popular to being labeled a freak. Just like Tim, he was one of Tim's people now. Monday rolled around as the day carried itself moderately slow. Lunch was especially hard for Eric as none of his friends would let him sit next to him, then he spotted Tim, his new friend. Eric walked over to Tim as Tim greeted him. "Hey Eric" Eric only sighed and sat down and then started to eat his lunch. He wanted out of there as fast as he could get out of there, so he ate his food really fast. Tim opened his mouth hoping to relieve some pain of Eric's "Listen I am sorry for what happened with Ben." That's when it struck him, Tim knew about it. "How?" Eric asked. "How what?" Tim didn't know what he was talking about "How did you know about Kristen and I?" "When you sit in the back of the room you hear everything, I over heard you talking to someone else saying that you were going to get Kristen in the back of your car and that you didn't care if Ben was your best friend." There was a long moment of silence before Eric opened his mouth again. "You...you...YOU knew that I wanted Kristen...and....and YOU did ABSOUTLY NOTHING to make sure Ben had Kristen that night...so...so I wouldn't fuck up the relationship? YOU KNEW AND YOU DID NOTHING ABOUT IT! Because you of Ben is dead! You could have stopped it and you didn't!" Tim didn't believe what he was hearing. "I tried, I told Ben to keep...." "Save it for later, I hope you rot in hell for this. Because of you my best friend is dead!" Looks like Tim's short-lived friendship was now over. Eric went back to his old friends as he told them the story. After school was over that day, Tim was walking past his locker; of course it was in an old, dusty, and dark hall. Eric and his friends jumped Tim beating him to a bloody pulp. Tim screamed for help as he saw one last thing before he was knocked out, and that was Eric's face.
