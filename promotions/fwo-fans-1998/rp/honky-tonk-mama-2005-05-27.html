Honky Tonk Mama

27 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The steel guitar had everyone's attention at Klondike Mike's Honky Tonk. Stetsons, denim, and cowboy boots for as far as the eye could see. Sally Apostle and David Cross returned to their booth in the back, each carrying a beverage of choice. With Alex out of town doing some promotional work while his Momager had him unofficially on the shelf, David thought it'd be a good opportunity to talk to Sally about her trying to get Alex to leave the fWo. The country bar wouldn't have been his first choice, but if that's where Alex's mother wanted to go then that's where they were going. Besides, she looked smoking hot in those jeans. She looked incredible in anything she wore. David didn't even protest when she tried to get him to wear a cowboy hat like the one she wore. Some people weren't meant to accessorize, and David Cross was one of them. Sally slid into the booth across from David and took off her black cowboy hat. She wiped the condensation off her glass with her paper napkin and dabbed at her forehead. "Wow. I had no idea you were such a good dancer." David chose to cool himself off by holding his chilled Budweiser against his forehead. "My martial arts training keeps me light on my feet. That, and working as the mop boy in a peepshow palace. You pick up a few moves watching naked woman working hard for their money." He looked around the club. "Of course, those moves don't translate well into country music." Sally smiled and sipped her rum and cola through her straw. "I bet." A waitress walked by and picked up the empty glasses and bottles from the previous round. David wasn't a big drinker, but luckily the dancing was helping him burn off some of the alcohol. The beer was still kicking his ass, but this was what Sally wanted to do tonight. "It's good to see you smiling," he told her and looked at her glass. "Even if it is fuelled by rum and country music." With a giggle, Sally toasted him and motioned over his shoulder. "Did they really card you at the bar?" Another sip. Ugh, it was hard keeping all that liquor down. "They sure did. It's flattering I suppose." "Flattering?" Sally sat back with an amused look on her face and tossed a piece of complimentary popcorn at him. "You're not an old man David." "Sally, I've had more facial work done than Cher." The smoke machine sent a blanket of fog across the floor while the music sang of partying until the sun came up. Sally looked at David. "Well how old are you?" she asked out of curiosity. David smirked. Damn, not only did he have to pee but he really was drunk. He fumbled in his pocket and pulled out an old and tattered driver's license which he slid across the table. Sally picked it up and held it up to the neon light. She squinted and held the license close to her face before setting it back down on the table. "Is this real?" Maybe eating something would help. Hello free popcorn, crunch crunch. "Yeah. I never bothered to renew my license after I had the first skin graft a couple years back." Sally seemed to look at Crucifix in a whole new light. "You certainly don't show your age." David adamantly shook his head. "Oh no. I'm very immature for my age." The waitress brought another basket of popcorn, as well as two fresh drinks. The cola in Sally's drink was a little clearer this time, which didn't seem to stop her from pulling the straw out of the glass and sipped an ice cube into her mouth. "Even your body looks good," she mumbled as she chewed. It took a good five seconds before it dawned on David what she just said. "Wait, you've been checking out my body?" Sally spit the ice cube back into her glass. "Uh, well when you're wrestling and stuff." She stood up and grabbed him by the wrist. "C'mon, let's dance."
