L'Invasion

17 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization L'Invasion January 17, 2000 ::The scene begins in city of Marsielle, France, the weather is foggy, and the part of town we see is very old. Geoff Green is lugging around his camera equipment glancing at his watch every once and awhile. He looks down a narrow street, seeing nothing except the damp darkness. He looks up at the street sign and doesn't seem to be a able to read. He pulls out a map of the city and begins pointing to himself. He realizes to get where he is going, he needs to go down the dark street. Geoff silently, hesitatingly walks down the street, wishing for better lighting. As Geoff gets to the center of the street two men approach him from the other way, obviously lost, Geoff goes to the two men for directions.:: Geoff Green: Hey there can you folks help me ? Man #1: Quoi ? Je ne parle pas l'anglais. Geoff Green: Oh Damn, I forgot. I've been listening to the Flying Frenchie speak french for two years, and I still can't speak a damn word. Man #2: Ze Flying Frenchie ? Il est tres magnafique ! A vendredi le Frenchie gange le "Title" ! Geoff Green: Hey this guy knows some fWo talk, pardon, but have you seen "Rain" or what is it, "Pleut?" No that cant be it. Tres grand ? Man #2: Ze Rain ! Il est un imbecile..... Geoff Green: Down two corners to the right ? See you later. ::Geoff Green quickly eludes more talk with the French duo, since he only understood two words in the whole conversation. Green walks in a hurried pace as he passes an ally. A dark figure speaks from inside the ally.:: Rain: Geoff, where are you going ? You are late. ::Geoff spins around shaken, as he gets closer to the dark ally he sees the seven foot plus Internet Champion, sitting omniously on a set of crates which lay at the back entrance of a bakery. He is donning a trench coat, his black sunglasses rest over his motionless eyes, and a half empty bottle of wine sits at his side. Geoff cautiously makes his way over.:: Geoff Green: Rain, am I glad to see.... wait.. no. Rain: Your late. you were supposed to meet me here a half hour ago, my patience grows thin with your incompetence Geoff. You've been doing this job for two years and you still cannot sufficiently make it to your job on time. Geof Green: Im sorry, I've never been in France. You seem to know your way around here. Rain: My past is thick, and a past I do not care to discuss, I know my way around the world, my past was one without guidance, and talking of my past would only waste my time. My past is never important. Geoff Green: I never ment to pry. Is this where you want to do the interview ? Rain: here will be suffice. Geoff if you want to make your way around France for the next couple weeks I suggest you learn some of the language or you will ned up in the american embassy stripped of all your belongings. ::Geoff silently sets up his camera, as Rain takes a drink at the wine bottle and sets it back in it's place. Rain stares coldly into the camera and speaks.:: Rain: France. Filled with a history that dates to the beginning of modern humanity. Of course the God's of the Storm have watched this history, as the God's have been here for much longer, waiting and threading. Waiting for a chosen one to carry out their duties. I am he. I'm not going to say that I detest the French, I don't. I've been to many places and the french are upstanding people, I do though detest France's representatives in the fWo. Rain: Courage, a proud american, Primetime, a proud sellout. The Frenchie a tasteless Frenchman. The God's respect your skill but also know that my skills are that much greater. If all of the French were as the Frenchie is, the nation would have ended back in 1745. On Wednesday, I battle against the Frenchie, his first match in France, his return home. Im sure the fans will not be behind me, this I have prepared for. In a disturbed time I sent Frenchie back here, due to careful masterminding. I stripped of his World Title, a title that should have rightfully been mine, if it weren't for the... the mistakes. Now you believe redemption would be to take my title and flaunt it in front of me ? Redemption, yes. Realistic ? No. Frenchman, you are sliding as you lost on Sunday, and you will lose on Wednesday, and on Friday, your return home... will not be a happy one. Rain: Friday, my second match in France, and one of the biggest matches of my career. I put my Internet Title on the line against the International Champion, Eron. Eron is a silly man, lost in a world of defined personality. Eron will be the next Mike Heftel. The God's have blessed me with this chance, a chance to hold two of the most respected titles in the fWo, at once. Not a chance, but a reality. Eron has no respect for the God's or the Storm, but the Storm will attack you with all his might, and victory will be mine, I will prevail. Going into Invasion 2000 I will be on a high after defeating the Frenchman. Then I will defeat a lowly Eron. I have held the Internet Title longer than anyone before, and you have held the International title longer than anyone before. Friday your streak will come to an end and mine will be continued. The Storm, has shown his power before, and Im sure Eron, through your arrogance and self-absorbtion, that you cower, and you will not realize this... until you set foot into the ring with me, nowhere to run, nowhere to hide. .....Remember When it RAINS....it POURS. Fade to Black.
