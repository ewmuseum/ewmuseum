Tough Choices - Part Four

18 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
David picked himself up out of the snowbank. "I think you must be lost," he said to the gaint Juggernaut standing before him. The look of disdain on his face was reflected back at him in Creed's sunglasses. "Nobody around here is going anywhere with you." Vic's eye twitched behind the mirrored lens. "Don't make me tell you again Alex." Telling his son would not be necessary. He'd heard the demand and responded to it without saying a word. His actions spoke volumes; Alex launched himself into the air and lashed out with a kick to the Main Man's head. Vic blocked it with his thick arm and shoulder, further padded by his heavy coat. The Mecca twisted into a leg sweep as soon as he landed, forcing his father to jump backward to avoid the takedown. Vic's back hit the side of the car and Alex dove at him, driving his knee into his father's ribs. The blow drew a painful grunt from his father, but Alex wasn't finished yet. The Mecca avoided Vic's grasp, then delivered a stiff kick to his father's thigh. Before he could recover, Vic reached out and snatched Alex by his burlap shirt. Pulling him close, the Main Man brought his heavy forehead down on top of Alex's nose. The headbutt filled Alex's eyes with tears after the crunch of cartilage filled his ears. Vic drove a knee of his own into Alex's midsection and grabbed him around the waist with both arms. David had held back to let Alex vent on his old man, but moved too late to stop Vic as he powerbombed Alex onto the stone steps. To keep him from inflicting more damage, David flew into his own assault on the Main Man. His roundhouse kick was both fast and crisp, splitting the Juggernaut's bottom lip and rocked his head back. "I told you I'd kill you if you ever hurt Alex again," threatened Crucifix. Vic wiped the blood from his mouth. "Show me." The two combatants didn't hesitate. Creed lunged forward to grapple with the smaller man, but David had other ideas. He ducked inside Vic's reach, and hit the Alaskan five times in the torso before spinning away. Vic stopped in his tracks, surprised at the impact of the blows. He pulled the sunglasses off his face and towered over Cross like a small mountain. Blood roared in his ears and he charged again. David started to run as well, looking every bit as focused as the Alaskan was. It was a mismatch, like a skier charging at an avalanche rather than heading in the opposite, healthier, direction. David took flight seconds before the two collided, leaving Kodiak Vic Creed nothing to grab but air. Both of Cross's knees caught the Main Man full in the chest and face. The impact knocked Vic off his feet, sending him sprawling onto the stairs with David crashing down on top of him. Alex barely managed to roll out of the way and tumbled into the snow. Vic shook his head and sat up, trying to clear the cobwebs. Suddenly, a lighting bolt shot down Vic's spine, knocking him back down to the cold steps and sending his body into spasms. Blood hemorrhaged from his nose, splattering against the stone as Vic sputtered and stared at the sky. David looked down at his fallen foe and trembled, but not from the cold. Had he not lost his footing at the last second before impact, David was certain he would have killed Kodiak Vic Creed with that focused blow. Even so, thanks to Creed's jacked up muscular neck he was only suffering from what appeared to be a partial paralysis rather than a broken spine. In any case, the Main Man was helpless at his feet. David looked around in the dark to see if he could find a large stone to finish him off with. He didn't find a stone. He did find Alex, sitting in the snow, staring at him with a look of horrific amazement on his face. "What... what did you do to him?" "Mountain Splitter," David said in a voice he barely recognized. "Let me help you up." Alex crawled out of the snow and limped onto the steps. He watched his father's trembling subside, blood clinging to his hair like clumps of tomato paste. David retrieved his wooden shovel. "Head back to the temple Alex," he said as he tightened his grip on the shovel's handle. "What exactly are you planning to do with that shovel?" "Cleaning up the trash," David said with his eyes locked firmly on the Main Man. "Dave, you can't do this." "Sure I can," David said and rapped his knuckles against the wooden blade. "Japanese wood is very strong." "But not bulletproof," gurgled Kodiak Vic Creed. Alex turned around. Vic was swaying on one knee, cradling a sawed-off shotgun in his hands. The weapon belched thunder and the blade of the shovel exploded. Alex twisted from the impact of, something, with his arm and fell into the snow. Despite the icy cold that enveloped him, his arm felt like it was on fire and he shrieked in pain. Seconds passed and the burning was replaced with a numbness. Alex barely felt it when Vic grabbed him by the ankle and dragged him out of the snow. His head bounced off each step as he was dragged to the car. "Dave...?" he croaked. Vic lifted Alex off the ground and sat him down in the back seat. For a moment, Alex could see over Vic's shoulder and saw the handle of the shovel sticking out of the snow, along with a pair of legs. The purity of the white snow was violently violated with red... so much red. Vic let Alex fall back onto the seat. "It's just a flesh wound," he said after examining Alex's bloody shoulder. "But... Dave..." "I hate that chop sockey shit." Vic reached under the front seat and pulled out a small first aid kit. He pulled out a thick gauze pad and taped it to Alex's wound. "Get some rest, boy. Your Mother wants to see you. She's got some good news for you." Unconsciousness came before the tears could.
