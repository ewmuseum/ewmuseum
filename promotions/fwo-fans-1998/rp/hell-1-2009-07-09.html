Hell (1)

9 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
April 23rd 2009 - 2:00 a.m. The arena was quiet, the parking lot was empty, and the moon was suspended high in the sky. KATSURO stumbled outside of the arena in Seattle. He scanned the parking lot for his rental car. Eyes finally focusing in and locking on the target. He adjusted his white fur coat and began the trek to his vehicle. As he approached he noticed a shadowy outline standing back towards the vehicle, leaning against the side of the car. KATSURO smiled, finally some one out here looking for his autograph. He'd finally get to tell some one to fuck off and watch them run away. “Hey buddy, get the fuck off of my car!” KATSURO shouted. To his astonishment the figure never flinched. KATSURO's grin grew a bit as he finally reached the vehicle and spun the man around. The face stopped him cold in his tracks. Ice blue eyes glared back at him underneath a mop of unmanaged hair. The first thing he felt was the right hand reaching out and grabbing his face. In the next moment he felt his face cascade through the glass of the passenger side window. KATSURO tried to shake it off, but the man pulled him from his car and began pounding him with left and right hands until KATSURO was face first on the pavement, blood trickling from his mouth and nose. Then it was darkness for KATSURO... April 23rd 2009 - 3:00 a.m. The man was silent in the drivers seat of the car. Katsuro's screams of help from the passenger seat were muffled by the duct tape and the sock that sat in his mouth. Tell him why The man smiled, his grin growing as his arm shot out and smashed Katsuro across the mouth. Katsuro's face smacked off of the passenger side window before he sat quiet and still. The duct tape on his arms wasn't loosening. “I'm supposed to tell you why I'm doing this Katsuro... Why you're in the car with me and we are driving south.” The man said as he slowly pulled off at a rest area and parked far away from any other cars. “I'm sure your first question is, why me?” The man said, and he quickly responded to his own answer. “Truthfully, it's because they'd never know you were missing. Your lifestyle has led you to this cross roads in life. All of the partying, the girls, trying to live the lifestyle of a professional wrestler and a college kid at the same time...” He paused, letting Katsuro think for a moment. “Your lifestyle is your problem Katsuro, and I plan to fix it. The only thing you've accomplished the entire time you've been walking around backstage was spitting in someone's face. You are a failure, not because you don't have the talent, it's because you don't have the desire.” Katsuro nodded his head, so far everything the man had said made sense and was sadly true. “The second question you have would be 'Where are we going?' The answer to that question, is Los Angeles.” Katsuro watched as he shifted the car into drive and began entering the highway again. The rest of the ride he would be silent.
