Coming in From the Cold

10 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempest walked along the windy streets, shivering, with her duffel bag thrown over her shoulder. She smiled a little, even as she pulled her coat tighter against the bitter wind. Her coal black locks swished over the fuzzy purple trim of her long black velvet coat as she walked. It'll all be ok. I know it will. Ric Chronos tried to ruin me, didn't work. Monte Burns took my chance at the Cruiserweight title, but now I can pursue it again. Now I've even got a new friend somewhere, a new mystery admirer. How fun is that?! She grinned and skipped just a little, then looked around almost self consciously. Things were indeed getting better. The fans she believed would never see her the same way after the revelations about her past, seemed to have rallied to her side. I was so sure they'd hate me. They'd think I lied to them and all. But I guess after what Chronos did, they saw him as being worse. Of course he was worse. She shook her head with a little growl as she turned down the street. That's ok. Chronos will get his, sooner or later. Between me and Eddy... and Papa, we'll find a way. I wish Eddy had gone with me to Hong Kong. Papa wanted to reassure him. But I guess I understand considering everything he's been through. At least he agreed to see me. It was great of him to say that I could go after the Cruiserweight title, but it's strange. I know how hard he struggled for it. She reached an older building, decorated in the sort art deco style of the late twenties and stopped. It was the hotel Eddy had chosen to stay in, away from the crowds and reporters. She stepped toward the door and paused briefly to remove her gloves before remembering she didn't have any. She looked at her pale hands, now wind burned as well. I am such a yutz sometimes. How I lost my gloves in Hong Kong I'll never know, but my hands are gonna fall off from the cold around here! Anyway, here goes nothing. She took a deep breath and stepped through the door to talk to her brother for the first time, with the hope that they might yet be family.
