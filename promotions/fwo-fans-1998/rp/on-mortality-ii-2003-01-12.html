On Mortality II

12 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
They call it insanity and perhaps they are right. Though I once read that those capable of questioning their sanity should be comfortable in it. The label of "Insane" never bothered me. What does bother me is the stigma that such is somehow a sick, distorted creation of a mind hell-bent on nothing but destruction, without feeling or caring. Then again, I gave up feeling and caring long ago. The echoes of my pulse upon my brain are almost constant now, and the racket of it all makes my head hurt. There is no rest for the weary; only torment, lies, and torture. Torture? Yes. The torture of facing what you know is an uphill battle that you know you've already won but no one will accept. Life is torture. I entered my apartment after an evening of drinking by myself and leafed through the books on my shelf. I do not have many, as I've never had much time for books, but those I have I always held sacred; Treasure troves of thought and acknowledgement of the human condition...of my condition...no, not my condition. I am eternal. Humanity has no claim on me. I will outlive it. The filthy lies that I have endured will end. Already, they are gathering. Can you not see them? The witnesses to all I have endured. Downfall is among them, but he is no longer my concern. Here rise Stephanie O'Brien, her father, and Burkenstock. Here rise the millions who knew my story and died; the story that you have refused to believe. These lives can attest to it, and still, you will doubt. I hate you. These are dark times for the world. Very dark times indeed. I look at the microcosm that is the fWo, and I need look no further. Those who fight for a living have always led darker lives, for sure, but the unnatural horrors I see committed there draw a shudder from so deep within my being that I can scarcely stand through it. They've got women wrestling men, which is a concept so ridiculous, I cannot even give it words; not to mention the fact that everyone's trying to kill each other. Not that I look down upon the use of the tool of death, I just abhor it's careless use with lack of purpose. There is a difference between killing and murder. What I see attempted in the fWo is the latter. There is another man who has been shot and lived. Though his ailments paled in comparison with mine, he overcame what few do...his name is Neo. I am intrigued by this man and will keep my eye on him in the future. Chances are he wants to be Jack Lundin, and we can't be having that, now can we? Kyle Dillon makes all his attempts in vain. Even if he gets the contract he so seeks, he will still find himself in the usual circumstance of being one greatly inferior. Kyle Dillon is mortal. Kyle Dillon will die. When that day comes, I will win. I wandered into my bedroom last night for no apparent reason, and Escape caught my eye, so I peeled it from it's place and played on its sharpest point with my index finger. The blade drew blood, and I took comfort in this. At least I could still bleed...at least an illusion of mortality. I contemplated doing the deed, but the time was still not right. I will die, I think...but only of my own accord. Death will not take me until I am willing and ready. The business I have here is yet to be finished, but when it is even those without death will die.
