Afghan Whigs, "Going To Town" It all made sense now. Every last piece. Ric dropped his father's journal into his lap, letting the pages close. He recollected those days, with the clarity that one's mind reserved only for the most traumatic of events. So much more was explained now - he understood that the "mistake" wasn't really him, rather, it was his father's indiscretion with some Chinese bimbo that had caused his mother to leave them for good, starting Mike Chronos down the road to alcoholism, a road that would eventually claim Mike's life. Well, now he knew that facts about that bitch - he knew everything. And he was going to make sure that she paid, even though she no longer lived. But that was pleasure. Business called, and he knew that he would have to let this stew a little - he had some plans for making it enjoyable, but he had to focus. As much as he knew that Ross wasn't on his level, he knew that if he allowed himself to be distracted, Ross might do the improbable and get a lucky pin count. He couldn't have that happen, now could he? So, first Ross would pay, then, so would she. Oh, how she was going to pay.
