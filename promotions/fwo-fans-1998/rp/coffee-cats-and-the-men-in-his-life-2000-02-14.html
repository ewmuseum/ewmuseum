Coffee, cats, and the men in his life

14 February 2000

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Coffee, cats, and the men in his life February 14, 2000 The whine of music cranked up, and bright green lights shone from in front of him. It was time. His time. The time of the Dame. He walked out in his pink dyed leather short shorts and looked around himself. Your order sir? Double S was in Starbucks, and he watched as one of the workers walked over and turned down the Jazz music that had been inadvertently boosted in volume when one of the vacuous employees had bumped into the boom box. Your order, sir? Sid turned his head forward. The young Starbucks woman was starting to grow annoyed. It was his time... after waiting for ten minutes in the line it was his time to order. Uh... I want something creamy. You know, with cream... The Starbucks woman rolled her eyes. It was clear that she had been down this road before. Fools who didn't understand their coffee, who couldn't tell the difference between a double shot Mocha and a half Americano with foam. Cream. Okay sir. Cream. Mocha has cream. We can also add cream to a coffee of your choosing. All you have to do is simply pick one. Simply. Pick. One. Sid Snow shuffled a foot. I'm sorry, I'm just so distracted, you see... Sid looked around himself, glancing at the door with a tired face. ...you see, I was supposed to meet someone here... David. David said he would meet me here, and he's so good with coffee. Well, beer too. To tell you the truth, he's more of a beer man than a coffee man, but he understands coffee. Plus I'm a wrestler you see, a wrestler who... well, I'm trying to make a name for myself but there is this big meanie and his cat. He calls himself Hellrazor, and he uses this cat to try and expose his sensitive side, but I'm not so sure it's working. He's all mean and stuff. I mean, I try and do some hardcore moves because it's what the fans want, but this Hellrazor guy really enjoys hurting people. I mean, who is like that? There is this guy Jim Brambles who enjoys hurting people... I mean, he's always pulling the wings off of small bugs while masturbating in the bathroom with the water running. He thinks we don't know but he always yelps and screams when he's in the middle of it. The most awful things. Now don't get me wrong, I don' t think Hellrazor is like that at all. I mean, some people have been calling him a cat fucker, but believe me I know cat fuckers and Hellrazor is no cat fucker. This guy who used to date David... guy named Rob... now he was a cat fucker. Of course, one day he got involved with a tabby who hadn't been declawed and that was a real mess. I guess they had to actually sew parts of his testicals back on like that Bobbit guy. No, Hellrazor is a nice guy and I really do like his cat, but he's still a big meanie. I'm fighting this Frank Snow guy who despite having the same last name as me is of no relation for the title. If I win I'm going to give Hellrazor the first title shot. Maybe if people are nice to him then he will quit being so mean. Oh, I wish David was here. Of course, David has been sneaking around I think... The waitress stopped him. Okay, that's it. Go to the back of the line. Sid blinked. What? The waitress clenched her fists. The back. Of the line. Go now. Sid pouted and went. I wonder what Rudo is doing right now?
