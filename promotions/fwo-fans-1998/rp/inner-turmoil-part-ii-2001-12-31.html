Inner Turmoil, Part II

31 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He sat alone, inside of his dark locker room. It was only a few minutes before Countdown To Oblivion 1 would begin. After he asked Monica to leave him alone for a while, he sat down, turned out all of the lights and buried his face in his hands. Why are we so down? Come on, crack a smile, turn the lights back on... I can't see, you know. Go away... leave me alone. Man, I really would love to leave you alone, but... I'm kind of... part of you. That makes it really hard for me to leave you alone. Well, you left me alone for eight years. Can't you just disappear again? Nah, you don't want me to leave. We used to be happy together. A perfect couple. You, the outer-shell of a human being and I, the inside, the brains behind the entire operation. Why did you try to throw all of that away? I didn't try to do anything. You just... left, and I forgot about you. I thought I was doing pretty well on my own, until earlier today in the car. I could tell Monica was upset by the way I was reacting about it all. Who cares what Monica thinks or feels about anything? She turned on you once, you don't think she'd do it again if she had the chance. No. Don't bullshit me! I know how insecure you are. You're always fearing the worst out of any situation, this one isn't any different and you know that. How about you and I try to be more optimistic about our ventures? I think it could help us out in the long run pretty well. I think I could do that, just go away. Get off my back, I don't need you. Oh contraire mon ami. You need me. Just as I need you. Without me, you'd be dead. And without you, I'd be nonexistent. It's almost like a marriage... From hell. No, no. Come on, we can be happy together. Do you honestly think you would have beaten Burns without me? From August to December 21st, you got your ass handed to you every single night by Burns. Monica helped me, not you. And what would you have done if she hadn't helped you? I don't... That's right, you don't know. She just hit Monte with the bat, you did the rest with my aid. I told you to get the fucker and put him through that announce booth. I told you to do it from the ladder. I told you to basically paralyze Monte Burns and you came damn close to it. I still don't believe you. I can't believe you. You'll believe me after tonight. After you dismantle Elijah J. Flair. You remember what happened to The Ego? Of course I do. I ended his career. No... we ended his career. I was there too. You were beginning to let me back into your life, but then you closed it back up. That doesn't explain anything. Any time you release anger, rage, fear, that is all coming from me. The part you hate most about yourself. And we both know that it's me you hate the most. But you're going to have to live with it, bear and like it. You and I kid... we're going to shed a lot of blood over the fans Wrestling organization. Like it or not. I'm not that kind of person. Don't lie to yourself. You did it to Davis. You did it to Burns. If Austin Davis got to see the tape of what you said about him back in August... he'd want to kill you. I'm going to make you more enemies than friends. But what about Ivy and Monica becoming friends? Don't worry about Eli's bitch. Worry about Eli and not the woman. She won't do you any harm, just let her have her fun with Monica. Destroy Eli, Donaven. Make Eli's life a living hell. I know you can do it. I know I can do it too. Good. If we're going to be together, we might as well make the best of it. What's Eli going to have to do to survive you? Learn to swim.
