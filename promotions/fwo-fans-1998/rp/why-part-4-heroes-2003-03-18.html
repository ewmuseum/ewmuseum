Why? Part 4: Heroes

18 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The fWo have arrived in Turkey in preparation for Warfare, following the disaster on Slaughter. The atmosphere backstage is all rather subdued... up to a point. Borst's wandering up and down the corridors, whistling to himself, with a cigarette hanging from his mouth. Yalenchka on the other hand, seems slightly upset. "What's wrong with you woman?" Borst said to his woman, blowing smoke in her face as he spoke. Yalenchka sighed and looked at the floor. "Darling..." she said. "My father... he is lost." "What? Under all those bricks and shit like Brand Frontier?" Borst said with a grin on his face, getting horribly over excited. "He's been squashed? Lost under the ruins?" Yalenchka shook her head. "He has not shown up to anything recently. He missed a plane somewhere and has not been seen since. He is lost somewhere in the world. He never was good with directions." "You're upset because that old fuck has disappeared for a bit?" Borst responded. "I thought you hated that git. He SOLD you remember." "I know, I know..." she purred. "But I still miss him, strangely. I hate him, but can't stop loving him just like that. Besides, he had my wallet." Borst scoffed and shook his head. "Fucking hell woman. Forget him. Besides, have you any idea how much these people pay me here? Don't worry about losing a couple of quid or anything. I bought you, so I suppose I should buy stuff for you in return." Yalenchka raised her eyebrow slightly and ran her hand through her hair. "Is that all you are worried about? Money?" "Yeah... the fWQuo don't like me, but can't get rid of me, and pay me lots." Borst said, starting to get bored with the conversation. "But hey. What can you do? Anyway woman, you shouldn't be down. You never know, old Borislav might have made it to the arena and been crushed like Brand Frontier. So slap a smile on your face for a bit!" And with that Yalenchka punched him in the chest and stormed off. Borst just grinned and waved as she ran away. "Very nice. So tactful." A voice said. Borst turned to see Martyr standing behind him. "You definitely seem to have a way with words." "Oh piss off." Borst replied, placing his finger on Martyr's chest. "You've gotta be loving Brand Frontier being crushed though. Now he's gone, you get to be good friends with that ginger guy." Martyr quickly pushed Borst's finger away. "Ginger guy? What are you talking about?" "I've seen you, lusting after that ginger guy with those big man tits. Short fuck, was trying to find Frontier the other day. He was pretty close to Brand I think, and now he's gone..." Borst nudged Martyr and winked. "Well, you know." "Aimz?" Martyr asked, looking confused. "Amy is a woman, sweetheart." "You what? I'd always assumed it was a he. Another of those sissy's with long hair." "Well, you're tragically mistaken. She's quite female, and I'm quite straight." "Homophobic are we? Oh dear. You just kinda... have the look. No offence or anything. No offence to that Aimz fellow either. Just looks a bit manly, deep voice, long sissy hair, adam's apple, all that..." Martyr rubbed both of his hands down his face and sighed. "I'm sorry mate." Borst said, patting Martyr on the shoulder. "Regardless, I think you'd make a good couple. You'd look good together. And don't worry, I won't tell anyone that you're..." "I can't begin to describe how stupid you are. Take a few hits too many to the head back at that Sanitarium or whatever?" Martyr replied. "Aimz is... gah, forget it." Martyr turned around and walked off, shaking his head and muttering to himself about Borst. "What was that all about?" asked a passing Matt Panzer. Borst turned and shrugged. "He just came out or something." Panzer just stared at Borst for a moment. "Anyway, I'm gonna head back to the hotel and revise some weights and hometowns for Warfare." He said, before starting to walk away. Panzer suddenly stopped and turned to Borst. "Oh yeah. Before I go, congratulations!" "Congratulations?" Borst replied, looking quite confused. "I know I was trying to wind him up, but I didn't think I did that good a job." "What? They've not told you yet?" Panzer said, looking quite surprised. "Well don't tell anyone I told you, but I was in a meeting with a few of the board members, and they told me what you did on Sunday." Borst looked at the floor and scratched his head, looking slightly uneasy. "The parents of those kids have written you a long long letter." Panzer said, as Borst started to edge away, looking quite ashamed. "Borst, you're a hero!"
