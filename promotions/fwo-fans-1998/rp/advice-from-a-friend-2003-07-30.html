Advice from a friend...

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The time surrounding pay-per-views in the wrestling business were always tumultuous. There was the added pressure of another major market television show to deal with as well as the pressure of another night of high octane, body-pounding action in the ring. This time was no exception. On the contrary, two days removed from the fWo Meltdown Pay-Per-View and everything was more chaotic than ever. Both business and professional lives had been built and shattered in the course of two days, now everyone leaned back with the hopes of healing, of moving on. Brandon Roche was no exception. He had seen the metamorphosis of a close friend from amateur to enemy to champion, it had been an exhausting journey and Brandon wouldn't change a moment of it. Believing that all things happen for a purpose he had always simply moved on instead of dwelling on the things that should have been. Most things that is, his past with Creed seemed to never be able to stay there. With the sole exception of that monster though, the man known as Brand Frontier looked to the future with bright eyes. His friend, Eli Flair had been felled in battle against the Alaskan Juggernaut, another casualty in this never-ending war. How many more would have to be hurt before Creed was taken down? Even now he, himself was recovering from a concussion delivered by the hands of the Main Man and the title he coveted so greatly. Shaking those thoughts from his head the tall Irishman looked out the double-pane window. With Angel and Aimz off on a shopping spree of some kind Brandon thought it would do him some good to have a talk with someone who's "Been there. Done that." In regards to the major issues that swirled in his head. Brandon had been thinking about life, about real life that is, and he needed to make some decisions on the direction he wanted to take. Talking to Eli, especially with the similarities in their situations couldn't do anything but help. With that, Brandon pulled himself from the view and decided to see exactly what Mr. Flair had been up to for the past few hours. "You awake in there champ?" Brandon slowly peeked his head in Eli and Angel's room. Most of the morning had past and besides hearing a scuff or two the 'King of Extreme' had been quiet. "C'mon in, Brandon," called Eli from the room. He sat on the edge of the bed, his leg straight out, immobile underneath the Ace Bandage, "Girls already left, I take it?" Glancing around the room, realizing it was nothing like he expected, Brandon shot back, "Ya realize what time it is man? They've been gone for a few hours." "Whew," said Eli as he pulled his hair out of his face, "up after Amy? That's a first. I couldn't fall asleep, though... between my knee, between staring at Angel's arm, between the weight of Sunday night's shit-storm I doubt I even fell asleep before dawn. What time is it?" He grabbed his alarm clock, noting Brand's amazement that they even owned one - right next to the baby monitor. "Damn. What time you gotta be at the arena?" "Um..." Noting Eli's minor delirium Brandon tried to steer the conversation to a more jovial bend and to the matters at hand, "Today's Wednesday man, I've got a radio interview this evening but the shows, um... tomorrow."
