Challenge.

30 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A new year was coming, and change was in the air. 3vil had been defeated, disbanded. This came as little surprise to Pierre Delacroix. Ric, Brian, and himself were the bad guys, the villains. In all honesty, he'd hate to live in a world where evil wasn't defeated. It just so happened that he was called to wear the black hat in this life. This wasn't the issue. Their dissolution, as Ric called it, came at the hands of Primetime. The same Primetime who a year and a half ago killed off the French Foundation. The same Primetime who a little over a year ago, sent Frenchie out of the FWO. This was the issue. Primetime had fallen victim to Austin Davis, a man who, while certainly a legend in his own white trash mind, hadn't wrestled in seemingly over a year. This was not the image that suited Frenchie, not for himself, not for his partner, and certainly not for a man he'd guided through the early parts of his career. A year ago, Frenchie had mocked the former surfer, Mitch Wilson, about this very same problem, always associating with the same people. Slugger, Ice, Primetime, and Frenchie. Constantly. Now it was Frenchie's turn to face the truth. For the last four months or so, he'd found himself side by side with the same man he'd spent eighteen months with in the Foundation. It was time for the tie to be severed. Permanently. That Frenchie had never beaten Primetime in a singles match didn't concern him. The challenge had to be made. And it would be made tonight. Of that he was certain.
