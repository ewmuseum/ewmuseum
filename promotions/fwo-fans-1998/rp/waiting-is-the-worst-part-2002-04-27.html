Waiting is the Worst Part

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"What am I going to do with you?" Miyuki asked, shaking her head at a prostrate Tempest, lounging on her bright red sofa. Tempest looked up with a sigh and then let her head fall back against the armrest as she said, "What? So I'm confused... That's hardly a change for me." "Yes, but why now? Why right before Cyber Slam and what should be the biggest night of your career?" Miyuki asked, wringing her hands as she paced, just behind the sofa. Tempest sat up with a shrug and said, "Why what? I have every intention of making Cyber Slam the biggest night of my career." She looked at Miyuki curiously and watched her friend's face for a hint of her meaning. Miyuki rolled her eyes and said, "Do I have to spell it out for you? After talking about how Monte's just jerking your chain, NOW of all times, you decide to help him out. Tempest, now is not the time to reconsider. What will you do in that ring if you're thinking about him as a friend or... or more, when you should be drop kicking him halfway to China?" Tempest stood up and shook her head, tossing her long black ponytail over her shoulders and said, "Miyuki, aren't you the one who told me to reconsider?" Miyuki looked at her, with a classic 'Oh yeah, that was me wasn't it?' expression. Tempest chuckled and said, "Yeah that's what I thought. It's not like I'm gonna go hand him the Cruiserweight title as a peace offering. I helped him out cuz.... well, he's been trying to help me and I really didn't want to see it." Miyuki nodded, a bit too rapidly, suggesting bit of frustration and said, "Oh, so now everything is ok? Just like that?" "No, not just like that!" Tempest said with a snap of her fingers. "I'm just trying... to not be his enemy, that's all. He hasn't really done anything to deserve that. I've always said I didn't have to hate an opponent or despise anyone who ever beat me, so... don't ya think I have to live up to that now?" Miyuki nodded, even if her gaze darted to the side anxiously. "I suppose so. I'm just worried about what happened last week." Tempest laughed and said, "Well, we can't be arrested for fighting inside the ring!" "No! That's not what I mean," Miyuki answered, laughing. "Just... you helped Monte and turned around and lost to Donaven Winters. Can you really reconcile feeling close or friendly to someone with fighting them?" Tempest paced to a nearby window and gazed out it in thought. "I suppose that's what we have to find out. Monte... Monte knows I'm a fighter, that I'm not going to give up easy or not try as hard because of anything he's said to me. And if he doesn't know that, then he doesn't know me as well as he'd like to think." Miyuki nodded and watched Tempest's face, sensing the movement of her thoughts and just listened as Tempest said, "I was so sure back at Body Count that he was lying to me. Eddy was had just gotten hurt, all that stuff that happened with me and him and Ric was still fresh and I guess my nerves and... my heart were still raw from it. I don't think anyone could have made me believe in them, least of all Monte." Miyuki nodded and said, "So... what made you think differently? I know it wasn't my urging; you never listen to me." She snickered and Tempest grinned at her and said, "I do too!" They laughed and Miyuki turned to begin gathering her brief case up from the table as she said, "But you still haven't answered my question." Tempest shifted her weight from foot to foot and said, "The answer... is that I'm not really sure. But the fight at the restaurant made me think. I may have been right, but I was cruel too." She looked up with a confused expression and said, "I've never been like that outside of Hong Kong, where people were trying to really genuinely hurt me. And I dunno what made me feel that way again, but I didn't like it. If just trying a little harder, giving Monte the benefit of the doubt will move me away from that feeling, then it's worth it." "And if he is trying to hurt you?" Miyuki asked. Tempest paused and said, "If he is, then he'll live to regret it. But tonight will be the chance to find out." Tempest rubbed her arms, seemingly nervous at the idea. Miyuki watched her for a moment and said, "And what if he's not? What will you do if he's telling the truth?" Tempest turned to her and a moment of silence stretched between them. Finally she shook her head and said, "I don't know."
