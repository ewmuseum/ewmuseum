Why? Part 1

18 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Why? What a question. Why oh why oh why? The fWo? A wrestling promotion? Why? Why sign for the fWo? Borst HATES wrestling. It's well documented. He hates the wrestling industry and everything about it. He'd gone out of his way for years to bemoan the entire wrestling industry, and even stepped up his grumbling to back those words with actions. He'd invaded a CWL event, completely destroying the set and having to be cuffed and carted away by security. "He came, he saw, he destroyed... and chances are he'll never be allowed near another wrestling show again in his life. Although judging by the look on his face, it wouldn't bother him one bit." Wrong, well partially wrong - at the time. He wasn't technically allowed near a 21W wrestling show, but it didn't stop him leading a gang of tA fighters onto the set and destroying everything in sight. Borst of course was at the centre of this, breaking the leg of the 21W Champion, Ace Carter. The ramifications of this attack led by Borst saw 21W go on hiatus. And we're still partially wrong at this point. He wasn't technically allowed near a fWo wrestling show, but it didn't stop him running in, setting the video screen on fire and in turn contributing to 3vil taking control of the fWo. The mere fact that Borst got a baseball bat wrapped around his head after setting the screen alight didn't take anything from his moment. Yet again, he'd successfully hurt another wrestling promotion. Heck, he'd even given away WWF PPV results live on a tA show before. He was on a roll. He'd gone out of his way to try and destroy the wrestling industry. He'd often admit that completely eradicating it is past even him, but he'd do his best to hurt the industry as much as he could. Even if it meant just ridding the world of one or two of these pesky wrestling promotions. "He came, he saw, he destroyed... and chances are he'll never be allowed near another wrestling show again in his life. Although judging by the look on his face, it wouldn't bother him one bit." Oh no, he'll never be allowed near a wrestling show again. And of course he'll never want to either. The sad thing is people believed it. You would have thought Borst would have believed it too. He's gone from the sports entertainment industry for a year, and suddenly it's announced that Borst has signed with the fWo, one of the aforementioned promotions that he'd attacked. He made a half arsed attempt to make the fWo look bad, but you can't help but wonder how much truth there was in all that with the fWo board at the time not having the best interests of the promotion at heart. Conveniently, they're gone, and Borst's suddenly settled down and is the first ever US Champion. A bit of pottering around later, and now he's pretty much an established fWo Superstar, holding the US Title, representing his most hated country, and he's managed to acquire a crazy Russian mail order bride, and a freaky father in law. Bizarre.
