Harem - 7 - Cold Day In Hell

12 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Amy grimaced as she looked the hospital's results over. ...Everything was fine, Why did she have to be this afraid all of the time? A happy, healthy human being was ticking away inside of her, and the man she loved most - who held the most domain over her - had no sweet clue. There it was ...the best reason she had. Worse yet was coming; The second most powerful man, despite what Amy herself thought of his control over her conscience, was about to find out about all of this through an act that could be considered, to those that knew him, nothing short of being driven by a Joan of Arc-like bravery. And considering his usual reaction to all things regarding his daughter's independence, the redhead was walking into the fight of her life. She'd come dangerously close to considering the possibility of actually being murdered when she "shared the news" with the vehement and overwhelming nerviousness that was plaguing her lately. It began as innocently as anything else in Amy's life. Yes, the same old underlying negativity in a seemingly innocent act which, this time, was being conducted by the usual victim jn going to visit her family home. Hours after she'd arrived, the questioning had appeared to have ended...the perfect opportunity - All alone with "Mom and Dad". "So..." She began....one again, 'innocently enough'. "Things've been great with Brandon - We decided on a wedding around New Years. I know that's pretty snowy, especially since we want to have it around here - he doesn't have as much family to move around and all...but I dunno, it just seems right." Her mother beamed. Dad seemed to, as well...and was that a mumbled "He's not so bad"? This might be easier than she thought... "Just don't get in too deep right yet. You two have enough kids to take care of already..." He'd tried to chuckle, "God knows y'don't need to have one that's really got your blood running through him." Yeah. What a shame that would be, eh? She didn't want to jump right in...no, he'd figure it out too quickly - The man was no fool. "You're probably right." Sorry, didn't sound sure enough. "Probably...what's probably?" He questioned, though assuming it was just his daughter's usual way if saying "You're right" without damaging her pride. Of course, Amy knew exactly what he was thinking and found herself laughing in her mind - Not at all upset at her dad's usual write-off , just amused when connecting his assumption of her protecting her pride to the number of times she'd heard from others how many traits she shared with him. Neither would risk their pride in this game of mental, parental cat-and-mouse and, regardless of how angry it had become in the past, apologies were rarely handed out,,, Though they'd probably be demanded in this fresh round, considering the curve ball Aimz was about to toss Forrester - provider and all-around egocentric father figure to her entire biological flock. "Probably is an easy way to say 'By the way, pops...I'm three months pregnant.' in response to your spiel about how BL and I don't need a baby." "You're...what?" As Amy prepared herself for the war, her father's face rapidly lost color... [Little Note: Sorry this was so short and spaced from the others. It's been a rough week, especially since breaking my wrist has inhibited my typing for any length of time. Baby steps have gotten me this much, though...the next part should be a little quicker. This was written at random times when my wrist felt okay on my Palm Pilot.:)]
