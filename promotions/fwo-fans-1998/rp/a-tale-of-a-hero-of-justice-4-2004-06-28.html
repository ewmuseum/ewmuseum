A tale of a Hero of Justice (4)

28 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Present Day 75 degrees out, raining. Canadian Military Site "Perceval!" The voice of Dr. Abraham Velsyngh rang through the small lunch room. Immediately from across the room Waylon Perceval looked up to cross eyes with his mentor. The other scientist around the room lowered their heads and continued to eat their lunch but kept their ears open for what was to happen. Dr. Velsyngh stormed across the room and met hard with Percevals table, knocking it slightly into his stomach and causing Waylon to slightly lose catch of his breath. Others that had witnessed the Doctor hitting the table quietly laughed to themselves, taking pride in the fact that they had witnessed their professor walk into wood. Velsyngh was now even more irritated knowing that he had made himself look somewhat like a fool. "Yes, Dr. Velsyngh?" Waylon managed to let the words escape from his mouth just after swallowing a small gulp of potatoes mixed with peas and corn. Velsyngh's face with red with anger, "Perceval! You low life scum! If you didn't have that goddamn cursed brain of yours I would have seen you walk out of this facility long ago, but this time I have caught you, you little sonofabitch." Velsyngh had hated Perceval for years and had been trying to get him kicked from the project but had never managed to scrape up a good enough reason. Velsyngh is the head of a government funded project for the military, specializing in making the real Captain America. Specializing in creating a super serum that would turn your average Joe Blow into the next Mr. Universe. Waylon had volunteered to work for the project and had been on the payroll for five years since he had first graduated from University. It was from the first day that Velsyngh had decided just how much he hated Perceval, from the way he talked to the goddamn way that annoying turd poured his goddamn coffee! Oooooooh how he hated Perceval and now he had found his opportunity to get rid of him. "But sir, what are you talking about?" Perceval attempted to flash a a pathetic smile, in turn showing parts of his teeth and the rest being covered with a greenish yellowish mash potatoes like grin. Velsyngh pulled an object out from behind his back and slammed it down hard onto Perceval's plate, traces of food splattered all around, but primarily landed all over Waylon's face and chest. With a piece of chicken hanging from Waylon's hair he leaned forward to see a dead rabbit lying on his plate. It was at this moment that the rest of the room of scientist's leaned in their seats to look at what was discovered. However what is important to remember is that the rabbit that lie dead on the plate was no ordinary rabbit, it had been injected with serum and it's body had been physically altered to the point of death, one leg was twice the side of the rabbit's body and it appeared as though the poor rabbit's heart had literally exploding leaving an open wound on it's chest. Velsyngh leaned in close to Perceval and smiled, the smile was a strange one, a smile Waylon had never seen from the doctor before. A smile that no other scientist could see either. "You listen to me Perceval, you are out, and you are over. You'll never work in this field again, you'll never belong to anything as long as I am alive. Injecting animals? You sick little creature, we told you the serum wasn't ready for testing but poor anxious ol' Perceval had to do it, looks like there is going to only be one thing that will be fast around here. You know what that is?" Velsyngh asked in a very sarcastic manner. Waylon looked around the room, looking at the laughing faces of all of his peers, and the over the shoulder of Velsyngh to see two rather large security officers waiting for the doctor to finish. Velsyngh began to nod slowly, "Yes Perceval, those are guards, here to walk you out. You may however go to your desk first to grab your things, and then you are dismissed." Waylon thought for a moment of taking his fork and ramming it hard and fast into Velsyngh throat, he thought about pulling it in and out over and over again quickly ripping apart the throat of the man that stood there and made a fool of him. Waylon lifted his fork up quickly and the room gasped, only for them to draw silent when he slammed his fork into the rabbit. Velsyngh jumped slightly when he saw Waylon do that and he pulled back, "Get out of here Perceval!" He turned his head around to the rest gathered in the room, "Now the rest of you can continue to eat in peace." With that Velsyngh left, just like that. The guards began to move as if to grab Perceval but he held out his hand and they stopped, "Please," Waylon almost begged, "Just give me a moment." Waylon stood up and scanned the room, most of his fellow co-workers just sneered at him, but Waylon was determined to walk out of their with a bit of pride, he wasn't going to let them get him down, he was going to rise up for once in his life and tell that Velsyngh what a real man he was. Waylon proceeded to walk down the aisle before banging his knee hard on a chair and slamming hard on the ground hitting his nose hard on the floor, "Oh god!" Waylon yelped as he jumped up and put his hands immediately to his nose, which began to bleed, "Oh god," he repeated himself as he ran out of the room and heard the laughter as the door closed. Waylon turned and ran down the hallway as fast as he could and he ran into the men's room, "Fuck!" He screamed as he punched one of the bathroom stall doors, "Ow!" H quickly followed up with as he winced and held his hand in pain. Finally resorting to a crying fit which landed him on the floor and leaning up against the bathroom wall. Waylon cried hard, he let it all out, images of his past of being tortured at school, at home, at university, at everywhere flooded his mind. Thoughts of Sara came back to him, whom he loved, she was the only love that he had, the only beauty in his life. After all, she was the reason why he had become so deeply involved with this project, if he could find a way to make himself look stronger and be stronger than surely she would love him. After all it was his small physique which had always turned her off. He could envision Sara in his head, looking at him with those soft eyes and saying... "You know Waylon, if only you had muscle..." "I hate it!" Waylon screamed again and then slumped back against the wall again. As he slowly stopped crying he could hear the footsteps of men walking down the hallway. He stopped himself from making noise and tuned his ears onto the conversation that was occurring outside of the bathroom door. ****************** Velsyngh clenched his fist hard, "Listen to me you baboons, I don't care if he ran away crying, you find that pest and get him out of the building, before he steals something.." "Yes sir." Responding one of the guards, an average looking man, blonde hair, pointless in life. "Check all around, I'll check the bathroom." Velsyngh pointed in the opposite direction as if to tell the men to go. They obeyed and left him, Velsyngh turned and entered the bathroom. ***************** The bathroom door opened and quickly Waylon moved himself into a stall and sat on the toilet seat. He kept dead still, making a point to announce his presence there. Velsyngh who's real intentions to go to the bathroom revealed themselves when he passed gas at a rather loud level, "Oh this is going to be hard times," Velsyngh muttered as he held himself and ran into a stall, whipping his belt off and throwing his knickers down he rested himself on a bathroom seat and continued to spread his stench to further degrees of disgusting. Perceval sitting on the stall himself lowered one foot to the ground and then the other. He was going to do it, he was going to get back at the doctor. He moved in front of the stall that Velsyngh was in and stopped. It was then that Waylon thought about what he was really doing, he could get in a lot of trouble for this. "Who's out there!?" Velsyngh yell from behind the stall door, "Give a man some peace you pervert!" Waylon had an urge that he could not control as he kicked the bathroom stall open and winded up and threw his body weight forward as hard as he could and slammed his knuckles into Velsyngh nose, as he did that Velsyngh head shot back and slammed against the back wall of the bathroom. Velsyngh would then slump forward. Perceval stood for a moment with a large smile as he looked down at the doctor who had ridiculed him. "Shitty situation hey Velsyngh?" But of course the doctor did not respond to Waylon's comment because he had been knocked out. Perceval leaned in and grabbed out Velsyngh wallet, attached to it was Velsyngh security card. Waylon ripped the card from the wallet and then rooted through the jacket that Velsyngh was wearing, inside he would find Velsyngh keys to his 94 Honda Civic which he slid into his pockets. Waylon ran over to the sink and quickly ran the tap, he wiped the blood from his nose and face, looking back at the unconscious body of Velsyngh and an idea dawned upon him. He ran back over to the body and undressed Velsyngh and slid on his jacket and pants, the pants being so tight they held onto Waylon's legs. Waylon looked in the mirror, "Hmmm, this might work." Looking absolutely ridiculous Waylon left the bathroom and moved down the hallway toward the sector in which the serum would be. He had a mission.
