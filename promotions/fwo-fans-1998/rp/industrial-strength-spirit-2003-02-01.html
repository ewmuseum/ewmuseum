Industrial Strength Spirit

1 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Amy was undergoing a little 'therapy' after her shooting. The kind every person needs...a few days on the road with a great band of their favorite genre. She'd been invited to tag along with Valerian's Garden for a few shows in Canada, the more recent landed her in her hometown at a small 'hardcore' all-ages club she'd all but grown up in. VG were opening for the redhead's favorite punk band and friends Choke...she was positive nobody would be disappointed, especially not the friends she got to see again thanks to this little vacation. Of course, they'd arrived hours early for set up and she was already busying herself by toying with the control panel to the sound systems, having always been a musician and 'tech-junkie' when Angel leaned against the small booth she was seated in, watching the little redhead's two-toned eyes dart all around the array of knobs, meters and blinking lights. "Let me ask you, how long have you been a wrestler?" "Huh?" Amy was distracted almost entirely, seated in front of a legion of knobs and meters. She was 'home' in any sense of the word, fiddling with the volume of the mix CD they'd been playing through the empty club's stereo. It took another minute for Angel's comment to stick somewhere within the redhead's cluttered mind, but she eventually managed to respond without looking away from the mixing board. "Uhhh...two...rounding three years pro? Started young..." "I've been singing in front of people for ten years or so, Amy," said Angel, "and I still get nervous when it's time to go out there and do my thing." She was busy lacing up her knee- high boots, faded and worn from years of use. "But there's nothing like performing in front of a roomful of people, am I right?" Amy'd caught the woman's tone and decided to spin her chair around for conversation's sake. "Nothing at all. Why, though? Got some pre-show jitters?" She smirked. "No... yeah," said Angel. She gave herself a once- over, brushing some lint off her black corset, before taking a long drink from her water bottle. "I'm always nervous before a performance. But tonight... fuck, girl! Look at me! I'm actually thin again! I've been singing through my entire pregnancy but tonight I've actually gotta put on a show again." She laughed - half nerves, half amusement. "I wish I could drink tonight." "Nine months, no real act? I'd think you'd be dying of anticipation!" Amy gave a crooked half-smile while one brown eye and one blue drank in the sight of the little "hardcore" club - though emptier than usual a few hours before a show - she'd practically been raised in. "Don't worry about the crowds here...I've known half of them long enough to tell you they'll be nothing but supportive. We've always had a good scene here, and if you wanna drink...well...drink!" "Condon doesn't sell or allow it...but he gives me the keys to open up a few hours before gigs whenever I'm around for a reason, y'know." She smiled again at the thought of 'The Pavillion"s owner - an older fellow who'd employed her randomly throughout her teens, he'd always done anything he could to keep her out of trouble with the public. "So just do whatever makes you comfortable and tell the guys to, too. Walk around naked for all I care..." Another grin sprouted, followed by a chuckle that shook her wound considerably less than it had days before...she was healing already. "...just try to be clothed when my friends decide to start showing up to lend a hand." "Hah! I don't think the world's ready to see my pasty ass naked," laughed Angel, doing a faux- rave to the music. She caught a glimpse of her reflection in one of Sex's plexiglass shields, and ran a hand through her hair. It might look like she was simply being vain, but she was simply driven by a desire for everything - EVERYTHING - tonight, to be perfect. "But listen to me... as if I'm the only one who's had an interesting week. How's your recoupin' going?" "I dunno...the doctor said three weeks before I get back in the ring, so I'm wrestling next Wednesday if I can get someone who won't cripple me." She smirked to herself this time, fiddling with a little knob on the control panel to bring out the bass presence in the song Angel was enjoying so much. "You won't see me mashing any buddies into the walls tonight...I think I'll just sell t-shirts for you guys and the openers or something, lots of sitting. But thank you for getting me away from the guys - the other guys - for a bit, though...I wasn't sure how much more of Brandon's concern I could take without an increase in the dosage of these painkillers." The little redhead reached for a small brown bottle in her pocket, tapping it to shake around the pills with another chuckle at her own twisted form of humor. "Well listen," cautioned Angel, "don't push it. Elijah's been wrestling on a bad knee for I don't even know how long and it's not getting any better. The last thing you need is to tear those stitches. Do you even have stitches?" She waved it off. "Never mind. And don't mind Brandon so much, he's middle aged at thirty and hasn't learned to relax yet. I've already told Elijah that he's going to have to invite the two of you next time we hit a rave." "I do have stitches........I think........." Her reply was already questioned as she furrowed her brow, straining to remember while she stared off into seemingly nowhere and finally came to a blank conclusion. "............I don't really remember a whole lot of what they told me, morphine does that to ya." She giggled. "But yeah...I'm not sure if BL even knows what a rave is...then again, nothing could be worse than trying to explain what Godsmack is to him the other day, or letting him hear what's always in my discman for the very first time. Poor fella...at least you and Eli are on the same page. Brandon and I aren't even in the same book." "That bundle'a joy asleep on the bus? Pretty much guarandamntees we've gotta be on the same page." Angel's gaze drifted to the back doors where Mickey and TJ busily rolled in three cabinet speakers, "But I'm not worried about the two of you... I could see it in both your eyes on New Year's, at the handfasting and the afterparty." "I'm not at all worried, it's just tough as hell. Remember, you didn't have to teach Eli all about your culture and try not to look like an overjoyed kid while doing so." Amy gazed off into the distance again before an amused smirk spread across her face. "You know...I really hope little MJ doesn't ever get as lost as I am." "Hah!" Angel jumped up and down for a few seconds, testing her wind, her relaxation level, and the knee- high spike- heeled boots she hasn't worn in months. "It would hardly be a tragedy for MJ to be a lil' Amy. Besides," she added with a wink, "you haven't seen my overjoyed, hyperactive kid motif, have ya?" She exchanged some incomprehensible hand- gestures with Mickey before turning back toward Amy. "Mick's jonesing for a ciggie, feel like giving his axe a whirl for a bit? In the mood to sing something..." The redhead was about to mouth a gracious 'Sure!' in reply, but paused the moment she opened her mouth when a second thought hit... "How'd you know I play...?" "Lucky guess," replied Angel with a giggle, "Just wanna warm up a bit and all that. You know the riff of Sanitarium?" "First song I ever learned." Amy jumped at the opportunity to toy with a guitar. She had a rather pricey electric at home that she always managed to forget. Before she even reached for TJ's, the redhead vowed to nab her acoustic AT LEAST before leaving town for another minute...it was the perfect way to pass the time, not to mention the gleefull hours of torturing Brandon. It was already shaping up to be one of the best nights in an awfully long time.
