Over the top? Probably.

11 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The following was filmed shortly after Sunday Slaughter in Borst's locker room.* "What the fuck went on there?" Borst asked, going nose to nose with Yalenchka. "Why couldn't Great Dragon see what he was doing?" Yalenchka spat in Borst's face and shoved him hard. "Why are you asking me?" she belted back in her thick yet strangely seductive Russian accent. "I did nothing I tell you!" Borst wiped the spit from his face and turned to the old man sitting on the bench in the corner of the room. "You." Borst said, pointing at Borislav. "You old fucker. I get you a job here, you old shit. I try my best to impress you by sticking up for you, and going out there to kick that third world superstar's arse, and YOU feel you should interfere." Borislav, not understanding a word Borst had just said just said in silence and twiddled his long grey beard. "You're one fucked up bastard you know? Selling your own fucking daughter in your wife selling business thing... THEN, then feeling bad about it, coming to check on me, because you think you might have done something wrong." Borst shook his head. "Terrible." "I bought a wife, you old shit. That's all. I didn't buy any in-laws, no extra family, just the woman. I got fed up with all that in-law shit with my old woman, but I thought I'd be nice and tolerate you. I go to Silver Fox last night in a private meeting, slap him around a bit and get you a job, not that you can fucking fight or anything I doubt though..." "Oh he can..." Yalenchka said quietly. "You have to be able to defend yourself if you are selling women. He also fought bears with me, but used to cheat a lot. He cheats all the time. At monopoly, cards, selling women, and fighting." "I fucking hate a monopoly cheat." Borst grunted before turning back to Borislav. "Cheating in fighting, no such thing. As long as you win, you win. But poking your shrivelled old nose into my business, making me look like a fool, after all I've done... just TOLERATING you turning up was enough. I even made you a proper Russian breakfast pouring my best vodka on your cereal! Fuck, get out." "Don't talk to my father like that!" Yalenchka yelled, randomly punching Borst in the back of the head. "What?" Borst yelled. "The guy SOLD you." "
