Beginning of the End

28 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He was alone. Whenever he prepped for a big match, he liked to be alone. No publicists, no assistance, no whores. Nobody standing around, chirping and talking up Kodiak Vic Creed's valuable air. Nobody distracting his train of thought, or disrupting his focus. Vic finished taping up his wrist and turned his attention to his knee brace. Tonight wasn't just another match. It was the match. It was the culmination of all the blood and agony that had gone on between himself and Brand Frontier. Without fail, in every promotion they had ever been in together, any time Frontier and Creed faced off in their final match Brand always walked away with the gold. Always. "Not tonight," Vic said aloud. "No God damn way." Vic stood up and rubbed his wrists. Tape done, brace done, boots cinched. The Juggernaut stared at the pale brick wall across from him. He had to focus his thoughts, tune out all outside distractions. Frontier was going to come at him like a runaway train. If his head wasn't in the game, losing the World Title was the least of his worries. No, scratch that. Losing the World Title, the title he had held for longer than anyone in the history of the fWo, was his main worry. Bones heal. Chicks loved scars. It was all secondary to being the World Champion. On the edge of the rusty sink sat two vials. Vic planned on doubling his dosage tonight and hoped it didn't kill him in the process. It was necessary going into Thunderdome. He needed two things tonight: no pain, and all power. He opened his shaving kit and pulled out a syringe, checking the needle tip for breakage. Brrrrt! Brrrrt! "The Hell?" Vic looked around and tried to find the source of the muffled chirping. He heard the sound again. Nobody was supposed to bother him before his match. Nobody was supposed to call. In fact, Vic had specifically told his assistant to make sure the batteries had been taken out of his cellular phone to prevent interruptions. Vic unzipped his gym bad and picked up his tiny phone. "Who the fuck is this!" Creed demanded. "Vic, is that any way to talk to an old friend?" Mother. Fucker. Without hearing another word, Vic knew that Darryl Vishanti was about to make life difficult for him. "What do you want Darryl?" "Why Vic, I just called to wish you good luck in your match tonight. There's so much on the line, I thought you could use all the good fortune you could get." Darryl didn't give two shits about the Main Man. Not unless he could make a buck off of his blood. "Cut the crap. What do you want?" "Well Vic, since you asked I'll make this brief. I know how much you like your little rituals before your fights, matches, whatever you want to call what it is that you do." Darryl didn't hide his disdain from Creed, which only served to further aggravate the big man. "This... Thunderdome event that you're competing in has generated a surprising amount of interest in the gambling community. Especially amongst the group of European clients I have.
