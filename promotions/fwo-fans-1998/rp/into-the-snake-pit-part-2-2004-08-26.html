Into the Snake Pit - Part 2

26 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Raindrops started to patter against the small window, beating a rapid rhythm against the glass. It was the only noise Nick heard as he sat in the dark, cold room somewhere beneath the club. Somewhere above him, people were drinking, dancing, and embracing life. This room was never about life. Many times, Nick had dragged people down into this room and let them cower in the dark until his father passed judgment on them. Tonight it was his turn to be judged. But he had no idea why. It had only been a few days ago that Nick Brandish was lying on a weight bench, pressing over six hundred pounds to his chest to warm up before he went out to the ring. After setting the bar back onto the rack, Nick sat up and wiped the sweat from his tattooed face. That's when he noticed the postcard lying on the floor in front of the door. It looked like someone had simply slid it underneath while he'd been working out. Once curiosity got the best of him, Nick stood up and crossed the room. He bent over to pick up the postcard and his hand froze in mid-air. He recognized the image on the card as one of the orate statues in Bangkok. Like a hard slap in the face, the image brought back harsh memories for the big man. Nick recalled being fifteen years old when his father took him to that very statue of a priestess kneeling in worship. It was late at night, and Nick never took part in his father's nocturnal affairs. Tonight was clearly an exception. He noticed another car had pulled up beside the statue and several people stood in a circle with only the moonlight to illuminate the area. "Tonight you learn about true strength Nikolas," his father said as he caressed the back of the boy's neck. His touch was gentle; his voice was like steel. The circle of men opened up as Nick approached, his father walking behind him. At the base of the statue, Nick could make out the shape of a bound woman and immediately recognized her as one of his father's prostitutes. There wasn't much demand in Bangkok for a hooker that was older than eighteen, but this woman was very talented at what she did. Her list of dedicated clients were long and distinguished, which made her a valuable asset to Nick's father. She struggled against the ropes which had rubbed away at her skin, leaving bloody streaks in her flesh. She'd been bound for quite some time. Nick couldn't stop looking into her eyes. He saw her fear, and... something else. Yes, Nick felt it too. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] In his father's hard world, she had been like a rainbow in the bleakness. She came to him after his father beat him, or abused him in other horrible ways. She came to him like an angel. An angel of Mercy. Of Hope. His father's words stabbed into Nick's chest. "You thought I didn't know," he said as more of a statement rather than a question. A hand coiled around Nick's throat and jerked the boy's head towards the bound woman. "Do you love her?" A warm breeze blew across Nick's face like a soft kiss. He didn't know what the right answer was supposed to be. He only knew the true answer. "Yes. I do." "More than me?" The fingers squeezed tighter against his flesh. Nick felt his father's body press against his back, rooting the boy in place. His voice came softly with the reply. "Yes. More than you." The grip on his throat eased and the fingers brushed his skin. "I see." Footsteps from behind told Nick that his father was walking around him. The youth was almost as big as the men who stood around him, but when his father slowly walked in front of him Nick felt like he was an inch tall. Those chestnut brown eyes that had often stared at him like prey rather than son, stared at him now the same way. Nick knew the backfist was coming but didn't raise a finger to stop it. His head snapped to the side, blood and broken teeth flying from between his lips as he dropped to the ground. She cried out but her voice was muffled by the rag shoved down her throat. Nick's vision started to blur. The kick to the stomach helped him regain his focus as his ribs broke. Now his problem was breathing, rather than seeing. "Pick him up," his father ordered. Numerous hands lifted to boy to his feet. He couldn't look at his father's face, nor wipe the ivory crumbs of his teeth off his bloody lips. His strength was gone. "Feeling powerless, boy?" Nick was silent, offering only a faint gurgle as a reply. "Look at her," his father ordered. "Look at the woman you love so much." Muffled screams pleaded, not for herself but for Nick's safety. The boy watched the shadow of his father kneel down beside her writing form. Grabbing a handful of her hair, he jerked her head up. "Look at her!" he snarled. Nick did as he was told. "Now, you two have become quite close despite your little age difference. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule]" The words his father used made Nick feel weak. Dirty. Insignificant. "You think you can trust her? You know when she's telling the truth?" "She has no reason to lie to me," Nick spat in bloody defiance. "Then look into her eyes for the truth." She started to squirm again, harder this time. The rope bit into her flesh like tiny saw blades. "This whore you love so much," his father stated with a bitter smile on his face, "is your mother." It was there instantly. Even a boy as young as fifteen, who knew so little about the World and the people who lived in it, recognized a look of shame when he saw it. Her muffled protest became silent and she closed her eyes. Nick wanted to say something, to deny it, to say it was all a trick. He wanted something to take away the love he now found himself hating, both for her as well as himself. He squeezed his eyes shut, trying desperately to hold back his tears, and almost missed his father telling one of the men to let them go. Nick's eyes opened, thinking his father meant to let him and... her, go. He was actually referring to the Rottweilers. The barking caused her to open her eyes, just in time to see her end. The men held Nick, making sure he didn't miss a single bite or tear. His father held his hand over Nick's mouth so he didn't miss the sound of the growling beasts, the muffled screams of agony as her breasts were torn from her torso, flesh from her face bitten off. She ran out of voice long before the three huge animals reached bone. Love, twisted and bastardized as it was, left Nick Brandish that night. Hope, goodness, purity, all were crushed under his father's boot heel. By the time the animals had finished eating, Nick was finished as well. He didn't resist when the men dragged him back to the car and tossed his broken body into the back. Alone in the car, he didn't bother holding back his tears. They just wouldn't come. It was like trying to get butterflies to sprout from his fingertips. The driver's door opened and he heard his father talking to one of his men before he got into the car. "Go and get Omas from the club. Bring him to me at the house." Nick didn't see his father sit down, only felt the car start to move. As he looked out the rear window, he could see the silhouette of the Priestess in the moonlight. "There is no beast without cruelty, my son." Those same words were written on the postcard the Monster now held in his hands. His father wanted him to come home.
