Interlude IV, or 'Full Circle'

10 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Post-Unleashed. The Codemaster, Allen Speck, smiled to himself. He had somehow convinced SOMEONE to let him have some time off, so that he may deal with the fact that he's had some seriously screwed up dreams since signing on board with the fans Wrestling organization. He even described them, IN DETAIL, to Twitch and Thumper for a two hour session that none of the three ever wished to remember, ever again. The Codemaster insisted that he'd be ready for Meltdown, but wasn't sure if he'd be ready for in-ring action until some reasoning behind his sleeping habits could be explained. His best friend, Leigh Landers, was unfortunately not as lucky as he was. Management had been rather upset with Leigh's inability to show an emotion other than happiness/optimism, and were finding it difficult to market her properly. The segment with Mitch Wilson, though it succeeded in keeping him over, did nothing for her outside of hearing the wrath from fWo management about seeing yet another woman bleed. They decided to give her some time off, as well. They possibly could let her go. And that was something the Codemaster didn't want or need to hear right now, to have his best friend being forced out of a sport she really enjoyed. * . * Coral Avalon held his neck in pain. He had just finished his match with Voran Xias, and wasn't terribly happy with the outcome of that little expierence. For one thing, Voran stiffed the living hell out of him, and while he looked good in his match with his own title, Voran decided to not let Coral get any more offense after he had successfully defended his title. However, it simply was not the Avalon way to get frustrated. He was happy to have used the style that gained him his small little bit of stardom in a ring different from that of an Action! Wrestling ring. Besides, he wanted to talk with Lance Knight. Lance was a student of Alex Lawson, who happened to be a friend of Coral's, and it'd be good to talk to Lance. Besides, he needed all of the friends he was able to make, given the fact that he was often lost in the crowd and nervous around people. Behind him was Allison Lindum, crossing her arms in front of her stomach and looking concerned for her boyfriend. He had just wrestled twice, and had only two days to recover in time to wrestle Keith Scott Zimmerman in what she had learned only an hour ago would be the main event of a pay-per-view. It was a huge match, and she just prayed that Coral wasn't going to screw this up. * . * Leigh Landers had never bladed in her seven year career. So, of course, the first time she tried it, she did a .6 Muta and got yelled at for it. Hell, between the fact that she wasn't remotely as over as fWo management hoped. This was mainly due to her inability to cut a decent promo, and her methodical and vastly different wrestling style. Not to mention that the fWo roster was full of female wrestlers including Aimz, Jade, and Kali Saturos/Jessica/Eternity/whatevershedecidestocallherselfnextweek. Leigh strolled down the hallways, feeling light-headed, as one would, when they do a bladejob. She just wanted to shower, take the trip back to the hotel, and sleep for an eternity. Or, at least until next week. Leigh could settle for that. Of course, the fact that she probably shouldn't drive tripped her off immediately that she should probably find Allen to assist with this problem of hers. * . * Allen Speck glared at the prop he held in his hands, the Megaton Hammer. He had asked fWo's respectable producers to make him some video game props to better his persona. If he was going to be stuck being the Codemaster, then he was going to have the coolest-looking props in the HISTORY of cool looking props. His first prop, the MAGIC Hammer, was designed so that it would break and be replaced with the Megaton Hammer. Now, the quest to get the Megaton Hammer is probably something that'll be saved for another day, but suffice to say, it wasn't easy! Oh no, sir. Things are never easy when you're the Codemaster. He knew he had that other prop, the mask of Vega, somewhere in that duffle bag... he should probably look. * . * "Coral, you're never going to find Lance if you keep blindly roaming the halls." Allison said to Coral, as they walked down the halls of the Pepsi Arena. Her words got the attention of Coral, who turned around and looked at his girlfriend. "Hey, I've never been in this arena, it's like a labrynth of darkness, wanting to... you know. Eat my soul or somethin'." Coral said. Of course, he spent so much time looking at his lovely girl that he wasn't paying attention to what was in front of him. * . * Now, the inevitable "bumping-into-one-another" action occured, here, at this point. The Megaton Hammer of the Codemaster dropped to the floor with a loud, audible THUD. Allison stopped short, and nervously scratched her head. She couldn't believe how clumsy Coral could be when he wasn't in the ring. Meanwhile, the Codemaster blinked, and double-checked his bag. "Dammit, it ain't in here." "What?" Coral asked, skipping the whole 'apology' thing on account of the Codemaster skipping it. "The mask! The mask I brought out to the ring for that stupid Animals debacle." the Codemaster said, angry, "Dammit, I paid fifty bucks to have that made." "I, um..." Coral started to say, but then he realized just who he was talking to and what he was refering to, "Oh." "Say, bro, you look kinda familiar." the Codemaster said. Having spent most of his night battling the forces of evil for a few weeks off, the Codemaster didn't really have time to catch the match that Coral Avalon just had with the fWo United States champion, Voran Xias. The Codemaster wasn't so stupid as to not know that Coral had just wrestled, but there was just something odd about this kid that he hadn't picked up on just yet. "I'm, uh, Coral Avalon." The Codemaster snapped his fingers. "Day-amn, boy. Why didn't you say so in the first place?" the Codemaster asked, "You were the J-Man's last student!" "You mean Joey." Coral corrected. "Yeah, him." "I don't think we've ever actually met, but I'm Allen Speck. I was his first student!" the Codemaster introduced himself, holding out his hand for a handshake, which he got. When that was done, the Codemaster glanced over at Allison. "And who is this fine-lookin' piece of work you got?" he asked Coral. "Oh, I'm Allison Lindum. I'm Joey's sister-in-law, and his girlfriend." she introduced herself, pointing at Coral when she came to the 'his girlfriend' part. "Dang, at this rate, every Lindum girl's gonna get herself a wrestler. First, Keri, now you." the Codemaster said. He was, of course, refering to Keri Lindum, the wife of Joey Malone and the woman that had been his manager for most of the latter stages of his career. Allison blushed, but soon, the quiet nature of this evening was about to be broken, for the third element of this roleplay was introducing herself right now. And yes, I did break kayfabe by saying the word "roleplay" in a roleplay. Screw you, RP feds! You taught me nothing! "Ohmigosh!" the voice of Leigh Landers shouted, as she caught a glimpse of whom the Codemaster was speaking to, and immediately hugged Coral as a greeting, "Coral! It's been so long!" Coral gagged, mainly because Leigh was cutting off his oxygen, and partially because he could see the look of jealousy on Allison's face. Of course, Allison, as of yet, had no way of knowing that she knew Leigh Morris Landers, until Leigh finally let go(allowing the blue on Coral's face to fade back to a decent pinkish colour), and turned toward Allison. "Oh, Leigh. I didn't recognize you with... uh... the dry blood and the stitches." Allison said, meekly. Of course, this didn't stop Leigh from nearly strangling her to death in the guise of a big hug. When she was satisified that all of the strangers had been hugged, Leigh smiled at them in her usual fashion, although the incident with Mitch Wilson had definitely hampered her abilities to look truly happy. So, to both Coral and Allison, she looked somewhat scary. "I haven't seen you two since Joey got married! How've you been?!" Leigh asked. "I, uh, I've definitely had an interesting year." Coral said. This was an understatement, of course, given the fact that Coral's fought an awful lot in the last year. Between Jeff Garvin and the Bantam title, getting fired from IWO, his large, dysfunctional, and affluent family; Allison, and his lack of any decent home. He had meant to ask if Frank Heimdal would be willing to let him stay in his halfway house for wayward wrestlers, but was a bit nervous to do so. "Oh, well, that's great!" Leigh said with a smile. She tapped the Codemaster in the shoulder. "Isn't that right?" Leigh asked him. "Oh, oh... right... right." he said. Allison and Coral simply stood around, glaring at these two. Coral merely shrugged, "Okay, I think I'd better be on my way." "Aww, really?" Leigh said, with a pout. "Well, I just wrestled and would like to find Lance, and possibly find some ice." Coral said, holding his neck upon the very mention of ice. "Well, okay then." Leigh said, with a small smirk. With that, Coral and Allison brushed past the two fWo wrestlers and wandered further down the hallways, presumedly where Lance Knight would be. Meanwhile, the Codemaster and Leigh Landers both shrugged and walked together in the opposite direction. So ended this odd meeting.
