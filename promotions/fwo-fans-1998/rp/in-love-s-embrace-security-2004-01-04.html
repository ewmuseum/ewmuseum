In love's embrace: Security

4 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 19th, 2003 Brandon Roche shuffled a bit. He ran his free hand through his hair as he moved his other arm. The weight on it was more reassuring than he imagined it would be. It was a weight he feared he'd never feel again either, life can be like that sometimes. Most of the time, if you asked some. He blinked the morning haze from his eyes as he scooted up in bed, inhaling deeply. The smell of musk and peaches filled the room. It made him smile. A big smile, a long one, a comfortable one. Amy Campbell moved a bit as Brandon got comfortable again. She draped her arm over his chest as she seemingly sunk back into her contented dream state. 'Seemingly' being the operative term. She was inching as close as possible to Roche, who was the warmest of them, because of the room's temperature stirring her uncomfortably awake - As the chill Brandon experienced when she rubbed icy toes along his calves demonstrated. Sensing he'd been awake even before the cold jolt, Amy sighed a contented sigh and fought to open her eyes, a feat almost too great when she'd grown so relaxed. But finally her goal came to light and the redhead gazed as high as her sleepy lids would allow. "Morning." She mumbled, smiling lazily. "Yeah, it is. A good one from where I'm sitting too." Brandon grinned, cheesey as it was. He rubbed his hand down her bare back, almost as if making sure he wasn't dreaming. "I suppose you're going to beg me to stay in bed and sleep the morning away with you?" His eyebrow cocked, Mr. Paragon was ready for Amy's normal early morning fight, especially after not going to sleep until the wee hours of the morning itself as it were. The redhead grinned. She was far too tired to fight as adamantly as usual, so biting her own lip with her sweetest grin would have to work for now. "Well why would you want to leave?" With that look Brandon looked down into her eyes, then to their bodies pressed together. Pulling himself away from that and peering once again into her miss-matched eyes, he found himself lost for words. "I know what might make you run, though..." Brandon had thought she was following up with some wise crack as usual, but instead he saw her cringing, "I think we should stick by the day we set for a wedding. It'd be too much, too upsetting to push it all back now just because of the things you're going to be putting behind you in a few days." That thought sent the big chill down Brandon's spine. He had just gotten comfortable with the thought of them together again, in his mind things were better, but still not where they should be for such a commitment. "I...well, I..um... I don't know if that's such a good idea." A chill shot up Amy's spine as well, only this one was even less pleasant. "We're not teenagers, most of the crap doesn't apply. We didn't even break up, Brandon...you went away to sort your head out, and at Countdown it's done." She spoke softly, but there was a definite anger welling up. How could he doubt them? "Unless there's...someone else." That wasn't a thought she needed! "Or something's happened with the kids..." He sighed, "No..no... nothing has happened with the kids dearheart. And you... you're my everything. There couldn't be anyone else." Brandon cupped her face in his hands. "Let's just distance ourselves away from all of this a bit. Just take some time to be us, a couple, getting back in the groove so to speak. And no, I'm not just trying to snowball you before you even ask, I fully plan on keeping my word. You're mine." "Getting used to one another again?" Amy sighed herself, "I'm pretty sure we took a nosedive into THAT pond last night, Brandon. Are you afraid something about me's changed? Don't let him hold us back from anything..." "Him who?" With that Brandon raised his eyebrows, indicating in a small way that it had very little to do with him and more to do with 'them'. "Let's just have fun. Give it a few weeks, as you just pointed out tonight, er... last night slash this morning is our first time together in any sense in almost a month. I think it'd be great if we wait until we can look at each other and not wonder if the other person is 'thinking too much'..." "Waiting for you to make your mind up isn't what I call fun." She pulled away from Roche momentarily, rolling to stare at the ceiling as she spoke. "I know what I want to do, and I've known since you asked me to. This time for 'fun' isn't for us, it seems to be for you." The chill between them, both literal and figuritive, wasn't comfortable at all. "It can be... if you let it. Stop looking so much into it. Half the stuff for the weddings been bought. And for the record sweetheart," Brandon said, rolling over half on to her. "I want you to be mine, my wife, my lover, my best friend. All the nice and corny stuff." "When you finally end it with Creid, can I finally be all of those things?" Brandon again stroked her face, "You are all those things already, we just need paper and things to make them official later, when it's us and not the business." Amy was only halfway contented by the answer, but gave up her fight much easier than usual. She was too worn to continue - it was a battle she couldn't win right now, but might see another day. Brandon could see that the conversation was far from over. Amy was just biding her time, waiting for a better time to plan their fairy tale life. He looked forward to it, even if she didn't believe him. With that thought Brandon rolled his weight over and on to his love. She had won one battle at least, the rest of the day would be spent in bed. [This has been an Aimz/ Brand Frontier collaboration.]
