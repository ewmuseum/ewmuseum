Blade Job - Chapter 3 - MacKay's Mortality

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*New York* Kevin MacKay was worried. No, worse than that... he was nearly shitting his pants with fear. For years Kevin MacKay had been a troubled soul. During the hardcore wrestling heyday, Kevin had been in a bus with roughly a dozen other wrestlers, traveling to Chicago for a rare ECW road trip. The company had called up "The Philly Killer" just hours before, telling him that the Blue Meanie had cancelled, and they were short a jobber. Would he like to go in Meanie's place, and travel a couple hours in a crowded bus to let New Jack puree him alive in front of a few dozen apathetic fans? You're damn right he would. So he got in the bus and they started the cold trip from New York to Chicago. MacKay remembers that much. Then there's the long, black moment in time that he simply can't remember... and then he was climbing out a ditch with snow in his mouth, in his ears, down the back of his pants. He had lost his shoes somewhere in the burning hulk of a bus that rested on it's back at the bottom of the ravine like a beached whale. Eleven wrestlers had died. He survived. And what did he do? He went on... He wrestled. The boys in the bus would have done the same thing, if it had been them that survived and not him. Him alone. Now, Kevin sat alone in his apartment. The uneasy feeling he had yesterday had settled in, gnawing at him all last night, until he had to make that call. Today, it was even worse. He paced his apartment, mostly with the worry that he had no idea why he felt this way. The IPW show was over and he had a good match with Malice. Nothing spectacular, mind you, but he managed to get some good technical wrestling out of the big brute before Malice decided to end the match with his trademark grunt, powerbomb and pin. In that order. MacKay sighed. It never ceased to amaze him that these big, slow guys could get so over with the fans. Oh well. Maybe he'll be booked to win next week. He looked down from his apartment window, listening to the wind hurl rain against the building. He needed to piss. Scowling at nothing in particular, Kevin glanced at the reflected lights of New York outside. One block over and several floors down, the arena's sign shimmered in puddles splashed by passing cars. The fWo sign was on the marquee, big as life and blinking it's garish self to the world, even though the show itself was still months away. He would be there soon, he reminded himself. He had a man to meet there, and then hopefully this nightmare would be over. Mackay moved to the bathroom, his gait surprising fluid for a man of his size. He emptied his bladder and washed his hands. One foot in the bathroom and one foot in his den, the wrestler felt a presence in the room. A cold wave of fear, like an icy dagger, chilled his spine. Ben? he wondered, just before a gloved hand grabbed his chin, wrenching back his jaw to expose his throat. The gleaming razor sliced him from ear to ear, cutting carotid arteries and jugular veins. It slit MacKay's esophagus, the steel scraping bone behind his Adam's apple. Bursts of silver light exploded in his brain as the killer grasped him by the hair and dragged him across the room. Gurgling, gasping, and drowning in blood, MacKay clawed at empty air while the killer used arterial spurts to spray-paint the walls. The last thing the wrestler saw before his mind shut down was his own reflection in the apartment windows. His own cut throat was a raw, bloody smile.
