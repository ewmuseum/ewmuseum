Vic Creed's My Immortal

29 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 25th, 2003, Christmas The sun is warm against my face, easing the chill from the winter breeze gently blowing against my skin. The snow's melting a little. I even saw a white rabbit running across the cemetery while I was walking from the parking lot. It's a beautiful day. My daughter deserved to be buried on a beautiful day. I'm so tired of being here Suppressed by all my childish fears And if you have to leave I wish that you would just leave 'Cause your presence still lingers here And it won't leave me alone The golden sunlight shining through the trees reminds me of her hair. When she was little, my sister would tie her hair into little ponytails. They were like tails from a comet, sparkling in the Heavens. It was like a cascading halo over my little angel. I hear that she had one lock that wasn't singed in the car fire. One single piece of golden hair, dirty with smoke and blood. That was all that was left of my angel's halo. Because of me. These wounds won't seem to heal This pain is just too real There's just too much that time cannot erase Maybe part of me expected Darryl to be bluffing. She was innocent. She was everything good in my life, everything I tried to lock away. Now she's gone. Forever. When you cried I'd wipe away all of your tears When you'd scream I'd fight away all of your fears I held your hand through all of these years But you still have All of me I always tried to protect her. Even when I wasn't there for her, I made sure she had everything she wanted. My sister raised Sarah, and did a good job of it, but she was still my baby. When Santa didn't deliver on the pony she asked for, I did. It didn't go over well with my sister, but she managed. When Sarah was sick, I paid the bills. When she needed clothes, I dressed her. I was in the shadows for most of her life, but I was always there with her. I cared for my daughter. I protected her. Today, I bury her. You used to captivate me By your resonating mind Now I'm bound by the life you left behind Your face it haunts My once pleasant dreams Your voice it chased away All the sanity in me The priest gave his little speech about her going to a better place. She's dead. There is no better place. The World was a better place with her in it. Now she's gone and everything's darker. The sunrise is a lie to me. The light is gone. The sky, as blue as her eyes, watches me. "How could you?" it asks in an unheard voice. I used to have answers. Now all I have is questions, and emptiness. These wounds won't seem to heal This pain is just too real There's just too much that time cannot erase The priest walks away as Sarah Creid is returned to the Earth. I'm alone with her now. There's so much I want to tell her, to explain, but I know she can't hear me anymore. Her heart broke once when she thought she'd lost me. My heart is with her now; it always was. My heart is being returned to the Earth. All I'm left with is Gold, and for the first time in my Life it isn't enough. I've tried so hard to tell myself that you're gone But though you're still with me I've been alone all along There's no reason to watch them shovel dirt on top of the casket, so I start walking back to the parking lot. My baby's gone, and all I have left are memories of her smile that melted me inside, her eyes that saw through my lies, and her halo that kept reminding me that there was still something Good in the World. I want to say she left something good inside me. But inside, I'm as dead as she is. [This interview posted on behalf of the handler of Vic Creed.]
