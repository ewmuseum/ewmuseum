Thai-Fighter - Part 1

14 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After hearing what Shygen told me only a short time ago, I started off with the basic training or jump rope, running laps and weight lifting. To my surprise, it wasn't enough for her and even she was surprised at how much I lacked in the beginning. For days on end, I could hear nothing but the sound of her voice, banging in the back of my head as I made a quick pass around the corner, with her in the car screaming at me with all her force behind her tone. I could only block it out as I look for a safe spot to rest. But with Shygen just seconds behind, a rest would be no longer then seconds, so I had to press on. The temple was on a few hundred yards away and with my legs already rubber I wondered if I could even make it past the next set of streetlights. "Fast Padrona. This is your country we are talking about," She would yell out as we continued down the road. My ever move being ripped apart by her so loving words of affection. I couldn't tell what made this situation so enjoyable. The fact that I was seeing my country and the fact I would fight for it, but there was no time to think about that, not while Thailand's most feared trainer stands behind you, barking orders out one after another. "Don't let up on me now. We have only just begun," were the words I long dread, but it was the truth. Stopping now would mean failure to perfect my skills and I would not allow that to happen. Not when I am this close to perfection and the achievements that will follow. But for now I could only run to the temple and hope I make it there in one piece. As we drew near to the temple, my eyes looked upward at the greatest structure this side of the boarder. This was no story or plot written out, but my true life unfolding in front of my eyes. As we reached the doors, Shygen pulled up along side me as I tried to breathe in precious air into my already damaged lungs. She smiled for once and pointed towards the large doors, which we lead me to my quest. "Inside you will find what you have came to see," she said, leading me forward till I could reach out and touch the cool metal of the temple door. "You must make a choice now. Enter and we continue. Leave and we never speak of you again. It's your decision." I took a few glances at the doors and back down the streets of Thailand. My mind raced with excitement, but was filled with confusion. Shygen open one of the doors and walked inside, almost knowing I would follow. I took one last look at what I was fighting for, and entered the temple, of what some would call, the road to perfection.
