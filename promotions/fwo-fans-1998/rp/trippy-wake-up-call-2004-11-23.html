Trippy Wake-up Call

23 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"It's all I can do. No sense in letting myself go crazier." Vodka martinis tonight. Yesterday was a funeral. Let's forget it, shall we? Amy rubbed her eyes and stared blankly at the laptop. More e-mail...more e-mail...more 'I'm sorry', more sympathies and regrets and regards and blahblahblah. 'My brother died. Go away, asshole!' Then, mail that mattered. An old friend who caught up like nothing had happened. She always had occasional phone contact with him, but never anything like this... hello it said and it started out fine, right? hi amy...hey...har... but who knows what this MGD will do to me in time okay, about the crazy thing. like where I said I'm crazy. it's true. my little blue dog, he tells me what to do. okay you got me it's not that crazy. but maybe it's worse since it's so real, tied to reality and existing in the shadows of reality, visible on the very edges of reality, perceivable only to those with a heightened sense of whether or not they're being fuycked. fucked too. earlier today, it came back, I was driving home and my mother wanted me to help her paint the kitchen, as in the walls, but I was in the mood to paint on a canvas, as in art, and if I don't get to do what I really want to be doing for more than a day or two, I start thinking, how could this be happening, here I am just wanting to learn how to paint, how to get my ideas out in paint form, and that's all I really choose to care about for the time being, and yet two days can actually pass where I don't even get to touch a canvas. if I had any sort of control now, and I mean a decent amount, things would be reorganized, prioritized, possibly even alphabetized if I got that efficient- but I'm not in control, I'm the guy in charge of sweeping up after the party, and I'm allowed to keep anything I find on the ground. you know how cool people just get out of bed and, assuming they don't have to go right to work that day, start doing whatever it is they love to do? I don't have that option. I start sweating whether or not I'm going to be able to make it through this day to begin with, and once I get over the nightmares I had, and stop dreading all the things I have to do that I don't want to do, I think, out of all the things I could do with my free time, which one is most likely to get me somewhere? hopefully somewhere far away from here, and that goes for several planes... then I remember all the guilty pleasures I could almost immediately indulge in that would make me temporarily forget about all this crap- and the battle begins. oh, and sometime after that, I have an actual day. I've been thinking about getting a lizard lately, maybe a greek tortoise. or a chameleon. something that's kool to watch, and that wouldn't mind me picking it up once in a while. and maybe putting it in a little maze that I made for it. with some ramps and stuff. and maybe some food at the end for motivation. at this store today, they have like bongs and patches and stickers and beads and posters and t-shirts and exotic pets etc. and there's this tarantula as big as my hand called a bird eater, in this tiny little plastic thing with no places to hide, and right next to it is this mouse the size of a... I can't think of what it was the size of, but it was tiny, like a baby, and it was just sitting there with its head buried, shaking. who knows? who knows? these thing happen all the time, apparently. hooked up the ol' texas instruments TI-99 4A in my room the other day. yep, got about ten games for it, including, but not limited to, Burgertime, Pole Position, Amazing, Blasto, and Hustle. and so I play it a bit, now and then. it passes the time and keeps me high. we do things like that, little things that we think are gonna be so cool, and maybe they are for a while, but maybe we're over-rating them, maybe we're giving ourselves better scores than we deserve because we wish things really were that exciting. but a year later and it's usually gone, and then you remember it, and it turns out that it's only worth remembering if someone is there to remember it with you. or were there when it happened. goddamit salinger there was this patch that said "fuck you you fucking fuck" and I thought about getting it, sewing onto somewhere, a jacket maybe, and that would be the jacket that I wear on days like this one, when I'm sure that everyone is a fucking fuck. last night I was so high that, when my buddy was describing this crazy near accident he had on the freeway, I suddenly tried to imagine my body slamming into a concrete wall at seventy miles an hour, and I really started picking up on something, completely zoning out of the conversation, and then, I felt it. what it would be like to be dying. not just the pain, but the realization too. I don't know which part was worse, but it scared more than anything I can remember at this moment. it wasn't just like, "okay, now try to imagine a wall coming at you at seventy miles an hour" and then you watch this movie in your mind of a person exploding after being hurled into a wall, and you say yeah that would suck whatever. I figured it out. I mean I pieced together all the actual feelings I've ever had that would be relevant and did a damn good job of multiplying them to the necessary levels of pain, fear, and regret. I don't know, I've been thinking about it a lot. when my body was lying all curled up and mangled after the impact, and the two minutes that I had to endure before anyone came to my side to say "hold on, just hold on" were seeming like an eternity longer than the eternity that has been my life so far, and my body was throbbing with a pain so intense and complete that I actually wished I would just die... I don't know what I was thinking. I wasn't ready for it. there were some things. but there are no things. there are only feelings. the feeling of being human, I was going to miss that. there's so much more we could be doing, and here I am dying at twenty-five. I guess it was all a dream. Ha ha. Get it? I don't. Sometimes I lay down to go to sleep, and I'm reviewing the day I just had, and I think, what if there actually is a god, as in, the all powerful, ultra intelligent kind, the kind who controls everything that there is, and yet at the same time, for some reason gives us free will, or what we believe to be free will, and he just witnessed, in his god-like way, everything that happened to me since I woke up, every little thought I had, knowing full well everything that has happened to me since I've been "born" or whatever that's all about, and of course he would have this plan that is beyond our understanding, even beyond our understanding of why it's beyond our understanding, and anyway, I give him a little piece of my mind. like "fuck you, you fucking fuck." I'm playing host to a trillion germs. some is airborn. some is just learning how to fly. so there. fuck the music industry too. somebody ruins everything. you can run red lights in Detroit as long as there ain't no cops around. can ye do that now that you're a big star? ya didn't think so did ya... I hate Detroit. I love it. I hate it I love it I hate it I love it I hate it I love it I hate it I love it nope. hate it here it is, this letter is fine. you just got that tattoo of the dragon/ cause it was a grand idea, only a grand person who is capable of flying away on a dragon someday would have thought of it/ it's easy to believe/ a book I had, when I was a youngster, and here it goes this kid went to school and on the way home he found a baby dragon, and he says mom can I keep him, and after much hesitation its all right, and the dragon stays in the boy's room, and perches on the end of his bed, and he's a very tame dragon, and now the boy's off to school, and mum feeds the dragon some pancakes, lots of pancakes, and the dragon is growing fast, in a couple days he's too big to fit in the boys room, eats hundreds of pancakes, chases a bread truck down the street, gets stuck in the house with arms and legs sticking out the window, I don't remember what happened to the dragon after that. I'm sure he was fine. don't cry little girl. but if you were crying, if you had to cry, I'd lick your tears up like an animal would. we are animals. I got my tattoo today, come to think, and it's titled "organic tattoo by a metalhead named jeff who just woke up off the couch." you can tell. i miss yours. they all seem to count. this is a tense moment. we don't know what's going to happen. it could be over here, or over there. many possibilities are being considered. they might try and take it from us. or they could simply pressure us until it's no longer of any use. the decision is being made as we speak. it's no longer in our hands. if we objected, it would buy us a few minutes at the most. we need free reign; total control. it's the only way the project can be completed. if we lose this connection we have, we'll have to find another way. the subjects are getting restless. I'm afraid that if we stop now, they may lose all interest, and the project would have to be terminated. livelivelive this is useless and i know it That intoxicated online rambling meant something. She remembered her friend fondly - he'd actually lived with her brother for a short time when they both moved to the states. One of her only real friends. One of a few select thinkers left in her circle. What the hell WAS her circle, again? Instead of being a complacent and afraid housewife, Amy needed to break out again. Not break up, break out. That meant becoming a bit of her old self again, right? Brandon had been suggesting it for months. Even he noticed her trying too hard to be some form of perfection in personal life. But how does one go about reverting like that? I guess...no....yeah. Her brother wasn't there to lean on, and her friends were busy rambling. She didn't want to become either of those - dead, or as stunned and dependent as poor J seemed to be turning. Maybe it's time to start thinking for yourself again? Or maybe this was another concept soon to fail. Hopefully - definitely - not.
