Your Friendly Neighborhood Lunatic's WebBlog - #1 - the FWO returns.

2 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome all to the FWO of new. I just wish to welcome you all back to a product I am deeply proud of. Over the coming weeks, months, years, I hope to give you all a backstage look into the events that have crafted this fine organization, and my continuing involvement in not only the Fans Wrestling Organization, but the professional wrestling business in general. My name is Jack Harmen, but most people know me by my ring name, High Flyer. I've wrestled all over the world(except Antarctica, DAMN YOU PENGUINS!), and here, I shall share my experience and knowledge to all those willing to listen. But today, on the rebirth of the organization I love so dearly, I would like to share my thoughts on the independent movie "The Wrestler", starring Mickey Rourke and Marissa Tomei. I was invited to an advanced screening in Los Angeles, California, two months before the movie was set to be released to the general viewing public, and I must say, I was deeply enthralled and excited to see our business treated with such respect. Never before have I seen OUR world crafted with such realistic bravado and stunning clarity as Darren Aronofsky‘s "The Wrestler." Before I even start, I highly recommend going to the theaters and giving money to these fine filmmakers. Granted, there’s numerous layers of people being paid that don’t deserve it, but four cents for a dvd purchase is not enough compensation to the brilliance that is “The Wrestler.” And for Darren Aronofsky to be fighting for us, questioning why we, as professional wrestlers are not contracted to the Screen Actor’s Guild as stunt men, well, I can only raise my glass high and say, “Here here!” The wrestling business is not without it's share of casualties and tragedies, and Randy Robinson exemplifies this like no other. He's a man I can relate to. Someone who's chosen this business because this business is the only place where he can feel at home. Where diving off the top rope and being shot with a staple gun makes more sense than cutting meat at a deli counter. And like many other professional wrestlers, Randy has lost a lot of what made him a person outside of the ring, and can only cling to the fading career slipping through his finger tips. I'm a lot like the Rammer. I put this business before my family years ago, and it ended up in a messy divorce from my ex-wife Kate Young. So much so that she hid a daughter from me for a year and a half. And all I could do was cling to was my work. My life as High Flyer took greater importance over my life as Jack Harmen. I may not be "crazy," as my character would incline you to believe, but I certainly took on a split personality, one of euphoric joy and enthusiastic exuberance, versus a desolate sad misery of an existence. And when the FWO closed it's doors in 2006 after the messy riots of Countdown, I was at a loss for months. Not only did I have to deal with a sticky divorce, but I battled other legal worries during my contracted time to the FWO that forced me away from the ring, forced me away from what I love. And I felt pieces of myself dying inside with every moment. I felt like Randy, looking up to the rafters for the glimmer of hope, heart pounding in my chest, only wishing for death to alleviate this nuisance of a pain. But friends, friends conquer all else. In this business, you need people close to you to keep you grounded to reality. Randy didn't have a tag team partner who was closer than a brother. He didn't have a protégé that reminded him of a daughter. He only had the sport. He only had himself. Now? Team VIAGRA enters the FWO, and I find myself surrounded by loved ones for the first time since the Legion of Dairy's treacherous turn in 2004 against me. Which, reminds me, I owe EGG nog & cHEESE two swift kicks to the jugular. So, if you see those Count Douchingtons, please, inform me which direction to send the locomotive, and I will promptly crash into their craniums. Because while I may have found love and joy in my life, both in and out of this ring, I still hold my grudges. I still house anger capable of destroying nations, and when the FWO returns to weekly wrestling performances, I may have reservations... I may wish to repent for my past sins, but I will not stop on my quest for vengeance, for glory... for love... And I will leave none left standing in my wake. Even if it kills me.
