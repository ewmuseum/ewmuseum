Not to be faint of heart.

12 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Second day home, and Amy couldn't help but feel 'down'. She'd always found it depressing to be in any major routine, and loved the travel her job allowed. Unfortunately she was also in no mood for people, so when she needed to go to Wednesday Warfare for one of the few matches she had booked this month, the redhead got in and back out as quickly as she could. It's not just that she wanted to avoid one person, but all of them; She'd grown much more uneasy in public since her life had started being almost put on parade by snoopy reporters - not to mention the threatening note she'd received on New Year's Eve, just after Brandon had kissed her. Everything had her keeping to her own moreso than usual, though she hadn't minded the few talks Ivy'd almost forced upon her these last few days. All things considered, Amy wasn't exactly at ease when she stepped out of her bedroom considerably early in the "morning" to find a pair of shoes just inside her appartment door. She didn't have time to think before the intruder identified themself. "Amy, it's only two thirty in the afternoon...God forbid you be awake!" A short girl who looked to be around Aimz' age called to her from the couch. The familiar face didn't put her mind to ease in the least - it was one of her older sisters. Sisters meant family, family meant a barrage of questions...questions Amy didn't want to answer. She couldn't bring herself to really even acknowledge her thoughts, though. It was way too early - at least for someone who normally doesn't even look at her bed until seven, eight or nine o'clock each morning. The younger of her older sisters was always bright and cheery, even though she did have a temper and a 'potty mouth' to put Amy's frequent cursing to shame. About 5'2", she didn't exactly look formidable but, as Amy had learned long ago, she was a force to be reckoned with if you woke her up early, and she was lucky her little sister had never been quite the same - the redhead was always rather calm after waking up, usually just too tired to kick and rave. "Paula..." Amy's grogginess still held her at bay, her voice was softer and sounded almost younger than usual. "....wait a se-----when did I give you a key to my appartment?!" Aimz' realization caused the older girl to laugh aloud, "You didn't. Mom has one!" "And she gave it t--------when did I give HER a key?!" Another wonder took Amy back a bit. Her mother could be the most meddling force on earth when it came to her youngest daughter's life, as everyone around her was well-aware. "You didn't." Paula laughed again, brushing a strand of a frizzy mass of off-colored hair behind her ear. "Darcy gave it to her before he moved out. That guy was a real dick..." Amy thought through it; Leave it to Darcy to open her up to eternal hell before he ran off. "Dick isn't the word for it...if I ever see that b---" "Temper, temper!" She'd watched her little sister's face go suddenly red. "Anyway...everyone wantsta' see you! Mom sent me to pick you up - she knew you'd be all day getting there if she didn't send someone." "And if she came herself she'd get an earfull." Aimz chuckled to herself. "Or maybe she just didn't want to find a married man on your couch." She'd meant it entirely in jest...but Amy didn't see things that way. Instantly, the redhead shoved the smaller woman backward, pointing at her with a hand shaken by sudden rage. "You shut the fuck up about that, okay?" Paula didn't need to use any sisterly intuition to guess she'd hit quite the touchy nerve; Amy was rarely physical with any of her siblings, she'd always been the 'bad apple' but remained as close to her family as she'd been as a toddler...so this was quite the event. The elder quickly apologized, actually mirroring the same words her younger sister was speaking simultaneously: "Look, I'm sorry..." Aimz hadn't expected to be touched with a bit of sisterly wisdom just then, however. She cocked her head in wonder as Paula corrected her apology in a firm tone, telling her "You've been apologizing for everything lately, what's up with that? I had no business running my mouth right there, you don't have to apologize for anything. I don't know why you're so mild and mannerly suddenly, but stop. I remember when you wouldn't take shit from anyone...don't let that change or you're gonna get stepped on." "You mean..." "You've gone soft? Yep." "Seriously?" Amy stuttered a bit, staring a hole into the floor. "Shit...life got to me more than I knew." "It has...and you do not wanna turn out like Colleen." She laughed over their elder sister who, upon marriage, had given up beer for wine and fun for rich brunches. "Stay true to who you are, don't let people control your moods. You're probably gonna punch me for this, but don't take no for an answer...and you know what I'm talking about." Funny, that was the second time she'd heard that same advice in 48 hours... Why didn't she just put it to use, though? She'd changed too much lately, too many people had torn down the sheild of anger and pride she'd veiled herself in. Before she could do anything, she needed to reconstruct that shelter.
