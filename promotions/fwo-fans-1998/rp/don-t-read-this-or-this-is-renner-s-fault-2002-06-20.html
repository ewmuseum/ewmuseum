Don't read this (or, "This is Renner's Fault")

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Attacked. The single word played in Jon Crisp's mind. It gnawed at him, ate at him like a vulture toying with its felled feast. In another word, this is how he felt. Betrayed. Perhaps it wasn't Donaven Winters' fault. Perhaps this dumb, ugly whatever-it-was really had a firm grasp on his brain, his soul, his elbow, whatever. Perhaps Crisp should feel sorry for his lost friend, his once-upon-a-time ally. Or, perhaps he should beat Grendel's control out of "Omega", and turn "Omega" back to Donaven. That sounded like a much better plan, Crisp thought to himself. That sounded wonderful. It fits my needs, moves me further to my goal, which is... He was stopped. There was a knock at the door. It was a reporter. "Hello, Jon, Todd Newton, E! Entertainment," began Mr. Newton. There was a whammy standing with him, and a cameraman. "Er..." was the only reply Jon could muster. "I wanted to know a few things, if you please." "I...can't," Jon said reluctantly, looking at the whammy. "I've had too many reporters asking me this, or that. No more reporters, please." Jon began to close the door. However, he was stopped. A red hand, not cloth-like, but rather leathery, as if this was actual skin, gripped his arm. Jon looked up...and found the whammy, grinning at him. Well, grinning at him is rather a helpless description, because, really, the whammy can't change his expression. All it can do is make a laughing sound and grin. So, this is what it did. "Oh, I anticipated your reaction," said Todd. "Now, do you want to Press Your Luck?" Newton laughed maniacally, and the whammy tightened its grip on Crisp's arm. How in the hell did I get wrapped into this, thought Crisp. He had no idea, but he knew how to get himself out of such a jam. "I said, no...more...reporters." Crisp grabbed the whammy and punched it in the head. The whammy fell back, against the wall. He bounced off the wall, into Jon's waiting arms. Crisp lifted the whammy up, and tossed him head-first into the door across the hall. The whammy made pained sounds, much like he does on his game show. Newton looked up. Crisp was pouncing on him. Crisp grabbed Newton and put him in a headlock. He called attention to the cameraman. "Say it, Newton," barked Crisp. "You're watching E!" whined Newton. "Not that, idiot." "iMLB...he may have a shaved head, but he won't have sex with your daughter yet!" "No, not that. Come on, tell me what everyone wants to hear." "PARTY AT JAMES' HOUSE!" "What?" "Um...I'm gay!" "I didn't care to know that. Tell the camera what I want to hear." "You're going to be the next Internet Champion!" "Do you stake your reputation on it?" Newton's face was turning a bright red, as Crisp put more and more pressure around his neck. "YES! I do!" Crisp dropped Netwon, who crawled around the floor, looking for oxygen to gasp. "I'm going to be the next Internet champion, and your reputation sucks anyway," said Crisp. He slammed the door behind him.
