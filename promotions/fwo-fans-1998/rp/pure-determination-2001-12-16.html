Pure Determination

16 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In less than one week, Monte Burns and Donaven Winters will have finally, once and for all settled their differences. One of these two men would be left standing all alone in the ring, victorious after three and a half brutal months of one of the most personal confrontations either men had been apart of. The mental damage that Donaven had sustained in the previous months had nearly driven him back into mental therapy again. He had strongly considered it, but he was strong enough to go about this on his own. Everything he had feared had been brought back. Monica Davidson, came back trying to ruin his life, just so Burns could be happy. He was happy, but Donaven wasn't out. For the first time in his entire life he wasn't going to give up. From his father's beatings, to attempting suicide, to just plain not giving a damn anymore, he finally cared. He finally cared about life and about himself. Monte Burns had awakened a giant that had been dormant since November 20th, 1976. At Twilight, Burns was going to see that the giant inside of the poor boy from Detroit was awake and seeking fresh blood. He was finally realizing this as he sat in his locker room inside of the Delta Center. He had the lights off, door locked. He was staring up at the ceiling he couldn't see, eyes wide open. Some light seeped in underneath the door. The only sound was the occasional sound of movement outside of the door. And every few minutes the heating system would kick in making a light humming sound. The frown that had almost always been pasted onto his face was turned slightly askew into a sort of smile. His thoughts ran back and forth between Monte Burns and Monica Davidson. One more week. He thought. One more week and I can finally claim victory over the rich boy. He deserves everything that he has coming to him next Saturday. I'm going to send him into a world of hurt, he'll bleed. I'll laugh as I hear the sound of bone and sinew cracking between my own hands. Burns... vengeance is mine. You started this entire mess. First over the title, then you fucked with my life more than Monica could do alone. You're going to pay hard. You will regret ever bringing Monica into all of this. I promise you this. What had once been a quiet, reserved individual, Donaven had become slightly bloodthirsty. Almost if he was searching for malevolence. This new attitude had turned Winters into a completely different man. A man that had been waiting for something dramatic like this entire situation to happen. Almost as if he had found the Donaven Winters that he was just over eighteen months ago. He was filled with the determination to succeed and strive. He was looking for blood, he would find it at Twilight in the Last Man Standing match. Too bad for Monte Burns that Donaven Winters had found his will, his way. He had found everything he had longed for. He found himself, he found Pure Determination. The Fallen Angel had motivation once again, and Saturday was going to be a bloody mess.
