Needless Sacrifice

28 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 15th, 2009 Post-FWO ReAction Chronos walked through the hallway, his bag rolling behind him. Skin splotched from the heat and exertion, he didn't bother to notice - or even care - how everyone either turned from him or stared him down. He was marked, he knew it - but he just couldn't summon up the depth of emotion to bother to defend himself from the unspoken accusations and recriminations of those in the locker room. Absently, his hand massaged his temple, a slight wince occurring when he reached through his hair to touch the battered segments of his skull. The wide berth afforded him by the other wrestlers suited him just fine in some ways, allowing him time to think - something that he hadn't had since he began his dual life in the reformed fWo. His actions as killjoy and his "job" as Senior Road Agent/Head of Security had made for many busy nights - it was a relief to leave right after his match for once, to spend time trying to figure out what he was going to do now that everything had blown up in his face again. Shoving open the outside doors, Chronos looked either way for fans, and seeing none, moved forward towards the edge of the arena sidewalk, looking for a taxi. If he had looked a little hard, though, he might have noticed the cause of his recent pain sitting on top of a stack of boxed crates, taking in the warm Hawaiian sun. Perched like his namesake, the predatory Krow looked down at Chronos, hopping down from his seat with a deadpan expression on his face. Chronos, hearing the ‘thud’ of his footsteps, turned curiously and eyed the daunting Hardcore Champion; but instead of preparing for battle, Chronos lowered his gaze and simply averted his eyes towards the streets. “Here to finish the job?” Chronos impassively asked with a sigh, which coincided with the sudden reminding throb on his face…the very area which Krow’s knee had struck just hours ago. “You could say that,” Krow calmly retorted as his footsteps grew closer. Chronos closed his eyes and did not say a word. He accepted his consequences silently as he dropped his bag and left his arms to loosely dangle at his sides. Then the footsteps stopped. Chronos awaited his punishments, but received none. After a long lull, Chronos opened his eyes once more and noticed the Satanic God standing beside him stoically, looking out towards the beautiful Hawaiian skies. Chronos opened his mouth to question Krow’s intent, but he was cut off by his raspy voice. “Allow me to apologize, Ric.” Chronos narrowed his eyes in confusion, as Krow turned his head slightly to acknowledge the legend’s presence. “We attacked you with blind rage, yet offered no explanation. Though you may hinder our cause, a man who puts so much of his life into this business… deserves the courtesy of an elucidation.” Chronos lowered his gaze unemotionally. “I know the reason. It’s the same reason everyone will have for attacking me. I accept the consequences of my actions.” Krow shook his head disappointingly and looked back out towards the rising moon in the Hawaiian sky. “And what actions have you done to me, that would constitute revenge?” Krow questioned as he peered from the corners of his eyes and scanned Chronos’ forehead. “Though your body is riddled in injuries and bruises…I have yet to see any ‘stab wounds’ upon your forehead. Therefore, by process of elimination, I can assume that you aren’t the man I brutalized a few weeks ago.” Chronos’ eyebrows arched in confusion as he brought his eyes up towards the Satanic God. “And, to be honest, if anything, it is killjoy who I am expecting to seek revenge. So no, Ric, your masked endeavors aren’t what triggered our seemingly blind attack upon you,” Krow commented with an amused smirk. “Then…what the hell was it about?” Chronos asked, annoyance starting to edge his voice. Krow’s eyes still admired the beauty of the skies. “Because you are a representation of this company’s folly.” “What?” Krow turned softly towards Ric and stared into his listless eyes. “Ric Chronos is a legend to the FWO alumni, is he not?” Ric lowered his gaze again. “I’m not so sure anymore.” “And that is what I mean. Here I stand, speaking to a man who has held numerous championships, sacrificed years off of his life, and shunned his family to the side all for the good of this company; and how do they repay you?” Krow asked rhetorically. “By allowing you to rot in your own self-pity.” “I don’t blame anyone for what’s to come.” “And that is your problem. Somehow, someway, you’ve allowed this wretched beast to enter your mind and make you feel as if you deserve all of the brutality that may come your way,” Krow curled his lip and sharply turned towards the streets. “Little known fact about me, Ric…I, too, once wore a mask.” Ric turned to listen. “And with that mask, I ignited war with all of my peers. I brutalized bodies, destroyed relationships, and annihilated the good and sane of that company. I was a lone devil in a city of weak angels. And do you know what happened to me when I removed my mask?” Krow asked, as Ric listened on. “Nothing. Oh, sure, they all tried to attack and attain vengeance – but without that mask, I felt more powerful than ever before. And while I caused even more anarchy without that mask than I did with it, you stand here in self-depression, accepting a fate that can be changed.” “That mask did not give you any powers, Ric. It simply allowed you to share the consequences with another. And now, for the first time in a long time, you are a lone wolf; you have no pack. There is no 3vil, there is no World Peace Organization, and there is no killjoy. Like the FWO, you felt safe in numbers – but you are weak alone,” Krow calmly stated. “Where the hell are you going with all of this?” asked an increasingly agitated Ric. Krow smiled amusingly. “Well, I want to mentally prepare you for our match.” “Our match? What match?” “The match that will take place two weeks from now,” Krow’s grin grew. “It seems as though your company is giving you the opportunity for revenge themselves. And if that is the case, I do not want to waste my time on facing a stoic corpse; a shadow of a man. I want to face the man behind the mask. I want to face the killjoy that caused mayhem. I want to face the terrorizing beast that reigned over this company for months.” Ric stood firmly silent. “I want to destroy you, just as I want to destroy this company…” Krow turned to walk away, but stopped directly behind Ric and spoke over his shoulder. “…at its strongest,” he chuckled lowly. “After all, what fun is it to poke a stick at a dead body, when you could light a living man on fire?”
