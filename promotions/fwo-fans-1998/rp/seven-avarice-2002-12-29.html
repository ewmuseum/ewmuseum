Seven: Avarice

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Greed is the desire for material wealth or gain, ignoring the realm of the spiritual." Ric looked her in the eyes, fixing her with his stare. "I think it's you that doesn't understand." "Well, then," Lira snapped back, "Why don't you try explaining it to me again." "Simple. What happened, happened. It's over. I'm not going to put my life on hold just for one mistake." Lira sighed, her exasperation growing. Nearby, she watched as one of the stagehands began pulling out items that, although unrecognizable now, would soon end up as part of the ring. The entire arena was a flurry of activity, and it had taken an extraordinary effort for her to keep Ric apart from it long enough to try and talk sense to him. She hadn't really wanted to leave Dallas, but Ric had refused to even consider the notion of not making Countdown. "What you don't understand, mister, is that if this happens again, you'll end up losing yourself, and then you won't even have a life to put on hold - just like your father." Ric opened his mouth, anger flushing him, but before he could speak, he decided to change his course of action. Setting his chin, he just glared at Lira. "What? Can't argue that point, can you? The bottle killed your father, and I don't want to see you ending up like him - dead, and alone." "I... am... nothing... like... him." Ric replied through clenched jaw and gritted teeth. "I am something he never was - a champion. I've dedicated my life to being the best, and I am - at least, I'm better than he ever was. And soon, I'll have the World Title back, and again I'll have the proof that I'm better than him." "Is this what this entire thing is about? You have a relapse, and instead of seeking help, you're looking for a shiny gold trinket? You're planning on going out there tonight and calling out some kid - a kid who outweighs you, is faster than you, and is at least as strong - all because you want to prove to everyone that you can still be a better wrestler than a dead man?" "No, Lira. I'm going to put Frontier back in his place, not just for any little trinket, not just for a belt, but for *my* belt. That title still rightfully belongs to me, and I will possess it again - I'm the only one who can bring respect back to that title, and I can only do that if I have a clear shot - and to do that, I have to put people back in line. That's just the way of the world, and has nothing to do with my father." "Well... well.. just be that way. I don't know how long I can watch you do this to yourself, though. So promise me one thing." "What?" "Promise me, that if whatever it is you have planned doesn't work out, that for once - just once, you'll do as I say. You'll start going to the AA meetings? You'll make an effort, at least?" Ric was struck back for a moment - the request was an odd one for someone as in control of their life as Ric was, but it didn't matter. Tonight, he was certain that he'd be able to goad Frontier right into the position Ric needed him to be, and that shortly thereafter, his precious belt would be back into his hands. So, what did the promise matter? It wasn't like he was going to lose. Besides, he'd say anything to get his shot at the gold, and if it didn't work - well, he'd try something else. After all, that title really *was* his by rights... "Sure. I promise."
