My day in Yankland: Lunchtime

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
...fffffzzzzz... - 12.57 - The camera kicked into action with Borst's face filling the screen. "It's about lunchtime now, and here we are, out on the streets of... some... place... in Yankland." He turned the camera round and panned across the busy street, where cars were flying past, yellow cabs stopping here there and everywhere, and there were people. Lots and lots of people. "Fascinating creatures..." Borst said as he continued to pan the camera from left to right. "Get the fuck out of the way, can't you see I'm filming." he muttered as the hundreds of inconsiderate people on the street continued to walk past and in front of him. Some people eh? "Right, I'm not really sure where to begin with this research..." ...fffffzzzzz... ...fffffzzzzz... - 12.59 - Suddenly, the camera is shoved in the face of middle-aged man. He didn't look too pleased, but to make sure he didn't get away, Borst had grabbed the man's arm. "What's this?" Borst asked. "What's this we're walking on?" The man looked a bit confused, and answered. "It's the sidewalk." Borst quickly let go of the man's arm and turned the camera around so that his face filled the screen. "What the fuck?!" he exclaimed, before turning the camera around to face the man again. "PAVEMENT." Borst said in the most retarded voice he could manage. "PAVEMENT." Shortly afterwards, the man quickly scampered off. "Weirdo." Borst muttered to himself. "Fucking weirdo." ...fffffzzzzz... ...fffffzzzzz... - 13.05 - "WHO ATE ALL THE PIES?! WHO ATE ALL THE PIES?!" The camera was directed at a now nervous obese man walking down the street. "YOU FAT BASTARD! YOU FAT BASTARD!" bellowed Borst at the top of his voice. The obese subject of the taunt turned towards Borst and began to run at him, shouting profanities at him, but quickly gave up and walked off in the opposite direction. "Lazy fuck." ...fffffzzzzz... ...fffffzzzzz... - 13.12 - The camera turned on once more in a noisy environment. It was unclear as to where Borst was this time, largely due to the fact he still had the lens cap on. "Well as you can see, I'm in a bar." Borst said. "I can't see it through... aha, lens cap off, you can see the bar." Borst pointed the camera at the pretty boy bartender, who winked at the camera. "Don't do that please." Borst asked politely. "It makes you look like a fucking bender." The camera turned around and the familiar shot of Borst's face appeared once more. "Seeing as it's lunchtime, I'm going to order some food in this strange land." And the camera turns to the bartender. "What food do you have here?" Borst asked. "Umm... nuts?" the bartender replied, pushing the bowl on the bar towards our cameraman, Borst. "Nuts? What kind of a pub doesn't do a nice pub lunch?!" Borst replied. "I'm sorry sir but..." "Fuck off." ...fffffzzzzz... ...fffffzzzzz... 13.15 The camera was pointed at a scrawny timid guy sitting alone in the corner, drinking a glass of orange juice. Every so often, a peanut would hit him on the head, causing him to occasionally glance in the direction of the camera. "Come on you little shit!" Borst bellowed. The camera turned around to show Borst sitting at the bar next to an empty bowl. "Even the skinny ones are lazy..." ...fffffzzzzz... ...fffffzzzzz... - 13.23 - Borst's face appeared once more. "A very enlightening experience so far... I think. Possibly pointless, possibly not. Ah what the fuck, it's only lunchtime. Plenty of time to run wild in the land of opportunity." Borst paused for a moment and thought hard. "Land of opportunity... hmmm, but to do what?" ...fffffzzzzz...
