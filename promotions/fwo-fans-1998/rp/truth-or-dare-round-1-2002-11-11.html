Truth or Dare - Round 1

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday November 11th, 2002 11:03pm Home Everything had come crashing down just hours ago. It began with a journalist and her sneaking into Amy's past... It ended with an elaborate lie, loosely based on truth. Aimz was sitting on her couch, with the entire appartment empty and in practical darkness...just sitting there, silently re-reading the well-written slander. Hello and welcome to the first edition of my little editorial... Recently, the mainstay of wrestling (A form of "entertainment" that I watch corrupting all of your children, and even the more mature adults.), dubbed fWo, re-opened it's doors...bringing with it a world of controversy, drama and generally immoral ways. I, of course, am taking it as my sole duty to reveal to the world exactly how disgusting fWo really is, and what these people you allow to influence your sons and daughers are really like. It's 3 AM as I sit, jotting these words down. Why am I not in bed, warm and comfortable? Well, I've been doing some research lately...and my findings are far too unsettling to sleep on. I know you're wondering what I've found in my journey to expose wrestling for the cess pool it really is so I'll name my first "victim", rather, a young lady who's proven again and again to be a bad influence on all of us. Drinking, use of hallucenogenic drugs and punk rock "music" are the name of the game... But the name of the proprietor is Amy Campbell. That's right, the poor little girl everybody's pitying these days...what's her crime? Aside from the obvious few hundred, she's a mother. Mhmm, the 20 year-old girl has a son...a son just rounding the ripe age of 4, at that. Little, innocent Aimz was having sex at 17? That and more...she's got a criminal record a mile long dating as far back as 15 years old. Everything ranging from vandalism, possession and even attempted trafficing of illegal substances... Now she's a mother, too? Is THAT the sort of impression your teenaged daughter needs, to open her legs as soon as she hits 16? Because that's not the only message Amy's past and present is sending...if anything, your little girl would learn open her legs with a beer in her hand and a pill in the other, just as she's getting ready to have only God knows what area of her body pierced. Oh, and did I mention that, obviously, she's also a poor mother? Her child currently resides with Beverly and Forrester Campbell, Amy's dear parents. She sees her little boy maybe once a month, and most-likely under the influence each and every time...not to mention that, at any time, her son can flick on the TV and watch mommy-dearest attacking random people, cursing and dressing like she lives in a cardboard box. This is the sort of "good example" fWo allows among them, the same person they pay to influence your children... I rest my case. By the time she'd gotten a hold of the article, it was in every possible medium across North America, most-likely. By the time her boyfriend, who hadn't ever heard the word 'Child' so much as uttered by Amy, got a hold of the article, he was on his way out of the appartment as fast as he could go, lest he do something he might have regretted. Unfortunately, he had...he'd had a few too many drinks and, by the time he returned, Amy was in no mood to speak to. She began the moment he stepped inside the door frame, sarcastically snuffing "Great to see you finally decided to grace me with your presence." Darcy gave no reply; his eyes said it all. With the menacing stare he gave, had they been daggers they would've pierced through her rather mercilessly. Amy was taken aback by the look he shot her, but she still managed a sarcastic grin. "Wow, love you too." He briefly considered holding his silence, as if to deprive her of the satisfaction. However, he thought better, knowing Amy would never give up. So, Markson simply growled "Stay away from me." Those were not the words the redhead wanted or needed to hear...they hurt badly, but she took it out with her usual form of childish anger. She couldn't find words, and instead ended up hurling the half-empty bottle she'd been nursing from across the room to shatter. "Oh goodie!" Darcy clapped with mock enthusiasm. "We're both mad. This will make things considerably easier!" "I'm not sure if you bothered processing what's happening to me..." Amy growled, standing up to face him, her nose to his chin. "But my reputation...yes, me, that girlfriend you sometimes act like you have...my reputation was just pissed on, and I have absolutely NO way to redeem it if people choose to believe the shit in that fucking article." Darcy scowled. "Don't even start, Amy. Don't even fucking try. Fuck your reputation, because it's Darcy Markson who just got pissed on." She didn't even bother slapping him, though she felt ready to...the energy just wasn't there; She was too mentally and physically drained. Instead, she raised her normally placid voice considerably. "YOU got pissed on?! Spare me that sob story...some ditz media fuck went too far into MY personal life and then twisted it around to make me look like a fucking slut just to sell some papers!" "Oh, boo hoo, the world thinks you're a slut now. If you expect to be a goddamn celebrity, then expect to get your rep trashed by the tabloids. This isn't about your rep, my rep, or anybody else's....this is about you being a lying, manipulative bitch!" "Wha........what did you just say?" Amy stared blankly...beyond anger, or any emotion. She was, for the first time in a long time, just astonished...even past the point of words. Darcy stepped forward a little, and the menacing stare again returned. "I said, you are a lying, manipulative bitch, who only cares about getting what she wants, no matter what the cost, no matter who she hurts in the process." The look on her face was that of pure and absolute confusion, mixed with what looked like fear...she'd never seen Darcy angry enough to say these sort of things, but the scent of Jack Daniels on his breath indicated that they might've been influenced a little. "Where the fuck did that come from?!" "Oh, isn't it just a little too obvious? We start dating again, you try to lock me down after all of a month, and then I find out you have a kid that you had all the time in the world to tell me about, but didn't. You don't have to draw me a fucking picture Amy." "You think I'm with you to try and get a father for my kid?!" At that point, she was past confused and well into anger again, getting 'in his face'. "And the fucking thing isn't even mine!" Darcy ignored her reply; she could have told him that World War 3 had just broken out, and it wouldn't have fazed him, he was so blind with rage and grief. "And to think I was beginning to trust you....to think that I actually fell for your little plan...." Amy grabbed his hands, trying to reason. "There's no plan...I'm taking care of him for a friend who's...well...not among us anymore. I swear! I know that sounds so fake...but I'm not lying. You know me, Darcy...I'm not like that. I was afraid of scaring you off, so I didn't mention it. But now you know anyway...he's not mine, I'm too loyal to let him go, so he's with my mom half the time." She was visibly shaking, over-emotional and incredibly upset as she looked pleadingly into cold, unforgiving eyes. "...you're really scaring me. I'm afraid of you like this..." Normally, Darcy was pretty even-tempered. Normally, an argument such as this would've ended a lot earlier, and they'd already be on to apologies. Unfortunately, the normal Darcy has been replaced by a burned, slightly inebriated Darcy. "You give me one good reason why I should buy that dumbass sob story." "Because I'm about three seconds from curling up at your feet sobbing?" She still had the same look...one that showed the pain she was in from behind normally bright, beautiful eyes. "Call my mother, if you want...she's been nagging at me to tell you for ages now. I don't know why you can't believe me...this is me, Darcy." "I don't want to talk to your mom, I don't want to talk to you, I don't want to talk to anybody....How do I know you're not hiding anything else? How can I even be sure that the Amy I know isn't made up too, in an effort to not scare me off?" "Because the Amy standing in front of you right now is the same one you knew last year, the only things she's hiding for fear of scaring you off..." She was getting angry again, but suddenly returned to a relatively calm, though afraid state...trying to goad Darcy into doing the same. "...are the extent of my feelings." "Well do whatever you like at this point, because I'm about as scared off as you can get." "I'd like to make you believe me," She retorted, sinking back onto the couch with her head bowed to the floor. For a flash, Darcy could see how vulnerable she now was. Instinctively, he moved towards her to hold her as he always did. He stopped himself, however... "I'm not getting burned again, Amy. Not for you, not for ANYBODY. Do you hear me? I'm not getting burned again!" "I know you were hurt...but that was in high school, years ago." Aimz almost choked up for a moment but she relented, staring hard at him. If she could get him to trust her, he might finally open up. "But her name wasn't Amy Campbell, Darcy...do you honestly think I have it in my heart to hurt you?" "Oh save it, Jane wasn't the only one....she just hurt the most. All the others took their shots, it all adds up. Fuck, you don't even know me..." "Don't you EVER tell me that." It was time to get angry again, and Amy did so...jumping up from the couch and getting back into Darcy's face, close enough that their chests were just barely touching. "If you think for one fucking second that I haven't dedicated every single moment of the last three months of my life, and the year before, to you then you're a blind fool. I might not know you as well as your other whores, but that's ONLY because you never bother telling me..." Darcy opened his mouth to speak, but Amy interrupted and continued... "I seem to just be a little fun when you get bored and a nice doll to take out your anger on but I fucking stick around, hoping that you'll get a clue someday and realize that life's not quite so peachy for me, I've been hurt too...ever thought of that? Every guy who's been with me either cheats or just walks off, and how's that supposed to make me? You're the one guy in a LONG time...one guy I've let myself open up to, and the FIRST guy I've come to love so fuck off with that 'You don't even know me' and 'I've been hurt so much' garbage." He began wrenching his hands, attempting to keep himself occupied physically so as not to lose total control. "Well Amy, all I see right now is you hurting me with your deception. If this is your idea of opening up, if I have to get hurt in the process, then screw it. You can say all you want about guys hurting you, but guess what? You're drawing the blood this time." "I told you...he's not mine, he's an obligation purely out of loyalty and I didn't tell you because I was afraid you'd run away again." Amy was also trying not to lose total control but not to anger, rather to tears that seemed to be building fast behind closed eyelids as she tugged painfully at her own hair, so frusterated that Markson remained pointing fingers, assuming she was a liar. "I don't want to hurt you, I never wanted to hurt you...I pray everyday that you'll stay forever, because that hope's the only thing keeping me somewhere near sanity right now." Darcy remained silent, shaking his head. "What?" She opened her eyes again, this time they were wide as ever, displaying nothing but raw emotion. "Now you find out how much I care about you, and you're still the same 'manly' Darcy who's not allowed to give a shit, not allowed to say how you feel?" This time it was Amy who shook her head. "That must be a difficult life." "Jane loved me too, Amy. And I got burned all the same. If that's supposed to make me open up, just because you say those three magic words, you're wrong. If you expect them to be a ticket to make me open up, you're wrong." She'd backed away slightly, but took a step up again and stared into his eyes with an oddly un-revealing expression. "Darcy...could you tell me how you feel about, what you think of me, me in a word or two?" Darcy looked down. He almost felt ashamed, even as he assured himself that it wasn't really his fault. He looked back up, flipping his long, dark hair back with it. "No Amy, I can't. Even if I had any idea before now, you threw me all out of whack with this shit. I don't know what I feel about anything." "Look at me." She grabbed his jaw, almost violently, and made him face her. "I haven't lied to you, Darcy Markson. This fucked up situation, the stupid media...it doesn't change anything between us, and it shouldn't. As messed up as it's going to sound, especially with you standing here and calling me a liar outright......I love you." Darcy surged with anger, lowering his brow in a pure reflection of it. He didn't have to stand for...no, it was useless. He lowered his head once again, "I know." There was no use fighting it. He could tell by the look in her eyes. Amy could lie if she wanted to, lie with all her conviction, but her eyes would always betray her. 'I know' may not have been the response everybody would want to hear after finally saying 'I love you', but this was Darcy Markson, so Amy knew that even hearing 'I know' from him was nearly a Godsend. Her mental exhaustion was making her physically ill, but she was trying with all her might not to let it show. "Then why won't you ever just tell me that, tell me how you feel?" "Because it's my job to worry about how I feel....I speak through actions, not words. I say what I need to say, and I say it when the time is right. That's just the way I am, Amy." She continued staring at him, unwavering and not even taking a moment to blink. "Can now be one of those right times?" Darcy looked up. "I don't want anything, anything at all....other than my Amy back." "I never left and I never will." Amy bowed her head again. "And I hope you never will." He began to feel it again....Amy's words of him saving her from the brink of insanity echoed in his mind. The light flickered in his head, the light that fueled the old dream. No, he thought to himself. Now is not the time. But perhaps, one day? An argument for another day, he thought again, ending the matter before it could express anymore. "Darcy?" Her eyes were wide as she watched him stand there, staring at her blankly for a moment. "...what's on your mind?" "Wondering how, in about thirty minutes, I can go from wanting to kill you to.....wanting you at my side even more." "I think you're starting to see how you feel about me..." She smiled, incredibly brightly and for the first time in what seemed like years but was, in reality, about twenty four hours. "Hmm?" Darcy kissed her forehead, then tightly embraced her. "We'll discuss the kid thing in the morning....I'm just glad to have this taken care of." "What was 'this'?" Amy tightly pressed her forehead to Darcy's collar bone. "And did I prove it?" Darcy smiled. "Let's just say that the only person who has to worry about Darcy's wrath is the journalistically-impaired son of a bitch who was willing to trash your life for the sake of a story." At least now she had a reason never to drink again...seeing what the mixture of alcohol and anger had done to Darcy, Amy had been intimidated and terrified...she never wanted to end up like he had, and probably never would again. With the end of their own personal duel came peace for the evening, finally. Life within the home that night had returned to some state of relative normalcy. Aimz had until morning to relax, to keep the outside world shut out... The operative term is "Until morning"...until morning, all hell WOULDN'T break loose. Until morning, while she was alone with Darcy, Amy Campbell was just Amy Campbell...not what people wanted to make of her, not what people would believe was her. She was just her and she aimed to keep the silence for at least that night...the next day, she'd deal with the people. The next day, she'd make the first attempt to clear the lie and switch back around the life that the reporter changed...she'd get rid of the demons, every bottle in her fridge and pill in her drawer. She'd become a good role-model and finally give up what was corrupting her. Maybe, among all the lies Sandra Peake had written, there was good...she'd managed to motivate Amy to prove the world's image of her wrong, all over again. ...as for "her" son, that was a battle for another day.
