Starting over

29 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Deacon enjoyed driving, but after a match, he normally passed on that job. In this case, he was in the car with his normal driving partner from 2 years ago - Chris Shepherd. "Beautiful country up here." Chris said. "We never went to Canada with the CSWA did we? "No. Mostly state side 'cept when I first start wit' t'em." "The battle royal!" Chris said with a laugh. "I guess tonight brought back some memories." "No," Deacon said. "T'is much different. I won that match." A smile crossed his face. That match was a 'newcomers battle royal' and it set him up as one of the forces in the CSWA. "Yeah, those were the days, huh?" "T'ey were. I had lot of fun doing t'ose shows. Life much easier then." Deacon stopped talking, both men knowing the unspoken subject of the comment. "So..." Chris began after several long moments. "What is the plan for the fwo?" "Like the match, wing it. They professional here. I heard some have ego, but that anywhere you go. If they pro in ring, I not care. I heard t'at t'ey want me to work with newer wrestler." "You comfortable with that?" Chris asked. "If t'ey hire him, he must be good." "When does that start up?" "Real soon. T'ey want me in mix early. I met some guys in back who would like to work wit' me also in future. If t'ey not playing me, then I should have angle for long while." "Sounds good, then." Chris said. "Isn't this wild, so much like first entering the CSWA. It's like we're starting all over again, but this time, we have some heat already." "I need t'is... start over is good." Deacon said. God is good, he thought. In 6 months, he had went from "on his deathbed" to re-starting life. Walking into the fWo, starting in a match just like his beginning of the CSWA, led him to wonder - will I be as successful here as I was before? Deep down, one way or the other, he knew that he would succeed. One life touched was all he needed, and he knew that starting at Warfare, he would begin building that part of his 'show'.
