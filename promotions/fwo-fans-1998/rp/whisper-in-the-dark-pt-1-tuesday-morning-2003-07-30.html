30 July 2003

"When a man inflicts pain on another, whether flesh against flesh, or with a weapon, the only feeling that drives through his body is his questionable morals and the feeling of driving a body part onto the hard bone and flesh of a body." "But when a man is taking the pain, the one being attacked, he feels so much more. The body becomes damaged, your flesh becomes sore, your breaths become shorter, and you question yourself even more then when you are the one swinging your fist." "Maybe I've had this wrong. Maybe the assault I was given did more for me then it did for them. Maybe this has opened my eyes to my ways more then I have ever realized. I've waited so long to get what has been coming to me, and now it has arrived. I shouldn't sit sore. I should stand proud." The light flickered on, as Eron's adjusting eyes turned to the door. Kerry Chandler stood, wearing a mauve tie on a lime green pinstripe suit. Eron wondered if it was Disney policy to make Tuesday's horrible clothing choices day, but the last thing he should be is the fashion police. Kerry looked down at Eron, sitting uncomfortably in a plastic chair, a TIME Magazine rolled up in his hand, watching Eron's pupils adjust to the change of light. Chandler looked around the room for a moment, before looking back at Eron. "Who were you talking to?" asked Kerry, his Lugz driving shoe spinning in small circles on the spaghetti sauce coloured carpet. Eron shrugged slowly with his left shoulder, "Just myself." Kerry once again looked around the room, this time leaning his body over from the door, his head wandering around the small space to see if there was another body anywhere. "In the dark?" Kerry asked curiously. Eron placed the TIME magazine next to the rest of the magazines, as it unrolled, displaying it as the May, 1986 issue about Hitler's Forged Diary. Eron put his hand on the small table where the magazines sat, using it to help him stand his body up. Eron's body moved in a tired, harsh style, displaying he was still sore from the attack. Moving his eyes up Kerry Chandler's ugly shit, before reaching his eyes. "Yes. In the dark. I am guessing they have finally requested my presence?" Kerry turned his face away from Eron's. To look in Eron's piercing blue eyes, one eye surrounded by a thin, barely healing scar told a story Kerry refused to read. It was formerly his job to understand and take care of Eron as his assigned agent, but ever since Eron was attacked, Chandler has felt a sense of failure, and if he seen that failure in Eron's eyes, Chandler knew he could never with it. Eron sensed this, so he tried to make eye contact with the proud, Princeton graduate whenever his eyes had to turn to Eron's direction. Head down, Kerry replied. "Yes, we all talked together about your position in the fans Wrestling organization, your contract, former conduct. All of the stuff we feel more comfortable without you there. We did settle out a few things." Eron stepped out of the room while Kerry pushed down the light switch and closed the door. Kerry pointed to one of the corridors as the correct one to walk down as Eron began to move in that direction. "What, exactly did you settle?" Eron asked, his voice sharp with curiosity. "Well, we already decided that those who attacked you will all be heavily fined. We all felt their conduct was inappropriate and that action should be..." Chandler was cut off. "Who, is getting fined, exactly?" Eron slid in, his eyes fixed on Kerry's, whose eyes stared at his feet from each step he took on the carpet. "We... can't disclose that. Sorry. It is for the safety of the company." Eron sneered, his heart darting to the side. "Real fucking fair. I get one drug offence and I'm thrown out the door. A group of guys decide to beat me to internal injury and they get slaps on the wrists. Nice to hear Disney enjoys to promote injustice." Kerry looked back at Eron, whose head was turned to the side, his upper lip curling against his teeth, a hereditary habit of his.
