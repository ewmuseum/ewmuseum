Trust (Building)

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, this was it. Over the past month, I think Sean and I had really begun to grow closer, not necessarily as a team, but outside of the ring. The lunches that turned into multi-hour discussions about how to improve things in the fWo (more specifically the tag division), the workouts with Brandon, and even the time in the hospital just recently. And it was the thoughts of Brandon and the hospital that really made me think. I had been just seconds away from losing one of my best friends, and yet it hadn't been me who had saved him, it had been Amy. While it was true that I had wrestled Brandi to the ground, taking her weapon away from her, Amy had been the one to actually put herself in the way of the bullet that would've certainly ended Brandon's life. She'd spent days in the hospital as a result, yet I was the one lauded as a hero by the press. There was just something intrinsicately wrong about that, and it bothered me. Amy was the true hero, and I had just been along for the ride, and while I knew that the important people knew who the real hero was, my attempts at shifting the press to Amy's actions had more or less fallen on deaf ears. I guess the press here in Canada was nearly the same as in America, and since they couldn't get into Amy's room for an interview, I became the de facto celebrity. But at least there had been some good news to come out of the hospital, and my mom was really bugging me to send her some pictures of my new "niece". I was going to have to take Ivy up on that offer to go laptop shopping. I really felt happy for Angel and Eli, and it was great to see two people so in love that they could work their careers around their new family. Plus, I got to hang out with the rest of Valerian's Garden, and they're genuinely some of the coolest guys I've ever met. I can see why Eli trusts Angel's safety so much when she's with them, as Mickey, TJ, Sex, and Nine all seem to have a great deal of love of their own for the new Mrs. Flair. With my extended family growing, and my brother-in-arms and I finally starting to see things eye-to-eye, I was confident and happy as I drove my rental car to the arena for BodyCount. I had ended up by myself, as Brand and Eli were coming together later, so that they could spend some more time at the hospital with their respective women, and Ivy and Sean were going to grab a quick bite before the show, but I didn't really mind. It had given me time to think, time to clear my head, and, most importantly, time to focus on the goal at hand: winning the fWo tag team championships. After the little encounter with the Covingtons on Wednesday, I was ready to show the rooks that we meant business, and regardless of how much time they had spent in developmental, there was no substitute for a year in the bigs. As long as Sean and I were on the same page, and I had no real reason to believe that we wouldn't be, tonight would be our time to shine. I could only hope that my partner would be as focused and ready for the match tonight as I was. Which Sean Stevens would show tonight: the brash, confident superstar that had single-handedly carried our first match to a victory in spite of my protests and desire to help; the dazed and grief-stricken man who had needed a little assistance from me just to keep our second match from turning into a trainwreck; or the extremely focused workaholic who had dismantled two men nearly on his own yet was too stubborn to look for help when he needed it?
