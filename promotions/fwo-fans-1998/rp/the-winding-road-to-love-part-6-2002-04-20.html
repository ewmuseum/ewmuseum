The Winding Road To Love (Part 6)

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Seated at a desk with a Krispy Kreme donut in hand, a police officer flips through a magazine, as a row of empty jail cells sit behind him. It had been a slow night already, and it seemed like he would be spending another night alone on his shift. As he held the magazine up and let the centerfold spread out, his eyes lit up and a smile came across his face. Seconds later, the clanking of a nightstick on the gate to his right startled him. "Johnson, got two coming in," said the officer from the other side of the gate. He watched Officer Johnson set the magazine down and walk over to the gate. As Johnson opened the gate, the second officer shook his head. "You better keep your eyes on these two rather than those tits in that magazine!" "Sorry, Sergeant," Johnson said nervously while he opened the gate. "I won't let it happen again." As the gate opened, the Sergeant pulled forward his two handcuffed suspects...Monte Burns and Tempest. Both looked like they had calmed down a bit from the way they had acted in the restaurant, obviously realizing that things had gotten out of hand. Johnson took Tempest by the arm and led her to the first cell, opening the door slowly and putting her inside. Once inside, he had her grab the cell bars and then uncuffed her wrists. A moment later, he led Burns away from the Sergeant and did the same with him, this time in the second cell, the one right next to Tempest's. Just before leaving the area, the Sergeant turned back to Johnson. "Keep an eye on those two, Johnson," he said with a stern look on his face. "They were at each other's throats just a few minutes ago." "Yes, Sergeant," replied Johnson with a nod before taking his seat once again at the desk. For a moment, both were silent. Tempest looked up, as did Monte and their eyes met for a moment. Then they looked away quickly. Tempest paced while Burns fumed silently and after a moment they both wheeled toward the central cell bars the divided them and blurted in unison, "This is all your fault!" The both jumped back at the coincidence and looked at each other strangely. Tempest broke the ensuing silence as she said, "Well... I mean it wasn't nice to butt in front of me. Throwing the table wasn't your fault." "Well, no," Burns said nodding his head. "Of course, I guess it wasn't all your fault either. Maybe...I don't know. I guess I was partly at fault too." Tempest nodded and said, "You could always have let me have the table. I dunno, I just don't get what privilege is like I guess." Monte looked over at Tempest's face and saw the look in her eyes. He knew that she was being completely honest, and that was certainly something that he admired in her, despite what he said in public. "That's probably a good thing, Tempest," he said looking down at his hands. "Sometimes when you're used to getting everything you want, you can't really appreciate what you do have around you. The only time you appreciate something...or someone...is when they're out of your hands. And even then, sometimes, you don't appreciate them for the right reasons." Tempest looked at him curiously and said, "Maybe so. Then again, I've tried real hard not to be the kind of person who's only known for their suffering. The flip side is ... why should you be only known for the fact that you haven't? I always said I hated the guys who made a lifestyle out of misery and whined about everything they didn't have and all the bad stuff that happened to them." She sighed a little and said, "Maybe I'm more like them than I thought." Once again, Monte saw the look on her face and wanted to go towards her. Yet, something kept him from moving from his seat on the bed in the cell. He was cursing himself for not going over to the bars, for not saying exactly what he felt at that moment. Instead, he tried to comfort her without saying too much to give in completely. "No, you're nothing like those guys," he said with a nervous chuckle. "Tempest, when I first got here in the fWo, I looked at you and laughed. I wondered how someone could be so happy and think they would be successful in the wrestling business. That's when I started to realize how much I envied you, because your life is about living, not about matches or wins or titles. Whether you have a title belt or you're in the opening match, you're able to be happy with your life. In all honesty, I admire that in you." "Really?" Tempest said as she looked up. She caught herself and looked back down. If admiration was something she enjoyed having it wasn't obvious now. "I just... that's the way I am. Or the way I try to be at least. Doesn't always work out. And I guess lately it's been harder than usual. Keeping the fans laughing isn't that tough, but me..." She shook her head. "The wins and the titles do mean something to me. Every time I look at the Cruiserweight strap I think, 'You're legitimate. No one can tell you you're a bastard or a whore or feel justified drowning you in the nearest river'." Obviously, Burns was taken aback by the way Tempest had ended her statement. He remembered, of course, that she had been through a lot, and he could tell that there was some hurt deep inside of her that she tried desperately to keep below the surface. "I don't know if it means anything or not," he began with his head down. Monte paused for a moment before looking over at her. "But you don't have anything else to prove to me. After seeing what you can do in the ring the past year, I'll never make the mistake of underestimating you again. You're...you're one of the greatest competitors I have ever faced." Tempest smiled a little bit. "Thanks. And likewise, I may not always seem like it, but I do respect what you can do in the ring," she said. "It's just..." She shook her head and chuckled a little as she continued, "I'm not that good at separating things, ya know? When Ric tricked Eddy into almost ending my career, it was hard to separate what happened in the ring form my personal life. And sometimes I'm not sure I want to separate it. I almost feel like I'd be taking something away form the fans if I did. But it's risky." "To tell you the truth," Burns said while shaking his head, "I don't exactly have much of a personal life, so I don't really have to worry about that I guess." Tempest nodded and looked up at him, then back at the floor. She repeated the motion and cleared her throat. At last she managed to say, "Actually I do appreciate that you were trying to help last week. It's just..." She chuckled bitterly and shook her head as she said, "Do you realize there are people dumb enough to think that the only reason I'm still cruiserweight champ is because you allowed me to be, cuz you... Well because you uh..." She waved her hand in his direction, not completing the thought. Monte knew exactly what she was saying, because he had heard the whispers as well. Everyone from road agents to front office personnel to fellow wrestlers had asked him about it. He couldn't believe how many of them had wondered. While he couldn't rule out some subconscious effects completely, Monte knew that he had lost because Tempest had been the better wrestler on that night. He just wanted to make sure she knew that as well. "Tempest," he began softly, in a tone he used rarely, "anyone who knows me, knows how much I care about winning. I have been a part of the wrestling business for as long as I can remember. Titles are what I've always been about, usually at the expense of other things in my life. If there's one thing I wouldn't do, it's throw a title match. You beat me that night, Tempest. You simply beat me." She nodded and said, "I know. It's just that I guess I fell like most people don't. I think..." She stood up and smiled, almost apologetically. "It's not that I can't appreciate your helping me. I just... can't quite get used to the idea, especially when people look at me and say that kind of thing. I'm not prepared to just let you be my hero." She shrugged a little and added, "I'm not sure I'm prepared to let anyone be." Finally, Monte decided he needed to stand up. Burns rose from his seat on the bed and walked over to the bars separating the two cells. He stood there silently, looking over at Tempest. Every bit of her appealed to him, from her sparkling eyes to her soft, smooth skin to her stubbornness. He wanted so bad to reach out and touch her right now, but he knew he wouldn't be able to...physically. "I'm not looking to be your hero, Tempest," he spoke slowly. "I just want to be with you. Not above, not below. Just with you." Tempest stood for a moment, jut watching him curiously. The emotions that flickered across her face raged from surprise to disbelief and ended up with a contemplative look. " I... I don't know if that will ever happen or not, but..." She broke the tension with a small laugh and said, "I told Miyuki it would be easier if you just liked me for now." She shuffled nervously and said, "It's just hard when the only people you're sure loved you are long dead. I'm still trying to learn to love my brother." Burns could see the pained expression on her face, a young woman who had been through much more than anyone should have to. Despite all that had happened, he felt for her greatly and was becoming less and less inhibited about expressing his feelings. "Tempest, no matter what happens the rest of the way," he said while looking her direction, "I just want you to know that I'll be there for you in whatever capacity you feel comfortable with." Tempest took a deep breath and smiled. "I'm not sure what capacity that'll be, but... I'll try to at least allow you be my friend. I mean not even allowing that isn't fair. Just... try and respect me when it doesn't come naturally a little?" She looked up at him hopefully. "I'll try, Tempest," he said with a smile. "I'll try." She took a step closer to the wall between the cells and pointed at his shirt. "Although I admit, you do look particularly hunky in garlic cream sauce." She grinned and chuckled lightly. Monte smiled as well, chuckling at the change in demeanor from only a while ago. Just the fact that Tempest was smiling once again made him feel good. "I guess so," he said glancing down at his shirt before looking back over at her. "And you don't look so..." "Away from the bars!" The shout came from Officer Johnson, who had come around to check on them. The two of them sighed and returned to sit on their beds, slumping their shoulders once more at their predicament. After the officer had watched them for a moment, he turned and walked back over to the desk where he had sat before. As he walked out of sight, Burns and Tempest looked up once more and locked eyes again and smiled.
