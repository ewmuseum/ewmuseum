Buzzed: Side A

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2:35 AM The aftermath of the Friday Bash... Leigh Landers has been in very strange situations in her young life. There was the time that she had accidently walked in on her younger brother, Benjamin, while he was trying to convince his father to let him win the Regional Title with one hand tied behind his back. Then there was the time that Daniel Phillips unintentionally got to first base with her while trying to apply a dragon sleeper on her during a training session. However, nothing could compare to the feeling of being the designated driver of three universally diverse people in her best friend, Allen Speck, the serious bloke who ended up in very awkward situations in Adam Nowell, and the typically obnoxious Sanchis Guilherme, whom Leigh could barely stand. In Leigh's expierences with the man, from the five years she's known him, Allen Speck and alcohol mixed together usually equalled trouble. It wasn't like he was a violent drunk, it was more of the strange dreams that he got afterwards that he'd always tell Leigh about at awkward times of the morning. Not to mention that he did this thing when he was drunk where he'd run a contest with the other drunks around him to see who could punch themselves in the face the hardest, and it would go until someone would get knocked unconscious, and that person would be designated the winner. Allen usually lost those games. Tonight, however, he had won and was sleeping the sleep of the knocked unconscious in the passenger's seat of the card. Adam was a pleasant drunk, though he did have noticable bruises on his face from Allen's little game earlier in the evening. He spent most of the night trying to get to know one of those well-dressed, out of place girls that had ended up with Leigh and the gang... the red-headed one, actually. Most surprising in this entire "getting drunk"-process was Sanchis. When he got completely drunk, Sanchis ducked out of Allen's "punch yourself until you're unconscious" game and had a long, drawn out, and respectful conversation with the other Avalon girl. This was one of the few times Leigh could remember Sanchis actually have a conversation with another woman that didn't involve A) constantly refering to her as the Spanish word for bitch, B) not trying to do a bad pick up line, or C) didn't involve Sanchis refering to himself in the third person. It's too bad that you had to get Sanchis DRUNK to be respectful, because that probably would've been the only way he could've ever gotten laid. So, anyway, Leigh had the FUN TASK of driving these three crazies to the nearest hotel, and come to terms with the fact that she'd be driving Allen to Boston tommorow. Now, this was a trip that involved Speck and Nowell both groaning in pain from a couple of migraines, Nowell throwing up whatever he had for lunch that day, and Sanchis respectfully requesting a change in clothes thanks to Nowell's throwing up. Finally, she reached the hotel in Landover, and quietly turned the vehicle off. She was tired, but she had to press on and at least get everyone to the hotel room. She came to the stark conclusion that everyone was gonna end up in the same hotel room tonight, and got out of the car. Once out, Leigh sighed to herself and stared upwards at the stars for a long, agonizing moment, before she looked down at her left forearm, and more importantly at a long scar that ran down it. A series of thoughts ran through her head about how she got the scar, but she shook her head to clear her head of such thoughts, and quickly turned to open the door for Nowell and Sanchis. These two would exit the car and go their seperate, drunken ways. Leigh then circled around to help Allen out of the car, and try to support his weight with her body. She was somewhat strong despite her dorky presence, but the awkwardness of Allen's weight presented her with some problems in trying to bring him inside the hotel. Midway there, on the elevator to the third floor of the building, Allen managed to wake up. "Ugh... did I... uh... win...?" he asked Leigh, his weight still being supported by Leigh. "Um, not really." she quietly answered. It took Allen a few seconds to actually figure out what his best friend told him, "....Oh." Allen ended up trying to put more weight into his feet, since he didn't want his friend to break her spine trying to support his two hundred and seven pound body, and because she just HAD to try and support him while under his left arm. If one had been paying attention, this was the same arm that possessed the shoulder that had been injured by Benjamin Morris. Whom, incidentally, was Leigh's stepbrother. Well, anyway, before we went into THAT tirade, it should be noted that Allen could barely support his own weight and this was proven when his attempted step forward ended up being more of a tumble as he slipped from Leigh's shoulders and struck the floor hard. Leigh sighed, welcomed the weight being lifted off her shoulders for a moment, then stepped over Allen's body and pulled him out of the doors of the elevator. Leigh wasn't as stubborn as her stepfather and stepbrother, but she had to be somewhat stubborn in this case, and she slowly helped Allen return to his feet. After a bit of effort, and after a bit more stumbling, Leigh finally got Allen to their hotel room, and dumped Allen on the bed. Exhausted from this bit of exercise and fumbling about, Leigh walked backwards until her back reached the wall, and then slid down against it until she was seated on the floor. This was where she fell asleep, despite her own bed not being too far away. She would be woken up four hours later by the sound of a cellular phone.
