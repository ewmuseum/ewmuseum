Disillusionment: Book Six - Chapter I

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pussy. Two weeks in a row now, those that I am scheduled to face have avoided the arena. They've avoided the man who would have been their doom. They've avoided the man who has been accused of a murder, committed another, and escaped from a maximum-security penitentiary. Shawn Arrows and Brandon Louden, that's all they are, pussies. To make matters worse, now I have to fight a Canadian. Yeah, they have the Disciples is Canada too. Canadians have been the people that have been the most open to my teachings. They want to learn that the United States government isn't as powerful as it really is, because if that's true, then it means that Canada isn't just the United States' bitch as we have all been taught to believe. Of course, Canada is still a bitch...it's just that it's the Disciples' bitch instead. It has been said that hell hath no fury like a woman scorned, but I'm hoping that I can prove that wrong. Spending those couple of days in the Disciples' penitentiary made me realize a few things. Mostly, though, it made me realize that lately, I've been lazy. A year ago, I was a continuous ass in the Disciples' side. I'd break into their fortresses and sabotage their weapons of mass destruction, or I'd break into their medical facilities where they would be housing prisoners they would be performing tortuous medical experiments on. Those were some fucked up experiments. The doctors working for the Disciples would seriously drain human beings of their blood, just to see how long they could live. Terrifyingly painful exploratory surgery would be administered to patients who would be given no anesthetic, just to test the pain threshold of the human body. It wasn't known to anyone before they started their experiments, but the Disciples proved that human life can cease, merely as a result of pain. For no other reason...no loss of blood, no damage to organs, simply painful experiences are enough. If you didn't hate the Disciples from what I've told you already, let me give you some more reason... The human reaction to mass murder is never a good one, so I hope this disgusts you as much as it should. Hitler was responsible for the deaths of six million Jews...remember, he was a Disciple. Stalin, another Disciple who I have not yet talked to you about, killed as many as twenty million of his own "countrymen." Those twenty-six millions deaths aren't even the tip of the iceberg, though. Throughout the 1990s, Olaf Heersh killed over one hundred million people in the nations of China and India, simply because he knew there were over population issues in both countries, and he wanted to see how many he could kill without being noticed. One hundred million people, one sixtieth of the world's population, were killed for shits and giggles. That's the kind of stuff that makes me sick. I need to go back on the offensive like I used to. Since I escaped from the holding facility, the Disciples haven't been after me like they used to be. They've...gone easy on me. I haven't even heard any announcements about the reward on my head, something that occurred several times daily before I got sent to the pen. I think I know why they're going easy on me too...they think I've gone soft. I haven't stricken out against the Disciples in several weeks, and they no longer see me as a threat. I don't know...maybe they figure I got caught in the ventilation system in the prison and died there. Whatever the reason, though, they've been giving me many opportunities. Today I was simply walking down the streets, and several police, who all work for the Disciples, walked by me in a group. Not a single one of them even gave me a second look. And so I'm gonna get up off my lazy ass and make a difference. No more small time, though. This time, I need to get some serious shit going. I'm not going to be happy taking pot shots at a low-level Disciple or breaking into a fort, only to steal some rounds of ammunition. I have a bigger target in mind now. This time...it's Olaf Heersh.
