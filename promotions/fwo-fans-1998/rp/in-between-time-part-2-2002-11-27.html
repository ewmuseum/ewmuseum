In Between Time(part 2)

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
- Ediciones Sin resolver - He stood in front of an 'SCW' backdrop, dressed in his ring attire - black trunks, trademark 'X' in various places on the pants leg, and rear, white sleeveless shirt, and a pony- tail. He could've paid a couple of extra bucks to have his name scroll across the bottom of the screen, or something ... but, he didn't have to. You knew who he was ... even if you sat there, pretending you didn't. He gazed into the camera lense ... almost as if he was trying to intimidate somebody ... anybody ... maybe, it was his intended target, Max Blackshire. A ruthless, cunning, psychotic individual who used the art of the mind game to get inside his opponents head. Maybe he wanted Max to feel his anger ... his pain. Maybe he wanted him to see his level of intensity ... his ... focus. Or maybe ... just, maybe ... he was staring at no one in particular. Maybe his rage was directed at whoever it was watching the video. Maybe Sean 'Triple X' Stevens had made a decision after his last matchup. One that required him to stop playing, get serious, and focus. Maybe ... you, the viewer were sitting on your couch, wondering, aloud, paranoid ... for nothing. Maybe not... "It must suck to be you, Max Blackshire." "I mean ... you put all of your brain power into these elaborate schemes, designed to throw everyone off, reveal your unpredictability to the public, and to even scare us, perhaps? ...only to fail and fail miserably. I mean, think about it for a second ... you attack me. Cost me my match. ...the Superior Title. Then you give me this long winded speech about what you're gonna do to me ... how you're gonna do it. Even tossing in an insult of two ... if that's what you want to call it. You all but label me corporate. Pretty much called me fake ... and, said you'd destroy the real me, even though I've preached and proved time and again ... that *I* am *me* all day, everyday, twenty- four- seven, three- sixty- five, including leap year. Then ... you get me." Stevens had about two days worth of beard under his chin, and a mustache covering his top lip. He shook his head, slightly... smirking, as he returned his attention to the camera. "You GET me, Max. And, sure ... you can give us this entire spiel about you wanting to twist me, then break me. About you taking your time. About it being a slow process. But, the fact still remains ... You Had Me. Right in your grasp. Ripe for the pickings, if you wanted. I was injured - AM injured. Nowhere near one hundred percent. And, if there was a chance I could be beaten ... could be taken to my limits ... that was it." He paused, looking down for a moment. Reflecting. "I guess that's the difference between us, Max. If I could've ... you wouldn't be here. If I were healed up, hadn't wrestled in God Knows When, and had time to deliberate like you did ... your ass would in fact be in somebody's hospital. Do I think it's gonna be easy? No. Not hardly. And, I'll be honest ... right now ... I can't put you out of comission like I want to. My ribs are too sore. I can hardly breathe at night ... let alone lift anything. But, it won't always be like this. As the weeks pass ... my body will heal. And, the froggier you decide to be ... the more yours will weaken. And, then it'll happen. I'm sure of it. Ed Brown will get his head out of Linda Johns' cleavage, miss all the bad blood brewing between the two of us, and notice the dollar signs ... and, he'll sign it ... and, you'll step in the center of the ring with me. *MY* ring." "Then ... I'll show you what real pain is. I'll beat you inside and out, every way conceivable, until you're ready to throw in the towel ... but, guess what, Max? ...I won't let you. See, you cost me something dear to me. The chance to shut the Dallas Winstons' of the world up. The opportunity to plant my boot print on the face of Scott Johnson ... not to mention, you cost me the Superior Title. And, for that..." Stevens removed his shirt, as his taped ribs become visible. For a moment, he just stands there... then speaks, again. "...for *THESE* ... you'll pay." ------------ The spotlight shut off ... Sean Stevens reached for his shirt, and put it back on. He began his walk down a very long corridor toward an exit. It had been a very long day ... working the independents usually did that to you, though. You work hard ... put on a hell of a show ... get paid a couple of bucks ... then, go home, and wallow in self pity, wondering why you're in a business that seems to reward only a few, while the rest end their careers broken, with nothing to show for it. That wasn't Sean. He was one of the lucky ones. Blessed with a certain amount of talent that grew with each passing year. Gifted on the microphone ... with the ability to hold a crowd in the palm of his hand. Why was he in Superior? Two reasons. One, he was let go by the Fans Wrestling Organization without ever really understanding why. Two? ...Because he wanted to be there. But, on this day ... something would change him. Possibly ... forever. "Sean ... Cell phone. It's ringing and you almost forgot it. You owe me!" One of the writers - Robert Johnson - yelled, drawing Sean's attention in his direction. He tossed the phone and Sean caught it. "Not if I didn't catch that phone, I wouldn't have." he smirked. "...thanks, though." He placed the cell to his ear. "Is it possible that the great 'Triple X' took the time out of his busy schedule for us little people?!?" Instantly, he knew who it was. "Could it be that his lazy ass friends have just been too lazy to call? Luck...?" Sean grinned ear- to- ear. "That you?" "It's me ... how are you, partner?" "Missing you ... but, I've managed. How have you been? Where have you been?" Lucky seemed to ponder this for a moment ... answering carefully. "How have I been? Depends on your definition of fine." Triple X interupted. "You're not dead yet ... so you're fine in my book!" Lucky paused for a minute ... 'Could he know?' ... he put his mind at ease. 'Nah ... he couldn't possibly.' "Nope ... not dead... yet. But, you'll never guess where I am? Go ahead ... Try." "Umm ... Detroit. That *IS* where you live, right?" "Up until a month ago ... Yes." "Sooo ... where are you ... Now?" "Do me a favor, Trip. Hang up your phone ... look up the number I called from and call me back later. We need to talk ... in person." "Will do ... when are you coming to town? Soon I hope." "We'll see..." They both hung up simultaneously ... and, Sean did exactly as asked. He checked the number Luck had dialed from and couldn't help but smile as he recognized the area code. "That bastard's in Orlando..."
