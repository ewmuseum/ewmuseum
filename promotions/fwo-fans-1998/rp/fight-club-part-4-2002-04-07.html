Fight Club part 4

7 April 2002

APRIL 6, 2002 Tomorrow night Sunday Night Slaughter will continue the march to Cyberslam. Television will continue to make heroes into legends and legends into icons. Tonight, seven hundred miles away, a fWo house show creates several more casualties. I'm staring at a photo. Me and Angel. A month ago, maybe. Holding it in my blood- crusted hands. In the neverending quest for Cyberslam immortality, they're putting Winters in the ring with his hardcore title. In my neverending quest for wrestling immortality, I suggested a barbed wire match. Chris Universal said "Take some developmentals at a house show and let me see what you mean." The Alpha and Omega. A is for Alpha. The Beginning. Omega and his lies will tempt you. Will threaten to destroy you. Are you strong enough? It's one thing to see two men torn to shreds by barbed wire when both men know what they're doing. A violent, beautiful piece of art. When one man has been in the business for less than a month.... it's decidedly less so. I don't mind the bloodletting, but it's kinda.... pointless. It's a decent juxtaposition between a man like Donaven Winters and a man like "Number One" Nathan Meyers. This picture of Angel and me is a record of a different man than who was in the ring tonight. This is a man who has a future, possibly as a bodyguard or some similar 'official' capacity. Hell, he's six foot nine with tattoos and scars - who would mess with him? Nobody, if they knew he's torn countless men apart with barbed wire. With the Wheel of Death. In the Firehouse. The Torture Chamber. The House of Pain. The Cell. Call it what you will. His violent nature has destroyed all relationship in his life, save one. It's only a matter of time. Angel. She's currently at her record label with Poison Ivy, renegotiating her contract. So far, his career choice hasn't come between them. So far. What does God tell Man when he doesn't want him? Maybe it has and he's too blinded by red to see it. Maybe he can't look past his maddening hatred for Donaven Winters to know what happens around him. What does Man tell God when he doesn't need him? It doesn't matter. Tired of jumping at shadows. Tired of imagining problems when none exist. The same thing. Inside, athletic tape holds wounds together. Outside, the moon sheds its light silently.... Nothing. I am Eli's dulled sense of pain.
