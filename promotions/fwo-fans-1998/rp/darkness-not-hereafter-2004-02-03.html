Darkness: Not Hereafter

3 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
His story continues elsewhere. It takes place in a place that isn't here or there, and isn't fact or fiction. It's something more and less than both, a place of riddles and extremes and nothingness. He isn't there long, but you'll get to know that soon enough. I'm sorry I lost it in the last part, but that isn't easy for me to watch. He is partly me you know, and I don't like to watch him destroy himself. He will know this soon enough as well. It wasn't as bright as I expected it. And you weren't there. I was alone. I looked for you, or for anyone. I didn't see you. I hoped to see your smiling face or your red lips. I hoped to hear your laugh and feel your kiss. It had to have been hell because I think; I think I was dead for those eternal seconds I lay there. All I remember is his face as he walked over to me. I remember that grim look, that faint smile and smirk. He looked familiar yet completely foreign to me. Like the face of my hero and greatest enemy and truest love all soldered together and then washed away under the deepest waves of the ocean. He answered none of my questions, but instead just stood there, smiling. His face changed then, to his true appearance I suppose. He no longer was shrouded by the masquerade of other identities. He was just who he was. He had a pale face and a funny voice; two eyes that haunted me with their dark circles. He smiled at me. "I brought you here." He said. I chose not to respond. "You know who I am. Just think. I am the reason you are where you are. I LET you exist. I didn't make you, nor am I the reason you exist, but I brought you to where you are today. And I'm not ready to let you go. You have so much more you must do for me before you are ever let go. You have so much more to prove. I am not done with you. Not even close. Besides, I already lost Smooth... I will not lose you as well." I stared at him blankly, "What do you mean you lost Smooth?" "He's dead." It was then I felt my heart began to race, and the air begin to force its way into me. I doubled over, and he chuckled. He started to wave goodbye, and I tried to speak. It failed. He spoke for me, "Oh, you wish to know who I am? You know. You know who I am. But if you must... just call me... Call me T. Oh, and tell him... your maker... good luck with that bleeding ulcer." He let out a small laugh and I jerked awake. I looked up in the eyes of my savior. My angel. And I heard her speak. "Where's the FUCKING ambulance?" It was beautiful music to pass out to.
