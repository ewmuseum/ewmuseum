Harem - Prologue

30 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HAREM - Harem, as an Arabic word, is meant to represent someone's 'secret place'. Either a physical location or 'Land inside of the mind'. A means of escape from cruel reality, it can come in the form of a closet, room of a house, nestle in the woods or state of meditation and reflection. A tale appropriately titled Harem would deal with such subject matter - A meditation, escape from a previous state or some secretive mental 'place'/situation. Along these same lines, someone's Harem of sorts could be used to deal with the situation placed before them. Though this escape isn't necessarily good, and many try their hardest to venture away from these individual hiding places found in drugs and alcohol, others find different forms of Harem. An open mind will expose you to this...interpretation can be a gorgeous thing, and that's precisely what the definition of the word Harem is - The term itself is diverse and left open-ended. An opinion and mind left the same is nothing short of a timeless, priceless asset to someone's well-being and overall sanity. ---------------------------------------------- Amy was stumbling backstage...not like she'd normally be found. Looking nauseous and sore in combination was regular for her in the past while, but now she had an added aire of panic about her. Stitches still bleeding considerably, the redhead groaned when she sat beside her best friend and 'assistant' in the locker she and Brand were to share that evening. Of course, Renee Williams couldn't be held from commenting by a herd of wildabeast. "Jesus, you look like death warmed over." "Good to know." grumbled Amy, "Forget warmth, though." Her friend shrugged and patted the former hardcore champion on the back gently. "At least you showed Winters what's what. You looked good out there." Aimz' eyes shut tightly. When she leaned back against the wall to rest her head against it, it was with a sigh and no smile. She'd won 'in spirit', and honestly didn't care about what it said on paper. But something did matter to her in regards to the match's outcome and for it, she was to admonish her own behavior. Forget Winters. Forget rules. She'd forgotten the rules, but that wasn't even the problem... "I looked terrible out there." She cringed, "I wrestled fine, yeah. I might've even been better than usual. I could give a fuck less if I threw the match, but why I threw it's going to piss me off forever." Her fist was travelling, but the blonde beside her caught hold just before it could connect with the wall. "He used my temper against me, Renee. It's like he wanted me to put my all into decimating him...he didn't go easy on me by any means, but I think he wanted me to go overboard just so that would forever stick with me. I don't care what the others think, but Winters knew I'd consider myself weak to let my temper get the best of me to that degree. He was the one in control, not me." Williams looked in thought for a moment and soon chose a surprisingly blatant subject change... "One thing..." She matched the oddly colored wolf eyes of her longtime friend, "When he kneed you in the stomach, you backed up. You looked scared." "Did I?" Amy tried to cover it, but the after-affect of that jolt was still with her. Some odd adrenaline rush, as if her body had reacted without her brain to protect itself...but why?" "Come on, Ames...you've gotta remember that." The redhead raised an eyebrow, "It was just weird is all. He didn't even get me that hard, but I suddenly got shakey and rushed back. I didn't think to do it, so that sorta confused me. I wasn't scared of him, but that sent me to a sudden panic." She frowned and looked to the floor. "Again, I don't think I was in control there. But neither was he..." "Oh well," Williams nodded, "No big deal." At that point, Renee had given it little more than a second thought. Amy, however, was striving to figure her action in that split second out. It was like her to do something without conscious thought, sure...but those instances never involved backing off, protecting herself - They were always defensive. This, on the other hand, was simply confusing. Maybe nothing, but maybe something was seriously wrong and she didn't know it, but her body did? It was her stomach that got hit, after all - She'd been shot near there and, since just before her birthday, was frequently getting sick though that was written off generally as a flu that was hanging on. As Renee had said, though - It was no big deal, no immediate issue. She was being forced to see a doctor in the morning anyway, so whatever mystery there was could be solved there. Of course, the possibilities still stood... She laughed to herself as the conversation continued. Little more than silly thoughts.
