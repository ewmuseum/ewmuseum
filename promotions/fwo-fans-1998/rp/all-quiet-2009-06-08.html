All Quiet

8 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The large house was quiet tonight. Fairview Reed was locked away in the library, endlessly searching ancient texts and tomes. Mr. Yip was entrenched in his room, silent and inconspicuous as usual. The Assistant was in the basement gym, honing and toning her impressive physique. Burlington Werner was…indisposed this night, as he was last night and would be again tomorrow. And Siobahn McPhineas was aimlessly wandering their spacious rented manor somewhat at loose ends. Sure, there was a litany of things she could be doing, but sometimes just walking around with a cup of tea in her hands proved to be more productive for her than sitting at a desk staring at a blank screen, or making phone call after phone call after phone call. Sometimes just letting her mind wander was the catalyst she needed to accomplish what needed to be done. The full moon outside lit the interior of the house with its pale, ghostly glow. Siobahn moved to the huge picture window and looked out over the lake. The waters were calm and the night seemed at peace, and anyone looking at Siobahn would have thought the same of her. However they couldn’t see beneath her serene exterior to witness the tumultuous thoughts that she was dealing with. She smiled and sipped her tea. Mr. Reed’s tenure as a professional wrestler had taken an odd turn lately. His lower back had been weakened week after week and finally injured to the point of seriousness. Burlington Werner had declared that Mr. Reed could not participate in any strenuous physical activities for a reasonable period of time due in part to the circumstances of the ‘Free Wrestling’ promotion. Some loathsome cad actually had the audacity to hurl Mr. Reed into the waiting hateful clutches of the audience! Only the timely intervention and Herculean strength of The Assistant saved Mr. Reed from more serious injury, but the damage had been done and the reclusive ‘wrestler’ was forced to weeks of bed rest. With their only weekly engagement necessarily put on hold the entire household had gone quiet with individual activities. Somewhere off in the distance a wolf howled. Siobahn knew that their reprieve was only temporary; she had at least one important meeting with someone she’d never met in her future and that meeting would drastically change the course of their future. Mr. Reed was extremely anxious to continue his quest for The 9 Books and she knew that the Witch of Tiger Mountain would provide her with the first clue. That in turn would necessitate her entourage’s return to life on the road – a life she wasn’t entirely looking forward to. These last months on Mercer Island were comfortable, peaceful, and …well… boring, and Siobahn was loath to lose that. She was also fairly certain that another meeting was imminent; again with someone she’d never met before and again with far-reaching results. At least the two meetings would be working towards the same results – the road beckoned… A shooting star raced across the night sky. Siobahn smiled and nearly made a wish, but what did she have to wish for? Here she was, reasonably safe and secure among her dearest friends, engaged in the quest of a lifetime. Right where she was supposed to be. Who could ask for anything more?
