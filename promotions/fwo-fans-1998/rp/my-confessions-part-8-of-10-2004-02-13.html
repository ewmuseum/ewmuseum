My Confessions - Part 8 of 10

13 February 2004

MY CONFESSIONS Part Eight - Reaction "They're not coming..." Distane has just finished their set to a massive reaction. Valerian's Garden is onstage in ten minutes, soon as they can get their gear on, up, and tuned. Their charismatic, beautiful, reclusive lead singer usually needs two to three hours to properly 'prepare' herself for a performance between clothes, makeup, and mental prep. She's down to her last ten minutes. "I can't believe it, TJ," said Angel, as she sank against the back door of the building, "I reached out to them... introduced them to their granddaughter... showed them that I've made somethin' outta my life. And they didn't even show up?" TJ put an arm around Angel's shoulder. "I dunno what to tell ya, kiddo... there's nothing I can say, is there?" "No... not really." Angel's been crying all day after the fights she's had - this town brings out the worst in her. Makeupless, dressed in a black 'Misfits' tanktop and puke- green cargo pants, she's about as far away from the 'Goth Goddess of the Club Scene' that her fans are used to seeing her as. "Well... ten minutes," continued TJ, "If you don't think you can go on, we'll understand." He kissed her on top of the head and walked back inside. Angel rubbed her arms, shivering from cold or anxiety - probably a little bit of both. A beep from her pocket - one missed call on her phone. She grabbed it quickly... and when she saw the name "IVY" on the display, she started to cry again. Her parents didn't call. They didn't care. The only people in this time zone who do are inside... but this is something they won't be able to help with. Angel pressed "Redial" on the display and waited... Halfway around the world, Ivy McGinnis cursed leaving her cell phone on overnight. She figured it was Sammy, or Merritt, or someone who wanted her to do more work, but she reached for it anyways. "...hey.......?" "...They didn't come..." "Who..huh...whaa?" Ivy rubbed her eyes, then came halfway to cluing in. "Wait....Angel? Who didn't come?" "My parents," replied Angel, squeezing her eyes shut, "They've never heard me sing, they didn't come to the show... they didn't call! I haven't seen 'em in eight years, they just met their granddaughter and they treat me like I don't exist! Like I don't matter!" She buried her eyes in her hand. "I've played in front of thousands, Ivy... but I can't get the attention of the only two people I've wanted... what's the matter with me?" "Angel!" called TJ from inside, "Time!" "Shit..." She spat on the ground as she held the phone like a piece of precious hardware, "Ivy, it's time." "Hold on a second," countered Ivy
