In The Shadows - My Take On War

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello Retards, Well before I get to the crap all of you internet junkies are waiting to hear let me get some stuff off my chest first. First of all I'm not digging this whole overseas tour thing we're doing for the fWo. I came to the fWo thinking I'd be in my house country. But I'm stuck in places like Algeria. Algeria. Come one. The fWo sends me on an autograph session in Algeria. I sign my name on a picture and these Algerians look at it like I have three heads in the picture. I came to the conclusion they can't read. They couldn't read my name. Why the hell am I signing autographs for people who can't read? Not to mention I'd rather be on home soil due to all this war talk. Who knows what country really hates our guts. If you ask me, Bush is going to start world war III. The guy is going after Iraq and for what? Oh that's right, because he dad is yapping in his ear about it and he wants to finish the business his father couldn't. Yeah Iraq probably has dozens and dozens these "weapons of mass destruction" but so does some other countries Bush. You're an idiot. A complete idiot. North Korea is just waiting for us to invade Iraq. North Korea is the country with the weapons of "mass destruction". North Korea is the one who's firing practice missiles. North Korea is going to send nuclear missiles over to the US if we invade. Real smart Bush. Way to put your entire country in jeopardy because you're and idiot and your letting your dad back in the presidents' seat. By you defying the UN Bush it's like giving them the finger. Thanks for killing us all with your idiotic presidency. We here in the United States just want to say thank you for causing a major backlash in which you don't see coming and will hit home before you can stop it. And thanks to the fWo for putting all of our lives in danger by making us go overseas to perform. You really care for the safty of the people that make you money don't you? Pfft. Oh, and another note. fWo website crew still hasn't updated my stuff. Wonderful job guys. Really. I hope you get paid enough money to terminate those zits on your faces. Now to what everyone has been asking me. I open up my email and the billions and billions of my fans, yes I get email, wanted to know why I dislocated Deacons shoulder on Sunday night. Why? You guys really are retards. Deacon is a friend of Flair. Anyone who is a friend of Flair is an enemy of Steven Shadows. Besides, the guy actually thought he was going to be able to call me out like that and get away with it. Think again. But I hear he's recovering rather quickly, which is good. Because I think we should dance. One on one in the ring. Deacon thinks he's in the same league as me? We'll find out now won't we Deacon. The message I'm sending is simple. Flair isn't safe, his family isn't safe and his friends aren't safe. Sure the fWo has put a stoppage on Flair and I getting at each other. And I'm not going to fuck up my chance of beating the hell out of Flair at Cyberslam you can bet your last dollar on that one. But what I will do is make Flair suffer. Suffer more than he's ever suffered. Wednesday I'll be at Warfare. I have no idea what third world country we'll be participating in this week. I'm told to get on a plane so I get on the plane. All I need to know is that Flair will be there with that little whore Ivy. And his good buddy Deacon will be there too. Wednesday I'll offer Deacon the opportunity of a life time. To get in the ring with me and face the greatest living legend this business has ever seen. Unlike the war the US is going into, my war with Flair will be quick and painless. For Flair that is...not his loved ones. Peace, Steven Shadows
