Best Strategy Meeting Ever

30 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Some “other” wrestling promotions have pay-per-views that only last a couple hours. Not the FWO, no sir! The FWO has pay-per-views that last all weekend*! *Disclaimer: Well, there wasn't a whole lot of stuff going on overnight after the Frenchie/killjoy match, although dedicated viewers were privy to hours of quality, HD footage of an empty arena. Also, for the purposes of the Internet, Tuesday Wednesday is considered part of the weekend. Anyway, most wrestlers went home to sleep after last night, and Max Danger, accompanied by his manager and noted rabbit aficionado Leigh Landers, was no exception. I mean, pretty much everyone left, although I wouldn't be surprised if Lowell spent the night here. Max was looking confident as always, thanks in no small part to the expensive suit he had on and the ACW World Title contained in the bag he carried over his shoulder. Leigh had a bit of a skip in her step, excited about the opportunity Max had tonight. The chance for a shot at every title in the FWO! Who would they get for a tag team partner? EEEEEE! She couldn't help but get a little ahead of herself. One thing worried her though. Max's partner seemed a little... off? She knew about Mike Bear's history in the FWO. Four time Tag Team Champion. Two time World Champion. Main-evented Cyberslam. Unfortunately, she wasn't quite prepared for the reality of his, shall we say, style. Mike went missing for weeks with no explanation from his wife, even after repeated phone calls. When he finally resurfaced, he ran out in the middle of Max's match with Dave Morey just a few days ago to discuss Meltdown “strategy”, causing a distraction and a loss for Max. Then after the match he runs off again before they can get a hold of him, shows up at Meltdown Saturday to be a lumberjack in the Ross/Morey match and instead proceeds to get drunk at ringside, and finally disappears for a third time. Is he even going to show up for the match? “Yo Danger, my man!” Well I guess that answers that question. Running down an adjacent hallway towards them was the unfortunate looking Mike Bear, his jeans and FWO t-shirt in tatters, fresh claws marks on his face. In one hand, a clipboard, the other held a rather angry looking cat, squirming and clawing to get free. Unfortunately, Mike Bear is pretty big and he had a good hold on the cat. Max and Leigh turn to look at the unusual sight. Mike ran right up to the duo and stood upright, looking a little too pleased with himself for somebody who would probably get the FWO in trouble with the ASPCA and PETA, two fights they definitely didn't need right now “So, Marv. Can I call you Marv?” Max struggled for words, for once. “Uhhh...” “That's great! So Marv, what do you think of this cat?” He motioned with the clipboard to the cat under his arm, as if he thought Max would confuse it with the dozens of other stray cats backstage at the Key Arena. “I mean, do you think he could win a wrestling match? I mean, not against anybody good. Could he beat someone like El Janito from Mega Job or Keith Scott Zimmerman?” Max glanced over to Leigh, as if to ask, “This is my partner?” “That's ok, you don't have to answer right now. One sec.” Mike walked off a couple feet, opened a utility closet, and threw the cat in, slamming the door before it could escape. He walked back to Max and Leigh who were in utter shock at what they had just seen. Mike just had a big smile on. “So, down to business, right? Meltdown strategy! I worked up a whole list of strategies we could use to win the Meltdown match, and I got 'em right here.” Mike tapped the clipboard and puffed out his chest a little. He was still met by blank stares as Max and Leigh wondered about the cat. Mike's smile wavered a little. “Some of these strategies are real winners, I'll tell ya!” Nothing. Mike broke down. “OK you got me! I just had my intern come up with these, I'm sorry! Still, just because Fish came up with them doesn't mean they won't work. I mean take number one here: Hide under the ring until the end of the match. Right?” Mike nodded to them and motioned with his hands for them to keep up. That shook Max out of it. “Yeah, you went over that during my match on Wednesday.” Mike snapped his fingers. “Oh yeah, that's right.” “The match I lost.” “Right, yeah, try not to do that tonight.” Max twitched a little. “So number two...” Leigh interrupted this time. “You said that one on Wednesday, too.” “Oh, nuts, ok. Let's see, ah! Number three: Kidnap the families of the other wrestlers. That... might be a little heelish. Let's put that on the maybe pile.” Max nodded in agreement. Leigh's eyes were wide with horror. “Number four: Buy entry number 20. That's got legs. How much do you have on you?” “Max retrieved his wallet from his jacket pocket and checked. “Like 60 bucks.” “Awesome, so between the two of us we've got 62 dollars. Who's number 20?" "Isn't it a secret?" "Damn you're right, and that was the perfect plan!" Leigh shook her head. “Shouldn't you just try and eliminate all the other teams?" She was met with dead silence and blank stares. Mike slowly shook his head. "If we tried to win like everyone else does it, we wouldn't have a plan, now would we?" Max slowly nodded along with him. “Yeah, that's right. Oh hey, Do you know where we could get some grappling hooks? We could totally ninja our way to the rafters." "I know a totally awesome grappling hook guy! No, wait, that was the Batman cartoon I was watching." "You saw that too!" "Yeah, and then the Joker comes in and Batman's like POW! Good times..." Mike and Max reflect on the awesomeness of Batman: The Animated Series. Leigh clears her throat loudly, to no avail. Mike has completely forgotten the list he made Fish work overnight to pull together at the last minute back on Tuesday, but manages to have an epiphany of his own. "Oh, I've got it! The heavy guys are always the toughest to eliminate in a battle royale. What's the best way to gain 300 pounds in 5 hours?" "Twinkies?" "There would have to be a lot of them. I'll get my intern, Fish to look into it." "We could get two people to impersonate us -- your intern, Leigh -- then surprise everyone at the end!" "Awesome! The fine Underground tradition of impersonation. A solid plan... Oh wait, you mean convincing impersonation. Yeah, I'm not sure Fish can pull off 6'3"." "Lifts." “Well, there might be a slight problem of not being trained in wrestling or any kind of physical activity. I think we might be better off actually being in the ring ourselves.” Mike stroked his goatee, a little confused. That last bit made entirely too much sense. No better time for Max Danger to have an epiphany of his own. "Or, hey! We could beat them with this." Max reached into his bag and pulled out Armsy McDanger, the prosthetic arm. Mike's eyes went wide. "Oooooo. Does it do tricks? Can I touch it?" Max got a little nervous. “...s-s-suuuure." He handed it over tentatively to Mike, who threw his clipboard aside and got a little too friendly with the limb. "I'm gonna call you Armsy McGrizzly." Max was starting to get a little confused. "But it's not hairy." "YOU'RE RUINING THE MOMENT!" "I'm going to have to ask for that back now." Max tried to pull it out of Mike's hands but he resists. After a brief struggle, Max finally jerked it free and returned it to his bag. Mike pouted. "Aw man! Guess it's back to the search for another cat to be our manager. Oh, say, do you know any felines who can do a double moonsault?" "Besides Catwoman? Um..." Max thought about it for longer than should be necessary. "Nope. Sorry." "The search goes on...” Mike looked off to the side, behind Max and Leigh. They both turned around to see what he's looking at. Seeing nothing Max waved his hands in front of Mike, slowly, then frantically. He looked at Leigh, confused, and she checked to make sure he was still breathing. She shrugged, satisfied that he probably wasn't going to die. They walked off as Leigh tried to convince Max that the original plan of actually trying to win was the best plan. Mike's just gonna stay here a while.
