Where We Was???

1 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Man dis some bullshit!” CJ yells storming out of the Key Arena. “Whad da fuck Ro!?” Roland, dressed finely in his black with red pin striped suit, strolled out the door behind CJ. “Look man I tole you niggas not to fart in her office last week!” “My bad.” O added as he walked out the door. “Look niggas,” Junious chimed in as he walked to the front of the group. “We jus gots ta make a betta impression.” “A better impression!? We ain’t won a damn match yet!” CJ muttered kicking at the rocks. “An dis week we got bumped,” added the elder Slaughter. “Ro you besta get on dat phone an’ get our asses back on reAction!” “Yeah!” added Big O. “Man fuck dat… yall ain’t gon’ be treatin me like a stepchild! I got you two matches! What happened?!” Washington yelled pounding on his chest with both hands. “I been there fo’ yall… I put up dat money fo’ yall could get that highlight tape! Who sent that shit to Beaven, HUH? Me that’s who! Yall ain’t gon’ be puttin this shit all on my plate!” “So what you sayin’?” questioned June Bug. “You sayin we ain’t doin our thang cuz?!” “I’m sayin YOU AIN’T WINNIN!” Roland shot back letting the bird out of the cage so to say. “Hold up cuz,” interjected CJ with each hand edging for room between his friend and his family. “Ro’s right Junie. We ain’t won shit.” “I know he’s fuckin right!” Junious screamed slamming his fist into the rough concrete surface of the parking garage. “Why the fuck ain’t we winnin?!” “Why? Cause we ackin like we ain’t been here befo’,” CJ answered honestly. “Nigga back in LA we owned the city… every little bullshit promotion we could find so we could cash a check was standing in line fo’ the name cuz. A few niggas runnin round like a buncha low rent thugs…” “This is the big time June Bug… and it’s time to send a message.” Roland pled refusing to break eye contact with Junious. Junious stood there then turned his back to his friends, his family and people he trusts. His head nodded in agreement, “I feel ya.”
