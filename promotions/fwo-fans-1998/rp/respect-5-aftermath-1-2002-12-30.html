Respect (5 - Aftermath 1)

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Well, that didn't go at all how I had planned." I found myself in my locker room once more, completely oblivious to the rest of the world. All I could think about was the match I had just had, the match I had just lost, and the further disrespect showed to me by Sean Stevens after everything was said and done. I just couldn't figure the man out. I had taken him to the limit in our past two matches, yet he had still managed to pick up the victory. That alone was upsetting enough, but you'd think with all that I'd put him through during the matches themselves, he'd at least be man enough to shake my hand afterwards. If you take the whole misunderstanding about Ivy out of the picture, then the only real reason I can find as to why he'd dislike me so much is because I came up into the fWo so quickly. Maybe he doesn't think I've paid my dues, and just rode the popularity of the Survivor contest into the fWo, but that's hardly the case. All the nights in Dom's sweaty little gym, getting stretched into positions I didn't know my body would go into, getting pummeled into the mat time and time again until I learnt how to take a bump, the months overseas on tour in Japan, the constant heartbreaks of seeing other people get called up in my place because I wrestled a stiff style (the only way I knew how to wrestle, thanks to Dom and Alex)...didn't all that amount to a lot of dues paid? Dammit, I wasn't about to let him make all that hard work meaningless. Hell, I would've won that match if I hadn't been so aggressive. My strategy had been to come out and hit him hard, fast, and often, but at the end there, after I had done a nice job of what I had set out to do, I got overconfident, and Sean was able to use that against me. That's twice now that he's countered the Knightfall, and I aim to make sure there won't be a third time. How's that old saying go? "Fool me once, shame on you. Fool me twice, shame on me." Shame on me, indeed. I could've had that one, I could've had the one at CyberSlam, but I keep making the rookie mistakes that cost me the match. It's not like I'm even a rookie anymore, but any time I get in these big pressure situations, I tend to get too into the match, and it ends up costing me big-time. If it hadn't been for Brand, I'd probably still be looking for my first win on an fWo PPV. Maybe the problem is that I've let this get too personal. With GV, he made things personal, but I kept it in the ring and I ended up coming up with the big win at OSW's Head-On Collision. With Sean, this beef between us dominates my thoughts when I'm awake, and I'm afraid I'm going to start having nightmares about him beating me any day now. He's inside my head, and he's got that advantage before we even lock up. I've got to do something about that before tomorrow night, or I'm going to lose my third straight match to him, and then what am I going to have to do to earn his respect? Better not to think about that now. I need to unwind, ice my sore muscles, and get a good night's sleep. Tomorrow night is a must-win situation for me, and I can't fail again. I put on one hell of a show tonight, and as long as I can keep my wits about me tomorrow, I'll be able to put that one away.
