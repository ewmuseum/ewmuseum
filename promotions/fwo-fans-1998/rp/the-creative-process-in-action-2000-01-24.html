fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization The Creative Process in action January 24, 2000 Sid coughed nervously to himself and studied his notes. He had never given a solo interview before, and he was feeling slightly overwhelmed by the whole experience. Things were never this difficult back at the apartment he shared with David Skimmer in the Village. Sid pondered for a moment what David was up to, and then decided it was best not to think about. Over the last few weeks their relationship had become rocky, and this trip to England and France was going to serve as a nice break for the both of them so they could evaluate what future they had, if any. That stupid job... I can't believe it. I travel all over the place and that big meanie... David was a freelance wrestling writer and Sid had met him almost a year ago when he was trying to get backstage at CyberSlam to do some interviews. They had hit it off well, but... Rob. Stupid Rob. David had recently been spending a lot of time with some guy named Rob Snyder and Sid was beginning to suspect that they were seeing each other behind his back. It was enough to make him want to... well... he wasn't sure what. He mainly felt like collapsing in the corner and crying to himself. Life on the road was hard. Not the good kind of hard either. But the bad kind of hard. Usually because there wasn't any of the good kind. Sigh. Sid caught himself sighing aloud and made a mental note to stop it. People who sigh aloud are totally lame. He looked down at his interview cards again and read aloud what he had written so far. Hey fWo! My name is Sid Snows. You are all ded! Now I'm going to have secks with Alex Baldwin. Jealous, Slugger? Sid grumbled and crumpled the paper up and tossed it into the garbage. How did Machina make it so easy? He was the best at interviews... so good that everyone should give him lots and lots of money. He wished David was around to talk things over with. David was a very sensitive man, and Sid always felt more manly when he was around him. At the same time, Sid hated himself for wanting to see him. His mind wandered to Slaughter, and how Machina and Rudo had confronted the Underground. Maybe he could learn from what they did. All the same, they were going after the wrong people. If it was up to Sid, he'd go after Slugger... I mean Perfect. Or Fear maybe. Fear looked pretty hot in those leather pants things... I wonder if Fear would have a match with me? Sid rubbed his chin. It was possible. He would also have to work on getting Perfect into the ring... but how? It would take some thinking and concentration. He looked out of the window at some of the British men on the sidewalk. No worry about losing his concentration here. Icky poo. Sid picked up another piece of paper and started to work. Somewhere else, another interview was going on.
