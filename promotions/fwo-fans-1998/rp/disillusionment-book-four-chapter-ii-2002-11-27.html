Disillusionment: Book Four - Chapter II

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Let me ask you a question... As you've been around me, learning what I have to tell you and hearing about the life I lead, have you become afraid? Do you ever wonder if me telling you what I know is going to get you in trouble too? Do you ever sit back and wonder what would happen if the Disciples learned about your connection to me? Well ya better start. I learned something knew today...you're wanted by the Disciples too. Even though you most likely do not believe what I have told you, they think you know too much. I didn't give them their name, and I've never spoken about you to anyone, but somehow, they know. They've charged you with a crime against a civilian too, even though you did not commit one. I was eavesdropping on a radio that a gas station attendant was listening to as I filled up my bike. They're saying that I committed the murder of Mr. O'Brien, which is true. I wouldn't so much call it "murder" as "doing the right thing" but I was responsible for bringing his life to an end. Here's the kicker, though......even though you weren't even their when I did it, they are calling you an accessory to the crime. They're saying you stood guard, making sure no one else entered the store while I "maliciously" tortured and killed O'Brien. Maybe before you thought I was full of shit, but what do you think now? Now that they have you for a crime that you never even dreamed of committing, do you think that maybe I'm a little less crazy? When the Disciples view someone as a threat, they take steps to eliminate them. You're screwed. I'm going to try to help you through it, but eventually, the Disciples are going to capture you. They are going to torture you. And they are going to kill you. No one else that I've met has ever had the ability to survive that I do, and I doubt that you do either. I can try to help you, but I'm not going to walk you through it to the point where I put my own ass in danger. They must not think you're that big of a threat, though. The reward on your ass is only five-hundred grand. I wonder what Freud would say about this... "Bounty Envy." What made you keep hanging around me anyway? Didn't you know that eventually, they would find you out? The Disciples have people everywhere. No doubt they've seen us speaking to each other at one time or another. I wouldn't go home tonight if I were you. In fact, if I were you, I'd never go home again. You do and you're dead. You have to run. Get out of the city tonight. They'll be combing this place like a four-year-old with lice. The best thing for you right now is distance. I even went to the liberty of getting you your own motorcycle. It's parked behind the seven-eleven on Elm Street. The keys are underneath the dumpster. It wasn't easy for me to get, so try to make it last you a while, okay. Check the gas tank for detonators. The Disciples kind of operate in the same way as the Mafia. You don't have time to say goodbye to your friends and your family, and the Disciples probably have people following all of them around anyway, just hoping to get to you. Your old life......it's over. You don't have any friends. I'm not your friend. If it came down to it, I would tell people that I didn't even know you. That may sound selfish, but I have to cover my own ass. You'll learn that selfishness is the only way to operate. It no longer makes sense to try to help anyone, because they can stab you in the back in an instant. You need to get your ass running. Wrestling doesn't sound so important anymore, does it. I dunno, I still think about it. I'm not a loose canon, but I'm insane. I'll calculate and destroy, and that's what makes me scare myself.
