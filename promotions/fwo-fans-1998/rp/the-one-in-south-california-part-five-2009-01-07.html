The One In South California - Part Five

7 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pages 69 to 85 of The One In South California. *** *** *** *** *** *** I/E. HOME-345 WHITE BIRCH STREET Swarms of POLICE cars and DEA AGENTS have flooded the yard and driveway of the home, with fully-armed agents posed with their guns, waiting to be called in for action. About a dozen agents are at the house KICKING doors open and pointing M1014 Combat Shotguns and MP5 submachine guns at the four or five young MEN occupying the living room, who hit the deck, hands behind their heads. AGENT Everyone one the floor, hands on your heads! Hands where we can see them! If you scumfucks cooperate, we can get all get things done and you can start hiring attorneys! OCCUPANT We don't have anything anymore, man! Honest! God, you've got the wrong place, I swear to God! AGENT Do not fuck with us! We've got men on every square inch of this house, we will find every goddamn thing you have! (To a couple of other agents) Search their persons, I'm running check-up. (Into a walkie talkie) Under the front porch, tear up the garden, I don't care if you have to dig all the way to Bom-fucking-bay, this was a legit source and this home is hot, I guarantee it. The officers outside are running metal detectors and generally tearing up the premises, uprooting plants and breaking open anything made of wood in pursuit of illicit goods. Local police officers look on, allowing the feds to do their work, while still trying to look somewhat useful. OFFICER #1 You ever been a part of somethin' like this? OFFICER #2 Nah, man. Not at all, this is crazy. I'm expecting Tom Clancy to pop out at any moment. OFFICER #1 Yeah. (Ponders) Wait, what do you mean? OFFICER #2 You know, Tom Clancy. He was in Clear and Present Danger, Patriot Games, shit like that. OFFICER #1 No. No, he wasn't. He wrote those books, man. OFFICER #2 Nah, I remember. Definitely him. And he was in Air Force One, too. OFFICER #1 Are you kidding me? That's not Tom Clancy, that's Harrison Ford. He was Indiana Jones, for Christ's sake. What are you, fucking retarded? OFFICER #2 I don't know, man. I think you're wrong. Anyway, what are we supposed to do? These federal guys are pretty much taking control. OFFICER #1 We get to go on friendly neighborhood duty, just let the neighbors know what's up and let them know they're safe, you know? OFFICER #2 Oh, cool. Which house do we go to first? OFFICER #1 Well, I'm going across the street. Don't follow me. Really. CUT TO: INT. CROWN VICTORIA-LATER Nuñez rests his head on the steering wheel. Lowell is shirtless. He has the word "FRED" poorly tattooed across his lower abdomen region. Both are sweaty and look as through they've been idling for quite some time. In the back seat, the hitchhiker buckles and unbuckles his seat-belt repeatedly. NUÑEZ Stop clickin' that! HITCHHIKER Sorry, bro, didn't know it was bothering you. I'll stop. The hitchhiker buckles his seat belt for the last time, which almost sends Nuñez over the edge, but he's able to suppress his anger. The car begins to move again. Nuñez calms down a bit and Lowell look pleased, peering out his window. LOWELL Hey, look at this guy! Look! He's trying to squeeze his way in! HEY BUDDY, WE WERE HERE FIRST! WAIT YOUR FUCKING TURN! Nuñez grabs Lowell and pulls him away from the window. The hitchhiker sticks his head out the window entirely. HITCHHIKER Hey you, yeah you, fuckhead! Get the fuck away from our car! We drive a fuckin' Crown Victoria! That's a whole hell of a lot better than your piece of crap bicycle! Yeah! Did mommy and daddy buy that for you for graduation? Hmmm? Is it a 10-speed? Can you ghost ride it? Well, then ghost ride it into a fuckin' bus you CUNT! NUÑEZ Hey psycho, shut the fuck up, don't get us into some shit. HITCHHIKER Don't worry about it. This is between me and fuckstain! Did you hear that!? I called you a FUCKSTAIN! You STAIN of FUCK. The big, athletic, spandex-wearing BICYCLIST dismounts his bike. BICYCLIST What was that? Traffic stops completely. Lowell looks, panicked, at Nuñez. LOWELL Gimme the cigarette lighter. I'll make this guy disappear. NUÑEZ You just keep ya mouth closed and don't instigate shit. HITCHHIKER I said your bike's a piece of crap and so is your face. Got somethin' to say? Whatever. Fuck you. Only homos and liars with one testicle ride bicycles. Tell me: Do you sniff your own bike seat after a good, long run? Do you love the smell of taint? Does it give you a little, y'know, a little tweak down there? A little penile jolt? You big, bald queer. The bicyclist doesn't say another word. He picks up his bike and drives the back tire through the passenger's side window. Lowell screams. LOWELL Am I hurt?! NUÑEZ There's cops up ahead. Come on, let's go. We gotta get outta here. We're gonna have to foot it the rest of the way. LOWELL Walk?! HITCHHIKER Oooooooooh, big man! Big man usin' his bike to smash things! Where did you learn that, chief, your abusive alcoholic step-father? Did he give you a little too much attention? Did bedtime reading include Penthouse Letters and mutual masturbation? Gotta get all that aggression out by fucking with other people's property?! HULK MAD! HULK SMASH! The bicyclist reaches through the already open back window and pulls the hitchhiker out of the car, kicking and screaming. HITCHHIKER (CONT'D) OH FUCK! OH NO! The sound of PUNCHES connecting with a human face. Lowell and Nuñez escape through the driver's side door. On the other side of the car, where the scuffle is taking place, a COP arrives and tries to restrain the angry bicyclist. The bicyclist shrugs him off and SLUGS the hitchhiker in the face again, effectively laying him out on the concrete. Lowell and Nuñez navigate through the rows of immobile cars and disappear into an alley. FADE TO: I/E. HOME-346 WHITE BIRCH ST. A police officer stands at the door and rings the bell. A barely post-coital Cassidy swings open the door. CASSIDY What the fuck do you want?! Realizing who it is, Cassidy adjusts herself and turns white. CASSIDY (CONT'D) Oh. DEAN Ahem.. Is there uh, a problem here, officer? OFFICER #1 You tell me. Everything legal here? Consentual? Nothing against your will? CASSIDY Wait, are you asking if he's raping me?! OFFICER #1 I wasn't talking to you, miss. CASSIDY Huh? Dean tucks a cigarette into his lips and lights it. DEAN I'm sure that's not why you're here, sir. OFFICER #1 The brains of the operation, I see. No, in fact, speaking of operations, the reason we're here is just to let you know not to be alarmed about the goings-on across the street. We received a tip that your neighbors were involved in some illicit activity. Not to worry; we caught it early on, and we're taking care of the problem now. Your neighborhood is absolutely safe, we will be paying extra close attention to ensure this. But I'm also here to ask you if there's been any strange activity coming from the house across the street in recent weeks. Any late-night problems, anything out of the ordinary, anything you found strange about them? CASSIDY Yeah! They play music at all hours of the night. Loud music. Really shitty music, too. That electronic music, and not the cool stuff either, like Aphex. They played that fucking Abercrombie mall blaster shit. (Emulating the beats) Oompcha oompcha oompcha with some American Idol reject song sped up over top of it or whatever. OFFICER #1 Uh, yeah. You've been a huge help. Thanks. With that, the officer departs. DEAN Abercrombie techno? CASSIDY I know. Don't you hate that bullshit? DEAN Gimme your phone. Cassidy hands her cellphone to him. CASSIDY I'm gonna go change. Dean takes it and finds Lowell in the phonebook as Cassidy walks away. DEAN Lowell. You're a fucking idiot, first of all. (Brief pause) I'll tell you why: because the house is 345, not 346. (Brief pause) No, I can't just go across the street. The reason I know it was 345 is because 5-0's got more guys on that bitch than there are retards in your family tree. No weight, no play. Game's over, motherfucker. I'm not gettin' my ass shot up because you fucked off the wrong people. Dean snaps the phone shut. DEAN (CONT'D) Cass, get your shit, we're going back to Santa Barbara. CUT TO: INT. FAST FOOD RESTAURANT Lowell and Nuñez sit in The Jelly Roll, a fast food restaurant specializing in jelly and breasts. It's essentially Hooters, but with a wide assortment of jelly related items to pick from on the menu. Lowell cuts into his croissant, which sits in front of him on a small decorative plate, grape jelly oozing out the middle and begins to chew as obnoxiously as one would expect him to. LOWELL So, my brother called me while we were leavin' that hitchhiker guy to die. NUÑEZ This some kinda excuse for why you were so fucking slow? LOWELL No, no. He told me that the shipment of stuff got re-routed to Pasadena. Nuñez nods and sips a cup of coffee with lemon jelly squirted in it for extra "flavor." It results in a viscous yellow slime floating around the top of the coffee. He doesn't look to be enjoying it very much. LOWELL (CONT'D) Why the long face? There's jelly! And tits! What the fuck is there to think about?! NUÑEZ I'm just tryinna figure out what steps I took in my life that led my ass to find its way here. With you. LOWELL Funny how things work out, huh? NUÑEZ I wouldn't say "funny." LOWELL I remember back in, oh, 1993, when I was just a wee lad spending my days playing Dr. Mario. I would spend hours--days--trying to figure out how the game was kicking my ass so badly. I didn't know you could turn the pills sideways. One time, I just got so angry that I boomeranged the Super Nintendo controller right through my TV set. My dad would have kicked the holy living shit outta me, but he was fucking his secretary upstairs. She looked like the mom on "Who's the Boss?" Lowell finishes his jelly-filled croissant and shoves the plate aside. LOWELL (CONT'D) I'm dead tired, man. We should go find a bench and sleep for a few hours. NUÑEZ I know you're sayin' shit like that just to piss me off, so I'm gonna be a big man and just let it slide. Because if I let it get to me, it'll mean your ass. LOWELL Uh, yeah. Yeah, you totally get that I'm joking. We get each other, bro. It's a joke. NUÑEZ If you're done with your jelly shit, I'ma get up, I'm'unna maybe get myself a Vitamin Water, and then we're gonna get outta this motherfucker. I'm gonna pray that in those 3 minutes, you learn how to keep your mouth shut and get some shit done. Call y'boy, let'm know what's goin' down. CUT TO: I/E. STOREFRONT-SUNSET JUNCTION, SILVER LAKE, LOS ANGELES While Cassidy explores one of the small stores, Dean sits on the ground outside, wrists resting on his knees and duffel bags at his feet. Cassidy's phone begins to buzz in his pocket, and he looks at the call ID before answering. DEAN Lowell, I thought I told you that I couldn't fucking help you? (Brief pause) Re-routed? Sure. Look, I have no doubt someone's shipping something somewhere. But I do doubt that you know where the fuck that somewhere is. And I'm just not sure if that "something" encompasses federal agents. (Brief pause) Y'know, fine. We've come too far at this point. You get to Franklin ASA fucking P. We'll have a car, we'll go from there. You strapped? Then you're gonna have to get pieced up on the way there. I don't know, figure somethin' out. Go to a gun shop, get some sticky fingers going. Cassidy and I are taken care of. If we're scoring an interception here, it's gonna take more than two pistols to coup the crop, even if I am the Kid. Seriously. And we're doing this on my terms. Get one with a silencer. Call when you're near Sunset and we'll meet you on Franklin, go to Pasadena from there. Dean snaps the phone shut and enters the store, seeing Cassidy blissfully gossiping with the pseudo-Goth girl, HIBISCUS, at the counter. CASSIDY Dean c'mere! DEAN Cassidy, we need to go. CASSIDY Okay but come over and meet Hibiscus-Marie. DEAN Hibiscus-Marie. Yeah. Is that your last name or is a hyphen involved? HIBISCUS It's my name. DEAN Yeah, I'm aware. I'm glad we got to the bottom of that one. (Fake charming smile) Who or what is responsible for that being your name? HIBISCUS I don't know. What makes any name feel right? DEAN Ah yes, the age old question. So, your parents didn't give you that name, then. HIBISCUS No. DEAN Okay, another mommy and daddy question: did they beat you too much or did they not care enough to beat you at all? CASSIDY Dean, be nice! She's is a really close friend of my sister. DEAN Alison? CASSIDY No, Roxy. DEAN Oh, well then! She's family! I hope you know I jest. It's love, sugar, it's all in affection, Hibiscus. HIBISCUS Hibiscus-Marie. DEAN Yes, Marie. Now, Hibiscus-Marie, let me ask you a question: do you drive to work, or do you use one of those faggoty little Vespas to get around? HIBISCUS I drove. Why? DEAN Do you have any kind of sentimental attachment to your car? HIBISCUS What? DEAN A lot of history? HIBISCUS No, I mean, it was my mom's. DEAN Oh, so it probably brings back a lot of painful memories of your mom neglecting you in the backseat with the windows up while she shopped, then. (A smirk and a pause) You might be wondering why I'm asking you so many personal questions. Believe me, darlin', it has zero root in any interest of mine. I'm gonna cut to the point: we're gonna need to borrow your car. HIBISCUS Um. How 'bout "no." DEAN No, no, no. Hibiscus-Marie, we're a family. And families help one another. You will get your car back. HIBISCUS And just what in the fuck am I supposed to do in the meantime? Dean looks around the store. DEAN Sell overpriced t-shirts to hipsters, apparently. Where do you live? HIBISCUS Los Feliz. DEAN Let's not chisel away question-by question here, sweetheart. Give me an address. HIBISCUS Well, I'm staying with a friend of mine, White Birch Street. DEAN Tell me it's 345. HIBISCUS No, why? DEAN Don't worry about it. What house is it? HIBISCUS 346. DEAN Oh, good. I know that area well. What time is it now? Like 3? 4? You'll have a car in the driveway tonight, I promise you. HIBISCUS What do you even need my car for? Why can't you take a cab? DEAN Listen, dear: the sooner you stop asking questions, the sooner we can be out of here, and the sooner you'll have your car back. CASSIDY It would help us out a lot. Hibiscus sighs and pulls her keys out, dropping them onto the counter. DEAN 'Atta girl. Dean snatches the keys from the counter. CASSIDY Thanks, Biscuits! CUT TO: EXT. STREET-WATTS Lowell nervously follows Nuñez down the street. NUÑEZ We ain't got all day, move y'fuckin' ass, kid. LOWELL You, uh.. You sure this is a safe neighborhood? I mean, I wouldn't want to have to get medieval on a'ybody, you know? NUÑEZ Naw. It's not safe. And when you start talkin' with that accent, it gets less safe. Quit actin' so fucking nervous, this is my turf. What'd your boy have to say? LOWELL Oh, right, yeah. Dean said we've gotta get quote-unquote pieced up and get to Franklin Street fast. He said to get a silencer. NUÑEZ Right, right. Call him up, tell him to meet us in South Pasadena. I'ma step inside, get some cash, get strapped like a backpack, and we'll take the blue line, transfer, get up there on the rail. Don't get in any fuckin' trouble while I'm gone. Hide in a bush or sum'n. Nuñez walks up the steps to his home, while Lowell crouches near the steps, trying not to be noticed as he calls up Dean. LOWELL Hey, uh, we're gonna meet you in Pasadena. Wait. South. South Pasadena. Nuñez is inside gettin' all Mexican with his familia or whatever and gettin' his... (Whisper) ...guns... (Normal voice) ...and stuff. We're gonna take the train right to the station. Adrenaline's rushin', Dean-o! I can feel it comin' in the air tonight! The day is ours! Wait at the station for us, we'll be there. White Honda. Check. We're comin' faster than Speedy Gonzalez losing his virginity. (Looking around and shouting) I mean, Mexicans have more stamina than white guys! Not that I'd know, I fuckin' hate white people! CUT TO: INT. NUÑEZ'S HOME KITTY, a pretty young black woman sits in a beaten-up chair in the living room of the small home. She has a small baby girl on her lap, whom she playfully bounces atop her knee. NUÑEZ Daddy's home! Kitty nearly lets her jaw hit the floor, picking up the baby and standing up. KITTY Baby! What are you doing here? Nuñez is genuinely happy to see her, showing uncharacteristic affection, and takes the baby from her, kissing it and holding it up against him. NUÑEZ I got an offer on the outside, made my way out. KITTY Oh God. We have to run, don't we? I don't know if I can do this again, baby. I can't handle all this. I thought that was the last time. I thought you were gonna get out and just live a clean life for us. (Becoming more visibly upset) You promised me. NUÑEZ Not yet, mama. After tonight, I'm callin' Cash and Bone. Jester. I'll have enough weight to unload to those guys that we'll have money to leave by tomorrow. You could quit the hospital. We're gonna move to Sacto, 'member? Never work another day. Neither of us. Prep schools for Montana, and we'll be out of LA for good. I'm serious. He hands Montana back over to her mother and takes his wifebeater off. KITTY (With an air of exhaustion, shaking her head) I'm tired of running, Jay. NUÑEZ I gotta get ready though. Don't go anywhere while I'm gone. I'ma be back tonight. We can start lookin' for houses in the mornin'. He walks over to the closet and searches deep within until he finds two backpacks. He steps out of the closet and pulls open a drawer, pulling out a Kimber Eclipse Target semi automatic pistol and a clip, shoving it into the gun with a loud CLICK. He tosses it into one of the backpacks, along with a suppressor. He pulls out a small 10" barreled HK416 assault rifle and 2 STANAG magazines. Opening another drawer, he pulls out a black bandanna, throws it in the pack, and a black San Antonio Spurs jersey, which he pulls on as he heads back out into the other room. NUÑEZ (CONT'D) I'll be back before you know it, arright, babygirl? He kisses Montana and Kitty on the cheek and then makes his exit. Nuñez comes out of the house with one backpack on his back and an empty one, which he tosses to Lowell and leads him down the street. LOWELL Wait... this is empty! What the fuck, maaan? NUÑEZ You think I'd trust your dumb ass with a weapon? 'Fuck outta here. LOWELL So what the fuck am I supposed to do?! NUÑEZ First, you stop talkin' that shit to me, less you want your jaw wired shut. When we get to Pasadena, you're not goin' in the house, nigga. We've gone through too much to fuck this up now.
