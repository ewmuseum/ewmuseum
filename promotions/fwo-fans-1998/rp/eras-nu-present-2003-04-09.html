Eras: Nu Present

9 April 2003

Soundtrack: "Time Consumer" by Coheed and Cambria Series: Eras (1/4) It had been a week since Kevin had debuted in the fWo. Since then he had spent some time getting to know a few other members on the roster, but everything had changed once Silver Fox had returned. Fox had asked Kevin to come to his office; he apparently had wanted to talk to him about his... status or something. So on Sunday night as Slaughter was being taped, Kevin made his way into the makeshift office of Silver Fox. Kevin cleared his throat, "You wanted to see me sir?" "Oh yes..." Silver Fox acknowledged his presence, "please have a seat." Kevin sat down in the chair, his hands clenched into fists, sweating. He was nervous, almost afraid. Afraid of rejection. Afraid of being sent back to Orlando. Afraid because he hadn't been booked for Slaughter. Afraid because nobody had even noticed his debuted. Afraid for so many reasons. The biggest of which was that he was afraid of the words now coming from the mouth of Silver Fox himself. "Kevin, I'm not really sure how to put this. We were..." Silver Fox paused, and Kevin's heart stopped, "pleased, yes, very pleased with your performance last week. Things have been hectic around here as of late, and I hadn't been able to connect with you, so let me just say that your future, it looks bright here in the fWo." A droplet ran down Kevin's face, but it wasn't sweat, it was a tear of joy. He had made it. A smile formed on his face, and Silver Fox extended a clipboard to him. "I can't give you a full contract yet, but we have one that is quite a step up from the developmental contract we had you on. I know the figures aren't huge, but they'll get bigger, and this should set you until after Cyberslam where we can hopefully hammer out something more... permanent. Sound okay?" Silver Fox explained. "Of course..." Kevin said, beaming as he raced to sign his name on the paper as fast as possible, as if he was still afraid Fox would take it away as some sort of awful joke. Silver Fox smiled back, "Great great..." He stood up from his desk and extended his hand towards Kevin. Kevin too stood, and shook his hand. "I see great things for the Dynamo young man." Fox said. "I won't let you down sir. You won't regret this!" Kevin replied enthusiastically. Kevin sighed to himself as he closed the office door behind him. He was a member of the fWo. Not a trainee, not a staffer, a real, honest-to-god wrestler. He felt warm on the inside; it was a feeling of elation. A feeling that took him higher than he had been for years. A feeling of emotion that he often saw in his father's face as a child. It was a feeling of happiness. It was a feeling that he was unaccustomed to, but a feeling that he could easily grow used to. A feeling, and a moment, he hoped would last forever. He smiled as he walked back towards the food service area, listening to the fans in the background as they filled the arena with their noise. One day they would cheer for him, and Kevin... He couldn't wait.
