BETWEEN ANGELS AND INSECTS Prologue Seven years ago, Alicia McGinnis Flair plunged to her death from the Golden Gate Bridge, while driving to an arena to meet her husband, Elijah, for a wrestling event. Eight months ago, give or take, Eli Flair was finally able to come to terms with the fact that she's gone. While that might seem pathetic to some, it's much less so when you know some more facts about him. When he lost Alicia, they had been together in some fashion for the better part of seven, eight years. It was Alicia and her family that helped Eli cope with the death of his mother when he was fifteen. She had been a part of his life for more than a third of it. Adding to the problem is the fact that every time Eli Flair has thought he found someone who could ease his loneliness.... It's ended badly. Very badly. Either the girl has died, or she's emotionally and psychologically torn his heart out and squeezed as hard as she could. Until now. Three months ago, Eli met a girl at a club in Hollywood, named Angela. She's a Goth/industrial singer in an extraordinarily talented but overlooked band called Valerian's Garden. They have a regular gig at a club called "Fast to Nowhere." She and Eli have little to nothing in common, but they have a strange kind of bond. But they also have tremendous ambition. Eli Flair, to continue to be the wrestler he has been for the past seven and a half years, and Angela, to get her band signed and climb out of the shithole that California has become.
