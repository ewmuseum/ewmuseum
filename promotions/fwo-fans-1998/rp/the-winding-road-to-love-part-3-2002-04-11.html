The Winding Road to Love (Part 3)

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Get my music ready," came the words from the mouth of Monte Burns. "I'm going out to the ring after this commercial break." The co-producer looked up from his desk near the curtain and frowned. It wasn't that he was surprised by Monte's request; wrestlers often showed up at the entrance demanding to go out without warning or notice. Still, it always disappointed him to feel like he was at the mercy of the wrestlers even though he was one of the men that allowed them to look so good on camera. Of course, the feelings of the producer were of no concern to Burns right now. After all that had gone on the last few weeks between he and Tempest - from relevation at Body Count to the beating the following week to their latest tense encounters backstage - Monte felt as if there must be some kind of force working against him on this one. Monte had always gotten whatever he wanted, whether it was toys and gifts as a kid to taking over his father's company as an adult. Even things as simple as drinks and food were brought to him whenever he asked. But now, at a time when he should have been entering his prime as an athlete, Monte Burns was being told "No" by a young woman from Hong Kong. Despite what Burns might say publicly, the fact that Tempest had turned him down following Body Count had only served to heighten his desire for her. It wasn't necessarily about lust, although Monte certainly recognized how nice Tempest's fit body was. But Monte saw something in her playfullness, her zest for life that he had lacked for so long. When he sat down one day to think back on enjoyment in his life, he was shocked to find that he had so little moments that he could look back upon and have a good laugh about. With the beautiful and precocious Tempest around, Burns would surely have his fair share of moments that he would remember for a long time to come. Tonight, however, would be about a little bit of business and a little bit of pleasure. Despite his feelings for Tempest, Burns understood that he still had a job to do as a wrestler, and part of that job included competing in and winning an important match at Cyberslam, the equivalent of the Super Bowl in professional wrestling. What better way to cap off a wonderful first year in the fWo than to defeat his love at Cyberslam and capture the fWo Cruiserweight Championship for the fourth time? Yet, aside from the title, Burns wanted to prove himself to Tempest one last time. He knew that Tempest respected guys for what they did in the ring, and he felt confident that by putting on an excellent match with her on the biggest stage possible, things could possibly change between the two. "One minute, Burns," said the producer, temporarily putting Monte's thoughts on hold. Now it was time to tell the world just how he felt about things and just what he was looking forward to in the future. As the beginning chords of "Nobody's Real" started up, Burns stepped forward just behind the curtain. A long smile crept across his face. Monte Burns was confident once again.
