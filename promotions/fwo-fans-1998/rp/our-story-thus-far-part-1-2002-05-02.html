Our Story Thus Far (Part 1)

2 May 2002

Las Vegas, Nevada April 27, 20002 The Bellagio Room 342 The bed's large enough for several people, but there's only Jasmin Desjardin, face down in the center of it. Her head is cocked to the right, keeping the phone held in place between her ear and the mattress, while she starts to wind down from the events of the day. CyberSlam had been what she expected, save for the hope she'd held out of finding Pierre there. She had hoped, but knew that he hadn't been there. She would have sensed him had he been there, of this she was certain. All that was left was to call home, talk to her husband, and crash, before abandoning her search and returning home the next day. Thomas had picked up on the first ring. The conversation had been the same as it had for the last three weeks. No news to report on her quest. She was no closer than she'd been three weeks ago, and she'd racked up a pretty good bill for her troubles. That only led to more money arguments, as the real problem was left unsaid. Why was Jasmin spending her time hunting down her friend and former student while leaving her husband by himself and her current class of trainees in the hands of another teacher? Why was he so important? She hadn't been faithful to Thomas, and it was getting harder and harder to pretend that he was blissfully unaware. There would be a lot of work to do when she returned. An hour had passed by the time their conversation had ended, and God only knows how much that would add to her bill. When the day came when she did run across Monsieur Delacroix again, she would make sure that he recompensated her for her expenses during this quixotic journey. Mere seconds passed after hanging up the phone before it rang again. "Mrs. Desjardin, this is the front desk. Someone left a message for you while you were on the phone. Would you like me to play it for you?" "Absolutely, yes." "Jasmin, this is Mike Baer. You've been trying to reach me. I'll be in Vegas on Thursday. If you're still there, I'd like to meet you. We have....some things to talk about." Jasmin grabbed a pad of paper and jotted down his phone number. This was exactly what she needed to hear.
