Absent Elements

6 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 1, 2003 Seattle, Washington. One day removed from the fWo Ruahh~! Show most of the road crew and technicians have packed up and moved out. On to their next destination, they've left the trials and tribulations of Seattle behind. If only it was that easy for everyone. Though the show was over the events still lingered in the thoughts of Brandon Roche. Once again, this time by someone he called friend, he was kept from that bastard Vic Creed. Once again another wrong would need to be righted before he could get at the man that had single handedly destroyed his life. If that was his only concern Brandon would have counted himself lucky. No, it wasn't that easy though, in his life he had learned that things were not darkest before the dawn. They were just gray, before becoming even darker. Every once and awhile there would be a glimmer, a golden moment, but it would oft times prove too fleeting to latch on to. Too quickly it would pass, in the blink of an eye the gray would return. Much like it had done now. Months ago Brandon saw an angel walking in the rain. He had seen her earlier, in a meeting with the other members of the fWo, sitting in the corner, keeping to herself. His breath was lost. When he saw her in the rain though it was as if he was given the opportunity he needed to get to know her. Little did he know how fast he would fall for that angel. With her in his life a golden light shown through the gray. What had he done to really earn her in his life? It was a question he often asked himself. Now though and with the events of the last week planted firmly in his mind, he wondered what he had done to be teased with happiness so often and never have it. The knock on the door broke his thought. After the third rap on the heavy door Brandon finally stretched his legs and approached the barricade. Not even peeking through the peephole he knew who it was. In the past her showing up would have resulted in the door being thrown open and an ungodly long embrace afterwards. This time however he simply reached for the tarnished latch, flipping it. Walking away, he greeted the person still on the other side, not exactly with the kindest expression either, "Come in if you want." Almost the same as she'd been when he first contacted her in October, Amy was soaked well through to the bone - shivering just enough to be visible in the cool rain and sighing with a slight grin when she finally staggered into the far warmer, dry room. Her relief didn't last long though - the almost pleasant expression she had quickly dimmed when her eyes briefly met Brandon's and instantly the smaller redhead looked away, her gaze lingering on the floor. There was a bandage spotted on the outside with blood patched across Amy's forehead, inhibiting much along the lines of a pleading look from her...her eyebrows didn't want to raise in the slightest, at least not without a gush of blood and pain. So she made do...eyes finally meeting Brandon somewhere around the chest. "Look..." She began and just as quickly hesitated, "I...well, what can I say?" "To start, maybe an explanation as to why I had to hear something like that from someone like Kinkade instead of you?" He looked to her, trying not to simply let his heart melt as it did every time he looked to her. He deserved answers. "Or how it all happened? When was this? Any one of those would be something to say..." Amy sighed, "When I had just turned eighteen, I was going to art school in the states...New York, specifically, using the school as an excuse to get experience fighting. I met him because he was trying to protect me, we were hanging out and got kind of inebriated one night and...well, you know how that kind of thing winds up. Once, Brandon...I looked at the situation on my hands and didn't want to deal with it - I got out of his way as quickly as I'd come in." Huffing a bit at her response, Brandon probed further, "So if that's all it was then why the big cover up? A few weeks ago when he hugged you, and I supposedly let my imagination run wild, you lit in to me for not trusting you. What exactly am I supposed to be feeling now, confidence in our future?" As much as he tried the mighty hero simply couldn't let this go, not with his history. He knew Amy Campbell was nothing Anastasia was, not by a long shot but the fact remained he had been burned enough to not let his feelings cloud his judgment. He looked at her now though, not being able to tell if allergies had set in or if in fact those were tears harboring themselves on her eyelids. Shaking his head a bit, he turned his back on her once more and trodded off, attempting to latch his suitcase he let his words sink in with Amy and awaited the next step in understanding. "How can I tell you what you should think? All I can do is answer your damned questions." She shot back at him and suddenly retracted, blinking away tears as she made her way back towards the door slowly. "I didn't tell you because it wasn't anything, plus...well...you're the hero. You're Brand Frontier and it's your downfall sometimes; I didn't want to mention anything because it wasn't something I was proud of an certainly wasn't 'moral' - you would've looked down upon me for it." "And..." The redhead suddenly recalled particular wording in his past statement, "I sure as hell have not done a thing with or even SPOKEN to Kellen without wanting to pry his eyes out since. He's changed entirely...and I didn't even feel right near him THEN, so don't you dare try to make any accusations - I'm not the whore you married, remember?" Another scathing remark hit the tip of Amy's tongue, "I'm the whore you left her for." "Listen Amy, you need to calm down. Lower the voice. We don't have to conduct ourselves like this." Brandon had raised his hands and inched closer to Amy, if it was an unconscious decision or not she was retreating, both emotionally and physically. "All I'm saying is that when that bastard tried to pull the rug out from under us and I asked what was up you could have simply told me what fueled his fire. You believe the hype machine the fWo uses too much if you think I'd look down on you for making a mistake, even a mistake as big as Kinkade. I wasn't trying to accuse you of doing anything with him since we've been together, but it was obvious that there was something more going on than his lust for that belt you saddled yourself with." He had now managed to close the gap between them, standing a few inches from her now her shivering was more evident. The warmth of the room had been comforting to her but the icy words exchanged by the two kept her from calming down. Brandon continued, not wanting Amy to use the silence as a cue to exit, "I want you to come to me with the darkest secrets you have. If I can't live with them then let that be my decision, not one you made for me. I love you. I really don't know a simpler way to tell you that, if you let me, I'll always be here for you." "I know..." Was Amy's immediate reaction, but she still backed up a pace. "It's just...yeah, have you got any idea how many messages Ana's left on my voice mail since she found out you were mad? I get told almost every day that it's gonna be the end for us because I'm not nice enough for you...I get kinda edgy." Listening to her words Brandon decided that the time for being a pushover was long gone, he didn't exactly want to be a dead horse but Amy really needed to understand the level of commitment he had for her. Maybe if he didn't simply forgive and forget, or melt at her words she would understand. Maybe. "Edgy is one thing, and maybe we can chalk it up to the winners you've picked in the past but I've given you no reason to be edgy around me. If you want to listen to what someone on the outside looking in is saying then you're not who I thought you were. Screw that red head back on straight and realize what we are together." "Well what are we?" Amy's eyes were pleading for answers now, "All I see is two people who happen to live and sleep together...I really do love you, Brandon - But that's what we seem to be to you." Exhausted, Brandon could only respond with what was in his heart, no forethought or planning, no script, just his feelings, "And I really do love you, Amy. You said you couldn't tell me how to feel and the same holds true for you. I can only be there for you, every second of every day. I can only think of you a hundred times with the blink of an eye. I can only be there for you... if you let me." He used those words to finally close the distance between the two and as he touched her shoulders softly he continued, "We're two people, in love, taking it one day at a time. That's all I've got right now. If you need more I don't know if I can give it. You are everything to me." "I don't ask for anything more than understanding," Amy managed a meek smile, sighing with relief while she untied her rain-logged hair to dry in the open air. "And I'm sorry for holding back, I just didn't want you to...well...yeah, be put off by any mistakes I've made." Pulling her to him Brandon shirt soaked through, the wetness along with the cool air caught his breath, as did the fact that he was finally holding her. After such an ordeal he didn't know if he ever would again. He knew how skittish she was under the best of circumstances, and this sure as Heaven hadn't been on of those. Knowing though that what they shared, if given the chance should be enough he steadied himself for the coming storm. He knew it was bound to get worse before it got better. "Just know that I've made my share, maybe we'll sit down one day and share. We can tell old war stories and you can see how much of a jackass I used to be." Brandon consoled, "It'll be alright Amy, if nothing else, we'll survive." The hug continued, both afraid of letting go. In more ways than one... [This has been a Brand Frontier/ Aimz collaboration.]
