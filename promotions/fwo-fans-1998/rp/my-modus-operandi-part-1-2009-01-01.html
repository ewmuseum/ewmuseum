My Modus Operandi - Part 1

1 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“I love you.” Her voice sounded so sweet in the chilly night of New York. A city otherwise filled with crime and violence became a haven of a strong and powerful sense of love. Even in the night, I could make out every distinct feature of beauty on her face. Her beautiful sky blue eyes sent me into a dream world full of lovely fantasy. Her smooth and soft skin sent an excited chill up my spine, as she carefully caressed the side of my rough and prickly face. I closed my eyes momentarily in order to allow all of my five senses to grasp the beauty of this moment. I felt her hand take hold of mine, and our unity slowly heated our chilly fingers. I opened my eyes and looked deep into her gaze. Everything about her was perfect; her cute button nose, her long silky brown hair, and her gorgeous smile. There was nothing on her that was unpleasant. ”I love you too,” I replied timidly through a quivering voice. She smiled wide and emotional tears filled her eyes. My heart fluttered in response. I had never been one to verbally censor my emotions; but emotions of love were foreign to me. Never before had my lips quivered at speaking my feelings towards another. Than again, prior to this moment the only emotions that I ever evoked were those of hatred and rage, or of depression and sorrow. Never had an emotion taken over my body in such a way that I quivered not out of fear, but out of cheerful emotional overdose. I felt warm tears forming in my eyes, and they slightly burned due to their infrequency upon my face. But her strong and comforting embrace calmed the sensation and I gladly accepted her love with an embrace of my own. An embrace was also foreign to me. I could feel my knees grow slightly weaker, as if I was imagining myself on a cloud. Such imagery and feeling seemed like something out of a horrid commonplace love story, but they were real to me. In cases of physical contact with another, I was usually weak in the knees from physical or emotional pain…never of pleasure and delight. I grasped her tightly and pressed my face upon hers. I whispered soft words into her ear. “I will never let anyone hurt you. I will always be there for you.” “Fuck you!” I stuttered in shock. “W-what?” “Let go me of me, you bastard! Let go me of me!” she shrieked in horror and moved violently to swat my embrace away. I quickly released my grasp, but held onto her shoulders softly and stared into her eyes, looking for the answer to her sudden emotional outburst. She was no longer shedding tears of joy, but tears of terror. She moved her body around violently and beat her fists into my chest rapidly. I could feel my eyes widening in horror in my attempts to keep her calm. ”What happened?” I asked timidly. “What did I do? What did I do?” ”You’re lying to me and you know it!” she screamed as she finally connected with a stiff punch into my lip. I quickly released my grasp on her and pushed her down into the snowy clumps below us. I pressed my chilly hand onto my swollen lip and looked down at her as she crawled into a fetal position and shook horribly before me. ”Go ahead, hurt me again! Does it make you feel more like a man? Do you feel stronger, you sick fuck!” ”Jessica…,” I muttered. “Don’t you dare say my name with such love and compassion, you heartless son of a bitch!” she shouted back at me. Her gaze returned to mine and a frightening chill took over my body. Never before had a person’s scornful stare intimidated me, why did her stare do that? It was because those eyes were eyes that I trusted, eyes that delivered onto me a great sense of calm compassion and serenity. They were eyes that I could rest with. However, they were now eyes that shot a whole through my most inner id. “Jessica, I didn’t do anything to you,” I said with a voice of reasoning. ”Didn’t do anything to me?” she yelled in disbelief as she struggled to get to her feet. “Then what do you call this?!” She pulled back the neckline of her beautiful white winter coat and revealed a large bruise across her neck and shoulder. I looked at the bruise in shock and took a few steps forward to console her, but she shrieked in horror and anger and her scream froze me in my place. ”Who did this to you?” I asked through gritted teeth; clenching my fists of vengeance while thoughts of ghastly revenge ran through my mind. She looked at me in pure shock. “What are you nuts? You did it!!” ”What? I would never…” ”Never what, hurt me? Oh really? Then what do you call this…and this…and this?” She continued to rip articles of clothing from her body, and with each article of clothing that fell softly to the snowy ground came the revelation of another bruise, another mark, another cut that was left on her body. With every exposure of physical torment upon my love, I felt a sense of helplessness take over my body. She accused me of all of her physical torment, but I promised never to hurt her again. I pleaded for her to stop, but she wouldn’t. Her voice ripped through my hand-covered ears and pierced through my heart and soul. I collapsed to my knees and clenched tighter onto my ears until they grew numb. I tried to cloud her accusations, but they continued entering my field of audible range. I begged for her to stop over and over, but my requests fell on deaf ears…at least, for awhile. I heard a soft thud. She stopped yelling. An eerie silence took over. I could hear every soft snowflake falling to the ground, but the world seemed to have stood still. I felt a warm sensation over my otherwise chilly hands. I cautiously lifted my eyes to my hands and saw white of winter frost become tainted with the red of crimson blood. Questions filled my mind as I my frightened stare remained fixated on my open wounds. My head became woozy. I felt my body rocking softly back and forth. I felt my body rush to the ground, but felt no painful sensation. I could feel the rumble of flesh smacking soft snow, but felt no cold feeling press upon my face. My body began growing numb, and just before my eyes closed for the final time, I heard her soft cry. “What did you do?” Regret. Shame. Depression. So, these are my final emotions. …before awaking to the dismal sounds of “Hurt” by Johnny Cash. “I hate this song,” I muttered through the sleeve of my trench coat, as I nestled my heavy head deeper into my arm. “Well, that’s too damn bad, ain’t it? Surprised you ain’t dead yet, son. I never saw a son of a bitch drink two bottles of that fuckin’ ignition fluid and wake up 15-minutes later to tell the tale, you some kinda’ alien or somethin’?” The rough raspy voice, aged by father time, came from Clinton Coldren. Coldren was one of the few men in this town that I could stomach for more than a minute. “If this is the kind of music people listen to, then yes,” I said with a docile tone in my voice as he lifted my head up. With a few stretches and cricks to my neck, I pushed myself to a vertical base and snatched my leather trench coat off the bar stool and headed towards the exit. “Callin’ it a night already?” Clinton asked with a chuckle in his voice, finding comedy in my liver’s inability to maintain my newfound alcoholic lifestyle. I raised a hand and gave a simple wave towards Clinton without turning around. But, as I went to exit Clinton’s fine establishment, I was persuaded to pause by the familiar sound of fist meeting face. I stopped only a few feet away from the door and concentrated on my hearing, not a simple task while under the influence. I questioned the legitimacy of the violent sound until I heard it a few more times, each one growing more violent than the next. The sounds were coming from below my feet, in the cellar. I continued listening to the violent orchestra, all the while knowing that Clinton was staring directly at me. Suddenly, the roar of a blood-thirsty crowd quickly followed the violent ensemble and my eyes widened from remembrance. That noise, coupled together in a beautiful concert of raw human brutality created imaginary sounds that echoed in my head. The sounds of crowds chanting various derogatory comments, cheering for blood and gore; the sounds of elbow drops and suplexs; the sounds of flesh smacking metal rails and ramps all began surrounding me like the roar of a thunderstorm. I shook my head a few times slightly to shake the imaginary sounds away… Only to find that not all of them were imaginary. I heard a man scream out, “Who’s next, mother fuckers?!” and it all came from below my feet, in the hell of this once serene bar. “There a problem, son?” an impatient Clinton asked. I didn’t look towards Clinton, I looked outside. Snow filled the sky and covered the ground in its chilly remnants. “You know, sometimes. The best sounds in a bar are those that are natural.” ”The fuck does that mean?” Clinton asked frustratingly. “That means, if you want to keep your competitors angry…it's wise not to depress them with that crap music.” Clinton froze for a moment, he quickly fumbled with his remote to replace the docile tunes of Johnny Cash with a more earsplitting rock band that could envelope the entire audible range of his bar. He turned to speak to me, possibly to come up with some cheesy cover-up story, but before he could mutter the first syllable of his first word, I was out the door and into the chilly blizzard of winter.
