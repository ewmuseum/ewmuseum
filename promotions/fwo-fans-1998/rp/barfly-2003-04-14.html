Barfly

14 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The bar was dimly lit and smokey. The normal neon "Budweiser" signs were replaced with opaque, black "Guiness" signs complete with the harp logo. This was England after all. The place was fairly empty, it was getting around closing time. Meanwhile, the bartender calmly wiped down the bartable, cleaning the mess that his former patrons had made. Not many of the few remaining people were at the bar, most sat around it in booths. Except for Griff Truxell. He sat front and center at the bar, leaning over resting his elbows on it. A pint of "the black stuff," as those limey fucks so often called it, rested in front of him half-full. He knew he wouldn't be able to get a decent, even cold glass of Tequila here. Griff was tired, beaten. Under his brown leather duster and "The Truth" T-shirt he was bruised. To make things worse his jaw still hurt, which was evident every time he took a drink. He played around with the two English pounds and some change which was also in front of him. Griff laughed to himself, looking at the Queen's image. "God damn she's ugly," he though to himself, "doesn't matter how much of this shit I drink, she's still ugly." The smile he had was brief. He just kept thinking back to Slaughter, to Warfare, to all of it. To everything that had happened to him in the brief two weeks since he joined the fWo. Fox, Smooth, those two clowns running the show. They made him fly all the way out to bumfuck middle of nowhere in this third world hellhole to what? To disrespect him? To disrespect Greatness? Some audacity from those two hacks. One, one couldn't step into a ring now-a-days without breaking a hip or dislocating something. A fucking "has been," trying to live vicariously through the people he quote, unquote "now employs." The other, well the other just simply was a "never was;" period. Then there was that kid. Griff struggled for the name. "Covington," he whispered, "Carson Covington." Griff scoffed loudly, garnering a glance from the bartender. Some fucking "rook" trying to mess with Wrestling Itself. Wasn't going to happen. Hell could freeze over and that boy wouldn't split a hair on Griff's head. Griff took a sip and winced. He needed to sleep, Warfare was in two days, and then Cyberslam. He downed the rest of his beer and slammed the glass to the table, causing the coins on the bar to rattle. Griff nodded at the bartender with a smirk and stood. Smoothing his duster out he walked towards the door. "Oy, you're a quid short," the bartender yelled, picking up the change. Griff threw a middle finger up arrogantly as the door slid shut.
