Ghosts of the Past [4] - Die Another Day

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Go back to 3... September 23rd, 1999 After walking a solid half hour, newfound friends---no---companions...no, new aquaintances had found their way to a small...well, you could call it a diner. Amy'd had her fake ID readied for use, but there wasn't even a bouncer...Kinkade just lead her straight on inside to be nearly flattened by the smell of smoke and hard liquor. He stepped up to the bar, ignoring stares while she gingerly crept behind - paying a little too much attention to the odd stares cast her way. Kellen glanced back at the little redhead and took hold of her elbow again, pulling her up a little closer. This time, however, Amy never struggled - It did seem like he was honestly concerned for her safety."You drink?" "Depends," She chuckled, "Do you buy?" "Yeah." He replied back "Usually just for myself though, so you'll have to elaborate on what you want or the first drink that I buy you will probably be the last." "Southern Comfort." Kinkade said across the bar as the tender handed him a glass and a bottle, watching on as he knocked the glass aside and took a swig "And whatever she asks for." "One fifty one...don't care how." Amy's request was met with a bottle and shot glass, but she followed Kinkade's image with a grin - going straight for the little bottle. Amused enough, he watched her for a second until she sat down and smiled back at him with a raised eyebrow. "Something on your mind or what?" "You're French...?" "No kidding!" The redhead laughed, "Nothing gets past you. Was it the ten mile thick accent or just ESP that tipped you off?" Kellen chuckled himself, looking back to his bottle for a moment while Amy continued... "So I'm a foreigner, French and wearing a...what was it, a 'RAPE ME' sign?" She winked, "Alright, so it's your turn now; What's your sign, if I may be quite so corny?" Well...she was a confident one, alright. Her sweet and curious stare was a mask, though... "My sign says 'STAY AWAY'... guess that makes two of us that don't follow instructions." Kinkade replied, taking back another mouthful from his bottle and quickly changing the subject from himself. "So anyway... you seem like a nice enough kid, how'd you end up in hell?" "Believe it or not, I went to the trouble of lying to get here." Kinkade's eyebrows arched with a look that all but screamed the girl's apparent ignorance and Amy smirked again. She curled up on the otherwise uncomfortable, rough wooden chair and laid her head to the side on a shoulder, cringing slightly. "I'm going to art school all the way across the city, but that's not why I'm here - just something I was good at and used a scholarship to as an excuse to get somewhere where I could do something I like just as much. Drawing pictures doesn't relieve much energy or aggression...punching shit does. And since the walls around my house were getting kinda dented, I figured I might as well try it out on a softer surface and make some money doing so..." She smiled an unusually sweet smile, lifting her head only to cock it slightly to the side. "Apparently I'm the first person with the drive to want to come here for a start to a career in beating the shit out of ugly things for sport." "So you made special effort to come down here?" Kinkade said with sarcastic smile "Forget the art school, there's a sanitarium across town too. Really... you need to pack your shit together and go home tomorrow because you've driven down a pretty narrow road, I look at you now and tell myself that there's still time for you to turn around and head back out, things only get narrower from here onward believe me." He shifted uncomfortably in his seat for a moment. "Example, tonight you watched me knock lumps out of some guys face... disturbing right?" "Pretty." Aimz answered. "Tonight was a good night." He continued sternly "Bad night... six or seven people would be dead right now, and that's exactly why you can't stay here." "You've never seen me punch." Amy snickered, "I'm not afraid...that very well could land me in a coffin, but it only makes me tougher to come where the guys are toughest - if you think of it one way." "Fair play." Kinkade replied "But I've seen a lot of people come and go with that very same attitude, punches win the money around here but it's the bullets that keep it." Another pause followed as he reached over the bar and pulled out a glass to finally pour his drink into, a quick sip later he continued. "I just don't want to paint anything less than a realistic picture, you know? If I said that you could do your art class stuff during the day and live a normal life whilst coming out here at night to do what I do I'd be a fucking liar, for every person that you beat up and take money from there's another bullet with your name on it." Kinkade said matter-of-factly, picking up his glass again and looking across the room as though he wasn't going to offer any more advice on the matter. His eyebrow shot up, almost expecting a slap in the face for bringing reality to her doorstep...but Amy had only cocked her head. "I'm not afraid of bullets, either." She remained adamant. "When it comes, it comes. I'm not leading any double life...I'm just here to learn and get by. I need money and I need to relieve a little aggression before I shoot someone myself, so I'm here and the drugs keep me crazy enough to stay." And with that, she returned his arched, assuming eyebrow without hesitation. "Nothing wrong with a little of what the doctor didn't order, even I'll agree with you there." Kinkade volleyed back with a giveaway wry smile. Amy laughed again, "Didn't expect you to disagree, or even be surprised. I'm nuts enough to think I could survive here anyway...and as far as that giant 'Rape Me' sign goes, I dunno what's bringing that on. Then again, two differently colored eyes aren't much of a turn-off to anybody...look at David Bowie!" She winked at him and cringed as another mouthful of 151 burned its way through her throat and chest. It hurt, but Kinkade could tell she wasn't exactly new to such a hard drink. "But seriously...compared to some of the weirdos and wannabes I've seen around, I'm surprised you don't have a little harem of blondes who can't quite afford tit jobs, but'd have 36DD and three dicks in their hands if they had a chance." Kinkade laughed out loud for a moment, bringing Aimz to a halt. "Believe me." He began "You're talking to a guy to whom it wouldn't matter if your eyes were pink and orange, when you've walked a certain distance in a pair of shoes like mine you start paying far less attention to what people look like on the outside and a lot more who they are inside. So please, no more put downs about how your eyes look... ...they're beautiful." It was Amy's turn to stare as Kinkade leaned back into his chair a little, thumbing at the screw top to the bottle in front of him. For a second she pondered how long it had been since this guy was actually nervous in front of someone, let alone someone half his size...but she darted straight back to her own fear - she was only eighteen, but nobody had ever provided her with quite so awkward a moment... Just then - definitely a 'first time in a long time' - the redhead formed a sentence of few words. "Err...thanks." A few more moments of awkward silence followed as Kinkade poured out the remains of his Southern Comfort bottle into a glass before quickly changing the topic of conversation. "Anyway... enough about you, lets talk about me." Kinkade said, breaking the silence as the two chuckled to themselves for a moment at the sheer audacity of his comment. "Yeah, here I am pouring my little Canadian heart out and I still don't know a damned thing about you." Amy replied with a smirk, "So how'd you end up here?" "Well." Kinkade said with a sigh, his facial expression suddenly glazing over as though everything they'd talked about and shared in the time that'd passed had been engulfed with misery "My story is basically the reason you and I are never going to agree on why you're here." He leant forward a little onto the bar, rubbing his temples as though what he wanted to say was physically hurting him. "You're the first person around here that I've ever seen seek this place out, most folk around town are here because they did something wrong that fucked up their life or because someone did something to them that fucked up their life." Kinkade said, pausing for a moment before reaching for his drink and taking back another mouthful. It appeared for a good while as though he wasn't going to finish what he was saying at all. "So..." Amy said a little cautiously "...which one are you?" Kinkade didn't need words, he swiftly moved his hand towards his left eye and after a couple of seconds digging around left Amy sitting astounded as in one swift motion, he pulled out his eyeball and dropped it into the glass of whiskey he'd poured. "Guess that makes me the latter." He said with a scowl before shying away from Amy slightly, waiting for her to get up and leave. The small redhead didn't back up at all, and that only worsened the expression on Kinkade's face. Instead of running away, she laughed! "Trying to scare me off?" Another chuckle, "Gross as it might be, you're gonna have to do a lot more than that to freak me out. I've got mental scars to double that...just because you can see - no pun intended - something doesn't mean it's any scarier." "Well." Kinkade said taking his eye back out of the glass and popping it into the socket "You're the first person in about five years that didn't run a mile... so I guess you passed the test." "Out of the glass and back into your head? That can't be healthy." Amy added, unflustered by Kinkade's disfigurement. "Nope... but the alcohol gets into my bloodstream a lot quicker so whatever important stuff you have to say, do so now because in about five minutes I'm gonna be in no fit state to answer." Kinkade said turning back to Amy "You got a place to go tonight? Because I'm gonna get the hell out of here while I can still walk." "Yeah, I've got a place to go...think I can manage to call a cab this late?" "We'll catch the same one and it can drop you off first, you think the people around here are dangerous... you've seen nothing until you've experienced some of the fucked up cabbies." Kinkade got to his feet and flicked through his change so that he could make the call "So you doing anything tomorrow night? I know a place called The Bitter End over in New York City if you're interested... I'd rather you come out with me than be walking the streets like you were tonight." A couple of moments passed whilst Amy thought over the proposal before Kinkade spoke up again "One forty seven, Bleeker Street... you gonna be there?" "Absolutely," She smiled, suddenly rather meek and noticing her overenthusiasm, which might put the rather hardened 'street' fighter off slightly. "Err...yeah, I guess I could go." "Good deal... now let's get the hell out of this dive." Kinkade grunted with a quick grimace "I think I drank enough whiskey tonight to fuck up my good eye as well." With plans made, drinks finished and cab hailed, the two were in their respective 'homes' within the hour... Within three, they'd be either passed out or fast asleep... The past few hours had been eventful, indeed...but even still, neither could even fathom the events of the next twenty four. Continue...
