Interview

25 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
12:03, eve of Twilight...somewhere in the backstage area. 'Oh, what was that damn song...' A wolf in sheep's clothing the ultimate disgrace wrapped up as a gift of god exploding in your face... 'Leave it to me to search for fitting music when I can't even open my eyes...' Amy Campbell was lost in herself for the first time in far too long. Unfortunately, it took an extreme injury to entice her to think so thoroughly...rather appropriately, a head-splitting concussion. Her face seemed a mess; painted a sickening reddish brown by drying blood, her forehead was swollen and already showing bruises. She'd put her guard down long enough for Graphic Violence to take advantage of the size different and it had cost her tremendously, because the man obviously knew his neanderthal fighting technique rather well. 'Bet he's bashed a few heads mercilessly against floors before. Heads or coconuts...' She would've smiled, or laughed over her verbal stab at the deliberate and very brutish force 'The Graphically Violent one' had employed to put her in that condition, but at that point Amy wasn't even certain that she was concious. All she really knew were her thoughts, she certainly wasn't aware of any feeling aside from the throbbing, searing burn that was quickly growing in the depths of her skull... 'Well, Amy Campbell...you've really got yourself fucked over this time.' Was she even alive? The inability to conciously move seemed to dash her hopes. It was then that against all odds, and finally supporting her prayers, Aimz felt the cold of a tiled floor devloping against her cheek. And that was the last of unconciousness' hallucinogenic comfort she was allowed... (Note: This was written on a Palm m125 because it's Christmas day and I've had way too much spare time to learn how to use the little writing utensil on it, so excuse the length and errors in spacing...I'll fix it up soon.)
