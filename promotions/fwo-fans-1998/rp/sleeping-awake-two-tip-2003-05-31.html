Sleeping Awake (Two) - Tip

31 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Double Meanings! Sunday June 1st, 2003 1:34 AM Hotel "Wow, depression doesn't look good on you. Put some make-up on." Keep on repeating...something... Keep on...repeating...something... Much to Amy's sudden chagrin, Renee Williams was seated on the bed of her hotel room with, as usual, an opinion. ...keep on repeating... ...TIP, TOP! End... Since CyberSlam, Aimz had been the hardcore champion...but there was a key behind it: She was the injured hardcore champion. The hardcore champion who had absolutely nothing to do but sit with her belt around her waist, not used or defended...hell, it didn't even function as a belt, what good was the thing to someone with broken ribs? I gave it all, I gave it up then... What good was someone with broken ribs? None. What good was someone with broken ribs and an arch enemy who had nothing better to do than go after them week after week while in their weakened state? Absolutely none. That did nothing for ratings, she just prayed the 'executives' wouldn't figure that out anytime soon - especially considering how low her paycheck during this injury time already was. I took it in and got enough, but... She'd been back, happy, for two shows. Lance Knight once, even after her ribs had been mangled the week before by him, and then the redhead had finally recieved her chance to beat the ever-loving snot out of Kellen Kinkade once and for all... I try to cancel out whatever I can't laugh about. Needless to say - Things never went as planned. She won, retained the title in her first two real defenses and that was worth celebrating...what wasn't worth the celebration, however, was the effect the latter match had. So what does that say? There can't be any way... ...And that was the purpose of Renee's presence. Amy was injured all over again, but this time refused just to sit at home with nothing but Brand Frontier's ex wife coming over to pester her to pass the time. Angel was busy hunting for a contract, so being anywhere but on tour for Amy was a no-go, because there was nobody for her outside of fWo in that case. Being on the road kept Brandon close and everybody else relatively far - After all, it was the only means of escape from everybody sour in day to day life she had...so she stayed touring as usual, just brought one of the few good points of the outside world with her. I've done my worst and no one knows. "Y'know, Renee...you say an awful lot for someone who's getting their vacation paid for." Amy didn't look terribly amused, but her telling half-smirk indicated that she was at least enjoying the company - and the company saw that fact, beaming accordingly. "Hey, it's not my fault Brandon's not getting the job done." The comment went unnoticed...for all of a half second. "...........Say whaa?" One more day to fool them all... Excellent, Renee was getting a rise out of her. "Oh, sure...you say he's just busy and obviously can't be around all the time, but I know what's really going on." "Start running."...Quite the rise. There was a momentary stare-down, but it broke with a violent fit of laughter - literally. As Amy chuckled, she swiftly kicked her long time best friend. "You're damn lucky I've known you long enough to realize you could kick the shit outta me." She winked, "So how's...home?" "Home?" "Yeah, around the valley. Do my parents still hate me?" One more day to drop the ball... Renee's expression dimmed from its usual perky, uplifted state. "They never hated you, y'know. They're just bitchy...you know that. And yeah, your grandma constantly nabs me at the grocery store...you can probably start talking to your mom and dad, I guess they've been watching and reading about Brandon and actually kinda like him." "That's..." For a second, she was at a loss for words. Her parents - father especially - actually liking Brandon...that was something Amy never thought she could even dream for. "......scary." "Scary? It's GOOD. You can take him up to visit, this time without hostility." Amy grumbled, "Nope. You know them...even if they do like him, they wouldn't admit it to me for a while - It would mean they were wrong about him initially." That much was true; Her parents wouldn't dare come straight out and admit they were wrong about something...according to them, they were adults and therefore didn't make mistakes or wrong judgements. They were perfect in their own eyes, and she was the flaw in the otherwise well to-do gene pool... ...One more day to fade away. The blonde snuffed at her, "Ames...you'll never know unless you try." "I know already, and I don't care to put up with them for another second regardless of what so-called revelations about my love life they've come to." Fill a head with sand just to make it better... "Like I said - You don't know that they'll react the same way. If you have some patience and...well...try to be civil..." I had the chance to beat it in, but I never... Aimz shivered slightly at the thought of bending to her parents' exact ideals by acting civil while they treated her like dirt. She'd done that through most of her teen years just so she'd keep a place to live...now she was relatively 'adult' and didn't have to take that crap - Which she'd proven many times before. ...I should have taken it right then. "Not gonna happen." With a sudden mood switch went the topic, and Amy made it apparent enough that it wasn't going down that road again. She nearly hated her parents for various things and their hatred of everything male that came near her - even...no, especially Brandon - was only the very tip of it. On the flipside, Brandon's only remaining parent was in the hospital...which meant nights for Amy without even him. Regardless, the subject did a 180. "So how's...stuff?" ...And almost failed at first... One more day to fool them all... Thank God Renee picked up the change..."Stuff's good, do you have any clue how much I'm getting paid to be your 'assistant'?!" She beamed, "I don't even have to do anything and I'm getting salary regardless! It rules." One more day to drop the ball... Amy smirked, "I'm gonna start making you earn that salary, y'know. If Ivy got you hired, she was having a blind moment...but I've forgiven her. You're just not allowed to sit around and piss me off is all, whether or not I'm 'just sitting around' injured..." One more day to fade away... "Speaking of sitting aroud, we've got zilch to do tonight." The redhead sighed: They were, in fact, stuck with twenty four hours of absolutely nothing leading up to RUAHH~! "This is what I get for showing up a day earlier than Brandon...I should've gone with him to visit his mom." "Bah, old ladies aren't nearly as cool as.........me." Renee's smile still hadn't faded for whatever reason. She watched Amy get up and fidget through a small, temporarily free stack of CDs with the lingering question of fun still at the tip of her tongue..."So what could we do tonight? There's nobody fun here yet..." "There's always someone fun here, earlier than everyone else too." "Who?" The answer wasn't far gone. Both girls jolted up to stare at one another, smiling their telepathic smiles. It was easy! "....Ivy." ...Now you've been taken, you won't stop shaking, embrace every minute...you're all that's left in it. It's not happening... Some other day, you'll fool them all.
