Business Becoming Usual, Part 1

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nobody thought he could do it. Nobody thought he'd make it into the second round, at least in terms of hand-to-hand combat inside the ring against a former world champion. Then, when he did it, the smart marks claimed that Ross, said former world champ, didn't belong in the Internet Title Tournament, that it was a step down, and that the logical move was for him to get "the rub". Now, facing Omega in the quarterfinals, he'd get his, and go back to the middle of the pack. The lower-end middle of the pack. Fuck that. He is me. I'm here to stay. Ross got his ass beat the way it was coming to him, the way he deserved. His Survivor push is over. His oral sex adventures on Universal pushes are over. Now he's just one of us. We are those who came in on our own merit, not through some jackass contest. We WORK for our achievements, rather than get them presented on a silver platter via winning a contest that you could win on WQFU. We are those who bust our ass night in and night out, and take the cab to and from the hotel to the arena, not the private limo, because the company wants to "show off their latest star". Well, guess what Ruben. You've just found out what it's like to mess with someone who takes the cab. Not fun, is it? But I digress. You're last week's news. And tomorrow night, I'll make more news. I'll bring Donaven Winters back. This Omega bullshit makes Donaven look like fucking Barney. The freak turned him into one of his freaks, just when he was going somewhere. Universal had this planned all the way. He had this roadblock set up, just in case I made it past his boy. That fucking paranoid moron; he doesn't understand what my destiny is: To give him the first credible champion he's had since never. And, if it has to include beating the piss out of Omega, Beta, Alpha, Version 7.0 Unlimited Access all over the ring, then business is business. But first, I have some business of my own to take care of tomorrow night. One small, retarded obstacle out of the way. Three bigger, even more retarded obstacles yet to hurdle.
