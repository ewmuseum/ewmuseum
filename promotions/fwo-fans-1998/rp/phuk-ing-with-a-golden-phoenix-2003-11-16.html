PHUK-ing with a Golden Phoenix

16 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tokyo, Japan. A beautiful city, steeped in culture and history. If you're David Cross, it's just another place to get your ass kicked. "C'mon guys, I'm one of you," he pleaded to the two men standing before him. "I'm a PHUK'er from the States!" After he said it, David realized that perhaps they didn't use the acronym for Phan Hiroshi Ultimate Karate in Japan. Hopefully the two surly-looking Asian gentlemen didn't speak English, or at least weren't easily offended. The kick to his midsection told him he was wrong on both counts. It's said that in space, no one can hear you scream. In the distant foothills outside Tokyo, at the remote temple of the Golden Phoenix, it's much the same. You can scream all you want, but nobody can hear you. David took advantage of that, secure in the knowledge that his masculinity would not be questioned by anyone overhearing his shrieks. "Can't we all just get along?" Cross gasped as another kick lifted him off his feet and sent him tumbling down the flight of stone steps that led to the front of the temple. The first man cleared all the steps in one leap and met David as he landed at the bottom. His leg swung in a high arc, before dropping it straight down onto Cross' head... or where it was just moments before. The second man joined the first at the bottom of the steps, both men dropping into combat stances. David got up and dusted himself off. He admired their identical red Gi's. "You've got matching pajamas," he quipped as he wiped the blood from his lips. "His and Hers outfits. Does that mean you're going steady?" Obviously, both men left their sense of humor in their other set of pj's. Punches and kicks peppered Crucifix, but every block was parried. Dust flew off his jacket from ever impact. The martial ballet continued as David worked his way around both men without once launching an attack of his own. He used his speed to keep both men off-balance, occasionally cracking a "yo mama" comment to ensure both men pressed the attack without realizing what he was doing. They caught on to the ploy quicker than David had hoped. A stunning hook kick spun Cross like a top. He dropped down and swept the first man's legs out from beneath him, twisting back to his feet and kneeing the falling man in the chest as he continued to fall to the ground. Blood was flowing more freely from David's split lower lip. He blew a crimson spray into the second man's eyes. Just as the blinded man opened his eyes, he was greeted with the bottom of David's foot that stopped inches from his face. "Are we done here?" asked Cross as he held his foot in place. "Enough!" David turned his head. Standing at the top of the steps was an elderly bald Asian man wielding a walking cane, the handle of which looked like a duck's head. Beside him stood the most beautiful woman David had ever seen. Her luscious black hair gently cascaded over her slender shoulders, and her jade green eyes took his breath away. Both wore Gi's, and neither one looked like they should be in a temple. The old man looked like he should have been buried about a year ago, and the woman... well David dreamt of places where he'd like to see her. The elderly man shook his cane. "Fighting outside the temple is forbidden." He looked down at the two men who had been standing at the temple entrance minutes before. "Why are you attacking visitors? He might want to buy a souvenir." The first man spat something in rapid Japanese. David tried to catch all of what was said. He looked offended. "Wait wait wait. He said I did what to his horse?" The woman smiled and melted Cross in his shoes. "He said you claim to be a student of the Golden Phoenix. He said you were from America." "Clearly he has been trained," the elderly man said as he studied David carefully. "He did fend off their attack. And his Whispering Ram technique was accurate. Sloppy, but accurate." "I'm a student of Master Hiroshi." David set his hands on his hips. "And there's nothing wrong with my Whispering Ram, thank you very much." The elderly man raised a thick, caterpillar-like eyebrow. "He trained with your father?" he asked the woman. David's mouth dropped open. "M-Michelle?" Her green eyes sparkled. "David? David!" Michelle ran down the steps and hugged David tightly. Cross lost himself and hugged her back, enjoying the feel of her body against him and the smell of her hair. Like fresh flowers on a spring day. The mental image of Master Hiroshi pulverizing his testicles into a bloody paste prompted David Cross to regain his composure. He stepped back from the young woman. "Wow, you look..." A thought of his left nut rolling around somewhere in his shoe. Better get a grip. He bowed. "It is a pleasure to see you again after so many years." Michelle Hiroshi bowed back "It has been a long time." She smiled. Even her teeth looked gorgeous. David knew he was probably going to be maimed by Michelle's father when he returned to the States. It would be worth it. "This is Master Yoeda," she said as she motioned toward the elderly man. "Piss you off I must not," David said to Yoeda as he bowed. "Throw down you can." He whispered to Michelle, "I loved him in Episode Two. He's not as green as I thought he'd be. A little taller though." Master Yoeda nodded. "Master Hiroshi mentioned that you'd be coming by. You may stay here as long as you wish." "Well the fWo got me a room at this pretty swank hotel." Cross looked over at Michelle, who tossed her hair back with a flowing sweep of her hand. "But it would be an honor and a privilege to stay with you." "Excellent. You will join us for training as well, I trust?" David was well aware of how hard Golden Phoenix practitioners trained in Japan. He also had his schedule with the fWo to maintain. Still, it meant there'd be a chance Michelle might do the splits. "Wouldn't miss it for the world." Cross followed behind Michelle and Yoeda. "This way," Yoeda said over his shoulder as he walked through the huge metal doors leading into the temple. "He can stay in the room beside me," Michelle volunteered. "That way I can help him with the routine around here." She turned her head slightly and David's eyes locked with hers. "I wouldn't want him getting lost." Oh I'm already lost, David thought to himself. And I never want to be found.
